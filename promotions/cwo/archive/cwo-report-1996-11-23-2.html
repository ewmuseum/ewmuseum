CWO REPORT 11/23/96 (fwd)

23 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(LA Hospitial News Conference Room.) CHRISTINE FERRARA: Hello, Christine Ferrara, CWO correspondent, I am here to bring you a very special report. The time is 6 am West Coast time. I was called to be told that there has been a great change in the condition of the CWO wrestler, Sandman. If this great change is for better or worse, is yet to be confirmed. Last night I was able to get a word with Sandman's friend/partner, Talon. Let's see what Talon had to say about, what seemed to most people as a cheap attack on Sandman by, Bane. (We go to the hospital waiting room.) FERRARA: Talon, I am sure you as well as everyone else, is shocked at the actions of Bane on Sandman. What has happened to Sandman's power, endurance, and unbeatable determanation to win? TALON: You see what happened to Sandman is, he has been backstabbed 2 times now by Foul. This time wasn't as bad as the time before. When they were going to go at it for the Heavyweight Title on PPV, that hurt Sandman inside. It took out his will to win, be strong, and not give up. Although he didn't show any emotions of this, it is what he felt inside his soul. And now this, I can't believe the actions of Bane. That is just the actions of a weak man, taking out a unconscious soul. Bane I'd like to see you do that to me. To take out a conscious man. Or are you to weak to do that? You better hope the Pres. don't let me get in the ring with you, ANY style match, I will revenge Sandman while he is unable to compete. And in WARGAMES, Mister, all bets are off! FERRARA: Thank you, Talon. We are all wishing the best for Sandman. (Fades back to LA Hospitial News Conference Room.) Reporter: That was a very disgruntled Talon. Here comes the personal physcian of the CWO, Dr. John Rupke, let's hear the word. DOCTOR JOHN RUPKE: Please be seated. Ladies, gentlemen, press, friends of Sandman. I am here to inform you on a great change in the condition of Sandman. Sandman's condition has taken a great change for the worse. What Bane did, when he ripped out the IV, he tore part of a vein, this is something we connot take care of because of the Sandman's very rare blood type. In fact, we have never seen his blood type before. So if we do have surgery, it would take out a lot of blood that we could not replace. The only thing we can all do is hope for a miraculous recovery of Sandman. I'll take any questions. FERRARA (to camera): I can not believe it. This is a real blow to Talon, Sandman's friends, and the CWO, Sandman never returning? I just cannot belive it. (You notice Talon heading towards Sandmans room.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, November 23, 1996. As you can see, CWO Correspondent Christine Ferrara giving us a look at the condition of Sandman, which, according to Dr. John Rupke, is not good. We will keep you posted as information comes in to us. Meanwhile, we are one day away from great CWO PPV excitement, NOVEMBER PAIN: ULTIMATE WARFARE, make sure you check out the CWO REPORT SPECIAL EDITION: THE STORY SO FAR this weekend. Last night, however, the superstars of the CWO were in Gulf Shores, AL, putting on a great show for our fans there. Let's look at the results. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 22, 1996 GULF SHORES, AL Deadly Sins vs. Highlanders Sins defeated the Highlanders in 11:47 when Sloth pinned Gavin McCloud after the Repention (superplex by Pride, Slothsault by Sloth). Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli vs. Mad Doctors Ghost MDG pinned Manocelli after a Sudden Death splash in 19:17. After the match, Gold Kard and Don King commanded MDG to deliever several more Sudden Death splashes, requiring Manocelli to be taken from the ring on a stretcher. Unholy Duo (pAYnE and Hate) vs. The Bad Guys Bad Guys defeated the Unholy Duo in 5:18 when Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson pinned Payne after both Bad Guys struck him with illegal objects (Bad Guys' Bash). Sorrow vs. Johnny Reb Johnny Reb made Sorrow submit to the Texas Cloverleaf in 12:51. STREET FIGHT: David & Goliath vs. Biblical Powers Biblical Powers defeated David and Goliath in 6:31 when Savior pinned Goliath. MOD Damion vs. "Big" Rich Clark Clark defeated Damion by disqualification when Doomsday attacked him in 9:00 after he had just Choke Slammed Damion. Sandman Jr., Miguel Sanchez, and the Biblical Powers made the save. GangLord vs. Akillies Ganglord pinned Akillies after a legdrop in 13:57. After the match, Ganglord continued to beat on Akillies, but Wildfire made the save. Inferno vs. Bane Inferno defeated Bane in 13:09 by disqualification when Bane refused to release the Merciful Death sleeperhold when Inferno was in the ropes. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Gold Kard (champion) vs. Joe Eagle Gold Kard pinned Eagle in 8:16 with a rollup. After the match, Gold Kard was attacked by the Tower of Power, which prompted the rest of Gold Kard International to come out, and the two teams brawled all over the building. CHAMPION'S CHALLENGE: World Tag Team Champions Creatures of the Night vs. North American Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses The Bosses hit the Spike Piledriver early, which led to a surprisingly quick victory, with Pit Boss #2 pinning Night Slinger in 3:09 after a vicious clothesline. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in St. Petersburg, FL, for a great card there and then tomorrow it's Tampa, FL, and NOVEMBER PAIN. Let's look at the lineup for St. Petersburg. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 23, 1996 ST. PETERSBURG, FL Team Blitzkrieg (Kamikaze and Mysteria) vs. D. E. A. T. H. (Benton and Green) Sandman Jr. vs. Steel Highlanders (Conor and Gavin McCloud) vs. Unholy Duo (Payne and Hate) Hellhound vs. Ben Slocum Alliance (Tiger Rogers and David and Goliath) vs. Apocalypse (Bane, Plague and Pestilence) Wildfire vs. Wayne Carpenter The Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jazzman Taylor) vs. Biblical Powers (Savior and Moses) Joe Eagle vs. Akilles Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli Talon vs. Panther NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses (champions) vs. The Bad Guys (Joe Rucker and Ron Johnson) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally, let's look at the lineup for tomorrow's NOVEMBER PAIN. Like I said, CWO REPORT SPECIAL EDITION: THE STORY SO FAR will be out later today, detailing the events that have led up to this PPV, but here is an advance preview of the lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ EYE ON THE STORM: Bad Guys, Sin, "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. Gods of Thunder, Highlanders Battle Cat, Mondo Gecko, Regulators vs. Ganglord, Sorrow, Eternal Wanderers Sandman Jr. Miguel Sanchez, "Big" Rich Clark, ??? vs. Maximum Overdrive, Baby Waldo, Mercenaries Samoans, Biblical Powers vs. Deadly Sins, Unholy Duo Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli, Joe Eagle, The Big Cool vs. Gold Kard, Brent "The Ace" Alles, Pit Bosses Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, Hoss and Little Joe vs. Steel, Panther, D. E. A. T. H. Inferno, Tiger Rogers, David and Goliath vs. Dark Raven, Hellhound, Creatures of the Night Flyboy, Clark Gray, Hollywood Muscle vs. Wildfire, Akillies, Kamikaze and Mysteria ???, Talon, Flame Spirits vs. Bane, Scourge, Apocalypse Hangman, Rooster, Mad Doctor's Ghost, Lafayette vs. Johnny Reb, Judgment, Southern Pride TEXAS DEATH MATCH (winner gets a shot at the World title on the 12/3/96 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER): Master of Darkness Damion vs. "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum FINAL BATTLE ROYAL FOR WARGAMES WINNERS WORLD SERIES OF WRESTLING (4 out of 7 FALLS), WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP ON THE LINE: Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see, Bane's vicious attack on Sandman has left doubts about who the fourth member of Talon's team will be, if it will be anybody at all. We thought it might be Lord Foul, but he said he will not be at the PPV. However, Lord Foul did have some comments for Bane, and we take you to them now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Lord Foul the Despiser sits at a large table within his Blackpool castle. His silver hair tied back into a tight ponytail, he sits and calmly sips his wine.) LORD FOUL: Bane, something rotten, something... Foul. You "accused" me of wrestling for the fans, of wrestling for the money, of wrestling for the honor. You are correct, I wrestled for all of those things, Bane, and what exactly is it that you wrestle for? To maim people? To beat up defenseless hobos and attack police officers? (Foul brings up his silver chalice to his lips and slurps down another sip of the red wine.) LORD FOUL: I wrestled for the honor of the CM2WF when they needed me, and now we've got Bane running rampant in the CWO. The reporters have asked me all the way from across the ocean, who can stop Bane? Sandman? Well it seems Bane has taken care of Sandman, peace be with him during his recovery. Damion? Damion has come close, but even in his last match Bane came out on top. Gold Kard? He may be perhaps the only one with the skill to do it, but he's not out for what's best for the CWO. (Foul sets down the goblet and grabs his old CWO world title, which he sets on the table.) LORD FOUL: Many men in the CWO gunning to be the greatest of ALL TIME. Well, let me tell you, with two world titles, a North American and a World Tag Team Title, and a Six-Man Title, I'd say that makes me the best all round competitor in this league, now ask me again, who can stop Bane? Bane, my friend, you remind me so much of myself! You walk into the ring and intelligently maim your opponent until he is nothing, then pin him, you're smart, Bane, but I've been doing it LONGER than you have! (Foul grabs an envelope out of his coat pocket and holds it up so that the camera can see it.) LORD FOUL: A check, a check from the offices of the CWO, with more zeros in it than Ovagadro's number. A check for good ol' me to come back to the states, and pin Bane in the middle of the ring, but the question is do I really want to bother? Do I want to save men like GKI, or Eagle or Damion from the coldness of a man like Bane? Or would I rather just do as I have done in the past and ally with such a man, well, let me tell you, Lord Foul would ally with such a man... but Lord Foul the Despiser is no more, for I have hidden behind the mask for too long. Bane, when you step into the ring with me, it will be against Hile Troy, and I will not have my limos in waiting, and I will not have my butler and my cook inside the arena, I will have your head between my legs, when I Viledriver your damn head right through the ground! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Strong comments from Lord Foul, who now apparently wants to be known by his real name, Hile Troy. Anyway, a newcomer to the CWO is "Lonestar" Mike Williams, and he immediately set his sights on one of the top competitors in the CWO, a man who will be going for the World Championship on Sunday, "Master of Pain" Barry Hart. We now take you to comments from Lonestar. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens up with 'Lonestar' watching Barry Hart's flash in digust on a CWO monitor.) "LONESTAR" MIKE WILLIAMS: So... this is yer little way of introducing rookies to the CWO, eh, Barry? Interesting. Now, after actually seein' and hearin' yah speak and address the public, I'm not so sure that you were even a worthy opponent to showcase mah skills on. You have the skills it takes to win the title, but you are *sorely* lacking in the ol' brains department. Why would a grown man go around fondling and cuddlin' his <shudder> butt?!? I didn't come here to fight people with deranged emotional relationships with their @$$es! You may be the second contender to the World belt, but to me you were a test, and just remember that I *did* give you one hell of a fight. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Well, that's all the time we have for today... wait a minute... I'm just being told we have a special report on Sandman, we take you live to his hospital room now. _______________________________________________________________________________ FERRARA: Johnny, we have heard that Sandman is fastly deteriorating, let's go see if we can get a word with Talon about his condition. (Camera goes into the room, Talon doesnt notice them, he is blinded by tears. The camera zooms in on Sandman's heart monitor. Talon grips Sandman's hand.) TALON: Stay in there, man. (Sandman starts to twitch, he opens his eyes looks at Talon.) Sandman (mumbling): Thank... you. (The camera zooms back in on Sandmans heart monitor. The wave of lines suddenly turns into a flat line. A loud beeping sound goes off. The camera moves again to Sandman who lays motionless.
