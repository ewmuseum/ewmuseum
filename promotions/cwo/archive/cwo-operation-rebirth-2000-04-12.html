— Announcement —

Hi, this is [name withheld], fed-head of the CWO, and we are currently having a "reboot/recharge" (similar to what WCW is currently doing). If you're interested in getting involved in a fed that has been around for 4 years +, drop me a line. Right now we still have 5 slots left for the World
Championship Tournament that will kick off the next phase in our evolution.
After those 5 slots are filled, the rest of our new applicants (up until we close our doors at around 32 handlers or so) will be placed into a battle royal for our Television title.
Also, we need tag teams, and if you have a tag team you'd like to enter, they will be entered into the World Tag Team Championship Tournament.
So, if you have a singles wrestler OR a tag team that you would be interested in having wrestle in the CWO, let me know. Thanks for your time.
Sincerely, [name withheld] CWO fed-head http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5403/
