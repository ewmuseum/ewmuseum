CWO REPORT 11-9-97 (fwd)

10 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
KAPTAIN KRUNCH: It's been a busy week for the CWO's uncrowned World Tag Champs. We fought the Neutered Brothers last night. We might have lost the match, but so what? Those two little girls were too scared to put the titles up against us. But at least they know now that when the time comes that they're forced to face us in a title match, they just don't have what it will take to beat us when those belts are on the line. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Tonight we take on those goody goody fruits, Wayne Schillo and Brian Williams. A sure win. Tomorrow we get the Highlanders. Those skirt wearing cowards were out on the last FEVER crying how they need protection in the CWO. What you two idiots had better realize that this is the sport of pro-wrestling. If you can't hack it, you'd better just do yourselves a favor and get the hell out. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: And I get to meet one of the CWO's resident freaks, the Sandman. Big man, you might think you have something intimidating going with those dorky contacts you've been wearing, but intimidation goes only so far. But you'd better ask yourself what the hell you're supposed to do when you're up against somebody who can't be intimidated. What are you supposed to do then? I'll tell you. Get ready to explain to the whole federation why you couldn't get the job done once I decide that my hands hurt too much from punching you upside your stupid head. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Lastly, as strange as it sounds, not one team has approached the Mercenaries to back out of the upcoming tag team tournament at November Pain. Well, you still have a few weeks to go before you can drop out. I mean, the Mercenaries are not monsters. We're not going to beat you up if you decide to drop out. We will beat the hell out of you if you decide to stay in. Why? Simple. We are THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD. It's self explanitory. __________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey sitting at the CWO REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Hello everybody and welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Last night the CWO was on the Notre Dame campus for another House Show. Let's get to the results of last night's action. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 11/08/97 South Bend. IN Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Stonewall Mason after the Rattler. Time of match: 6:43. Skyhawk pinned Wrath after the Skydive. Time of match: 4:32. Jade Dragons defeated Coldfront when Kenji Ishida pinned Steve White after the avalanche. Time of match: 13:29. Masashi 'Lightning' Lanther defeated Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson by countout. Time of match: 17:27. 'College Guy' and 'The Hammer' defeated Mercenaries when Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo made Korperal Punishment submit to the Boston Crab. Time of match: 5:47. Brent 'The Ace' Alles and Lord Hile Troy wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Holocaust/Red Raven defeated The Dark Side by disqualification when the Creatures of the Night attacked. Time of match: 14:57. Mondo Gecko and Gold Kard wrestled to a double disqualification, with both the CPE and GKI getting involved. Time of match: 2:29. World Tag Team Champions the Newton Bros. defeated Bad Guys in a non-title match when Howard Newton pinned Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker after the small package. Time of match: 14:59. After the match, the Mercenaries attacked the Bad Guys, resulting in both locker rooms emptying out into the arena to help restore order. WASHEY: Tonight the CWO returns to the comforatble confines of the CWO Arena for the weekly televised Showcase show. Here's the scheduled matches for tonight's card. CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE 11/9/97 CWO Arena HOUR #1: NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson vs. Masher Maccabee (champion) Mercenaries vs. Highlanders "The Ultimate Heel" Union Caine vs. Painmaster HOUR #2: Kaptain Krunch vs. Sandman The Big Cool vs. Creatures of the Night LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: The Focus vs. Southern Dynamite (champion) WASHEY: Now it's time to get to the interview portion of the program, and unlike yesterday, I'm happy to say that we actually have some. First off, we have the "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher. Let's go to his comments. ______________________________________________________________________ (The new CWO broadcaster, Double K Krash Kelly is in the home of "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher for a special interview) Kelly:Hello everyone, this is Double K Krash Kelly and I'm in the home of "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher. Mr. Deutscher, thank you for granting this special interview. Deutscher:Krash, you and I go way back, you know your always welcome here. Kelly:Ok, lets talk about Terror Night. You had a match against the former CWO World Champion, Foolkiller. Deutscher:There isnt much I can say, he beat me. Kelly:Lets talk about the end of the match. Yes he nailed that devastating piledriver, but after the match, he nailed you with another piledriver causing some damage to your neck. Deutscher:Yes he did do some damage to my neck. And for the first time in my career, I was scared. After that second powerbomb I couldnt move. Kelly:What was going through your mind as you were being taking away on that strechure? Deutscher:Everything. What direction this was gonna take my career, how I would be able to suport my wife and kids. Kelly:Yes how is Jaime taking this. Deutscher:She doesnt want me to return to the ring, and I dont know what to do, I dont want to disappoint the fans, but I dont want to disappoint my wife. Kelly:Now at November Pain, you are slated to take on Carlos Maia. Deutscher:Weather I return to the ring or not, Im gonna get that stupid piece of trash and give him the ass whooping he deserves!! And as for Foolkiller, I'll have my day with him as well!! Kelly:Thank you for your time!! Deutscher:Thank you, oh by the way, say hi to Tom for me!! _________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up today we have probably the hottest stable in the CWO, and it's not GKI. It's the Unit, and here they are with their comments. _________________________________________________________________ (The camera opens inside a dark, smoky sports bar. On several big screen TVs scattered throughout the establishment, the Montreal Canadiens are decimating yet another pitiful victim. In one corner of the room, a rowdy crowd has gathered to cheer on the Habs, and it is to this corner that the camera heads. As it gets nearer, the members of the UNIT can be seen. Skyhawk and "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday both wear Canadiens hockey jerseys, and the Newton Brothers, Howard and Steve, wear Montreal T-shirts. Several empty pitchers litter the table, and Johnny B. Goode is in the process of emptying yet another as the camera approaches.) TV ANNOUNCER: ...and there's the pass...no, it's intercepted! He skates across the blue line, no one on him! There's the windup, he shoots...HE SCORES!!! (The bar goes ballistic as the Canadiens celebrate yet another goal. When Johnny B. Goode finally notices the camera, he hits the mute button on the remote control, creating a resemblance of quiet.) GOODE: Back again, huh? Wanna beer? (The camera shakes from side to side, signalling a negative.) GOODE: Your loss. So what you boys want this week? More info on us? You see, right now, we're the big boys on the top of the mountain. Everyone wants a piece of us. Sure, Sean just took a loss to Focus, but it was a clean victory. That's all we care about. Winning isn't everything, it's the journey to the end that matters. HOLIDAY: You know, it's funny. I come out week after week accepting the challenges that get thrown my way. Then, the one time I make a challenge, it gets ignored. Yeah, Union Caine, I'm talking about you. I'm still waiting to see if you're man enough to step up to the plate and do a rematch. See, the way I figure it, you and the rest of your Empire buddies are all just full of hot air. You guys come out, week after week, saying no one's accepting your challenges. But when someone challenges you, do you accept? Hell, no! I never thought I'd say this, but I gotta quote Wendy Thomas here: "Quit whining and start winning!" S.NEWTON: While we're on the subject of Empire, let's talk about the Dogs of War. (Crowd booes this name.) S.NEWTON: You boys say that no one's accepting your challenges, huh? Okay, let's do it! Us and you, one fall, no countout, no DQ. You wanna show everyone how much you know, well, we're the kings of the mountain, babe! We've said it before, and we'll say it again. You wanna piece of this? Then get in the ring! H.NEWTON: Now, on to the next team. Deadly Sins, you guys are the CWO North American tag champs. Congratulations on being number two. Now, at the next PPV, we got a belt vs belt match with you guys. That means, no DQ, no countout, winner takes all! We've destroyed you before, and we'll do it again! S.NEWTON: Damn straight! H.NEWTON: But here's the way it works, Sins. After we beat you for those belts, we will prove once and for all that we are the greatest team here in CWO. Walking around with both tag titles will show everyone that we are not a team to be trifled with. GOODE: Careful, you don't want to get too confident, guys. They are the North American champs, after all. You don't get a title in the CWO without having proved something, remember. SKYHAWK: Oh, yeah? Then how the hell do you explain Bloodbath? See, punk, I had you beat right there in the middle of the ring! Then out of nowhere, Wildfire lays me upside the head with a baseball bat! Now, I've just about had it with being hit in the head! Between Tommy Boy and his steel chair...and I'm not done with you yet, kid...and Wildfire's bat, I'm damned lucky I don't have a couple of concussions! GOODE: Geez, calm down, 'Hawk. You're getting a little too red there! SKYHAWK: Oh, I'm just getting started. Now, you, Tommy Boy, you and me got a Death Match this Tuesday night on FEVER. That means that we keep going until one of us can't make a ten count. I don't know if you can count that high, but don't worry, I'll help you out. See, I'm gonna totally destroy you, so that you don't have to worry about making the ten count. And here's what else. Next week on FEVER, I want Wildfire again! We fought before, you moron, and I had you beat until those other jackasses the Cabin saved your hide. This time, it's just you and me. So what do you say, Wildfire? You big enough to step in the ring with me again? Or are you gonna keep pearl harboring anyone that says something you don't like? You go to Tom Krepps and tell him you want me. Krepps, you got my number! GOODE: CWO, I wish I could tell you that these next couple of weeks are going to be nice to you. But the fact is, we're a little unhappy right now. And when the UNIT gets unhappy, people get destroyed. So any of you boys in the back of the locker rooms who want to try to cheer us up, it's really quite simple. All you gotta do is get in the ring. That's it. Just get in the ring. And when you do, you'll know it's time to go to school, because we're gonna show you the right way to wrestle. (The crowd starts chanting, "Get in the ring! Get in the ring!") (The camera fades to black as all five members of the UNIT flash the "U" sign.) ___________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up on the REPORT, we hear a few words from the Painmaster. Let's go to his comments now. ___________________________________________________________________ [Painmaster] I've said it time and time again. I've always said that I am one of the top wrestlers here in the CWO. And at TERROR NIGHT, I proved it again by dismantling Brian Williams. Cane, you're next on the list, pal. And Eagle, you'd better be watching this, because at NOVEMBER PAIN, you're gonna get the same treatment. Maybe ending your career will finally get me a title shot around here. ____________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Finally for today, we hear from somebody new to the CWO and set to make his CWO debut. Here is Wesley "The Rock" McMichael. ____________________________________________________________________ (Wesley "The Rock" McMichael walks through a field in Texas. He is in blue jeans, cowboy boots, a t-shirt, and a black leather jacket that says "The Rock" on the back) McMichael:You know my daddy always told me, dont mess with a texas twister cause it'll get ya. Now the texas twister is in the CWO ready to do some damage. Johnny Reb, Joe Eagle, Gold Kard, I dont really care who you are. Be ready CWO, cause I can feel the whirl winds a blowing!!!! _______________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody Wednesday on the REPORT! (fade to closing credits.)
