CWO REPORT 2/21/97

21 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens up on DOOMSDAY prior to their match against the Wild Bunch. The Mercenaries, wearing their titles, flank Maximum Overdrive, while Baby Waldo stands in the background with his title draped over his shoulder.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Wild Bunch, you three morons don't know what you're getting yourselves into. First off, let's get one thing straight. These titles belong to us. Your claims that we've disgraced these belts are total lies, and reveal your jealousy of our abilities. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: The only teams that were disgracing these belts were the teams that had them before us. They were only defended maybe on PPV's. We've taken these belts and made them worthy. That figures, since the only team that's worthy to hold them is us. KRUNCH: As for your whining about our ways of getting things done around here, I suggest you mind your own business, before you find yourselves one of those groups of people we've been beating up on. PUNISHMENT: Your interview really made us laugh. Going on about how you're going to do this and you're going to do that. You three idiots forgot that first you have to win these titles. We've beat every team into the ground that have stepped into the ring looking to gain these. We've turned back all challenges. KRUNCH: Tonight, you will come up short, despite all your claims, your guarantees, and your empty threats. (Krunch, Punishment and Waldo hold up their belts.) PUNISHMENT: You want these titles? (Krunch, Punishment and Waldo drop the titles to the floor in front of them.) KRUNCH: Come and get them, punks. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, February 21, 1997. Last night, the superstars of the CWO were in Biloxi, MS, let's look at the great action that went down there last night! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS Thursday, 2/20/97 Biloxi, MS Mason-Dixon and Attitude Dance wrestled to a double countout. Time of match: 1:31. Sandman Jr. pinned Bloodbath after the Permanent Dream flying legdrop. Time of match: 14:07. Inferno pinned Mondo Gecko after the Inferno's Blaze. Time of match: 3:51. Masher Maccabee struck Mondo Gecko with the Television Championship belt before Inferno pinned him. Maximum Overdrive pinned Chris Greco after the Pileup power bomb. Time of match: 7:46. CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE 1: Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers pinned North American champion 'Monster' Rich Clark after the flip off top rope. Time of match: 18:56. Goliath (a member of the Alliance along with Rogers) attacked 'Monster' Rich Clark outside the ring before rolling him back in for Tiger Rogers to get the victory. Bane made Goliath submit to the Merciful Death after outside interference by 'Monster' Rich Clark. Time of match: 13:52. The Focus pinned 'Lonestar' Mike Williams after the Focal Point (top rope powerbomb). Time of match: 11:18. Focus used the ropes for leverage. Biblical Powers defeated Da Hood Rats when Moses pinned Warren after The Commandment (springboard moonsault). Time of match: 8:32. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Doomsday (Baby Waldo and Mercenaries, champions) and Wild Bunch (Battlecat and Regulators) wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 11:46. Mondo Gecko defeated Panther by countout. Time of match: 3:58. Panther was attacked by the Masked Rose while outside the ring and chased him back to the dressing room - thus earning the countout. CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE 2: North American Tag Team champions the Bad Guys defeated World Tag Team champions the Creatures of the Night when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Night Crawler after the Bad Guys' Bash (double strike with steel chairs) and outside interference by the Pit Bosses. Time of match: 9:15. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Huntsville, AL, let's look at the lineup for that great card! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 21, 1997 HUNTSVILLE, AL Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Mondo Gecko Deadly Sins vs. Regulators Gold Kard vs. Johnny Reb Mercenaries vs. Alliance Hellhound vs. Savior Clark Gray vs. Battlecat Steel Vs. Bloodbath Wrath vs. Mondo Gecko NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Big Cool vs. the Bad Guys (champions) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Pit Bosses vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, "Raven's Cage"): Dark Raven vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: One thing on everybody's minds these days is the presence of the Masked Rose. This stranger has attacked several CWO superstars, and the pattern is very unclear. First, Wayne Schillo was attacked on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER a couple of weeks ago in his match with Cake Boy. Then, Bane was attacked not only at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, causing him a countout loss to Deadly Sin Wrath, but also at several house shows. Now, the Masked Rose has struck again, this time against Panther. What is the connection? We'll keep you posted. Now, though, let's give some analysis to other events currently happening in the CWO. Everybody has seen the descent of Clark Gray since the unfortunate death of his cousin and partner, Wayne Carpenter. He doesn't seem to be getting any better, and now the CWO superstars who face him have to contend with a very unstable person, including Battlecat, who takes him on tonight in Huntsville, AL. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Battlecat is in the interview area.) BATTLECAT: Clark Gray, my sorrow goes out to you for a loss of a relative and partner, but your attacks have been unacceptable and I am tired of seeing my ally Chris Greco get hurt. It's time you face someone who can fight back and teach you a lesson. Pain or no pain, you're taking on the King of the Jungle and I am tired of seeing your acts go unanswered. When you come and face me, YOU WILL GO DOWN! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: On Wednesday, the CWO was shocked by the loss of a superstar, Miguel Sanchez, when he was defeated in a "loser leaves town" finisher through the table match for the North American championship by his once best friend, 'Monster' Rich Clark. Here then, is the farewell interview from Miguel Sanchez. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Miguel Sanchez is in his locker room, cleaning out his stuff, wearing an EC F'N W shirt and track pants.) MIGUEL SANCHEZ: Rich, I had no idea that your hatred towards me was so great, and so powerful that you would be willing to end my career with it. But, you did. Now, I have to leave the CWO, after being the Rookie of the Year, and winning both the CWO Television and CWO North American Titles during my stay here in only the last year. I hope that all of you can remember some of the great matches that I had here, against such awesome competitors as Sandman Jr., Brent "The Ace" Alles, and Gold Kard, as well as the many others that I can't even go into right now. But, as I leave I want to show a montage of some of my greatest hits while I was here. (A video plays, with "Six Foot Foam Skull" as the background music. It shows some of Sanchez's best moments, such as him hitting Sandman Jr. with a Guillotine Leg Drop from the top rope to the floor, while Sandman Jr's head was on a chair, and him putting Gold Kard through a table at Valentine's Massacre. It ends with Rich Choke Slamming Miguel through a table and then pinning him to win the CWO North American Title. Back to Sanchez.) SANCHEZ: Well, I bid all of you, even Rich, good luck in your future matches, as I say, farewell. (He grabs his duffel bag, and walks out. Left sitting in the room is a mask. FADE TO BLACK.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Thanks, Miguel, and of course, we wish you the best in your future endeavors. One of Sanchez's former compatriots, Moses, had a tough match on Wednesday as well for the CWO World Championship, in a steel cage strap match against Wayne "Keuka Guy Schillo. We take you now to comments from Moses after that match. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Moses and the Savior are in the ring, Moses is bloodied, and The Savior is making sure that he's still conscious, which he is, but is extremely upset.) MOSES: Wayne Schillo, congratulations on winning our match. It was an honor to even be in the ring with you, and I hope that you may dance again sometime, with the results being different. But, onto more pressing matters. This Sunday I get my re-match with Tiger Rogers. I know that you are a good competitor, Rogers. You've already proved that to me. But, what you haven't proven is that you can beat me. With Savior watching my back, Sunday will be the end of your extremely short title reign. I held that title for close to three months, and I will get it back. And, to make sure that none of your associates interfere's in the match, I want a special stipulation. Titles can change hands on Count-outs and DQ's, that way you don't try anything funny to keep your belt. I know how you work, Rogers, and I can tell you this. You may think that you are good, but I KNOW that I am better. SAVIOR: Jimbo Jones, Inferno, or whoever else may get involved in this match on Sunday, watch out, because, even though this is my second to last week as a professional wrestler, I'm still not afraid to hit any of you with a Revelations Powerdrop to the floor to make sure that you don't get involved in the match. Watch out, because we're coming for you. (Moses gets up and begins to exit the ringside area, but he is limping a bit. FADE TO BLACK.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: The man who took the title and career in the CWO away from Miguel Sanchez now has comments for us, "Monster" Rich Clark. _______________________________________________________________________________ ("Monster" Rich Clark is in a dark room. Nothing much is visible, with the exception of a few whips and chains, and the CWO North American Title, which sits on a pedestal in the middle of the room, in the middle of a spotlight.) "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: Miguel, I'm sorry for what I did Wednesday night. I never meant for anything to come between us, but I guess that I proved to be the MASTER OF HARDCORE, eh? Now, I have my first title in the CWO. It may not be the best belt here, but I do finally have some gold around my waist. Goliath, you're gonna get a shot at this belt on PPV, at SPRING BREAK. Where that's coming from, you may not know, so I will explain. Last Friday, at VALENTINE'S MASSACRE, you came out of nowhere, and attacked me after I kicked the living s#!t out of Sandboy. Now, I'm giving you the oppertunity to fight me for this coveted title on Pay-Per View, because I want the whole world to see me kick your ass all over the f@#king building. That's right. At SPRING BREAK, you and me, No Count-Out, No DQ, FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE IN THE STATE OF FLORIDA MATCH! That means we can start out in the arena, and take the f@#king match to DISNEYWORLD if we have to. I want your hide, son, and I'll get it anyway that I can. Are you ready to face the ONLY true monster on this planet. Fear me, god-boy, for I am a god. I can take life, and I can give it. There is no escaping me, and you'll find that out at SPRING BREAK. (He grabs a whip, and cracks it a few times, then bites it. FADE TO BLACK.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Other superstars around the wrestling world are reacting to the imminent retirement of the Savior, we now hear from a wrestler not associated with the CWO, Redemption. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Redemption is in the interview area.) REDEMPTION: Savior... through all the different leagues we have danced and danced. Though it was clear that I was the superior one... (smiles) I give my thanks and respect to you. For you pushed me to levels that can only be achieved when faced with greatness. As you now retire, let it be known that the Cabin salutes you... (extends hands out) MAY your lord bless you.... _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Yesterday it was announced that because the tag team of Benton and Green, known as D. E. A. T. H., attacked a ringside fan, that they would be suspended indefinitely. Now left with only one man in Blood Inc., the manager, Da Boss, speaks out about the suspension. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Da Boss stands tall in his expensive Armani suit. He removes his sunglasses and begins to speak.) DA BOSS: RANDY JOHNSON, just who do you think you are suspending the most outstanding tag team that you have in this league? I am here to file a protest on the suspension of D. E. A. T. H. I feel that my clients have been unfairly punished due mainly to their race. Would we be having this conversation if it were Johnny Reb or Sandman involved? Ask yourself Randy Johnson, would it be the same? It's a shame that we even have to go through this. In the immortal words of Spike Lee, "Can't we all just get along?" ____________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr, who is shaking his head.) STARR: Well, we hope to have a response from CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson for you soon. While the Deadly Sins take on the Regulators tonight in Huntsville, AL, another team is also gunning for them, and they are the Highlanders. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens in a CWO studio; in it are Gavin and Conor McCloud, the Highlanders.) GAVIN MCCLOUD: Well, it seems the Deadly Sins are barking on about how they're the undeclared champions here in the CWO... ha! Don't make me laugh... those two couldnae punch their way out of a wet paper bag! Ye reckon ye could be great champions, Sins? Well, we reckon you'd be pretty damn useless, an' if ye want to get a title shot, ye'll have to go through some o' the top teams in this federation, and that means us! CONOR MCCLOUD: For once in our live, we agree with tha' Pit Bosses on somethin'. Too many teams around here think they're David Copperfield, runnin' around with their hocus-pocus mumbo-jumbo. Ye think ye're scary? Ye're not even funny! So whilst tha' Pit Bosses boot around the Creatures of the Night, we're going to do the same to you, and then it's the turn of the Eternal Wanderers. GAVIN: It's time for this fed to be rescued from characters like ye, Sins, and put in the hands of real men. Have ye got the guts to get in the ring with us? Come and have a go if ye think ye're hard enough... just remember.... CONOR: THERE CAN BE ONLY ONE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Finally today, on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER this week, Brent "The Ace" Alles found himself the victim of a vicious attack by the members of Doomsday. We now take you to footage taken from Alles in a hospital bed. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Alles is shown in a hospital bed, with his mother hovering over him.) ALLES' MOTHER: Are you sure you're going to be all right? BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: Mom, shut up, and get the hell out of here, I've got business to attend to. (Alles turns to the camera.) ALLES: Well, I'm sure this is a real pretty sight for most of you in the CWO, seeing me here in this hospital bed. I had a slight concussion from those two underachieving losers the Mercenaries giving me their Flying Walenda, and two cracked ribs from a Pileup power bomb and a splash from that big fat oaf, Waldo. So, Doomsday, if you wanted me to get the message, I got it loud and clear. (Alles winces in pain, then continues.) ALLES: But my eyes are wide open, gentlemen. Schillo, I watched the tape, I saw you up in the rafters, now I want you to be a man and come out and say that you ordered a hit on me. The truth will set you free. If it is the truth, I'm not surprised. Maybe I'm getting through to you after all, Schillo, that this league isn't about fun and games, it isn't about kissing the girls and making them cry, it's a hellhole warzone, and you have got to sink to lower depths than anybody else to make it here. (Alles takes a sip of a purple-colored beverage, and spits it out.) ALLES: What the hell is this?!? INTERN: It's grape juice... you may have internal injuries.... (Alles throws it against the wall.) ALLES: Get me a bacon cheeseburger, dammit, internal injuries my @$$.... (Alles winces in pain again, calming down slightly.) ALLES: Schillo, you have got the challenge of a lifetime ahead of you at SPRING BREAK '97, going up against a man with no soul, Bane. You think I'm a bad guy, Schillo, because I put your friend out of action for a few weeks? Schillo, you are facing a man who will put you out of action FOR LIFE, without even batting an eye. Wake up, Wayne, it's time to stop being the pretty-boy college kid, and start getting down in the depths with the rest of us, or Bane will tear you apart. I used to be just like you, Schillo, thinking I had to be the nice guy, everybody's friend, please the fans. Well, those fans aren't going to pay your medical bills, and they aren't going to pay your funeral bills, so you'd better wise up and concentrate on the matter at hand. (Alles pauses for a moment, thinking.) ALLES: Now, Overdrive, if you were contracted by Schillo to put a hit on me, let me say this, you and your goons will never make it in the Mafioso, pal. I'm still standing, I'm still breathing, and come SPRING BREAK '97, I'm going to be totally focused on tearing you apart, pal. Let's face it, both of us are turning into the old world order around here, both of us FORMER Television champions, but now neither of us have seen a title shot in weeks. I've got nothing to lose, Overdrive, how many times do I have to say this, I ripped out my dreams and stomped on them until they faded away, there is nothing you can do to me, Overdrive, nothing, but there is PLENTY I can do to you. At SPRING BREAK '97, Brian "The Hammer" Williams (laughs coldly), you're going to think you had it easy after what you see me do to Overdrive. CO1 - EOT. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
