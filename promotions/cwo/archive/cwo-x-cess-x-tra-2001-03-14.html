CWO X-Cess X-tra 3/14/01

14 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO X-CESS X-TRA 3/14/01 fX network 11:30 PM (Eastern) RATING: TV-14 (L) Hosted by: T. J. Marks Tonight’s Guest: Mondo Gecko Show opening plays, then we go to the Staples Center in Los Angeles where we got to the luxury box high above the arena floor. When we get up there, we see T. J. Marks, dressed in a business suit, sitting in a chair, across from him is Mondo Gecko. Gecko is dressed in jeans, silk black shirt, cowboy boots, and a gold chain.
MARKS: Welcome to CWO ACCESS EXTRA, with me T. J. 'On the Mark" Marks! And with me is the greatest wrestler of all time... Mondo Gecko.
GECKO: T. J., you are too kind, man! MARKS: Somebody has got to tell the truth here babe! GECKO: I know it dog! MARKS: Well in this interview, we are going to talk about the past, the present, and the future. Sort of get to know the superstar of the CWO!
GECKO: Well then, let’s get this show a rollin! MARKS: All right, when you first were brought in by Randy Johnson, Tamara
Wilcox, and Lance Russell, you were called the future. Yet, you got stuck in the lower ranks.
GECKO: What you got to know is simple, this had always been a big man’s division. Whether you have monsters such as Goliath, Bane or Foolkiller or fat pieces of trash like Lord Hile Tory and Brent Alles. So as soon as I got in here I was told by Old Man Alles that I was going to have to 'rassle with the little folk."
MARKS: I hear ya man, he screwed you! GECKO: Ain't that the truth. You know that I had outwressled everybody they put me in and I still wasn't getting a title shot?
MARKS: Lets go back to the first title reign with you and Masher Maccabee. You beat him for the title for your second reign and yet the suits still didn't like you. GECKO (Sneering) Me and that damn jackass joker brought the houses down. He tried to hurt my wife, embarrass me in front of people and yet I still whipped his ass. But they suits were two busy with ... (sarcastic tone)... Wayne
Schillo... Brian Williams... and Brent 'Ass' Alles.
MARKS: Seems that the Alles family hated you from the start. GECKO: Look at what I did during my first year in the CWO. I was the most popular wrestler by the fans, a two-time TV champion and Cruiserweight champion. I had it all yet I was held down. Seems that the Alles didn't like my style of wrestlers?
MARKS: Your style? GECKO: I was exciting, flamboyant and talented... Alles and Foolkiller were plodding, big and limited. But bigger was better and that is what killed me.
MARKS: And then they took out your friends. GECKO: They fired the Regulators because of 'bad behavior'... or more likely punking out the Mercenaries. big time Alles favorites. They forced
Pistolerro to unmask and then retire his ass. They lied to his face when he got surgery... but ooops... everybody loves those Alles... and Allen Carter.
MARKS: It must have ate at you to see two fat guys hold all the glory in the CWO. GECKO: I used to hate wrestling in the CWO... knowing that my friends Taggart and Hollister had better chances at World glory than I did. MARKS: Well you ended up getting your World Title reign... after the Alleses screwed up. GECKO: (Laughing) That whole Darkness saga dropped the CWO into a big black hole. Wrestlers got pissed that a no name had the title. The fans hated a bunch of stiffs leading the group. Hell, the Outlaw Riders were the hottest thing on the market, yet we had to wait until the CWO almost went bankrupt before we got our shot!
MARKS: Why is that? I mean you are the man! The guy who creates excitement. GECKO: Because I was a Cruiserweight! And that is where the CWO wanted me to stay. But all I know is when I was the champion, our ratings shot up, our product was hot and I carried the league.
MARKS: And when Carter and the company took the title away from you… GECKO: I knew that the CWO was not for me! It was made for fat men! MARKS: When we come back, Gecko talks about his return to the CWO, his relationship with Thomas, and how Tamara played into all of this.
COMMERCIALS Camera goes back as we see Gecko and Marks sitting in chairs high above the
Staples Center. Both men are well dressed and are relaxed in the luxury suite as the cameras continue to roll.
MARKS: Welcome back, fans, to ACCESS EXTRA with Mondo Gecko. Gecko, where did we leave off?
GECKO: About how I left the CWO, T. J. my man! MARKS: So why did you turn your back on the CWO? I mean you are the greatest wrestler in the history of the league.
GECKO: But look at my treatment! I mean they humiliated my wife twice on national TV, they fired numerous friends of mine! Hell you know that the reason Carr was fired because he was too rough in the ring? Alles gets to attack women, Myers beats up the owner and commish of the league and they got nothing!!! Gee I hate to play the race card, but I find it a bit ironic!
MARKS: I hate to think that the Alleses would ever be racist? I mean such fine upstanding men they are...
(Both men start breaking out in laughter at T. J.'s joke. Gecko is holding his side from a laughter fit.)
GECKO: That was a good one dog! I give you props for that one! MARKS: But seriously! You and the Outlaw Riders still ran things here! Then you re-appear when Wildfire comes knocking on your door.
GECKO: Wildfire fought, cried, and bitched to get us in here to help ratings. And when we got here... we were put in the bottom, by Brent Alles of course, and then forgotten.
MARKS: And that caused the Brute Squad to retire. GECKO: I remember talking to Taggart and Hollister, telling them that they will be all right, that the CWO wouldn't forget them for wrestling through injuries.
And then the CWO basically butt f$%^ed them for all their hard work. So they are in Japan now being treated like gods.
MARKS: So now you are back in the CWO, thanks in part to FOX. Why come back though? GECKO: Because I am the damn best wrestler to ever step foot here. Look around at who has come and gone in the past four years here since I have arrived. Bane, Goliath, Lord Hile Tory, Wayne Schillo, Diamondback Myers,
Sean Holiday, Joe Eagle, and Mark Fulcrum. I have beaten all of those names up there and still survived in the CWO. Hell the best group of all time was the Outlaw Riders. Do you know why that is?
MARKS: Because they had you. GECKO: No!! It was more than that. We were supposed to be around for only two months. Instead we survived for two years, gaining new members along the way, but retaining the core of the group. Look at who we had in the Outlaw
Riders. Taggart, Hollister, Wildfire, Jesse Ewiak, Hollywood Haskins, Carr,
Iceman X, Wesley McMichael, El Grande Pistolerro, Marc Fulcrum, and a couple of other hangers on. Everyone was a World champion. We spat in the face of authority and we were the winners in the end. True Americans, Dark Side,
Riders of the Storm, even GKI. they were the corporate groups. They had the suits and Alles family behind them. We did it without the Alles family and even allowed the Alles family to be part of it. That is why the Riders were great!
MARKS: So now you bring along the Hellions, the 'blue-chip' prospects of the
CWO! Why them and what does this mean?
GECKO: This is a new beginning for me and the Outlaw Riders! The group is now younger, stronger, and quicker than the older version. Granted they are not as big as the Brute Squad, but this group is meaner and hungrier and mark my words you are going to see us dominate once again... YOU CAN BANK ON IT!
MARKS: Well part three of our interview will be next, and kiddies... if you though Gecko was outspoken now... WAIT ‘TIL HE IS EXTREME!!!
COMMERCIALS Back to the show. MARKS: Welcome back to ACCESS EXTRA, with me, T. J. "On the Mark" Marks! We are here with the one the only, the Main Eventer... MONDO GECKO! Mondo baby, we are going to play a game of what comes to mind.
GECKO: Sounds pretty funky to me there chief. MARKS: It is simple. I am going to state a wrestler’s name, and you respond by saying the first thing that comes to your mind.
GECKO: I can say whatever I want? MARKS: That is what ACCESS EXTRA is all about! Shall we begin? GECKO: Let the part begin baby! MARKS: All right then... Brent Alles. GECKO: Smithb**** fat a** with little talent! MARKS: Jesse Ewiak. GECKO: Whiny underachiever! MARKS: Lord Hile Troy! GECKO: Crusty old brown-noser! MARKS: Brute Squad GECKO: Greatest tag team ever!! MARKS: Foolkiller! GECKO: Stupid but talented big man! MARKS: Dave Thomas! GECKO: A Genius! MARKS: FOX! GECKO: The only show with the f%^&in b**** for me! MARKS: Beauregard Boys GECKO: Biggest reason for inbred hicks to stop incest! MARKS: Tamara Wilcox. GECKO: A goddess and my inspiration! MARKS: Hellions GECKO: Future of the CWO!! MARKS: So why come back and what is your relationship with the Executive
Producer? GECKO: Man, going for the jugular, aren't we T. J? MARKS: Just trying to get the scoop. My apologies, Gecko man! GECKO: No sweat. Let’s say that the Executive Producer wants the CWO to get big ratings. That is why I am here. The contract I signed is good money but also much more. I got TV appearances, promotional deals, perks... and power!
MARKS: What do you mean by power? GECKO: For years, I was held back by all the suits and Alles family. I was not allowed to be me, the hardcore, confident, franchise player that I truly am.
I sat back and watched as every other wrestler got the push and spotlight that they deserved. Even a nutball drunk like Sandman Junior got his chance.
Well it is my time now. The CWO is going to get the full X-rated version of Mondo
Gecko and there is nothing they can do to stop me... YOU CAN BANK ON IT!!!
MARKS: That’s it for ACCESS EXTRA. We’ll see you next week. If I feel like it. SHOW ENDS
