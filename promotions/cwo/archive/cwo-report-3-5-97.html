CWO REPORT 3/5/97

5 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everyone, and welcome to the REPORT for 3/5/97. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night we saw another explosive TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, we take you back to it in our FEVER FLASHBACK. _______________________________________________________________________________ FEVER FLASHBACK 3/4/97 PARIS, FRANCE DARK MATCH: Ten-man battle royal, the winner receiving a shot at the World title on the 3-18-97 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Participants were Bane, Joe Eagle, Arlon Flaym, The Focus, Ganglord, Inferno, Master of Darkness Damion, Sandman, Sandman Jr., and Wildfire. Ganglord was eliminated in 4:43 by Joe Eagle. Enraged, Ganglord then pulled Joe Eagle out of the ring and the two brawled back to the dressing room. Joe Eagle was officially eliminated at 4:45. The Focus was eliminated by Sandman Jr. at 5:55. Sandman Jr. was eliminated by Master of Darkness Damion at 8:03. Inferno was eliminated by Arlon Flaym at 9:05. Master of Darkness Damion was eliminated by Arlon Flaym at 10:09. Wildfire was eliminated by Sandman at 12:07. Arlon Flaym was eliminated by Sandman at 14:53. Sandman was eliminated by Bane at 20:02. Bane won the battle royal. MATCH ONE: Serpentmaster defeated Clark Gray in 11:04, pinning him with an inside cradle. Comments from Johnny Reb about his match later on that night with Gold Kard. Comments from the Regulators about their match with the Eternal Wanderers tonight. The Regulators then receive a strange delivery, apparently from the Brotherhood of the Rose. MATCH TWO: Regulators defeated Eternal Wanderers in 19:52 when Regulator Fire pinned Exile after the Tombstone Piledriver. Comments from Bloodbath about his desire for a rematch with Masher Maccabee. MATCH THREE (TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP): Champion Masher Maccabee defeated Maximum Overdrive in 14:21, pinning him after the Jersualem Jackknife followed by the Homentaschen Hangover (somersault leg drop). This came after Brent "The Ace" Alles attacked Overdrive outside the ring from the crowd, delivering an Allesbreaker on to the concrete floor. MATCH FOUR: "Lonestar" Mike Williams defeated Panther, pinning him with an inside cradle after Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers, along with the Alliance, distracted Panther. MATCH FIVE: Highlanders defeated "Mr. Extreme" Chris Ohloff and Brad "The Man" Couchman in 10:17 when Gavin McCloud pinned Ohloff with an inside roll-up. After the match the Highlanders were attacked by the Eternal Wanderers and Ohloff and Couchman, with Couchman piledriving Gavin through a table. Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, and Hoss and Little Joe make a surprise appearance, saying that they have been "blackballed" from the CWO for unknown reasons, and that they refuse to leave the ring until somebody comes down and talks to them. CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson comes down to the ring and tells the four men that if they can beat a foursome at the SHOWCASE SPECIAL for SPRING BREAK '97, that they can have the chance to wrestle in the CWO again. The four men accepted, and left the ring. MATCH SIX (SUBMISSION MATCH): No official decision was rendered in this match between Gold Kard and Johnny Reb, as special referee Don Frye (from the UFC) was knocked unconscious by an attack by Serpentmaster. Serpentmaster had attacked Johnny Reb earlier in the match, but was taken away by security. However, Serpentmaster returned for another attack, this time letting a cobra he pulled from a bag hidden under the ring bite Johnny Reb. Gold Kard then applied a stumpuller until CWO officials pulled him off of Reb. Reb was taken away to a local hospital to be treated for the snake bite. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: A few updates from last night are in order. Johnny Reb was taken to a local Parisian hospital, where fortunately they were able to administer anti-venom and save him from possible harm. Reb will be staying at that hospital overnight, but will be flying back to Panama City, FL, tomorrow, in order to compete in the SPRING BREAK '97 PPV. Also, Sandman was injured in that battle royal last night, when Bane through him out, Sandman hit his head and suffered a slight concussion. He is out of the SPRING BREAK '97 PPV, but hopes to return to action in the weeks to come. Turning our attention to the SPRING BREAK '97, let's do a run-down of the great matches involved on this PPV. (SPRING BREAK '97 CONTROL CENTER graphic comes up.) SPRING BREAK '97 PPV FRIDAY, MAR. 7, 1997 7:30, CLUB LA VELA, PANAMA CITY, FL WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Bane vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) STARR: The big one, a collision between one of the most dominant champions the CWO has seen, Wayne Schillo, and the vicious, merciless, and yes, possibly even soulless competitor known as Bane. Does Schillo possess the ability to sink to Bane's level, or can he make him wrestle his kind of match? Wayne Schillo has always been known for his "most loved" status here in the CWO, especially with the ladies. However, recent actions have shown that Wayne might not be the total "good guy" he says he is. Shades of gray enter into this intense World Championship matchup. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (STREET FIGHT): Pit Bosses vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) STARR: They've been champions since the CWO restarted back in September, and they have faced all competitors and turned them away. Now, however, the Creatures have to deal with former North American Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses, and the Bosses think they have the Creatures' number in this Street Fight match. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP, "BATTLE OF THE GIANTS", FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE IN THE STATE OF FLORIDA: Goliath vs. "Monster" Rich Clark (champion) STARR: 7', 401 pounds of Goliath going up against 7'4", 446 pound "Monster" Rich Clark in what should be a very violent encounter indeed between these two gentlemen. Clark has been making his presence known by attacking people who have received World championship shots over him, hopefully he can stay focused on the BIG task at hand for him at SPRING BREAK '97. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Attitude Dance vs. Bad Guys (champions) STARR: An encounter between the Big Cool and the Bad Guys ended in disqualification at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, and now the Big Cool's allies, Attitude Dance, get a shot at the North American titles. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP ("IRON MAN/IRON BARS" CONTEST, STEEL CAGE, TAPED FISTS, MOST FALLS IN 30 MINUTES WINS THE MATCH): Mondo Gecko vs. Masher Maccabee (champion) STARR: A proud moment for Mondo Gecko was when he won the 10-man battle royal at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II to determine a new Television champion after Brian "The Hammer" Williams was forced to vacate the title due to injury. However, that moment of happiness would be very fleeting indeed, as Masher Maccabee upset the popular youngster from Oakland, CA, on the following TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Now, in a steel cage, these men try to see who can win the most falls in 30 minutes, both of them with their fists taped, this will definitely be an intense one indeed. Masher Maccabee has cost Mondo Gecko several matches over the last couple of weeks, as well as accidentally injuring the manager of the Wild Bunch, former CWO broadcaster Tamara Wilcox, so you can be sure that Mondo is looking for revenge in this one. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Panther vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) STARR: Last night on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER Rogers cost Panther a victory by distracting him, enabling "Lonestar" Mike Williams to cradle him for the win. Now Panther will seek revenge AND the Light Heavyweight title in this matchup at SPRING BREAK '97. LAST MAN STANDING MATCH, WINNER GETS TITLE SHOT AT APRIL ARMAGEDDON PPV: (4 men split up into two teams in an elimination match, if one team eliminates the other without losing a man, those two men must then fight) Gold Kard, Moses, Serpentmaster, Johnny Reb STARR: An already intense match has become so much more after last night's terrible attack on Johnny Reb by Serpentmaster, in which a cobra bit Johnny Reb. Something to think about, though, is the fact that Johnny Reb may actually have to team with Serpentmaster! Or Gold Kard, for that matter, in a match that first may see people having to compete with their worst enemy, and then maybe turn against him until only one man is left standing. BATTLE OF THE DARK SIDE: Master of Darkness Damion and Hellhound vs. Bishop and Dark Raven STARR: This encounter came about as a result of Bishop's attack on Master of Darkness Damion at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II. Long-standing Dark Side member Dark Raven then defected back to the man who claimed he was Raven's "true master," Bishop. The Unholy Alliance has been making overtures to Hellhound to join them, but Hellhound remains in steadfast support of his "master," Damion. These two sides collide in what can only be called the Battle of the Dark Side. SUBMISSION MATCH: Wildfire vs. Ganglord STARR: In a scaffold match a couple of months ago, Ganglord put Wildfire out of action for some time. Wildfire returned with a vengeance at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, aiding his friend Bloodbath in a victory during a Buried Alive match. Now, these two arch-enemies meet in a match where one of them must utter the words, "I Quit," in order to win the match. TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS: Chris Greco vs. Clark Gray STARR: A very sad situation continues, as Chris Greco and Clark Gray, both formerly of the popular Four Aces organization, once again meet on a CWO PPV. Last time Gray had the upper hand, defeating Greco by submission with the Abdominal Stretch, and this time Greco finds himself once again going up against a man who was once as close to him as a brother. LUMBERJACK MATCH: Maximum Overdrive (w/Doomsday) vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles (w/Unholy Alliance) STARR: An attack by Doomsday on Brent "The Ace" Alles, which may or may not have been "ordered" by CWO World champion Wayne Schillo, fuels this encounter, as two former Television champions meet in a match where they'll get by with a little help from their "friends." On Overdrive's side, the members of Doomsday, on Alles' side, the strange misfits of the Unholy Alliance. This one could definitely be a hot one. Highlanders vs. Eternal Wanderers STARR: Two top teams collide in a tag team war furthered by last night's encounter between the Highlanders and Ohloff and Couchman. You can be sure the fiery Scotsmen will be looking to make the Wanderers pay for their insolence. TRIANGLE MATCH (winner receives shot at LH title on upcoming SHOWCASE): Talon vs. Sandman Jr. vs. Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung STARR: Three of the top light heavyweights in the league find themselves going against each other in this unique sort of match. The winner will have their eyes on the prize, the Light Heavyweight Championship, at an upcoming SHOWCASE. (EYE ON THE STORM graphic comes up.) Focus vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley STARR: On the EYE ON THE STORM, held for free on the PREVUE CHANNEL right before the event, Focus continues his feud with Benjamin Blake Bentley. These two men have met many times over the last couple of months, and a clear victor for the feud has not been found, maybe it will happen at EYE ON THE STORM. Big Cool vs. Regulators STARR: A lot of talk concerning the Regulators these days, whether it's about title contention or that strange delivery given to them by the Brotherhood of the Rose last night on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Nevertheless, the Regulators meet the Big Cool on EYE ON THE STORM as well. (DARK MATCHES graphic comes up.) Arlon Flaym vs. Vlad the Impaler WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Wrath, Pride, and Sloth vs. Baby Waldo and Mercenaries (champions) (SHOWCASE SPECIAL graphic comes up.) STARR: Also, five great matches on the SHOWCASE SPECIAL, taping tomorrow in Daytona Beach, FL, tickets are still available for that great card, here's the lineup. Mason-Dixon vs. David "The Destroyer" Davis and Inferno Battlecat vs. Bloodbath Steel vs. "Lonestar" Mike Williams Da Hood Rats vs. "Mr. Extreme" Chris Ohloff & Brad "The Man" Couchman Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, Hoss and Little Joe vs. Kamikaze, Mysteria, Akillies, Louie the Laker STARR: You can't afford to miss all of this great CWO action, the SPECIAL and EYE ON THE STORM are of course for free, but the big daddy of them all, SPRING BREAK '97, comes to you only on PPV, live Friday night at 7:30 from Club La Vela in Panama City, FL. Until then, this is Johnny Starr for the CWO REPORT, saying we'll see you tomorrow on the SHOWCASE SPECIAL, and Friday at SPRING BREAK '97!
