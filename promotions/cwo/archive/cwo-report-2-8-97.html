CWO REPORT 2/8/97

8 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on MOD and Creatures of the Night) MOD DAMION: Highlanders, I told you that you were going to lose last night. I told you Hell's Pets were hungry for a victory and nothing was going to stop them. Now tonight you face my World Champs, my Creatures of the Night. They too are going to tear you apart to just show you what you are getting into by mixing things up with The Dark Side. Not many people live too longly to tell others what they have felt after walking into our DARKNESS. Now you, Hile Troy, you got a cheap win over me thanks to Johnny Reb and now you have my shot at the World title. Tonight I show the world and mainly the brass of the CWO that I deserve that shot. Even though you have the chance to win neither you or Wayne Schillo will have much time to enjoy the victory after the PPV because now that the "MONSTER" Rich Clark has seen the power of the Dark Side and joined with me I will take him to the top and who ever holds the CWO world title, we will be coming for you and soon the belt will be coming back to the Dark Side. Tonight though, I humiliate you, Troy and Highlanders, you lose for the second time in two nights. NIGHT SLINGER: Yes, Highlanders, like Master Damion said you two will lose. Last night Hell's Pets started your losing streak and tonight we continue it. NIGHT CRAWLER: Highlanders, you are now getting a taste of what it means to be on the hit list of the Dark Side. If you want some insight as to what it is like, look up Ben Slocum or run to Johnny Reb and ask him what is wrong with him and why, and you will find out just what happens when you get the master pissed. ALL THREE: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (The TOP is in a pre-show "interview" segment, with Joe Eagle playing the role of interviewer.) JOE EAGLE: CWO fans, this is Joe Eagle here, and I am with one of the great Tag Teams of the CWO, the Big Cool! Gentlemen, it seems that you've been receiving a distinct lack of respect from the rest of the CWO lately. Any comments? JIM "JAZZMAN" TAYLOR: Bad Guys, you made a Big mistake when you messed with the Big Cool. We end up having to forfeit to those country hicks, Hoss and Little Joe. You throw us in that garbage truck, and proceed to beat us senseless. Real brave, coming from behind and throwing a bag over our heads.... JUMBO JENKINS: I lost my appetite! You guys make me sick... RUSSELL HILL: And NOBODY wants to see JUMBO get sick! JAZZMAN: As for the Wild Ones, you fought a good fight, but we're still shocked. Give us another chance, and you'll receive the beating of your lives! Ron Johnson... we know you're here in Binghamton tonight, and your little buddy probably isn't far behind. When we get in the ring on Valentine's Day, you'll be walkin' out with two broken hearts, because WE'LL have the gold around our waists! JUMBO: Pit Bosses... you'll be eaten alive! EAGLE: You better watch your back, CWO. You never know when the Tower Of Power might just lose their patience and blow up on you!! We know the fans like a fair fight, and that's just what we're gonna give 'em! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Tiger Rogers, we meet again and this time I might have a little surprise for you. Last time you kept up with me because your chump buddy, Jimbo Jones, helped you out. Well this time not only will Battlecat be in my corner, but I might have a certain lovely lady in my corner to help me out. Tiger Rogers, I turned you from a cat of prey into a pussycat. Well, get ready to be skinned, Mr. Rogers, because you will be in Mondo Gecko's neighborhood. And when you are in my neighborhood it won't be pleasant. Peace out baby! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, February 8, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Binghamton, NY, let's look at what went down there. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 2/7/97 Binghamton. NY (Binghamton Civic Center) Mondo Gecko defeated Tiger Rogers by countout. Time of match: 13:32. Cake Boy made 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White submit to the Head Scissors. Time of match: 5:58. Deadly Sins defeated Hoss and Little Joe when Sloth pinned Hoss after the Repention. Time of match: 20:53. Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Wrath after the headscissors roll and outside interference by Bane. Time of match: 12:39. The Big Cool defeated Pit Bosses when Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor pinned Pit Boss #1 after the dropkick. Time of match: 9:42. Joe Eagle accompanied the Pit Bosses as a "bodyguard." Sandman Jr. pinned Ganglord after the Permanent Dream. Time of match: 15:46. Masher Maccabee defeated Bane by countout in :42 when Wrath attacked, leading Bane and Wrath to brawl back to the dressing room. Hile Troy defeated 'Master of Darkness' Damion by disqualification when Sandman attacked. In a surprising move, 'Master of Darkness' Damion made the save, and he and Sandman started to brawl. That brought down both Johnny Reb and "Monster" Rich Clark, as Clark attacked Sandman, and Reb attacked Damion, choking him with a towel with the confederate emblem on it. In the confusion, Hile Troy slinked away. Time of match: 10:23. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night defeated Highlanders when Night Crawler pinned Conor McCloud after the Day to Night. Time of match: 5:40. During the match the Mercenaries came to ringside and videotaped the match, apparently for their own purposes. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight is the big one, Madison Square Garden in the city that never sleeps, New York, NY. Let's look at the lineup for tonight's big MSG card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW SCHEDULE 2/8/97 NEW YORK, NY (MADISON SQUARE GARDEN) Hell's Pets vs. Coldfront Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Hile Troy Baby Waldo vs. Cake Boy Maximum Overdrive vs. Phantom Freightrain Mercenaries vs. Revolution Pit Bosses vs. Eternal Wanderers Masher Maccabee vs. Panther NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Gold Kard (champion) vs. Miguel Sanchez WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting off with Jason "Flash" Varga. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Jason "Flash" Varga is walking outside the arena with a baseball bat, tapping it against his hand.) JASON "FLASH" VARGA: Oh, yeah, CWO... the Flash has arrived! Two big wins, and I'm on my way! Baby Waldo, you felt the Flash Flood. And as for you, Overdrive, the only name change for me might be to Mr. Kick-Everyone's-Butt. Yeah, the enforcer is here, and ready to take care of business! (Varga begins to pace nervously) VARGA: Alles, you better not show your face here tonight. You'll be prayin' for the commish to come save your scrawny butt! Now that I've got the equalizer on my side... (Varga pretends to listen to the bat talking to him.) VARGA: What's that? Oh, don't worry... you'll get your shot at Alles. Then the rule breakers better beware! Flash is officially on duty! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: This week, CWO Light Heavyweight champion made a unique challenge to Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, the CWO World Champion, who is also under 240 pounds, qualifying him for the Light Heavyweight division. Moses challenged Schillo to a "Belt vs. belt" match, where the winner would get the World title, and the loser would get the Light Heavyweight title. We now have Wayne Schillo's response. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo is in the interview area.) WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: Moses, you're a good light heavyweight champ, but you need to remember that being the heavyweight champion is an entirely different playing field. You see, you're primarily a tag team wrestler. You might be able to get by as Light Heavyweight Champ, but in a Heavyweight title match, your weaknesses are much more obvious. You are a very good wrestler, and I have the utmost respect for what you've accomplished, but the fact remains that this Heavyweight title is something that I have worked very hard to attain, not once, but twice. If you really want to have a shot at the title, you can do so on only one condition: It MUST be a strap match. Just you and me, no managers, no Savior to help you. One on one, with a strap to make sure you can't run away. What do you say? _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: If Ganglord wasn't enough of a threat in the CWO, he now has went back and got two of his "friends" to aid him in his war against Bloodbath and others here in the CWO. We take you now to comments from Ganglord. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera open on GangLord, T. A. S., and two other men standing in a dressing room.) T. A. S. (laughing evilly): Well, well, well, we warned everyone that they would be comin', and now they are here, so CWO, listen up, 'cause we got the newest and best tag team to hit the CWO right here standing beside us. GANGLORD: Yep, I am am gonna be the one that introduces them, they are Warren and Nate but you'll be callin them Da Hood Rats! Warren, Nate, why don't you take your chance to walk up here and talk to da CWO. WARREN: I'd like to start things off by saying we ain't holdin' nuttin' back, we came here for one reason and this is to win the tag team championships and we will go through anyone to get them and... NATE: And to help our buddy Ganglord to finish the jobs he needs to do to win the belt he wants. T. A. S.: So, CWO, you best be on the lookout for these guys 'cause they's here and now you cant run! (camera fades out) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today, we'll see you tomorrow!
