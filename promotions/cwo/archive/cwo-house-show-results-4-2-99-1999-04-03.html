House Show Results - 4/2/99

April 3, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 4/2/99 Philadelphia, PA (First Union Spectrum) "Playboy" Wayne Schillo opened the show up with an in-ring hype segment. He was accompanied by two of his "playmates."
Warhawk pinned 'Mr. Technical' Cooper Young after the War Bomb. Time of match: 13:23. Col. Taggart defeated Fallen by disqualification when the Creatures of
Darkness attacked. Time of match: 6:53.
Snake Martin pinned Man Mountain Marko. Marko nailed his signature move, the Chi-Town Hammer, but "Playboy" Wayne Schillo was down at ringside distracting the referee. While Marko tried to get at Schillo, a barely recovered Martin rolled him up from behind for the win. After the match
Schillo and Martin beat on Marko, but Simon St. Severe made the save. Time of match: 11:15.
Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned Sandman after the rollup. Time of match: 12:54. Iceman X pinned Simon St. Severe after the Ghetto Buster. Time of match: 7:41. Iceman X continued to brutalize Severe after the match, but Marko made the save, returning the favor from earlier in the night.
CWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Creatures of Darkness defeated Reb and Fantastic when Night Crawler pinned
'Irresistible Assassin' Eddie Fantastic after the flying legdrop, pulling the tights. Time of match: 21:05. Creatures of Darkness retained the CWO
World Tag Team Champions title.
Television Champion Vladimir Popalov pinned Southern Dynamite in a non-title match after the Russian Sickle. Time of match: 6:21.
Brian 'The King' Derks defeated World Champion Darkness by disqualification when the Dark Side (Sandman, Warhawk, the Fallen, and the Creatures of
Darkness) attacked. Time of match: 5:56. Cooper Young, Col. Taggart, Brent
"The Ace" Alles, Johnny Reb, and the "Irresistible Assassin" Eddie Fantastic came down to make the save (or really just to brawl with the Dark Side).
The show ended on the crowd cheering on the men who drove off the Dark Side.
