CWO REPORT 3/2/97

2 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Bloodbath in the cemetary with a dim red light shining.) BLOODBATH (dark mysterious voice): Masher Maccabee, why don't you put your money where your mouth is? Put your world TV title on the line against me! You can't stop me, I will take your title! And when I take your title, I will take your SOUL! You can't battle the power of the darkside, REST IN PEACE! (The camera fades out as the mysterious bells begin to toll.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, March 2, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO finished up its British Invasion tour, with a final stop at Belfast, North Ireland. Let's look at what went down on that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS Saturday, 3/1/97 Belfast, North Ireland Arlon Flaym pinned Tommy Lightning after the Hurricanrana into a Cradle. Time of match: 13:29. Moses pinned David 'The Destroyer' Davis after the springboard moonsault. Time of match: 16:03. Mondo Gecko pinned Inferno after the headscissors roll. Time of match: 7:18. Panther made Kurt 'Thrill from the Chill' Gerstung submit to the Eclipse (combination sleeper and Oriental thumb). Time of match: 7:23. Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned 'Master of Darkness' Damion after the dropkick and outside interference by Bishop (fireball). Time of match: 7:07. Gold Kard pinned Johnny Reb after Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash). Time of match: 15:52. Mercenaries defeated Deadly Sins by disqualification. Time of match: 22:46. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Champion Masher Maccabee defeated Bloodbath by countout. Time of match: 6:21. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night defeated The Big Cool when Night Slinger pinned Jumbo Jenkins after the Lights Out. Time of match: 9:53. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Don't forget to watch a great SHOWCASE, coming out later today. Here's the lineup! _______________________________________________________________________________ SHOWCASE 3/2/97 HOUR ONE - TAG TEAMS: _____________________ Wildfire and Bloodbath vs. Eternal Wanderers Highlanders vs. Sandman and Talon Chris Greco and Tommy Lightning vs. Pit Bosses HOUR TWO - LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHTS: ______________________________ Johnny Reb vs. Sandman Jr. Focus vs. Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Mondo Gecko vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: A stipulation has been agreed upon by the CWO Executive Committee for the Wildfire-Ganglord grudge match at SPRING BREAK '97, it will be an "I Quit" match. Here now are comments from Wildfire. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire is at his home in Venice Beach, CA, with his lovely manager Miss Amber.) WILDFIRE: As everyone knows, I have a tough match at Spring Break 97. I do remember what happened the last time I got into the ring with Ganglord. It's something I have to live through all the time! And now to all the CWO fans, I will make you this promise! I promise to rid the CWO of that human piece of trash, Ganglord! MISS AMBER: Hey, T. A. S., you know I've been with Wildfire for quite some time, and I've heard all the comments you've made towards me! (yelling) You want to talk sh*t about me, well go ahead, and at Spring Break 97, Im gonna toss your @$$ out of the CWO along with Ganglord! (Wildfire laughs a little and puts his arms around Amber.) WILDFIRE: OK, save your energy for Spring Break! I have one more order of business to attend to. Hile Troy, you are one of the greatest superstars in the CWO! I have a question to ask you. Will you train me for my match at Spring Break? MISS AMBER: Whatya gonna do when the WILDFIRE runs WILD on you! (The camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: On the EYE ON THE STORM for Spring Break '97, Benjamin Blake Bentley continues his intense feud with this man, The Focus. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: Well, what can I say. Benjamin Blake Bentley, I beat you again. If you ever want to wrestle me again, consider it a signed match. I am not afraid of anybody, and I will take on anybody who wants to take me on. A match, a fight, whatever. Consider this somewhat of an open challenge to this federation. There are a lot of great wrestlers and champions in the CWO. There is no getting around that fact. It makes me proud to be here with such great competitors, but I will still beat whomever I step in the ring with. I don't plan on leaping right to the top, but after time, I will climb to the top of the heap. Give me the time that I need, and I will accomplish all of my goals. A few more victories, and who knows... maybe a title shot will be in order. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr, as CWO MARCH MADNESS graphic comes up.) STARR: Fans, if the excitement of SPRING BREAK '97 wasn't enough, we now are able to announce to you a great event coming to the CWO in the latter half of this month, CWO MARCH MADNESS. Taking place across the country at house shows, this 64-man tournament will definitely be a showcase of all of the talent here in the CWO. We will post the seeds in just a moment, but for more information on this great event, go to: http://members.tripod.com/~KeukaGuy/seeding.txt or e-mail: [detail withheld] for more information. Here then are the seeds and schedule for CWO MARCH MADNESS. _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO MARCH MADNESS SEEDS NORTH: 1. Masher Maccabee 2. Moses 3. Johnny Reb 4. Goliath 5. Joe "The Mean Guy" Rucker 6. Gavin McCloud 7. Bloodbath 8. Pit Boss #2 9. Wrath 10. Exile 11. Arlon Flaym 12. Vlad the Impaler 13. David "The Destroyer" Davis 14. STARR: Make sure that if you live in one of those areas, that you go and get your tickets right now to be part of this first exciting March tournament that we hope will become a tradition here in the CWO. That's it for the REPORT today, we'll see you tomorrow!
