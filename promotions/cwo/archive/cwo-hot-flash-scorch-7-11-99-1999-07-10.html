Hot Flash - SCORCH 7/11/99

July 10, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's more CWO excitement headed your way this week on SCORCH! On last week's SCORCH we saw the Fallen regain his name as the TRUE Master of Darkness, Damion, as the Creatures of Darkness (now returned to their old name, Creatures of the Night), Night Crawler and Night Slinger rejoined him as well. And now, it's WAR between Damion's Disciples and the Dark Side!
The war begins this Sunday on SCORCH, as Damion and his Creatures take on
Wrath, Warhawk, and Sandman of the Dark Side! Should be hot!
Brian "The King" Derks is slated to take on "The Avenger" Jesse Ewiak. Word is that Brian is looking for a tag team partner. Maybe this match is a test to see if Ewiak can pass muster?
Get ready for another wild one, as Smilin' Doc Watson of the Headhunters takes on North American Champion Sandman Jr. for the title in a FALLS COUNT
ANYWHERE match. Possibly not good for the Smilin' one, as this match is certainly Sandy's forteit, but you can be sure the Organization AND the
Outlaw Riders will be looking to get involved, so both men had better stay sharp!
Finally, it's a big main event, pitting MILLENNIUM against EMPIRE, with one half of the World Tag Team Champions, Johnny Reb, going up against the CWO
Living Legend himself, Lord Hile Troy! These two men have had their battles in the past, and now with the current rivalry between their two factions underway, the feelings of antagonism have escalated to a level that just might explode!
All that and more, as we are two weeks away from the first ever ROCK THE
HOUSE, and you can see more action on the 7/11/99 edition of HEAT!
