CWO REPORT 11/15/96

15 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on the Dark Side who look rather happy considering who we are looking at.) MASTER OF DARKNESS DAMION: Ben Slocum, I cannot wait 'til NOVEMBER PAIN to get my hands on you, and tonight, I have sent one of my pets after you. Slocum, tonight, Hellhound here will break you in two. HELLHOUND: Yes, Master, tonight I will take... I will take Ben Slocum deep into the DARKNESS and what I bring back will be just an empty shell of the former wimp. Master, after I finish with him, he will be in no shape to give you any challenge at the PPV. MOD DAMION: That's good, my pet, I know you will do just that and more. Also tonight, my favorite Creatures of The Night face the Biblical Powers. The entire CWO may not be ready for the ramifications of this match. Biblical Powers, the forces of the night come for you tonight. NIGHT SLINGER: Biblical Powers, tonight, me and Night Crawler are going to show the CWO that we indeed are the best tag team in this federation and that there ain't no one here who can do anything about it. NIGHT CRAWLER: After tonight we begin to prepare for the big NOVEMBER PAIN wargames. Soon we get to destroy the Alliance and that pestering Inferno. MOD DAMION: Slocum, like I told you, your nightmare has only just begun and for the rest of the CWO, stay away from the ring when my Creatures meet the Biblical Powers, I am not responsible for what may come of this. (Camera fades out on the Dark Side.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, November 15, 1996. Last night the superstars of the CWO were in Phoenix, AZ, let's look at the results of that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 1996 PHOENIX, AZ Hellhound (w/Master of Darkness Damion) vs. "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum (w/Sondra) Slocum defeated Hellhound in 7:28 by disqualification. After the match, Master of Darkness Damion delivered a Darkness Falls tombstone on a chair outside the ring. The Confederate Associates made the save. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Baby Waldo vs. Miguel Sanchez (champion) Sanchez pinned Waldo after a splash in 6:40. Pair of Aces (Flyboy and Clark Gray) vs. Lafayette and Rooster Lafayette and Rooster defeated the Aces when Lafayette pinned Gray in 4:59 after a leapfrog rollup from the corner. After the match, Rooster tried to put the Roosterlock on Flyboy while Lafayette brawled with Gray, but Hollywood Muscle came down and made the save, and the Four Aces proceeded to beat on Lafayette and Rooster until security came and broke it up. STREET FIGHT: Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson vs. Bane Bane pinned Johnson in 19:35 after a bloody match. After the match Bane put the Merciful Death on Johnson, prompting the other members of the Tower of Power to come out and make the save, which then prompted the Apocalypse and another unknown man to come out and brawl with the TOP on Bane's behalf. Deadly Sins (Pride and Sloth, w/Lust) vs. Tower of Power (Joe Eagle and Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli) Deadly Sins defeated the Tower of Power in 18:39 when Pride pinned Manocelli after Repention (superplex by Pride, Slothsault by Sloth). NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Highlanders (Conor and Gavin McCloud) vs. The Pit Bosses (champions) Pit Bosses defeated the Highlanders in 13:42 when Pit Boss #1 pinned Conor McCloud after the Spike Piledriver. After the match the Bosses were attacked by the Big Cool. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Biblical Powers (Savior and Moses, w/the Evangelist) vs. The Creatures of the Night (Night Crawler and Night Slinger, w/MOD Damion, champions) Biblical Powers defeated the Creatures of the Night in 14:51 when Savior pinned Night Slinger with the Revelations (top rope powerdrop). WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: "Big" Rich Clark vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion, w/Brian Williams) Schillo pinned Clark after the Keuka Crack (kick to the chin) in 15:21. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in Denver, CO, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 1996 DENVER, CO Johnny Reb vs. Flyboy Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli vs. Rooster Inferno vs. HellHound Hangman vs. Master of Darkness Damion TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS: Gods of Thunder vs. Bad Guys "Master of Pain" Barry Hart vs. Sandman Jr. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: The Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jim "Jazzman" Taylor, w/Russell Hill) vs. Pit Bosses (champions) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Joe Eagle (w/Russell Hill) vs. Gold Kard (w/German Choklat, champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night (Night Crawler and Night Slinger, w/MOD Damion, champions) vs. D.E.A.T.H. (Benton and Green, w/Da Boss) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now go to comments from CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area with the lovely Kellen.) SANDMAN JR: YOOOOOOOOOO! First on Friday, I get to beat up Barry Hart. Hart, you're always talking about your butt... prepare to kiss it good-bye. And Hellhound, you little devil dog, you're getting yours too. There is only one man who is allowed to raise some hell here is the CWO, and that's ME! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: Apocalypse, you may have won, but don't think this thing is over. SLOTH: The only way we lose is when other teams cheat, and once again, that has been proven true. You should've been facing another Repention, but you cheated.... LUST: NO ONE CHEATS AGAINST THE DEADLY SINS AND GETS AWAY WITH IT! NO ONE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As we all know by now, at this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, Bane threw Sandman off a balcony. We now take you to scenes from Sandman's stay in a local LA hospital. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens up in a local LA hospital, where CWO correspondent Christine Ferrara is standing with a microphone in her hand.) CHRISTINE FERRARA: This is Christine Ferrara for the CWO, and we are at the hospital that Sandman has been taken to after the horrible incident that happened at TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER this week. Let's see if we can get a word from the Sandman. (The camara goes into the hospital room, where we notice a very large injured man. He's laying in great pain, and looks to be awake. The camera zooms in on Sandman.) SANDMAN (barely able to speak): Get... that... cam... era... out... of... my... face. FERRARA: Sandman, how are you feeling? When will you return? Are you going to make Bane pay for his actions? Are you going to be the same... SANDMAN (with anger in his voice): Can't you people see, I'm in a hospital... I am in a hospital, the immortal Sandman. FERRARA: Yes, I see that, Sandman, but if you would please answer some quest... SANDMAN: I'll answer your damn questions. Bane, you may think you have won. Heh, not even close, this is far from over. You have done what no other man has ever done to me. Put me out of commision. If i were any normal man, I would give up, and say you have won. Bane, when we first stepped into the ring, I saw a man that had a lot of potential, strength, someone who could drive fear into the soul of any normal man. But you see, Bane, I am no normal man. I am immortal, and can never be killed. You may have put me out of commision, and quite by suprise i might add, but this just adds to the fire, and we are going to jump into that fire at NOVEMBER PAIN in WARGAMES. FERRARA: Sandman, what if you don't recover by then? SANDMAN (very angry): I WILL RE..... (Sandman passes out, a buzzer goes off and nurses come rushing in to the room, pushing the interviewer out of the room and closing the door.) FERRARA: Well, Sandman might be worse then we thought. I'll try to get some information from the doctor when he comes out. Wait, here comes Talon, let's hope we can get a word with him. (Talon notices the camara, pushes the camara out of his face. Talon tries to get into the room but the nurses tell him what happened. He looks very concerned.) FERRARA: Talon, a word, please. (Talon walks over to the camara.) FERRARA: What do you think of what has happened to your companion, Sandman? TALON: I think this is just sick. The officials should have done something, but NO, they didn't, and now he is in a worse condition then was first to be expected. BANE, you messed with the wrong man, and when you mess with Sandman, you mess with Talon. I'm coming after you while Sandman is in this condition. Bane, I will kick your @$$ for what you have done. (Talon shoves camara and walks away angrily.) FERRARA: Well, that's it for now. This is Christine Ferrara reporting for the CWO. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Thanks, Christine. We now go to more comments from CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Ganglord and T. A. S. are in the hotel after Wednesday's match.) T. A. S.: Well, G, look like there are a lot of people here in the CWO dat just don't like you very much these days, oh well, F*** 'em is all I got to say. Mondo, we definately want to rematch you, you gots very lucky dis time. GANGLORD: He sure did, man, some dumb areial move, because we both know dat he couldn't handle a real fight wit da strongest man around, dat for sure and this new guy, what's his name... uh... T. A. S.: Campfire, I think. GANGLORD: Well, whatever. You think you can handle da G, well bring it, man, 'cause I ain't fakin', I will put you down. Now next Tuesday I got the biggest match so far, Barry Hart, I will not be an easy competitor, you can bet on dat. T. A. S.: Ya know sumptin', G, wit' all these people gunnin' for us we could get jumped at anytime. So we's going to have to be on the look out at all times. GANGLORD: True. But ya know, all them should be thinkin the same thing! (Camera fades out as both men flash their gang sign.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire is in the interviewing area with the lovely Miss Amber.) WILDFIRE: GANGLORD! You think you're so DAMN good after what you did to Tub-O-Blub? Well, the only thing you proved to me is that you are a COWARD! I can't wait to get you in the ring!! I can't wait to show you that your not the best. And don't underestimate me, 'cause when you play with Wildfire, you're gonna get burned! (Wildfire leaves the room.) AMBER: What ya gonna do when the WILDFIRE runs wild on you?!? (Camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Regulators and Battlecat are in the interview area.) REGULATORS & BATTLECAT: WE CHALLENGE GANGLORD AND TWO PARTNERS OF HIS CHOICE TO A SIX MAN TAG MATCH, WE DON'T CARE WHO IT IS, BECAUSE WE ARE GONNA TEACH YOU, GANGLORD, NOT TO HURT OUR FRIENDS. TUB-O BLUB, WE ARE DEDICATING THIS MTACH TO YOU AND GET WELL SOON! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR (to off-camera): Why the heck are they shouting? And why are they speaking in unison? (Johnny notices that he's back on. STARR (to camera): Oh... sorry. Anyway, finally, next week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, as we found out yesterday, will now feature as its main event a triangle match in a cage for the CWO Television Championship, now held by Miguel Sanchez. The other two competitors will be the former champion, Brent "The Ace" Alles, and a man who claims he should've been the champion all along, Johnny Reb. We go to comments from Johnny Reb and General Lee. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb appears with General Lee behind a cage set up in the interview area.) JOHNNY REB: Well, two was company, and now three's a crowd. But that don't bother ol' Johnny Reb much. Tell 'em, General.
