REPORT 9/13/97

13 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) CWO REPORT: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, September 13, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr, Mitch E. Washey is on vacation. Last night the CWO was in Baltimore, MD, let's see what went down at that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 9/12/97 Baltimore, MD Painmaster defeated Wrath by disqualification. Time of match: 5:18. Lust hit Painmaster with her whip, causing the DQ. Nation of Denomination defeated Mason-Dixon when Amish Farmer pinned Beauregard Dixon after the falling splash. Time of match: 13:32. Mondo Gecko pinned Benjamin Blake Bentley after the reversed inside cradle. Time of match: 8:55. Deadly Sins defeated Pyro-Technics when Pride pinned C-4 after the Repention. Time of match: 24:30. Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned Vegas High Roller after the Flash Flood. Time of match: 12:57. Heat and Coldfront defeated The Law when 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat pinned Jermain Justice after the Jimmyplex. Time of match: 18:44. Lord Hile Troy pinned World Champion Johnny Reb in a non-title match after the small package, pulling the tights, following an attack by Gold Kard. Time of match: 9:40. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also last night was FIGHTIN' FRIDAY NIGHT, in which Joe Root and David "The Truth" Thomas "took over" the show. Let's see what went down on our FIGHTIN' FLASHBACK. _______________________________________________________________________________ FIGHTIN' FLASHBACK 9/12/97 MATCH ONE: "Monster" Rich Clark defeated Sandman Jr. by disqualification in 7:11 when North American Champion Masher Maccabee attacked Clark with the North American Championship belt. Maccabee continued to attack Clark after the bell, but was then caught in a chokehold and choke slammed to the mat. Interview with Brent "The Ace" Alles, who states that his personal business is nobody else's business, and also his plans for Lord Hile Troy at SEPTEMBER SLAM II: REVENGE IS THE NAME OF THE GAME on September 28, 1997, in San Antonio, TX. MATCH TWO: Light Heavyweight Champion Jason "Flash" Varga and Joe Eagle defeated Holocaust and Red Raven when Varga pinned Raven in 17:06 after the Flash Flood flying clothesline. After the match all four men were attacked by Gold Kard International (Gold Kard, Lord Hile Troy, Moses, and Benjamin Blake Bentley). MATCH THREE: The referee stopped the match in 13:19 when World Champion Johnny Reb had Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil, in the Texas Cloverleaf. This came after a vicious attack by Painmaster on Fergus, in which a steel chair was literally bent over Fergus' head, busting him wide open. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight, we are in Washington, D. C., let's look at the lineup for that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 13, 1997 WASHINGTON, D. C. "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat (w/Tommy Bomber and the Coldfront) vs. Pride (w/the Deadly Sins) Sandman Jr. vs. Moses Vegas High Roller vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Lord Hile Troy Gold Kard vs. Holocaust NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Big Cool (champions) vs. Nation of Denomination WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Joe Eagle vs. Johnny Reb _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with Television Champion The Focus, who gets a chance at SEPTEMBER SLAM II: REVENGE IS THE NAME OF THE GAME to eliminate his most hated enemy Benjamin Blake Bentley from the CWO, once and for all. Of course, if he loses, not only does he lose the Television Championship but he will also be out of the CWO. Let's hear from The Focus. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The camera shows The Focus in the far left corner of a bright white room. He is facing the wall.) THE FOCUS: Hmmm... it's funny. Benjamin Blake Bentley... has the time come for me to drive you out of the CWO? Has it really come this far? I remember our first encounter to this day. You beat me, because you cheated. (The Focus walks over to the right hand corner, crosses his arms, and faces towards the camera.) THE FOCUS: At first, I was a bit confused. I didn't know whether or not to retaliate, or to stick by my morals, and just keep everything legal. Well, I decide to change my morals, and use whatever it takes to win my matches. Key in point, my most recent match against triple B in Andover. Ha! I win by pulling your tights. It came full circle. Well, at the next pay per view, Benjamin, things will come full circle for the final time. I will win, and I will send you out of the CWO. This television championship drives me. (The Focus walks back to the left hand corner, and sits Indian Style facing the wall again, beginning a meditative state.) THE FOCUS: Rage drives me. And my rage will drive you out, Benjamin... out forever. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Also on September 28, in San Antonio, TX, will be a "Texas Twister" match between Diamondback Chris Myers and Sandman Jr. against the Reverend and his dark minion Sandman. The rules for this match are simple: there are none. All four men will fight at the same time, falls count anywhere in the building, and there are definitely no countouts or disqualifications. This one is going to be a wild one, folks, and it all stems from the Reverend and Sandman's apparent desire to eliminate both Myers and Sandman Jr. from the CWO. Let's hear now from the Diamondback. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Diamondback Chris Myers is shown on a basketball court in Phoenix. The moon is up and shining off the nearby desert sand. Myers is in a black denim shirt and blue jeans and the wraparound shades. In his hand is that KOHO hockey stick, and at his feet are three orange balls.) DIAMONDBACK CHRIS MYERS: They say every now and then, a man looks fear right in the eye, and he doesn't flinch. Well, in two weeks, I'm going to be looking fear, agony, misery, and dispair right into two black hourglass eyes and a dark hooded face, and I'll be damned if I am going to blink. (Myers brings the hockey stick down to one of the ball, lines up, and brings the stick back over his shoulder, holding it their for a split second before hitting the ball with a loud CRACK! The ball goes flying off into the distance.) MYERS: Sandman, you do not concern me. REVEREND, though, you should be worried. Very worried. Because of you, I can't see without these damn shades, even at night. You know what it's like to go from 20/20 vision to being damn near blind? (Myers takes another swing, sending another ball into the night.) MYERS: Reverend, you have pushed me to an edge. I've looked over that edge, and don't happen to like what I see. So, I'm coming back from that edge, and guess what? The way back leads through you. So, on September 24th, in San Antonio, I'm coming at you with everything I got. And I'm not going to spout anything about taking your soul or eyesight or some garbage. What I am going to do is walk in there with Sandman Jr., and we're going to clean up some trash in CWO. (Myers hits the third ball over a nearby fence as the camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's it for the REPORT today, we'll see you tomorrow.
