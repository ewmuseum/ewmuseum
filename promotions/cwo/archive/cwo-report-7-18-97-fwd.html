CWO REPORT 7/18/97 (fwd)

18 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
> (Camera opens with clips from last night's FEVER. Joe Eagle >confronting Wayne Schillo, GKI argueing with Joe Eagle and his friends, and >Sandman and the Robed Figure attacking Diamondback Chris Myers.) > > MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to a long awaited edition of the >CWO REPORT. FEVER was held last night in Orlando, Florida and let's look at >the results: > >THURSDAY NIGHT FEVER: >Orlando, FL 7/17/97 > >Show opens with closing moments from CWO Rocking Independence Day > >Interview with Wayne Schillo. Moments after the interview began, Jason >"Flash" Varga and Joe Eagle came to the ring to confront Schillo about the >developments that occured at the PPV. Brian "The Hammer" Williams came down >to the ring to seperate the two men. Schillo offered no explanation for the >recent happenings concerning Ninja Waynitsu and left the ring. > >Lord Hile Troy defeated Exodus in 11:53 following a rollup. In the closing >seconds of the match, Exodus punched Gold Kard, who was standing on the ring >apron. The distraction was enough for Troy to sneak up and get the victory. > >Interview with Diamondback Chris Myers. During the interview, Benjamin >Blake Bentley walked out. Using a can of hairspray, he blinded Myers and >delivered 3 Nobility finishers, one in the ring and two on the concrete. >Myers' manager, Dallas, grabbed a chair and smashed Bentley with it, >knocking him out. > >Interview with the Mercenaries. > >CWO North American Tag Team Champions, the Mercenaries, defeated the team of >X-Factor and Marc Mission in 5:03 after Krunch pinned X-Factor following the >Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. > >The Focus defeated Jason "Flash" Varga by countout in 10:09. During the >match, Ninja Waynitsu came down to the ring and attacked Varga. Varga and >Waynitsu fought back to the back area, where members of GKI attacked Varga. >Meanwhile, in the ring, Focus told GKI referee, Spencer Doloff, to stop the >count. Doloff refused, signalling for the bell after the mandatory 10 >count. Focus attacked the ref, executing the Focal Point. > >"Gigolo" Jimmy Heat pinned Mustafa Rahim following a dropkick, and using his >feet on the ropes for leverage. Prior to the match, Rahim announced he was >dropping the Panther gimmick. After the match, Heat was attacked by Brother >Justice and Steel. Rahim challenged the Deadly Sins to a match at the >upcoming Team Challenge Series. > >Interview with GKI. During the interview, Mondo Gecko, Joe Eagle and Johnny >Reb appear and state that if GKI refuses to sign a match with them at the >upcoming Team Challenge Series, then the CWO wrestlers will strike against >GKI, crippling the federation. > >The cage match that was supposed to be Bishop and Dark Raven vs. Vlad the >Impaler and Serpentmaster never happened. Bishop and Raven attacked Vlad >and Serpentmaster in the locker room area. The two teams fought all the way >down to the ring, with Raven and Impaler using everything that they could >get their hands on to beat on Vlad and Serpentmaster. In one of the most >heinous acts in CWO history, Bishop threw fireballs at both Vlad and >Serpentmaster, who were completely engulfed in flames. > >Interview with Monster Rich Clark. > >CWO Television Champion Diamondback Chris Myers pinned the Sandman in 10:23 >following a small package after Sandman went for the Sandblast. Sandman >attacked Myers after the match, executing a pair of Sandblasts. Dallas ran >back to the locker rooms for help, and Sandman Jr. ran down to the ring with >his Singapore cane. Breaking the cane across the back of Sandman had no >effect, and Sandman Jr. was chokeslammed. A robed figure then appeared, and >locked Myers into some kind of surfboard submission hold as the program went >off the air. > > WASHEY: Serious developments taking place at last night's FEVER. >It promises to heat up even more with the House Shows that have been >scheduled for this week. The CWO will make some far off apperances >overseas, with the first show scheduled for tonight in Papua, New Guinea. >Let's look at the schedule for that card. > >CWO HOUSE SHOW >Papua, New Guinea 7/18/97 > >The Regulators vs. Brother Justice > >El Grande Pistolerro vs. Matsuhiro Torunaka > >SUBMISSION MATCH >Johnny Reb vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley > >LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP >TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS MATCH >Mondo Gecko (Champion) vs. Moses (challenger) > >CWO WORLD TITLE MATCH >Gold Kard (Champion) vs. The Focus (challenger) > > > WASHEY: Let's get to the interviews. First off, we hear comments >from the Focus, made prior to last night's FEVER. >____________________________________________________________________________ > > Look, this goes out to everybody in the CWO, and the GKI. If I >say that I want something done my way...I mean it. That includes >stopping a ten count. If I want to win by count out, I'd just pummel my >opponent with a chair, as long as no bodyguards were around, roll into >the ring, and sit until the count was over. I could probably win every >match that way, but who cares. Every match I learn something new about >myself, and my opponent learns exactly why he should have never stepped >into the ring with me. Flash, if you want a rematch, simply ask. Other >than that...I couldn't care less about you. Finally, Chris Myers...what >an interesting character indeed. Do you want to put the title on the >line against me? Do you feel that I deserve the shot? I certainly do, >so I will try my best to make it so. No more sitting around for me, it's >time to get the job done. Period >__________________________________________________________________________ > > WASHEY: We will now hear comments from Cold Front, one of the tag >team fixtures in the CWO. >__________________________________________________________________________ > >(Scene opens with Steve White, minus his usual smile, and Kurt Gerstung >standing in the CWO studios in front of a matte with their name on it.) > >STEVE WHITE: As you can probably see, my friend and I aren't in the best >of moods......why should we be. We've been on one Hell of a losing streak >for what seems like half a year now. But all that's about to change, and >we're going to start rebuilding with a win over one of the top-ranked teams >in the CWO. Regulators.......prepare to suffer an unprecedented loss this >week.......for we aim to win by hook or by crook. You're loss will signal >the return of the superiority of the ColdFront which was present in the >first few months of the CWO, and lacking lately. We've taken time off, >regained what we lost, and are prepared to claim the belts that are >rightfully ours. > >KURT GERSTUNG: Right on. Regulators! You may claim to be one of the true >talents in tag-wrestling today, but we'll be more than happy to prove you >wrong! Ya see, the speed and the skill (points to Steve) are back thanks >to some straight out planning, training, and the rebuilding of our styles >from the ground up! Prepare to see the rafters, cuz once the 'Icebreaker' >hits, that and black is *all* yer gonna see! > >WHITE: Preach on brother Kurt......Now, don't get us wrong Regulators. We >respect you as athletes, and a team. It's just that we're far superior >than you, and are willing to prove it. You won't be the last team that's >going to fall in our path. So we're not picking on you. If you hate us, >fine, if you don't fine. We couldn't care less right now....that's what >got us in the red int he first place...and as my friend here would >say......'The Icemen Cometh, and Kicketh some butt along the way!' > >(Scene fades out to chuckling) >_____________________________________________________________________________ > > WASHEY: We now will hear from former CWO Television Champion >Benjamin Blake Bentley. Please keep in mind that his comments were recorded >prior to his attack on Chris Myers last night on FEVER. >_____________________________________________________________________________ > >(Scene opens with Bentley in the CWO studios) > >BBB: I'm going to make this short and simple. Meyers, due to your >cheating, and obvious partisan influence on Meyering, you got a pin on me. >I will not let this pass easily. I will be a thorn in your side until the >belt is back in the glorious hands of GKI, and my dignity is regained. > As for my upcoming match with the Focus, I couldn't care less. I can take >you, Focus, blindfolded, and I have much bigger proverbial fish to fry. > >(Fadeout) >_____________________________________________________________________ > > WASHEY: Now we will hear from 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat, who came out >with a win over Mustafa Rahim, formerly known as Panther, last night on FEVER. >_____________________________________________________________________ > >(Scene opens with a montage of the 'Gigolo' ,Jimmy Heat, hitting on women >in a nightclub. By the end of the cut segments he has 4 girls around his >arms.) > >HEAT: I told em I'd do it! I told em! Not only did I beat that kitty >raheem-mustafa-kama-moleki-ahmed-shabaz, but I showed my adoring fans that >it's gonna be great thanks to the fact that I'm already a wanted man, and >it's only my FIRST day in the CWO! Man! If rafiki-jamal-rahim is gonna >play the race card, an' try an call me honky....LET HIM! > > Looksee here Raheem! Not only do I got the win, the pin, and the fin >(flashes cash taken from his win), but I got *all* the chicks fit to hang >with the 'Gigolo'! Not too bad for a fishbellied cracker like myself, eh? > > I'm not rubbing any of this in your face, I mean....you, a veteran of the >CWO, probably get beaten routinely by newcomers. (smiles) It's just that >I'm getting my point across for a lil' something I want next week. And it >doesn't even concern you, but it can if you want it too. I want a piece of >Steel. That goonie bastard belted me with a chain, and unless there's a >neked lady at the other end, I AIN'T GONNA TAKE IT! I'm gonna whip ya >Steel, in the next best fashion to a chain! Can ya say 'leather strap' my >goonie bastard fiend? I knew you could. I'm gonna whip that cowhide >across your back, Jimmyplex your beaten carcass, and drag ya to a 1, 2, 3, >4 victory! DAMN! I'm just too good! Ain't that right ladies?! > >LADIES: (together) That's right Jimmy! > >HEAT: Ya see? I ain't alone! Everyone thinks I'm great! Steel, a word >to the wise, keep Kamal-Raheem-Jamima away from the ring, cuz I'm too >talented to piddle with the likes of ya, and talent, my brother, makes for >strange bedfellows.....looking for a meaning there? Well, just keep it in >mind. Hah! Catch ya later my ebony brother! Word up, wigga! > >(Scene fades out to the laughter of Heat, the ladies, and the buzz of dance >music in the background) >______________________________________________________________________ > > WASHEY: Finally today, we will hear from the CWO's North American >Tag Team Champions, the Mercenaries. >______________________________________________________________________ > >KAPTAIN KRUNCH: X-Factor and Marc Mission. Just who are the two of you? > >KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: And what in the hell were the two of you doing in the >ring fighting us? You had no business being in that ring tonight with the >greatest tag team in the CWO. > >KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Oh well. They paid for it. Now what's next? > >KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Who knows? Hey, did you see Gold Kard spouting off >again? Maybe we didn't hit him in the head hard enough. We'll tell you >what, you saurkraut-eating goon. Why don't you get your Vegas loser and >that Torunkaka guy and put them in the ring with us? We have a little >payback to take out on them. > >KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Let's make it for the next FEVER, why don't we? That is, >if you have the guts. > >KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: In the meantime, you can tell them just how it felt to >be picked up eight feet in the air, clotheslined, turned inside out and >falling into a heap in the middle of the ring by the one and only, TEAM THAT >RULES THE WORLD!!! >_______________________________________________________________________ > > WASHEY: As if Gold Kard and the GKI didn't have enough to deal with >from the CWO wrestlers, as we saw last night, but now they have a direct >challenge thrown at them. Will they accept? We'll have to find out later, >because that's all the time we have for today. So long everybody! > > (fade to closing credits.) > > >
