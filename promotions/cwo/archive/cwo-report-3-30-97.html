30 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, March 30, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT concluded with a series of exciting matches. Let's look at what went down last night in Grand Rapids, MI! _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT FINALS GRAND RAPIDS, MI - 3/29/97 Johnny Reb pinned Masher Maccabee after the missed Homentaschen Hangover. Time of match: 12:18. Night Slinger pinned The Focus after the Lights Out avalanche. Time of match: 9:54. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned CWO Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 20:51. Brad 'The Man' Couchman pinned Joe Eagle after the Piledriver. Time of match: 21:39. Night Slinger pinned Johnny Reb after the Lights Out and outside interference by Gold Kard. Time of match: 13:33. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Brad 'The Man' Couchman after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 11:06. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Night Slinger after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 8:17. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's right, Brian "The Hammer" Williams is the first ever CWO MARCH MADNESS CHAMPION, and he remains undefeated here in the CWO. He now takes that undefeated streak into TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, where he has been granted a World title shot against Bane! Will Williams be able to capitalize on his recent good fortune, or will Bane be able to put him down for the first time? STARR: Don't forget about the great SHOWCASE coming out later today, it should be a great one, as we are a week away from the next chapter in CWO PPV excitement, APRIL ARMAGEDDON, featuring the return of THE ROCK. However, for two gentlemen, their minds are on another piece of steel in the form of a ladder, Mondo Gecko and Masher Maccabee, colliding in a rematch for the Television title. Let's get comments from the champion, Mondo Gecko. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the CWO School of Hard Knocks training center. A man is training with a wrestler in a head gear. Gecko is watching with approval.) MONDO GECKO: Well, well, well, Dave Thomas, or Mr. Chicken McNuggets himsel,f has decided to manage and beat up me and Tamara. Well, I have news for everybody, first I want to unveil my first big surprise and that is the person who will be in my corner, lets go in the ring for my manager's training. (The headgeared manager executes some karate kicks and arm throws on the bigger opponent, sending him to his back. Then from out of nowhere we see the smaller wrestler execute a perfect spin kick followed by a MOONSAULT! The wrestler takes off the headgear and reveals that the wrestler is TAMARA WILCOX!) MONDO GECKO: Well, whaddaya think about that, Wendy's Thomas, this guy she just beat up is about 245 lbs and you are about 250, give or take an extra value meal or two. So now, all of a sudden it doesn't seem so easy for you to be a nuisance for me. TAMARA WILCOX: Hey, Thomas, I saw your new movie last night, you know, the movie of your life story, "LIAR, LIAR". You have had a lot to say about me and I am sick of it. First of all you have had a lot of sexual innuendos about me, when it is you who hit on me and were jealous of me being a better broadcaster than you. So now you say you're gonna love watching me and Mondo get punked, well Thomas, it is going to be you and Masher getting punked when me and Mondo gets Mondo Extreme with you two. Mondo, should we reveal the other one? MONDO GECKO: I DON'T THINK SO! THOMAS AND MASHER, GET READY FOR AN ALL OUT WAR, BOYS, 'CAUSE I AM GONNA GET YA, DIG IT! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's it for the REPORT today, we'll see you tomorrow.
