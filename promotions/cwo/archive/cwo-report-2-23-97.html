CWO REPORT 2/23/97

23 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens up on the members of DOOMSDAY prior to Saturday Night's house show. The Mercenaries flank Maximum Overdrive and Baby Waldo stands in back of Overdrive. Waldo and the Mercenaries are holding their CWO World 6 Man Tag Titles.) MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: Tonight the team of DOOMSDAY will be very busy as we have a lot to accomplish. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: First, me and my partner get to take out the Wild Ones. One of the many pathetic tag teams in this federation. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Last night, everybody saw us destroy the Alliance. As if they stood a chance. We're showing the CWO ranking committee that they have made a serious oversight in ranking all of these second-rate teams above us. We're taking them out one by one. OVERDRIVE: I step into the ring with the man who only two days ago was talking from a hospital bed. I hope you're ready to go back to it, because that's exactly where you're going to be headed after I kick your butt. Get ready to taste the Pileup for the second time. KRUNCH: Now on to other things...... PUNISHMENT: Like this Unholy Alliance garbage. OVERDRIVE: Let's see what we got here. Dark Raven. The guy that got kicked out of MOD's camp because he was such a loser. Next we got a jerk named Vlad The Impaler. KRUNCH: I have news for you chump. Halloween was over months ago. The only nightmares anybody should be having is you when you look into a mirror. OVERDRIVE: You say you've set your sights on Waldo? I'm sure he'd be more than happy to bust every bone in your body. Now we have another guy named the Bishop. What this guy's about, I'm not sure, but then again, I really don't care. You've allied yourself with the wrong guy as you'll soon find out. And last but not least, a guy who underwent a makeover and now calls himself the Serpentmaster. Look freak, the Zoo's just down the street. Why don't you do yourself a favor and hang out at the reptile house? It'll be much safer than this federation. KRUNCH: You four are going to find out Tuesday night exactly why we hold these belts, and why we're one of the most feared groups in this league. DOOMSDAY's gonna come calling, and it's going to be all over for each and every one of you. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Johnny Reb, you are one of the top lightweights around here and it's gonna be a good old fashioned fight and that is what it is all about. Reb, let's get one thing straight, I am for real and I am ready to rock your world, baby! I set to collect gold and that is either the TV title or the Light Heavyweight title. Reb, you might be Southern Lightning but I am the West Coast thriller and I am ready to get Mondo Extreme! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Sunday, February 23, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Little Rock, AK, former home of our esteemed president, and "Bubba" would have been proud at the action that went on last night. Let's check it out! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS Saturday, 2/22/97 Little Rock, AK Regulator Ice pinned Dark Raven after the Choke Slam and outside interference by Master of Darkness Damion and Hellhound. Time of match: 8:23. Mercenaries defeated Wild Ones when Korperal Punishment pinned Battlecat after the small package. Time of match: 1:39. Before the match Mondo Gecko and his bodyguard Cannonball Grizzly were viciously attacked by Masher Maccabee, leaving Battlecat to fight the Mercenaries alone. Ganglord pinned Chris Greco after the neckbreaker. Time of match: 4:40. Exile pinned Wildfire after the End of the World. Time of match: 9:22. Ganglord attacked Wildfire outside the ring before the End of the World moonsault - thus aiding in the victory. Gold Kard pinned Serpentmaster after the rollup. Time of match: 16:20. Maximum Overdrive pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Pileup power bomb. Time of match: 13:40. Doomsday came down with Maximum Overdrive for the match. Highlanders defeated Biblical Powers when Conor McCloud pinned Moses after the armbar takeover reversal. Time of match: 10:03. Johnny Reb defeated Mondo Gecko when the ref stopped the match when Gecko was in the Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 9:59. CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE (ELIMINATION MATCH) World Tag Team Champions the Creatures of the Night defeated North American Tag Team Champions Bad Guys when Night Slinger pinned Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson after the Lights Out. Night Crawler eliminated Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker in 14: 10. Night Slinger eliminated Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson in 25: 30. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (NON-TITLE): Goliath defeated champion Wayne 'Keuka Guy' Schillo by disqualification when "Monster" Rich Clark attacked Goliath. "Monster" Rich Clark also choke-slammed Schillo, leading Doomsday (who were around the ring, "protecting" Schillo) to make the save. Time of match: 5:59. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Don't forget our great SHOWCASE card, coming out later today, let's look at the lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ SHOWCASE 2/23/97 Tommy Lightning (debut) vs. Sin (last match in CWO) Panther vs. Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Talon Johnny Reb vs. David "Destroyer" Davis Focus vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (REMATCH): Moses (Challenger) vs. Tiger Rogers (Champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: As you saw by the report from Little Rock, AK, CWO Television Champion Masher Maccabee continues his assault on Mondo Gecko. We take you now to comments from Maccabee. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Masher is sitting in a park in Crown Heights, while Rabbi Solomon throws food at the pidgeons.) MASHER MACCABEE: Mondo Gecko... sounds like silly putty, doesn't it? You bring back P. N. News? That's just what this league needs, another piece of trash just like you. You blame Rabbi Solomon for losing your belt? A man of the faith? A harmless old man, only there for spiritual guidance? You are a sore loser and a fool. Look at me, and what do you see... the future of the CWO! I am a genetic wonder, and no one will take this belt from me, no one! Gecko, you cartoon character, I'm going to bar mitzvah you in the ring and make a man out of you! All it takes from your retarded brain is one mistake and... BANG! The Hangover. Iron Man Match? How 'bout throwing it in a Steel Cage, so there is no interference? Maybe even taped fist again? I want to make this a war, "Mondo" and I want you to bleed. Come and get my precious 16 pounds of gold! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: CWO officials have announced that the final match between Mondo Gecko and Masher Maccabee will indeed be a true Iron Man contest, with iron bars around them, their fists taped, 30 minutes to see who can score the most victories. Of course, in the cage, there will be no disqualifications, no countouts, it will be a true test of each man's endurance. Leading up to that, though, Mondo Gecko keeps his grueling schedule, including a shot at the Light Heavyweight Championship next week on SHOWCASE. Let's hear from Mondo Gecko. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Tiger Rogers, the last time we meet you got cannonballed right in the middle of the ring and next week I will upset you for your Light Heavyweight title. Tiger Rogers, as I said, I will beat you and I will also unveil a new manager for the Wild Bunch. I came from the streets of San Francisco and I had to scrap and hustle for food and money everyday of my life. I have put myself through college, wrestling school, and the Japan & Mexican leagues. Tiger Rogers, with my NEW MANAGER in my corner who I will introduce at the match you will experience EXTREME pain when MONDO EXTREME runs all over you, DIG IT! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Yesterday on the REPORT, Panther made a very aggresive challenge to Johnny Reb, stating that Reb still had his "dues to pay." Today, we have a response from Reb himself. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb appears in the interview area.) JOHNNY REB: Panthuh, y'all talk about payin' dues... well son, ah've paid mah dues. Ah've been through the wars. Ah've given these fans what they have wanted. And what they want, Panthuh, much as y'all lahk ta think different, is fo' their man ta get it done in the ring. The rest takes care of itself. See, because if you ain't gettin' in done in the ring, well, nobody wants your damn autograph. Nobody calls linin' y'all up to visit the schools. Y'all wanna do the fans a favor, y'all get it done between the ropes. Y'all wanna have a showdown with me? Well, come an' get some Panthuh. See, y'all don't wanna face facts. Y'all don't wanna stop livin' in yo' fantasy world. Well sign the line my friend, and watch the fantasy come to a screechin' halt. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Finally today, Brent "The Ace" Alles returned to action last night in Little Rock after a brief stay in the hospital following Doomsday's vicious attack on him Tuesday night. However, "The Ace" took some time out to do a "special report" for us, we take you to that footage now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is shown walking down a Detroit street, wearing jeans, Timberland boots, and his trademark Syracuse jacket. A cold wind ruffles Alles' short-cropped hair.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: Since I've got some time on my hands, I figured I'd do a little research for the CWO and visit the old neighborhood of CWO "superstar" Maximum Overdrive here in Detroit, MI, only about 3 hours away from my home in Michigan, on the better side of the state, Western Michigan, Grand Rapids to be exact. What I found was quite shocking and of course I wanted to share it with the fans of the CWO. (Alles is shown walking into a barbershop. An eldery man, bald with glasses, greets him hello and shakes his hand.) ALLES (showing him a picture): I was wondering, do you remember this kid from the old days? BARBER: I sure do. Real tall, gangly kid, always running in here to keep from getting beat up by the young toughs in the area. I always wondered why he was so afraid, being as big as he was, but I paid it no mind. (Alles is next shown going into a local grocery store, where he chooses to talk to a cashier with a hopelessly out-of-fashion red bouffant hairdo.) ALLES (showing her the picture): I was wondering, ma'am, do you remember this guy? CASHIER: I sure do. Always running in here trying to get away from the young toughs in the area. He would put a grocery bag over his head and hide in the corner, trying to make it like you couldn't see him. Well, he's almost 7' tall, for crying out loud! How you gonna miss him? (Alles is next seen entering Overdrive's old high school. He enters the office of the school principal.) ALLES: I was wondering, Mr. James, what kind of student was this kid, Maximum Overdrive? MR. JAMES: Well, we were glad to see that he entered wrestling, since we didn't think there was much else he could do with his life. Too bad he can't even succeed in the field he's in. As I remember, he always sat in class and played with a big ball of dryer lint. Strangest damn thing. (Alles is seen back on the street that he started from.) ALLES: So there you have it, an indepth look at the history of one of our "top" superstars, Maximum Overdrive. I know I was truly enriched by the experience, how about you? (Alles smirks as the camera fades.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. Don't forget to watch SHOWCASE, coming out later today, and we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
