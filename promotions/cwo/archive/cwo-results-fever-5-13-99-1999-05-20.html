Results - FEVER 5/13/99

May 20, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FEVER 5/13/99 Washington, DC (MCI Center) HOUR #1 Johnny Reb was shown tied up in a darkened room. Darkness kicked the show off, discussing the current state of affairs in the
CWO, especially the presence of his twin brother, Maximum Overdrive.
Darkness stated that the Fallen would take care of Maximum Overdrive this very night. They then brought out a captured Johnny Reb, and tried to hang him above the ring! Eddie Fantastic came out, and Darkness demanded a rematch between his Creatures and Reb and Fantastic at MAY-HEM, in a cage, in order to save Reb. Fantastic acquiesced, but Darkness proved he was the prince of lies, ordering the Dark Side to hang Reb anyway! Fantastic then tried to make the save, but was beaten down. (Foolkiller couldn't make the save, as he hadn't arrived at the arena yet due to... travel problems.) Of all people, Diamondback Chris Myers and "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum then came down to make the save, clearing the ring of the Dark Side. In exchange for that, they demanded a World Tag Team Title match, and after a little hesitation, they got it.
A mock interview of Man Mountain Marko was played (Marko was played by Wayne
Schillo in the parody).
'Playboy' Wayne Schillo defeated Brian 'The King' Derks by disqualification when Man Mountain Marko attacked.
Wack Pack (Calvin and Southern Dynamite) defeated Mondo Gecko/Black Jack
Hollister when Southern Dynamite pinned Mondo Gecko after the Hobbes'
Revenge and outside interference by Kent Alles. Calvin also used the ropes for leverage. Deliverance is coming? Brent 'The Ace' Alles defeated Hollywood Haskins by disqualification when
Iceman X attacked.
Maximum Overdrive pinned Fallen after the Pile-Up. HOUR #2 Strictly Business was shown in a meeting with their fan club, three members strong. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE MATCH: Strictly Business defeated Bad Company (Ewiak and Taggart) when Mr. Eagle pinned Col. Taggart after the Eagle Shock (tiger driver). (Ewiak was handcuffed to the ringpost by Kent Alles and couldn't make the save.)
Strictly Business retained the CWO North American Tag Team Champions title. Also, Bad Company has to break up forever. After the match was over, Hardcore Sensation appeared on the Jumbotron, and blew up Joe Eagle's car! Cris Marcus cut a promo on his match with Simon St. Severe that night. "The Ultimate Athlete" Cris Marcus pinned Simon St. Severe after The X and outside interference by Smilin' Doc Watson, Marcus also pulling the tights for good measure.
Wildfire did an in-ring interview, but was brutally attacked by his former
Men of Respect partner, Union Caine (now of the True Americans)!
NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE MATCH (HIGHLANDER MATCH): Similar to a Last Man Standing match, champion Sandman Jr. and Fergus McTavish battled in a brutal match until both were attacked by Black Jack
Hollister. After Hollister's brutal attack, neither man could make the ten count, and the match was ruled a draw. Sandman Jr. therefore retained the title.
WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE MATCH: Diamondback Chris Myers/'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum and Reb and Fantastic wrestled to a no contest when the Dark Side attacked both men. Time of match: 13:00. Foolkiller finally got to the building and made the save, clearing the ring! Foolkiller then announced to Darkness that at MAY-HEM, he would be in the cage with Reb and Fantastic as well, and if he had the guts, Darkness would get in there too!
