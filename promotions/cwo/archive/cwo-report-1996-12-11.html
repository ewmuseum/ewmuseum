CWO REPORT 12/11/96

11 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Wednesday, December 11, 1996. Tonight, the superstars of the CWO will be in Fort Wayne, IN, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 11, 1996 FORT WAYNE, IN The Biblical Powers vs. Flame Spirits Cake Boy vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart Black Death vs. Exile Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Dark Raven Lafayette vs. Hile Troy CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE (SPIN THE WHEEL, STRAP MATCH): Miguel Sanchez (TV champ) vs. Gold Kard (NA champ) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (FOUR CORNERS OF DEATH, NON-TITLE): "Big" Rich Clark vs. Master Of Darkness Damion (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now take you to comments from CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sin is in a dark room filled with red candles, dimly lit. He stands on a dark red pentagram, evilly cackling.) SIN: So Sanchez, you thought you'd get off that easy, did you? Hmmm, use a little cheating technique, eh? Well, it seems you wont be able to "lay back" for a while, huh? (Laughs wildly) Oh, and don't even think this is over! Oh, no, this is far from over. It may appear that I'm in a "losing" streak... but now with the Deadly Sins alongside me... it may seem that I'll have a little extra power. Sloth and Pride... whenever you need help, I'm right there. You need but ask and you got my assistance. So, SanCheez. you better watch out. 'Cause I'm still not fully satisfied with my return from your cheap move. Watch your dreams carefully. 'Cause you may see me in them very shortly. Oh, and I almost forgot. Who ever is doing the music up in that cracker box. You're not playing my tune. Some fat donut eating slob forgets to play my music correctly. You will feel my wrath if I do not get "I'm your boogie man" played. That is by White Zombie. NOW FIND IT. SANCHEZ, WATCH YOURSELF. DEADLY SINS. YOU GOT YOURSELF a new team mate. (All the candles blow out and you can still hear sin cackling.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the team of DOOMSDAY, standing in their training complex.) MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: Well, well, well. It seems as if we have gotten the attention of GKI with our alliance with the uncrowned CWO World Champion. You had just better watch your mouth, Kard. You've seen what we've done to Sanchez, Sandman Jr., and their big stupid buddy Rich Clark. It'd be a shame to have something like that happen to you... or worse. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: I think you've got your hands full there, Kard. I mean, you actually trust those drooling morons from the Dark Side? Hey, I've got some bridges I'd like to sell you. It looks like DOOMSDAY and Schillo won't be the only guys that you have to worry about. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: It's time you woke up and started living in the now, GKI. This might have been another federation a long time ago, but there's no way that you're going to take it back to your stone-age preferences. There's no way that we're going to let you do it, either. OVERDRIVE: Gold Kard, you're nothing more than some simple shyster that only has the resources of money to keep him happy. It's time the illusion was shattered to the rest of the CWO and you are revealed to be the con-man that you really are. KRUNCH: Hey, how 'bout that Choklat chick? She's pretty hot. PUNISHMENT: Yeah, she'd look pretty good on the couch in the Dorm Room with a beer in her hand and one of those killer dresses crumpled up in the corner. KRUNCH: Schillo's been teaching us how to pick up chicks. Maybe we could try his stuff out on her. (Camera fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens with the camera zooming in on a blank CWO monitor in a bare cement room.) Narrator: They took on all comers in singles competition.... (Screen flashes 'Thrill' thrashing the then seemingly invincible Stealth V.) Narrator: ...or tag.... (Screen flashes Steve White delivering the 'Coldsnap' to Kamikaze.) Narrator: ...they were great champions.... (Screen flashes the Coldfront holding up the CWO tag-belts.) Narrator: ...and they overcame insurmountable odds.... (Screen flashes The 'Coldfront' as some of the last people in a Battle Royale.) Narrator: ...not much has changed.... (A full-screen montage of the Coldfront from their return match against the Gods of Thunder plays to 'More Human Than Human' by White Zombie, topped off by a clip of Kurt Gerstung's Top-rope hurricarana finishing it and the monitor exploding.) Narrator: The 'Coldfront' is back... just in time to heat things up for winter. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Ganglord and T. A. S. are discussing the Scaffold match at CWO CELEBRATION with Wildfire.) T. A. S.: Well, Wildfire, looks like you lost yet another one, huh? Wonder why that keeps happening? Could it be that you messed with the wrong person when you decided to mess wit da G-lord right here by my side? GANDLORD: That would be the reason, see, an angry man does not fight as well as a confident man and Wildfire, you're angry and I... well, I am the image of confidence. I ain't gonna say much more 'bout this because after CELEBRATION, there will be a celebration... MINE! T. A. S.: Oh, and by the way, you best watch your back, because... well, to put it bluntly, if we have it our way, you won't even make it to CELEBRATION, punk! (Camera fades out as both men go back to their planning.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area with Kellen.) SANDMAN JR.: Troy, you don't ever touch my manager... there are some things us fan favorites never do, and that's hit, hell, even touch women... if you EVER do anything like that again, I'll forget that I'm reformed... you've been warned.... KELLEN: And Tamara, who are YOU to call anyone a bitch? You have no talent, no brains, and let's face it, you're ugly and you sag. Stay out of my business, or you'll end up with your face in the dirt. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: I have paid my dues in this federation and now I would like to challenge the light heavyweight champion to a match on the next Showcase. I have paid my dues and worked my way up the ladder. I can beat anybody in this federation and I feel that the fans need a true peoples' champion. Next Showcase it will be an extreme sensation when I win the title and bring it back to the people. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb is in the locker room area. Rebel Yell is tending to General Lee in the background.) JOHNNY REB: Ah knew it was too good ta be true. Ah had him beat. But dammit, ah should'a known. Damion, y'all have promised that ah will never hold gold in this organization... well let me make y'all a promise of mah own. You will be taken out of the Van Andel arena in an ambulance! Y'all saw it tanaht, Damion. Johnny Reb has some friends in low places. So don't y'all worry. If y'all step outta line on Sunday, I'll have some people ta knock back inta it. You have no where ta run Sunday, Damion. Y'all must step between the ropes and look me in the eyes. You cain't. Ah have had your best Damion, an' it ain't been enough. Ah'm still here, and come Sunday y'all will fall to the best finisher in the CWO. Look inta mah eyes, Damion. You know it will end Sunday. Look inta mah eyes.... (Fade to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Regulators are in the interview area, talking about their match against Beauregard Dixon and Jackass Joel at the CWO CELEBRATION SHOWCASE SPECIAL.) REGULATOR ICE: Beauregard Dixon & Jackass Joel, you two will know what the law around here is when you face the Regulators. For a while, Bane, Ganglord, Tiger Rogers, and other rulebreakers have tried to cause harm to the superstars of the CWO. Now we are going to regulate some justice around here. REGULATOR FIRE: Beauregard & Jackass, your time is here to face the REGULATORS. We are going to kick the crap out of you and prove to the federation that we belong in this league and we will do that by regulating justice. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today. We'll see you tomorrow!
