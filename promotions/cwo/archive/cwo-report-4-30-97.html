CWO REPORT 4/30/97

30 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Wednesday, April 30, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night was another great TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, let's take a look at what happened in our FEVER FLASHBACK! _______________________________________________________________________________ FEVER FLASHBACK TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 4/29/97 "LIVE" FROM DENVER, CO DARK MATCH: "Master of Darkness" Damion pinned Matsuhiro Torunaka after the Darkness Falls. Time of match: 10:15. Moses and Savior discuss a mysterious dealing, and Moses also expresses some comments about his current state of affairs in the CWO, including a warning to Gold Kard International and his desire to win MAY-HEM and get the title shot at SUMMER HEAT. MATCH ONE: Moses defeated Centurion in 12:11 after an armbar takeover. In-ring interview with the Coldfront, who talk about how they were unwilling to become heels and are instead rededicating themselves to the sport. They also introduced Matthew "The Man" White, who issued a warning to Alles that he would pay for his actions that may have crippled "Lonestar" Mike Williams for life. MATCH TWO: Attitude Dance (North American champion Jason "Flash" Varga and Joe Eagle) defeated Mason-Dixon in 12:40 after Varga pinned Beauregard Dixon after the Flash Flood. Comments from Diamondback Chris Myers, who will be joining the CWO, debuting at MAY-HEM. MATCH THREE: Ganglord defeated Exile in 13:07, pinning him after a DDT. Hour #2 begins with Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) coming down to the ring and offering Wild Side T-shirts to announcers Kyle Melinn and Joe Root (no shirt was offered to David "The Truth" Thomas). After Melinn said that they were about to have an in-ring interview with the North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys, the Wild Side asked if they could stay and watch. Melinn said sure, and then the Bad Guys came out for their interview, talking about their upcoming match Sunday for the North American titles with the Mercenaries. The Wild Side then attacked the Bad Guys, delivering double chair shots to each member of the team, including Herbie Versmells! Taped comments from El Grande Pistolerro from the McNicholls arena concerning his match with Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil. Humorous segment with McTavish, who can't read his own invitiation to the MAY-HEM battle royal. More comments from El Grande Pistolerro (this time from the locker room in a split-screen) about his match with McTavish tonight. MATCH FOUR: El Grande Pistolerro defeated Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil, in 12:11, pinning him after the Spinning Wheel Kick. MATCH FIVE: Highlanders defeated Brother Justice when Gavin McCloud pinned Jermain Justice after the Quickening (superplex by Gavin, flying splash by Conor). MATCH SIX (TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP): Champion Inferno defeated Maximum Overdrive in 13:06, pinning him after the Inferno's Blaze power bomb. Taped comments from "Monster" Rich Clark about his match for the held-up World title against Bane and Goliath this Sunday at MAY-HEM. In-ring interview with Wayne "College Guy" Schillo and Brian "The Hammer" Williams. The interview was interrupted by Brent "The Ace" Alles, who not only restated his accusation concerning Wayne's conduct towards Amanda Bloom, but also dropped another bombshell, saying that Wayne was kicked out of his own college for alleged credit card fraud. Alles then revealed paintings of Brian Williams, "Lonestar" Mike Williams, Maximum Overdrive, Mondo Gecko, and Schillo. Red X's were drawn over both of the Williamses. Alles said that it was his mission to do what he did to both "Lonestar" and "The Hammer" to the other three men pictured in the paintings. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Definitely a lot to mull over in the next few days, but for now, let's look at the lineup for tonight's House Show in Erie, PA! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW 4/30/97 Erie, PA Ganglord vs. Sandman Jr. Masters of Hardcore vs. Creatures of the Night Mondo Gecko vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Da Hood Rats vs. Regulators TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Masher Maccabee LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Moses vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) Goliath vs. "Monster" Rich Clark Bane vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
