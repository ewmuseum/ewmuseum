CWO REPORT 1/24/97

24 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Bloodbath is in a cemetary with a dim red light shining.) BLOODBATH (low mysterious voice): Well, well! Mondo Gecko! You chose to seal your fate by steping in the ring with Bloodbath! Well, when I'm through with you, then you will REST IN PIECE! I'm here for one reason! REVENGE! Ganglord, Im comming for you! You wont be able to stop me! I have a coffin prepared for you! It's big enough to fit you and that no good piece of garbage manager! I hope you sleep and dream well cause when we get in the ring, it gonna be a living hell! REST IN PIECE! (Bloodbath mysteriousley vanashes as the camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO Control Room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, January 24, 1997. Tomorrow night is TRIPLE THREAT in Chicago, IL, and all of the superstars of the CWO are gearing up for that great event. However, last night the CWO was in Kokomo, IN, let's look at how that card went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 1/23/97 Kokomo, IN WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (street fight, non-title): Bad Guys defeated Creatures of the Night when Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker pinned Night Slinger after the Bad Guys' Bash. Time of match: 16 : 54 Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Johnny Reb after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 12 : 43 Highlanders defeated Pit Bosses when Conor McCloud pinned Pit Boss #2 after the fist to the midsection. Time of match: 13 : 20 Mondo Gecko pinned Bloodbath after the flying elbowdrop. Time of match: 11 : 15 Tiger Rogers pinned Battlecat after the left hook. Time of match: 2 : 10 Tiger Rogers was attacked by Hile Troy after the match. Gold Kard pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the dropkick. Time of match: 10 :0 5 Vincent 'The Lip' Manocelli pinned Sandman Jr. after the Kiss of Death flying forearm. Time of match: 17 :0 6 _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in Aurora, IL, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW 1/24/97 AURORA, IL FRIDAY, JANUARY 24, 1997 AURORA, IL LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Mondo Gecko vs. Moses (champion) Beauregard Dixon vs. Sin Coldfront vs. Blood Inc. Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Steve Keller "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. Tiger Rogers Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Gold Kard NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Pit Bosses vs. Eternal Wanderers (champions) Big Cool vs. Deadly Sins _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We turn our attention first to the situation surrounding the death of CWO superstar Wayne Carpenter. We now take you to the CNN feed about the case shown earlier today. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CNN feed is shown.) CNN ANCHORPERSON: Federal investigators, acting on information provided by security forces at the Cyberspace Wrestling Organization arena, today questioned several wrestlers from the professional wrestling league in connection with the bombing at the arena two weeks ago. Three members of Blood, Incorporated, a self-styled "heel" or villain organization, spent four hours with investigators, but neither authorities nor athletes would comment on the questioning. Meanwhile, in California, Wayne Carpenter, the sole casualty of the bombing, was buried in a private ceremony. Christopher Greco, who performs under the name of Flyboy, and Carpenter's tag team partner Steve Keller, attended the ceremony; however, the fourth member of the Four Aces organization, Clark Gray, did not. "Clark should have been there," Greco said in an interview afterward. "I have no idea where he was. Wayne's parents, Clark's uncle and aunt, were really upset. I don't know what's gotten into him." Authorities would only say that they were "following leads" and that they would be speaking with the remaining Aces. They were quick to point out, however, that none of the Aces were suspects in the blast. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We will keep you posted on this situation as it develops, of course. However, let's now turn our attention to tomorrow night's PPV. Bane, freed of guilt when it was discovered that brief CWO wrestler Scourge was responsible for the notorious "Bane's rampage," now sets his sights on two of his most hated enemies tomorrow night, Hile Troy and Sandman, with the North American title at stake. We take you to comments from Bane. _______________________________________________________________________________ (It is a night time and it is poorly lit outside. Only one image can be seen. It looks as if it was taken right from the movie "Exorcist." Bane is standing under a lamp in the pitch blackness of night. You can only make out his image as he talks. The scene is quite disturbing.) BANE (his voice is deadly and evil sounding, all of hell's fury is heard in it): I am not finished with you yet, CWO. Despite all of your pitiful attemps to rid me, I am back. I forewarned you that nothing can stop the unstoppable. I went as far as to mock your primitive court system. Even they were unable to thwart me. You cannot defeat anarchy. I am the pure embodiment of chaos. Only the strong shall survive, and I am the only strong one in the CWO. I have broken your rules, and have crippled your members. Then you fools, instead of running for your lives, award your misfortune by handing me a meaningless award. You applaud me for destroying your world: pathetic. I can even witness my hand working through the CWO's members. The coward who lit a pipe bomb is only a mock image of myself. A cowardly boy who tried to be a man, tried to be me, by setting of an explosion. Son, whoever you are, you can NEVER be like me. It seems to me that when I was away having fun, people were changing there attitudes towards me. One especially is Sandman. You weak old fool. You felt what it was like to be at the hands of a true man, and you were scared. Now, in your cowardness, you wish to extend your hand to me in companionship. My response is that, unlike you, Bane needs no man. Bane doesn't need to rely on others for help, and I don't need an old feeble man like you standing behind me. In your last ditch effort to save yourself, you did the only option left to you. You tried to escape your Bane by becoming one with it. There is no escape from me, and you will meet your fate this Saturday when I get my hands on you. You see, unlike you, I fear nothing. You, on the other hand, fear this Saturday because you know that you will have to step into the ring with me again for the last time. You will step into the ring because of your foolish pride, and you will then get hurt. You say that you want a title, well I want you. I want you to suffer to a point that you can't even imagine in your dreams, and when you wake up there will be even more anguish when you see your meaningless title in my hands. That also means that I have to go through that frail man known as Hile Troy. This is a minor obstacle. In my entire existence here, I have never once thought about the weakling Troy. He meant nothing to me, exept a name that I was going to punish. Today, he is still only a name, and he will get punished. He fled across the ocean to escape his Bane, but he realized that he could never truly escape it, and now he has come back to face his worst nightmare; me. He considers himself noble, but he will be on his knees in mere hours begging me to stop. To me, Troy, you are a peasant, a pissant. I have no need for you, and your life is none of my concern. I do not care for your titles, but I know that the both of you do. That is why I am going to take them from you. And believe, gentlemen, I take what I want. Just ask the Deadly Sins. One last thing before I depart, Sins.... your whore is under my thumb. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: In the main event, Johnny Reb tries to finally realize his dream, and thwart the "promise" of CWO World Champion Master of Darkness Damion that Reb would never hold gold in the CWO while Damion was here. We take you to comments from Johnny Reb. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb, backed by the Confederate Associates and General Lee, are in the interview area.) JOHNNY REB: Schillo, Damion. It don't really mattuh ta ol' Johnny Reb. Both of you are gonna fall tomorrow naht at Triple Threat. GENERAL LEE: That's raht! Mistuh Schillo, the lahts were out and Johnny Reb had a knock on the head. But ta say that y'all were in the clear, with that goon platoon y'all associate with and what has transpired in the past... well suh, Johnny Reb is a man who can take care of his own affairs. So next time we meet, when Johnny has both of you in his sahts...not gettin' jumped from behind, not flailin' in the dark, you will have to face down your fear. You will have to face down Johnny Reb an' the Texas Clovuhleaf. JOHNNY REB: Damion, fool me once, shame on you. Fool me twice, shame on me. Johnny Reb is nevuh fooled twice an' this saturday naht, i will take the CWO World Heavyweight gold! GENERAL LEE: Good day, suhs. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also involved in a TRIPLE THREAT match, the Creatures of the Night try to extend their impressive title reign, but they have to go through two tough teams to do it, the Biblical Powers and the Deadly Sins. Let's hear from the CWO World Tag Team Champions. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Creatures of the Night in the interview area.) NIGHT SLINGER: Just a couple of days away from the great PPV on Saturday. We shall have an easy time eliminating the Biblical Powers as the Deadly Sins were so right in saying, but Sins, we will also have no problems dealing with the two of you either. NIGHT CRAWLER: Sins, our master hired you to do a job for him a long time ago and the two of you were unable to get it done. Now the time has come for us to prove finally that you guys were the weak link in the team that the master made. We have proved to him that it was not us by winning the CWO World tag belts and then by defeating EVERY team that had the nerve to try and take them away from us. NIGHT SLINGER: Powers and Sins, the Darkness from which we come from is more powerful in 1997 then it was in '96 and that makes for some serious trouble and pain for the four of you. BOTH MEN: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!! (Camera fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Next week on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, we will see the return of one of the most popular tag teams in the CWO, Mason-Dixon. You will remember that the Creatures of the Night were responsible for Mason's injury during a match between both of those teams. Now, Beauregard Dixon has a slight detour into singles action, and he is looking for revenge against a member of the Dark Side, Hellhound, at the SHOWCASE SPECIAL: TRIPLE THREAT on Saturday. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mason-Dixon and General Lee are in the interview area.) BEAUREGARD DIXON: At the Showcase Special, Ah plan ta start the ball rollin' on a big Weekend for the Confederate Associates. Tell 'em, General. GENERAL LEE: Yes suh. This weekend will be the greatest that the Confederate Associates has known! The attack on the Creatures of the Naht will be spearheaded at the Showcase Special by Beauregard hisself. Hellhound, prepare fo' pain. DIXON: An' we ain't forgot about the blueblood neither. Boy, on Showcase, ah will drag y'all down inta the guttuh. Then let's see what y'all can do. LEE: Bentley, Hellhound, be ready. On Showcase, y'all will be getting yo' wakeup calls. Good day, suhs. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Another man currently on the disabled list is popular young superstar Wildfire. The man responsible for his injury is Ganglord, and as he recently resigned with the CWO, you can be sure he's going to be making more trouble, especially for Wildfire and Bloodbath. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera fades in as T. A. S. hangs up the phone and whispers to GangLord.) GANGLORD: They will? That's great... (T. A. S. stops him from talking further) Oh yeah, hello, CWO, that's right, I'm back, the Lord is back in charge here and my first problem is you, Bloodbath, you've been talkin alot of S#!T lately and I don't like it. Wildfire, you knew there was no chance of you even getting close to beating the best and that would be me, the G, so ya had to bring in this big loser Bubblebath to try to stop me, well he didn't do a very good job, did he? You haven't senn the ring in what was it now, 9 WEEKS?!? Hahahahahaha! Well, when you come back, we... um... ah... I will finish the job. T. A. S.: Yeah man, you had to call in reinforcements, now maybe it's our turn to bring in some of da boys from around da way to assist us. Anyway, we need... we'll just have to see, won't we, Campfire and Bubblebath? GANGLORD: Until then, Bath, you watch your back, I have nothing to do the night of Triple Threat, I might have to visit you maybe! (Both men laugh uncontrollably as camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Well, he may have thought he put Wildfire out for good, but Wildfire is looking forward to making his return sometime in the month of February. We recently visited him and the lovely Miss Amber in their home. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire is sitting at home watching the clip of Ganglord injuring him.) WILDFIRE: The first thing I would like to say is thank you! Thank you to all the CWO fans for standing behind me and wishing me a fast recovery! You guys have been great! And rest assured, I WILL BE BACK! Ganglord, this is a warning! I haven't forgotten what you did to me how long ago! When I come back I will stop at nothing to do exactly what you did to me! Count your days, Ganglord, 'cause they're numbered! MISS AMBER: What ya gonna do when the WILDFIRE runs wild on you! (The camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT, and of course at TRIPLE THREAT! See ya!
