CWO REPORT 6/12/97 (fwd)

14 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who are covered in blood and holding the CWO North American Tag Team titles.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Regulators, tonight you saw and felt the power of the Mercenaries. We took all you could dish out and came back for second helpings. And we also took your titles. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: After all, the Mercenaries see something we want, we take it. Besides, what the hell is Randy Johnson going to do about it? NOT A DAMN THING!!! You don't have the guts to put your titles up against a team that you know you can't win against. But I guess that you two morons knew it already, because you didn't want to make it a title match. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: GKI showed us the light on FEVER. It's anarchy rules in the CWO. Survival of the fittest. We proved to you and everybody in GM Stadium that you don't deserve to hold the titles. WE do. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: And if you want these back (holds up title), it's no big deal. You just have to beat us to do it. But just remember one thing, Regulators......you were REGULATED by THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD!!! ________________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens up on a steel plated CWO logo. A small fuse on the bottom corner of the logo is lit and the logo explodes. When the smoke clears, the logo is undamaged, and a montage of CWO superstars rotates around the logo. Fade to the CWO Control Desk and the host, Mitch E. Washey.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. We had a great House Show last night in Vancouver B.C., so let's get to the results of that show. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS GM Stadium, Vancouver B.C. 6/12/97 Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned Matthew "The Man" White after the Allesbreaker, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 13:54. Coldfront defeated Bloody Fangs when 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White pinned Vlad the Impaler after the Coldsnap (free-fall into a shoulderbreaker). Time of match: 20:51. FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH: CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Moses pinned Mondo Gecko in this non-title match that went all over and outside the building, but ended in the ring after a thrust kick by Moses. Time of match: 17:57. The Big Cool defeated Bad Guys by disqualification when the Bad Guys struck the Big Cool with steel chairs in the ring. Time of match: 1:58. The match was short in time but the brawl afterwards was tremendous, as security had to escort both teams out of the building. DETROIT STREET FIGHT: Mercenaries defeated CWO North American Tag Team Champions the Regulators in this wild non-title match when Kaptain Krunch pinned Regulator Ice after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 14:07. After the match, the Mercenaries left with the CWO North American Championships. CWO North American Champion Jason 'Flash' Varga defeated Gold Kard by disqualification in a non-title match when David "The Truth" Thomas and Masher Maccabee attacked. The Tower of Power (Joe Eagle and the Big Cool) came down to make the save. Time of match: 11:16. Vegas High Roller pinned CWO World Champion Joe Eagle in a non-title match after the Vegas Swinger (swinging Samoan drop), which came after Gold Kard struck Eagle with a metal briefcase. Time of match: 15:08. WASHEY: Controversy is running wild in the CWO, with the situation of GKI and now the Mercenaries claiming that they are now the CWO North American Tag Team Champions. It's almost a total state of complete anarchy. Tonight we are in Seattle, WA at the Key Arena. Let's go to the rundown for that card. FRIDAY, JUNE 13, 1997 KEY ARENA, SEATTLE WA The Focus vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Mason-Dixon vs. Moses and Masher Maccabee El Grande Pistolerro vs. Gold Kard Deadly Sins vs. Brother Justice TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Sandman Jr. Wrath vs. Carlos "The Brazilian Lover" Maia TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, LUMBERJACK MATCH): Johnny Reb (w/Mondo Gecko, El Grande Pistolerro, and Mason-Dixon) vs. Matsuhiro Torunaka (w/Gold Kard, David "The Truth" Thomas, Moses, Masher Maccabee) WASHEY: CWO action coming to Seattle. Let's go to comments from some of the stars of the CWO. We're going to start with "Monster" Rich Clark, who has something to say to Gold Kard about the challenge made to him on FEVER. ________________________________________________________________________________ ["Monster" Rich Clark & the masked man are sitting together outside, by a pool, each having a nice cool Bud, just relaxing for a bit. Clark sees the CWO camera crew, and begins to make some comments.] CLARK: Alright, I've been away for quite a while, not really saying much, but it's time to put an end to all of that. I've got quite a bit say and not much time to say it, so here we go. First off, I guess I should congradulate Joe Eagle on obtaining that victory over me at SUMMER HEAT. I should, but I won't. There is ONE word that I can think of to refer to you, Eagle. FLUKE!!! That's it. You beat me once, but there is no way you could do it a second time, and trust me, WHEN I beat Gold Kard, I'll take you on at Rockin' Independance Day, and you better be prepared for the ass whoopin' of your lifetime, 'cause I'm gonna give it to ya! That brings me on to the next subject. Gold Kard, you want me in a match on Tuesday, fine. You want the stipulation to be that if I lose, I never get another World Title shot, that's fine to. But I want you to agree on one thing, if not, all deals are off. If GKI gets involved in the match in ANY capacity, whether the ref sees it or not, GKI is disbanded, never to form again. You got that. I'm bringing my little buddy here down to ringside, and he's already taught me how to beat you. In case you don't know, this man who sits beside me has beaten you before, and will again soon enough. MASKED MAN: Wait a second, I'm back in the CWO? RICH: That's right. Next week on FEVER, you will be in my corner. After that, you'll be wrestling. Congrats to you, friend, you've made it back to the big time. MASKED MAN: YES!!! After spending five months in indie feds, I'm finally back to the CWO. I've honed my skills, and will be ready to kick anyone's ass when I return. Get ready CWO, it's time to get EXTREME!!! RICH: Alright people, that's enough for now, but I'll be back before you know it. Prepare for a three time World Champ, CWO, because you're lookin' at him right now. [he chugs the whole beer and then cracks open another one. He looks very relaxed and comfortable, seemingly ignorant of what's to come.] ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Let's go now to some video we have of the Masters Of Hardcore. ________________________________________________________________________________ [The Masters of Hardcore are coming out of the CWO Towers, and are not looking very happy, when they see the camera, they get downright mean.] "MR. EXTREME" CHRIS OHLHOFF: THAT'S IT!!! I'VE HAD IT WITH THE BULLS#!T POLITICS OF THE CWO!!! I'M OUTTA HERE. YOU WORTHLESS BASTARDS CAN TAKE YOU'RE CONTRACTS AND SHOVE IT. WE DISAPPEARED AFTER THE LAST PAY PER VIEW BECAUSE WE FELT CHEATED, BUT NOW WE'VE BEEN PROVEN RIGHT. IT SAYS RIGHT IN THE RULES THAT IF YOU'RE NOT AT THE RINGSIDE AT THE START OF THE MATCH, YOU'RE NOT ABLE TO OBTAIN A VICTORY. NOW, AT SUMMER HEAT, WE DEFEATED TWO OTHER F*CKING TEAMS, AND WERE AWARDED THE BELTS. THEN THOSE IDIOTS SCOTTSMEN THE HIGHLANDERS COME OUT, FRESH AS F*CKING DAISES, AND BEAT US FOR THE BELTS. NO I'DONT THINK SO. WE WORKED OUR ASSES OFF TO GET THOSE BELTS, ONLY TO HAVE THEM TAKEN AWAY BECAUSE THE CWO DOESN'T LIKE US. WELL, F*CK YOU. WE'RE OUTTA HERE. WE'LL WRESTLE ONE MORE MATCH, AND THAT'S IT. OF COURSE, THAT MATCH WE WANT TO BE AGAINST THE HIGHLANDERS, AND HERE'S THE STIPS. WORLD TAG TEAM TITLES ON THE LINE, COVERED CAGE, NO BRAWLING ALLOWED. EVEN THOUGH WE ARE LEAVING, WE BOTH WANT TO HAVE OUR NAMES GO DOWN AS CWO TAG CHAMPS. WE'RE WILLING TO GIVE UP THE VIOLENCE THAT WE LOVE SO MUCH TO PROVE WHO THE BETTER MEN REALLY ARE. NOW, GET READY, BECAUSE THOSE BELTS ARE OURS. BYE-BYE, CWO, HAVE A GREAT TIME!!! [They both walk off without Couchman ever saying a word.] ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: We apologize for the language used in that last interview. We'd just like to let everybody know that the veiwpoints expressed by Mr. Ohlhoff are not neccessarily those of the CWO REPORT producers. Next up, we have Moses, who shocked the whole federation in the start of a series of bombshells that have rocked the CWO to it's very core. ________________________________________________________________________________ LIVE from GKI Mansion: [Moses is sitting the rec room at the GKI Mansion all by himself, watching Jimmy Swaggert on TV, laughing his ass off. He sees the camera, stops laughing for a second, and then starts up again.] MOSES: (through laughter) HAHAHA!!! I fooled you all. I joined the ranks of GKI almost a month before SUMMER HEAT, but did I ever let it on? Nope. Now, GKI is the most powerful stable in the CWO. The Light-Heavyweight Champ, the future World and TV Champs, and the best assembly of WRESTLERS in the WORLD. I don't care if you're "HARDCORE", I don't care how big you are, no one can OUTWRESTLE ANYONE in GKI, and that's a fact. Now, as for my Light-Heavyweight Belt, I hereby announce that it is the GKI Light-Heavyweight Belt, and will only be defended under MY terms, against opponents that I pick. Now, I see that my "old buddy" Rich Clark is bringing someone back. Well, I choose him to be the first victim on the NEW Moses. Your first match back, you'll be facing me, son. Get ready for the beating of your life, because that's what I'm going to serve you when you come into MY HOUSE. Get ready for the NEW Moses, CWO, because I'm not taking any prisoners anymore. HAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! [He turns back to the TV as the camera goes back into the studio.] ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Hmm. That's an interesting connection. Swaggart and Moses. Oh well. Finally for today, we have Diamondback Chris Myers, who I think expresses the feelings of a lot of CWO members. Let's go to him right now. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Chris Myers and Dallas are backstage, watching the end of FEVER, and the shock of what occurred is on both their faces) Dallas: You know what, Chris? I don't know who this Lord guy is, but I can honestly say I'm surprised. Myers: Yeah. Now I know why Roller accepted my challange! His boss is pretty much running this fed, now! Dallas: Are you saying there's no chance in hell you can beat him? Myers: Did I say that? Dallas: Just checking. (Myers notices the camera) Myers: Good thing you're here. Cameraman: Isn't it always? Well, what are your thoughts about what happened here tonight? Myers: Let's work our way backwards. The whole mess with GKI shouldn't be happening in the first place. I don't know how CWO let this happen. I would like to say it's obviously to the marketing genius of Gold Kard, but that's nonexsistent... Dallas: More like blind luck, if you ask me. Myers: But what's done is done. Hopefully, this is the time for CWO to rally together, although in all honesty, it's probably going to be the Wild Things vs. GKI. Well, there comes a time for everyman to step up and do the right thing. Plus, grab a little bit of the glory. Cameraman: What do you mean? Myers: Let's just say Diamondback Chris Myers just declared war on Gold Kard International! Cameraman: WHAT? Myers: You heard it right! I think the more people to take on these morons, the better! Now, this isn't taking anything away from the Wild Things, but Gold Kard knows how you work. He's watched you in CWO for a long time, and knows how to get inside your mind and screw with you. But...I'm new. I'm a wild card, if you will. I may not be able to win the war by myself, but if I can make it that much harder for GKI to function in this fed, then by all means, let the games commence! Dallas: The war gets underway Tuesday against that greasy moron, the Vegas High Roller! I don't believe he wants to pit finishers, Chris! Myers: I've said before and I'll say it again. The Rattler is simply the best finisher in wrestling today. And you want to pit your Drop against my Rattler? Chummer, I've left 7 people behind me with that move, and you're going to be number 8. Bet on it! ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today on the REPORT! We'll see everybody tomorrow. So long everybody!! (fade to closing credits)
