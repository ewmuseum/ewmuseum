CWO REPORT 3/31/97

31 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Monday, March 31, 1997. Tomorrow night is FEVER, and it should be a big one, as it's right before this weekend when APRIL ARMAGEDDON is scheduled to begin. Let's look at the lineup for tomorrow night's FEVER! _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 4/1/97 OKLAHOMA CITY, OK HOUR #1: Centurion vs. Jason "Flash" Varga Wildfire and Bloodbath vs. Masters of Hardcore (Ohlhoff and Couchman) Johnny Reb vs. Masher Maccabee HOUR #2: TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: The Focus vs. Mondo Gecko (champion) "Lonestar" Mike Williams/Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung vs. Highlanders WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Bane (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now take you to comments from CWO superstars. Currently trying to make a mark here in the CWO, Arlon Flaym is slowly moving up the rankings. His next target? Apparently, Ganglord. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Arlon Flaym is in the interview area.) ARLON FLAYM: What up, dawgs? Ya'll mus' be trippin; on whyz I'm talkin' in deeez ebonics. Becaws I jus' got cheated out by dat Ganglord... yeeeeeuh. OK, enough with that. I'd rather speak English. Ganglord... it seems you like to pull those cheatin' moves. Arlon Flaym is mostly a fair fighter but on occasion I myself engage in some of the rule-bending. So, to make a fair cheatin' fight. I want to kick your @$$ in a steel cage match. That's right, you and me. Steel cage. Let's see who the real man is. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: They were down for a while, but now the Alliance is back with a vengeance, and they have a message for the CWO in general. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Alliance is in the interview area.) JIMBO JONES: Ya know... it's great to be the most hated manager in professional wrestling. Everyone wants a piece of me, but I'm a manager and if one of you morons have the guts to put your hands on me you'll hear from my lawyers in no time. I am the master of managing, want a little proof (he holds up the North American Belt and the Lightweight Title)... there's your proof. Now, I am not only the master of managing, but I am purely a businessman. I do what is best for my clients, and as you can see, I do it well. I've been asked by a man by the name of Gold Kard to do a little business with him and destroy a few punks who think they're the hottest thing in professional wrestling. I'm a businessman, and my clients saw it in their best interest to destroy these guys. INFERNO: Yeah, we here in the Alliance are equal opportunity destroyers. You may like us, you may not... but, as the wrestling leader of the Alliance, I am here to say we're turning our face a little. We've received a few negative reactions from fans, and it's gotten to us. There are quite a few of you CWO fans who clap your hands when we walk down the aisle, who get a thrill when we send some no-name punk through a table, and we appreciate you. From right now I'd like to say... we're not changing the way we fight, nor are we changing WHO we fight, like I said, we're equal opportunity destroyers, but if you've got enough sense to cheer us on, go right ahead. For those few of you who clap your hands for us, it gives us a little edge, so I'd like to say... thanks. DAVID "THE DESTROYER" DAVIS: Now, I'd like to go ahead and get it out of the way, just because you like us, it doesn't mean you have to like Jimbo, he's here for our benefit, not yours. Now, let's move on to another matter, THE ROCK. Like we said, we're equal opportunity destroyers. And we'll fight along with the other guys on our team, but when that cage is lifted, it's the Alliance and the Alliance alone. If you've walked down the aisle with us to begin the match... you'd better be ready at the end because when we start we're a team, but when they lift the cage it's every man for himself. We Alliance boys like to stick together, and we'll punk you in no time! TIGER ROGERS: I'd just like to say I'm the future of professional wrestling, and I've held on to this belt through opponent after opponent. I've done more than any CWO wrestler has with this belt, and I deserve a little respect. Sure, my tactics aren't the best... But I get the job done! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: At APRIL ARMAGEDDON, the team of Wildfire and Bloodbath will get their shot at the gold, as they take on the North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys. Let's hear from the challengers. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire in the locker room with Miss Amber.) WILDFIRE: You know something, Bad Guys, I'm sick and tired of people coming into the ring and costing me matches! And if you two... (Wildfire hesitates) think that you can do what ever you want, well, you're wrong! When I came back off of a nine week injury back in February, I said it was time to kick a little @$$! And that's exactly what I'm gonna do! (The room goes dark, then there is a flash of red light and Wildfire is suddenly standing in a cemetery.) WILDFIRE: But this time, I'm not doing it alone.... (A red glove shoots out of the ground and Bloodbath emerges from the grave.) BLOODBATH (dark mysterious voice): Bad Guys, not only will you have to deal with the wild side, but you will have to enter the dark side! Remember, I fear no man, and I feel no pain, and at APRIL ARMAGEDDON, we will be champions! REST IN PEACE! WILDFIRE: We're bringing the gold back for the fans of the CWO! Whatya gonna do when the WILDFIRE runs wild on you! (The camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: On the special one hour EYE ON THE STORM presentation before the PPV, Jason "Flash" Varga will take on Arlon Flaym. We heard from Flaym earlier in this broadcast, now let's hear from "Flash." _______________________________________________________________________________ (Jason "Flash" Varga is in the interview area.) JASON "FLASH" VARGA: Well, well, well... another CWO pay per view is upon us, and the fans won't be able to watch their favorite, the Flash. Man, whose idea was it for me to be wrestlin' this Arlon Flaym guy, anyway? He's not NEARLY the man that I am! The fans will speak, and they'll say "What about Varga?" Maybe they put me on "Eye On The Storm" so the whole world would watch the free preview of the PPV. Yeah, that's it! TOO MANY people called to preorder, they knew they'd be overbooked if I was in THE ROCK! Oh, man, is THAT a relief! Flaym, you're MINE this weekend... get ready for the Flash Flood, 'cause you'll never be the same afterwards! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Joe Eagle and Russell Hill are in a bar in South Philly, watching the Flyers game.) JOE EAGLE: Ganglord, you are in BIG trouble now! Your two little friends won't be able to help you when me and the Big Cool get done with you. And if 'da Floor Mats get in my way to get to you... oh man, it's not gonna be pretty. RUSSELL HILL: Hey, Joe, did you see that hit? EAGLE: Yeah, but it's just making me anxious for this weekend. Ganglord, prepare for pain... the Tower of Power brand PAIN! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Finally today, Masher Maccabee has David "The Truth" Thomas in his corner, and the two of them are looking ahead to APRIL ARMAGEDDON, where Maccabee gets a shot to reclaim the Television title in a ladder match. Let's hear from the Masher. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Masher and David "The Truth" Thomas are sitting in a dark room with a desk between them, obviously planning strategy.) MASHER MACCABEE: Hey, Mondo... Passover's coming up, and I'm not going to pass you over for a beating, son! You remember a guy by the name of Randy Savage? Well, all you ever seem to do is copy his lame ass and you drag Tamara Likescox around like she's the lovely Elizabeth! What happened to them? PUNKED. SPRAY PAINTED. BEATEN. What do you think is going to happen at Armageddon? THE EXACT SAME ***DAMN THING! That belt is mine! DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS: That's right, Mondo Geeko. You and that stupid Tamara don't know the pain you're going to receive in that match. Tamara, you're so dumb you probably think NAFTA is a brand of ice tea. It's going to be no problem sending you two boobs... no, not you, Tamara, both you and Wilcox... down for the count! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on FEVER!
