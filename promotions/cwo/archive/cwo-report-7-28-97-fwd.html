CWO REPORT 7/28/97 (fwd)

29 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch W. Washey sitting at the CWO REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Due to technical difficulties yesterday, we weren't able to come out with a REPORT, so we will combine the results of Saturday's House Show with Sunday's. Let's go to the results for both of those shows. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS Saturday, July 26 Lincoln, NE Brother Justice defeated Coldfront when Jermain Justice pinned Kurt Gerstung after the Final Justice. Time of match: 5:06. Wildfire pinned Mustafa Rahim after the Firebawl (backflip moonsault). Time of match: 12:35. Mondo Gecko pinned Wrath after the flying elbowdrop. Time of match: 19:05. Sandman Jr. and The Focus wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Deadly Sins defeated North American Tag Team Champions the Mercenaries when Pride pinned Kaptain Krunch after the Repention (superplex by Pride, moonsault by Sloth) and outside interference by the Regulators. Time of match: 5:59. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Masher Maccabee pinned El Grande Pistolerro after the Homentaschen Hangover flying somersault legdrop. Time of match: 10:40. Masher Maccabee defended the CWO North American Champion title. WASHEY: Last night, we appeared in Salt Lake City, home of the Utah Jazz. Let's see the results from last night. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS Sunday, July 27 Salt Lake City, UT Exodus and Vegas High Roller wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Light Heavyweight Champion Moses pinned X-Factor after the Commandment (springboard moonsault). Time of match: 3:18. Brent 'The Ace' Alles defeated Red Raven by countout. Time of match: 6:06. TAG TEAM TRIANGLE MATCH: Regulators won a triangle match. Regulators eliminated Mason-Dixon in 7:04 when Regulator Ice pinned Stonewall Mason after the Total Regulation. Regulators eliminated Ghost Riders in 19:51 when Regulator Ice pinned Blaze after the Total Regulation. Joe Eagle pinned Vegas High Roller after the Eagle Shock (tiger driver). Time of match: 14:32. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS TITLE MATCH: The Big Cool defeated champions the Mercenaries when Jumbo Jenkins pinned Korperal Punishment after the Star Flash (backbreaker by Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor, flying big splash by Jenkins) and outside interference by the Bad Guys, who struck the Mercs with stee l chairs. Time of match: 16:34. The Big Cool won the CWO North American Tag Team Champions titles. North American Champion Masher Maccabee pinned Jason 'Flash' Varga after the Homentaschen Hangover flying somersault legdrop. Time of match: 13:41. STREET FIGHT: 'Monster' Rich Clark/Miguel Sanchez defeated World Champion Gold Kard/Matsuhiro Torunaka when 'Monster' Rich Clark pinned Matsuhiro Torunaka after the boot to the face. Time of match: 15:39. WASHEY: Congratulations going out to the Big Cool, who managed to defeat the Mercenaries, but not without help from the CWO World Tag Champs the Bad Guys. Tonight, we're in Reno, NV for a House Show. Here's the lineup for that card. MONDAY, JULY 28, 1997 RENO, NV Reverend vs. Steel Judgement vs. Benjamin Blake Bently. Holocaust vs. Sandman Jr. SUBMISSION MATCH: Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Sandman vs. Diamondback Chris Myers (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Vegas High Roller and Matsuhiro Torunaka vs. Mercenaries (champions) Gold Kard, Moses, Masher Maccabee, Lord Hile Troy vs. Johnny Reb, Brian "The Hammer" Williams, Joe Eagle, Mondo Gecko WASHEY: It's time for the interviews for the REPORT. Bloodbath has some comments, and I think they are the first we've heard since he appeared at RID. Let's go to him now. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Bloodbath stands alone in the cemetary, behind him are the grave stones of all the superstars that were laid to rest, along with several more dug up graves) BLOODBATH (dark and mysterious):At Team Challange, Im teaming up with "Monster" Rich Clark, Sandman Jr., and Chris Meyers. Sandman, Reverend, Red Raven, and BBB, are the next four victims that will be laid to rest. And after that, my next quest is to lay to rest the remaining pieces of trash in Gold Kard International. And after I do that, I will take the CWO World Title from Gold Kard, and the GKI will be no more!!! REST IN PEACE!!! (the camera fades out as Bloodbath disappears into the shadows) ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our only other interview today is from CWO newcomer "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat. Here he is. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens up with the 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat relaxing in a hot-tub full of women who are obviously too young to do anything wrong, and stupid enough to do it......they're laughing it up, drinking, and having an all-around good time...Heat turns to the camera and speaks.) 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat: See-dubbleya-oh! See-dubbleya-oh! What oh what is this slop ya call competition doing in the same ring with me?! First, ya toss that militant moron Rahim-Jaleel-Mohammed to me....girls (looks to girls) GIRLS: (in unison) You beat him Jimmy! HEAT: Then it's that over-rated bouncer 'Lone Wimp' Deuscher's turn.....I knocked the man out for Cripes' sake! Then....hah....that goonie bastard Steel stepped in the ring with me....HAH! I drug his limp carcass tethered to a strap aroun', an aroun', and where he stops...(looks to girls) GIRLS: (In unison) aroun' he goes, an where he stops JIMMY KNOWS! HEAT: I beat that schmoe too! So what's yer next idea to try and stop the 'Heat Wave'? Throw them to me *AGAIN*! I love it! If this is what it takes to get to the top, hey....I'm all for it! Hell! The Deadly Sins don't need to worry about me slacking off around ya guys! I'll probably have a lot of fun, what me being a relative unknown in the See-dubbleyah-oh and all, beating your more experienced butts. Just let me tell you....the next time one of ya fruity-booties gets in the ring, I'll do a lil' stylin'.....and profilin'......and generally show you just how right I can be...and how wrong you always will be..... ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody tomorrow on the REPORT! (Fade to closing credits.)
