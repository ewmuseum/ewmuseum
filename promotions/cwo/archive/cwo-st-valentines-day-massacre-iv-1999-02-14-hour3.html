ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE IV 2/14/99 - HOUR #3

February 22, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MELINN: And look at this! Fantastic is on fire! Repetitively slamming
Night Crawler’s head into the turnbuckle!
(The crowd counts along to 10, and Night Crawler is then slumped in the corner as Fantastic backs up… and nails him with a dropkick!)
MELINN: Big dropkick by the Assassin! Night Crawler is down! Cover, 1… 2… no! He got his shoulder up.
(Fantastic picks Crawler up from the mat and gives him a big headbutt!) MELINN: Headbutt by Fantastic… whip into the ropes… Showstopper! 1… 2… no! A kickout! THOMAS: That was close! MELINN: Fantastic with a lateral press, 1… 2… and another kick out! THOMAS: Got to put him away! MELINN: The “Irresistible Assassin” going to the top… flying splash… no! Night Crawler rolled out of the way! THOMAS: Oh, big mistake! MELINN: Night Crawler rolling over for the cover! 1… 2… no! Logan Fett made the save! THOMAS: Good thing, because I think Fantastic was out! (Fett has to leave the ring, as Night Crawler clubs Fantastic with a double axhandle.) MELINN: Tag now made to Night Slinger… double body slam by the Creatures! And here comes Logan Fett! THOMAS: We’ve got a four-way brawl! MELINN: All four men brawling in the ring… wait a minute… the Creatures just tossed Fett over the rope!
THOMAS: Look at that power! MELINN: Night Slinger with a legdrop on Fantastic, cover, 1… 2… no! He got his shoulder up!
THOMAS: I think Fett’s out cold, Melinn, he hit the pavement hard! MELINN: That spells trouble for Eddie Fantastic, because now… oh no! Night
Crawler with the gorilla press!
THOMAS: It may be all over! MELINN: Fantastic… down to the canvas! Night Slinger… big splash! THOMAS: Day to Night! MELINN: 1… 2… 3! TONY THE K: The winners of this contest in 7:06, and STILL CWO WORLD TAG
TEAM CHAMPIONS, Night Crawler and Night Slinger, the Creatures of Darkness!
(Crowd boos as the Dark Side’s theme music plays.) MELINN: Now come on! Again the Creatures of Darkness and the Fallen attacking these guys after the bell!
THOMAS: They’re still trying to prove something to Darkness! (Fallen has a mike.) FALLEN: For weeks… MONTHS… I have tried to prove myself to my lord Darkness. I have left a pile of sacrifices in his name. But tonight, this shall be… the FINAL sacrifice…
MELINN: Oh no… come on… (Night Crawler tosses a chair in the ring.) FALLEN: Dogs of War… prepare to become ashes… THOMAS: I don’t like the looks of this! MELINN: The Fallen… no! God no! He just Tombstone piledrivered Logan Fett on to the steel chair! And now… he’s going to do it again! Come on!
Somebody stop this! (Fallen does indeed do it again.) THOMAS: He could have very easily broken Logan Fett’s neck right there. MELINN: And now they’re going after Eddie Fantastic! Come on… (The crowd suddenly goes nuts as Johnny Reb runs down to the ring!) MELINN: Wait a minute… it’s Johnny Reb… it’s Johnny Reb! (Reb has a steel chair… and nails Night Crawler with it! Night Slinger gets a chairshot as well, and then the Fallen! The Fallen takes the first one, but Reb winds up and nails him again, sending him down!)
MELINN: Johnny Reb is cleaning house! (The Dark Side members retreat outside the ring.) THOMAS: Well, if anybody knows about fighting the Dark Side, it’s Johnny Reb! MELINN: Johnny Reb earlier attacked by the Dark Side on SCORCH, and maybe he
’s finally woken up and realized just what we’re up against here!
FALLEN (furious, not miked, pointing at Reb): Again you defy us, Southerner? This is far, FAR, from over! MELINN: Ominous words from the Fallen, but now CWO medical crews are down to the ring, and it doesn’t look good for Logan Fett.
THOMAS: Another sacrifice… to DARKNESS. MELINN: All right, fans, I’m told our cameras have caught up with CWO World
Champion Diamondback Chris Myers, who has spent the entire show looking for the lost Dallas! Let’s go there now!
(Myers is shown backstage.) MYERS: DALLAS! DALLAS! Darkness, you bastard, where are you? Where have you taken her? (Col. Taggart appears on the scene.) TAGGART: Diamondback! MYERS: What do you want, Taggart, I don’t have time… TAGGART: Chris, it’s time for the main event. Listen to me, son. I know you’re upset. That’s what Darkness wants. He wants to split us all apart against each other. It almost worked. I’ve got a lot to apologize for, I know, but right now, we’ve got a crowd of people expecting one hell of a match from you and me. Win or lose, I’m proud to be fighting a man like you.
MYERS: I don’t care about the main event… I have to… TAGGART: Myers, don’t you see?!? Darkness wants that! If we don’t go out there and give the people what they want, then we’re going to look bad in their eyes! And that’s how the Dark Side worms their way into people’s hearts, when the good guys don’t do what they’re supposed to do!
MYERS: But Dallas… TAGGART: I know, I know. But kid, I’m sure Dallas is all right. Darkness isn’t going to hurt her, she’s too big a trump card. And I promise you, I’ ll do everything in my power to help you find her. I owe you that much.
But right now, we have to go and give the people what they want… what they deserve! MYERS: All… all right. TAGGART: That’s the spirit, man! Now let’s go! (Taggart and Myers walk off. Tony the K is in the ring along with referee
Dominick Waugh. CWO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP graphic appears on the screen.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit. It is for the CWO World Championship!
(Crowd cheers.) TONY THE K: Introducing first, coming down the aisle... (“Bad Company” by Bad Company plays as the fans cheer.) TONY THE K: Weighing 322 pounds, from Phoenix, AZ, representing Bad Company,
Col. Taggart! (Col. Taggart makes his way down to the ring, slapping hands with the fans.) MELINN: A great man on his way to the ring, and a great speech by him to
Diamondback Chris Myers.
THOMAS: Great speech, my ass! He just wanted his shot at the World title! MELINN: Well, that’s probably true, but what he said made sense… the Dark
Side… Darkness… his whole agenda has been to turn the fans against the “good guys” of the CWO, and it almost worked, if not for Bob Koster, and for once, thank you, Bob, thank you.
(Taggart is in the ring, playing to the crowd.) TONY THE K: And his opponent... ('Cult of Personality' by Living Color plays as the fans cheer.) TONY THE K: Weighing 275 pounds, from Phoenix, AZ, the CWO World Champion,
Diamondback Chris Myers!
(Myers makes his way down to the ring, the gold belt around his waist. He still seems obviously distracted. He does get in the ring, though, handing the belt to the referee, who displays it officially. Myers prepares himself for the match.)
MELINN: All right, our big main event, and although the specter of DARKNESS looms over it, it should still be a great one!
(The bell rings as both men circle each other in the ring. Myers strikes first with a spin kick!)
MELINN: Big spin kick! Myers drawing first blood! THOMAS: I can’t believe you would even bring up blood after that bloodbath we saw earlier. I’m still feeling nauseous!
MELINN: Myers now with an armbar applied… but Taggart gets to the ropes. (Myers gives Taggart the break, but then ties his arm up in the ropes… and dropkicks him in the arm!)
MELINN: Myers with a dropkick to the arm, and Taggart has to roll outside the ring. THOMAS: Good move on his part, keep the Col. from being at full efficiency. (Taggart tries to shake the pain off outside the ring.) MELINN: Col. Taggart trying to get back in the ring now, Myers putting the boots to him while he does, now Myers with a slam… spinning elbowdrop… no!
Taggart moved out of the way! THOMAS: Big break for the Col… hey, he’s so gung ho about his military experience, just what war was he in anyway? Guam?
MELINN: Col. Taggart seeing action in Desert Storm, Truth, read up on these guys sometimes. Taggart now on the offense, as he whips Myers into the corner… and follows it up with a dropkick!
THOMAS: Good move! MELINN: Taggart now with a whip into the ropes… oh man! Myers with the
Frankensteiner! 1… and Taggart gets his shoulder up.
THOMAS: That was pretty friggin’ amaz… MELINN: Myers with a kick to the midsection! Myersplex! 1… 2… no! A kickout! THOMAS: I thought he had him! MELINN: You can tell that even with all the distractions in his life, that competitive spirit is starting to burn in the World Champion, good to see.
Myers now whipping Taggart into the corner… and he charges in with a clothesline! (Taggart rolls out of the ring.) THOMAS: Col. Taggart was probably hoping that Myers would be too distracted so he could win the title, but that doesn’t look like it’s going to happen now!
MELINN: Taggart rolling out of the ring after that… Myers again trying to stomp on him before he gets back in but this time Taggart stops him with a shoulderblock!
(Myers staggers back as Taggart gets in the ring.) MELINN: Col. Taggart now in the ring… and a series of lefts and rights drives Myers back into the corner! Taggart setting Myers up top!
THOMAS: Here we go! MELINN: Taggart… superplex! And a cover! 1… no! Myers gets his shoulder up! THOMAS: Still a lot of fight left in him… (Crowd suddenly boos as Sindy Sinn comes down to ringside!) MELINN: Oh no… come on! Not her! (Col. Taggart hits Myers with a kick.) THOMAS: Just who is she here for, anyway? MELINN: I don’t know, but wherever she goes, trouble is sure to follow her. (Taggart notices Sinn at ringside.) TAGGART: GET THE HELL OUT OF HERE! SINN: Oo, big military man, why don’t you try some maneuvers in my area! THOMAS: Whoo hoo! Take her up on it, Taggart! It’s lonely in the military! MELINN: Look out! Myers from behind, Taggart distracted by Sindy Sinn. THOMAS: Hey, maybe she’s here… MELINN: Oh, don’t start that again! Myers now trying to whip Taggart into the corner but it’s reversed… and Taggart with a big European uppercut!
(Taggart slams Myers to the mat.) MELINN: Taggart with a fistdrop! Going for another one… no! Myers rolls out of the way! THOMAS: These two pretty evenly matched, Myers, for being a World Champion, is 25 years old!
MELINN: Certainly doesn’t have the experience that Taggart does, but what he lacks for in experience, he makes up in heart, and I’m sorry we had to be reminded of that as of late. Myers slamming Taggart to the mat… spinning elbowdrop… no! Taggart rolls out of the way!
THOMAS: This match is eventually going to be who makes the mistake at the wrong time, it could go either way!
MELINN: Taggart with a cradle but Myers is out before a 1 count. Both men back to their feet… Myers with a dropkick!
(Taggart is down as Myers connects with a spinning elbowdrop!) MELINN: Myers with a spinning elbowdrop! Another one! And a third one! THOMAS: He’s pouring it on! (Col. Taggart rolls outside the ring.) MELINN: Taggart rolling out of the ring to escape the assault… oh man! Myers hurtling himself over the top rope with a death defying flying cross body press! THOMAS: That’s insane! MELINN: You have to love this, Darkness trying to devalue the CWO World
Championship and the man who holds it, but as you listen to this crowd, that plan has backfired in a big way!
(Myers slams Taggart into the ring apron!) THOMAS: Whoa! This is a lot more violent than I thought it would be! I guess the MASSACRE brings that out in people!
MELINN: All the gold on the line, Taggart desperately wanting to become
World Champion, it is his dream… Myers already fulfilling that dream twice, and now he’s trying to gain the crowd’s trust back… no! Myers trying to whip Taggart into the guardrail but it’s reversed!
THOMAS: Point to Taggart! (Taggart rolls back in the ring, slowly followed by Diamondback Chris Myers.) MELINN: And you can see Sindy Sinn still at ringside, licking her lips, what is she up to? THOMAS: I don’t know, but she can come whisper in my ear about it any time! MELINN: Taggart and Myers now both back in the ring, Taggart with a body slam… into the ropes… slingshot legdrop! Tremendous move and a cover, 1… 2… no! I thought he had Myers but Myers kicked out!
THOMAS: Myers was a millisecond away from being an EX-World Champion for the second time! MELINN: Taggart… big chop! And a dropkick! Cover, 1… 2… no! Man, how close can you get? THOMAS: Taggart is within reach! MELINN: Taggart now relentless, fistdrop… (Taggart plays to the crowd, getting a good reaction.) MELINN: Crowd has to be torn, they love both of these guys… THOMAS: Shows you that everybody in here has all their taste in their mouth! MELINN: Taggart with an elbow smash… rollup… no! Myers with a reversal! 1…
2… and Taggart gets his shoulder up!
(Both men get to their feet quickly, but Myers stuns Taggart with a right hand!) MELINN: Myers with a quick right hand… and he throws Taggart out of the ring! (Myers goes outside the ring.) THOMAS: This match is all over the place! MELINN: Myers and Taggart brawling outside the ring, and these men possess the highest level of respect for each other, but with the World title on the line, anything goes!
(Col. Taggart rolls back in the ring, followed by Diamondback Chris Myers.) MELINN: Both men back in the ring, Myers with a left jab… whips Taggart into the ropes… Frankensteiner!
(Taggart gets back to his feet, mostly out of instinct… but Myers floors him with a dropkick!)
MELINN: Myers with a dropkick! Cover, 1… 2… what the?!? (Sindy Sinn pulls Myers out of the ring.) MELINN: Come on! THOMAS: She’s gotta have it! MYERS: What the hell is wrong with you? SINN (cooing): Chris… come on… I know you want it… (Myers shoves her backward as the crowd erupts! Sindy is flustered, to say the least.) MELINN: Myers having none of that! THOMAS: What’s wrong with him? I mean, I know he’s not Dark Side, MAYBE, but come on! MELINN: Myers now back in the ring… and another dropkick to Col. Taggart! Myers with a whip into the ropes… Frankensteiner! THOMAS: That move’s served him well tonight! MELINN: Myers with a spinning elbowdrop… no! Taggart moved out of the way! (Myers holds his elbow in pain.) MELINN: Taggart cradles Myers up for the pin! 1… 2… no! So close, but
Myers kicked out! THOMAS: How many nearfalls has Myers had?!? MELINN: Myers will not give up! Col. Taggart dropping an elbow, now a fist… no! Again Myers manages to evade the assault of the Col.!
(Taggart shakes his hand in pain.) MELINN: Myers with yet another opportunity… now he whips Taggart into the corner, no, a reversal…
(Taggart charges in but Myers moves out of the way as Taggart catches himself on the ropes!)
MELINN: No! Taggart is in trouble! THOMAS: That move backfired big time! MELINN: Taggart tied up in the ropes, and Myers is going to work! Spinning elbowdrop! THOMAS: Taggart had no way to go that time! MYERS: Come on, Col., let’s rock! MELINN: Myers now with a rollup for the pin, 1… and Taggart gets his shoulder up. (Both men get back to their feet as Myers goes for a spin kick but it’s ducked… and Taggart flattens Myers with a clothesline!)
MELINN: Taggart exploding with a clothesline! Taggart now whipping Myers into the ropes… Myers with the Frankensteiner!
1… 2… no! A kickout! THOMAS: Man, is this a back and forth match or what? MELINN: Both men to their feet again as Taggart rakes Myers in the eyes. THOMAS: Oh, there’s a real sportsman-like move! MELINN: Desperation setting in, and now Taggart whips Myers into the ropes…
Myers with a dropkick!
THOMAS: Serves him right! MELINN: Myers doing what he always does, fighting on… both men to their feet… Taggart with a chop… Myers with a right hand!
(Taggart is down and Myers applies an armbar!) MELINN: Diamondback Chris Myers with the armbar… wait a minute… what? Fans… we have to go backstage… I don’t… I don’t believe it!
(Camera goes backstage, revealing legions of DARKNESS SECURITY FORCES, armed to the teeth, escorting almost all of the CWO stars out of the arena into paddywagons!)
THOMAS: What the hell?!? MELINN: It looks like all of the CWO stars are being put into paddywagons! What is going on? THOMAS: I don’t know, but I’m suddenly very frightened! Take Melinn, he’s a better worker!
MELINN: Will you be quiet? Fans, I don’t know what to make of what we’re seeing, but… well, let’s focus on the match, I guess. Taggart fighting his way to his feet, trying to get out of the armbar… and he does! Big right hand by the Col.!
THOMAS: They don’t know what’s going on, they might as well fight! MELINN: Taggart with an elbow smash! And now a series of lefts and rights, he’s backing Myers into the corner!
THOMAS: Keep it up! MELINN: Taggart setting Myers up top… oh man! Top-rope DDT! THOMAS: He got him! MELINN: Taggart… wait a minute… he’s setting Myers on the top turnbuckle again! THOMAS: Why didn’t he go for the pin after that top-rope DDT?!? Idiot! MELINN: Taggart wanting to make sure Myers is truly finished off, I guess, and now he’s going for a superplex!
(He does but it’s blocked by Myers with a fist to the ribs. Myers pushes
Taggart off the turnbuckle!)
MELINN: Myers blocking the superplex! Myers from the top… missile dropkick! THOMAS: He got him! (Both men are down as the referee begins a 10 count.) MELINN: Sindy Sinn watching on as both men are down in the ring, what a battle this has been, fans, I’ve been told there are no CWO wrestlers left in the building!
THOMAS: I’m getting out of here! MELINN: I think we’d better stay put… Myers back to his feet… and he’s putting on the scorpion deathlock!
THOMAS: This is it! Taggart might submit! The so-called submission specialist! Boy would I love that!
MELINN: Taggart in a lot of pain… but wait a minute… he’s… he’s fighting his way out of the deathlock… and he does so… and he turns it into the
Annihilator! (Crowd pops!) THOMAS: Ah! No way! MELINN: An incredible move on the part of Taggart… but Myers is able to reach the ropes. THOMAS: I thought it was over. MELINN: So did Taggart, but he’s got Myers in trouble! Taggart whipping
Myers into the ropes…
(Myers catches Taggart with a surprise kick to the midsection, and then…) MELINN: RATTLER! THOMAS: He got him! MELINN: That has to be it, all Myers has to do is make the cover… wait a minute… damn it! The Dark Side attacking!
(Sandman, Wishbone, Warhawk, and Wrath all are in the ring attacking the two others. Sindy Sinn smiles with glee outside the ring.)
THOMAS: You figured this was going to happen! MELINN: The Dark Side attacking Myers, Darkness vowed revenge on him after he exposed his scheme for what it was. What… no! Sandman just powerslammed the referee!
THOMAS: I can’t believe Sandman is still walking after that barbed wire match with Foolkiller earlier!
MELINN: You can see the scars beginning to heal, but that doesn’t matter now, because Myers and Taggart are alone, there is literally nobody to help them!
THOMAS: This may be the darkest moment for the CWO! MELINN: Taggart and Myers are at the mercy of the Knights of Darkness and
Wrath… wait a minute… oh no…
(The crowd boos even louder as a smiling Darkness walks out, applauding. Behind him come the Fallen and the Creatures of Darkness, and they have with them… Chris Greco and Dallas in chains!)
MELINN: No! THOMAS: It’s Greco and Dallas! And they’re in chains! MELINN: This is not good… not good at all… Darkness now leading Greco and
Dallas down to the ring… what’s going to happen…
(The Creatures of Darkness and Fallen make Dallas and Greco get in the ring. Taggart is kicked outside the ring, mostly unconscious from the Rattler and the subsequent beat-down by the Dark Side. Darkness has a mike.)
DARKNESS: Now… is everybody having a good time? (Crowd boos.) DARKNESS (smiling): That’s what I thought! Now, Chris Myers, you had your chance. You could have been one of us. Part of my dream. My vision of a new CWO. But no, you had to resist. So now… you pay the price.
(Crowd boos.) MELINN: Pay the price… what? DARKNESS: You see, Myers, I already have what is most close to you… friend… lover… but I want the only other thing you have in your life… your precious
World title.
(Crowd boos. Myers is held in place by Warhawk and Wrath. Wishbone has… something… in his hand. Sandman stands fearsomely, his arms crossed.)
MELINN: What? No! DARKNESS: You see, I could have had the Alleses just give me the title, but that would never do. No, I want you, in front of all these people, your
FANS… to surrender your title to me!
MELINN: Don’t do it! (Darkness offers Myers the mike.) MYERS: G… g… go to hell, Darkness… (Crowd pops as the Knights again beating Myers down! A cloud passes over
Darkness’ face, but the smile then reappears.)
DARKNESS: Gentlemen please! I want him conscious. Well then, my dear Mr. Myers, perhaps you need some persuasion… (Darkness snaps his fingers.) DARKNESS: My Creatures… if you please… (The Creatures tie a protesting Greco to the ropes!) MELINN: What… no… GRECO: Chris, don’t do it! Don’t! (The Creatures beat on him a bit. MELINN: This is sick! THOMAS: Shh! Don’t make them come over here! DARKNESS: Now, Mr. Myers, my Knight of Discord, Wishbone, loves chaos, but he loves pain even more, and he’s found a new way to administer it.
Wishbone, if you please. (Wishbone holds up the object he had in his hand, and it crackles with electricity!) MELINN: Oh god… oh no… (Wishbone moves forward… and jabs it into Greco!) GRECO: AhHHHHHHHHHH! MELINN: No! He’s helpless! (Wishbone withdraws. Greco twitches, his face contorted in a grimace of obvious pain.) DARKNESS: The belt, Mr. Myers. (The microphone is offered.) MYERS: Chr… Chris… sorry… can’t… GRECO (breathing heavily): Don’t worry about me, Chris, don’t give it… DARKNESS: Wishbone! (Wishbone jabs it with it again.) GRECO: AHHHH! (Greco thrashes around as best he can, trapped as he is. Myers’ head falls forward.) MYERS (muttered): God forgive me… DARKNESS: Again. (Wishbone does so. Greco screams a scream of unholy pain!) DARKNESS: Enough! This is getting us nowhere. (He snaps his fingers.) DARKNESS: Sandman! (Sandman steps forward and grabs Dallas by the throat! She screams!) MELINN: Oh god! No! That’s enough! DARKNESS: I see that torturing your friend isn’t going to do any good, so maybe I’ll up the ante a little. You see, Mr. Myers, I surround myself with soulless individuals, and I assure you, the Sandman has no moral compunctions whatsoever in inflicting bodily harm upon your love… Dallas.
DALLAS (coughing and sputtering): Chr… Chris… don’t… MYERS: All right! All right! Damn it! Let her go! DARKNESS (grinning): That’s what I thought. Sandman… (Sandman lets Dallas go.) DARKNESS (smiling no longer): Now Myers… on your knees… (The belt is handed to Chris, who against his will holds it up in the air.) MYERS (through gritted teeth): It’s all yours, Darkness. DARKNESS (holding his hand to his ear mockingly): What’s that? I didn’t hear? MYERS: I SAID IT’S ALL YOURS! (Darkness takes the belt.) DARKNESS: That’s what I thought. CWO… gaze upon your new champion! (The crowd boos rabidly.) MELINN: What a sickening display… this is the lowest moment in the CWO’s history… DARKNESS: Now, my Knights of Darkness… TAKE HIM. (The Knights start beating on Myers. Meanwhile, the Fallen and Creatures of
Darkness take care of a semi-revived Taggart before he can come in and make the save.)
MELINN: They’re tearing Myers apart! Somebody has to stop this! THOMAS: Who? You? Me? MELINN: Oh… oh no… Wishbone… he’s setting up a steel chair… THOMAS: They’re going to do him like they did Gold Kard! DALLAS (screaming): No! Don’t! (A barely conscious Myers is placed on the top turnbuckle by Wishbone…) DARKNESS: Only you can save him, Dallas! Join me! Join the Dark Side! MELINN: No… DARKNESS (growling): JOIN OR HE’S FINISHED! (Dallas eyes bat left and right in fear. Her head then bows forward.) DALLAS: All… all right… DARKNESS (again feigning deafness): What? (Dallas raises her head, tears streaming from her eyes.) DALLAS: All right! Just… just don’t hurt him… please… DARKNESS (grinning): Excellent! Wishbone, let him down! (Wishbone merely tosses Myers back in the ring. Darkness places his arm around Dallas’ shoulder. You can see her flesh squirm at his touch.)
DARKNESS: All in all, a perfect night! Dark Side, let us celebrate! (The crowd boos as the Dark Side leaves, their theme music playing.) MELINN: God, I… I don’t know what to say… THOMAS: It’s all over, Melinn, it’s all over! (Myers, rolling out of the ring, starts crawling up the ramp.) MYERS: D… Dallas… no… MELINN: Fans, we’re out of time! For everybody in the CWO, good night, and we hope to see you again, if there’s a CWO left to see! Good night!
(Closing creds come up and we then fade out. For the record, Diamondback
Chris Myers defeated Col. Taggart by disqualification in 14:27.)
