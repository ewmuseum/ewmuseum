REPORT 9/12/97

12 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, September 12. I'm Johnny Starr, Mitch E. Washey is on vacation. Last night the CWO stopped in Andover, MD, let's look at the results of that great card! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 9/11/97 Andover, MD Southern Dynamite pinned Beauregard Dixon after the inside cradle. Time of match: 3:41. The referee stopped the match between Mustafa Rahim and Sean Holiday when Rahim had Sean Holiday in the Eclipse after an attack by Exodus. Time of match: 13:39. Amish Farmer pinned Stonewall Mason after the inside cradle. Time of match: 15:17. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Diamondback Chris Myers after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 9:46. Both men shook hands after the match. Skyhawk pinned Jehovah's Witness after the Skydive. Time of match: 11:34. Television Champion The Focus pinned Benjamin Blake Bentley after the inside cradle, pulling the tights. Time of match: 4:46. Newton Bros. and Deadly Sins wrestled to a 30 minute time limit draw. Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo pinned Vegas High Roller after the dropkick in the corner. Time of match: 15:52. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in Baltimore, MD, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 12, 1997 BALTIMORE, MD Wrath vs. Painmaster Mason-Dixon vs. The Nation of Denomination Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Mondo Gecko Deadly Sins vs. Pyrotechnics Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Vegas High Roller "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat and Coldfront vs. Mustafa Rahim and Brother Justice WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Johnny Reb vs. Lord Hile Troy _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with the wild man himself, Sandman Jr. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area.) SANDMAN JR: First, I wanna give a big shout out to Miguel Sanchez, who had a brilliant idea. A year ago, we had the most brutal match the CWO had ever seen, and in my opinion, it's still the most brutal. The stretcher match. Remember that? Yeah, I lost it. But that moron Sanchez thinks he can beat me again, at a pay-per-view, in a stretcher match. Well, as much as I'd like to beat Sandman one-on-one at a pay-per-view, I want you again Sanchez. Stretcher Match. At SOME pay-per-view, or some other kind of special event. This is not for a house show, or even a TV show. This is PPV! Plus, let's make it interesting, Sanchez. A barbed-wire stretcher match. You up for it? I know I am. Now, next week, I have to beat up Carlos Maia at Showcase. I think I'm doing the world a favor here. "The Brazilian Lover"? Let's see how much youi love this cane stuck down your throat! Then again, if it's a Brazilian cane, you might like it. You love Brazillian things, right? Now, the house shows. Man #1 I get is, hey, it's the only guy I get! Anyway, Moses, remember when you were the champ? Yeah, I know it was a real long time ago, and it's tough to remember. But I remember, cause I got like 3 title shots against you, and I never beat you. That hurts my self-esteem. I'm gonna raise it again by destroying you. I don't like you, I don't like GKI, and I don't like losing! And I'm not gonna lose to you anymore. Now Sandman, you keep getting into my face by getting in Chris's matches, and neither one of us appreciates it. Come September Slam, you and the Reverand are gonna be put out to pasture, and you'll see who the superior Sandman really is! Your costing me the Light Heavyweight Title is gonna hurt you a LOT more than it hurt me! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: They wrestled to a 30 minute time limit draw with the Newton Bros. last night in Andover, MD, we got some comments from the Deadly Sins right before that match. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: The Newton Bros? Who the hell are they? SLOTH: Cannon fodder. Red-shirts. Next! PRIDE: Oh, then we get PyroTechnics. I ask again, who? SLOTH: Be happy we have two easy days, you got a singles match coming up! PRIDE: Oh yeah, Jimmy Heat! Man, I ain't no singles wrestler, but I don't have to be! Heat, you messed with the wrong guys when you messed with us. Plus, let's be fair, I am SOOOO much sexier and better looking than you are! SLOTH: It ain't enough we're gonna beat you at September Slam II, we get to beat you up here! COLDFRONT, me and WRATH, and even LUST are gonna make sure you don't interfere in this match, cause we're saving an ass-whupping just for you. And Tommy Bomber? Another nobody getting in our business. WRATH: At next week's Friday Night Fightin', assuming it actually takes place, I got a two of 3 falls fight with Miguel Sanchez. Sanchez, you hate that Junior guy, so you can't be all that bad, but WHAT were you thinking signing a 2 of 3 falls with ME? I mean, I can beat you with one fall, why you want to lose to me twice in one night? You suicidal? Guess you are, cause ain't NO WAY you're gonna beat me ONCE, never mind twice! Then, Friday, I get to beat up the Painmaster. You think you know what pain is? You think you're the MASTER of pain? You know no such thing. Until you face me! And when I get you in the Repention Rack, you WILL know pain! LUST: The time is coming up for you Jimmy... you are gonna pay for putting your hands on me... and your penance will be painful, cause *I* said so! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: The Unit are starting to make their mark here in the CWO already, and they're wrestling quite a few House Shows to the delight of the fans. Let's hear from The Unit. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The camera opens in The Unit's locker room. Skyhawk is leaning back against one of the lockers, wearing only a towel, which shows off his muscular build, and Oakleys. Nearby, Steve and Howard Newton are looking over the CWO ratings. Both are dressed in jean shorts and "Most Dangerous Men In Wrestling" T-shirts. The door to the locker room opens and Sean Holiday and Toni Newton enter.) SEAN HOLIDAY: You guys hear the news yet? SKYHAWK: What news? HOLIDAY: We're all going to be fighting at Andover in Maryland on the 11th. STEVE NEWTON: You're kidding. Who against? TONI NEWTON: Well, you and Howard are facing the Deadly Sins... HOWARD NEWTON: The Sins? Why do they keep giving us low life trash to wrestle? I mean, we're former champs! Look at the dork Painmaster. He's a former champ in a different fed and he's getting title shots already. What the hell's going on here? It's bad enough we got to face the Highlanders again-- SKYHAWK: Who are still in that World Tag match at Slam II despite losing two in a row lately, including to you guys. H. NEWTON: Exactly. But they're gonna make us face the Sins, too? HOLIDAY: Well, look at it this way: the Deadly Sins supposedly set some kind of record for holding on to some tag belts here in CWO. If you and Steve beat them, maybe we can finally get the idiots upstairs to pay some serious attention. S. NEWTON: What do you mean, "IF" we beat them. They're already pinned. Just let me in the ring. T. NEWTON: Oh yeah, Skyhawk, you're facing Jehovah's Witness. SKYHAWK: Well, I guess it looks like my current undefeated streak is going to continue. As long as he doesn't try to give me a "Watch Tower" pamphlet, it'll be a good match. HOLIDAY: What if he does? SKYHAWK: (Laughs.) Then I'll have to kick the crap out of him. S. NEWTON: What about you, Sean? What moron do they have you facing? HOLIDAY: Mustafa Rahim. H. NEWTON: That guy Panther? HOLIDAY: He's not Panther anymore. Now he's just a pus-- T. NEWTON: SEAN! (Smacks him upside the head.) HOLIDAY: (Laughs.) Sooooorry! Naw, seriously, I'm taking him pretty seriously. After all, "Mr. I-Am-The-Law" does have his cohorts Steel and the Justice Brothers with him, so I'm going to have to watch my back. But I don't think there's any way he can beat me. I guess I'll just have to give him a vacation--a Permanent Vacation! T. NEWTON: So we're all set for Andover? Good. Now just a quick thing. We've got about two weeks until Slam II. You guys know that the Cabin is going to try to get themselves DQ'd to prevent you from beating them too hard. Why not petition the CWO for a non-DQ, non-time limit match? S. NEWTON: Better yet, let's make it a "Submission or Surrender" match. (Suddenly grabs the camera and scowls into it.) How 'bout it, Cabin? Guru, you up to having your boys tough it out? Want to show us all just how tough you guys are? Well, c'mon then and get some. We're not going anywhere. And the only place you're going, is HELL, baby! (Pushes the camera away violently as the scene fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Finally today, we have comments from the World Champion, Johnny Reb. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb and Rebel Yell are in the interview area.) JOHNNY REB: Eagle... y'all said ah made mah bed. Well, son, y'all have just done the same. The war is on Upset Kid. Y'all think Johnny Reb has been in the guttuh the last couple of weeks? Well, suh, you ain't seen nothin' yet! REBEL YELL: Listen up, and listen up good Eagle. We know what y'all are really about, and we know that while y'all maht be pulling the wool ovuh some people's eyes, the Confederate Associates see how it really is. You, Eagle, are a snake with a yella belly, and y'alll are gonna pay at the hands of Johnny Reb. REB: Ah am the CWO World Heavyweight Champion. Eagle, y'all didn't get the job done and you know it and it kills you. Well, Johnny Reb always gets the job done, suh, and come September Slam, ah will get the job done again. Ah will knock you down. Ah will wear you out. And ah will remain the CWO World Heavyweight Champion. Nothing y'all do Eagle can change that. YELL: Eagle, y'all know this is the bettuh man. Come September 28, everyone else will know it too. Good day, suh. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's it for today, but before we go we remind you to watch FIGHTIN' FRIDAY NIGHT later this evening. Here's the card in case you forgot: _______________________________________________________________________________ FIGHTIN' FRIDAY NIGHT 9/12/97 (taped Tuesday night in Richmond, VA) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Holocaust and Red Raven vs. Joe Eagle and Jason "Flash" Varga Sandman Jr. vs. "Monster" Rich Clark _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all for today, see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
