CWO REPORT 5/2/97

2 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Wild Ones are sitting in the Gund Arena, lounging around and watching tapes of recent CWO action.) MONDO GECKO: Ya know, ever since Cleveland lost the Browns it's been kind of gloomy here. Well, the Wild Ones are here to wow the crowd up again and that is what we are going to do. Bottom line here is that we are going to get extreme against Coldfront. Steve White and Kurt Gerstung, seems you guys have the same problems that we have with the league. Namely, Brent Alles, GKI, and Bane. Well, if you ever need help, let us know. But today, you guys are going to feel extreme war when you face the Wild Ones. We are going after the North American Titles and you guys are currently in our way. GET READY FOR MONDO EXTREME ACTION! EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: Coldfront, you two are fine competitors and we look forward to facing you two in the ring in the great city of Cleveland. However, we are here too for one thing and that is to win titles. Seems the Bad Guys currently have the tag team titles. Hey, after Mayhem, we will bring those belts around our waist. So right now, Coldfront, you guys will be receiving a beating at the hands of the Wild Ones. VIVA LA WILD BUNCH Y VIVA EL CWO! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Joe Eagle is in the Eagle's Nest interview area.) JOE EAGLE: Hello, CWO fans, and welcome to another installment of the Eagle's Nest. I am joined tonight by my frequent guests, the Big Cool! Welcome back, gents! (The Big Cool make their entrance.) JIM "JAZZMAN" TAYLOR: It's always a pleasure to be here, Joe. JUMBO JENKINS: Don't you ever have any other guests? EAGLE: Naw... there's nobody in the CWO that could keep up with you guys. RUSSELL HILL: Nobody in or out of the ring, my man! EAGLE: And, besides, nobody else ever asks to be on. Well, back to the matter at hand... you guys have a big match tonight, and I know you want to talk about it, right? JENKINS: Right after I hit the donut table backstage. Later, boys... (Jumbo Jenkins runs out of the picture, as fast as he has ever moved.) EAGLE: Uhh... Jimmy? TAYLOR: All I know is that the Creatures of the Night are finished! We couldn't get by you when you guys had the belts, but we're a new team now. Jumbo's been adding muscle, and he might even go on a weight loss plan soon! HILL: Oh, yes, Jenny Craig's a-callin'! EAGLE: And, of course, I'll be facing some nobody called Wrath. Fans of the CWO, come on down to the arena tonight and show Joe Eagle that you remember the good ol' days. I'm the comeback story of '97, and that means that the world title is just around the corner. Hell, if that overweight tool Goliath can get in on a title match, so can Joe Eagle. HILL: Joe Eagle is da one, the once and future King of the CWO. Ladies and gentlemen, when the 60-man Battle Royale at May-hem ends, there will be three bodies in the ring: Jazzman, Jumbo, and the Eagle. And, since we already have a deal worked out, Jazzy and Jumbo have agreed to throw themselves out to allow Joe the chance to get his belt back. EAGLE: The CWO's never seen somethin' like this, and there can be no other! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, Brian "The Hammer" Williams, Jason "Flash" Varga and "Big Guy" Jim Peer are in Wayne's house on the lake, watching the review of last night's house show in Erie, PA.) JASON "FLASH" VARGA: DAMN! That fool Moses got the Light Heavyweight belt! I told you guys that Tiger Rogers was worthless. Now I won't have 2 belts to wear after May-hem! (Varga is frowning, and goes to the kitchen to get a beer.) WAYNE "COLLEGE GUY" SCHILLO: Sure, you want 2 belts. I can't even get into the championship match! I'm gonna have to win the 60-man thing just to get a shot! BRIAN "THE HAMMER" WILLIAMS: Uh... I'm gonna win it. SCHILLO: Didn't we already have this argument? (Varga tosses a beer to Schillo, and cracks his own open.) VARGA: Screw it. Let's just go out on the boat and fish. SCHILLO: Yeah... after we win the 6-man titles tonight, you'll have your 2 belts. PEER: Yeah, man. Three belts would have been too many! VARGA: Maybe it's a blessing in disguise! Or a sign from above! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, May 2, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Cleveland, OH, let's see what went down on that great card! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 5/1/97 Cleveland, OH Wild Ones (Mondo Gecko and El Grande Pistolerro) defeated Coldfront when Mondo Gecko pinned 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 15:09. Alliance (Tiger Rogers, Inferno, Goliath, David "The Destroyer" Davis) defeated Gold Kard International (Gold Kard, Masher Maccabee, David "The Truth" Thomas, Mysterious Masked Man) when David 'The Destroyer' Davis pinned Gold Kard after the flying elbow. Time of match: 38:16. Da Hood Rats defeated Deadly Sins when Warren pinned Sloth after the flying chest stomp. Time of match: 25:56. Joe Eagle pinned Wrath after the Eagle Shock (tiger driver). Time of match: 9:59. The Big Cool defeated Creatures of the Night when Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor pinned Night Crawler after the Star Flash. Time of match: 24:59. Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned The Focus after the Nobility, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 3:39. North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys defeated Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) in a non-title street fight when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Bloodbath after the Bad Guys' Bash (double strike with steel chairs). Time of match: 37:51. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: College Guys (Jason "Flash" Varga, Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, Brian "The Hammer" Williams) defeated Southern Pride (Johnny Reb and Mason-Dixon) when Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Stonewall Mason after the clothesline. Time of match: 19:29. College Guys won the CWO World 6-Man Tag Team Championship. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see, the World 6-Man Tag Team Championship changed hands last night, congratulations to Jason Varga, Wayne Schillo, and Brian Williams on the winning of those titles. Tonight we have a stop in Buffalo, NY, before moving on to Montreal for this weekend's MAY-HEM. Let's look at the lineup for tonight's card in Buffalo. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW 5/2/97 Buffalo, NY Johnny Reb vs. Gold Kard Joe Eagle vs. Bane Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) vs. New Men (Spartacus and Akillies) "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher vs. "Mr. Niceguy" Steve White David "The Destroyer" Davis and Inferno vs. Masters of Hardcore Goliath vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses vs. Highlanders _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with one of the newest superstars to join up with the CWO, Diamondback Chris Myers. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Diamondback Chris Myers is shown in his gym in Pittsburgh, doing bench presses. Dallas stands beside him, counting off the reps) DALLAS: 23... 24... one more, Chris! (Myers gives one last PUSH and puts the weights back on the rests.) DALLAS: 25! Not bad. DIAMONDBACK CHRIS MYERS: Thanks, Dallas. (Myers turns and notices the camera pointed at him.) MYERS: Hey! You guys from the CWO? CAMERAMAN: Yep. Just wanted to get some comments before MAY-HEM. MYERS: Sure! (Myers grabs a Gatorade and takes a long pull.) MYERS: I guess I made a hell of an entrance on Tuesday, which is what you should come to expect from DCM! However, making an entrance and wrestling your first event are two totally different things in and of themselves. My first appearance in a CWO ring is going to be during the Battle Royal, where the winner gets a World Title shot at Summer Heat! DALLAS: That belt would look good around your waist, Chris.... MYERS: True, but that would be getting ahead of ourselves! I would like nothing better then to hold a World title, but first, I have to go through 59 other guys, who have been here a lot longer and know what to expect! Myself, though I have been around a while, am a lot more green in this fed. I'm basically going in blind, I admit. Because it's a battle royal, I can't use all my high flying moves. But, for all those who think all I am is a high flyer, think again. I can brawl, and I can get technical. I am an all around wrestler, and anything I can do to get the crowd to chant my name, I'll do! DALLAS: CWO, don't think of Diamondback as another hot-shot rookie who's coming in with a bang and then leaving! Not by a long shot, boys! He's here to stay, and to win gold one way or another! MYERS: By the time Sunday night is over, I'll have a bunch of CWO wrestlers gunning for me. And that's where I'll start, beating them all one by one. I'll get that World belt. It's just a question of when. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Another wrestler involved in the 60-man MAY-HEM battle royal is Sandman Jr., let's hear from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area.) SANDMAN JR.: YOOOOOO! First, I want to tell Wayne Schillo good luck in the triangle match on Showcase, 'cause I'm gunning for that belt, and I'm also gunning for the Focus! You want to help me crush Focus, fine. But I WILL attack you and win the match, so be warned. As for the battle royal, I'm searching out two men: Focus again and that jerky BBB. I'm gonna toss you two out first, THEN I'll go after whoever else I have to. I love battle royals, I'm DAMN good at them, and you'll see why Sandman Jr. should be the NEW Heavyweight Champ! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also participants in the battle royal, the Deadly Sins have comments. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: Eternal Wanderers, that was the best match we've had in a LONG, LONG time, possibly the best match the CWO has ever seen! We want a rematch, no CO, no DQ! SLOTH: Ya know, the three of us might have to fight each other in the battle royal. PRIDE: Fine. Then let's make a pact right here and now that we'll team with each other until we're the last 3 men, THEN kill each other. WRATH: It is only fair that way. And that is the way it shall be done. PRIDE: Oh yeah, one last thing. ALL 3 SINS: LUST, STOP FOLLOWING US AROUND! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally, let's hear from Panther and Brother Justice. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Panther and Brother Justice are all dressed in designer suits. Jerome and Jermain are wearing black suits while Panther is wearing a neon green suit.) PANTHER: Well, it seems that I have fallen on some hard times here in the CWO, but with May-Hem just around the corner that is all going to change. When that 60 man free for all is over we will be the last people standing. That's right, just the four of us. JEROME JUSTICE: Man, are you going crazy, there is only three of us. PANTHER: Well, that would appear to be the case, but.... (Steel comes walking in, Jerome and Jermain are prepared to attack.) PANTHER: It's OK, boys. Me and my man Steel have patched some things up. Now that he has terminated the services of Da Boss, he has seen the error of his ways. STEEL: That's right, I realize I am not greater than the sport of wrestling. I am only one of it's components. There are a few people out there who need to be taught the same lesson. Starting with you, Sandman Jr. Sure, it is easy for many of you out there to look at my win-loss record and turn up your nose, but I don't think Sandman Jr. can do the same. Sandman Jr., more than once you have felt my wrath and more than once you have been defeated. What has come before will come again. JERMAIN JUSTICE (Still in a state of shock): Hood Rats, I know you still runnin' from us SUCKAS! You come out here and shoot off ya mouths, but whatcha got to back it up? You ain't nothin but a bunch of street punks! JEROME: Rats, we cleaned up scum like you for fun back in Detroit. Now I know that some other people here are vocal about being from Detroit as well. I am proud to say I'm from the motor city and any time any other of you Detroit guys want to step in the ring for some fun against your neighbors, let us know. PANTHER: CWO, we are unified and electrified! The question is, what are you gonna do to stop us? _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
