CWO REPORT 11/13/96

13 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We open on a scene from last night's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, with Bane and Sandman in the balcony, Bane has the Merciful Death locked on.) MELINN: And you can see what is happening here, Bane has Sandman locked in the Merciful Death sleeperhold, security trying to get to these two guys, but the Apocalypse, Plague and Pestilence, are holding them off. Sandman and Bane brawled up to the balcony during the break. THOMAS: Sandman is out, Melinn! MELINN: Wait a minute... what's Bane... he's lifting Sandman up... OH MY GOD! Bane just threw Sandman off the balcony! THOMAS: I don't believe it! MELINN: Sandman crashing through an awning, at least that broke his fall, but my god... Sandman is not moving... and look at Bane, he's staring down at Sandman! And if you could see behind that mask, I bet you would see laughter! This is sick! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Fade to the CWO REPORT opening. We go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Wednesday, November 13, 1996. What you have just witnessed is one of the most shocking things ever in the CWO, Bane throwing Sandman off the balcony of the LA Sportatorium last night. Sandman was taken to a local hospital, where he was treated for numerous lacerations, as well as a cracked rib. Luckily, Sandman's fall was broken a bit, or it may have been more serious than that. The feud between Sandman and Bane rages on, these two forces will collide at NOVEMBER PAIN in a WARGAMES match, one of the many WARGAMES matches that will determine the final entrants into the two-ring battle royal, the winner of that gets a shot at the World title at CWO CELEBRATION in December. Tonight, though, the superstars of the CWO will be in Las Vegas, NV, let's look at the card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 1996 LAS VEGAS, NV Ganglord vs. Mondo Gecko TEXAS DEATH: David & Goliath (David "The Destroyer" Davis and Goliath) vs. Deadly Sins (Pride and Sloth) Sin vs. Bane Wildfire vs. Steel Mad Doctor's Ghost vs. Joe Eagle Tiger Rogers vs. Battlecat Steve Keller vs. Panther WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Southern Pride (Johnny Reb and Mason-Dixon) vs. The Dark Side (Hellhound and the Creatures of the Night [Night Crawler and Night Slinger], champions) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (table match): Miguel Sanchez vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles (champion) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, steel cage): Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) vs. "Big" Rich Clark _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: As you can see, one of the big matches on that card is a defense of the CWO World 6-Man Tag Team Championship. We go to comments now from both the champions, the Dark Side. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on Hellhound and his partners, the Creatures of the Night, Night Crawler and Night Slinger.) HELLHOUND: Again we get to fight and beat up another team right? (Hellhound looks toward Night Slinger.) NIGHT SLINGER: That's right, big guy. Tonight we get to beat on the Master's long time enemy here in the CWO, Johnny Reb, and his honky tonk friends, Mason-Dixie. Southern Pride, tonight you guys have a chance to take these gold belts from around our waists, but like the Master has told you, Johnny Boy, that as long as he is around and you are in the ring you shall never hold any of the gold in the CWO and that includes these belts. NIGHT CRAWLER: Mason-Dixie, we have seen what a loser Johnny Reb is and the two of you have suffered many deafeats at the hands of the team from The Dark Side, what makes you think that as a group of losers you can defeat the most powerful force? Us! HELLHOUND: Hey, guys, do I get to beat on Johnny Boy for the Master or what? NIGHT CRAWLER: Shut up mutt. Southern Pride, tonight you're "Daylight Breaks" and our "Forever Night" shall engulf you! ALL THREE: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!! (As the camera fads the three men turn and walk out. Just before the camera has gone to black Hellhound turns and holds his belt to the camera.) HELLHOUND (yelling): IT'S ALL MINE! (Fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Another match on the Las Vegas card is Sin vs. Bane. After seeing the limits that Bane will go to get rid of an opponent last night, all of us should say a prayer... for Sin. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Setting: It is dark as usual, and nothing stirs. The drip of water can be heard in the back ground. Bane is sitting down and is hard to make out.) BANE (his voice raspy, and menacing): Forgive him Father, for Sin knows not what he does. The hell which I spawn will devour you, Sin. I am your guilt, your priest, and your Bane. You shall bow down before me, and you shall beg for forgiveness. Your contirtion will make no difference, and your penance shall make you suffer. Your penance will be me and all of my wrath and fury. The sinner shall suffer eternal torment. You get your first twenty minutes with me tonight. Remember before you say your prayers, Sin; your Bane has arrived. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Another match on the card is Tiger Rogers vs. Battlecat, this exciting and excited newcomer to the CWO had comments for us. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Battlecat is in the interview area.) BATTLECAT: WELL, I SHOWED THE WORLD I WAS READY FOR THIS LEAGUE AND I HAD A SPECTACULAR DEBUT. CLARK GRAY, YOU WERE A GREAT COMPETITOR AND IT WAS A THRILLING MATCH, BUT I AM NOT GOING TO BE DENIED MY DESTINY. SO I HAVE AN OPEN CONTRACT TO ANY ONE IN THIS FEDERATION, FAN FAVORITE OR HEEL, TO STEP IN THE RING WITH THE MIGHTY BATTLECAT. WHEN THIS CAT ROARS BE PREPARED TO FEEL HIS WRATH! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Why is he shouting? Anyway, the main event will be a non-title affair for the World Championship, in a steel cage, in which CWO World Champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo gets a shot against the mammoth "Big" Rich Clark before Clark gets his title shot on Thursday. Can Schillo get the job done against the 7'4", over 450 pounds behemoth? We go to him for comments. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo is in the interview area.) WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: I just returned from a weekend trip to Ethiopia... someone told me the people there were malnourished, so I taught them how to pick up women who will buy stuff for them! Now that I'm back, I'm ready to defend my title! Rememer, little Schillomaniacs - do your homework, love the ladies, and ALWAYS beat the bad guys! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Uh... OK. Now we go to comments from one of the menacing forces in the CWO, two members of Doomsday, the Mercenaries. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens up on the Mercenaries.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: All right, CWO, listen up! The Mercenaries are getting sick and tired of getting shafted right and left in this league. Give me a break! The Deadly Sins ranked #1 in the federation?!? Bull****! Everybody saw us take the so-called #1 tag team and destroy them inside a steel cage! KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: The Creatures of the Night don't want any part of us, since we're the only team that they haven't beaten in this league. They've been pretty quite as of late, because they know that we're the better team. KRUNCH (rewinding tape of last match with Deadly Sins): Check this out. (Watches as he flies off of turnbuckle, clotheslining a bloody Pride, who does a 360 before hitting the canvas.) KRUNCH: How much pride did he have getting his butt kicked like that? Everybody knows the truth. Twice we've taken the so-called 'world champions' to the limit, and came up with squat!! We got disqualified with the Pit Bosses, and a good thing too, since we were about to not only end their title run, but also their careers! PUNISHMENT: Both the Pit Bosses and the Creatures of the Night know who really should be wearing gold. That's why we're calling out the so-called World "chumpions". No time limit, no DQ. We know that we should be the champions, you know that we should be the champions, and the rest of the world knows that we should be the champions. Don't like it? Deal with it, because THE MERCENARIES ARE THE TEAM THAT RULE THE WORLD!!! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: And finally, we haven't heard from him in a while, but the always unique Jackass Joel finally has some comments for us. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on Jackass Joel painting a barn white.) JACKASS JOEL: Darkness, darkness, I get so damn sick and tired of hearing about it. We crushed you in the PPV, sent you to your blessed darkness, showed you that you are actually yellow. when it comes to one on one in the ring, you run or lose. Onlywhen you can sneak attack or pick on non wrestlers like Sondra are you BIG S##T. Well heed my warning darkness, you soon will get to see the light totally, because what's good for the goose is good for the gander. You better watch your back everywhere you go, because you never know when you will become victims of a new LIGHT, hope you look good in white. Like hosptial limos? (Jackass Joel laughs loudly and brays like a donkey as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today, we'll see you tomorrow!
