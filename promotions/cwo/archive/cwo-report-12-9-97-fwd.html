CWO REPORT 12-9-97 (fwd)

9 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 12/6/97 Indianapolis, IN Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor and Kaptain Krunch wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum and Masashi 'Lightning' Lanther wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 5:34. Brent 'The Ace' Alles defeated Sandman by disqualification when the Dark Side attacked. The CPE made the save. Time of match: 10:54. Mason-Dixon defeated Creatures of the Night by disqualification when ther Dark Side attacked. Time of match: 4:55. The Dark Side were made to leave the building after this. Mondo Gecko pinned Gold Kard after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 14:28. Bad Guys defeated Pit Bosses when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Pit Boss #1 after the Bad Guys' Bash. Time of match: 16:15. Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Joe Eagle after the spinning elbowdrop. Time of match: 16:30. Mercenaries defeated Dogs of War when Korperal Punishment pinned 'Irresistible Assasin' Eddie Fantastic after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 17:31. Light Heavyweight Champion Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned World Champion Johnny Reb after the Flash Flood. Time of match: 16:52. (Diamondback Chris Myers and "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum are shown in their gym in Pittsburgh, PA, after an intense workout. Chris is wiping his face off with a towl, while Mark takes a swig of Gatorade before looking at the camera) Fulcrum: Hey, Pit Bosses... Welcome back. Sorry to welcome you back with a loss on FEVER. I've heard all about you, #1. Or, High Roller. Chris told me about your match a few months ago. So, when GKI dumped you, you managed to make up with a guy whose neck you broke. And I thought I'd seen dysfunctional friendships. Well, we're the champs, and we've got to defend. And after we beat you, we'll go on to CELEBRATION and defend against Foolkiller and Union Caine. The Empire. A week away, boys. Think you're ready? You can never be. Especially for us. You look at you...two big guys, with two devestating finishers. But look at us. We're high flying, Chris can tech, and I love to brawl. Plus, we can hit our finishers no matter how big you are. So, Caine? Foolkiller? I can't wait for Sunday. I like being a champ. And you too ain't going to break my vibe. Speaking of vibes, let's talk about vibe breakers: Pride and Sloth. You two will get a lesson in wrestling when the Wild Cards step in the ring with you this Sunday. You talk about how they insult you...well, you deserve to be insulted! When was the last time you looked in the mirror, Sloth! Jesus Christ! I see you, and I think "Eric Cartmen". Fight with the cat for pot pie, big guy? Myers: Mark's said it all about the Empire and the Bosses, but let me talk about the Sins. You three keep saying...VASTLY OVERRATED when talking about me. Let me say this...6 months. 2 belts. And how long did it take you two to even GET a title shot? Wrath, if you think I'm such a fluke...let's step in the ring and see who's better. And Empire...it's short and sweet right now, but come Sunday...it'll be brief and bloody. And we're walking out with those North American belts around our waists still. Bet on it. (Both give the Riders sign as we fade out) Masher Maccabee is in the shiny new "Masher's Gold's Gym" smack in the middle of Crown Heights, NY. He is training, and sweating heavily. "You know, a lot has happened to the Mash lately. The TV strap, GKI, the North American belt, losing... and my lack of a world title shot. Well let me tell you this-- Fergus, you got lucky. No one escapes the Hangover twice. I've gone back to my roots, here in the toughest city in the world, training hard, working out to regain my EXTREME edge with the man who got me started in this business, Paul E., and let me tell you this Fergus-- come CWO Celebration, when I lay your battered body on top of the tables, and stand atop the scaffold, you will know your time has come. Then, I will deliver the most devastating Homentaschen Hangover of all time, the most devasting Hangover there will ever be. I'm going to cripple you Fergus. No broken ankles this time, I'm talking SPINES! In other business, the fact that I am the most dominating superstar in the business today seems to go unnoticed. I do believe I kicked Mondo Gecko's ass this past Sunday, yet no one is calling for a title shot for the master of the Jerusalem Jackknife and the Monster Mash Driver #2. Well, I'll take care of it for all of you. I expect, and DEMAND a world title shot in January if I win my belt back at Celebration. If I don't get it... maybe Mr. Bookerman "the Ace" will be Hungover through some tables as well. I am the toughest S.O.B. in the CWO, and if anyone has a problem with it, try to whip my ass, cuz I'll sure as hell kick yours!" (Camera opens on Hellhound who is standing alone in the Darkness panting very heavily with drool dripping from the side of his mouth to his shirt that is soaked.) Hellhound: I listened to what people had to say about the master and the Dark Side yesterday and I saw that ppppeople were not very happy with him. First some wannaba complains that his girlfriend wont talk and that its the masters fault. Hey stupid its not his fault she don't love you no more. Then my like buddy, my play toy El Grande Pistol comes out and talks bad bout me. It hurt my feelings. I help him off the cage like the master would have wanted me too and he got mad. Now he wants to thank me in the ring so I will play more with him and finally rid the master of part of CPE.. He hates those guys you know. After he finishes with wannabe Jr. he is going to kill them all. Oh, Oh, Oh, there is that guy who thinks he is the master but really he is just another wannabe too. He thinks he can beat the master of DARKNESS but no way man. The Wildfire is only little bug in master plan. The real ph... phe... phenom is here in Dark Side big bad Bloodbath. He scary you know almost like master. But my freind I like him. Sandman too he my friend. Hellhound have lots of new friends now and soon more. But I not allowed to say who but you know soon. You see Sandman he going to take care of old business when he show Brent Alles what the true DAKRNESS feels like. That guy should never have steped into our NIGHT our Darkness because he not going to like it. (just then the door flies open and Hellhound curls up into a ball and rolls to a pair of feet. As the camera pans up it reveals those feet belong to MOD Damion.) Damion: Hellhound my pet I hope you were behaving yourself or shall I punish you like I will those who step in my way. Wildfire you never had me beat I would never say I quit. You are weak, wearker than even the Wannabe Jr. and CPE you boys having fun. You come and try to prove each week to yourselves that you are not afraid of the Darkness, not afraid of being thrown off a 30 foot high cage. Well I know the truth I see the fear in your souls. Your NIGHT will come when I show you the true power that is the force known as the DARK SIDE! Night Slinger: (Coming through the open doorway) I see that the pair of old should have stayed gone Brothers are back together and now back in this punny federation. They do see one thing straight and that is with the exception of the Dark Side the tag teams here is this CWO are pathetic. Not even worth the time of day or night to get in the ring with. Night Crawler: (steps into camera veiw with the rest of the members of the Dark Side behind him.) You know I think we have sat back long enough. The master has told us he has thing pretty much under control and that it is now time to make our move time to stike gold. We are going into the tag team dance off with the intentions of leaving with a shot at the world belts. I don't care who has 'em they are ours. The greatest tag champions the CWO has ever seen are about to get back on top. There aint nothing none of you all can do. MOD Damion: December will go down as the month that Hardcore met the DARKNESS and went into soft cuddly mush. I will destroy you Sandman Jr. Your body shall remain here in my cell left to rot away while I lock your soul in my FOREVER NIGHT. All members: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!!
