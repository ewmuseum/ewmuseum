CWO REPORT 5/10/97

10 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, May 10, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Albuquerque, NM, let's look at what went down there! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 5/9/97 Albuquerque, NM Steel made Sandman Jr. submit to the The Gank. Time of match: 18:23. Alliance (Inferno, Tiger Rogers, Goliath, David 'The Destroyer' Davis) defeated Tower of Power (Joe Eagle, Jason 'Flash' Varga, The Big Cool) when Inferno pinned Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor after the small package. Time of match: 29:58. Centurion pinned The Focus after the Arakkis (slingshot brainbuster) and outside interference by Benjamin Blake Bentley. Time of match: 13:04. Wrath made 'Lone Wolf' Ryan Deutscher submit to the Repentence Rack. Time of match: 14:30. Panther made Benjamin Blake Bentley submit to the Eclipse. Time of match: 13:52. STRETCHER MATCH: Maximum Overdrive defeated Brent 'The Ace' Alles in a stretcher match. Time of match: 31:20. Deadly Sins defeated New Men when Sloth pinned Spartacus after the Repention (superplex by Pride, moonsault by Sloth). Time of match: 25:18. CWO Television Champion Johnny Reb pinned Gold Kard after the crucifix. Time of match: 12:17. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see, Brent "The Ace" Alles did indeed show up at the house show last night to take on Maximum Overdrive, despite doctor's orders. However, Alles' rib and neck injuries proved too severe for him to withstand Overdrive's assault, and he was again taken out on a stretcher. Looking towards tonight's show in Salt Lake City, UT, let's take a look at the lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, MAY 10, 1997 SALT LAKE CITY, UT Mercenaries vs. Ghost Riders Baby Waldo vs. Centurion Diamondback Chris Myers vs. Serpentmaster Vegas High Roller vs. Bloodbath Gold Kard vs. Moses TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Goliath vs. Johnny Reb WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Wild Bunch (Mondo Gecko and the Regulators) vs. College Guys (Jason "Flash" Varga, Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, Brian "The Hammer" Williams, champions) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Inferno vs. "Monster" Rich Clark (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with the Wild Bunch, who will be going after the World 6-Man Tag Team Titles currently held by the College Boys: Schillo, Varga, and Williams. Let's hear from the Wild Bunch. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wild Bunch are in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: College Guys, you got something that we want and that is those World 6-Man Tag Team Titles. So now, you guys got to put up or shut up and we are going to win those titles. The fans of the CWO need some wild excitement and we are the people to do just the trick. Bottom line here, College Guys, is that we have been ignored in the title department for too long and we aren't going to take it anymore. Varga, Schillo, and Williams, you guys may be the poster boys of the CWO for good reason but we are ready to become the new torchbearers here. GET READY FOR MONDO EXTREME ACTION! REGULATOR FIRE: College Guys, you know, maybe Mondo can praise you guys because he is a very good guy, BUT WE DON'T! To us, you guys are nothing but a bunch of pretty boys who have been pushed by the front office because you are pretty and the women love you. Well, we deal with punishment, pain, violence, and titles. As far as your bodyguard Maximum Overdrive goes, we deal our enforcement in the ring and with just the two of us. NO sneak attacks, no blindsiding, and we do it ourselves, we don't hire thugs. Varga, we have some respct for you but not much because you too are a pretty boy. BOTTOM LINE HERE IS WE ARE GOING TO DESTROY YOU THREE AND WIN THE SIX MAN TAG TEAM TITLES! TELL 'EM, ICE! REGULATOR ICE: WELL! IT GOES LIKE THIS: COLLEGE GUYS, THIS IS THE REAL WORLD AND IN THE REAL WORLD YOU GOT TO EARN OUR RESPECT THE HARD WAY, BY BEATING IT OUT OF PEOPLE. AND THAT IS WHAT WE HAVE DONE. So you guys bring the titles and everything you got because we are going to win the belts and become the CWO 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS! OOOOOOHHHHHHH WWWWWHHHAAATTTT AAAAAAA RRRRUUUUSSSSHHHHH! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Now, as we promised, some comments from Brent "The Ace" Alles. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Alles is shown standing on the farmland next to his home in Byron Center, MI. The day is overcast and melancholy, a breeze ruffles Alles' hair. Alles is deep in thought, as Amanda Bloom stands a little ways away from him in the field, her arms crossed, her hair blowing in the wind.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES (not talking to the camera): My grandfather farmed this land. Always told me that "you reap what you sow, boy, you reap what you sow." So now, with some busted ribs, and a sore neck, I'm looking back on what I've done over the past year, and I guess my grandfather was right. (Alles walks over to Bloom and massages her neck for a moment as she smiles up at him, somewhat sadly.) ALLES: Should I be surprised that I'm the #1 target in the CWO? I guess not. You reap what you sow. I put Brian Williams out of action for a while, and I may have put "Lonestar" out of action for good. Do I apologize for that? No. They got in this business knowing that there are people like me with shattered dreams and souls that have to find an outlet for the pain and anguish that confronts us every day. The only thing that calms the storm in my abyss now is my beautiful Bloom. (Bloom hugs Alles tenderly, careful to avoid his taped ribs.) AMANDA BLOOM: When is it going to end, honey? When is it going to end? ALLES: I told you, Mandy, I can't promise anything. This road you've chosen with me is a hard one. I've done my deeds here in this organization, and now they're coming back to haunt me. And I can only take solace in the fact that it looks like young Schillo and young Williams are finally getting the point, that there's no point in playing to the crowd, kissing the girls and making them cry, the only thing that matters is surviving in this hellhole warzone and getting everybody before they get to you. (Alles turns to the camera.) ALLES: That was my mistake, Overdrive. I let you get to me before I got to you. You know, if it didn't hurt, I'd laugh, because your pathetic display of "toughness" is just that, pathetic. You think you're a leader because you got that fat tub of lard and those two underachieving losers of a tag team behind you? Huh? It's the blind leading the blind, Overdrive, and if you think you've intimidated me, you're wrong. But I will be watching my back more often. You see, that's the difference between you and me: I'm a stand-up guy; you may not like me, I don't give a damn. But I stand on my own two feet, I'm the Clique of One, I don't need thugs, I don't need hitmen, I don't even need any friends, all I need are these two fists... (He clenches them.) ALLES: And my signature move. You see, Overdrive, you do that move to me and I'm still walking. I do that move, the move I originated and perfected, and people get sent to the hospital or the morgue. So if you think you humiliated me by using my own move on me, you can forget it. Because the fact that I'm still walking proves that if you're going to do something, do it right. You reap what you sow, Maximum Overdrive, and on June 1, Myrtle Beach, SC, you will reap the whirlwind. CO1 - EOT. (Alles and Bloom start to walk back to his house as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today, we'll see you tomorrow!
