SCORCH 11/14/99 preview

November 14, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Today on SCORCH you can expect great CWO action as usual! With the "qualifiying matches" announced on this past FEVER for NOVEMBER PAIN '99 of
Foolkiller vs. Carr, Myers vs. Gecko (w/Troy as the special referee!), and
Carter vs. Caine, what else is going to happen on the way to NP99? Tune in tonight on SCORCH to find out!
Speaking of one of the men involved in a qualifying match at NOVEMBER PAIN
'99, the Elite monster CARR (with LT. CAMMY LEBEAU) takes on the mysterious masked KRESLYN. Kreslyn had better hope this is not a "Fatality" encounter!
BRIAN "THE KING" DERKS will be taking on former Millennium Man GOLD KARD
(accompanied of course by the lovely GERMAN CHOKLAT). We haven't heard much from "The King" lately, but apparently he has a major announcement to make tonight!
The Television Championship will be on the line, as champion ICEMAN X (with
VACHYNA) goes up against "IRRESISTIBLE" EDDIE FANTASTIC (with "MASTERMIND"
VINCE DAVINCI). Fantastic and his partner JOHNNY REB just came off a stellar run as World Tag Team Champions, could Fantastic be looking to make a name for himself in singles competition?
And finally, in our main event, SIMON ST. SEVERE, the "Preacher of Pain", continues his quest for revenge against the ELITE, as he takes on
"REGULATOR" JESSE EWIAK in what should be a hard-hitting matchup! All that and more, tonight on SCORCH! Don't miss it! http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5403/
