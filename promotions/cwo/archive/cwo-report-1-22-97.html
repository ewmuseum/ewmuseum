CWO REPORT 1/22/97

22 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO Control Center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Wednesday, January 21, 1997. So good to be back with you after a long absence. Last night saw a tremendous return of TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, let's take a quick look at the results from that great card in Springfield, IL! _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER (1/21/97) RESULTS DARK MATCHES: Hoss and Little Joe defeated Sandman and Talon when Little Joe pinned Talon after the Flying Brand in 19:59. Bane defeated Johnny Reb by disqualification in 7:35 when Hile Troy attacked him, forcing to break the Merciful Death sleeperhold he had applied on Reb. MATCHES ON THE TELEVISED BROADCAST: Regulators (Fire and Ice) defeated Deadly Sins (Pride and Sloth) when Fire pinned Pride after a powerslam in 13:19. Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned Clark Gray with a duckunder takedown and a bridge in 8:30. Bad Guys (Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson and Joe "The Mean Guy" Rucker) defeated the Biblical Powers (Savior and CWO Light Heavyweight champion Moses) in 25:32 when Johnson pinned Moses after a tackle in which he speared his shoulder into Moses' groin. Bloodbath pinned Sin after Sin missed a flying headbutt in 14:19. Highlanders (Conor and Gavin McCloud) defeated D. E. A. T. H. (Benton and Green) in 15:31 when Gavin pinned Benton after the Quickening (superplex by Gavin, flying splash by Conor). CWO TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Gold Kard defeated champion Maximum Overdrive by disqualification in 8:11 when Brent "The Ace" Alles attacked Gold Kard CWO WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: The Dark Side (Hellhound and CWO World Tag Team Champions Creatures of the Night, Night Crawler and Night Slinger) defeated Revolution (Black Death, Cake Boy, and Lafayette) in 7:43 when Hellhound pinned Black Death after a vertical suplex. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Tiger Rogers upset champion Hile Troy in 10:08, pinning him after an Inverted Powerbomb after Troy was struck by an empty champagne bottle by Sandman. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Eternal Wanderers (Exile and Exodus) defeated the Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jim "Jazzman" Taylor) in 22:09 when Exile pinned Jumbo after the End of the World (Exile puts opponent in a Boston crab, Exodus comes off the top rope with a moonsault). _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in Middlebury, IN, let's look at the great card planned for our midwest fans tonight! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 22, 1997 MIDDLEBURY, IN WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Master of Darkness Damion (champion) vs. "Innovator of Violence" Miguel Sanchez WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night (champions) vs. The Highlanders TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Joe Eagle vs. Maximum Overdrive (Champion) Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. Black Death Hell's Pets (Dark Raven and Hellhound) vs. Alliance (Inferno and Tiger Rogers) Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Panther Regulators vs. Revolution (Lafayette and Cake Boy) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now take you to comments from CWO superstars. Of course, everybody's minds are on the big PPV coming this Saturday night from the Rosemont Horizon in Chicago, IL, TRIPLE THREAT, where every match involves three entities from the CWO, either singles or tag. One of the great matches involves the CWO North American Tag Team Championships, and we have comments from the current champions, the Eternal Wanderers. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Exodus and Lord Guru are coming out of a shadowed building. Exodus has a dark bag over his shoulder.) EXODUS: My Lord... How much longer? LORD GURU (solemn expression): He is ready, Exile can be let loose after the Triple Threat. EXODUS: My lord... (pulling out the belts) we have honored thee, and these are our tithe. We serve you... (looks at the camara) Bad Guys, Pit Bossess... (chuckles) you dare claim what we hold, you dare step in the ring with us? Let there be a reckoning. For the Eternal Wanderers are the North American Tag champs... and there is nothing that will stand in our way! LORD GURU: I have a message for the Biblical Powers. (He opens up a scroll.) LORD GURU: Savior... we will have our dance, it matters not where.. WWAW, UXW or here... for a REVELATIONS is coming, and you will face your REDEMPTION. (Smiles) Get ready for a rude awakening. Let us go... (As they turn, the camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tiger Rogers' surprise upset of Hile Troy has started quite a buzz about this young man, notwithstanding that Sandman may have been largely responsbile for his victory, smashing a champagne bottle over Troy's head. However, there is no denying that the Alliance may finally be on the verge of greatness in the CWO, we go to comments from them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Alliance is standing in the back of the CWO locker room.) JIMBO JONES: CWO! The Alliance has been silent for quite a while now... But, the time has come to make our presence felt.... INFERNO: At Triple Threat, I will defeat Flyboy & Steel... I will take care of both of you, in short time... and in a short time, I will make my way to the CWO World Title... and make the Alliance to the greatest stable in CWO history! TIGER ROGERS: Phantom Freightrain... Black Death... Your time has come to meet Tiger Rogers... At Triple Threat, the CWO will realize why the Alliance is as good as they are, why we will gain every title in the CWO... the Alliance will rule the CWO! DAVID "DESTROYER" DAVIS: Tag teams of the CWO, get ready to meet David & Goliath... We're ready to meet any and all comers... and we're starting at Triple Threat. CWO, get ready, the Alliance is here! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn our attention to the tragic death of CWO superstar Wayne Carpenter. Many of the superstars in the CWO have had reactions to this sad event, speaking out about it now are Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo and Brian "The Hammer" Williams. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Schillo and Williams are in the interview area.) WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: First Sandman, now this. Don't you guys get it? It's just show business! This is going TOO far! Whenever they find out who's responsible for this, I want them in a steel cage, a 60 minute Iron Man match. Whoever is responsible for this needs to pay dearly, and as someone who tries to stand for what's right in the CWO, I'm someone who plans on collecting that payment. This sort of behavior is intolerable. BRIAN "THE HAMMER" WILLIAMS: I may be new to this fed, but I have had the opportunity to meet Wayne Carpenter at bars after house shows, and he was one of the most wonderful men in the CWO. I'm apalled at what has happened, and I too want a piece of the killer. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: A question on everybody's minds these days is: are the Tower of Power and Russell Hill headed for a reconciliation? The ToP seemed dissatisfied with Hill's performances, however, he then went on to win Manager of the Year. So, the ToP definitely has some questions to answer to themselves, but for now, we go to comments from them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Tower of Power are all in a dark, smoke filled room. They are discussing the future of the CWO.) JOE EAGLE: All I'm sayin' is that, no matter what we do, the rankings never seem to improve. Let's just go out there and give the fans what they want... the best ever from the TOP! VINCENT "THE LIP" MANOCELLI: I think you're right, Joe. These are the BEST fans in the WORLD! JIM "JAZZMAN" TAYLOR: We're finally getting back to title shots here in the CWO, and the Wanderers better look out. The Big Cool's gonna put the big freeze on 'em! JUMBO JENKINS: Pass the gravy? EAGLE: And, as for the PPV, there's too many people to list. We'll just have to beat 'em ALL! Whaddya say boyz...ready to go? MANOCELLI: Let's get 'em! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Two men making an impact upon tag team wrestling here in the CWO are the Regulators. With a surprise victory over a team that was high in the vote-getting for the CWO Tag Team of the Year Awards, the Deadly Sins, the Regulators have shown that they have come to be noticed. Along with the Wild Ones, Mondo Gecko and Battlecat, they hope to make '97 their year. Let's go to comments from all four men. _______________________________________________________________________________ REGULATOR FIRE: Revolution, you two will be crushed as we roll over you in our path to find tag team gold. Lafayette & Cake Boy, get ready to experience hell on earth, because we will regulate the law around here in the CWO! REGULATOR ICE: Revolution, you two may think you will be involved in a battle, but it will be a glorified slaughter by the Regulators. We have stated that the tag team belts will be ours this year. And we will do anything to win the belts, by any means necessary! BATTLECAT: Tiger Rogers, last time we met, you beat me, this time, however, will be different. '97 is going to be a new year for me, for I am more experienced and prepared now for you. Tiger Rogers, I have a bunch of new moves that will knock you out. This year I am now going to pursue the tag belts and the T.V. title belt and I am going to start with you, Tiger Rogers. Tiger Rogers, we will see who is the real King of the Jungle! MONDO GECKO: What up, dudes! It's a great year for the Wild Ones, as I am challenging Bloodbath to a match on Wednesday since he has an open contract. Bloodbath, you are going to be in for a shock as I give you a real attitude adjustment when I Frankensteiner you all over the ring. Moses, you have something I want badly and that is the Light Heavyweight belt. Even though the belt isn't on the line, once I beat you for the strap you are going to have to give me a title shot. I am the true daredevil of the CWO and the people's choice for the federation. So look out! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Mondo Gecko stepping up to fill the open contract for Bloodbath on Wednesday, we'll have to see how he will do against the monster of a man from Death Valley. However, let's take a moment to comment on that open contract situation. The reason Bloodbath had an open contract was because the CWO was still in contract negotiations with the wrestler known as Ganglord. However, those contact negotiations have finally been finished, and Ganglord will be rejoining the CWO. We've all seen how Ganglord put CWO superstar Tub-o-Blub out of wrestling, probably for good, and he then continued his reign of terror by putting Wildfire on the DL as well. However, Wildfire's "friend" Bloodbath wants to gain revenge on the man from Sacramento, CA. Let's go to comments from Bloodbath. _______________________________________________________________________________ (A dim red light is shown on a shadow of a man.) BLOODBATH (low mysterious voice): Let this be a lesson, CWO. I'm here for one reason. REVENGE! I don't care who I have to go through, but I will have revenge! Ganglord, I am a man from your past! And I am back to claim your life! Ganglord, I travel through the darkside! You won't know when or where I will strike! But when I do, I will make sure you rest in peace! (the camera fades out as the mysterious bells begin to toll) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That last part's not a copyright infringement, is it? Oh well. Finally, we end our REPORT today with comments from the Dark Side, who head into TRIPLE THREAT with some very tough matches. As we take you back to last night's FEVER, you can see how MOD laid out Johnny Reb in the ring while the lights were off. However, Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo then rushed the ring, and delivered a Keuka Crack that topple the big man out of the ring. As Schillo tried to help Reb up, the disorientated Reb thought that Schillo was the one who attacked him, and DDTed the young man from Syracuse, and put him in the Texas Cloverleaf until CWO officials tore him off of Schillo. We have not received comment from either of those two men about the situation, but you can be sure that the Master of Darkness is very pleased about how his human game of chess progresses. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens in a dark room. Within the shadows, several figures can be made out, the Creatures of the Night, Hellhound, Dark Raven, and Master of Darkness Damion. MOD Damion begins to speak.) MOD DAMION: The new year is here, the CWO is back and The Dark Side is still here to rule this federation. Tonight, all of The Dark Side will see action as I get my minions prepared for the Triple Threat PPV. Tonight my Creatures face a team who thinks they should be wearing my World Tag Team championship belts... they are gravely mistaken. These belts belong to the Dark Side and no one shall ever remove them. They are locked in the DARKNESS that surrounds me, that is all powerful. NIGHT SLINGER: Highlanders, like my master has said, we own these belts, no one can take them from us. We have faced all the competition the CWO has served up for us and we devoured it. Leaving no leftovers for anyone. We are the best team in the CWO and we have the award to prove it. NIGHT CRAWLER: Highlanders, we felt that a match with you would give us an easy victory to start the new year and give us a good boost going to the PPV. Tonight we will let it all hang on the line, this is a non-title match so we have nothing to lose. We will show every team in the CWO that the Creatures of the Night are still the team to beat and the most powerful and dominant force in the league. MOD DAMION: Yes, yes and also my newest creation, Hell's Pets will see action as the improved Hellhound and the always great Dark Raven face a couple of foes from the past in the Alliance. HELLHOUND: Yes, Master I am better I have improved. I ate a special diet over the holidays and now I feel stronger than ever and I will show Tiger Rodgers and Inferno just how strong I am as me and Raven work our way to the top of the Tag ranks to get you more gold, Master. MOD DAMION: That's right, my pet, more gold is just what I want. As for myself, I have the pleasure of reintroducing Miguel Sanchez to the CWO champ myself. Sanchez, I will continue to reign supreme and you will just fall to the wayside as the DARKNESS I control rolls right over you. ALL FOUR (Dark Raven is silent): LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera closes on those words.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's it for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
