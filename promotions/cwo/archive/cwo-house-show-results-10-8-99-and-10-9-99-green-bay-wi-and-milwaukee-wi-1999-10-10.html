House Show Results - 10/8/99 and 10/9/99 - Green Bay, WI and Milwaukee, WI

October 10, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 10/8/99 Green Bay, WI 'Roast Beef' Jason Allanson pinned Hollywood Haskins after screwed up outside interference by Iceman X. Time of match: 6:39. Iceman X and
Hollywood Haskins argued after the match. Magic Group defeated Creatures of the Night when Necromancer pinned Night
Crawler after the Salad Bowl. Time of match: 6:19.
Mustafa Rahim and Brent 'The Ace' Alles wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Beauregard Boys defeated Yakuza when Shirley Beauregard pinned Secret Agent
Super Dragon after the Option 73. Time of match: 9:55.
In a match pitting Finisher vs. Finisher, Carr pinned Foolkiller after the
Fatality. (The match had no disqualifications, so the move was legal.)
This came after outside interference by Gecko. Time of match: 14:32. Syne pinned Wildfire after the X-iled (reversed Russian legsweep). Time of match: 12:20. Gecko/Lt. Cammy LeBeau defeated Gold Kard/German Choklat when Gecko pinned
German Choklat after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 12:44.
'The Main Event' Steve Davis pinned Lord Hile Troy after the belly to belly suplex into a bridge. Time of match: 11:47.
World Champion Allen 'Mr. America' Carter and Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky defeated the returned Deadly Sins when Geoff 'The Attitude
Adjuster' Bondavarsky pinned Pride after the Rhino Charge. Time of match:
21:56. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 10/9/99 Milwaukee, WI Wizard made 'The Jaw Jacking Gigolo' Andrew Dixon submit to the Shoulder
Separator. Time of match: 13:15.
Foolkiller pinned Deliverance after the power bomb. Time of match: 10:21. Gecko pinned Necromancer after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 16:16. Master of Darkness Damion/Night Crawler/Night Slinger defeated Doomsday
(Overdrive/Mercenaries) when Night Slinger pinned Korperal Punishment after the Lights Out avalanche. Time of match: 16:44.
Sandman Jr. pinned Television Champion Iceman X in a non-title street fight after the Permanent Dream flying legdrop. Time of match: 23:28.
CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Man Mountain Marko pinned Simon St. Severe after the DDT. Time of match: 14:46. Man Mountain Marko retained the CWO North American Champion title. Man Mountain Marko ws attacked after the match by the Brute Squad! That brought the Hardcore Sensation down to make the save, and their match then began with the Brute Squad.
CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Brute Squad defeated Hardcore Sensation when Black Jack Hollister pinned
'Hard Impact' Kurt Insanity after the Texas Stampede. Time of match: 24:05.
Brute Squad retained the CWO North American Tag Team Champions title. World Champion Allen 'Mr. America' Carter made Union Caine submit to the
American Dream. Time of match: 26:59.
