CWO REPORT 2/9/97

9 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO Control Room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, February 9, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the superstars of the CWO were in Madison Square Garden in New York, NY, bringing the Big Apple the purest form of wrestling excitement. Let's look at how the card went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 2/8/97 New York. NY (Madison Square Garden) The Coldfront defeated Hell's Pets when Kurt 'Thrill from the Chill' Gerstung pinned Dark Raven after the Icepick (DDT). Time of match: 7:43. Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned Hile Troy after the throat punch. Time of match: 11:16. 'Master of Darkness' Damion delivered a Darkness Falls to Hile Troy outside the ring before Bentley pinned him. Cake Boy made Baby Waldo submit to the Head Scissors. Time of match: 13:54. Maximum Overdrive pinned Phantom Freightrain after the Pileup (power bomb). Time of match: 5:09. Mercenaries defeated Revolution when Korperal Punishment pinned Lafayette after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 19:28. Eternal Wanderers defeated Pit Bosses by countout. Time of match: 5:54. Panther made Masher Maccabee submit to the Eclipse (combination Oriental thumb and sleeper). Time of match: 10:23. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Gold Kard pinned Miguel Sanchez after the Die Flieder Kross. Time of match: 4:21. Gold Kard was attacked by Sandman after the match. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wayne 'Keuka Guy' Schillo pinned 'Lonestar' Mike Williams after rolling over the Lonestar Press. Time of match: 11:03. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Don't forget the great SHOWCASE we have this week, coming out later today. Let's take a look at the lineup for two great hours of wrestling. _______________________________________________________________________________ SHOWCASE 2/9/97 Kurt Gerstung vs. Ron Johnson Johnny Reb vs. David Davis Sandman Junior vs. Sin Mondo Gecko vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley Eternal Wanderers vs. Pit Bosses Panther vs. Dark Raven Hile Troy vs. Tiger Rogers LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE (no dq): Moses (champion) vs. Talon _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: One of the CWO superstars really making a run towards the top lately is Cake Boy. This bizarre individual has only one defeat so far in his tenure in the CWO. We take you now to comments from Cake Boy. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Cake Boy is in the interview area, stroking Toodles the Chimp, his "manager.") CAKE BOY: Hey all you big burly men with your big bulging muscles, if you're looking for an opponent... why don't you try me on for size... I think you'll see I fit... real tight... (Cake Boy gives a big smooch to the camera.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR (muttering): That guy gives me the creeps. Anyway, we have another update on the continuing friendly challenge between CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Moses and CWO World Champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo. Moses made the inital challenge, a "belt vs. belt" match. Schillo responded, saying that it had to be a strap match. We now have a return from Moses in this volley. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Moses is watching the CWO Report back in his locker room, and listens to Wayne Schillo's response to his challenge. He hears it, knows the camera is there, and immediately makes his response known.) MOSES: A Strap Match? Hmm... well, it sounds good to me. I know that everything you said was true, about the Heavyweight Division being a totally different ballgame, but I know I'm ready to handle it. You don't know who I've been wrestling, and where I've been wrestling, but, let me tell you, I can handle it. On Feb. 16, my oppenent at an unsanctioned event in another fed will be 7'7, 585 pounds. I know what I'm doing in the ring, and let me tell you this, Strap Match, in a cage to make sure none of your enemies, or my enemies interferes, sounds like a great idea to me. Schillo, you've got yourself a deal. (He grabs his duffel bag, and walks off.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We will definitely keep you posted on further developments in this scenario, especially when and where this match will take place. However, Moses' partner in the top-ranked Biblical Powers tag team, Savior, also had some comments for us, of a very shocking nature. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Savior is standing in a cemetery, wondering amongst the gravestones. He stops beside an open grave, one which has no name on it, but instead, the date of death, which is February 23, 1997. He looks up at the camera and begins to speak.) THE SAVIOR: I have been doing a lot of thinking lately. Thinking about my past, my present, my future, wondering why I'm even here. I still don't know why I'm here, and I have come to a decision. As of March 1, 1997, I have retired from the sport of professional wrestling. All three federations that I currently compete in will be notified of this decision, and I decided to tell the CWO first. I would like to go out in a blaze of glory, however, which is why I challenge whomever wears the CWO World Championship Belt on February 28 to give me a non-title match-up at the House show. This is just so I can prove myself as a competitor, and that's why it's non-title. After that, The Biblical Powers will no longer exist as a team, and I will be nothing but a bystander, guiding the career of my younger brother, Moses. The Evangelist already knows of this decision, and he has accepted that his services will no longer be needed. That is all for today. (He walks off through the graveyard into the church, which was in the background.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Savior apparently making a very personal decision to give up the spotlight, for reasons yet unknown. Savior was a great competitor in the ring, and we feel fortunate that he will continue on in at least a diminshed capacity here in the CWO. Now, however, we turn our attention to one of the big matches at this week's upcoming PPV, ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, Friday night at 9:00 PM, "live" from the Cow Palace in San Francisco, CA. Originally, Sandman was supposed to face "Monster" Rich Clark for the North American title in a match that was dubbed "the Monsters of the Ring." However, Sandman lost the North American title to Gold Kard on last week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Gold Kard was set to go up against the CWO's own "Innovator of Violence", Miguel Sanchez, at the same PPV, but Gold Kard thought he could possibly buy Sanchez off, making him a member of Gold Kard International. We take you back to last week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Footage is shown from the 2/4/97 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, as Gold Kard is in the ring.) GOLD KARD: But anyway, that is now why I'm here tonight. I am here tonight to welcome the newest member into Gold Kard International! Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome the "Innovator of Violence," Miguel Sanchez! ("Six Foot Foam Skull" by Shallow, North Dakota plays as Miguel Sanchez makes his entrance, the Biblical Powers behind him.) MELINN: What?!? THOMAS: I don't believe it! MELINN: Miguel Sanchez apparently the newest member of Gold Kard International, and he's brought the Biblical Powers with him. This is a shocking development, to say the least. (The Pit Bosses hold the ropes for Miguel, as he enters the ring, followed by the Powers.) GOLD KARD: Herr Sanchez, all I have to say is, welcome to the fold! Now, your first official duty as a member of this great organization will be to tear up this contract, a contract that laughingly says that you will face me at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II. As we all know, Gold Kard International members do not fight each other, we protect each other! (Miguel Sanchez takes the mike.) MIGUEL SANCHEZ: You know, never let it be said that the "innovator of violence" doesn't do his homework. And I've been talking to someone, someone you know real well, Gold Kard. And that man is "Master of Pain" Barry Hart. GOLD KARD: Yes, what of it? SANCHEZ: He had some real interesting things to say, about how this organization is run, and about how the "employees" of Gold Kard International are treated. You see, he told me that he was basically just a pawn on your chessboard. GOLD KARD: I don't like where this is going, Herr Sanchez. Herr Hart could not take the pressure, so he left. We all know that you can take the pressure, you have proven yourself here in battle many times. But do not underestimate the value of this off er, Herr Sanchez, of the power that it can give you. SANCHEZ (chuckling): Power. Yeah, right. Last time I checked, you don't have any power here at all! (Crowd cheers as Gold Kard glares at Sanchez.) SANCHEZ: So what my final decision is, you could give me a million dollars, you could give me a billion dollars, it wouldn't be enough. When we meet at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, I am going to kick your @$$, buddy, and that belt now around your wai st only makes the deal a lot sweeter. Let's go boys. ("Six Foot Foam Skull" by Shallow, North Dakota, plays as the fans cheer as Sanchez and the Biblical Powers leave as Gold Kard has a fit in the ring.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Miguel Sanchez is standing in the middle of MSG, with most of the lights off, and the ring empty. He looks to be deep in thought.) MIGUEL SANCHEZ: Gold Kard, I get my chance now. February 14, Valentine's Day. San Francisco, California. The Cow Palace. It is my time to shine, right on PPV. I now must ask one thing. I would like a table match. This is my only request, I hope to see it granted. Table Match. Gold Kard, what do you think of that? Is that not just fun, or what (laughs)? C'mon, you have be willing to do something along those lines, it's just proper, man. You know that you want it. Just do it, what have you got to lose, except that CWO North American Title that you've got around your waist. (He goes into the dressing room area, and we hear some commotion.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now have a response to those taped comments from Gold Kard. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Gold Kard is in the interview area, flanked by the Pit Bosses.) GOLD KARD: First of all, dumbkopf Sanchez, how dare you make demands of me?!? I am the North American champion for the second time! This continent has to deal with the fact that a foreigner, that a German, posseses the symbol of excellence of this country. As I have stated many times before, I am the master wrestler. There is no disputing this. So how dare you, Herr Sanchez, make demands of me? The slave does not make demands of the master. However, Herr Sanchez, you say you are a student of history, at least in this league. Well, Herr Sanchez, I too am a student of history, and I know that when you won your first title in this league, that you did it by beating that dumbkopf Alles in a table match, to capture the Television championship, a lesser belt, if I do say so myself. So, many would say that I should be afraid of facing you in such a match. However, I see it another way, that this is a test of my abilities, of my supremeness here in this organization. When I beat you, when I humiliate you, in that table match, not only will I show my superiority over you, but also over that dumbkopf Alles. We will see who REALLY was the mastermind of the plot to take over the CWO, which won 1996 Story of the Year, I don't mind mentioning. So, Herr Sanchez, in this one case I shall grant the slave a boon from his master, I will face you in a Table Match at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE, and at the end, when you lay on your back, splinters of wood piercing your flesh, pain clouding your vision, you will know that you have been defeated by the master wrestler, the symbol of excellence on this continent, to sum up in two words: GOLD KARD! Good day, Herr Sanchez. (Gold Kard spits on the ground as he and the Pit Bosses leave.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
