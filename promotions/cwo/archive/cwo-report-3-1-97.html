CWO REPORT 3/1/97

1 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Regulators are in the interview area.) REGULATOR FIRE: Mason-Dixon, you good ol' boys are one tough team but you guys are going up against the new generation superstars. We were brought here into the CWO to do one thing, and that is regulate justice and become the dominant force in wrestling. Mason-Dixon, we respect you two for following the rules and being talented, but you will go down at the hands of the Regulators. REGULATOR ICE: I love Ireland, home of the Irish, leprachauns, and the pot of gold. Mason-Dixon, when we travel to Dublin, it will be a long night for you two as we regulate some justice for the CWO. You guys are two tough hombres and we love a good clean match, but we will destroy you guys and continue our quest for tag team domination. We will regulate the CWO for JUSTICE WILL BE SERVED! _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Big Cool are in the interview area.) JIM "JAZZMAN" TAYLOR: Highlanders, you couldn't get it done last night in Scotland. Don't think that things are gonna get easier tonight, when the Big Cool comes at you. JUMBO JENKINS: Gavin... Conor... I've never eaten anything from the Nordic Lands... hope it doesn't taste like fish... JAZZMAN: Highlanders, you're gonna feel the power of the Big Cool, and the Twer of Power! In the words of Joe Eagle, "If you're in my way, you're goin' down!" _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb and General Lee are in the interview area.) JOHNNY REB: Tiguh Rogers, ta'naht y'all gotta stare down the barrel of a gun that is cocked, loaded, an' ready ta fire!! Y'all wear gold now son, an' in this league, that puts a bullseye the size of Alabama on yo' back. Well, Rogers, Johnny Reb is packin' the heat, and ta'naht in Dublin, Ireland, ah will take the CWO Light Heavyweight Championship. GENERAL LEE: And don't think that we all don't do our homework! Wee seen how y'all won that belt. The only thing keepin' that around yo' waist is that pack'a goons y'all run with. Well, the back-up is on call, so don't be gettin' no ideas. That belt, and yo' dreams are gonna belong ta' Johnny Reb. REB: Y'all lahk ta slug it out, Rogers? Well, come climb in the guttuh with Johnny Reb. Come an' get some boy. LEE: Good day, suh! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens in a dark ally, there are several shadows that seem to move about as the camera pans around looking for the interviewers to show. Suddenly, as if out of nowhere, the Creatures of the Night and MOD Damion appear.) MOD DAMION: You see, no one owns the night like I do. Bishop, you may have momentarily caused me some pain and physical damage with your fire, but as for your wrestling skills, you need to try harder if you plan on attempting to enter my Night and take me on. As for you, Pit Bosses, you think you know what DARKNESS is, you think you know what it is like to walk in our eternal night, well tonight you may know more than you want as my Creatures tear you apart. NIGHT SLINGER: Pit Bosses, we have danced the dance before and then we led and tonight nothing will be different. We will do the leading and the two of you will do the learning. NIGHT CRAWLER: After tonight your ignorance will be forgiven as we take payment out of your bodies. You will truly be able to say that you know what a walk with the Dark Side really means. MOD DAMION: Tonight, Pit Bosses, we show you why you were just the NA tag champs and not the World tag team champs. That title belongs to me and the Dark Side. ALL THREE: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (With that the three men disapear just as suddenly they appeared. Suddenly there is a short loud male scream as the camera falls to show the camera man running away.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo is in the interview area.) WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: Savior, tonight we wrestle in your retirement match. Win or lose, I wish you the best of luck in whatever endeavors you pursue after the CWO. You were 1/2 of the almost unbeatable Biblical Powers, and you've proven to be quite a good wrestler. Wherever you may go after this, remember that Keuka is never far away, and since that ratio's 8 to 1, you're more than welcome to come by. Good luck. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Saturday, March 1. I'm Johnny Starr. Our British Invasion tour continues this week, last night stopping in Dublin, Ireland, let's see the results of that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS Friday, 2/28/97 Dublin, Ireland 'Lonestar' Mike Williams and 'Master of Darkness' Damion wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 6:27. David and Goliath and the Mercenaries wrestled to a 30 minute time limit draw. Hellhound and Inferno wrestled to a double countout. Time of match: 1:46. The Big Cool defeated Highlanders by countout. Time of match: 4:56. The Focus pinned Benjamin Blake Bentley after the Focal Point. Time of match: 13:20. Mason-Dixon and Regulators wrestled to a double countout. Time of match: 8:39. CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb defeated Tiger Rogers by countout. Time of match: 19:44. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, taped fist match): Champion Masher Maccabee pinned 'Master of Darkness' Damion after the flying elbow drop. Time of match: 24:53. CWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Champions the Creatures of the Night defeated Pit Bosses when Night Slinger pinned Pit Boss #2 after the Lights Out avalanche. Time of match: 14:56. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Champion Wayne 'Keuka Guy' Schillo pinned Savior after the Keuka Crack. Time of match: 18:45. After the match Schillo and Savior shook hands and embraced, as Savior received a standing ovation from the crowd as several CWO wrestlers came down to the ring and wished him well. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: A very emotional farewell last night to a CWO superstar, Savior, fighting the match of his life before his retirement. Wayne Schillo may have come out on top, but I'll bet that when Schillo recounts his toughest matches, that this one will be right up there. Our best wishes to Savior in whatever lies ahead for him. Tonight, the CWO wraps up its British Invasion tour, stopping in Belfast, North Ireland, before heading to Paris, France next Tuesday for TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Let's look at the lineup for tonight's North Ireland card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW MARCH 1, 1997 BELFAST, NORTH IRELAND Arlon Flaym vs. Tommy Lightning David "The Destroyer" Davis vs. Moses Inferno vs. Mondo Gecko Kurt Gerstung vs. Panther MOD Damion vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley Gold Kard vs. Johnny Reb Mercenaries vs. Deadly Sins TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Bloodbath vs. Masher Maccabee (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night (champions) vs. Big Cool _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Inferno, you had your butt tossed over the ropes personally by me during the battle royal. Now I know you're still a little bit hot under the collar about that, but guess what, I don't give a damn. I am going to fly all over the ring and dropkick you into the next millenium. It will be a long night for you in the land of the leprachauns when I get Mondo Extreme on you, baby. Belfast, North Ireland, home to one of the great cruiserweight champions in the world, Dave Findlay. Well, get ready, you fun-loving Irish, because Mondo Gecko is going to eat, drink and be merry at the expense of the walking cigarette lighter. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Yesterday we saw another episode in the continuing mindgames between Brent "The Ace" Alles and Maximum Overdrive, who will meet in a Lumberjack match at SPRING BREAK '97 next Friday, only on Play Per View. We now go to comments from "The Ace." _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is an Irish pub, having a few good pints of Guiness as the Chieftains do a rare pub performance, playing a sporty gigue.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: Ah, these are good times. This British Invasion tour has been a lot of fun, I have always admired this country, Ireland, its beauty, its mystery, and especially its Guiness. (Alles drains the pint and continues.) ALLES: All right, let's get down to business. Overdrive, this is turning into a real Saturday Night Live between us, isn't it? I poke a little fun at your upbringing, you poke a little fun at me in return. The only thing is, Overdrive, I struck first, as I always do, and I still have the upper hand, pal. You see, everything I do is a calculated risk. Put you through a table with an Allesbreaker? Sure, it might injure me, but now I know, Overdrive, that in the back of mind you fear that move like no other. The Pileup? A power bomb? Who doesn't use a power bomb these days? But for pure originality, you turn to the best damn finisher in the sport, the Allesbreaker, created, honed, and perfected by yours truly. (The Chieftains finish playing, as the pub crowd applauds appreciatively.) ALLES: Now that little skit you pulled yesterday, you want to pretend that was me who jumped out the window and left my wallet for you thugs to use, fine, you go ahead and live in your little world of delusion. However, let me stress two things. 1) It only proves what I've been saying all along, that you and your goons are nothing but a bunch of petty thugs that Schillo has in his back pocket to "protect" him. You've meant nothing to this sport, and you never will. 2) You are so worried about this match we've got coming up that you will stoop to any level to get at me. Too late, Overdrive, I've already got you, and come SPRING BREAK '97, you bring your goons, I've got the Unholy Alliance behind me. Do I trust them? Hardly. But I know that they hate you guys like nobody else. So if you fall out in their part of the ringside, Overdrive, you'd better beware, because you've got a fire-throwing maniac, a guy who thinks he's a vampire, a mute killer, and a guy who thinks he's a snake all ready to do damage to you. On your side? That fat piece of nothing Waldo, and those two goofs who spend most of their time these days whining about not getting title shots, the Mercenaries. Hey, guys, you know why you don't get title shots? Because you're losers. Plain and simple. Just like your "master," Overdrive. We're down to less than a week, Overdrive. Try not to bite off your fingernails in fear. (Alles orders another pint as the Chieftains begin another rousing song.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's it for today. Be sure to tune in to the premiere of SATURDAY NIGHT FIGHTIN' and SHOWCASE tomorrow. We'll also be back tomorrow with the REPORT, so until then, we'll see you at ringside! (Closing creds and music come up as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ Copyright 1997 Just B Cuz Productions http://web.syr.edu/~bgalles/cwo.html Cyberspace Wrestling Organization (CWO) All Rights Reserved
