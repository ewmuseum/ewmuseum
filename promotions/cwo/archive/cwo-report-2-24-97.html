CWO REPORT 2/24/97

24 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Monday, February 24, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Tomorrow night is the dawning of a new format for TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, let's look at the lineup! _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 2/24/97 HOUSTON, TX HOUR ONE: _________ Steel vs. Bane NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Arlon Flaym vs. "Monster" Rich Clark (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (no dq, no co, no tl): Biblical Powers vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) HOUR TWO: _________ Ganglord vs. Joe Eagle 8-MAN MATCH: Unholy Alliance (Dark Raven, Serpentmaster, Bishop, and Vlad the Impaler) vs. Doomsday (Maximum Overdrive, Baby Waldo, Mercenaries) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung vs. Masher Maccabee (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: A newcomer has arrived in the CWO, and his name is Centurion. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The icon of Castle Revelstone. Cut away thousands of years ago by giants of the land, it stands as a minor blemish on its face. Set in an adamant background of the Westron Range, it's security is of the utmost in the world. No Earthly force, no man made force, and certainly no physical force could bring it harm. Its rock is of a solid grey granite. With great rock as it's father, the soothing ocean as it's mother, and the sun as it's guardian high above, this is the pinnacle of serenity. Past the great walls that protect the front from any invasion, massive bars stretch across the courtyard like cold teeth from some malevolant beast. The courtyard is now dimly lit as night has set fall. At regular intervals of about one hundred yards, guard towers stretch out from the main precipice of the walls, and then even beyond the courtyard walls lay a second internal wall, archers on the top of it as sentries, rotating in patrol. After all of this organized defense, in the most vertical spot of the Castle Revelstone, stretching from its main body, many spires of rock shoot out, these are the tower rooms which house the lords. Within one of the farthest towers facing the ocean, a small spot can be seen. Just as Revelstone is meager to the mountains, so is this man to the castle. Yet, he remains not only the leader of the Castle, and not only all of the mountains that surround, and the sea which crashes into them, but the entire land. Small only in sight, coercion would be wise when confronting the man, known by the name, Centurion. Upon entering his abode, it can be seen that Centurion has not veered from his usual appearence. Encompassed with fine robes, Centurion seems to be enveloped in aristocracy. Tied back into a tight ponytail that drops off at shoulder length is his hair, a dashing color of silver. All about the room are pictures, titles of nobility, various weapons of high efficiency, and in the back a glass case with his NWF title inside. The noticable factor is Centurion's eyes, orbs of emerald proficiancy locked at attention in narrow slits. Centurion holds in his hands a small hourglass which watches the event of the gravity occur once more sand trickling from top to bottom. Holding the hourglass at eye level, the man peers to it almost as if he were trying to examine each individual grain of sand, to watch its destiny as it becomes weighed down from the fine dust that surrounds it, and is then run through pressure which exists in the middle, before trickling into the bottom, being buried by its brothers. The Centurion nods his head wickedly.) THE CENTURION: The Lords are taught to learn much from the Earth. We do more than live on it and watch it as life trickles by, we study it, utilize it, live it. Live. There is much that can be learned, as is evident by the events before my eyes. You see, the Earth does not lie, it knows not of corruption, greed, hatred. Not the hate that the race of man brings to it. Factories, industries, chemicals, malevolance, hatred. Hate. It only knows of the rules which is lives by. Creation, life, death, and the most important law of all... the law of time. You see, time is the most powerful force in the world. It is measurable by the other laws and forces, life and death. You live from birth to death, and time determines how long. Time can be measured only in one's life, not in its stores of power. How inappropriate, so many of us lead such useless lives not worthy of time's mention. Time is so powerful, that it is not even tangible to the wicked hand of mankind. Seas, oceans, trees, animals, each other, these are all things that humans can harness, use and make advantage of. Waste. But time, time does to us what we do to the rest. Time is so powerful that man's evil footprints cannot set down on it's beaches. Time toys with us, as we destroy everything else. Time cannot be destroyed, it cannot be created, it can be only compromised. It is the one thing you always beg for more of, but yet at the same time always waste in the most boisterious and flamboyant ways. (Locking his fingers around the timeglass, the man again knocks the glass over. This time, however, as the grains trickle down to the narrow catacombs of the middle, and then to the abyssal defeat at the bottom, his eyebrow raises in interrogation, and he now has noticed something that strikes him as mysterious as his face contorts about and the veins in his forehead pulsate from the back of his head to the front as his mind tries to deduce, uncover and foreclose on what his eyes have told him. Flipping the glass over for a final time, Centurion leans back into his chair, pondering the question that lays before him. He appears to be struggling with a question that he cannot quite describe the answer to, and the difficulty annoys him. Until he arises.) THE CENTURION: This has occured to me, that the challenges of life themselves can be explained by this device. Man is born free, and is everywhere in chains, wise words from a wise man. So as mankind is born into this world, he shall begin at the very top of the Earth, pure and unscathed by the vile tendencies of his race. However, as time eats away at his life, his purity quickly falls victim to the greed and utter crippling determination of his race. He sinks towards the middle, into the gauntlet, where the best competitors will stay, and the weaker shall fall. Social Darwinism if you wish? Mankind will be in a rush to get his goals across. Those of us that are weaker, that have something to prove, that have unset accomplishments, things that have not been done. People in this world, people of the CWO. You know that time is ticking on you my friends, now you must ALL face the Centurion at one time! You know that your dilemmas with each other are not over with, and before you end all of your petty feuds with each other, you must all face Centurion. I will run over all of you untrained and undisciplined men. Schillo, Bane, Damion, Sandman, all figures of religion, Alles, Gold Kard, Rebels, Rich Clark, men, spirits, dead and roaming individuals, all shall fall to me. I will walk through the ranks of the CWO, and you shall all tremble and quake from the wrath that I will bring. I will move my pieces and then I shall capture the final endgame, checkmate. It is my destiny, my task to sit and familiarize the CWO with the rules of time, life and death both, wrapped into one powerful force. I am aware of my mortality, in the one simple physical sense. Mentality, spiritually, I am immortal, unbeaten. (As the last words escape from the man's mouth, his eyes quickly explode from the concentrated slits that they were to large circles, churning with thoughts of domination. White fingers gnarl around the sides of the glass, tight, using avarice and bitter hatred. Then, leaning back, he grasps the glass which he learned with, and quickly propells it to the marble floor causing the glass to shatter about the room. The sand, light on the floor and motionless, lies beneath Centurion's robes. Sensing he has learned all he needs to accomplish his goals, he leans over and blows the sand about the room.) THE CENTURION: CWO, your time... is up. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We will definitely be looking forward to seeing that man compete in the squared circle sometime in the near future. However, turning to other matters, a very frustrated young man in the CWO currently is Wildfire, and we go to comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire in the locker room after his loss to Exile.) WILDFIRE: You just don't know when to quit, Ganglord! You want a stipulation for our match at Spring Break? Well how about an "I quit match"? If you think you're man enough to do it! Your torment is just beginning! Come Spring Break, I have a... (laughs a little) I will finally gain my revenge! I will put you out of the CWO! AMBER: What ya gonna do when the Wildfire runs wild on you! (The camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tomorrow night on FEVER, the Unholy Alliance collides with Doomsday in an eight-man tag match, let's go to comments from the leader of the Unholy Alliance, Bishop. BISHOP: Doomsday has a lot to talk about. Lots of talk. Talk has never impressed me very much, so talk all you want. You won't be talking quite so loud or acting quite so proud after we've taken you all, limb from limb. You will be dealt with. As for you, MOD, it was a very poor decision for you and Hellhound to attack my Raven. I would advise you to travel in large numbers keeping a close lookout behind you. You never know who or what is waiting around the corner. Soon, the Unholy Alliance will begin its reign of terror over this league. But enough talk, actions speak much louder than words. (Bishop ignites a giant burst of hellfire at the camera as it fuzzes out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Also on FEVER tomorrow night, Joe Eagle collides with Ganglord! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Joe Eagle is in the interview area.) JOE EAGLE: Oh, yeah, back where I belong! Tuesday Night Fever... the one place for the once and future king of the CWO! Fans, I owe all of you an apology. I have been... well, distracted for the past few months. But all of that is going to change, effective immediately. You are looking at a former world champion who is now just a shell of a man. I have found what has been missing from my life: the gold around my waist! And now is the time for my glorious rise to the top of the world! Ganglord, you better be ready for the toughest fight of your life, 'cause if you're not ready, you'll be in for a LONG night! Watch out, CWO...Joe Eagle's back and you're gonna be in TROUBLE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: If he didn't have enough troubles with Benjamin Blake Bentley, the Focus now makes a challenge to one half of the North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys, Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: Ron Johnson, eh? The rude guy, eh? I'm gonna make an example out of you. Do you want a ring lesson? Well buddy, you're gonna get one. I have got my sights on Benjamin Blake Bentley, and he's the only guy I'm worried about. Whatever opponents I'm put up against, I'm not worried about them. I worry about nothing. I've got the talent and the desire to do whatever I choose in the ring, and I choose to kick the crap out of one Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson. Got it? _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Earlier we heard from Bishop concerning his challenge towards Master of Darkness Damion and the Dark Side. Here now is a response from the Master of Darkness Damion. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on MOD and the Creatures of the Night who stand on either side of their master, holding their CWO tag titles.) MOD DAMION: Bishop, where do you get off just showing up and claiming to be the true ruler of the Darkness? This I have proved time and time again to everyone. I beat Sandman, who tried to lay claim to my Darkness, I beat Bane, who also thought he knew what Darkness meant and soon you and I will collide and I will once again prove to the world but mainly you that I am the one true "Master of Darkness." You steal my young prodigy Dark Raven. I heard you complimenting how much he has improved, think about it, that is all because of me, my tutelege. I made Dark Raven what he is, I made him the first 2 time Light Heavyweight Champ of the CWO and you take him from me and start to claim to be the reason for his success. Bishop, you and Dark Raven will pay for his betrayal and for your interferance. Lastly, Bishop, Hellhound is my pet, he will have no part in the Unholy Alliance. Stay away from him or pay with your life. Now to Tuesday Night Fever. Biblical Powers, you wanted my Creatures for a no DQ, no countout, no time limit match and you got it. Now is the time to pay. NIGHT SLINGER: Biblical Powers, you boys have picked the wrong time to get into this type of match with the Dark Side. Things are a little low right now with the Dark Side, the master is pissed about the loss of Dark Raven and the interference of the Unholy Alliance and you two will be his focus of revenge. NIGHT CRAWLER: Yes, Biblical Powers, despite the triumphant victory of "Monster" Rich Clark the master has not had much to be pleased about and tomorrow night we will give him something to pleased with. That is your heads! MOD DAMION: Biblical Powers, tomorrow night you suffer for the sins of the Unholy Alliance and then, CWO, I will take my frustrations out on all of you. ALL THREE MEN: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera closes on MOD, then fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Notice that Hellhound was nowhere to be seen in that group, you have to wonder whose dark influence will be greater, Bishop's, or the self-professed Master himself, Damion. We will keep you posted in the weeks to come, and remember, Master of Darkness Damion and Hellhound collide with Bishop and Dark Raven in an explosive tag team match at SPRING BREAK '97, coming up next Friday. Also on that great PPV will be a "Last Man Standing" match between Gold Kard, Johnny Reb, Serpentmaster, and Moses. A very interesting situation is developing between these four gentlemen, especially between Johnny Reb and Gold Kard. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Gold Kard is in the GKI boardroom, wearing an Armani suit and sipping champagne. German Choklat, a lovely young African-American woman also attired in a professional yet attractive business suit that teasingly displays a hint of cleavage, is massaging his shoulders.) GOLD KARD (chuckling maliciously): Johnny Reb, dumbkopf, what a whiner you are. All these months I have heard you whine about how you are cheated out of this, cheated out of that. Well, let me tell you, Herr Reb, I am obviously the top wrestler in this league, and yet I have not received one World title shot. Not one! We have all seen me beat that dumbkopf Schillo many times, and I'm... er... sure I can beat Bane... um... possibly. Anyway, enough of that. At SPRING BREAK '97, while the college dumbkopfs are drinking themselves into comas, I will be putting you into a coma, Johnny Reb, whether you are my reluctant partner or my enemy, I shall crush you as I shall crush the others, earning the title shot at APRIL ARMAGGEDON and truly being the last MAN standing. Bank on it, dumbkopfs, I may be all that's left of the once-great Gold Kard International, but I am truly all that is needed. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's it for the REPORT today, we'll see you tomorrow on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER!
