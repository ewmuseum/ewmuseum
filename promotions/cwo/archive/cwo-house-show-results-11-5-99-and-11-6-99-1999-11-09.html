House Show Results - 11/5/99 and 11/6/99

November 9, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 11/5/99 Lincoln, NE (Pershing Auditorium) Matsuhiro Torunaka pinned Vegas Villain after the Asai Moonsault. Time of match: 2:16. Kamikaze pinned Marc 'Compton' Daniels after the California Jam, pulling the tights. Time of match: 4:10.
Deliverance pinned 'The Jaw Jacking Gigolo' Andrew Dixon after the fisherman suplex. Time of match: 12:06.
Carr pinned Master of Darkness Damion after the Fatality. Time of match: 6:23. Hollywood X-Press defeated Magic Group when Hollywood Haskins pinned Necromancer after the Hollywood Bomb. Time of match: 13:09. Gold Kard pinned 'The Main Event' Steve Davis after the Die Flieder Kross. Time of match: 8:19. CWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Hardcore Sensation and Brute Squad wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 19:23. Brute Squad retained the CWO World Tag Team Champions title. Mustafa Rahim pinned Lord Hile Troy after the Mustafa's Missle. Time of match: 8:15. HOUSE SHOW 11/6/99 Salina, KS (Bicentennial Center) Matsuhiro Torunaka pinned Vegas Villain after the reversed rollup. Time of match: 2:54. Maximum Overdrive pinned North American Champion Man Mountain Marko in a non-title match after the inside cradle. Time of match: 8:11.
'Roast Beef' Jason Allanson pinned Syne after the Mashed Potatoes. Time of match: 12:03. Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky defeated Wildfire by countout. Time of match: 8:46. Creatures of the Night defeated Yakuza when Night Slinger pinned Secret
Agent Super Dragon after the reversed small package. Time of match: 9:57.
Simon St. Severe defeated Gecko by countout. Time of match: 5:08. Beauregard Boys defeated Mercenaries when Ron Beauregard pinned Korperal
Punishment after the Option 73. Time of match: 11:59.
FOUR-WAY STEEL CAGE MATCH: Sandman Jr. won a four-way match. Sandman Jr. eliminated Union Caine in
21:43 after the Permanent Dream. Sandman Jr. eliminated Allen 'Mr. America'
Carter in 27:34 after the Permanent Dream. Sandman Jr. eliminated Foolkiller in 28:42 after the rollup.
