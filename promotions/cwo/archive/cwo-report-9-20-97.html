CWO REPORT 9/20/97

20 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, September 20, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr, Mitch E. Washey is on vacation. First, let's take a look at what's been going down the past couple of days at CWO House Shows. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 9/18/97 Hartford, CT Red Raven pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Raven's Flame and outside interference by Holocaust. Time of match: 7:56. Sandman Jr./Diamondback Chris Myers defeated Pyro-Technics when Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Detonater after the Rattler. Time of match: 13:59. Sandman pinned Joe Eagle after the Sandblast and a DDT outside the ring by Johnny Reb. Time of match: 18:06. Newton Bros. defeated Coldfront when Howard Newton pinned Kurt Gerstung after the Powerline. Time of match: 12:32. Sean Holiday pinned Gold Kard after the Permanent Vacation. Time of match: 11:35. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS TITLE MATCH: Wild Ones and champions The Big Cool wrestled to a double countout. Time of match: 5:47. The Big Cool retained the CWO North American Tag Team Champions title. Skyhawk pinned 'Monster' Rich Clark after the Skydive and an attack by Masher Maccabee on Clark with the North American title belt. Time of match: 16:04. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 9/19/97 Portland, ME Holocaust made Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor submit to the Ashes to Ashes. Time of match: 11:22. Col. Taggart made Red Raven submit to the Annhilator. Time of match: 13:29. Mondo Gecko pinned Gold Kard after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Gold Kard was attacked before the match by Holocaust and Red Raven. Time of match: 4:54. Sean Holiday pinned 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia after the Permanent Vacation. Time of match: 5:03. Wrath made Television Champion The Focus submit to the Repentence Rack in a non-title match after a pre-match attack by Benjamin Blake Bentley, the Reverend, and Sandman. Time of match: 3:46. TEXAS DEATH MATCH: Deadly Sins defeated Mason-Dixon in a Texas Death match. Time of match: 40:07. SUBMISSION MATCH: World Champion Johnny Reb made Joe Eagle submit to the Texas Cloverleaf in a non-title match when Eagle passed out from the pain. Time of match: 13:51. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in Montepelier, VT, let's take a look at the lineup for that great card! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW 9/20/97 Montepelier, VT Sandman Jr./Diamondback Chris Myers vs. Pyro-Technics Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Lord Hile Troy Holocaust vs. Gold Kard Vegas High Roller vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo CHAMPION'S CHALLENGE: World Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys vs. North American Tag Team Champions The Big Cool CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE: Light Heavyweight Champion Jason 'Flash' Varga vs. World Champion Johnny Reb _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now take you to comments from CWO superstars, starting with "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens in the CWO studios where Jimmy Heat stands accompanied by Tommy Bomber. 'Wigglestick' by Horton Heat bounces along happily in the background as Heat speaks.) "GIGOLO" JIMMY HEAT (shaking head in shame): I can't belive it See-dubble-yah-oh. I just can't. No Unkie Jimmy on Fever? No title shots? No groveling at my feet, ladies not included. I just can't believe it. I hate being in disbelief. But I like dishin' it out! Ever since I came here, people have been talkin' bout me behind my back, kissin' my butt in the ring, and passin' me up for any *real* matches I could have. Well... soon enough... that's gonna change. Ya see... I've already beaten the Law and Moleeki Rahim Jumanji, or whatever the hell his name is this week. I've beaten the top stars in the See-Dubble-Yah-Oh and been looked over for it! Case in point! My good ol' boys the ColdFront an' me *beat* the Eunich, Law, *and* are going to make the Deadly Sins feel sinned against at 'Slam'. What's it gotten us? A Heapin' Helpin' of Hooey. Does a guy have to have 4 people in his lil' club to make an impact here? 'Cause I can sure as *hell* tell ya it doesn't take talent! (He shakes his head in disgust.) HEAT: See-Dubble-Yah-Oh... as soon as Mr. Bomber here has his contract cleared... prepare to give us our due credit. (Scene fades out as Heat stands there gyrating his hips in glee and Bomber waves to the camera moronically.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Recently, I had the pleasure and privelege to interview former wrestler turned manager Johnny B. Goode, who led the Unit into battle up until a vicious attack by The Cabin may have ended his career permanently. The Cabin and The Unit are set to collide at SEPTEMBER SLAM in a "submit or surrender" match, but for now let's go to my special interview with Johnny B. Goode. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The camera fades in, showing Johnny Starr sitting in Johnny B. Goode's living room. Starr is wearing casual dress clothes, and Goode is wearing jean shorts and a polo shirt. Both his legs are covered by a blanket, and sitting beside him is his wife, Katrina Goode, who is dressed much the same as her husband.) STARR: Fans, I'm sitting here at the home of Johnny B. Goode, leader, manager, and friend of "The Most Dangerous Men In Wrestling," The Unit. Johnny, thank you, and thank you as well, Katrina, for having me visit for this interview. JOHNNY B. GOODE: No, thank you, Johnny Starr, for being enough of a friend to come visit. It's bad enough that I was put out of action by Lord Guru and those cowards in the Cabin, but it's really sad that you're the only CWO official to even visit since that attack. STARR: Well, I don't know how to answer that allegation, Johnny. But it does bring a question to mind. Why is the Unit currently accusing the CWO of "ducking them," as they put it? GOODE: It's really quite simple. We've said it before, and we'll continue to say it. We haven't been given our due since our arrival here. I watched Fever the other night, and I hear that slob Wendy Thomas--- STARR: Um, that's Dave Thomas. GOODE: Whatever. As long as he acts like a woman, he'll be "Wendy" to the Unit. Anyways, I'm watching, and he says that CWO is a lot like Missouri in that the motto here is "Show Me." Well, Thomas, what more do you want to see? We've beat most everyone we've gotten in the ring with. STARR: This is true. You have suffered several defeats, however. GOODE: Sure, but how many of them could be considered fair? So far, the only fair decision against us was the Wild Ones defeating the Newtons in Syracuse, and we do give them credit for that. All the other losses have been tainted. Hell, take last Tuesday's Fever. The Cabin storms the ring to attack us, and the ref DQs us? My hat's off to the Highlanders for turning down that decision, because they know they couldn't fool the fans with that one. STARR: There are claims in some quarters, though, that the Unit is asking for too much, too fast. GOODE: Oh, what a.... (He sighs heavily.) GOODE: Look. Let me put it this way. Let's say you've been a champion many times in other federations. Respected federations. And you're well known and respected. Now one day, towards the end of your contract in such a federation, you hear of a younger, more exciting federation that's garnering a lot of attention. So you decide to go visit it. With me so far? STARR: Yes. GOODE: Now, assume for a second that when you get there, the officials all decide that any title you may have held elsewhere is small potatoes compared to THEIR titles, and as such, they pretend they don't know you. "Sorry, guys, but you'll have to prove yourselves all over again," they tell you. But then, ignoring their own words, they proceed to offer title shots to other former champions within weeks of their appearances. I thought that when the Newtons were first scheduled to meet the Big Cool at Slam II that things were finally taking off. Then we lost to the Wild Ones and we're taken out of the match. Okay, fine. I can live with that. But when the same team that was just taken out of the North American title match beats a team that's scheduled to fight for the World title--- STARR: But it was a disqualification, Johnny. GOODE: Bottom line: who went to the pay window that night? Newtons? Or the Highlanders? STARR: Well, the Newtons, of course... GOODE: Exactly. A win's a win. And as it turns out, the Highlanders lost two in a row. But they're still in that match for the World title. Now I'm not just singling out the Highlanders here, they're just a good example of the favoritism displayed here in CWO. And it seems that Painmaster is getting his turn now, too. He's only been here, what? A week or two at that? And he's getting shots already? KATRINA GOODE: Johnny, don't get your blood pressure going. Remember what the doctor told you. STARR: What exactly did the doctor tell you, Johnny? Lots of fans are wondering if you'll ever be able to walk again. GOODE (sighing): I wish I knew how to answer that question, Johnny Starr. I really do. If my knee had been healthy at the time Lord Guru decided to practice his golf swing, I could answer yes. But since it was still weakened from that car crash years ago... well.... (He shakes his head.) GOODE: Let's just say none of us are very optimistic at this point. STARR: Toni Newton says she's not managing the Unit right now, that you are. She's very admantant to that point, saying she's only there for support. Do you agree with what she says? GOODE: The Newton family and I go back a long ways. Howard and I were the initial members of the Unit, along with Joey Ellison, wherever the hell he is now. I helped train Steve when he was ready to turn pro, and Ike Newton and I gave Toni a running start when she was ready, too. I look at Toni like my favorite neice, so for her to drop her business over in JIWC to be here for me, well, that means a lot. When Howard told me she was here, I told her I wanted her to take over managing the guys for me. She told me no, that that was my job. She's been here every weekend trying to push me through my rehab. But as far as I'm concerned, right now, she is the manager of the Unit. STARR: Does this mean you won't be appearing any time in the future with the Unit? GOODE: I didn't say that. It could be months from now, it could be years. But one thing you can count on: I will be back. STARR: Okay, we're just a little more than one week away from September Slam II. Anything else you want to say before we end this? GOODE: Lord Guru, Exodus, Judgment, Blaze, Venegance... you guys really don't know what you've done, do you? Guru, you found out the hard way that you don't mess with us. How's your back, anyways? Come Slam II, I'm sure your knee will matching it for the pain. Now, win or lose, we want you to remember one thing: we've never backed down from anyone. We're not about to start now. So get in the ring, boys, and we'll show you how to wrestle. STARR: The right way? K. GOODE: Oh, yes, definitely the right way! (Johnny B. Goode laughs as the camera fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Let me tell you, no matter what you think of The Unit, Johnny and his wife Katrina, two excellent people, I was truly honored to be let into their home for that interview. Well, that's it for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
