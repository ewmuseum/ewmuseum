House Show Results - 7/17/99

July 18, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 7/17/99 Los Angeles, CA (Great Western Forum) Sandman pinned Night Crawler after the Sandblast. Time of match: 16:52. Carr/Wildfire defeated Yakuza when Wildfire made 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin submit to the Wild Thing. Time of match: 16:46. Night Slinger pinned Wrath after the Lights Out. Time of match: 3:22. Darkness defeated Master of Darkness Damion by disqualification when the
Creatures of Night attacked while Darkness had Damion in the Blackout. Time of match: 2:05.
Mondo Gecko/Black Jack Hollister defeated Diamondback Chris Myers/'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum when Mondo Gecko pinned Diamondback Chris Myers after the swan dive. Time of match: 10:23.
Allen 'Mr. America' Carter made Brian 'The King' Derks submit to the American Dream. Time of match: 13:16. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Champions Reb and Fantastic defeated Lord Hile Troy/Gold Kard when Johnny
Reb pinned Gold Kard after the rollup. Time of match: 13:08.
WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Col. Taggart defeated champion Brent 'The Ace' Alles by disqualification after Allen Carter attacked him. Time of match: 12:29.
