CWO EXTREME 04/30/2006 Ford Field, Detroit, MI http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling2/cwo2k -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Three long Black Presidential Limos Arrive at the area and are being escorted by 18 Officers on Motorcycles. Rose bearers come out of all three limo and start sprinkling rose on the floor. Prince David Tita and Ohar come out of the first Limo, Princess and her best friend come out of the second limo and then the King and Queen come out of the third limo and walk their way to the back with security. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- SHOW OPENING -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 1 - -The PA roars with Bitches by Selfless Mind Indulgence. The new stable song was now already becoming a fixture loved and hated by the crowd as Crimson walked down towards the ring microphone in hand and with a gentle lowering of his spare hand the music slowly died away- Crimson: "As you know my partner in crime and I were in a match up for the first time since we came back and as usual........we were on the winning side. To be fair what do you expect to a five man side who's best wrestler is an aging old man.......... But enough about the past I know you want to see and paid good money to admire the founding father of our pack and I shall not disappoint.......and neither for that matter shall he for bringing with him he has a new member to our pack this very night. This member will bring a whole new dimension to our pack and make us one of the most feared in CWO history. Now without further ado I wish to introduce you to the wrestler you love to hate and wish to be........WOLFBANE!" The PA slams into full blast playing the same song once again and long tense moments pass by as everybody wondered who the new member of their pack was going to be. Before a loud growling is heard over the speakers and the music subsides and all that can be heard is growls before Bane's voice appears Bane: "W**RES and B**TARDS! Let me introduce you TO.......RAZOR!" Bane finally appeared at the top of the ramp almost being dragged by what appears to be a large mountain wolf of near four and a half feet in height and as sleek and agile looking as most pure breeds. Bane is smirking his now legendary smirk Bane: "As you can see he's not one for words but let me reassure everyone in the back that this dogs bite is much worse than his bark. You see me and Crimson here thought it best before embarking upon our deal with the devil I mean Mr. Foley that we should get some protection as it were a third member before others joined our ranks." The crowd was completely silent most extremely scared for this wolf was either very well trained or definitely wild as it was pulling hard around the steel chain collar around its neck which was being held tightly by Bane. Melinn (whispered): "Bane is completely insane" Thomas (extremely shaken): "I.....I.....I er I don't know I think that it's a er.......a erm......a wise move by Bane" Melinn: "You just don't want to be that things lunch!" Thomas: "Well do you?!?!" With nothing more said by the two wrestlers they made their slow walk back up to the back their faces alight with pride and sick satisfaction -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- COMMERCIAL -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 2 - FIRE AND ICE (Phoenix and Frost) vs. JAPANESE CONNECTION (Kazuo Hirata and Rising Sun) (Julianne Michaels is in the ring.) JULIANNE MICHAELS: This contest is a tag team match scheduled for one fall. Introducing first... At a total combined weight of 440 pounds... "The Ice Wolf" Frost and Phoenix... Fire and Ice! (Fire and Ice come down to the ring and get in.) Their opponents... Led to the ring by Tiger Lilly... At a total combined weight of 495 pounds... Kazuo Hirata and Rising Sun... the JAPANESE CONNECTION! (The Japanese Connection come down to the ring and get in.) Rising Sun starts against Frost. The bell rings. They lock up. Rising Sun takes Frost down with a bodyslam. Rising Sun executes a DDT on Frost. You can hear a few scattered fans cheering for Rising Sun. Rising Sun hits Frost with a springboard twisting moonsault and goes for the pin! Kendall Dolemeyer counts: One, shoulder up! Rising Sun goes for a monkey flip but Frost blocks it. Frost goes for a backdrop but Rising Sun counters with a sunset flip and goes for the pin! Kendall Dolemeyer counts: One, two, kickout! Rising Sun gets a side headlock on Frost. Frost grabs the ropes after 5 seconds. Rising Sun goes for a vertical suplex but Frost blocks it and does one of his own! Frost tags out to Phoenix. Kazuo Hirata tries to come in but is stopped by the referee. Fire and Ice whip Rising Sun into the ropes... they hit Rising Sun with a double backdrop. Phoenix and Frost whip Rising Sun into the ropes... they hit Rising Sun with a double spin kick! Frost leaves the ring. Phoenix hits a swinging neckbreaker on Rising Sun! The crowd cheers. Phoenix nails Rising Sun with a cobra clutch suplex! Phoenix takes Rising Sun down with a swinging neckbreaker! The crowd is cheering Phoenix. Phoenix goes for a bearhug but Rising Sun counters with a facerake. Rising Sun tags out to Kazuo Hirata. Phoenix nails Kazuo Hirata with an elbowsmash. Phoenix whips Kazuo Hirata into the ropes... Phoenix goes for a roundhouse kick but Kazuo Hirata counters with a cradle suplex! Kazuo Hirata takes Phoenix down with a flying cross body press and goes for the pin! Kendall Dolemeyer counts: One, two, kickout! Kazuo Hirata goes for a belly-to-belly suplex but Phoenix counters with a facerake. Phoenix goes for a punch but Kazuo Hirata blocks it and counters with a roundhouse right. Kazuo Hirata goes for a belly-to-belly suplex but Phoenix counters with a facerake. Phoenix throws Kazuo Hirata out of the ring! Phoenix rolls out under the bottom rope. Phoenix goes for a cradle suplex but Kazuo Hirata counters with an elbowsmash. Kendall Dolemeyer counts: 1. Kazuo Hirata whips Phoenix into the guardrail! Kendall Dolemeyer counts: 2. Kazuo Hirata takes Phoenix down with a backbreaker! Kazuo Hirata takes Phoenix down with a back suplex! Kendall Dolemeyer counts: 3. Kazuo Hirata gets back into the ring. Phoenix climbs back into the ring. Phoenix gets a bearhug on Kazuo Hirata. Kazuo Hirata tries to escape the hold... Kazuo Hirata reaches the ropes after being trapped for 25 seconds. Phoenix chops Kazuo Hirata. The crowd WHOOS! Phoenix kicks Kazuo Hirata. Phoenix runs into the ropes... Kazuo Hirata hits Phoenix with a clothesline! Kazuo Hirata nails Phoenix with a backbreaker! Kazuo Hirata is going for the cover. Kendall Dolemeyer counts: One, two, kickout! Kazuo Hirata plays to the crowd. Phoenix springs to his feet and nails Kazuo with a neckbreaker! Phoenix tags out to Frost. Frost hits Kazuo Hirata with a flying dropkick! Frost executes a fisherman buster on Kazuo Hirata! Frost hits a snap suplex on Kazuo Hirata! Frost covers Kazuo Hirata. Kendall Dolemeyer counts: One, two, shoulder up! The crowd is cheering. Frost hits Kazuo Hirata with a jab. Frost hits Kazuo Hirata with a fisherman buster! Frost whips Kazuo Hirata into the ropes... Kazuo Hirata goes for an Asai moonsault but Frost counters with a Tombstone! The crowd is cheering! Frost executes the Frostbite on Kazuo Hirata and goes for the pin! Frost goes for the pin. Kendall Dolemeyer counts: One, two... Rising Sun tries to come in for the save but Phoenix blocks him... three! The winners are Fire and Ice! Time of match: 0:06:36. The fans cheer Fire and Ice. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- COMMERCIAL -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 3 - -The camera comes up on Gold Kard strutting through the backstage area before stopping abruptly and taking a couple of steps backwards looking extremely nervous. The camera pans out to a wider frame and suddenly his nerves seem entirely justified as Razor the new mascot/member of Bane and Crimsons stable is stood directly in Gold Kard's path. The wolf's head is over half the height of the ageing German wrestler and there really wasn't a way of getting around the wolf who was now growling loud enough for the camera's to hear him clearly. Just as Gold Kard looks ready to turn away and find a new way of getting around the wolf a mocking laugh comes from an open doorway and from out of the shadows Bane comes out and stands extremely cool right behind Razor.- Bane: "What's wrong old man? Don't you like my pet? He's harmless unless I say TACKLE!" -Razor went from his prone position to on top of Gold Kard in a matter of split seconds it's large athletic muscles moving into the right position first before with it's force/weight/momentum took the wrestling legend off his feet. Razor stood with all four paws upon Gold Kard's body, the wolfs claws digging into the man's body slightly. Bane wandered around to Gold Kard's top half who by the look of his face was trying valiantly to get out from under the wolf.- Bane: "Next week at Fever....you'll wish I'd let Razor show you what he could do.....you've been warned old man." -Bane walks off and leaves Gold Kard struggling underneath the weight of Razor before a loud high pitched whistle is heard and Razor without a glance back at Gold Kard jumped off the prone wrestler and followed at the heel of the offstage Bane. As Gold Kard rose to his feet a few specks of blood began to show through his clothing from where the wolf's claws had dug into the mans skin.- -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- COMMERCIAL -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 4 - -A hand pushes open Col. Taggart's door firmly without knocking and before the Col. could stand up in anger Banes voice boomed out- Bane: "Sit down!" Bane whistled once again and Razor came striding into the office and Bane commanded Bane: "Up!" Razor lifted his front two paws up onto Col. Taggart's desk and the wolf seemed to take up a lot more of the space in the room than seemed humanly possible, later Col. Taggart could of sworn the wolf was trying to outstare him. Bane: "Col. you know why I'm here, I'm sure Foley has briefed you on the situation already, I'm just here to make sure as this is your area of......command" Bane couldn't help a sneer come to his face as he said the last word. Bane: "At this upcoming Pay Per View.....if anybody lays a hand on me, I want them banned from a title shot for a month and the match given in favor of the wrestler who didn't hit me.......understand?.....good" Bane hadn't even waited for a response he was that confident about Foley's power that he wanted to use it for all it was worth before his 'penance' was complete for him to come back to CWO again. Bane strode out of the Col.'s office without Taggart even saying a word and once the whistle sounded again Razor took his paws off of the desk and strode out of the office also it's tail high in the air. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- COMMERCIAL -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 5 - A rundown of the PPV ends the show. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- CREDIT FOR MATCH WRITING: None CREDIT FOR PROMOS: WolfBane, Crimson, Prince David Tita
