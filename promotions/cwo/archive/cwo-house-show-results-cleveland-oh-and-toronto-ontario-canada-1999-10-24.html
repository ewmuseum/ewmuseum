House Show Results - Cleveland, OH and Toronto, Ontario, Canada

October 24, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 10/22/99 Cleveland, OH (CSU Convocation Center) Matsuhiro Torunaka pinned Vegas Villain after the crucifix. Time of match: 6:41. Maximum Overdrive pinned Master of Darkness Damion after the Pile-Up, pulling the tights. Time of match: 8:56.
Gold Kard pinned Mustafa Rahim after the Die Flieder Kross, pulling the tights. Time of match: 9:31.
Magic Group and Beauregard Boys wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Yakuza defeated Foolkiller/Union Caine when Secret Agent Super Dragon pinned
Union Caine after the Sushi Bar. Time of match: 10:42.
Hollywood Haskins pinned North American Champion Man Mountain Marko in a non-title match after the Hollywood Bomb. Time of match: 5:47.
Brute Squad defeated Simon St. Severe/Syne when Black Jack Hollister pinned
Simon St. Severe after the sidewalk slam, pulling the tights. Time of
match: 18:52. Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Carr after the Rattler. Time of match: 26:47. HOUSE SHOW 10/23/99 Toronto, Canada (Air Canada Center) THREE WAY DANCE: 'Roast Beef' Jason Allanson won a triangle match over 'The Jaw Jacking
Gigolo' Andrew Dixon and Deliverance. Dixon eliminated Deliverance in 10:07 after the One Night Stand. Allanson eliminated Dixon in 12:46 after the
Mashed Potatoes.
'The Main Event' Steve Davis pinned Cruiserweight Champion 'Airborne' Mark
Fulcrum after a belly to belly suplex into a bridge. Time of match: 16:28.
(Fulcrum's title was not on the line.) Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky defeated Sandman Jr. by disqualification after Sandman Jr. hit him with the cane in the ring. Time of match: 10:35.
Television Champion Iceman X pinned Wildfire after the jumping DDT. Time of match: 18:30. (The title was not on the line.)
Deadly Sins and Hardcore Sensation wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Gecko pinned Lord Hile Troy after the swan dive. Time of match: 17:33. Creatures of the Night defeated Mercenaries when Night Slinger pinned Kaptain Krunch after the Lights Out. Time of match: 18:04. CWO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH: Allen 'Mr. America' Carter pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the second rope flying clothesline. Time of match: 34:32. Allen 'Mr. America' Carter retained the CWO World Champion title.
