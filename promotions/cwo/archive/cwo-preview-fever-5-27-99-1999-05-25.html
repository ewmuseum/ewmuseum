PREVIEW - FEVER 5/27/99

May 25, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The CWO comes to you live this Thursday from the Cow Palace, in San Francisco, CA, 3 days away from MAY-HEM '99! Rumor has it that there's bad blood between the DARK SIDE and VICE PRESIDENT
KENT ALLES and his HEADHUNTERS? Will it erupt on FEVER? Tune in to find out!
The man with the money, WILDFIRE, takes on the man with the stripper, SNAKE
MARTIN, in what should be a wild bout.
The OUTLAW RIDERS will be on hand. What does MONDO GECKO have to say, three days away from his (snicker) "matchup" with KENT ALLES, in the CAGE?
HARDCORE SENSATION will see action against the team of VLADIMIR POPALOV and
FERGUS MCTAVISH, THE WHIRLING SCOTTISH DEVIL of the SHEETS FOUNDATION.
Only, with JULI ASHTON still missing, apparently in the clutches of Fergie, will it actually be him or his brother WILLIE?
It's a battle that might hearken back to the Revolutionary War, as BRIAN
"THE KING" DERKS takes on Television Champion ALLEN "MR. AMERICA" CARTER for the title!
"DARK SHOGUN" AKUERI HAKUJIN of the SHEETS FOUNDATION will get a chance to show off his considerable skills against the incredibly unorthodox and deranged CALVIN!
"IRRESISTIBLE" EDDIE FANTASTIC will see action against yet ANOTHER member of the SHEETS FOUNDATION (boy, do they run this fed or what?), unofficial "King of the Falls Count Anywhere" match, SECRET AGENT SUPER DRAGON. Both men have been requesting this match for a long time, but with DRAGON entering a
FCA match against SANDMAN JR. on Sunday for the North American title, and
FANTASTIC wrapped up in the WARGAMES, will either of their minds be truly on this match?
Also, DIAMONDBACK CHRIS MYERS and "AIRBORNE" MARK FULCRUM will be in the house. No telling what they're going to do and what side they're truly on as we are three days away from the big event!
All that and more, including a look at the intense rivalry between LORD HILE
TROY and BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES, on this week's edition of FEVER!
