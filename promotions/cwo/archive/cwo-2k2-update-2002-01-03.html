[detail withheld] ) joined on 12-31-01 Wildfire (Handler: [name withheld], [detail withheld] ) joined on 1-1-02 BRINGING YOU UP TO SPEED 2001 was a tumultuous year for the CWO. Diana Christensen, an executive for the fX network, had a radical idea known as EXTREME MEASURES that just didn't seem to click with the viewing address. Ratings slowly got lowered, and even a last ditch "Tournament of Champions" couldn't help things. The
CWO was cancelled from fX and seemed, after being in operation in some form since 1995, dead.
However, six fateful numbers meant a rebirth. Brent "The Ace" Alles hit the Michigan Millions lottery, winning $23 million in the process. After paying off some gambling debts, he amazingly had enough money left to purchase the rights to the CWO.
Still, as recently seen, the climate for creating a new wrestling organization, or even trying to ressurect an old one, was cold indeed. Due to the failure of CWO: EXTREME MEASURES on fX, Brent found himself unable to get a TV contract, a major roadblock. So, much like Anigo (sp?) Montoya in
The Princess Bride, Brent decided to go "back to the beginning," Grand Rapids, MI. And then, tragedy struck. Brent "The Ace" Alles was doing a local press conference about trying to resurrect the CWO from the ground up, starting locally and eventually, once
TV exposure in the form of syndication or network was achieved, trying to take the organization once again to a national level. As he left the press conference, Brent was a victim of a hit and run. The vehicle sped away before anyone could even get the license plate number.
Brent was taken to a local hospital in Grand Rapids and eventually released. His diagnosis is guarded and was not released to the general press, but Brent is now wheelchair-bound. His wrestling career at the age of 27... apparently over. BACK TO BASICS Still, wheelchair-bound or not, Brent is determined more than ever to make the CWO work again.
The CWO will return to action on Saturday, January 11, 2002, at the Deltaplex in Grand Rapids, MI. Already, former CWO superstars Wildfire, Gold Kard, Dave Lupus, and El Suicida have committed to wrestle on this special event, what many are calling the last chance for the CWO to finally fulfill its destiny in the wrestling world. Television cameras will be on hand for this event, in the hopes of selling the CWO to a network or syndication deal.
We hope to have updates for you through the week before the big event of other people slated to appear. Local talent from Grand Rapids is also expected to be involved. Tickets are on sale for the Deltaplex, get yours soon!
CWO2K2... forget what you know... everything has changed... nothing will ever be the same!
