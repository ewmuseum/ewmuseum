CWO HOTLINE (Week of 11/10/97 - 11/16/97)

9 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi fans, this is Johnny Starr with your CWO HOTLINE REPORT! 1. Several CWO superstars have recently gave notice to the CWO and will be leaving as of November 9, 1997. These stars are: "Monster" Rich Clark Miguel Sanchez Moses Reverend Former World Champion Clark and former North American and Television Champion Sanchez bost stated that their partings were amicable with the CWO and they have both expressed interest in returning in the future. The Reverend, enigmatic and mysterious as always, had no comment as to why he was leaving the federation. However, the question now remains: who truly is behind the events that have been termed by some as "Something Has Returned?" Sources tell me that we will find out this week on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER who is the true mastermind behind the corruption of superstars such as Bloodbath and Sandman, and all of the vicious sneak attacks that have been happening to CWO fan favorites over the past few months. The Moses situation is a bit more touchy. Apparently, there has been more infighting behind the scenes of Gold Kard International, and Moses was reportedly unhappy with his place in that same organization. Moses had been frustrated as of late at regaining the Light Heavyweight Championship, which he held three times during the past year. There have been rumors at even a physical skirmish between Moses and the CEO of GKI, Gold Kard. Gold Kard International, of course, had no comment, but are now down to three members, Kard, Lord Hile Troy, and Masher Maccabee. It will be interesting to see if any "new hires" to Gold Kard International will happen in the next few months. 2. Col. Taggart suffered a back injury after a postmatch attack by Brother Justice. It is unknown at this time how long he will be out, as doctors are still conducting more tests. At this time it is thought that newcomer and former NFL star Wesley "The Rock" McMichael will be taking Taggart's place with Ranger Taurus for the upcoming Wayne Carpenter Memorial Tag Team Tournament. 3. There may be some new tag teams coming in for the upcoming Wayne Carpenter Memorial 32-Team Tag Tournament, to be held in Wembley Stadium in London, England, over a 3 day period, with the finals taking place on the NOVEMBER PAIN '97 PPV, held live from Wembley Stadium on November 23, 1997. Already we know that such teams as the WWF's Legion of Doom and the WCW's Steiner Bros. will be participating in the event, more information of course to come. That's it for the hotline this week, we'll see you ringside!
