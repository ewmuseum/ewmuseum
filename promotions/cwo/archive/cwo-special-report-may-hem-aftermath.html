CWO SPECIAL REPORT: MAY-HEM AFTERMATH

5 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO SPECIAL REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO SPECIAL REPORT, MAY-HEM - THE AFTERMATH. It is Monday, May 5, 1997, and I am of course Johnny Starr. Last night was quite possibly one of the most exciting events the CWO has ever had, as Joe Eagle lasted 56 minutes and 37 seconds to become the 2nd annual MAY-HEM winner. He will now go on to SUMMER HEAT on June 1, 1997, from Myrtle Beach, SC, but for now let's look at all that happened at MAY-HEM last night! _______________________________________________________________________________ MAY-HEM RECAP Johnny Reb defeated CWO Television Champion Inferno in 14:16, rolling him up with a grapevine like maneuver for the pin, and winning the title. In a wild match, CWO North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys defended their titles against the Mercenaries in 19:31 when Joe "The Mean Guy" Rucker pinned Kaptain Krunch after the Bad Guys' Bash (double strike with a steel chair). CWO North American Champion Jason "Flash" Varga defeated Tiger Rogers by disqualification when the rest of the Alliance attacked him, beating him unmercifully until Tower of Power (Joe Eagle and the Big Cool) made the save. The Highlanders defeated the CWO World Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses to win the titles in 14:23 when Conor McCloud pinned Pit Boss #1 after the Quickening (superplex by Gavin, flying splash by Conor). After the match the Pit Bosses had a violent breakup as Pit Boss #1 attacked Pit Boss #2, piledriving him on to a steel briefcase, injuring his neck severely. In the three-way match for the help up CWO World title, "Monster" Rich Clark and Bane were the first two men to start in the steel cage surrounding ring #2. "Monster" Rich Clark knocked Bane unconscious with a choke slam in 13:42 and handcuffed him to the ring. Clark then went to the other ring to fight Goliath, and although Goliath wowed the crowd with a series of incredible power maneuvers, Clark was able to rally back and pin Goliath in 29:28 to win the World title for the second time. Joe Eagle survived 56:37 to become the 2nd annual MAY-HEM winner, last eliminating Sandman Jr. to do so. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil, who definitely made an impression last night. (Still photos are shown from the EYE ON THE STORM match.) STARR: Fergus was one of the four random competitors to be chosen for the Lottery Match during the EYE ON THE STORM broadcast. His partner was Bloodbath, and the two went up against Arlon Flaym and Nate of Da Hood Rats. As Bloodbath Tombstone Powerbombed Nate outside the ring, McTavish delivered a devastating top-rope piledriver to Flaym inside the ring that he calls the Highland Fling. Bloodbath and McTavish won by countout as Nate could not get back in the ring, thus earning the #'s 59 and 60 entry spots, but Bloodbath then protected Arlon Flaym from further assault. This would come back to haunt Bloodbath, who was attacked by McTavish before both of them were scheduled to come out. A bloodied Bloodbath was easily thrown out by Sandman Jr. However, now Bloodbath is scheduled to meet Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil, at SUMMER HEAT. Let's hear from the Devil. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on a large, red-headed Scotsman engaged in a heated debate in the empty office of the CWO Director. Its Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil. He is sitting at the Director's desk watching a large screen television. His voice is mired with a thick Scottish accent.) (Televison beer commercial): Less filling! Fergus (yelling at the television): Tastes great! (Televison beer commercial): Less filling!! Fergus (yelling at the television): Tastes great!! (Televison beer commercial): LESS FILLING!!! Fergus: Och, ya electonic bugger, I said, "TASTES GREAT!" (Fergus grabs the 24 karat solid gold paperweight off the desk and hurls it at the television screen. The screen explodes and a small fire starts within the now useless set. The CWO janitor, Pepe, peeks in the office from the doorway and quickly retreats, saying...) Pepe: Um, I come back and clean when you ready. Fergus: Oooh, now I gots ta waits some more for da Director. I cants waits ta share this haggis with 'im! (He points to a large haggis laying on the desk blotter.) Fergus (looking at the camera): Speaking of waiting me camera-guy, guess what I'm a waiting for da Director ta give me. Camera-Guy: Um, is it two wee sheep so you can make some haggis? Fergus: (laughing) Ha! Nah, t'ain't no clones in da CWO! Least not yet! Nay, I'm here's ta axe him ta sign me up fer a match wif dat puddin' head, teat sucklin', pansy arsed BLOODBATH! (Fergus stands up and leans across the desk, looking right into the camera.) Fergus: Aye, ya heards right! That bugger doan know what can a worms he's opened! Dere's a sayin' in Scotland: "A smart man knows not ta piss in da wind." Well, BLOODBATH, you done an' pissed in da wind of The Whirling Scottish Devil. An I doan likes dat one bit. (Fergus sits back down behind the Director's desk) Fergus: So dat's why I'm here. I wants da Director ta get off his padded arse an signs me up ta a match wif BLOODBATH right now! An I wants one ting added to it. Ya listenin' BLOODBATH? I wants our match ta be a STRAP MATCH! I doan wants ya runnin' off when I gets ya in dat ring! I wants ya right where I kin lay a right proper beatin' on yer sorry bones! So how bout it? Are ya ready an waiting fer a STRAP MATCH wif The Whirling Scottish Devil? Cause I sure am! Oh, and one last ting.... (Fergus jumps up on the Director's desk, turns his back to the camera and lifts up his kilt, mooning the camera-guy.) Fergus: (over his shoulder) Gets ready for The Whirling Scottish Devil! ITS NOT OATMEAL!!!! (just then, the Director returns to his office) Director: Hell, not again! Hey, you moron! You can't do that! Get the hell off my desk and put your pants back on! (Camera fade, much laughing and hooting can still be heard.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Last night was a very tough night for the four members of the Alliance, with Inferno dropping the Television title to Johnny Reb, and Goliath and Tiger Rogers failing to win their respective title matches. Add that to the humiliating 4 second throw-out of David "The Destroyer" Davis, and you should have a very dejected bunch. However, the Alliance is undaunted, and they're back to replotting how they can get back what they once possessed. Let's hear from them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Jimbo Jones is standing in the back CWO Locker Room, along with the rest of the Alliance.) JIMBO JONES: CWO... the wrong's been done, we've gotten nothing but crap from each and every one of the officials, and who am I supposed to go to? Randy "Texas Rose" Johnson. You've done nothing but give me 100% crap since coming here, I've been a better buisnessman that you ever will be, I'd be a better president than you ever will be, and I'M A BETTER WRESTLER THAT YOU EVER HAVE BEEN OR WILL BE! If I've got to beat you senseless in the squared circle to prove that my men don't get cheated out of wins and belts, I'm not afraid to do it. As for my boys... they've got a little bickering of their own! DAVID "THE DESTROYER" DAVIS: I've received an assignment from Jimbo as the official Alliance hitman, and my next target is the GKI, each and every one of you. I, along with the rest of the Alliance, will make 100% sure, that you never set foot in the CWO again! My personal challenge to Gold Kard still stands, me against you, Gold Kard, with Jimbo above the ring in a cage, and if you win, Jimbo is lowered and you get 5 minutes in the ring with him! GOLIATH: RICH CLARK! I'm not done with you, I beat you in Florida once. I beat you again! You think you escaped me once? I'll beat you again! INFERNO: Johnny Reb, you won this time, but by no means will you ever get away from Inferno with the belt around your waist again. This is a perfect example of how the CWO official have landed a travesty on me, that was the quickest, cheapest count I've ever seen. Not to mention a full hand of tights. So let's settle this one more time, no DQ, no countout, submission match! HECK! Let's put it in a cage so you can't whine about the Alliance interfering! You've got nothing on me! Never mess with fire my friend... you'll always get burnt! TIGER ROGERS: I've been training relentlessly for the past month, trying to gain the North American title, and bring it back to the Alliance... The time has come, and the Tiger will strike again! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: A newcomer to the CWO definitely made an impression as well last night in the MAY-HEM battle royal, as Diamondback Chris Myers lasted 26 minutes and 3 seconds and tied Japanese superstar Matsuhiro Torunaka for the most throw-outs at 6 apiece. Let's hear from the Diamondback and the lovely Dallas. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Diamondback Chris Myers and Dallas are shown backstage at MAY-HEM, after the huge battle royal. The cheers of the fans celebrating the win of Joe Eagle can be heard in the locker room.) Dallas: Here, drink this. (Dallas hands her man a bottle of Gatorade, which he gulps down.) Dallas: Don't talk, let me handle it. (Dallas turns towards the camera as Myers wipes his bruised face) Dallas: Well, CWO, you've shown why DCM wanted to come here! The competition tonight was top notch, and Myers craves it! What was the official time? Cameraman: Uh... 26 minutes and 3 seconds. Dallas: 26 minutes and 3 seconds. Not bad for a debut, no? Granted, he didn't win... Myers: And I came so close, too! Dallas: Shh! Rest, Chris, you had a tough night. Anyway, and he eliminated 4 of the CWO's best... Camerman: Five... Dallas: FIVE! Five superstars in this fed. Now do you see what you're up against, CWO? Myers: Dallas, let me talk! Dallas: Ok, ok! Just don't wear yourself out... Myers: This is talking, not wrestling! Though your concern is touching. (Myers turns towards the camera. His face is bruised from the night's activities, but he's wearing a huge smile.) Myers: Fans, what did you think of old Diamondback? Granted, I didn't win, but hey, for my first event, coming in cold, I know I did rather well! Going toe-to-toe with the best in the CWO... that's what I live for, not wimping out of title defenses and having 8 guys to watch my back! Now, this was my first show, but I'm only going to get better! And it's going to be at your expense, CWO. I'm not calling anyone out, I'm just stating the facts. There's an old saying that a friend told me. "With most wrestlers, they have to say they're going to kick your ass. With Arn Anderson, you just know he's going to kick your ass!" CWO, add Diamondback Chris Myers to the equation. (Myers turns away, but turns back) Myers: Oh, and Mondo Gecko and the Wild Bunch....while I'm flattered by your offer, I'm already in another stable. If anyone has ever heard of the NEF'nW, I'm in the Riders on the Storm. But, I'm honored that, even before seeing one match, you offered me a membership. I never forget a compliment, so, even though I'm declining, I'll be more then happy to help you whenever your need it. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: They came down at the same time last night in the MAY-HEM battle royal, but thank goodness they weren't in the same ring, because the feud between Benjamin Blake Bentley and The Focus continues to escalate every day, as evidenced by this tape we received yesterday from the Bentley camp, let's take a look. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens with Triple B, Benjamin Blake Bentley, rushing out of his limo into a public restroom. I did a wrong, suh! BENTLEY: Pathetic. Truly pathetic. A grown *man* groveling and whining at work is bad enough, but at his home is sickening. FAKE FOCUS: I know, suh! I'll stop, suh! I'll stop! I thought we were cool, suh! Aftah you spared me an' all aftah our last match, suh! BENTLEY: Don't remind me... you mongrel... I'll give your sorry carcass one last chance.
