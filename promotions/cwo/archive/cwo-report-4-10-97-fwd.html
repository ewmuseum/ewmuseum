CWO REPORT 4/10/97 (fwd)

11 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1. RESULTS FROM LAST NIGHT'S CARD HOUSE SHOW 4/9/97 Bangkok, Thailand Joe Eagle pinned Serpentmaster after the Eagle Shock. Time of match: 6:41. Deadly Sins defeated Masters of Hardcore when Pride pinned Brad 'The Man' Couchman after the piledriver. Time of match: 22:11. Clark Gray made Mondo Gecko submit to the Abdominal Stretch after outside interference by Brent "The Ace" Alles. Time of match: 4:43. Alles then attacked Gray after the match. Hellhound and Baby Waldo wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 12:53. Moses pinned Kurt 'Thrill from the Chill' Gerstung after The Commandment (springboard moonsault). Time of match: 4:41. CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Tiger Rogers pinned The Focus after the Inverted Powerbomb in a non-title match. Time of match: 8:44. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS TITLE MATCH: Champions the Bad Guys defeated Mercenaries when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Korperal Punishment after the Bad Guys' Bash (double strike with steel chairs). Time of match: 10:05. CWO World Champion 'Monster' Rich Clark pinned Sloth after the Choke Slam in a non-title match. Time of match: 24:45. _______________________________________________________________________________ 2. TONIGHT'S CARD THURSDAY, APRIL 10, 1997 SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, Brian "The Hammer" Williams, Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Unholy Alliance (Serpentmaster, Vlad the Impaler, Bishop, champions) Maximum Overdrive vs. Bloodbath Regulators vs. Mercenaries Inferno vs. Bane David "The Destroyer" Davis vs. "Mr. Extreme" Chris Ohlhoff Sandman Jr. vs. Baby Waldo LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: El Grande Pistolerro vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, no-DQ): Johnny Reb vs. "Monster" Rich Clark (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ 3. COMMENTS (Flash Varga is in the interview area with his bodyguard Jim Peer and Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Well, Flash, you had quite a match during the PPV, didn't you? JASON "FLASH" VARGA: DURING the event? You must be confusing me with someone else, because I was AT HOME WATCHING The Rock! Arlon Flaym, you had the misfortune of having to feel the power of a mad Varga. It's a good thing I don't have this problem very often: I like to stay calm, relax, and tip back a few. Just like my fans, right? STARR: Well, if you say so... VARGA: That's right, I say so! Fans, you know who the man is, and tonight Goliath is gonna find out. I want some gold around this chubby waist, and me and Peer are gonna get us some. Hope you like water, Goliath, 'cause the Flash Flood's 'a-comin'! Peace, out! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Brian Williams and Gordy Alles, I want to help you two out in your cause against the jerk Brent Alles. I have seen enough to get sick to my stomach and I want to help out very much. In fact, if I may, I want to team up with you, Brian Williams against Brent "Ace" Alles, if you would. Alles, how could anybody do that to their own father?!? I am tired of seeing people like you run around in this league and destroy good people like this. In fact, I challenge you to a falls count anywhere match, Alles. You see, I know that you are nothing but trouble in this league and It is time that I put a stop to it. TAMARA WILCOX: Amanda Bloom, or whoever you are, you are nothing but a second class witch and if you ever try to touch Mondo I will knock you out. I am tired off hearing on how you two knocked out poor "Hammer" Williams and Gordy Alles. In fact, if you two want, I will get in the ring and beat you up, just like I did to Fat Boy Thomas. MONDO GECKO: Alles, GET READY FOR MONDO EXTREME ACTION. ALLES JUST A REMINDER, SINCE I WILL BRING THE LADY, MAKE SURE YOU BRING THE TRAMP! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Regulators are in the interview area.) REGULATOR FIRE: So the Masters of Hardcore want a match, well we are free anytime, boys, and we would like to challenge you to a two out of three falls count anywhere match. You see, we are tired of hearing you guys tell everybody how you guys are so big and bad. Hey, we are bigger than you two and we can be meaner tha you two when we want to be. So now, Ohlhoff and Couchman, you guys have gotten your wish and will be taking us on if the CWO sign the match. Bottom line is this: we are ready to win the tag team titles and if we have to beat the **** out of you two hardcore wannabes than so be it. TELL 'EM, ICE! REGULATOR ICE: WELL!?! Masters of Hardcore, you guys want a fight then you got it! We are tired of hearing from most of the teams in the CWO about how screwed over they have gotten. Well Masters of Hardcore, we don't have anything planned, so how about it then, you guys against us in a two out of three falls, falls count anywhere match. OOOOHHHH, WWHHAATT AAA RRUUSSHH! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Highlanders are in the interview area.) GAVIN McCLOUD: We've been quiet for a while now, but now I think it's time to speak up a little. Where have we been, I hear ye ask? Well, contrary to tha' rumours spread by our biggest fan, Dave Thomas, we havenae been down tha' pub. Not all tha' time, anyway. We've been in tha' gym, and in tha' tape room, gettin' ready for some of tha' biggest matches of our careers. CONOR McCLOUD: An' ye know what? It paid off for us, big time. We took care of tha' Wanderers an' tha' so-called Extreme Team at April Armageddon by using their strengths against them - who says we're just thick headed brawlers? We've proved to the championship committee that we're worthy of another shot at the titles... and at May-Hem, it us. It's the Bosses. An' it's not going to be for the faint hearted. GAVIN McCLOUD: Bosses, we didnae really rate ye when we joined this league, but since ye've left the entourage of that goose-stepping ponce, ye've impressed us with ye're attitude and ability; but make no mistake - when we hit that ring, we're going to do everything we have to to make sure we leave that building with the belts strapped round our waists. In our last title challenge, we got distracted by a war of words with tha' Dark Side - not this time. This time we've got tunnel vision, an' all we can see is gold. Just remember.... CONOR McCLOUD: THERE CAN BE ONLY ONE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area with Kellen, Jr., looking a LOT less crazy then he has been.) SANDMAN JR.: Focus, I said I'd take you out, and I did it! Now, it's time for me to get a belt! Maybe I'll take on Rogers again... or maybe Goliath... Maccabee? Or maybe I'll go right after that big idiot who knocked my buddy out of the CWO, "Monster" Rich Clark... Clark, I may not go after you just yet, but ALWAYS look behind your back, 'cause I'm gonna cane you to oblivion one of these days... count on it! As for this week, Baby Waldo, I've fought you and your cronies, this time I'm taking you out... and Steel, nothing against you in particualar, but I'm going to have to beat your @$$ too, because I AM THE EXTREME ICON!!! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: Hey Lust, who do you think you are? You think you can just help out a Sin when you want? You're out of the loop, get over it! SLOTH: Hey guys, what are we gonna do with our share of the cash from the Rock? WRATH (looking at House Show schedule): Umm... you shouldn't be worrying about that now, Sloth... look. SLOTH: WHAT? First I have to fight with Pride against those lame "Masters of Hardcore", and THEN I have to fight Clark? Who's great idea was THAT? PRIDE: At least we get to beat up Davis and Inferno some time later... that'll be sweet! WRATH: I get to put out the pistol of the Mexican... El Grande, you will be smoking, and not just out of you rear-end as usual. You will be cooked, fried, and delivered to McDonalds. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the team of DOOMSDAY. The Mercenaries flank Maximum Overdrive while Baby Waldo stands behind him. Waldo's neck is bandaged from the match against the Unholy Alliance on Fever.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Well, it looks like the Regulators want ANOTHER beating from us. I can't believe these two morons. When the hell are they going to learn? What in the hell is it going to take for us to knock some sense into their heads? (Korperal Punishment holds up a steel chair.) KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Maybe this will work. You know, Regulators, we saw your interview and are glad to hear that you're tired of playing games with us. Does that mean that you'll finally try to give us a challenge instead of getting your butts kicked like before? I hope so, because frankly, you don't make us look very good kicking your tails week in and week out. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: You know, we may have lost the CWO 6 Man Tag Team Championships the other night, but I think better things are in store for us. We've been tearing up the tag team ranks the past few weeks and we get a shot at the North American tag team champions, the Bad Guys, at MAY-HEM. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: You mean, the Bad Girls. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Oh yeah. Anyway, Bad Girls, we're not going to spend a lot of time like a lot of other teams that will go unnamed- KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Well, maybe we'll name one... KAPTAIN KRUNCH: All right. Well, like the REGULATORS, for instance. We're not going to spend five or ten minutes yakking it up, making all these stories about how we're going to take the titles from you and blah, blah, blah. You know it's going to happen, so why bother? We'll save our breaths and turn it over to the big man. MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: Some may call what happened to the team of DOOMSDAY Tuesday night a setback. Well, I don't. My boys holding the 6 Man Tag Titles proved what I've told the entire federation since we arrived here. We are capable of holding ANY title at ANY time. As for the Unholy Alliance, you just watch yourselves. We liked holding those belts and beating up all those chumps that thought they were going to take the titles from us. Sooner or later, we're going to get a rematch and the shoe's going to be on the other foot like it should have been at Fever. That's about all that DOOMSDAY has to say except I saw a few comments the other day from an old buddy of mine that I still have a score to settle with. That's right, Alles. By the way, good job on your flunky old man the other night. You must have had a pretty dysfunctional childhood, guy. But I guess you beat me to the punch. I was going to have that senile fool carted out on a stretcher. Maybe I'll have to find another way... but you know as well as I do that the book between us isn't finished yet. The next chapter still has to be written, and it's going to be in your blood. Just remember, Alles, when you least expect it, I'm going to be there.... _______________________________________________________________________________ Copyright 1997 Just B Cuz Productions http://web.syr.edu/~bgalles/cwo.html Cyberspace Wrestling Organization (CWO) All Rights Reserved
