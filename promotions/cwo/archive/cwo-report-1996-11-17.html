CWO REPORT 11/17/96

17 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on the entire Dark Side in a pre-recorded comment.) MOD DAMION: Tonight, all the CWO has much to fear. Tonight, myself and all my minions are in action and that means disaster for the rest of you. HELLHOUND: Master, tonight I will do good for you and I will get the win over the pathetic little wana-be Sandman Jr. MOD DAMION: Yes, my pet, you shall get the win tonight and may your feast be great. Also tonight, my assault on Ben Slocum continues as I send out the first lightweight champion of the CWO, Dark Raven. The newest member of the Dark Side shall go out there and do further damage to the man whose soul I hold in the palm of my hand. Slocum, tonight the deranged Raven will tear you apart for me. Slocum, by the time November Pain gets here you will be begging me for mercy and forgivness as we enter the ring. Also tonight, my Creatures are in action against the failing Mercenaries. NIGHT CRAWLER: Mercenaries, you are doing us a favor by asking for this title match to be inside a cage. For we will destroy you once and for all. Tonight my partner and I shall be together, unlike the Crypt, and together we are the best team in this league. Mercenaries, this is likely to be the bloodiest match ever in the history of wrestling all together. NIGHT SLINGER: Mercenaries, we are going to the ring with one game plan and that is to physically remove the two of you from the CWO forever. You were a part of the master's select few who were to be able to help us rid this league of Slocum and his pals, but now it is you two who are facing extinction. MOD DAMION: Like he said, Mercenaries, tonight you face extinction with nowhere to run or hide. Your very lives are on the line tonight, may you both REST IN PEACE! (After a short pause he continues.) MOD DAMION: As for myself, I am to face a former champion, Sandman. A man who just recently was thrown off a balcony by a man who also claims to control the night, but I showed him otherwise... isn't that right, Bane, and tonight, Sandman, if you dare show up I will show you too just who truly rules the night and the CWO. Sandman, time to put your LIGHTS OUT! For all who are to wrestle tonight, beware, for all the Dark Side is in the building creeping around in the shadows somewhere. ALL FOUR: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, November 17. We are one week away from CWO PPV excitement, NOVEMBER PAIN: ULTIMATE WARFARE, where teams of 4 will meet in deadly Wargames matches, the winners going on to a final two-ring battle royal, the winner of that gets to take on whoever the CWO World Champion is at CWO CELEBRATION in December. Before that, though, the superstars of the CWO are going at it all over the country, and last night was no exception, as we were in the heartland, Lincoln, NE, for a great card there. Let's look at the results. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 1996 LINCOLN, NE Wayne Carpenter vs. Tiger Rogers (w/Jimbo Jones) Rogers pinned Carpenter with the Inverted Power Bomb in 10:47. Sandman Jr. (w/Kellen) vs. Hellhound (w/MOD Damion) Sandman Jr. pinned Hellhound after the Permanent Dream flying legdrop in 10:41. Rooster vs. Johnny Reb (w/General Lee) Johnny Reb pinned Rooster in 5:09 after the Jackson's Revenge flying headbutt. MOD Damion vs. Talon (subbing for the injured Sandman) MOD pinned Talon after the Darkness Falls tombstone piledriver in 9:55. After the match, MOD continued to deliver piledrivers until Ben Slocum drove him off. "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum (w/Sondra) vs. Dark Raven (w/MOD Damion) Dark Raven pinned Slocum in 8:40 after the Death From Above flying dropkick. After the match MOD Damion delivered a Darkness Falls tombstone piledriver to the Slocum, and then, grabbing the house mike, claimed that he "has the power to knock you unconscious." TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Brent "The Ace" Alles (champion, w/Gold Kard) vs. Joe Eagle (w/Russell Hill) Alles pinned Eagle after a back body drop, using the ropes for leverage in 10:11. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Gold Kard (w/German Choklat, champion) vs. Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli (w/Russell Hill) Gold Kard pinned Manocelli in 11:03 after Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash). NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses (champions) vs. The Big Cool (w/Russell hill) Pit Bosses defeated the Big Cool in 14:21 when Pit Boss #1 pinned Jim "Jazzman" Taylor after the Spike Piledriver. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (steel cage): The Mercenaries (Kaptain Krunch and Korperal Punishment) vs. Creatures of the Night (Night Crawler and Night Slinger, champions) Creatures of the Night defeated the Mercenaries in 15:10 when Night Slinger pinned Kaptain Krunch. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Don't forget about the great SHOWCASE still to come today, and also next week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Here's the lineup for FEVER, 2 1/2 great hours of wrestling action: _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY, 11/19/96 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER BOSTON, MA TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (steel cage triangle match): Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Johnny Reb vs. Miguel Sanchez (champion) WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: The Dark Side (Hellhound and the Creatures of the Night, champions) vs. Unholy Trio (Sorrow, Payne, and Hate) Akillies vs. Phantom Freightrain Samoans vs. Highlanders Flyboy vs. Bane Mondo Gecko vs. Sandman Jr. David and Goliath vs. Bad Guys Joe Eagle vs. Wildfire Eternal Wanderers vs. Steel and Panther DARK MATCHES (not shown on the televised broadcast): Hangman vs. Savior "Master of Pain" Barry Hart vs. Ganglord Talon vs. Sin Judgment vs. Maximum Overdrive _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you saw on the House Show Report, Sandman was obviously not able to make his match against Master of Darkness Damion. Sandman is still recovering in the hospital after Bane threw him off a balcony on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. We take you now to CWO correspondent Christine Ferrara for a special report on Sandman. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Christine Ferrara is in a press conference room at LA County Hospital.) CHRISTINE FERRARA: Thank you, Johnny. I'm here at LA County Hospital, waiting for a press conference on Sandman's condition. It will start in about 1 minute. Let's take a look back at how Sandman wound up in the hospital. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Footage from TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER is shown of Bane and Sandman in the balcony, Bane has the Merciful Death sleeperhold locked on.) MELINN: And you can see what is happening here, Bane has Sandman locked in the Merciful Death sleeperhold, security trying to get to these two guys, but the Apocalypse, Plague and Pestilence, are holding them off. Sandman and Bane brawled up to the balco ny during the break. THOMAS: Sandman is out, Melinn! MELINN: Wait a minute... what's Bane... he's lifting Sandman up... OH MY GOD! Bane just threw Sandman off the balcony! THOMAS: I don't believe it! MELINN: Sandman crashing through an awning, at least that broke his fall, but my god... Sandman is not moving... and look at Bane, he's staring down at Sandman! And if you could see behind that mask, I bet you would see laughter! This is sick! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Christine.) FERRARA: That makes me quiver every time I watch that. AH! Here is the doctor now. (Doctor John Rupke enters and stands before the microphone.) DOCTOR JOHN RUPKE: Ladies and gentlmen, fans, press. You are all here to hear about the condition of the CWO wrestler Sandman. I will say this, Sandman WILL NOT be able to wrestle at CWO NOVEMBER PAIN: ULTIMATE WARFARE, in his WARGAMES match against Bane and the Apocalypse. Sandman has suffered a severe concussion, he will be out of wrestling for 3 weeks. My medical staff and I will keep a close eye on him to make sure his condition does not worsen. Thank You and I will take a few questions now. FERRARA (turning to the camera): WHAT A SHOCK! Sandman's friends will have to find a new member to help them against Bane and his minions at WARGAMES. Or will they have to forfeit the match or suffer a handicap? I will try to get a word with Sandman about the doctor's decision later. As for now, this is CWO correspondent Christine Ferrara, back to you, Johnny. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Thank you Christine, we hope to also have comments from Talon and the Flame Spirits about their team's outlook on WARGAMES now that Sandman will not be able to compete. Meanwhile, a team looking to make a name for itself in the CWO is the Bad Guys, we have comments from them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Bad Guys, Joe "Mean Guy" Rucker and Ron "Rude Guy" Johnson, are in the interview area with their manager/parole officer, Herbie Versmells.) JOE "THE MEAN GUY" RUCKER: Man, this fed is a joke. You all are a bunch of wimps, whining and complaining whenever someine hits you with a phone, a chair, or a spectator. Don't y'all get it? This is WRESTLING! Wrestling is FIGHTING! And there ain't no rules when it comes time to throw down. RON "THE RUDE GUY" JOHNSON: This is crazy! You've got those two pansy-ass punks, the Armpit Bosses, whose only hope of retaining their titles is to have someone else interfere. Those Samoans? They look like Richard Simmons on steroids! The tag team picture in the CWO is pathetic. We'll take on all comers. Challenge us to a no-DQ match, and we'll see who the real men are. HERBIE VERSMELLS: I've been consulting with my associates, Mike Hunt and Winnie Feltersnatch, and they've assured me that Ron and Joe are by far the most talented two men in the whole CWO. Soon, Ron will have the Light Heavyweight title and the team will have the Tag Titles, and there is no way any of you wimps in this fed can do anything to stop it! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: One of the big WARGAMES matches at next week's NOVEMBER PAIN PPV will be the Tower of Power, Joe Eagle, Vincent Manocelli, and the Big Cool, going up against Gold Kard International, Gold Kard, Brent "The Ace" Alles, and the Pit Bosses. We take you now to comments from both the ToP and GKI. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Tower of Power is in a meeting room, discussing the week's action.) RUSSELL HILL: All right, boys, let's figure this out... JOE EAGLE: What's to figure? No matter WHAT we do, we can't catch a break! JIM "JAZZMAN" TAYLOR: Yeah! Did you see our match? You guys come to the match, to take in a great battle, and what happens? Those Spit Bosses attack you, and get US disqualified! HILL: Where's the Bad Guys? They're the only ones who got a fair shake last night! EAGLE: If I have to destroy every person in this league,... HILL: Hold on, Joe. You saw what Bane stooped to. Could you ever do it? EAGLE: That was pretty low. Even I wouldn't do that to Sandman... and he's... well, he's the only one I couldn't beat twice. Now I might not get my chance. There's only one way for us to get to the top, boys. That's to stick together. If we come apart at the seams, that's where they can get us. Right? ALL: Right! (As everyone leaves, Vince Manocelli is heard muttering "Even if it means my chance to return to the title, I have to stop GKI.") _______________________________________________________________________________ (Gold Kard International are in the interview area, laughing.) GOLD KARD: Well, well, well! Here we are, one week from the demise of another "supergroup" in the CWO. BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: That's right, Joe Eagle, maybe you thought you were a "legend" back in the CM2WF, but you were a LOSER, I should now, I had to try to sell tickets with you as champ! GOLD KARD: But after next week, it will not matter, for you shall be where all "legends" go, in the ground! Only one team's walking out of WARGAMES, and it's going to be GKI! PIT BOSS #1: Big Cool, by now you've realized that you're never going to touch these North American belts. PIT BOSS #2: That's right. But you've been a thorn in our paw for so long now that we're taking that thorn out, and crushing it into dust. BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: Joe Da Eagle, bring your "Ace" bandages, because you will be bleeding so much, everybody's going to look at your loser Philadelphia jersey, and think it's Christmas come early. GOLD KARD: Gold Kard International! Don't get into a cage without it! (GKI laughs as the camera fades.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: A recent newcomer to the CWO, Ganglord has quickly made a name for himself as someone not to be messed with, especially with his career-threatening attack on Tub-O-Blub at TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. We take you now to comments from Ganglord. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera fades in to GangLord training with T. A. S.) T. A. S.: Well, G, looks like there are a lot of people here who don't like us much, so we's gots a few challenges to reply to, first of all, to this 6 man tag thing goin on, sure man, we're in if you are, that is if you think you can handle it. GANGLORD: Yeah, man, that sound like a helluva lot a fun, but I have noticed one problem, there's only one of me. T. A. S.: Ah, yes, I've been workin' on dat since none of da boys dat you used to chill wit from back in da hood can come here to help, prior commitments to the court, I have decided to do this, that's why the camera is here, this is an offical notice: ANYONE IN THE CWO WHO WANTS TO TEAM UP WIT DA GANGLORD, SPEAK UP SO WE CAN DO DIS. GANGLORD: Yeah, dat's good thinkin' and now Wildfire, boy, you dont's know when to shut your mouth, do ya, well I will just have to shut it for you then, punk. T. A. S.: Yeah, and that little hoe of yours too! (Camera fades out as T. A. S. and GangLord begin talking about prospective partners.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Nobody works harder on training than the Four Aces, we were recently able to catch up with them during a training session. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The camera opens on the Four Aces in a tag match, Flyboy and Clark Gray against Hollywood Muscle. It's pretty obvious that HM is only going half speed, as the Pair of Aces run through some of their more famous moves. Finally, Flyboy calls a halt and addresses the camera.) FLYBOY: Lafayette, Rooster, make sure your insurance is paid up. Clark and I are going to take you sons of fishes apart once and for all! CLARK GRAY: You bet! You scumsucking b******* are among the foulest... WAYNE CARPENTER: No offense, Lord Foul! GRAY: ...most miserable pieces of dirt ever put on this earth -- and we're going to put you UNDER IT! STEVE KELLER: And then we'll set our sights on someone else... (pointing to the camera) it could be YOU. ALL: FOUR ACES... FLYING HIGH! (Camera fades out as the Four Aces resume training.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you Tuesday at FEVER!
