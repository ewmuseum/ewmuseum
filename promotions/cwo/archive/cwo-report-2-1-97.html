CWO REPORT 2/1/97

1 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, February 1, 1997. Last night, the CWO continued its wild New England tour, stopping in Montpelier, VT. Let's look at what went down on that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE RESULTS 1/31/97 Montepelier, VT Biblical Powers defeated D. E. A. T. H. when Moses pinned Green after the armbar takeover. Time of match: 10:55. Joe Eagle and Baby Waldo wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Jason 'Flash' Varga and 'Monster' Rich Clark wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Johnny Reb defeated Hell Hound by disqualification when Master of Darkness Damion attacked Reb when he had Hellhound in the Texas Cloverleaf. MOD then delivered the Darkness Falls tombstone piledriver to Reb, prompting Mason-Dixon to make the save. Time of match: 10:16. Tiger Rogers pinned Phantom Freightrain after the head to knee. Time of match: 11:56. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Moses pinned Mondo Gecko after the armbar takeover. Time of match: 8:23. Jackass Joel pinned Sloth after the body slam. Time of match: 17:57. The Big Cool defeated Hoss and Little Joe when Jumbo Jenkins pinned Hoss after the Star Flash. Time of match: 3:34. 'Master of Darkness' Damion pinned Maximum Overdrive after the vertical suplex. Time of match: 5:53. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wild Ones defeated Creatures of the Night when Mondo Gecko pinned Night Crawler after the flying dropkick. Time of match: 17 : 40 _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Concord, NH, let's look at the lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, FEBRUAY 1, 1997 CONCORD, NH The Big Cool vs. Deadly Sins Johnny Reb vs. Ganglord Mason-Dixon vs. Revolution TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams Pit Bosses vs. Eternal Wanderers Gold Kard vs. Miguel Sanchez Bane vs. Maximum Overdrive 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Coldfront and "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. the Darkside (Hellhound and the Creatures of the Night, champions) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: The thing everybody seems to be talking about these days is the feud between Johnny Reb and Master of Darkness Damion. This feud has seen its highs and lows, but it is at a fever pitch now, and at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, Friday, February 14, in San Francisco, CA, these two men will take each other on a very dangerous Barbed Wire Towel match. In MOD's corner, former CWO champion Rebel Yell. In Reb's corner, his manager, General Lee. Both of these corner men will have a tough choice to make, allow their man the choice for revenge, or stop the fight before serious injury occurs. We take you now to comments from Master of Darkness Damion. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on MOD Damion, standing in a dimly lit room. He steps forward to speak.) MOD DAMION: Sandman, you got one thing right last night that I heard while I sat watching the CWO report, and that was that there is a lot happening in the CWO right now around its top superstars. You are having problems with a number of people who are chasing you in the ranks and on top of all that you what to try and prove that you rule the night. Well, Sandman, I believe those idiots, those drunken b*******, the incompetent Highlanders put it best: "There can only be one," and dream boy, I am that one. I rule the Night, I own the DARKNESS, you had a chance to prove things different but you could not. I pinned you, I proved true that I am "The Master Of Darkness." Soon, Sandman, I will be adding to the Dark Side, soon you will see once again that I rule the NIGHT, soon, I will beat you in the middle of the ring again, and soon, JOHNNY REB, I WILL EXTRACT MY REVENGE ON YOU. Last night, Johnny, you got in the ring with one of Hell's Pets, one of the wildest, most untamed beasts in all of the CWO. Johhny, I had great pleasure watching my pet tear you apart limb from limb. Hile Troy, you got a lucky win over me and now you have double your trouble, not only do you now have a so called ruler of the night, Sandman, after you, but also I, the true ruler of the powers of the NIGHT, MOD Damion, after you. You will suffer the true consequences of getting in the way of the forces of the Dark Side, the powers of the Night. See you all in my "Forever Night". LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (With that MOD disappears into the shadows of the room.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Master of Darkness Damion is correct in saying that a lot of turmoil surrounds the top superstars of the CWO. If Sandman hasn't already picked enough fights, he now has this intense newcomer after him as well. Let's hear from Bloodbath. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Bloodbath is in the cemetary with a now open grave reading SANDMAN.) BLOODBATH (mysterious voice): Now you have made a GRAVE mistake! By accepting my challenge! Well, yes, you did defeat me, but as the old saying goes, you learn from your mistakes! I now know what you're capable of! I have felt the Sandblast, and have learned to adapt to it! You want it in a cage? That's just fine with me! But let's up the stakes a little! I challenge you to a steel cage buried alive match! I will take your title and bring it to the dark side! YOU WILL REST IN PIECE! (Bloodbath's expression seems to get darker.) BLOODBATH: Now I have a message for Bane! I know that you have been embarrassed! I know you want revenge! Well I too have been embarrassed, and I too want revenge! We have a match on Tuesday Night Fever next week! I will prove to you that I am a worthy partner! Come to the realm of the dark side and it will show you the way! (Bloodbath jumps into a coffin sitting next to the grave laughing as the camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
