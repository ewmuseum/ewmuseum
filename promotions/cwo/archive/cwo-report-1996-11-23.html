CWO REPORT 11/22/96 (fwd)

23 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with "Master of Pain" Barry Hart in a cowboy hat and spurs.) "MASTER OF PAIN" BARRY HART: HI-YO, SILVER, AWAY! (Starts humming the theme to the Lone Ranger.) Lonestar, you want to wrestle me. the man who will be the first ever two time CWO World Champion. The man whose BUTT RULES! The man who took the CWO by storm and is now about to put it away. I always heard that old saying, what is it now... uh... oh yeah, I remember now. Only steers and queers come from Texas. There isn't anything in there about wrestlers coming from Texas. How long have you been in this federation anyway? Can't be too long. Why in the hell do you want to wrestle the #2 ranked wrestler in all of the CWO and the #1 ranked Light Heavyweight contender and former and soon to be reunited with my gold CWO World Champion belt. Lonestar, you aren't even gonna get a chance to be a star, let alone be a Lonestar. You made a big mistake coming after me and now you will pay for it. When I put the Cardiac Arrest on you, I will put on you tombstone, "Here lies the Lonestar, died trying to be something he isn't, A WRESTLER!" HA! HA! HA! By the way, Lonestar, don't stare too long at my butt, that will only cause more embarrassment than I am already gonna give you. MY BUTT RULES! (The scene ends with Barry using the cowboy hat to wipe his butt.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Friday, October 22, 1996. The superstars of the CWO were in the Bahamas last night, soaking up a little sun and kicking a little butt, let's look at the results of that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 21, 1996 BAHAMAS Panther vs. Johnny Reb Reb made Panther submit to the Texas Cloverleaf in 13:07. Mason-Dixon vs. Deadly Sins Sins defeated Mason-Dixon in 10:59 when Pride pinned Mason after a moonsault. Bane vs. Master of Darkness Damion Bane defeated Damion by disqualification when the Creatures of the Night attacked him when he had the Merciful Death sleeperhold on Damion. The Creatures were in turn attacked by the Apocalypse, and all three men brawled all over the arena before Bane fled from law enforcement officials. Mad Doctor's Ghost vs. Akilles MDG pinned Akillies with the Sudden Death splash in 6:17. 'Lonestar' Mike Williams vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart Hart pinned Williams in 14:55 in a tough match after a savate kick. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Dark Raven vs Talon (champion) Dark Raven pinned Talon in 12:45 after a savate kick, regaining the championship. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Flame Spirits vs. Pit Bosses (champions) Bosses defeated the Spirits in 17:11 when Pit Boss #2 pinned Griffen after the Spike Piledriver. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Samoans vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) Samoans defeated Creatures of the Night by disqualification in 5:42 when Master of Darkness Damion and Hellhound attacked them, breaking a pinfall attempt. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Schillo pinned Alles after the Keuka Crack kick to the chin in 11:11. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in Gulf Shores, AL, let's look at the great lineup planned for our Alabama fans tonight! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 22, 1996 GULF SHORES, AL Deadly Sins vs. Highlanders Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli vs. Mad Doctors Ghost Unholy Duo (pAYnE and Hate) vs. The Bad Guys Sorrow vs. Johnny Reb STREET FIGHT: David & Goliath vs. Biblical Powers MOD Damion vs. "Big" Rich Clark GangLord vs. Akillies Inferno vs. Bane NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Gold Kard (champion) vs. Joe Eagle CHAMPION'S CHALLENGE: World Tag Team Champions Creatures of the Night vs. North American Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: One of the great matches on the card tonight is between Johnny Reb and Sorrow. Reb's been on a roll as of late, let's get comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb appears with General Lee in the interview area.) JOHNNY REB: Well Sorrow, we all meet again. Y'all are gonna step in tha ring with ol' Johnny Reb in mah home state? Have you got manure fo' brains? GENERAL LEE: Mah man has been on a tear as of late. Sorrow, y'all are just another one who will fall to the Texas Cloverleaf! REB: Ah've made y'all say "uncle" once... ah'm gonna do it again, son! Ah'm dedicatin' this match to the people of the great state of Alabama. Gulf Shores... Sorrow. Come an' get some, boy. LEE: Good day, suh. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also on the card is a match between two of the most intense competitors in our sport, Inferno and Bane. We now go to comments from Bane. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Setting: It is in a dark cold ally at night. Garbage cans containing fires lit up the errie setting. Bane is seen at the end of the ally and he is slowly walking forward.) BANE (his menacing, bone chilling voice echoes): I am expected to extinguish you this Thursday, Inferno. Believe that I will. You wanted to meet your destiny, because it would boost your pathetic rank. Remember that the brightest flame dies the quickest, as will be demonstrated with you shortly. You, however, are not the only idiotic individual in this league. It seems that Pussycat was complaining about my affairs with Sandman. Well Pussy... cat, that is none of your business. You should have learned to stay out of affairs that do not concern you. (A drunken bum stumbles up to Bane.) DRUNK: E-e-ecuse me-e mista. Y-you wouldn't... (The man doesn't even finish his sentence. Bane grabs him by the throat and lifts him into the air. The man grasps at Bane's hand to no avail. He cannot get a word out of his throat.) BANE: You cannot escape your Bane, Pussycat. I will hunt you down, and I will have your hide. You should tuck your little tail between your legs, and run away. Your Bane shall arrive later. (The bum's body is now limp in Bane's hand.) BANE: There is one more aspect that amuses me. It seems that the law has now decided I'm not fit to be part of MY society. You stupid people. When shall you learn that Bane is held by no laws. I am the Bane of society, and nothing can ever retard my aggression. I am bound to no law, and you are foolish to believe I am. I do what I want, I take what I want, and I destroy who I want. I murder the murderers, I steal from the thieves, and I rape the rapist. (Just then a beat cop comes strolling down the ally looking for crime. He turns a corner and comes across this hellish spectacle.) Cop: Holy Sh*t! It's Bane! (The cop, trembling, fumbles for his gun. Bane throws the bum who groans when he lands. Quicker than a battlecat, Bane thrusts towards the officer and connects with a punch. The officer crumbles, and Bane picks him up. He applies the Merciful Death and holds it on for several minutes.) BANE: This is your law. It no longer protects you. Your Bane has arrived. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny, who sits in silence for a moment.) STARR: Well, as you can see, Bane has gone way over the edge, and somebody from the CWO needs to stop him, can Inferno do the job? It remains to be seen. Now, to other news. They beat them in a match by disqualification this week, and now Blood Inc., Panther and D. E. A. T. H., has a special challenge for the CWO World 6-Man Tag Team Champions, Hellhound and the World Tag Team Champions Night Slinger and Night Crawler, the Creatures of the Night, together known as the Dark Side. We take you to the comments from Blood Inc. _____________________________________________________________________________ (Blood Inc. with Da Boss are in the interview area.) DA BOSS: Dark Side, we now have beat you on two occasions, I think people around the CWO are starting to see who the most dominating three man combination in the world is. D. E. A. T. H., along with Panther, have proved that they are the elite combination. Now I say, let's give you one more try to beat us. Although we have beaten you on two occasions, you still have OUR belts. GREEN: Dark Side, it's kind of pathetic having champions like you here. Without some outside help, we would be wearing the belt right now. You know that we had you, it was all over! BENTON: Dark Side, next time, that is if you are men enough to sign the dotted line again, let's do this thing right. PANTHER: That's right, Dark Side, let's put this thing in a CAGE! You heard it right here, Dark Side, we challenge you to a cage match! DA BOSS: Now Dark Side, we wouldn't want you to feel all lonesome so I let my Man Steel here have some fun with one of the top ranked wrestlers here in the CWO. STEEL: Dark Raven, you are a JOKE, how did you get to be ranked #3?!? Your screams of pain were music to my ears. So tell me, Raven, how does it feel to be GANKED?!? BLOOD INC. (all in unison): To all the fans, you can KISS OUR @$$! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Currently on a leave of absence, Lord Foul the Depiser still makes his presence known from time to time. We now go to taped footage of the Despiser. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The camera hovers over the top of Lord Foul's Blackpool Castle. The stone structure itself stands boisteriously like an icon of strength jutting up and out of the mountains it was cut from. Set within, the individual towers point into the sky like an accusing finger, as if Foul were making a challenge to the mighty himself, after zooming in, we see Foul up close.) LORD FOUL (with a whimsical voice): I have seen the tape that has been broadcast over the airwaves of the CWO. My eyes have seen the atrocity that has been cast down upon the land I used to call home. The entire CWO, it would seem, has found the very epitomy of what is rotten and vile in this world, the CWO itself seems to have found it's Bane. (Lord Foul moves over to the edge of a tower.) LORD FOUL: Bane, you are but one piece on this masterful chess board of the CWO. In chronological comparison to myself, you are but a pawn, lost and homeless in this world like a ship that cannot find it's way back to the shore. For a man who claims to be intelligent, you have failed to study your enemy. For whom was it that crippled the former President of the CWO at a pay per view? Me... and who was it that met a challenge set forth by the President and at the same time ended the career of Brent "The Ace" Alles, at least for a time? Me... you will come to realize that you are nothing more than a shadow, a mere semblance of what I am. (Foul peers over the edge of the tower and looks down into the churning ocean, where water meets and smashes over the rocks that inhabit the shore.) LORD FOUL: You know Bane, there is a great deal of foreshadow of your life in those rocks down there. You stand adamant and ignorant, much like the stone which lies at the salty bottom... and I, much like the water, crash in and takes a small piece of you before returning back, only to smash into you again, and cripple you slowly, but surely. Bane, you are an ignorant fool, I have dealt with your kind since the inception of time itself, and I have always triumphed... no, I will NOT be at the next pay per view to slaughter you, nor at the next pay per view... for good thing's come to those who wait. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: You can bet fans of the CWO are just waiting to see the eventual matchup between those two superstars. However, as much as Bane has been vilified by many, some actually support his actions, if you can believe it. We now take you to comments from Ganglord. _______________________________________________________________________________ (GangLord and T.A.S. are sitting on a poarch talking to each other.) GANGLORD: What is all this crap, all these people goin' after Bane because of what he did, sayin' it was dishonarable an all dat, well, Bane, I think what you did was great, you saw a job that needed to be finished and you took care of it, now that's what I call a man who knows his priorities, right, T? T. A. S.: Yeah, Bane did exactly what should have been done, now Akillies, we will not lose another match, especially against you, we will finish the job we started! GANGLORD: Yeah, CWO, now it's our turn! (Camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally today, since returning to the CWO in his new identity, Sin has been gaining a lot of ground. We now go to comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ SIN: Hey, fools... Sin here... guess what... I've been approached with a lot of stress as of late... my fellow brothers have "removed" me from being a priest.... and as you all may have heard... I brought wrath upon them... I'm no longer a priest of the Temples of Syrinx... and no longer do I have faith in my previous religion. The only one I found true WAS the prince of darkness. I was lead astray by these con artists... the priests... no longer will you see me praying or happy. It's all down hill from here... and to tell you the truth, I'm out to win now. Not just to let my presence be known. All you wrestlers better watch your tails. Cause the SinMan is going to be Sinning REAL soon. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT. Also, look for a special CWO REPORT EXTRA: THE STORY SO FAR for the upcoming NOVEMBER PAIN PPV, sometime this weekend, check your local listings for time and station. Until then, this is Johnny Starr, saying so long for now!
