CWO REPORT 2/27/97

27 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on MOD Damion, who stands stroking the head of Hellhound, who is sitting at his masters feet.) MOD DAMION: Bishop, do you see this! Hellhound is totally content in the Dark Side and has no desires to leave, for he knows the punishment that would be coming his way. That torture and punishment is what you will feel starting tonight, Dark Raven. We had one match prior to you being welcomed to the Dark Side, and in that match I dominated you and now yes, you are indeed a better wrestler, but still, you are small, and compared to myself and Hellhound, you are much weaker and not as talented. We will prove this tonight. Bishop, you have caused enough havoc that tonight the Dark Side begins the turn around and shows you that you are nothing and that I, the "Master of Darkness" Damion, is just that, the MASTER. Ask anyone, I own the NIGHT, I am the fear that the CWO knows. Tonight I will take your souls and discard them because to me they are worthless empty shells. Tonight I rule! LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera fades to black as Damion finishes.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Masher Maccabee, the last time we met it was under your rules, your advantages, and you had help from your weasel manager. Back home we have guys like that on the street, we call them con-men or hustlers. Well in London, home of Big Ben and Wimbleton, the TV title will be returned to its rightful owner when I kick your @$$ and win back my TV belt. You had your fun as the TV champion but now it is time for me to get Mondo Extreme on your sorry @$$. SO when I kick your teeth in and get my belt back I will be partying with Tamara, Fergie, Princess Di, and the Wild Bunch, drinking tea and eating crumpets. GOD, I LOVE BEING A WILD ONE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Regulators are in the streets of London to give an interview.) REGULATOR FIRE: Bad Guys, you two think that you are real bad asses but we beg to differ. We have had our eyes on the tag team gold for a long time and we are dying to get our hands on those belts. Rude Guy & Mean Guy, you two punks are going to be brought to justice and we will reposess your belts. It's time that the people have true champions and that Law is served. REGULATOR ICE: Rude Guy and Mean Guy, it's time for you two scum to hand your belts over to the new generation stars. You guys are a bunch of thugs who need to be beaten up and turned in to justice. For years the CWO has needed justice and that is what we will give it. So let the games begin for JUSTICE WILL BE SERVED! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in to control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT, live via satellite her in jolly ol' England, London, to be exact. Our thanks to SKY TV for the access to a satellite hookup. Yesterday there was no report as we here in the CWO were busy flying over to the British isles for the CWO's BRITISH INVASION tour this week. Last night the superstars of the CWO rocked London's Wembley Stadium, let's see how the card went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS Wednesday, 2/26/97 Wembley Stadium, London, England Ganglord and Wrath wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Vlad the Impaler pinned Baby Waldo after the Long Kiss Goodnight (bite to the neck). Time of match: 17:21. Vlad the Impaler was attacked by the rest of Doomsday after the match - prompting the Unholy Alliance to make the save. The two foursomes then brawled back to the dressing room. Da Hood Rats defeated Deadly Sins by disqualification. Time of match: 8:07. Moses pinned Serpentmaster after the The Commandment. Time of match: 7:57. 'Master of Darkness' Damion/Hellhound defeated Bishop/Dark Raven when 'Master of Darkness' Damion pinned Bishop after the Darkness Falls. Time of match: 21:27. Joe Eagle and Sandman wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. The Big Cool defeated Mercenaries when Jumbo Jenkins pinned Kaptain Krunch after the inside cradle. Time of match: 16:17. CWO TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Champion Masher Maccabee pinned Mondo Gecko after the Jerusalem Jacknife followed by Homentaschen Hangover. Time of match: 14:07. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Champions the Bad Guys defeated Regulators when Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker pinned Regulator Ice after the Bad Guys' Bash. Time of match: 26:43. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wildfire defeated champion 'Monster' Rich Clark by countout. Time of match: 13:52. 'Monster' Rich Clark was attacked outside the ring by Goliath - the two then brawled back to the dressing room - causing the countout. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we head up to Glasgow, Scotland, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FEBRUARY 27, 1997 GLASGOW, SCOTLAND Sandman Jr. vs. Hellhound Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Master Of Darkness Damion Moses vs. Sloth Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Johnny Reb Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Maximum Overdrive 8-MAN ELIMINATION MATCH: David "The Destroyer" Davis/Goliath/Inferno/Tiger Rogers (The Alliance) vs. The Unholy Alliance (Bishop, Dark Raven, Serpentmaster, Vlad the Impaler) WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Wild Bunch (Regulators and Battlecat) vs. Doomsday (Baby Waldo and Mercenaries, champions) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, street fight): The Bad Guys (champions) vs. the Highlanders CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE (STEEL CAGE): Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (World Champ) vs. Masher Maccabee (TV champ) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: The former Light Heavyweight champion, Moses, has had a bit of trouble getting his belt back from Tiger Rogers. Here now is an intense encounter with Moses. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Moses is throwing stuff around in the dressing room, including the contents of his locker: a bible, a cross, and many other things. He seems very pissed off about the officiating as of late here in the CWO.) MOSES: TIGER ROGERS, YOU ARE THE LOWEST PIECE OF HUMAN GARBAGE ON THE FACE OF THIS PLANET! I CAN'T BELIEVE THAT YOU NEEDED HELP FROM INFERNO TO BEAT ME ONCE AGAIN! IF YOU CAN'T DO IT YOUR OWN SELF, THAN YOU SHOULDN'T EVEN STEP IN THE RING! NOW, I WANT YOU IN THERE, ONE MORE TIME, THIS TIME WITH MANAGERS BANNED FROM RINGSIDE, TWO REFFERIES TO KEEP THE INTERFERECE NON-EXISTANT, TITLES TO CHANGE HANDS ON COUNT-OUT AND DQ, AND A SCIENTIFIC TEST OF SKILL MATCH, WHICH MEANS NO BRAWLING. THEN WE CAN FINALLY SETTLE THIS, ONCE AND FOR ALL, WITH NO INTERFERENCE TO HELP YOU OUT. YOU'LL BE A SITTING DUCK FOR MY UNBELIEVEABLE ATTACK, AND WHEN I GET THROUGH WITH YOU, YOU WON'T BE MAKING IT TO SPRING BREAK, BECAUSE YOU'LL BE IN A HOSPITAL RECOVERING FROM THE MOST SEVERE BEATING OF YOUR LIFE. IT TAKES A REAL MAN TO WIN A TITLE WITH NO HELP, AND TO KEEP IT IN THE SAME FASHION. IT TAKES A MAN OF GOD TO BRING DOWN A CHEATING HEATHEN LIKE YOU. YOU BETTER START YOUR TRAINING, SON, BECAUSE YOU'LL NEED ALL THAT YOU CAN GET TO KEEP THAT BELT. I GUARANTEE THIS, SIGN FOR THE MATCH, AND I'LL TAKE THAT TITLE FROM YOU INSIDE OF TEN MINUTES. IF I DON'T IT BECOMES A NON-TITLE MATCH AT THE TEN MINUTE MARK, AND YOU WON'T HAVE TO WORRY ABOUT LOSING ANYTHING. IS IT A DEAL, ROGERS? I SURE HOPE SO, BECAUSE THEN I HAVE ABOUT NINE AND A HALF MINUTES TO KICK YOUR ASS BEFORE I PIN YOU. (He starts whipping stuff around, punches a locker, and then walks out of the room.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: On Friday in Dublin, Ireland, Benjamin Blake Bentley continues his feud with one of the latest newcomers to the CWO, the Focus. Here now are comments from the Focus. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: OK, I'll keep this simple. Benjamin Blake Bentley... on February 28, we meet again in Dublin. I beat you last time, and I will beat you again. Period. I am afraid that you have brought out the bad side in me. I will not rest until you show me the respect I deserve, and admit that I am your peer. You will lose again, Benjamin, and I don't care how I score the victory. Call it extreme, but you will see a side of the Focus you will never want to see again. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We haven't heard from them in a while, but the Eternal Wanderers finally break the silence with these comments. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens up in a localized region of the Andes.) EXILE: Oh Lord, Oh Lord... why have you forsaken me? (Shivering... he stands near the wall... a tiger is sneaking behind him. Suddenly it pounces... Exile, reacting with the instinct of a mad lost man, grabs the tiger and tosses it. The tiger renews its attack. Suddenly, Exile spins and punches it in the eye. The Wounded animal tries to retreat as Exile jumps on it and pummels it. He keeps pummeling and pummeling until suddenly, Exodus arrives. He grabs Exile, tossing him and trying to restrain him. Exile, lost in bloodlust, attacks again, only to stop at a whispered: "Stop.") LORD GURU (whispering): Stop. (Exile drops to the ground, mesmerized.) EXODUS: My Lord... why is this happening? (a whispery and tired voice) Why is he like this? LORD GURU: It is the hatred... it is the anger. Repressed he has been, he loses his mind. (Sighs.) However, we must focus it, harness it. I have a mission for you... (looks into the eyes of Exodus, who cringes) you must fight a singles match. I think It is the way for both to go. However... (looks at Exile) you are still a tag team. I need you to show the world that you still are champions. (Smiles.) EXILE: Darkness, oh Darkness... (sigh) EXODUS: Regulators? (Smiles.) Regulators... you have sneaked past us for too long. It is time that we show you what it means to face the Cabin. And Pit Bosses, there will be another time. For you fought your kind of match... WE challenge you to our kind of match. An Apocalypse Fire Match. Ring of Fire, and burning coals outside the Ring. You want a real fight? (Laughs out loud.) Bring it on! For the Eternal Wanderers will be champions! LORD GURU: Excellent. and soon the CWO will find out a little secret.. (a whipered voice) or a big secret. (Fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Another man on the brink of madness is Sandman Jr., we take you to comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area, he has a crazy look in his eye.) SANDMAN JR.: Bloodbath... I'm sorry... I didn't want to have to hurt you, but I DID have to... I have to hurt everyone now... it's not something I want... there's a reason for this... "Monster" Rich Clark is that reason... I thought you were a man, almost a friend there, Rich... but then you got Sanchez knocked out of the CWO... I ain't gonna let that stand... I'm gonna get revenge for Miguel sometime, Clark... and I won't be responsible for what I do to you... but first... Hellhound... you and all that Darkness crap is going to the wayside... I'm gonna crush you, dog-boy... then on Showcase... Johnny Reb, one of the best wrestlers in the CWO, bar none... well, Reb, like I've been saying, I won't be responsible... and I'm sorry.... Then in 2 weeks, at SPRING BREAK '97... I can earn a title match... all I have to do is get past Talon and Gerstung... you both will feel my pain, but more importantly, you'll feel your OWN PAIN! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: On this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, an 8-man match between the Unholy Alliance and Doomsday exploded into a 16-man match, as the Dark Side, Brent "The Ace" Alles, and the Deadly Sins, all attacked their respective enemies. The Deadly Sins have made Doomsday their targets, and they will be going up against them for the CWO World 6-Man Tag Team Championships at SPRING BREAK '97. We take you now to comments from the Sins. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: Mercenaries and Doomsday... you fellas really aren't bright at ALL! Everyone wants to kick your ass, and we're MORE than happy to oblige! SLOTH: But first, we just want to remind those drunken morons the Highlanders that we WILL get to you sometime in the future, and it won't be pretty. PRIDE: First, me and Sloth get to destroy those ghetto-trash Hood Rats... you boys are SOOO dead... any friend of Bane is an enemy to us! WRATH: Like YOU, Ganglord! I will tear you apart, beat you with your seperated limbs, and thoroughly DESTROY YOU! SLOTH: Then, for some reason I have a singles match... Moses, evil WILL beat good... and I am SO VERY EVIL! PRIDE: Then we get to take on those morons, the Mercernaries! Prepare for the total ass-kicking we should've given you MONTHS ago! SLOTH: Finally, we get a title shot at Spring Break... the 3 of us vs. the Mercs. and that fat Waldo guy... who wears a diaper... man, that is messed up... you 3 WILL go down, and there WILL be new 6-man champs! THE DEADLY SINS! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: The Deadly Sins may have new opponents at SPRING BREAK '97, if the Wild Bunch has anything to do with it. Tonight in Scotland, it's the Wild Bunch against Doomsday for the World 6-Man Tag Team Titles. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wild Bunch is in the interview area.) BATTLECAT: Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries, YOU HAVE SOMETHING WE WANT AND WE WILL TAKE IT. Last time you guys escaped with a double countout to hold on to your belts, this time we will not be denied, for the Wild Bunch will win the belts. The fans of the CWO need a three man team that they can be proud of as champions. Baby Waldo, that is all you are, a big fat baby, and while the Regulatores take care of your friends, I will show you why you never send a boy to do a MAN'S JOB! REGULATOR FIRE: Mercenaries, we have beaten you two thugs before and we are ready to take your six man titles. It has been a long time since we got to take soem trash out of the CWO and that is what we will do. Mercenaries and Baby Waldo, we are ready to do one thing and that is win the six man belts, and we will succeed. REGULATOR ICE: The countdown has begun and soon the Wild Bunch will be the six man tag champions. Doomsday, that is what it will be for you. FOR THE WILD BUNCH WILL RULE THE WORLD, BABY! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: On this week's FEVER, Joe Eagle had a dangerous encounter with Ganglord. We now go to his reaction after this intense matchup. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Joe Eagle is in the interview area with the TOP. His ribs are heavily taped.) JOE EAGLE: Ganglord, I don't know who you think you are, but NOBODY treats Joe Eagle like this. You drop me through a table, and think I won't be back? WRONG! My ribs might be achin', but Eagle's still a-bakin'! And this time, just to make sure things stay even, I've got my "boyz" with me... JIM "JAZZMAN" TAYLOR: Watch out, pal, 'cause things are gonna get bad REAL QUICK! EAGLE: I've had it with the shoddy treatment the vets are getting from the new guys around here. I've paid my dues... Joe Eagle might have been down for a little while, but he's back with a vengence! I am a former World Champion... and NOBODY is going to treat ME with that kind of disrespect! Ganglord, you better get your troops together, 'cause you'll NEED a gang behind you. The TOP is on the loose, and ain't nobody stoppin' us now! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: As we've talked about before, that 8-man match on FEVER this week devolved into an all-out brawl between many different factions. Two of those factions, Maximum Overdrive and Brent "The Ace" Alles, will meet at SPRING BREAK '97 in a Lumberjack Match. Here now are comments from both Doomsday and Brent "The Ace" Alles. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the team of DOOMSDAY in the locker rooms after Tuesday Night Fever. Maximum Overdrive is being attended to by the medical staff, who are taping his ribs following the Allesbreaker given to him by Brent "The Ace" Alles. Baby Waldo is sitting in a corner sucking his thumb and the Mercenaries are facing the camera. Kaptain Krunch has his neck bandaged after getting bitten by Vlad The Impaler following their 8 man tag match. Korperal Punishment is holding the CWO tag team rankings for the past week.) MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: I commend you, Alles. Not too many people have done to me what you did tonight. I'm going to take enourmous pleasure in running you out of this federation. (Overdrive winces as another layer of tape is added.) OVERDRIVE: Of course, I will be back in action soon. I see that you and I have a series of meetings scheduled for the upcoming week. You thought you knew what pain was about? Get ready to play the Pain Game, punk. Now my Mercenaries have something they want to say. (Korperal Punishment holds up the CWO Tag Team rankings.) PUNISHMENT: What I am holding in my hands are the tag team rankings for all the CWO tag teams for the last seven days. What I want to know is how can a team like the Deadly Sins be ranked higher than us when they have one of the worst records in the league? How can a team like David and Goliath rank above us when they have a record below .500? KRUNCH: Somebody's got to learn how to count, or somebody's screwing the Mercenaries over. I think Randy Johnson had better get off his fat butt and get this mess sorted out. It's about time he started to earn his paycheck, instead of getting paid to watch the "Dukes Of Hazzard" reruns on TNN. PUNISHMENT: The last week saw the Mercenaries take on three teams and defeat two of them. This week, three more will fall, including Pride and Sloth. KRUNCH: Of course, we've got a little more than just beating them in mind. I guess they just couldn't handle it after Overdrive ran their little loser buddy Sin, out of the CWO. Guess what, Sins? You're going to find out just how smart he was. The Mercenaries are declaring war on any and every tag team in this federation until we take our rightful place as the CWO World Tag Team Champions. (The Mercenaries turn and watch as Overdrive is being attended to.) PUNISHMENT: Of course, if you wind up in the same condition as Overdrive here, consider yourself lucky. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is in a London cafe.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: You know what, Overdrive? Everybody's been asking me, "Ace", why did you do what you did on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER? Are you insane. (Chuckles.) Well, why don't you tell me, Overdrive? As I've said many times before, there are no limits to the depths I will sink to beat a man. You think you're a "bad" man, Overdrive, because you hang out with a bunch of synchophantic thugs? All that proves is that they are as big of idiots as you are. I want you to keep that memory of the Allesbreaker, though, Overdrive, because whether it's in Glasgow, Scotland, tonight, or at Club La Vela in Panama City, FL, you will feel it again, my "friend," this I promise. CO1 - EOT. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Finally today, the Pit Bosses have a shot at SPRING BREAK '97 for the World Tag Team Championships. So far they have been frustrated in their battles with those champions, the Creatures of the Night, but they feel they've got what it takes to put them down when the right time comes. We take you now to the Pit Bosses. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Pit Bosses are shown in a Monte Carlo casino.) PIT BOSS #1: Seeing that we were closer than we usually are to Monte Carlo, we figured we'd check it out. PIT BOSS #2: That's right. What a dump! Give us Vegas anyday. PIT BOSS #1: No way anything beats the City of Lights, and there's no way anything beats the Pit Bosses either. PIT BOSS #2: Creatures of the Night, we'll admit, we're having trouble with the equation concerning you, but being the mathematical geniuses we are, rest assured, we will figure it out. PIT BOSS #1: Club La Vela, as the sun sets in the sky and the heat from the beach steams into the cool night air, many people say that we should be worried, taking you goofs on in your element, the darkness. Well, as we've said before, when it's most dark, we turn on the lights, and then we put them out, as we Spike Piledrive you into the ground. PIT BOSS #2: And finally, Wanderers, get a grip, get a life, and get a clue. We're finished with you two goofs. We've got nothing more to prove. We whipped your tails so soundly that they're STILL talking about it. Hot Coals of Doom match, or whatever the hell you call it, please. Come back when you're ready to deal with reality, boys, the reality that you got your tails whipped by the premiere tag team in the CWO today, the Pit Bosses, and just as you two mumbo-jumbo mystics found out, those other two horse-blinders idiots, Night Crawler and Night Slinger, will find it out as well. PIT BOSS #1: TAKE IT TO THE BANK! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today on the REPORT. We'll see you tomorrow!
