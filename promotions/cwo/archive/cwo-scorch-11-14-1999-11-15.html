HAHAHAHAHAHA!

MARKO: Did you watch the same match I did? The kid had game, Les. How loud did I have to shout and how hard did I have to hit before I got my crack at the spotlight and my first belt here?
SHEETS: I thought Alles gave you the TV strap as a ploy to keep you in the fed, so they could get me to re-sign when I was out of my coma? (smirks)
MARKO: Smartass. The point is, Max deserves a shot. I don't expect much out of it, I mean, he won't take the title, but at least it gets him closer to the top of the card.
SHEETS: Whatever. You're the boss, Kong. (leaves) MARKO: Yeah. Yeah, I am. (The CWO logo is show on the screen in 3-D... as a chair comes crashing through the screen. Heavy metal music plays as we show a montage of CWO action. We go to a roaring crowd in New Orleans and
"Double K" Krash Kelly and Talson Edward Geddy) KELLY: Hello and welcome to CWO Sunday Scorch! I am "Double K" Krash Kelly and with me as always is Talson Edward Geddy! GEDDY: Your learning! Man Mountain Marko comes out to the ring in street clothes, with his North American title over his shoulder. He has sunglasses pushed down to the tip of his nose, and, as he makes his way to the ring, he scans the crowd. He stops midway between the entrance and the ring, and raises the belt high in the air with his right hand. The crowd, surprisingly, gives him props in the form of pops, as a sizable cheer erupts from the crowd. Marko smirks, makes his way into the ring, and grabs the microphone.)
MARKO: Heh. Like your cheers mean a damn thing. No, let me just lay down the law for you people. There's a reason I came out here alone tonight.
(The cheers turn into a chant of "JAILBIRD! JAILBIRD! JAILBIRD!") MARKO: Cute. Real cute. Yeah, I spent some time in prison last week, like, all of 24 hours, before Lester came and bailed me out. Lord Hile Troy. (Crowd cheers.) MARKO: And here's the conclusion I have reached: (pause) Number one: Yakuza. SASD, you and me have been through thick and thin together, we started out when it was just you and me and LeFonQue, rental-caring through the mountains of northeastern Tennessee. I want you to know, I got your back, you and Hakujin. Roast Beef and Deliverance aren't gonna be able to come within 50 feet of you, all you gotta do is say the word.
Two, Maximum Overdrive, you got cajones, boy, to step to me and try to take me on. I respect your talent, but respect only goes so far, before I have utter contempt for you. And contempt, as I know, will get you nothing but pain.
Three, Lord Hile Troy, look you limey bastard, I don't have a problem with you, but I do have a problem with people who have a problem with me. It must suck to be little more than a tool to the front office. You call yourself a competitor, but you let yourself get led around by the nose...
special "enforcer" for Gordy my Italian ass. Oh, and you step into my square again, when you and I aren't booked in the same ring, and I'll knock your rotten yellow teeth down your throat.
Four, Gordy Alles. That display of power I showed you on Fever is but a taste of what I can do. I said it before, I'll say it again, you got beef with Lester, you beef with Lester. I've been busting my ass for far too long for this company to let front-office politics keep me down now. Not when I can get access to a mike anytime I want. So, if you can't get things with you and Les taken care of, that ain't my fault. 'Cause the next time I have to show you who's really in charge, I'll do it, at the expense of your health.
Five, Lester Sheets. "The Professional." Les, me and you go way back. You got me started in this business, and it's about time I let you know, I appreciate everything you've done for me, from the bottom of my heart. Fed me and gave me a roof over my head when I was just starting out, kept my name alive when Larry in EWA wanted to fire me, kept me booked when I was getting paid by appearance in UWF. But, for everything you've done for me, it's what you’re doing to me now that I'm having difficulty with. See, this thing with Alles is hurting both of us, G. And it's about time it stopped, all right? I know you're at home watching, and I see my time out here is about at an end, so I'll put it to you slow, and in words of one syllable: This thing with Gord needs to stop, and stop soon, or.... I'll be forced to walk.
(Crowd gasps and some cheer.) MARKO: That's no threat, 'cause I don't make threats, I make warnings and promises. (Marko pauses, starts to say something, but thinks better of it and walks away.) Then, Alles asks me to be on his team. I agree, being a nice guy and all. So, after being the next-to-last man eliminated, I figure I'll finally have my shot. But no, the front office decides to stick me in a feud with Simon.
