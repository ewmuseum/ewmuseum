CWO: Operation Rebirth Update

12 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Johnny Starr is in the CWO control room.) STARR: Hey, fans, Johnny Starr here with you, and it has been announced -
Saturday, April 22, the CWO will be REBORN as everything's up for grabs! A
15-man tournament for the World title, with the man who last held the title before it was vacated, Maximum Overdrive, receiving a bye, will kick off the first show of the "new era." Yes, SATURDAY NIGHT FEVER will kick off with the first round of the tournament, here are the current brackets:
(Tournament brackets are displayed:) 1. Maximum Overdrive (tentative) 16. BYE 8. Mustafa L. King 9. "The Flash" Patrick Houston 5. Jersey Shore 12. Christoper "Spot" Light (tentative) 4. Wildfire 13. 6. Johnny Reb (tentative) 11. "Ultimate Fighting Machine" Richard Gracie 3. Foolkiller or Eddie Fantastic (tentative) 14. 7. Master of Darkness Damion 10. Allen "Mr. America" Carter (tentative) 2. Gold Kard 15. STARR: Now, some of those men are listed as "tentative", as contact negotions are still going on with them. We will of course keep you posted.
(An image of the Darknight Express appears to the side of Starr.) STARR: In other news, the Darknight Express have re-signed with the CWO, and you can expect them to continue to make waves in the tag division.
However... (An image of the Beauregard Boys comes up.) STARR: Rumor has it that a deal is close to being reached with former CWO
World Tag Team Champions the Beauregard Boys, and their manager the Big Cat.
Of course, the Boys had the titles vacated on them almost right after they won them, so they'll have a lot of pent-up frustration to take out on the denizens of the CWO, you can be sure!
(Regular shot of Starr.) STARR: More to come of course, but keep tuning in, as we begin the countdown to the new CWO!
