Are you looking for some awesome E-wrestling action? Well, then look no further, because the CWO is here to fulfill your need, daddy-o!
Originally starting out on a local BBS in Grand Rapids, MI, we're expanding out into the 'Net. So, if you're just getting into E-wrestling and need a good first league to start in, the CWO is for you. However, we provide enough "sophisticated" angles, flashes, and matches to satisfy veterans as well.
Every week we have House Shows reports on Saturday, Sunday, and Monday. These cover two House Shows per report. Also, we have a live "televised" card every week as well, TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. And, we have a PPV (Play
Per View) roughly every month.
The CWO currently uses TNM 6.0 (soon to become 6.1) to simulate the matches, although there is some "booking" to advance the angles, etc.
So, if you are interested, get in touch with me at the address below, and
I'll send you a character sheet. Currently, I'm going to allow two wrestlers per person. You can submit a tag team, two singles wrestlers, two singles wrestlers who work as a team, whatever.
So, if you think E-wrestling was fun before, you ain't seen nothin' yet! Join the CWO today! Brent G. Alles CWO Commissioner E-mail address: [detail withheld]
