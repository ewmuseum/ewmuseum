FEVER 9/23/99 - EXTRA

September 24, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Back to FEVER. A heated argument is going on between the members of Millennium - Lord Hile Troy, Gold Kard, Diamondback Chris Myers, and “Airborne” Mark Fulcrum – and Allen “Mr. America” Carter and Geoff “The
Attitude Adjuster” Bondavarsky. Myers and Fulcrum are holding Troy back, who’s trying to get at Bondavarsky.)
TROY: BLOODY BASTARD! BONDAVARSKY: I said it wasn’t my fault, Troy! CARTER: Whoa, whoa, calm down, gentlemen! Let’s look at the bigger scheme of things here! We’ve got some Elite tail to kick!
MYERS (coldly): Yes we do, Carter, but let me tell you this. That idiot friend of yours Bondo, doing what he did, that’s not going to go unanswered.
So tonight, we’re gonna kick Gecko’s tail, but this isn’t settled, Carter, not by a long shot.
(Millennium leave as Carter glares at Bondavarsky.) BONDAVARSKY: What? It wasn’t my fault! (The camera hovers over the capacity crowd to the center of the ring, where
Tony the K and Referee Jeremy Lang stand waiting)
TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a forty-five minute time limit! Introducing first...
("Stars and Stripes Forever" by John Phillips Sousa plays as the fans rise to their feet cheering!)
TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 809 pounds, accompanied to the ring by Geoff "The Attitude Adjuster" Bondavarsky, the team of "Mr. America"
Allen Carter, "Diamondback" Chris Myers, and "Airborne" Marc Fulcrum! (The men come down to the ring, Myers and Fulcrum now keeping their distance from Carter and Bondavarsky.)
MELINN: Millennium issued the challenge tonight as a sort of birthday present to Hile Troy, and the Elite were quick to answer it. Thus, we have the six-man grudge match before us.
THOMAS: And this close to September Slam IV, this match is going to give a lot of steam to somebody. But you can see, that they’re not a happy team anymore after what Bondo did to Troy!
MELINN: An honest mistake, but one that’s not sitting well with Millennium, and you heard what Myers said to Carter, that what Bondo did was not going to go unanswered. I wonder what that meant?
THOMAS: Do you want me to draw you a picture? MELINN: Wildfire and Fulcrum will meet in what is a highly anticipated battle, a "unification" match of sorts. Wildfire will put his Light
Heavyweight Title up for grabs against Fulcrum's Cruiserweight Title. The winner will get to name the belt and the division as he wishes.
THOMAS: This is just going to be a warm-up for Carr... and Brent Alles had better watch every second of it. For Gecko and Carter, this will just be a taste of things to come.
TONY THE K: And their opponents... ("Escape From the City of Angels" by Ithaca plays as the fans boo.) THOMAS: I swear these guys have different music every week! How do the fans know to boo? MELINN: I guess their powers of deduction are a little better than yours are, Truth. THOMAS (chuckling): Good one... Melinn! TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 799 pounds, accompanied to the ring by
Tamara Wilcox and Lt. Cammy LeBeau, representing the Elite, the team of
Mondo Gecko, Carr, and Wildfire!
(The Elite head down to the ring.) MELINN: We've said it a hundred times already, but obviously the big task in this match will be to get past that monster, Carr.
THOMAS: They'd be best to let Carter and Myers worry about Carr, they know how to handle the big men. The real test will be for Fulcrum to try and match two high-flyers in Gecko and Wildfire.
(The Elite enter the ring, glaring at and sizing up their opponents.) MELINN: There was at least some sort of unity between the True Americans and
Millennium here. However, that all changed in our last match, and now they are a fractured team.
THOMAS: They certainly are! And they both know who they have to look up to, and that's the Elite!
(It is decided that Fulcrum will start off... against Carr!) MELINN: The smallest man from one team starting against the largest man from the other team! A world of contrast for Marc Fulcrum here!
THOMAS: I hope he knows what he's getting into! (Carr and Fulcrum both move in for a lockup, but Fulcrum ducks!) MELINN: Fulcrum dodges the big monster... kick to the midsection, chop across the chest, a second one... no effect!
THOMAS: He's just smiling at Fulcrum! MELINN: Carr grabbing Fulcrum and he TOSSES him into the corner! The big man charging in... Fulcrum dodges out of the way! "Airborne" unloading on
Carr in the corner...
(Carr charges out with a clothesline, but Fulcrum ducks it! Marc then goes back to hammering away on Carr as the crowd pops!)
MELINN: Fulcrum with a rake to the eyes, and he can just NOT get this man off his feet! Fulcrum off the ropes... flying forearm!
THOMAS: He's got him rocking! MELINN: Fulcrum coming at him again... he ducks a clothesline from Carr... back off the other end... (The crowd gives a heel pop!) MELINN: Carr has Fulcrum by the throat! He lifts him up... CHOKESLAM! THOMAS: He could pin him right now if he wanted to, after that ONE move! MELINN: Carr tags out to Wildfire... the current arch-nemesis of Fulcrum, and those two men will meet this Sunday in a “unification” match.
(Wildfire quickly goes to work stomping away on Fulcrum.) MELINN: Wildfire hammering away on a dazed Fulcrum, Carr really put him down with that chokeslam.
THOMAS: And then he was generous enough to tag out to let his little buddy go to work! He does have a heart!
MELINN: Wildfire sending Fulcrum in for the ride... reversal... Fulcrum with a spinkick, Wildfire ducks it... the Elite member off the ropes...
(The crowd pops!) MELINN: SUPERKICK! Wildfire with a superkick! THOMAS: Atta boy! MELINN: Wildfire catching Fulcrum getting back to his feet, swinging neckbreaker! Fulcrum getting the short end of the stick thus far in the contest.
THOMAS: He might be able to hit and run, but the Elite can hit him hard enough to put him down and keep him down!
MELINN: Wildfire headed up to the second rope... flying dropkick! Gecko wit h a wide grin on his face as he watches Fulcrum get dismantled.
(Wildfire bounds off the ropes, but is hit from behind!) MELINN: Wildfire headed for the ropes, but Carter caught him with a clothesline to the back of the head! Fulcrum getting back to his feet…
(The crowd pops!)
THOMAS: Ahhh! MELINN: Fulcrum with a kneelift right to the face! Wildfire has been busted open! Fulcrum with a cover...
(ONE…) (TWO...) MELINN: NO! Wildfire got his shoulder up just in time… now bleeding from the face. (Fulcrum tags out to "Diamondback" Chris Myers.) MELINN: The man who issued the challenge, former World Champion, Chris Myers has hit the ring!
(Gecko and Carr both enter the ring as well!) THOMAS: Here we go! MELINN: Carter now in the ring as well, and all six men are going at it! (Fulcrum and Myers hit Carr with a double dropkick, knocking him out of the ring... Gecko and Carter dive towards each other!)
MELINN: Gecko and Carter... Gecko and Carter! They're hammering away at each other in the corner!
THOMAS: Give him the left, Gecko! The left! MELINN: Wildfire back to his feet... Myers sends him in for the ride... SPINEBUSTER! (On the other end, Gecko Irish whips Carter across the ring.) MELINN: Carter with a reversal... Gecko into the corner, Carter charges in... Gecko gets his knees up! Gecko out of the corner... spinkick!
THOMAS: Hell yeah! MELINN: Give him credit, pound for pound he's probably the best athlete in the CWO, but he isn't the legal man, Wildfire is! Myers off the ropes now... spinning elbowdrop!
THOMAS: Why the hell does he want to make himself dizzy with that damn move? MELINN: Myers off the ropes a second time... nobody home! Wildfire kips back up to his feet... standing moonsault! He hooks the leg!
(ONE...) (TWO...) MELINN: NO! Myers kicks out at two! THOMAS: These guys are going all out tonight! Just look at how fast they're moving in the ring.
MELINN: Wildfire bringing Myers back to his feet... knife-edge chop across the chest, a second one... Myers fires back with a hard left hand! He throws a wild one...
(Wildfire ducks the punch, catching Myers in a waistlock.) MELINN: Wildfire with a waistlock... Myers reverses it into a rollup! (ONE…) (TWO...) THOMAS: Only one guy uses a rollup like that, and he's an old fart! MELINN: Definite signs of Troy there with that reversal. Both men back up to their feet...
(The crowd pops!) MELINN: RATTLER! MYERS DROPPED HIM! HE DROPPED HIM! THOMAS: Quit shouting, Melinn! Damnit all! MELINN: Myers isn't making the cover? (Myers points across the ring... to Gecko!) MELINN: Myers wants Gecko, and the World Champion is more than happy to answer the call! (Gecko enters the ring, somehow being counted as the legal man.) MELINN: Here we go, we are in overtime, here on Fever! This is it! THOMAS: Better be careful what you wish for, Myers! (Myers moves out to lockup with Gecko, then shrugs, spins around to his corner and tags Carter instead! The crowd erupts!)
MELINN: Carter quickly in the ring... he tackles Gecko! THOMAS: Break it up, Lang! (Carter and Gecko roll around the mat hammering away at each other.) MELINN: Throw out the scientific wrestling, throw out the aerial skill, this is just a damn fight!
THOMAS: And Gecko is winning! Winning! MELINN: I dont know what fight you're watching! Carter bringing Gecko to his feet... he's got him up by the throat!
CARTER (shaking furiously): DIE COMMIE! (Carr quickly enters the ring, blasting Carter in the back of the head with a hard kick!) MELINN: Oh no! (Carter drops Gecko and falls to the mat.) THOMAS: You can't get around that! (Carr is made to leave the ring.) MELINN: Gecko now headed up to the top rope... and he's like a bird to water up there... he leaps off... flying dropkick! Right on the mark!
THOMAS: Perfection! Pure perfection! MELINN: Gecko staying on top of "Mr. America", sending him in for the ride... another dropkick and Carter goes down.
(Myers shouts words of encouragement from the outside.) MELINN: Gecko bringing Carter back up... and Carter catches him off guard with an elbow sma...
(Gecko quickly knocks Carter down with a clothesline!) THOMAS: Off guard, eh? MELINN: Gecko picks it back up as quickly as he drops it, and now he tags
Carr back into the match. Allen Carter had better get back to his feet and fast!
(Carr enters the ring and screams to the crowd, a la Vader.) MELINN: Carr measuring Carter as he gets back to his feet... big right hand rocks the former World Champion. Carr with a second one, he sends "Mr.
America" into the ropes... reversal... THOMAS: No big deal. MELINN: Carter with a clothesline, Carr still on his feet! Carter with an elbow smash, and still Carr is on his feet!
THOMAS: And Carter's no small man either! MELINN: Allen Carter off the ropes... (The crowd pops!) MELINN: Jumping sidekick! That'll put Carr down! Carter follows it up with an elbowdrop and makes a cover...
(ONE…) MELINN: No! THOMAS: Trying to pin Carr is going to be a waste of time. They might as well focus on Gecko and Wildfire, not that that'll help them enough.
MELINN: Carr getting back to his feet unfazed... Carter with a rake to the back! He bounds off the ropes... another jumping sidekick puts Carr down a second time!
(Carter makes the tag to Myers.) THOMAS: They better enjoy it while it lasts, it's only a matter of time before Carr hits someone with the Fatality... he doesn't give a damn if
Gordy Alles has banned it or not!
MELINN: Myers off the ropes... spinning elbowdrop! He makes a cover... (ONE...) (KICKOUT!) THOMAS: They can't even keep him down for a two count! MELINN: Myers now tagging Fulcrum back in, and he's headed up to the top rope... he's signaling for the Air Fulcrum!
(Cammy LeBeau quickly leaps up onto the apron, giving Fulcrum an above look down her shirt. A slightly drunken Fulcrum can’t help but stare.)
THOMAS: HA! Shouldn't have celebrated Troy's birthday before the match, eh
Fulcrum? MELINN: Fulcrum getting his wits back, he leaps... (The crowd pops!) MELINN: NOBODY HOME! Carr easily rolled out of the way of that one, and now he's lifting Fulcrum up!
THOMAS: Just like I told you! MELINN: Carr with Fulcrum up in a gorilla press... and he tosses Marc across the ring! It wasn't the Fatality, but that doesn't mean that it didn't hurt!
(Carr moves slowly across the ring, stalking Fulcrum.) MELINN: Carr backing Fulcrum into the corner... _brutal_ chop across the
Chest... followed by a forearm smash! Another chop... big right hand!
THOMAS: He's tearing him apart! MELINN: Carr with a kneelift... he sends Fulcrum into the ropes... (The crowd pops!) MELINN: JUMPKICK! Fulcrum with a jumpkick... but it had no effect! He goes for a second try, and a dropkick sends Carr against the ropes...
(Carr wobbles and flips over the ropes out to the floor.) THOMAS: He's still on his feet, though! MELINN: Fulcrum with a sneer on his face... he heads off the ropes... PLANCHA! "Airborne" Marc Fulcrum over the ropes with a plancha! He lifts
Carr up and sends him into the cornerpost!
(Both teams eye each other wearily on the floor, not getting involved.) MELINN: All these guys are chomping at the bit, but they're smart enough to save some for Sunday.
THOMAS: I hope so! We sold a hell of a lot of tickets! MELINN: Fulcrum charging in... no! He was going for a running shoulder block, but Carr sidestepped it, and Fulcrum crashed into the post!
THOMAS: Pfff... idiot! MELINN: Carr rolling Fulcrum back into the ring, and he makes the tag to
Gecko. (Gecko comes in the ring, taunting Fulcrum to get up.) THOMAS: Gecko knows he's got him right where he wants him. MELINN: The World Champ pulling Fulcrum up, and he gets a kick to the gut for his trouble! Fulcrum sends Gecko into the ropes, reversal... Gecko catches him with a dropkick!
THOMAS: I think I've seen enough dropkicks for this match. MELINN: Gecko now once again headed to the top rope, measuring Fulcrum up... THOMAS: Time to show em' what Randy taught you, Geck! (Gecko comes off the top with a flying elbowdrop, and hits the mat!) MELINN: Yeah, that's about how much he learned, all right! Fulcrum now moving across the ring, and he tags Myers!
(Myers enters the ring, eyes seething with hatred for Gecko.) MELINN: Myers and Fulcrum both send Gecko in for the ride... double clothesline! "Diamondback" now off the ropes... spinning elbowdrop, but the
Champ moves out of the way!
THOMAS: Damn that move! All the stupid spinning! MELINN: Gecko grabbing Myers in a front facelock... leaping DDT! Right on target! (Gecko plays to the crowd, who cheer, only because Myers is back up!) THOMAS: How the hell? MELINN: Myers shaking his head in pain, but he's still back up! He grabs
Gecko from behind... german suplex!
(Myers turns Gecko around on the mat, picking him up and hitting him with a second German suplex!)
MELINN: Gecko paying for his momentary lapse of concentration! Myers now picking him up...
(The crowd pops!) MELINN: RATTLER! THAT'S how you do it! THOMAS: Tell that to Carr! (ONE...) (TWO...) MELINN: NO! Myers had a cover, he had it won, but Carr broke the count! Here comes Carter... flying forearm sends Carr out of the ring! THOMAS: Get in there, Wildfire! MELINN: Myers sends Gecko into the ropes... but Gecko comes back at him with a flying cross body block!
(ONE...) (TWO...) MELINN: Myers rolls it over! (ONE…) (TWO…) THOMAS: I can't look! MELINN: Kickout! Myers slapping the mat in frustration... he makes the tag to Fulcrum! (LeBeau leaps onto the apron again, temporarily distracting both Myers and
Fulcrum, until Myers shoves her off the apron!)
MELINN: LeBeau sent off of the apron... Myers now rolling to the outside of the ring to help Carter against Carr.
(Gecko moves in from behind, hitting Fulcrum below the belt!) THOMAS: That alone should put Gecko on a cereal box! MELINN: Gecko stumbles over to his corner and tags Wildfire, and now we've got all four non-legal men on the outside of the ring brawling!
(Referee Lang is distracted by the chaos on the outside, and doesn't see
Wildfire grabbing his Light Heavyweight Title!)
MELINN: Wildfire with his belt... he charges at Fulcrum! (The crowd pops in bloodlust!) THOMAS: Goodnight! MELINN: Wildfire just slammed Fulcrum in the back of the head with his title belt... a title belt that may not exist after this Sunday, and now he's headed up to the top rope...
THOMAS: He's going to finish him off in style! MELINN: Wildfire leaps... TORNADO DDT! He makes the cover… (ONE…) (TWO...) (THREE!) THOMAS: They got it! Ha ha! MELINN: Once again Wildfire and his belt prove to be a nuisance for Fulcrum, but it got them the victory, right before the PPV no less! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners of the match, in a time of 15:52... The Elite! MELINN: Wait a minute… damn it, no! Carr’s got Carter up in a Gorilla
Press… and he drops him on to the steel steps!
THOMAS: A variation on the Fatality! (Carr rolls Carter back in the ring as Wildfire continues stomping away on
Fulcrum, stomping him out of the ring.)
MELINN: Bondavarsky trying to avenge Carter but Carr’s got him by the throat… CHOKE SLAM OUTSIDE THE RING!
THOMAS: He’s unstoppable! MELINN: Gecko sending Myers into the steel steps, and now… oh no! Gecko throwing a ladder into the ring!
THOMAS: He’s going to get the ladder match started earlier! MELINN: This is sick! Here comes Lord Hile Troy and Gold Kard down to the ring! (They get in but Carr quickly grabs them both by the throat!) MELINN: For the love of god! DOUBLE CHOKE SLAM! DOUBLE CHOKE SLAM! THOMAS: Alles, get out of the country, do NOT show up this Sunday in San
Diego! MELINN: Gecko… oh god… he trapped Carter’s leg in the ladder and stomped down on it! (Carter thrashes around in pain.) THOMAS: Carter’s not going to be doing ANY climbing when Gecko gets done with him! MELINN: Gecko tossing Carter into the corner… somebody stop this! (Carter is slumped in the corner… as Gecko takes the ladder… and drives it into his nether regions!)
MELINN: My god! My god! (Gecko takes the ladder again and this time charges, screaming, ramming it right into Carter’s face!)
MELINN: Please! Stop! THOMAS: It’s Do or Die, Carter, and I think we know what your choice is! Hah! MELINN: Carter bleeding from the mouth… god, he’s spasming… this is sick… and now look at this… that bastard of a World Champion… putting the ladder up… climbing to the top!
(Gecko displays his belt to the crowd from the top of the ladder as the boos rain down.) THOMAS: You the man, Gecko! (Carr and Wildfire both applaud.) MELINN: This is absolutely disgusting… (The lights go off!) THOMAS: What now?!? Dark Side?!? (The lights go back on… and all three Alleses are in the ring, Brent, Kent, and Gordy!) MELINN: It’s the Alleses! The Alleses are in the ring! (Carr lunges at Brent, but he ducks and kicks the big man in the midsection… and delivers the Allesbreaker as the crowd pops!)
MELINN: ALLESBREAKER! ALLESBREAKER! THOMAS: Get those idiots out of there! (Wildfire goes after Brent Alles as they start brawling. Meanwhile, Kent and Gordy got the ladder… and push it down, sending Gecko over the ropes and out of the ring to the floor! The crowd pops again!)
MELINN: Look at that! Gordy and Kent just did a number on that back-stabbing World Champion! THOMAS: This isn’t fair! MELINN: And now look at this… Gordy Alles has got Wildfire… ALLESBREAKER by
Gordy Alles! Can you believe it!
THOMAS: Kill them, Carr! (The Alleses raise each others’ arms as the crowd pops.) MELINN: Brent “The Ace” Alles has one chance in hell to survive that match against Carr on Sunday, and it is his father, Gordy Alles! A family reunited, here on FEVER! We are way out of time, folks, but what a night it has been! We’ll see you Sunday, at SEPTEMBER SLAM IV: DO OR DIE!
