CWO REPORT 7/24/97 (fwd)

24 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Joe Eagle and Jason "Flash" Varga are in the interview area) Eagle: So, Gold Kard, you're not enough of a man to take me on yourself? Fine. If you're bringing Toyota, or Hiroshima, or whoever that guy is, then I'm bringing the best tag team partner a guy could ask for... Varga: I AM THE MAN!!! Eagle: That's right, you are. GK, bring all your "buddies" to the ring tonight...I don't care. You can even bring Schillo, or Ninja Waynitsu, whoever he is today. I'll kick his ass as soon as I'm done kicking yours! Varga: Gold Kard, you've asked for too much, now. When you feel the power of the soaring Eagle, and then the SMACK of the Flash Flood, you're never gonna get up. You can't survive when you begin to compete at the Attitude Dance! ________________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey, seated at the CWO REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Hello everybody and welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. We have a lot to cover in this program, so let's get down to business. Monday night, the CWO Down Under tour wrapped up in Auckland, New Zealand. The prior interview given by Joe Eagle and Jason "Flash" Varga was taped prior to that card. Let's have a look at the results. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 7/21/97 Auckland, NZ 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat pinned 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia after the Jimmyplex. Time of match: 10:46. Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned The Focus after the stomp, getting a very fast count from GKI referee Spencer Doloff. The Focus then delivered a Focal Point to not only Bentley but Doloff as well! Time of match: 12:15. Deadly Sins defeated Steel/Panther when Sloth pinned Steel after the Repention. Time of match: 14:34. Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo defeated Brent 'The Ace' Alles by disqualification when Joe Eagle attacked him. Security had to separate the two. Time of match: 5:39. Regulators defeated Coldfront when Regulator Fire pinned Steve White after the Total Annhilation. Time of match: 8:58. Vegas High Roller pinned Brian 'The Hammer' Williams after the Vegas Swinger, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 14:17. Attitude Dance defeated Gold Kard/Matsuhiro Torunaka by disqualification when members of Gold Kard International attacked. The Big Cool came down, and another wild brawl similar to Sunday night's house show ensued. Time of match: 5:55. WASHEY: FEVER was held last night and it was a wild one. Here's a quick rundown of everything that happened. CWO FEVER 7/23/97 DES MOINES, IA Show opens with last week's confrontation between Wayne Schillo, Joe Eagle and Jason "Flash" Varga. Interview with Fergus McTavish Fergus McTavish pinned Marc Mission in 12:40 with the Scottish Fling. Sandman and his new partner, the Reverend, defeated Coldfront by submission in 12:04 after the Reverend locked on the surfboard style submission hold on Steve White. CWO TELVISON TITLE MATCH A wild match between challenger Benjamin Blake Bentley and Diamondback Chris Myers that ended in a double DQ. Sandman Jr. was in Myer's corner throughout the contest. After the match, GKI stormed the ring and were closely followed by Johnny Reb, Joe Eagle and Jason "Flash" Varga, and a wild brawl took place. Sandman came down and was knocked to the concrete by Sandman Jr. and a steel chair. Fergus McTavish and Billy also ran down to the ring, where Billy was able to put a good amount of Ben Gay in Benjamin Blake Bentley's trunks, causing him serious pain. Interview with Johnny Reb. Update on the condition of Serpentmaster and Vlad the Impaler, as well as developments concerning Bishop, and the wrestler formerly known as Dark Raven, Red Raven. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH Masher Maccabee pinned Red Raven in 14:15 with the Homentaschen Hangover, retaining his new title that was presented to him by GKI on last week's FEVER. Interview with Brent "The Ace" Alles, and his father, Gordy. Footage is shown from RID PPV where Gavin McCloud had his ankle broken by the Bad Guys. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH The team of Conor McCloud and Fergus McTavish, sustituting for the injured Gavin McCloud, won by DQ after the Bad Guys stormed the ring and attacked them with steel chairs. In turn, the Bad Guys were attacked by Korperal Punishment, who chased them out of the ring. CWO WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH Prior to this match, Gold Kard made the announcment that he would fight a randomly picked individual. That individual turned out to be Brian "The Hammer" Williams. Gold Kard won the match in 12:55, following Die Fleider Cross, and interference from not only Ninja Waynitsu, but also GKI referee Spencer Doloff, who actually struck Williams with a pair of nunchucks, setting Williams up for Kard's finisher. The show closes with comments from Ninja Waynitsu. WASHEY: Very disturbing developments at the end of last night's FEVER. But I must stress to everybody that Ninja Waynitsu has not been proven to actually be Wayne Schillo. However, with Schillo's refusal to discuss this matter, it may be left up to each and every one of us to decide for ourselves. Let's go to the interviews. Today, we start off with Lord Guru. Let's go to his comments. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Far in the Mountains of the Himalayas, One man is seen sitting at the Top of a cliff. He is serious, calm and quiet. His right arm, Garbed in a Silver casing is set over his legs. He is speaking softly, his voice an eerie echo. Suddenly Behind him, Comes out a man, A man who is huge. Standing at 7'2, 443 lbs, Judgement is an imposing Figure. He looks down at Exodus and stays behind him, quiet and serene. Finally, Exodus speaks) Exodus: My brother.. Judgement... *even tone in his voice* Does Our Lord Worry that I am Not ready? Does he send you to see how I progress? Yes.. I lost my first match here in the CWO after coming back. However, I have a goal. And that goal is to Get Even with Three men. My lord Understands that. Judgement: *his voice is thick and it booms* Exodus.. They have Been Judged. They are Guilty of their Crimes. However, Tome for you to look forward to the future. Exodus: *his voice echoes in a harsh tone* NOT YET! One of the TWO will pay... then The TWO will pay. *his eyes blaze* For *rubs his arm* This was their fault. And our belts.. WE lost them to the one that Remains. Exile is Gone on his mission.. Me, *smiles* I'm ready to begin mine. CWO.. Before, The Lords Cabin was something that you made fun off. Now... We are the L.O.T.R... The "Lords of the Ring." GKI, YOu will all pay. *his hands tighten over his right arm* Especially you.. High Roller. *his eyes blaze with Fury. And Lord Hile.. Their is only one Lord, and His name is GURU. <Fade out....> _______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up we have the duo of the former CWO World Champion, "Monster" Rich Clark, and a former CWO Television Champion, Miguel Sanchez. _______________________________________________________________________________ "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: Joe Eagle, I proved it on Sunday night down in Australia that your victory at SUMMER HEAT was nothing but a fluke. You are a sad pathetic FLASH IN THE PAN, and now that I've beaten you in a Coal Miner's Glove Match, I've proved that. Now, it's time that I get my shot at Gold Kard. I told you that I would beat Eagle, I KNOW I can beat you, but I'm willing to wait. For once the CWO World Title is in the back of my mind. I'm waiting for TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES, where I get to kick Sandboy's ass. This is gonna beat great. Not only that, but I have my best friend Miguel Sanchez at my side. It's going to be great. The time's coming for championships to be won, and when everything's settled, I'll once again rule the CWO. MIGUEL SANCHEZ: That's right Rich. You and me are gonna get the CWO Tag Team Championships, and I'll be right there beside you when you win the World Title. Now, I still haven't settled ths thing with Sandman Jr. He called me a sell-out, and I made him an offer. Sandman Jr., if you're listening. I challenge you to a match on FEVER, for the week after TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES, just to give you some time to prepare for me. Remember, Sandman Jr., I've already beaten you once, and I will do it again. Get ready for Miguel to kick your ass!!! ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Finally today, we have comments from the CWO North American Tag Team Champions, the Mercenaries. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, just after their DQ loss to McCloud and McTavish on FEVER.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Bad Guys, you must really want to lose those titles a lot sooner than you planned. Stick your noses in one of our matches again, and we're going to cut them off and hand them to you. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: It's no secret that we have a serious problem with a couple of idiots like yourselves holding the measuring stick of tag team greatness in this federation. But by getting involved in a match that we had as good as wrapped up, you've not only endangered your title reign, you've endangered your careers. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Our teams might be put together in the Team Challenge Series, but you'd better get out a dictionary and look up the meanings of our names. MERCENARIES. We don't look out for anybody but ourselves. You might be on our team, but the fact remains that you still have something we want. And that's something that we're going to take. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Ask the Regulators how bad we beat their asses. If a couple of schmoes like those two retards can take the titles you were holding, and we beat them, it tells us a lot about how easy it's going to beat you. Hell, I don't even know why we have these titles. We're not second best in the CWO, we are the top tag team in this federation. Maybe it's time that THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD goes out and proves it. _______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Well, that's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody on the REPORT tomorrow. So long everybody! (fade to closing credits.)
