MICHAEL POSTEMA: Now he can cheat and not have to worry about what the fans think! KYLE MELINN: Myers with an armbar applied now, forcing Dagger down to his knees. He snaps the arm, bending it at the shoulder.
(Dagger winces in pain, Myers then releases the hold and lays into Double D with a series of chops) KYLE MELINN: Myers chops Dagger across the chest, Dagger comes back with a forearm smash, he follows it up with a face rake.
MICHAEL POSTEMA: See, now that is how Myers has to do things! Go to the eyes, pull the hair, use low blows, do what it takes!
KYLE MELINN: Myers swinging blindly, Dagger easily scoops him up and slams him down to the canvas. Dagger with an elbow drop, but Myers rolls out of the way.
MICHAEL POSTEMA: C'mon Dagger, don't blow your chance! KYLE MELINN: Myers off the ropes, dropkick! He bounds off of the ropes, spinning elbow drop! He drops a second one, a third one!
MICHAEL POSTEMA: I hope you see this, POSTEMA! I hope you see this! KYLE MELINN: Myers waiting for Dagger to get back to his feet, he sneaks in out of the corner... RATTLER! Forget about it! Myers with a dominating title defense.
(Lang counts three, and Myers receives little to no pop from the fans) MICHAEL POSTEMA: Myers looks like he's going to cry! He's addicted to the cheers of these morons!

KYLE MELINN: Myers now finally with a smile on his face as he spots Dallas, and he's asking for a microphone from Tony the K. MYERS (tapping the microphone to get it in order): A little late, but your still here, and that's all the counts, I've got to talk to you, and to all of these people out here.
MICHAEL POSTEMA: I'd love to see that video pop on the screen right now. MYERS: Now... I know that you all saw this so-called video with myself and Chris Greco in the locker room. I know you all heard the things that

MICHAEL POSTEMA: What an egoist! The more he does those things, the more fans he gets, the more the CWO pays him. Your a sucker Myers, you got caught, just admit it!
KYLE MELINN: No one wants to hear what you have to say, Postema! MYERS: But now... I've got to ask everyone for just ONE thing in return. I've got to ask that you ask yourselves, "why?" Why would I hide in back and say these things? You know I'm not a shy guy, and just WHY, of all people in the world, would Darkness be the one to have a video of this?
