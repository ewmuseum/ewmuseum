CWO RE-GENERATION: Accepting New Members!

4 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO RE-GENERATION: ACCEPTING NEW (AND RETURNING) MEMBERS! from fed-head Brent Alles ( [detail withheld] ) The CWO, an e-wrestling organization in existence in some form since 1995, is now accepting new handlers (as well as re-accepting our past handlers as well). We only accept "made-up" wrestlers (meaning, no wrestlers from the
WWF, WCW, etc.). Try to avoid being a "clone" of a "real" wrestler as well; originality helps. (By the way, if you're a female handler with a female wrestler or a male handler with a female wrestler, feel free to contact me as well. If we get enough interest, we might even have a Women's division. We'll see.)
WHAT DOES IT MEAN TO BE A CWO HANDLER? Simple! We need to bring your creativity and enthusiasm for e-wrestling to the CWO. If you've had a character in the CWO before, we welcome you back with open arms. If you've been in other e-wrestling federations before and want to try something new, we're happy to have you and your prior experience. If you're new to the world of e-wrestling, you have a place here too! One nice thing about the CWO is our wealth of experience with this stuff; we will certainly be glad to help you along.
As an approved handler for the CWO, we will just need you to fill out a character sheet for your wrestler. The match results will be determined using a set of rules I've come up with, which will be provided to you for your reference as well. Your participation is also key, as it betters your chance of winning matches.
On the CWO webpage (currently found at www.geocities.com/orangeace_99 , though we will soon be moving to Angelfire), your wrestler (s) will have their own section, including a Poser-generated picture, important facts about your wrestler, a record of his matches and titles won or lost, and an archive of the interviews you have done for your wrestler in the past. The website will also keep track of how much "money" you have earned for the year, as well as a ranking system for all players involved. We'll also possibly have a chat room and/or message board for you to give feedback when you feel inclined.
SHOWS The CWO's first show under the revamp will take place on Tuesday, 3/13/01. World Championship and Cruiserweight Championship Tournaments will start then. The first 8 heavyweights I receive (and approve), as well as the first 8 cruiserweights (225 pounds and under) will be placed into the brackets and away we go. The deadline for receiving wrestlers (and their character sheets) will be Saturday, March 10, 2001, by midnight Eastern time, as I will release a line-up for the first show then and begin writing it as well.
On 3/20/01 will be our next show, where the Hardcore (anything goes, falls count anywhere) Championship and Tag Team Championship tournaments will start. (So, the deadline for receiving tag team submissions is Saturday,
March 18, 2001, again by midnight Eastern time. The first 8 tag teams I receive will be placed in the tournament.) The Hardcore tournament will consist of both heavyweights and cruiserweights; the next 8 submissions I receive after I've filled the World and Cruiserweight Championship
Tournaments. This could certainly produce some interesting pairings! (NOTE: If you SPECIFICALLY want to compete in the Hardcore tournament, let me know.) (NOTE: Cruiserweights will certainly be allowed to compete for the other Championships besides the Cruiserweight Championship, but will be encouraged to focus on that division as much as possible.)
On 3/25/01, we will be having a PPV, MARCH MADNESS, where the finals of the above four tournaments will take place. There will be 12 matches in all on the PPV, and even if you lose in the first round of the tournament, your character can still appear and definitely have an impact on the PPV if you so choose!
In April we will begin having "house shows", one a week to start. This will allow you to make challenges of other wrestlers in the organization in an effort to win more "money" (more on that below). Our weekly "televised"
show will continue as well, on Tuesday every week. We will also have one PPV a month. INTERESTED? If you're interested, you can send a description of your proposed character(s) to me at [detail withheld] and after approval I'll send you a character sheet to fill out. Once that's back to me, we're ready to rock and roll.
I say "characters" because you are allowed to have more than one… possibly. Here's what I'm looking for as "stable limits," meaning how many people you can have under your control.
You can choose from the following: ? One heavyweight ? One cruiserweight ? One tag team ? One heavyweight, one cruiserweight ? One heavyweight, one tag team ? One cruiserweight, one tag team So, the only thing you can't really have is two heavyweights, two cruiserweights, or two tag teams. Also, for purposes of the "ladder system"
(described later), your cruiserweight will never be able to take on your heavyweight in a singles match. Your tag team could take on your heavyweight or cruiserweight, either in a handicap match or by teaming up with somebody not under your control, for storyline purposes.
A VERY BRIEF HISTORY OF THE CWO In 1995, 8 "handlers" on a BBS in Grand Rapids, MI, began what would grow into a worldwide sports entertainment extravaganza. The CWO (Championship
Wrestling Organization, though it has also been called the Cyberspace Wrestling Organization and even for a brief time the Cyberspace Mark II Wrestling Federation [oy vey]) tried to provide players with a fun experience of creating a wrestler/character and pitting him/her/it against others around the country and indeed soon around the world. From weekly
"televised" shows to major "pay per view" events, exciting storylines and grueling action came to characterize what the CWO was all about.
The CWO entered a bit of a lull in 2000, and some were wondering if the spark was gone. As the first year of the new millennium (shut up) closed, the CWO seemed as if it was on its last leg.
However, through a conversation between long time fed-head Brent Alles (myself), long time league contributor [name withheld], and many others, some interesting ideas were discussed about how to reenergize the CWO. This press release represents the beginning of those ideas, an idea called
CWO: RE-GENERATION. Definitely not an attempt to forget the brief though rich history of the CWO, RE-GENERATION represents an attempt to "reboot" the CWO, to allow those who have been great in their contributions and participation in the past to once again "suit up," but also to allow new blood to enter into the organization in order to hopefully make their way to the top.
First, every CWO wrestler will be under a situation where their wins and losses suddenly matter. Every CWO wrestler receives a base salary, beyond that are the bonuses from winning matches as well as winning and defending titles - sort of like how the XFL rewards their teams and their players for winning. In addition, for our purposes, cutting promos and participating will add to a player's earnings.
Winning a match by pinfall or submission $500 Winning a match by DQ or CO $250 Draw $250 (to each wrestler) Winning (or defending) the World Championship by pinfall or submission $500 Defending the World Championship by DQ or CO $250 Winning (or defending) the Hardcore Championship, Cruiserweight Championship, or Tag Team Championship by pinfall or submission $250 Defending the Hardcore Championship, Cruiserweight Championship, or Tag Team Championship by DQ or CO $125 "Cutting a Promo" (writing a piece for your character - backstage interview, etc.) $50-250 (varies)
Coming up with a merchandise item for your wrestler $100 base, variable amount after that based on "sales"
Submitting House Show Challenges $250* Evaluating Promos $250* *this will be divvied up amongst your wrestlers if you have more than one in your "stable". Italics represent your participation as a player, and are not really related to the storylines per se.
The earnings are tabulated for one entire year of your contract. (Say you signed on 3/5/01 - we would tabulate your bonus starting then until 3/4/02, with a "payout" on 3/5/02.)
The four divisions of the CWO - World, Hardcore, Cruiserweight (225 pounds and under), and Tag Team - will all operate under a "ladder system." That is, there will be the Champion of the division, and his Top 10 Contenders underneath. To get to a title shot, you have to work your way up the ladder to be the #1 contender, not to mention defending yourself once you're on the ladder from those beneath you hungry for your place.
The way it will work is that if you beat somebody above you on the ladder, you take his or her spot. They then are put on the ladder one rank below you (which will subsequently move everybody below that down as well). If you are of a lower rank than the person you are facing and get beat, you will move down one rank, basically "switching places" with the person who was previously below you.
For an example, say the World Championship situation was as below: WORLD CHAMPION: Brent "The Ace" Alles 1. Gold Kard 2. Lord Hile Troy 3. Foolkiller 4. Rooster Cogburn 5. Tub-O-Blub 6. Australian Assassin 7. Allen "Mr. America" Carter 8. Mad Doctor's Ghost 9. Bane 10. Pit Boss #1 Let's say there's a match, either a challenge match at a House Show or a (randomly generated) match on CWO EXTREME, pitting #3 Foolkiller against #7 Allen "Mr. America" Carter. If Carter wins, he gets the #3 spot and Foolkiller moves down to #4 (therefore displacing everybody else on the list accordingly). If Foolkiller wins, Carter moves down 1 to 8, and Mad
Doctor's Ghost moves up 1 to 7 (even though he wasn't wrestling). The same is true for the World Champion, who if they are defeated switch places with the person who beat them (usually the #1 contender). If the #1 contender gets beat, he moves down to #2, and the #2 person moves up to #1 (lucky for them!).
Keep in mind that your rank compared to somebody else's will make it either easier or harder to beat him or her. For example, a person ranked 9 is going to have a lot harder time defeating the World Champion than somebody who is ranked 2. Plan your strategies accordingly.
RE-GENERATION hopes to move in a different direction with the storylines, including our "major" storyline for 2001-2001, EXTREME MEASURES.
CWO EXTREME MEASURES From the ashes the CWO arises… Thought to be dead and gone from the wrestling world, the Fox Broadcasting Company (thank you, Uncle Rupert) has purchased the CWO from the Alles Family, to be shown primarily on fX but also on the Fox Sports Network affiliates around the country, not to mention pay per view events and possibly occasional specials on the Fox Network itself!
The CWO now has a new Executive Producer… and SHE has big plans for the wrestling organization. Something SHE likes to call "Extreme Measures."
(By the by, inspiration for this came from an idea by Steve Harper on Wrestleline called "Lockdown". Gotta give him props.) Every wrestler who signs a contract with the CWO also signs a waiver, the "Extreme Measures" clause. Any building, any arena, where CWO is putting on a show will be considered “The Extreme Zone” on the day of the show. The hours that contracted CWO wrestlers are inside the building, they have waived many of the rights of decent society they had before stepping inside the door. Essentially, what it boils down to is that the competition is not relegated to the ring. There are rules in the ring, and those rules will be strictly enforced (excepting of course the Hardcore division, where anything goes). Backstage, anarchy rules. If you are attacked backstage and injured, you will have no legal recourse against your attacker or the company.
(NOTE: Wrestlers enter the buildings each week through metal detectors – no weapons [other than traditional folding chairs, pipe wrenches and whatever is usually lying around the arenas] will be allowed in. Don’t expect murders or anything like that.)
CWO TOTAL ACCESS Also written into a CWO wrestler's contract is a waiver, a la “Real World” or “Survivor”, that allows for everything and anything you do or say in the
"Extreme Zone" to be shown on TV. CWO will hide cameras in locker rooms and corridors to capture all of the backstage “reality.” Roving reporters with microphones and camera crews enter the “Extreme Zones” and move back and forth between warring factions to get interviews and to find people who have managed to get away from the hidden cameras. These crews have signed the same contract as the wrestlers and are at risk just like journalists in a war zone. As stories develop over time, we will see how they parlay their influence and their ability to get people’s messages out without bias into keeping themselves unharmed. They had just better not piss off the wrong guys.
Beyond that will be candid interviews with wrestlers taken outside the "Extreme Zones" of the world, either at the wrestler's home, on the streets, wherever. These will provide insights into the wrestler's character.
STILL INTERESTED? HOPE SO! So, if you are interested, again feel free to contact Brent Alles at [detail withheld] with a description of your prospective character and we will get you started! Hoping to hear from you!
