SCORCH 3/21/99 Results

March 23, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCORCH 3/21/99 Wichita, KS HOUR #1 The True Americans had an interview, accepting the challenge from the Sheets
Foundation. Doc Watson was shown in footage from earlier in the day, "making peace" with
Ranger Taurus by offering him a cigar. The cigar blew up in Taurus' face.
Wayne Schillo made 'Smilin'' Doc Watson submit to the Boston Crab. Time of match: 9:32. Taurus attacked Watson after the match. He then challenged
Warhawk to a match on the SCORCH before this Sunday's PPV for the North
American title.
Chris Greco pinned 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum after the Flying Bodypress. Time of match: 4:58. Tommy Lightning provided commentary during the match. Iceman X of the Outlaw Riders provided the distraction to help Greco beat
Fulcrum. CHAIN MATCH: Vladimir Popalov defeated Masher Maccabee in a chain match after outside interference by Sandman Jr. Time of match: 16:18. Sandman Jr. then tried to attack Popalov but Popalov stopped him, delivering a reverse neckbreaker
and a chokeslam. That brought the rest of the True Americans down to ringside, and then the Sheets Foundation after that. Both teams brawled, and finally Fergus McTavish came down to join the fun, beating on Sandman
Jr.! Security had to separate all parties involved.
HOUR #2 Brian 'The King' Derks defeated Wildfire by submission with the King's
Crusher after outside interference by Union Caine. Time of match: 14:40.
Mercenaries and Joe Eagle/Flash Varga wrestled to a double countout. Time of match: 6:29. CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Secret Agent Super Dragon pinned Cris Marcus after the small package. Time of match: 14:23. Secret Agent Super Dragon retained the CWO Light
Heavyweight Champion title.
