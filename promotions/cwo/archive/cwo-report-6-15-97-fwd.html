CWO REPORT 6/15/97 (fwd)

15 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who are finishing the interview watching the Regulator's comments. Kaptain Krunch and Korperal Punishment are wearing the CWO North American Tag Team Titles.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Get a load of these guys. What a joke. Regulators, do you think by changing your colors that it's supposed to intimidate us? Is it supposed to intimidate us that you are part of the Wild Things? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Wild Things. I guess it must have taken you geniuses a long time to sit around and think that one up. You claim to have more talent than us? Well, all I can say to that is that it wasn't US that were carted off on a stretcher at SUMMER HEAT. It wasn't US that lost the street fight earlier this week. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Go figure. Regulators, we're still standing by what we told you punks earlier. You want these titles back? Come and get them. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: In case you haven't figured it out by now, it's total and complete anarchy in the CWO. It's every man for himself, any way he can get the job done. And as long as you idiots keep trying to accomplish what you know you can't, THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD will have these belts for a very, very long time. Deal with it, girls. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens up on a steel plated CWO logo. A small fuse on the bottom corner of the logo is lit and the logo explodes. When the smoke clears, the logo is undamaged, and a montage of CWO superstars rotates around the logo. Fade to the CWO Control Desk and the host, Mitch E. Washey.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Last night's CWO House Show was held in the Rose Gardens of Portland, OR. Here's the results of Saturday's show. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS ROSE GARDENS, PORTLAND OR 6/14/97 <> Brian 'The Hammer' Williams defeated Bishop by disqualification when Bishop threw a fireball in Williams' face. Time of match: 17:04. Lord Hile Troy/Vegas High Roller defeated David 'The Destroyer' Davis/Inferno when Lord Hile Troy pinned Inferno after the small package. Time of match: 21:15. Dark Raven and X-Factor wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 3:32. Serpentmaster pinned Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker after knocking him unconscious with the Snake Bite, pulling the tights. Time of match: 14:52. Sandman Jr. pinned Vlad the Impaler after the Permanent Dream flying legdrop. Time of match: 15:32. Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo defeated Brent 'The Ace' Alles by disqualification when Vegas High Roller and Matsuhiro Torunaka of Gold Kard International attacked. Jason Varga and Brian Williams made the save. Time of match: 7:27. Gold Kard pinned Mondo Gecko after Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash) and outside interference by Moses. Time of match: 6:53. WASHEY: A great card last night. Now, it's time for the interview segments of the REPORT. First off, we have Fergus McTavish. Let's go to him now. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil watching televison with Billy in Fergus' surprisingly luxurious penthouse apartment. They are sitting on a leather couch and are watching a replay of the Fergus/Bloodbath match at 'SUMMER HEAT'.) Fergus: Ha! Will ya look at dat! (On the TV is Bloodbath breaking out of the casket) Billy: Yeah. That was scary! I'd never seen anything like that! Fergus: Ah, I've seen dat before! One time we thought me Uncle Angus was dead and so we gots him all ready ta bury. Turns out he was just really drunk and passed out. Och, ya shoulda seen the looks on everybody's face when he started trashin' about! Aye, were our faces red! Billy: (rolling his eyes) Yeah, yeah, whatever. Listen, are you ready for your match with WRATH on Sunday? Fergus: Aye, but what kinda name is WRATH? Billy: Don't you know anything? Its one of The Seven Deadly Sins. Fergus: Never heard a dat clan. Billy: No, you don't understand. The Sins are Gluttony, Pride, Greed, Envy, Lust, Sloth, and WRATH. Get it? Fergus: Oh, so WRATH is the youngest one in da clan is he? Billy: Geez, didn't you see the movie 'SEVEN' with Brad Pitt? Fergus: Nay. Da only films we gets ta see in Scotland are SEAN CONNERY films. I tink its a law or something. Now dere's a real man, dat SEAN CONNERY. He'll kick yer arse an den ask ya if ya wants some more! Makes ya proud ta be a Scotsman! (Billy gets that naughty look in his eyes.) Billy: (smiling that wicked grin of his) Well then I guess you didn't hear what WRATH said about SEAN CONNERY. Fergus: (turning around quickly) What did he say about SEAN CONNERY? Billy: He said he was gay. (smiling again) Fergus: GAY!?! He said SEAN CONNERY was GAY!?! Dat tears it! Nobody, nobody insults SEAN CONNERY! Ya stupid bugger, ya've gone an insulted the Saint of Scotland! Are ya ready ta tangle wif The Whirling Scottish Devil? Cause I sure am! Oh, and one last ting.... (Fergus jumps off the couch, turns his back to the camera and lifts up his kilt, mooning the camera-guy) Fergus: (over his shoulder) Gets ready for The Whirling Scottish Devil! ITS NOT OATMEAL!!!! (Billy falls off the couch and is rolling on the floor, laughing hysterically) (camera fade, much laughing and hooting can still be heard) ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: I don't really know what that guy's problem is. Next up, we have comments from one Sandman Jr. ________________________________________________________________________________ SANDMAN JR: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHH where to start! First off, JOE EAGLE, good job in dumping that jerk RICH CLARK off his high horse. But one day I might be going after that belt myself, so always be watching. Next, EL GRANDE PISTELLERO, you beat me fair and square, and I ain't gonna whine and complain. Use that title match wisely. But if you lose, let me tell everyone, I WANT A SHOT AT MOSES! You turn on the fans, and you join GOLD KARD INTERNATIONAL!??!! I'll be honest, I DO kinda like DAVID THOMAS, cause he didn't completely abandon me when I became a "good guy", but those guys are all bad news. So, if I get my hands on you ONE MORE TIME MOSES, you're going DOWN! Finally, and perhaps most importantly, THE FOCUS! Well Focus, there's several things going on. First, you beat BENTLEY. Made him say he quit. As far as I'm concerned, you beating him meant I beat him, so BENTLEY, do whatever the hell you want, I'm through with you. But as for you FOCUS, I said early on I was focused on destroying you, and I haven't done that. So here is what I propose: remember the Carson City Silver Dollar Match, where you had to grab the Silver Dollars and bash your opponent to win? Well, sine the next pay-per-view is in MY HOMETOWN, PHILADELPHIA, I propose as BROTHERLY LOVE SINGAPORE CANE match. The objective? A ladder. Climb it, grab the cane, crush your opponent with the cane, get the pin. Plus, we can make it for any title you or I (*I*) may have at the time... so, you up for it, FOCUS... one month and we can end all this... you up for it? ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up is one of the members of GKI, Masher Maccabee. Here he is with his thoughts. ________________________________________________________________________________ Masher is sitting in front of a laptop, typing a message to some marks... "Ah, computer jukies, now that I have returned on-line let me just state that despite my differences with Tortillanaka, the addition of Moses, a brother, to GKI, makes me the happiest wrestler in the world. To add to my happiness, I am going to get some gold, the kindGerman Choklat can't wear. Varga! I want your belt. I challenge you to a cage match, NO ESCAPE, NO DQ. I want you to feel the Hangover, Snuka Style. GKI, it's just tooooooooooo sweet!" ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: (to somebody off camera) Did that guy say what I actually think he said? You rolled the right clip, right? That wasn't that other guy from....never mind. (back to camera) Now we bring you the CWO World Tag Team Champions, the Highlanders. ________________________________________________________________________________ GAVIN McCLOUD: "Well, look what we've got our hands on again [kisses his title belt]. When are tha' idiots gonna learn...ye can pound on us, ye can beat us up, but in tha' end - the Highlanders ALWAYS get tha' last word in. Those so-called Hardcore boys thought they could knock us out of tha' match before we even got in the ring, but if ye gonna do that, ye better be sure ye get the job done properly, and you, boys - ye're amateurs." CONOR McCLOUD: "Y'know something? It's a bonny time to be a fan here in the CWO, despite the lawlessness that goes around here. You've got our good mates, tha' Regulators as the North American champs. Ye've got Joe Eagle, a guy everyone can look up to, as our World Champ. And you've got us, the two-time world tag team champs. In fact, it would be a grand day indeed if not fer one weed growing amoungst tha' roses, and I'm taling about.....Gold Kard International." GAVIN McCLOUD: "We've still got a bone ta pick you, ye goose-stepping git, from way back in the CM2WF - an' we've got the longest memories in the book. We owe you a good beating, an' now's a good a time as any to pick up on old debts. Whaddya say, Goldy? Gold Kard International, against us and two partners to be announced? Got the balls to get in the ring with us, or are ye just gonna mince around and hide behind your thugs like normal? Either way, we're gonna get ye. Ye cannae hide from us forever......remember....." CONOR McCLOUD: "There can be only one!" ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Lines are being formed and sides are being taken with the situation regarding the GKI. Finally on the REPORT, we bring you comments from the Deadly Sins. ________________________________________________________________________________ LUST: I'm BAAAAAAACCCCCCCCCCCKKKKKK! PRIDE: That's right, our better half, Lust, has returned from her exile to lead the Deadly Sins to glory... but some of you are asking, just why in the hell would we help the MASTERS OF HARDCORE after THEY had just eliminated me... SLOTH: It's real easy folks: WE WOULDA DONE THE SAME THING TO THEM! They caught us unaware and distracted, but if we got the chance, we woulda done the exact same thing... plus, any team that attakcs those Scottish morons can't be THAT bad! PRIDE: So, we didn't get OUR belts this time... so WHAT! We'll get them, and get them soon... that is ultimately what matters... and with Lust at our side, no one can stop us! SLOTH: BIG COOL, you're #1 on out hit-list... we wanted you out cause you don't deserve the belts... then, it's wither those PUNKS the REGULATORS, or those jerks THE HIGHLANDERS! Either way, we WILL be getting our belts, and soon at that! WRATH: Also now that Lust is at our side, I will gain a singles belt, one I rightfully deserve! JASON VARGA... JOHNNY REB... even you, JOE EAGLE... once the Wrath has fallen upon you, you WON'T be getting up! LUST: Be prepared for the pain... oh yes, the pain you will feel will feel OH SO GOOD to me... cause I know you want the pain, and I know you want my pleasure... ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Uhhhh, yeah. Well, that's all the time we have for today. We'll see you Wednesday with another edition of the CWO REPORT. So long everybody!!! (fade to closing credits)
