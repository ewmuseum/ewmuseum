CWO REPORT 6/14/97 (fwd)

15 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens up on a steel plated CWO logo. A small fuse on the bottom corner of the logo is lit and the logo explodes. When the smoke clears, the logo is undamaged, and a montage of CWO superstars rotates around the logo. Fade to the CWO Control Desk and the host, Mitch E. Washey.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to another edition of the CWO REPORT. We have a few things to cover, so let's get right down to the results from last night's House Show in Seattle, WA. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS Key Arena, Seattle WA 6/13/97 () Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned The Focus after the reversed inside cradle. Time of match: 15:04. Mason-Dixon and Moses/Masher Maccabee wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 4:40. El Grande Pistolerro defeated Gold Kard by disqualification when Moses and Masher Maccabee attacked. Mondo Gecko and the Regulators came down to make the save. Time of match: 5:04. Deadly Sins defeated Brother Justice when Sloth pinned Jerome Justice after the Repention (superplex by Pride, Slothsault by Sloth). Time of match: 11:06. Sandman Jr. pinned CWO Television Champion Benjamin Blake Bentley in a non-title match after the Permanent Dream flying legdrop. Time of match: 9:08. Wrath pinned 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia after the gorilla press, pulling the tights. Time of match: 16:54. LUMBERJACK MATCH: Johnny Reb (with Mondo Gecko, El Grande Pistolerro, and Mason-Dixon) made Matsuhiro Torunaka (w/Gold Kard, David "The Truth" Thomas, Moses, and Masher Maccabee) submit to the Texas Cloverleaf with the lumberjacks brawling outside the ring. Time of match: 18:23. WASHEY: Tonight the CWO will be appearing in Portland's Rose Garden Arena. Here is the scheduled matches for tonight's card. SATURDAY, JUNE 14, 1997 Rose Gardens, Portland OR Bishop vs. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams Vegas High Roller and Lord Hile Troy vs. David 'The Destroyer' Davis/Inferno Dark Raven vs. X-Factor Serpentmaster vs. Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker Sandman Jr. vs. Vlad the Impaler Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo Gold Kard vs. Mondo Gecko WASHEY: Don't miss the excitement that only the action of the CWO can deliver on. Let's get down to some comments from CWO stars. First, we'll hear from part of the CWO's newest stable, the Wild Things. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Miss Amber, Wildfire, Bloodbath and "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher in the locker room talking to an unknown man, but when the five notice the camera the mystery man ducks back into the shadows) WILDFIRE:OK, listen up cause I got alot to say. First of all, GKI!! You think you can come in here and take over the CWO like you are, well you got another thing comming!! Cause your messing with the Wild Things, the WILDEST stable in the CWO and were gonna take care of business!!! DEUTSCHER:Im sick and tired of all this bullsh*t!!! So we got a little suprise for you GKI, well actually its a big suprise!!! BLOODBATH(dark & mysterious):Mr. Commishiner, you have also discrasced the CWO and for every action there is a reaction. I have a suprise for you... (Bloodbath picks up a blood red bag and removes a shovel from it) BLOODBATH(dark & mysterious):Remember this, Mr. Commishiner? This is the very shovel that put you out of action some time ago, and I intend to use this very shovel to dig out the final resting place for each and every peace of trash GKI member!!! REST IN PEACE!!!! WILDFIRE:And there is another matter of business that I want to take care of!! Moses, you seem to walk around might prowd of yourself!!! Well Im issueing a challange for next week Tuesday Night Fever!! You and me for the CWO light heavyweight title!! If you think your the best then you have nothing to lose, and except my challange!!! But I warn you, about another certian former superstar of the CWO that under restimated me, and well he aint here anymore!!! DEUTSCHER:And dont think I dont have a suprise for everyone, including GKI!! I have the newest member of the Wild Things and I cant wait to drop the bombshell on everyone!!! WILDFIRE(yelling):WHAT YA GONNA DO WHEN THE WILD THINGS RUN WILD ON YOU!!!! (the camera fades out) ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: (to somebody off camera) ...run wild on you? Isn't that comment trademarked or something? (back to camera) Now we'll hear from the CWO tag team, the Mercenaries, who have supplied the CWO REPORT with a video taken at a recent press conference. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who are seated at a table surrounded by several reporters. The CWO North American Tag Team titles are lying on the table, one in front of Kaptain Krunch and one in front of Korperal Punishment.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: All right. The Mercenaries have called this press conference today to address some issues that have recently come to light. We will speak our part, then field questions from everybody. First off, the Mercenaries will now be known as the reigning CWO North American Tag Team Champions, after defeating the then-champions, the Regulators on June 12 in Vancouver B.C. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Secondly, the Mercenaries will decide who we feel is worthy enough to face us for the titles. The Mercenaries will no longer take orders from the CWO Championship Committee, who we have felt have sided against us from the first day that we were signed into the CWO. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Finally, the Mercenaries will no longer be associated with the team known as DOOMSDAY. We feel that Maximum Overdrive has kept us in the backseat, while placing his own priorities at the forefront of the group. We will now take questions from the audience. REPORTER #1: What makes you think you are in fact the CWO North American Tag Team Champions? Just because you have the titles in your possession does not make it so. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: We stated that on June 12, the Mercenaries clearly defeated the Regulators in a Detroit Street fight- KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Defeated them? More like DESTROYED them. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: We know that the match was a non-title match set up by rules dictated by the CWO Championship Committee. But the Mercenaries are no longer bound by the rulings set up by the Committee. We won the match, and in doing so, we won the titles. REPORTER #2: How do you think that the CWO will respond? I mean, you are violating direct match rules and orders. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: We are violating NO orders. WE are the ones who decide the rules, not the CWO Committee. WE are the Champions. The CWO will not lift a finger to stop us. After all, the CWO has more important things to worry about, like Randy Johnson's induction into GKI. REPORTER #3: You said that the CWO Championship Committee has been biased against you. What proof do you have of this claim? KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Look at our record. We are one of the most established teams in this federation. Yet we are ranked BELOW a team like the Deadly Sins. A team that has a losing record ranked #3? You tell me what's wrong with that picture. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: As one of the top tag teams in this federation, we were not even invited to the four way tag match at the SUMMER HEAT PPV. Who were? The Masters of Hardcore. A team that we went out and beat. The Deadly Sins, who have never been able to defeat us. The Big Cool. A team that has a 50/50 record against us. And the Highlanders. A team that has been on the run from us from day 1 since we stepped into this federation. What did the Mercenaries get? KAPTAIN KRUNCH: A six man tag team match against three goofs at one of the pre PPV shows. In other words, the [censored] shaft, while the Regulators go out and get a shot at the North American Tag Titles. REPORTER #1: You said that you have now disbanded with DOOMSDAY? What were the factors leading up to this decision? KAPTAIN KRUNCH: First off, when we came to this federation, Maximum Overdrive told us that he would take us to the top. Well, that was good and fine for the first few months. We got shots at both tag titles, but kept getting screwed. The Champions then, the Creatures of the Night and the Pit Bosses, kept giving out non-title matches to us, which we would win. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Then to "pacify" us, they finally gave us title shots, only to cheat to get the win and keep their titles. Overdrive kept us from going out and putting those two teams in body bags on more than one occasion. "Things will work out", he kept telling us. Well, we waited. And waited. And waited. We saw all these other teams going out and getting title shots while we stood around watching everybody else get their piece of the pie. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Then things started to happen with Brent Alles. Overdrive would get us to help him beat the hell out of Alles. Soon, it was an obsession with him. He wanted Alles out of this sport and was going to do whatever it took. Soon, the Mercenaries and Baby Waldo didn't matter anymore. It was "get Alles". "Take him out". It was fun for only so long. Then it got boring. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: I think the crowning moment was thowing him off that balcony. Then it was like, "what the hell are we doing this for?" It wasn't OUR fight. A few days after the PPV, we gave our notice to Overdrive and walked out. We haven't seen him since. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: That's all the time we have for now, but we'll take one more question. REPORTER #2: How about the Regulators? KAPTAIN KRUNCH: What about them? REPORTER #2: Have they had any comment concerning the situation? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: If they did, we haven't heard it. I haven't heard anything resembling a tag team clone talking since we won the titles, have you Krunch? KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Nope. That should tell you something right there.. This interview's over. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: But before we go, here's one final comment that the Mercenaries have and you can quote us.....THE MERCENARIES ARE THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD!!! KAPTAIN KRUNCH: It's anarchy in the CWO!! (The Mercenaries grab their titles and leave, amid a flurry of reporter's questions and flash bulbs popping.) ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: The CWO will have their hands full with those two, as if the situation with GKI wasn't enough. Strangely enough, we have comments from the other half of the Wild Things, the Wild Bunch.
