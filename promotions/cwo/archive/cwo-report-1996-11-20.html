CWO REPORT 11/20/96

20 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Wednesday, November 20, 1996. We are only a few short days from CWO PPV excitement, live from Tampa, FL, NOVEMBER PAIN: ULTIMATE WARFARE, in which the superstars of the CWO will go up against each other in teams of 4 in WARGAMES matches, the winners of those matches going on to a final two-ring battle royal, the winner of that getting a shot at the CWO World championship at CWO CELEBRATION in December. Add all of that to the intense Texas Death match between "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum and Master of Darkness DAmion, and of course the "World Series of Wrestling," a best 4 OUT OF 7 FALLS match between the former World champion "Master of Pain" Barry Hart and the man who beat him for the belt, Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, and you have the makings of the most explosive CWO PPV ever. Building up to that, the superstars of the CWO are in the islands this week, starting with a card in San Juan, Puerto Rico tonight, let's look at the lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 20, 1996 SAN JUAN, PUERTO RICO Johnny Reb vs. Sandman Jr. STREET FIGHT: Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson vs. Flyboy Hoss and Little Joe vs. Flame Spirits Sin vs. Master Of Darkness Damion. Lafayette and Rooster vs. The Big Cool WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Blood Inc. (D.E.A.T.H. and Panther) vs. The Dark Side (Creatures of the Night and Hellhound, champions) Jackass Joel vs. Hangman Phantom Freightrain vs. Payne Steel vs. Dark Raven _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see, the big match will be the World 6-Man Tag Team Championship defense, with the Dark Side going up against Blood Inc. We have comments from both teams. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Da Boss, Panther, and D. E. A. T. H., Benton and Green, are in the interview area.) DA BOSS: Well, I see this federation finally did something right, they gave me boys a shot at some belts. Dark Side, we have already whipped you once and still you signed on the dotted line. Does that make you brave or STUPID? BENTON: I don't know what's going to feel better, kicking your @$$ or wearing the belts around our waist. One thang is for sure, when you walk down the Crossroads, you never come back the same. PANTHER: It's becoming a regular sight here in the CWO, that sight is seeing Blood Inc. leaving people face down on the canvas after receiving the @$$-kicking of their life. Well, Dark Side, you are next on the list. DA BOSS: Dark Side don't try to run cause we are gonna find you, and when we do it ain't gonna be pretty. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on The Dark Side. Hellhound looks like he is talking to his World 6-man Tag Team Championship belt. The camera zooms in.) HELLHOUND: Do not worry, I will not let them take you away from me, you are mine and always shall be mine. I love you too, my pet. (Master of Darkness DAmion sees the camera and steps in front of Hellhound.) MOD DAMION (to camera): Tonight marks the final week leading up to the big PPV, November Pain. Ben Slocum, I will be spending my time this week warming up for our match and first on the list is SIN. Sin, are you ready for the fight of your life? I will be bringing all the power of the DARKNESS with me to the ring and I shall unleash its fury on you, mister, and all that you can do is stand there and take your beating like a man for I have a mission and that mission is to get to November Pain this Sunday and finally remove one Ben Slocum from the CWO! Now, my Creatures and Hellhound are also in action tonight in order to warm up for the great war that is waiting for them at the PPV. NIGHT SLINGER: D.E.A.T.H. and Panther, you boys tried once and did not succeed, what makes you think that this time shall be any different? It appears that we are going to have to get real serious this time and make sure that you three think long and hard about wanting to get back in the ring with the Dark Side after tonight. Believe me, you see what we have in store for you, you will wish that you never put your names on the dotted line. HELLHOUND: I love this belt it is my pet, my prize possession and there ain't nobody who will take it away from me. Tha Master has said that as long as I beat the men in the league who try to steal it away from me and my friends that I can keep it, but if I fail him he will get very angery and take away my shiny pet. NIGHT CRAWLER: You see, what the big man is trying to say is that nothing you do will be enough. He and we will keep on going, as long as we hold these titles we will always rise up and continue to press on until we have finally taken hold of your souls and thrown them away, only then will we stop the pounding you will receive. MOD DAMION: The Dark Side is coming and with us we bring the NIGHT and all that roams within! Nothing can stop us, not even the CWO. ALL FOUR: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!! (Camera fades on Hellhound who is stroking the belt he carries as Damion pets him on the head and behind the ears.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also on the card, a match between Steel and another Dark Side member, Dark Raven. We go to comments with Steel. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Steel is by himself in the interview area.) STEEL: I know many of you piece of crap fans thought you would see one of Blood Inc. out here by himself. Well take a good look, here I am, you know why I can stand out here alone, I'LL TELL YOU WHY, it's because this whole damn federation is scared to death of what we will do next. Well, Dark Raven, what are you thinking? Are you looking over your shoulder, calling your buddies to watch your back? I know you are, and that is a real good idea, because you are going to need them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: They may have been in his shadow at one time, but Akillies and Team Blitzkrieg, Kamikaze and Mysteria, are poised to make their move here in the CWO. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The hardly recognizable remnants of the once powerful Chaotic Dynasty stand before a camera.) KAMIKAZE: You know, the list of great teams in this league goes on and on, and it didn't used to. The tag team scene used to be pathetic, I mean it was so pathetic, the Gods of Thunder ruled the league! MYSTERIA: Now you're talking sad... KAMIKAZE: Now we've got the Deadly Sins ranked at number two, with only four wins? Something about that doesn't sit right with me, probably the fact that they dont deserve it! MYSTERIA: While we move down from tenth place to fourteenth place? KAMIKAZE: Yes, well that is all going to stop right now. We've gotten the word from Lord Foul to kick it into high gear while he isn''t around. AKILLIES: That's right, because everyone is TALKING about stopping Gold Kard International, but let's face it boys, there's only ONE group in the history of the CWO that has ever been able to step up to that challenge... you're looking at it. KAMIKAZE: That's right, which is why as of right now, things are about to change! The Chaotic Dynasty is no more, but you've still got Team Blitzkrieg and Akillies to deal with! Phantom Freightrain had the displeasure of being the first example! AKILLIES: How true... because those fans out there that really track things know that we have signed ZERO challenges, but that is all about to change! No longer will we be looked over! First challenge on the plate... MYSTERIA: Against the "Bad Guys"... you might be able to club people around in the ring, but Kamikaze and I, we'd like to see how you fare against a more, shall we say, elegant team, that can block your punches and then jump into the air to kick you in places you cant scratch! AKILLIES: And myself, I've already taken care of the one challenge against Freightrain, we'll worry about the rest later! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: He may have his mind on the WARGAMES match against Doomsday on Sunday at NOVEMBER PAIN, but Sandman Jr. is never at a loss of words about ANYTHING, here are his comments. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area.) SANDMAN JR.: Barry Hart... you're something else... use MY finisher to win, but holding the tights... gotta say, I was impressed.... but Kel, who's butt rules? KELLEN: Yours does, Sandy. SANDMAN JR.: Yeah, it does, and if I ever get my hands on him again, I'm gonna cane his ass so bad he'll never show it again. Hellhound, you put on a good match, but you saw who the man is. And Gecko, if I ever face you again, lightning does NOT strike twice. Now, along to other things. First off, this week's victims. First, Johhny Reb. You looked impressive against my best friend/worst enemy Sanchez. Didn't win though... the losing streak will continue.... then, me and Steel get to tango... don't know much about you kid, only that you'll lose.... finally, the big pay-per-view.... myself, Sanchez, Clark, and Snuka... I mean... our mystery partner against those b******s in Doomsday... boy, we're gonna crush you guys.... we're gonna wupp all kinds of @$$... and anyone who doesn't like it is gonna get CANED! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Still stirring up trouble in the tag team ranks, here are the Deadly Sins. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE (sighing): Who do we have to destroy this week, Sloth? SLOTH: Says here that we get Mason-Dixon first. Those inbred hillbillies, why must we waste our time? Who else.... PRIDE: I beleive it says next is the Highlanders... a couple of guys who name themselves after a movie character... boy is THAT lame.... SLOTH: Finally, we get to the big pay per view! We and the Unholy Duo, who we taught that WE'RE the baddest-assed team here, versus the Biblical Powers and the Samoans. Hey, that's great! We hate BOTH those teams! LUST: Biblical Powers! Your powers are NOTHING compared to those of the Deadly Sins! And Samoans, we're gonna crush in your fat faces until you scream for mercy, then we're gonna hurt you some more!!! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: He's made a name quickly for himself here in the CWO, and now Mondo Gecko wants a shot at the gold. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: I challenge the winner of the Light Heavyweight title match on Saturday to a match! I can beat either of you two and when I do I will be partying in the streets of San Francisco, home of the greatest team in football, the 49ers! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Well, that's all the time we have for today.... (An intern rushes on the set.) STARR: What the...? INTERN: Johnny, we just received this tape! You'll never believe it, we... we have to show the footage! STARR: Well, OK... ladies and gentlemen, I guess we have some surprise footage to watch, I'm not sure what we're going to see, let's look at the tape. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Fade up to footage apparently shot with a hand-held camera, the holder of which is unknown. The night has fallen and all is still outside. Before you looms a huge building. It looks familiar, but you cannot fully make it out. From the shadows a hulking figure emerges, and enters the building. All is quiet in the building and the figure emerges from the door. It is Bane, dressed in a black trench coat, and wearing his ever present mask. He strides cooly down the hallway, acknowledging no one. The few people who are still there are either too busy to notice or just mind their own business. Bane calmly opens the door to the stairwell and continues to walk calmly up several flights of stairs. Meanwhile, not a soul stirs, and even if they did they quickly turn away for they do not want to face their Bane. Slowly and menacingly Bane walks down the corridor until he reaches a door. There he stops and slowly turns the knob to open the door. It is dark inside the room but a table lamp casts some of the darkness away. It is enough light to reveal to slumbering face of the ex-Television Champion: Sandman.) BANE (his voice is cool and deadly): Greetings, Sandman. You appear to be in ill condition. (Bane walks over to the bed and stands directly over the still sleeping Sandman.) BANE: For your sake, I hope you do not recover. You believed yourself to be a strong man, someone who is, quote, "immortal". I showed you the limits of your strength. I showed you how I control your fragile life. You should should have run, Sandman, you should have run far away. Instead, you struck up the courage to come looking for your Bane. It is true that the valiant die only one death, and you were valiant. You were also a fool. A pathetic, weak, old man. I will not further disrespect a dying man. You should have learned by Marc "Compton" Daniels. I destroyed him, and now he is nowhere to be found. He was nothing to me, just as you are nothing. Maybe in death you will get the dignity you thought that you deserved. In life, I took that dignity away. (Bane pauses for a second to look around.) BANE: I see that you received flowers. Probably from that pissant Talon. You do not know the cost that you bestowed upon him when you made him your partner. The silly child seems to want to avenge you. What a waste of youth. He should be coming after you, because it was you who now have signed his death certificate. Do not fear, though, I will take as much pleasure hunting him as I did you. As for that tag team of yours, well, they are about to meet with their unfortunate fate also. It is too bad you won't be able to see the beautiful pain that I am going to inflict upon them. But that is how I will have it. (Suddenly the door swings open. An orderly walks in carrying a tray of pills for Sandman to take. He closes the door behind him, not knowing who is in the room. His astonishment quickly turns to fear.) ORDERLY (voice shaking with fear): I... I... know you. You're... B-B-Bane. Get out of here, I-I'm calling security! (Bane looks him dead in the eye, grabs him and puts him in the Merciful Death. He keeps it on until he is completely unconscious, then lets go.) BANE: I will not be interrupted! Now where was I. Oh yes, Sandman. I was telling you about your cowardly friends. It seems that that old fool you once associated yourself with, what was his name, oh yes, Foul; Lord Foul. Well, it seems he has been receiving a lot of notice as of late. The poor fool actually thinks that he can beat me. What a pity that he didn't learn from you. He should stay across the ocean. He is safe there for a while, but eventually his Bane will catch up with him. (Bane stares at Sandman. He looks down and you could almost make out a smile on Bane's face. Sandman is sweating and it looks as if he is having a nightmare in his sleep.) BANE: Do you know how helpless and pathetic you look right now? I hold your soul in my palm. I would be happy if I harbored those type of feelings, but alas I don't. I must be going now Sandman, but I have something to give to you. (Bane reaches into his coat and pulls out a red rose. He rests it on top of Sandman's heaving chest. Bane then leans over a whispers in Sandman's ear.) Bane: It's lights out, baby, your Bane is finished with you. (With that Bane rips out all the IV's and instruments that are attached to Sandman. Sandman's eyes open wide as if in pain, then quickly passes out. An alarm goes off and footsteps can be heard running down the hall. Bane leaves the room in time to see a security guard running to the room followed by a doctor. The guard cannot stop in time, and Bane levels him with a clothesline sending him to the floor. The doctor screams, Bane silences him with a punch that shatters his jaw. Bane slowly walks away, and fades into the shadows. If you strain your ears you might be able to detect a strange sound. It resembles the sound of laughter. The camera shuts off.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny, who sits in stunned silence for several moments.) STARR: I... we... I.... (Starr hangs his head, speechless. He is shaking, his head shakes from side to side.
