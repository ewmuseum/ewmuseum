CWO REPORT 11-17-97 (fwd)

17 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey sitting at the CWO REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Hello everybody and welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Due to technical difficulties, we were not able to come to you over the weekend with the results of the House Shows that occurred Friday, Saturday and Sunday. So without further delay, let's get to the results from this past weekend. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 11/14/97 Binghamton. NY (Binghamton Civic Center) 'Lone Wolf' Ryan Deutscher pinned Gold Kard after the Stun Gun. Time of match: 17:51. 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat pinned Mondo Gecko after the Jimmyplex. Time of match: 4:54. 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat refused to let up after the bell rang. The CPE made the save. Ranger Taurus defeated Hellhound by disqualification when the Dark Side attacked. Time of match: 16:18. Foolkiller pinned Kaptain Krunch after the jumping DDT. Time of match: 6:32. World Tag Team Champions the Newton Bros. defeated Holocaust/Red Raven in a non-title match when Howard Newton pinned Red Raven after the Powerline. Time of match: 7:30. 'The Ultimate Heel' Union Caine pinned Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo after Schillo missed a kneedrop. Time of match: 15:34. Caine was attacked by the Unit after the match, prompting the rest of Empire to come down and make the save, and a brawl commenced. Creatures of the Night defeated Dogs of War when Night Crawler pinned 'Irresistible Assasin' Eddie Fantastic after the Day to Night. Time of match: 12:30. Creatures of the Night and Dogs of War brawled back to the locker room after the match. CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Champion Southern Dynamite pinned El Grande Pistolerro after the rollup. Time of match: 17:27. Southern Dynamite retained the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion title. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 11/15/97 Buffalo, NY (Marine Midland Arena) Skyhawk pinned Painmaster after the Skydive. Time of match: 12:55. Holocaust defeated Master of Darkness Damion by disqualification. Time of match: 0:46. This match didn't last long as the Dark Side attacked Holocaust and Red Raven (who was at ringside) shortly after the match began. "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday pinned The Focus after the Permanent Vacation. Time of match: 12:17. Lord Hile Troy pinned Wildfire after the Dragon Suplex, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 14:57. Bad Guys defeated Creatures of the Night when Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker pinned Night Crawler after the hit with the cellular phone. Time of match: 15:42. Light Heavyweight Champion Southern Dynamite pinned Tommy Bomber after the somersault legdrop. Time of match: 18:42. CWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS TITLE MATCH: Champions the Newton Bros. defeated Dogs of War when Howard Newton pinned 'Irresistible Assasin' Eddie Fantastic after the Powerline. Time of match: 21:44. World Champion Johnny Reb defeated Hellhound by disqualification in a non-title match. Hellhound was biting Johnny Reb and would not get off of him when the referee ordered him to - leading to the disqualification. Time of match: 13:52. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 11/16/97 New York, NY (Madison Square Garden) Wesley 'The Rock' McMichael pinned 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat after the Texas Tombstone. Time of match: 4:45. Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned Lord Hile Troy after the Allesbreaker. Time of match: 18:47. 'College Guy' and 'The Hammer' defeated Mercenaries by disqualification when Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo was attacked by the Law. Time of match: 9:40. Mondo Gecko pinned Gold Kard after the crucifix. Time of match: 10:56. Harvesters of Sorrow and Brother Justice wrestled to a double countout. Time of match: 6:27. Tommy Bomber defeated Bloodbath by disqualification when Wildfire attacked him. Wildfire then proceeded to attack Bloodbath until the Dark Side made the save. Time of match: 6:17. Mason-Dixon defeated Highlanders when Stonewall Mason pinned Conor McCloud after the Southern Spike. Time of match: 15:51. After the match the Highlanders delivered a double piledriver to both Mason and Dixon. SUBMISSION MATCH: Sandman Jr. made World Champion Johnny Reb submit to the STF. Time of match: 11:47. The match was mired in controversy as Johnny Reb claimed he didn't submit, and then punched the referee. Sandman Jr. then hit Reb with the Singapore cane, and the two had to be separated by security. WASHEY: This is the week where the opening rounds for the Wayne Carpenter Memorial Tag Team Tournament begin. As a result, FEVER will be the only regularly televised card coming out this week. While we're on the subject of the tournament, the CWO is running over with tag teams coming in, ready to compete for the one million dollar purse awarded to the winner. Our first interview of the day is from one of those teams ready to get down later in the week. ______________________________________________________________ The camera opens with a shot of sixty plastic figurines, meant to represent the men who are entered in the Wayne Carpenter Memorial Tag-Team Tournament, filling a large hand-made miniature ring. Two teams are missing from the group, one because they remain a mystery to this day, and are represented by two question marks on the outside of the ring, but still on team is missing. The scene remains the same until a cane come crashing down into the centre of the ring. The camera follows the cane up until we are met with the cloar covered body of Phantom Lord. Phantom Lord:"Ladies and gentlemen of the Cyberspace Wrestling Organization, I hope you did not think that we had left. While it may have been the doing of a false prophet that brought my men and myself to this federation, his sudden disappearance will not have any sort of an ill effect on us. On the contrary, it has only raised our resolve, and desire to demonstrate to you all the power that I control. And I can not think of a better way to demonstrate this to you all then by leading my team into battle at your precious tournament, and leaving with your money. As I look over the field, I am overcome with one recurring thought. What is that thought, you all ask. Well allow me to make a much more lasting impression on your feeble minds." The camera pans back down to the ring and Phantom Lord removes his cane from the ring. Seconds later, two large feet come crashing down on the ring, crushing all that was inside. The camera pans back up to see that Phantom Lord has been joined by both Pain and Suffering, the Harvesters Of Sorrow. Phantom Lord:"You see, I have in my possession the single most devestating tag-team know on the face of this planet. The Harvesters Of Sorrow. And with my guidance, we will crush all those that stand in the way of our million dollars!" Pain:"That's right PL! I took the time to see that along with the so-called great CWO tag-teams, there will alos be a bunch of crippled old-timers like The Steiner Brothers and the Legion of Doom. Boys, you may have been good in the eighties, but this decade belongs to one team, and it's us!" Suffering:"Scotty, how's that back of yours? You too Animal. I;m sure that after a seat in the Angry Chair, all of your problems will be cured. As for the rest of you CWO nobodies, we may have been held back by a preacher in the past, but he's nolonger in the picture. He offered to save you from all the devils of mankind, and you turned him away. Well geuss what, we're those devil's and there's nothing left to save you now!" Pain & Suffering:"You enter the ring as men, but you leave as dirt!" Phantom Lord:"You see, when I first arrived, I made it quite clear that your little titles meant nothing to us, but much to my surprise you listened. A million dollars is more than enough to peak our interest. And it is also more than enough to make us want to destroy all who dare to step in our path. Infact, I am so sure of my teams ability, that I am willing to stake our careers on this tournament. Should we fail to win your million dollar prize, I shall pull my men from your precious federation. Is this a large risk? Not in my opinion. I am merely making this a level playing field, for you see, all who enter the ring against my Harvester of Sorrow will be making a similar gesture. Nothing stands between my men, and money. Dogs of War, you will have the misfortune of being the first to fall, but you will not be the last. sixty-two bodies seperate us from our goal, and after November Pain, sixty-two bodies will litter the ground behind us. The preacher warned you that the end was soon to be upon you, and now the end has arrived. Take care of our money, for we will be there to claim it." Phantom Lord, Pain and Suffering walk away from the camera as it begins to lower back down to where there was once a ring filled with figures, but now is just a mess of collapsed cardboard and many broken bodies over the floor. ______________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up is a surprise of sorts for the CWO. Let's go right to the interview. ______________________________________________________________ (The scene opens with flashbacks of the Four Aces--Chris Greco, Clark Gray, Steve Keller, and Wayne Carpenter--in solo and tag action, highlighting the closeness and cameraderie of the four men. The music is hard-driven and up-tempo. Slowly, the mood becomes more somber, we see scenes of an ambulance leaving a bomb-damaged arena, and then a newspaper headline: "Wrestler Loses His Last Match--Wayne Carpenter submits to his injuries)". The scene shifts to Chris Greco's press conference, as he says "The Four Aces will be leaving the CWO, effective immediately. We may or may not be back." Cut to present-day Greco, a little older and grimmer in black trunks and boots, but looking no less determined.) GRECO: We're ba-ack.......... (The other three Aces--Gray, Keller, and Tommy Lightning, join him on camera. They are also wearing black.) GRECO: No introductions--you either remember who we are, or you're going to learn quickly. The Four Aces are returning to the CWO, and what better time and place than at the tournament to remember our brother-in-arms, Wayne Carpenter. KELLER: Rest in peace, partner. GRECO: I'd like to thank Commissioner Alles for reinstating us in the organization--we're looking forward to a long and successful run in the CWO, starting at Wayne's tournament, and with the Highlanders and the Big Cool. GRAY: Conor, Gavin, it's going to be good to see you again, but you know the business as well as we do. KELLER: Taylor, Jenkins, we thought your cholesterol would have taken you out by now!!! But Tommy and I are going to have to do it ourselves.....and it'll be a lot more fun this way! GRECO: Get ready, CWO.......the Aces are high again...... (The camera fades out) ____________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up we have a first interview given by the newest tag team to enter the ranks of the CWO. ____________________________________________________________ <camera opens in the head offices of CCWA in Henderson, TN. Southern Dynamite is sitting behind his desk with his feet on the desk, Pepsi in hand.> Dynamite: How's everyone in the CWO doin' today? Well, I'm here like I said I would be. I've brought in a new tag team to the CWO since the Nation bolted. I guess the Highlanders just scared the bejesus out of them or something. Maybe they're with Reverend's church? I don't know and I don't care. I brought them in with the hopes of forming a stable or at least an alliance between them and other wrestlers. That's history now. These two men in front of me are the future of wrestling. Both men are young and full of exuberance. They are ... Masked Man #1: We are Youth in Action. We're here to cause some havoc in the tag team rankings. Masked Man #2: We want a match against anyone and we will show you that we mean business. CWO, prepare for the Youth Blast! Dynamite: There you have it. A new tag team in the CWO just like I promised. You'll be hearing more of us in the future. <camera fades> ______________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our last tag team interview today comes from a team that has gone under a vicious transformation of sorts, and has to be considered one of the favorites to win the tournament. Here are the Mercenaries. ______________________________________________________________ (Camera fades in on the Mercenaries, who are watching the video replay of their match with the Big Cool on the last FEVER. The match is down to the closing seconds, and Korperal Punishment picks up the remains of a shattered bottle and uses it to stab Jim "The Jazzman" Taylor, enabling the Mercenaries to get the win.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: One of our greatest moments. The Mercenaries taking out one of those fat lardbags. (The Mercenaries turn towards the camera. Punishment is holding a plastic bag that contains the blood streaked shards of the bottle used in the incident.) KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Just to let all you other tag teams know, the Mercenaries have been very generous over the last couple of weeks. We have warned every tag team competing in the tournament what was going to happen once they got in the ring with us, and gave you the opportunity to drop out of the tournament with no bodily damage. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: But now the deadline is up, and I must say, I am a little disapointed that nobody turned in their notice to leave the tournament. But I also would have to admit that I would be lying if I said that I'm not going to enjoy ripping every tag team apart that we come across in the tournament. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: But to every other tag team that is going into that tournament thinking that they're going to take it all, all I have to say is that by the time we get finished with you, you'll be begging us to just end your careers. You know why? Because we can. Just ask the "Fat"man Taylor, sitting in his hospital bed, getting fed through a straw. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: The Mercenaries saw your last pathetic interview, and it was so sad, it almost made us laugh. In fact, it DID make us laugh, the way you were carrying on, crying that you "couldn't do the job by yourself". You couldn't do the job when your fat ass partner was there! Now I suppose your buddy, Joe Eagle, is going to try something to get us back. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Well, here we are! But I think it's better to just cut your losses and learn a lesson. Just ask the guys we've laid out in the past... Miguel Sanchez, "Mini-Mind" Rich Clark, Sandman Jr., the Deadly Sins, the Bad Guys, Brent "The Ace" Alles, the Creatures of the Night, and the Regulators. You know, the team that got sick of us punking them on a regular basis, so they decided to leave the CWO. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: So by all means, carry out your little "plan". The Mercenaries are ready for anything you can pull. Why? Because we are the most vicious team in this federation, and because we are the team that's going to win that tournament. But behind those reasons, there is the biggest one.... KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Because we are THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD!!! _____________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our final interview for today comes from The Focus, responding to the earlier comments made by Wrath. _____________________________________________________________ [The camera appears on a completely dark room. The only light is from the eyes of The Focus, which have been digitally altered to appear bright white.] Wrath, am I a pathetic loser? I always thought that I took loss in stride. Ever since my first match in the CWO, when I got cheated out of a win with Benjamin Blake Bentley, I've understood that loss is the most terrible thing in this business, but it's also inevitable from time to time. So, if a pathetic loser is someone who dared to take on a man of your immense size, and give you the beating of your life, in the match of your life...then I am indeed a pathetic loser. You may want to watch your words big man, so they don't come back to haunt you. I will haunt you. So many men have faced me in this organization. Constantly busting me open, and beating the hell out of me. I almost like it. Perhaps I'm just used to it. I am one of the best wrestlers in the CWO. Bar none. Wrath, you are not. You will never be. You are nothing. I am everything. _____________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody Wednesday on the next REPORT! (fade to closing credits.)
