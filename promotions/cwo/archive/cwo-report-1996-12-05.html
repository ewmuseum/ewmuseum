CWO REPORT 12/5/96

5 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, December 5, 1996. Last night, the superstars of the CWO were in Olympia, WA, let's see how that great card went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 4, 1996 OLYMPIA, WA Coldfront vs. Gods of Thunder Coldfront defeated Gods of Thunder in 5:54 when Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung pinned DC after a top rope hurricarana. Mike "Lonestar" Williams vs. Lafayette Mike "Lonestar" Williams pinned Lafayette with the inside cradle in 9:54. Sin vs. Johnny Reb Johnny Reb made Sin submit to the Texas Cloverleaf in 7:32. Highlanders vs. Deadly Sins Highlanders defeated an obviously distracted Deadly Sins team in 4:05 when Gavin pinned Pride after the Quickening (superplex by Gavin, splash from the top rope by Conor). Black Death vs. Tiger Rogers (substituting for Bane) Tiger Rogers pinned Black Death after a flying dropkick in 6:52. Cake Boy vs. Sandman Jr. Cake Boy made Sandman Jr. submit to the Head Scissors in 9:30. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Dark Raven vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo Dark Raven pinned Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo quickly in 2:41 after nailing him with the Death From Above flying dropkick. Before the match, Schillo was brutally beaten by the Dark Side, however, strangely, Doomsday arrived to drive the Dark Side off. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Steel vs. Miguel Sanchez (champion) Miguel Sanchez pinned Steel after the Moonsault in 9:55. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (steel cage): Blood Inc. (Panther and D. E. A. T. H.) vs. The Dark Side (Hellhound and Creatures of the Night) (champions) The Dark Side defeated Blood Inc. in 7:03 when Night Slinger pinned Panther after the Lights Out avalanche. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in Portland, OR, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW THURSDAY, DECEMBER 5, 1996 PORTLAND, OR Johnny Reb vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart Maximum Overdrive vs. Dark Raven Baby Waldo vs. Hile Troy Mercenaries vs. Gods of Thunder SPIN THE WHEEL: The Biblical Powers vs. The Bad Guys Jackass Joel vs. Goliath Phantom Freightrain vs. Panther NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Hoss and Little Joe vs. Pit Bosses WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (Texas Death): "Big" Rich Clark vs. Master of Darkness Damion (champion) STEEL CAGE: Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. Mad Doctor's Ghost _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now go to comments from CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (WILDFIRE is in the interview area just as Miss Amber is leaving on a business trip.) WILDFIRE: GANGLORD! You have pushed me over the limit! Since I have been in the CWO, you have cost me two matches! Well, on this week's FEVER, you got a taste of what the WILDFIRE "CAN" do! I told you not to underestimate me! (WILDFIRE laughing to himself.) WILDFIRE: I have proven to you that I can beat you at your own game and tonight was just a sample of what I'm gonna do to you at CWO CELEBRATION! WHATCHA GONNA DO WHEN THE "WILDFIRE" RUNS WILD ON YOU! (WILDFIRE begins to laugh again as the camera fades out!) _______________________________________________________________________________ (GangLord and T.A.S. are celebrating yet another victory.) T. A. S.: Well, G, now that we've taken care of all the other problems here in the CWO, there is still one that needs to be cleared up, that is, with this punk, and I do mean punk, in every sense of the word, Wildfire, but look, he messed wit us now, he's lost his last two matches and because of you, G, that's who he should have learned not to mess wit da best! GANGLORD: And then after we showed you what's going to happen on that scaffold, you came down after I had already won, a little late on da planning by the way, and attacked me with a garbage can, as all us see that dent I put in that can, the next one's going to be in yo head, boy, so now you and that lil' streetwalker you got wit' you, by the way, guys, if ya only got 10 bucks, call Amber, she'll give ya a good time, she's in the yellow pages! But anyways, boy, we both goin' up on dat double scaffold but you might never come back just like Tub-o-but. Up there, man, no one can save you! (Camera fades out as T.A.S. starts looking through the Yellow Pages.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens on a wild, windy afternoon in the glens of Scotland; the scenery is sparse, the rolling hills decorated with bushes of thistles. On the top of one of these hills is the Highlander, Conor and Gavin McCloud.) GAVIN MCCLOUD: Och, we're just taken' a wee break here to remind ourselves of our roots every now again; remember what is real and important to us. Take a good look around you; this is a rugged country, and it breeds rugged men. Tough men. Men like us. We've got a lot of teams chasing after us at the moment - teams with poncy, overblown names like Deadly Sins, Apocalypse, Blitzkrieg - and I've got ta ask ye - what's wrong with ye? This is wresslin', not the end of the world. So bring on; keep jumpin' in the ring with us, chests puffed out and egos swelled, and we'll keep knockin' ye down to size. Tell 'em lad.... CONOR: THERE CAN BE ONLY ONE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny as the CWO CELEBRATION CONTROL CENTER graphic comes up.) STARR: All right, here we go with your CWO CELEBRATION CONTROL CENTER. Celebrating the one-year anniversary of the CWO, this should be a night to remember. However, it may be the last night the CWO every promotes a card. As you know, Gold Kard now has control over the league, he is the primary stockholder. However, he has given one man, Hile Troy, the chance to win back the CWO if he can defeat him. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (winner gets control of the CWO): HILE TROY vs. GOLD KARD (w/German Choklat) STARR: The eyes of the wrestling world will certainly be on that one, keep in mind, Gold Kard has said that if he wins this match, he will be firing those who do not meet his "standards." Yours truly, Johnny Starr, may find himself out of a job. The other night on FEVER, "Killer" Kyle Melinn, who has been with the CWO from the start, left in disgust at the actions of Gold Kard International. He may be gone as well. We all know where the loyalties of David "The Truth" Thomas are, however, he was once a member of Gold Kard International! Anyway, that match is perhaps eclipsed by the World Championship showdown that will be taking place. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: JOHNNY REB (w/Rebel Yell) vs. MASTER OF DARKNESS DAMION (champion) STARR: Damion has vowed that Reb will NEVER hold a title while he is still in the league, Reb wants to shatter that vow. Reb now has former CWO World Champion Rebel Yell in his corner, it might make the difference. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (SPIN THE WHEEL): MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE vs. MIGUEL SANCHEZ (champion) STARR: These two have a long history in the CWO, and now they will meet for the Television Championship. The match will be a Spin the Wheel match, as Sanchez wants to prove himself the most "hardcore" wrestler in the league. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO vs. DARK RAVEN (champion) STARR: We all witnessed the horrible attack by Dark Raven on Schillo last Tuesday night on FEVER, trying to suffocate him with a plastic bag, and hitting him in the throat with a lead pipe. Obviously, Schillo could not get a rematch at the World title, Reb gets it because he won ULTIMATE WARFARE in November. So, Schillo has opted for the next best thing, and starts his vengeful mission against the Dark Side starting with Dark Raven. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: BIBLICAL POWERS (Savior and Moses) vs. CREATURES OF THE NIGHT (Night Crawler and Night Slinger) STARR: The Biblical Powers are so far undefeated in tag competition, except for a loss in a WARGAMES match. The dominant Creatures will have their hands full with the power of Savior and the speed of Moses. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: DEADLY SINS (Pride and Sloth) vs. PIT BOSSES (champions) STARR: The Deadly Sins will have to focus past their manager being kidnapped, apparently by the Apocalypse and Bane, and try to defeat the Bosses in this title match. LOCKED STEEL CAGE: SANDMAN vs. BANE STARR: You know all about this one, the question is: will Bane be in this, or will he be in jail? We're not making a joke, authorities are looking to apprehend Bane, however, commissioner Randy Johnson has stood fast in the signing of this match, in his words, he wants it to be finished. And in this locked steel cage match, Randy Johnson will be at ringside, only he can stop the match when he determines that one man is unable to continue. BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES vs. "MASTER OF PAIN" BARRY HART STARR: A GKI member no more, Brent "The Ace" Alles goes up against the former CWO World champion, who is still a card-carrying member of the organization that might take over the CWO. CAKE BOY vs. SANDMAN JR. STARR: The very... um... unusual newcomer to the CWO, Cake Boy goes against the newly "reformed" Sandman Jr. DOUBLE SCAFFOLD MATCH: GANGLORD vs. WILDFIRE STARR: A very dangerous match, this hot feud moves into this area, where you not only have to knock your opponent off 1 scaffold, but two! Besides those big matches, here are others: ROOSTER vs. PANTHER FLYBOY vs. SIN HELLHOUND vs. AKILLIES INFERNO vs. TALON LAFAYETTE vs. JOE EAGLE JACKASS JOEL vs. BABY WALDO TIGER ROGERS vs. CLARK GRAY VINCENT "THE LIP" MANOCELLI vs. MONDO GECKO BENJAMIN BLAKE BENTLEY vs. BATTLECAT "LONESTAR" MIKE WILLIAMS vs. "BIG" RICH CLARK BLACK DEATH vs. BLOODBATH DAVID AND GOLIATH vs. HOSS AND LITTLE JOE GODS OF THUNDER vs. BAD GUYS MERCENARIES vs. THE BIG COOL ETERNAL WANDERERS vs. FLAME SPIRITS TEAM BLITZKRIEG vs. MASON-DIXON D. E. A. T. H. vs. HOLLYWOOD MUSCLE APOCALYPSE vs. REGULATORS HIGHLANDERS vs. COLDFRONT BRIAN "THE HAMMER" WILLIAMS vs. JUDGMENT STARR: So there you have it. The complete lineup for the entire event, some of those matches will be on the SHOWCASE SPECIAL, two will be on the EYE ON THE STORM, and the rest will be on the PPV spectacular. Don't miss the CWO's 1st birthday party, it'll be a blast! That's all the time we have for the report today, we'll see you tomorrow. (Closing graphics and music comes up.)
