CWO REPORT 1/25/97

25 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, January 25, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Tonight is TRIPLE THREAT, if you have not ordered this PPV yet, I implore you to do so. Some great matches, some intriguing combinations, you can't afford to miss it. Meanwhile, the CWO was in Aurora, IL, last night, home of Wayne and Garth, let's look at the results from this EXCELLENT card! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 1/24/97 Aurora, IL NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Eternal Wanderers defeated Pit Bosses when Exile pinned Pit Boss #2 after the End of the World (Exile with a Boston crab, Exodus with a moonsault). Time of match: 12:04. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Moses pinned Mondo Gecko after the The Commandment. Time of match: 18:54. Blood Inc. defeated The Coldfront when Steel made 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White submit to the The Gank. After the match Steel and Panther got into a shoving match. Time of match: 9:23. Sin defeated Beauregard Dixon by countout. Time of match: 5:17. Steve Keller defeated Benjamin Blake Bentley by reversed decision when Bentley refused to let up after the match. Time of match: 7:31. Tiger Rogers pinned 'Lonestar' Mike Williams after the left hook. Time of match: 14:45. Gold Kard pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after Die Flieder Kross. Time of match: 15:59. The Big Cool defeated Deadly Sins by disqualification. Time of match: 4:16. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As we said before, tonight is TRIPLE THREAT, let's look at the complete lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ TRIPLE THREAT: SHOWCASE SPECIAL: Sandman Jr. vs. Talon vs. Dark Raven Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Beauregard Dixon vs. Hellhound Mondo Gecko vs. Steve Keller vs. "Lonestar" Mike Williams Tiger Rogers vs. Phantom Freightrain vs. Black Death DARK MATCH: Hoss and Little Joe vs. David and Goliath vs. Revolution EYE ON THE STORM: Battlecat vs. Clark Gray vs. Bloodbath Flyboy vs. Steel vs. Inferno TRIPLE THREAT: Highlanders vs. Coldfront vs. Mercenaries Vincent Manocelli vs. Rich Clark vs. Baby Waldo Joe Eagle vs. Jackass Joel vs. Panther Big Cool vs. D. E. A. T. H. vs. Regulators Brent Alles vs. Gold Kard vs. Brian Williams TV CHAMPIONSHIP: Maximum Overdrive (champion) vs. Miguel Sanchez vs. Sin NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Eternal Wanderers (champions) vs. Pit Bosses vs. Bad Guys NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Bane vs. Sandman vs. Hile Troy (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Biblical Powers vs. Deadly Sins vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Master of Darkness Damion (champion) vs. Johnny Reb vs. Wayne Schillo ________________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: One of the matches on the SHOWCASE SPECIAL involves Beauregard Dixon vs. Hellhound vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley. As we discussed yesterday, Beauregard Dixon's partner, Stonewall Mason, was injured by the Creatures of the Night several weeks ago, we look forward to Mason-Dixon's return as a tag team next Tuesday night on FEVER. However, you can be sure that Dixon will be looking for revenge against the Dark Side anyway he can get it, starting with Hellhound. We take you now to these comments from Hellhound. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on Hellhound who is running in circles looking all around for something or someone.) HELLHOUND (to himself): Hellhound is to give first interview alone today but master is not here and camera is... what do I do. I know what to say so I talk to camera alone... (to camera) Saturday, Hellhound has chance to move up the CWO rankings just like master wants, all I have to do is win the triple threat match. I get to go in ring against someone named Ben Blakley and another called Dixon. I know Dixon, I hurt his partner and put him out of wrestling for a while, well now I will do the same to you so you can see your friend in the hospital. For you, Ben Blakley, you have name same as masters old enemy, Ben Slocum and for this I will do to you the harm and suffering that the master did to Slocum. (Just then MOD Damion appears.) MOD DAMION (to cameraman): What the hell are you doing here? I told you to wait til I finished with my interview before starting this but you did not listen and fir that you pay. (The camera falls to the ground still running and we can hear the cameraman screaming.) MOD DAMION: NOW DARKNESS FALLS! (There is a thud heard, then silence. Still running, the camera then sees MOD Damion approach Hellhound.) MOD DAMION (to Hellhound): I told you to wait for me, you did not listen either, I hope you remembered what I told you to say to the camera? HELLHOUND (on his knees at Damion's side): Yes, Master, I did just like you told me. I said it all just right and to please you I will win the triple threat match at the PPV. MOD DAMION: Yes, my pet, I know you will win, all the Dark Side shall leave the ring tonight victorious. My Creatures will win and retain their titles, I will win and keep this world belt, Dark Raven will begin his way back to the light weight title and you, Hellhound, had better win. BOTH MEN: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Hellhound grabs the camera and throws it at the wall and the screen goes blank.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you saw in the HOUSE SHOW REPORT for today, last night Blood Inc. members Steel and Panther got into a shoving match after their win over the Coldfront last night. It is not clear why this happened, after all, they won the match. However, this interview we just received may shed some light on the status of Blood Inc. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Panther is in the interview area, alone for the first time without Da Boss or any other members of Blood Inc.) PANTHER: Man, I can't wait for Triple Threat! I have some stiff competition in Joe Eagle and Jackass Joel. I have been watching these two veterans go at for some time now, and man are they tough! I know I'm going to have my work cut out for me, but I'm gonna give it my best effort and with a little luck I'll come out on top. Everybody out there, I would like youto meet some people. (He looks off camera and motions for the ladies waiting there to come in. In walks three very attractive women, all wearing extremely tight yellow leather full body suits with spike heels.) PANTHER: These beautiful women you see before you are the KITTENS! I know they sure make me PURRRRR! Oh well, back to the point: at Triple Threat, I have my wor... (In enters Da Boss with a more than slightly disturbed look upon his face and interupts Panther.) DA BOSS: BOY! What do you think you are pulling? Out here giving compliments to a bunch of second rate wrestlers, and not to mention, who in the hell are these dolled up streetwalkers?!? PANTHER: I suggest you watch what you say about the ladies! Now in the past I have done what you asked, played by your rules, and look where it got me. I still have yet to win my first championship. DA BOSS: Now listen here, I OWN you until that contract runs out, you do what I say and when I say to do it! Do you UNDERSTAND me? PANTHER: Yeah, I understand, but let me tell you something, BOY! You don't OWN me, I have been carrying Blood Inc. for a little too long now. I have done all your dirty work and took care of all your business. Now it's time for me to do something for me, not only something for me but something for the people who come fill these arenas everywhere the CWO goes. That's right, I'm gonna do something for the FANS! DA BOSS (slaps Panther in the face): Now let's get one thing straight, I'm Da Boss, you got that? PANTHER: Yeah, I got it. (Da Boss turns to leave, and Panther attacks him.) PANTHER (with the Da Boss still on the floor): I think our relationship is through. (Camera fades out slowly.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: How about that? Apparently Blood Inc. is now down to 3 members, as Panther has pledged his allegiance to the great fans of the CWO. We'll have to wait and see the reaction of the rest of Blood Inc. to this "defection." All right, let's turn our attention now to the North American Championship. Tonight on TRIPLE THREAT, it is on the line, as champion Hile Troy goes up against the deadly Bane and a traitorous Sandman. We take you back to this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER to show you what happened between the former leaders of the Chaotic Dynasty. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Footage from 1/21/97 TNF is shown, with Hile Troy fighting Tiger Rogers.) MELINN: Rogers whips Troy into the ropes... big boot to the face! And... oh no! Troy falling back and crashing into referee Spencer Doloff, Doloff is down. THOMAS: Now go to work, Tiger! MELINN: Rogers with a crossface submission applied... trying to get Troy to submit, but it doesn't matter, Doloff is still trying to get up after his collision with Troy. THOMAS: I heard him say it, Melinn! I heard him say "I Quit!" MELINN: He said no such thing! Rogers playing to the crowd, Troy is trying to crawl under the ropes out of the ring... wait a minute... oh no! It's Sandman, what's he... NO! Sandman with an empty bottle of champagne, and he smashes it over the head of Troy! THOMAS: Maybe I should rethink my opinion about Sandman after all! MELINN: That bottle shattering over the head of Troy, he could have a concussion, he is bleeding from the head, this is sick, these men were the best of friends once, they co-formed the Chaotic Dynasty, and Sandman has made it very clear what his agenda is now. THOMAS: I love it! MELINN: Referee Spencer Doloff finally recovering, I'm not sure if either him or Rogers saw it, Rogers was too busy jawjacking with some members of the front row. Rogers has the bleeding Foul up... Inverted Powerbomb! Forget about it! 1... 2... 3! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We know about the implications between Sandman and Troy, however, the quickly rising superstar, Tiger Rogers, certainly got a boost of self-esteem when he beat the North American champion in a non-title match, interference by Sandman notwithstanding. We take you to comments from Tiger Rogers. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Tiger Rogers is in the interview area.) TIGER ROGERS: Hile Troy! Being the champion that you truly are, wouldn't you be interested in giving a title shot to a worthy opponent? Say an opponent who defeated you in the middle of the ring? An opponent, who you turned around and attacked not 3 days later? Hile Troy, your time is up... 1997 has been a great year so far for Tiger Rogers, you're next on my list... next time, it won't be such an upset! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today. We'll see you tonight at TRIPLE THREAT! (Closing creds and music come up as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ Copyright 1997 Just B Cuz Productions http://web.syr.edu/~bgalles/cwo.html Cyberspace Wrestling Organization (CWO) All Rights Reserved
