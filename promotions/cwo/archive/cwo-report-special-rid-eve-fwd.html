CWO REPORT SPECIAL: RID EVE (fwd)

6 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey, who is standing in the middle of an illuminated ring.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to a special edition of the CWO REPORT, on the eve of the CWO July blockbuster, Rocking Independence Day. Everybody here at the CWO hopes your 4th of July was excellent and I can say for a fact that the wrestlers that will participate in tomorrow's showdown are ready to go. Speaking of tomorrow's PPV, we have breaking news to report in that some of the matches reported on earlier have been altered. Let's have a look at the revised card for the PPV: CWO ROCKIN' INDEPENDENCE DAY 7/6/97 TENTATIVE LINEUP - CARD SUBJECT TO CHANGE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Gold Kard vs. Joe Eagle (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Highlanders vs. Bad Guys (champions) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Masher Maccabee vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mercenaries vs. Regulators (champions) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Benjamin Blake Bentely vs. Diamondback Chris Myers (champion) WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Brent "The Ace" Alles, Vegas High Roller, Matsuhiro Torunaka vs. Jason "Flash" Varga, Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, Brian "The Hammer" Williams (champions) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Mondo Gecko vs. Moses (champion) GRUDGE MATCH: Johnny Reb vs. Lord Hile Troy SINGAPORE CANE MATCH: Sandman Jr. vs. The Focus BURIED ALIVE MATCH: Fergus McTavish vs. Bloodbath "Monster" Rich Clark vs. ??? 8-MAN TAG: Mason-Dixon, Wildfire, "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher vs. X-Factor, Marc Mission, Dragon Express Steel vs. Wrath Ghost Riders vs. Alliance (Inferno and David "The Destroyer" Davis) CWO LUCHA LIBRE (winner receives shot at Light Heavyweight title at 7/13 SHOWCASE): El Grande Pistolerro vs. "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia EYE ON THE STORM: ROCKIN' INDEPENDENCE DAY Lord Guru's newest member of the Cabin vs. Goliath The Big Cool vs. Deadly Sins vs. Brother Justice Miguel Sanchez vs. "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat DARK MATCHES: Bishop and Dark Raven vs. Bloody Fangs Reverend Child Stealer vs. Ganglord WASHEY: As you can see, the most notable revision of this card will be a rematch for the CWO TV Title, as Benjamin Blake Bentley will try and take back his title from Diamondback Chris Myers, who defeated Bentley on the FEVER held earlier this week. Also, as you can see, a few new matches have been signed at the last minute. Speaking of the CWO's Television Champion, he's got a few words to say before his big rematch with Bentley. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Diamondback Chris Myers and Dallas are shown driving down I-70 through Pennsylvania in Chris' 1963 Corvette. The windows are down and Chris is pushing 80) Dallas: Chris, slow down! We're going to get pulled over! Myers: No sweat, Dallas, because I'm THE MAN! Dallas: You sound just like Matthew White. Myers: Good God, you're right! Heaven forbid I sound like him! (Dallas smirks at this comment) Myers: Oh come on, a little good natured fun? Dallas: Just get it all out of your system now, Chris, because Sunday is going to be all work. Myers: Wrath? We've watch how many tapes on him? Seven! Seven tapes of him wrestling! I think I know the guy inside and out, Dallas. Dallas: It's not him I'm worried about... Myers: What, Lust? Dallas, you could pound that woman into the pavement anyday of the week! Dallas: One, this is now a title match. Wrath is hungry, Chris. Two, he has the Deadly Sins at his back, and how many times have Pride and Sloth jumped in to save him? Third, GKI is pissed at you! Myers: Yeah, did you see the look on Bentley's face when I beat him! I told him I'd win that belt! Dallas: Well, bet on him to show up during the match! Myers: If any of GKI show up, especially that Gold Kard, I'll gladly take time from beating Wrath to show them a thing or two...I can't believe he slapped you! You would think someone of his upbringing would treat women with some respect... Dallas: After everything you've seen Gold Kard do in his time here, you think he has honor? Myers: Point taken. Ok, this is what we're going to do, then. One, we're going to beat Wrath at ROCKIN' INDEPENDENCE DAY. Two, we're going to give BBB his rematch as soon as possible. GKI owns the championship committee, so we might as well get that out of the way as soon as possible. Dallas: Anyone else you think should get a title shot? Myers: Was that sarcasm? Dallas: Could be... Myers: Dallas, I don't want to be the kind of champion that waits for people to come to him! I want to go out and beat people! It's the only way to increase my coverage! Dallas: You have the TV belt, Chris. You HAVE to wreslte once a week. Isn't that enough? Myers: I think Ryan Deustcher should get a shot. He called me out, and the least I can do is give it to him. Dallas: Just him? Myers: That's all I can think of off the top of my head. Dallas: Well, wake me when we get to D.C., ok? Myers: No sweat...and don't worry... (Myers reaches into the back seat and rubs the CWO Television belt) Myers: ...Wrath, GKI, and the world ain't going to take this hard-earned baby away from me. ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Just to let everybody know, the CWO Championship Commitee decided to take Panther out of the title match after this interview was done, due to an ankle injury he suffered at FEVER at the hands of Wrath and the Deadly Sins. Speaking of which, we now have comments from the Deadly Sins, who are involved in a 3 way tag team match tomorrow against the Big Cool and Brother Justice. ________________________________________________________________________________ PRIDE: The BIG COOL, eh? More like the Big Dummies. We showed them last month that the Sins are the best... but they want some more? We'll give them some more. SLOTH: First, WHO's been disputing out win-loss record? Get some referees who aren't paid off and aren't blind and you'd see a perfect record! COLDFRONT, I don't know who you slep with to get in this triangle match, and quite frankly, it scares me to find out. Regardless, you'll be facing the same fate as the BIG COOL. Losing to the DEADLY SINS! WRATH: First of all, PANTHER, I didn't mean to bust your ankle. Mind you, I don't CARE, but I do want you to know it ws unintentional. Now, CHRIS MYERS... did I call this? DID I? I called you a ROCKY MAIVIA-type, and boy, are you proving me right... you just won the TV belt, just like MAIVIA won the I-C belt. Here's another coincidence: you beat BBB, he beat HHH. Remember the first time ROCKY fought against someone with talent? HE LOST THE BELT! Guesss what, MYERS... I'm YOUR OWEN HART! It's about time I got a title shot, and once you get in the REPENTION RACK, it's all over for you... and that belt's as good as mine! LUST: THE DEADLY SINS are destined to win the titles... and CHIRS MYERS... if you or your slut girlfriend get out of line, you have to deal with ME... and I KNOW you don't want THAT! ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up we have the tag team that will not only enter the ring as challengers tomorrow against the Regulators, but for the first time in 3 weeks, will not have the CWO North American Tag Team titles with them. Here are the Mercenaries. ________________________________________________________________________________ KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Regulators, the hours are ticking down to the Pay Per View, and so's your time with those belts of ours that you stole on FEVER the other night. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: We've beaten you more times than we can count. Sunday will be no different. Unlike your the last PPV match, your manager won't be able to bail you out when we kick your asses. Nobody's going to be there as we stake our claim to become CWO North American Tag Team Champions. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: And don't think we've forgotten about you, GKI. You're the main reasons why those belts are in the hands of those morons. Gold Kard, we've vowed to deal with you once we put away those clones that are posing as the champions. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: And there isn't a damn thing you can do about it. Nobody's going to be able to help you, because they'll be laying by the wayside. We are THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD, and you're going to find out that you aren't going to be able to make a move without the Mercenaries making it our personal business. ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: I know that the Mercenaries are one of the toughest teams in the CWO, but I think in this case, they may be biting off more than they can chew. Finally, we have words with Sandman Jr. on the Focus and their Singapore Cane match. ________________________________________________________________________________ SANDMAN JR: You know FOCUS, this is kinda funny... I finally get you in MY kinda match, a Singpaore Cane match... but I can't 'focus' on YOU. All I can think about is how MIGUEL SANCHEZ sold both me and himself out... but I will be ready, FOCUS... cause I don't need to be 100% to beat you... all I need is a cane in one hand, a Coke in the other, and the beautiful KELLEN to be my back-up, and I'm one happy guy. And THIS happy guy is gonna beat you. One way or the other, FOCUS. One way or the other. ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody on Monday with a complete rundown of all the action that took place tomorrow, as well as comments from some of it's participants. So long everybody! (fade to closing credits.)
