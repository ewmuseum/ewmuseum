CWO REPORT 6/25/97 (fwd)

26 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens up on closing moments of CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER.) MELINN: Gold Kard outside the ring now, and now Clark is out there as well, he's got a steel chair... and he brings it down right across the back of Gold Kard! FERRARA: Listen to that impact! MELINN: And I have never cheered anybody before for using a steel chair, but I'm cheering now, as Clark nails Gold Kard in the head with a chair! FERRARA: Gold Kard is out cold! MELINN: Clark rolling Gold Kard in the ring.... (Clark again thrusts his hand in the air, as the crowd cheers.) MELINN: And Gold Kard is unconscious, he has no way to fight the Choke Slam if Clark gets it on him... wait a minute... what the... the ring! The ring has opened up! "Monster" Rich Clark is being dragged down into the ring! FERRARA: This is impossible! (The ring swallows up Clark, and then a gout of flame comes from the spot where he was taken into.) EVIL VOICE ON THE LOUDSPEAKER (laughing): Something... has returned.... MELINN: I don't know what to say... except that Gold Kard is face down in the middle of the ring, and the referee, I guess he's going to have to declare him the winner, because Clark has vanished! ___________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on clips of several CWO wrestlers in action. The words CWO REPORT scroll up and explode. Fade into host, Mitch E. Washey.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Tuesday Night FEVER was held last night in Boston, MA and the ongoing war between the CWO and the forces of GKI is escalating as we speak. Let's look at a rundown of last night's card: CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER BOSTON, MA 6/25/97 Masher Maccabee defeated X-Factor with the Homentaschen Hangover in 12:27 Interview with Wildfire, where Ganglord announces his return to the CWO to aid in the fight with the GKI. Bishop and Dark Raven defeated the Regulators in 16:55 after Dark Raven pinned Regulator Ice following a power elbow drive and pulling the tights. Regulator Ice was attacked by the Mercenaries in the closing seconds of this match, as the Mercenaries bashed him in the head with both of the North American Tag Team Titles. Interview with Fergus McTavish CWO Television Champion Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned Fergus McTavish with the Nobility in 10:22, retaining his title. After the match Bentley grabbed Billy, the little boy that appears with McTavish, and spanked him. Bentley went to give McTavish another Nobility, but Bloodbath appeared, chokeslamming Bentley. The Highlanders rushed the ring, but McTavish, apparently confused over what was happening, DDT'd Conor and started fighting with Gavin. A very unkempt Brent "The Ace" Alles made an appearance and challenged a number of CWO wrestlers such as Maximum Overdrive, Baby Waldo, Serpentmaster, Wayne Schillo and Brian Williams. Overdrive and Waldo came down to the ring and attacked Alles. Gordy Alles, Brent's father, ran to the ring to aid his son, but was attacked by Waldo and Overdrive. GKI came down to the ring and Gold Kard ordered Overdrive to stop his attack. Overdrive refused, giving Alles a second Pileup Powerbomb and Gold Kard terminated the contracts of Overdrive and Waldo. Gold Kard approached Gordy Alles, saying he could help his son if Alles would accept his offer. Gordy Alles accepted and GKI took "The Ace" away. Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Vegas High Roller with the Rattler in 18:05. Interview with Brother Justice The match between the CWO World Tag Team Champions the Highlanders and the Big Cool degenerated into a four way brawl when the Bad Guys and the Deadly Sins appeared. The bell was rung at 14:07, with the decision resulting in a no contest. "Monster" Rich Clark was counted out in his match with Gold Kard in 5:05, under mysterious circumstances when Clark disappered through a hole in the ring. With Kard's victory, this means he will get the CWO World Title match with Joe Eagle at the July PPV. WASHEY: Due to circumstances beyond the control of CWO REPORT staff, we have not recieved a copy of this week's House Shows. We will now move to the interview portion of our REPORT. First off today, we have comments from Bloodbath, who made an appearance at last night's FEVER. Let's go to him now. _____________________________________________________________________ (There is a lone blank grave stone in a cemetary. A bolt of lightening strikes the ground and Bloodbath emerges from the ground and the grave stone now reads "Whirling Scottish Devil") BLOODBATH (dark & mysterious):First you cost me a shot at the CWO World Title, then you managed to stuff me into a casket, but you made one fatel mistake. YOU DID NOT DESTROY ME!!! You see, I am no mear mortal, I fear no man, and I feel no pain!! I know what its like to be thrown into the ground, I know how it feels to see the six feet of dirt being piled on. Are you afraid of the dark? (Bloodbath breaks out in this eere, evil laugh) BLOODBATH (dark & mysterious):Now, the time for talking is done. But after I bury you alive, I have made arangements to bury your little friend too!!! REST IN PEACE!!!!!!! ______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Bloodbath scheduled to meet Fergus McTavish at the PPV, this time in the CWO's first "Buried Alive" match. Next up we have comments from one Diamondback Chris Myers, who is taking the fight right to GKI, with his win over Vegas High Roller on last night's FEVER. ______________________________________________________________________________ (Diamondback Chris Myers is shown sitting on a turnbuckle in the ruined ring at the Fleet Center in Boston. He's looking down at the hole where "Monster" Rich Clark was dragged through. Dallas is nowhere to be seen) Myers: What a sight, huh? Seeing a 7'4", 400+ pound man get dragged through the middle of the ring. Watching that idiot Gold Kard manage to retain his "#1 status" for Rockin' Independence Day? (Myers looks down at the corner of the ring, where Brent Alles had sat only hours before, drinking his problems away) Myers: Alcohol. To quote a great American philosopher, Homer Simpson, "Alcohol. The cause of, and the solution to, all of life's problems." I remember watching "The Ace" when I was debating whether or not to apply to CWO, and anticipating the day that I could wrestle a guy like Brent Alles. Now...now, it's up to GKI to save Alles from the bottle. They'll probably do it, mind you, but can they save Alles' soul? I don't think so. Only Brent can do that. (Myers looks at the empty seats, and his voice echoes throughout the Fleet Center) Myers: MYERS HITS THE RATTLER! COVER, ONE, TWO, THREE! Roller, I told you I could beat you. Your Vegas Swinger couldn't stand up to my Rattler. Now, I'm not going to run around saying "GKI is crumbling, yay!" Nope. I am going to say that I beat you, and that GKI CAN be beaten. Gold Kard, Masher Macabee, the Japaneese guy whose name I don't want to butcher, High Roller, Benjamin Blake Bentley...I can't blame Bentley for wanting to join GKI. Hell, they're the only reason he's walking around with that TV belt around his waist. Rats know their own kind, and they tend to stick together. (Myers stares back down at the hole in the ring) Myers: Seeing the biggest man in CWO get dragged down into this ring made me think. In my mind, Rich Clark was CWO's last chance. But instead, someone decided that his own needs were more important then this fed's. When everyone should be coming together to fight GKI, they're all fighting for a share of the scraps. (Myers jumps over the ring ropes to the floor and walks to the announcer's booth, which hasn't been torn down yet by the roadies) Myers: We're fighting over a share of the scraps, while guys like Joe Root get fired for telling "The Truth", which David Thomas hasn't told since the 4th grade. Well, I'm sick of it. I came here because everyone seemed to stand for CWO, even those people who didn't seem to give a damn about the fans. Now everyone is going their own direction, while GKI stands in the middle, laughing at us. Well, I ain't moving. I worked hard to get here, and I plan to work hard to stay here. GKI, you better not get in the way of Chris Myers, because I have a goal, and I don't plan to be stopped. You saw what the Rattler did to Vegas High Roller. Pray you don't experience it first hand. (Myers turns and walks backstage as the camera fades to back) ____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Myers is expressing comments that I'm sure he shares with several CWO members. One tag team that doesn't, however are the Mercenaries, who are standing by with their comments. ____________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who are wearing their CWO North American Tag Team titles. They are in front of a video monitor, watching footage from last night's Fever.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Did you see that shot that I nailed Ice with? He was comatose! Then you came along and hit him again! What a brilliant move! And the Regulators think that they stand a chance against US? What a joke. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Speaking of jokes, how about those announcers? Did you hear some of the crap that they were saying? KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Yeah. Even that Thomas guy. Everybody thinks we still aren't the champions. How many times do we have to tell people that we ARE the CWO North American Tag Team Champions? We beat the Regulators for s***'s sake! Now we're being called the challengers at the PPV? Look at the titles we've got. Does it LOOK like we're the challengers? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Thomas said that when we beat the Regulators at the PPV we'll be the UNDISPUTED champions. That just shows you how clueless he is. We ARE the undisputed champions already. Wake up. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: How about that interview with that one tag team? Brother Justice. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: I thought for a second that somebody switched into a promo for Comedy Central. Let us tell you something, "Brother Justice". The only reason that you haven't said anything is because you were scared to. And in case you haven't gotten it through your walnut-sized brains yet, there is no justice in the CWO. Only anarchy. And just like anarchy, the Mercenaries are the ones who rule. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Besides, BUTTHEAD Justice, we've already met in the ring, and to be completely honest with you, the only riot you MIGHT be able to control is a group of kindergarteners complaining that they didn't get any cookies before their mid-morning nap. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: As for the "Law" having arrived, the only thing I've got to say to you four morons is that THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD was here long before you were, and we'll be here long after you've left. _____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Do those two guys honestly try to make enemies out of everybody that they open their mouths to? Our next interview is from one of the members of GKI, here is Lord Hile Troy. ______________________________________________________________________________ Sitting in the masters quarters of his lavish mansion in Blackpool, England, we see Hile Troy before a huge video screen with footage from previous matches on it Lord Hile Troy: (noticing cameraman) Hey... hey you there! How DID you get past the security? Cameraman: Umm... you called for me Mister, er... Lord Hile Troy, from the CWO? For the interview segment? Lord Hile Troy: Ahhh yes, seems that fans around the world can't get enough of Gold Kard International, led by the benevolance himself, Gold Kard. Come, aim the camera at my video wall and sit down to watch these, they're great! The camera heighth lessens as the video wall comes into view. Shots of Brent "The Ace" Alles and a much younger looking Kyle Melinn come into view as Lord Troy speaks. Lord Hile Troy: This one has to be simply one of my alltime favorites! See... way back when, in the original CWO, Alles was just as gullable as he is now! This is the match where I retire him from the CWO! TV MONITOR (Kyle Melinn): And Gold Kard is up on the ring apron! What is he doing? Gold Kard, with a can of golden hairspray and he shot Alles right in the eye with it! No! Lord Foul up from behind! Dragon Suplex! One... two... three! TV MONITOR (Dave Thomas): I love it! I love it! Foul wins! Lord Hile Troy: (while fast forwarding) Yeah, even back then Kard was as shrewed as he is now. Back then, he held the CWO Television I believe, or it may have been the lightweight one, I'm not sure... the point is that the Chaotic Dynasty held ALL of the belts in the CWO! CWO Cameraman: ALL of the belts? Lord Hile Troy: That's right my minimum wage friend... _each_ and _every_ belt that the league had. Now then, here's another good clip, this is RARE footage of me wrestling against the Four Horsemen of WCW in a Team Challenge Series TV MONITOR (Kyle Melinn): Foul gets the tag, and he's in the ring and on fire! He throws Arn Anderson into the ropes... Anderson reverses it... sticks his head down for a backbody drop, and Foul PLANTS him into the ground with the viledriver! Lord Hile Troy: That match got held in Bombay, so most of the fans never got to see it, but it happend, and the CWO got saved by me, and it wouldn't be the last time either... CWO Cameraman: You know, I'm really supposed to be here to talk about The Wild Things and Johnny Reb... Lord Hile Troy: <laughing outloud> Johnny Reb? Oh... I have got some _GREAT_ footage on that one!... Here's a clip first off, of him being hung by a scaffold by Master of Darkness Damion, he dosen't look so tough here does he? (Troy Fast-Forwards) Lord Hile Troy: He's another shot... this time when he thought he was going to join the Chaotic Dynasty, so he turned his back on Rebel Yell, and then we all SACKED him! CWO Cameraman: How does all of this effect your match with him at the pay per view? Lord Hile Troy: I understand that it's been labeled as a 'grudge match' eh? Appropriate, appropriate... there's a lot of bad blood between the two of us. Here's your main factor for that match. I am english blood, regal and royal, an autocrat. Johnny Reb, on the otherhand, is an extra left over from "Hee-Haw". He's never been a world champion. Myself? I've held ALL of the belts in the CWO at one point or another, except for the Television Title, which he USED to have! It's worthless tin when you take the time to compare it to what I've held before! CWO Cameraman: Okay.. what about the Wild Things? Lord Hile Troy: You know, I wasn't sure on that one, I didn't know what to think. Then, I went to the man himself, Gold Kard, and I inquired about these guys, and he said to me, with accent of course "Herr Troy, if there has ever been a time when you need not worry, this is it." Kard and I have seen some TOUGH battles, and we've had some of the GREATEST matches you'll find in ANY CWO archieve, I trust the man. Wild Things, an ocean of rookies, going up against the masters. Gold Kard, Maccabee, High Roller, Moses, it dosen't matter. When you're dealing with GKI, you're dealing with number _one_+! _________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: (muttering to himself) ....yeah, he's "number one" all right. (To camera) Our final interview for today is from a man we haven't heard a lot out of lately. Here is the "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher. __________________________________________________________________________ ("Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher stands in the locker room area running over the tape of Tuesday Night Fever) DEUTSCHER:Im really getting sick and tired of this!! I came to the CWO for one reason, to be a champion!! I havent had a match here in a long time!!! Well you know what I have to say about that? Screw it!!! Screw all the bookers, all marketing suits!! Now its time for me to do a little ass kicking!! And Im gonna start with Diamondback Chris Meyers!! Yeah Meyers, you are a great superstar, but you make me sick!! You parade around thinking your the best, so what, your in the top ten, big deal, if your so great then how come you dont have a title? I'll be in the ring waiting for you, but the question is, will you be there too?? You see, some may think that a "wolf" that strayes away from the pack is weak, well Meyers, maybe this "lone wolf" took out his pack!! I'll be waiting!! (the camera fades out) _____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Well folks, that's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody on the next REPORT. (fade to closing credits)
