CWO REPORT 9/21/97

21 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, September 21, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Montepelier, VT, let's look at what went down there. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 9/20/97 Montepelier, VT Sandman Jr./Diamondback Chris Myers defeated Pyro-Technics when Sandman Jr. pinned C-4 after the Permanent Dream. Time of match: 12:58. Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned Lord Hile Troy after the reversed small package. Time of match: 6:23. Holocaust defeated Gold Kard by countout when Gold Kard walked back to the dressing room. Time of match: 11:46. Vegas High Roller pinned Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo after the Vegas Swinger, pulling the tights. Time of match: 8:16. World Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys defeated North American Tag Team Champions The Big Cool in a rather amazing non-title "Champions' Challenge" match when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Jumbo Jenkins after the Bad Guys' Bash, pulling the tights. Time of match: 43:31. CWO WORLD CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: This was supposed to be another "Champions' Challenge" match between the Light Heavyweight Champion Jason "Flash" Varga vs. World Champion Johnny Reb, but before the match, Joe Eagle came down and demanded that Jason give up his title shot to him. Jason, after some hesitation, did so, and the match was on. Joe Eagle and Johnny Reb then wrestled to a double disqualification, with outside interference not only from the Tower of Power but Mason-Dixon and Rebel Yell as well. Time of match: 13:44. Johnny Reb retained the CWO World Champion title. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Don't forget about the great SUNDAY SHOWCASE coming out later today, let's look at the lineup! _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE 9/21/97 CWO Arena HOUR #1: Lord Hile Troy vs. Judgment X-Factor vs. Diamondback Chris Myers Miguel Sanchez vs. Sandman HOUR #2: "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia vs. Sandman Jr. Pyro-Technics vs. Coldfront LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Wildfire vs. Jason "Flash" Varga (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We take you now to comments from CWO superstars, starting with the CWO's own hardcore icon, Sandman Jr. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. and Kellen are in the interview area.) SANDMAN JR.: At Fightin' Friday Night, Rich Clark was lucky to beat me by DQ. If that moron Maccabee hadn't interfered, I was ready to small package you and get the win. Next time Clark, next time. Now at Fever, it was announced that a big 6-man tag is gonna take place as a warm-up for Sep. Slam II. Me and Chris, along with a guy I respect, though I don't necessarily LIKE him, the Focus, will be taking on Bentley, Sandman, and the Reverend. Well, Bentley, I still owe you, and I KNOW Chris still owes you. As for Sandman and Rev, you boys are in for a preview of pain! If that's what you really want, fine. But if you were smart, and nobody ever said either of you was, you'd stay away and wait to face defeat at Slam. No matter what, we will win here, and we will win there, and life will be... TOOO SWEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEET! (Kellen pours a Coke down Jr's throat, he gulps it and spits it in the air.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: At September Slam II, revenge is the name of the game, and "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat and the Coldfront will be looking for revenge against the Deadly Sins for their post-match attack on Heat at the TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES. Let's see if the Sins are worried about their upcoming match. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: Pulling the tights, eh Heat? The only way you could be a primarily tag-team wrestler was by pulling the tights. We may be low-lifes, but you're the lowest form of man on the planet. When me, Sloth, and Wrath get our hands on you guys, it's gonna be all over for you and the Front. SLOTH: Gold Kard, we ain't got a problem with you, and Brent Alles, we don't give a rat's ass about you either. We were after Heat only at Showcase, so don't be getting involved in our matches. Marc Mission and X-Factor saw what happens when you face the Sins, and it will NOT be any different for the rest of you. WRATH: Everyone knows how we will defeat Jimmy Heat and the Coldfront, Tommy Bomber be damned. After that though, I want a shot at the TV title. Who is the champ anymore? Chris Myervia? No, he got beat. Oh wait, it's the Focus. The heel/face guy, can't decide. Well, I know that I'm a heel, and a bad-ass. You got any guts, if you can beat Bentley, I want the next shot. And if I don't get it, you'll be in trouble. LUST: First, Chris O'Brien, next time I'll leave the keys to the handcuffs IF you perform better. As for Jimmy Heat, he'll never know my handcuffs, cause he isn't worthy. All he is worthy of is getting beat up, and he's gonna get what he's worth, at the earliest possible opportunity. Then Wrath will get a TV Title Shot, and the Sins will get Tag Title Shot, and that WILL be the bottom line, cause I damn well said so, and who's gonna argue it?!? _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally today, we hear from "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher, who is slated to take on "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia at SEPTEMBER SLAM II. _______________________________________________________________________________ ("Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher is in an old run down appartment in the slums of Chicago.) DEUTSCHER: You know something, when I first arrived here in the CWO I had everything going for me! But then I made the mistake! The big mistake of caring what the fans of the CWO thought of me! Then I started to slip farther and farther in the rankings! WELL, I'M SICK OF IT! Now I'm back here in the slums of Chicago! Back where I was born and raised! Back where I learned the only important rule of fighting, "DO ON TO OTHERS AS THEY DO ON TO YOU, BUT DO IT FIRST!" And that's why I'm gonna start kicking a little @$$ here in the CWO! And as for the fans of the CWO, if you dont like me, then you can kiss my @$$ and that's the bottom line! (Camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Looks like Deutscher has been watching "Cause Stone Cold Said So" a few too many times. Anyway, that's all we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
