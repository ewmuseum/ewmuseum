CWO REPORT 11-8-97 (fwd)

9 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey sitting at the CWO REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Last night, the CWO made a stop in Minneapolis, MN. Here are the results for last night's card. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 11/07/97 Minneapolis, MN Wrath pinned Hellhound (substituting for Judgment) after the Repentence Rack. Time of match: 12:06. Red Raven pinned 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum after the Raven's Flame, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 10:39. Skyhawk pinned The Prophet after the bodyslam. Time of match: 5:17. El Grande Pistolerro pinned Brian 'The Hammer' Williams after the Springboard Frankensteiner. Time of match: 10:21. Coldfront defeated Harvesters of Sorrow when Kurt Gerstung pinned Pain after the Icebreaker. Time of match: 19:31. Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo defeated Mondo Gecko by disqualification when The Law attacked him. Time of match: 3:52. CPE Enforcers defeated Brother Justice when Col. Taggart made Jermain Justice submit to the Annhilator. Time of match: 9:51. Col. Taggart was injured in this match. Television Champion Bloodbath pinned Mustafa Rahim after the Tombstone Powerbomb in a non-title match. Time of match: 5:07. World Tag Team Champions the Newton Bros. defeated Mercenaries when Steve Newton pinned Kaptain Krunch after the Newton Bomb. Time of match: 19:39. WASHEY: Tonight the CWO will be appearing in South Bend, IN. Here's the lineup for tonight's card. Bell time is at 8 pm. SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 8, 1997 SOUTH BEND, IN Diamondback Chris Myers vs. Stonewall Mason Skyhawk vs. Wrath Jade Dragons vs. Coldfront Masashi "Lightning" Lanther vs. Ron "Rude Guy" Johnson Mercenaries vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo and Brian "The Hammer" Williams Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Lord Hile Troy Red Raven and Holocaust vs. Master of Darkness Damion and Hellhound Gold Kard vs. Mondo Gecko WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, no DQ): Bad Guys vs. Newton Bros. (champions) WASHEY: As strange as it sounds, we have no interviews for today. We'll see everybody tomorrow on the REPORT! (fade to closing credits.)
