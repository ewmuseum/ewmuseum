Writers needed

21 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, my name is Brent G. Alles, Executive Director of the Cyberspace Wrestling Organization (CWO), and we are currently looking for writers for our league. As a writer, you would be responsible for one "hour" of our televised schedule each week, and would also be involved with general angle and character development for the wrestlers. The workload is roughly three matches a week, not too tough. So, if you are interested, let me know, and I will send you a sample match to write-up, and from that I will judge if you are a good enough writer to suit our needs. PLEASE DO NOT SUBMIT IF YOU ANTICIPATE ANY DRASTIC CHANGES IN YOUR LIFE SOON. I have had writers in the past few weeks say that they wanted to write for the league, and then pulled out only after a couple of days, usually leaving a card left behind unwritten. So, if you want to write for one of the better e-wrestling organizations, drop us a line, we're looking for a few good men! Sincerely, Brent G. Alles Executive Director, CWO Operations
