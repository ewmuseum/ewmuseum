REPORT 9/14/97

14 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, September 14, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr, Mitch E. Washey is on vacation. Last night the CWO was in our nation's capital, Washington, D. C., for some memorable action as only we can provide. Let's look at what went down in last night's capital combat! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 9/13/97 Washington, D. C. 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat pinned Pride after the Jimmyplex, pulling the tights in an impromptu lumberjack match with Heat having the Coldfront and Tommy Bomber on his side, and Pride having Deadly Sins Sloth, Wrath, and Lust on his. Time of match: 13:59. Sandman Jr. pinned Moses after the Permanent Dream. Time of match: 7:53. Vegas High Roller pinned Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo after the Vegas Swinger, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 11:06. Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned Lord Hile Troy after the reversed inside cradle. Time of match: 19:17. After the match Alles asked Troy to shake his hand, but Troy spit in his face, prompting a brawl between the two back to the dressing room. Gold Kard pinned Holocaust after Die Flieder Kross. Time of match: 10:04. Nation of Denomination defeated North American Tag Team Champions The Big Cool in a non-title match when Jehovah's Witness pinned Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor after the rollup after Wildfire struck Taylor with a bat. Time of match: 15:09. Joe Eagle pinned World Champion Johnny Reb in a non-title match after the Eagle Shock. Time of match: 22:20. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Don't forget about the great SUNDAY SHOWCASE coming your way today, let's take a look at the lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE 9/14/97 CWO Arena HOUR #1: Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat X-Factor and Marc Mission vs. Deadly Sins Sandman vs. Skyhawk HOUR #2: Jerome Justice vs. "Monster" Rich Clark Ghost Riders vs. CPE Enforcers CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: "Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia vs. Jason "Flash" Varga (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's it for today, but before we leave, let's hear some comments from the former CWO World Champion, Gold Kard. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Gold Kard is in the interview area.) GOLD KARD: For those of you who thought I went away, I say hah! While Johnny Reb and Joe Eagle wreck their "friendship" over the World belt, my schemes begin anew. My Gold Kard International still rules the CWO, even if we have lost some of our business acumen. Fergus McTavish, you Scottish dumbkopf, your days are numbered. You may think that we are in a weakened state and therefore vulnerable to your attacks, but you are wrong. When my hitman Painmaster takes you out, that will end your threat. And if by some small chance you manage to survive the onslaught of the Painmaster, then the rest of Gold Kard International will be there to attack the rest of your diseased carcass! Now, to address a certain individual and his psychotic freund, Holocaust and Red Raven. Holocaust, I don't know you, I don't want to know you, but what I know is that you and your friend Raven have been hampering my efforts to get back to the #1 contender status for the World Championship belt that belongs around my waist. Holocaust, you claim that I have betrayed me people. Hah! No one takes more pride in their German heritage than myself. So, at SEPTEMBER SLAM II: REVENGE IS THE NAME OF THE GAME, I shall have my revenge against you, Holocaust, and your career here in the CWO will be a very brief one, as I shall bring an apocalypse upon you, as befits your name. GOLD KARD INTERNATIONAL FOREVER! (Gold Kard laughs maniacally as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
