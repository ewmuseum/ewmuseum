CWO REPORT 12/15/96

15 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
_______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, December 15, 1996. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the superstars of the CWO were in Allendale, MI, at the Grand Valley State University Fieldhouse, putting on a great show for the great people of West Michigan. Let's look at how the card went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT SATURDAY, DECEMBER 14, 1996 ALLENDALE, MI (GRAND VALLEY STATE UNIVERSITY FIELDHOUSE) "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. Sandman Jr. "Lonestar" Mike Williams pinned Sandman Jr. in 13:58 after the latter missed with the Wild Thing splash. Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo Benjamin Blake Bentley and Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo wrestled to a double disqualification in 6:55. After the match, Bentley delivered his finisher, the Nobility. The Coldfront vs. Lafayette and Rooster Lafayette and Rooster defeated the Coldfront in 8:59 when Lafayette reversed a rollup by Steve "Mr. Niceguy" White. Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart Brent "The Ace" Alles defeated "Master of Pain" Barry Hart by countout in 5:23. Pit Bosses vs. Deadly Sins Pit Bosses defeated Deadly Sins by disqualification in 17:39 when Sloth hit Pit Boss #2 with a chair in the ring. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title) Gold Kard (champion) vs. Hile Troy Gold Kard pinned Hile Troy after Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash) in 9:28. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Master Of Darkness Damion vs Moses Moses pinned Master of Darkness Damion in 10:19 with the Commandment (springboard moonsault). _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight is of course CWO CELEBRATION, only on PPV, coming to you live from where it all began: Grand Rapids, MI, at the Van Andel Arena. Not only that, but we also have the SHOWCASE SPECIAL as well as the EYE ON THE STORM, lots of great CWO action. We now take you to comments from CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Miguel Sanchez is watching the CWO report, and sees the comments of Sin. While "Big" Rich Clark keeps watching, Miguel faces the camera.) MIGUEL SANCHEZ (to camera): So, Sin. You have to be a woman, and bring friends to ringside. Well, fine. You want FIENDS at ringside, then I'll bring out some of my best friends out. THE BIBLICAL POWERS WILL ACCOMPANY ME TO RINGSIDE. It doesn't matter to me, because I'll put you in that Fire Pit. There is no escape from me. I'm here to kick ass and chew gum... and I'm all outta gum. (They keep watching, and see Maximum Overdrive's comments.) "BIG" RICH CLARK (to camera): So, Maximum Overdrive, you think that I'd sell my family down the river for the CWO Title. Well, you've never been more wrong. If you ask where we were when Keuka Guy was getting his ass kicked ever night, well, where was he when Miguel was getting his ass kicked by you, Baby Waldo, the Mercenaries, and Sandman Jr. He was left out there, all by himself, taking the beating of a lifetime, and, the next week, he was back, beating people up once more. That's why he's the Innovator of Violence, and that's why he's the CWO champion. But, you want to see what I've could do to prove you wrong. Well, I'd like to introduce to you a man. In fact, THE man. The man who taught both Miguel and I how to wrestle. The man who will manage both Miguel and I whenever we wrestle from now on. My FATHER, "Beautiful" Brian Clark. (A man who resembles "Big" Rich, is about 6'3, 245, and around Ric Flair's age comes out.) "BEAUTIFUL" BRIAN: So, you dare to insult my son, and my family. Well, I know this much. HE is destined for greatness. I won over 35 titles in my wrestling career. Rich is only 25 years old. He has many years ahead of him. A title at this time in his career would just kick it off on the right foot. Don't wait for him, beacuse he's already watching, and waiting for you. MIGUEL SANCHEZ: See, we are prepared for ALL comers in the CWO. You want it, come and get it. Just remember, you want a match for the CWO T.V. title, you better think of a special stipulation for the match, because I don't wrestle unless I can make someone bleed. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Joe Eagle and the Big Cool are in the interview area.) EAGLE: Hello, CWO fans! The Celebration will soon be among us, and although I can't be there for the competition I would like to pass along my personal gratitude to the fans. I can't tell you how much I appreciate your welcoming us back from our exile of the last 6 months. JIM "JAZZMAN" TAYLOR: Same here, Joe, same here. The Big Cool can't wait to get started on the new phase of our lives. JUMBO JENKINS: The buffet spread is MUCH better in this locker room! JOE EAGLE: These guys will represent the New TOP well against the Mercenaries, and starting next week the whole TOP will be re-energized in our new quests: to gain not only the cheers of the fans, but the CWO championship belts! _______________________________________________________________________________ (WILDFIRE and AMBER are in the locker room after the GANGLORD interview.) WILDFIRE: You did cost me four matches, Ganglord, and yeah, you are right, I would only be getting one victory out of this but I DONT CARE! Just this one win over you will be enough! Like I have been telling you the past weeks, DON'T UNDERESTIMATE my power! But you did and now at the CWO CELEBRATION you will pay for it! AMBER: And as for you, T. A. S., if my man wins, then I want your @$$ in the ring for 5 minutes, (laughs) if you think you can handle a defenseless lady like myself! WILDFIRE and AMBER: What ya gonna do when the WILDFIRE runs WILD on you! (Just then the lights begin to flicker and then go out, all that is seen is a faint red light on a shadow of a mysterious figure.) MYSTERIOUS VOICE: GANGLORD, I AM A MAN FROM YOUR PAST AND I HAVE COME BACK TO GAIN MY REVENGE AND WHEN I DO YOU WILL NEVER BE THE SAME AGAIN! (The mysterious bell begin to toll again as the camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on MOD Damion and the rest of the Dark Side.) MOD DAMION: Johnny Reb, tonight at CWO Celebration you think that I will break my promise to you, that you will never see the glitter of gold around your waist. Johnny, no chance. I will take you deep within my DARKNESS and after all is said and done I will be standing tall, still the ruler of the CWO, still the World Champion. Johnny Reb, I know that you still see me in your dreams and in your nightmares. You still see my face laughing as you fall from the scaffold in our first big meeting and then every time you have your sights set on some title belt there I am, right in your face, and tonight, that's how it will be. I know you fear me, I can see it in your eyes. Reb, there is nothing you can do to stop me in my quest to rule supreme in the CWO. In other corners of the night my Creatures have another opportunity to prove to the non-believers just how powerful the Dark Side is and that they are the most fearsome tag team in this federation. NIGHT CRAWLER: Biblical Powers, it seems that you guys have gotten the attention of some of the people in the CWO. Well, let me tell you this: you have indeed grabbed the attention of the Dark Side, more specifically, the Master and Dark Raven. We have promised him personaly that we will beat the two of you and Moses, you specifically shall be punished. NIGHT SLINGER: Moses, you hold something that belongs to the Dark Side and Dark Raven. He is not happy but more importantly, the master is not happy, so we will be the first in line to beat you up for what you have done. Savior, don't feel left out, we have some big plans for you too. The two of you shall know the true power of the forces of the NIGHT and the Darkness that we call home. MOD DAMION: Yes Moses, you will pay, my Creatures of the Night shall beat you and Savior, and before the PPV I will beat you, hurt you and make sure that you are not 100% for the biggest match of your life to date. Hey, Rebel Yell, you and General Lee had better keep your noses out of my business, if not you have seen the results; all the Dark Side will be in the arena so at anytime I will bring them out to keep you two out of the way. It will be just you and me, Johnny, and I know you can't beat me, I know your weaknesses and I know your fears, these will be your undoing. ALL FOUR: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!! (Camera closes on the Dark Side as MOD Damion and the rest walk away. Dark Raven turns to face the camera runs towards it. All we hear is screams of fear and pain as the camera falls to the ground and the screen goes blank.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman is in the interview area.) SANDMAN: Bane, I've just been sitting back since we last meet, watching you make a fool of yourself all over the USA. Taking out workers, civilians, old ladies. Bane, you may be able to take out these mere mortals. Bane, when we step inside that cage tonight, you better take a good look at the cage, that will be your final resting place, tonight Bane I will take your mind, body and soul. Tonight I will finish what you began. You see, I am immortal. You thought you finished me in the hospitial pulling the plug, eh? You can not kill an immortal, and you are no immortal. You even made pre-arrangements for my funeral, read the name on the stone again, Bane. Bane, when I finish you off permanently tonight, and after paramedics take your lifeless carcass from the steel cage, your grave will be complete and I will have your soul. R. I. P., LIGHTS OUT! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today. Don't forget that this week TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER will be MONDAY NIGHT FEVER, as we move back one night for a couple of weeks for the holiday season. I'm Johnny Starr, we'll see you tonight at CELEBRATION!
