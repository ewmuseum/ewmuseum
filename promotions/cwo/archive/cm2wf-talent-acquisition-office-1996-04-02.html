CM2WF Talent Acquisition Office

2 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings. My name is [name withheld] and I am in charge of Talent
Acquisition for the CM2WF (Cyberspace Mark 2 Wrestling Federation), representing Mr. Brent G. Alles, Executive Director of CM2WF Operations.
Those of you who participated in the short life of the CWO know the already proud tradition of E-wrestling that Alles Enterprises promotes.
We have an emphasis on role-playing, and good feuds, angles, flashes, etc. are much appreciated. The league uses its own set of rules to determine the outcome of the matches. These rules are relatively simply, but allow for a greater depth in character than formerly using TNM did (no slam against the great simulator TNM, but Mr. Alles simply felt he needed something different).
Currently we have 13 handlers signed for the league, and we are looking for up to 7 more. Each handler may have 3 wrestlers in the league, two of which should compose a "regular" tag team.
The CM2WF's title situation looks like this: CM2WF World Title (currently held by Lord Foul the Despiser, who won a tournament for it) CM2WF North American Title (currently held by Sandman [non-ECW], who also won a tournament for it)
CM2WF Television Title ("lottery match" will be held on this week's Tuesday Night Fever to determine the champions)
CM2WF World Tag Team Titles (tournament currently in process) CM2WF North American Tag Team Titles (tournament currently in process) CM2WF 6-Man Tag Team Titles ("lottery match" will be held on this week's Tuesday Night Fever to determine the champions)
The CM2WF "television" schedule is as follows: x Tuesday Night Fever, our raw T-N-T explosive program held every week, involves all handlers.
x Slammers, a program run by our Assistant Directors, content is up to them x Showcase, a program that shows 6 matches taped at the 3 House Shows held every week x One PPV per month. Our next one is this Sunday, April 7, and is the CM2WF ULTIMATE ROYALE from the CM2WF Arena in Grand
Rapids, MI. The rules for the Ultimate Royale are as follows: Two rings are set up, side by side. Two men start in each ring, in an over-the-top battle royal situation. Every two minutes, another two men come down to the ring. A coin toss determines their location, both men could go into one ring or one could go in each. The match progresses until there is only one man left in each ring. Those two men then fight it out in a no-DQ, no-countout match. The winner receives: $1,000,000!
We hope to make the ROYALE a 60-man event, so please get your characters in soon. The rules for the CM2WF and a sample character sheet can be found at Mr. Alles' home page, at:
http://web.syr.edu/~bgalles/ where you then go into the CM2WF by hitting the link right next to a picture of Ric Flair and Bobby Heenan.
Thank you very much, and we hope to be doing business with you and your e-wrestlers very soon. Sincerely, [name withheld] CWO Talent Acquisition
