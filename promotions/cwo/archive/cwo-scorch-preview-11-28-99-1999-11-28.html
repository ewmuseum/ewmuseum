SCORCH Preview - 11/28/99

November 28, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One hour before the NOVEMBER PAIN '99 PPV gets underway, it's SCORCH! And there are a lot of questions to be answered before we get to the big event itself.
NECROMANCER, the member of the macabre MAGIC GROUP, sees action against "THE
MAIN EVENT" STEVE DAVIS. Davis at one time was apparently slated for greatness, but now has the opening matchup? Is there trouble between Davis and the front office?
Another mysterious individual, SYNE, will see action against GOLD KARD. With Millennium now "dead," Gold Kard has to go it alone against the enigmatic wrestler, although GK will have GERMAN CHOKLAT by his side. Will she tip the side in his favor?
The ROYAL COURT will be on SCORCH, as BRIAN "THE KING" DERKS, his QUEEN
MARLESA, and his "royal composer", AMADEUS, will see action against the unusual team-up of Elite member WILDFIRE and "THE JAW JACKING GIGOLO" ANDREW
DIXON (with the two estranged ALLESES, KENT and MARY). Did Kent make a deal with the "man with the money," Wildfire?
Finally, he's slated to be the special referee for the DIAMONDBACK CHRIS
MYERS vs. GECKO match at the PPV, but LORD HILE TROY will also have to see wrestling action tonight, taking on another Elite member, JESSE EWIAK.
Ewiak claims he is the "legend eliminator," will he try to issue a warning to Troy on Elite leader Gecko's behalf? We'll see.
All that and more as we countdown the minutes to another great CWO PPV,
NOVEMBER PAIN '99, but as always, it's a hot Sunday night on SCORCH!
