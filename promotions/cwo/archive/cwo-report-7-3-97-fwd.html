CWO REPORT 7/3/97 (fwd)

3 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey, standing in front of the empty ring at the arena that FEVER was taped at last night.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. CWO's FEVER took place last night in Pittsburgh, and I can tell you that things are ready to explode. With only four days left until the Rocking Independence Day PPV, last night's show only escalated tensions that could have serious repercussions in the future. Let's look at a rundown at the results of last night's card. CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER PITTSBURGH, PA 7/3/97 Show opens with interview with Johnny Reb. Reb talks about his upcoming match with Lord Hile Troy at the PPV. "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia appeared to talk about his upcoming match with former mentor El Grande Pistollerro at the PPV as well as his future plans in the CWO. Johnny Reb pinned "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia with a DDT in 11:11. During the match, Lord Hile Troy and Benjamin Blake Bentley came down to ringside and attacked Reb once the match was over. Diamondback Chris Myers came to the ring to help, but it was the appearance of "Monster" Rich Clark that finally drove away Troy and Bentley. Interview with the Highlanders. Attitude Dance defeated the former CWO World Tag Team Champions, the Highlanders, in 14:55 after Eagle pinned Gavin following the Flash Flood. The Focus defeated Marc Mission in 11:40 with the Focal Point. "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat made his debut. Panther got the win over Wrath by DQ in 14:43. Outside interferance and rulebreaking were constant in this match, with the bell finally being run with the run in of the Deadly Sins. Brother Justice appeared to make the save, and it appeared that Panther may have injured his ankle. Interview with GKI. Gold Kard talked about the upcoming match for the CWO World 6 Man Tag Team Titles. Both Vegas High Roller and Matsuhiro Torunaka expressed concern about tagging with Brent "The Ace" Alles because of his condition over the last few weeks. Kard assured them that everything would be fine. The Mercenaries won by DQ over Gold Kard and Matsuhiro Torunaka in 12:33 when Vegas High Roller hit Korperal Punishment with a metal breifcase as the Mercenaries were making a pin attempt on Gold Kard following the Mercenaries Flying Clothesline. Prior to the match, Gold Kard stated that GKI were the only reason why the Mercenaries had been able to keep the belts as long as they had. The Mercenaries took offense to this, bashing both Kard and Torunaka with the belts to start the match. After the match, the Regulators ran down to ringside, executed the Total Anihilation on Kaptain Krunch and were able to take their North American Tag Team Titles back. Diamondback Chris Myers defeated Benjamin Blake Bentley to become NEW CWO TV Champion in 12:39 following the Rattler. Gold Kard tried to break up the pin, but was held back by Myers' manager, Dallas. Kard slapped Dallas after the match, and went on to brutally attack Myers. Kard demanded that referee John Myering reverse the decision. Myering refused, and was fired by Gold Kard. FEVER closed with interview from CWO World Champion Joe Eagle, talking about his upcoming World Title defense against Gold Kard. WASHEY: Some stunning developments last night. We go now to our interviews and will start things off with two interviews made by Benjamin Blake Bentley, first BEFORE FEVER took place, and AFTER FEVER. _____________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens with Triple B standing in the CWO studios, TV title draped over his shoulder, and with a smug expression on his face.) BBB: So, Panther is it? Looks like I have a 'match' against you at R.I.D. (laughs) I just have one thing to say to the CWO booking committee. If you keep with your current ability in finding proper opponents for me, prepare to pack up your belongings in a box. I have yet to meet one man in the CWO that has beaten me soundly. So, what does someone in your position, and of your mentality do? Set up a round featuring me, the epitome of athleticism in the CWO, with a mid-carder named after an animal I have a rug of in my den. How appropriate. Panther. This is your big, first, and last chance at the title, and after I procure the Nobility, I will walk all over your limp body like the mat decoration it is. (Scene fades with Triple B curtsying to the camera, as his theme music is heard in the background.) _____________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens up in the CWO locker rooms just after Fever. GoldKard is seen exiting the locker room smiling. Bentley looks furious due to his loss to Chris Meyers.) BBB: "Meyers...........don't let this win get to your head. Tonight there were several factors to my loss, and none of them were your talent. First off, I have a horrendous stomach flu. Most likely from the horrendous food the current CWO producers provide as a 'courtesy'. Second, I had to watch my back. There are plenty of unworthy people besides yourself that are looking to drive a knife in my back simply because I've upstaged them in every way possible. Third. That kissing cousin of yours was making moves at me all night. I can not be expected to perform at a normal level with some toothless mongrel coming onto me.......Meyers....I'll say this once......our rematch....my second coming....will be a turning point in your career. Expect to lose, and lose badly. For the future of the CWO is not some hayseed from the Arizona desert that spent his childhood eating strange desert mushrooms and chasing vultures....the future is GKI. See you then, and make sure to bring the belt. (With this Bentley smiles sinisterly and the scene fades to black.) ______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: CWO REPORT has not been notified to whether or not matches will be changed as a result of Myers' win last night, but as far as we here at the REPORT know, the matches will stay the same as scheduled. Next up, we have comments from both Fergus McTavish and Bloodbath, who will be meeting each other for the Buried Alive match at the PPV. We will start with McTavish and then go to Bloodbath's comments. ______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on the Camera-Guy walking down a hallway towards the CWO dressing rooms. He stops at a tartan painted door. Loud laughter can be heard coming from behind it. The Camera-Guy knocks 3 times and eventually the door is answered by Billy, the young friend of Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil.) Billy: What do you want? (Billy only has the door open a little bit. In the background is Fergus sitting on a leather couch watching a big screen TV.) Camera-Guy: I'd like to do a quick interview with you and Fergus if you don't mind. Billy: Awwww, we're watching 'The TICK'. Fergus is watching TV so I'll do it, but make it quick. (Fergus can be heard laughing in the background as The TICK yells, 'Spoooon!!!') Camera-Guy: Ok. Any comments you'd like to make about Fergus' BURIED ALIVE match with Bloodbath? Billy: Yeah. But first we'd like to issue an apology to The Highlanders. Guys, I know you were trying to help us out and Fergus starts fighting with you both. But after receiving two Nobilities and a Tombstone Powerbomb, Fergus wasn't all there. Not that he is all there at the best of times but you know what I mean. Camera-Guy: Yeah, I think I do. Billy: So Gavin and Connor, no hard feelings, ok? Fergus:(yells out to Billy) Hurry up boyo! Yer missin' da best part! Billy: (yells back) Ok, I've just got a couple more things to say! (Turns back to the Camera-Guy) Billy: Now, about Bloodbath. He wants a BURIED ALIVE match? No fair! That's his specialty match! We demand a 'Scottish' touch to it! Bloodbath, if you win, you get to bury Fergus in dirt. But if Fergus wins, he gets to bury you in..... (Billy grins his trademark naughty gin) Billy: OATMEAL!!!! Camera-Guy: Wow! That's some touch! And what about Bloodbath saying he was coming after you next? Billy: Hey, I'm not scared of him. He's running around, pretending he's dead, trying to scare people, but not me! Camera-Guy: You're not scared of Bloodbath? Billy: Nope. And you know why? 'Cause I'm friends with the craziest wrestler in the CWO. Compared to Fergus, Bloodbath is Rated 'G'. Camera-Guy: Good point. (whispers) Actually, Fergus scares the hell out of me! Billy: Yeah, tell me about it. (Billy turns to leave.) Oh, I almost forgot! Benjamin Blake Bentley, you embarassed me on national television! Don't think I'll forget what you did to me! Spank my ass will you! I'll get you, just you watch! So BBB, get yourself ready for Billy, friend of The Whirling Scottish Devil! (Billy turns his back to the camera and drops his jeans, mooning the Camera-Guy) Billy: (over his shoulder) Get ready for Billy! ITS NOT OATMEAL!!!! (In the background, Fergus falls off the couch and is rolling on the floor, laughing hysterically) (camera fade, much laughing and hooting can still be heard) ______________________________________________________________________________ (There is a cold dark cemetary, in it, a lone dug grave, with a grave stone that reads Fergus McTavish, and out of the shadows steps the 7' 4" Bloodbath) BLOODBATH (cold, dark & mysterious):The time for talking is done. Fergus McTavish, you have no idea what you've gotten yourself into. Remember the last time you tried bury me, what happened? Yes you did beat me..... (Bloodbaths eyes roll back into his head) BLOODBATH (cold, dark & mysterious):YOU DID NOT DESTROY ME!!! And at CWO Rockin Independence Day I am gonna bury you alive!!! Fergus, REST IN PEACE!! (Bloodbath drops into the open grave, it explodes into blood red smoke and Bloodbath disappears as the camera fades out) ______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: We now go to comments from X-Factor and Marc Mission, who will be meeting the New Men this weekend. ______________________________________________________________________________ (The camera fades in on two guys playing tennis. As it gets closer, they are revealed to be X-Factor and Marc Mission.) X-Factor: (He dives for a ball out of his reach and grunts and falls hard on the concrete) Oh SH*T, oh, whoops, sorry. (Speaking to Marc) Love-30. Marc Mission: (Surprised)WHAT!! You cheat, it's only Love-15!! X-Factor: Whatever! (Looks into the camera) Ain't nothing like a good game of tennis to get out frustrations. (Stops) Don't get me wrong, we are not frustrated. Why should we be, we are the future of CWO, we are the "new men". Marc Mission: (In the background) YEAH!! NEW MEN!! X-Factor: (shakes his head) At ROCKIN INDEPENDENCE DAY, (Marc interupts) Marc : Come on, serve the damn thing, will ya? X: (Points his racket at Marc) Shut up, or I come over there and kick you a$$. Anyways, at ROCKIN INDEPENDENCE DAY, we move one more step up that ladder, when we walk all over those New Men. (He serves the ball to Marc) Marc: (swings at the ball, but missed) OH Great! X: (jumps up) ACE!! LOVE-40!! Marc: (throws his racquet down): LOVE-30!! Get it right, dammit. X: (laughs hysterically) Who gives a rat's warted a$$? All that I care about is getting in that ring, manhandling those two little girls, and walking out the victors, and moving one more step in that long and winding road that is called the CWO World Tag Team Championship. Marc: (runs over when he hears that. Out of breath and wipes the sweat off his face with a towel.) Did you say Tag Team Championship. I want a belt. Can we have one. (he starts to get excited and jumps around) I get a belt, I get a belt! X: (slaps him on the head) You idiot. (continues to laugh) No, we don't get one, but hey, MARC!!! (marc looks around at X) I promise you this, it won't be long. Marc: (Marc shakes his hips) You promise? X: (Looks in the camera and sticks his tongue up his nose) Marc, would I lie to you? (He snickers) GAME, SET, MATCH point. Marc: (shocked look) What? Already? Wait, you lie!!! ______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up we have an interview with "Lone Wolf" Ryan Duetscher. Let's go to his comments. ______________________________________________________________________________ ("Lone Wolf" is in an old run down appartment in the clums of Chicago Il) DEUTSCHER:You know, since I've come to the CWO I have notices one thing. I have be come soft. I have become weak!! Well NO MORE!!!! I've returned to the bad streets of Chicago to prepare for CWO Rockin Independence Day. Someone asked me if I was looking past Matthew "The Man" White. All I see is another @$$ that I have to kick to get to the CWO World Title!! And for Diamondback Chris Meyers, yeah you did beat me once but that is something you will never do again!! You come on tv with your training videos, saying that you are the best, well you make me sick!! And now after the PPV, Im gonna whip your @$$ too!!! And as for the fans of the CWO, either your with me or you can all kiss my @$$!!! (the camera fades out) ______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: We have a surprise for you, and that's comments from the man who made an appearance at the last PPV. ______________________________________________________________________________ (Ganglord in some type of locker room area) GANGLORD:Ladies and gentleman, we pulled off the biggest suprise of the century. Now that I am a part of the wildest stable, The Wild Things, I'm ready to help take on Gold Kard and the rest of the morons that he has employed. Now I know Im not on the card at CWO Rockin Independence Day but you can bet.... (Ganglord gets a smirk on his face) GANGLORD:You can rest assured that I will make my presence known in some way!! (the camers fades out) ______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up, we have one of the tag teams that will be involved in the CWO North American Tag Team Title match at the PPV. Here are the Mercenaries. ______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who are standing in the remains of what was once their locker rooms. Both men are bloodied, Krunch has several brusies and Punishment has a bandage on his head.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: We have two things we want to discuss. First, Gold Kard. Buddy, if you thought that the Mercenaries were going to just hand those belts over to you, you were sorely mistaken. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: The two men you see standing before you are the reason we had those titles, not that group of goofs and misfits that go under the name of GKI. You made the biggest mistake of your entire careers. To hell with all these other teams and stables in the CWO trying to take you down. The Mercenaries can do it all by ourselves, and we're going to start with Vegas High Roller, Matsuhiro TorunKAKA and the man that leads the pack of rats, Gold Kard. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Now to the Regulators, who proved themselves to be more than nothing but a couple of cheapshot thieves last night. Girls, you may think you proved something by stealing our titles but the fact of the matter is at the PPV, you won't be fighting just one guy. You will be fighting a team that has beaten you more times than we can count, and we will win those titles outright in a matter of days. We've been in the ring with you before, and we know you don't have what it takes to beat us. NO team in this federation does. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Just remember that when the bell rings, the titles are handed over to us, and the two of you are staring up counting the lights. We are THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD, and we're coming straight at the two of you losers! ______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Finally today, we have comments from a new tag team that's set to make their debut in the CWO. Let's go to comments from the Dragon Express. ______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens up with a panning view of a scenic Japanese sunset against the Pacific Ocean. All that's heard is the sound of breakers against the jagged rocks, and a periodic scream) VOICE: Japan. Land of the Rising Sun...home to macro economics, countless martial arts, and even more numerous success stories......prepare to witness one for yourself. (Scene cuts to 4 men fighting on a beach. The two that are winning obviously outclass their opponents in every imaginable way. The first is a small man and is working over his opponent with fast, stabbing chops, kicks, and the occasional aerial kick. The 2nd is a huge man. Well over 400 pounds, 6ft, and demolishing all opposition with crushing power moves, and the occasional charging move. The camera then cuts to the two men standing in the CWO studios. The smaller one steps forward and speaks.) Ukyo Yamigawa: So CWO. Ah you prepahed foh the Asian Expieahnce?! Ah you prepahed to be shown just how horrible your atheletes really ah?! Ah you prepahed foh the Dragon Express!? Togethah, I Ukyo Yamigawa and the man you may call the Great Fuumi (big man nods to the camera) will completely destroy all yoh hopes, dreams, and will set the CWO tag scene on it's eayah! (Ukyo snaps to a stance, ready to attack, while the Great Fuumi uncrosses his arms and bows to the camera.) ______________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Well, that's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody on the next REPORT! (fade to closing credits)
