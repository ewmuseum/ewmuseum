CWO REPORT 12/12/96

12 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
_______________________________________________________________________________ (The CNN news logo flashes before the screen. The music plays and Wolf Blitzer appears on the screen.) BLITZER (professonal voice): Good Morning, I'm Wolf Blitzer reporting live from Youngstown, Ohio. In the wake of those brutal attacks by what police suspect to have been commited from a professional wrestler known only as Bane, it would seem that the infamous masked man has disappeared from the earth. He has so far been able to elude all police efforts to bring him in. Cohorts of his known as the Apocalypse were brought in for questioning, but their information was useless. They have been released without any further involvement. As it would have seemed that this villain known as Bane had dropped off the face of the earth, and was never to be seen again until early this morning in this town in Ohio. It would appear that a highway officer pulled over a blue Ford Thunderbird that was speeding early in the morning on some rural highway. There were two occupants in the vehicle, a large man and a woman. When the officer approached the car he was assualted by the man and beat severly. The officer was beat mercilessly, and is in a state of unconsciousness at the local hospital. Evidence found at the crime scene was a mask that resembles the one that Bane wears to the ring. Police speculate that it was pulled off in the scuffle. Also, there was a lipstick inprint on the officer's cheek which may suggest that the woman was also involved. This woman may be the one that was kidnapped, and has not been found as of yet. The woman who was kidnapped was also part of the same wrestling league as Bane, the Cyberspace Wrestling Organization, or CWO, and was known as Lust. The two suspects drove off but shortly abandoned their vehicle, and their whereabouts are currently unknown. Federal investigators are doing the best they can to stop this man before any further violence can be commited. This is Wolf Blitzer reporting live for CNN News. We will keep you posted of any further breakthroughs in the "Trail of Terror." _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, December 12, 1996. I'm Johnny Starr. As you can see, the Bane situation escalates, but still, CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson has refused to cancel the now "unsanctioned" Locked Steel Cage match between Bane and Sandman at CWO CELEBRATION. Many have questioned his motives, Johnson is under a lot of fire, but the match still remains, if Bane is indeed able to show up. Turning to other matters, last night, the superstars of the CWO were in , let's look at how that card went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 11, 1996 FORT WAYNE, IN The Biblical Powers vs. Flame Spirits The Biblical Powers and the Flame Spirits wrestled to a double disqualification in 14:59. Cake Boy vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart "Master of Pain" Barry Hart won by countout when Cake Boy fled back to the dressing room in 16:08. Black Death vs. Exile Black Death pinned Exile after a leg drop in 7:51. Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Dark Raven Brian "The Hammer" Williams pinned Dark Raven in 14:04 after the Keuka Smack (boxes opponent's ears, then an uppercut). After the match, Williams was attacked by the Dark Side, but Johnny Reb, Rebel Yell, Wayne Schillo, and Beauregard Dixon arrived to drive them off. Lafayette vs. Hile Troy Hile Troy defeated Lafayette by countout in 13:03. CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE (SPIN THE WHEEL, STRAP MATCH): Miguel Sanchez (TV champ) vs. Gold Kard (NA champ) Gold Kard defeated Miguel Sanchez in 12:30 after Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash), dragging him around and touching all four corners. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (FOUR CORNERS OF DEATH): "Big" Rich Clark vs. Master Of Darkness Damion (champion) Master of Darkness Damion defeated "Big" Rich Clark in 17:47, throwing him into all four pits filled with illegal objects. After the match, Master of Darkness Damion refused to let up, prompting Johnny Reb to make the save. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in Cincinnati, OH, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW THURSDAY, DECEMBER 12, 1996 CINCINNATI, OH Johnny Reb vs. Talon Beauregard Dixon vs. Hellhound Deadly Sins vs. Blood Inc. Sandman Jr. vs. Dark Raven LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wildfire vs. Moses (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (STREET FIGHT): The Bad Guys vs. The Pit Bosses (champions) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. MOD Damion (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now go to comments from CWO superstars. As you just saw, one of the big matches in Cincinnati tonight will between the now "reformed" Sandman Jr. and the two-time former light heavyweight champion, Dark Raven. Let's get comments from Sandman Jr. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area with Kellen.) SANDMAN JR.: Dark Raven, I've been watching the stuff you've been pulling the last few months... but how did it feel, losing to Moses? And how's it GOING to feel facing SANDMAN JR?!? PAINFUL! And "Lonestar" Williams, I have nothing against you... you're just the right guy at the wrong time.... we'll have a good match, but I will win it, guaranteed. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Sandman Jr.'s former associates, the Deadly Sins, currently without their manager Lust because of the Bane situation, also had comments for us. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: ARRRRRRRRGH! I'm still pissed off! Blood Inc, you haven't won a match yet... that trend is going to continue, cause we're gonna annihilate you.... SLOTH: Then, Creatures of the Night... we've beaten you before in a non-title match... we'll do it again if we have to... and it appears we do... they should be our belts, but we'll take, heh heh, PRIDE, in knowing we stomped the champs AGAIN. PRIDE: Then we get our preview match with the Bosses. The powers that be in the CWO want to see us win those belts on a PPV, so they won't give us a title shot a day early... understandable... just remember what we do to you Saturday won't be a TENTH of how we're gonna kill you on Sunday! SLOTH: And then, WE WANT THE APOCALYPSE! You have our manager, we want her back, and we'll literally KILL YOU if we don't get her back NOW! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: One of the big matches at CWO CELEBRATION will be a scaffold match between newcomers Wildfire and Ganglord. These two men have developed an intense hatred for each other in a relatively short time. We go now to comments from Wildfire. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire is alone in the interview area watching a Ganglord interview.) WILDFIRE: You know, T. A. S., you are wrong. I didn't lose another match, I was cost another match. And as for you, Ganglord, I'm not an angry man, I simply said that you shouldn't underestimate me! But you did, and now you're gonna pay for it! (Just then Miss Amber bursts through the door and hands Wildfire an envelope. Wildfire reads the contents of the envelope and begins to smile!) WILDFIRE: And as for watching my back, well maybe you should watch your back! You see, 'cause I have met someone from your past... your dark past.... (The camera fades out as four mysterious bell begin to toll.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Currently the CWO Television champion, Miguel Sanchez has a lot on his mind these days, including a showdown with his arch enemy, Maximum Overdrive, at CWO CELEBRATION, as well as trouble with the mysterious Sin. We now take you to comments from Miguel, and his "little" buddy, 7'4" "Big" Rich Clark. They are joined by some very special guests. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Miguel Sanchez is relaxing with his friends Tommy Dreamer, "Big" Rich Clark, and The Gangstas. He is discussing the "Mass Transit Incident" with New Jack. He looks up, and seeing that camera, stops talking to New Jack.) MIGUEL SANCHEZ (to camera): So, Sin. You think that you've got me right where you want me, don't you? You got a Television Title shot, and were soundly defeated by my superior skill. Face it, the fans are on MY side, and with their support, I will never lose. You think that by burning me, you'll get rid of me. Never in my life have I ever heard something so wrong. I'm the one who suggested a "FOUR CORNERS OF DEATH MATCH" to Sandman Jr., and he turned it down. Now him and I are allies, but we had one of the greatest matches in CWO history. I have something to tell you, Sin. I am the "King of Hardcore", and my friend Tommy Dreamer has decided to let me take his title for use in the CWO. From now on, I shall be referred to as "The Innovator of Violence," Miguel Sanchez! I challenge you to a FIRE PIT MATCH at the next CWO Fever open spot. There will be a flaming pit at ringside, and to win, you must put your opponent into the pit. Let's see how you take that. "BIG" RICH CLARK: Hey, MOD. I beat you last week, but you still have the belt. Miguel has beaten you twice, and I have once. I will wear the CWO Title around my waist, and I can just taste the victory. I am the best thing going in the CWO. I have lost 2 matches since I came here, and there isn't anyone whom I fear. When I get that belt, and I will, I will bring all of the dignity that the belt deserves. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, you were an honorable champion, but now you have associated yourself with those LOSERS, Doomsday. Baby Waldo, you're an idiot, Maximum Overdrive, a loser, The Mercenaries, bigger losers. You are nothing, and you will never be anything. I'm getting that belt as soon as possible, and I'll NEVER lose it because I'm the biggest man in the world. (The camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As we saw in the HOUSE SHOW REPORT, Master of Darkness Damion was victorious in the Four Corners of Death match... just barely. You can expect "Big" Rich Clark to remain a major obstacle for his title reign in the future. We take you now to comments from the CWO World Champion, Master of Darkness Damion. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on MOD Damion who is watching a tape of the last TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER.) MASTER OF DARKNESS DAMION: You see there, Gold Kard, you tell me not to get in your way. Well, that is fine, as long as you stay out of the Dark Side's way in return. I have vowed that Johnny Reb shall never know what it is like to hold any gold in the CWO, and I will do anything to keep that trash from winning even if it means going through you. I tell you: stay out of my way too. As for you, Johnny Reb, if you did not have those so-called friends of yours come in and help you, my friend, you would not have made it to Sunday and the CWO would have never seen you again. Count yourself lucky for now! Johnny Reb, I have looked into your eyes before and what I saw was a scared shell of a man. Let's look at your past: I have thrown you off the scaffold, I have pinned you for the three count many times and most recently my pet Hellhound dragged you around the ring on the end of a leather strap and beat you. Come Sunday you will walk into my NIGHT one last time and as you look into MY EYES you will not see fear but what you will see is your maker, your MASTER, and your end. For within my eyes you will see the fires of hell burning and within the flame is the remains of your limp burning carcass. As for your friends, they like the others, will be dealt with soon enough. Mercenaries, you know the wrath of the Dark Side, as do you, Schillo, but you, Brian Williams, you have yet to enter my "Forever Night," I promise you, it's as bad as they all saw and worse. Now to more pressing present matters. "BIG" Rich Clark, you think that you are so great. You think you should be champ and now you want to prove it. You ask ME to a four corners of death match. Boy, are you stupid. I have died many times only to return bigger and stronger than before. You, however, shall die only once and be gone forever. I may just seal your fate tonight and eradicate you from the face of my Earth forever. I will show you not only the four corners of death but also the FACE OF DEATH: mine. I know you sweat as you approach the ring knowing that in a few short minutes I your master will be coming out to deliver a severe beating to you. Clark, I hope you last a while as I do love the match of death, in fact, I LIVE for it. I will see you in my world tonight boy! LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today. We'll see you tomorrow!
