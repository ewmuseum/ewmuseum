CWO Update — FEVER Review/Preview (3/11/06)

Mar 11, 2006, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Kyle Melinn and David "The Truth" Thomas are in the CWO studio.) KYLE MELINN: Hi fans, Kyle Melinn and David "The Truth" Thomas here with your CWO UPDATE for 3/11/06 - the FEVER REVIEW/PREVIEW! Last week on FEVER, the MADNESS 2006 Tournament began... and the FEVER division had some surprising results. DAVID THOMAS: Especially surprising was Brent Alles' upset of CWO World Champion Nightrain... Nightrain was the #1 seed... Brent Alles the #32... but with the help of DMT, Brent Alles was able to advance in the tournament. KYLE MELINN: Let's take a look at last week's FEVER! (A montage of images is shown, revealing the results as shown below.) FEVER 3/11/06 * An injured Vincent Frost announced that he was taking a leave from the CWO. * Doom announced his presence in the CWO. * Dimension X (DMT, Blade Batiste, and Jen Styles) cut a promo. * Wulf Erikssen and Dagenham Dave cut a promo discussing their status as "Enemies of the State" (as declared by President Foley). * The new World Tag Team Champions the Falconis then made an appearance... calling the Army of Darkness out... The Army of Darkness then came from the crowd and proceeded to do a beatdown on the Falconis. * Brent Alles is shown backstage being confronted by President Foley. Foley tells him that he made him the #32 seed on purpose, to humiliate him. Nightrain then appeared and Foley told Brent that the World Champion was in the tournament to win $1,000,000 (instead of the CWO World Championship title shot at CWO FOREVER). Foley then offered Nightrain $25,000 to knock Brent out of the tournament tonight... $50,000 to knock him completely out of the sport with an injury! Nightrain slammed Foley against the wall, not happy with that offer, but he then told Brent he would take Brent out of the tournament for letting him down over the whole OWC debacle. * Brent "The Ace" Alles defeated Nightrain to advance in the tournament after outside interference by DMT. Brent made Nightrain pass out to the ACS after the attack by DMT. * Backstage, Foley was not happy with DMT's interference giving Brent a win, and was ready to fire DMT (since he said anyone who helped the "Enemies of the State" - Brent, Wulf, and Dagenham - would be fired) but DMT claimed he wasn't helping Brent... he was helping HIMSELF by getting Nightrain's attention (and presumably a title shot down the line). Foley seemed OK with that, although it still seemed he might have plans for DMT... * FEVER Champion "Fallen Angel" Brad Wheatley cut a promo backstage, claiming he would beat Vampire Hunter later that night and prove that HE was the Future of the CWO, not Vampire Hunter. * Xavier Falconi was shown being treated by medical crews, his future tournament match that night definitely in question! * Doom defeated Gold Kard to advance in the tournament, pinning him after the Doomstone (Tombstone). * Backstage, Gold Kard gave credit to Doom for defeating him, and then announced his retirement from in-ring action... as he is the new FEVER Vice President! Gold Kard warned President Foley that it was Gold Kard in charge of FEVER, not Foley, and that 5 of the 9 CWO Board of Directors support Gold Kard... not Foley. Foley seemed to think he could change that... * Ryan Justice cut a promo backstage, referencing past matches with Lord Hile Troy (apparently outside the CWO). * Ryan Justice defeated Lord Hile Troy to advance in the tournament. * Backstage, the Hell Risers, allies of the Army of Darkness, were shown laid out... the perpetrator was unknown, but a crew member thought he saw Xavier Falconi leaving the scene after it went down... * DMT defeated BT Savage to advance in the tournament, pinning him after the YJ Stinger (suplex into a powerslam). * A1 Skyraider proved he just might be "The Future" of the CWO as he defeated Hall of Famer Sandman Jr. to advance in the tournament. * In a battle of former tag team champions (and once again current tag team partners and The Future members), Adam Smoke defeated Tyler Bomb to advance in the tournament. * Brad Wheatley defeated Vampire Hunter to advance in the tournament. KYLE MELINN: As you can see, a lot of exciting action... and President Chris Foley is definitely not making a lot of friends these days! DAVID THOMAS: No, but a lot of CWO wrestlers aren't making friends with him either... and as a result of Nightrain's actions, Foley has booked him in a match on EXTREME where he'll defend his World title against four randomly chosen men from those eliminated in the first round of the tournament. Foley is calling it his "Second Chance at Glory" match... seems generous to me! KYLE MELINN: A lot of intrigue going on in the CWO these days, but all eyes will be on the second round of the MADNESS tournament this week... we will of course focus on the FEVER Division match-ups... but you can check out CWO Online (http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling2/cwo2k) to see the updated brackets and the matches that will take place on our sister show EXTREME as well! DAVID THOMAS: Let's take a look at this week's matches on FEVER! FEVER 3/11/06 DARK MATCH (nontelevised): Flame vs. Dagenham Dave 1. MADNESS TOURNAMENT (round 2): Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Surge 2. AMAZONS TAG MATCH: Bobbi Jo & April Raines vs AMAZONS Champion Amy Savage & Tigerlilly 3. MADNESS TOURNAMENT (round 2): Doom vs Ryan Justice 4. MADNESS TOURNAMENT (round 2): DMT vs A1 Skyraider 5. Tyler Bomb & Vampire Hunter vs Japanese Connection 6. MADNESS TOURNAMENT (round 2): Adam Smoke vs. FEVER Champion "The Fallen Angel" Brad Wheatley KYLE MELINN: Some great match-ups there, but of course one of the most intriguing ones is Brent Alles vs. Surge... those two are long-time rivals... and now add in Brent Alles being an "Enemy of the State" and having a $25,000 to $50,000 bounty on his head... it will be interesting to see how Surge reacts to that! DAVID THOMAS: I can't wait to see the AMAZONS Tag Match! I only wish they were doing it in lingerie! KYLE MELINN: Oh stop! Also, Doom vs. Ryan Justice in a battle of two newcomers... DMT vs. A1 Skyraider... DAVID THOMAS: It will be interesting to see if there will be any repercussions from DMT's "disagreement" with President Foley last week! KYLE MELINN: The Future members Vampire Hunter and Tyler Bomb take on the recently returned Japanese Connection... DAVID THOMAS: And another member of The Future, Adam Smoke, will take on Brad Wheatley, the FEVER Champion, who remains in the tournament and therefore does not have to defend his FEVER Championship AGAIN this week! Lucky guy! KYLE MELINN: All that and more as FEVER comes to you from Yokohama, Japan... don't miss it! http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling2/cwo2k
