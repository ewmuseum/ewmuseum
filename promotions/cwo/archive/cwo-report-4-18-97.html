CWO REPORT 4/18/97

18 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on the members of DOOMSDAY. The Mercenaries flank Maximum Overdrive, who stand in front of Baby Waldo.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Hey, Regulators. We saw your interview yesterday, but you never mentioned our names. Could it be that we beat the fact into your stupid heads that you are a team that can't beat us? I mean, last time we heard from you, you were telling the whole federation that you were going to regulate the Mercenaries or something. You two loudmouth punks couldn't get the job done, just like we told you. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Enough about those losers. This Friday, we take on the Bad Girls yet again in a non-title match. I guess they knew how lucky they got last time we met and decided not to risk their titles this time around. But this time's going to be different, because the Mercenaries are ready for that illegal finisher that you girls have. MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: On Fever, Waldo and myself lost to a team called Wild Side. Wild Side, you may have won the match, but it doesn't change the fact that you are a couple of friggin' morons. But we have matters more important to attend to than you two nothings. Alles, you just don't know to leave well enough alone, do you? Your interference in my match last night cost me a sure win. I'm going to take great pleasure in beating you to a pulp tonight. You think you are something special, some kind of 'rebel'. You took that chick and had to brainwash her because no woman in their right mind would want to be with you in the first place. I'm going to destroy you tonight, just like I've done in the past, and there isn't a damn thing you can do about it, punk! KAPTAIN KRUNCH: And that goes for ANYBODY that tries to stop DOOMSDAY from getting what we want in this league. Wild Bunch, Regulators, Deadly Sins, Unholy Alliance. You all are marked and we can't wait to start carting all of you out on stretchers. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, April 18, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, continuing our short tour of the Great White North, let's see what went down! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/17/97 Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada The Big Cool defeated Creatures of the Night when Jumbo Jenkins pinned Night Slinger after the Star Flash (backbreaker by Taylor, flying splash by Jumbo). Time of match: 5:30. Chris Greco/Tommy Lightning defeated Wild Ones when Chris Greco pinned Mondo Gecko after the Flying Bodypress. Time of match: 15:42. Maximum Overdrive pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Pileup (power bomb). Time of match: 19:40. Mercenaries defeated Vlad the Impaler/Serpentmaster when Kaptain Krunch pinned Vlad the Impaler after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 3:21. Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned 'Master of Darkness' Damion after the Nobility (inverted piledriver) and outside interference by Hellhound. Time of match: 6:47. Deadly Sins defeated Regulators when Sloth pinned Regulator Ice after the Repention (superplex by Pride, Slothsault by Sloth). Time of match: 7:54. Joe Eagle pinned Johnny Reb after the Eagle Shock (modified tiger driver). Time of match: 11:54. STRAP MATCH: Bane won the strap match against North American champion Goliath, dragging him around and touching all four turnbuckles. (This was a non-title affair.) Time of match: 33:32. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Alaska, let's see the lineup for tonight's card! _______________________________________________________________________________ FRIDAY, APRIL 18, 1997 JUNEAU, AK Mondo Gecko vs. Brent 'The Ace' Alles Brian 'The Hammer' Williams vs. Gold Kard Panther vs. Pride Steel vs. Baby Waldo Brother Justice vs. Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Inferno vs. Masher Maccabee (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Bad Guys (champions) vs. Mercenaries WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Tiger Rogers/Goliath vs. Pit Bosses (champions) Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo vs. 'Monster' Rich Clark _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars. A new superstar is set to arrive here in the CWO, and he is Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera shot opens on a shipyard. Forklifts are moving large crates around and loud foghorn sounds can be heard in the background. The camera pans down to a large, red-headed Scotsman using a telephone in a phone booth. Decked out in a kilt and tartan beret, he appears to be involved in a heated debate. His speech is mired with a thick Scottish accent.) FERGUS MCTAVISH, THE WHIRLING SCOTTISH DEVIL: Speak up! I canna hear ya! (Fergus is yelling into the phone, a finger jammed into his ear to block out the foghorn.) FMTWSD: Aye, I gots here okay! I tells ya, that boat ride was somethin'! I gots ta sleep on dis nice wooden crate! Felt just like home! Course dere was none a yer haggis ta eat but I mades out okay! (Two tough looking dockworkers spy Fergus talking and start to walk towards him.) FMTWSD: Ma, stop buggin' me! I tolds ya I'm lookin' forward to my trip ta CWO! (Listening to the phone, Fergus does not notice the dockworkers approach him.) FMTWSD: What will I find dere? Och, who knows? Probably a whole bunch of lassies still suckling their mudder's teat. (One of the dockworkers taps Fergus on his shoulder. He either does not feel it or he just chooses to ignore him.) FMTWSD: Ooooh, I kin see it now, Ma. A whole bunch a wrasslers I doan like, all ready and waiting for me ta lay a beating on dem. What's dat? Och, yer right, it will be so much harder on them seeing as how I dinna like them! (The other dockworker speaks up.) DOCKWORKER #1: Hey sweetie! (points at Fergus' kilt) Nice skirt! How about a kiss? DOCKWORKER #2: Yeah! Just hope your beard don't tickle! (The dockworkers start laughing.) FMTWSD (into the phone): Just a sec Ma. (Fergus spins around, phone handset still in his hand, and slams one of the dockworkers right in the face, breaking his nose. The dockworker's nose busts wide open and he collapses in a heap. The force of the punch has ripped the handset right out of the phone box. The second dockworker unloads on Fergus with a roundhouse right, catching him in the jaw. Fergus turns back to face the second dockworker. He casually spits out a tooth and unloads with a fierce kick to the dockworkers groin. The second dockworker falls to his knees and Fergus smashes him in the face with the phone handset that he is still holding. The second dockworker crumples like a paper doll.) FMTWSD (bringing the phone handset up to his ear): Okay Ma, I'm back. Hello? Hello, Ma? Ah, hell, musta got disconnected! Ya stupid bugger, its all yer fault! (Fergus kicks the unconcious dockworker.) FMTWSD (into the camera): Hey ya wee lassies in the CWO! Get yerselves ready fer Fergus McTavish, The Whirling Scottish Devil! I'm lookin' fer a fight! Now any of you sewer urchins ready to take on dis here Scotsmen? Doan be fooled by dis kilt here. When I comes to fight, I comes to fight. And when I'm done, I'll make a nice haggis outta yer innards. Ya know, a Haggis would be nice right about now. (Fergus turns to walk away.) FMTWSD: Oh, and one last thing, CWO.... (Fergus turns his back to the camera and lifts up his kilt, mooning the cameraman.) FMTWSD (over his shoulder): Gets ready for The Whirling Scottish Devil! ITS NOT OATMEAL! (Camera fades, as much laughing and hooting can be heard.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr, with an incredulous look on his face. STARR: Uh... yeah. Anyway, we now hear from the Focus, who addresses his upcoming tough schedule, including the likes of Wayne Schillo, Sandman Jr., and Bane. _______________________________________________________________________________ THE FOCUS: Well, it appears if I have a lot on my plate here in the CWO. Bane, I've got to go through you next Sunday, and that's not an easy feat to say the least. I don't expect a technical matchup, but believe me, I will fight you until I die if I have to. I just don't quit, period. Wayne Schillo, I want you in the ring. Sandman Jr., I want you outside the ring. I will kick your @$$ around any arena in the world. Perhaps you're not up for the challenge. You can never break my focus, for it is who I am. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally today, we hear from the Oakland sensation, two-time Television champion Mondo Gecko, talking about his match tonight against Brent 'The Ace' Alles. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Brent 'The Ace' Alles, the biggest crybaby in the CWO and the biggest little weasel. You stuck your nose in my business and cost me the match against Clark Gray. In Canada, I am going to kick your @$$ all the way from post to post. You have gone on and said how rough a childhood you had and how you always got picked on. Try growing up in the streets of San Francisco where you have to battle drugs, gangs, crime, and poverty. How about coming from a middle class family with 6 kids whose parents had to take two jobs each to make sure we had a roof over our heads and food on the table. Try having to work during high school every year until your senior year because the family needs money for the bills. Alles, you were very lucky that you had a lifestyle of yours because YOU WOULDN'T SURVIVE A DAY IN MY WORLD! So when we meet up in the squared circle I am going to give you a wrestling lesson you won't forget. It seems that you are jealous of me and Tamara because we have accomplished more in our first 4 months than you did in your entire career. GET READY FOR MONDO EXTREME ACTION. By the way, since I am bringing the lady, YOU CAN BRING THE TRAMP! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is in the interview area, watching Mondo Gecko's comments. Amanda Bloom clutches to him as usual.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES (snorting derisively): Ha. You know, that's funny, Gecko, seems to me I heard that last line before. And I think that pretty much sums everything up about you and your time here in the CWO. I thought I had mental problems, you take the cake, pal. You can't decide which REAL professional wrestler you want to emulate next. You're living in a world of fantasy, Gecko, but guess what? Just like I did to Maximum Overdrive, just like I did to Brian Williams, just like I did to Wayne Schillo, I am going to bring you into reality, kicking and screaming. I haven't had the blessed opportunity to snap the Allesbreaker on your puny little neck yet, punk. You want to talk about hard times? I don't give a damn about what your childhood was like. That isn't what this is about. What this is about, Mondo, is your warped perception of reality getting in the way of your education, and as the teacher, I'll complete that tonight. Let's get "Mondo Extreme?" (chuckles) What a bunch of crap. You're another of these poor misguided fools, Mondo, who think that this CWO is just a big party where you can show off for all your lady friends. Wrong, wrong, wrong. You ask Brian Williams how much of a party it was when I damn near broke his neck. And maybe that's all you're going to get as well, Mondo Gecko, or maybe I will go "extreme" on you, and take you to a new depth of hell and pain that you've never felt before. You were warned, Gecko, to stay the hell out of my way, and now you're going to reap the whirlwind. CO1 - EOT. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
