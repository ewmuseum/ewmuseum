CWO Terror Night '98 - Results

31 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO TERROR NIGHT '98 RESULTS San Jose Arena, San Jose, CA Steele pinned Vulture after the Steele Driver. Time of match: 5:51. Brent "The Ace" Alles and Hollywood Haskins were interviewed and had some friction regarding their teaming up in the Triple Cage of Terror later that night. 'Mr. Technical' Cooper Young pinned Dane Griffen after the inside cradle. Time of match: 9:39. After the match Southern Dynamite and "Dragonfire" Rich Johnson came down to the ring. Southern Dynamite tried to say something about Darkness, but Tom Krepps ordered his mike to be shut off and security to arrest them, but all three men (Young, Dynamite, and Johnson) fled through the crowd. Dark Side (Fallen, Night Crawler, Night Slinger) defeated The Unit (Holiday, Skyhawk, Newton Bros.) when Night Crawler pinned Howard Newton after a shocking attack by Skyhawk. Sean Holiday revealed his allegiance to be to the Unit. However, Skyhawk would go on to betray the Unit and join the Dark Side. Time of match: 9:59. A woman known as Sindy Sinn, former manager of Holiday when he was a heel, also made her appearance during this match, aiding the Dark Side. She left at Skyhawk's side. Texas Roughriders had an interview. Lunatic Fringe (Maia and Kamikaze) defeated Texas Roughriders (Taurus and McMichael) when 'Killer' Carlos Maia pinned Wesley 'The Rock' McMichaels after the Killer Move. Time of match: 8:34. Lester Sheets came out before the Light Heavyweight Championship match to do his usual "stirring up." CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Champion 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin and Secret Agent Super Dragon wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin retained the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion title. After the match both men shook hands and bowed to each other. CWO TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE): In what has to be considered an upset, champion Wishbone pinned Sandman Jr. after hitting him with his own cane after Zero distracted Jr. Time of match: 12:42. Wishbone retained the CWO Television Champion title. Because he won, Sandman Jr. had to "grant a wish" to Wishbone. That turned out to be to give the wrestler formerly known as Sandman his name back. Zero was revealed to be Sandman! Sandman then lit the Singapore cane on fire and struck Sandman Jr. across the back with it three times, burning him severely! Diamondback Chris Myers and Union Caine had a heart to heart about their involvement in the Triple Cage of Terror. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Deadly Sins (Pride and Sloth) defeated the Rat Pack (Gold Kard and Flash Varga) when Sloth pinned 'Chairman of the Board' Gold Kard after the big butt splash. Time of match: 15:44. Deadly Sins won the CWO North American Tag Team Champions titles. Sandman Jr. was shown being treated by the CWO medical crews for his burns, but left before being taken to a hospital, attacking the medical crew in the process. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Champion Joe Eagle defeated Wildfire by countout, thus winning $1,000,000. Wildfire was counted out after Union Caine delivered the Cainedriver to him in anger. (Wildfire struck him accidentally.) Russell Hill and Union Caine seemed to be at odds during this match, and Hill left the ring in disgust, followed by a confused Eagle. Time of match: 10:28. Joe Eagle retained the CWO North American Champion title. CWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Champions the True Americans (Carter and Washington) defeated the Dogs of War (Fett and Fantastic) when Allen 'Mr. America' Carter pinned 'Irresistible Assassin' Eddie Fantastic after an elbowdrop and outside interference by Ricky Kidd. Kidd, form champion tag team partner with Fantastic as Simply Irresistible, was in the Americans' corner. He tripped Fantastic, setting him up for the elbowdrop and the pin. Time of match: 16:22. True Americans retained the CWO World Tag Team Champions title. The True Americans were dressed up like Team Johnny Reb in a not-so-subtle dig at the Triple Crown winner and his entourage. Sandman Jr. had been storming around backstage looking for Wishbone and/or Sandman. He found the former, and battered him back towards a dumpster with another Singapore cane! He then threw Wishbone in the dumpster and started it on fire! Luckily CWO crews were able to put the fire out quickly, but Wishbone was severely burned and had to be taken to the hospital. Sandman Jr. was taken away by the police. TRIPLE CAGE OF TERROR: This was a wild one, as not one but two people were thrown off the top of the cage! Diamondback Chris Myers threw Kamikaze of the Lunatic Fringe off the top first (the Fringe had entered the structure to help Brent "The Ace" Alles). Alles and Maia then proceeded to throw Myers off. Both Myers and Kamikaze had to be taken out on stretchers. While he was being taken out, Alles and Maia knocked the stretcher over. The winner of the match was Mondo Gecko, but in a shocking fashion he got the submission over his former best friend and CPE member Col. Taggart! Knocking out the former military man with a pair of brass knuckles, Gecko used the camel clutch to gain the submission victory when a referee stopped the match. Gecko left with "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum, revealing an alliance with the Outlaw Riders. Speaking of the Riders, the team of "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum and Cris Marcus did not last long, instead brawling throughout the entire match. Others involved the match included Chris Greco and Tommy Lightning of the Four Aces, Hollywood Haskins of the Thomas Family, "The Avenger" Jesse Ewiak, Fergus McTavish, and Union Caine of the Men of Respect. Mondo Gecko earned a shot at the World title at NOVEMBER PAIN '98. CWO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (STRAP MATCH): Foolkiller defeated champion Masher Maccabee in a brutal, bloody strap match (Maccabee lost the coin toss). Time of match: 19:05. Foolkiller won the CWO World Champion title.
