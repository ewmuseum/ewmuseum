CWO REPORT 3/24/97

25 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everyone, and welcome to the REPORT for Monday, March 24, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. I am glad to be joined today by a special guest, the commissioner of the CWO, "Texas Rose" Randy Johnson. Commissioner Johnson, thank you for being here. "TEXAS ROSE" RANDY JOHNSON: It's mah pleasure, Johnny. STARR: Before we get to our questions for you, Commissioner, let's look at the lineup for tommorrow night's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER! _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 3/25/97 HOUR #1: Chris Greco and Tommy Lightning vs. Big Cool APRIL ARMAGEDDON TITLE SHOT ON THE LINE: "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Moses Wildfire and Bloodbath vs. "Lonestar" Mike Williams and Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung HOUR #2: TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Panther vs. Mondo Gecko (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mercenaries vs. Pit Bosses WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. Bane (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny and Randy.) STARR: All right, the first thing I'd like to talk to you about, Commissioner Johnson, is the match between "Monster" Rich Clark and Moses with the April Armageddon title shot on the line. We all know that Moses was the Last Man Standing at CWO SPRING BREAK '97 in that exciting match, why must he prove himself again tomorrow night? JOHNSON: Well, ah wish ah had more information for ya, Johnny Starr, but all ah know is that a legal document was put forth to mahself and the Championship Committee, and really, our hands were tied. STARR: Who was the driving force behind this contract? JOHNSON: Ah'm not at liberty to say. STARR: All right, but, the match in itself is a bit of a mismatch. Moses, a light heavyweight, the Light Heavyweight Champion at one point actually, against the 7'4" "Monster" Rich Clark? It doesn't make sense. JOHNSON: Like ah said, Johnny Starr, mah hands are tied, the Championship Committee's hands are tied, and if we wanted to avoid legal action, we had to make that match. STARR: All right, what about the match between Wayne Schillo and Bane for the World Championship? JOHNSON: As you know, Bane won that 10-man battle royal in Paris, France, giving him the right to fight for the title on the FEVER following that. Well, since Bane won the title at SPRING BREAK '97, mahself and the Championship Committee determined that Bane should still fight for the title, except he will be defendin' it in a rematch against the former champion, Wayne Schillo. STARR: All right, the big news on the hotline concerns former CWO superstars Sandman and Talon. What is their situation? JOHNSON: I'm sorry to say that we were not able to come to contract agreements with those two gentlemen, and they have decided to pursue their wrestling careers elsewhere. We wish them well in their future endeavors. STARR: Let's talk about the next big PPV, APRIL ARMAGEDDON. Has a main event been decided yet? JOHNSON: Yes, it will be either Wayne Schillo or Bane vs. Moses or "Monster" Rich Clark for the CWO World title, depending on the outcome of tomorrow night's FEVER. Also, we will see the return of the ROCK, an eight-cage affair that will be held in a specially constructed structure in a nearby warehouse, with giant TV screens to relay the action to those people in the Baltimore arena. STARR: Will the ROCK be a 16 man affair like last year? JOHNSON: The final number of men involved is not determined yet, but it may be in excess of that number. We're still in negotiations with various factions of the CWO. STARR: Someone that has expressed anger recently here in the CWO is Masher Maccabee, the former Television champion who claims that he was not beaten at SPRING BREAK '97 for the title in the IRON MEN/IRON BARS match as he thinks he scored a pin that would've tied the score, thus allowing him to retain the title. What are your thoughts on this situation? JOHNSON: Ah can understand Mr. Maccabee's frustration, ah only wish he would go about expressin' it in a different way. However, ah have reviewed the tape, and Mr. Maccabee certainly has a case. It's hard to determine what happened first, the referee's hand hittin' the mat a third time or the time limit expiring. So, mahself and the championship committee has come up with this solution: at APRIL ARMAGEDDON, Masher Maccabee and Mondo Gecko will meet in a ladder match with the Television championship at stake. Hopefully this will finish the controversy. STARR: Finally, somewhat of a personal question. In your wrestling career, you were known as the "Texas Rose." Because of this, some have been quick to point the finger at you over this growing "Brotherhood of the Rose" situation. Do you have anything to do with this group? JOHNSON (angrily): No ah do not, and ah resent the implication. Ah don't know what the Brotherhood of the Rose's game is, but let me tell ya personally that ah'm sick and tired of their disruption of CWO events. Ah am hereby giving CWO wrestlers full authority to remove these gentlemen from the building... by any means necessary. STARR: All right, well thank you very much, Commissioner Johnson. JOHNSON: It's mah pleasure. STARR (turning to the camera): Before we sign off today, let's hear some comments from CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is slumped in a chair, his eyes filled with tears and a smile covering his face. The Regulators, Battlecat, and Tamara Wilcox are celebrating his TV championship reign with him as champagne is poured over Gecko, who is bruised and sore from his match with Masher Macabee. Gecko looks up and speaks.) MONDO GECKO: Going into spring break, the day before it happened I got a call from the hospital in Oakland to hear that my grandfater died of diabetes in a coma. He was 82 years old and suffering from cancer (starts to cry) I said there in front of God that I would not only avenge Tamara and my friends for Masher's acts but I would dedicate this match to my grandfather. (looks to the sky) Grampa, LOOK AT ME, I DID IT! I DID IT! (Gecko then stands up and raisese his hands in glory and high fives the rest of the Wild Bunch and hugs Tamara Wilcox.) MONDO GECKO: Now I wanted to get some things off my chest and first is to Masher Macabee. I give you credit, you gave it everything and I am impressed with your ability, but bottom line here is that I wasn't going to let anything derail me from my quest. I proved though, that I can hang with anybody in this federation and be as hardcore as they come. Tuesday Night Fever I get to wrestle an ally of mine in Panther. Panther, you almost beat Tiger Rogers and you're a great wrestler and a stand-up guy but nothing is going to take away my TV title. Panther, get ready because it is going to be Mondo Extreme time and I will fly all over you and prove who the real predator around here is. Thomas, you got a big mouth. If you ever try to lay your hands on me or especially Tamara I will pimp smack you silly. You overgrown ignorant Bobby Heenan wannabe. TAMARA WILCOX: Thomas, I am tired of hearing your stupid comments. You were a second rate broadcaster and it is a shame that Kyle Melin has to carry the whole show because of your ignorance. Melinn, can't wait to see you in April Armageddon, cutie! And as I said before, Mondo is the real champion around here in the CWO! _______________________________________________________________________________ (The scene opens a day after the match with the Highlanders. Both the Eternal Wanders are standing in a dark cave, Lord Guru is sitting near a fire. Each of the Wanderers have a dark and sinister look on their face. Looking closely, one can see the new scar that is on the arm of Exodus from the table. He stands and walks to the fire. Placing a knife in, he pulls it out and cuts the wound. Holding back a yell of pain, Exile now stands and pulls out of the fire a container containing molten metal. Dripping it on the wound, you see Exodus holding the pain, mentally controlling the aunguish of having the metal sear over his wound. Finally, Lord Guru stands up and puts a leaf of brown-green origin over the wound. Pressing down on it, Exodus screams. Pulling back the leaf, the wound is now gone, and filled within the hole is a metal filling. Standing up, Lord Guru walks over to the other side of the fire and closes his eyes in a meditation. In walks Guru Crush Storm, the disciple of Lord Guru. He looks at Exodus and smiles a chilling smile.) GURU CRUSH STORM: So... Ohlhoff and Couchman have found the Cabin. What will you do, Exodus? EXODUS (a cold dispassionate look on his face, rubbing his shoulder which strangely is healed): What will I do? I will finish the job. Highlanders... (looks at the camera) you faced the Power of the Eternal Wanderers. And Pit Bosses, congratalations with the win for the belts. You deserve it. However... keep those belts nice and shiny for us. We'll be coming for them soon. Still... (quietly, but alert) what are we going to do, Exile? (He looks over at Exile who is sitting down.) EXILE (a wicked grin crosses his face): Why... Couchman, Ohlhoff, "extreme," huh? (laughs maniacally) You don't know extreme, boys. EXODUS: Extreme team... you just caused yourself a world of hurt. And we have a little surprise for you. A few old friends say hi. I think you'll recall them as the Hearttakers! (Out of the shadows, "Hearttattack" John Payne and Mark "Taker" Creep come out. They both are somber... dressed in the new Grey attire that they sport. Mark "Taker" Creep is carrying a body bag and Heartattack John Payne has one hand in black electric tape and the other with brass knuckles.) MARK "TAKER" CREEP: Extreme team? (hisses) Better name than in the UXW.. (a whispery hoarsy voice) Your mistake was attacking the Wanderers, children... and though we didn't finish you off in the UXW, you will be terminated here... in the CWO, by the Wanderers. "HEARTATTACK" JOHN PAYNE: Oh, how I wish we could get our hand on you two! (a wicked grin) However... contracts keep us busy elsewhere. Couchman, Ohlhoff... as an old friend says... THE REVELATION has been REVEALED and it is upon you! For the path you lead, leads to destruction and doom. EXODUS: Extreme team... you want us, (a cold look, but one full of fury) YOU HAVE US! Either way... you are in for a fight that you won't survive! (Fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire in the locker room after his WIN over Ganglord, he and the lovely Miss Amber are celebrating.) WILDFIRE: Ohhhhhh Yeahhhhhhhh! At last, vindication is mine, I have beaten you, Ganglord! I have gained my revenge! AMBER: T.A.S., I told you that you would get an @$$ kicking if you got involved! WILDFIRE: Not to take anything away from Ganglord, you are a great superstar but this time it was MY hand that was raised! Now it's on to bigger and better things! I'm on the road to the gold in the CWO and nothing's gonna stop me! I'm gonna bring home the gold for the fans here in the CWO! (Miss Amber hands Wildfire a piece of paper.) WILDFIRE: I have an open contract for anyone to face me! Just remember, when you play with fire, you're gonna get BURNED! AMBER: What ya gonna do when the WILDFIRE runs WILD on you! (The camera fades as Wildfire and Amber continue to celebrate.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Bloodbath is in a dark cemetary with an old falling apart gravestone next to him.) BLOODBATH (dark mysterious voice): Someday... somewhere... we will meet again... (Suddenly the grave stone is struck by lightening, the name now reads BANE.) BLOODBATH: I met my Bane, and it may have beaten me, but it did not destroy me! That is the first mistake you made, Bane! It will be your fatal mistake! I have chosen your final resting place! You claim to have the power of the dark side, well Bane, I AM THE DARKSIDE! This is my home, Bane, and I'm waiting for you! Not only will I take your soul, but I will take the CWO WORLD TITLE! (All light is gone except for a faint blood red light shining on Bloodbath.) BLOODBATH: Rest in HELL! (The camera fades out as Bloodbath vanishes.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's it for the REPORT today. We'll see you tomorrow on FEVER!
