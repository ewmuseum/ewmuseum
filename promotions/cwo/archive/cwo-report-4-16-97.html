CWO REPORT 4/16/97

16 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Footage is shown from last night's FEVER as the crowd in the Tokyo Dome reacts as Randy Johnson makes his entrance.) MELINN: And here comes CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson! The Texas Rose coming down, hopefully to restore order to this match. THOMAS: Yeah, right. MELINN: Johnson in the ring, he's got a mike... ROOT: "Monster" Rich Clark has some nasty gashes from that shovel attack, we may not have a World championship match after all. RANDY JOHNSON (with Japanese translation right behind him): Bane, as the Commissioner of this league, I order you to give me that shovel or I will throw out this match! You will have no chance of regaining your world championship, unless you give me that shovel! THOMAS: Give it to him, all right! MELINN: Well, he better not, if he wants another chance at that belt, "Monster" Rich Clark trying to get back to his feet after that vicious attack with the shovel, and it looks like Bane is indeed handing that shovel to Commissioner Johnson. ROOT: Very brave move by Commissioner Johnson, of course, during his long and fabled wrestling career as the "Texas Rose," this man would back down from no one. MELINN: All right, and... oh my god! Bane looked like he was going to hand the shovel to Commissioner Johnson, and then he must have changed his mind, as he just swung it at Johnson and hit him right in the head! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Fade to the CWO REPORT opening, as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome back to the CWO REPORT for Wednesday, April 16, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. What you just witnessed was one of the most horrifying attacks we've ever seen in the CWO, of course perpetrated by Bane. Randy Johnson was taken to a Tokyo hospital, where he was treated for a concussion as well as a head laceration. Bane then went on to win the World championship for the second time, after viciously attacking Clark with the shovel he made out of the first CWO World Championship. Clark also spent some time in the Tokyo hospital, being treated for lacerations to the face and the torso, among other minor injuries. However, before we get to the rest of the REPORT for today, let's go to a press conference held this morning in which acting Commissioner Jim Longo had some comments about the situation concerning the World championship. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Jim Longo is shown in front of the press.) JIM LONGO: Thank you all for coming here today. As you know, CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson is currently recuperating from the injuries he sustained at the hands of Bane Sunday night in the Tokyo Dome. I will be acting as commissioner until he is ready to continue. REPORTER #1: Mr. Longo, what is the situation with the World championship? LONGO: Well, in one aspect, our hands are tied, as the bell was rung, signaling the start of an official match. Whether we like it or not, Bane won the match and also the title, and it will go down in the record books as a victory for him. However, we have reviewed the tape, and due to the actions that Bane took before the match even began, that is, attacking Clark and Johnson with the shovel, and also taking into account Bane's apparent disdain for the championship and this organization it represents, we have decided to hold the title up until MAY-HEM. REPORTER #2: What does this do to the main event? LONGO: Well, we're already going to have two rings, side by side, in order to facilitate the main event for the PPV, the two-ring 60 man battle royal known as May-Hem. So, this is what is going to happen. Currently, the CWO Championship Committee feels there are three men with a legitimite claim to the World Championship. One is the current champion Bane. Another is the former champion "Monster" Rich Clark. And the third person is the North American Champion Goliath. Goliath, being the North American Champion, is the natural #1 contender, and also holds a victory over "Monster" Rich Clark at CWO SPRING BREAK '97. So, the main event now will involve these three men, with one of the two rings surrounded with a steel cage. Two of the three men will start in the cage, and the object will be to incapacitate your opponent and handcuff him to the steel cage, and then escape into the second ring, where the escapee must then fight the other man not originally in the cage. The winner of this match will be the uncontested CWO World Champion. (Reporters start clamoring.) LONGO: All right, thank you very much for coming, no further questions. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: All right, there you have it, the main event for CWO MAY-HEM now involving Bane, "Monster" Rich Clark, and CWO North American Champion Goliath in a unique match. Two of them will start in a steel cage, and one of those two must knock the other to a point where he is able to be handcuffed to the cage. Then, that person has to get out of the cage and face the final man. Should be an exciting match, indeed. Also, it should be noted that Bane has been heavily fined for his actions and the newly made CWO World Championship belt has been confiscated from him until the May PPV. Without any further ado, let's look at what else happened last night at FEVER in our FEVER FLASHBACK! _______________________________________________________________________________ FEVER FLASHBACK 4/15/97 TOKYO DOME, JAPAN MATCH ONE: Arlon Flaym defeated "Master of Darkness" Damion in 11:30 after pinning him with a cradle. "Master of Darkness" Damion then went on after the match to deliver a Darkness Falls tombstone piledriver to Flaym on a steel chair. MATCH TWO: Highlanders defeated Mason-Dixon in 13:19 when Conor McCloud pinned Beauregard Dixon after the Quickening (superplex by Gavin, flying splash by Conor). Masters of Hardcore then attacked the Highlanders after the match, driving Conor McCloud through a table with the Hardcore Bomb. Ohlhoff and Couchman then did the same thing to their manager, "Beautiful" Brian Clark, firing him. The Masters then expressed their displeasure towards the CWO hierarchy, saying that they had been screwed in the ratings and that they deserved a shot at the North American Tag Team Championships because of their performance in the tag team triangle match at APRIL ARMAGEDDON. MATCH THREE (TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP): Champion Masher Maccabee defeated Panther in 14:18, pinning him after the Homentaschen Hangover (flying somersault legdrop). Comments from Wayne "College Guy" Schillo and Brian "The Hammer" Williams. MATCH FOUR: Wayne "College Guy" Schillo and The Focus wrestled to a double countout in 11:03. They then proceeded to brawl back to the dressing room. Interview in the ring with Mondo Gecko and Tamara Wilcox. Gecko expressed his desire to form an alliance against Gold Kard International. MATCH FIVE: Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) defeated Maximum Overdrive and Baby Waldo in 15:39 after Bloodbath pinned Maximum Overdrive after the Tombstone Powerbomb. MATCH SIX (WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP): Bane defeated "Monster" Rich Clark to win the World championship for the second time. Bane attacked Clark before the match with the shovel he made out of the first World Championship belt. (The new belt, along with a special trophy that would be presented to the winner of the match by the Super Japan promotion, were at ringside.) CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson then came down to ringside and demanded that Bane give up the shovel, or immediately be disqualified. Bane then hit Johnson with the shovel, and also broke the special trophy over him. Bane then went on to make a heavily bleeding Clark submit to the Merciful Death. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We are in the Great White North this week for House Shows, starting with a show in Calgary, Alberta, Canada tonight! Let's see what's in store for our great Canadian fans. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEWS WEDNESDAY, APRIL 16, 1997 CALGARY, ALBERTA, CANADA Maximum Overdrive vs. Panther Baby Waldo vs. Ganglord Brad "The Man" Couchman vs. Conor McCloud Moses vs. Pride David "The Destroyer" Davis/Inferno vs. Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Dark Raven vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Serpentmaster/Vlad the Impaler vs. Pit Bosses (champions) STEEL CAGE (BISHOP'S ARENA): Bishop vs. Johnny Reb _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars. Last night the Highlanders were viciously attacked by the Masters of Hardcore Ohlhoff and Couchman, we now have a response from them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Highlanders are in the interview area.) GAVIN McCLOUD: We're weeks away from one of tha' biggest fights of our careers - matchin' up against tha' Pit Bosses for tha' Tag Team championship belts - and what do we have to put up with? Some buncha morons who we've already clobbered several times swipin' us from behind. It's not going ta work, mind ye. CONOR McCLOUD: So, tha' so-called Extreme Team is complain' about their lack of title shots - time ta break out tha' violins and start cryin', it's soooooo unfair. Listen up, ye pair of no-talent jessies: we've fought over 40 matches in this federation, paid our dues, an' proved ourselves worthy to go fer tha' belts. And you, what have YOU done? Nothing at all. We've got a REAL team to prepare for, not a pair of whining, trumped up poofs like you. GAVIN McCLOUD: But now you've done it - you've gone and got us a little ticked off. As many people know round here, we don't start many scraps, but we sure as hell finish them. You've started a fight ye cannae win, now it's time to reap tha' whirlwind. Remember.... CONOR McCLOUD: THERE CAN BE ONLY ONE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also on FEVER last night, Wayne "College Guy" Schillo and The Focus fought to a double countout, and as far as The Focus is concerned, the fight's not over yet. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: Wayne Schillo, I'm going to make this short and sweet. You gave me a hell of a match on Tuesday. You kicked out after my finisher, and I kicked out after yours. I want a rematch. A falls count anywhere match. This way, no countouts. I'm positive I can beat you. Mr. President, sign the match for next week. Are you up to it, College Guy? _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally today, we have comments from Brent "The Ace" Alles. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is in the interview area. Amanda Bloom clutches to him, in tears.) AMANDA BLOOM (choking and sobby): Daddy... the bad men tried to hurt me... took me away... daddy.... BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES (coldly, to the camera): All right, Overdrive, you wanted my attention again, you've got it. I had a mission in mind, you all know what it is, my education of Brian "The Hammer" Williams. I disposed of you at SPRING BREAK '97. Whether you like it or not, I beat you fair and square in the ring after the Allesbreaker, even with all your hired goons outside. But you couldn't take that, could you? Couldn't take the fact that you were showed up by your better. So, like the punk thug that you are, you resort to kidnapping this defenseless woman here, just to get my attention. Now, she wakes up in the nights screaming, and I have to deal with it. And I can, Overdrive, I can, because my soul's been through a lot worse. But your soul hasn't. No, Overdrive, I can see now that I didn't quite get the job done the first time, and I have to go back and chop up the left-overs. Fine by me. I'm telling you, Overdrive, and this isn't hyperbole, this isn't an idle threat, if you ever come near this beautiful Bloom of mine again, I will kill you. I'm very serious here. This isn't some B. S. pro wrestling interview to sell a pay per view or increase a senseless feud. This is reality, which I know you have a lot of trouble dealing with. You want me in the ring, fine, I'll kick your @$$ over and over again 'til you finally get the point. But you stay away from the only thing that matters in my life right now, my beautiful bloom, or I will find you and kill you. It's as simple as that. And don't think that's an idle threat, because I know where I can find you, and there won't be a court that will convict me, because you won't be found. Do I make myself clear, Overdrive? Are you getting the point? Because you will, even if I have to lose that last shred of my soul to do it. And as for you, Brian and Wayne, don't even think that because your hired goon Overdrive is after me, and that's all you are, Overdrive, a hired goon, don't think that I haven't forgotten you two punks' education. MAY-HEM is coming, boys, and what sweet succor it would be to the last shred of my soul to throw all of you out and shatter your dreams at a World Championship shot just like all my hopes and dreams have been shattered, except for my beautiful bloom. CO1 - EOT. BLOOM (sobbing): Daddy... make the bad guys go away.... ALLES: I'm serious, Overdrive, you don't know who you're dealing with here, and we haven't even begun yet. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
