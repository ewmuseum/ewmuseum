CWO REPORT 4/3/97

4 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on the members of DOOMSDAY. Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries, who are holding their CWO 6 Man Tag Team titles, flank Maximum Overdrive. They see the interviews with the Deadly Sins and the Wild Bunch and start to laugh.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Wild Bunch, when the hell are you going to get a clue? You can't beat us! You're ready to take our titles? I thought you were last time and what happened? We knocked you three losers on your cans! KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Oh, now you've got the CWO TV Champion on your team?!? Oh, we're SOOO scared! You three are nothing but talk! You've been running at the mouth for the last two months saying, "We're going to beat you Doomsday! We're going to do this! We're going to do that!" The truth is, you're outclassed. You have no chance at winning these titles until we decide to give them up. And we don't plan on it anytime soon. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Well, it goes a lot like this, Wussie Bunch. We're going to get in that ring and beat you within an inch of your pathetic lives. You might have scored a few cheap wins over the Mercenaries in the past, but your track record of meeting us for these titles marks you as LOSERS! And that's the way you'll stay after tonight is over. But the Mercenaries also have a match with the Creatures of the Night, who don't seem to be as dominant as they once were. Creatures, we're going to beat you like we did when you were champs in those non-title matches you gave us because you were too scared to put the titles up against us. MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: A lot of people have been wondering where the members of Doomsday have been since the PPV. We've been taking some well deserved R 'n' R and are ready to get back in the ring and do what we do best, and that's beat people up! Deadly Sins, my boys have beaten you like a couple of drums in the past. The future will be no different. Get in the ring with the Mercenaries and get another notch in the loss column, you overranked morons. Doomsday is taking no prisoners! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, April 3, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the superstars of the CWO were in Roanoke, VA, let's look at what went down on that great card! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/2/97 Roanoke, VA Mercenaries defeated Creatures of the Night when Korperal Punishment pinned Night Crawler after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 19:56. The Focus pinned Chris Greco after the backslide. Time of match: 5:19. Arlon Flaym pinned El Grande Pistolerro after the clothesline. Time of match: 9:41. Mason-Dixon and Eternal Wanderers wrestled to a 30 minute time limit draw. Wrath made Wildfire submit to the Repentence Rack. Time of match: 13:39. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Champions Doomsday (Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries) and Wild Bunch (CWO Television Champion Mondo Gecko and the Regulators) wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 11:59. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Champions the Pit Bosses defeated David 'The Destroyer' Davis/Inferno when Pit Boss #1 pinned Inferno after the missed falling headbutt. Time of match: 13:52. TRIANGLE MATCH: 'Monster' Rich Clark won a triangle match. 'Monster' Rich Clark eliminated Goliath in 25:30 after the Choke Slam. 'Monster' Rich Clark eliminated Bane in 42:23 after the Choke Slam. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in our nation's capitol, Washington, D. C., with another great lineup of CWO action. Let's see what's on tap for tonight! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW 4/4/97 Washington, DC Wild Ones (Mondo Gecko and El Grande Pistolerro vs. "Lonestar" Mike Williams and Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung David "The Destroyer" Davis vs. Hellhound Mercenaries vs. Eternal Wanderers Joe Eagle vs. 'Master of Darkness' Damion Vlad the Impaler vs. Sandman Jr. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Mondo Gecko (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Big Cool vs. the Bad Guys (champions) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb vs. Goliath (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars. Tonight in Washington, Mondo Gecko and El Grande Pistolerro take on the team of "Lonestar" Mike Williams and Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung. Let's hear from the Gecko and the Pistolerro. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko and El Grande Pistolerro are in the interview area, along with their manager Tamara Wilcox.) EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: "Lonestar" & Gerstung, you guys are two of the greatest high flyers in the league and we respect you two. But the Wild Ones are on a mission and that is to capture tag team gold and we will do whatever it takes to win. We came here and bonded because both of us want to hold championship gold and we are going to do that. It is time for the Wild Ones to get extreme in the CWO and we are ready to do whatever it takes to win. "Lonestar," we have a date with destiny at APRIL ARMAGEDDON and I am ready to SURVIVE! MONDO GECKO (laughing): Hey Thomas, how did you like getting dropped like a bad habit... (continues to laugh) oh god, Tamara, I don't think that I could have hit a spin kick better myself. Oh god... anyway, Gerstung & Williams, you guys are class acts and we respect you but we are ready to go all out in order to climb the ladder for the title. Bottom line here, we are gonna be as extreme as we can be, baby. Jason "Flash" Varga, you will get your shot at my TV title and I am ready to take you for a wild ride. I have a lot of respect for you and I will help you out anytime you need help. But I am gonna fly the skies and soar all over you and take you down baby. IT'S TIME FOR ME TO GET MONDO EXTREME! TAMARA WILCOX: Thomas, you have had a lot to say about me and I am tired of you talking nonsense and treating Kyle like dirt. You say you can't hit a lady, well all you do is cheap shot people and trash them from behind. Johnny Reb, thanks for helping out Mondo and if you want our help you got it anytime, anyplace. Bottom line, Thomas, is I am gonna kick your teeth in and send you back to Wendy's! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: He's got a "No Holds Barred" match at APRIL ARMAGEDDON against Gold Kard, but tonight he gets a shot at the North American title. Let's hear from Johnny Reb. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb and General Lee are in the locker room area.) REB: Well, Gold Kard, y'all found yo'self some back up. Well ta'naht, ah go on the offensive! Ah preform the flanking manuever... ah draw first blood. Ta'naht, ah make our match at APRIL ARMAGEEDON for the North American Title! LEE: That's Raht! Any ally of Gold Kard now has a bullseye square on their back. Goliath, the bigguh they are, the harder they do fall. Ta'naht, in our nation's capital, y'all are gonna hit rock bottom! REB: Kard, the stakes will be raised for Sunday naht, because ah am walkin' out the North American champion. Ah am gonna restore honor to that title, somethin' it has been lackin' fo' a long while! LEE: Goliath, ta'naht it's Johnny Reb with sling in hand. Good day, suh. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: On this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, the Focus' first title shot here in the CWO was unfortunately disrupted by an attack by Masher Maccabee, who was determined to make sure Mondo Gecko would be the man he would face in the ladder match for the title at APRIL ARMAGEDDON. The Focus now responds to what happened on Tuesday night. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: Masher Maccabee... let's keep this short. You interfered in my match on Tuesday, and basically took away my title belt. I had that match won, and you knew it. You have made an enemy and his name is the Focus. Do you think you can deal with it? Probably not. Prepare for hell, boy, because you've brought out the side of me that no one wants to see. You'll know what I mean soon. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
