CWO REPORT 2/5/97

5 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Wednesday, February 5, 1997. Last night's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER featured some incredible action and developments here in the CWO, let's go to our FEVER FLASHBACK! _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER FLASHBACK 2/4/97 "LIVE" FROM PROVIDENCE, RI (PROVIDENCE CIVIC CENTER) Announcers (1st hour): Jerry Covenant and Mike Postema Announcers (2nd hour): Kyle Melinn and David Thomas Other broadcast staff: Johnny Starr and Christine Ferrara DARK MATCHES: Doomsday (Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries) defeated CWO World 6-Man Tag Team Champions North and South ("Lonestar" Mike Williams and the Coldfront in 10:00 when Korperal Punishment pinned Kurt "Thrill From the Chill" Gerstung after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Doomsday won the CWO World 6-Man Tag Team Championships. Clark Gray defeated Ganglord by reverse decision in 12:03. Ganglord pinned Gray after the Ghetto Bomb (tiger driver), but refused to let up after the match, prompting the ref to disqualify him. Chris Greco (formerly Flyboy) came down to make the save, but Gray was not appreciative of it, and him and Greco then proceeded to brawl back to the dressing room. Show opened with some comments from both the "Innovator of Violence" Miguel Sanchez and "Monster" Rich Clark. Clark said he would join the Dark Side as long as Master of Darkness Damion would keep his promises. Gold Kard defeated CWO North American Champion Sandman in 15:21 after Hile Troy struck Sandman with a champagne bottle. Gold Kard won the CWO North American Championship for the second time. Comments from the Regulators. Highlanders defeated the Regulators in 13:33 when Gavin pinned Regulator Fire after a legdrop. Comments from Bane, regarding Bloodbath and the Sins. Bane defeated Bloodbath by submission with the Merciful Death. Bane then dragged Bloodbath off to an unknown location. Biblical Powers and the Alliance (Tiger Rogers and Inferno) battled to a double disqualification in a short match. Hour #2 began with Gold Kard storming the ring, and announcing the newest member of Gold Kard International, Miguel Sanchez. However, this turned out to be false, as Sanchez said that no price would be high enough for him to join, and that he would take the North American championship away from Gold Kard as ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II. ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II CONTROL CENTER, with comments from several CWO superstars. Comments from Jason "Flash" Varga, who expresses his disgust over Brent "The Ace" Alles' injury-causing attack on Brian "The Hammer" Williams. Cake Boy came to the ring, originally, he was supposed to have fought Brian "The Hammer" Williams. Cake Boy demanded an opponent, and he got one in a hurry, as CWO World Champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo stormed the ring. A match ensued, going back and forth between both men, and then Schillo was attacked by an unknown man wearing a black mask with a rose on it. The man struck Schillo across the throat with a pool cue, and Schillo was then pinned by Cake Boy in a surprise upset in 9:13. Schillo had to be taken from the building by stretcher, fortunately, his larynx was not seriously damaged. Hoss and Little Joe come to the ring for their match against the Big Cool. The Big Cool have a short interview with Christine Ferrara, but then are ambushed by the CWO North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys, who beat them up and throw them in the back of a garbage truck, driving off with them. Hoss and Little Joe won by forfeit. Masher Maccabee made his TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER debut, defeating Sin in 7:33 after the Jerusalem Jacknife followed by the Homentaschen Hangover. Maccabee then called for Alles to come down to the ring. Alles, on suspension from the CWO, risked his job by doing so, and then proceeded to enrage Maccabee with a callous border-line anti-Semetic remark. CWO Commisioner Randy Johnson came out to break the two men up, threatening to fire either or both of them if they didn't desist. Alles left, but not until he looked up at the rafters, finding the Masked Rose standing there, watching him. D. E. A. T. H. defeated the Deadly Sins when Benton pinned Pride after the Crossroad (top rope power bomb). After the match, Bane attacked the Sins with a crowbar, and knocked out Sloth with ether. Sloth was dragged off by Bane and stuck in the back of a hearse, along with Bloodbath. Bane drove off with the two men to an unknown location. Johnny Reb defeated Hile Troy by disqualification in 9:51 when Master of Darkness Damion attacked Reb when he had Troy in the Texas Cloverleaf. MOD Damion delivered the Darkness Falls tombstone to each of the men in the ring, then Hellhound brought down a strand of barbed wire, and Damion proceeded to savage Reb with it, until Mason-Dixon made the save. Comments from the CWO World Tag Team Champions, the Creatures of the Night. Deadly Sin Pride is shown arguing with Lust. Wrath then runs up and says he knows where Bane took Sloth. Pride and Wrath take off, as Pride tells Lust to stay in the building. Creatures of the Night defeated the Eternal Wanderers in 7:18, retaining the World Tag Team titles. Southern Pride and a bandaged Johnny Reb then hit the ring with steel chairs, brawling with the Creatures and MOD Damion. However, we then go to a graveyard, where the Deadly Sins have arrived to find Ganglord, already burying Bloodbath alive, working on doing the same to Sloth. The Sins chase Ganglord off, get Sloth out of the grave, and then lightning flashes as a hand sticks through the other grave, apparently Bloodbath's. The Sins scream and run off as we fade out. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: One of the most exciting TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER's in a long time, and it raises more questions than it answers, if you can believe that. One of the major questions: is Ganglord working with Bane? If so, this could be a very dangerous combination of two of the most deadly men in our sport. The results of last night have changed the upcoming ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II PPV slightly, let's look at the updated lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II (card subject to change) ________________________________________________________ WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Hile Troy vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Highlanders (Conor and Gavin McCloud) vs. Creatures of the Night (champions, Night Crawler and Night Slinger) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Gold Kard vs. "Innovator of Violence" Miguel Sanchez NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: The Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jim "Jazzman" Taylor) vs. Bad Guys (Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson and Joe "The Mean Guy" Rucker) (champions) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (10-MAN BATTLE ROYAL): Panther Sandman Jr. Benjamin Blake Bentley Inferno Brent "The Ace" Alles Maximum Overdrive Sin Mondo Gecko Steel Cake Boy LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Tiger Rogers vs. Moses (champion) 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Doomsday (Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries, Kaptain Krunch and Korperal Punishment, champions) vs. North and South ("Lonestar" Mike Williams and the Coldfront (Steve White and Kurt Gerstung) MONSTERS OF THE RING: "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Sandman (champion) SCIENTIFIC MATCH: Chris Greco vs. Clark Gray VEGAS ODDS MATCH: Pit Bosses vs. Eternal Wanderers (Exodus and Exile) BARBED WIRE TOWEL MATCH: Master of Darkness Damion (w/Rebel Yell) vs. Johnny Reb Bane vs. Wrath BURIED ALIVE MATCH: Bloodbath vs. Ganglord EYE ON THE STORM: Savior vs. Masher Maccabee Deadly Sins (Pride and Sloth) vs. Mason-Dixon (Stonewall Mason and Beauregard Dixon) DARK MATCH: Battlecat vs. Talon SHOWCASE SPECIAL: ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE (SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 16) 8-MAN TAG MATCH A (elimination): Joe Eagle, Jason "Flash" Varga, Regulators (Fire and Ice) vs. D. E. A. T. H. (Benton and Green) and Hell's Pets (Hellhound and Dark Raven) 8-MAN TAG MATCH B (elimination): Confederate Associates (Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, Hoss and Little Joe) vs. David "The Destroyer" Davis, Goliath, and Revolution (Lafayette and Black Death) SURVIVORS OF MATCH A vs. SURVIVORS OF MATCH B in an elimination match _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we kick off our week in the great state of New York, starting at the Turning Stone Casino in Rome, NY. Let's look at the great card lined up for you fans in the central New York area tonight! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW 2/5/97 ROME, NY (TURNING STONE CASINO) Cake Boy vs. Sloth Goliath vs. Sandman NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Revolution vs. Bad Guys Joe Eagle vs. Savior Mason-Dixon vs. The Alliance Sandman Jr. vs. Mondo Gecko Pit Bosses vs. Regulators LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb vs. Moses (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now take you to comments from CWO superstars. The bizarre Cake Boy has been making an impressive run of it lately here in the CWO, especially with a surprise upset last night of CWO World Champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo in a non-title match. Of course, Schillo had just been struck in the throat by the man known only at this time as the Masked Rose, but never let it be said that Cake Boy is not one to take an advantage when he sees one. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Cake Boy is in the interview area along with Toodles the Chimp, his "manager".) CAKE BOY: It ssseems that the wresstlers in this league are afraid to get down and dirty with me. Well, I'm looking for a few good men, who are bold enough to step into the ring with me. I'm sstarting with you, Brent "the ace" Alles... I have an ace for your hole, baby. (Cake Boy licks his lips.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Ummm... yeah. Anyway, the Wild Bunch are united for action this week in New York, we go to comments from them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Wild Bunch are in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: It's going to Mondo Extreme for all you Wild Ones out there because Mondo Gecko is in the house! First in line will be Sandman Jr. It seems that you have had a real attitude problem lately and I am sick of it. Sandman Jr., you may be one of the top dogs here but when I get done with you its going to be the pound for you, baby. All I have to say, Sweet Dreams, because I am going to knock you into the Dream world! Then the Wild Bunch Tour will be squaring off against Tiger Rogers. Tiger, you proved to be a pussycat when you went up against the Wild One before. Jimbo Jones, I am gonna knock you out if you get in my face again and I might have a little surprise in store for you two. Tiger Rogers, get ready because Da Gecko man is gonna have a new fur coat after I am done with you. Benjamin Blake Bentley, Mr. Rich Boy, it is going to be curtains for you becuase I am gonna cause a Stock Market Crash. You have been born with a silver spoon in your mouth and you need outside help to win. Well, baby, I am gonna soar through the air with the greatest of ease and after I am done, you're going to be bankrupt for paying all of your medical bills 'cause I am gonna put you in a world of hurt! BATTLECAT: Big Cool, you guys seem to be in our way for the quest for tag team gold. Well, after the Wild Ones burn you guys up, you better find a new job, because the aerial assault we are going to put on you two will be too much to handle. You guys are big and bad but we are fast, talented, and better than the rest so get ready, Big Cool, for the Wild Ones are gonna run wild over you two! REGULATOR FIRE: Pit Bosses, you two have caused too much trouble around here and we are sick and tired of it. In New York it is going to be snake eyes for you two thugs. We said earlier that we would enforce the rules around here and Pit Bosses, you two are going to be the first victims of our new Regulating the CWO tour. REGULATOR ICE: Pit Bosses, you two thugs have sneak attacked others, beat up wrestlers, and done numerous douple and triple teaming. Well, big men, get ready, because the Regulators are going to beat on you and end your reign of terror. Bottom line here is that we are tired of hearing about how bad you guys are. Well, baby, it is time to pay the piper and we are going to take out the trash, Pit Bosses. REGULATOR FIRE: Bad Guys, be thankful that the belts aren't on the line when you take us on because when we beat you guys, you still will be able to walk out as the champs. But soon when we get our tag title shot we will bring the titles back to the people. It is about time the people have had time to be proud of their champions and soon they will be able to cheer the new North American champions, THE REGULATORS! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Sandman Jr.'s been a little quiet lately, but he's got plenty to say now, we take you to him. _______________________________________________________________________________ SANDMAN JR.: Yo, Williams, sorry Alles attacked you after our match, if I get a chance to beat him up for you, I'll do it. Now, on to the immediate future: First, Gecko, we've fought before. You won. This time though *I* have to win, and I will. Then, Inferno thinks he's burning up the rankings, and that's fine, until he has to face ME! Then, Ganglord! You've been wreaking havoc lately with everyone, I'm gonna PUT YOU OUT! Then I have to take out Sin, that moron who's friendly with those other morons, the Deadly Sins... well, I'll crush you AGAIN, Sin, and then I'm gonna win the battle royal to be TV champ... because this league needs a champion to look up to, and that's ME! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: As you saw in the FEVER FLASHBACK, the Sins are in a world of turmoil. After Bane's attack on them last night, CWO Commisioner Randy Johnson has made a strange decree: next week on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, Bane must "face the gauntlet," as he will take on all three Deadly Sins, Pride, Sloth, and Wrath, one at a time, in a steel cage! Can you believe that? Bane is a very tough individual, but can he beat all three of these deadly men, in a cage no less? We take you now to comments from the Sins. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: OK, you idiots want to know what's going on with the Sins, FINE, we'll tell you! First off, DEATH, you were lucky! Second, like, for some reason, everyone wants a piece of Sloth since Bane attacked... tell them what you're gonna do, big man! SLOTH: First off, I'm gonna destroy that little fruit cake Cake Boy. He beat both Sandman Jr. AND that Keuka freak? HAHAHA! He won't beat ME though! Then I gotta take on Bloodbath! You'd think he of all people would know what we're dealing with, but if he wants to fight, fine! They'll all face me, and Pride here won't be too far! PRIDE: You got it, Sloth! Finally, I'll be legally in the ring with you, when we take on those morons Hoss and Little Joe. They REALLY deserve an ass-kicking! And soon enough, those hicks Mason-Dixon are going to fall too! WRATH: Speaking of falling, Ron Johnson, the Rude Guy, will face the power of Wrath, and you know Wrath will destroy Ron Johnson! Rude Guy, you are but the second of many to fall to Wrath, and then there will be BANE! BANE! You will fall like others. You are big, you are bad, but you will be NOTHING! PRIDE: Hey! Speaking of NOTHING, that's exactly what YOU did for us Lust! What is UP with that??? LUST: Bane is HUGE! He had a crowbar! What could I do?? I like pain, but NOT death! SLOTH: Get the cameras out of here now! This is Sins business! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Finally, he may be suspended from the ring, but unfortunately his mouth is not suspended from talking. We take you to comments from Brent "The Ace" Alles. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is in the interview area. A smirk is on his face.) ALLES: Well, well, well. So little Jason "Flash" Varga thinks I'm a bad man, does he? Let me tell you something, "Flash," oh, by the way, he was my favorite character on the Dukes of Hazzard, not so long ago there was another young punk who came in here thinking that I'm a bad man. His name was Brian "The Hammer" Williams, and where is he now, huh, Varga? Sucking soup through a straw, as that pretty girl of his wipes his brow. If that's what you want, "Flash," then you come on down to the CO1, we don't need anyone, but we will beat everyone. Plenty of pain for all. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
