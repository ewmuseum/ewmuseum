CWO REPORT 10/6/97

6 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO REPORT 10/6/97 Producer: Chris O'Brien ( [detail withheld] ) Executive Producer: Brent Alles ( [detail withheld] ) (Fade in on the CWO Control Center in Grand Rapids, MI. The words CWO REPORT come up in blue letters as the lights come up on the desk. Sitting behind the desk is Chris O'Brien, wearing a Pittsburgh Steelers jersey and blue jeans) O'BRIEN: Hello, and welcome to the brand spanking new CWO REPORT! It is I, "The Original" Chris O'Brien, and we have a brand new set, a brand new crew, and a brand new host! Hey, Postema....look at me! I'm hosting my own show! HA! (Chris takes a sip of water) O'BRIEN: Ok, now that I've gotten that out of my system, let's go over the results from SUNDAY SHOWCASE, held yesterday morning! __________________________________________________________________________ CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE 10/5/97 CWO Arena, Grand Rapids, MI "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday defeated Exodus by pinfall after the Permanent Vacation (inverted bulldog). Time of match: 10:56. The Deadly Sins and The Reverend/The Sandman wrestled to a no contest. Time of match 12:28. After the matchup, the Highlanders came to the ring and attacked the Sins. Moses defeated CWO Heavyweight champion Johnny Reb by countout after Masher Maccabee DDT'd Reb on the outside. Time of match: 13:54. Diamondback Chris Myers defeated 'The Brazillian Lover' Carlos Maia by pinfall after a small package. Time of match 9:15. The Mercenaries defeated the Bad Guys by DQ after the Bad Guys delivered the Bad Guy's Bash (double strike with steel chairs) to Korperal Punishment twice. Time of match: 7:03. CWO Light Heavyweight champion Jason 'Flash' Varga and Masashi 'Lightning' Lanther from the SJPW wrestled to a twenty minute draw. __________________________________________________________________________ (Back to O'Brien.) O'BRIEN: So, after that great event, there's just one thing I want to know. What posses Conor and Gavin McCloud to hit the ring and attack Pride and Sloth? I mean all the Sins have been doing is mouthing off about how they're being overlooked by the tag team committee. But the Highlanders already have to deal with the Newton Brothers at TERROR NIGHT later this month. Are they trying to make more enemies, or send a message to CWO? I'm betting the latter. After SEPTEMBER SLAM, it's obvious the Highlanders ARE the best team in this league, and they have the belts to prove it. Ok, now let's look at the lineup for tomorrow's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER! (O'Brien grabs a paper from the desk, looks at it, and looks at the camera) O'BRIEN: Seem to have grabbed the wrong one... (Chris looks at another, and then another, and another) O'BRIEN: I don't seem to have the schedule in front of me. OFF-CAMERA VOICE: Sorry, the fax machine jammed, we don't have it. O'BRIEN: The machine jammed? Come on, we just bought that thing yesterday! OFF-CAMERA VOICE: Nothing I can do, Chris. O'BRIEN: Sigh. Ok, guys, we don't have the schedule, but we have interviews with CWO superstars! OFF-CAMERA VOICE: Actually, Chris, we don't. O'BRIEN: You're kidding... OFF-CAMERA VOICE: Nope. We only got one. O'BRIEN: Well, it's promising! Ok, roll it! ______________________________________________________________________ ("Airborne" Mark Fulcrum is shown on a motorcycle outside the Portland Arena, where a CWO house show has just occured. He's in his leather jacket and has a blue bandanna around his head.) FULCRUM: Well, I just wrestled my first match here in CWO. Me and Jimmy Heat went to a 20 minute time limit draw. Now, most wrestlers would be upset with that kind of results. Not me. You see, I only get fustrated over losses. Sure, a draw means I didn't win...but it means I didn't lose, either. I look at tonight as a kind of 'feeling-out' process. You know, a welcoming party type deal. Because the cheers I got tonight were out of this world. This is a world class fed. And I'm going to deliver something that'll make them cheer harder. When a crowd cheers for me, I get better. When they boo me, I get better. It's when they really couldn't care, I get ticked off. I work my butt off to get them whipped up. They pay top dollar to see the best fed in the world, and I deliver. So, CWO, get ready. I'm not going to toss out names in hopes of getting opponents. No, I'm hoping that the stars of this fed seek out better competition. And when they do, they're come looking for me. I'm "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum, and you're all targets. ___________________________________________________________________________ (Back to O'Brien.) O'BRIEN: Strong words from the young man. The Light Heavyweight division just got interesting. Well, guys, I guess that's it for today. Before we go, two things. One, if you want any interviews for REPORT, the address is [detail withheld] . Two, this is our first show, so please, bear with us. So, we'll see you guys Wednesday, for a full FEVER recap! I'm Chris O'Brien, saying so long! (Closing creds and music come up as we fade out.)
