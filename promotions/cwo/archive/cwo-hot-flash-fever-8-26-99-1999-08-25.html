Hot Flash - FEVER 8/26/99

August 25, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FEVER AUGUST 26, 1999 LIVE FROM: Atlanta, GA (Phillips Arena) LOGFILE GENERATED: 8/15/99 WRITERS: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ), Brent Alles ( [detail withheld] ) TAPED FOR SCORCH: Vladimir Popalov vs. Brian “The King” Derks 1. Southern Dynamite vs. Secret Agent Super Dragon 2. Darknight Express vs. Creatures of the Night 3. Sandman Jr. vs. Man Mountain Marko 4. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (2 out of 3 falls): Geoff Bondavarsky vs. Wildfire (champion) 5. “The Avenger” Jesse Ewiak vs. Carr 6. Gold Kard vs. Foolkiller 7. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (TORNADO MATCH): Diamondback Chris Myers and “Airborne” Mark Fulcrum vs. Johnny Reb and “Irresistible” Eddie Fantastic
(champions) 8. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Master of Darkness Damion vs. Mondo Gecko (champion) (Footage is shown from last week's FEVER, as Union Caine is fighting Lord
Hile Troy.) MELINN: Caine grabbing a hold of Troy... ICONOCLAST! A hybrid of several moves... and I'm no wrestling scientist, so don't ask me to explain it, but he knocked Troy cold with it! Caine with a cover...
(ONE..) (TWO..) (THREE!) THOMAS: Troy is NOT going to be happy about this! MELINN: He suffered a loss to the Television Champion, you're damn right he won't be happy about it, to say the least!
(Smash cut as Foolkiller and DaVinci are now in the ring as Caine stomps on
Troy.) MELINN: And now it's time for Empire to take their shots, you had to know it was coming as soon as Foolkiller came out onto the runway.
(Smash cut.) MELINN: Here comes Gold Kard! (Gold Kard sprints down the aisle, a black stick in his hand!) THOMAS: What's he got? MELINN: Gold Kard hitting the ring, and he jams that stick into the throat of Caine... it's a shockstick! He's got a shockstick! He hits
Foolkiller in the back, and the big man drops to the mat! (Gold Kard holds up the stick, triggering it so the camera can see the spark. He then gives DaVinci a dosage, and produces a spray paint can!)
MELINN: Gold Kard reviving his longtime friend and foe, Troy, and now both members of Millennium are lining up Empire!
(Troy and Kard get the men next to each other, on Caine they paint a "Y", on Foolkiller a "2", and on DaVinci a "K") (Johnny Starr is in the CWO Control Room.) STARR: Hey, fans, Johnny Starr here with you for your FEVER HOT FLASH, and that was of course a scene from last week's FEVER, where Millennium Man Gold
Kard did a number on two members of Empire, Foolkiller and Union Caine, but this week Gold Kard will collide with Foolkiller, and if you think the 6'9"
former World Champion won't be looking for revenge, you've got another think coming! (Back to footage from last week, as Super Bad Ass Sweet Daddy Jones is in the ring with the Foundation. Jones grabs the mike, much to the dislike of the fans.)
SUPER BAD ASS SWEET DADDY JONES: You know, I've been running around silent these past couple of weeks, hell, almost a month, brothers and sisters, opting to run the Sheets Foundation closer and closer toward true main event status. I had made a pledge to remain mostly silent about my crew's accomplishments until we became true title contenders. Well, something has come up that I feel needs to be addressed, and needs to be addressed now. That something, is the supposed "return" of Lester Sheets.
(Crowd pop.) JONES: Now I remember the huge ass heat that he could pull down for this little motley crew that stands behind me. But I ask you, did he do anything to get real gold around anyone's waist?
(The fans cheer, as they remember vividly the two Light Heavyweight title turns for SASD and the TV run for Popalov.)
JONES: Let me tell you a little something on your beloved Lester Sheets. The best thing that could have happened to me, these men in the ring, you fans, and the CWO in general, is that night that Sheets stepped over the line of "sports entertainment," and into the real world and insulted those bangers over three months ago. It landed him in a hospital bed, and put me in my rightful place, as leader of the Sheets Foundation. It took a chubby little white man to put together something that a strong, young black man could take to the top. And I know what's gonna happen when you get back
Lester. You're gonna try to take back what you think is yours. Les, you will not take what is mine, and that's control of the Foundation. You might as well come in and announce your retirement when you come back, fat man, because when and if you hit this ring, to make your return speech, I'm coming out with you here, and announcing the end of the Sheets Foundation, and the forming of the Jones Foundation.
(Smash cut. The crowd begins a chant, "Lester, Lester, Lester...") JONES (looks around with a smirk): You think he's coming out? No, he ain't here, he'll be here... NEXT WEEK.
MELINN: WHAT?!? Lester Sheets returns next week?!? (Smash cut.) JONES: On the 26th. And I'll be ready. See, this is a hostile takeover, and there's nothing he's gonna do about it, no no. Les has always had others fighting his fights for him. And there's no one here in CWO for him to turn to. And that's that. This is now the Jones Foundation, so you best live with that.
(Back to Johnny.) STARR: That big announcement, as indeed, this Thursday on FEVER, we will see the return of the 1998 CWO Gift of Gab and Manager of the Year winner Lester
Sheets! Should be interesting now that his Foundation has been taken over by Super Bad Ass Sweet Daddy Jones! Besides that, Foundation member Secret
Agent Super Dragon will be in action against Southern Dynamite. Also, the
Foundation needs a fourth man for its TEAM CHALLENGE '99 match against long time enemies the TRUE AMERICANS? Who's it going to be?
(Image of Darknight Express and Creatures of the Night comes up.) STARR V-O: Also on FEVER, in tag team action, it's going to be Darknight Express vs. the Creatures of the Night. (Image of Sandman Jr. and Man Mountain Marko comes up.) STARR V-O: How about this? A match between the hardcore icon and True
American Sandman Jr. against the man with the plan, Man Mountain Marko?
(Image of Geoff Bondavarsky and Wildfire comes up.) STARR V-O: Also, Marc Fulcrum's antics not withstanding, the Light Heavyweight title remains the belt to grab lately in the CWO, and this week on FEVER, Geoff Bondavarsky of the True Americans will try to do just that in a 2 OUT OF 3 FALLS match against the champion, Wildfire!
(Image of "The Avenger" Jesse Ewiak and Carr comes up.) STARR V-O: Speaking of teams that still need a fourth man, Brent "The Ace" Alles, Simon St. Severe, and "The Avenger" still need one, but that's not going to concern them tomorrow night, as Jesse has to go up against that monster of a man, Carr! The challenge was made last week, let's take a look.
(Footage is shown of Carr from last week's FEVER.) CARR: ...AS FOR "THE AVENGER"... LAST TIME... I TOOK YOUR PRIDE! THIS TIME... I AM GOING TO TAKE YOUR CAREER AND YOUR SOUL, BOY! YOU'RE GONNA GET
A CRASH COURSE IN PAIN! AND I'M NOT GOING TO WAIT UNTIL TEAM CHALLENGE '99,
NEXT WEEK... _BOY_... I AM GOING TO PUT YOU OUT OF YOUR MISERY!
(Back to Johnny.) STARR: Yeesh. That's one guy I never want to have mad at me. (Image of Myers and Fulcrum and World Tag Team Champions Reb and Fantastic come up. Reb and Fantastic have DaVinci by their side.)
STARR V-O: They've collided several times in the past months, but this week on FEVER it's going to be a TORNADO match between these two teams! That means all four men are allowed to fight at the same time, and falls count anywhere in the building! Should be a hot one!
(Image of Master of Darkness Damion and Gecko comes up. Mondo Gecko has
Tamara Wilcox by his side.)
STARR V-O: And finally, in our main event, it's former World Champion Master of Darkness Damion against the current CWO World Champion Gecko. Is Gecko crazy to accept this kind of a match days away from his big TEAM CHALLENGE
'99 match against Alles and his Aces? Tune in to find out!
(Back to Johnny.) STARR: All that and more, as FEVER is LIVE from the Phillips Arena in Atlanta, GA! Don't miss it! (Fade out.) http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5403/
