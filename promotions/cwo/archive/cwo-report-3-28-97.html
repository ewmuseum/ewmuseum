CWO REPORT 3/28/97

28 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The show opens up with the TOP and Jason "Flash" Varga in the interview area, along with their associates Jim "Big Guy" Peer and Russell Hill.) JOE EAGLE: All right, CWO, let's get down to business! The TOP is back, and ready to RULE THE WORLD! JASON "FLASH" VARGA: Oh yeah, Joe, you've got that right. Tomorrow night in the tournament we are gonna take cae of business! All the fans out there know that I'm the man, and Talon is gonna go home in a body cast! Maybe I'll even use a "chicken-wing" submission hold! Eh, eh, you get it? EAGLE: Yeah, whatever, Varga. Bane, you get the pleasure of a beating from the man of the hour, the King of the World, the one that used to be called "Upset Kid". Bane, you can forget about this tournament. It's over, my man. JIM "JAZZMAN" TAYLOR: As long as we're all here, let's talk about some people who are near and dear to our hearts. Hood Rats, you and Ganglord made a big mistake when you messed with us. If you want the full force of the TOP and our buddies against you, we can arrange it. Let's see... Joe Da Man, The Varganator, me and Jumbo, the Big Guy Peer, and maybe even our college buddies Schillo and Williams. You street punks will be horsemeat when we get done with you. Tell 'em, Jumbo! JUMBO JENKINS: Hood Rats... you wouldn't taste good on the grill... perhaps in a nice urban stew! EAGLE: CWO, you better be ready, 'cause the TOP and the Flash are here and READY! VARGA: Peace, out, and have a drink for me! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Friday, March 28, 1997. Last night the MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT continued in Phoenix, AZ, with the second round of the Down and Left Regionals. Let's see what happened! _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT DOWN AND LEFT REGIONALS - ROUND 2 PHOENIX, AZ 3/27/97 CWO Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers pinned one half of the CWO World Tag Team Champions Pit Boss #1 after the Inverted Powerbomb. Time of match: 29:58. David 'The Destroyer' Davis pinned Inferno after the falling headbutt. Time of match: 5:22. Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned Talon after the Flash Flood. Time of match: 9:56. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Hellhound after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 8:18. Chris Greco upset Wayne Schillo after the Flying Bodypress. Time of match: 6:29. Schillo was hit by a chair by Brent 'The Ace' Alles before Greco, unknowing of the attack, hit with the Flying Bodypress. Greco did not even get a chance to apologize, as Schillo immediately chased after Alles. Brad 'The Man' Couchman pinned Conor McCloud after the Piledriver. Time of match: 7:52. Clark Gray pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after Wayne Schillo hit Alles with a chair. Time of match: 19:54. Joe Eagle defeated CWO World Champion Bane by countout after outside interference by 'Monster' Rich Clark. Clark and Bane brawled back to the dressing room. Time of match: 14:19. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight is the Sweet 16 of the tournament, with all the regionals combining in St. Louis, MO for the quarterfinal round. Let's look at the complete lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT QUARTERFINALS ST. LOUIS, MO 3/28/97 Masher Maccabee vs. BYE Johnny Reb vs. Exile "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Night Slinger The Focus vs. Gold Kard CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Tiger Rogers vs. David "The Destroyer" Davis Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams Chris Greco vs. Brad "The Man" Couchman Clark Gray vs. Joe Eagle _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr voice-over, over a montage of action from the first SATURDAY NIGHT FIGHTIN'.) STARR: Don't forget about tomorrow night's premiere of SATURDAY NIGHT FIGHTIN', either. You got a sneak preview of this program a few weeks ago, and this is a forum where you will see top notch action in the wildest matches imaginable! No countouts, no disqualifications, SATURDAY NIGHT FIGHTIN' is not for the faint of heart. Don't miss it! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: One of the matches on SATURDAY NIGHT FIGHTIN' will be a non-title match involving World Champion Bane against Brent "The Ace" Alles. Both of these men have never met before in the CWO, and both personify intensity. We take you to comments from "The Ace." _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is in the interview area. His arms are crossed against his chest and his chin rests on his fist. He is in thought for a moment, then speaks.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: A lot of talk lately here in the CWO has been about monsters, monsters of the ring, 7'4" giants, raving lunatics, and cold, soulless men like yourself, Bane. You gave me a warning, fine. But my question is, Bane, what threat do you see from myself? What does a monster fear, Bane? Does he fear the cold reality of sanity? Because that is what I'm bringing into the ring when I take you on at SATURDAY NIGHT FIGHTIN'. The title's not on the line? Fine, I can understand that, I'm not ranked high enough. But what I'm going to teach you, Bane, is a little respect. You treat that belt like a piece of garbage, that's fine, I'm not so fond of this league myself. But what you will not do, Bane, is treat me like a piece of garbage, because Merciful Death notwithstanding, it can't compare to the greatest finisher in our sport, created and perfected by myself, the Allesbreaker. So bring it on, Bane, and when the smoke clears, and the darkness comes, I don't fear monsters, but I ask you again, Bane, what do you fear? _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Popular superstar Panther has been under fire for the past couple of weeks by the Alliance. However, he has found friends in the form of Southern Pride, Johnny Reb and Mason-Dixon. We go now to comments from the flamboyant Panther. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens up with the camera closing in on Panther in the interview area. You can see a very disturbed look on his face, he isn't wearing his normal flashy ring attire, but rather he is wearing black tights and black boots. He begins to speak in low voice.) PANTHER: I'm sure some of you are wondering why I have chosen to come out here without my vallets the Kittens, and why I'm dressed the way I am, well I plan to tell. It's been a strange couple of weeks for me, I finaly had the chance to bring home some gold for the fans here in the CWO. Well, I failed. Now, I could come out here and tell you why I lost, but that doesn't matter. The bottom line is still the same, the Light Heavyweight Belt still belongs to Tiger Rogers. Well, let me tell you something, Mr. Rogers... (Panther begins to raise his voice) YOU SURE ARE A BIG MAN! It takes a big man take have his buddy help him get a win. Well let me tell you something, I'm sure you already know. YOU ARE NOT MAN ENOUGH TO BEAT ME! I have a real simple solution to our problem. Let's take it to the cage! In fact let's take it to the cage and make it a little more interesting, let's put up the barbed-wire. One of us isn't going to walk out of that cage. That is, if you are man enough, Rogers. (Panther pauses and rubs his forehead.) PANTHER: Now as for you, Mr. Gold Kard, I'm not sure where you get your hidden agenda, but let me tell you something. I think you put up a pretty good talk, my friend, but let's see what you can do with Johnny Reb in the ring. As for you, Reb, we have had our differences in the past, but now comes the time for you raise your level and take it to Gold Kard. I'm not sure how much it means to you, but BROTHER I have got your back, because IT MEANS A LOT TO ME! Rogers, if you want to fight, then let's fight, but keep one thing in mind. YOU DON'T THROW ROCKS AT A MAN CARRYING A MACHINE GUN! (Panther throws down the microphone and walks away.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: A very determined young man in Panther, we'll have to see if Rogers does accept his challenge, and on what level that match will take place. However, a teammate of Tiger Rogers in the Alliance currently holds the CWO North American championship, and he is a bit perturbed these days as well. We take you now to Goliath. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Golliath is in a CWO dressing room along with Jimbo Jones) JIMBO JONES: There has been a deep injustice in the CWO... my man, the CWO North American champion, has been denied his rightful show at the World Championship. This man, the mammoth Goliath, was declared the monster of the ring, was declared the CWO North American Champion, and the man he pinned gets a World Title shot? There is one thing this man is after and that's the World Title. CWO executives, hear me now... there are no excuses, as the North American champion, Goliath is the automatic #1 contender, so sign the match NOW! GOLIATH: ARGGGGGGGGG! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Perhaps a Champions' Challenge or Belt vs. Belt match will take place soon between Bane and Goliath, but for now, Bane has his mind focused on "Monster" Rich Clark at ARMAGEDDON, and Goliath will be joining the Alliance, the Dark Side, and Doomsday as one side in the return of THE ROCK. Let's look at the entire lineup for this great PPV event, taking place next week in Baltimore, Maryland on Sunday, April 6. _______________________________________________________________________________ APRIL ARMAGEDDON (FEATURING THE RETURN OF THE ROCK) APRIL 6, 1997 "LIVE" FROM BALTIMORE MARYLAND 6:30 STARTING TIME THE ROCK: (Six steel cages, two random men from each team in each cage.
