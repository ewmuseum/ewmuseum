House Show Results - 4/3/99

April 4, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 4/3/99 Norfolk, VA (Scope Coliseum) Sandman Jr. defeated the Vegas Villain in 3:00. Simon St. Severe pinned Hollywood Haskins after the Cross Bearer. Time of match: 1:52. Man Mountain Marko pinned 'Assassin 64' Sean Holiday after the Chi-Town
Hammer, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 11:46.
Wrath pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after Alles broke out of the Repentence
Rack. Alles was too weak after that effort to kick out of a rollup, and
Wrath pulled the tights for good measure. Time of match: 9:34.
STREET FIGHT: In a match that went all over the place, Mondo Gecko finally pinned 'Smilin'' Doc Watson in the ring with a Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 7:45. FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH: Another match that went all over the place, Allen 'Mr. America' Carter and
Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky defeated Secret Agent Super Dragon and 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin when Allen 'Mr. America' Carter pinned
Secret Agent Super Dragon in the stands after Sandman Jr. hit Dragon with a
Singapore cane. Time of match: 12:32.
Ranger Taurus pinned Brian 'The King' Derks after the tilt-a-whirl slam. Time of match: 4:39. Taurus was attacked by Smilin' Doc Watson after the match. WARGAMES: The first wrestlers to enter Wargames were Johnny Reb and Sandman, who had to stay in for 5 minutes. The coin toss was made. The Dark Side got the advantage! Fallen entered the cage, and the Dark Side enjoyed a two on one advantage for two minutes. Then, Lord Hile Troy entered the cage. Troy and
Reb brawled with Sandman and the Fallen for two minutes, and then surprisingly the World Champion Darkness entered to give the Dark Side a 3 on 2 advantage! That continued for two minutes, then 'Irresistible
Assassin' Eddie Fantastic entered the cage to even the odds. Troy, Reb, and
Fantastic brawled with Darkness, Sandman, and the Fallen for two minutes, and then Warhawk entered the cage. 4 on 3 advantage for 2 minutes, and then finally the big man got in the cage, Foolkiller! The Match beyond began, and both teams turned it up a notch. Finally though, it was Wrath who came down to ringside and handed the Fallen a rolled up rag with what was probably chloroform on it. Fallen used it on Johnny Reb to knock him out, and then put him in a headscissors for appearance sake. The ref, seeing Reb not moving, called for the match to end. The Dark Side were unfortunately the winners! As the cage was lifted, both teams continued brawling, and eventually Troy, Fantastic, and Foolkiller were able to clear the ring, as
Foolkiller nailed a Thunderfunk on Darkness! The show ended on Fantastic and DaVinci tending to Reb, and Troy and Foolkiller challenging the Dark
Side to get back in the ring as the crowd went nuts. Time of match: 24:04.
