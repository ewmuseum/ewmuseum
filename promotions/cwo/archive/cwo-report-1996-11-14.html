CWO REPORT 11/14/96

14 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: What up, dudes? Ganglord, I saw what you did to Tub-O-Lard on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, beating his leg with a chair. WELL GET READY TO RUMBLE, BECAUSE I AM GONNA KICK YOUR @$$, GANGLORD! Ganglord, you seem to be great on jumping guys from behind, beating them with chairs and breaking all the rules. Well, I am gonna fly through the air and knock you out of your boots. Ganglord, get ready for a war! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens to see Ganglord and T.A.S. celebrating after their win against Tub-O-Blub.) GANGLORD: See, T, I told ya I had it in me, I took that fat piece of lard down in three minutes, man, I put dad fat boy down several times. Now Dat be power. T. A. S.: Yeah, man, now next we got's to face Dat Geeko guy or whatever. Man, let's do da same to dat little punk. GANGLORD: No Problem, dog, then after I put dat punk to rest I got a little score to settle wit' Akillies and those punk friends of his, they should have let me finish da job on fat boy, now dey gonna havta PAY! T. A. S.: Dat dey will, dat dey will, but first I gots a present to celebrate your first win here in da CWO.... (T. A. S. opens the door and 3 scantily-clad ladies walk in.) LADIES (seductively): Hey, G! GANGLORD (to camera): I thinks it's time for you to leave.... (Camera fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, November 14. You just heard Mondo Gecko's comments about Ganglord's actions against Tub-O-Blub, we take you back to this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER to witness that brutal attack. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Footage from 11/13/96 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER is shown.) MELINN: Wait a minute... Ganglord is not stopping... he's dragging Tub-O-Blub over to the corner, draping his leg over the ropes, what's he going to... NO! Ganglord coming off of the turnbuckle, right on to Tub-O-Blub's knee! THOMAS: He wants to break it open and find if it really has a creamy nougat center! MELINN: Shut up! I... oh no... Ganglord's got a chair... Ganglord going up top... coming off the top turnbuckle with it... he nails Blub's knee with it! Blub is hurt, writhing in pain! Wait a minute... here comes Akillies and Team Blitzkrieg, Kamikaze and Mysteria! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Akillies and Team Blitzkrieg were enough to keep Ganglord at bay until medical attention could be received. Tub-O-Blub was taken to a nearby hospital, and we go to comments from a Dr. John Rupke, Blub's personal physician. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Dr. John Rupke is in his office.) DR. JOHN RUPKE (pointing to an X-ray): As you can see, Tub-O-Blub suffered torn cartilage in his right knee. If I can speak frankly, due to his huge weight, Tub-O-Blub is pretty hard on his knees anyway, and the attack by Ganglord aggravated what was already an unhealthy situation. We are treating Tub-O-Blub, and putting him on a diet to reduce his weight a bit for health reasons. We are not sure when he'll be able to return to the CWO, but as his personal physician, I will keep you posted. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Thank you, Dr. Rupke. Anyway, on to brighter news. Last night the superstars of the CWO were in the city that never sleeps, Las Vegas, NV, putting on a great show for those people taking a break from the gambling. Unfortunately, Vegas Showgirl was nowhere to be found. Anyway, let's look at the results of that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 1996 LAS VEGAS, NV Ganglord vs. Mondo Gecko Gecko pinned Ganglord after the Top Rope Frankensteiner in 13:56. TEXAS DEATH: David & Goliath vs. Deadly Sins David & Goliath defeated the Deadly Sins when Sloth failed to make a ten count after David "The Destroyer" Davis nailed him with the Destroyer Dropper (flip off top rope) in 21:56. Sin vs. Bane Sin defeated Bane by disqualification in 12:45 when Bane had the Merciful Death on Sin and he was in the ropes, Bane refused to release the hold before a five count and was disqualified. After this, Bane attacked the referee, who had to be taken out on a stretcher. Wildfire vs Steel Wildfire and Steel wrestled to a double disqualification in 6:52. Mad Doctor's Ghost vs. Joe Eagle MDG pinned Eagle with the Sudden Death splash in 10:11. At the urging of Don King and Gold Kard, MDG then delivered several more splashes to Eagle, requiring the Philadelphia native to be taken out on a stretcher. Tiger Rogers vs. Battlecat Rogers pinned Battlecat in 9:16 with the Inverted Power Bomb. Steve Keller vs. Panther Panther made Keller submit to the Eclipse (combination Oriental thumb and sleeper) in 6:04. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Southern Pride (Johnny Reb and Mason-Dixon) vs. The Dark Side (Hellhound and the Creatures of the Night) The Dark Side defeated Southern Pride when Night Slinger pinned Beauregard Dixon in 14:53. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (table match): Miguel Sanchez vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Sanchez defeated Alles in 7:55, winning the title, when he Moonsaulted Alles through a table. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, steel cage): Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. "Big" Rich Clark Schillo defeated Clark, escaping the cage in 15:17 after nailing him with a Keuka Crack (kick to the chin). _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see, we have a new Television Champion after last night. Miguel Sanchez defeated Brent "The Ace" Alles last night in a "Table Match," in which the stipulation that the first man to put the other man through a table won the match and the title. Now, next week on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, we were supposed to have a match for the Television Championship between Johnny Reb and Brent "The Ace" Alles in a cage. However, CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson has decided to now make the match a TRIANGLE CAGE match between Alles, Reb, and the champion Sanchez. That should be intense! Taking the place of Sanchez in his match against Hangman on FEVER will be Sanchez's associate, Savior from the Biblical Powers. We now go to a reaction from Alles: _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is in the locker room, having his head bandaged from the match.) ALLES: All right, Sanchez, you want to play dirty? I'll show you dirty. I've been so concerned with driving those idiots Eagle and Reb into the ground, maybe I overlooked you. But come next Tuesday, you and that Foghorn Leghorn wannabe will see why they call me the Ace, because I'll be the only one coming out of that cage alive. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Well, tonight we are in Phoenix, AZ, let's take a look at the card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 1996 PHOENIX, AZ Hellhound vs. "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Baby Waldo vs. Miguel Sanchez Pair of Aces (Flyboy and Clark Gray) vs. Lafayette and Rooster STREET FIGHT: Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson vs. Bane Deadly Sins vs. Tower of Power NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Highlanders vs. the Pit Bosses WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Biblical Powers vs. The Creatures of the Night WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: "Big" Rich Clark vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Get ready for a brawl on this one, Phoenix fans, as the most intense force in the CWO, Bane, collides with the rowdy and unorthodox Ron "Rude Guy" Johnson of the Bad Guys in a Street Fight. We go to comments from Bane. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Setting: In a seedy looking bar. The lights are turned down low, and the place is torn apart. Bane is standing there in the middle. About him lay the bruised and bleeding bodies of all the patrons.) BANE (anger in his voice): So another member of the Tower of Flour wants me. You weak, stupid fool! Rude Guy, you have a rude awakening in your future. That obese loser Joe Eagle had to drink his Bane off his mind, as well as you and your disgusting beer drinking partner. You are so weak that you need alcohol in your life so you can escape from reality. I AM reality. Just like my father, you need alcohol to live. Well his Bane came to him, just as it will come to you. You believe that you are a bad guy, and fancy yourself to be hard because you were in jail for not paying your parking tickets. I mock you. Go back to jail and hide, Rude Guy. May be there you will be safe, but may be not. No walls can hold me in or keep me out. I am unstoppable, and I shall have you as my next victim. Your life's term is up Rude Guy. Your Bane has arrived. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Of course the big match on the card pits CWO World champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo against the 7'4", 481 pound "Big" Rich Clark. Last night in Las Vegas, Schillo managed to narrowly escape from the cage after stunning Clark with the Keuka Crack. Tonight, though, Schillo has nowhere to escape, he must take Clark on head to head. We know the type of person Schillo is, he's not going to run and hide, he's not going to try and get himself disqualified, he must beat the giant. Can Schillo nail the Keuka Crack and protect his title from the behemoth? We'll have to see. Now, though, we go to a piece of footage we have received of Lord Foul at his home in Blackpool, England. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Lord Foul sits in his chambers with his brother.) LORD FOUL: You know, I sit here, and through the privilege and power of my satellite dish, I watch affairs from afar, and the CWO is going to hell and back... BERIK TROY: Gold Kard International is putting prices on people's head, and that head would be YOURS, if I hadn't called you back here. LORD FOUL: A very good point indeed... we both saw what happened to Sandman, being pitched off of the top of the arena, and crashing to the cement... BERIK TROY: Yeah, and that could have been you that this psycho Bane chucked off the roof, we'll send your former friend a nice fruitbasket, perhaps a card. LORD FOUL: Then you've got Joe Eagle and Schillo and others, trying to find a leader to go against the GKI... BERIK TROY: See... you see what happens when you leave the league? It crashes, Alles was right, without you these leagues all crash, you know if you were their, they'd be calling you out to go defend the honor of the league. LORD FOUL: I... mm, yeah... unfortunately for the fans and the victims, I am in rehabilitation, back home where I belong. They always talk about Lord Foul's School of Wrestling, well, this IS Lord Foul's school of wrestling. BERIK TROY: The question the CWO wants to know, and keeps asking over and over, is when are you going to grant them an interview on camera so they can ask the HARD questions? LORD FOUL: Only you and I know that, but it will be soon enough, and let me remind you ALL, that you might think your running the league, that your the number one contender, well let me remind you, who was number two or higher for six periods in a row? BERIK TROY: The future CWO Heavyweight champion of the world, that's who! (Fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally, a newcomer to the CWO has arrived, and his name is Wildfire. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire is in the interview area, sitting really calmly.) WILDFIRE: My name is Wildfire! I was just sitting back with the lovely Miss Amber.... (Amber waves to the camera.) WILDFIRE: Watching TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. (Wildfire's expression changes and he starts yelling.) GANGLORD! You disgust me! What you did was bogus. you almost ended a great wrestler's career. Well I would like to make a public challenge! I want you in the ring next week. I want you to try and do the same thing to me! I just want you to remember one thing. I want you to remember that if you play with fire, you're gonna get burned! (Amber jumps into the picture.) WILDFIRE: And Ganglord, you tell T. A. S. that if he trys to interfere he's gonna be on the receiving end of a FIREBALL! (The camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today.
