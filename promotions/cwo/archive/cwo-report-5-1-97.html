CWO REPORT 5/1/97

1 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Show opens with Mondo Gecko and the Regulators in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Brent "The Ace" Alles, how are you and the tramp doing these days? It must irk the hell out of you that I am one of the only men you can't play mind games with and intimidate. Bottom line, "Ace", is that I know why you were picked on in school, because you were a weenie, a loser who wet the bed, played with dolls, and ate his boogers. So now you are pissed off with everybody else and you are trying to play your mind games with me. Brent Alles, soon, at May-Hem, I will win the battle royal and in doing so I will destroy you and get rid of the stench in the CWO. So, Brent Alles, get ready for mondo extreme action, baby, and by the way, tell your little slut to stop groping me and calling me. We all know she wants a REAL MAN but tell her to find some other dog or something, SEE YA, *****! REGULATOR ICE: Hood Rats, you guys are the vermin of the CWO and it is time for the Regulators to destroy you two goofs once and for all. You guys say you have had it tough hanging in the ghetto and you will punk us. Hey, we grew up in the mean streets of Detoit where anything goes and the "Hitman" Hearns rules! So boys, get ready for war because when the Regulators run over you they are going to have to use dental records to ID you two geeks. REGULATOR FIRE: Hood Rats, you guys are ugly, stupid and pathetic and we can't wait to destroy you two once and for all. It is time for the Regulators to win titles and clean up the CWO. Hood Rats, you guys are what's wrong about the CWO, punk gang members who think they are tough. Well, punks, get ready because you two are going to be on the receiving end of TOTAL REGULATION! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, May 1, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. We are only 3 days away from what is quickly becoming one of the most talked-about PPV events the CWO has ever had, of course I'm talking about MAY-HEM, which not only has the 60-man, two ring battle royal, but also the explosive World title match between the Monsters of the Ring, Bane, "Monster" Rich Clark, and Goliath. Up until then, we have some great house shows this week, like the one in Erie, PA, last night, let's see what went down in Erie! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/30/97 Erie, PA Ganglord and Sandman Jr. wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Masters of Hardcore defeated Creatures of the Night when Brad 'The Man' Couchman pinned Night Crawler after the Hardcore Bomb. Time of match: 7:16. Mondo Gecko pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 11:04. Da Hood Rats defeated Regulators when Nate pinned Regulator Fire after the Gangsta Drop. Time of match: 20:44. TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS MATCH: Brian 'The Hammer' Williams defeated Masher Maccabee, 2 falls to 0 . Brian 'The Hammer' Williams won fall 1 in 5:04 after the Keuka Smack. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams won fall 2 in 8:19 after the kneedrop. CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Moses pinned Tiger Rogers after the The Commandment. Time of match: 16:33. Moses won the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion title. Goliath made 'Monster' Rich Clark submit to the Goliath Grappler after outside interference by Bane. Time of match: 12:52. Bane made Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo submit to the Merciful Death after outside interference by Brent "The Ace" Alles. Time of match: 7:28. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see, Moses captured the CWO Light Heavyweight title for a second time last night in Erie, however, Tiger Rogers still has a shot at title gold this weekend at MAY-HEM, where he will be going after the North American championship currently held by Jason "Flash" Varga. Moses remains one of the 60 competitors involved in the MAY-HEM battle royal. Let's take a look at tonight's House Show lineup, in Cleveland, OH! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW 5/1/97 Cleveland, OH Wild Ones (Mondo Gecko and El Grande Pistolerro) vs. Coldfront 8-MAN TAG MATCH: Alliance (Tiger Rogers, Goliath, Inferno, David "The Destroyer" Davis) vs. Gold Kard International (Gold Kard, Masher Maccabee, David "The Truth" Thomas, Mysterious Masked Man) Da Hood Rats vs. Deadly Sins Joe Eagle vs. Wrath The Big Cool vs. Creatures of the Night Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. The Focus NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, street fight): Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) vs. Bad Guys (champions) WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: College Guys (Jason "Flash" Varga, Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, Brian "The Hammer" Williams) vs. Southern Pride (Johnny Reb and Mason-Dixon) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Now let's take a look at the lineup for this Sunday's PPV, MAY-HEM. _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO MAY-HEM SUNDAY, MAY 4, 1997 "LIVE" FROM THE MOLSON CENTRE, MONTREAL, QUEBEC, CANADA 6:30 PM WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: (One ring surrounded by a steel cage which two men start out in, one man must handcuff the other to the cage and then face the other man in the other ring): Bane vs. "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Goliath (w/Jimbo Jones) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Highlanders vs. Pit Bosses (champions) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Tiger Rogers (w/Jimbo Jones) vs. Jason "Flash" Varga (champion, w/Jim Peer) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mercenaries (w/Maximum Overdrive) vs. Bad Guys (champions, w/Herbie Versmells) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb (w/General Lee) vs. Inferno (champion, w/Jimbo Jones) 60-MAN MAY-HEM BATTLE ROYAL - WINNER GETS WORLD TITLE SHOT AT SUMMER HEAT (Two men start out in each ring, one more man is added to each ring every two minutes) PARTICIPANTS: "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher "Mr. Extreme" Chris Ohlhoff Akillies Arlon Flaym Baby Waldo Beauregard Dixon Benjamin Blake Bentley Bloodbath Brad "The Man" Couchman Brent "The Ace" Alles Brian "The Hammer" Williams Centurion Chris Greco Clark Gray Dark Raven David "The Destroyer" Davis Diamondback Chris Meyers El Grande Pistolerro Exile Exodus Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil Ganglord Gold Kard Hellhound Jermain Justice Jerome Justice Jim "Jazzman" Taylor Joe Eagle Jumbo Jenkins Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung Masher Maccabee Master of Darkness Damion Matsuhiro Torunaka (from Super Japan) Matthew "The Man" White Maximum Overdrive Mondo Gecko Moses Nate Night Crawler Night Slinger Panther Pride Regulator Fire Regulator Ice Sandman Jr. Serpentmaster Sloth Spartacus Steel Steve "Mr. Niceguy" White Steve Keller Stonewall Mason The Focus Tommy Lightning Vlad the Impaler Warren Wayne "College Guy" Schillo WILD CARD Wildfire Wrath EYE ON THE STORM: LOTTERY MATCH (two random teams of two, winners come down last in MAY-HEM, losers go out first) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Before we leave today, let's get some comments from the Wild Side! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire is standing in the locker room with a bent steel chair, Miss Amber beside him.) WILDFIRE: I told you, Bad Guys! I told you that you would get the message. Now you cant dodge us anymore! (Wildfire picks up a magazine, the headline is CWO Ratings.) WILDFIRE: We are now number two in the rankings, and we want our rematch. As a matter of fact, we want it on Tuesday Night Fever following the May-Hem! We are the uncrowned North American Tag Team Champions and nothing is gonna stop us from gaining what belongs to us! (The room goes dark, there is a burst of fire and then the figure of Bloodbath stands before the camera.) BLOODBATH (dark and mysterious): Bad Guys, you have been running from us ever since we were screwed out of those titles! Well, last night on Tuesday Night Fever you got the message! Like Wildfire said, we are the number two contenders! We will get our rematch, and when we do, we will complete our quest and be crowned with the North American Tag Titles! And when we're through with you, your souls will then REST IN PEACE.... (There is a burst of flames as the lights come back on and Bloodbath has vanished.) MISS AMBER: What ya gonna do when the uncrowned North American Tag Champions, WILD SIDE, runs WILD on you! (The camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
