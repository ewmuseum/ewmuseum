CWO REPORT 12/6/96

6 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The CNN News logo is seen on screen and the theme music is heard playing. The coverage of another bizarre crime has made its way to the national media. Covering the incident is ever ready Wolf Blitzer.) BLITZER (professional as always): Good evening, everyone. This is Wolf Blitzer, reporting live from Atlanta Georgia where a strange event has happened here at the Federal Court House. (Behind Wolf the court house can be seen. Much activity is taking place, and police are all over the scene.) BLITZER: As you can see, police are on the scene of this happening, as well as some fire fighters, who had to put out the lawn of the Court House. It seems that someone sprayed a flammable substance on the lawn and ignited it. The burning grass spelled out a distinct message. It read: "Beware the fury of the patient man." This eerie message was left by a man caught on surveilance camera. Lets take you to some of the footage. (Some footage is shown of a huge masked individual. The quality is poor, and it has poor lighting. The figure can be seen igniting the lawn.) BLITZER: Guards working the graveyard shift came out to investigate, and were then assaulted by this strange man. Both were severely beaten until unconsciousness, and both had to be taken to the local hospital where they remain in critical condition. As you saw by the video footage, the perpetrator was a hulking male. It may very well be the same individual known as Bane who is suspected as of yesterday for the crimes committed in Florida. At present this man is still at large, and his whereabouts are totally unknown. Federal investigators have been called in to probe this situation which is quickly growing out of control. This is Wolf Blitzer reporting live at Atlanta Georgia. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, December 6, 1996. As you just saw, the Bane situation seems to grow every day, the CWO PR team has a nightmare on their hands. However, as we've said before, the show goes on in the CWO, and great action was had last night in Portland, OR, let's look at how the card went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT THURSDAY, DECEMBER 5, 1996 PORTLAND, OR Johnny Reb vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart "Master of Pain" Barry Hart pinned Johnny Reb in 13:06 after the Cardiac Arrest (shooting star press). Maximum Overdrive vs. Dark Raven Dark Raven pinned Maximum Overdrive in 11:08 after the Death From Above flying dropkick. Baby Waldo vs. Hile Troy Baby Waldo and Hile Troy wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Baby Waldo had the Cradle Rocker on Foul when the bell rang for the time limit, Waldo refused to let go, until CWO officials managed to get him off of Troy. Mercenaries vs. Gods of Thunder Gods of Thunder defeated Mercenaries in 13:59 when AC pinned Kaptain Krunch after the Lightning Spike (double Tombstone piledriver on a chair). SPIN THE WHEEL (submission match): The Biblical Powers vs. The Bad Guys The Bad Guys defeated the Biblical Powers when Savior submitted to a sleeperhold by Joe "The Mean Guy" Rucker in 18:33. Herbie Versmells, the parole officer/manager of the Bad Guys, added leverage to the hold for the win. Jackass Joel vs. Goliath Goliath made Jackass Joel submit to the Goliath Grappler (horizontal backbreaker hold) in 9:06. After the match, Goliath refused to let the hold go, prompting Hoss and Little Joe to make the save. Phantom Freightrain vs. Panther Panther made Phantom Freightrain submit to the Eclipse (Oriental thumb and sleeper) in 11:28. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Hoss and Little Joe vs. Pit Bosses Pit Bosses defeated Hoss and Little Joe when Pit Boss #1 pinned Hoss in 8:31. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (Texas Death): "Big" Rich Clark vs. Master of Darkness Damion (champion) "Big" Rich Clark defeated Master of Darkness Damion in 23:23 when Damion failed to make a 10 count after a Choke Slam. However, Gold Kard then came to ringside and said that since "Big" Rich Clark did not have the words "title match" on his contract, that the title switch was invalid. Clark then Choke Slammed Gold Kard to the cheers of the crowd. STEEL CAGE: Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. Mad Doctor's Ghost Mad Doctor's Ghost escaped the cage in 17:22 after a Sudden Death Splash on Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo. After the match, MDG was attacked by Brent "The Ace" Alles. Alles injured Mad Doctor's Ghost, who had to be taken away on a stretcher. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we will be in Pierre, SD, let's look at the card for there. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FRIDAY, DECEMBER 6, 1996 PIERRE, SD STREET FIGHT: Bad Guys vs. Lafayette and Rooster Mad Doctor's Ghost vs. "Big" Rich Clark Mason-Dixon vs. Mondo Gecko and Battlecat Sandman Jr. vs. Rooster Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Hile Troy Flame Spirits vs. Team Blitzkrieg LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Talon vs. Dark Raven (champion) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (Spin the Wheel, non-title): Miguel Sanchez (champion) vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Sandman vs. Gold Kard _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: It is unknown at this time whether or not Mad Doctor's Ghost will be able to wrestle "Big" Rich Clark tonight, as the brutal attack by Brent "The Ace" Alles on him left him injured in a still as yet unknown capacity. We will keep you posted as we receive information. We have comments from "The Ace", though, we take you to them now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles sits on a highway overpass, as the cars stream through on both sides. He is alone with his thoughts for a moment, then turns to the camera. A sign reading "EXIT 77 - 84th Street - Byron Center" can be seen in the distance.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: Byron Center, MI. The place where it all started. The place where I was born and raised. Whenever things get hectic, this is where I come to try and sort it out. (Camera dissolves to Alles walking down a quiet country street, 104th street, to be exact.) ALLES: You know, since Tuesday night, things have been raging in my brain. My dreams... my dreams, well, they're never going to be fulfilled now. I wanted the CM2WF back so badly that I blinded myself to the company I kept. Now, I can't trust anybody. Wayne Schillo, Hile Troy, I know you want my help against Gold Kard International, but sorry, I can't trust you two either, especially you, Troy. The only one I can trust is myself. I am the Clique of One, I don't need a team, I don't need an alliance, I don't need anything but myself. And at CWO CELEBRATION, I will be wrestling under the CWO banner, and maybe after that I'll be wrestling under the CM2WF banner. It doesn't matter to me. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart, why don't you ask Mad Doctor's Ghost about how much of a pushover I am? He may never walk again! I make no apologies for that. I suffered the ultimate humiliation on Tuesday night when that fat piece of nothing covered me for a "pinfall." It won't happen again. Schillo, Troy, who knows if you can get the job done against GKI, but let me tell you, if there's anyone who knows that organization, it's me, and "Master of Pain" Barry Hart, you can talk about your @$$ all you want, I will KICK IT back to Butte, MT, when we meet up in the ring. You are the first step, the "crown jewel" of GKI, the former World Champion. After it's all said and done, and you've felt your upper vertebrae crack and your tendons rip from the Allesbreaker, there won't be a Cardiac Arrest, but you will be going to the hospital. The face card is showing, Hart, and you're about to draw an Ace. CO1, coming at you. (Camera fades.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now go to comments from other CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Creatures of the Night and Hellhound. Hellhound seems to be playing with something in the background behind the Creatures who come forward to talk.) NIGHT SLINGER: Well, boys, you wanted it and now you got it. Blood Inc., you wanted another chance at our titles, you got it, and you wanted it in a steel cage, you got that too. The one thing you will not get are these belts. You see we are the best team in this federation and no one has been able to stop us, not even you. NIGHT CRAWLER: Your first mistake was coming back for another round of severe beatings. Your last mistake is and will be wanting to get into the steel cage with us. Panther and D. E. A. T. H. DEAD is certainly what you may very well be after we get finished with you. (Laughing can be heard in the back and it is coming from Hellhound who is still playing with something.) NIGHT CRAWLER (to Hellhound): What the hell are you doing? HELLHOUND: Me and my pet [his 6-man title belt] are just play wrestling, I was showing it what I will do to these fools who want to take away my pet. (To his belt:) Don't worry, I will not let anyone take you away, you are mine and will always be mine. Blood Inc., no one will ever take away my pet. It is mine, like I am always the master's pet. NIGHT SLINGER: Tuesday night, Panther and D. E. A. T. H., will be the last time you get a chance at our titles, we will finally get rid of you pests. Our night will engulf you and the three of you shall be lost forever. We will deliver all of your souls to the master and he will reward us for it. NIGHT CRAWLER: Boys, I don't think you really thought out this little plan of yours. This big beast named Hellhound has proven all along that he can stand toe to toe with anyone in the fed and you heard him: when it comes to his belt, nobody better lay a hand on it or they suffer the consequences. ALL THREE: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera fades as Hellhound begins to roll around with his belt again.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens up on Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo in the Dorm Room Discussion area.) SCHILLO: Hello again, fans! Today, I come to you with great news. Remember MDG's former manager, Brian Williams? Well, he's my college roommate, and he's been watching what has been happening in the CWO. While he's happy enough with his retirement, he can no longer sit back and watch me come back to our dorm night after night after being ganged up on by GKI and Damion's group. So, without further delay, please welcome back to the CWO... Brian "The Hammer" Williams! (Brian Williams, 5'10, 190, comes out wearing a Keuka College T-Shirt and warmup pants.) WILLIAMS: It's good to be back, Wayne. When I was in the CWO before, I was a manager, but I think now I want to have a more "active" role in what goes on. SCHILLO: You mean, you didn't come here to manage me? You came to wrestle?? WILLIAMS: Yep. SCHILLO: CAN YOU BELIEVE IT! Fans, this man has trained with me since we were very young! He taught me how to fight! This is unbelievable! WILLIAMS: There still may only be two of us against everybody else, but two is better than one, and I plan on doing some damage against my competition in the CWO. SCHILLO: GKI, Damion! DO YOU HEAR THIS? Now there are TWO GUYS FROM KEUKA! You can no longer gang up on just me - you've gotta deal with "The Hammer" too! WILLIAMS: That's right, Wayne! When people gang up on you, I hunt them down and settle the score! I get you every apology you have coming to you! I'm "The Hammer!" YOU CALL, I HAMMER! SCHILLO: Look out, CWO - this man is on a mission! BOTH: FIVE TO ONE!! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb is in a wrestling ring with Stonewall Mason. Rebel Yell is on the outside with General Lee shouting commands.) YELL: That ain't gonna get it done next Sunday! Do it again. Good job Stonewall. Don't let up! (Reb and Mason lock up, Reb whips Mason into the ropes and then hits with the Rebel Rollup.) YELL: Bettuh, but it still ain't crisp. Let's work on the Clovuhleaf. Stonewall, remembuh that countuh! (Mason lies in position on the mat. Reb goes to lock on the Texas Cloverleaf, but gets caught ducking his head as Mason manages a small package rollup.) YELL: "Bad mistake Johnny...again." (Mason assumes the position, this time Reb avoids the small package but struggles turning the hold over on the larger Mason.) YELL: Gotta power ovuh Johnny...that tub-a-guts Damion ain't much smalluh than Stonewall. Lookin' bettuh. Take five. (To the camera:) The tahm is gettin' close at hand. Believe you me, Johnny Reb will have the kinks worked out. Johnny Reb will be polished... Johnny Reb will be Champion of the World! REB: Come on, Stonewall. Lets see ya reverse ol' 'Lights Out'! MASON (smiling): Let's do it, kid! _______________________________________________________________________________ (The TOP are in the middle of a heated arguement. Joe Eagle and Russ Hill are in the middle of it. Vince Manocelli, Jazzman Taylor, Jumbo Jenkins, and an unidentified woman are in the room as well.) JOE EAGLE: Russ, we didn't need you to beat GKI! Quite frankly, things are not going as well as they had been. RUSSELL HILL: Well, Joe, you know you are welcome to do as you please. But, remember one thing... I still hold your contract. You are bound to me as long as you are in the CWO! WOMAN (to Jazzman): Does this happen all the time? JIM "JAZZMAN" TAYLOR: They've always argued. I've never seen Joe like this before, though. He said he's really tired of being booed all the time. EAGLE: Hill, you're scum. Things were going so good, then you and Alles had your "grand idea"... HILL: Don't give me that s#!t, Eagle! You wanted this as much as I did! EAGLE: You're worthless. Come on, babe, let's go. VINCENT "THE LIP" MANOCELLI: What about this weekend, Joe? You still wrestling? EAGLE: Yeah, I'll be there. Oh, Russ... I forgot to mention, did you ever read the fine print we added to the contracts? Take a look right now, OK? HILL (reading the contract): "The contract shall become null and void if all members of the stable agree to leave Russell Hill Management, inc." So what, Eagle? Just because you're leaving doesn't mean they will. Right, guys? (Eagle leaves the room, laughing and with his woman latched to his arm. Manocelli follows quickly after him. Hill looks at The Big Cool, almost pleading with them to stay. Jazzman walks out of the room. Jumbo stops and watches the procession, looking as if he will stay. Just as Hill begins to smile and relax, Jumbo grabs a sandwich, walks out, and closes the door behind him.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: You sons of b****es... YOU SONS OF B****ES! What the hell do you think you're doing?? SLOTH: Apocalypse, imitation Bane, you b******s think you're funny? Well, two things you didn't count on. 1) An angry Deadly Sins team getting angrier, and 2) I highly doubt you're enjoying Lust's company! This will cost you all DEARLY! I don't know what your deal is, but it won't get you past us! PRIDE: We're gonna get you sons of b****es! If Lust doesn't kill you herself first! Oh yeah, Mondo Gecko, BattleCat... we're gonna take ALL THIS AGGRESSION OUT ON YOU! Be prepared! SLOTH: And Sin, you want to tag with us? More than happy, but just hold on until we kick the hell out of those scumbags Apocalypse... there's gonna be HELL to pay! And Pit Bosses, we haven't forgotten you either! We're getting the belts, we're getting the girl, and we're the best damned tag team this federation has ever seen! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the locker room after his loss to Cake Boy.) SANDMAN JR.: ARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRGH! I can't believe I lost to that fa... KELLEN: SANDMAN! You're not like that anymore, remember? SANDMAN JR. (through gritted teeth): ...that fast, talented newcomer Cake Boy... (unclenches teeth) I'm telling you, I think that's Ricky Kidd, that fla... KELLEN: JUNIOR! SANDMAN JR.: Oh, right... anyway, Rooster, you're the next guy on the "Reformed Junior" tour... we were friends and enemies both in CM2WF, and it's pure enemy from here on in... Hile Troy, I know you're back, but I'll be damned if I'm gonna be one of the guys you take out on your own little comeback tour... I am SANDMAN JR.! And you're gonna feel pain if you think you're gonna win.... then, Tuesday Night Fever... me and Exile... Exile, prepare to visit Parts Unknown, cause I'm gonna make you run there screaming.... KELLEN: You also get to take on Cake Boy again at CWO Celebration... SANDMAN JR.: YES! Cake Boy, you got a big BIG upset, it will NEVER EVER EVER EVER happen again, no matter where you place your hands! KELLEN: JUNIOR! SANDMAN JR.: Damn, sorry... _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens up on a Las Vegas casino. A nerdy patron hits the jackpot on a slot machine.) NERDY PATRON: Oh my god! I won! I won! (The Pit Bosses come over.) PIT BOSS #1: I'm sorry sir, that was an illegal transaction. I'm afraid we'll have to confiscate your "winnings." NERDY PATRON: What? What do you mean... I won... (Pit Boss #2 takes the nerdy patron and smashes his face into the slot machine. The nerdy patron slumps to the ground, bleeding.) PIT BOSS #2: Well, that's that. (He begins scooping up the quarters.) PIT BOSS #1: Deadly Sins, you'd better wake up... we don't give a hoot and a holler who kidnapped that slutty manager of yours... hey, Boss 2, do you remember seein' her on the strip a couple of months ago? PIT BOSS #2: Indeed I do. Had to lower her prices just to get some business. A 2-for-1 sale, if I can remember correctly. Pride and Sloth must have taken her up on it. PIT BOSS #1: Anyway, all we've heard since being in this league is "Deadly Sins this... Deadly Sins that...." Well, your records aren't that priceless, but I guess the CWO bigwigs figure you're "talented" enough to take on the Bosses. PIT BOSS #2: We are the most dominant team in this organization. Ask the fat Samoans, they're gone now. Ask the Creatures of the Night, we've beaten them before, hell, we should be wearin' both the belts, if you ask me. PIT BOSS #1: That's OK, though, because we're more than happy to wear these belts, and you know what? We're going to keep on wearing them until we're sick of wearing them, then we'll beat those powderpuff Creatures and become the WORLD Tag Team Champions. PIT BOSS #2: Deadly Sins, keep your minds on business, because that's what our minds are ALWAYS on. Sloth, you fat piece of trash, or Pride, you pretty mama's boy, remember this: you are only one Spike Piledriver away from being sent to Butterworth Hospital. PIT BOSS #1: Best damn tag finisher in the sport. Screw the Dogs of Blunder, screw Mason-Dixie, our's is the best! And Deadly Sins, you'll find that out at CWO CELEBRATION. (Nerdy patron struggles to his feet, groaning. The Pit Bosses proceed to stomp on him as the camera fades.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
