CWO REPORT SPECIAL EDITION: THE STORY SO FAR (fwd)

23 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT SPECIAL EDITION: THE STORY SO FAR - NOVEMBER PAIN: ULTIMATE WARFARE graphic with music comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT SPECIAL EDITION for the NOVEMBER PAIN PPV. The main focus of the PPV will of course be on the 10 WARGAMES matches, the winners of which go into a final two-ring battle royal, the winner of that getting a shot at whoever is the World Champion at CWO CELEBRATION on December 15, 1996 in Grand Rapids, MI. For those of you who don't know the rules of WARGAMES, here they are: _______________________________________________________________________________ 1. Two teams of four compete in a two-ring, steel-cage enclosed "battlefield." 2. One man from each team starts, they will be in the cage for 2 minutes. 3. After the 2 minutes are up, a coin toss is held, the winner of which gets the "advantage," being able to enter another man into the cage. The match continues, with the team having the "advantage" being able to have 2 men on 1. This goes on for 1 minute. 4. At that 1 minute mark, a man from the other team enters, making it 2 on 2. 5. Every minute after that, another man comes in, until all 8 men have entered the ring, then THE MATCH BEYOND begins. 6. In THE MATCH BEYOND, first man to submit or be ruled unable to continue loses the match for his team. STARR: Let's have a look at the WARGAMES matches. _______________________________________________________________________________ EYE ON THE STORM: Bad Guys, Sin, "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. Gods of Thunder, Highlanders STARR: A real Rogue's Gallery here, Bad Guys, Sin, and Williams will have to find a way to work together if they want to go on to the battle royal for the World Championship shot. Gods of Thunder and Highlanders have developed a close bond, they may have the advantage. _______________________________________________________________________________ Battle Cat, Mondo Gecko, Regulators vs. Ganglord, Sorrow, Eternal Wanderers STARR: Seven newcomers involved in this match, with Sorrow being the veteran. Again, Battle Cat and his friends may have the advantage of camaraderie. _______________________________________________________________________________ Sandman Jr. Miguel Sanchez, "Big" Rich Clark, ??? vs. Doomsday (Maximum Overdrive, Baby Waldo, Mercenaries) STARR: This one will be a very intense matchup. Not so long ago, Sandman Jr. and Miguel Sanchez were bitter enemies, in fact, they battled each other in a Stretcher Match at TERROR NIGHT '96. However, Doomsday targeted them both, and now they have done the previously unthinkable, team up to eliminate Doomsday. Sanchez of course brought in his 7'4", 481 pound friend, "Big" Rich Clark, to help, Clark has also been attacked by Doomsday in the past. However, Sandman Jr. has still not told us who his contribution to the team will be, he only gave us the hint that the person he's bringing has appeared in both ECW and the WWF! Should be very interesting indeed. _______________________________________________________________________________ Samoans, Biblical Powers vs. Deadly Sins, Unholy Duo STARR: Well, if Sandman Jr. and Miguel Sanchez are getting along, at least for NOVEMBER PAIN, the Biblical Powers and Deadly Sins, although claiming allegiance to Sanchez and Sandman Jr. respectively, are not. After all, Biblical Powers and Deadly Sins would have a natural enmity, don't you think? Also, the Unholy Duo has had their problems with the Powers, and vice versa. The Sins have vowed to destroy the Samoans, they couldn't get the job done at TERROR NIGHT, they're going to try to do it here. _______________________________________________________________________________ Tower of Power (Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli, Joe Eagle, The Big Cool) vs. Gold Kard International (Gold Kard, Brent "The Ace" Alles, Pit Bosses) STARR: Claiming that they're out to destroy every organization in the CWO, starting with the Chaotic Dynasty, and now going after the Tower of Power, Gold Kard International goes for the win here in WARGAMES. Of course, Alles and Kard have had several disparaging remarks about Eagle and Manocelli, which have done nothing except to anger them. Gold Kard has claimed that his best-case scenario would be that one of his men wins the battle royal, and then "Master of Pain" Barry Hart, also a Gold Kard International member, wins the title back from Schillo. If that happens, the member of Gold Kard International would, of course, decline the shot, and that could very well wreck the CWO CELEBRATION PPV, which is what Gold Kard International is hoping for to bring about the destruction of the CWO and the resurrection of the CM2WF. _______________________________________________________________________________ Confederate Associates (Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, Hoss and Little Joe) vs. Blood Inc. (Steel, Panther, D. E. A. T. H.) STARR: Blood Inc. trying to put their street smarts to work against the revitalized team of Joel, Frieghtrain, and Team Bonanza. _______________________________________________________________________________ Alliance (Inferno, Tiger Rogers, David and Goliath) vs. The Dark Side (Dark Raven, Hellhound, Creatures of the Night) STARR: Inferno and Dark Raven have been feuding since September, when Dark Raven upset the former shootfighter in the CWO Championship Tourneys. That feud spread to be the Alliance vs. the Dark Side when Dark Raven joined the latter organization. In an attack on Dark Raven by the Alliance, Master of Darkness Damion was targeted in the crossfire, furious, he declared revenge, and now sends his men, including the World Tag Team Champions, the Creatures of the Night, to hopefully destroy the tough guys of the Alliance. _______________________________________________________________________________ Four Aces (Flyboy, Clark Gray, Hollywood Muscle) vs. Wildfire, Akillies, Kamikaze and Mysteria STARR: We've said it before, but this could possibly be the "friendliest" WARGAMES match all night. Hollywood Muscle have had a friendly rivalry with Team Blitzkrieg, and Flyboy and Clark Gray have no problem with Wildfire and Akillies. However, as the Four Aces have stated, they can be aggressive without losing their respect for the other team. When the belt is on the line, everything goes up in the air, but you can be sure that these guys will keep the faith. Originally, Tub-O-Blub was supposed to be in this match, teaming with Akillies and Team Blitzkrieg, but when Ganglord put Tub-O-Blub out of wrestling, the newcomer Wildfire vowed to help his new friends in whatever endeavors they had. _______________________________________________________________________________ ???, Talon, Flame Spirits vs. Bane, Scourge, Apocalypse STARR: Bane has added a fourth man to his team, Scourge, who we don't know a lot about, go figure. If he's anything like Bane and the Apocalypse, though, Talon and the Spirits are in big trouble. They already are in big trouble, as Bane eliminated the man who supposed to be the team's captain, Sandman. There are rumors that Talon tried to get Lord Foul, now known by his real name, Hile Troy, to be Sandman's replacement, but in a prepared statement, Troy basically said "no dice." So, almost at the zero hour, you have to wonder: who can Talon possibly get to fill Sandman's shoes? _______________________________________________________________________________ Hangman, Rooster, Mad Doctor's Ghost, Lafayette vs. Johnny Reb, Judgment, Mason-Dixon STARR: Four men hand-picked by Gold Kard himself will be going up against Southern Pride and Judgment. Hangman and Rooster have both had their run-ins with Johnny Reb, this will be another intense one. _______________________________________________________________________________ TEXAS DEATH MATCH (winner gets a shot at the World title on the 12/3/96 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER) Master of Darkness Damion vs. "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum STARR: We've all heard about this one. Back in May, when the CWO was the CM2WF, Master of Darkness Damion began his mindgames assault against Slocum, taking his sister Sondra, locking her in a cage, lifting her above the arena, and dropping her, endangering her life. Luckily, they were just trying to scare her, and the cage stopped inches from the ground. Slocum vowed revenge, but Damion left the league to tour Japan before he could ever extract any real revenge. When the CWO began again, the feud heated up, and Damion and Slocum have been battling all over the country. At TERROR NIGHT, Slocum thought he had the Dark Side finally licked, shutting all of them into coffins. However, Damion renewed his physical and mental attacks on Slocum, culminated with a recent shocking display where Dark Raven dumped a bucket of lamb's blood on Slocum as his sister Sondra, put in leg chains by the Creatures of the Night, watched on helplessly. And so, this match was signed, not only to hopefully finish this bloody feud, but also to give the winner a shot at the World title on the December 3rd Tuesday Night Fever. The rules are simple, you have to pin your opponent, and then he has to make a 10 count, or the match is over. So, you basically have to batter your opponent to a state of unconsciousness. Master of Darkness Damion has the Darkness Falls tombstone piledriver, Ben Slocum has the Modern Day Bomb, a top rope power bomb. Both are devastating finishers, both are capable of knocking an opponent out, the question is, who can apply theirs first? The eyes of the wrestling world will be on this big matchup. _______________________________________________________________________________ FINAL BATTLE ROYAL FOR WARGAMES WINNERS STARR: 40 men go in, only 1 will survive. You first have to throw your opponent into the other ring, then the last two survivors will fight each other in a pinfall only match, the winner of which gets the title shot at CWO CELEBRATION. _______________________________________________________________________________ WORLD SERIES OF WRESTLING (4 out of 7 FALLS), WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart STARR: One man is for the fans, one man turned his back on them. Both men possess incredible wrestling talent and awesome finishers, Schillo with the Keuka Crack, which has been compared to Shawn Michaels' Superkick in several wrestling commentary circles, Hart has the Cardiac Arrest, a Shooting Star Press who some say is even better than Marc Mero's. Hart of course is now a member of Gold Kard International, and Gold Kard went so far as to put a $2,000,000 bounty on Schillo's head. So far, nobody has been able to collect. However, Hart wants to get his title back badly, and so this match was signed: the World Series of Wrestling. Four out of seven falls, if you can imagine such a thing. Also, in this match there will be no countouts, no disqualifications, and no time limit, there will be a definite, uncontested winner. In the weeks leading up to this, though, Schillo has been under a tremendous amount of pressure, with tough title defenses against "Big" Rich Clark, Brent "The Ace" Alles, and others, while Hart has stuck to some lower-ranked competition, honing his skills for the big match. Which man will have the endurance to survive? To find out, you'll have to tune into CWO NOVEMBER PAIN: ULTIMATE WARFARE, tomorrow night on NOVEMBER PAIN. Until then, this is Johnny Starr with the CWO REPORT SPECIAL EDITION, thank you for joining us.
