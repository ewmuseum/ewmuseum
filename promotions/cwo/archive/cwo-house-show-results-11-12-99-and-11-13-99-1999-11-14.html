House Show Results - 11/12/99 and 11/13/99

November 14, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 11/12/99 Daytona Beach, FL (Ocean Center) Jesse Ewiak pinned Syne after the Ewiakbomb, pulling the tights. Time of match: 6:27. Union Caine defeated Carr by disqualification after Carr delivered the
Fatality. Time of match: 3:32.
Television Champion Iceman X pinned Hollywood Haskins after the Ghetto
Buster, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 5:28.
Lord Hile Troy pinned Diamondback Chris Myers after using the Viledriver. Time of match: 6:47. North American Champion Man Mountain Marko pinned Maximum Overdrive after the Chi-Town Hammer. Time of match: 10:28.
Foolkiller pinned Wildfire after the Thunderfunk. Time of match: 8:04. Beauregard Boys defeated Reb and Fantastic when Shirley Beauregard pinned
Johnny Reb after the Option 73. Time of match: 8:31.
CWO WORLD CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Sandman Jr. pinned Gold Kard after the Permanent Dream. Time of match: 16:54. Sandman Jr. retained the CWO World Champion title. HOUSE SHOW 11/13/99 Jacksonville, FL (Coliseum) Mustafa Rahim pinned Simon St. Severe after the senton. Time of match: 8:53. Deliverance/'Roast Beef' Jason Allanson defeated Allen 'Mr. America' Carter/Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky when Deliverance made
Bondavarsky submit to the Squealer. Time of match: 14:09.
Gold Kard pinned 'The Jaw Jacking Gigolo' Andrew Dixon after the missile dropkick. Time of match: 9:32.
Magic Group defeated Yakuza when Necromancer pinned 'Dark Shogun' Akueri
Hakujin after the Salad Bowl. Time of match: 12:09. Gordy Alles was the special guest referee for this match, and was accused by Lester Sheets of making a fast count. Gordy and Lester battled after the match, and Gordy
delivered the Allesbreaker to the Sheets Foundation 3.0 leader! The Yakuza attacked Gordy after that, but Lord Hile Troy came down to make the save! Reb and Fantastic defeated Creatures of the Night (Master of Darkness Damion and Night Slinger) when 'Irresistible' Eddie Fantastic pinned Night Slinger after the Showstopper. Time of match: 20:41.
Gecko pinned Cruiserweight Champion 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum in a non-title match after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 9:31.
World Tag Team Champions the Brute Squad defeated Mercenaries when Black
Jack Hollister pinned Kaptain Krunch after the powerbomb, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 14:40.
Lord Hile Troy pinned Brian 'The King' Derks after the small package. Time of match: 16:08.
