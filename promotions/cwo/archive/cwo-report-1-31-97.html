CWO REPORT 1/31/97

31 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, January 31, 1997. Great CWO action last night in Augusta, ME, let's look at what went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 1/30/97 Augusta, ME Regulators defeated The Big Cool when Regulator Fire pinned Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor after the Tombstone. Time of match: 15 : 58 Sandman Jr. pinned Sin after the Permanent Dream. Time of match: 7 : 41 Wild Ones defeated D. E. A. T. H. when Mondo Gecko pinned Benton after the crucifix. Time of match: 14 : 28 Wrath pinned Phantom Freightrain after the dropkick. Time of match: 10 : 30 Mercenaries defeated Eternal Wanderers by disqualification. Time of match: 17 : 51 Goliath made Steel submit to the Goliath Grappler. Time of match: 15 : 41 Revolution defeated Mason-Dixon when Lafayette pinned Beauregard Dixon after the Bayonette. Time of match: 24 : 42 Deadly Sins defeated The Coldfront when Pride pinned 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White after the Repention. Time of match: 20 : 43 Hile Troy defeated Talon by countout. Time of match: 3 :0 6. In a previous stipulation, Sandman said that if Hile Troy defeated Talon, that he would win the North American championship. However, since Troy defeated Talon by countout, he did not win the title. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Sandman pinned Bloodbath after the Sandblast. Time of match: 12 : 45 _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Montpelier, Vermont, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FRIDAY, JANUARY 31, 1997 MONTEPELIER, VT D. E. A. T. H. vs. Biblical Powers LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Mondo Gecko vs. Moses (champion) Joe Eagle vs. Baby Waldo Jason "Flash" Varga vs. "Monster" Rich Clark Hellhound vs. Johnny Reb Phantom Freightrain vs. Tiger Rogers Jackass Joel vs. Sloth Hoss and Little Joel vs. The Big Cool TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Master of Darkness Damion vs. Maximum Overdrive (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night (champions) vs. Wild Ones _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens up on Maximum Overdrive in Doomsday's training center. The other members of Doomsday are nowhere to be seen.) MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: You know, anytime I'm feeling kind of bored, I can always tune in to the interviews of various CWO superstars and get a good laugh. Sin, I don't know whether or not you would be better suited for a career as a comedian or David Copperfield's flunky. Face it, you little worm. I've beaten you in the middle of the ring for the three count. No outside interference, no questionable circumstances, a clean pin. If you're that hard up to find out a second time what it's like to get in the ring with a REAL wrestler, one that doesn't have to rely on cheap magic tricks you can buy at the local five and dime, then by all means, I will meet you in the ring again until you have learned your lesson. But you had better ask around this federation because I have a feeling you might not know what you're getting into. Go and ask Sandman Jr. Ask Miguel Sanchez what's happened when they decide to force the issue. I've put them both on the shelf. Sanchez has never been the same since, and neither has that cheap Sandman copy. Now on to more worthy subjects. Williams, you may be undefeated and the current CWO TV Champ, but that's about to change. Yes, you beat me cleanly. But trying to get the job done the second time is the hard part. I'm watching you, boy. I'm studying you. I will find a weakness, and the next time we meet, I will exploit it, to become the first CWO two time TV Champion. _______________________________________________________________________________ [Sandman Jr] YOOOOOOOOOO! First, Sin, I'm gonna take care of you, just like I'm gonna take care of ALL the Sins, if Bane doesn't get them first... how great would that be, 3 jerks and 1 big jerk all taking each other out? Anyway, Sin is 1st. Then, it's the new TV Champ Brian Williams. Wayne Schillo is a good guy, and you must be too if he's your pal, BUT you have something I want. The TV Title. Even if it's non-title, I still want to prove I am the man, and to do it means going through you. Sorry. ----------------------------- [Wrath] Phantom Freightrain, that is such a stupid name. And you too are stupid if you think you're going to beat me. Be prepared to lose in my singles debut! [Pride] For us though, it's those idiots Coldfront. Man, you guys are FREAKS! We're gonna tear you apart.... [Sloth] Then it's the Big Cool. What a stupid freaking name. We'll show you how cool you really are. We've had unresolved battles in the past, but we'll resolve them UPSIDE YOUR HEAD! [Lust] BANE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Panther dressed in bright gold sequenced tights with a large black panther down the right leg. He has the lovely Kittens surrounding him, the three women are in tight gold body suits!! Panther although in good company has a perplexed look on his face.) Panther: I'm not sure what is going on in this federation. My last two victories have been disgracefull. From outside assistance from my former manger to Johnny Reb. I could see such a thing from Da Boss, but from Johnny Reb, I didn't count on that. In fact I'm not sure what to think anymore. The only thing I know is it's a bunch of crap, from Bane and Sandman to MOD Damion and Johnny Reb. It's time to get back to what's important, the FANS!!!!!!! That's right we have a lot of big ego's in this federation, they all think they make the world go around. Johnny Reb is off whining that he has never held gold in the CWO, well pitty party for you my friend. It's not what you get, it's what you give. I know that many of the other wrestlers out there will not like what I'm saying, but it's all TRUE!!! Now for the guys who really know what this sport is all about, I tip my hat to you. For the rest of you, well have our discussion in the ring.(Panther takes a deep breathe) Panther: That was just something I had to get off my chest. Now as for what's in my future, that is up to the Great Fans of the CWO!!!!! _______________________________________________________________________________ Don King is sitting at a long table, next to a genuine CWO hero- Mad Doctor's Ghost. King: Ladies and Gentlemen! Something tragicastic has occurred! I believe that in the CWO, I have noticed a violation of trademarkulence! It seems to me that... MDG: AAARRRGHHH!! I'M A DEAD GUY!! MY GIMMICK RULES! RICH CLARK IS NOT DEAD, HE'S A TOOL! King: Anyway, it seems that the MDG gimmick has been stolen by a would-be contender in the CWO. As the manager and promoter of Mister Ghost, it is my job to ensure that his legacyosity is protected. We feel that Rich Clark is engaging in some dishonestulicious activity, and we want to settle the score. A coffin match, soon, to decide who gets the rights to be the 'Dead Guy' of the CWO. If MDG loses, he'll retire to the Dead Guy ranch, and never come back. If he wins, however, Rich Clark will not be able to be a freaky dead guy without MDG's permission and training. Are you man enough, Rich? Will you accept the challenge? MDG: AAAAHHH!! I'M DEAD! I'M THE ONLY DEAD GUY WRESTLER! RICH CLARK IS A LIVING GUY IN DISGUISE! DON'T TRUST HIM!!! AAAAAAARRRGGGHHHH!! DEAD GUYS HATE COPYRIGHT INFRINGEMENT!! WWWWOOOOOAAAHHHHH!! (MDG starts flailing his arms, tips over the table, and runs away.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Setting, an abandon warehouse. You notice many forms of wieght lifting machines about the dark building. Suddenly, a man walks in from behind, what looks to be some sort of mediatation room.> SM: <looking very intensely into the camera> Bloodbath, heh, a rookie challenging me for my title? You think you can withstand this power, this experience of the man in the CWO who's held the most titles in history? Bloodbath, you are a fool, I will give you one shot at this title, in a steel cage. This will be the only shot you get fool, so make it your best rookie. As for Hile Troy, I see Talon is going to have a run-in with you. I am willing to put my title on the line for that match. I have confidence in Talon, that he will win. Under any circumstances that you would happen to win Troy, you will regain this belt. Of course the CWO commisioner will approve, because this match involves you Troy. Oh one more thing Troy, I'm starting to grow a sudden liking to steel cage matches, the blood pleases me. I want one more match with you Troy, in the locked steel cage, only in this match, only one will walk out of there concious. The rules of the match will be, the winner is announced when the other is no longer able to continue. I've been waiting for this match for a long time. Rich Clark, you seem to not be scared of nightmares, perhaps you do have dreams, but those won't last to long. Once you feel the power of my darkness you will have only nonstop nightmares for the rest of your life. MOD, another claimer of the night, are match is approaching, we will find out who is the real "Master of Darkness". Yes it may seem there is a lot to be dealt with nowadays in the CWO. That is why I'm here. Bloodbath your name will soon fit you well, when you will be drowning in your own blood. Troy, old man, you are going to want to get that retirement check after Talon and I are done with you. Clark, you fool, you dare challenge the darkness, you will now find out what kinda of monster i really am, sleep with one eye open, you never know if i'll be there. MOD your claim to rule the night will soon be over, and i will be sole owner of the Darkness. LIGHTS OUT! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
