CWO EXTREME 3/13/01 Results

13 March 2001

CWO EXTREME 3/13/01 Results Diana Christensen, the new Executive Producer of the CWO, opened the show with a greeting to all the wrestlers and personalities of the CWO, who were gathered in and outside the ring. She explained the concept of EXTREME
MEASURES and TOTAL ACCESS.
Omar Martinez defeated Mary Alles (Diana’s “surprise” opponent for Martinez) by pinfall after the Martinez Missile Dropkick in 3:55, advancing in the
Cruiserweight Championship Tournament. Match Heat: 21.
Brent “The Ace” Alles was shown in his hotel room, briefly holding one of the TOTAL ACCESS crew sent to interview him by Diana hostage to an armbar.
Alles promised Christensen that he would take part in her little “sociology experiment,” but on his terms. He then let the crew member go.
Wildfire (w/Laura) defeated "The Professional” Brian Thompson in 5:48 by pinfall after the Wildfire Driver, advancing in the World Championship
Tournament. Match Heat: 14.
The Blue Dude defeated Hector Fuerza in 2:54 by pinfall after the Suicide
Driver, advancing in the Cruiserweight Championship Tournament. Match Heat:
13. Jesse Ewiak was attacked backstage by the Hellions (Bishop and Maverick), who the announcers theorized were working for Mondo Gecko.
"The Avenger" Jesse Ewiak defeated Mondo Gecko (w/Tamara Wilcox-Gecko) by pinfall in 5:30 after the Ewiakbomb v3.0, advancing in the World
Championship Tournament. Match Heat: 22. Diana was informed that Brent Alles had left his hotel room. She didn’t seem to care. Backyard pinned Geoff Bondavarsky after the Flying Butt Splash in 4:31, advancing in the Cruiserweight Championship Tournament. Match Heat: 13.
Johnny Reb pinned Maximum Overdrive after a superkick in 5:52, advancing in the World Championship Tournament. Match Heat: 15. After the match,
Overdrive delivered the Pile-Up Power Bomb to Reb again. Johnny Reb angrily confronted his former True American teammates, wondering why they didn’t help him against Overdrive. Violence almost erupted between
Reb and Omar Martinez before Geoff Bondavarsky calmed them down.
El Suicida pinned Brian “The Piper” McTavish after the Phoenix Splash in
2:53, advancing in the Cruiserweight Championship Tournament. Match Heat:
18. Allen “Mr. America” Carter defeated “Preacher of Pain” Simon St. Severe when the referee stopped the match at 7:14 when Carter had Severe in the American
Dream (figure four sleeper). Prior to the submission hold, Geoff
Bondavarsky came down to ringside and struck Severe with a steel chair while the ref was distracted. Carter advanced in the World Championship
Tournament. MATCH HEAT: 15. After the match, the True Americans continued to beat down on the “Preacher of Pain.”
Diana, apparently satisfied with the night’s proceedings, left the arena early to go back to her hotel. A TOTAL ACCESS cameraman followed her, wanting her reaction to the first EXTREME. Once in the limo, Diana got a rude surprise, as a hostilely smiling Brent Alles turned around to greet her as the show ended!
