CWO REPORT 11-14-97 (fwd)

14 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey at the REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to another edition of the CWO REPORT. We have a lot to cover today, so let's get down to business. FEVER came out last night, and what a wild card it was. Let's go to a quick rundown on what happened. CWO FEVER "LIVE" FROM SYRACUSE, NY (ONONDAGA WAR MEMORIAL) Skyhawk defeated Tommy Bomber in 12:07 in a Texas Death match following a DDT. Bomber failed to rise to his feet by the count of 10, giving Skyhawk the victory. Interview with Union Caine and Vince DaVinci. The Mercenaries defeated the Big Cool in 17:04 when Kaptain Krunch pinned Jim "The Jazzman" Taylor after Korperal Punishment stabbed Taylor in the upper chest area with a broken bottle. Interview with the Wild Ones. Interview with Gold Kard and Lord Hile Troy. Moments after the interview started, Brent "The Ace" Alles came to the ring. Troy and Alles agreed that they would change their match at November Pain to a "British Rules" match that will be held inside a steel cage. In a TV Title match, Lord Hile Troy lost to TV Champion Bloodbath by countout in 59 seconds after Bloodbath tombstone powerbombed him outside the ring on the concrete, knocking him out. After the match, Wildfire dropped from the ceiling and attacked Bloodbath. Interview with the McCloud Clan, announcing that the Highlanders are now at war with the CWO. Footage from last week's FEVER of Mark Fulcrum challenging Johnny Reb, and Tom Krepps announcment that Reb will face Fulcrum on FEVER. Interview with Masher Maccabee. In a CWO North American Title match, Mondo Gecko defeated Masher Maccabee in 19:12 by DQ after Maccabee hit Gecko with the Homentaschen Hangover using a steel chair. While preparing to do it again, Fergus McTavish appeared on the Jumbotron and proceded to demolish Maccabee's sports car with a sledgehammer. Maccabee ran to the parking lot where a brawl took place. Interview with Wrath. The SPJW tag team of the Jade Dragons defeated the Highlanders in 13:28 after Ishida pinned Conor with the Dragon Strike. After the match, the Dragons were attacked by the Highlanders. Interview with the Dark Side. In a non title match, Mark Fulcrum beat CWO World Champion Johnny Reb by countout in 10:55. During the match, Sandman Jr. made his way to the ring, and was forced back to the locker rooms by Tom Krepps, but not before Sandman Jr. was able to hit Reb with his singapore cane. After the match, Team Johnny Reb and Sandman Jr. were both attacked by the Dark Side. Fulcrum joined Sandman Jr. and Team Johnny Reb to fight them off. WASHEY: Due to the upcoming Wayne Carpenter Memorial Tag Team Tournament, all of the CWO's House Shows have been pushed back a day and one House Show has been taken off the schedule. Here is the linup for the House Show that will take place Friday in Binghampton, NY. FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 1997 BINGHAMTON, NY (BINGHAMTON WAR MEMORIAL) "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher vs. Gold Kard Mondo Gecko vs. "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat Ranger Taurus vs. Hellhound Foolkiller vs. Kaptain Krunch WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Holocaust and Red Raven vs. Newton Bros. (champions) Union Caine vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo Dogs of War vs. Creatures of the Night LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: El Grande Pistolerro vs. Southern Dynamite (champion) WASHEY: Please remember that this will take place tomorrow night. Don't miss it. Now let's get to our interview portion of the REPORT. Today we will start off our interviews by hearing from one of the tag teams that participated in last night's FEVER. All the way from the SJPW, here are the Jade Dragons. ________________________________________________________________________ [Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami, the Jade Dragons, are standing in front of a CWO/SJPW banner...] Kenji Ishida: November 11th. On this date, we face our greatest test in CWO. On that night, we face the Highlanders, former CWO tag team champions. This is a team whose reputation reaches as far away as our homeland. To face them is not only an honor, but a privelage. Shinji Usami: Highlanders, we know that defeating you will be one of the hardest tasks we can face while we are in CWO. We know that our battle will be fair and honorable. Kenji Ishida: And if we do defeat the Highlanders, it will only be a short time before we will be able to challenge for the CWO tag team championship. We have yet to hold a title, but this time, we know we are almost ready for such a task. Shinji Usami: Highlanders, on November 11th, you face the Jade Dragons. Win or lose, it will be a confrontation you will not soon forget. [camera fades out] _________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: After what went down last night between these two teams, I'm sure that the Jade Dragons will have a hard time forgetting, either. Our next interview comes from the "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher _________________________________________________________________________ ("Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher and his wife Jaime, are sitting in the docters ofice) Deutscher (yelling):What do you mean, retire?!?!? Jaime:Calm down dear!! Docter:You have sustained damage to your neck and spinal cord and I just think it would be in your best intrest... Deutscher (still yelling):You listen here, no damn docter is gonna tell me when I should retire!! Docter (now yelling):Now you listen, this is my office not a wrestling ring, now if your gonna act like that then get the hell out of here, no you are in no shape to wrestle and at this time I'm not gonna give you clearnce to get back into the ring!!! Do you understand me!?!?!? (Deutscher starts to smile) Deutscher:You know for a docter your not that smart!! Docter:What do you mean? (Deutscher grabs the docter by the hair and slams his head onto his desk) Jaime (stunned):Ryan, what are you doing!?!? (Deutscher then stands on top of the desk, picks up the docter and gives him the stun gun breaking the desk in half) Deutscher:Forget it you stupid piece of trash, I'll get a second opinion!! (As the docter lays in between what used to be his desk, quivering, Deutscher takes his wife's hand and they leave as the camera fades out) _________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our next interview is from the Riders Of The Storm. Let's hear what they have to say. _________________________________________________________________________ (The camera opens on the gym of the Riders on the Storm in Pittsburgh. The winter winds howl outside, and through the windows we can see it's nighttime, and a few snowflakes are falling. The camera focuses on the ring in the middle of the gym, where Ace, Deck, "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum, and Diamondback Chris Myers, sporting a leg brace, are all standing on the ring aprons in their wrestling gear. The Wild Cards are on one side, while Myers and Fulcrum are on the other. Ace currently has Fulcrum in an armbar, which Mark is trying his best to reverse. Dallas is standing on the ring apron, wearing a Washington Capitals jersey with her name on the back, and she's holding a piece of paper in one hand) DALLAS: Ok, guys! I've got the lineup for next week, and the REPORT sent some cameras over, so gather around! (Ace lets go of the armbar, and all four men gather in the ring) DALLAS: Now, don't get upset... MYERS: What? DALLAS: Well, whoever made up this match has some major issues, let me say. ACE: Cards vs. Nation of Denomination? DALLAS: No... FULCRUM: Myself vs. Jumbo Jenkins? DALLAS: Not even close! MYERS: Myself vs. Sandman Jr? DALLAS: You're close... (Dallas hands the paper to Chris, who looks at it. His eyes widen as he scans the schedule) MYERS: Issues? This guy was probably dropped on his head as a kid! FULCRUM: What? Let me see! (Mark takes the paper) FULCRUM: Well, either Chris is right, or this guy smokes WAY too much crack. ACE: What is it? FULCRUM: It's the Dark Side vs. me, Chris, Sandman Jr...and Johnny Reb! DECK: Are they nuts? There is no way either of you can coexist in the same ring with that backstabber! MYERS: Ok, ok, ok...it kind of makes sense, but we can't blame the scheduler... FULCRUM: Who can we blame? (All eyes turn to "Airborne") FULCRUM: Me? DALLAS: Honey, you were the one who had to get involved in that brawl at the end of FEVER... FULCRUM: Well....I love a good brawl! What can I say? DALLAS: We're not blaming you, Mark.... ACE: I am. DALLAS: Ace, play nice. Well, we've got the cameras here, so let's talk. Deck? DECK: Ok.... (Deck loosens up, and looks at the camera with a serious look) DECK: It seems we have another Wild team here in CWO. I'm talking about Gecko and Pistolerro. Hey, guys? You talk about us on FEVER, and you say we run our mouths for a team that hasn't proven anything in this fed. You say we're just a couple of runners up to the greatness that are the Wild Ones. I got the impression you think we couldn't lace up your boots. Well, I just happened to take a look at the latest rankings, and guess what I saw? (Deck gets a copy of CWO Magazine handed to him by Ace) DECK: Ok, it says right here....well, the Wild Ones are ranked number 8. Not bad, considering the tag teams around here are among the best in the sport. Now, where are we? Let me see....I think I'll start at the bottom...no....no....not here....we're not ranked lower then the Wild Ones! Up a little more....ah! Number 4! 5-0-0, guys. Now, for a couple of rookies, that ain't too bad, woudln't you agree? Hell, for a team that hasn't done much of anything, we sure have a lot of teams lining up for shots at us! And how many teams here in CWO think you're worth fighting? How many? We'll be waiting for a reply. ACE: Now, let's talk about something really important! DECK: Kellen does look awfully hot on this cover... ACE: No, you ninny! DECK: Ninny? ACE: Yeah! I'm talking about the Carpenter Cup! 32 teams, some of the best from around the world! We've got champs from the CWO, WCW, WWF, AAPW...the list goes on and on! And you know what? Anyone out there who's betting, bet on the Wild Cards to win! You see, we do realize that a lot of people, fans and wrestlers alike, in CWO think we haven't done anything in the world to deserve the ranking we got! Aside from pissing off the Deadly Sins...and that ain't too hard, believe me...we've pretty much beat up on the lower enchelon teams here in CWO. That changes next week, because we're throwing everything we've got into that tournament! We've had Chris and Mark, who are also in the tourney, doing tag team work with us for the past week! Winning the Cup not only gets us $1,000,000 bucks and a neat silver trophy, but it gets us something we love...respect! Something that makes the top teams here take notice! DECK: Here that, Wild Ones? GOOD teams, like the Newton Brothers, the Deadly Sins, the Highlanders, the Mercenaries. ACE: My point exactly! So, guys, bet it all on Deck and I, because we are going to win that cup, come hell or high water! (Mark Fulcurm starts singing "Tubthumping" By Chumbawabma) FULCRUM: Oh, Johnny Boy, Johnny Boy, Johnny Boy...you get knocked down and don't get up again, 'cause Fulcrum's going to beat you down! DALLAS: Uh, Mark, you should stick to wrestling... FULCRUM: Sorry, couldn't resist. Hey, Reb, quite a match we had Tuesday, huh? Kind of the match that makes everyone else take notice. Mainly, they noticed how damn good I am and how much of a coward you are. To make the World champion walk out of a match....boy, I must have been whipping you pretty good! And don't even blame it on the Singapore Cane, because I've taken more shots like that in a bar fight! It's just like I said before...you're nothing but a paper champion. Now, everyone knows just how talented I am, and how lousy you are. So why did I try to save your sorry hide from the Dark Side? I didn't. I was helping out Sandman Jr, the man you called "crazy". Yeah, he is. But he's got more heart, more guts, and WAY more balls then you ever will. And CWO has decided to stick myself, Myers, Junior, and the World champ in a eight man tag. Boy oh boy...Reb, don't expect me to save your sorry hide. I'm doing this match to WIN. And I damn well believe "Airborne", Diamondback, and the hardcore icon of CWO can whip up on the unholy ones, with or without your help. MYERS: My first match back.... (Myers points down to the leg brace he's wearing. It's painted red and white, and on the knee is a picture of the Stars and Bars) MYERS: You like this? Reb, I'm wearing this for my own safety. Technically, I could not be wearing it, but I want to be around for a long time. Plus, I want you to take a good look at this flag, before I drive it into your forehead. I've been injured twice here in CWO. Once, the Reverend took my eyesight and my TV belt. Now, you stretched my tendons. Well, Reb, my leg is fine, but I want to make sure it's fully healed, for the sole purpose of being 100% when I step in the ring against you, somewhere down the line. Now, I see I'm teaming up with you. Well, I damn well haven't forgiven you for what you did to me at TERROR NIGHT. And I probably never will. And don't expect me, Mark, or Sandman Jr. to carry you next Tuesday. Because I'm out to win. And if I get a little revenge along the way, great. But I want someone to get pinned, 1-2-3, on the Dark Side. And I'll to anything to get it. Johnny, champion, you almost got beat by a rookie. A rookie. Only the fact you walked away saved your sorry ass from getting pinned. Well, if he could almost beat you, I'm damn sure DCM can. When Sandman Jr. takes that belt from you at NOVEMBER PAIN, I want the next crack at you. Belt or no belt, I'll introduce you to a world of pain no one ever has, curtouesy of the Rattler. DALLAS: Scotland, get ready, because the Riders on the Storm are going to show you just why we're the best in the business! (All four flash the Riders sign as the camera fades out) ____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our next flash comes from another of the SJPW superstars making their foray into the CWO. Here is Masashi "Lightning" Lanther. ____________________________________________________________________________ [Masashi Lanther is seen on the set of his new movie. He is filming a scene where he is fighting men in Ninja attire. He fights his way through 6 of them, then the director calls 'Cut' Lanther walks to his chair, and grabs a bottle of water. He takes a drink, then looks at the camera...] Masashi Lanther: "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat. Carlos Maia. A draw against Jason Varga. So far, I have done well in CWO. While I may not be doing as well as I had hoped, I am patient enough to work my way up the ladder. I had a title shot for the Lightheavyweight championship once, and when I am ready, I will earn another. November 8th is Ron "Rude Guy" Johnson. If I defeat him, then I have climbed another step. Southern Dyanamite, enjoy your title reign, for when the lightning strikes a second time, you, or whomever holds that title, may find themselves on the losing end. It may take me a week. It may take me a month. It may even take me a year, but eventually, I will have another title shot. Until then, I will continue to do what I do best: Perform to the best of my abilities, and give the fans the high flying action they have come to expect from me. [The director calls for Lanther that it is time to film another scene. Lanther nods to the camera, puts his bottle down, and walks back to the set. The camera fades] ____________________________________________________________________________ _____ WASHEY: During last night's FEVER, I witnessed one of the most heinous acts in my tenure of this sport. One wrestler stabbing another with the remains of a broken bottle. Up next is the team responsible, and they will be one of the teams that will be participating in the upcoming Wayne Carpenter Memorial Tag Team Tournament. Here are the Mercenaries. ____________________________________________________________________________ _____ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, just after their match with the Big Cool on FEVER. Both are still covered in the blood of Jim "Jazzman" Taylor.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: I hope every tag team in the CWO was watching that match tonight. What you saw is just the start of what's going to happen to ANY tag team that gets in our way in that tournament. That one million dollars belongs to the two guys you see in front of you, and there's nothing you can do about it!! KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: For too damn long the Mercenaries have been in this stinking federation and have had every single tag team in this federation duck, run and hide from us. Well, tonight we have given you a reason to. We're sick and tired of all these tag teams around here shooting off their damn mouths, saying that they can beat the Mercenaries, and having to either stoop to do it, or if they are wearing gold, stick us in a non title match. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Wild Ones, we heard your crap tonight, and unless you want us up close and personal, you'd better just keep your mouths shut and pray that our paths don't cross in that tournament. Why? (Korperal Punishment holds up the remains of the blood-streaked bottle used to stab Taylor.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Because we're out for BLOOD! KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: To all the other teams in this tournament, you only have a few more days to get out of it, unless you want to end up like the Big Fools did tonight. Don't be stupid. Ask yourselves, how can the Mercenaries NOT win the whole thing? KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Tell 'em why. They're a stupid bunch. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Because WE ARE THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD!!! ____________________________________________________________________________ ___________ WASHEY: Disgusting. Those loons shouldn't be running around without straitjackets. Finally today, we have one more SJPW star to hear from. Here is Tsutomu Kobayashi. ____________________________________________________________________________ ___________ [Camera opens on Tsutomu Kobayashi standing in front of a CWO/SJPW banner..] Tsutomu Kobayashi: Undefeated. I am undefeated. Until I came to CWO, I had yet to win a match, and in SJPW, I am winless. That does not mean that the competition in CWO is less than in SJPW. The CWO has competitors who equal the best SJPW has to offer. I do not dwell on the why, but accept the fact that it has happened. First Tiger Kikuchi, one of my father's old foes, then Gold Kard, a man whose credentials speak for themselves, and recently, Kurt Gerstrung. Who will I face next? I do not know. I can only say that I shall continue to attempt to remain unbeaten. To be ranked #14 is an honor I never thought I could achieve here. I was sent to CWO on a whim by the SJPW promoters, thinking the CWO wrestlers would be glad to find a Japanese opponent they could dominate. Then the CWO promoters booked me against Tiger Kikuchi, one of Japan's icons, and I defeated him! Then I defeated Gold Kard on Sunday Showcase, the same night as Terror Night '97. [pauses] Tsutomu Kobayashi: So now I have proven that I am not a joke. I am not some Japanese jobber that can be overlooked. I am a serious threat to title holders in CWO. I will continue to strive towards contention for a title. I know my fellow SJPW wrestler, Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, another old foe of my fathers, wishes the Lightheavyweight title. My goals are higher. I hope to one day earn a title shot at either the Television or North American championships. Lightheavyweight titles are worthy titles, but as my father is a former All Asian champion, I wish to earn a greater title, to honor my father. [pauses] Tsutomu Kobayashi: I wish to now make an open challenge. I am willing to face any CWO wrestler in any type of singles match. I do not care how big they are, I will face anyone. I also now make a challenge to The Focus. Focus, you were once a wrestler in my father's promotion, K-Pro, and I was impressed with your skill. Would you be willing to wrestle me, and allow me to face a man I consider to be one of the best wrestlers in CWO? I await your answer. [Tsutomu bows to the camera as it fades out] ____________________________________________________________________________ _____ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody tomorrow on the REPORT!! (fade to closing credits.)
