CWO House Show Results - 11/21/98

22 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 11/21/98 Topeka, KS 2ND ANNUAL WAYNE CARPENTER MEMORIAL TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT (3RD ROUND): Lunatic Fringe defeated Newton Bros. when 'Killer' Carlos Maia pinned Steve Newton after the fisherman suplex and outside interference by Brent "The Ace" Alles. Time of match: 24:34. Union Caine/Joe Eagle defeated Outlaw Riders when Joe Eagle pinned Mondo Gecko after the reversed cradle. Time of match: 28:29. 'Dragonfire' Rich Johnson/'Mr. Technical' Cooper Young defeated Texas Roughriders when 'Dragonfire' Rich Johnson pinned Wesley 'The Rock' McMichael after the Dragonfire. Time of match: 19:41. NON-TOURNAMENT MATCHES: SUBMISSION MATCH: Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky made Col. Taggart submit to the armbar submission. The ref stopped the match at 16:51. Before putting him in the armbar, Mondo Gecko came down to ringside and struck Taggart right in the face with a steel chair, knocking him out. Warhawk pinned Bobby Orr after the sunset flip, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 11:59. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Thomas Family (Hollywood Haskins, Masher Maccabee, and "Double D" Damian Dagger) defeated Unit (Sean Holiday and the Newton Bros.) when 'Double D' Damian Dagger pinned Howard Newton after the Look and outside interference by Warhawk. Time of match: 24:35. Thomas Family won the World 6-Man Tag Team Championships. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS TITLE MATCH: Creatures of Darkness defeated Rat Pack when Night Crawler pinned 'Chairman of the Board' Gold Kard after the Day to Night. Time of match: 21:33. Creatures of Darkness retained the CWO North American Tag Team Champions title. Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Rattler. Time of match: 16:30. Brent G. Alles
