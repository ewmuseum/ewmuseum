CWO REPORT 4/11/97

11 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hi, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, April 11, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. The CWO's ORIENT EXPRESS tour continues this week, last night stopping in Seoul, South Korea, let's see what happened on that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/10/97 Seoul, South Korea WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Champions the Unholy Alliance (Bishop, Serpentmaster, Vlad the Impaler) defeated Wayne Schillo/Brian 'The Hammer' Williams/Jason 'Flash' Varga when Bishop pinned Wayne Schillo after the Hell's Inferno (fireball). Time of match: 14:10. Maximum Overdrive pinned Bloodbath after the rollup. Time of match: 4:05. Mercenaries defeated Regulators when Kaptain Krunch pinned Regulator Ice after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 24:47. Inferno pinned Bane after the Inferno's Blaze and outside interference by 'Monster' Rich Clark. Time of match: 12:19. David 'The Destroyer' Davis pinned 'Mr. Extreme' Chris Ohlhoff after the moonsault. Time of match: 10:06. Baby Waldo made Sandman Jr. submit to the Cradle Rocker. Time of match: 16:28. CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Champion Tiger Rogers pinned El Grande Pistolerro after the Inverted Powerbomb. Time of match: 4:46. NO DISQUALIFICATION MATCH: Johnny Reb made the ref stop the match when CWO World Champion 'Monster' Rich Clark was in the Texas Cloverleaf in a non-title match after outside interference by Bane, who struck Clark with the shovel made out of the World Championship belt. Time of match: 15:04. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Taipei, Taiwan, let's look at the lineup for that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FRIDAY, APRIL 11, 1997 TAIPEI, TAIWAN Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams Dark Raven vs. Johnny Reb Mason-Dixon vs. Tiger Rogers and David "The Destroyer" Davis Wrath vs. El Grande Pistellero Justice Brothers vs. Da Hood Rats LIGHTS OUT MATCH: Panther vs. Inferno Big Cool vs. Highlanders NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Goliath (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: This past Tuesday on FEVER, we witnessed one of the most shocking acts ever, as Brent "The Ace" Alles delivered his signature move, the Allesbreaker, to his own father. Alles definitely seems out of control, however, our cameras were able to catch up to him and the equally unhinged Amanda Bloom as Alles returned to his hometown briefly. _______________________________________________________________________________ (A beige Buick Century drives up to Byron Center High School. Brent "The Ace" Alles, wearing jeans and a T-shirt, along with his Syracuse jacket, exits the car, along with a sullen looking Amanda Bloom.) AMANDA BLOOM: This is it, daddy, this is where you went to school? BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: Yep, this is Byron Center High School, in all its glory. BLOOM: Why are there no people here right now, daddy? ALLES: It's Spring Break, figured I could slip in here unnoticed. My family's looking for me, screw 'em, they'll find me when I want them to. (Alles and Bloom walk into the school.) ALLES: Seems so quiet in here, brings back a lot of memories. BLOOM: Good memories? ALLES: Not really. Oh, I had a few, but I wasn't one of the popular kids, wasn't able to hang with the "in" crowd, was mostly an outsider. No, I couldn't be popular, not like Mondo Gecko, not like Brian "The Hammer" Williams. So I turned to wrestling, and that's where I really excelled, that's where I finally felt like I had a home. BLOOM: I hate those bad men in the CWO, especially Brian! ALLES: Don't you worry, hon', I'll protect you from them. You're the only thing in my life right now that matters, besides teaching Williams what I have to teach him. And as for the rest of those goofs, Mondo Gecko found out what happens when he calls me out, he went down for the count. Same thing with that idiot Gray. He thinks he knows pain, he thinks he knows grief? I'll show him pain, I'll show him grief. And Maximum Overdrive, he thinks he's got my number too, Mandy, but I already beat his @$$ once, I really don't have much to prove there. (Amanda and Alles sit in silence for a moment.) ALLES: All right, now you've seen it, let's get the hell out of here. Too many memories. (Amanda and Alles exit and get in the car, as the camera shows it driving away into the distance.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today on the REPORT, we'll see you tomorrow!
