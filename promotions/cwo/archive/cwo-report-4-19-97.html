CWO REPORT 4/19/97

19 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Mondo Gecko and the Wild Bunch are in the interview area. They are watching the comments of Brent Alles and laughing.) MONDO GECKO: Hey Alles, you talk about warped reality and fantasy world, YOU'RE LIVING IN A DREAM WORLD, CHUMP! You play these stupid mind games on everybody, think you are tougher than you really are but yet you are ranked all the way in 30th place and you don't have a clue about what wrestling is about. You and your little slut, Amanda Bloom, cheap shot everybody but when it's a fair fight you run like cowards. I am tired of you and the Gold Kard International. I can't wait to get my hands on you and teach you a wrestling lesson that you will never forget. As far as who I emulate, I am proud to have learned from your father, Randy Savage, and Public Enemy during camps and workouts. You see, unlike you I respect the older superstars of the wrestling world because they paved the way for us. You talk about how you are going to break my neck and how I bring my lady friends to watch my matches. Tamara Wilcox is a friend, manager, and training partner. Unlike that bimbo Bloom, she can execute moves and sentences without having to think for an hour or so. You broke Brian Williams' neck because he wasn't ready for you but in our match I will have my little friends at ring side. (Regulator Fire & Ice step forward and stand next to Gecko) REGULATOR FIRE: Alles, you're nothing but a puny punk who needs to be beaten a couple of times so you can not mess with the Wild Bunch. Alles, we have hated you ever since we came here and if you even cheap shot our friend, YOU ARE GOING TO FEEL TOTAL REGULATION! REGULATOR ICE: HEY ALLES! IS THE PRETTY BOY AFRAID OF THE BIG BAD REGULATORS? It seems the Mercenaries are brain dead after we beat the snot out of them twice so they think they own us. Well, that is what you expect from two guys named after cereal types. Bottom line, Alles, is we are going to destroy you and give you an @$$ kicking you will never forget. MONDO GECKO: Alles, you better pray to god that I am in a good mood because ask Masher, Panther, BBB, Focus, and anybody else who has felt the pain of the Frankensteiner, YOU don't get UP! GET READY FOR A MONDO EXTREME WAR, BBAABBYY! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, April 19, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Juneau, Alaska, for some great action in our northernest state, let's see what happened! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/18/97 Juneau, AK Brent 'The Ace' Alles and Mondo Gecko wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Gold Kard after the Keuka Smack (ear ringer and an uppercut). Time of match: 10:04. Pride and Panther wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Baby Waldo pinned Steel after the headbutt. Time of match: 4:16. Wild Side defeated Brother Justice by countout when Da Hood Rats attacked Brother Justice outside the ring. Time of match: 3:09. CWO TELEVISION CHAMPION TITLE MATCH (LUMBERJACK MATCH): Inferno (w/Jimbo Jones, Tiger Rogers, Goliath, and David "The Destroyer" Davis as lumberjacks) pinned Masher Maccabee (w/Gold Kard, David "The Truth" Thomas, and the Masters of Hardcore as lumberjacks) after the Inferno's Blaze and outside interference by Mondo Gecko (he struck him with the championship belt). Time of match: 15:30. Inferno won the CWO Television Champion title. Mercenaries defeated North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys in a non-title match when Kaptain Krunch pinned Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson after Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker accidentally struck Johnson with a steel chair. Time of match: 8:33. World Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses defeated Goliath/Tiger Rogers in a non-title match when Pit Boss #2 pinned Goliath after the Spike Piledriver. Time of match: 10:43. Wayne Schillo defeated 'Monster' Rich Clark by disqualification when Brent 'The Ace' Alles attacked. Time of match: 6:03. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: As you saw, Inferno is the new CWO Television Champion after last night's stunning upset over Maccabee in a wild lumberjack match. While the lumberjacks brawled all over the arena, Mondo Gecko was able to strike Maccabee with the Television championship belt, and after an Inferno's Blaze power bomb, Inferno found himself the new champion. However, Masher Maccabee will get a rematch next week on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, you can be sure he will be pulling out all the stops to get his belt back. Whomever holds the belt on the day of MAY-HEM will of course face Johnny Reb, who earned that shot by beating Mondo Gecko a few weeks ago on FEVER. Meanwhile, the CWO continues its stay in Alaska tonight with a stop in Anchorage, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, APRIL 19, 1997 ANCHORAGE, AK Hellhound vs. Joe Eagle Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Bloodbath Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung vs. Mondo Gecko Gold Kard vs. Johnny Reb Creatures of the Night vs. Hellhound (substituting for the injured Bishop)/Dark Raven LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): 'Lonestar' Mike Williams vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses (champions) vs. Highlanders NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (steel cage, escape): Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Goliath (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ STARR: An interesting change in that card is found in the tag match between the Creatures of the Night and the Unholy Alliance team of Dark Raven and Hellhound. You may remember that Hellhound and Dark Raven briefly teamed as Hell's Pets while both were in the Dark Side, and now they will be going up against their former teammates due to Bishop's broken arm at the hands of the Dark Side Wednesday night. That's about it for today, before we go, let's look at the lineup for tomorrow's great SUNDAY SHOWCASE! _______________________________________________________________________________ SHOWCASE 4/20/97 HOUR #1: Centurion vs. Wrath Attitude Dance vs. New Men (Akillies and Spartacus) El Grande Pistolerro vs. Ganglord HOUR #2: Dark Raven vs. Exile WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): "Lonestar" Mike Williams and Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung vs. Pit Bosses (champions) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Tommy Lightning vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Closing creds and music come up as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ Copyright 1997 Just B Cuz Productions http://web.syr.edu/~bgalles/cwo.html Cyberspace Wrestling Organization (CWO) All Rights Reserved
