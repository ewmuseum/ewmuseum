CWO REPORT 4/9/97

10 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tonight's house show: WEDNESDAY, APRIL 9, 1997 BANGKOK, THAILAND Joe Eagle vs. Serpentmaster Masters of Hardcore vs. Deadly Sins Mondo Gecko vs. Clark Gray Baby Waldo vs. Hellhound Moses vs. Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Focus vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mercenaries vs. Bad Guys (champions) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): "Monster" Rich Clark (champion) vs. Sloth _______________________________________________________________________________ (Arlon Flaym is in the interview area.) ARLON FLAYM: Well, well, well. Ganglord. It looks as though you HAVE been defeated. And let this be a lesson to anyone and everyone. If you think you can push your way through Flaym without some reckoning, review your thoughts. And you, El puebla tu tene poco huevos pendahoe blah blah. Don't think I'm going to be scared by your "Nothing stops me, blah blah." I'll bury your head so far into your torso, you'll need to drop your pants to say Hello. Ganglord. Think you and your homies want another shot. Bring it on. And better make that shot a buckshot, cause yo' nine milameeta didn't fase dis. The Flaym Blazes on! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire and Bloodbath are in the locker room after April Armageddon.) WILDFIRE (very upset, and yelling): You knew you couldn't beat us! We had you beat, we should be the North American Tag Team Champions right now! (Wildfire grabs a chair and and slams it into a locker.) WILDFIRE: We want a rematch! But not just any match! We want you in a STEEL CAGE match! (The camera turns to Bloodbath, who has a very disturbing look on his face.) BLOODBATH (dark and mysterious): We have been delt a grave injustice! We were robbed of the titles.... WILDFIRE (cutting in and yelling): Yeah, you're damn right we were robbed! You know it, the fans know it, and the CWO championship comittee knows it! You mark my words, Bad Guys, we will gain our revenge! You like no rules, the WILD SIDE can handle no rules! Now it's time to kick a little @$$! (Wildfire walks out of the locker room and slams the door.) BLOODBATH: We will be champions, next time.... (The camera fades out as the mysterious bells toll.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (The MASTERS OF HARDCORE are sitting in their favorite bar, each with a beer, both CHRIS & BRAD have stiches on their foreheads, and they don't look too happy about it. Brian is sitting there, also looking none too impressed with Sunday Night's match. "ThunderKiss '65" by White Zombie is playing on the stereo system in the bar.) "MR. EXTREME" CHRIS OHLHOFF: Eternal Wanderers, last night you found out that the war is not over. We aren't finished with you yet. It's just this simple, you wanted to get rid of us, and now we're gonna get rid of you. BRAD "THE MAN" COUCHMAN: That's right, Wanderers. We may have been busted open, but it's through no fault of yours that that happened. Now, on to more pressing matters. Brian, how's that Highlanders situation going? "BEUATIFUL" BRIAN CLARK: I'm working on it. Don't worry, by the time I'm done, we won't be having to deal with those b******* any more. Things are still falling into place, and guess what? When I work things out, neither of you will ever have to worry about anything ever again. "MR. EXTREME" CHRIS OHLHOFF: I'll trust you on that one, because you are the man when it comes to swinging huge suprise deals, and I know that this one will be just as big. BRAD "THE MAN" COUCHMAN: Highlanders, Bad Guys, Regulators, Creatures of The Night, we don't give a s***. Just be prepared to fight it out against the BEST tag team in the world, because we are not going to be stopped anything, and that's the bottom line, plain and simple. (THEY pay for their beers, and walk out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Cameras open on a mountaintop, clouds all around. Zeus is sitting alone.) ZEUS: Well, it has begun. We have made our mark with a succesfull weekend. Beware, because we will not stop until we have rightfully returned glory to Olympus. I issue a challenge to any and all that feel they deserve a shot at the New Men, but beware, a New Man gives their best first.... (With that, a crash of lighting and thunder and a silhouette of a big man crushing rocks in bare hands is seen as the camera fades.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wild Bunch are in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Clark Gray, you have made a huge mistake signing on for this match because I am gonna kick your teeth in. I am tired of you saying how much grief you have been through because of the death of your cousin. I understand because I had my grandfather die the day of Spring Break '97. But you have had a bad attitude and have been a punk lately. So Clark Gray, it is time for me to get extreme on your sorry hide because I am on a new mission and that is to become the best wrestler in the CWO. Clark Gray, your future looks very bleak because I am going to TEAR YOU APART, BBAABBYY! REGULATOR ICE: Mercenaries, you guys seem to have had a lot to say about us the last time we heard from you. Bottom line here is that we are going to kick you so hard your dog will bleed. We are tired of having to watch as overrated chumps like you guys hold the belts. Well, it is time for us to get a little more agressive and a little more meaner in the ring. You two had better pray that the CWO officials are there because we are going to DESTROY YOU TWO! REGULATOR FIRE: Mercenaries, you losers will be dealt with by us when we annhilate you guys for good. You see, we came here to regulate the CWO and that is what we are going to do, NO MATTER WHAT! We are tired of playing games with you pathetic chumps. It is time for us to get down to war and get serious about our work. Mercenaries, GET READY FOR HELL BECAUSE THAT IS WHERE YOU TWO ARE GOING. EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: Tiger Rogers, I finally get my chance at championship gold and I am not about to let it slip through my fingers. For years I have dreamed of this moment, waiting to hoist the belt up in the air and make my fans and family proud of me. Now I get the chance to knock you off the top of the mountain and win the title. Tiger Rogers, you have been nothing but a cheater and what is going to happen when you must face me and not be able to rely on Jimbo Jones to bail you out. Thursday will MARK THE ERA OF THE NEW CHAMPION, EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO, VIVA LA WILD BUNCH! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Bane is standing in a lit room where you can see him clearly. He is sweaty and his muscles are so tense that they seem to want to burst out of his flesh. A fire of hate glows in his eyes that burns everything that beholds it. He has his arms streched out and is holding a shovel on the back of his shoulders.) BANE (hatred hisses from his lips): Clark... you dared to say that you disposed of your Bane? By doing this, you only disposed of yourself. I showed you who the real monster is, and this monster will eat you alive. My back was on the mat for three seconds, but do not ever fool yourself into believing that you beat me. No man beats the unbeatable. I took what you hungered for... a pathetic belt. A belt means nothing to me, but it is a symbol for you. A symbol that you are something. You have no belt now, and you have once again become nothing. You call yourself a monster, but I know that you are not. Inside, you are a trembling child afraid of something that you cannot understand. I am what you had your daddy look in your closet and under your bed for. I am what you have always feared. A weak little man once asked me what I am afraid of. I fear nothing and no one. I bathe in fear, I f*** fear, I breed fear. Some say I do not possess a soul. I have no need for one. I have no needs, no feelings, nothing but hate. (Bane takes the shovel off of his back, and rests it standing up on his shoulder. The head of the shovel seems golden. The letters C W O can be seen upon the belt.) BANE: Here is your precious belt, Clark. Do not worry. You will be seeing it a lot in your future. You will feel its gold caress your skin once again as it pounds against your flesh. You will become one with your title again as it penetrates your hide. (Bane stares at the camera his eyes boiling with rage) BANE: Run, "monster," your Bane is after you and he is pissed. Your Bane has arrived.
