CWO — Anniversary Preview

CWO Report Special Edition: The Story So Far

14 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT SPECIAL EDITION: THE STORY SO FAR graphic and music comes up as we go to Johnny Starr and David "The Truth" Thomas are on the CWO REPORT set.)
JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to a CWO REPORT SPECIAL REPORT, THE STORY SO FAR for the upcoming CWO PPV, CWO CELEBRATION, our one-year anniversary. I'm Johnny Starr, we have 16 great matches to profile, let's dive right in!
DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS: Hold it, Starr, you forgot to introduce me! STARR: Oh yes, and David "The Truth" Thomas has... fortunately... agreed to join us. Anyway, let's first look at the two matches that will be on
EYE ON THE STORM! EYE ON THE STORM: D. E. A. T. H. vs. Hollywood Muscle STARR: A feud is brewing between Blood Inc. and the Four Aces, this tag match might be one to watch.
THOMAS: That's right, but those two pretty boys of Hollywood Muscle can't deal with the street smarts of Benton and Green. Did you see that segment the other day, with those idiots answering fan mail? Pathetic. I bet they wrote of it themselves!
EYE ON THE STORM: Cake Boy vs. Sandman Jr. STARR: The very bizarre Cake Boy has made his first target in the CWO the now "reformed" Sandman Jr., who will have to try his hardest to stay that way in his battle against this new adversary.
THOMAS: Don't even get me started on the "reformation." Sandman Jr. is going to learn that when you quit swimming with the sharks, and become a bleeding-heart, that the other sharks will smell that blood and devour you.
Flame Spirits vs. Eternal Wanderers STARR: The Flame Spirits have proven to be a surprising tag team; they are capable of upsets at any time, as they have beaten both the Creatures of the Night and the Pit Bosses and non-title affairs. Griffen and Wolf will have to contend, though, with th e very tough and unorthodox team of
Exodus and Exile, along with Lord Guru, many people in the CWO have been buzzing about championship potential for that team.
THOMAS: Including myself, of course! The Flame-outs will not be able to contend with Exile and Exodus, the brilliance of Lord Guru will see to that!
Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. Dark Raven STARR: Originally supposed to be a Light Heavyweight championship defense,
Dark Raven then dropped the title to Moses.
THOMAS: A fluke! Anyway, Schillo's never beaten Dark Raven before, there's no reason to think he'll do it here.
STARR: However, Schillo, the former CWO World Champion, is looking for revenge. As we show you footage from the December 3 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, you can see a vicious attack by Dark Raven on Schillo, trying to suffocate him with a plastic bag! This, alon g with several other factors, led to
Schillo losing the title to Master of Darkness Damion.
THOMAS: Hey, can we replay that suffocation part again? I love it! Mercenaries vs. The Big Cool STARR: Are they fan favorites? Are they rulebreakers? Do they care? The
Mercenaries have their own agenda, and they are going up against the now
manager-less Big Cool. THOMAS: The biggest mistake they've ever made! Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart STARR: Now calling himself the Clique of One, Brent "The Ace" Alles takes on his former stablemate, "Master of Pain" Barry Hart.
THOMAS: He's the Clique of One because nobody WANTS to be his friend. Anyway, this is a tough one to call, I have to admit. Even though I think he's an idiot for leaving the gravy train, Alles still has the serious wrestling skill to beat Hart. However , Hart's aerial excellence could be the deciding factor in this contest.
Team Blitzkrieg vs. Apocalypse STARR: Apparently, the Apocalypse will be at CWO CELEBRATION, after their recent arrest by the authorities.
THOMAS: And they're mad, let me tell you! Team Blitzkrieg will be blitzed by Plague and Pestilence, whose short time in prison probably didn't make them any happier.
SCAFFOLD MATCH: Wildfire vs. Ganglord STARR: A huge rivalry has erupted between these two men ever since they arrived in the CWO. As we show you footage from CWO televised and house show events, you can see Ganglord's interference has cost Wildfire a number of matches. Now, the young Venice Beach resident wants revenge in the worst way, on top of a scaffold 20 feet above the ring!
THOMAS: Yikes! I'm afraid of heights! Anyway, Wildfire's an idiot, hopefully Ganglord will break him like he broke Tub-O-Blub. Of course, if he breaks Wildfire, gravy probably won't pour out.
Deadly Sins vs. Pit Bosses STARR: Formerly supposed to be for the CWO North American Tag Team Championship, the Bosses dropped the belts to the Bad Guys, and now this match is a non-title affair.
THOMAS: How many times can the Sins be screwed out of a title shot? Even though their anger will carry them a long way, they are missing their manager, Lust, ever since Bane, or whoever, kidnapped her. The Bosses are going to be more aggressive after lo sing the titles, if you can even imagine that. Look for the Bosses to win it in a tough one.
LOCKED STEEL CAGE: Sandman vs. Bane STARR: The one that everybody is talking about. We have seen the CNN reports on a masked man causing terror across the country. Is it Bane?
No one knows for sure, but the evidence certainly lends credence to the theory. THOMAS: Hey, in this country, you're innocent until proven guilty. STARR: That's true. However, as CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson put it: "An open invitation... to both men... to settle the score." We know
Sandman will be there, however, will Bane?
THOMAS: If Bane is there, he better take a good look at that cage, he might be spending more time in one soon enough!
STARR: We tried to get comments from CWO superstars about the Bane situation, but most were pretty tight-lipped about it. However, some people did speak up. Let's get their comments.
(Ganglord and T.A.S. are just chillin' at home.) GANGLORD: Ya know sumptin', T, even wit' all da stuff dat been goin' on in my career lately, ya can't help but notice da man, Bane, and how everyone been gettin' on da boy's case, well, Bane, here's my message to you: keep doin' what you doin', man, 'caus e I got nuttin' but respect for a man that knows what his job is and will stop at nuttin to finish the job. So to you, Bane, 'nuff respect to ya, man!
T. A. S.: Yeah boy, keep it, man! (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area.) SANDMAN JR.: Y'know, I've been watching what Bane's been doing, and while in the past I may have loved it, I don't anymore. I like the fact he kidnapped that... THING... Lust, but he's messing with Sandman, THE toughest man in CM2WF/CWO HISTORY! Bane, y ou can mess with innocent civilians all you want, but Sandman is going to take care of you once and for all. And then hopefully the cops will kill you. If Sandman doesn't do it first!
(Back to Johnny and David.) THOMAS: Well, Ganglord knows what time it is, but Sandman Jr., as always, is an idiot. STARR: Nevertheless, possibly the most anticipated match of the night,
Sandman collides with Bane again.
Hoss and Little Joe vs. David and Goliath STARR: These two teams have had some run-ins lately, some are saying this might be the sleeper match of the night.
THOMAS: Why, because it'll put us to sleep? TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (SPIN THE WHEEL) Maximum Overdrive vs. Miguel Sanchez (champion) STARR: Trying to prove that he is the "Master of Hardcore," Miguel Sanchez goes up against his arch enemy Maximum Overdrive in a Spin the Wheel match.
THOMAS: Master of Hardcore, whatever. Go play some bingo with your friends and those blue-haired old ladies. Maximum Overdrive knows what time it is, and he'll get the job done. The only bad thing he's done in month has been teaming with that idiot col lege goof, Schillo.
STARR: As you watch the footage, you can see the many attacks that Overdrive has perpetrated on Sanchez, Sanchez seeking to settle the score with Overdrive at CELEBRATION.
THOMAS: Don't forget, though, that Sanchez also has his mind on that nutcase Sin, that could prove the difference, mental concentration, since
Sanchez already has a problem with that, as well as tying his shoes.
NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Gods of Thunder vs. Bad Guys (champions) STARR: The new North American Tag Team Champions put their titles on the line now against the Gods of Thunder. These two teams have had run-ins in the past, with the titles now on the line, this match becomes even more intense!
THOMAS: That's right! And even though the Gods of Thunder FINALLY seem to be coming out of their slump, the Bad Guys are just too bad for them, they're going to keep those belts!
NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (BATTLE FOR THE CWO): Hile Troy vs. Gold Kard (champion) STARR: All eyes will be on this one, as the one-year anniversary celebration of the CWO could be its last as well if Hile Troy cannot defeat Gold Kard and win the stock needed to take control away from Gold
Kard.
THOMAS: It's not going to happen. So, anyway, Starr, did you put your application in at Mickey D's yet? (Laughs)
STARR: Very funny. Hile Troy, formerly known as Lord Foul the Despiser, has a long history with Gold Kard, Gold Kard once pretended to be his "#1 fan." This was shattered when Gold Kard broke a boombox against Lord
Foul's head. A long feud then progres sed, and it culminates this Sunday, with all the marbles at stake. Hile Troy has to, I mean HAS TO, win this match.
THOMAS: No he doesn't. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Biblical Powers vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) STARR: The Creatures of the Night have proven to be the most formidable team in the league ever since they won the titles. However, if there is a team that can take the titles away from them, many feel it is the Biblical
Powers.
THOMAS: Those two nutcases are too worried about passing the collection plate to concentrate on the titles. And with Moses stealing the Light
Heavyweight belt away from Dark Raven, you can be sure Night Crawler and
Night Slinger will have something to "d iscuss" with that guy.
WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb vs. Master of Darkness Damion (champion) STARR: The main event, on a night of many great matches. Master of Darkness Damion has vowed that Johnny Reb will never hold the gold as long as he is in the league. Johnny Reb has vowed to break that vow of MOD's.
Johnny Reb also has General Lee AND f ormer CWO World Champion Rebel Yell in his corner, that may prove the difference.
THOMAS: Yeah, for Damion. With those two idiots advising Reb, there's no way he can win! STARR: Anyway, as you can see, 16 great matches, a true PPV spectacular for the CWO. Anyway, we hope you enjoyed the CWO REPORT SPECIAL EDITION,
I'm Johnny Starr, for David "The Truth" Thomas, we'll see you Sunday!
