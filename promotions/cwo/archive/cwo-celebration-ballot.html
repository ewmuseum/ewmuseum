CWO CELEBRATION BALLOT

25 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The CWO's final PPV of the year, CWO CELEBRATION, is coming soon, on December 14, 1997, to be exact. CWO CELEBRATION has always been known as a time to recognize the fans of the CWO who have made us what we are. So, this year, we have decided to let the fans of the CWO pick the #1 contender to the World Championship currently held by Johnny Reb! Below are the top 10 choices, choose one and send your ballot in. Ballots will be collected until midnight on November 30, and the winner will be announced on the December 2nd TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Who is eligible to vote? Well, basically everybody who is reading this: handlers, fans, passive observers, whatever. Send your vote in today, and be part of CWO history! 1) Masher Maccabee: A former North American champion, Masher Maccabee has had some good battles with Johnny Reb in the past. 2) Diamondback Chris Myers: Myers had a match with Reb at TERROR NIGHT '97, and lost it due to outside interference from Reb's trainer, Rebel Yell. Some say that Myers is due for a rematch. 3) Lord Hile Troy: The "legend" of the CWO, he has held the World title before, and it may be his time to do so again. 4) El Grande Pistolerro: The daredevil Mexican luchador has proven himself time and time again in the ring, and may prove an exciting match to Johnny Reb. 5) Sandman Jr.: The "hardcore icon" of the CWO lost his match at NOVEMBER PAIN '97 due to outside distraction by Master of Darkness Damion. Should he get a rematch? 6) Joe Eagle: The comeback story of 1997 is back on the championship trail with an impressive victory over Painmaster at NOVEMBER PAIN '97. Eagle had a great match with Reb at SEPTEMBER SLAM II, and let's remember that Reb was the one who betrayed Eagle at TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES, where Reb won the title. 7) Mondo Gecko: One of the most popular superstars in the CWO, Mondo Gecko's high-flying, devil-may-care attitude may prove exciting to watch against the World Champion. 8) Brian "The Hammer" Williams: The CWO March Madness Champion for 1997, Williams would love a shot at the World Champion. 9) Southern Dynamite: Former Light Heavyweight Champion, this would be a "battle of the South." 10) Gold Kard: The CEO of GKI, which now financially backs the Dark Side, was beaten by Johnny Reb at TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES and has never received a rematch. Is this his time? There you have it, the ten choices for the #1 contender to the World title at CWO CELEBRATION. Send your vote in today!
