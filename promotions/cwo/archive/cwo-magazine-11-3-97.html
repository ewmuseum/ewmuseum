3 November 1997

CWO Magazine Nov. 3, 1997 Features: A Night of Terror... Le Ann Stevenson Departments: Krash Kourse.... Krash Kelly ************************************************************************ A Letter From the Editor Welcome to the first issue of the CWO Magazine. Let me tell you a little about the magazine and what's going to be in it. The CWO Magazine will be published once a week. We will go behind the scenes at Tuesday Night Fever and Sunday Showcase as well as House Shows. The new CWO broadcaster, Double K Krash Kelly will have his weekly column, Krash Kourse, featuring his opinion on the happenings in the CWO. Every week starting with the next issue we will showcase a CWO wrestler by taken an in-depth look at their career, even before they joined the CWO. All this and much more in the weeks to come. If there is anything you want to see in the CWO Magazine just drop me a line at [detail withheld] . Remember, this is your magazine! ************************************************************************ Krash Kourse by: Krash Kelly Hello everyone, I'm Krash Kelly. Welcome to the Krash Kourse! This week I want to talk about Terror Night 97. I want to talk about the CWO World Champion Johnny Reb. Now there will be another article in this publication about Mr. Reb, but I have my own opinion. Lets go back to the championship match. Johnny Reb taking on "Diamondback" Chris Myers. Now Myers had a lot going for him. He was on a roll in the win column and all signs pointed to a new champion being crowned at Terror Night, but that was not the case. Now in my own opinion, Myers was robbed of the world title and should be champion right now. But Johnny Reb couldn't take a loss like a man and had to cheat. That's right, cheat! Not only was Chris Myers cheated, but you the fans as well. All the fans who new that Chris Myers could beat Reb. Next time Reb, don't be so selfish and take a loss like a man. That's it for this week, I'm Krash Kelly. ************************************************************************ A Night of Terror by: Le Ann Stevenson One of the most gruesome sites took place last Sunday at CWO Terror Night 97. It was the triple cage of terror... (Pictures of the bloodied faces of Wildfire and The Focus) Although the team of the CPE did come out on top, both teams suffered a great deal. Especially El Grande Pistolerro, who was thrown from the top of the thirty feet structure and went crashing through a table. (Picture of Pistolerro crashing through the table) Now we haven't received any word on the condition of Pistolerro but we hope for the best. In the second part of that triple main event, Sandman Jr. came out on top in the special Anniversary match. Now Sanchez won the first part by knocking Sandman Jr. off the scaffold. (Picture of Sanchez throwing Sandman Jr. off the scaffold.) As Sandman Jr. went crashing through table it seemed to be over. But the young superstar came back to win the second and final parts of the match, gaining the victory. And in the CWO Championship match, Johnny Reb, the CWO World Champion, successfully defended his title with a little help from Rebel Yell... (Picture of Rebel Yell hitting Myers with a chair.) I'm sure Chris Myers will not take this lying down and will receive a rematch. In another of the three title matches, the Newton Brothers defeated the Highlanders to become the new CWO World Tag Team Champions. (Picture of the Newton Brothers with the world tag team titles.) There was an interesting twist in the CWO North American Title match. With the injury to the ankle of Fergus McTavish Masher Maccabee was slated to defend the title against a mystery opponent. And that opponent turned out to be none other than Mondo Gecko. Gecko brought McTavish to ringside with him but that proved to be too much for The Whirling Scottish Devil as he came into the ring and hit Maccabee with one of his crutches, giving Maccabee the win by DQ. (Picture of McTavish hitting Maccabee with the crutch.) In other action, Masashi "Lightning" Lanther got the victory over "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat. Foolkiller got the 1 ... 2 ... 3 on "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher. Now Deutscher did have some damage done to his neck, we didn't receive any word on the condition but we hope for the best for him and his family. The Law got the win over the Cabin. The Painmaster got an UPSET victory over Brian "The Hammer" Williams. In tag team action the Mercenaries put a "w" in the win column by beating Mason-Dixon. Carlos Maia nailed Wayne "College Guy" Schillo with the following in love to get the win. Then there was the stretcher match. Brent "Ace" Alles taking on Lord Hile Troy. Now Amanda Bloom, the girlfriend of Alles, went into labor during the match so Allis left to go to the hospital... (Picture of Brent Allis getting in the ambulance with Amanda Bloom.) But Lord Hile Troy wasn't gonna let the match end there. Troy flagged down a taxi and followed the ambulance to the hospital. He then proceeded to attack Alles at the hospital. We then lost camera feed and received no further word on the situation. (Picture of Troy and Alles going at it at the hospital.) Southern Dynamite defeated Carlos Maia that night on Showcase to become the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion. He then went on to successfully defend that title at Terror Night against Moses. And last but certainly not least the Deadly Sins defeated the Big Cool to become the new CWO North American Tag Team Champions. All in all it was a great night. Now don't forget to watch CWO November Pain on Nov. 23! ************************************************************************ Editor: Ryan Deutscher ( [detail withheld] ) Editor-in-chief: Brent G.
