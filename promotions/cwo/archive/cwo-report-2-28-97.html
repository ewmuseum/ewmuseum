CWO REPORT 2/28/97

28 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens in the streets of Glasgow; walking around this ancient city are The Highlanders, Conor and Gavin McCloud.) GAVIN: Och, it feels good ta be back on native soil; we grew up not too far away from here, and ta here people speak Scottish again is like music ta my ears. And right here, in front of a packed crowd of frenzied Celts, The Highlanders go up against the Bad Guys; we've beaten ye once on your soil, now, welcome to our world. CONOR: An' this is gonna be a part of our warm-up runnin' into Spring Break - and our head-ta'-head with those big girls' blouses, the Eternal Wanderers. So, ye've got a big surprise coming for the CWO? We're all gonna be shocked and surprised, and tha' federation will never be tha' same? Ye mean ye're actually gonna win a match? Well, lets not go oe'er tha' top.... GAVIN: Ye keep mumblin' yer mystical bollocks, Wanderers, an' we'll keep on trainin' - and then we'll see who'll come out on top. Oh, and on a final note... Deadly Sins, ye bunch of poofs, come and have a go if ye think ye're hard enough; my Gran used ta' cook tougher haggis than you lot. Remember.... CONOR: There can be only one! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the streets of Glasgow, Scotland. The CWO team of DOOMSDAY is walking down the streets. Overdrive is still showing effects of the Allesbreaker he suffered on Tuesday Night Fever. Waldo and the Mercenaries are wearing their CWO 6 Man Tag Team Titles. They walk into one of the many pubs on the street and sit down at the bar. A report comes onto the local news about the card being held in the city, along with interviews by the Deadly Sins, Brent "The Ace" Alles, and the Wild Bunch. DOOMSDAY breaks into laughter as they watch.) MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: So Alles, you think that by using your tactics of desperation that you can run me from this league? Guess again. You can stoop to whatever level you can sink to, it just shows that you're grasping for whatever last remnants of a career you may have had. I know now that I really have nothing to fear from a scared little man like yourself. BARTENDER (in a Scottish accent): That Alles guy was just in here a few minutes ago. I think he's in the toilet. OVERDRIVE (getting up): Well, let's just see if he's still here. (Overdrive walks to the bathroom. Before he reaches the door, a crash of glass can be heard. Overdrive opens the door, revealing a shattered window. The running of feet can be heard, fading in the distance. The Bartender is laughing his head off.) BARTENDER: Just before you came in here, he couldn't stop talking about how he wasn't afraid of ye. HA HA HA HA! He even left his wallet behind. (Overdrive picks up Alles' wallet and takes out one of his credit cards.) OVERDRIVE: You boys hungry? (The Mercenaries and Baby Waldo grab a menu and start to order. Waldo orders three of everything on the menu. Overdrive hands Alles' credit card over to the Bartender.) OVERDRIVE: Here. Put it on this. Alles won't mind. While we're waiting for the food, what's on the CWO's 6 Man Tag Team Champions' mind? KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Well, I'm glad to hear that the Wild Bunch are actually going to try to stay in the ring to fight us tonight. It was a pretty difficult match last time, because they kept trying to run back to the locker rooms. None of those guys wanted to tag in to take us on. I can't help but think that their interview sounds like it did last week when it was all we could do to keep them inside the ring. Do you think it's medically possible to grow a spine in seven days? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: As for the Deadly Sins, it's going to take a lot more than those two underachievers to beat us in the ring. We've shown them before we are more than capable of handling them. As for beating us at Spring Break for the titles, I don't think so. We've taken on better teams than they can put together, but those teams weren't that much better, come to think of it. (The Bartender begins to bring plates and plates of food out to the team. After two hours of eating, DOOMSDAY prepares to leave. But before they do, Overdrive takes another credit card from Alles' wallet and gives the Bartender a $300 tip.) OVERDRIVE: Thanks for everything. The service and the food was really great. BARTENDER: Thank ye. You four just go out and whup them scoundrels justly tonight. OVERDRIVE: Well, here's a ticket for a front row seat. Enjoy. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, February 28, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. We are currently in the middle of our British Invasion tour, and last night we were in Glasgow, Scotland. Let's check out the results of that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS Thursday, 2/27/97 Glasgow, Scotland Hellhound and Sandman Jr. wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned 'Master of Darkness' Damion after the Flash Flood. Time of match: 1:22. 'Master of Darkness' Damion was attacked outside the ring by Bishop - who hit him with a fireball to the face - which led to the quick victory. Sloth and Moses wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned Johnny Reb after the dropkick and outside interference by Gold Kard. Time of match: 15 : 32 Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned Maximum Overdrive after the Allesbreaker. Time of match: 8:28. Brent 'The Ace' Alles pulled the tights for the win. Alliance (Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers, Inferno, David "The Destroyer" Davis, Goliath) defeated Unholy Alliance (Bishop, Vlad the Impaler, Dark Raven, Serpentmaster) in an elimination match. Survivors: David 'The Destroyer' Davis, Tiger Rogers. Goliath eliminated Bishop in 8: 57. David 'The Destroyer' Davis eliminated Vlad the Impaler in 30: 43. Goliath eliminated Dark Raven in 34: 59. Serpentmaster eliminated Goliath in 35: 39. Serpentmaster eliminated Inferno in 38: 51. David 'The Destroyer' Davis eliminated Serpentmaster in 38: 17. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Champions Doomsday (Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries) defeated Wild Bunch (Battlecat and the Regulators) when Baby Waldo made Battlecat submit to the Cradle Rocker. Time of match: 25:45. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Bad Guys defeated Highlanders when Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker pinned Conor McCloud after the hit with the cellular phone. Time of match: 16:05. The obviously partisan crowd was enraged by this, and security had to escort the Bad Guys out as fans tried to attack them on their way out. CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE (STEEL CAGE): World Champion Wayne 'Keuka Guy' Schillo pinned Television Champion Masher Maccabee after the Keuka Crack. Time of match: 12:07. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Dublin, Ireland, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FEBRUARY 28, 1997 DUBLIN, IRELAND "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. Master Of Darkness Damion Mercenaries vs. David and Goliath Hellhound vs. Inferno Big Cool vs. Highlanders The Focus vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley Mason-Dixon vs. Regulators LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, taped fist): Masher Maccabee (champion) vs. Master of Darkness Damion WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Pit Bosses vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Savior (retirement match) vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Quickly becoming one of the most despised men in the CWO, CWO Television Champion Masher Maccabee currently has his sights on destroying the career of one Mondo Gecko. Let's go to the Masher for comments. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Masher is sitting in the locker room, flexing in front of the mirror.) MASHER MACCABEE: Mondo... Mondo... I've whooped your @$$ over two continents! DIG THAT! I took your belt, and now, I'll take your life, and maybe Tamara as a prize. She'd look good next to Rabbi Solomon. Gecko, you stupid punk, don't you get it? I am the future of the CWO, I am the greatest physical specimen ever to enter the ring. I can't be beat, and I won't be beat. There won't be any quick pins, Mondo, I am going to torture you, crucify you, real bad! Remember what happened to the English when they messed with the Americans, they got an @$$kicking! Well, you can eat all the tea and crumpets you want, Mondo, it just gives me more incentive to destroy you! I am the WORLD TELEVISION CHAMPION, and I will destroy anyone who tries to take what I possess. CWO, bring it on! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow!
