CWO REPORT 5/9/97

9 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Wild Bunch is at a classy looking bar in Mexico City as they are celebrating a great night with two victories. Mondo Gecko and Tamara Wilcox are cuddling together, the Regulators each have a women on their side, drinking beer by the pitcher. El Grande Pistolerro is being swarmed by five beautiful women and they are hovering and cuddling with him.) EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: Last night was a great night, beating a former friend and student Lizmark Jr., and then seeing Gecko beat Maximum Overdrive. Tonight, however, will be another great night. First, I am going to pull off an upset and beat the massive Centurion. Centurion, you are a tough competitor, mi amigo, and I am looking forward to our encounter. However, as they say, the bigger they are, the harder they fall, and that is what you are going to do. I am on a roll as of late and nothing is going to stop me from winning gold here. MONDO GECKO: Gold Kard, the biggest loser in the CWO, I am going to put you in a world of hurt and this time I am going to get very nasty. You are nothing more than a common racist and soon I will do the CWO a great favor by ridding them of you. Gold Kard, GET READY FOR MONDO EXTREME EXCITEMENT! REGULATOR FIRE: This is going to be great, for not only do we get to beat up seven other teams to get a World Title shot at Summer Heat, but we get to destroy the Bad Guys and prove that we should be the North American Champions. The Regulators are here to regulate the CWO and win titles. Tell 'em, Ice! REGULATOR ICE: WELL! It seems that the Bad Guys want a beating! Well, we don't have a problem with that and we would love to deal you two chumps a beating and it will be a preview of what will happen at Summer Heat when we win the titles. OOOOHHHHH WWWWWHHHHHAAAATTTT AAAAAA RRRRRUUUUUSSSSSHHHHH! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, May 9, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night was not only a very explosive FEVER from Santa Fe, NM, but also some great action was held after the televised portion of the show was finished. Let's first take a look at what went down on FEVER on our FEVER FLASHBACK! _______________________________________________________________________________ FEVER FLASHBACK 5/8/97 "LIVE" FROM SANTA FE, NM DARK MATCH (STRAP MATCH): Matthew 'The Man' White defeated Brent 'The Ace' Alles in a strap match. Time of match: 22:51. DARK MATCH: Diamondback Chris Myers defeated Centurion and Steel in a triangle match. Centurion eliminated Steel in 1:45 after the Arakkis (slingshot brainbuster). Diamondback Chris Myers eliminated Centurion in 11:15 after the spinning elbowdrop. MATCH ONE: Masters of Hardcore ("Mr. Extreme" Chris Ohlhoff and Brad "The Man" Couchman) won an eight team battle royal to determine the #1 contender to the World Tag Team Championships. Brother Justice was the wild card team. Here is the order of elimination: 1. Ghost Riders (Blaze and Vengeance) were eliminated at 4:50 when Korperal Punishment eliminated Vengeance 2. Mercenaries (Kaptain Krunch and Korperal Punishment) were eliminated at 7:21 when Jermain Justice eliminated Kaptain Krunch 3. Regulators (Regulators Fire and Ice) were eliminated at 8:21 when Ohlhoff eliminated Ice 4. New Men (Akillies and Spartacus) were eliminated at 9:46 when Couchman eliminated Akillies 5. The Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jim "Jazzman" Taylor) were eliminated at 10:04 when Vlad the Impaler eliminated Taylor 6. Brother Justice (Jermain and Jerome Justice) were eliminated at 11:47 when Couchman eliminated Jerome 7. Vlad the Impaler and Serpentmaster were eliminated at 14:05 when Couchman eliminated Vlad after Serpentmaster unwittingly struck Vlad IN-RING INTERVIEW: With the Unholy Alliance. Bishop congratulated Dark Raven and Hellhound on their fine performance in the battle royal, and said that Hellhound had been sent to Japan to pursue Master of Darkness Damion and the Creatures of the Night. Vlad the Impaler and Dark Raven then grabbed Serpentmaster and forced him to his knees. Bishop said that because Serpentmaster had attacked Hellhound he was a traitor, and that he had to pay. Bishop then did the Hellfire (a fireball) right in Serpentmaster's face. Bishop was going to do another one, but Vlad the Impaler then rocked Bishop with a right hook. Vlad then attacked the already injured arm of Bishop. As Dark Raven tried to defend Bishop, another man, a huge man, appeared and threw Dark Raven into a wall. The big man then picked up Serpentmaster and carried him off, with Vlad following behind. MATCH TWO: CWO North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys (Joe "The Mean Guy" Rucker and Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson) defended their titles again in a street fight with the Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) when Johnson pinned Bloodbath after the Bad Guys' Bash (double strike with steel chairs). Herbie Versmells, the Bad Guys' manager and parole officer, held the leg of Bloodbath providing extra leverage for the pin. "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher was down at ringside to protect the Wild Side from any funny business, but both he and Wildfire were hit with steel chairs as well, preventing them from saving Bloodbath. IN-RING INTERVIEW: With Maximum Overdrive. Overdrive challenged Brent "The Ace" Alles to face him in the ring. Alles then appeared in the balcony with a microphone, saying that he wasn't going to walk into an ambush, and that he was finished with Overdrive. The Mercenaries then attacked Alles from behind with steel chairs, causing him to fall over the balcony guardrail. As Alles clutched to it for dear life, the Mercenaries struck his hands with the chairs, and Alles fell from the second floor down to the first. Luckily, he was not seriously injured from that, however, Baby Waldo then dragged him to the ring, where Maximum Overdrive delivered the Allesbreaker, Alles' own signature hold, on a steel chair to him. Alles had to be taken out on a stretcher. MATCH THREE: CWO Television Champion Johnny Reb defended his title in a match with two-time former Television Champion Masher Maccabee, pinning him after a belly-to-belly suplex in 12:55. This was a tough match, made even more incredible by the fact that Masher Maccabee was able to survive almost three minutes in the dreaded Texas Cloverleaf submission hold. IN-RING INTERVIEW: With "Monster" Rich Clark. Clark talked about how he had driven Bane from the league, and was going to do the same with Goliath and Wayne "College Guy" Schillo. Clark also said that he was not impressed with Joe Eagle's victory at MAY-HEM, and that 57 minutes in that contest would be nothing compared to 20 minutes in the ring with him. The masked man that has been accompanying Clark as of late then angrily confronted Clark about when he would be able to get back in the ring. Clark did his best to assuage him, and the pair left to the cheers of the crowd. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: All right, first of all, let's give you an update on Brent "The Ace" Alles. By some small miracle, Alles managed to escape serious injury, he has a bit of a neck strain and a couple of fractured ribs, but other than that there were thankfully no serious internal injuries. However, doctors have definitely advised Alles not to wrestle for a long time, however, Alles is scheduled for a Stretcher Match this week against Maximum Overdrive. Will he show up? For his sake, we hope not, but Alles' desire for revenge against Maximum Overdrive now must be very strong indeed, so anything is possible. Anyway, like we said at the beginning of the program, after FEVER went off the air, the night wasn't over, as more great action came to the fans in Santa Fe, NM, and let's take a look at what else happened last night. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 5/8/97 Santa Fe, NM Brother Justice defeated Attitude Dance when Jerome Justice pinned Joe Eagle after the Final Justice. Time of match: 13:06. Sandman Jr. defeated Tiger Rogers by disqualification. Time of match: 10:51. El Grande Pistolerro defeated Centurion (substituting for Chris Greco) by countout. Time of match: 11:16. New Men defeated Maximum Overdrive/Baby Waldo when Akillies pinned Maximum Overdrive after the Last Stand (slingshot brainbuster). Time of match: 11:30. Gold Kard pinned Mondo Gecko after the dropkick, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 10:21. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams defeated Goliath by disqualification when the Alliance attacked. Time of match: 13:05. SUBMISSION MATCH: Benjamin Blake Bentley made The Focus submit to the figure four. Time of match: 13:55. CWO North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys defeated Regulators when Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker pinned Regulator Ice after the Bad Guys' Bash (double strike with steel chairs). Time of match: 17:28. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we take a left turn into Albuquerque, let's look at the lineup for that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FRIDAY, MAY 9, 1997 ALBUQUERQUE, NM Steel vs. Sandman Jr. Alliance (Inferno/Tiger Rogers/David "The Destroyer" Davis/Goliath) vs. Tower of Power (Joe Eagle, Jason "Flash" Varga, and the Big Cool) The Focus vs. Centurion Wrath vs. "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher Panther vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley STRETCHER MATCH: Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Maximum Overdrive Deadly Sins vs. New Men TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Johnny Reb (champion) vs. Gold Kard _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with the Wild Side, who once again last night were cheated out of a North American Tag Team Championship victory by the Bad Guys. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire storms into the locker room and Bloodbath follows along with "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher and Miss Amber.) WILDFIRE (yelling): THIS IS BULLS***! THERE'S NO DAMN EXCUSE FOR THIS! THIS IS THE SECOND TIME WE GOT SCREWED OUT OF THOSE F****** TITLES! THEY KNOW THEY CAN'T BEAT US! THEY HAVE TO USE THAT LITTLE PENCIL NECK MANAGER OF THEIRS! (Lone Wolf tries to calm Wildfire, but he gets shoved out of the way.) WILDFIRE (yelling): I'M SO SICK AND TIRED OF THIS HAPPENING! WE GOT SCREWED, YOU KNOW IT, THE FANS KNOW IT, AND THE CWO CHAMPIONSHIP COMMITTEE KNOWS IT! WELL, BAD GUYS, THIS LITTLE FEUD IS FAR FROM OVER! WE FOUGHT OUR WAY UP THE RANKINGS OF THE CWO, ONLY TO GET SCREWED TWICE! YOU CAN BET THAT RANDY JOHNSON WILL, OR WHO EVER THE F****** COMMISSIONER IS, WILL BE HEARING FROM US! THOSE F****** TITLES BELONG TO THE WILD SIDE! (Bloodbath just stands there, with an dark and mysterious look on his face.) WILDFIRE (yelling): ALL I HAVE TO SAY IS THAT YOU BETTER WATCH OUT, BAD GUYS, 'CAUSE YOU'RE GONNA PAY! YOU'RE GONNA PAY IN EVERY SENSE OF THE WORD! 'CAUSE EVERY TIME WE SEE YOU, WE'RE GONNA BEAT THE HELL OUT OF YOU, UNTIL WE NOT ONLY TAKE THOSE TITLES, BUT WE DRIVE YOU OUT OF THE CWO! (Lone Wolf comes in front of the camera, you can still here Wildfire in the background.) "LONE WOLF" RYAN DEUTSCHER: All I have to say, Bad Guys, is that you're not gonna get away with this! Not only was I watching that man that claimed to be "unbeatable", but I was watching you two little punks! I was watching you "cheat" many teams out of those titles! Well, now you've stepped to far into the "WILD SIDE", and there is no possible way of escape! You mark my words, the WILD SIDE will.... (Wildfire cuts in) WILDFIRE (yelling): THIS F****** INTERVIEW IS OVER! (Wildfire picks up a chair and slams it into the camera. The camera gets all fuzzy and fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Obviously, a very emotional Wildfire, and the Wild Side will be definitely looking for a rematch for those titles. However, another great match on FEVER last night was the Television championship match between Johnny Reb and Masher Maccabee. Although he didn't win the title for the third time, Maccabee impressed many by managing to survive almost three minutes in the Texas Cloverleaf and almost come back and win the match. Let's hear from the Masher. _______________________________________________________________________________ Masher is strolling through a blissful suburban neighborhood in New York's Capital District.) MASHER MACCABEE: Ooooo, look who's back on the mic. Everybody's favorite Bagel Boy. Now that I have gotten my life back together after a big change, losing my belt to Inferno, it's time to kick some serious keister! Oy vey, everywhere I go, Mondo Specko is running off at the mouth, Alles is talking, Wayne "Drop Out Guy" is talking, oy vey! All everybody does is talk. Well, it's time "The Enforcer" of GKI starting living up to my moniker as the CWO'S Toughest Man. I grant an open challenge to anyone possessing championship gold, if you think you can withstand the baddest finish on the planet, the HOMENTASCHEN HANGOVER, get in the ring. Highlanders, Bad Guys, 6-Man Champs, who ever you are, me and GK will take your belts too! Jews have always made great jewelers, and I'm looking to work on a little gold! Clark, you're big, but you aren't bad, and if I ever get you in the ring, I'm going to take out hundreds of years of oppression on your @$$ and take that belt to GKI, where it belongs! There's only one thing left to say: WE'RE TAKIN' OVER! GKI 4 LIFE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: A man who also impressed a lot of people as of late is Sandman Jr. who almost won the MAY-HEM battle royal but was thrown out by Joe Eagle after Sandman Jr. threw everything he had at him and Eagle managed to hang on by a thread. Let's hear from the CWO's "extreme icon," well, sort of. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area.) SANDMAN JR.: Well, I hate to say this... but... dammit... JOE EAGLE, you won fair and square and deserve the title shot... congratulations... (Kellen steps out from behind him, she was obviously sticking the Singapore cane in Jr.'s back to get him to congratulate Eagle.) SANDMAN JR.: WAYNE SCHILLO, you also fought great, but I HAVE to have that Light Heavyweight title... so thanks for helping me beat FOCUS, but I HAD to beat you... MOSES, I have nothing but respect for you, but I need the Light Heavyweight belt... NEED IT... so please, don't take it personally when I hurt you badly... what else... hey FOCUS, you said something about beating up BENTLEY? I have an idea, once again proving there is no "off" button on the genius switch... let's set up a triangle gimmick match! I'd prefer barbed wire, but if you two can think of something better, it's possible... TIGER ROGERS, I couldn't beat you when you had the belt... but now you're less.. focused... so be prepared to get caned... STEEL, you and I have gone the distance before, and we'll be doing it again... be prepared, though, for a loss this time! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: They weren't able to be in that eight team battle royal last night, but that doesn't mean the Deadly Sins have went away, let's hear from them now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) SLOTH: NO, we will NOT talk about that embarrassment of a pay-per-view, so stop asking... all I WILL say is all of you better PRAY that me and PRIDE aren't in this tag team battle royal... 'cause if we are... oh man, you guys are ALL dead! WRATH: DEUTSCHER... you call yourself the LONE WOLF... well, a wolf who strays from his pack doesn't last long... and when you face WRATH, you won't last at all! PRIDE: NEW MEN, all men are susceptible to Sins... especially DEADLY ones.. and we're as deadly as they come... CENTURION, AKILLIES, you guys may think you're all that, but we'll show how you ain't NOTHING! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally today, the CWO recently was proud to announce a working agreement with the Superstar Japan Pro Wrestling organization. As part of that arrangement, CWO superstars like Master of the Darkness Damion and the Creatures of the Night will be working with the organization over the summer, and in a talent exchange, several Superstar Japan wrestlers will be competing in the CWO in the months to come, starting with the man who has held the Superstar Japan belt several times, Matsuhiro Torunaka. Let's take a look. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Hard-driving techno music plays as as a montage of images of Matsuhiro Torunaka is shown, including a deadly spin kick, a dragon suplex, a flying dropkick, and a moonsault. Torunaka is then shown lofting the Super Japan Pro Wrestling championship high in the air, and then is shown superimposed over the Japanese flag. Graphics come up: "MATSUHIRO TORUNAKA.") _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We of course look forward to seeing him in action in the months to come, we already saw an incredible performance by him in the MAY-HEM battle royal, where he lasted over 30 minutes and tied Diamondback Chris Myers for the most men thrown out. That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
