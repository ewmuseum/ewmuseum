CWO REPORT 11-6-97 (fwd)

7 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey sitting at the REPORT desk.) WASHEY: Hello everybody and welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Last night the CWO held the start of the weekly House Shows in Milwaulkee, WI. Here are the match results for last night. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 11/05/97 Milwaukee, WI Sandman Jr. pinned 'The Ultimate Heel' Union Caine after the Permanent Dream. Time of match: 19:22. Hellhound and Holocaust wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. El Grande Pistolerro pinned 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia after the Springboard Frankensteiner. Time of match: 6:15. Wild Cards defeated Mason-Dixon when Ace pinned Beauregard Dixon after the Dead Man's hand. Time of match: 17:20. Painmaster and Master of Darkness Damion wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 12:19. Bad Guys defeated CPE Enforcers when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Ranger Taurus after the Bad Guys' Bash. Time of match: 13:59. After the match, the Bad Guys were attacked by the Mercenaries. It took several minutes before security was able to restore order. FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH: Mondo Gecko pinned Reverend after the roundhouse kick. Time of match: 8:09. North American Tag Team Champions the Deadly Sins and Masher Maccabee/Moses wrestled to a double countout in a non-title match. Time of match: 2:47. World Champion Johnny Reb pinned Diamondback Chris Myers after the Southern Hospitality Suplex and outside interference by Rebel Yell in a non-title match. Time of match: 9:55. WASHEY: Tons of action happening last night. If you missed it, you really lost out on a good show. Tonight, the CWO will be appearing in Des Moines, IA. Here's the lineup for tonight's card. Bell time is at 8 o'clock. THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 1997 DES MOINES, IA Tsutomu Kobayashi vs. Kurt Gerstung Foolkiller vs. Red Raven Creatures of the Night vs. Mason-Dixon Master Of Darkness Damion vs. Wildfire Highlanders vs. The Nation of Denomination The Wild Cards vs. the Harvesters of Sorrow "The Ultimate Heel" Union Caine vs. Col. Taggart The Dogs of War ("The Irresistible Assassin" Eddie Fantastic and "Pagan Horse" Logan Fett) vs. Brother Justice "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday vs. The Focus WASHEY: Now it's time for our interviews. First off today, we have a tag team that will be meeting Brother Justice tonight in the Des Moines show. Here are the Dogs of War. _______________________________________________________________________ (Eddie Fantastic and Logan Fett stand outside the Empire Inc. office building. Both men, dressed to kill, glare into the camera. A crowd begins to gather as the "Irresistable Assassin" Eddie Fanastic speaks....) Fantastic (pulling his long red hair into a pony tail) "What the hell does a man have to do? We beat the living dogshit outta the "Bad Guys" and still haven't had any challenges. After leaving this federation a year ago I heard that the competition was scarce but this is damn near ridiculous. We didn't come here to get a paycheck for slacking around and giving interviews all week long. Fett, myself and the rest of Empire are here to drive a point into the heart of the CWO. But how the hell can you accomplish that when nobody steps inside the squared circle with ya?" Fett (While looking at peace with the world, he strokes his long black sideburns as he puffs on a large cigar) "Time heals all wounds. That phrase is proven with Kurt Gerstrung. A little over a year ago I debuted in the CM2WF, and within weeks there were legal papers at Empire's doorstep. What were the papers for you ask? A movement headed by none other than Gerstrung himself, with several other wrestlers, taking action in order to have my finishing manuever banned. What were the consequences? Let me just say that Gerstrung's neck will never be the same. The "Zen Power Slam" broke his neck and put him out of professional wrestling for several months. Now, we return. Under the same name, same stable, same attitude. I'm calling you and your partner Steve White out. You'll forever be linked to the "Pagan Horse", when your old and decrepid...you'll remember me. I put you through the true meaning of pain, are you actually that forgiving? Life is full of lessons Gerstrung. Steo inside the ring with me again and class will be in session." (Both men turn and walk inside the Empire Inc. skyscraper. The camera pans up to view the enormous building as a outilining figure of what appears to be Vince DaVinci emerges. Slowly the "Imperial March" begins to play as the view fades to black.) _________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our next interview also comes from one of the CWO wrestlers that will be appearing tonight. Here is the Focus with his comments. _________________________________________________________________________ [The camera appears on a black room. The only light that is shown is from The Focus' eyes, which have been digitally altered to appear red] Wrath...it seems set that we will face at November Pain. I'm not sure if you care too much, but I happen to know how to do many things that you do not. One of them is to set aside business from personal. When I got in that cage with you on the same team...I knew rage like I've never felt before. I hate you Wrath. Yet, I kept it inside. Then, I hit you...accidently. Perhaps you could have been a man about it, but as your name indicates...you're an angry person. We share that trait. However, I know how to hone my rage better, and stronger than you. I will defeat you at November Pain, and I will show you the hell that your perpetual sin just may lead you to. My hell is worse. My wrath is worse. You are nothing. I am everything. __________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: The Focus apparently responding to the comments made yesterday by Wrath. Finally today, it was yesterday that the tentative schedule for November Pain was announced. Our last interview comes from one of the tag teams participating in that gigantic tournament, and have to be considered one of the heavy favorites to win. I'm talking about none other than the Mercenaries, and here they are with their comments taped after their attack on the Bad Guys during last night's House Show. __________________________________________________________________________ KAPTAIN KRUNCH: What's the matter Bad Girls? It's been over a week now since we've called you out at the PPV, and it seems as if all you can do is ignore the Mercenaries. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Our little attack on you was our way of sending a message that ignoring your problems won't make them go away. Along with the two tag teams that are holding the gold in this federation, you two morons are nothing but a couple of highly overrated chumps. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: It's about time that you two had better wake the hell up and realize that you have a serious problem, and it's knocking on your front door. Of course, there isn't a damn thing that you can do about it..... KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: But at least show the CWO that you can act like a couple of men instead of two ostriches, hiding your stupid heads in the sand, hoping to ride out the storm. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Moving on to better things, we have an announcement to make concerning the big tag team tournament coming up at November Pain. What the Mercenaries are going to do is allow any of the teams that have entered up until a week before the event to pull out completely. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: No questions asked. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: If you don't, and you happen to be any of the second rate teams that the Mercenaries are going to come across during the course of that tournament, we are going to beat you like you have never been beaten before. We are going to embarrass you like you have never been embarrassed before. And you are going to lose like you have never lost before. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Hey, when you look at it that way, doesn't it make sense to get out while you still can? Especially when you know deep down inside that you don't stand a chance against THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD? ___________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody tomorrow on the REPORT! (fade to closing credits.)
