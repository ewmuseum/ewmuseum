HOT FLASH

CWO HOT FLASH (August 8, 1999) On August 29, 1999, the CWO will once again present TEAM CHALLENGE, this year from the UTC Arena in Chatanooga, TN! Teams of four will compete in elimination matches, and this year's there's a big prize at stake!
You see, from each match, one of the survivors will move on to a final match. That three-on-three elimination match at the end of the night will go until there is only one survivor, and that man will then get a World title shot the following Thursday night on FEVER! So obviously, all teams will be gunning for that!
We don't know all the team matchups yet, but we can announce this one, and it is big! EMPIRE Foolkiller, Union Caine, Johnny Reb, "Irresistible" Eddie Fantastic vs. MILLENNIUM Lord Hile Troy, Gold Kard, Diamondback Chris Myers, "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum
Can you imagine a match with 8 of those caliber athletes? We can't either, but it is going to happen at TEAM CHALLENGE '99!
Also, the challenge was made and accepted, and now CWO World Champion Mondo
Gecko (and three of his Elite) will take on Brent "The Ace" Alles and whatever three guys he can come up with to take them on! Considering Alles doesn't have too many friends in the CWO based on his despicable actions in the past, that might be hard! Alles has said that he will take on Gecko and his team by himself if he has to, but that's obviously suicide. Plus, Alles needs to find a team that can win, because if he does, he gets the World title back! But if he doesn't, he will lose his Vice President status and never get a shot at the World title ever again!
Plus, the Dark Side (Darkness, Warhawk, Wrath, and Sandman) will be at TEAM
CHALLENGE '99, and Maximum Overdrive wants to take his brother's team down!
Will Master of Darkness Damion and his disciples, the Creatures of the
Night, join him in his quest? We'll have to see!
More matches are to be announced, including a TRUE AMERICANS vs. SHEETS
FOUNDATION match that should rock the house, but you can find out a lot about what's going to go down at the PPV by watching FEVER this week!
FEVER AUGUST 12, 1999 Chapel Hill, NC (Dean Smith Center) LOGFILE GENERATED 8/1/99 WRITERS: Ryan Deutscher ( [detail withheld] - hour #1), Brent Alles ( [detail withheld] - hour #2) DARK MATCHES: Brian “The King” Derks vs. Man Mountain Marko Darknight Express vs. Strictly Business Master of Darkness Damion vs. Vladimir Popalov This week FEVER comes to you from Chapel Hill, SC! And based on what happened last week on FEVER, you can expect more tension between everybody involved in the upcoming TEAM CHALLENGE '99!
"THE MAIN EVENT" STEVE DAVIS is scheduled to take on one half of the CWO
North American Tag Team Champions, HOLLYWOOD HASKINS! It should be interesting to see Davis, who's EXTREMELY full of himself, thinking that he should always be "The Main Event," go up against another guy with a HUGE ego, Haskins!
"AIRBORNE" MARK FULCRUM of MILLENNIUM claims to be the "Cruiserweight" champion. But is he HARDCORE? SANDMAN JR. of the TRUE AMERICANS will put him to the test in a FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE match!
And speaking of the TRUE AMERICANS, ALLEN "MR. AMERICA" CARTER and OMAR
MARTINEZ will team up against former CWO NA Tag Team Champions, the HARDCORE
SENSATION! Should be another brutal match!
A change of pace for CWO Television Champion and EMPIRE member UNION CAINE this week. Instead of taking on a brute or brawler like Man Mountain Marko or "The Avenger" Jesse Ewiak, he's going to take on a Light Heavyweight,
SOUTHERN DYNAMITE! Does "Southern D" have what it takes to hang with the
Television champion?
Speaking of "THE AVENGER", JESSE EWIAK will be taking on the "DARK SHOGUN" AKUERI HAKUJIN of the SHEETS FOUNDATION in what should be an interesting matchup of brawling and martial arts.
MUSTAFA RAHIM will be in the house, and apparently he's ready to make a
"Hit" on somebody else. Who will hire him to do it, and who will be the one to be in the crosshairs?
ELITE member BLACK JACK HOLLISTER is the North American Champion. He's also one of the toughest hombres out there. Recently, the Elite "ate their young" by attacking the Hollywood X-Press. Hollywood Haskins' biggest fan,
CALVIN, didn't take kindly to that and has challenged HOLLISTER to a match for the NA title! (If that doesn't prove Cal's insane, we don't know what will!)
And in our big main event, it's the MILLENNIUM MEN, GOLD KARD and LORD HILE
TROY, up against the current CWO Light Heavyweight Champion WILDFIRE and his brutish bodyguard/partner CARR or the ELITE. Gold Kard and Troy are going to have to use all their years of experience to deal with the psychotic monster that is Carr!
All that and more on another red hot FEVER! Don't miss it! http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5403/
