SCORCH 10/24/99

October 27, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCORCH 10/24/99 TAPED (10/21/99): Little Rock, AK (Alltel Arena) AEN (ALL ENTERTAINMENT NETWORK) 7:00 PM EDT RATING: TV-14 (L, V) WRITTEN BY: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) EXECUTIVE PRODUCER: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5403/ (The SCORCH symbol slams through YOUR television set and we cut straight to Krash Kelly and Talson Edward Geddy standing by for another action packed night of CWO SCORCH Wrestling... second to none in the world of sports entertainment... the SCORCH arena is packed and fans jam out to "Spacejam"
which can be heard in the background as fireworks shoot off on all four corners of the ring and near the entrance as the camera catches two fans dressed up just like the Brute Squad and yet another fan is wearing a
Diamondback Chris Myers official CWO leather jacket and an "Airborne" Marc Fulcrum bandanna! Krash Kelly is positive and upbeat while Talson Geddy is concerned with his mind somewhere else...)
KELLY: Fans, once again... CWO is on the air with SCORCH and we are back and ready to rumble with four action packed matches. First off we have Lord
Hile Troy taking on a newcomer here in the CWO, Kreslyn. Let's get to the ring... SCORCH MATCH ONE: Kreslyn vs. Lord Hile Troy REFEREE: Jeremy Lang. (Tony the K is in the ring along with Lang.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Introducing first, coming down the aisle...
('Kreslyn' plays as the fans boo.) TONY THE K: Weighing 240 pounds, from parts unknown, Kreslyn! GEDDY: This turkey should be with the CWO Fever Female Cheerleader Squad! KELLY: He has a tough job taking on his Lordship tonight! (Kreslyn comes down to the ring and gets in.) TONY THE K: And his opponent... ('New Millennium' by Dream Theater plays as the fans cheer.) TONY THE K: Weighing 240 pounds, from Blackpool, England, Lord Hile Troy! KELLY: Evenly matched as far as weight is concerned... it's going to be a battle of wits! (Lord Hile Troy comes down to the ring and gets in, grabbing the rope and not really paying much attention to Kreslyn.)
DRUNK FAN (to Troy): Go back to England, your Lordship! TROY: (To Drunk Fan) Just stay there until after the... (Troy never gets to finish his exchange with the fan since Kreslyn nails Troy with a chop and then another one two in a row! Kreslyn with a roundhouse on Troy... but Troy refocuses and hits Kreslyn with a well timed elbow smash and Kreslyn is caught by surprise and rolls outside the ring!)
DRUNK FAN (to Kreslyn): That's why you're in last place, LOSER! KRESLYN: (To Drunk Fan) Shut up and have another beer! KELLY: Looks like one of our many loyal fans has really helped beer sales in Little Rock... Kreslyn making a rookie mistake and letting a fan get to him... his Lordship waiting in the ring for Kreslyn...
(Kreslyn sees Troy waiting in the ring and gets back in the ring but not before Troy stomps on Kreslyn... Troy slaps on a leglock but Kreslyn is too powerful and breaks the hold... Troy continues to attack the newcomer but
Kreslyn counters by whipping Troy into the corner and following up with a hurricanrana!) KELLY: Big hurricanrana by Kreslyn… and now the FIGURE FOUR! FIGURE FOUR LEGLOCK APPLIED BY KRESLYN! KRESLYN keeping the hold on TROY! (Troy's shoulders hit the mat... Lang counts... 1... 2... thr... he sits back up! Kreslyn releases the hold!) KELLY: Kreslyn already with a pin attempt but the veteran Troy is able to squeeze out of that one... Talson, aren't your going to say anything tonight?
GEDDY: Okay, what's Bill Clinton's idea of safe sex? KELLY: I know I'm going to regret this... what? GEDDY: When Hillary's not around. KELLY: Whatever... Kreslyn continuing to hammer his Lordship... Kreslyn doesn't let up... GUILLOTINE LEGDROP! Another cover...
(1... 2... kick out!) KELLY: Kreslyn with a lot of offense... GEDDY: Just like Notre Dame last week... and Green Bay... KELLY: Kreslyn with a modified sleeperhold... keeps it on securely... (Troy’s face starts to show some evidence of pain...) LANG: (To Troy) Do you submit? KELLY: Kreslyn with most of the pin covers... but he's using up Troy’s energy fast... (Troy shakes his head no... Troy powers back to his feet!) KELLY: Kreslyn can't believe that Troy is still in the fight! Kreslyn releases the sleeperhold... Kreslyn with a forearm smash! Kreslyn goes for a DDT...
BUT TROY COUNTERS IT WITH A LOW BLOW! (Before Kreslyn can regain his bearings, Troy whips him into the ropes… KELLY: Troy tries to follow up but is met with Kreslyn's running forearm smash! Kreslyn slaps a TEXAS CLOVERLEAF ON TROY! Troy reaching for the ropes... YES!
(Lang orders Kreslyn to release the cloverleaf and Kreslyn is visibly upset!) KELLY: Kreslyn not happy with having to break the hold… but now a powerslam on Troy! Kreslyn with another cover... (1... 2... thr... kick out!) KELLY: Close there… Kreslyn hits Troy with a European Uppercut! What’s this… it looks like he’s going for the Viledriver on Lord Hile Troy!
GEDDY: Kreslyn likes to use his opponent’s moves against them! KELLY: VILEDRIVER ON... NO! TROY GETS OUT OF THE VILEDRIVER AND ROLLS KRESLYN UP IN A SMALL PACKAGE! TROY WITH THE COVER! (1... 2... 3! Kreslyn is pinned!) TONY THE K: The winner of this contest in 4:32, Lord Hile Troy! KELLY: And as usual... the veteran outsmarted the rookie by letting the rookie tire themselves out and Troy was waiting for his chance and he picked just the right moment and Lord Hile Troy gets another win!
GEDDY: This is supposed to be exciting? Give me hooters! That's exciting! KELLY: We’ll be right back. (Commercial for a brand new movie coming out soon, Star Warz: The Phantom Stooges... with Curly Skywalker, Larry One Knobi and Moto-Dee-Two... commercial for Herbal Essence Shampoo... for five dollars it BETTER be close to sex... a Clinton Commercial About Staying Drug Free... and a TERROR NIGHT preview... back to SCORCH and match two!)
KELLY: Next up it's two of the Millennium Men taking on the Mercenaries! The Mercenaries will be meeting the Creatures of the Night in a scaffold match at TERROR NIGHT ’99, a match that has a lot of CWO officials nervous!
SCORCH MATCH TWO: Myers/Fulcrum vs. Mercenaries REFEREE: Chris Connor (Tony the K is in the ring along with Connor.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team contest is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Introducing first, coming down the aisle...
('Ace of Spades' by Drain STH plays as the fans cheer.) TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 489 pounds, representing Millennium, Diamondback Chris Myers and 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum! (Myers and Fulcrum come down to the ring and get in.) TONY THE K: And their opponents... ('Iron Man' by Black Sabbath plays as the fans cheer.) TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 555 pounds, Kaptain Krunch and Korperal Punishment, the Mercenaries! (Mercenaries come down to the ring and get in.) KELLY: Krunch and Fulcrum starting for both teams... did you listen to Maximum Overdrive's comments about the Mercenaries? Former world champs in another league?
GEDDY: The CWO is what counts... and you can tell that those two idiots ever held anything but a microphone considering their current standing in the
CWO... KELLY: They _have_ held titles here in the CWO before... GEDDY: Couldn't tell now. KELLY: Kaptain Krunch starts against Fulcrum and applies a headlock... 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum reaches the ropes... (Referee Chris Connor warns Krunch to let go... Krunch does and hits Fulcrum with a bodyslam!)
KELLY: Krunch with a big bodyslam. The question is, what does all of Millennium think about Diamondback getting a chance at getting another world title shot? It will be his second in less than a month... and…
KRUNCH: IT'S TIME FOR SOME PAIN, FULCRUMB! GEDDY: Krunch mouthing off again. (Kaptain Krunch applies the STF...) KELLY: STF applied... Fulcrum looks like he's fading... but NO... not quite yet. (Connor asks Fulcrum if he submits and Fulcrum yells no!) KELLY: MYERS WITH THE SAVE! Myers lays out Krunch... Myers tries to whip Krunch into the ropes... no dice... but Fulcrum connects with a dropkick and Krunch falls outside the ring! (Myers pounds on Krunch outside the ring, but Korperal Punishment makes the save.) KELLY: We’ve got everybody going at it outside the ring… (Fulcrum tries for a flying elbowsmash off the ring apron...) KELLY: NO ONE HOME! Fulcrum gets a major brick... no cigar today! Krunch moves out of the way, both teams really taking to the streets here outside the ring as all four men are going at it!
(Fulcrum whips Krunch into the guardrail...) KELLY: But Fulcrum says hello to Krunch with Mister Steel Chair and then again introduces Krunch to Mister Ringside Steps! Korperal Punishment tries to make the save for Krunch but Myers takes care of Korperal Punishment via the
Suplex Express! Again Fulcrum slams Krunch into the guardrail while Korperal Punishment gets revenge on Myers via a bodyslam. (Krunch rolls back into the ring but Fulcrum doesn't let up stomping on Krunch relentlessly!) KELLY: Everybody back to their respective corners… and KRUNCH MAKES THE TAG TO PUNISHMENT! Korperal Punishment with a single-leg takedown… going for the figure four… no, Fulcrum counters with a quick kick-off.
(Fulcrum gets back to his feet and nails a dropkick on Korperal Punishment!) KELLY: Fulcrum now climbing the turnbuckle... the crowd knows what's coming... AIR FULCRUM (450 Degree Splash)! FULCRUM NAILS PUNISHMENT RIGHT
IN THE BREADBASKET! GEDDY: I felt that over here... KELLY: FULCRUM GOES FOR THE PIN... (KRUNCH TRYING TO MAKE THE SAVE.....) KELLY: Here comes Krunch into the ring to rescue Punishment... RATTLER! MYERS WITH A RATTLER ON KRUNCH! GEDDY: NO WAY TODAY, Myers with a RATTLER... and Krunch is seeing stars right now! Lots and lots of pretty little stars!
(CONNOR COUNTS 1... 2... THREE! CONNER CALLS FOR THE BELL!) TONY THE K: The winners of this contest in 3:41, Diamondback Chris Myers and “Airborne” Mark Fulcrum! (Crowd cheers, but it’s short-lived, as the enraged Mercenaries start beating on Myers and Fulcrum.)
KELLY: The Mercenaries not happy with losing to Myers and Fulcrum, but they’ re going to make them pay for it! Mercenaries throw Diamondback Chris Myers out of the ring… and now Kaptain Krunch has Fulcrum up on his shoulders!
(Korperal Punishment climbs to the top…) KELLY: MERCENARIES’ FLYING CLOTHESLINE! GEDDY: Forget about it! (Mercs continue to stomp on Fulcrum, but the fans cheer as the Creatures of the Night rush down to the ring.)
KELLY: And here come the Creatures of the Night! These two teams brawling in the ring! They’re going to go up on the scaffold one week from tonight, but they’re not going to wait that long! Well, while security takes care of this, we'll be back after these messages!
(Fade to commercials...including this one!) Announcer: Ever wished that you could shut a person up with a remote control like you do the TV?
NOW YOU CAN! Our PutASockInIt (tm) 1000 can shut that annoying twerp up in a second! Use it on your co-workers! Use it for telemarketers (it works over the phone!) ! Use it during meetings when the boss is putting the entire staff to sleep! Use it on your husband/wife if they snore! Just go to www.putasockinit.com and order yours today! (Fade back to SCORCH) SCORCH MATCH THREE: Brian "The King" Derks vs. Carr (w/Lt. Cammy LeBeau) REFEREE: John Myering. (Tony the K is in the ring along with Myering.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Introducing first, coming down the aisle...
(Theme music plays as the fans boo.) TONY THE K: Weighing 286 pounds, from White Cloud, MI, Brian 'The King' Derks! (Brian 'The King' Derks comes down to the ring and gets in.) KELLY: The CWO fans are obviously not his loyal subjects... TONY THE K: And his opponent... ('Living Dead Girl' by Rob Zombie plays as the fans boo.) GEDDY: And they don't like Carr too much either... Carr has been on a tear, tearing EVERYONE apart in the CWO with his Fatality!
KELLY: That illegal move. TONY THE K: Weighing 345 pounds, from Boston, MA, accompanied to the ring by Lt. Cammy LeBeau, Carr! KELLY: Cammy can take care of herself also, one of the toughest managers here in the CWO male OR female!
(Carr comes down to the ring and gets in.) KELLY: And here we go… give Derks credit… he’s going right on the offense… (Brian 'The King' Derks whips Carr into the corner... Carr bounces back from the corner... Brian 'The King' Derks hits with the running forearm smash.)
GEDDY: Derks has to keep on the pressure. (Brian 'The King' Derks whips Carr into the ropes... Brian 'The King' Derks hits with the back body drop!) KELLY: The King ripping it up here... Brian 'The King' Derks whips Carr into the corner... (Brian 'The King' Derks tries to whip Carr into the other corner...) KELLY: REVERSAL BY CARR... he charges in... BUT DERKS GETS OUT OF THE WAY JUST IN THE NICK OF TIME! And now CARR TOSSED OUTSIDE OF THE RING BY DERKS! GEDDY: Derks with all of the moves here so far Carr just waiting and sizinghis opponent up...
KELLY: Bad pun under the circumstances. GEDDY: How in the hell did Derks get the strength to throw Carr around like that? Both men on the outside brawling Derks with a chair to Carr... no effect... hasn't Derks watched the films from other matches with Carr?
KELLY: Carr can't be handled with traditional outside the ring methods, Carr rolls back into the ring and Brian Derks...
_REVERSE NECKBREAKER_ BY DERKS! Going for the pin... GEDDY: How the hell is Derks getting all this power? (Myering counts... 1... 2... shoulder up!) KELLY: Derks almost getting the win there, and he hits Carr with a DDT! Derks whips Carr into the ropes... CARR WITH A BOOT TO DERKS’ FACE, DERKS IS STUNNED! (Cammy climbs to the apron and is yelling at Myering...) KELLY: Cammy distracting the referee... GEDDY: Not as distracting as Juli Ashton! KELLY: True, but Cammy could kick Juli around any day! Wait a minute… no! FATALITY! GEDDY: Stick a fork in Jerks... he's the main course for tomorrow... KELLY: Myering didn’t see that illegal move being done, and you can only imagine what’s going to happen in the TERROR CAGE, where Carr can do anything he wants! Referee turned around now, and he could count to 20...
it wouldn't matter! This one is OVER! TONY THE K: The winner of this contest in 2:21, Carr! (Carr plays to the booing crowd.) KELLY: Get ready to take on that monster one week from tonight, in the TERROR CAGE… we’ll be right back. (Fade to commercials, including this one... Camera fades into darkness. Suddenly, a welder's torch is ignited.) VOICE OVER: Sunday....October 31... (Sparks start to fly as the torch cuts into what looks like a pipe.) VOICE OVER: Joe Louis Arena....Detroit, Michigan.... (Gloved hands can be seen placing two pieces of piping together. The welder's torch binds the two pieces together.)
VOICE OVER: CWO Terror Night '99.... (Strobes start to flash as the camera falls back from a set of scaffolding. Lit from the back, the tower of metal looks ominous.) VOICE OVER: The Creatures of the Night.... (Half of the screen shows slow motion footage of the Creatures of the Night walking to the ring.)
VOICE OVER: The Mercenaries.... (The other half of the screen shows the Mercenaries performing the Flying Clothesline to Damion at September Slam.) VOICE OVER: In one of the most dangerous matches ever to be held in a wrestling federation. 20 feet above the ring, both teams will fight until one team falls to their doom....
(Fade to footage of the Mercenaries, standing 20 feet atop what looks to be a similar scaffold.)
KAPTAIN KRUNCH: The Mercenaries have been doing their training on top of this scaffold for the past 7 days and we're going to keep training up here all the way until the night when we're going to take those sorry excuses for wrestlers -
KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: That would be the Creatures of the Night - KAPTAIN KRUNCH: We're going to take that sorry excuse for a tag team, grab them by their heads and toss them off of this platform 20 feet to either the concrete or the ring below -
KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: At this point we don't care where the hell they land. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: But in case some of you are having a hard time trying to visualize exactly what happens when somebody gets thrown off one of these things, we brought a couple of visual aids to help you out.
(Korperal Punishment picks up a pumpkin. You can clearly see the words "Night Crawler" written in black marker across it. Punishment hefts it up over his head.) KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: This is what's going to happen when I take Night Crawler and wrap my hands around his skinny neck! (Punishment throws the pumpkin from off of the scaffold. In slow motion, you see the pumpkin rolling over and over before it splatters on the concrete below. Camera pans back up to the top of the scaffold, where
Kaptain Krunch is holding a pumpkin with "Night Slinger" written across it.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Night Slinger, it's going to be a different story for you. I'm going to take my time beating the living hell out of you and when you're on your knees, begging for mercy, I'm going to throw my head back, laugh my ass off, then pick you up and chuck you off the top of this platform.
(Kaptain Krunch takes his pumpkin and throws it off and again, the camera goes into slow motion as the pumpkin spirals down towards the concrete, breaking apart when it hits not far from Punishment's pumpkin.)
KAPTAIN KRUNCH: So there you have it. 20 feet in the air, no safety nets, on a platform 6 feet wide. The Mercenaries are more than ready. Are you?
(The CWO Terror Night graphic fades in, superimposing itself over the image of the scaffold.)
VOICE OVER: CWO TERROR NIGHT....LIVE ONLY ON PPV! DON'T MISS IT! (Back to SCORCH.) SCORCH MATCH FOUR: Jesse Ewiak vs. "The Main Event" Steve Davis REFEREE: Kendall Dolemeyer. (Tony the K is in the ring along with Dolemeyer.) TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Introducing first, coming down the aisle...
('My Own Prison' by Creed plays as the fans boo.) TONY THE K: Weighing 295 pounds, from Palm Bay, FL, representing the Elite, Jesse Ewiak! (Jesse Ewiak comes down to the ring and gets in.) TONY THE K: And his opponent... ('Bulls on Parade' by Rage Against the Machine plays as the fans boo.) TONY THE K: Weighing 264 pounds, from Miami, FL, 'The Main Event' Steve Davis! GEDDY: Two matches in a row where the fans hate both wrestlers! Plus no good looking chicks either...
KELLY: Is that all you think about? GEDDY: What else is there... the Main Event and the Main Dork get in the ring here... ('The Main Event' Steve Davis comes down to the ring and gets in. The bell rings and we are underway.)
KELLY: This match underway… Davis with an eye gouge, tries to whip Ewiak into the corner... but Ewiak reverses it and nails The Main Event with an elbowsmash.
(Punch by Ewiak.) GEDDY: Neither man really doing much at the start here... KELLY: Hiptoss by Ewiak on Davis out of the corner... (Jesse Ewiak climbs to the top turnbuckle…) KELLY: Ewiak hits with the flying cross body press... 1... 2... KICKOUT! Ewiak now with a suplex on Davis! GEDDY: The crowd is booing Ewiak, as well they should. KELLY: Ewiak setting Davis up on the turnbuckle... SUPERPLEX! EWIAK (to crowd): THAT’S IT! KELLY: Ewiak going for the Ewiakbomb… he hits with it! Cover… no, he’s lifting him up again! He HITS HIM WITH A SECOND EWIAKBOMB!
GEDDY: And he’s not finished yet! KELLY: THIRD EWIAKBOMB! Ewiak going for the pin now... here's the count... 1... 2... KICKOUT! I thought for sure Ewiak had Davis nailed that time with THREE Ewiakbombs but then again I guess he's earned his nickname with endurance like that! KELLY: Does Davis have anything left tonight after that? (Ewiak goes for a kneedrop but Davis rolls out of the way!) KELLY: Nobody home! (The crowd booing Davis... Davis with the eye gouge... Davis runs into the ropes...) KELLY: Davis trying to mount a comeback… _SPINEBUSTER SLAM BY EWIAK_! (Ewiak follows up with a quick kneedrop...) KELLY: Ewiak making a cover, he’s got his feet on the ropes! (1... 2... Myering sees it...) CROWD: BOOOOOOOOOO! KELLY: The fans really want to see Ewiak lose tonight... (Ewiak goes for the splash but 'Davis gets his knees up! KELLY: And it may happen after that! 'The Main Event' Steve Davis hits with the neckbreaker... and a low blow!
CROWD: BOOOOOOOOOOOO! GEDDY: The crowd doesn't like ANYONE tonight! Davis whipping Ewiak into the corner but Ewiak reverses it, Ewiak with a kick to the midsection!
KELLY: Davis rolling outside the ring, Ewiak waiting patiently... why is Davis playing to a crowd that hates him? DAVIS: I AM THE MAIN EVENT IN THE CWO, NO ONE CAN EVER BEAT ME! FANS CLOSEST TO DAVIS IN THE FRONT ROW: SHUT UP AND WRESTLE! (Ewiak, tired of waiting, climbs outside and grabs a steel chair and slams Davis upside the head!) GEDDY: Finally, Ewiak did something right, he shut Davis up! KELLY: Ewiak rolling back into the ring… Davis returns to the ring and is met with a dropkick from Ewiak! Davis retreating outside the ring again while Ewiak waits inside the ring.
(Davis climbs back in slowly...) KELLY: Short clothesline by Ewiak, who whips Davis into the ropes... back body drop by Ewiak! Ewiak going for the Ewiakbomb again... no! Davis counters with a back body drop of his own! And now… Power bomb by Steve
Davis! GEDDY: This could be it! KELLY: Davis… MAIN MOVE (suplex DDT)! Davis just nailed Ewiak with his suplex DDT out of nowhere! Where did that come from? All match Ewiak with
Ewiakbombs and Davis is still here! KELLY: Dolemeyer with the count... but here comes the Elite! (Gecko, Carr, Wildfire, and the Brute Squad – Taggart and Hollister – hit the ring and beat on Davis as the referee calls for the bell.)
TONY THE K (quickly): The winner of this contest in 6:52 as a result of a disqualification, “The Main Event” Steve Davis!
KELLY: The Elite protecting their own… and now, lord help us, Gecko has the mike… GECKO: You see… it doesn’t matter who it is, everybody in the CWO is going to learn that you can’t hang… with the Elite! And to the Alleses… both of you… Myers… Foolkiller… and whomever else they get to be in that cage with you… realize this… there are monsters this Halloween night… and this monster goes by the name of Carr.
(Gecko slaps Carr’s chest as Carr laughs maniacally.) GECKO: At TERROR NIGHT ’99, Alleses… Myers… Foolkiller… the Elite will get the title shot… we will make one of you submit… but before we do… we will make you hurt… we will make you scream… and we will make you BLEED!
(Gecko drops the mike and plays to the booing crowd, along with the rest of the Elite.) KELLY: One week from tonight, it’s TERROR NIGHT ’99, and the Elite, along with Kent Alles and Andrew Dixon, are ready to go into the TERROR CAGE with
Brent “The Ace” Alles, Gordy Alles, Foolkiller, Diamondback Chris Myers, and an as yet unnamed fourth man! We’re out of time for tonight, we’ll see you next week!
