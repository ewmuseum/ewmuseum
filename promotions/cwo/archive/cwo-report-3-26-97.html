CWO REPORT 3/26/97

26 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Wednesday, March 26, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night was another exciting TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, let's take a look at what happened on our FEVER FLASHBACK! _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER FLASHBACK 3/25/97 "LIVE" FROM SAGINAW, MI DARK MATCH: Acidic Bench defeated Vegas Villain in 10:02 after the Acidic Kick. Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, and Hoss and Little Joe stormed the ring before the actual opening match could begin. Jackass Joel demanded the chance to prove themselves, saying that they were promised it at the SHOWCASE SPECIAL for SPRING BREAK '97 and it went unfulfilled. CWO officials tried to get the foursome to leave the ring to no avail, when an explosion of light and smoke occurred. When it cleared, Jackass Joel and friends were gone and in their place was a regal white-bearded man calling himself Zeus, along with a known CWO wrestler, Akillies, and a new wrestler named Spartacus. Zeus claimed that they were the New Men, and that the time of testing for the CWO and, more specifically, Centurion, had begun. Another explosion of light and smoke (with thunder), and the three men were gone. The Big Cool defeated Chris Greco and Tommy Lightning in 15:11 when Jumbo Jenkins pinned Chris Greco after the Star Flash (backbreaker by Jazzman, flying big splash by Jumbo). After the match the Big Cool was attacked by Da Hood Rats. Interview with Gold Kard in which he "declares war" on Johnny Reb for costing him the shot at the World title, and also talking about their upcoming "No Holds Barred" match at APRIL ARMAGEDDON. Gold Kard also reveals his "alliance" with the supergroup going by the same name (Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers, North American champion Goliath, David "The Destroyer" Davis, Inferno). Gold Kard stated that the Alliance's mission was to eliminate Johnny Reb and his friends Mason-Dixon and Panther. "Monster" Rich Clark defeated Moses in 17:05, pinning him after the Choke Slam. "Monster" Rich Clark won the title shot at APRIL ARMAGEDDON that Moses previously had from being the Last Man Standing in that match at SPRING BREAK '97. After the match "Monster" Rich Clark was thrown a bouquet of roses from the rafters, apparently from the Brotherhood of the Rose. "Lonestar" Mike Williams and Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung defeated Bloodbath and Wildfire when the North American tag team champions the Bad Guys hit the ring, attacking Williams and Gerstung with steel chairs, then turning their attention to Bloodbath and Wildfire, who will face them at APRIL ARMAGEDDON for those belts. Comments from both Masher Maccabee and Mondo Gecko are heard concerning the upcoming ladder match for the Television title at APRIL ARMAGEDDON. Mondo Gecko defended his Television championship, defeating Panther in 8:06 by pinning him after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Comments from the Pit Bosses concerning their match tonight against the Mercenaries and their upcoming match against the Regulators at APRIL ARMAGEDDON. World Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses defeated the Mercenaries in 13:42 when Pit Boss #1 pinned Kaptain Krunch after the Spike Piledriver. Comments from Bane concerning his rematch tonight against Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo and also a warning to Brent "The Ace" Alles not to interfere in his matches ever again. World Champion Bane defeated Wayne Schillo in 10:17, when the ref stopped the match with Schillo in the Merciful Death. Moments before, Schillo was tossed over the top rope by Bane and he hit his head on the steel guardrail, opening a deep cut. After the match was awarded to Bane, he refused to let go of the hold, prompting "Monster" Rich Clark to make the save. "Monster" Rich Clark then had Bane by the throat, getting ready to Choke Slam him, when Bane kicked him in the groin. Bane then put the Merciful Death on Clark, and CWO officials, security, and wrestlers swarmed the ring to separate the two. The show ended with both Bane and "Monster" Rich Clark being escorted to different parts of the building. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Luckily, Wayne Schillo was not seriously injured last night, he did require stitches for that deep cut, but otherwise he's OK, and should be able to participate without difficulty in the MARCH MADNESS tournament continuing this week. BISHOP: I was watching one of the latest events and I saw this peon Wildfire get in front of the camera and jabber on like a spider monkey. Quite frankly, I'm disgusted. The Unholy Alliance is going to take his pathetic little no talent having butt and feed it to the lions. This is our little mission for the week. Because all that annoys us must be taken care of. As for the rest of you, we have been quiet as of late, but that won't last long. Bloodbath... you speak of evil? You speak of the darkside? Wake up, youngblood, you want a bloodbath?
