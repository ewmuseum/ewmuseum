REPORT 9/11/97

11 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, September 11, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr, great to be back with you here on the report as Mitch E. Washey is on vacation. And the first thing we have to go over is another explosive FEVER that took place this Tuesday in Richmond, VA, let's look at what happened in our FEVER FLASHBACK! (Fever Flashback graphic and music is cued.) _____________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER FLASHBACK 9/9/97 "LIVE" FROM RICHMOND, VA HOUR #1: MATCH ONE: Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Red Raven after the Rattler in 5:02. MATCH TWO: World Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys defeated the Mercenaries in 15:02 when Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson pinned Kaptain Krunch after a spike piledriver. Mustafa Rahim with comments about the new member coming to The Law. Footage is shown from last week's FEVER, where Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil, hit North American Champion Masher Maccabee in the face with a haggis stuffed with a brick. Cut to Fergus then confronting David "The Truth" Thomas, Thomas calling Fergus a nasty name, and Fergus then beating the tar out of him. MATCH THREE: Television Champion The Focus defeated Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil, pinning him after a German suplex and a strike by Masher Maccabee with the North American Championship belt. HOUR #2: Interview with The Unit, as they state that their manager Johnny B. Goode may never be able to use his legs again. They vow revenge on The Cabin. Toni Newton, sister of Steve and Howard, is introduced. MATCH FOUR: Painmaster and Lord Hile Troy wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. After the match, Gold Kard asked the Painmaster if he'd be willing to do a "hit" on Fergus McTavish, in exchange for a North American Championship shot. Painmaster agreed, and then while Gold Kard tried to address his situation with Holocaust, an angry Masher Maccabee stormed down to the ring and asked Gold Kard how he dared put Masher's title up without his permission. Gold Kard tried to calm Masher down by reminding him of the challenge he faced ahead from "Monster" Rich Clark. Maccabee, obviously not impressed, made some derogatory comments to the 7'4", 446 pound monster of a man, prompting him to come out from the curtain. Clark raised his hand in the air, signaling a choke slam, possibly a sign of things to come. The Coldfront do an interview in which they reveal a new bodyguard, Tommy Bomber, a new alliance with "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat (who comes down to join in the interview), and a new attitude as they pledge their allegiance to the AAWA, touting its supposed superiority over the CWO. MATCH FIVE: "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher and Ganglord defeated the Highlanders by countout when the World Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys attacked the Highlanders outside the ring, prompting both teams to brawl back to the dressing room. Interview with Carlos Maia, who puts down Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, threatens "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher, and clotheslines Johnny Starr, all to the boos of the crowd. MATCH SIX: In a "Champions' Challenge" match, World Champion Johnny Reb defeated North American Champion Masher Maccabee by disqualification in 4:35 when Moses attacked Reb while he had Maccabee in the Texas Cloverleaf. Moses and Masher Maccabee then doubleteamed the World Champion, prompting Jason "Flash" Varga to appear and try and go to the ring to help Johnny Reb, except he was stopped by Joe Eagle, who told Varga that Reb had "made his bed, and now he has to sleep in it." Varga still tried to go down, but Joe Eagle asked him menacingly who was his friend and who wasn't, and Varga finally begrudgingly backed down. All looked grim for Johnny Reb, until Fergus McTavish hit the ring with a steel chair! Moses scattered, but Masher Maccabee took a good shot to the face, further injuring his already injured nose from last week's attack by Fergus. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: I tell you, folks, there's no better wrestling program on today than TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Anyway, tonight we are in Andover, MD, let's look at the great card to be held in the USAir arena. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 11, 1997 ANDOVER, MD Southern Dynamite vs. Beauregard Dixon "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday vs. Mustafa Rahim Amish Farmer vs. Stonewall Mason Brian Williams vs. Diamondback Chris Myers Skyhawk vs. Jehovah's Witness Wayne Schillo vs. Vegas High Roller Newton Brothers vs. Deadly Sins TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Focus vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: As you saw on the FEVER FLASHBACK, it was quite an eventful night for North American Champion Masher Maccabee, we have comments from him now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Masher is sitting in the locker room, icing his nose.) MASHER MACCABEE: Fergus McTavish, you Scottish piece of trash, I should kill you. I'd rather torture your ass and watch you squeal like the b$%#h that you are. You ain't no devil, son, because I'm your Satan, and when I slap you in the Monster Mash Driver and drop you on your head, the only thing that will be oatmeal will be your brains, and that's the bottom line, because MASHER says so! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT! (Closing creds come up as we fade out.)
