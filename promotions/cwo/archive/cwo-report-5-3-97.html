CWO REPORT 5/3/97

3 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, May 3, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Buffalo, NY, two days before the great MAY-HEM PPV coming your way, let's see what went down in Buffalo! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 5/2/97 Buffalo, NY Johnny Reb made Gold Kard submit to the Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 10:35. Joe Eagle pinned Bane after the Eagle Shock. Time of match: 19:47. Wild Side defeated New Men when Bloodbath pinned Spartacus after the Tombstone Powerbomb. Time of match: 11:59. 'Lone Wolf' Ryan Deutscher pinned 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White after the Stun Gun. Time of match: 12:17. David 'The Destroyer' Davis/Inferno defeated Masters of Hardcore when David 'The Destroyer' Davis pinned Brad 'The Man' Couchman after the Destroyer Dropper. Time of match: 25:23. Goliath made Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo submit to the Goliath Grappler. Time of match: 8:20. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 9:21. World Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses defeated the Highlanders in a non-title match when Pit Boss #2 pinned Conor McCloud after the Spike Piledriver. Time of match: 12:04. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tomorrow is MAY-HEM, let's take one more look at the line-up! _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO MAY-HEM SUNDAY, MAY 4, 1997 "LIVE" FROM THE MOLSON CENTRE, MONTREAL, QUEBEC, CANADA 6:30 PM WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (one ring surrounded by a steel cage): Bane vs. "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Goliath (w/Jimbo Jones) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Highlanders vs. Pit Bosses (champions) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Tiger Rogers (w/Jimbo Jones) vs. Jason "Flash" Varga (champion, w/Jim Peer) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mercenaries (w/Maximum Overdrive) vs. Bad Guys (champions, w/Herbie Versmells) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb (w/General Lee) vs. Inferno (champion, w/Jimbo Jones) 60-MAN MAY-HEM BATTLE ROYAL - WINNER GETS WORLD TITLE SHOT AT SUMMER HEAT (Two men start out in each ring, one more man is added to each ring every two minutes) PARTICIPANTS: "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher "Mr. Extreme" Chris Ohlhoff Akillies Arlon Flaym Baby Waldo Beauregard Dixon Benjamin Blake Bentley Bloodbath Brad "The Man" Couchman Brent "The Ace" Alles Brian "The Hammer" Williams Centurion Chris Greco Clark Gray Dark Raven David "The Destroyer" Davis Diamondback Chris Meyers El Grande Pistolerro Exile Exodus Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil Ganglord Gold Kard Hellhound Jermain Justice Jerome Justice Jim "Jazzman" Taylor Joe Eagle Jumbo Jenkins Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung Masher Maccabee Master of Darkness Damion Matsuhiro Torunaka (from Super Japan) Matthew "The Man" White Maximum Overdrive Mondo Gecko Moses Nate Night Crawler Night Slinger Panther Pride Regulator Fire Regulator Ice Sandman Jr. Serpentmaster Sloth Spartacus Steel Steve "Mr. Niceguy" White Steve Keller Stonewall Mason The Focus Tommy Lightning Vlad the Impaler Warren Wayne "College Guy" Schillo WILD CARD Wildfire Wrath EYE ON THE STORM: LOTTERY MATCH (two random teams of two, winners come down last in MAY-HEM, losers go out first) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now go to comments from CWO superstars, starting with a very angry The Focus after having Benjamin Blake Bentley steal another victory from him Thursday night in Cleveland. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: DAMMIT! What the hell is going on here? I lose to Benjamin Blake Bentley, because he cheats?!? Great. I kicked this guy around every arena we faced at, and now this... we're back to square one. Mr. President, wherever you are, remedy this situation! I want triple B again next week. Whatever stipulations you want, I don't care. As for you, Sandman Jr., I'm going to win in our match... period. However, we have a common enemy in Benjamin Blake Bentley... so let's kick his @$$! Bring the damn cane. GOD! Perhaps my focus has temporarily been broken... perhaps. Trust me, Sandman Jr, and Wayne Schillo... you will lose. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: They're getting ready for MAY-HEM, here they are, the Wild Bunch. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The entire Wild Bunch is in the locker room watching the TV monitor and looking at stats of CWO wrestlers. Gecko, The Regulators, Wilcox, and Pistolerro are all talking together about something... BIG!) TAMARA WILCOX: Look Mondo, I am telling you, I say we go for it and ask him to be part of it. He is the man we could use to be the top stable in the CWO! He is big, powerful, strong, and talented, and he would go great with the Regulators or with you and Pistolerro here. EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: I am not sure about it, Tamara. It is an awful big risk to take in an unknown and put him in with the Wild Bunch, we don't accept just about anyone. REGULATOR ICE: Look, the guy seems cool; he is tough, aggressive, and tenacious, HE IS MY KIND OF GUY! REGULATOR FIRE: Besides, he is a top superstar and we need all the help we can get against the GKI, Brent Alles, Bad Guys, and the rest of the CWO scum. MONDO GECKO: OK, it is settled! CWO, two things: first, Sunday, in Montreal, Canada, home of the team that has won more championships than any other sports team, the Montreal Canadians; Mondo Gecko, along with the other four members of the Wild Bunch, will be the last man standing in the 60 man battle royal. Hey, the last big battle royal the CWO had, for the TV belt, I came out as the winner. Now the CWO has another big card and I am ready to eliminate 59 other men, no offense, guys, and win the CWO World Heavyweight Title shot! Now, I would also like to offer to a special CWO superstar a chance to be part of the Wild Bunch. It seems that this individual has caught our eye and we are eager to have him join the Wildest Bunch in the CWO. Drum roll, please.... (Regulators "play the drums" while Mondo clears his throat.) MONDO GECKO: DIAMONDBACK CHRIS MYERS & DALLAS, I would like to proudly announce that we want you to become the newest members of the Wild Bunch. I have heard praises, all about you, and I want you to be part of our stable so that you can help us become the best stable in the CWO. TOGETHER WE ARE GOING TO CAUSE SOME MONDO EXTREME ACTION, BABY! EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: Let the good times roll! CWO, it is time the Wild Bunch takes control of all the problems of this league and soon we are going to destroy you all: Brent Alles, Bane, GKI, and you other geeks. VIVA LA WILD BUNCH! REGULATOR ICE: WELL, 60 men are going to be fighting for the chance to be competing for the World title in an upcoming event. Hey, it is time the Regulators joined in on the fun. Watch out, CWO, because we are ready to do whatever it takes to run over people in MAY-HEM. OOOHHHH WWWWHHHHHAAAAATTTTTT AAAAAA RRRRRUUUUUSSSSSHHHHH!!!! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow at MAY-HEM!
