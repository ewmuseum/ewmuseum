CWO 2000: Join the Evolution!

17 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you looking for a new fed? The CWO 2000 is currently looking for new members. We have been in operation since 1996, and have just begun using
TNM7, a program designed by Oliver Copp, as an "administrative tool" for our league. I say "administrative tool," because our league is not strictly simmed. We book based on storylines, handler participation, "fan" reaction, etc.
We run one weekly show, FEVER (we may add one more), and 6-12 PPVs a year
(we're still deciding on how many). We have the following titles:
World Television Cruiserweight (220 pounds and below) Hardcore (tentative) World Tag Team World 6-Man Tag Team (tentative) So, if you're looking for an established fed to join, that will allow you a great amount of character control and input into the general storylines, the
CWO 2000 may be for you! If interested, complete the below Wrestler Entry sheet and send it to
[detail withheld] . Entries received by this Tuesday midnight that are accepted will be placed on the FEVER line-up for 7/26/00, when we will return to action after a brief hiatus. All entries are considered on a case-by-case basis, of course, based on character and how he/she might fit in the CWO 2000.
Thanks!
