CWO Sunday Morning Showcase 6/10/01

CWO SUNDAY MORNING SHOWCASE 6/10/01 (Matches taped 6/6/01) HOSTS AND COMMENTATORS: Johnny Starr and T. J. Marks CWO on the web: http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling2/cwo2k Contact the CWO at [detail withheld] SEGMENT ONE CWO OPENING SHOW OPENING The SUNDAY MORNING SHOWCASE opening plays, and then we join our hosts in the studio as they welcome us to Sunday Morning Showcase. They discussed what happened in the CWO this past week, including the shocking incident that occurred on EXTREME involving Gordy Alles. They hope to have an update on the Alles’ condition soon.
EXTREME 6/6/01 RECAP COMMERCIALS SEGMENT TWO BacKyaRd vs. Gold Kard (with German Choklat) Tony the K is in the ring along with referee Chris Connor. TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Introducing first, coming down the aisle...
'Dragula' by Rob Zombie plays as the fans cheer. TONY THE K: Weighing 225 pounds, from Trenton. NJ, accompanied by Kool
Kevin, BacKyaRd! BacKyaRd comes down to the ring and gets in. TONY THE K: And his opponent... 'Ride of the Valkyries' by Wagner plays as the fans boo. TONY THE K: Weighing 245 pounds, from Berlin. Germany, accompanied to the ring by German Choklat, Gold Kard!
Gold Kard comes down to the ring and gets in. The bell rings. Gold Kard hits with a dropkick! BacKyaRd rolls outside the ring. Gold Kard goes for a baseball slide but BacKyaRd moves out of the way! BacKyaRd nails Gold Kard with a DDT!
BacKyaRd rolls Gold Kard back in the ring. BacKyaRd climbs to the top turnbuckle and nails Gold Kard with a flying headbutt! BacKyaRd goes for the pin! 1... 2… Gold Kard kicks out!
Both men get back to their feet. BacKyaRd hits with a chop. Gold Kard hits with a forearm smash. BacKyaRd hits with a chop. Gold Kard hits with a forearm smash. BacKyaRd hits with a kick. Gold Kard nails BacKyaRd with an eyerake.
Gold Kard hits with a kick to the midsection. Gold Kard hits with a standing dropkick! Gold Kard climbs to the top turnbuckle… he goes for a flying knee drop but BacKyaRd rolls out of the way!
BacKyaRd climbs to the top turnbuckle… and hits with a flying legdrop! BacKyaRd goes for the pin! German Choklat distracts the referee! BacKyaRd goes after German Choklat! Gold Kard clips BacKyaRd’s leg from behind.
Gold Kard climbs to the top turnbuckle. German Choklat is distracting the referee. Kool Kevin shoves Gold Kard off, sending him down hard to the mat!
BacKyaRd goes for the pin! 1... 2... Gold Kard kicks out! BacKyaRd hits with a chop. Gold Kard hits with an elbow smash. BacKyaRd hits with an elbow smash. Gold Kard hits with a forearm smash. Whip into the ropes by
Gold Kard… BacKyaRd hits with a clothesline! Gold Kard rolls outside the ring. BacKyaRd goes outside the ring. BacKyaRd and Gold Kard brawl outside the ring. Gold Kard rolls back in the ring, followed by BacKyaRd. Gold
Kard hits with a forearm smash. Gold Kard whips BacKyaRd into the ropes… and hits with a clothesline! Gold Kard goes for the pin! 1... 2...
BacKyaRd kicks out! Gold Kard hits with a choke on ropes. The referee makes his count... 1 ... 2 ... 3 ... 4 ... 5 ... the referee warns Gold Kard. Gold Kard lets go. Gold Kard hits with an elbow smash. Gold Kard goes for a hurricanrana but BackYard counters with a power bomb! BacKyaRd goes for the pin! 1... 2... thr... Gold Kard kicks out! BacKyaRd throws Gold Kard out of the ring. BacKyaRd nails Gold Kard with a flying clothesline off the ring apron!
BacKyaRd tries to slam Gold Kard into the ring apron, but it's reversed! Gold Kard rolls back in the ring, followed by BacKyaRd. Gold Kard applies a figure four. BacKyaRd reaches the ropes.
Gold Kard releases the hold. Gold Kard rakes his boot across BacKyaRd’s face. Gold Kard hits with an elbow drop!
Gold Kard climbs to the top turnbuckle. Gold Kard goes for the Die Flieder
Kross (flying somersault splash)... he hits! Gold Kard goes for the pin!
Kool Kevin distracts the referee! German Choklat attacks Kool Kevin! BacKyaRd comes from behind, but Gold
Kard is ready for him! Gold Kard hits with a German suplex! Gold Kard goes for the pin! 1… 2… 3! BacKyaRd is pinned!
MATCH STATS Gold Kard pinned BacKyaRd in 5:17 after the German Suplex. COMMERCIALS SEGMENT THREE Back to the studio, as Johnny and T. J. answer some fan e-mails and take some phone calls. COMMERCIALS SEGMENT FOUR MATSUHIRO TORUNAKA AND HIROSHI FUJINAMI vs. BRUTE SQUAD (Carr and Blackjack LeBeau, w/Lt. Cammy LeBeau) Tony the K is in the ring along with referee John Myering. TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team contest is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Introducing first, coming down the aisle...
Japanese synth music plays as the fans cheer. TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 445 pounds, Matsuhiro Torunaka and
Hiroshi Fujinami! Torunaka and Fujinami come down to the ring and get in, playing to the crowd. TONY THE K: And their opponents... 'I Disappear' by Metallica plays as the fans boo. TONY THE K: At a combined weight of 700 pounds, accompanied to the ring by
Lt. Cammy LeBeau and representing the Hellions, Carr and Blackjack LeBeau, the Brute Squad!
The Brute Squad come down to the ring with Cammy and get in. The bell rings. Torunaka starts against Carr. Carr whips Torunaka into the ropes…Torunaka hits with a sunset flip! Torunaka goes for the pin! 1...
Carr kicks out! Torunaka hits with a flying forearm off the ropes! Torunaka hits Carr with a dropkick! Torunaka hits Carr with a leaping clothesline and finally he goes down! Torunaka goes for the pin! 1...
2... Carr kicks out! Torunaka tags to Fujinami. Fujinami applies the Oriental spike. Carr counters with an eyerake.
Carr tags to Blackjack LeBeau. Blackjack LeBeau nails Fujinami with a clothesline, then plays to the crowd. Blackjack LeBeau whips Fujinami into the ropes… and nails him with a big boot!
Blackjack LeBeau tags to Carr. Blackjack LeBeau and Carr pound on Fujinami. Torunaka enters the ring and a wild brawl commences! Fujinami and Torunaka pound on Blackjack LeBeau and Carr.
The referee tries to get Torunaka and LeBeau out of the ring. Fujinami hits
Carr with a chop.
Fujinami runs back into the ropes… Lt. Cammy LeBeau nails Fujinami from behind with a martial arts kick! Fujinami staggers forward… Carr hits with a choke slam! Carr plays to the crowd. Fujinami tags to Torunaka. Torunaka climbs to the top turnbuckle… and hits with a missile dropkick! Carr is still on his feet! Torunaka runs back into the ropes… Carr catches him with a chokeslam!
Carr tags to Blackjack LeBeau. Carr and Blackjack LeBeau nail Torunaka with a double back suplex! Blackjack LeBeau whips Torunaka into the corner.
Blackjack LeBeau hits with a running elbow smash, then faceslams him down to the mat! Blackjack LeBeau goes for the pin! 1...
Fujinami makes the save! Fujinami is made to leave the ring. Blackjack
LeBeau tags to Carr.
Carr hits with a chop. Carr hits with a kick to the midsection. Carr throws Torunaka out of the ring.
Carr goes outside the ring and grabs a steel chair. He hits Torunaka with it! LeBeau sends Fujinami into the steel steps! Carr hits Torunaka with the chair again! And a third time! And a FOURTH time! Torunaka is out cold. Torunaka is rolled back in the ring by Carr. LeBeau rolls Fujinami back in the ring.
Carr hits Torunaka with the Fatality! Blackjack LeBeau hits Fujinami with the Texas Stampede (running power bomb)! They both go for the pin! 1...
2... 3! Both men are pinned! MATCH STATS Brute Squad (Carr and Blackjack LeBeau) defeated Matsuhiro Torunaka and
Hiroshi Fujinami when Carr pinned Torunaka and LeBeau pinned Fujinami after the Fatality (Gorilla press drop on to the turnbuckle) and Texas Stampede
(running power bomb), respectively. Time of match: 5:03.
COMMERCIALS SEGMENT FIVE Back to the studio, as Johnny and T. J. preview what’s coming up next week, including EXTREME, house shows, etc. They talk about how Wildfire will apparently now have to “jump through another hoop”, as he will have to take on Mondo Gecko, and the winner of THAT match will then take on Foolkiller later in the night for the World title. They also detail upcoming on sale dates and talk about INFERNO, coming up on June 24, 2001.
COMMERCIALS SEGMENT SIX “IRRESISTIBLE” EDDIE FANTASTIC vs. WILDFIRE (w/Laura) Tony the K is in the ring along with referee Kendall Dolemeyer. TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Introducing first, coming down the aisle...
'Civil War' by Guns 'N' Roses plays as the fans boo. TONY THE K: Weighing 220 pounds, from Bucksnort, TN, 'Irresistible' Eddie
Fantastic! Fantastic comes down to the ring and gets in, playing to the crowd. TONY THE K: And his opponent... 'Bawitdba" by Kid Rock plays as the fans cheer. Weighing 232 pounds, from Venice Beach, CA, accompanied to the ring by
Laura, Wildfire! Wildfire comes down to the ring and gets in. The bell rings. Fantastic plays to the crowd. Wildfire nails Fantastic with a kick to the midsection.
Wildfire whips Fantastic into the corner. He goes for a handspring elbow but Fantastic nails him with an elbow smash! Fantastic whips Wildfire into the corner. Fantastic charges in but Wildfire nails him with a forearm smash! Wildfire throws Fantastic out of the ring. Wildfire waits in the ring while Fantastic riles up the crowd.
Wildfire goes outside the ring. Fantastic reverses Wildfire into the steel ringpost! Wildfire is rolled back in the ring by Fantastic. Fantastic applies an
Achilles' tendon hold.
Wildfire reaches the ropes. Fantastic releases the hold. Fantastic climbs to the top turnbuckle. Fantastic hits with the moonsault!
Fantastic goes for the pin! 1... 2... Wildfire kicks out! Fantastic climbs to the top turnbuckle. Fantastic goes for a flying headbutt but Wildfire rolls out of the way! Wildfire climbs to the top turnbuckle… and hits with a flying splash! Fantastic rolls outside the ring. Wildfire waits in the ring. Fantastic rolls back in the ring. Wildfire taunts Fantastic. Fantastic nails
Wildfire with an eye rake. Fantastic hits with a chokehold. The referee makes his count... 1 ... 2
... 3 ... 4 ... Fantastic lets go. Fantastic goes for the pin! 1...
2... Wildfire kicks out! Wildfire hits with a chop. The crowd goes “WHOO!”
Wildfire whips Fantastic into the ropes... Fantastic hits with a clothesline!
Wildfire rolls out of the ring. Fantastic plays to the crowd. Wildfire rolls back in the ring.
Wildfire hits with a double axhandle. Fantastic hits with a kick. Wildfire hits with a punch. Fantastic nails Wildfire with a forearm smash. Wildfire is whipped into the ropes... Fantastic hits with a backflip smash!
Fantastic goes for the pin! 1... 2... Wildfire gets his shoulder up! Fantastic applies the dragon sleeper! Wildfire could be out! Wildfire does not submit. Wildfire manages to reach the ropes. Fantastic releases the hold. Wildfire rolls out of the ring.
Fantastic argues with the referee. Mondo Gecko runs down to ringside and attacks Wildfire with a steel chair! Gecko rolls Wildfire back in the ring.
Fantastic goes for the Showstopper (belly-to-belly superplex)... he hits! Fantastic goes for the pin! 1... 2... 3! Wildfire is pinned! MATCH STATS 'Irresistible' Eddie Fantastic pinned Wildfire after the Showstopper (belly-to-belly superplex) in 5:19. Show closes with Gecko celebrating what he did to Wildfire, and Fantastic celebrating his victory.
