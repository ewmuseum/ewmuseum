CWO REPORT 7/1/97 (fwd)

2 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey, who is standing in front of the Lincoln Memorial in Washington D.C.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to a special edition of the CWO REPORT. We're coming to you from our nation's capital where in just a matter of days, the CWO will turn this city upside down with the PPV extravaganza, CWO's Rocking Independance Day. But before we get to that, the CWO made an appearance in Pittsburgh, PA for the last House Show that will take place before the PPV. Let's get to the results for that card. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 6/30/97 PITTSBURGH, PA Coldfront defeated Mercenaries when 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White pinned Kaptain Krunch after the Coldsnap (shoulderbreaker) and outside interference by the Regulators. The Regulators tried to get their belts back, but were prevented from doing so by members of Gold Kard International. The Mercenaries were once again able to escape with the belts. Time of match: 14:27. 'Monster' Rich Clark pinned Steel after the Choke Slam. Time of match: 3:58. Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the dropkick. Alles was obviously inebriated during this match and actually broke free from his father Gordy and Gold Kard, who urged him not to get in the ring. Time of match: 11:28. Vlad the Impaler defeated Bishop by disqualification when Bishop threw a fireball in his face. Time of match: 16:33. Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Vegas High Roller after the Rattler (sideways DDT). Time of match: 7:11. Dark Raven pinned Serpentmaster after the reversed rollup. Time of match: 5:29. TEXAS TORNADO MATCH: Mason-Dixon defeated Moses/Masher Maccabee in a wild match when Beauregard Dixon pinned Moses after the missed Commandment. Time of match: 22:43. North American Champion Tiger Rogers pinned Johnny Reb in a non-title match after the Inverted Powerbomb. Jimbo Jones held the leg of Johnny Reb to aid in the pinfall. Time of match: 14:52. Highlanders defeated Gold Kard/Matsuhiro Torunaka when Conor McCloud pinned Matsuhiro Torunaka after the neckbreaker. Time of match: 10:05. WASHEY: A sold out crowd witnessed the finest action that the CWO has to offer. But as promised, let's take a look at the upcoming PPV with the card that will kick off the event, the Eye Of The Storm. EYE ON THE STORM: ROCKIN' INDEPENDENCE DAY The Big Cool vs. Deadly Sins vs. Coldfront A big match between three of the CWO's top ranked tag team title contenders. Some may dispute the win-loss record of the Deadly Sins, but everybody has to remember that most of the Sins' losses are due to the teams unfortunate habit of getting themselves disqualified. The Coldfront picked up a big win over the Mercenaries last night and are heading into this contest with some sort of momentum. As for the Big Cool, they remain a force to be reckoned with. With fantastic all around skills, the duo of Jumbo Jenkins and Jazzman Taylor remain a strong contender to the World Tag Team titles. One thing is for certain. The winners of this match will manuver themselves into a #1 contender postion for a World Title shot. Steel vs. Wildfire Steel needs to assert himself in this match against one of the members of the Wild Things, after coming off an embarrassing loss to former CWO World Champion, "Monster" Rich Clark. Wildfire is one of the members of the biggest stables in the federation and is coming off a spectacular win over former CWO Television Champion Miguel Sanchez. Steel could pull off the upset, but with the confidence that being in the Wild Things and his last win brought, Wildfire is in a sure position to win. Mason-Dixon vs. Brother Justice The Southern born and bred Mason-Dixon will have their hands full with a tag team looking to make a name for themselves. Brother Justice let the whole federation know what their plans are in the CWO at last Tuesday night's FEVER, but have not been heard from since. Look for a classic tag team match as the wily veterans take on a hungry young team that could be seeing a North American Tag Title shot very soon. "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher vs. Matthew "The Man" White We heard from Deutscher last week, claiming that he was going to start adding a lot more notches in his win column. Even though he didn't get this match with the man he directly challenged, Diamondback Chris Myers, White is no pushover. But the question that everybody will be asking is whether or not the "Lone Wolf" is looking past White. On the other hand, will White be concentrating on his match with Deutscher, or wondering about what beer he will drink after the match is over? Who will emerge the "Man"? It's anybody's guess. WASHEY: An incredible lineup for the free show before the PPV. Now let's look at the main PPV card with analysis on each and every match. CWO ROCKIN' INDEPENDENCE DAY 7/6/97 WASHINGTON, D.C. Bishop and Dark Raven vs. Bloody Fangs This match will be hard to predict a winner, but one thing is for sure. Tempers and emotions will be running high. Bishop was a former member of the same stable as Vlad the Impaler and Serpentmaster. After he left, he joined forces with Dark Raven, who had been up until then, part of the stable run by Master of Darkness Damion. Attacks on one team by the other have been standard the past few months. Will this match settle the hatred these four men feel for each other? Doubtful, but this should be a brutal match, nonetheless. Look for a possible DQ in this match, especially if Bishop engages his habit of throwing fireballs. New Men vs. X-Factor and Marc Mission X-Factor and Marc Mission gave an interview last week, describing their intentions of reaching the top of the federation. The New Men haven't been seen on a televised show since almost their debut in the federation. X-Factor and Mission seem to want to win more. Their will to win could be all they need to pull off the victory in this match. El Grande Pistolerro vs. "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia A rematch of a bout that took place this weekend saw Pistolerro beat Maia in under 3 minutes. But don't let it fool you as this match has the chance to become one of the most exciting on the card. Both stars hail from south of the border where the action is more high flying and risk taking than it's USA counterpart. Not much is known about Maia, but if he's anything like similar wrestlers hailing from that part of the world, this match has the potential to be a barnburner. Look for Pistolerro to bring his CWO experience and momentum from his win to get the victory in this match. Will Maia have enough to overcome it? We'll have to wait and see. Ghost Riders and ?? vs. Goliath, Inferno, David "The Destroyer" Davis The last interview we witnessed from the Ghost Riders featured some talk of a mystery man entering their ranks. Rumor has been running wild in CWO locker rooms as to who this person is. We'll find out come July 6th as the Ghost Riders meet the team of Goliath, Inferno and Davis. This is a hard match to predict, not only because of the number of participants in it, but because the psychological advantage that the Ghost Riders may have. Will Goliath, Inferno and Davis prepare for any occurance, or could the Ghost Riders already have the match won? Diamondback Chris Myers vs. Wrath Myers is one of the hottest young rookies ever to make a debut in this federation, and he's on a roll, pulling off wins almost every time he fights, and scoring some very impressive victories over established CWO veterans. Wrath has been suffering in competition lately, but a victory over Myers could turn all of that around. Can Wrath avoid the Rattler, the signature move that has ended in a victory several times for Myers, or will Myers fall prey to Wrath's penchant for rulebreaking, suffering a rare loss? "Monster" Rich Clark vs. GKI's New Man Former CWO World Title holder "Monster" Rich Clark lost an important match to GKI's Gold Kard on last week's FEVER due to the interferance of an unknown individual, resulting in a lost World title opportunity. Could this person be responsible for the mysterious circumstances surrounding that controversial loss? Look for Clark to take out his frustrations on this person with the exclamation point of his arguement being the Choke Slam. But Clark could have the same problem in this encounter that Goliath, Inferno and Davis have in theirs. How can you prepare for an opponent that's unknown? BURIED ALIVE MATCH: Fergus McTavish vs. Bloodbath One of the most outrageous personalities in the CWO takes on the man that he beat at the last PPV in a match that will not end until there is a clear winner. Bloodbath is looking to settle the score of that last match that ended in a controversial fashion when the small boy that is always present with McTavish distracted Bloodbath long enough for McTavish to get the win. Bloodbath made an appearance during McTavish's match for the TV title on FEVER, chokeslamming him to the ground. This match will be a wild one as Bloodbath has vowed to pay back not only McTavish, but also the small boy. SINGAPORE CANE MATCH: Sandman Jr. vs. The Focus Talk about a signature match. Sandman Jr. in the style of match that he claims he can never lose, meets the Focus, who is out to prove him wrong. Will Focus not only be able to make the cocky Sandman Jr. eat his cane, in addition to his words, or will the little Sandman be in his own element, allowing him to dish out the kind of punishment that this match is notorious for? One thing is for certain....the winner won't be able to sit down for a couple of weeks. GRUDGE MATCH: Johnny Reb vs. Lord Hile Troy These two men have been bitter enemies for as long as anybody can remember. Troy's return to the CWO brings back bitter memories for the former CWO Television Champion. Look for this match to be a brutal affair with nothing really settled. One match between these two will not be enough to erase the hatred that flows through these two men's veins. Can Troy shake off months of ring rust to be able to compete at the same level as JR? His tag team outings have said yes, but fighting in singles is somewhat of a different affair. However, the hate that these two men have could make this the most violent on the card. This match could fill the void that once was filled with the fued between Maximum Overdrive and Brent Alles. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Mondo Gecko vs. Moses (champion) With the GKI takeover, several CWO wrestlers banded together to counter the GKI stable. Mondo Gecko, former TV Champion, knows what it takes to win the gold. However, you can bet that GKI forces will be waiting in the wings to make sure that everything goes the way they want it. Gecko could add another title to his list, but Moses, as well as the rest of GKI, won't give that belt up without a fight. These two can showcase an incredible amount of ability between the two of them, however the bigger story is the groups that are standing behind each of them and the role that they could play in this title encounter. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Brent "The Ace" Alles, Vegas High Roller, Matsuhiro Torunaka vs. Jason "Flash" Varga, Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, Brian "The Hammer" Williams (champions) Lots of tangibles in this match. The biggest subplot of this match will be rumors surrounding the clean cut, all American Schillo and Williams funding a string of "hits" against Alles, who many have seen is nowhere near his ability these days, have been flying constantly for the last 7 or 8 months. The title belts play a secondary role in this match, and as a matter of fact, have not been defended very many times in recent memory. As many of us witnessed on FEVER, Alles has gone considerably downhill from his days as one of the CWO's biggest talents. Will GKI be able to get Alles to pull together long enough to score the win, or will things go the College Guys' way? Will the rumors be confirmed about the contracts? Maybe......and maybe not. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Panther vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley (champion) Champion triple B will defend his title against the little heard from Panther. Not really knowing much about Panther could be a setback against Bentley, but Bentley's impressive ring skills have withstood the tests of all challengers so far. Will Bentley be able to hold onto the gold, or will the challenger win the match and retire to the shadows where he seems to lurk? NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mercenaries vs. Regulators (champions) If you don't know the story behind this match, you must have been living under a rock the past month. Seconds after defeating the Bad Guys at the last PPV and winning the titles, the Regulators were viciously attacked by the Mercenaries. The next week, the two teams met in a Detroit Street Fight match that the Mercenaries won and immediantly declared themselves the champions, taking the belts with them. Since then, the two teams have played games of cat and mouse, with the Regulators usually coming out with the short end of the stick. This is an interesting matchup, considering that the Mercenaries convincingly defeated the Regulators over the weekend, but the Regulators got a measure of revenge last night, costing the Mercenaries in a match against the Coldfront. This match will do to the team that wants it the most and will do everything in their power to make sure that they're the ones that leave with the titles. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Masher Maccabee vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) Another GKI member gets a shot at gold as Maccabee takes on the powerful Tiger Rogers who was quite busy during the weekend, defending his title at two House Shows. But will Rogers have the help he needed to keep his title like he did over the weekend? Maccabee has the whole GKI behind him. Lots of outside interferance is possible in this match, as it seems Rogers always has a cornerman, but look for GKI to somehow worm their way into this. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Highlanders vs. Bad Guys (champions) A rematch from this past weekend that saw the Highlanders lose their titles to the former North American Tag champs. The Highlanders are coming off a win against two members of GKI from last night, but if the Bad Guys land their signature move, the Bad Guys Bash, this match is history. Both teams are not known for their scientific skills. Look for a wild match, both inside and outside the ring as this will lift the roof right off the arena. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Gold Kard vs. Joe Eagle (champion) Finally, the main event. Kard won the title shot after beating "Monster" Rich Clark this past FEVER. Eagle is untested as champion and has not hardly given a title defense in his one month reign. Was his win over Clark a fluke? "Der Master Wrestler" knows every trick in the book and Eagle will have to be in top form if he expects to leave with his title intact. This match is classic scientific/rulebreaking vs total power and strength. Also count on GKI somehow making an appearance, as well as Eagle's group. With a majority of GKI's wrestlers in title matches this PPV, GKI could wind up with all the cards with a Gold Kard win. The future of the CWO is uncertain and lies in the hands of these two men... WASHEY: Well, there you have it. A total rundown of the upcoming PPV spectacular. Normally, this would be the portion of the program where the interviews would be featured, but strangely enough, we don't seem to have any.* Well, that about wraps things up.
