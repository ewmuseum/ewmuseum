CWO REPORT 6/30/97 (fwd)

30 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey standing in front of the Pittsburgh Civic Arena.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to another edition of the CWO REPORT. We're coming to you live from downtown Pittsburgh, where the CWO will be making an appearance tonight for a special Monday night card. Yesterday afternoon, the CWO was in Dayton, OH for an afternoon show. Let's look at the results. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 6/29/97 DAYTON, OH Benjamin Blake Bentley/Lord Hile Troy defeated Bloody Fangs when Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned Serpentmaster after the Nobility (spike Pedigree). Time of match: 9:09. Inferno pinned Wrath after the piledriver. Time of match: 8:47. Deadly Sins defeated Bishop/Dark Raven by disqualification when Bishop threw a fireball in Pride's face. Time of match: 21:07. Sandman Jr. and Vegas High Roller wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 8:20. Wild Ones defeated Gold Kard/Matsuhiro Torunaka when Mondo Gecko pinned Gold Kard after the reversed cradle. Time of match: 8:29. El Grande Pistolerro pinned 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia after the Springboard Frankensteiner. Time of match: 2:34. Mercenaries defeated North American Tag Team Champions the Regulators in a non-title match when Kaptain Krunch pinned Regulator Ice after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. The Mercenaries were once again able to leave with the North American Tag Team Championships. Time of match: 16:42. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION TITLE MATCH (TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS MATCH): Champion Tiger Rogers won the match, 2 falls to 0. Tiger Rogers won fall 1 in 7:30 after the Inverted Powerbomb. Tiger Rogers won fall 2 in 17:40 after the flying dropkick and outside interference by a drunken Brent 'The Ace' Alles. Inferno accompanyed Rogers to the match, but was unable to stop Alles from interferring. CWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS TITLE MATCH (NO DQ): Bad Guys defeated champions the Highlanders to win the titles when Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker pinned Conor McCloud after the Bad Guys' Bash. Time of match: 21:18. WASHEY: I guess congratulations are in order to the Bad Guys, NEW CWO World Tag Team Champions. As I mentioned in the top of the program, we will be appearing at the Pittsburgh Civic Arena for a sold out show. Here's the lineup for tonight's card. MONDAY, JUNE 30, 1997 COLUMBUS, OH Mercenaries vs. Coldfront "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Steel Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo Bishop vs. Vlad the Impaler Vegas High Roller vs. Diamondback Chris Myers Dark Raven vs. Serpentmaster TEXAS TORNADO MATCH: Mason-Dixon vs. Masher Macabee/Moses NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Johnny Reb vs. Tiger Rogers (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Gold Kard/Matsuhiro Torunaka vs. Highlanders (champions) WASHEY: We have to remind everybody that there is a typo in the card, as the World Tag Team title match will instead be a regularly sactioned match, since the Highlanders were defeated last night in Dayton. In other CWO news, there will be no House Shows sactioned this week as the CWO gears up for it's Fourth of July weekend spectacular. In other news, there are no interviews to report on, so that will wrap things up for today. Be sure not to miss tomorrow's REPORT, as we will be bringing you the results from tonight's card, as well as in depth coverage of the upcoming Rocking Independance Day PPV, as well as any interviews that we recieve. So long everybody! (Fade to closing credits.)
