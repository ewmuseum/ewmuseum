CWO REPORT 1/27/97

27 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Monday, January 27, 1997. A reminder to the handlers of the CWO that House Show challenges are due by midnight tonight. Now, to tomorrow night's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, which has become incredibly exciting due to an announcement made today by CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson. Let's look at the line-up for tomorrow's card. _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 1/28/97 ST. LOUIS, MO DARK MATCHES: "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Sandman Jr. Highlanders vs. David and Goliath HOUR 1 Sin vs. Bane Bloodbath vs. Talon Coldfront vs. Mason-Dixon Phantom Freightrain vs. Dark Raven HOUR 2 Flyboy vs. Steel Wild Ones vs. Tower of Power TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Maximum Overdrive (champion) vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams Pit Bosses vs. Eternal Wanderers #1 CONTENDER MATCH (no disqualifications, no countouts, no time limit): Master of Darkness Damion vs. Hile Troy _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's right, fans, tomorrow night's match between the former World champion Master of Darkness Damion and another former world champion, Hile Troy, will be a no disqualification, no countout, no time limit affair. The winner of the match will get a shot at the World title at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II on Friday, February 14, at the Cow Palace in San Francisco, CA. You can't afford to miss tomorrow night's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, we hope to see you there! This is Johnny Starr with the CWO REPORT. (Closing creds and music come up as we fade out.)
