SCORCH 2/28/99 - Hour #1

March 4, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCORCH 2/28/99 - HOUR #1 MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT - NORTH DIVISION - ROUND 1 LIVE FROM: Minneapolis, MN (Target Center) AEN (All Entertainment Network) 8:00 PM EST RATING: TV-14 (L, V) PRODUCER: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) EXECUTIVE PRODUCER: Brent G. Alles ( [detail withheld] ) http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5403/ (The CWO montage is shown, with clips from past Fevers and PPV's. Our famous spinning Mother Earth is shown, and then branded with the CWO logo as intense orchestral music begins to play.)
VOICE-OVER: The CWO... you ain't seen nothing yet! ("Living Dead Girl" by Rob Zombie plays as the CWO logo floating in space comes up, glimmering as lasers reflect off it. Suddenly, the logo explodes into a ball of flame, and as the pieces fly into space, it reveals a warp hole through which the shot zooms, revealing footage of CWO Superstars executing their finishing moves. Finally, the last shot is on the screaming fans of the sold out Target Center. On the screen, the caption, "LIVE! FROM
MINNEAPOLIS, MN!" blazes up in digital flames, and the crowd roars as lasers and pyrotechnics light up the sold out coliseum. The camera pans the throbbing masses as they hold their signs aloft, trying to get their precious 15 seconds of fame. Among those singled out, "DARKNESS FEARS
TAGGART!", "OUTLAW RIDERS 4 LIFE... UNIT 4 EVER!", and "MYERS.... WE ARE
SORRY!" After taking another long shot across the crowd, then camera comes to rest on the broadcasters' booth, where both T. J. Marks and Jerry
Covenant sit, both clad in nicely cut tuxedos. A digital explosion under their faces reveals a caption with their names, and both men look around in exuberance as the fans begin chanting, "CWO! CWO! CWO!")
MARKS: WELCOME EVERYONE TO CWO SUNDAY NIGHT SCORCH! We are live from the jam-packed Target Center as all these fans are ready for the 1st round of the
March Madness Tournament! I am T. J. Marks and alongside me is Jerry Covenant! COVENANT: And as we saw last week, Darkness is in control once now... but team CWO seems to have gotten itself together! MARKS: The CWO Team has a huge surprise in store for the Dark Side and we are set to bring you our opening match of the Tournament!
(We go to the ring as Chad Johnson and Jeremy Lang are in the ring.) CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 10 minute time limit. It is the 1st match in the 1st round of the North Division of the CWO March Madness Tournament. Your referee is
Jeremy Lang. Coming down the aisle... ('Milque Toast' by Helmet plays as the fans cheer.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 225 pounds, from parts unknown, The Focus! (The Focus walks to the ring, in his usual serious and intense mood, but this time acknowledges the crowd a bit. The Focus gets in the ring and stares at the entranceway to the locker room.)
MARKS: The Focus making his return to the CWO after not being seen for a while! CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('All in the Family' by Korn plays as the fans boo.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 265 pounds, from Hollywood, CA, accompanied to the ring to the ring by Vachyna and Lt. Cammy LeBeau, representing the Outlaw
Riders, the CWO North American Champion, Hollywood Haskins! (Haskins and the vivacious LeBeau walk down to the ring. Haskins has a look of confidence as he shows off his new NA Title belt. Vachyna is in her usual skimpy and tight outfit while LeBeau is wearing her usual military fatigues.)
MARKS: If anybody didn't think that Haskins was unbearable and arrogant before he was North American champ... this is going to be bad!
(Haskins does his own version of the "Dirty Bird" as he gets a good pop from the crowd, mostly the females.)
MARKS: Haskins with his own little dance step to infuriate the crowd. (The bell rings.) MARKS: All right, this match underway, Haskins whipping Focus into the ropes... Haskins with the superkick! Cover! 1 ... 2 ... thr... shoulder up!
COVENANT: The North American Champion starting quickly in this match! MARKS: Haskins now sneering at The Focus as he catches him in a powerslam and he is now paintbrushing Focus!
COVENANT: Well this is how Haskins operates and you can see he has no respect for the Focus.
MARKS: The first match of the March Madness tournament is underway as Haskins goes to suplex Focus... Focus with the small package! 1 ... 2 ... thr... and a kick out! (Both men get back to their feet as the Focus goes for a backslide…) MARKS: The Focus going for a backslide but Haskins reverses it! 1... 2... thr... no, a kick out! HASKINS: I am going to send your ass back to the retirement home were you belong! (Focus fires back with a chop!) MARKS: The Focus trying to mount a comeback as he goes for the backslide but Haskins reverses it! 1... 2... kick out! Haskins goes for the guillotine leg drop... nobody there! The Focus with the reverse neckbreaker as he applies the Fujiwara armbar now as Haskins is forced to reach the ropes!
COVENANT: The Focus can out mat wrestle Haskins and that is what he has to do right now because Haskins is taking it to the ex TV Champ!
MARKS: The Focus with a double axhandle as he runs into the ropes... Haskins with a dropkick. Haskins goes for the pin! 1... 2... kick out!
(Haskins argues with the referee.) MARKS: Haskins jawing with Lang about the count as he goes up top.... Haskins hits with the moonsault! And Haskins now has Focus in the katahajame. COVENANT: Well Haskins has been training with Gecko and Cammy on submission holds and you can see here what he has learned. This is a huge change from what we saw three months ago!
MARKS: Haskins it seems has finally shed that goof gimmick as he releases the hold (Haskins connects with an elbow smash.) MARKS: Haskins dropping the ex TV champ with a dropkick as he goes up top... Haskins with a moonsault! HASKINS: YOU PATHETIC SCHMUCK, ITS TIME FOR A CLOSE-UP! COVENANT: Haskins just trying to humiliate The Focus, he should have just made a cover instead....
(The Focus gets up and nails Haskins with a low blow, which doubles over "Woody".) MARKS: That may have been the break the Focus needs as he hammers Haskins with a karate kick. The Focus whips Haskins into the ropes... big back body drop...
Focus with a dropkick! The Focus is going up top... wait a minute... Vachyna is distracting The Focus! (Haskins seizes the moment and slams The Focus from the top rope.) COVENANT: Vachyna has done her job as Haskins is back in control thanks in large part to his manager!
(Haskins delivers a dropkick.) MARKS: And Haskins is now going for the kill as he gets The Focus into position... HOLLYWOOD BOMB! THIS MATCH IS OVER! 1... 2... 3!
CHAD JOHNSON: The winner of this match at 8:56, HOLLYWOOD HASKINS! (As Haskins and his entourage celebrate in the ring, the camera fades to black as we go to commercials including this one.)
(NBA Final Four-style music plays in the background.) VOICE-OVER: 16 men... (Superstars of the CWO are shown in a fast montage.) VOICE-OVER: One title... (The CWO heavyweight title is shown.) VOICE-OVER: One DARKNESS... (The Dark Side are shown in all their dark glory.) (MARCH MADNESS '99 graphic comes up.) VOICE-OVER: It's CWO MARCH MADNESS '99, live, Sunday, March 28, only on PPV! (Fade out. When we come back Johnny Starr is in the back with Brian “The King” Derks. Derks’ friend and manager, the Leech, is close by.) STARR: All right, fans, welcome back to SCORCH, as we are in the 1st round of the North Division of the 1999 CWO March Madness Tournament. We are taking a short break from the ring action, though, to speak to this young man, a newcomer to the CWO, Brian “The King” Derks. Now, Mr. Derks…
DERKS: That’s KING Derks to you, peasant. STARR: Uh… right. Well, King Derks, you’ve been impressive so far in the CWO, but now I’ve heard through the grapevine that you have set your sights on CWO superstar “Double D” Damian Dagger.
DERKS: That’s right. I am the King and I will make “Double D” my slave. STARR: Uh… OK. That sounds really serious, Brian? How do you plan on doing that? DERKS: The king will be adding a lot of people to his list of slaves. REPORTER: What do you mean list of slaves? DERKS: What I mean is that my slaves will soon have to pull me to the ring on a cart and I will have a horse whip to hit them when they walk too slow.
STARR: Uh… right. Well King, since you are new, why are you taking on a person that is ranked higher than you are?
DERKS: I am not a milquetoast, I do not believe I should wrestle people ranked under me because people that are ranked under me I say are plebeians.
STARR: O-K. Well, that is all the time we have for Brian “The King” Derks. DERKS: Don’t you dare cut me off! We’ll go to commercial when I say we’ll go to commercial!
STARR: Oh, shut up. We’ll be right back… (Derks glares at Starr as we fade to commercial.) COMMERCIALS (When we come back we see Chad Johnson and Chris Connor in the ring for the next match.) CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 10 minute time limit. Your referee is Chris Connor. Coming down the aisle...
(Eerie sounds of the night are heard plays as the fans boo) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 280 pounds, from parts unknown, accompanied to the ring by Fallen and Night Slinger, one half of the CWO World Tag Team
Champions, Night Crawler! (The Creatures and the Fallen make their way down to the ring, bathed in that eerie purple glow. As Night Crawler gets in the ring, the lights go back on as
Fallen and Night Crawler are huddling around the ring.) MARKS: One half of the World Tag Team Champions and even though he received a low seed you can’t automatically count him out.
COVENANT: This could be one of the upsets! And his opponent... ('Bad Company’ by Bad Company plays as the fans cheer.) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 322 pounds, from Phoenix, AZ, accompanied to the ring by Rachel Thomasson, Col. Taggart!
(The fans cheer as Thomasson and Taggart make their way to the ring as fireworks go off in the rafters. However, as Taggart gets to the ring,
Fallen and Night Slinger start pounding on him before the bell.) MARKS: WHAT A BUNCH OF COWARDS! Darkness sending his goons to take out Taggart and they might accomplish the job tonight! (Connor makes the other Dark Side members leave the ring.) MARKS: Thank goodness Chris Connor is making the Dark Side leave the ring, good to see him standing up to them. Night Crawler now, though, delivering a brainbuster and goes for the pin! 1... 2... and a kick out! Night
Crawler goes for the DDT... Taggart with a block… and he hits with a Northern Lights Suplex! COVENANT: Taggart can out wrestle Night Crawler and that is what he has to do as Night Crawler would love to make this a brawl and send it outside!
MARKS: Yeah, so the Dark Side would get their hands on him! Taggart is in control as he delivers with a chop and whips Night Crawler into the ropes...
Taggart with the dropkick. Taggart goes outside... slingshot legdrop misses the mark! (Night Crawler puts a half Boston crab on.) COVENANT: Night Crawler has a big opportunity... take out one of Darkness' enemies and advance into the tournament. MARKS: Darkness has to be happy as Night Crawler is firmly in control of this match. Taggart reaching the ropes, Night Crawler has to break the hold…
(Night Crawler picks Taggart up from the mat…) MARKS: Night Crawler delivers a power bomb! Night Crawler climbs to the top... flying elbow drop finds the mark!
COVENANT: He’s pouring it on! MARKS: Night Crawler now running into the ropes... Night Crawler with a DDT! COVENANT: Got him! MARKS: And now Night Crawler applying the Fujiwara armbar in the middle of the ring. COVENANT: Rachel is desperately trying to rally her man on the outside and I for one am glad Connor threw out the rest of the Dark Side.
MARKS: I am too as Night Crawler releases the hold and waits for Taggart to get back to his feet…
(Night Crawler runs into the ropes…) MARKS: Taggart with a spinning enzugiri kick! Taggart goes for the pin! 1 ... 2 ... thr... no, Crawler got his shoulder up! (Taggart stomps away on Night Crawler.) MARKS: Taggart stomping away, and now he’s going for the Annihilator... but Night Crawler gets to the ropes in time! (Taggart goes for the spinning elbow drop but Night Crawler rolls out of the way!) COVENANT: Taggart looks a bit spent and you can bet that it has to be a result of getting jumped earlier on!
MARKS: No doubt of that as Night Crawler delivers a leg drop. (Night Crawler stomps away on Taggart.) MARKS: Night Crawler stomping away, now a legdrop. He picks Taggart up from the mat... big snap suplex on the military man. Night Crawler now runs into the ropes... BIG SPLASH! THAT COULD BE ALL AS NIGHT CRAWLER GOES FOR THE
PIN! 1... 2... thr... no, a KICK OUT BY TAGGART! (Night Crawler picks up Taggart but is distracted by Rachel who is on the top apron and is yelling at him.)
COVENANT: That may not be the smartest thing to do, as The Dark Side has no qualms about beating up women!
MARKS: Night Crawler now turning his attention back to Taggart... POWER BOMB... NO... TAGGART WITH A BACKDROP! Taggart is on fire now... DRAGON SUPLEX! AND TAGGART HAS THE ANNIHILATOR ON! NIGHT CRAWLER TAPS OUT! CHAD JOHNSON: The winner of this match at 9:47, COL. TAGGART! MARKS: Big win for the Col., and we’ll be right back! (Taggart slides under the ring and makes his way through the crowd with Rachel as both play with the crowd. The camera fades out as we got to CWO House Shows, Budweiser Frogs, Ford Trucks, and NCAA March Madness. When we come back, we see Chad Johnson and John Myering in the ring.)
CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 10 minute time limit. Your referee is John Myering. Coming down the aisle...
('Wherever I May Roam' by Metallica plays as the fans cheer.) TONY THE K: Weighing 395 pounds, from Houston, TX, accompanied to the ring by Lt. Cammy LeBeau, Ranger Taurus!
(The crowd noise increases as the Outlaw Rider duo appears at the ring entrance. While their pyrotechnics go off over the ring, they make their way to the ring,
Taurus with a cold intense stare on his face, psyching himself up, while LeBeau appears happy yet ready for a fight tonight.) MARKS: Another Outlaw Rider in this hour as Ranger Taurus also has his work cut out for him.
COVENANT: This is an 8th seed vs. a 9th seed match, and those can be the toughest to call! CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... ('The Secret of My Success' plays as the fans boo) CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 227 pounds, from Successville, USA, accompanied to the ring to the ring by Miss Juli Ashton, a member of the True Americans,
Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky! (The eager and eccentric duo make their way out, Juli dressed scantily with an American Flag outfit, complete with two stars in the appropriate spots. Bondavarsky waves sparklers about him as the fans boo. Bondavarsky gets in the ring, while LeBeau starts staring down an intimidated Juli.)
MARKS: This should be a great one… and we are underway… Taurus going for a clothesline but Bondavarsky ducks... Bondavarsky with a dropkick! Taurus is still up... 2nd dropkick drops the big man.
COVENANT: This is what Bondavarsky needs to do, stick and move, stick and move. MARKS: Bondavarsky with a slingshot somersault splash, and now he’s going for the armbar submission but Taurus counters it with an eyerake.
COVENANT: The classic matchup of big man vs. little man and force I gotta favor the big man as Taurus can be such a dominating force!
MARKS: Well I think Taurus is now a bit angry as he hammers The True American with some hard rights and whips him into the ropes! Big Lariat! Taurus goes for the pin! 1... 2... and a kick out!
(Taurus picks Bondavarsky up from the mat but Bondavarsky meets him with a thumb to the eye.)
MARKS: Bondavarsky with a thumb to the eye, back into the ropes... and right into a big boot of Taurus!
(Bondavarsky staggers into the corner...) MARKS: Bondavarsky in the corner… Taurus with an avalanche! TAURUS: TIME FOR AN ASS WHIPPING LITTLE MAN! COVENANT: You gotta wonder how angry Taurus still is at Snake as we saw him get jilted out of a title shot on FEVER!
MARKS: Taurus is still seething as he nails Bondavarsky with an elbow smash and drops him with a double axehandle. Taurus goes to the 2nd rope... he goes for the splash... nobody home!
(Bondavarsky gets to his feet.) MARKS: Bondavarsky with the double axhandle... Bondavarsky with a backspin DDT as he goes for the pin! 1... kick out! (Taurus rolls out of the ring.) COVENANT: This could be big! MARKS: Taurus is out of the ring as Bondavarsky runs into the ropes.... Bondavarsky went for a senton splash but Taurus turned it into a power bomb! COVENANT: Wow! (Taurus grabs a steel chair and then proceeds to bash Bondavarsky with it twice as Juli looks on in horror.)
MARKS: Taurus can sense he is ready for the kill as he rolls an unconscious Bondavarsky into the ring! OH NO... TAURUS WITH THE TEXAS STAMPEDE AND BONDAVARSKY IS OUT COLD! COVER! 1... 2... 3! CHAD JOHNSON: The winner of this match at 4:58, RANGER TAURUS MARKS: Taurus with a dominating win here and you got to bet that he could be a huge player in the Tournament here! Folks, we got to take a break for commercials but stay with us.
(Camera fades out to commercials as we see more promos for CWO House Shows, CWO Merchandise, NBA on NBC, Ford Trucks and Buffy the Vampire Slayer show.) (Back to SCORCH as Starr is in the center of the ring.) STARR: Ladies and gentleman, at CWO St. Valentines Day Massacre, the Men of Respect, Wildfire and Union Caine, suffered an embarrassing loss to former member Joe Eagle and his friend Jason "Flash" Varga. Now, on last week’s
FEVER, Wildfire and Union Caine had an altercation in the locker room. Since then we haven't been able to get a word from either Union Caine or Wildfire, but now the latter is about to break his silence. Ladies and gentlemen, Wildfire!
("Too Close" by Next plays as the fans cheer. Wildfire walks out in a navy blue Armani suit, with Oakley sunglasses, a Rolex, and his hair tied behind his back. Kelly, along beside him, is wearing a very elegant evening gown that would make President Clinton blush. Wildfire climbs into the ring along with
Kelly, and walks over to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Welcome Wildfire. Now it is my understanding that your loss has really hit you personally...
WILDFIRE: Well, I would like to get one thing straight. I am tired of people saying that this money has changed me so much...
(Crowd gives a mixed reaction.) WILDFIRE: As much as I didn't want to admit it, they were right. When I went to the ring at Valentine’s Day Massacre, there were fans that were booing me.
And that’s something that doesn't sit very well with Wildfire. STARR: It does seem that you’re not as popular as you once were. WILDFIRE: WELL THAT STOPS TONIGHT! AND THERE IS ONLY ONE THING I CAN DO! (With that, Wildfire takes his sunglasses and throws them into the crowd.) WILDFIRE: $900.00 SUNGLASSES! (He takes off his Rolex and throws it into the crowd.) WILDFIRE: $2,000.00 ROLEX! STARR: Wildfire, what the hell are you doing?!? (Wildfire then tears off his jacket, and tears off his shirt to reveal a black flaming Men of Respect T-shirt that reads "IT’S TIME TO KICK A LITTLE ASS"
on the back. Wildfire tears off his pants to reveal his flaming red tights.) WILDFIRE: $10,000.00 SUIT, NOW NO MORE! AND THAT IS EXACTLY WHAT YOU ARE UNION CAINE! (The crowd cheers!) WILDFIRE: CAINE, you wanted me in the Men of Respect just for my money, but read my lips: I QUIT.
(Crowd cheers.) WILDFIRE: AND THE NEXT TIME YOU GET IN MY FACE, YOU’RE GONNA GET YOUR ASS KICKED! NOW SAY IT WITH ME EVERYONE... WILDFIRE & CROWD: WHATYA GONNA DO WHEN WILDFIRE AND THE NEW MEN OF RESPECT RUNS WILD ON YOU! (The camera fades out.) COMMERCIALS (When we come back we see Chad Johnson and Dominick Waugh in the ring for the next match.) CHAD JOHNSON: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 10 minute time limit. Your referee is Dominick Waugh.
Coming down the aisle... ('Lunatic Fringe' by Red Rider plays as the fans boo.) CHAD JOHNSON: weighing 225 pounds, from Los Angeles, CA, accompanied to the ring by Fury, Kamikaze!
(The whacked out Kamikaze makes his way down to the ring, backed by the red- headed monstrous Fury. The duo makes its way to the ring almost lost, as
Kamikaze seems to wander around the ring as the crowd boos while Fury stands on the outside, arms crossed.)
COVENANT: This guy is not all there. CHAD JOHNSON: And his opponent... (Eerie sounds of the night are heard as the lights go off. Then, the intense theme music of the Dark Side begins to play as the fans boo.)
CHAD JOHNSON: Weighing 312 pounds, from parts unknown, accompanied to the ring by Sindy Sinn, the Fallen!
(Sindy and the Fallen make their way down to the ring, bathed in the eerie purple glow. Sindy has her usual "come hither" smirk on her face, but the
Fallen is all business. However, as Fallen makes his way into the ring, he is attacked by Kamikaze!)
MARKS: Kamikaze wasting no time as he is going up top... senton on Fallen! COVENANT: You know Kamikaze is whacked out when he goes after the Fallen like that! MARKS: Kamikaze with the slam and he goes up top once again.... somersault senton splash! (Fallen rolls outside the ring.) MARKS: Fallen rolling out of the ring… and now these two are brawling outside there. COVENANT: I don't know how sound of a strategy this is as Fallen is bigger and stronger and right now is taking it to Kamikaze on the outside.
MARKS: The Fallen rolls back in the ring, stomping on Kamikaze as he gets back in. And now the Fallen goes up top... flying elbow drop! Fallen goes for the pin! 1... 2... and a kick out!
(Fallen hits with an elbow drop.) MARKS: Fallen into the ropes... legdrop missed the mark! COVENANT: Big break for Kamikaze as Fallen is in pain on the mat and this could be the turning point for the match!
MARKS: Kamikaze is going up top once more.... California Jam hits the mark! This could be it as Kamikaze goes for the pin! 1... 2... no! Fallen got his shoulder up! COVENANT: That was close! MARKS: Kamikaze now delivering a dropkick… follows it up with a swinging neckbreaker. Fallen whipped into the ropes... Kamikaze with a clothesline!
And Now he’s got the crossface chickenwing on! COVENANT: Fallen is going down a bit and this would be a huge upset if Kamikaze could pull this out! MARKS: Fallen going down but look at this.... Fallen counters by driving him back into the turnbuckle. Fallen with a boot to the gut... oh no…
COVENANT: He’s got him in position! (Fallen nails the Tombstone!) COVENANT: DARKNESS FALLS! FALLEN WITH THE COVER! 1... 2... 3! CHAD JOHNSON: The winner of this match at 7:47, FALLEN! (Crowd boos as Fallen’s theme music plays again, Sindy Sinn applauding outside the ring.) MARKS: The Fallen progressing, and you just know he is going to be a force to be reckoned with in this tournament. Stay tuned, we’ll be right back.
(Logo and music come up as we fade to commercial.) END HOUR #1
