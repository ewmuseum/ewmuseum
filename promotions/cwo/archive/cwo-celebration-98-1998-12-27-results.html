CWO CELEBRATION 12/27/98 - Results

January 7, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWO CELEBRATION - RESULTS 12/27/98 Pontiac Silverdome, Pontiac, MI 20 MAN BATTLE ROYAL (WINNER RECEIVES WORLD TITLE SHOT IN JANUARY) – RESULTS: 1 ) 'Assassin 64' Sean Holiday eliminated by Brent 'The Ace' Alles at 2:46. threw out nobody. 2 ) The Focus eliminated by Diamondback Chris Myers at 10:42. threw out nobody. 3 ) 'Chairman of the Board' Gold Kard eliminated by Joe Eagle at 10:56. threw out nobody. 4 ) Joe Eagle eliminated by Vladamir Popalov at 11:26. threw out 'Chairman of the Board' Gold Kard. 5 ) Sandman Jr. eliminated by Union Caine at 11:36. threw out nobody. 6 ) Sandman eliminated by Wrath at 12:57. threw out nobody. 7 ) Wishbone eliminated by Vladamir Popalov at 14:15. threw out nobody. 8 ) 'Double D' Damian Dagger eliminated by Warhawk at 14:43. threw out nobody. 9 ) Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky eliminated by Wrath at 15:00. threw out nobody. 10 ) George Washington eliminated by Warhawk at 16:13. threw out nobody. 11 ) Wrath eliminated by Union Caine at 16:22. threw out Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky, Sandman. 12 ) Wildfire eliminated by Man Mountain Marko at 19:05. threw out nobody. 13 ) 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum eliminated by Warhawk at 20:13. threw out nobody. 14 ) 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin eliminated by Diamondback Chris Myers at 21:55. threw out nobody. 15 ) Vladamir Popalov eliminated by Man Mountain Marko at 22:45. threw out Joe Eagle, Wishbone. 16 ) Warhawk eliminated by Man Mountain Marko at 22:48. threw out 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum, 'Double D' Damian Dagger, George Washington. 17 ) Union Caine eliminated by Brent 'The Ace' Alles at 23:23. threw out Sandman Jr., Wrath. 18 ) Man Mountain Marko eliminated by Diamondback Chris Myers at 24:34. threw out Vladamir Popalov, Warhawk, Wildfire. 19 ) Brent 'The Ace' Alles eliminated by Diamondback Chris Myers at 25:17. threw out 'Assassin 64' Sean Holiday, Union Caine. Winner: Diamondback Chris Myers threw out 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin, Man Mountain Marko, The
Focus, Brent 'The Ace' Alles.
Johnny Reb made Allen 'Mr. America' Carter submit to the Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 13:18. CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Southern Dynamite defeated Secret Agent Super Dragon by disqualification. Time of match: 6:23. Secret Agent Super Dragon retained the CWO Light
Heavyweight Champion title.
Masher Maccabee and Hollywood Haskins wrestled to a double countout. Time of match: 4:08. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Newton Bros. defeated Snake Martin (substituting for the injured Wesley “The
Rock” McMichael) and Ranger Taurus when Howard Newton pinned Snake Martin after the Powerline. Time of match: 7:42. Newton Bros. retained the CWO
North American Tag Team Champions title.
Col. Taggart made Mondo Gecko submit to the Annhilator. Time of match: 6:43. After the match Taggart’s son Michael put the hold on Gecko as well. CWO TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Cris Marcus pinned 'The Avenger' Jesse Ewiak after the X and outside interference by Snake Martin. Time of match: 6:31. Cris Marcus won the CWO
Television Champion title.
Fergus 'Whirling Scottish Devil' McTavish pinned Fallen after the Highland
Fling. Time of match: 5:03.
WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (FOUR-WAY MATCH): Creatures of Darkness defeated Lunatic Fringe and Deadly Sins and 'Dragonfire' Rich Johnson/'Mr. Technical' Cooper Young in a four-way match. Time of match: 8:52. Night Slinger pinned Kamikaze after the flying cross body. The “renegade” team of Johnson and Young mysteriously no-showed, even after Johnson had been seen earlier in the night in a segment with
Commissioner Krepps. The Deadly Sins were not a factor in the match as
Wrath led Lust and Pride away, claiming that somebody was attacking Pride’s car. When they got to the parking lot, nothing had happened, much to the chagrin of the Sins.
WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (IRON MAN MATCH): The match was a draw, 2 falls to 2. Foolkiller won fall 1 in 20:47 after the Thunderfunk. Foolkiller won fall 2 in 28:54 after a jumping neck snap off the top rope. Lord Hile Troy won fall 3 in 35:43 after the top rope bulldog. Lord Hile Troy won fall 4 in 52:51 after the Viledriver.
Foolkiller retained the CWO World Champion title.
