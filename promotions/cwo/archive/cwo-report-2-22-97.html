CWO REPORT 2/22/97

22 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo is in the interview area.) WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: First off, I'd like to apologize to Moses for any injury I may have caused him. I have tremendous respect for him as both a wrestler and as a man. He stands for many of the same things I do, and I wish him luck in the future. As for you, Brent Alles, I don't know who you think you are, accusing me of setting up a hit on you! I've always been able to take care of my own problems... I beat you in the cage, didn't I? I am more than certain that Brian can handle his own problems! I think that you may have been hit on the head a little too hard to think that I am responsible for this. You really should get your own life straightened out before you worry about mine. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Saturday, February 22, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Huntsville, AL, let's see how that card went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS Friday, 2/21/97 Huntsville, AL Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned Mondo Gecko after the Nobility. Time of match: 7:44. After the match Bentley was attacked by the Focus. Regulators defeated Deadly Sins when Regulator Ice pinned Sloth after the Choke Slam. Time of match: 15:43. Gold Kard pinned Johnny Reb after Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash). Time of match: 18:06. Mercenaries defeated Alliance when Kaptain Krunch pinned Inferno after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 4:45. Savior pinned Hellhound after the Revelations top rope powerdrop. Time of match: 17:59. Clark Gray and Battlecat wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 5:47. Bloodbath pinned Steel after the Tombstone Powerbomb. Time of match: 6:41. Mondo Gecko pinned Wrath after the flying elbowdrop. Time of match: 4:30. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Champions the Bad Guys defeated The Big Cool when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Jumbo Jenkins after the Bad Guys' Bash (double strike with steel chairs). Time of match: 11:34. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Pit Bosses defeated Creatures of the Night by disqualification when Hellhound attacked. Time of match: 4:20. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (NON-TITLE, STEEL CAGE) Dark Raven pinned Wayne 'Keuka Guy' Schillo after the Death From Above. Time of match: 8:52. After the match, a gout of flame came from a corner of the cage, and when the smoke cleared, Bishop, Serpentmaster, and Vlad the Impaler appeared, and the foursome, known as the Unholy Alliance, proceeded to beat on Schillo until security broke into the cage and detained all four men. Brent "The Ace" Alles could be seen in the rafters, watching the whole thing, smirking. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Little Rock, AK, let's look at the lineup for that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, FEBRUARY 22, 1997 LITTLE ROCK, AK Regulator Ice vs. Dark Raven Mercenaries vs. Wild Ones Ganglord vs. Chris Greco Wildfire vs. Exile Serpentmaster vs. Gold Kard Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Maximum Overdrive Highlanders vs. Biblical Powers Johnny Reb vs. Mondo Gecko CHAMPION'S CHALLENGE (elimination): Bad Guys (NA champs) vs. Creatures of the Night (World champs) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) vs. Goliath _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: The most besieged man in the league, as usual, is CWO World Champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo. Looking ahead to his World championship match with Bane at SPRING BREAK '97, Wayne now has to deal with Brent "The Ace" Alles AND this new "Unholy Alliance," led by the mysterious man known only as Bishop. We take you to comments from the Unholy Alliance. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Unholy Alliance is in the interview area.) BISHOP: I'm here to announce that tonight was just a taste of what will happen to anyone that crosses our paths. How does it feel to be overpowered by superior talent and numbers, Schillo? Did you like it? I know we did. I felt your body bruise as I kicked your stomach in. I felt your bones crunch as we delivered our package of pain. But don't feel special, Schillo, you're the first of many to come. And don't think we're done with you. We're just getting started. SERPENTMASTER: I am slithering my way down the road to revenge. I'm ssick and tired of not being able to wresstle a match without outside interferencce. Ssso from now on, the Unholy Alliance will be guarding my back. And asss for thiss Black Rose, I think he needss to be taught to mind hiss own affairssss. (Serpentmaster kisses his python.) VLAD THE IMPALER (heavy Hungarian accent): When Bishop contacted me over in the European leagues to ask me to join his Unholy Alliance, I couldn't wait for the chance to sink my teeth into the competition in this league. I'm sure you will all find me to be a pain in the neck. I've chosen my first victim, Baby Waldo. Your fat mounds of flesh contain much blood, I'm sure. I'll see you in your nightmares. BISHOP: The Unholy Alliance is here and we are looking to make our impact on this league very soon. As for you, MOD, Raven is with us now... so stay away from him. As for you, Hellhound, we here at the Unholy Alliance feel that you have talents that are left unappreciated... why don't you join us in our struggle to dominate this league. If you join us now, you can rule with us as equals, if you keep your distance, you will be shown no mercy just like all the others. Make the right choice. As for Raven here, he's back where he belongs. With myself as his mentor and, by the way, his talents improve beyond my expectations, I think that Raven will be preying upon a good number of you in this league, I'd watch my head if I were you. (Bishop pats Raven on the head, as the Unholy Alliance laughs.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight in Little Rock, Johnny Reb goes up against the former Television champion, Mondo Gecko. Let's get comments from Johnny Reb and General Lee. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb and General Lee are in the interview area) JOHNNY REB: Well, y'all maht have dropped the gold Mondo Gecko, but that doesn't mean ah'm not gonna be comin' hell bent lookin' fo' blood! LEE: That's raht! Mistuh Gecko, y'all looked good in that battle royal. But y'all ain't gonna beat Johnny Reb throwin' him ovuh ropes. This is gonna be knock down, drag out. And if y'all thought 6 matches in a week was hell, y'all wait til y'all are on yo' face feelin' the Texas Clovuhleaf! Then, suh, y'all will know hell. Y'all will know torture. REB: Y'all ain't ready fo' this. Gecko, prepare ta be grounded. LEE: Good day, suh. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: A rematch for the Television championship has been announced for SPRING BREAK '97, between current champion Masher Maccabee and this man, Mondo Gecko. _______________________________________________________________________________ MONDO GECKO: Masher Maccabee, you have something that belongs to me and that is my TV title and I WANT IT BACK! I am tired of your manager interfering so that is why I have decided to bring a friend to Spring Break and here he is! (Out of the shadows steps out former WCW wrestler P. N. News, now known as Cannonball Grizzly.) MONDO GECKO: You see, I have taken all the steps to making sure that you don't interfere. I also challenge you to a 30 minute Iron Man match. You say how you're the best wrestler here, well, if you are man enough, let's get it on. I AM GONNA KICK YOUR @$$, DUDE, AND THERE IS NOTHING YOU CAN DO ABOUT IT! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That match has indeed been signed for SPRING BREAK '97, and it will be a 30 Minute Iron Man match, most decisions during that time period wins the match. Another match at SPRING BREAK '97, on the EYE ON THE STORM broadcast held right before the PPV, will be between the aristocrat Benjamin Blake Bentley and the newcomer the Focus. These two men have already had some great battles, and the feud is definitely heating up. Let's hear from the Focus. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: Well, well, well, Lonestar... how did it feel to be hit with the focal point? Not too good, I suppose. I'm going to use that move to go straight to the top. Now to other matters. It seems, Benjamin Blake Bentley, that we have another matchup coming at Eye on the Storm. You see, this time I don't want two referees, or any special stipulations. They don't seem to help me defeat you, so who cares? The one thing I AM going to do is beat you at your own game. I am gonna cheat my ass off. If you beat me for a third time, Benjamin, I may just say forget it, and call it quits between me and you. However, this may have just begun. I declare war on you Benjamin Blake Bentley, and my focus is set on victory. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: On this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, newcomer Jason "Flash" Varga was taking on Bane, but the match was once again disrupted by this mysterious Masked Rose. Here now are comments from "Flash" regarding this situation. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Jason "Flash" Varga is in the interview area with his bodyguard Jim "Big Guy" Peer) JASON "FLASH" VARGA: All right, CWO, it's time to get something straight. The Flash don't need NOBODY'S help to win in the ring. Everybody and their mother saw me take care of Bane, and then the Masked Rose went and stuck his nose in where he wasn't wanted. Look, kids, you can ask anyone from around the country. They'll tell you that I AM THE MAN! Peer... who's the man? PEER: Uhh... you are! VARGA: Yeah, ME! Bane, you can tell 'em... you felt the Flash Flood, and it was a good one. The New Enforcer is in town, takin' care of business and giving the fans what they want! Rose... if you want a fight, I can give it to you. And as for Brent Alles... it's a good thing you're in the hospital now. You didn't want to have to feel the grain of this baseball bat against your head! My man Schillo's got enough to worry about without having to listen to you, too. You evil-doers better watch your back - the Enforcer is here, Flash is his name, my man Jim Peer's in the house, and I AM THE MAN! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Yesterday, the tentative lineup for SPRING BREAK '97 was announced, and one of those matches, on the EYE ON THE STORM broadcast, is between two of the top tag teams in our sport, the Regulators and the Big Cool. Here now are comments from the Regulators. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Regulators are in the interview area.) REGULATOR FIRE: Big Cool, two of the biggest guys around here will face the justice soon. For the time to regulate the CWO is upon us and it is time for the Regulators to take back the league and enforce justice here. Jumbo Jenkins & Jim Taylor, you two have gotten under our skin and now we are going to beat the tar out of you and regulate the CWO FOREVER! REGULATOR ICE: Big Cool, you two are going to be put on Ice when the Regulators are done with you. In fact, we might rename you two thugs the Big Easy because it will be a easy time beating you big fat chumps. I can't wait for Spring Break because it will be one more victory for the Regulators baby! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Some rather aggressive comments by the Regulators concerning the also-popular Big Cool. Another match on the SPRING BREAK '97 card will be for the Light Heavyweight championship, currently held by Tiger Rogers of the Alliance, and he will go up against popular young superstar Panther. Here are comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Panther is dressed in a bright Red Suit and a silver tie and hat. He is surrounded by the "Kittens" who are dressed in yellow leather body suits. He is signing autographs for numerous fans in the CWO. He then looks into the camera and begins to speek, while continuing to sign autographs.) PANTHER: WOW! Friday, March 7, in Panama City there is going to be some celebrating going on! Tiger Rogers, I'll be having my eye on you with great expectations for our showdown in Panama City! (Panther looks at the fans he is signing autographs for.) PANTHER: This is what the business is all about, look at these people! It took me a long time to figure this out, and I did a lot of things wrong in the process. Now it's time for me to start doing the right thing, it's time for me to do something for the Fans. So Tiger, after I put your shoulders to the mat for the 1... 2... 3, I'm inviting you to the Fans' Victory Celebration! UNKNOWN FAN: Panther, can I have an autograph? PANTHER: Sure! FAN: Not YOURS, the KITTENS! PANTHER: Oh! That reminds me of another person ungrateful of greatness. Masked Rose, here you go around attacking people while they are working, what kind of man is that? I used to be a lot like you, Rose, listen here man and listen good. You may feel good about the way you're doing things but it doesn't take much of a man to attack someone while his back is turned. If you want a match, that's fine, I'll be glad to sign the bottom line, the question is, are you man enough to take on someone when they are ready for it? One last item to address, Johnny Reb, don't think I've forgotten about you. There comes a time when a man has to pay his dues, my friend. Your dues in this federation are long overdue, and I'm the fans Repo Man, so when the time is right I'll be knocking on your door. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Finally today, yesterday we saw footage of Brent "The Ace" Alles in his hospital bed, after the vicious attack performed against him by Doomsday on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. The question still remains: did CWO World Champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, a man who prides himself on being "most loved," put a hit out on "The Ace" in response to his injuring Brian "The Hammer" Williams? That remains to be seen, but a match between "The Ace" and Maximum Overdrive has been signed for SPRING BREAK '97, here now is Maximum Overdrive responding to Alles' comments yesterday and the match ahead of them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on Maximum Overdrive as he finishes hearing Brent "The Ace" Alles' comments from the day before.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: ...you're going to think you had it easy after what you see me do to Overdrive. CO1 - EOT. (Maximum Overdrive turns to the camera and begins to speak.) MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: You think you're a tough guy there, Alles? Yeah, you look pretty intimidating lying there in that hospital bed. I've seen your interview and I've heard your comments. Pretty pathetic, gimp. By the way, how's that hospital food? I hear it gives you the runs. (Overdrive leans back in his chair and crosses his hands behind his head.) OVERDRIVE: Let's get something straight, jerk. I've never really had anything against you. Sure, I got sick and tired of your whining from time to time. But even if we were contracted by Schillo, which I won't say either way as to whether we were or weren't, it was all business. But I must admit I did take some pleasure in leaving you crumpled on the mat and kicking you off that stretcher for good measure. (Overdrive leans forward.) OVERDRIVE: Let's get some other facts straight. Yeah, we were former TV Champions. But the big difference between us is that even though you may have been a good champion, I was a GREAT Champion. The CWO's biggest loss was when that belt left my hands. I may have not gotten another shot at that belt, but I've got bigger fish to fry for the time being. As for the two of us being the "old world order" around here, let me enlighten you on something. You are nothing but a fossil of the past. I am part of the future of this league. When we step in that ring together, that's going to be something I'm going to take great pleasure in, teaching you the hard way. (Overdrive leans back in his chair again.) OVERDRIVE: So you've got nothing to lose, you've ripped out and stomped your dreams, and there's nothing I can do to you, huh? (Overdrive gets up and gets right into the camera.) OVERDRIVE: What I did to you to put you in the hospital wasn't nothing. Get ready to find out exactly what I'm capable of, punk. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
