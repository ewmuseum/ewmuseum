CWO REPORT 11/24/96

24 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, November 24, 1996. Our top story is of course the condition of CWO wrestler Sandman. Yesterday on the REPORT, we saw Sandman flatline in his hospital room. After that, there was a total media lock-out, and we don't know whether they were indeed able to resuscitate Sandman or if he... if he passed away. We literally have heard nothing, even from Sandman's best friend, Talon, who is still planning to be at NOVEMBER PAIN tonight. It seems flippant to talk about anything else, but one thing Sandman was... IS... a wrestler first, and everything second. So let's look at the results of a card held last night in St. Petersburg, FL. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 23, 1996 ST. PETERSBURG, FL Sandman Jr. vs. Steel (subbing for Panther) Sandman Jr. pinned Steel after the Permanent Dream flying legdrop in 6:10. Highlanders vs. Unholy Duo (Payne and Hate) Both teams were counted out in 11:35 in a wild brawl of a match. Hellhound vs. Ben Slocum Slocum pinned Hellhound in 15:52 after the Modern Day Bomb (powerbomb from the second rope.) Alliance (Tiger Rogers and David and Goliath) vs. Apocalypse (Bane, Plague and Pestilence) The Alliance defeated the Apocalypse in 8:53 when Goliath made Plague submit to the Goliath Grappler (horizontal backbreaker hold) as Bane and Pestilence brawled with Rogers and Davis outside the ring. Wildfire vs. Wayne Carpenter Carpenter pinned Wildfire after a neckbreaker in 9:34 after outside interference by Ganglord. The Big Cool vs. Biblical Powers Biblical Powers defeated the Big Cool by countout in 11:39 after Moses hit Jumbo Jenkins with a chair. Joe Eagle vs. Akilles Akillies pinned Joe Eagle with an inside cradle in 12:56. Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli Manocelli pinned Alles after the Kiss of Death flying forearm in 15:41. Talon (champion) vs. Panther Panther made Talon submit to the Eclipse (sleeper and Oriental thumb) in 12:42. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses (champions) vs. The Big Cool Pit Bosses defeated the Big Cool in 15:32 when Pit Boss #2 pinned Jumbo Jenkins after the Spike Piledriver. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight is NOVEMBER PAIN, let's look at the lineup one more time for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ EYE ON THE STORM: Bad Guys, Sin, "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. Gods of Thunder, Highlanders Battle Cat, Mondo Gecko, Regulators vs. Ganglord, Sorrow, Eternal Wanderers NOVEMBER PAIN: Sandman Jr. Miguel Sanchez, "Big" Rich Clark, ??? vs. Maximum Overdrive, Baby Waldo, Mercenaries Samoans, Biblical Powers vs. Deadly Sins, Unholy Duo Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli, Joe Eagle, The Big Cool vs. Gold Kard, Brent "The Ace" Alles, Pit Bosses Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, Hoss and Little Joe vs. Steel, Panther, D. E. A. T. H. Inferno, Tiger Rogers, David and Goliath vs. Dark Raven, Hellhound, Creatures of the Night Flyboy, Clark Gray, Hollywood Muscle vs. Wildfire, Akillies, Kamikaze and Mysteria ???, Talon, Flame Spirits vs. Bane, Scourge, Apocalypse Hangman, Rooster, Mad Doctor's Ghost, Lafayette vs. Johnny Reb, Judgment, Southern Pride TEXAS DEATH MATCH (winner gets a shot at the World title on the 12/3/96 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER): Master of Darkness Damion vs. "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum FINAL BATTLE ROYAL FOR WARGAMES WINNERS WORLD SERIES OF WRESTLING (4 out of 7 FALLS), WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: I am now joined, via satellite, by CWO World Champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo. Wayne, I know that you, along with many other CWO wrestlers and officials, are very upset with the whole Sandman situation, I'm wondering if we could start with comments about that. (Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo is shown via satellite.) SCHILLO: I am very upset about what has happened to Sandman, Johnny. For some, wrestling is a way of life, while for others, wrestling is more a hobby, a job, a way to pay the bills. Personally, I very much enjoy wrestling in the CWO. Coming out into the arena to the roar of the fans, the adoration of the women and children - that's why I wrestle. There are much less dangerous jobs that pay better than the CWO, but I wrestle for my fans. I know they're inspired when they see a 5'10", 190 pound professional wrestler take on the toughest guys in the CWO, and win on skill, wit, and sheer determination. I wrestle clean, I wrestle for my fans, and I love my job. But to me, it's still a job.. I go home to my dorm at the end of the day just like anyone else, I go looking for single women, and I put the day's wrestling behind me. STARR: And we certainly see that love of the sport in your performance. What about Sandman? SCHILLO: For men like Sandman, wrestling is not a job, it's an entire way of life. To this man, there is nothing more... the meaning of life is wrestling. To see this poor man be turned on by the Chaotic Dynasty makes be sick. Sandman worked so hard to put himself in a position to win, only to be betrayed. Now, Bane has gone further. He has taken away from Sandman the only thing that matters in his life. Bane, you've really let me down, man. When you did that to Sandman, you didn't just hurt Sandman, you hurt every single man in the CWO. As champion of the CWO, I must be more than just a man hho carries a belt, I must be a leader. As champion, I am sending out a request to each and every member of the CWO, the ToP, GKI, Four Aces, all of you guys out there... go to the hospital, see Sandman, tell him you're pulling for him. Some of us are "good guys," and som of us are "bad guys," but more importantly, we're all MEN. We need to be men of dignity, noble in our actions. Children grow up wishing that they could someday be like us, and whether we like it or not, we need to be role models. STARR: I know that you have a special plea for every CWO fan, every CWO wrestler, every CWO official. What is it? SCHILLO: Go see Sandman. Get your blood tested to see if it matches his blood type. Set aside your differences, and do it out of respect for a fellow CWO member. There will be plenty of time for fighting after Sandman recovers. Let's focus on Bane now. We need to show Sandman that things like this will not be tolerated... not while I'm champ, not if I have anything to say about it. STARR: Have you heard anything about Sandman's condition? I know we saw him flatline yesterday, but we are hoping, despite the media lockout, that the hospital was indeed able to revive him. SCHILLO: I'm on my way to see Sandman now. I don't know if they'll let me in, I hope they do. Bane, if you're man enough, I'll see you someday, ANY DAY, in a steel cage. I'd like to be the first to show you that your behavior will NOT be tolerated in the CWO while I'm champ. I'm sure there will be many more men coming after you after I'm through with you. Watch your back. STARR: All right, Wayne, what about tonight's match against "Master of Pain" Barry Hart? SCHILLO: Here's another example of someone who just doesn't have a clue. He had a lot of fan support, they loved seeing him do those high-flying maneuvers. But then he hangs out with a creep like Gold Kard, and he gets twisted by the lure of money. Well, Barry, you're not going to get this belt if you don't care about it, that's my sincere promise. STARR: What about the presence of Gold Kard at ringside? We know that you've had trouble with him in the past. In fact, he put a $2,000,000 bounty on his head! SCHILLO (chuckling): I'm still standing, Johnny, so I must be making a lot of paupers here in the CWO. That whole bounty thing is a mark of desperation from a desperate man. Kard knows that Hart can't beat me, so he tries to pay some of the low-lifes we have here in the CWO to take me out. It's not going to happen. STARR: All right, thank you very much, Wayne, and good luck tonight at NOVEMBER PAIN. SCHILLO: Thanks, Johnny. STARR: In other news, the wrestler formerly known as Lord Foul the Despiser, now known by his real name, Hile Troy, finally held a press conference. We take you now to that conference. _______________________________________________________________________________ (A room bustling with reporters and illuminated by the flash of bulbs makes the scene. Several reporters can be heard going over questions. CWO Commisioner Randy Jackson comes out.) RANDY JACKSON: Ladies and gentlemen, we thank y'all for comin' to this latest press release from the International Offices of the CWO. Without further adieu, let me bring out the man who you're clamoring to see, the newly named... Hile Troy! (An explosion of flashes go off as Troy enters.) REPORTER #1: Mr. Foul... Lo.. Lord Foul! Why is it tha... HILE TROY: Now, now, now, ask me with the right name in front of your question or it won't get answered! REPORTER #1: Mr. Troy, you just raised my first question, why the name change? HILE TROY: Lord Foul the Despiser was a man, and an entity in the CWO which destroyed dreams, which destroyed the league, and when I step back in through the ropes of the Cyberspace Wrestling Organization, it is not going to be for that reason. I will wrestle first for my personal dignity, and second for the love of the fans... REPORTER #2: Mr. Troy.. why is.. HILE TROY: Thank you for saying it right, next! (Reporters all shout their questions.) HILE TROY: I feel like I'm in a Battle Royal here people! One at a time! Yeah you, the fat bald guy! REPORTER #2: Mr. Troy, what is the current standing as far as your relationship with Sandman is concerned? HILE TROY: I called him up on the phone yesterday, and he wouldn't talk to me, because he was UNCONSCIOUS, try asking me that question when I can give you an answer! REPORTER #3: Hile, will your wrestling style change now that you seemed to have changed your philosophy? HILE TROY: Let's get one thing straight, first off I haven't changed my strategy or my philosophy. You're going to see me wrestle each man differently, if I'm in the ring with, let's say, Keuka Guy, then I'd think he and I would have a good clean wrestling match, if it was Damion, than you should expect me to wrestle the way that I did six months ago when I was dislocating shoulders with a dragon suplex. REPORTER #4: The question that's on my mind is, why are you DUCKING the CWO? You've been sitting over there in England on your duff for a month now, and you claim to be a top CWO superstar? How long of a rest do you need? HILE TROY: You want something to duck? Meet me outside in ten minutes, hot shot! No one in this league ever toured the world like I did. I'd like to see YOU go from Los Angeles to wrestle one guy, then go to London to wrestle another the next day, and then sign autographs in Atlanta, and cut the ribbon on a Pet Store in Grandville the next day, all of this when I don't even have a belt! ANYONE that says any different can step into the RING with Hile Troy, and we'll see how it goes! REPORTER #5: Troy... Bane has made some snide remarks at you as of late, the BIG question is WHEN will we see the match of the year, you and Bane, in ANY kind of match whatsoever, and what kind of match would you like? HILE TROY (slight pause): I hereby announce that the long awaited comeback of Hile Troy will occur whenever Bane wants it to! I am issuing a challenge to you Bane, at the December pay per view if you want, in the street outside this building if you want, the fans want it, the offices will sign it, and after I become the first man to pin you to the ground for three seconds, I'll get PAYED for it! Bane... you haven't a chance... in... hell... VOICE FROM THE BACK: Before that match, dumbkopf, how about me! (Gold Kard appears as the crowd murmurs excitedly. Gold Kard walks up to the podium. Both men stare at each other intensely, Gold Kard with a smirk on his face. Gold Kard grabs the mike.) GOLD KARD: Welcome back, old man! It's appropriate that you now refer to yourself by the name of that ancient city that was destroyed by the Greeks, because, a) you're as old as that city, and b) you will be destroyed just like it! (Randy Jackson pushes himself in between the two men.) JACKSON: We don't want any trouble here. KARD: Shut up, fat dumbkopf! Anyway, you can make all the overtures to Bane that you want, we all know that the one man you are truly afraid of in this league is... ME! (Troy tries to get at Kard, again, Jackson keeps them separated.) KARD: I will make this brief, as I know it's time for your nap soon. I have recently acquired enough stock in this organization to take control! (The crowd gasps.) KARD: If I wanted to, I could fire all of you right now and run this league the way I want, making it the CM2WF. However... however, I am, as always a sporting man. So, what I propose is this: at CWO CELEBRATION, the "1 year anniversary" of this pathetic league, one last time, you and me. How ironic, that the 1 year anniversary shall ultimately prove to be the end of the league as we know it as well. You claim to be a standard-bearer, you've got one chance to save your pathetic league from my, and Gold Kard International's, control. What do you say, old man, or do you still fear me? TROY (hesitating, then): One more time? One more time to kick your sorry German ass? You've got it! I am sick and tired of this, and let me tell you, not only will I "save" the CWO, but I am going to destroy you! KARD (bemused): We shall see. Until December 15, then, old man, you'd better be ready for the match of your life, or the CWO comes crashing down around you. Oh, and Jackson, don't get too comfortable. You'll be the first one to be tossed out on your fat ass, dumbkopf, when GKI, and the CM2WF, takes over. (Kard leaves as Jackson and Troy discuss matters with each other.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: All right, you just heard it, Gold Kard and Hile Troy will happen at CWO CELEBRATION on December 15, 1996. The thing Hile Troy will be fighting for, the CWO itself. But before that, tonight, we have NOVEMBER PAIN, and we'll see you there! This is Johnny Starr for the CWO REPORT, saying see you tonight!
