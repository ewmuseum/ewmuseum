FEVER 1/25/99 - Results

February 2, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FEVER 1/25/99 Results Atlanta, GA (Georgia Dome) Show opens with a press conference with Chris Greco, Steve Keller, and Tommy
Lightning, as Greco of course denies that he is part of the Dark Side and claims the tape we saw last week was false.
President Gordy Alles, Vice President Kent Alles, and Commissioner Tom
Krepps came out. They called out Steve Keller and Tommy Lightning, who reluctantly obliged. Gordy Alles then welcomed the two men to the Dark
Side! The shocked men were then shown a contract that was apparently signed by Chris Greco. (Greco was the behind-the-scenes manager of the two men.)
Gordy Alles then said that on behalf of Darkness he wanted the two men to take care of a nuisance known as the Outlaw Riders, namely Ranger Taurus and
Snake Martin. Alles threatened the two Aces with firing if they didn’t do as he said.
Chris Greco was confronted by the other two Aces, who were of course very angry with him even though he once again claimed innocence. Greco’s friends left him in a state of distress.
Unit came out to confront Diamondback Chris Myers. Again, one of the CWO’s stalwart heroes had to defend himself against the accusations. And again,
Myers left his former friends in a state of unease.
MATCH ONE: Warhawk defeated Man Mountain Marko by disqualification. Time of match: 7:55. After the match, Warhawk was attacked by the Unit! The
Fallen, Wishbone, and the Creatures of Darkness came down to make the save. comes down to join the fray! MATCH TWO: Flash Varga pinned 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin after the Flash
Flood. Time of match: 7:22.
After the match, Lester Sheets and the Foundation came down to talk to
Hakujin. What they talked about was unheard, but certainly it’s nothing good for the rest of the CWO.
A slightly distraught George Washington spoke in a short interview. MATCH THREE: Deadly Sins defeated Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky and George Washington when Pride pinned Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster'
Bondavarsky after the Repention. Time of match: 9:58. MATCH FOUR (CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION TITLE MATCH): Diamondback Chris
Myers pinned 'Double D' Damian Dagger after the Rattler. Time of match:
3:16. Diamondback Chris Myers retained the CWO North American Champion title. After the match, Myers had an interview, again denying the charges put forth against him. Dallas came down for the interview, but was strangely absent during his match. When Myers looked to Dallas for support, she slapped him!
She then accused him of having an affair with Sindy Sinn, and said she had the pictures to prove it! Dallas left Myers in the ring, and then Sindy
Sinn came down to rub up against a still protesting Myers. Hour #1 closed with an increasingly bewildered and frustrated Myers going after Dallas.
HOUR #2: The “renegades” Southern Dynamite and “Mr. Technical” Cooper Young had a short segment. Outlaw Riders came out to basically talk trash about the Dark Side. However, Darkness ended up turning the tables on them by saying that one of the Riders had already turned traitor! A paranoid Mondo Gecko then questioned all of the Riders, trying to find out who the traitor was, if there is one at all.
Vladimir Popalov had a short segment, threatening Carter. Before his match began, “The Avenger” Jesse Ewiak challenged the Dark Side as well. MATCH FIVE: Allen “Mr. America” Carter defeated “The Avenger” Jesse Ewiak by disqualification in 8:05 when Vladimir Popalov attacked Carter. Popalov then proceeded to deliver the Russian Sickle to everybody that got in the ring. Bad Company leader Col. Taggart (the group that Popalov was supposedly a part of) came down to calm him down, and ended up getting a
Sickle to the back of the head for his efforts! Popalov stormed off, but not before being stopped by Lester Sheets, who, just like earlier with
Hakujin, claimed he had something he wanted to “discuss.” The Foundation and Popalov left together.
A promo for newcomer Simon St. Severe was shown. A typically arrogant
Television Champion Cris Marcus claimed not to be worried.
MATCH SIX: Simon St. Severe defeated Cris Marcus in a non-title match in
3:55 after the Cross Breaker. Severe then demanded a title shot, and will apparently get it at AFTER DARK.
MATCH SEVEN: Ranger Taurus and Snake Martin defeated Steve Keller and Tommy
Lightning after Martin pinned Keller after the Lap Dance in 4:39.
MATCH EIGHT: Wildfire and Johnny Reb wrestled to a double disqualification in 6:24. When we came back from break, Wildfire and Reb were still fighting, as Reb threw Wildfire into a stack of chairs before security broke the fight up.
MATCH NINE: Lord Hile Troy defeated Wishbone by disqualification in 6:22 when the other Knights of Darkness attacked. All seemed lost, but then Troy was saved by none other than Gold Kard! The two CWO stars shook hands after they cleared the Dark Side out, although Troy said to Gold Kard that if it was a trick, he would kill him. Gold Kard said, “Fair enough,” and the show ended on that high note.
