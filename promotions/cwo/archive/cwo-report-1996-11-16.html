CWO REPORT 11/16/96

16 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, November 16, 1996. Great CWO action last night in the mountains of Denver, Colorado, let's look at how it turned out. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 1996 DENVER, CO Johnny Reb (w/General Lee) vs. Flyboy Reb pinned Flyboy with a German suplex in 7:10. Vince Manocelli (w/Russell Hill) vs. Rooster Rooster made Manocelli submit to the Roosterlock (figure four) in 6:09. After the match Rooster was attacked by Flyboy. Inferno (w/Jimbo Jones) vs. HellHound (w/Master of Darkness Damion Inferno pinned Hellhound after a legdrop in 11:03. Hangman (w/"Notorious" Nicole Lane) vs. Master of Darkness Damion Hangman pinned Master of Darkness Damion after the Skullcrusher (Shooting Star Press with a brick in his hand) in 17:27. After the match, Hangman continued to deliver Skullcrushers until the Dark Side ran him off. TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS: Gods of Thunder (AC and DC) vs. Bad Guys (Ron "Rude Guy" Johnson and Joe "Mean Guy" Rucker, w/Herbie Versmells) Bad Guys defeated Gods of Thunder, 2 falls to 1. The Bad Guys won the first fall in 14:11. The Gods of Thunder won the second in 20:07. The Bad Guys won the deciding fall in 29:33. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart vs. Sandman Jr. Hart pinned Sandman Jr. in 15:52 after a flying legdrop, pulling the tights. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jim "Jazzman" Taylor, w/Russell Hill) vs. Pit Bosses (champions) Pit Bosses defeated the Big Cool by disqualification in 20:28 when Joe Eagle and Vincent Manocelli attacked. This brought down the rest of GKI and the Tower of Power, and the two teams brawled all over the building. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Joe Eagle (w/Russell Hill) vs. Gold Kard (champion, w/German Choklat) Gold Kard pinned Joe Eagle with Die Flieder Kross in 13:27. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night (Night Crawler and Night Slinger, w/MOD Damion) vs. D.E.A.T.H. (Benton and Green, w/Da Boss) Creatures of the Night defeated D. E. A. T. H. when Night Crawler pinned Benton in 14:51. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we will be in Lincoln, NE, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 1996 LINCOLN, NE Wayne Carpenter vs. Tiger Rogers (w/Jimbo Jones) Sandman Jr. (w/Kellen) vs. Hellhound (w/MOD Damion) Rooster vs. Johnny Reb (w/General Lee) MOD Damion vs. Sandman "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum (w/Sondra) vs. Dark Raven (w/MOD Damion) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Brent "The Ace" Alles (champion, w/Gold Kard vs. Joe Eagle (w/Russell Hill) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Gold Kard (champion, w/German Choklat) vs. Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses (champions) vs. The Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jim "Jazzman" Taylor, w/Russell Hill) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (steel cage): The Mercenaries (Kaptain Krunch and Korperal Punishment, w/Maximum Overdrive) vs. Creatures of the Night (Night Crawler and Night Slinger, w/MOD Damion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: This week at the House Shows, CWO World Champion Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo was put to an extreme test by the 7'4", 481 pound "Big" Rich Clark. In two hard-fought matches, Schillo was barely able to prevail in each one. Schillo had favorable comments for his opponent, though, we take you to them now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo is in the interview area.) WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: You know, Big Rich, you fought a great match. I don't think I've ever been drawn out to 15 minutes against anyone! You've really earned my repect, man. I may have beaten you twice this week, but they were close, hard fought, and clean matches. It really takes a true man to try to win the title fairly. Too often, people around here try to cheat to win. It's nice to see someone who still can bring dignity to the CWO. If you're ever in a bind, Big Rich, let me know. You're a great wrestler. Schillomaniacs, let Big Rich be an example to you. He did his homework, loves the ladies, and always beats the bad guys. I don't know who my next challenger will be, but whoever it is, I feel I'm ready for anything now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As we have heard by now, on next week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, the main event will be a triangle match in a cage, with the Television Championship at stake. New champion Miguel Sanchez will be going up not only against the former champion Brent "The Ace" Alles, but also Johnny Reb, a man who feels he should rightfully be the Television Champion. We take you now to a music video of Reb. _______________________________________________________________________________ ("Sweet Home Alabama" by Lynyrd Skynyrd plays in the background as various images and voice overs flash on the screen.) (Johnny Reb vs. MOD from TNF:) KYLE MELINN: Johnny Reb signaling for the Texas Cloverleaf... he's got it on! Right in the middle of the ring, MOD's got nowhere to go, he could submit... MOD gives it up! (Southern Pride vs. Sorrow, Payne, and Hate from TNF:) KYLE MELINN: Reb with a DDT, he's going for the Texas Cloverleaf, he's got it cinched in! Sorrow gives it up! (Johnny Reb vs. Hellhound from Showcase:) KEVIN RINALDI: Hellhound is down, Reb's got the patented Texas Cloverleaf applied... forget about it! (Johnny Reb vs. Sandman from TNF:) RANDY JOHNSON: Here we go! DAVE THOMAS: It's over! KYLE MELLIN: Reb with the Texas Cloverleaf applied! Sandman has nowhere to go, this is it.... (A black screen with the words "Coming Soon" written is shown.) TONY THE K VOICE-OVER: The winner of the match, and NEW CWO TELEVISION CHAMPION.... (Fade to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Will Johnny Reb be able to beat two of the most dangerous men in the sport, inside a cage, to fulfill his dream? We'll have to wait until next week's FEVER to find out. That's it for the REPORT today, we'll see you tomorrow.
