CWO REPORT 3/27/97

27 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Show opens on the Deadly Sins in the interview area.) PRIDE: We lost the 6-man match... dammit... if Lust was still around, we woulda won.... SLOTH: Forget Lust. Forget that stupid tournament. It's time for the Deadly Sins, Pride and Sloth, to start doing what we do best: kicking asses and winning tag team titles! WRATH: I will win the tournament. I will crush Masher Macabee like a bad matzah ball... I will win it, then it's on to titles! The Deadly Sins rule EVERYTHING! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, March 27, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the MARCH MADNESS tournament continued with the second round for the UP AND LEFT regionals. Let's look at what happened! _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT UP AND RIGHT REGIONALS - ROUND 2 AUGUSTA, ME - 3/26/97 Masher Maccabee pinned Wrath after the Jerusalem Jacknife followed by a Homentaschen Hangover. Time of match: 13:03. Arlon Flaym and CWO North American champion Goliath wrestled to a 30 minute time limit draw. Johnny Reb defeated Vlad the Impaler by disqualification when the Unholy Alliance attacked. Time of match: 6:30. Exile pinned Moses after the small package and outside interference by Gold Kard. Time of match: 14:51. 'Monster' Rich Clark pinned Kurt 'Thrill from the Chill' Gerstung after the Choke Slam. Time of match: 5:47. Night Slinger pinned Panther after the elbow drop and outside interference by CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Tiger Rogers. Time of match: 12:59. The Focus pinned one of the CWO North American Tag Team Champions Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson after the small package. Time of match: 12:34. Gold Kard pinned 'Lonestar' Mike Williams after the reversed inside cradle. Time of match: 9:53. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) DOWN/LEFT WINNER__________ _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Phoenix, AZ, for the Down and Left Regionals 2nd round, let's look at the lineup! _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT DOWN AND LEFT REGIONALS - ROUND 2 PHOENIX, AZ 3/27/97 CWO Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers vs. Pit Boss #1 Inferno vs. David "The Destroyer" Davis Talon vs. Jason "Flash" Varga Hellhound vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams Wayne Schillo vs. Chris Greco Conor McCloud vs. Brad "The Man" Couchman Clark Gray vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Joe Eagle vs. CWO World Champion Bane _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now take you to comments from CWO superstars. The man formerly known as Battlecat has made a change, and now he's making another change, losing weight to become a Light Heavyweight. We take you to comments from this Wild Bunch member. _______________________________________________________________________________ (El Grande Pistolerro is standing outside of Alcatraz looking at the main building with Tamara Wilcox. His eyes gaze up to the prison cells where such notorious criminals such as Machine Gun Kelly, Al Capone, and the Bridman have stayed.) EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: "Lonestar" Williams, you are an admirable opponent and I have the utmost respect for you. But I said it before, I am here in the CWO to represent the AAA, CMLL, and other Mexican federations. My goal is to become the Light Heavyweight champion and I will do everything it takes to do it. I am the ultimate daredevil in the CWO and I will show the great fans of the CWO that I am the real deal high risk taker. Tiger Rogers, you and your creep manager have stuck your nose in other people's business for too long. I have promised my people and my God that I will win the Light Heavyweight title and bring it home to the CWO fans. "Lonestar", with our match at the SHOWCASE SPECIAL for APRIL ARMAGEDDON, I will shake your hand before and after the match but my friend, get ready to get shot down for Grande Pistolerro is ready to make his mark. VIVA LA WILD BUNCH! TAMARA WILCOX: Over the past year I have seen this talented wrestler not get the breaks that superstars have gotten. I signed with him to help him win the CWO Light Heavyweight title and gain respect in the wrestling world. Jimbo Jones, if you try to interfere I will make you pay! EGP, we have a lot of work to do, baby! _______________________________________________________________________________ ("Monster" Rich Clark is in a gym, working out by himself. his body bruised and scarred, his bones mending, his skin stitched back together. He is not looking very happy. His head shaven bald, his body is covered with more and more tattoos everytime he is seen. He is doing the benchpress, and about 500 pounds is on the bar. He seems to be lifting it with ease. We hear his voice, counting the reps, "... 48... 49... 50!" He puts the bar down, sits up, wipes the sweat from his brow, and begins to speak.) "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: BANE! You cost me the CWO North American Title, and my self proclaimed Monster of the Ring title. For that, you will pay. April 6th, it's just you and me. CWO World Heavyweight Title on the line, and there can be only ONE winner. That man WILL be me. I can guarantee that. You wanna know why? Well, that's a secret, known to only me, and the other man involved. I can tell you this, though, it's the LAST thing that you'd expect. APRIL ARMAGGEDON will be your judgement day. If you're ready, then so am I. I only hope that you felt good when I broke your Merciful Death last night. Just tell me one thing. How did it feel to be overpowered? I'm just asking because it's never, EVER happened to me. I hope that you keep that belt in good condition, because I want it to look real good around my waist. Have fun being champion, Bane, 'cause your days are numbered. (He gets up and walks towards the free weights. He picks up a bar that has about 250 pounds on it with one hand, lifts it up, over his head, and throws it through a window. Fade to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: The man who was originally supposed to take on Bane at APRIL ARMAGEDDON, Moses, as a result of winning that Last Man Standing match at SPRING BREAK '97, was very distraught after losing the title shot to Clark on this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. Let's hear from Moses. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Savior is sitting in the locker room after last night's FEVER match between Moses and "Monster" Rich Clark. Moses comes out, looking incredibly pissed off, and begins to yell at everyone in sight, including Savior.) MOSES: WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT?!? I BEAT THREE OTHER GUYS, FAIR AND SQUARE AT SPRING BREAK, AND I GET THE SHAFT ONCE AGAIN WHEN IT COMES TO A TITLE SHOT! "MONSTER" RICH CLARK, YOU BETTER BE READY FOR ME, BECAUSE I WANT A RE-MATCH. I DON'T CARE IF YOU'RE WILLING TO PUT THE TITLE SHOT ON THE LINE OR NOT, I WANT YOU IN THE RING, AND I WANT IT NOW! AS WELL AS THAT, I'D LIKE TO, IF POSSIBLE, ENTER MY NAME AMONGST THE NAMES OF THE SUPERSTARS COMPETING IN THE ROCK AT APRIL ARMAGGEDON. THAT'S RIGHT, I'M NOW ELIGIBLE FOR THAT COMPETITION. NOW, ONE LAST THING, GOLD KARD, I HAD TO CARRY YOUR SORRY BUTT THROUGH THE LAST MAN STANDING MATCH AT SPRING BREAK, AND YOU HAVE THE NERVE TO INSULT ME! YOU'RE NEXT, NAZI. AFTER RICH, I WANT YOU IN THE RING. I'VE BEATEN YOU ONCE, AND I'LL DO IT AGAIN. SAVIOR: Moses, calm down, my brother. You have to maintain a cool head if you want to win. Keep calm, and don't allow your emotions to overcome you. After I left you at Spring Break, I thought that I'd never come back, but your performance in that Last Man Standing match was one of the most amazing wrestling matches that I've ever seen. Now as I've taught you, focus your energy into not throwing tirades, but trying to become more skilled as a wrestler. All that you have to do is to make sure that things don't get too hectic, and that you can take on al of the challenges that you have made. MOSES (calming down): Yes, you are right, my brother. I'm sure that there are better things that I can be doing with myself, rather than yelling and screaming about the injustices in this world. Well, I think that we better be off, and back to the basics once again. SAVIOR: Yes, that's more like it. It's time to train like you never have before, for you don't know what the future holds. Only time will tell. (They both grabs their stuff and get out of there. Fade to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Moses announcing his eligibility for THE ROCK at next week's APRIL ARMAGEDDON, and in fact he has been added to the list of competitors for that great match, more on that later. Now, though, let's hear from a team that will be taking on the Highlanders and the Eternal Wanderers in a triangle match at APRIL ARMAGEDDON, the Masters of Hardcore. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Masters of Hardcore sit, laughing about recent developments in this league. Laughing about their recent attack on The Eternal Wanderers, lauging about the reaction of the announcers after The Masters debuted their finisher, the Hardcore Bomb at Spring Break. Laughing about the pathetic state of the tag team division here in the CWO.) BRAD "THE MAN" COUCHMAN (laughing): How'd ya like that, Wanderers? Did that feel good, getting powerbombed and legdroped through a table at the same time? Did you enjoy it? I hope so, 'cause we really enjoyed putting you through it. You see, you may be associated with the Hearttakers, a team that we BEAT twice in the UXW, if I remember correctly. Isn't that right, Chris? "MR. EXTREME" CHRIS OHLHOFF: Now, we just proved that we are the men to beat here. We may not have the Tag Team titles, but it's only a matter of time. Once we prove ourselves against team after team, inmproving our records to a great height, the number one ranked team, we want The Pit Bosses, the Regulators, or whomever else has those tag team titles in the ring. Those belts will be ours. It's inevitable. We have been THE premiere force in tag team wrestling in the WORLD for the past six months, and the 15 Tag Team belts that we've held in that time are our proof. Right now, our energy is focused on the CWO, but soon enough, we'll have those belts and be ready to move on. Maybe. We might even choose to stick around for a while. Hey, you never know? COUCHMAN: That's right. Sometimes we grow bored of a federation after we conquer it. That's what's happened in other feds because we do it so bloody fast there's no challenge in it for us anymore. I'm hoping that we need to work our way to the top. Hey, we can dream, can't we? OHLHOFF: See, we know how to win, because it's all that we do. We're three and one so far, and only getting better. C'mon, bring it on, boys, we're ready for anything. (They walk out. Fade to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: This weekend on SHOWCASE, CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Tiger Rogers will defend that title against the man who won the shot at it in a triangle match at CWO SPRING BREAK '97, Sandman Jr. Let's hear from him now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area with Kellen.) SANDMAN JR.: YOOOOOOOOOOO! Did you all like the way I wrecked everyone in my little Triangle Match? Those 2 guys are great, GREAT competitors, but I NEED this belt, and I said, and I've shown, I'll do ANYTHING to get it! Tiger Rogers, you're good, REAL good, but I am the man... I am the master, and ruler of the CWO... and I am going to whip your ass! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire, with the lovely Miss Amber, is in the locker room after the match on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER.) WILDFIRE: Bad Guys, you think you're gonna intimidate us?!? You think you're gonna scare us?!? You think you're gonna beat us... (Bloodbath bursts throught the brick wall of the locker room.) WILDFIRE: Well think again! You stuck your noses in our business on Tuesday Night Fever! You even had to bring chairs, well you'll find out soon enough that me and Bloodbath aren't afraid to use weapons too! BLOODBATH (dark mysterious voice): You will rest in HELL and we will be champions! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Finally today, some words from Gold Kard. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Gold Kard is in the interview area, chuckling evilly.) GOLD KARD: Moses, Moses, Moses... you are not a student of history such as myself, especially not of this league. Nazi? You associate me with that disgrace from my people's past? It is true that in my momentary madness, I thought myself a World War II German general, known as Der Iron Kross, but now I am totally cured of that megalomania, just ask Dr. Sigmund Jung, my personal psychiatric advisor. Nazi? Nazi? (spits) HOW DARE YOU? I am greater in battle than all der sums of der 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Reichs combined! Where der Nazis failed in warfare, I will succeed! My Blitzkrieg upon this organization will leave nothing in its path! I am der master wrestler, genetically designed to be der best! And I have conquered all! Almost, that is. And while my next conquest is der dumbkopf redneck Johnny Reb, don't think that you are leaving my mind for one moment, Jew. You are inferior to me, not because of your race, though that is a part of it. You are inferior to me because I am der master wrestler, and I am der CWO! That is why I cost you your chance at being der MARCH MADNESS champion, and I will cost you much more in der months to come. You can depend on it, JEW. (Gold Kard laughs maniacally as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Again, we have to apologize for the anti-Semitic and racist statements uttered by Gold Kard, they are definitely not the views and opinions of the CWO. However, to end on a brighter note, the complete lineup for the upcoming APRIL ARMAGEDDON PPV can now be found on the CWO home page, the address can be seen in the closing credits. Until tomorrow, then, this is Johnny Starr saying so long for now!
