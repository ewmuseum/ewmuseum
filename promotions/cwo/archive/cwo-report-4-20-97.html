CWO REPORT 4/20/97

20 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Jimbo Jones is standing in a back CWO Locker room with Inferno and David Davis. Jones is holding 3 of the 4 CWO Titles around his shoulders.) JIMBO JONES: Baby baby baby... I've said it once, I'll say it a thousand times... the master of managing, Jimbo Jones, has proved that the Alliance IS the stable to beat here in the CWO! Right now I am holding 3 of the 4 singles belts in the CWO! Inferno, baby, ya did it! TV champion, and it's staying that way. The troops are ready for the Masher & Co. The Alliance is the stable to beat. And we're merely one all important match away from having the Monopoly baby! We'll have 'em all after the triangle match! And my trainee, "The Destroyer" David here is ready to take on all comers who want a shot at ANY of the Alliance boys. That's right, you want a piece of us, you gotta go through the Destroyer first! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, April 20, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO wrapped up its short stay in the north with a show in Anchorage, AK, let's see what went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/19/97 Anchorage, AK Joe Eagle pinned Hellhound after the elbow smash and outside interference by 'Master of Darkness' Damion. Time of match: 9:44. Bloodbath pinned Benjamin Blake Bentley after the Tombstone Powerbomb. Time of match: 5:41. Kurt 'Thrill from the Chill' Gerstung and Mondo Gecko wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Johnny Reb made Gold Kard submit to the Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 10:03. Creatures of the Night defeated Dark Raven/Hellhound (substituting for the injured Bishop) when Night Slinger pinned Hellhound after the Lights Out (avalanche). Time of match: 15:40. 'Lonestar' Mike Williams pinned Light Heavyweight Champion Tiger Rogers in a non-title match after the Lonestar Press. Time of match: 17:18. Highlanders defeated World Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses in a non-title match when Conor McCloud pinned Pit Boss #2 after the Quickening (superplex by Gavin, flying splash by Conor). Time of match: 7:18. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION TITLE MATCH (STEEL CAGE): Jason 'Flash' Varga escaped the cage after nailing Goliath with the Flash Flood. Time of match: 11:45. Jason 'Flash' Varga won the CWO North American Champion title. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see from the main event of last night, we have a new North American champion, Jason "Flash" Varga. However, the Alliance have vowed revenge for this upset, and while Goliath is still locked into that three-man situation for the World title at MAY-HEM, it will now be CWO Light Heavyweight Champion Tiger Rogers against the new North American champion Varga at MAY-HEM, and since Varga is a light heavyweight himself, both titles will be on the line! More on that later in the first ever MAY-HEM CONTROL CENTER, but for now, let's get comments from two new CWO superstars, Brother Justice, who have been having problems with Da Hood Rats as of late. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Jerome and Jermain Justice are both wearing black Armani suits and Killer Loop Sun Glasses.) JEROME JUSTICE: Hood Rats, what is wrong wit' you SUCKAS! Do ya punks have a death wish? The last thang ya wanna do is interfere with Justice. We came here to control punks like you. Now it seems dat we need to make an example outta ya! Rats, ya like breaking da rules, how bout dis, let's have a fight were there ain't no rules. JERMAIN JUSTICE: You heard him, RATS! You think ya so bad, let's see whatcha got. I thinks it's time that everybody here in the CWO see what kindo of ghetto slum you really are. I can see ya now out there, talking big wit' your boys, but when the time comes dat you gotta face Justice, stand warned, JUSTICE WILL BE SERVED. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr, as MAY-HEM graphic comes up.) JOHNNY STARR: All right, we are now ready for our first ever MAY-HEM CONTROL CENTER, as the big event is only two weeks away, May 4, from the Molson Center in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Of course everybody's mind is on the 60-man two ring battle royal, but even eclipsing that is the situation involving the World title, with three of the "monsters of the ring" going at each other. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (TWO MEN START IN A CAGE, ESCAPEE HAS TO FIGHT THE OTHER MAN IN THE OTHER RING): Bane vs. "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Goliath STARR: Two of those three men will start out in a steel cage surrounding one of the two rings, the object will be to reduce the other to a state where he is able to be handcuffed to the ring, so that the other can make his escape. However, he then must contend with the other man not in the cage, and the winner of those two men will be the new CWO World Champion. Confusing? A little, but that's MAY-HEM for you. Also on the card will be an explosive match involving the World Tag Team Titles. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Highlanders vs. Pit Bosses (champions) STARR: We've seen a little bit of friction between the Pit Bosses over the past few weeks, hopefully they will be able to get it together to take on the current #1 contender, the Highlanders. Also, just announced earlier on the REPORT, we now will have a title vs. title situation with both the North American and Light Heavyweight championships on the line. TITLE VS. TITLE NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP AND LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Light Heavyweight Champion Tiger Rogers (w/Jimbo Jones) vs. North American Champion Jason "Flash" Varga (w/Jim Peer) STARR: The Alliance is going to be looking for revenge since Varga defeated Alliance member Goliath last night for the title, and they're looking for Tiger Rogers to get the job done. For the North American Tag Team Championships, the Bad Guys will try to continue their impressive yet dubious reign as champions, going up against the Mercenaries. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIPS: Mercenaries (w/Maximum Overdrive) vs. Bad Guys (w/Herbie Versmells) (champions) STARR: Finally, the Television championship is on the line at MAY-HEM at well. The current titleholder, Inferno, will be going up against the man who earned a shot at that title by defeating Mondo Gecko a few weeks back on FEVER, Johnny Reb. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb (w/General Lee) vs. Inferno (w/Jimbo Jones) STARR: All of that, though, pales in comparison to the 60-man, two-ring battle royal that is known at MAY-HEM. Two men start out in each ring, one man comes down to each ring every two minutes after that. (Footage is shown from last year's event.) STARR VOICE-OVER: The winner of MAY-HEM of course goes on to SUMMER HEAT to get a shot at the World Champion. Last year's MAY-HEM was an exciting event, and wrestling legend Sabin Rene Figaro was last year's winner, and he of course went on to win the title at SUMMER HEAT. (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Every superstar in the CWO not already mentioned in one of the above matches will be involved in this great event, along with a few surprises. Super Japan star Matsuhiro Torunaka has been invited to participate, and the CWO has stated that several other "wild cards" are possible as well. Also, the EYE ON THE STORM broadcast will feature a lottery tag match, where the losers will be the first ones in the ring, and the winners will be the last to come down to the ring! EYE ON THE STORM: LOTTERY TAG MATCH (winners come down last, losers in the ring first) STARR: You can't afford to miss this great PPV, believe me, MAY-HEM is one of the most dangerous matches the CWO has to offer, and it's going to be wild! Add that into the three-man World title situation, the title vs. title match between Rogers and Varga, the tag team championship matches, and the Television championship match, MAY-HEM is going to be great! That's about all the time we have for today, but before we sign off, we remind you to watch the great CWO SUNDAY SHOWCASE, coming out later today, featuring a match between El Grande Pistolerro and Ganglord. Let's hear from Pistolerro before we leave you today. _______________________________________________________________________________ (El Grande Pistolerro is in the interview area.) EL GRANDE PISTOLERRO: Ganglord, you are a perfect example of what is wrong with the CWO today. It's people like you that destroy the talent rich federation and all that the good wrestlers try to achieve. Ganglord, I came here to America to escape drug dealing scum like yourself who run my country. Now I see the same thing that happened in my country happen here because of people like you. Ganglord, you are nothing but an oversized thug who is going to get his butt kicked by me. It's time for the CWO to have their own International superstar. WCW has Rey Misterio, WWF has Mil Mascaras, ECW has Sabu, and the CWO will have myself. Ganglord, I am on a quest to become the newest Light Heavyweight champion and you are in my way, bastardo. Get ready to go to hell, estubido gringo, because the "Bandit" is coming and I am going to shoot you down and move on to become the new Light Heavyweight champion soon! VIVA LA WILD BUNCH! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Closing creds and music comes up as we fade out.)
