Year-End Awards _____________________________________________________________________ Behind the Scenes by Lance Russell Hey there fans and once again you're reading Behind the Scenes from yours truly, Lance Russell. Folks, this has been a trying time for me with the apparent "truth" of Chris Myers and his baptism into the Dark Side.
However, what scares me is the alliance of Darkness and CWO President Gordy Alles. Because if memory serves me correct, there is a lot of bad blood between the trio and if we are wrong... they will have screwed Myers..... FOREVER!
The last time the group was seen as a team, Maximum Overdrive, Gordy Alles and Diamondback Myers were in the TEAM CHALLENGE SERIES! Myers ultimately cost
Overdrive a chance at getting rid of the Unit when he refused to smash
Howard Newton with a chair. Overdrive and Alles ended up leaving the CWO for a long period of time and Myers became the most popular star in the CWO's short
History. Sources close to me have said that Maximum Overdrive has never gotten over that and to this day is still irate about what has happened. So as
Southern Dynamite and others have informed me, a cold wet night at a bar,
Krepps and the Alles family with Maximum Overdrive, the birth of DARKNESS!
It was there that the brain child of Alles was born and Overdrive was given an army of monsters and assassins to run roughshod over the CWO.
So when you go back to Myers and see how his entire life in the CWO has been turned upside down... you got to wonder how the Dark Side got involved. For now with the recent announcement at CWO AFTER DARK, Myers is a marked man.
Fans that once cheered him and reveled in his accomplishments are now turned off and disgusted with him. Myers has become a lone wolf and disgusted with the CWO, the fans and his friends... everything that he has treasured in the past. If I didn't know better, I would swear that Darkness has accomplished his personal goal in teaching Myers a lesson and destroying all that he holds dear to him!
____________________________________________________________________ Rookie Watch by Zorro 2000 Hola mi amigos, it is me, Senor Zorro 2000 with the Rookie Watch as we take another look at a rookie. This New Year is going to be a kick as everybody says and I cannot wait to see these young men come into their own. I gotta hand it to the Outlaw Riders but they got themselves a perfect enforcer in newcomer Snake Martin. Gecko, sensing that he was the target of a lynch mob decided to make the Outlaws bigger and therefore used some opening in the group to get this huge mammoth of a man to fill the bill! And what is scary about this guy is that he could be a huge force in the CWO during the year of 1999!
Picture this: a 6'5", 315 pound man who can fly from the top rope, very agile and a former bouncer. And this guy has lots of guys around him to help him out. The first thing that has been done by the Outlaws is that he has been sparring with Ranger Taurus and Hollywood Haskins in an effort to improve his power game and wrestling skills. Now comes the scary part and that is how this man will do in the CWO this year... very, very good! The
Outlaws are in the race to dominate the CWO but with all the depth they have and with all the skills of Martin, he can just build his repertoire and confidence in tag matches while getting his dues. But make no mistake, 1999 is going to be a big year for the big man called SNAKE!
_____________________________________________________________________ On the Mark by T.J. Marks Hey everybody it is T.J. Marks with another addition of "On The Mark" and the topic on hand is the upcoming pay per view Main Event as Col. Taggart challenges Chris Myers for the World Title. What was once considered a friendly encounter and possibly a dream match has now turned into a battle and struggle for the freedom of the CWO. Now that Chris Myers has apparently joined the Dark Side, Col. Taggart once again finds himself facing a former friend who is now bent on destroying the CWO tradition.
Talking to Col. Taggart, this past year had been the most frustrating in a sense as one by one former close friends of his have been turned against him.
First Ranger Taurus left the CPE and formed the Outlaw Riders, then Mondo
Gecko shocked the world and turned his back on Taggart and even injured his son. But this has taken the cake as Myers has seemingly thrown away the CWO tradition and all of Taggart's tutoring. The Dark Side also represents everything that Col. Taggart despises and has fought against. So for now,
Col.
Taggart has been busy in his "dungeon," training for a total war against- all-odds situation. Looking at this matchup, there are so many questions that I hope get answered. Neither of these two have ever met one on one or in tag action yet they have been extremely dominating. Col. Taggart has held the CWO TV
Championship and three times was a 6-man Tag champion. He has been the best wrestler in terms of record and perhaps is a great submission and mat wrestler. Diamondback Myers is perhaps the best fighter in the game with his Rattler and skills. Myers has beaten almost everyone alive... except
Col. Taggart and that has got to be on his mind. The only question now is can Taggart get the Annihilator on and stop the Dark Side... or will Myers retain his title and carry out the Dark Side plans.

STRENGTHS 1) Rattler: Probably the most devastating finisher in the game, he has become only the Second "Triple Crown" winner. That sideways DDT has beaten everyone from the likes of Foolkiller to even arch rival Brent "The Ace" Alles.
2) Heart: This kid has been through hell and back and even thrown off a 30 foot high steel cage by Alles. But he never gives up and has come back time and time again even though all else around him fails.
3) Experience: He has been in more pressure matches than anyone else and has been on the spot almost every time. Myers knows how to get ready and prepared for the big matches and usually is tougher in those matches.
WEAKNESSES 1) Injuries: In the past year Myers has been Thunderfunked on to a steel chair, thrown off a steel cage and beaten to almost near death. His body has been worked over where he is almost an injury or two away from paralyzation!
2) Emotional: Because of his deep hatred for Brent Alles, Myers not only injured himself but drove friends away with his sullen attitude during that time. He gets sidetracked easily when either friends or his pride is insulted or hurt and now that his friends distrust him, he is almost an emotional wreck.
3) Close Enemies: Ex-Friends of Myers are almost an arm long now a days from Mark Fulcrum to Mondo Gecko to Warhawk and even Col. Taggart. No doubt these men have told everyone else around the locker room how Myers acts, wrestles and thinks.
OUTLOOK: The betrayal of Myers got him a world title but also cost him his fans and all his friends. With the Dark Side now behind him, Myers could quite possibly dominate the federation and destroy all the records... but at what price?!?
* NEWTON BROS. Combined weight: 519 pounds Tag Rank: North American Champs Title History: World Tag Champs, 3x North American Champs (current) STRENGTHS: 1) Technical skills: These two young men posses incredible suplexes and a vast knowledge of wrestling holds and moves that puts the rest of the league to shame. 2) Speed: They are always a step or two ahead of their opponents and what makes the Powerline and Newton Bomb so devastating is that they executing with tremendous explosiveness from their speed.
3) Johnny B. Goode: Perhaps the smartest man in wrestling today, he knows everything there is in pro wrestling and guides these two young men in their careers. Management, or the lack thereof, in the Championship Wrestling Organization.
Originally, I planned this article as a scathing report on the sad state of affairs that the so-called "managers" have allowed their jobs to become. I wanted to document the impending downfall of the once-noble institution of taking a man you've trained for long hours with, putting him in the four corners, and watching him excel to victory. But then I realized, there's only so much you can say about the lackluster performances of the people who second their charges to the ring. I mean, c'mon, what can you say about the likes of Tamara Wilcox, Julie Ashton, General Lee, et cetera, ad nauseum. I mean, these people are walking jokes already. For example, Wee Billy... "Life is hard for a man named Wee." (rimshot)
So, I'm shifting focus (and his sister), and looking solely at what the CWO offers in the way of Manager of the Year. First of all, there's me. We'll get back to that in a minute.
Vachyna - ... *heh* ... *snicker, snicker* ... Oh, c'mon, her claim to fame is that she has an, ooh, ooh, "risqué" name. Apart from standing at ringside and looking... all right, I guess, for a transsexual, she hadn't done anything for her athletes. I guess a good hunch in the hay is good for morale and release, but, hey, how far's that gonna get you in the sport?
Lust - Same thing, different twist. I actually can respect, to a small degree, what Lust has done during her time in the sport. I mean, to take two completely untalented morons like the Deadly Sins, and to actually mold them into a team up for title contention... makes you proud. Then, of course, you look again and you see that the obstacle on their way to success is a complete lack of strategy or concentration. Again, the "poke-in-the-hay" theory comes into play. Oh, and Lust, you get the crowd riled up by coming out butt-naked, and instantly you have heat. Guess what? So can I. If that doesn't make you sad about your lot in life, I don't know what will.
Lt. Cammy LeBeau - Please, sista girlfriend, I've played Street Fighter II
Turbo, and I've come out on top in matches with you every time. So, go sit your blonde-headed, blue-eyed, fatigue-wearing, fatigue-inducing, no-title- having, no-title-winning, no-title-shot-getting, GI-Joe-pretending, beret- wearing, pants-pissing, Sheets-lusting, "wish-I-was-but-I'm-never-gonna-be"
rudy-poo sorry ass all the way in the back of whatever Head Start bus you walked off of, and take your no-talent tag team wit'cha.
Now, to Vince DaVinci, the head of Empire. I gotta hand it to you, pal, you've done many great things during your tenure here as manager of one of the greatest stables in CWO today. Foolkiller is a consistent box office draw, several World title reigns under his belt, and just impressive and mean as all hell, but calculating, focused. Fantastic, Fett, the Dogs of
War; countless tag titles and opportunities for them as well. I've watched you and I have to say, from losing Union Caine to the Men of Respect, to ordering FK to put Ace Alles in traction, to all the people put out of the sport by your crew, to the infamous "hypodermic" incident, you've persevered, fallen off the top and landed on your feet, and you've set a precedence for all the managers to follow. A standard by which no other manager in CWO can begin to measure up to. Until today. You see, Vince, the key word is "precedence." What has come before. You represent the
"been-there, done-that" of the CWO. You've played all your cards, and you've won every time. But, I've seen the cards you're throwing, and I bet
I have the upper hand. 'Cause, me, Nasty Naked Lester, I represent the
"going-there, gonna-do-it-better" set of the CWO. I've seen the things you do, and I manage to go one step ahead, a pace you can't hope to keep up with.
Until the day I signed my contract, only one manager really made a difference in the CWO, only one manager had the cajones to get ahead, to set the world on its ear. Now, there's only two.
And the showdown is imminent. And Vince, Cammy, Lust, Vachyna, Wee, Lee, Wilcox, Thomas, Ashton, all the way up to the great Gordy Alles himself, you have, what I call the "Lester
Sheets 4-step Method of Coping" when you realize that I'm unapproachable in the realm of impeccable management.....
1)Shut your eyes. 2)Step back. 3)Open your eyes. 4)Watch me shine. 'Cause the world is mine. Today. - Sheets ________________________________________________________________________ A WOMEN'S TOUCH by Lt. We'd like to thank everybody who voted in the CWO Awards, a total of 23 submissions were received. With no further adieu, let's take a look at the winners!
* WRESTLER OF THE YEAR: Foolkiller (15 votes) Not much of a surprise here, huh? Foolkiller shocked the CWO by making his return in March, and then went on to have a Match of the Year candidate against Lord Hile Troy at APRIL ARMAGEDDON '98. (More on that later.) He would be defeated by Diamondback Chris Myers on August 18th, 1999, but would rebound to take the title back from Masher Maccabee at TERROR NIGHT '98 (the two also had a grueling match at SEPTEMBER SLAM III). Foolkiller would close out the year as World Champion, and for that, not to mention his 6'9"
frame and immense power and presence, led CWO fans to vote him 1998 Wrestler of the Year. RUNNERS-UP: Diamondback Chris Myers and Masher Maccabee (3 votes apiece) The only two others to win the CWO World title during 1998, Myers and Maccabee had intense and impressive matches not only with each other but with Foolkiller and many others as well. Myers was not really a surprise, since he had been on the rise ever since arriving in the CWO, but Maccabee definitely has to be considered a surprising break-out star for 1998, as nobody expected him to WIN the World, title, and certainly not many expected him to defend it as admirably as he did.
OTHER: George Washington (2 votes) * TAG TEAM OF THE YEAR: True Americans (Allen "Mr. America" Carter and George
Washington) (11 votes)
A stranger duo you couldn't imagined, yet it worked. The xenophobic loony known as Allen "Mr. America" Carter apparently saw potential in a young
Mexican wrestler known as Omar Martinez. Using his considerable persuasiveness, Carter actually got Omar to renounce his Mexican heritage and become an American, at first calling himself Marty, but eventually settling on a name determined to rile up disgust in every American, George
Washington. Still, the combination of Carter's savvy with Omar... I mean,
George's... agility and quickness. They won the World Tag Team Titles on
October 12, 1998, from the always impressive Mercenaries, and even though they would lose those titles in a shocking upset to the "renegade" team of
"Dragonfire" Rich Johnson and "Mr. Technical" Cooper Young (who won the 2nd
Annual Wayne Carpenter Memorial Tag Team Tournament to get the shot) on
November 30, 1998, they still provided a humorous and controversial if not brief title reign.
RUNNER-UP: Newton Bros. (Howard and Steve) (6 votes) Not many fans remember that these two roughhousin' college boys were at one time CWO World Tag Team Champions (in October and November of 1997). That's not because they were bad champions, it's just because they are more known by the belts they have laid claim to and dominated for many months, the CWO
North American Tag Team Championships. Winning those titles from the
Mercenaries (there they are again) on April 12, 1998, they would go on to defend those titles until October 19, 1998, when another shocking upset occurred, this time at the hands of the "Rat Pack" team of Gold Kard and
Flash Varga. The Newtons would eventually get those titles back from new arch enemies the Creatures of Darkness on November 29, 1998, and they would close out 1998 holding those belts. An excellent sense of continuity (well
DUH, they're brothers), amateur-hewn technical skills, and willingness to mix it up whenever needed, the Newton Bros. should remain near the top of the ladder in 1999 as well.
OTHERS: Mercenaries (3 votes), Rat Pack (2 votes), Creatures of Darkness (1 vote) * SECOND/MANAGER OF THE YEAR: Lester Sheets (10 votes) Well, it certainly was an INTERESTING year with Lester Sheets. His Sheets
Foundation started out with Secret Agent Super Dragon and Man Mountain
Marko. Marko would leave in a fit of rage (he is now forging a singles career on his own) but there is no denying the effectiveness Sheets had on the Dragon's career. Through careful strategy and planning, Sheets conspired with his man to find a way to beat the "Dark Shogun" Akueri
Hakujin on November 29, 1998, for the Light Heavyweight title in a stunning
2 out of 3 falls match (and Match of the Year candidate). Sometimes people can overlook the always-working mind of Sheets because his mouth seems to work faster than it does. Nevertheless, mostly through the success of the
Dragon, Sheets earns his spot as the top manager of 1998.
RUNNER-UP: Lt. Cammy LeBeau (5 votes) Hey, you try managing all those egos! Through a storm of diverse wrestling styles and personalities, Lt. Cammy LeBeau of the Outlaw Riders maintains the calm. Perhaps it's her military training that allows her to keep this
"Suicide Squad" of men in order. We'll say one thing, that training HAS given her a body to DIE for. Not only is it aesthetically pleasing, but it helps when somebody - male or female - tries to mess with her.
OTHERS: "Mastermind" Vince DaVinci (4 votes), Lust (2 votes), Vachyna (1 vote) * MOST IMPROVED: Hollywood Haskins (7 votes) At first, he seemed like a joke. He seemed more interested in his "budding" movie and television career than what happened in the ring. However, during the course of 1998, quietly (ironically enough) Haskins was building his skills in the ring. That led to a reign as Television Champion from August
11, 1998 to September 27, 1998 (when he was dethroned, in another bit of irony, by Mondo Gecko, his hated enemy who would later become his LEADER and
RECRUITER in the Outlaw Riders). Holding his own against World Champions
(Foolkiller) and holding his own in tag action with the man he would later betray, Masher Maccabee, Haskins showed that he was not only a man to be reckoned with in HOLLYWOOD, but in the CWO as well.
RUNNER-UP: "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday (6 votes) You may or may not know him. He's the quiet one. The guy who gets things done. But it wasn't until his former best friend betrayed him that fans really got to see this man shine. His rage propelled him to new heights of excellence, and his growing skills in the ring have many fans' tongues wagging about what he may do in 1999.
OTHERS: Brent "The Ace" Alles (5 votes), Col. Taggart (3 votes), Joe Eagle
(2 votes) * NEWCOMER OF THE YEAR: "Dark Shogun" Akueri Hakujin (11 votes) From his debut, this man made himself a force to be reckoned with. Maybe it was the mysterious aura he had about him, one that was forged through wrestling all over the Orient, that made fans stand up and notice. Maybe it was the great skill he showed, equal in martial arts or mat wrestling. Or maybe it was the high caliber matches he had, with Secret Agent Super Dragon and others. Whatever it was, this Light Heavyweight Champion (from
September 6, 1998, until November 29, 1998, when he was beaten by his chief rival the Dragon) always got your attention.
RUNNER-UP: "The Avenger" Jesse Ewiak (7 votes) There was, of course, the head-scratching story involving Ewiak, Wishbone, and his girlfriend Rachel Thomasson (first she's dead, then she's alive, then she's dead again, then she finally shows up alive - you try to figure it out). However, a lot of fans focused on this man's ABILITY in the ring, not just the backstory surrounding him. A Television Champion from November
29, 1998 (when he defeated arch enemy Wishbone), until December 27, 1998
(when he was defeated by cocky Cris Marcus - fellow Newcomer of the Year candidate - of the Outlaw Riders), Ewiak used his impressive strength and determination to win a lot of fans over in 1998.
OTHERS: Wishbone (3 votes), Cris Marcus (2 votes) * COMEBACK OF THE YEAR: Foolkiller (10 votes) The crowd was immediately on his feet when this man made his return in March
1998. From there it wasn't long until he recaptured the belt that he always considered his, the CWO Heavyweight Championship of the World. The fact that he moonsaulted then champion Lord Hile Troy THROUGH THE RING to do it only impressed fans more. The fact that he managed to capture the World title again in 1998 after he had lost it to Myers only lends more testament to this man's drive and tenacity. What does he do now for an encore?
RUNNER-UP: Lord Hile Troy (6 votes) Oh yeah, the guy who WAS moonsaulted through the ring. In one of the better vanishing acts in 1998, Troy went home to reexamine his life. No spring chicken (he's in his lower 40's, no real determination has ever been revealed), many fans whispered that maybe he had been put in his place.
Wrong. Returning to the fold for what many consider to be HIS event, CWO
CELEBRATION '98, he battled Foolkiller in one of the most thrilling matches ever, a 60 minute iron man match where he almost... almost won. The anguish shown by Troy after his defeat was heartbreaking, but the respect and good sportsmanship he showed by saluting Foolkiller after the match was truly moving. The "legend" is back!
OTHERS: Brent "The Ace" Alles (4 votes), Sandman (2 votes) * MATCH OF THE YEAR: WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (iron man match): Lord Hile Troy vs. Foolkiller (champion) - CWO CELEBRATION '98 - 12/27/98 (7 votes) By the narrowest of margins this was the match that was chosen this year. Actually, Troy and Foolkiller split the vote, as their other match (the one at APRIL ARMAGEDDON '98 where, you know, Troy went through the ring) for 5 votes itself. However, many fans were awed by the sheer endurance shown by both men, as they battled ferociously for 60 minutes and managed to score 2 falls apiece on each other. A last minute rally by Troy proved futile, but when it was all said and done, there was no need for sudden death, because neither man deserved to lose that night. One for the ages.
RUNNER-UP: WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Diamondback Chris Myers vs. Foolkiller (champion) - CWO FEVER 8/18/98 (6 votes) Destiny. It can be a hard word for wrestlers. Especially when they wonder why they haven't fulfilled it yet. Myers was supposed to have been World
Champion in June. No other CWO World Champion had been able to survive the
"May-Hem Curse" (the winner of May-Hem being able to defeat them at SUMMER
HEAT). Foolkiller did only because Brent "The Ace" Alles decided to begin his war on Myers in earnest (more on that later). However, on this night
Myers was finally able to fulfill his destiny, beating Foolkiller (never mind that Masher Maccabee had his hand in it as well). The fans celebrated with Myers as he finally had gotten to his dream: the Heavyweight
Championship of the World. OTHERS: WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Foolkiller vs. Lord Hile Troy (champion) - APRIL ARMAGEDDON '98 - 4/12/98 (5 votes) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (2 OUT OF 3 FALLS): Secret Agent Super Dragon vs. "Dark Shogun" Akeuri Hakujin (champion) - NOVEMBER PAIN '98 - 11/29/98
(2 votes)
TRIPLE CAGE OF TERROR - TERROR NIGHT '98 - 10/25/98 (2 votes) * PPV OF THE YEAR: Terror Night '98 (8 votes) RUNNER-UP: April Armageddon '98 (5 votes) OTHERS: CWO Celebration '98 (4 votes), November Pain '98 (3 votes), May-Hem
'98 (3 votes) * FEUD OF THE YEAR: Diamondback Chris Myers vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles (9 votes) These two gentlemen formed a new definition of hatred. Some say Alles was consumed by jealousy over Myers' "golden boy" status in the CWO. Both men are roughly the same age, and Alles publicly wondered why the fans supported
Myers over himself. So, Alles did a Salieri and decided to destroy Myers.
Going to any depth imaginable (and some that were even lower), Alles waged a physical, mental, and spiritual battle on Myers, and Myers was brought to the brink of insanity. Myers was eventually able to prevail, putting the final nail in the coffin by eliminating Alles from the 20 man battle royal at CWO CELEBRATION '98 when Alles surprisingly refused to strike Myers with a steel chair at the urging of his father and brother Gordy and Kent (you may know them as our "great" new President and Vice President). However, some say that Myers never fully recovered from his war with Alles, and it may have actually turned him to the DARK SIDE. Time will tell.
RUNNER-UP: Foolkiller vs. Lord Hile Troy (5 votes) They had exactly 3 matches in 1998, two championship and one involving a
6-man tag. However, the rivalry between these men sizzled when they were within the same vicinity and kept fans wondering whether it would reignite when they were apart. The sides changed (Troy was a bad guy the first time,
Foolkiller was not; it flip-flopped for CELEBRATION) but in the end these two gentlemen would ultimately show their respect for each other after battling it out for 60 minutes. The war ended, not surprisingly, with a handshake.
OTHERS: "Dark Shogun" Akueri Hakujin vs. Secret Agent Super Dragon (4 votes), Hollywood Haskins vs. Mondo Gecko (2 votes), Mercenaries vs. Outlaw
Riders ("Airborne" Mark Fulcrum and Ranger Taurus)
* STORY OF THE YEAR: Coming of Darkness (8 votes) Who or what was Darkness? The answer would not be revealed until early last month, but CWO fans were kept guessing just who the former "Master of
Darkness" Damion (now calling himself the Fallen) would bow down to. The answer would turn out to be Maximum Overdrive, who may not have been the most threatening CWO presence before, but now as Darkness he seems downright deadly. 1999 should see just how much influence Darkness is going to have on the CWO.
RUNNER-UP: Omar Martinez becomes George Washington (5 votes) Most Mexicans are proud of their heritage. Most proud would be the luchadors, who represent the finest in professional wrestling in Mexico since earlier this century. So it was an incredible thing for CWO fans to see Omar Martinez renounce his heritage and claim he was an American. But not just any American, oh no, he claimed to be none other than the father of our country, George Washington! Humorous to some, disgraceful to others, it certainly got a lot of people talking when it happened.
OTHERS: Alles and the Lunatic Fringe's Assault on Dallas (4 votes), Mondo
Gecko joins the Outlaw Riders (3 votes)
* GIFT OF GAB: Lester Sheets (12 votes) Hey, don't take our word for it. Feast your eyes on this: "Ahem, yes, well, it seems that the powers that be have decided to grace your homes... yet AGAIN... with my presence. Back in the house, and feeling oh-so-good. The holidays were great; my wife Sheila provided the turkey, and
I provided (little hip thrust) the stuffing. Anyway, allow me to introduce myself, at this time. I am... the maker of champions, the molder of dreams, the king of pain, the hot-tub staple extraordinaire, the master of satire, the Trouble Man, the greatest director in the violent world of sports entertainment today, the man... with the plan... and the red right hand...
So bad, I made your 97-year-old grandmother crap her pants last night; so good, I gave her the Sheets-meat anyways, I am none other than... Lester "My
First Name's Not Moe" Sheets!"
Any questions? RUNNER-UP: Mondo Gecko (4 votes) Hey, you may hate this guy's guts (most people do) but you have to admit he knows how to work a crowd. Whether it was getting them on his side in the past or getting them to put death threats out on his family now, Mondo's never been at a loss for words.
OTHERS: Foolkiller (3 votes), Hollywood Haskins (2 votes), Lord Hile Troy (1 vote) * MOST POPULAR: Diamondback Chris Myers (11 votes) A surprising award, since now people just wonder what the heck is going on with this guy, but there was no denying that any arena this man graced his presence with responded with some of the loudest roars the CWO has ever seen. One only hopes that Myers gets out of this mess he's in now and reclaims that presence in 1999.
RUNNER-UP: Foolkiller (5 votes) No matter what he did, crowds ate up everything this guy did. Just by thrusting his prized World Championship belt in the air and roaring, the crowd would do the same. What can I say? People like to root for the irresistible force, I guess.
OTHERS: Fergus McTavish (3 votes), "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday (2 votes),
Col. Taggart (1 vote)
* MOST HATED: Mondo Gecko (10 votes) Guess who? You hate him even before he opens his mouth. That cocky smirk on his face. The utter contempt for you, the average fan. Just the fact that he would betray some of his best friends without batting an eye would make your blood boil. Then he opens his mouth. And he whines. Or he complains. Or he wonders why the people won't respect him, because after all, HE is the top superstar in the CWO. No matter what, people love to hate this man, the leader of the Outlaw Riders, Mondo Gecko.
RUNNER-UP: Allen "Mr. America" Carter (8 votes) If you weren't born in the United States, you'd already hate this guy, because he hates you. Nevertheless, the xenophobic Carter still had a few supporters (which is pretty scary), but still, most of the time most of the people wanted to see this guy bleed and bleed hard.
OTHERS: Hollywood Haskins (3 votes), Ranger Taurus (2 votes) * That's it for the awards. It will be interesting to see what happens to all involved in 1999. But you have to agree, 1998 was one heck of a year for the CWO!
