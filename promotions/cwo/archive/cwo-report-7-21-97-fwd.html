CWO REPORT 7/21/97 (fwd)

21 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey, standing in downtown Auckland, New Zealand, in front of the sports arena where the CWO will be appearing tonight. A few CWO posters outlining the matches for tonight are covered with strips of tape that read "Sold Out".) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to another edition of the CWO REPORT. We are coming to you from Auckland, New Zealand, where the CWO makes it's final stop tonight on their Down Under tour. It's been a wild ride so far and tonight will prove no execption. Last night we appeared in front of a sold out crowd in Melbourne, and here's the results of what went down. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 7/18/97 Melbourne, Australia Attitude Dance (Joe Eagle and Jason "Flash" Varga) defeated Brother Justice when Joe Eagle pinned Jermain Justice after the Eagle Shock. Time of match: 12:58. Miguel Sanchez pinned a drunken Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Moonsault. Time of match: 5:51. Masher Maccabee pinned Sandman Jr. after the Homentaschen Hangover. Time of match: 16:28. Wrath pinned The Focus after the missed somersault legdrop. Time of match: 12:54. CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Moses pinned Mondo Gecko after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 16:41. Moses won the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion title. COAL MINER'S GLOVE MATCH: 'Monster' Rich Clark defeated Joe Eagle in a coal miner's glove match. Time of match: 15:28. Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) defeated Gold Kard/Matsuhiro Torunaka by disqualificiation when other members of Gold Kard International attacked. Members of the Wild Things then came down, and a wild brawl ensued. Time of match: 24:21. WASHEY: As mentioned before in the top of the program, our last House Show for this tour will happen tonight, then it's back to the States to prepare for FEVER on Tuesday. Here's the lineup for our last stop. CWO HOUSE SHOW 7/21/97 "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia vs. "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. the Focus Steel & Panther vs. the Deadly Sins Wayne "College Guy" Schillo vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Coldfront vs. Regulators Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Vegas High Roller Attitude Dance vs. Gold Kard/Matsuhiro Torunaka WASHEY: There's the card for tonight. It promises to be as explosive as all the others we've seen so far this past week. Let's get to the interviews for the REPORT. First off today, we have some comments from a man who will be meeting Fergus McTavish on FEVER tomorrow night. Here's Marc Mission. __________________________________________________________________________ The camera opens in a big field. Then a guy comes into the picture doing cartwheels. He stops after he finally falls. He gets up, and it is revealed to be Marc Mission.) Mission: Jesus!!! I never can do six in a row. (the interviewer questions Mission) Interviewer: Where's X-Factor? Mission: Why the hell would I know? Just because I team with him, does that mean that I am with the dirty bast*rd every day? interviewer: HEY!! Sorry. Mission: I mean, it is really starting to get on my nerves, that X-Factor. (plugs up one nostril with his finger and blows out mucas on the ground) When was the last time that you saw a victory for us . . . NEVER!!! Now, let me ask you this. Why not?! Two words. X . . . Factor!! FACT!!! He is really starting, as I said before, to get on my nerves. Just like that Scot Fergus McTavish!!! interviewer: What? What has he done to you? Mission: Nothing, why? That is the beauty of it. No one has done anything to me in CWO. They are afraid. They go straight to X-Factor because they know who is weaker . . . he is. Interviewer: Why the sudden change . . heeeee (Mission slaps the interviewer) Mission: Enough about X-Factor, dammit! Fergus, you whirling scottish devil, you. Bring you little friend, Billy I think his name is. I fight ya both. (He picks up the interviewer) Hey, are ya alright? I think it was X-Factor. He ran right past here and just smacked ya good. Interviewer: (in a quiet whisper) Yeah right! Mission: What did you say. (He whales away on the beaten reporter) Take that . . . and that . . . and that!! HEHEHEHEHE!!! Mission: Too sum it all up. Fergus, this Tuesday, you get your going to receive you Mission. And if you wish to accept it, too bad!! You're going down. (Mission starts off. He is doing cartwheels. He makes it to five and then falls.) Mission: MY GOD!!! Hey, what is this? OATMEAL? Out here? Oh yeah, Fergus, this IS OATMEAL!!! ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our only other interview for today is with the CWO North American Tag Team Champions, the Mercenaries, with comments taken immediantly following the House Show Saturday night in Sydney. Let's go to them now. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who have torn their locker rooms apart.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Regulators, you two pieces of trash don't know when to quit, do you? Haven't we beaten some sense into your mishapen heads by now? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: You keep pushing for something. If you want another shot at these titles, just go ahead and ask! Don't beat around the bush, sticking your nose in where it doesn't belong! We'll be more than happy to kick your asses one more time. Apparently, you've forgotten how that feels. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Regulators, you two make me sick. You really think you're a threat to ANYBODY? We've beaten you down so many times, we've lost count. Not only did we beat you for the titles almost two months ago, but the CWO Championship Comittee was forced to hand them over to us after we destroyed you at the PPV. What more do we have to do to show you that you have no chance in hell of EVER getting these back. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Well......there's ONE thing that we haven't done yet. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Huh? Ohhhhh yeah. I think I'm reading you loud and clear. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: This is THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD, and this interview's OVER! (Punishment reaches for the camera and the camera is cut off into static.) ____________________________________________________________________________ _____ WASHEY: Whatever they're planning, I don't think it can be good for the Regulators. Well, that's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody on the REPORT tomorrow. So long! (fade to closing credits.)
