CWO REPORT 12/9/96

9 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Monday, December 9, 1996. Tomorrow night is TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, let's look at the lineup for that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER MILWAUKEE, WI NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb (w/General Lee and Rebel Yell) vs. Gold Kard (champion, w/German Choklat) WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Blood Inc. (Panther and D. E. A. T. H. [Benton and Green], w/Da Boss) vs. The Dark Side (Hellhound and the Creatures of the Night [Night Crawler and Night Slinger], champions, w/Master of Darkness Damion) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Miguel Sanchez (champion) vs. Sin The Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jim "Jazzman" Taylor, w/Russell Hill) vs. Regulators (Fire and Ice) Baby Waldo (w/Maximum Overdrive) vs. Sorrow Lafayette and Rooster vs. Apocalypse (Plague and Pestilence) Sandman Jr. (w/Kellen) vs. Exile (w/Exodus and Lord Guru) Team Blitzkrieg (Kamikaze and Mysteria) vs. Highlanders (Conor and Gavin McCloud) Mad Doctor's Ghost (w/Don King and Gold Kard) vs. Wildfire (w/Miss Amber) Gods of Thunder (AC and DC) vs. David and Goliath (w/Jimbo Jones) Ganglord (w/T. A. S.) vs. Phantom Freightrain (w/Dapper Dan) Sandman and Talon vs. Flyboy and Clark Gray _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tomorrow night's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER will mark the beginning of a new format for the flagship show of the CWO. The first part will be covered by an as yet unnamed announcing team, while the second half will be announced by Kyle Melinn and David "The Truth" Thomas. Should be exciting and interesting! We'll get to comments from CWO superstars, but first, this commercial message. _______________________________________________________________________________ **COMMERCIAL BREAK** WE HOPE YOUR HOLIDAYS ARE THE BEST THEY CAN BE, AND FOR YOU & YOUR FAMILY TO ENJOY A HEALTHY, HAPPY AND PROSPEROUS NEW YEAR IN 1997 THE STAFF @ CMG ENTERPRISES Your Computer Wholesale Specialists ******************************************************************************** Home of the affordable "PENTIUM" System. STARR: A newcomer to the CWO, Cake Boy is quickly making his mark, especialy with a recent victory over Sandman Jr. We now go to comments from this very... er... unique individual. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Cake Boy is in the interview area.) Cake Boy: Okay, wresstling fanss. Now that I am undefeated, I plan to move on to bigger and better thingss. My nexsst target is that hunk of a man, Barry Hart.... I'll look at your butt, big fella. I want you in the sssquared sssircle real ssoon. Yummy. (Cake Boy takes a big bite of cake.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Um... yeah. Another newcomer to the CWO is Black Death, let's go to comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Black Death is in the interview area.) BLACK DEATH: I have encountered a minor setback in my fight against evil in this league. But I remain unfazed. I target the insane EXILE for my next opponent. The world has no place for the demon tormented such as yourself. I will deliver you from evil and into the darkness. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: And finally, we have comments from the French wrestler Lafayette. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Lafayette is in the interview area.) LAFAYETTE: France has declared war on this league. My first mission is to obtain the Gold Kard's title. France will not stand for this outrage of a wrestler to be a champion. Watch out Gold Kard, Vive la France! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Well, that's all the time we have for today. A reminder to handlers that House Show Challenges are due by midnight tonight. This is Johnny Starr, saying so long for now, we'll see you tomorrow on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER!
