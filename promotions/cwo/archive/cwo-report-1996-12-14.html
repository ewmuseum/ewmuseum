CWO REPORT 12/14/96

14 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
_______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera fades into Master of Darkness Damion, who stands with Hellhound at his feet and Dark Raven behind him, running on and off camera to the sounds of thuds as if he is running into something.) MOD DAMION: CWO, tonight, you will see for the first time my new tag team, Hell's Pets. These two beasts shall go straight to the top of the CWO and bring me even more gold. Pit Bosses, Bad Guys, or whoever has the NA tag belts, watch out, because it is you I am sending them after. The tag division shall know even more fear now as The Dark Side shows their power and superior wrestling and fighting abilities. (Just then Dark Raven goes by MOD carrying some kind of a carcass that appears to be dripping a red fluid, it is hard to tell if it is human or not. Hellhound turns to see this and runs after Dark Raven and the sounds of mad animals fighting over food can be heard off camera.) MOD Damion: Just look at those two, since joining the Dark Side, the Dark Raven has been at the top of the singles rankings and the two time CWO light heavyweight champion, and Hellhound continues to move up the ladder too. Do they look ready to take on the tag teams of this fed or what? I have them just clawing to go into the ring and together rip apart some poor souls who have no idea what kind of hellish NIGHT they are about to enter! LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!! (Camera closes on MOD Damion who is looking in the direction of his pets and just stands there laughing maniacally, as the camera fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, December 14, 1996. I'm Johnny Starr. We are only one day away from the ultimate in CWO PPV excitement: CWO CELEBRATION, our one-year anniversary celebration. Of course, as you all know, our first anniversary may also be our last, for if Hile Troy cannot beat Gold Kard, then the CWO will cease to exist, and the CM2WF will rise again. On a personal note, this may be the last time you see me if that happens, as I have been informed that I am on the "downsizing" list sent out by the offices of Gold Kard International. You can be sure that everybody on the "downsizing" list will definitely be rooting for Hile Troy tomorrow night. However, the CWO is still promoting great cards, like the one last night in Cleveland, OH, let's look at the results from there. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT FRIDAY, DECEMBER 13, 1996 CLEVELAND, OH Mercenaries vs. Blood Inc. Blood Inc. defeated the Mercenaries in 17:20 when Steel made Kaptain Krunch submit to the Gank (Texas cloverleaf). Ganglord vs. Jackass Joel Jackass Joel pinned Ganglord after the Mule Drop (flying neckbreaker off the ropes) in 8:51. Baby Waldo vs. Battlecat Baby Waldo made Battlecat submit to the Cradle Rocker in 12:08. After the match, Baby Waldo refused to let the move go, prompting Mondo Gecko to make the save. Highlanders vs. Hollywood Muscle Highlanders defeated Hollywood Muscle in 12:05 when Gavin McCloud pinned Wayne Carpenter after the Quickening (superplex by Gavin, splash from the top rope by Conor). Hell's Pets (Dark Raven & Hellhound) vs. Biblical Powers Biblical Powers defeated Hell's Pets in 23:08 when Savior pinned Hellhound after the latter missed with a flying splash from the 2nd turnbuckle. Maximum Overdrive vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley Benjamin Blake Bentley pinned Maximum Overdrive in 12:56 after outside interference by Miguel Sanchez. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night vs Deadly Sins Deadly Sins defeated the Creatures of the Night in 13:10 when Sloth pinned Pride after the Repention (superplex by Pride, Slothsault by Sloth). _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we will be in Allendale, MI, on the great campus of Grand Valley State University, at the Fieldhouse. Let's look at the lineup for that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, DECEMBER 14, 1996 ALLENDALE, MI (GRAND VALLEY STATE UNIVERSITY FIELDHOUSE) "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. Sandman Jr. Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo The Coldfront vs. Lafayette and Rooster Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart Pit Bosses vs. Deadly Sins NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title) Gold Kard (champion) vs. Hile Troy WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Master Of Darkness Damion vs Moses _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now take you to comments from CWO superstars. The mysterious Sin has had a change of heart lately, and he turns his dark thoughts towards current CWO Television Champion Miguel Sanchez. Let's hear from Sin. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sin is standing alongside a fiery pit, laughing loudly and evilly.) SIN: So, SanCheez. You have fallen directly into the trap. Well, I take your challenge wilfully. And maybe this time you will learn some respect. I know your style by now, and I'm aware of the fact you will most likely have some sort of help alongside you. So that's why I ask the Deadly Sins to join my side in this match. You will have your day in court. Then you will have your eternity in hell. (Sin laughs wildy and throws something into the fire which makes it engulf with a gigantic boom. After the fire dies down to normal size he is gone.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: If they were in the background before, Doomsday have come back to the forefront of conversation among CWO fans. We have more comments from them, we take you to them now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the members of DOOMSDAY in their "Dungeon" Training Center.) MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: You know, it really makes me mad when people start sticking their noses in other people's business. People keep asking, "Why has Schillo done this? Why has Schillo turned his back and joined forces with DOOMSDAY?" Let me put the questions to rest once and for all. When you want to get a job done, who do you call? Some person who you have to keep watching to make sure that they'll do the job to your satisfaction? Do you call a person that you worry about will only do a half-assed job? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: HELL NO! You call the people you know can get the job done! You call the biggest, baddest people you can get, and that's us! OVERDRIVE: You know, DOOMSDAY might not go about getting the job done the way other individuals think that we should go about getting it done. But you know what? When it comes down to the finished product, the results are the same. Satisfaction is guaranteed. Granted, Schillo may have a different outlook on life than the members of DOOMSDAY, but that doesn't mean that DOOMSDAY will attempt to brainwash Schillo. We do things our way, he does things his. That's fine with us. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: DOOMSDAY has never been afraid to admit that they respect certain people. Schillo was the only one that had the guts to listen to our proposition. Schillo didn't judge us by what we did in the past. It's what we can accomplish now that matters. And when the deal is done, and we part our ways, we do it on the best of terms. OVERDRIVE: You have all these idiots claiming to be fan favorites, but let me ask all the "fan favorites" where the hell they were when Schillo was getting beaten to a bloody pulp? Notice that until DOOMSDAY started to get involved, nobody showed up to help him out. What's happened? It's become nothing more than people who are out for their own interests. You talk about ["Big" Rich] Clark being a good guy? He'd sell his own family down the river if he could become World Champion. PUNISHMENT: Now don't take that literally, Clark. We don't want to give you any ideas. OVERDRIVE: DOOMSDAY has been labeled as the "bad people" for our methods of getting things done. That's fine, call us fan favorites or call us rulebreakers, we don't care. KRUNCH: We know that we could be called fan favorites for the stance we've taken lately. Hearing the cheers of the fans, hearing the boos of the fans doesn't matter to us either way. It's all about getting the job done. If the fans like what we're doing, fine. If the fans don't like it, fine. OVERDRIVE: Now you're probably asking yourselves, "What is the job you're talking about, DOOMSDAY?". For too long MOD and his drooling group of zombies have been running wild in this federation. DOOMSDAY, with the help of Schillo and his partner, will destroy the lock that those punks have on some of the gold in this federation. MOD, you think you know fear? You think you know pain? You don't have a clue. I said two weeks ago that the CWO hasn't seen anything out of our group yet. I guarentee that we will put you and your zombies to rest. KRUNCH & PUNISHMENT (together): SATISFACTION GUARANTEED! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: One of the big matches at CWO CELEBRATION is the dangerous Scaffold match between Ganglord and Wildfire, two newcomers whose feud has been very intense every since they both arrived at relatively the same time here in the CWO. We go now to comments from Ganglord. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Ganglord and T. A. S. are watching replays of their interferences against Wildfire.) GANGLORD: Well, T. A. S., we definitely did good this time and ya know, as bad as I want to beat this boy, and even if he does have some major bad thing from my past, ooo, I'm scared, It makes no difference, Wildfire, you want to know why, well I'll tell ya, ya see, I already cost you four matches and all those four possible victories could have been yours, and the most you can get out of it is one simple victory over me. Not saying it'll happen, because I plan to take any action to make sure you do not, think to yourslef, was it worth it? I guess we'll see at Celebration, won't we. T. A. S.: Yes, Wildfire, and whatever it is you think you got on us, well, I'm sure it won't affect the G here, because he has only one thing on his mind: throwin' your @$$ to the floor. And one more thing: if we win, which we will, we want a shot at a title, any title. GANGLORD: Wait, let's not get ahead of ourselves, but come to think of it, I would look good in GOLD! (Camera fades out as both talk about the current champs.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As part of this great PPV weekend, the CWO SHOWCASE SPECIAL will come out tomorrow. One of the great matches on that show will be Mondo Gecko and Battlecat against Lafayette and Rooster. We take you now to comments from Gecko and Battlecat. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko and Battlecat are in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Lafayette and Rooster, we are gonna fly all over you and kick you tails in! Battlecat and I have come here to win the tag team titles and we plan to do that by what ever means necessary, and we WILL become the new tag team champions. BATTLECAT: Rooster and Lafayette, you two opponents will now meet your fate when you go down at the hands of the Wild Ones. As my partner said, we are on a gold hunger, and we will not be satisfied until we get our hands on the gold! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also part of that SHOWCASE SPECIAL will be the tough team of the Regulators against Beauregard Dixon and Jackass Joel. We go now to comments from the Regulators. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Regulators are in the interview area.) REGULATOR ICE: Beauregard Dixon & Jackass Joel, you two will know what the law around here is when you face the Regulators. For a while, Bane, Ganglord, Tiger Rogers, and other rulebreakers have tried to cause harm to the superstars of the CWO. Now we are going to regulate some justice around here. REGULATOR FIRE: Beauregard & Jackass, your time is here to face the REGULATORS. We are going to kick the crap out of you and prove to the federation that we belong in this league, and we will do that by regulating justice! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today. We'll see you tomorrow. Don't forget to watch the SHOWCASE SPECIAL this weekend, as well as the CWO REPORT SPECIAL EDITION: THE STORY SO FAR. This is Johnny Starr, have a good day. (Closing graphics and music come up as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ Copyright 1996 Just B Cuz Productions http://web.syr.edu/~bgalles/cwo.html Cyberspace Wrestling Organization (CWO) All Rights Reserved
