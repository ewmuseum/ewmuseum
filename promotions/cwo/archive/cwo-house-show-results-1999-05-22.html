House Show Results

May 22, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 5/21/99 Alexandria, LA (Rapides Coliseum) Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Omar Martinez after the Rattler. Time of match: 6:54. 'The Avenger' Jesse Ewiak and Wildfire wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. 'Playboy' Wayne Schillo defeated Col. Taggart by disqualification when he hit Schillo with a chair. Schillo tried to hit Taggart with the chair while
Taggart was distracted by the Playmate who was with Schillo, but Taggart took the chair away from him and hit him with it. Unfortunately the ref saw that and called for the DQ. Time of match: 12:09.
Mike 'Money' Sexton/Snake Martin defeated Wack Pack when Mike 'Money' Sexton pinned Calvin after the missed flying elbowdrop. Time of match: 9:04.
Allen 'Mr. America' Carter made Brian 'The King' Derks submit to the American Dream. Time of match: 6:21. Maximum Overdrive pinned Wrath after the Pile-Up. Time of match: 4:47. Joe Eagle made Simon St. Severe submit to the half nelson. Time of match: 14:40. STRAP MATCH: Lord Hile Troy defeated Brent 'The Ace' Alles in a strap match. Time of match: 31:19.
