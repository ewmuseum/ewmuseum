CWO REPORT 8/31/97 (fwd)

31 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on the Mercenaries prior to their match with the Highlanders.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Highlanders, we hope your ready for the beating of a lifetime. We don't give a damn as to whether or not you used to be the World Tag Team champions of the CWO. You are the past, and tonight, you're going to meet the present and the future. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: The whole time you were champions, you never once put the titles up against us. We can't say we blame you, because when the bell rang at the end of the match, you'd be leaving the ring a lot lighter than you did when you came in. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Tonight is a little prelude of what's going to happen at the triangle match at the PPV. Bag Ladies, we hope you're watching really close, because you're time as champions is coming to a close. It's time for the Mercenaries to take those titles and start doing something with them other than having them gather dust sitting on your mantlepieces. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: We could have embarrassed you at Team Challenge, but we chose not to. It wouldn't have accomplished anything except to show everybody two things that they already know. Number 1: We can kick your asses. And number 2: WE ARE THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD!!! ________________________________________________________________________________ (Ganglord at his home in Venice Beach CA, just after seeing the CWO Report) Ganglord (laughing):Well Mr. Skyhawk, you think that Ganglord is some kind of push over, well you are sadly mistaken, I've beat bigger and badder men then you will ever be. And as for the rest of your "unitless" friends, if they want to get in volved, I might just have a little insurance policy to take care of them!!! Sleep well for tomorrow you have a gang buster waiting for you!!! (the camera fades out) ____________________________________________________________________________ ____ (Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey sitting at the CWO REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Hello everybody and welcome again to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Yesterday was a busy day for the CWO, as there were two House Shows around the New York area. Here are the results for both cards. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 8-30-97 Binghamton, NY (Civic Center) "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday pinned 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia after the reversed small package. Time of match: 8:41. El Grande Pistolerro pinned Wrath after the flying clothesline. Time of match: 8:35. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Diamondback Chris Myers after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 13:41. Both men shook hands after the match. Mercenaries and Highlanders wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 9:39. Light Heavyweight Champion Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned Gold Kard after the Flash Flood. Time of match: 3:40. Before the match, Gold Kard was attacked by Holocaust and Red Raven. North American Tag Team Champions The Big Cool defeated Matsuhiro Torunaka/Vegas High Roller in a non-title match when Jumbo Jenkins pinned Matsuhiro Torunaka after the Star Flash. Time of match: 11:30. World Champion Johnny Reb made Red Raven submit to the Texas Cloverleaf in a non-title match. Time of match: 6:39. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 8-30-97 Syracuse, NY (Onondaga War Memorial) Wild Ones defeated Newton Bros. when Mondo Gecko pinned Howard Newton after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 11:30. Skyhawk pinned Ganglord after the Skydive. Time of match: 14:41. Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned Lord Hile Troy after the Allesbreaker, pulling the tights. Time of match: 18:08. Vegas High Roller pinned Brian 'The Hammer' Williams after the Vegas Swinger, pulling the tights. Time of match: 5:43. Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Television champion The Focus after the Rattler in a non-title match. Time of match: 12:08. North American Tag Team Champions The Big Cool defeated Mason-Dixon in a non-title match when Jumbo Jenkins pinned Stonewall Mason after the Star Flash. Time of match: 21:47. CWO WORLD CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: The referee stopped the match in 31:47 when Johnny Reb had Joe Eagle in the Texas Cloverleaf. Joe Eagle was unconscious from the pain of the hold and could not respond to the referee when he asked him if he wanted to continue. After the match, Johnny Reb refused to relinquish the hold, almost risking disqualification, but fortunately Mason-Dixon managed to get him off of Joe Eagle before the referee reversed his decision. Johnny Reb retained the CWO World Champion title. WASHEY: A lot of happenings went down yesterday at both House Shows. Getting on to the interview portion of our program, today we open things with comments from Diamondback Chris Myers. Let's hear his comments. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Diamondback Chris Myers is shown in the CWO interview area. He is in his wrestling attire, along with the wraparound shades he is required to wear after the fireball in the face from the Reverend) Myers: So, someone has put forth a contract for Chris Myers and Sandman Jr. to take on the silent freak Sandman and the "scary" Reverend at SEPTEMBER SLAM. Revenge is indeed the name of the game. Sandman, am I supposed to be frightened of you? I have beaten you twice, once in a ladder match. And yet you insist on meddling in my affairs, and popping up where I least expect it. Chummer, you may be big and bad to some, but you're a minor nuscience to me. Now, my friend Sandman, Jr. has a major gripe with you, and that means I have a major gripe with you. Minor nuscience, major gripe. Never a good mix, tall, dark, and ugly. My main gripe, however, isn't with the Sandman. It's with the Reverend. I don't know who you are, or where you came from. You just made the very bad mistake of messing with Diamondback Chris Myers. Nearly blinding me is one thing. I can live with that, because the word is nearly. But...you cost me the TV title, and I can't accept that. Now, the Focus is a fine champion, and I didn't mind dropping the title to him. He's a wrestler, like me. But you coming from nowhere and blinding me...it was the way I lost that belt. Now, since I don't have a title, I'm just going to have to take something from you. Your hide. Where I come from, in the desert, it's said you only have to be afraid of things you don't see, like spider, snakes, and scorpions. When you and Sandman were hanging back in the shadows, I was intimitated because of all the rumors flying around. But now that I've seen you in the light of the day...I'm not scared. I'm amused and annoyed. Reverend, I'm going to have 70,000+ screaming San Antonioans screaming for me and Sandman, Jr. and I'm making a request from them. Fans, when you come to SEPTEMBER SLAM, you're going to see a Texas Tornado match. Weapons are legal, falls count anywhere. Bring something. A cookie sheet, a baseball bat, a joystick, I don't care. It's going to be a war, and I know what I'm bringing. (Myers holds up a KOHO hockey stick) Myers: This stick I got from Trevor "The Hammer" Hextall of Team Canada, one of the best tag teams around. We're not on the best of terms, but the man is the most hardcore wrestler around today. Sandman, and you, Reverend, are going to have an up close and personal meeting with this stick. And then you're going to meet the Rattler. And Sandman, Jr. is going to introduce you to his cane and his Permenent Dream. Face it, chummers...you're just plain fragged. ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Myers is wanting payback for that heinous attack on him a few weeks ago at the last FEVER. Next up is somebody with a lot in common with Mr. Myers. Here is Sandman Jr. ________________________________________________________________________________ [Kellen] We just don't get a break in this league, do we, Jr? [Sandman Jr] Nope, not at all. First person I want to talk about is the FOCUS. I beat you in a falls count anywhere match, something I'd been asking for for a long time. And you know what? I respect you. I'm sure you don't care, but I no longer want to battle you every week. Plus, I hope you run that jerk BENTLEY out of the CWO. OK, that's all I got to say to you. Now, WILDFIRE, may I ask WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT? I mean, I got no problem with someone wanting to be like Sting, but you handed me a bat. A bat is VERY similar in shape to a cane, and I was SORELY tested to use it, especially since I didn't know what you were going to do to me. You want to fight the corruption, the jerks, the scum of this league, man, I'm right behind you, but stop with the secretive stuff, eh? Next, JASON VARGA, I like you a whole lot, you're a wholesome guy, a good guy. Got a problem with someone? Get VARGA, he's your man. Well JAY, you are my man. You also have a belt I need to win, cause I've been in a BIG time drought in wrestling-land. Not ONE belt in any fed in like... a LONNNNNNNNNNG time. JAY, I hate that it's YOU I have to beat for the belt, but I do what I have to. So shine it up for me, and let's keep it a clean fight. Then, at SEPTEMBER SLAM, me and my best bud CHRIS MYERS get to battle those punks the REVERAND and SANDMAN. It's falls count anywhere. Hear that SANDMAN? ANY freaking WHERE! I could take you out to a truck stop, let you get pummeled by a bunch of unshaven brutes, and pin you on a deep frier! Would you like that? Cause I'd like to do it to you. I'm glad I'm with MYERS, cause even though he's not trying to be, he's hardcore, and you two aren't. So look out, we're gonna destroy you. Oh yeah, to finish up, at the SLAM, MIGUEL SANCHEZ is on the free-for-all Eye of the Storm. Not even good enough to make the main card, eh Miguel? HAHAHAHAHAAHAHAAA! ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Finally today we have the Deadly Sins, and with them as always, Sloth. Here they are. ________________________________________________________________________________ [Wrath] By the time you all see this, I'll have just whipped that punk PISTELERRO's butt. But you know, I noticed I'm stuck..., not stuck, but... dammit, I'm wrestling with the Sins against HEAT and the FRONT. I want that damn TV Title, and I'm doing this. But fine, cause this is my challenge, and even if it's not accepted, there's gonna be SOMEONE getting crushed: I want the winner of BENTLEY-FOCUS. I don't care if it's the Blue Blooded Bum, or the guy who's a "tweener", I just want the belt, and I don't care who I have to kick the ass of to get it! [Pride] Yeah, you tell them Wrath! But don't get us wrong, we can't wait to beat up on that PUNK JIMMY HEAT and the COLDFRONT, but we want some gold action. As usual, the CWO is trying to keep us down. [Sloth] Yeah really, a triangle match for the tag belts and WE'RE not in it? Fine. Fine. Cause we'll beat HEAT and the FRONT. We'll beat them like we beat everybody else. But then we want shots at the gold. BOTH belts, cause we want to hold both simultaneously. And we can do it. [Pride] We're the best tag team here, we're the most overlooked tag team here, and soon, we'll be that champion tag team here. [Lust] Heat, I've had plenty of time to get over what you did to me. You are SOOOOOO dead. Don't ever put your hands, or anything else, on me again. Or you'll be hurt harder than you thought possible. ________________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. Be sure and tune in tonight for the CWO Showcase. So long everybody! (fade to closing credits.)
