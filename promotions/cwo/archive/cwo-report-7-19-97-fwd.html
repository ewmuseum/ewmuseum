CWO REPORT 7/19/97 (fwd)

20 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey, soaking up the rays on a beach somewhere along the coast of Australia.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Hello everybody and welcome again to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Last night, the CWO appeared in a House Show in Papua, New Guinea, and it was a success. The card itself was top notch, so without further delay, let's look at what went down. CWO HOUSE SHOW Papua, New Guinea 7/18/97 Brother Justice defeated Regulators when Jerome Justice pinned Regulator Ice after the Final Justice and outside interference by the Mercenaries. Time of match: 13:06. El Grande Pistolerro and Matsuhiro Torunaka wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. SUBMISSION MATCH Benjamin Blake Bentley made Johnny Reb submit to the abdominal stretch. The ending was controversial, as this match was refereed by GKI referee Spencer Doloff. Reb claimed he didn't submit, and then knocked Doloff to the canvas. Bentley and Gold Kard t hen tried to attack Johnny Reb, but Reb fought them off to the cheers of the crowd. Time of match: 9:07. TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS MATCH Light Heavyweight Champion Mondo Gecko won the match, 2 falls to 1. Mondo Gecko won fall 1 in 6:51 after the cradle. Moses won fall 2 in 12:00 with the Commandment. Mondo Gecko won fall 3 in 18:44 after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Television Champion Diamondback Chris Myers pinned 'Lone Wolf' Ryan Deutscher after the Rattler. Time of match: 16:47. World Champion Gold Kard pinned The Focus after Die Flieder Kross and outside interference by Benjamin Blake Bentley, as well as pulling the tights. Time of match: 9:35. Before the match, Gold Kard claimed that if he couldn't beat the Focus in less than 10 minutes, then he would hand over the World title to him. WASHEY: The match between CWO Television Champion Chris Myers and "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher was a late addition to the card. Doctors cleared Myers to wrestle after he suffered the attack on FEVER by Bentley, then Sandman Jr, and the mysterious robed figure later on in that card. As for the next card, the CWO will make a stopover in Sydney, Australia to a sold out show. Here's the rundown for today's card: CWO HOUSE SHOW Sydney, Australia 9/19/97 Panter vs. El Grande Pistolerro Sandman Jr. vs. Steel "Mr. High Impact" Mysterio vs. "The Whirling Scottish Devil" Fergus McTavish Mason-Dixon vs. Brother Justice Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Lord Hile Troy NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH Mercenaries (champions) vs. the Big Cool (challengers) CWO WORLD CHAMPION TITLE MATCH LUMBERJACK MATCH Gold Kard (champion) vs. Joe Eagle (challenger) WASHEY: The residents of Sydney are in for a wild night, CWO style! It's time for the interviews for this portion of the program. We start off today with the group of El Grande Pistolerro and the Regulators. Here they are with their pre-recorded comments. _________________________________________________________________________ El GRande Pistolerro and the Regulators are standing in the locker room listening to the comments made by GKI, Cold Front, and Teh Mercenaries Pistolerro: Maccabee, you have somethign that belongs to me and that is teh North American Title belt. For such a bad ass you really need all the help you can get to win, I guess kissing Gold Kards butt and washing his cars help in the long run. Bottom line is this. I DEMAND that in the Team Challenge that I take on Matsuhiro Torunaka or Vegas Swinger, both preferably in a Survivor series matchup. I don't care who it is I am going to show the CWO why I ma the best wrestler ever from South of Teh Border. I have paid my dues and I am ready to win the North Americna Title. Soon the CWO fans will be well repersented. In Fact Monster Rich Clark, since you are the head of your team I am asking you and demanding that yopu let me be on your team. I watn GKI as much as anybody on this planet. ARIBA LA RASA Regulator Fire: First Off Mercenaries, we are going to make you pay for taking our titles away. Whether it be getting even on a house card or in a challenger series, YOU WILL pay. You two Demolition wannabe's are nothing but uverrated bums who need to learn how to wrestle. You pulled every trick in the book at RID but we are going to be ready for you two. Brother Justice, you guys whine and moan about how racist the CWO is and what White Trash is! Hey we grew up in the same neighborhood, the same area and in the same place you guys did. We don't care about your whining and today we will show you how we take care of business! Coldfront you guys think your SUPERIOR THAN US!?!?! Hey when have you guys held a Tag Team Title. Never! So you know what we are going to show you two what true domination is and burry you two once and for alll. Regulator Ice: WELL!!!! It seems that the mercenaries want to play rough. Mercs' you two are dead men walking and you don't even know it! When we get opur hands on you again, in a House Show, Team Challenge, or in a back alley, we will show you two how things are done! Cold Fornt don't think for one moment that we will bury you tow. We are one of the bets teams today, and if you guys are so dominant and superior, than why haven't you won any titles. WE were teh team to beat the Bad Boys and we will beat them again! GET READY CWO BECAUSE WE ARE READY FOR AN ALL OUT WAR, OOOOHHHHH WWWWHHHHAAAATTTT AAAA RRRRUUUUSSHHH _____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up we have some rather disturbing comments made by one Bloodbath. His taped interview will speak for itself, I'm sure. _____________________________________________________________________________ (In a dark cemetary, their is a lone grave. The blood red gloved hand of Bloodbath shoots out of the ground as Bloodbath emerges from the dirt) BLOODBATH (dark and mysterious):Something has returned. The future of the CWO will never be the same again. (a white light now shines on Bloodbath as he begins to float up to the heavens) BLOODBATH:Something has returned. Something has returned. Something has returned. (Bloodbath decends to the sky as the camera fades out) _____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: If you're like me, your skin is crawling about now. Next up we have the tag team of the Deadly Sins, who are responding to the comments made by a certain group of CWO wrestlers. _____________________________________________________________________________ PRIDE: First of all, seems someone wants a little gang warfare...we're cool with that. The Justice Bros., Steel, and what's he now, Mustafa Kahim or something like that? Doesn't matter, we'll beat you guys down like dogs. SLOTH: Ya see guys, it ain't a black thing. We don't like you for the sole reason that you're annoying. You could be any color. Plus, you're in our way for total domination of the CWO... that makes you marked men, not that you're black. So, be prepared to lose a turf war, cause we're the masters here! WRATH: So, ROCKY MAIVYERS has said he will defend the TV Title against the best thing ever to happen to the CWO, me. MYERS, you got guts. Brains are sorely lacking though. I am going to break every bone in your body. I'm gonna bring gold to the SINS. Oh yeah, you also gotta look out for GKI, a bunch of guys we couldn't care less about. MYERS, be prepared to face your doom! PRIDE: That reminds me... CLARK AND SANCHEZ... we had the mispleasure of teaming with you guys in the past... God, was THAT a mistake... I thought CLARK ws turning over a new leaf, but that didn't last... too bad... cause now, we have to beat BOTH you down like dogs. Which is fine by us. SLOTH: Another two guys obsessed with GKI... WHO FREAKING CARES? Let them run over this league, does it REALLY matter? Not to us. As long as we get our rightful tag team titles, we couldn't care less what happens here. LUST: So many little boys, so little time. But CHRIS MYERS, you are the most yummy, even more so than MIGUEL SANCHEZ... I'll take you away from that whore, and you'll BEG to stay with me... and if you're lucky, I'll let you. If you're unlucky, you won't be recognizable. __________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up is one of the members of the CWO's most dominating stable at the present time. Of course I'm talking about GKI and here is one of their members. __________________________________________________________________________ Masher Maccabee is relaxing at the GKI Corporate Pool, "I know I haven't said too much this summer, but recent events have been too much for me to keep quiet. I have gone from being the most feared man in this federation to a useless midcarder. Why? NO GOLD. I am very happy in GKI, I even have Brother Moses to relate to, but I should have the North American title around my waist. There is no excuse. Look what happens anyway, that wimp Inferno leaves the federation! I hereby voice my desire to enter the tournament for the vacant belt, and rest assured, it will be added to the GKI stable. No one, not even the suits in the back can hold back the nastiest finish on the planet, and I'm not talking about that wimpy Rattler, a blatant ripoff of my patented double arm DDT, I'm talking about the Jerusalem Jackknife and the vicious Homentaschen Hangover. Ask Silly Putty Gecko about what that combo did to him when I humiliated him for the TV strap. While there is GKI business to attend to, Masher is back and ready to move back into the upper eschelon of the CWO/GKI. Anyone who thinks they can beat me for the NA title, bring it. For a tuneup, how about a finisher vs. finisher match "DIamondback" for the TV belt? I don't think you have what it takes to beat the greatest TV champ of all time. GKI 4 LIFE!" ___________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: (Camera comes back in to find Washey trying to figure out the hand signal) Oh to hell with it. Lastly today, we have an interview from one of the participants in today's card at Sydney. Here is the one, and the only, Sandman Jr. ___________________________________________________________________________ SANDMAN JR: Ok, let's look how this past week's been for me. First, I lose in MY TYPE OF MATCH to the FOCUS... not only did I lose, but it was a fair loss... how much does that suck... plus, that little punk, a punk I thought I was done with, BENTLEY, gets in my face... Now, it seems that the big guy himself, the SANDMAN, has a problem... well, you know what? I kinda like all this! Number one, I want a match with BBB. Sure, it woulda been nice if you had a belt, but I don't care, you need an ass-whipping. THEN, I want a tag match, me and CHRIS MYERS, along with THE two most beautiful women in the CWO, KELLEN and DALLAS, vs. ANY combo team GKI has to offer. ANY!!! But you know who I want? BBB and SANDMAN! Finally, SANDMAN... in two years I've been here, we've NEVER faced each other... how odd... well, I do want a match with you... but something this monumental has to be bigger than TV... I want you at a pay-per-view, SANDY... and I'll show you, and everyone else, that SANDMAN JR. is VASTLY superior to YOU! __________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Well, that's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody on tomorrow's edition of the CWO REPORT. So long everybody! (fade to closing credits.)
