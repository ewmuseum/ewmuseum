CWO REPORT 6/27/97 (fwd)

27 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on clips of several CWO wrestlers in action. The words CWO REPORT zoom by on the screen. Letters spelling out "Countdown to Rockin' Independance Day" appear on the screen and are blown up by a massive explosion. Fade into host Mitch E. Washey.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Things in the federation are really heating up as we are rapidly approaching the July 4th PPV. On a side note, the CWO will schedule three House Shows this week, tomorrow, Sunday and a special card on Monday. Let's look at the lineup for tomorrow's card. SATURDAY, JUNE 28, 1997 CINCINATTI, OH Matthew "The Man" White vs. Steel Steve "Mr. Niceguy" White vs. Vlad The Impaler ??? vs. Wildfire Moses vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Diamondback Chris Myers vs. Wrath "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia vs. Panther Wayne "College Guy" Schillo vs. El Grande Pistolerro. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): BBB/Hile Troy vs. The Regulators (champions) WASHEY: An exciting night is coming to the Cincinatti area. Be sure and get your tickets, as they're going fast. Let's get to some interviews. First up today, we have two CWO individuals we haven't heard from in a long time. I'm talking about Marc Mission and his partner, X-Factor. Let's hear what they have to say. ______________________________________________________________________ (The two are standing beside a ladder proped up against a wall.) X-Factor: You know, it's hard making a name for yourself in this great sport, but you just can't start something and expect to reach the top just like that (snaps his finger). (Marc Mission is nodding his head, as X-Factor climbs on the first step of the ladder) This ladder that I am perched on, represents the CWO. Each time you earn a victory, you can climb the ladder knowing you deserve it. Time after time, match after match, you work your way up knowing it can be pulled out from under you at any time. Then, you're back at the bottom. With nothing, but another challenge. (Marc Mission steps in front of the camera) Marc Mission: YEAH!! But not us! We are the new breed of wrestlers in the CWO and coming very soon to a CWO arena near you, we present US, X-Factor and Marc Mission, the fresh talent sweeping the world of wrestling. (He takes time out to spit) Don't get me wrong, CWO's tag team division is the toughest around, but WE can and WILL compete here with ANYONE, that's a fact.(He turns around and spits and walks off) X-Factor: That's right Marc, take heed, run, hide, whatever. The fact of the matter is that we are here in CWO for one reason and one reason at all. And that is to climb this ladder until we reach the very top. (The two start to climb the ladder but a fighting with each other to go first.) ____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up we have the Focus. Here's his thoughts. ____________________________________________________________________________ [ The Focus is shown sitting on the floor of a room with all white floors ] Sometimes I truly wonder hwether or not my CWO career is really worth anything. Sometimes I wonder whether or not I should just hang up my boots, and call it quits. But then I realize that the one thing that drives me in my career is beating guys like Sandman Jr., and Benjamin Blake Bentley. I have several wins over our current TV champion, and I'm sure one more is in order. That should happen soon. BBB, how about one more match with your good pal, huh? You know I deserve it, and you know you can't win. Now to more important matters...Sandman Jr, we have a Singapore Cane match soon. You claim to be some sort of God of extreme. What you fail to realize is that all you need to go hardcore, is the right motivation. Beating you at your own pathetic game is my motivation. I love to match wits with people, especially people as dull witted as you. It's almost a challenge to sink that low. Ha! You can pretend that you have a chance against me, but it's a painful fact that you don't. Period. _____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Interestingly enough, we have comments from one of the individuals mentioned in the Focus' interview. We go now to the one and only, Sandman Jr. _____________________________________________________________________________ SANDMAN JR: YOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! FOCUS, looks like we got us a cane match. You DO realize this is a match I can't lose? I can't! So, FOCUS, your pestering of me is gonna be done after this. Your hospital stay, on the other hand, will be just beginning. Now, RICH CLARK has a mystery man... and I know all you fans have warmed up to CLARK since he hates GKI... but I haven't forgiven you for getting rid of my buddy. Now, this mystery man... I think I know who it is... and MYSTERY MAN, if you're listening, just one question: Why CLARK? Why not ME! I coulda got you back in, but you went back to THAT jerk... I can't understand it... ____________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: One of the biggest stables in the CWO is standing by, and I'm not talking about GKI. Here's the College Guys. ____________________________________________________________________________ (Joe Eagle, The Big Cool, Russell Hill, "Big Guy" Jim Peer, Jason "Flash" Varga, Wayne "College Guy" Schillo and Brian "The Hammer" Williams are all seated around a large table. All are wearing conservative suits, except for Varga who is wearing his sequin cape, emblazoned with "I am the Man!" He is also drinking beer from a VERY large mug. The cameras start in the middle of the conversation.) Eagle: All I'm sayin' is that GKI is gonna take over if we don't do something, and do it quick! Schillo: Fine, then, Joe. What are we gonna do about it? Williams: Uhh...I've got an... Jazzman Taylor: Let's just stick together, and get 'em before they can even head for the ring! Varga: Let's get 'em drunk, and have a picnic! Peer: I like his idea. Eagle: SHUT UP! And why are you wearing that thing? I told you this was a formal gathering! Varga: This IS my formal wear! Schillo: Guys! This is stupid! What in the world are we gonna do? Williams: I think I know... Jumbo Jenkins: Perhaps we could just wage all out war? Eagle: It won't work. Now that they've got the Commish behind 'em, we can't even get out of our dressing rooms without a "security escort." Ugh. Williams: Guys? Schillo: Hey! Brian wants to talk! Williams: Well, here's what I'm thinking...first, we need to get everyone together. We need that Diamondback dude, and maybe that Moses guy, too. Eagle: I think I see where you're going... Williams: Then, we'll...well, turn off those cameras, first of all... (The cameras suddenly go dead) _______________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: (to somebody off camera) Isn't Moses aligned WITH GKI? Oh well. (back to camera) Incredible as it seems, we have an interview from Moses, with one of his friends in the CWO, Masher Maccabee. Let's see what they have to say. _______________________________________________________________________ [Moses sits in the weight room of GKI's Mansion. Masher Macabee is training in the background, Moses looks like he's just about dead from exhaustion after finishing his vigourous workout. He sees the schedule for the next CWO PPV, and finds out that he's supposed to defend his Light Heavyweight Title against Mondo Gecko.] MOSES: What the...? WHY DO I HAVE TO DEFEND AGAINST THIS LOSER? I didn't approve of this, who set this up. I'll murder him. There is no way that I'm defending this belt on July 6th against Mondo Gecko. Isn't that some kind of lizard? That's not a freakin' wrestler. [Masher comes over and calms him down, and the two men whisper for a bit] MOSES: [with a smile coming to his face]Oh, so THAT'S what's going on. OK, that's cool with me. You guys better make sure it goes through, alright. Gecko, prepare for the beating of a lifetime, 'cause that's what your getting. Oh yeah, one more thing. If any of your wild freaks come down to ringside to interfere, all stips are off. I want a fair match, no interference from either side. In fact, let's make this a scientific test of skill match. I'll see if you can out wrestle the the best light heavyweight wrestler in the world. If you beat me, I will concede, any who could do that deserves this belt, but you won't beat me, because no one besides me is worthy of this belt. Your time is done, Gecko, and on July 6th, I'll make sure of that. [Moses takes off into the showers as Macabee continues working out in the background.] __________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: The Deadly Sins are up next, along with their long lost manager, Lust. __________________________________________________________________________ PRIDE: I want to start off with this warning to LORD HILE TROY: It ain't happening, bud. I know you think you're gonna make me #1 on your comeback tour, but it ain't gonna happen. You may be one of the best ever, but there is NO WAY IN HELL I'm gonna let you beat me, so get the very thought out of your mind! SLOTH: Look at this, ONCE AGAIN we're looked over for a pay-per-view shot. But what's this, a TRIANGLE match... and lookie here, the BIG IDIOTS, and the COLD BOYS are gonna be our opponents... can you say SQUASH? COLDFRONT, BIG COOL, we're the new #1 contendors after beating both your asses. Get ready for it, cause this may be the "Eye of the Storm", but we're a hurricane running through you guys... did I just say that? WRATH: First off, SCOTTISH BOY, in case you didn't notice, it took THREE piledrivers from the top to put me down. If I face you again, you'll need four at least, but it won't happen, cause when I get you in the REPENTION RACK, it's all over. Now, CHRIS MEYERS. Everyone's calling this punk the future of the CWO. Problem is, CHRIS, you're a ROCKY MAIVIA type-- get some wins early, show some promise, and then get beaten down like a dog. Well, call me the dogcatcher, cause I'm gonna be the one who stops you. I don't give a rat's ass about your feud with BBB or GKI or whoever... just be thinking about ME, cause at the pay-per-view, I'm gonna take you out! LUST: CHRIS MEYERS, once you get a load of me, you won't be able to concentrate on Wrath. Not that'd it'd matter if you did. The DEADLY SINS are the PREMEIR organization in the CWO! Not GKI, not the WildSide. It's US, and if you don't like it... heh heh... you may be getting a spanking from me, and it'll hurt you a WHOLE lot more than it'll hurt me! ___________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up, we have a tag team standing by that needs no introduction. Let's go to the Mercenaries, the self proclaimed CWO North American Tag Team Champions. ___________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who are wearing their CWO NA Tag titles. They are standing in front of a video monitor and holding up a videotape.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: You know, we've given a couple of interviews where we've been sitting back watching the way we take apart teams like the Regulators. Due to popular demand that we've been getting, we have put all our greatest matches on tape so that you, the fan, can view the Mercenaries in all their destructive glory. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: We call it "The Mercenaries Greatest Hits". And when we mean hits, we mean hits. Check it out for yourselves. (A video clip comes on the screen showing the Mercenaries bashing teams like the Deadly Sins over the heads with steel chairs. Another clip shows the Mercenaries attacking men like "Monster" Rich Clark and Miguel Sanchez. Shots of the Mercenaries winning the CWO World Six Man Tag titles, plus action in many of their title defenses are shown. Footage of the Mercenaries pummeling teams like the Masters of Hardcore, the New Men, the Big Cool and several of the CWO's other "top" tag teams are shown in rapid succession.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: But that isn't the best part of the tape. (More video clips come up on the screen, showing the Mercenaries attacking the Regulators following the Regulators victory over the Bad Guys at the last PPV. The Mercenaries attack both Regulators and execute their signature move, the Flying Clothesline from the top rope to the arena floor.) KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: We even have clips that have never been televised and are only on this video! (The last video clip shown was taken from the Detroit House Show where the Mercenaries beat the Regulators for the NA Tag Titles. Both teams are bleeding profusely in the middle of the ring, fighting it out. Regulator Ice throws Punishment out of the ring and follows him to the outside. Kaptain Krunch is in the ring punching Regulator Fire in the corner and hits him with a low blow. Fire crumples to the canvas and Krunch goes to the outside, picking up a steel chair. He approaches Ice from behind and blasts him with the chair, knocking him out cold. The Mercenaries get back in the ring and deliver the Flying Clothesline to Regulator Fire and get the pin. When the bell rings, the Mercenaries climb out of the ring, grab the tag belts, and stagger off to the back.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Now I know that everybody has to be asking themselves, "Where can I get one of these fine videos of tag team wrestling excellence? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: It's EASY! You can buy them at your local House Show when we come to your town! Maybe if you're lucky, we'll even autograph them for you! If there are no House Shows in your area, call our toll free hotline 24 hours a day! (A phone number appears at the bottom of the screen.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Be sure and pick up our second installment of this video when it comes out, probably after the upcoming PPV, where we destroy the Regulators once and for all! And any other tag team stupid enough to get in our way! KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: This commercial was brought to you by THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD! How can you go wrong? ______________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our last interview today is from "Monster" Rich Clark, who suffered a rare defeat at Tuesday Night FEVER. Here he is with his masked partner. ______________________________________________________________________ [Rich Clark is standing with his back to the camera, looking at the ring in the Fleet Center in Boston, where earlier in the night he was dragged down underneath the ring by an unknown assailant. Standing beside him is the masked man.] "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: Now this is just great. GKI is running wild, and some idiot who has a personal vendetta against me ahs ruined my chance at getting my world title back. Don't get me wrong, I know who you are, "unknown man", and when you finally decide to stop being a wimp and grow a set of balls and reveal yourself, I'll be there to eliminate you from ever competing in the CWO again. You don't know what you've gotten yourself into. DO you realize how many men I've put out of competition with my devastating Choke Slam? More than you can count. Well, I'm tired of always having to clean up the messes around here. I'm tired of having to deal with every backward ass f*** in the CWO. Well, I'm telling you right now, that you are ALL NUTS. The only people here who have some semblance of sanity are me, this man standing beside me and Chris Meyers. Meyers I think you're doing a great job with your taking apart GKI one at a time, and if you need any help with that, my buddy and I are definately willing to help. Which brings me to another thing. You all wanna know who this guy is. Who all have been wondering who this man is for the last three months. Well, I'm gonna tell you right now. Buddy, take off your mask. [the man takes off his mask to reveal none other than former CWO Television and North American Champion, MIGUEL SANCHEZ] MIGUEL: That's right boys, I'm back. It took FIVE months, but my lawyers found a loophole, and I have gotten back into the CWO. I'm surprised that i was let back in, consider I beat Gold Kard by pinfall in February, one of few men to do that, taking his precious gold away from him. Now, I have one thing to say to you in GKI. Prepare to recieve the beatings of your lives. Moses, you're first. I challenge you to a match whenever you want, because I was responsible for bringing you here, and I'll be the one to take you out for good. GKI, Rich & I, and anyone else who wants to help us are taking you apart one by one. You can go out and buy people's contracts, you can fire us is you want, but the fact is that whether in the ring or outside the f****** building, you guys are going down. Have a nice day. RICH: Well well well, GKI. It seems that the ball is now in your court. It's your move, and your entire future depends on it. [The two of them turn around and walk out, with RICH's new theme Sick of You by GWAR blaring away on the PA system.] _________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody tomorrow on the REPORT! (Fade to closing credits.)
