This week I want to talk about the Reverend. In the few months he's been here he has caused more trouble than Howard Stern. I mean who does this guy think he is. He has some mysterious power over the phoneme of the CWO, I am of course talking about Bloodbath. For the last few months he has been costing Bloodbath matches. Well he took control of Bloodbath and the Sandman. BIG DEAL! I have been watching the CWO for the last few months before I came here, and I've seen you guys over come worse things then some weird looking guy who doesn't know when to put the Halloween costume away. Get over it, Bloodbath and the Sandman are just a couple of sell outs and we don't need them! I'm Double K Krash Kelly, and this is Krash Kourse. *********************************************************************************** Nothing But the Truth by: David "The Truth" Thomas Hello, morons, I'm David "The Truth" Thomas, and you should be honored that I have actually considered spending 5 minutes to write this column for this piece of trash, CWO MAGAZINE. Enjoy these few pages, as they're the only thing worth anything in this rag. Anyway, I want to discuss the topic of gang warfare here in the CWO. It seems like every time you turn around you see yet another faction splintering off. You've got the CPE, which stands of course for Can't Pin Everybody, who think they're so bad by t aking the worst dregs of society this league has to offer. You all know my thoughts about that loser Mondo Gecko and his bimbo manager Tamara Wilcox. And now he has the audacity to challenge my main man Masher Maccabee for the North American Championship? Fat chance, Geeko. The list goes on and on, Southern Pride, Empire, Riders on the Storm, blah blah blah. All of these factions know that they simply can't compare to the great organization ever, of course I'm speaking of Gold Kard International. Even though we're down to four members, we still rule the ring, and everybody knows it! And believe me, before 1997 is out and we move into 1998, Gold Kard International will truly shock you, mark my words! All of this gang warfare, however, really does the league no good. There's so much infighting that people can't concentrate on the truly "evil" force in our league, the Dark Side. Master of Darkness Damion has been strangely low-key lately, and that can't be good for anybody. All of these factions had better get their act together or this whole league might be lost. And that's the truth. *********************************************************************************** The Change of a Champion by: Le Ann Stevenson Since winning the CWO World Championship at Team Challenge, Johnny Reb has taking on a new attitude. Joe Eagle extended the hand and gave Reb the chance to win, but Reb shrugged it off. But when Eagle went for the win Reb nailed him from behind... (Picture of Reb nailing Eagle with that flying elbow) And now two weeks ago at Terror Night, when all seemed lost for Reb, his manager, Rebel Yell, hit Chris Myers with a chair knocking him cold. But what's in store for the champ? At November Pain he has to defend the title against the CWO's hardcore icon Sandman Jr. Many speculate that Reb doesn't have enough to get the win. But Sandman Jr. will have to watch out for Rebel Yell. Who will prevail, attitude or hard-core? This question and many more will be answered in just a few weeks at November Pain. ************************************************************************************* He Who Walks on the Darkside by: [name withheld] "He Who Walks on the Darkside". CWO fans alike used to refer to Bloodbath that way. But since the "phenom" of the CWO "crossed over" and became a part of the Reverends Church, there was really no man that could carry on, until now... (Picture of Wildfire, high above in the rafters) Now there is a new man. A new "phenom" of the CWO. The once flamboyant superstar, turned to an image of silence and mystery. He lives high above the rafters, waiting for the Reverend. Waiting for the chance to extract revenge on his long time friend and tag team partner. Where ever the Reverend and his church are, you can rest assured that Wildfire is not far behind. Now these two superstars are slated to battle at November Pain for the CWO World Television Championship. Now Wildfire has already staked his claim to the TV title this past week on Tuesday Night Fever... (Picture of Wildfire hitting Bloodbath with the bat and taking the television title) On Tuesday Night Fever, for the first time since his arrival to the CWO, the Reverend pulled back. It is clear to this reporter that the Reverend, as powerful as he may be, wants no part of the new "phenom" of the CWO, Wildfire. Who will prevail, the Reverend and his church, or the leader of the dark side, Wildfire. This one important question will be answered November 23rd, at November Pain. ********************************************************************** Behind the Scenes by: Lance Russell Hi there, folks, and welcome to the CWO Magazine Behind the Scenes with me, Lance Russell! I got some doozeys of rumors for you and I hope all you kids out here listen up because some things may shock you... Brent Alles has recently had problems with GKI and is continually getting attacked by Lord Hile Troy. Sources close to me have Alles listening to offers from CPE but also another party has offered to help. It seems that three old enemies of "The Ace" have problems with GKI and are willing to for "an army" to stop them... could the CWO have allies in a weird contingent of its own in the battle against The Reverend? After a recent house card I saw a heated argument between The Reverend & THE CABIN in which both sides had to be restrained by security! Seems the Cabin and Lord Guru aren't too fond of The Church and tempers could soon flare... The Eagle has landed! Joe Eagle is recovering nicely from his injury he sustained and seems to have his goals in place. The ex-CWO World Champion has stated that next time he comes after Johnny Reb he will have an insurance policy of its own... finally, CPE seems to be getting stronger and stronger as the days go on and Gecko has ore plans of expansion. Recently a hard luck tag team has been seen sparring with The CPE Enforcers and looking good. This team seems to have repented to the light so to speak and I can't wait to see them in action... finally, will Dave "Wendy's" Thomas make another managerial appearance and who will his man be? We have seen Joe Root and Tamara Wilcox retire to become managers and "The Truth" is jealous! It seems that the Truth wants a piece of the Golden pie that haunts a certain superstar... well, folks, that's all the time I have left. Tune in next week for more Rumors in Behind the Scenes! ********************************************************************** Trivia by: Matrat (1)-What was the name of the tag team that the Demolition defeated for the World Wrestling Federation Tag Team Championship at WrestleMania VI?
