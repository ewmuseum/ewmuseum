FEVER 11/11/99 Preview

November 10, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FEVER NOVEMBER 11, 1999 New Orleans, LA (New Orleans Arena) HOUR #1: [name withheld] HOUR #2: [name withheld] Fever comes from the Big Easy this week, but it's never "easy" for the superstars of the CWO, considering the turmoil going on right now!
SYNE still wants revenge against the ELITE. His next target? The "hitman for hire" currently hanging his guns with the Elite, MUSTAFA RAHIM. We saw
RAHIM sink to new lows last week against CARTER and BONDAVARSKY (who thankfully were basically all right after being sent off the ramp in a dumpster). Will the TRUE AMERICANS be looking for payback?
The WAYNE CARPENTER MEMORIAL TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT continues, as the BEAUREGARD BOYS take on GORDY ALLES' "Chosen Team", "ROAST BEEF" JASON
ALLANSON and DELIVERANCE. Just how far is Gordy Alles going to "push" those two, anyway?
MAN MOUNTAIN MARKO continues to defend his North American title, after last week's tough defense against MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE. This week it's "THE MAIN
EVENT" STEVE DAVIS. Will Davis live up to his billing?
Another round two match in the tournament should be a hot one, as the returning BONDAVARSKY and CARTER (fresh out of the hospital) take on the red-hot MERCENARIES. The Mercenaries may have their ultimate sites set on the World Tag Titles currently held by the BRUTE SQUAD, but you can be sure they wouldn't mind winning the Tournament to get the NA Tag Titles and be the #1 contenders! (Ironically enough, that's how the Brute Squad got THEIR title shot.)
"AIRBORNE" MARK FULCRUM declared MILLENNIUM "dead." However, apparently the
CWO top brass think differently as they've booked Fulcrum, DIAMONDBACK CHRIS
MYERS, and LORD HILE TROY, yes, you saw that right, to go against "THE JAW
JACKING GIGOLO" ANDREW DIXON (Kent and Mary Alles at his side) and the ever macabre MAGIC GROUP. Can Troy cooperate with two men who he isn't exactly seeing eye to eye with right now (an understatement, that)? Does he even want to?
The final tournament match will pit the HARDCORE SENSATION against the YAKUZ
A of the SHEETS FOUNDATION 3.0. Last week the Sensation ended the CWO careers of the DEADLY SINS, will they do the same to the YAKUZA this week?
The World Tag Team Championships will be on the line as well, as MASTER OF
DARKNESS DAMION joins NIGHT SLINGER to go against the BRUTE SQUAD in what should be a hard-hitting match indeed.
And, finally, as you saw on SCORCH, the World Championship will be on the line, as champion SANDMAN JR. seems to be picking fights with everybody, and this time it's with "the man" himself, FOOLKILLER! Will it be "Funkin'
Time" for the CWO's Hardcore Champion? All this and more on this week's FEVER! Don't miss it! http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/5403/
