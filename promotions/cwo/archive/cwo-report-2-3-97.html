CWO REPORT 2/3/97

3 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Monday, February 3, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. A very explosive CWO SHOWCASE yesterday, let's show you quickly the results. _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO SHOWCASE 2/2/97 Announcers: Kevin Rinaldi and Tamara Willcox Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson defeated Steve Keller by countout in 12:18 after Keller's former partner, Clark Gray, apparently mad with grief, attacked him with a chair and dropped him on a cinder block. Keller had to be rushed to the hospital, as his orbital socket was shattered. Keller will be out for about 10 weeks. Mondo Gecko flash. Mondo Gecko defeated Tiger Rogers in 17:06 after the cannonball. Benjamin Blake Bentley defeated the former Flyboy, now calling himself by his real name, Chris Greco, in 9:35 after an elbow drop, pulling the tights. Masher Maccabee flash, in which he seemed to be calling out Brent "The Ace" Alles. David "The Destroyer" Davis defeated "Lonestar" Mike Williams in 12:07 after a flying headbutt. The Big Cool defeated Revolution (Lafayette and Black Death) when Jim "Jazzman" Taylor pinned Black Death after a kneelift. Johnny Reb pinned Sin in 13:10 with an inside cradle. Dark Raven defeated Dark Raven in 16:04 after Johnny Reb DDT'ed Panther while the ref was out. In a non-title match, Television champion Brian "The Hammer" Williams defeated Sandman Jr. in 10:22 after the Keuka Smack. After the match, though, Williams was savagely attacked by Brent "The Ace" Alles. Alles delivered an Allesbreaker on to a chair to Williams, and Williams was taken away on a neckbreaker, suffering a neck sprain. He will be out around 6 weeks. Before the Light Heavyweight championship match, Bane entered the ring and made a very chilling threat towards the Deadly Sins. He then attacked Moses AND Talon, but was driven off by both of them and his old enemy, North American champion Sandman. Talon and Light Heavyweight Champion Moses wrestled to a double disqualification in 10:33. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: As a result of Brian "The Hammer" Williams' neck injury, CWO Commissioner "Texas Rose" Randy Johnson was forced to vacate the Television title. That title will now be put up for grabs in a 10-man battle royal at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II. More on that later. Now, though, we take you to a press conference held earlier this morning. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson is shown behind a podium as cameras flash.) "TEXAS ROSE" RANDY JOHNSON: Thank you very much, thank you, everybody, for comin' here today. It is mah deep regret ta announce that Brian "The Hammer" Williams has suffered a neck injury, fortunately not that serious, but one serious enough to keep him out for at least 6 weeks. Because here in the CWO we have a 30-day defend clause, and Williams will not be able to meet that, ah am forced to vacate the Television title. REPORTER #1: What will be done about the vacant Television title? JOHNSON: The title will be put up for grabs in a 10-man, over the top rope battle royal at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II. The participants in that royal will be announced later today on the CWO REPORT. REPORTER #2: Are you going to take any actions against Brent "The Ace" Alles? JOHNSON: Oh, yes. Brent "The Ace" Alles has been fined $5,000, as well as an additional fine covering Williams' hospital costs. Also, ah have suspended Mr. Alles for 11 days, and if he even sets foot in the ring, then ah reserve the raht to terminate him on the spot. REPORTER #3: What about Steve Keller? JOHNSON: Steve Keller unfortunately suffered a cracked orbital socket injury. Mr. Clark Gray has been fined $10,000 for that action, as well as coverage of the medical costs, and was going to be subjected to a lengthier suspension, possibly a termination, but at the request of one Mr. Christopher Greco, formerly known as Flyboy, ah have suspended him for 11 days as well, and now Mr. Greco will meet Mr. Gray at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II in a Scientific match, per Mr. Greco's request. REPORTER #4: What do you think is causing all of this lawlessness in the CWO right now? JOHNSON (angrily, pounding his fist on the table): ***dammit, ah know what you're implyin', mister! This league will have law and order, and ah will provide it! I will stick to my claims, and as I said, if Brent "The Ace" Alles, or Clark Gray for that matter, sticks their face even in an arena, then they may very well terminated from the CWO forever! And to all the Banes, all the Sandmans, all the Master of Darkness Damions, and all the rest of you that think that this is a "lawless land," well think again, because ah am the Commissioner, and ah will have ORDER! (Johnson, breathing hard, composes himself.) JOHNSON: That's all for now. Thank you very much. (A flurry of questions comes up as Johnson walks out along with other CWO officials.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: A very perturbed commissioner providing a press conference earlier today. As promised, we now have the complete ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II lineup for you, to be held on Friday, February 14, at the Cow Palace in San Francisco, CA. Card subject to change, of course, but this is the final tentative lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II (card subject to change) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Hile Troy vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Highlanders vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (MONSTERS OF THE RING): "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Sandman (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: The Big Cool vs. Bad Guys (champions) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (10-MAN BATTLE ROYAL): Panther Sandman Jr. Benjamin Blake Bentley Inferno Brent "The Ace" Alles Maximum Overdrive Sin Mondo Gecko Steel Baby Waldo LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Tiger Rogers vs. Moses (champion) 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: "Lonestar" Mike Williams and the Coldfront (champions) vs. Hellhound, Dark Raven, Cake Boy SCIENTIFIC MATCH: Chris Greco vs. Clark Gray VEGAS ODDS MATCH: Pit Bosses vs. Eternal Wanderers BARBED WIRE TOWEL MATCH: Master of Darkness Damion (w/Rebel Yell) vs. Johnny Reb Bane vs. Wrath Gold Kard vs. "Innovator of Violence" Miguel Sanchez Bloodbath vs. Ganglord EYE ON THE STORM: Savior vs. Masher Maccabee Deadly Sins vs. Mason-Dixon DARK MATCH: Battlecat vs. Talon SHOWCASE SPECIAL: ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE (SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 16) 1ST ANNUAL CWO BOWL 8-MAN ELIMINATION TOURNAMENT 8-MAN TAG MATCH A (elimination): Joe Eagle, Jason "Flash" Varga, Regulators vs. D. E. A. T. H. and Mercenaries 8-MAN TAG MATCH B (elimination): Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, Hoss and Little Joe vs. David and Goliath and Revolution WINNING MEMBERS OF MATCH A vs. WINNING MEMBERS OF MATCH B in an elimination match _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: So there you have it, 13 great matches on the PPV itself, 2 on the EYE ON THE STORM, and the 1st ever CWO BOWL 8-man elimination tournament. You definitely don't want to miss any of those three great events! However, the road to our next PPV is sure to be an exciting one as well, starting off with TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER tomorrow night in Providence, RI, at the Providence Civic Center! _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 2/4/97 PROVIDENCE, RI (Providence Civic Center) Gold Kard vs. Sandman Regulators vs. Highlanders Bloodbath vs. Bane Alliance (Tiger Rogers and Inferno) vs. Biblical Powers Cake Boy vs. unknown opponent The Big Cool vs. Hoss and Little Joe DEBUT: Masher Maccabee vs. Sin D. E. A. T. H. vs. Deadly Sins Johnny Reb vs. Hile Troy WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Eternal Wanderers vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now go to comments from CWO superstars, starting out with a response from Brent "The Ace" Alles. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is shown walking down a quiet country road, wearing jeans, boots, and a Syracuse jacket. The sky is gray, cloudy, and ominous.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: Well, well, well, so the big bad commissioner has slapped me with a fine and a suspension, has he? So be it. I did what I said I was going to do. Williams, why didn't you listen to me? Why didn't you listen to that pretty little girlfriend of yours? I warned you, I warned her, that if you stayed in this league, that I would not be responsible for what happens. (Alles walks a little, thinking.) ALLES: And yet I AM responsible for what happened, aren't I? What did it feel like, Williams? What did it feel like, your neck slamming down into the chair? What hurt worse... the chair... or the Allesbreaker? So as you're sitting in your hospital bed, Williams, with that pretty little girl of yours, I know that you will be thinking of me, of what I did, of what I can do, and what I will do, if you choose to come back. (Alles stops and leans up against a tree.) ALLES: You see, "Hammer," you do have a choice. If I were you, I'd stay the hell out of this league for the rest of your life. Marry that pretty little girl of yours, unless she sees the light as well and realizes what a loser you are. Become a hardware salesman, or a truck driver, or anything less dangerous than running into my path again. (Alles stares off into the distance for a moment.) ALLES (not looking back at the camera): This isn't fun and games, Williams. I see you and Schillo running around like a couple of morons, screaming "FIVE TO ONE!" and all that nonsense, kissing the girls and making them cry. Well, Williams, when I delivered the most powerful, punishing move in our sport to you, THAT was your wake-up call. This is reality. This is a place where people run around hitting each other with glass bottles, a place where a man goes into a hospital and rips out the life support of another wrestler. This isn't a circus, Williams, it isn't a pick-up bar, it is a hellhole warzone with no way out. (Alles chuckles quietly, looking down, his arms folded.) ALLES: You know, I used to complain so much about this league, how I wanted it to be the CM2WF all over again. But do you know what, Williams? I like it here. I LIKE IT HERE. And even though people are starting to count me out, saying that I'm slipping in the rankings, that I'm far away from ever being a World Champion, well, I say you go ahead and think that way, but that way I am always down in the cellars, always trying to get out, and I have sent a message to anyone and everyone in the CWO that I am not to be trifled with when I'm in that New York State of Mind, if you will. (Alles starts to walk again, as the breeze picks up a little, blowing his hair slightly out of place.) ALLES: And as for you, "Keuka Guy," don't be getting any funny ideas about revenge and justice. Those two things don't exist here in the CWO. (Alles holds his finger to his head, staring at the camera intently.) ALLES: You see, I see you, Schillo, I see you and Williams in my mind's eye, laughing at me, not thinking I'm a threat. Well, I am a threat, Wayne Schillo, and if you're smart, which I know you're not, you'll stay the hell out of my way, or I will give you a wake-up call just like I did Williams. Hell, maybe I'll do it anyway. This isn't fun and games, Wayne, you can run around trying to be "Most Loved," but I will have them inscribe that on your tombstone, because that is where you are headed if you mess with me. You may think you need to "avenge" your friend, Wayne, well I say to you what I have always said, "It is business... never personal." I don't give a damn about the World belt, I have shattered every dream that I have ever had, just to make me a stronger person. What are you going to do to me, Schillo? What are you going to do to a man who ripped out his own heart, put it on the ground, and stomped on it until it stopped beating? (Alles looks up at the gray, ominous sky.) ALLES (softly): What are you going to do? _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Brent "The Ace" Alles with a very dangerous conversation right there. In this newfound "lawless land" of the CWO, everything seems to be crashing down, and you may see an armageddon at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II. One of those explosions will be between Johnny Reb and Master of Darkness Damion. What more can you say about this feud? And now these two men go into a barbed wire "hellhole warzone," to quote Mr. Alles from a few moments ago, and they have to depend on the strong stomach of their seconds, General Lee and Rebel Yell, respectively, to not cost them the match. The tension has driven Johnny Reb almost to the brink of madness, as yesterday on Showcase he attacked the popular youngster Panther, only because Panther had criticized Johnny Reb's earlier actions this year. We take you now to that attack from yesterday's SHOWCASE. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Footage is shown from the 2/2/97 SHOWCASE, with Kevin Rinaldi and Tamara Willcox announcing.) KEVIN RINALDI: Panther drops the elbow onto Raven's chest!! Panther snaps on an armbar, trying to tear Raven's shoulder out of the socket!!! Raven nails Panther with his free hand! Raven kicking and stomping on the helpless Panther, clawing at his face!!!! Raven into the ropes... Panther with a backdrop!!! Raven taken by surprise and... oh no... Raven slammed into the ref after the backdrop and the ref is down!!!!! And now look at this! Johnny Reb hitting the ring, he DDT's Panther!!!! What is going on?!? Referee coming to, Johnny Reb leaving, Dark Raven rolling over for the cover, 1... 2... 3! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now take you to comments from Johnny Reb. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb and General Lee are in the interview area.) JOHNNY REB: Well Panthuh, y'all saw what happens when you call Johnny Reb out. Y'all say that you're for the fans now... well son, ah know bettuh than any that it takes more than words for a cat ta' change it's stripes. As far as that is concerned, y'all are either with me, or y'all are against me. Don't evuh make that mistake again. But that is anothuh issue for anothuh day, suh. Raht now, ah'm lookin' straight at Valentines Day. LEE: That's raht! Be warned Damion, at St. Valentine's Day Massacuh, you will pay. Y'all started this long ago, son. Next Friday, your chickens come home ta' roost. REB: You're mistake, Damion, was that y'all didn't get the job done. Ah've been through the Darkness, ah've been through the agony, and ah survived. Next Friday, y'all won't be that lucky. Next Friday, ah finish it. No more gold. No more Darkside. No more you. LEE: Damion, this man is your judge, jury, and executioner. Sentence to be carried out February 14th. Count your blessings as y'all count the days. Good day, suh. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: In these days of darkness here in the CWO, it's hard to find a ray of hope, but thankfully we do have young, fresh talent who are excited just about wrestling itself. It is good that these young men are here, because without them we might lose our own joy for this great sport. One of these young men is Mondo Gecko, and we take you to comments now from the energetic youngster from Oakland, CA. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Ahh, yeah, baby. Mondo Extreme action is coming your way and I can't wait as the Wild Ones get ready to achieve the dream as they try to win the tag team titles. But I am not here to talk about my competition, I am here to do two things: First, I want to offer our help to Mr. Keller and Mr. Greco, it seems two true class acts are having problems with all the wanna be tough guys here and I am sick of it, so Greco, me and Battlecat want to offer you a chance to win the six man tag championships. If you want the opportunity, get back to us. To the lovely Tamara Willcox, after seeing you in action (smiles sheepishly) I want to reward you by asking you to by my manager and help me defeat Moses! I need a beautiful, crafty, tough lady to guide my career and you seem like the woman to do so. I await you decision and I hope we can have a beautiful realtionship! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: It is not always when we can end our broadcast on a high note, thankfully, we were able to do so today. This is Johnny Starr, we'll see you tomorrow on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER.
