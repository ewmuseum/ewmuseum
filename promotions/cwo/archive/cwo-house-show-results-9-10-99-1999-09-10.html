House Show Results - 9/10/99

September 10, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 9/10/99 Thibodaux, LA (Civic Center) Kamikaze pinned Vegas Villain after the California Jam. Time of match: 1:11. Matsuhiro Torunaka pinned Marc 'Compton' Daniels after the springboard legdrop. Time of match: 6:30.
Beauregard Boys defeated Darknight Express when Ray Beauregard pinned Demolition after the senton. Time of match: 6:51. Brian 'The King' Derks pinned 'Roast Beef' Jason Allanson after the reversed rolling cradle. Time of match: 7:18.
Creatures of the Night defeated World Tag Team Champions Reb and Fantastic in a non-title match when Night Crawler pinned 'Irresistible' Eddie
Fantastic after the flying elbow drop. Time of match: 18:06. North American Champion Black Jack Hollister pinned Deliverance in a non-title match after the Texas Stampede. Time of match: 9:23.
Union Caine pinned Gold Kard after the Iconoclast. Time of match: 8:16. Television Champion 'The Main Event' Steve Davis defeated Foolkiller by disqualification when Foolkiller hit Davis with a chair in the ring. Time of match: 19:40. Gold Kard attacked Foolkiller after the match but
Foolkiller delivered a Thunderfunk to him, sending the crowd home happy.
