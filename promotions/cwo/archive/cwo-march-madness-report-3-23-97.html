Time of match: 5:31 Wrath pinned Pit Boss #2 after the Repentence Rack. Time of match: 2:53 Arlon Flaym pinned Gavin McCloud after the cradle. Time of match: 6:28 CWO North American champion Goliath pinned Sandman Jr. after the power bomb. Time of match: 8:46. Johnny Reb made Baby Waldo submit to the Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 11:58 Vlad the Impaler pinned Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker after the Long Kiss Goodnight (bite to the neck). Time of match: 11:31 Exile made Bloodbath submit to the Boston crab. Time of match: 10:30 Moses defeated Stonewall Mason by countout. Time of match: 4:43 RIGHT REGIONAL - ROUND 1 - ATLANTIC CITY, NJ - 3/20/97 'Monster' Rich Clark pinned Wildfire after the Choke Slam. Time of match: 10:18 Kurt 'Thrill from the Chill' Gerstung pinned Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor after the moonsault. Time of match: 5:15 Panther pinned Regulator Fire after the bridge pincover. Time of match: 11:11 Night Slinger pinned 'Mr. Extreme' Chris Ohloff after the slingshot suplex. Time of match: 9:10 The Focus defeated Benjamin Blake Bentley by reversed decision. Bentley pinned the Focus after the Nobility with his feet on the ropes, and another referee informed the match's referee of the occurence, which led to the reversed decision. Time of match: 7:06 Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson defeated Bishop by disqualification after Bishop threw fire at him. Time of match: 8:45 'Lonestar' Mike Williams pinned Exodus after the inside cradle. Time of match: 10:09 Gold Kard pinned Beauregard Dixon after Die Flieder Kross (flying somersault splash). Time of match: 15:03 DOWN REGIONAL - ROUND 1 - DALLAS, TX - 3/21/97 CWO Light Heavyweight champion Tiger Rogers pinned Battlecat after the Inverted Powerbomb. Time of match: 7:55 Pit Boss #1 pinned Maximum Overdrive after the neckbreaker. Time of match: 7:34 Inferno pinned CWO Television champion Mondo Gecko after the Inferno's Blaze (power bomb) and outside interference by Masher Maccabee. Time of match: 9:33 David 'The Destroyer' Davis pinned 'Master of Darkness' Damion after the Destroyer Dropper (flip off the top rope) and outside interference by Bishop. Time of match: 3:03 Talon pinned Dark Raven after the Jekyll Jump (top rope Frankensteiner). Time of match: 4:47 Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned Korperal Punishment after the Flash Flood. Time of match: 19 : 53 Hellhound pinned Sandman after the leg drop. Time of match: 6 : 32 Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Pride after the dropkick. Time of match: 6 : 55 LEFT REGIONAL - ROUND 1 - LOS ANGELES, CA - 3/22/97 Wayne 'Keuka Guy' Schillo pinned Warren after the kick to the head. Time of match: 7:19 Chris Greco pinned Jumbo Jenkins after the Flying Bodypress. Time of match: 6:47 Conor McCloud pinned Regulator Ice after the kneelift. Time of match: 13:57 Brad 'The Man' Couchman pinned Night Crawler after the Piledriver. Time of match: 7:44 Clark Gray pinned Serpentmaster after the cradle. Time of match: 14:03 Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned Kaptain Krunch after the rollup. Time of match: 17:27 Joe Eagle pinned Ganglord after the Eagle Shock. STARR: That's it for the CWO MARCH MADNESS REPORT, but before we go, we want to take just a brief look at the ongoing "Brotherhood of the Rose" situation. Recently, the Regulators received a "gift" from this mysterious organization, we now have comments from them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Regulators are in the interview area.) REGULATOR FIRE: To the Brotherhood of the Rose or whoever you guys are who sent us the present, we want to make something clear. If you guys want our help in ridding the CWO from all these rulebreaking punks, like Gold Kard, Baby Waldo, Masher Maccabee, and Bane, that is fine with us. We came here with two things in mind: to win the tag team titles and to enforce law and order in the CWO. So if you guys want our help, fine. However, if you guys try to set us up and sneak attack us, you guys will pay... DEARLY! REGULATOR ICE: To the people who gave us the gift, thanks! If you guys want our assistance in cleaning up the CWO, fine, but we aren't hired goons, and if you try to double cross us we will beat you guys into a pulp. But if all you guys want is an alliance to get rid of the trash in the CWO then we are all for it and would love to be an assistance! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
