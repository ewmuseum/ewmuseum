CWO REPORT 12-2-97 (fwd)

2 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey sitting at the CWO REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Hello everybody and welcome back to a special edition of the CWO REPORT. I hope that everybody had a wonderful Thanksgiving last week. The results of November Pain are still etched in a lot of people's minds, and several superstars of the CWO have been making requests to have interviews done regarding performances at November Pain. As you'll remember, November Pain was held just over a week ago at Wembley Stadium. The CWO European tour was a total success, and the CWO has promised to look further into expanding our opportunities overseas. Every show was a sellout and there were people lined up for several blocks, trying to get some tickets to witness one of the CWO's greatest PPV's. After taking a much deserved break, the CWO will be in action tonight for FEVER. Here is the scheduled lineup. TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 12/2/97 TAMPA BAY, FL HOUR #1: Highlanders vs. Wild Cards NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: The Focus vs. Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil (champion) Steve Keller and Tommy Lightning vs. Mercenaries HOUR #2: Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Painmaster 8-MAN TAG ACTION: The Unit vs. Empire TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Wildfire (champion) vs. Master of Darkness Damion WASHEY: As I mentioned in the top of the broadcast, we have several interviews from the stars of the CWO. Let's get to some comments made earlier by Masashi 'Lightning' Lanther. ___________________________________________________________________ [The camera opens on Masashi 'Lightning' Lanther standing in front of a CWO banner...] Masashi Lanther: The Carpenter Cup is behind us, and I for one am glad. Tsutomu Kobayashi is an up and coming rookie, but he wasn't experienced enough for us to get through the first round. It's just as well, because I'd prefer to wrestle singles. So now, on December 7th, I face Southern Dynamite, former Lightheavyweight champion. Dyanamite, I was truly hoping that our match would be for the title, but Jason Varga regained that belt from you. Pity. I had truly hoped my time had at last arrived. [sighs] Masashi Lanther: Dynamite, I am going into this match with the intentions of winning. I know you will too. If I defeat you, then maybe, just maybe, the CWO will grant me another title shot against Varga. Last time, he and I went to a draw. This time, I know I can defeat him. So Dynamite, you are all that stands in my way at the moment. I will fight fair, but don't expect me to show you mercy __________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up we will hear from the tag team from the SJPW that has made a big impact in the CWO over the last few weeks. Here are the Jade Dragons. __________________________________________________________________ [The camera opens on the Jade Dragons looking at a video tape of their loss to Foolkiller/Union Caine, and then watching them defeat the Highlanders. They turn off the tape, and look at each other dejectedly] Kenji Ishida: Those two men proved to be the better team. We were too cocky after defeating the Steiner Brothers! Shinji Usami: At least the Highlanders didn't get passed them either. Kenji Ishida: Small consolation. Shinji Usami: Kenji! Don't let this loss get you down. CWO has a tough tag team division. We are young, untested. Remember that loss to the Brothers of God in K-Pro? Kenji Ishida: How can I forget? Our only match, and we were defeated! Shinji Usami: Yet you kept your spirits up when we arrived in CWO. It's our first loss here. We still hold a win over the Highlanders, and they are former 3 time CWO world tag team champions! Kenji Ishida: You have a point there Shinji. Shinji Usami: Exactly! Our focus is on the Highlanders. Remember what they did to you and Kikuchi-san. They attacked the two of you after you offered them your hands in friendship! Then they dared to claim that you got in their way! Kenji Ishida: Lies! They knew exactly what they were doing! They can make all the protestations of innocence that they want! They declared war on the CWO, NOT SJPW! I don't want their aplogies; I want REVENGE! Shinji Usami: calm down Kenji! I'm on your side, remember? We'll get a match with them. Somehow. Even if we have to beat up some officials like everyone else does, we'll face them in the ring again. And when we do, they WILL lose, just like they did the last time! Kenji Ishida: I know they will. We ARE the better tag team. Kikuchi-san was an excellant partner, but you and I together are more than a match for the Highlanders. We will see justice done. A debt must be paid, and it must be paid.....IN BLOOD! [The camera fades out as Shinji once again attempts to calm his tag team partner down] _____________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up we will hear comments from "The Brazillian Lover" Carlos Maia. _____________________________________________________________ {"The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia is seen in a hospital, flirting with a beautiful nurse. He appears to be ok, without any injuries. When he notices the camera, he whispers in nurse's ear. Joe Root appears with a huge smile in his face.} "THE BRAZILIAN LOVER" CARLOS MAIA: Ahh... How it was the check-up, Joe? JOE ROOT: Well, the doctor said that I was in great shape. Just asked for moderate with the drinks... "THE BRAZILIAN LOVER" CARLOS MAIA: so let's go to the next bar celebrate! {Both men laugh at the tirade.} "THE BRAZILIAN LOVER" CARLOS MAIA: Heh, that was funny... All right, time to check my business... What do I have for this week? JOE ROOT: Well, a match against Jason "Flash" Varga... "THE BRAZILIAN LOVER" CARLOS MAIA: Another match with him... Nothing that was not planned... Hehehe... Southern Dynamite must be still looking to the mirror and remembering that shot... Dynamite, let's remember HOW you got the belt... Remember? I was scheduled for a match against Schillo in Terror Night PPV, but I had to face you in Showcase some hours before... JOE ROOT: Almost 7 hours, to be exact. "THE BRAZILIAN LOVER" CARLOS MAIA: Yeah! At some point, Schillo came down and got my attention... Then you nailed me from BEHIND!! Sooooo... I paid with the same treatment. And I'll bet you're still ugly! {He laughs without control} JOE ROOT: Ok, calm down man... {He turns to face the camera} Mr. Varga... Next Showcase you will face this man again {points to Maia rolling on the floor laughing on loud} *sigh* and this time, he will get your belt 1-2-3 without powder in your eyes... {Maia keeps laughing until some nurses and a doctor surround him.} DOCTOR: Ok, let's get him to a room! He is having an attack! Get him, move! every second counts! JOE ROOT: Hey!! Doctor, wait a minute, you can't... {The scene fades as the nurses put Maia on a stretcher and goes inside the building, Joe Root running trying to stop them.} _______________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our next comments come from the Mercenaries. _______________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries a few days after November Pain. They are back in their training complex, watching tapes of their matches during the Wayne Carpenter Tournament. When they notice the camera, they turn to speak.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: The Mercenaries were on their way to win that whole thing. One million dollars. Hell, they had the check already made out to us. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: It sure sent a panic through the CWO suits when they found out that we had been knocked out of the tournament. But there are three guys I figure that should really be panicking right about now. That's the Big Cool and Joe Eagle. Now I know that you guys were probably all liquored up when you came barging into our locker rooms and somehow managed to make us miss our next tournament match, knocking us out of our chance to win that money. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: But all you did is let the two of us know that there is still a job to be done. The Big Cool should have been sitting at home on their big butts during that tournament, drowning their sorrows in a couple gallons of ice cream. But then, I guess "Jazzman" Taylor must have eaten the hospital out of all the ice cream they had during his stay. (The Mercenaries laugh, and point over to the remains of the glass bottle that was used to stab Taylor. It has now been bronzed, and is hanging on their wall.) KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: You guys want some more of what you got a few weeks ago? Sounds good to us. We have a LOT more where that came from. And as for Joe Eagle, you should have kept your nose out of this one, moron. The Mercenaries will make sure that we take a couple thousands of those dollars that we lost out of your ass, too. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Big Cool, you're stupid for wanting to come back after what we did to you. Smarter people would have taken our advice and retired a long time ago. Joe Eagle, you're just as stupid for wanting to get invovled in the war you can't win. When we get rid of the Big Cool once and for all, who's going to help you? You think we can't deal with you, you oversized goon? Look up "Monster" Rich Clark and ask him what we did to him. He learned his lesson, just like you are going to. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: And that lesson is, when THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD sends a message, you don't question it. You just do it. ______________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our next message is from Sandman Jr. ______________________________________________________________ [Sandman Jr] Johnny Reb? Who gives a **** about Johnny Reb right now? You want to hear about him first, fine. Johnny Reb had his ass and his belt saved by that piece of **** Damion. That belt was as good as mine, you all saw that. But it was robbed. Like my woman.... DAMION! You claim to have to powers of the dark side with you... pal, you don't know power OR dark sides until you messed with Sandman Jr... I want Kellen back, and I'll kill you to get her. Not just beat you, not pinning or making you submit. No, I'm gonna break every single bone in your body. But first you'll be screaming where Kellen is... and I swear to God, if you've done anything to hurt her in any way... not only will I kill you, but I'll kill everyone else in that freak show of yours.... so give back Kellen RIGHT NOW, or you'll be hurt more than you ever thought ****ing possible! _______________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up we have comments from the first tag team to simutaneously hold both the CWO North American Tag Titles and the CWO World Titles, the Deadly Sins. _______________________________________________________________ MELINN: Newton Bros. pounding on the Deadly Sins in the corner... and they whip them into each other! Howard Newton clotheslines Sloth out of the ring! Howard Newton outside the ring now, taking it to Sloth, and Steve Newton with a rolling reverse cradle... wait a minute... Pride rolls through! He's got the tights! He's got the tights! 1... 2... 3! THOMAS: Yes! TONY THE K: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of this contest in 19:16, and NEW CWO WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS AND STILL CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, the Deadly Sins! <The Deadly Sins in the locker room, Pride and Sloth with the WORLD Tag belts on their shoulders, Wrath and Lust with the North American belts on theirs> [Pride] We told you it was going to be done, and hot damn, we did it! The Deadly Sins, the new and UNDISPUTED CHAMPIONS OF THE WORLD! Strawberry and Fig Newton stood no chance, and they got clobbered. In a clean pin, I might add. Now, everyone wants a shot. You got Strawberry and Fig who'll want a rematch. The Higlanders. The Mercernaries. The Wild Ones. Hell, even the Myers and Fulcrum are gonna want these. But they're OURS, and ain't NO ONE gonna take 'em from us! [Sloth] Then there's the Wild Cards... those losers we saw at the Tournament... yeah, the Mercs probably would've beaten you anyway, but we decided it'd be more fun to whip your asses anyway. Just a little something to remind you to NEVER EVER mess with the Deadly Sins, cause it's just a VERY VERY bad thing [Wrath] Focus, you probably think you're pretty hot about now... you beat me in the middle of the ring, 1-2-3... but how did you get me in the cradle? BY POKING ME IN THE EYES!!! That's a "real man"? I don't think so! You're just a punk little cheater, Focus. I hate punk little cheaters, and I hated you before, so now I REALLY want to break your back. You've felt the Rack several times... well, the next time will be the last time, Focus. Cause after that, your career will be over. Wildfire, you have the TV Belt that belongs to me. After I destroy The Focus, you're next. And Lord Hile Troy, I got you at Showcase. You're a legend. But legends are both old, and get replaced. I'm replacing you, Troy. I'm the resident bad-ass in the CWO. You got a problem, you're gonna be dealt with. [Lust] Dallas... I guess you like it rough... I'm into that too... see you soon, sweetheart... and as for you, Tamara Wilcox... while I'd LOVE to roll around with you all over a ring, I don't see it happening any time soon. Unless you ROYALLY piss me off, that is. ______________________________________________________________ WASHEY: While on the subject of the Sins, we here at the REPORT have come across some footage shot during the Wayne Carpenter Tournament. Here is some footage that we came across of the Unit, prior to the Newton Brothers/Deadly Sins title vs. title match. ______________________________________________________________ (The camera fades in within the UNIT's locker room. "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday and Skyhawk both lie on benches, wearing only towels and looking dejected. As Johnny B. Goode paces the floor, CWO World Tag champs Steve and Howard Newton sit across the room, watching. Both men wear jean shorts and "Most Dangerous Men In Wrestling" T-shirts, and Goode wears jean shorts and a "Pray For Your Sins" T-shirt. Several cases of Guiness beer cans litter the floor, reflecting the UNIT's reputation for partying.) GOODE: I do not believe this! How could we possibly lose in the first round of that tournament? And to the Creatures of the Night, nontheless? How? HOLIDAY: No excuses, Johnny. We got sloppy. GOODE: Damn right you got sloppy! This is not the way I trained you guys! Whatever happened to being a UNIT, huh? Watching each other's back? Geez, Sean, there was nothing stopping you from breaking that pinfall! SKYHAWK: Lay off him, Johnny! I was just as surprised as he was. That's the first time I haven't been able to kick out of a leg grab, okay? So we got upset! Life goes on! GOODE: That's exactly the attitude I'm talking about! If you guys can't act as a team out there, how do you expect to act like a team when you face Empire this upcoming Tuesday? S.NEWTON: Don't worry about Empire, Johnny! God, calm down! You're gonna have a heart attack here! (Johnny B. Goode points his finger at Steve Newton.) GOODE: Don't even start, Steve! You and Howard haven't been wrestling your best lately, either! Sure, you whipped the Pups of War's asses last Tuesday. But you guys were incredibly sloppy doing it! H.NEWTON: What is your problem, John? We've beat everyone so far that they've thrown at us! And even if...IF...we were sloppy, we still dominated them and won, right? S.NEWTON: Dominated, my ass! We nailed those suckers! (The Newton Brothers share a high-five.) GOODE: You guys just don't get it, do you? Not one of you four get it! I've spent the last couple years of my life building you guys up to where you are right now! I've watched you guys rise to the top of the wrestling world! Look at the titles we've won! Look at all the awards! How did you get them? SKYHAWK: By being the best! GOODE: And how did you get to be the best? HOLIDAY: By listening to you. (Johnny B. Goode suddenly explodes and slams a locker over onto its side.) GOODE: SO WHY THE HELL AREN'T YOU LISTENING TO ME NOW? Sean, Skyhawk, you guys are incredible singles competitors. As a tag team, you guys could be the best non-regulatory team in the world! And you two... (Johnny turns back to the Newton brothers.) GOODE: ...you two are without a doubt, the greatest tag team in the world today! You both are also outstanding singles wrestlers. But the whole problem here, boys, is that you've all become complacent! Sean and 'Hawk already paid the price by being kicked out of the tournament. If you two don't get your act together, you're gonna get beat by the Sins at NOVEMBER PAIN. S.NEWTON: C'mon, Johnny, who cares if they're the CWO North American champs? Like Dad always says, "First runner-up is still first-place loser." We've kicked their asses before, we'll do it again, and when we do, we'll be the first team in CWO history to hold both tag titles at the same time! H.NEWTON: So forget the Sins, already, okay? They're as good as pinned! Let's look ahead to Tuesday, okay? We got Empire. Big whoop. We've beat the Pups, Sean ain't gonna have any more trouble with Union Caine as long Krepps keeps the cameramen off the ring apron, and Foolkiller? SKYHAWK: Aptly named, but we ain't fools. He's a big man, but I'm better. So, no, Johnny, we don't expect any trouble there, either. As a matter of fact, I'm gonna do something here I've never done before, okay? I'm gonna guarantee you a victory. Okay? We've never done that before, but this Tuesday, you're gonna see that we know what we're doing. We will win that match. GOODE: I can see that you gentlemen have made up your minds. For right or wrong, I guess you'll have to learn your own way from here on out, huh? HOLIDAY: Aw, c'mon, Johnny, don't be like that. We still need you with us. Now, c'mon, what do you say? Huh? Here, have a beer! (Sean Holiday tosses Johnny B. Goode a beer as the rest of the UNIT open new ones. As the cameraman discreetly backs out of the locker room, we can see that Johnny has still not opened his.) _________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our next and last interview comes from......well, I'll let you judge for yourselves. _________________________________________________________________ (A small dark figure is in a dark mysterious cave, the figure removes its hood to reveal the long golden brown hair of a woman) Mysterious Woman:Now that the soul of Bloodbath is laid to rest the CWO Television Title now truely belongs to the dark side. Belongs to the new phenom of the CWO... (Wildfire emerges from the shadows, raising his arms in the air as a dim blood red light shines opon him) Mysterious Woman:Driven to silence he has now laid to rest his one best friend, but now he has an even bigger task... (Wildfire lifts his bat and points its at the camera) Mysterious Woman:Master of Darkness Damion, you no not the real power of the dark side and Wildfire will destroy you as he did Bloodbath, long live the night... (the camera fades out as the mysterious bells begin to toll) __________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody on the next REPORT! (fade to closing credits.)
