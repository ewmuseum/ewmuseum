SCORCH 3/7/99 - Results

March 9, 1999

SCORCH 3/7/99 - RESULTS LIVE FROM: Austin, TX 1999 CWO MARCH MADNESS TOURNAMENT SOUTH DIVISION - ROUND 1 HOUR #1: The show opened with a horrifying sight, as former CWO announcer/manager
(and now wife of Mondo Gecko) Tamara Wilcox was found by the Outlaw Riders, drenched in blood with her hair cut. The Dark Side, of course, was responsible. The Outlaws vowed vengeance.
Secret Agent Super Dragon pinned Vegas Villain after the Sushi Bar. Time of match: 7:58. Iceman X and Warhawk wrestled to a double countout. The Riders were shown beating up the Lunatic Fringe and “Airborne” Mark
Fulcrum back in the locker room.
'Mr. Technical' Cooper Young defeated 'Double D' Damian Dagger by disqualification. Time of match: 6:59.
Sandman won his match over fellow Dark Side member Night Slinger as Night
Slinger forfeited to him. In a joke afterwards, Kent Alles brought down
Brent "The Ass" Alles (not the real thing, but a cruel mockery) to quickly get pinned by Sandman to "justify" him advancing in the tournament.
HOUR #2: Lord Hile Troy pinned Korperal Punishment after the Viledriver. Time of match: 9:54. An interview with Jeb, cousin of “Dragonfire” Rich Johnson. 'Assassin 64' Sean Holiday pinned Brian 'The King' Derks after the Permanent
Vacation. Time of match: 7:23.
“Dragonfire” Rich Johnson had an interview, basically saying that the CWO had to band together to fight the Dark Side.
Gordy and Kent Alles came down and replaced Garfield Lewis and Leroy Brown as the announcers.
Vladimir Popalov pinned Southern Dynamite after the Russian Sickle. Time of match: 1:04. Australian Assassin defeated Diamondback Chris Myers by forfeit when Myers failed to show up for their match. After the match Gordy and Kent Alles celebrated with the Assassin and his sometimes partner Vegas Villain with a six pack of Fosters’ beer.
