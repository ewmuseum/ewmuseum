CWO REPORT 4/27/97

27 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Sunday, April 27, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Omaha, NE, let's look at what went down at that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/26/97 Omaha, NE Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Keuka Smack (ear ringer and an uppercut). Time of match: 10:17. Wild Side defeated Regulators when Wildfire pinned Regulator Ice after the Firebawl (backflip moonsault). Time of match: 16:58. Joe Eagle pinned Mondo Gecko after the Eagle Shock (tiger driver). Time of match: 10:47. Ganglord pinned Tommy Lightning after the running elbow. Time of match: 10:05. Da Hood Rats defeated Mason-Dixon when Warren pinned Stonewall Mason after the Flying Elbowdrop. Time of match: 17:40. Johnny Reb pinned Gold Kard after applying the Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 11:54. World Tag Team Champions the Pit Bosses defeated Highlanders when Pit Boss #1 pinned Conor McCloud after the Spike Piledriver. Time of match: 11:08. Unholy Alliance (Dark Raven, Serpentmaster, Vlad the Impaler) defeated College Guys (Jason "Flash" Varga, Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, and Brian "The Hammer" Williams) when Dark Raven pinned Brian 'The Hammer' Williams after the Death From Above flying dropkick. Time of match: 19:17. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We will have the MAY-HEM control center for you in just a minute, but first, let's take a look at the lineup for SHOWCASE, coming out later today! _______________________________________________________________________________ SHOWCASE 4/27/97 HOUR #1: Aces (Chris Greco, Tommy Lightning, Steve Keller) vs. Unholy Alliance (Vlad the Impaler, Serpentmaster, Bishop) Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles (w/Amanda Bloom) Creatures of the Night (w/Master of Darkness Damion) vs. Mercenaries (w/Maximum Overdrive) HOUR #2: The Focus vs. Bane Deadly Sins vs. Eternal Wanderers (w/Lord Guru) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Conor McCloud (w/Gavin McCloud) vs. Tiger Rogers (w/Jimbo Jones) (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny as the MAY-HEM graphic comes up.) STARR: All right, here we go with your MAY-HEM CONTROL CENTER, we are only one week away from the second annual happening of this great event. MAY-HEM 60 MAN BATTLE ROYAL STARR: Of course, the focus is on the 60 man, two-ring battle royal, although there are some unusual rules. Two men start out in each ring, and then every two minutes after that another man is added to each ring. The object, of course, is to throw your opponent over the top rope and on to the floor. Not only will every CWO superstar not involved with a match on the card be involved, but there will also be some "wild cards," including Super Japan's Matsuhiro Torunaka and a few other surprises. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Bane vs. "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Goliath STARR: However, in the big main event, the World title, currently held up, will be decided between three of the "Monsters of the Ring," 7'4" "Monster" Rich Clark, 7' Goliath, and the merciless, soulless Bane. One of the two rings will be surrounded by a cage, two of the men will start in there. The object will be for one of the men in the cage to beat their opponent so senseless, that they will be able to be handcuffed to the cage. However, after escaping the cage, the escapee must then fight the other man in the other ring, and the winner of that will be declared the uncontested CWO champion. Should be a great one. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Highlanders vs. Pit Bosses (champions) STARR: Although rumors abound of dissension in the Pit Bosses' ranks, yesterday on the REPORT they seemed to indicate everything was OK. However, they will face a very tough challenge in the Highlanders, as this is their second attempt at taking the World Tag Team Championship belts on a CWO PPV. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mercenaries vs. Bad Guys (champions) STARR: Love them or hate them, the Bad Guys have had a virtual lock on the North American Tag Team Championships these past few months. However, the Mercenaries, who have been on an impressive winning streak these past few months, definitely have a chance to become the new champions at MAY-HEM. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Johnny Reb vs. Inferno (champion) STARR: This was originally supposed to be Johnny Reb vs. Masher Maccabee, but Inferno shocked the wrestling world by taking the title from Maccabee, and then successfully defending it in a rematch. So, Johnny Reb now takes on the dangerous Inferno, backed up by the Alliance. Johnny Reb currently holds the World 6-Man Tag Team Championship along with Mason-Dixon, but you can be sure that adding the Television Championship to his resume would be a very good thing to Johnny Reb indeed. TITLE VS. TITLE Light Heavyweight Champion Tiger Rogers vs. North American Champion Jason "Flash" Varga STARR: Another Alliance member involved in a big match at CWO MAY-HEM will be Tiger Rogers, who seeks to avenge the Alliance against Jason "Flash" Varga, who took the North American championship from Goliath only a few weeks ago. Since Varga is also a light heavyweight, both titles are on the line, and it is very feasible that one of the two men could walk out with both titles around their waists. EYE ON THE STORM: LOTTERY TAG TEAM MATCH STARR: Besides those great matches, there will be one more match on the EYE ON THE STORM broadcast, and this match will be very important to the outcome of MAY-HEM indeed. Two teams will be chosen randomly from the roster of men involved in MAY-HEM, and then will fight in a tag team contest on EYE ON THE STORM. The winners of the match will be the last men to come down to the ring, and the losers will be the first ones in the ring! As you can see, this match has very important consequences, and it can be friend against friend, foe against foe, just like the MAY-HEM battle royal itself. You can't afford to miss this great PPV, only one week away, in the Molson Centre in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. One of the men who will be involved in the MAY-HEM battle royal is Mondo Gecko, and he has some comments. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: MAY-HEM is right around the corner and I along with the rest of the Wild Bunch have entered our names into the 60 man, two ring battle royal. In one night I get to kill two birds with one stone. Not only do I get to beat up Brent "Loser" Alles and GKI, but I will survive 59 other men and win the shot for the World title. That has been the ultimate dream for me ever since as a little kid I saw Randy Savage win the title at Wrestlemania IV. Now, I will be one of the smallest competitors around in the battle royal but I will still win the shot, I GUARANTEE IT. Gold Kard, first off, take some public speaking lessons because nobody can understand you. Bottom line is, I have had enough of your racist @$$ in the CWO and you and GKI are stinking up the joint! So now, not only are you at war with Moses, The Alliance, & Johnny Reb, but you have a war with ME! I am going to get rid of the GKI if it is the last thing I do. Which leads me to my next victim, Brent "The Ace" Alles and his little witch, Amanda Bloom. You two are nothing but street trash who have no care for anything you do. Alles, you need to resort to mind games to be competitive in this league and I am sick of it. The only reason you win matches is because none of the wrestlers in the CWO want to hit a lady. However, my girlfriend and manager Tamara Wilcox will knock you into the next century, Amanda, if you try anything during our matches. Alles, you think you are so bad and mean but what is going to happen when you realize that your mind games won't work on me. GET READY, CWO, FOR MONDO EXTREME ACTION, DIG IT, BABY! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: The Bad Guys will be going up against the Mercenaries at MAY-HEM, but they've got a lot of other teams on their tail, including the Wild Side. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire is at his home in Venice Beach, CA, with the lovely Miss Amber. Bloodbath is nowhere to be found) WILDFIRE (unexpectedly calm): Regulators, you guys are one of the greatest teams in the CWO and it will be a pleasure to face you, but just like you have your quest to the CWO World Tag Titles, we also have a quest.... (Suddenly, Wildfire's expression changes and becomes intense.) WILDFIRE (intense): That's where the Bad Guys come in! You have something that belongs to us! Those North American Tag Titles! As we all saw in Topeka, KS, we have the support of the fans! We're gonna convince the CWO Championship Committee that we deserve another match to gain those belts, one way or another! Next week on Tuesday Night Fever our voices will finally be heard! Tell 'em, Bloodbath.... (The room is DEAD silent, the lights go off, and then there is a faint red light showing the figure of Bloodbath.) BLOODBATH (dark and mysterious): Regulators, we are also looking forward to facing you! But you will find out that we are not "a walk in the park!" Bad Guys, you still choosing to hide from us is only adding fuel to the fire! And after we're through with you, the only thing you're gonna feel is the fiery depths of HELL! Rest in peace! (Thunder sounds and lighting strikes as the camera fades out to the mysterious bells.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Both of those two men will also be in the big MAY-HEM battle royal. Finally today, just added to the list of names that will be in the MAY-HEM battle royal is this man: "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Somewhere in the slums of Chicago a figure of a man steps out of the shadows.) MYSTERIOUS MAN: My name is "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher and I am here in the CWO for one reason! To kick a little @$$! I don't care who I have to face or who I have to beat up but I will be the CWO World Champion! (Deutscher steps out of the shadows into a dim street light.) DEUTSCHER: I have a little message for you, Bane! I have been watching you for some time! You talk about facing your bane, that it will destroy you! You think you strike fear in me, well guess again! I have been waiting a long time to whip your @$$ and now you or anybody else is not gonna stop me! (Deutscher gets a little grin on his face.) DEUTSCHER: And as for the fans of the CWO, if you don't like my style then you can kiss my @$$, I'm not here to please you, I'm here to get the job done! And I'm starting with you, Bane, so watch out, your BANE has arrived.... (The street light dims as the camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Before we leave, let me say that you can find the complete lineup for MAY-HEM on the CWO homepage, at < http://web.syr.edu/~bgalles/cwo.html > STARR: That's it for today, don't forget to watch SHOWCASE, coming out later today, and we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
