4/10/99 House Show Results

April 11, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 4/10/99 Sioux City, IA (Municipal Auditorium) Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned Night Crawler after the rollup. Time of match: 18:06. Newton Bros. defeated Bad Company when Howard Newton pinned 'The Avenger' Jesse Ewiak after the backbreaker. Time of match: 11:54. Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky pinned Television Champion Vladimir Popalov in a non-title match after the springboard splash. Time of match: 15:29. Night Slinger pinned Lord Hile Troy after the Lights Out avalanche and outside interference by Brent "The Ace" Alles. Time of match: 3:53.
CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Black Jack Hollister defeated Fergus 'Whirling Scottish Devil' McTavish by disqualification when the Sheets Foundation attack. Time of match: 8:40.
Fergus 'Whirling Scottish Devil' McTavish retained the CWO North American
Champion title. Black Jack Hollister was injured during the match.
Mondo Gecko pinned Cris Marcus after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 9:10. Darkness made 'Assassin 64' Sean Holiday submit to the Blackout. Time of match: 13:06. Lord Hile Troy came down to make the save!
