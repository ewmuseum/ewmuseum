CWO REPORT 12/13/96

13 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
_______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on MOD Damion.) MOD Damion: Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, did you see the match last night? I took that big moron "Big" Rich Clark in a Four Corners of Death Match and tonight I will destroy you once again and show you that I am the one true ruler of this fed and only I should be the one wearing the World Championship belt. As for your new found friends, they had better keep their noses out of my business, but if they don't, I will have the Dark Side and all my pets ready to come to the ring and destroy them too. I will see you in the ring when I continue to drag you deeper into my total DARKNESS and my "forever eternal NIGHT." LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Friday, December 13, 1996. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the superstars of the CWO were in Cincinnati, OH, let's look at the results of that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT THURSDAY, DECEMBER 12, 1996 CINCINNATI, OH Johnny Reb vs. Talon Johnny Reb made Talon submit to the Texas Cloverleaf in 12:53. Beauregard Dixon vs. Hellhound Beauregard Dixon pinned Hellhound in 16:17. After the match Dixon was attacked by Dark Raven, with Johnny Reb making the save. Dark Raven, in his madness, bit the referee John Myering and nailed ring announcer Tony the K with a chair. Deadly Sins vs. Blood Inc. Blood Inc. defeated Deadly Sins in 21:10 when Steel pinned Pride. Sandman Jr. vs. Dark Raven Dark Raven pinned Sandman Jr. in 15:42 after the Death From Above flying dropkick. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wildfire vs. Moses (champion) Moses pinned Wildfire in 13:44 after outside interference by Ganglord. Moses was attacked before the match by Dark Raven. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (STREET FIGHT): The Bad Guys vs. The Pit Bosses (champions) Bad Guys defeated the Pit Bosses in 15:17, winning the titles when Joe "The Mean Guy" Rucker pinned Pit Boss #1. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. MOD Damion (champion) Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo was accompanied by the members of Doomsday (Maximum Overdrive, Baby Waldo, and the Mercenaries), and Master of Darkness Damion was accompanied by the Dark Side (Dark Raven, Hellhound, and the Creatures of the Night), so this match turned into a de facto lumberjack match of sorts. In a hard-fought match, Master of Darkness Damion pinned Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo in 18:18 after the Darkness Falls tombstone piledriver. As the Dark Side and Doomsday brawled back to the dressing room, MOD continued to beat on Schillo, until Johnny Reb tried to make the save. However, he was also given a Darkness Falls for his efforts. Then "Big" Rich Clark ran out and nailed MOD with a chair, and then chokeslammed him through a table to the cheers of the crowd. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Whew! What a wild one the main event was last night, action in and out of the ring, Doomsday involved, Johnny Reb involved, "Big" Rich Clark involved, of course the Dark Side involved, you definitely can't miss the CWO when it comes to your hometown! Our cameras were able to catch up with Clark when he left the arena, we take you to those comments now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The camera catches up with "Big" Rich Clark as he's leaving the arena.) "BIG" RICH CLARK: So, Damion, how'd ya like that one? You think that you are the best because you have the CWO belt. Well, that'll soon change, because that belt WILL be mine. It's all a matter of time, and yours is just about up. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: In other news, as you saw on the House Shows Report, we have new North American Tag Team Champions. The match at CWO CELEBRATION between the Pit Bosses and the Deadly Sins is still scheduled, but is now of course a non-title affair. We will have comments from the Pit Bosses later on in the broadcast, but for now, let's look at tonight's card, coming to you from Cleveland, OH. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW FRIDAY, DECEMBER 13, 1996 CLEVELAND, OH Mercenaries vs. Blood Inc. Ganglord vs. Jackass Joel Baby Waldo vs. Battlecat Highlanders vs. Hollywood Muscle Hell's Pets (Dark Raven & Hellhound) vs. Biblical Powers Maximum Overdrive vs. Benjamin Blake Bentley WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night vs Deadly Sins _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As we said, we now take you to comments from the Pit Bosses, who lost their North American Tag Team Titles last night in Cincinnati. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Pit Bosses are in the interview area, smiling grimly.) PIT BOSS #1: Well, well, well. It seems we underestimated you boys. You know, maybe it's our fault, we were so focused on the Deadly Sins this Sunday that we didn't expect a challenge from you. PIT BOSS #2: That's right. But Bad Guys, let me tell you something... I don't care what prisons you've been in, what kind of life both you have lead, let me tell you, we have lived the life! We have done things that you probably can't even imagine. We are lower than the low, dirtier than dirt, and we'll prove it to you when we get a rematch. PIT BOSS #1: Enjoy those belts while you can, Bad Guys, because when the Pit Bosses are done with you, you're going to WISH you were back in Attica! PIT BOSS #2: Bank on it! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: The very strange individual Cake Boy will meet Sandman Jr. in the EYE ON THE STORM for CWO CELEBRATION this Sunday. However, Cake Boy has also set his... um... sights... on "Master of Pain" Barry Hart. We take you now to comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Cake Boy is in the interview area.) CAKE BOY: Barry Hart... last time I felt that our time together was not quality time... so I left. This time I want to play a little game. I want you inside a steel cage. Dance for me big boy. Dance. (Cake Boy licks frosting off of cake leaving large build up around mouth.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Uh... oh, we're back. Another newcomer to the CWO, Black Death, has apparently set his sights high. Let's get comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Black Death is in the interview area.) BLACK DEATH: Now that I have purged my first opponent, I am looking to move on to even greater evil in this league. I see the evil that lies in the CWO and I must eliminate it. I'm starting with you, Brent Alles. You will be purified. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: "The Ace" has a response to those comments from Black Death. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is in the interview area.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: Black Death, maybe you haven't been here long enough to understand. I am not evil. I am not good. I don't go courting the fans' praise, nor do I avoid it. I am my own entity. I am the Clique of One. I need no allies. I need no friends. All I need is myself and my God-given ability, which is more than you'll ever have in your pitiful life, my "friend." So, when you speak of evil, and purity, and purification, look no farther, my friend, because "The Ace", the CO1, is the purest form of wrestling ability available on the market today. Forget 99 44/100% pure, I'm the real deal, so bring it on. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: A former competitor in several European leagues, Lafayette has brought his unique style of wrestling to the CWO. We go to comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Lafayette is in the interview area, a French flag behind him.) LAFAYETTE: Zis week I will shallenge ze Gold Kard. He iz German swine. I speet in hiz general direction. He will kiss my boot. He will lick it. He will grovel like a beaten dog. He is a disgrace, and I am ze French pride. Vive la France. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: One of the burning questions of CWO fans these days is: what's up with Doomsday? Why have they allied with Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo? You can ask the members of Hardcore Heaven and Sandman Jr., Doomsday are not the nicest guys in the world. Can Schillo maintain his "clean-shaven" image hanging out with these thugs? Well, we'll have to see, but for now, we take you to comments from Doomsday. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the members of DOOMSDAY.) MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: It seems the team of DOOMSDAY has been the talk of a few individuals the past couple of days. "Big" Rich Clark, you may be one of the biggest individuals in this federation, but your brain is one of the smallest. If I were you, I'd consider shutting my mouth. Or would you like some more of what we've dished out to you in the past? (Doomsday starts to sing the song "Three Blind Mice".) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: You had better get something understood Clark, you big goof. Schillo made the career move that will guide him to the top of the CWO. You claim to have respect for him? Where the hell were you all those times when he was getting beaten down by the Dark Side or some of the other scum in the CWO? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: I'll tell you where you were. You were back in the dressing rooms trying to figure out how to tie your shoes. And the joke of it all is that they were velcro ties! After that, you couldn't figure out how to open the door to your dressing room and your even stupider friend, the burrito-muncher, Miguel Sanchez, spent two hours trying to open an unlocked door. If you two idiots ever team up, here's the ideal name for your tag team: Dumb and Dumber. MAXIMUM OVERDRIVE: Sanchez, it's only a matter of time before that TV Title is strapped around my waist. I hope you polish that belt up real nice, because come this Sunday, it's going to be in the hands of DOOMSDAY, the hottest group of wrestlers in the CWO. I'd tell you to have your stupid buddy help you out, but he'd probably get lost in his own dressing room. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: MOD, don't flatter yourself. We've tangled with your butts before, and needless to say, you didn't impress the Mercenaries or Doomsday. So you might as well stop going around claiming that we're afraid of you, because we aren't. You may be riding high on the gold at the present moment, but Doomsday, Schillo and his partner Brian Williams, will guarantee you right now that it won't stay that way for long. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally, we haven't heard from them in a while, but the Four Aces are still flying high in the CWO. We take you to comments from them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The camera opens on the Four Aces surrounded by bags and bags of mail.) FLYBOY: Hey, everyone, happy holidays! The Four Aces have been kicking back reading our fan mail - and as you can see, there's a lot of it! CLARK GRAY: Let's share some with the viewers, guys! STEVE KELLER: Good idea, big guy! Here's one. "Dear Four Aces: My name is Randy Robinson. I am eight years old. I think that Clark Gray is the coolest wrestler in the world." WAYNE CARPENTER: No accounting for taste! GRAY (mock-swinging at Carpenter): Shaddap, goldilocks! KELLER (still reading): "When my parents asked me what I wanted most of all for Christmas, I told that that what I wanted more than anything was a picture with your names on it, especially Clark Gray's name. Your friend, Randy Robinson." FLYBOY: Randy, it's yours! One autographed picture of the Four Aces, coming up! CARPENTER: Here's a good one! "Dear Studmuffins! All of us in the offices of Bender, Binder, Bonder, and Bunn Law Firm think that you guys are totally hot! PLEASE" - and that's underlined three times - "tell us that you're single! Sincerely, Cathy, Cathy, Donna, Teri, and Barbi!" KELLER: Wow! GRAY: Five of them, four of us... what do we do? KELLER: You mean before or after our girlfriends massacre us? FLYBOY: Sorry, ladies, but all of us are in committed relationships, with some wonderful women. In fact, I want to say hello to Sheila now - hi, baby! GRAY: I love you, Maureen! CARPENTER: Joanne, you're the best! KELLER: Patti, you're better! GRAY: Oh, God... guys, listen to this one. "Dear Four Aces. My name is Mike O'Brien and I have leukemia. I am ten years old. What I want more than anything in the world is to meet you guys, and maybe the Keuka Guy, too. Your friend, Mike O'Brien." CARPENTER: Good lord... we have to meet this young man. KELLER: Clark, let me see that... hmmm, Seattle... I'll call the airline and book us four seats. FLYBOY: Wait until I've talked to Schillo, he might want to come along. It's a letter like that that makes us sit back and realize how lucky we all are! GRAY: You're right, Flyboy. God's been good to all of us, we're all in good health, and we've got long careers ahead of us. FLYBOY: This one's weird. "Dear Four Aces. I think you guys kick ... uh, butt! I love watching you guys put your opponents down for the kill! You should be even rougher! Show no mercy! Only then will you get the respect you deserve! The weak deserve no compassion! Yours in blood, Tony Dornan." Geez, what's this guy's problem? GRAY: Sounds sick to me! CARPENTER: Listen, Tony, wrestling is a sport with rules, not a street fight. We don't kill our opponents, we pin them. I think you need to get psychological help, because fans like you, we don't need. KELLER: I may call myself the Killer, but I'd never think of actually being one. And "yours in blood?" Get real! FLYBOY: And speaking of blood... Blood, Inc., you can take your threats and stick them where the sun doesn't shine! Clark and I beat you clowns once, and any of us can do it again! Name your time, name your place, and we'll be there! ALL: FOUR ACES - FLYING HIGH! (The camera fades out as the guys start playfully tossing mail at each other.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today. We'll see you tomorrow.
