CWO REPORT 11/3/96

3 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for November 3, 1996. Great action last night at Madison Square Garden in New York City, let's take a look at how it went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 2, 1996 NEW YORK, NY MADISON SQUARE GARDEN Samoans (Grog and Smog) vs. Deadly Sins (Pride and Sloth) Samoans and Deadly Sins battled to a 30 minute time limit draw. Bane vs. Joe Eagle Joe Eagle defeated Bane by countout in 5:07 after Sandman powerslammed Bane on the concrete floor. Unholy Duo (Payne and Hate) vs. Biblical Powers (Savior and Moses) Biblical Powers defeated Unholy Duo in 14:29 when Moses pinned Hate. Sandman vs. Cyrax Sandman defeated Cyrax by disqualification when Bane attacked him in 10:58. Flame Spirits (Griffen and Wolf) vs. Mercenaries (Kaptain Krunch and Korperal Punishment) Flame Spirits and Mercenaries battled to a 30 minute time limit draw. Gold Kard International (Gold Kard and Brent "The Ace" Alles) vs. Bad Guys (Joe "Mean Guy" Rucker and Ron "Rude Guy" Johnson) Gold Kard International defeated the Bad Guys by disqualification in 14:20 when Herbie Versmells hit Brent "The Ace" Alles with his cellular phone. Judgment vs. Master of Darkness Damion Master of Darkness Damion pinned Judgment in 2:22 after the Darkness Falls piledriver. After the match was finished, MOD Damion proceeded to deliver subsequent Darkness Falls tombstone piledrivers until "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum showed up and chased him off again. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Talon vs. Dark Raven (champion) Talon pinned Dark Raven in 9:21 after the Jekyll Jump (top rope Frankensteiner), winning the title. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: The Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jim "Jazzman" Taylor) vs. the Pit Bosses (champions) Pit Bosses defeate the Big Cool in 12:10 when Pit Boss #2 pinned Taylor after a double dropkick. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Lafayette vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo defeated Lafayette by disqualification in 5:34 after being attacked by Blood Inc. (Panther, Steel, D. E. A. T. H.), who beat him severely until they were strangely driven away by Doomsday (Maximum Overdrive, Baby Waldo, Mercenaries). _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Well, Dark Raven losing the Light Heavyweight Championship in a shocker to Talon, you can be sure Dark Raven, along with the Dark Side, will be looking for revenge against that youngster, if they aren't too busy with the Alliance. However, the strangest thing, as you saw, was that Schillo was helped by the members of Doomsday when he was attacked by Blood Inc., who were apparently trying to collect on the now $2 Million bounty put on his head by Gold Kard. Is Schillo going to become a member of Doomsday? Judging from this interview we have with him, you never know, but in the CWO, we have learned that anything is possible. We take you now to comments from Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo. _______________________________________________________________________________ WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: Hello, CWO! Hello, ladies and little Schillomaniacs! I've finally achieved my dream! I have become the CWO WORLD CHAMPION! Through hard work and perserverence, I've provided an exmple for all the fans to follow. I'm just a little guy, and... (starts crying) this means... so much to me... I'd like to thank the fans for all their support. I will do my best to be the most adored and honorable champion the CWO has ever had. Parents, tell your childen not to have nightmares about my CWO career ending at the hands of GKI. I will remain here, as long as there are fans out there to keep me going, I will be there. Ladies, I'll be staying at the Motel 6 the night of Showcase if you'd like to stop by. Kids, be sure to do your homework, love the ladies, and always beat the bad guys. See you around, CWO... I hope to make you proud. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Admirable comments from Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, we hope to get his reaction to the Doomsday situation soon. However, tonight we will be in Atlantic City, at the Trump Taj Majal, for great CWO action. Let's look at the lineup! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 1996 ATLANTIC CITY, NJ TRUMP TAJ MAHAL Sin vs. Sandman Baby Waldo vs. Clark Gray Deadly Sins (Pride and Sloth) vs. Unholy Duo (Payne and Hate) Maximum Overdrive vs. "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum Sorrow vs. Bane Gold Kard International (Gold Kard and Brent "The Ace" Alles) vs. Tower of Power (Joe Eagle and Vincent "The Lip" Manocelli) Sandman Jr. vs. Lafayette NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses vs. The Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jim "Jazzman" Taylor) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Mercenaries vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) MATCH MUST BE WON BY FINISHER: "Master of Pain" Barry Hart vs. Master of Darkness Damion _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: And now, we go to another segment on the continuing Lord Foul the Despiser saga. _______________________________________________________________________________ (A returning hero to his country, the ever tired Lord of wrestling makes his way down the hallway into one of the many rooms in his castle. He quickly switches to proper attire and lies down into his quarters bed. He quickly falls to sleep as images fade in.) VOICE: And the *NEW* CWO world heavyweight champion, Lord Foul the Despiser! (An image of Foul holding his first belt is shown.) VOICE: Brownsville Bomber with a powerbomb! 1... 2... 3! (Echo and a reflash of the image) 1... 2... 3! We have a major upset here! (Echoing) Upset... upset... Lord Foul *lost* the title! (Image of a defeated Foul is shown.) VOICE: Gold Kard, apparently the number one Lord Foul fan in the world, and the Chaotic Dynasty is that much stronger. (A dark red image of Gold Kard breaking a stereo over Foul's head is shown, and Foul himself begins to toss while in bed.) VOICE: Oh... my... god... (echo) Oh... my... god! Foul just... Gold Kard cracked that stereo right over his head! This is serious damage! (Various shots of Rebel Yell pulverizing Foul a week later.) VOICE: And World Champ Rebel Yell just beat Lord Foul in his *OWN* match, and in *LONDON* no less! (Foul begins to stir about faster.) LORD FOUL: (while asleep) No... No! (An image of former CM2WF superstar Foolkiller is shown.) FOOLKILLER: You're nothing but the past! Old man! (Echo) Old Man! (echoing more) Old man... you can't cut it in here! I WILL destroy you! VOICE: Foolkiller with a vicious sneak attack on Foul and Rebel Yell! He's got Foul up in the air, Thunderfunk! *Another* leaping piledriver, and Foul may be seriously hurt! (Foul kicks his legs violently in bed.) VOICE: Foul seeking retribution and he got it tonight with a victory over Foolkiller in the cage! However, Foul suffering many cuts and bruises himself! Lord Foul and his ex-partner Sandman facing off toe to toe in this Texas Death match which has run *over* forty minutes, and both men are bloodly and battered! BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES' VOICE: That's right fans, at the next pay per view, the CM2WF will face off against the WORLD, against WCW, WWF, Japan, Mexico, and Europe, our feature match, the Four Horsemen against a united Chaotic Dynasty! VOICE: And Ric Flair, jumping Foul while he's alone, clamps on the figure four! And Foul sits alone in the ring while all four horsemen take turns battering him! (Sweat drips from the face of Foul.) VOICE: Lord Foul, walking down the aisle way, headed back to the locker room to leave Sandman alone against Flair, where is he going? YOUNG MEN'S VOICES: Your our *HERO* Lord Foul! LORD FOUL: No! Can't... won't! VOICE: And the expected winner of the tournament, Lord Foul the Despiser, suffers his second loss in a row, against Gold Kard! SANDMAN'S VOICE: You've betrayed me for the last time! (Echoing) Last time! Last time! BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES' VOICE: That idiot old man, he ruined my league! FOOLKILLER'S VOICE: Old Man! Old Man! Old Man! (Foul leaps up violently from his bed and covers his face.) LORD FOUL: That's it, I can't take anymore! (Fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT! (We go to Lafayette in the interview area as the closing creds come up before fading out.) LAFAYETTE: I doo not know whoo yoo zink yoo arr Sandman Jr. I am Lafayette, ze most talented restle-errr en all uf ze world. I speet on yoo. Yoo turn to mud. Go cry to yourr muzzer, yoo leetle babee.
