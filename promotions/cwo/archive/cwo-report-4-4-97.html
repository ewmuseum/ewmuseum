CWO REPORT 4/4/97

4 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, April 4, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. We are only two days away from the next chapter in CWO PPV excitement, APRIL ARMAGEDDON, featuring the return of THE ROCK. However, before that, exciting CWO action going on in the Mid-Atlantic area, including our nation's capital last night, Washington, D. C., let's look at what went down! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/3/97 Washington, DC Wild Ones (Mondo Gecko and El Grande Pistolerro) defeated 'Lonestar' Mike Williams/Kurt 'Thrill from the Chill' Gerstung when El Grande Pistolerro pinned Kurt 'Thrill from the Chill' Gerstung after Gerstung missed a spinkick from the top rope. Time of match: 5:20. David 'The Destroyer' Davis pinned Hellhound after the moonsault. Time of match: 14:23. Mercenaries defeated Eternal Wanderers when Kaptain Krunch pinned Exodus after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 13:47. Joe Eagle pinned 'Master of Darkness' Damion after the Eagle Shock (tiger driver) and outside interference by Bishop (fireball). Time of match: 8:35. Vlad the Impaler pinned Sandman Jr. after the dropkick and outside interference by Serpentmaster. Time of match: 11:03. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned Mondo Gecko after the Flash Flood and outside interference by Masher Maccabee (struck Mondo Gecko with the Television championship belt). Time of match: 5:41. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): The Big Cool defeated champions the Bad Guys by disqualification. Time of match: 11:46. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Johnny Reb defeated Goliath by disqualification when members of the Alliance attacked him. Time of match: 13:39. Mason-Dixon and Panther made the save. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Wheeling, WV, let's see what's in store for our fans there tonight! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW 4/4/97 Wheeling, WV STEEL CAGE: Arlon Flaym vs. Ganglord Exile vs. Panther Mercenaries vs. Masters of Hardcore (Ohlhoff and Couchman) Chris Greco and Tommy Lightning vs. Deadly Sins Gold Kard vs. Johnny Reb Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles 'Master of Darkness' Damion vs. Bishop NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Dark Raven vs. Goliath (champion) Vlad the Impaler/Serpentmaster vs. David 'The Destroyer' David/Inferno WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Regulators vs. Pit Bosses (champions) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars. Panther is first up, and he's got an announcement for the CWO. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Panther is dressed in a black suit and is wearing a dark pair of Ray Ban sunglasses.) PANTHER: Well, well, well... it seems that it has become a popular thing as of late to come and rush a match. In light of this disturbing trend I have found it to be a wise idea to protect one's self from this hideous act. (Panther looks to the back.) PANTHER: Boys, come on out here! (Two very Large Black Men walk from the back wearing white suits and Killer Loop sunglasses.) PANTHER: Ladies and gentleman, allow me to introduce my bodyguards. This is Jerome and Jermain Justice, you can simply call them "Brother Justice." Tiger Rogers, Gold Kard, or whoever is out to get me or Johnny Reb, you are going to have to go through these gentlemen. JEROME: SUCKAS! Listen up, I ain't here to play no games, I'm here to see that Justice is served! UNDERSTAND! PANTHER: Rogers, Kard, I've got my eyes on you! You had better hope that they don't get their hands on you. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Finally the Dark Side have broken the silence ever since their betrayal at the hands of Hellhound. We now here from the Dark Side. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on MOD Damion and the Creatures of the Night. MOD looks terrible, worse than he normally does. It's as if he has not had any sleep for weeks and the Creatures look no better. MOD moves forward and with a horse voice speaks.) MOD DAMION: HELLHOUND! HOW COULD YOU DO THIS TO ME! You of all people, you were to be the next... the next CWO world champ. But now you are nothing, you will amount to nothing. Bishop, this is all your doing and for that you shall pay. You will pay with everything you hold close to you. The Dark Side shall have its revenge. (Damion starts to whip things around the room in anger as the Creatures of the Night step into the light.) NIGHT SLINGER: As you can see, the master is not very impressed with you, Bishop, or you, Hellhound. It was no skin off our backs when you took that lightweight so-called mute from us but the master's pet was over the line. Within the weeks to come, you will surely feel the true power of the Dark Side and its one true ruler and Master... our Master, MOD Damion. NIGHT CRAWLER: The punishment does not end after April Armageddon! NO! It may last for all eternity. We shall haunt your every waking and sleeping hour of every day. You will feel what we have felt for these last few days. The torture, the torment, and the suffering. Only what you four, the Unholy Alliance, will go through will be ten times worse than that which we have faced. (MOD who has still a look of rage in his eyes returns to the camera.) MOD DAMION: THE ROCK! Our second time through this match will be much more successful than the first. The Dark Side shall return to dominance and my Creatures will show the world once again what real champions are and what the Dark Side is all about. The terror begins tonight! ALL THREE: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! MOD DAMION: YOUR MASTER HAS ARRIVED! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire is with the lovely miss Amber. Bloodbath is on hand with his head bowed down.) WILDFIRE (very calm and cool): I see what you're doing, Bad Guys! You're trying to get to us early 'cause you know you can't beat us! We are gonna win those North American Tag Titles and bring them home for the great fans of the CWO! I've tasted sweet revenge not to long ago and we're gonna do it again! BLOODBATH (dark and mysterious): Bad Guys, you have made your fatal mistake! On Tuesday when you jumped us with chairs you made a GRAVE mistake! You have stepped into the realm of the wild side and there's no turning back! Come April Armageddon, when we are standing in the center of the battle field with our hands raised, and your souls have been taken by the maelstrom, only your carcasses will remain, for the Bad Guys that once WERE the best.... (Bloodbath raises his hand high as the lights go out) BLOODBATH: Bad Guys, REST IN HELL! (The lights come back on and Bloodbath has mysteriously disapered.) WILDFIRE: And as for you Wrath, the only thing that will be put out will be the strecher that takes your broken body after I kick your @$$!!! AMBER: Whatya gonna do when the WILDFIRE runs wild on you!!! (The camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
