REPORT 9/15/97

15 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Monday, September 15, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr, Mitch E. Washey is on vacation. Yesterday saw a great SUNDAY SHOWCASE for the CWO, let's take a look at the results! _______________________________________________________________________________ SUNDAY SHOWCASE RECAP 9/14/97 HOUR #1: MATCH ONE: "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat defeated Brent "The Ace" Alles in 7:05, pinning him in 7:05 after the Jimmyplex and an attack by Lord Hile Troy. Troy and Heat then continued to beat on Alles after the bell, prompting a save by the Deadly Sins. MATCH TWO: Deadly Sins defeated X-Factor/Marc Mission when Sloth pinned Marc Mission after the Repention. Time of match: 8:21. MATCH THREE: Skyhawk defeated Sandman by disqualification in 11:59 when The Cabin attacked. The rest of The Unit came down to make the save. HOUR #2: MATCH FOUR: Jerome Justice defeated 'Monster' Rich Clark by disqualification when the CPE Enforcers attacked him. Time of match: 7:05. MATCH FIVE: CPE Enforcers defeated Ghost Riders when Ranger Taurus pinned Blaze after the Texas Stampede. Time of match: 6:59. MATCH SIX (CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH): Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia after the Flash Flood. Time of match: 12:42. Jason 'Flash' Varga retained the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion title. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tomorrow night is TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, let's look at the lineup! _______________________________________________________________________________ TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER 9/16/97 "LIVE" FROM CINCINNATTI, OH HOUR #1: El Grande Pistolerro vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams Mercenaries vs. Mason-Dixon NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (TRIPLE THREAT): Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil vs. Painmaster vs. Masher Maccabee HOUR #2: Southern Dynamite vs. Vegas High Roller Newton Bros. vs. Highlanders TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: The Focus (champion) vs. Mustafa Rahim _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: One of those great matches tomorrow night will involve the Vegas High Roller going up against the newest man to enter the CWO, Southern Dynamite. Let's hear from the High Roller and that skunk of a manager of his, Joe Root. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Vegas High Roller and Joe Root are in the interview area, both wearing shades and their hair slicked back, as well as ridiculously loud leisure suits.) VEGAS HIGH ROLLER: Hey, who loves ya, baby? Anyway, it looks like I got another Southern hick to take care of. As if Johnny Reb bein' the World Champion wasn't enough, now we gotta have more of these people who marry their sisters in the CWO. It's a cryin' disgrace. JOE ROOT: That's right, Southern Dynamite, prepare to be a dud tomorrow night when you get Vegas Swingered into oblivion! VEGAS HIGH ROLLER: Now that's settled. Let's go on to SEPTEMBER SLAM II: REVENGE IS THE NAME OF THE GAME. You know, Wayne "Loser Guy" Schillo, you're really funny, saying that you've put a bounty on me. Gold Kard's offer still stands, pal, and I'm still gonna collect on your hide. JOE ROOT: That's right! The odds are a million to one of you even coming close to beating my man the High Roller, Schillo, and you're not gonna be in Vegas, and it's not gonna be your lucky day! VEGAS HIGH ROLLER: I guess the stipulation of the match is that if I lose, you get five minutes in the ring with my good friend and close advisor Joe Root. Get it outta your head, jerk-off! It ain't gonna happen! JOE ROOT: That's right, but even if I did, I'd still smack the taste out of your mouth, "Loser Boy!" VEGAS HIGH ROLLER: SEPTEMBER SLAM II, revenge may be the name of the game, but when I finally collect that $2,000,000 from Gold Kard for putting you out of wrestling, revenge is definitely going to be sweet. TAKE IT TO THE BANK! (Vegas High Roller and Joe Root grin as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's it for today. We'll see you tomorrow on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER!
