House Show Results - 3/5/99

March 6, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 3/5/99 Lexington, KY Newton Bros. defeated Lunatic Fringe when Howard Newton pinned 'Killer' Carlos Maia after the Powerline. Time of match: 8:28. 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum pinned Chris Greco after the Air Fulcrum. Time of match: 16:55. TEXAS BUNKHOUSE MATCH: Sandman and Wishbone defeated Ranger Taurus and Snake Martin when Wishbone pinned Ranger Taurus in a wild match. Time of match: 12:56.
'Mr. Technical' Cooper Young pinned 'Double D' Damian Dagger after a spinning leg lariat. Time of match: 11:15.
Television Champion Allen 'Mr. America' Carter pinned 'Dark Shogun' Akueri
Hakujin after the jumping side kick. Time of match: 14:27.
Joe Eagle defeated Man Mountain Marko by disqualification after Marko hit him with a chair in the ring. Time of match: 5:32.
George Washington pinned Cris Marcus after the Seeing Stars, pulling the tights. Time of match: 5:51.
Mike 'Money' Sexton pinned Lord Hile Troy after the legdrop, pulling the tights. Time of match: 11:57. STEEL CAGE SUBMISSION MATCH: The ref stopped the match when Fallen had Johnny Reb in the Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 16:33. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Foolkiller defeated World Champion Darkness by disqualification when the
Dark Side attacked. Time of match: 9:31. Lord Hile Troy and Johnny Reb made the save.
