Press Release - FEVER 1/25/99

January 28, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The CWO will be rocking the house in Georgia this week as we get ready for the big show coming up this Saturday as part of SUPER BOWL WEEKEND, CWO
AFTER DARK, only on the FOX network. You can find out about that great card by going to the CWO website, but let's take a look at what is scheduled to go down on FEVER this week!
The big man, MAN MOUNTAIN MARKO, who there's been a lot of buzz recently, will collide with another big man, WARHAWK of the DARK SIDE. This one should be explosive!
In Light Heavyweight action, it's two former LH champions going at it as
JASON "FLASH" VARGA goes up against "DARK SHOGUN" AKUERI HAKUJIN. After the shocking disbanding of the RAT PACK last week (not to mention the apparent retirement of GOLD KARD) Varga now finds himself alone. Will he keep to his goofball ways? Or is it time for him to head up that ladder again? The
Shogun definitely wants to get back up the ladder as well, and earn himself a title shot against either TOMMY LIGHTNING or the current champion SECRET
AGENT SUPER DRAGON, who ever comes out of CWO AFTER DARK on top.
ALLEN "MR. AMERICA" CARTER is busy with singles action this week, so GEORGE
WASHINGTON will team with GEOFF "THE ATTITUDE ADJUSTER" BONDAVARSKY against the DEADLY SINS. The Sins will get a shot at the World Tag Team titles this weekend on AFTER DARK, let's hope that they don't think of the TRUE
AMERICANS as only a warm-up match! The North American Championship will be on the line as "DOUBLE D" DAMIAN
DAGGER of the THOMAS FAMILY collides with current champion DIAMONDBACK CHRIS
MYERS. Obviously, the wrestling world is abuzz with what was revealed last week on FEVER by DARKNESS... Myers and CHRIS GRECO, two men who were thought irreproachable by CWO fans, acting like total "heels." Who is the true
Diamondback Chris Myers? And how will this current controversy in his life affect not only this match but his upcoming World title match against champion FOOLKILLER on Saturday as well?
The aforementioned ALLEN "MR. AMERICA" CARTER will meet "THE AVENGER" JESSE
EWIAK of BAD COMPANY. Ewiak's been red hot as of late, as both of these men look to get themselves back into title contention.
Television Champion CRIS MARCUS of the OUTLAW RIDERS will collide with a newcomer, SIMON ST. SEVERE, THE PREACHER OF PAIN. Not much is known about this guy, but one thing's for sure, Marcus will be cocky and confident as always!
JOHNNY REB takes on MAN OF RESPECT WILDFIRE in what should be a great technical match... provided the increasingly duplicitous UNION CAINE doesn't try to get himself involved. And what will JOE EAGLE do? Obviously he doesn't have much love for either man in this contest, but he still is a MAN
OF RESPECT. Intrigue abounds...
More OUTLAW RIDERS action as two tough hombres known as RANGER TAURUS and
SNAKE MARTIN take on STEVE KELLER AND TOMMY LIGHTNING. Apparently rumor has it that something big is going to happen to Steve Keller and Tommy Lightning on FEVER. What do they think about the shocking video of their friend CHRIS
GRECO? You'll have to turn in to see.
Also, if that wasn't enough, LORD HILE TROY will see action against one of the DARK SIDE, in retaliation for his refusal to join last week. Should be hot!
All that and more, as the CWO gets ready for AFTER DARK with a red hot
FEVER! Don't miss it!
