CWO House Show Results (Week of 10/11/98-10/17/98)

19 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 10/16/98 Mankato, MN (Civic Center) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT TOURNAMENT MATCHES: Wildfire pinned Steve Keller after the Wild Thing DDT. Time of match: 4:54. Chris Greco pinned Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky after the Flying Bodypress. Time of match: 11:10. George Washington pinned Calvin after outside interference by Geoff Bondavarsky. Time of match: 5:11. 'Killer' Carlos Maia pinned Flash Varga after the DDT, pulling the tights. Time of match: 4:08. Johnny Reb made Kamikaze submit to the Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 13:06. 'Pagan Horse' Logan Fett pinned 'Double D' Damian Dagger after the Zen Powerslam, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 0:30. NON-TOURNAMENT MATCHES: 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum pinned Clark Gray after the Air Fulcrum. Time of match: 17:20. Union Caine (substituting for the injured Brent 'The Ace' Alles) pinned Foolkiller after the Cainedriver. Time of match: 10:03. Unit ('Assassin 64' Sean Holiday and the Newton Bros.) defeated World 6-Man Tag Team Champions the CPE (Mondo Gecko, Col. Taggart, Lamar 'Terror' Tyson) when Howard Newton pinned Tyson after the Powerline. Time of match: 29:35. The Unit won the CWO World 6-Man Tag Team Championship. Allen 'Mr. America' Carter pinned Diamondback Chris Myers after outside interference by Brent 'The Ace' Alles. It turns out that 'The Ace' wasn't injured after all, it was merely a ruse to get at Myers. Time of match: 20:35. Myers delivered a Rattler to both Carter and Alles after the match. HOUSE SHOW 10/17/98 Duluth, MN (DECC) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT TOURNAMENT: Secret Agent Super Dragon pinned Dane Griffen after the small package. Time of match: 19:34. Tommy Lightning pinned Bobby Orr after the Lightning Strike. Time of match: 10:34. Lightning was attacked by Secret Agent Super Dragon after the match at Lester Sheets' behest. Mondo Gecko defeated Wayne 'Cool Guy' Schillo by reversed decision. Schillo pinned Mondo Gecko with his feet on the ropes, but Tamara Wilcox informed the referee of the infraction and he overturned his decision. Time of match: 17:55. 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum pinned 'Mr. Entertainment' Kris Knight after the Air Fulcrum. Time of match: 8:45. The Focus pinned Cris Marcus after the small package and after 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum hit Marcus with a chair. Time of match: 10:17. Vulture pinned Sandman Jr. after the Devil's Dance and outside interference by Wishbone. Time of match: 15:56. NON-TOURNAMENT MATCHES: Between the tournament and the non-tournament matches, Joe Eagle came out to hawk his new T-shirt to the crowd. He was attacked by the Focus while he was doing this. Lamar 'Terror' Tyson pinned Steve Keller after the swinging DDT. Time of match: 7:32. Joe Eagle returned and called the Focus out to get his butt kicked for attacking him earlier in the night. The Focus obliged, but then pinned Joe Eagle after the small package, pulling the tights. Time of match: 4:19. THREE-WAY DANCE: Wishbone defeated Calvin and Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky in a three-way dance. Time of match: 4:51. Wishbone made Calvin submit to the Politrick while Zero beat on Bondavarsky outside the ring. Wishbone and Zero continued beating on Calvin and Bondavarsky after the bell, and Sandman Jr. made the save, hitting both Wishbone and Zero with his cane. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION TITLE MATCH (SUBMISSION MATCH): Champion George Washington made Johnny Reb submit to the figure four leglock after Carter knocked Reb out by hitting him with a baseball bat. Time of match: 22:29. George Washington retained the CWO North American Champion title.
