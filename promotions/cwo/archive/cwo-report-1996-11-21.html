CWO REPORT 11/21/96

21 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fade in to setting: Bane is alone in a gym. His immensly muscular body is rock hard, and the vains are swelling so as it looks like they are about to burst. It is obvious that he has just finished working out.) BANE (not a hint of tiredness in his deadly voice): I am not here to talk about what I did to Sandman. You are only hearing my voice because I wish to speak to Lord Foul. I have witnessed your interview, and you dared to speak my name. You are fool enough to challenge your Bane. I once believed that you may have been intelligent like myself, but stupidity shown through when you uttered my name. You fool. You wrestle for those pathetic people and for those sickening promotors. You wrestle for pride and for money. I will take away your pride, and I will destroy you, but not for money. You wish to make a comeback, and maybe you will show your face at November Pain. Maybe you will fill Sandman's missing shoes. Maybe you should stay in England. I will show you pain, and I will put you once again into retirement. You disgust me. You never were the man that I am. You could never be the man that I am. For you to say differently is insulting. I control you, and you will NEVER beat me. Lord Foul, your Bane has arrived. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, November 11, 1996. First off, I would like to apologize for my lack of professionalism yesterday in the wake of that footage of Bane's attack on Sandman. We are not going to show you that footage again, we're not going to give Bane that pleasure, but let me just say that hospital officials were able to stabilize Sandman before much damage could be done. Bane, and whoever was taping that horrible incident, escaped from the hospital before security could apprehend them, he is still at large. We expect him to be at NOVEMBER PAIN on Sunday, as well as House Shows this week, including one tonight. Along with the Apocalypse and a mystery partner, Bane will be taking on Sandman's friends: Talon, the Flame Spirits, and a mystery partner. However, after yesterday's actions, law enforcement officials will need to make a decision on what course to take. We could very well see a warrant for his arrest, we'll keep you posted. Anyway, enough of that. Last night, the superstars of the CWO were in San Juan, Puerto Rico, let's look at the results of that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW REPORT WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 20, 1996 SAN JUAN, PUERTO RICO STREET FIGHT: Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson vs. Flyboy Flyboy pinned Johnson after a backdrop in 12:58. Hoss and Little Joe vs. Flame Spirits Both teams wrestled to a double countout in 13:42. Sin vs. Master Of Darkness Damion Sin pinned Damion after a flying dropkick in 10:43 after outside interference by "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum. Lafayette and Rooster vs. The Big Cool Lafayette and Rooster defeated the Big Cool by countout in 6:04 after Jim "Jazzman" Taylor missed a dangerous dive off the ring apron against Lafayette. Johnny Reb vs. Sandman Jr. Reb made Sandman Jr. submit to the Texas Cloverleaf in 10:04. WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Blood Inc. (D.E.A.T.H. and Panther) vs. The Dark Side (Creatures of the Night and Hellhound, champions) Blood Inc. defeated the Dark Side by disqualification in 19:00 when Master of Darkness Damion attacked Panther, breaking a pinfall attempt. Jackass Joel vs. Hangman Hangman pinned Joel in 2:57 after the Skullcrusher (shooting star press with a brick in his hand). However, after the match, Hangman refused to let up, delivering more Skullcrushers, and the referee consequently awarded the decision to Jackass Joel as a result of a reversed decision. Phantom Freightrain vs. Payne Freightrain pinned Payne after the Boogie Woogie Choo Choo power slam in 9:34. Steel vs. Dark Raven In a surprise upset, Steel made Dark Raven submit to the Gank (Texas cloverleaf) in 7:31. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in the Bahamas, let's look at the great card planned for there. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 21, 1996 BAHAMAS Panther vs. Johnny Reb Mason-Dixon vs. Deadly Sins Bane vs. Master of Darkness Damion Mad Doctor's Ghost vs. Akilles 'Lonestar' Mike Williams vs. "Master of Pain" Barry Hart LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Dark Raven vs. Talon (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Flame Spirits vs. Pit Bosses (champions) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Samoans vs. Creatures of the Night (champions) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see, Bane is scheduled to face Master of Darkness Damion on this card, provided law enforcement officials do not call for his arrest. We have comments from both of these men. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens and from the shadows, Master Of Darkness Damion emerges.) MOD DAMION: Bane, the CWO thought our first battle was such a great war, they decided to have us two go at it again. I can tell you this, Bane, the outcome shall be the same this time as last time. You gave me everthing you had and it still wasn't good enough. Bane, I beat you in our first match, I showed you that I truly rule the night and its DARKNESS. Bane, you may have succeeded in ridding the CWO of the other fool who claimed that he was the rightful ruler of the Night, but tonight you will find out once again that there is one one true MASTER OF THE DARKNESS and that is ME, not you, ME! Bane, there are a few who do fear you but I am not one of those men, for I fear no one, they all fear me, including YOU! I can hear your thoughts, Bane, you wonder: can you truly beat your Master? Yes, Bane, I am your master, you roam within the night, the Night which I control and Bane, NO, you cannot defeat me. Let the battle continue, Bane, and I shall end it by extinguishing your internal flame like you have attempted to do with the fool Sandman. Bane tonight your DARKNESS FALLS as your LIGHT's go OUT! (With this, MOD Damion goes through the moves of delivering his finisher, the Darkness Falls tombstone.) MOD DAMION: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera fades to black on Damion.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Setting: The night has fallen and darkness encompasses everything. The only light that shines comes from the moon above. This light revels rows and rows of tombstones. It also reveals something more fearsome: Bane.) BANE (the air is cold and his breath can be seen coming out of his venomous mouth): Master of Darkness Damion, greetings. I know how fond you are of the dark, something you know nothing about. You tell yourself that you are the "master of the dark." That you own the dark. You own nothing, because I gave you nothing. I am the one who owns EVERYTHING! This would include your precious night, as well as your precious body. I am rather fond of the night myself. The night holds fear. People cower and run for shelter when it comes. They do this because they know the night holds me. I own the shadows. I am the bringer of the night. Let me take you on a little tour of my art. These are some of my favorite masterpieces. (Bane walks down a row of grave stones. You can make out the names that are on them. Marc Daniels, Sandman, Flyboy, and so forth. Finally Bane stops at a stone of a freshly dug grave. The Inscription read: Damion: He now belongs to what he loved. Along side this grave is another stone with loose dirt around it also. However, you cannot clearly make the name out.) BANE: Do you like my work, Damion? I have given you a new place to relocate that coffin of yours. You will be moving here after our match. You said that you taught me who the master of the darkside is. You taught me NOTHING! I am the teacher and you are the pupil. Pay attention, Thursday, Damion. You are about to learn more than you ever wanted to know about the darkness. Your Bane has arrived. (Bane walks slowly away. While he is walking you get a quick glimpse of the grave resting next to MOD's. The title reads: FOUL.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also on the card, a World Tag Team title defense by the Creatures of the Night against the huge Samoans. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Creatures of the Night, who are talking to each other, as they see the camera, they stop and come forward.) NIGHT CRAWLER: Well, Slinger, looks like we got us a pair of fat lazy ex-champs coming after our titles tonight. NIGHT SLINGER: That's right. Samoans, you do not deserve this chance but the brass of the CWO can't find any one we haven't beaten yet so they are giving us sad sorry teams like you two to beat again. You know, Crawler, this should be fun, a good fight that we will should certainly win but yet a good fight and this shall prove a great prep match for NOVEMBER PAIN. NIGHT CRAWLER: Samoans, you guys were once champs, we beat you then and now that you are a pair of nobodys you think you can take away our belts. Not a chance, fat boys. Maybe our prize for this victory tonight should be that lovely creature you bring to the ring with you. Just ask Sondra Slocum what great men we are, she passes out everytime we get near her. NIGHT SLINGER: Samoans, we shall let you into our Forever Night but as of yet we have not decided on whether or not to let you leave with all that you enter with. The price for entry may very well be your very souls. The collection is starting to grow and your sorry lives will just get lost in there with all the other souls we have eradicated. NIGHT CRAWLER: See you tonight, boys, when the moon is at its highest point in the sky, when the forces of DARKNESS are at their greatest strength. BOTH MEN: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (As the camera fades, the Creatures of the Night go back to talking about what ever they were talking about before the camera arrived.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Bane's actions have really riled up most of the CWO superstars, here is one man with comments towards him, Battlecat. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Battlecat is in the interview area.) BATTLECAT: Hey Bane, you must think that you are so tough that you beat up on an innocent man in the hospital. Well, how about you take on me for size, you cowardly piece of turd! I challenge you to a match, ASAP, I am gonna teach you a lesson and that is not to mess with helpless people. You're gonna find out my bite is worse than my bark! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally, we have some comments from Mason-Dixon. (Beauregard Dixon and Stonewall Mason appear in front of a large Confederate Flag drapped over the back wall of the interview area) DIXON: Well, Mason and ah have noticed that we've begun a slow slip down through the rankin's... maybe we was still a little tied up with the Dahk Side... at any rate, that's gonna' change real quick! MASON: It starts with you, Deadly Sins! Prepare to be stepped on, because Beauregard an' ah have y'all fixed as just two mo' stones on our way back to tha' top! DIXON: The rest of the CWO is hereby on notice... the Confederate Associates will soon be back in the drivuh seats, an' we're bringin' shovels with us! MASON: Sunday Naht is comin' soon...y'all have been warned.
