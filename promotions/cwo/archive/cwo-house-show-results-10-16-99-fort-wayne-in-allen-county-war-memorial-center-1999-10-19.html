House Show Results - 10/16/99 - Fort Wayne, IN (Allen County War Memorial Center)

October 19, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 10/16/99 Fort Wayne, IN (Allen County War Memorial Coliseum) Geoff 'The Attitude Adjuster' Bondavarsky pinned Mustafa Rahim after the
Rhino Charge. Time of match: 5:46.
'The Jaw Jacking Gigolo' Andrew Dixon and Sandman Jr. wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw.
Beauregard Boys defeated Magic Group when Shirley Beauregard pinned Wizard after the Option 73. Time of match: 13:34.
Master of Darkness Damion pinned Maximum Overdrive after a DDT on to a steel chair. Time of match: 5:39.
Hardcore Sensation defeated Creatures of the Night when 'Radical' Kirk
Madness pinned Night Slinger after the Sensational Bomb. Time of match:
20:57. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Man Mountain Marko pinned Lord Hile Troy after the Chi-Town Hammer. Time of match: 17:08. Man Mountain Marko retained the CWO North American Champion title.
CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Brute Squad defeated Mercenaries by disqualification when Maximum Overdrive attacked them, delivering a Pile-up to both Brute Squad members. That brought Damion and the Creatures back out to brawl with Doomsday! Time of
match: 13:52. Brute Squad retained the CWO North American Tag Team Champions title. CWO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Foolkiller defeated Allen 'Mr. America' Carter by disqualification when
Sandman Jr. attacked Foolkiller. Time of match: 26:23. Allen 'Mr. America'
Carter retained the CWO World Champion title. Sandman Jr. then proceeded to attack Carter, but was stopped by Foolkiller, who Thunderfunked him to end the night!
