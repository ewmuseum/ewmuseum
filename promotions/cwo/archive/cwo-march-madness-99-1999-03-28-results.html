MARCH MADNESS '99 PPV (and pre-PPV SCORCH) Results - 3/28/99

March 30, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCORCH (PRE-PPV) 3/28/99 DARK MATCH: Chris Greco defeated Vegas Villain in 3:00 after a missile dropkick. Brian 'The King' Derks made Cris Marcus submit to the King's Crusher. Time of match: 5:22.
'The Avenger' Jesse Ewiak pinned "Playboy" Wayne Schillo after the Ewiakbomb. Time of match: 6:33. Wildfire and Union Caine wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 5:52. Hollywood Haskins/Mike 'Money' Sexton defeated Mercenaries when Mike 'Money' Sexton pinned Korperal Punishment after the Hollywood Hangover, pulling the tights. Time of match: 6:22.
NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (THREE-WAY DANCE): Ranger Taurus defeated 'Assassin 64' Sean Holiday and Warhawk in a three-way dance. Time of match: 12:23. Ranger Taurus pinned 'Assassin 64' Sean
Holiday after the double clothesline. Ranger Taurus won the CWO North
American Champion title.
Man Mountain Marko came out to do a very intense interview, finally calling
Mondo Gecko out, who obliged. The two brawled to close the show.
CWO MARCH MADNESS '99 3/28/99 St. Louis, MO (TWA Dome) SWEET 16: Col. Taggart made Fallen submit to the Annihilator. Time of match: 8:16. Mondo Gecko defeated Man Mountain Marko by countout when "Playboy" Wayne
Schillo came down to the ring and attacked Marko. Marko gave chase, and was then counted out. Time of match: 3:35. Marko delivered the Chi-Town Hammer to Gecko after the match.
Fergus 'Whirling Scottish Devil' McTavish pinned Foolkiller after the Highland Fling and outside interference by the Creatures of Darkness, who were hiding underneath the ring. Time of match: 11:30.
Sandman Jr. pinned Masher Maccabee after the Permanent Dream (flying legdrop). Time of match: 4:18.
Sandman pinned Secret Agent Super Dragon after the Sandblast. Time of match: 1:55. Lord Hile Troy pinned Australian Assassin after the top rope Dragon Suplex. Time of match: 0:09. 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin pinned Wrath after the Triad Bomb. Time of match: 5:39. Simon St. Severe pinned Flash Varga after the Cross Bearer, pulling the tights. Time of match: 6:05. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Champions the Renegades (Johnson and Young) defeated Newton Bros. by countout. Time of match: 9:44. Renegades retained the CWO North American
Tag Team Champions title.
SEMI-FINALS: Mondo Gecko pinned Col. Taggart after the flying legdrop, pulling the tights. Time of match: 9:46. Fergus 'Whirling Scottish Devil' McTavish and Sandman Jr. wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 13:00.
Lord Hile Troy pinned Sandman after the Viledriver. Time of match: 9:15. 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin pinned Simon St. Severe after the Triad Bomb. Time of match: 4:27. In an amazing segment, Brent "The Ace" Alles held his own brother hostage at gunpoint, demanding that he be reinstated AND be put in the Final Four-Way!
(Filling the gap left by the double DQ of Sandman Jr. and Fergus McTavish.) Gordy Alles was forced to agree. "The Ace" then revealed the gun to be a toy, and escaped through the crowd before the DSF could catch him.
TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (RUSSIAN CHAIN MATCH): Vladimir Popalov defeated Allen 'Mr. America' Carter in a chain match. Time of match: 8:20. Vladimir Popalov won the CWO Television Title.
FINAL FOUR-WAY Mondo Gecko defeated Lord Hile Troy and 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin and
Brent "The Ace" Alles in a four-way match. Time of match: 9:06. Mondo
Gecko pinned "Dark Shogun" Akueri Hakujin. Troy had the cover on Hakujin, but Alles pulled him out of the ring. Alles and Troy then brawled backstage.
WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Reb and Fantastic defeated the Creatures of Darkness by disqualification when the Fallen attacked. Time of match: 8:07. Creatures of Darkness retained the CWO World Tag Team Champions title.
WORLD CHAMPION TITLE MATCH (LUMBERJACK MATCH) Darkness made Mondo Gecko submit to the Blackout after Cris Marcus betrayed the Outlaws and delivered The X to Gecko outside the ring. Time of match:
19:23. Darkness retained the CWO World Champion title. After the match Darkness kept the hold no Gecko as the lumberjacks brawled back to the dressing room. The lights went off... and when they came back on, Empire was in the ring! Foolkiller delivered a Thunderfunk to Darkness, and the night ended with the crowd cheering for Empire!
