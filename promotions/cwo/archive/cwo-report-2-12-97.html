CWO REPORT 2/12/97

12 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO CONTROL CENTER.) JOHNNY STARR: A very short CWO REPORT for you today, folks, we will have an extensive one out tomorrow with our special segment, THE STORY SO FAR, in which a roundtable discussion will be held, profiling and previewing the great ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II PPV coming up this Friday. If you haven't seen it before, here is the lineup for that great card! _______________________________________________________________________________ ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II (card subject to change) ________________________________________________________ WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Hile Troy vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Highlanders (Conor and Gavin McCloud) vs. Creatures of the Night (champions, Night Crawler and Night Slinger) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Gold Kard vs. "Innovator of Violence" Miguel Sanchez NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: The Big Cool (Jumbo Jenkins and Jim "Jazzman" Taylor) vs. Bad Guys (Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson and Joe "The Mean Guy" Rucker) (champions) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (10-MAN BATTLE ROYAL): Brent "The Ace" Alles Benjamin Blake Bentley Cake Boy Inferno Maximum Overdrive Mondo Gecko Panther Sandman Jr. Sin Steel 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Doomsday (Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries, Kaptain Krunch and Korperal Punishment, champions) vs. North and South ("Lonestar" Mike Williams and the Coldfront (Steve White and Kurt Gerstung) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Tiger Rogers vs. Moses (champion) MONSTERS OF THE RING: "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Sandman (champion) SCIENTIFIC MATCH: Chris Greco vs. Clark Gray VEGAS ODDS MATCH: Pit Bosses vs. Eternal Wanderers (Exodus and Exile) BARBED WIRE TOWEL MATCH: Master of Darkness Damion (w/Rebel Yell) vs. Johnny Reb Bane vs. Wrath BURIED ALIVE MATCH: Bloodbath vs. Ganglord EYE ON THE STORM: Savior vs. Masher Maccabee Deadly Sins (Pride and Sloth) vs. Mason-Dixon (Stonewall Mason and Beauregard Dixon) DARK MATCH: Battlecat vs. Talon SHOWCASE SPECIAL: ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE (SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 16) 8-MAN TAG MATCH A (elimination): Joe Eagle, Jason "Flash" Varga, Regulators (Fire and Ice) vs. D. E. A. T. H. (Benton and Green) and Hell's Pets (Hellhound and Dark Raven) 8-MAN TAG MATCH B (elimination): Confederate Associates (Jackass Joel, Phantom Freightrain, Hoss and Little Joe) vs. David "The Destroyer" Davis, Goliath, and Da Hood Rats (Warren and Nate) SURVIVORS OF MATCH A vs. SURVIVORS OF MATCH B in an elimination match _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Last night's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER was as explosive as ever, let's look at what happened in our FEVER FLASHBACK! _______________________________________________________________________________ FEVER FLASHBACK TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, CLEVELAND, OH, 2/11/97 DARK MATCHES: Dark Raven defeated Hile Troy after the Death From Above flying dropkick and outside interference by Sandman. The ref stopped the match after Goliath had Bloodbath in the Goliath Grappler (horizontal backbreaker hold). Bloodbath was attacked right before this by Ganglord with a shovel. TELEVISED BROADCAST: Mondo Gecko defeated Chris Greco in 13:47. Mason-Dixon defeated the Pit Bosses in 18:22 after Beauregard Dixon pinned Pit Boss #1 after the Southern Spike piledriver. Miguel Sanchez defeated Panther in 8:22, pinning him after the Moonsault. Maximum Overdrive pinned Sin in 5:42 after the Pileup power bomb. After the match, Sin knocked out the ref and continued to attack Maximum Overdrive, but then Doomsday attacked, laying Sin out. The Deadly Sins made the save, and all eight men brawled back to the dressing room. Highlanders and Eternal Wanderers wrestled to a double disqualification. The Regulators were serving as the Highlanders bodyguards during this match, and they were needed, as the CWO World Tag Team champions Creatures of the Night attacked, and all four teams brawled all around the arena. Benjamin Blake Bentley defeated Focus in 7:57 after the Nobility (inverted piledriver.) Footage was shown of last week's attack on the Big Cool by the CWO North American Tag Team champions, the Bad Guys. Attitude Dance (Joe Eagle and Jason "Flash" Varga) upset the Bad Guys when Varga pinned Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson after the Flash Flood in 10:16. Masher Maccabee defeated Ganglord in 8:57 after the Jersualem Jacknife followed by the Homentaschen Hangover. Comments by Sandman and Master of Darkness Damion were seen, before their blindfold match. Master of Darkness Damion defeated Sandman in a "Master of Darkness" blindfold match in 3:03. Sandman rolled out of the ring after an incredible flying elbowdrop by MOD, and then "Monster" Rich Clark attacked from the crowd, chokeslamming Sandman on to the concrete, and rolling him back in for Damion to make the pin. Comments from the Deadly Sins regarding the Running of the Gauntlet match against Bane. In the Running of the Gauntlet match, Bane defeated Pride in 9:58, making him submit to the Merciful Death. He defeated Sloth in 19:38, also making him submit to the Merciful Death. Finally, Bane successfully ran the gauntlet, as he made Wrath submit to the Merciful Death in 26:53. Lust came into the ring, and Bane took off his mask and kissed her. He then threw her to the ground, called her a whore, and left the cage. Before the Sins could gain revenge, Ganglord and Da Hood Rats hit the ring area, brawling with the Sins. An alliance between Bane and Ganglord seemed apparent, however, Bane told Ganglord to "stay the hell out of my way" and to do what he wanted with the Sins, as he was finished with them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Before we sign off today, we have comments from the Highlanders, who will be trying to end the long World tag team title reign of the Creatures of the Night this Friday at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens in a CWO studio; in it are Conor and Gavin McCloud, the Highlanders!) GAVIN MCCLOUD: Well Dark Side, I hope you looked and looked long at last night's Fever. We're tired of yer run-in's and yer interferin' with other people's matches, so we're gonna ensure it dinnae happen to us on Friday. We've got a pair of good men, the best men, to watch our backs - and with the Regulators coverin' our backs, we can concentrate solely with matter in tha' ring. CONOR MCCLOUD: And what's this I see? Yes, it's Mister Chuckles, Damion, trying to give us tha' willies again. C'mon Damie-Boy, I know ye can be much more frightning than that....like if ye put a sheet o'er ye head, cut two eyeholes in it and shouted 'boo'! You want tae welcome us to yer world? Well, I'll give ye this promise, right here, right now. If ye even try to interfere with our match this Friday, I'll be welcoming my fist to yer face - and that reality, not some mumbo jumbo bollocks. GAVIN: Look out Dark Side, because here comes the sun. We're not scared of ye or yer hocus-pocus tactics, and on Friday night, we're gonna take care of business and take those belts back to who truly deserves them - the fan. Just remember.... CONOR: THERE CAN BE ONLY ONE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's it for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
