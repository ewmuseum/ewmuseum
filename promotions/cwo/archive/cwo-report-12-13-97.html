CWO REPORT 12/13/97

13 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up. We go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, December 13, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr, Mitch E. Washey is on vacation. We are merely one day away from the year-end spectacular that is CWO CELEBRATION, our third such "party for the fans" which of course includes all of the great matches you have come to expect from the CWO. First and foremost on everybody's minds, of course, is the "three-way dance" between the "CWO legend" Lord Hile Troy, the popular Mondo Gecko, and the CWO World Champion Johnny Reb with the belt on the line. Before we talk about that, though, we've had some great house shows this week leading up to CWO CELEBRATION, let's first take a look at some comments from CWO superstars, and then the results of this week's House Shows, starting with the Four Aces, who will be taking on the Alliance in an eight-man tag match this Sunday at CWO CELEBRATION. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The camera fades in on a room in the Four Aces' training facility. Mildly put, it's a wreck. Chris Greco is throwing things and cursing--remarkably--in three different languages at once. The other Aces--Clark Gray, Steve Keller, and Tommy Lightning--have obviously given up on trying to calm him down and are concentrating on minimizing the destruction) TOMMY LIGHTNING (as Chris Greco reaches for a poster of Wayne Carpenter hanging on one of the walls): NO! CHRIS GRECO (through clenched teeth): Take it. Get it out of my sight and put it somewhere safe. NOW. (Tommy scampers to comply.) GRECO: That son of a-- CLARK GRAY: GUN! GRECO: I was hitting my stride against Wildfire! How dare that creep interfere? KELLER: What did you expect? GRECO: I expected a clean match against Wildfire, and that's what I got, until Rogers stuck his face in! (He turns to the camera.) GRECO: Listen up, CWO. I want Rogers in the ring. Boston Streetfight, Texas Death, Steel Cage, Mongolian battle axes at twenty paces, I don't care which! I want a shot at him! And Rogers, your memory isn't so good, because I used to kick your @$$ every time we locked up, and I can't wait to do it again! (The camera fades out as Chris begins a fresh round of demolition.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Image of the Alliance and the Aces comes up against the CWO CELEBRATION backdrop.) STARR V-O: At CWO CELEBRATION, two returning factions go head to head as the Alliance collides with the Aces in what should be a great eight-man contest. (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Next we go to Sandman Jr., who of course is on the warpath ever since the Dark Side decided to mess with his woman, Kellen. Let's hear from the CWO's very own "hard-core icon." _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sandman Jr. is in the interview area.) SANDMAN JR.: I hear Damion come out talking about how life is useless, how it's stupid, how only mortals worry about it. I'm glad to hear that, MOD. You know WHY? Well, first of all, I'm a fan of Raven, and I can swear I heard it before from him. But more importantly, come Celebration, when I get my hands on you in the Cane match, I WILL kill you. I will grab the chane from the pole, winning the match, THEN I'LL CANE YOU TO DEATH! YOU'LL BE BLEEDING EVERYWHERE, BUT I WON'T STOP HITTING YOU! <WHACK> <WHACK> <WHACK> NON-STOP! YOU'RE A F***ING DEAD MAN DAMION! And I swear, I'm gonna get revenge on you, you're gonna cure Kellen first, but then, YOU ARE SO F***ING DEAD! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: A very emotional speech from Sandman Jr., and really, who can blame him considering what the Dark Side has put him and Kellen through so far. (Image of Sandman Jr. and Master of Darkness Damion comes up against the CWO CELEBRATION backdrop.) STARR V-O: Sandman Jr. and Master of Darkness Damion set to collide this Sunday at CWO CELEBRATION in a Singapore Cane Pole match, and it should be very explosive indeed. (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Another match on the PPV pits SJPW stars the Jade Dragons against the Highlanders, in which has swiftly become a match of honor. Let's hear from the Jade Dragons. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Jade Dragons are walking down the street in Grand Rapids.) KENJI ISHIDA: Deadly Sins, you defeated us. Nothing else needs to be said. Still, we gave you a battle, and next time, you may find youselves so lucky. What matters now is this sunday. Once more, we clash in battle with the Jade Dragons. Once more, the brigands who claim to know so much of 'honor' will face true warriors in combat. SHINJI USAMI: Kenji! Stop being so serious! The Highlanders are a tough team! However, they made a grave mistake when they attacked you and Kikuchi-san. They thought they were making a point. Instead, they started a war. A war they WILL lose. Highlanders, we don't care if you are former 3 time world champions. We beat you once before, and we can do it again. KENJI ISHIDA: Highlanders, you wanted a war, and now you've got one. You tried to attack the Wild Cards, but we put a stop to that, didn't we? You learned the hard way that when we said we would harry you at every turn, we meant it. You take us too lightly, and now you will pay for your folly! SHINJI USAMI: To quote a favorite movie of mine, "There can be only one", and when all is said and done, the Highlanders won't be the 'one'; it will be the Jade Dragons. Highlanders, prepare for war, for that is what the Jade Dragons are going to give you at CWO Celebration! _______________________________________________________________________________ (An image of the Jade Dragons and the Highlanders against the CWO CELEBRATION backdrop is shown.) STARR V-O: So there you have it, Dragons against Highlanders. Right now, we have the Highlanders through satellite link-up.... (Highlanders are shown in a split-screen.) STARR: Gentlemen, I have to ask you, how do you feel right know? GAVIN McCLOUD: Screwed. Screwed again by tha' CWO, by tha' championship commitee, an' by tha' wrestlers we once thought we could call friends. Have we nae done enough for this federation? We bring respect an' honour to tha' titles, we help it get world-wide recognition, an' this is how we get rewarded - fast counts, biased refs... I dinnae know why we bother sometimes. But ye here this, Krepps... NO way are ye runnin' tha' Highlanders out of this federation. We've got a contract, an' we're prepared ta take it all tha' way if we have to. CONOR McCLOUD: An' then we've got some team called tha' Jade Dragons, who we make ONE little mistake after a match and drop them on their wee heads, want ta insult us. Now lads, ye could have let it lie, an' let bygones be bygones... but ye had ta run off your mouths. Not just against us, but our entire clan an' nation; an' now you've made tha' ultimate mistake - WE'RE RIGHT P****D OFF! The Bad Girls mocked us, an' then we took them down in under thirty seconds... but tha's NOTHING compared ta what we'll do ta ye! Ye don't like us? FINE! But ye've got no right ta start mocking tha' honour of our clan, an' our nation. Instead of two of the hardest men in tha' world wantin' ta tear ye throats out, ye've 2 MILLION! GAVIN McCLOUD: Ye FIRST mistake will be ye last, Dragons. We've got nae complaint wi' Japan, just you, ye pair o' big-mouthed, self-centred twats. We're gonna tear ye heads off and spit down ye throat... an' we won't be on our own, either. A proud nation is on tha' march, Dragons, and ye've only got yeselves ta blame for what's coming soon. STARR: All right, thank you, gentlemen. (Back to a single screen.) STARR: And now, for a change of pace, let's hear from the World Tag Team Champions and their associates, the Deadly Sins. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Deadly Sins are in the interview area.) PRIDE: Oh God, I guess it's really true. We really DO have to face Joker and, well... Joker, The Wild Cards. Why? What have they actually DONE? Well, whatever. Cards, you're facing not only the Tag Team Champions of the World, but the team that is STILL North American Tag Champs of the World! That's right, we were never beat, someone... KREPPS... stole the belts out of our lockers. Doesn't matter. You won't be winning them or anything else. You're going down. SLOTH: After we make those sorry punks the Cards into pancakes, you know who else is clamoring for a shot? The CPE Enforcers. I gotta ask again: what the hell are you enforcing, who you enforcing for, and HOW do you enforce? In the ring? I've seen monkeys show more talent in the ring then you two. You REALLY want to get in the ring with the Sins, who, since we haven't reminded you in the past 5 seconds, are the TAG TEAM CHAMPS, we'll see if we can make it happen. PRIDE: And the re-united Pit Bosses want a match... how can you even trust each other? Besides, you haven't been back long enough... nah, you ain't getting these belts, cause you ain't getting a shot. Settle on beating the crap out of Myers and Fulcrum, before we get a chance to do it. SLOTH: Jade Dragons, you can go back to SJPW and tell them "Ah so. We lose to great tag team world. Deadly Sins kick asses us." And you'd be right. Thing is, we completely dominated and destroyed you, and you're a team we DON'T hate! Imagine what we coulda done if we DID hate you! In other words, don't do anything, like request a rematch, that'd piss us off. WRATH: Focus, it still isn't over. Look over your back constantly, cause LOOK OUT!, it's Wrath right there, ready to make you submit. See this shirt I'm wearing? "Wrath 3:16" it says on the front. The back? "I Just Made Your Ass Repent!" I WILL make you submit! I WILL make you repent! You'll cry in pain as the TRUE master of inflicting it does so. You're gonna fall and fall hard, and there ain't nothing you can do to stop me. Be prepared to be embarassed. As for Lord Hile Troy, you know as well as I do that I had you beat if not for that punk the Focus. Even though Reb is our buddy, I do kinda hope you win the belt, cause then *I* am gonna be the man to unseat you. I won't challenge a friend like Reb, but an antique, outdated, shriveled-up old man like you? Yeah, I'll do that. So good luck, not only will you need it, but if you win, then you got ME on your back! LUST: Tamara Wilcox, do I LOOK like a fighter? I'm a lover baby. But if you want to roll around in the ring with me, that's fine. You're in for a beating though. I can't wait to pin you down. (She licks her lips as the camera fades.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny, who looks mildly uncomfortable.) STARR: Uh... yeah. Anyway, from those comments, we can give you three other matches that will be part of the great CWO CELEBRATION card, starting with.... (Image of Lust and Tamara Wilcox comes up against the EYE ON THE STORM backdrop.) STARR V-O: Lust going against Tamara Wilcox. The CWO's women's divsion is long defunct, but if it were to start back up, you can be sure that these two women would be glad to compete in it. Also.... (Image of Wrath and the Focus comes up against the CWO CELEBRATION backdrop.) STARR V-O: Wrath takes on the Focus in a submission match, the feud between these two men has really heated up here in the latter half of 1997, will this be their final confrontation, or will it continue into 1998? Whatever the case, one of them is going to have to say "I Quit" in order for the other to get the victory. And finally.... (Image of the Wild Cards and the Deadly Sins Pride and Sloth comes up.) STARR V-O: For the World Tag Team Championships, they beat the former three-time champions the Highlanders to earn the shot, the Wild Cards go up against the Deadly Sins. (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We here in the CWO are of course honored to be associated with the greatest wrestling federation in Japan today, the SJPW. There superstars have provided us with a lot of great action, and they should continue to do so at CWO CELEBRATION, especially Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, who gets a shot at the Light Heavyweight Championship, currently held by Jason "Flash" Varga. Let's hear from Lanther. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Masashi Lanther is standing in front of an SJPW "Frank A Marano Jr. tag team tournament" banner. He is wearing a costume of blue and gold this time.) MASASHI "LIGHTNING" LANTHER: Southern Dynamite, we had a well fought battle, but in the end, I prevailed. If you ever wish to face me again, I will be happy to oblige. This Sunday, at CWO Celebration, I face my second chance for the Light Heayweight title. Jason Varga, it seems we come full circle. My last title shot was against you, and it went to a draw. You lost the title, then regained it, with a little help. You defeated Carlos Maia, a man I've also defeated, and now the stage is set. You and I will face for a second time. You are an excellent wrestler, Varga, but you are facing one of the best Light Heavyweights in all of Japan. Between my win over Southern Dynamite, and my victory of Super Tengu in SJPW, I've defeated two of the best in the world. Now it comes down to you and I. Are you ready to once more lose your title? I have every intention of taking that title from you, Varga. I've worked hard to earn this shot, and this time, you will not be saved by the time limit. This time, Lightning WILL strike twice! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: When we come back, as promised, a look at what happened this week during the House Shows. (Logo and music come up as we fade to commercial.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Black and white image of Lord Hile Troy is shown as music plays.) VOICE-OVER: One is a legend.... (Mondo Gecko is shown hitting the Top Rope Frankensteiner.) VOICE-OVER: One may just be the future of the CWO.... (Johnny Reb is shown holding the World Championship belt high.) VOICE-OVER: And one is the champion.... (All three men are shown on the screen.) VOICE-OVER: This Sunday... only on PPV... the legend... the future... and the champion... all collide in a three-way dance in which only one can win... only one can be champion.... (The CWO CELEBRATION logo explodes on to the screen.) VOICE-OVER: It's the end of the year, it's time to party! CWO CELEBRATION, this Sunday, only on PPV! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: All right, fans, welcome back to the REPORT. Now, let's take a look at what went down at this week's House Shows. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 12/10/97 Saginaw, MI Sandman pinned Wesley 'The Rock' McMichael after the outside interference by Dark Side. McMichael hit the Texas Tombstone on Sandman but Master of Darkness Damion distracted the referee. Bloodbath then came off the top rope with an elbow drop to McMichael's head - and then rolled Sandman on top of McMichael for the pin. Time of match: 11:54. North American Tag Team Champions 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum/Diamondback Chris Myers defeated Youth in Action when 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum pinned Youth #2 after the Air Fulcrum. Time of match: 13:18. Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo pinned El Grande Pistolerro after the kneedrop and outside interference by Hellhound. Time of match: 12:52. The Big Cool and Wild Cards wrestled to a 30 minute time limit draw. Mondo Gecko pinned Brian 'The Hammer' Williams after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 6:59. Brother Justice and Foolkiller/'The Ultimate Heel' Union Caine wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 8:10. Southern Dynamite and Joe Eagle wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Bad Guys defeated Highlanders when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Conor McCloud after the Bad Guys' Bash. Herbie Versmells held the leg of Conor for the pin. Time of match: 14:44. Sandman Jr. defeated Bloodbath by disqualification. Sandman Jr. was attacked by the Dark Side. The CPE made the save. Time of match: 10:22. World Tag Team Champions the Deadly Sins defeated Chris Greco/Clark Gray when Pride pinned Chris Greco after the tumbleweed. Time of match: 12:29. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 12/11/97 Kalamazoo, MI Hellhound and Painmaster wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 16:42. 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat defeated Foolkiller by disqualification when Empire attacked. Foolkiller then Thunderfunked Heat. Time of match: 9:29. Texas Roughriders defeated Mason-Dixon when Wesley 'The Rock' McMichael pinned Stonewall Mason after the Texas Tombstone. Time of match: 20:45. 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia pinned Wrath after the dropkick. The Focus interfered against Wrath - helping Maia with the pin. Time of match: 11:27. Steve Keller/Tommy Lightning defeated Devil Dogs when Tommy Lightning pinned DevilDog Joey after the splash. Time of match: 9:19. Tommy Bomber and Chris Greco wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 6:21. Wild Ones defeated Dogs of War when Mondo Gecko pinned Logan Fett after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 16:59. Joe Eagle and Gold Kard wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 5:40. The Big Cool defeated Mercenaries when Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor pinned Kaptain Krunch after the flying knee to face. Time of match: 14:21. Light Heavyweight Champion Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned Television Champion Wildfire after the Flash Flood. Time of match: 18:27. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 12/12/97 Michigan State University, Lansing, MI 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia pinned The Focus after the Falling in Love. Wrath interfered in this match - setting the Focus up for the pinfall. Time of match: 5:31. Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo defeated Masashi 'Lightning' Lanther by disqualification when the Law attacked. Time of match: 5:09. 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat pinned Clark Gray after the Jimmyplex. Time of match: 4:10. Painmaster defeated Mondo Gecko by disqualification when "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher attacked him. Time of match: 14:10. El Grande Pistolerro defeated Gold Kard by countout when Gold Kard walked back to the dressing room. Time of match: 11:30. Tsutomu Kobayashi pinned Mustafa Rahim after the reversed backslide. Kobayashi was attacked by Gold Kard after the match. Time of match: 16:11. Sandman defeated Foolkiller by disqualification. Sandman was attacked by Empire early on in this match - leading the Dark Side to make the save. The two teams then brawled around the arena. Time of match: 1:08. North American Tag Team Champions 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum/Diamondback Chris Myers defeated Mason-Dixon when 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum pinned Beauregard Dixon after the Air Fulcrum. Time of match: 4:55. STEEL CAGE I QUIT MATCH: Mondo Gecko made World Champion Johnny Reb submit to the Boston crab in a non-title match. Time of match: 10:44. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 12/13/97 (matinee) University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, MI Mondo Gecko defeated Master of Darkness Damion by disqualification when the Dark Side attacked. Time of match: 2:18. Foolkiller pinned 'Lone Wolf' Ryan Deutscher after the DDT. Time of match: 8:29. Hellhound pinned 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum after the elbow drop and outside interference by Master of Darkness Damion. Master of Darkness Damion was forced to leave the building after this by Tom Krepps. Time of match: 19:07. Sandman pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Sandblast. Time of match: 9:18. Bad Guys defeated Jade Dragons when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Shinji Usami after the Bad Guys' Bash. Time of match: 10:28. Tsutomu Kobayashi defeated Gold Kard by countout when Gold Kard walked back to the dressing room. Time of match: 16:18. Wild Ones defeated Creatures of the Night when Mondo Gecko pinned Night Slinger after the cradle. Time of match: 16:10. Dogs of War defeated Pit Bosses when Eddie Fantastic pinned Pit Boss #1 after the Showstopper, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 15:54. Television Champion Wildfire defeated World Champion Johnny Reb by disqualification when Bloodbath attacked. Time of match: 18:54. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight, check your local listings for time and station, is the CWO SATURDAY NIGHT SPECIAL, let's take a look at what you're going to see LIVE from the Orbit Room in Grand Rapids, MI! _______________________________________________________________________________ SATURDAY NIGHT SPECIAL 12/13/97 "LIVE" FROM THE ORBIT ROOM, GRAND RAPIDS, MI FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE IN THE ORBIT ROOM: Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Sandman THREE-WAY DANCE: SJPW's Tsutomu Kobayashi vs. Gold Kard vs. Joe Eagle FOUR-WAY MATCH (winner gets a shot at the World Tag Team Championship in January): Pit Bosses vs. Bad Guys vs. Creatures of the Night vs. Dogs of War _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: He was attacked by him Tuesday night on FEVER, and now Brent "The Ace" Alles will take on Sandman in a Falls Count Anywhere match on SATURDAY NIGHT SPECIAL. Let's hear from the "Ace." _______________________________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is in the interview area, his arms folded.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: You know, Sandman, it seems you and I have been down this road before. And I don't care if you're a "dark minion" of Damion, I think you know that crap doesn't impress me much. What does impress me is that you're an over 350 pound guy who can powerslam a guy off the top of the turnbuckle like he's nothing. That impresses me. So I'm not going into this Saturday's match against you with any lowered expectations. I know that I am in for the fight of my life. But, Sandman, what you don't realize is that this is my hometown, and this truly is going to be the weekend of the Ace. So bring the Dark Side, bring a bucket full of weapons, bring the pain, but bring the realization, Sandman, is that I'll Allesbreaker you anywhere... ANYWHERE... and that's it. CO1 - EOT. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Should be a great one. Before we leave, today, let's take one more look at the lineup for CWO CELEBRATION. _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO CELEBRATION '97 VAN ANDEL ARENA, GRAND RAPIDS, MI SUNDAY, DECEMBER 14, 1997 WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (THREE-WAY DANCE): Lord Hile Troy vs. Mondo Gecko vs. Johnny Reb (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Wild Cards vs. Deadly Sins (champions) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (SCAFFOLD MATCH, TABLES IN THE RING): Masher Maccabee vs. Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Foolkiller and "The Ultimate Heel" Union Caine vs. "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum and Diamondback Chris Myers (champions) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (casket match): Bloodbath vs. Wildfire (champion) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: SJPW's Masashi "Lightning" Lanther vs. Jason "Flash" Varga (champion) STEEL CAGE STREET FIGHT (match will continue until both members of a team are unable to continue) Mercenaries vs. The Big Cool SINGAPORE CANE POLE MATCH: Sandman Jr. vs. Master of Darkness Damion Highlanders vs. SJPW's Jade Dragons SUBMISSION MATCH: The Focus vs. Wrath Mustafa Rahim and the Prophet vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo and Brian "The Hammer" Williams Southern Dynamite vs. "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia 8-MAN TAG ACTION: Chris Greco, Clark Gray, Steve Keller, and Tommy Lightning vs. Tiger Rogers, Goliath, David "The Destroyer" Davis, and Inferno El Grande Pistolerro vs. Hellhound Mason-Dixon vs. Devil Dogs "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher vs. Painmaster EYE ON THE STORM: CWO CELEBRATION '97 Wesley "The Rock" McMichael and Ranger Taurus vs. Brother Justice Lust vs. Tamara Wilcox _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's it for today. We'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT!
