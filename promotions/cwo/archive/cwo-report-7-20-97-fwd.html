CWO REPORT 7/20/97 (fwd)

21 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Diamondback Chris Myers is sitting on the beachfront in Sydney, Australia, watching the waves roll in. He has the CWO TV belt across his lap) Myers: So much to comment on, so little time...let's do this on a first come, first serve basis. FOCUS. A man who takes no flak from anyone, comes into the ring, does his job, wins, and leaves. And now he wants to challange me for this belt. (Myers runs his hand through his brown hair) Myers: I guess this means I've arrived in CWO. With all this GKI/CWO stuff going on, people are still taking notice of Diamondback Chris Myers. Focus, you've got the guts to challange me, so sign the contract, and I'll put this belt on the line. Now, we move to Masher. Guess what? The Rattler is my finishing move. I thought of it all by myself, and NO ONE else in wrestling today uses anything like it. I ESPECIALLY didn't copy it from you. Now, you want a shot at this belt, huh? What if I said no? Not because I'm a wimp, but because I don't think you deserve it. Macabee, being with GKI does not automatically give you a title shot, and I don't care how much stock Gold Kard owns. You have to do a lot more to impress me and get a shot. Wrath. You still think I'm some hotshot rookie? I defended this belt against two of CWO's toughest, Bentley and Sandman. You had a title shot, and I have enough honor and pride to give it to you. But don't take me lightly, because I'll be hitting you with everything I have, and you will go down. Bentley. You are nothing but a two-bit coward. First, you have to try to indimidate a referee to even come close to beating me. Then, you spray me with hairspray and Nobility me three times, twice on concrete. And then what happened? MY MANAGER BEAT YOU UP! Dallas took you out with one swing of a steel chair! To quote a great American figure, "You're really beginning to annoy me, son!" Make up your mind...do you want to beat me for this belt or do you want to just beat me up? Either way, I'll Rattle your ass eight ways from Sunday. And last, we come to Sandman and his little friend. Sandman, you were supposed to be this big and bad guy who EVERYONE in CWO fears. Well, I beat you. Fair and square. And you had to jump me from behind and get your robed friend to surfboard me as FEVER went off the air. Chummers, let's settle this fairly. Sandman Jr., you said you wanted a tag match with me against Sandman and his cronie. FINE BY ME, MAN! LET'S SHOW THESE PUNKS JUST HOW EXTREME WE CAN BE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey, standing on a street corner somewhere in downtown Melbourne, Australia.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to another edition of the CWO REPORT. Last night, the CWO appeared in Sydney to a sold out crowd of CWO Australian wrestling fans. Let's look at what went down last night! HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 7/17/97 Sydney, Australia El Grande Pistolerro pinned Panther after the Springboard Frankensteiner. Time of match: 7:47. After the match Pistolerro was attacked by members of the Law until other members of the Wild bunch made the save. Sandman Jr. pinned Steel after the Permanent Dream. Time of match: 6:59. Fergus 'Whirling Scottish Devil' McTavish pinned 'Mr. High Impact' Mysterio after the Highland Fling. Time of match: 5:40. Brother Justice defeated Mason-Dixon when Jermain Justice pinned Beauregard Dixon after the Final Justice. Time of match: 7:52. Lord Hile Troy pinned Jason 'Flash' Varga after the Dragon Suplex. Time of match: 13:57. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: The Big Cool defeated the Mercenaries by countout when the Mercenaries brawled back to the dressing room with the Regulators. Time of match: 5:18. CWO WORLD CHAMPION TITLE MATCH (LUMBERJACK MATCH): Gold Kard (w/Lord Hile Troy, Moses, Masher Maccabee, Benjamin Blake Bentley, and Matsuhiro Torunaka) pinned Joe Eagle (w/Jason "Flash" Varga, The Big Cool, Johnny Reb, and Mondo Gecko) after Die Flieder Kross while the lumberjacks brawled outside the ring . Time of match: 18:16. Gold Kard defended the CWO World Champion title. WASHEY: Tonight, the CWO continues it's tour of Down Under with a show in the beautiful city of Melbourne. Let's look at the rundown of that card. CWO HOUSE SHOW Melbourne, Australia 7/20/97 Brother Justice vs. Attitude Dance Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Miguel Sanchez Masher Maccabee vs. Sandman Jr. Wrath vs. the Focus CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH Mondo Gecko (champion) vs. Moses (challenger) COAL MINER'S GLOVE MATCH "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Joe Eagle Gold Kard/Matsuhiro Torunaka vs. Wild Side WASHEY: This card has the potential for another blow out. Few tickets are remaining, and the ones that are are being snapped up. Forget the WWF and WCW. They might have what it takes to go over to Europe, but they don't have what it takes to take the action of this sport to the Land Down Under. Let's get to the interviews. We heard from Masher Maccabee yesterday, now another member of GKI is standing by with comments. Here's Lord Hile Troy. ____________________________________________________________________________ _______ Sitting at the club house of a GKI owned golf course and country club is the living legend, Lord Hile Troy. While not working on his golfing game, he is busy shewing away the fans. Turning his head, he notices the CWO cameraman Lord Hile Troy: I'm glad you showed up, I've got a world of things I can tell you this time. First on the GKI agenda, the North American title belt is right where it belongs, back in GKI hands! Secondly, that TV title belt will once again be in our grasp as well. Don't even fret BBB, there's no way that the flash in the pan, Myers, can keep that title from a sound perfectionist like yourself, or myself for that matter. Take it from me kid, we'll go places, walk the strips, live the life. Lord Hile Troy: Speaking of a "flash in the pan", how about Jason Varga? Looks like the poor little college boy is a little confused as of late, everyone is pointing fingers at Schillo, the little punk who cost me the world title way back when. There is nothing I like _more_ than watching these idiots try to figure out our plans. (A waiter brings Troy a wine spritzer) Lord Hile Troy: The man himself, Gold Kard, with the belt that he has seeked for oh so long. The Sandman, he may he a little confused for now, but his true colors will show eventually. We've got Alles in rehab, and he's coming along nicley. But the real story is this strike business. The wrestlers from the league which I saved, are now trying to strike against us? I can't believe you guys are actually using your brains! However, you made one mistake, you gave us the chance to fight back with the Team Challenge Series. Gold Kard and I, the two wisest veterans in the CWO, along with Maccabee and Moses, this shall be no contest! ____________________________________________________________________________ _______ WASHEY: One of the CWO's most violent feuds is being carried out between the North American Tag Team Champions and the former champions, the Regulators. As you have seen from the House Show results, both teams have managed to interfere in the other's matches on this overseas tour. We saw the Regulator's comments yesterday, now let's hear from the Mercenaries. ____________________________________________________________________________ _______ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who are laughing about their interferance costing the Regulators their match against the Cold Front. The Mercenaries are wearing the CWO tag team titles across their shoulders.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Regulators, do you have ANY idea of the crap you're saying? We're overrated bums who need to learn how to wrestle? Where the hell were you all those times where we kicked your asses? You can try to get even, but it's a futile effort. You are a couple of never-were's, and it was time that we exposed you for the idoits that you know you really are. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: You think we're dead men walking? What a joke. You want to show us NOW how things are done? What's the matter, did you go to a real wrestling school in the last few weeks? Face it, you can't show us a damn thing because we've seen your best shots, taken your best shots, and we've gotten up from your best shots. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: You are the two biggest fakes walking in this federation. Do you have ANY idea of the jokes that all the other tag teams are saying about you in the locker rooms? You've provided us with many hours of entertainment, both IN the ring and OUT of the ring. Say what you want, but everybody knows that you have no chance in hell of EVER getting these titles back in your hands, unless WE decide to give them up. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: That goes for ANY of the tag teams in this federation. Coldfront, Big Cool, Deadly Sins, Brother Justice. NO tag team can defeat us, not even the so-called World Champions, the Bad Girls. When we decide to go for those titles, it's all over. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Hey. Why don't we try to take BOTH titles? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Why not? After all, we are THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD!!! ____________________________________________________________________________ ________ WASHEY: Well, that's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody tomorrow on the REPORT. So long everybody! (fade to closing credits.)
