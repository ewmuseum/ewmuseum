CWO REPORT 2/19/97

19 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Wednesday, February 19, 1997. Last night's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER was of course as explosive as ever, let's get the results in our FEVER FLASHBACK! _______________________________________________________________________________ FEVER FLASHBACK 2/18/97 "LIVE" FROM CHARLOTTE, NC HOUR #1 (Jerry Covenant and Mike Postema announcing) Comments from Mondo Gecko, celebrating his Television title win at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, lead off the show. Comments from the Highlanders regarding their loss at SVDMII and also their match tonight against D. E. A. T. H. Highlanders (Gavin and Conor McCloud) defeated D. E. A. T. H. (Benton and Green) by countout in 13:23. Comments from Bane regarding the attack by the Masked Rose on him and also his match tonight against Jason "Flash" Varga. Jason "Flash" Varga defeated Bane by disqualification in 8:41 when the Masked Rose attacked HIM (Varga). Bane and the Masked Rose then brawled back to the dressing room. Brent "The Ace" Alles defeated Cake Boy by disqualification when Doomsday attacked him. Each of the members of Doomsday delivered their finisher to Alles, requiring him to be taken out on a stretcher, which Maximum Overdrive kicked over once before being detained by security. During the beating, Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (whom Doomsday have acted as "protectors" for in the past) was seen in the rafters with a sad look on his face before turning and walking away. Comments from Masher Maccabee regarding the state of the CWO and his match tonight for the Television title against Mondo Gecko. Comments from Mondo Gecko about the match as well. Masher Maccabee defeated Mondo Gecko in 8:34 in a Taped Fists match to win the Television title, rolling up the former champion in a small package for the win. After the match, Maccabee continued to beat on Gecko, prompting CWO broadcaster Tamara Wilcox to try to protect him by covering him with her own body. Maccabee picked Wilcox off of Gecko and threw her back, causing her to accidentally hit the top turnbuckle with the back of her head, knocking her unconscious. Inferno defeated Chris Gray in 1:42, pinning him after a piledriver. Hile Troy makes an impromtu interview, announcing his retirement after tonight. HOUR #2 (Kyle Melinn and David Thomas announcing) Comments from Wildfire and Bloodbath about their match tonight with Da Hood Rats, and also talking about the aftermath of the Buried Alive match at SVDMII. Comments from Da Hood Rats and Ganglord about the match tonight as well. Wildfire and Bloodbath and Da Hood Rats (Warren and Nate) quickly battled to a double disqualification in 1:18. While Da Hood Rats doubleteamed Bloodbath, Ganglord attacked Wildfire and Ghetto Bombed him through a table. Interview with the Pit Bosses, as they announce their plans for the CWO World Tag Team Champions, the Creatures of the Night. Footage is shown of last week's FEVER, where Benjamin Blake Bentley used the ropes for leverage to pin the Focus. Comments from the Focus about his match with Bentley tonight. Comments from Bentley, in which he fires his butler. Benjamin Blake Bentley defeated the Focus in 7:19, pinning him after a clothesline and pulling the tights. Even though the Focus had requested a second referee outside the ring for this match, the second referee was distracted (on purpose) by the attractive valet Bentley brought down with him. Comments from the Deadly Sins about SVDMII and their match tonight against the Biblical Powers. Biblical Powers defeat the Deadly Sins in 10:30 when Savior pinned Pride after the Revelations top rope powerdrop. Comments from Johnny Reb about Southern Pride's match with Doomsday tonight for the World 6-Man Tag Team Championship. Also, Reb makes a challenge to Miguel Sanchez. Comments from Doomsday about their match with Southern Pride tonight. Southern Pride (Johnny Reb and Mason-Dixon) defeated the World 6-Man Tag Team Champions Doomsday (Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries, Kaptain Krunch and Korperal Punishment) by disqualification in 4:00 when Maximum Overdrive pulled down the rope, causing Johnny Reb to fall out. While Doomsday double-teamed Mason-Dixon, Johnny Reb was attacked by Gold Kard, who choked him with a gold chain. Hile Troy defeated Master of Darkness Damion in 22:46, pinning him with a small package. After the match many of the superstars of the CWO came down to wish Troy well in his retirement. Also coming down, though, were Sandman and Talon. Sandman claimed that Troy couldn't retire since Sandman had a contract to fight him at SPRING BREAK '97. However, Sandman agreed to fight a "protege" of Troy's, but if he beat the protege, he then would get to fight Troy as well. Hile Troy now has 17 days to find such a protege, or he must get in the ring one more time and face the Sandman. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we are in Columbia, SC, let's look at the great lineup planned for you folks down there. _______________________________________________________________________________ WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 19, 1997 COLUMBIA, SC D. E. A. T. H. vs. Higlanders Bloodbath vs. Mondo Gecko Mercenaries vs. Regulators Joe Eagle vs. Maximum Overdrive Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Master Of Darkness Damion vs. Gold Kard Savior vs. Bane LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung vs. Tiger Rogers NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Wild Ones vs. Bad Guys (champions) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (loser leaves town table match): "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Miguel Sanchez (champion) WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (strap match in a cage): Moses vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: A new superstar has arrived in the CWO, and his name is Arlon Flaym. _______________________________________________________________________________ ("Limelight" by Rush plays and Arlon appears in the ring in an empty arena as green light cascades down around him.) ARLON FLAYM: Hello, ladies and gentlemen. My name is Arlon Flaym. I'm here to win. I'm here to take the championship title. I'm just here for that. I want everyone to know I'm not some new rookie guy in here to meet big time wrestlers. I don't care if you're a king or a little street sweeper, sooner or later you dance with the reaper. ("Limelight" plays again as we fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: After last night's vicious attack by Gold Kard on Johnny Reb, you can be sure that the fiery Southerner will be looking for revenge. We take you now to comments from Johnny Reb. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb and General Lee are in the interview area) JOHNNY REB: Gold Kard, what have y'all done? Signed your own death warrant, it looks like from here. Jumpin' me from mah backside... chokin' me out. Well, Kraut, ah am your judge, jury and executioner. Miguel Sanchez can wait; ah want your sorry ass. GENERAL LEE: Gold Kard, history seems ta repeat itself a lot. Last tahm you jumped on this man... well, we still see that limp come out every once in awhile. REB: Gold Kard, y'all fancy yourself to be the 'master wrestler'. Well, it seems that now y'all are gonna be taught a lesson at the hands of Johnny Reb. Ah will teach you respect. Ah will teach you honor. And ah will teach you once and fo' all not to come lookin' fo' Johnny Reb. LEE: Friday naht, with 7,000 screamin' Southerners in his corner, Johnny Reb is gonna take you apart. Good day, suh. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: The macabre Cake Boy is still seething after being eliminated from the 10-man battle royal for the Television championship at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II. We take you to comments from Cake Boy. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Cake Boy is in the interview area with Toodles the chimp.) CAKE BOY: Bentley, you may have thrown me out of the battle royal... but I've never been too big a fan of group activity... that's why I'm looking to get you... one on one... let's see just how yummy of a man you are... mmmmmm. (Cake Boy licks his lips.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Another person experiencing a lot of frustration these days is Sandman Jr. We take you to comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Kellen and Sandman Jr. are in the interview area, Kellen is looking scared as Jr. is pissed.) SANDMAN JR.: DAMN IT! (slams his fist into the wall) I shoulda won that TV belt! I haven't won a thing in months! Bloodbath, tomorrow night in Biloxi, MS, I'm going to take all this out on you! It's going to be a bloodbath, all right, it'll just be ALL YOUR BLOOD! AARRGGHH! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight in Columbia, SC, the tough team of the Regulators take on the Mercenaries. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Regulators are in the interview area.) REGULATOR ICE: Mercenaries, you two thugs have tormented the teams of the CWO for too long and we are serving you warning. GET READY TO BE REGULATED! We are tired of all these rulebreakers taking advantage of the CWO's good nature and we are going to put an end to it. Mercenaries, get ready to go to Hell becasue when we are done with you two losers that is where you will be headed. REGULATOR FIRE: Mercenaries, you have been given fair warning and now you must pay for your attacks. We came here with two goals in mind and they were to win Tag Team gold and to clean up the CWO. Mercenaries, we are going to do the right thing with you two by taking out the trash. Get ready, Mercenaries, because you have outlived your stay. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Even after last night's vicious attack by Masher Maccabee, Mondo Gecko is luckily OK, and he is ready for action not only in singles competition, but with his friend Battlecat as well as they go after the North American Tag Team Championships, currently held by the Bad Guys. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wild Ones are in the interview area.) BATTLECAT: Bad Guys, you two thugs have discraced the belts and caused chaos in the CWO. Wednesday, the Wild Ones will continue their roll and take the North American titles. It has been my dream to win gold and bring back respect to my country, my family, and my god. Now with the help of my talented partner Mondo Gecko I will help restore the Mexican tradition and win the titles. MONDO GECKO: Hey, Bad Guys, you two bums definitely need an attitude adjustment and we are just the guys to do it. We have climbed the ranks of the tag team ladder and now it is only a short period of time before we knock you out and take those titles back to the people. DIG IT! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: One person in particular who was angered by Maccabee's attack on not only Gecko but CWO broadcaster Tamara Wilcox as well was the World Champion, Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo is in the interview area.) WAYNE "KEUKA GUY" SCHILLO: Masher Maccabee, you've gotta be one of the biggest morons in the CWO. I try to be a nice guy, I try not to hurt people too badly, but you should know better than this... You've been in the CWO long enough to realize that every wrestler who hurts someone weaker than he is will be doomed to face me the next week in a steel cage. I love the ladies, and what you did to Tamera is inexcusible. Tamara, Mondo Gecko, I hope you're both OK. As for you, Masher, I'd look into retirement before next week comes. You do NOT want to face me in that cage next week. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today on the REPORT, we'll see you tomorrow.
