CWO REPORT 2/20/97

20 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Thursday, February 20, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the superstars of the CWO were in Columbia, SC, let's look at what went down on that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 2/19/97 Columbia, SC Highlanders defeated D. E. A. T. H. when Conor McCloud pinned Green after the Quickening. Time of match: 14:58. After the match D. E. A. T. H. attacked a ringside fan, beating him to a bloody mess. Mondo Gecko pinned Bloodbath after the Top Rope Frankensteiner. Time of match: 5:04. Regulators defeated Mercenaries when Regulator Fire pinned Kaptain Krunch after the Tombstone. Time of match: 11:31. Before the match, the Mercenaries attacked the Regulators with steel chairs, however, the Regulators managed to rally back and win the match. Joe Eagle defeated Maximum Overdrive by countout. Time of match: 12:34. Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Flash Flood. Time of match: 9:33. Gold Kard pinned 'Master of Darkness' Damion after the elbow drop. Time of match: 10:30. Savior defeated Bane by countout. Time of match: 3:56. Bane was attacked again by the Masked Rose and chased him back to the dressing room, causing the countout. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Champion Tiger Rogers pinned Kurt 'Thrill from the Chill' Gerstung after the power bomb. Time of match: 2:07. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP Wild Ones defeated champions the Bad Guys by countout when Attitude Dance attacked the Bad Guys outside the ring, leading both of those teams to brawl back to the dressing room. Time of match: 1:17. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (FINISHER THROUGH THE TABLE MATCH, LOSER LEAVES TOWN) 'Monster' Rich Clark pinned champion Miguel Sanchez after the Choke Slam through the table. Time of match: 20:56. 'Monster' Rich Clark won the CWO North American title. Miguel Sanchez now has to leave the CWO. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (STEEL CAGE STRAP MATCH) Champion Wayne 'Keuka Guy' Schillo pinned Moses after the Keuka Crack, dragging him around the ring and touching all four turnbuckles. Time of match: 9:19. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Well, first of all, let's address the D. E. A. T. H. situation. After suffering a loss to the Highlanders, D. E. A. T. H. took it out on a ringside fan who had been taunting them throughout the match. That fan luckily did not suffer severe injuries, and it is unknown at this time if he will be pressing charges against Benton and Green, together known as D. E. A. T. H. However, CWO Commissioner Randy Johnson has indefinitely suspended the team until all further investigations and processes involving the team are complete. As always, we'll keep you posted. However, tonight we are in Biloxi, MS, let's look at the lineup for tonight! _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 20, 1997 BILOXI, MS Mason-Dixon vs. Attitude Dance Sandman Jr. vs. Bloodbath Inferno vs. Mondo Gecko Chris Greco vs. Maximum Overdrive CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE: Light Heavyweight champ Tiger Rogers vs. North American champ "Monster" Rich Clark Goliath vs. Bane "Lonestar" Mike Williams vs. The Focus Da Hood Rats vs. Biblical Powers WORLD SIX-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Wild Bunch (Regulators & Battlecat) vs. Doomsday (Baby Waldo and the Mercenaries, champions) Panther vs. Mondo Gecko CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE: Creatures of the Night (World champs) vs. Bad Guys (NA champs) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: One of the great matches you will see tonight involves the CWO World 6-Man Tag Team Championships. Stagnant for a while, the title situation for these belts have really heated up in recent weeks, and the Wild Bunch hope to add them to their resume tonight. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Regulators are in the interview area.) REGULATOR FIRE: Doomsday, a perfect name for you three chumps because Thursday will serve that for you three when we take your Six-Man Tag Team belts. It is about time that Law and Order are enforced and the Wild Bunch will take those belts and bring them back to the people. Baby Waldo & Mercenaries, you three have sealed your fate by taking on the three of us. REGULATOR ICE: Doomsday, you'd better polish those titles up and wear them in the mirror because after Thursday that will be the last time you three ever will be wearing the titles. We said last year that we would wear gold soon and we like those belts that you are wearing and WE GUARANTEE THAT WE WILL WIN! BATTLECAT: Doomsday, you three beat up on a team that was smaller than you and you won. Well, I did myself a favor and got two huge friends to help me out in my quest to win the tag team gold. I said that I would bring pride back to my country, my family, and my fans. I am tired of you three disgracing the belt, beating up people by ganging up on them, and breaking the rules. Thursday will be the last time you guys wear the Belts because the Wild Bunch are Taking them from you BY ANY MEANS NECESSARY! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Yesterday you saw an angry Sandman Jr. make a threat towards Bloodbath, well now the man from the grave responds to this challenge, when he meets Sandman Jr. tonight in Biloxi, MS. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Bloodbath is in a foggy cemetary with several gravestones.) BLOODBATH (dark mysterious voice): Sandman Jr., I never had anything against you! But if you want to tangle with the darkside then I will be more than happy to accomodate you. As you can see, there is plenty of room for your body to lay in its eternal resting place. You have now sealed your fate, REST IN PIECE! (The fog thickens as the camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: If Mondo Gecko didn't already have his hectic schedule to worry about this week, wrestling on the average two matches a night, he now has to contend with taking his prized Television championship away from Masher Maccabee and getting revenge for his attack on him and CWO broadcaster Tamara Wilcox. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko is in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Masher Macabee, you did something that you should have never done and that WAS PISS ME OFF! IT'S PRETTY PATHETIC WHEN YOU NEED TO BEAT UP ON A LADY AND NEED YOUR MANAGER TO WIN MY BELT! Masher, all you are is a pathetic thug who needs to be taught a lesson and I want you in the ring as soon as possible. I went through ten other men to win that title and I am not about to let some overgrown gorilla take it from me. But the worst part is you beat up a good female friend of mine in Tamara Wilcox. Tamara, thank you for trying to protect me and I will repay you, I promise. Masher, you had better pray that I am in a good mood because when I get my hands on you, I will kick your @$$ from here to hell! DIG IT, PUNK! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: As well as enraging Johnny Starr, Masher Maccabee has also angered the CWO World Champion, Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo, who made a challenge to Maccabee yesterday on the REPORT. Today, we have a response from the Television champion, Masher Maccabee. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Masher Maccabee is in the interview area along with Rabbi Solomon.) MASHER MACCABEE: Schillo, I outweighed you in Hebrew School, you little goyem. I will maul you in the cage, because when you feel the wrath of the Hangover, not only will I have the TV Strap, but I'll have a win over you, making me the "uncrowned" CWO World Champion. It will look good on a real man, KooKoo Guy, you pathetic Shawn Michaels wanna-be. I don't even need to bring the keg full of whoop-ass for you, I'll only need a beer ball to bust you open. Why don't we make this a TAPED FIST cage match, KooKoo? Then we'll see who the real man in the CWO is! Remember, all it takes is one mistake and... BANG! Homentaschen Hangover, the hottest finish on the planet. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Another feud already heating up in the CWO is the already existing one between Wildfire and Ganglord. Wildfire was put on the shelf for a while, thanks to Ganglord, and now he's back with a vengeance. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Wildfire is in the locker room with Miss Amber, playing back the comments of Ganglord on a monitor.) WILDFIRE: You talk about revenge, Ganglord! You talk about watching my back! Well... (Wildfire laughs a little) I haven't begun to extract revenge for what you did! I laid at home for nine long weeks, hearing you say how great you are and how you beat me and how I will never come back! Well guess what, big man, I'm back! You think I'm afraid of you? You think I'm afraid of what you say? You and that punk manager of yours seem to be the ones that are afraid! You want a match with your kind of rules! Well you got it! And you can rest assured that I wont stop untill I have gained my REVENGE! You just wait, 'cause Ganglord, there ain't no way in HELL that you will ever extinguish this WILDFIRE! AMBER: What ya gonna do when the WILDFIRE runs WILD on you! (Wildfire and Amber leave as the camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: The wrestler known as Cake Boy has gone through a radical change, he is now known as the Serpentmaster, and he has declared war on the CWO. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Serpentmaster is in the interview area.) SERPENTMASTER: I've had about asss much asss I can take from the delinquentssss in thiss league. (Serpentmaster let's a rattler he is holding bite him on the neck.) SERPENTMASTER: I'm injected with the venom of the snake now... and I'm a crazzzzy basstard looking to hand out sssome pain to thossse who would attack me from behind. Watch your neckssss. Oh, and as far as Toodles the chimp, well, see for yourself.... (A python is shown with a large chimpanzee-shaped figure in his belly. Serpentmaster laughs maniacally as the camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Finally today, this week on TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER Hile Troy announced his retirement, winning his retirement match against Master of Darkness Damion. However, Sandman has a contract to wrestle Troy at SPRING BREAK '97, on March 7 in Panama City, FL, live on PPV. Sandman has said, though, that if Troy can find a protege to take his place, that he will face the protege instead. However, if the protege is defeated, then Troy will have to give up his retirement and get in the ring to face Sandman. He will also have to face Sandman if he does not find a protege. And so, the search begins.... _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens on a small bar in the outer skirts of Kentucky, filled with some of the most revolting human beings imaginable, but also some of the toughest. Ripping through the dusty road, a limo pulls up, black and sleek, long in length. Several of the yahoos on the outside of the bar chortle in laughter as it arrives. The front doors of the limo open, then the driver opens the black door on the side of the limo. Inside is a man in a trim, slim Armani suit, silver hair pulled back into a ponytail, wearing a dark pair of sunglasses which add to his persona of styles. The Kentuckians, drunken and obese at the same time, walk the distance between themselves and the man. Holding bottles that undoubtedly contain warm whiskey or other alcohol, they grasp their hands into tightly worn fists and make violent gestures against both the driver and his passanger, however, little do they know that this is by far no normal man, this is Hile Troy.) HILE TROY: Gentlemen... drunkards... booze hounds? Whatever you guys like to call yourself. Just here to find myself one tough son of a gun, if you could ever so kindly step aside, I'll make my way into the bar. DRUNKARD #1: Iee don't thank so budday... y'all look to me like yer city folk! We don't take kiandly ta city folk! HILE TROY: Listen fellas, don't want any trouble, don't need any of it either, I'm just here, like I said, to find myself a guy that can take care of himself in a fight, that can take punishment, that knows what it's like to suffer. DRUNKARD #2: Y'all want a fight! I given ya a faight! (Stupidly, and with staggering balance, the hobo lunges forward towards Troy with a punch, nearly falling on his face from the strength of the liquor. Troy side steps the punch and destroys the drunkard with an elbow shot, then kicks and rolls him over.) HILE TROY: Maybe you boys don't know who I am, maybe you didn't see me kick Rebel Yell's ass, or Johnny Reb's ass. Maybe one of you fellas can give me what I need, eh? Did you all know that I'm not even from this country, better yet, I'm from England? I think that ALL Americans are idiots and slobs, ESPECIALLY you Kentucky-fried rubes! What are you gonna do about it? (Angrily, the last two of the men both swing at Troy, but he is able to duck one punch, causing one fat idiot to his the next. Troy grabs the other man and pounds him in the gut with a knee then, viledrives him down onto the dirt knocking him out cold.) HILE TROY: Conlon... start the car back up, it looks like this is going to take longer than we thought.... (Camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
