CWO UPDATE 1/18/97

18 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fade into Johnny Starr.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO UPDATE for Saturday, January 18, 1997. A reminder that all of the handlers in the CWO have until midnight tonight to complete the CWO AWARDS ballot and/or send in a Contract Check, if they have not already done so. A big TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER next week, as the 1996 CWO AWARDS will take place along with some great action which will be announced tomorrow. That leads into the next CWO PPV, TRIPLE THREAT, next Saturday, January 25, 1996, live from the Rosemont Horizon in Chicago. Every match at this PPV will be a triangle match. This can lead to some very interesting possibilities, as you will see in the week to come. Almost everybody can probably remember the last great triangle match in the CWO/CM2WF, when Foolkiller took on Lord Foul the Despiser and Johnny Reb for the World title. (Footage is shown of that match. Back to Johnny.) STARR: We can announce some of the great matches that will be taking place on the PPV, let's get right to it! CWO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP Master of Darkness Damion (champion) vs. Johnny Reb vs. Wayne "Keuka Guy" Schillo STARR: A very intense three-way match here. Schillo, the former champion, will be looking to get his belt back. We all know the history between MOD Damion and Johnny Reb. The question is: will Reb and Schillo co-operate against MOD, or will it truly be every man for himself? We'll have to wait and see. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Creatures of the Night (champions) vs. Biblical Powers vs. Deadly Sins STARR: Again, a sense of history has developed between all three of these teams. The Creatures have had a virtual lock on the World tag team titles ever since they re-entered the CWO. They also have possesion of the World 6-Man Titles with Hellhound. The Powers fought the Creatures at CWO CELEBRATION in December, but they were ultimately frustrated, winning by disqualification. Also, the Biblical Powers and Deadly Sins have been natural hated enemies ever since the Powers arrived. This one should truly by great. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Hile Troy (champion) vs. Sandman vs. Bane STARR: We should not that this one could very well turn into a twosome, as the legal status of Bane is unknown at this time. We will keep you posted on the situation, CWO officials are working as hard as they can to keep this match signed. If it remains how it should, then this may be the most explosive match on the card. The flamboyant Troy, rejuvenated by his recent victory over Gold Kard, not only has to contend with his former partner and co-leader of the Chaotic Dynasty, Sandman, but also with a marauding Bane who now seems to have his sights set on Troy. And let's not forget the intense feud that Bane and Sandman have had in the past year, in which we've seen just about everything! NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Eternal Wanderers (champions) vs. Pit Bosses vs. Bad Guys STARR: These three teams have been doing a three-way dance anyway for the past few months, it seems only fair to put them all in the ring at TRIPLE THREAT. Both the Pit Bosses and the Bad Guys can lay claim to the NA titles, they now have to go through the powerful and unorthodox team of the Wanderers to get them back. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Maximum Overdrive (champion) vs. "Innovator of Violence" Miguel Sanchez vs. Sin STARR: Overdrive and Sanchez have been feuding since September, and Sin now adds himself into the mix with some very strange behavior and attacks against Sanchez. With the "Innovator of Violence" involved, expect this one to be a bloody brawl. Well, that's it for now, we will announce all of the other triangle matches tomorrow, until then, this is Johnny Starr, saying, so long for now! (Closing creds roll as we fade out.)
