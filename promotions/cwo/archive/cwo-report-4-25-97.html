CWO REPORT 4/25/97

25 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Friday, April 25, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Kansas City, MO, let's see what went down there. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/24/97 Kansas City, MO Dark Raven pinned Joe Eagle after the inside cradle, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 13:53. El Grande Pistolerro pinned Chris Greco after the Spinning Wheel Kick. Time of match: 13:10. Arlon Flaym pinned The Focus after the Hurricanrana into a Cradle. Time of match: 10:40. Moses and Sloth wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Johnny Reb made Masher Maccabee submit to the Texas Cloverleaf. Time of match: 8:17. Masters of Hardcore defeated Mason-Dixon when 'Mr. Extreme' Chris Ohlhoff pinned Beauregard Dixon after the Hardcore Bomb. Time of match: 7:10. Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) and North American Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys wrestled to a double countout. Time of match: 12:29. STEEL CAGE ESCAPE MATCH: Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo defeated Television Champion Inferno in a non-title match. Time of match: 7:21. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight we're in Topeka, let's look at the lineup for that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ FRIDAY, APRIL 25, 1997 TOPEKA, KS WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Southern Pride vs. The Unholy Alliance (Dark Raven, Vlad the Impaler, Serpentmaster) (champions) Sandman Jr. vs. Gold Kard Deadly Sins vs. Maximum Overdrive and Baby Waldo Wrath vs. Brad "The Man" Couchman Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) vs. Coldfront Big Cool vs. Regulators NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Jason "Flash" Varga (champion) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with the chairman of the board, of GKI that is, Gold Kard. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Gold Kard is in the interview area.) GOLD KARD: First of all, let me address that dumbkopf Mondo Gecko in saying that Herr Alles has nothing to do with us. I will forgive your ignorance this one time, idiot, for maybe you did not know that Herr Alles was the one who cost me my control of this league. Still, he has his own way, and I have mine. And rest assured, Lizard Boy, that Gold Kard International: myself, Herr Thomas, and Herr Maccabee, will not rest until you and your pathetic friends Moses and Johnny Reb are destroyed. If we meet up in that 60-man battle royal at MAY-HEM, so much the worst for you! Now, however, let me respond to another dumbkopf who dares challenge me, Sandman Jr. I do not know why you have singled yourself out for destruction, Herr Junior, but I will provide it. You spend your whole life emulating men that you have no chance of living up to. Well, tonight, you face a true original, the master wrestler, the chairman of the board, Gold Kard! You will cane nothing, dumbkopf! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Also tonight in Topeka, the Regulators in a big return match from this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER against the Big Cool. Let's hear from the Regulators. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Regulators are in the interview area.) REGULATOR FIRE: So Big Cool, how does it feel to have the match of your life and still not be able to beat us? You see, we are ready to do everything it takes to win the tag team belts and if that means beating you guys up, then that is fine with us. I am tired of these chump teams such as the Masters of Hardcore, Mercenaries, Bad Boys, Deadly Sins, and all the other geeks get title shots every time they want one yet we get one shot, almost win the belts in our first try and then we don't even get a damn rematch. Well, once we destroy everybody in the CWO, then we will get the belts where they belong. We came here to the CWO to eliminate all the crap here and win titles and when we set out to do something then we mean it. Tell 'em, Ice. REGULATOR ICE: Big Cool, it seems you two tons of fun want to throw your weight around. Hey, we don't mind, we love to throw you two guys around as well. You guys are tough, you gave us a fight on Tuesday Night Fever, but we still beat you and on Friday we will beat you two guys again and advance up the ladder into the tag team title scene. Personally, I am tired of the Masters of Hardcore, Mercenaries, Deadly Sins, Bad Boys, and other crap teams all getting the title shots while we get the shaft. Things are going to change, though, because once we get those belts around our waists, we are going to keep them for a long, long time. OOOOHHHHH WWWHHHAAATTT AAAAA RRRRRUUUUUSSSSSHHHHH! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: They've got the shot at the World Tag Team titles at MAY-HEM, let's hear from the Highlanders. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Highlanders are in the interview area.) GAVIN McCLOUD: Och, what's that high pitched noise I hear? Some poor animal in pain, perhaps? Maybe an experimental new jet engine? Oh no, my mistake - it's tha' "extreme team," whining as normal. Ye think we're scared of ye, ye little nancy boys? We've faced tha' best here in the CWO - the Pit Bosses, Eternal Wanderers, the Biblical Powers - and ye think we're gonna be run out of the federation by a pair of green-nosed kiddies? Not a bloody chance, laddie. Now keep away from us, or we'll do considerably more than interfere in yer matches. Now, on to more pressing matters.... CONOR McCLOUD: First of all, we'd like to raise our pints and say a big 'Cheeeeeers' to Fergus McTavish - och, it good to see another Scot in this federation; watch yer backs, CWO - the Celtic Invasion starts here. Tell ye what, Fergus; if ye ever need any help, or if ye'd like to have a shot at the six-man tag titles, gives us a tinkle and we'll get it sorted. GAVIN McCLOUD: An' last but not least, just before our shot a' the belts at May-Hem, my brother gets ta take on Tiger Rogers for the Light Heavyweight title! Unexpected, but och, we'll take the bugger anyway. I'll be watchin' his back an he'll, he'll.... CONOR McCLOUD: ...I'll be poundin' that so-called hard man intae the mat. Ye know something, Rogers? All yer opposition so far have been namby-pamby, jump-around types; well, get ready fer a surprise, because I'm gonna brawl you out tha' arena. THERE CAN BE ONLY ONE! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Finally, today, more mysteriousness from the masters of the macabre, the Lord's Cabin. _______________________________________________________________________________ (We find Lord Guru chanting in a flamed circle. Two burning effigies of the Masters of Hardcore burn within. As Guru chants, Exodus walks up to his Lord and looks grim. His arm is still in pain and he looks with regret at the sleeping Exile. He understands that time has come for his departure and he looks with regret.) EXODUS (a cold, harsh, dark, grim voice): Couchman, Ohlhoff... you have been blessed in not meeting us in the battle field. Call it what you will... (a heavy sigh) as others will. We are not running... but it is time for a new beginning... (his voice heavy with anger) we will be back! This I swear by the blood that flows from this body! Let it be known... Couchman and Ohlhoff, when next we meet on the battlefield, you will come to witness what it means to truly feel hell on this plane! (Exodus looks at Lord Guru, and as he does, a white skull appears within the fire, full of fury. A man, garbed in a dark brown trenchcoat and a skullface mask, walks out. Holding a biker chain, he walks forth, his mask the only part aflame.) EXODUS: As we depart, Couchman, Ohlhoff, CWO... prepare for the gate of hell to be opened... for... the time has come for the Ghost Riders to grace the CWO... and it means war! (Turning away, the blazing face of Blaze walks up to him. Taking off the mask, his white drawn skull is revealed, a chilling effect to the bone.) BLAZE (cold and monotonous voice): CWO... the gates of the darkest pits of life have opened. Prepare for the coming... prepare for the end. (As Lord Guru still chants, from out of the fire a black skull appears.) VOICE: VENGEANCE... WILL... BE... SERVED! (Fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's it for today, we'll see you tomorrow on the REPORT.
