HOUSE SHOW 2/2/02 Seagate Centre, Toledo, OH (Part of the Toledo Auto Show) The show opened with Allen Carter cutting an in-ring promo, wearing a neck brace and saying that so soon after his release from the hospital for the neck injury he suffered that he wouldn't be able to wrestle in the main event tonight. Carter managed to turn himself heel for the night with this action, as well as insulting the city of Toledo itself. That brought "The
Avenger" out to cheers from the crowd, as Ewiak told Carter that they still had unfinished business and that Ewiak would be in the cage later on tonight, and he would either accept a victory by forfeit, showing what a
COWARD Carter was, or he would take Carter on if he "grew a pair." The crowd voiced its approval as Ewiak left the ring, and Carter grumbled over the choices he was presented.
Dennis Menton pinned Ron Beauregard (CWO Tag Team Champion) after the Bionic
Elbow (flying elbowsmash off the ropes). Time of match: 9:19. Shirley was at ringside. There was a ref bump. Ron get Dennis in the Bear Hug, like he usually does when they were going for the Option 73. Shirley was on top to deliver the Option 73 but instead changed his mind and went after Omar
Martinez (who was at ringside). Dennis Menton then hit his finisher, the ref woke up and made the three count. There was of course tension between the Beauregards after this match.
Ganglord pinned Sandman Jr. after the Gangbusta (tiger driver) and outside interference by Samarian. Time of match: 9:04.
Omar Martinez pinned El Suicida after the Martinez Missile (flying dropkick) and outside interference by Wildfire against Suicida. Time of match: 9:18. Shirley Beauregard (CWO Tag Team Champion) pinned Charlie Phreak after the flying elbowdrop. Time of match: 9:53.
Samarian defeated Wizard by disqualification. Samarian had delivered The
Nightmare (suplex into a sit-out powerbomb) to Wizard and was ready to pin him when Sandman Jr. attacked him with the Singapore cane, drawing the bell.
Time of match: 9:21. Magic Group (Necromancer and Sorcerer) and Pit Bosses (Mike Masario and Joey
Davola) wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 9:20.
Dave Lupus pinned Wildfire (CWO Television Champion) in a non-title match after a bulldog headlock, using the ropes for leverage. The special guest referee for this match was El Suicida, who (purposefully or not) missed
Lupus' feet being on the ropes. Wildfire of course wasn't too happy about that. Time of match: 9:22.
STEEL CAGE (both escapes and pinfalls allowed) 'The Avenger' Jesse Ewiak pinned Allen 'Mr. America' Carter after a powerslam off the top turnbuckle. Carter was trying to escape when Ewiak shook the ropes and Carter fell, crotching himself on the turnbuckle. That set him up for Ewiak's pinning move. Time of match: 9:18. After the match the Pit Bosses attacked Carter in the cage, trying to deliver the Spike
Piledriver to him again, but Ewiak made the save, as both he and Carter drove the Bosses out of the caged ring to send the crowd home cheering!
