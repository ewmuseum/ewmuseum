House Show Results - 8/20/99

August 21, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW 8/20/99 Louisville, KY (Broadbent Arena) Kamikaze/Matsuhiro Torunaka defeated Australian Assassin/Vegas Villain when
Kamikaze pinned Vegas Villain after the California Jam. Time of match:
4:28. Wildfire pinned Deliverance after the double underhook piledriver. Time of match: 7:54. 'Dark Shogun' Akueri Hakujin pinned Calvin after the Triad Bomb. Time of match: 4:29. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE MATCH: Hollywood X-Press defeated Wrath/Sandman when Hollywood Haskins pinned Wrath after the Hollywood Bomb. Time of match: 11:32. Hollywood X-Press retained the CWO North American Tag Team Champions title.
Lord Hile Troy pinned Television Champion Union Caine in a non-title match after a small package. Time of match: 12:16.
'The Avenger' Jesse Ewiak pinned North American Champion Black Jack Hollister in a non-title match after the Ewiakbomb. Time of match: 12:33. Man Mountain Marko pinned Vladimir Popalov after the Chi-Town Hammer. Time of match: 5:39.
Reb and Fantastic defeated Diamondback Chris Myers/'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum when Johnny Reb made ‘Airborne’ Mark Fulcrum submit to the Texas Cloverleaf.
Time of match: 15:35.
