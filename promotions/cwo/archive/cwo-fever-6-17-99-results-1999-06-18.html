FEVER 6/17/99 - Results

June 18, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FEVER 6/17/99 LIVE FROM: Rockford, IL (Metrocentre) HOUR #1: Kent and Gordy Alles started off the show, discussing current affairs in the
CWO and laying down some matches and stipulations for tonight. They said that either Brent “The Ace” Alles would join the Dark Side, or he would have to defend his #1 Contender status against not only Mondo Gecko, but
Foolkiller as well! Myers and Fulcrum then came out, and Myers said even though Darkness caused him the ultimate humiliation, he would still work with the Alleses to get a title shot. Myers and Fulcrum offered to take out
Foolkiller, Mondo Gecko, and Maximum Overdrive in a six-man match at SUMMER
HEAT ’99 in exchange for what they got. They also stated they would have a
“mystery man,” apparently a former World Champion who may or may not be working for the CWO right now!
Brian 'The King' Derks, the man who will face Man Mountain Marko for the
Television title at SUMMER HEAT ’99, made the Vegas Villain submit to the
King's Crusher (head squeezer). Time of match: 3:54.
Mustafa Rahim had an in-ring interview, discussing his return to the CWO and his immediate goals, mostly concerning Omar Martinez and Union “Thomas
Jefferson” Caine.
Vladimir Popalov pinned 'Smilin'' Doc Watson after the Russian Sickle. Before Popalov nailed his finishing move, Wrath of the Dark Side “accidentally” nailed the Headhunter with a steel chair. When Kent Alles confronted Wrath about that (actually slapping him in the face!), Wrath tried to attack him, which led to a Dark Side/Headhunters brawl! Time of
match: 2:51. Backstage, Gordy Alles and Darkness managed to make peace between their two factions… for now.
Cris Marcus pinned Maximum Overdrive (who discussed his feelings about the
Myers and Fulcrum situation before the match) after the inverted atomic drop, using the ropes for leverage. Diamondback Chris Myers attacked
Overdrive outside the ring, setting him up for the pin by Marcus. Time of match: 6:04. In a match set up at the beginning of the night by Kent Alles, Hollywood
X-Press (Hollywood Haskins and Iceman X) had to defend their #1 Contender
Status to the World Tag Team titles by defeating Strictly Business (Mr.
Varga and Mr. Eagle), which they did when Iceman X pinned Mr. Varga after the Ghetto Buster, pulling the tights. Again, botched Dark Side interference (this time by Warhawk and Sandman) led to the Headhunters’ defeat. So, another brawl broke out between both factions! Time of match:
5:27. HOUR #2: “The Ace” was interviewed backstage by Johnny Starr, and basically said what we already knew, that he wasn’t going to join the Dark Side.
'The Avenger' Jesse Ewiak pinned 'Playboy' Wayne Schillo after the Ewiakbomb. Time of match: 10:05. After the match Ewiak was attacked by Mad
Doctor’s Ghost, and then Schillo put him in the Playboy Crab. Referees tried to break it up, but MDG fought them off. It took a security force to finally clear the ring.
True Americans were shown at the Blockbuster Awards, in their usual state of nuttiness. Wildfire (who had a brief interview with Johnny Starr before his “match”) defeated Lord Hile Troy by forfeit, as the CWO’s living legend no-showed. Troy’s whereabouts and status are unknown at this time. Hardcore Sensation defeated Yakuza (with Super Bad Ass Sweet Daddy Jones) when 'Radical' Kirk Madness pinned Secret Agent Super Dragon after the
Sensational Bomb. Time of match: 8:30. Hardcore Sensation retained the CWO
North American Tag Team Champions title.
THREE WAY DANCE: Brent 'The Ace' Alles defended his #1 Contender status by defeating Foolkiller and Mondo Gecko in a triangle match. Time of match: 8:58. The ref stopped the match when Brent 'The Ace' Alles had an unconscious
Foolkiller in the Alles Clutch. Foolkiller was unconscious due to a chairshot right in the face by a mysterious masked man in a bodysuit. The match had its distractions from the get-go, as Myers and Fulcrum (who did guest commentary for the match) attacked Gecko during the match, which brought out Col. Taggart, Hollywood X-Press, Strictly Business, and the
Creatures of Darkness among others to join the fray. Gordy and Kent Alles both came out to try and control the chaos, and indeed it was Kent’s distraction of the referee that led to Foolkiller getting hit by the chair.
The show ended with Melinn and Thomas wondering if the mystery man was Myers and Fulcrum’s mystery man, and with Darkness in his luxury suite, enjoying all the chaos he had apparently created.
