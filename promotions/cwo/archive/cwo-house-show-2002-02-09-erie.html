CWO House Show Results - 2/9/02 - Erie, PA

HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 2/09/02 Erie, PA - - - - - ARKANGEL PROMO by [name withheld] (+1 Heat for Arkangel) The scene fades to a cemetary. Arkangel is seen walking around the cemetary. He turns around to look at the camera, as he begins to speak. ARKANGEL: Finally, the Angel of Death has come back to his first home. This is where I belong, and when the time comes I will be here for all eternity.
My plan is being carried out as we speak. As the darkness takes over and consumes everything in its path, there will be nothing left but evil. The silence will be unbearable, and the darkness will create lunacy throughout mankind. The hour of suffering and pain is upon us all, if you're not with me you are against me, and if you are against me then you will pay the ultimate price. Mondo Gecko, just one day ago I completely destroyed your ass in the middle of that ring in front of the entire world. I told you if you wanted to play mind games then you are messing with the wrong person.
You may be an unstoppable force, but darkness has arrived, and with it, it has brought the Angel of Death. As I slowly creep up on you, you will not know what hit you. Invincible to the eye, and a soul with the color of the midnight sky, you will not stop the Angel of Death from reaching his goal.
You are looking at the strongest force in the world, and no matter what you try and do you can't stop it. That is what is so pitiful about this whole thing. You just have to sit back and watch my destruction happen. Mondo
Gecko, this could all stop if you would just surrender to the dark side. I just want you to know that every person who's soul I take, and destroy will be on your conscious. This is all your fault. Gecko it is time for the pain and sacrificing to continue onwards, more devastating and relentless then ever. I can think of things to do to you that not even humans can think of in their worst nightmares. Your soul belongs to me, and for all eternity you will be indebted to me. It is time for you to burn in hell, and rot away like the coward you are.
Sandman, you whiny little pathetic figure. It is time once and for all to get rid of the second biggest problem in this fed. And that would be you. It is time for me to shut you up for good, and the only way i am going to do that is to beat you until you can't move or go on any longer. For when we meet the words NO MERCY will be the only thing I will be thinking of when I get my hands around you. It is time for the Angel of Death to take yet another victim on his quest for eternal domination. With the Sickness roaming the earth, there is no escape. Be prepared to get down with the sickness, because if you are against us you will pay the price. It is time for the deep sleep sandman. No more pleasant dreams as you sleep away in your little bed. I am everywhere Sandman, whether or not you want to believe it. As you sleep at night, I want you to think about where i am, and when I will choose my next spot to strike. Any place that is dark, I am there. Any place that your imagination takes you I am there. Whenever evil or darkness lurks I am there. Whenever there is murder I am there. And when you step into that ring, I WILL BE THERE!!! Be prepared to look the face of death and fear right in the face. For I will extend death's hand, and you will have no choice but to SUCUMB TO THE ANGEL OF DEATH and the DARK SIDE!!!
The nightmare is here...and there is no escape!!! Fight me if you dare, but you will not survive, because I WILL NOT LET YOU!!!
Arkangel rolls his eyes into the back of his head, as he begins to laugh menacingly. He stops and looks into the camera, as he motions for the camera to follow him. He walks a little ways, and stops. He falls on his knees to the ground. He begins to rip up the grass and the dirt in complete hysteria.
He begins to chant something but it is too muffled to hear. He stares over as points to the camera man to look down. Arkangel laughs menacingly as the camera shows a tombstone. The tombstone reads:
Here lies Mondo Gecko, and Sandman Jr. Courtesy of the ANGEL OF DEATH ARKANGEL!!! Arkangel continues to laugh hysterically, as the scene fades back to normal. - - - - - Wizard pinned Major Slayer after the belly-to-belly suplex.
