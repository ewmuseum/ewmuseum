CWO Card Archive

House Show Results 10/2/97 - 10/4/97

5 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 10/2/97 Cheyenne, WY Kurt Gerstung pinned Skyhawk after the Icebreaker after Tommy Bomber hit Skyhawk with a steel chair. Time of match: 11:51. Ranger Taurus pinned Moses after the Texas Stampede. Time of match: 12:31. Steve White/'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat defeated Nation of Denomination when Steve White pinned Amish Farmer after the double leg cradle. Time of match: 13:21. Sandman Jr. defeated 'Master of Darkness' Damion by disqualification when The Dark Side attacked. Time of match: 5:10. Newton Bros. defeated Brother Justice when Howard Newton pinned Jerome Justice after the sunset flip. Time of match: 22:07. Hellhound pinned Diamondback Chris Myers after the rollup and outside interference by 'Master of Darkness' Damion. Time of match: 14:05. CWO NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS TITLE MATCH: The Big Cool defeated Wild Ones by countout when the Dark Side attacked. Time of match: 31:23. The Big Cool retained the CWO North American Tag Team Champions title. Lord Hile Troy pinned Sean Holiday after the small package. Time of match: 16:10. World Tag Team Champions the Highlanders and the Creatures of the Night wrestled to a double countout. Time of match: 20:39. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 10/3/97 Boise, ID Amish Farmer and Painmaster wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 7:52. Judgment pinned Diamondback Chris Myers after the Judgment Call and outside interference by Johnny Reb. Time of match: 19:40. Wild Cards defeated Coldfront when Deck pinned Steve White after the Dead Man's Hand. Time of match: 18:53. Sandman pinned Television Champion The Focus in a non-title match after the Sandblast, using the ropes for leverage. Time of match: 8:42. Deadly Sins defeated Ghost Riders when Pride pinned Blaze after the Repention. Time of match: 7:09. Southern Dynamite pinned North American Champion Masher Maccabee in a non-title match after the Rebel Edge and outside interference by Fergus McTavish. Time of match: 18:45. TEXAS DEATH MATCH: Bad Guys defeated Mason-Dixon when Stonewall Mason failed to meet a ten count after being pinned by Joe 'The Mean Guy' Rucker. Time of match: 40:23. Skyhawk pinned World Champion Johnny Reb after the sunset flip when Diamondback Chris Myers came down to ringside and distracted Reb. Time of match: 13:10. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 10/4/97 Portland, OR 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum and 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Amish Farmer defeated Sandman Jr. by countout when Sandman Jr. chased after an interfering Miguel Sanchez. Time of match: 15:30. Skyhawk and Wrath wrestled to a double disqualification when the Deadly Sins and the Unit got involved outside the ring. Time of match: 1:56. Sean Holiday pinned The Prophet after the Permanent Vacation. Time of match: 9:30. Mustafa Rahim defeated Kurt Gerstung by countout when Gerstung missed a flying shoulderblock and crashed into the steel guardrail. Time of match: 6:44. Brent 'The Ace' Alles pinned Lord Hile Troy after the inside cradle. Time of match: 12:53. Holocaust made Gold Kard submit to the Ashes to Ashes. Time of match: 6:52. CPE Enforcers defeated Brother Justice when Col. Taggart pinned Jerome Justice after the top-rope DDT. Time of match: 16:03. CWO NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Mondo Gecko defeated Masher Maccabee by disqualification when the Dark Side attacked Mondo Gecko. Time of match: 7:05. Masher Maccabee retained the CWO North American Champion title.
