CWO REPORT 12-5-97 (fwd)

5 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey sitting at the CWO REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. The CWO appeared last night in South Bend, IN. Here are the complete results for that show. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 12/4/97 South Bend, IN Clark Gray made Painmaster submit to the Abdominal Stretch. Time of match: 18:04. 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia pinned Chris Greco after the Falling in Love. Time of match: 4:05. Maia refused to let up after the bell rang - prompting Southern Dynamite to make the save. Bloodbath/Sandman defeated Wild Cards by countout. Time of match: 7:47. 'The Ultimate Heel' Union Caine pinned Mustafa Rahim after the Cainedriver. Time of match: 7:17. Dogs of War defeated Jade Dragons when 'Irresistible Assasin' Eddie Fantastic pinned Shinji Usami after the Moneymaker. Time of match: 15:07. Foolkiller defeated Wesley 'The Rock' McMichael when the ref stopped the match when McMichael was unconscious in the Texas cloverleaf. Time of match: 11:56. North American Tag Team Champions 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum/Diamondback Chris Myers defeated Mercenaries when Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Korperal Punishment after the small package. Time of match: 16:21. World Champion Johnny Reb pinned Lord Hile Troy after the elbow drop and outside interference by Mason-Dixon. Time of match: 14:42. WASHEY: Tonight the CWO will be showing up on the campus of Ball State University. Here is the complete lineup for tonight's card. FRIDAY, DECEMBER 5, 1997 BALL STATE UNIVERSITY, IN Tsutomu Kobayashi vs. 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher vs. "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat Youth in Action vs. The Unit Southern Dynamite vs. "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia Steve Keller and Tommy Lightning vs. Brother Justice TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wildfire (champion) vs. Sandman Dogs of War vs. Mercenaries WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Bloodbath vs. Johnny Reb (champion) WASHEY: Let's go to the interviews for today. _________________________________________________________________________ <Sandman Jr at the hospital with Kellen> [Sandman Jr] Damion... it wasn't personal up until 2 months ago... you wanted to get the title belt, I was in your way. But then you started doing... "things"... to Kellen... evil things, things I can't explain... then you cost me my match for the world title, and you steal Kellen... you do more of your "things"... she can't even talk about it... you're scum, Damion... and I don't care how many members of your flock there are, I don't care how many people you get to help you, you're dead. They'll be dragging me off to prison for what I do to you, cause you'll be dead. And it'll be the right thing to do, cause you shouldn't be left alive. You're too much a piece of sh*t to live. Call me "Wannabe Jr" all you want... the fact is, I'm gonna tear you limb from limb, and you're gonna wannaME to stop, but I won't. Until your heart stops beating. And Reb, I want more than your respect, I want your gold. And after I kill Damion, and if I don't go to jail for it, I'm gonna get that belt. At least now you realize I'm no push-over, for all the good that knowledge will do you once I beat you! ____________________________________________________________________ Jade Dragons [The camera opens on the Jade Dragons working out in a gym. Both men go through various exercises, from situps to bench presses. As soon as they are done with their workout, they stop to drink some water, and wipe the sweat off their faces with towels. They get up, and walk towards the camera...] Kenji Ishida: Highlanders, you thought that once again you would take advantage of another team? Your attempt to attack the Wild Cards had a stumbling block: Us. We warned you Highlanders. You want a war, you've got one. You and your entire clan of brigands and thieves are now on notice: The Jade Dragons will be everywhere you go. Everytime you try an after match attack, the Jade Dragons will be there to stop you. Shinji Usami: Highlanders, all this talk of honor is nothing but lies spewing from your lips. You know nothing of honor. We, on the other hand, do. We know that attacking an opponent after they have defeated you in fair combat is dishonorable. You, on the other hand, continue to do so. What the other tag teams in CWO will not do, we will. We will confront you Highlanders. Confront you with the truth. You are nothing but cowards who cannot stand to lose. Kenji Ishida: A week from this sunday Highlanders, we shall face in combat once more. Once more you shall fall to us, for unlike you, we have seen your measure, and we know that we are the better team. You lack the courage, the intestinal fortitude that we do. What you lack, we excel in. Who cares if you are former 3 time CWO world tag team champions? You are no longer the same men who held those titles. Shinji Usami: You are nothing more than shadows of your former selves. In the end, even you will realize this. A pity that you must learn this at our hands. We offered them to you in friendship, and instead, you forced them to become weapons of war against you. You started this Highlanders, and we, the Jade Dragons, will finish it! Kenji Ishida: This coming tuesday, before we face the cowardly highlanders again, we have an opportunity to win the CWO world tag team titles. This is a great honor to us to be chosen for this opportunity. Shinji Usami: In some ways, we do not feel worthy of this chance, but we will do our best to prove that we deserve it. The Deadly Sins are a strong team, and it will take much effort on our part to defeat them. Kenji Ishida: Deadly Sins, you are worthy opponents. We go into this match with the knowledge that we face the best team in CWO at this time. We will not make claims that we will defeat you. Only karma can determine that. Shinji Usami: However, do not overlook us either. If you do, you may find yourselves with out your titles when you must face the Wild Cards. Then the Highlanders would find themselves in a position to face us for the titles. Kenji Ishida: How ironic would that be Shinji? [chuckles] Shinji Usami: Very ironic Kenji! [chuckles also] Kenji Ishida: Deadly Sins, we are ready to face you. Are you prepared to face us? Shinji Usami: Pray to buddha or whatever god you pray to that you or. For if you are not, then we WILL be the next CWO tag team champions! [the camera fades out as Kenji & Shinji pack up their gym bags and leave] _________________________________________________________________ [Pride] Ooooohh... looks like we struck a nerve with our D-S bit... never mind the fact that Vince McMahon keeps BEGGING us to join the WWF... besides how Eric Bischoff is offering us the TBS Network to show all Sins matches 24 hours a day to go WCW... besides Paul E. offering us a sandwich to join ECW... never mind all that. We've pissed off a lot of people with D-S, and you know what? GOOD! We weren't TRYING to make friends with the bit! [Sloth] You know who else we're pissed at? Tom Krepps! Yeah, we "gave" him the North American belts... no, he DIDN'T steal them from our dressing room... yeah right. Well, that's fine. Those belts mean nothing anyway. Look who the champs are! Two singles wrestlers: Fulcrum and the VASTLY OVERRATED Myers... and look who's challenging them! Two MORE SINGLES WRESTLERS!!! Well, if that's the competition holding those belts brings, good ridance. [Pride] Geeze, we almost forgot to congratulate our good buddy Johnny Reb on beating that moron Sandman Jr. Good luck kicking the ass of Mondo Gecko (that'll take you a whole 5 seconds) and that antique Lord Hile Troy (10 seconds). [Sloth] And don't the Wild Cards like EVER go away? I mean, these guys just keep dissing us and dissing us... somehow they became #1 contenders, my mind boggles on that one... anyway, you guys want a match with us at Showcase... no way. Not even non-title. NOT gonna happen... but you know, we MIGHT know someone who'd be willing to take you on... MAYBE. [Pride] Finally, Jade Dragons... you guys might be big shots in SJPW... you guys may have beaten the Highlanders... but to us, that means a whole lot of nothing. Be the big cheese in Japan, beat the skirt wearers, the only thing is, at Fever, when you face the CHAMPIONS OF THE WORLD, you're gonna lose. And THAT is the bottom line. [Wrath] Focus, it's obviously not over... it's not going to BE over until I beat you down, once and for all, and you start screaming "Wrath, I'm sorry, you really ARE the man!" Then I'll leave you alone, THEN I'll leave you be. But since I don't see that happening any time soon, I'm gonna keep making your life a living hell. You're supposed to be this great talent, this great guy who "focuses" on beaing his enemy... and cheats to do it... what would your mother think? And keep calling me nothing, cause when I beat you, you say "NOTHING beat me"..."NOTHING made me cry"... and, the one I look forward to most, "NOTHING put me out of wrestling!" At Showcase, you WILL say "I QUIT" when you're in the Repentance Rack... end of story. ___________________________________________________________________ Masashi "Lightning" Lanther [Masashi Lanther is walking down the street in Honolulu. He is wearing a leisure suit, but still has his mask on...] Masashi Lanther: Last weekend, I scored a great victory over Super Tengu, a man considered the best cruiserweight in SJPW. Sadly, he is the son of my old tag team partner, the Fire Ninja. I am here in Honolulu because this weekend, Tiger Kikuchi and I have an important tag team match. [pauses] Masashi Lanther: The camera crew informs me that on December 14th, I face Jason "Flash" Varga for the CWO Lightheavyweight title. Jason, we are no strangers to each other. The last time we faced, we went to a draw. Then you lost the title, so I've patiently waited for a rematch At long last, that chance is here. [pauses] Masashi Lanther: Jason, you are a tough opponent. One of the toughest I've faced in my long career. But you have what I want Jason. I want that title, and I will do everything in my power to win it Jason. I will come at you with all the skills I possess Jason. Will you do the same, or will you underestimate me? The choice is yours Jason, but know this: To underestimate me is to know defeat. If you want to keep that title, be prepared, for I will be. [the camera fades as Lanther walks off...] ________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody tomorrow on the REPORT! (fade to closing credits)
