March Madness Contest!

March 9, 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey CWO fans, it's March, and that means it's time for MARCH MADNESS. Enter the CWO MARCH MADNESS CONTEST, and win a free 1999 Pro Wrestling
Illustrated Almanac! The Almanac is packed with information, such as vital information on more than 500 wrestlers, tons of title histories, and results of every pay-per-view card ever!
To enter, send the below information to < [detail withheld] >: Your Name Your Mailing Address Finishing Move that you would use if you were a real wrestler. All entries will be put in a tournament, and you will actually be facing off against each other, the winner of course getting the Almanac!
Entry deadline is March 21. Updated tournament brackets will be shown all week long to the contest entries, and then, on March 28, you will receive
FREE OF CHARGE the latest CWO PPV, MARCH MADNESS '99, where the final winner of the tournament (and the Almanac) will be revealed!
Enter today! (If you are interested in the CWO in general, drop < [detail withheld] > a line, and we can get you added to the mailing list.) Thanks, Brent G. Alles CWO fed-head
