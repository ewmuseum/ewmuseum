CWO REPORT 1/30/97

30 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO Control Center.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Thursday, January 30, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO kicked off its New England tour, starting at the Portland Civic Center in Portland, ME, let's look at all the great action that took place there last night. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 1/29/97 Portland, ME (Portland Civic Center) Cake Boy pinned David 'The Destroyer' Davis with the small package. Time of match: 7:06. Black Death pinned Bloodbath after the rolling knee. Time of match: 6:45. Baby Waldo defeated 'Lonestar' Mike Williams by countout. Time of match: 11:05. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 15:19. During the match Maximum Overdrive came down to the ring and took a seat, watching the match. Eternal Wanderers and Highlanders wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 6:10. Panther made 'Master of Darkness' Damion submit to the Eclipse after outside interference by Johnny Reb (DDT'ing MOD outside the ring on to a chair). Time of match: 9:29. LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Moses (champion) pinned Dark Raven after the armbar takeover. Time of match: 5:28. NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Bad Guys (champions) defeated The Big Cool when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Jumbo Jenkins after the shoulder tackle to the groin. Time of match: 7:22. WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title, steel cage-escape): Wayne 'Keuka Guy' Schillo escaped the cage after he splashed 'Monster' Rich Clark. Time of match: 9:22. During the match Doomsday surrounded the cage, although they did not interfere, and celebrated with Schillo after his victory. In rage, Clark then Choke Slammed a ring technician who was there to take down the cage, before going back to the locker room. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Augusta, ME, let's look at that card's lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW THURSDAY, JANUARY 30, 1997 AUGUSTA, ME Regulators vs. Big Cool Sandman Jr. Vs. Sin Wild Ones vs. D. E. A. T. H. Wrath vs. Phantom Freightrain Mercenaries vs. Eternal Wanderers Steel vs. Goliath Revolution vs. Mason-Dixon Deadly Sins vs. Coldfront Talon vs. Hile Troy NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Sandman vs. Bloodbath _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: As you can see, the main event on this great card is a non-title matchup between North American champion Sandman and Bloodbath. This situation came about as a result of this week's TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. We take you back to footage of an incident between these two men. ________________________________________________________________________________ (Footage from 1/28/97 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, with Jerry Covenant and Mike Postema providing play-by-play and commentary.) [JC] - The referee over against the ropes, arguing with Sandman over something, I don't know what. Talon was easily defeated there! Blood getting to his feet, he walks over and swats at Sandman who jumps down off the mat. [MP] - Sandman rolled something into the ring at the same time though! It's a champagne bottle! [JC] - It seems Sandy has been drinking a lot of the bubbly as of late, the referee trying to get in between Bloodbath and Sandman, and Sandman pulls the referee outside of the ring! Bloodbath spins around and Talon clocks him with the bottle! [MP] - Bottom's up! [JC] - Not even funny, Sandman trying to help the referee to his feet now of course, he gets the referee back into the ring... one... two... three! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now take you to comments from Bloodbath. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The scene is a cemetry, with a grave stone reading SANDMAN.) BLOODBATH (mysterious voice): Sandman, you have entered the realm of darkness, and now you must pay! You tried to cost me my match, well, it didn't work! As you can see, I have a grave already prepared for the funeral! I want a shot at your title! And when I'm through with you, then you will REST IN PIECE! As for you, Troy, you came down and gave me a helping hand. For that I owe you a debt of gratitude! If you ever need a hand just step in to the realm of darkness and I will be waiting! (The camera fades out as Bloodbath mysteriously disappears.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: At last night's house show, Johnny Reb cost Master of Darkness Damion a match against Panther, DDT'ing him on the concrete floor, allowing Panther to score the submission win with the Eclipse. This escalates a situation that was already escalated by Johnny Reb on this week's FEVER, when he cost Damion a chance to regain the World title. Let's go back again to TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Footage is shown again from the 1/28/97 TUESDAY NIGHT FEVER, with "Killer" Kyle Melinn and David "The Truth" Thomas providing play-by-play and commentary.) MELINN: Damion has Troy up in that Tombstone piledriver position... he nails it! Troy driven into the mat by that, and it looks like that Damion will be going after a second title at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE... Damion with a cover... wait a minute... who's this... THOMAS: It's Johnny Reb! MELINN: Johnny Reb rushing the ring with a chair, and he nails Master of Darkness Damion with it! THOMAS: Thats not fair, Melinn! MELINN: Normally Hile Troy would have been disqualified, but there are no disqualifications in this match. Reb has the chair on the mat now, he picks MOD up... and he DDT's him on the chair! This crowd has erupted! Reb draping the still unconscious Troy over MOD, and the referee has to count, 1... 2... 3! TONY THE K: The winner of the match in 10:17, Hile Troy! THOMAS: I hope they ban Johnny Reb from the league for that! MELINN: And Johnny Reb is asking for the mike! JOHNNY REB: You said that while you were in this league, that ah wouldn't hold any gold. Well, mister, if that's the case, then you're not going to hold any either! This is war! (Johnny Reb slams the mike down and walks away.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now take you to comments from both Master of Darkness Damion and Johnny Reb. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on a very angered and serious looking MOD Damion.) MOD DAMION: Johnny Reb, you have just sealed your fate, your doom, your ERADICATION. Johnny, I make this vow to you and the CWO now, no longer will it be just gold that you will not see but soon the CWO will be lost to you forever. I shall eliminate you from the CWO forever. You have cost me the chance for gold one time too many. As for you, Sandman, you think you are the power of the night, well, I proved that wrong before when I pinned you 1... 2... 3... in our one and only meeting proving that I alone am truly the "Master of Darkness." I control all that is night, all that is dark, and all that is EVIL! This brings up my next point. A man, no, a MONSTER, who I have done battle with a number of times has begun to impress me with his new found hatred for the world. His trips into my Darkness, my "Forever Night", have finally paid dividends and now his true power has been released. "Monster" Rich Clark, we have had many good battles and now it seems we have some common enemies and some similar goals, these being destroying all competition, specifically Sandman and gaining the most sought after prize, the CWO World Championship belt. You know my abilities, my power, and you know that I can help you with both of your goals. With me, you can destroy all your enemies and bring the World belt back to the Dark Side, what do you say, "MONSTER," join the Dark Side and take control of the CWO, strike more fear into the hearts of your opponents than ever. As for everyone else in the CWO, know this, The Dark Side is as powerful as ever and may strike at anytime so beware of what lingers within the shadows. LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera fades to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Johnny Reb is in the interview area.) JOHNNY REB: Well, Damion, enough is enough. A man can only take so much. Forces have worked against me since mah return to the CWO. Tahm an' tahm again, the screws have been put ta' Johnny Reb. Well ah have decided to neutralize the biggest screw. Now, y'all will feel mah pain. Now, ah will plunge you into the darkness! Wherevuh y'all go, ah will be there. Title match or not, the tables will turn. You will meet your fate as ah meet mah destiny. Now is the tahm Damion. Come an' get some, boy. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: On the heels of this ever-increasing hot feud, this match has just been signed for ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II. It will be a barbed-wire towel match between Johnny Reb and Master of Darkness Damion. The loser of the match will be when the towel person determines their man unable to continue. This will be a bloody one, those without strong stomachs may want to turn away during this match. In Johnny Reb's corner, holding the towel, will be General Lee, and in Master of Darkness Damion's corner will be that turncoat, former CWO World Champion during our first incarnation, Rebel Yell, who you may remember turned against Johnny Reb at CWO CELEBRATION. We'll keep you posted on any further developments in this dangerous feud. Turning to other matters, the Highlanders made a little bit of a faux paus lately, forgetting who held the CWO World Tag Team Championships. We take you now to comments from the Highlanders. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens in a dark living room; a fire burns brightly in the corner, and the only light comes from a small lamp and a television screen. In the room, seated on a couple of leather armchairs are the Highlanders, Gavin and Conor McCloud.) GAVIN MCCLOUD: Right lad, here's yer whisky - I'll start the tape.... (A video of the last Tuesday Night Fever begins; dissolve to about an hour later when the Highlanders' interviews spot comes on. In a humorous interview, the Highlanders, forgetting that the World Tag Team champions are the Creatures of the Night, not the Deadly Sins, made a challenge to them, calling them the Sins through the whole thing.) GAVIN (chokes on whiskey): Did we really do that? Oh God, no, after all the time we've spent trying to establish ourselves as a credible contender... they're going to think we're the comedy relief now... in the same league as the Bushwhackers... arrrrgh! (Gavin slaps his forehead repeatedly.) CONOR (looking at his glass): I think we better stay of this stuff for a while, brother.... GAVIN (still slapping head): A month's hard work, down the khazi, nooooooooo.... _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr, who smiles wryly.) STARR: Well, Gavin McCloud needn't have worried, because the CWO Championship Committee has determined that the Highlanders will indeed meet the Creatures of the Night at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II for the CWO World Tag Team Championships. Hopefully the Highlanders' training regiment doesn't include lots of sauce, if you catch my meaning. Well, another tag team war is set to explode at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, and it is between the Pit Bosses and the Eternal Wanderers. We take you now to comments from both teams. _______________________________________________________________________________ (A very berated Exodus sits, upset over the situation that recently happened in the mount of Khitomer, where his partner undid their Lord's conditioning in an effort to gain the killer instinct which is Exile's nature.) EXODUS: Pit Bosses, the war is not over. We may not have those belts... (looks at the camera coldly) and it is your fault. We danced once and WE lost, we danced twice and you escaped. Next time, there will be a reckoning. Bad Guys... we have a score to settle but that time will come and when it... (Suddenly, a scream rises from the cave and Exodus actually cringes.) EXODUS: See what has happened, Bad Guys, Pit Bosses, for you have brought your doom down on you. However... (smiles) we seem to have a greater destiny. Creatures of the Night... (sighs) you have reached the mountain top and we admire that the most. However... (a shudduring quiver enters his voice) the 4th of February... your time will come. For on that night, your mountain will crumble and it will crumble hard. The CWO has no idea what they have wrought. The wrath of the Lord is upon you... prepare for the comings of the REVELATIONS! (A sad smile from Exodus as the camera fades.) EXODUS: Soon, doom... (fading) doom will be... (fading more, a faint whisper) upon us all... (Fade out.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Pit Bosses are in the interview area.) PIT BOSS #1 (laughing): I love it, I love it! PIT BOSS #2: What's up? PIT BOSS #1: We requested it, and we got it, Eternal Wanderers, at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, prepare for a Vegas Odds match! PIT BOSS #2: A Vegas Odds match, are you serious? We haven't wrestled one of those since our days in the LVWF! PIT BOSS #1: That's right, Wanderers, like we said, we don't buy that mumbo-jumbo of yours, and we are going to ground you guys in reality. Two socks filled with silver dollars, they can be lethal weapons if you know how to use them right, and we do know how to use them right, don't we, Pit Boss brother? PIT BOSS #2 (laughing): We sure do, well now, Wanderers, that we've got the Vegas Odds match in our favor, your dreams are going to crumble like rain, aren't they? PIT BOSS #1: OOGA-BOOGA, Wanderers! We're going to start calling you Siegfield and Roy, not only because of that magic crap, but for other reasons we don't want to go into right now. See you at the MASSACRE, boys, it's certainly going to be one. PIT BOSS #2: TAKE IT TO THE BANK! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: A Vegas Odds match has been signed between the Pit Bosses and the Eternal Wanderers for ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE, two socks filled with silver dollars on poles on opposite sides of the ring, the first team to retrieve the socks is the winner, not to mention the fact that they may then use those socks however they please, and you can be sure the Bosses have some definite ides about how to use them. Anyway, before we sign off today, we take you to comments from the mysterious Sin. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Sin is surrounded by a flaming pentagram, standing in the middle with a very fierce look.) SIN: Ok, Underdrive! Your @$$ is done.... stick a fork in him, HE'S DONE! Nobody calls me trash... NOBODY! I'll sign the line... and you're going to lose... Oh yeah, Underdrive, my magic tricks do me some good. Maybe we need a test for you to see, eh? (Sin looks upward and raises a Cane. The flames shoot high above Sin, when they die down. Sin is gone.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today.
