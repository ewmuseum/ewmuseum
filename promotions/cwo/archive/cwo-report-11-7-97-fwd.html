CWO REPORT 11-7-97 (fwd)

7 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey sitting at the CWO REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. The CWO held a House Show last night in Des Moines, IA. Here are the results for that card. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 11/06/97 Des Moines, IA Tsutomu Kobayashi made Kurt Gerstung submit to the Jumbo Sleeper. Time of match: 11:05. Foolkiller pinned Red Raven after the Thunderfunk. Time of match: 12:34. Mason-Dixon defeated Creatures of the Night by disqualification when Hellhound and Master of Darkness Damion attacked. Time of match: 19:05. Wildfire defeated Master of Darkness Damion by disqualification when Bloodbath attacked. Time of match: 13:20. Nation of Denomination defeated Highlanders by countout when the Deadly Sins attacked the Highlanders outside the ring and the two teams brawled back to the dressing room. Time of match: 10:51. Wild Cards defeated Harvesters of Sorrow when Deck pinned Pain after the Dead Man's Hand. Time of match: 15:20. Col. Taggart defeated 'The Ultimate Heel' Union Caine by disqualification when Foolkiller attacked. Time of match: 14:10. Dogs of War defeated Brother Justice when 'Pagan Horse' Logan Fett pinned Jerome Justice after the Zen Powerslam. Time of match: 6:03. The Focus pinned "Assassin 64" Sean Holiday after the Focal Point. Time of match: 8:40. WASHEY: Tonight the CWO makes a stop in Minneapolis MN. Here is the rundown of that card. FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 7, 1997 MINNEAPOLIS, MN Wrath vs. Hellhound (substituting for Judgment) "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum vs. Red Raven The Prophet vs. Skyhawk Brian "The Hammer" Williams vs. El Grande Pistolerro The Harvesters of Sorrow vs. Coldfront Wayne "College Guy" Schillo vs. Mondo Gecko Brother Justice vs. CPE Enforcers TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Mustafa Rahim vs. Bloodbath (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Newton Brothers (champions) vs. Mercenaries WASHEY: It's now time for our interview portion of the REPORT. First off, we have the man who will be meeting the CWO Heavyweight Champion at November Pain. Here he is, the hard-core icon, Sandman Jr. ________________________________________________________________________ [Sandman Jr.] You know, Johnny Reb... I've been working for this league for a long, long time. Remember when it was the CM2WF? I was aroud back then. I've seen world champions come and go. Hell, I remember when The Mad Doctor's Ghost was the champion... that was a dark time. I never got the shots at the World Belt back then... haven't been getting those shots recently either... man, can you believe I've only held one belt here in 3 years? And it was the Lightweight belt, the least prestigious of all the belts. And let's not even get into who I lost it to. But what matters now is that YOU'RE the world champ, Reb. You've beaten guys like Lord Foul, Sandman, Gold Kard, Joe Eagle, and my bud Chirs Myers... hell, I don't think I've beaten all those guys. But the CWO ain't the WWF, Reb. What do I mean by that? I mean, you won't be allowed to just give up the belt. Someone has to beat you, you have to lose. Many have tried, and have failed. Some miserably. But I'm something different. Am I better than the other guys? Not necessarilly. Am I more skilled, more charismatic? Maybe. But that's not the main reason. There's 2 reasons, Reb. The first, Rebel Yell is good, but Kellen could take him apart. Therefore, your outside interference wild card is trumped. So, it'll be one on one. The other reason? Cause I take it to a different level. Sandman, Lord Foul, Gold Kard, they try and out-power you. Eagle and Chris tired to keep it on the mat. Me? Yeah, I'll try power, but I know you're a little stronger. But I can do mat, which you also may be better at. But I can also go aerial. Can you do that, Reb? Can you counter it? No. Then of course, there's the hard-core edge. The edge that makes me the #1 contender, the edge that's made me the icon of the CWO. And it's that edge, Reb, that edge is what's gonna take me to the top by beating you and becoming the new CWO World Champion. The kind of champion that people can be proud of. Reb, let's go to the extreme! ________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Our only other interview today is from Wrath, responding to the comments made yesterday by Bloodbath. Here he is. ________________________________________________________________________ [Wrath] Hey Focus, you must have me confused with someone who gives a damn of what you think. Did you see what I did to Fulcrum? You did. You saw how I destroyed him. You were there when I destroyed you for the TV Belt! And you say I'm not a team player? Did our team win? And was I not one of the guys who escaped to help? Hell, I did a lot more for our team than you did, all you did was attack me, a guy who was ON YOUR TEAM! You want more of me, you got it pal. But man, I'm telling you, it's a mistake. A BIG mistake, cause you're gonna get in the Repention Rack, and then it's ALL OVER! You say I'm nothing? Then NOTHING beat you for the belt! Then NOTHING put you in the Repention Rack! It's like Nohbdy attacking the cyclops! Well, it won't matter. Soon, you'll do down, just like Bloodbath will. Focus, prepare to repent! _________________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody tomorrow on the REPORT. (fade to closing credits.)
