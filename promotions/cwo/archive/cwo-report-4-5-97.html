CWO REPORT 4/5/97

5 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, April 5, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Wheeling, WV, let's look at what went down on that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ QUICK RESULTS 4497 Wheeling. WV Mercenaries wrestled the Masters of Hardcore to a double countout. Time of match: 10:03. Exile pinned Panther after the End of the World. Time of match: 4:40. STEEL CAGE (no escape): Arlon Flaym pinned Ganglord after the Hurricanrana into a Cradle. Time of match: 6:05. Chris Greco/Tommy Lightning defeated Deadly Sins when Tommy Lightning pinned Sloth after the dropkick. Time of match: 24:53. Gold Kard pinned Johnny Reb after the Die Flieder Kross. Time of match: 20:56. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the Keuka Smack. Time of match: 10:32. 'Master of Darkness' Damion pinned Bishop after the Darkness Falls tombstone piledriver. Time of match: 11:44. NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Dark Raven defeated Goliath by countout. Time of match: 1:45. Vlad the Impaler/Serpentmaster defeated David 'The Destroyer' Davis/Inferno when Vlad the Impaler pinned Inferno after the moonsault. Time of match: 14:28. WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Regulators defeated Pit Bosses when Regulator Ice pinned Pit Boss #1 after the Choke Slam. Time of match: 7:19. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Don't forget about tonight's exciting SATURDAY NIGHT FIGHTIN', let's see what's on tap for tonight wild event! _______________________________________________________________________________ CWO SATURDAY NIGHT FIGHTIN' 4/5/97 Spartacus vs. the Focus Mason-Dixon vs. Eternal Wanderers Bishop vs. Centurion _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Also, tomorrow is SHOWCASE, let's look at the lineup for that. _______________________________________________________________________________ SHOWCASE 4/6/97 HOUR #1: Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Baby Waldo Creatures of the Night vs. Eternal Wanderers Steel vs. Ganglord HOUR #2: Clark Gray vs. Akillies Wrath vs. Arlon Flaym LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Tiger Rogers vs. Dark Raven _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: And all of that, of course, is leading up to tomorrow night's exciting PPV event, APRIL ARMAGEDDON, featuring the return of the ROCK, let's see the complete lineup for that great PPV event. _______________________________________________________________________________ APRIL ARMAGEDDON (FEATURING THE RETURN OF THE ROCK) APRIL 6, 1997 "LIVE" FROM BALTIMORE MARYLAND 6:30 STARTING TIME WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Bane (champion) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Regulators vs. Pit Bosses (champions) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Wildfire and Bloodbath vs. Bad Guys (champions) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (LADDER): Masher Maccabee vs. Mondo Gecko (champion) NO HOLDS BARRED: Gold Kard vs. Johnny Reb TAG TEAM TRIANGLE MATCH: Highlanders vs. Eternal Wanders vs. Ohlhoff and Couchman GRUDGE MATCH: Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams 6-MAN ACTION: Ganglord and Da Hood Rats vs. Joe Eagle and the Big Cool DOMINATION MATCH (need 3 falls in a row to win): Chris Greco vs. Clark Gray TIME OF TESTING: Centurion vs. Spartacus THE ROCK (six cages, two men from each side in each cage, first team to escape wins $25,000, after every cage has had members of a team to escape, the cages are lifted, and then a "Best of the Rest" battle royal is held, with the winner receving $25,000): ALLIANCE (Tiger Rogers, Inferno, Goliath, David "The Destroyer" Davis) DOOMSDAY (Maximum Overdrive, Baby Waldo, Kaptain Krunch, Korperal Punishment) DARK SIDE (Master of Darkness Damion, Night Crawler, Night Slinger) WILD CARD: Wayne Schillo vs. UNHOLY ALLIANCE (Vlad the Impaler, Bishop, Serpentmaster, Dark Raven, Hellhound) DEADLY SINS (Wrath, Pride, Sloth) WILD CARDS: Panther, Stonewall Mason, Beauregard Dixon, Moses EYE ON THE STORM: Sandman Jr. vs. the Focus Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. Steel Jason "Flash" Varga vs. Arlon Flaym Tommy Lightning vs. Kurt "Thrill from the Chill" Gerstung DARK MATCH: El Grande Pistolerro vs. "Lonestar" Mike Williams _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars. Still holding the CWO World 6 Man Tag Team Titles is Doomsday, let's hear from them. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries, who are still wearing their CWO World 6 Man Tag Team Titles.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Well, what happened Wussie Bunch? Hey, you know something? We still have the titles? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Oh my God! You're absolutely right! But I thought the Wussie Bunch was going to beat us for them! KAPTAIN KRUNCH: It's just like they've done in the past, and it's a little thing called CHOKING! You couldn't get the job done! You three are nothing but a trio of LOSERS! (Punishment holds up the "L" hand sign) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: If you EVER get in the ring with us again, we're going to end your careers! Speaking of ending careers, (turns to Punishment) what did you think of the Creatures of the Night? KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: I think a lot of things about them. EX-champions. An overrated team. A team that we went out and destroyed last night. As I went up to deliver the Flying Clothesline to Night Slinger, I couldn't help but think that if they still had the titles, we would be the CWO World Tag Team Champions. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Well, everybody knows that the Pit Bosses got away with a criminal act when they gave us that title shot only to keep their titles. Pit Bosses, it won't be long now when the bell is going to ring and you're going to be looking at the new CWO World Tag Team Champions. But that's a little ways off. Because when it's all said and done..... KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: The Mercenaries ARE THE TEAM THAT RULES THE WORLD! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: He may have an intense matchup with Mondo Gecko at APRIL ARMAGEDDON for the Television title, but somewhere on the horizon Masher Maccabee is going to have to deal with the Focus, who he basically prevented from becoming the Television champion. Let's hear from Masher. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Masher is well-dressed and looks ready to travel. David "The Truth" Thomas stands next to him, wearing his "GO MASHER!" T-shirt.) MASHER MACCABEE: Blah blah blah, Focus. Stop scaring me so much. I wrote the book on being a bad guy son, so don't be getting all jumpy now. All I want is Art Deco in the ring so I can beat the hell out of him with that ladder and get MY belt back. You want to interfere, Spanky? Bring it. You'll get punked too. As for you, Likescox, the "TRUTH" is, yes a man can't hit a lady, but hey, that just means we'll beat up Silly Putty even more while our special surprise holds you back. That's right, Pamela Anderson is coming to the ring with the TRUTH and I to whip your ass! Bring your spin kicks and your slaps and screams, it doesn't matter. Both you and Gecko are finished, and that's the bottom line! Tell 'em, TRUTH! DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS: I couldn't say it any better, my main man. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tomorrow night, before the actual PPV gets started, an exciting expanded version of EYE ON THE STORM, will be seen, showcasing some of the newest talents to enter the CWO, including this man, The Focus. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Focus is in the interview area.) THE FOCUS: Hey, Sandman Jr... we have got ourselves a match at the Eye on the Storm. I figure if I can beat you, then maybe I'll be in the next Pay Per View. I've been on a roll here lately, and you're just the next person in line to get the Focal Point. After that, no one gets up... except you, Mondo Gecko. You earned my respect on Tuesday, and that's no easy feat. If you keep the title on Sunday, I'd like a rematch... hell, even a triangle match with you, me, and Maccabee. Any way is fine with me. Sandman Jr., you sure as hell better be ready, because you will lose... guaranteed. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see you tomorrow at APRIL ARMAGEDDON, and don't forget to watch SATURDAY NIGHT FIGHTIN' and SHOWCASE! So long for now!
