CWO REPORT 12-6-97 (fwd)

7 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey sitting at the CWO REPORT desk.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Hello everybody and welcome to another edition of the CWO REPORT. The CWO was on the campus of Ball State University last night for a House Show. Let's look at the results of that card. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 12/5/97 Ball State University, IN 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum defeated Tsutomu Kobayashi by countout. Time of match: 17:07. 'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat pinned 'Lone Wolf' Ryan Deutscher after the flying spinning leg lariat. Time of match: 18:23. Wild Cards (substituting for the Unit) defeated Youth in Action when Deck pinned Masked Youth #2 after the Dead Man's Hand. Time of match: 18:52. Southern Dynamite pinned 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia after the flying headbutt. Time of match: 10:15. Southern Dynamite was struck by a mirror by Joe Root after the match. The pair then proceeded to beat on Southern Dynamite until Youth in Action made the save. Brother Justice and Steve Keller/Tommy Lightning wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 4:34. Wildfire and Sandman wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Mercenaries defeated Dogs of War when Kaptain Krunch pinned 'Irresistible Assassin' Eddie Fantastic after the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Time of match: 13:06. World Champion Johnny Reb defeated Bloodbath by disqualification when the Dark Side attacked. Time of match: 16:55. WASHEY: Tonight, we will be making a stopover in Indianapolis, IN. Here's the lineup for the card. SATURDAY, DECEMBER 6, 1997 INDIANAPOLIS, IN Jim "Jazzman" Taylor vs. Kaptain Krunch Masashi "Lightning" Lanther vs. 'Airborne' Mark Fulcrum Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Sandman Mason-Dixon vs. the Creatures of the Night Gold Kard vs. Mondo Gecko Pit Bosses vs. Bad Guys Joe Eagle vs. Diamondback Chris Myers Mercenaries vs. Dogs of War CHAMPIONS' CHALLENGE: Light Heavyweight Champion Jason "Flash" Varga vs. World Champion Johnny Reb WASHEY: Let's go to the interviews for today. _____________________________________________________________________ (Mondo Gecko, Ranger Taurus, and El Grande Pistolerro are sparring in the "CPE Dungeon" as the prepare for CWO CELEBRATION '97!) GECKO: I got a big week ahead of me with two of my favorite wrestlers to take on in Gold Fart and Masher Maccabum! Goldielocks, you have been a pain in my ass for a while and December 6th I get to kick the snot out of you and send you crying back to the Dark Side, you pathetic coward. SUNDAY SHOWCASE, I got a lot of surprises as I will show the world why I will be the next CWO World Heavyweight Champion. It is time that the CPE starts taking control of the chaos here and Johnny Reb you are in for a world of hurt! TAURUS: Seems like dem Deadly Sins is talking big now that they stole the World Tag titles. Hey fat guy and little man, if you two feel froggy than get your butts in the ring with me and Wesley and We will take those titles and bring them home to CPE baby! PISTOLERRO: Hellhound, as you are going to find out at CWO CELEBRATION, payback is hell! you thought it was funny throwing me off a 30 foot high steel cage and I am going to show you otherwise! Last time you had your buddies from the Dark Side helping out, this time I am going to send you to the hospital PERMANENTLY!!! ___________________________________________________________ (Brent "The Ace" Alles is seen wandering the streets of Byron Center, MI. The Christmas decorations are out in full force, and a general feel of the holiday is in the cold wintry air.) BRENT "THE ACE" ALLES: Seems like I'm always coming back to my hometown to gain a little stability in my life. And honestly, right now my life is pretty stable, finally. I've got a good woman in Amanda, a beautiful son in Alex, and I've finally patched up my relationship with my family. (Alles ducks in to the Family Restaurant, where he is immediately greeted by some patrons. Alles shakes hands before sitting down in a booth.) ALLES: Can't beat this small-town life. Anyway, I've even finally gotten the respect of my mentor and trainer, Lord Hile Troy. Now, believe me, things still aren't hunky-dory between us, as I don't trust him any further than I can throw him. However, it's time to put the past behind us and head towards the future. (Alles rifles through the latest copy of CWO Magazine.) ALLES: You know, I look at these ratings and I see myself in the top 20, after a long drought. I'll admit it, my head wasn't on straight for most of this year, I was so consumed with gaining revenge on everybody, that I didn't focus on my career. (A friendly waitress brings Alles a cup of coffee, as he smiles back at her with thanks.) ALLES (taking a sip of coffee): You know, my father had one wish for me when he was training me. He wanted me to be World Champion. Maybe that's an unrealistic goal for a father to have for his son, but that's the way my dad is: he wants the best. And now that I've put the past behind me, it's time to move towards the future. 1997 is almost done, 1998 will truly be the year of the "Ace." (Alles smiles as he sees Amanda Bloom enter with his son Alex. She sits down and smiles, and they begin conversing as the camera fades out.) _______________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on MOD Damion who is standing in front of the entire Dark Side. They are all present for this as their master has commanded.) MOD DAMION: Sandman Jr, you talk too much. You see the force standing here. You see former CWO World heavyweight champions, you see former CWO World Tag team and Six-man team champions, former TV Champs. Every member of the Dark Side has held gold in this pathetic federation, the CWO. Wannabe Jr., what have you done? Hurt some people, thrill some of the idiots who pay to watch you wrestle, run guys out of this fed? I've done it all too. You see, I control this: the strongest force in the CWO and after you the CPE shall continue their lessons and do I have some good plans for your pathetic souls, CPE. Just wait. Jr., you threaten my life. You are stupider than I thought. Everyone with half a brain knows life means nothing to me. Life is useless, it's a weakness in mortals like yourself, like Reb, and all of those who have knelt down before my power, the power of the DARKNESS! Jr., just ask the beauty you keep by your side, she will tell you all about the Darkness... oh wait, that's right, you say she won't speak... maybe that's because I have her tongue... (MOD holds up a glass jar that is full of some liquid and there is something inside the jar but it cannot be made out just what it is.) MOD DAMION: Wannabe Jr. They won't be taking you to jail after our match, the only place they will take your filthy carcass is the morgue. I am going to be the one who kills you. I will take your soul and then I will take back my prize. Kellen, my dear, I will see you soon... very soon. ALL THREE: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (camera fads to black) ______________________________________________________________ ("Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher at his home in Chicago IL. He's sitting in front of the TV with a six pack and watching the replay of Tuesday Night Fever with his lovely wife Jaime. After the stun gun Lone Wolf breaks out into laughter) DEUTSCHER: Look at that! JAIME: Yes, dear... (Deutscher turns to the camera) DEUTSCHER: You want to talk your sh*t to me, Painmaster!?! Well, do it all you want 'cause I won't have any problem dropping your a*s! Painmaster, there's only one thing you're the master of! And you and I both know what that is! Now why don't you come to Tuesday Night Fever next week and I'll have another stun gun waiting for you! How about you, dear, wanna see me kick the Painmaster a*s again? JAIME: Sure! DEUTSCHER: I would drop you tonight, tomorrow night, and any other night and that's the bottom line 'cause Lone Wolf said so! (the camera fades out) ______________________________________________________________ (Pit Bosses are in the interview area.) PIT BOSS #1: Well, well, well, didn't a lot of jaws drop and eyes roll back into heads when everybody saw the triumphant return of the Pit Bosses in the 1st Annual Wayne Carpenter Memorial Tag Team Tournament. PIT BOSS #2: They sure did, brother! And though we've had our differences in the past, believe me, the Pit Bosses are now united, and we are going straight to the top to regain our past glory. PIT BOSS #1: I'm a little ashamed of the things I did as the Vegas High Roller, but hey, you live, you learn. And thankfully, my Pit Boss brother here was forgiving enough to take me back. PIT BOSS #2: Hey, knock it off with the mushy stuff, you're going to make me cry! PIT BOSS #1 (laughing): Well, at least some things haven't changed. Like the shoddy state of the tag team division here in the CWO. It's not going to be to of a high-stakes bet that the Pit Bosses will sooner or later get the World Tag Team Championships around our waist. PIT BOSS #2: Deadly Sins, it amuses us to no end that you two are actually considered the best tag team in the world right now. We remember the times that you couldn't shine our boots, and believe me, boys, those times are coming again. PIT BOSS #1: It's time to get hard-core on the CWO tag scene, Vegas style. PIT BOSS #2: BANK ON IT! _________________________________________________________ (Wildfire stands alone in a dark cave, with a dark blood red light shining on him and reflecting light off the Television Title belt. He stands in silence as we hear the voice of Miss Amber) AMBER: Master of Darkness Damion, you felt the true power of the dark side on Tuesday Night Fever. If your boys didn't come to your rescue, you would have succumb to the Wild Thing!! Wildfire will have his day with you, and the next time, he will destroy you!! Now on to Bloodbath. You were Wildfires best friend. But you betrayed him, now you will have to face "The new phenom of the CWO" one more time. And this time, he is gonna bury you once and for all, as he destroys every member of the Dark Side. You see Bloodbath, you had the true power of the dark side and you used it for evil, now that Wildfire posses the true power, he will take it all the way to the CWO World Championship. Thats right Johnny Reb, your days are numbered, cause just as the dark side will fall, one way or another, you will fall too, either to Wildfire or someone else!! CWO Be ware!! (lighting flashes and thunder crashes. Wildfire points his bat at the camera and then smashes it) _____________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody tomorrow on the REPORT. (fade to closing credits.)
