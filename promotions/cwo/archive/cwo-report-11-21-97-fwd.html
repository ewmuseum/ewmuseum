CWO REPORT 11-21-97 (fwd)

22 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey sitting at the CWO REPORT desk. A British and American flag are flying behind him.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to the CWO REPORT. We're continuing with the Wayne Carpenter Memorial Tag Team Tournament, and have passed the opening rounds. We're into the second round of action now, and there have been some surprises and more than a few upsets along the way. The second round has shaped up like this. CWO WAYNE CARPENTER MEMORIAL TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT ROUND 2 32. Pit Bosses vs. 5. Jade Dragons 24. Foolkiller and Caine vs. 13. Gerstung and Bomber 18. Harvesters of Sorrow vs. 11. Creatures of the Night 26. Wild Ones vs. 3. Highlanders 29. Wild Cards vs. 8. Mercenaries 21. Taurus and McMichael vs. 16. Damion and Hellhound 19. Prophet and Rahim vs. 10. Schillo and Williams 4. Myers and Fulcrum vs. 31. Carpenter and Lightning WASHEY: Be sure not to miss a single bit of this exciting tournament, where the finals will be decided at November Pain tomorrow! One of these teams will win one million dollars. That brings us to our interview segment of the REPORT, where we will hear from two tag teams that are involved. First off, let's go to the comments made from the Dark Side, then we will hear from the Mercenaries. _______________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the entire Dark Side who stand in a dark room the only light comes from some unknown source and does not create much light.) MOD Damion: CWO you have seen the power of the Dark Side before and on lately that power has been growing. On Tuesday at Fever you witness the strongest force here in the CWO grow even stronger. Yes you all saw Gold Kard enter the ring at MY side and then when confronted by those losers he once thought were his friends they abandoned him. Gold Kard brings to the Dark Side the wealth I need to continue my dominance over this puny fed. Soon I shall rule you all and my pets and Dark Minions shall stand at my side with me watching my back and money bags will be there to he has seen the power of the Dark Side and like those before him has been drawn to the forces that are deep within the NIGHT hidden in the DARKNESS. Night Slinger: It seems that money and wealth is just falling into the hands of the Dark Side. Like the Master said first Gold Kard and now the Tag Tourney with a 1 million dollar prize waiting for us. Sean Holiday and you little friend Skyhawk you two will be the first of many to fall by the way side as me and Crawler make our way to the finals and then we will hand over the money to our master MOD. Night Crawler: Like the man said the money will be ours and we will be back on top of the CWO tag team rankings and then we will welcome back the NIGHT into the CWO as we become the Tag champs again. MOD Damion: Yes the Dark Side will soon bring back the DARKNESS that once ruled you all and we will bring order back to the CWO. I will soon bring back order to the CWO. Hellhound: Master and me get to fight together and I will get big reward if me and he win. We have a first fight against the Brothers Justice. I have listened to justice before only the Master and he has told me that we shall win and that I will destroy all the enemies of my master and make him happy. MOD Damion: Listen up CWO the Dark Side is growing and will continue to grow in size and power and it starts with this Tag Tournament. I have three teams going after my prize and we will win it all. All seven: LONG LIVE THE NIGHT!!! _________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on the Mercenaries after their victory in the opening round of the tournament.) KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Welcome to another interview given by the CWO's most underrated team, and the team that will go on to win the Carpenter tournament, the Mercenaries. What we're going to do right now is address all of the comments made by some of the lesser talented tag teams that have been spouting off about what they think they're going to do to us in this tournament. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: And to all of the others who think that they are going to be the ones to win it, we can only say that sooner or later, you're gonna cross the Mercenaries' path, and when you do, we're going to tear your heads off. The only team that's going to be winning that million dollar match is us. Not the Highlanders, not the Dork Side, not the Jade Dragons, not the Harvesters, nobody! KAPTAIN KRUNCH: Now looking at our next match, we see that we're going to get our hands on the Wild Cards. Well, it looks like all the crap you were spouting earlier this week on FEVER is finally going to catch up with you when we are going to break our feet off in your a$$es. You might think you're something special, but the thing that separates you from the Mercenaries is that you just THINK you're special. We KNOW that we are, and it's going to be that confidence that's going to allow us to beat you to within an inch of your stupid pathetic lives, just like we will to whoever is the next team to get in our way. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Everybody saw what we did to the Big Cool. Everybody saw what we did to Heat and White on FEVER. Everybody saw what we did to whoever those illegal immigrants were in the opening round of the tournament, and it's just going to be more of the same. First we win the tournament, then we get the money, and then we're coming for those World Titles, and we don't give a damn as to who is holding onto them. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: And we know everybody is asking why that is..... KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Because we're the team that rules the world, and you're not!!! _______________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Two very confidant teams heading into the second round of that massive tournament. Our final interview for today comes from the Focus as he gears up for his match tomorrow on November Pain. _______________________________________________________________ (The camera appears on a black room. There is no light except from the eyes of The Focus, digitally altered to appear red.) THE FOCUS: Tsutomu Kobayashi. A challenge for me, hmmm? I accept your challenge. It's curious to me...why would you put forth a challenge to me. Do you respect me? Are you simply trying to make a name for yourself through me? If that's the case, then don't bother. If you need someone to wrestle, I'll do it...and I'll beat you. That's all there is to it. So, talk to whomever you need to talk to in order to get this deal signed. I'll consider it a warm up match. No disrespect to you Kobayashi, but I simply have another man on my mind...Wrath. A deadly sin. Perhaps. I see Wrath falling to the hands of discipline...to the hands of The Focus. You are a deadly sin, I am the pinnacle of concentration. We meet again quite soon. I hope you're prepared, for I certainly am. I hope you are ready for the disgrace of losing to me. I could put you out of this sport. I could make you a broken man. Don't unleash that side of me Wrath. Don't make me hurt you. You are nothing. I am everything. ________________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody on the next REPORT!!! (fade to closing credits.)
