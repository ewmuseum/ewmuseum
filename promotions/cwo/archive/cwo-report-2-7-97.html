CWO REPORT 2/7/97

7 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Show opens up on the Wild Ones in the interview area.) MONDO GECKO: Big Cool, you are right in our way for the tag team titles and we are going to fly over you two. We have been on the back burner for too long and in Ithaca, where wrestling is top dog, The Wild Ones will heat up the place and melt the Big Cool. BATTLECAT: Big Cool, you big bad brawlers have run roughshod over this federation and we are going to put a stop to it. Bottom line, tomorrow night, we are ready to take you two down and rise to the top of the tag team rankings. We have gotten our skills together and we are ready to take the tag team titles and put them around our waist. Chris Greco, if you want, we will watch your back and make sure you get a fair fight. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens on a pair of figures wearing skirts then from behind them step out MOD Damion, and Hell's Pets [Hellhound and Dark Raven], who starts kicking and hitting the dumbies.) MOD DAMION: Highlanders, look at Dark Raven back there, I have dressed those dumbies up to look like you guys and look at him kicking the stuffing out of them... (laughs at his comments) just like Hell's Pets are going to do to the pair of you two tonight. Dark Raven is hungry and so is this beast here, Hellhound. (Hellhound is looking at Dark Raven, who is tearing the head off the bigger of the two dumbies. Then Hellhound realizes that the camera is on him.) HELLHOUND: Highlanders, before the Creatures of the Night destroy you, me and Raven are going to tear you apart limb from limb. (Just then the head of one of the dumbies hit Hellhound in the chest and he holds it to the camera) HELLHOUND: See look at this after the match maybe we will take off your heads and make ourselves a nice trophy case starting with yours. (Hellhound finishes and joins Dark Raven by the hanging dumbies and tears one down and is all over it as the camera shows stuffing flying around.) MOD DAMION: These two beasts are ready for war and Highlanders, you have upset the Dark Side and now you start to pay with your blood, your souls, and your lives. Welcome to my world, my pain, my DARKNESS... LONG LIVE THE NIGHT! (Camera pans to Hell's Pets as they destroy what's left of the dumbies, then fade to black.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Camera opens up on the team of DOOMSDAY prior to the matches at Thursday's House Show. Maximum Overdrive's face is slightly bandaged. The Mercenaries flank Overdrive, and Baby Waldo stands in the back. The Mercenaries and Baby Waldo are wearing their CWO 6 Man World Tag Team Championships.) OVERDRIVE: Well now, it seems that a little no-talent punk called Sin has just [censored] with the wrong man. You'd better get ready to change your name to Slime, because after I get done, a little streak of it is all that's going to be left of you. Get ready to add your name to the list of wrestlers in this federation I've destroyed. (Baby Waldo pokes Overdrive in the back and points to himself, then at the camera.) OVERDRIVE: It also seems we got a piece of prime intelligence from a certain Jason Varga. "Whoop-ass"? Maybe you should change your name to Mr. Originality. Waldo is not only going to beat your worthless butt in front of your hometown crowd, he's going to embarrass you. Then you can try to explain to all of your family what happened when you couldn't get the job done. KAPTAIN KRUNCH: As for the three idiots that we won these titles from, if you have any hopes of getting these back, forget about it. At the PPV, we will show you for a second time the reason why we took these titles. KORPERAL PUNISHMENT: Face it, worms. You were just keeping these titles warm until we decided it was time to take them, just like any tag team is that is holding onto their titles. When we decide the time is right, we will not be denied. Creatures, Bad Girls, we haven't forgotten about you. In fact, it's just the opposite. These titles that we hold now show us what we are capable of, and believe me, your days are numbered. OVERDRIVE: Just like yours are, Sin. You made the biggest mistake of your life and now it's time to pay the fiddler. _______________________________________________________________________________ (CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the CWO Control Room.) JOHNNY STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the REPORT for Friday, February 7, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. We are one week away from what is shaping up to be the most dangerous PPV the CWO has ever put on, ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II. From potentially bloody barbed wire matches to a match where you need to bury your opponent alive to win, this one won't be for the faint of heart. Leading up to this great PPV will of course be the same great live action you're used to from the CWO, and last night was no exception as we were in Ithaca, NY, at the Hill Center gym at Ithaca University. Let's look at what went down. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 2/6/97 Ithaca. NY (Ithaca University, Hill Center Gymnasium) Cake Boy made Hoss submit to the Head Scissors. Time of match: 9 : 51 Wild Ones defeated The Big Cool when Battlecat pinned Jumbo Jenkins after the Spinning Wheel Kick. Time of match: 19 : 55 Bloodbath and Sloth wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 8 : 40 Hell's Pets defeated Highlanders when Dark Raven pinned Conor McCloud after the Death From Above flying dropkick. Time of match: 13 : 51 Inferno defeated Sandman Jr. by countout. Time of match: 5 : 39 Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned Baby Waldo after the Flash Flood. Time of match: 14 : 18 NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Bad Guys defeated Regulators when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Regulator Ice after the Bad Guys' Bash. Time of match: 20 : 29 WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Wayne 'Keuka Guy' Schillo pinned Chris Greco (Flyboy) after the Keuka Crack. Time of match: 10 : 11 _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Tonight we are in Binghamton, NY, let's look at the lineup. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW 2/7/97 BINGHAMTON, NY (BINGHAMTON CIVIC CENTER) Tiger Rogers vs. Mondo Gecko Cake Boy vs. "Mr. Niceguy" Steve White Deadly Sins vs. Hoss and Little Joe Wrath vs. Ron "The Rude Guy" Johnson The Big Cool vs. Pit Bosses Sandman Jr. vs. Ganglord MOD Damion vs. Hile Troy WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Creatures of the Night (champions) vs. Highlanders _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: On Wednesday night, when the CWO came to the Turning Stone casino in Rome, NY, "Monster" Rich Clark came down to ringside to watch Sandman's match with another giant, Goliath. Sandman proceeded to brawl with the 7'4", 481 pound Clark, and was subsequently counted out. We now go to "Monster" Rich Clark with a response to this incident. _______________________________________________________________________________ ("Monster" Rich Clark stands alone, in the middle of the ring after last nights house show. The arena is black, except for one spotlight right on Rich. "Organ Grinder" by Marilyn Manson is playing over the loudspeakers. Rich just stands alone, pondering his next move.) "MONSTER" RICH CLARK: Sandman... what you did on Wednesday was unspeakable. You say that you have no fears, yet you attacked me while I was simply scouting you for our match next Friday night. I had no intention of attacking you, nor did I sense your fear. Now I do. You are nothing but a scared little Sandboy, and that is what you will always be. Paranoia is a fear that many have, but you, my friend, have taken it to an extreme. Prepare for what just may be the final match of your career, because you never know what may happen next. I have already rid the CWO of Manocelli. You are next, then Schillo, and after that, I will find a new cause. You are not ready, and that is why I will beat you in San Francisco. Prepare to meet your maker. (The arena goes black. The words "Hit List" appear on the screen. Pictures of Manocelli, Sandman, Schillo, and three photos of the outlines of CWO wrestlers appear underneath them. There is a red "X" through Manocelli's photo, and a red circle around Sandman.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Moses has been surprisingly effective as a Light Heavyweight Champion, in fact, he is as of this point undefeated in singles competition. With a tough rematch this weekend on SHOWCASE against Talon in a no-disqualification match for the belt, and a match against the quickly rising Tiger Rogers at ST. VALENTINE'S DAY MASSACRE II, Moses certainly has his work cut out for him. We go to comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Savior is off on a mission. It is just Moses standing there, with his CWO Light-Heavyweight Championship around his waist. He seems very proud of himself.) MOSES: Well, I am just thrilled with myself now. I have been undefeated in any federation for the past three months in singles competition. CWO, none of you can beat me. Johnny Reb, you almost won the CWO Heavyweight Championship, but you couldn't beat me. Come to think of it, I want a shot at Schillo. In a title trade match. Winner by pinfall gets the World Title, loser by pinfall gets the Light Heavyweight Title. C'mon, Wayne, what do you say to that? CWO top brass, sanction that one for us if he agrees. Now, on to Steel. What the hell were you thinking? You attacked me from behind. Now, that was just wrong. I don't care who the hell you think you are, you NEVER, EVER attack someone from behind. It's just not done. You wanted a shot at the Light Heavyweight Title, then just challenge for it. It's that simple. Get it? Good. (He walks off. FADE TO BLACK.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny Starr.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today. We'll see you tomorrow!
