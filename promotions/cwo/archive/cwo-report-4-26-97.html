CWO REPORT 4/26/97

26 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, April 26, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. Last night the CWO was in Topeka, KS, let's look at the results of that great card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 4/25/97 Topeka, KS WORLD 6-MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Southern Pride (Johnny Reb and Mason-Dixon) defeated the champions Unholy Alliance (Dark Raven, Vlad the Impaler, Serpentmaster), winning the belts when Stonewall Mason pinned Serpentmaster after the Southern Spike piledriver. Time of match: 9:33. Gold Kard pinned Sandman Jr. after the rolling reverse cradle. Time of match: 13:10. Deadly Sins defeated Maximum Overdrive/Baby Waldo when Sloth pinned Maximum Overdrive after the Repention. Time of match: 21:41. Wrath made Brad 'The Man' Couchman submit to the Repentence Rack. Time of match: 19:53. Wild Side defeated Coldfront (Steve White was substituting for the out of action 'Lonestar' Mike Williams) when Bloodbath pinned 'Mr. Niceguy' Steve White after the Tombstone Powerbomb. Time of match: 27:58. The Big Cool defeated Regulators when Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor pinned Regulator Ice after the Star Flash (backbreaker by Taylor, flying splash by Jenkins). Time of match: 5:53. North American Champion Jason 'Flash' Varga defeated 'Monster' Rich Clark by disqualification when Tiger Rogers attacked. Goliath also attacked Clark at this time and a brawl between the four men commenced. Time of match: 2:32. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you saw, we have new World 6-Man Tag Team Champions in Johnny Reb and Mason-Dixon, congratulations to them. Tonight we are in Omaha, NE, let's look at the lineup for that card. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW PREVIEW SATURDAY, APRIL 26, 1997 OMAHA, NE Regulators vs. Wild Side (Bloodbath and Wildfire) Joe Eagle vs. Mondo Gecko Ganglord vs. Tommy Lightning Da Hood Rats vs. Mason-Dixon Gold Kard vs. Johnny Reb Brent "The Ace" Alles vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams The College Guys (Jason "Flash" Varga, Wayne "College Guy" Schillo, Brian "The Hammer" Williams) vs. Unholy Alliance (Dark Raven, Vlad the Impaler, Serpentmaster) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (non-title): Pit Bosses (champions) vs. Highlanders _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We now turn to comments from CWO superstars, starting with the Regulators, who have a match with the on a roll Wild Side tonight in Omaha. They are joined by Mondo Gecko who also has a match tonight, against the equally hot Joe Eagle. Let's hear from the Wild Bunch. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Regulators and Mondo Gecko are in the interview area.) REGULATOR FIRE: Wildfire and Bloodbath, you guys are the most hardcore team here and we can't wait to face you, but get ready for a night of hell. We are on a mission to win the tag team titles and we can't wait until we get our hands on them. Wildfire, we like your style but if you think you guys are going to run all over us then your are sadly mistaken. It is time for the CWO to have the rules enforced and that is what we are about to do. Tell 'em, Ice. REGULATOR ICE: WELL, Bloodbath, you are the phenom of the CWO but if you think that your parlor tricks and your looks are going to intimidate us, then think again. We came here from Detroit, Michigan, home of the bad boys, and we plan on doing what ever it takes to win the titles. It is time the CWO felt the power of the Regulators and Wild Side, you two will be the first two to feel it this weekend! MONDO GECKO: Joe Eagle, you are a tough wrestler and a good man but I am going to put you in a world of hurt when I hit you from all over the area and show you what MONDO EXTREME ACTION IS REALLY ABOUT! The CWO needs a leader and I am the man to lead the troops to battle. It seems that GKI, Bane, & Brent "The Ace" Alles are causing all the trouble here and I am ready to put a stop to it. Bottom line here is that MONDO GECKO IS READY TO GET MONDO EXTREME, BABY! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Despite the everpresent physical and mental attacks by Brent "The Ace" Alles, the College Guys in the CWO seem to be holding up well. We turn to them for comments. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The College Guys are at Wayne Schillo's home.) WAYNE "COLLEGE GUY" SCHILLO: Here, at my house, we are trying to teach Brian Williams how to have a little assertiveness. The guy's only got one loss, he's ranked #1, and he doesn't even have a title shot at May-hem! Let's watch as 'Flash' Varga tries to teach him how to show that he's the boss.... (Jason "Flash" Varga and Brian "The Hammer" Williams are in a room together.) JASON "FLASH" VARGA: Come on, Bri, lemme hear ya try it... "I'M THE MAN!" BRIAN "THE HAMMER" WILLIAMS: I am the man? VARGA: IS THAT IT? COME ON! "I'M THE MAN!" WILLIAMS: I'm the what? VARGA: You gotta raise on up, bring it in from way down: "I'M THE MAN!" WILLIAMS: I'm the man. SCHILLO: Who knows? Maybe it's working. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As we said yesterday on the REPORT, Wayne Schillo will be at next week's FEVER to address the charges that Brent "The Ace" Alles has leveled against him, we look forward to hearing what Wayne REALLY has to say about this issue. Another man behind a lot of controversy here in the CWO is Masher Maccabee, and we go now to comments from him. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Masher Maccabee is in the GKI conference room, pacing.) MASHER MACCABEE: That's it! Obviously, I am not nasty enough. You want to see a heel? I'll show all of you CWO marks a bad guy! You thought what I did to Tamara Likescox was bad? You just wait! Johnny Reb, you Confederate *******, any time you want some of the Hangover, come and get it! Art Deco, the Jerusalem Jackknife is coming your way. Inferno, you have a harder time lighting a fire than Mike Foley, and I will kick your @$$ too. I am a bitter bad guy, CWO, I've been screwed by everyone, except of course, the man, DAVID "THE TRUTH" THOMAS, and Herr Kard. To mock Burrito Boy Pistolerro, "Viva la GKI!" CWO, May-Hem is mine. I can't be beat, and I won't be beat. That's the corporate line. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: Tonight in Omaha, the Pit Bosses go up against the Highlanders in a non-title match, but you can be sure they are looking ahead to the actual title match they have with the two Scots at MAY-HEM. Let's hear from the Pit Bosses. _______________________________________________________________________________ (The Pit Bosses are in the interview area.) PIT BOSS #1: You know, everybody's been trying to spread rumors about us, to paint some kind of picture that we're about ready to break up. PIT BOSS #2: Jealousy breeds a lot of lies, and that's what we're getting right now. Me and Boss #1 have been together a lot of years, starting in the Las Vegas Wrestling Federation as ham-and-eggers, and then hooking up as a team. PIT BOSS #1: And once we hooked up as a team, there was no stopping us! Sure, there's been ups and downs in our career, I mean, when we went over to the FoPEW, we hit rock bottom. But we didn't break up, we kept on going, and we went back to where we made our fortune in the first place, the CWO! PIT BOSS #2: And it took a while for us to get warmed up, a North American Tag Team Championship here, some other victories there, but finally, we were able to dethrone the Creatures of the Night. Those guys held those belts for 6 months, they took on all contenders, and nobody could beat them. Nobody, that is, except the Bosses. PIT BOSS #1: So for all of you wannabee National Enquirer reporters out there, you can quit spreading your damn lies. We may fight a little, but when we're in the ring, we fight a lot! PIT BOSS #2: And you can take that to the bank! _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's all the time we have for the REPORT today, we'll see you tomorrow!
