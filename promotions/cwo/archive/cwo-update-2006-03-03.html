CWO Update — FEVER Review/Preview (3/4/06)

Mar 3, 2006, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Kyle Melinn and David "The Truth" Thomas are shown in the CWO Studios.) KYLE MELINN: Hello fans, Kyle Melinn here, along with David "The Truth" Thomas, and we are so glad to be with you on CWO UPDATE to provide your FEVER REVIEW/PREVIEW! DAVID THOMAS: That's right... last week we made our TRIUMPHANT return to announcing the "mutha ship" of the CWO... and with one day before the PAYBACK pay-per-view, there was definitely a lot to talk about! KYLE MELINN: That's right... a lot of things coming out of PAYBACK... which is still available on CWO Online... so check that out first if you haven't seen it... DAVID THOMAS: Don't worry... we'll wait. KYLE MELINN: All right, this week on FEVER, the annual MADNESS Tournament begins! There is a field of 32 wrestlers for this tournament, drawn from the current roster, past superstars, and hall of famers! The full brackets will be made available on CWO Online on Monday, but for now, here is the top half of the brackets, the "FEVER Region," if you will, and what 1st round matches we will see on FEVER 3/4/06! Nightrain vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles Surge vs. Xavier Falconi Doom vs. Gold Kard Ryan Justice vs. Lord Hile Troy BT Savage vs. DMT A1 Skyraider vs. Sandman Jr. Adam Smoke vs. Tyler Bomb Vampire Hunter vs. "Fallen Angel" Brad Wheatley (champion) KYLE MELINN: Truth, a lot of interesting first round matches there... care to make some predictions? DAVID THOMAS: Of course! First off, you have the Nightrain vs. Brent "The Ace" Alles match. Now, you may be wondering, since the winner of the MADNESS 2006 tournament gets a shot at the World Championship at CWO FOREVER, our biggest pay-per-view of the year, in April, why is Nightrain, the current World champion, in it? Well, the reason is simple... if NIGHTRAIN wins the tournament... not only does he get to pick his own opponent for FOREVER... but he gets $1,000,000 as well! If that's not incentive, I don't know what is! And for that reason, even though Brent is on a mission to "redeem" himself here in the CWO, I have to go with Nightrain... he's just got more momentum right now coming off his big win over long-time nemesis Vincent Frost at PAYBACK! KYLE MELINN: Of course, at PAYBACK, we saw that CWO President Chris Foley is not happy to have Brent Alles back, and he had First Strike and the Fighter Jets attack him after the International Tag Team Championship match... President Foley calls those four men "The Future"... and there may be more "Future" members to come... and many of them will see action in the tournament matches this week! Also, President Foley WILL be present on FEVER to explain his actions this past Sunday at PAYBACK! DAVID THOMAS: He's got SOMETHING against The Ace returning, that's for sure! Anyway, next up is the Surge vs. Xavier Falconi match... Surge is not wrestling regularly for the CWO right now... though his return IS rumored... but for that reason, I'm going to go with my boy the X- Man, Xavier Falconi, to advance in the tournament! KYLE MELINN: The winner of those first two matches will certainly lead to an interesting 2nd round match-up! But what about Doom vs. Gold Kard? DAVID THOMAS: Not much is known about this Doom guy... he's been announcing his arrival for a while now on CWO Online... but this Saturday we'll get to see him put his money where his mouth is... and he's going to have to do it against former CWO World Champion Gold Kard, who as you know, is always another guy I'll pick hands down! KYLE MELINN: OK, a lot of rumors swirling around about Gold Kard and just what his status in the CWO will be... but our next match-up pits newcomer Ryan Justice against hall-of-famer Lord Hile Troy... DAVID THOMAS: This is a tough one for me to pick, since Troy is a Hall of Famer, but he's not wrestling regularly these days... as painful as it is for me to say this, I think Ryan Justice is going to pull an upset here! KYLE MELINN: Bold pick... but how about BT Savage vs. DMT? DAVID THOMAS: Well, DMT's got solid momentum after defeating Adam Smoke this past Sunday at PAYBACK... and you know he wants to get that championship gold around his waist again... I would say BT Savage is in big trouble this Saturday, Melinn... DMT is my pick! KYLE MELINN: Next up is yet another match involving a Hall of Famer, this time with A1 Skyraider of "The Future" going up against Sandman Jr.! DAVID THOMAS: Sandman Jr. is always wild and unorthodox... but again, he's not competing regularly for the CWO right now, and A1 Skyraider apparently has new motivation from CWO President Chris Foley, so I'm going to say he'll possibly get the win in a tough one! KYLE MELINN: Beyond that, you've got "Future" members Adam Smoke and Tyler Bomb against each other in a 1st round tournament match! These two men were tag team partners and tag team CHAMPIONS before... what's going to happen this Saturday when they face each other in the tournament? DAVID THOMAS: Well, just when you thought that all the "Future" members would be on the same page again, now you've got two of them facing each other ALREADY in the tournament! Still, I think CWO President Chris Foley will make sure cooler heads prevail... but since Adam Smoke has had more singles match experience as of late... I'm going to go with him as a pick... but honestly, it's pretty much a toss-up! KYLE MELINN: And finally, you've got Vampire Hunter... also of "The Future"... going up against the new and current FEVER Champion, "The Fallen Angel" Brad Wheatley! DAVID THOMAS: We got our first look at "The Fallen Angel" last week, Melinn, as he shocked the world by winning the FEVER Championship! I definitely liked what I saw in this kid... and I think he's going to continue his winning ways this Saturday! Even though his FEVER Championship is not on the line... I think you're going to see him start making his drive towards being in the main event at CWO FOREVER! KYLE MELINN: Brad Wheatley was the #4 seed, being the FEVER Champion... the first four seeds were all given to the current singles champions... and then all of the rest of the seeds were randomly determined... except for the #32 seed, which President Chris Foley ASSIGNED to Brent "The Ace" Alles... no doubt we'll get some explanation of that when President Foley is LIVE in the ring this Saturday on FEVER! Fans, the annual MADNESS tournament always brings us highly contested, hard- fought matches, and this year will prove no exception! The road to CWO FOREVER begins this Saturday on FEVER! For David "The Truth" Thomas, I'm Kyle Melinn, saying we hope to see you there! EOT http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling2/cwo2k
