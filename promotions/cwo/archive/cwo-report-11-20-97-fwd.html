CWO REPORT 11-20-97 (fwd)

22 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens on Mitch E. Washey standing in a field somewhere in the Scottish Lowlands.) MITCH E. WASHEY: Welcome everybody to another edition of the CWO REPORT. The CWO European Tour is well underway, and it comes to a point this Sunday at CWO November Pain at Wembley Stadium! In case you missed it, CWO held a special FEVER last night in Glasgow, Scotland. The people were great, and we continue to see a very fanatic following of the CWO in the British Isles. Let's go to some quick results of last night's FEVER. CWO FEVER 11-19-97 Painmaster defeated the Prophet with the Ultimate Pain in 5:05. Interview with the "new" Joe Eagle. Eagle ran down to the ring, and a brawl with Painmaster took place. Bad Guys defeated the Harvesters of Sorrow in 3:16 after Rucker pinned Suffering with a powerslam. Interview with Lord Hile Troy. Interview with "The Brazillian Lover" Carlos Maia. "The Brazillian Lover" Carlos Maia defeated Tsutomu Kobayashi in 6:15 after performing a rana. Interview with the Highlanders. Highlanders defeated Holocaust and Red Raven in 8:04 after Gavin pinned Holocaust after the Quickening. Interview with the Dogs Of War. Southern Dynamite pinned the Focus in 9:41 after the Rebel Edge, retaining his CWO Light Heavyweight Championship. Interview with Fergus McTavish and Billy. The Mercenaries defeated the team of "Gigelo" Jimmy Heat and Steve White in 8:10 after White was pinned following the Mercenaries' Flying Clothesline. Interview with the Wild Ones and CPE. Masher Maccabee won by DQ over Jason "Flash" Varga after Fergus McTavish raced down to the ring and attacked Maccabee. Maccabee retained his CWO North American Championship. El Grande Pistolerro and Ranger Taurus won by DQ over Bloodbath and Sandman in 9:22 after interference by Gold Kard and Master Of Darkness Damion. Interview with Gold Kard and MOD Damion. Masher Maccabee was attacked by Fergus McTavish and Billy, who used the door from a moving dumptruck to knock Maccabee unconcious. Then they buried him in a load of manure. Interview with the UNIT. CWO World Tag Champs, the Newton Brothers defeated the Dogs Of War in 10:31 after Eddie Fantastic was pinned following a Newton Bomb onto a chair. Interview with the Dark Side. In a wild match, Johnny Reb, Sandman Jr., "Airbourne" Mark Fulcrum, & "Diamondback" Chris Myers got the win over the Dark Side. Reb and Jr. had several altercations during the bout. The last altercation resulted in Reb knocking Sandman Jr. to the mat. Reb attempted to make the cover on a fallen Night Slinger, but Sandman Jr. came off the top rope with the Permanent Dream legdrop on Reb, shoved Reb off of Night Slinger and made the pin himself, winning the match for his team in 20:47. After the match, a huge brawl took place with both teams, Reb and Sandman Jr., and Mason Dixon. WASHEY: It was an amazing card last night. Earlier today, a portion of the opening rounds took place for the Wayne Carpenter Memorial Tag Team Tournament. Let's look at what went down, or more appropriately, WHO went down. WAYNE CARPENTER MEMORIAL TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT - DAY 1 In a complete and total surprise, the Pit Bosses made their return to the ring, beating the Bad Guys by DQ in 16:35, advancing to Round 2. In what was termed an upset win, the Jade Dragons defeated the Steiner Brothers after Rick Steiner was pinned in 6:47 following the Dragon Strike. Foolkiller and "Ultimate Heel" Union Caine defeated the Nation Of Denomination in 11:16 after Jehovah's Witness was pinned following the Cainedriver. After the match, the Nation Of Denomination was brutally beaten by the winners. It is not known if they will be able to return to the ring. Kurt Getstung and Tommy Bomber got the win over "Gigelo" Jimmy Heat and Steve White after White was pinned in 3:46 after the Tommy Knocker. WASHEY: A ton of action has taken place over the last few days, and it's going to get even better in the next few days. Let's get to the interviews. First off, we have comments from Sandman Jr. that were taped prior to last night's FEVER. ______________________________________________________________ [Sandman Jr] I just remembered, a bunch of guys I can't stand have left the CWO... let's take a look at each individually! First, Rich Clark. Turned his back on me and the fans, and even Sanchez at one point. Just scum. Then there's Sanchez, who proved he was the jerk I always told you he was. Then, Moses, the guy I wrestled a BAZILLION times for the Light Heavyweight Title, and I could never beat him. Only AFTER he lost it did I beat him. Dammit. Finally, the biggest piece of sh*t of all, the Reverend. The smartest thing he ever did was run out of here. The dumbest was messing with me and Kellen. Rev, stay gone, cause if you come back, YOU'RE DEAD! That's all for now. I'll have more for you fans of the hard-core icon after my Fever bout. __________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Next up we have Brother Justice. Let's go to them now. __________________________________________________________ (Jerome and Jermain Justice are in their own gym in their hometown of Detroit, Michigan. They pause during their workout to give this interview.) Jermain: "Yo, Yo, Yo suckas, whas up? I guess you have noticed that you have not been seeing much of Brother Justice lately. Well I hope you have enjoyed your time off from gettin your heads busted. Well just so all you ladies know we have been doing some training for the upcoming Wayne Carpenter Memorial Tag Team Tournament. I know we are not going to be listed as the favorites for this tournament, and that's just the way we like it. Jerome: "That's right all of our lives we have been underdogs. Well have been training hard for this event and we plan on taking care of whoever gets in our way. It starts with you Master of Darkness Damion and Hellhound, and after we dispatch of our undeserving opponents we will go through whoever we have to on the way to the crown. We don't care if we have to go through the Bad Guys, The Steiners, Jade Dragons, Wild Ones, Hell you get the point. The point is we are in the best shapes of our lives and we have the ring experience we greatly needed. When the whole thing comes to pass and it's time for the winners to collect their rewards it will be Brother Justice standing tall." (Jerome and Jermain end their interview abruptly and continue with their workout.) ______________________________________________________ WASHEY: We now will hear comments from Mustafa Rahim and the Prophet. ______________________________________________________ (Mustafa Rahim and The Prophet stand side by side rubbing their right fist into the palms of their right hands. The Prophet begins to speak.) The Prophet: "Alas, I have seen many nights by day. I have seen victory become defeat, and defeat become eternal. My words have echoed through your minds, my image in your nightmares. I am not here in search of the golden idol, I am here to enforce my will. I have seen the other side, I have lived through death. Beware, the Prophet has spoken." Mustafa Rahim: "I'll tell you who else is spoken and that is the Empire. Yeah I understand that Foolkiller has been a world champion and I understand that Eddie Fantastic, Logan Fett, and Union Cane have been on a roll since their entrance. What I don't understand is how so many of the so called "STARS" of this orginization have let this transpire. Hell, we have a race between the babyfaces of this league and the Empire to see who is whipping someone's a$$ this week. Let it be know Empire you have the attention of the Law, and with that I have only one thing to say watch your backs, and THIS DAMN INTERVIEW IS OVER!" _______________________________________________________________ WASHEY: We have the Wild Cards up next. Let's go to them now. _______________________________________________________________ (The camera opens on the Riders on the Storm gym in Pittsburgh. The lights are all out, except for the lights in the ring, where Ace and Deck, the Wild Cards are shown standing) ACE: Deck, I think it's time. DECK: Another one? So soon? ACE: On the eve of this big moment, we should play a little word association, shouldn't we? DECK: I guess....ok, I'm game! ACE: Great! CWO....it's time for another installment of the Wild Cards unofficial tag team rankings! DECK: I'll start....Youth in Action? ACE: We're not talking about us! DECK: No, the new guys! ACE: I said, this ain't about us! DECK: I mean the two new dudes! Dynamite's friends? ACE: Oh.....uh.....not a major concern. DECK: The Bad Guys? ACE: Last I checked...still very bad. Very bad. DECK: The Wild Ones? ACE: Won't even make the semis. Mondo's too hurt. DECK: The 'revamped' Enforcers? ACE: Wesley McMichael. Watch him play football. He sucked. So will they. DECK: Brother Justice? ACE: What, they're still around? DECK: The Highlanders? ACE: They're the footsoldiers in Clan McCloud's war.....aka pawns. DECK: The Jade Dragons? ACE: If they ever get the team assembled, they MIGHT be good. DECK: The Four Aces? ACE: Oh, man...watch them while I was growing up....good guys, but can they go without Carpenter? DECK: The Mercenaries? ACE: Sickos who should be banned for what they did. And they enjoyed it... DECK: And last, the Deadly Sins? ACE: Guys, there is a reason you never one a title before. It's called lack of talent. And there's a reason you have a belt now. DECK: It's called luck. ACE: CWO...that Cup's as good as ours. (Both men flash the Riders sign as the camera fades out) _____________________________________________________________ WASHEY: Now we will hear some comments from Tsutomu Kobayashi concerning his match in the upcoming Tournament. _____________________________________________________________ [The camera opens on Tsutomu Kobayashi is walking in the highlands of Scotland. He is wearing a heavy coat, and his face betrays his discomfort at the chilling wind. He gazes over the hills for a moment, then turns to the camera...] Tsutomu Kobayashi: Scotland. A very cold and damp place compared to my homeland, but beautiful and serene in it's own way. Never in all my life did I ever expect my wrestling career to take me to this place. However, I go where the CWO sends me, and on November 18th, I face another man out of his element in this land, Carlos Maia. Carlos, I have studied tapes of your matches. I saw your match against Masashi "Lightning" Lanther up close, so I know how tough you are. Still, after my match with Gold Kard, a former world champion, I no longer feel intimidated by your talents. Respect them, yes. Fear them, no. I have proven myself to be more than capable of upsetting others when they least expect it, so don't make the mistake of underestimating me. It could cost you the match. Speaking of my countryman, Masashi and myself must face the Mercenaries on the 21st in the Carpenter tournament. If we do well, maybe Lanther-san will be my partner for the Frank A. Memorial Tag tournament that SJPW will hold in December. Regardless, I give the Mercenaries the same warning I give Maia: Do not underestimate me. I know how skilled the two of you are. Know that I too have skill. Skill enough to beat you. Lastly, Focus. I still await word from you concerning my challenge. Will you face me? I know you are a man of great skill, and this would be a challenge I would face willingly. I hope you will respond soon. [The camera fades out as Tsutomu turns back to towards the hills, staring out at them with awe on his face] _____________________________________________________ WASHEY: Finally today, we will hear from the Jade Dragons. _____________________________________________________ [The camera opens on the Jade Dragons walking through the streets of Glasgow..] Kenji Ishida: Highlanders, CWO has come to your homeland. How happy you must be. Know this though: The Jade Dragons hunt you Highlanders. You struck the first blow, and now it is time for vengeance. Here, in this cold, wet place, in front of your kinfolk, in front of your countrymen, we shall exact JUSTICE! [people stop and stare at the duo] Shinji Usami: Kenji! Calm down! Highlanders, what you did to Kenji and Kikuchi-san is unforgiveable. They offered you their hands in friendship, and you attack them? So now you face not one, but two promotions in war. Your declaration of war was against the CWO, not SJPW. However, your actions have condemned you. For now, we, representatives of SJPW, have declared war on you. You made a choice Highlanders, and now, forevermore, you must live with the consequences of that choice. Kennji Ishida: Highlanders, win or lose, the Jade Dragons WILL confront you after your match with Red Raven & Holocaust. Unlike you two, brigands & theives that you and all your clan are, we will not strike like an assassin in the night. We will confront you face to face, like TRUE WARRIORS! We are not skulking cowards and backstabbers like you two are! [people stare once more, and some talk amongst themselves in whispers, pointing at the Dragons] Shinji Usami: Highlanders, you are not ready. [The camera fades as the Jade Dragons continue to walk through the streets, people staring at them as they walk] ________________________________________________________ WASHEY: That's all the time we have for today. We'll see everybody tomorrow on the REPORT! (fade to closing credits.)
