CWO REPORT 9/27/97

27 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CWO REPORT opening comes up as we go to Johnny Starr in the control center.) STARR: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the CWO REPORT for Saturday, September 27, 1997. I'm Johnny Starr. The CWO has been kickin' up some dust this week in the great Lone Star State, with three great house shows before the big event in San Antonio this Sunday, SEPTEMBER SLAM II, the Alamo Dome is completely sold out, but you can still see it LIVE on PPV. We'll get to September Slam II later, but for now let's look at what happened at the house shows. _______________________________________________________________________________ HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 9/24/97 Ft. Worth, TX Bloodbath and Sandman Jr. wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. El Grande Pistolerro pinned 'The Brazilian Lover' Carlos Maia after the Springboard Frankensteiner. Time of match: 10:53. Diamondback Chris Myers pinned Wrath after the Rattler (sideways DDT). Time of match: 11:09. Gold Kard pinned Holocaust after the Die Flieder Kross. Time of match: 9:47. CWO LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION TITLE MATCH: Wildfire and Jason 'Flash' Varga wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. Jason 'Flash' Varga retained the CWO Light Heavyweight Champion title. CPE and Deadly Sins wrestled to a 30 minute time limit draw. North American Champion Masher Maccabee pinned Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo after the Homentaschen Hangover in a non-title match. Time of match: 16:06. World Tag Team Champions the Bad Guys defeated North American Champions The Big Cool in a "Champions' Challenge" match when Ron 'The Rude Guy' Johnson pinned Jim 'Jazzman' Taylor after the flying double stomp. Time of match: 20:46. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams defeated World Champion Johnny Reb in a 2 out of 3 falls match, 2 falls to 1. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams won fall 1 in 8:27 after the Keuka Smack (boxing of the ears followed by an uppercut). Johnny Reb won fall 2 in 10:56 after Brian 'The Hammer' Williams submitted to the Texas Cloverleaf. Brian 'The Hammer' Williams won fall 3 in 14:23 by disqualification when Johnny Reb refused to let go of the Texas Cloverleaf when Williams was in the ropes. Joe Eagle made the save, commencing in a brawl between Reb and Eagle back to the dressing room. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 9/25/97 Houston, TX Skyhawk pinned Vegas High Roller after the Skydive (backflip splash). Time of match: 13:17. Skyhawk was attacked by the Cabin after the match. The Unit made the save. Mondo Gecko pinned Wrath after the cannonball. Time of match: 11:56. Newton Bros. defeated Nation of Denomination when Howard Newton pinned Jehovah's Witness after the Powerline. Time of match: 25:54. 'Lone Wolf' Ryan Deutscher pinned Television Champion The Focus in a non-title match after the reversed backslide. Benjamin Blake Bentley aided in Deutscher's reversal by spraying The Focus in the eyes with something, which the referee was out of position to see. Time of match: 10:17. Red Raven/Holocaust defeated Pyro-Technics when Holocaust made C-4 submit to the Ashes to Ashes. Time of match: 22:39. After the match Red Raven and Holocaust continued to pound on the Pyro-Technics, and both were carried out on stretchers. Sandman Jr. and North American Champion Masher Maccabee wrestled to a double disqualification. Time of match: 12:18. Deadly Sins defeated Brother Justice when Sloth pinned Jerome Justice after the Repention. Time of match: 13:06. Jason 'Flash' Varga pinned Johnny Reb after the small package in a "Champions' Challenge" match. Time of match: 18:33. After the match Johnny Reb blindsided Varga and put him in the Texas Cloverleaf, again prompting Joe Eagle to make the save, causing another arena-wide brawl, this time with The Big Cool and Mason-Dixon and Rebel Yell joining in. HOUSE SHOW RESULTS 9/26/97 Austin, TX Fergus 'Whirling Scottish Devil' McTavish defeated Mustafa Rahim by disqualification after Brother Justice attacked following the Highland Fling (top-rope piledriver). Time of match: 1:11. Mondo Gecko and El Grande Pistolerro made the save and a six-man brawled commenced between McTavish and the CPE against the Law, with security finally having to break it up. Holocaust made Painmaster submit to the Ashes to Ashes. Time of match: 13:10. Sandman Jr. pinned Kurt Gerstung after the Permanent Dream flying legdrop. Time of match: 10:28. Red Raven pinned Gold Kard after the Raven's Flame, pulling the tights. Time of match: 5:23. Lord Hile Troy pinned Brent 'The Ace' Alles after the reversed inside cradle, pulling the tights. Time of match: 12:06. Steve White/'Gigolo' Jimmy Heat defeated CPE when Steve White pinned Ranger Taurus after the backslide, pulling the tights. Time of match: 19:45. Wayne 'College Guy' Schillo and Vegas High Roller wrestled to a 20 minute time limit draw. TEXAS DEATH MATCH: Attitude Dance (Joe Eagle and Jason 'Flash' Varga) defeated Mason-Dixon in a Texas Death Match when Stonewall Mason failed to meet a ten count after being pinned by Jason 'Flash' Varga. Time of match: 56:11. World Champion Johnny Reb made Benjamin Blake Bentley submit to the Texas Cloverleaf in a non-title match. Time of match: 7:19. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you can see, some hot action in venues all across the great state of Texas, today the superstars have off, all in preparation for tomorrow night's big event, SEPTEMBER SLAM II: REVENGE IS THE NAME OF THE GAME. Let's provide you the lineup for the card, which you can also find on the CWO homepage, at: http://web.syr.edu/~bgalles/cwo.html _______________________________________________________________________________ SEPTEMBER SLAM II: REVENGE IS THE NAME OF THE GAME "LIVE" FROM SAN ANTONIO, TX 9/28/97 6:30 STARTING TIME CARD SUBJECT TO CHANGE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Joe Eagle (w/Russell Hill) vs. Johnny Reb (champion, w/General Lee and Rebel Yell) WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP (TRIANGLE MATCH): Mercenaries vs. Highlanders vs. Bad Guys (champions, w/Herbie Versmells) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: "Monster" Rich Clark vs. Masher Maccabee (champion) NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Bloodbath and Wildfire vs. The Big Cool (champions, w/Russell Hill) TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP (loser leaves CWO): Benjamin Blake Bentley vs. The Focus (champion) LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (no DQ, there must be a winner): Moses vs. Jason "Flash" Varga (champion, w/Jim Peer) THUNDERDOME (all 8 men in a cage, weapons allowed): The Law (Mustafa Rahim, ???, Brother Justice) vs. CPE (Mondo Gecko, El Grande Pistolerro, CPE Enforcers) RESPECT MATCH: Brent "The Ace" Alles (w/Gordy Alles) vs. Lord Hile Troy 6-MAN ACTION: "Gigolo" Jimmy Heat and the Coldfront (w/Tommy Bomber) vs. Deadly Sins Wrath, Pride, and Sloth (w/Lust) BOUNTY MATCH: Vegas High Roller (w/Joe Root) vs. Wayne "College Guy" Schillo (If Schillo wins, he gets 5 minutes in the ring with Joe Root.) 8-MAN SUBMIT OR SURRENDER MATCH: The Unit (Sean Holiday, Skyhawk, Newton Bros., w/Toni Newton) vs. The Cabin (Exodus, Judgment, Ghost Riders, w/Lord Guru) NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP #1 CONTENDER MATCH: Painmaster vs. Fergus McTavish, the Whirling Scottish Devil TEXAS TWISTER MATCH: Reverend and Sandman vs. Diamondback Chris Myers and Sandman Jr. (All men fighting at the same time, falls count anywhere in the building.) Gold Kard vs. Holocaust X-Factor (w/Marc Mission) vs. Southern Dynamite "The Brazilian Lover" Carlos Maia vs. "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher EYE ON THE STORM: Miguel Sanchez vs. Red Raven Ganglord vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams DARK MATCH: Nation of Denomination vs. Australian Assassin and Vegas Villain _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: We will provide you with a full run-down on what has gone on in the CWO leading up to this PPV during the EYE ON THE STORM. With no hyperbole I can tell you folks, that this one may be one of the most intense CWO PPV's of all time, just because of all the tension that seems to be present in most of the matches. Before the PPV, though, is a great SUNDAY SHOWCASE, let's take a look at the lineup for that show! _______________________________________________________________________________ SUNDAY SHOWCASE 9/28/97 HOUR #1: Gold Kard vs. Vengeance SJPW Action "Lone Wolf" Ryan Deutscher vs. Lord Hile Troy HOUR #2: Painmaster vs. Brian "The Hammer" Williams SJPW Action Mercenaries vs. Joe Eagle and Jason "Flash" Varga _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: As you saw in the line-up for September Slam II, there has been a slight change due to the serious injuries incurred to the Pyro-Technics at the hands of Red Raven and Holocaust. Southern Dynamite will now be taking on X-Factor (along with Marc Mission) in a singles match, and the Nation of Denomination will be taking on Australian Assassin and Vegas Villain in the dark match. Speaking of Red Raven and Holocaust, let's hear from them now. _______________________________________________________________________________ (Red Raven and Holocaust are in the interview area.) HOLOCAUST: We have been as active lately in the league as we had planned to be. We had a few private affairs to take care of. A pity we had to dispose of those losers Pyro-Technics... so disappointing to have yet another failure. I guess one could say that they went up in smoke. RED RAVEN: Yeah, uh huh. HOLOCAUST: Now that we have refocused our efforts to dominate this league, our presence will soon be felt. RED RAVEN: Gonna make you all feel it, uh huh. HOLOCAUST: We plan on heating up and bringing ourselves to our appropriate places in the rankings. On top. RED RAVEN: Who's the man? Uh huh. HOLOCAUST: So anyone out there looking to grab a quick defeat can feel free to step in our way to the top. RED RAVEN: I told y'all we won't stop. Can't hold us down. Uh huh. HOLOCAUST: Rather than wasting our time with petty feuds, we're looking to take on any challengers who are willing to wrestle against us. Look out league. We're about to set this place on fire. RED RAVEN: Fire, uh huh. HOLOCAUST: And Gold Kard... (Holocaust laughs menacingly) at September Slam II, I will remove this mask, and you will know my secret identity, but I don't think you'll be able to withstand it. RED RAVEN: Won't be able to withstand the fire! (Flames shoot from Red Raven's hands as both men laugh.) _______________________________________________________________________________ (Back to Johnny.) STARR: That's it for today, we'll see you tomorrow on SHOWCASE and SEPTEMBER SLAM II!
