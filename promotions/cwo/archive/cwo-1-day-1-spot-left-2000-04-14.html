The CWO World Championship Tournament brackets are almost filled, but there is still one slot left! If you'd like to not only be involved in the "relaunch" of the CWO, but also get a chance at our World title right off the bat, fill out the below Wrestler Submission sheet and get it back to me. First come, first serve on that slot (and the deadline is midnight EDT Saturday), all other submissions received (up to our maximum handler limit of 24) will be placed into an upcoming battle royal for the CWO Television title. Also, we need one more tag team for our upcoming World Tag Team Championship tournament, so if you're interested in submitting a tag team instead of a singles wrestler, feel free. Just send in two sheets. Any questions, feel free to contact me at [detail withheld] . Thanks!
