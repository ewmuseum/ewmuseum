[detail withheld] ! Ganglord pinned Brian McTavish after the Gangbusta (tiger driver). Time of match: 5:52. ARKANGEL HOUSE SHOW PROMO by [name withheld] (+1 Heat for Arkangel) The lights begin to flicker on and off and finally fade to complete darkness. DEEP AND SERIOUS VOICE: The nightmare has arrived. There is no escape" "Rapture" by Dragonlord begins to play. Arkangel comes down to the ring, wearing his spiked armor and carrying his golden spike. He descends down the runway as the lights begin to flicker on and off again. He gets in the ring and steps up onto the top turnbuckle, holding his spike in the air. Arkangel grabs a microphone and sits in the corner. He begins to speak.
ARKANGEL: As you lay there in the darkness thinking about things to come, what comes to mind? Do you think pleasant thoughts, or only evil intentions?
You see Wildfire, over the span of the next few days you better be asking yourself these questions. But you see there is no answer, but there is a problem. You see Wildfire, you are the problem. And to clear up this problem, the only solution is to terminate it. Wildfire, tonight you and I will meet in a match. You may think it will just be a normal match, but that is where you are wrong. Like so many who have dared cross my path you will be destroyed just like them. You can't comprehend the evil intentions that race through my head day in and day out. The darkness of my soul has consumed my entire body, and after the match you will have no choice but to surrender to the dark side. This may not be a title match, and you may have nothing to lose.....but that is where you are wrong. You see, when you face me, your life is on the line. You can run, but you can't hide. Doomsday has arrived and it is finally time for evil to triumph over good. The saga is over, because darkness will reign supreme. No one is safe, not even you.
Wildfire, you will know what it is like to face a fire that is out of control. I am a raging inferno and I will engulf anything in my path including you. You are playing with fire, not only will you lose, but you will be burned. If you struggle, you will be destroyed. So, my advice is to take your loss like a man, or not to show your face at all. You can't stop an inferno, you can only hope to contain it.
Confrontation with me is inevitable. Fight me if you dare, but you won't survive because I won't let you!
Arkangel laughs menacingly and drops his mike. He steps up and raises his spike, as the lights begin to flicker.
VOICE: ANGEL. OF. DEATH. Arkangel defeated Wildfire (television champion) by disqualification when
Wildfire hit him with a steel chair.
Time of match: 7:43. Dave Lupus pinned Gold Kard after the Tower of London (delayed brainbuster). Time of match: 7:41. Da Hood Rats (Big Balla and Lil G) defeated Pit Bosses (Mike Masario and
Joey Davola) when Big Balla pinned Joey Davola after the Balla Bomb (reverse big splash from the top). Time of match: 6:30.
'The Avenger' Jesse Ewiak pinned Sandman Jr. after the Ewiakbomb v3.0 (chokeslam into a powerbomb). Time of match: 6:56. CWO TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP(non-title): EWA (Dennis Menton and Omar Martinez) defeated Beauregard Boys (Ron and Shirley) when Omar pinned Shirley after the Martinez Missile (flying dropkick). Time of match: 12:18. Major Slayer pinned El Suicida after the Slayer Slam (chokeslam from the top turnbuckle). Time of match: 12:51.
CPE (CWO Champion Mondo Gecko, Col. Taggart, and Blackjack LeBeau) defeated
Samarian and the Darknight Express (Demolition and Dimento) when LeBeau pinned Demolition after the Texas Stampede (running powerbomb). Time of match: 18:30. After the match, Samarian berated both of the DKE for the loss, leading them to attack the big man and send him out of the ring to send the crowd home cheering!
