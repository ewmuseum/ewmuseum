Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

UCWF was established on 21 March 2001, ran a weekly show called Fury and a pay-per-view a few times a year, and decided its matches in a way it set out plainly in its own rules: every match is a best-roleplayer-wins match, graded sixty per cent on quality and forty on quantity. What actually happened in the ring was then produced by a wrestling simulator, ZEUS PRO 4.0.5, with the results writer adding his own spin to parts of it. Every show script it left signs itself, at the foot, “Generated by Zeus Pro”.

The six that survive run from a Fury in September 2002 through Breakout, Immortality, Wrestle Insanity, Resurrection and a last Fury from Green Bay on 20 June 2003. They are long, and they are strange in the way simulated matches are: move after named move, a referee called Gangsta Mac counting, and then a live band opening the show — Project 86 performing Resurrection’s own theme song, Linkin Park playing Wrestle Insanity. One street fight is fought outside the building and through the window of a police station.

The roster page is the other thing it kept: nineteen wrestlers with a height, a weight, a hometown, an alignment, a theme song and a finisher apiece, from Beverly Hills and Little Italy and one address given as The Compton Crackhouse. Three stables sit above them. How it ended isn’t in the surviving record — the last thing the federation wrote, on 7 August 2003, apologises for a server outage that had lost some of it and promises to get back as much as possible over the next few days.

Storylines

The arcs below are recovered from a full read of all six of the fed's own surviving show scripts.

Mumrat, Coley Walsh, and the birth of Unforeseen Vengeance Written by AI, 2026

At Fury 2002-09-08, owner Mumrat cuts a ring promo calling out Coley Walsh for going "AWOL" from his own stable; Walsh runs in and the two brawl until security separates them (fury-2002-09-08). Walsh resurfaces mid-match at Immortality 2002, in the middle of the show's own Extreme/World Hell in a Cell, spearing Mumrat and slamming him through the cage roof into a stack of tables. In the same chaos a masked man teams with a returning The Fox against Scorpion, then unmasks as Solidus, billed "the man that Brax has trained," and the four of them declare a new stable, Unforeseen Vengeance, formed to "destroy all on-comers" (immortality-2002). The stable is never named again in the surviving record — UCWF's own June 2003 stable roster lists ANTItrust, American Bad Boyz and Money Train, and nothing else.

The Johnson brothers Written by AI, 2026

Johnny Johnson steals the Intercontinental title off Scorpion with a kendo stick at Fury 2002-09-08, promising to make his "national debut" and to "shatter" his own brother's "greatest IC Champ ever" legacy — the surviving record's first mention of Sean Johnson (fury-2002-09-08). Sean himself first appears only as a tease, silently pointing at Johnny from the entrance at Resurrection moments after Johnny loses the World title to a quick count (resurrection-2003), then as a taunting apparition at the end of Wrestle Insanity's own World title match, which Johnny loses when a fabricated news report naming him alongside a dying convicted murderer costs him the distraction that lets Bobby Linken steal the pin (wrestle-insanity-2003). The brothers finally meet in the ring at Breakout, in a Barbwire Beat Down for Johnny's own Extreme title against Bryon Wade Osbourne that instead turns into Sean and Osbourne teaming up to throw a third man, Flashing Thunder, off the stage before the two of them fight each other to a draw (breakout-2003). The rivalry closes at the last surviving Fury: Sean beats Johnny in a Loser Leaves UCWF match with the Texas Cloverleaf, writing his own brother out of the fed (fury-2003-06-20).

The Notorious Don Joe Giovanni and Ace Of Spades Written by AI, 2026

The Notorious Don Joe Giovanni wins the World Championship off Johnny Johnson at Resurrection on a quick count by special guest referee Mumrat (resurrection-2003). Billed "Legend already made" against Ace Of Spades's own "Legend in the making," Giovanni survives a 60-minute Hell In A Cell Ironman defense at the last surviving Fury, three falls to two, time expiring with Ace collapsed from blood loss as Giovanni lines up a barbwire bat swing he never throws (fury-2003-06-20). Weeks later, billed "Part 1: A Battle For Respect II," the two meet again at Breakout inside a dome-type cage reachable only through a roof hatch at the top of a 20-foot ladder. Both men fall from the ladder once already; on the second climb Ace kicks Giovanni off it entirely and pulls himself through the hatch alone to win the title (breakout-2003).
