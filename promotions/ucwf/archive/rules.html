UCWF Rules

June 2003

UCWF Rules ~~SERIOUS APPLICANTS ONLY PLEASE~~ 1)No Slacking. By Slacking, I mean not roleplaying for a match or not even checking weekly to see if you even have a match. 2)All Matches are BEST ROLEPLAYER WINS matches. To win, you must beat your opponent in size, quality, and originality. Roleplays grading scale is 60% Quality, 40% Quanity Just because you have a long roleplay DOES NOT garuntee the win! 3)We have 1 weekly event, that being UCWF Fury. If you are not able to roleplay one week, simply email me at [detail withheld] or get in touch with me on AIM at [detail withheld] and tell me you won't be able to compete in the event that week. 4)Missing RP's without notice will greatly affect your status in the federation. The tolerance policy in the UCWF is 2 missed weeks and you're fired. 5)Using other people in your roleplays is fine, just be sure they do not get seriously injured in it. For example, you can hit someone over the head with a chair in your roleplay, but you can't break their leg with a baseball bat. If you do attack your opponent, you must have them give up a struggle and you may not just kick there ass. If you are going to use someones character, you must use them in the way they would act. That means that they must be the character that they usually play. If you are unsure if your roleplay does that, please contact UCWF officials about it before posting so we can clear up the situation together. If you do post one that goes against a persons character, your RP may be pulled off the RP board and may not count twords you match. Also, don't make using another character a habbit. It will get old fast and it will decrease the quality of your roleplay if you do it too many times.(I have it in big text because it's something I warn you not to forget) 6)The process of how I do my results is a simple one. I use a Simulator called ZEUS PRO 4.0.5. to make my results then add my own spin onto alot of the parts of the results. If you have a problem with this, there isn't too much I can do about that. It's just an easier way to keep the fed organized. Note: We may at times experiment with writen results at times. 7)We do have monthly Pay-Per-Views in which We like to build up the matches so it makes it interesting when I release the results. Therefore, give alot of effort into Pay Per View's because they are what really give you a big name. When it is the week of a Pay-Per-View, there will be no Fury due to the fact of that would be cutting things to close for roleplaying. 8)There is to be NO Killing of anything or anyone in your Roleplays. First time you do it, you are suspended for 2 Cards and stripped of all titles. 2nd time, you are fired no questions asked. Also, please DO NOT have anyone get injured in your roleplays without approvel of UCWF owners and that will only be if it is your own character. That is also against the rules. 9)No Denying (By denying, that means don't go and change something someone did to you. If it is not a lawful thing to do by UCWF rules, contract an owner) Otherwise, merely go with the flow. 10) This rule regards the OCC board. If you have a complaint, dont attack someone on the RP board, simply bring up the complaint there addressing it to UCWF officials and we will take care of thing. DONT TRY TO TAKE CARE OF THEM YOURSELF!!! Thats what we are here for. Another thing about this board, its definately good to compliment people on RPs and idea here. We encourage it. Also if you want to find someone to become allies for, try it here. These are the basics of the UCWF set up to make this game fun. Now that you know the rules, go fill out an App. If you have decided to join and get ready for the ride of a lifetime with one of the internets leading efederations, the UCWF (Established March 21, 2001)
