IWF (Dec 1996) — Studio update broadcast

IWF Update 2/13

13 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Montage of highlights of the IWF superstars runs as the IWF logo fades in.) *** IWF UPDATE *** (Cut to Juan Hunglowe.) JUAN HUNGLOWE: Hello I'm Juan Hunglowe and this is IWF Update for Thursday, February 13, 1997. Six days until the War of the Alliances begins in Atlanta. This coming Wednesday at the Omni in downtown
Hotlanta, not 1, not 2, but 3, yes 3 federation championships will be up for grabs. We'll get into the War of the Alliances a little later. First let's recap what happened last night at Wednesday Night Fights from the
Manhattan Center on the City College of New York campus.
Sparhawk and 'Wrecking Machine' Dan Parks wrestled to a double countout at 15:42 Heavy Artillery defeated Maximum and Wildman at 11:19 when Wildman was disqualified for hitting Grenade with the timekeeper's hammer.
Panther defeated Terminator when Terminator did not appear for the match. The lumberjack match for the IWF US title between US Champion 'All American' Atlas and Flawed to Perfection was ruled a no contest at 14:28 when members of Power Team USA, the Golden Alliance, and the Empire of
Power began fighting on the outside.
IWF Universal Heavyweight Champion Ricky 'Top Gun' Santana successfully defended his belt by pinning I-Roc Titan in 27:24 after Concrete hit I-Roc with a chair.
JH: Let's see what the IWF has in store for the fans tonight. 2/13- IWF House show, USAir Arena, Washington, DC Amish Warrior vs. Cableman Street Warriors vs. Barbarian Warriors Sonar vs. Alan 'Sideswiper' Kalord The Big Cool vs. Widowmaker IWF Television Title Match: Butterball (champion) vs. Darlock IWF US Tag Team Title Match (non-title) Death & Destruction (champions) vs. Flying Thunder JH: Let's get comments from the Amish Warrior regarding his match tonight with the Cableman.
(Cut to Amish Warrior in interview area.) AMISH WARRIOR: Yettle you hit me with one of your objects, I know them well. Perhaps there is some justice in my loss at the hands of your
Cableman. Make sure you know this, I may have followed your tactics in the past Yettle but from now on the Amish Warrior will find his own way in the IWF, a path that shall be followed by all the loyal fans. And yes one day that path will lead to you and Cableman and I shall exact my revenge!
(Cut back to Hunglowe.) JH: What a great card you folks in Washington DC have tonight. Tickets are still available at the box office. And don't forget, Wednesday,
February 19th. The Omni, Atlanta. War of the Alliances. A 3 round battle between the alliances. First two members of each alliance will be picked for a tag team match. The winners of the matches will then advance to the second round, and participate in a mini-battle royale. The last 2 men left standing in the ring will win the battle royale. If the 2 men are from the same alliance, that alliance will be declared the winner.
However, if the two men are from different alliances, they will advance to the third round, a singles match against each other. The winner of the singles match will have their alliance declared the winner of the War of the Alliances. Here's the first round matchups
Power Team USA vs. Dad's World Order Thunderdome Warriors vs. Golden Alliance Win Inc. vs. Empire of Power And that's not all, we also have many title and inter-federation matches. WWA vs. IWF Earth's Last Hope vs. Amish Warrior WWA vs. IWF 'Nature Boy' Bobby Elliot vs. I-Roc Titan IWF TV Title match Butterball (champion) vs. Sao IWF TV Tag Team Title match Assault & Battery (champions) vs. Apocalypse IJWF Title Match The Great Muta (champion) vs. Sonar IWF/WWA Winner Take All match Ricky 'Top Gun' Santana (IWF Universal Heavyweight Champion) vs. Ty 'The Trashman' (WWA World Heavyweight Champion) JH: Let's go to comment from the 'Nature Boy' on his inter-fed match with I-Roc Titan. (Cut to Bobby Elliot in his home.) NATURE BOY: Wooooo! Wooooo! Mr. I-Roc, Woooooo! Titan! Come Feurary 19th,
I'm gonna style and profile all the way to the victory baby. Wooooo! So when you come down that aisle to step into the squared circle with the real deal, The Real World's Champion, The Nature Boy don't be afraid of those butterflies rumbling in your tummy. Wooooo! I don't think you know what you're getting into when you sign a match with this limosine riding, jet flying, whelling dealing, kiss stealing, son of a gun. Wooooooo!
Wooooo! Wooooo! (The Nature Boy struts and lets out several more Woooooos) (Cut back to Hunglowe.) JH: That's all the time we have today. See you tomorrow with the next IWF
Update!
