IWF (personal-homepage, 1996)

"IWF Title change"

3 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last night, Sunday November 3rd, The Russian Nightmares defended there IWF TV
Tag-Team title against Death & Destruction (better known as The Franchise and
The Regal). Both teams put on a awesome show of what wrestling was all about. In the end though, the scientific skill of Death & Destruction prevailed. With Boris lying semi-unconscious on the mats, The Franchise kept
Nikolia busy while The Regal dropped a flying leg drop over the prone body of
Boris. Franchise came back to make the pin, while Regal charged Nikolia, giving his partner just enough time to get the 3 count.
If you are thinking of trying a new federation, this one is still taking on new wrestlers (especially tag-teams). In about 2 weeks time we will be setting up a major brawl. Hope to see you soon
-John Dough (President of IWF Wrestling)
