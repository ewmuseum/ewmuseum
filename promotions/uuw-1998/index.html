Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

UUW's own earliest surviving activity is an October 1998 recruitment drive built around interview promos rather than match scripts, run on a show it called Talk Is Cheap. Its own founder, Nick “The Bulldogg” Demola, opened the 21 October 1998 edition by disowning an estranged on-air half-brother, “Nightshade” Jeremy Locke, who had promised to make him “deal with me” whether Demola wanted him around or not.

That same broadcast recruited “Iceman” Jeff Westfall, self-billed as the reigning IWA World Heavyweight Champion, who arrived promising to win UUW's own tournament for its World Heavyweight Title on top of the belt he already held, alongside Mark Marleau, Mariafro, the Polish Crippler, and a masked figure calling himself the Dehumanizer. By its 6 January 1999 talent search the promotion was naming Jack Vars, Nick Demola himself, Ricky Deathwish, and Levi Ash among wrestlers who had ranked as high as 28th and 47th on a newsgroup top-200 list.

Demola stepped back from his own promotion on 27 February 1999, in a post he called “a packing post”: “for my precious UUW…the fed that I created…I must say goodbye. I'm leaving it in good hands and I am content with that.”

No later document survives from the promotion itself; a rival federation's own recruitment ad, five months later, refers to UUW in the past tense as already closed.
