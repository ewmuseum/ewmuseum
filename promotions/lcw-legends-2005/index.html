Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Legends Championship Wrestling, LCW, billed itself as “The premier online wrestling organization” and “It's Not Fake...It's Fantasy!”: a fantasy fed that booked wrestlers of the 1970s, 1980s and 1990s, with Nick Bockwinkel as its general manager. Its front page of January 2009 was a blog. It posted LCW Showdown television results every Wednesday, pay-per-view results, breaking news from the wrestling business and a countdown of the Top 250 Pro Wrestlers of the 70s, 80s, and 90s, which stood at number 33 on 12 January 2009. Its menu named twelve pay-per-views, Ascension, Descent, Breaking Point, Torment, Uprising, SuperClash, Beach Brawl, Caged Heat, Survival Project, Night of Horrors, Redemption Day and Endgame, and a collection of its events from SuperClash 2005 to SuperClash 2008.

The show posted on 31 December 2008 reported what happened at the Endgame pay-per-view of 28 December: Magnum TA kept the LCW Universal Championship in a fatal fourway against Dr. Death Steve Williams, Manny Fernandez and Nikita Koloff; Ric Flair beat The Rock in an Ultimate Endgame match in which the loser had to leave the fed, helped by interference from Paul Roma; and Paul Orndorff became the International Champion. Bockwinkel then announced a twenty-man battle royal for the first contendership of 2009, which Macho Man Randy Savage won by last eliminating Nikita Koloff, and the show of 7 January billed the two against each other in its main event. That week Paul Roma was given one last chance to earn a place among the Horsemen, against Flair. The cards named LCW champions beside them: Brutus Beefcake (Television), Ken Patera (United States), Superstar Billy Graham (Heritage), the Midnight Express and then the Nasty Boys (World Tag Team), and the Bolsheviks and then the Hart Foundation (United States Tag Team). On 31 December the page carried breaking news of WWE's release of Barry Windham, with the fed's hope of bringing him back, and on 9 January news of a wider round of WWE releases and his return on that week's Showdown.
