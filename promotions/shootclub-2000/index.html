Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Shootclub Wrestling Alliance was the umbrella of shootclub.com, a web-based wrestling game that opened in the summer of 2000. Its players signed wrestlers into one of ten separate territories, among them Empire State Wrestling, Golden State Wrestling and a league of its own in Memphis, each with its own promoter character and championships, and the alliance drew them together for shared pay-per-views.

By August 2001 the site said it had been thriving for over a year, and its fifth pay-per-view, Crowning Glory, live from the Mid-South Coliseum in Memphis, was built to answer the question its opening video posed: who is your world champion? Nine finalists from the territories entered a tournament for the title, and each promoter brought a gold plate bearing his league's logo to be set into the sides of the new belt.

The game's own software did much of the booking. Its monthly King of the Mountain was a gauntlet whose order the program drew at random, its webmaster explaining that he had deliberately ignored friendships and tag partnerships when writing the code; its rankings of the top wrestlers across every territory were published by points.

Its newsgroup posts were samplers, each ending with a link to the full report on the site and an invitation to join its training program, Shootcamp. How long the alliance ran beyond October 2001 is not in the surviving record.
