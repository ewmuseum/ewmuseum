Shootclub Crowning Glory

26 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://shootclub.com The Shootclub Wrestling Alliance, an organization uniting the 10 separate Shootclub territories, put on its fifth pay-per-view event on Sunday, August 26th. The show named the FIRST-EVER Shootclub WORLD heavyweight champion. Please take a look at this sampling and if you like what you see, click the link and visit the site! Once there, you can join immediately and be placed into our training program, SHOOTCAMP. Thanks for reading, and we hope you'll enjoy CROWNING GLORY... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- An animated Shootclub logo appears on the screen, and soon, faint impressions of images from Shootclub's past appear as a voice is heard... VOICE OF JAKE COLOSSAL: "For over ONE YEAR, Shootclub has been THRIVING... GROWING... EXCELLING. The empire expanded. The talent flourished. The world watched. The bar was raised. With success... with expansion... the inevitable question was posed." *WHO* IS YOUR WORLD CHAMPION? Quick flashes are shown of the nine finalists... "HOTHEAD" MAT HELLION (ESW) KILLJOY (GAW) "JUST" STEVE BLUDER (LSW) DAEMON "THE DESTROYER" (NSW) SCOTT MAVERICK (PCW) SETH ANARCHY (CPW) CANAAN (HCW) GATOR (GSW) "THA ANSWER" JEREMIAH STEWART (MSW) COLOSSAL: "After tonight, THE QUESTIONS STOP. SHOOTCLUB will have its man. And the WORLD... will have its CHAMPION." THE SWA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- A SHOOTCLUB INTERNATIONAL and COLOSSAL PROMOTIONS production LIVE FROM THE MID-SOUTH COLISEUM IN MEMPHIS, TN!!! The SHOOTCLUB WRESTLING ALLIANCE's World Heavyweight championship event... SWA CROWNING GLORY!!! LIVE ON PAY-PER-VIEW! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Purple and gold fireworks fill the rooftop with brilliant light as we kick off the Shootclub Wrestling Alliance's most eagerly anticipated pay-per-view offering EVER! The crowd is on their feet and, once the loud explosions die down, the air is filled with their enthusiastic cheers! As we scan the arena, the fans show off their great signs, such as... "John:90210" "Maverick's the man!" "Vote on FWLNet!" "Sporks Rule!" "Give me Anarchy or give me death!" "KILL THE SERVER" The camera swings around to the ring, where two familiar faces are seen, with microphones in their hands... JOHN RODEO: "Welcome! Welcome to MY city, the city that I put on the map, Memphis, 90210! And welcome, friends and lovers, to SWA's Crowning Glory - where tonight, I promise you, we will crown - for the first time ever - the SWA World Heavyweight champion! SOMEONE is strapping on that gold TONIGHT! It's SWA's Crowning Glory, where than can be only one. Alongside for the ride here is my colleague, returning for a repeat appearance thanks to the good word I put in with JC... he is the 'News Man' KENT NEUMAN! Kent, take it away." KENT NEUMAN: "You got it, John! What a night for the Shootclub Wrestling Alliance! Tonight, here in Memphis, Tennessee..." [The self proclaimed Multimedia Maven Rodeo cuts Kent off...] RODEO: "Kent, perhaps you need a miracle ear. Or maybe *I* just need a miracle. [slaps Neuman's shoulder] Pay attention... ahem... [yells] We ARE in MEMPHIS, 90210!" [The crowd offers a pop as Kent shakes his head in exasperation...] NEUMAN: "If you say so, John. I must've missed the sign. One thing, though, I am SURE we can agree on, is that we will witness HISTORY in the Mid-South Coliseum! Nine hopefuls walked into this building tonight, but only one competitor will walk away with the SOLID GOLD, diamond-studded championship belt in their possession." RODEO: "And that belt will signify that they are the BEST that this business has to offer. Not to mention it's the perfect accessory. It goes with ANYTHING, Kently." NEUMAN: "Again, Johnny, I'll take your word for it. In addition to the single-elimination tournament for the World championship on the card, we will have a United States title defense by three month champion '200 PROOF' ADAM EDWARDS! He will give the opportunity of wrestling that closely-held title away to TWO men in our semi-main event, as he faces THE YETI of PCW and GAW representative DRAGON SUNAMI in a TRIANGLE TITLE MATCH!" RODEO: "You said it, K-Dawg... Edwards is perhaps the first great United States champ in the title's short history. Is he unbeatable? For the summer thusfar, yes. For tonight? I'd say all bets are off." NEUMAN: "Alright, with that out of the way, it's time for our OPENING MATCH here at CROWNING GLORY!" RODEO: "AND, it's our FINAL qualifier for the tournament, to cement the eight men that will enter the final battlefield for the strap! This match is without a doubt THE single most IMPORTANT wrestling match in these two kids' LIVES. [pauses] But NO PRESSURE guys!" Take a look! First, coming down the aisle now are Joker and Jailhouse Rocker, the two promoters with representatives in the opening match! They each have small velvet bags in thier hands, and they enter the ring... NEUMAN: "Here are MSW Promoter Jailhouse Rocker and the promoter of GSW, Joker, here to offer the first pieces to our puzzle this evening. Throughout the opening round, the league promoters will come to ringside with these small bags. Joker, will you open yours up?" Joker unties the string around the end and lets a glittery piece of gold slide out into his free hand. The camera gets a close up... it's a beautiful jewel-encrusted Golden State Wrestling logo! It looks great! RODEO: "Each promoter has one of these gold plates, which they will offer at ringside to be placed onto the World heavywweight championship belt at the end of the evening! These plates will become the sideplates on the strap, to help get the message across that this title will be representin' for ALL the promotions of SHOOTCLUB!" Rocker unveils his MSW plate for the camera and holds it up for the crowd. Both plates are taken away by Orlando Morales and the two promoters head over the the announce booth with Rodeo and Neuman to describe the match! SWA World Title Tournament Qualifying Match! #8 seed GATOR (GSW) vs. #9 seed MSW Television champion "THA ANSWER" JEREMIAH STEWART (MSW) The eighth and final spot in the running for the World title will be determined with this clash. Stewart is a New Yorker who clearly has skills. Gator is from the Land Down Under, and is among the most talented heavyweights in the business today. This contest promises to be interesting... and with big rewards. The contest looked to begin with a lockup, but Gator was able to sneak his hand around Stewart's throat! Gator's choking him out! Stewart starts placing kicks into Gator's knee... kick after kick, Gator refuses to relent, but begins to hunch over. Jeremiah takes Gator into a headlock... with the choke still applied! Now Stewart starts throwing knees into Gator's head! Gator finally releases! But, he immediately shoves "Tha Answer" to the ropes... shoulderblock sends Stewart down! Gator runs the ropes... LEGDROP! Cover! 1............... 2............ Nope! Gator picks him up... whips him into the corner! Follows him in... AVALANCHE by the big man! Gator stays in the corner, grabs Jeremiah around the waist, and flies out of the corner with a belly to belly suplex! Wow! Gator goes for the pin again! 1.............. 2.................... KICKOUT! Gator is on a roll! He drags Stewart over to the ropes, and places his throat across the middle cable, choking him again! Ref John Appleton attempts to get a break, but Gator's giving him a tough time! The ref is trying to physically pull the Golden State rep off of Stewart, but he isn't moving if he doesn't want to! Gator is risking disqualification! When Appleton warns of calling for the bell, Gator finally backs off! "Tha Answer" is in a bad way! Stewart is wheezing on the mat as Gator approaches him again... but throws a mean punch! Gator staggers! He approaches again... a BRUTAL kick to the kneecap! Gate grasps at the knee... Jeremiah pulls the hair... INTO A SCHOOLBOY! 1...................... 2............................. NO!!! Stewart outta nowhere with a pinfall attempt! Gator gets up, pissed! Drops an elbow... MISSES! Stewart rolls right to the floor! He reaches in and grabs Gator's leg... pulling him hard towards the outside! No, just the legs! He raises the stunned Gate's leg and WHIPS it against the apron! What impact! Gator sits up and punches Stewart in the head... the MSW star fires back! Gator flies onto his back and gets his leg slammed against the ring apron again! Stewart pulls it to the corner, oh no, the ringpost! Gator sits up again, his legs straddling the post. Gator pulls his legs in with all he has! BUT STEWART LETS GO! He outsmarted him! Jeremiah then quickly grabs the legs again and, against the ref's wishes, CROTCHES HIM ON THE RINGPOST! Gator sits up, his eyes closed and wincing, and falls onto his back in the corner! Stewart, with his second wind, climbs onto the apron... to the top rope! Right over the GSW's favorite son! He comes off... SIT UP VERTICAL SPLASH ONTO GATOR'S CHEST!!! He might have even caught him on the mouth with that one! It was high-impact all the way! Stewart pumps his fist! This could be it! He pulls Gator in, away from the ropes! 1........................ 2......................... he got the shoulder up! Gator did it! How quickly the tide has turned in the match! And the tide turned more than once. The youthful enthusiasm of these two wrestlers, in the beginning stages of their careers, really shined through. Neither of them refused to stay down, having visions of starting their careers at the very top. Stewart remained focused on the legs and lower, LOWER abdomen of Gator, but Gator refused to be put away. At one point, Gator reversed a figure four and nearly got a submission himself! But with Gator having trouble on his feet, he had a hard time getting upright. "Tha Answer" Jeremiah Stewart had perched himself on the top rope once again! As he soars threw the air, Gator CATCHES HIM! In one fluid motion, he turns all the momentum into a MASSIVE POWERSLAM clear across the ring!!! Gator grabs his leg momentarily. Hey, he's got the lateral press, and the legs hooked!! 1....................... 2............................... NO NO! At the count of 2, Stewart barely managed to get his leg under the bottom rope, thus breaking the count! Gator gets up quickly, with a limp, and quicky goes to apply his version the sharpshooter, which he calls the Gator Twist!!! This is it! NO! Into another schoolboy by Stewart! ONE............... TWO.................. NO! Gator is up! He goes for Stewart... Stewart reaches for the leg and rolls over, and up into a HALF CRAB!!! SUBMISSION HOLD! Wow, how did he do that? Gator is SCREAMING!!! He's TOO FAR from the ropes!!! It's OVER! BUT WAIT! Gator is twisting... shifting his weight, trying to turn on his back! But that's gotta be WRECKING his back too! Gator places his free leg around Stewart's torso... Pulling him down, squeezing! Stewart hits the mat! GATOR HAS TURNED OVER! He folds Jeremiah's legs and turns back over onto his stomach! What is he... OH MAN! Gator uses everything he has to get onto bended knee, PUILLING BACK WITH THE GATOR TWIST!!!! GATOR TWIST!! THE BELL RINGS!!!!! THA ANSWER has tapped out! But look at him, he's on the mat shaking his head "No"! GATOR has advanced to the quarterfinals against Mat Hellion of ESW! Or wait... has he? The ref John Appleton and ring announcer Orlando Morales are conferencing... and Orlando makes his announcement! ORLANDO MORALES: "Ladies and gentlemen... the FIFTEEN MINUTE TIME LIMIT *HAS* EXPIRED IN THIS FINAL QUALIFYING MATCH! THEREFORE, THIS MATCH... IS A *DRAW*!!!" Time ran out for Gator and Stewart! The shorter time limit for the qualifier caught them by surprise! They gave it a helluvan effort! But neither advances, which means Mat Hellion will get a bye in the opening round! Neuman and Rodeo head back into the ring from their ringside table... NEUMAN: "REALLY tough luck for Gator and Tha Answer. But with their performance tonight, they've got the eyes of the World on them, and they will continue to impress I'm sure!"
