SWA Colossalcade 2001

http://shootclub.com SHOOTCLUB, the Ultimate Fantasy Wrestling Experience , has just released its year-end pay-per-view offering - the biggest event in Shootclub history - SWA COLOSSALCADE 2001! The Shootclub Wrestling Alliance (SWA) is the governing body that organizes the THIRTEEN regional territories (such as Empire State Wrestling [NY], White North Wrestling [Canada], and Rising Sun Wrestling [Japan]) that make up Shootclub. Each territory contains dozen of original fantasy wrestlers, created by players from all over the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and beyond! The bi-monthly SWA pay-per-views such as "Colossalcade" spotlight the BEST wrestlers/players from our regional leagues in interpromotional competition for our game-wide WORLD championships, the most prized titles in Shootclub, and perhaps in the entire e-wrestling world! SHOOTCLUB.COM opened in July of 2000 and offers the most unique online fantasy wrestling gameplay on the 'Net. We invite you to check out the site and find out for yourself what more and more people are discovering every day... that Shootclub IS the ultimate in fantasy wrestling! LIVE FROM THE PHILIPS ARENA IN ATLANTA, GA!!! The SHOOTCLUB WRESTLING ALLIANCE's YEAR-END EVENT... SWA COLOSALCADE 2001 <img src=' http://shootclub.com/images/ppv/ccade.jpg '> LIVE ON PAY-PER-VIEW! WE... ARE... LIVE! Breathtaking fireworks burst high above the heads of the Atlanta fans, lighting up the Philips Arena! The cameras roam around to catch the rabid SWA fanbase in their element! Posterboard is blanketing the crowd, some of them reading... "The Legend versus The Starr" "Version 3.0 'bout to drop!" "Daft Dave says 'I Do'" "Virgil says: 'Tiz dah seezon if ya will'" "Get Well Rocker" From there, we go to center ring, where we are met with two familiar faces... the voices of the Shootclub Wrestling Alliance! JOHN RODEO: "Wassup?!? Wassup, and welcome to Hotlanta, 90210 - home of TLC and my hot-headed homegirl, Lisa 'Left Eye' Lopes! And just like Left Eye did to Andre Rison's pad, the SWA is gonna LIGHT UP the Philips Arena tonight! WELCOME TO COLOSSALCADE TWO THOUSAND AND ONE! Fortunately for me, I am your humble host John Rodeo, and unfortunately for the rest of you, you are not." KENT NEUMAN: "And speaking of misfortunes, I have the misfortune of working along John Rodeo tonight - that's right, I am your play-by-play man, Kent Neuman." RODEO: "Did you say FORTUNE, Kentles?" NEUMAN: "Actually no, I didn't." RODEO [shakes his finger]: "Can't fool me, Big K. Let's save those FORTUNES of which you speak for a qualified professional, like Miss Cleo. Speaking of Miss C, I dialed up the digits and gave her a ring the other day looking for pay-per-view predictions." NEUMAN: "Really? What do ya have for us?" RODEO: "Well, in my FREE 3 minutes, she got as far as pronouncing AFWA's name correctly. [sighs] I guess she couldn't get that fortune cookie cracked fast enough, so I hung up. THAT'S mis-fortune, Neu-Neu. But regardless, it don't take no phony Jamaican to predict BIG THINGS here tonight - at COLOSSALCADE 2001!" NEUMAN: "Was there a POINT to all that?" RODEO [thinks for a moment]: "Shootclub's real - Psychics are a work." <b>NEUMAN:</b> "Speaking of work, Johnny, let's earn our money and run down the card here at Colossalcade 2001, LIVE from Atlanta!" <b>RODEO:</b> "Follow my lead, Kentucky Joe. [turns to camera] FIVE championships will be decided... first off, the first SWA Cruiserweight champ will be determined! Should be more competitive than the O-Town tryouts!" <b>NEUMAN:</b> "Does ESW have what it takes to expand NATIONAL? The top stars of the Empire State will meet some of the SWA's best in a six man bout!" <b>RODEO:</b> "Eric Dark takes an impressive streak into a U.S. title match with the winner of last month's Centerstage tournament, Cerebral Assassin! Is the Brainiac afraid of the Dark? We'll see, Kent." <b>NEUMAN:</b> "Just like we'll see SWA World tag gold defended by the stable championship team Crazy & Hazey, as they meet the capable Andrews Street Boyz!" <b>RODEO:</b> "And one we're all looking forward to... perhaps the most unique and wild match to ever be broadcast by the Shootclub Wrestling Alliance! It's 26 men, hardcore to the core, in the SWA SHOOTOUT! The winner is named SWA Hardcore champion, and THAT'S a SHOOT, brutha!" <b>NEUMAN:</b> "Double main event! Yes, double main event! The intimidating AFWA puts up his World title against the 2001 King of the Mountain, Murder by Me! AND it's do or die for two of Shootclub's greatest, as 'The Legend' Erik Mendell and Richard Starr enter this ring this evening, one man doing so for the very last time. The one with his shoulders pinned to the mat, never wrestles again. End of story." <b>RODEO:</b> "Nothing worse than losing a talent in their prime. But these stubborn workhorses are too proud. They believe in themselves, in their abilities, and in the fact that they'll fight another day... without having to worry about the other doing the same. Mendell and Starr, loser goes home for GOOD." <b>NEUMAN:</b> "That's the card, and it's gonna be a doozy, guaranteed." ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- REC.SPORT.PRO-WRESTLING.FANTASY FEATURED MATCH ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- SWA vs. ESW Six Man Match! DR. LOVE (ESW), ESW champion MICAH KING (ESW) & PHEONIX (ESW) w/ NURSE CANDY HART vs. ??? The ESW contingent makes their way to the ring, led by Dr. Love and Nurse Candy! The crowd, aware of the recent changes in the Empire State, cheer the team's arrival! Dr. Love has wrestled Hot Commodities Incorporated from its founder, Paul Pratt, and has beaten the evil ICON into retirement! Without the politics of Pratt, ESW is on its way to being a national force to be reckoned with! The video wall lights up, and we see the face of Shootclub International majority owner, JAKE COLOSSAL! The crowd cheers the boss! JAKE COLOSSAL: "Thank you. You know, a lot has happened here in Shootclub the past couple of months, and I've yet to comment on any of it. I've been far too busy with the day-to-day, the trips to Japan, and what not. In fact, I missed our last pay-per-view while preparing Rising Sun Wrestling for its grand opening. But I got the full report, and word has it that The Good Doctor thought he was running the show at the Colossal Cup!" Doctor Love shakes his head. Colossal suddenly, uncharacteristically, lets a scowl etch across his face. COLOSSAL: "Don't shake your head at me. I got my information from a VERY reliable source. YOU thought YOU could take over. You thought YOU were calling the shots. But I knew better than to leave the SWA unattended. With the egos back there, there's NO WAY. I left the SWA in VERY capable hands. Someone who doesn't lure the top talent from the SWA with false promises. Someone who ALWAYS had the Shootclub Wrestling Alliance's best interests in mind." The camera pulls back to reveal... COLOSSAL: "MR. PAUL PRATT... my PARTNER." RODEO: "Pratt and Colossal are on the SAME PAGE? They're making SNOWMEN down in HELL, Neuman!" NEUMAN: "What a shocking development! The Shootclub International partners, standing side by side!" Love turns and looks at Candy Hart, confused! The crowd is showering Colossal and Pratt with boos! PAUL PRATT: [patting Jake on the back] "Yes, Jake my friend, I did what was BEST for the SWA. Everyone knows I ran ESW from the VERY beginning. I knew my place in Shootclub... but YOU, Doctor, think you're BIGGER than the SWA. You think you're going NATIONAL. Tonight, we're going to burst that bubble... and we're going to burst it with a IRON FIST." Pratt walks past Colossal, heading for the curtain... COLOSSAL: "One down, one to go. The one question that I am asked more than any other is about the Atomic Wrestling Association. Up until now, I have refused comment. The AWA put Hostile City Wrestling out of business. Is the AWA a part the SWA? [chuckles] People like to make comparisons between Omega Supreme and Doctor Love. You see, there's a WRONG way to do things in business, and there's a RIGHT way. The AWA management team committed a hostile takeover of the Hostile City. That was a poweplay within the Next Star Sports & Entertainment Group Incorporated." [Read http://shootclub.com/cgi-bin/web.py?pg=rp&id=387 for an explanation] COLOSSAL: "DOC LOVE is trying for a powerplay within SHOOTCLUB INTERNATIONAL. That's the TOPS, Doc... you DON'T do that. With that said, I'd like to just make this official..." The camera pans over to find the AWA's commissioner <b>J-DUB</b> and AWA champion MANSON PAIN standing on the other side of Jake! COLOSSAL: "Welcome to the Shootclub Wrestling Alliance, J-Dub, welcome Manson Pain, WELCOME ATOMIC WRESTLING ASSOCIATION. Pain, they're expecting you in the arena." The AWA champ exits... COLOSSAL: "Dr. Love, time to put you in your place. In New York, where you belong... and we're gonna send you there ON YOUR BACK. This is the PRESIDENT of Shootclub International, Jake Colossal, and you're VERY welcome." SWA vs. ESW Six Man Match! AWA champion MANSON PAIN (AWA), GSW champion DEATH BY ME (GSW) & PCW champion "WENDIGO" BISHOP BLACK (PCW) w/ "EVIL PROMOTER" PAUL PRATT<br> vs. DR. LOVE (ESW), ESW champion MICAH KING (ESW) & PHEONIX (ESW) w/ NURSE CANDY HART Here comes the SWA team, led to the ring by PAUL PRATT! They are overwhelmingly booed as they move towards the center of the Philips Arena! Bishop Black is new to the SWA game, but as PCW heavyweight titleholder, is certainly earning his stripes in Shootclub! Death By Me has been running roughshot over GSW as the heavyweight champ, and also holds the tag titles with Murder By Me, who gets a World title shot later tonight! As we get set to go here, Pratt is yelling across the ring at Love, who is shaking his head. Wait a second, HERE GOES PRATT! Running across the ring at Love! Love sidesteps Paul, who goes stumbling out to the floor! The crowd explodes with laughter! Love points and laughs, and gets NAILED by Manson Pain in the back! The bell rings! Love took his eye off the ball and paid for it as we start the match! The Wendigo goes for his physical equal, ESW champ Micah King! Black throws a clothesline, but it's ducked and Micah King drops Bishop Black with a headbutt! Death By Me attacks the much-smaller Pheonix and dumps him to the floor! Micah King throws a kick that sends Black to the outside! That leaves Love and Pain in the ring as we finally have it down to one on one! Pain picks up Love... side backbreaker! Off the ropes, elbow drop! Cover! 1........ 2... no! Manson picks up Love, and Love gets a shot in Manson's gut! And another! Love is cut off with a knee to the face by Manson Pain! Sends Doc to the ropes... off the opposite ropes goes Pain... flying forearm! Pinfall attempt! 1..... 2..... kickout! Pain pulls Love to the corner, and there's Death By Me, just climbing back to the apron from the floor! Tag to DBM. Death By Me lifts Love and PRESSES HIM in the air over his head! He drops him onto his shoulder and into a POWERSLAM! What a fluid manuever! Cover by DBM! 1.................... 2................... IN COMES MICAH KING! He saved Love! Micah King picks up Death By Me and sends him to the ropes, Micah charges in, Death DUCKS, Micah hits the ropes, CLOTHESLINE BY DBM SENDS MICAH TO THE CONCRETE! Micah gets up rather quickly and reaches in... PULLING DBM TO THE FLOOR! Uh oh, we're getting out of hand already! DBM and Micah exchanging rights on the outside! But DBM is the legal man with Dr. Love! The ref Bruce Bucknall goes to the floor to attempt to get DBM in the ring with Doc! LOOK AT LOVE! He's climbing the ropes, eyeing Death By Me on the floor! he's going to take him out! OH NO! Pratt is screaming to Bishop Black! Black runs down the apron and takes the Doctor's legs out from under him! Love is CROTCHED ON THE CORNER! Black turns to the crowd and raises his arms... but here comes PHEONIX! He DROPKICKS Bishop Black off the apron! Pheonix helps Dr. Love off the turnbuckle, and BOOM! Manson Pain just came off the top rope and hit Pheonix with a flying BULLDOG! OUT OF ABSOLUTELY NOWHERE! He rolls Pheonix to the floor, and grabs Doc Love... Pain with a reverse DDT! Meanwhile, on the floor, ref Bucknall is still trying to contain Death By Me vs. Micah King! Death By Me is taking over on King, and dumps him with a body slam! Stomp to King! Make that two! DBM picks up the ESW champ and rolls him into the ring, and OH MY GOD! There's PHEONIX!
