A June 1995 custom-wrestler federation with its own attribute-and-earnings economy (stamina, power, agility, and a per-match cash table wrestlers could spend to raise their stats) that was still producing numbered match cards as late as February 1996, though the card texts themselves survive only as undecoded attachments. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Powerhouse Wrestling League opened 21 June 1995, asking would-be wrestlers to split 100 points across five attributes — Stamina, Power, Agility, Smartness, and Ring Ability — and list their ten most-used moves, with a World Championship tournament promised once enough had signed on.

Its own economy paid wrestlers in cash for match outcomes — $500 for a win, $250 for a loss, $1,000 for winning a title match, $500 for losing one — convertible at a rate of $100 per point into any attribute a wrestler chose to raise.

The only other surviving document is a bare announcement, eight months later on 18 February 1996, of “just the second set of matches for the PWL.” The actual card text, for that set and for the league's first, survives only as an unreadable attachment; no match result of any kind is recoverable from the surviving record.
