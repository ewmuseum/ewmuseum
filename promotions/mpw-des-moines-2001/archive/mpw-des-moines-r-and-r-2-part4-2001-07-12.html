MPW R&R #2

12 July 2001

[Part 4 of 5] [The camera cuts back from commercials right to Julie Stewart in the middle of the ring with a mic.] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Shawn Jamison VS Taylor Knox Writer: [name withheld] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ JS: Introducing now the participants for this semi-main event!! Coming to the ring first...standing at 6'3 tall and weighing in at 255 pounds...he hails from SHORT HILLS NEW JERSEY!!!!...He is SHAWWWWWWNNNNNNnn JAMISOOOONNNNNN!!! ["Criminal" by Eminem plays as Shawn Jamison slowly walks to the ring and enters over the top ropes jumping. He then slowly walks to each ringpost and flips every side of the arena off. He then sits on a ring corner waiting for his upcoming opponent.] JS: And his opponent...standing in at 6'1 and weighing 230 pounds... he hails from...HUNTINGTON BEACH!!!....he is...TAYLORRRRRRR KNOOOOOOXXXXX!!! ("Why can't we be friends" By Smashmouth starts to play) (Taylor walks out from behind the curtian and soaks in the boos. Some people remember him, his dumbass antics, and the time he beat a Chris Courtade character. His fat one armed manager Tommy wobbles behind him. Tommy is trying to fix his undersized plastic baby arm in the right place so all the ladies will be wet for his burrito.) (I am not big into intros and pyro cause they don't make the man so let's just say Taylor walks down the ring like every wrestler before him while his manager waits off to the side. One camera flash goes off and it was probably your mother waiting in heat for Taylor after the show.) SH: These two are two of the men that had a lot of impact last week on the inagural R&R! WJ: I am just kinda amazed to see One Armed Tommy even out here after being attacked by who we now know as Johnny Knuckles, OZ's manager. You have to know Knox is going to get some revenge for this one. [All of the sudden over the Grand Ball Room PA system the the first line of "Only One" by SlipKnot plays.] #Pain - made to order...# [The fans in the Ball room give a huge pop for there home state favorite SlipKnot music. All of the sudden on "The Silo" the letters "TNL" pop up in huge white letters with a dark blue outlining!] #I'm not the second coming, I'm the first wave...# #Better get an army, cuz the fire left me unscathed...# #You betcha bottom dollar I'm the top of the shit pile...# #So stick around cuz I'll get to you in just a few...# #Step off the achtung, get back or get stung...# #Idiotic sense of yourself, are you that dumb?...# #Entire legion of me, totalitarian...# #The one and only muthafucka, top of the world man...# [The fans in the arena give a huge POP as they realize that the "TNL" stands for "The New Legend". ] #Cuz I can't stand it, planned it, gonna crash land it...# #In other words, consider me branded...# #Another version of me, another version of you...# #Both barrels - what you gonna do?...# [All of the sudden from the curtain emerges none other MPW President Kenneth Morlock! Kenneth Morlock is wearing a black leisure suit with a dark blue tight fitted undershirt. Morlock also has on a pair of dark black dress shoes. Kenneth Morlock also has a black microphone in his right hand. Kenneth Morlock stands at the end of the entrance way and begins to speak.] KM: Knox this is all for you. Last week on R&R you took out one of my best friends. Not only did you send him to the hospital with your antics but then you attacked him while he was on a stretcher. Well let me tell you, Lefty will be back sooner or later, you can guaruntee that! [Fans give a huge face pop] KM: But I decided to punish you in another way! NEXT WEEK ON R&R, IT WILL BE A TAG TEAM MATCH! TAYLOR KNOX AND ONE ARMED TOMMY will be on one side of the ring, and your opponents will be OZ AND JOHNNY KNUCKLES! You two want to start your own personal war in my fed? Well, let me be the one that moderates it and ends it because I will also be the Special Guest Referee for this match. [Kenneth Morlock walks back through the curtains to a HUGE FACE POP!] SH: OH my lord! I cannot believe that! Knox and Tommy have to take on OZ and Knuckles next week on R&R! Morlock is really shaping next week on up. WJ: What the hell is he trying to prove? Knox cant face OZ and Knuckles by himself. SH: He wont be by himself. He will have One Armed Tommy to back him up! WJ: Like I said, Knox cant face OZ and Knuckles by himself!! I mean we have already seen what Knuckles can do the way he attacked Tommy and the way he kept pestering in OZ's match earlier. [In the ring, Knox is still amazed at what was just said and is just starring at the entrance way as all of the sudden Jamison move in from behind and nails him with a hard lariat sending him onto the top rope. Jamison pulls Knox off of the top rope and whips Knox to the other side of the ring onto the ropes. Knox comes off the ropes and is met with a vicious dropkick to the face. Knox is laying on the mat after the dropkick and Jamison moves in and tries to lock in a figure four leg lock. Knox kicks Jamison off and begins to rise mto his feet. Jamison shakes off the quick cob webs and rushes at Knox but Knox meets him with a vicious sidewalk slam. Knox rolls up Jamison for the quick pin....1..........Jamison kicks out fast and both Jamison and Knox both rush up to there feet.] SH: Looks like Knox is trying to take the easy road by trying to end this match as early as possible. WJ: Knox just knows that he has to save some of his energy for next week when he wrestles in that handicap match! [Knox and Jamison make it up at the same time and this time Knox rushes at Jamison and is met with a square right to the jaw. This knocks Knox a couple steps back as it surprised him more than it hurt him. Jamison moves back in quickly though and nails Knox with a few more right hands. This knocks Knox back into the corner. Knox seems to be a little dazed but shakes off the cobwebs but looks up and is met with a superkick to the jaw. This sends Knox up against the turnbuckles hard and then crashing to the mat in the corner. Jamison nails Knox with a boot right to the top of the head. Jamison then pulls Knox up and nails him with a short arm clothesline sending Knox right back to the mat. Jamison scoops up Knox and sets him up in a bulldog position. Jamison has a devious smirk on his face as he begins to run with Knox in his arm. Jamison gets to almost the other end of the ring and slams the bulldog down into the mat. Knox hits the mat hard and now begins bouncing around in pain.] WJ: See now that is what I am talking about Jamison isnt one of my hero's for nothing. Even though he hasnt shaken my hand or had me in the ring, that is cool! SH: You seriously need to get a hobby. The guy is never going to want to have you in the ring. He wants to win, not play with has beens! [Willie Jones pauses for a moment at the quick heel change in Harple.] SH: Naw, I was just kidding. WJ: DAMN! I knew you were a wuss! [Jamison picks up Knox and tosses him outside the ring. Jamison follows as he slides under the bottom rope. On the outside Jamison picks up Knox and whips him into the metal guard rail. Knox hits the guard rail and stays on them with his arms over the rail. Jamison goes over under the ring and pulls out a steel chair. Jamison rares back and nails Knox in the gut with the chair. Knox falls to the ground hard. All of the sudden One Armed Tommy comes over and hits Jamison in the back with his fist. Jamison feels no affect and turns around and nails One Armed Tommy across the top of the head with the chair also. Jamison then drops the chair and drags Knox over towards the announcers table. Jamison lays Knox right in front of the announcers table. Jamison then jumps up onto the announcers table.] SH: What the hell is he fixing to do? WJ: I dont know but I like it already! [Jamison comes off of the announcers table with a moonsault. Knox somehow moves out of the way and Jamison hits the concrete floor hard. Knox gets to his feet as Jamison is down on the floor. Knox scoops up Jamison and drags him onto the announcers table. And sets him up in a DDT position on the announcers table!.] SH: Oh my GOD! This is INSANE! He is gonna try and put him through our announcers table! WJ: I am getting the hell out of dodge! [Stony Harple and Willie Jones both get up from the announcers table and throw there headsets down because the cord goes no further. All of the sudden on the announcers table Taylor Knox DDT's Shawn Jamison into the table. The table smashes under them from the devestating impact! Jamison is now bleeding from his forehead from the move and Taylor Knox is also down and out. All of the sudden EMT's and an MPW technical crew run out from the back. The EMT's rush over to Knox and Jamison checking them as the technical crew rushes and tries to hook back up the announcers because the impact unplugged all the wires and now they have no sound.] [The EMT's have rolled Knox off of Jamison and Knox is now starting to rise to his feet as the EMT's continue to check on Jamison who is now bleeding profusly from the head. Knox pushes the EMT's out of the way and scoops up Jamison and slams him back down onto the stretcher that EMT's brought out. Knox then slides in the ring. The EMT's look confused but turn around and begin to strap Jamison to the stretcher. All of the sudden out of nowhere Knox comes flying off the top ropes with an Asai Moonsault and takes out the three EMT's as they fall and knock over the stretcher. Knox is also down. The technical crew is still working to get the headsets working again. A few more technical people also run down and set up a new table. This table is a plain brown table. Harple has now moved in and is trying to make sure the EMT's are alright. Harple moves all three EMT's over towards the side of the ring. All of the sudden from the entrance ramp Kenneth Morlock is seen running down to the ring. Morlock rushes over and picks up two of the small EMT's and motions for Harple to get the other one. Harple grabs the third and Morlock and Harple carry the EMT's down the aisleway and out the curtain. The technical people motion for Willie Jones to put the headset back on and test it.] WJ: TEST! TEST! TEST! Yes we have the power back thanks guys. I honestly do not know what just happened but DAMN! Taylor Knox is trying to kill everyone! Harple will be back in a little bit...I think...But what is going to happen here? Knox is still down and Jamison is strapped to that stretcher. [Knox is starting to gain some strength and makes his way up to his feet. Knox looks over at Willie Jones and gives a deadly smirk as Knox grabs the stretcher and rolls it with all his strength into the metal gaurd rail. The stretcher hits the guardrail and the momentum sends it right over the gaurd rail as it hits. The stretcher is now facing down with the bloody Jamison having his body still strapped to the floor. Knox goes over to the guard rail and hops over. Knox turns the Stretcher onto its side and you can now see the face of Jamison with blood all over it. Knox finally undo's the straps on the stretcher and Jamison falls to the mat. Knox picks up the stretcher and slams it down on Jamison. Jamison jumps from the impact but looks to be almost totally out of it. Knox picks up Jamison and whips him to the guard rail. Jamison hits the railing and flips over. Knox then proceeds to follow Jamison over the guardrailing. Knox picks up Jamison and walks him back over to the replacement announcers table.] WJ: What the hell are you going to do now? Fans he has already knocked us out once so if he decides to get crazy again dont worry sound will come back. [Knox bangs Jamison's bloody head off the table as Willie Jones gets up and tosses his headset out the other end of the table so that no wires are under the table. Knox then places Jamison on the table face up. Knox then proceeds to climb into the ring and up to the top ropes. There is no movement at all from Jamison. Knox comes off in a leg drop position and out of nowhere Jamison rolls out of the way! Knox hits the replacement announce table hard and goes right through all the way to the concrete floor. Jamison is showing little movement and now Knox is showing no movement at all! Willie Jones grabs his headset and takes a seat at the ring announcers smaller table.] WJ: I honestly think these two are trying to kill themselves. Knox has been pulling out everything. Jamison is bleeding everywhere and now I need another damn table. And WHERE THE HELL DID MORLOCK TAKE HARPLE AND THOSE EMT'S? [All of the sudden from the entrance ramp comes out none other than Oz and Johnny Knuckles. Knuckles is carrying a wooden two by four and nails One Armed Tommy once again in the back of the head. One Armed Tommy goes down hard. Oz and Knuckles then make there way over to where Knox is layed out. Referee Angie Daddie doesnt know what to do so she just lets them go right by. Oz goes around one side of the ring and Knuckles goes around the other side. Knuckles goes right to Knox and picks him up from the rubbled table. Knuckles puts Knox in a position between his legs and then pulls him up and powerbombs him back down to the concrete floor hard! Oz then moves in and scoops up Knox, and puts Knox into a pump handle and then lifts Knox up onto his shoulder where Oz has now slid Knox towards his back and finish's Knox off with an inverted neck breaker, similar to a Diamond Cutter.] WJ: Oh my lord. That is Crazy! Knuckles put him back down with a hard powerbomb and then Oz nails his OZMOSIS on Knox! When the hell will these two be done with each other? Morlock has already said they will fight next week, why cant Oz just wait till next week? [Johnny Knuckles picks up Shawn Jamison and puts him over his shoulder and walks the ring steps and steps into the ring. Oz picks up Knox and rolls him under the bottom rope before he slides in under the ropes also. Oz drags Knox to the center of the ring and Knuckles places Jamison over the knocked out Knox. Angie Daddie counts....1...........2...........3333333333!!!!!] WJ: Man oh man! That was crazy! That was BRUTAL! THAT WAS MPW!! Jamison got the win because Oz and Knuckles aniahlated Knox after Knox aniahlated Jamison. I dont know what will happen next with these guys but I do know that they better come set me up another damn table. [All of the sudden Oz and Knuckles walk out of the entrance way as a couple more EMT's rush out down to the ring to get Knox and Jamison. As they come out a huge POP is heard for Stony Harple who comes out again and the technical crew with another table!] WJ: Ladies and gentlemen we are gonna go ahead and cut to a commercial while they reset us back up here and clean this rubble. Stay tuned the main event is next! ---[Cut to commercials]--- ---[Cut to Part 5]---
