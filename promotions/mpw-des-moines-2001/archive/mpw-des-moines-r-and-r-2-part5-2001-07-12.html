[Part 5 of 5] [The camera cuts back from commercials to a plain old brown announcer's table with a lot of wires sitting on top of it and under it. At the table is once again Stony Harple and Willie Jones.] WJ: That was one hell of a match. SH: Yes it was. Morlock wouldnt let me come back out till it was over for fear I might get hurt! WJ: I didnt see him trying to protect me! SH: And on that note. All of the EMT's that were injured were taken to IOWA Baptist Medical and are all fine. WJ: Oh yeah that is always good to hear! Is My guy Dosinger fine? SH: WHat the hell did Dosinger have to do with anything that just happened? WJ: Nothing, I just wanted to make sure he was alright... [All of the sudden over the Grand Ball Room PA system the the first line of "Only One" by SlipKnot plays.] #Pain - made to order...# [The fans in the Ball room give a huge pop for there home state favorite SlipKnot music. All of the sudden on "The Silo" the letters "TNL" pop up in huge white letters with a dark blue outlining!] #I'm not the second coming, I'm the first wave...# #Better get an army, cuz the fire left me unscathed...# #You betcha bottom dollar I'm the top of the shit pile...# #So stick around cuz I'll get to you in just a few...# #Step off the achtung, get back or get stung...# #Idiotic sense of yourself, are you that dumb?...# #Entire legion of me, totalitarian...# #The one and only muthafucka, top of the world man...# [The fans in the arena give a huge POP as they realize that the "TNL" stands for "The New Legend". ] #Cuz I can't stand it, planned it, gonna crash land it...# #In other words, consider me branded...# #Another version of me, another version of you...# #Both barrels - what you gonna do?...# [All of the sudden from the curtain emerges none other MPW President Kenneth Morlock! Kenneth Morlock is wearing a black leisure suit with a dark blue tight fitted undershirt. Morlock also has on a pair of dark black dress shoes. Kenneth Morlock also has a black microphone in his right hand and the MPW Regional title drapped over his left shoulder.] #All I hear is human noise...# #You made your own fuckin' choice...# #I belong to only me...# #Silence for my revelry...# [The fans in the arena give Kenneth Morlock a huge face pop as Kenneth Morlock walks down the aisleway slapping fives with the fans and letting the fans rub the MPW Regional title.] #I can over die over time...# #Filthy hands, stay away from mine...# #Every reason is a right to hate...# #Painful clutch - death is fine, gimme mine...# [Kenneth Morlock reaches the ring steps and climbs them smiling the whole way. The fans in the arena continue to give Morlock a huge POP as steps into the ring. Kenneth Morlock walks to the center of the ring and stands there smiling.] #Only one of us walks away...# #Somebody explain this...# #You don't care cuz you don't exist...# #What the fuck is this another joke?...# #Reckoning!...# [The fans continue to give Kenneth Morlock a huge pop as he raises the mic to his lips.] KM: IOWA! [The fans once again give a huge POP as he mention there home state!] KM: Let me be the first to THANK each and every one of you for making MPW and R&R a complete success last week. Last week a sell out! This week a sell out. Great ratings from those who are watching on TV. Mid-West Pro Wrestling is climbing the food chain faster than I even thought it would. And once again THANK YOU ALL! [The fans give themselves a huge POP as Kenneth Morlock stands in the ring clapping for the fans.] KM: Now that right there is what it is all about. But for the wrestler's of MPW it is about something totally different. It is about this piece of gold right here on my shoulder. Last week we saw Stone Harris walk away with the first piece of hardware, but this one right here is the big dog. The one that will show who runs with the pack and who sits with there tails between there legs on the porch. [Suddenly "When Worlds Collide" by Powerman 5k begins to blare over the Grand Ballroom, PA system. {CROWD POP for the song} After a few moments of the music and fans reaction the man responsible for making the mystery man match against Watterson comes out from behind the black curtained off area. This man is none other then the MPW President, Rob Loney~! Rob gives off an evil smirk and pulls a microphone from his back belt loop. He looks around with an arrogant smile as he lifts the microphone up to his mouth as "When World Collide" by Powerman 5k fades out.] RL: Yes, Kenny, I think it is about time we figure out how we'll distribute the Regional title. Now, do you have any suggestions? KM: I think we should give the fans of MPW what they want to see. And everyone knows that they want to see a CAGE! But not only a normal cage! But an MPW CAGE OF DEATH! [A Huge face pop is heard from the fans and also a huge CAGE pop is heard.] Rl: Hmmm... Not a bad idea Kenny, but since this is my federation I think I'll have to add to your "Small" Idea. [Rob Loney looks at Kenneth Morlock with a very cocky smile.] RL: We'll have to do a _FIVE_ man cage of death... [All of the sudden Kenneth Morlock interupts.] KM: First of all! Fifty-fifty! Second, there is no way we can have a FIVE MAN CAGE OF DEATH MATCH! [Rob Loney laughs and pocks Morlock in the chest.] RL: It's mine, and you know it. Second of all we can... We put all the names in a hat. Pick 10 guys. 10 guys, 5 matchs! Winners get put into the bloody mach. KM: You ever touch me again and the only thing that will be yours is a hospital bed over at Des Moines medical. Now if we do happen to do this the 10 men will be in qualifying matches and the final five would have the CAGE OF DEATH match at the MPW PPV in Chicago Illinois, in what we are turning into our outside supercard at Grant Park. [Rob Loney takes a step back and his eyes grow some what wide.] RL: Ya know something Kenny, I like your minor addition to my "MONSTER" of an idea. I'll accept that idea and on August 5th in Grant Park, Chi Town, the AWP will put on it's _FIRST_ super card. KM: It wasnt anything for you to accept. MPW is my baby too! My stroke goes a hell of a lot further than yours ever will here in MPW! RL: What ever you say... Hell, since we've got nothing as big as this in July lets use the PPV name World Coming Down for this August 5th Supercard. And Kenny... [Rob Loney begins to leave the ring. As he reaches the asile way he laughs.] RL: If you think this is your baby I guess you can make all the disicions, but can you do if you don't have any talent? KM: What the hell do you mean? Everyone knows MPW would rather work for me anyday before even considering working for you. I brought you in for the ride and I will take you out also! [A cocky smile grows across Rob Loneys face.] RL: Kenny...Just remember I will be here next week just like you... [Rob Loney then walks through the black curtain. Kenneth Morlock stands there for a few moments and then drops the mic infuriated. Morlock then leaves the ring and walks to the back to a huge FACE POP!] WJ: Damn I love President Loney he is much cooler than old Kenny boy! SH: President Morlock is much cooler. But I dont want to argue with you because I know how you feel about it! WJ: Thats right you know how I feel about him and his ho! Oh! Where is Julie, isnt it time to start another match? [Julie Stewart in the ring gives Willie Jones a dirty look and then flips him off as the crowd gives a huge pop!] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ MAIN EVENT!! Des Moines City Title Match "Nasty" Nick Hart VS Stone Harris (c) Writer: [name withheld] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ JS: And now for the MPW R&R MAIN EVENT!!! This match is scheduled for a thirty minute time limit and it is for the MPW DES MOINES CITY CHAMPIONSHIP!!!! [All of the sudden there is a huge POP heard for the mention of MPW, the title and Des Moines! ] JS: Coming to the ring first, the challenger....he stands in at 6'1 tall....he weighs in at 229 pounds and he hails Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada!!!....he is "NASTTTTYYYYY" NICKKKKKKKKKK HAAAAAARRRRRTTTTTT!!! ("Bombtrack" by Rage Against the Machine BLASTS over the loudspeakers as Nick Hart makes his way to the ring accompanied by his bodyguard, Jake McRae. Nick stands at the top of the entryway fists extended in the air, Jake behind him. Fireworks go off as the song explodes into it's main riff. As the first verse begins, Nick and Jake make their way to the ring, the fans pelting them with boos from all angles. From the looks of things, Nick is in his usual ring attire: a pair of black tights with the usual red designs and a pair of black boots with the red trim and red tassles. Jake is wearing his normal attire as well: a pair of white camouflage pants, a black trenchcoat, and black combat boots. Nick enters the ring and climbs the turnbuckle hoping to appeal to the crowd, only to hear more boos. After telling the fans to shut up, Nick steps off the turnbuckle and starts to warm up for his match.) JS: AND THE CHAMPION!!!!!!! [Huge pop for a champion!] JS:He stands in at 6'2 tall...he weighs in at 242 pounds, and he hails from...OAKLAND CALIFORNIA!!!!.....THE MPW DES MOINES CITY CHAMPION.....STOOOOOONNNNEEEEE HARRRRRIIIIISSSSS!!! [The methodical drum beats of "Walk on Water" by Ozzy Osbourne begin to play from the public address system. The camera pans over to the entrance where Stone Harris begins his walk to the ring, Harris has the MPW Des Moines City title drapped over his right shoulder as he stares blindly at the squared circle. Harris is wearing a pair of black wrestling shorts which read "Stone" on the tail end in blood red lettering. He has short, jet-black hair, and is sporting black knee pads. Stone climbs the ring steps and goes into the ring threw the middle ring and hands referee Joe Mamma the MPW DMC title. Joe Mamma shows the title to Nick Hart and then hands it to the time keeper.] WJ: This right here is the match I have been waiting for. After a match that just happened with Knox and Jamison as bloody and grueling as it was, this one should be just as bad because both men want that MPW Des Moines City title. SH: Finally I agree with you on something. Both of these men are ging to give it one hundred and ten percent to walk out of here with the title of champion writter across there back! [Harris and Hart move right in at each other with a hard collar and elbow tie up. Both men are giving it all they got but neither man is budging. Thye stay in the tie up for a few moments longer until finally both men push off. The fans give a decent size pop as they see both men not backing down. Hart stands there and smirks for a moment and Harris see's his gaurd is down and moves right in for a clothesline. Hart ducks out of the way as Harris turns around Hart jumps up for a standing drop kick. Harris see's it and pushes it off as Hart hits the mat. Harris goes for an elbow drop and connects, finally a move has landed. Hart absorbs the elbow drop and rises to his feet right after Harris. Harris grabs Hart and scoops slams him over again down to the mat. Hart hits the mat hard and is looking up at the lights dazed for a moment as Harris scoops him up again.] WJ: Damn, I didnt think anyone was gonna connect a move the way these two started off. But now it looks like Stone is taking care of "Nasty" Nick Hart! SH: I for one would love to see Stone Harris retain his MPW DMC title and get his hands on the man that just got carted out of here Shawn Jamison! WJ: I dont think we will be seeing from Jamison any time soon he was bleeding pretty bad before they got him out of here. [Stone Harris has Hart in an upright position Harris lifts Hart and nails him with an Uranage Slam. Hart is down once again as Harris rises to his feet and drags Hart over to the corner of the ring. Hart seems to be a little dazed as Harris climbs the ropes and goes to the top. Harris pauses for a moment to look out into the crowd before coming off with an elbow drop from the top rope. Hart rolls out of the way! Harris nails the mat and begins to roll around grabbing his elbow. Hart raises up to his feet and grabs Harris and locks him in an arm bar submission. Harris hollars a little bit as Joe Mamma moves into position. Harris just keeps hollaring "NO" as Joe Mamma ask's him if he wants to give it up. ] WJ: Looks a little bit like the "Nasty" one has taken over control. SH: Listen to the way Harris hollars. That elbow drop really had to hurt his elbow. But I guess those are the breaks when you take it to high risk maneuvers. WJ: Werent you a high risk wrestler? One of those high flyers... SH: Well, yes, yes I was. WJ: I thought you were one of those guys that ruined the sport! [Hart finally releases the arm bar and pulls Harris up by his hurt arm. Hart scoops Harris into the air fast and comes down with a variation of a shoulder breaker on Harris's elbow. Hart then drops Harris onto the mat as Harris clutches his elbow as Hart laughs and looks over at Jake McRae and gets a thumbs up. Hart turn back around towards where Harris now on his knees pulling himself up. All of the sudden out of nowhere Harris pushes from his knees and nails Hart with a lariat. the impact from the lariat send Hart to the mat as Harris once again falls and clutches his elbow in pain!] WJ: Aww the poor little champion is a little hurt! HA! I love it! What kind of moves is he going to pull off if he cannot use his arm?!! SH: I have seen many, many men pull out stunning comebacks hurt or not he knows he is a champion! [Harris pulls himself up to his feet clutching his arm as Hart is now getting up also. Hart rushes at Harris and tackles him to the mat. Hart then proceeds to slam Harris's arm into the mat. Hart then rises to his feet and scoops Harris up by the hair as he scoops him all the way into the air in a vertical suplex position. Hart holds it for a moment and then slams Harris down face first into the mat as he releases the suples frontwards. Hart then moves in again and tosses Harris out of the ring right in front of Jake McRae. McRae smiles as Hart follows out of the ring. Hart motions for McRae to go get something as Hart picks up Harris and holds him facing out. McRae comes back over with a chair and swings it at Harris. Harris somehow ducks out of the way and the chair nails "Nasty" Nick Hart! McRae looks shocked as all of the sudden he raises the chair to hit Harris and is met with a superkick into the chair which nails McRae in the face.] SH: I told you he was gonna get himself back into the match. Might not have been to conventional but oh well! WJ: I gotta give it to him, it is like I always say, "By hook or by crook". [Harris stands there for and takes a breather before scooping hart up and sliding him into the ring. Harris then follows into the ring by sliding in under the bottom rope also. Harris is still favoring his elbow as he scoops up Hart and puts him in a piledriver position. Harris jumps up and slams the piledriver down into the mat. Hart hits the mat and looks to be out as Harris goes for a cover......1..........2..........All of the sudden out of nowhere Jake McRae who has just gotten up pulls the ref out of the ring. Joe Mamma gets into the chest of McRae and begins verbal assulting him before he slides back in the ring. Harris does not seem phased at all by the count being stopped. Harris scoops Hart back up and whips him across the ring into the ropes. Hart comes off the ropes and is met with a vicious sidewalk slam by Harris.] SH: That right there was one hell of a move by the champion Stone Harris. He doesnt wear that belt for no reason! WJ: Most people wear belts to hold there pants up. SH:I meant...nevermind! Harris takes this match! No doubt! [It takes Stone Harris a few moments to rise up to his feet after this devestating maneuver. Harris once again scoops up Hart and this time nails Hart with a Hangmans Noose neckbreaker!] SH: I told you! I told you! He just nailed Nick Hart with his finisher "The Cessation" WJ: Nick hart is out! [All of the sudden Jake McRae jumps up onto the aprom and grabs the ref and begins hollaring at him. The ref begins hollaring back as all of the sudden from the entrance way comes running out none other than Shawn Jamison. Jamison is still in his bloody wrestling clothes and now a huge gauze on his forehead. Jamison also has a baseball bat in his hand. The baseball bat is no ordinary baseball bat, the bat seems to be wrapped in chain link fence. Harris has his back turned as he looks at the ref and McRae. McRae smiles at Harris and motions for him to turn around as he continues to hold the ref and holler at him. Harris waits a moment and then turns around and barely ducks the swinging bat. Harris grabs Jamison and nails "The Cessation" on him also. Harris then hollars at Jamison as Hart gets up and grabs Harris and Nick applies a Front Headlock on Harris and looks to be going for a DDT with one hand, then uses the other hand to lift Harris off his feet before falling forward, dropping Harris's face into the mat.] WJ: Oh man this is getting crazy! Jamison is back wounded and has a bat but Harris somehow knew it was coming and nails "The Cessation" on Jamison but is then met by Hart who nailed his finisher "The Face Plant Special!" SH: And all this time our senior referee is still arguing with McRae! [Harris is now laid out in the middle of the ring as Jamison makes his way back to his feet and Hart picks up the bat. Hart looks at the bat and seems amazed at what is wrapped around it and hand the bat back to Jamison. Jamison takes the bat and nails Harris a few times in the gut before nailing him once in the head. Harris is now busted open from the forehead as Jamison spits on Harris and leaves the ring and the entrance way to a horrid amount of boo's! Hart makes the cover as McRae hops down off the mat. Joe Mamma makes the cover.....1.............2............33333333333!!!!!!!!!!!] WJ: I knew Hart was gonna take it! NEW CHAMP! NEW CHAMP! NEW CHAMP!!! SH: That is crap! Joe Mamma is supposed to be the best ref we got and he let himself be volnurable to Mcrae and we have a new champ. Well, congrats to Hart even though it was by crook! [Jake McRae and Nick Hart celebrate for a few moments in the ring as Julie Stewart makes the announcement!] JS: AND THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH AND NEW MPW DES MOINES CITY CHAMPION........"NASTYYYYY" NICKKKKKKK HARRRRRRTTTTTTTTT!!! WJ: Ladies and gentlemen thank you for tuning in to another great R&R! SH: Tune in next week for some more action packed MPW and R&R! [The camera fades out as Nick Hart and Jake McRae are celebrating in the ring with the MPW DMC title over a fallen bloody Stone Harris!]
