MPW (Des Moines, 2001) — Card

MPW R&R

5 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 4 of 4] [Cut's back to the announcers table from the commercials. Stony Harple is sitting there smiling as Willie Jones seems to be looking everywhere but at the camera.] SH: Tonight has been an amazing night here for MPW. The inaugural R&R has been a complete sucess. From the first match where Hart and Thunder wrestled an amazing show. WJ: SHOW? The match sucked! The only thing that made it half way intresting was the mysterious masked man attacking Thunder at the end of the match. I wouldnt mind shaking that person's hand! SH: Then we has the Jensen and Harris battle. And I do mean battle. These men went to all heights and had no limits to what they would do. They turned it into a hardcore match at the drop of a dime and showed no mercy. WJ: Yeah it was kinda nice to see the good ole drunk take the win it that one. Just proves that alcohol does stimulate the brain! SH: Yeah, but then we saw one of my personal favorites get carted out of here on a stretcher from the hellatious beating he took in his match. Lefty, my prayers are with you! WJ: I am sorry I cant say I second the motion. The damn loser got what was coming to him. Taylor Knox showed the man what a beatdown really is! SH: My personal favorite had to be the man I picked to win the four man walking away with it. "Bootylicious" Romeo Studd gave it all he had and was the man on top. WJ: I dont think he really looked on top while Trapp was beating him with that chair after the match. Looked to me like he was sent right back down to the bottom. And how the hell can you say he gave it all he had when OZ was brutally attacked by Knox? It went from the scheduled four way to a triple threat that quick. SH: Don't you like to say, a win is a win, is a win. By hook or by crook. Boy who do I sound like now? WJ: A jackass. But also tonight we saw the emergence of the newest MPW superstar, Shawn Jamison! He came out and said he would be in the battle royale tonight, and you know he has to be the odd's on favorite since he hasnt had to fight anyone else tonight. SH: Also in the backstage area we saw Jim Dosinger! Was he here to start trouble or just to watch some healthy compeition? I guess we will have to find out as the night goes on. But I am getting word that we are ready to start our Battle Royale! Julie take it away. [Julie Stewart steps into the ring through the middle rope and stands in the center of the ring as (sic) by SlipKnot begins to play over the PA system. ] #Enemy - Show me what you wanna be...# #I can handle anything...# #Even if I can't handle you...# #Readily - Either way it better be...# #Don't you fuckin' pity me...# #Get up, get off...# JS: Here it is the moment you have all been waiting for the MPW Battle Royale for the Des Moines City Title!!!! [The fans in the arena give a huge POP for the mention of the title and there hometown.] JS: And here are your participants, NICK HART, AUBRY JENSEN, TYLER ROSE, JOHNNY THUNDER~! [Julie pauses as these four men begin to make there way to the ring.] #What the hell am I sayin'?...# #I don't know about malevolent...# #Sure as hell decadent...# #I want somebody to step up, step off...# #Walls! Let me fall! FUCK YOU ALL!...# #Get a grip, don't let me slip 'til I drop the ball...#\ JS: HIGOR ROSA, KENDALL TRAPP, STONE HARRIS, and OZ~! [Julie Stewart pauses once again as these four men walk towards the ring.] #I - I want it all!...# #Fuck this shit, I'm sick of it...# #You're goin' down, this is a war!...# #Who the fuck am I to criticize your twisted...# #state of mind?...# #You're leavin' me suspect - I'm leavin' you grotesque...# #Feels like a burn from which you never learn...# #Cause and effect, you jealous ass...# #Press your face against the glass - SUFFER!...# JS: TAYLOR KNOX, ROMEO STUDD, AND SHAWM JAMISON~!~!~!~! [Julie Stewart quickly leaves the ring as these three man make there way down to the ring.] #I - I want it all!...# #Fuck this shit, I'm sick of it...# #You're goin' down, this is a war!...# #I've just begun...# #It's about that time...# #Gotta get mine...# #YOU CAN'T KILL ME...# #CUZ I'M ALREADY...# #INSIDE YOU...# #SICK...# [The eleven men in the ring all stand around staring each other down.] SH: I didn't know Higor "The Rebel" Rosa was gonna show up here tonight. WJ: I heard he was gonna be here but I thought it was an ill placed rumor. SH: I heard Rosa and Morlock are old drinking buddies. WJ: Buddies or not, Rose goes out early and Jamison takes home the gold. SH: I am gonna have to put my money on Nick Hart or maybe Romeo Studd. WJ: Too bad lightning doesnt strike twice in the same place or those might be good assumptions. [In the ring everyone just seems to start bum rushing everyone. OZ has somehow found KNOX and they are throwing some furious lefts and rights. STUDD and TRAPP have met up and are doing the same. JAMISON seems to be focusing on JENSEN! HART and HARRIS have met up in the middle of the ring. ROSE and THUNDER are in the corner going blow for blow as ROSA makes his way around the ring scoping out the action. All of the sudden out of nowhere JENSEN gets whipped through the heart of the action in the middle of the ring. As JENSEN hits the traffic he stumbles around in the middle of the ring and is met with a flurry of fists and elbows from HART and HARRIS who have now turned away from each other and towards JENSEN. JAMISON smiles as he see's he is all alone. All of the sudden out of nowhere from behind JAMISON is met by a hard lariat from ROSA. JAMISON hits the mat face first as ROSA and KNOX who has finally broke free from OZ, begin to stomp on the back of JAMISON.] SH: Well, it doesnt look like your boy is in too good of a position face first down on the mat. WJ: Actually I think he is in perfect positon as he cant be thrown out if he is face down on the mat. So THANK YOU! SH: So far it looks like a couple of early on fueds are starying right back up here in the Royale with Studd and Trapp going at it and Oz and Knox meeting up! WJ: I think Trapp and Studd are gonna kill each other. Not that, that is a problem or anything. [Back in the ring. KNOX and ROSA continue to stomp on JAMISON. HART and HARRIS continue to pound on JENSEN as all of the sudden the throw him into the ropes and they come off with a double drop kick that sends JENSEN right over the top rope.] SH: Jensen has been eliminated! Jensen has been eliminated! WJ: Maybe I was wrong on which drunk goes out early! [HARRIS and HART stand there and smile for a moment when all of the sudden HARRIS nails HART with a clothesline from hell sending HART down to the mat hard. Meanwhile on the other side of the ring ROSE and THUNDER have became entangled in with TRAPP and STUDD. All four men seem to be throwing blows at each other as all of the sudden TRAPP and THUNDER grab ROSE and nails him with a double suplex tossing him right over the top rope and onto the floor.] SH: And there goes "High Rollin" Tyler Rose! WJ: What no hollaring about ELIMINATED! ELIMINATED!?? SH: He was one of my underdog picks to win it all! WJ: Underdog, overdog doesnt matter, right now he is an ELIMINATED DOG! [Back in the ring JAMISON has made it back up to his feet finally as ROSA and KNOX begin to fight amongst themselves. JAMISON takes a few steps back and is met by bumping right into OZ who is standing there with a devious look on his face. JAMISON looks up scared but then is pushed out of the way by OZ as OZ grabs KNOX by the back of the neck and picks him up and slams him back down face first. JAMISON and ROSA both see this and back completly away from OZ as he picks KNOX up again by the waist and slams him back down with a gutwrench suplex. JAMISON looks over at ROSE and notices ROSA is fully focused on OZ and KNOX. JAMISON takes a step back and out of nowhere nails ROSA with a superkick. The superkick hits with such force that ROSA goes flying over the top rope. SH: There goes Morlock's old drinking buddy as Higor Rosa is eliminated! WJ: Well, looks like they got a little more time to put back a few now dont they! [All of the sudden from the entrance ramp you can see a masked man holding a baseball bat. The masked man runs down to ringside and slides in the ring and out of nowhere nails Johnny Thunder. Thunder goes flying over the top rope and is eliminated. The masked man slides out of the ring and nails Thunder a few more times with the bat before taking off his mask and revealing that he is JOE DOSINGER!] SH: It is DOSINGER! I knew he was here for a reason tonight. And it looks like Johnny Thunder was the reason. He came out and attacked Thunder not once but twice with that bat costing him two seperate matches. WJ: I dont really know the guy too well, but I like him already. [Inside the ring JAMISON has shouched himself into the corner. OZ and KNOX are still going at it. All of the sudden out of nowhere OZ picks up KNOX with a sidewalk slam position and slams him all the way down to the mat over the top rope. OZ does not look content with just an elimination and OZ also eliminates himself by hoping over the top rope and landing on KNOX with a flying leg drop.] WJ: What a damn idiot. He eliminated the man he wanted but then eliminated himself? Some people really need help. SH: I totally agree and I believe you are one of them. OZ didnt come in to win the title. He came in for revenge and he is getting it right now as he and Knox are battling all the way back to the locker room. WJ: Anyways, we are now down to five. Trapp, Studd, Hart, Harris and Jamison. And I am still standing by what I said. Jamison is taking it all. [HART and HARRIS are still battling around by the ropes. HARRIS has hit HART with move after move but HART will not go out of the ring. HARRIS nails HART once again with a vicious clothesline. HART hits the mat hard and HARRIS scoops him up once again and puts him over his shoulder with his head facing the ropes. HARRIS drops HART down on the top rope. HARRIS laughs to himself for a moment as HART is on the mat holding his neck. Out of nowhere TRAPP comes flying through the air with a cross body block. Somehow HARRIS see's it coming and catches him and nails him with a shoulder breaker. All of the sudden as this is happening JAMISON and STUDD somehow get entangled in exchanging lefts and rights. All of the sudden out of nowhere JAMISON nails STUDD with a vicious clothesline sending STUDD over the top, almost at the same time though on the other side of the ring HARRIS scoops TRAPP up and suplex's him over the top rope.] WJ: DAMN! Trapp and Studd eliminated at the same time on different sides of the ring. SH: Well, looks like we are down to the final three. Hart, Harris and Jamison. [Back in the ring HARRIS takes a breather as JAMISON moves in on the fallen HART! JAMISON scoops up HART and is trying to push him over the top rope HART is struggling and fighting with all his might but finally goes over the top. JAMISON puts his hands in the air to assume victory when he is met with a superkick to the back of the head and goes over the top by HARRIS!] SH: JAMISON AND HART ARE GONE! HARRIS WINS! HARRIS WINS!!!!! WJ: Lucky ass man! If Jamison would have turned around he would have killed Harris! SH: But he didnt and HARRIS WINS! HARRIS IS THE MPW DES MOINES CITY CHAMPION. [All of the sudden from the entrance ramp (sic) by SlipKnot begins as Kenneth Morlock and Rob Loney walk out onto the entrance ramp. Rob Loney has the MPW Des Moines City title drapped over his shoulder as both men walk down to the ring. Both men enter the ring and Kenneth Morlock quickly shakes the hand of Stone Harris. Loney pulls the title off of his shoulder and hands it to Harris and extends his hand. Harris shakes the hand of Loney and holds the title high over his head. Harris continues to celebrate as Morlock and Loney both exit the ring and leave the aisle way. All of the sudden as this happens Shawn Jamison slides back in the ring and taps Harris on the back. Harris turns around and see's Jamison with his hand extended. Harris looks weary as he puts his hand in Jamison's. Jamison raises both there hands and Harris raises his title once again. All of the sudden out of nowhere Jamison nails Harris with a clothesline. Harris hits the mat and Jamison picks up the title and lays it flat on the mat. Jamison picks up Harris and nails him back down onto the title with a vicious DDT!] SH: OH MY GOD! THAT IS OUR NEW CHAMPION BEING DEMOLISHED THERE! HE CANT DO THAT! WJ: Oh yes we can, but folks we are out of time. Tune in next week for R&R! [Camera cuts out as Jamison stands over Harris in the ring]
