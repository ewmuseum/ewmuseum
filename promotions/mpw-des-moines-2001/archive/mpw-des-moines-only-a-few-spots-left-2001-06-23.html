The Application ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ INTRODUCTION ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ MPW is a fed that has been masterminded by none other than [name withheld] and [name withheld]. (Did I just say Mastermind and mention those two names?). Right now this is a project that is all on our hands. We have worked out all the little rough edges and I believe we are ready to set this bad boy in motion. MPW, Mid-West Pro Wrestling will be a federation based out of the Mid-West(Of Course), with it's headquarters in Des Moines, Iowa. Yes, this is an indy type fed. For now at least, maybe as we get older and better we will move it up but for now it is Indy. MPW will travel to Iowa, Illinois, Missouri, Wisconsin, Minnesota and Nebraska. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ EXPECTATIONS ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ The only actual thing we expect from the people that join MPW is for them to have a good time here. We don't want to make this a hard ass fed to join. We want this to be a fed where guys and gals can join and compete for fun. But yes, we are going to expect RP's and yes we are gonna expect everyone to have an open mind when it comes to angles and other things. But other than that, like I said. It is a game and we will have some fun! ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ WEEKLY WORKINGS ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ 3.1.Trash Talkin (flash packet) Role-plays will be sent out in packs . Which means there will be one (1) per week. This does not mean you have to role-play once a week. You are allowed up to two (2) role-plays per packet, which gives time to hype up feuds and stories it also allows you to build your character. 3.2. R&R~! This is the MPW weekly show, following the same concept of most other weekly shows this will be where titles are decided and feuds built. Impact will happen on Thursday of every week. It is a two hour show. It will contain between, 6 and 8 matches. 3.4. MEGA EVENT The Mega Event's or PPV's as some would call them are as followed... Jan - Glory Daze March - Conspiracy May - Mayhem July - World Coming Down September - Master of Puppets December - Countdown to Extinction - Battle Bowl for number one contendership. The Mega Events will be placed at dates that the presidancy requires on each of these months. 3.5. WEEKLY UPDATES Weekly updates are sent directly after every event. Updates include the latest happenings and going-ons in the federation, the rankings, the latest card, and schedule. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ MPW SHINY STUFF ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ In MPW there are only two title. Both titles are prestigious enough in themselves. MPW Regional Title: This title is for the absolute best. The hardest worker need only apply for this title. If you hold this title you can proudly say that you are one, if not _THE_ best this federation has to offer. This title is usually defended at least once a month. MPW Des Moines City Title: This title is second to none. If you win this belt you can consider yourself among the best. Winning this belt also puts you in a situation that is like none other because this belt will be defended on EVERY single show that MPW puts on including IMPACT. So if you can win this belt be prepared for an uphill battle to keep it! A Lightweight title and a Tag Team title will both be in the works as soon as both divisions fill up. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ THE SUITS AND WORKERS OF MPW ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ 5.1. MPW President: ---Rob Loney--- Rob Loney is a younger guy that is known to have a big mouth. On ocassion he's known to run his mouth a little to much and get himself into a little bit of trouble. Will he open his trap and piss off the up coming roster of the MPW? Or will he sit in his office and kiss the asses of each and every member? Who knows, but he'll for damn sure make the MPW the fed of all feds! MPW Vice-President: --Kenneth Morlock-- Kenneth Morlock has again decided to step into another federation. But this time it will not be to wrestle or to get gold, but this time to oversee wrestling and to hand out the gold. Kenneth Morlock has went both good and bad in his wrestling career. How will he help rule the MPW? With an Iron Fist or with his tail tucked firmly between his legs? Only time and MPW will tell. 5.2 MPW Announcers - -Willie Jones - The heel announcer of the MPW. Has no regard for anything or anyone. He is a man that will say whatever is on his mind. He is an ex-wrestler who never really got a chance to make it to the big time because he broke his leg and had to have major surgery on it. He still walks with a cane and has trouble getting up fast - Stony Harple - Stony is the face announcer of the two man crew. Stony tries to be a nice guy to everyone but can honestly say that he hates Willie Jones. Stony was also an ex-wrestler the black lightweight superstar was an absolute legend in this same region and is still well known around the mid-west region. Backstage Interviewer - "Wacky" Reefer Jacksom - He is an older man, in his mid- to late 40's. He got caught up in all the movements in the 60's and 70's and has never been the same since. Many times he will ask questions so off the wall that no one can believe he was even hired. MPW Ring Announcer: -Julie Stewart- Julie is a beautiful young women. She couldn't be a day over twenty-one. She has always wanted to be in the wrestling business but decided young that wrestling herself wasnt a great idea so she decided to take up an offer given to her by Kenneth Morlock. REFEREES -Joe Mamma- Senior MPW Referee. He is in the majority of the matches and all of the title matches and Main Event matches. -Angie Daddie- A rookie referee here in MPW. She still tends to make rookie mistake just like any newcomer to any field. Seldom will she ever get the Main Event matches or title matches. Usually works undercard matches and dark matches.
