Let me be the first to say thank you all for making this possible! And be the first to welcome you all here to the very first R&R! You are either gonna love them and you are gonna hate them, but one thing you will do is listen to them! Making his way down to the announcers table first, he is from right here in Des Moines IOWA! Jones is also using his wooden cane to help him walk to the announcer table. The huge Jones has a devious smile as he begins his slow walk to the announcer table. Jones makes it to the announcers table and sets his cane to the side of the table as he puts on his headset and sits down.] SH: It is about time you got here. [Trapp looks out over the crowd and the mixed reaction he's gotten so far, Trapp grins indifferent to the response] Trapp: So here in the MPW...The Wicked Wonder himself...Kendell Trapp will leave a permeant mark on the world of wrestling, once again...this time in... the state of Idaho!! [motions to a decent looking country girl in the front row]...and when it's all said and done, we're gonna be rollin' around in the hay [winks]... ["Just Got Wicked" by Cold blasts over the PA system as Trapp ducks out of the ring and heads back up the aisleway with his arms raised in the air to the mixed reaction of the crowd!] SH: Some harsh words for the other three participants of the four way battle tonight by "Wicked" Kednall Trapp! Jake is wearing his normal attire as well: a pair of white camouflage pants, a black trenchcoat, and black combat boots. Ding!] SH: Wow, Willie, the excitement in this room is captivating! It's giving me goosebumps... you can feel it, too, right? WJ: Let's not go there, Stony... please, just get on with the match. [Johnny Thunder starts things off by going right after the shorter Nick Hart. Thunder lunges forward to grab Hart by the head, but Hart ducks down underneath the long arms of Johnny Thunder. Hart backs up, jumping slightly like a schoolboy who just caught a game-winning touchdown... taunting his opponent... trying to cause trouble. Thunder doesn't let it get to him, though, and charges Hart once more... and once more Nick shifts out of the way. This time, however, Nick takes advantage of Johnny Thunder's mistake -- Hart grabs Thunder by his neck, leaps over the shoulder of Thunder and pulls down the big man, face first onto the mat. Thunder's head bounces off of the mat as Hart lands on his rear.] SH: Nick Hart, showing some strategy in this match. WJ: Strategy? That's not strategy... it's natural. Maybe _that's_ where your parents went wrong. SH: There's no need to bring my parents into the conversation, Willie. [Hart pulls himself back up... looks at the crowd and... smiles? There it is -- that cocky, "I'm better than you" attitude that Hart plays so well. But sometimes, just sometimes... when you mess with the bull, you get the horns...] WJ: Look at this guy, who's he think he is? [Obviously, Johnny Thunder thinks the same thing... the bigger man grabs Hart by the back of his hair, spins him around and nails a huge clothesline! Thunder gets to his knees and wraps his large hands around the throat of Nick Hart. The referee asks for Thunder to release... and after a few seconds, and numerous pleads, Johnny lets go of Nick Hart. The camera shifts towards the ring side where Jake McRae is fuming at the referee for letting Thunder choke Hart for that long. Meanwhile, Thunder takes advantage of the referee being distracted... Nick Hart lays on the mat, hands covering his throat... Thunder pulls Hart to his feet, scoops and slams him. Nick arcs his back in pain as Thunder reels his right leg... and slams it on Hart's nuts... not once... not twice... but three excruciating times. Everyone in the crowd -- at least the male portion -- gives out a painful moan in unison. The referee now turns around, the cries of injustice spewing from the mouth of Jake McRae fall on deaf ears. Nick Hary catches his breath, spots Thunder wobbling, rebounds off the ropes behind him, leaps into the air like a cat leaping from one ledge to another... and nails Thunder with a shoulder block that rocks the ring! Crowd pop!] SH: Wow! Did you see that? The ring was absolutely shaken apart, Willie Jones. Tonight, Nick Hart is proving that he has _heart_, no pun intended. SH: He is trying desperately to reach that level of excellence here tonight, Willie. [Following the shoulder block, Nick Hart yanks at the right leg of Johnny Thunder... possible Freudian slip?... Hart pushes the leg upright and then unleashes a series of kicks to the underside of Thunder's knee. One after the other, the kicks do their job... they add just a little bit more of discomfort to Thunder. With each blow, Thunder's face cringes, feeling the tendons expand and contract... feeling the muscles bruise. Nick Hart, not one to give up on an obvious advantage, stops the kicks to Johnny Thunder's knee... Still holding Thunder's leg in the air, Hart steps over the injured leg, crosses Thunder's long limbs, and lays down on the mat, pulling... appling pressure to the knee that he recently, brutally attacked...] WJ: Figure-four leg-lock... whoo... this is getting exciting. SH: You don't have these two in the ring and expect to see an out-and-out brawl. They are technical wizards, mastering the art of the arm bar and the headlock. WJ: Maybe, but they can't master the art of buy-rate, Stony-ony. [Hart releases the figure-four, backing up, appreciating what he has done. Hart takes in everything: the crowd... Johnny Thunder's pain-filled body... Jake McRae's cheers... and then something happens. Hart climbs the turnbuckles. Nick Hart stands atop the turnbuckles like a man reaching the aex of a mountain, so proud and so full of life... all the while, Jake McRae yells "Get down from there, boy! You have no business up there!" Indeed, McRae know what he is talking about. Being a former wrestler himself, he has seen it too many times... he's about to see it again. Thunder begins to pull himself together, fighting through the pain to stand up to go at it again with Nick Hart. Meanwhile, Nick Hart continues to stand atop the turnbuckle, staring into the crowd. Crowd pop!] SH: Nick Hart has just come down the hard way! [Hart leans over and falls down onto the mat, laying in the fetal position. Hart is yelling in pain as is Jake McRae, who is almost feeling the exact pain that Nick Hart is... the noise level continue to raise as the referee asks Nick if he quits...] WJ: Come on now, the guy ain't gonna quit. Johnny Thunder fights the pain in his right knee in hope that Nick Hart feels just a little bit more pain than he.
