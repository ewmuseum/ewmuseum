[As Studd slides under the ropes, Jensen is on him quickly as he keeps him down with several stiff boot shots to the head. Studd continues to try and get to his feet, but "The Barroom Hero" nails him with another stiff boot shot that keeps him down. Jensen then grabs a hold of Studd as he pulls him onto his feet, turns and fires him into the far ropes. As Studd comes back through, Jensen nails him with a big boot shot to the face as Studd hits the mat hard. "The Barroom Hero" yells out and says "Bootylicious my arse" as the crowd boos. Jensen grabs a hold of Studd by the back of the neck as he pulls him back up and then scoops him up and drops him down across his knee with a shoulder breaker as Studd rolls to the mat in obvious pain as Jensen stays down on a knee and glares out into the crowd. Jensen wastes a good ten seconds and then goes for the lateral press on Studd... Harple: 1...2..no ! Studd kicks out. Jones: Aubry wasting a little too much time there Stony. Harple: He's got to go for the pin any time he has the opportunity. [Aubry glares up at the ref as he grabs a hold of Romeo by the head and then pounds on him with a series of right hands to the head before releasing him back to the mat. "The Barroom Hero" then gets back on his feet as the crowd continues to boo and he tells someone in the front you to "sit down fat ass". Jensen then grabs Studd by the back of the hair and pulls him up to his feet. Then Jensen hooks the Romeo's tights and keeps him in a vertical suplex for a few seconds then sends him crashing down. Aubry takes a second to recover and then leans back for another pin attempt...] Jones:1...2...no ! Romeo gets his shoulder up once more ["The Barroom Hero" glares at Angie Daddie as he holds up two fingers and "The Barroom Hero" holds up just one. Aubry grabs a hold of Romeo as he pulls him back onto his feet and then leads him towards the ropes as he sends Romeo over the top rope and down hard to the floor as the fans boos once again. "The Barroom Hero" quickly steps out to the ring apron and then hops down to the floor near Romeo.] Harple: This is more like it Jones, if Jensen wants to make a name for himself he's going to have to earn it. [Jensen grabs a hold of Studd as he pulls him up, but Studd responds as he nails "The Barroom Hero" with a pair of punches to the gut that release Studd from Jensen' grasp. Studd then lands a low blow that leaves "The Barroom Hero" doubled over in pain as Studd works back onto his feet. Studd then pounds Jensen with a stiff jab to the chops and then a hard right to the head. Studd then grabs Jensen, turns and shoots him into the ring steps as Jensen hits shoulder-first and remains down on the floor. Studd wastes little time as he heads to the corner and pulls a steel chair away from the security barrier. Jensen remains down on the floor as he tries to shake out the effects of hitting the ring steps. Studd waits as "The Barroom Hero" slowly works up to a knee as Studd then tosses the chair in front of his head and hits it with a drop kick as the chair clangs off the head of Jensen who goes back down to the floor. Studd gets back on his feet as he grabs the chair and then places it down over the head of "The Barroom Hero" and then drops down onto the chair with a leg drop as the crowd cheers. Studd rolls off and grabs the chair once more as he returns to his feet and then blasts Jensen across the chest with a chair shot as Jensen curls up in pain.] Harple: Jensen looks like he's about ready to puke. Jones: Wow, Studd must have hit him with all he had… Harple: No, I think it's due to the fact that Jensen no longer has a blood alcohol level…but an alcohol blood level. Jones: He's never met a beer he didn't like. In fact he looks like he just came from one. [Romeo slides the chair under the bottom rope and then turns his attention back to Jensen as he pulls him up off the floor. Romeo jams a knee lift into the gut of "The Barroom Hero" and then rolls him back into the ring. "The Barroom Hero" is slowly getting up to a knee in the ring as Romeo heads to the ring apron as he hops up onto it and grabs a hold of the top ropes as he waits patiently. Jensen is now back up on his feet as he stumbles and then turns to face the ropes as Romeo then executes a sunset flip as he rolls Jensen up for the cover...] Jones: 1...2...no ! [Jensen nails Romeo with a double kick to the sides of his head to break the pinning combo. "The Barroom Hero" rolls over to his right as he works back up to a knee as Romeo does the same. Both men are back on their feet as Romeo swings and misses with a punch as Jensen then hooks him from behind as he lifts Romeo up and drops him back with a belly-to-back suplex. Romeo holds his lower back as Jensen sits up and takes a quick breather. "The Barroom Hero" slowly gets back on his feet and then heads for the chair which is now near the corner.] Jones: Maybe he's going to take a seat before he does the old technicolor yawn. Harple: The room has got to be spinning for that man. He probably couldn't even pass a sobriety test and say his ABCs. Jones: Stony, I don't think he could in the first place. Harple: You leave the jokes to ME! Alright pip squeak? [He picks it up and waits as Romeo begins pulling himself up along the side ropes with his back to Jensen. As Romeo turns around, "The Barroom Hero" strikes with a shot from the blunt end of the chair into the gut and then another as Romeo doubles over in pain. Jensen then heads to the middle of the ring as he unfolds the chair and sets it up. He then heads back over to Romeo as he pulls him away from the side ropes and then whips him to the front side of the ring. As Romeo comes back through, Jensen sends Romeo face-first down onto the chair with a drop toe hold as the crowd reacts to the bump as Romeo goes down hard holding his face.] Harple: That'll clear your sinuses ! Jones: And again the new vicious attitude of "The Barroom Hero" coming into play as he may now have the Bootylicious one on the ropes ! [Aubry then scrambles as he makes the lateral press on Romeo ...] Jones:1...2..no ! Romeo gets a shoulder up as Aubry shows signs of frustration on his face. ["The Barroom Hero" grabs Romeo by the hair as he pulls him back onto his feet and shoves him back-first into the near corner. Aubry goes in to follow up, but Romeo begins fighting back with a back elbow shot to the chops and then another as Aubry stumbles back holding his mouth. Romeo then waits and as "The Barroom Hero" turns, he drops him with a clothesline. Romeo rolls over and gets back on his feet as Aubry takes a bit longer to get back to his. As he does, Romeo heads in after "The Barroom Hero" and wraps his left arm around the head of Aubry as he floats behind him and then puts him into a sleeper hold as Aubry begins to flail his arms out in front of his body. Romeo grimaces as he grinds the hold on as "The Barroom Hero" fires in with a back elbow shot to the gut and then another. Romeo releases Aubry from the sleeper as "The Barroom Hero" heads into the opposite side ropes and then comes back in after Romeo who ducks under a wild clothesline. Aubry continues into the near ropes and Romeo scoops him up as he comes off and drives him down to the mat with a Tilt-a-Whirl Slam as the crowd responds. Romeo makes a quick cover... Harple: 1...2..thr...no ! "The Barroom Hero" kicks out! Jones: Near fall for Studd, Jensen still has something left in his tank ! Harple: The only tank he has is the drunk tank. [Studd gets back up on his feet as he points out towards the table as the crowd pops. Studd then grabs a hold of Aubry as he pulls him up off the mat. "The Barroom Hero" responds as he nails Studd with a hard right to the head, but Studd answers with a right of his own. Then both men let loose with a flurry of right handed punches to the head of the other. The action breaks as "The Barroom Hero" grabs Studd by the arm and then turns and fires him into the opposite side ropes. As Studd comes off the ropes, Aubry crushes him with a double axe handle blow to the forehead as Studd goes down to the mat. Studd is quick to recover as he gets right back up and catches the right foot of "The Barroom Hero" as he went to deliver a kick to the gut. Studd spins Aubry around and clubs him with a clothesline that pins "The Barroom Hero" up against the side ropes. Studd then backs up and does a little Sugar Ray Leonard rope-a-dope and then works over "The Barroom Hero" with a flurry of punches to the gut. These punches push Jensen back but suddenly he comes out of nowhere and nails Studd with a hard lariat. Romeo Studd stumbles back into the ropes.The crowd errupts as Kendell Trapp runs down the aisle and leaps up onto the apron! Studd turns around and Trapps grabs him by the back of the head and drops down to the floor clothesling him across the top! Trapp laughs at Studd and throws his arms up into the air and looks out to the crowd and drops back against the guardrail as the fans slap him on the back!] WJ: I knew it was just a matter of time before he came out here and stuck his nose in this match. SH: That may have been the major break Jensen needed to take the match. [Jensen is standing talking to the ref as Trapp slides into the ring under the bottom rope and waits on Studd to turn around again, as he does Trapp hoists him up into the air and delivers the WICKEDSHOT over the top rope! Studd grabs his throat trying to catch his breath as Trapp rolls under the bottom rope and cockily jogs back up the aisle throwing in an occasional high 5. Jensen see's Trapp leave and lays on top of him for the cover....1........2......333333!!!] SH: Jensen has yet another win here in MPW! He beat Harris last week and he beat Studd this week. He is on a major roll. WJ: The damn drunk only got that win because Trapp helped him. I hate drunks!! SH: Looks like we are going to have another interuption here... (The lights at the top of the entryway dim as the camera pans across the fan-filled floor from its press box location.) (Suddenly, the opening chords of Alice In Chains' "I Know Something" play over the loudspeakers. Just as suddenly as the lights went out, they flash back on as "I Know Something" explodes over the loudspeakers. With new entrance music and his bodyguard, Jake McRae in tow, Nick Hart appears at the top of the entryway with his fists thrust up into the air. White fireworks explode on either side of Nick as he and Jake McRae make their way to the ring.) (It's quite obvious to the booing crowd that Nick's match isn't this early in the card. It's obvious to him, too. He's wearing a pair of black Adidas sweatpants, a white sleeveless shirt, and a pair of white crosstrainers. Jake, of course, is wearing the same thing he always wears: his black trenchcoat. The two stroll down to the ring area with smug looks on their faces, somehow succeeding to irritate the crowd.) (The two enter the ring finally, where Nick climbs a turnbuckle and begins to play to a less-than-grateful crowd. As Nick's doing this, Jake is standig towards the edge of the ring, arms folded. Nick hops off the turnbuckle, and catches a mic that was thrown from ringside. With that, he opens his mouth and says...) NICK HART - Now, it's come to my attention that I, Nick Hart, will be going up for the Des Moines City Title against the current champion, Stone Harris... (Cheers from the crowd.) NICK HART - What you people don't seem to realize is how big an accomplishment this is for me. It's only my second week as an MPW wrestler and I'm already vying for the only title in the land...Even you people from this backwater state can understand how cool that is! (Boos from the crowd.) NICK HART - I can only attribute my success to one thing: my performance in the ring...Anyone who watched me wrestle Johnny Thunder last week will be able to tell you how I was in control of the entire match... (More boos from the crowd.) NICK HART - I mean, did anyone who watched the show actually think that Johnny Thunder could beat me...me; Nick Hart; the Hart Stopper; the Pop Topper; the Emperor of Excellence? (More boos from the crowd.) NICK HART - I didn't think so...But I need to tell you people right now, that tonight will go down without a hitch just like last it did last week. Stone Harris stands no chance of defeating me in this very ring! You people will get a show tonight, a show you won't soon forget! Tonight will be the night that Nick Hart claims his first title ever in his MPW career! (Nick raises his hands only to recieve more boos from the crowd.) NICK HART - I promise you all that before the night is through, you will see the Des Moines City Title wrapped around my waist. In fact, I *GUARUNTEE* that you will see the Des Moines City Title wrapped around my waist before the night is through. Last week at the battle royal, I only lost because Jake wasn't there to coach me, right Jake? (Nick looks over to Jake who nods affirmatively.) NICK HART - Tonight will be different, there will be no flukes this time! This time, I'll win that title even if it kills Stone Harris! And that is final! (With that, Nick throws down the mic and he and Jake exit the ring area. "I Know Something" by Alice In Chains picks up where it left off and blares over the loudspeakers as the two leave.) SH: Looks like Nick Hart has pretty much guarunteed that he is going home with the gold tonight! WJ: I believe him, and I also believe we have to cut out for some more commercials right here but stay tuned Knox and Jamison and Hart and Harris still left tonight. ---[cut to commercials]--- ---[Cut to Part 4]---
