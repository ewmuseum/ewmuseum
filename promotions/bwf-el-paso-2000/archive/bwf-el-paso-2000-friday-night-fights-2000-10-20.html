BWF (El Paso, 2000) — card

BWF: Friday Night Fights

20 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WEPT Channel 33 Your Hometown Station El Paso - Las Cruces NEXT: BWF FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS 12:00 AM - The Cindy Margolis Show 1:00 AM - Battle Dome 2:00 AM - Coach (fade) Double D Entertainment In cooperation with Channel 33 WEPT And University of Texas - El Paso (UTEP) Public Access Television PRESENT: (Trumpets flare as scenes of a checkered BWF past plays across the screen. Images of EPCW appear in black and white interspersed with images of Terrence Goldworthy, Jimmy Cougar, Jason Starr, and so many more. A graphic of the BWF Heavyweight Title hovers into hovers into view)
The Best Wrestling Federation PRESENTS FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS (The lights come up on a ring surrounded by bleachers with approximately 75 fans in attendance. Against the far wall behind the ring, a huge BWF TEXAS banner hangs reading BWF WELCOMES YOU TO FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS. Bobby Ladd stands at center ring holding a microphone in a blue blazer and khakis)
Bobby: Welcome wrestling fans to the inaugural edition of BWF FRIDAY NIGHT
FIGHTS from the studios of El Paso's Hometown Station, WEPT Channel 33!! I am your host, Bobby Ladd, and I'll be calling the action for you from here at ringside. And what an impressive show we've got for you tonight!!
Tonight you will witness on your television screens, the crowning of a new
BWF Heavyweight Champion and new Tag Team Champions as well!! Before we go to our first matchup here tonight, I want to take you back to some video tape submitted by one of the participants in tonight's heavyweight title tournament.
(fade) (Scene opens to a dingy basement. The light is out of focus, and as it focuses in, you can hear the sound of nails screeching on a chalkboard. The familiar cackling of Adam Kuhl can be heard, as the camera focuses in on the One Man Crime Spree. He's dressed in black jeans, and a cut of black shirt, with white letters that read, simply, BWF.)
Adam Kuhl: I'm back! And this time, I am going for the one thing that's eluded me my entire career. Dennis Windham, you wanna talk about how you never got to carry the torch for this federation for so many years?
Well, I've been around since the beginning, buddy, since even BEFORE the beginning! I've been the top guy in this company on several occasions, but never, NEVER, did I get the chance to hold the World title. Well, that ends
NOW. I don't care if it's not a "world" title, it's the BWF title, and it means more to me than just about anything! So, Windham, Barnes, or whoemever else wants to take it from me, just try and beat me!
(fade - As the camera returns to the studio, we see Bobby has moved over to a set featuring the BWF logo and a podium)
Bobby: It would appear, fans, that Kuhl has a serious ax to grind with the likes of one Dennis Windham. However, ladies and gentlemen, we're going to take you now to highlights of the first round matches that were taped earlier this evening and we'll see that tonight will not be Kuhl's night to take the title.
Match #1 - Dewey vs Lance Nelson Bobby: Fans, we have to apologize from the get go, for our first round substitution. We realized that Dewey was advertised to matchup against
Lance Nelson. But apparently the AA poster child fell off the wagon and we were forced to make a move. In against Lance is an impressive rookie by the name of Desmond Walker. Walker actually debuted early last spring in the
Winnipeg-based BWF, working under a mask. A CFL athlete for the Winnipeg
Bombers, Walker came into this match with something to prove. And so did the returning Journeyman. However, Nelson, who's confidence seemed to be seriously hurting, could not establish control in the early minutes of the match. As you can see, Walker had Nelson sinched in to a variation of a head/shoulder lock when the Journeyman went to the ropes. However, the rookie reversed sending Nelson in for the ride. Walker executes a picture perfect tilt-a-whirl backbreaker drop to send Nelson to the mat. The former
BWF champion stumbles back to a vertical base to again receive the Irish
Whip. Nelson off the farside ropes and into a spinning back fist by Walker.
Walker then to the mat attempts the pin for the upset, but only gets a two count. Walker now discussing it with the referee and Nelson, the ring
veteran now with a small package for the three count! And a very winded and shaking Lance Nelson advances to the semi-finals. Winner: Lance Nelson @ 8:11 by pinfall Match #2 - Adam Kuhl vs Chaz Darby Bobby: Our second first round match featured the One Man Crime Spree taking on Chaz Darby. After the earlier comments, we all expected an extremely focused Adam Kuhl to take the battle to Darby. Well Kuhl took it to Darby alright, but he was far from focused. Despite a suicide plancha onto Darby on the outside, Kuhl never got to ply his trade as he shoved the referee into the turnbuckle for the immediate disqualification, giving Darby the easy ride into the second round.
Winner: Chaz Darby @ 3:22 by Disqualification Match #3 - The Steve vs Dennis Windham Bobby: Fans, as you can see from the footage, this one quickly degenerated into a slobberknocker. The former BWF Champion, The Steve, appeared to be under the assumption this was a wrestling match. Windham, however, had mistaken it for a hardcore match as he attacked the Steve from the get go
with anything he could get his hands on. The Steve seemed to have the crowd's support, as they clearly remembered him from his "seasoning" days down in the LSWA. But Windham carried the battle outside, testing the referee's 10 count and his patience as he worked over the Steve on the guard
rails outside. The Steve managed to get in a couple of stiff shots as he realized his title chances were dwindling fast, but Windham was a house on fire with a score to settle, a debt to pay, and something to prove. Windham finishes off the Steve as he pulls back the protective mats at ringside and executes a vicious piledriver. As emergency medical technicians attend to
Barnes, Windham advances to the semifinals. Following that match...
WINNER: Dennis Windham @ 8:34 by Pinfall (camera cuts to the side to see Dennis Windham emerging from the back...) Bobby: Ladies and gentlemen, Dennis Windham, now joining us here in the studio. What can we do for you, Mr. Main Event?
Windham: Well Bobby Ladd, I've got alot on my mind. And it's time too talk. I've been quiet for too long. Bobby: What's on your mind? Windham: Betrayal. Injustice. Bobby: Might I say common themes in many of your better dissertations. And why is that? Windham: Who was the last North American champion of the BWF, Bobby? Bobby: Well, technically, you..... Windham: Who beat me for it, Bobby? Bobby: Uh, No one to my recollection Dennis. Windham: Exactly...let me ask...who was the last BWF World champion? Bobby: Well..again, that'd be you. Windham: And, again...who beat me for the title? Bobby: Uh..well..no one. Windham: Exactly..If I get that..and you get that...why can't the ******** running these companies get that? I'm sick to death of busting my *** day in day out and getting screwed like this..
Bobby: I can see your point... Windham: I mean, for Gods sake man..now I have to start over in a town I've already conquered..it's a vicious cycle. And further more, they don't even acknowledge there MARQUEE man..no, Instead I hear this and that about Lance
Nelson, about Jimmy Cougar, about Jason Starr...I'VE BEAT THEM ALL, BOBBY!
When will I be given CREDIT? What exactly DO I HAVE TO DO? I've already run
Starr and Cougar out of the business..Nelson is a shell of what he used to be..and Dennis Windham is STILL STANDING...but hell man, you wouldn't know it if you turned on BWF television on channel 932 or picked up PWI
Magazine..you'd think I'd be sippin' on margaritas in Mexico with those gimps I just mentioned. It's sick man. And, now...I have to sit here and watch Adam "One Man Jobbing Spree" Kuhl come on TV and cry like a little baby about not getting his just desserts. WAH WAH Adam...if you actually worked in the ring you MIGHT be given a shot...Your not half the wrestler or an ounce of the man I AM Kuhl. You didn't get the job done tonight, son, so it won't happen today. But we can settle this one way..and I think you know how..so button up your baby talk and come on down to do the studios, and
I'll teach ya all about the food chain........
(Windham storms away from the set) Bobby: Fans, we'll be right back. (commercial) Bobby: Fans, we're gonna go to our first semi-final heavyweight title match in just moments. But before we do, let's run down the events that occurred in our last first round matchup.
Match #4 - Star Spangled Kid vs Matt Meyers Bobby: This match was a real let down in my opinion. Meyers was showing clear indications of ring rust as he went in against the Kid in his first match out of "retirement". Meyers "Kid Rock" act was laughable and drew serious heat from the crowd and the local boy, SS Kid, was able to capitalize. However, as you can see later in the match, both men are winded and the normally airborne Kid is using rest holds to regain his wind. Both these men suffering from two much tag team time in the ring over the last year. Here we see the Kid now regaining his footing and coming back with a series of precision drop kicks and he takes Meyers out to take the win with the Star Spangled Press.
Winner: Star Spangled Kid @ 9:16 by Pinfall Bobby: Which brings us, wrestling fans, to the here and now. We've got a full house and these fans are ready for a new heavyweight champion. During the break, our cameras caught up with one of the competitors advancing to the second round.
(Dickinson is shown talking to Nelson as he towels off the sweat and checks his boots) Nelson: I don't care!! I can't beat Darby!! I almost got taken out by some rookie I've never even heard of! If Dewey had of showed up, he probably would have pinned me!! I don't have the edge anymore!
Dickinson: That was just warm up! This is your big opportunity!! You can go out their and prove you still belong here! You two put on some of the greatest matches in history!! HEY GET THAT CAMERA OUT OF HERE!!
(fade back to the ring) Match #5 - Chaz Darby vs "The Journeyman" Lance Nelson (The "crowd is chanting "Nelson" as the ring announcer climbs in) Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, our next match is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first from Trenton, NJ, weighing in at
282 lbs, the former BWF World Heavyweight Champion, Chaz Darby!! And his opponent, from Mud Creek, TX weighing in at 278 lbs, the former longest reigning BWF World Champion and former LSWA Heavyweight Champion, "The
Journeyman" Lance Nelson!!
(ding ding) (Highlights of the match are phased in over Bobby's commentary) Bobby: Fans, this was another page out of the wrestling history books. The match opened with an impressive display of genuine mat wrestling that many thought was dead and gone. The feel of the match quickly shifted as each man attempted to out brawl the other. Both men suffered from repeated impact into guard rail and turnbuckle. Near fall followed near fall with neither man being able to seal the deal. As the ten minute time limit loomed, Nelson began repeatedly trying for his big finishers. He seemed to have difficulty hefting Darby up, but managed to execute the Journeyman
Edge. He then followed it up with a Nelson Arch and set up for the figure four. Darby reversed it and Nelson quickly broke the hold. Desperate for the win, Nelson mounted the turnbuckle slowly and attempted a moonsault!!!
But by this time, Darby has managed to get to his feet and Nelson plummeted into a Sudden Impact superkick! Darby covered for the 1-2-3. As you can see, a crushed and tearful Nelson was led from the ring by BWF officials. I wonder if we've seen the last of the Journeyman. Let's take a commercial break.
Winner: Chaz Darby @ 8:49 by Pinfall (commercial) Bobby: Welcome back, fans. We've still got much more BWF action to come including a double main event with a four corners match to crown new tag team champions and the finals of the heavyweight title tournament. But first, let's take you back to our second semi-final matchup.
Match #6 - The Star Spangled Kid vs "Mr. Main Event" Dennis Windham Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, our next match is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first from Washington, DC weighing in at
225 lbs, the Star Spangled Kid. And his opponent, from Sweetwater, TX, weighing in at 272 lbs, here is "Mr. Main Event" Dennis Windham.
Bobby: In what has to be considered unfair to the youngster, the Star
Spangled Kid barely got a chance to compete for the Heavyweight Title. The match was in its second minute with the Kid making pretty decent in roads when Adam Kuhl appeared and attacked Windham giving him the disqualification win. It would seem that Kuhl is as unpredictable as ever. Kuhl seemed quite content to bludgeon Windham into a blood mess, but security personnel quickly put a stop to it, but the damage had already been done. The SS Kid eliminated in the semifinals.
Winner: Dennis Windham @ 3:01 by Disqualification Bobby: I've got a feeing that we're in for a heated feud between Kuhl and
Windham regardless if there's a belt on the line. Who would have thought that a year ago these two would be in a position to main event the BWF!!
But that's a matter for another time. Our next match is ready to go and we are minutes from crowning new TAG TEAM Champions!!
Match #7 - Mexicans vs Pony Express vs Solid Gold vs Chaos, Inc. Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, our next match is set for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first from Tijuana, Mexico, weighing in at a combined weight of 515 lbs, the team of El Burrito Hombre and El Gringo
Macho!! Next from New York City, weighing in at combined weight of 502 lbs, here is Solid Gold!! From Winnipeg, MB, weighing in at 454 lbs, the team of
Dewey & Chip Darling. And finally, from the State Mental Hospital, the team of Blackdog Manson and Mr. Happy!!
DING DING Bobby: Fans, this match was beyond bizarre. What started out as a four corner tag match ended up a strange clogomeration of a handicap and single match. As we announced earlier, Dewey was in no shape to participate. And before the match, Candyman, aka Travis King, of Solid Gold was arrested for violation of his parole!! This left Darling and more or less teaming with
Kid Sexy!! The powerhouse Mexican team really focused on the deranged pair as the match seemed to have lost all rule of law. Meanwhile, Darling and
Kid Sexy seemed to be discussing how best to work in the situation!! In the end, they were all but ignored!! After El Burrito and Mr. Happy had bludgeoned themselves to near inconsciousness, Happy actually fell into Kid
Sexy for the tag!! Chip was more than happy to let Kid Sexy do the hard work, but the tandem seamed in line as Sexy draped Happy across his knee allowing Chip to execute a very poor Winnipeg Jam. Before El Gringo and
Blackdog could react, Chip covered and we had new tag team champions!!
However as a stunned Sexy and Chip celebrated, the ring was stormed by none other than Dylan Moore, Max Jackson, Dex Blacke, and a 350 lb monster known as Cruz. They annihilated the new tag champions and spat on them with disgust before leaving the ring area!! The Mysfits have arrived and made
clear their intentions, but the question remains...What about Dewey? Stay tuned to this situation. Fans, when the Friday Night Fights return, the finals of the Heavyweight Championship tournament. Stay tuned!!
(commercial) Bobby: Welcome back, fans. Now it's time for the moment we've all been waiting for. The match to crown a new BWF Heavyweight Champion! Let's go to the ring! Match #8 - Chaz Darby vs Dennis Windham - Heavyweight Title Match Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the Friday Night Fights Main
Event!! This is for the BWF Heavyweight Championship. Introducing first making his way to the ring from Trenton, NJ weighing in at 283 lbs, Chaz
Darby!! And his opponent, weighing in at 272 lbs, Dennis Windham!
Bobby: Fans, as you can see, both men going into this match very tired. Both have already wrestled twice earlier in the evening to advance. But clearly going in with the advantage is Darby. His first match with Kuhl had ended early while Windham had not only fought a hardcore battle with the
Steve, but had been beated down by Kuhl in his second match. But both men proved why they were ring veterans. This match was for twenty minute time limit and it seemed like both men were tempted to carry the other one to legendary status. Darby attempted every offensive in the book, with Windham proving his mastery of mat wrestling by answering with every counter hold
possible. Darby seemed to get more and more agitated as the match progressed and from my perspective seemed to be taking liberties with his opponent. At one point, Darby came off the ropes and hit Windham with a swinging neckbreaker that appeared to snap Mr. Main Event's neck a little too far. An incensed Windham got to his feet and began swinging his
trademark haymakers in an attempt at restitution. At this point, in slow motion, you can see Darby remove something from his boot and gouge the forehead of Windham. As the blood poured into Windham's eyes, he nailed
Darby with a boot to the midsection and followed it up with an elbow to the
back of his neck. Windham now takes to the ropes and drops a knee again into the neck. At this point Windham picks up the foreign object, apparently some sort of pin knife and begins raking it across the forehead of Darby!!! With both men bloodied, the ref seemed torn between calling for a double DQ and awarding a no contest! However, with the title belt up for
grabs, the match continued. Darby then gets a boot into the nether regions below the belt sending Windham to the mat. Darby now climbing to that top turnbuckle. And he let's fly with a savage elbow drop!! But Windham rolls out of the way. Both men now fighting exhaustion and blood loss to get to
their feet. Darby makes it there first! Darby with a European upper cut and Windham is all but knocked out. Darby now playing to the crowd as he sets up for the Sudden Impact but Windham playing dead as Darby turns around
INTO THE TEXAS TWO STEP!!! Reverse Russian Leg Sweep!!! Windham covers!!
1-2-3!!! WINDHAM WIN THE BELT!!! WINDHAM IS THE BEST WRESTLING FEDERATION
CHAMPION!!! And now Windham's family is here to celebrate with him! His wife and three week old daughter celebrate the just coronation of Mr. Main
Event!! Ladies and gentlemen, we are all out of time!! Tune in next week for another edition of Friday Night Fights! For the BWF, I am Bobby Ladd!!
(a split screen shows Windham celebrating while another shows a bloodied and angered Darby confronting Dickinson in the back.)
Darby: How dare you!! I was supposed to win that belt!! That was part of the deal!! Dickinson: There was no deal!! You're just ****** because you got beat by the BWF Heavyweight Champion! Now get out of my television studio!!
Darby: With pleasure. (The camera follows a sweating and bleeding Darby into the night as we fade to black)
