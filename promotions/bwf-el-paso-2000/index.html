Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

By its own account the promotion had been running under one name or another for five years by the middle of 2000, its current branding dating to a relaunch on 28 December 1998. Its cards moved between a fan-run pay-per-view calendar and Sunday-night house shows, opening the year with a four-part "Love Thy Neighbor" broadcast and building through the spring toward a self-declared crown jewel, "Seven Deadly Sins."

The spring's own paperwork tells one story: a company boss thought gone for good returns backstage mid-broadcast, seizes control, and installs a new ownership pairing over the objection of the fed's own president, Dwain Dickinson, before selling the company out from under its own roster. A hardcore champion, Adam Kuhl, keeps coming up short of the one title that has eluded him despite having "been the top guy in this company on several occasions," and a heavyweight named Dennis Windham carries the in-ring end of the same power struggle from house show to house show.

By October the promotion had left the page behind for a real timeslot, broadcasting "Friday Night Fights" out of a public-access studio in El Paso, Texas, in partnership with a local university station. Its opening episode looked back at its own earlier eras in black and white before crowning a heavyweight champion out of an eight-man tournament held that same night.

The held record breaks off in November 2000 with a plain recruitment call: three titles standing, a roster the promotion itself called thin, and an open invitation for wrestlers with a regional style. No closing notice survives to say how the story ended.

The record breaks off in 2000 and resumes in August 2001 with a broadcast framed as a return from time off the air: the same announcer, the same authority figure and the same production credits carry over. A weekly show and a pay-per-view cycle follow across six weeks, crowning inaugural and new champions, turning two of the Journeyman's own brothers against him, and ending Meltdown 2001 with a title change and the founding of a new stable around a masked ally and a returning veteran.
