Now among the many top stars here in the CWAA whom the Masked Man has provoked has been Marc Thomas, Jak Hammer's primary ally here in the CWAA. Is Jak Hammer with Marc Thomas? Is he with the Masked Man? Or is he playing his own mind games, if you will, with the top contenders? Lawson, so I speculated that the Masked Man may be an operative of the TV Champ and the Major- but now he appears to be aligned with Jak Hammer- perhaps Lawson's main threat. It just gets more complicated every week! In the meantime, Jak Hammer has just helped the Masked Man to defeat the CWAA Heavyweight Champion for the title belt. (EL produces a paper from an envelope in his jacket inside-pocket) EL: "The AEWA maintains a high standard for the defense of its championships, and demands full compliance with the rules and regulations surrounding the defense of AEWA-sanctioned championships in AEWA-sanctioned matches. The AEWA as a very clearly-defined set of rules and regulations surrounding the defense of the CWAA Heavyweight Championship. At the conclusion of each calendar month, the AEWA announces a date and challenger for a mandatory CWAA Heavyweight Championship defense. Any additional CWAA Heavyweight Championship contests are at the incumbent champion's discretion, but are subject to the rules and regulations regarding the AEWA's sanctioning procedures. The AEWA had announced, in accordance with its policies, that on Sunday, June 15th, 1997, Max Robertson would face Marc Thomas for the CWAA Heavyweight Championship. Max Robertson chose not to face Marc Thomas, and Marc Thomas failed to appear on hearing that Max Robertson would not meet him. A substitute opponent was procured without sufficient notice by the AEWA, and his agreement to participate in the match was not arranged within the bounds of the AEWA's policies. Hence, the AEWA takes the following actions, effective immediately: 1) the unnamed opponent who defeated Max Robertson on June 15th was not recognized by the AEWA as a challenger for the CWAA Heavyweight Title, and was therefore ineligible to obtain the CWAA Heavyweight Championship 2) the contract signed by the unnamed opponent who defeated Max Robertson on June 15th did not meet the standards of the AEWA. The opponent did not sign his own name, and therefore the contract was invalid, and therefore the match was not sanctioned by the AEWA and the subsequent title change is null and void 3) Max Robertson failed to meet the requirements of the AEWA's champions by failing to defend the title against the officially-designated challenger named by the AEWA, and therefore has disqualified himself as CWAA Heavyweight Champion 4) Marc Thomas has failed to fulfill his duties as AEWA-designated challenger for the CWAA Heavyweight Championship, and therefore has disqualified himself as CWAA Heavyweight Champion. In summary: The match between the so-called Masked Man and Max Robertson on June 15th, 1997, was not officially sanctioned by the AEWA, and the CWAA Heavyweight Title did not change hands. Max Robertson has forfeited his rights as CWAA Heavyweight Champion. Marc Thomas does not win the forfeited title, as he has forfeited his rights as number one contender. Therefore, the CWAA Heavyweight Championship has been declared vacant, and a procedure for declaring a new CWAA Heavyweight Champion will be forthcoming."
