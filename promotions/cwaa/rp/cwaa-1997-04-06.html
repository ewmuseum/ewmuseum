So far Max is playing to his strengths rather than trying to neutralize Sheridan's. Max works bet on the mat, and he is obviously looking to wear down Sheradin's arm strength to maximize the impact of a double chickenwing later in this match. Another wrestler might want to work on the Dreamer's legs to take away the champ's aerial game, but I guess as long as Max keeps him down on the mat in a hammerlock, then Sheridan's aerial strategy is not an issue anyway. (Sheridan strains to break free, and reached out with his left foot to reach the bottom rope.) So far, neither man has gone for a real high-impact weapon, opting instead to grind the opponent down. In a way that will work to Sheradin's advantage- Max picks him back up- Irish whip- spinebuster slam! 1... 2... kickout! In a way, it is to Sheradin's advantage that the pace remain slow- he has proven to have more staying power than Max, who cannot match Sheradin's ability to wrestler a longer match.
