CWAA — Roleplay

CWAA, 26 January 1997 (Part 1 of 3, author redraft posted 28 Jan)

26 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eric Lane: Welcome everyone to this, out first edition of Championship Wrestling Association of America, coming to you from the world-famous Sportatorium in Dallas, Texas. I understand there may be some talent scouts from the AEWA here to see the CWAA's youngsters in action, and here's what they'll be watching this evening. Former bouncer Josh Barnes squares off against Healer's Havoc. "The Preacher" Kore Wrench is in action tonight, facing Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson. Joey Haven tells us he'll be bringing the so-called "Hardcore" style from up in Philadelphia to Jak Hammer. The team of Twister will be here, facing a mystery team. And in our feature bout, Max "The Professional" Robertson, takes on my guest at this time, "The Dreamer", Randy Sheridan. (Enter Sheridan) (Crowd pops as "Dream On" by Aerosmith and the Philadelphia Symphony plays. The 170 pound Randy Sheridan makes his way to ringside, exchanging high five's and handshakes with fans. At ringside, he shakes hands with Lane.) EL: Welcome to the CWAA, Randy. How does it feel to be wrestling your first ever feature match? Randy Sheridan: It's great, Eric. I'm not really much of a veteran, since I only made my pro debut over the summer, but I've already felt a title around my waist, and I'm looking forward to trying to become the CWAA champion. EL: How does the CWAA compare to some of the bigger organizations you've been in? RS: I love it. My first ever match was in a big organization against a big star named Robski. (Crowd boos - they've heard of him.) I got my butt kicked. Here in the CWAA, we all start off even, and we all start off hungry. We're young, and here you'll see the talent get a chance to shine, rather then being thrown to the wolves. EL: Your nickname is "The Dreamer." Could you tell us where that name came from? RS: Nobody thought I could do it. My friends, my family, the other guys in wrestling school, they thought I was too small, not talented enough, whatever. They told me I was dreaming if I thought I could make it as a professional wrestler. Well, I know that dreams come true, because mine did. So I'm proud to be called the Dreamer, because it shows what I had to overcome to get to the ring. That's why my big move is called the Dream Come True, and I hope to use it to put away MaxRobertson. EL: Which brings me to my final question. What do you know about "The Professional" Max Robertson? RS: Not much, Eric. But I'll find out tonight, just like he'll find out about me. And if he's anything less than a total professional tonight, he'll get beat. I've got a real good record against people who take me lightly. EL: (Shaking Sheridan's hand) Good luck tonight, Randy. He's looking to be his mark in the CWAA, and we'll see what he can do later tonight. (Exit Sheridan) EL: But before then, we've got four exciting matches, and when we come back after this break, Joshua Barnes and Healer's Havoc will launch the CWAA's inaugural event. Back in a few... (The historic first commercial break offers the audience the opportunity to squander their hard-earned cash on psychic hotlines, used cars (local cable stations for the indy fed), quality legal representation, and the quicker- picker-upper.) (The 6'5" 292 pound former Bloomsburg, IN bouncer, Joshua Barnes comes down the aisle, self-consciously adjusting his glasses, which slide down his noise, lubricated by the nervous perspiration, no doubt. He wears long black jeans and an emerald-green ring jacket. As he removes his jacket, an "IU" (Indiana University) tattoo is revealed.) EL: While we wait for Healer's Havoc to arrive, I'll get a few words from Mr. Joshua Barnes. Joshua...? (Barnes looks around nervously, whispering to the man behind the camera, "now?". Apparently getting an affirmative answer, the man removes his wire-rimmed glasses, wipes the sweat from his short black hair.) EL: Joshua, your professional debut and the CWAA's debut, as well, will begin in a scant few moments. Any thoughts? Joshua Barnes: Ummm....Hi there. I'm Josh Barnes, and I've just joined up...signed up with the CWAA. I'm never wrestled before- at least, not in the ring. But I was a bouncer at the Country & Western Bar over in Fort Wayne and this wrestler came in, and told me that I could get a better job as a wrestler. And then the bar was shut down, and I was out of work... So I came down here, and now I'm in. Anyway, I'm really looking forward to getting into the wrestling ring, and competing against the other wrestlers soon. So...(pauses, uncertain of what to say) Anyway, thanks for listening, and I hope to see everyone soon. Bye. EL: He seems a little nervous, folks, and in his situation, who wouldn't be. Well, I can see Healer's Havoc coming to the ring now, and- eww! He... he doesn't seem to have..any teeth. A big gum-filled feral grin covers his face, and...is that a dog collar he's wearing? coming behind him is, I assume, Mr. Tip Smith, Havoc's manager. (From the aisle, Healer's Havoc could be heard to holler:) HH: I'm very pleased to be in the CWAA. I have one thing to say Joshua Barnes I'm going to beat you within an inch of your life! (HH then picks up a chair and bangs it on the floor a few times.) EL: Well, that was...interesting. Havoc is no small man, but Barnes looks to have at least 30 pounds on him, despite their similar heights. There's the bell, and the CWAA has officially started its operations. (Which makes this a good time for another commercial break.) EL: Barnes is handing his glasses to our lovely ring attendant, and Healer's Havoc comes running at him with a double sledge in the back. Another, and another, and he has Barnes up against the ropes and punches away at his back and ribs. Now he whips Barnes into the ropes, and Havoc catches him in a sleeper, but it's a bit early, and Barnes escapes and slips behind him with a hammerlock, but Havoc legs it for the ropes, and Barnes has to release the hold. The ref calls for a clean break, and Joshua Barnes backs off carefully. Tip Smith appears upset about something, but from here I can't quite make it out. so far, this has been an even matchup between a couple of unpolished powerhouses, and we'll be seeing a variety of different styles here tonight- Barnes has Havoc in an armbar now, and really is wrenching it on- Jak Hammer is another big powerhouse we'll be seeing, while I understand Lt. Lawson is more of a mat technician, and our feature match will showcase a pair of young high flyers. Barnes whips Havoc into the ropes, clothesline! Havoc on his back, and Barnes heads to the corner and gets down in a three-point stance, I think I know what's coming here...Havoc staggers back to his feet, and Barnes knocks him right back down with a tackle that could have gotten him into the IU football program had he not opted for collegiate wrestling. Barnes pulls him back up by those long black locks, sends him back into the ropes, and spinebuster slam! 1..2..hooks the leg...3! That's it! Joshua Barnes over Healer's Havoc in our first match ever, and the crowd sounds quite pleased. They've really taken to the shy rookie, who accepts his little wire-rimmed glasses from the ring attendant, and, in all honesty, seems a little embarrassed by the attention he's getting as he leaves the ring. Meanwhile, Healer's Havoc does not look at all pleased with the results, but he'll get another chance in due time here in the CWAA.
