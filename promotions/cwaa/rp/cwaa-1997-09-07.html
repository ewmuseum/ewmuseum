Last Time, on Championship Wrestling: Crimson Joe Reed (to Max Robertson): One day you'll learn that an important part of becoming a world champion is overcoming your mistakes. But I've got to be honest, overestimating yourself like this and presuming to dictate to the World Champion and the AEWA are mistakes you're going to find it real tough to overcome. I'll see you in 2 weeks. **************************************************************************** * Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome back to another exciting week of Championship Wrestling! We’re back in the world-famous Sportatorium in beautiful downtown Dallas, Texas, and if you missed our broadcasts from Reunion Arena, you missed an awful lot. We have Six Man Tag Team champions now, and they are none other than Joshua Barnes, Randy Sheradin and Kore Wrench! The Trailer Park Assassins split the grand prize in the big over-the-top-rope battle royal! We have new- and old- CWAA Tag Team Champions, as Twister, with their old friend Larry Allen, exacted revenge on Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson. And the crowd was thrilled by the spectacle of AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Crimson Joe Reed, and CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Marc Thomas, battling for over an hour, until Joe Reed emerged the victor with his patented Kentucky Backsmasher. But his celebration was interrupted by Television champion, Max Robertson, who along with his preferred partners in crime, Lawson and Sherrick, accused Reed of having ducked Robertson when HE held the Heavyweight Title- and tonight no one ducks anyone, as Reed and Robertson go one on one for the biggest prize of them all- the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion! On a more somber note, the AEWA and CWAA officials met this week, and the AEWA Board has announced that Joshua Barnes has been suspended for 30 days from today for striking referee Chris Herman. Joshua is a popular young star, someone whom I have always held in the highest regard, and I’m sure he is very remorseful for his actions, but laying hands on a referee just will not be condoned under any circumstances, and I’m sad to say that it will be one month before we see young Joshua back in the squared circle. Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer were set to face Barnes and Randy Sheradin- we’ll talk about that in a few moments. Elektro and Sgt. Mark Sherrick will meet in one-on-one action, and, as I mentioned, the special feature match pits CWAA TV Champ Max Robertson against AEWA World Champ Crimson Joe Reed.
