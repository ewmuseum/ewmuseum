Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America Eric Lane: And the Major on the outside just crotched Joshua Barnes with that flagpole! Wow! This is not Joshua's day- he got that steel flagpole- well, you saw where he got it. Meanwhile, a 2 count there for Sheridan- after that blow, I almost missed a near pin in the ring! Lawson now rolls to the floor- right over by Barnes and the Major- and now Lt. Lawson kicking at the fallen Barnes! Chris Herman has to put an end to this! Scuffle on the floor- and look out! (Boos as the masked man dressed in black races to the ring and slides in under the bottom rope behind Sheridan) EL: That masked man is back again! What is he doing- hey! He just kicked the Dreamer! ********************************************************************** Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome back for another exciting week of CWAA action, coming at you here from the world-famous Sportatorium in beautiful downtown Dallas, Texas. We have three- count 'em- three big title matches on tap tonight, beg- wait a second. (The crowd boos as Major Victor Augustus Mayhem, military strategist extraordinaire and manager of CWAA Television Champion Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson, heads to the ring.) EL: What in tarnation is he doing out here now? His own charge isn't due to be out here until near the end of the program- and where is the Lt., anyway? Well, let's see what's going on here... (The Major walks by Eric Lane without even acknowledging his presence- already for Lane this is a pretty good week- and heads to the ring, where he summons ring announcer David Myers to hand over the house mike.) (Points to a front-row fan) MM: For your intolerable insubordination, drop and give me 20! (The booing increases, and the Major plows on, despite the additional insubordination of the ringsider who shows no signs of ever getting out of his seat to give the Major 20 of anything.) And I have a message for a Mr. Marc Thomas. (The crowd boos again at the mention of Thomas' name.) MM: Thomas, more than any other opponent of the Lieutenant- (By the way, Thomas has never opposed Lawson, but the Major has his own version of the truth for just about any subject.) MM: -you have demonstrated the aptitude and attitude to succeed in our organization. Now I can't make any promises, but someone like you could go far- EL: I wish they'd both go far. So, son, come on in here- (Thomas smiles at Hammer and tells him to wait before heading to the ring.) MM: -all I would need would be for you to sign this enrollment form, right here. (Thomas climbs into the ring.) EL: OK- what exactly is going on here? Is Marc Thomas going to align himself with Mayhem and Lawson? What about Jak Hammer? MM: (Who can see his Lt. stomping away at Jak Hammer's legs, having slammed the flagpole down on Hammer's leg) That's right. Now I know it sounds to good to be true- MT: (Taking back the mike, still unaware of what's going on behind him) Let me see this. (Snatches the form from the Major's hands, and looks it over quickly. I don't know what's going on around here- Jak Hammer is set to face the Lt. for that TV title later this evening- OK, I can see Hammer up on his feet, and he and Thomas look livid. Well, couldn't happen to a nicer guy. Like I was trying to say before all this started- oh, now what?
