Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America Max Robertson: I'm tell you if you'll SHUT UP! During last week, I thought, and I thought - how can we decide this? After all, I only have the needs of the federation at heart (he sneers), and then it came to me. We're going to have a little fun. Two matches. The first, a tag-team match - these guys are all tag-team partners, so that shouldn't be a problem - but, instead of their normal partners, everyone is going to switch: Sheridan and Hammer on one side, and Thomas and Barnes on the other. Then, after Sheridan and Hammer beat Thomas and Barnes - no disrespect of Thomas intended, but any team with that other idiot is bound to lose - then Sheridan and Hammer wrestle, and the winner has the right to get beat up by me during a CWAA Heavyweight title match. David Myers: Now wait a minute, what makes you think these people will accept... MR: And if they refuse my terms, I'm going to give the next title shot to the Masked Man, and they'll have to wait another four weeks for another chance. ********************************************************************** Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome to another exciting week of CWAA action, here in the world-famous Sportatorium in the heart of beautiful downtown Dallas, Texas. As you just saw, CWAA Heavyweight Champion Max "the Professional" Robertson has announced that he will defend his title only against the winner of a tag team match which would see enemies aligned against friends. By the end of tonight, we hope to hear from the parties involved as to whether or not they'll accept this extraordinary challenge. In the meantime, the CWAA and the AEWA continue to insist that Robertson must face Marc Thomas, and that the match must take place right here, next week, for the CWAA Title. We are just seven short days away from that confrontation, and Robertson adamantly insists that if he doesn't get his way, he'll be here all right- to take on the mysterious Masked Man who has terrorized the CWAA for this past month. All this, and some big announcements about the upcoming King of E-Wrestling Tournament, the next big AEWA Pay Per View extravaganza, Battle Klash II, and, of course, the situation surrounding the CWAA.
