Twister (Paul "Tornado" Langford and Travis Conley) (0-2) The month in review: The biggest development is clearly the start of the CWAA Heavyweight Title Tournament, and we are just weeks away from crowning out first title holder. Within 2 weeks of the end of the tourney, an 8-team single elimination tournament for the CWAA Tag Team Titles will take place. Both titles must be defended once every 30 days against a number-one contender named by the CWAA; champions may defend their titles more frequently against contenders of their choosing. More title news- the AEWA Light Heavyweight Championship will be defended in the Sportatorium on March 23rd against CWAA contender to be announced. A CWAA Television Title will be created in the coming months, and will be defended every week thereafter. This will be the last CWAA Title created. The Jak Hammer-Joey Haven feud has escalated quickly and steadily. In their first ever encounter, Jak Hammer ddted Haven on the arena floor to win by count-out; in a post-match altercation, Joey Haven assaulted Hammer with a chair. A rematch was a no-contest as Hammer accosted Haven on his way to the ring, and only the intervention of Twister forestalled additional damage. A no-disqualification and no-countout match was won by Jak Hammer, both men exited with injuries which hampered their performances in the first round of the title tournament. Negotiations are underway to sign a steel cage match between the two, after each has had at least a week to recover from the damage suffered on February 16. The alliance between Randy Sheradin, Joshua Barnes and Max Robertson has had some serious tests lately. Robertson's attempts to neutralize the double team tactics of Double Jeopardy caused Barnes to suffer a disqyualification loss to Gary Jackson- a loss which prevented a Barnes-Robertson match in the second round; in their only prior meeting, Robertson defeated Barnes by submission, but refused to release the double chickenwing submission after the match had ended. The chickenwing was also applied following that earlier match to Randy Sheradin- a reconcilation between Sheradin and Robertson was effected by Kore Wrench. After Sheradin and Robertson made their peace, the relationship between Barnes and Sheradin began to show signs of strain when allegations were made which cast Randy Sheradin's integrity in doubt. Sheradin has been alleged by an anonymous source to have bribed officials in the past in an attempt to receive an ill-gotten tag team title elsewhere. Since Sheradin's resume lists the ACWF Tag Team Title as his main accomplishment, the CWAA has established contact with the ACWF, and the ACWF has begun an investigation into the matter. When the CWAA opened, there were only two tag teams, Double Jeopardy and Twister, who have crossed paths on several occasions now, with Twister getting the better hand in solo encounters and Doule Jeopardy's savvy use of foreign objects, notably a loaded mask in Eddie Intellecta's case and an ankle brace worn by Gary Jackson, earning the wins in their tag team matches. Healer's Havoc and BD Brown have also entered the tag team ranks and seem to have been drawn into a series of scuffles with Double Jeopardy as well, scoring submission wins in a tag team match and in a first round match between Healer's Havoc and Eddie Intellecta. Mark Sherrick burst on the scene by rummaging through a supply room and producing a dented chair which he insists was used as a foreign object in the match which led to his father's premature demise. Sherrick has vowed to exact his revenge on today's wrestlers, and debuted with a victory over BD Brown in the first round of the title tournament. Injury Reports: Stan "The Powerhouse" Stevens is recovering nicely from what was feared to be a hamstring strain following an attack by Lt. Lawson; Stevens is not expected to miss time. Jak Hammer has seriously bruised ribs, and will not see action this week in order to prepare for upcoming battles with Joey Haven. Joey Haven has 4 stitches in his forehead for a laceration suffered in a match with Jak Hammer. The injury will not cause him to miss any action beyond this coming week. Major Victor Augustus Mayhem claims to be suffering from a sore larynx following a chokehold by Stan Stevens. Mayhem refuses to submit his alleged inury to the scrutiny of medical personnel. Gary Jackson continues to wear an ankle brace for undisclosed problems with his ankle. Like Mayhem, Jackson refuses to seek treatment or provide documentation substantiating his claim. And he sometimes wears the brace on his right ankle, sometimes on his left. -kl
