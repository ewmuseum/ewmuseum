CWAA: Established Regional Fed Has Roster Vacancies

20 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Special Announcement from the AEWA In January, 1997, approximately 1 year after the AEWA was conceived and launched, the AEWA launched its new regional federation, the Championship Wrestling Association of America. The CWAA is a booked federation hosting one card per week. AEWA Champions make title defenses against CWAA contenders, and CWAA headliners display their skills in front of AEWA audiences on AEWA Pay Per Views and free for alls. The CWAA brings you its action every Sunday from the Sportatorium in Dallas, former home of World Class Championship Wrestling. The CWAA roster is kept very small, and all who are willing to roleplay will have ample opportunity for airtime. Now that you know the general idea, here are the specifics: 1. The CWAA roster was initially open only to AEWA handlers, but roster spots are now open to anyone who submits an acceptable application form. You may send any 2 wrestlers or 1 tag team as long as they are not currently wrestling in the AEWA. AEWA wrestlers are not encouraged to leave the AEWA for the CWAA. The CWAA, with its indy fed feel and pipeline to the AEWA via title opportunities and ppv appearances is an ideal setting for you to experiment with new character ideas, or for wrestlers of yours who have built careers around indy feds or have found it hard to get a break in the bigger organizations. World champions may feel out of place; rookies are encouraged to get their feet wet in front of our small but enthusiastic crowds and by meeting opponents with a similar level of experience. 2. Every Wednesday by 10:00 PM Eastern Time, the lineup for the following Sunday is announced- often the lineup is announced Tuesday nights. The lineup will vary in length from 4 to 8 matches (5 is the norm), and one match will be designated as the main event. Sunday nights, by 10:00 PM Eastern Time, our weekly program, titled ÒChampionship Wrestling Association of AmericaÓ, will appear in your mail box. Your host will be Eric Lane (no heel-friendly color commentator is expected to join the CWAA just yet- these small-time feds have a limited budget!), and he will conduct all interviews conducted in the ringside area or locker room for the benefit of the viewing audience; in-ring interviews on the house mike are conducted by ring announcer David Myers. Flashes from remote locations will on occasion be aired on this program; there will be no separate program roleplay program. 3. The creation form can be obtained by writing [name withheld] at [detail withheld] . 4. The CWAA Heavyweight and CWAA Tag Team Championships must be defended once every 30 days, and the contender and defense date will be chosen by the CWAA Championship Committee. The CWAA Television Title is is defended on each and every edition of Championship Wrestling Association of America, with contenders to be chosen at times by the committee and at times by the reigning title holder. 5. Roleplays are to be submitted to [name withheld] for distribution as part of the Sunday evening Championship Wrestling Association of America program, and are not to be sent to the mailing list from any players. Host Eric Lane and ring announcer David Myers conduct all interviews as described above. Any wrestler who fails to roleplay for a prolonged period of time will find himself relegated to opening matches and padding the records of more consistent roleplayers.. 6. All matches are booked. Decisions are made on the following criteria: quality roleplaying (which player does the best work), storyline development (which result makes more sense at the time), creation sheet comparison (which wrestler matches up best against the other skill- and attribute-wise). Which of the above factors carries most weight varies from match to match. To avoid conflicts of interest, the CWAA booker does not have wrestlers of his own. 7. The roster will never exceed 30 wrestlers at a given time, and has hovered around the 20 mark since it opened. Admittance to the CWAA will initially be on a first-come, first-served basis. ÒFirst-comeÓ means the first wrestlers for whom a completed and acceptable creation sheet has been submitted. 8. At the end of each calendar month, a special CWAA report is sent out. It lists the previous monthÕs match results, rankings for CWAA Titles, the announcement of the Championship CommitteeÕs #1 contenders, and a recap of major storylines. Look here for announcements of AEWA Championships to be defended on CWAA programs and for CWAA wrestlers who will appear on AEWA pay per views/free for alls. If anyone has any questions, feel free to contact [name withheld] at [detail withheld] The above outline does not describe what I hope to develop in the CWAA- it describes what already does happen consistently in the CWAA. In the three months the federation has been open, one-third of one card went out as much as a day late- the vast majority of cards are sent between 6 PM and 10 PM eastern time on Sunday evenings. The CWAA has good working relationships with its mother-federeation, the AEWA (2 CWAA matches have appeared on CWAA events, and 2 AEWA World Titles have been scheduled to be defended on CWAA programs against CWAA competitors). Wrestlers from the AEWA, FWA, and ACWF have appeared on our programming, as well as one independent tag team not affiliated with any organization in particular, and CWAA matches have aired on FWA and ACWF pay per view events. The CWAA seeks to recreate the feel of an early-mid 80s regional organization- we have no pay per view events, there is one card per week, and the majority of wrestlers have no gimmick. -kl
