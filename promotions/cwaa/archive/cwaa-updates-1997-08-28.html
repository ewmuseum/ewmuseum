CWAA Updates (the August gap recap)

28 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A hard drive fatality in CWAA Headquarters threw us for a loop for a couple of weeks, and the past few cards have not been posted to the ng. For those of you who do read the CWAA cards here, this is a recap of the events of the past few weeks: On the August 17th card in the Dallas Sportatorium: "Maniac" Marco Moura debuted and defeated Curtis Hansen "The Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa defeated Jimmy Dangerous and attacked him after the match with a kendo stick The Alliance of Power (Jak Hammer and CWAA Heavyweight Champin Marc Thomas) defeated Twister (Paul "the Tornado" Langford and Travis Conley) "The Professional" Max Robertson defeated Joshua Barnes for the CWAA Television Championship. Before the match, Max tried to humiliate hi sopponent by using a joy buzzer! Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas double-teamed AEWA World Champion Crimson Joe Reed, and Thomas hit his Icebreaker (Diamond Cutter_ maneuver on Reed twice, "pinning" him after each execution to prove he could beat Reed twoce (as they had a 2 of 3 falls match scheduled for a week later) The team of Lt. Lawson and Sgt. Sherrick defeated The New Sensations for the ACWF Tag Team Championship, despite the fact that the pin was scored by an illegal wrestler on the wrong man On Saturday, August 23rd, the CWAA arrived in Reunion Arena for a two-day all-star event The Six Man Tag Team championship was decided in a single elimination tournament. In the first round, Twister introduced the third memebr of their Forces of Nature team, Casey Spahn, and Mother Nature assumed her real name, Kelly Perich. The trio fell to Lt. Lawson, Sgt. Sherrick and Max "the Professional" Robertson. In other first round action, Joshua Barnes, "the Dreamer" Randy Sheradin and "the Preacher" Kore Wrench defetaed Double Jeopardy (Gary Jackson and Eddie Intellecta) and thier friend from the AEWA, Red Dog. In the finasl. Wrench, Sheradin and Barnes scored the pin over Sherrick, Lawson and Robertson. Dave "the Freak" Johnson defeated Little Yvette in their long-anticipated first meeting A 20-man ove-the-top-rope battle royal was held, for a $25,000 grand prize. "The Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa put on a teriffic display, but his attempts to use his kendo stick on Cooter Lee Roy and Orbit of the Trailer Park Assassin were foiled by Jimmy Dangerous, who helped ensure Tokugawa's elimination. Cooter and Orbit split the prize- literally! They ripped the check in 2 and each left with one half of the grand prize. On Sunday, the card resumed, and ACWF Tag Champs Sherrick and Lawson battled Sheridan and Barnes to a 20-minute time limit draw The CWAA Tag Team Champions (Michael Anderson and Byron McNultey) appeared to retain their titles against Twister, but Travis Conley's old friend, Larry Allen of the Dallas Cowboys, pointed out that Anderson had used the ropes for an illegal leverage advantage. When the champs assualted Allen for exposing them, he belted Anderson to give Twister the advantage. Langford and Conley took the match and became the first-ever 2-time CWAA Tag Team champions. Elektro defeated Dave "the Freak" Johnson with the aid of Little Yvette CWAA Television Champion Max Robertson retained his title when Barnes was disqualified for striking referee Chris Herman. The popular Barnes has been slowly losing control of his rage over the course of the summer, and Robertson appears to have his number at this point. AEWA World Heavyweight Champion Crimson Joe Reed defeated Marc Thomas 2 falls to 1 in a match that lasted over 60 minutes. The end came when Reed kicked out following an Icebreaker and rallied to execute his pateneted Kentucky Backsmasher. The turning point may have been when Senior Official Jeff Howard ejected Thomas' partner Jak Hammer for not possessing a valid manager's license (Hammer had been menacing Reed's manager, the Lady in Red). Following the match, Robertson, Sherrick and Lawson returned to the ring. Robertson cited his previous CWAA Title reign and the fact that a proposed Robertson-Reed match fell through in the spring (he received his AEWA title shot against Heavy Metal, who was more than twice the size of the 200-pound Robertson), and demanded that Reed stop ducking him. Reed pointed out Robertson's relative inexperience and chided him for not paying his dues and for expecting too much too soon. Robertson's needlign convinced Reed to offer a title match September 7th in the Sportatorium to give Max in one night all the pain and sufferinf Reed endured in his decade-long climb to the top. Well, now you're up to date on CWAA goings on- we return to action on Sunday, September 7th. The champions are: AEWA World Heavyweight Champion: Crimson Joe Reed CWAA Heavyweight Champion: Marc Thomas CWAA Television Champion: Max Robertson CWAA Tag Team Champions: Twister (Paul Langford and Travis Conley) ACWF World Tag Team Champions: Lt. Lawson and Sgt. Sherrick CWAA Six Man Tag Team Champions: Joshua Barnes, Randy Sheradin and Kore Wrench Thanks to [name withheld] and [name withheld] for helping to get the cards out to the CWAA members during the CWAA Headquarter's technical difficulties. -kl
