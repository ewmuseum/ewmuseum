(-) Recap of Major Events: All three championships stabilized over the past month, as Max Robertson survived a no-dq/no-count out encounter with former champion and arch-nemesis "Dreamer" Randy Sheridan. TV Champion Lt.Daniel "Lethal" Lawson has been on a roll, moving from 10th to 5th to 2nd in the rankings, scoring a dq win over Randy Sheridan, a submission win over Little Yvette, and taking Jak Hammer, who outweighs him by nearly 100 lb., to a double count-out. The tag team titles were in jeopardy in the board room as well as the ring- Paul Langford needed to obtain a medical relaease in order to be cleared to defend his share of the tag team, and he also was forced by the CWAA to ensure that his face would be sufficiently protected from further injury- behind a catcher's mask! But the most dramatic story of the past month (apart from a cancelled card, but let's not talk about that) was the sudden arrival of a mysterious masked man dressed in black who has silently interfered in several matches, including the Robertson-Sheridan title match, and even sent Marc Thomas to the hospital in an alleged locker room attack not captured on camera. The identity of the individual has been the subject oif much speculation- his interference on behalf of Jak Hammer led some to conslude it was Thomas, but the attack on Thomas himself has dispelled such theories. The attacks on Kore Wrench and Marc Thomas have led others to speculate that Major Mayhem and Lt. Lawson have contratced a hired mercenary. Whoever he is, Kore Wrench, Marc Thomas, Randy Sheridan and Max Robertson would all love to get one chance in the ring face to face with him before things go any further. Twister goes national: the reigning CWAA Tag Team champions have become the CWAA's first export. Max Robertson, Lt. Lawson, Jak Hammer, Joey Haven, and Joshua Barnes have all appeared in CWAA matches at AEWA, FWA, and ACWF events, but Travis Conley and Paul Langford have become the first stars to use their CWAA success as a foundation for a career elsewhere, as they have just been inked to a deal with the UWF. They will continue to compete here in the CWAA, as well. Who's Hot, Who's Not: Hot: Joshua Barnes- the self-effacing ex-bouncer has bounded up the rankings and is no longer seens as just Randy Sheridan's sidekick. Barnes is on the verge of title contention himself, and has even passed his partner in the contender's rankings Not: Mark Sherrick- nothing is wrong with his in-ring performance... except the results. Joshua Barnes and Little Yvette have both pinned him cleanly in the middle of the ring in recent weeks. Hot: Marc Thomas- the new number-one contender has come a long way since being ousted in the first round of the CWAA Title Tournament. And his victory over Randy Sheridan can not have escaped the notice of CWAA Heavyweight Champion Max Robertson. At present, a date for his match with Robertson has not been determined. Not: Randy Sheridan- still the most popular of the CWAA stars, Sheridan failed in two title chances this month, but was not pinned in either contest. The loss to Marc Thomas cost him his number one contender's ranking, however. Roster Changes: In: Little Yvette, who impressed the CWAA so quickly that he already has had one shot at the TV title. Out: Joey Haven, whose feud with Jak Hammer was one of the biggest stories of the CWAA's early months, has left for pastures new. In: Jimmy Dangerous, a high flyer from the Great White North has signed on and will be debuting in June. Injury Report: Paul Langford- broken nose. Not expected to miss any time, but may be required to wear the extra protection for two-three more weeks. Jak Hammer- strained knee. Not expected to miss any action, but may not be 100% for several week.
