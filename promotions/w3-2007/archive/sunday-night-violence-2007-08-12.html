Sunday Night Violence, 12 August 2007

12 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ryan Greede vs. Spade Ryan Greede started the match off with a slap to the face of Spade. Spade didn't appreciate it, and immediately speared Greede, following up with some vicious blows to the face. Greede eventually mounted a comeback, but was shut down with a dragon suplex. With the crowd clearly behind Spade, he picked up Greede and delivered the "Ace of Spades" backbreaker, and pinned him for the 1-2-3. Spade climbed to the top turnbuckle to celebrate as the show cut to a commercial.
~Coming soon to W3~ He is a man without fear... He is a man without pain... He is a man with a burning desire... He is man coming to get you... He is on the top of his game... He is ready for a fight... Get ready for him... Prepare yourself for... Kyle Kaos ~Next Week~ ~On on Sunday Night Violence~ Qualifying Match Pike Andrews vs. Tha Serial Thrilla The next match would be the first of three qualifying matches to determine the other three participants that will join El Novo in a Fatal Four way Match for the W3 Championship next week on Violence. Pike Andrews was in the ring taunting fans at ringside, as Tha Serial Thrilla made his way to the ring. Tha Serial Thrilla received a mixed reaction from the fans, until he started exchanging harsh words with them. Andrews and Thrilla gave their all to come out as the winner, and put on one heck of a show for the fans. Toward the end of the match, Pike Andrews went for a back body drop, but Tha Serial Thrilla countered it, and executed his finisher "Perfection at its best", and picked up the pin fall.
The show takes a break from in-ring action to show the backstage area. The camera shows the name on a certain door and the name plate reads ‘Chris Roman’. The door is shut but a fist comes into frame and knocks on the door. After a few moments of waiting, Chris Roman opens the door.
Chris Roman’s face shows signs of displeasure as the camera backs out to show Cody Clark as the ‘knocker’. Roman now looks shocked, and doesn’t speak. Not for lack of voice, but because Clark beats him to the punch.
Cody Clark: Ah, you are here. Figured you might have left. Clark’s words imply that he and Roman know each other, which is a correct assumption. Roman takes a moment to answer, and also a moment to regain his composure. Within five to eight seconds, Roman is collected and he answers in his usual, arrogant way.
Chris Roman: Yeah, I'm here. I like to oil myself after my matches-what the fuck are doing here?
Roman’s choice words don’t faze Clark, by the look on the Epic Messiah’s face but Clark doesn’t miss a chance to put Roman in line. Or attempt to.
Cody Clark: Don't forget who you're talking to Chris. No need to be so hostile. I want to talk to you about something important.
Clark seems sincere, which is a rare description of him, unless he is talking about his career.
Chris Roman: You aren't my boss anymore Clark, I don't have to watch my tongue with you. But I will, I'm intrigued.
Roman folds his arms and waits, Clark smiles, getting exactly what he wants. He clears his throat and begins...
Cody Clark: Heh, well you still have to watch your tongue. I don't want it anywhere near me. But anyway, I figure you are going to need some back up, here. We both are going to, with the way we run our mouths.
Roman’s expression instantly turns into anger. Roman: You forget Clark, what happened the last time I let you watch my back? You betrayed me. Why should I believe you this time? For all I know, you could come up on it or some shit. There is no way of trusting you in my book.
Without a moment’s hesitation, the Epic Messiah fires back. Cody Clark: Listen up; you came here for a fresh start? Correct? Good. I'm willing to bury the hatchet because we both know when we start taking over this company, people aren't gonna want us around. Do you really want to stand alone against an entire roster of malicious, jealous, relentless....cunts?
Clark knows Roman can’t deny this offer; it makes too much sense for the two of them to watch each others backs. They’ve known each other for a long time, way longer than either of them has known anyone in W3.
Chris Roman: You make a good point Clark. We both know each other better than anyone else here...
Clark drops his guard a little bit, after getting through to Roman. Cody Clark: Word. And I know how it feels to be fucked over by the Iconoclast, if you didn't hear it happened to me too. I'm not apologizing, but consider this an apology. Even though I don't apologize. Whatever, we cool?
Clark extends his hand, waiting for Roman to accept the partnership. Chris Roman: Well, it's never going to be like Iconoclast days. I'm never going to look up to you again, you'll never be my mentor, but I guess I can trust you. Yeah, we're cool.
This sign of mistrust doesn’t faze Clark; he just smiles away, as per usual. The two men shake hands and before Clark goes to walk away, he turns back to Roman...
Cody Clark: I shouldn't be anyone's mentor. I drink too heavily and use the Lord's name in vein.
Roman takes Clark’s word with severe irritation. Chris Roman: God, do I give a fuck about those things? But yeah, you shouldn't be anyone’s mentor for different reasons.
Clark laughs a little bit and continues walking away. As he proceeds down the hall we hear him call back...
Cody Clark: Alright bitch, I'll catch you later. Stay sassy. The scene then fades. Qualifying Match Cody Clark vs. Jesse Reckless This match started off with Cody Clark basically embarrassing Jesse Reckless. After a brief moment, Jesse went to clock Clark, but Clark seen it coming and hit him with a step over toe hold. Jesse Reckless started pulling all sorts of moves out of his arsenal, but Clark seemed to have him scouted, as he reversed almost everything Jesse had to offer. Clark eventually hit "The Big Dirty", when Jesse tried delivering an Irish whip, and pinned him for the win. After the match while Clark was celebrating his win, Jesse grabbed a steel chair from the ringside area, and tried ambushing Clark who seen it coming, and hit him with a Vandaminator.
Television Championship Match Heather Phoenix vs. Serenity ( C ) Heather Phoenix was escorted to the ring by her new "friend", Ryan Greede. Greede sat at the ringside area, and provided color commentary for the match. This match was back and forth throughout the entire match with highflying moves. Phoenix hit Serenity with a DDT, and went to the top rope for a 450 Splash, which was the high point of the match as the crowd couldn't believe it, and started chanting "Holy Sh!T!" Serenity somehow kicked out, and eventually took control of the match. Using desperation moves, Serenity gave Heather a beating. While Serenity was taunting the crowd, she signaled for her finisher. Ryan then reached under the ropes, and grabbed Serenity's foot taking her attention away from Heather. Heather seen this, and came running toward her, and hit her with her signature move, "The Comatose", a spinning heel kick to the face. Heather then picked her up, and gave her the "Firebomb", and pinned Serenity to become the new W3 Television Champion! Ryan Greede jumped into the ring, and hoisted Heather up on his shoulders as Chris Phoenix's music hit. Chris Phoenix walked straight to the ring, with a microphone in his hand. As soon as Chris's music stops, he begins to speak.
Chris: "I am not going to put up with this anymore!" Heather was then handed a microphone from Greede, who had received it from the time keeper.
Heather: "What are you talking about?" Chris: "You know damn well what I'm talking about. Ryan, you can get your ass out of this ring and out of my building because your ass is fired!
Ryan ripped the microphone out of Heather's hand, and yelled back at Chris. Ryan: "What? You can't fire me!" Chris: "I just did! Take him away security!" Heather instantly became angered, and grabbed the microphone again. Heather: "You can't do this Chris!" Chris turned, and exited the ring. The crowd started to cheer as Phoenix's music hit, and security hit the ring to escort Greede from the building. Heather climbed out of the ring, and stomped toward the back as the scene cuts to a commercial.
MAIN EVENT Qualifying Match Billy Boltz vs. Christian Lee The main event of the evening was the best match of the night. Billy Boltz took on Christian Lee in the final qualifying match to determine the participants in next week’s W3 Championship match. Both competitors put on an awesome display of talent, as almost every fan rose to their feet. At one point, Christian Lee had Boltz in the corner, giving him the classic ten punch while the fans were counting out loud, then Boltz stumbled to the mat. Christian then climbed to the top rope, and jumped off with a flying frog splash, but came up short when Boltz rolled out of the way. Boltz quickly rose to his feet, and took advantage of the situation, by delivering vicious boots to the back, and midsection of Lee. Boltz then climbed to the top rope, and delivered a frog splash of his own, but connecting with Lee. Boltz stood in the corner waiting for Lee to climb to his feet, and then signaled for his finisher, “The Blackout”. Once Lee staggered to his feet, Boltz turned around, and executed the springboard back flip reverse DDT, and pinned him for the win. While Boltz was in the ring celebrating, El Novo walked down to the ring to extend his hand in respect. Boltz wanted no part of it, and gave Novo a cheap shot, right to the face. They both started trading vicious left and rights as Cody Clark ran down to the ring to join in on the action. As Cody started in on Boltz, Tha Serial Thrilla ran down to the ring to join in. All four men continue beating on each other, as the W3 security staff storm the ring to stop the insanity as the show comes to an end.
