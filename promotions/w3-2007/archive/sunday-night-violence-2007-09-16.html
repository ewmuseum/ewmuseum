Sunday Night Violence, 16 September 2007

16 September 2007

The camera zooms in on ringside commentators Ian Sparks and James White seated behind a table with the W3 logo on the front.
Ian Sparks: Ladies and gentlemen, we have an awesome show for you tonight on Sunday night Violence. Tonight, the much anticipated debut of Seth “Taurus” Dominics, the question being, how will W3 react now that it has STD.
James White: That’s not all, tonight we will also have two men facing off in a rubber match. Alex Rettop and Shane Tallin have each wone one match, and now the rubber match will be a tables match.
Ian Sparks: That’s right and the main event tonight, the final rematch between Cody Clark and Chris Roman. They face off tonight in a steel cage, and Cody is going to have a hell of a time defending that big gold belt from the worthy adversary of Chris Roman.
James White: Now, without further delay, let’s go to ringside. Justin Davis vs. Kyle Kaos Both competitors walk down to the ring with an air of confidence about them. When the bell rings, they lock up and push each other back and forth for a little bit before Kyle overpowers Davis and pushes him into a corner. The ref tries to break it up but Kaos slaps Davis before backing up. Davis looks pissed and spears Kaos to the ground before leveling a number of punches to his head. The ref makes him get up and the two men circle each other. Davis hit’s a couple of deep arm drags before hitting a flying calf kick on Kaos. Mid way through the match, Kaos stops Davis’ run by poking him in the eyes when the ref isn’t looking. Kaos hit’s a number of different kinds of suplexes, before Davis reverses one and hit’s a belly to belly suplex of his own, flinging Kaos upside down into the turnbuckles. Kaos writhes in pain and grabs his lower back. Davis spends a few minutes working on Kaos’ lower back with elbow drops and stomps, before lifting him up and hitting him with a hard back suplex. Davis hits him with a sidewalk slam, and continues holding onto him as he follows it up with a backbreaker. Davis then puts Kaos in a reverse STO and bridges backwards grabbing under Kaos’ neck before pulling on all the muscles in his body, causing Kaos to tap out.
Winner: Justin Davis by tap out Lacey Quinn vs. Seth Dominics When the bell rings, Quinn lunges forward, but Seth dodges around him and goes for the quick roll up, but only gets two. Quinn jumps up and hit’s a hard lariat on Seth. Quinn stomps him a little bit and then lifts him up, hitting a number of stiff punches to Seth’s face. Quinn goes to punch Seth with a big haymaker, but Seth ducks it and lands some jabs to Quinn’s face. Seth pushes Quinn into the corner and jumps to the second rope, doing the ten count punches as the crowd counts along. Quinn stumbles out of the corner and receives an enziguri from Seth. Seth then grabs Quinn in a front face lock, but it doesn’t take Quinn long to stand to his feet and push Seth into the corner. The ref tries to separate them, and Seth lets go, but as Quinn is backing up, he takes a big swing at Seth, which Seth ducks, causing Quinn to spin around and hit’s the turnbuckle. Seth hits him with a bunch of quick jabs before lifting Quinn in a suplex position, and slamming him forward on top of the turnbuckle. Seth runs up the corner and hits Quinn with a super belly to belly suplex. Seth drops a couple knees on Quinn’s head and uses some leg drops as well. Seth stand in front of Quinn, as he rises to his feet before hitting him with a dropkick to the face, sending Quinn back into the turnbuckle. Seth pushes Quinn to the top rope and follows him before hitting him with a high roller and pinning him for the three.
Winner: Seth “Taurus” Dominics by pinfall Heather Phoenix vs. Jen Blackhart Heather starts out by slapping Jen across the face, which causes Jen to jump on Heather. The two women roll around for a bit, punching and pulling hair as the ref tries to pull them apart. The ref finally separates the two, and they stand up, but Heather quickly spears Jen. Jen rolls out of the ring holding her ribs and Heather tries to go outside, but the ref stops her as she yells at him. After convalescing, Jen rolls back in the ring, and Heather stomps her before she has a chance to stand up. Jen grabs Heathers leg and takes her down, before crawling over her body and leveling a number of punches to her head. Jen stands up and challenges Heather to fight honorably. Heather stands up, and rushes Jen, but Jen hits her with a couple arm drags, and holds onto one of them, turning it into an armbar. Jen holds the armbar for a while, wrenching on it and causing Heather to yell out. Heather scrambles to her feet and grabs the top rope, as the ref forces Jen to break the hold. The two women glare at each other from across the ring and lock up. Heather this Jken with some elbows and pushes her back into the corner. Heather backs up on her own, but then lunges forward with a couple elbows to Jen’s jaw. The ref yells at Heather as Jen climbs to the second rope. Jen leaps off the rope and goes for a cross body, but Heather pulls the ref in front of her, and Jen hits them both, sending everyone to the mat. Jen slowly stand to her feet, as Heather and the ref lay on the mat. The crowd boos loudly as Clark runs down the entrance ramp and slides into the ring. Jen spins around and sees Cody, who hits her with the big dirty, and drapes Heather over her. He wakes the ref and dives out of the ring. The ref takes a moment to come to, and starts to count, but Heather only gets a two. Both women slowly stand up, and start trading right hands back and forth. Heather suddenly tries a step up enziguri. Jen ducks it and immediately locks Heather in an STF. Heather struggles a bit before grabbing the bottom rope, and breaking the hold. Jen stands up, and Heather gets to her feet as well. Jen goes to grab Heather, but gets kicked in the gut, Heather lifts her to her shoulders, and goes for the firebomb, but Jen reverses it and rolls Heather up on a small package and grabs the ropes as the ref counts to three.
Winner by pinfall: Jen Blackheart Heather jumps up in shock as Jen slides out of the ring and quickly makes her way to the back as Heather glares in anger, and Cody slams his hands on the ring apron. Jen stops at the top of the ramp to raise her arms in celebration.
Women's Elimination Match All six women come to the ring, and the bell rings out. Immediately Trinity rolls up Capelli and grabs the ropes as the ref counts to three, eliminating her. Capelli rolls out of the ring with a surprised and angry look on her face as Trintiy waves to her cockily. The other four women engage in a brawl as trinity sits on the top rope, just watching. Angel Connors and Kimmie Kannibal engage in a hair pulling contest, and wander too close to Trinity’s corner, as she leaps off and hits Angle with a flying hurricarana, sending her to the outside. Trinity then focuses on Kimmie Kannibal. She Irish whips her into the corner, and hits her with a DDT as she stumbles out. Trinity then lifts Kannibal to her feet and hits her with a quick release northern lights suplex into the corner. Kannibal hit’s the turnbuckles upside down, and Trinity wastes no time in turning her right side up and lifting her to the top before following her and hitting her with a sadistic drop and covers her for the three, eliminating Kimmie Kannibal, leaving only Angle, Trinity, Krissy Delight, and Pinky Lee I the match. Kimmie rolls out just as Angel stands to her feet on the outside and gets hit with a plancha from Trinity. Meanwhile, Krissy Delight is slamming Pinky Lee’s head into the top turnbuckle. Krissy grinds Pinky’s forehead against the rope, moving it back and forth causing Pinky to scream out.
Trinity throws Angel onto the ring apron, and follows her up, before lifitn gher onto her shoulders, and jumping off the apron to the floor below, hitting Angel with a Ravens edge. Angel falls to the ground limply, as E.M.T.’s immediately rush down the ramp to check on her. Trinity gazes on laughing as they load her up onto a stretcher and wheel her to the back. Krissy throws Pinky over the top on Trinity’s side. Trinity takes notice, and grabs Krissy’s legs, sending her to her back. Trinity then climbs onot the apron, and jumps over thew ropes, hitting Krissy with a leg drop. Krissy rolls to the outside, where she talks to Pinky for a moment, and both of them slide into the ring together. They go for a double clothesline on Trinity, but she ducks it and spins around, hitting them both with a dropkick to the back, sending Krissy to the ground, and Pinky into the corner. Pinky stumbles backwards out of the corner, and Trinity rolls her up for the three, eliminating her. Krissy slides into the ring, and catches Trinity by surprise, hitting her with some rights to the face. Trinity blocks one of the, and hits Krissy in the midsection with a bunch of knees before lifting her in a suplex position, and dropping her with a brain buster for the three.
Winner: Trinity Cut to backstage where Cody Clark is walking through the hallway. On his way to the ring, Waylon Cash approaches Cody Clark and stares him in the eye for a few minutes before spitting in his face.
Waylon Cash: There’s your answer you son of a bitch. Cody looks on in disbelief and wipes the spit from his face as Waylon walks away. [commercial] Zachriel 'Darkside' Donahue vs. Waylon Cash The two men stand in the ring, eyeing each other up for a long time before locking up. Darkside uses his strength to push Waylon Cash against the turnbuckles. The ref breaks them up and Darkside steps back respectfully, giving Waylon a smile. As Waylon steps out of the corner, Darkside rushes him, but Waylon dives out of the way and this him with a drop toe hold, sending him into the turnbuckles. Darkside quickly stands up and spins around as does Waylon. The two men trade some punches back and forth and Darkside throws Waylon out of the ring by the back of his neck. Suddenly, Cody Clark runs down the entrance ramp with a chair in hand, but Waylon doesn’t notice. Waylon stands to his feet, and Cody clocks Waylon in the back of the head with the steel chair, causing Waylon to fall to the ground, and the ref to ring the bell.
Clark raises the chair up and brings it down several times on Waylon’s head, and ribs as he screams at him. Darkside rolls under the bottom rope, and just walks back past Cody as he continues his assault on Waylon. Blood poors out of Waylons head, darkening the concrete entrance ramp as Cody stomps on him a number of times, and continues pounding on him with that chair. It doesn’t take long for the entire security team to come out and restrain Cody, dragging him to the back while EMT’s tend to Waylon Cash. The crowd looks on in stunned silence as Waylon is lifted to his feet by one of the medics, and refuses help as he hobbles up the entrance ramp on his own, holding a formerly white towel to the back of his head.
Shane Tallin vs. Alex Rettop There are already two tables set up in the ring as both men walk down the entrance ramp. The bell rings and the two men lock up. Tallin hit’s a deep arm drag and Rettop gets to one knee, starring across the ring at him as he challenges Rettop to stand. He does and the two men lock up again. They struggle back and forth before Rettop kicks Tallin in the gut. He levels some hard forearms to Tallin’s back, and throws him, shoulder first, into the steel ringpost. Tallin stumbles out of the ringpost, and gets hit with a back suplex from Rettop. Rettop wastes no time as Tallin lays on the ground, in setting up one of the tables, slanted in the corner. Tallin attacks Rettop from behind and Irish whips him into the ropes, only to get speared into the table already set up. The bell rings and Rettop glares at Tallin before rolling his prone body onto the other table. Rettop climbs up onto the top tunrbuckle and hit’s a frog splash, slamming Tallin through the other table.
Winner: Alex Rettop After the match, Rettop walks out of the ring and grabs a microphone from ringside. Rettop: (still catching his breath) "Did you see that, everyone? That's me, shutting Tallin up once and for all." He goes back in the ring, and looks at Tallin. Rettop: "I'm sick and tired of dealing with you, Tallin. I'm moving on. Ladies and gentlemen, I've been listening to some people in the back complaining about how I don't belong here. Guess what? I plan on proving it, to you the fans, to Miss Phoenix, and to everyone in the back. What will it take? Well, I can't wait to find out, and I hope you'll be willing to be deal with me."
Rettop drops the mic, and walks out of ringside with Yuchi, as "Bleed It Out" plays. [commercial] Jose Ramon vs. Dan Razor vs. Casey Turbo vs. Logan Quicksilver Jose Ramon immediately jumps Logan Quicksilver as Turbo and Razor brawl back and forth a little bit. Turbo Irish whips Razor into the ropes, and goes for a leg lariat, but Razor ducks under it, and Turbo hit’s the ground hard. Razor kicks him under the bottom rope. Turbo quickly stands up, and gets hit with a flipping senton over the top rope from Razor. Meanwhile, Jose stomps Quicksilver up and down, before lifting him up and Irish whipping him into the ropes. Quicksilver loses his footing, and gets his arms tied up in the ropes, rendering him helpless as Ramon lowers blow after blow down on his head. The ref is too busy yelling at Turbo and Razor to notice. Ramon backs up, and runs forward nailing Quicksilver in the face with a dropkick, and busting him open. At the refs command, Razor and Turbo rolls back into the ring, with Razor getting the upper hand. He hit’s a number of knees to Turbo’s gut, but Turbo wraps his arms around Razors midsection, and pushes him backwards, hard into the turnbuckle. Turbo stands upright and hits Razor with right hand after right hand, sending him to a sitting position in the corner. Turbo then back up, and runs forward, but razor stands up in time to bend down and flip Turbo over the ropes, sending him to the concrete floor. Ramon un wraps Quicksilver’s arms as Razor hit’s a baseball slide on Turbo. Seeing his opportunity, Ramon quickly hits Quicksilver with the Hype factor and pins him. The ref doesn’t recognize the pin right away as he is yelling at Razor and Turbo, but when he sees it he starts counting at two, Quicksilver kicks out and Razor slides Turbo into the ring. Ramon whips Quicksilver into the corner and pushes him down into a sitting position. He runs to the parallel corner and climbs to the top as Razor lifts Turbo into a sitting position on his shoulders. Razor flips Turbo over just as Ramon leaps off the rope. At the same time Razor hits Turbo with the Damnator, and Ramon hits Quicksilver with the Posterized. Ramon rolls Quicksilver out of the corner and pins him a half count before Razor pins Turbo. The ref counts to three and Razor jumps up celebrating as if he has won. When he turns around and sees the ref raising Ramon;s hand, he pushes the ref. He argues with the ref a bit, befor eramon grabs his shoulder and spins him around, but Razor hits him with a discuss punch, sending him to the ground. Ramon rolls out of the ring as Razor celebrates. Slowly, without Razor noticing, Quicksilver and Turbo rise to their feet and hit razor with a calf kick leg sweep combo.
Winner: Jose Ramon by pinfall El Novo’s music plays is as he walks to the ring. Pike Andres music hits, but he doesn’t come out. Suddenly, Cody Clark emerges form the crowd behind el novo with a chair in his hand. He slides under the bottom rope undetected, and slams the chair against the back of Novo’s head before rolling him out of the ring and demanding a microphone from the ring attendant.
Cody Clark: Look, no one gives one damn about any of the rest of these sorry S.O.B.’s. Each and every fan in this arena today, came here to see the one and only W3 champion, Cody Clark, so what say we lower that cage and get my sorry ass opponent out here so we can give these pathetic fans what they really want.
Chris Roman vs. Cody Clark We come back from commercial and the cage has been lowered around the ring as Cody stands in the middle of it, waiting for his opponent. Roman’s music hits and he comes down to the ring with an angry scowl on his face. He keeps his eyes on Cody as he enters the cage. The two men stare at each other as the ring attendants pad lock the cage shut. Immediately Roman jumps on Cody, and throws him into the side of the cage. Cody bounces off and this the ground as Roman dives on him and punches him ten times right between the eyes. Roman lifts him up and goes to clam his ehad into the cage wall, but Cody blocks it and grabs the back of Roman’s head, throwing him forward into the mesh steel. Roman recovers quickly, and the two men trade right hands. Cody gets the upper hand with some quick right, and backs Roman against the cage. Cody quickly backs up and runs forward, hitting Roman with a splash against the cage. They brawl some more, Roman seemingly out of it a little. Clark slams Romans head against each side of the cage as the crowd boos him mercilessly. The fourth time, Roman bounces back and blood drips down his face. Cody this Roman with a big Dirty, and walks over to the cage wall. He climbs up, and gets about halfway up when Roman claws his way over, and grabs hold of Cody’s ankle. Cody tries to kick him off, but Roman uses his leverage on Cody to pull himself up. He climbs the cage and meets Cody before hitting him with a Russian leg sweep off the cage onto the mat below. The two men rolls around, and Roman is the first to stand. He takes a minute to catch his breath, and Cody stands up as well. Cody goes to spear Roman, but Roman dodges and Cody runs headlong into the side of the cage. Quickly, Roman grabs him and rakes his forehead against the side of the cage. He Irish whips Cody into the ropes, and hits him with a hard lariat on his way back as the crowd begins to scream for Chris Roman. Roman goes for another lariat, but Cody ducks it and bounces back with a cross body block. Both men hit the mat and lay there for a bit, before using opposite cage walls to stand to their feet. Both men climb up the cage at the same time as the crowd chants on Roman. Simultaneously, they reach the top and climbs over. They both get halfway down and drop, hitting the ground at the same time. Both men lay on the ground as the two referees converse. They take one of the monitors and watch several replays as the crowd falls dead silent waiting for the verdict. They seem to agree and one of them speaks silently to Lesa Fox. She stands up and raises the mic to her lips.
Les Fox: The referees have decided that the result of this bout is a tie as both competitors hit the ground at the exact same time. As the result of a tie, still your W3 World Heavyweight champion, Cody Clark!
Cody hears his name and stands up, raising his hands high in the air. He walks around the ring and grabs Roman by the hair. Cody reaches into his pocket and pulls out a pair of steel handcuffs, wrapping one end around Roman’s wrist and one end around part of the cage wall. He punches Roman in the head several times, bloodying him more and more each time before ripping the mic from Lesa Fox’s hands.
Cody Clark: You see Roman? You see what happens when you mess with the champion. I am a god to these people, and there isn’t a god damn thing you can do about it. LONG LIVE THE CHAMP!
Cody grabs his belt form the time keeper and raises it high above his head as Adriel Phoenix’s music plays. The new GM walks out onto the entrance stage with a microphone in her hand.
Adriel Phoenix: Cody Clark, all night you have been taking it upon yourself to interfere in matches for no particular reason. You have excersized a brazen disregard for the rules as well as your fellow wrestlers. Unfortunately, there ins;t a lot I can do as long as you hold that belt.
Cody Clark: You’re damn right you can’t. As Cody is yelling at adriel, Waylon Cash leaps over the barricade with his head wrapped in white bandages and a pair of bolt cutters in his hands. Cody doesn’t see Waylon break the chain of Roman’s cuffs and revive him.
Cody Clark: Look GM lady, I have the gold, I am your championa nd that means you can’t fucking touch me!
Adriel Phoenix: You’re right, as long as you hold that belt, there isn’t a lot I can do. That is why, effective immediately, you are stripped of the W3 world heavyweight championship!
Cody Clark’s jaw drops to the floor as the ref rips the title from his hands. Cody tries to put up a fight, but thinks better of it.
Adriel Phoenix: And now that you are no longer the world heavyweight champion, I can say the two words I have been waiting the last hour and a half to say: you’re fired!
Cody screams at the GM and drops the microphone as he kicks the barrier and throws a tantrum at ringside. While he is doing this, Waylon Cash and Chris Roman stand up and walk up behind him with evil grins on their faces. Slowly, Cody turns around, and the two men quickly hit him with a double clothesline. Roman lifts Cody up and slams his head against the barrier a number of times, before both both men grab Cody and drag him to the entrance ramp. Roman lets go as Waylon continues dragging him up the ramp. Roman runs around to the side, and grabs a couple of wooden table, setting them up on the floor on the side of the ten foot entrance stage. Waylon drags Cody to the stage and grabs the back of his head, and his waistband before throwing him off the side of the stage, through the table below. Cody lays there unconscious as we fade to black.
