TPPWF: Opening for New Handlers

15 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently we have a few openings in the Three Pistols Pro-Wrestling Fed
(TPPWF) due to some of our handlers finished school and no longer have internet access over the summer. Other handlers have been on haitus and we frequently reduce the "deadbeats".
First of all, the TPPWF is based on wrestlers from the WWF and NWA/WCW ciruit from the 1980's to the present. We have a slick looking homepage to check us out at this URL:
http://www.geocities.com/colosseum/2830 If you are interested in joining, here are a few things you must know. This fed uses WLS 5.3 and TNM 6.12 software to determine the outcomes of the matches! Plus the handlers MUST BE ACTIVE! That means that you HAVE to submit interview segments and make challenges in order to get in the ring. This is where some handlers have lacked and are no longer with the fed.
Basically, if you like to be involved with a fed where you get e-mail everyday, where be it interviews (which are great), card reports or card previews; this is for you!
Here's some wrestlers that are available: Jeff Jarrett, Big Boss Man, Justin Bradshaw, Brian Pillman, Demolition,
Rowdy Roddy Piper, Steamboat, Snuka, Duggan, 911 .....
Basically, the homepage has a list who's taken. Look at it and you'll see who's left and the ones listed above are new "Free Agents". These are the ones you can draw from.
TPPWF has two cards a week except for the next 3 months we have cut it down to 1 due to summer related actives. If you're interested, drop me a note back with possible wrestlers you'd like to have. Each handler gets 4 in his/her stable. If you are new to e-wrestling, this is not an imtimidating fed and is easy to join in the flow of things. TPPWF prides itself on creating fueds and angles to make it interesting if you're guys are midcarders or title contenders.
Of course, we need active handlers to make this happen. TPPWF currently has 20 handlers and the action in the fed is extreme high. Seems right now everyone wants a piece of Hogan. Will Hulkamania die in the TPPWF???
If you're just interested, mail me and I can give you the TPPWF handbook with the guidelines how things work. I will also send a recent card report and you can see if you like the writing style!
Looking forward to hearing from you and see you in the ring! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [name withheld] TPPWF Fedhead and Booker Contributor to the SCT wrestling newsletter [detail withheld]
