Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Three Pistols Pro-Wrestling Fed's one surviving document catches it already mid-run, in June 1996, working through the ordinary churn of a real-wrestler roster league: handlers graduating, dropping off over the summer, or getting weeded out as “deadbeats.” The fed cast real wrestlers of the day — Jeff Jarrett, Big Boss Man, Justin Bradshaw, Brian Pillman, Demolition, Rowdy Roddy Piper, Steamboat, Snuka, Duggan, and 911 among the names its own recruitment post lists as available — decided outcomes with the WLS 5.3 and TNM 6.12 simulator engines, and ran two cards a week, cut to one for a summer stretch. Active handlers were required to write interview segments and issue challenges to earn ring time; by its own account it then carried twenty handlers and enough heat around one of its Hulk Hogan portrayals that the post asks, half-joking, “Will Hulkamania die in the TPPWF???”

The post points new applicants to a GeoCities homepage listing which real wrestlers were still free to draw from, and offers a handbook and a recent card report to anyone who wrote in.

No founding date, card, or closure notice survives beyond this single 15 June 1996 recruitment post — how long the Three Pistols Pro-Wrestling Fed had already been running by then, and how or when it ended, are both outside the surviving record.
