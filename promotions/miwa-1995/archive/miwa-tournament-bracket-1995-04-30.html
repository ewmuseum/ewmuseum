[MIWA] Here we go!

30 April 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Finally, we're ready to get this thing off the ground. I now have enough players to organize a 16-man tournament for the World title, as well as a 4-team tournament for the tag titles. These are the entrants for each: For the World Title: 1. Feudstarter 9. The Anarchist 2. Sean McGill 10. Juggernaut 3. Ric D.C. 11. Midnight 4. Handsome Floyd 12. Kris Connally 5. Darkness 13. Blacksphere 6. Tommy "The Machine" Gunn 14. Cave Man 7. The Earl of Doom 15. Iron Cross 8. "The Harvard Man" Lee Ivy 16. Retribution For the Tag Team Titles: 1. Technical Mastery 3. The Vipers 2. Pressure Point 4. Thunder & Lightning These are all the wrestlers I have significant stats for at the moment. There are _20_ people involved in this at the moment, with more still coming. If you are going to submit a character, I am afraid that since these are the first 16 I received, they receive preference to gain slots into the tournament. Your character will NOT be ignored if he/she is not listed above, it just means that they will not immediately be eligible for the World Title or the Tag Team Titles. It's gonna be one hell of a ride, folks. Hold on tight.
