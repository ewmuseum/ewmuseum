[MIWA] First tapings (LONG) (INTRODUCTIONS)

4 May 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The lights go up on a packed arena, with two announcers sitting at the broadcast booth drinking coffee and talking casually, unaware that the camera is on) [Ryan] ...so he says to the waiter, "That's not a chicken, it's a kid in a chicken suit!" Get it? [Silver] Yeah, I get it. Man, your jokes get worse every we...hey, what's that? [Ryan] What's what? [Silver] That little blinking red light on the side of the camera over there. [Ryan] Blinking red...my God, we're on the air! [Silver] (dropping his coffee) WHAT?!?! [Ryan] (hastily tossing the cup of coffee over his shoulder into the crowd, and grabbing a microphone) Greetings, everyone, and welcome to the FIRST EVER Maximum Impact Wrestling Association tapings! We're here in lovely Hoboken, New Jersey for this grand event. I'm Gideon Ryan, and this... (points at Silver, who is fidgeting quite a bit due to the newly-spilled coffee that landed in his lap) [Ryan] ...this is my colleague, Jonas Silver. We'll be your guides to the action in this new federation for the weeks ahead, delivering to all you fans out there some of the best professional wrestling in the world. [Silver] That's right, Gideon! Here in the MIWA, we're starting strong with a good blend of seasoned veterans, as well as some fresh meat...er, newcomers to this grand sport. [Ryan] Watch what you say there, Jonas. Some of that "fresh meat" can beat you into a hairy brown pudding. [Silver] They'd have to catch me first. But enough of this silliness, on to more important things. In the coming weeks, you folks will be seeing the tournaments to fill the vacant titles. In a few minutes we will be revealing the brackets for both the 16-man MIWA World Championship tournament, as well as the 8-team MIWA Tag Team tournament. [Ryan] The North American Title, second only in importance to the World Title, will also indirectly be determined by the World Tournament. The two men who lose in the semi-finals of the 16-man tournament will fight for the North American Title. And all of these title-determining matches will be held on Wednesday, May 24 at HIGH IMPACT '95! [Silver] Phooey. I wanted to say that. [Ryan] Say what? [Silver] (in his best announcer's voice) HIGH IMPACT '95! [Ryan] There you go, you said it. Better now? [Silver] Yes, much. Thanks. [Ryan] No problem. So without further ado, let's go to the High Impact '95 Control Center! (camera fades out, but not before we hear Silver muttering something to the effect of "Damn, that was some REALLY hot coffee...") ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Coming soon, to an arena near some of you... ************************* * * * H I G H * * * * I M P A C T * * * ************************* The quest for the gold ends here! [Maxwell] Good evening, everyone. I'm Darlene Maxwell, and I'll be filling you in on developments in the weeks leading up to High Impact '95. As Gideon and Jonas just mentioned, the first few weeks of this federation will be solely devoted to running the tournament for filling the vacant titles. Every show, four more matches of the tournaments will be held, until there are only three matches left. These three matches will be the title matches themselves, and are the first three matches signed for High Impact '95! Here's how the tournament pairing have matched up so far: (scene changes to a video screen, divided in half with the picture and name of a wrestler on each side) (the image of a handsome black-haired man in a t-shirt appears on the left, while that of a man in a black bodysuit and eyeless black mask with a raven on his shoulder forms on the right) [Maxwell] The exciting youngster Kris Connally will face off against the mysterious masked grappler known only as Darkness. In another match... (images change to that of a large muscular man with a brown ponytail in red and black tights on the left, and another large man with a black flattop and silver, red, and black face paint on the right) [Maxwell] ...the veteran Feudstarter will square off against the titanic Earl of Doom in what promises to be a true battle of the big men. Feudstarter had the following things to say about the tournament... (scene changes to an alley) [Feudstarter] SHUT UP AND LISTEN! I'M BACK, AND READY TO KICK SOME BUTT! All of you are just wasting your time in this league, because the greatest wrestler, and the greatest wrestling mind is going to be the MIWA World Champion. I've destroyed every one who has ever gotten in my way, and I just got tired of Electric Youth following me everywhere, so that is why I am here, for new competition. The last man who got in my way, some of you may know him, he was the man that used to be known as Rad Pitbull. Now he's holding a tin cup in some alley hoping someone will give him a spare dime. (Feudstarter looks at the tournament grid) [Feudstarter] Look at the competition I have to face... Ric D.C., Tommy Gunn, Midnight, Cave Man, I mean come on, I could go to a kindergarten and get a better fight. MIWA, BEWARE, BECAUSE A MAD MAN IS ON THE LOSE, AND HE WANTS SOME GOLD, AND THERE AIN'T NUTHIN GONNA STOP HIM. AS USUAL, THIS INTERVIEW WAS YOUR PLEASURE! WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! (scene changes back to Maxwell) [Maxwell] Obviously a great deal of confidence there. We'll have to see how he does in the tournament, though. Now, in another match... (yet another screen change, now with a handsome blonde-haired man on the left, and a masked man holding a grenade on the right) [Maxwell] ...the man who, in my opinion, is going to be the biggest stud in the MIWA, Handsome Floyd, will meet the diabolic Anarchist. We'll see if Floyd can wrestle as good as he looks... *dreamy sigh heard in background* [voice from offstage] Darlene! Snap out of it! [Maxwell] Huh?!?! Oh. Sorry. In another match... (screen changes to a large, tough looking man in red boxing shorts on the left, and a man with short blonde hair and the Irish flag in the background on the right) [Maxwell] ...Tommy "The Machine" Gunn will face the challenge of Ireland's own Sean McGill. In what promises to be yet another exciting match... (screen cuts to images of a powerfully built man with black hair on the left, and a man in a dark blue hooded cloak on the right) [Maxwell] ...the massive Retribution will meet the challenge of the lord of the dark, Midnight. Also signed... (images change yet again, this time to a monstrous man in a black tank top on the left, and a man in an old German military uniform and a monocle on the right) [Maxwell] ...the Juggernaut will face off against the man from Berlin, The Iron Cross. We caught up to the Juggernaut earlier, and were able to get these thoughts from him... (scene changes to the locker rooms) [Juggernaut] I only have a few things to say. First, if anyone's got a challenge... bring it on. I'll take on all challenges. If I win... excuse me, WHEN I win the MIWA Championship, this offer will still stand. I'm not going to hide behind a belt. You want the belt? You come take it from ME. Some of you may ask why I am so confident. Well, when you are the STRONGEST and TOUGHEST man in MIWA then you can tell me differently, otherwise KEEP YER MOUTH SHUT. Secondly...that's all I have to say. Like I said, you want me, you know where to find me! (back to a shot of Maxwell, who's still holding a picture of Handsome Floyd) [Maxwell] Big words from a big man. Speaking of big men, though... (screen changes to show an image of a towering man with a club on the left, and a man in a gold mask on the right) [Maxwell] ...the prehistoric Cave Man will take on Blacksphere. And, in our final singles match of the tournament... (another screen change, with a picture of a man with long dirty-blonde hair on the left, and a man in a polo shirt on the right) [Maxwell] ...Ric D.C., formerly of the USeW, will be squaring off against "The Harvard Man" Lee Ivy. (scene cuts back to Maxwell, who now has a Handsome Floyd t-shirt on) [Maxwell] And that's just the singles brackets! The tag team tournament promises to be just as exciting, with the following first-round matches slated already... (something fizzles, and we get another shot of Maxwell) [Maxwell] Ummm...hehe...it seems as if the video monitor burned out. Anyway, here's the lineup. The team of the Vipers will face off against Technical Mastery in what promises to be a knock-down, drag-out fight. The team from Japan, Phantom Flight, will be met with the challenge of the mammoth team known as Big Time. We actually ran into Big Time before, and they had this to say... (scene cuts to the Canadian wilderness, where two tall bald men are standing) [Tall Bob] Grrrrrr... we`re gonna win... arrgghh... ggrrrrrrrrrrr... titles... [Large Larry] (foaming at the mouth): Yeah, grrarrhhh, titles, heheheheh, grararh... ours... heeheheh. [Tall Bob] (starting to foam): TITLES... OURS... Graaaaahhhh. [Large Larry] (still Foaming): Heheheheheeh... [Tall Bob] (foaming) Heheheh... titles.... [Large Larry] (foaming profusly at the mouth): ....OURS. (scene cuts back to Maxwell) [Maxwell] Well, they're no "Harvard Men", but they just might have what it takes to win them some titles. Also scheduled is the team of Zodiac II meeting up with the Council, and Thunder & Lightning facing off against the team of Pressure Point. We also had this tape sent to us by Pressure point, showing them training... (scene cuts to the tape...) On-Location with PRESSURE POINT: (The scene is a simple basement. Rather large with a wrestling ring and a lot of weights, punching bags, etc. The room is dark. Suddenly a deep voice booms out) [Capt. America] COME ON GUYS!!! PUSH, PUSH, DON'T STOP, YOU CAN'T STOP, NOT NOW. (groans can be heard emanating from the background) [Capt. America] That's it, remember your destination, remember your goal. The only one's who can hold you back are yourselves now. (more groans of exertion come from the depths of the basement) [Capt. America] Good, very good. O.K. both of you up. Time to show the world what they are about to face. Comet (a sly laugh) give us some light. (A ball of flame shoots and the room is lit, displaying Capt. America and Sal Defazio, along with a man on fire) [Capt. America] That's enough Gregg, now we have some light. (switches light on, as Gregg "Comet" Matthews extinguishes himself, but appears unscathed.) Ladies and Gentlemen, I present to you PRESSURE POINT. Not since I was champion those years ago have I been as proud to say I was part of this sport as I am today. You will find out who I am in due time, but this is not about me, it is about my men. Tell 'em guys. [Comet] I am Gregg Matthews, otherwise known as Comet. I won't bore you with the background stuff, I'll get right to the point. My partner Sal and I along with Capt. America have a very simple goal, and that is the MIWA Tag Team Championship. We have watched as wrestling has been turned into a sport of gimmicks and where it seems that everyone is looking to sneak in through the back door. Well we are coming, and we are walking right on in. [Sal] That's right everybody. We are prepared for the toughest fights of our lives. We aren't dirty but we're DAMN tough and we have the heart, desire and determination to take it all. [Capt. America] But don't you people think for ONE second that my men here are just going to let people break the rules and not fight back. That is not how I trained them. Our plan is very simple, fight with honor, but fight as is necessary. We would prefer a clean fight, good solid wrestling. But I know some of you guys are not capable of that. Well test your luck. This isn't just fun and games anymore, this is the real deal with real stakes. No more sparring matches, this is the time, this is what we thrive on. You wonder what we are all about. We are about strength, inside and out. We are about dignity. We are about honor and courage. But we are also about striving for success. [Comet] We will meet the biggest and the baddest and that's what we want. We want to prove ourselves. Plain and simple, no gimmick here. We are guys who want the title and are prepared to do whatever we need to to get it. [Sal] So be prepared, because we are. When we hit the ring at the MIWA TAG TEAM Tournament, all hell is gonna break loose. And so are we. Pressure Point is here, and we're here to stay. [Capt. America] CASE CLOSED!!! O.K. guys, back to work, 10 miles let's go. ("Live and Let Die" by Guns n' Roses plays as the team runs off into the sunset) (scene cuts back to Maxwell, who now has a Handsome Floyd poster behind her) [Maxwell] And that, folks, is what's going on in the next few weeks. All this is leading up that grand date of May 24, and High Impact '95, where the titles will be won, once and for all! And now, back to Jonas and Gideon to wrap things up. (scene fades out as Maxwell rushes off to find Handsome Floyd's locker room) [Ryan] Gee, I wonder who she's picking to win the tournament. [Silver] I dunno- I don't like Floyd's chances too much if she gets her hands on him. [Ryan] *chuckling* Good point. So, who do you see winning the tournaments? [Silver] Oh, that's easy. The world title will go to Feudstarter, while the Tag Team belts will go to Thunder and Lightning. [Ryan] I think I'm going to have to disagree with you there, Jonas. I see the Juggernaut winning the World Belt- he seems to have the drive to do so. As for the tag belts...Well, Pressure Point seemed rather impressive there, I think they can pull it off. [Silver] You can't be serious. They're managed by a comic book character, for crying out loud! [Ryan] So? You're rooting for guys named after a weather report. [Silver] Why you... [Ryan] (turning towards the camera as Silver begins to remove his broadcasting equipment) Well, that's about it for this week, folks- just an overview of what's coming up. Next week, we'll have the first matches of the tournaments, with the following lineup: Retribution vs. Midnight Kris Connally vs. Darkness Tommy "The Machine" Gunn vs. Sean McGill Phantom Flight vs. Big Time (Ryan ducks a tackle attempt by Silver) [Ryan] For myself and Jonas Silver, see you this Saturday! (Silver appears behind Ryan holding a broken table leg, and the scene fades to black just before he swings it) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ NOTES: I apologize for two things.
