A complete flash-in-the-pan story: a self-titled Commissioner locked a full 16-man/4-team World Title tournament bracket and staged one broadcast introducing it, before the promotion folded roughly two weeks after founding with not a single match ever contested. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Maximum Impact Wrestling Association announced itself under construction on 27 April 1995, with six singles wrestlers and three tag teams signed and room for more; its own plan was to fill every title by tournament, building toward a first major card tentatively called “High Impact I.”

By 30 April enough wrestlers had signed for the Commissioner to lock a full bracket: a 16-man tournament for the MIWA World Championship (Feudstarter, Sean McGill, Ric D.C., Handsome Floyd, Darkness, Tommy “The Machine” Gunn, The Earl of Doom, “The Harvard Man” Lee Ivy, The Anarchist, Juggernaut, Midnight, Kris Connally, Blacksphere, Cave Man, Iron Cross, and Retribution) and a four-team tournament for the MIWA Tag Team Championship, its losing semifinalists to meet afterward for a new North American Championship.

The promotion's first broadcast, taped in Hoboken, New Jersey and posted 4 May 1995, introduced announcers Gideon Ryan and Jonas Silver and set the date for the tournament finals — Wednesday, 24 May, at “High Impact '95” — then walked through the full bracket in vignettes: Feudstarter (“I've destroyed every one who has ever gotten in my way”), Juggernaut (“you want the belt? You come take it from ME”), and the debuting tag team Pressure Point, presented by a manager billed as Capt. America alongside wrestlers Comet and Sal, promising “the toughest fights of our lives.” Ric D.C., one of the sixteen World Title entrants, was introduced as formerly of the USeW. The broadcast closed with the next week's first-round pairings announced and both announcers picking their favorites to win.

No further broadcast survives. The tournament that was meant to crown the MIWA's first champions never produced a single contested match in the record, and the promotion folded within roughly two weeks of its own founding.
