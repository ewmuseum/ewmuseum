SuperLeague: TNM Booking Association

7 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey RSPWF I'm making my first post on here in a long time. And it's to try and bring back the SuperLeague. For those of you that don't know, SuperLeague consisted of about 4-7 Individuals who each ran their own fed using TNM. We've done a bunch of different formats. From a Tiered format (2-3 Main Feds, and then Smaller Indy feds) to an NWA Format (1 World Heavyweight and Tag Team Champions, and they toured all the other regions). And I want to bring it back. I like the idea of an NWA Style SuperLeague with one World Champion that tours the regions, and also World Tag Team Champions. And if we get enough regions, maybe even a World Television Champion, or something along those lines that tours the regions. I say we run the league in real time With the champion visiting 1 fed every 2 weeks, and the week he visits he can't appear on his feds card for 1 show. (Each fed runs 2 shows a week and a supercard a month) I'd say the regions should be (But aren't limited to): Northeast United States (New York, Massachussetts, Pennsylvania) Southeast United States (Alabama, Tennessee, Georgia) Southwest United States (Texas, Arizona) Traditional Japan "Jr's" Japan Mexico United Kingdom Canada Each of these regions has a very distinct style. If there are any others that should be added, don't be afraid to request them. Anyone interested? If so, fill out this form, and mail it to [detail withheld] Name: Email: Organization: (Name, and Region. By Preferance) If your name is region specific, such as Texas Championship Wrestling, you may want a different name for another region choice in case your first or second choices are taken. We won't double up until we really start to get full (5-7 Promotions) We will start if we get 4 entries.
