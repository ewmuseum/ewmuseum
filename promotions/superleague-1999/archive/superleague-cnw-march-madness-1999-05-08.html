Heres your chance to join something different!

8 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello RSPWF, I'm [name withheld], owner of CNW in the Alliance known as SuperLeague. What is
SuperLeague you ask? Well, it's an alliance of several federations that use TNM
7 to share their results with everyone. We use real wrestlers and book three cards per month (game time, which is usually about 4 days per month). After you sim a card, you write up a report about what happened at the card, any run-ins or angle advancements, heres one I did for March 2000, for the CNW PPV called
March Madness. Emilio Charles Jr. pinned Christian with the Frog Splash in 0:15:42. Rating: 1/2* (Emilio Charles Jr. won the CNW World Lightweight Title.) Val Venis and Hardcore Holly defeated Too Cold Scorpio and The Godfather when V. Venis pinned Godfather with the Money Shot in 0:26:45. Rating: 1/2* Two-out-of-three-Falls Match: Lizmark Jr. beat Kaz Hayashi 2 falls to 1: x Lizmark Jr. beat Kaz Hayashi via the Springboard Moonsault in 0:18:24 x Kaz Hayashi beat Lizmark Jr. via the Flying Senton in 0:19:47 x Lizmark Jr. beat Kaz Hayashi via a tiger driver in 0:21:48
Rating: *** 1/4 Flaming Barbed Wire Baseball Bat No-Countout-No-DQ-Falls-Count-Anywhere Match: Al Snow pinned Lance Storm after a German suplex in 0:10:53. Rating: **** (Al Snow retained the CNW Extremist Title.) D-Lo Brown pinned Konnan with the Low Down in 0:20:28. Rating: ** 3/4 Triangle Match: Plata defeated Ken Shamrock and Mark Youngblood when Plata pinned Shamrock with the Flying Cross Body Press in 0:36:59.
Rating: ** 1/2 [March Madness Match]: War Games No-Countout-No-DQ-Match: South Of The Border (Abismo Negro, Apolo Dantes, Espectro and Hector Garza) defeated The Brood (Edge, Gangrel, Sting and Vampiro) when Dantes made Edge submit to the Apolo 13 in 0:30:27.
Rating: *** 1/4 Jerry Lynn pinned Tatanka with the Top-Rope DDT in 0:21:35. Rating: *** 1/2 (Jerry Lynn won the CNW United Nations Title.) Hulk Hogan and The Patriot defeated The Lightning Express when Patriot pinned Horner with the Stealth Missle in 0:16:50. Rating: ** (Hulk Hogan and The Patriot won the CNW Global Tag Team Titles.) Taz made The Rock submit to the Tazmission in 0:26:22. Rating: ***** (Taz won the CNW Global Network Title.) Emilio Charles got revenge on Christian by beating him for the CNW Lightweight Title. The Frog Splash was enough to put the member of the Brood away. Val Venis and Hardcore Holly also got revenge on Ethnic Elite Members Godfather and Too Cold Scorpio. Venis eventually hit the Money Shot on his former partner for the win.
Lizmark Jr. and Kaz Hayashi again showed fans why CNW Lightweights are the best in the World. In a great 2/3 falls match, Lizmark upended Kaz this time around.
Al Snow retained his CNW Extremist Title in a falls count anywhere barb wire baseball bat match. He defeated Lance Storm.
South of the Border did it! They defeated the Brood in the March Madness match. Gangrel, the leader of the Brood was absolutely mad that Edge had given up to the Apolo 13, and said he didn't need anyone like that in the Brood. He had
Vampiro and Sting hold Edge while he beat him, but Christian came down to make the save with a baseball bat and cleared the ring. It seems as if Christian and
Edge are no longer in the Brood. And they clearly said that on the mic. They said they were tired of the gothic gimmick. Edge claimed he should now be known as Sexton Hardcastle in CNW, and Christian would go by his full name, Christian
Cage. Together, The Suicide Blondes had been reformed in Championship Network Wrestling, and they were in no way done with the Brood. Jerry Lynn bested Tatanka in a great match to regain the CNW United Nations Title. He hit the Top Rope DDT and Tatanka couldn't get up before the three. The Lightning Express came down to the ring wondering who would be their opponents. Stanfield came down, and the X-Press just laughed when he got in the ring. Stanfield said that they wouldn't be laughing when they saw their opponents, TEAM USA! Hulk Hogan and the Patriot came to the ring to a HUGE ovation. The Patriot hit the Stealth Missile and Team USA won the CNW Global
Tag Team Titles.
Taz, definentaly pissed off at the Rock, got the win with the Tazmission. Taz is the new CNW Global Network Champion. He told the Rock to "Shove that up his
Ethnic Ass", and left the ring to a standing ovation, because everyone else was fed up with the Rock's antics as well.
Thats how a report for a card goes, and you have to do three of them for each month. Usually two televised shows and a PPV or something like that. Now, in real life, SuperLeague has "Big-Leagues" and Indy feds. The big leagues are
CNW, NWW and CWWA. Run by Me, Matt and Doug, respectively. Also we have quite a few indy federations.
Now, heres the hard part. You have to draft a roster of a certain number of people (I think 20 singles and 8 tags right now.) When you join SuperLeague, you become one of the indy feds. And you're put on a six month trial period
(game time) In which the Big Leagues evaluate your process. At the end of six months, we can keep you as an indy fed, bump you up to being a big league or terminate you completely from SuperLeague.
Whats the difference between an Indy fed and a big League you ask? It's simple. Talent. If you're an indy fed, you're talent can still be hired by one of the big leagues if they wish. If you're a big league, your talent stays with you until they quit, you fire them, or their contract expires and they don't want to renew. After every month, you try to draft five more people.
