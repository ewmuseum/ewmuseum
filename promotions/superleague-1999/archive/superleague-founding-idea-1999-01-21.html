TNM7 SuperLeague

21 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, basically here is the idea... As you may have noticed, it is possible to have Talent Limitations inwhich several feds work off the same talent pool. For example, if Steve Austin is in my federation, he can't be in yours as well.
I just think, since we all love TNM, that it would be fun to have any interested parties get in. We have a "draft" for talent, and we each run our circuit, sending(through E-Mail) information after each card is run. So, basically, I am the WWF, and you are the WCW, and someone else is ECW, and someone else is NWA, and we just have fun by ourselves, but still in some way have fun with each other as well.
If you are still interested, contact me. If you have any questions, contact me. I'll see if we can get this started soon. I am considering limiting it to 5 guys MAX because there is only so much talent. Share opinions.
