Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

TNM SuperLeague began as a pitch posted 21 January 1999 for pooling several simulator-run circuits together under one shared talent limitation — “so basically I am the WWF, and you are the WCW, and someone else is ECW, and someone else is NWA” — each running its own circuit and mailing results after every card.

By that May, its own structure had settled: three “Big Leagues,” CNW, NWW, and CWWA, held their rosters permanently, while a growing slate of “Indy feds” served a six-month trial before being kept, promoted, or cut, all drafting from the same pool of real, contemporary professional wrestlers — twenty singles and eight tag teams per circuit, five more drafted every month.

The alliance's own member reports carried the actual results: CNW's own March Madness card, circulated as a worked example of how a report should read, closed on Taz forcing The Rock to submit to the Tazmission for the CNW Global Network Title, and turned mid-card on Edge and Christian breaking from Gangrel's Brood, dropping their “gothic gimmick” for new names, Sexton Hardcastle and Christian Cage, and reforming as the Suicide Blondes.

How SuperLeague's alliance held together past that report isn't in its surviving record.
