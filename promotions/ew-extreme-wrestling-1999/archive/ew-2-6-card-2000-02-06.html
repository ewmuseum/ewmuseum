EW (Denton, 1999) — Card

Extreme Wrestling: 2.6 Card

6 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(B.J. Perris walked out to the ring in front of a loud Campus Theater crowd. Strolling around the ring until they quieted down, he spoke.) <Perris> Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Extreme Wrestling! (The fans cheered, causing Perris to stop and wait for them to quiet down once again. While waiting, Mean Darlene, followed by Jake Morgan walked out to the ring. Climbing into the ring, she snatched the mic away from the president.)
<Mean Darlene> So ... Justin Hart, you want to know what it is with the women here, is that it? First it's Tara and now it's me ...
Well let me tell you a little something ... this is Extreme Wrestling ... where the wrestling is extreme ... the men are extreme ... and the women are oh so extreme! And if you don't like it ... well, then that's your problem!
You see as much as I don't like her, Tara Collins has stood up to YOUR KIND time and time again ... how many of you in the back have wondered the same damn thing and how many of you have turned around and had your shoulders pinned to the mat by the Texas Terror?
Each and ever match, she's stood toe to toe with some of the best in the business ... And no matter what she does ... no matter what I do here in Extreme Wrestling, some big joke of a wrestler has a hard time believing. Well believe what you want to believe Hart but the facts remain ... she held the tag titles and is one of the only people here in Extreme Wrestling to fight for the Texas
Heavyweight and Television Titles ... They say that behind every great man is a better woman but today it's Jake
Morgan who stands behind me ...
(There were whistles and laughter as Darlene smiled over her obvious innuendo.) ... but I have to ask you Hart, when you look over your shoulder behind you, who do you see standing there? While you're thinking that one over, why not get your ass out here so I can kick it!
(An angered Justin Hart followed by Neowolf stormed out to the ring. The fans booed the Tag Team Champs heavily as Hart stood nose to nose with Darlene.
Chants of "Kick his ass!" came from the crowd which brought Jake Morgan in closer just in case. Without a mic between them, whatever was being said between the two couldn't be heard but there was certainly some bad blood brewing between the two.
Before things could escalate further between Darlene and Hart, the Texas Terrors hit the ring. The trio jumped all over Hart and Neowolf, hammering away at them. With the EW security hitting the ring to break up the brawl that broke out, Jake Morgan cleaned a little house, taking both Ron Collins and Neowolf to the outside with a Double Clothesline, knocking them over the top rope. Hart hit a Snap Suplex on Tara, only to take a Drop Kick from Mean Darlene to the face.
After clearing the ring, Mean Darlene was grabbed and held back by Jake Morgan as the security ushered the Texas Terrors to the back. Tara was screaming and pointing her finger at Hart and Neowolf.
With order restored, referee Peter Daye was in the ring and giving instructions to Hart and Darlene before calling for the bell and starting the match.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** It was obvious that Hart took Darlene lightly as he slowly strolled out of his corner ... a big mistake a Darlene launched herself into action with a Spear, taking him down. After hammering away with an assortment of Punches and Kicks,
Darlene found herself in the ring with one very angry wrestler.
Dumping Darlene with a Gorilla Press Slam, the match turned in his favor which was being watched very closely by Jake Morgan, who was also keeping an eye on
Neowolf.
Launching Darlene into the ropes, Hart took her off her feet with a Knee to the stomach, flipping her through the air. Standing in the center of the ring with his arms raised in the air, Hart was booed by the masses, even using him for target practice as he was peppered with several used cups and assorted small items.
Neowolf climbed up onto the ring apron and was shouting at the fans. Seeing
Neowolf making a move was all the excuse Jake Morgan needed as he rushed the ring and drove a shoulder intot he small of Neo's back, knocking him to the stage floor.
Inside the ring, Hart pulled Darlene to her feet only to take a Low Blow. Jumping into the air and wrapping her legs around Hart's waist, she pulled him over and onto the mat. Rolling her body, Hart's shoulder's were pinned to the canvas ... the only problem was that Peter Daye was trying to get Morgan out of the ring.
Seeing Darlene, Daye dropped to the mat and slapped a 1 count as Hart threw his body back and rolled over the top of Darlene, ending the match with a 3 count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans booed the outcome as Justin Hart held a hand out to Darlene to help her to her feet only to have it swatted away. Hart never saw what the fans were cheering for until it was too late as the Offspring dove into the ring and hammered him in the head with chair. Hart fell backwards to the mat and took a second shot over the head as he rolled to the floor where Neowolf was being hammered by Jake Morgan. Following a Powerbomb onto the stage floor, Hart grabbed Neowolf away and pulled him to the back.
With the Offspring in the ring with a chair and Mean Darlene still on the mat,
Morgan was back in the ring in a hurry. The two former champions were eyeing each other as the fans went silent. Neither man gave ground as they watched each other. Finally, as the fans got back into it with a cheer and a chant of
"Extreme", the Offspring tossed the chair down and exited the ring.
With Morgan helping Darlene to her feet, the first match was over as they headed to the back. As the fans settled down, the EW Texas Heavyweight
Champion, Jason Flint strolled out with his entourage of the State Club Boyz and Kimberly Davis. Dressed in a t-shirt, jeans, boots and tape halfway up his arms, Flint looked ready for a hardcore war.
His boyz followed carrying a ladder and an assortment of other weapons to the ring. They didn't have to wait long for Raskal to make his entrance as he ran out and began hitting the State Club Boyz before diving into the ring and attacking the champ.
Without a referee to watch over things, Dan Glass, the "Electric Referee" ran out and called the for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** It was a grinder as Flint and Raskal went back and forth, first beating on each other with their fists in a good old fashioned brawl before the intensity picked up and Flint went to his arsenal of weapons.
Flint flattened Raskal with a garbage can and used it until it was flattened on one side then pulled the Klown up and DDT'd onto it. After wrapping a chain around his fist, Flint pulled Raskal up and began hammering away with punches, opening him up in the process. Flint was taken down with a Drop Toe Hold and took a Boot to the back before Raskal came off the ropes with a Splash.
Grabbing up the ladder, Raskal pressed it up over his head before throwing it down on top of Flint. Hitting the ropes, Raskal bounced off and dropped an
Elbow on top of the ladder driving it into Flint. Tossing it aside, he pulled
Flint up to his feet and hit him with a Head Butt, knocking him out of the ring. With a running start, Raskal dove oout of the ring and took down Flint and the State Club Boyz with a Suicide Dive before once again having to fight the three of them.
Glass was slow to leave the ring as Raskal was swarmed over. Kicking and Punching him, the Boyz gave Flint a minute to catch his breath before rolling him back into the ring. Back inside the ring, Flint dropped a Knee into
Raskal's bloody face then went back to the weapons where he beat his opponent across the back with a chair. Throwing it down, he pulled Raskal up and into a
Side Headlock before hitting the ropes and putting Raskal facefirst into the chair with a Bulldog.
Rolling over Raskal gained a fast 2 count before a kick out. While beating
Raskal with a 2x4, Flint instructed his Boyz to set up a table on the outside of the ring. With the table set up, Flint pulled Raskal to the top turnbuckle and looked to be setting up a big move when the Klown pushed the champion off, sending Flint flailing through the air, shattering the table. Without a second thought, the challenger threw his body off the top as well and landed a Senton on the floor as the Boyz pulled Flint out of the way.
Again Raskal found himself in a beatdown from the State Club Boyz. With Raskal being held, Flint came in a fired away with a few jabs before winding up with a big Punch only to have Raskal duck out of the way at the last second, hitting
Tony in the kisser and knocking him down. With a Punch of his own, Raskal floored Flint then hit Joe and Curtis with Head Butts.
With a Whip, Raskal sent Flint off the stage and into the crowd down below where he was caught. As security moved in and backed the fans away, Raskal dove off the stage and dropped Flint Body Press.
Both men were down as the fans chanted "Extreme!". Raskal was first to his feet, standing over the downed Flint. Grabbing up a chair, Raskal pounded it over the back Flint before tossing it aside. What happened next, no one would have expected but the "Electric Referee" from the stage jumped off and hit
Raskal with a Bulldog onto the floor. Now all three were down as well as the
State Club Boyz.
Down on the floor, Flint was back on his feet, kicking chairs out pf his way as he cleared some space around Raskal. Staggering away, he climbed the steps back up to the stage top, making his way to the edge looking down at the unmoving
Raskal. Taking some steps back, Flint was met by a pleading Kimberly. She knew what was about to come and didn't want Flint to do it.
Stepping around her, Flint took a few steps back before running off the stage and flipping through the air and landing a Corkscrew Splash. Both men laid there surrounded by the fans who were chanting the Champion's name. Neither moved as they began counting as Flint's arm laid across Raskal's body.
... 1 ... ... 2 ... ... 3 ... ... 1 ... ... 2 ... ... 3 ... ... 1 ... ... 2 ... ... 3 ... The fans continued to count as a man in a suit ran out from the back, hit the floor and slapped out a count before calling for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (It was Niles Neptune who ran from the back, now calling for help with three men down on the outside of the ring. The fans watched on as the EMT's rushed out from the back and helped Flint, the profusely bleeding Raskal and Dan Glass to the back followed by Kimberly Davis and the State Club Boyz.
The fans were cheering for several more minutes as the ring was cleared of all the debris and B.J. Perris stepped out from the back.)
<Perris> Our next match is an Over the Top Battle Royal to determine the new #1
Contender to the EW Texas Heavyweight Title ...
Eight men ... 6 already named and 2 mystery wrestlers ... Jake Morgan ... the
Offspring ... Brian Cage ... Ron Collins ... Felix Wade ... and as the loser of the today's title match, Raskal ...
(Five of the six walked out to the ring ... Raskal was bandaged and walking with a noticeable limp from his previous match. The Offspring was nowhere to be seen.
The fans started cheering as standing at the entryway, the new Extreme Wrestling Television Champion, the Offspring, pats the golden center-plate of the leather strap draped over his shoulder. The braids that cover most of his masked face partially conceal the large purple rings around his "eyes." Pulling the belt from his shoulder and looking at it, he speaks.)
<Offspring> On January second, I stepped into the ring with Jyhad and started something that, before this Thursday, hadn't finished. That night, in the center of the ring, Chad Derringer and I finally finished a battle one month delayed. And as a result…
(The 'Spring straps the belt on, the snaps in front, then spins it around.) I am the Extreme Wrestling Television Champion! (The crowd, recognizing the enthusiasm, pop.) I can now move on. Once again, I carry a title. Once again, I have proven that in spite of the repeated attempts of those who would write the Sudden, Violent
Revolution off, we have not finished.
As I promised you all… it has begun. Again! (Waving his arms to encompass the crowd, the champion continues.) Down in the ring I know that all of you are anxiously awaiting the chance to kick each other's asses in route to winning the right to face off with the EW
Texas Heavyweight Champion. I wish you all luck, but I have this…
(The 'Spring indicates the title belt.) And being a former champion, I can request my title shot when I'm ready… so
I'm going to bow out.
(The Offspring looks around at the crowd, who seem remotely upset that he is pulling himself from the bout.)
But instead of going to the back-I'm going to come down to ringside… (The TV champ walks down to the ring area, removing his belt and placing it on the table next to the bell. Sitting down at the table, the 'Spring seemed to be positioning himself for a first hand view of the action.)
And I'm going to make sure this match is decided fairly. I've seen more than one swerve in the past few weeks, and this time, I'm going to make sure JUSTICE is served!
(There was a mixture of cheers and booing as the Offspring took his seat. The booing grew even louder as Justin Hart walked out from the back.)
<Justin Hart> Well ... if he doesn't want in ... then I do! As one half of the tag team champs, I deserve a crack at Flint just as much ... if not more than the rest of these guys!
(Hart climbed into the ring and began pointing at Morgan who eyed him across the ring.) <Perris> With six men now inside the ring, that leaves us with our last two mystery wrestlers! The last two men before we get underway!
First allow me to introduce to you, at 6'4" and 265 pounds ... the former IWA
Southern Heritage, Texas State and World Champion ... Dennis Windham!
(The large dark haired man was an impressive sight as he strolled out to the ring dressed in his jeans, cowboy boots and sleeveless leather jacket.)
And finally ... a man with too many accolades to his credit ... here is Niles
"the Original Playboy" Neptune!
(Niles Neptune jogged out to the ring despite the heavy kneebrace and climbed in, taking a short stroll around the ring and bouncing off the ropes.
With Perris taking a place outside the ring and referees Johnny Green and Peter
Daye positioning themselves, the bell rang and the match was underway.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Things started slow as everyone eyed the rest of the people in the ring, sizing them up. In a few short seconds, everyone drew opponents ... Felix Wade and Ron
Collins began hammering away at Brian Cage, Windham and Neptune were trying to gain control as they locked up, Hart was beating on Raskal who was bleeding from under the bandage on his head while Jake Morgan stood back and watched.
Brian Cage was the first to dumped out of the ring as Collins and Wade threw him out together.
Windham tossed Neptune aside and went after the bigger man, Jake Morgan. Raskal had been Whipped across the ring into Collins and Wade, only to start another double teaming. Hart was taken to the mat by Neptune who decided to work over his arm.
Windham was next to go after trying to slug it out with the former Champion. Two down, five more to go. Collins and Wade were now hitting each other after working in unison. Hart with his arm scissored, was trying to left Neptune and was having a hard time trying especially with Raskal Stomping on him. Morgan dashed across the ring and leveled Ron Collins with a Clothesline, knocking him over the top rope and to the floor. Three down.
Morgan's own momentum carried him over the top rope and almost out of the ring. Holding on for dear life, Morgan was being pounded on by Felix Wade. Across the ring, Raskal was Stomping on both Niles Neptune and Justin Hart, breaking up Neptune's hold. With Raskal Body Slamming Hart, Neptune wandered across the ring and drove a Forearm into Wade's back. Turning, Wade took a Drop
Kick to the head that knocked him back into the ropes and over the top as
Morgan pulled them down as he regained his footing in the ring. Four down.
With a quick nod for the save, Jake Morgan repaid Neptune with a withering
Forearm to the head followed by a Short Arm Clothesline before moving on.
Pulling Raskal off of Hart, Morgan began exchanging blows with the bleeding
Klown. Dazed from Morgan's shots, Neptune wasn't ready as Hart came in and began throwing him around the ring. Hart had easy pickings as he landed an Atomic
Drop followed by scooping him up and tossing him over the top rope. Five down, three left.
Taking a break, Hart watched as Raskal took a beating before dropping Morgan with a Low Blow followed by a Double Arm DDT. With Morgan down, Raskal climbed the ropes when Hart struck ... charging across the ring and throwing himself against the ropes caused Raskal to lose his balance and fall out of the ring.
There were now only two men left inside the ring. Morgan and Hart. The two squared off in the center of the ring and began circling each other. Hart got the fast upperhand with a Boot to the stomach followed by an Ace
Crusher, driving Morgan into the mat. Pulling him to his feet, Hart sent him across the ring with an Irish Whip. Hart dipped his shoulder as Morgan returned, dropping him to the mat. The fans were booing as Hart raised his arms into the air.
Pulling Morgan up again, Hart sent him into the ropes again but on the return, it was Morgan who dropped Hart on the seat of his pants with his Shoulder Butt.
Dropping quickly, Morgan landed a Leg Drop and followed it up with a Big Belly-to-Back Suplex, shaking the ring in the process. The fans were cheering as Morgan waited until Hart regained his feet before
Spearing him to the mat. Jumping back to his feet, Morgan bellowed before pulling Hart back to his feet and Whipping him across the ring. On the return,
Morgan's boot raised right into Hart's face, flooring him.
Lifting him into the air, Morgan again shouted out before dropping to the mat with a Brainbuster. It never came as Hart dropped out Morgan's grasp, slightly behind him. Grabbing Morgan, Hart dropped back with a Side Russian Leg Sweep.
Rolling over the top, Hart pounded away with stiff Punches. Tossing Hart off of him, Morgan looked mad. Driving a Boot into Hart's midsection, Morgan lifted him off of his feet and planted him with a Death
Valley Driver. Before doing any more, Morgan saw that Hart's partner, Neowolf had run out to the ring to catch the action.
Getting up, Morgan went to the ropes and was motioning for Neowolf to come in and get some as well. Neo, ever ready to take the bait hopped up onto the ring apron where the two began shouting at each other.
That was the wrong thing to do. The Offspring, jumped up from his ringside table seat and onto the apron where he tried to cool both men down. Morgan took a step back as Spring intervened, stepped back and laid a Forearm into
Neowolf's head. Jumping up and hitting the ropes, Spring wrapped an arm around
Neo's head and pulled him off of the apron and down on top of and through the ringside table. The fans went nuts as the Offspring "cleaned house".
Inside the ring, Morgan was so focussed on the Offspring and Neowolf that he never saw Hart charging at him from across the ring. The fans yelled out but it was too late ... or was it? Stepping to the side, Morgan caught Hart's arm and twisted his body, throwing Hart over the top rope out of the ring and onto
Neowolf with a Hip Toss.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Morgan stood in the center of the ring with his arms raised listening to the cheers as Mean Darlene came out to the ring, ending the show.)
