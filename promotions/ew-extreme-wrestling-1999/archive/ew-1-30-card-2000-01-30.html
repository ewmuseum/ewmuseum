EW (Denton, 1999) — Card

Extreme Wrestling: 1.30 Card

30 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens in the back of the Campus Theater with President B.J. Perris standing with his three referees, Peter Daye, Johnny Green and newly hired Dan
Glass, in his "Electric Referee" outfit.
As they stand in the hallway, Perris seems to be addressing something.) <Perris> Okay ... we have three big matches this evening kicking off with the
Tag Title match ... Johnny, you'll work inside the ring and because of the number of wrestlers, I'm going to assign Glass to work the outside.
(Glass smiles as he nods his head in agreement.) Then Peter, you'll work the Offspring/Neowolf match following ... (Glass gets a big grin on his face as he slaps his hands together.) <Dan Glass> YES! Then I get the Texas Heavyweight Title Match! (Perris casts a disparaging look at Glass.) <Perris> No ... I told you once, you're not going to be working any of Flint's matches ... and I meant it.
(Green slaps Glass on the back.) <Johnny Green> Oh ... too bad ... guess it looks like my turn ... <Perris> No ... you're not working his match either. (Daye laughs out loud at the other two refs.) Sorry Peter, you're not working it either ... (All three referees look at Perris in disbelief. If not any of them, then who? Before Perris can continue, Kat Collins, dressed in a jeans and a tight tube top, walks by carrying her trophy won for the Evening Gown match against
Kimberly Davis. As the four men stop and look at the attractive Kat, she smiles, giving a wink to Perris.) <Kat Collins> Looking good, boss ... (Without another word, she wanders off down the hallway. Leaving them in the back, we make our way to the ring where Skip Westervelt and Chip have made their way into the ring to a heavy chorus of boos.)
<Skip Westervelt> You know ... I feel like I've done this before ... oh wait, I did do this just a few short weeks ago when Jason Flint and myself hammered the
Collins for the Tag Team Titles only to have them unfairly taken away by B.J.
Perris ... (Westervelt pauses to shake his head.) And now, I'm here with another partner to have a second go at the belts ... with an added extra to make this match just ever so slightly interesting for you fans because who wants to see the Collins get their asses kicked again?
We don't ... do you? (Several shouts spread amongst the audience as Westervelt continued.) In order to make this just a little more interesting, the illiterate Bob Cage
... (Skip stops as Chip leans over and whispers something into his ear, causing
Skip to laugh.)
Oh sorry, Brian Cage and Ike Rogers have been added to this match but as I understand it, Mr. Rogers couldn't hack it in MY neighborhood, leaving him without a partner ...
Who the HELL is this ... (Skip stopped as someone in a dirty and ill-fitting suit made his we to the ring, stumbling along as he went. As he grabbed at the mic, getting it on the third try, Skip and Chip took a step back, waving their hands in front of their faces from the obvious bad smell.)
<Bob> My name ish Bob ... (The crowd was quiet as they waited for more from the man who seemed to sway as he stood there looking out at the audience.)
And I manage the bestesth ... (Again he stopped, this time he hiccupped.) wrestler in the whole world ... (The man swung his arm around to the back, nearly falling over as he did so. A man dressed in a sleeveless flannel shirt with jeans and work boots came out from the back and made his way into the ring. Not the tallest man in the world, he looked powerful ... not ripped like many wrestlers, just big.)
Felixsh Wade ... the "Bouncer" ... (Taking the mic away from Bob, this person had a few words as the fans grew a little restless.)
<Felix Wade> I'd be more than happy to step in and ... ("Blind" by Korn started to play as Brian Cage came out from the back and approached the ring. The booing was loud as the Texas crowd let him know how they felt about him, which brought several rude gestures from the wrestler.
Cage climbed into the ring and looked over his prospective partner and smirked as he looked over at Westervelt and Chip. The booing continued as they were joined by Justin Hart and Neowolf. Hart, with a mic in hand, spoke for the pair.)
<Justin Hart> I want to know just what the HELL is going on here! I'm looking into the ring and I see two guys without a match under their belts getting a shot at the Tag Titles? There is no way that you are going to leave us out of this match!
(Chants of "KICK HIS ASS!" rose from the crowd as Hart looked around the ring as the two referees for the first match made their way to the ring.)
I think the only ones we're missing are the so-called Tag Team Champions! (Queen's "We are the Champions" blared over the PA system as Tara Collins led brother Ron to the ring, carrying both tag titles over her shoulder. Dropping the belts ringside, both Collins' dove into the ring and suddenly there was an
8 person brawl.
Green and Glass were going to have their hands full as they did their best to clear the ring after nearly two minutes of a slugfest between the wrestlers that saw Felix Wade's manager, Bob tossed from the ring by Neowolf and Hart as well as Brian Cage worked over by Tara and Ron Collins while his partner seemed to have better luck against Skip and Chip in the far corner. After getting four wrestlers in the ring ... Tara, Cage, Chip and Hart inside the ring, Green called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The rules were simple ... the first person to get a pinfall or submission became the new tag champions.
Tara went after Cage which left Chip and Hart to have a go at it. Chip was in all kinds of trouble right from the start taking several Forearm Uppercuts and a Boot to the midsection before a Leaping Hair Grab Face Slam. Chip barely kicked out at the 2 count.
Across the ring, Cage had no problems hitting a woman as he Whipped Tara hard into the corner before hitting her with a Head Butt that seemed to stagger her.
Grabbing her by the hair, Cage found her ready as she delivered a Head Butt of her own as she came out of the corner with a Snapmare into a Kneedrop off the second rope.
Hart put Chip down with a Body Slam and was firing away with Jabs at Skip
Westervelt, who rushed into the ring. Skip was bounced out of the ring after an
Atomic Drop. Skip then complained to Dan Glass about a closed fist as Hart pounded away at Chip.
Ron Collins was tagged in and came off the ropes with a Slingshot Leg Drop and dropped for a 1 count. Pushing Cage into the corner, Ron used his foot to choke
Cage only to take a shot in the "Family Jewels" causing him to crumble up on the mat. With a Death Valley Driver, Cage tagged in Wade and set up Collins for a Double Suplex. Wade got a near pinfall only to take a Stomp in the back from
Tara Collins.
On the outside of the ring, Hart and Neowolf were battling it out with Skip and
Chip, having set up a table on the stage area and Powerbombing Chip through it.
The "Electric Referee" Dan Glass was warning them about the double teaming. Cage broke that up as he decked Neowolf with a chair and was in turn taken down with a Drop Toe Hold by Hart.
Inside the ring, Tara was Body Slammed and tossed out by Wade who was sent over the top rope with a Drop Kick from Ron Collins. All 8 wrestlers were battling outside the ring. Both Tara and Ron were taken down by a Double Clothesline from Wade while Cage was taking turns firing away with Punches on Chip, Skip and Neowolf while Hart was busy setting up another table.
Tara's Low Blow stopped Wade who was then knocked down by a Swinging Neckbreaker from Ron. They then took turns with Leg Drops. Hart grabbed both
Collins by the hair and smashed their heads together before dragging them around the ring towards the table. As he did so, Wade's drunken manager leapt off the ring apron and onto Hart's back. Trying to get the man off his back,
Hart pitched his body forward sending the lush through the table with a makeshift Body Slam, then took a Double Drop Kick himself thanks to the Texas
Terrors who had recovered.
Neowolf took Skip, Chip and Cage down with a Spinning Leg Lariat from the ring apron. Skip Westervelt rolled off of the stage area and crashed on the floor below. Green watched closely as Ron Collins had Hart in the air with his Bow and Arrow Submission while Tara was busy with Double Axe Handles to his stomach. Tara continued to ask for the submission but Hart wasn't quitting.
Seeing his partner in trouble, Neowolf took down Tara with a Clothesline then
Stomped Ron, breaking the hold.
With Felix Wade attending to his fallen manager, Cage set up a metal chair before lifting Chip for a Body Slam and tossing him onto the chair, crushing it underneath his weight in the process.
Neowolf pulled Ron Collins to his feet and hit him with a Standing Side Kick, right into Hart's arms who took him down with a Side Russian Leg Sweep. With
Ron down, Neowolf scooped up Tara who slid off behind and dropped down to deliver a Low Blow. Grabbing Tara up, Hart Gorilla Pressed her into the air and dropped her on her face before rolling Ron into the ring.
Whipping Ron into the corner, Hart followed with a high Corner Splash. After a couple of Forearms, Hart pushed Ron up the ropes as the fans all got to their feet for the big finish.
On the other side of the ring, Cage pulled Chip up onto the ring apron as he directed Felix Wade to set up yet another table. Chip was hit several times until he quit struggling before being lifted into the air by the very strong
Cage.
On the outside of the ring, Neowolf was pulled to his feet and swung around in the air before being lifted and brought down on his head with the Collins'
"Texas Toast" Piledriver. Neowolf was out as Tara dropped across him for the cover. Inside the ring, Hart pulled Ron Collins to the top rope and dropped with a
Superplex that shook it. At the same time, Brian Cage had lifted Chip up for a
Powerbomb only to take a chairshot to the back of his head from Felix Wade's manager, Bob. Dropping like a ton of bricks, both Chip and Cage fell through the table.
Johnny Green slapped out a count with Justin Hart dropping for a cover while
Dan Glass, on the outside of the ring did the same with Cage landing on top of
Chip. With a three count, both referees called for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (There was pandemonium as Hart bounced into the air as Green raised his arm while on the outside, Dan Glass was pointing down as Brian Cage, who in his eyes was victorious. Seeing what was going on in the ring, Felix Wade pointed it out to Glass. Always ready to help, the fans began chanting "You got screwed!" as Tara watched on still holding Neowolf to the floor.
Getting into the ring, Glass grabbed Green by the shoulder and spun him around, pointing to the still downed Brian Cage. Green smiled as he shook his head and pointed towards Hart. As the two referees argued, Tara Collins was on the outside shaking her head in disbelief as she sat there holding Neowolf's shoulders down.
Both refs were arguing until Johnny Green looked back before cold-cocking Dan
Glass. With Glass out, Green collected the belts and handed them to Hart. Felix
Wade argued briefly before thinking better of it and collecting his manager and getting out of there before Brian Cage regained consciousness.
Hart hoisted the belts in the air before being hit in the back and taken down with a Reverse NeckBreaker by Tara Collins. After "crowning" Hart, she turned on Johnny Green and threatened him, angrily leaving the ring with her brother and disappearing into the back.
Green smiled as he looked around at the fallen bodies inside and out of the ring before heading into the back. Leaving the ring area for the back, referee
Peter Daye is seen flat on his back with the members of the State Club Boyz standing over him, each with an evil grin. The largest one rubbing his knuckles before one by one, they were taken down by a dark clocked assailant swinging a baseball bat. The person looked down and laughed before taking of as Jake
Morgan ran into view followed by Mean Darlene and several of the EW Security force in tow.)
<Jake Morgan> That's not 13 ... (Leaving the back for the ring and the next match where the Offspring was patiently waiting for his opponent to appear. He didn't have to wait long as
Neowolf appeared, supported by his friend Justin Hart, he weaved a wobbily path towards the ring with much needed help, most likely still feeling the effects of the "Texas Toast" from the previous match.
Hart offered some last second instructions before all but pushing him into the ring and helping him into a corner where he was propped up. Everyone waited until the last participant of the match, the referee showed up ... and it wasn't the intended Peter Daye that came out from the back but the "Electric
Referee", Jason Flint's handpicked, Dan Glass. Entering the ring, he called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Seeing Neowolf's condition, the Offspring came out pointing to the far corner and his opponent who barely moved. Glass danced back and slapped his hands together wanting some action. Spring shrugged his shoulders but obliged as he moved to Neo's corner.
With a Snap Suplex, Spring struck first hopping up onto the ropes and landing a
Split Leg Moonsault for a cover. Glass dropped for a count but stopped on the
2, claiming Neo's shoulder up.
On his feet, Spring wanted to know what was up before dropping down fast with a
Leg Drop and following it with a Standing Senton, again going for the quick cover and getting another 2 count. Complaining did no good as Spring was taken down with a Standing Side Kick to the head. Neo fell back against the ropes trying to steady himself with the Offspring down and was unable to capitalize on his attack.
Rolling to his back and kipping to his feet, Spring barely ducked under another
Kick, reaching in and taking his opponent down with a Leg Drag. From behind,
Spring applied a Leg Scissors and rolled his body around the ring, taking Neo with him in a Rolling Leg Scissors Cradle. The pair rolled until Neo's shoulders were down to the mat were Glass dropped and hit a 2 count. Using momentum, Neo rolled his shoulders off the mat but continued to roll as Offy rolled his body in unison, again putting the shoulders down for a 2 count.
The fans cheered the moves as Neo tried to clear his head. Spring was not about to let him do that as he came in with a Drop Kick to the knees. Bouncing off the ropes, Spring landed a Corkscrew Senton and got a 1 count. Climbing the ropes, Spring leapt off with a Shooting Star Press, spinning through the air and hitting canvas as Hart pulled Neowolf from the ring. Wasting no time,
Spring bounced to his feet and ran for the far ropes and dove over the top, taking Neo and Hart to the stage floor with a Plancha. From the apron, he hit a
Moonsault on the unmoving Neowolf as the fans cheered.
Pulling Neo in by the hair, Spring took the action back into the ring. With a
Whip into the ropes, Spring Leap Frogged over Neo before flattening him with a
Drop Kick on the return. Grabbing the man's legs, Spring fell back and sent Neo into the opposite turnbuckle with a Slingshot Catapault. Kipping to his feet and with his back to his opponent, Offy caught the staggering man's arms, dropping to the mat with a Backslide. Glass counted to 2 until Spring lost his grip.
With another Whip into the ropes, Neo found himself flat on his back from a
Hurricanrana as Offy bounced to his feet and climbed the ropes and held his arms overhead as the fans clapped. Returning to the match with a Hip Toss,
Spring grabbed the seated Neowolf with an Inverted Facelock and pulled him slowly to his feet. Lifted for what appeared to be a Dragon Suplex, Spring shifted his weight as he tossed the man up over his shoulder. Getting the weight just right across his shoulders, Spring flipped him over so that Neo was looking up at the ceiling. Holding onto his head and neck, Spring spun Neowolf out from his body as he sat down into an Inverted Spinning Neck Breaker. Seeing the manhandling, Hart was up on the apron shouting at the referee. Spring came over with the two going into a staring contest. There was a heavy cheer as the fans anticipated a change in the programming only to find the ref getting inbetween the two.
He warned both men but did nothing to get Hart off of the apron thus giving
Neowolf much needed time to get back into the match. Finally Hart dropped from the apron and the match continued. As Offspring turned back towards his opponent, he took a Double Thrust to the throat. Staggering backwards, Spring was caught and thrown over with an Ipponzei then taking a Kick to the back of his head. Pulling Spring to his feet, Neowolf missed with a Spinning Back Kick as his target ducked under it then hit the mat from a Diving Clothesline.
Jumping up quickly, Spring hit the ropes and flipped backwards but rather than land a Moonsault, he landed on his feet near Neowolf. From a standing position, he dropped a Leg across Neo's throat before pulling him up and throwing through the ropes and out onto the stage floor. With Neowolf out on the floor, the
Offspring dashed across the ring, throwing his body back against the ropes and taking off once again across the ring ... with a Handspring, Offy jumped up onto the top rope and took a couple of seconds to steady himself, almost falling back into the ring before diving through the air and landing on top of
Neo with a Somersault Leg Drop.
On his feet, Spring staggered away holding onto his tailbone landing heavily onto the Neo and the hard floor. Grabbing Neo's legs, the Offspring lifted him into the air from behind and dropped to the floor again with a Face First
Powerbomb. The fans were cheering as they sensed the end of the match coming.
As the Offspring returned to his feet, Justin Hart had seen enough. Rushing in, he pushed the Offspring back away from his tag partner. Glass moved in as the
Offspring shoved back before the two were hammering away at each other. As the two slugged it out, they soon disappeared into the back as the fans rose to their feet straining to see the action.
Referee Dan Glass checked on the condition of Neowolf who was unmoving. Looking back towards the entranceway to the back, Glass waited and watched ... look from the entranceway to the unmoving Neowolf. Some seconds later, he called for the bell before moving to Neowolf and rasing his arm into the air.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were booing loudly as Glass called for help as Neowolf laid there unmoving. Moving towards the back as he was pelted with items from the fans,
Glass stopped in his tracks as B.J. Perris walked out from the back.)
<Perris> I think we have a little problem here ... (A chant of "Kick his ass!" started as Perris walked up to the referee.) Now ... with the Offspring disappearing into the back fighting with Justin Hart and our no Disqualification Policy ... you are well within your rights to count him out ...
(The fans started booing as Glass nodded his head in agreement.) But ... seeing as Mr. Neowolf has not even moved since being Powerbombed into the floor ... I am overruling your decision ... this match is a No Contest!
(Glass argued as the fans cheered the decision change from the President who strolled to the back with a smile on his face.
Leaving the ring, two men were brawling in the back ... not the Offspring and
Justin Hart as expected but the "Bouncer" Felix Wade being egged on by his manager Bob the Drunk, and Brian Cage were locked in a struggle, both men hammering away with an assortment of Punches and Forearms. After an Eye Gouge,
Cage was getting the better of Wade who had dropped to a knee and was taking
Forearms across the head and back until the masked Desperado floored Cage with a chair to the back.
In the background, the battling Hart and Offspring were visible still fighting it out until the EW Security swarmed over the hallway and desperately worked to pry the wrestlers apart.
Back inside the ring, "Remote Control" by the Beastie Boys blared through the
PA system as the EW Texas Heavyweight Champion, Jason Flint, made his way from the back. Following closely behind by Kimberly Davis and Andrea, the Electric
One is the embodiement of confidence. Slapping a few hands here and there,
Jason Flint made his way towards the ring. Pausing to soak in the fans
"electricity," Flint snatched up a mic and climbed into the ring.) <Jason Flint> Now for the most part, the Electric One is here to one.....fight
Jyhad, the jiggling Hardcore Messiah. For that, you fans shall see what hardcore here in Extreme Wrestling is all about!
(The fans cheer for the upcoming matchup) But the only thing that the Electric One really needs to address is the overturning of the new ref, Dan Glass' decision from the show where
I......your....champion and Skip Westervelt, the TV Champ, won the Tag Belts. (The fans jeer a bit, remembering back to the time of the debut of the "new" EW referee.) All I wanted was to have a FAIR, IMPARTIAL judge who can call it right down the middle, so all participants would be satisfied with the results, where the winners where known and the losers were made. Where the law was the law and the buck stopped here, but it seems that this was not the case. Ah well, too bad for Dan.
(Flint shrugs innocently.) But since the Offspring is so hellbent on claiming that the EW belt is not prestigious enough without it on his waist, lets bring out the Holy War Messiah and show to all of you EWers that Jason Flint IS the BEST damn champion this promotion has ever known!
(Flint tosses the mic down and waits patiently for his opponent to come out when B.J. Perris, weaqring a referee's black and white shirt made his way to the ring.)
<Perris> You want fair and impartial, Flint ... then you've got it ... you may have stolen one out from under my eyes a month ago ... but you're not going to do that today!
(Perris pointed at the belt resting around Flint's waist as the Champion looked on not believing what he was hearing. He didn't get a chance to argue as the silent Jyhad made his way to the ring. With both men inside, Perris called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** A cocky Flint charged out of his corner and dipped his shoulder into Jyhad trying to put him down. Nothing doing. Bouncing off the ropes, Flint did again, hitting the larger Jyhad with no luck. Jyhad was not going to be taken off his feet. The big silent man motioned for Flint to come again. Shrugging his shoulders, Flint came on and hit Jyhad, only to be taken off of his feet thanks to Jyhad's Shoulder Butt. Flint rolled out of the ring before further damage could be done.
At Perris' urging, Flint rolled back into the ring ready to continue. Locking up in the center of the ring earned Flint a seat on the mat as Jyhad tossed him off and across the ring. Nodding his head, Flint came in and locked up with
Jyhad again but as Jyhad pushed forward, Flint ducked to the side and twisting the arm into an Arm Bar, twisting even further into an Arm Wringer before booting him under the armpit then twisting the big man off of his feet and onto his back.
With Jyhad sitting up, Flint came in with a Drop Kick to the face and rolling him up for a pin attempt that was kicked out of on a 2 count. After smacking
Jyhad's head into the top turnbuckle, Flint took him down with a Small Package.
Jyhad kicked Flint off before a 3 count but the Champ, still with a hold of
Jyhad's leg, FLint was able to turn him to his stomach before stepping over and locking on a Half Boston Crab. Perris watched the pain etched face of Jyhad.
Thinking quickly, the big man grabbed the referee and pulled him into Flint, breaking the hold. As Jyhad limped away, Flint put him back down with a Leg
Clip before hitting the ropes and coming down with a Knee Drop.
Pulling Jyhad to his feet, Flint sent him across the ring into the far ropes. On the return, Flint sent him over the ropes with a Leg Scissor Takedown. Holding the ropes as he slammed Jyhad down on the stage floor, Flint flipped back into the ring and called for a mic.
<Jason Flint> Since you people come to Extreme Wrestling in order to see just that and not my Mad Skillz ... then as your Champion, I feel that's my duty to give you just that ... "Electric Style!"
Dropping the mic as the fans cheered, Flint took off across the ring and jumped up onto the ropes. Spinning as he did so, he threw his body backwards, landing an Asai Moonsault. Jumping to his feet and heading to the edge of the stage,
Flint turned his back to the fans before launching his body off , flipping through the air and landing on his feet below. The fans gave a standing ovation as Flint bowed to them. Grabbing up a couple of chairs, he headed back up onto the stage.
After slamming one down over Jyhad's head, he laid it over his face then climbed up onto the ring apron where he jumped off and slammed the other chair down onto Jyhad's head. With a smile on his face, Flint gave his "All the Way to the Top" signature before pulling Jyhad to his feet. The bleeding "Hardcore
Holy Warrior" looked anything but that as blood trickled down into his face.
After a Superkick, Flint setup one of the chairs then German Suplexed Jyhad on top of it, crushing it in the process.
Perris spun Flint around and warned him to move the match back into the ring. Shrugging his shoulders, Flint left Jyhad and re-entered the ring with the remaining chair in hand. Climbing up the ropes, Flint jumped off, swinging the chair underneath him and landing an Arabian Facebuster.
A little slow to get back up, Flint did so with help from both Andrea and
Kimberly. With some help from Kimberly, Flint pushed a table from under the ring inside. After leaning it up into the corner of the ring, Flint went back outside and helped Jyahd to his feet and into the ring. Knocking him over with a Roundhouse Kick, Flint caught a chair tossed into the ring by Kimberly.
Waiting for Jyhad to stand, Flint smashed the chair over Jyhad's head. The big man wobbled then fell to the mat.
As the fans cheered, Flint made a gesture with his thumb across his throat before checking the table in the corner. Pulling Jyhad back to his feet, Flint, with his back to the table, wrapped his arms around the big man's waist from behind and lifted him off of his feet. With a quick snatch, Flint tossed Jyhad backwards and threw him back through the table. Dragging Jyhad to the center of the ring, Flint came off the top rope with a Corkscrew Splash and hooked a leg for the cover. This one was mercifully over.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were cheering as Flint celebrated in the ring as Perris handed him the Texas Heavyweight Belt. Lifting it, Flint never saw the man in the dark robes slide into the ring until Andrea shouted a warning. The man approached clutching a baseball bat and threatened both the ref and Flint who backed away, slipping behind Perris. With the unknown man moving in closer, Flint's State
Club Boyz rushed the ring only to be hit by the baseball bat just as they had earlier.
As the man hammered the three, Flint gathered some courage and tugged the hood off of the robed individual. As it pulled away, the man in a tattered white and bloody red t-shirt, , jeans and black combat boots, snapped his spiked blue haired head around and grinned at the Champion ... his face painted black with white and neon blue outlining his eyes and mouth along with the word "Crazy" on his forehead was a sight to see.
Even with a different look it was easy to see that it was Raskal! Raskal was back! Surprised by Raskal's visage, the Champion ducked out of the ring into the arms of Andrea and headed to the back as the "Killer Klown" climbed the ropes and raised his bat into the air as a chant of "EXTREME!" rose from the standing fans as the show came to an end.)
