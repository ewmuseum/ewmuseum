Extreme Wrestling: 5.30.99 Card

30 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(With music blaring in the background, we find a lone man dresssed in jeans and a gray t-shirt standing in the center of a well-worn wrestling ring. Waiting for the cheers of the fans surrounding the ring to die down, the man pushes his long reddish hair out of his eyes before raising a mic to his lips as the music dies away.) <Perris> Greetings ladies and gentlemen … I am your host, B.J. Perris and welcome to our first Extreme Wrestling card on this Memorial Day Weekend! We have a great card for you today with plenty of action … (The fans were cheering loudly in what appeared to be a rather large backyard in the center of which was a beatup ring. Several rows of chairs surrounded the ring on three sides with the side nearest the brick house standing room only. Along the back fence were several tables … selling t-shirts and other souvenirs as well as food. The smells coming from the large cast iron BBQ were out of this world as witnessed by the long line. To the back of the property was a single “port-o-potty” that had a waiting line. The fence surrounding the property had several people peering over from the other side, trying to get a free look at the action. All told, there was perhaps 200 or so people watching the action.) Before we get to our first match, I must warn you all, what you are about to see contains violence, adult themes and strong language. It may not be suited for all members of the viewing audience. Your parental discretion is strongly advised. This is by no means what has been termed as “Backyard Wrestling” … all of these men and women are trained professionals. This … is … EXTREME! Please do not try this at home! (“No Fucking War” by 7 Year Bitch blared out over the loudspeakers as a dark haired woman in a black tube top and a tight knee length skirt with impossibly high heels made her way to the ring to a chorus of catcalls and whistles. The busty woman did not look too happy as she entered the ring, glaring at
Perris who had a smile on his face.) Ladies and gentlemen … may I present to you, Mean Darlene! (A chant of “Show your tits” started from a group of fraternity guys that quickly spread. Tearing the mic from Perris’ hands, she looked out at the fans.) <Mean Darlene> I don’t know what the fuck you are doing by putting me in this get-up but I am not wearing it and if you don’t like it … too fucking bad! (An unhappy Darlene kicked off her shoes first then with a quick yank, tore the skirt off revealing a pair of compression shorts underneath before dropping the mic and walking off. After grabbing up the mic, an angered Perris grabbed her by the shoulder and twirled her around.) <Perris> And just where the Hell do you think you are going? You work for me, bitch and if I tell you that you are wearing that outfit, you’ll wear it and like it! (A surprised Darlene grabbed the mic back from Perris.) <Mean Darlene> Is that so? Well … what if I tell you to go fuck yourself, asshole? (Darlene raised her arms in the air as the fans cheered for her. It was easy to see who they liked and who they didn’t. Handing the mic back to Perris, she again started to leave the ring before Perris grabbed her and roughly shoved her to the mat. The fans were booing as “Burn it Up” by the Offspring started up. Coming to the ring was a man with a head full of long and a rather thick mass of tight braids
… the ends frayed out and bleached … the hair hanging down and completely covering his face. Underneath it all, barely peaking out was a black mask obscuring his features. The shirtless buff man in his tights, sported tattoos on both arms … a purple shield looking much like a bat and on the other arm, a purple and black yin-yang symbol sitting in the forehead of a skull. With a hop over the ring ropes, the man was in the ring staring down Perris.) <Perris> Look Offspring, I don’t know what you are doing out here but I pay you to wrestle … not to speak. (The Offspring grabbed away the mic from Perris who took a few steps back from the larger wrestler. Holding the mic in one hand and pointing at Perris with his other, Spring had something to say.) <Offspring> Where I come from, we don’t treat ladies like that. If you are looking for someone to push around … maybe you’d like to try that with me? (The fans rose from their seats in anticipation of what was about to come. With
Perris stepping back, the Offspring turned to help up Darlene but was hit from behind by a Spinning Back Kick. Rolling over and kipping to his feet, the Offspring spun around and dropped
Perris with a Spinning Kick of his own. Hitting the middle ropes, he came off with a Moonsault on top of the downed Perris. Back on his feet, he helped Darlene up before the two left the ring to the chanting of his name. Extreme Wrestling was underway! Helped to his feet by the referee, a wobbly Perris made his way into the house as a lovely Hispanic women walked to the ring. In a long gown and heels, the young woman entered the ring.) <Veronica Torres> Standing six feet even and weighing in at 221 pounds … here is Pep Preston! (A man with short messy brown hair in red trunks and matching boots made his way to the ring to a slight cheer from the fans, slapping a few hands as he went. Inside the ring, Preston bounced off of the ropes as he waited for his opponent.) And his opponent, from Denton, Texas … standing five feet and eleven inches and weighing 185 pounds, here is the Trashman! (A thin man wearing a gas mask hauling a metal garbage can behind him full of weapons came running from the house. Dressed in jeans and a t-shirt with the words “Got trash?” across the front of it, the Trashman tossed his can into the ring as the referee called for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Trashman came flying across the ring and took it to Preston with several Punches and a Double Axe Handle across the back before tearing off the gas mask revealing short bright orange hair and a not so sane look in his eye before pulling a toaster from the can and smashing it over Preston’s back. The fans were cheering as Trashman unloaded on Preston with the items from his can. The worst blow came following an Irish Whip as T-man dropped Pep with a blow across the head from a crutch that splintered. To make things worse, he dug the sharp remains into Pep’s head, busting him open. Following a Body Slam, Trashman grabbed up the can and made his way up the ropes before coming off with a Splash, demolishing the can. Having the pin,
T-man pulled up Preston before the ref could count out the three. Preston rocked Trashman with a DDT after blocking a Body Slam and with the weapons not being used, showed his superior mat skills putting his opponent in several pinning predicaments before Trashman rolled to the outside of the ring
12 minutes into the match. After battling into the seats and being Suplexed onto a chair, the momentum swung back to Preston but a reversal of an Irish Whip into the ring apron quickly ended that. Following a massive chairshot to the head, Trashman pulled the announcer’s table away from Perris who had returned to the ring. After driving Preston through the table with a Shooting Star Press, Trashman pulled his opponent back into the ring before taking him up the ropes and dropping him to the mat with an incredible Super Hurrincanrana. It was academic as the ref slapped out the three. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The fans were cheering as Veronica Torres returned to the ring.) <Veronica Torres> Your winner, at 19:22 … the Trashman!!!!!!! (The ref helped a very bloody Preston to his feet and out of the ring as the
Trashman made his way to the house celebrating with the appreciative fans while several men in matching shirts cleared the ring of debris.) Our next match of the afternoon, with a 20 minute time limit … from Denton,
Texas … standing six foot, one inch and weighing a svelte 280 pounds here is …
Chippendale Chad!!!!!! (“Bump & Grind” music started playing as a young overweight man in tiny leopard print tights made his way to the ring. Stopping in front of a group of pretty ladies, Chad swiveled his hips in tune to the music. Despite being out of shape and overweight, several ladies tucked dollar bills into his tights as he continued to the ring. Inside the ring Chad did a little dance for Veronica Torres before grabbing the mic.) <Chippendale Chad> Well … well … well … you’ve seen the dancing. You’ve seen the beautiful body … (Chad flexed his biceps and did several poses that brought some laughs from the fans.) Now … are you ready to see this promotion’s best athlete? (As several people in the front row were laughing, Chad made his way to the ropes and pointed at them, threatening them but stopped as Torres took back the mic.) <Veronica Torres> And his opponent, from Carrollton, Texas … here is El Diablo Rojo! (Covered from head to toe in red with little horns on his head, the masked wrestler ran to the ring, sliding under the ropes as the ref called for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Chad charged across the ring and was taken down with an Arm Drag that shook the ring as the large man hit the mat. A second, then third time Chad came at EDR and each time was taken down with the same move. Sliding from the ring, Chad needed a breather. EDR was not about to give him the needed time as he threw himself back against the ropes and went running across the ring. Using the ropes to catapault himself into the air, EDR came flying off with a Plancha but was caught out of the air and dropped across Chad’s knee with a Back Breaker Drop. Chad then used the ring posts as he pummeled EDR before Whipping him into the metal ring steps. Turning his attention to the fans and jawing with them, Chad gave EDR time to recover and get back into the match with a Dragon Sleeper. Using his weight advantage, Chad slammed the masked man back into the ring apron breaking the hold. Hammering away with Forearms, Chad drove EDR to his knees before pulling him up and dumping him with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex onto the grass. Taking the match back inside the ring, a tired Chippendale Chad used his weight to slow down EDR which brought a few “Boring” chants from the fans. To his credit, El Diablo did his best to quicken the pace of the match with several high risk moves such as a Standing Moonsault and a couple of Sentons off of the ropes but wasn’t able to get the pinfall. An exhausted Chad was Whipped through the ropes and appeared to have lost the match following a Suicide Slingshot Leg Drop as EDR dropped on top of him for the pin but the bell rang as ref Peter Daye dropped to the grass outside of the ring for the count. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Torres entered the ring as Chad left for the house while El Diablo Rojo showed some life still in him with a back flip off of the ring apron before signing autographs for the appreciative fans.) <Veronica Torres> This match has finished in a Time Limit Draw! (The fans were booing over the result of the match as El Diablo Rojo worked the match much harder in the later stages, actually carrying it to completion.) There will be a short intermission before our final match of the evening. x-x-x-x-x (There was a break in the action as several of the EW’s wrestlers came out and signed autographs and mingled with the fans … El Diablo Rojo … the Trashman …
Pep Preston … the Offspring and Mean Darlene during the short intermission. The festivities stopped as B.J. Perris made his way into the ring. The tapping on the microphone got everyone’s attention.) <Perris> During this break, it is my distinct pleasure to introduce you all to a man each and every one of you will getting to know in a very short amount of time. Without further ado … here is the “Violator” … Ronnie Smith!!!!! (A man with a clean shaven head wearing black trunks and boots came walking out to the ring. Inside the ring, the man stood with his arms folded over his chest, looking out at fans. Taking the mic from Perris, Smith walked to the near ropes where he pointed at each of the wrestlers signing autographs until he was pointing at the Offspring.) <Ronnie Smith> It’s going to be my pleasure meeting each of you in the ring and stepping over your fallen bodies as I move on to the titles in this place. (Dropping the mic, the man left the ring just as quickly as he entered as the fans booed. With the interruption over, the wrestlers went back to signing autographs until
Veronica Torres made her way into the ring.) x-x-x-x-x (The wrestlers made their way to the house, disappearing inside as the fans chanted “Extreme … Extreme … Extreme …”.) <Veronica Torres> Our final match of the afternoon set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit … introducing first, from Lewisville, Texas weighing 245 pounds and standing six foot three … here is Billy Eagle!!!!!! (The handsome American indian came out to the ring wearing a red, white and blue singlet done up like the Texas state flag. White tape on his wrists and red boots finished his outfit.) And his opponent, being led to the ring by Extreme Wrestling President, B.J. Perris … at five feet, ten inches and weighing 210 pounds … the “Violator” …
Ronnie Smith!!!!! (Perris and Smith walked to the ring as the fans booed heartily for the pair. Smith threatened several fans that were being pushed back by the EW Security people. Once they were inside the ring, Billy Eagle asked for the mic from
Veronica Torres.) <Billy Eagle> I don’t know who you think you are or what you mean to this promotion’s President but you are not climbing to the top over my body. Not today or any other day. (The fans were cheering for Eagle as he charged out of his corner after dropping the mic.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Pushing Smith into a corner, Eagle began Chopping away at the smaller man’s chest … red welts rising from each blow that landed. Reversing positions, Smith began driving Forearms into Eagle. Eagle put down Smith with a Clothesline that caught him by surprise. With his opponent holding his jaw, Billy Eagle started a War Dance around the ring. Returning to the action, Eagle dropped a Leg Drop to the back of Smith’s head. Back on his feet, Eagle started bouncing off of the ropes for added steam. Back and forth across the ring, Eagle went until Perris reached in and tripped the wrestler up causing him to hit the mat facefirst. Referee Peter Daye was over warning his boss who backed away from the ring pleading innocence. After dropping Elbows onto Eagle, Smith had a pin following an Overhead Belly-to-Belly Suplex but Eagle had a shoulder up before the ref’s hand slapped the mat for a third time. From this point for the next 10 minutes, Smith had control of the match dishing out punishment as he pummeled Eagle with Punches and Forearms or drove him into the canvas Body Slams and Suplex variations refusing to take the pin. After 15 minutes, Eagle came out of a Side Headlock taking Smith down with a
Russian Leg Sweep out of nowhere and appeared to have the match but was pulled off of Smith as Perris grabbed his leg and pulled. That got Eagle’s attention as he came to the ropes chasing Perris. A Reverse DDT put Eagle out of action as Daye counted 1 … 2 … 3 … *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Perris jumped into the ring and raised Smith’s arm into the air celebrating the victory as Veronica Torres made her way back into the ring.) <Veronica Torres> The winner of the match at 16:19, the “Violator” Ronnie Smith
(Perris took the mic from Torres as Smith left the ring and made his way to the house.) <Perris> And that ladies and gentlemen brings us to the end of our show except for this last little bit of business. Darlene, for insubordination … you’re fired … (The fan’s boos quickly turned to cheers as Mean Darlene, dressed in her more usual attire of a tank top, jean shorts and heavy black combat boots made her way to the ring followed by the Offspring.) <Mean Darlene> Is that so? <Perris> That’s the way it is. You’re fired. <Mean Darlene> Then I guess it doesn’t matter if I do this? (With that, she reached back and nailed Perris in the face with a straight right hand that staggered him. The fans were chanting “Kick his ass!” as Darlene climbed the ropes. Perris, angered by the blow moved forward but was grabbed by the Offspring and lifted into the air then dumped on the canvas with a Body Slam before Darlene jumped off the top and landed a Leg Drop onto Perris. Kipping back to her feet, Darlene grabbed up the mic.) <Mean Darlene> Now … that, ladies and gentlemen brings us to the end of our show! (She and the Offspring high-fived before leaving the ring to the cheers of a satisfied crowd.) x-x-x-x-x Black Cat Entertainment©1999
