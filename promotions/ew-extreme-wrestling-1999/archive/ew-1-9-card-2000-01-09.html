The Fraternity trio left the ring with Flint as a familiar face hit the ring ... the baseball bat brandishing Trashman ... After he cleared the of Flint and his boys and Tara was pulled to the outside by Ron and Kat, Johnny Green in his referee uniform snatched the belt up off of
Peter Daye who was just starting to come to. Shaking his head, Green left the ring with the belt.
The Offspring was about to give chase to Green when the Trashman held out an arm and stopped him. Ready to go around him, Spring was again met with resistance as Trash shoved him back with a hand to the chest.
Ron and Kat, with big smiles on their faces, stood there expecting the belt to be handed to Tara as Johnny Green approached. The smiles melted away and turned to complaints as Green walked right on by and smacked Flint, who was being held up by two of the Frat guys with the belt flush in the face, knocking all three to the ground.
Not knowing what Trash was doing, Offspring stood there with his arms out wanting an answer. All he got was a disgusted wave as Trash left the ring and the show came to an end to a mixed reaction of booing and cheering.
