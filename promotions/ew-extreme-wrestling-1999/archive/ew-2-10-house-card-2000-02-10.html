EW (Denton, 1999) — Card

Extreme Wrestling: 2.10 House Card

10 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( A black Lexus is seen pulling up in the back of the Campus Theater as several of the EW Security Force come running out and surround it. One security man waving a walkie-talkie attempts to have the car back up when one of the back doors opens ... before large red numbers burst onto a black screen in rapid succession.)
... 5 ... ... 4 ... ... 3 ... ... 2 ... ... 1 ... (Rob Zombie's "More Human than Human starts to play loudly as clips of Extreme
Wrestling's Superstars in action passes before your eyes ... Justin Hart ...
Tara Collins and the Texas Terrors ... Andrea ... Jake Morgan ... the Offspring
... Extreme Wrestling's Television Champion, Skip Westervelt and finally, the
EW Texas Heavyweight Champion standing in the center of the ring lifting his title belt skyward ...
With an explosion, the scenes disappear from the screen and are replaced by the logo of Extreme Wrestling as it moves forward, filling the screen. The song suddenly stops and with a smooth transition, fades out ...
With a just as smooth fade in, the camera pans a small but very vocal crowd of fans sitting in the small studio area around the ring, raising signs for their favorite EW performers ... moving around, passing a wrestling ring before the massive mountain of a man known as Key walks out, the Director's chair under one arm and several black "boards" under his other. Following him is Hollywood, who receives a less that warm reception. Arriving at the small ringside platform, Hollywood waits for Key to open his chair, then sits. Motioning for the crowd to silence-which they do not-Hollywood reaches over to take a microphone from Key.)
<Hollywood> Good evening, everyone. I'd like to welcome you to MY show. (Hollywood smiles from his chair, in spite of the chorus of boos.) As you all may know, the Offspring's Television Title Defense has been removed from tonight's card. It is being replaced by the "Love Triangle" match between
Tara Collins, Kat Collins and Chip. I hope this pleases you all, but if not…
(A dramatic pause.)
It really doesn't matter. (The statement hangs in the air… the fans seeming to react badly. Hollywood could care less, gesturing toward the back.)
Now, if the stage manager would please report to me, we can get things started. (Walking out from the back-and drawing an instant pop-comes a nearly six-foot, amply proportioned red haired vixen, her long legs leading into a black mini-skirt. Holding a purple megaphone to her chest, she walks out to
Hollywood, mounting the platform and handing him the megaphone. Reaching down, she grasps the "marker" for the first match, holding it overhead as Hollywood announces the participants for the first match.
After Dennis Windham and Felix Wade along with his manager Bob the Drunk made their way to the ring, the red haired woman snapped the marker closed, then returned to Hollywood's side when "Blind" by Korn started to play and the lights dimmed momentarily.
The fans were buzzing as Brian Cage walked out carrying a hockey stick as he made his way to the ring.)
Ah yes ... I almost forgot ... add one more to the match ... (Cage walked to the ring and hopped up onto the apron, calling for a mic.) <Brian Cage> Here's my pass and new contract ... (Hollywood smiled and nodded his head as Cage waved his hockey stick in the air before entering the ring.)
Now ... I'm here to trim the fat!!!!! (Pushing referee Dan Glass out of the way, Cage struck, hitting both Windham and Wade over the head with the hockey stick. After hitting Wade in the throat with the stick, he broke it over Windham's back. With both wrestlers downed,
Cage pulled Glass to his feet who then called for the bell and the start of the match.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The two silent undercarders were no match for the brutal Cage who ripped into the two with the hockey stick ... okay, what was left of it. Glass became involved, warning Cage who was choking Windham with the remains of the stick in the corner.
The fans were cheering Cage who tossed the stick aside before pulling him up and putting him down with a Spinning Neckbreaker. Not forgetting his manners,
Cage pummeled Wade before planting him with a ChokeSlam.
The match went to the outside where Cage liberated a chair and set it up. Lifting Wade up with a Fireman's Carry, he dropped backwards squashing the chair with Wade's body. He was joined outside the ring by Windham where he was
Stomped but ended that quickly as he drove an Uppercut into the "Family
Jewels". Windham was on his knees coughing as a laughing Cage nailed him over the back with a Chair.
After pushing Wade into the ring, he grabbed Windham by the hair and dragged him in as well. Pushing Glass out of the way, he sent both opponents into the far ropes and knocked them both down with a Double CLothesline that had the fans hollowing.
With Windham down, Cage climbed the ropes pulling Wade up with him. After waving his arm in the air, he dropped off the top with a DDT, bouncing Wade's head off of Windham's body. With the two on top of each other, Cage covered them for the 3 count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Cage left the ring with his arms raised over his head after leaving the ring and heading to the back. After the camera flashed a smiling Hollywood who was nodding his head in approval, it moved over to his lovely redheaded assistant who was holding her "marker". Holding it in front of her, she smiled as the camera zoomed in on her "assets" ... er ... I mean her marker.)
<Redheaded Lovely> That's a wrap! (Fading out, the show broke away for a commercial.) x-x-x-x-x Clips of K-STaR's syndicated programs The A-Team and Knight Rider were followed
Ricki Lake and Real TV which were in turn followed by new shows Strip Poker and
10's.
After scantily clad women and men flashed across the screen, a snazzy little ditty about the Dallas Mavericks and highlights flashed by before returning to the program.
x-x-x-x-x (Back from the commercial, Hollywood stood, and smiling at his female companion stepped through the ropes and into the ring. Producing a microphone from the inside of his black jacket pocket, the Director coughs lightly over the surface of the black foam covering, seemingly pleased with the chorus of boos he draws from the crowd.)
<Hollywood> You've waited and waited, Denton. You've waited here, in this middleclass wasteland, for your taste of something better. Finally… Hollywood has come to your corner of the world…finally… there is a time for ACTION!
(The fans, almost the exact second the word leaves Hollywood's mouth, pop. Confused, but pleased, he continues, unaware that standing in the wrestler's entrance, the EW Television Title Belt over his shoulder and draping down over a Tim Duncan San Antonio Spurs basketball jersey, is the Offspring. The young, oftentimes outspoken wrestler lifts a microphone toward his braids. Hollywood goes on.)
I knew you'd all come to see it m… (Interruption comes with a series of pointing fingers and a single, trite phrase…) <Offspring> Excuse me? (Turning inside the ring, Hollywood glares over the frames of his glasses. He regains his composure quickly, responding as the crowd starts to wane back from cheering to relative silence.)
<Hollywood> Yes, I did. Your match has been moved to Sunday. Now if you'd kindly… <Offspring> These people-La RAZA!-came here to see the Offspring defend the
Television Title! Would you cheat them?
(Hollywood fakes a shudder, gesturing toward his platform and the beautiful red-headed vixen he refers to as his "Stage Manager." As she steps into the ring, he points to her, a wicked smile on his face.)
<Hollywood> Cheating them? What do you think a bunch of hicks want to see,
Offspring? A scrawny, mop headed Latino pogo stick, or… this?
(The fans seem torn, some cheering for the Offspring, some catcalling for the leggy femme. One specifically calls out, "Less talk, more <beep>".)
<Offspring> I think these people know exactly what they… (The Offspring's upper body continues to move as if he is speaking, however no sound can be heard beyond the boos of the crowd. Key, standing on the floor just outside the ring, holds a piece of cable in his hand, the center connecting segment of it popped loose and two copper threads almost, but not quite touching.)
<Hollywood> What's that? I can't hear you. (As EW Security moves in on the Offspring, the youngster pumps a fist in the air.) Security, does he have a ticket? (After a short conversation, the man seeming to claim leadership of the trio of security men shakes his head.)
Then please escort him out of my building. And Offspring? All these people might have wanted to see you… But that really doesn't matter. You've been cut from this scene. Live with it.
(As the Offspring is escorted through the door, his fist pumps again, then a single digit extends from it, saluting his "boss." Hollywood seems at ease with the gesture, putting one of his hands on his lady associate's shoulder and sliding it down her back, along the side of her hip, and then spinning it toward the doorway, waving.)
Now, then… before we were so rudely interrupted, I was about to inform you that due to the fact that both of them are unable to fight their ways out of a wet paper bag, Felix Wade, his vodka swilling manager, and Dennis "Momma Don't
Let Your Babies Grow Up to Be Me" Windham have been given their walking papers.
However if either of them would like to come out and try to convince me that
I've misjudged their talents, now's the time.
(Hollywood stood waiting for thirty seconds or so, conversing with his stage manager and shuffling his feet. Finally, he spoke again.)
I thought not. The Director has an eye for talent… (Walking over to the edge of the ring, Hollywood kicks the marker with the names "Windham vs. Wade" written on it off the apron to the floor.) I mean, "Feewish Wabe?" What the f<beep> was that supposed to be? I try to keep an open mind when people come across the casting couch, but let's call it like it is. Of course not all of your "Extreme" talent is so lackluster. Take for example the Extremeorama…
(The young lady leans in, speaking into the Director's ear.) Pardon me, again… the Extremist, Jake Morgan. (The fans, at least those not staring at the ring's occupants with slacked jaws, pop upon hearing the name of one of their heroes. A brisk chant of
"Morgan! Morgan! Morgan! Morgan!" goes up. Hollywood, nodding, gives the chant a second before speaking over it.)
Mr. Morgan and I spoke recently about this weekend's Texas Heavyweight Championship Match. He told me all about how it took that weasel with the funky hair a small army of hired ninjas to beat him out of the title…
(The stage manager leans in again. This time Hollywood looks at her and speaks into the microphone once more. The fans seem confused, booing in spite of the mention of two of their heroes.)
No, no, you're wrong. I was specifically told there were thirteen. Clubbed him with canes, like that rich boy who got caught shoplifting in Singapore. 13 of them, right Key?
(Key, standing outside the ring, nods once.) But I figured a man who was so shamed, so mistreated, deserved to have his pick of referees for his title re-match. And his choice was, in my estimation, the best choice a man can make. Allow me to present to you this Sunday's main event referee… the head grip of Hollywood 2000 Productions… KEY!
(Key, standing outside the ring, bows… once.) And since Key knows this sport better than Peter Daye, better than Dan Glass, and better than Johnny Green, I am giving him sole discretion over what is considered legal and illegal inside these ropes. With a few exceptions.
(The fans seem to tense up, their almost faded out chant stopping entirely.) One, the State Club Boyz, as I have said, will be watching on Television at home. Even if they buy tickets, any appearance by any one of the three, any pair of the three, or all of the three will result in instant forfeiture of the
EW Texas Title.
Secondly, anyone from the locker room is to keep a fifteen foot perimeter around the ring. If you wish to come out and watch the main event-who can blame you? My show, after all, is the best thing going today. But you WILL not become a factor in the match. If you step out of that locker room, come down to this ring, and so much as breathe on the ropes… you will be FIRED!
And lastly, and I do this for her own good you must understand, Jake Morgan's valet Mean Darlene is barred from ringside as well. A title match featuring the
Extremster and the Eclectic one is really no place for a woman. I don't want to see her hurt, and I don't want to see her in the arena.
(Hollywood pauses. The fans seem upset with his final ruling, but as his companion fiddles with the collar of her blouse, apparently scratching an itch, they seem to be diverted for a moment.)
Now, unless someone else would like to play "Open Microphone Night at Hollywood's," we'll get on with the show. (Dropping the microphone, which the stage manager picks up the delight of some ringside fans, Hollywood resumes his chair, screaming out, "ACTION!"
The camera faded out as Hollywood made himself comfortable. Fading back in, the camera found the EW Texas Heavyweight Champion Jason Flint in the back giving his boyz ... Curtis, Joseph and Tony, last minute instructions.)
<Jason Flint> Okay boyz ... this is it! The big one ... your professional debut against that ... that ... clown!
(All four started laughing when Kimberly Davis slapped Flint on the back, getting all four's attention.)
<Kimberly Davis> Uh guys ... this isn't a joke ... Raskal is looking to kill you ... (Flint cut her off with a wink and a wave of his hand.) <Jason Flint> Kimmie Baby ... I got a plan ... (There was a knock on the door as Niles Neptune poked his head inside.) <Niles Neptune> Time to go, guys ... (The Boyz strutted out of the room followed by Flint and Davis. After the State
Club Boyz passed through the doorway, Niles held up his hand, halting Flint and
Davis.)
<Jason Flint> Wah?!?!?!?!? <Niles Neptune> Sorry Jason ... orders of Hollywood ... <Jason Flint> Ouch! (Flint looked back at Kimberly who slapped the back of his head.) <Kimberly Davis> I got a plan ... (She rolled her eyes as the camera returned to the ring where the "Krazy Klown" was waiting for his opponents. He didn't have to wait long as the State Club
Boyz ran out to the ring. With the fans cheering, Raskal launched himself over the ropes and knocked all three men to the floor.
Jumping up, Raskal began hammering away at each of them before looking up and lifting his fist into the air, listening to the cheering of the fans. Referee
Peter Daye called for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** That brief instant allowed the SCB to get back into the match as Tony hit
Raskal from behind with a Boot to the back. He was joined by the other two before they rolled Raskal back into the ring.
Raskal was sent into the far ropes by Tony and Joe, then was taken to the mat by Curtis with a Splash off the ropes. Daye hit the mat twice before Curtis was tossed off. Getting up slowly, Raskal was hammered down several times by
Clotheslines from each of them. Joe dropped down and started Punching away before he was tossed off.
Raskal was being set up for a Piledriver, when he struggled out of it, taking
Tony and Joe down with a Double DDT. Curtis was backing away begging off as
Raskal motioned for him to come and get it. Curtis was on his knees when he took a boot to the head. He too took a DDT. Raskal was up the ropes and listening before jumping off and landing across Curtis with an Elbow Drop and making the cover.
Raskal was knocked off of Curtis as Tony slammed a trash can over his back. Joseph came off the top rope and landed a Leg Drop. Daye was arguing with Tony but was able to get the trash can away from him. Tony stopped Stomping Raskal and pushed the ref out of the way from behind into Tony, who knocked him to the mat with a Clothesline. The fans were booing as the SCB stood over the ref and hurled down insults at him. Curtis went so far as to empty out his nostril on the ref.
From behind, Raskal pulled him down and rolled him up with a Small Package but got no count as the ref was still down on the mat. The fans were counting as
Curtis struggled to get free.
... 1 ... ... 2 ... ... 3 ... ...1 ... ... 2 ... ... 3 ... They counted until Tony dropped a Forearm across Raskal's back. Joseph and Tony lifted Raskal and put him down with a Double Suplex. Curtis bounced off the ropes and landed a leg across Raskal's throat.
They continued their onslaught as Jason Flint and Kimberly Davis strolled out to the ring. Hollywood watched as the pair made their way to the ring. They continued to make their way to the ring a Flint raised his hand into the air and laughter was heard coming from whatever was in his hand. The SCB stopped their beating of Raskal as they came over and helped Kimberly up onto the ring apron. Flint turned back towards the fans and held his hand up and again there was laughter, not unlike that of Raskal's maniacal sounds.
With a Forearm to the back, Tony was taken down with a Suplex. Turning towards the others, Raskal made a beeline for Flint. Turning just in time, Flint bashed whatever he was holding into Raskal's head causing him to stumble backwards.
Tony and Curtis never got a chance to finish off the "Krazy Klown" as Flint was pulled off the ring apron by Jake Morgan who had come ringside. Curtis and
Joseph left the ring and rushed Morgan who was hammering away at Flint.
Back inside the ring, Raskal fell back across Tony and was holding his head where he had been hit. Daye looked around then dropped to the mat and slapped out a fast 3 count, calling for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (The camera panned the studio, passing over Hollywood who put out a hand stopping the massive Key who had moved forward to intervene. Niles Neptune and the EW Security flooded the studio and swarmed over the wrestlers, seperating them.
The State Club Boyz, not knowing they had lost the match pulled Tony from the ring and made their way to the back while Flint pointed at a smiling Morgan, who was telling Flint to bring it on.
Moving back to the ring, the camera zoomed in Raskal who was still laying out on the mat and the small device from which laughter was pouring out of before breaking away for a commercial.)
x-x-x-x-x Clips of K-STaR's syndicated programs The A-Team and Knight Rider were followed
Ricki Lake and Real TV which were in turn followed by new shows Strip Poker and
10's.
After scantily clad women and men flashed across the screen, a snazzy little ditty about the Dallas Mavericks and highlights flashed by before returning to the program.
x-x-x-x-x (Fading back in, the camera followed Skip Westervelt in a referee's uniform as he made his way through the back of the studio. He had a smile on his face before the camera broke away once again only to focus in on Tara Collins shadow boxing in her dressing room while brother Ron Collins watched.
There was sweat boring down her brow when she slammed one fist into the other.) <Tara Collins> Let's go! (Walking off, Tara was followed by Ron Collins, disappearing from the room. Moving back to the ring, Hollywood was busy announcing first Chip then Kat
Collins who had come out in a long gown and heels, only to do a little seductive dance before tearing it off her body. The fans whistled and cheered as she revealed her bodysuit underneath.
Tara came out and was immediately visibly upset over Kat's antics as she entered the ring. Moving across the ring, Tara and Kat had a few words before
Ron stepped in and seperated the two.
With Skip Westervelt rushing out from the back and making his way into the ring, he signaled for the bell.)
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** This one started slow as all three combatants started circling each other ... no one wanting to make the first move. Chip didn't want to make the first move on either of the ladies and neither Collins wanted to hit each other.
Okay ... Tara didn't mind as she struck first, wiping the goofy look off of
CHip's face with a big Slap that floored the obese man. Westervelt was over quickly with a warning but was backed up as Tara threatened him with a raised fist. Skip wasn't taking any crap from Tara as he stood his ground.
Kat ended that argument as she took Tara down with a Drop Kick. She followed it up with a Leg Drop before being pulled off by Chip who tossed her across the ring. Referee Skip Westervelt was egging Chip on to pin Tara but the big man turned his attention towards Kat ... he never got to his destination as Tara took him down with a Leg Clip, sweeping him off of his feet. Grabbing a meaty leg, she called Kat over. With a Collins on each leg, the ladies fell to the mat with a Leg Splitter. As Chip bounced into a seated position with his eyes wide open, Tara spun around and booted him in the face. Flopping across Chip brought on a 2 count ... a very slow 2 count.
Tara pushed Skip back only to take a shove back from him. After waving him off,
Tara turned back to Chip only to take a shot from Kat. Grabbing one of Chip's legs, Kat worked a Spinning Toe Hold, weakening the leg then dropping a Knee into his thigh. Chip was in pain as Skip seperated the two. Pushing Skip back, she turned back towards Tara and pulled her to her feet, then throwing her over with an Arm Drag and working the arm over with an Arm Bar.
A tap on the shoulder caught her attention. Turning, Kat was knocked to the mat by a Clothesline from Chip. A Kick to the back spun him around before he took a
Drop Kick to the knees. Pulled back up, Tara Whipped him into the ropes and sent him for a ride before taking him down with a Leg Scissor Takedown.
Grabbing his legs, she fell back and sent him across the ropes with a Slingshot right into a Drop Kick from a waiting Kat Collins. Kat got back to her feet and was knocked back into the ropes with a Kneelift that snapped her head back.
Grabbing an arm, Tara tossed her over with a Japanese Arm Drag. She dropped across Kat for a 2 count before Chip dragged her off of her sister by the foot.
Helping her to her feet, Chip wrapped his arms around Tara and pulled her in close. Puckering his lips, he pulled her closer. The look on her face didn't exactly show that this was something that she wanted. Rearing back with her head, she smashed it into Chip's kisser.
Falling backwards, Chip landed with Tara on top of him and had his legs covered as Kat dove across him. Westervelt took a long time to drop to the mat, slapping a 2 count before Chip pushed them off.
Tara was arguing with Westervelt as Kat Stomped ther downed Chip. Turning, Kat nailed Tara with a Spinning Kick to the back. Chip pulled himself to his feet using the ropes, taking a Kick to the chest. With a staggering Chip, Kat attempted to Suplex him but couldn't pull him off his feet. A second time netted the same result ... Chip was just too heavy. A third attempt worked as
Tara came in and helped her sister.
Getting back to their feet, Tara and Kat went nose to nose and were having words. Pushing Kat back, Tara dropped across Chip but was pulled off by her sister. Kat dropped down for a count, getting a 1 before Tara pulled her off.
Again, they were in each other's face ... before the pushing started. Kat pushed first before Tara came in with a Forearm, knocking Kat back into the ropes ... right into the waiting arms of Ron Collins. Kat hit him with a Back
Elbow but was caught up by Tara and spun upside down before being dropped with the "Texas Toast".
With Kat out, Tara yelled at Westervelt before dropping down across an unmoving
Chip. Reluctantly, Skip slapped out a 3 count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Tara left the ring to boos as she instructed Ron Collins to pull Kat from the ring and bring her to the back. The trio made their way to the back as the camera left the ring to zoom in on Hollywood's lovely assisstant.
Holding her "marker" in front of her, she blew a kiss into the camera as she pulled the top up and slammed it down onto it's lower half.)
*CLACK* <Redheaded Lovely> That's a wrap! x-x-x-x-x Black Cat Entertainment©2000
