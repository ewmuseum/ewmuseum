A play-by-mail wrestling game whose four member leagues crowned their own champions from 1997 into 2004, capped by a season-ending Almanac and annual Top Manager and Top Wrestler awards. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Imaginary Wrestling Federation ran as a play-by-mail wrestling game, its own four member leagues each crowning champions from 1997, per every league's own title-lineage sheet. Its all-time ranking system weighted its own six championships unevenly: a World title reign was worth 140 points, the Intercontinental and Global titles 90 each, a World Tag Team or World Six-Man reign 75, and the Ultimate Royal only 30.

Its own Annual Awards named a Top Wrestler for three seasons: Avatarr the Collector for 1997-98, over runners-up Crono-Father, Lobo the Crippler and Left-Out Lefty; Doctor Quiero for 1998-99, the same season he held one member league's own World Heavyweight Championship, over Deadbolt, “Mac Daddy” Freddie Fuller and Jarrett “Highspeed” Chase; and Donnie Brasco for 1999-2000, over “The Phoenix” Steve Christiansen, Mystic Michael Magic and Patrick “Paradigm” Shift.

Its own Almanac tracked an all-time points ranking across every member league, current, by the site's own statement, only as of June 2001; the ranking credits its own real managers by name rather than the wrestlers they played, and those names are not reproduced.

One member league's own title-lineage sheet states outright that the game “closed after cycle #79,” dating that cycle to September 2004; a fan-run history site independently calls it “no longer in operation.” Nothing in the surviving record is dated later than that closure.
