=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= I C W International Championship Wrestling Application and Info Version 1.0 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Most of you all know me as FORMER GCWF Co-President.I've decided to give my half of the organization to Co-President [name withheld] so begin working on the ICW project.The ICW will NOT be hardcore.The only time it will would be in special matches.The ICW relies on wrestling talent.The reason I sent you guys this info is because I think you are ALL great roleplayers.So here's the info you will need to know about the ICW. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= *First Pay-Per-View* The First Pay Per View Extravaganza will be on July25th-27th "ICW Wrestlebowl '97" Taking place in United Center in Chicago,Illinois.The Main Event will be a 32 Man Tournament to declare a NEW ICW World Heavyweight Champion.Along with a 20 Man Battle Royal to detirmine the Can-Am champion.Also,there will be 10 other featured matches. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= *Weekly Number Of Shows* There will be one show a week.Yes I said one show but this one show is SPECIAL!!! ---------------------------- ICW Monday Night Extravaganza ---------------------------- (The BIG ONE.The place where you want to be showcased.The maxium number of matches will be 20 matches,full of SUPRISES.Takes place in the major arenas.) There will be a house show on Wednesdays,Thursdays,and Fridays.Since no House Shows are televised unless it's a special card,ICW House Shows have summarized matches. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= *ICW Championships* There are 6 Major Championships in the ICW...... -ICW World Heavyweight Championship. -ICW Can-Am Heavyweight Championship. -ICW Cruiserweight Championship. (The weight limit is 220.) -ICW World Tag Team Championships -ICW Six Man Tag Team Championships -ICW Stable Championship. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= *ICW Rankings* The ICW rankings are detirmined by your win-loss record. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= *Application* (NOTE:If you were in the GCWF before I sent you this app,you don't have to send me an app because your app is on file.) You can have a limit of 2 Wrestlers and a Tag Team!
