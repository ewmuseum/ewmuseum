=========================================================== UWA Universal Wrestling Alliance FAQ Version 1.0 =========================================================== 1.History of UWA The Universal Wrestling Alliance was the brainchild of California Multi-Millionaire Scott Addison.UWA was planned to be be started to rival the two major federations at the time,World Championship Wrestling,Chaired by Ted Turner,and the World Wrestling Federation,Chaired by Vincent McMahon.However,Due to lack of funding,Mr.Addison had to put his idea to a halt,at least for the time being.He recieved a phone call in october of the year 2002,From none other than former World Championship Wrestling owner Ted Turner.Turner heard about the plans for the rival federation that never got off the ground.Turner wanted to get back in thw wrestling business,so he helped Addison Fund UWA.Currently UWA is broadcasted once a week on In-Demand Pay-Per View.But talks between Turner has been talking with USA,ESPN,and FX to broadcast the show,as it will be an expected ratings booster. =========================================================== 2.Basic Information Q:What is UWA A:UWA is an Online E-Federation where wrestling fans come to role play.It is run through a BCC Format. Q:What's the line minimum for my Roleplay's? A:No Line Minimum...We here at UWA Grade on Quality Q:Alright...How many wrestlers can I handle? A:One singles wrestler or one REAL Tag team.You can't pick The Rock and Triple H and make them a tag team. Q:Where is the promotion mainly based...or is it global? A:If you read the history at the top we are appearing once a week on Pay-Per View...And for now are primarily based In Northern Calfifornia...However we are gradually moving down...but for the time being we are most likely to be seen in:Sacramento,Elk Grove,Redding,Eureka,and Red Bluff.We do shows in smaller cities like Truckee and Knights Ferry too but not as often. Q:At the top you listed that ted Turner was negotionating with USA,ESPN,and FX...Which is the most likely to pick you up? A:USA Wants wrestling back on their network because of a large ratings drop compared to when the World Wrestling Federation broadcasted their Raw is War show.They have shown the most interest...However have declined to any more talks until some big name superstars Can arrive Q:Okay...I Want to join...Where's the application? A:No Application.The only thing you have to do to join is send me a list of wrestlers your interested in handling and a sample RP.Why have you fill out a Giant application if you want Raven?...I Already know his Height/Weight and character info...it'd be a waste of your time ============================================================ 3.About the Owner My name is Scott Addison...I am a 23 year old Graduate of the University of the Pacific.I majored in Technology and am now currently working at Home,and am employed by Activision's silicon valley branch.So I Do Have plenty of time to get you your Results for our weekly card. I have ran only one E-Federation in the past...with the same rules but a different concept as UWA...and it was called TWZ(The Wrestling Zone).I Started it in late 1999 and it Ran Almost three years until it's closing in August of Last Year.I Closed it because I believe it had a good run...and I wanted to start something new.The Gimmick for TWZ was a small federation that gained a Cult following in and Around Sacramento California.Eventually I got tired of the gimmick,Which was one of the more major reasons I closed it,And decided to try something new...Maybe with a gimmick that would allow Big time superstars(IE:The Rock,Goldberg,Scott Steiner Etc.)and still make sense funding wise.Thus,UWA was Born. ============================================================ 4.Joining. If you have any more question,I'd be very happy to answer them,My E-Mail adress is posted below.If what i've posted above is enough for you here is how you join.Send me a list of wrestlers,It can be as big or as small as you like,and a sample RP.Send them to [detail withheld] Your application will be reviewed by me and my staff the same day...and you will recieve a critique and if you were accepted or not.I hope to see you among the ranks of UWA int he future! -Scott Addison
