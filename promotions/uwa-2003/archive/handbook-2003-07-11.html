Closing I.Introduction a.What is the Universal Wrestling Alliance? The Universal Wrestling Alliance or UWA is an E-Mail Role Playing Federation.He run one Show a week and use PBP Results. b.Who created it? The UWA was Created by Scott Addison c.What is the fictional story behind it? UWA was the brainchild of California Multi-Millionaire Scott Addison.It was thought up in late 1999 to bring competition to the two dominant Federations at the time...WCW...Chaired by Ted Turner...and WWF...Chaired by Vincent McMahon.Plans for the federation to begin were halted however due to lack of funding and it was put on the shelf.In early 2001 marking the sale of WCW to Vincent McMahon Addison pulled it off the shelf to try and start it up again...but many investors thought putting money into it would be risky...Since the WWF had become so powerful.Two Months after the sale Addison recieved a phone call from former WCW owner Ted Turner.Turner had learned of the possible WWF Rival and was interested it funding it.The funding he was able to give was enough to establish the promotion in the means of Staff and Venues...but they weren't too big.At the present time UWA is broadcated Only to Surrounding Area's of Northern California and Is boradcasted once a month on In Demand Pay-Per View.As of right now Turner is Having talks with USA...TBS...and FX about broadcasting the federation nationwide and interest is being shows because it would be a possible ratings booster. II.Whose in Charge a.Scott Addison Addison Chairs UWA He is 31 Years old and Has worked for promotions such as ECW and WWF.He has also worked many smaller promotions like XPW,MLW,and CZW.He's about 6'2 with short brown hair and his causal attire consists of Either a Armani Suit...or a Leather Jacket and Jeans.He plays a large role in the federation.He doesn't stay a Face or Hell he's more in the nuetral department and resembles Eric Bischoff. b.Adam Wilson Adam Wilson Is the President of UWA.he is 27 years old 6' 200 Pounds. Pretty Decent build. He works out regularly so he has tone. Brown hair. Hair just kind of lays there, no real hair cut. It's short though. Brown Eyes.He was made for strictly business. He doesn't play games. He is the type of guy that wants to see everything fair, and every gets what they deserve. He always seems to have a serious look on his face. He never lets personal problems get in the way of business. if he can help it c.James Kurn James Kurn is the Commissioner of UWA.Kind of like Mick Foley. People love him, but is a dumb ass at some times. If People get pissed at him, and let him know it. He'll put them in crappy matches and screw em over. He'll try to be funny but his jokes are dumb.He is 26 Black Hair normally, but bleached. He's got blue eyes, White skin, 6'2" 227 III.The Basics a.A BCC Roleplaying system We here at UWA feel a BCC role-playing system would be more realistic.For those who do not know what a BCC mailing system is:You send your Role-Play's to Scott Addison,Adam Wilson,and James Kurn only.We relay the RP's to the rest of the federation individually b. How we Handle Fueds/Story Lines The UWA Staff believe a federation is no good without Fueds or Angles.That's why the federation does it's best to supply them.Do not worry...everyone will be in a fued. Backstage Interviewers UWA Employes 3 backstage interviewers aa.Jonathen Coachman bb.Terri Runnels cc.Mark Lloyd VI. The Titles a. UWA Universal Champion This the the biggest title in UWA and holding it automatically makes you the best wrestler in the promotion...and makes you one of the best wrestlers in the world.This belt is usually not defended at Mayhem but will be on occasion.it is mainly defended at PPV b. UWA TV Champion This means you are the second most powerful singles wrestler in UWA and there is a good chance you will recieve a push.This title is defended mainly at Mayhem c.UWA Tag Team Champions If you hold these your are the best team in UWA VII. The Rulebook a.Roleplaying Guideline Here in UWA we have no Role-Play line minimum.RP's are judged on quality and quality only.If it's a long droning rp about crap it won't score very high with us...but if it's entertaining and a Good read it will score high.It's simple.If you RP good you win your matches...length is not a factor.Also...No OOC comments in your rp's at all. b.Wrestler Conduct You may not use another Wrestler in your RP without written consent from that wrestler.The permission must be sent ot the staff.You may not attack an Interviewer in your Role Play's without the staff's Permission either.We do not condone RP's taking place in an arena.If you do have it take place in the ring prior to the event there wouldnt be any "Screaming Fans" in the audience.If you have your wrestler come down to the ring for a promo and you play it like there are people in the crowd...your Role Play quality will go down. c.Event Rules Do not ask for a title shot here.If you are good you'll get a title shot end of story.No complaining...he decide matches to the best of our ability...if you lose take the loss and try to redeem yourself.Complaining will just make you look childish and further prove why you lost.If you would like an interview/in ring promo with your wrestler to be published in the results send it to the staff and we will consider adding it.
