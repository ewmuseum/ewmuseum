Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Mountain State Wrestling Federation opened in West Virginia in April 2000 as a small, regionally rooted promotion, run on a closed weekly flash system built around a single Friday-night deadline.

Its own handbook set the terms plainly: quality over quantity, one flash packet a week folded into a card called Engagement of Words, and a Sunday show, Sunday Mountainer, that carried the angles forward.

The promotion staged its wrestling at a high school gymnasium in front of a crowd of several hundred, with commentary carried by two house announcers trading insults across the desk.

Its opening card put "The Insane One" Haze through a chairshot-and-powerbomb sequence against "Lumberjack" Greg Rothwell and had Haze walk away, on his own two feet, with the win.

Two titles anchored the promotion from the start, the Mountain State Championship at the top and the Mountainer Championship defended nearly every card beneath it — a small federation's own two-belt hierarchy, set out before either had been contested even once.
