that is saying a lot! Who Runs It? - The Mountain State Wrestling Federation (MSWF) is ran by 15 year old Honor Student, [name withheld]. He has been in e-wrestling for over 6 years and has a firm gasp on the running of it. He has handled characters such as "The Insane One" Haze, "Five Star Dan Brown, and his most popular one..."Playboy" Aidan Madison. You may have never heard of them, but don't worry because Brad tends to stay on the low down. He isn't too well known throughout the e-wrestling community, but he plans to have this fed become popular, yet stay small.
1b. How Does It Run? - The Mountain State Wrestling Federation is a "closed flashed system." Mean, every Friday there will be a flash packet called "Engagement of Wars" that will come out featuring all of you flashes. This will be the only chances you have to flash for your match, so make it count. After the packet comes out, Bug (Brad's ever- so-popular nicknae) goes to work on writing great cards. Brad's major is Journalism and Writing, so the cards will be written professionally and have great actions. The MSWF's card, "Sunday Mountainer," will come out every Sunday..go figure, followed by the weekly update.
1c. Why Should I Join? - The MSWF is a regionally based feds, one of the few you see out there now. The best regional fed, in Brad's eyes, is Nick Demola's DWF. Still, there are problems in the DWF and Brad is going to try to correct those in the MSWF. Obviously, Brad is going to make this a
_FUN_ federation to be in. Just join and find out for yourself. 2. Flashing 2a. How It Is Done In The MSWF - I mentioned this breifly above, so I hope you have somewhat of an idea how it is done here. Once again, the MSWF is a "closed flash system." All flashes are due to Brad at
[detail withheld] every Friday Night at MIDNIGHT EASTERN TIME. From here, Brad will form all the flashes into one, big, fun to read flash packet called, "Engagement of Words." It'll be then sent out to everybody in the federation for your enjoyment.
2b. Quality - One of the most agrued over issues in e-wrestling is that over quality/quanity. Now, here in the MSWF, QUALITY is a lot more important than the quanity of a flash. Brad would rather read a GOOD 40 line flash than a boring 200 line flash. In this case, the 40 line flash would win.
2c. Quanity - Quanity is something that Brad would like to see, but keep it to the point where Brad doesn't get bored of reading it. It isn't too hard of a concept for you to understand.
3d. Strats - Strats are what make the MSWF turn! Without the, there would be no angles and Brad would have NO CLUE to go with matches. Basically, strats are what you want to do in the match. A basic layout of one is like this:
Entrance: (If you wish, you can write a completly differenct entrance than that one you application...it is up to you.)
Attitude: (Attitude of your wrestler during the match. What is your wrestler thinking? How does he feel about this match?)
Spots: (What moves do you want your wrestler to do? Put them here.) Reverses: (Are there anybody special reverses you want your wrestler to do? Put them here.)
If Wreslter Wins: (Put what your wrestler will do if he wins.) If Wrestler Loses: (Put what your wrestler will do if he loses.) Pretty easy, isn't it? That is just a BASIC format of a good strat. I would like, if you want to have a better chance of winning, to go into MUCH MORE DETAIL! First and foremost, it'll make my match writting job A LOT easier making the card that much better!
3. Cards 3a. "Engagement of Words" - How many times do I have to repeat myself? LOL! :-) Once again, for the third time, you send your flashes into Brad before MIDNIGHT eastern time on Friday.
3b. "Sunday Mountainer" - This is where all the action takes place. The wreslters of the MSWF will step into the ring and show the world what they are made of! 3c. Super Cards - Well, there are NO SET DATES for Supercard. They are bascially when Brad feels there is enough heat and enough angles to do one. This will be where all angles either end or HEAT UP EVEN MORE!
4. Fictional Characters 4a. Tim Straka - The "heel" annoucer. He'll be the one making all the wise cracks and all that fun stuff. Basically, everybody hates him. 4b. Walter Ramsey - The "face" annoucer. He'll be the one telling Tim off for making fun of people and everything. He is the one with the larger knowledge of wrestling and is fun to listen to.
4c. Kristin Bioux - Basically, the "hottie" of the MSWF. She'll be the one you can use in your flashes as an interviewer. 5. Titles 5a. Mountain State Champion - The top of the mountain! Whoever hold this title is pretty much the best wreslter in the MSWF, and possibly the best around! 5b. Mountainer Champion - Whoever hold this title is a pretty good wrestler! Although, this is a REALLY tough title to hold to because it is defended almost every card. 6. Ending - Yup, a pretty simple application. It is just to give me an idea on what your character is like. Believe me, I'll get to know your characters better as the fed goes on and you send me strats and everything. Well, I hope to see you in the MSWF and have some fun.
