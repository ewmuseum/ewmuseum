[MSWF/SAMPLE] Sunday Mountainer

2000-04-09
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A tree covered mountain side is showen. You can hear the sound of rushing water, birds singing, and people laughing. Everything is so peaceful. Suddenly, a scream of total murder is heard. The screen cuts to 4 block, green letters that say, "MSWF." The scene then cuts into the packed Brooke High School Gymnasium in Wellsburg, West Virginia.
Around five hundred people pack the roll out basketball seats, along with about another hundred sitting in chairs at ringside. The ring, which is pretty crappy looking, has a single spotlight on it.]
[The camera then swings around to the annoucers table where Tim Straka and Walter Ramsey are sitting. Both are wearing nice suits and have smiles on their faces. Walter Ramsey is overweight and it is obvious.
Tim Straka is looking nice and pretty has Walter Ramsey begins to speak.] [WR]:"Welcome to MSWF's SUNDAY MOUNTAINER!!" [The Crowd Erupts] [WR]:"I'm Walter Ramsey!" [TS]:"And I'm Tim Straka. Let me be the first to say that you are in for QUITE A SHOW tonight! The MSWF Front Office is trying their best to give you, the fans, what you want! I mean, you're the ones that are too out of shape to get into the ring like the cheesy wrestlers of the
MSWF." [WR]:"Yeah, way to go, Tim! I'm sure we just gained some fans with that one." [TS]:"Shut up. When I what your thoughts, I'll ask you fatty." [WS]:"Okay, let me start talking about the show before I kill you for being the way you are."
[TS]:"No." [WR]:"Screw you, Tim. Tonight, we have TWO great matches for you. For our first match, we have 'Five Star' Dan Brown going up against the seventeen your old sensation, Aaron Rogers!"
[TS]:"Sensation? I'm not quite sure where you get of saying that. I mean, the kid is only 17 and he already made the wrong decision by getting into the world of wrestling."
[WR]:"You have no room to talk. I mean, YOU'RE ALSO in the world of wrestling." [TS]:"Well, yeah. . . Let's get to our first match.] ***************************** "Five Star" Dan Brown v. "Young Sensation" Aaron Rogers ***************************** [WR]:"Both men have already made their entrances and are about go at each other." [TS]:"Wake me up when this is over." [WR]:"I promise I won't." [Aaron Rogers makes the first move as he jumps around Dan Brown and fligs him over his back, laying Brown out. Rogers goes to the ropes and starts to show boat already as Brown shakes the cob webs out from the early, high-impact move. Brown goes to the turnbuckles and pulls himself up. Rogers sees this and runs into the back of Brown, crushing
Brown between the body of Aaron Rogers and the ring post.]
[WR]:"OUCH! That ring post won't give out much, and Rogers was going full steam into Brown. I'm sure that is going to leave a mark on Brown."
[TS]:"Shut up, Fatty. I'm trying to sleep here." [Brown slumps down in pain as Rogers climbs to the top rope and sits down. He grabs the head of Brown and sits him down against the ring post. Rogers leaps off, wraps his legs around Brown and slams his face right into the mat as Rogers goes flying to the other end of the ring!]
[Rogers looks back at the motionless Brown, whose nose is bleeding from the impact. Rogers gets a breif smile across his face, then Brown wipes it off as he, fast as can be, leaps up onto his knee and leaps at
Rogers taking him down with a VERY LOW clothesline. Rogers grabs his private area as Brown rolls out of the ring from the momentum he picked up. Rogers rolls out of the other side of the ring to gain his breath.]
[TS]:"Okay, there is WAY too much noise for me to get any sleep around here so I might as well watch some of the match."
[WR]:"Good. You were missing a really good opening bout. Brown and
Rogers are giving it their all."
[TS]:"If both men are giving it thier all, then why are they both laying on the outside of the ring?"
[WR]:"Well, I can't really explain that one." [TS]:"Oh, look at that! I finally said something that you couldn't proove wrong." [Brown climbs back into the ring and notices that Rogers isn't in it. He walks to the other side and sees that Rogers is laying on the concrete floor holding his privates, still! Brown gets a childish smile and climbs to the top rope!]
[WR]:"NO!!! DON'T DO IT!! YOU'LL KILL YOURSELF!" [TS]:"That'd be a real big loss to the world...Not." **CRASH!!!** [Brown leaps of the top turnbuckle and totally nails himself AND Rogers against the concrete floor. Both men have to be dead, or atleast seriously injured! After a few moments of motionless, Brown begins to pull himself to his feet.]
[TS]:"Dangit, I thought he was dead. I was actually getting into the match their for a moment."
[WR]:"I can't believe that Brown is actually getting up! But, I don't think Rogers is going to be getting up...Well, nevermind."
[Dan Brown picks up Aaron Rogers and throws him into the ring. Brown goes for the cover. . .]
1..... 2..... KICKOUT!.... [WR]:"How did Rogers kick out of that?!? I thought for sure that was going to be the end. I'm totally amazed!"
[TS]:"Well, you would be. Anything amazes you these days." [Brown looks down at Rogers in disbelif as Rogers climbs to his knees and hits Brown with a stiff uppercut. The blow sends Brown to his back.
Rogers climbs on top of Brown and starts to throw a fury of punches into Brown's face.] [TS]:"YEAH!! This is what I'm talking about!! PUNCH HIM!! BLACK EYE! BLACK EYE!" [WR]:"Aaron Rogers isn't too happy with Dan Brown right now. If anything, I'd watch out for some illegal weapong or something."
[TS]:"Illegal weapons?!? BRING THEM ON! COME ON! LET ME SEE THEM!" [WR]:"Exactly what I didn't want to hear out of you." [Brown shoves Rogers of him and the two of them really get into in! They roll back on forth on top of each other punching each other in the face. The bloody nose that Dan Brown once had has now growen to bigger proportions. Aaron Rogers sees this and grabs Brown's head and slams it against the mat, but Brown blocks it and elbows Rogers right in the ribs, knocking the wind out of him.]
[TS]:"HEY! That is an illegal shot!" [WR]:"You? Complaining about an illegal shot?" [TS]:"NO! I'm just really excited about that! Both these 'newbies' are learning how to fight now!"
[WR]:"How are they learning to fight?" [TS]:"They are learning to fight dirty! I hate to rip somebody off like this, but 'CHEAT TO WIN!'"
[WR]:"Dear Lord, how'd I get this annoucing job with this idiot?" [TS]:"The question is, how I'D get this annoucing job with this FATTY?" [Brown takes advantage of the short-of-breath Rogers and picks him up. Brown whips Rogers into the ropes and goes for the big foot, but Rogers dives underneath is and comes back off the other set of ropes and hooks
Brown into a powerful sleeper hold.]
[WR]:"WOW! Rogers really changed the style of this match from drity to technical in a matter of minutes!"
[TS]:"WHY?!?!?" [WR]:"Why, what?" [TS]:"Why did it have to turn into a total snooze fest again?" [WR]:"Actually, this is a VERY good match up. We are seeing a little bit of everything in this match."
[Brown slumps down, but you can tell he is still very much awake. He reaches and trys to rake the eyes of Rogers, but all attempts fail.
Brown, without warning, slumps down and all his limps become motionless. Rogers releases the hold, getting a little cocky, and begins to show boat.]
[TS]:"NO YOU MORON! Put the hold back on!! He is going to get up!!!!! YOU IDIOT!" [WR]:"I hope he hears you and come over here and beat the living snot out of you." [TS]:"Living snot? We're professionals Walter, please." [Brown regains his composure and gets confused why Rogers let go off the hold. He doesn't care though, he is still in the match. Brown goes up behind Rogers and taps on his shoulder. Before Rogers can turn around though, Brown rolls him up in a VERY TIGHT small package!...
1.... 2...... 3!!!!!! [Aaron Rogers can't kick out! BROWN WINS!] *********************** Your Winner: Dan Brown *********************** [WR]:"What a surprize ending!" [TS]:"DANG! What did I tell you, Rogers?!? You twit! You shouldn't have let go off the hold! You could've won!"
[WR]:"Calm down, Tim. Aaron Rogers will have another chance soon." [TS]:"Yeah, but another chance will not help him get back at Dan Brown! I mean, I'm getting sick of seeing the 'good guy' beating the 'bad guy' all the time. Won't it EVER change?" [WR]:"Well, Timothy, it looks as if the way the world works will never change. Heh." [TS]:"Hey, don't you go calling me 'Timothy.' You know I hate that!" [WR]:"Okay, Timothy." [TS]:"I hate you." [WR]:"Okay. Before 'Tiny Tim' he blows his top." [TS]:"Too late for that." *************************** "The Insane One" Haze v. "Lumberjack" Greg Rothwell *************************** [Before the bell can even ring to begin the match, Greg Rothwell attacks "The Insane" Haze from behind making him fall to his knees.
Greg takes the head of Haze and runs it up and down the ropes, leaving burns on the face of Haze.]
[WR]:"WOW! Rothwell sure show no remorse on Haze in that one! Haze's face is going to burning and stinging for days to come."
[TS]:"If that baby can't even take that, then he doesn't belong in the
MSWF. I've seen better stuff than that."
[WR]:"What would that be, Tim?" [TS]:"It would be what I'm going to do to you if you don't shut up, and maybe even lose a few pounds!"
[WR]:"Don't you think you've cracked enough fat jokes on me yet?" [TS]:"Oh, believe me, there are going to be a lot more to come." [Haze falls to the ground holding his face as Rothwell points and laughs at him. Haze suddenly jumps up and takes Greg Rothwell's knees out making him fall down also. Haze gets up and starts to pound on
Rothwell, paying special attention to his ribs.]
[TS]:"I'm not one to be nice, but Haze is showing some promise. He has a game plan and he is following it."
[WR]:"My thoughts exactly, Tim." [TS]:"If I'm thinking the same things as you, I don't want to be thinking what I'm thinking, if you know what I mean."
[WR]:"You know, that was almost funny." [Rothwell grabs the kicking foot of Haze and whips it around. Haze's head goes flying right into the turnbuckles, leaving a big bump on the back of his head. Rothwell waste no time in picking Haze up and whipping him into the ropes. Rothwell connects with a big boot to the face, which sends Haze out of the ring.]
[WR]:"Greg Rothwell is just dominating this match by his power." [TS]:"It is a no-brainer, really. Rothwell stands around 6'7'' while
Haze only stands about 5'9''. Just imagine the weight difference."
[WR]:"Wow. I'm surprized. You mention weight and didn't say anything about me. I'm proud of you."
[TS]:"Oh yeah, you're fat Walter." [Rothwell steps over the top ropes but Haze is waiting on the very edge of the ring and pulls him out, viciously. Greg Rothwell's head hits the steel ringpost with a big impact. Haze has a big chance here to turn the tides and takes advantage of it. He jumps on the back of Rothwell and begins to throw lefts and rights into the the back of Rothwell's head. Rothwell gets a second wind though and throws Haze off of him.]
[TS]:"Haze had a nice chance there, but Rothwell is just too strong." [WR]:"I can't believe I'm saying this. But, I think, the only way for
Haze to win is take Rothwell's head off with a freaking steel chair."
[TS]:"THAT IS MY BOY, WALT! You finally realize that wrestling is all about steel." [WR]:"Unfortunately." [Haze crawls back into the ring for safety's sake. Rothwell follows him into the ring but the quicker and much smaller Haze runs behind
Rothwell and bends his neck over the top rope!] [WR]:"HAZE IS CHOKING ROTHWELL!" [TS]:"Actually, Haze isn't as stupid as I thought he was. He is trying to wear the big guy down so he has a better chance going toe-to-toe with him."
[WR]:"Yeah, he'll be going toe-to-toe since he has to look up to see him nose-to-nose. HA! I made a funny."
[TS]:"Pathetic." [WR]:"Yeah, I've been around you too long." [Haze falls out of the ring, making Rothwell sling shot and fall down in the middle of the ring. Haze reaches under the ring and pulls out a steel chair.]
[TS]:"YES! YES! YES!" [Haze climbs back into the ring climbs to the top rope. Rothwell begins to stand up and Haze prepares to leap off the top rope with the steel chair.]
[WR]:"NO! Haze will ends Rothwell's career if he does this!!! NO!" [Haze jumps off the top rope! NO! NO! NO! Greg Rothwell caught him in mid-air!!! Haze drops the chair!!! ROTHWELL POWERBOMBS HAZE RIGHT ONTO
THE CHAIR!]
[TS]:"OH MY GOD!! Even I'M a little bit amazed by that." [WR]:"Lord almighty! Haze has the biggest heart in the world, but I don't even think God himself could evercome that!"
[Rothwell goes for the pin. . . 1. . . . . . 2. . . . . . . . . . 3. . .NO! KICKOUT! [Rothwell looks down at Haze with amazement in his eyes! He has NO CLUE how Haze got up from that one.]
[TS]:"Haze looks like he has been through a car wreck, but he still wanting to go on in this MIS-MATCH!"
[WR]:"I can't believe this!! Haze should be given some kind of reward! Rothwell has put him through HELL AND BACK, but Haze is still ticking!" [TS]:"Simply amazing." [Haze pulls on the arms of Rothwell to help pull himself up. Rothwell, instead of beating on Haze, stares at him in amazement! Haze head butts
Rothwell in the stomach, sending him to the ground. Haze grabs the legs of Rothwell and applies a BOSTON CRAB!]
[WR]:"Haze is going to keep the big guy grounded by injuring his legs as much as possible! A pretty smart idea."
[TS]:"I'm not sure how effective it is going to be, though. Haze doesn't weigh that much and Rothwell is an AWFULLY large man...those two just don't go together for much pain. They cancel each other out."
[WR]:"Yeah, you do have a point Tim. I wonder how much heart Haze has left, or stupidity in that case. I'm trying not to be mean, but if I were Haze, I would have not gotten up from that powerbomb onto the chair...he is risking something serious here."
[Rothwell is screaming out in pain! Apparently Haze is doing some damage!! The referee goes down on the mat.]
[Referee]:"Do you give up?" [Rothwell]:"NOO!! GET OUTTA MY FACE!" [Rothwell takes his free hand and, without much effort, sends the referee flying across the ring.]
[TS]:"WHOA! I sure wouldn't want to be in the ring with that guy! I kind of feel sorry for the referee now."
[WR]:"Yeah, the MSWF is no place for stuff like that." [TS]:"Seriously." [Rothwell turns over, and grabs the ropes. Haze releases the hold, but before he can even realize what is going on, Rothwell has him up on his shoulders. DEATH VALLEY DRIVER!!!!]
[WR]:"HOLY-JUMPING-JELLY-FISH! Rothwell just turned Haze into lifeless pudding with ONE MOVE!"
[TS]:"Idiot." [Rothwell goes for the pin. . .] 1. . . . . . 2. . . . . . . REVERSAL!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Haze somehow, someway, reverses the pin and has Rothwell down on the mat!] 1. . . . . . . 2. . . . . . . . . 3. ! . ! . ! . ! . ****************************** Winner: "The Insane One" Haze ****************************** [TS]:"HOW?!?!?!? HAZE SHOULD'VE BEEN DEAD!" [WR]:"I'm speechless." [TS]:"Although, Haze doesn't look too good. Rothwell is walking out of the ring but Haze is laying motionless."
[The referee signals backstage for help. A herd of EMT's and Paramedics come rushing to the ring pushing a stretcher, a neck brace, and all the equipment they are going to need.]
[WR]:"This doesn't look good at all. I think Haze used everything last thing he had in his body to win this match, but it led to some kind of serious injury."
[TS]:"I think that Haze was DEAD after that Death Valley Driver and his nerves twitched so hard that it flipped him over and Rothwell didn't have the strength to push a dead body off of him."
[WR]:"HEY! COME ON! One of our wrestler's is seriously injured and you're joking around about it."
[The paramedics carefully place the neck brace on Haze and place him onto the stretcher. The gymnasium is silent as the EMT's and Paramedics wheel "The Insane One" Haze out of the school and to Weirton Medical
Center.]
[WR]:"Ladies and Gentlemen, I just got word that our reporter, Kristin
Bioux, is on her way to the Weirton Medical Center right now. We should have a report on Haze soon. But, for now, this is Walter Ramsey along with..."
[TS]:"Tim Straka..." [WR]:"Saying goodbye and see you next Sunday! Keep HAZE in your prayers!" [TS]:"Sure thing."
