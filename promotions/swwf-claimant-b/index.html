Supreme Wrestlers World Federation, pitched 29 October 1997 with its own title lineup and a twelve-event monthly pay-per-view calendar, though no surviving document shows any show, roster or result was ever actually run. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Supreme Wrestlers World Federation announced itself on 29 October 1997 — “Its here! Finnaly SWWF...has Arrived” — billing itself “The Elite Wrestling Federation” and capping membership at 45. Its own operator introduced the promotion by way of his own record running four earlier, shorter-lived feds, each lost, in his own account, to thin staff and members who wouldn't roleplay.

It planned itself around a 70% roleplay, 20% angle, 10% strategy weighting across two or three weekly shows: Saturday Night Supreme, its flagship card; Tuesday Night Live Talk, an interview-only program with no matches; and a tentative Thursday Night Extreme Zone for its wilder gimmick bouts, plus a bimonthly “Canvas Clash” supercard.

Its rules document named seven titles — a World Heavyweight Championship down through an International, United States, Television, and Skyweight title, plus World and United States Tag Team belts — and set out a full twelve-event annual pay-per-view calendar running from a New Year's Eve show through a December “Winter WarZone.”

No card, roster, or result of its own survives. Its rules text was later reused near-verbatim by a different claimant operating under a different name elsewhere in the same newsgroup.
