A True Federation For True RPers....

29 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XXWarningXX XX This Fed is For Dedicated Members Only. XX XX If you are a Piss Ant, Whinning Moma's Boy Do Not Come Any Further XX "The Fed that Has Been stated as the Best of All Time" "The new Revelutionary Wrestling Fed in Sports Entertianment today" "Were Men become ICON's, And ICON's stay ICON's" Supreme Wrestlers World Federation " The True Fighting ground for True Wrestlers" "The Elite Wrestling Federation" (Enter The New Revolution) Its here! Finnaly SWWF The Ultimate In E-Mail Federations has Arrived. But with an Impact! First of all, I want to tell you about my expeirience with running feds and a little `bout myself. When I first started my first fed, WWWF, About a year ago, I hardly knew a thing about Feds. Now I can write Cards better than anyone I know. Now I have this Feeling, this desire to get back into the Spot of running a federation, And this time it will be my greatest Federations ever. I have thought out great plans for a new federation, things such as how to keep it running, how to write cards under a lot of pressure, and how to make time to write Cards. Yes I do have a Job, yes I do go to school, and yes I do have a social life. But I now know that I Can write a great Card in a short amount of time. My Card will be a all out hell of a Card! No boring Matches, No Jobber Matches, and know Match that carrys on and on. Its just going to be a heck of a card! Now onto other things, I am going to keep this Fed at a limit of 45 Members. So if you want to join you better hurry up and Join now...Before all the spots are taken. Now I have ran many feds such as AEMW, BTWF, and RWF. But they have all failed because Lack of Staff and sometimes Lack of RPing . If you dont RP then you will be fired. And another thing is you may all know me as "The Blazer" Barry Blades. I was in many feds before, and now I have decided to get back into the Business of running feds..Well enough about the Introduction, lets get on with the application. (Introduction) now let me tell you guys a little something about SWWF. First off its based on 70% Role Play, 20% Angle, 10% Stragety...But Role Playing will play by far the biggest Factor. Ok I know that you all get bored with Fantasy Feds, well I dont want that to happen with SWWF. I want SWWF to be a place you can really call your favorite Fed around. I have put a lot of things in SWWF To make this a great fed, and to keep you interested. For one you can send me Stragities/Sneak Attacks. Those are things like Who you want to fued with, Who you want to focuse on beating, what kind of stragety will you use in your matches, and things like that. So as you can See I want to make SWWF The best Fed Around, and I want you all to love this fed, so We can all have fun..If you like the fed, and the role plays are doing well, then I will be a happy camper, and have no problem writting the Cards for you. Ok now lets get on with the show. (SWWF Weekly Shows) Ok there will be 2 Shows a week. And These Cards will be mostly fully written out, with some dark Matches. These Cards will be written good, exciting and with lots of Action and detial. Here are the Shows! (Saturday Night Supreme) Ok This will be are biggest Card of the week. It will be the Monday Night Raw or Nitro of SWWF. It will have About 5-7 full written out matches, along with some Dark Matches. The Main Event will always be good, and will have tons of live action! This Show will be Hosted by "Cool" Ray Roland, Barry Blades, & Jim Williams... (Tuesday Night Live Talk) This show will be held every week, and it will have Interviews, An update Center, and tons of all the wall uncensored stuff, there wil be no matches because this is a show just for interviews, but belive me there will be tons of action, who knows who will get into it on this show! (Thrusday Night Extreme Zone) This Show may not happen yet, but if it does it will be A Great Card. It will have off the Wall Kind of Matches and Matches only SWWF Can think of. It will have about 5 Matches, and the Main Event, always being Off the Wall, and Extreme...This show will be hosted by "Bubba" Bob Silvers, & Dante Jones. (Canvas Clash) This will be our "Clash of the Champions" it will be held every two months, and will have the same PPV quality type matches, Big Titles are defended, and there are no dark Matches. All Live Matches, It will be hosted by "Cool" Ray Rolan, Barry Blades, and Jim Williams. (CWF Singles Titles) Ok These will be the Official Titles of CWF. Titles can be handed down to the #1 Contender, and Titles can be stripped. Well here they are... (World Heavyweight Title) his is the #1 Ranked Title, it will be defended up to once a month, and if you hold this your the best wrestler in SWWF. (International Title) This is the #2 Ranked Title, and will be defended up to Twice, maybe once, a Month. If you hold this you are the Second Best in SWWF. (United States Title) This is the #3 Ranked Title here in the SWWF. Screw Europe this is America! This will be defended Twice A Month. ( Television Title) This Title is the #4 Ranked Title, If you hold this Title You will be the true worker of the SWWF! ( Skyweight Title) This is for those who are under 240 Pounds. This is a big Title for those Skyweights! This Title is to be defended Twice a Month! (SWWF Tag Team Titles) (World Tag Team Titles) his Title is the #1 Ranked Tag Team Title, If you hold this Title it means you are one of the Best Tag Teams in the World. Its an honor to have this title. This Title is defended Twice a month. (United States Tag Team Titles) This Title is defended Once a week, and is the #2 Ranked Tag Team Title. This represents The U.S.! So if you have this you may not be the best world wide but you are in your home country! (SWWF Pay Per Views) PPV's are all Live Matches and the Biggest Events and matches take place! January- New Years Bash (Held On New Years eve! After Words is a Big New Years Eve Party in the Arena..) Febuary- Ring Wars (A Survivor Series Type PPV!) March- March Madness (This Will be a Special PPV, always held somewere in Other Country's, Like Japan, New England, ETC.) April- Canvas Chaos (All Stipulation Matches, all titles on the line!) May- Spring Bash (Springs Biggest Event!) June- King of Extreme (This is A Tournament to Crown the King of Extreme) July- Summer Clash (This will be Held at a beach, and has plenty of summer action) August- Hardcore Heatwave (The Wrestle Mania of SWWF) September- Ring Riot (A SWWF Style Royal Rumble) October- Judgment Night (SWWF's Type of Halloween Havoc) November- Extreme Fest (Main Event is a Giant Steel Cage Match) December- Winter WarZone (The Biggest Event of the Winter! Always a good card, and always a big Main Event!) (Other Things about SWWF) (House Shows) Dont Let these fool you, just because there not fully written out and just brief summeries doesnt mean a thing. These are great cause you get a lot of matches, and they also bost up the win-loss %. (News Letter) This may be one of the best things about SWWF, in this news letter you will find articals, Rankings, Win-Loss Records, Ratings, and a whole Lot More. These will be out once a week and they are fun to read! And you may also put your articles into the news letter. Just E-Mail [detail withheld] with the article! (Injury List) Ok You can suffer Injury's in SWWF. Like if your oppenant just Dominates you in Role Playing you will most likely Be Injured, or you may be Injured to develop an angle, You can also be injured if you are going out of town, and you will recover when you get back. The longest someone can get injured for in SWWF is 3 Months, but there are ways of Recovering Fast.. (Contracts) Ok I will send you out Contracts for a certian amount of Money when you fill out the application. This will explain your Contract Terms, and what you can do and how long you will stay, ETC. (SWWF Application) Well have you decided, well if you want to join just fill out this app. You can have up to 4 Wrestlers, Please make a Singles, Cruiserweight, and Tag Team if possible, because we dont want to run short of Tag Team's and Cruiserweight's here in SWWF like most other feds, Ok here is the app. I also might be bringing in a Six Man Title depending on how many wrestlers we get. Note: Fill out same app. For tag team or singles. Singles or Tag Team: Name: Height: Weight(Skyweights are under 240): Style(Technical,Lucha Libra,High Flyer,Power, Mat Wrestler, Submissionist, or make up your own style): Finisher: Finisher Description(What the Move's Technical name is): Set Up Finisher(Move You do before Finisher): Home Town: Manager: Gimmick(Explain Your Wrestlers Gimmick, who is he like, is he a Sycho, and Ex Con, Explain): Face/Heel/Nuetral(Good Guy,Bad Guy, or both): 5-10 Moves Your Wrestler does everymatch: Entrence Theme: Ring Attire(Outfit your wrestler wears): Real Life Wrestlers You Most compare to: Stable: Any Special Ring Entrance(Does Fire Works shoot,what happens when they come out): Trade Mark Move(Move he does everymatch that his not his finisher Like Undertaker walking on the ropes): Ok Fill out the app. and send it to [detail withheld] Thanks for joining the New Elite Force In Wrestling!
