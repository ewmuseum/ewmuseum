\_/ \_/ \_/ \_/ - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - (Dissolve into Jack Smith seated behind the Update Desk. The video wall behind him displays the Last Stand logo.) [Smith] Hello again, USeW fans! I highly suggest you get your VCR's rolling, becuase this is a COLLECTOR'S EDITION of Mania Magazine -- very likely the last one EVER! Well, as so many of you know by now, a chapter in e-Wrestling history will close sometime in the next few weeks, but the final chapter in the USeW promises to be the most thrilling and most exciting yet! EIGHT great matches featuring the best and brightest of the USeW, all of which could be a main event in any arena around the world, but we will have them for you all in ONE PLACE -- San Antonio, Texas, the Alamo Dome for the USeW Last Stand! Headlining this card will be the World Heavyweight Championship match between the current champ, the 600-pound Gorilla, and the newly-crowned United States Heavyweight Champion, the Nightcreeper! This will be a 2-out-of-3-falls match, and any outside interference is barred from ringside. It will be just the Gorilla, the Nightcreeper, and the referee in that ring, so the better wrestler will be the one who walks out of that arena with the World Heavyweight Championship around his waist. A lot of people are saying that the Gorilla's victory at the Holiday Rush over Rick O'Malley was, in some ways, a fluke or an upset. But ask the Gorilla or any of his Gorilla-maniacs that question, and they will tell you otherwise. We tried to get comments from the Nightcreeper and Matt D. Tall, but rumor has it they are in some top-secret training facility and program preparing themselves for this match. We'll just have to wait and see if the Creeper has what it takes to put the Gorilla down for the count. You want tag team action? We got it! The USeW World Tag Team Titles will be defended not once, but TWICE at the Last Stand! The first defense will pit the current World Tag Champs, Darren and Duane Machismo, against the current #1 contenders, the Gladiators. Here with their thoughts on the match at hand are Cobra and thunder..... - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Cobra: Finally! The day that ALL tag teams of the USEW have feared! The day that the Gladiators get a shot at the titles! Thunder: And to establish us as the undisputed champions, we not only have to wrestle once, but twice! Cobra: It will be done, Thunder! We've been the #1 contenders for a LONG time without getting a shot! Machismos, you're first! Thunder: We've already beaten you tow in the Summit and it's time for history to repeat itself again! Cobra: And once we get those belts, Windsor, Iconoblasts, it doesn't matter WHO we face! It is time to put it all on the line! Thunder: And we're ready to fight! Pugils up! *The Gladiators raise their pugil sticks and clink them together* - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Those of you who followed the Road Warrior Cup at the last Summit will remember that the Gladiators BEAT the Machismos in the tournament. Recent talk from Dr. Anthony Strange suggests that the Machismos are still smarting from that loss, and they are dead-set on revenge come the Last Stand. The other half of this mini-tournament will pit the two teams who were wrongly disqualified during the triangle cage match at the Holiday Rush -- former World Tag Champs, the Iconoclasts, and the team of Bobby "The Cavalier" Windsor and "California Kid" Key Atkinson. Windsor and Atkinson are both top competitors in the Light-Heavyweight divison, but they have to deal with the well-schooled Lord Thomas Imperius and the ever-dangerous Steve the Insane. Should be some very exciting and very action-packed matchups all around. Now another piece of fallout from the Holiday Rush involves the USeW Light-Heavyweight Championship. As many of you may recall Taurus brutally attacked Swashbucker Jack Hornblower with his branding iron just before the champ's scheduled match with challenger "Bullet" Bobby Dean. The after- effects of this beating greatly hampered the Swashbucklers ability to wrestle, and if it weren't for a timely distraction by Valentine the Magician, it would very well have been Bobby Dean defending the USeW Light-Heavyweight crown instead of Jack. Therefore, the Light-Heavyweight gold will be up for grabs as Taurus and Swashbuckler Jack square off in a TEXAS DEATH MATCH! No limit on pinfalls, no limit on submissions, no limit on countouts, these two will just fight it out until one of them is no longer able to continue. The last man standing will be deemed the Light-Heavyweight Champion! We received these pre-recorded comments from the Bull-man earler in the week.... - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - (Cut to Taurus in the locker room.) Taurus : Taurus finally get match with Cap'n Jack. Taurus get match with no messy rules to get in way of Taurus destroying Cap'n Jack. Taurus mad Taurus no have matches for so long... so now Taurus take it out on Cap'n Jack. Texas Death Match! Taurus' kind of match. If Jack show up, Taurus hurt Jack bad and take title. Jack no show up, Taurus hunt Jack down on street and hurt Jack worse. Cap'n Jack will wear Taurus' brand when the night is through! (bellows and gives "hook 'em horns") (Fade out.) - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Taurus and the Swashbucker are both very tough despite their being in the Light-Heavyweight division. This brawl could get very ugly very quickly. One of the longest-running feuds in the USeW will also draw to a close in San Antonio as one of the most dangerous tag teams in the league, Ready 2 Rumble, will face one of the most unpredictable teams around, Wild Hazard, in a Chicago Street Fight match with the Amazing Mr. Jones confined to a straight- jacket and suspended above the ring! This is an attempt by the USeW to level the playing field somewhat, but then again, I don't think there's such a thing as a level playing field when you're dealing with Wild Hazard! This feud goes all the way back to the beginning of the USeW and the World Tag Team Tournament. Hazard and the Rumblers faced off against each other in the first round with the Wild Ones advancing with the help of Mr. Jones falling from the rafters.... well, actually a CPR dummy facsimile of the Amazing one. This led to an exchange of post-match beatings and soakings until the two teams met again as part of a 10-man steel cage elimination match at the first USeW JAM. And here's what happened then..... - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - RJ: Reverse DDT by Beast . . . and a drop kick . . . no! The Saracen got out of the way! STF: NOT juvenile, and it worked. RJ: Saracen tags in Dave Kunze who sees his first action of the match, and he starts by delivering a kick to the midsection, and Beast is doubled over . . . now he calls for the "Eliminator"? STF: If this is what I think it is, get a hospital bed ready for Beast. RJ: Bill James is tagged in . . . double Irish whip near side . . . OH MY GOD!!!! (Crowd gasps in horror as Beast's head gets rammed almost completely THROUGH the cage!) SM: Good night! I have never, EVER seen anyone get hurled into a steel cage with that much force, Ross! He got busted wide open on that one! RJ: And I think he's out cold, too! And look at James and Kunze just flip Beast around in there like a rag doll! This is sickening! =========================(cut to later in the match)=========================== RJ: And Kunze gives Bourne a little "assistance" out of the cage, and yanks Brute back into the ring! STF: It's fun time now for these guys. They're gonna put some big-time hurt on Brute. Just you watch. RJ: Tag to James . . . double whip near side . . . and a THIRD Eliminator! SM: I think Foxx was right -- Brute WILL be joining Beast in traction! RJ: >*whump*< Lateral press by James . . . . . . >*whump*< but it's all academic now . . . . . . HE PULLED HIM UP!!!!! James pulled Brute off the mat! STF: I told you they were going to have some fun. Unfortunately for Brute, it's all at his expense! Look at this -- they're going for a FOURTH Eliminator! RJ: My God! This is INSANITY! They're not satisfied with the win! They want to end someone's carEEEEEEEEER . . . (Brute is slammed into the cage again and flops lifelessly to the mat) Good gracious! I think his neck might actually be broken . . . oh, please . . . look at this . . . >*whump*< one finger cover by Dave Kunze . . . . . . >*whump*< and here come the paramedics again . . . . . . >*whump*< and this match has finally ended! >*DING DING DING DIIIIIINNNNNGGGGGG*< HB: Here are your survivors -- Dave "Kruncher" Kunze . . . "Battery" Bill James . . . READY . . . to RUUUUUUUUMMMMMMBLE!!!!! - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Those cage shots resulted in serious neck injures to both Beast and Brute -- Brute being sidelined for eight weeks, Beast for three. However, the feud raged on, and circumstances in another federation involving Bill James and Dave Kunze resulted in the scheduling of a 6-man tag match at the Holiday Rush with Ready 2 Rumble and Marcus Prime taking on the team of Wild Hazard Beast, "Devastator" Derek Machismo, and the Electric Youth. However, this match did not end without its fair share of controversy..... - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - STF: And Prime telling him to tag in Kunze! They're going for the kill, Rossie! RJ: Kunze circling Beast like a buzzard... And he's got the armbar! NO!!! Beast slips away! And a diving tag to Machismo! STF: RATS!!! They had him!!! RJ: Clothesline by Machismo! And a powerbomb! Now Kunze goes airborne... three, four, five presses by Derek... and drops him face-first onto the mat! SM: They've got Kunze where they want him, but can they put him away? RJ: Machismo trying for a PowerDriver.... it would be his third one.... YES!!! The PowerDriver finds the mark again! SM: And that's the third one Kunze has had to take! >*whump*< RJ: Lateral press.... but Kunze find the ropes and buys himself some time! SM: I don't know how much time it's gonna buy him... as there's the tag to the Youth. RJ: Kunze reaching for his corner.... CUT OFF!!! The Youth got him first! And he's going for another Electrocution..... HE GOT IT! STF: Please, please PLEASE don't let it end like this.... >*whump*< RJ: And into a lateral press.... STF: Kick out, Dave.... >*whump*< RJ: Count of one TWO..... STF: KICK OUT!!!!! >*whump*< RJ: THREE!!!!! IT'S OVER!!! STF: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Now shortly after this match concluded, Prime and Ready 2 Rumble complained to the Commissioner's Office and showed him this videotape.... (video wall shows replay of pinfall from another angle) claiming that Dave Kunze started lifting his shoulder off the mat right..... THERE just before the three count. Now even though one of the other referees for that match, Larry DuRocher, agrees with the Hardcorps, Commissioner Freeman does not and stands firmly behind his decision. Nonetheless, the Board of Directors has mandated that Ready 2 Rumble and Wild Hazard will face off again now that both members of Wild Hazard are back to healthy. And with the Street Fight stipulations, all four wrestlers can bring anything and everything they want to the ring and use it legally in the match. Now we all know that Wild Hazard is famous for bringing a rather bizarre assortment of objects to the ring with them, but let's not forget that James and Kunze are dangerous enough as is. How much more deadly will they be once they get their hands on some weapons? We'll find out. Meanwhile, in the time leading up to and since the 6-man encounter, the other member of the Hardcorps, Marcus Prime, has sent some VERY pointed comments toward his former UeWF ally, the American Eagle. Prime has been questioning the Patriot's fan support and loyalty as well has his work ethics inside the ring. Well, this has brought about a "Loser Waves the Flag" match between these two perennial superstars at the Last Stand. There are no pinfalls, no countouts, no submissions, the only way to win this match is to have your opponent's corner person wave his or her flag to stop the match. Here now with his take on this match and another announcement is the American Eagle..... - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - ***Camera opens on the American Eagle in his locker room. He is wearing a martial arts outift, complete with black belt, with an eagle on the back, carrying both the American and Japanese flags. His face is painted, with an American flag over one eye and the Japanese flag over the other. He proudly carries his American flag in his left hand with the Japanese flag in his right.*** AE: Greetings USEW fans! It's been a long time but it's finally here! The day I get my hands on Marcus Prime! You've attacked me, my friends, and the beliefs that my country and I were based on. A lot of fans have asked me: 'Eagle, what have you been doing during the layoff?'. Well, I've been training for this day, since I knew it would eventually come. I've been honing my martial arts skills, gaining my black belt in several fields. That is why I proudly wear the uniform of my sensais. Now before you say that I've been 'learning my enemy', stop right there. This is not, and I do not want to make it, America vs. Japan. Despite the problems between the two countries, we have not been Japan's enemies for fifty years. No, this is American Eagle vs. Marcus Prime! And now, I'd like to introduce my flagbearer...Lady Liberty! *Lady Liberty enters the screen, dressed in her usual patriotic outfit. She gives the Eagle a big hug and takes the Japanese flag from him* LL: It's great to be back in the email wrestling, right where I belong: at the side of the American Eagle! It sure feels strange to hold this, but don't worry: I won't be waving it! AE (smiling): You said it! (holds up the Amercian flag) Well, Old Glory, I'll have to leave you here for now. But don't worry, I'll see you being waved soon enough! (puts down the American flag). Now, Marcus, I'm going to do one more thing to prove a point with you. You've tried to make a point over popularity and doing what's right. Well, I happen to know that my fans love me! LL: They sure do! We get tons of fan mail each and every day! AE: And I'm going to prove it to you, Prime. I'd like to introduce Steve Johnson, head of the USeW sound crew. Thanks for coming Steve. SJ (entering the srceen): Thanks for asking me here Eagle. AE: Now, according to the contract, what are you going to do during my match with Marcus Prime? SJ: Turn off the heat machine. AE: So, however loud the fans yell, that's how loud it will appear? SJ: That's right. There will be no artifical manipulation of crowd noise during your match. AE: You see Marcus! When I come out to face you, give a listen. We're going to show you what the fans think of me! And what they think of you! ***Zoom in on the two flags painted on the Eagle's face. Fade to black.*** - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Accusations of audio and video tampering have been FLYING all over the USeW since the Holiday Rush 6-man tag event. You can believe that President Rory DeLeon will be keeping very tight reins on the production crew all throughout the evening, not just during this match. And last, but certainly not least, "Mr. Devastation" Dan Sledge will collide one-on-one with former USeW World Champion, "Irish Whip" Rick O'Malley. These two first met in a non-title match way back when on national TV with O'Malley prevailing. Dissatisfied with the outcome, Sledge attacked O'Malley from behind with his trademark chain. Now these two individuals were supposed to have a Chain Match at the Holiday Rush, but the Irishman had a commitment to defend the World Heavyweight Championship, so "Manhunter" Mark Bourne was sent in to substitute for O'Malley.... and Sledge ran roughshod all over him. Now Rick O'Malley has his opportunity to avenge the loss of his friend as he tangles in a Chain Match with Dan Sledge, but this will be a FOUR CORNERS Chain Match! Pinfalls do not count in this match -- you have to incapacitate your opponent, then proceed to touch all four turnbuckles in order without being cut off or stopped. As as usual with these matches, the 10'-long chain binding these two men together can be used as a weapon in the match. >*whew!*< I'm exhausted just talking about this event! This event promises to be the Big One of the USeW, so call your local system administrator and tell him or her to keep you on-line for the USeW Last Stand! Until then, I'm Jack Smith. See ya!
