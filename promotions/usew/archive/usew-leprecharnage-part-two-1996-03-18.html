Leprecharnage Part Two (fwd)

[Boni] Oh puleeeze....these two are nothing to get excited about. These fans wouldn't know talent if it jumped up and bit them in the.... [Hoss] BONI!! Our fans are the most knowledgeable around...I've seen them appreciate even the lowlifes in this fed if they put on a good showing. Let's see if these two coming to the ring can prove me wrong. (Boos are starting to be heard as "Here Comes the Hotstepper" by Ini Kamoze starts playing in the arena. Machismo and Matthews come out and start insulting fans.) [Motormouth] Making their way to the ring at a combined weight of 505 lbs, from "The House of Style" in Los Angeles, California, led to the ring by Tara Jensen..here is Shane Matthews and Duane Machismo...the team of SMOOOTH CRIMINAALS!!!!!! [Hoss] Well the Criminals have entered the ring and Machismo will start it off. And what a way to start! He flies across the ring and attacks the White Tiger as he was talking to his partner. The Tiger is taking some major forearms across the back. [Boni] Tiger is taking some major blows to the head and back. Machismo sets him up for a suplex, Tiger blocks it and takes Machismo to the ground and the surprised Tiger quickly tags out to the Dragon! [Hoss] The Dragon locks up with Machismo and pushes him to the ropes. WOW!! He let loose with a wicked chop that Machismo wasn't expecting. Machismo over the shock of the blow hits him back with a forearm of his own. [Boni] The Dragon lets loose with a barrage of martial arts kicks and punches. He sends him to the ropes with an irish whip. Machismo ducks under the Dragons clothesline. Bouncing off the ropes again though Machismo is caught with a back bodydrop. The Dragon starting to take control now. Dragon going for the pin! 1!! And a kick out by Machismo. [Hoss] Dragon tags out to White Tiger! Tiger comes in and gives a couple of chops to Machismo. Tiger tags Dragon and whips Machismo into the ropes. Machismo off the ropes and is floored by The Connection with a beautiful double drop kick. [Boni] Machismo got it right in the chin. He looks totally out of it now. Dragon going for the pin!!! He gets a two count. Machismo still has something left in him. How long can he stand up to the punishment the Connection is laying on him. [Hoss] Tara is just screaming at Machismo to tag out! Machismo would if he had the strength but the great teamwork of the Connection is keeping him in there with no real opportunity to tag out. [Boni] The Dragon with a wristlock keeps up the pressure. Dumb move.....it might apply pressure but it might give Machismo the chance he needs to get his bearings and think of a way to get out of the ring. [Hoss] Good point there Boni! (2 minutes go by with Machismo in the wristlock and on his knees as the Dragon is kicking him and chopping him everytime he gets up.) [Hoss] Machismo finally up to his feet he bounces off the ropes and breaks the hold with a forearm to the head! [Boni] Dragon punches back! Machismo lets loose with a big kick to the stomach and finally dives to tag out to Matthews. We are at the five minute mark of this match and Matthew finally seeing some action in this match. [Hoss] The Dragon chops him as soon as he enters the ring. You can hear Tara consoling Machismo and shouting at the Tiger. Dragon with a standing dropkick and Matthews is down. It looks like nothing is going right for the Smooth Criminals. [Boni] Matthews gets up right away and punches the Dragon in the face. He then grabs him by the hair and chucks him over the top ropes. What a smart move as this would seem to be more up the Criminals alley. [Hoss] Matthews going outside and the count begins. Matthews lets loose with a couple of forearm uppercuts. The Dragon whipped into the railing. The count reaches 4. Matthews picks up the Dragon and tries an Enziguri Kick. He MISSES as the Dragon ducked out of the way!!! Matthews is stretched out on the ground. Machismo runs over and kicks the Dragon in the head. The White Tiger comes over and scares off Machismo. Matthews gets up and kicks the Dragon in the leg. [Boni] Matthews trying to slow up the Dragon. He sets him up for the whip into the ringpost! [Hoss] The Dragon reversed it!!!!! Matthews hit it full force on the shoulder. Matthews staggers to his feet. The Dragon taking it back into the ring slides in and waits for Matthews. [Boni] Matthews gets up from the devastating move by Dragon and slowly slides himself back into the ring. Dragon all over him right away stomping and kicking Matthews. Dragon sets him up for a backbreaker! Matthews is writhing in pain as he hits the ground. The Dragon lays into him with a punch to the head. [Hoss] Matthews in trouble here as he needs desperately to tag out to Machismo. The Connection have been in control almost the whole match. Reaching the thirteen minute mark the Dragon picks up Matthews and nails him with another backbreaker. The Dragon could be setting him up for a Boston Crab or a Scorpion Deathlock. [Boni] Matthews on his hands and knees begging for mercy as he knows he is in big trouble. Dragon slaps Matthews across the face and then kicks Matthews in the chest. Matthews in desperation punches the Dragon in the groin. Oooooh, that had to hurt! [Hoss] Both men laid out on the floor. Matthews beginning to stir and he slowly moves to his corner hearing encouragement from Machismo and Tara Jansen. White Tiger is screaming encouragement also but the Dragon is only beginning to stand up. The TAG IS MADE as Machismo enters the ring. [Boni] Tara Jansen dragging Matthews out of the ring. Machismo walks into another chop by the Dragon. Dragon locks up with Machismo and pushes him into the ropes. A few more chops by Dragon and Machismo is able to stick his head under the top rope to break the hold. The ref backs Dragon away and as soon as Machismo gets back in he's met with a barrage of chops and kicks and finishes it off with a headbutt. Machismo is sprawled out on the floor!! [Hoss] This is incredible action and the crowd is really loving every moment of it. The Dragon picks up Machismo he looks over at the White Tiger it looks like he wants to tag but decides not to thinking that he might be able to finish Machismo off himself. [Boni] Machismo with a stomp on the foot. He punches the Dragon in the face. The Dragon being pushed into the ropes is punched one more time by Machismo. The Dragon being sent for the ride. He reverses the whip. Machismo coming back off the ropes. Dragon gets caught telegraphing a back body drop. Machismo nails him with an elbow smash to the back of the head. [Hoss] Machismo tags in Shane Matthews! Matthews hits Dragon with a knife-edge chop. The Dragon fires back with a chop but Matthews blocks it. Matthews nails Dragon with an Enziguri kick and tags in Machismo. [Boni] Oh My God! Machismo and Matthews just nailed the Going out of Style. Matthews goes for the cover!!!!!!! Dragon kicks out right away. The Criminals are beside themselves in disbelief, Tara yelling for Machismo and Matthews to do it again. [Hoss] The Criminals setting it up again. NAILED IT!!!!! The White Tiger running into the ring to help gets nailed by a clothesline by Machismo. Matthews goes for the cover!!! 1!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!! 3!!!!!!!!! Winner: Smooth Criminals +3 Time: 21:00 minutes [Motormouth] The winners of this match at the 21 minute mark are the SMOOOOTH CRIMINAAALS!!!!! [Hoss] The Smooth Criminals getting a big scare from the Chinese Connection but have managed to win the match. Tara Jensen mocking the Chinese Connection, as White Tiger is attending to his fallen partner. [Boni] I just don't understand why Red Dragon didn't tag out to White Tiger when he had the chance. They were doing a wonderful job of teamwork in the beginning. He must have started feeling confident from the success they were having in the early stages of the match. It wouldn't have done any good anyways...Smooth Criminals were just too much for those lightweights. [Hoss] Good point..except for that last comment! You might be starting to get the hang of this after all Boni! [Boni] What the hell is that supposed to mean? [Hoss] It's open to interpretation, Boni. Next on tonights jam packed card is a match up that could very well be the most violent match up of the evening. Three weeks ago, Comanche War Dog was stripped of the North American Title for a few reasons. One he began calling it the Native American Title and two...the USeW was looking to make the fed more competetive. USeW president, Paris Burns came out and announced that the North American title would no longer exist which opened a 4-way match for the new World Television Title! [Boni] The participants of the event were reigning Television champion at the time, Haplo...former North American champion, Comanche War Dog...another former North American champion and current World Television champion, Justice D. Nyde...and top contender for those belts..Big Tommy Gunn!! [Hoss] It was a beautiful match up filled with excitement and action. When the dust settled Haplo had retained his status as champion and Comanche War Dog was livid. Blaming it on Big Tommy Gunn his anger grew. [Boni] The following week Big Tommy Gunn had a non-title match up with USeW World Champion, 187. During this match up, Comanche War Dog decided to pay a visit and beat on Gunn during his match. [Hoss] Now...March 17th. St. Patrick's Day. Leprecharnage. Big Tommy Gunn......Comanche War Dog.....and an indian strap! Yes!! An Indian Strap match where the winner must beat the opponent to a point where he can then proceed around the ring and tag all four corners! [Boni] Here are what these two men had to say about tonights match up!! (Comanche War Dog sits cross-legged on a blanket beside a smouldering fire. He is in deep meditation, suddenly his eyes open and he addresses the camera in an ice cold voice) [CWD] Big Tommy Gunn, you have made a mistake like many before you. You chose to participate in taking what is rightfully mine. Did you not think that there would be consequences for your actions? You have agreed to meet me on the battlefield. At Leprecharnage we will be joined by a leather strap. The ancesteral method of settling disputes among my people. You are fool enough to face the Comanche War Dog in an Indian Strap Match?! Think long and hard, Tommy Gunn. As the cowhide falls upon your flesh, as your blood and sweat spray upon it's length. The pain you will experience will only be surpassed by the agony of my people for centuries of mistreatment. As the strap tightens around your battered body --- the Comanche War Dog will claim revenge. Your foolhardy acceptance of this match will be the end of Big Tommy Gunn. ********************** (Big Tommy Gunn is in the gym working the speed bag. Sensei Ichi-ban stands to one side watching closely. As the camera closes in, Tommy stops the bag with one hand and turns to face the camera.) BTG: 187, you probably expect me to blame my loss to you on Comanche War Dog. Well, surprise, I figure I lost that match even before the outside interference. I made the mistake of playing your game: fists, knees, and knuckles. Next time, I'll keep my head and we'll see if you can beat me when I apply my strength scientifically, the way the Sensei has taught me to. Sensei: Hai, and also 187, Tommy-san and I owe you a bit of recompense. Your obvious displeasure with the War Dog's interjection into our match was the mark of an honorable man. Our doubts of your willingness to face Tommy man to man reflect poorly on us and demand an apology, which we herewith wish to offer. (Tommy and the Sensei both bow to the camera.) BTG: But now, on to the matter at hand. Commanche War Dog, I don't know why you blame me for the loss of your title. I didn't pin you. I was eliminated from the Four-Way Dance at the same time you were. In point of fact, if anyone has a right to be angry, YOU are the one responsible for Nyde covering ME. Sensei: You seek to blame your own failings on others, Comanche. You complain of injustice and unfairness, but when given a chance to compete fairly and honorably you scorn it. The true warrior looks not at others to excuse his failures, he looks within to correct them. BTG: At Leprecharnage, War Dog, we'll face each other in an Indian Strap Match. Bad idea. You can't run. You can't dance. And you can't stay far enough away to keep me from sinking my meat-hooks into you. Just remember, Dog, this was all YOUR idea. (Tommy returns to working the speed-bag as the Sensei bows the camera man out of the gym.) [Hoss] These two men have no love loss between them and what we have tonight is the making of perhaps one of the deadliest matches this year! Let's go to Motormouth with the introductions! [Motormouth] Now coming to the ring....weighing in at 218 lbs and standing at a height of 6'....from the Comanche Nation in Arizona....he is the former USeW North American Champion....Comancheeeeeee Warrrrr Dogggggg!!!! (War drums and battle cries are heard as Comanche War Dog comes racing down the aisle in full war paint waving the Indian Strap. As he hits the ring the ref and ring announcer slide to the outside in a hurry.) [Hoss] Comanche War Dog has brought the goods...but can he really deliver? Big Tommy Gunn will have something to say about that for sure! [Boni] I think the War Dog can definetely deliver! This is his match! Big Tommy Gunn doesn't have a chance. After tonight, Big Tommy G will be so bruised and beaten that his regular bad looks will actually be improved! [Hoss] The man is a superstar in his own right...he has been facing top competition lately and with the circumstances of late...he's going to want to pick up his game tonight to ensure the victory. Lets go to ringside. [Motormouth] Now introducing his manager, Sensei Ichi-ban. Leading his man to the ring, from Chicago, Illinois, standing tall at 6'4" and 295 lbs. Here is......Bigggg..Tommyyyyy...GUNNNNNNN!!!!!! (Sensei Ichi-ban walks slowly to the ring as Big Tommy Gunn follows close behind shaking hands with the fans. "Another One Bites The Dust" by Queen is playing and the crowd is going wild.) [Hoss] Both men in the ring now and the ref is explaining the rules to them one last time. He now begins fastening the strap to both men and checking to see if its firmly in place. [Boni] The bell has rung and the two men appear to be exchanging words in the center of the ring. Big Tommy Gunn is staring the War Dog down...OH MY GOD!!! Short clothesline with the strap by Comanche War Dog! The impact of that blow combined with the speed of delivery sent both men to the mat hard!! [Hoss] War Dog to this feet first and he begins dragging Gunn up as he climbs to the top rope. Tommy Gunn with no where to go tries shaking the ropes but Comanche War Dog keeps his balance and comes of the top with a Tomahawk Chop! Both men go crashing into the mat! [Boni] Comanche War Dog applies a rest hold on Gunn early in this match knowing that it could go the distance. Tommy Gunn appears winded after a quick attack by the former NA champ. [Hoss] War Dog picking up Tommy Gunn and going for a suplex. Gunn with the block and now a vicious knee to the midsection of Comanche War Dog. Taking two steps back from the blow, the War Dog ducks and goes behind Gunn using the strap to take Gunn off his feet in a sweeping motion. [Boni] Give credit to the wily native. He knows Big Tommy Gunns weakness and hes going to work early on that weak left knee. That brace he wears is such an obvious reminder that his knee is an easy target. [Hoss] There he goes as he starts motioning for the Indian Death Lock. Big Tommy Gunn with a quick blow to the head before the move can be applied. A hard yank on the strap and Gunn sends the War Dog to the mat head first. Both men knowing what it takes to win this match and the moves are already being seen with that leather strap. [Boni] Right now both men are grappling on the ground as every time one of them get to his feet the other one yanks him back to the mat. A punch to the head blocked, and Big Tommy Gunn delivers with one of his own. [Hoss] Now both men back to their feet and Comanche War Dog pushes Big Tommy Gunn into the ropes. He begins whipping him against the ropes with the strap. Gunn wincing in pain from each blow. The strap marks can be seen from here at the announcers table! Now the War Dog slings Gunn off the ropes and clotheslines him again with the strap!! [Boni] Gunn is clutching his throat and Comanche War Dog is sending boots to his head. Now he again drags him to the corner and begins climbing the top. Looks like another attempt at a Tomahawk Chop but this time Gunn shakes the ropes and the War Dog ends up yelling a war cry of a different nature! [Hoss] Taking the advantage, Tommy Gunn wraps the strap around the neck of Comanche War Dog and delivers a vicious snap mare from the top rope down to the mat. Comanche War Dog is in pain. [Boni] The War Dog makes a desperate attempt and sweeps Gunns legs out from under him before he can pick him up. Still in pain however, Comanche War Dog cant capitalize and he remains resting on the ground. Big Tommy Gunn to his feet again and he stoops down and again the War Dog sweeps his legs out from under him. This time instead of getting to his feet, Gunn begins pounding on the head of Comanche War Dog. [Hoss] Sensei shouting out instructions to his man now and Big Tommy Gunn succesfully gets Comanche War Dog back to his feet. A big roundhouse to the noggin and Big Tommy Gunn begins wrapping the strap around the War Dogs forearm. Fujiwara armbar in effect and the strap is adding extreme pressure! War Dog is screaming and in retaliation pokes Gunn in the eyes. [Boni] Nursing that arm, the War Dog with another leg sweep and Gunn is back on the ground. Now, he picks Gunn up and begins running with Gunn in tow. [Hoss] Comanche War Dog has just launched himself over the top rope and Gunns arm is yanked as he follows awkwardly after him into into the steel guard railing. Taking a few seconds to rest, War Dog slides into the ring and tugs on the strap so Gunn gets into the ring. As Gunn begins climbing through the ropes, War Dog delivers a boot to the head. Followed up with an elbow drop and another! [Boni] War Dogs strategy in full effect as he pulls another leg sweep and goes for a Spinning Toe Hold but the strap gets in the way and both men are temporarily tied up. [Hoss] Comanche trying to free himself but he's left himself defenseless. Gunn still on his back sends a couple of devestating blows to the head of Comanche. Now both men to their feet and they lock up. A knee lift by Big Tommy and he tries a short clothesline with the strap. War Dog ducks and hoists him over in a back body drop. The impact sends him crashing down backwards on top of Big Tommy Gunn. [Boni] Comanche War Dog grabbing the left knee of Gunn and drives an elbow onto it. Quickly to his feet and he applies the Indian Death Lock! The strategy is there Tim but why isn't the War Dog going for the corners? [Hoss] No idea Boni but the pain is evident on the face of Tommy Gunn. Comanche releases the hold and delivers a knee drop to the back of Gunns left knee. The fans have been slowly getting louder as they begin chanting Tommy! Tommy! War Dog gets Gunn to his feet and continues dominating this match as he wraps the strap around Gunn's throat and flips over top of him yanking his head in greusome fashion! [Boni] The War Dog begins tying Gunn's arms with the strap and is delivering boots to that left leg of Gunn. No mercy is being shown by Comanche as he continues showing why he is the master of this match. [Hoss] A sudden burst of anger and Gunn powers out of his predicament by whipping his arms back and sending War Dog flying over him. Quickly to his feet he punches Comanche to the head and then drags him up by the hair. We're approaching the 12 minute mark of this match and neither man has made an attempt to go for the corners. In answer to your previous question Boni, I think both men are looking to put a serious beating on each other. They don't want the win ..they want the torture. [Boni] A couple of hard blows by Gunn and Comanche is teetering. Wait! War Dog has just hit the canvas and a quick slide under the legs of Gunn! He raises the strap up hard between the legs of Gunn and Gunn is singing soprano! [Hoss] Another attempt at a Spinning Toe-Hold but Gunn regains his composure long enough to prevent the hold. To his feet now and with an obvious limp, Gunn slams the War Dog to the mat and is met with a quick counter by Comanche as he does yet another leg sweep and slaps on a Spinning Toe Hold. Gunn is in serious pain and Comanche could be getting nearer to accomplishing the win. If Gunn cant stay on his feet then he can't provide much resistance when the Dog goes for the posts. [Boni] The War Dog is starting to go for a corner! Is he going to make the rounds?! No. Big Tommy Gunn with still enough strength to get back to his feet. A chop to the chest of Comanche, Comanche going for a dropkick but is swatted down by Gunn. Gunn with the momentum, swoops down and picks him up and delivers an enormous powerslam. Gunn takes Comanche into the corner and slams him into the turnbuckle. [Hoss] Now it looks like Senbsei Ichi-ban is instructing Gunn on what needs to be done next. Comanche has gotten a second wind and without hesitation climbs to the top rope. Gunn resets his attention once again on Comanche but after a few moments of struggling to get Comanche off the top, he is met with a Tomahawk Chop. [Boni] Comanche with another wear down move as he applies a hamstring stretch. The momentum has been shifting so much between these two men but in my opinion, Comanche has been sticking to his game plan much better than Gunn. [Hoss] Comanche continues to work the legs while Gunn who is constantly trying to get away from his brawling behaviour of past has needed to revert to that mode during this match. [Boni] Comanche now tying Gunn up again as he delivers chops and kicks to his head. He begins making his way to the corner and suddenly Gunn lunges through the ropes!! Comanche is yanked viciously through the ropes and Gunn has taken it to the outside. Powerslam on the concrete and he throws the War Dog back in. [Hoss] Gunn is going for the corners!! 1 post!! 2 posts!!!! And Comanche has kicked the left knee of Gunn from behind causing it to give out and he falls to the ground. Groggy, Comanche then picks him up and delivers a kneebreaker to the left knee of Gunn. Spinning Toe Hold again!! How much more punishment can Big Tommy Gunn take?!?! [Boni] Not much more I think. His limp has increased dramatically in this match and now the War Dog is whipping the strap across the legs of Big Tommy Gunn! [Hoss] The welts on Gunns legs and back can only hint at the pain he's in! Bringing Gunn to his feet now and Comanche with a deadly enziguri that levels Gunn immediately. Taking the strap, Comanche begins rubbing it into the eyes of Gunn and the pain is causing him to thrash about on the canvas. [Boni] Again Comanche lifting Gunn up and I think hes heading to the corner...back body drop out of nowhere by Gunn!! A boot to the head of Comanche and a huge powerbomb!! [Hoss] Gunn is on fire!! Hes just doubled up the strap and is waving his finger at the War Dog!!! What's he saying? He's telling Comanche War Dog he's a BAD DOG!!!?!! He's whipping him without mercy and Comanche War Dog appears to be fading fast! Another boot to the head and Gunn is limping towards the corners!! 1 post!!! 2 posts!!!! War Dog is stirring... 3 posts!!!!! War Dog falls back down!! 4 POSTS!!!! DING!!! DING!!! DING!!!! [Motormouth] Your winner of tonights Indian Strap Match....at a time of 27 minutes and 20 seconds........BIGGGGGG TOMMMYYYYYY GUNNNNNNNNNN!!!!!!!!!! Winner: Big Tommy Gunn +6 Time: 27:20 [Hoss] What a comeback by Big Tommy Gunn. The match was back and forth for quite a while but it really looked like Comanche War Dog was going to add another win to his specialty match. [Boni] Well...Big Tommy Gunn showed he had what it takes to win but he proves what an idiot he is by taunting the War Dog with that Bad Dog comment. You don't defeat a man by humiliating him. [Hoss] Right...like the War Dog wouldn't have done the same. Anyways...it appears that Comanche is now receiving medical treatment in the ring....no....he's just shrugged off the EMTs and he's on his feet a bit dazed. That last whipping Gunn gave him has him bleeding in various areas..the welts are huge! Do you think this is the last Big Tommy Gunn has heard of Comanche War Dog? [Boni] It's all in the hands of Comanche War Dog now. Will he accept tonights loss and concentrate on other matters? Or will he seek revenge once again on Big Tommy Gunn who he felt cost him his title. Big Tommy Gunn...you survived the beating tonight...but I'm sure there will be others you wont be so lucky against. [Hoss] Everyone is entitled to their opinion. Personally, I think Big Tommy Gunn could be looking at a bright future here in the USeW. By the way, don't you have an interview to conduct? [Boni] Oh yeah. I forgot. Thanks Tim. [Hoss] Anytime Boni. Glad to be rid of you for a bit. (Boni runs off, nearly running right into Mr. Wrestling IV, who looks to be a bit put out.) [Hoss] Once again Mr. Wrestling IV has joined me to work our next match between the USeW's Rookie Sensation, Doug Carr, and the legandary Suicidal Sean Altman, but before we start the match, how's your car IV? [MWIV] That jerk Sean Altman parked in my spot, so the vallet put mine in Boni Blackmail's. And the stupid broad had it towed! Hey Boni, thanks a lot!
