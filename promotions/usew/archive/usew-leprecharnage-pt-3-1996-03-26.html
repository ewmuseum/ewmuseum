He goes for the quick tag to Warhammer. The powerhouse of the team moves in and quickly bodyslams Schmidt to the ground. Schmidt quickly up and locks up with Warhammer. Warhammer tosses Schmidt to the ground. Schmidt slowly gets up and shakes the cobwebs out of his head. [Hoss] Schmidt goes over and talks things over with Lavin. Schmidt locks up with Warhammer after a small conference with Lavin. BIG HEADBUTT by Warhammer and Schmidt goes down. Warhammer on him for the quick cover. Schmidt kicks out of it after a one count. [Boni] Warhammer gets up and pushes Schmidt back down and drops an elbow on him. Schmidt quickly rolls out of the ring and Lavin is there to talk to him. Warhammer is in the ring trying to pump up the crowd. [Hoss] Schmidt is back in the ring now and Warhammer bounces off the ropes and starts to run at Schmidt. Schmidt ducks under one clothesline but gets DEMOLISHED on the way back by a big clothesline. Schmidt is down. Warhammer picks him up and powerbombs him to the mat. Schmidt is motionless and Warhammer goes for the cover. 1!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!! 3!!!!!!!!!!! Winners: White Knights +3 Time: 3:36 [Motormouth] You're winners and still USeW champions the WHIITE KNIGHTSSSSSS!!! [Hoss] That was incredible! The White Knights wasted no time! Was that the quickest World Tag Team victory ever? The Equalizers did not do well at all! [Boni] It looked like The Madman couldn't get anything going as he was obviously overmatched by the Warhammer William Knight. It looked as if the Equalizers had a bad night. We all know how talented The Equalizers are...they are top contenders in their federation and perhaps they were just overwhelmed by the fact they were facing an undefeated and seemingly unbeatable, White Knights. Too bad though, the match had the makings of a classic.
