DCW - Rules & Regulations ---------------------------------------------- 1. WELCOME Welcome to DCW - Downtown Championship Wrestling. DCW is a completely scripted E-Fed based in Queens, New York. The league was founded by Dennis Hall, a sports writer from New Jersey, who has the aim to bring only the very best E-Wrestling to the "Big
Apple". To find out more about DCW keep on reading. 2. GENERAL STUFF DCW focuses on good Angles, rather than on long, boring match descriptions. Therefore the cards consist of only short match descriptions with commentary in between.
The Outcome of the matches is decided by Roleplaying. To roleplay simply send in a Promo or Interview for your Wrestler for every show. We do not require Match Strats, however If you want to attack somebody or do something which should occur on the card simply mail me your request and I'll see what I can do.
3. SHOWS Currently we do have one show every week - "Retribution". The show takes place every Sunday afternoon (from Lost Battalion
Hall
in Queens, NY) and usually features 10 - 12 matches.
Also, there will be one PPV every month. 4. TITLES In DCW there are four Titles. Those Titles are: DCW Heavyweight Title DCW Tag Team Titles DCW Television Title DCW Brass Knux Title The Heavyweight and Tag Titles must be defended twice every month, while the Television and Brass Knux Title must be defended on every card, if not otherwise noted. While all DCW titles are usually defended in ordinary wrestling matches the BRASS KNUX
Title is only defended in Gimmick matches.
5. CHARACTERS In DCW there several NPC's - Non Player Characters. Those can be Referees, Announcers, Commentators, etc. These are some of the most important NPC's in DCW:
Dennis Hall - The CEO of DCW. Hall is the mastermind behind DCW. He hires and fires people whenever he wants to and gives a crap about the fans. He is in his mid 40's, has short, blond hair and enjoys showing off his wealth.
Ross Gorden & Fred Hayes - The Commentators. Gorden is in his late 40's and a native of Austin, TX. He started his career in the buisness as a Referee. Hayes is a former Pro Wrestler. He is in his late 30's and a native of Long Island.
Bud "The Stud" Hall - The cousin of Dennis Hall. Bud is the most annoying human being in the history of mankind. Sometimes he does Play-By-Play with Ross Gorden and Fred Hayes, but altogether he is just plain annoying.
6. SIGN UP In DCW you may have up to two Single Wrestlers or one Single and one Tag Team. You may not have two Tag Teams or anything exceeding three Wrestlers. Only fake Wrestlers will be accepted.
