Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

IWA opened on the twentieth of June 2001, one of three unrelated feds sharing the acronym "IWA" swept into this project's own GeoCities census at once. Its own served Title History page records all five of its own titles vacant; its two weekly shows, Monday Night Kaos and Wicked Thursday, each carry only a blank card and a blank results page, alongside a Pay-Per-View page in the same shape.

The fed's own Roster page names exactly two wrestlers — Taz and Sabu, both real performers, each with a placeholder 0-0-0 record — and its own Stables page reads "None at this time." Its own Rules page caps each handler at two wrestlers, bars racism from the roleplaying board, and promises every roleplay a read. Nothing in the held corpus shows the fed ever staging a match.
