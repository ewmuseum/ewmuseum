We then realize that those are gunshots, and a sudden flurry opens up revealing Brian Nelson, J.R. Neubs, & Adrian Cadido sitting around a table inside an EWA stuido.} Brian Nelson - Hello everyone, and welcome to this week's edition of EWA Wednesday WAR! All the matches happened last night, and we'll be giving you the reports of what went down. Adrian Cadido - That's right, Brian. Let's begin right away by going to Kent! _____________________________________________________________ "THE MASTER" MISTER BIG vs. STEVEN "EDGE" SPECTOR by: Kent ______________________________________________________________ Sly Kent: Welcome, to our little part of Wednesday War, the hottest thing on Wednesdays since ALF!!! Steve Clark: ALF ran on Wednesdays? Sly Kent: Who knows, I was trying to make a point. Steve: Oh. Sly Kent: Let's get to the match. First Mister Big came down and he was met by a number of boo's. Also people were chanting, Vader wantabee, Vader wantabee. He didn't look to happy, but he didn't really pay much attention. Then came Spector and did this place ever erupt. The crowd was going crazy for the former 2 time NA Champion, as well as DPW Skyweight Champion. Right as the bell rang these two went at it. Big took early control when he nailed a nice powerslam on Spector. Big then whipped Spector into the corner, but made the rookie mistake of trying to splash him this early in the match. Spector got out of the way in plenty of time and started to chop away on the realing big. Steve: Then Spector went up top and BULLDOGED Big to the mat, with incredible force behind it. Spector then whipped Big into the ropes and proceded to nail him with a spinning wheel kick. The move almost took off Big's head! Then they went back and forth for about 3 minutes. Then Mister Big nailed a Powerbomb and went up top to try to hit the Masterbomb! Spector moved and then he brought Big to his feet and hit him with an incredible Jumping DDT! This set Mister Big up for the Cherry Blossom Bomber. Edge just got Mister Big up for it, and he covered and got the 1-2-3. Winner via pinfall at 10:09 -- Steven "The Edge" Spector _________________________________________________________ "ECSTACY KID" KEVIN JADE vs. FANTASY by: Kent _________________________________________________________ Mike: This one features the PCW's very best Kevin Jade, facing off against that perfume wearing FREAK Fantasy. Sly Kent: I bet he's your fantasy Mike. Mike: Get real, Sly. I love women, which I have many of. Sly Kent: You mean those hooke.. Mike: On to the match. Things started out with Jade getting a mixed review from the fans. Sly Kent: There were slightly more boo's from the crowd then cheers. Then came the gay man himself, Fantasy. This guy wasn't popular with the crowd, especailly the men. Mike: But he was popular with Dark Destroyer, who as we all know, thanks to the detective skills of Gunnar Gaines is gay! All of the women in the crowd were hiding their little boys to avoid haveing them get molested or something. Sly Kent: Then the match started and Fantasy was rubbing his chest in front of Jade so Jade came up and slapped the boy. Jade then whipped him into the ropes, but Fantasy came back and nailed a nice flying crossbody. Fantasy didn't go for the pin, because he was rubbing Jade all over. Jade pushed Fantasy off of him and Jade quickly sprung to his feet. Fantasy then charged in but Jade countered with a Mexican armdrag takedown. Fantasy then took some control when he hit some nice moves including a flying forearm and a legdrop off the top, but he could only get a 2 count. Then Jade got momentum by whipping Fantasy into the corner and nailing him with a splash in the corner. Fantasy tried to kiss Jade so Jade gave him a knee to the lower abdominal area. Mike: Very, very lower abdominal area. Damnit just say it, he nailed him with a knee to the boys!! But Fantasy seemed to enjoy it. Jade then hip tossed Fantasy out of the corner. Fantasy got to his feet and tried to kick Jade in the stomach, but Jade caught his foot and hit the Ectasy Kick, which is his signature enziguri, or the move that knocked Shawn Michaels out of commision. Sly Kent: That move has beaten 7 wrestlers, and Fantasy was the next on the list. Jade scored the 1-2-3 with one finger and it was all over. Another win in the books for "Ectasy Kid" Kevin Jade. Winner via pinfall at 10:01 -- "Ectasy Kid" Kevin Jade ____________________________________________________________ NEGAFORCE vs. RETURNERS by: Kent ____________________________________________________________ Sly Kent: These are two teams that haven't had much wrestling action here in the EWA. As a matter of fact, this is Negaforce's debut. Just to let you know, the Returners consist of Locke Coles and Sabin Figaro while Negafore consits of Malachite and Neflyte. Steve Clark: Both teams are in the ring and the bell rang. Things started out with Malachite and Sabin in the ring. Sabin took early control by hitting a nice suplex as well as an awesome Lou Thez press that only got the two count. Malachite took some control by nailing a nice clothesline and a standing reverese neckbreaker. Malachite hit a Cradle Suplex but that wasn't enough to put Figaro away. Sab then tagged in Locke. Locke quickly hit a nice crossbody but that wasn't enough either. Malachite tagged in Neflyte. Neflyte and Locke went back and forth with Neflyte mostly in control. Neflyte then hit a big splash off the top that was AMAZING!!!! Sly Kent: It sure was Steve. Neflyte then tagged his partner in. Neflyte placed Locke on his shoulders and then Malachite went to the top. But Locke reversed things and did a victory roll before Malachite could do anything. It almost finished off Negaforce but it only got a 2 and a half. Locke tagged in his partner, Sabin Figaro and Sabin went for the Bum Rush on Malachite but he isn't the legal man. Neflyte then caught Sabin in a Frankensteiner. Neflyte went up top again and hit a Cannonball Splash that ended things for the Returners! Winners via pinfall at 9:23 -- Negaforce ______________________________ MENACE 2 SOCIETY vs. DREAM TEAM ______________________________ CTG: Right hi there. Caesar the Geezer here and it's time for me to go through these matches we had earlier... first we had The "Dream" Team verses Menace 2 Society! Unfortunately, this match couldn't even happen, due to some static between both Porter and Dreamer supposedly backstage. Dreamer couldn't find another partner, and the match was won by forfeit by Menace! Winners - Menace 2 Society ______________________________ EXTREMIST TITLE ****************************** GUNNAR "THE GRIZZLY" GAINES vs. EQUALIZER ______________________________ Brian Nelson - Gunnar "The Grizzly" Gaines showed us WHY he's the EWA Extremist Champion tonight. He came rushing down to ringside, slid in the ring, and NAILED Equalizer in the back of the head with the belt. That completely knocked him out. Gaines then went outside and brought a table into the ring. He picked up Equalizer and GRIZZLY SLAMMED him into the table. Need I say more? Although Equalizer looked the best of his career, Grizzly can't be stopped now. Winner - Gunnar "The Grizzly" Gaines ______________________________ OSAMI KARELIN vs. DEEP FREEZE ______________________________ Brian Nelson - A great match here with plenty of thrills for the fans. Both men showed us what they're made of tonight. Karelin came down to ringside, by the way, with his brother Alexander Karelin, who was the 84 and 88 Greco-Roman Gold medalist. Karelin took control at the outset of the match. He displayed amazing agility. Springboard dropkicks, clotheslines, and even a hurricanrana to the outside. But when Osami was getting up from that, Psycho Sarah got involved and nailed him with a kick to the ribs. Deep Freeze then took control. He rolled Osami into the ring, brought him to the second rope, and prepared Osami for a tombstone all the way down. Not to be. Osami wrapped his legs around Deep Freeze's neck and turned it over driving Freeze's head into the mat. He then picked him up and went wild with the shootfighting technique that only Osami Karelin could bring to the EWA. This match came to a close when Osami was reaching through the second rope and trying to grab Psycho Sarah. Deep Freeze was on the outside and nailed Osami in the face with an iceball. The referee saw this and awarded the victory to Osami Karelin. Winner - Osami Karelin by DQ ______________________________ CRAIG JAYHAWK vs. SOUL AVENGER ______________________________ Brian Nelson - Craig Jayhawk and Soul Avenger started this one off with a nice handshake, in which Soul Avenger used to his advantage. He slung Jayhawk with one hand into the mat. Jayhawk applauded this move, and then took complete control after hitting a frankensteiner. After a few more minutes of rest holds, Jayhawk nailed the Clash and added another V to his win column. Winner - Craig Jayhawk. ______________________________ DARK DESTROYER vs. DARK SLAYER ______________________________ CTG: Right next up was the Dark Destroyer verses Dark Slayer!!! And guess what? Dark Slayer pulled off the upset of the year and BEAT DD!!!! In case you couldn't tell I was being sacastic DD kicked his ass and got the Flatliner on him in five minutes! Dark Slayer got his ass kicked, but I'm sure it won't be the last we hear of him! Winner - Dark Destroyer ____________________________ MAIN MEN vs. MICK MORTON & LAYNE LARSON ____________________________ CTG: Next up we had the Main Men verses the WEW's pathetic excuse for an invasion. Of course the Main Men did get in a lot of offense. Great high power moves such as chokeslams and ghostbusters and Powerbombs. But in the end Morton managed to nail Arcin with the Smoosher and got the three count. Oh yeah, I almost forgot, the Knight and Jobba came down to ringside to watch this. But, if you ask me both these teams suck and neither of them could beat the Knights! We're not gonna see those tag titles change anytime soon! Trust me! Winners - Morton and Larson ______________________________ NORTH AMERICAN TITLE MATCH! ****************************** MANIACAL MAILMAN vs. "HOLLYWOOD" JOHNNY DREAMER ______________________________ Brian Nelson - Dreamer came into this one with high hopes of finally reaching superstardom. Everything Mailman threw at him, Dreamer would come back. The fans really appreciated the fast paced back and forth style of this one. Dreamer pulled out his arsenal of piledrivers, powerbombs, suplexes, and DDT's. Was this enough to stop the EWA North American Champion? No way. Mailman was just to powerful for the youngster. He clamped on the Fold, Spindle, & Mutilate to make Dreamer submit. After the match, Dreamer threw a tantrum, kicking the ropes, stomping the mat, and I could swear I saw a tear. Cheer up, Johnny. Winner - Maniacal Mailman ______________________________ TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH! ****************************** GOOD KNIGHTS vs. KEMPO & DARKLORD ______________________________ CTG: Our main event. The only two decent Tag Teams here in the EWA got head to head. Well both men put on a pretty good show! Kempo & Darklord put on some pretty good double teaming and held their continutity well. And the team of the Knights showed off some of their more aerial stuff. In fact I think they pulled off the move of the year. Arnie frankensteined Darklord off the shoulders of Jobs who himself was standing on the top rope! What a move! Nearing the end, though, Kempo and Darklord really took control and came very close to winning the titles! In the end is when the surprises went down. Super Scott came down to ringside to a chorus of cheers and boos, and gave the 4 Horsemen sign to Kempo, which Kempo returned graciously. That's when it happened....Super Scott PUNCHED KEMPO! HE LEVELED HIM! Kempo fell back in disbelief and... Jobs rolled him up from behind while he was distracted and got the 3 count! The Knights keep the belts! But what of Super's situation with the Horsemen?
