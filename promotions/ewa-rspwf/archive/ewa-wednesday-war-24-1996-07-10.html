No matter though. Freeze stayed on his game the whole match and fought Jayhawk punch for punch, hold for hold. Jayhawk had the advantage through most of the match as he had been working on the leg of Freeze. He set Freeze on the top rope, went behind him, and let Freeze put him in a headlock.
