INDEX - Recent News - Injury Report - Champion list and Top 10 - Signed matches ***************************** RECENT NEWS ************************** - DPW TIES SEVERED! Recently, the bond between the DPW and the EWA was severed. An official in the EWA recently was said to say that "the reason that this (the separation) has happened is due to internal conflicts and disagreements." Whatever the case, the DPW is no longer with us. *Side note: I would like to thank my friends who supported the EWA and me through this, and I also apologize for letting the EWA get word of Alan's and my disagreement. - WEW INVASION!? Indeed the WEW is here! Recently, at "Offensive Dreams", Mick Morton and Layne Larson turned against the fans, and the EWA. Bringing ZOG into the picture with them, they have challenged 3 of the EWA's men to a match at our next PPV, Extreme Realization. So far, two men have stepped up: Arcin, former World champion, and shootfighter Osami Karelin. One left, and we go to war! - BEST OF THE BEST TOURNAMENT AND TITLE NOTE Keep in mind that the event was _not_ sanctioned by the EWA, but their title is recognized by us and can be defended here. - OD AND ROLEPLAY VOTING "Offensive Dreams" was given an "8" overall by all of you. Glad to see that you all enjoyed it. As far as the roleplay package vote? Package = 1. Format that we use now = INFINITI. Tough call. : ) - EXTREME REALIZATION - OUR NEXT PPV July 29th, prepare for the realization of what the EWA is: EXTREME. Extreme Realization is closer than you think! And after Sunday the 14th, we will be announcing just a few of the MONUMENTAL matchups signed, sealed, and to be delivered on that historic night! ***************************** INJURY REPORT ************************* - Extremist DARK SLAYER recently passed away in a plane crash, much like the Titanic and Masked Marvel did long ago. Our condolences to his friends and family. - LOBO and PYTHON have taken leave of absences from the EWA due to injuries and contract disputes. ******************** CHAMPION LIST and TOP 10 ******************** EWA World Champion - "Dreamlover" Trey Porter [WC] EWA North American Champion - Maniacal Mailman [NA] EWA Television Champion - Hollywood [TV] EWA Extremist Champion - Gunnar "Grizzly" Gaines [EX] EWA World Tag Team Champions - The Good Knights [WTC] TOP 10 (SINGLES) 1.
