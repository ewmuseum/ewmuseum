EWA World Title: Phoenix vs. HellShock

18 February 1997 (recovered via a 14 July 1999 repost)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, folks, I dipped into some of my old stuff in my hard drive and I found something that some of you might be interested in. If you remember EWA, you'll remember that it was one of the top organizations of it's time with IIWF and some others. Here's a magnificent EWA Title match from February 18, 1997 on EWA Tuesday Extreme. Enjoy!
-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==- HELLSHOCK <Champion> vs. PHOENIX -==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==-==- By: Miles -==-==-==- ASHLEY COX: We're back and we're ready for the EWA World title match! ERIC GIBSON: Finally, we get to the good stuff. AC: This should be quite a match between these two bitter rivals. As everyone knows, Phoenix defeated Hellshock at Night of the Extreme II just a week ago, and because of that victory, he gets a shot at the
EWA World title tonight.
EG: Don't bet on him winning though. Hellshock has my bet tonight. AC: Oh really? Well, I'm sure you'll eat you words as usual. EG: What's going on back by the curtains? Joe Blow's not even in the ring yet. AC: Somebody's coming down to ringside. IT'S MAGUS! Magus has come down here to ringside! He's now going in the ring! What's he going to say?
(Magus motions for the ring attendant to give him the mic, which he does.) MAGUS: Don't worry .. I'm just here to make sure that this match is a
CLEAN one. Which in turn means ... if you try to interfere, your ass is getting a whippin'!
CROWD: YYYYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHH!!! (Magus hands the mic to Joe Blow, who has come in the ring. He then goes outside of the ring.)
AC: Magus is gonna make sure no one interferes here tonight. Wait, now there's something else happening down the aisleway. What's going on?
EG: Someone's bringing out a BURNING CROSS! AC: Why is that cross there? EG: Well, I'm hoping Hellshock uses it during this match. AC: I wouldn't doubt if he did. They're putting that cross right in the middle of the aisleway, and I guess it's gonna stay there. Well, let's get up to Jow Blow for the main event here tonight!
JOE BLOW: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall, and it is for the EWA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!! Introducing first, from Vancouver, British Columbia, weighing 238 pounds,
Phoenix!! CROWD: YYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH! ("Tomorrow" by Ozzy Osbourne plays over the PA as Phoenix makes his way to the ring. He wears a black trenchcoat, and a skin tight, long sleeved black shirt. He has black electrical tape wrapped around his knuckles, wrists, and up his forearms. He wears black tights with a silver Phoenix spreading it's wings on both outer thighs. He also wears a pair of black boots like Sting use to wear. Gabrial flies in front of him and lands on the top turnbuckle.)
JOE BLOW: And his opponent, he is the EWA World heavyweight champion, accompanied to the ring by Tragedy, from Portland, Oregon, weighing
257 pounds, here is Hellshock!!!
SOME: BBBBBBBBBBBOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! OTHERS: YYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHH!!! ("Jump In The Fire" by Metallica plays over the PA as Hellshock comes out from behind the burning cross, accompanied by Tragedy. Hellshock wears black wrestling tights and black boots with a red cross on one.
He is also wearing one of those KKK pointy hats, which he takes off before he wrestles. He has long black hair, perfectly slick, his skin is white, he has a two-inch cross embedded into his forehead and he has a glove on his hand.)
(DING, DING) AC: Both of these guys are ready, and this should be a great match. There were some excellent matches at Night of the Extreme II, but this one may outdo all of them! Magus has picked up a a chair and taken a seat in front of that burning cross, and Gabrial has flown up to the balcony, and it looks like we're underway.
EG: Well, I hope so, because tonight Hellshock's gonna give Phoenix the wrestling lesson of his life.
AC: That's your opinion. Hellshock and Phoenix walk out to the middle of the ring and now they're staring eye to eye. Both of these men obviously have a deep-seeded hatred of one another. Both men now back off, and lock up! Hellshock backs up Phoenix into the ropes, and delivers a stiff right hand!
EG: Hellshock is gonna keep the gold tonight! No way Phoenix can beat him! That's what I say, and I'm never wrong.
AC: We'll see about that. EG: Sure. Just keep you eyes on the match and try to stop thinking about me. Back in the match here, Hellshock has started chopping away at Phoenix's chest, but Phoenix kicks Hellshock in the midsection, and starts chopping away on Hellshock!
AC: Phoenix now whips Hellshock into the corner, and runs in after him with a BIG clothesline! Hellshock sags a little in the corner, and
Phoenix picks him up the legs and pulls him out...AND HE'S GOING FOR
THE PURGATORY! EG: Already? AC: Hellshock breaks it though with a kick to the midsection! Hellshock's back up now, and NAILS Phoenix with a clothesline! EG: These guys are giving it all they've got so far. AC: They're two great athletes. EG: Not as good as me though! AC: Yeah, right. Hellshock now picks Phoenix up, but Phoenix hits him with a few good elbows to the midsection. Hellshock is staggered, but comes charging in, and gets hit with a beautiful dropkick by Phoenix!
Phoenix is going over to Hellshock. Phoenix picks Hellshock up, and
PLANTS him with a power bomb! A cover!
1.... 2... NO!!! AC: Phoenix thought he had Hellshock there. Hellshock rolls out of the ring, and Phoenix bounces off the ropes, and hits Hellshock
OUTSIDE the ring with TOPE OVER THE TOP ROPE! Hellshock's got to be hurting after that one!
EG: Phoenix picks up Hellshock up and whips him HARD into the guardrail! Hellshock moves the rail back and almost hit a couple of fans that time!
AC: Phoenix now is going over to railing to the fans!?! What's going on? Wait a minute! Phoenix comes running and hits Hellshock with a clothesline OVER the railing! Phoenix is showing his offensive arsenal here so far tonight! I don't think he's as good of a sport as we all think!
EG: He's still a punk! He looks weird with that white face. AC: And Hellshock doesn't? He's got a cross in his forehead!! EG: Yeah, but that looks cool. AC: Anyway, Phoenix rolls Hellshock back into the ring and climbs in after him. Hellshock comes off of the ropes, and Phoenix rocks him with a powerslam! The cover!
1... 2... NO!! AC: I don't know how Hellshock's been able to hold on so far tonight! He's definately on the verge of losing that belt! EG: I'm still banking on Hellshock, but Phoenix seems to be getting lucky and hitting all of his moves! Phoenix pulls Hellshock up, and nails him with a double-underhook piledriver! His face went right into the mat! Where's Hellshock at? C'mon man!
AC: Phoenix now feels comfortable and goes to the rop rope. He goes off with a flying bodypress, but Hellshock moved, and Phoenix hits the mat HARD! Hellshock somehow gets to his feet after that brutal beating and goes over to Phoenix. He picks him up for a slam, but delivers a backbreaker instead! Hellshock drops an elbow now, and picks Phoenix back up again! A northern-lights suplex!
1... KICKOUT!! AC: The challenger won't go down without a fight! EG: But he will go down! Hellshock picks up Phoenix and delivers a
DDT! A cover! 1... KICKOUT!! EG: Hellshock gets a little confident and goes to the top rope. He comes off and delivers a FLYING ELBOWDROP to Phoenix! Phoenix looks like a beaten man now!
AC: Phoenix IS in pretty bad shape! He looks like he might be out of it! Hellshock still goes on the offense though. Phoenix is now back to his feet and Hellshock hits him with a few NASTY forearms, and now with a elbowsmash! Phoenix falls into the corner, and Hellshock comes in after him! Hellshock DELIVERS a clothesline right to the face of
Phoenix! Phoenix falls to the mat, and Hellshock sets up for his next move, which is a kneedrop right to the back of Phoenix's head!
Hellshock looks in control and being so, whips Phoenix into the ropes for a backdrop, but Phoenix rolls him into a small package!
1... 2... 3!!! AC: WAIT, IT WASN'T THREE! Hellshock kicked out just in time! EG: Now that almost ruined my prediction! Both men back up, and they're really going at it with fists, chops, and forearms!
AC: Phoenix misses with a clothesline and Hellshock hooks Phoenix into a backslide! 1... 2... NO!! AC: Both men are back up to their feet and Hellshock goes for a clothesline, but Phoenix reverses it into a German suplex! 1... 2... KICKOUT!! AC: Both men are up, and Hellshock with a kick to the midsection that doubles over Phoenix! Piledriver by Hellshock! Here's the cover!
1... 2... 3... EG: YES! Wait, the ref's only holding up two fingers! It was only was only a two-count! How in the hell did he kick out of that! He must have not have gotten all of it! Phoenix should be done!
AC: The look on Hellshock's face would say he's confused too. Hellshock picks Phoenix up now, and Phoenix decks him with a big right! Phoenix is coming back after that vicious piledriver!
Hellshock kicks Phoenix in the midsection and sets him up for a power bomb, but Phoenix reverses it into a Frankensteiner! Phoenix stands up and charges at Hellshock with a flying bodypress, which sends BOTH men over the top rope and to the floor!
1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... AC: Phoenix finally gets back to his feet and rolls Hellshock back into the ring, and now Phoenix comes in too. I think the championship's about to change hands here!
EG: Hellshock looks to be in trouble, but I think he'll pull through it! Phoenix is going to the top rope now, but Hellshock sees him!
Hellshock runs in, but Phoenix jumps over the top of him! Hellshock now with his back to Phoenix, and Phoenix is hooking him with a German suplex! Hellshock's holding on to the top rope though!
AC: Hellshock seems to have a death grip on that top rope and won't go into Phoenix's German suplex!
EG: THE ROPE! THE ROPE JUST SNAPPED OFF THE TURNBUCKLE! The top rope fell off! AC: And Phoenix DROPS Hellshock with a German suplex right on the neck! 1... 2... 3!!! AC: We've got a NEW World champion!! (DING, DING) EG: I don't believe it! Hellshock was in COMPLETE control! AC: Just not at the end. JOE BLOW: Here is your winner, in seventeen minutes and fifty-four seconds, and _NEW_ EWA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, PHOENIX!!!
CROWD: YYYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHH!!! AC: The crowd's going ballistic as the referee wraps the title belt around the waist of the new champion! Hellshock's still down on the mat though. He was really drove into the mat on the back of his neck with that German suplex! I think he'll be okay though. Both men put on a tremendous show here tonight!
EG: Yes they did, but it was a travesty that this punk Phoenix won the belt! What a worthless champ!
AC: Who's that coming down the aisle now? It's Dick Johnson! What does he have to say? (Dick Johnson comes to ringside and grabs the mic.) DICK JOHNSON: Hello, people. I don't want to spoil Mr. Phoenix's victory here, but I must let everyone make note that the circumstances that have occurred here should NOT go against Mr. Hellshock...
That is why, though I cannot reverse the refferee's decision, I MUST order a special rematch to be held in the middle of March that will decide everything ONCE and FOR ALL!
(Crowd pops mildly...) EG: It's about time someone made a good decision around here! If that rope wouldn't have broken, Hellshock would still be the World champion!
AC: Well, that's debateable, but we'll see if he can win back the title in his rematch. Well, that about does it here tonight, so for the self-proclaimed "Every Woman's Dream" Eric Gibson, I'm Ashley Cox, saying so long everybody!
(Camera shows Phoenix snapping Hellshock back in a German suplex, and his hand being raised while he's being presented with the EWA World title and then the camera fades to black.)
