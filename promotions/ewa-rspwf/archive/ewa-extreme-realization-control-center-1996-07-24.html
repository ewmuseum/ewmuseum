EWA Extreme Realization PPV: Control Center

24 July 1996

I_______________ASSOCIATION_______________I -=-======////EWA ER Control Center 7/24/96\\\\====-= v^v^MaCrO by [name withheld] w/ [name withheld]v^v^v (The scene opens to two men sitting behind a large table. One has slicked back black hair, and wears a tan suit. This is "Slick" Rick Baptist. Alongside him is a shorter man with shoulder-length brown hair, a black suit and a frown on his face. This is Jack Jenkins) Slick Rick - Welcome to the summary report of our next PPV, Extreme Realization, coming your way this Sunday! It's sure to be a spectacle to remember for years to come. Jack Jenkins - Spectacle? The only spectacle in the EWA right now is watching you make a fool out of yourself, Rick. SR - Oh yes, with me today is Jack Jenkins. <Jack leers at Rick, Rick laughs> SR - Are we ready to run through the card? Let's get to it! EWA vs. WEW ---------------ARCIN, SUPER SCOTT, & "GOLDEN BOY" MATT SUITZ---------------- vs. -------------"DEVASTATOR" MICK MORTON, LAYNE LARSON, & ZOG-------------- SR - Ever since our last PPV, "Offensive Dreams", went down, this "Team WEW" has been wrecking havoc on the EWA. Led by their manager, Duke Derringer, Larson, ZOG, and Morton are set to try and "take over" the EWA. Not to be, says Arcin, Matt Suitz, and the outspoken Super Scott. Of course, Jack, you have to wonder how "Team EWA" will get along. JJ - You mean "Team Super Scott"? I see it as this, Rick. Why not just give Suitz and Arcin the night off, and put the Super one alone in there with WEW? He'll wipe the mat with em by himself! SR - Well, I would feel more confident myself with former EWA World champion Arcin and the "Golden Boy" in there, but hey, I'm not going to bet against my own league. We'll have to see what happens there, and I'm sure the outcome will be interesting. -------KEMPO & DARKLORD vs. THE JOAD FAMILY------- JJ - Here's the one I'm looking forward to, Rick. Those country hicks getting a beating from the former FASW World Tag Team champions, K & D. SR - True, they had tremendous success in the FASW, and in the EWA also, but don't give the Joad's the short-end just yet. Even though Daddy Joad is coming in with a small shoulder injury, I'm going with the Joad's to pull the upset. JJ - THE JOAD'S? ARE YOU NUTS RICK? These are the guys that call home to 'Momma' every second! These are the guys that think "Skanky Mama" is SEXY! <laughs> SR - Tune in and we'll see who will get a boost in the tag rankings for sure. SPIN THE WHEEL MATCH ----------------OSAMI KARELIN vs. "ECSTASY KID" KEVIN JADE----------------- SR - This promises to be a good one. Placed on the wheel are 6 stipulations, 3 chosen by each competitor. And here are the six that will be in jeopardy Sunday night: 1. I'm Too Sexy Match - Match ends when opponent has something broken. 2. Shootfight Match - It's the real deal if this happens! No holds barred. 3. Strikezone Match - Bed of nails surround ring, with tables. Must throw opponent through table onto the bed to win. 4. UFC Style Match - Tapping out, and a cage will be used for this if chosen. 5. Unforgiven Sin Match - 64-foot cage, glass platforms, weapons, the WORKS! 6. Kickboxing Match - Old fashioned kickboxing will have to be used. What do you think, Jack? JJ - I think that I want to see one of Jade's selections there. Osami is just so....so....shootfighterish! SR - That's what the fans love about him. We'll see who can take it Sunday. --------------------TOYOTA HURASHI vs. SIR JOE--------------------------- SR - We saw the return of Hurashi last night on RARE, and wow, he looked impressive. JJ - He always was, Rick. He dominated the EWA when he was here long ago, and I don't have any doubt that we've seen the last of him. SR - But have you checked out Sir Joe's "extreme mentality" recently? The guy is getting better every day it seems like, and I wouldn't doubt the upset here. JJ - WHAT? To Super Scott's lackey? You have to be kidding, Rick. Hurashi wins it. SR - That's Jack's opinion, but I have a feeling it will be close. ---------------------LORD OF THE RING BATTLE ROYALE---------------------- Participants: Lord Condemn, J.C. Snow, Shinara, Mongol, "Maniac" Mike Gorman, "Hollywood" Johnny Dreamer, Lord Foul, Mister Big, Warrchild, Mr. Ouija, T.R. Ela, 4 Masked Men SR - The great thing about LOTR battle royale's is that not only did we make it up first <smiles>, but it's a great opportunity to display young and old talent alike. We have rookies like Foul, Snow, Mongol, and Mister Big taking on veterans like Gorman, Dreamer, Ela, and Ouija. Don't forget the Threshold of Pain in there, and of course Lord Condemn defending the title he won quite a while ago. JJ - What is this, Rick? _Four_ masked men? SR - Huh. I dunno, Jack, must have just been added by our bookers. Nevertheless, I look forward to seeing them _and_ possibly a new crowning of the LOTR! SAILOR MOON MATCH -----------------NEGAFORCE vs. SOUL AVENGER & MYSTERY PARTNER------------ SR - Okay, here's one that I'm sure we'll _all_ remember for years to come. The Sailor Moon match... JJ - What the HECK is wrong with being a Moonie? <smiles> EVERYTHING! THEY SUCK! It's a DAMN cartoon for heaven's sake! SR - As you recall, Soul Avenger, Kevin Jade, and a few other wrestlers in the EWA saw Negaforce's, uh, "unique" behavior, and dubbed them Moonies. Thus, the match's name and stipulations. Let's go over the rules: 1) Strikezone Match rules (see Spin The Wheel match summary) 2) You have to injure your opponent so bad that they must be taken to the hospital 3) The loser must sing the Sailor Moon theme song while in the hospital. JJ - The stupidest thing I have ever heard, Rick. You're not going to allow this are you? SR - <chuckles> Hey, if they want to take that chance, so be it! I, for one, would NOT want to lose this! Someone's going to be highly embarrassed Sunday! ---------------------STEVEN "EDGE" SPECTOR vs. LT. NICK DRAVIN-------------------- SR - Yes, this is Drac's brother, who competed in the DPW for a while. I believe Spector and he fought a few times down there, but now their war has escalated to new heights since Spector's last match with Nick's brother, Drac "Raven" Dravin. JJ - Yeah, I remember. I guess Nick thought that Edge was going to hurt his brother more afterward, and he threw his "grenade" at Spector. I don't see any harm in that, Spector needs a good @$$ kicking in my opinion. SR - Drac promptly DDT'd Nick after that, and Spector didn't appreciate what Nick did either. They're slated for war on the 28th. NO-DISQUALIFICATION - TRIANGLE MATCH ------DEEP FREEZE vs. "TRICKY" DICK NIXON vs. "GOLDEN BOY" MATT SUITZ----- SR - Wow, talk about a heated TV title situation. These three, along with EWA Television champion Hollywood, have a 4-way feud beyond the realm of just competition. Keep in mind that in this one, the winner faces whoever the TV champion is, either Black Venom or Hollywood, in a match on the next card. JJ - <puts hand in saluting form> Hail to the chief! HE IS NOT A CROOK! <laughs> Nixon is going to win this, and I'm going back home smiling! SR - Don't count out Suitz or Freeze either, Jack. All of these guys are TV championship material, and that's the scary thing. Being no-DQ it makes it even worse. Sure to be one of the most classic triangle matches you'll ever see in the EWA. --------------------------HELLSHOCK vs. DRAC "RAVEN" DRAVIN--------------------- SR - Well the fans are indeed in for a treat in this one. EWA SUPERstar Drac Dravin takes on the former FASW and EWA World champion, Hellshock. We know how this started, don't we Jack? JJ - I've loved every minute of it, Rick! From discussions of trees to kicking Dravin's @$$ in the ring after his match with Maniacal Mailman, these two are ready! I can't wait! SR - I've personally never seen Dravin so "extremely-tempered" than I saw last night. He was indeed insane, and if I were Hellshock, I wouldn't want him after me. Those two make history, I'm sure, at ER. TELEVISION TITLE - CAGE MATCH -------------------HOLLYWOOD (champion) vs. BLACK VENOM------------------ SR - Black Venom, a man that has suffered through a lot of hard times here in the EWA, finally has recieved something he has truly earned, a Television title shot. Unfortunately in his way is Hollywood, a man that has proved that he's hard to stop. JJ - Ever since 'Wood beat Magus for that belt, he has beaten all comers and hasn't stopped once, Rick. Venom comes into the match with a lot of rest time, which could be the advantage that wins him that title. SR - I tend to agree, but never sell Hollywood short. He's sick of interference, so, at his request, this is a cage match. Should be nothing less than spectacular. TAG TEAM TITLE - TRIANGLE MATCH --GOOD KNIGHTS (champions) vs. DEVIL'S DISCIPLES vs. MENACE 2 SOCIETY- SR - Well it's finally come down to this. One of the most-anticipated tag team match to _ever_ take place in the EWA, we see three of the greatest teams EVER compete for those belts. Menace 2 Society, current PCW Tag Team champions. Devil's Disciples, former EWA World Tag Team champions and just returning to the EWA. And, of course, the champions of almost 2-and-a-half months, the Good Knights. JJ - That's one LONG reign, Rick, and I suggest it comes to an end tonight. There's just too many good teams in there not to have new champions. But, like you said about Hollywood, Rick, don't sell the Knights short. That Jobba won't, that's for sure. SR - Who is the tag team that is worthy to carry the EWA into the next century? Find out! EXTREMIST TITLE MATCH --------------GUNNAR "GRIZZLY" GAINES (champion) vs. SILENCER-------------- SR - The RETURN of Silencer, ladies and gentlemen! One of the most anticipated comebacks will take it's first run in the EWA Sunday, Jack. JJ - Yeah, unfortunately for "Mr. FASW Icon" he ran into a Gaines. Gunnar will take this boy out faster than two jugs of wood grain, and that's pretty damn fast. SR - I tend to disagree, Jack. Silencer is indeed a star, and stars usually get their way somehow. A battle from the past shoots into the future. Silencer vs. Gunnar, part 2, for the Extremist title! We'll find out who deserves that title! PCW WORLD TITLE - WALL OF TORTURE MATCH ------------------------UNKNOWN (champion) vs. TAROT-------------------------------- SR - Pacific Coast Wrestling is ready to showcase two of their biggest stars tonight, Jack, those being in the shape of newly-crowned World champion Unknown facing rising star Tarot. Jack? JJ - From what I saw watching PCW Hotter Than Hell last Sunday, these two can take a _lot_. It was obvious to me though, that Tarot is the supreme _master_ of the mind game, and Unknown has to watch out for that. I predict a new champ. SR - I tend to disagree, Jack. Unknown had the advantage in the Alaskan Death match, and he has the advantage in the Wall of Torture match. You have to be strong to go through with it, and Unknown is just a little stronger. We'll see if the "cards come into play" again Sunday at ER. NORTH AMERICAN TITLE MATCH ------------------MANIACAL MAILMAN (champion) vs. MAGUS--------------------- SR - The rematch from when Magus was TV champ, Jack. What a classic they had at Night Of The Extreme. JJ - I don't see them going 75 minutes in this one though. Magus, the jerk that he is, will keep nagging the leg of Mailman, something he didn't do in the first match. I predict another new champ, unfortunately, in Magus. SR - You're rooting for Magus, Jack? JJ - No stupid, I said I'm _predicting_. Hopefully, I'm wrong. SR - We'll see when the Mailman and Magus collide. WORLD TITLE - LADDER MATCH --------"DREAMLOVER" TREY PORTER (champion) vs. DARK DESTROYER------- SR - Well, supercards are said to be built around the main event, so here it is. I can already see the word "classic" floating in my head, Jack. The ladder match, part 2. JJ - Their match in FASW was indeed one not to be forgotten, Rick, but let's remember this is the EWA we're talking about here! Unfortunately for the Destroyer, Porter _IS_ the master of that ladder, and he'll find out Sunday. SR - Don't forget the Destroyer's battles with Psychrobat in those memorable Caged/Ladder matches last year. Both these men know their way around a ladder, and they both certainly know how to be champions. Only one will prevail though, Sunday night. Will the Cartel or Horsemen be involved? Only time will tell. That about wraps it up, folks! Make sure to tune in Sunday night, July 28, for the Extreme Wrestling Association's presentation of EWA: EXTREME REALIZATION! Till then, this is Rick Baptist with the "Infamous" Jack Jenkins, saying keep it RARE! JJ - Wrong show, Rick. SR - ......Shut up, Jack.
