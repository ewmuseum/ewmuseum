EWA Update 6/14/96

14 June 1996

Welcome to the EWA Update for 6/14/96! RARE will be in your boxes on Sunday, in what is sure to be a newsmaking day. A _ton_ of matches announced for "Offensive Dreams" in this issue! Read on... INDEX - Recent News - Injury Report - Champion list and Top 10 - Signed matches **************************** RECENT NEWS ********************************** - EXTREMIST TITLE HEATS UP Without a doubt, the most hotly contested battle for a title right now is in the Extremist title division, with the Dark Destroyer being our current champion. Among the contenders for that honor are Gunnar Gaines, Soul Crusher, Dark Slayer, Craig Jayhawk, and Reignfire. There will be a 4-way match scheduled for OD (Offensive Dreams) announced later in this issue. - TV and NORTH AMERICAN TITLES IN THE NEWS Magus, our TV champion, and Maniacal Mailman, our NA champion, have both been in the headlines as of late with their matches scheduled at OD. Magus takes on the determined Hollywood, in what will be their fifth encounter, and the Mailman takes on an opponent to be announced at a later date. - WORLD TITLE SPECULATION The question right now on the minds of everyone is whether or not "Dreamlover" Trey Porter recieved the EWA World championship fairly. As you recall, "Offensive" Alex Adams was stripped of the title when he was arrested right before their match, being a supposed suspect for the murder of the late "Super" [name withheld]. Their rematch is set to go at OD in the main event, and there are speculations that it will be a respect match. The Adams/Super trial starts in a week. - "OFFENSIVE DREAMS" - THE NEXT EWA PPV Coming to your computer screens on June 30, 1996, the EWA will come live from Toronto, Canada in the Skydome for "Offensive Dreams", our 5th PPV. Keep your eyes on this for details. - WEBSITE WORKER WANTED Interested in working on the EWA Web page? We are in _drastic_ need of an update, but with me knowing nothing of how it works, I need the volunteer services of one of you. If interested, and having the knowledge to do it, email me as soon as you can. ****************************** INJURY REPORT ****************************** - DRAC DRAVIN'S coma seems to be more serious as ever, as he might be paralysed at this point. Our prayers are with him and Robyn. - MONKEYSHINE recently broke his arm in a weightlifting accident. He will be out for about a month. *********************** CHAMPION LIST and TOP 10 ************************ EWA World Champion - "Dreamlover" Trey Porter [WC] EWA North American Champion - Maniacal Mailman [NA] EWA Television Champion - Magus [TV] EWA Extremist Champion - Dark Destroyer [EX] EWA World Tag Team Champions - The Good Knights [WTC] TOP 10 (SINGLES) 1.
