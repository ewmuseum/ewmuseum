WORLD TITLE - "Dreamlover" Trey Porter vs. Soul Avenger Slick Rick - WELCOME to the 25th edition of EWA Sunday Night RARE! <streamers fly, fireworks, etc. In the ring, a fireworks-coated '25' sign lowers down, with the crowd's approval behind it> SR - Can you believe we've had TWENTY-FIVE episodes of RARE? WOWEE! Hi, I'm Rick Baptist, and joining me this week, as he does every other week since about 3 months ago, Jack Jenkins! Jack Jenkins - <boos are heard for Jack> The "Infamous" Jack Jenkins! Listen here people, you would be NOTHING without the influence of me! <turns to shout at people> I MADE YOU DAMMIT! SHUT UP! SR - Settle down! GEEZ! But folks, check out the arena! LOOK DIFFERENT? IT IS! The EWA has now moved out of the Pond, and have purchased our OWN arena! EWA ARENA is born! JJ - This place is just as crappy as the pond, except it's not as cold! @#$% you buddy! SR - Whoa! Anyway, we have summarized matches for you tonight ladies and gents. Let's get to those right now! _______________________________________________________ "HOLLYWOOD" JOHNNY DREAMER vs. LORD CRUEL By [detail withheld]@aol.com _______________________________________________________ Slick Rick - Folks, this one was no less than exciting. In what was well over a 20-minute match, we saw some great reversals and maneuvers. In the end, Dreamer pulled out a victory with a german suplex. Some insiders in the sport often wonder why Dreamer does not do as well in the EWA as in others like PCW. Maybe this is the beginning of his shine. Winner: "Hollywood" Johnny Dreamer by PF _______________________________________________ Extreme Rules CRAIG JAYHAWK vs. MACKAVELI By [detail withheld]@aol.com _______________________________________________ Slick Rick - Well this was indeed a wild one. The crazed punk-rocker Craig Jayhawk, a man who is only a young age of 17, takes on the dangerous, extreme rookie, Mackaveli. Mackaveli has definitely proven his "extreme" title, and showed it off tonight quite well. Using chairs and other miscellaneous weapons, Mack taught Jayhawk a lot about punishment. Nearing the end though, Mackaveli made the mistake that Jayhawk was waiting for. As he set Craig on a table in the middle of the ring, Mack climbed to the top turnbuckle and _missed_ a legdrop while Jayhawk moved out of the way. Jayhawk then picked up a nearby chair, and with a loud native-american yell, smashed the chair on Mackaveli tomahawk-style and scored the pin. Jack Jenkins - Get to the fun part, get to the fun part! SR - Yes, yes. Well, the "fun" did indeed happen at the end. While Jayhawk stood victorious in the ring, "Maniac" Mike Gorman did the heinous act of charging to the ring and CANING Craig Jayhawk! When Mackaveli got up, he CANED him down too! He even caned the _referee_! When Gorman finished, he walked to the dressing room to a chorus of boos. Winner: Craig Jayhawk by PF _____________________________________________ BLOODWOLVES vs. DYNAMICS By [detail withheld]@aol.com _____________________________________________ Jack Jenkins - Lemme do one of these Rick! Okay, now, <whispers> the tag match right? Slick Rick - <sighs> Yes Jack... JJ - Okay! Now, this was a GREAT tag match between two GREAT teams! After an impressive display of power moves by the Wolves, countered by the Dynamics quickness, it was hard to pick a winner. In the end, though, the DPW-haters prevailed after "Neon" Leon Dixon hit Wolf #2 with a piledriver, and Andre Reich made the pin with a flying senton. After the match though, <whispers again> what does this say, Rick? SR - Oh geez! <snatches paper back> I would have _hoped_ you could read! Anyway folks, after the match Jobba the Butt, the bodyguard of the EWA World Tag Team champion Good Knights, came to the ring and completely SQUASHED Wolf #1. Obviously this is a message to the Wolves concerning their upcoming match for the championship. That Jobba is a load. Anyway, the Wolves get a double-backfire here, but are sure to emerge again. Winners: Dynamics by PF _______________________________________________________ "OFFENSIVE" ALEX ADAMS vs. DEATH By [detail withheld]@aol.com _______________________________________________________ Slick Rick - Not being able to get word from the former champion, "Offensive" Alex Adams did NOT show up for this match. DEATH was informed a bit ago though, and did not make the trip out to the EWA arena. We hope to get information on Alex's supposed alcohol problem as soon as we can, but we wish the best for him. Winner: No-contest _______________________________________________________ Cage Match MAGUS vs. "GOLDEN BOY" MATT SUITZ By [detail withheld]@aol.com _______________________________________________________ Slick Rick - Guaranteed to be a knock-out, drag-down affair, Matt Suitz and Magus have one of the most intense verbal wars in the history of e-wrestling. For those of you who know the "Golden Boy", you know that he is the master of talking smack. And for those of you that know the former EWA TV champion, Magus, you _know_ he doesn't back down from any challenge. The stage was set, and as the cage lowered, the intensity rose. And as soon as the bell rung, these two were all over each other! Suitz slammed Magus's head into the cage, but Magus quickly recovered slamming Suitz into it as well! The action continued in this form, until the two finally started to tire out. Being an enclosed cage, the only way to win was by pinfall or submission, and each man knew that. Late in the match, Magus hoisted Suitz up to the top turnbuckle, and tried a superplex. Suitz blocked it twice, and shoved Magus off. This is where it got controversial. Matt Suitz stayed on the turnbuckle, and came off with a tremendous forearm shot to Magus's head, right with that forearm pad. Suitz got the 1-2-3, but speculation abounds over Suitz's winning ways. Jack Jenkins - <mimicking> "Speculation abounds"....geez Rick, can't you admit the "Golden Boy" is all he says he is? He's GREAT! SR - And he's your winner tonight as well. Winner: "Golden Boy" Matt Suitz by PF ___________________________________________________________ Tag Team Title Match GOOD KNIGHTS (champions) vs. DREAM TEAM By [detail withheld]@aol.com ___________________________________________________________ Slick Rick - Well, I don't know how to explain this match. We knew from the start that the Dream Team, comprised of "Hollywood" Johnny Dreamer and "Dreamlover" Trey Porter would be challenged in this one because of both of them having matches tonight, but we didn't expect what happened. Jack Jenkins - It was GREAT! Every time Dreamer tried to tag Porter, Trey would just ignore him! Hah! SR - Funny for you, _not_ for Johnny. Double-teamed throughout the match by the champions, Sir Jobs-A-Lot and Arnold Hamenegger finished Dreamer off with ease. After the spike-piledriver was delivered, possibly to mock Kempo & Darklord's finishing move, the Knights pinned Dreamer, and Porter left the ring. I think it's safe to say that static abounds that team. Winners and STILL Tag Team champions: Good Knights by PF ______________________________________________________ Television Title Match HOLLYWOOD (champion) vs. DEEP FREEZE By [detail withheld]@aol.com ______________________________________________________ Slick Rick - We thought RARE was exciting enough already? We didn't count on this NEXT surprise that went down! Jack Jenkins - Neither did I, but it certainly was pleasant! SR - Folks, we saw a _great_ match between Wood and Freeze, they put on a tremendous show. Even though Hollywood was in control just a bit longer, that was stopped when Psycho Sarah distracted the referee so Freeze could throw an iceball in the face of the TV champ. Nearing the 25-minute mark, we got the surprise I referred to earlier. "TRICKY" DICK NIXON suddenly stepped out from the curtains and entered the ring! Stepping up to Hollywood, he DECKED him with a foreign object and left the ring, making the match a no-contest. After the match, I interviewed the former EWA superstar, and he had this to say: ----->Slick Rick - "Tricky" Dick Nixon, I must say that this is a TOTAL shock! ----->"Tricky" Dick Nixon - Well, it shouldn't be! I've been carefully plotting out my comeback for almost three months now, and what better time could it happen at than Independence Day Weekend? Heheheh. ----->SR - I know the question on everybody's mind now is pertaining to what you're doing here. But before I get to that, I want to ask you, where were you all of this time? ----->DN - I had a problem that doesn't concern you! Let's just say that Bob Dole is BEYOND help! What matters is that I'm back! I'm BACK! And my agenda is just as important as it has _EVER_ been! ----->SR - You certainly look in tip-top shape! It's most obvious that this break hasn't put you back physically! ----->DN - Correct, Rick. You're finally getting the hang of kissing my ass! Look, not only am I back in the best physical condition that I've ever been in, I've come back... EXTREME! So, let's get down to business, Rick. What am I doing here? That's the question on everybody's mind? I'LL TELL YOU! Gold is the only thing that's elluded me, here in the EWA! I've had all of the executive power that a man could ever want or need! So, I had to make an Executive Decision, so to speak. What title should I capture first? I figure that the competition is so tight here, that I should work my way up. So, that would just lead me to the Television title, now wouldn't it? ----->SR - The Television title is our _ENDURANCE_ title! ----->DN - SHUTUP! I've got the endurance! I've got the EXTREMITY! So, I'm challenging the TV Champion to a title match at Extremity Personified!!! It's the _NEWLY EXTREME_, "Tricky" Dick Nixon versus the TV Champion. And right now, that would be you, Hollywood. Oh, poor, Hollywood. Somebody could really feel sorry for somebody like you... being the namesake for the single most corrupting piece of society!!! Well, I don't give a good God damn if you're named after horse $hit! THE TITLE IS MINE!!!!! ----->SR - PLEASE, MR. NIXON! This is _LIVE_ TV! ----->DN - Ah, BLOW IT OUTTA YOUR ASS! Just remember this, people! "Tricky" Dick Nixon is BACK! He's back, and he's _EXTREME_!!! ----->Fans - BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!! Slick Rick - The fans were certainly up in arms over that, and I can't blame them! Nixon is back! Winner: No-contest when "Tricky" Dick Nixon interfered _____________________________________________________ Total CHAOS!! Match KEMPO & DARKLORD vs. THRESHOLD OF PAIN By [detail withheld]@aol.com _____________________________________________________ Slick Rick - This had to be one of the most anticipated tag matches to happen in the EWA, and we got to see it tonight on RARE! And, trust me, it was 5 stars! Jack Jenkins - I like this ToP, they're willing to lay it all on the line. SR - They did just that! In this Total CHAOS!! match, taken from the league, there were no countout's, no DQ's, and basic VIOLENCE! While Kempo and Shinara battled it out near the railing on the outside, Darklord and WarrChild took it to the dressing rooms! Later on, it was out in the parking lot, and let's just say that more than one car's window was shattered! JJ - THAT WAS MY CAR RICK! SR - Oh please, Jack, do you know how many announcers have said that line? At any rate, it seemed that the end was near when Kempo piledrived WarrChild on top of a truck, but it was not to be. As WarrChild fell from the truck, he landed flush on Darklord, and into a pinning perdicament. As the referee registered the 1-2-3, Shinara whacked Kempo over the head with a tire iron, and furthered the bloody mess of K & D. It looks to me that the Threshold of Pain are for real, people. Winners: Threshold of Pain by PF _____________________________________________________ SUPER SCOTT vs. "ANARCHIST" AARON ADAMS By [detail withheld]@aol.com _____________________________________________________ Slick Rick - Much like the Alex Adams/Death match, Aaron Adams did not show up as well, a face that he eluded to in their last interview. Super Scott was indeed in a rage after that, and picked on one of the fans at ringside who was wearing a souvenir mask. It turned out that Super Scott realized who the masked man was, and immediately walked back to the dressing room. More on this in the weeks to come. _______________________________________________________________ World Title Match "DREAMLOVER" TREY PORTER (champion) vs. SOUL AVENGER By [detail withheld]@aol.com _______________________________________________________________ Slick Rick - It seems that we are guaranteed a classic whenever the EWA World champion, "Dreamlover" Trey Porter, steps into the ring, and the challenge from the Avenger was no exception. After a hard battle which threw both men back and forth, the end neared when Soul Avenger stood on the apron attempting to bring Porter back into the ring with a suplex. Porter twisted in the air, and landed on the Soul Avenger. 1-2-NO! Avenger got out and reversed it! 1-2-NO! Porter then scurried out of the hold, and managed to get his "Dreamlover" clawhold on the Avenger, taking him out and picking up another win. In the end, the still-upset Super Scott came to the ring, and verbally bashed Porter. It seems as if their feud is heating up, and we will see that one LIVE next week on RARE! Till then, thanks for tuning in... Jack Jenkins - <to fans> DIE YOU IDIOT! DIE!! SR - See ya later, and keep it RARE for another 25!
