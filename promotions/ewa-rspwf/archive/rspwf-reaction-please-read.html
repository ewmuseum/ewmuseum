RSPWF Reaction -- [Please Read]

Dear EWA, PCW, DPW, and e-wrestling participants and friends, Some of you know me, some of you don't ... but whatever the case, if you voted in the 1996 RSPWF Awards, please read on. Being in a college thrown into seclusion, I didn't really get a chance to dig into the "outerworld" technlogy has much as I would have liked. The school's server has been down for a while, and going on the Internet itself has been a struggle. Staying in touch with email has basically been the only way I have been able to stay in touch with you all also, because my IRC server has recently been shut down as well. After giving up my one-year possession of the EWA and being out of e-wrestling altogether for the past 3 months, I finally got to enter the halls of the participants once more starting last month, and am enjoying the sport again. At any rate, let's get to the point. I just got my hands on a copy of the RSPWF Awards, and I have a few things to say regarding them. First of all, I would like to thank those who voted for my characters in their respective categories. Seeing Tarot and HellShock up there really was a high tribute. They accompanied a lot of great names and surprised me. Characters aside, the highest achievement I felt while reading the Awards was the mentioning of the EWA. Seeing that a match I wrote, OAA vs. DTP, won match of the year ... achieving second on the "Best Announcer" list ... EWA Sunday Night RARE recieving best show ... EWA angles being mentioned highly ... An EWA feud I worked on being 1st place ... and the EWA being the 3rd best fed of the year (write-in vote) --- These are _very_ special for me to see. Accomplishing what we have with the EWA over just one year has been amazing, and it's a real tribute to see that our work was rewarded. This letter's purpose is mainly to thank those of you that voted us so high, and that I thank you for noticing my co-workers and my hard work that year. Dropping the EWA was the hardest thing for me to do. I started the league from scratch with inspirations from the FASW and others, basically guessing as I went. I had the best writing staff until the end of my tenure as well ... Kent transforming into a great writer, Scott T. becoming the master of the 'dark' match, Ken covering everything I couldn't do, Brian N. becoming a legend. The EWA truly set a landmark for things to come. Recently, the EWA has had some bad times. SKLOKAZOID ([name withheld]) has taken over, and he is _very_ capable but low on time. Working together with him is producing a better product, but negative comments and allusions can not help. In fact, it surprised me to see such anti-EWA comments in the Awards themselves. I'm not sure how they appeared there, but I feel that not only is that not fair to me, but to the EWA as a whole. Our product continually thrived to be the best that it could, and many of our members felt that we did achieve that. Sure, learning is a hard process, but the end product showed what effort could do. At any rate, I would like to thank you all again for your recognition of the EWA. Honor is a great thing to have attained ... let's try to keep it intact between _all_ leagues. -Rick B.
