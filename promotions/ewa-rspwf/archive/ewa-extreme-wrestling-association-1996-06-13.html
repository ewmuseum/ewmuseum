EXTREME WRESTLING ASSOCIATION The EWA: Extreme Wrestling Association, is a quality fantasy wrestling league circulating on the net since October of 1995. Since then, we have provided some of the best plotlines, roleplays, and match writings around. Our titles: World, North American, Television, Extremist, World Tag Our superstars: "Dreamlover" Trey Porter, Maniacal Mailman, Magus, Dark Destroyer, "Offensive" Alex Adams, Drac "Raven" Dravin, Hellshock, James "Joker" Kiljoy, Steven "Edge" Spector, Hollywood, Gunnar "Grizzly" Gaines, Psychrobat, "Devastator" Mick Morton Our writers: ~ [detail withheld]@aol.com - Head Booker & Board Member of the EWA, EWA RARE Simulator, Creator ~ [detail withheld]@aol.com - EWA Board Member, EWA RARE Simulator [detail withheld]@ArkansasUSA.com - EWA Board Member, EWA WAR Simulator ~ [detail withheld]@msn.com - EWA Board Member, EWA WAR Simulator Our Web Site: Currently being remodeled....but you may see the new construction at: http://ctdnet.acns.nwu.edu/~greatone/ewa/ewahome.html Our final words: We have dedicated our work to the recently-passed away [detail withheld]@aol.com . Quality is our number one factor, and we strive to keep this at hands at all times. Our 4 supercards, and our 2 weekly shows have been great fun for us to write, and our roleplayers make these the best they can be. Unfortunately, the EWA is full at the moment, but if you would like to recieve our cards, updates, and roleplays, become an EWA fan by emailing [detail withheld]@aol.com . The EWA - Where the _real_ men WRESTLE! -PrezRick
