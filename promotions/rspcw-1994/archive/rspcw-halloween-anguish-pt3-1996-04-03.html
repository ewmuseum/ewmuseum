Halloween Anguish (3/3)

3 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The following was taped on April 3rd, 1996] SCOOP: Welcome back, fans. As you can see the ladder is in place in the middle of the aisle, and that belt that Mark Destructo brought with him is about to be raised 15 feet over the ring in preparation for the big ladder match coming up in just a few minutes!
FISH: You know, I really cannot get over the utter cowardice of Chris Taylor! I mean, this is the man that *demanded* this match in the first place, and then to back out it with almost no notice like that...
SCOOP: I can't condone that attack on "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor's character, but, current politics of the e-wrestling world set aside, I can't think of anything that would keep Chris Taylor away from, quite possibly his last chance at revenge on the Heartbreak Kid, who has said that this would be his last singles match here tonight!
FISH: Fear, plain and simple. He realized what a big mistake it was challenging Petrow to "any kind of match" in the heat of the moment like that, and he's just trying to cut his losses. Come to think of it, this is the first intelligent thing that Taylor has ever done!
SCOOP: Stop it, Fish! But nonetheless, Petrow and Mark Destructo *will* meet tonight, in a match between two men who have both retired World's titles, but bare very little similiarity otherwise!
FISH: Well, "The Heartbreak Kid" is not a big guy, but he is an extremely intelligent wrestler who occasionally bends the rules in his favor...
SCOOP: Bends the rules? If Joe Petrow merely "bends the rules", then Adolf
Hitler was a good guy that just went too far! There is absolutely nothing that Petrow would not do if it meant gaining an advatange in a fight!
FISH: Exactly, he bends the rules just a little bit... SCOOP: Still, these tactics also mask the fact that Petrow is a tremendous ring technician who can fly with the best of them. He also has an extremely high pain threshold, which he will definitely need against his opponent tonight.
FISH: That's right, Mark Destructo, quite possibly the most awesome physical specimen in the world today! The man's incredible strength and surprising agility in the ring have allowed him to steamroll over every man he's faced!
SCOOP: Indeed, with the exception of a certain Taylor... FISH: The greatest travesty in pro wrestling history! SCOOP: ...Mark Destructo has been untouchable as of late. So truly we have the immovable force versus the irristable object tonight!
FISH: You know, it breaks my heart to say this, but Mark Destructo has just too much power, and too much support from the genius Thomas
Jones III to lose this match. Petrow, I hope your insurance payments are up to date!
SCOOP: Oddly enough, I'm going to disagree with you here. It's going to take quickness and stamina to win a ladder match, and if anyone can negate the presence of Jones it is The Heartbreak Kid! But enough talking, let's go down to ringside for our main event!
[*ding* *ding* *ding*] ANNOUNCER:LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, *THIS* IS OUR MAIN EVENT OF THE EVENING... A SPECIAL CHALLENGE LADDER MATCH! [Loud cheers from the crowd] ANNOUNCER:THE ONLY WAY TO WIN THIS MATCH, IS TO BRING THE LADDER INTO THE
RING, AND USE IT TO RETRIEVE THE BELT SUSPENDED 15 FEET ABOVE THE
RING. THERE IS NO REFEREE, AND NO TIME LIMIT FOR THIS MATCH!
ANNOUNCER:INTRODUCING FIRST...MAKING HIS SPRING RESIDENCE IN MOUNTAIN VIEW
CALIFORNIA, A FORMER TWO-TIME RSPCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION,
AND UNIVERSITY OF MICHIGAN ALUMNI, "THE HEARTBREAK KID" JOOOEEEE
PETROOOOOWWWW!!! [Joe Petrow emerges to an earth-shattering ovation, to the music of Bob
Segar's "Old Time Rock 'n Roll." He comes to the ring wearing his varsity jacket and maize and blue pant-length trunks, slapping hands along the way.]
SCOOP: Well, if nothing else, Heartbreak Kid has the home crowd advantage tonight! FISH: I can't believe the way he's pandering to the people! He must've been beaten by nine thugs recently!
SCOOP: You are way out of line there Fish, I'd watch myself if I were you! ANNOUNCER:AND HIS OPPONENT... [The PA system blares "Sweet Dreams" by Marilyn Manson] ANNOUNCER:...LED TO THE RING BY HIS MANAGER THOMAS F. JONES III, HE HAS HELD SINGLES AND TAG TEAM TITLES THROUGHOUT THE WORLD,
HAILING FROM CHICAGO, ILLINOIS AND WEIGHING IN AT 368 POUNDS,
THE SELF-PROCLAIMED TOUGHEST MAN IN PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING TODAY
MARK DESTRUCTO!!!! [A mixture of cheers and boos. One fan is showing waving a sign that says "THE REAL WORLDS CHAMPION: JOE PETROW^H^H^H^H^H^H^H^H^H^H MARK
DESTRUCTO!!!"] FISH: Even in Petrow country, Mark Destructo has his fans! SCOOP: Mark Destructo methodically making his way past the ladder on his way to face [The crowd roars, as a man from the back rushes out with a steel chair.] FISH: Oh my God! SCOOP: It can't be! [The man cracks Thomas Jones III over the head with the chair, laying him out cold, then rushes the ring.]
SCOOP: He's heading to the ring! FISH: JESUS CHRIST, IT'S CHRIS TAYLOR! [Chris Taylor is dressed in boots, blue jeans, and a t-shirt half ripped off of his body. He has a large Band Aid on his head, a swollen nose, and a couple of bruises on the left side of his face]
SCOOP: CHRIS TAYLOR IS HERE! AND HE'S GOING NUTS ON THE HEARTBREAK KID! FISH: I don't know what happened, but it looks like he's already seen some action tonight!
SCOOP: I knew there had to be a reason Chris Taylor wasn't here, and the reason is written all over him!
FISH: Don't start jumping to conclusions now! SCOOP: Right now Petrow is being hammered from pillar to post! From the looks of things I'd say Petrow had something to do with this!
FISH: Yeah, yeah, The Heartbreak Kid is the source of all evil in this world! SCOOP: Meanwhile, Mark Destructo is looking over his manager, who appears to be out cold! The medics are coming over to take a look...
FISH: Aw no! "Hurt Stuff" grabbed Petrow by the hair and sent him out
HARD over the top rope! And Taylor's right back on him again!
SCOOP: Petrow tried to run for cover, but Taylor's got him again, and smashes his head into the guard rail! He's gonna
FISH: HERE COMES DESTRUCTO! [The crowd roars as Destructo rushes in and nails both men] SCOOP: MARK DESTRUCTO WITH AN INCREDIBLE RUNNING DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE THAT FLIPPED BOTH MEN OVER! FISH: Destructo connecting with a series of double axe-handles to the back and neck of Chris Taylor! Don't think he's just gonna let that little incident with his manager slip by!
SCOOP: Thomas Jones III has been taken into the back for medical attention, we'll try and keep the fans updated on his condition.
Meanwhile, Petrow is back on his feet, and rakes the eyes of Mark
Destructo from behind! FISH: So we've got a three-man ladder match now!? SCOOP: So it would appear to be! There are no official titles on the line in this match so that shouldn't be a problem. Petrow now in control of Chris Taylor, grabs him by the hair, and with a running start
LAUNCHES TAYLOR INTO THE LADDER! FISH: Taylor's down on the ladder, and HBK makes himself an idiot sandwich! SCOOP: Petrow with a big legdrop to the back of Taylor's neck, but that seemed to hurt Petrow a bit as well!
FISH: But here comes Destructo, going after Taylor once again! SCOOP: So far this has been more of a handicap match than a true three way! FISH: Hey, if Taylor had showed up on time, or better still not showed up at all, he wouldn't be having this problem!
SCOOP: I don't think it was Chris Taylor's fault for not being here on time, and there's no way he would ever give up a chance to get some payback on Joe Petrow, even if it means taking on Mark Destructo as well!
FISH: Well he may be rethinking that strategy right now, as Mark Destructo has him pressed high in the air like a barbell! Or should I say, like a dumbell? HAHAHAHAA!
SCOOP: You're a riot, Fish! Indeed Destructo is holding him up in the air like a trophy, and walking him back to the ring!
FISH: But Petrow's up! And he's got the ladder! He's running with the ladder? What the...
[Huge crowd pop as Petrow lands his unorthadox manuever!] SCOOP: My God, I've never seen anything like that! Petrow planted the ladder like a pole vault, ran up the ladder and dove at Mark
Destructo! A lucky break for Chris Taylor, as he actually got knocked into the ring! FISH: Petrow scurries up the ropes like a chipmunk as Mark gets to his feet [collective "oohs!" and "aahs!" from the crowd] SCOOP: MOONSAULT TO THE FLOOR ON DESTRUCTO! FISH: Who's he think he is, the 1-2-suicidal-3 Kid??? SCOOP: Petrow up on the apron, running all the way around the ring to the delight of this capacity crowd!
FISH: Truly the greatest showman of our time! Hey, he's coming over here! Petrow, you're back all the way, man! You're the greatest! SCOOP: Fish having a rather sugary conversation with The Heartbreak Kid, preening on the apron right in front of us! Guys, I hate to interrupt your lovefest, but Chris Taylor is on his feet and right behind..SUPERKICK!
FISH: AAAAHHHHHHH!!! [Chris Taylor's superkick to the back of Joe Petrow's head sends him flying off the table, crashing through the announcers table, and into
Fish. Scoop heads for cover, as Mark Destructo heads back into the ring to do battle with Taylor. Eventually, Scoop returns to his unbroken portion of the table]
SCOOP: EDDIE!? Fans, I'm sorry, but Joe Petrow came crashing into my broadcast partner, and apparently both of them are out! Taylor and Destructo are fighting in the ring now, and I sure as hell hope they keep it that way! Hey, we need some help out here!
SCOOP: Well, the show must go on. Chris Taylor with a standing side headlock, trying to slow down the breakneck pace of this match up to this point, if this even is a match! Destructo sends Taylor into the ropes, Taylor ducks the clothesline, but he couldn't get out of the way of a solid knee to the gut.
SCOOP: Petrow is starting to stir here beside me, but I don't think he's in any hurry to rejoin the action. Destructo picks up Taylor once again in a military press, and this time succeeds in slamming him to the mat! I think..wait a minute..Petrow, what in the world are you doing?
SCOOP: Joe Petrow is taking the headset off of the unconscious body of my broadcast partner and..hey, watch it!
PETROW:Is this on? SCOOP: WHAT ARE YOU *DO*ING? PETROW:Hey, who just hit me with that shot? SCOOP: That was Chris Taylor. You should have been paying attention! PETROW:Taylor, huh. Damn, that was a good shot! Take a good look Scoop, you see what's going on there?
[Petrow points to the ring, as Destructo was setting Taylor up for a piledriver, but Taylor reverses it into a backdrop.]
PETROW:You see, myself included, we're three guys who are beating the living shit out of each other, for no better reason than the spirit of competition!
SCOOP: It seems like you and Chris Taylor have something else going on! PETROW:HEY, I NEVER TOUCHED TAYLOR BEFORE THIS MATCH! SCOOP: I never said you did! PETROW:Good! Because I didn't, and that's the truth! Whatever problems he had today, and whatever reason he wants to take them out on me right now I really don't care! I rebuilt the RSPCW for one reason and one reason only: to cut through the bullshit and get back to what's great about this sport! Yeah, I had to twist a few people's arms to get things going, but what we've got right now [points to ring, as Chris Taylor is working over Destructo's leg] is the end result! The RSPCW is here, and here to stay! And if you ain't willing to fight, the door's open, you can just get the hell out!
That includes you Chris Thomas! If you don't think you can cut it here, drop the belt off at the office tomorrow morning! But if any of you think you've got the balls to take me and these guys out, you know where to find me brother! Now if you'll excuse me, it's payback time!
[Petrow throws down the headset, and heads out. Meanwhile, Chris Taylor has tried for a Figure Five leglock, but Mark Destructo grabs Taylor by the throat, and begins to work his way to his feet.]
SCOOP: Comments from the demented RSPCW Commissioner and competitor Joe
Petrow. Mark Destructo escaped that figure five with a handful of neck, and he's getting ready for one of his vicious chokesl..
[A load groan emminates from the crowd.] SCOOP: Petrow makes his prescence known with a low blow to Mark Destructo! Petrow turns to Taylor, and makes a sign! _BOTH_ men have got Mark
Destructo by the throat, and they have *picked* him up and executed a double chokeslam! Now Petrow turns around rakes Taylor in the eyes!
SCOOP: The medics have *finally* made their way out here with the stretcher to attend to my fallen partner! Meanwhile, Petrow makes his way outside the ring and grabs the ladder, and slides it into the ring!
I don't know how he expects to win the match now with both men still in the ring and very much in this match! Petrow back in the ring and...he's gonna use it as a weapon!
SCOOP: Petrow has that ladder with both hands over his head, coming at Chris Taylor! But Taylor blocks it! Both men struggling with the ladder, as Mark Destructo is back on his feet! And Destructo is back off his feet, as both men just clobbered him with the ladder!
SCOOP: This time Taylor breaks up the team, using the diversion to kick
Petrow in the ribs! A second time! Petrow lets go, and Taylor
_flattens_ Petrow with a ladder shot! [cut to a shot of the broadcast table] SCOOP: And yes, the Fish has been loaded on the gurney to finally get some medical attention!
[back to the ring] SCOOP: Petrow trying to get back to his feet, but Taylor butts him with the ladder, sending the Heartbreak Kid sprawling to the ropes. The medics are finally taking Fish out of here, and as with Thomas Jones all of us here in the RSPCW hope that this is not a serious injury.
SCOOP: Chris Taylor runs towards Petrow for a clothesline, but Petrow pulls the rope, and TAYLOR FALLS OUT OF THE RING ON TOP OF FISH ON
THE GURNEY! PETROW WITH A PLANCHA, BUT TAYLOR MOVES, AND *HE*
FLATTENS THE FISH AGAIN! Fans, the Fish can be an idiot sometimes, and he never picks up a check, but *nobody* deserves this kind of treatment!
SCOOP: But back to the action, Taylor drags Petrow away from the carnage, and he...no...YES! Taylor has the figure-five leglock slapped on outside the ring! Petrow screaming in pain, as there are no ropes to grab, and no escape in sight!
SCOOP: But we've forgotten all about Mark Destructo! He's back on his feet and he has the ladder set up! Mark Destructo is climbing the ladder, and he's going to win this match!
SCOOP: Chris Taylor has no choice but to release the hold and go after Destructo! Destructo is already two steps up, and very close to that belt! But here comes Taylor racing into the ring!
SCOOP: Taylor is climbing the other end of the ladder! Mark Destructo reaches for the belt, but Chris Taylor slaps it away! Taylor and
Destructo now duking it out on top of the ladder, while outside the ring Petrow is limping over to the timekeepers table!
SCOOP: Taylor ducks a huge haymaker by Destructo, grabs him by the head and *rams* him face first into that top step. Taylor's climbing high, but he's not going for the belt! Taylor...SUNSET FLIP OFF
OF THE LADDER! TAYLOR HIT THAT MOVE LIKE A SUPER POWERBOMB, AND
MARK DESTRUCTO ROLLS OUT OF THE RING AND LIES MOTIONLESS ON THE
FLOOR! SCOOP: But that took a toll on Chris Taylor as well! Meanwhile, the Fish has finally been carted out of here, and Petrow has evicted everyone from the timekeeper's table, and has set up outside the ring by
Destructo! SCOOP: Chris Taylor is on his feet, but leaning heavily on the ropes for support! Petrow is up on the apron, he's got Taylor's arms from behind, and he ties Taylor up in the ropes! Chris Taylor is struggling, but he is virtually immobile!
SCOOP: Mark Destructo starting to stagger to his feet outside the ring by the table. Petrow is back on the ring apron, and...he's raising his arms in the air!
[the crowd cheers in anticipation] SCOOP: Petrow jumps off and onto Mark Destructo's back! He's riding him piggyback, and he's locked on a sleeper hold! Destructo trying desperately to shrug off the Heartbreak Kid, but he's got his legs locked around that tree-trunk upper body of Destructo, hanging on for dear life and probably cutting his oxygen supply further!
SCOOP: OHHH! Destructo rams Petrow backward hard into the steel post... but still Petrow holds on! Destructo now staggering towards the table...I don't know what he...HE'S DOWN! DESTRUCTO DOWN ON ONE
KNEE! SCOOP: Petrow releases the leg scissors and manuevers Destructo face down on the table! Destructo, still trying to fight it, but he's fading fast...HE'S OUT! Joe Petrow's sleeper hold has rendered the giant
Mark Destructo unconscious! Petrow now struggling with Destructo, pulling him further onto the table!
SCOOP: Fans, I don't know what Petrow has in mind here, but right now Mark Destructo is lying face down on the timekeepers table, and Chris Taylor is still unable to free himself from the ropes! It seems that Joe Petrow has a clear path to climb the ladder now, and as he goes back into the ring it appears that that is what he's going to do!
SCOOP: Petrow is back in the ring, and has the ladder set up to go for the belt! He looks like he's going up...but he stops! The
Heartbreak Kid is pointing at Chris Taylor, and the crowd is on fire! Petrow now walks away from the ladder, and goes to the trapped Chris Taylor!
SCOOP: Joe Petrow with a series of hard right hands to that injured face of Hot Stuff, and he is at whatever mercy that Petrow has! Petrow backs up, and starts to stamp his foot!
SCOOP: The crowd getting into it now, this noise in the arena is deafening! Petrow winds up, and delivers a brutal superkick to the jaw of Taylor! OH NO! Chris Taylor had nowhere to fall, and he took the full brunt of that blow! Taylor is now bleeding from the mouth, and he is dazed, if not unconscious himself!
SCOOP: Petrow walks towards the ladder again, and this time for sure he... NO, PETROW AGAIN POINTS TOWARDS TAYLOR! What more can he possibly do to this man!? Now he points towards Destructo as well! What is going on?

Taylor and lays him down over the top rope, and goes outside the ring onto the apron. Hey now, wait a minute...Petrow points towards Destructo, still laying on the table outside the ring and now he...IS HE GONNA SUPLEX TAYLOR ONTO DESTRUCTO AND THE
