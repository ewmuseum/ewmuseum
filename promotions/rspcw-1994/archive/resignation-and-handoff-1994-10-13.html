I Resign as President of the RSPCW

13 – 16 October 1994
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You can all breathe a collective sigh of relief, because I have decided to resign as president of the RSPCW and even more importantly, this is the last article I will EVER be posting to rec.sport.pro-wrestling. I am somewhat disappointed in the personal attacks leveled against me by perfect strangers over the last few weeks, particularly the unsolicited and base name calling some of you have stooped to on here. It almost has taken the fun out of doing the
RSPCW for me. The RSPCW will be going to e-mail effective immediately and
[name withheld] will be taking over the responsibility of mailing to all the competitors of the RSPCW. I still will be doing the bulk of the work for the
RSPCW, however I feel it inappropiate for me to retain the title of president since the RSPCW is going to be an "e-mail" federation now. To all you of you on rspw I can only say, I think some of you take "professional" wrestling too seriously, perhaps you ought to look into some REAL sports, like football, basketball and hockey where the action isn't fixed and an illusion. Any of you
"missing" my writing can look for me on rec.sport.basketball.pro where I will continue to add my keen insights on the New York Knicks every week, also

rec.sport.baseball,
rec.sport.football.pro
, and rec.sport.hockey. I can't
promise you people that you won't be seeing my name around again since I am currently writing a book on the history of "professional" wrestling, _The
History of Professional Wrestling in America_, and I am going to be holding out for the possibility of starting a new wrestling organization if one or both of the big two, WCW or WWF, takes a deep plunge by the end of the decade. Until then, remember "the only REAL 'professional' wrestling is in the fantasies of those who watch it."
In boycott of the dismal "sport", [name withheld] (A.K.A. The man the rspw loves to hate) ---------------------------------------- [A companion post, three days later] ---------------------------------------- In article <37qc9r$ [detail withheld] > [detail withheld] (Brad Williams) writes: > >If this is indeed the "last" post, will you be posting under a mask >and not actually leave the federation? > >Brad No. I, the new Chief of Broadcasting for RSPCW, will (for now) make one SHORT post each week summarizing the current events in RSPCW. The "full broadcasts"
will go out by e-mail to the competitors and interested fans (who need only e-mail to me to get on that list). The reason for the weekly posts is for the benefit of newcomers to RSPW who might be interested in following or joining RSPCW.
Jason will remain as Commissioner and Head Booker for RSPCW because WE the
RSPCW competitors appreciate his imagination.
