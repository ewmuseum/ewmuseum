Reyna Upsets Hamada for the Japan Title

11 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera pans down on the new RSPCW set, with theme music that's sort of a combination of an Alannis Morrisette song and the ESPN Sportscenter theme. Fade to a front shot of new RSPCW special reporter Dan Eastman on the set.]
DAN: Ladies and gentlemen, we have some incredible late breaking news from the recently completed supercard held just hours ago at the Tokyo Egg
Dome. The main event was supposed to be a unification match between
RSPCW Japan Champion Kenji Hamada, and FJRSP Champion Shuichiro
Nakamura. However, just days before the big match, Nakamura pulled out of the match with an aggrevated groin injury, the nature of which is best left unsaid. But the RSPCW representive said to find him a worthy opponent, and he would still defend his title that night.
[Cut to a wide angle shot of a capacity crowd at the Egg Dome, and highlights from the match. The Japanese commentary is heard very softly in the background.]
DAN: Hamada entered the ring, not even knowing who he would be facing, but assumed it would be a top contender in Japanese wrestling. So Hamada was stunned to see an American walk the aisle towards the ring, and nearly everyone in the crowd was shocked to hear the name of "Maverick"
Michael Reyna. Reyna had seen some tag team action in Japan with his partner Desirable Danny Dynamite, but nobody had every seen him wrestle in a heavyweight singles title match before. Nobody figured he would be a match for the Shootmaster.
DAN: And indeed, although Reyna got his licks in from time to time, it was nearly all Hamada, putting on a devastating display of kicks and chops to Reyna, who had fought a grueling tag match in America barely
24 hours before. When Hamada signaled for the lariat, everyone assumed that Hamada was on his way to another swift defense of his title.
DAN: But somehow, Michael Reyna found the strength and the speed to avoid the lariat, and latched on to this sleeper hold. Hamada fought desperately to free himself with a series of elbows and grabbed for the ropes. But Reyna had the eye of the tiger, and the grip of the python, and refused to release the hold. The crowd refused to believe what it was seeing as Hamada slumped to the canvas, but as the arm was raised and dropped for the third time, the bell sounded, and
Michael Reyna celebrated the greatest upset in RSPCW history, becoming the *NEW* RSPCW Japan Champion! After the match, the new champion had these words for our Japan correspondant Takao Inoue:
[A bloody Michael Reyna, with tears in his eyes, holds the RSPCW Japan Heavyweight Championship belt over his right shoulder.] REYNA: My God, this is unbelievable! Danny, I guess you know what my "personal business" was now! It was only about a day or so ago when I was licking my wounds in the locker room with you after our defeat, when I got a call from my agent. He told me that if I could get my sorry ass on a plane ASAP, I could be in the main event on a Japanese supercard wrestling for the RSPCW Japan
Championship! I was hurting, and I wasn't gonna do it, until I thought a little bit about what's been going on lately.
I thought about all those matches you had in that e-wrestling tournament, the FWLI World Title tournament, the shots at the
RSPCW World title on the In Your Face tour. And I just watched you and cheered you on. But what really got to me, was our tag match the other night. When we got to the ring, what were the words displayed on those big lights? "The Players Club?" No!
"Desirable Danny Dynamite!" I thought back on that, and I realized something. No matter how you look at it, I'm living in the shadow of Danny Dynamite!
So I got on that plane, without telling anyone, and came here tonight! And 63,000 pairs of eyes were on me! And I was tired, I was in pain,
I was jet-lagged, and yeah, I was in there with a great champion, the man who defeated Chris Taylor for the belt! But he gave me that one little opening, and when I got that sleeper hold on, and I held on to that hold for dear life. And after all that I had to go through to be here, <pats the belt> I won my first major heavyweight singles title!
Danny Dynamite, nothing's changed between you and me. We're still partners, still friends to the end, and we're still gonna take those
RSPCW World Tag Team Titles! But now you have to realize, that *I'm* the one packing the gold now, and it's *my* name that's going to be in lights now! And NOBODY...nobody, is gonna take this away from me.
DAN: A very excited Michael Reyna celebrates the moment, but once the euphoria wears off, there will be a lot that Mr. Reyna will have to consider. The RSPCW Japan champion has a lot of responsibilities in
Japan, and it's possible that the title defenses he will have to make may conflict with his ability to participate in the tag tournament match at Glory Days, and Michael Reyna may be faced with some very difficult decisions in the near future. Only time will tell how this situation will be resolved. For our entire staff, this is Dan Eastman with your special RSPCW report.
