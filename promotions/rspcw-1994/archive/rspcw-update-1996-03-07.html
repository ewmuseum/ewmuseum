RSPCW Update, 7 March 1996

7 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The RSPCW theme music plays in the background, as the camera pans to SCOOP sitting at the control desk.] SCOOP: From the wrestling group facing more legal action than the World
Whining Federation, this is your RSPCW Update for March 7th, 1996!
History was made last Monday night in San Francisco, as "Hitman" Chris Thomas became the 8th RSPCW World Heavyweight Champion, winning the Octagon Survivor Match with a final pinfall.
[Cut to the scene where Chris Taylor attempts to tag Joe Petrow. The footage changes to match the situation being described.]
SCOOP: However, the match was not without controversy, as late in the match the Heartbreak Kid Joe Petrow, who had taken several beatings outside the ring before, inexplicibly refused the tag from Chris Taylor, getting himself disqualified and setting Taylor up for the final pinning manuever!
SCOOP: But Petrow was not satisfied with merely causing Hot Stuff to lose the RSPCW World Title, as he came back to the ring and displayed his new devastating finishing move, The Bullet Plane to Hell!
SCOOP: The conspiracy began to unfold when Petrow and Chris Thomas teamed up to administer more punishment to the former champ! Help was on the way when NUKE came into the ring with a baseball bat to clear the ring and save the fallen Taylor. Or so we thought!
SCOOP: With one brutal thrust NUKE made his true intentions known, and with this ferocious manuever dubbed the "Atomic Surfboard", the unholy alliance of the Heart Attack was reborn!
SCOOP: After Petrow spoke of leaving singles competition to concentrate on tag-team mayhem, NUKE grabbed the mike and made these comments:
NUKE: I STARTED THINKING ABOUT HOW, NO LESS THAN TWENTY FOUR HOURS AFTER PETROW LEFT, I WAS UP IN FWLI TOWERS, WITH ALL THE SHIRTS
TELLING ME, "YOU GOTTA SMILE A LITTLE MORE NUKE!" "YOU GOT TO BE A
LITTLE MORE FRIENDLY WITH THE FANS!" LIKE A _FOOL_ I DID EVERYTHING
THEY ASKED ME, AND WHAT DID IT GET ME? I'VE GOT NO TITLES! I'VE GOT
NO FRIENDS! AND I'VE GOT NO RESPECT! AND THEN I THOUGHT ABOUT ALL
THE GOOD TIMES I HAD WITH THIS MAN! [Petrow pats him on the back in approval] I THOUGHT ABOUT ALL CARNAGE WE LEFT IN OUR WAKE TOGETHER!
AND I THOUGHT ABOUT WHAT A DAMN GOOD FRIEND THIS GUY WAS TO ME! SO
"FLAGBOY", I GOT ONE THING TO SAY TO YOU: "I'M BAAAAACK!"
SCOOP: The party continued with new Heart Attack member Chris Thomas extolling his greatest when Chris Taylor finally recovered, came into the ring, wrestled the bat away from NUKE and caught Petrow with a shot to the head, and cleared the ring of the Heart Attack!
SCOOP: The fired up ex-champ got on the mike and made the following challenge: TAYLOR:PETRRROOWWWW! God damn you, you son of a bitch! The only thing you know how to do is run away from me! The only reason I'm not the champ right now is because you ran away from me again! Well it's going to stop dammit! I know you have the power, I want a match with you next week! I don't care what kind of match it is,
I just want your ass in a body cast! I don't care what your
"agenda" is now, you either make the match, or I swear to GOD you're a dead man! SCOOP: The RSPCW fans have been wondering ever since whether or not The
Heartbreak Kid would accept the challenge of "Hot Stuff" Chris
Taylor, and if he did, just what kind of match it would be! Here no, is Petrow's reply! [Cut to a plain grey room, with tarps hanging and laid out everywhere. The segment is done using the MTV Cam, a black and white camera that films at
15 frames a second and is constantly moving all over the place.]
PETROW:You know Taylor, I don't like being threatened like that on national television, but I sure as hell don't like being attacked with a baseball bat! You don't want to KNOW what I did to the last guy that tried that on me! But just as I was about to brush you off and get on with my life, I remembered that you do have one thing that I want! I gave you something several months ago that was very important to me, the *REAL* World's Heavyweight Title Belt! So
I'll tell you what, you bring that belt with you to Monday Night
WAR next week, and we'll have ourselves a good old fashioned ladder match! Bottom line, the man standing in the ring with the belt is the winner, and you'll have the honor of being my last and greatest conquest! If you got the balls to back up your mouth
Hot Stuff, let's get it on! SCOOP: The gauntlet his been picked up and thrown right back in the face of Chris Taylor. And on top of that, it was just announced that next week's card will be from Crisler Arena in Ann Arbor,
Michigan! Petrow obviously went to great lengths to return the favor for Taylor's epic victory at *his* home university last year, and one has to wonder if Chris Taylor is willing to look past a hostile crowd to accept the challenge next week!
SCOOP: But there are two matches that definitely WILL be on the card next week! The RSPCW World Tag Team Title tournament kicks off when the Players Club sends "Desirable" Danny Dynamite and Michael "The
"Maverick" Reyna against Johnny Missle and The Flying Red Dragon!
And "Executioner" Mark Engel gets his shot at the...what's this... fans, I have just been handed a bulletin that I have been asked to read by RSPCW Commissioner Joe Petrow: "There are unconfirmed reports that a coalition of fantasy wrestling federations are taking steps to insure that their wrestlers will not participate in the
RSPCW. There are reportedly threats of fines, suspensions, and lawsuits for breach of contract, and Chris Thomas has also apparently been asked to surrender his world heavyweight championship. Despite these reports the RSPCW intends to continue the promotion and have the next Monday Night WAR card as scheduled."
SCOOP: <deep sigh> Well, I guess that ends our report for this week, as we are not sure if any of these matches will be taking place this Monday.
But we have the time slot, so if nothing other than to watch The
Heartbreak Kid dance for an hour we will be coming to you again LIVE this Monday night! For the RSPCW Control Center, this is SCOOP signing off!
[The ending music is played, as a perplexed SCOOP is discussing the matter with his producers.]
Joe Petrow RSPCW Interim Commissioner [detail withheld]
