MegaWrestleFest

10 April 1994 — Madison Square Garden, New York, NY
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to Madison Square Garden in New York City for the first big spectacular card in RSPCW history!!! Here are the summmary and results of all thirteen matches that took place at this MEGAEVENT yestday at 1:00PM in a standing room only Paramount in MSG!!!!!
Taped interviews with four competitors in Iron Man Tournament, they took place one hour before the card: -"The Sadistic Swissman" Daniel Amsler said "Ain't no doctor or quack gonna stop me from wrestling today in the Iron Man I am gonna destroy all three of my opponents tonight so that there won't be anyone left to wrestle in a final match against me for the belt and you can count on it!!!"
- "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor said "Oh boy the butterflies are going crazy in my stomach cause today is the biggest opportunity of my career so far. Today when the final match is over and the hand of the 1st RSPCW Heavyweight Champion is raised, I believe in my heart that man will be me."
- Steve 'Intensity' Newhook said "Look here all I have to say is that I am going to beat the other three losers today and walk out the RSPCW World Champion!!! Can't you see I'm preparing for my match take your camera and mike and get outta here right now!!!"
-"The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow said "I'm gonna repeat what I've said before I will do anything and I mean ANYTHING today in the Iron Man Tournament to get my hands on the gold cause there is no doubt in my mind I am walking out of
Madison Square Garden today the 1ST RSPCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!!!!!" 1ST MATCH- FOR THE RSPCW TV TITLE (Most falls in 30 min time-limit) "Dr. Destruction" Don Martin vs. 'Dashin' [name withheld] w/valet Cheryl
The match started off tentative as both men used stalling tactics, when a masked man appeared at ringside and scooped up Cheryl and dragged her back to the lockeroom.
Dave started to run down the runway after the man only to be hit from behind by Dr Destruction with a clothesline then put into a devastating gorilla press slam on the concrete floor. The Dr. then carried Dave, on his shoulders, back to the ring and placed him on the top turnbuckle. Dr. signaled to the crowd he was going to go for a superplex off the top turnbuckle and as he cinched it in he brought Dave high into the air and they landed hard into the mat. Dr. then used the momentum to put Dave into a pinning position and went for the pin 1,2....Dave grabbed Dr. Destruction's head and rolled him into a small package 1,2,3 and Dave won one fall.
Dr. Destruction was furious and hit Dave with a barrage of clotheslines and bodyslams and used his
'Destructor Ice Clamp' (chicken wing powerbomb into a press position) to win his first pinfall. With fifteen minutes lest, both competitors began to heat it up with high impact maneuvers including 'Dashin' Dave using his patented but never duplicated 'Dash Smash' (2 1/2 moonsault splash from the top turnbuckle) onto Dr. Destruction outside the ring.
But Dr. Destruction retaliated with a vicious suplex of Dave onto the ring barricade. Dr. Destruction then got back into the ring as the referee grabbed him by the shirt and reprimanded him.
The referee didn't see that behind his back in the first row was Jeff 'Comet' Halley who jumped over the steel barricade and gave 'Dashin' Dave his infamous Astral Blast
[after being put into a backbreaker position, spun around and sent flying up into the air (hitting the concrete floor in this case), opponent is hit with a double knee drop (from the ring apron in this case) from the top turnbuckle]
outside the ring. 'Dashin' Dave nearly knocked out cold was pulled into the ring by Dr. Destruction and rolled up for an easy pin.
With one minute left, a semi-conscious Dave never got up to mount any more significant offense as the time keeper rang the bell signaling the end of the match.
Winner: Dr. Destruction won 2 falls to 1 fall The new RSPCW TV Champion is "Dr. Destruction" Don Martin!!! Jeff 'Comet' Halley got into the ring to celebrate with the new RSPCW TV Champion as they headed back to the interview area.
Time of bout: 30:00 After match interview with RSPCW TV Champion Dr. Destruction who said "I don't wanna talk about the disappearance of that bimbo Cheryl. All I have to say is that I will annihilate anyone who dares to try to take this title away from me in the 20 minutes needed to do it."*BOOS* Jeff 'Comet' Halley interjected "The chickens are coming home to roost today at
Megawrestlefest!!!! COCK-A-DOODLE DOO!!! *BOOS* Let's get ready to celebrate Dr. Destruction cause were blasting off tonight!!!!!" *DEAFENING BOOS*
2ND MATCH-IRON MAN TOURNAMENT (1ST ROUND 15 MIN TIME-LIMIT) Steve 'Intensity' Newhook vs. "The Sadistic Swissman" Daniel Amsler With the vengefulness of an executioner, the Sadistic Swissman systematically went after the back of Intensity. Sadistic used elbows, knee drops, kicks, various bodyslams and five consecutive backbreakers to put Steve into a state of writhing agony. The Sadistic Swissman finally put Intensity into his "Spinning Torture Rack"
which caused the referee to call for the bell not waiting for Steve to submit on his own, so that no career ending injury would result from it.
Winner: The Sadistic Swissman with his "Spinning Torture Rack" which the referee called for the bell not waiting for Steve to submit. Time of bout: 9:21 3RD MATCH- IRON MAN TOURNAMENT (1ST ROUND) "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor vs. "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow The Heartbreak Kid apparently still brewing from his pinfall loss at the last show attacked Chris from behind with a metal chair, as Hot Stuff was making his way to the ring. Security had to come out and wrestle the chair from
Heartbreak before the match could even start. When they finally did, Heartbreak brought a wobbly Hot Stuff inside the ring where he went after his right knee with the intent of tearing it out of the joint. Hot Stuff given no fair opportunity to mount any offense appeared to be out of this match early. The Heartbreak Kid realizing time was running out climbed to the top turnbuckle to give Chris a "Heart
Attack" (moonsault drop kick to the solar plexus) and when
Chris got to his feet Heartbreak went for it....but Chris somehow managed to duck under it. With little energy left,
Chris waited for Heartbreak to get up near the corner then when he was fully upright Chris went for a 'Victory Roll'
and used a handful of tights to get the pinfall before a stunned Garden crowd. The Heartbreak Kid was enraged at this turn of events and took the referee and threw him over the top rop and ran down the runway after Hot Stuff, who had quickly left the ring area after his victory.
Winner: Hot Stuff with a 'Victory Roll' and a handful of tights shocked the Garden crowd. Time of bout: 14:59 Interview with The American Dream Team Mark 'Samson' Engel said "Today here in Madison Square Garden, me and my partner
Jason "Manifest Destiny" Clark, we will fulfill our "American
Dream" which is to be the 1ST RSPCW WORLD TAG CHAMPIONS!!!"
* MEGA POP* Jason "Manifest Destiny" Clark said "The only justice that will take place today in our match will be issued by
The American Dream Team. *POP* When all is said and done and the smoke clears later on I promise to my partner and you fans out there that The American Dream Team will be bringing home the gold." *MEGA POP*
4TH MATCH- SPECIAL FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH "Abdullah the" [name withheld] vs. TBA at match time "Abdullah the" [name withheld] is announced first and ran down to the ring mike and demanded "Whoever you are you come to the ring now and face your destiny of despair!!!
HAHAHAHA!!!!" There was a stir in the crowd behind Abdullah when...OH MY GOD!!! One of the biggest human beings lept forward from the crowd, over the steel barricade, and at what appeared to be 7' 4" maybe 500 lbs approached ringside in African dress. He stepped over the top rope, tapped
Abdullah from behind and grabbed the mike and he said "My name is The Masai Giant, from Nairobi, Kenya and I am going to treat YOU, Abdullah, to the Deep Darkness of Africa, my homeland." *HUGE POP* He then threw Abdullah against the ropes and with one hand lifted him ten feet into the air by the throat and held him up for what seemed an eternity. He then put Abdullah into a front facelock and gave him the most thunderous neckbreaker (The Nairobi Neckbreaker) and daintily put one foot voer him as the referee counted the three count. He then left the ring giving hi-five to fans as he made his way back to the lockerroom with Abdullah bewildered in the ring.
Winner: The unknown opponent, newcomer The Masai Giant, in quick fashion in his debut match Time of bout: 3:15 5TH MATCH -"BEST BODY IN THE RSPCW" MATCH 2 OF 3 FALLS 30 MIN TIME-LIMIT "The Body" [name withheld] vs. "The Bod" [name withheld] In a much anticipated matchup, both wrestlers put on a wrestling clinic. It seemed like each wrestler had a countermove for the other wrestler's move. Twenty minutes into the bout neither man had scored a pinfall, although there had been fifteen close ones. Both wrestlers frustrated by the match so far, went outside the ring, on opposite sides and stood staring across at each other. The timekeeper rang the bell as time had expired in the match and the referee declared the match a draw.
In a strange twist of events, "The Body" [name withheld] grabbed the ring miked and asked The Bod to come over to his side of the ring cause he wanted to talk to him. With the crowd clamoring to find out what was going on, both men smiles on their faces, The Body said " I have an announcement to make. Me and The Bod here realized that sometimes the sum of the whole is greater than the sum of the parts. We today in Madison Square Garden have gained tremendous respect for one another's ability. We have come to the conclusion that we have perfectly compatible ring skills and wrestling ability so much so that we are forming a new tag team *HUGE POP* called 'The Total Package'. We will use our perfect bodies together to capture the gold from whoever are the tag champions after Megawrestlefest."
*POP* They raised each other's arms and proclaimed one another "BEST BODY IN THE RSPCW". *HUGE POP* Winner: A time-limit draw as neither man scored a pinfall in a display of wrestling at its best. They decided to align forces after the match and go for the tag belts.
Time of bout: 30:00 1st Intermission next matches: 2nd round of Iron Man Insane Asylum 3rd Round of Iron Man Corporal Punishment [continued, part 2 of 3] End of 1st Intermission Interview with The Masai Giant who said "To all the fans out here today in the mecca of professional sports, Madison Square Garden,
I am here for one purpose in the RSPCW and that is to rid the world of wrestling of egomaniacs and twisted minds such as
Abdullah, The Sadistic Swissman, and Law and Order. *HUGE POP* I want to restore wrestling to it's glory days which i fell in love with as a teenager in Nairobi, Kenya watching old American wrestling shows with such greats as Bob Backlund, Lou Thesz, and Bruno Sammartino. With the support of you fans and the other wrestlers here who believe as I do, we will succeed in bringing wrestling back to what it once was." *MEGA POP*
6TH MATCH-IRON MAN TOURNAMENT (2ND ROUND) Steve 'Intensity' Newhook vs. "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow Steve 'Intensity' Newhook, the valiant wrestler he was this day, came to the ring with his backed heavily taped after suffering the vicious onslaught of The Sadistic Swissman and he was ready to wrestle. The Heartbreak Kid nearly furious form his earlier lost was teased by Steve who would catch him off guard with a slap, drop kick, or punch to the face then run outside the ring. Heartbreak finally reached his boiling point and ran outside the ring after Steve after Steve spit in his face.
Steve ran around the ring twice not knowing Heartbreak had stopped chasing him and prepared to ambush him. When Steve came around to where Heartbreak was he was grabbed and thrown into the steel barricade back first. Heartbreak then rammed his back against the ring apron numerous times. Bringing the action back into the ring, Heartbreak threw him into the corner and hit him with a clothesline that nearly took off Steve's head. With three minutes remaining, Heartbreak decided to put Steve into an abdominal stretch and when the ref was asking Steve if he wanted to quit, Heartbreak grabbed the top rope for more leverage.
Steve in agonizing pain was able to use the strength in his loose arm to throw Heartbreak toward the opposite rope so hard that he went over the top rope face first. The referee seeing this rang the bell and ended the match on a over-the-top-rope disqualification of Intensity. Steve argued to the referee that it was momentum that took Heartbreak over but to no avail as the referee pushed him and walked away.
Winner: The Heartbreak Kid on a questionable over-the-top -rope disqualification of Steve 'Intensity' Newhook Time of bout: 13:58 7TH MATCH- IRON MAN TOURNAMENT (2ND ROUND) "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor vs. "The Sadistic Swissman" Daniel Amsler One of the quicker matches of the eveninga, the match going Sadistic Swissman's way when he mistimed in trying to hit
Hot Stuff with a cross boyd press while he was laboring on the ropes but Chris ducked out of the way and Sadistic went over the top rope to the outside of the ring. As the Swissmand slowly made his way to the ring apron Hot Stuff signaled that he was going to suplex him into the ring. He grabbed the Swissman's tighs and pulled him straight up into the air, holding him in the position.
From nowhere came the masked man again and pulled the leg of Hot Stuff, he and the Swissman fell into the ring with the Swissman having him in a pinning predicament 1,2 ........Hot Stuff reversed the position and with a handful of tights again got the three count. The Sadistic Swissman realizing what happened pummeled Chris as he tried to escape back to the lockerroom and viciously attacked him from behind picking up the steel barricade and toppling it on Hot Stuff. Security had to come to pull the
Swissman off a possibly injured Hot Stuff. Winner: Hot Stuff whens when a masked man pulled his leg while he was suplexing the Sadistic Swissman into the ring but somehow Hot Stuff manage to reverse the pinning position and used a handful of tights again to win it.
Time of bout: 12:24 Interview with Law and Order Law and Order come out with the Judge carrying his gavel and his noose, and the Bounty Hunter wearing his cowboy hat. The Judge said "Today at Megawrestlefest I am going to find the so-called
Dream Team guilty in my court of law, and I will sentence them on the spot to a deadly downfall in the ring with us...HAHAHAHA!!!!"
The Bounty Hunter joining in said "We are going to bring those no-good opponents of our's to face justice." The Judge then said "As far as you go Masai Giant, you must be itching for the
Hangman's Noose. If plan on sticking your nose in our business I will hold you in contempt of my court and you will face the same justice as the American Dream Team!!!! After we win the belts here today, Law and Order will forever be THE AMERICAN
NIGHTMARE!!!! HAHAHAHA!!!!!" 8TH MATCH-INSANE ASYLUM MATCH (Three against each other) "Insane in the Membrane" [name withheld] vs. [name withheld] vs. 'Maniac' [name withheld] It was a clear setup in this match, as [name withheld] and 'Maniac' [name withheld] after being in the cage for five minutes ganged up on Insane in the Membrane subjecting him to numerous bodyslams against the cage, spiked pildrivers, double
DDT's, double clothelines against the cage. The final humiliation for Brian was the two rubbing his face against the cage till it bled heavily then choking him over the head with a plastic bag filled with his own blood. Maniac and Insane then continued their cowardly deed as they rigged the cage so no one could get in to help Brian. Finally a wrestler came down, look liked 'Wild'
Will Comer, who had a tool (the jaws of life) which he used to tear the cage open as Insane and Maniac scattered away form the scene. A bloodied unconscious "Insane in the Membrane"
[name withheld] had to be carried away on a stretcher and rushed to a nearby hospital. Winner: [name withheld] and 'Maniac' [name withheld] as they ganged up on Insane in the Membrane. Time of Bout: 20:00 Unscheduled interview with 'Wild' Will Comer who said "First, I want to say that to 'Maniac' [name withheld] and [name withheld] that what you did in that cage today was despicable. If I was you guys I would pack my bags and head for the hills cause when Insane in the Membrane get his hand on you two its lights out!!! As for the new TV Champion Dr. Destruction, who couldna't win the belt without help, you think you can annihilate anyone who gets in the ring with you? Well, I think it is time that
'Wild' Will show these fans out here what I can do in the ring so at the next TV show in front of the whole world I am going to see if you can live up to what you say. My father always said I never saw a challenge I didn't like and the TV title would sure look good around my waist." *POP*
9TH MATCH- IRON MAN TOURNAMENT (3RD ROUND) Steve 'Intensity' Newhook vs. "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor In what had to be the best match of the evening, as far as pure wrestling goes, these two men put on a masterful display of the chess of wrestling. Although, there were heated exchanges and flared tempers throughout the match, it was obvious that the two men wanted very much to impress the crowd. The match ended when both men exhausted, got into a test of strength and Hot
Stuff, the cleverest of the two this day, converted it into an inescapable inverted backslide for the 1,2,3 count.
Winner: "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor in one of the best scientific matches so far in the RSPCW won with an inescapable inverted backslide and qualifies for the final of the Iron Man.
Time of bout: 13:15 10TH MATCH- IRON MAN TOURNAMENT (3RD ROUND) "The Sadistic Swissman" Daniel Amsler vs. "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow A strange match right from the start as the Sadistic Swissman showed none of the offensive ferocity he showed earlier. The Heartbreak Kid used a clothesline and a bodyslam to pin the
Swissman in record time. Both men walked back to the lockerroom side by side as if nothing took place in the ring. Everyone was confused about this match wondering if the Swissman was reinjured his neck and didn't want to shirk his obligation to wrestle tonight.
Winner: The Heartbreak Kid in one of the strange matches of the evening pins the Swissman in record time and qualifies for the final of the Iron Man.
Time of bout: 13 seconds 11TH MATCH- THE CORPORAL PUNISHMENT MATCH LOSER SAYS 'I QUIT' OR REFEREE STOPS IT "The Bandwidth Mountie" vs. "The Admiral" [name withheld] The Admiral went over well with the Garden crowd as he used his ring smarts to stay one step ahead of the Mountie for most of the match. Both men brought an arsenal of boots, clubs, sticks, and weapons to the ring. The Admiral gained an extra edge when The Mountie got caught in the ropes. The Admiral used his club, boot, and stick also slapping, punching, and kicking the Admiral and still could not get The Mountie to say 'I quit'.
The referee finally untangled a bloodied Mountie who gave the
Admiral a brutal boot below the belt and pulled out of his tights what appeared to be a stun gun. He used it numerous times on the
Admiral until the referee decided to ring the bell ending the match and security with medics came into the ring to rescue to
The Admiral and take him to the hospital.
Winner: The Mountie after rendering the Admiral unconscious with what appeared to be a stun gun and the referee using his discretion stopped the bout.
Time of bout: 19:01 2nd Intermission upcoming next matches for the RSPCW WORLD TAG TITLES RSPCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE [continued, part 3 of 3] End of 2nd Intermission 12TH MATCH OVER-THE-TOP-OF-THE-CAGE MATCH FOR THE RSPCW WORLD TAG TITLES Mark 'Samson' Engel and Jason "Manifest Destiny" Clark (The American Dream Team) vs. "The Judge" [name withheld] and "The Bounty Hunter" [name withheld] (Law and Order) A back and forth match in which the ADT held the slight edge for a while as the two members climbed the cage numerous times only to be caught in time by Law and Order from reaching the ground. The ADT were both on opposite sides of the cage seconds from the ground when tow masked men, one on each side of the cage, came from nowhere and pushed the ADT back up the cage where Law and Order brought them back inside the cage. The match was headed toward a decisive end when Jason "Manifest Destiny" Clark was clocked in the back of the knee by The Bounty Hunter with a loaded object while he was preparing with his partner Samson to do the "AmeriPress Suplex" on The Judge. Manifest fell to the mat grimacing in pain as his knee had been then buckled under the pressure of him and the Judge falling to the mat from the top rope and he could not get up from the mat.
Left for all intents and purposes to fight on his own, Mark 'Samson' Engel could not fend off the attack of Law and Order and after pummeling him for five minutes they gave him the Hangman's Noose (double team neckbreaker) with the rope they tied him and Manifest together in the ring and climbed the cage, touching the ground to become the first RSPCW
World Tag Team Champions. Law and Order went back into the cage to do more damage to the ADT but down the runway came
The Masai Giant who came in the cage and gavea both members of Law and Order his thunderous Nairobi Neckbreaker. The
Masai Giant then untied the ADT and carried Manifest back to the back in his arms because Manifest could not even walk.
NEW RSPCW TAG CHAMPIONS - LAW AND ORDER Winner: Law and Order with the help of two masked men and an injury to Manifest Destiny during the match.
The ADT was saved from further harm when The Masai Giant came to their rescue. Time of bout: 26:52 13TH MATCH- FINAL IN IRON MAN TOURNAMENT (No time-limit) FOR THE RSPCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor vs. "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow In a match that exemplified the superior conditioning of RSPCW athletes, these two fearless competitors battled back and forth for 56 minutes with neither man establishing any clear momentum in the match.
At this point a frustrated Heartbreak Kid threw Chris outside the ring through the ropes and from under the ring apron came the new tag champs who put a vicious beating on
Hot Stuff while the referee's back was turned. After mercifully being pulled back into the ring by Heartbreak
Kid, Hot Stuff was covered for an easy 1,2....Wait a second Mark 'Samson' Engel grabbed the arm of the referee, preventing the three count and tried to explain to him what had happened to Hot Stuff. But before Mark could explain
Law and Order came and attacked him from behind as Hot Stuff pushed Heartbreak into the ropes and rolled him up for a
1,2, 2 3/4....and Heartbreak kicked out. Chris then gave Heartbreak the "Hot Shot" (slingshot suplex off the top turnbuckle) and climbed up to the top rope to give
Heartbreak his "Hot Drop"(leg drop off top turnbuckle). Meanwhile, Law and Order were annihilating Samson on the outside when down the runway came The Masai Giant again and he immediately went to rescue Samson as he clotheslined both members of Law and Order.
Chris readied to do his "Hot Drop" as he jumped, he misjudged how far he had to jump, and his head hit the top turnbuckle when he jumped. The Heartbreak Kid capitalizing on this major mistake took Chris and monkey flipped him into the top turnbuckle and as he came off the turnbuckle rolled him up into a pinning position using his legs on the ropes, he got the 1,2,3 count. The 1ST RSPCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION
IS "THE HEARTBREAK KID" JOE PETROW!!! Winner: The Heartbreak Kid wins after a seesaw battle that lasted for over an hour long when
Hot Stuff misjudged his "Hot Drop". Time of bout: 1:01:07 There was more though, from under the ring apron came The Sadistic Swissman who came into the ring. Dwon the runway came the masked man who had been here earlier and he got up to the ring mike. He pulled off his mask, he was no man or wrestler, it was Cheryl the valet for 'Dashin' Dave.
What the hell was going on? She announced to the crowd "Ladies and Gentlemen, you have just witnessed the coup of the century here at Megawrestlefest today. The first and only lady of wrestling, myself, Chaotic Cheryl, you heard me right, Chaotic Cheryl has assembled the greatest entourage of wrestlers, The Defiance Alliance, this sport has ever seen. As a matter of fact, you have already met them all here today so it should come as no suprise to you as I introduce the Defiance Alliance as the following:
RSPCW World Champion "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow RSPCW World Tag Champions Law and Order RSPCW TV Champion Dr. Destruction "Future" Light Heavyweight Champion Jeff 'Comet' Halley and of course, the Defiance Alliance Enforcer himself,
The Sadistic Swissman." One by one all the members of the Defiance Alliance came into the ring by Cheryl's side, all with very estatic and devious smiles on their faces. Apparently, her plan all along was to get her hands on all the gold in the RSPCW and the Defiance Alliance accomplished that at Megawrestlefest with a clean sweep.
But as the Defiance Alliance celebrated in the ring before a shocked Garden crowd, we heard what appeared to be a big explosion in the arena and down the smoke-filled runway stood a man barely visible through the cloud of smoke. He walked through this cloud of smoke to ringside and standing tall, it was none other than NUKE. He simply stood at ringside with his eyes focused on one man, The
Heartbreak Kid. The Defiance Alliance, under order, from Cheryl cleared the ring and headed back to the lockerroom as NUKE stood standing the whole time focused on the new RSPCW World Champion.
