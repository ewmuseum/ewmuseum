Glory Days (2/2)

8 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Cut to the set of the RSPCW MegaWrestleFest Control Centre, featuring an extreme, yet tasteful decor. Dan Eastman sits behind the desk.]
EASTMAN: Good evening wrestling fans, and welcome to YOUR MegaWrestleFest III update! Well, the events of Glory Days have begun to give shape to the scheduled matches at MegaWrestleFest III, and by the end of the night all four matches will be set! The one still in question is the Main Event "Last Laugh" match for the RSPCW World Heavyweight
Championship between challenger "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor, and the champion, the winner of tonight's No Holds Barred match between
"Hitman" Chris Thomas and Mark Destructo. The very interesting rules for this match have been released, and like FISH mentioned earlier, like an Ironman Match this is guaranteed to last one full hour. But unlike an Ironman Match, in which the winner is determined by the man who wins the MOST falls in the hour, the winner of the Last Laugh match will be the person who scores the LAST fall in the one hour period! That means that even if you've been pinned 10 times earlier in the bout, if you score the very last fall, you win the match! This unique stipulation will likely lead to some very unusual strategy being employed, and it will be very interesting to see how the participants in this match can adapt to these rules.
EASTMAN: The other three matches that have been signed have now been set. In the finals of the RSPCW World Tag Team Title Tournament, the
Players Club team of "Desirable" Danny Dynamite and RSPCW Japan
Champion "Maverick" Michael Reyna, will meet the Heart Attack team of "Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petow and NUKE for the belts. It will be interesting to see how the Players Club approach this match, because later that same night, they will actually be facing EACH
OTHER for Reyna's RSPCW Japan Title! And of course, lurking in the shadows for either of these men is "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx, who earned a shot at the Japan title after MegaWrestleFest III by virtue of his victory tonight. And earlier in the program we learned of the arrival of the Iceman to the RSPCW. Indeed, he is here, he has signed The Jammer's open contract, and he will be make his RSPCW debut against him as well!
EASTMAN: These four great matches have already been signed, and we hear that more matches will soon be announced! The most spectacular wrestling event of the year will deservedly be held in the most spectacular location on earth, Aloha Stadium in Honolulu, Hawaii, on Sunday, September 29th. If you can't be there in person, by all means be sure to subscribe to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy or the RSPCW Mailing List to get in on the action for this, the great-granddaddy of all fantasy wrestling events! This has been
Dan Eastman, with YOUR RSPCW MegaWrestleFest II Update!
[cut back to SCOOP and FISH at the announcer's table] SCOOP: Indeed, MegaWrestleFest III is shaping up to become the greatest event in the history of fantasy wrestling!
FISH: [wiping his brow] I tell you, if it's anything more exciting than what we just saw in the ring, I'm not sure I'll be able to take it!
Why is it that the only thing we have to drink around here is scalding hot coffee? SCOOP: But the night's not over FISH, we still have our big main event to come. This match came about as a result of the donnybrook that took place on our Halloween Anguish card, and the time since then has only served to intensify the rivalry between these two men!
FISH: That's right, "Hitman" Chris Thomas has really come into his own since joining The Heart Attack, and he gave Mark Destructo quite a licking at Halloween Anguish, but in this kind of match, I can't think of how the much larger and more powerful Destructo can possibly lose!
SCOOP: On that note let's head back to the locker room, and the omnipresent
Dan Eastman, who's standing by with Destructo and his manager, Thomas
Jones!
[Dan Eastman is standing by with Mark Destructo and his manager, Thomas Jones. Destructo has what appears to be the FWLI Championship belt across his shoulder.] EASTMAN: Thank you SCOOP, I'm here in the locker room with Mark Destructo and his manager, Thomas J-
[Mark Destructo stops Eastman in mid-sentence and grabs the arm that's holding the microphone.]
MARK: I don't think you gave me the proper introduction, boy! That should be, "The Eternal, Undisputed FWLI World Heavyweight Champion
Mark Destructo." EASTMAN: Aw c'mon, you walked away from that belt months ago! MARK: I walked away from the FWLI, but I never walked away from this belt! I lefted that place because they couldn't offer me a challenge anymore! And after I successfully defended this belt against BOTH
Joe Petrow and Chris Taylor, the two so-called legends of our sport, AT THE SAME TIME, there's no doubt that none of the pretenders left in that federation could EVER be the champion that I am!
EASTMAN: Well whatever, we're not here to talk about the FWLI, we're here to talk about the RSPCW, and your title match with tonight with Chris
Thomas!
MARK: That little punk!? I destroyed his ass when I defended this belt against him, why should tonight be any different!? EASTMAN: Well, he is a part of the Heart Attack, and he does have Mina is his corner now? MARK: So what!? He's got the support of two other losers! And as for Mina, my manager's gonna have a field day with- [Mark's speech is interrupted, as Todd Newkirk enters the interview session with HIS FWLI belt over HIS shoulder.]
NEWKIRK: What the hell am I hearing over here? You trying to pass yourself of as me? The REAL FWLI champion is right here, pal! And the
REAL reason that you hightailed it out of the FWLI is that you knew you'd have to deal with me! You saw what I did earlier tonight! I eat big stupid dinosaurs like you for breakfast! My only regret is that I never got the chance to throw you around the ring and take this belt from you personally! But I'm gonna let you off the hook tonight! You just hand over that bogus belt to me right now, and everything will be fine!
MARK: Why you smug little...[makes a move at Newkirk] JONES: HEY! HEY! [holds Destructo back] Calm down! You've got a match tonight for the RSPCW World's Title! Why do we need to haul around this piece of trash belt anymore!? If this guy wants this belt so bad, I say let him have it!
MARK: You know [Mark slides the belt off his shoulder into his arms], you're absolutely right! Here you go! [Mark makes like he's presenting the belt to Newkirk, and Todd instinctively reaches out to grab it. Destructo then "let's him have it" right across the head. Newkirk crumples to the ground, and Jones takes both belts and puts them over Destructo's shoulders.]
EASTMAN: MY GOD, WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU TWO DOING!? MARK: If there was ever any doubt as to who the REAL FWLI World's Champion is, I've just unified the title! JONES: Hitman, we've got this man's shoulders covered, now we're going for one for the waist! [Both men laugh maniacally and walk off the set.] EASTMAN: NEWKIRK'S HURT, WE NEED SOME HELP HERE QUICK! SCOOP, BACK TO YOU! [Back to SCOOP and FISH] SCOOP: We've just witnessed yet another horrible, cowardly attack by Mark
Destructo, this time on FWLI World's Champion Todd Newkirk! That was totally uncalled for!
FISH: Why doesn't Eastman ever send it back to me? SCOOP: WHAT!? How can you...oh, never mind! Let's go to the announcer for our main event!
[DING! DING! DING!] ANNOUCNER: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THIS IS OUR MAIN EVENT OF THE EVENING, A NO HOLDS BARRED MATCH FOR THE RSPCW WORLD'S HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! THERE ARE NO COUNTOUTS, NO DISQUALIFICATIONS, AND NO TIME LIMIT! THERE MUST BE A WINNER! ANNOUNCER: INTRODUCING FIRST, THE CHALLENGER, WEIGHING IN AT 368 POUNDS AND HAILING FROM CHICAGO, ILLINOIS! A FORMER MULTI-TIME, MULTI-FEDERATION WORLD'S HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, AND THE SELF-PROCLAIMED TOUGHEST MAN IN PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING TODAY,
AND BEING LED TO THE RING BY HIS MANAGER, THOMAS F. JONES III,
THIS IS MARK DESTRUCTO!!! [The PA system blares "Sweet Dreams" by Marilyn Manson, as Destructo and
Jones head to the ring. Destructo has both FWLI belts on his shoulders]
SCOOP: Fans, just so we make things absolutely clear, the RSPCW recognizes nobody but Todd Newkirk as representing the FWLI as their World's Champion, and does not condone any of the claims made here by Destructo and Jones tonight!
FISH: You can spout all the legal mumbo-jumbo you want SCOOP. The fact is, this man was never beaten for that title, and he's carrying both of those belts with him tonight! That's good enough for me!
SCOOP: You're going to get us in real trouble FISH, if you don't shut up now! We're going back to Eastman to get some words with the
RSPCW World's Champion, the Hitman, Chris Thomas!
[Eastman back in another section of the locker room with Chris Thomas and
Mina. Chris is dressed in his usual jacket and pant-length wrestling trunks, with the RSPCW title around his waist. Mina is dressed in a rather uncharacteristic full-length orange dress, reaching clear to the floor.]
EASTMAN: Fans, I just want to mention real quick that Todd Newkirk does not seem to have suffered any serious in-
[Chris Thomas cuts Eastman off] THOMAS: Hey, enough about Newkirk, you're not here to talk about him, you're here to find out what's on the mind of the RSPCW World's
Heavyweight Champion, the "Hitman" Chris Thomas!
EASTMAN: [understandably annoyed] Fine, then what IS on your mind? THOMAS: What's on my mind is that tonight, I'm facing the man who gave me the pride I have in this title today! But what's also on my mind is how he did that, by making all those horrible comments about my manager! [Mina gives a sultry, yet angry, stare into the camera] You can put down me, The Heart Attack, and the
RSPCW Title all you want, but when you go after Mina, you cut me to the bone! You brought up our match several months ago, and
I'll be the first to admit that you beat the crap outta me then.
But three things have changed since then. I've got Mina by my side, which gives me all the extra incentive I need to beat scum like you! I've got The Heart Attack on my side, who've taught me all I need to know to beat scum like you! And most importantly, this time _I'm_ the champ! I've spent my whole career working for this belt, and I'm not about to lose it to you, Chris Taylor, or anybody else! Mina!
[Mina rips off her dress with one pull, revealing underneath her traditional white spandex outfit, with orange miniskirt, two orange boys, and orange high-heel shoes. Eastman has become a drooling wreck.]
THOMAS: It's time to fight! [walks off the set with Mina] EASTMAN: ....back to you, uh, somebody... [Back to SCOOP and FISH] SCOOP: Someone get that guy a tissue! FISH: Well I'LL at least try and stay rational here! The Hitman brought up something I hadn't thought of before: the fact that he's been training with The Heart Attack! Sparring with NUKE would give him an idea of how to handle the big guy, and if anybody can come up with a strategy to beat Destructo, it's gotta be Petrow! Maybe we might have a match here after all!
SCOOP: Both good points FISH, but there's no need to speculate any longer, because the Hitman is on his way!
[One of those versions of "Hartbeat" is playing, as Thomas and Mina make their way to the ring.]
ANNOUNCER: AND HIS OPPONENT...FROM CALGARY, ALBERTA CANADA, WEIGHING IN
AT 232 POUNDS, THE ONLY MAN IN RSPCW HISTORY TO HOLD BOTH THE
RSPCW WORLD LIGHT-HEAVYWEIGHT AND HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIPS,
LED TO THE RING BY HIS LOVELY MANAGER MINA, THE CURRENT REIGNING RSPCW WORLD'S HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION..."HITMAN" CHRIS THOMAS!!! SCOOP: The champ's on his way to face, quite literally, the biggest challenge he's ever faced for his title!
FISH: He's giving up over 130 pounds here, he'd better have himself a real good strategy! SCOOP: Hitman's hit the ring, and immediately starts trading blows with
Destructo! FISH: That's NOT a real good strategy! [DING! DING!] SCOOP: There's the bell, as if it matters! Chris Thomas employing much the same strategy, or lack of it if you well, that he did at
Halloween Anguish! But he doesn't have quite the same emotion as he did then, and it shows, because Mark Destructo is taking him apart!
FISH: If he doesn't remember that he's Chris Thomas, and not NUKE, he's gonna lose real soon!
SCOOP: Destructo has the Hitman backed into the corner, and he's punching and kicking him into oblivion! The referee getting in there now trying to break things up!
FISH: WHY!? Anything goes! SCOOP: Destructo realizes this too, as he picks up the referee and throws him halfway across the ring! But Mina's used this opportunity to hand her man a steel chain!
FISH: Now where was she hiding that!? SCOOP: The Hitman has wrapped that chain around his fist and gotten back to his feet. Destructo attacks, but the Hitman blocks it, and starts going to work on his head with the chain! And now it's
Destructo who's backing up!
FISH: Thomas Jones ain't gonna let this go on for long! SCOOP: Hitman's got Destructo back in the corner, still peppering him with the chained right hand! And the referee AGAIN trying to get in there and break things up!
FISH: When is he going to learn? SCOOP: Destructo grabs the referee, and does a double-noggin knocker with the Hitman to stop the onslaught!
FISH: Even the referee is a weapon! I hope Mad Dog Vachon isn't with us tonight! SCOOP: Jones has just tossed a chair to Destructo, and he nails the Hitman right across the back! One more shot sends Chris down, and he rolls out of the ring to avoid more punishment!
FISH: Hitman lying face down on the floor, and Destructo comes out to continue the attack!
SCOOP: Destructo picking up Chris by the trunks...and slams his head into the ring apron! And again! And a third time!
FISH: Hitman desperately sprawling out of the way, trying to find some kind of safe haven!
SCOOP: But Destructo's stalking him, and nails him from behind with a double axe handle that sends Chris tumbling into the timekeepers table. He seems to be feeling around for something over there!
FISH: What the hell, use the bell! SCOOP: He must have heard you, he's got that bell and he turned around and nailed an unsuspecting Destructo!
FISH: Destructo on the retreat now...I notice he's bleeding a bit from the chain attack earlier...I guess that proves he's human after all!
SCOOP: Chris Thomas has grabbed a cable from around ringside, and he's going after Destructo! He jumps on him from behind, and he's wrapping that cable around his neck!
FISH: That's a place where every man is vulnerable! If the Hitman can cut off Destructo's air, he's going down!
SCOOP: Chris has got himself piggybacked on Destructo, but he might be better off standing on his own! Destructo running backwards...
FISH: Both men go falling over the railing and into the crowd! We've got a problem here! SCOOP: Get those fans out of the way now! Destructo on his feet first, picking up the Hitman in a military press and...OH! drops him stomach first onto the railing, and Chris goes tumbling over!
FISH: That might be the turning point right there, the Hitman's hurt! SCOOP: Destructo climbing over the apron to get back at the Hitman. He picks him and sends him down again with a kick to the gut!
FISH: At least the referee has learned to stay out of the way now! SCOOP: He's only recently gotten back to his feet after meeting Chris
Thomas's head a few minutes ago! Destructo picks him up and tosses him back in the ring.
FISH: Now _there's_ a novel idea! SCOOP: Destructo climbing back on apron, and stepping over the top rope to get back in FISH: Here comes Mina! SCOOP: Mina just pulled up the top rope... FISH: There's another place where every man is vulnerable! SCOOP: ...and Destructo falls into the ring, and _he_ looks to be in great pain! FISH: I don't care if she is a woman, Thomas Jones has to get in there and stop things like that from happening!
SCOOP: The referee coming in for a look, but the Hitman pushed him out of the way, and goes back to work on Destructo!
FISH: He's got that chain around his fist again! SCOOP: Your right, and he's trying to reopen that cut he started earlier! Five shots to the head seem to have done the trick, and..WHOA! FISH: Destructo ain't out yet, he just shoved the Hitman down with one hand! SCOOP: Destructo back to one knee, but Chris is back is well, pounding away on Destructo's crimson forehead! And again, Destructo shoves him out of the way!
FISH: That cut doesn't seem to be having any effect on the big guy! SCOOP: Destructo back to his feet, but still leaning on the ropes for support. Hitman comes back with a kick to the gut!
FISH: Destructo caught him! SCOOP: Destructo has a hold of that leg, and he's got Chris hopping! Chris jumps for the back brain kick, but Destructo ducks it, and the Hitman crashes to the ground!
FISH: Here comes Mina again! SCOOP: Mina comes up from behind and rakes the eyes of Mark Destructo! FISH: This time Jones chases her away, but the damage has already been done! SCOOP: The Hitman takes advantage of the situation, and lands an uppercut low blow to that area Mina was working on earlier!
FISH: I'm surprised, it seems like these No Holds Barred rules are actually working in the Hitman's favor!
SCOOP: Destructo falls to the mat! Hitman grabbing the legs now... Sharpshooter? FISH: No! SCOOP: Hitman drops an elbow to the inner thigh of Destructo! FISH: That might have been even more inner than that! SCOOP: Hitman runs over to the turnbuckles, and jumps up to the second. He's measuring Destructo...and nails him with that patented elbow drop!
FISH: He's on for the cover! SCOOP: 1..and a BIG kickout by Destructo! FISH: It's like he's barely affecting the big guy! SCOOP: It looks like Chris is starting to get a bit frustrated here! FISH: Who does the Hitman think he is, Andrew Golota!? SCOOP: There seems to be no level to depths that Chris Thomas will stoop to tonight to successfully defend the RSPCW Title! Chris has got the legs, is he gonna do it again!?
FISH: No, he's stepping over! SCOOP: It looks like the Hitman is finally going for his patented finisher! He's having some trouble turning Destructo over, as he's fighting it with all he's got...HE'S DONE IT! The Hitman has procured the Sharpshooter in the center of the ring!
FISH: And given all that damage he's done to Destructo's nether region,
I'm not sure if he can power out!

SCOOP: JONES JUST THROWS A HANDFULL OF SOME KIND OF POWDER IN THE HITMAN'S FACE, AND THE HOLD HAS BEEN BROKEN! FISH: Mina's not the only one who can play dirty! SCOOP: As long as these two are at ringside, I'm not sure if we'll ever have a winner!
