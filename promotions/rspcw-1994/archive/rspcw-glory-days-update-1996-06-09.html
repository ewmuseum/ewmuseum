RSPCW Glory Days Update, 9 June 1996

9 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera pans down on the new RSPCW set, with theme music that's sort of a combination of an Alannis Morrisette song and the ESPN Sportscenter theme. Fade to a front shot of announcer SCOOP on the set.]
SCOOP: Good evening ladies and gentleman, and welcome to your almost fully Joe Melillo compliant RSPCW Special Update! Halloween
Anguish has come and gone, and we have lots of information for you today on the upcoming television special "Glory Days", and the great-granddaddy of them all, MegaWrestleFest III! But first, I'm happy, more or less, to report that both Thomas Jones III and my broadcast partner Fish have both fully recovered from the injuries they suffered at Halloween Anguish. Now, let's get you up to date on all of the exciting happenings during the past couple of months!
Halloween Anguish will no doubt be remembered as the turning point for the RSPCW. Several key members of the organization had threatened to boycott the league, including the World Champion himself. Commissioner Petrow, however, worked around the clock, up to the very last minute, and managed to pull the card together and ensure the stability of the RSPCW, and the brand of hardcore, exciting wrestling found no where else today!
[highlights of the various matches shown over commentary] SCOOP: After World Champion Chris Thomas won a very uninspired walkover, the Hit Man was challenged by the multi-federation World Champion monster Mark Destructo! Destructo's challenge fell on deaf ears until Destructo started making desparaging remarks about the one most dear to the Hit Man, his lovely valet Mina! The enraged
Chris Thomas tore into Mark Destructo, and had to be forceably ejected from the arena by seven armed security guards!
The first round match of the Tag Team tournament also appeared to be a washout, until the Players Club made their dramatic last minute entrance and stunned their opponents in a mere eight seconds to advance to the semi-finals! Danny Dynamite made it clear to all that the Players Club would settle for nothing less than complete domination of the tag team ranks!
And what a main event it was! Originally scheduled to be a ladder match between "Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow and "Hot Stuff" Chris
Taylor, later replaced by Mark Destructo when Chris Taylor failed to show up and the arena, eventually turned into a match between all three of them, when a badly beaten Chris Taylor stormed the ring, laid out Destructo's manager Thomas Jones III with a chair and tore into both his opponents!
The match was a fierce war from start to finish, featuring broken tables, and flattened announcers! After this devastating move through yet another table, it appeared that Hot Stuff was on his way to certain victory, when out of the back came NUKE to crash the party! Complete chaos took over, and as the wrestlers came out of the back to attempt to break it up, NUKE demonstrated his new deadly manuever, that vicious face-slamming reverse chokeslam he calls the QUARK, that was reportedly what caused
Chris Taylor to nearly miss this match! NUKE was originally suspended indefinitely and fined $50,000 for his actions by the RSPCW Board of Directors, but after appeal to, of course, Commissioner Petrow, all penalties were dropped. This has understandably angered the Board to no end, but it appears that they are powerless to overrule Petrow's ruling at this time!
Now, Petrow had promised Mark Destructo a title shot at Chris Thomas if he did not lose the ladder match that night. Thomas Jones III and his attornies have successfully argued that since there was no winner in that match, there was no way Mark Destructo could be declared the loser. Therefore, at the RSPCW TV special Glory Days, Mark Destructo will in fact, meet the Hit Man Chris Thomas for the RSPCW World's
Heavyweight Championship! Furthermore, Chris Thomas has demanded a match where anything goes to gain revenge on Destructo, so this match will be a special No Holds Barred match! Let's get some comments from the champ on this match!
[Hit Man is in a private wrestling ring, where he has just finished throwing 2 jobbers out of the ring. There is a blonde at ringside, with a red bow in her hair. She is wearing a white spandex outfit, with an orange miniskirt, and two orange bows. The suit also has orange trims.
She is cheering Thomas on. There is a third jobber lying on the mat, who Thomas places in the Sharpshooter. The jobber submits, but Thomas keeps the Sharpshooter on a little longer. He eventually releases it, grabs the RSPCW Heavyweight Title, and looks at the camera]
HM: Destructo, see what just happened here? I bet these punks put up a better fight then you can even hope to accomplish. You think you have a chance at beating me? You're looking at the man who just WALKED through that Octagon match to become the RSPCW World Heavyweight Champion. I mean come on, if I can take out 7 other men in one match, then just think of what I can do to one. But just in case you decide to cheat to get the upper hand, I'm going to have a little insurance.
[the blonde woman walks up to the camera] HM: Say hello to my new manager, Mina. And you know Destructo, after the comments you made about her, don't be surprised if she kicks your a** when I'm done with you.
MINA: *laughs* Mark Destructo, do you honestly think you can beat this man? Well if you do, I just have one thing to say to you: Wake up!!
It's not going to happen!! You're looking at the greatest champion in all of wrestling! Destructo, this man is going to dance all over your face, and when he's done, don't be surprised if I have a little something for you to.
HM: Destructo, watch out, because the Hit Man's got you in his sights!!! [Fade to black] SCOOP: Whoever comes out of this match as the champion will have little time to savor his victory, as his opponent at MegaWrestleFest III has already been decided!
[Cut to footage from NCW's Ringstorm] SCOOP: At the recent NCW PPV Ringstorm, during the World's Title Octagon

KANE: NUKE is now settling into a REVERSE CHINLOCK on Rogers! He seems to be intent upon keeping this strong man on the mat! NUKE's showing a lot more technique than...

Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow)
KANE: WHAT ARE YOU DOING... AIBORNE: WOW!!! HBK!!! Welcome to the booth, dude! KA$H: OH MY GOD!!! Mister Petrow...I'm such a fan of yours! KANE: Ozzy...Stacey...I can't believe you like this... AIRBORNE: SHUT UP, KANE! Mister Petrow, would you like my seat? KANE: Uhhhhh...NUKE still has the hold locked in...okay "Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow...JUST WHAT are you doing here? KANE: I see you're just as humble as ever, Petrow. HBK: Y'know, it's good to see they had the sense to put my Heart Attack partner in crime in this match, and no doubt he's going to win it all!

WHO'S THAT!!! FANS, SOMEONE HAS JUST STEPPED OVER THE GUARDRAIL AND IS APPROACHING THE RING... KA$H: IT'S HIM!!! KANE: OH NO!!! Fans...It's Petrow again! "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow has made his way down to ringside! The side judges don't see him yet...I'm calling SECURITY!
AIRBORNE: He's just there to give moral support to his friend NUKE! KA$H: Scott Rogers comes off the ropes...RUNNING ELBOWDROP ON NUKE'S BACK...He goes to the ropes for another...OH! AIRBORNE: WAY TO GO, DUDE! KANE: Security's on the way! What...HEY! PETROW JUST TRIPPED ROGERS! ROGERS HIT FACE FIRST ON THE MAT! The side judges are now all over Petrow! Didn't the referee see it? AIRBORNE: Dude, your talking about I.C. Nothen... KANE: Oh...right...NUKE now staggering back to his feet! Rogers is starting to stand...BUT NUKE CATCHES HIM...OH NO! HE SIGNALS FOR "THE
ATOMIC BOMB" (spinning piledriver)...HE NAILS IT!!!! THIS IS SO UNFAIR!!! SCOOP: But not even Petrow's interference could deny Chris Taylor his destiny, and with the Figure-Five leglock he became the NEW NCW
World's Heavyweight Champion! And as promised, Chris Taylor became the number one contender in the RSPCW, and at
MegaWrestleFest III he *will* wrestle the winner of the Thomas/Destructo match for the RSPCW World's title. However, Petrow has made this match a special "Last Laugh" match! Nobody knows exactly what this is, and right now even Petrow isn't talking about it, but I'm sure we'll find out more about this match in the weeks to come.
SCOOP: But before this Octagon Match, Hot Stuff got his hands on yet *another* title belt, as he won a tournament in Nagoya, Japan to become the first RSPCW Japan Champion, defeating "Solor Bomber" Shuichiro Nakamura in the finals. However, contractual obligations to another organization forced him to fly back to
Atlanta the next day for a grueling match, then back to Kobe, Japan the very next day to make his first defense against "Shootmaster"
Kenji Hamada. Still, Taylor was well on his way to making a successful first title defense, when the mysterious Mina made her
RSPCW debut, making her way into the ring when the referee was knocked out here, and devastating Taylor with a vicious crescent kick with that loaded high-heel shoe, costing him the Japan title, and proving that Mina will be an extremely valuable asset for her man Chris Thomas.
SCOOP: Speaking of the RSPCW Japan title, as promised that title *will* be defended at MegaWrestleFest III, and in recognition of his tremendous performance in a recent interfederation e-wrestling tournament, "Desirable" Danny Dynamite of the Players Club will get his shot at the Japan title. Champion Hamada was scheduled to defend his title at a Tokyo supercard two days from now against
Shuichiro Nakamura, but Nakamura has suffered a injury and will not be able to compete at this card. We'll bring you more information on this breaking story as it becomes available...
SCOOP: In the meantime, the stars of the RSPCW have been keeping busy on the road on the "In Your Face!" tour. The quarterfinals of the RSPCW World Tag Team Title Tournament were completed, and champ Chris Thomas made several successful defenses of his title.
