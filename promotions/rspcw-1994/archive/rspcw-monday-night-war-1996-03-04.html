RSPCW Monday Night WAR, 4 March 1996

4 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Cut to a deserted drive-in in the middle of nowhere. "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow walks onto the scene carrying a boom box. The sound of crickets chirping is interrupted when Petrow presses the "Play" button and the RSPCW theme music plays. One by one the screens light up with action from RSPCW cards past, from Petrow pinning Taylor to win the first
RSPCW title, to the American Dream Team winning the tag titles from the
Natural Born Annihilators, to NUKE winning the World title from the Giant
BOB. Pan to a scene of a jam-packed drive-in, with a mile long line of cars waiting to get in, and fade in the "RSPCW Monday Night WAR" logo.
Cut to a packed crowd of screaming fans in the Cow Palace, and pan down to announcers SCOOP and FISH at ringside.]
SCOOP: HELLO EVERYONE AND WELCOME TO THE RETURN OF THE RSPCW, AND THE FIRST INSTALLMENT OF MONDAY NIGHT WAR! THIS IS SCOOP AND THE FISH HERE TO BRING YOU ALL THE ACTION TONIGHT! FISH: WELL I'M SURE GLAD TO BE HERE, GIVEN RECENT CIRCUMSTANCES I WAS BEGINNING TO WONDER IF I STILL HAD A JOB! SCOOP: You'd better be ready to work tonight Fish, because the action is going to be all over the place in our big main event tonight!
FISH: That's right, tonight "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor tries to legitimize his RSPCW World Title when he defends against seven men in the
Octagon Survivor Match! SCOOP: That's the purpose of our special eight-sided ring tonight, as every man has his own corner to occupy. We went over the rules in one of our earlier updates, but put it in the simplest terms the last man to survive will be the world heavyweight champion.
FISH: "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor has been given a distinct advantage in that he cannot be eliminated from this match by disqualification or countout. If he can exploit those rules then he is definitely the favorite to retain his title, but if he wrestles like the goody two shoes wimp that he is then it's anyone's guess who'll come out on top tonight!
SCOOP: And before that we have the warm up match, as the man whom Joe Petrow "snubbed" for the Survivor Match, NUKE, will be in action in singles competition against "The American Dream Male" A.J. Charisma!
FISH: Well, I think Petrow put it best when he said that NUKE was "too soft" to be involved in this match. NUKE has not been himself these past few months, and I wouldn't at all be surprised to see
The American Dream Male wipe the mat with him tonight!
SCOOP: Fans we'll be getting to that match in just a moment, but first we have some pre-recorded comments from a man who hopes to make his prescence known in a big way in the RSPCW, "Flagman"...
FISH: Ah ah ah! SCOOP: Oh, excuse me, that's MISTER Flagman, Joe Melillo! [Cut to a plain blue backdrop. MISTER Flagman is waving his American flag with great vigor.]
FLAGMAN: I...have always been proud to be an American! But I...am not proud of what America is becoming! When I see plebians like Joe
Petrow running around our streets it makes me cringe in agony!
But what's even worse is to see a man of his low moral character actually heading up a major wrestling federation! This is a man who has been more unfaithful than Bill Clinton, more loathesome than Bob Packwood, and more devoid of integrity than John
Kennedy. This man has...no MORALITY! I am very near to bringing the FWLI under my control, and putting the decadence there to an end! But the RSPCW concerns me much more! I see that "Interim" label you put on yourself Petrow,
I know you don't have the integrity to commit to anything very long! Sooner or later you're going to slip up, and when you do,
I will be there to take control! That's right, I am throwing my hat in the ring to become...The President...of the RSPCW! And the fans will finally have a president that they can emulate after, and postulate for! AND PETROW! I WILL DO ANYTHING!...anything, to accomplish my goal, because I'm fighting for the heart, and soul, and the MORALITY, of the American people! And I feel, like
GOOOODDDD!!! [resumes fervent flag waving...]
SCOOP: Well, Pat Buchanan, I think you may have found your running mate! FISH: Or Bob Backlund at least! SCOOP: Let's head up to the ring for our introductions! [The bell rings three times to get the crowd's attention, as the Announcer stands in the ring.] ANNOUNCER:Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is scheduled for one fall! ANNOUNCER:Introducing first, from the Rugged Streets of East New York, weighing in at 263 pounds, here is "The American Dream Male"
A.J. Charisma! [The music for "American Males" plays as Charisma makes his way to the ring. He is wearing a black with yellow polka dot jacket, with matching tights, boots, and bow tie.]
FISH: Fans the FWLI is in a period of "reorganization" now, so tonight they are running a series of "classic" matches from their past.
So tonight you would see Greg "The Barber" Roberts win what for the FWLI passes for a four star match over Brian "Ice Man" Knight,
"Ice Queen" Rebecca Knight and "Maniac" Melanie go to a double countout, and Chris and Blaze Taylor defeat NUKE and Marc Destructo.
So if you want to see a woman pin a "world champion" you can change the channel now, but for the real action keep it locked right here!
SCOOP: But getting back to Charisma, here's a man who, formerly known as
Jason Clark, actually held the RSPCW World Tag Team title and ran the RSPCW during its glory days in late 1994 and 1995!
FISH: That's right, but unfortunately he began to let his ego get in the way of success, left the federation to work the Ferris Wheel at
Cedar Point, and since then has lived a Fairly Wasted Life, Indeed. SCOOP: Will you stop it! Nonetheless A.J. Charisma is on the comeback trail, and what a boost to his career it would be if he were to take out the mighty NUKE tonight!
FISH: The ONCE mighty NUKE! He ain't got what it takes anymore! ANNOUNCER:And HIS opponent... [The air is shattered by a defining explosion, and the sound of "UUUUUUUUUURRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRAGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH" Then the music cuts to Diesel's theme.]
ANNOUNCER:From Parts Unknown, weighing in at 340 pounds, NUUUUUUUUKKKEEEE! [The fans scream wildly as NUKE methodically makes his way down to ringside.] SCOOP: Well NUKE certainly seems to be taking this match seriously tonight! FISH: It don't mean nothin' until he gets in the ring! SCOOP: NUKE is just now doing that...and he's blindsided by Charisma! FISH: Just like I said! NUKE should be dominating this match, but this new "attitude" of his just doesn't allow him to do it!
SCOOP: Charisma working over NUKE in the corner with a series of hard right hands! A.J. attempts to whip NUKE into the corner...
FISH: NUKE reverses it! SCOOP: NUKE just sent Charisma HARD into the far corner! Charisma staggers out and RIGHT INTO A CHOKE SLAM BY NUKE!
FISH: WOW! Even I have to admit, that was devastating! NUKE might have him beat right here!
SCOOP: But NUKE is not going for the cover! Instead he picks up Charisma and sets him up! FISH: It looks like he's going for the Atomic Bomb! SCOOP: NUKE has got Charisma up, starts the spin, and piledrives him hard into the mat! FISH: We're only thiry seconds into this match and A. J. Charisma has been completely decimated!
SCOOP: Charisma is at NUKE's mercy, but again NUKE is not going for the pin! Now he's reaching for Charisma's legs! FISH: Is he going for a Boston Crab? SCOOP: It looks that way...no, it's a giant swing! He's really got Charisma going now! FISH: I'm getting dizzy just watching this! SCOOP: NUKE is still going around with Charisma, he has built up some incredible speed! FISH: How long can he keep this up, and what is he going to do with him??? SCOOP: NUKE has held onto this for over twenty sec...OH MY GOD! FISH: [in horror] I have never seen anything like that in my life! SCOOP: Fans, Charisma was thrown a good 12 or 15 feet in the air for about thirty feet of distance! If he had landed in the stands he would be in the tenth row!
FISH: He would have been better off landing in the stands, as opposed to that aisleway that has no padding whatsoever!
SCOOP: The referee has counted to seven, but he could count to 700 if he wants, this match is over!
FISH: The ref hits 10, it's over! [The bell rings. Half of the fans are cheering, half are too shocked to know what to do.] ANNOUNCER:Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match as a result of a countout...NUUUUUUUKE!
[NUKE raises his hands and lets out a loud bellow. He then races for the nearest television monitor.]
NUKE: DID YOU SEE THAT PETROW!? YOU THINK I'M TOO SOFT NOW??? WELL YOU AIN'T SEEN NOTHING YET!!! [NUKE walks off to the locker room, stepping on Charisma on the way out.] SCOOP: What were you saying about NUKE losing his edge? That was a truely awesome performance tonight!
FISH: I don't know, maybe what Petrow said lit a fire under him! Whatever the case I think that NUKE has just put the fear of God in everyone else in the RSPCW!
SCOOP: Fans, the physicians are looking over A. J. Charisma, when we come back we'll try and get a report on his condition, and set the stage for our main event, coming up next!
[Cut to a commericial] [Two teenage boys are watching a wrestling match on TV] "And ANOTHER brutal body slam by Destructo!" "That wasn't quite as vicious as his fourth one, but it made his seventh one pale by comparison!"
BOY1: What a great match. BOY2: Yeah. [Suddenly, a masked figure stumbles through a wall behind them. His mask falls off, revealing NUKE carry a large sack.]
NUKE: SO, FANTASY WRESTLING GOT YOU DOWN? WELL RIP INTO IT! [NUKE reaches into his bag and pulls out what appears to be a loaf of bread. Upon further inspection, it is a loaf of bread in the shape of a teddy bear. NUKE bites the head off, and a loud ripping noise is heard.
NUKE throws two more of them to the boys.] NUKE: TEAR INTO THE CRUST! [Boy1 bites into the bread, and the television explodes.] NUKE: THAT FLUFFY SPONGY TASTE! [Boy2 bites into the bread, and the ceiling collapses on all of them. NUKE emerges from the rubble.]
NUKE: NEED A LITTLE EXCITEMENT? RIP INTO A DOUGH JOE!
