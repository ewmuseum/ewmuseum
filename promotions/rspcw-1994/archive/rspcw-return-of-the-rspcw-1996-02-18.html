Message from Interim Commissioner Petrow

18 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Prologue: -------- I certainly never expected to be writing a letter as the commissioner of anything three months ago. I had left the rat race of fantasy wrestling leagues and was perfectly happy with my character's career in the RSPWF. After the final slap in the face from the League
Formerly Known As The RSPCW, I didn't expect to ever be seen in these parts again.
But through no fault of mine, I was seen in these parts again, and again, and again. It seems that once I left the name "'Heartbreak Kid'
Joe Petrow" became public domain, and everyone started using it. The
LFKATR continues to make subtle pot shots at me (such as ripping me to pieces in effegy), but I merely laughed it off. Then a federation I was never involved in throws my name around in a deliberate attempt to draw me out of retirement. I contacted them about it and even offered to work with them a little bit, and the response was extremely enthusiastic. But when I started saying where I wanted to go with my character, I never heard from them again. It seems they all want to use the name, but not the character that goes behind it. A kind of sterilized Heartbreak Kid, if you will.
The original RSPCW grew out of necessity, and so too does this one. Fantasy wrestling needs the RSPCW, and I am here to fulfill that need. The purpose of this RSPCW is not to slander (though to be sure there will be some of that), but to bring about my vision of what I feel fantasy wrestling should be.
So The Heartbreak Kid is back. But be careful what you wish for... - Joe ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- SPECIAL ANNOUNCEMENT FROM INTERIM COMMISSIONER JOE PETROW, AT RSPCW HEADQUARTERS IN MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIFORNIA After nearly a year of being a mere puppet organization, the RSPCW is officially breaking all ties with Fantasy Wrestling League International and returning to its function as a separate ruling body. All RSPCW titles are hereby declared vacant, with the exception of the RSPCW World
Heavyweight Title, which is being awarded to the last man with a legitimate claim to that title, "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor.
However, to insure the integrity of the RSPCW World Title, as well as to give the best wrestlers in fantasy wrestling the opportunity to vie for this prestigious title, Mr. Taylor will make his first title defense against seven other men in the first even Octagon Survivor match, on the first showing of RSPCW Monday Night WAR on March 4th. Whoever wins this match will be truly deserving of the title of RSPCW World Champion.
Next Sunday we will discuss the rules of the Octagon Survivor match, announce the first three of the seven challengers in the title match, and hopefully have some comments from the World Champion.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- RSPCW Roll Call of Champions: RSPCW World Heavyweight Champion: "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor (Decision of Commissioner Joe Petrow) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Joe Petrow Interim RSPCW Commissioner
