Halloween Anguish (1/3)

3 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A young couple is watching a PPV broadcast of "The Bridges of Madison County" Franchesca stands longing for the handsome stranger who walks into her life, when all of the sudden a deafening explosion sends the couple flying off of their couch. The screen goes black, and a voice is heard...]
VOICE: "The Bridges of Madison County" are closed for repairs. Stay tuned for something far more stimulating...the RSPCW!!!
[cut to a jam-packed arena full of screaming nutcases] SCOOP: HELLO EVERYONE, AND WELCOME TO THIS UNSCHEDULED, UNPREDICTABLE, AND MOST DEFINITELY UNCENSORED PPV BROADCAST OF THE RSPCW! FISH: [looking in disbelief over the unruly crowd] It's amazing what a pirate satellite and 10,000 gallons of free beer can do!
SCOOP: The powers that be in the e-wrestling world were able to shut down our orginially scheduled broadcast, but thanks to some creative ingenuity and a lot of free time, we're back on the air LIVE from
Crisler Arena in Ann Arbor, Michigan for Halloween Anguish!
FISH: Only a month late, that's pretty good by FWLI standards! SCOOP: Folks, our scheduled lineup was supposed to be the same as our advertised Monday Night WAR lineup, featuring The Players Club against Johnny Missle and The Flying Red Dragon in first round
World Tag Team title tournament action, RSPCW World Champion "Hit Man" Chris Thomas defending the title against "Executioner" Mark Engel, and "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow taking on "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor in a ladder match for the unofficial "Real Word's Championship". But unfortunately, and the crowd is not yet aware of this, many of the competitors have decided not to show up!
FISH: That's right, supposedly showing loyalty to their federation, the
Players Club and "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor have boycotted tonight's program, and will not be here tonight! That leaves us with the
World title match, and while both competitors are here tonight, they are here under very strange circumstances!
SCOOP: The story of Chris Thomas is very bizarre. Rumors had it that he, too, was abandoning the RSPCW and his world title. But we recently obtained some shocking amateur footage of a meeting between Chris
Thomas and RSPCW Interim Commissioner Joe Petrow at RSPCW
Headquarters, and well, since it defies explanation, lets just go to the footage. [cut to a small apartment complex in Mountain View, California. A resident is filming his family at play outside.]
MAN: Ok, sweetheart, say hi to the camera! [suddenly unrecognizable sounds of a fever pitched argument are heard in a nearby apartment.]
MAN: Hold on a second, ok? Man, what is going on over there...[man walks over to the apartment and sees inside] My God! That's "The Heartbreak
Kid" Joe Petrow and "Hit Man" Chris Thomas" What's going on in there???
[Man hides in nearby footage, and is able to get close enough to clearly hear the converstion.]
PETROW:SO YOU'RE JUST GONNA WALK AWAY FROM THE WORLD'S TITLE? THOMAS:I'M SORRY JOE, BUT YOU KNOW I HAVE NO CHOICE! PETROW:LISTEN PAL, I BELIEVED IN YOU WHEN NOBODY ELSE WOULD! Everyone else said you didn't belong in that tournament, but I knew you had the talent to win it! And I knew you had what it takes to carry the New
Generation of the RSPCW! I took a big gamble on you, and this is the way you repay me? By going back to being a lightweight nobody in a federation that has no idea how to use you??? And how am I going to run a federation without a champion???
THOMAS:Joe, you know how much this title means to me! But I have a lot of friends in the FWLI that are going to turn their back on me forever if I stay here any longer! You don't care about the fans or the sport, you just want to slam the FWLI and everybody else you feel like throwing a temper tantrum at! I'm sorry Joe, it's over.
GOODBYE! [Thomas throws down the belt, walks out the door.] PETROW:WHAT ABOUT MINA??? [Thomas stops cold in his tracks] THOMAS:...you gave me your word, dammit! PETROW:Desparate men do desparate things. I wish it hadn't come to this, but you leave me no choice. As long as you're the champion, I have to do whatever it takes to keep you wrestling right here. You walk out that door, you'll regret it for the rest of your life, and you know it. Now c'mon back in, and let's talk about your strategy for the match.
THOMAS:[a resigned look of defeat on his face]...you son of a bitch... [cut back to Scoop and Fish at the announcers table] SCOOP: Obviously whatever Petrow said to Chris Thomas had a very profound affect! FISH: Yeah, but what's a Mina??? SCOOP: Perhaps we'll find out tonight, but back in the locker room we have what I would say is the most depressed World Champion in wrestling history. And his opponent tonight! That story is even more bizarre!
I caught up with Mark Engel at the airport and he had these comments: [Scoop is standing outside gate 27 at Metro Airport, awaiting the arrival of "Executioner" Mark Engel, as passengers file off of the plane]
SCOOP: I am awaiting the arrival of "Executioner" Mark Engel, tonight's challenger for the RSPCW World Heavyweight Champion. Engel has been known for upseting many top atheletes such as NUKE and Chris
Taylor, and he seems to be catching Chris Thomas at just the right
MAN: Get out of my way! [A large man with a long, dark beard, wearing a straw hat and suspenders, looking like he just walked out of the 16th century, shoves Scoop aside and heads down the terminal.]
SCOOP: Well, I don't...[looks closely] Oh my God! Mark, Mark Engel! [Scoop chases after the man] ENGEL: Whaddya want! SCOOP: Uh [flustered], I was hoping to get some comments before your big match tonight for the RSPCW World championship!
ENGEL: Big match??? This is the biggest crock of shit match I've ever associated with! The RSPCW is a goddamn farce of a federation, and
I'm embarassed to have to show my face in it tonight! The only reason I'm here is because I'm on a special mission from the FWLI!
That spineless jellyfish Thomas doesn't have the guts to dump that rusty belt he's wearing because Petrow is holding the fate of some blond-haired bimbo under his nose! Well, "Hit Man", I'm going to relieve you of all your problems tonight. After tonight you're not going to have to be concerned about anyone's welfare but your own! And I'm going to take that "world championship" belt, and
I'm gonna hock a big loogie on that sucker, and I'm gonna pitch it into the nearest trash compactor! And that's gonna be all for the
RSPCW!!! SCOOP: Um, "Executioner", what's with the get-up? ENGEL: Heh, you didn't think I would use my best gimmick for these granola eating rednecks in denial did you? No, I'm using something far more appropriate for the idiots I'm dealing with tonight. Tonight, you refer to me as "Z Amish Gangsta!" Thomas, I'm a hundred times holier than thou, and I'm gonna lift you to a higher plane, sucka!
[Engel stalks away] FISH: That's the most bad ass mother farmer you're ever gonna see! SCOOP: Yeah...so that sets the stage for tonight, as our unwilling champion defends against the medieval misfit in what should be an epic contest tonight [Fish rolls his eyes] Let's go down to ringside for the introductions!
[bell rings three times] ANNOUNCER: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS SET FOR ONE FALL,
AND IT IS FOR THE RSPCW WORLD'S HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! [cheers from the crowd] INTRODUCING THE CHALLENGER!
["Amish Paradise" by "Weird Al" Yankovic plays over the PA system, and Engel makes his way to ringside with some moves reminiscent of the late Akeem]
ANNOUNCER: FROM A SMALL DELAWARE BACKWOODS VILLAGE, WEIGHING IN AT 256 POUNDS, "Z AMISH GANGSTA" MARK ENGEL! [Engel funkies his way over to a nearby aisleway camera] ENGEL: I'm gonna get medieval on that sinners buttocks! There ain't no way that...[stops, as Engel is attacked from behind]
SCOOP: OH NO! Some fan from ringside has just... FISH: OH MY GOD! Is that who I think it is! SCOOP: Mark Engel has been attacked by Brian Pillman! What the hell is a real wrestler doing here in the fantasy world? What's the history behind this, Fish?
FISH: I dunno. Poetic justice, maybe? SCOOP: What? Meanwhile "Hitman" Chris Thomas is making his way out and... he walked right past both of them! Brian Pillman is still pounding on Mark Engel, and Chris Thomas is walking to the ring!
FISH: Hey, smart move by the champ, why should he get involved when he's got a world title to defend?
SCOOP: Chris has just a blank expression on his face as he walks into the ring and drops the belt on the mat.
FISH: Pillman has taken the battered Engel back down the aisleway, and out of the arena! SCOOP: What about the match? FISH: Well, the referee has counted up to 8 now! SCOOP: WHAT!? FISH: Ten! It's over! [the bell rings] ANNOUNCER: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, "Z AMISH GANGSTA" MARK ENGEL HAS BEEN COUNTED OUT! THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH, AND STILL RSPCW WORLD CHAMPION, "HITMAN" CHRIS THOMAS! [the crowd boos loudly, and Thomas gets a look of slight disappointment and disgust on his face as his hand is raised]
SCOOP: This crowd is incensed, and rightly so! Chris Thomas has just picked up the victory, and he couldn't care less!
FISH: Well, Petrow said that as long as he was the champion he wouldn't let him leave, so it would seem that the only incentive he has is to lose that title!
[boos turn to huge cheers as another man comes out of the audience wearing a championship belt, grabbing the house mike and walking into the ring to confront Thomas]
SCOOP: IT'S MARC DESTRUCTO! THE FWLI WORLD CHAMPION! FISH: He's getting right in the face of the champ Chris Thomas! MARC: Is this on? Let me tell you people something right now! [unhooks the FWLI World Championship belt and holds it in his hand.] This belt, is history! [Slams down the FWLI World title belt, to thunderous cheers] I'm moving on to greener pastures! I'm going to a new federation, where everything is quantified to the finest detail! I'm gonna show the wrestling world the way e-wrestling
SHOULD be done, and if I have to screw the FWLI in the process then so be it! [HUGE cheers from the crowd]. But before I go, I want to assure my spot, as the *greatest* e-wrestler in FWLI history! I never got a chance to wear the belt I really wanted, the RSPCW
World's Heavyweight title! And now you hold that belt, a man who
I demolished just a few months back! You haven't done nothing tonight, if you got the guts, give me a title shot right here, right now!
[even more huge cheers from the crowd, as Destructo hands over the mike to the downtrodden Thomas]
THOMAS:You want this belt? You really want this belt. Here [hands the belt to Destructo]. I hope you're happy now...
[Thomas turns back, walks out the ring, and heads for the exit. The crowd boos loudly, as Destructo, and in rage picks up the microphone!]
MARC: Hey boy, I'm talking to you! I came here for a fight tonight, you ain't getting away that easy!
[Thomas keeps walking] MARC: Ok, fine! If you don't want to give me a fight, maybe Mina will! [Thomas stops in his tracks, fists clinched in rage!] MARC: Yeah, I'm sure she's twice the man you are anyway! And when I have her right where I want her, I'll show her what real Backlot action is supposed to be like!
[Thomas lets out a primal scream and runs back to the ring at Mach 3, tackling Destructo and landing a flurry of punches]
SCOOP: WHOA! Whatever button Destructo just pushed, it was enough to put
Thomas into a rage like I've never seen before!
FISH: These guys have just tumbled out onto the floor, going at like nobodies business! And I can't believe this, but the much smaller
Thomas is giving Destructo the beating of his life!
SCOOP: Get some security out here! Oh no, they're heading this way! Go to commercial! FISH: But this is a Pay Per... SCOOP: [hysterical] I SAID GO TO COMMERCIAL DAMMIT! [abrupt fade to commercial. A little boy, wearing a CDWF t-shirt stands in a wrestling ring holding a soft drink, as the massive NUKE heads down to ringside and enters the ring]
ANNOUNCER: Normally little Alex would never stand a chance of beating the massive NUKE. But today [boy takes big gulp of drink], Alex is drinking SPRITZ!
ALEX: You're in *my* world now, NUKE! [cut to some outtakes of their match, with NUKE pounding on Alex, hitting him with a Bullet Train to Hell, locking him in a Giant Swing (the camera takes a birds eye view of Alex screaming in horror), and finally finishing him off with an Atomic Bomb.]
[Alex sits dazed in the ring] ANNOUNCER: Ok, maybe Spritz won't make Alex a better wrestler, but to be sure, let's pit him against former WWF World Heavyweight
Champion Pat Patterson. [Camera pans over to a large, balding fat man in sumo garb, with a sly smile on his face] PAT: Heh heh heh... [Quick shot of Alex with the fear of God on his face, before a final shot of NUKE walking away from the ring]
NUKE: Good luck kid! ANNOUNCER: Obey your thirst, SPRITZ!
