MegaWrestleFest III (2/3)

29 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[pan to scenes from the first MegaWrestleFest in Madison Square Garden, with voiceover by Dan Eastman]
EASTMAN:The first major card in RSPCW history, MegaWrestleFest set the standard for fantasy wrestling PPV's that exists to this day!
The main event featured an Ironman tournament to determine the first RSPCW World's champion! In the finals it was Chris Taylor battling Joe Petrow for over an hour, before a number of thugs interferred, and made Petrow the new RSPCW World's Champion!
But the celebration was cut short, as Petrow laid eyes for the first time on genetic wonder of a man known as NUKE! This has been Dan Eastman, with a MegaWrestleFest moment!
SCOOP:Well, that segment was taped beforehand, but it seems very ironic and appropriate given what just happened here earlier!
FISH: It's been a very odd two years since then: NUKE put Petrow out of wrestling, NUKE brought Petrow back into wrestling to form a team,
Petrow abandons NUKE, Petrow abandons the partner he was supposed to face NUKE with, NUKE reunites with Petrow, and now NUKE stabs
Petrow in the back! It's the classic American friendship!
SCOOP:Alright, the show must go on, let's go back to the ring for our next match! [DING! DING! DING!] ANNOUNCER:LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THIS IS A SPECIAL CHALLENGE MATCH SET FOR
ONE FALL! INTRODUCING FIRST, THE INTERNATIONAL RENOUNED WRESTLER
AND RECORDING ARTIST, THE JAMMER! [The Jammer makes his way to the ring, slapping nearly every hand thrust in front of him] SCOOP:Alright, The Jammer is ready to get it on at MegaWrestleFest! I wonder why he's not doing his rap tonight?
FISH: Didn't you hear? Rap is illegal in Hawaii! It got passed as a rider to that bill that legalized same-sex marriages here!
SCOOP:Whatever, FISH! Of course the Jammer is big, but the real story here is the RSPCW debut of the ICEMAN Dean Diamond!
FISH: Well, I hear that he is a lethal weapon skilled in 14 different martial arts! Unfortunately, he prefers to wrestle in 40 degree temperatures, so a match in Hawaii in broad daylight in an open air stadium may not be to his advantage!
ANNOUNCER:AND HIS OPPONENT! FROM BOULDER, COLORADO, THE MYSERIOUS "ICEMAN" DEAN DIAMOND! [Iceman Dean Diamond is being wheeled to the ring inside of a transparent walled refrigeration unit. Large quantities of a cold blue fog is emitted from the unit. "In The Meantime" by Spacehog plays on the PA]
SCOOP:Well THIS is certainly a different kind of entrance! FISH: He's been in for several hours, I hear. If he's able to store enough of this coldness in his body, then maybe he can
[BOOM!!!!] FISH: OH NO! SCOOP:Either Richard Jewell is in the arena, or the refrigeration unit couldn't take the load any longer!
FISH: That blue fog has being replaced by black smoke! SCOOP:The fans have had enough of this farce, and so has the Jammer! He runs down and rips the door open, and brings the Iceman out!
FISH: Let's see how this guy fares under the spotlights of RSPCW scrutiny! SCOOP:Jammer leads the Iceman back to he ring with a series of punches! Jammer rolls the Iceman into the ring! FISH: Has the match even started? [DING! DING! DING!] SCOOP:It has now! The Jammer sends the Iceman into the ropes, and drops him with a spin kick right on the button!
[a chant starts up in the crowd] FISH: Are they saying what I think they're saying!? SCOOP:It sounds like "We Want Fruit" to me! And The Jammer is waving his arms, saying to bring it on!
[large quantities of produce are heaved into the ring, as the referee seems helpless to do anything about it]
FISH: Iceman hasn't even moved since that spinkick. He's turning into a pool of sweat! SCOOP:Maybe we'll call him the Squonk after this! The fruit barrage seems to have stopped, and the Jammer is making a pile in the middle of the ring!
FISH: What is he thinking of doing here!? SCOOP:The Jammer back over to the Iceman, he *bodyslammed* the Iceman on that pile of fruit!
FISH: And the crowd is going bananas! I can't believe I said that! SCOOP:The Jammer is going up to the top rope, this has gotta be in! Jammer off the top with a big legdrop on the Iceman!
FISH: The Alabama Jam! SCOOP:Jammer for the cover...1...2...3! FISH: Hey! Iceman's shoulder wasn't on the mat, it was on a guava! [DING! DING! DING!] ANNOUNCER:LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, HERE IS YOUR WINNER...THE JAMMER! [Jammer's song "She's Hot!" plays, as the Jammer heads back to the locker room in triumph] FISH:An unfortunate situation, really. I just...now what is this? [Six men dressed as penguins head towards the ring. They hoist the
Iceman on their shoulders, and carry him back to the locker room]
SCOOP:A rather inauspicious debut for the Iceman Dean Diamond! FISH: Well, this was a bad situation for him in a lot of ways. But you can bet he'll be a top contender for the RSPCW Siberian Title!
SCOOP:Well, it's nothing but title matches from here on out! Coming up next is the match for the RSPCW Japan Championship! Standing by
Dan Eastman, here is the challenger, "Desirable" Danny Dynamite!
