The Gauntlet

24 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[RSPCW Commissioner Joe Petrow sits behind his desk at RSPCW Headquarters] PETROW: For dramatic purposes I would love to be making this challenge from an FWLI broadcast, but I understand that they have orders to shoot me on sight, so this will have to do.
For some time now the FWLI and the RSPCW have been waging a war of attrition. They have ridiculed us, decried us, and practically tried to make us look like the anti-Christ of e-wrestling. All
I did to bring this on was call those people a bunch of idiots with no sense of tradition. Most importantly, they tried to snuff us out before we could get on our feet, attempted to exterminate us before our time! But now the RSPCW is a full-strength, fully functional, LEGITIMATE e-wrestling federation! And it is about damned time that we get the respect that we deserve!
There are two ways that we can continue this war. We can continue with the taunting interviews, the fake boardroom discussions, and those oh-so-witty "investigative reports" Or we can settle our differences the way they should be settled: in the ring!
So this is our challenge to the FWLI, and especially to that pompous mental midget in charge of things, you know who you are! You get the three best wrestlers you have in the FWLI. It doesn't matter who, just so long as they are representative of your organization.
And you prepare them to do battle with myself, Mark Destructo, and our very special friend. Winner take all. We can even do this in a neutral organization like the NCW, so that you have nothing to cry about when we kick your asses all over the arena! And we can see just who has the right to call themselves the true descendants of the legendary RSPCW!
FWLI, he ball's in your court. Do the right thing chico. "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow RSPCW Interim Commissioner [detail withheld]
