RSPCW Japan Heavyweight Championship Tournament

15 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last night, April 15th, in Nagoya, Japan, history was made, as the RSPCW held its tournament for the RSPCW Japan Heavyweight Championship. The final round saw former World Champion "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor face off against current FRSPF (fj Rec Sport Prowrestling Federation) champion
"Solor Bomber" Shuichiro Nakamura. After 35 minutes of epic competition, we join Japanese broadcaster Takao Inoue with the call of the climax:
井上：中村さんトープーロプーが入りました。 そうして、今のスーパーボム…無くなした！ タいラーさんが逃避出来ました！ タいラーさんも反発しました… BULLET TRAIN TO HELLが出た！！！ 1…2…3！！！やった！！！ CHRIS TAYLORさん勝ちました！！！ Congratulations to new RSPCW Japan Champion "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor! Taylor will continue his Japanese tour until the end of the month, and if he is successful in retaining the title he will be defend that belt at
MegaWrestlefest III this summer against a top American contender, in the first fantasy wrestling match in America to be broadcast entirely in
Japanese.

RSPCW Japan Heavyweight Champion: "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor (defeated "Solor Bomber" Shuichiro Nakamura in tournament finals in Nagoya, Japan on April 15th, 1996)
