Hamada Defeats Taylor for the Japan Title

19 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chris Taylor has had a very busy week. April 15th, Taylor won a tournament in Nagoya, Japan, to become the first RSPCW Japan Champion.
But there was no time for celebration, as he immediately boarded a charter plane to Atlanta, to compete in the FWLI Heavyweight Championship tournament. There he fought RSPCW World Heavyweight Champion Chris
Thomas in the first round, and lost an extremely hard fought thirty minute battle, and afterwards it appeared that the two champions were once again on good terms. But Taylor, fulfilling the obligations of his new championship, returned to Japan to face off against "Shootmaster"
Kenji Hamada in Kobe. [cut to various video clips of the match] Hamada, known as one of the stiffest workers in the business, caught Hot
Stuff with several hard kicks and nearly won the match early. But Hot
Stuff reached back into his own martial arts background, and for a while it appeared more like Pancrase than professional wrestling.
Eventually the match settled down to a more conventional style, with
Taylor holding most of the advantage. But at the twenty-two minute mark, a series of reversals results in this Hamada collision with the referee, rendering the official unconscious. We know join announcer Takao Inoue's call of the finale:
井上：大変な事に成りました！浜田が醒めた‥ ス―パーキクだ！ それで必ずTaylorさんの勝ち！ でも行司はまだ気がつきません！ ああ、だれか来ますよ！みなが出ました！ [...] 1‥2‥3！！！ As you just saw in that incredible footage, the mysterious Mina entered the ring and _devastated_ Chris Taylor with a Crescent Kick to the throat with her spiked heel shoe, leaving him easy prey for the pin, and making
Kenji Hamada the *new* RSPCW Japan Champion.
No connection has been made between Mina and Kenji Hamada, but she has been previously linked to RSPCW World Champion Chris Thomas. What Mina's intentions, and possibly the Hitman's intentions, were is still unknown, but as a result Chris Taylor is no longer RSPCW Japan Champion, and his injuries will force him to miss the rest of the RSPCW Japanese tour.
This will no doubt bring new fuel to the fire brewing between these two! This been an RSPCW Special Update...
