MegaWrestleFest III (3/3)

29 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Danny Dynamite stands with his manager Sarah Simpson] EASTMAN:Alright Danny, first of all I think your fans would like to know how you are feeling right now.
DANNY: Physically, I'm in a lot of pain right now. My neck screams at me at the slightest twitch, and to be honest, I really have no desire to get out there and wrestle again. But Mike, it's the mental pain that hurts more. I could have saved myself a beating, but I believed in our team, cause I thought you did too. But I guess you're just looking out for yourself now, huh? I don't know if our friendship can be saved...I don't even know if I want to try. But I know that right now, I have a chance to go out and beat some _sense_ into your head, and by God I'm gonna do it!
Sarah, I don't want you at ringside for this; this is something between me and Mike. See you in the ring, "friend"...
[Danny walks away from Eastman and Sarah] EASTMAN:A very reflective Danny Dynamite on his way to the ring! SCOOP, back to you! SCOOP:Thanks Dan! It's very interesting to look back at the circumstances which led to this match in the first place! Based on his performance in a recent interfederation tournament, Danny Dynamite was named as the challenger to face the Japan champion at MegaWrestleFest 3, which at that time was "Shootmaster" Kenji Hamada. But without telling anybody, "Maverick" Michael Reyna went to Japan to take the place of an injured challenger, and summarily won the Japan title, and thus became Danny Dynamite's opponent in this match!
FISH: And I think that's where the seeds of discontent were first planted, because after winning the title Reyna said he was upset at all the attention paid to Danny Dynamite, and felt he was being overlooked.
And frankly, I thought he made a good point there! SCOOP:Danny Dynamite said they were equals, always have been and always will be. It's a shame that jealousy seems to have ripped apart yet another lifelong friendship.
FISH: But you know who the big winner in all this is going to be? It's man who's just arrived in his VIP section, "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx!
He doesn't have to do *anything* tonight, and he gets the shot at the exhausted winner Wednesday night on This Wednesday in Waimanalo!
[cut to a shot of Matt Foxx and Knockout, dressed in their finest, sitting at a lavishly set table in the aisleway a few rows back from ringside]
SCOOP:Indeed, Matt Foxx is in the catbird's seat in more ways than one! I just hope he remembers that he's just a spectator tonight, and not a participant! But enough talk, let's go to the ring!
[DING! DING! DING!] ANNOUNCER:LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THIS MATCH IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL, AND
IS FOR THE RSPCW JAPAN CHAMPIONSHIP! AS THIS MATCH IS FOR THE
JAPAN CHAMPIONSHIP, THIS MATCH WILL BE FOUGHT UNDER JAPANESE
RULES. WRESTLERS GOING OUT OF THE RING WILL HAVE A 20 COUNT
TO RETURN, AND THE TITLE *CAN* CHANGE HANDS ON A COUNTOUT OR
DISQUALIFICATION! INTRODUCING FIRST, THE CHALLENGER, ONE HALF
OF THE PLAYERS CLUB..."DESIRABLE" DANNY DYNAMITE! [Danny Dynamite makes his way to the ring, ignoring the same fans he high-fived early in the night, though he does stop to stare a bit at
Matt Foxx before continuing on]
SCOOP:Danny Dynamite on his way, he really doesn't look up for this match! FISH: I'm really going to enjoy this, The Players Club at war with each other! ANNOUNCER:AND HIS OPPONENT! [fireworks and sparklers go off, as Michael Reyna makes his entrance] ANNOUNCER:THE OTHER HALF OF THE PLAYERS CLUB, AND THE REIGNING RSPCW JAPAN HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION..."MAVERICK" MICHAEL REYNA! [Reyna has returned to his bad-ass sunglasses and belt over the shoulder look, as he heads to the ring, praising himself at every opportunity]
FISH: Now there's a man who's got the look of a champion! SCOOP:The Japan title seems to have made a monster out of Michael Reyna! Danny Dynamite looks on in disgust as Reyna enters the ring. FISH: Reyna kisses the belt and gives it to the referee. I hope that wasn't a kiss goodbye!
[DING! DING! DING!] SCOOP:And we are underway! Reyna and Dynamite are in each others face, locked in an icy stare. Reyna lets loose with a smirk, and Danny
Dynamite just slapped it off!
FISH: So much for the techincal battle! SCOOP:The two lock up, and they're jockeying for position along the ropes! Dynamite finally has Reyna backed into a corner, but the referee is quick to get in and separate the two!
FISH: Reyna just used that to his advantage, and lands a sucker shot to the jaw of Dynamite!
SCOOP:Dynamite is reeling, and Reyna follows up with a rake of the eyes! FISH: I like this aggresiveness, but he has to remember, he *can* lose the Japan title by a disqualification!
SCOOP:And Reyna just raked his fingers across Danny Dynamite's back! They are not fighting like partners at all!
FISH: And Matt Foxx and company are laughing their heads off! SCOOP:Dynamite bails out of the ring, but Reyna goes right out after him! Reyna tries to slam Dynamite's head into the ring apron, but Dynamite blocks it and returns the favour! Dynamite grabs Reyna by the hair..
and he takes him face first into the ringpost! FISH: Dynamite's fighting dirty too! Whatever the result, it looks like the Players Club is history in the RSPCW!
SCOOP:Dynamite picks up Reyna, and dumps him down on the guard rail! FISH: Reyna had some damage done to that area in the tag match, so I'm sure he's feeling that!
SCOOP:The count is up to 15 now, and Dynamite realizes that, as he slides in and out of the ring to break the count. Dynamite back over to
Reyna, he's got him in the run...Reyna reverses it, and whips Danny hard into the guard rail!
FISH: Again, this all has to feel doubly painful after having already wrestled earlier! SCOOP:Reyna follows in with a kick to the gut! He takes the doubled over
Dynamite, and..oh no, he's not going to..
FISH: He's setting Dynamite up for a piledriver! SCOOP:He's got him up there! And he nails the piledriver on the floor! Thank God they were on the mat out there, but Dynamite is still in big trouble now! FISH: If Reyna will go to these lengths to beat his partner, imagine what he'll do to anybody else who tries to take this belt from him!
SCOOP:Michael Reyna is back in the ring, it looks like he'll be content to win this match by countout!
FISH: That's good enought to keep the belt! SCOOP:The count has started over, but I don't think it'll make much of a difference! The ref is up to 5, and Dynamite hasn't stirred since.
FISH: 7...8...and Dynamite's trying to get to his feet! Even I gotta give him credit for guts here!
SCOOP:He's on his knees at 12...13...14, and Dynamite's up! FISH: He looks like a newborn foal out there! The referee should stop this before he really gets hurt!
SCOOP:Dynamite's at the apron at 17, he's gonna make it back! FISH: Reyna realizes this too, he's over to break the count! SCOOP:Reyna pulls Dynamite up top, it looks like he's trying to suplex
Dynamite back in! FISH: He might have beat the count, but he's ripe for the pin now too! SCOOP:Reyna gets him up, Dynamite slips behind! Dynamite with a roll-up... 1...2..Reyna pulls the trunks and reverses the roll-up! 1...2... and Dynamite just kicks out in time! FISH: Reyna got sloppy and Dynamite almost pulled it out! But Reyna's saying that's it! SCOOP:Dynamite staggering to his feet, Reyna's coming from behind...sleeper hold by Reyna! FISH: That's how Reyna won the belt! SCOOP:Dynamite trying to get a grip on Reyna! He's got it, and he hits a back suplex to get out of the hold!
FISH: Reyna hit his head hard there! He wasn't able to get fully behind
Dynamite in applying that sleeper, and he paid the price!
SCOOP:Both men are down, and the referee is counting them both! FISH: Dynamite is starting to get to his feet, but so is Reyna! SCOOP:They've finally made it back up...Reyna runs to the ropes, he tries a clothesline, but Dynamite ducks out of the way! Dynamite stumbles towards Reyna, Reyna bounces off the ropes, and their heads collide!
FISH: Dynamite goes down, but Reyna fell over the top rope and landed face first on the floor!
SCOOP:An unfortunate move for both men! The referee is counting out Reyna, while Dynamite is still down on the mat!
FISH: The ref is up to 10, and Reyna is still down! SCOOP:Dynamite has only gotten to his knees himself! Reyna's got his senses back and tries to pull himself up, but the ref is already up to 16!
FISH: He's pulling himself up on the apron, but I don't think he's gonna make it! SCOOP:19...20! Reyna's counted out! FISH: What a way to lose a title! [DING! DING! DING!] [the referee presents Danny Dynamite with the Japan title belt, but
Dynamite seems too stunned to realize what is going on.]
ANNOUNCER:LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE WINNER OF THIS BOUT, AS A RESULT OF A
COUNTOUT, AND *NEW* RSPCW JAPAN HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION.."DESIRABLE"
DANNY DYNAMITE! SCOOP:Well, Dynamite is the new champion, but I don't know how satisfied he feels with this victory!
FISH: Michael Reyna is calling for the mic! REYNA:Danny, what kind of a man are you!? After all we've been through, you're gonna accept a cheap victory like that and take away that belt I worked so hard to get!? Well I *don't* accept it! If you're any kind of man, you'll give me a re-match right now and let's finish this right!
[Danny Dynamite drops the belt, and screams "COME ON THEN!"] SCOOP:Reyna's getting back in the ring! FISH: Are they gonna let this happen? SCOOP:The ref says its okay! Reyna and Dynamite are trading punches in the center of the ring!
FISH: It looks like Dynamite is getting the better of it! SCOOP:Reyna's stopped throwing punches, he's just absorbing them from
Dynamite now! Dynamite whips Reyna into the ropes, and floors him with a big dropkick!
FISH: Danny Dynamite is fired up! SCOOP:Dynamite picks up Reyna and sends him for the ride again! He hits a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker! Where is he getting the energy to do this!?
FISH: I think Michael Reyna is at the mercy of Danny Dynamite now! SCOOP:Dynamite picks up Reyna again! Is he setting for a piledriver! No, Reyna's *powerbombed* by Danny Dynamite! FISH: Reyna's out now! But Dynamite isn't even trying to pin him! SCOOP:Danny Dynamite is trying to prove a point here! Dynamite picks up
Reyna again, sets him up, and delivers another powerbomb!
FISH: This isn't even funny anymore! SCOOP:Dynamite is signaling for one more! FISH: Matt Foxx has left his VIP table, and he's coming to the ring! SCOOP:Foxx stops off to grab that championship belt! Dynamite has Reyna set up delivers a sit-down powerbomb! Referee down for the pin..
and Matt Foxx comes in to break it up! FISH: Matt Foxx is working over Dynamite, as this will be his foe for the
Japan title Wednesday night!
SCOOP:Foxx is beating Danny Dynamite with the championship belt! He picks up Dynamite, and nails some kind of reverse running bulldog!
FISH: The Attitude Adjustment! This is a preview of what we'll see
Wednesday night! SCOOP:Reyna is starting to stir! He's got the championship belt in his hands! FISH: And look at Foxx! He's holding up Danny Dynamite...is he setting him up for Michael Reyna!?
SCOOP:We might be seeing the beginning of a new alliance here! Foxx is telling him to do it!
FISH: Go ahead! You're much better off with Foxx than with this backstabber! SCOOP:Talk about backstabbers! Reyna's nodding his head! Reyna off the ropes with the belt...AND HE NAILS MATT FOXX!
FISH: Big mistake, Reyna! SCOOP:Foxx goes down, and Reyna kicks him out of the ring! And the the security is here to take Matt Foxx outta here! I guess the matters tonight haven't changed his opinion of Foxx at all!
FISH: But what's Reyna gonna do now? SCOOP:Reyna goes over to Dynamite who's kneeling in the corner...he put the Japan title belt on Danny's shoulder! Now Reyna turns around, gets down on his knees, and holds his arms out!?
FISH: Is he _crucifying_ himself in front of Danny Dynamite!? SCOOP:It almost looks likes he's inviting a beating here! Dyanmite isn't sure what to make of this either! Dynamite grabs Reyna's arm and spins him around to face him! Dynamite is staring into
Reyna's eyes...and he's offering Reyna his hand!
FISH: Aw, no! Don't do Mike, I was just starting to like you! SCOOP:Reyna pauses, and shakes Danny Dynamite's hand! And the two embrace in the center of the ring!
FISH: I think I'm gonna be sick! SCOOP:Michael Reyna raises Danny Dynamite's hand high in victory! Danny
Dynamite is the new RSPCW Japan Champion, and the Players Club lives on!
FISH: They'd better not think about the tag titles anymore, it looks like
NUKE has that pretty well covered!
[The Players Club lean on each other for support and head back to the locker room to a standing ovation]
SCOOP:Well I'm glad we that settled! Maybe I should take a page out of
Dynamite's book and forgive you for being a total jerk! Or maybe not!
FISH: The feeling's mutual, pal! SCOOP:Coming up next, the battle of the FWLI titans of 1996! We've already heard comments from the disposed champ Mark Destructo, now let's hear these pre-recorded comments from the reigning FWLI champ, "Nature
Boy" Todd Newkirk!
