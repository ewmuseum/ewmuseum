Halloween Anguish (2/3)

3 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The following was taped on April 3rd, 1996] [The crowd is still abuzz, but somewhat more subdued than before, at what has just taken place between Chris Thomas and Mark Destructo, once again holding his old FWLI World Championship Belt. SCOOP now stands in the ring with Mark and his manager Thomas Jones III.
SCOOP: Welcome back here at Chrisler Arena, we just witnessed an amazing scene between World's Champion Chris Thomas, and this man right here, Mark Destructo. Thomas was going absolutely nuts, and it took over half a dozen security personnel to escort him from the area. Mark, I gotta ask you, what in the world are you doing here??
[Thomas Jones takes the mike away from SCOOP] JONES: Mark Destructo doesn't have to answer an idiotic question like that! The reason we're here is simple: Mark Destructo is the undefeated, undisputed World's Heavyweight Champion, and we take offense to anyone that thinks otherwise! But before we get into that subject, I want to call Commissioner Joe Petrow out here and give him a piece of my mind!
[The crowd roars its approval] JONES: C'mon, "HBK", I wanna talk with you! [The crowd starts chanting "Petrow! Petrow!". Finally Petrow appears and heads towards the ring, to thunderous applause. Petrow walks into the ring and looks Thomas Jones dead in the eye.]
JONES: Petrow, it's no secret that there's a lot of bad blood between us! It's also no secret, that I had a lot to do with your leaving the
FWLI! [The crowd oohs and aahs] Now, I like everybody else simply wrote you off as a backstabbing jerk. But me and Destructo, well, let's just say that lately we've gotten a taste of our medicine.
And sure enough, it tastes awful! So, with the benefit of hindsight, I would like to formally apologize for all that has happened in the past!
[Thomas Jones extends his hand. The crowd yells at Petrow, most of them yelling "Don't Do It!" Finally Petrow extends his hand, but instead reaches for Jones' other hand and grabs the microphone away, to the crowd's approval.]
PETROW:What's done is done, and an apology is worth about as much to me as a kick in the ass! I'm sure you didn't come here to give warm fuzzies to the Heartbreak Kid!
[The crowd laughs as Petrow returns the microphone to Jones] JONES: OK, if that's the way you want to be, then I'll get straight to the point. This man right here was the greatest FWLI World's
Champion of all time, and given its lineage, that makes him the greatest RSPCW World Champion of all time! All we're asking is that we get a shot at Chris Thomas and take the belt that's rightfully ours!
[Jones passes the mic to Petrow] PETROW:Y'know, either my hearing is shot to hell, or you got dropped on your head one too many times as a baby, because I thought I just heard you call Mark Destructo the greatest RSPCW World Champion of all time!? Brother, the greatest RSPCW World Champion of all time is standing in this ring, but it sure as hell ain't you!
[Mark Destructo's eyes open wide as he glares at Petrow.] JONES: Oh c'mon! Let's look at our most common enemy. My man has totally dominated "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor, whereas he seems to get the best of you every single time you meet!
[Jones and Destructo laugh amongst themselves over this little joke.] PETROW:It's true that I've some difficulties with Chris Taylor, but I'm sure that I could give a much better performance against his WIFE!
[The crowd roars, and Destructo goes ballistic. It takes all of Thomas
Jones' persuasion skills to keep Destructo from attacking. Instead, he picks up the microphone.]
MARK: PETROW! You've been ducking me for over a year now! You think you're so great, then bring it to the ring, anytime, anywhere!
[The crowd gets even louder. Petrow picks up the mic, looks around to work the crowd a bit, then speaks.]
PETROW:You know Mark, that gives me an idea. I'm _supposed_ to have a ladder match tonight for the "real" world's title. But right now,
I'm missing two things: my opponent, and a belt. As luck would have it, you happen to have both! So if you're willing to put your money where your mouth is, we can get it on right here tonight! And if by some miracle you don't lose this match, the shot at Chris Thomas is yours!
[Petrow chucks the microphone at Mark Destructo.] MARK: Petrow, I'm gonna rip your <***>ing head off! [Officials come in to separate the two, and eventually they both head back to the lockers.]
FISH: WOW! I don't believe what we just saw! SCOOP: What was supposed to be Joe Petrow vs Chris Taylor will now apparently be a Petrow/Destructo ladder match for that faux FWLI World's title belt! Who saw that coming!? FISH: Destructo has had to deal with the shadow of The Heartbreak Kid looming over him for over a year now, and tonight he gets the chance to destroy the legend once and for all!
SCOOP: This match has been a long time in coming, and it's gonna tear the roof off of the Chrisler Arena! Well, now we've got a World
Tag Team Title Tournament match to deal with, so let's head back to the ring for the introductions!
[*ding* *ding* *ding*] ANNOUNCER:LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL WITH A TEN MINUTE TIME LIMIT, AND IS A FIRST ROUND MATCH IN THE RSPCW WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE TOURNAMENT! ANNOUNCER:INTRODUCING FIRST, FROM PARTS UNKNOWN, AT A TOTAL COMBINED WEIGHT OF 489 LBS, JOHNNY MISSLE AND THE FLYING RED DRAGON! [Missle and Dragon head down to the ring to the WWF's official "Japanese
Wrestler" music.]
FISH: Missle and Dragon a fine team from the independent circuit. SCOOP:Well, they *were* to meet The Players Club duo of Desirable Danny
Dynamite and "Maverick" Michael Reyna, but contract negotiations feel through, and they are not here tonight, so this "match" is just a formality.
FISH: Just as well, wouldn't want those pretty boys getting embarassed on national TV! ANNOUNCER:AND THEIR OPPONENTS, AT A COMBINED WEIGHT OF 460 LBS, HERE ARE
"THE MAVERICK" MICHAEL REYNA AND "DESIRABLE" DANNY DYNAMITE, THE
PLAAAAAYYYYYER'S CLUUUUUUUUUB!!!!!! [No music is played, and as expected nobody comes down the aisle. The crowd boos their disapproval.]
FISH: Well, we all wanted to see The Players Club show up for this match tonight, but hey, this ain't no fantasy world.
SCOOP:The referee has just given the signal, and the bell is rung. He's started the official count.
FISH: The second non-match of the night, this must be... [Suddenly the crowd responds to something on the far side of the ring] SCOOP:Hold on a second, two men have just come out from UNDER the ring, and.. YES, it's the Players Club!
FISH: WHAT? SCOOP:Missle and Dragon are in the ring celebrating, and they have no idea what is going on! Dynamite and Reyna are the the ring, running at their opponets!
FISH: Both men into the ropes...a double roll-up! SCOOP:1..2..3! Johnny Missle and The Flying Red Dragon both pinned just like that! Incredible!
[*ding* *ding* *ding*] ANNOUNCER:LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, IN AN ASTONISHING EIGHT SECONDS, THE WINNERS AND ADVANCING TO THE SECOND ROUND, THE PLAYERS CLUB! [Danny Dynamite grabs the mic] DANNY:To hell with politics! Those belts are gonna be ours! FISH: Missle and Dragon are livid, and rightly so! Those bums lead us to believe they weren't coming, then they pull a stunt like that!?
SCOOP:It just goes to show that you have to be prepared for anything in the RSPCW! Fans, we haven't seen a whole lot of action in the ring tonight, but I promise there's nothing short of Armageddon coming up in our Main Event ladder match between The Heartbreak
Kid Joe Petrow and Mark Destructo, so stay tuned! Joe Petrow Interim RSPCW Commissioner [detail withheld]
