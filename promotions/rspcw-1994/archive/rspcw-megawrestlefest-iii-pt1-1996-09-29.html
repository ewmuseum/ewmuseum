MegaWrestleFest III (1/3)

29 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Cut to "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow standing in the ring with the microphone] SCOOP:Fans I apologize for the cut in, but Petrow's got the mic and he's in the ring! PETROW:[holding his hand in a fist, with the thumb and index finger sticking out, shaking it back and forth] Shakka bruddahhh!!
[Hugh cheers from the fans, waving their pineapples, surfboards, and other assorted items] PETROW:As most of you know, I spent six of the best months of my life in this area, and I consider it my second home! And when I finally left to conquer new worlds, I promised that one day I would come back, and together, we would show the world what the Hawaiian
Hardcore scene is all about!!
[More cheers. Several leas are thrown in the ring] PETROW:Now before we get this sucker underway, I got a few things I have to say! First off, there's a little wrestling event that's gonna happen someday, if the hundreds of people working on it can get their act together, called the Blue Oyster Open!
[Laughs from the crowd] PETROW:Now, in this Blue Oyster Open, they claim that they're gonna have a match, in which they're gonna get all the big corporate feds together, and come up with _one_ World's Champion! Now that's a dandy idea they came up with there, but I got one question.
Where, the FUCK, is the RSPCW in all this??? [More roars] PETROW:But of course, that's a stupid question, because the answer is obvious, it's because the RSPCW is the bastard stepchild of fantasy wrestling! And you know what? I'M DAMN PROUD OF THAT!
I worked my ass off to make it that way! You know why? Because when your the black sheep in the family, you do ANNNNYTHING you want! That's the way it was for MegaWrestleFest I, so that's the way it is today!
[Roars again] PETROW:But I'm gonna lay this out to all the Oystermen out there. If you got the balls, let's make it a Quadruple Dance, and we'll see how the rest of you stack up against our boys! If you don't wanna do that [pulls out a ticket from his pocket] hey, I guess the RSPCW can make itself known in other ways too! [more cheers follow. Petrow puts the ticket back in his pocket]
PETROW:And as far as one of those other rumours go [turns to look SCOOP dead in the eye, and stares for a good ten seconds, before turning back to the crowd], all I've got to say about that, is that if he thinks I'm gonna give all this up to go someplace where fourteen writers plot out the future a year in advance, and STILL can't get a card out once a month...[puts his hands to cheeks and speaks in a high-pitched cute voice] "he don't know me very well, do he?"
[lots of laughter and cheers from the crowd. Some poi splatters in the ring] PETROW:And one more thing. Chris Taylor...the reason you got off easy last week is simple. Ever since you came back to the RSPCW, you've done nothing but "mail it in" each and every night! I've had to carry your ass so often, I'm disgusted to even be in the same ring with you! But I'm gonna promise something to each and every one of you out there! Today, you are going to see the greatest wrestling card in the history of the RSPCW! And Taylor...if you should do
*anything* to make me break that promise, then I'm gonna get on the next plane to Atlanta, and kick each and every one of the Omni
Horsemen's asses myself, because the RSPCW fans are the greatest fans in the world, and they deserve to see the greatest matches of their lives!!!
[Petrow slams down the microphone and storms out of the ring and down the aisle. Frantic fans scream wildly and litter the ring with coconuts.]
SCOOP:What the *hell* was that all about!? FISH: I don't know! I've followed Petrow through all the ups and downs for close to three years now, and I don't think I've ever seen him so totally insane like *that* before! Maybe it has something to do with those ludicrous rumors you've been tossing around lately!
SCOOP:Like I said before, these sources have never been wrong before, and they were quite adamant in telling me this information was accurate!
Maybe Petrow had one of his patented last minute changes of heart. Whatever it was, like I said before, I'm glad to have been wrong, and if what Joe said is true, I humbly apologize for what I said!
FISH: You'd better! He beat up some medics at Glory Days, so don't think you're untouchable!
SCOOP:But anyway, while the ring attendants are clearing the ring, let's talk about what we're gonna see here today! After nearly seven long months the RSPCW will finally have tag team champions, as the Heart
Attack team of "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow and NUKE take on the Players Club team of "Desirable" Danny Dynamite and "Maverick"
Michael Reyna in the finals of the RSPCW tag team title tournament! FISH: You left out two very important details there, the fact that Reyna is the reigning RSPCW Japan Champion, and that later today he will defending that title against his very own partner, Dynamite! So you've got to wonder if one of these guys is gonna be willing to hang his partner out to dry against the Heart Attack in the hopes of improving his own title chances later on!
SCOOP:I *know* that Danny Dynamite is not that type of individual, but you have to wonder about Reyna, given the way he's been acting these days! Also today, the long awaited RSPCW debut of the ICEMAN Dean
Diamond, as he takes on international superstar The Jammer!
FISH: And after many months of wondering "what if?", we finally answer the question of who the REAL FWLI Champion is, as the officially recognized champ "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk takes on the ex-patriated
Mark Destructo for the Unified FWLI Title!
SCOOP:Fans, I should point out for the record that this match has NOT been sanctioned by the FWLI, so the officially recognized FWLI title is
*not* on the line today, but the pride of both men certainly is! And of course, out big main event, a one-hour "Last Laugh" match for the RSPCW World's Heavyweight Championship, as the champion
"Hitman" Chris Thomas takes on former 3-time champion and RSPCW legend "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor!
FISH: The Heart Attack grabbing all the World title gold and the end of
Chris Taylor's career in the same night- that's why this is the greatest day in the history of fantasy wrestling!
SCOOP:Alright, speaking of the Heart Attack, let's head back to the locker room to Dan Eastman, who's standing by with Petrow and NUKE!
EASTMAN:Thank you SCOOP! Heart Attack, you guys are coming off a tough loss to the Players Club last week on WAR, how do you plan to come back from that today?
PETROW: Loss!? What match were you watching? The match I saw had myself and NUKE dominate those two punks every time we were in the ring together! The only reason Danny Dynamite is still alive, is that he is very skilled at the art of running away from us! But that's not going to happen today, because we are focused, we have no other distractions, and we have intent to fulfill the quest we began when this team was formed in late 1994: to become the RSPCW
World's Tag Team Champions!
NUKE: PLAYERS CLUB! I'VE CARRIED MUCH BIGGER LOSERS THAN PETROW TO THE WORLD'S TAG TEAM TITLES, AND TODAY, I'M TAKING THOSE BELTS AGAIN! PETROW: What? Anyway, the time for talking is over, and the time for celebrating is almost here! NUKE, we're out of here!
EASTMAN:Alright, the Heart Attack is ready for action! SCOOP, back to you! SCOOP:Thanks Dan, without any further ado let's go to the ring! [DING! DING! DING!] ANNOUNCER:GOOD EVENING LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, AND WELCOME TO MEGAWRESTLEFEST THREEEEE!!! [loud cheers from the crowd] ANNOUCNER:LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE
FALL, AND IT IS THE FINALS OF THE RSPCW WORLD'S TAG TEAM TITLE
TOURNAMENT! INTRODUCING FIRST... [the Heart Attack theme music plays] ANNOUNCER:FROM EVERYWHERE AND ANYWHERE..."THE HEARTBREAK KID" JOE PETROW AND NUKE...THE HEART ATTACK! SCOOP:They're on their way to the ring! NUKE is doing his same silent stalk to the ring, but Petrow's actually slapping some hands out there!
FISH: Well hey, this is a big night for him. He's already proven that he's the king of singles competition, and today he starts a new chapter as the king of tag team wrestling!
SCOOP:Let's go back to Eastman, who's standing by with The Players Club! [Dan Eastman stands with Danny Dynamite, Michael Reyna, and "Sweet" Sarah
Simpson. Reyna is once again coordinated with Dynamite's wardrobe, and is not carrying the RSPCW Japan title belt with him. Some muffled yells can be heard in the background]
EASTMAN:Alright, I'm with Dynamite and Reyna, and Mike, I gotta ask you what does this match mean to you, given that you already have a title defense tonight against the very man you are about to team with?
REYNA: Hey, I'll admit, I've been kind of focused on my Japan title lately. It's the first major heavyweight singles title that I've won, and it's very important to me. But my alliance with this man is also very important to me, and there's _no_ reason that we can't be competitors and allies at the same time!
DANNY: Heart Attack, did you really think we were just gonna self destruct and hand those belts over to you on a silver platter?
Sorry, it ain't gonna happen! Mike and I have been together too long to VOICE: RRRAAAIIII!!!! [Dynamite and Reyna duck for cover as a chair goes flying over them] VOICE: GET THE HELL OUTTA MY WAY! [The Players Club leave the set. Eastman is about to leave, when the person comes into view]
TAYLOR: YOU STAY PUT! EASTMAN:Chris Taylor, what the hell are you doing? [Taylor goes over to pick up the chair and comes back. A very concerned
Blaze is by Taylor's side]
TAYLOR: I THOUGHT I'D HEARD EVERYTHING FROM PETROW UNTIL TODAY! I CAME
BACK HERE TO THE RSPCW, WHEN *NOBODY* ELSE WOULD, NOT EVEN HIS
PARTNER CHRIS THOMAS, I CAME BACK HERE TO MAKE THIS WORK! BECAUSE
WHATEVER I THINK OF JOE PETROW AS A PERSON, I STILL BELIEVE IN THE
SAME IDEALS OF THE RSPCW THAT HE DID! THE RSPCW WOULDN'T *BE* HERE
IF I HADN'T COME BACK TO MAKE THIS WORK! AND YET THAT SON OF A
BITCH GOES OUT IN FRONT OF *MY* FANS AND TELLS THEM THAT I'M
"MAILING IT IN!??" BLAZE: chris... TAYLOR: BACK OFF, BLAZE! YOU CAN FAULT ME ON A LOT OF LEVELS, PETROW,
BUT YOU *CANNOT* FAULT MY DESIRE AND INTEGRITY! I GIVE IT 100%
EACH AND EVERY TIME I STEP IN THE RING, AND *THAT* IS THE REASON
WHY I AM A LEGEND IN THIS SPORT! YOU WANT A GREAT MATCH, PETROW?
THEN I'M GONNA GO OUT THERE AND KICK THE SHIT OUT OF YOUR GOLDEN
BOY IN THE FIRST TEN MINUTES, THEN STOMP ON HIS LIFELESS BODY FOR
THE REST OF THE HOUR! MAYBE *THAT'S* WHAT YOUR FUCKING HARDCORE
FANS WANT TO SEE!!! [Taylor slams the chair down and stalks away, Blaze following close behind] EASTMAN:.... SCOOP:A *very* irate Chris Taylor there... FISH: That speech by Petrow really seemed to have an affect on Chris Taylor! I haven't seen him that upset since his brother came up and asked if he could borrow some money!
ANNOUNCER:AND THEIR OPPONENTS...BEING LED DOWN TO RINGSIDE BY THEIR MANAGER
"SWEET" SARAH SIMPSON..."DESIRABLE" DANNY DYNAMITE, AND THE RSPCW
JAPAN CHAMPION "MAVERICK" MICHAEL REYNA...THE PLAYERS CLUB! [The Players Club make their way to the ring, seemingly unaffected by their close encounter with Chris Taylor]
SCOOP:There they are, making their way to the ring for the first half of their title doubleheader.
FISH: Well, they may present a united front right now, but I *know* that they are both thinking ahead to their match later tonight! They're gonna find out real soon that if you don't give your full attention to the Heart Attack, you're gonna be in big trouble!
SCOOP:The war of words has already begun, as the referee holds up the
RSPCW World Tag Team title belts for all to see. The announcer takes them out of the ring, and we're all set to go!
[DING! DING! DING!] SCOOP:It looks like The Heartbreak Kid and Danny Dynamite are going to start things off for their teams. And Petrow is holding his hand out for a handshake?
FISH: Hey, if nothing else Joe Petrow is a true sportsman! SCOOP:Right, FISH! Dynamite looking at fans, as if to ask for their advice...and he *spits* in Petrow's hand!
FISH: And Petrow's enraged! You *don't* embarass him like that on national television! SCOOP:Petrow and Dynamite lock up, and back up towards the ropes! Dynamite with a headlock, but Petrow shoves him off and sends
Dynamite to the ropes. Petrow tries a hiptoss, but it's blocked and reversed by Danny Dynamite!
FISH: NUKE's coming in! SCOOP:And Reyna comes in to meet him! This is breaking down in a hurry! Reyna's pounding on NUKE in one corner, and Dynamite has Petrow in the opposite one! FISH: I can't believe that Players Club is getting the best of this! SCOOP:Reyna and Dynamite getting set to run the Heart Attack together! And there they go..but Petrow jumps on NUKE, and NUKE catapaults
Petrow back! FISH: THE NUKESAULT! SCOOP:Petrow got Dynamite with that moonsault, and NUKE floored Reyna coming in with a vicious back elbow! The referee is in NUKE and
Reyna's face trying to get them back to their corner!
FISH: I'll give him credit, he's got more guts than brains! SCOOP:While the ref's attention is elsewhere, Petrow drags Dynamite by the leg over to the corner, and slides out of the ring.
FISH: He's gonna ram that leg in the post to soften Dynamite up for the figure-five! SCOOP:Petrow's got him set up...but Dynamite catches Petrow flush in the face with a kick from his free leg!
FISH: He got Joe good with that one too! SCOOP:And Dynamie quick to his feet, grabs the ropes, AND NAILS PETROW WITH
A PLANCHA ON THE FLOOR! It's obvious that Danny Dynamite isn't holding back tonight!
FISH: They're gonna have to take their chances to get it done, but I gotta admit, the Players Club are putting up a good fight!
SCOOP:Dynamite throws Petrow back in the ring, and takes him over to his corner, where he tags in Reyna! Both of them send Petrow to the ropes, and combine for a HIGH double backdrop! Reyna goes over to
Petrow and slaps on an armbar.
FISH: Reyna's really cinching in on that armbar. Just goes to show that you don't have to be suicidal to be effective. Reyna is wrestling smart now!
SCOOP:Petrow trying to work back to a standing position...both men now on their feet...and Petrow squirms out of the hold and into a hammerlock on the Japan champion! But Reyna with a back elbow shot of his own!
Reyna back with a headlock, but Petrow shoves him away, and Reyna goes face-first into the turnbuckles! Reyna stumbles backwards, and
Petrow floors him with a superkick to the back of the head!
FISH: There's the first big advantage for the Heart Attack, and that might be all they need!
SCOOP:Petrow makes the tag, and NUKE officially enters the match for the first time. Reyna trying to getting up, but NUKE continues to keep him down with those stomps to the back!
FISH: That's what Taylor claims he's gonna do to Thomas tonight! Fat chance! SCOOP:NUKE finally tires of doing that, and picks up Reyna. He's got him set up, is he going for the Atomic Bomb already!?
FISH: Hey, you don't get paid by the hour! Might as well get it done now! SCOOP:The crowd is going nuts! NUKE lifts him up, but Reyna goes over the top! He flips completely over, and now he's trying to get NUKE down with a sunset flip!
FISH: Petrow up to the top rope! He leaps! SCOOP:Petrow jumps on NUKE, and the weight of both men comes crashing down on Michael Reyna!
FISH: Kind of modified Neo-Nagasaki there! This match is over, but why isn't the referee counting!?
SCOOP:Because it was an illegal move, FISH! The ref is explaining this to Petrow and NUKE right now.
FISH: But it doesn't matter, Reyna's in a world of hurt and it's only a matter of...what is Dynamite doing!?
SCOOP:Danny Dynamite just snuck into the ring and dragged Reyna back to his corner! FISH: Why wasn't the referee watching *that* illegal move? SCOOP:Petrow back out of the ring, and Dynamite back out in before the referee noticed. Now he makes the tag to Reyna, who still hasn't moved on his own power since that double squash!
FISH: He's an idiot, he could have won the Japan title in a walkover! SCOOP:Danny Dynamite is the proverbial house of fire on NUKE, with a series of punches to head!
FISH: But NUKE is just absorbing them, as if they're having very little affect! SCOOP:Dynamite notices this too, and switches gears with a dropkick. But *that* doesn't even budge the big guy! Dynamite going for a body slam! FISH: Oh boy, is he getting desparate now! NUKE just blocked that easily! SCOOP:NUKE reverses that, and he's going for a bodyslam of his own. Dynamite going..wait, he holds on for the cradle! 1..2..kickout by NUKE, but that was pretty close!
FISH: The big guy is ticked off now! SCOOP:Both men on their feet, NUKE running at Dynamite for a clothesline, but Dynamite gets out of the way, and takes the off balance NUKE into an abdominal stretch. He's reaching up with the leg..he's got over the head of NUKE, and turned it into an octopus hold!
FISH: I can't believe he's got NUKE in this kind of trouble! SCOOP:We don't see that move very much in America, but it is very painful submission hold that is well known in Japan! Michael Reyna is back on his feet cheering Dynamite on, and the referee is right on top of Petrow to keep him from interfering!
FISH: A lot of strange contortions on NUKE's face for a man who supposedly doesn't feel pain!
SCOOP:NUKE is bearing down, and he someone manages to flip Danny Dynamite over him and escape the hold!
FISH: If Danny Dynamite were 20 pounds heavier I don't think NUKE could have gotten out of that!
SCOOP:NUKE tries to follow up with an elbowdrop, but Dynamite rolls away! Dynamite's heading for the top rope! Reyna's coming in the ring, what is trying to do? FISH: Maybe he wants to create a diversion! He's got the referee's attention now! SCOOP:NUKE slow getting back to his feet, as Dynamite is perched waiting for him on the top rope! But here comes Petrow from behind, and he shoves Dynamite hard to the mat!
FISH: Whatever Reyna wanted to do, it backfired on him! SCOOP:NUKE back on his feet, and makes the tag to Petrow! NUKE heads back over to Dynamite, and Petrow's going up top!
FISH: It looks like they're setting up for the Nuclear Surfboard again! Reyna was able to save him last week, can he do it again? SCOOP:Once NUKE gets Dynamite set up that's the _only_ hope he has! And
NUKE has Dynamite in the Razor's Edge position!
FISH: Reyna dropped off the apron!!! SCOOP:Michael Reyna is heading back down the aisle! What is he doing!? Petrow off the top rope, NUKE down with Dynamite, AND HE NAILS THE NUCLEAR SURFBOARD! FISH: Sarah Simpson trying to talk to Reyna, but he's just waving her away and walking back to the locker room!
SCOOP:Back in the ring Petrow was about to go for the pin, but NUKE is asking for the tag! Petrow looking to the crowd, and they seem to give the OK, as he tags the big guy back in! NUKE back over to
Danny Dynamite in a piledriver position. He starts the spin...AND
HE NAILS THE ATOMIC BOMB! FISH: NUKE just put the exclamation point on this match! SCOOP:NUKE with the one-handed cover! 1...2...3!!! WE'VE GOT NEW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS! [DING! DING! DING!] SCOOP:Petrow is running around in celebration! Eastman is back with Reyna, let's go there now!
EASTMAN:Michael Reyna, how could you leave your partner out to dry like that, you sold him up the river!
REYNA: Hey, when you see a no-win situation, are you supposed to go down with the ship!? No! You pull out the liferafts and you live to fight another day!
[the picture cuts away to Petrow grabbing the belts and running into the ring. He hands one to NUKE, and holds the other one high over his head.]
REYNA: Danny Dynamite let me get squashed by those two psychos just so he could get his hands on what I got! But that's not going to happen!
I am the RSPCW Japan Champion, and I intend to stay that way for a long time! Danny, I'll see you later in the ring, if you can make it there!
ANNOUNCER:LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE WINNERS OF THIS BOUGHT, AND *NEW* RSPCW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, THE HEART ATTACK! SCOOP:Some disgusting comments by the traitor Michael Reyna! FISH: Enough about the Players Club, this is the Heart Attack's moment, one they've waited for for two long years!
SCOOP:Petrow seems especially emotional now, as he hugs NUKE in the middle of the ring! Now the two of them raise each others arms in triumph!
FISH: Truly a great moment in the history of wrestling! SCOOP:Petrow turns to head to another part of the FISH: WHAT!?? SCOOP:NUKE JUST CLOBBERED PETROW FROM BEHIND WITH THAT TAG TITLE BELT! FISH: NUKE HAS JUST BETRAYED HIS BEST FRIEND! WHY!!?? SCOOP:NUKE HAS DROPPED THE TITLE BELT AND PICKS UP PETROW BY THE HAIR! HE'S GOT HIM BY THE BACK OF THE NECK...REVERSE CHOKESLAM! HE GAVE PETROW THE QUARK FACE FIRST ON THAT TITLE BELT! FISH: I DON'T UNDERSTAND THIS AT ALL! SCOOP:NUKE has both belts with him, and he's heading over to the timekeepers table! He's got the house mic!
NUKE: YOU'VE JUST WITNESSED THE BEGINNING OF A NEW ERA IN THE RSPCW! IN
THE PAST, EVERYBODY USED NUKE TO GET THEIR HANDS ON THE RSPCW TAG
TEAM TITLES! BUT TONIGHT, I TURNED THE TABLES! I USED _YOU_ TO
GET THESE BELTS, PETROW! *I* DID ALL THE WORK IN EVERY TAG TEAM I'VE
EVER INVOLVED IN! SO *I* AND *I* ALONE DESERVE TO BE THE RSPCW WORLD
TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS! BECAUSE NUKE ALONE IS BETTER THAN ANY TWO
WRESTLERS IN THE WORLD TODAY! [NUKE drops the mic and head to the locker room, holding both belts high, being pelted by lots of fruit on the way back. A steady stream of blood is coming out of Petrow's nose in the ring, and covers his hands as he tries to stop it.]
SCOOP:It's hard to feel sorry for a guy like Joe Petrow, but that came totally out of nowhere! We've got some doctors coming in to look at Petrow, as some have already helped Danny Dynamite to the back.
So NUKE wants to be the only tag team champion now!? FISH: I can't believe I've just seen my favorite tag team destroyed like this! I could see the problems brewing in the Players Club, but I really didn't think NUKE would ever have it in him to turn on his only true friend like that!
SCOOP:Ambition is a grievious fault indeed. The medics are trying to help
Petrow back.. FISH: He's loose again! SCOOP:Petrow is shoving everyone away from him! FISH: I'm surprised any doctor would come within a mile of this guy! SCOOP:Petrow wants the house mic too! What more can he say tonight? PETROW: [breathing heavily in a very nasal voice] Heh, I didn't think you had it in you big guy! I thought we knew where we were coming from, I thought belts didn't matter as much as hearing the screams of other people suffering at our hands! But you're just a greedy piece of garbage like the rest of them, huh? Well lemme tell you something, _NUKE_, you don't start feuds at MegaWrestleFest, you end them! You want those belts for yourself? Then you get your ass out here later tonight and you _fight_ me for them straight up!
But let's go one step further! You lose, your ass is history! And if I lose, you'll never see the Heartbreak Kid in RSPCW ever again!
I'm not asking you, I'm _ordering_ you to do this as commissioner! And I'm _telling_ you that I'm gonna kick your seven-foot ass!!! [Petrow puts the mic down, and goes back to the medics, who help him back to the locker room]
FISH: Did I just hear Petrow challenge NUKE to a loser-leaves-RSPCW match later tonight!? NUKE must have scrambled his brains with that
QUARK! SCOOP:I don't know what's happening now, but it looks like if Petrow says its gonna happen, its gonna happen! Well...welcome to
MegaWrestleFest! FISH: The rest of the matches had better not be like this! SCOOP:Let's go now to a very special "MegaWrestleFest Moment!"
