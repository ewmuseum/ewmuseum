Petros: The Freshmaker!

19 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Joe Petrow walks past an arena in a busy Metropolitan area. He notices a sign that says: F W L I M a i n E v e n t Melillo vs Charisma **** SOLD OUT **** But Petrow doesn't fret, because he's the freshest man in town!] JINGLE: Doo doo doo doo, doo-doo, do-Wah! [Quickly Petrow reaches for his roll of his delictable candy, and eats a piece. Instanly two beautiful women are at his side!]
JINGLE: It doesn't matter what comes, fresh goes better in life, [Petrow and his beauties walk up to the ticket counter, and the lovely young ladies create a diversion. In a move of impeccible freshness, Petrow slides past the ticket counter and enters a door that says "EMPLOYEES ONLY"]
JINGLE: with Petros, fresh and full of life. [Cut to action in the wrestling ring, where one man clotheslines the other, and both men fall out of the ring. Suddenly, a wave of freshness comes over the crowd, as Petrow saunters down the aisle...]
JINGLE: Nothing gets to you, staying fresh staying cool, [The referee is counting, as both men try to get back in the ring. Suddenly
Petrow reaches from behind, grabs both men by the trunks, and they fall to the ground, a very unfresh manuever. Both men are counted out, as the referee calls for the bell.]
JINGLE: with Petros, fresh and full of life. [Both men are ready to pound Petrow, but Petrow just gives a sly smile, and shows the men his Petros mints. Both men instantly recognize true freshness in the making, and give him a big smile.]
JINGLE: Fresh goes better, Petros freshness, fresh goes better [Petrow sits high on the shoulders of both men, and the crowd gives this veritable fountain of freshness a standing ovation.]
JINGLE: with Petros, fresh and full of life! [Petrow gives one last smile to the camera and shows off the candy.] ANNOUNCER: Petros, the freshmaker!
