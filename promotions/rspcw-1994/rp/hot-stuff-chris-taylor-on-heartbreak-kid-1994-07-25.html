Hot Stuff Chris Taylor, on the Heartbreak Kid's Retirement

25 July 1994
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In article <[detail withheld]>, [detail withheld] wrote: (...the operator's own statement on Joe Petrow's retirement, served in full on this wing's own news page, quoted here...)
I've got to say that the RSPCW just won't be the same without The Heartbreak Kid around. Not only was he a talented wrestler, but he brought out the best in his competition. I know this better than anyone. Kid, I regret that we never got to work as a team, because
I'd like to see anyone try and stop us. If you ever step back into the ring and need a partner, or an opponent, let me know. And just for the record, I know that you hate my guts about as much as I hate yours. That doesn't change the fact that you have my respect. I won this belt from you, and now it will always be known as the Joe
Petrow belt. If you never come back to the ring then I guess I have the honor of being the only one to ever take YOUR belt away from YOU. :)
We don't agree on a lot of things, Kid, but all of RSPCW should agree that you will be missed. Good luck.
********************************************** "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor RSPCW World Heavyweight Champion [detail withheld] To be the man, you've got to beat the man! -"Nature Boy" Ric Flair **********************************************
