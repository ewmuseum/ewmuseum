Smoky Mountain Region

captured 13 June 2003

What this is The roster page of the Smoky Mountain region, one of the promotion's three regions, from its Homestead site, as captured in the Wayback on 13 June 2003: the vacant regional titles and a paragraph for each of eight wrestlers.
smokymountain Smoky Mountain Regional Champion: Title Vacant Smoky Mountain Regional Tag Team Champions: Title Vacant Iraqi Strangler (6'0 - 225 lbs from Baghdad, Iraq) Heel Iraqi Strangler is a mysterious masked wrestler and little is known about him. He loves to perform high flying maneuvers from the ropes and he intends on competing for the Junior Heavyweight Championship. The only thing that is know about Iraqi Strangler is that is 16 years old and that he lied on his Visa application to stay in the United States. He is also one half of the tag team known as "The Annihilators" with his partner, The Shriekster. The Shriekster (6'7 - 280 lbs from Parts Unknown) Heel The Shriekster acts like an animal, but he is a master of psychological mind games. He is well educated in savate and kick boxing. He is also big and strong, but very flexible as well as a master in the martial arts. The Shriekster wears a mask to conceal a horribly disfigured face, but removes the mask to reveal his face to everyone. He can also imitate predatory animals by growling, barking, howling, and nother various animal sounds to freak out his adversaries. He is also one half of the tag team known as "The Annihilators" with his partner, Iraqi Strangler. "Arrogant" Robbie Avalon (6'3 - 235 lbs from Ft. Lauderdale, Florida) Heel People often compare Robbie Avalon to Bret Hart based on his look and his wrestling style. Robbie has held numerous titles in various promotions and has also wrestled for IWA, WFWC, and Ace Canada of Ace International Wrestling. He is also one half of the tag team known as "The Midnight Express" with his partner, "Too Pretty" Johnny Paradise. "Too Pretty" Johnny Paradise (6'4 - 245 ;bs from Hollywood, California) Heel People often compare Johnny Paradise to Shawn Michaels based on his look and wrestlung ability. Johnny has also held many titles in various organizations and has wrestled for IWA, WFWC, BCWA, and Ace Canada of Ace International Wrestling. Johnny is also one half of tag team called "The Midnight Express" with his partner, "Arrogant" Robbie Avalon. The Punisher (6'4 - 258 lbs from Punishville, United Kingdom) Neutral The Punisher has wrestled in various organizations. The last time he was seen in the ring was back in the GPWF in 1996. He did fairly well in the IWA from 1986-1990. He is also one half of the tag team known as "Primal Fear" with his partner, The Zodiac. Exeter Blue (6'5 - 285 lbs from Parts Unknown) Neutral Nothing is known about the mysterious Exeter Blue only that he is from somewhere in the arctic. The Warden (6'0 - 275 lbs from New York City, New York) Neutral The Warden is an ex-con who has spent the last twelve years in prison for aggravated battery to a police officer. He is a prison weightlifting champion and arm wrestling champion, as well as a bodybuilding champion. No one would hire him when he was released from prison, however his manager 'The Guard Dog" Snoop Jackson managed to secure him employment with the APWA. Corey Garvin (6'1 - 235 lbs from Moundsville, West Virginia) Neutral Corey Garvin started his wrestling career in the AWL adn then went on to the UWA where he won his first World Heavyweight Championship. After the UWA went under, he went overseas to Japan where he has been wrestling for three years until recently signing a long term contract with the APWA.
