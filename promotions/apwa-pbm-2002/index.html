Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The American Pro Wrestling Alliance's own founding notice, posted 18 May 2002, pitches itself as reviving play-by-mail fantasy wrestling, organised into three regions — Mid South, Mid Atlantic and Smoky Mountain — and affiliated with a wider umbrella body it called the United Wrestling Promotions.

It set no cost to join, a dollar charged per match plus special fees for tournaments, a weekly television program it called Saturday Night Slammasters with results mailed out every Monday, and a monthly bulletin covering rankings, results and news. Its combat system asked each player to distribute damage points across their own chosen moves rather than working from a fixed simulator menu.

A second notice two days later, on 20 May 2002, was still recruiting match writers to help produce the bulletins; no further document, card, or result survives.
