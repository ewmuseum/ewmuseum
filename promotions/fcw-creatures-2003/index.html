Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Fantasy Creatures Wrestling, FCW on its own page, was a website e-fed. Its home page of August 2003 explained that a group of people wrestle online by roleplaying matches on a forum to determine a winner, and that here the players took it a step further and played a wide range of creatures: “From humans to demons, all manner of creatures will face off against one another on this site to win matches and titles.”

The page set the fed in a dark world where wrestling, or exhibition fighting, had become big business since the discovery that humanity was not alone, and called FCW the premier fight federation, watched on television across the globe, offering full on hand to hand combat. Each player took the role of a fighter signed to FCW, expected to fight hard and put on a good show for the fans, and whether that fighter rose to the top or vanished into the low card was left to the player. The menu listed Home, How to Play, Characters, Rules and Application.
