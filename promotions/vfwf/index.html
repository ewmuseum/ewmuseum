Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Violence Fantasy Wrestling Federation was introduced in early April 1997 by its own founder, posting under the handle Cactuskind, calling for “roleplayers, trash talkers, and members that want to be involved” and warning off spectators outright: “if you want to sit back and watch the action, go somewhere else!” Its early cast included The Acer, The Reverend, the Powers of Destruction and “Playboy” Ronnie D.

It billed itself as “not TOO violent…just enough violence to please some, but sane,” offering a mystery-opponent gauntlet run for a shot at its World Title and a cage-match format, Sunday Night Steel, “the best way to settle feuds.” A second pay-per-view, announced that April, built toward a stable feud between the Highway Brawlers and the Order Of Destruction.

It closed sometime that summer. On 20 August 1997, replying to a fan who had spotted a VFWF sign in a real WWF broadcast crowd shot, a former handler confirmed the closure directly — “There was an old e-fed league on the net called the VFWF. It closed down” — and recalled it fondly: its authority figures, President Cactuskind and President Harley Sr.; its three stables, the Highway Brawlers, the Order Of Destruction, and his own Combat Air Patrol, which joined the Order Of Destruction “towards the end”; and his own wrestler, Allen “The Acer” Carter, a former World Heavyweight Champion and the fed's undefeated all-time Internet Champion, with “Playboy” Ronnie D remembered as his main rival turned ally.

No card, match, or result of its own survives; everything known of its championships and stables comes from that one recollection.
