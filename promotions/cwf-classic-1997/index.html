A fantasy federation on the fwrestling.com hub whose pages list shows from Friday Night Assault on 17 October 1997 through four shutdowns to June 2001, then hold the whole of its July 2005 return as Sunday Night Brawl, with a new roster set against Jeff Jericho's rival GPW, to its farewell of 24 January 2006. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Classic Wrestling Federation's own show archive opens on 17 October 1997 with Friday Night Assault and lists its first run in eras. The first ends in April 1998; the second starts with Thursday Havoc and Summer Smash '98 in June and ends on 29 December 1998; the third opens with Snowstorm '99 and Super Card II in February 1999 and ends on 30 January 2000; the fourth, from the third annual award show and Super Card III in March 2000, ends on 23 April; and the fifth begins with Super Card IV: Alive on 31 March 2001 and ends with Sunday Night Brawl on 17 June 2001. The page notes three of the shutdowns, each followed by a reopening with every title vacated. Its title histories hold five belts across those years, the World Heavyweight, National, Unified and Mid-Atlantic titles and a tag title, with 'CWF Closed' written where the shutdowns fall. Of those eras only the archive list and the title histories survive in the collection; none of the show pages do.

The 2005 return survives in full. Sunday Night Brawl came back on 17 July 2005 from the Kiel Centre in St. Louis, with owner Mark Xamin announcing a World Heavyweight title tournament, Jeff Jericho installed as commissioner and a roster of new signings that included Eric Dane and Tyrone Walker as Team Danger. Summer Smash 2005 on 4 September at AllTel Stadium in Jacksonville made Walker champion, and Paul Blair, who had beaten Xamin in a Hell in a Cell with Triple X in his corner, took the belt from him the same night. From the next Brawl the pages cast Jericho's GPW against the CWF, with Blair, Triple X, Lu Yen and Chemical X on Jericho's side, Maniac and Magnus Thunder against them, and Kris Lewis and Ricky Chambers arriving as Canadian Made. Fall Fever on 2 October in Toronto ended with Jericho beating Maniac to become sole commissioner while Blair kept the World title, and from October a co-president walks out on Brawl to overrule Jericho.

The last results page is the Brawl of 8 January 2006, with beat-the-clock matches for the last place in Last Man Standing and a tornado tag that ended in a roster-wide brawl; the Last Man Standing card was posted on 11 January and put back to 29 January. On 24 January 2006 its site-updates log says the CWF is closing its doors, and the farewell, signed by a co-president, says it is closing for good 'effective immediately', points its storylines to a federation at /host/naewf on the same hub, and counts seven eras and nine years. A last log line, dated 29 April 2006, says the CWF 'will be returning shortly with a new concept'; nothing later survives. Its six Hall of Fame inductees are Jeff Jericho, Steve Dart, Triple X, Mark Xamin, Z-Pac and Paul Blair.
