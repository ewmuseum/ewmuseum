Wrec Hannibal Name Wrec Hannibal Nickname N/A Height 6'2" Weight 225 lbs. Hometown Philadelphia, PA Associates Tony Floyd Alignment Neutral .:Wrestling Style:. Technical/High Flyer .:Entrance Music:. "Metalingus" by Alter Bridge .:Ring Attire:. Wrec comes to ring wearing a black shirt (Opinional), black tights with red bones running down the side spelling out Hannibal. He has black boots with dark red laces.
Outside the ring: Wrec Hannibal has short black hair on top and short on the sides with blue eyes. Usually wears a black shirt with writing on it, blue jeans and black shoes. He is meduim built and has a great wrestling ability. Very Atheletic.
.:Signature Moves:. Agony of Defeat: Electric Chairdrop - The attacker stands behind the victim and sticks their head between the victim's legs. The attacker stands up so the victim is sitting on their shoulders. The attacker pulls the victim forward and sits down, dropping the victim down face first into the canvas.
Flatliner: Reverse Russian Leg Sweep - The attacker stands face to face with the victim. The attacker grabs across the front of the victim with one arm and hooks the victim's leg. The attacker falls backwards and drives the victim's face/chest into the mat.
Blood Rush: Inverted Facelock Swinging Neck Breaker - The attacker applies an inverted facelock on the victim. The attacker quickly spins to a side, wrenching the victim's neck and dropping them face first into the mat.
.:Setup Move(s):. N/A. .:Finisher:. HELL FROM ABOVE - Somersault Legdrop. .:History:. Wrec Hannibal was a fan of wrestling his whole life. He started wrestling at a young age. He did high school wrestling and got a scholarship to Ohio State where he meet Seth Creed. Seth Creed took him under his wing and showed him the basics of amature wrestling. Wrec liking what he was learning decided to followed his mentor Seth Creed to the NAeWF. Wrec spent a couple months there before NAeWF shut down. Wrec then took a plane to the WCCW and also to the HCWF looking for work. Wrec ran into a man named Demonlord and teamed with him and El Diablo in a six man tag match in the WCCW. Wrec turned his back on them and joined the SNC in the semi final. Wrec then left the WCCW cause the WCCW shut down. HCWF noticing Wrec has talent, picked him up and gave him a title shot against Boogman.
Even though Wrec left the WCCW Demonlord followed Wrec seeking revenge. So once Wrec beat Boogman for the HCWF TV Tile, Demonlord showed his face. They had a fued going on for awhile. In the couple month Wrec was in the HCWF he manage to take the TV title, Alaska State title, and the HCWF Hardcore title. But this was not enough for Wrec. He also went to the CeWF and teamed with Isaac Knyte and won the CeWF Tag gold. After awhile Wrec became a target for the Nighthawks. They took Wrec out and Banished him from the NeWA. Never to seen again.
Then 3 years later, Wrec Hannibal returned from the darkness in which he went into. Taking out all of his anger in the BWA at first, winning the Ironman title four times. Wrec not stopping there teamed with an old friend named Dragonwulf. Forming a Final Judgment. They went into the PNW and BWA going after their tag gold. On the way they also manage to win the WfWA World Tag title and giving Wrec his first ever World title. Final Judgement was the first ever tag team in the WfWA history to hold two regional title which was BWA and PNW and also the WfWA World titles. Dragonwulf then went on a top secret mission which left Wrec Hannibal alone to defend his title by himself. Wrec ask Jason Lockheart after beating Lockheart for his Oregon State title a few days before to be his partner. Lockheart accepted and they went on to lose the BWA tag titles but won the SSW tag titles. Lockheart then stepped down due to Dragonwulfs return. Now Final Judgement is reformed once again but Wrec Hannibal seeks to gain more gold, which led them to the SWF. Dragonwulf later was called back to the military and Wrec Hannibal went on to become the first ever SWF heavyweight champion. But this was not an easy task for Wrec Hannibal had to over come a 15 man tournament. After holding the SWF title, he received an a deal with the BJWC. Without thinking twice he went over to the NeWA and joined the BJWC while also wrestling in the SWF which was a WWA federation. Tai Haven found out about Wrec Hannibal’s deal and banned Wrec Hannibal and Dragonwulf from the SWF. Wrec left the SWF and got a shot at the NeWA CW title. Wrec won his match and held onto the NeWA CW title for six months before retiring from wrestling for awhile due to issues at home with Tiffany Chaos. Now after all this time of resting and watching the new talent come in, Wrec comes back for another shot at the SWF.
