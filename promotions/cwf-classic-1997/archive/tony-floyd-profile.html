Tony Floyd Name Tony Floyd Nickname N/A Height 6'10" Weight 346 lbs. Hometown Hell, New Mexico Manager N/A Alignment Face .:Wrestling Style:. Brawler and Powerhouse .:Entrance Music:. "Open Your Eyes" by Alter Bridge .:Ring Attire:. Black wrestling boots, pair of blue jean shorts, Black, shoulder length hair, on his hands are black weight lifting gloves, and on his right arm near the shoulder is a tattoo that reads
“In god we trust, Vengeance is mine, Sayeth the Lord, No Fear” .:Signature Moves:. Weapon of Mass Destruction: Throws his opponent in to the turnbuckle and spears them back into the turn buckle as they are falling away from it; he then bull dogs them head first into the mat.
The Reign of Fire: When the opponent is hanging on to the turn buckle, Floyd will come over and slap the guy in the chest and arm area except when he he makes contact he grabs the muscle and pulls away, not taking the skin off but makes the opponent realing in pain.
The Executioner: A power bomb on the turnbuckle without the padding being there. .:Setup Move(s):. N/A. .:Finisher:. THE BOMB FROM HELL - Three power bombs with the four power bomb going into a pin. .:History:. Floyd started off just like any other wrestler. Either by knowing some in wrestling or another wrestler. In his case, he had a cousin that was a wrestler in some different feds. After talking to his cousin for a year or so Floyd Decided to get in to Wrestling. For the next few years he trained hard and learned all different types of moves. A year later Floyd got his first break in the NAeWF as Spawn, a loud, fowl mouth that was bent on destroying peoples lives. While he was there he met up with another wrestler by the name of Steppenwulf, later on changes his name to Dragonwulf. There the two formed a tag team called Blood Omen who went on to win the NAeWF world tag team championship. Floyd also went in to single competition and won the Hardcore Championship belt. In which he held both belts until the fed closed up. There a few days later he hears about his Father’s death and decides to go back home and help his mother out and pay the bills until she could get back on her feet. For the next three years he stayed there until his Cousin called him up and told him about the PNW.
There he signed on as Spawn once more. An on his first match he asks for a handy cap match against three wrestlers and goes on to beat them with the greatest of ease. While there he meets up with his cousin and Dragonwulf who already have formed the tag team of Final Judgment. But he also met another wrestler who went by the name Shadow Ninja. By this time Floyd had already join the BJW. While in the PNW, Floyd won the tag team belts in the BJW with a partner who right after winning the belts walked out of that fed. By this time Floyd’s better judgment got the better of him and he joined to more feds the BWA and the SWF. In the SWF he got to know Shadow Ninja a little better but still continued on his ways but at this point he went with his real name instead of Spawn. But his attitude was pretty much the same, but more of a fowl mouth then destructive. Floyd did go on to win the Tri-Cities Championship, but with this win it made Floyd way to cocky of himself. The result of that was him losing his title to Darkest Dark. Instead of learning from his mistake he continued to make a few more. At this point Ninja couldn’t stay on the side lines anymore and decided to help Floyd out. In which he did by controlling Floyd’s temper and then strengthens his spirit. As Floyd was getting ready for his match-up with Canis, Floyd got into a car accident in which broke his arm in four different places, and killed Ninja. For a while Floyd went to therapy for his arm. But with Ninja’s death, Floyd took on a different personality. Instead of being a hell raiser, he comes back as someone that you could really rely on. But on his right shoulder he has his believes tattooed on which reads: “In god we trust, Vengeance is mine, Sayeth the Lord, No Fear”
