"Golden One" Jeff Jericho Name Jeff Jericho Nickname The Golden One Height 6'4" Weight 266 lbs. Hometown Toronto, ON Manager N/A Alignment Heel .:Wrestling Style:. All-Rounder .:Entrance Music:. "Play the Game" by Motorhead .:Description/Ring Attire:. Wears a sparkling gold robe down to the ring with "Golden One" written on the back in black. Wears white boots and white trunks. When he is not wearing his ring gear you can usually find him decked out in one of his thousands of expensive custom made Italien silk suits.
.:Signature Moves:. Spinebuster. DDT. Boston Crab. .:Setup Move(s):. N/A .:Finisher:. GOLDEN PEDIGREE - Kick to the gut, put opponents head between thighs, hook arms and drive their face into the mat.
JERICHO ANKLE LOCK - Ankle lock submission. .:History:. May 15, 1996 is the day the wrestling world will never forget. Perhaps the most contraversial, arrogant, gifted athlete to ever grace the ring debuted in CWWF and changed the course of wrestling forever. Jericho led a new crop of wrestler, one's who could talk a blue streak on the mic/entertain the fans but also back it up with top level talent in the ring, which would revolutionize the industry. Jericho's classic pranks, plots, schemes had never been seen or heard of before causing wrestling to change from a cookie cutter outfit into a real adult action show. Why, Jericho even performed some of the most vicious attacks ever on TV all in an effort to win the World title, which he did 22-times over 9 years. The Golden One time and time again earned his spot at the top through sheer skill and intellegence to stay a step ahead of the competition.
To learn more about the history of Jeff Jericho go to his website at: www.geocities.com/golden_productions/ .:Accomplishments:. 2x CWWF World Champion ICWF World Champion EWWF World Champion 4x EWWF Intercontinental Champion 2x EWWF United States Champion 3x EWWF Canadian Champion MWWF United States 5x CWF World Champion 3x CWF National Champion 2x CWF Tag Team Champion 2x CWF Unified Champion MWF World Champion RWF Intercontinental Champion MCW World Champion SWF Tag Team Champion W3 Tag Team Champion 7x AWF World Champion 2x AWF Intercontinental Champion AWF Tag Team Champion 3x IOA World Champion 3x IOA Tag Team Champion NAW World Champion Overall, 22 time World Champion. Member of the SPN Hall of Fame. First member of CWF Hall of Fame. Former CWF Owner. Former CWF Wrestler of the year (1998). Created The Superstar Corporation (TSC) which dominated CWF, RWF countless times capturing all titles available.
Held the CWF/MWF World titles at the same time. Held the IOA/AWF World titles at the same time. Wrestled as Triple J in IOA but later revealed his true identity to shock the world.
