.:Signature Moves:. Cut'n'shoot: Mongolian Chop, opponent is thrusted in abdomen, Angus pounces to the rope and shooting himself, spear style in the ribs.
The A.C.: Standing Crossface submission, with more emphasis on straining the back of the neck.
Migrain: Opponent whipped into corner, Angus charges at them, shoulder thrusts. opponent begins to stumble, they're met with a sharp roundhouse kick.
.:Setup Move(s):. N/A. .:Finisher:. END OF ALL - Reverse Suplex set-up, when opponent is being slammed, Angus reaches around the opponent's torso to pull them down for a sidewalk slam.
.:History:. Mysterious, strong aggressive, predator... seeks to be the best, a perfectionist. Only trusts one person, Abby, but can be very loyal when approached the right way. Full of integrity, if allied, loyal all the way, if crossed... you don't want to know.
Being bigger than the other kids all throughout High School, Angus began shaping his frame into an indestructible machine early on... it was soon tested how strong he really was when he was involved in a fatal accident, killing his family, leaving him all alone. Knowing he would gain weakness from the accident, his mind shifted into a new, higher level of training, teaching him self 3 different types of martial arts, each pin-pointing a certain mastery: submission, stealth & agility, and defense. Throughout the last three years, Angus, formerly known as "Rage" has begun to reach his true potential... living well, the money claimed from the insurance and inheritance, Angus hired Abby Grace, to maintain accounts and personal business. She will often accompany him to the ring and serve as "Grace under Fire". After WwCW ended last year, Angus has spent all his time priming himself for a new fed, an he hopes to make CWF his new domination station.
.:Accomplishments:. WwCW North American Champion x2 (as Rage) CWF National Champion
