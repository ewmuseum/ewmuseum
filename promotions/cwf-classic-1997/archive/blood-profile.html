Blood Name Blood Nickname N/A Height 6'4" Weight 245 lbs. Hometown Charlotte, NC Manager N/A Alignment Heel .:Wrestling Style:. Technical/Martial Arts .:Entrance Music:. "Happy" by Mudvayne .:Ring Attire:. Blood wears black elbow pads, black wrist bands, and black fingerless gloves. He wears leather pants which often are different colors on different nights he's wrestling, but always have the same black design on each leg. He also wears black boots.
.:Signature Moves:. Blood Lust: Pumphandle Lift In To A Diamond Cutter. Arising From the Ashes: Blood knocks his opponent down in the ring. He then climbs out to the apron, and awaits for them to get up, facing him. He springboards off the top rope, executing a summersault. As he summersaults over the opponent, he grabs their head, causing them to flip over, drilling them with a devestating DDT.
Blood Lock: Elevated Boston Crab. .:Setup Move(s):. N/A. .:Finisher:. LEGEND KILLER - Blood boots his opponent in the gut, and sets them up in a powerbomb position. He lifts them up to his shoulder, then drives them back first to the mat with a devestating powerbomb, using as much impact as he can.
.:History:. Blood started his career in 1997, when he first came to the CWF. The american who grew up a native of Japan soon became the CWF Assault Champion after an impressive victory over ShadowMaxx. He would continue his career in other federations as well, becoming the RW Extreme Champion, and WEWF Tag Team Champions with Chris Reinhardt. It would be long after before he would return to his home in the CWF, and capture the Unified Championship from Mike Van Pro.
