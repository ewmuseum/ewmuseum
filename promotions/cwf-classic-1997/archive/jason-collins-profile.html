"All That" Jason Collins Name Jason Collins Nickname All That Height 6'2" Weight 245 lbs. Hometown Beverly Hills, CA Manager Sienna Alignment Neutral .:Wrestling Style:. Technical/Submissionist .:Entrance Music:. "Survival Of The Sickest" By Saliva .:Ring Attire:. Black tracksuit bottoms with "All That" written in bold white lettering down his right leg & white sneakers. Also Black tape around both wrists.
.:Signature Moves:. JC Cutter: Stone Cold Stunner. Midas Touch: Opponent is grounded Jason climbs to the top buckled and performs a devastating elbow drop.
Cali Lock: Sharpshooter. .:Setup Move(s):. N/A. .:Finisher:. REALITY CHECK - Death Valley Driver. .:History:. Grew up in Beverly Hills, California. His father Roger Collins was a famous movie producer so Jason was given everything he ever wanted and needed. Jason soon got bored with not working for a living so he decided to take up a hobby, bodybuilding.What started out as just a bit of fun eventually turned into a career. After a few years of training he was competing in events nationwide and it wasn't long before he was winning. That's when his ego became hugely inflated. The once shy timid lad was now very cocky and arrogant, with loads of self confiedence. He let all the success of his bodybuilding career go to his head and he quit the sport claiming that it was "No longer good enough for JC". He set about finding himself a new career and thats when he found wrestling.
Since entering this great sport his attitude has changed. Gone are the days when he viewed the opposition as inferior. He now respects and never underestimates his opponents, no matter who they are. Many people believe that this change is down to one person, his manager Sienna. You see Jason has always known what he wanted from life and that was to be famous. However he has often gone about things the wrong way. His former employers X3 (XXX Wrestling) Were the first company to bring Jason into the wrestling world. They knew that he had alot of talent but they also knew that he came with alot of baggage. He's not exactly the easiest person to control, he's a bit anti-authority at times. So the then X3 owner Faith decided that Jason needed someone to guide him someone to keep him on the right side of the tracks. Faith made a few phonecalls and hired Sienna as his manager/agent.
Jason wasn't too happy about this as you can imagine. He said that he "didn't need a babysitter" X3 thought otherwise and that's when the rift became apparent. From that day Jason lost all respect for the managment team at X3. He would turn up late for shows and would deliberately go against the companies orders. Ownership at X3 changed from Faith to Josh Walker and then to John Raide but it didn't matter who was in charge to Jason, he still hated the place. Eventually and after weeks of trying to keep it together X3 closed it's doors to the delight of Jason. He claimed that there lack of vision was there downfall. Jason believed that X3 held him back.
XRW (X-Rated Wrestling) was his next move. Where he instantly became a ratings hit. His debut was a 15 man over the top rope battle royal which he won after eliminating James Icon whom his next match would be against. At the XRW pay-per-view "American X" Jason tokk on James Icon in a Hard Knock VICE match. Which is a brutal match that involves a cage being placed outdoors with no ring. Inside that cage are various weapons and to win you either escape out of the cage or you are the last man standing. XRW haven't had the problems with Jason that X3 did but nobody in the XRW management team knows to this day where Jason is. They still have him as an active member on there roster eventhough he hasn't competed for a long time. Many people believe that the he has quit but just cannot be bothered to tell the XRW.
Several weeks after his last match for the XRW he appeared on Television for a little known company called NSW (No Surrender Wrestling) At their weekly show, Overdrive. Outraged by this XRW threatened legal action, stating that Jason was still under contract to them. NSW responded by releasing a statement declaring that all Jason's ties with XRW had been severed therefore he has everyright to sign a contract with NSW.
Jason's NSW career could soon be over after the superstar claimed that he is ready to quit. Source: ESPN.COM
The outspoken superstar was involved in a world title tournament match against John Murphy. The pair had won their respective matches to get to that match only for NSW owner James Higgins to announce that the match was now a triple threat. Erin Jacobs had been allowed to enter the world title hunt via the backdoor. Jason was furious, his temper got the better of him and it all came out at a houseshow in Denver where he stated that Erin Jacobs was a joke of a superstar and that he had absolutly no respect for him. He went on to say that James Higgins was a disgrace and he could stick NSW up his ass.
The triple threat match at Overdrive ended with Jason being pinned by Erin Jacobs. Jacobs and Murphy would now move on to NSW's pay-per-view Genocide to decide who would become the world champion. No suprise at the event as Jacobs won the match and now wears the coverted belt around his waist. On the same night Jason had the opportunity to make up for that defeat at overdrive when he took on David Black for the No Surrender championship but his head was never in the match and evidently Black picked up the win and the No Surrender title. After the Genocide pay-per-view Jason was backstage reportidly causing damage to NSW equipment. He hasn't been seen since that fateful night.
