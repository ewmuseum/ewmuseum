Paul Blair Name Paul Blair Nickname The Ruler, King of the Low-Down, good for nothing, evil tricks Height 6'5" Weight 275 lbs. Hometown River Falls, WI Manager/Associates Robin Cradle Alignment Heel .:Wrestling Style:. All-Rounder .:Entrance Music:. BlairVision Theme .:Description/Ring Attire:. Blair comes down with a robe that says "BlairVision" on the back. He takes this off before the match and has on red Blair t-shirt and blue jeans. He has Robin Cradle with him who comes down wearing a different colored tight skirt each week. .
.:Signature Moves:. DDT Figure-Four Leglock Full-Nelson .:Setup Move(s):. Can use anything but a simple clothesline usually does the trick. .:Finisher:. BLAIRKICK- Blair's version of a superkick. .:History:. This will be added later. Basically if you can think it, Blair's already done it. Blair is a man who's been in the business for 10 years as of the last week in August. He has changed many times since then. Blair started off the All American boy who came down bringing the American flag to his matches giving him one Patriotic feud after another. A few years later, Blair decided to join the corporate world and joined the Warriors of Darknes in the UWL. This version of Blair lasted until about 1998 or 1999. The next major change in Blair was he left the Warriors of Darkness and the UWL to come join the CWF. He had his first match on September 20, 1998 and defeated CWF star 2Badd. He would go on to win the Unified Title less than a month later. He has been back several times since then and always had some success. He's making his final return here this time around with the idea that he needs closure and it needs to end where his major success all started.
