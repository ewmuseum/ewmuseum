Chemical X Name Gary Scarletti Nickname Chemical X Height 6'5" Weight 279 lbs. Hometown Brooklyn, NY Manager N/A Alignment Neutral .:Wrestling Style:. Brawler, Boxer, Street Fighter .:Entrance Music:. "Dipset Anthem" by Dipset .:Ring Attire:. Chemical X regularly wears black or other dark street clothes. He doesn't dress like a street thug but more like a business man. Usually what he's wearing is described.
.:Signature Moves:. Right Hook: Chemical X's knock out punch, its basic and its simple but when he throws the right hook grown men fall.
Combination Move: Chemical X will grab his opponent and knee him in the face. Then while the opponent is still recovering he kicks them in the chest.
.:Setup Move(s):. N/A. .:Finisher:. THREE PIECE - Chemical X will fight his opponent into the turnbuckle. He will then start hooking left and right to the face of the opponent. Then just as the opponent beings to walk off the turnbuckle Chemical X will grab them by the abdomen and toss them right back into the turn buckle. He then draws back his right arm and punches the opponent in the jaw three more times knocking him on the ground.
.:History:. Chemical X was raised in the Brooklyn streets, adopted by an Italian Crime Family after his real family was gunned down protecting the same italian man. He was a street fighter, fought in the Notorious Brooklyn Fight Club, 'The School of Hard Knocks' he fought there from the time he was 14 to 18. He then left Brooklyn and became a military Man, he did 4 Years in the Marines. Most of what he dis is classified. While in the Marines and shortly after he got a Masters in History and Law. He's been wrestling ever since.
.:Accomplishments:. CWF National Champion
