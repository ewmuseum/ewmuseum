[Dan poses as a huge pyro blasts off in front of him. After it dies down, Dan continues his walk to the ring as the crowd still greets him with boos. Dan enters the ring, and poses again as a huge blast of pyro erupts from around the ring. He takes his belt off, and hands it to the referee.]
TT: Both men are in the ring now. What a showdown! CWF's two champions doing battle tonight!
BC: Lynch has this match, Teddy! I can just feel it! *DING! DING! DING!* TT: The bell sounds as Lynch and Sartyr lock up! [Lynch uses his size advantage, and pushes Sartyr back in the corner. The referee calls for the break, but Lynch pokes Sartyr in the eye.]
TT: Lynch with a cheap shot! [Lynch pulls Sartyr out of the corner, and snapmares him. He applies a rear chin lock, pushing his knee in to Sartyr's neck for pressure.]
TT: Lynch has the early advantage here, as he applies pressure to that rear chin lock. BC: Lynch is a great athlete, Teddy! Probably the best in the CWF today! TT: I don't know that I agree with that, but nevertheless, Lynch is a tough competitor! [Sartyr starts to get to his feet, and begins delivering elbows to Lynch's gut. He throws Lynch off of him, and in to the ropes. Lynch knocks Sartyr down off the rebound with a shoulder block. Lynch hits the ropes again, hops over Sartyr's body, hitting the opposite ropes, and is caught by a perfect dropkick.]
TT: Sartyr with an amazing dropkick! [Sartyr whips Lynch in to the corner, following in with a clothesline attempt. Lynch gets his boot up, and nails Sartyr in the face. Lynch boots Sartyr in the gut, and puts him in a powerbomb position.]
BC: Here it comes, Teddy! He's going for the D Bomb! [Lynch lifts Sartyr in to the air, begins running as Sartyr struggles, and just before he can drop him, Sartyr falls over Lynch's shoulders, and rolls him up with a sunset flip.]
TT: Sartyr with a sunset flip! 1 . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . And Lynch kicks out! TT: Sartyr with a near fall! BC: He ain't gonna win the match that easy, Teddy! [Both men get to their feet, and Lynch quickly drops Sartyr with a clothesline. Lynch drills Sartyr with massive rights as the referee tries to seperate the two. He gets back up, and starts stomping at Sartyr's head.]
TT: Lynch is pissed! He's beating the hell out of Sartyr! BC: This is great! Lynch is punishing Sartyr! [Lynch picks Sartyr up and whips him in to the ropes. Lynch bounces off the opposite ropes and attempts a clothesline. Sartyr ducks under and hooks in a sleeper hold.]
TT: Sleeper! Sartyr hooks in a sleeper! BC: No! He can't beat Lynch like that! [Just as it seems like Lynch is almost out, he backs to a corner, crushing Sartyr's body. He falls to the ground as Sartyr tries regaining his strength in the corner. Sartyr grabs Lynch as he begins to get up, and tries getting him in position for a reverse DDT. Lynch kicks back hitting Sartyr below the belt, but the referee does not see it. Lynch grabs Sartyr with a rollup pin, and gets his feet on the second rope.]
BC: Lynch has the pin! TT: He has his feet on the rope though! 1 . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . No! TT: Sartyr kicks out! BC: That was a slow count! [Lynch argues with the referee as he is upset with not getting the three count. Sartyr grabs Lynch from behind with his own rollup.]
TT: Sartyr has Lynch now! 1 . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . Kickout by Lynch! TT: He almost had him there! [Both men get back to their feet. Lynch runs in towards Sartyr, and is caught with an inside cradle.]
TT: He has Lynch again! 1 . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . And Lynch kicks out again! TT: It looked like he was going to get it there, Bobby! BC: Not even close, Teddy. [Lynch is to his feet first, as Sartyr gets up. Lynch nails him with a massive clothesline, causing him to flip over from the impact. Lynch picks Sartyr back up, and puts him in the powerbomb position. He lifts Sartyr up, begins running, and throws Sartyr over the top rope and in to the barricade!]
BC: The D-Bomb! He just threw Sartyr over the top with the D-Bomb! TT: Sartyr is hurt, Bobby! His spine made full contact with the edge of the barricade! [Lynch slides out of the ring. He grabs Sartyr and throws him face first in to the ring post. He grabs Sartyr again, and sets his face on top of the steel steps. Lynch climbs to the apron, and backs off to get some running distance. He runs along the apron and dives off for a headbutt. Sartyr moves and just before Lynch makes contact with the steps, Sartyr grabs the back of his head and drives him face first in to the steps.]
TT: My god, Bobby! That was some sick impact! BC: Lynch isn't moving, and Sartyr is barely able to stand! [Sartyr grabs Lynch, and pulls him over to the announce table. Teddy and Bobby move out of the way as Sartyr rolls Lynch on top of the table. He delivers three forearms to Lynch's face, then slides back in to the ring. He climbs to the top rope as Lynch lays lifeless on the announce table.]
TT: What the hell is he doing?! [Lynch dives off, and connects with the Tuck 'N Cover.] TT: Dear Lord! Neither men are moving! Sartyr just hit Tuck 'N Cover! BC: The ref is out here now, checking on both men! [Sartyr finally begins moving, as he slowly gets to his feet. He grabs Lynch and rolls him back in to the ring. He slides back in after Lynch and picks him up. Lynch pokes him in the eye. He grabs Sartyr, and drops him throat first on the top rope. He walks over to the corner, and begins taking the turnbuckle pad off. Sartyr gets up, and clotheslines Lynch from behind. Lynch turns around, leaning against the corner to hold himself up.]
TT: Lynch pulled that padding off, but Sartyr caught him! BC: Lynch looks like he's out of it, as Sartyr is waiting near the opposite corner! [Sartyr runs full speed at Lynch who is in the corner. He attempts a splash, but Lynch moves out of the way, and drives Sartyr's face in to the exposed turnbuckle. As Sartyr stumbles back, Lynch rolls him up, and extends his feet to the top rope.]
TT: Lynch has his feet on the ropes! He can't win like this! 1 . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . 3! BC: Lynch wins! The ref didn't see the feet on the ropes! TT: Sartyr was robbed! Lynch used the exposed turnbuckle and leverage from the top rope to beat the National Champion!
Announcer: Here is your winner, DAN LYNCH! TT: Sartyr's head is busted open after that shot to the exposed turnbuckle! BC: Either way, Lynch has beat him tonight, Teddy! [The lights in the arena suddenly dim, as Mark Xamin appears on the CWF-Tron. A smirk comes to his face just before he speaks.]
MARK XAMIN: It seems that I have had a thorn in my side the past few weeks. One of my...FORMER employees has decided to make a reappearance in MY company. Blood decided that he would interject himself in to one of my matches, injuring three of my superstars. The next week, he decided that he would try to ruin the match I signed for the National Championship! And last week, not only did he attack one of my up and coming superstars, but he also...as he put it...made a sacrifice out of Maniac! Well Blood, you want to play mind games, then keep your eyes on the ring!
[The CWF-Tron goes blank.] TT: What the heck is the boss talking about?! BC: Look at that Teddy! [A crucifix is slowly lowered towards the ring. Pinned to it is a woman, apparently unconscience.]
BC: That looks like Amanda Johnson, Blood's wife! [The crucifix stands in the ring, when suddenly Blood jumps over the barricade. He slides in to the ring, and instantly checks on his wife. He unties her from the crucifix, and makes sure that she is alright. Mark Xamin walks out to the front stage, as Blood and his dazed wife turn their attention to him.]
MARK XAMIN: You're not the only one that knows how to play mind games Blood! I've been running this damn show for over seven years! I'm not about to let some punk like you ruin it's success!
[Blood stares a hole through Mark, when suddenly, Amanda kicks Blood between the legs. Blood falls to his knees from the pain.]
TT: His wife just kicked him where it counts Bobby! BC: This is awesome! That punk is getting what he deserves! [Maniac jumps the barricade, and slides in to the ring. He picks Blood up, and boots him in the gut. He lifts Blood high in to the air, and drops him with the Headhunter, as Mark looks on with a smile. Mark slowly approaches the ring, as Maniac begins to tie Blood's wrists to the crucifix.]
BC: The boss has tricked Blood! This is great Teddy! TT: Looks like Mark is getting redemption for last week. [Amanda walks over to Blood, who is now tied to the crucifix, and slaps him across the face. Blood looks forward in disbelief as he struggles to get loose from the rope holding him to the crucifix. Maniac grabs Amanda, and kisses her, as Blood is forced to watch. Maniac then turns to Blood, and spits at his face.]
TT: What disrespect! BC: I have a new appreciation for Maniac! Last week, he was in a casket. This week, he spit in the face of the man who put him in the casket!
[Mark steps in to the ring, and begins choking Blood as he yells at him.] MARK XAMIN: How does it feel, Blood?! You want to screw with me?! It's time that you learned your lesson!
[Mark releases Blood, and nods to Maniac. Maniac slides out of the ring, and reaches under the apron. He grabs a gas can, and slides back in to the ring with it. Amanda and Mark smile as Maniac begins pouring the gas on to the crucifix. He throws the gas can aside, and Mark begins yelling at Blood again.]
TT: This is just sick, Bobby! BC: I'm loving this! I haven't found Maniac this entertaining since...well...I've never found him entertaining! But this definately takes the cake!
MARK XAMIN: Throughout the years Blood, I have terminated your career here in the CWF! And somehow, you keep coming back! Not after tonight, Blood! Tonight, I am burning your career!
[Mark pulls out a zippo lighter. He lights it up, and throws it at the base of the crucifix. The crucifix erupts in flames, as Blood desperately struggles to break loose from his binds. Mark signals for the crucifix to be lifted.]
TT: Dear god! Blood is going to burn alive! This has gone way too far! BC: Blood has crossed a line which he should have never crossed. As a result, he's going to get burnt! Hahaha.
TT: That's not funny, Bobby! Someone needs to help him! [Mark, Maniac, and Amanda smile as the crucifix is now high above the ring. "Play The Game" by Motorhead suddenly blasts throughout the arena, as the three in the ring quickly look towards the entrance. Jericho runs towards the ring, chair in hand. Maniac, Mark, and Amanda quickly escape through the crowd, looking towards the ring at Jericho.]
TT: It's Jericho! Jericho has come to Blood's aid! BC: Damn it! And I was just starting to enjoy this! [Jericho signals for the crucifix to be lowered. It finally touches back down in the ring, and Jericho quickly unties Blood. Blood's body slumps on to Jericho.]
BC: I think Blood is out, Teddy! TT: Jericho orders the EMT's for help. BC: Jericho has done the right thing here, Bobby! Blood may have done alot of evil things, but this is taking it way too far!
[EMT's rush to the ring, and strap Blood down to a stretcher. Jericho follows the EMT's to the back. The cameras follow backstage.]
TT: Stay with us fans! We're going to the back! [Jericho leaves Blood with the EMT's, who approach the ambulance. Maniac suddenly appears, as he attacks the EMTs.]
TT: Where the hell did he come from?! Maniac is beating the hell out of the EMTs! [Maniac grabs the stretcher, and wheels it over to a hearse. Sitting outside of the hearse is a casket.]
MANIAC: Time to go for a ride, Blood! TT: You've got to be kidding me! The man can't even defend himself! [Maniac rolls Blood in to the casket, and loads it in to the back of the hearse. He slams the door shut, and quickly gets in to the driver's seat. The hearse speeds out of the parking lot.]
BC: Looks like Blood is going for a ride with Maniac! TT: This isn't right, Bobby! What the hell is Maniac going to do with Blood?! We'll be right back after this commercial, folks! Stay with us!
COMMERCIAL BREAK [Fade back in to a shot of the ring.] Announcer: The following is tonight's main event! It is a fatal four way match scheduled for one fall!
["The One You Love To Hate" by Ro Halford blasts throughout the arena as Eric Dane walks out to the entrance area. The crowd roars with boos as multiple jets of pyro blast off in front of Dane.]
Announcer: Making his way to the ring. Hailing from New Orleans, Louisiana! Weighing in at two hundred fourty five pounds, "THE ONLY STAR" ERIC DANE!
[Dane continues his walk to the ring, taunting the crowd. He slides in to the ring, and walks to the center of the ring. He raises his arms high above his head, as pyro blasts off from each corner post. His music dies down as he turns towards the entrance area, awaiting his opponents.]
TT: Dane will be going for the World Heavyweight Championship at Summer Smash. But he better not look past tonight!
[The Blairvision Theme erupts in the arena as the crowd again falls in to a frenzy of boos. Blair and Robin Cradle walk out to the entrance area, as a jet of pyro blasts off on each side of Blair. Robin walks back to the back, as Blair continues his walk to the ring.]
Announcer: Heading to the ring. from River Falls, Wisconsin. He weighs two hundred seventy five pounds, "THE RULER" PAUL BLAIR!
[Blair slides under the bottom rope, and is instantly held back by the referee. He walks over to his corner as the music cuts, and he awaits the other two opponents.]
BC: It looked like Blair wanted to start this match early, Teddy! [The lights darken as "Symphony Of Destruction" by Megadeth hits the arena. A series of purple and green lights strobe at the entrance area as the crowd erupts in cheers. Mike Sloan walks out to the entrance area.]
Announcer: On his way to the ring, from Mobile, Alabama! He weighs in at two hundred fourty five pounds, "THE DARK HORSE" MIKE SLOAN!
[Flames erupt at the entrance area, and Sloan walks right through them. He walks down the ramp as the fans continue to cheer. He enters the ring, and waits in his corner for the last opponent to be introduced.]
BC: Sloan won't have what it takes tonight, Teddy. He's in the ring with three of CWF's greatest.
TT: Don't count him out, Bobby! I have a feeling that he will make a major impact in tonight's match.
["Believe" by Breaking Benjamin begins to blare in the arena as the lights dim. The crowd has mixed reactions as Tyrone Walker walks out to the entrance area. He poses for the crowd as sparks shoot up in front of him. Suddenly, a series of pyros blasts off all over the entrance area as Walker poses for the crowd.]
Announcer: Coming to the ring, from Detroit, Michigan! He weighs in at two hundred thirty pounds, "THE BLACK JESUS" TYRONE WALKER!
[Tyrone darts to the ring, and slides under the bottom rope. He instantly attacks Sloan, as Blair and Dane begin to exchange blows as well.]
TT: The bell hasn't even sounded, and these four men are already beating the hell out of each other!
[Sloan and Walker exchange blows, as Blair and Dane square off on the other side of the ring. Blair gets Dane up against the ropes and attempts the Blair Kick, but Dane quickly drops down and slides out of the ring.]
TT: Did you see that, Bobby! Not even a minute in to the match, and Blair has already went for the Blair Kick!
*DING! DING! DING!* [Walker gets Sloan back up to the ropes and attempts a clothesline, but Sloan ducks under and flips Walker over the top rope to the outside. Him and Blair look at each other as Sloan begins running towards Blair. He runs past him, and dives through the middle rope, hitting Dane with a plancha dive. Blair runs towards the side which Walker is getting up on, and hits him with a baseball slide.]
BC: This isn't fair, Teddy! It's supposed to be a fatal four way, but Blair and Sloan are working as a team!
[Blair grabs Walker, and slams him face first in to the steel steps. On the other side, Sloan whips Dane towards the ring post, but Dane reverses the whip. Sloan's skull bounces off the ring post and he falls to the ground. Blair throws Walker in to the ring, and Dane slides in on the other side of the ring. Blair picks Walker up, and Dane quickly hits Blair with a clothesline.]
TT: It looks like Dane and Walker are going to be teaming up here. [Dane pushes Blair back to the corner, as Walker follows over. The two men begins stomping away at Blair, as Sloan slides in the ring. Sloan runs full speed at the three men, and nails a devestating splash in the corner. Sloan grabs Dane out of the corner, and puts him in a powerbomb position. Before he can do anything. Walker grabs him from behind in a reverse face lock. Walker pulls Sloan down for a reverse DDT, but Sloan holds Dane's trunks during the move, causing Dane to be dropped with a piledriver.]
TT: That was nuts! Walker hit Sloan with a reverse DDT, and Sloan hit Dane with a piledriver at the same time!
BC: Walker turns his attention back to Blair, and recieves a boot to the gut! [Blair connects with a forearm to Walker's head, then pushes him in to the corner. He picks up Dane, and whips Dane in the corner. He smiles as he picks Sloan back up. He lifts Sloan in the air for a back body drop, but instead of falling backwards, he runs forward, forcing Sloan to dropkick the back of Dane's head. Dane and Walker crack heads together. Dane twist around out of the turnbuckle, and falls to the ground. Walker falls straight forward, hitting Dane below the belt with a headbutt. At the same time, Sloan falls forward with Blair, nailing him with a bulldog.]
BC: No! They can't do that to Dane! TT: Walker just hit Dane below the belt with a headbutt, and thanks to Sloan, Blair hit Dane in the skull with another headbutt!
BC: Sloan has the cover on Dane! 1 . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . . No! Dane somehow kicks out! TT: That was a close one, Bobby! [Sloan whips Dane in to the corner, then quickly climbs to the middle turnbuckle and begins pounding on Dane's face with right hands. Walker slowly gets to his feet and makes his way over to the same corner. He gets underneith Sloan, and lifts the man high on to his shoulder as Blair begins to get up. Dane gets himself to the top rope, and dives off for a spear on Sloan, as he falls towards the ground, Blair hits him with a massive Blair Kick in mid-air. Blair makes the cover on Dane.]
TT: Blair has the cover! 1 . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . . And Walker makes the save! TT: Blair would have had it if Walker wasn't there! [Walker throws Blair out of the ring, and slides out after him. He begins pounding on Blair, as Sloan slowly gets up. Sloan picks Dane back up, and drops him back to the mat with a DDT. He quickly covers Dane.]
TT: Sloan might have it here! BC: No way! 1 . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . . And Dane gets his foot on the bottom rope! BC: That's ring awareness, Teddy! [Sloan backs Dane in to the corner, and nails him with shoulder thrusts, as Blair and Walker continue to battle on the outside. Walker slams Blair's head in to the barricade, then whips him in to the steel steps. Inside the ring, Sloan nails Dane with a massive clothesline in the corner. Sloan lifts Dane up to his shoulders, and drives him to the mat with The Scorned.]
TT: That should be it, Bobby! 1 . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . . And Walker pulls Dane out of the ring! BC: That was a close one, but Walker was able to stop Sloan from getting the win! [Walker helps Dane to his feet, and both men are hit with a baseball slide by Sloan. Sloan grabs Dane and begins slamming his head in to the barricade, as Blair grabs Walker and rolls him back in to the ring. Blair follows him in, and whips him in to the corner. He hits Walker with a series of body shots, as Dane nails Sloan with a low blow on the outside.]
BC: Now that's thinking ahead! Dane with a low blow on Sloan! [Blair pulls Walker out of the corner, and again starts laying in a series of right hands. Dane lifts Sloan in to the air, and drops him with the Star Driver, leaving Sloan out cold on the outside of the ring. He slides back in to the ring, and admires his work on the outside as Blair nails Walker with a series of rights.]
TT: Sloan is out, Bobby! BC: That Star Driver is a devestating finisher! I don't expect that Sloan will make it back in to the ring anytime soon.
[Blair hits Walker with a clothesline before backing off. Walker gets back to his feet, and stumbles in to Dane. Dane quickly turns around, and is met with a Blair Kick. Walker falls through the ropes and to the outside.]
TT: Blair Kick! Dane didn't see it coming! BC: Blair has the cover! 1 . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . . . 3! BC: Blair did it! Blair just pinned Dane! TT: Dane is back to awareness now, and he can't believe what just happened! [Dane slides out of the ring as Walker gets up. He pushes Walker, and the two instantly are nose to nose.]
TT: This doesn't look good, Bobby! ["Play The Game" by Motorhead blasts throughout the arena as Jeff Jericho walks out to the entrance area, microphone in hand.]
JEFF JERICHO: That was one hell of a match! And Summer Smash, Summer Smash will be one hell of a pay-per-view! Not only will we see Eric Dane and Tyrone Walker fight for the World Heavyweight Championship, but I have thrown Johnny Keel from the match and put Mike Sloan and Paul Blair in to face each other in a number one contenders match!
TT: What an announcement! [Dane and Walker continue to stare each other down, as Sloan walks up the entrance area staring at Blair.]
TT: Summer Smash! Dane versus Walker for the World Heavyweight Championship! Blair versus Sloan in a number one contenders match! We're out of time folks! See you next week!
