Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Degenerate Wrestling Inc. introduced itself through a series of recruitment posts in November 1999, its self-described owner and publisher casting the promotion as an answer to federations run by people “too stupid to hire an accountant” or “too lazy to find the kind of athletes that know how to work a crowd as well as a turnbuckle.” He and his partner, an ex–Indy Car racer turned promoter, would not wrestle themselves but promised a regular on-camera presence on the promotion's own planned television show, alongside a competitive wage and full benefits for the athletes they signed.

Billing itself “By the Best, For the Best,” the promotion promised handlers full character biographies, custom entrance themes, and a small, selective roster, announcing its television debut for 9 December 1999.

A promotional short, “The Steamier Side of DWI Wrestling,” filmed at a hotel in Reno, Nevada, showed the promotion's two owners unwinding with its in-house lawyer and a secretary after the new federation's invitations had gone out — and closed on a hotel sign revealing whose room they had actually been using: “The Reno Hilton Proudly Welcomes Vince McMahon and Family.”

No further document, roster, or broadcast survives; the promotion's own planned December debut is not confirmed to have aired.
