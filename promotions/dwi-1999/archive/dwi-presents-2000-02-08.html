DWI Presents...

8 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— 8 February 2000 —

Okay, here it is. Kick back, open up a frosty one, put some classic rock on the ol' CD player, and read what e-wrestling shows are SUPPOSED to be like........ This is only the third broadcast by young upstart league Degenerate Wrestling Incorporated, however, if you are currently stranded in a has-been-never-will-be federation where their Pay Per Views pale by comparison, then you need to click on http://jackleer.tripod.com/ and give them the once-round. After you check out the site, and are suitably impressed, fill out an app - IF you are better then the average handler. DWI has a very skinny roster, compared to most efeds, but one look at the RP board, and the talent contained therein, and you will know why 80% of applicants are rejected.
