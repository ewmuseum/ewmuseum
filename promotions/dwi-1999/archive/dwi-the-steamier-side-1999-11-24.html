DWI Wrestling is a new promotion owned by Stevie Z and "Smutman" Jack Leer. While neither of these gentlemen will be competing in the ring, both will still continue to have live television exposure on the DWI Wrestling television show.
We are currently looking for charismatic and talented athletes who have excellent mic skills to add to the DWI roster. The promotion will begin airing on December 9th. All interested professional sports entertainers are invited to check out our promotion's official website at:
http://jackleer.tripod.com DWI Wrestling offers a competitive wage along with a full benefits package, as well as the opportunity to rub shoulders with some of the most talented personalities in the sport today.
