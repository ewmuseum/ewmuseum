DWI Still Turning Away Talent

28 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DWI Wrestling Bound to be imitated, bound to be innovative, and bound to be around a long, long time. Too many time have I seen promotions run by morons who couldn't balance a checkbook or weren't smart enough to read the fine print when signing a contract. Too many times have I seen these promotions close because the owners were too stupid to hire an accountant, too lasy to find the kind of atheletes that know how to work a crowd as well as a turnbuckle, or too stupid to book an arena before promoting a show.
It gets a little redundant watching the same things happen over and over. Everyone promises to be bigger than McMahonand Turner but they end up being smaller than the local high school wrestling team.
But not DWI Wrestling. Stevie Z and I may have just started hiring talent, but that doesn't mean we don't know what we're doing.
Before I give you what the marketing boys want me to tell you, let me just tell you about DWI Wrestling, and myself.
My name is Jack Leer, perhaps you have seen one of my many magazines on the shelves of your local adult bookstore orrented one of my videos at the local
Stop And Go, or perhaps you've read about my battles with the Supreme Court orwith Canadian Officials in your hometown paper. I have been called many things in my day, from smutmonger to skin-peddler to corruptor of Western civilization to champion of the under-class to true American hero, and I'd be a liar if Idenied any of them.
I started my professional wresting for one reason and one reason only, to stand up against the snobs who talk down to "white trash" like myself. But the things I saw that were happening behind the scenes are what really made me pissed. Corrupt management, incompetent business practices and some of the worst money management ever. How these people ever thought they were going to stay in business I'll never know, but they never lasted long. I might not have a degree in business but I know how to hire someone who does.
So I did just that, and I got together with this ex-Indy Car racer turned promoter named Stevie Z, and we created DWI Wrestling. Now I'm not looking for alot of staff, I've already got some great names in the sport coming aboard, but I am interested in finding some fresh perspectives, some 'new blood' if you will.
Please stop by our website and see what the promotion's all about and what we have to offer our athletes. We pay a verycompetitive salary with a full benefits package as well as offering you an opportunity to grow along with
DWI Wrestling.
http://jackleer.tripod.com/ -Jack Leer P.S. I included some material from the boys in marketing and promotions.
