DWI (1999–2000) — Card

Thursday Night Trauma

21 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 4 —

(The camera pans along East Washington avenue, just a few blocks away from the Tingley Coliseum Arena, location of tonight's D.W.I. venue. It's a quiet suburban neighborhood dotted with one-story ranch homes and well-groomed lawns. The sun has just gone down and a cool feeling of serenity envelops the peaceful community). (Dressed in the familiar spandex suit and bright red cape, LA RAZA walks down the street in the direction of the Arena. He whistles quietly to himself as he walks. Having just arrived from Phoenix via a twelve hour drive, boredom and anticipation has him alive with energy and ready to hop into the ring with "Total Elimination" Eli Flair. After the last couple of weeks, an evening in the ring is just what he needs to soothe his frayed nerves.) (Suddenly, a red pickup drives up and blares its horn.) BEEEEEP! (The lone luchadore turns his head as the driver leans out of the pickup truck's window. It's a young man wearing a D.W.I. baseball cap and a black leather jacket.) Driver: Hey, LA RAZA! (The Mexican daredevil points at his chest in quandary, as if there could be another man by the name of LA RAZA on this tiny, deserted street.) LA RAZA: ¿Sí? Driver: What's the deal!? They just cancelled the show tonight! I've been waiting since December for the D.W.I. to come to town! (The luchadore's eyes widen beneath his mask.) LA RAZA: ¡¿Qúe?! The show's been cancelled? Driver: Yeah! You been in a cave or something? LA RAZA: No, I've been on the road! What happened? Driver: I guess that Jack Assterson crashed his S.U.V. into a school bus full of kids and the D.W.I. execs decided to postpone the show! LA RAZA: ¡Por Dios! That's horrible! Driver: Yeah, it's a damn shame, but what about me?! I paid twenty bucks for those tickets! LA RAZA: Was anyone hurt? Driver: I don't know, man! All I know is that you guys better not cancel the show in Amarillo, or I'm going to write Jack Leer a letter! Got it? (The driver climbs back into his truck and squeals angrily down the road, leaving the luchadore choking in a cloud of noxious exhaust. LA RAZA, shocked at this surprising turn of events, turns around and reverses his direction, heading back towards the Interstate he recently left behind.) LA RAZA: ¡Ai carumba! No show. I hope no one was hurt. (A commercial DWI Wrestling commercial comes on, featuring a wide variety of official merchandise.) (The announcer voices over the shots of DWI official wrestler merchandise.) Announcer — That's right people, if you act now, and order today, we'll send you, for no additional charge, a official "Jake Cavalry — AWOL in the DWI" T-shirt! Fade to Black. (The screen fades out to white, and then back in again.) (An ambulance siren can be heard very quietly, as if in the distance. It slowly gathers volume, but all you can see is the Black screen. The screen fades slowly up to show the outside of a hospital Emergency Ward Ambulances Only area. Suddenly, an ambulance comes screaming around the corner up the street, towards the hospital. The streets are wet from the rain that has just stopped falling, and the reflections of the ambulance lights play off the shiny asphalt.) (The ambulance turns onto the hospital ramp, coming to a screeching halt at the hospital doors. We see a close up of the back of the ambulance, the rear doors filling the screen. The doors fly open with a boom, and a low bass rumble begins to be heard in the background. Doctors and nurses come rushing out of the hospital to assist the ambulance EMTs. A stretcher is pulled from the ambulance, wheels lowered and locked. The camera can't get a good enough look at the stretcher to see anything though...) (The stretcher is wheeled into the hospital, surrounded by doctors and nurses. One doctor is barking orders left, right, and center.) Doctor: I need 50 mgs of Thorazine, 25 mgs of Dopaditetrazan, and an IV drip, STAT! Nurse: Yes doctor, on their way. Doctor: Okay, everyone, down to TR1. Move, people. MOVE! MOVE! MOVE! (They wheel the stretcher into one of the emergency rooms, the doors flying open as they wheel the stretcher in. As the doors close, we see that it is called TRAUMA. As the doors slowly swing closed on their hydraulic hinges, the camera focuses in on the word. As the doors fully close, it becomes apparent that someone has spray painted something over the words. It now reads on the door.) THURSDAY NIGHT TRAUMA (The screen fades to white as the opening crash from the DWI Trauma Theme starts to play.) LEER PUBLISHING .... In conjunction with STEVIE Z PRODUCTIONS ....

(Alfred jumps down from the apron and nonchalantly strolls away, leaving the ref to turn back to the action, but Stanley has already jettisoned the chair, and is rolling Cupid back up into the ring.) (Once they are back in the ring, Stanley begins to walk in a circle around Cupid, waving to the fans and to Sarah. The crowd is booing hysterically, however Sarah is clapping and cheering. Her parents glance around nervously, not quite sure what to make of everything.)0 (Stanley pulls Cupid to his feet, and sets him up.) Samson — SUPLEX! (Stanley whips Cupid over and brings him back down to the mat with a THUD.) Samson — Now what the hell is Alfred doing?! (The camera shows that Alfred has not been idle over the last few minutes. With the help of someone else, he has managed to stack 2 tables on top of each other next to the ring.) Samson — Oh no, this CAN'T be good. Barry — I sure hope The Bringer of Lover can avoid those tables! Samson — <glancing sideways at Barry> I thought you HATED Cupid? Barry — Well, I do, but I hate that British goof a whole lot more! (Alfred jumps on the ring apron again, and again the ref runs over and begins yelling at him. Stanley calls for a microphone, which someone tosses him.) Stanley — I told you that the P.N.S. campaign was going to take more forceful action and it starts as of tonight. I will show this worm that it is not a good idea to try and blind Lawrence Stanley. So Cupid, an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth....happy landings from everyone at P.N.S. headquarters. (With that said, Stanley hauls back and begins to nail Cupid in the head with the mic. Three ear-splitting BOOMS! Come through the PA system before the sound guy manages to kill the mic.) (Stanley grabs Cupid and puts him into the 'Facedown' as the crowd boos mercilessly and violently.) Samson — Holy cow, sound like there's gonna be a riot here tonight! Barry — Hell we've already almost had two! (The lights of the arena go out again. The crowd goes silent.) Barry — NOW WHAT THE HELL!?! (Suddenly, the silence is broken by the entrance music of the WWF's Chyna - "Don't Treat Me Like A Woman") Samson — Don't tell me we have a NEW member of the DWI Roster! Barry — IT CAN'T BE!! IT CAN'T BE!! (The crowd cheers loudly, thinking that the Ninth Wonder of the World has signed with the DWI.) (To their dismay, out of the back steps a much smaller, more feminine-looking woman dressed in a short black leather mini skirt and a low-cut black leather belly shirt (in all actuality the top looks like a sports bra). Her face is unseen however as it is hidden behind a black leather mask.) Samson — IT ISN'T!! Barry — So who the hell is this then, and how much are we gonna be sued for using the music? (She walks down to ringside and up to where Alfred is standing.) (With a roar, Stanley drops Cupid and rolls out of the ring, running towards where they are standing.) (The woman pulls out a can of pepper spray from her top and points it at arm's length towards the butler, ready to spray just as Stanley reaches them.) Samson — Oh no, LOOK OUT! (Stanley grabs Michelle's arm and twists it hard, causing her to scream in pain.) (Stanley twists her around in an arm lock, and reaches out and yanks off the woman's mask to reveal her identity.) Barry — HOLY COW! (It is none other than Michelle Solomon. She looks at Stanley with hatred. Stanley looks at her with shock, but it is quickly replaced with a look of rage when Stanley realizes that Michelle intended to blind his beloved friend. He gives Michelle's arm another hard twist, which causes her to trigger the pepper spray.) (Going very wide of her intended target she nails one of the audience members square in the face.) Barry — <laughing> I bet that guy doesn't think those are such great seats now! (Michelle drops the pepper spray.) (The man dressed all in blue flies backwards, clutching at his eyes. His wayward elbow flies out and nails the cup of beer of the person next to him, spraying it in the face.) Samson — I have a very bad feeling about this.. (The guy and his girlfriend are both dressed head to toe in red.) (They immediately grab him and he starts punching him, and his girlfriend begins kicking the poor guy, who is still clutching at his eyes.) (Before you can blink, another 15 of the guys dressed in blue and another 20 of the guys in red have joined the fray.) Barry — Ummm, Sam? You ever seen the movie "Colors" with Sean Penn and Robert Duvall? Samson — Yeah, Barry why? Oh no, you don't think-- Barry — Something tells me we have a full-fledged war between the Bloods and the Cryps breaking out right under our noses. Samson — Good God Almighty, I think you're right.. (Fights have now broken out all over the audience now, with the Bloods and the Cryps beating the crap out of each other and out of anyone who gets between them. The group who had been scuffling earlier comes flying out from behind the curtains, still fighting, both with each other and with DWI security. "Big" Bobby Clobber comes running out from behind the curtain as well, and surveys the arena.) (All over the place it is total chaos. Between the battles being waged, and the mass confusion of people running for the exits, all is in disarray.) (Stanley shoves Michelle hard, and she bounces off the guardrail and falls to the floor in a heap.) (Bobby shakes his head and speaks into his walkie talkie. Immediately the members of DWI security cease their fighting a start to run back up the ramp, joining Bobby at the top. Bobby speaks once more into his walkie talkie again, and he and the entire security team leave behind the curtain.) Samson — Barry, are you thinking what I'm thinking? Barry — CALL 911?! Samson — No, Barry, I'm thinking about GETTING THE HELL OUT OF HERE!! Barry — I'm with you my little friend! Samson — <hurriedly> Thanks for tuning in people, be sure to watch next.ahh, hell, LET'S GET GOING! (He and Barry throw their headsets down and start making a beeline for the exit. A couple members from the security team run out and assist them back up the ramp.) (The fights are growing larger now, and it looks like half the arena is duking it out with the other half!) (Sirens can be heard in the backgroundand the sounds of helicopters.) (By now Stanley, Cupid, and a host of other wrestlers are involved in the fracas as well, standing side by side, differences temporarily forgotten in the face of the new danger.) (Searchlights from helicopters begin to pierce threw the skylights in the roof, giving the place a totally surreal look. With the sound of smashing glasss, police men on ropes begin to descend into the mob, large white letters denoting SWAT on the backs of their bullet-proof vests.)
