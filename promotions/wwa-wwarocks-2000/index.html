Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Wrestling Association, which its chief executive also called the World Wrestling Alliance, was a booked federation run by e-mail from its site at wwarocks.cjb.net. Its handlers dated it to the fall of 1998, and a recruiting post of 2001 said late 1996; its cards went out to a mailing list and onto the newsgroup, a weekly Monday Night Live and, by September 2001, a Friday WWA Live.

Its shows ran as scripted arena television, called by Rich Manning and Sammy Knight, with full entrances and jumbotron vignettes; its headwriter built the matches from the strategies and oncards its handlers sent in, and its War of the Words roleplay packets filled the days between. Lady Red was its on-screen owner.

Its 2001 story was the PTA, the Prime Time Assassins, led by Crimestarter Jim, a three-time WWA Heavyweight Champion, beside Xavier Stone. At One Night Only, its summer pay-per-view from Melbourne's Rod Laver Arena, Jim and Stone beat Rosco Riggins and Celestial Warrior for a title shot, and Bedrock, the British Behemoth, beat them both to keep the WWA Heavyweight title; the Frat took the tag titles from the Blonde Brigade, and Jayson Carter joined the PTA that night.

WWA Live from Knoxville on 21 September 2001 opened with a tribute to the victims of the attacks of 11 September. By then Bedrock's son Rizer had joined the PTA too; Rosco Riggins laid its members out backstage one by one and kept his International title by making Rizer submit, and Jim and Stone took the tag titles when the referee who called the finish revealed a PTA shirt.

How it ended is not in its own surviving record, whose last document is a roleplay packet of 28 September 2001. A wrestler biography at the WWO, captured in 2003, says it closed its doors after a three-and-a-half-year run.
