War of the Words, 21 September 2001

Well, let's see what a few of our WWA Wrestlers have to say this week: (The Blue Screen of Wisdom flashes on) 1)Ktulu and Heather 2)Down South Connection 3)The Vindicator 4)Crimestarter Jim 5)Bedrock (WWA Heavyweight Champion) 6)Rosco Riggins (WWA International Champion) On with the show... ================================= KTULU AND HEATHER ================================= [Scene fades in. Ktulu and Heather are sitting on a sofa, looking at a blank TV] Ktulu: Wart Gunn? Heather: That's his name. Ktulu: That's a funny name. Heather: You do realize we were only able to purchase 30 seconds of air time for this promo. Ktulu: How much time is left? Heather: About 19 seconds. Get talking. Ktulu: OK. [Ktulu sits there, just scratching his chin.] Heather: Fifteen seconds. You better say something bad about Wart Gunn real quick. Ktulu: I'm thinking, I'm thinking! Man, I wish we had more time. Heather: Look, Wart Gunn is a waste of space! He doesn't do anything! Four seconds. Ktulu: OK. Wart Gunn, I'm going to pound your face into Hamburger! There will be no Hamburger Helper for your ugly face when I'm- [Whoops. The thirty seconds are up and the scene goes black. Ktulu is too poor to afford more air time for now. Oh well. SAYONARA!] ==================================== THE DOWN SOUTH CONNECTION ==================================== [The WWA camera begin's to roll. The scene is the inside of a very small dark room. The room has one light and it is hanging on a string from the ceiling. There is one small squared table in the center of the small room. On two ends of the table opposite one another sitting in there chairs starring at each other are none other then both members of WWA's newest tag team, Down South Connection. "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock is sitting with his body turned towards the camera. Morlock is wearing a pair of dark blue jeans and a light blue colored shirt. Kenneth Morlock is an individual with a decently nice build. He stands six foot three inches tall and weighes in about two hundred and thirty pounds. He has his short brown hair in it's usual perfect part to the side of his head. His hazel/brown eyes are focused right into the eyes of the other member of Down South Connection, "The Expert" Matt Allen. Allen is sitting with his back almost totally turned away from the camera. The camera man takes a few steps over and is now right almost dead center between them. Matt Allen is wearing a pair of black jeans and a dark red shirt. Allen is a huge individual. He stands at a towering six foot eleven inches and tips the scales at around the three hundred five mark. Allen has medium length dirty blonde hair pulled back in a pony tail and his dark brown eye's are focused right into Kenneth Morlock's eyes. The room is completely silent as Morlock begins to speak.] "TNL" KM: Fear!? "TE" MA: Anxiety!? "TNL" KM: Apprehension!? "TE" MA: Uneasiness!? "TNL" KM: Common goal? "TE" MA: Feeling and emotions that come to mind when the rest of the WWA roster realizes what they are stepping into now that Down South Connection has entered there midst! "TNL" KM: You know WWA, this would usually be a time where another person or team would be running down a list of past accomplishments to make themselves sound more than what they are. "TE" MA: Just looking and listening to use you can see that we dont need any small talk to back up what we are. Have we proved time and time again that we are the hottest tag team to hit the world since Salt and Pepper? Damn right. "TNL" KM: Have we won more tag team gold than Mr. T, wears around his neck? No doubt. "TE" MA: The thing that set's us apart from anyone else, and we do mean anyone else, is the fact that not only are we the best, but we damn sure know we are the best. True and true, not to be dimmed for anyone. We ride this horse as high as we can, and dammit we DARE anyone to come KNOCK US OFF THIS HIGH HORSE! "TNL" KM: You see the horse we ride is a horse that will ride us right into the spotlight here in WWA. It is a horse that will ride us right to the WWA Tag team gold! And that there is more than a promise. Because by the time we are done, it will be more like a way of life. A life that not many will want to lead. But it will be a life that I will damn sure plead guilty to on all counts. "TE" MA: Where does this life in WWA start? With a tag team called Dark Ages. "TNL" KM: Dark Ages? They must be referring to the time's before Down South Connection was formed. Because trust me, tag team wrestling, was, and I am not from France but excuse my French, <beep> up. "TE" MA: He is not just referring to WWA in that instance. Though it should be mainly focused on WWA because looking at teams like Dark Ages, it can damn sure be said true and true. You see El Diablo and Apocalypse. You have both been caught up in one hell of a heap of trouble. How exactly does it fel knowing that you have been led to an ultimate slaughter? "TNL" KM: Slaughter is actually an understatement when you look at the total picture. Hell, I wouldnt even say picture when it comes to business dealing with Down South Connection. I would say more like Masterpiece! Because the beating we will be handing to El Diablo and Apocalypse, Dark Ages, will be one hell of a masterpiece that sets the balls rolling and the pens in motion all across WWA. TE" MA: Hell yes! A motion that set's us right into the top tag team in WWA. SO note this Dark Ages, you make the wrong decision and step into that ring with us, and it might insight one hell of a riot. A riot that only we control and a riot that only we take part of, so watch the steps you make Dark Ages, they may be your last. [The WWA camera begins to fade out to black as Kenneth Morlock reaches up and turns out the light and the room goes black.] ================== THE VINDICATOR ================== (The scene opens up to the Vindicator compound on this brisk September day. In New Jersey, signs of summer have been slowly dwindling over the past few weeks, but a large American flag flies proudly in the wind. The camera cuts to the large lavishly decorated dining room of the Vindicator, where Vindy and Veronica are about to have lunch. Veronica is showing Vindy several different catalogs with decorations inside.) Veronica: "Vindy, having an entire arena decorated is more challenging Than it first appeared to be. Are you sure you still want to have this Wedding at the PPV?" Vindicator: "Of course, what better place could we have the wedding in?" Veronica: "I don't know....how about a big church?" Vindicator: "A big church can't hold 15,000 people. We need that space to fit your family and the thousands of Vindicatorians that will be in attendance." Veronica: "Yes, but a lot of these decorations weren't really designed to be in an arena." Vindicator: "We'll make them work!" (Just then Mrs.Thistlewick enters the room with a tray of sandwiches.) Vindicator: "Thanks Mrs.T...Hey, by any chance, do you know how to make Monte Cristos?" Mrs.Thistlewick: "I'm sorry, Mr.Vindicator. I don't know how to make those." Vindicator: "Darn. You know who used to make the best Monte Cristos?" (The doorbell rings. Vindy gets up and runs to the door. He opens it and to his surprise.) Veronica: "Kingsley!" Vindicator: "V, how did you know?" Veronica: "Know what?" Vindicator: "That he is here right now!" Veronica: "He is? I was answerring your question about the Monte Cristos." Vindicator: "Yup! He is here. Kingsley, I'm so glad to see you. Please come in." (The former butler enters the compound which he has cleaned from top to bottom for many loyal years.) Kingsley: "Master Vindicator, it is nice to see you too." Vindicator: "Kingsley, this is Mrs.T..err...Mrs.Thistlewick. She's been filling your shoes for the past year." Mrs.Thistlewick: "Delighted to meet you. You've got to be a saint for being able to put up with this man for so long!" (Kingsley's eyes light up as he sees this elderly woman who is about the same age as him.) Kingsley: "You can call me Chester." (Mrs.Thistlewick flashes him a smile.) Mrs.Thistlewick: "My name is Agnes." Vindicator: "I never knew your name was Chester...." (Out of nowhere, a noticably slimmer Diddy jumps into Kingsley's arms.) Vindicator: "Oh yeah, here is another guy who was filling in your shoes before Mrs.T came along. Meet Diddy!" (This catches the proper butler off guard a bit.) Kingsley: "My word....nice to meet you....Diddy." Vindicator: "Why don't you join us for lunch." Kingsley: "That would be splendid. Can I first sanitize my hands?" Vindicator: "Ha ha ha...Come on, my doorbell is sanitary....Oh right, the monkey. Sure, go right ahead, you know where the bathroom and kitchen are. Take your pick." (Vindy enters the living room again.) Vindicator: "Hey, you didn't start eating." Veronica: "Of course not...That would just be rude." (Moments later Kingsley enters the living room and Veronica stands up.) Vindicator: "Kingsley, this is my fiancee, Veronica." Veronica: "Nice to meet you." Kingsley: "Likewise. Master Vindicator, you've made quite a choice." Vindicator: "Thanks. I thought you were going to ask Veronica what she was getting herself into." (Veronica chuckles.) (The three sit down at the table.) Kingsley: "Agnes, why don't you join us?" Mrs.Thistlewick: "Sure..." Vindicator: "No way, she's working....Just kidding!" Kingsley: "You always had that twisted sense of humor, Master Vindicator. I'm glad to see you haven't lost it." Vindicator: "Yes, well since she's sitting I guess you can whip us up some Monte Cristos...and don't burn them this time!" Kingsley: "Pardon me?" Vindicator: "I'm kidding again!" (Vindy smiles.) (Time passes by as the four chat at the table.) Vindicator: "So Kingsley, how about coming back here to the Vindicator compound? How about coming home?" Kingsley: "I don't know...I really like the family I am with now though. I'd hate to disappoint them." Vindicator: "They're a family, but you're family...You're in my family." (Mrs.T and Kingsley are exchanging glances with each other, as Vindy and Veronica pick it up.) Veronica: "Say Vindy, I was supposed to pick up my wedding dress today? We should probably go get it." Vindicator: "I thought you had your...uh...Yeah, we should go right now." (The happy couple leaves and lets the two housekeepers continue their conversation.) Veronica: "Did you see how Mrs.Thistlewick was looking at Kingsley?" Vindicator: "I thought she went through menopause already." Veronica: "Even if she did, older people have feelings too, you know." Vindicator: "Yeah, I guess you're right. Let's go for a drive." (The two get into the Golden Viper parked in front of the compound and leave. Time passes by, several hours, as a matter of fact. The Vindicator and Veronica arrive back at the compound. Vindy opens the door and lets Veronica in first.) Vindicator: "We're home...Hello?" (They don't hear anyone at first.) Vindicator: "That's odd. Where can they be?" Veronica: "Hello?" Vindicator: "Let's split up. I'll check upstairs...You check downstairs." (The two split up. Veronica checks the kitchen, living room, dining room, and the sauna, but they're not there. Vindy checks the bathroom and then goes to Mrs.Thistlewick's bedroom.) Vindicator: "Oh GOD!!!" (Vindy catches Kingsley and Mrs.Thistlewick doing the unthinkable in her bedroom. Of course, none of this is shown to the viewer, merely implied by the look of horror on Vindy's face.) Vindicator: "I'm blind...I think I am blind!" (Vindy stumbles back and towards the stairs. He catches himself on the bannister just before falling down. Veronica rushes up to help Vindy.) Veronica: "Vindy, what happened? You sounded like you saw the grim reaper!" Vindicator: "V...I saw something more horrific...." (Vindy is hyperventilating.) Vindicator: "I...saw....Kingsley....and....Mrs.T...." Veronica: "And?" Vindicator: "And...they...were....making babies!" Veronica: "What? Oh dear!" (A few moments pass and a clothed Kingsley and Mrs.Thistlewick come out.) Mrs.Thistlewick: "Mr.Vindicator, are you all right? We heard you scream." Vindicator: "I saw...you two....you know!" Kingsley: "You still want me to work for you again." Vindicator: "Yeah, you dirty old man...but keep the lewdness to a minimum!" (The scene fades to black as Kingsley and Mrs.Thistlewick have smirks of their faces for once, as Vindy rubs his eyes and tries to regain his vision.) ================== CRIMESTARTER JIM ================== (The scene opens up in Knoxville, Tennessee. There is all kinds of bright orange flags flying around because the University of Tennessee resides in Knoxville. It is not football weekend in Tennessee but the spirit still lives on. There are plenty of people out on the town on this Thursday night and Crimestarter Jim and Johnny are among them. You see the two members of the team, walking around the campus of UT. It is about 7:56 p.m. There aren't too many people walking around the campus but there are a few. Some of them are in classes and some of them are just in their dorms or at parties, since UT is the number one party school in the country :0) CRIMESTARTER: "I really hate wearing this crap on my nose!" (Crimestarter's nose is still bandaged up from the headbutts from Bedrock last week on Live) CRIMESTARTER: "I am going to hate wearing that damn mask tomorrow night!" JOHNNY: "Yeah, I would hate it too. I think you need to wear it though or it will be worse man." CRIMESTARTER: "That's alright because Bedrock paid the ultimate price and we won't see him for sometime, which I am quite happy about!" JOHNNY: "You know that Rosco is the number one contender for the WWA Heavyweight title?" CRIMESTARTER: "Yeah, I realize that. I didn't think that the prestige for that title could get any lower, but if Rosco gets it will just go downhill. That title will come back home to the "PTA" one day but right now I am completely focused on tomorrow night because it is going to be a night that the WWA will never forget!" JOHNNY: "The "PTA" could definitely make a huge impact tomorrow night in the Thompson-Boling arena. UT won't know what hit them after tomorrow!" CRIMESTARTER: "But the WWA will definitely know what hit them. Last week, we took out their Heavyweight champion and this week we are going to take 3 titles from them and really hit them where it hurts!" JOHNNY: "I really hope that Rizer comes prepared because Rosco might make his life a living hell tomorrow in that ring!" CRIMESTARTER: "I think the big man will definitely come prepared and he will walk out as the new WWA International Champion. Xavier had his shot at the title but Rosco tried to choke him out and just like that match, I will not let that happen! Rosco can try and cheat all he wants, but it will not happen!" JOHNNY: "Well, here is where it is all going to take place!" (Jim and Johnny walk up to the Thompson-Boling Arena and stand at it looking at the outside of the building) CRIMESTARTER: "Yup, Johnny tomorrow night we are going to make history. It will be the biggest night of title changes in the long history of the WWA! I can't believe how long the WWA has been running strong and I have been here since the beginning!" JOHNNY: "Yes, we have. We have ventured around the wrestling world but we have turned the WWA into the "PTA" stomping grounds!" CRIMESTARTER: "The federation will officially have a name change in due time as well, when my complete takeover takes place. I don't see anyone stopping me from achieving my goal. Especially not members of the Frat or Mark Haley and Celestial Warrior.." JOHNNY: "well, Jim remember that he actually changed his name last week!" CRIMESTARTER: "Oh yeah, I about forgot about that stupid name change. Does he think that the deeper darker image is going to scare me and the rest of the "PTA"? Him and I go back a ways, I remember facing him a couple times for the WWA title and he actually got lucky once and defeated me. That doesn't happen very often but it did on that night at the PPV! His Forces of Light failed the first time around at stopping the "PTA" and they once again failed this last time because he went out and got guys like Noah Prejudice, Mark Haley and Rosco Riggins. You have to have better players than that to stand up to the "PTA"!" JOHNNY: "I don't see why anyone tries to compete with the talent that we have, there isn't anyone better at finding talent than you!" CRIMESTARTER: "No there isn't is there? I have found talent in unique places and surprised the hell out of people as well as far as bringing talent into the "PTA". I can tell you right now that one person that I would definitely not go after right now is Mark Haley! The man left his dying mother to come back and fight against the "PTA", why kind of man is he anyway? JOHNNY: "I couldn't believe it when I heard him say that!" CRIMESTARTER: "That is just sickening, that he would leave his mother. Just like that was sickening his wrestling has been the same and he won't be any trouble tomorrow night. He will wonder why he ever agreed to the match in the first place. Just makes our gaining of the WWA Tag team titles that much easier!" JOHNNY: "You and Xavier definitely have the momentum going into that matchup!" CRIMESTARTER: "I think the Frat have been doing more celebrating than they have anything. We haven't seen them in quite a while but I am sure that they will show up tomorrow since this is such a party campus. I can tell you one thing though, they won't be celebrating after tomorrow night. They will be licking their wounds." (Jim continues to look at the building) CRIMESTARTER: "Let's go get a drink, Johnny." Doctors an' nurses runnin' 'round telling orders or followin' 'em. Total blood chaos by th' looks of it. But what th' 'ell am Ah doin' 'ere? This nurse is comin' up t' me an' talkin' t' me. Ah can see 'er mouth move but Ah'm buggered if Ah know what she's sayin'. Somethin' 'bout loss o' blood an' if Ah can 'ear 'er. She thinks Ah'm unconscious. What if she thinks Ah'm dead?! "Er." Bloody 'ell, Ah'll 'ave t' do better than that if Ah'm gonna convince 'er that Ah'm awake. "Er. Ah'm not dead yet." What's she bloody smilin' fe? Well, Ah guess it is funny. The idea o' me bein' dead. Still. Ah wouldn't mind knowin' what 'appened. Why Ah'm in 'ospital an' all. "What 'appened." An' then Ah remember. CRACK! An' again. CRACK! The boy. Me son, me own son. Just a few hours ago ye tied me oop an' whipped me 'alf t' death. Silly dogs, ye should 'ave done th' other 'alf. Like a pack o' dogs, Hyenas, ye surrounded me and picked me away, piece by piece because Ah'm the big elephant an' ye knew none o' ye could o' taken me down alone. Ah'll 'ave the scars t' remind me. ========================================== ROSCO RIGGINS (WWA International Champion) ========================================== [A podium sits, the background highlighted by a magnificent cathedral wall, light pouring through stained glass windows, through to the dimly lit church. Dust flutters through the light, creating dancing shadows upon the walls and floor. The church itself is empty, row upon row of pews empty. Bibles lay in their holders, some upon the lightly cushioned seats but each and every one is devoid of humanity... That is except the last one. In it, leaning forward with his forearms resting on his legs, is Rosco Riggins. Former NATV Champion. Former Prime Time Assassin. Current International Champion. The man who took Bedrock to the limit...the only man ever. And now the man how carries the WWA upon his shoulders. A task not insurmountable but a heavy responsibility to say the least. He wasn't always in this position. He wasn't the man who everyone thought would have to stop the PTA. That lay in another man. A task that with a crack of a whip...many, many cracks, was transferred upon this man. It isn't a task he asked for. Then again this isn't a task he is going to throw away to someone else. He knows he has to do it, no one else. Why him? Well who else is there? Mark Haley? Absorbed by personal problems. Noah Prejudice? Plagued to the Nth degree and taking a well needed rest. William Karnes? Blonde Brigade? Bill Bartlett? All could help. All could be a major part in the war about to come. But none are ready. Maybe someone from the past. Maybe someone here but hasn't stepped back into the limelight. Maybe someone to come? No. He has to do it. He knows it. He has known it for a long time, he just has never been able to live up to it. But now...now he has no choice. He has to live up to it. Some would say his first step is next week. His first battle as a leader...as THE leader of the WWA. He will take William Karnes and The Fallen Angel into battle against the forces of the PTA once again. There will be no turning back. No questions asked. All three men know what they have to do. But...if this is so, then why is this man, Rosco Riggins, in a church? Finding faith? Praying? Reaffirming? Maybe he is simply thinking. Maybe he is simply remembering. Maybe he is simply reaffirming. ========================== IF YOU WANT TO HOST WAR OF THE WORDS, SEND JOSH AN EMAIL AT [detail withheld] ========================== ***COPYRIGHT WWA 2001***
