WWA Newsflash, 23 September 2001

************************ WWA NEWSFLASH September 23rd, 2001 ************************ ***NOTES FROM THE HEADWRITER*** First off. I am sending the newsflash out on Wednesday and this will be the same day from now on as far as the newsflash goes. From now on we will be doing the scheduling two weeks in advance. So, pay attention to the schedules. We will send out another schedule on Wednesday afternoon for the following week. Send any match requests to Josh at [detail withheld] or Wallie at [detail withheld] After looking at our roster, both divisions are open! If you know of any good roleplayers, send them to the WWA. We definitely need to spice up the tag division again, we were building it up and we had a couple good teams quit this last week. RECRUIT, RECRUIT, RECRUIT!!! We are doing better in this and I appreciate the hard work of Wallie! :0) When sending in oncards and strats make sure you are running spell check and checking your grammar. I know that Charles used to mention this a lot and I think that I am going to have to start doing it too. It makes the shows look even better if everything is spelled correctly and is done neatly. We have a couple characters in our federation that we use as company people but they actually have handlers. So if you wish to use the following people in your flashes/oncards contact the appropriate people: LADY RED (OWNER OF WWA- Contact Jerry at [detail withheld] LEVIATHAN - Contact Charles at [detail withheld] If you have any arena's you would like to see the WWA visit, please send them to me. WHERE TO SEND STUFF FOR YOUR WRESTLER: (WotW held late Thursday's and Monday's) [detail withheld] Strats/Oncards [detail withheld] (Strats are due on Tuesday's and Oncards are due on Thursday nights) If you have any questions, contact me at [detail withheld] [name withheld] *Headwriter/CEO* ****************************************** !!!WWA LIVE!!! Orlando, Florida - TD Waterhouse Centre FRIDAY SEPTEMBER 28th ****************************************** Dark Matches (Summary) - Ktulu [vs] Wart Gunn - Johnny Jobbs-Ville [vs] Gargoyle ------------------------------------------------- BLAINE RICHARD [vs] EL PHANTASMO MEXICO LOCO - Richard, a new star to the WWA looks to establish himself with this match. EPML looks to be but a speed bump on his way up to the top. DOWN SOUTH CONNECTION [vs] DARK AGES - The Down South Connection are being heralded as the next big thing among the tag division. Standing in their way is the team that have had a stay longer than anyone else on the roster, the Dark Ages, a test to say the least. NATV TITLE MATCH LAWRENCE "THE MACHINE" SAMPSON [vs] BILL BARTLETT - Sampson has been on a roll, defeating such well knowns as Mark Haley. Bartlett is a long time veteran not about to give up his first WWA title. With the NATV rules in place you know this will be a barn burner! WWA MAIN EVENT! ROSCO RIGGINS/FALLEN ANGEL/MARK HALEY [vs] CRIMESTARTER JIM/XAVIER STONE/RIZER - With both tag team and international champions in this match up you can't but know this will be one for the ages. Every member of this match has a past. The question of who will prevail lies in the future ============================== !!!WWA LIVE!!! Charlotte, NC - Charlotte Coliseum FRIDAY October 5th =============================== SCHEDULE TO BE ANNOUNCED THIS WEDNESDAY =>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=> SCHEDULE DISCLAIMER =>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=>=> All cards are subject to change. The WWA reserves the right to change any cards as it see fit. This includes the actually matches and the order that the match appears on any card. The number one reason a card or match will be moved is when one or both parties don't roleplay for the match. This won't get the match canceled unless both parties have quit or been removed from the fed. However, it does mean that an advertised main event match will be moved up on the card if one party doesn't RP. We will no longer reward someone with a main event match if they don't put the roleplaying effort forward. So for a main event to remain a main event *BOTH* parties must RP for it or it will be moved accordingly. It takes two wrestlers to make a great main event. ************************* UPDATED WWA ROSTER ************************* **SINGLES ROSTER** Bedrock (WWA World Champion) - Bill "Sidewinder" Bartlett(NATV Champion)- Blaine Richards Crimestarter Jim (1/2 WWA Tag Champions)- Jayson Carter - Johnny Jobbs-Ville- Ktulu- Lawrence "The Machine" Sampson- Noah Prejudice - Rizer - Rosco Riggins (International Champion) - Shaun Borders - "Showtime" Mark Haley - The Fallen Angel - The Vindicator - Xavier Stone (1/2 WWA Tag Champions) - Judas - **TAG TEAM ROSTER** The Blonde Brigade- The Dark Age - The Frat - Down South Connection - © 2001 - WWA Productions ===== This message comes to you courtesy of the Headwriter and CEO of the World Wrestling Association, [name withheld].
