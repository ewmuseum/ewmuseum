War of the Words, 24 September 2001

24 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, let's see what a few of our WWA Wrestlers have to say this week: (The Blue Screen of Wisdom flashes on) 1)The Dark Age 2)Anastasia Romanov 3)Rosco Riggins (WWA International Champion) 4)The Fallen Angel 5)"The Machine" Lawerance Sampson 6)Crimestarter Jim (1/2 WWA Tag Team Champions) On with the show... ================= THE DARK AGE ================= (The scene opens up in a cafe where The Dark Age are talking over some coffee) EL DIABLO: Hey did I tell ya, I got a call from her last night. APOCALYPSE: Her? EL DIABLO: You know...HER. APOCALYPSE: Oh, her...what did she say? EL DIABLO: That shes coming. APOCALYPSE: Coming where? Here? EL DIABLO: No, its obviously to sophisticated for you. (Apocalypse looks puzzled at the use of big words, but El Diablo keeps talking) EL DIABLO: Ok, we need to really get the Blonde Brigade and I mean bad...got any ideas? APOCALYPSE: Hit them with a car? EL DIABLO: Its been done. APOCALYPSE: Break their knees with steel chairs? EL DIABLO: Been done. APOCALYPSE: Throw them off the Jumbo Screen thing? EL DIABLO: Been done. APOCALYPSE: Well hell I don't know, damn. EL DIABLO: Well, maybe we could beat them if there was only one left. APOCALYPSE: What do you mean? EL DIABLO: Come closer and I'll tell ya. (Apocalypse leans in and they begin to whisper as the camera fades to black...) ======================== ANASTASIA ROMANOV ======================== (The scene opens on the busy streets of downtown Los Angeles, outside of "Madame Rachel Larue's House of The Occult". Anastasia Romanov stands there with her arms folded across her chest, her blue eyes fixed on the shop's front door. The willowy beauty wears a pink, baby doll T-shirt and form-fitting, baby blue denim jeans. She completes the look with matching, open-toe heels, a Prada purse slung over her shoulder. Her blonde hair is tied in a messy ponytail, a few stray wisps framing her face. Ana pauses to take a deep breath before entering the darkened store. Upon entrance, the leggy blonde is greeted with more than a few familiar sights, including shelves and displays filled with mysterious books, potions, and objects. Ana picks up one of the potions and inspects it, reading the label.) Ana: (reading aloud) "Madame Larue's Arousal Potion: Guaranteed to Unleash the Sex Goddess Within"? (chuckles, placing the potion aside) She can't be serious. (shakes her head) This is insane. I don't even know why I bothered coming here. Voice: It's because you wanted the truth, child. (Ana gasps in surprise and turns to see Madame Rachel Larue standing before her. Rachel Larue is a striking woman with long, auburn hair and violet eyes, appearing much younger than her years. She wears a form-fitting, black dress and heels, a crystal pendant draped around her slender neck. She eyes Ana coldly, her arms folded across her chest.) Ana: Madame Larue, I'm sorry. I didn't know that you were standing there. Madame Larue: (sniffs) There's a lot that you don't know. Did you come to my shop to make fun of me again? Ana: No, not at all. I apologize if I offended you. It's just that...well...this whole occult thing is very new to me and still a little hard for me to swallow. (Madame Larue nods) In actuality, I came to your shop for help. After my last visit, you stated that you knew some things about my father. I was hoping that you could offer a bit of clarification on that. Madame Larue: (smiles) As I predicted, the skeptic has become a believer. Ana: (nods) My father deserted my mother and I when I was just a child. After that, she destroyed every reminder of him, not even leaving so much as a photo around. I've tried to ask her about him but she refuses to discuss him. All I know is that his name is Dmitri Romanov and he was an immigrant from Europe, without any family in the United States. I once hired a private investigator to find him but came up empty. So, when you mentioned knowing something about him, I jumped at the chance to see you again. Madame Larue: How long have you been so curious about your father? Ana: (sighs) For as long as I can remember. My mother and I have never had the best of relationships and have almost nothing in common. So, I always wondered what my father was like. I've always wondered if it was from him that I got a lot of my characteristics. Madame Larue: And you've also wondered if he thinks about you as much as you think about him and if he misses you, as well, correct? Ana: (nods) I think any child that grew up without a parent would wonder the same thing. (Madame Larue grabs Ana by the hand, a small smile moving across her lips.) Madame Larue: Come, child. Let's see if The Goddess will allow me to share my gifts with you this afternoon. (Moments later, the two women are seated across from one another at a small, round table in one of Madame Larue's secret rooms. The room is very dark, the scent of jasmine in the air and a variety of candles offering the only source of light.) Madame Larue: Give me your hand. (Ana nods and obliges. Madame Larue grabs Ana's hand and turns it palm up.) Madame Larue: As you have probably surmised, I am going to give you a palm reading. This is a very old practice and was taught at many institutes of higher learning. (Ana merely nods, her eyes focused on Madame Larue.) Hopefully, this will enable us to learn more of your father, if The Goddess deems fit. (Madame Larue whispers a small prayer before beginning her work. Madame Larue begins to touch and inspect Ana's hand, recognizing the shape of the palm and fingers. She looks at the finger prints, palmer lines, ridges, and the skin's color.) Madame Larue: What did your mother tell you about his desertion? Ana: She said that he didn't want to be saddled with the responsibilities of having a child so he left us. Madame Larue: Well, it appears that she lied. There was another reason for his departure and it stemmed from an infidelity. An infidelity regarding your mother and another man. Ana: (laughs bitterly) Why am I not surprised about that? Madame Larue: (holds up her free hand, signaling for silence) It would seem that your mother also told another lie. She told your father that you were not his child, that you were the child of her lover. Ana: (gasps) What? How could she do that? Madame Larue: Your father left. He was heartbroken and never once came in contact with your mother again. (She pauses for a moment) There...there isn't much else left. There is one small thing but I'm not sure what it means. Ana: What? Please, tell me what it is. Madame Larue: One word seems to be coming to my mind: Miami. Ana: Miami? Madame Larue: (shrugs) I'm not sure what it could mean but it popped in my head a few moments after reading your palm. (frowns as she releases Ana's hand) I'm sorry that I couldn't be of more help. It appears that my gifts failed me today. Ana: (smiles) No, you were wonderful. Thank you so much. I learned more than I thought I would. But I do have one major question. When you read my palm, were there any signs indicating whether my father was dead or alive? Madame Larue: Sadly, I'm not sure of that. The fact that all of the information came so quickly gives me hope that he may indeed still be out there. But I can't give a definite answer to his fate. Ana: Thank you. That's all I needed to hear. (Ana reaches in her purse, producing a handful of bills. Madame Larue throws up her hand.) Madame Larue: Save your money. I did what I did because I wanted to help you, not for profit. Ana: Thank you very much, Madame Larue, but I can't leave without paying you. It wouldn't be right. Madame Larue: Your respect was payment enough. Besides, I have a feeling you will need all of your money and resources for the days ahead. (Ana merely nods as the two women stand to their feet. As Madame Larue walks Ana to the door, one thought flashes through Ana's mind: Next stop: Miami.) TBC? ========================================== ROSCO RIGGINS (WWA INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION) ========================================== One... ___THWACK___ [The sound of blunt impact across skin reverberates through the black screen.] By one... ___THWACK___ By one... ___THWACK___ By one, until there was no more. I told you Jim...I _TOLD_ you if you wanted war you were going to get it. I promised you Jim. I promised you and your little group. I promised and if there is one thing I do not do is break my promises. [The black starts to take substance, eventually fading into a wide smile. A wide smile full of white teeth. That is all, just the smile and just the mouth, the voice coming from this very mouth. In the back ground the light tapping of skin upon something unknown continues quiet and steady.] First it was your muscle Jim. One shot ___THWACK___ Another... ___THWACK___ ..and they were taken care of, simple as that. Then it was your boy wonder. Your star pupil. Jayson Carter...The Standard. ___THWACK___ Simple as that. Then Jim...it was you. You and your running mate Xavier Stone. ___THWACK___ ___THWACK___ Only then did the world see who it was Jim. Only then did they know who managed to take apart the PTA in the span of a single evening. The man who beat your monster. The man who destroyed your four hundred and thirty pound monster. One again Jim, I kept my promise, I always do. In the end Jim you got your licks in. I backed you into a corner and Like the wounded dog you are, you fought back. You won your gold. Your won your precious tag titles Jim. Shows perhaps there is at least an iota of skill in your little support group. [A slight chuckle] That's fine Jim. That's fine. But I will make one more promise old friend. This....this is not over. [The camera pans back from the smile to a black background. On this background stands Rosco Riggins, himself dressed all in black setting a most surreal scene itself. All that is really visible is his head, his wide smile and his hands. His hands..in which he holds a thick oaken axehandle, cradled in one palm, a deathgrip on the handle.] No Jim, this isn't over. ___THWACK___ Simple... ___THWACK___ as... ___THWACK___ that. ================== THE FALLEN ANGEL ================== [Scene opens up somewhere in downtown Los Angeles, outside a small complex of apartment buildings. It is late at night, and the area in which these buildings are in is not exactly the safest place in the world to be. Not surprisingly, there are very few people on the streets outside the building. However, two people are seen, and not surprisingly, they are easily recognizable to us.] [The first person is the Fallen Angel, dressed in his customary all black. He is wearing black boots, black pleather pants, and a black leather jacket. His black and crimson hair is tied back in a ponytail, and despite the late hour, he is wearing his dark sunglasses. Over his shoulder he carries a black duffel bag.] [Walking aside the Fallen Angel is Runaway. She is wearing black sandals, khaki pants, and a form-fitting leather jacket. Her long blond hair is tied back in a ponytail as well. While the Fallen Angel walks with a certain poise in this dangerous part of town, Runaway seems much less secure. She is clinging onto the Fallen Angel's free arm, and is constantly looking back over her shoulder.] [As the two reach the door to one of the buildings, the Fallen Angel notices that the door is already open, instead of being locked like it should be. He shrugs, then pushes open the door. Walking down the long hallway, occasionally a head peeps out of a door to see who has entered the building, but then pops right back in when they realize that it is the Fallen Angel.] [Finally, the Fallen Angel and Runaway reach the end of the hall, and the Fallen Angel unlocks the door to that apartment. The apartment is small, and spartanly furnished. A couch, doubling as a bed, takes up one wall, while a small TV takes up another wall. The apartment does not have any windows (not surprisingly, considering its location), and is lit by a single lightbulb hanging from the ceiling. Off of this living room are a small kitchen and the bathroom, making up the rest of the apartment. Fallen Angel shuts the door behind him, and tosses his duffel bag on the floor. He walks into the kitchen, speaking to Runaway as he does so.] FALLEN ANGEL: "Make yourself at home." [Runaway takes a quick look around the apartment, and almost imperceptably shakes her head. She crosses the living room, and sits down on the couch.] RUNAWAY: "You live here?" [Fallen Angel returns from the kitchen with two sodas, and tosses one to Runaway. He sits down on the couch next to her.] FALLEN ANGEL: "It's not much, but I'm rarely here. I have better places to be...more important things to focus my mind on." [The Fallen Angel opens and downs his can of soda in under ten seconds, then crumples up the can and hurls it across the apartment. Runaway gets a concerned look on her face.] RUNAWAY: "Are you alright?" [Fallen Angel shakes his head slightly.] FALLEN ANGEL: "It's just...Friday night. Another innocent soul giving into the corruption of the dark side. The damned fool..." [Runaway tries to comfort the Fallen Angel.] RUNAWAY: "There was nothing you could do..." FALLEN ANGEL: "That's exactly the problem. I need to be able to do more...after what happened to Rhapsody, I swore that I would no longer just be able to stand by and watch - and that's exactly what I ended up doing. What is wrong with me?" RUNAWAY: "There's nothing wrong with you. Look, I've only known you for a few short weeks...but in that time you've saved my life, and I owe you a debt I can never repay. You can't save everyone...but by saving just one life you're making more of a difference then most people can ever hope to make." [Fallen Angel shakes his head.] FALLEN ANGEL: "I still feel as if I need to do more...I'm fighting a losing battle here, and now that i've become a Fallen Angel, I feel as if I'm fighting the battle alone." RUNAWAY: "You're not fighting alone, and you never will be fighting alone. This battle may take time...and it will take time...but in the end, light will prevail. I know it will." FALLEN ANGEL: "I pray that you're right...for all of our sakes." RUNAWAY: "Well, look, you have another chance this coming Friday..." FALLEN ANGEL: "I know, and I can't afford to waste this chance. I've blown it too many times in the past. I have to win this time. If I don't win...if the light doesn't win...the results could be catastrophic." RUNAWAY: "You can do it...I believe in you." FALLEN ANGEL: "Thanks...I just don't know if I believe in myself anymore." [Runaway is not sure how to respond to that, and the scene is slowly immersed in a deep silence. Fade to black.] ================================== "THE MACHINE" LAWERANCE SAMPSON ================================== (Recorded moments after The Machine's match on WWA LIVE! after turning his back on the Dark Age, we see Nick Myers standing in front of the camera, with a microphone.) Nick: "Nick Myers here, just dispatched by the higher-ups in the WWA to get a word from Lawerance Sampson as soon as possible about the treachery he implemented into his six-man match tonight." (Just then, through the curtains, storms "THE MACHINE" LAWERANCE SAMPSON! He is wearing a pair of black, Zubaz workout pants, black elbow pads, white and blue athletic shoes, and white tape around his wrists. He is sweating somewhat from his encounter just moments ago. He is definitely IRATE!) Nick: "Mr.. Sampson! Mr. Sampson! Can I have a word wit-" Sampson: "The nerve of those Dark Age bastards! I can't believe it! What were they thinking?! I CAN'T BELIEVE THEY DID THAT TO ME!!" Nick: "But, Mr. Sampson, it was YOU, not Dark Age, that perpetrated some underhan-" Sampson: "Nick, shut up. Are you trying to tell me that they did nothing WRONG?!" Nick: "We-" Sampson: "I SAID SHUT UP!" (Nick's mouth shuts quickly) Sampson: "I can't believe those no-good, disgustingly UGLY, no-talent losers! What were they thinking?! Treating The Machine like that. Are you going to look me in the eyes, Nickie boy, and tell me they did nothing wrong?!" (Nick doesn't say anything. Lawerance grabs him by his collar with his right hand) Sampson: "Well, answer me, you minimum-wage warthog!" Nick: "I ju-" (Sampson tosses Nick away from him, into the wall, causing Nick to slump down to the ground. Lawerance picks up the microphone and looks into the camera) Sampson: "The Dark Age ... BAH! They treated me like I was frickin' Rosco Riggins out there! Don't they realize who I am?! I am LAWERANCE SAMPSON! I am THE MACHINE! *I* am the star out there ... Not them! They don't have the charisma of The Machine. They don't have the raw, GOD-GIVEN TALENT that The Machine has. They aren't the stars in the ring ... THE MACHINE IS! I am the center of attention out there. No one wants to see Dark Age get their asses whipped. They want to see the Machine in that ring. They want to see The Machine smacking around little punks like William Karnes. They want to see the Blonde Brigade get their collective ass handed to them. They want to see Bill Bartlett get his head shoved so far up his own ass he can enter an amateur contortionist contest and be disqualified on the account that they don't accept professionals! THAT is what the people want to see ... Rather they admit it or not, The Machine is the STAR of WWA LIVE! In fact, it's no longer WWA LIVE. No ... From here on out, we're going to call it THE MACHINE HOUR! Got it?! It's THE MACHINE HOUR!! We all know that The Machine equals ratings ... The Machine equals chubby, stupid WWA fans in the seats ... The Machine equals fat, horny mommas salivating over his young, virile, gorgeous body ... The Machine equals THE STAR OF THE FRICKIN' SHOW!! Dark Age, you made a mistake tonight ... You didn't do what you were told. You paid for your mistake. The Machine got his revenge on you not listening ... From here on out, Lawerance Sampson decrees ... I OWN THIS SHOW!! *I* am the star here ... No one else. All eyes are on me." (Lawerance throws down the microphone as the camera zooms in on Nick, who is still sitting in the floor, and begins to fade. Suddenly, the camera is jerked back around.) Sampson: "Weren't you paying attention?! THE CAMERA LOVES ME!! KEEP THE CAMERA ON ME AND NOT THAT STUPID LITTLE THING SITTING IN THE FLOOR!! YOU GOT ME?! Oh, you idiot ..." (Lawerance kicks the camera man, causing him to drop the camera. Lawerance grabs the camera man by the head, and slings him down the hallway, and then kicks the camera, breaking the glass.) [STATIC] Sampson: "I AM THE STAR!!" [BLACK-OUT] ============================================= CRIMESTARTER JIM (1/2 WWA Tag Team Champions) ============================================= (The scene opens up backstage at the Thompson-Boling Arena in Knoxville, TN after the latest Friday Night Live. The fans are filing out of the arena as are the wrestlers in the backstage area. They are all talking about the events that took place earlier in the evening and there is a lot to talk about. This is a night which saw every member of the "PTA" taken out by Rosco Riggins, a night when William Karnes walked away from the WWA, possibly forever. Plus in the main event we saw Crimestarter Jim and Xavier Stone walk out with the WWA Tag team titles and once again, Crimestarter Jim took a member of the WWA family and brought them into the "PTA". Everyone is talking about what happened on the night besides the "PTA", they are in their locker room celebrating the events that took place. You see inside the "PTA" locker room both members of the Dark Age, Jayson Carter and Sasha, Rizer, Johnny "The Lawyer" Cochran and the new WWA Tag Team champions, Crimestarter Jim and Xavier Stone) CRIMESTARTER: "Did I not tell everyone that we would walk out with some of the WWA gold tonight?" (Crimestarter pops open a bottle of champagne and pours a glass for everyone as he pushes one of the Tag team titles up on his shoulder) CRIMESTARTER: "Xavier and I took the WWA Tag team champions who are probably now partying at some fraternity and we made them look like the losers that they are. They defeated two women too win the tag team titles and if you can't beat two women you shouldn't even be in the WWA!" (The Dark Age begin laughing at the remark from Jim) CRIMESTARTER: "What the hell are you two laughing at?" (Jim points in the direction of Apocalypse and El Diablo) CRIMESTARTER: "Three matches in a row, the Blonde Brigade have defeated you guys in some manner and it has to stop! Those women weigh a total combined weight of 250 pounds while you guys weigh an astonishing 829 pounds! What the hell is wrong with that picture? You guys should be pushing those girls around like they aren't even there." (El Diablo quickly chimes in) EL DIABLO: "But Jim, we had them tonight. That bastard Sampson walked out on u.." CRIMESTARTER: "We will take care of that little twerp in due time. I really saw a lot of good things in him going into that match but after what he did tonight, my thoughts on him have definitely changed. But back to the issue at hand. You guys need to find a way to put those women out of there misery. Next week, you have to wrestle the new tag team, the Down South Connection, I don't expect anything less than a win. After that happens, I would suggest that you find the Blonde Brigade and show them what kind of men you really are!" (Apocalypse and El Diablo nod their heads at Jim and continues drinking their champagne as the door swings open and in comes the newest addition to the "PTA" team, referee Josh Phillips and Jim immediately shakes hands with him and pours him a glass of champagne) CRIMESTARTER: "I must say Josh, that was so beautiful what you did tonight. No one expected you to come down and make the call on the Fallen Angel tapping out. You have only been with the WWA for a little less than a month and you have shocked more people in a month than guys like Mark Haley have done in 3 years!" JOSH: "Jim, I had to come down to that ring tonight because I didn't want to see any kind of injustice done in that ring. Xavier had the X-tension locked on the Fallen Angel and Austin Cooper was not doing his job and was lying on the outside of the ring. I just had to come down and do something and I did the right thing!" CRIMESTARTER: "That you did man, The Fallen Angel was tapping out and we just needed someone to make it official and you were the man to do it. It is great to have you aboard and there are definitely big things in store for you as the official "PTA" referee!" JOSH: "I was called to the WWA offices after the match was over, they weren't too happy with me, but I told them to kiss my a** and walked out. They are not going to tell me what I can and cannot do. I am "PTA" inside and out!" (Jim smiles and him and Josh hit glasses as they continue to drink champagne) CRIMESTARTER: "Xavier, you and I are going to bring some life back to these titles. The value of these titles has been diminished over the last couple months because we had two women holding them, we had the Frat holding them, we have had The Destroyers of Darkness hold them and tonight we immediately brought new life to them!" (Xavier nods as he pushes the other tag team title up on his shoulder) CRIMESTARTER: "Now, we have to hold onto them as long as possible. We will take on all comers and show them exactly what the "PTA" is all about!" (Jim takes another drink) CRIMESTARTER: "There are a few people in this federation that definitely have huge marks on their asses now. Rosco Riggins just made his target a little bit bigger tonight after attacking all of us in the backstage area! I hope everyone does know that he attacked us all from behind because that is the kind of man he is!" (Every member of the "PTA" nods their head in appreciation for what Jim is saying) CRIMESTARTER: "He thinks that by attacking us from behind, the wrestlers in the WWA are going to start looking up to him because their champion is gone. It won't happen, he just don't have the respect of anyone in the backstage area because he only cares about himself. No one is ever going to look up to him. Next week, we are going to have the opportunity to put Rosco in his place during that big 6 man event. The WWA thinks that if the continue to put Mark Haley and the Fallen Angel together that they will be able to provide some spark for the WWA. Both of those guys showed this last week that they don't have what it takes anymore to compete with the "PTA"!" (Sasha stands in the corner talking to Jayson Carter as Jim continues to talk) CRIMESTARTER: "Next week, we are going to have to be as good as we possibly can and take out Riggins, Haley and The Fallen Angel. We showed Bill Bartlett this past week what happens if you f**k with the "PTA" and next week we are going to do the same thing to those three guys." (Jim points to the Dark Age) CRIMESTARTER: "And you guys are going to do the same thing to the Blonde Brigade. Right?" (Both members of the Dark Age nod their heads) CRIMESTARTER: "Ok, let's get the hell out of this joint and go celebrate somemore!"
