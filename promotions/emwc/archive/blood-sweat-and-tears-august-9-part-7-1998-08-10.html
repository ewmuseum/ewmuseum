Blood, Sweat, and Tears, August 9: Part 7 of 13

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Taylor and James just lie there, motionless. The crowd is in sheer silence as they both lay there, their chests heaving... We hope. Suddenly, a battered shape emerges from the wreckage... Taylor tries to cover his face, so Casey punches him in the chest, causing
Taylor to cover his chest. James takes that oppurtunity to pound Taylor's face again!]
JS: James just punishing Taylor now! He's pounding those fists into him... RB: I had fists pounded into me once... TM: How did it turn out for you, Mox? RB: WARDEN!!! [Taylor is lifted to his feet and hit with a short clothesline, followed by a kneedrop. James lifts Taylor up and SLAMS him to the concrete!
All the while, they're battling forward, towards the exits.] JS: Some hot action going now... RB: Hot action? WARDEN! [The camera pans out to see the whole commotion. James and Taylor are two small figures, only picked out from the crowd by the spotlight and the fact that they're sharing punches, not popcorn.
Taylor gets up and slams a fist between James' eyes, which is returned promptly. Taylor sends a wild boot out, which is caught by James. James slams
Taylor's knee into the corner of a chair belonging to an audience member. This draws quite a few boos as James quickly grabs a hurting James and hits a reverse DDT _right_on_the_concrete!_]

The guardrail nearly collapses from the impact and then really does buckle when
James charges in with a cross body press! The sound of the steel collapsing is deafening, and the pop that ensues is twice as loud. James picks Taylor up and hits a big DDT on Taylor... RIGHT TO THE GUARDRAIL!]
JS: MY GOD! JAMES HAS DECIMATED BOTH TAYLOR AND THE GUARDRAIL! [Security tries to stop the fans from rushing the ring, and succeed to an extent. A wild male fan, dressed in nothing but a Kevin Slater t-shirt, runs through the ring and is quickly brought down by security as James takes Bobby
Taylor down with a big running powerslam in the aisle!]
TM: BEST DAMN FANS IN THE WORLD, STEGGY! THE BEST! RB: WOK! WOK! WOK! [James and Taylor, adrenaline running high now, trade blows all the way up the aisle. He tackles James and tries to stuff the foam heart in Casey's big mouth as coins and other small projectiles rain down on him!
Suddenly, James rips a chair out from under a spectator, sending the chair leg into Taylor's mouth. Taylor flies back but stays on his feet as James grabs some nachos from a fan, and after sampling the EMWC's finest prop, slams the rest in Taylor's face!]
RB: FOOD FIGHT!!! [At that moment, the blinded Taylor is handed a soda, which he throws in James' face! James is stunned as Taylor leaps in his direction, smashing into him with a clothesline.
Taylor wails away on James with fists, stopping only to wipe the cheese off of his face. Casey lifts a knee into Taylors groin as they both get to their feet and then James carries Taylor over with a powerslam.]
JS: They're about 3/4 of the way to the exit now, folks. We have a cameraman on hand if they do leave the bowl of the arena!
TM: And knowing these two, they will! [Taylor is lifted up with a huge high-angle bodyslam! James picks Taylor up and kicks him, staggering Taylor backwards. He kicks Taylor in the kneecap, sending
Taylor sprawling forward. But James catches Taylor, hooking his arms. He turns around and hits a big underhook suplex, sending Taylor flying through the exits! James follows him as does the cameraman!]

James. But Casey ducks, and Taylor's legs slide through the bars on the guardrail, crotching himself.
Taylor's eyes roll back into his head as Casey James peels him off of the rails and drags him up the stairs...]
JS: And they're going up the stairs again! [Up the 100 level... Then to the 200's... And finally, with great effort, James drags Taylor up to the 300 level, the cheap seats. They battle in the concrete aisles, the fans huddled outside peering in through the glass, trying to see the spectacle.]
TM: Oh goody! They're re-entering the bowl of the arena! And you know how steep it is up in the 300's!
JS: Very steep, Todd. And the steps are tiny. RB: And those are the seats where all the riff-raff sit! JS: MOXY! RB: That's the name, don't wear it out! [James tosses Taylor down the short, steep aisle of the 300 section, and Taylor rolls into the short concrete barrier. Security is on edge as Taylor is kicked in the groin and stomach by James. The crowd, looking on, is almost silent as they fear what could happen any moment now...
... And then, a collective gasp rises from the huddled masses as James lifts
Bobby Taylor high in the air.]

There is a resounding thud as James' chest hits the concrete.] TM: NO!!! JS: TAYLOR GIVES HIM A LIFT AND JAMES CRASH LANDS! [James is barely moving as Taylor rushes down the stairs. Taylor find James on his hands and knees, trying to regain his senses...]

BOBBY TAYLOR: Smooth as a baby's ass... Too bad I have t' wreck it! JS: HE WOULDN'T! TM: Mox... The scary part's over... [... He pulls the hammer back and...] RB: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! [... DRIVES it through the wood onto James' head! We follow Taylor down the dim hallways, through the main floor, where Taylor stops to steal a hotdog.
He walks to one of the entrances to the bowl of the arena, and walks down the same aisle that they left through. The guardrail is back up and the fans are going wild as Taylor brings James down that aisle.
The throngs of fans reach to touch the two men, and security tries to clear a path. Taylor dumps the near-dead James over the guardrail and climbs over himself. The crowd is on their feet cheering as Taylor tosses James in the ring.] JS: Ladies and gentlemen, this one is over. A tremendously hard-fought battle, it's a shame it'll end the way it did.
[Taylor climbs into the ring and drags James out to the centre of the ring.He looks at James, battered and bruised, down for the count... And then covers, he himself battered and bruised, almost unconscious.]
JS: ONE! TWO! THRE -- NONONONONONONONO! [The crowd gives a surprised pop as James lifts a shoulder!] TM: AMAZING! CASEY JAMES IS ALIVE! RB: We are all, Todd... Except for the zombies... And that Deathbringer guy. TM: GO AWAY! [Taylor gets up and puts his hands on his hips, positively perplexed. He takes a breath, a struggle at this point, and lifts James up.
He hooks the head of James under his arm...] JS: HERE IT IS! HERE IT IS! CATTLEBUSTER COMIN' UP! [But before he can hit it, James pushes him to the ropes. Taylor is about to bounce off the ropes, but instead hits the referee!!!
The two fly over the top as James slides out of the opposite side of the ring.He reaches under the ring, and pulls out a bottle of the Kessler's alkyhol.
He slides in the ring and opens the bottle, takes a swig, and seals 'er up, waiting for BobbyTaylor.]
TM: Now THAT is what I call a true American hero! Half-concussed, been through hell... And _still_drinking!_ Brings a damn tear to my eye, Steggy... It really does.
[And then, what transpires next seems to pass almost in slow motion. Taylor slides into the ring... James gets into a three-point stance... Taylor stands up... James charges and... S M A S H ! ! !] JS: HE HIT HIM WITH THAT BOTTLE OF KESSLER'S! [The bottle shatters into thousands of pieces, some stuck in Taylor's face, on impact. The crowd is booing like crazy as James drags Taylor to the centre of the ring and covers.]
TM: There is no way Taylor will EVER get up! And look who's come to! [The referee has just awoken. He slides into the ring and sees all the glass... ... And shrugs. There's no way he can know what happen, though he's probably made a good guess, so he has not choice but to clear some glass away and make the count...]
TM: ONE! TWO! T H R E E ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! [The bell rings as James rolls off of Taylor. Ken Graham's voice erupts over the PA system...]
KG: Here is your winner..... CASEY JAMES! JS: Casey James shattered that bottle over the head of Bobby Taylor and he has won this match. Taylor's head and body is covered in Kessler's whiskey, and
Casey James, although completely exhausted appears to be thrilled at what he just did.
TM: Casey James just showed Taylor what it takes to be the Outlaw! You gotta do anything you can, anything you have to do….and Taylor just couldn't pull the trigger.
JS: Casey James is looking around the arena, soaking in the boos. Oh no…he spotted Taylor's wife and kid in the front row. Look at the grin on his face….now he's asking for the mic. He's got the mic…but now he's digging in his boot for something. What's he got now?
TM: Oh my god! He's got a damn cigarette lighter! He's got a goddamn cigarette lighter! (James flips open the lighter, and holds it over the unconscious and alcohol soaked Taylor.)
CJ: Hey little lady….they say Bobby here is one of the sport's hottest rising stars….well, he's about to get a hell of a lot hotter!
(Taylor's wife screams out in terror and hops over the barricade, sliding into the ring to cover her husband's prone frame.)
CJ: Isn't this just about adorable? Boy….it's pretty hot in here. Any of you people feel the heat rising? No? Hmmmm….(igniting the lighter)….how 'bout now?
(With one beefy arm, James reaches down and pulls Taylor's wife to her feet.) CJ: Excuse me miss….wouldn't want to singe that pretty little dress of yours. (And in one of the most horrendous acts ever seen in an EMWC ring, James drops the lighter.)
JS: TAYLOR'S ON FIRE!!!! TAYLOR'S ON FIRE!!!!!!!! THE OUTLAW HAS BEEN SET ON FIRE BY CASEY JAMES!!!!!!! (An alert ringside attendant rushes into the ring where he is able to extinguish the blaze before it does too much damage. Taylor's wife collapses on the canvas in tears, screaming her husband's name. With a wicked grin,
Casey steps through the ropes, and out to the floor, walking over to where
Taylor's son is seated.)
CJ: Hey junior….dry those eyes! Uncle Casey's here now! (The crowd roars it's disapproval of the "Blackheart" as he continues to live up to his name.)
CJ: Well, kiddo…your daddy may have lost another one. But I'll tell you somethin'….he sure was a house of fire in there tonight.
(James begins laughing uncontrollably as security begins to usher him away from ringside, and a medical crew enters the ring to take care of Taylor's burns.)
JS: Casey James is a sick, sick human being!!! Not only does he light Bobby
Taylor on fire, but he tortures Taylor's wife and child while doing it! He should be fined…he should be suspended…he should be fired….hell, he should be arrested!
TM: Or at least he should be told this building's a no-smoking area! JS: You think it's funny?!?! You find this amusing?!?! You're as sick of a bastard as James is!!!! Casey James should rot in hell for something like this!!!
(A final shot of Taylor's son is seen. As the camera zooms in, we see that
Casey James has left a little souvenier for his son, the cigarette lighter.)
JS: Casey James is a sick animal, and I hope he gets what he deserves very damn soon! TM: Speaking of fire…..I'm being told that Lori Dane is standing by…with Serge
Annis! An unenviable task to say the least. Lori?
[The camera cuts to somewhere in the back of the locker room area. Lori Dane stands in front of a blank, and bare white wall, with the notable exception of a small advertising flyer for the IIWF's final show ever, IIWF: Forever, from just last week. Several names are written in big lettering... Steve Kowalski...
Joe Petrow... Brody Thunder... Otto Verhoeven... Lori speaks in a low tone voice, almost nervous, or afraid of the presence that presides in the room with her.]
LD: Ladies and gentlemen, I am standing here in the locker room of... "The
Epitome of Evil", Serge Annis, a man who shockingly made an appearance here tonight against Gabriel Whitecross in the North American title match up.
[Lori walks across the room, stepping over an overturned steel garbage can, it's contents spread out all over the floor in a giant mess. The camera catches up with her, and re-positions itself in front of the mirror, the sink, and
Serge Annis. Annis, still clad in his black wrestling gear, stands arms stretched out, leaning against the edge of the sink. Serge's heavy body is a sweaty mess, and his chest pants heavily. His eyes can be seen in the reflection of the mirror, staring down into the sink and at the small pool of blood that drips into it from his bloodied nose, courtesy of the big chairshot by Whitecross.
Lori waits a moment, as she builds the courage to speak to the monster of a man that she stands beside.]
LD: Serge...? [The Epitome of Evil's face doesn't even twitch. He continues to stare into the pool, acting as if Lori's words have fallen upon death ears.]
LD: Uhmm... Mr. Annis? [Serge seemingly snaps out of his trance, and his eyes wander over to look at
Lori Dane. Serge's emotionless face does not change... only his icy cold, blue eyes fixate on Lori. Knowing that she has captured the former IIWF World champion's attention, she slowly begins to speak.]
LD: Serge Annis... pardon my asking... but what brings you to EMWC? [Serge does not respond right away. He waits. As a mere moment seemingly passes by forever, the dark, mean, and restless voice of Serge Annis speaks.]
SA: I am here... to start anew. [Serge's slow words end, but his eyes continue to stare through Lori. Lori clears her throat and asks another question.]
LD: Start... anew? [Serge moves for the first time since Lori entered his locker room. He slowly turns his body to face her. Most of the blood that drips from his nose has dried, and the sweat that covers his horribly scared body glistens in the light. Emotion apparent, Serge's face slowly shows the look of anger, the same that matched his face earlier that night inside the ring.]
SA: I spent over twenty months in Portland. Twenty long. Hard. Restless months... in the IIWF. I never took a break. I never took extended time off. I sacrificed my career elsewhere... to climb to the top of the mountain in the
IIWF. And it took me eighteen damn months, to do what should have been done in six.
I was held back. I was screwed. I was punished. From the early days of my stay... to the dreaded and haunted memories of Genesis. I took everything that the IIWF had to dish out on me...
...and I survived. [Serge turns his body, and his head to the sink. He leans down and cups his hand under the noozle and turns the tap ever so slightly, to fill his massive hand with water. He lets go, and brings his hand up to his forehead and runs his wet hand through his hair, mixing together a blend of blood, sweat, and now, H20. Another moment passes.]
SA: I wore the gold of the all mighty IIWF. And then the ship sank... I waited too long... in the end... I was too late. I was told to forfeit the gold. I was told to go in and do 'what was necessary' to be done... I did 'what had to be done'...
[Serge's eyes glare back at Lori.] SA: And I did it. And in the end Miss Dane... I was forgotten. Forever. The mistake of Serge Annis was practically deleted from history. The future... was saved. [Annis' angry emotions seemingly die after this, and he slowly turns his head, and then his frame back to the sink, and resumes his earlier pose, leaning against the ends of the sink. A confused Lori Dane speaks.]
LD: Serge... that doesn't explain... that doesn't justify what you did to
Gabriel Whitecross earlier tonight.
SA: Doesn't it? [Serge turns his head, facing Dane.] SA: Doesn't it? I am here... in the EMWC... for the opportunity, to start anew. I'm not going to make the mistakes of the past, and allow people to walk all over me... neglect me... and damn right _abuse_ me!!!
[Serge's final words are spoken with extreme anger, as he shouts at Lori Dane. Dane flinches, seemingly afraid of the very words this man speaks, if not, the threat of possible violence like in so many of his past interviews in similar situations. The anger slowly subsides, but the harsh resentment in his words does not.]
SA: It's happened twice... and it won't happen again... [As the camera pans back a bit, Annis stands upright and tall, and turns around to face the camera. The horrific scars that cover his body show to the world.
Serge's words no longer are aimed at Lori, but rather at the camera... or someone watching at home.]
SA: I let myself be abused twice in my life... and it sure as Hell won't happen a third time. The EMWC. A place where Serge Annis can restart his Holy reign of terror upon the wrestling world, and do it right from the beginning.
Gabriel Whitecross, you merely served as an example. I don't know anything about you, or why you would be so foolish enough to sign a contract against an unknown opponent. While you sit at home... and lick your wounds... I want you to know something.
It wasn't personal. It was a message. A message to all the other EMWC superstars out there. A message to the fans. A message to every single goddamn man, woman or child that still doubts the validity of Serge Annis' name.
Serge Annis has arrived in the EMWC, and you can damn well bet... that I mean business. The mistakes of the past are not to be repeated... instead, I intend to capitalize. I've learned my lesson the hard way, losing over a year of my damn life... and all... all for a swift kick in the ass and a simple "See ya, now get lost".
[Serge slowly shakes his head no.] SA: Not again. [The 6'8 resident of Oakville Ontario steps back. He points into the sink, where the small pool of blood has collected from his bloody nose. The cameraman steps closer to the sink, and zooms in on the crimson red mess that lies in there. Serge continues to speak.]
SA: I spilt more blood than anyone else in Portland for that promotion. From the day I entered... to the goddamn day that I left.
Tonight... Serge Annis has spilt his first blood in the EMWC. [The camera cuts back away from the sink, and zooms in on Annis' cold, emotionless face.] SA: And to serve notice to each and everyone one of the superstars in the back... ...there is plenty more, where that came from... [Serge's icy blue eyes stare into the camera, as Lori Dane quickly signs off, and the cameras cut back to ringside.]
JS: Fans…there are no words to follow that with. Our Junior Heavyweight Final is next…I…I…go to the ring!
