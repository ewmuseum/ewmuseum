An Evening To The Extreme, September 30: Part 2 of 15

7 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Bobby throws the microphone out of the ring. He sets up the table in about the middle of the ring. He points to Vampyro who begins to run to the ropes. He jumps, flips back and … ASAI
MOONSAULT which breaks the table in half. The Tokyo Kid screams in pain as Vampyro shakes the table pieces off and stands up and goes back to stand beside Bobby Storm. They both look around at the audience and lift their right arms high in the air.]
JS: I think they mean business. Bloodstorm's continuing to pummel the Tokyo Kid and Fuji now! Why?! They won the match, what more do they want?!
TM: WHAT THE-?!? JS: Two men just leapt into the ring! Who is that?! TM: I think- Yep! It's the House of Lords! JS: You're right! Style and Logan are in!!! Logan drops Storm!!! He drops Vampyro!! He's using that awesome heartpunch!! Logan and Style have taken the newcomers down hard!
[Aladdin Style continues to stomp on the motionless forms in the middle of the ring as the psychotic martial arts master Anthony
Logan looks expressionlessly. The Dragonstrike Heart Punch has done it's damage, and the returning House of Lords stand alone in the middle of the ring. Logan raises the microphone as Style rolls the unconscious wrestlers out of the ring.
AL - "Time dims the memory and dulls the blade, and history is gradually forgotten despite the efforts of those who live to record. In this, The Sport of Kings, months fly like days and even the events of a year ago are forgotten.
For who here now feels the deja vu of this moment. Who feels the tension of know what will come next? It takes no Prophet to understand what follows. It has all happened before. It will all happen again. The House of Lords have returned to EMWC."
[Aladdin Style takes the microphone from his brother, a malicious grin on his face. ] AS - "Weeeeeeeeeeeeee'rrrrrreee baaaaaaaack!!!!" [A great heel pop fills the arean. Despite the words of Logan, the crowd does remember the short tour the Lords have just a few months ago ... and the damage they caused. ]
AS - "Down Boys! I bet you're feeling the butterflies in your stomach now! Let me ease your fear a little ... we aren't here for your right now. We've got a different agenda ... The World
Tag Titles can wait. We're looking for our old friends The
Fraternity Boys!"
[ Big face pop. ] AS - "G-Star Champions. You've beat the 16 best tag teams in the world. You've been unanimously declared by the sheets as the best tag team in existance. You've beaten everyone!
But there's one tag team you haven't beaten. One duo who took you apart and left you for dead in the middle of the ring.
One team who's confidence in their ability to beat you that they said they could do it in ten minutes, or they would refuse to take your World Tag Titles.
Live on EMWC pay per view, The House of Lords and The Fraternity
Boys put on the best tag team match in the long history of professional wrestling. It was true art. The Fraternity Boys left with the titles that night, but The House of Lords left with the clean pinfall at just over the 12 minute mark"
[Style hands the microphone back to Logan. ] AL - "Fraternity Boys, the illusion that you are the greatest tandem alive is about to come to a rest. The suspension you are under now seems no longer a punishment, but a godsend. But this fortune is temporary. It is inevitable that we shall meet again.
This is not hearsay, this is not the opinion or politics of the sheets. Being the last team standing in a tournament of 16 teams does not make you the best, if the best were not invited to join.
We are the House of Lords." AS - "We're not bigger than you. We're not badder. We're just so goddamn much _better_!"
["New Orleans is Sinking" blasts through the arena speakers, and a chorus of boos follow The House of Lords as they make their exit. ]
JS: An impressive debut by Bloodstorm. But the real story is the House of Lords! The House of Lords have returned, and they took Bloodstorm down hard!
TM: Did anyone tell them that the Fraternity Boys were suspended? JS: Erm..doesn't everyone know that?! TM: Apparently not, cause the House of Lords just called them out, and they ain't here! JS: Well, I understand that a lawyer for the Fraternity Boys has attempted to contact the EMWC President, and that situation may be settled very, very soon.
TM: Great, just what we need. The return of the drunks. JS: It would be great to see them back in the ring, so I hope that that situation is settled very, very soon. Earlier tonight, before we came on the air, the TV Title match was held. "Doctor of Love" Dave Bryant took on Sinister and Paragon in a triple threat match. It was a very hard fought contest with Sinister nearly gaining the win on several occasions. The end came when
Bryant nailed Sinister with a chair, knocking him to the floor.
Bryant then tossed the chair to Paragon, who caught the chair. Bryant hits the chair with the Call Me In The Morning, knocking the chair into Paragon's face, and knocking him out cold. A three count later, and Dave Bryant was still the TV Champion.
While Bryant was celebrating, a new wrestler in the EMWC by the name of Edward Love raced down the aisle and assaulted the fallen
Paragon. Love then hit Paragon with his move, the Love Bug, through a table. Paragon needed to be carried out on a stretcher, and early reports are stating that he is unable to move, and may never wrestle again.
TM: Ouch. JS: So ends the EMWC career of Paragon, a rather short one but he did win a TV Title out of it. However, someone who has had a very successful career here in the EMWC is Curtis "Thunder"
Hansen. He has been the North American Champion, he has been the
EMWC World Champion, and he is the Franchise. But the rumors are running wild, is tonight Curtis Hansen's final night in wrestling?
TM: Let's hope so. JS: Well, he's about to come out here and hopefully, he'll answer that question for us. Fans, here comes the EMWC's
Franchise! [And as Stegglet finishes setting up the entrance of one of the most popular foreign superstars in Japan's history, the PA kicks in with the resounding guitar solo of Angus Young! The music crescendos to a peak before the vocals begin to come on........
And a hand parts the curtain..... ....and out steps a living, breathing Franchise, clad in black and gold.......] JS: OH YEAH!!!! HANSEN'S HERE!! LISTEN TO THIS OVATION FOR THE FORMER CHAMP! I CAN HARDLY HEAR MYSELF!!!!! RMB: YOU FEAR YOURSELF????? TM: NO!!!! HE FEELS HIMSELF!!!! RMB: OH YEAH! RIGHTEOUS!!! TM: Ewwwwwwww....... [The Tokyo Dome has become the focal point of all earthquake watches around the globe as the pop for the superstar shakes the rafters of the baseball stadium! Thunderous ovation! Hansen steps through the curtain and carefully walks forward a few steps......spinning around and looking completely at the capacity crowd.....
"This is good. Yes, it is!"] JS: Hansen certainly looks in the best shape of his career, his upper body more defined, his spirit back. But one has to wonder about that trick knee that cost him a match in another territory just a week ago and almost prevented this match, or any future match, from ever taking place.
[Indeed, even as Hansen soaks in the pop, he walks with a noticeable limp. The black leather jacket is his trademark, and it shows. He dons new tights tonight - a pair of short trunks, black with gold trim running down the sides, the word "Tradition"
with the international symbol for "no" across it on the rear! The crowd reacts totally to this as Hansen turns around and jokingly points to his ass. The characteristc grin, albeit for a second, is shown once more.]
[The song continues as Hansen walks up the aisle and up the steps, slingshotting himself over the top rope and garnering another huge standing ovation. As he stands in the middle of the ring, he thrusts his fist into the air. At the same time, fireworks begin to go off behind him and flames shoot out from the posts, beckoning the crowd for another huge pop. They give this and, at the same time, thrust their fists into the air, a sign of respect from the pure Japanese wrestling crowd. Hansen pulls out a microphone from his jacket, and the crowd quiets....]
CH: Well.....it's damned good to be back in this country.... [big pop!] Of course, last time I was here, my head was bashed in with a steel chair, but the past is the past, right?
[Pause] Or maybe, just maybe.....the past is the key to the future. Don't be alarmed if ya don't follow me, 'cause I ain't joking when I tell ya that this is pretty damn complicated....
[Hansen looks to continue, but pauses and points out a solitary sign in the arena.......third row, fourth seat. A 15 year old
Japanese teenager stands and holds up the card which says,
"WORKRATE!" Hansen grins and acknowleges it.] CH: Cutter get to you, too? [Grin and pause. The fan shouts something vulgar to Hansen in
Japanese, a phrase the camera man nor the announce crew can decipher. But Hansen and the crowd seemingly understand and the crowd begins to become annoyed with the rogue in the crowd. But
Hansen calms them down.]
CH: Ease up there, fireballer......you'll get exactly what your sign wants in just a few short hours.
[Big pop!] 'Cause this one looks to be a damn fight. Y'see....you have Kevin Slater and you have Curtis Hansen.....two blood rivals....and no matter how good friends we've been, we can all face the facts...
We were born rivals. We'll die rivals. That's how it's gonna be. [Pause] We've fought before, sure. We each have a win and we have a draw between us. But this one....this one's significant....
This one's the final match. Back where we both started. Back where this thing started up..... This is where it ends. _Period_. Y'see, after this one, there ain't gonna be a rematch, their ain't gonna be no cryin' and moanin'...
Nope, after 60 minutes of pure hell, all I'm gonna see is the blood, sweat and tears on the faces of both competitors....
Then I'm gonna look straight into the World Title....._my_ World
Title.....and I'll hold it up in its glory for all the world to see. 'Cause that's what Franchise's do.
[Big pop!] And Kevin Slater's the one taking a walk on the Wild Side. [Hansen flashes the grin and the crowd goes nuts....] As for addressing rumors of my retirement.... [the crowd immediately drops to dead silence......and Hansen looks around the crowd, his face stoic. From rafters to ringside, he looks at the faces covered in emotion, seemingly concerned with what looks to be a final announcement to close out
Hansen's career. Then he stops......
.....and he puts the microphone in his side, dropping his head at the same time and shaking his head.....all in complete silence....as if to say, "No, I can't bring myself to do it."
...And he drops the mic to the ground, making a resounding _CRACK_. Just as soon as he came, gone is the Franchise, without all the fanfare.] RMB: What's wrong with this picture here? Hansen walks out of a ring and the fans aren't cheering.
JS: Let's look at the bigger picture here. Hansen looked like he was just about to make an announcement regarding the rumors surrounding his retirement, but....
TM: It didn't happen! He's too much of a damn wuss. Stupid has- been. Get outta here. You're not needed.
JS: One could argue you're a has-been. TM: One can't argue with a foot shoved down their throat. JS: Point taken. Let's go to our next match....it's a match for the North American Title! Mark Langseth..Diamondback Chris
Myers..the gold! Ken Graham, take it away!

(The crowd cheers) RA: First to make his way to the ring is the challenger. He’s from Phoenix, Arizona and weighs 265 pounds. Here is the “DIAMONDBACK” CHRIS MYERS!!!! (“Humans Being” by Van Halen plays throughout the arena as Chris comes through the curtains to a HUGE response. He stands at the top of the ramp, hands on his hips, staring over the crowd, for a few seconds, before making his way down to the ring. He slap hands with the fans down the aisle. He gets in the ring, and stands in the middle of the ring. As the lights go out and lasers carve a diamond around him, he crosses his hands across his chest and makes the 'I Love You' sign with both hands.)
KG: And his opponent, the North American Champion. He’s from Pittsburgh, PA and weighs in at 231 pounds. Here’s MARK LANGSETH! (After being announced, "Save Yourself" by Stabbing Westward plays over the PA. A loud heel pop resounds throughout the arena. Mark Langseth comes out from the back and starts making his way to the ring...very slowly, to the ire of the fans. He's dressed in his usual clothing, black T-shirt, baggy jeans, and black boots, but all have the look of being brand new. On his left shoulder, his long, thick, silvery chains reside, with some of the links trailing behind him. On the other shoulder sits the
North American title belt, also reflecting the lights of the arena, making a glow on each shoulder appear. With a smirk on his face, and his eyes looking over the crowd, doubting their hate for him, one can see that the man is confident about his performance here tonight. Before he enters the ring, he goes over the front row, where an gang of Cult of Personality fans can be seen. Mark looks over their sign, "Myers=More Gold For the
CoP!", and starts laughing a bit, shaking his head as he does so.
He then tries to control his laughter as he set his chains in the corner and rolls into the ring. He then finally stops his amusement over the sign, hands over the belt to the ref, and then goes to his corner to prepare for the match. Both Myers and
Langseth walk to the center of the ring with the referee as if it were a prize fight. Myers and Langseth lockup into a stare down as the referee gives the two combatants a few words about the rules.)
TM: Enough referee. They know the damn rules. JS: It’s just a ritual of wrestling. RMB: THEY’RE OFF! DING, DING, DING!!! (No sooner did the referee stop talking than Langseth went on the offensive with an eye poke. Myers grabs for his eye as Langseth pushes him towards the ropes and off...)
TM: Myers reverses! (Now, Langseth goes into the ropes and bounces off, but as Myers, still holding his right eye, attempts a clothesline Langseth dives down and clips his left leg out from underneath him.
Quickly, Mark hops up and stomps away on Myers as he attempts to get to his feet.) TM: Langseth is going full out for this one. RMB: Why waste time? Finish your opponent when you can. They aren’t getting paid by the hour, just the match. (Relentlessly, like a man possessed Langseth drops a vicious double axe handle over Myers’ back, who was on all fours. The move drops him to the mat once again. As he rolls over Langseth continues his barrage with a lightning fast elbowdrop across
Chris’ chest.)
RMB: Look at Mark go. TM: The Diamondback is on his back. JS: Oooh, that wasn’t good. TM: Yes it was. RMB: No man, it wasn’t. JS: Langseth going for the cover. ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! JS: Myers kicks out. RMB: And look at Langseth go! (Realizing he wasn’t going to get the three count, Langseth immediately grabs Myers by the legs and drops a knee into the side of his left leg. Then, stomps on the left leg, one, two, three times before driving another knee into it. Chris grabs for his leg and tries to keep Langseth away to no avail, because as
Myers grabs the ropes to possibly pull himself out of the ring to gain a breather, Langseth grabs his legs again, spreads them and drops a headbutt to Myers’ abdominal area.)
RMB: Langseth is acting like a rabid animal. TM: What animal? RMB: How about a Wolf? TM: Cool! RMB: What? TM: Cool! RMB: I thought that question was going somewhere. I didn’t expect you to stop there. TM: Cool! JS: Mark grabs onto the top rope, jumps up on the second rope to get elevation and comes down with a monstrous kneedrop to the abdomen of Myers... (Myers curls up into a ball as Langseth continues his onslaught by grabbing Myers’ legs and slingshoting him up against the bottom rope.)
RMB: Throat choke! (Mark stands up with a smirk on his face. The crowd starts chanting “MYERS, MYERS, MYERS!” Mark pays it now heed and peels the stunned and hurt Myers up by the hair. He pulls the apparently woozy Myers into his body hard, where he raps his right arm around Myers’ head. Mark pauses for a second to gloat over his good fortune and executed game plan.)
JS: Langseth is showing a lot of confidence. (Langseth boost all two hundred and sixty five pounds of Myers up into a vertical suplex, but Myers size advantage comes into play as he shifts his weight and slides out the back. He lands on his feet, and in one fluid motion grabs Langseth around the waist and pulls backwards, hitting a perfect belly to back suplex. The crowd goes wild.)
RMB: Diamond-man pulled that move out of his rear-end. JS: Both men are down, but even after that, Myers still looks the worst for wear. (Langseth gets to his feet first, and as Myers gets to his feet, pushes him into the ropes and Irish whips him across the ring.
Myers rebounds off the ropes and surprises Langseth with a flying shoulderblock. Both men go down. Myers, with a surge of energy, gets back up to the his feet at the same time Langseth does. He scoops up Langseth and slams him hard to the mat. He immediately runs into the ropes, bouncing off and dropping a spinning elbowdrop to Langseth’s chest. Myers goes for a cover.
ONE! KICKOUT!) JS: Myers is in control now, but he’s still feeling the affects of Mark’s early attack. (Myers rolls off of Langseth to grab a breather. He gets to his feet and grabs Langseth’s head, only to get his trunks grabbed and slung through the ropes and out of the ring. Mark quickly rolls out of the ring, but when he gets to the outside Chris is gone.)
JS: The big man is showing his speed. TM: He slid back into the ring. RMB: WATCH OUT MARK!!! (Moxy’s words are too late. When Mark realized what had happened, he turned around and was greeted by a baseball slide from the Diamondback. The impact shoved Langseth to the floor.
Quickly, Myers scales the turnbuckles to the top, perched, waiting for Langseth to get up, and as he does...)
RMB: DAMN! That had to hurt. JS: Myers went for a suicide splash onto Langseth, but Mark moved out of the way at the last moment allowing Myers to hit the concrete floor hard. TM: Looks like Myers is holding onto that left leg Langseth was working on earlier. (And indeed he is. A tired Langseth doesn’t waste any time capitalizing on the situation and stomps on Myers leg. He walks over the time keeper’s table and pulls the chair out from underneath him. The referee is counting both men.
Three... Four... Langseth folds up the chair, walks over to Myers who’s still on the floor holding onto his knee and drives the chair down into the side of that injured left leg.)
JS: Why doesn’t the referee disqualify him for that. RMB: It’s about giving the fans what they want, a match. That’s mild considering what could happen in a match. (The referee stops counting and yells at Langseth to drop the chair or get disqualified.) RMB: Ooh, what a threat, DQ the champion. That’ll teach him. TM: Apparently, this is a new referee. We don't DQ for minor offenses in the EMWC! We want a winner in every match!
(Langseth smirks at the referee and goes to nail Myers again across that knee, but the referee grabs the chair and yanks it out of Mark’s hands. Instead of going after the referee, he decides to continue his assault on the bad wheel of Myers. He drops a knee into the left leg, then grabs Myers by the head and rolls him into the ring. Langseth quickly rolls in after him and grabs the bad leg...)
TM: ANKLE-LOCK SUBMISSION!!! (As Mark grabbed Myers’ leg, Myers, realizing what was about to happen, with all his reserved energy pushes himself up off the mat to a standing position. Langseth still has Myers’ leg in his grasp. Myers, quickly spins around to face Langseth and...)
JS: ENZUGARI!!! Out of nowhere. RMB: Desperation move. That seems to be all that Myers has left. (The crowds roars with excitement over the surprise move. The kick to the side of Mark’s head sent him flipping in the air to the mat. Myers, pulls himself up by the ropes and hops over to the turnbuckles. He sits on the top rope, then puts his feet on the second rope, waiting for Langseth to get to his feet and...
“WHAM!” ...Missile Dropkick sends Langseth back down to the mat hard. Still favoring his left leg, Myers uses the ropes to pull himself up. He walks over to Langseth and grabs him by the hair and pulls him to his feet and Irish whips him into the corner. At the same time, Myers backs up into the opposite corner and runs at Langseth and...
“SMACK!”) JS: Handspring elbowsmash. Diamondback is showing his incredible stamina tonight and listen to the crowd. (The crowd cheers loudly as Myers side steps out of the corner and Langseth walks out, legs buckling, from the last move, and just drops to the mat, face first, in the middle of the ring.
Seizing the moment, Myers rolls Mark over and goes for a cover. ONE! TWO! THRE...KICKOUT!) TM: Damn! Can you get any closer? (Myers is the first to his feet. He nails Langseth with a punch to the head, then a forearm smash to the chest before pushing
Langseth into the ropes. Suddenly and surprisingly, Langseth rakes Myers face. An, as has been his plan from the start,
Langseth goes after the Diamondback quickly. First a kneelift to the stomach followed by an Irish whip into the opposite ropes.
On the way back Mark goes for a lariat, but Myers ducks it and asLangseth spins around, the Diamondback hits a hurricanrana on
Mark. Both men get to their exhausted feet at the same time, but
Myers seems to have the energy edge at this point. He grabs a wondering Langseth and pulls him into a front facelock...)
TM: IT’S RATTLER TIME!!! (The crowd first cheers wildly, but then the cheers switch to
“Ooohhhhs”.) RMB: Langseth’s turn to pull a rabbit out of the hat. JS: Langseth had no other choice but to trust forward slamming Chris Myers into the corner back first. (The move saved Mark from the inevitable, “The Rattler”, it also crushed the referee between the turnbuckles and Chris Myers back.)
JS: Myers is grabbing the back of his head. I believe he hit it against the referee’s forehead. RMB: Headbangers ball! TM: But he hasn’t let go of the front facelock, it’s still RATTLER TIME!!! JS: And the referee is out cold. RMB: OUCH! (The crowd lets out a loud “OOOOOHHHHHH!”.) JS: The family jewels have been rearranged. (After driving Myers, back first, into the corner failed to relinquish the hold, Langseth dropped down to his knees and in one smooth movement uppercut Chris in the balls, dropping the
Diamondback, and more importantly, getting him to release the hold. The crowd is now booing.)
RMB: Man has to do what a man has to do to win. (Mark goes to work once again on Chris’ hurt left leg. Much slower than before, but with the same intensity, Langseth stomps on the leg and follows that up with a kneedrop to the knee which after hitting, he holds there for a moment then starts to grind back a forth across it.)
RMB: Charlie-horse city. TM: Damn! That’s gotta hurt. (Chris nails Mark in the face with a right hand jab that forces
Mark off his leg. Angered and tired, Langseth grabs Myers by the head helping him to his feet, then quickly taking him back down to the mat with neck breaker. Myers lays there motionless.
TM: I think it’s over. Now, if only the referee can wake up. RMB: Damn slacker. He’s slacking on the job. Fire his ass! (Suddenly, the lights flicker, and the voice of Serge Annis is heard.) SA: Heh heh... hey Mark. Look up. I dare you. (Mark forgets about Myers and looks up to the ceiling. A body then drops from the catwalk above the ring. The body falls until the hangman's noose tied around it's neck seizes, and stops the body fifteen feet above the ring.)
TM: It's Gabriel Whitecross! Serge Annis has killed Gabriel
Whitecross! That bastard! JS: No, it's just a dummy. A dummy that looks like Gabriel Whitecross, with a Whitecross t-shirt on. I have to think that hangman's noose around the neck is a form of symbolism.
RMB: No doubt. Annis is playing mind games with Langseth here, no question about it. JS: And Langseth looks none too happy about it. (In the ring, Langseth stares in disbelief at the dummy overhead and shouts out some unmentionable profanities. The lights flicker again, and drop to blackness for a moment. The crowd errupts when the dummy starts to spark, and ignites in flames right above
Langseth's head!)
JS: Oh my God. TM: Now -that's- symbolism. JS: Annis is a sick man, no doubt about it. TM: Look at Langseth... he's just staring and.. HEY! Who touched my shoulder? Moxy! RMB: It wasn't me man! (The camera does a quick cut to the table at ringside, and shows that Serge Annis himself has leaped over the retaining barrier and is taking Langseth's beloved chains from the time keeper while everyone's attention is turned to the flaming dummy above the ring. Annis grabs the chains and runs to the isle with them, heading back to the locker room.)
JS: Serge Annis has just stolen Mark Langseth's beloved chains! I can't believe it! TM: And Langseth doesn't even know it yet! He's still looking up. (As Annis disappears through the curtains, the lights return to normal.) JS: The lights are back up, the dummy is still overhead, Annis just went to the back... and Langseth just gave a field goal kick to Chris Myers head! And, the referee is still out cold.
RMB: Langseth is angry and he's taking it out on Myers. He didn't like that show from Annis at all. And he doesn't even know Annis thieved the chains.
TM: Thieved the.. what? Langseth is now peppering Myers with closed right hands! He's going to school on him!
(The crowd erupts again as Serge Annis makes his way back out through the curtains, grinning madly. He carries the chains and a microphone. The chains are seemingly dripping wet.)
JS: He's back. Serge Annis: Hey... Langseth! (Langseth looks up at the flaming hanging body.) SA: No, over here you dummy! (Langseth eyes around, and spots Annis standing halfway up the isle. Langseth gets off of Myers and walks to the ropes, shouting at Annis.)
SA: Hey Langseth... are you by any chance, missing anything? (Annis grins wider as he holds up the chains. Langseth looks shocked as Annis drops them on the floor, in the isleway.)
SA: You want them back? Then come get them! (Langseth forgets about Myers and rolls to the outside and starts walking up the isle, set to retrieve his chains.)
JS: Serge Annis is up to something here. TM: Hey, he's got matches?! JS: Oh no... (Oh yes. Annis lights a match and grins as Langseth approaches. Once within five feet of the chains and Annis, Serge drops the match onto the chains. They ignite in fire as the kerosine they were just dipped in burns.)
TM: Yes! He just set those chains of fire! RMB: Flaming bodies, now flaming chains. There's too much fire out here for my liking. (Langseth looks on in horror, staring at his chains as they are engulfed in the flame. Sensing the moment, Annis lunges forward and clotheslines the unexpecting Langseth down to the concrete.)
JS: A trap! It was a trap! TM: Well d'uh. (Annis lays a series of kicks into Langseth as Mark covers up.) JS: Annis is peppering him... wait, Annis pulls him up to his feet. What's he doing... hand under the arm and around the throat... oh no...EPITOMIZER!!! Serge Annis just hit an
Epitomizer onto Mark Langseth on the floor! TM: Yes! Now that's action! Why is everyone boo'ing? They hate
Langseth too! JS: Maybe so, but he didn't deserve that. And now Annis is rolling him into the ring. Mark Langseth has been knocked out, and now he's been rolled into the ring!
