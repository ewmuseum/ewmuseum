An Evening To The Extreme, September 30: Part 15 of 15

7 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Another heel pop emerges from the crowd, but this time the fans seem to wonder whether it's a wise choice to boo out this man]
Deathbringer: Ever since I left the wrestling world a few months back, I had been searching for a new league, a new home. I traveled all around the world, I visited many big cities, many federations... but it was not until today that I could honestly say - these grounds will become my new personal graveyard...
As I look at the roster of this league, I recognize many names. Many foes from the past have gathered here in the EMWC. Probably they were sure to never ever see me again. But they were wrong.
And I wouldn't be surprised if some of them left this league right after this night, just to avoid what can not be avoided...
I read the name of Casey James... "Blackheart"... I know you from the days when they called you "Whitebread"... yes, I am sure you wish that no one ever calls you by that name again... but for me,
James, you will always be that small, preliminary, patriotic jobber...
I read the name of Tiger Claw... And I think back to the days when we stepped into the squared circle against each other... I was standing at the side of Dan Kauffman, but that did not matter, as I almost ended your career on my very own that day...
I read the name of "Quickstrike" Chris Quigley... Now we just dealt with each other in this other, now defunct league... You always seemed to care about me, although I never cared about you... Quigley, should you step into my path once more, I will
_truly_ take you six feet under...
I read the name of Serge Annis... Oh, Annis... There is so much I want to tell you, there is so much that I would like to show you... But we have time for that when you join me on the Dark
Side... And to accomplish this, I will, sooner or later, end your existence...
I know most of you other mortals, too, be it "Playboy" Ronnie D,
Derek Mota, Christopher Stonebreaker, Simon Lebec or Luke
Steele... But of all those comrades from the past, Annis, Quigley, Tiger
Claw and James certainly gave me the toughest time in that other league...and so it does not astonish me as I find your names once again on the list of the five most hated athletes in EMWC...
And you mortal fans out there better get ready to see another name to appear on that very list... The name of Deathbringer...
And I am certainly aiming for the number one spot... [Deathbringer again laughs in his evil sounding way, and the crowd gives him another heel pop]
Yes, mortals... I have returned to the squared circle. And take this for sure... I will not leave until I have gathered what rightfully belongs to me... The crown and the gold of the EMWC...
The Reaper has returned... and with him your nightmares will return... I will leave you now... So, mortals... Sleep well... [The lights drop again, and as they come back, Deathbringer's gone] JS: HE'S GONE!!! The Deathbringer is gone! Hansen and Slater have been laid out! We're outta time, fans, we'll see you next week!
(Fade out)
