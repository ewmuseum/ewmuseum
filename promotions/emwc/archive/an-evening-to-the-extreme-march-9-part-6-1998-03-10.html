Get yours today, and you will look.... [cut to Hansen from clips of many interviews] JUST_TOO_DAMN_GOOD!! [fade out] TM: WHAT THE HELL DID WE JUST SEE!??!?! When did we become the Home Shopping
Channel? If Hansen needs extra money to pay for his thirteen year old whores, let him moonlight as a cab driver! I don't need this kinda crap from the Boy
Scout...
JS: Take it easy, Todd. He bought the time, he can do with it as he pleases. Folks, it's main event time...and this is gonna be a big one! Four men...a stretcher...and a career is gonna end. This is going to be one hell of a war!
Let's go down to KG one more time... KG: Ladies and gentlemen...this is our main event of the evening... (BIG pop) KG: It is a stretcher match with no countouts, no disqualifiations, no pinfalls, and no submissions. To win this match, you must place one member of the opposing team on a stretcher and have them carried back to the locker room.
And in a very special stipulation...the man who is the loser of this match...MUST LEAVE THE EMWC!!!
(HUGE pop) KG: Introducing team #1... ("Outlaw Blues" by Pat Benatar begins to play as the crowd explodes in heel pop.) KG: They are accompanied to the ring by Brian Lau...representing The Syndicate...they are...TOOOOONY STARKS...and "THE OUTLAW...J.W. HARRRRRRDIN!!!!! (Enormous heel pop as the evil trio steps out from the curtain. Hardin in his customary Stetson and duster, Starks wearing his trademark white towel like a poncho. Lau leads the duo, an evil grin on his face.)
KG: And their opponents... ("Something Wicked" by Nuclear Assault begins to grind over the PA as the lights take on a reddish tint.)
KG: First, he is a former North American Champion...DIRT DOG...UNIIIIIIQUE ALLLLLLLAH!!!!! (BIG pop) KG: And his partner...he is the EMWC World Champion..."ERA OF DEFIANCE"...GAAAAAAABRIEL WHIIIIIIIITECROSS!!!! (ENORMOUS pop as Whitecross and Allah step through the curtain. The drunken
Allah staggers down the aisle, looking a bit worse than usual. Behind him trails the champ with the belt draped over his shoulder. His sleeveless leather jacket is thrown aside, and there's nothing but intensity in his eyes.
Both men stride to ringside and pause as Hardin and Starks both have chairs...) JS: This is gonna get wild!!! This is no DQ!! These men can do whatever they want. The Syndicate's got steel chairs, and they won't let Allah and
Whitecross into the ring...Starks took a swing at Allah but missed! That was a close one...
LD: Allah looks like he's about to fall down. He looks even worse than usual... TM: I don't understand...he was fine when we polished off that bottle of
Cuervo before the show.
JS: Allah's got a chair now...HE THROWS IT AT STARKS!!! Starks batted it away with his own chair...but now Allah is hurling steel chairs into the ring. He's lost it...
LD: The World Champion is glaring at J.W. Hardin, who's returning the stare. Lots of bad feelings there, and their match at the Rumble did little to make those go away. HERE HE COMES!!!
JS: HARDIN SMACKS HIM WITH THE STEEL!!! And Whitecross keeps coming!!! He didn't even feel it!!! Whitecross...TACKLES HARDIN!! Fists are flying already!!!
TM: Allah's filled the ring with steel chairs...and he rolls in, Starks nails him!!! The Age of Rage is certainly over. Starks blasts him again...
JS: Whitecross is pummeling the Outlaw!!! The champ's lost it!!! He's choking him!!! This is wild!!! It's no DQ...anything goes!! The stretcher is outside on the floor, ready to be used!!
LD: Starks puts his chair around the head of Allah...LEGDROP!! Right on the steel...and Allah's head gets smashed in that chair!
JS: Whitecross pulls Hardin up...and fires the big guy across...CLOTHESLINE! HE NAILED HIM! Whitecross pulls him right back up...backhand chop! Another chop...Hardin's been backed into the ropes...CLOTHESLINE!! OVER THE TOP TO THE
FLOOR!!! LD: Hardin's been knocked to the floor!!! Allah's got that chair wrapped around his head...STARKS IS STOMPING ON THE CHAIR!!! He's driving the steel into the skull of Allah!
TM: Now we need a match like this all the time...Whitecross is up top...rare for him...FLYING CLOTHESLINE!! KNOCKS HARDIN INTO THE STANDS!!! HE SENT HIM
UP AND OVER THE BARRICADE!!! JS: GOOD LORD!!! Whitecross leveled Hardin!! Starks pulls Allah up...wraps his leg over the neck...ROCKER DROPPER ON THE CHAIR!!! OH MY!!! Allah's nose driven straight into the steel! And Starks grabs Allah by the afro...and slams his face to the steel again.
LD: Whitecross just hopped the railing...and he's going in after Hardin. He hooks him...lifts...HANGS HIM OUT TO DRY ON THE BARRICADE!!! Oh wow...Hardin landed ribsfirst on the steel. And he's down on the floor. Whitecross back over the railing...and he's trying to take apart the steel barricade.
JS: Starks is wrapping the chair around the leg of Allah...stomping on the chair!! He's trying to break Allah's leg. To the ropes...legdrop on the chair! Allah's leg is wrapped in that chair...
TM: This is pure and total chaos...Whitecross has taken apart that barricade...and he's setting it up over here by us. Whitecross is pulling
Hardin up to his feet...he lifts...CROTCHED ON THE STEEL!!! The Outlaw's gonna be singin' in soprano for a while...
JS: Whitecross hops back up on the apron...running CLOTHESLINE!!! HARDIN TO
THE FLOOR!! Great move by the World Champion!! And he's trying to pick up the barricade. Hardin's trying to crawl away from this shot...Whitecross is holding the steel barricade high overhead...he's gonna nail him.
LD: Starks fires Allah to the ropes...BAKCDROP OVER THE TOP-ONTO WHITECROSS!!! GOOD LORD!!! Allah went over the top and onto the barricade that Whitecross was holding!! He wiped him out!!
JS: Allah landed on top of that barricade...and now both men are down. Hardin was saved by Allah! Starks is coming out on the floor...and he's gonna try and stretcher Allah. He pulls Allah up...and puts him on the stretcher. He's telling them to take him out...and they start to go. They have to take Allah all the way out for The Syndicate to win.
TM: Starks just tossed Whitecross into the ring...and he's got Hardin up. This could be trouble for the World Champion. JS: They're about half way down the aisle with Allah! He still hasn't moved-HE SAT UP!! Allah sat up and hopped off the stretcher.
LD: Hardin and Starks with the doubleteam on Whitecross. They've got him in the corner, and the double team is on. Pounding on him with lefts and rights...
JS: Allah's walking down the aisle...staggering's more like it. Hardin's setting up Whitecross for a piledriver...Starks is putting a chair underneath him...and Starks is going up the ropes. Oh no...SPIKE PILEDRIVER ON THE
CHAIR!!!! GOOD GOD!!! That could kill a man!!! TM: Wow. That was vicious. Whitecross' neck may have snapped from that. An ordinary man's neck would.
JS: But the World Champion is no ordinary man!! Nevertheless, Whitecross is down and out...Hardin and Starks are celebrating. They think they've finished off the champ...Allah's got a chair...DIVE ONTO STARKS!!! CHAIR DIVE!!!! DID
YOU HEAR THAT CRACK?!?!?! LD: Hardin's pulled the champ up...over near the ropes...OUTLAW'S CURSE!!! ONTO THE STRETCHER!!! The gurney nearly collapsed from that impact!! TM: Whitecross got saved by that stretcher. If it wasn't there, he would have hit solid concrete and this would have been over.
JS: And Allah's still on the attack...Hardin's telling them to take away the stretcher. Allah's going up top with the chair...he's all the way up top, holding the chair to his chest...SOMERSAULT SPLASH WITH THE CHAIR!!! GOOD
LORD!!! WHAT IMPACT!!! Starks' ribs may have collapsed from that!! I've never seen a move like that. A 450 splash with a chair. Wow.
LD: And the gurney's about halfway down the aisle...Hardin's got brass knucks...HE NAILED ALLAH!!! Right between the eyes!!! Allah's been busted open with those knucks!
TM: BLOOD!! We've got blood!! JS: And Whitecross is back up!! Much to the delight of these fans, he's back to his feet. Allah's in trouble now...CATTLE BUSTER ON THE CHAIR!! Dirt Dog
Unique Allah has been brutalized. He can barely move. Allah's laid out...Whitecross is on his way back out here. Hardin is trying to revive Tony
Starks. Starks is in a lot of pain after that steel chair 450. Hardin's pulling him to his feet.
LD: Whitecross is at ringside...and he tossed the barricade into the ring!! Gabriel Whitecross is back in the ring...and he's brought the ringside barricade with him. JS: Starks is pulling up Allah...he's going for the kill. Whitecross...TACKLES HARDIN AGAIN!!! WHAT VELOCITY!!! WHITECROSS HAS THE
KNUCKS!!! RIGHT HAND!!! ANOTHER!!! OVER AND OVER!!!! He's pounding Hardin with the knucks. Starks is putting Allah up top...and he's going up behind him. Tony Starks is on the top rope...he's lacing the arms...tiger superplex on the way...he stands up...AND TURNS AROUND!!!
LD: NO!!! GOOD GOD, NO!!!! JS: STARKS HAS IT HOOKED!!! TIGER SUPERPLEX OFF THE TOP...TO THE CONCRETE!!!! GOOD LORD!!!! BOTH MEN ARE WIPED OUT!!! ALLAH'S NECK HIT THE CONCRETE AT A HORRIBLE ANGLE!! THAT WAS AWFUL!! TM: Tony Starks is the most cold blooded bastard I've ever met. He may have killed Allah, and he may not be done! Starks is pulling himself to his feet...and he's pulled Allah up too...KATHA JIME!!!! HE LOCKS IT ON!!!
JS: Whitecross sends Hardin to the ropes...SPINEBUSTER ON THE BARRICADE!!!! Hardin's spine nailed the steel!! The crowd has erupted!!! Whitecross is on the warpath...he's going for it...FAMILY NAME!!! THE ANKLE LOCK!!! Hardin's in trouble!!! This can snap the ankle!!!
TM: Starks is choking the life out of Allah!!! Allah's foaming out the mouth, and Starks won't let go!!! Allah's in trouble...
JS: Whitecross is putting on the pressure...Lau's on the apron! Brian Lau's on the apron...and Whitecross is going after him now!!!
TM: NO!!! You can't hit Lau!!! You can't hit a genius!!! JS: Allah's been laid out by Starks, and he still won't let go. Look at those eyes! That's a cold blooded killer! Allah's not moving...and Starks finally picks him up, and tosses him onto the stretcher. I think Allah's out cold.
LD: Whitecross has Lau by the collar...and Lau tossed something to Hardin!! What's that? TM: HANDCUFFS!!! Lau tossed Hardin a pair of handcuffs. Look familiar Lori? JS: Hardin's got those cuffs...and he's cuffing Whitecross...to the ropes!!! Hardin's handcuffed Whitecross to the ring post...Starks has sent Allah...HE'S
GONE!!! Allah's been sent out on the stretcher!!! And he's out of the EMWC!!!
TM: NOOOO!!!! NOT HIM!!! WHY? WHY? WHY? JS: But this one's not over yet...Starks and Hardin are both in the ring...both with a steel chair. OH MY!!! They're pounding on Whitecross!!
The World Champ's being brutalized by The Syndicate! (And the crowd erupts as a figure rushes down the aisle...) JS: IT'S BOBBY TAYLOR!!! TAYLOR'S ATTACKED HARDIN!!!! He's pounding on The Outlaw!!! TM: CASEY JAMES!!! TIGER CLAW!!!! THEY WERE RIGHT BEHIND TAYLOR!!! JS: SHIN KICK!!! Claw nailed Taylor, and nearly took his head off! Taylor's been knocked flat!
TM: YEAH! He may have gotten the jump on The Syndicate at the Rumble, but this time, they were ready!
JS: Claw and James are pounding on Taylor...they're setting him up...James with the reverse power-Claw up top...SYNDICUTTER!!! GOOD LORD!!! And Taylor's face hit the steel! James and Claw continue their assault on Bobby Taylor, and
Hardin's got some spurs...Hardin's gonna cut Whitecross!
TM: Hardin's going to slice Whitecross wide open with those spurs...and Starks is holding him still. This is gonna be great!
JS: This could get real ugly. Hardin's got the spurs...and he's gonna slice him wide open. Whitecross is in big trouble! WHO'S THAT?!?!?!
(Another figure comes flying down the aisle, this one apparently wielding a...a...sock? Some type of sock filled with something as it obviously sags with weight. The figure slides into the ring, and begins swinging that sock.)
JS: RYAN HOWARD!!! RYAN HOWARD!!! "INTREPID" IS HERE!!!! HE'S GOT A SOCK!!!! WHAM!!! He nailed Claw across the face, and sent him to the floor. WHITECROSS MUSCLED STARKS OVER THE TOP!!! Hardin and James are backing off...Howard's swinging that sock around like a maniac!
LD: What a way to make an impact! Ryan Howard has made the save. Whitecross was about to be cut like a pig, but "Intrepid" has made the save.
TM: But who's gonna save him? James and Hardin are gonna take this kid out! JS: Ladies and gentlemen...we've got a stand off but we're outta time. Howard's in there with The Syndicate!!! We gotta go!! So long everybody!!!
