An Evening To The Extreme, April 6: Part 7 of 11 (Hour Three, Part 1)

9 April 1998

EMWC TV Title Match EMWC TV Champion "Crimson" Joe Reed (w/ Lady In Red) vs "Ringmaster" Thomas William (w/ Jack McAndrews) Writer: Ryan H. ________________________________________________________________________ [The Lady in Red gets in the ring with both the EMWC TV Title and a cordless microphone. Reed is still recovering from the earlier match, and is breathing heavily. He takes the EMWC belt and holds it above his head. Then, he lays the belt down, in front of him. He speaks into the microphone, and directs his comments towards the locker room.]
CJR: One down... and one to go. Tommy Williams, here's your chance. I'm here, [points down on the mat, where the belt is lying] and the belt's here.
You want my belt? [He pauses, looking around at the crowd in the United Center.] Come and get it. (Reed throws down the mike…and Thomas William charges down the aisle, with Ryan
Rankin and Jack McAndrews in tow. He slides into the ring, and pops up, staring at Joe Reed.)
JS: And we should be in for a doosie with this one, folks. Reed has just barely scathed by in defending his title once tonight, and will now have to attempt a second defense against
Thomas William, who is no patty-cake himself. LD: And don't forget, -my- Romeo is at ringside, so Big Red will have his hands full on the in and out.
TM: Of your dirty tw.. [Todd is abruptly "cut off."] JS: Well, here's the bell, and this match is ready to roll... [With the ringing of the bell, Joe Reed tiredly pushes himself from his corner, where he had been reclining, rotating his arm in a full circle, eyeing William intently. William, on the other hand, begins to strafe across the ring, his eyes feral in nature, eyeing his prey. With the snap of a twig, William launches an offensive, both men locking up. Neither shows much of an upper hand at first, but William takes the smallest of edges, and immediately executes and armwringer, twisting hard on Reed's arm.
With Reed in a tied-up situation, Williams wrenches harder, and then directs an elbowsmash into the base of Reed's own, sending the larger man down in pain as he follows with several consecutive others.]
LD: Hey, Romeo... TD: It's nice to see Lori in a non-drug induced dream-state.. but in the ring, this match is becoming a nightmare for Reed, as
William has the upper hand easily over the TV champ. JS: And this armbar he has just locked on won't help matters much, and could end the match considering that it'll take some strength to power out of it..
[William continues to wrench back with the crucifix armbar, but Reed's size proves beneficial, his reach slowly, but surely making it's way to the ropes, and gaining access, the hold being broken. Reed moves to his hands and knees, lightly favoring his right arm, as William drops a knee down over the back of his head, and then again, but Reed does not falter. Before Reed can fully stand, though, William whacks him over the back of his head with an elbow, and then a second time, stunning him enough to execute a standing armbar, though it doesn't last more than five seconds, as Reed was already near-enough to the ropes.]
JS: Todd, if you were Reed, what would you be thinking of doing right now? TM: Umm.. throwing Lady in Red onto a bed for several hours of wild, lustful sex? JS: Why do I bother..? William with a snapmare takedown on the big man, and immediately goes back to stomping into that arm.
LD: Why is Romeo ignoring me? He knows he wants it.. [William brings Reed back to his feet, attempting another armwringer, but the crowd explodes as Reed draws William in for a mammoth lariat, knocking him three-sixty! Reed stumbles into the ropes after the effort, though, taking a deep breath. William rises to his feet, shaking off the small stun, only to be caught in the clutches of the big man, who easily drops him back onto the mat with a back suplex.
This time, it is Reed who brings William to his feet, deciding he needs the win, and now, locking up a front-face lock and jerking him, but the Kentucky
Backsmasher ain't happening, as his right arm falters, giving William an opening to drop a fist into the back of his head, and again, using the momentum to wrap Reed up in a body-scissors and fall back with a front-facelock, wrenching on the weary Crimson one.]
JS: A body-scissors/front-facelock submission executed by William, I must say, he is doing a wonderful job in an attempt to win this title. TD: Yeah, that move is perfect for the circumstances, and Reed is going to have trouble getting out of it.
LD: And here comes Romeo.. he talking to the ref and gaining his attention... just as Reed taps the bottom rope with his foot!
JS: Wonderful teamwork in a singles bout by Rankin and William.. and here comes Lady in Red! Rankin is skipping Dodge on that one. TD: And the referee sees the current status and forces William to break the hold, but with a move like that, the damage is already done.
[William smirks wryly and draws Big Red to his feet, lining up and hitting a nice shot to the forehead- and out of nowhere, Reed fires right back! William throws a left, and Reed matches, then a right, and the same! Ducking a right haymaker, Reed begins to dance a circle around William, hitting left jabs, the count reaching five. Five, broken only for Reed to spin and "jack" William hard in the face, the crowd roaring loudly as "Ringmaster" drops.
Tending his right arm a bit more, Reed steps back for a bit, only moving to intercept William as he rises, scoop-slamming him down, mostly with his left arm's strength. Winding up, Reed drops an elbow across William's throat, slumping over him for the pin, gaining one and a half.]
JS: That match against Osbourne really took it's toll on Reed, as he is clutching desperately now to maintain a shimmer of hope to win this match.
TM: Reed is back on his feet, though.. he will not be denied. He seems to be sizing up William for something.. running lariat!
LD: William caught his arm and counters! Wakigatme! He's got that variation armbar locked on tight in almost the center of the ring!
[William strategically situates his feet against the mat, using a foothold for leverage with his pull, Reed grimacing in pain as he kicks his legs out in search of the ropes. After a few moments, though, William releases, his right arm immediately favored as he is pulled up to his feet.]
LD: Enziguiri by William! That one plastered him hard over the back of his head! He's definitely going to try and put Reed out, now.
JS: He's going for a piledriver.. no, FACEBUSTER! That one hit Reed hard, sandwiching his face between the mat. LD: Holding the pickles -and- the onions. TM: This does not look good, Reed is busted back open from the earlier contest with Osbourne, and the blood his quickly covering his face.
LD: And William is back on the attack.. though I wish it were Romeo.. [William picks Reed up, slapping him across the face with a toying chuckle, before booting him in the gut, twice, and whipping him to the ropes. On the rebound, the crowd gasps as he cracks Reed's jaw with a superkick, immediately rolling over him, hooking the leg for the pin.]
LD: ONE.... TWO.... TM: KICKOUT!! Reed miraculously got out of that one, how, I don't even think God knows. JS: Let's be politcally correct and add Amaterasu, Buddha, Confucius, Yahweh, Lao-Tzu, and Satan to that list. LD: Satan.. always gotta slip one in for Waco.. [William stands and wastes no time in laying into the bloodied mug of Joe Reed, kick-steppin' it hard into his face. He then glances down to the fallen warrior and moves towards the turnbuckles, the crowd gasping.
Reaching the top, he waits for Reed to stand, before leaping off..] LD: HIGH CROSS BODY!!! TM: ONE-HANDED POWERSLAM BY REED! He drove William hard into the mat with that one!! Amazing!! Simply amazing!!
JS: This crowd is on their feet as he wearily pulls William to his feet.. he's goin' for the Kentucky Backsmasher! TM: NO! HE SWIVLED THE FRONT-FACELOCK! HE HIT THE ACE CRUSHER AGAIN! JS: REED FOR THE PIN!! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! LD: No, No, No!! Go get him, Romeo!! JS: Maybe he's reading your mind, as Rankin is already in the ring and laying the boots into Reed..This is a very different side of
Ryan Rankin, and it's obvious that Gen Next is having some effect on the youngster. Rankin laying in the kicks…and he pulls William out of the ring.
Gen Next is leaving the area. Big night for Joe Reed as he successfully defends his title, not once, but twice in one night! He's put himself in the record books tonight. Joe Reed, battered and weary, leaves the ring too…holding the TV Title high above. Great night for him.
LD: That sucks. That'll put a damper on the party later tonight. TM: As will your scorching case of crabs, I'm sure. JS: What a way to kick off the second hour…what? Why? Fans, I'm told that
Matt Malone is on his way out here, and there he is….
[ 'The Mouth' Matt Malone proceeds up the aisle, talking into his mobile phone, which is slightly out of the ambience created by the hundred or so following Followers of the Church, who surround the ring when he steps in. As usual, he is wearing his dark blue suit, with a crimson 'A' logo on the back of the jacket. He exchanges his mobile phone for a mic...]
MM: Honor, Sinister, is something I have never claimed to have. Honor is atotally arbitrary concept. One man`s honor is another man`s shame. For example, some people may find it an honor to be in your presence. Sorry, let me rephrase that...the STUPID would find it an honor to be in your presence.
I, and anyone with any sense, would find it a total shame. [ Malone smiles smugly then continues...] It`s also a shame, Sinister, that you have such an over-inflated ego of yourself. First of all, you consider yourself to be a life-threatening cancer.
Perhaps, in metaphorical terms, you are. You are a cancer within EMWC, within the world, and in blunt terms, you need to be removed. I`m no surgeon
Sinister, I`m simply a prophet. But I know that any of the Gods of the Church of Armageddon could end this 'life-threatening problem'. Cancer though, is not a problem for the Church of Armageddon. We are beyond such mortal ailments.
What mortal ailments are you beyond? None, I suspect. Certainly pain is within your understanding. Suffering is within your comprehension. Torture you must know of. But never to the extremes as lavished by the God of Pain, Dakota Bundy. [ All at once, the Followers drop to their knees and start bowing towards the aisle as Dakota Bundy appears. His image is without a doubt, one scary sight.
His face has been stitched up more times than Frankenstein. His eyes glow with menacingly evil intent. His fingernails are filed to a cutting point. Not the largest of men, but certainly one with the potential to be the deadliest. The veteran steps into the ring, next to Matt Malone. The Followers bend to their knees and chant 'Bundy, Bundy, Bundy' in an eerie tone.]
MM: Just two weeks ago, Dakota Bundy & Creed battled beyond the merits of normal men. Now fully rested, Bundy is back Sinister. The ichor flows through him like no other god. His determination to make his mark should not be questioned. He has the Church and it`s beliefs to fight for Sinister. What do you have? What do you have that could take you beyond anything you have ever endured before?
We`ll find out next week. I`ve requested from The President a match between you and Dakota Bundy next week. A submission match. Do you have the stupidity to accept?
[ Malone raises an inquisitive eyebrow to the camera] Because let me tell you something Sinister, you may think Bundy is as 'dumb as a rock' because he doesn`t speak. But he speaks with his actions. You on the other hand...it`s nothing but yabba-yabba-yabba. Sinister, you`re not dangerous, nobody cares who you are...and as for being ominous...well, I`ve
seen salads that are more ominous. You are not a problem Sinister, you are not even an irritation anymore. You want to declare WAR! on the Church of Armageddon? Well let me just get you a crucifix and some nails, because that`s the kind of pointless self-sacrafice you`re going to make. Sinister, the Church of Armageddon...Bundy, Tonnage,
McGriff, Ammon...there is a bond and understanding between these gods that cannot be broken. The Church is too powerful to be defeated. So let me tell you one final thing Sinister, you *will* worship at our feet or die by our hands.
[ The Followers repeat " You will worship at our feet...or die by our hands over and over again as Bundy & Malone walk back up the aisle and a Crimson 'A'
appears on the ring mat, oh so mysteriously...] JS: Now that's a creepy guy. This whole Church thing deeply disturbs me. These guys have the potential to rule the EMWC someday, and if that day comes…it will be a very dark one for the entire wrestling world.

LD: And that hunk is going to be performing... TM: Shut up, Lori! EVERY wrestler is a hunk to you... Even Tubbage. LD: Bite me! TM: I think I just may... BITCH! LD: BASTARD! JS: You're both bitches and bastards, so shut up! Our next match is the debut of "Playboy" Ronnie D as he takes on Jeff "Madfox" Matthews. These two have had a bit of a history, as Ronnie D started off by attacking Matthews at the EMWC's
Rumble in the Rockies.
The next week, Ronnie was injured at the hands of Brody Thunder and could not compete in the match he was scheduled for against Jeff Matthews. In fact, he's been on the shelf for a month and a half.
TM: However, that hasn't kept him from harassing Matthews. LD: Or looking good... JS: No, it hasn't hindered either. In fact, he even hired "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin to do his dirty work for him, resulting in several attacks.
[Footage of Ronnie D and Hardin pummeling Matthews is shown, with Ronnie laying a crutch into Matthews' back, and Ronnie paying Hardin afterwards.]
JS: Ronnie even insinuated that Matthews' wife Debbie had slept with him! And now that his knee is well enough to compete, Matthews is going to make him pay!
TM: Not if Ronnie and his money have anything to say about it... ________________________________________________________________________ Jeff "Madfox" Matthews Vs "Playboy" Ronnie D Writer: Ron D. ________________________________________________________________________ [The camera takes a long shot from the top of the United Center. MARQUEE MAN The words quickly scroll off to the side, and just as soon, the flatline forms ICON As soon as those words scroll off, the word GOD OF WRESTLING blip across the screen.
