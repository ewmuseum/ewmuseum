An Evening To The Extreme, September 30: Part 8 of 15

7 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Caleb, struggling with fatigue, blood loss, and even a bit disillusionment, gets up to his knees, and then to his feet.
Blinking slightly, he looks at the massive structure around the ring... The barbed-wire... The plexiglass... The chickenwire...
The glass roof... And he takes a deep breath before grasping the side of barbed wire in his hands, putting a leg up in the mesh, and swiftly following suit with the other leg.]
JS: Temple going up top... What could he want there? [Quickly, with steps interspersed with grunts of pain as he scales the wire, Temple makes his way up top. Rolling over the top of the "cage" and onto the glass roof, he examines his hands carefully, checking out his wounds. As Ezra peels himself off the concrete, Temple stands up on the glass, and looks around uneasily, trying to find his balance. Not so much that he is off- balance, just that the glass makes every step seem a little more dangerous. The erection around the ring wavers slightly, and
Dick Longfellow stands in the ring, looking up at Temple uneasily.
Ezra, now standing on his feet, looks up at Temple who stamps his foot, half in a motion urging Ezra to make his way up the cage, half testing the glass...Hoping it will support them both.]
TM: He wants Ezra... up there. This could get ugly... And fast. JS: And Ezra responds! [Ezra takes a step back towards the guardrail... And charges the barbed wire cage! He leaps up the side and scales swiftly and easily, not even wincing at the small barbs shredding his palms.
He gets over the top of the cage... And is booted in the side! Ezra almost rolls over, but quickly regains his senses, and realizes that he is 15 feet above the ground on top of an unsteady structure. Temple backs away, having already warned
Ezra, and lets the youngster get up to his feet.]
TM: Ladies and gentlemen... This could get nasty. If you have children, put them to bed now.
[Ezra slowly gets to his feet, and goes to meet Temple in the centre of the...Roof? He looks at Temple, and looks around him...
At the crowd, now looking on in awe, wondering what could happen next... Down at Dick Longfellow, who stares up, hoping nothing goes wrong... At least, not to him... And back at Caleb Temple...
A fraction of a second too late, as he is scooped up by the southern man and SLAMMED to the glass platform!]
TM: WHOA! I think I just saw the Killing Box shake! JS: Get them off! Get them off! This is _not_ safe! RMB: WOK! WOK! GET THEM THE WOK! [Ezra is dragged up by Temple, and has his head placed between
Caleb's legs. Caleb grabs him by the waist, lifts him up and...]
TM: PILEDRIVER ON THE GLASS! HOLY SMOKES! JS: Oh... My... [The frame off the Killing Box, the metal, plastic and glass, sways violently as Temple gets up carefully, hoping not to disturb the structure further. As he stands up, an odd creaking is heard...]
JS: NNNNNNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOO!!! [... The crowd gasps... ... And the cage steadies itself.] RMB: PHEW! That was close! TM: This is _not_ what wrestling is about, Steggy. This is wrong... Just plain wrong. These two guys should _not_ be up there.
JS: For once, I agree. This is not good... [Temple, now looking a little worried, lifts Ezra up, hook the leg over his head and... Hesitates. He looks around, wondering what to do next, if this next move might be too brutal for the fragile Killing Box to withstand...]
JS: Oh god... This has to be the biggest mistake we as a whole, the EMWC, have ever made... Why did we send then up there, Todd?
TM: Big Poppa Buyrate. RMB: CRIPES! Get us fired, will ya? TM: Bah. I have tenure... Right, Steggy? JS: I've heard... Things. TM: Who needs you, anyway? [... Temple looks out, clenches his teeth, and determinedly
DRIVES Ezra onto the glass, the crowd gasping in fright!]
JS: SIMON EZRA MIGHT! He needs all the help he can get after that
Arabian Facebuster on the glass! And look at that Killing Box!
This is sick! It's just swaying... And swaying... Lordy! TM: Look at the fear in Temple's eyes... You know he doesn't want to be up there. [The huge metal frame shakes, shivers and sways... ... Suddenly, from under Ezra's face, a thin white line appears... It slowly, but surely, snakes along the glass... The camera picks it up, the announcers pick it up, the crowd picks it up... But not Simon Ezra. And not Caleb Temple. The bolts holding the cage together groan with pain as Ezra is lifted once more to his feet.]
JS: OH MY GOD! THE GLASS IS BREAKING! THE GLASS IS BREAKING! TM: THEY NEED TO GET OFF! JUMP SHIP! JUMP SHIP! RMB: ACK! THOMEBODY HELP DEM! [The white line snakes towards the edge... ... CCCCCCCCCRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAASSSSSSSSSSSSSS SSSSHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] RMB: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! JS: HHHHHHHOOOOOOOOOLLLLLLLYYYYYY!!! Barbed wire, plexiglass, chickenwire and pure glass... Collapsed over the competitors.

JS: Oh... My. This... Is... Bad. TM: I... I'd say something... But it's so... Shocking. I... I saw it coming... But...It's so shocking.
RMB: Wok. [Longfellow has no choice but to call for a count himself, and he looks around at the stunned crowd, a few of them near tears, before laying down the final count.
