Noise Pollution, 3 March 2000

3 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Backstage, after An Evening to the Extreme has gone off the air. We are inside an empty locker room, but it does not stay empty for long. Soon, the door is flung open, and into the room walk Alex Martinez, and his manager, Lady Veronica. Martinez is clearly not happy, as evidenced by the scowl on his face. Veronica's expression is somewhat guarded, her thoughts are her own. Martinez announces his irritation when he slams his fist into a nearby locker, leaving a sizable dent in the thing.] AM: Dammit, I can't freaking believe that. Freaking Casey James, getting pinned by Matthews! Dammit! And then that jackass Willis! Coming in, and attacking Jack and I from behind. What the hell was that? Hey Willis, you want to lay out Myers, get your ass in line. 'Cuz the next time you think you're gonna pull something like that, I'll make sure you spend the next ten years sucking your meals through a straw. [Martinez runs a hand through his sweat soaked hair, and lashes out at another locker, this time with a strong kick, swearing loud enough that the next few seconds of EMWC television consists of a long bleep. Veronica approaches Martinez, and puts an arm on his shoulder.] LV: Calm down Alex. Just calm down. Kicking lockers aren't going to do you any good. You have to focus on what's next. Next week, you and Casey James are taking on Matthews and Tiger Claw. [Martinez's face grows even redder, his temper spiraling out of control.] AM: F[BLEEP]ing Masterson! So this week, he gives me one good partner, and one partner that's about as lost in the ring as Marky Langseth is at a whorehouse, and then, next week, instead of giving me Jack Greene, you know, the guy who knows what the hell he's doing in the ring... he gives me the guy who screwed up? What the hell is this? I don't give two pieces of crap about Casey James and Tiger Claw, let 'em fight somewhere where they ain't in my way. Masterson, I know right now you're sitting in your room, laughing your ass off. You got V.D. Vitrone on one side, running up your phone bill, as he tries to get a date with the three hundred pound walrus he took the prom, and hearing "no" in seven different languages on one side, and you got your little Japanese friend Sushi or whatever his name is on the other side, shoveling rice cakes into his mouth and watching Pokemon, but I want you to listen up. Don't jack me around, Masterson. Don't go playing your little games. I ain't Nero, I won't come out every week, and put my chin out for you to lay me out. I ain't one of these little curtain jerkers you're playing around with. I'm Alex f'n Martinez, the Last American Badass, and I will lay you out on your ass if you screw with me. You wanna know what a bitch it can be to have in your company? Go get yourself some old tape, from when I was making Chris Blue's hair turn white and his face go pale. I ain't the sort of guy you wanna cross, and you are coming very close to finding out why... [Veronica has a rather pleased look on her face. This is, apparently, how she wants to see Martinez, aggressive, ready to smash everything and everyone in his way.] LV: But I haven't even told you the best part yet. AM: What's that Rhoni? LV: The winning team of this match, gets to determine the stipulations for their match at Rising Sun Rumble. So if you and James win, that means you get to pick your match against Matthews. You can determine exactly how you are going to end him, once and for all. And, I've got an idea for a match... [Martinez nods his head, somewhat mollified by that revelation.] AM: Yeah? Whatcha got in mind? [Veronica leans in close, and whispers something into Martinez's ear, and from the way Martinez's head nods, and the way satisfaction crosses his face, it seems obvious that whatever the idea is, it is very pleasing.] AM: Damn Rhoni, you are one evil bitch. No wonder I keep ya around. I woulda never thought of that. LV: That's why you keep me around. AM: Yeah, I guess so. [Martinez turns, to look into the camera, his face still full of rage, but this time, it is more focused, more goal oriented, rather than just a general aggression towards the world.] AM: So that's it then. One more hoop I gotta jump through. Well then, I guess I'll do it. Guess I'll take Masterson's crap one more night, 'cuz it'll means I get to choose how I'm gonna put Matthews on the shelf for the rest of time. I ain't happy about it, but hell, Claw and Matthews will prove perfect outlets for that. Casey... I hope you're listening. I ain't taking your crap. You come up, and you fight. Don't drop the ball, like ya did tonight. Don't be a jackass, and don't get in my way. I ain't got no love for ya Case, and I'll be glad to end you. You and your little "issue" with Tiger Claw means exactly _nothing_ to me. You don't mean anything to me. And if you drop the ball again, well hell, I'm liable to drop you. And Claw and Matthews? Hell, what can I say that ain't been said a thousand times? Claw, I can respect your abilities all day and all night, ain't gonna mean nothing when I get in the ring with you. I got respect for a whole lotta people, and I've kicked all their asses. Yeah, you're tough. Hell, maybe, just maybe, you're tougher than me. But I don't know that yet. And I know that if you wanna find out, all ya gotta do it bring it. I'll be more than happy to test myself against you. And Matthews? You're a dead man. Ya hear that? Dead man. Your days are numbered. I ain't losing this match, 'cuz I got very specific plans on how to end you. I ain't losing this match, 'cuz its just too damn important. You're gonna learn why they call me the Last American Badass. And you, Claw, and Casey if he gets in my way, are all gonna learn exactly what it means to get yourselves.... BURNED!!!!!! [Martinez and Veronica leave the locker room, and the camera fades to black.] [From darkness, fade to show the face of Alex Martinez.
