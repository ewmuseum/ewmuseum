"Jammin" Alan James: Welcoming Committee

5 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone. I just wanted some feedback on this recent role-play. My character is generally a flashy type wrestler, and this is meant to be an introduction into the league. I apologize before you even read it, though, for the generic setting. Usually when I join a league, I like to start off with a press conference. So, please respond. Constructive criticism is always appreciated, and complements enjoyed. So..... read, read, read! Also,
I have a feeling MSN is going to maul this one, so beware. Another thing...... about the whole Idolization part...... that doesn't mean you,
Ronnie D. No one idolizes you. Well, maybe lovesick teenagers, who laugh at all your sexual orientated jokes....... :)
============= EMWC ============= <><><><><><><><><><>< "Jammin" Alan James -in- ~Welcoming Committee~ <><><><><><><><><><>< [From the black, the view begins to shift. Colors are seen from various regions of the screen, and coagulate in the center of the view. As they combine, they form an EMWC Logo, in bright blues and greens. The logo falls apart. The colors pool at the bottom of the view, and then jump up to read
"Jammin" Alan James. The scene then fades.....]
[ From that black, a new view is now constructed. The view has been shifted to a smallish room, where the air is filled with haze. Chairs are aligned horizontally throughout the room, and the chairs are filled by what seem to be reporters. All of them are dressed drably, in brown and black hues. The green, shag carpeting is littered with various briefcases and bags. As the camera pans upwards, the head of the room is now visible. A large wooden podium is at the head of the room, where a figure in a sharp, black suit stands. As the view shifts to the left, a large television is set up, but is turned off. The camera swings again past the podium, to the right now. An oak table stands adjacent to the podium, where a chair stands, empty. The view continues to shift past the chair, where a large window provides a look at the outside of the building. It shows what seems to be a runway, because a plane is beginning to taxi towards the strip. When a loud BOOM is heard, the camera swings back to the source of the sound. It was.... just the man in the suit, tapping on the microphone in front of him]
Sharp Dressed Man: As an EMWC official- [We can now assume that he is an EMWC official.] Sharp Dre… EMWC Official: I am always on the lookout for new talent. An attempt to always keep the EMWC flowing with originality, excitement, and that certain EMWC style.
[As the official smiles, in the corner of the screen, a jet black plane is visible through the windows. As it aligns itself with the runway below, it begins its descent]
EMWC Official: Well, as I was strolling through California the other day, I had the chance to see a man wrestle, in one of the federations they have down there.
[A journalist stands up in the back] Journalist: Stealing from other federations, Ed? [The journalist chuckles a bit, as does "Ed", or the EMWC Official, or the
Sharp Dressed Man, or whatever the hell you want to call him]
Ed: No... no... no. This man just caught my eye. [The jet black plane has..... just landed. It continues down the runway a bit, and slowly comes to a stop. Some of the journalists begin to gesture towards the plane, but the main attention still is on Ed]
Ed: I think, as some of new have noticed, he has just arrived. So, before he gets in here, I'll try to fill in some background.
[The plane is now beginning to taxi towards the building] Ed: He is a very powerful man, maybe a little too flashy at times, but when
I saw him, he put on quite a show. He's also relatively new to the sport-
Journalist: So you got 'em cheap, huh? [All in the room begin to laugh, and the plane has opened its door. A large staircase folds from the opening, and the chrome handrails glimmer in the sun]
Ed: No... we didn't get him TOO cheap. But, like I was saying, he is new to the sport, but we saw what he had. And he had potential. He was.... EMWC material. I liked what I saw from him. In the ring, he was very competitive, powerful, all that great stuff. But, what convinced me, was the fact that he had heart. The determination was seen in his eyes, like wildfire. He was going to make a name for himself somewhere, so I figured we should snatch him now, before he had a chance to get away. So, ladies and gentlemen, I put together a little piece. Just something to let you get acquainted with him.
Don, if you'd please get the lights.... [The view swings to the back, where "Don" hits a small switch on the wall. The room goes dim, but light still filters in from the windows. Ed rolls the television cart over behind the podium, and presses a button on it. The screen lights up, but it is showing only snow. A loud hissing noise escapes the speakers, and some of the members of the press draw back. Ed takes his seat, and the camera focuses on the television.....]
[An EMWC Logo appears on the screen, and underneath it reads "Is proud to present:" The screen goes black for a moment, but is lit up again, as a man punches another in the face.]
*SMACK* [The journalists wince at the blow] ======================= Sweet: Houston slides the object to Douglas on the outside and covers Jammin
’ Alan James. . .
1 . . . 2 . . . 3 . . . Jonny: NO!!! HE KICKED OUT!!! James is bleeding from his face but he’s not staying down as he has kicked out!!!
===================================== [The camera shifts again, this time showing the wrestlers, apparently later in the same match]
========================== Jonny: James whips Houston into the ropes....................... and catches him into the Atomic Sideslam!
========================== [The screen fades to black once more, and the lights come on in the room. The journalists sit calmly, watching as Ed walks up again to the podium] Ed: Well...... sorry if that was less than spectacular. We didn't have much time to put it together you see.........
[A voice is heard from the back of the room, and the view flies back just as the doors in the room crash open. In strides...... "Jammin" Alan James.
Wearing blue jeans and a black T-Shirt, he looks massive compared to those in the room. His long brown hair is pulled back into a pony-tail, and his eyes appear to be filled with blue ice. He flashes a large smile, and begins to walk towards the podium]
Ed: Ladies and gentlemen, "Jammin" Alan James....... [ Flashes begin to go off from all over the room, causing it to light up. Shadows vanish along the walls, only to re-appear. James walks to the podium, and shakes Ed's hand. They turn to face the room, and shake again, for the cameras. As the flashes begin to die down, James walks behind the podium, and stands next to Ed]
Ed: Well James, for the rest of the EMWC Staff, I extend a warm welcome to you. Now, I'm sure you've prepared a statement to all of these.........
[Ed motions towards the press with his arm. James whispers something, and Ed chuckles. As Ed sits down, James steps to the podium]
James: Actually I have. Although I only did it on the plane ride over, so don't tear me apart if it isn't great.
[The press laughs, as does James] James: I didn't think that was all that funny, but I'll continue anyway. I am totally blown away at where I am now. Yesterday, when I had awoken, I can assure you that I had no idea I'd be standing here, talking to all of you.
My main concern was the fact that I had run out of cereal………. [The press chuckles again] James: Many of you don't know me. I guess that's warranted. Competing in the
NWC, although a top federation itself, does not compare to this. While the
NWC is still searching, the EMWC has already FOUND. It is there. It is known as one of the greatest federations in wrestling, and has a long standing tradition of excellence. When I had gotten the call from Ed, asking if I would like to be a part, my response was "how soon."
[James smiles again, and looks downwards] James: Before, I had spoken of tradition. Although not a rookie myself, I am new to the league. Hopefully, I can carve out a chunk of that tradition in my name. I am ecstatic to be here, and look forward to times that lay ahead of me.
[James reaches along the inside of the podium, pulls out an EMWC cap, and flashes a large smile as he places it on his head. The flashes go off again, and James continues to smile]
James: Now…. Before we all fall asleep, I’m sure that all of you have some questions that you’d like to ask me?
[The reporters’ hands fly into the air, and are accompanied by much shouting. James motions with his hands to relax, which the reporters do. One of them stands….]
Reporter: Hello. I just wanted to ask you how you came to be a part of the EMWC. James: As I think Ed told you, while he was enjoying a short stay in California, he had happened to catch a show in one of the nearby arenas. He like what he saw, gave me a call, and the rest is history.
[Another reporter stands] Another Reporter: What are your feelings about the league? James: Good question. The talent in this league is immense. Living legends walk the isle…… sit in the dressing rooms…….. brawl on the mat. It’s just going to be incredible to me. Having to fight people whom I’ve idolized?
Man. It’s going to be a fun stay. Same Reporter: Do you think you can handle the competition? James: I give no promises, but….. yes. Hopefully I can. That’s what I’m here for, and I’ll do my best at all times. And maybe I’ll get a chance to put on a show at the same time.
[The seated Ed chuckles a bit, remembering James’ over-the-top entrances. A female reporter stands now…]
Female Reporter: Anyone in particular you have a distaste for in the league? James: I always try to consider myself one of the "Good Guys." I work hard for what I get, but try never to lower myself to a level of indignity.
Although I don’t want say any specific names, yes, there are a few in this league who are not to my liking. Of course, meeting a person in the ring and watching them on television are two different things altogether. Maybe I’ll find out that some of the bad guys aren’t so bad after all.
[James clears his throat] James: Okay guys, look, I’ve really got to go get everything settled with my agent, so I can make sure that I wrestle under EMWC contract. I’ll take one more before I skeedattle.
[Several reporters yell out, but one stands] Standing Reporter: Will the EMWC pay for your huge entrances? James: What’s with all the hype about my freaking entrances???? I’ll bet most of you haven’t even seen them!!! I’m sick of this crap already!! You know, sometimes I just-
[Ed stands to his feet, and approaches the podium] Ed: Alan, you’ve had a long flight. Why don’t you take a seat. I’ll field this one. [James walks over and takes a seat at Ed’s vacated chair] Ed: I can assure you that Alan will have full backing from the EMWC. Just as we take care of our other wrestlers, we’ll take care of him. Now, ladies and gentlemen, I’m afraid that I have to draw this conference to a close. As
Alan has already expressed to you, we have to go finalize the deal. So, for the last time, the newest entrance into the EMWC is "Jammin" Alan James.
Alan, you have a bright future ahead of you in the EMWC, and I’m glad you chose to come aboard.
[The view begins to shift to black, as more and more flashes come from the seated press. The cameras are popping, and a logo hazes onto the screen. It reads:
================= I’m here. Pay Attention. ================= And the view finally fades completely]
