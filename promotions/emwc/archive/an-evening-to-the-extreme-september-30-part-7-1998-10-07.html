[James points down to the fallen C.O.P members.] CJ: It's called taking advantage of a good situation. Unlike you who just takes advantage of a live, uncut microphone.

anger is too much as he powers up to his feet, and swings at

James is pushed down the other end of the hallway. The camera follows Annis out, and the doors quickly shut, locking Annis away from the action. Annis shouts out loud in frustration, his anger taking control. He plants a hard kick to the door, denting the door. The camera's cut back to ringside, as Annis begins to charge at the door, shoulderblocking it, in an insane attempt to break down the door.]
JS: Jeez, there's pure chaos in every direction. This night has been wild, and it's about to get a lot more wild. Up next is our
King of the Death Match semifinal match. The winner of this match will fight in the finals on October 31st at Devil's Night.
But the real story in this one is the stipulations. Fans, the ring crew's on their way out here, and you're about to get your first look at perhaps the deadliest ring ever created. Feast your eyes..on the Killing Box!
[A crew of EMWC ring hands slowly trot out, four to a group, each carrying four monstrous panels... One, a panel of chicken wire, another a panel of plexiglass, a third being made of barbed wire mesh and the fourth a sheet of solid glass, quite thick in it's own right.]
JS: This is certainly an interesting match, Texas Death rules, taking place in possibly the most bizarre structure I have ever seen. Our construction crew toiled endlessly this weekend to make this structure known as the Killing Box. TM: #1, baby! JS: While our faithful ring crew sets this up, let's get a few words from our two competitors...
[Fade in on Simon Ezra and his apparent paramour, sitting on a small, Japanese-style wooden bridge that crosses a shallow stream, side by side, legs dangling off the edge. It looks to be a park or a garden, judging by the surroundings, and the cloudy blue sky and faint breeze stirring Ezra's hair places the time in the afternoon. Ezra's wearing his red leather racing jacket, unzipped and revealing his shirtless chest beneath, a pair of torn, faded jeans, and a pair of sneakers so mangled that the laces may very well be all that's holding them together, his face hidden behind a half-mask that simply bears the image of the lower two-thirds of a skull. The girl is wearing a faded green pull-on hooded sweatshirt, an ragged pair of jeans, and a pair of white sneakers that actually look relatively new, or at least intact. Ezra is looking in the general direction of the camera, but not specifically at it... or, possibly, anything at all.]
Ezra: Have you ever heard the fable about the frog and the scorpion, Caleb? [Ezra brushes a lock of his snow-white hair back and out of his face.] Ezra: It seems that once upon a time, a scorpion needed to cross a river. Now, why this is remains a mystery- it's possible that he just wanted to see the other side, or perhaps it was the start of a great journey for him, the tale of which would be told in the litanies of scars he'd both deal, and receive...
[Ezra pauses for a moment, then continues.] Ezra: Regardless of the "why", however, the scorpion was there, at the edge of the river. He wandered up and down the bank, looking for a way to this promised land he envisioned on the other side, but to no avail- he was stuck. After a while, though, he heard a croaking near the bank, and he scuttled over their to investigate, conquering a small obstacle on his way.
Upon arriving at the source of the sound, he found a wise old bullfrog submerged in the brackish water, only the very top of his head peeking out of the mire which he'd willingly entered into. Now, this frog had made the journey across the river many times, so many times, in fact, that he was nearing the end of his days, and so his experience was vast. The scorpion, in comparison, was young, very young, and had never traveled very far from home before- this was his first great journey.
[Ezra nods solemnly, the part of his face exposed looking very, very serious.] Ezra: The scorpion requested that the frog take him across the river on his back. The frog resisted, saying that the scorpion would sting him, and kill him. After much arguing, and much reassuring from the scorpion, however, the wise old frog gave in and came up on shore that the scorpion could climb onto his back.
The scorpion did so, and the frog gently waded back into the river, so as not to upset his passenger. He swam a quarter of the way across the river, and then stopped, nervous, because the scorpion had moved, trying to keep himself dry. The scorpion stilled himself, though, and the frog, reassured, continued across the river.
[The girl moves around so she's behind Ezra, straddling him from behind, and wraps her hands around his waist, resting her head on his shoulder, face against his neck. Simon reaches up with his left hand and idly runs it through her hair, in a disarmingly gentle gesture of affection, before continuing.]
Ezra: The frog kept swimming, and the unlikely duo made it halfway across the river, and kept going. They were three- quarters of the way across when the scorpion suddenly stung the frog. The frog began flailing as the venom began doing his work, and the scorpion was dislodged. "Why did you do this?" cried the frog. "Now we're both going to drown!" The scorpion response was simple, and one of the great truths in the universe.
[A pause.] Ezra: "Because I'm a scorpion", he said. "A scorpion can't change it's nature." [Another pause.] Ezra: And so they were both swept under, into the dark, unclean river where the frog had spent his entire life, and, presumably, they drowned. I say presumably, because the story ends there- it doesn't say whether or not the scorpion was miraculously washed ashore to continue it's journey, or whether the frog, surely crippled, if not worse, managed to make it to the shallows, where he could at least continue breathing.
[Ezra blinks, slowly, languidly... an audible inhalation of breath, and he continues, as the camera slowly zooms in on his face.]
Ezra: I suppose we'll just have to see for ourselves how it ends, won't we? [Ezra stares at the camera- yes, _at_ the camera. He's looking right into it, focused, clear-eyed, coherent... and it's a little scary, because his eyes look so cold, so dead, so utterly empty...]
Ezra: There is a morale in this, Caleb... The operative phrase this time isn't "Fear me." No... it's fear for yourself.
[Fade to black.] TM: Well, he's normal. JS: Look, ladies and gentlemen! The "cage" is set up and we are ready to go! This is... One of the oddest moments in EMWC history. The capacity crowd, which would normally be screaming at the top of their lungs, is in dead silence, just... Looking at that... That... Thing. I have to say, the entire EMWC staff has to be nervous right now, as do the fans... And probably the wrestlers.
TM: You know it, Johnny. We wrestlers all put on a tough face... But when you do something like this... It makes ya damned nervous. It doesn't matter who you are, when you're stepping into a ring like that, you gotta have some jitters.
RMB: I know I'm nervous. I wet myself twice already. TM: *sigh* [The camera pans out to a wide shot from the cheap seats, and we see the figure of Ken Graham stepping into the massive structure in the ring.
The crowd murmurs as they overlook the Killing Box. It almost glistens...Hauntingly... as the lights reflect off its walls. One side is left open, and opposite it is a wall made of chickenwire.
Another wall is made of plexiglass, and opposite it is a barb- wire mesh, much like a chainlink fence. And on top, shining almost beautifully is a sheet of glass that looks heavy enough to crush the biggest of men, and strong enough for men to walk on.
The corners, usually adorned by turnbuckles, are adorned with boards. Thick wooden boards, each with perils adorned. On one, nails are fastened to the board. On another, barbed wire is wrapped around the board. On a third, broxen glass is glued to the board, and on the last board... Three bombs, sure to leave a nasty mark.
The slight frame of Ken Graham steps into the ring, and he looks at the structure, shaking his head... Nervously. Dick Longfellow paces around the ring, biting his lip, nearly in... fear.
Graham raises the microphone to his lips, not even bothering to grin, and starts the announcements.]

written underneath. He steps into the ring, and goes to his corner, the one with the bombs in it, and gently relaxes against the bombs... Is he really that carefree?]
