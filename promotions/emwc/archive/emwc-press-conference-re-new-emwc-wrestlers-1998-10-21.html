Press Conference RE: New EMWC Wrestlers

21 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fade in) (The scene is a crowded press room. Flashbulbs are popping, voices are rising, the sienna hair of a reporter who's supple curves are overflowing within her skintight blouse, lips pursed ever so slightly as her gellationous orbs of molten lava that we call "eyes" focus-wait...wrong fed. Anyways...it's packed to the rafters, and there's only one reason why. That reason is about to become known as the EMWC President Chris Blue enters the room, and causes a great stir.)
CB: Welcome folks...there's one reason why we've called this press conference...and one reason alone. Tonight, the EMWC is very proud to announce the newest members of the EMWC roster!
(The reporters begin to buzz with anticipation.) CB: When we began this quest about two weeks ago, we were only looking to fill three spots. But the quality of applicants was so very high, we at the EMWC decided to increase the number of applicants we'd accept...to TEN!
And now...the lucky ten.... A former EMWCer who didn't quite get going his last time in, and one of the
"king of the indies"...SETZER VAN STRIFE!
One of the hottest young prospects currently in wrestling..."HEARTBREAKER" STEVE WALLACE! Definitely the most controversial star to apply...THE PINK PASSION! A star in the regional ACW promotion, now making an attempt to break into the big time...SHADE!
Coming from the UWF, one of the best light heavyweights in the world...VORTEX! A former star in the TCW promotion...making a comeback after a long layoff...OSHIO MUSHITO!
Another former EMWCer making a return...and another one of the best Light
Heavyweights in the World...SENGIR!
Our scouts were very high on this next star. A very hot prospect...REBEL WITHOUT A CAUSE! One of the most unique stars ever to appear on an EMWC broadcast...WUGDULLAH THE BUTCHER! And last, but definitely not least...another former EMWCer...and the #1 Free
Agent on the market..."MADFOX" JEFF MATTHEWS!
We look forward to seeing these stars in action in the weeks to come. And now,
I've got another meeting to attend, so I'm out of here!
(The reporters begin to rush to the phones, as the President steps down from behind the podium and exits the room.)
(Fade out)
