An Evening To The Extreme, March 9: Part 4 of 6

10 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to near pitch blackness. The camera sees only a thin, dim streak of light, emanating from behind a rounded structure; the curvature of the shadow on the floor speaks for itself. The cameraman backs up a step or two, and allows more light to reach him. From this position, it's clear that he was standing directly behind a huge stone pillar. The massive obelisk stretches mutely into the air, reaching a ceiling which is far too high for the meager light to reach; the end of the stone disappears into complete darkness above. The floor is made of the same dull rock, and while smooth, has not a hint of polish or decoration. The pillar, however, is not so blank; its surface is inscribed with thousands of distinct, but totally illegible characters. It appears to be some sort of writing, perhaps ancient
Sanskrit or some other tongue no longer used. The cameraman leaves his quiet recess, and steps out around the pillar. The sight which greets him is both grand, and sinister.
The enormous chamber is constructed entirely of the same dark, featureless stone as the pillar. Dozens of similar pillars, each wider than two men standing side by side, span the structure, supporting that invisible roof.
Two rows of these stretch in front of the cameraman, to the rear wall of the room; next to each of these, a tall pole supports a metal brazier, in which a pile of coals sheds the only light. Much of the room is shrouded in darkness, beyond the reach of the torches; it is obviously much larger than the portions which are visible. Under the cameraman's feet lies a carpet of finely woven black silk, which conceals the flagstones of the floor. The carpet apparently reaches the entire length of the room, for it is long enough to reach the end which the camera can see. At that end of the room stands the goal of the cameraman's search.
The flagstones are raised here, into a dais longer than the height of three men. Upon this dais sits a heavy stone altar. The altar is topped with two candelabras, apparently made of brass, which sit at either end of the stone.
Each has eight branches, filled with long black candles. All are lit. The altar's surface is also covered with a thick piece of black cloth; upon it is drawn a red capital "A", which appears to be dripping toward the floor.
Directly behind the altar is a wide black cauldron, which bubbles and steams from its unknown contents. Behind the cauldron stands the awesome form of
Tonnage. To his left stands Biff McGriff. Each is wearing the odd costumes they brought with them when they won the EMWC tag titles. Felicia and Louie kneel before the altar, draped in their cowled habits. Tonnage and Biff are staring intently into the cauldron, but look upward immediately after the cameraman steps toward the altar.)
[T] Approach, infidel. State the purpose for your invasion of our sanctum. [Cameraman] I, um, I was summoned here by- [Louie] He lies, master! No one called his ilk here, to sully the purity of our worship! He has been sent by some foul agent of those ignorant fools, who dare to deny your power!
[Felicia] Yes, dread lord! Punish him for this outrage! Teach him to show the proper respect for the worshippers of Armageddon!
[Biff] The words of my loyal consort carry much wisdom. He should be taught his proper place in the order of the world. But I wonder, how should we go about his punishment?
[Cameraman] (gulp) Punishment? [T] Perhaps we should make this insect learn his place. But for now, he will serve us, as a conduit to the unbelievers. They will hear our words...and know fear.
Acolytes, come to me. (Felicia and Louie walk up to the cauldron. Tonnage turns to Louie.) Servant. [Louie] (kneeling) Yes, my lord? [T] Do not kneel to me, faithful one. You are far too loyal and capable an ally to be treated in such a manner. There are many others who will be granted the priviledge of grovelling at my feet, those who are truly worthy of complete humiliation.
Amongst those ranks of feeblemind reside the fools to whom we have already shown our might. Fraternity Boys, you have been laid low, swept aside by the relentless tide that is the Church of Armageddon. Your empty threats and meaningless promises impress only those who share in your decadence and sloth. It is your lack of morals which has led to your downfall, and it is the reason you will *never* retake what was never yours to begin with. Our
EMWC Tag Team titles will never be permitted to reside in your corrupt presence again, of that you can be most sure.
[Biff] The fetid stench of the nonbelievers is corroding the EMWC, and the Church of
Armageddon has arisen to end all of that. No longer will such heathen swine as the Fraternity Boys be permitted to exist in any but the most menial of statures. And they are just the beginning. There are many more numbered among the unfaithful who need to be punished for their sins. And so it shall be, as proclaimed by the Gods of Armageddon.
[T] The God of Power has spoken. And he speaks with great wisdom. For too long, we have stood aside, and allowed the mindless, the ignorant, the decrepid, the weak to have their way. Too long, we have taken no action to correct the horrible mistakes which the huddled masses have wrought. No longer.
As we stood upon the pinnacle of greatness, and looked down upon the dens of filth and depravity that had been built by the weakness of humanity, we were rightfully disgusted. The sight which met our eyes was the epitome of all that is twisted and ruinous. It was the bastardization of everything that had been there before, and should be there still had the corruption not destroyed what once was beautiful. We gods looked at each other and asked,
"What had happened? Where is the wonder, the beauty, the splendor that had once been our world? What has become of the place from whence we came? Who has done this terrible thing?" The answer was obvious...it was you. It was all of you, the mindless members of society, who had allowed our world to plunge into the depths of lawlessness and godlessness. *You* had lost your way, and allowed all things to do the same. *You* had become the soulless heretics that we most despise. But we realized that you were not entirely at fault for this descent into madness. Your *heroes* had led you all astray.
Yes. Those whom you admire, whom you so blasphemously idolize, had started your feet down the path of debauchery. It was they who were most to blame for the horror that has befallen the world. Had they been more righteous, more willing to accept moral responsibility for themselves, perhaps you would not have become the shameful, wicked parodies of the people you were meant to be...were *ordained* to be. Is that truly the case? We shall never know.
What is clear, however, is that those whom you revere must be punished for their insolent and vile transgressions against this world. *Our* world.
In fact, it may be that their punishment is the only hope for your salvation... You have become godless, horrid phantoms of your true selves. You have denied that for which you were meant, instead embracing deceit, and permitting the pestilence of vice to pervade your beings. Long have we, the Gods of
Armageddon, looked down in shame upon your chaotic self-destruction, and spat in the face of all you now claim to be right and true. Long have we known that your presumptuousness will be your undoing, and that once again order and lawfulness will claim its own. Unfortunately, this has not come to pass without assistance. Your shameless promotion of decay and laxity has progressed to such a state that the natural order of things cannot reassert itself; nature has been shunted too long to reaffirm the proper status quo.
But this shall not continue much longer. You have lost your ability to control yourselves, because you have deprived yourselves of the order of morality. You have listened to false prophets, and believed them when they glamorized the violence and lasciviousness which has plagued society since time immemorial. Thus, the time has come for us to act.
You have chosen to worship greed, to accept as role models hypocrites, who deserve only to be mocked and scorned. Well, your decision has not gone unnoticed. You have left morality behind? So be it. *We* shall provide it for you instead. You have chosen the path of darkness and cruelty, rather than that of light and life? Very well. We shall be as dark and cruel as you require us to be. We shall lower ourselves to your level, in the faint hope that you might see the truth. Through darkness, we might bring you light. We shall punish the heathens whom you have chosen to worship, and bring unto them every foul happenstance that they would glorify. You choose to worship violence? Then we shall give you violence, in abundance. You choose to entertain yourselves with the misery of others? Then your heroes'
misery will bring you joy. And when you have seen that their ways are evil and backward, the Church of Armageddon will show you the way back to what was meant to be. You will follow us, because you know you have been led from the path you weer intended to take...and we shall return you to the stability and righteousness for which this world was made.
In order that we may do so, we must begin a long and arduous process of crushing those whom you wrongly hold in esteems. Two such loathsome creatures are the Awesome Alliance. How *dare* they use such words to describe themselves?! These miserable vermin infest our world and our sport, making a mockery of all that the Gods of Armageddon have made pure and wholesome.
Alliance, your vile ways have continued for too long. They will continue no longer. You will be the first of many examples that we shall set. You will be the first among many of the icons of sin to fall by the wayside, as the irresistable force of nature's will sweeps you into the abyss. Your obliteration will hail the change, and signal the new coming of the age of prosperity and intolerance for the evils that infest society. You will be sacrificed, to your followers' hunger for agony. And that sacrifice will begin the journey home.
You will be called back to the Temple of Armageddon, worm. Count yourself fortunate that we will be merciful this time. That is more than the
Alliance can expect. Now begone! (The cameraman, practically gasping with fear, turns tail and flees across the room, away from the altar. There are no more torches, and the picture eventually fades into blackness...)
