Blood, Sweat, and Tears, August 9: Part 13 of 13

10 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Amidst a sea of flash bulbs, "Wild Thing" Kevin Slater earns his nickname as he looks death square in the eye, and LEAPS OFF THE TOP OF THE CELL….floating through the sky, seemingly in slow motion, he comes closer, and closer, and closer, until….)
JS: WHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! TM: OHHHHHHH YEAHHHHHHHHHHHHHH RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIGHTEOUS!!!!!!!!! RMB: HEY!!! You stole my line!!! TM: Use one of mine! RMB: Umm..okay. No! It's not that small!!!! TM: Why you little- JS: GOOD HEAVEN ON EARTH!!!!! KEVIN SLATER HAS DESTROYED OUR BROADCAST TABLE
WITH A MASSIVE SPLASH OFF THE TOP OF THIS DAMNED CELL!!!!!! THE TABLE HAS BEEN
OBLITERATED AS HAS THE BODIES OF ALL THREE COMPETITORS!!!!!!
TM: Actually, Steggley….it looked like he caught Starks a lot harder than he caught Joe Reed. Somehow, Reed was off to the side, and did not catch the full impact of that death defying move.
JS: And it appears you are right…Joe Reed is actually on his feet!!!! And he has hurled first Starks, now Slater back into the cell!!!! The referee races to relock the door, somehow. Reed rolls Slater into the ring…and now he's going in after him. Joe Reed is on the attack and victory is imminent! He covers Slater!!!!
ONE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THREE!!!! NO!!! NO!!! BARNES SAYS IT WAS TWO!!! IT WAS TWO!!!! TM: You cannot get a closer count than that one right there!!!! Absolutely amazing! Kevin Slater is showing a lot more guts than I ever have given him credit for, and after that dive off the cell, I'm surprised we're not seeing them.
RMB: Wow…you did the straight and punch line in the same joke. TM: I'm talented like that. JS: Slater is still down though, and The Syndicate is screaming at Starks to wake up. Lau, Matsuoko, James, and Steele are trying to root their partner on to victory. Reed pullls Slater up to his feet…..fires him to the ropes….Slater back…clothesli-FLOATING DDT!!!! FLOATING DDT!!!! FLOATING DDT!!!!!! SLATER
NAILED HIM!!!!!! TM: Floating DDT? Isn't that-?!? (The camera zooms in on the suddenly grinning face of Luke Steele. Brian Lau stares Steele in the eyes, and it becomes apparent that The Syndicate has been fooled. A right hand from Steele that floors Lau makes it even clearer!)
JS: THE FIGHT IS ON!!!! THE SYNDICATE HAS ATTACKED LUKE STEELE!!! AND HERE COMES MYERS TO HELP OUT!!!! TM: STEELE TRICKED US!!!! HE'S IN THE DAMN CULT OF PERSONALITY!!!! HE TAUGHT SLATER THAT MOVE!!!!!! JS: SLATER COVERS!!!!! ONE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! KG: Ladies and gentlemen, "Crimson" Joe Reed has been ELIMINATED from this match. That means that either Kevin Slater or Tony Starks will be our NEW
WORLD CHAMPION!!!!! JS: SLATER SCORED THE PIN WITH THE FLOATING DDT!!!!! TM: REED'S GONE!!!!! That leaves Starks and Slater!!!!! JS: And Slater is down…Starks rolls into the ring, he's dazed as well!!!! TM: A dejected Reed's leaving the cell…and HE DIVES INTO THE FRACAS ON THE
FLOOR!!!! HE'S THROWING PUNCHES AT STEELE AND ANYONE ELSE AROUND!!!!
JS: It's getting wild out on the floor. And we're down to two men in the ring!!!! Starks immediately tosses Slater into the corner…and he works him over with those heavy shots to the head and body!!! Slater's in trouble…..Starks has got a pair of chairs…and he's setting Slater up top for something big here!!!! Something major is about to be pulled out of the arsenal of Tony Starks!!!
TM: Maybe a superplex…looks like a head and arm off the top!!!! He's trying to hook it…KICK TO THE GUT BY SLATER!!! ANOTHER KICK!!!!!
JS: SLATER HOOKS HIM….TORNADO DDT ON THE CHAIRS!!!!! THAT'S IT!!!!! ONE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THREE! SHOULDER UP!!!!! SHOULDER UP!!!! TM: Barnes says it was a two!!! That was too damn close!!!! JS: Slater's going for it all now!!! He's gonna finish this one!!!! He's calling for the Wild Ride!!!! He's gonna finish him off with the Wild Ride!!!!
Slater moves in for the side suplex….up into the torture rack…swings him-
OH!!!! He nailed Mike Barnes!!!! Slater nailed Mike Barnes, and he went down like a rock!!!!
TM: Barnes is down, and Slater dropped Starks out of the move!!! JS: Kevin Slater is trying to revive the referee…shaking him, slapping him a little, to no avail!!!
TM: What the-?!?! The camera man inside the cell is getting into the ring!!!! RMB: That's going a bit too far to get a closeup. JS: The cameraman's behind Slater….WHAMMMO!!!!! HE NAILED SLATER WITH THE
CAMERA!!!!! HE KNOCKED HIM FLAT!!!!!!!!! OH MY GOD!!!! WHO THE HELL IS
THAT?!?!? (Stegglet's question is answered as the camera man removes his hat…revealing…..) JS: TIGER CLAW!!!! IT'S TIGER CLAW!!!!!! HE KNOCKED SLATER OUT COLD!!!!! HE KNOCKED HIM OUT WITH THAT CAMERA!!!!!!! TM: Claw's helping Starks to his feet now…and he's telling Starks to hold
Slater. Starks hooks the arms of Slater….and Claw's gonna drill Slater with one of those shin kicks.
JS: Claw's dancing around…getting it loose….SHIN-HE HIT STARKS!!!! HE HIT STARKS!!!!!!! TM: STARKS IS OUT!!!! CLAW'S SHIN KICKS ARE LETHAL AND HE JUST KNOCKED TONY STARKS OUT COLD!!!!! JS: SLATER GRABS CLAW!!!!! NORTHERN LIGHTS…BOMB!!!!!!! HE PLANTED CLAW!!!!!! RMB: Mike Barnes is waking up!! Barnes is back in the action!!! JS: Slater pulls Starks up….he drags the thumb across the throat, Slater's gonna finish him off! KATHA JIME!!!! KATHA JIME!!!! THE JUDO CHOKE IS LOCKED
ON BY SLATER!!!!!!! TM: Starks is out though!!! Tony Starks is already out cold!!!! He can't break it, he can't defend himself…this isn't fair!!!!
JS: HE REVERSED IT!!! STARKS GOT THE ENERGY SOMEWHERE!!!! HE REVERSED THE HOLD LIKE ONLY HE WOULD KNOW HOW!!!!!!! TM: YES!!!! YES!!!! YES!!!! IT'S OVER!!! IT'S OVER!!!!!!!! JS: Starks has it slapped on….Mike Barnes is checking for the tap out!!!! TM: Slater's gonna have to put Starks through the cell again if he wants out of this one!!!
JS: Starks has it locked on!!! I think this match is over!!!! Slater can't find a way- what's he-?!? Arm behind Starks head…runs up the ropes…OH MY
GOD!!!!!!!!!!!! TM: SWINGING ACE CRUSHER!!!! SWINGING ACE CRUSHER!!!!! WHAT A GODDAMN COUNTER!!!!!!!!!!! RMB: OHHHHHHHHHH YEAHHHHHHHHHHHHH RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIGHTEOUS!!!!! JS: COVER!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ONE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! JS: SLATER DID IT!!!! SLATER DID IT!!!!!! KG: Your WINNER of the match…and NEW EMWC HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WORLD….."WILD THING"…..KEVVVVVVVVVIN SLAAAAAAAATER!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (THE CROWD EXPLODES WITH THE ANNOUNCEMENT!!!!!) JS: THE CROWD IS ON THEIR FEET!!!! MYERS AND STEELE ARE IN THE RING!!! LET
THE CELEBRATION BEGIN!!!!! THE CULT OF PERSONALITY WILL BE PARTYING ALL NIGHT
LONG HERE IN TEXAS!!!!! TM: No…no….no…no…no!!! WHY ME?!?!? Why does it have to be Slater!??!? WHY, WHY, WHY?!?!? RMB: Hot diggity dog, what a night!!!! JS: Kevin Slater is our new champion of the world!!!!! Fans, what a night it has been here at Blood, Sweat, and Tears deep in the heart of Texas. For Lori
Dane, Jon Keet-
TM: THE SYNDICATE HAS STORMED THE RING!!!! WE'VE GOT A WAR ON OUR HANDS!!!! THIS ISN'T OVER!!!! THE CELL IS FILLED WITH BATTLES!!!! (And with that…the lights cut to black.) TM: Did Blue forget to pay the power bill?!? RMB: DAMN!!!! TM: Not you, moron!!! RMB: Whew…. JS: Fans, we apologize for this. We have gone a bit over our time limit…maybe this is Texas Stadium's way of kicking us out.
TM: Stegglet, what the hell is that smell? RMB: Sorry. TM: Not you, moron!!! RMB: Whew…. JS: I'm not sure…but it smells like ga-WHOA!!!!!! (A single spotlight shines down from the center of the dome to illuminate a single person standing on top of the cell.)
JS: IT'S SERGE ANNIS!!!!! THE EPITOME OF EVIL!!!!!! RMB: Hasn't he done enough for one night?!?! JS: What the hell is he doing?!?! (With a coldness in his eyes unparalleled in the business, Annis raises his arms to sky…and pulls them down….)
JS: THE CELL!!!!! THE CELL!!!!! THE GODDAMN CELL IS ON FIRE!!!!!!!!! SERGE ANNIS HAS LIT THE DAMN CELL ON FIRE!!!!!!! TM: What the-!?! Let's get the hell out of here!!!! RMB: For once…Todd's right….we outtie!!!! JS: Fans, we're out of here!!!! Good night, everybody!!! (As the announce team runs for it, the image of a flaming cage is burned into our minds. And accompanying the haunting image…is a laugh…a cackle….the demonic laugh of Serge Annis echoing throughout Texas Stadium as he stands amongst a towering flame.)
(Fade to black.)
