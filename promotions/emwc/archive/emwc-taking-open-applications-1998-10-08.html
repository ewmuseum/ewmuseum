Taking Open Applications

8 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yep...the subject header is no lie. For the first time in ages, the EMWC is actually advertising that we have open spots. While I could fill these with people from our waiting list, or go out and recruit some people, I've decided to give someone a great opportunity.
In the past week or so, the EMWC has lost both "Playboy" Ronnie D and Curtis
"Thunder" Hansen to retirement. To many people, these two stars had become synonymous with the EMWC. So what I'm looking for are stars that can fill the giant boots left behind by these two great handlers, Ronnie D. and Adam
T. In addition to those two spots, we've got a few more open as well. So, this is your chance. This is the chance to break into the EMWC with a character destined for superstardom.
For ONE WEEK, after Friday, October 16 at midnight PST, we will no longer be accepting apps. So whether you're a big name or a small name, a hasbeen or a never was, we're considering each and every app that comes in in the next week.
At the bottom of this post, there's an application. Please fill that out, and along with a sample RP, send it in to:
[detail withheld] . NO AOL IM's PLEASE! Also DO NOT post your app from the website. So...there you have it. For one week, we're taking every application, every sample RP, and studying it, because we've got spots to fill, and we want you to fill them. So...good luck, and I look forward to seeing your work.
