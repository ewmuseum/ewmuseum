Obviously, you have done nothing to prepare for the devastation which awaits you. You have not seen what havok we can cause on those as insignificant as yourselves, and you have not studied the agony we have inflicted in the past. Had you done so, you would never have exposed yourselves to our wrath. Now, it is too late to turn back. What a shame...for you.
We are far more than advantaged in a match of this nature, fools. There are none better suited for it. What can you offer up against us? Your laughable overconfidence, audacity, and witless banter. What does Phalanx bring? The unmatchable power, the technical prowess, and the countless hours of acting in unison. Not to mention immense experience in just such a match. We have everything. You have nothing. Why you would even bother to present such a uselessly bold face is beyond comprehension. Your unenviable fate is as well known to all as it is to you. Your makeshift courage will merely make your crushing defeat that much more of a satisfaction. At last, your pitiful excuse for competition will be ground into the dust once and for all, and we shall assert beyond any contention that the Church of Armageddon reigns supreme. Enjoy your last moments, children...you will find them fleeting indeed.
