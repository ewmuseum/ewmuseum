Might As Well Make It Official Here Too

Aug 1, 2003, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I know there's some of you not on the mailing list... so lemme post here what I posted to it last night.
*** I'd better do this before my will gives out... In the past eight years or so, I've written a letter like this one several times... but somehow, someway...I always changed my mind before it could go out.
This time... I can't find any reason to not send it. The EMWC is officially closed as of today. Firstly, my apologies to everyone who RPd and stratted for this show for what amounts to nothing. There weren't many of you... but enough that I feel bad for pulling the rug out from under you.
My thanks to everyone still working hard every week [or so] on your stuff to put out the very best product in the game. My eternal thanks to everyone who has read our work for the past eight years and found some kind of enjoyment in it. We've always had a very special group of people who'd follow our stuff blindly even when they weren't involved with it... and that's something fairly unique in e-w. The knowledge that we produced material that entertained people who had no stake in it... it makes me feel good and it should do the same to all of you.
I could thank people for pages and pages. [name withheld] for creating this crazy place to begin with and drawing me into the sticky web of e-w. Guys like [name withheld] who gave us a chance when no one knew who the hell we were. [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld]... yeah, even Ronnie... they were all there when things really started rolling. [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld]... three absolute legends in this game who had no business taking a chance on a punk with an attitude who wanted to make as big of a splash as possible and didn't care how he did it. The big two... Bren and [name withheld]... the
"Franchises" of the EMWC forever. [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], Moxy, [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld],
[name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld]... more people trusting their hard work in the hands of someone who barely had a clue what he was doing.
[name withheld], [name withheld], Andy Doran, [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], Telly... everyone still sticking with this ship even when the captain looked ready to jump overboard. Like I said, I can list names for days... and anyone I skipped or missed, I'm very sorry... but know that my thanks go to you as well.
I'm sure you're all wanting to know why. Fact is... I probably really can't put it into words very well. It's a mixture of a lot of stuff...
oddly enough, the least of which is the lack of writers and the usual e-w bullshit. It's just... it's time, guys. That's the only way I know to say it. It's time.
Will I be back in e-w in some form? Eh... we'll see, I guess. Right now, I want to take a nice, long break... work on some other writing projects I've been pushing aside. I've got a few e-w things in mind though... I can't imagine not using them at some point... but... well, we'll see. If not, it's been one hell of a ride that has meant a lot to me for the past eight years. If so... well, then know that if I come back, I'll continue to put everything I have into what I do.
Umm... I've probably rambled long enough for the RSPWF vampires to rip me and for my "rivals" to light their celebratory torches. Enjoy it, boys.
One final thing... you all may not have always agreed with the way I've done things. It hasn't always been the right way... or even the wrong way... but it's always been my way.
And that... I'll never apologize for. Until we meet in this wacky e-w world again... Stay Extreme, kids. See ya soon. Christopher Blue EMWC Prez - 1996-2003
