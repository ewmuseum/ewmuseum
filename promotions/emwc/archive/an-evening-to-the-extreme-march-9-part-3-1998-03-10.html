An Evening To The Extreme, March 9: Part 3 of 6

10 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade up on the darkened interior of the Promenade Theatre in South-Central Los
Angeles. The credits from the film "Titanic" roll up on the screen -- and the lost love of poor Jack and Rose is still vivid within the room, the muffled crying of heartbroken movie patrons is clearly heard...the sniffling of noses....the gentle sobbing of those enrapt within the most popular film of all time is all palpable in the shot...
...As the lights are then turned on in the theatre... And we see that the entire audience_every single_member of the weeping, teary-eyed group.... Is an African-American man wearing a red and black sweatshirt that reads: "SHOWTIME IS CREED'S TIME!!" It is the Creed Army -- and as they continue to weep even after the warblings of Celine Dion have finished, a voice comes booming from the back of the
theatre:] Creed: Ain't this the most piss poor 'xcuse fo' a group o' men I ever saw in my life. Cryin' like a bunch of goddamn girls. [The red gloved warrior, dressed in his customary black streetclothes begins to make his way down to the front of the room. He slowly shakes his head, obviously disgusted by the sight of his entire fan base weeping in front of him. Several young men's voices are heard defending themselves...]
"But it's jus' so wrong!".... ..."It so unfair, Red Glove! It so unfair!".... "How could life be so, so cruel!"... "I feel so....betrayed...." [ The series of voices raises to a din -- and then one voice from the back is heard that clarifies the feeling of the house....]
"It jus' so wrong..............that a guy like Creed could win the Rumble In
The Rockies!" [An enormous moan is then heard, as if the mere thought is even too painful to contemplate...Creed stands in front of the screen, breaking now into a smile as he understands the nature of the "crying" by the Army.]
Creed: Yeah...yeah, you right. Y'all right, why it fair that a guy like Creed get a World Title shot?
"Oh, so bad...so bad...." Creed: A guy who carry the EMWC on_his_back the las' 8 months. "Unfair....so, unfair...." Creed: A guy who work ev'ry show, work ev'ry card, even when they ain't no one who know what the hell the EMWC be! A guy who leave the top ranked comp'ny in the world las' year to come to a place ain't no one care 'bout since '95! A guy who cleaned all the no work hunks of crap out o' the top o' the cards when ev'ryboy else o'ly after all the belts-- when Creed, the 'leventh ranked wrestler in the who' damn world don't get no shot at no US belt...no TV belt...no tag belt....
Nothin'. An' ain't never complain. Jus' keep workin'. Ev'ry_single_day. An' now the EMWC is the stuff. Big stars here. New guys with superpowers an' crap like that comin' in. Ev'rybody talkin' bout the EMWC, two matches in the IIeW, a guy like Creed, a guy like Creed who_o'ly_wrestle in the EMWC representin' in the D-Cup, comin'
hard....how many time? How many time Creed come at the people for the D-Cup? [The artificial weeping has stopped...and the response to Creed's question comes hard and clear from the Army...]
"FOURTEEN!! FOURTEEN TIMES!!" Creed: But why a guy like that deserve a title shot? That all I hear since Rumble In the Rockies. Why a guy like Creed, even if he scam both Byron an' Watkins the last month...even if he out workin' the D-Cup fo' the EM Dub...even if he been plannin' this return since NIA....
Why a guy like Creed get the shot? [Again, a moan goes up from the Army.] So...okay. Okay_MARKS_okay.... Anybody don't think this shot at Showtime should belong to Creed -- then get yo' whiny ass out of the backroom an' into the ring....
Any_damn_body in the EMWC...or anywhere else fo' that matter want_MY_SHOT! Want my shot that I been workin' fo' since November '96 when I got into this sport! Then lets throw, bruh. Lets throw. You call Blue -- you make the match, 'cause here my challenge to the 'tire world of professional wrestling....
'Tween now an' Showtime -- I will put my shot up 'gainst ANYONE who want it!! [Big POP from the Creed Army, many of the members now moving to their feet.] I will put it up ev'ry single week, if they make the match...I will put it up twice in one night...I will put it up in the ring, in the parking lot, in yo'
momma's house after Sunday Dinner.... It real....real easy...... You beat me --- you get the shot. You beat ME --- YOU get the shot. It real, real easy. [The sing-song chant, "Creed gonna kick yo' a-ass....Creed gonna kick yo' a-ass begins...] 'Course....the easy thing is to get the match.... 'Beatin' my ass.....now.....that ain't easy at all. All y'all marks better 'nderstand that. Beatin' Creed ass ain't half as easy as talkin' 'hind Creed back. B'cause there one thing....there one thing in this sport that the truth in 1998..... My name be Creed. I got one eye, I got one leg an' I got a line of people who want to kick my ass longer than the line of teenage girls waitin' to see "Titanic"....
But I the hardest son of a bitch in the building. 'Cause I the o'ly man in the world who will ....meet...and beat..... [The Creed Army roars the noted slogan along with the red gloved warrior.] ANYONE!! ANYWHERE!! ANYTIME!! Creed: You want Creed!! You want Creed, EMWC?!? Come_Get_Creed!!! [Creed raises his red left glove to the air, prompting the simultaneous thrusting of the left hands of every member of the Creed Army, the chants of his name roaring throughout the theatre as the shot fades....
"CREED!...CREED!...CREED!....CREED!] JS: WOW!!! Creed is putting it all on the line...every week till Showtime, he's putting his title shot on the line!!! Each and every week, against the best in the EMWC, and the best in all of wrestling. And tonight, he starts it off with a quest for vengeance. Three men, all who pinned Creed during his
"dark time" when he was laying down in the ring. Creed meets Maniac Jack, Alex
Canadi, and Cryer Corrigan in a gauntlet match. One at a time, until it's all over. Anyone who beats Creed, gets the title shot. This is gonna be something. Let's go down to Ken Graham for the introductions...
KG: Ladies and gentlemen...this is a gauntlet match with the World Title shot at Showtime on the line!
(BIG pop) KG: Introducing first...the man putting the title shot on the line...he is the winner of the 1998 Rumble In The Rockies...from the Bay Area in
California...THIS..._IS_...CREEEEEEEEEEEED!!!!! (ENORMOUS pop as "Ode To Joy" blasts over the PA. The lights drop down and the words, "ANYONE...ANYWHERE...ANYTIME" flash across the video wall. Through the curtain and into the crimson lights walks the man known as Creed. With his black trunks and boots, the only breaks in color are the red right kneebrace, red left glove, and red left eyepatch that make Creed Creed. He also wears a black mouthpiece in his mouth. The crowd begins to chant his name as he walks down the aisle. He slides into the ring to a HUGE pop, and takes his place on the midbuckle, thrusting his fist into the air. The Creed Army, covering the arena, return the gesture in stride, in particular, a certain masked man who bears an odd resemblance to the Masked Outlaw.)
KG: And opponent #1...MANIAC JACK!!!! ("Outlaw Blues" by Pat Benatar begins to play as the former disciple of Bobby
Taylor makes his way to the ring. The former EMWC fan is scrawny at best weighing barely 160. He wears tights and boots identical to Taylor, but lacks the confidence of the young cowboy. Jack steps to the apron, a look of fear in his eyes, as Creed glares at him coldly. Jack slowly steps into the ring-
Only to dive back to the floor as Creed charges at him shouting, "GET THE HELL
OUT OF MY RING!")
TM(laughing): Oh man...somebody get Jack a spine. Get him some new pants while you're at it cause Creed just scared the (BLEEP) outta him.
JS: Maniac Jack is on the floor. Creed's the guardian of the ring, and he won't let Jack back in. Jack tries to get back in...and Creed takes a wild swing at him, sending him back to the floor.
LD: Creed's lost it. This is not the same Creed we're used to seeing. TM: Nope...it's a lot better. Jack tryin-CREED SMACKED HIM UPSIDE THE HEAD!!! Oh man, this is great! Look at Jack...he's in shock. JS: Jack trying one more ti-And Creed slaps him across the face! This is horrible! He won't let Jack back in...the referee is reprimandin-Jack's in!!!
From behind...CLOTHESLINE ON CREED!!! UH OH!!! LD: CREED DIDN'T FEEL IT!!! He's looking at Jack like you'd look at a bug...KICK TO THE GUT...TRIANG-CREEDPLEX!!!! GOOD LORD!!!! HE PLANTED HIM!!!
JS: And Creed just kicked Jack to the floor...he tells referee Mike Barnes to count him. Barnes is a little surprised...but he's starting the count.
LD: It's almost like Creed's saying that this is his ring, and that Jack doesn't deserve to be in there. Barnes is still counting...and Jack's been counted out!
JS: That quickly...and Creed has beaten back one challenge to his title shot. Here comes Alex Canadi! He's next! Canadi on his way out-OH MY GOD!!! PESCADO BY CREED!!! CREED WENT FLYING!!!! GOOD LORD!!! He wiped out the youngster!!! LD: WOW!!! I've never seen that out of Creed. What a move!! And he hit
Canadi before he could get into the ring. This match may be over right now.
Creed probably could get the pin right now. TM: Creed's clearing people here at ringside...we're about to get EXTREME!!! JS: Creed's right over here next to us...SPINNING SPINEBUSTER ON THE BARRICADE!!! GOOD LORD!!! That could have snapped Canadi's spine like a twig! I can't believe it! Now what the hell is he doing? LD: He's lost his mind...Creed has snapped. Barnes is begging him to get back into the ring, but Creed's sending a message to the world with this one. He's pulling the ring steps over here...right in front of us now...good lord...what's he gonna do with those steps?
TM: He's gonna kill this kid!!! JS: Creed hooks Canadi...up into a supl-RED REIGN DRIVER!!! GOOD GOD!!! RED
REIGN DRIVER ON THE STEEL STEPS!!!! HE DROVE THAT KID'S HEAD AND NECK INTO THE
STEEL!!! OH MY GOD!!! LD: That suplex into a piledriver has Canadi in a lot of trouble. He's absolutely motionless.
JS: And Creed's not done yet...he's pulling poor Alex Canadi right back to his feet. He's clearing out our colleagues at the Mexican broadcast table. What's he gonn-CRIMSON TIDE THROUGH THE TABLE!!!!!!! CANADI WENT THROUGH THE TABLE!!!
What a mess!!! Spilnters everywhere...the monitors went flying...good lord. LD: And finally, finally...Creed rolls Canadi back into the ring. One handed cover...one...two...three!!! Creed clears him out...he's lost it!!! Listen to him!!!
C: MY HOUSE, BITCH, THIS IS CREED'S_HOUSE! JS: Creed may have snapped! This is truly his dark side. He has destroyed both Alex Canadi and Maniac Jack. Wow...and he's still got one more to go.
LD: Cryer Corrigan is on his way out here. Does he know what he's doing? If
I was him, I'd turn around and walk right back to the locker room.
JS: Creed's ready for Corrigan...he's waving him on...Corrigan slides in...he charges...quick go behind by Creed...GERMAN CREEDPLEX!!!! DROPPED HIM ON HIS
SKULL!!! Corrigan may be done for already. Can he get up from that?
LD: He's trying. Corrigan's crawling towards the corner...and he's back on his feet. Creed hooks him again...TIGER CREEDPLEX!!! GOOD LORD!!! Again right on the skull!!!
JS: This crowd is going nuts!!! This is the Creed they've wanted to see for months! Creed pulls him right back up...hooks him again...T-BONE CREEDPLEX!!!!
He's tossing this kid around like he's absolutely nothing!!! LD: Corrigan isn't moving...Creed pulls him back up, but he's like a rag doll. Just hanging there...Creed hooks the waistlock-Go behind by Corrigan...GERMAN
SUPLEX OF HIS OWN!!! Big move by Corrigan to turn the-OH LORD!!!
TM: CREED'S ON HIS FEET!!! CREED'S ON HIS GODDAMN FEET!!! JS: HOW DID-I can't believe it!!! He fires Corrigan to the ropes...CRIMSON
TIDE!!! THE ONE HANDED CHOKESLAM!!! HE DROVE HIM INTO THE MAT!!! And it's still not over...he's got Corrigan...he's got him up top...the crowd is chanting, they know what's coming...
"GFA...GFA...GFA" LD: Creed's got him up top...he's hooking Corrigan. Here it comes... JS: GOODNIGHT!!!! LD: FAREWELL!!!! TM: I ain't-Ah, what the hell...A-{BLEEPIN'{-MEN!!!!!! (In a sea of flashbulbs, Creed flies through the air in the GFA superbomb, and crashes down to the mat with incredible velocity.)
JS: ONE...TWO...THREE!!!!!!!!!!! ("Ode To Joy" starts again as Creed hurls Corrigan to the floor.) JS: HE DID IT!!! What a return to An Evening!!! Creed has beaten three men!!! (Among a sea of cheers, Creed climbs to the midbuckle, thrusting his fist into the air, igniting more cheers. And then-the universal gesture that means only one thing...
"I Want The Belt!") JS: Creed wants the belt and in just two months time, he'll be getting his shot!!! What a win for Creed!!! We've still got another hour to go so don't go anywhere fans! Oh what a night! Take a look at this...
[The view opens to a black background. Then, the following words appear in blocked, white text.]
What Others May Call BRUTALITY... [A quick montage of EMWC wrestlers using chairs, bells, 2x4 and iron pipes against each other.]
We Call It SURVIVAL. [A scene from the recent Rumble In The Rockies Pay-Per-View, specifically the
Rumble itself. A mass of wrestlers fighting each other for the victory.]
They Are The EPITOME Of Mankind, The Best That Humanity Has To Offer. [Ammon is shown flexing his *huge* arms from the inside of a wrestling ring at the camera.]
GRACE... [Several high-flying wrestlers doing amazing aerial moves from the top rope/turnbuckle.] SPEED... [A shot of 'Dangerous' Danny Dynamite doing his "Attitude Adjuster" on an unfortunate victim.]
POWER... [A shot of Sinister holding another wrestler in a vertical-suplex into a piledriver, the "Thunderstruck".]
AND NOBILITY. [Creed slowly raising his red-glove to a sold-out crowd, flashbulbs popping everywhere.] Unrestrained Violence And Savagery, This Is Not For The Light-Hearted. [Bloodied faces of wrestlers injured in battle, J.W. Hardin doing his "Cattle-Buster" on some poor soul.] Where Men Boldly Accomplish Feats Of Athleticism That No One Else Dares. [A montage of Sandini and Dynamite battling in their scaffold match, other scenes where EMWC wrestlers are doing suicidal moves.]
ANYTIME... ANYWHERE. This Is E.M.W.C. (Fade out) JS: Welcome back folks...we are live here in Rhode Island and we still have one more hour of action for you. Still to come, the tag team titles are on the line when The Phalanx meets the Awesome Alliance. Thomas William will meet Mad
Dog Watkins. Jeff "Madfox" Matthews meets the man coming off of the biggest win in his career, "Playboy" Ronnie D. And in the main event, The Syndicate of
J.W. Hardin and Tony Starks take on World Champion Gabriel Whitecross and Dirt
Dog Unique Allah in a stretcher match where the loser must leave the EMWC.
LD: We've also got comments coming from EMWC President Chris Blue, North American Champion Curtis Hansen, and much, much more. The announcement of another major superstar signing on with the EMWC...and much, much-
[The announcers are cut off in mid-sentence by a huge pop and it quickly becomes evident why. The Syndicate -- Tony Starks, Casey James, Tiger Claw,
J.W. Hardin and Brian Lau -- walk down the aisle... Starks and Claw lead the way, both men with stony expressions on their faces. Hardin is decked out in his trademark Stetson and duster, and chuckles at the antics of Casey James.
James has his hair in braids, held back by a black bandana, and replies to the crowd's heel pop by getting in the faces of nearby fans, shaking his head violently and screaming "Bahhhh! Bahhh!" at them. Brian Lau follows the group, dressed in an expensive suit, and wearing a twisted smirk on his face. The stable invades the ring. Starks grabs the ring microphone and looks out at the capacity crowd.]
TS: Big shock, huh? Yeah... I guess all the "insiders" aren't as smart as they really think they are. Jerks. Look at the men in this ring. Look at the level of talent. Look at the versatility. You want to know what the future of EMWC is? It ain't some damn martyr. It is us... pure and simple, double crosses and question marks.
I want to clear some stuff up real quick. Everyone knows that you cannot really go too far without your friends. See these guys?
They are my friends. We have known each other for a few years and have shared a lot of the same experiences. A lot of the same betrayals.
I guess I can't pass the mic without speaking on that PPV. Hansen, you are a hellified guy, for real. I got no problem with you. No problem with Slater. Dirt Dog...jesus, _brotha_. Boy you sealed your own fate. You had better get God's phone number from that moron boy of yours. You are gonna need to have God's ear. I am gonna own the rest of you. I am gonna put you and the entire EMWC through. No pullin' punches.
[Heel pop as Starks merely nods at the crowd and extends the mic to Hardin, who merely glares at the crowd from beneath his black stetson.]
JWH: Ya gotta know when to hold 'em... and know when to fold 'em. Which brings me to Bobby "Opie" Taylor. Ya pretended to be a cowboy fer a few weeks, but it was like yer momma just run outta quarters fer the horsie ride at the department store. The last quarter has fallen... Opie. The way I see it, ya only coulda showed yer face 'round these parts fer one reason... to finally
_learn_ from the real Outlaw. Yeah, when ya watched that IV goin' drip...
drip... drip...ya kept hearin' a lil' voice tellin' ya that Hardin has the answers. Nobody knows that like _this_ man. [Hardin hoists his thumb at Starks] I tore his knee up good and them months in the hospital and rehabbin' finally told him that Hardin has the answers. Look at him now... part o' the Syndicate, the biggest thing happenin' in rasslin'. Ya wanna know the truth, Opie? Come and get them answers. [Another heel pop as Hardin passes the mic to Casey James.] CJ: E... M... W... C! My god, it's good to be here. Last time Claw and I were here, everyone was on their feet, screaming at the tops of their lungs. A lot of words were spoken that night... Words that I hear a lot lately in the wrestling world... Hero... Legend... Icon... Lemme tell ya something...
A hero ain't nothin' but a sandwich. A legend ain't nothin' but a car. An icon? Icons are made of stone and wood, representations of folks long dead. What you see in this ring right ain't snack food, vehicles, or statues. What we _are_ is the most feared organization in wrestling today. We're the Syndicate. The baddest men on the planet. How do I know? Because I've fought each and every one of these men in the past. We're the best at what we do, and that's running things. This here... The EMWC... This is our turf now... _Our_turf!_ Can you dig it? Huh? Can you dig it?
[James jumps up to the second turnbuckle, holding his arms out wide.] CJ: CAAAAAAN YOUUUUUU DIIIIIIIIIG IIIIIIIIT? [The camera takes a position behind James, giving a shot of James' massive frame in front of a sea of jeering, screaming fans.]
CJ: I thought you might... Now, I give the mic to the man... The man with the master plan... Brian "Big Daddy Buff" Lau!
[The laughter of the rest of the Syndicate, and the shaking of Brian Lau's head clue the fans into the inside joke as Lau takes the mic.]
BL: Yet another frontier for us to conquer... The EMWC. We've been showing our presence here for some time now... Look at the men standing before you. J.W.
Hardin, a man who should be holding the EMWC World Title as we speak after
Rumble in the Rockies, and is the rightful holder of the Deschenes Cup. Tony
Starks, another man who deserves to be holding the title right now after a win over Gabriel Whitecross. Casey "Blackheart" James... A man whose talent is overshadowed only by his cruelty. And Tiger Claw... A fighting machine whose only purpose is to hurt people. These four men, banded together by my managerial expertise, are your nightmares. In the coming weeks, when you close your eyes, they'll be there. When you go into your fridge for a beer, you'll worry about taking a Blackheart Punch. When the EMWC wrestlers walk the halls here, they'll be looking in all directions, hoping to dodge the Cattle Buster.
Every breath that any of you take from now on, you'll thank your lord that the
Kataha Jime of Starks is not there to hamper it. Any pain you feel will remind you of the Golden Tiger Strike, or the Tiger Stretch...
The Syndicate is now your life. Deal with it, or fall by the wayside. That, fans and wrestlers of the EMWC, is all.
[The crowd gives a huge heel pop for the stable, who stay in the ring for a few seconds to drink in the jeers, raising their arms as if celebrating a victory.
After a while, Lau leads the group out of the ring, and back up the aisle amidst a hail of paper cups and crumpled up programs.]
JS: Ladies and gentlemen, this is a new era in the EMWC. The Syndicate is the most feared group of athletes in all of wrestling. Will the EMWC ever be the same? We'll find out in the next few weeks. At Rumble In The Rockies, the team of Tonnage and "Magnum" Biff McGriff with the help of Dakota Bundy defeated The Fraternity Boys to win the EMWC World Tag Team Titles. Tonight, they defend it against The Awesome Alliance. And my sources tell me...yep, there they are...
(A shot of the Greece section reveals the former EMWC World Tag Team Champions
The Fraterntiy Boys sitting in the crowd, beers in both hands.)
JS: The Fraternity Boys are here and you know that both of them want a shot at the winner here tonight. We've got some words from the champs...let's see what the Church has to say this week…
