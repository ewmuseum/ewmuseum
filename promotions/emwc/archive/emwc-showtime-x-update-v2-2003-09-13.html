Showtime X Update v.2

Sep 13, 2003, 12:00:00 AM

Update v.2 9-13-03 Sorry... been a busy week at work again. Working mandatory overtime everyday so I'm gonna make this one short. Next week... first match announced, I promise.
********* WELCOME! This is the second edition of the SHOWTIME X: ETERNALLY EXTREME Update! Some really good match ideas are being tossed around right now and I'm getting pretty excited about this. A lot of people are looking forward to this show so I want to make absolutely sure we have the best matches possible to present.
Still having some trouble contacting some old handlers. If you have contact with any former E handler, please pass along the info about the show to them.
I think almost all my e-mail about the show has been responded to. If you asked a question or offered your characters to be on the show and didn't get a reply, please let me know cause I may not have gotten the
e-mail. A few people have asked how I'll be handling booking for this show. Answer: I really don't know yet. I guess RPs would be the best way... but I don't expect some of the people to be able/to want to do a lot of
RPs. So, since it's the last show... it'll probably be mostly RP based with a mix of my own judgment. I guess that'll make sense. If anyone has suggestions on that, let me know.
And that's that. Questions/comments/concerns/etc? You know where I am. **************** We are 109 days away from the biggest event of our time...and the biggest event of all time, SHOWTIME X: ETERNALLY EXTREME. December
28th will set the wrestling world on it's collective ear as the greatest promotion it's ever known will take the grand stage for the final time.
In the upcoming weeks, we'll be announcing the various matches set up for this massive event... and if the rumors have the slightest bit of truth to them, we're all in for a night no one will _ever_ forget.
And starting _next_ week, this Update will get a very different feel to it. Stay tuned for that one.
Last week, we mentioned that names like Annis, Spector, Infierno, and
McBaine has been confirmed as appearing at the big event. This week?
We learned that ALEX MARTINEZ, KEVIN SLATER, "DREAMLOVER" TREY PORTER, and OTTO VERHOEVEN will be in attendance! Will they all be involved in matches? We haven't been able to confirm that, but their mere presence at the event will add to an already historic lineup of talent appearing.
Keep it tuned in to this Update each and every week for all the latest news on the event too big for anyone to miss.
********************** News & Notes @@@ ---We've seen several phone calls made from Chris Blue's office to Japan. Now who the hell could he possibly be trying to track down in
Japan? Better question... who _has_ he tracked down?
---It was reported earlier this week that Madison Square Garden has put a very substantial bid on the table to host SX. Many within the company believe that returning to the birthplace of the EMWC would be fitting way to close the door. Negotiations are ongoing with several venues...but I think you can cross a lot of the fifty arenas and stadiums we mentioned last week off the list at this point.
---Last week, we reported an attempt to put together a three way tag match between the Epitome of Cool, the Down Boys, and the Fraternity
Boys. One of the teams agreed to appear on the show... but we've yet to be informed in what capacity. The other two? No contact has been made as of yet. But there are _several_ former World Tag Team
Champions looking to step into the match in their place...some of which we haven't seen compete in years.
---Speculation continues to RUMBLE as to which annual E events will get tied into the final event. We talked last week about the possibility of a final King of the Death Match tournament... perhaps one to show the pretenders who did it first and who did it best. What other ideas will spill over onto the final show?
---The requests are pouring in. "We want the Woodshed!" "We want Frozen Ghost!" "WARGAMES!" So many classic match stipulations have been made eternally unforgettable by the EMWC... you couldn't fit them all in a Box.
@@@ Every week of this update, we'll be posting the dream card submitted by an EMWC employee... so this week, let's go with former World Junior
Heavyweight and North American Champion, Luke Kinsey!
Alex Martinez vs. Jeff Matthews vs. Caleb Temple - "These three goofs have been taking turns beating the crap out of each other since 1999, or thereabouts. Sin or Salvation, anyone? Temple taking Martinez's title, Matthews shocking everyone and winning it the next show out, the match at NIA 2001, Temple knocking up Veronica,
Sweet beJesus it just wouldn't be right if all three didn't get the chance to kill each other one more time. And really, the one thing the
EMWC has never done is kill someone on live TV, and who better then one of these sick bastards to do the deed?"
Sam Willis vs. Luke Kinsey - "GREATEST NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONS EVER, BITCH!" Trey Porter vs. Gabriel Whitecross - "Two of the guys responsible for the E blowing up in the mid 90's. Maybe the winner could get a lifetime's worth of Ben-Gay, or in DTP's case, some Rogaine." Simon Ezra vs. Serge Annis - "Just in case anyone forgets what the "E" in "EMWC" stands for, let's lock up two rejects of society in a cage, give the ol' Blood Angel plenty of his precious brown powder, hand Annis a Zippo or thirty and turn them loose! Blood, guts, gore, and a very important lesson for the kids out there: Being a freak isn't cool or unique or edgy, it means
God hates you. Fun for the whole family! Just make sure Moxy is nowhere near it."
Devon Case/Chris Courtade vs.Tiger Claw/Casey James - "The Kata Ha Jime against the guy who stole it. Wrestling's original badass versus wrestlings latest badass. A definite innovator versus imitator vibe here, although to be fair Case and Courtade did it better than most. Still, should be fun watching the Old Fart Patrol come out of the home to kick some imposter ass."
Jake Shaw vs. Steve Spector vs. Adam Rogers - "Granted, you'd sleep through the pre-show promos but the wrestling would be top notch...and I guess someone, somewhere is interested in that kinda thing." Frat Boys vs. Epitome of Cool vs. Down Boys - "It'd be a sin not to put these three teams against each other on the E's final blowout show. Or maybe, and this is just a thought, they could all beat up Strictly Business, sing another tune and walk out.
Either way, I smell buyrate~!" Eddie Van Gibson vs. Mark Langseth - "Yeah, fuck that thirty seven man match Mike Barnes suggested. The two best World champions EVER...and might I mention that yours truly holds a pinfall win over Mark Langseth, who similarly holds a pinfall win over Eddie Van Gibson....you do the math."
@@@ That's it for the second update. Two things to look forward to for the next one... A new format with a very special surprise. AND THE ANNOUNCEMENT OF THE VERY FIRST MATCH!!! Awww yeah, baby. Let's do this thing.
