Showtime X Update v6

22 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene comes to the outside of a Michigan state liquor store, where a disgraced and disheveled former EMWC Television champion walks out, holding a couple bottles of alcohol in each hand...
.... Hrm... Considering the amount of disgraced former EMWC TV champions, and considering his awful looking state, this guy could be nearly anyone who held that prestigious title! Well, from his very torn and filthy "Ego MAX" title, one can confirm that it is Tommy Stephens. An
EMWC reporter charges at the former EMWC'er, which gets Stephens so alarmed that he drops and shatters one of his precious drinks.]
Reporter: Tommy! Tommy! TS: Hey, back off, back off! I didn't do it! I didn't do anything last night, ok? The way she was acting and, and... And everything, she was...
Reporter: But, Tommy, I - TS: Look, please just don't take me in again, ok? I... I can't stay in that small little cell again, I can't! No booze, no TV, no... I can't do it, copper! I won't!
Reporter: Tommy! I'm not a cop! [Stephens relaxes a little bit, but he's still visibly on edge.] TS: You're not? What are you then? Concerned citizen? Vigilante? One of them rent-a-cops? Reporter: No, I was hired by EMWC to find you and try to see what you've been up to. I'm Bob. Bob from Reportemps.
["Bob" extends a hand, which Tommy looks at and backs away from.] TS: EMWC? What do they want from me? Those jerks fired me for just a little drinking here and there.
[Stephens shakes his head as he pulls out a bottle of Camo and swigs down an unhealthy dosage of vile liquor.]
TS: Elitists bastards. Bob: Umm... Well, shortly after you were... let go, EMWC decided to end their run for various reasons. But President Chris Blue decided to have one last show to cap off its history-making run and wanted to get back as many former EMWC wrestlers as possible.
TS: Why?!? Not like they ever wanted me there, anyway. [Stephens takes another quarter bottle full of his drink.] Bob: Hey, Tommy, this is a good thing. They want you back - being a former champion and one of the longest EMWC Television championship holders, it would be great to have you back.
TS: Yeah, tell 'em to shove it back in cause I'm doin' just fine by myself now, thank you. Got gigs all over this place, losing for the best and brightest. So shove it, little man!
[Stephens shoves Bob from Remportemps away and starts to stumble away.] Bob: But, Tommy, you could be reunited with Goku Waru! And the
Mysterious Suited Guy! TS: Bunch of big shot elites... stuffthings! [Bob's losing him... And, well, Stephens is losing himself as well. So desperate times call for desperate measures...]
Bob: But you could be reunited with Juan Vasquez and Luke Kinsey, be back with Ego MAX! [Tommy immediately turns around with wide eyes, looking almost alert.] TS: Juanny?!? [And with that, the shot fades out to our now-familiar generic television studio. Hark! What's that?! It's a god damn Showtime X logo! Kickass! The announcers seem brighter and cheerier already and neither has said a word. Damn, you're all gonna miss these magnificent bastards.]
JS: Tommy Stephens wants to be at Showtime X and so does everyone else in the wrestling world. It's the hottest ticket in the business!
TM: Tickets aren't even on sale yet. JS: Stop killing my hype. December 28th is just sixty-eight days away and the world is buzzing about the lineup we've put together so far.
Let's review the lineup before we go any further tonight... [A graphic pops up that reads "BORN TO BE WIRED" with photos of Bad Eye
McBaine and Serge Annis on both sides of it.]
JS: A barbed wire match that promises to be one of the bloodiest contests in the history of our business. On one side of the ring, a former Television Champion of this company, a man who has won countless titles in independent promotions across the country and one of the toughest bastards walking... Bad Eye McBaine.
TM: On the other, a former World Champion of this company, the only warrior who won the toughest World Title Triple Crown in the history of this sport, a former King of the Death Match finalist... the Epitome of
Evil, Serge Annis.
JS: We've yet to hear from either man but the words Annis passed along through the EMWC President when the match was announced are chilling to the bone. He asked for a match where he could take someone's eye...
again. TM: McBaine's not going to take too kindly to that, Steggs. JS: We've seen McBaine, both in the E and other places go through some of the most brutal matches in the history of the business. Remember his war with Casey James down in Texas?
TM: Was sick, Stegglet... just sick. And some of his matches in Grand
Isle were complete wars too. Serge Annis, Triple Crowner or not, has his work cut out for him.
JS: Indeed. Another match we announced a couple shows ago was the big ten man tag team match pitting the reunited Zokugun Sangai against a proverbial "dream team" of Junior Heavyweights.
TM: Last time, we announced that Team ZS would consist of TORA Wanizame, Kashan Akuma, Nijikon, and Takuya Nobu. Obviously, their final man has yet to be announced but we hope to hear from them in the upcoming weeks on this show.
JS: On the other side of the ring, previously we'd announced Juvenil
Infierno, November, and Michael Keening would compete... and this week, we're proud to announce that another former World Junior Heavyweight
Champion, Macht Kraftwerk, has joined the team!
TM: We haven't seen Macht in action in quite some time so it'll be interesting to see if he has any ring rust as he climbs into an EMWC ring for the final time.
JS: We said it last week... this match has the potential to steal the show. TM: Athletically, you may not find ten better competitors than the ones who'll be in there for this match. As the number one fan of the
Junior Heavyweight style, I'll be on pins and needles waiting for that one to go down.
JS: We're eagerly anticipating it... and so are the participants. Last time, we heard from Michael Keening. This week? Let's check in with someone from the ZS side... Takuya Nobu!
[Fade in on a titmouse.  Or rather, a bedroom slipper fashioned to resemble a titmouse.  Either way, it's menacing.  So watch out.]
Voice:  Come now!  Ice creams do not sell themselves. [Quick pan up through some lime-green hotpants, a sky blue Hello Kitty! nighty, a smirk, and a shock of hair that is alternately virginal white and and the kind of blue that, if it tasted like raspberries, they'd have to spell it RAZZ.]
Nobu:  Oi!  You!  Where are the sherbets?! [Nobu is standing in the middle of what appears to be an ice cream parlor, only the staff are all wearing lavender bunny suits and those costume-shop fairy wings.  One of the bunnies comes hopping from the back, a huge bucket of Rainbow Sherbet in between it's paws.]
Nobu:  This is more like it! [A slight "ahem" is heard from off-camera, and Nobu quickly snaps around, staring straight into the camera.]
Nobu:  Oh, that's right.  We're...  gonna win.  Go team. [Nobu quickly turns around, yelling "BREAK TIME" at the top of his lungs.  As the camera man backs out of the room, one of the bunnies plops a twister mat on the floor.  A vigorous game ensues as we fade to black.]
JS: Ooooohkay. TM: Beats a generic "Showtime X" backdrop, doesn't it? [The announcers cast a glance back at the banner hanging between them.] TM: ....or not. JS: Nobu looks... well, sorta ready... I guess. You just never know what to expect from Zokugun Sangai... inside the ring or out.
TM: Another match where we just don't know what to expect is the
Frozen Ghost match for the Eternal Triple Crown Championship that'll see Shane Destiny defend the title for the first, last, and only time against one of the men he defeated to claim the gold... Luke Kinsey.
JS: Kinsey and Destiny were on dueling hot streaks when the E decided to close it's doors... but at Showtime, one of those hot streaks will end permanently... on ice.
TM: Nice pun. JS: Indeed. I understand that both men are deep into their training regimens for this match...
TM: How do you train for a match that involves someone busting a
[BLEEPING] piece of ice on your skull? JS: ...good point. Regardless, we hope to catch up with both men next time on this show as we walk the road to Showtime.
TM: Speaking of the road to Showtime... wouldn't it be nice if we knew where the hell that road was going?
JS: Hehe... well, the fan poll is in top gear as we look to the people for the answer. We want this show to be all about the fans... and we want the fans to decide where the final EMWC event will take place.
Last time, we unveiled the five finalists. [The screen changes to display the five final venues... FleetCenter Staples Center Viking Hall Toronto Skydome Madison Square Garden.] JS: And after the first week of polling, we reget to announce two more venues have been cut from the list. The home of Showtime V and some great E memories, the FleetCenter in Boston has been removed from the choices.
TM: Plus, the home to No Imitations Accepted year after year, the
Toronto Skydome, has also been cut.
JS: So, after weeks of debate, we're down to three venues. Showtime X: Eternally Extreme will be held at either the EMWC's original hometown, New York City... our adopted hometown of Los Angeles, or the birthplace of Extreme, Philadelphia. Three great buildings... three historic venues... only one can be the big winner. The polling is continuing as we speak... send your votes to [detail withheld] and let your voice be heard!
TM: Extreme Honors?! JS: Soon, my friend... soon. The ballot is being prepared as we speak. One of the hot questions in the business right now is who will be named the greatest EMWC superstar of all time.
TM: I've heard a lot of names discussed... men like Tiger Claw, Eddie
Van Gibson, Mark Langseth... but I've yet to hear one name. The man who still holds the World Heavyweight Title.
JS: Chris Courtade has been very, very quiet during the entire buildup for this massive event. The questions have rumbled... who will he face, what will he do, will he even be there? Tonight...right now...
the Texan, the last man to hold the World Heavyweight Title breaks his silence. [The EMWC World Title, sitting, alone on top of a desk some dim lights shining off the gold plate. It's legacy entrenched in the history of the business, it's lore leftfor no one to question. To the men that have lost careers attempting to hold it, to the men that have left this business after losing it, fearing it could never be the same...it's the only fucking thing that's ever mattered. It's caused the best of friends to split at the seems, and it's caused near death to numerous family members that merely got in the way of the greed of men. Through barbed wire, through steel cages, through steel chairs that bent like sheet metal...it's the one thing that's remained constant throughout the existence of the EMWC, and it's now in the hands of the man that nearly burned the company to the ground. He sits alone, his head still shaven, his eyes still lurking into yours, wishing nothing more then see another man bleed in front of him. He traces a finger across the belt, top to bottom and side to side, as a smile cracks over his face, before quickly washing it away, as he speaks for the first time in a long time.]
CC: Been a long time...been a real, real long time. [Courtade nods his head.] CC: I've had a lotta time to reflect on it all, as the news came down. Had time to sit back, look at what it all took, what I all gave up for this. Regret set in...I'll admit...I no longer have a family, can't really turn to a friend, and beyond all that...I really can't look myself in the mirror.
[Courtade pauses ever so slightly.] It's too bad I don't really give a shit. [Courtade grins slightly.] CC: Ya see, that's what always seperated me from the rest...I just simply didn't care. Didn't give a damn what it took to get what I wanted. Didn't care who got hurt, hell I didn't even care if I got hurt. The ends always justified the means in my book, and before that bastard you call God, I swore by that...night in and night out.
[Courtade looks down, then back up towards the belt.] CC: Through every last man that stood before me, I never quit. I never listened to the hype...I never believed the horror stories. I took their best and I'm still here, and I'm still looking for one last fight. I'm look for one last mother fucker to cross my path...one last notch to add. It's clear who won't fight me again, that's for damn sure. The devil from the South's surely still gazing into a mirror talking about his dead kids, as if we give a shit. Hey, sorry...lil'
bastards got in my way...shit happened. I saw where big Alex talked about fighting one last time...
[Courtade wiggles his fingers arrogantly, then chuckles to himself.] CC: If only this were five years ago big man. I'm sure Matthews is in some dark alley, talking about fate, redemption, and all the other crap that he's feed himself for years. I saw where Case already has a match, against one of the icons in this business. Whoopty fucking do...he ducked me for the fifth year in a row, that's not a real big shock.
Since we started in this business, poor kid's never been able to leave the shadow that I cast...I guess he's finally trying to see the light.
[Courtade leans foward in the chair, and slaps the title belt once.] CC: But ya know who hasn't talked at all yet? Know who hasn't talked since I nearly snapped his frickin' neck?
[Courtade smirks yet again.] CC: You all recall him, I'm sure. He's the bastard that you all wanted to win at Showtime. The bastard that you all swore would beat me and anyone that crossed his path. The man that turned from a choir boy to the no frils roughneck. You know the man...
And he sure as hell knows me. Ya see, we've been knocking heads for a long, long time. Had what many call the best ladder match that didn't feature a queer ass
Canadian...and I don't even think I could climb a ladder again if I tried. He put his little green thumb into my neck, and attempted to paralyze me at War Games...and I piledrove his frickin' head into the concrete attempting to return the favor. He tried to steal Caleb's wife...I tried to kill her...
Some say tomato...some say toma-toe... [Courtade stops, pausing a second, collecting his thoughts..] CC: But through it all, we've been connected, joined at the hip if ya will. And since people have been throwing down challenges, and stipulations...I got one here for ya, if your willing to listen boy. Me and you...one more time. You pick a partner...and I'll pick a partner.
I figure that neck's gotta be a little sore...hell I even heard you were ordered to never return to a ring ever again...so why not make it a little more fair. Any mother fucker you can find...against me...and anyone I can find...
And we've alread established I don't have any friends in this business. One last time, Natural...one last time...the Legend Killer...versus the little engine that just fucking couldn't...the _true_ bad ass against the real life proof that wrestling instruction video's work.
One...last...time...you can prove that you had the balls to finally end my career... Or prove that you're still only half the fucking icon you think you are. [Courtade glares foward.] CC: Make the choice bastard...live for one last chance... Or die knowing that I buried your career in the cold concrete. [Fade.] JS: Chris Courtade with a challenge to Adam Rogers... for a tag team match? TM: More than just a tag team match, Steggy. I talked to Courtade earlier this week. This isn't your normal tag team match Courtade wants. This is a Dream Partner tag team match! This is a match where the company will go out for Courtade and Rogers and get them the partners they've always dreamed of!
JS: Can you imagine the kind of people those two would want to team with? Like Courtade says, he doesn't have any friends... and I don't know that Rogers does either after what he pulled the last time we saw him.
TM: I don't know who the hell would want to team with either of them... but my god, this could be a war of epic proportions.
JS: We haven't seen the Natural for months though. The last time we saw him, Courtade hit him with a piledriver on the concrete out in the parking lot. With a neck injury like that, can Rogers even wrestle again?
TM: Hopefully we'll find out in the weeks to come. JS: Other matches already announced... the Tag Team Scramble match to determine the Eternal World Tag Team Champions. We've got the Benjis,
Future Force, Eclipse, Strictly Business, the Towelz, the Rapturers, and a reunited Dynasty already confirmed. Seven teams already in and I understand even more will be announced on our next show.
TM: Raaaaawk. JS: With that many teams, you've gotta wonder what kind of rules we can expect for that match as well.
TM: Maybe I should enter that match. JS: Why? TM: Isn't it tradition for the ex-wrestler turned announcer to be a part of the battle royal-esque match?
JS: Hrmmm. TM: Think on that, Steggster. JS: And in our top billed match to date, Devon Case and Eddie Van
Gibson will collide in a match appropriately being labeled as "Battle of the Egos".
TM: Fitting. JS: We heard from EVG last week and the Hall of Famer seemed to be caught a little off guard by the announcement of his opponent.... but you can be Devon Case knows exactly who EVG is... and exactly what he needs to do to beat him.
TM: I'm going to try and get Case live via satellite here next week,
Steggy... he's gotta have a response towards EVG's words towards him.
JS: Indeed. Lots of stuff to look forward to next week... including what I understand will be Mark Langseth on hand to discuss whether or not his recent injuries will prevent him from competing in the final
EMWC event.
TM: Can't wait for that one. JS: Finally, fans... on the last Update, we saw a challenge from Alex
Martinez. Not aimed at any other wrestler... but rather at the officials of the EMWC. They all want Martinez at Showtime... we all want Martinez at Showtime... the fans all want Martinez at Showtime.
But Alex Martinez will not be at Showtime _unless_ the officials give him the one match he's always wanted...
....The Box. TM: A few weeks ago, you asked me if I thought the Killing Box would take center stage for the final time at Showtime X. I said I hoped it wouldn't... I said that after what happened to Casey James in that demonic structure, it should never be used again.
JS: And now? TM: And now... I don't know. A final EMWC event without Alex Martinez just doesn't seem right. It's just not right at all... but at the same time, is it worth the risk to give him what he wants?
JS: That's the question that rested heavily on the shoulders of the
EMWC Board of Directors over the past seven days... in particular, on the shoulders of this man... the man who we promised would give
Martinez his answer this week on Update. Ladies and gentlemen... the
EMWC President.
[We cut to the EMWC President, Chris Blue, seated behind a wooden desk, eyes turned down at the table before him.]
CB: Thanks, Jon. Fans, tonight I come before you to answer a question... a burning question... an ultimatum if you will, issued by one man. Alex Martinez, the Institution of the EMWC, has said that he will only appear on the final EMWC event... he will only appear on
Showtime X: Eternally Extreme if we give him the match he never got the chance to appear in...
....until now. [Blue slowly looks up.] CB: You've backed me into a corner, Alex. All along we've said this event is about the fans... what they want... their reward for all their years of support.
They want you. They want the Box. [Blue pauses.] CB: They got it. [The boss looks down again, picking up a remote control.] CB: When I heard your words last week, I knew you were crazier than ever. [Blue gestures to a TV over his shoulder.] CB: Lucky for you... there's someone just as crazy. See you at Showtime, Alex. [Blue clicks a button on the remote control and the screen comes to life... Scene opens with a shot of an open locker. Inside this locker, hangs a few items. Wrestling gear, towels, a silver chain with crucifix and a black cut off shirt. A hand slowly enters into view and grabs a towel from the locker and the camera follows the action of the arm. It belongs to Jeff Matthews. He quietly places the towel around his neck and just lowers his head. It appears that he has been working out, and has exhausted himself.]
JM: One last time. One last show. [sighs] JM: Although it pains me to see the end of the EMWC, it brings a final opportunity to light. It gives me a chance to finally right a few wrongs before we all say goodbye to the EMWC. As everyone saw, I beat
Juan Vasquez to become the #1 contender. I was labeled the new franchise by Mark Langseth. But I wasn't given my chance to regain the gold.
[pauses] JM: However, that is not what I want. That is not what I need as we close the door to our storied existence. A history that has seen me rise and fall. So before the world, before the fans, before all the other competitors in the EMWC.. I need to close a chapter of my life...
once and for all. [Jeff grabs the crucifix around his neck and lifts it up a slight bit] JM: Only two men in the EMWC ever made me question my sanity, question my faith and question all that I ever had accomplished.
[closing his eyes, knowing that what he is about to say could very well etch his fate in stone]
JM: No one man has ever crushed me.. mind, body and soul... like Caleb
Temple. When I tuck my daughters into bed, I see the fear still in their eyes. When I lay down next to my wife, I sense the pain and anguish I brought into her life because of Caleb Temple. I can't live without knowing that part of my life is over. I can't go on knowing that I didn't do everything I could possibly do to end the menace, to end the evil.
And then... [deep sigh as he keeps his eyes closed] JM: Alex always wanted me to forgive him. He always apologized for what happened in Hawaii. But as much as I wanted to, I couldn't. I couldn't erase the memories of having a man who I trusted with my own life, having stabbed me in the back. Caleb hid his true intentions, Alex. He wore the same face at all times... but you, you looked me in the face and then thrust the cold steel into my heart. You tore away my very understanding of what a friend was.
[pausing] JM: Caleb took away my sanity... Alex took away my compassion.... and now... I think its time that I, got my revenge... that I got my real redemption.
At Eternally Extreme.... Jeff Matthews... Caleb Temple.... Alex Martinez.... [opening his eyes... filled with rage and anger] JM: Killing Box.... [Fade to black.]
