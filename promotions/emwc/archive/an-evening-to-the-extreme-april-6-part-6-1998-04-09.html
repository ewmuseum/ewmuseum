An Evening To The Extreme, April 6: Part 6 of 11 (Hour Two, Part 3)

9 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[JJ] Uhhhhhh, let's just get to the match. And what a doozy it is! The Fraternity
Boys will be in rough waters tonight, as they try to take the EMWC World Tag
Team championship belts away from two people whom we know very well, Tonnage and the Magnum, Biff McGriff, Phalanx!
[EB] I must admit, I've never seen the big boys acting like this, but it doesn't matter. They still have all the talent in *that* ring, let me assure you,
Jomers. And when they get the Brat Boys in there, we can all enjoy the spanking Phalanx will give them. Imagine the arrogance of those twits, thinking they can match up to the Beast and the Magnum...no holds barred, no less! Sing it up, ring boy.
[Announcer] Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is scheduled for one fall, and is a No Holds Barred match for the EMWC World Tag Team titles! First, the challengers...former EMWC Tag Team champions, and the men who lost the belts to the *current* champions...Chris O'Brien...and Gus
Zampitella...the Fraterrrrrrrnityyyyyyyy Boyyyyyyyys! (The Frat Boys head for the ring, to a rousing chorus of cheers. They slap hands with members of the audience all the way to the ring. Chris is carrying a large bag over his shoulder, and he hands the bag to one of the Greeks, who are seated at ringside.)
[EB] What is that crap?! These dweebs have a bunch of greasy little helpers behind the barricade! They're just trying to duck their own stipulations!
[JJ] Well, Eddy, as long as the Greeks stay out of the ring, everything should be kosher. Besides, I don't think they have contracts with the EMWC, do they?
[EB] Oh very cute, Jomers. It's just like you to side with the chumps, anyway! Let's get to the *real* tag team in this match! [KG] And their opponents...at an awesome total combined weight of *nine hundred and twenty two pounds*!...from Albany, New York and Venice Beach, California respectively...they are the leaders of the Church of Armageddon...they are
Tonnage, the God of Destruction, and "Magnum" Biff McGriff, the God of Power...
they are the EMWC World Tag Team champions...they are...Phaaaaaaaaalaaaaaaaanx! ("Riders of Doom" from the Conan the Barbarian soundtrack blares from the PA system, as the two massive men step through the curtains. Both are wearing hooded black robes. Tonnage is carrying an ebon staff, whose top is decorated by an antlered skull. Each of the eye sockets bears a red gem, which glows with an ominous light. McGriff is bedecked with a longsword at his belt, a halberd in his right hand and a round shield on his left; the shield bears the dripping red "A" insignia of the Church. They drop everything they are carrying as they hit the ring. Removing their robes reveals black trunks and boots on McGriff, and a black singlet on Tonnage, which bears the Church insignia on the chest.)
[EB] My God, them boys are big! I never get used to seeing those guys! [JJ] They are the Clydesdales of wrestling, Eddy! Especially the Beast! I think he's actually put on a couple of pounds!
[EB] I hope not! He might start draining the protein out of my body by osmosis! Those morons can call him all the names they like, nobody else on Earth is that big and that muscular at the same time! I can't wait to see Phalanx rip the appendages off of these paper challengers!
[JJ] That remains to be seen, Eddy, but here's your chance! The bell sounds, and we're underway! Biff starts off with Chris O'Brien now...and it breaks down into fisticuffs immediately! Chris trying to brawl with the Magnum, and this probably isn't a good idea! Biff catches him with a *hard* shot across the nose, and hoists him overhead in a press slam! Effortless execution for the powerhouse of Phalanx! He hurls Chris across the ring into his corner! Gus
Zampitella leaps into the ring, joining his partner! Tonnage steps over the top rope! All four men are throwing punches in there! *That* didn't take long!
[EB] I can't believe you called McGriff the powerhouse, Jomers...and you were right, for once! That is scary! Chrissie is trying desperately to hold his own with the Magnum, and Gertrude ain't havin' any easier of a time with the Beast!
Both pairs of men trading shots, and naturally, Phalanx is coming out on top! Biff unloads on Chrissie with a big left, but the girly man comes back with a forearm smash! The Beast lays a twenty five pound knife edge on the chest of Gertrude, who flies back into the corner, but rebounds with a haymaker that almost backs the big man up a step! Chrissie lays another shot on Biff's head, but the Magnum gives him a boot to the left kneecap! He bends over, and Biff lands a big kneelift to the side of the head! Chrissie hits the mat! Gertrude gets a Tonnage elbow in the head, but grabs the trunks and pulls him to the ropes! The big man slips out between the ropes to the floor! Gertrude grabs the top rope...and slingshots over onto the Beast! He bounces off, but the big man drops to one knee!
[EB] Gus got a bit rocked with the impact, but he has the advantage! He grabs up a chair from ringside! He folds it up, and swings! Tonnage gets an arm up!
Oooh, what impact! That must have stung! The forearm of the big man is beet red from that chairshot, but he lands a counter punch to Gus' sternum!
The Frat Boy staggers back, and Tonnage charges with a huge shoulder tackle that drives Gus into the ring steps! Meanwhile, Biff is pounding on the face of Chris! Biff hoists him up, and a massive boot to Chris' face sends him out of the ring as well! Biff charges through the ropes to the floor and reaches into his tights! He has a pair of brass knuckles! Chris gets up and POW!
What a shot between the eyes by the Magnum! Chris drops to the floor! Tonnage has picked up Gus, and lifts him overhead with one hand, by the neck! The big man is choking the life out of the Frat Boy! Gus is turning blue! He's flailing for air...and he lands a kick to the family jewels! Tonnage staggers back, grabbing his crotch, and Gus is gasping for breath!
[EB] Ah, just lucky! Biff, on the other hand, is making easy work out of Chrissie! He's stomping away on the Brat Boys' head, and the girly man will soon be put away! Biff with a fist drop...and somehow, Chrissie got out of the way!
Didn't hurt the Magnum, of course, not with the knucks on, so he just aims another punch at the chump! Chrissie ducks it, and lands an uppercut that stuns McGriff! The girly man motions to his slimy cohorts...and one of them throws him a Sega Saturn! He winds up, and *bashes* Biff over the head! The damned thing shattered! Biff might have been busted open! Gertrude is slowly getting back to his feet, and the Beast is doing the same! Now the *other*
Brat Boy is looking for a free ride, and his filthy little buddies are only too happy to oblige! Into the bag...and out comes a frying pan! Gertrude gets his paws on it and...he whacks the Beast in the middle of the back!
The big man isn't down, but he's hurt! Gertrude winds up again, and SMACK! Right in the spine! Tonnage is down on all fours! The Brat Boy clambers up to the apron...and crashes down with the pan to the back a third time!
[JJ] A devastating attack by Gus, and the Frat Boys are turning up the heat! It looks like Biff was lucky there, because he doesn't seem to be be bleeding, but Chris is pounding away with lefts and rights! Another Greek gives up an item...this time it's a squeegee! Chris gives a big side swipe, right to
Biff's kisser! The Magnum hits the floor, and Chris is going for a pin already! The ref is over!
1!!! And Biff is out in a jiffy! Gus, meanwhile, has landed several more shots with the pan on Tonnage's back, and is winding up for another one! The big man is having trouble getting up, but he's standing...Gus steps in with a big swipe! The Beast ducks! Pow! Gus nails the ring post instead! Man, the vibrations must be killer! Gus drops the pan in agony, holding his hands under his armpits! Tonnage grabs him and...my God, a suplex onto the ring steps! What a crushing blow by the big man!
[EB] Ha! Chrissie tried another shot with the squeegee, but the Magnum kicked it out of his hand, and landed another shot with the knucks! The girly man goes flying across the concrete, right to the aisle entrance! Biff is shaking out the cobwebs, and he *nails* Chrissie with a running lariat! He and the Beast grab up their respective Brat Boys and...double Irish whip into the same ring post! Awesome! Now they're trading off! Biff smashes Gertrude in the face with a massive brass knuckle bomb! Tonnage scoops Chrissie off his feet...
power slam onto the bare concrete! Oh man! The big man is still rubbing at his back, but Chrissie just about folds in half the wrong way! *That* is pain, Jomers, and that is what Phalanx is all about! The Beast is stomping away at Chrissie's back now, and Biff is laying shot after shot into Gertrude's ugly mug with those knucks, which can only result in an improvement! Yuck!
Anyway, Biff's doing the jackhammer routine on Gertrude's face, and the Brat
Boy is split open! The Magnum picks him up, and puts on a hammerlock...he body slams Gertrude on the arm, right on those flimsy gym mats! The big man grabs up Chrissie, and gives him a seven foot body slam of his own, on the concrete!
[JJ] Phalanx are up to their old tricks, Eddy! They love to outmuscle their opponents! Gus is hurting after those shots to the head, and the slam on his arm couldn't have helped! Biff lays out the arm, and drops a big elbow on it! Gus clutches at his own elbow, and that must have hurt! Biff stomps the elbow out flat again, and drops an elbow- that hits only gym mat! Gus rolls out of the way, and fires back with a thumb to the eye! McGriff doubles over in pain, and
Gus lands a DDT on the floor! Gus up...and drops a knee onto the back of the Magnum's head! Tonnage has dragged Chris back to his feet, and drives him back first into the barricade! Chris is leaning heavily on that steel rail, trying to stay afloat here! Tonnage backs up a step, and charges in with an avalanche! Chris sidestepped him! The big man impacts on the railing, on his ribcage! He crashes to the concrete floor! Chris drags himself back to his feet...and one of the Greeks tosses him a Jello mould! He leaps onto the Beast, and pounds on his ribs with that metal mould! The reaction on the big man's face is obvious, but he manages to lay a big right hand on Chris'
head, and that send him sprawling! Gus has been kicking away at Biff, but the Magnum grabs that left foot and twists it violently! Gus hits the mat howling, grabbing his ankle!
[EB] Quick thinking by the Magnum, and everybody here is taking their licks! Chrissie is recovering from the beating he just took, and the big man is coming back from the same! Chrissie manages to get to his girlie feet, and runs at the Beast with a kneelift to the face! He swings with a left...and the big man catches his puny fist in that massive hand of his! He spins, and slams Chrissie into the barricade again! Biff is up, and he's dragging
Gertrude to the ringside barricade! He pulls off a section, and threads that left foot between the bars! Biff drops a big splash down onto the whole contraption, and Gertrude's ankle gets bent in all the wrong places! Biff stomps away on the barricade, crushing Gertrude's ankle even more! The Beast has regained his footing, although he's still shaky from the slight pummelling his ribs and back have taken! He grabs up Chrissie again...and slaps on a vicious bearhug! Listen to that girly man scream! The ref has gotten over there, and he's checking for a submission! Chrissie's calling for the bell,
I can see it! C'mon, ref, do your job! Nice job, you hack! That chump gave up, but you stood by and gave him time to rake the big man's face! I knew those suits had it in for Phalanx! Ain't much left of Chrissie, though, he's takin' a breather on the concrete while the Beast is rubbing his eyes!
[JJ] Please, Eddy! I really doubt the ref has any favorites here! Gus has managed to pull his leg out of the barricade, but Biff isn't letting up! He grabs the ankle between his own legs, and drives down to the floor! Mr. Perfect would have been proud of that! Biff backs up...and charges with a falling double axehandle to the ankle! Gus is in real trouble here! Biff grabs the ankle, and just starts wrenching at it! Gus is screaming again, and his arms are flailing for any escape...and he whips a hard knee up and over into Biff's face! Ouch! That caught the Magnum just above the eye, and he's cut open!
Blood is seeping into the eye of McGriff, I think! He just wipes it aside, and pounces on Gus! They're back to trading punches again! Tonnage has recovered his position, but Chris is far from in that good a shape! He's in a great deal of pain! That bearhug really took its toll on him! The Beast grabs ahold of Chris's neck, and hoists him into the air! One handed choke slam, applied with authority! Chris's back and head were crushed into the concrete, and he is *down*! Tonnage stands fully up...and staggers backward!
Keeping himself on top of this situation took more out of him than he was willing to admit! He's still on his feet, but he's only slowly making his way back to the ring!
[EB] *Now* we're gonna see some carnage! When Phalanx gets their hands on one of the Brat Boys at the same time, this match is over! The big man is heading back to the ring, albeit at a snail's pace, and meanwhile, Biff and Gertrude are beating the stuffing out of each other! Gertie gets a an old Eclipse coffee mug passed to him by one of those greaseballs at ringside, and he winds up! Biff has that brass knuckled fist of his cocked as well! WHAM!
Gertie shatters the mug over Biff's head, and that set of knucks grinds its way into that split forehead! The blood is coming out in droves now, and both men collapse to the floor! Tonnage has reached the ring now, and he's leaning heavily on the apron! He comes around the post, and drops a *huge* leg across
Gertie's face! He's over to help Biff up to his feet, and he grabs a towel from one of the ringside tables! He tends to that cut over Biff's eye, wiping the blood away! Both men are in bad shape, but they have Gertie right where they want him! Tonnage grabs the girly man by the legs, and pulls them under his arms! Could be a Boston crab, looking for a submission! No, wait! Biff has that folded chair again! Tonnage leans forward...slingshot! POW! Right into a chairshot from the Magnum, into Gertie's face!
[JJ] My God, what a shot! Gus is laid out on the concrete, his face a bloody mess! Tonnage lays down for the pin, and the ref is over for the count!
1!!! 2!!! Gus got an arm up! I can't believe it! He took one of the hardest chairshots
I've ever seen, and managed to kick out! This guy wants the belts badly!
Biff drags Gus back to his feet, and sets him up! Powerbomb on the floor! Devastating! He and Tonnage are planning something else...wait a second! Chris has come back down the aisle! He's managed to pull himself along the barricade, and is standing at the end of the aisle! He's got the squeegee again, and he charges! A running shot to the side of Biff's head! And one on Tonnage's ribs! I can't believe the size of the fight in these dogs!
Chris collapses, after that titanic effort! All four men are lying next to the ring, two of them bleeding profusely from the head! Tonnage looks to be in the best shape, but the punishment he's suffered to the back and ribs doesn't exactly make that a sure bet! He manages to stand, but he's still holding his abdomen! He reaches down with his free hand, and pulls his partner to his feet! These two titans practically have to lean on each other to stay upright, but they seem to be the ones who are ahead right now! I can't imagine what the Frat Boys must be feeling, if Phalanx are winning!
[EB] I can, Jomers, and it does my heart good! The big man and Biff are hurt, for sure, but the Brat Boys are in it way worse! Phalanx seems almost to push off each other...and drop twin elbows into the sternums of their opponents! Tonnage slowly gets to his feet, and drags Gertie up as well! He grabs the girly man round the chest...a massive belly to belly suplex on that floor! Gertie just got flattened by almost six hundred pounds of spine crushing power! That took a fair bit out of the big man, though, because he seems to be taking another break on the floor himself! At the same time,
Biff is starting to stir! He gets up on shaky legs, and drops on Chrissie's face with the brass knuckles! I can't believe he's managed to keep those things on the entire match! Chrissie is almost in convulsions after that shot, but somehow he's turning over! He grabs the barricade, and starts to drag himself back to a vertical base! He's up to one knee, but his back is giving him great trouble, and rightfully so after the job that the Beast did on it! He's back to a standing position...and one of those smelly hooligans back there hands him a spray can! What now, chemical warfare?! They'll stoop to any level!
[JJ] Anything goes, Eddy! Chris has every right to use that...whatever-it-is in this match! He's got the cover off now, and that nozzle looks awfully big for your standard aerosol... Tonnage has returned to his feet, and he's heading for Chris! He takes a step...silly string! Chris just sprayed the
Beast in the face with silly string, blinding him! The big man paws at his face, wiping it away as best he can! Chris has the can, and whacks Tonnage across the forehead with it! The big man drops to a sitting position, dazed and unable to see! Chris manages a weak looking kick to the big man's chest, and then a left hand with a bit more mustard behind it! He climbs up on the apron, and stomps on Tonnage's head! Chris backing up...spinning elbow into the big man's face! Silly string is all over the place, and Tonnage still has a fair bit in his eyes! Wait, Biff is on his feet! He pulls a small bag out of his trunks! Chris doesn't see him coming! Biff wheels him around, and whips a handful of...something out of that bag! He throws it into Chris'
face! It looks like...iron filings! My God, that could cause permanent damage! Chris is clawing at his face, staggering blindly around the ring...
and Tonnage catches him with a stiff front elbow strike to the jaw! Chris hits the floor in a hurry!
[EB] Oh yeah, Phalanx, lay it on thick! The Beast has managed to wipe most of that silly string out of his eyes, using that bloody towel Biff needed earlier!
Too bad they don't have another one, because Biff's cut is oozing some more! Biff uses the bag to swipe a bit of it out of his face, and Phalanx is back to their feet again! Gertie is stirring, but I don't think he'll have enough to save Chrissie from the pounding he's on the verge of taking! Tonnage gets ahold of Chris' neck...he and Biff are going to put him away! Wait, Gertie leaps! He knocks the Beast over with the tackle, and a wild punch manages to catch Biff on the left cheekbone, sending him to the mat! I have to give him credit, Jomers, Gertie can take a bit of a beating! He's pretty much drained after that effort, but he's saved his partner from what would have been a finishing hold for sure! Tonnage wasn't really affected by that feeble little charge, but he's had a hard enough time standing as it is after what's happened so far! Biff isn't much better off! Gertie manages to get hold of Tonnage by the mask, and he's helping Chrissie to his feet as well! Gertie has a chair, and Chrissie has that frying pan that his partner dropped earlier! They're winding up...WHAM! Gertie caught the big man in the face with the chair, and his partner landed a shot with the pan into the back of one of the Beast's legs! Tonnage crashes to the floor like a big oak tree!
[JJ] The resilience showed by both teams here is out of this world! It doesn't matter who wins this match, Eddy! Both teams should be proud of the efforts they've given here!
Chris has fallen back to the floor! Gus is still on his feet, and he lands another chairshot on the ribs of Tonnage! The big man is hurting right now!
Gus goes for a cover! 1!!! 2!!! Tonnage kicks out! Unbelievable! Gus looks frustrated...and Biff drags him off the big man! Gus throws a right hand, and Biff answers with one of his own! Those brass knuckles have finally slid off, but the blow had almost as much effect! Another blow by Gus, and it's answered again by the Magnum! These two men have suffered punishment that would kill most four legged mammals, and they're still ready for more! Biff with a thumb to Gus' throat! The Frat Boy staggers backward, gasping one more time!
Tonnage has regained his feet, as has Chris! Biff picks up a chair, and *nails* Chris in the head! He flies over the barricade into the front row of Greeks!
