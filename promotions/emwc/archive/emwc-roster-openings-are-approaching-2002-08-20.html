Roster Openings Are Approaching

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yeah, this post is what the topic says. After this coming PPV, I expect to have a few roster spots in all three divisions [heavies, juniors, tags] and I thought I'd make it official with a post on here. Still reading EMWC shows and thinking "I could kick Chris Courtade's ass!"? ... then I have two things to say to you. 1] Who couldn't? And 2] Send in your app, junior! If you've already sent me an app within the last month or so, no need to do it again... I'm still considering those as well. But I'm really looking for characters/handlers I'm not too familiar with. Maybe you've got a new character you're dying to try out... maybe you've got an old character you're dying to dust off. Maybe you think I hate you and don't want to see your name on an e-mail to me. Whatever the case is, I'm open to looking at any and all apps that cross my inbox in the next couple weeks. If you're reading this and thinking, "What the hell is this EMWC thing he's talking about?", hit up www.efeduniverse.com/emwc and check some of our past work out. The card archive is a little out of date, but all the PPVs are there as well as some of the "sorta weekly" cards and flash shows. If you'd like to see more cards, you can e-mail me I suppose and I'll toss a few links your way. There's an app on the page... I'd suggest filling it out there so you can look through the Player's Guide [as outdated as it may be]. Also, if you're curious as to what kind of people frequent our merry group... feel free to stop by #noise_pollution on Starchat and see if anyone yells at you [it may happen... don't blame me!] Anyways... think that's all I've got to say. Drop me an e-mail or IRC msg if you've got questions/comments/complaints/death threats. Chris EMWC Prez * Blood, Sweat, and Tears '02... coming soon on PPV!
