[The camera opens on a generic EMWC backdrop. A white banner, with
E-M-W-C, provides the background. Nothing fancy. No gyms, no offices, no
airplanes. Just a simple interview area. Sitting in a chair is one Diamondback Chris Myers. Myers wears a blue long-sleeved shirt and blue jeans, with a blue and white diamond painted around his right eye. He is sitting back in the chair, eyes looking upwards, staring at the ceiling. He does this for a
few seconds, before leaning forward, elbows on knees, hands under his chin, the shock of hair hanging in front of his left eye, staring into the
camera. He's silent for a few more seconds] CM: I remember when I came out here a few months ago. I came out here and revealed that it was I who was masquerading at the Masked
Outlaw.
I was the one who had everyone's attention. I was the person who destroyed Creed's psyche. I was the one who was called, and I quote,
'the signing coup of the year.' Yeah, I'll admit, coming in here was a big deal. Maybe, at the time, it was the signing coup of the year. This is the EMWC, where you always have to ask yourself "How can they top this?" Getting who was at the time the hottest rookie in the sport to come to the
EMWC was something to talk about.
Well, I've been here for over two months. And what have I done? [Myers runs his hand through his hair, before going on] CM: Not a thing. Not a god damn thing. I've wrestled three times I can recall. The first time was the team up with Kevin Slater before Showtime, and HE won that one.
The second time was against Tiger Claw, and I'd be lying if I didn't say he kicked my ass all over the ring. The third time was with Bobby Taylor against Claw and Casey James. And Bobby won that one for us. So...three matches, 2 wins, 1 loss, but I've done zip here. Maybe the hype was wrong. Maybe the D-Cup win WAS a fluke. Hell, maybe I really don't belong here. It doesn't seem right now that the EMWC should have gone out and signed me. Maybe the snickers
I hear in the locker room are right. Maybe the on-line newsrags were right when they cried 'Lucky!'
Maybe Creed was right. Maybe I am Minor League. [Silence. Chris takes a deep breath] CM: So, why don't I cut and run? Why do I put up with everyone saying "Myers is a wimp?" "Myers can't cut it?" "Myers is only here because he bought off the promoter?"
I'll tell you why. My friends and my fans. You may all sit out there and wonder about myself, Taylor, and
Slater. You think the Cult of Personality is a lie, that we're not friends. I met Dana, ok? I've played with Bobby's kids. Maybe we don't get together and go car shopping. The point is, the Cult of
Personality is one thing; friends banding together. We're sick of the lack of respect given to us.
No, I don't think respect is given. It should be earned. What I mean is people who decide to step in and interfere in our matches. The
Cult is about us making it, standing on our own two feet. And we're
doing it with people watching my back. As long as it's between
Slater and Starks, Taylor and I are going to step back and stay out of it. But if the Syndicate decides to stick it's nose back in,
all Kevin has to do is give the word... ...and someone's in for a world of hurt. They stuck up for me when no one else would. So, I'm in for the long haul if they need me.
Second, the fans. I know in this day and age, bad pays the bills. People may like it when flaming chairs and barbed-wire wrapped tables are used.
But I know there are people out there who like what I do, and what I represent; work hard, play hard, and it'll all come due.
So, I have to keep plugging away. Some wrestler don't give two cents about the people who pay their salaries, but I do.
What does this all mean, then? It means I need to go out there tonight and settle this once and for all. Chris Myers has to go out and show the entire EMWC...
and the entire wrestling world...that he can live up to the hype. That he does have the skills, and that he does belong here. I do it, by going out there and taking on three of the best wrestlers in the sport. First, you've got "Stunning" Scott Alexander. Now, Scott, I don't know much about you. All I know is this; you pal around with
Curtis
Hansen and Chris Quigley, two guys who don't rank to highly in my book. Now, I won't judge until I step in the ring with you one on one.
Next, Simon Lebec. Chummer, we've danced before. D-Cup, remember? Now, I had my foot on the ropes when you pinned me, but so what? The past is passed. Now, it's you and me in the ring. We can finally see, Lebec, who is better?
Last, Casey James. YOU I want to get my hands on most of all, for clocking me in the back of the head with a steel chair. Not only that, though. It's more than that. You see, James, out of the four people who are going to step into that ring, you're the best wrestler. You're got the talent, the accolades, and the reputation. I've always wanted to take you on, James, just to see if I could hang with 'Blackheart.'
Tonight, not only me, but the world is going to find out...can
Chris Myers step into the ring with the biggest stars in the sport?
More importantly, can he walk out a winner? I guess we'll find out. See you in the ring, guys.
