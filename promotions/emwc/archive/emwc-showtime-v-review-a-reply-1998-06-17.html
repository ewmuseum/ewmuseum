Showtime V Review: A Reply

17 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
>There is a quote by Shakespeare (how appropo :) "The lady doth protest >too much". Basically, the more you deny something...the more likely it >is. This is how I feel about the constant complaint that the EMWC is not>an IIWF copy.
The first time involved using the IIWF in the header as a >cheap plug for an EMWC show...now, in review of an EMWC PPV, one in which >an invasion by the IIWF plays a prominent part, it is claimed that the>EMWC doesn't need the IIWF to survive. "The lady doth protest too much." > Here's another quote from Shakespeare..."The lion hath whelped in the street." Any relation to the topic? Nope. Just as there's no relation between the EMWC and the IIWF, besides the fact that we're both great organizations. I didn't make either of the posts you speak of but I'm sure Adam had the best of intentions both times, the first, simply to promote the fed that he's very proud of, and second, to try and head this sort of criticism off at the pass.
And while the "invasion" was an angle on the PPV, I'd hardly call it one of prominence on a show that saw the return of "Dreamlover" Trey Porter to the
EMWC, J.W. Hardin lose the Outlaw name, and Kevin Slater win the EMWC World Title among many others. >The EMWC is an excellant fed. It really is. Both the IIWF and the EMWC>have benefitted from sharing the talents of some of the best handlers in >the hobby. Cross-over in this sport is inevitable, and I find it only >reasonable that you would make mention of a wrestlers accomplishments in>another fed (if only in tribute to the players themselves). The question>here is whether the EMWC can survive without the IIWF. The answer is, >they SHOULD be able too, but they just can't stop bringing up the >Double-Eye.
> Why stop something people are enjoying? To soothe an ego? Frankly, I received a lot of e-mails and messages about Showtime V, and nearly all of them were filled with praise, some even referring to it as the greatest PPV they've read.
But it seems that if there's fault to be found, leave it to the folks on RSPWF to find it. Even the staunchest critics on IRC had to give us credit for this one. People seemed to enjoy the angle with the Predators, and many are looking forward to facing them...what's wrong with that? And if the perceived
"rivalry" between the feds is used to build heat for it, what's wrong with that too? Again, I'm forced to mention that it seems that the people who think the IIWF are mentioned too much in our shows, are the ones looking for it. Because of the twelve IIWF mentions, nearly all were in relation to the Predators, the rest were referring to IIWF former/current champions (stuff I didn't write on all occasions.) I am not in the habit of censoring the handlers of the EMWC.
In certain situations, I have asked for certain parties to not mention one another, but this is a situation I have let go.
Frankly, I'm getting a little tired of this. There have been shots made at other organizations in the past or their presidents, but I haven't had to put up with the deluge of criticisms from them. They know how to take a joke...they take it in stride, take a shot themselves in their own show, and life moves on.
>This latest invasion seems to bear that out. Some months ago, then IIWF>champion Steve Kowalski appeared on an EMWC broadcast bearing the IIWF>heavyweight title. Following that, the IIWF asked both [name withheld] and>Chris Blue that should anymore "cross-over" matches occur, they be without>any measure of IIWF distintion. Now, without warning, a group of
IIWF>handlers have decided to stage a "takeover". A fed head cannot, should>not, control his wrestlers. There are exclusivity contracts signed.>However, thereis no RSPWF #200 for fed-heads. All a fed head has as his own>are his leagues initials and its reputations. Small reward for the
hours>of online time a quality fed takes. I find it sad that the EMWC has>again participated in the breech of good faith. I was asked by Dan not to use someone with an IIWF Title on an EMWC show, specifically for them not to have the title. Nothing was ever said about not mentioning where the people were from. Simon Lebec was shown with the title at
Showtime, but much like when Kowalski appeared, their intros were not written by myself, but by the handlers. The PRedators had no titles with them...yes they were referred to as former IIWF tag champs, but that's how [name withheld]
wanted to bring them in. You can't blame me for everything.... > >Admitted, the handlers have a place in this..but we are a vain bunch. I >joined the EMWC from the IIWF partially as good faith between the feds,>partially because I was hunting Hardin, Thunder, Claw and James, and a lot>because my ego was being stroked. I enjoyed my time there and received>better treatment than I probably deserved. I do not know if EMWC
President >Blue asked the current batch of handlers as he did me, nor do I care. >However, I find it hard to believe the angle spontaneously occured, and it >futher promotes the belief that EMWC survives on IIWF heat. I know it to>be better than that, but its track record is speaking for itself. I only>hope for the EMWC's sake that it is not alienating it's own wrestlers in>its pursuit of
IIWF angles.
> Gregg, whether you want to believe it or not, I did not engineer this angle. You don't believe me, fine, but [name withheld] will tell you the same thing. I was approached by him for this angle as a way to keep the Predators active during the IIWF's hiatus. I don't see how this angle, that has not even really got off the ground could possibly show how the EMWC survives on IIWF heat. Why don't you use the Thunder/D angle, or the Frat Boys/ Lords angle, or the
Slater/Hansen angle as an example of an EMWC angle since those were some of the hot ones at the time? None of those are influenced by "IIWF heat".
As far as me alienating wrestlers in pursuit of A IIWF angle, I've had no complaints of that, so I appreciate your concern, but in this case it is not warranted.
This is another case of an issue trying to be made, where there really is none. As I've said earlier in another post, the EMWC has been around for three and a half years, a claim that few other feds can make, so by dismissing us as a fed that survives strictly on "IIWF heat", you insult the history of this fed, as well as every handler who has ever stepped into it's rings, including, ironically enough, yourself.
Thanks for listening.... Chris (Why do I even bother? Oh yeah...pride!)
