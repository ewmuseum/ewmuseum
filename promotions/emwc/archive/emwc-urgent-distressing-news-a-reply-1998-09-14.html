Hehe.... Man, some people are REALLY uptight out there. Here's the scoop folks, in case some one would like to hear the truth.
The writer of this post sent me a sample RP, applying to the EMWC. I turned him down, stating that I didn't feel that his talent was at the same level as those in the EMWC. I invited to improve his skills and then reapply in the future. At that point, he became accusatory and hostile saying that if he was
42433-0 or whatever record he claimed to have and the champion of all these feds, how could he not be good enough.
I stood by my decision, and then he began to question the selection of his
"friend's" character, Eric Wayne. I happened to be speaking with Eric Wayne's handler and Ronnie D. in #EMWC on IRC at the time, and passed along his words to Wayne. Both were amused and decided to have some fun with this guy who was obviously so incensed at being rejected.
The others he mentioned did the same, out of jest. They weren't abusive unless you consider saying, "Plonk" repeatedly as being abuse. =)
Anyways, long story short. This guy needs to lighten up, he needs to remove the stick from his anal orifice, and he needs to move on.
Ordinarily I wouldn't even respond to such ridiculous crap, but seeing as though I'm bored today...here's my post in defense. Enjoy! Oh, and BTW, scroll back a few posts, and check out this week's card. =)
Hasta... Chris
