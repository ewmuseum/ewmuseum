Jeff “Madfox” Matthews vs. “Playboy” Ronnie D — EMWC An Evening To The Extreme

6 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The ring announcer's and commentators' text of the match between Jeff “Madfox” Matthews and “Playboy” Ronnie D at EMWC's An Evening To The Extreme, as the IIeW organizer kept it on his own site. The date is the one the career list on his “Playboy” Ronnie D page gives for the match (6 April 1998); the text itself states none.
[The camera takes a long shot from the top of the United Center. The camera swings around slowly, getting a shot of the ring from all angles. Signs are waved madly as Ken Graham steps into the ring.] KG: LAAAAAAAAAAAAAADIES AND GENTLEMEN OF CHICAGOOOOOO! [The crowd goes nuts.] KG: The following bout is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, he stands at six-foot FIVE and weighs in at two-hundred and FIFTY THREEEE POUNDS... From Durham, NORTH CAROLINA... JEFF "MAAAAAAAAAAAADFOX" MAAAAAAAAAAAAATTHEWS! [ The crowd goes wild as Jeff "Madfox" Matthews walks down the aisle. He's a clean shaven young man. Brown hair and eyes. He wears long black pants with white stripes on the sides. Written in the stripes are "MAD" on the right leg and "FOX" on the left leg. He high fives the fans as he comes to ringside. Jeff "Madfox" Matthews steps into the ring and sits on the turnbuckle waiting for the match to begin.] TM: And you know who's coming next... JS: Yay. KG: AND HIS OPPONENT! [The crowd boos, so much so that you can barely hear Ken Graham.] KG: He stands at six-foot TWO, and weighs in at two hundred FOURTEEN POUNDS! He hails from YOUR DAUGHTER'S DREAMS... Accompanied to the ring by "OUTLAW" J.W. HARDIN... "PLAAAAAAAAAYBOY" ROOOOOONNNNNNNIE DEEEEEEE! [The lights extinguish, and pink spotlights pan the arena, causing the little children to scream. A red heart-monitor graph appears on the big screen, as slow beeps go faster and faster, causing the heart monitor to go faster. Suddenly, the heart monitor flatlines, as a piercing beep resounds through the arena. The long beep stops, and the flatline forms the words MARQUEE MAN The words quickly scroll off to the side, and just as soon, the flatline forms ICON As soon as those words scroll off, the word GOD OF WRESTLING blip across the screen. Then, as "Sharp-Dressed Man" by ZZ Top kicks in and the pink spotlights converge around the top of the aisle, the infamous logo hits the big screen .-"""-. .-"""-. / `..' \ | P L A Y B O Y | ___ ___ _ _ _ _ ___ ___ ___ | _ \/ _ \| \| | \| |_ _| __| | \ | / (_) | .` | .` || || _| | |) | |_|_\\___/|_|\_|_|\_|___|___| |___/ `\ /' `\ /' `\/' The girls scream and the rest of the crowd boos as the curtains burst open and out steps the one, the only, the oft-imitated but never duplicated "PLAYBOY" RONNIE D! "Playboy" Ronnie D is a caucasian with skin of regular colour and complexion with long sandy brown hair that is let loose. He has a small tattoo of a red maple leaf on his right arm. The "Playboy" is of a medium build, like Chris Benoit or Eddie Guerrero, only slightly less muscular. He has a small birthmark on the right corner of his upper lip and very few scars on his forehead, because of his avoidance of any opponents that might make him less attractive to the ladies. To the ring, "Playboy" Ronnie D wears long, black tights with mettalic mother-of-pearl vinyl leopard spots on them with a huge mettalic red vinyl broken heart on the seat. His boots are black, with a small, mettalic red broken heart on the sides. He also has black fingerless gloves with a mettalic red vinyl broken heart on the back. Most notably, though, he sports a silver knee brace. He stands at the top of the aisle, with Hardin closely behind, posing for a pictures, before making his way down the aisle. The pink spotlights follow him down the aisle, as he steps onto the apron and through the ropes. He stands in the center of the ring, posing for more pictures, before the lights come back on. The crowd is booing him heavily as he takes the mic away from Ken Graham. Graham exits the ring, shrugging his shoulders.] Ronnie: Heyyyyyyy JEFFY! Jeffy Pop. You know lil' ol' me, I just need to hear my voice, so I got the mike! [The crowd boos.] Ronnie: Now, in the spirit of sportsmanship, I should offer you a warning. Jay Dub, what'll you do to Jeff if he gets out of line? Hardin: I'll kick yer scrawny lil' ass all the way back ta Durham, Jeffy Pop. [The crowd boos at this, as Ronnie gives the mike to Hardin to take outside of the ring. Ronnie fluffs his hair as the bell rings and the two combatants circle each other.] JS: We have ourselves a match, folks! After all this time, we finally have a match! [Ronnie lifts his hands in the air, challenging Matthews to a test of strength. Matthews accepts and they lock up! Matthews quickly brings Ronnie's hands down, having a significant amount more muscle mass than the "Playboy." Ronnie quickly weasels out of this by kicking Matthews in the groin, getting huge boos from the crowd!] JS: That's despicable! He hit him with a low blow! TM: Just the way Lori likes giving it... LD: Flip off. I'll give you head as soon as Ellen comes back for another season. TM: I hear you really liked Ellen... [Ronnie whips Matthews to the ropes, and charges him for a clothesline... Matthews ducks, but gets caught in a a DDT for his troubles! Ronnie sits up, and points at his head with his index finger in an "I'm smarter than chump-change here," drawing huge boos.] JS: Ronnie needs to use his mind much more these days, after his injury. He'll have to wrestle with that knee brace until at least Showtime... [Ronnie gets up, and claps his hands as he circles a dazed Matthews. He and Matthews lock up in a collar-and-elbow tie-up and Ronnie is quickly pushed to the ground. He rolls with the push and does a backflip to his feet, and does a few quick jumping jacks, drawing HUGE heat from the crowd!] TM: LORI! Stop checking out his ass! LD: I am _NOT_ looking at Ronnie's ass... I'm checking out his form! Er... He's doing those jumping jacks perfect! TM: Pfffft. [Ronnie goes to lock up with Matthews again, but as they are about to lock up, Ronnie bends down and grabs Matthews' ankles out from under him, sending him on his back. Ronnie flips him over, and holds him in a Boston Crab. Matthews gets to the ropes quickly, elliciting a cheer. Ronnie lets go and as Matthews gets up to his feet, Hardin gives him a fist in the groin to savour. Matthews, hunched over, staggers towards Ronnie, who hooks him up and drops him in a piledriver!] JS: A big piledriver there by Ronnie D and there's the cover! The count... 1... 2... Nope! [Ronnie argues with the ref, and gets up. He pulls Matthews to his feet, and sets him up for another piledriver...] JS: BACKDROP! [Lori screams as Ronnie goes _flying_ in the air, over the ref and into the turnbuckle! He staggers around quickly, with a dazed and confused look on his face, right into a Matthews' belly-to-belly suplex! The crowd goes wild as Matthews covers! 1... 2... Nope! Matthews then brings him up to his feet, and hits him with a piledriver of his own! He then quickly grabs his feet and spreads Ronnie's legs! HUGE knee into Ronnie's injured right knee, followed by a monster pop! Ronnie cries out in pain as Matthews spreads him again... And drops an elbow!] JS: This is reminiscent of the way Brody Thunder demolished Ronnie's leg in March. TM: And who won that match? JS: Really, if you think about it, who won? Sure, Ronnie got the win on paper, but he didn't wrestle for a month and a half afterwards... [Ronnie quickly scrambles away from Matthews, and slides to the outside. He nurses his recovering knee, which suffered ligament damage and a slightly torn ACL... The injury that puts many out for six months or more. He talks with Hardin, about strategy presumably, and slides into the ring.] LD: That Matthews is the real bastard child of the EMWC. How dare he hurt Ronnie! Next thing you know, this guy will go after my Romeo. TM: I never knew he was the type to go after Romeo... Although I hear Rankin ain't exactly a skirt-chaser. [Ronnie and Matthews lock up, and Matthews blasts a HUGE kick into Ronnie's knee cap! Ronnie reels back in pain and falls over the top rope! The crowd roars it's approval as Ronnie lays on the ground. Hardin runs over and starts slapping Ronnie, trying to get the "Playboy" awake enough to get back in the ring!] JS: Ronnie took the worst of that spill! [Ronnie finally re-enters the ring, shaking out the cobwebs. He locks up with Matthews, and sends him to the ropes. Matthews bounces off the ropes, and Ronnie catches him in a side suplex! Ronnie sits up, with a look of determination on his face. He lifts Matthews up and whips him to the ropes! He ducks down for a backdrop, but keeps his eyes up so he can see Matthews... Matthews takes the bait and sends a boot to Ronnie's face... That is intercepted by his hand. Ronnie hold Matthews leg in the air, and then sends a leg of his own right into Matthews' crotch, drawing huge boos! Matthews, bent over, is then caught in a Diamond Cutter by Ronnie D, bringing the house down!] LD: YAY! TM: Hot damn, he did it! That's the Foxden! JS: Ronnie going up top... Presumably for the Heartbreaker. [Ronnie sits perched on the ropes and then ascends to the top, where he stands. He leaps off, and puts his left knee out... He flies through the air and hits... ] JS: THE HEARTBREAKER! [The crowd boos like never before, as it appears that Ronnie has won. As he stands up to gloat, from the back emerges a man. He wears a black cowboy hat, black boots, black jeans, and a black t-shirt that sports the famous credo, "Evil, Mean & Nasty." That man is none other than...] TM: JUMPIN' JA-FREAKIN'-HOSEFATS! It's BRODY THUNDER! JS: The man that injured Ronnie D is in the house, as Ronnie would say! [Thunder stalks down the aisle, to a very mixed reception, as they have no idea what he will do. As Thunder makes his way towards the ring, Ronnie yells at Hardin, repeatedly: ] Ronnie: GET THAT... THAT ANIMAL THE HELL AWAY! [But Hardin, with the wave of his hand, assures him that Thunder is here to help, as he and Hardin are friends.] JS: This situation... Well... I don't know what to make of it. Hide nor hair. [Thunder reaches the ring, and climbs right in. He points a menacing finger right at Ronnie D and charges him!] TM: SECURITY! SECURITY! [Hardin, meanwhile, grabs the ref from the outside, and gives him a Cattle Buster on the outside to huge boos. Ronnie, on the inside battling Thunder, doesn't see this. Thunder and Ronnie trade blows, until Ronnie rips a huge boot into Thunder's groin, knocking his hat off! The crowd boos as Ronnie locks him in a full nelson, and calls Hardin over.] Ronnie: Hit the bastard! [Hardin looks relentingly at Thunder, who struggles to be free.] Ronnie: HIT HIM! [At this point, Jeff Matthews is wide awake, and sneaks behind Ronnie.] Ronnie: Remember the triple-cro -- [Ronnie doesn't get to finish his VERY antagonistic sentence as Hardin unloads a fist at Thunder... ... But Brody escapes Ronnie's clutches, landing on his rear, as a the huge hambone fist of Hardin connects with Ronnie's jaw! The crowd goes wild as the referee enters the ring and Ronnie falls over towards Matthews…into a FOXDEN! ] TM: It was a set-up! I don't believe it! JS: 1... 2... 3! That's what you get for shortchanging the Outlaw. KG: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match... JEFF "MADFOX" MAAAAAATTHEWS! [The crowd goes wild at the announcement, as Ronnie bounds up. Matthews escapes the ring quickly, as Ronnie leaps on Brody Thunder, wild with rage. He begins pummeling him with fists as Brody covers his face, but it doesn't last long as J.W. Hardin plucks Ronnie D up by the hair and delivers a HUGE boot to the midsection! The crowd is going nuts, as they realize what's happening!] LD: No! I can't watch! [Thunder slowly gets up and Hardin has Ronnie in a front-facelock, which is a set up for, you guessed it... The Cattle Buster DDT. Thunder straddles over and grabs Ronnie in another front-facelock, and it appears that they are going to give him what the crowd and many have wanted to see for a while... A double Cattle Buster DDT! As they are about to swing him 'round, from backstage, another man emerges, stopping Hardin dead in his tracks!] JS: IT'S "OUTLAW" BOBBY TAYLOR! He's going for Hardin! [Taylor rushes the ring, and Hardin lets go of Ronnie D. The two Outlaws clash in the ring and start duking it out, causing the crowd to roar in delight! At the same time, Ronnie D lifts a knee into Thunder's gut, doubling him over, and he catches him in a huge DDT! He jumps on Thunder and starts pounidg on Brody's face with tremendous fury, but Brody reverses it and returns the favour! As teh two roll around the ring Hardin and Taylor go toppling over the top rope in their efforts to demolish each other as EMWC security hits the ring!] JS: SHEER PANDAEMONIUM! THAT'S WHAT THIS IS! [Ronnie and Brody duke it out on their feet, with Brody backed against the ropes. Ronnie lets a fist fly, and knocks Brody clear over the top rope! Before the security can get in the ring, he snatches Brody's big black hat and puts it on his head!] MT: Ronnie just stole Thunder's hat while security were separating Taylor and Hardin! What a guy! [Taylor and Hardin point and try to grab each other as a throng of security separates them! Ronnie, meanwhile, swaggers over to Thunder wearing his hat, which goes well with the black tights, and gives him a patented Ronnie D hip-thrust and runs off into the crowd, as Thunder chases after him!] LD: That hat give Ronnie a certain appeal... MT: To gay cowboys... Or cowgirls, but who'd lay a cowgirl? Or Lori, for that matter? LD: Jerkoff. JS: Settle down! I can't believe what has just transpired, and you two are calling each other names! J.W. Hardin has turned his back on Ronnie D and his money, and Brody Thunder has popped up in the EMWC. What else can happen here tonight? TM: Don't ask…we still don't know who Hansen's mystery opponent's gonna be! It could be anyone! JS: Not to mention Creed vs Watkins with their careers on the line! Ladies and gentlemen, I've just been handed a note…apparently not only has Moxy Blue been able to recover from that attack earlier tonight by Ronnie D…but he has challenged the "Playboy" to a match…RIGHT HERE!!! NEXT WEEK!!!! What a blockbuster that one's gonna be!!!! LD: Ronnie's wrestling next week?!?!?! YES!!!! JS: That's going to a true battle to see who's the King of Cruisers! Two of the best cruisers in the world, tangling in that one!
