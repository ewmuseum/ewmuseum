On with the show! ------------------------------ [We fade in from black to the same generic TV studio we saw last week... and the most UNGENERIC ANNOUNCERS IN THE HISTORY OF THE F'N
WORLD!! WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!]
JS: Welcome once again to the Showtime X: Eternally Extreme update! TM: Can you believe we're getting paid to do a ten minute show? JS: Hush. TM: WHY DID YOU SAY THAT?!?! WHYYYYYYYYYYY?! JS: Ooooookay. Fans, we've got a BIG week here on Update as we've got
MATCHES to announce! A couple of them in fact.
TM: You think that'll shut up the people whining about not knowing the lineup for the show?
JS: Doubt it. TM: Well, dammit... we'll give it the good ol' college try. JS: Did you go to college, Todd? TM: School of Hard Knocks, baby. JS: ... TM: UCLA too, but whatever. JS: Ah. Okay... this show's getting too weird for me. I feel like we should be broadcasting for our old friends in-
TM: Come on, Steggs... we're trying to be nice. JS: Well, let's just hope that newsgroup guy doesn't review this show. TM: Couldn't be any worse than our other old friends' review. JS: Indeed. Fans, last time on Update, we announced that this week, we'd have the _official_ word from the EMWC President, Chris Blue, on just what in the world he's been in such heavy contact with Japan for.
TM: Geishas. Guaranteed. JS: Somehow I doubt it... but we'll have that news later in the show. For now, I think it's appropriate to add our second match to the
Showtime X: Eternally Extreme lineup. Last time, we announced that
Devon Case will collide with Eddie Van Gibson in what could be the final match _ever_ for both competitors.
TM: But if they're going out, you can bet they're going out in a blaze of glory, Steggs. Yakuzas, Asshole Drivers, Case Closedsedseds...
Canuckle Dusters... oh, it's gonna be a beautiful night as we broadcast
LIVE from Canada, Portland, Tampa, and Michigan!
[Stegglet giggles.] TM: Okay, so that's a lie... but dammit, I want a return of the
Shooting Gallery for the last EMWC show! COME ON! MAKE IT HAPPEN!
JS: Ahem... we'll talk a little more about the venue later in the show but for now... let's take a look at our next match to be announced.
[We cut to a shot of the World Tag Team Championships. PLUS?! A
VOICEOVER!] V: Once the most desired prize in tag team wrestling... in recent years, the World Tag Team Titles have fallen on hard times.
[A still photo of Dave Bryant thrusting the tag title high in the air.] V: Competitors who shouldn't have even been in the ring somehow found their dirty paws sullying our gold.
[A second shot... this time of the Fabulous Ones posing, flexing... and a picture of Mary Hart splashed behind them,]
V: But at Showtime X... the legacy of the World Tag Team Titles will be restored for the final time.
[A shot of the Down Boys... then the Epitome of Cool... then the
Fraternity Boys... ....and then the video pauses, dropping out completely.] V: Um... wrong tape, guys. [A few grumbles of anger come from off camera... and a new video pops up, this time with the tag titles yet again!]
V: We know you wanted that match... but hey, some people can't move on! So, you know what we always say around here?
Fuck them, they're wrong. Fuck them, we're right! The show must go on... and in the place of the teams we've always deemed as the greatest in EMWC history, we give a handful of teams the chance to become _the_ greatest... _the_ last team to wear the World
Tag Team Titles.
[A quick series of shots pop up... The Benjimans Future Force The Eclipse [who?!? Former tag champs... look it the fuck up.] ] V: Former tag team champions... [Another shot... The Towel Boyz.] V: Could-have-beens... [And one final shot... Strictly Business.] V: ...and teams we never thought we'd see again. It's a Scramble
Match to crown the final World Tag Team Champions... the Eternal Tag
Champs... and it will _only_ happen at Showtime!
[The five teams are superimposed around the tag titles... and we fade back to our announce team.]
JS: Five teams already in... and we're promised more to be added in the weeks to come. TM: It may not be the three way dance people were begging for but dammit, I'd rather see the Towel Boyz over the Down Boys every day of the damn week and fifty-six times on Sunday.
JS: Word life. So, the tag scramble match is added to Showtime as we will crown the Eternal World Tag Team Champions.
TM: Can you believe Strictly Business is gonna be there? JS: Some people can put the past behind them... some would rather be bitter fucks drowning in their own miserable lives.
TM: Hehe... you were in charge of contacting them? JS: It's not easy, man. You'd be surprised at how many of these guys are laying in a gutter now...dreaming of what once was.
TM: Simon Lebec? JS: Isn't he dead? TM: Deaths don't last in this business, Steggs... they're just like retirements. JS: Ahem... moving on. I mentioned we'd discuss the venue for the event... and this seems like a good time to do so. In recent weeks, we've talked about how many venues were bidding on the chance to host this final event... and how the list had been narrowed down on a weekly basis. Now, I'm proud to announce the final ten venues being considered...
The Toronto Skydome. TM: Home of No Imitations Accepted for several years... some great memories in that building, Steggs. The attack on Bobby Taylor... the first Casey/Claw match... Serge Annis winning the World Title... the ladder match with DTP and D. Good stuff.
JS: The Georgia Dome. TM: Was recently home to Showtime IX... probably a long shot since there's not much history there.
JS: The Fleet Center in Boston. TM: If you ask wrestling fans which event put the EMWC on the map permanently as a major player in this industry, you'll hear two answers... NIA '97 and Showtime V. We've already got the home of NIA on the list... and now we add the home of Showtime V. The Fleet
Center's been a big place for the E over the years, also playing host to BST a few years ago. Good choice.
JS: The MGM Grand Garden in Las Vegas. TM: Hrm... another long shot if you ask me. While the boys would love it, I don't see this as a very realistic option.
JS: In Los Angeles, the historic Grand Olympic Auditorium. TM: I'd love to see it because of the history of the building... plus, it's in LA. But I don't think it's likely to happen. We just had
Redemption there a few months ago and the place is just too damn small for an event of this size.
JS: Same thoughts towards Viking Hall in South Philly? TM: That's a tough one. That place housed what was arugably the greatest Pay Per View this company has ever had. It's the building that Extreme built. It stands a better chance than the Grand Oly in my opinion... but it's still a longshot because of the size.
JS: One of your favs... the Tokyo Dome. TM: Whoooo! Eh... I love the place but I can't see us running the big final show overseas. It's a shame but... well, I doubt it'll happen.
JS: Texas Stadium? TM: Home to several big E events...but would anyone but Courtade give a damn? JS: Heheh... what about our hometown's own, the Staples Center? TM: Now we're talking. It's got the history of big E moments... it's in our hometown. This is probably a front-runner, Steggs.
JS: And of course... what list of possible venues would be complete without the mecca of sports itself, Madison Square Garden?
TM: Another top choice. If you don't do it in your hometown, I'd almost think you _have_ to do it in the birthplace of the company. A lot of people forget that the EMWC was born in NYC... and why not kill the company in the place it was born.
JS: It all comes full circle according to Todd Michaelson... and fans,
I understand next week we'll trim the list down even further... to a final five where we'll have a very special announcement to make that involves _you_, the fans of this company.
TM: It's all for the fans, baby! JS: Indeed. It _is_ all for the fans... and when you hear what the
EMWC President has to announce right now, I think you'll agree. Fans, earlier today we caught up with a _very_ busy boss... and here's what he had to tell the entire world about his final wrestling event.
[We fade to a shot of the EMWC President sitting in his office with the words "EARLIER TODAY" superimposed on the screen.]
CB: Thank you for tuning in to Update to hear what I'm about to announce. The EMWC fans deserve a night unparalleled in this great sport's history for our final event... and that's exactly what you're going to get. Already announced is the tag team scramble match for the
Eternal World Tag Team Titles... as well as the _epic_ encounter between Devon Case and Eddie Van Gibson that already has people whispering "Match of the Year".
But now... it's time to get hardcore. [Blue smirks.] CB: For the past nine years, the EMWC has revolutionized the terms
"Hardcore", "Ultraviolent"... and most importantly, "Extreme". No one else has dared to do the things that we have... and for Showtime X, I went out and found two of the most violent men in the industry to do battle in one of the sport's most dangerous battlegrounds.
Serge Annis. [A shot appears over the President's left shoulder of a bloodied
"Epitome of Evil" from shortly after his epic House of Mirrors match with Simon Ezra.]
CB: He's a former World Heavyweight Champion... not just in the EMWC but in the UWF and the IIWF... the _only_ competitor to accomplish that feat.. a Triple Crown that will never be duplicated. He's a former finalist in the King of the Death Match Tournament.
And when contacted about this event, he asked for one thing... "I want to be let loose... I want the chance to take a man's eye again."
[A grin crosses the boss' face.] CB: Serge Annis once took a man's eye in a wrestling ring... ....a ring surrounded by barbed wire. And at Showtime X, he'll get the chance to do that again. [Blue pauses.] CB: And the man he'll face can't afford to lose an eye. [A second bloodied image appears over the boss' right shoulder.] CB: Bad Eye McBaine. A former World Television Champion... he's engaged in brutal, bloody matches all over the wrestling world. He's beaten some of the very best that this sport has to offer.
And at Showtime X: Eternally Extreme... just like Serge Annis... Bad Eye McBaine is Born To Be Wired. [Blue grins widely again... and we fade back to the announcers.] JS: Bad Eye McBaine and Serge Annis... in a Barbed Wire match?! TM: Good lord almighty. JS: That's... that's crazy! Those two men... good lord, who knows what the hell they'll do to one another in there!
TM: It won't be pretty that's for sure. JS: This has the potential to be one of the most violent matches this company, this sport has _ever_ seen. Serge Annis, the Epitome of
Evil... Bad Eye McBaine...BORN TO BE WIRED!
TM: Three matches announced... three matches you can not find _anywhere_ in the sport except for at Showtime X: Eternally Extreme. Promoters around the world have dreamed of promoting matches like these for years... and in the EMWC on December 28th, those dream matches will become a reality.
JS: And that's not all... the boss also has something else to announce. Roll the tape again.
[We cut back to the EMWC President.] CB: Three matches on the books... and this next one is a fitting addition to an already stacked lineup.
For several weeks, reports have been running wild as to contact I've had with several individuals in Japan. For several weeks, everyone has wanted to know just what the hell I had found in Japan that had
_anything_ to do with Showtime X. And now it shall be revealed... [Blue pauses.] CB: At Showtime X... on the grandest stage ever... Zokugun Sangai will be reunited. [You can almost hear the f'n pop, can't ya?!] CB: The greatest import from Japan since Toyota, ZS will be in the house on the 28th of December...
....and they _will_ be competing. A ten man tag team matchup of epic proportions as five representatives from ZS... who will be announced in the upcoming weeks... will collide with a EMWC Junior Heavyweight
"dream team".
Who will be a part of the "dream team"? Juvenil Infierno... November... Michael Keening... ....and two partners of their choice to be announced at a later time. [The match graphic appears behind the boss' shoulders... and we fade back to the announcers.]
TM: OHHHH HELL YEAH! JS: You had something to do with this, didn't you? TM: Hey, it's not easy to reunite the greatest five man team in our sport's history... even the boss needs some help from time to time.
JS: Which ZS members?! TM: The boss would have me strung up if I told you. You'll just have to wait and see. JS: Fine... but who will join the Dream Team of Junior Heavyweights? We've got two former champions in Juvenil Infierno and November... and a world-class superstar in Michael Keening... who the hell can they find to round out the team?
TM: I don't know... but if you know ZS like I do, they're chomping at the bit to put down the Dream Team and show they're still the best at what they do.
JS: This is gonna be one for the history books, fans... and we're still not done here tonight. We've still got more for you and... well, we've got our first comments from a competitor who hopes to be _on_ the
Showtime X card. Let's go to Luke Kinsey, who I understand we have live somewhere in the building.
[Cut to the generic EMWC backdrop that's been used the past few weeks, where Luke Kinsey stands front and center in a pair of black slacks with an open white shirt. He sneers at the camera and begins.]
LK: The minute the word was out that the E was gonna put on one last show, my phone started ringing off the hook, the fax machine went nuts.
Who was I gonna fight on the last show? What was I gonna do on the last
EMWC Pay Per View...ever?
Was I gonna fight one of my predecessors? Someone like DTP or EVG, guys who invented the schtick I perfected?
[Luke thoughtfully scratches his chin.] LK: Enticing, but no. Or what about Devon Case? The Golden God, Mr. Match of the Year? Everyone knows he and I have been on a collision course for years, and everyone around the locker room heard the words "Iron Man Match" being whispered.
[Luke takes a deep breath, almost dejectedly.] LK: Believe me, running circles around that overrated trouserstain is something I'd love to do.
But no. Or what about Chris Courtade? What about the EMWC World Title? It was common knowledge that I had Tulip Boy in the crosshairs, that I was about to take his title. Am I gonna fight him? Would I? Could I?
I'd _love_ to be the EMWC World Champ _Forever_... ....but there's just something else that's been bothering me. Shane Destiny. [Sneer.] LK: Still laughing it up, Shane? Still feeling good about yourself, since you _stole_ my Triple Crown Titles? Well good, you should. I hope you laugh it up Shane, I hope you knock up Chubbs and buy a timeshare in the Hamptons, because you're an aberration.
A glitch that needs to be fixed. Put on ice. [Lifting an eyebrow, Luke pulls a contract out of his pocket.] LK: Showtime X, the grand daddy of 'em all, that glitch is getting fixed, the system will be returned to normal operating conditions.
Because I'm getting my titles back. I'm proving once and for all that
_I_ was Ego MAX, and Shane Destiny was nothing more than a pathetic tag along, holding onto my coattails for dear life...
[Luke grumbles.] LK: ...that's what I get for wearing coats with tails... [...] LK: ...and in an EMWC original match to boot. Frozen Ghost, Shane-o, a match that hasn't been seen in YEARS...brushed off one more time at the biggest event ever, just so I split your empty head open and re-take the titles I never should have lost.
Congratulations Shane, at Showtime X you're gonna be part of history...and after your brush with greatness is over, that's exactly what you'll be.
_History._ Ain't that a bitch? [We fade from Kinsey's cold gaze to the announcers.] JS: Luke Kinsey has made the challenge to Shane Destiny. He wants his former Ego MAX partner... he wants him at Showtime X... he wants it to be for the Eternal Triple Crown championship...
....and he wants it on ice! TM: Frozen F'n Ghost, Steggs. We've only seen the match done twice... Simon Ezra and Derek Mota tore the house down in the first one... Ezra and Tiger Claw put the bar way the hell up there in the second.
And no one... _no one_... has even _dared_ to try and top it. JS: Will Shane Destiny accept the challenge? Will we see Frozen Ghost for the final time at Showtime?
We'll find out next- TM: It's a good thing I'm here, Stegglet. JS: Huh? TM: Eternally the insider... I knew what Kinsey was gonna say. JS: And? TM: And I had the foresight to get us an answer _now_. Fans, we're almost out of time but let's go live via satellite, to Shane Destiny's
Raleigh, North Carolina office to hear from him!
[The screen becomes a split-screen, as Shane Destiny sits at his desk, sporting a royal blue Sasha & Digweed: Northern Exposure T-shirt and blue jeans with bleach stains on them. He... for lack of better terms, does not look pleased.]
JS: Shane, I'm sure you... DESTINY: You can stop right there, Jon. Yeah, I saw Luke Kinsey... yeah, I saw his usual bragging, the denial that perhaps there was someone better than him, the grand claims of what could have been...
and it made me sick to my stomach. Just like how it always made me sick in Ego MAX, just like how it made me sick after I left, and it still makes me sick to this day.
JS: My journalistic integrity tells me that you're going to accept this challenge. [Stegglet chuckles, but Destiny remains slightly upset.] DESTINY: Pan back. You see what's hanging above me? [The camera on Destiny's half pans back, as the three belts of the
Triple Crown - the North American, Television, and Junior Heavyweight
titles - are framed above him.] DESTINY: These belts signify my struggle here in the EMWC. These belts signify the two years of hard work that I've put forth to prove to the world that I'm the best in this business... and I'm not done proving.
So... Kinsey wants to take me out of my environment. He wants to take this man to the Frozen Ghost... fine. I accept. I _hate_ the cold...
I hate using weapons except the ones given to me at birth... but if that's what Kinsey wants, fine.
[Destiny pauses.] DESTINY: So, Luke, what's going to be your excuse when I beat you
_this_ time? [And with that... with the appropriately ice cold eyes of Shane Destiny glaring into the camera... we fade to black.]
