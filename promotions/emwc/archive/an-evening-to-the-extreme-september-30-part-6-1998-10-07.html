An Evening To The Extreme, September 30: Part 6 of 15

7 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As the music continues to play for a few seconds, Chris comes through the curtains to a HUGE response. He stands at the top of the ramp, EMWC North American gold over his shoulder and his hands on his hips, staring over the crowd, for a few seconds, before making his way down to the ring. He always takes the time to slap the hands with fans, paying special attention to any wearing red and white ballcaps, the colors of his fans, the
Cavalry. He gets in the ring, and stands in the middle of the ring. As the lights go out and lasers carve a diamond around him, he crosses his hands across his chest and makes the 'I Love You'
sign with both hands...] [Ding! Ding!] JS: Here we go folks, this is going to be an AMAZING matchup right here between Annis, Myers, and Langseth for the EMWC North
American Title.
[Langseth quickly ties up with Myers in the center of the ring, where he gains the advantage and applies a standing headlock on
Myers while Annis watches both men carefully...]
JS: Myers backs Langseth up into the ropes and whips him off... TM: HUGE clothesline by Annis! He just LEVELED Langseth! RMB: I heard Langseth's head hit the mat all the way over here! [Langseth rolls to the side and quickly gets back to his feet, making sure to stay alert. Both Myers and Annis approach
Langseth, and both men swing at Langseth but Langseth ducks the attack and retaliates by clotheslining both men down to the mat with incredible velocity...]
JS: Langseth seems very determined here, you can tell just by looking at him. RMB: All I see when I look at him is that bandage on his head from Annis. [Langseth backs off to let Annis and Myers both get back to their feet. Both Myers and Annis back away from each other a bit, while the position of each man in the ring forms an almost perfect 90 degree angle. Annis watches as Myers and Langseth go at each other once again, Langseth blocks a punch from Myers and answers back with a punch of his own, followed by a few more hard blows to Myers' head...]
TM: Langseth is really taking it to Myers! JS: Langseth no in control scoops Myers up and bodyslams him to the mat! [Annis continues to watch both men very closely, waiting for the perfect moment to strike. Langseth diverts his attention from
Myers and charges at Annis, dropkicking Annis to the mat...]
RMB: WHOA! JS: Great agility shown by Langseth! [Annis gets back to his feet and gives Langseth the universal sign to bring it on. Myers slowly gets back to his feet and approaches Langseth from behind. Langseth charges at Annis but
Annis catches him in a thunderous power slam...]
JS: Great move there by The Epitome of Evil! He just tossed
Langseth around like a sack of potatoes!
RMB: Don't say that...your making me hungry. TM: Myers is sneaking up behind Annis now while Annis is pulling
Langseth back to his feet!
[Myers taps Annis on the shoulder and as Annis turns around Myers jumps with uncanny elevation and nails Annis with a huge frankensteiner recieving a deafening pop from the crowd...]
JS: WOW! WHAT A MOVE BY MYERS! [As Myers soaks in the jeers from the crowd, Langseth struggles back to his feet and tries to clean the cobwebs from his head by turning his back to the action and resting on the turnbuckle.
Myers pulls Annis back to his feet, but Annis fights back with a few hard right hands to Myers solar-plexus, stunning Myers momentarily, but still stunning him long enough to nail Myers with a big vertical suplex...]
TM: Myers has gonna have some bad back pain in the morning. JS: In the morning? By the look of Myers holding his back as he lays on the mat, I'd say he has some back pain right now!
TM Ahhh...who asked you? [Annis pulls Myers back to his feet, then turns around and slaps a resting Mark Langseth on the back. Annis then drops to the ground and rolls out of the ring as Langseth turns around and sees Myers standing in front of him...]
JS: Smart move by Annis! Langseth thinks Myers tapped him on the back! TM: Good for Annis. Bad for Myers. [Langseth kicks Myers in the mid-section, then swiftly sends him head-first into the mat with a DDT while Annis devilishly grins on the outside...]
JS: Quick cover by Langseth... Ref: ONE!! TWO!! KICKOUT!! TM: NO! Kickout by Myers! JS: Myers still has a lot of fight in him. [Langseth pulls Myers back to his feet and quickly scoop slams him back to the mat, then climbs to the top turnbuckle. Just as
Langseth leaps off of the top turnbuckle with a huge splash,
Annis grabs Myers by the leg and drags him outside of the ring, leaving Langseth to hit nothing but the cold hard canvas...]
JS: Annis saved Myers from a world of pain and handed it to
Langseth! TM: Annis now has Myers on the outside...this could get ugly. RMB: Annis is setting up a table! [As Annis sets up a wooden table on the outside, Myers struggles to slowly get back to his feet. Myers slowly creeps up behind
Annis and nails the unsuspecting Annis with a huge reverse
DDT...] [Huge crowd pop...] JS: Listen to that crowd! Myers just DDT'd Annis' head onto the concrete floor outside of the ring!
TM: Dangit, I thought we were gonna see someone go through the table! [Langseth rolls outside of the ring on the opposite side of Myers and Annis while Myers brings Annis back to his feet. Langseth makes his way over to Myers as Myers begins to punch away at
Annis. Langseth nails Myers from behind with a double axehandle smash, forcing Myers to turn around and fire back a few punches of his own. While they are brawling, Annis slowly rolls himself back into the ring...]
JS: Langseth and Myers are really going at it outside of the ring! TM: Uh oh! Look at Annis inside of the ring, he's sizing both men up for something big! [As Myers and Langseth continue to trade blows on the outside,
Annis backs up inside of the ring while still keeping his eyes on both men. As Myers stuns Langseth with a hard right hand, Annis charges from the opposite side of the ring andleaps over the ropes, nailing both Myers and Langseth with a huge suicide dive, forcing the table to break from the impact of Myers' and Annis'
bodies...] [Enormous crowd pop...] JS: ANNIS WITH A SUICIDE DIVE THROUGH THE TABLE! WHAT A MOVE! TM: I knew someone was gonna break that table! RMB: Annis is rolling Myers back into the ring... [Annis follows Myers into the ring and drops an elbow into the sternum of Myers, quickly following with a cover...]
JS: This could be it! Ref: ONE!! TWO!! KICKOUT!! JS: NO! Myers kicks out again! I don't believe it! TM: Jeez... [Langseth begins to get back to his feet on the outside while
Annis pulls Myers to his feet inside the ring. Annis has an almost emotionless look upon his face as he places Myers in a standing head scissors position, then piledrives him into the mat...]
[Huge crowd pop...] JS: Oh my goodness! Did you see Myers' neck jar as his head was driven into the mat? TM: Yeah... JS: He could be seriously injured! TM: Your point being...? [Annis now rolls Myers up for the cover once again...] JS: This has to be it! There's no way! Ref: ONE!! TWO!! SHOULDER UP!!!!! JS: Jeez! Myers once again finds the energy to keep himself alive! [As a frustrated Serge Annis stands back to his feet, Mark Langseth slides into the ring and dropkicks Annis in the back of the knee, sending him down to the canvas...]
JS: Langseth still has a lot of fight left in him! Don't count him out just yet! [Langseth rolls Annis over and immediately begins pounding on
Annis' face. After a lengthy sucession of hard rights and lefts,
Langseth pulls Annis to his feet and sends him back to the mat with a big neckbreaker...]
JS: Langseth seems to be gaining a great amount of momentum. TM: If you say so... [Langseth then rolls Annis over onto his stomach and begins to grind his heel into the back of Annis' neck, trying to wear him down...]
RMB: He's just squishing Annis like a bug! [Langseth then drives a hard elbow into the back of Annis' neck, then immediately rolls him back over for the pin...]
Ref: ONE!! TWO!! KICKOUT!! JS: Not quite enough to put Annis away... [Quickly, Langseth once again rolls Annis over, but this time goes to work on Annis' knee by kicking the back of Annis' knee, then dropping a big elbow directly into the back of Annis'
kneecap...] TM: Langseth needs to make up his mind on where hes gonna hit
Annis. Knee or neck? JS: He seems pretty determined to tear up Annis' knee at the moment. TM: Yeah, I can see that. But just a few seconds ago he was nailing Annis in the back of the neck.
[Langseth continues to nail Annis in the back of the knee with a few hard kneedrops. As Myers struggles to get back to his feet, he rolls outside of the ring and tries to pull himself back together. Langseth then grabs ahold of Annis' ankles and applies a Boston Crab in the center of the ring...]
JS: Oh my! Annis has nowhere to go! [As the ref checks to see if Annis taps out, Myers begins to climb back into the ring. Myers sees that Langseth is vulnerable and takes advantage of the situation by kicking Langseth in the side of the head, forcing him to break the Boston Crab he had applied to Annis...]
[Huge crowd pop...] JS: Chris Myers is coming back! TM: Oh joy. [As Myers and Langseth once again begin to trade blows, Annis grabs onto the ropes to try and pull himself back to his feet.
Langseth stuns Myers with a kick to the midsection, the takes him to the mat with a drop toe hold. Annis gets back to his feet and clotheslines Langseth in the side of the head. As Myers gets back to his feet, Annis pulls Langseth back to his feet as well. Serge kicks Myers in the midsection, then wraps his large hands around both mens' necks...]
[Enormous crowd pop...] JS: Uh oh! Annis lifts both men up... TM: DOUBLE CHOKE SLAM!!!! RMB: YES!!! [Annis quickly falls on Myers for the pin...] JS: THIS COULD BE IT!!! Ref: ONE!! TWO!! THRRRRR....SHOULDER UP!!!!! JS: OH MY GOODNESS! I THOUGHT HE HAD HIM!!! TM: Dangit... [Annis seems angry as he violently pulls Myers back to his feet. He signals for the Epitomizer and the crowd reples with a deafening pop...] JS: OH MY! THE EPITOMIZER! TM: This could be it! [Annis lifts Myers up and nails him with the infamous and devastating Epitomizer, but just as Myers hits the canvas,
Langseth hops back to his feet and dropkicks Annis, almost sending Serge over the top rope and outside of the ring. Langseth seems frustrated, and while Annis is still stunned Langseth tackles Annis, sending both men over the top rope and outside of the ring...]
JS: Both men went outside! TM: Gee...how perceptive of you. [Langseth lands on top of Annis on the outside, then rolls over and rolls himself back into the ring...]
JS: Annis looks to be out cold there on the mat! [Langseth rolls Myers over onto his stomach inside of the ring, then applies a crippling ankle lock with all the strength he has left...]
[Huge crowd pop...] JS: Look at Myers' face! He is screaming in pain! TM: He has nowhere to go! He has to tap! [Myers tries to reach the ropes, but he is a good two to three feet away. The ref continues to check to see if Myers wants to tap out, but Myers still never taps out. Suddenly, Myers seems to have lost his fight, as he lays almost lifeless on the canvas, and Langseth continues to apply the ankle-lock...]
RMB: Myers is out cold! JS: That he is! He's unconscious! [Realizing Myers can no longer defend himself, the ref signals for the bell...] [Ding! Ding!] JS: LANGSETH DID IT!! LANGSETH HAS WON HIS BELT BACK!!!! KG: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner by way of submission and NEW EMWC NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION...MARRRRRRRRRRRK LAAAAAANGSEEEEEETHHHHHH!!!! [Mark Langseth, realizing that he has won, releases the leg of
Chris Myers and grabs the North American belt that he came in here with at the beginning of the show. After looking the belt over for a bit, a grin starts forming on his face as he goes to ask someone at ringside for a mic. Once gotten, the new North
American champion staggers over to the center of the ring and begins to speak in a somewhat frantic manner. Seeing that they will be treated to more of Langseth's words, the crowd begins to jeer him.]
ML: And you all thought I couldn't do it! Ever single damn one of you thought both Annis and Myers would kill me in the ring! Well, look who's holding the belt now!
[The crowd's displeasure is even louder now as Mark starts standing up firmly and not wobbling about everywhere. In the background, one can see Serge Annis climbing the ropes and looking at Langseth holding up the North American. His eyes widen as he sees that the match is over. He then looks down at the fallen Myers, and goes over to him. Annis then picks up Myers by the hair and then quickly levels him back down to the ground with a clothsline. Even though Langseth is a prime target right now,
Annis is focused on putting the hits on the man who cost him the chance to grab the title. Annis wastes no more time as he props
Chris Myers up again and clutches at his neck, signaling for one of the most devastating moves in the sport today. But before
Annis could go through the motions of the Epitomizer, Myers woke up just at the right moment flail away, hitting Annis just enough to let him go. Myers then hits Annis with a forearm and another forearm, but both only stagger the big man. With the crowd cheering for Myers to topple Serge over the ropes, Chris hits
Annis with a standing dropkick, which gives the crowd what they wanted. Myers then exits the ring and starts brawling with the
Epitome of Evil on the outside. Meanwhile, Langseth goes on with his speech, not even noticing what is happening behind him.]
ML: Sure, go ahead, boo, but it doesn't matter because I've got this... [Langseth's words are interrupted by the lights turning out and the sounds of Nuclear Assult's "Something Wicked" blasts throughout the arena, meaning only one thing: Gabriel Whitecross is coming back to reclaim his belt. A deafening face pop almost drowns out the music as the aisle way is bathed in a red light.
With everyone looking at the entrance way, the rightful owner of the North American title belt steps through the connecting curtains.
It is as if the last seven months of Gabriel's life had never happened. The long grey hair and the trademark goatee hugging his angular face were still very much a part of his present persona
... but the hateful darkness that had recently lain behind his blue eyes was now gone ... replaced in its stead by the welcomed return of humanity ... Of appreciation for the fans' generous welcome ... Of appreciation for even still being alive to experience the rush of adrenalin that has was now feeling.
Gabriel pauses at the top of the aisle and looks around his immediate surroundings. Before him, the warring figures of Annis and Myers move steadily towards the lockerrooms. Deciding that discretion would be the more viable alternative to risking another trip into the Emergency Room, Whitecross, clad in his very familiar black sleeveless leather jacket, black denim jeans and white boots, moves to the left hand side of the walkway and stares daggers of undisguised hatred (tinted with the merest hint of fear?) towards the 'Epitome of Evil' as he and his nemesis barrel into the welcoming recesses of the backstage area.]
JS: Look! It's Gabriel Whitecross! It seems that he wants to get back his belt... TM: Who cares? I want to see what's happening with the Monster and the Boy Scout, not the Cripple and the Jobber! I want blood not talk!
[The house lights turn on again as Whitecross reaches the ring and starts climbing the apron, the fans still giving him the loud ovation. Langseth, on the other hand, does not look that happy to see his mentor come back. His arms are lowered and he stands in a slouched manner. As Whitecross comes into the ring, Langseth turns his back on him. Gabriel then motions for a mic.]
GW: After the longest, most Hellish walk through trepidation and agony that I have ever known, I am pleased to say ... It is good to be back ...
[Another resounding wave of cheers reverberate around the building, as a hint of a smile invades Gabriel's facial features
... A smile that rapidly disappears from view, as he sees that
Langseth still has his back to him.]
GW: Mark? ... Mark? [Now sensing that something is very wrong, Whitecross takes a step towards Langseth, but does not move to face him. Cautiously,
'The Era' continues to speak.]
GW: Mark, words cannot sufficiently convey the overwhelming sense of pride and gratitude that I now feel. My friend, you took this
Federation's North American Championship Belt and unselfishly defended the Title in my honour ... You generously stood in my place, not wanting the 'powers-that-be' to rip this golden accolade from my unconscious grasp and declare it vacant. The bravery and honour that you have displayed as of late is truly befitting of a Champion ...
[Whitecross pauses, and crowd falls silent. Gabriel sighs, as if reluctant to continue his line of thought. Eventually, the grey haired warrior brings himself to utter more words.]
GW: And this is why it makes what I now have to say so very much harder... Mr Langseth ... Mark ... I am ready to fight again ...I
... I am sorry to do this ... but I would like my North American
Title back.
[Mark, still having his back turned towards his mentor, stands still with his eyes closed. After a long bit of silence, Mark turns towards his mentor.]
ML: Oh, Whitecross, it's great to see you again. I just cannot believe that you could come back from such an awful attack! You know, you are right. This belt, as I said when I started to defend it, was always going to be yours, so here you go...
[Mark places the belt in the outstretched arms of Whitecross. After taking his hands away, Langseth puts on a large grin on his face and kicks Whitecross in the gut! Gabe bends over, dropping the belt in the process, and starts clutching his stomach.
Langseth takes a moment to pick up the North American belt and place it away from where Whitecross is. Mark then goes back over to Whitecross, rubbing his hands together and still grinning.
Langseth then cinches his former master and waits, just standing there in that position, wanting to hear what the crowd has to say. Of course, they aren't too pleased with Mr. Langseth's actions. Some of the loudest jeers ever for Langseth can be heard, and unlike other times, this isn't because the crowd just doesn't want him in the ring... they really hate him. The
Japanese crowd then quiets as Langseth brings down Whitecross with the DDT, landing Gabe's head straight down on the mat, knocking him out cold. Mark gets up and looks back at the face of
Whitecross. He then picks up the mic and starts slapping Gabe's face around.]
ML: Look Whitecross... wake up, I want you to hear this as best you can! [Mark continues his hits on Whitecross until he finally starts twitching again. With that, Mark starts circling the body of his former master.]
ML: Look Whitecross, in the first few weeks after you got injured, I thought it would be great to defend your honor by taking up your belt and continue your title reign. I was going to get revenge on Annis for you and take care of everything else that you couldn't do even when you were active!
[The crowd gets rowdier now, some throwing bits of trash into the ring. This stops when they see the EMWC crowd control escort those trash wielding fans out of the area.]
ML: But that was back then, when I was still a zero in this fed and basically brainwashed by you, Whitecross. When I went back to those tapes of that match you had against Annis at Blood, Sweat, and Tears, I saw you for what you were: just another weak man in this sport. You were out on your first Epitomizer! Granted, it is a pretty powerful move, but for someone that I would look up to... well, let's just say that one chokeslam shouldn't be enough. Then I said to myself, after seeing you, Whitecross, for the fraud that you are, that I should get rid of this "defender"
title and just make the belt mine. After all, Whitecross here was laying around while I was the one in the ring facing all of those who wanted a shot a the belt...
TM: What in the world is he talking about? Aside from tonight, he only defended the belt once, and he almost lost that one!
ML: ...Not to mention the many different attack I got from punks like Myers and madmen like Annis. Well guess what Whitecross?
Tonight, I did just that... I shed that asterisk off of my name and made the belt officially mine!
[Whitecross at this point has recovered a bit from the DDT and is up again. He's still a bit dazed and he staggers over to where
Langseth is, still looking for the belt, almost as if he doesn't know what has been going on. Langseth drops both the mic and the belt and goes over to his former mentor. Again, he kicks Gabriel hard in the gut, but instead of going for the DDT, Mark just starts hitting him with closed fists in a fury as he lets his rage take a hold of him. After many blows to the face of
Whitecross, Langseth ends the barrage of fighting with one more shot, an uppercut, which levels Whitecross and makes the crowd hush, all appearently concerned about the condition of the former
North American champ. Langseth then steps back, wipes away some sweat forming on his brow, and picks up the mic again.]
ML: You just don't get it, do you? This belt isn't yours anymore, it's mine! I lost your title reign against Myers, and just now, in the match with both Annis and Myers, I won it back. This time, however, it was for me; I have the belt, legally now. So what does this mean for you, Whitecross? Well it means I don't need you around anymore. This is it; no more being a slave to you or a pupil to you...
[Langseth again pauses, not to hit Whitecross again, but rather to think for a moment.] ML: You know, before that match last month, I actually believed in you. I actually thought you were someone that could improve me, not just in the ring, but outside as well...
And then what do I see? Huh, Whitecross? You good for nothing piece of... [Langseth laughs a bit, not so much because what he is saying is amusing, but rather to try and control his anger. The crowd, meanwhile, is as loud as ever, jeering Mark with every Japanese phrase they can think of. Langseth then turns his back on the recovering Whitecross to respond to some of the crowd's pleasantries. After bothering the crowd a bit, he turns back his attention to Whitecross.]]
ML: I wasted three good months on you, Whitecross. Three whole months that I can never take back and what do you come out here to say? Sorry? Could I do anything for you? No! You actually have the nerve to come out and demand back your belt, a belt that isn't even yours anymore! You know, someone like you deserves what Annis gave you at Blood, Sweat, and Tears and it's...
[The crowd then gets as loud as they can, interrupting the ranting North American champion, as they obviously object to what
Mark is spouting off. Langseth then takes a look around, in the hopes that the jeers will die off a bit so he can continue. But before he does continue on, he goes over to the fallen
Whitecross, who is now tucked in the corner, and bends down so that his face is right in front of his former master's.]
ML:...and it's a shame that Annis couldn't finish the job and put you away for good. [At this, the fans start throwing things at Langseth, who now wears a wide grin as he still stares into the eyes of Whitecross.
Gabe, however, now has his eyes open wide, with anger this time at what his former slave has said. Not caring about the way
Langseth has hit him before, Whitecross takes the collar of
Langseth and pulls him down, punching him on the right side of the face. This takes Langseth by surprise as he stumbles backwards, allowing for the angered Whitecross to get back up.
Langseth backs away in fear as the Era of Defiance slowly marches towards him. As Langseth still tries to back away from the awoken giant, Whitecross extends his hands and clutches at Mark's throat and then in one deft and blur-like motion Gabriel lifts Langseth into the air, and spins him into a breathtaking 'Rock Bottom'- like chokeslam. Whitecross then looks over his former pupil who is out cold, and with rage still in his eyes, he quickly drops down and starts applying the Family Name, the ankle-lock submission. As the crowd now give out a huge ovation at seeing
Langseth get what is coming to him, Mark awakens from his daze with a loud scream. After floundering around a bit, trying to make his way through the move that has taken down so many other wrestlers. In a last ditch effort, Mark kicks back, hitting
Whitecross in the groin. Gabriel releases the hold as he tries to deal with the pain. Langseth, though, gets up slowly and shakes his leg a bit, trying to push out the pain Whitecross caused him.]
[Mark then sees Gabe in the corner and lunges to him, jamming
Whitecross' back into the ring post. As Whitecross writes in pain from the shot in the back, Mark sweeps the legs out from under him, letting Whitecross eat some canvas. Mark then quickly goes on top of Whitecross and applies his own ankle-lock submission hold. Mark, still with a wide grin, is the one who twisting the ankle of Whitecross, making him actually grimace in pain, all to booing of the crowd. Langseth makes sure that he gets a good look at the face of Whitecross as he takes up the mic again.]
ML: Hey, Whitecross, how do you like this, huh? It's a shame I can't twist that neck of yours and finish you off for good...
[Whitecross, at this, powers out of the move, but Langseth had already begun to loosen the grip on his former mentor. Seeing the face of Whitecross in pain was all he needed as the fans continue to throw all sort of objects at the North American champion.
Taking caution, not only at the flying object being thrown in the ring, but also at the writhing Whitecross, Mark Langseth grabs his belt and the mic and rolls out of the ring.]
ML: Whitecross, just remember that I can and will do more next time... [At that, Langseth drops the mic and turns around to walk up the ramp and leave for the locker rooms. Whitecross, by this time, has gotten up, showing a little soreness in the ankle Langseth was twisting, but is all else fine, which bring another loud face pop from the crowd.]
JS: Pure chaos! Gabriel Whitecross returns! Mark Langseth attacks him, and what's-go, go go!
[The screen cuts to somewhere in the back of the Tokyo Dome, amongst all of the tech equipment. Laying in the middle of the wires and cords are Serge Annis and Chris Myers, apparently still going at it.]
JS: Oh my! They are still going at it! TM: I wouldn't have it any other wat Jon! RMB: I hope they don't disconnect my... [As if on cue, Annis' foot kicks Myers in the midsection, and knocks one of the wires out of it's socket, apparently being
Moxy's microphone. Annis begins to wail away on Myers, as his foot gets tangled amongst the wires and cords.]
JS: Annis is just pounding away on him without mercy! [Annis pulls Myers up, and heaves him in the direction of a wall, however Myers blocks, and instead throws Annis throw a set of doors, leading into another hallway.]
JS: Those two are tearing this place apart back there! Someone stop them! TM: Why would you stop it? This is the kind of stuff that everyone wants to see. [Myers bursts through the doors and lunges towards Annis. The camera man is set to follow through the doorway, however the door swings shut on him. A moment later, Myers' fellow C.O.P member, and friend Luke Steele arrives on scene.]
LS: [To camera man] Where'd they go? In here? [The camera nods yes, and Steele charges through the doors. The camera follows. Ten feet down the hall, Myers is laying on the floor clutching his knee as Annis holds a steel chair in his hands, grinning.]
JS: Good God, where does he find those things? TM: Anywhere he wants to. [Steele charges Annis and leaps into the air, landing on Annis and knocking him over onto the ground. The Real Deal plants several stiff rights into Annis' face. The crowd watching on the video screen are cheering.]
JS: Luke Steele is doing a number on Annis. TM: Now that is embarassing. RMB: [Checking head set] -lo? Hello? Mic check one. Hello? Am I on?
