An Evening To The Extreme, March 9: Part 5 of 6

10 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(From the silent, ominous and insidious darkness he speaks...his deep, monotone voice unable to disguise the savage intensity that burns within his soul. The words he utters are clear and decisive, cutting through the unsettling atmosphere like a knife).
"To the Syndicate...and more importantly, Tony Starks' way of thinking...at the
IIEW Invitational, they were robbed of the EMWC World Championship, by a man who wields no power within our Federation. They would call it a travesty...I call it justice."
(Suddenly, there is a muffled rasping sound, and the black is disrupted by the small orange-tinted flame, emitting from the recently struck matchstick. The tiny beacon of light moves slowly from right to left, and finally comes to rest upon the wick of what appears to be a large white candle, that has intricately designed Hebrew symbols covering its entirety. Caught within the warm glow,
Gabriel Whitecross' stern features can be seen. His long grey hair falls freely about his broad shoulders, and his face is contorted into a mask of seething anger. At the periphery of the light that is cast outwards, it becomes apparent that he is stripped to the waist, the large 'Seven Archangels' tattoo across his broad chest seemingly alive...writhing upon his skin.)
"Starks, I make no secret of the fact that I have a great deal of respect for your ability, within the arena of combat. It is also correct to assume that I realize that our clash at the IIEW Invitationals could have easily resulted in you claiming my beloved gold from me...But you were ultimately betrayed by the very ones that tried to help you. Because of their thoughtless interference, you lost the opportunity to dispose of the 'Champion without a belt' stigma that has unfortunately attached itself to your being. I know that you will say that Titles mean nothing to you...That they are just a worthless status symbol.
However, to me and many others, it is a worthy accolade to attain, and I defend the belt that I am fortunate to hold, with honour and respect...Qualities that are sadly lacking, when the name 'Syndicate' is mentioned".
(Gabriel pauses for a breath, but the expression etched upon his countenance remains the same.)
" JW Hardin, you still choose to show me no respect, despite the fact that I inflicted a very intensive beating upon your monstrous hide...But do you wish to know something?...I do not want it anymore, for to get civility from you would only poison the essence of my being. 'Outlaw', do not think for one second of time, that I will step silently into the background, and merely watch both you and your allies endeavour to rule the EMWC by oppression, for this will not happen. At the upcoming 'An Evening', this fact will be illustrated with great clarity, when Dirt Dog Allah and myself exact an agonising retribution upon both Starks and yourself. My partner may be deemed as
'unpredictable', but he and I share a common goal. This one reason alone, will ensure that our actions within the squared circle will speak louder, and more eloquently than words could possibly convey."
(Within a heartbeat, the flame is extinguished, returning the room to complete darkness.) "Syndicate...The battle lines have been drawn...Prepare yourselves for the longest crawl through pain that you will ever know, for you will surely receive it..."
(Fade) Scene: The EMWC logo is across a banner that stands behind a table with a lone chair and a few microphones. The buzz of reporters off camera quiets when a
Japanese male, mid thirties, short hair wearing a charcoal suit walks up and sits at the lone chair. It is Hakiro Matsuoko, he speaks:
HM: It is with great honor that I today announce that I have signed a contract with the American wrestling promotion: EMWC. I was contacted by them just after the turn of the year and we have been in talks that have just recently subsided. We have come to an agreement that is beneficial to both sides.
Reporter 1: Matsuoko Hakiro, you were quoted before as saying that you would never again work for an American promotion, how should we take this?
HM: I had said that. I have meditated on this decision very long. I see this promotion has stocked full of talent: Whitecross,
Sandini, Starks, Slater, Unique Allah, Claw, James and Hardin. I see this as an opportunity to compete against the best in this up and coming promotion. As many of you know, I compete in Super
Japan Pro. I will take on a full schedule in both promotions. In the past, I have seen some inferiority in American promotions. I see the EMWC as a kin to our beloved Japanese promotions. Reporter 2: You mentioned The Syndicate, you have a past with them. Do you plan on a reunion of sorts? HM: I am very close with Claw and Masa Lau. As many of you know, I helped to train Casey James. On occasion, I have even trained
Mr. Starks when he is on tour in Japan. I have no contact about joining the EMWC with any member of the Syndicate.
Reporter 3: What belts do you have your eyes on and what can we expect from you? HM: All belts are within my grasp. I have proven that I am among the elite athletes of this current times. As for what you can expect from me...expect the best as always. The fans who see me wrestle will not walk away from the arena complaining.
Reporter 4: Last question, what of the appearance of your former pupil Super Gaia? HM: I have not much to say about Gaia. He apparently holds me responsible for my promotions folding. He thought that he would get his big break there. As that I have instructed him, I know of his vaunted skills. I look forward to wrestling him as well.
Thank you all for coming today and I once again say that this is a great honor. (Fade) JS: Hakiro Matsuoko has entered the EMWC!! And that's another major coup for the EMWC! The "Angel of the Sun" is a major star all around the globe.
TM: Blue's really got the checkbook wide open doesn't he? Why doesn't he get us some real men like Kowalski, Thunder, Verhoeven? I get stuck watching flying pansies like Matsuoko. Just get the fly swatter out and I'll drop that sushi sucking geek down to the mat!
JS: Matusuoko is a major superstar, and a world class athlete, Todd. He's a great addition to the roster, and I guarantee, once you see him in action, you'll agree.
TM: Wanna bet on that one Stegglet? JS: Not gonna bait me Todd...no way, no how. We've still got two big matches to go. TM: Yeah, we've got the guy who ran like a coward from the IieW and the man who took down the biggest star in our sport. Oh yeah, this oughta be a close one.
LD: Jeff Matthews had to withdraw for personal reasons. He's still a fine athlete, and will be a great match for D.
JS: These two men had a run in at the Rumble when they eliminated each other from the Rumble match but tonight, they'll go one on one.

I guess if Jeff hits him, he'll do it because it's good for Ronnie, because Jeff is my hero, and he only does nice things. Maybe if I hit Bobby, it'll make him better... That's a nice thing."
[Jeff almost breaks a smile at this... But he keeps his calm. The crowd starts booing a bit.]
Ronnie: So Jeff, just remember, if you hurt me, you are going to hurt little

Ronnie: Jeff, that was almost as fun as your fiancee!
