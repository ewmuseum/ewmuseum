[Lori Dane walks down the locker room, talking to the camera crew.] LD: Look, are you sure my hair looks good? I've got millions of people who will see me and... why is the camera light on? There isn't much strategy in this match. It's basically a street fight in a cage. No interference by anyone. But Joe's been around long enough. He can certainly handle himself in a fight. Against Starks or Slater.
