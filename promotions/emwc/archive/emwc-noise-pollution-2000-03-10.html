Noise Pollution, 10 March 2000

10 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The facade of calm and serenity is provided through use of light neutral colors in one portion of a secluded psychiatric hospital in California. Leaning against pale coral-colored wallpaper are four males, all in a line. At the front of the line, a young Hispanic man, with wild eyes and wilder hair. Behind him, a squat man, who curls his hands into fists, before uncurling them. He does this over, and over, and over again. And then, there is the familiar man. Black cherry hair thrusting upwards in the front, Devil Elvis stands tall. He seems to be occupying himself by staring off into space, a vaguely angry look still fixed on his face. The final man in line looks as though he's been through hell. He is a white man, with light brown hair that flirts with getting in the man's eyes. The hair is in disarray, as is seemingly everything else about the man. Each of the men in line wears street clothes, and stands on a thick layer of light grey carpeting. It's quite clear that this hospital stresses making the patients feel at ease, as if they are at home. Black t-shirts seem to be all the rage at this particular hospital, as all four men in line wear them. The Hispanic man heading the line up wears a simple black t-shirt, the squat man wears a simple t-shirt bearing the name of a tattoo parlour, Devil wears an inside-out black t-shirt, and the scruffy man at the end of the line wears a tattered black t-shirt that simply reads "REDEMPTION," in fading white.] Squat Man: Hurry the f[CENSORED] up, you goddamned s[CENSORED]. [The young man at the front of the line, wiry thin and dangerous looking, whirls to face the squat man. For a moment, it looks as though the squat man is going to be made to regret his abrasiveness and use of a racial epithet. Perhaps fortunately for him, however, the young man walks out of the scene. The squat man takes his place at the head of the line, tapping a small foot impatiently. Elvis stays where he is, however, and so does the man behind him.] Scruffy Man: [Unintelligible grunt] [Devil snaps out of his trance-like state, and turns his head with unnatural speed to face the scruffy man.] Devil Elvis: Who are you, and why are you here? [The man doesn't seem at all surprised by Devil's sudden rapid- fire interrogation. He doesn't even look at Elvis as he replies with questions of his own.] Scruffy Man: What am I? Where am I? [Devil ignores the man's questions, and continues with his line of queries.] DE: Do you have a name? [The scruffy looking man swallows.] Scruffy Man: Not much use for a name... How about you? [Elvis' reply is without haste of any kind.] DE: I have many names. You... I will call you... [Devil looks down, and his eyes seem to focus on the man's "REDEMPTION" t-shirt.] DE: I will call you "Red," Red. [Red's indifference to his new name is not particularly surprising. He and Devil stand silently for several moments, before Red points ahead, and Devil moves along in the line. The camera follows him, and the EMWC junior heavyweight champion is shown being handed a tray full of food and medication. Elvis takes his tray, and briskly makes his way over to an unoccupied white table. He sets his tray down, before setting himself down. On Devil's tray is a sandwich that looks a little too perfect, as though it was cut into a circle and the meat inside was measured to fit just into the round bread above and beneath. Joining the sandwich are two clear cups about three-quarters full with dark blue and murky orange liquid, a half-yellow and half-red pill, and a smallish cup of yogurt. Having only had the time to give his food the once-over, Devil is joined by the shuffling form of Red. Red's tray is nearly identical to Devil's, only with different liquid medications and a few more pills. Red sets his tray down across from Elvis, and stiffly sits down across from the enigmatic professional wrestler.] Red[mumbled]: Hello. [Devil nods to Red.] DE: Hello again, Red. [Elvis rips open his yogurt.] DE: I propose a toast. [Lifting up the murky orange liquid medication, Devil gives Red another nod. Red, appropriately enough, picks up a reddish liquid medication.] DE: To newfound friendship and the wonders of mental health care. [The patients "clink" cups, and Red downs his medication. Devil pours his into his off-white yogurt, before shoving the yogurt cup aside.] Red: You should take your medication... DE[flatly]: No. Red: You should give me your medication... [Devil shrugs, and lifts the dark blue liquid.] DE: To Bill Masterson. [Red raises an eyebrow questioningly at Devil, but takes the blue liquid and downs it in one gulp.] Red: I used to be on that med... Blue always seemed to have the best flavor of the bunch I've taken in this place. [Instead of questioning the concept of "blue" as a flavor, Devil seems to have become preoccupied by something on the wall. He runs his fingers over the wallpaper, and it becomes apparent that he is tracing the outline of whatever is on the wall with a slender index finger. The camera is zoomed in, and a well-carved message can me made out. "There is nothing here... ..not even me." Red notices Devil's interest in the carved message, and speaks up.] Red: From what I've heard, a small, quiet man carved that. One of the nurses said that the guy was "all white, like a ghost, or a dead person." Must've been a pretty interesting guy... [Elvis' attention is once again focused on Red.] DE: True enough. [Elvis reaches down, and hands the yellow-and-red pill across the table.] DE: Enjoy. [Red nods.] Red: These ones are good... These ones make the past go away. [Red continues to nod as he swallows Devil's pill, in addition to two identical ones of his own. Fade to black, then fade again to show the face of Alex Martinez. The instrumental introduction to a swingy-kind of music strikes up as we all see the well-known catchphrase of "You're gonna get burned" mouthed in slow motion by the silent "Badass". The music? It's "Hell" by the Squirrel Nut Zippers, playing along to some fast-paced clips.] #In the afterlife# [An infuriated Devil Elvis interrogates Bill Masterson in the ring.] #You could be headed for the serious strife# [The infamous "Fireproof Tucker" spot. The Down Boys doubleteam Andrew Tucker, one Irish whipping the man into the other, who throws a fireball into Tucker's face.] #Now you make the scene all day# [The Epitome of Cool come to the ring, looking, well, COOL!] #But tomorrow there'll be Hell to pay.# [Devil again, this time hitting a "Descent" cradle piledriver on Masterson.] #Now the D, and the A, and the M and the N,# [Demon Boy Ishrinku brawls with "Bad Eye" McBaine.] #and the A, and the T and the I-O-N# [A skullface-painted William Craven stands, screaming, in the entranceway being sprayed with the "Blood Red pyro" that accompanies his entrance.] #Lose your face, lose your name,# [Alex Martinez is struck in the face with a ball of fire by Jeff Matthews, during that man's first stint as Caleb Temple.] #then get fitted for a suit of flame# [Pyro shoots skyward from all the ringposts at the opening of an EMWC program.] #Now the D, and the A, and the M and the N,# [A mysterious cloaked man stands in the ring, the lights are low, and he seems to hurl lightning at the Anti-Gravity Kid.] #and the A, and the T and the I-O-N# [Old footage of Serge Annis, casting down his arms and causing flame to shoot up from the ringposts.] #Lose your face, lose your name,# [Vandal Gomez counters a CUCO Travieso moonsault with a fireball, setting the mask worn by the "dickish rudo" on fire.] #then get fitted for a suit of FLAAAAAAAME!# [Simon Ezra sets Tiger Claw on fire in the Killing Box, then cut quick to a fullscreen image of the new EMWC Noise Pollution Logo.] Pan around the smallish venue, where about 3500 screaming fans have packed the place full. The lights are low. Fade to show the stage where the announcers sit. The three are hidden in deep shadow, sitting behind a long black desk, which bears an impressively embossed gold EMWC logo with the words "Where dreams become reality... and destinies are shattered" underneath it. Still behind the desk are photos of various EMWC greats of days gone by: Narcissus, Lorenzo Vasquez, Olaf Proet, Bruno Cicarelli, Marcus Freeze, Scott Pain, Roscoe Law, Mike Austin, Gabriel Whitecross, Curtis 'Thunder' Hansen, "Crimson" Joe Reed... all fantastic former World Champions of a league with it's own special place in the annals of wrestling history. Everything suddenly flares to life as lights go up in the studio, the crowd screams, and the announcers are revealed. Seated behind the desk are veteran sports journalist Jon Keeton wearing a dark blue sports jacket, Lori Dane, sitting on his left, looking stunningly simple in a white 'EMWC' t-shirt, and, finally, Todd Michaelson, on Keeton's right, looking less than thrilled to be here on the EMWC's secondary program. The camera then focuses in on Keeton. The live studio audience screams all the louder as the program finally begins.] JK: Hello, fans, and welcome to EMWC's Noise Pollution! I'm Jon Keeton, joined as always by the lovely Lori Dane, and former EMWC World Champion, Todd Michaelson. LD: Hi. TM: I'd still be champ too...if I hadn't gotten bored with it all. Y'know, bein' on top looks good from the outside, but after you've seen it all, done more than you've seen, and loved ALL the ladies in the world...well, it just gets old. [Evil stare at Todd from Lori.] JK: *Ahem*, yes, well... Nice opening statement there, Todd. Folks, last week was an eventful one for the EMWC. Feuds are heating up, wrestlers are running rampant, and not just one, but two titles changing hands on An Evening. TM: Oh! Can I tell 'em!? Can I? LD: Just do it and shut up, Todd. TM: We now have new Tag Team Champs...Setzer Van Strife and Dave Bryant...A-K-A, THE FOUNDATION! JK: Given the chaos that the Foundation has brought to the EMWC, I'd not exactly call that an upset, but that's not all they did. Fans, I'm sorry to say that the former Tag Champs, Strictly Business, are no longer with the company. TM: A melon on your melon will do that... LD: Let's not go there. I'm just wondering what poor Piper's gonna do now. TM: Who cares!? Let 'er go starve in an alley somewhere. Wrestling's man's work, anyway. *SMACK!* TM: OW! Don't worry, Lori, I was counting you as a man when I said that. *swish* TM: HA! Missed me! JK: Lori, Todd, please! A little professionalism. [Todd and Lori, who were both getting up, retake their seats.] JK: That's better... Now, the other title changing hands this week was the TV Title. In a shocking upset, VD Vitrone LOST the strap to none other than Vandal Gomez...a man who's only had 3 matches in this company. TM: It's a miscarraige of justice, I tell ya'. He had that cloak guy and Jacobs workin' with him. JK: And Vitrone had Langseth and CUCO Travieso running in to take out Gomez. Neither planned by him, of course, but that still made it a level playing field. 3 on 3. TM: Whatever... JK: An Evening also saw the debut of "Gabriel the Nightmare", who, I understand, is an old friend of Alex Martinez. Martinez got Gabe to help him fend off the team of Jeff Matthews and Devil Elvis. The match didn't have a definite winner, but Martinez did win the prize being contested on that night. He gets to choose the stipulations to his match with Matthews at Rising Sun Rumble... TM: Hey, you're leavin' a little something out there, Jon. At the end of that match, ol' Devil Elvis got carted off to the funny farm. Hence the opening bit for this program. JK: Very true. Bill Masterson made good with his threat to send Devil to an asylum on An Evening. TM: I just wanna know how the camera got in to catch Devil and "Red"s conversation. Kinda creepy...like there are cameras everywhere... One could be watching us right now! [Todd looks around, alarmed.] JK: Of course there is! It's right there! [Keeton points to camera, and Todd looks, again looking frightened. His facade of fear cracks, however, as the wannabe comedian/announcer breaks into a minor fit of hysterics, laughing at his own joke.] JK: Folks, we have to move on. First up this evening, we have words from Vandal Gomez. TM: He's dead meat! Taking on Langseth in only his 4th match!? Good lord... JK: I guess we'll have to see about that. Let's hear from the new TV champ. ====CUT==== [A flash of light. And then darkness. A flash of light. Darkness again. As your eyes adjust, you notice that the light is coming from a swirling spotlight far on the horizon. The stars can't be seen, and neither can the moon, as the night sky is covered in clouds. The spotlight swirls again, seeming to almost be cast down directly where this is being filmed. Then, a hoarse, scratchy voice is heard] Voice: Have you ever been taken to hell and back? [The scene moves downwards. At this point, the spotlight is not glaring, so all we can see is a dark shadow of a man standing near. The voice emanates from where he stands as he continues] Voice: I have. It's not a pretty place. [The spotlight suddenly flashes onto the figure, illuminating him. He is sickly pale, with long white dreadlocks hanging low in his face. He is immediately identified as Vandal Gomez. We can also see that he is not wearing a shirt, and his ribs are wrapped tightly with bandages. He clutches at his ribs lightly with his left hand, grimacing as he speaks. But at the same time, he is smiling. The light is gone in an instant, and we are left with the same darkened dialogue] VG: Most people are saying that I was put through "hell" in defeating VD Vitrone. That is far from the truth. My body was destroyed, yes. But my soul and my mind drove me on, pushing me up and to my feet. And even when it looked like my destiny was decided, that my body could take no more, I survived. And I won. [The light flashes again, and we see Vandal cringe, cough, and vomit blood. The light dies away, leaving us with the sound of Vandal heaving for a few more seconds before he resumes what he was saying] VG: The EMWC Television title... a title held by some of the toughest men to ever have stepped into an EMWC ring. A title revered by some, and sought after by others. And a title the current champion does not care about. [The light flashes, showing Gomez standing up straight, not clutching his ribs any longer. Also, it's become apparent that he does not have the EMWC Television title on his person] VG: What I do care about is this petty war of attrition going on between the Foundation and the EMWC in general. It sickens me that such a convoluted war goes on and on, with no end in sight. And it sickens me more that to reclaim a lost soul from within it, I myself must become involved. [The light shines once more, showing Gomez face filled with anger. As it goes past, he continues speaking] VG: Mark Langseth, you are nothing. A petty man fighting a war for no reason other than self-gain and self-love. You must prove to everyone that you are the best. That you alone deserve the title of "World Champion". If you truly were the best, you would not have to prove it... your actions would speak for themselves. Instead, you surround yourself with fools and heathens, all trying to "get a piece of the pie". All to get a piece of said "pie" yourself. [The light again. Gomez still bears the same expression of anger, though he is now clutching his ribs again] VG: I know what I do and the reasons I do it. I do not need to prove myself to anyone. And I need not answer to anyone. I am the Adversary. And as such, my burden is great, but my wraith is even greater. So while the Angel and Him stand by me, looking on, I do as I will, decimating and destroying by my own devices. They may call on me at times, and myself on them, but we are each our own. A piece of a broader puzzle... but our own piece nonetheless. [Light. Gomez cringing, coughing. No blood, but it is obvious there was serious damage done to his ribs] VG: I am an injured creature. My body is almost gone. But my mind and soul are blazing. And in their sights is you, Langseth. They are wanting your blood to stain my hands. They are wanting your flesh to pull apart, revealing your insides to the world around you. They are wanting this foolish war to stop. And thus, they are wanting the first casualty to fall at my hands. Unfortunately for you, you have been chosen as the first. The first of four. [The light, and Gomez is standing up straight, his fuchsia eyes almost burning with passion. His arms are extended outwards, as if he's a parishioner about to convert the masses. He shows no sign of pain during this next interlude, only a strange aura of now. Also, the spotlight has stopped moving. It is as if the world has suddenly stopped for Vandal, and all eyes are on him] VG: The first will fall to the Adversary, who will bear the wraith of the unspoken. As the first falls, the second eclipses the first, attempting to stop the slaughter. Within this attempt, comes the Angel, who will strike his vengeance down and destroy the second. The third and the fourth will flee, with the third slowing and falling to Him. The fourth will fall to the Son. And with the bodies of the weak stacked, the final day of Judgment will begin. [Suddenly, a flash of light flares up, as Gomez sets alight a patch of grass on the ground via a flame from his hands. My body is broken on the inside, but that does not matter. Because if you thought the flames that ignited you before were the worst that could happen, you truly underestimate the power I have come to bear. It will all be revealed to you when we meet. Begin to pray... [Gomez goes straight-faced, in a matter-of-fact manner] VG: ... and do realize that your prayers will only be answered with laughter. [The scene moves downwards, to the flames that are quickly spreading across the grass. As they spread, the brilliance of their ever expanding light engulfs the camera, making it impossible to see anything but bright light. With this image, we go back to the studio] ====CUT==== LD: Uh...very creepy. JK: It appears that Gomez has truly decided to own the title given him by...uh..."Mister Cloak".
