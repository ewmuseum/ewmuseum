MATCH! Ladies and gentlemen...to date...the line-up for DEVIL'S NIGHT!!! (HUGE POP!) CB: Thanks for your- [Suddenly, "Sexual Thing" by Poison starts up and the fans erupt in a chorus of booes.] JS: Don't tell me... this non-active wrestler is here to waste more time? [As the fans continue booing, Zack Malone emerges from behind the curtain wearing a black t-shirt reading "ZMWC -- ZACK MALONE
WRESTLING COUNCIL!" and a pair of faded, shredded jeans, with a
Body Shop baseball cap turned backwards on his head and a pair of
Oakley shades covering his eyes, his bombshell valet, Cheryl
Amber following him, dressed in a dress that leaves little else to be revealed. The music suddenly switches in mid tune, as
"Dreamlover" by Mariah Carey starts up and Trey Porter jumps out from behind the curtain as well, followed by the mammoth Drago,
Porter's bodyguard. Malone looks behind him and smirks, and
Porter gives him a thumbs up. Both men walk to the ring, Malone snatching the microphone from Blue on his way in. The music fades, as the male fans both curse at Malone and Porter, while making several catcalls towards Cheryl. The ladies however, do the same for Porter and Malone...]
ZM: Yeah... yeah... thank you, now just shut up for a minute. [Heel pop!] ZM: Y'know, I've been accused of a lot of things in my life. I've been accused of being a little too naughty. I've been accused of bein' just a little too nice. I've been accused of being a bit too good. And I've been accused of being very...
_very_... BAD! But I ain't _never_ been accused of WASTING ANYONE'S TIME! [Malone glares at the broadcast table.] TM: [over headset] Way to go... you got us in trouble. JS: [over headset] I could care less, when is he actually gonna wrestle anyway? All I ever hear about is how great Zack Malone is, what an athlete he is, what a Superkick he has... when are we going to SEE it?
[Malone paces around the ring, while Porter sits on the top turnbuckle, badmouthing a fan.]
ZM: I mean, everyone knows Zack Malone is THE BIG ATTRACTION! The MAN OF ACTION! You're lookin' at Captain Comeback Kidd himself, and what do I get when I come to EMWC? Besides the gift basket, one night with Chris Myers' chick, and a harem of whoreish secretaries... I get nothin' but insults. I mean...
c'mon... have you ever even _seen_ me wrestle? [Malone suddenly does a two fingered point towards the big screen, where clips of Malone in action from other Federations flash rapidly on the screen. All of them being vicious
Superkicks. They show the infamous Superkick that Brian Knight partially blocked, and ended up outside the ring with a broken jaw AND a broken hand. The last clip they show is of Trey
Porter, standing in the ring, with Malone stomping his foot, about to drive a Superkick home. The clip suddenly blacks out, and we go back live to the ring, where Porter seems a little agitated, while Malone is surprised.]
ZM: Oops... how'd _that_ one get in there? Hell, what difference does it make? Y'see, Trey Porter and Zack Malone? We _built_ this. We built The Body Shop. We built the art of being heels.
People watch us, and they take notes. Because we're not what you'd call two wanna-be flashes in the pan, who only get recognition because we whore ourselves around on the internet newsgroups... no. We've been here for _years_. We've been World
Champs, we've been main eventers, we've been allies n' enemies but no matter what we were, we always stole the show! And that my friends, is why we simply couldn't be around Ronnie D, Simon
Lebec and Billy... Billy Sexton? Seriously, how'd _he_ ever pass the initiation into The Body Shop? Fact is, the last month or two... it's been just a bad nightmare in the history of the most storied stable ever. These two men right here in the ring, we're the foundation on which everything good about wrestling is built... and the _only_ thing good about wrestling is _us_! So that works out real nicely.
[Malone smirks.] ZM: Ronnie D. might claim to be everything that Trey and myself are already, but everyone knows he's a pale impersonator and a pale imitation of greatness. That little chairshot, Ronnie?
I've been wantin' to crank you upside the head with a chair for a long time now. You joining up with Trey here kinda cramped my plan, but Zack Malone _always_ gets his way in the end, and here in the EMWC is no different! Tell 'em what it's all about, DTP!
[Malone tosses the mic over his shoulder, and it's caught perfectly by Porter, as if the chemistry between these two knows no bounds. Malone starts playfully pretending to punch Drago in the head, jumping up each time he does, while Porter begins to speak...]
DTP: Ooh, la, la! crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! DTP: You know for the past five months I have walked around EMWC with a monkey on my back. I think it is pretty obvious who that monkey is or was. For five months I came out here arm and arm with a man who has made a career out of ripping DTP off. A man who claims to be an ICON. [DTP laughs] Well as everyone in this sport knows, there is only one ICON and that is Trey Porter!
There is only one SELF-MADE Legend and that is Trey Porter! And on one night in September, I proved to the world that their is only _ONE_ and will only be _ONE_ "Dreamlover" Trey Porter. Ooh, la, la!
[DTP removes his sunglasses] DTP: You see Ronnie the reason you are sitting at home right now is because DTP decided it so. I've said it once and I'll say it again.......I am the beginning and ending of this business. I believe that was proven the other night. The other night I had enough and with the snap of my fingers I made your career vanish right before your eyes. Five months ago I made several bad decisions, but the other night I corrected them. Now some people out there might be wonder what the h3ll DTP is talking about.
Well let me refresh the memory of all you nimrods. I let Lebec into the Body Shop because of you, Ronnie D. For five months I kept my mouth shut while he single handedly embarrassed myself and the rest of the Body Shop. I also put up with you trying to steal my own spotlight, because I thought it was for the good of the Body Shop. Well enough was enough and I decided it was time for a change and that change came after you once again embarrassed the Body Shop at Wargames. You once again tainted the name that Zack Malone and I created and carried to greatness.
[DTP pushes his hair out of his face] DTP: Ronnie, I suggest you take a real good look at yourself and reevaluate your life and your choices. Maybe you should step up to the plate and face your greatest fear. That fear being the legend of "Dreamlover" Trey Porter. Ronnie if you were even half the man I am, then you wouldn't have retired. _ONE_ [DTP holds up his index finger] little beating and you hang up the tights.
Well I've had two career ending injuries and I still stand before you today as great as I have ever been.
[A fan with a "FREE RONNIE" t-shirt jumps into the ring. Drago quickly nails him with a forearm and the fan drops. Malone kicks the fan out of the ring and security quickly drags the fan out]
DTP: Typically Ronnie D fan. Hey geek, why don't you go play on the internet and put post after post of how great Ronnie D is, because lord knows that only no talent hacks think Ronnie has the stuff.
[DTP unzips his leather jacket halfway and then stops] DTP: Now onto other business. That business being Steven Spector, the man who is scheduled to do the time honored tradition at Devil's Night. What you received at the hands of
DTP at Wargames is only a taste of what I have in store for you.
For several months you have been running around EMWC waiting for the opportunity to get your hands on DTP. Well at War Games you let that opportunity slip right through your hands. But now you get a second chance. A second chance to prove yourself to the
bookers, to the other wrestlers, and most importantly to me! I don't impress easily Spector. So come Devil's Night when you are on one end of that leather strap and I'm on the other, I am going to take it upon myself to whip you like a stinking dog. Then I will drag you from corner to corner on my way to yet another in a long line of victories. Then I will stand in the ring with my
hand raised high. Ooh, la, la! But in my other hand......... [DTP unzips his leather jacket revealing the old EMWC Universal

DTP: Ooh, la, la! If you want it off me, then remove it yourself! OH GOD!! HE KNOCKED BLUE FORWAR- TM: INTO THE HEARTBREAKER!!! THE HEARTBREAKER ON BLUE!!! [DTP then picks up his old title and tosses it over his shoulder] DTP: Now for those of you who aren't taking this seriously.....[DTP signals Drago] [Drago picks up Blue and sets him up for a powerbomb. Malone quickly runs over and stops Drago. Malone then points to a table on the outside. Drago lifts Blue up and drops him to the outside through the table.]
JS: OH GOD!!!! OH GOD!!! BLUE HAS BEEN POWERBOMBED THROUGH A

(With a wicked grin on his face, DTP steps through the ropes, followed by the rest of The Body Shop. Trash flies towards the stable as they walk down the aisle.)
JS: We need to get a medical crew out here now! The EMWC President has just been powerbombed through a table! He got powerbombed through a table by that monster Drago, and he's hurt bad.
(The medical crew races down the aisle to the ringside area.) JS: Fans, this is a very bad situation, but we're being told to cut away. I'm told we just received this tape moments ago. It's from a wrestling superstar that is not under EMWC contract. He's not under contract with any wrestling promotion. However, the name is known all over the wrestling world. We're showing this tape under orders from President Blue himself, and I have no idea of the contents. But ladies and gentlemen...we're about to show the tape of a superstar we never thought we'd see on an EMWC show. Let's roll the footage now, and you can see for yourself...
[A nighttime scene on a beautiful beach, in the capital of Paradise. Cabo Wabo, Mexico. People dance, people sing, people down rum and tequilas while trying to limbo under a flaming bar. With the exception of the occasional singed chest hairs, this is nirvana.
And sitting on a far off bar stool, looking out amongst the controlled blissful chaos, is a very tan, very relaxed, very happy man.
"Sychosys" Joe Petrow. Newly golden highlights in his now short, spiky hair glisten off the artificial daylight created inside the open-air hut, as a bartender flips and whirls some bottles, and slightly bruises a cucumber to prepare Petrow's preferred drink. He speaks with a couple of seemingly important men, who leave as the camera approaches, the only discernable words being Petrow's parting
"Catch ya later Eddie!" comment. Then, as if he's done it a thousand times before, he turns to the camera to speak]
JP: So, what were you all expecting? What did you think would happen after August 1st, when I left on top of the wrestling world? Did you think I'd go back to my old stomping grounds, the hellholes of the world, trying to once again solve some greater truth?
Nah. I'm retired now. I paid my dues. Now I'm enjoying myself. [Petrow's comments are briefly interrupted by the bartender] BT: Here's your Screaming Viking mate! JP: Yo, thanks Robert. Oh hey, bring that out for me would you? BT: Sure! [The bartender reaches down, and pulls out some championship belt. He hands it to Petrow, who drapes it over his shoulder]
JP: Best damn Australian bartender in Mexico! Oh hey, y'all haven't seen this in a while, have you? I don't have much use for it myself, although as well as being a surprisingly good conversation piece, it makes an excellent walnut cracker. In other words, this baby is enjoying retirement as well.
Anyway, while flipping through the bull fights the other night, I see some guy from some EMWC promotion talking about wanted to get Sycho on Quigley. Well, it was all in Mexican, but the words "Quigley" and "Sycocito" made it well enough. So I had the English broadcast delivered to me by my private satellite because, well, you know, I'm Joe Petrow.
First of all, Quinn, when you're in Japan, use some better music. That Sakai Noriko song was the lamest thing GAINAX was ever associated with five years ago, and still is today. You wanna get over back there, dig up some Evangelion tunes. Even that Sinatra one. That'll work everytime.
As for getting sycho on Quigley...damn man. Don't you get tired of the same thing over and over? I've humiliated Chris
Quigley one hundred and sixty three consecutive times. And that's a documented fact. For all the 12-step programs he's gone through to recover from the humilation I've inflicted on him he could have climbed the Stairway to Heaven, and taken the greatest bump of all time when St. Peter booted his ass back down. But eventually, he takes a sledgehammer to his own head, and either forgets everything, or rewrites the facts into some ludicrous story that he can believe. And his heart goes on.
Fact is, humiliating Chris Quigley is great fun. But like shaving an albino wombat, it doesn't make much difference in the long run. BUT!
[Petrow smacks his head with an open palm] JP: Aw, dammit, I missed the spit take. Hold on... [Petrow reaches back, and takes a drink, then spits it out with overacted surprise] JP: You know, it just so happens I've thought of a way! I've thought of a way to rid Chris Quigley for wrestling once and for all!
But you know...do you guys deserve that? I still haven't figured that out yet. Do you deserve me dragging my ass out of this beautiful paradise, spoiled only by occasional visits from
Marty Warnett, and ridding your league of the most boring presence known to mankind? Do you have the love and affection to show me that would make me feel my efforts would not go unrecognized? Do you guys have what it takes to make me wanna go sycho for the good of all mankind one_last_time?
If so, show me a sign. Give me a reason. Start the revolution. Because I am the Eggman, the Messiah, and Willy
Wonka all wrapped into one. That is, if you really, really want me to be.
But til then, I'm in limbo. Literally. Ciao! [Sychosys walks off towards a flaming bar. Fade out.] JS: Ladies and gentlemen, you did not imagine what you just saw. You saw "Sychosys" Joe Petrow on an EMWC broadcast. Now...don't jump to any conclusions here. Although he did mention a pair of
EMWC superstars, he _did not_ state that he wanted to be in the
EMWC, nor have we stated that we'd want him here. That's a matter for Mr. Petrow's representative and for the EMWC President
Chris Blue to discuss. However, Mr. Blue did want this footage shown so that the EMWC could respond to the words of Mr. Petrow.
Does the EMWC want Joe Petrow? And if so, can the EMWC convince him to venture out of retirement and return to active competition? So, now the ball's in your court, fans. Let your voices be heard! We want to know...do you want Joe? Or do you want Joe to go? The answer lies in your hands. Fans...let's head down to the ring for our main event!

It is schedule for one fall with TV Time remaining.
Introducing first...he is a member of the Cult of Personality...DIAMONDBACK...CHRISSSSSS MYYYYYYERS!!!!!! (BIG face pop as "Cult of Personality" begins to play. KG: And his opponent... ("Something Wicked" By Nuclear Assault grinds to life over the PA as a reddish glow overtakes the arena.)
KG: Making his return to the EMWC rings..."ERA OF DEFIANCE"...GAAAAAAAABRIEL WHIIIIIIIIIITECROSSSSSSS!!!!!!!!! (Another HUGE face pop roars out as the former EMWC World Champion walks down the aisle, determination in his eyes, he rolls into the ring, and stares at his opponent for the night.)
JS: The EMWC return for Gabriel Whitecross, and a great night it is now! We've seen a lot of action, but the return of Whitecross has got to be the highlight for a lot of people. And this is gonna be- No! Don't let him ruin this moment!
(The crowd begins to boo harshly as the EMWC North American
Champion, Mark Langseth begins to make his way down the aisle, his eyes fixed on his former mentor. Langseth slides into the ring...and grabs the mic.)
ML: You boys were ready to party without me? Let's make this song a trio! (And with that, the lights extinguish. As the house lights come back, the heavily bandaged form of Serge Annis is standing in the middle of the ring.)
JS: Uh oh...things are getting itchy now. We've got four men in that ring who do not like each other at all! This is a big powderkeg, and all it takes is one spark to set it off! Fans, we're out of time! We're out of time! This situation is ready to explode! We'll see you next week!
