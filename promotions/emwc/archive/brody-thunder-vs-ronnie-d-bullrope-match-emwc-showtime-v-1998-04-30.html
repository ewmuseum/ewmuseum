Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The ring announcer's and commentators' text of the Bullrope match between “Lone Wolf” Brody Thunder and “Playboy” Ronnie D at EMWC's Showtime V, as the IIeW organizer kept it on his own site. The date is the one the career list on his “Playboy” Ronnie D page gives for the match (30 April 1998); the text itself states none.
