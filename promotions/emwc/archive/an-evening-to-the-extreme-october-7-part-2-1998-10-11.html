Annis: Where to start... where to start? Let's start with the facts shall we? For the last two weeks, Casey James has labeled me as his little attacker. The one who is attacking him when the lights are off, and leaving behind a flaming shirt or something.

North American championship to be concerned with. Not you.

[Annis runs a hand through his hair.] Annis: On to more important issues. Mark Langseth, you are the luckiest son of a bitch in the EMWC. As far as I am concerned, you are walking around the EMWC wearing -my- North American championship belt. Because you never beat me. No... you beat that worthless punk, Myers. And until you beat me Langseth, you'll never be a 'pure' champion. Langseth, I congratulate you on your independence from Gabriel Whitecross... but you won't be any kind of a man until you step in against the Epitome of Evil all on your own. Without me Langseth, you wouldn't be wearing that title. As far as I am concerned, you owe me... heh heh... and whether it be your title... or your very soul, Serge Annis shall collect. Oh... and Whitecross...
[Annis grins his usual sick sadistic smirk.] Annis: Welcome back to the EMWC. Heh heh... thanks to me, things have changed. Your world has changed. So if you ever want to come to me looking for revenge... I am not a hard man to find... Heh heh. Just ask Chris Myers.
[Annis' grin is immediately wiped off with the mention of Myers. Annis' look grows cold.] Annis: And Myers... Chris Myers... and all of his buddies in the
Cult of Personality. And people called Genesis wrestling's
'Culture Club'... Myers, I'll give you credit. You can brawl with the best of them. But frankly, you're no match for me. If you ever decide to make the mistake of getting into my face again...
I assure you, that you'll pay just like everyone else that dared cross my path... [Annis looks down at the black object he placed on the mat.] Annis: And having said that... it brings me to the most important topic of all... [Annis walks over to the object and stands over it.] Annis: Tonight, I step into this ring against Matt Harris, in the semi finals of the King of the Death Match tournament. I just want to speak about this Death Match tournament for a moment.
[Annis looks into the camera with his icey blue eyes.] Annis: A lot of guys come out here and lay claim to be hardcore. They wrestle a few 'Falls Count Anywhere' matches, and they think that they are the (BLEEP). Those kind of people, are what is wrong with hardcore wrestling. They don't know what hardcore means. Hardcore means competing with broken bones, while your body is cut up in so many places, your doctor needs a route map just to figure out where each bit goes... So the way I see it...
in order to be called 'hardcore', you have to live your life hard core. And if anyone's seen my matches, you'll understand where each and every one of the scars on my body came from. But being
'hardcore' isn't something to brag about. Everyone knows it when they see you bleed for them. There's no point in making a show out of it.
[Annis bends down and picks up the object, placing it under his arm again.] Annis: That is why I am in this tournament. I'm not in it for the glory. I sure as hell am not in it for the money. The reason I'm in it is to send a message to each and every superstar watching on the monitors in the back. That message being, that Serge Annis will go to any measure to cripple his opponent... and to hopefully, earn a little respect around here. Either way, you'll see me in the finals.
[Annis looks down at the object.] Annis: But, before that comes tonight's match against Matt Harris. Harris, I went to EMWC president Chris Blue, and I told him my idea for a match. He told me I was crazy. I told him he was right, but either way, I wasn't leaving his office until he agreed. So here I am tonight, all set and ready to announce the stipulation for tonight's Death Match...
[The crowd pop accordingly as Serge looks out to them.] Annis: Now then... who here has heard of a "Seven Tables of Fear" match up? [Crowd pop.] Annis: All right... now replace the tables... [Annis undoes a latch on the cover.] Annis: With seven of these... [Annis pulls off the cover, revealing a window pane of glass. The crowd begins a huge hardcore pop. Annis grins, and holds the glass up to his face, as he looks at the camera through the window in his hand.]
Annis: Only much... much bigger. [A huge hardcore pop.] Annis: Matt Harris. Tonight, you and I shall go at it in a
"Seven Pains of Glass" match. After tonight Harris... which ever one of us makes it to the finals at Devil's Night, truly deserves to be there. And Harris... I've already dealt with the Devil himself once... and I'm looking forward to doing business with him again in the Death Match final... at Devil's Night.
[Annis smirks, and drops the mic on the mat. "Hands of Death" plays once again, and Annis steps over the top rope, and leaps down to the floor, still holding the window pane. He looks through it again as he looks to a camera.]
Annis: Tonight Haris. I'll be sending you to Hell in a thousand tiny little pieces... (And with that, the lights extinguish, leaving behind an eerie feeling over the entire arena for a few moments. Soon, the lights reignite, and in the ring...is no one.)

Quigley emerges from behind the curtain wearing a pair of black jeans, a black tank top, and a brown leather jacket. His hair is brushed back, into a spiked look on front, and is still kinda wet looking. Quigley pays no mind to the fans as he walks down the aisle, not even pausing to appreciate an attractive redhead in the front row who lifts her shirt and exposes her assets to him... she's obviously attracted to the "rebel without a cause"
type... Quigley simply slides under the bottom rope into the ring, and gets to his feet alongside Stegglet, who is literally beaming at being chosen for this interview, which is a stark contrast to Quigley's half menacing/half bored facial expression.

Sure, you got your guys like Casey James, Trey Porter, Ronnie D, Ken Curtis, and of course, Curtis Hansen, and none of them are good enough to take the title right now. Trey Porter is a cripple. Porter. And about Curtis Hansen...
