Showtime X Update V3

23 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We fade in from black to a generic looking "studio" setup. Two leather chairs sit with a coffee table between them. On the wall behind the chairs, a digital clock is slowing ticking down...
days...hours...minutes... seconds. It's coming, my friends. It's coming.] VO: For nine years, the EMWC has been the elite force in professional wrestling. For nine years, the EMWC has stood atop the mountain as the premier promotion in our industry. For nine years, the EMWC has been simply... the best.
In ninety-seven days, the EMWC takes the stage for the final time... to do what they do best... to do what they have done better than any other for nine years.
In ninety-seven days... it's Showtime. And the wrestling world will never be the same. [The lights come up, revealing the man sitting in the first chair... the voice behind the voiceover. _The_ voice of the Empire Wrestling
Council. then, now, and always.]
JS: Hello everyone, I'm Jon Stegglet... [Stegglet chuckles to himself... knowing what must be done.] JS: Welcome everyone to sunny Los Angeles! Welcome everyone to some non-descript television studio that none of you recognize! And welcome to...
AN UPDATE TO THE EXTREME!!! [Cue canned cheers... and another chuckle from the golden throat of
Extreme.] JS: God, I've missed that. Well, after a couple weeks of press releases, the boys in the Extreme Towers seemed to think you all needed a face to go with the news you were getting... a voice you all know.
[Stegglet spreads his arms wide.] JS: So here I am... dragged back into service to come out here each and every week and tell you people exactly what's going on. It'll be my job to spread the rumors. It'll be my job to give you all the news. And in the end, it'll be my job to pump the hype higher than ever before.
Cause fans... this is it. One more night... one more night for the greatest company there ever was.
It's Showtime... the granddaddy of them all... for the last time. [The eternal EMWC play-by-play man looks down, obvious sadness on his face.] JS: This is strange. I feel so... alone. [Stegglet looks up at the camera.] JS: I think I may need some help. Any objections? [Seeing none and hearing none... cue some music. Cue "Ecstacy of Gold". Awww yeah. Let's get fiesty.] JS: Ladies and gentlemen... the former World Heavyweight Champion and the greatest colorman ever to hold a mic...
TODD MICHAELSON! [With his music blasting, Todd Michaelson steps into view... as usual, he's wearing Armanni from head to toe... and a big ol' grin on his face.]
TM: Damn good to be back, Steggs. JS: Indeed. TM: Now... I've been sitting around for a few weeks waiting for this to get going. So, tell me we've got something to talk about.
JS: Indeed. TM: A match? JS: Indeed. TM: Getting all those out of your system? JS: Indeed. TM: Rock. [Michaelson sits in the chair opposite from Stegglet as a video wall slides down behind the duo... and fills with a shot of one of the most infamous buildings in wrestling... Viking Hall.]
TM: Is that-?! JS: It's one of the possible venues. TM: Are you serious? JS: Well, isn't it fitting for Eternally Extreme 2 to take place in the home of the first one?
TM: Sure... if we like pissing money down a hole. JS: TODD! TM: Dammit, Steggs. I'm all for being sentimental... but that place holds a thousand people at best. We're having a show that could sell out the Rose Bowl, the LA Colisseum, the Pontiac Silverdome, and the
Galactic Arena of Mars.
JS: Mars? TM: I hear it's real close right now. JS: Oooookay. Well, anyways... there's still some very active negotiations going on about the venue... but Viking Hall has _got_ to be strongly considered.
TM: What about the rumor that we're looking at doing multiple venues? We've done that before. JS: It's a popular choice in some corners of the office. A place in
Los Angeles, a place in New York... paying tribute to the birthplace of
Extreme and it's adopted home.
TM: But then Keeton gets to announce. JS: Hrmm...good point. Maybe we can track down Moxy. TM: How about Moxy, Wayne Savage, and Lau in a three man team designed to drive each other crazy?
JS: Including our audience? TM: If necessary. Speaking of Brian Lau though... [Michaelson gestures to the video wall which changes to a shot of the
Mark of the Beast... a symbol that can only belong to one group.]
JS: The Syndicate. TM: Damn right. JS: Perhaps the most feared and dangerous group in the history of our business, the Syndicate is without a doubt one of the most important parts of EMWC history.
TM: And no one's confirmed them to appear yet?! JS: Unfortunately not. I understand we've been in contact with Casey
James, Tiger Claw, and Brian Lau... not to mention other Syndicate members Jack Greene, Bobby Taylor and... well, we want to have some surprises, don't we?
TM: Not me. Tell me something... have we reached Hardin yet? JS: Not from what I understand. He's proven very difficult to track down. TM: Hrm. Must be some kind of voodoo magic keeping him hidden. JS: Perhaps. But you can bet the E's officials will do all they can to further track down the rest of the Syndicate and confirm them for...
well, for something. TM: I wanna see Claw kill someone... period. If I don't get that, I'm gonna pout. JS: We'll see what we can do. TM: Box. Frozen Ghost. Whatever we can do. JS: You bring up an interesting topic. A lot of people are dying to see the gimmick matches we've helped make famous one more time. People asking for the two you mentioned... people asking for the Woodshed...
people asking for Stairway To Hell. What would you like to see? TM: The Woodshed.... seriously, Steggs... can anyone really top the
Woodshed match we saw Courtade and Langseth in a few months ago? I don't think we should even try. The Frozen Ghost matches have always been interesting... but we'd have to have two brutal son of a bitches involved to match the past ones.
JS: What about the Box? TM: ... JS: Todd? TM: The most dangerous match this company... this industry has ever known. It's destroyed careers... it's shortened men's healthy lives...
It took a finger. [Michaelson looks down.] TM: It's the most requested match in EMWC history. No one can imagine seeing us go out without it.
JS: But? TM: I just don't know, Steggs. Last time, someone lost a finger trying to top what had been done in it before. This time? I mean... do we really want our company to end with something... with something worse?
JS: ... TM: What? JS: That's the most thoughtful thing you've ever said on EMWC television, Todd. TM: Yeah, well... um... Lori's a whore! [Stegglet cracks a grin as he looks at the mini-Extreme Screen behind him which turns into a picture of the Japanese national flag.]
JS: Well? TM: I knew you'd ask me about this one. JS: You're the one with the connections in Japan, Todd. If anyone knows what Blue is up to over there, it's you.
TM: The rumors are running wild... and you want me to spill the beans? JS: Damn right. TM: I think I'll let the boss announce this one himself, Steggs... and after speaking with him tonight, he's promised he'll do exactly that next week.
JS: That's gonna be big news I think, Todd. TM: I think so too. I'm looking forward to it. JS: Well, we know our announcement for next week... and I understand we're just moments away from our announcement for this week. But before we go to that and wrap up this special Update... let's get this week's
"Dream Lineup". Last week, we heard from the final North American
Champion, Luke Kinsey. This week... we caught up with longtime EMWC competitor and former Television Champion, Maniac Jack! Jacko?
[The shot cuts to a pre-taped clip of Maniac Jack in front of a generic
EMWC backdrop.] MJ: What do I want to see? What do I want to see on the biggest show ever? Steve Spector vs Mark Langseth... in my opinion, the two greatest superstars ever to wear the World Title. These two fought a lot of times but never really settled anything. Point of No Return...
Greatness Personified... yeah, I dig it. JW Hardin vs Chris Courtade... the two biggest toughest Texans... well, except Bobby Taylor... I miss ya, Outlaw! Um... what was I saying?
Thirty Man Rumble... no reason... well, one reason. Can I be in it? Luke Kinsey/Juan Vasquez vs the Fraternity Boys... can you imagine the pre-show promos? I'd be in tears laughing. Man... this is hard. Can't I just fight for the TV Title again? [And with that... we cut back to the announcers, shaking their heads.] TM: Moron. JS: Can we pick someone a little better next time? Please? Fans, we're out of time for this week's update... but we've got one more thing to do before we go. It's time to announce the first match for Showtime
X: Eternally Extreme. Ladies and gentlemen... at Showtime X..._live_ on December 28... The former World Tag Team Champion...the former World Heavyweight
Champion... he is the Golden God...
DEVON CASE! [Michaelson smirks.] TM: And the man he will face at Showtime... his idol... a former World
Television Champion... another former World Heavyweight Champion... and an EMWC
Hall of Famer...
EDDIE VAN GIBSON! [The first "match graphic" appears on the screen, still shots of Devon
Case and Eddie Van Gibson with "Showtime X" written under their phots.]
JS: Devon Case. Eddie Van Gibson. TM: You don't get a better match than that _anywhere_ in the sport,
Stegglet. JS: Amen. Fans, it's one final night. One last chance to see the company that everyone hated to love and loved to hate. One final page of the history book to be written. It's Showtime... and it doesn't get any better.
TM: SHILL~! JS: For Todd Michaelson, I'm Jon Stegglet... we'll see you next week! [The graphic of CASE vs EVG stays on the screen as we fade to black.]
