The Midnight Crew Debut

28 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, I'm bored and the promotion's own President just gave the thumbs up on the admission of a new tag team I created... I being [name withheld], handler of Alex Reaver (ex-GLCW Great Lakes champ) and Jeremy (retired for now) Steele. Also running a fall project (invite-only, e-mail me for info)... Anyways, [name withheld] (you "marks" know him as Tex Violence and Simon Ezra, among others) helped me with this tag team idea and now, here they are. Feedback is cool, critique if you want, whatever. Just wanna kind of spread the word. -Zach ---- [Fade in: The shot opens on the main living room of a two-bedroom apartment in the Los Angeles area. From the looks of things as the camera does a quick pan, it's a mess. The only furniture in this part of the apartment is a glass coffee table, a rolling cart with a small television set on it, and a black leather sofa with a few tears in the upholstery. Stacks of CDs, magazines, dirty clothes, cigerette butts, and a few empty pizza boxes are scattered everywhere. Two halogen lamps in the far corners of the room produce the light source for the scene. From off-stage in one of the bedrooms, a man begins to walk into view, limping noticably with the aid of a cane. He has short, spikey brown hair and blue eyes, looking to be in his early 30's. He's dressed in a pair of baggy jeans, black boots, and a sleeveless t-shirt. On one of his wrists he wears a thin strap of leather with silver studs on it. Slowly, he walks to the couch and sits down. He stares at the camera with a smug look on his face.] MAN: Welcome. [looks off camera] Come on in, boys. [From off-stage, two men walk into the shot and towards the couch. The one on the right appears slightly bigger. He has bleach blond spiked hair offsetting his natural dark roots and a dark tan complexion from his Latin American heritage. He is dressed in a pair of loose fitting leather pants, black boots, and a wifebeater-style tank top. On the left is the smaller, younger looking man. He has long black hair with blue streaks dyed in, and his facial features resemble a younger version of the man on the couch. His attire constists of black boots, baggy black pants, and a blue spandex t-shirt. The two take their places behind the couch, with the man below them in the middle.] MAN: EMWC fans, before I begin, ya better take a moment and survey your surroundings. Take a deep breath, inhale, look around ya... make sure you know in the back of your mind what you're doin', where ya are... because years from now, when people ask, "Where were you?" you're gonna want to give 'em a straight answer. Ya see, every generation has its defining moments... a moment in time that paves the way for the future to come. This is one of those moments. For the past few years, tag team wrestling has been on the downslide... oh, there's a few good tags around, but the talent's spread too thin and nobody gives a rat's ass about the majority of 'em. About the only way to generate any interest is to take two hotshot singles and toss 'em together. A lot of people figure tag team wrestling is slowly but surely goin' six feet under, 'til it's nothing more than a memory of somethin' you used to know. Well, as the decade draws to a close, and things hit their creative low, the time's come... the time's come to unleash two raw talents into this business and take advantage of the weak competition. Right now, these faces and the names I'll give you, they don't mean jack... but I tell ya, they will. That's for damn sure. Years from now, you'll all be glad you got to witness this... the beginning. It starts with a little more than a whimper... but 'fore long, it'll be one helluva big bang. From this day forward, every moment is another page in the book, another chapter of the story... and when it's all said and done, the things we accomplish will forever be embedded in the mind of every wrestling fan in the nation. No, I'm not spewin' some hype for the fun of it... this ain't about strokin' our egos. It's about fortellin' the future... and _this_, boys and girls, is the future in the flesh. [With a smile, he cracks his knuckles.] MAN: The name's Chris, and let me finish the introductions. To your left is my kid brother... ain't he a doll, ladies? [laughs] Say hello to Tommy Wilson, the luckiest son of a bitch in the game right now... and why's that you ask? Simple. He was slummin' in some techno punk clubs in the area, dreamin' of bein' some fruity dancer before I injured myself and needed someone to carry on my legacy. The kid's got athleticism, I'll give him that, and he's a quick learner. [pauses] To your left, Julio Rodriguez, one of the hottest indy wrestlers in the state of California... destined to be a big fish in a small pond 'til I hooked him up with Tommy. Now, he's finally able to realize the dreams of grandeur he's always wanted -- drugs, sex, money, it's all here for the taking. I treat my boys well, ya see... because they're gonna make me famous. They're gonna make _us_ famous. And who's "us?" The Midnight Crew. EMWC, this place has seen a lot of people come and go... legends passin' through on their way out, risin' stars on their way up, and hotshot wannabe-superstars catching a break. Every once in a while in a big-time promotion like this, a few talented guys thought to be nobody's slip past the cracks and into the spotlight... onto the highlight reels... and arrive at greatness. The way I figure it, we're the ones that are hotshot ourselves to the top. It's not a matter of if, it's a matter of when. No amount of bullshit politics and red tape can delay the inevitable... because ya see, we're runnin' on a force much greater than fate or luck. It's not about kissin' ass or catchin' a lucky break. Any second-rate tag team can accomplish that. It's about crawlin' and scratchin' your way from the unknown to the glory... it's about goin' from shit to _bein'_ the shit based on pure will power. Everybody and their uncle's got _potential_... but only the chosen few make good on it. [Pausing, he reaches into his pocket, pulling out a package of cigerettes and a lighter. He takes a convient break to light up, takes a quick drag, then tosses his lighter to Tommy, who occupies himself with the small flame. Julio looks on, trying to grab the package before Chris puts it away.] CW: To sum it up, right now, we're just another bunch of no-namers talkin' a good game, and you're all wonderin', well, can they go? [nods] Trust me, boys and girls... they can. Maybe you won't realize it today, maybe not a month from now, but before long, the Midnight Crew is gonna be on the tip of every EMWC fan's tounge... There comes a time when ya got to realize, even the great ones started at the bottom. Right now, that's where we are... but in due time... that's all gonna change. There's not a damn team alive my boys are intimidated by... 'cause as far as we're concerned, these formulative times are just a warm up to greater things. This is just the beginning of what's gonna go down as one of those things you never forget. So, like I said before, take a moment and look around, sniff the smells, see, hear the sounds. Close your eyes, take a deep breath, and let it sink in. The future has arrived. [With another laugh, Chris takes one more drag, then dead-pans to the camera.] CW: Ready or not, here we come. [Chris reaches up, taking the lighter back from Tommy. He then motions for Tommy and Julio to exit out of the room, slowly pulling himself off the couch and walking back to the bedroom he came from with his cane. As the men walk off to an off-camera part of the apartment, the camera lingers on the sofa shot for just a few moments before... fade to black.] ---- Good lord, that was horrible! What kind of moronic, dumbass, stupid as dirt, piece of worm ridden filth would take a team like that into their fed?! (looks in mirror) Whoops. Never mind. — the promotion's own President, replying in the same thread
