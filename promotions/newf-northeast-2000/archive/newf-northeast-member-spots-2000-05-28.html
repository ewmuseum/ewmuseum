Member Spots going quickly

28 May 2000

I am close to filling the roster to the amount I'd like to start NEWF with. So if you want in, now is the time to join
NORTHEAST WRESTLING FEDERATION NEWF Hello fellow e-feders. I'm going to be straight with you, I'm writing you not because I have the hottest new e-fed that will be the best. I'm writing all of you more for your help almost.
I am 18 years old, and I'm going to college this fall, and I have a connection with an independent federation owner that has let me know I will be able to work with him during my college years. I hope to one day get enough $ to run my own federation, or at least write for a major one.
So here is what I ask of you all. I want to run an e-fed, and I want to run it successfully. However, I see alot of things wrong with e-wrestling and I hope that I can take those wrong things, and eliminate them in this e-fed. I think some e-fed president's don't appreciate the effort given by all members. That will not be the case here. Everything and anything you do to make our shows exciting and interesting will be appreciated. You will never be held down to a certain status. If you are a card opener, that does not mean you can never be a main eventer. If you show me you put forth the effort then you will get the shot you rightfully deserve.
The fed will be small like ECW was in 96. No pay-per-view but we will have huge events like the WrestlePalooza's etc. Cards will be held in small arenas, and if we get bigger rosters that will upgrade us to maybe live tv. When the fed gets alot of exposure throughout the internet and more people start to sign up we will move past the regional atmosphere and move to a nationwide fed.
There will be house shows every other day which will just simply be results. Just to get your wrestlers more matches.
Roleplays are not the only way you will win a match. Strategies will definetly win me over to a point. This fed will not be an e-fed where you have to be on 24/7 and only work on NEWF business. We'll only have 1 roleplay packet a week and 1 card a week.
Sometimes roleplays will not appear on the packet but instead on the television show. I'd like for members to interact with each other and form alliances and also to think up feuds on their own before coming to me for assigning them.
I. General Idea II. Roleplaying III. Angles IV. If I start low, can I rise? V. Cards VI. Rules In The Ring VII. Rules Out Of The Ring VIII. Titles IX. Cursing X. Pay-Per-View XI. Website XII. Application I. Northeast Wrestling Federation gives handlers a chance to be as real as they possibly can. Decisions will not solely be based on just how you roleplay. Original Wrestlers are the only ones that will be accepted! No simulators will be used to write matches or to chose the winners.
II. Roleplaying will be done once a week. I will send out a 'roleplay packet' through e-mail. There will be no website roleplaying. The calendar for roleplays will be sent out after I get the amount of members I want.
III. I will most likely after each big show e-mail all handlers individually or in groups, to explain the type of angle I would like your wrestlers to pursue. This is not definite, because I'm not God
:) If you have an idea that you think would be better, I will probably take your suggestion over mine. The least I will probably do is combined mine and your idea in some way. Thinking of your own angles goes into getting a push in this federation. Effort=Success.
After a big event, the fed will take 1 week off for angles and feuds to be thought up etc. IV. No doubt about it. It's all about effort here. Increasing effort by everybody makes it more enjoyable for everybody. There is lots of fun in competition, and if everybody gives their 110% there will be lots of fun.
V. This federation will be run independent style. We will have *1* TV show a week. Fully written (if anybody can write, it's appreciated). Now here's the thing. On that show we could only get maybe 5-7 matches, so there will be House Shows every other day with summarized results. This is another chance for wrestlers who weren't on TV to get over. If you get a feud over on a house show, most likely the next tv show will update that angle.
VI. Match rules are like ECW's. We are not some "EXTRME" fed, but we give the fans what they want. This is not to say that every match will have barbed wire etc. because if you've ever seen ECW, you know that's rarely the case. But there will be NO DQ's or Countouts in all matches.
VII. You *WILL* sometimes be in an angle that you dislike. If you tell me this, I will try and end your feud/angle as quickly as I can.
But it's part of the business. Don't bitch to me that you're not being used right, because you will be used right. I will give
Everybody a chance. VIII. We will start with 3 titles. Heavyweight (World), TV, Tag. If
I get enough Cruiserweight's to join I will make a Cruiserweight
Title.
IX. If you are given mic time at a house show (which if you've seen an
ECW show, everybody tries to get their 1 minute of fame on the mic)
you may say whatever you please. However, if it's the television show and you do an in-ring or interview, I will Beep your curses.
X. We will not have pay-per-view events, but we will have major cards every 2 months or so. Eventually if we get big enough we'll be on ppv. XI. No website yet. I am going to wait until I get the members I need to start this fed before I worry about the website.
