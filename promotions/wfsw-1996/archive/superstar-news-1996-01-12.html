[WFSW] Superstar News 01/12/95

12 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera zooms in on Taylor Skidmore and Pat Rickaby. Skidmore is dressed in a white dress shit, black vest and a red tie. Rickaby is wearing a WFSW T-Shirt. They are sitting at a desk with the WFSW logo on a blue screen behind them. They see the camera.)
RICKABY (startled): Ahh! (Skidmore whispers something to Pat and then laughs.) SKIDMORE: Welcome to the very first edition of WFSW Superstar News! (Rickaby is still trying to regain his composure.) RICKABY: Yes, we've, uh, got a great show today. For you. SKIDMORE: To start things off today, we've put together a list of the WFSW wrestlers, as of January 11th, 1996!
RICKABY (finally calm): Actually, *I* put it together... SKIDMORE: Well, whatever. Here it is. (Pictures of all the following wrestlers appear on the screen as they are introduced by Skidmore and Rickaby.)
FACES Singles: "All American" Randy Tuff The Cincinnati Knight Jerry "Goon" Gordan The Royal Guard Super White Samurai Tag Teams: The Royal Knights (The Cincinnati Knight & The Royal Guard) HEELS Singles: "Diamond" Dave Jersey Johnny Canuck Masked Mayhem 1 Masked Mayhem 2 Masked Mayhem 3 Mountie II "The Renegade Warrior" Jack Randall Steve "Air Raid" Anderson Zeus Tag Teams: Canadian Carnage (Johnny Canuck & Mountie II) Mayhem, Inc. (Masked Mayhem 2 & Masked Mayhem 3) (Screen fades back to Skidmore and Rickaby.) SKIDMORE: ...this is shaping up to be a great fed with all of these world-class superstars. Now I'd like to talk about
Gold in the Cold '96.
RICKABY: How 'bout that main event, the big battle royal for the WFSW World Championship title and the WFSW United
States Championship title. I know one man who will shock the world when he announces his entry in the WFSW and the
Battle Royal.
SKIDMORE: Oh ya? Who's that? RICKABY: Well, as much as I'd like to tell you, I'm sworn to secrecy. SKIDMORE: Whatever. RICKABY: There's also going to be some great singles and tag team action at Gold in the Cold.
SKIDMORE: That's right. And we can't forget the pre-show to the event, which will be the debut WFSW card!!! It has been officially titled "WFSW: Countdown to Gold in the
Cold '96". RICKABY: I didn't forget. It will be held January 26th,
1996. That will be a great card, too.
SKIDMORE: Next week, we will hopefully have more info on that. RICKABY: So, who are all these guys in the WFSW? SKIDMORE: I was about to get to that. RICKABY: Well.... SKIDMORE: Here are two flashes that have been sent to my office. First, here's one from "Diamond" Dave Jersey.
(Screen fades in to "Diamond" Dave standing outside a long white limousine with an arena in the background. He is has short spiked hair and wearing sunglasses and he is wearing a classy long gray
LondonFog coat with a stunning white silk scarf.)
"DIAMOND" DAVE JERSEY: WFSW, guess who's here in the best organization around? Yup, you guessed it, "Diamond" Dave Jersey, the one who is going to go to the top whether you like it or not!
I can safely say that I will be a force to be reckoned with here in the WFSW, so if anyone has any brains at all among you, you will let me be, because if you cross me you're going to get swapped. Fair warning and fairwell, everyone.
(Meanwhile the limo door opens with a bunch of beautiful women. "Diamond" Dave shouts "I'm coming girls." Screen fades to black.)
RICKABY (laughing): That guy's great! He's a personal good friend of mine, someone in the WFSW with some class! Hold on a sec...
(Rickaby reaches into his pocket and grabs his cellular phone. He dials a number.) RICKABY: It's ringing! SKIDMORE: What are you doing? Who are you calling? RICKABY: Diamond! Hey it's Pat, from the WFSW! I never knew you were joining! SKIDMORE: Oh, brother... (Pat continues his conversation for another minute or two.) RICKABY: Ok, see ya later Diamond! SKIDMORE: What was that all about? RICKABY: Oh, he had to go. He had some women over. SKIDMORE: Ya, right. Somebody will shut his mouth, like maybe one of these guys...here's "The Duke" David Jones and his men.
(Screen fades away. Scene now is in front of the WFSW logo.) THE DUKE DAVID JONES: Well Well, a new federation with talent. I'm here with three of the best fan favorites in this fed. They are the Super White Samurai and The Royal Knights (The
Cincinnati Knight and The Royal Guard). Let's hear from them. SUPER WHITE SAMURAI: I've been all over the wrestling scene trying to capture a singles championship, but came up short.
Now I'm going to aim for the WFSW World Championship. I know I have what it takes to win a title and I'm gonna do that!!!
THE DUKE: He's no joke! But here's the next WFSW World Tag Team
Champions, The Royal Knights.
THE ROYAL GUARD: I've been around for 3 months trying to win a single or tag belt but came up short, or not being booked on wrestling cards. But in this fed I'm out to get a title and I'm gonna have one!!!
THE CINCINNATI KNIGHT: What's up! Everybody we ready to rumble!! So, heels, get ready to take on the Royal Knights! And whoever trys to get in our way in the tag tourney better pray to make it to the next day.
THE DUKE: Well that's all for now but watch out everybody, the
Super White Samurai and The Royal Knights are coming to an Arena near you!!! And fans, please give us your 100% support! Thank you!
(Screen fades back to Skidmore and Rickaby.) RICKABY: Ugh! I'm glad that's over with! SKIDMORE: I see a title in those guy's futures. The WFSW just might be their lucky fed. RICKABY: No way. I could take those guys. SKIDMORE (laughing): I'd like to see that! RICKABY: Why are you laughing? SKIDMORE: ...oops, we're out of time for today. For Pat Rickaby and myself, thank you for joining us, and good night!!!
(Screen fades to black.)
