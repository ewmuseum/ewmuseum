NOR-CAL Update and Deadlines

Jun 6, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Howdy to all! The new NOR-CAL opens up with the first show in a little over two weeks. Forget tournaments to start with... we'll settle the title situation when the time comes. For now, let's focus on getting characters and angles over. Here's the card lineup. Enjoy! NOR-CAL Wrestling presents... The NOR-CAL EXPERIENCE! From the Alex G. Spanos Center in Stockton, CA Airdate: Monday night, June 21, 2004 Opening up NOR-CAL will be a Fatal Fourway, with the first man to score a pinfall or submission winning the match! It pits the conspiracy theorist JEREMIAH PHOENIX in the same ring with the large, hateful monster ERIK, the vengeful "FALLEN ANGEL" DAVID CROSS, and one of the rising stars in wrestling, the ultra-intense "PRIMETIME" MARCUS HICKS! All four of these men will be in the ring at the same time... who will escape with the win? The second match will mark the debut of one of the top tag teams in wrestling history into the ranks of NOR-CAL, when NYTRO & GLYCERINE team up to take on the makeshift tag team of VICK FREZNIC and "JACKHAMMER" VINCE LARSON. Larson has shown that he can't be trusted in the past, so can Freznic, the rookie entering his first official match, even trust his own partner? Third, we have what is sure to be a stiff, tough battle. The former shootfighter MARCELLUS NORWOOD makes his debut against the rugged veteran CHRIS CHRONIC. Whose mat skills will come out on top? Fourth on the docket is probably the strangest combination of the night. The flamboyant GLAM ROCK COP makes his debut against the ultra-strange FRANK KNIGHT. Glam has made a name for himself through some strange antics in the past, as has Knight. Who will be able to win the battle of the mind games? Fifth is what, on-paper, seems to be a complete and utter mismatch. The dangerous, deadly, super-huge MAD HAYASHI takes on the small, thin "BORN LUCKY" MIKEY COX in an Ultimate Weapons Table Match! The ring will be filled with weapons from the outset, and the only way to win is to put your opponent through a table! Will Hayashi eat Cox alive, or can Cox prove his moniker is not just a name... and get very lucky in winning this match? And will SPIKYJIM continue his issue with Hayashi and show up, despite being intentionally kept off this card? Finally, the MAIN EVENT pits four great wrestlers in the same ring with one another. "MODERN MESSIAH" SETZER VAN STRIFE teams with VINCENT LYONS to take on "THE RICH ONE" STEVE GREEDY and "THE WALKING CONTRADICTION" SABBATH!!! You have two bonafide legends in the ring with two up and comers, and only one team can come out on top! Will Van Strife and Sabbath's past issues overshadow all other things in this match? Or can Lyons or Greedy make a name for themselves with their famous partners and get a fall here? We WILL hear from a new tag team entrant from the stable of world-famous tag team manager "GENTLEMAN" JOHNNY RIFLEMAN! Their identities are unknown, but they will make their debut on the next NOR-CAL EXPERIENCE! We MIGHT hear from a mysterious tag team entry that has been rumored to be entering the halls of NOR-CAL. The only word we know that describes this team... is Hardcore. That could mean a lot of things. We WILL NOT be hearing from SPIKYJIM, as he has intentionally NOT BEEN BOOKED on this card, in order to keep potential chaos between him and Mad Hayashi limited. Strat deadline: Saturday, June 12th at 11:59pm eastern RP deadline: Monday, June 14th at 11:59pm eastern
