NOR-CAL WRESTLING UPDATE #1 5/20/04 - Note To All · Standings · Next Card Lineup · Handler Reminders (including IMPORTANT ROLEPLAY INFO!!!) · Website and E-Mail Info - HUUUUUUGE BOOKING INFO - NOTE TO ALL I just want to thank everyone for signing up for this little project. We've hit the ground running, and I'm sure you're going to love what we have planned for the first card. Seeing as how we won't have a flash show, and all promos will be on the card, all roleplays should be backstage promos or vignettes, and are due by Saturday, May 29 at 11:59pm eastern. If you want an in-ring, please write me as I don't want a ton of in-rings dominating the show. And please, I'm begging you, read the HUUUUUUUGE BOOKING INFO thing I've put at the bottom, as it will lay out all the questions you might have regarding the direction of the first cycle. I tend to steer a lot during the first cycle, just to get things started. And seeing as how (at last count) we have about six different corners of the e-wrestling universe coming together in this one fed, it may be good to let everyone get used to each other. Oh. And the Press Conference scheduled for tonight is pushed back a day. I'm waiting for one segment from a handler, and I don't want to put the show out without it. So if you're out there, get the segment to me by, oh, as early as possible on Friday. It's coming out Friday no matter what, and will be constructive in providing some background for everyone about the characters in the fed. So check that out, write me at [detail withheld] with any questions, visit the website at http://norcal.freewebpage.org/ (with an all new, updated roster section) and enjoy the lineup! NOR-CAL TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP CHAMPIONS - VACANT 1. 2. 3. 4. 5. · Next Card Lineup THE NOR-CAL EXPERIENCE Tuesday, June 1, 2004 From Florin High School in Sacramento, CA ALL MATCHES ARE ELIMINATION TAG NOR-CAL TITLE QUALIFYING MATCHES! - The rules are this... the last two men standing win the match. Even if they're from opposite teams. Two eliminations must occur, and there will be a 2-on-1 situation during the match. MATCH ONE "Primetime" Marcus Hicks & "Simply Golden" Jeff Keenan VS. "Jackhammer" Vince Larson & Tommy Lionheart - Hicks and Keenan, two of the most arrogant men in the sport, take on the rookie Larson and the returning hero (after a three year abscence from wrestling) Tommy Lionheart. Can Hicks and Keenan put aside their egos and work as a team, as they have the advantage of having wrestled consistently for longer than their opponents? Or will the combined good-nature of Larson and Lionheart get it done? MATCH TWO "Fallen Angel" David Cross & Nytro VS. Stone Harris & Frank Knight - This is the match of the mismatched teams. Both Cross and Harris are straight-ahead men that the fans love. Nytro and Knight, on the other hand, are always calculating and thinking their way through matches. Can these teams co-exist? Or is this just an explosion waiting to happen? MATCH THREE Vincent Lyons & Chris Chronic VS. Jeremiah Phoenix & Red Falcon - One of the fastest rising young stars in the entire wrestling world today, Lyons, teams up with a true legend in Chronic to take on two sadistic, suicidal freaks in Phoenix and Falcon. MATCH FOUR Stellar Stallone & "Born Lucky" Mikey Cox VS. Thorin Stonehand & Erik - The pure brawling technique of Stallone mixes with an old-fashioned arrogant heel in Cox to take on the shortest man in NOR-CAL, Stonehand, who also believes he's the last surviving dwarf from Middle Earth, and the giant, evil monster Erik. This should be, well, the strangest blend of talent on the card. Can Stallone and Cox come together, even though they both run their mouths? And how the hell will Stonehand and Erik even communicate? MATCH FIVE Spikyjim & Mad Hayashi VS. "The Rich One" Steve Greedy & Junya "Kamikaze" Kontani - On the other hand... this may be the most insane blend of talent on the card. In one corner we have the suicidal, homicidal Spikyjim teaming with the sumo gone berzerk in Hayashi. In the other corner is the cocky Greedy, who is earning respect in the wrestling world for his ability and looks to be a rising star. His partner is Junya Kontani. And if you know anything about Kontani, you know insane doesn't begin to describe it. What kind of nuclear device will these four set off in this match? MATCH SIX "Modern Messiah" Setzer Van Strife & "Primo" Chris Capri VS. "Walking Contradiction" Sabbath & Vick Freznic - Long ago in OWE, there was a feud between Setzer Van Strife and Sabbath. It looked to be one of the all-time greats, before OWE closed. Could this be the rekindling of that feud? And what of Capri, looking to take the leap into superstardom? And Freznic, in his first match ever, having to look at three of the top men in the business? · Handler Reminders ALL ROLEPLAYS DUE BY SATURDAY, MAY 29, 2004 · Website and E-Mail Info Official Website: http://norcal.freewebpage.org/ Webmaster, Publicist, Operator: [name withheld] - [detail withheld] - BOOKING PLAN FOR THE UPCOMING SHOWS There are four shows, then the PPV on June 29. We have a lot to get to before then. I want to have every title settled on the PPV, and I want everyone to get at least three chances to wrestle in this cycle. So here's the plan... THE NOR-CAL EXPERIENCE, 6/1 The 24 man roster is split into 12 random tag teams. Each team is put in an "Elimination Tag Match". The jist of it is this: You fight in a tag match until two men are left. Even if one guy from each side is left, that's the end of the match. The two men left will advance to the NOR-CAL Title Round Robin Tournament. The two who are eliminated will be put in the pool for the NOR-CAL Tag Team Title Tournament. The fun part about this is that every match will have a 2-on-1 aspect to it. So the two guys should easily beat the one, right? Well, that's where strategy comes in. Do you want your wrestler to jump his partner from behind and let the other two fight it out? Do you want to team with the partner and ensure your place in the tourney? There are a lot of options here. THE NOR-CAL EXPERIENCE, 6/8 The 12 men who move to the NOR-CAL Title Round Robin Tourney will be split into 3 groups of 4, making for six matches per round. All the first matches will be on this show. The 12 in the Tag Tournament will be joined by 4 newcomers to make a 16-man, 8-team tag tournament. Two of the first round matches will happen here. The winners move on to the semis in two weeks. The losers go into the Ladder Match Battle Royal for the Hardcore Bag of Goodies at the PPV. THE NOR-CAL EXPERIENCE, 6/15 The second pairings in the Round Robin Tourney happen here. The second group of first round Tag Tourney matches happen here, also. Same stips apply. THE NOR-CAL EXPERIENCE, 6/22 The third and final pairings in the Round Robin Tourney happen here. The semifinals of the Tag Tourney happen here, too. The winners go to the finals at the PPV. The losers go to the Ladder Match Battle Royal for the Hardcore Bag of Goodies ON THE CUTTING EDGE I - A DYNASTY BEGINS, 6/29 The Hardcore Bag of Goodies Ladder Match Battle Royal occurs with the 12 men eliminated from the Tag Tourney in the past weeks. The two teams left in the Tag Tourney fight for the straps. The 6 men who finish 3rd and 4th in the Round Robin Tourney will go into Pentagon Pain IV to fight for the Grindstone Title The 3 second place finishers in the Round Robin Tourney will fight in a Triple Threat Match, with the winner going to the NOR-CAL Title semifinals The semifinals and finals of the NOR-CAL Tourney will occur to crown a champion Got it? It might be confusing, but I'll guide everyone through it as the weeks go on. Suffice it to say, everyone on the roster WILL have a shot at a title come June 29th. So even if you lose a couple times... no sweat. You've got that one shot at gold guaranteed. Word. Nick
