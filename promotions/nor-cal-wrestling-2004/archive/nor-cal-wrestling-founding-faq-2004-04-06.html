NOR-CAL Wrestling F.A.Q. and Founding Announcement

Apr 6, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I read a quote a week ago that went something like this... "In the end, you will regret the things you did not do, not the things you did." And it hit home. In the past few months, I dedicated myself to my career. And for it, I got a huge promotion and am making a ton of money. I'm moving into a tremendous apartment on May 1. I've grown up in this game, done it all as a handler, done it all running supercards (including success AND failure in both). The one thing I've never done is to run a long-lasting, high-quality fed. I want to do it. I'm giving myself the goal of one year to run this fed. If it's more, great. But by hook or by crook we'll make it to one year. NOR-CAL Wrestling opens on June 1st. We already have some tremendous handlers who are signed on. I want more. It'll be a mid-size fed based out of Sacramento (my new hometown). It'll feature weekly cards (only 150-200k in length), with roleplays happening on the cards, and handler collaboration on angles being highly pushed. It'll feature strong champions, some of the best matchwriting out there, and original characters. And more. So take a look at the FAQ and toss me a line at [detail withheld] if you're interested. For years I've reveled in being a "flake". I've mocked myself time and again, and lived up to that moniker and joked about it. It's not funny anymore. I know responsibility now, and flaking on people who I like is not @#%$ funny. There will be naysayers, but I'll just have to prove myself again. We've got five so far, jump in if you want it. FAQ is below. NOR-CAL WRESTLING F.A.Q. V1.1 - Summary Of What We're About - Rules (very loose) - What do I need to do for the fed? - Roleplays (ONLY on-cards) - Strats - Angles (important) - Cards - Weekly - PPVs - Titles - Fed Characters - APPLICATION - Summary Of What We're About I've decided to do a fed that will be fun, a bit old-school, and innovative. The idea is NOR-CAL. The concept is simple. Weekly fed, on-card flashes. just like the "old days". But with some new twists. Everything in this fed is about telling the story and growing as handlers. And our attitude will be one of total rebellion. All conventions are out the window. This is not about egos, it's about putting out the best product possible. I believe in strong, long-running champions. I believe in building, rebuilding, and twisting feuds for months. I believe in developing new characters and handlers from the ground up, watching them come into their own over time. I do NOT believe in jobbing new people because they're new. I believe everyone should get a shot, everyone should have a chance once in awhile to be the top guy. I believe in a strong tag team division. I believe in excitement and loose rules during matches, and in handling. If you believe in that stuff, you'll love the way I run a fed. We're not starting out small, we're starting out moderately. Large gyms, small arenas. So go nuts on entrances and such. We're going to try to make this professional right off the bat. Another thing that's different. a miscellaneous board of directors runs the fed. You will NEVER see an "authority figure" on NOR-CAL television. EVER. I HAAAAAATE how everything in wrestling revolves around the people in power. It should revolve around the wrestlers, and this fed does. Thanks, and we hope you enjoy. - Rules Be active. That's it. You can be as racist, sexist, homophobic. whatever as you want. I don't care. It's your character, it's your time, you do what you want. But realize that it'll be harder to get over if you're all about cheap heat. And you'll make more enemies that way, for sure. And swearing is fine, but again, if you swear all the time it won't reflect too well on your writing abilities, and you won't get far. However, I love controversy. If you can get heat, can earn heat through good writing, you'll do okay too. - What do I need to do for the fed? - Roleplays (on-cards only) This is a closed flash fed. All roleplays go to [detail withheld] and are expected weekly. Write about anything, just get over. We REALLY REALLY REALLY like on-cards that involve handlers working together. You can write an ordinary promo, or you can work together to create a promo that really advances your angle. - Strats (VERY different) Here's the thing. I hate strats. Always have. I think you should be able to write a match based solely on the apps. But I understand the need to put over angles and certain characteristics and stuff. So just send in a brief match plan, with a couple of spots. I will not use huge strats. They suck. - Angles (important) How do you do angles in this fed? Simple. I'll post the e-mail addies of every handler in the fed at the end of each Update. Just go to them and start writing and figure it out. You can book it week to week, or plan it all in advance. Whatever works for you. It's strongly encouraged that you work with other handlers. Hell, that's what the fed's based on. I'm here to help along the way as a mentor-type, and to produce the cards. - Cards - Weekly There is one show, a weekly card, usually about 150-200k in length. It features 4-6 matches and will run on Sundays. It is unnamed as of now. - Supercards Supercards occur once every two months, and will be huge in size. Supercards will feature title matches, blowoffs, and also important plot points in characters' development and in angles. Two of the Supercards will be with other members of the RIWA. The other four during the year will be our own. These are our four yearly solo cards. Pure @#%$ Attitude (the most insane Supercard of the year, with lots of stips and such) The Hunt (there are some secondary matches on the card, but everything else on the PPV is based on "The Hunt", a special one-of-a-kind tournament that will rock the federation and shake up the rankings every year. It's our way of ensuring that new talent get a shot, while establishing a pecking order. More details as the Supercard nears.) Outbreak (Basic Supercard) Attrition (feuds continue, 4-Way Battle of Champions for top contender at Pure @#%$ Attitude) - Titles The World Title and World Tag Titles are the biggies. The secondary singles titles are on an even level, and will get better placement on cards determined simply by how hot the feuds for the titles are. NOR-CAL Title - The biggie. NOR-CAL Tag Team Titles - The biggie tag titles. Grindstone Title - A one of a kind concept, where the champion MUST defend the title every week, and there is a special stipulation with every title defense. It could be a cell match, it could be an amateur style match, a tag match, or anything in between. Hardcore Bag of Goodies - Just what it says. The winner gets a large Army-style duffel bag to fill with whatever they want for their Anything Goes-style title defenses. These matches are ALWAYS simple Hardcore Matches. Period. - Fed Characters The referees are random, the board of directors is secret. This fed is about you as a handler and your creations.
