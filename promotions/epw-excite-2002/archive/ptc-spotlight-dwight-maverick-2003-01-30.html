Thursday, January 30, 2003 ~EPW~ Dwight Maverick Handler's Name: Jon M. Wrestler's Name: Dwight Maverick Nick Name: The One True Vigilante Date of Birth: 12/19/1970 Blood Type: A+ Sex: Male Weight: 283 lbs. Height: 6' 10" Set up Move: Cowboy Attitude Finisher: Shotgun Vigilance Alignment: Neutral to Face Entrance Music: Wanted: Dead or Alive by Bon Jovi Wrestling Style: Roughneck/Power/Brawler/Striker Hometown: Wichita Falls, Texas Manager: None currently Main Quote: "Cause when th' One True Vigilante's on yer trail, not even GOD himself can save ya!" Signature Moves (List of 10 Moves): 1) Outlaw Drop (Backflip to Pancake 2) 2) Ol' Number 7 (Super Fall Away Slam from top turnbuckle) 3) Texas Two Step (Tilt A Whirl Sideslam) 4) Bad Whiskey (Triple H Clothesline 2) 5) Hell Bound (Test Full Nelson Slam) 6) Badlands Ambush (Reverse Death Valley 1) 7) Gallows Hill (Hangman Chokehold 2 (a turnbuckle move)) 8) Peacemaker (Kane Strikes (another turnbuckle move)) 9) Sidearm Slam (Undertaker Sideslam) 10) Frontier Justice (a modified Edgecator, Dwight's main submission hold, sometimes used in place of Shotgun Vigilance as a finisher when he wants to leave his opponent with a lasting lesson...) Wrestler's Biography: Born and raised on a ranch near the city of Wichita Falls, Texas, Dwight Maverick has always been a cowboy. Throughout high school he played football, a sport which his physical size and strength paid off in, and competed in any rodeo he could get himself into. After graduating with honors from Wichita Falls High School, he became one of the youngest men to ever compete as a professional bull rider. Three years into his career on the pro circuit, he made it to the National Finals Rodeo to compete for the title of World Champion Bull Rider. Little did he know that the event may well have been his last. He drew one of the grangiest bulls alive, one that was reported to be a bull no man could ride. Dwight's natural stubbornness got in the way of his better thinking, and he promised that he'd "Turn out." Unfortunately for the 21-year-old cowboy, the bull had other plans. He was thrown badly just 1 second before the eight-second whistle, and was almost ground into the dirt by the raging beast. The doctors said that he had about a 20% chance of surviving the affair, and a 10% chance of walking again. But the naturally stubborn Texan would prove the doctor's wrong as he made a complete recovery in record time. It was during this recovery process that thoughts of other lines of work began to flood his mind. One night while lying in the hospital bed flipping through the television channels in search of a rodeo, he happened to run across a broadcast of WWF Raw. After watching the show, his mind was made up. He would become a professional wrestler. After leaving the hospital, he immediately got on the phone and called up The School of Hard Knox in Mineral Wells, Texas. When he showed up to try out he was excepted on the spot, due to his physical attributes, natural stubbornness, and unyielding work ethic. Over the next year he became the top student at Hard Knox, and was accepted into the Texas Wrestling Association. He quickly dominated the superstars there and claimed the TWA Title in less than two months, impressing the hell out of the audiences and talent scouts alike. His first big break came when he was approached by a talent scout for the Hardcore Wrestling Federation. He signed up as a jobber, and within a month he was promoted to the main roster. He dominated all comers in this new arena, and claimed the Hardcore Title, the World Title, and the Tag Titles with his new found partner and friend, who would become his long-time partner and friend, The Archangel Dan Cichon, who also happened to be the owner of the HWF. It was also during this time that a feud would spark between him and The Reaper Jaxon Greene which would go on for years. The federation later merged with another promotion to form the XWF, or Xtreme Wrestling Federation. Dwight once again found himself at the bottom of the ranks, largely due to the corrupt new owner of the XWF. Despite the odds against him, he reclaimed the World Title in the short two weeks between the opening and permanent closing of the XWF due to bankruptcy. (*It turned out that the corrupt owner had spent more then three times his budget, but that's the way it goes...) After the death of the XWF, Dwight jumped through a series of different federations, including the Net World Order and Area 51 before returning to his ranch in Texas. While rethinking his future he got back in contact with his old friend Dan Cichon, who was at that point a member of the Canuck Heat Zone. He urged Dwight to try out, but before the Texan could call for an interview, the federation mysteriously closed. Still out of a job, the former rodeo cowboy began to fall into a bottomless pit of depression, frequenting the local bars and getting thoroughly sloshed everyday of the week and often becoming embroiled in vicious bar fights. The only thing that really kept him from going completely off the deep end during those dark days was the hard work of keeping his ranch going. Despite his tendencies to become drunk and disorderly, he kept his family's brand alive. The Six-Gun Ranch, of which he was the fifth generation owner, thrived while he slipped further into the dark. During this time of troubles, Dwight's mother took ill and passed away. Her untimely death was probably the biggest blow to the heart that the cowboy had ever endured, and it was probably the only thing, along with the guidance of his now widower father, that saved him from himself and the dark ways he had succumbed to. After her death, he got himself back together and began to train harder than he ever had before. He converted an old post barn on his ranch into a training facility, complete with a ring built from mesquite wood. It was in this "Brawl Barn", as he dubbed it, that Dwight worked himself back into shape, and became more athletic than he had ever been before. He eventually got a call from his old friend the Archangel, and was introduced to EPW owner Mr. Cool Levi by his old friend in April of 2002. After showing his soon to be boss MCL what he was capable of, he was offered a contract, which he signed on the spot. And thus Dwight Maverick arrived in the EPW, where he's been a dominating force ever since. He claimed the High Velocity Title once, the Ladder Title once, the Heavy-Pro Title once, and the top title of the federation, the World Title three times in his first six months in the federation. He left the federation on September 11 of 2002 to join the Army, where he served as a Ranger on a covert operation in Iraq. Old ties are hard to break though, and thus he returns to the EPW, to yet again prove that his ten years of hard earned experience in the business have made him the toughest wrestler to ever set foot in the ring.
