Sunday, February 02, 2003 ~EPW~ Time Bomb Handlers Name: Steven Wreslers Name: Time Bomb Nick Name: Timmy B Date of Birth: October 17 1981 Blood Type: AB Sex: Male Weight: 227lbs Height: 6’2 Set up Move: “Out Of Time” (Spinning neck breaker into a stunner) Finisher: “X-Plosion” (5 Star Frogsplash) Alignment: Heel Entrance Music: “Bad Boy 4 Life” By P.Diddy Wrestling Style: High flyer Hometown: Portsmouth, England Manager: n/a Main Quote: n/a Signature Moves (List of 10 Moves): Rolling Thunder Whisper in the wind Van Daminater Shuffle kick combo Spinning heel kick Buffalo sleeper Hurricanrana Monkey flip Jumping swinging DDT Dangerous DDT Wrestler's Biography: Not too much is known about him, he was born on October 17 1981 in Portsmouth England. He came from a very wealthy family, due to this wealth he has had the best education and facilities for his learning since being able to go to school. He graduated university at 19 where he studied wrestling and then joined NGW as a trainee, he won numerous titles and became a H.O.F’er within a year. As NGW merged into AW, he won a couple of titles and was undefeated throughout the time he was there. He has recently turned 21 and looking for a new place to ply his trade and that place is the EPW.
