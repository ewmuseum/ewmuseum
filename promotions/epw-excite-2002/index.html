A Primetime Central member fed run by its owner, MCL, with a Wednesday show, Excite, and a Sunday Drop, and pay-per-views such as Full of Gold, Raid of Hell and Exciter of the Ring II, played for World, American, Outlaw and Tag Team titles; MCL brought it back to PTC for a second run in September 2003. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

EPW, Excite Pro Wrestling, is on Primetime Central from late January 2002, when one of its wrestlers signed up for PTC's Global Title Contenders Tourney, a rumour credited to EPW was posted on 2 February, and its ratings for the week of 28 January stood at 3.04. By April it was posting its cards to PTC's news engine, signed "Source: Excite Pro Wrestling", with a pay-per-view, Full of Gold, announced on 21 April 2002 with its High Velocity, Revolution and World titles on the line. Its owner posted as MCL, and its weekly show was Wednesday Excite, with a Sunday Drop beside it: the card for 28 August 2002 was billed live from the Palace of Auburn Hills, and the Excite of 11 September 2002 was to be given over to a tribute from the whole roster, ending in fireworks and a flag across the roof of Madison Square Garden.

Through autumn 2002 its news told of staff changes (a president, a vice president and a commissioner named on 23 September), of more than fourteen signings in a few weeks, of Raid of Hell, Hearts of Death and Up The Ladder in Vancouver, and of the first EPW American Champion on 1 October. Its card of 25 January 2003 for Exciter of the Ring II set the Raw Core, American and Outlaw titles and a loser-leaves-town match for the World Title. Its last profiles here, of Dwight Maverick on 30 January and Time Bomb on 2 February 2003, were posted to PTC under its ~EPW~ banner.

Its owner came back to PTC on 21 September 2003, about seven months later, with EPW coming right behind him for what he called its second run there. Saying he owned the place, he recruited free agents on PTC's forum for its Freewebs site, and his last advertisement held, of 26 February 2004, sought a president, a vice president, a commissioner and match writers for an opening that March.

Primetime Central (PTC)

Member of EPW (Excite, 2002) (2002–2003)
Primetime Central's own front page of 27 January 2002 lists a wrestler of EPW among the entrants to PTC's Global Title Contenders Tourney and its ratings for 28 January to 3 February 2002 list EPW; from April 2002 the fed's cards and bulletins are posted to PTC's news engine under "Source: Excite Pro Wrestling", and its profiles of January and February 2003 are posted there under the ~EPW~ banner. PTC's channel line-up in a later capture lists it as Channel 7, Featuring EPW.
