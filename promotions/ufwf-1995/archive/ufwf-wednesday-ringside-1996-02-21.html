[UFWF] Wednesday Night Ringside

INTERVIEWS & NEWS FOR 02/21/96: [Camera opens on the UFWF Wednesday Ringside set.] [MG] Welcome again everyone to Wednesday Night Ringside, I'm Gene Okerlund. Some big news to pass along to all of you this week, the first being some interviews sent in to us that Jesse Ventura now proudly presents to you.
[JV] You done hogging the spotlight yet bald headed pigmy? [MG] Well... [JV] Good. 'Dats right folks.. 'da body in 'da house again! Lets get down to business with some words from the Coach.. leading his Mafia to the top in the UFWF!
{Scene opens in a luxurious house where Lex Luger and Buddy Landell are seated with Coach} Coach:Welcome everyone to my humble home. Did everyone like the job my men did on the Hart family?? What a bunch of punks. I have many plans for my men in the future but for now they're just looking for matches so I'll let them talk for themselves.
Lex Luger:Oh how I have waited for the day to get my first match and who do I want it with?? Let me think, how about my old friend Sting. I have no problem with kicking his butt all over the ring, especially since we both always knew that I was a better wrestler than him. Well I'll clear that up when we meet on Saturday. Buddy, you wanted to talk??
Buddy Landell:Oh yes. Ric Flair, you think that you're the true Natureboy, well I've got news for you, there's on one Nature Boy and that's me. I want you on Saturday to prove to the world that there is no one that can compete with the great one Buddy Landell.
Coach:The Mafia just keeps getting stronger and stronger and you can bet that we'll soon have gold around these wrestlers waists. By the way, is it true
Jim Ross got a STD from a guy?? Just curious, maybe Bobby can answer that for me. Till next time, this is Coach saying BYE!!!!!!!!
{Screen fades to black} [JV] The Mafia kicking asses and taking names upon their entrance into the UFWF. Now.. Todd Pettengill with a somewhat insider view on a new situation developing here in the UFWF regarding the Kings Court.
(scene cuts to Todd Pettingill who's standing outside the Royal Mansion.) Pettingill: Hello fans. I'm standing out front of the Harley Race's Royal Mansion, home of the Royal Court. I'm going to try and get a word with Mr. Race. (Pettingill rings the bell and Brother Love opens the door.) Pettingill: Brother Love, what are you doing here? Brother Love: I LOVE YOU...and what I'm doing here is none of your business. Pettingill: Well, is Harley here? (The Hulkster comes to the door.) Hulk: What are you doin here, dude? We didn't invite you. Pettingill: I'm here to talk to the Court. You guys have been awefully quite since things first started falling apart.
Hulk: Falling apart, brother? We have the UFWF TV champion Shawn Micheals,
Sabu and Cactus Jack. Does that sound like a stable falling apart?
Pettingill: And of course you have thrown your bandana into the ring. Hulk: That's right, brother. The dark power of Hulkamania is running wild. Its only a matter of time before the UFWF Title is around my waist, dude. And what is the UFWF gonna do when the King's Court runs wild on them? We're declaring war. Stay out of our way or get steam rolled. Now get out of my face.
[JV] Now upward and onward with some words from Ted Dibiase, and Devistation
Incorperated. Okerlund: Right now, I'm standing here with Devastation Inc. Fellas, it looks like one of your members is not present. Where's 911?
Dibiase: We aren't here to talk about that. UFWF officials, you have jipped us by taking the World Title away from Vader when he didn't lose the match in the first place.
Vader: Giant, haven't you learned to not mess with Vader?? Haven't you learned that I feel no pain? Giant, at UFWF Superbrawl I will show what time it is.. It's Vadertime! Don't forget it!
Okerlund: Mr. Dibiase, you also have two members of your stable who are going to be in that battle royal for the World Title.
Dibiase: That's right, and we are going to bring the UFWF World Title back to Devastation Inc. whether it's by Terry Funk or Psycho Sid.
Funk: Who wants to get in the ring with the toughest brawler there is in the UFWF? Between Sid and myself, we will take you OUT!
Sid: At UFWF Superbrawl, everyone will see who the Master is... I am the Master and at UFWF Superbrawl... Devastation Inc. will show the world that we are the Masters of the UFWF.
Okerlund: Legion of Doom Road Warrior Hawk and Animal, do you have anything to say this week?
Animal: YOU KNOW SOMETHING MEAN GENE! There have been a lot of punk tag teams going around here thinking that they're the best. Benoit,
Malenko... We challenge you to a match for those tag team belts! Show us what you got! Tell'em about it Hawk. Hawk: Ohhhhhhhhhhhhhh, what a ruuuuuuuuuuuuush! We've been going around here beating these second rate tag teams to the ground and we are tired of it. Dean Malenko and Chris Benoit, quit messing with those punks named Harlem Heat. Challenge a real tag team! Take us on in a match!
[JV] Dats all from me for this week.. back to you Jack! [MG] Thats Gene. [JV] What the f*ck ever! [MG] <ahem> Well.. Tony Schiavone, what do you have for us this week regarding the UFWF's next blockbuster ppv.. SuperBrawl!?
[TS] Well Gene, this week, the announcement of the brackets for the World Title tournament.. lets take a look. [Graphics pop up on the screen] The Undertaker Psycho Sid ------- 2 Cold Scorpio ------- The Crow ------- Sting ------- Lex Luger ------- Brian Pillman ------- Kensuke Sasaki ------- Diesel -------World Champion! Hulk Hogan ------- Masa Chono ------- Arn Anderson ------- Rick Rude ------- Terry Funk ------- Bret Hart ------- Meng ------- [TS] What a line up! What a night it is going to be! Plus.. don't forget.. Big Van Vader takes on The Giant, winner gets a World Title shot! Jr. Heavyweight Champion, Sabu versus former champion, Owen Hart! I-C Champion, Shane Douglas defends against former champ Tommy Dreamer in a steel cage match.. only pinfall and submission can get you a win in this one! But the cage wars don't end there, as T.V. Champ,
Shawn Michaels defends against Steve Austin in an "I Quit" cage match! And last, the tag team titles are up for grabs, as the current champions, Chris Benoit and Dean Malenko defend against the winners of this Saturdays tag match between Flair/Warrior and P.E.!
Once again, it all happens this Sunday, from the Tokyo Dome in Tokyo Japan.. you don't want to miss this one! [MG] Thank-you Tony.. looks like that is all the time we have this week.. [DH] Hey.. what about 'ol Dok Hendrix? [MG] Do you have any news for us this week Dok? [DK] Nah.. just wanted to get on camera once. [MG] For Jesse Ventura, Tony Schiavone, and Dok Hendrix, I'm Gene Okerlund saying.. see you next week! [UFWF theme music plays as the voice of Jim Ross interrupts: "This week on Slam Masters!!": Flair/Warrior -vs- Public Enemy T.V. title: Shawn Michaels -vs- Mr. Perfect Jr. title: Sabu -vs- Paul Diamond debute of Buddy Landell. ****************************************************************************** UFWF- ULTIMATE FANTASY...ULTIMATE WRESTLING...ULTIMATE FEDERATION!!! ****************************************************************************** --Wes.
