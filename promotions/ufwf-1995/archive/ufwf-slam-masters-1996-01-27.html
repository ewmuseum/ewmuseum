(Quick) RESULTS FOR 01/27/96: [Scene opens on the UFWF arena in New York. The fans go nuts as the UFWF theme music starts up again and fire works go off above the ring.]
[JR] Welcome everyone to another addition of Slam Masters! This Tuesday, not far from here in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.. the UFWF presents their 5th ever Pay-per-view event.. Blood, Sweat, and Fears!!!
[BH] What a night it is going to be! I have never seen a card loaded with this much potential for blood in my life!
[JR] Indeed, blood, spit, and puke is going to fly.. kids go get your parents and get them to order this one for you.. you ain't going to want to miss this one! Now, let's get right into things with a UFWF press confrence held earlier today with Tommy Dreamer!
(Tommy Dreamer is in a crowded room full of reporters He has called a press cofrence like most UFWF Superstars do)
Tommy: I have called this Press confrence to give everyone in UFWF a little bit of info but that can wait! First your questions.....
Joey Styles: I have know you for a long time Tommy but at BS&F you face the one and only Franchise Shane Douglas This could be your toughest match
Tommy: Well Joey I'm not afraid of Shane Douglas. And I got the upper hand by deciding the stipulation. Franchise may the best man win!
Vince McMahon: Why did you fire Woman?? Tommy: Two reasons Vince! One She Doesnt *bleep* that well and her husband is just a freak!
Joey Styles: OH MY GAWD! You cant say that Tommy! Tommy: Oh yeah Your right! Oh *bleep*in well! Jim Ross: Tommy you had quite a slobberknocker with Sabu, Yet you and the Royal Court get along rather well Tommy: The Past is Past! Tommy: No more questions! Now for my annoucement! We have decided to........ (Lost Audio contanct)
(we get audio back to hear just one thing) Joey Styles: OH MY GAWD!!!! [BH] What was it? For the love of god Ross what was it?!? [JR] It is simply unbeliveable.. Tommy Dreamer said that... (audio lost) (audio comes back on) [BH] My lord! [JR] Indeed. (unaware of the loss of audio) Remember fans.. you heard it here first.. now down to our first match of the evening.. Diesel takes on The Great Muta!!
1. "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel (356 lbs. Detroit, MI) w/ Ricky Steamboat -vs- The Great Muta (245 lbs. Japan) w/ "King" Harley Race & Court Jester --Diesel and Steamboat entered the ring first, Muta was announced, all eye's went to the curtain at the end of the aisle.. no one. Muta was announced once again.. no one.. no Muta, no Race, no Jester. Muta was announced one last time before the fans began to boo and Diesel left from the ring along with Steamboat.
[JR] What is going on here.. did Race back Muta out of the fight? [BH] Trust me Ross, neither Race or Muta would ever back out of a match.. [JR] Wait! I just got word that Mean Gene is standing by in the locker room area.. he has a hold of Harley Race.. Gene take it away!
(scene cuts back to Mean Gene who's standing outside the locker room. Behind the dressing room door Harley Race can be heard screaming at Muta.)
Mean Gene: Well fans, it sounds to me as though things are getting worse for the Royal Court as we go. I'm going to try and get a word with Harley Race about the recent defection of the Steiners, as well as the fact that
Muta has no-showed in his match with Big Daddy Cool. (Gene knocks on the door. Harley starts screaming at the door telling whoevers there to go away. Gene knocks again and the Court Jester throws open the door, almost taking it off the hinges.)
Jester: Listen brother, this is a closed door meeting. Now go away, dude. Mean Gene: Now wait just a minute. The fans have a right to know whats going on with the Royal Court.
Jester: Don't you worry about it, Mean Gene. The Court is as strong as ever, brother. We will be the leading force of the UFWF into 1996, and what are you gonna do, when the Royal Court runs wild on you.
Mean Gene: Hey, that sounds somehow familiar...can't put my finger on it. (as Gene ponders this Muta storms out of the locker room and right by the Jester and Mean Gene. Gene's concentration is broken so he just drops it.)
Mean Gene: Hey Muta. Muta: Stinky Americans, go back home where I respected. Gene: Muta!!! Muta!!! What's going on here Court Jester. Jester: Listen brother. When the reigning monarch of the UFWF decides to let you in, then he will, brother.
Mean Gene: What does the Undertaker have to do with this? Jester: I'm talking about Harley Race, little man. And you best watch out or maybe the power of the Court Jester will come raining down on you. I'm outta here, got some work to do.
Mean Gene: Well fans, hopefully we'll be able to get more on this in the upcoming weeks. Let's get you back to the action.
[JR] Looks like we won't be seeing Muta in action tonight.. or possibly even ANY night! The Court having a major falling out as of late.
[BH] Don't worry Ross.. Race has plans.. this is all part of a very complex plan. [JR] I'm sure it is Bobby, more than likely, Race has now plan, and his whole world is crumbling around him!
[BH] Ross.. watch yourself, the Court is still strong enough to bury you six feet under, with Michaels, Jack, and Sabu alone!!
[JR] You just may be right on that one Bobby. I believe were set for our next match.. let's take it back to Howard Finkel once again.
2. Eddy Guerrero (231 lbs. El Paso, TX) w/ Missy Hyatt -vs- The Crow (212 lbs. Parts Unknown) w/ Paul Bearer --An excellent arieal display from both men, both starting off strong with a good series of moves from both men. Highlights included a second rope DDT from Guerrero which scored a 2 count, a top rope frankenstiener from The Crow which also scored a 2 count, and a jacknife splash by Guerrero which missed allowing The Crow to sommersault splash Guerrero from the top rope for a pin.. 1... 2... 3.
Winner: The Crow, by pinfall. Time: 12:13 [JR] What a match! These two men really know how to work in the ring.. The Crow just edged out Guerrero on this encounter. [BH] I still say I could take any of these scrawny little lightweights any day of the week.
[JR] Of course you could Bobby. No scrawny guys in this next match.. all of them full fledged heavyweights.. let's get down to the ring once again. 3. World Champion: The Undertaker (321 lbs. Death Valley, NV) & Abdullah the Butcher (360 lbs. Sudan) w/ Paul Bearer -vs- Terry Funk (247 lbs. Amarillo, TX) & Psycho Sid (318 lbs. West Memphis, AR) w/ Ted Dibiase --Some big time brawling in this one.. but neither Sid or Funk managed to do much damage to the Undertaker. In a prelude to their match at BS&F's.. Abdullah and Funk brawled it out all the way around the arena, while UT put away Sid with a tombstone. 1... 2... 3.
Winners: Undertaker/Abdullah, by pinfall. Time: 8:43 [JR] The Undertaker is simply invinceable! [BH] Except to one force.. Vader! [JR] You may just be true.. I can't wait to see that match.. Thunderdome Casket Match.. coming to you at Blood, Sweat, and Fears!! [BH] I can't wait to see Abdullah and Funk's match.. pinfalls count anywhere, from the old ECW arena.. that one will be a classic for sure!
[JR] We have another classic coming up right here tonight.. to more lightweights square off in the ring as Psicosis makes his debute against the Modern day Kamikazee!
4. Psicosis (200 lbs. Tijuana, Mexico) w/ Paul E. Dangerously -vs- Hakushi (231 lbs. Tokyo, Japan) [Pre-match comments from Psicosis] (PSICOSIS is standing in the UFWF interview area, he is accompanied by a Spanish interiputer. The voice of the interiputer can be heard.) PSICOSIS: "For those of you who do not know me, I am Psicosis. I brought here to the UFWF by my NEW manager, Mr. Paul E. Dangersouly. Before I can to the UFWF, I used to wrestle in the AAA lucha libre promotion in Mexico. There I established myself as being the best Mexican wrestler ever and one of the best light heavyweight in the sport today. I have had many great matches over the years, but my matches came against Rey Misterio Jr. Yes my old friend, we are once again on opposing sides. I had hope that we, one day, could quit fighting each other and become friends. But friend this is impossible because it is our destiny to be on opposing sides and battle to the death so we can finally resolve how is the best light heavyweight in professional wrestling. But before we can continue our lifelong battle, I must defeat Hakushi tonight. Don't get me wrong Hakushi, you are a great aerial wrestler, but no one can match aerial skills with Mexican wrestlers. I hope that we can have a good match, but
I doubt it. After I get finished with you Hakushi, I'm coming after you again Rey Jr. Good-bye my friend, I hope to meet you in the ring very soon."
(The camera fades out...) --Psicosis made his debute with a huge bang.. entering the ring and tearing Hakushi to pieces. Hakushi fought back with a few moves of his own like the handspring elbow.. but little could be done to thwart the attack of Psicosis. Psicosis gained his first win in the UFWF with a tombstone piledriver that would put the Undertaker to shame. 1.. 2.. 3.
Winner: Psicosis Time: 6:27 [JR] An excellent entrance made to the UFWF by Psicosis, as he joins Kensuke Sasaki and Masa Hiro Chono in reforming the Dangerous Alliance. [BH] Not bigger.. but badder then ever! And I mean bad in a good way.. right.. do the kids still say that? [JR] Sure.. I guess so. We are just moments away from the main event, but right now we have Shawn Michaels standing by with comments on the upcoming match tonight against Dustin Rhodes and his match at
BS&F's against Steve Austin. Let's go to Mene Gene once again. (scene cuts back to Mean Gene who's standing by with Shawn Micheals. There is no Harley Race or Court Jester to be seen.) Mean Gene: Alright, thanks guys. Joining me now is the current UFWF TV Champ, Shawn Micheals. And with all that's been going on around you lately, you must be feeling a little perturbed.
Shawn: Well, well, well. It seems that everybody wants a shot at the ole HB
Kid. That's right, your Television champion, and who may I ask, better to examplify the UFWF, then it's main attraction, Shawn Micheals. Steve Austin thinks he deserves another title shot. That's alright with me, Mean Gene, just another succesful defense for me on my way to the gold.
Mean Gene: Actually I was talking about the happenings of the Royal Court. I mean, give me a break, forst the Steiners walk out and now this whole thing with Muta. Is it possible the Kingdom is crumbling.
Shawn: Crumbling? Gene Okerlund look down around my waist. You see the way that gold shines? Does that look like it comes from a crumbling court?
Noooo. What it looks like is the makings of the greatest champion of all time. The Heartbreak Kid, Shawn Micheals. The man that will tonight go on to his thrid, that's right count em, third succesful defense against Dustin Rhodes.
Mean Gene: I've heard he has a thing for gold. Shawn: It doesn't matter. Dustin will meet the same fate Steve Austin met last week. And will meet again at Blood Sweat and Fears.
Gene: Speaking of that, you still have to announce your stipulation for that match. Shawn: I'll be making an announcement during a very special news conference this week as to the stipulation I've chosen. Now if you'll excuse me, I have to go beat up the son of the son of a plumber. Good gawd, I'm outta here.
Mean Gene: Well fans be sure to tune in this Wednesday for that special announcement. And hopefully an update as to the rest of the happenings with the Royal Court. Let's get you back to the ring.
[JR] Dustin Rhodes already in the ring, and Shawn about to make his way to ringside any second.. I wonder what is up with the Kings Court.
[BH] Nothing Ross! How many times do I have to stress that point? Everything is just fine with the Court! [JR] For some reason.. I don't get that same feeling. 5. UFWF T.V. Title Match Champion: "Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (234 lbs. San Antonio, TX) w/ "King" Harley Race & The Court Jester -vs- Challenger: "The Natural" Dustin Rhodes (254 lbs. Austin, TX) w/ Ricky Steamboat & Diesel --The match started out as a good technical display, but soon began to decay as both The Court Jester and Diesel interfered. Eventually the two big men locked it up outside the ring with Diesel planting the black glove to the face of the Jester and knocking him cold. Cactus Jack then shot to the ring with a coal minors glove on and proceeded to brawl all the way back to the locker rooms.. cutting Diesel on the forehead in the process. Dustin Rhodes, who at the time was in charge of the match, was distracted by all of this, when he turned to face Shawn again..
some chin music put him to the canvas for .. 1... 2... 3. Winner: Shawn Michaels, by pinfall. Time: 11:55 [JR] Michaels with another successufl defence here on Slam Masters, that much closer to becoming number one contender to the title of his choice!
[BH] The T.V. title certainly is the stepping stone to fame here in the UFWF! [JR] It very well could be Shawn's.. but imagine loseing a match after all of the defences.. in other words, losing the T.V. title and having to start all over again! That would be simply crushing!
[BH] It could happen at Blood, Sweat, and Fears! [JR] It certainly could, but it is also on opprotunity for Shawn to pick up some more defences as each pinfall on Austin will count as a defence.
[BH] Now THAT is bogus! [JR] Actually, it is quite fair. Were all out of time this week folks.. I.. wait a minute.. it's Meng again.. he was out here last week.. what does he want this time? Meng: I may have been wrong in my estimations on Bret Hart, he's not a dumb American, he's a DUMB CANADIAN!!!! Either way, both countries have horrible wrestlers and I'd be more than happy to wrestle Bret Hart, just name the time and place.
(Meng throws down mic and notices Stu Hart in the front row of the crowd. He walks over to Stu and yanks him over the railing and throws him into the ring)
Meng: Now it's either you or your dad. You make the choice but no matter what, I'm taking out one of you today.
[JR] Oh my god.. what is Meng doing? Here comes Bret Hart blazing from the locker rooms.. and.. oh! Lex Luger nailing him from behind with a steel chair! How low.. what a set up! We are simply out of time folks, we must go.. see you at Blood Sweat and Fears!!
****************************************************************************** --Wes (Fed-Head, Head Booker, Head Match Writer) *NOTE: Blood, Sweat, and Fears will be delayed but will be out sometime this week.*
