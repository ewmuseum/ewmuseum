-[name withheld] Rules and Regulations 1. There is no line minimum. If I see that you can roleplay with 40 lines thats great your in. If you have roleplays that are 200 lines thats great your in. Remember it's quality not quantity! You can roleplay 4 crappy times and your opponent can rp 1 great entertaining rp and he'd win.
2. In this federation you send all you roleplays to the whole federation. I will send out e-mailing list so all you will have to do it copy & paste. 3. No TOSing, No Hacking, No Spamming ! That is one of the main rules. If you TOS somebody, that shows immaturity and childish and you will be terminated. Please just stay away from this.
4. If you can't roleplay for your match thats ok I don't mind. But please remember to IM me or E-mail me telling me when you can't roleplay. 4. If you lose a match up, please don't complain to me. It is always hard to judge a match when there is good rps between the two guys. If you have a problem with anybody in the fed, contact me.
6. There are the basic rules, and please, HAVE FUN! That is what federations are ran for. Roster Information Well, as you probably know, there is no members as of yet. That is why I am sending this information sheet out now. When I get around 20-25 members, then the fed will officially start up.

________________________Pay Per Views________________________ PWA Presents Thunderdome Live June 24th - This will be the first PWA Pay-Per-View! The main event will feature a 20 foot tall dome cage. Bolts of electricity will run through it. Mini-Land Mines will be placed on it. 6 Men will try to reach the top and grab the World Title!
PWA Presents Riddle Box 3 Live July 22nd - This is the second card of PWA! All titles will be on the line, and the main event will be a special triple cage match. Where you climb from one cage, to the next, then to the top to grab whatever title is hanging above!
Title Information PWA World Heavyweight Title - This is the highest title in the PWA Once you grab this title, you will go down in the record books and you control things around here, so to speak. This will be defended at a few Massacres and mostly every Pay Per View !
North American Title - Second highest title in the PWA and you are just a step away from getting the World Title. This title will be defended at every other
Massacre and will be defended at ever Pay Per View. If you hold this belt for
3 straight weeks, you got a shot at the World Title !
PWA Extreme Title - This is the title for the people who just don't give a damn about there body and love dishing out pain. This title will be defended in the most Massacre matches you may ever see !
PWA European Title - A lot of high impact action will be in this title. The man that has this title is a champion that still needs a little help. But hell a title is a title in my view. It will be defended at every Massacre and Pay
Per View !
PWA Tag Team Title - This is the title that two men hold on to. They fight at every Massacre matches and pay-per-views. There aren't alot of Tag Teams these days but the ones there are really make wrestling what it is today.
