Based in the Mid-South region of the US, VRW tours the states of Tennessee,
Arkansas, Mississippi, Georgia, Alabama, and maybe even Florida. All cards take place at some hot spot for instance, night clubs, bars, in the parking lot of a used car dealership, etc. With tradition thrown out the window, VRW takes a _hardcore_ approach, basically saying _anything goes_. If you think you have a set of big ones to compete in VRW, well come on down.
1.1 VRW HISTORY ---------------------------- Cody Walters, a member of the multi-millionaire club of Crye-Leike Real Estate has been a fan of pro-wrestling, especially when it would come to his home of Memphis, TN. He was looking at 40 acres of land that was for sale on Winchester and Germantown Rd, and realized that he could do something with that land. Bring in some wrestling personalities that everyone knows, and showcase their talent.
At the time, Walters was a fan of many Japan, garbage deathmatch style of wrestling, and he wanted to bring that style to the US. So Cody Walters went to the Union Planters Bank to get a loan for 15.5 million dollars. The loan was granted. So since being in construction since November of 1998, has been VRW's facility, the Junk Yard.
Walters has vowed to build VRW to a Mid-South mainstay, even if the profit skyrockets which could produce it to the national market. Here is
VRW. 1.2 MEET THE FACES ---------------------------------- Here are some personalities that you will be introduced with in the upcoming weeks on VRW broadcasts.
Cody Walters: Promoter/Founder of VRW. The big cheese of the organization, the man who signs the talent into Violence Reigns Wrestling.
Jimmy Lee Duncan: VRW Commissioner, the one who signs the matches for the card, suspends wrestler for things that go BEYOND the hard-core style of VRW, etc.
J.T. Riley: VRW's head commentator. The mouth of VRW. Behind the faces, a do gooder, and the one to call it right down the middle.
Kenny Myers: VRW's heel commentator. Normally drunk, Kenny Myers says what's on his mind, even if it's politically incorrect. He also loves to flirt with the women on hand at the card.
Jason Irving: The VRW's ring announcer. A small mexican man who really likes to enunciate his words. He is famous for stretching his words.
1.3 VRW Telecasts ----------------------------- The VRW is aired two times a week on the W-Net, Wrestling Network. One show is the telecast in which J.T. Riley previews "A Night at the Club" for that week. This show is called "Did You Hear the News?" Also in this show, the wrestlers, with their pre-recorded messages, state their views on their upcoming match at "A Night at the Club." "Did You Hear the News?" aires on Friday Nights.
"A Night at the Club" is the wrestling show aired weekly on Saturday Nights. "A Night at the Club" usually takes place inside of night clubs, bars, and parking lots. The wrestler's like this concept because after their match they can sit back, have a drink, and mingle with the crowd.
1.4 VRW Champions ------------------------------- In the VRW there are currently four titles. One will be on hiatus until a later time, but that will be explained below.
VRW Heavyweight Championship - The top belt in the VRW. If you have this, you are the top dog in VRW. This title must be defended at least once a month, and always defended at a pay per view. If this is not met, you are stripped of the belt.
VRW Violent Champion - This is the top hardcore belt in the VRW. You must defend this belt twice a month in any type of a stipulation match.
Whether it be as simple as falls count anywhere, or electrified barbed wire cage matches. The belts must be defended at every pay per view.
VRW Featherweight Champion - This is the top belt for wrestler 230 pounds and under. This belt must be defended twice a month and at every pay per view.
VRW Tag Team Champions - The tag team belts are on hiatus until the tag team division is alive, and strong.
1.5 Tournaments ------------------------- The VRW has decided that the only fair way to determine the title holders for the start of the VRW era is to hold a tournament for each title. The title tournaments will start on "A Night at the Club." and end at the first pay per view entitled, "Bar Room Brawl 99."
1.6 Super-Cards/Special Events ----------------------------------------------- Super-Cards are just like pay per views, but with one exception, they are not called pay per views. The first Super-Card of the VRW will be called
"Bar Room Brawl 99," It will take place in the "Hydrogen" night club in Ft. Lauderdale, Florida. Super-Cards and pay per views aire once a month and are on pay per view channels for $19.95, for three non-stop hard-core action. Always live and always guaranteed to bring you some of the most extreme action ever seen.

Just listen up and follow the rules and you will be all set.
Minimal Swearing - Things like ass, damn, hell, bitch, and jesus are aloud on the normal interviews for "Did You Hear the News?" If you have an in-ring for a pay per view it may be as vulgar as you want. If you use anything other than these words, please bleep them like so. ie. "You dumb mother f**ker."
Stay In-Character - I do not want people to have their wrestlers bringing up their opponent's handler, and things that happen in the RSPWF. It is that simple.
No Attacks - There will be _no_ attacks on other wrestlers, interviewees, announcers, or president's in your roleplays.
One Flash a Week - If you are scheduled for a match you must roleplay at least once, this way here, things will go along very smoothly.
