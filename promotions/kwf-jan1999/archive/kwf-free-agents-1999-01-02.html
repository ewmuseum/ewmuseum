Hello I am the co-founder and President of the KWF, King Wrestling
Federation. I hae posted on this board before and I have even attempted to sign a few free agents from this board to no avail.
Yet, once again I advertise my fed, the KWF, to be open to free agents and to anyone who wishes to be a part of a new era. For the KWF is Where the True Kings of the Ring Wrestle.
Our first Card, Tuesday Night Chaos, will be held on Jan. 5th,1999. Please feel free to view the league at the following link. http://www.members.tripod.com/~king_brothers/index.html Do you have it takes to bring a new league into the spotlight? [name withheld]
