Mega Pro Wrestling seeks card writers

28 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I am the president of Mega Pro Wrestling. MPW has been open for about a month, and has 20 wrestlers on the roster at the moment. Right now, there is only one weekly show, Saturday Night Inferno, and one PPV per month.
I am looking for one or two experienced card writers with HTML knowledge to write a couple or more matches per week. The job would also consist of RP judging, and columnist writing.
I am writing the shows myself at the moment, and I have two commentators.....Kirk Chaser(face), and the Bolivian Beast(heel). I would like for anyone interested to check out the MPW and some of the Inferno cards that have already been posted in order to get an idea of the match lengths and style I am looking for. Only look at the Inferno shows and/or the Triple Dropkick PPV, because all the older stuff like Mega Mania and
Wipeout were just "simulated cards" by Zeus. The MPW is "stand alone" fed.
We try to come up with innovative ideas and storylines. We don't need or want to be on any Top E-fed lists, because they all suck anyways. And they don't give an accurate measure of which feds are best.
http://www.angelfire.com/ky2/MPWarchives/MPWmain.html is the new addy for MPW. www.vonramrock.web.com was the old addy, but is now just a redirected link to the new addy. Thanks. MPW President - [name withheld]
