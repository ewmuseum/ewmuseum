How MPW works: the Rage and Anxiety divisions

20 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To all of you looking for a fed out there, MPW(Mega Pro Wrestling) has 2 spots open in it's Rage division. Let me explain how MPW works first though.
MPW has been open since June 1st, 2000. It has run well through the whole 6 months of existence, and continues running well. Up until mid-November, MPW had a weekly show called Rage held on Sundays, and every month a PPV with a pre-show before that. Then MPW was organized in to two divisions.
One division was the main division, Rage, for people who would wrestle on the weekly Rage show. Another division was formed called Anxiety, the minor league to Rage. Each Sunday before Rage, Anxiety would have a show, sort of as a warm up to Rage. This division helps rookies and people getting back in to e-wrestling work on their roleplaying to get them up to the Rage level.
It acts just as a real fed and has one title, the Anxiety title. On PPV
Sundays Anxiety would turn in to the PPV pre-show.
As of right now we have 2 spots open for the Rage division, and plenty of room in the Anxiety division. We currently have 25 people in the Rage division, with three moving up from Anxiety after the PPV next week. From there we will add two more if we get any new applicants. As for Anxiety, after the PPV we will have around 14 people, or more if more people join.
The MPW website is at http://www.geocities.com/megaprowrestling/ Check the site out, and if you think you would like to join, send in an application, found in the Member's Only section. All applicants will be accepted, two will be put in the Rage division, while the rest will go to
Anxiety until there is an opening to Rage.
Due to trying to keep the best competition in Rage, there will always be people moving up and down between divisions. If there is someone in Anxiety better than a person in Rage, then the person in Anxiety will be moved up, and the person in Rage will be moved down.
If you are new to e-wrestling and you are looking for a fed where you can get a good start and get help on roleplaying, then join MPW. So far Anxiety has taken several rookies from 1-4 line roleplays to quality 70+ line roleplays. And that is only with a month in the division.
If you are an experienced roleplayer who is looking for a fed then you can also apply to MPW, and if you are good enough you will be added to the Rage division.
Check out the fed and feel free to send in an application! Hope to hear from you soon! -- [name withheld] - Owner of MPW
