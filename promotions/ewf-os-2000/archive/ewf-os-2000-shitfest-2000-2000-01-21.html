EWF (OS): Shitfest 2000

21 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SHITFEST 2000!!! [Camera opens up to the EWF logo as "Pause 4 Porno" featuring Jake Steed starts to play. The logo fades and the camera shows the outside of the EWF Chicken
House. Inside, the floor is littered with feathers, corpses, and chicken shit.
Lillian Garcia stands in the ring with a megaphone as a good 15 or so fans look on.] Garcia: Ladies and gentlemen . . . Welcome to Shitfest 2000! [A couple of smart ass fans boo as the others are either asleep or don't give a shit.] Garcia: Our opening contest is scheduled for one fall. [Kurt Angle's theme starts to play as Kurt comes to the ring.] Syxx: This guy's a fucking faggot. Ross: I know, kid. But the question remains as to who his opponent is. [Kurt Angle takes the meg from Lillian Garcia as his music fades.] Kurt: You know, Kurt has been in this fucking sport for 4 months and Kurt is pissed off like a sex fiend with blue balls about it being so boring. I am here in the EWF for one reason. I am here to find some actual competition.
Who the fuck can beat me? Nobody! Nobody at all! ["War Machine" by Kiss starts to play.] Kurt: Oh shit! [Taz comes out of the soiled bed sheet (curtain) and is seen finishing a load of steroids and popping a few Andro caps.]
Syxx: Shit! Kurt is about to get fucked up bad! Ross: Syxx, light up some smoke and pass it around. Syxx: Aight. [Meanwhile, Taz enters the ring. Kurt Angle attacks Taz and Taz doesn't feel it. Taz reaches back and then comes forward with a powerful 100 mile per hour punch into Kurt's rib cage. Kurt starts to vomit blood.]
Ross: For some reason, kid, I am not going to go with Kurt on this one. Syxx: That fucker had it coming. [Taz T-Bones Kurt onto his neck and a snap is heard. Taz signals for the end. Taz plants Kurt on the top turnbuckle and follows him up there. Taz applies the Tazmission and hits a Tazmissionplex from the top rope to the concrete floor.]
Syxx: Holy fuck! Ross: My words, exactly. Syxx: It looks like Kurt has his first loss here. Ross: That does not come as a surprise to me for some reason. [Taz comes up and carves "Fuck You!" into his forehead with a razor blade. Taz pulls out a 6 inch blade and carves Kurt down his back. Taz starts laughing and walks to the back.]
Syxx: This man has definitely gotten a push. Ross: I'll second that. Syxx: Anyway, our next match should be good. Ross: That's right, kid. I figured that Ian had something up his sleeve. Let's go to the ring. Garcia: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first . . . ["Afternoon Delight" by the Starland Vocal Band starts to play as Tank Abbot and Ken Shamrock make their way to the ring.]
Garcia: Here are Tank Abbot and Ken Shamrock! [Disco Inferno's music starts to play as he and the Red Rooster come to the ring.] Syxx: What the fuck? Ross, this isn't what Ian had in mind. Ross: Ha! I pulled the wool over his eyes! [BANG!!!!!!!!!!!] Ross: . . . Syxx: Really? My .45 says differently. [The bell rings. Tank Abbot backs Disco into the corner and starts punching the shit out of him as Ken Shamrock rips Rooster's leg off and starts to beat him with it.]
Syxx: What a match. Shitfest 2000 is full of good shit! [Bobby Heenan sits down next to Syxx.] Syxx: Where the fuck did you come from? Brain: Well hello to you, too . . . Syxx: Shut the fuck up and watch the match. [Tank Abbot continues to punch Disco in the stomach. Disco is puking blood, but nobody seems to care. Ken Shamrock twists Rooster's head off and dropkicks it into the crowd.]
Brain: I remember getting an ass beating like this on a Sandwich Action. Syxx: Yeah, you probably took a good assfucking you piece of shit. Brain: You know, Syxx, you betterstart treating me with respect or else I'll leave. Syxx: Ooh . . . I'm scared now. You know, fuck you, you little bitch. You will stay here and fucking like it!
Brain: Says who? Syxx: [Raises his .45.] Says this! Brain: Okay, maybe I'll stick around for a little while. [One of the five light bulbs goes out.] Syxx: Fuckin' A! Brain: Dammit. See, kid. I told you that we should have run this show in a better building.
Syxx: Fuck you! Shut the fuck up and fix the fucking thing! Brain: Okay. [Bobby gets out of his seat. A boy brings him a stepping stool and a new light bulb.] Brain: Thanks, kid. [The boy passes by Syxx and Syxx calls him over. He whispers something to the boy and the boy runs off.]
Syxx: While the Brain is playing with himself, Tank and Ken are fucking shit up in the ring and wait . . .
[Jason Vorhees and Freddy Kruger run to the ring. Tank Abbot sees Jason and punches him in the face. Jason isn't phased by the punch. Jason runs the machete through Tank and punches his head off. Meanwhile, Freddy picks
Shamrock up with one hand and uses the other to slice him up.] Syxx: Fuckin' A! Tank and Shamrock are getting fucked up! [The boy returns with a bucket and gives it to Syxx. Syxx slaps the kid in the face and makes fun of his mom. Syxx steals the bucket, which looks to filled with water, and walks over to the Brain.]
Brain: Hey kid, can you give me some help? Syxx: Man, you need a lot of help. Brain: Shut up you fucking mark. Syxx: Hey, shithead, how many Weasels does it take to screw in a light bulb? Brain: Well . . . Syxx: Okay, how many fried weasels? [Syxx dumps the bucket of water on Bobby and Bobby gets electricuted.] Piece of shit . . .
[Syxx goes back to his seat.] Syxx: Now it's time for our next match. [A little kid is staring at Syxx.] Syxx: What the fuck are you looking at? [The kid runs away. It looks like a few more fans have entered the chicken house. Mike Tenay comes to the announce table.]
Tenay: Hey, kid. Syxx: Yeah, good. Our next match features my best friend, Fuckin' A Nash. Tenay: Kid, why do you feel that you have to swear so much? [Syxx pistolwhips him.] Tenay: OW! Garcia: The follwoing contest is scheduled for one fall . . . [nWo music starts to play as Fucking A Nash storms to the ring with an axe.] Tenay: Now why must Nash always have that weapon with him? Syxx: Say his name. Tenay: No. I don't swear. Syxx: [Repeatedly pistolwhipping Tenay.] Say the fucking name! You fucking asshole! [SUTN KOLD runs to the ring.] Garcia: Here is SUTN KOLD! [SUTN KOLD slides into the ring and Nash slams the axe in his face.] Tenay: Oh Christ! Syxx: No, not Christ . . . Nash. [Crazy Leon and Da Fucka runs to the ring and attack Nash. Nash throws both of them off, rips the axe out of SUTN KOLD, and slams it into the side of Da
Fucka's head. Crazy Leon leaps onto Nash. Nash takes Leon and Power bOmbs him on his head and through the ring.]
Tenay: Shit! Syxx: You got that right. Fucking A' Nash is awesome! [Mike Awesome's music starts to play as he and Judge Jeff Jones make thier way to the ring.]
Tenay: You said he was awesome. Syxx: No, he's better than awesome! [Jeff Jones sets up a table on the outside of the ring. Mike Awesome steps into the ring. Nash slams an axe into Mike Awesome's forehead and then power bombs him over the top rope and through the table. Jeff Jones looks on like he is watching a ghost.]
Syxx: Showed that fucker. Tenay: Unbelieveable! [Nash leaves the ring. He grabs Jeff Jones and his axe and brings them to the back.] Syxx: Our next match is something that we have . . . ["Boom Boom Boom Boom" by the Vengaboys starts to play as Fat Shit, Motherfucker, and Shitfucker make their way to the ring.] Garcia: Coming to the ring is the team of Motherfucking Fat Shitfuckers! Syxx: What the fuck are these guys doing out here, Tenay? [Fat Shit takes the mic.] Fat Shit: I'm a Fat Shit! Brood! You guys think you got the upper hand last time. This time, I have my lover and my adopted child, Shitfucker. We will fuck you up and eat you alive!
[Gangrel's music starts to play as he, Edge, Christian, and the Hardyz all come to the ring. Gangrel has the mic.]
Gangrel: Hey, Fat Shit! It's not my fault that nobody gives a flying fuck rather you live or die. As a matter of fact, you cans uck my cock because we are about to fuck you up bad!
[Fat Fuck and Yokozuna race to the ring. After about 10 minutes, their journey is over and they enter the ring.]
Yoko: I'm fat! Fat Fuck: I'm really fat! [The really fat guys all attack the Brood. Fat Shit and Gangrel start exchanging punches. Edge jumps at Motherfucker. Motherfucker power slams him hard. Shitfucker takes Christian and starts to eat him.]
Christian: Help! [The Hardyz attack Fat Fuck and Yoko. Fat Shit and Gangrel go to the outside. Fat Shit knocks Gangrel down and then hits him with the Shit Stain. Motherfucker brings Edge to the top rope and hits him with a power slam.] Syxx: Oh What a Motherfucker! Tenay: What?! [Shitfucker sits on Christian's face. Yokozuna and his 800 pound ass comes down onto Jeff Hardy with the Yoko Drop. Matt Hardy trips up and Fat Fuck rolls over him.]
Syxx: It looks like we know who won this one. Tenay: You think? ["WE'RE HARDCORE!" is yelled from the back. Fit Finley, Big Bossman, and Team
Barry (along with Clarence Mason) run to the ring. They start attacking everyone in the ring and it's a huge brawl.]
Syxx: Straight from EWF International . . . They are fucking shit up. Tenay: You got that right, kid. Hey, isn't that black dude Mr. Biggs . . . Syxx: You shut the fuck up! He's Clarence Mason! Tenay: Right. Syxx: I'm about to kill you. [The brawl goes on in the ring. Clarence Mason pulls out a canister of mutagen and consumes it. He holds a stick of dynamite and laughs. He turns into a huge stick of dynamite.]
Syxx: Oh fuck! He' going to blow! Tenay: Blow me, kid! [Clarence explodes and turns out to be a dud. The nWo ninja security raids the rings with swords and kills everyone in the ring. Lillian Garcia enters the ring.]
Garcia: This is horrible. This is just horrible! [Jordan Lee runs to the ring and spears Lillian Garcia, who apparently is not wearing any underwear.]
Tenay: Cat fight! Syxx: I am so hard right now. Tenay: I am, too. Syxx: Shut the fuck up you god damn faggot! [Ric Flair runs into the chicken house and sees Jim Ross' dead body from before and sees the hardcore title next to him. Flair covers Ross's body. 1-2-3.]
Syxx: There we go! Ric Flair is once again the Hardcore Champion. Tenay: What kind of shit is that? Syxx: That's fucking it. [Syxx takes Tenay by what's left of his hair and drags him out of the chicken house. Syxx walks by Taz and gets an idea.]
Syxx: Yo, Taz! Taz: [Looking back at Syxx with death in his eyes.] What do you want, brutha? Syxx: [Throws Tenay toward Taz.] Tenay's been making fun of your mother. Taz: What the fuck?! [Taz walks over to Tenay. Syxx goes back into the chicken house. As Syxx closes the door, you hear, "No, Taz! No! No! NOOOO!" then silence. Syxx shakes his head. HaRu comes by with Sandman and some weed.]
HaRu: Wanna go smoke? Syxx: Yeah, sure, why not. Sandman: What about the show? Syxx: Fuck it. It's about over anyway. HaRu: The show kinda sucked for a pay per view. Syxx: It's better than what the Big 3 have been producing lately. Sandman: This is true. Hell, I wasn't even on the last one. [Everyone laughs.] [Credits. Fade.] -[name withheld]
