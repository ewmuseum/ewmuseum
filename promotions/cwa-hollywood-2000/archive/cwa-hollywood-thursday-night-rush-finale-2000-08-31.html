<JackieDelShannon> Look at the balance Byron has! That's not something you learn, that's something you're *BORN* with! I may not like the man, but he's got skills!
{ Byron with a pin... 1... But Kazynski lifts the shoulder. Both men still down... Byron again with an armbar to lift them to their feet...} <ScottMuir> Tom's not kicking out with the usual umph. <JackieDelShannon> "Prime Time"'s got the right idea, tho - work a couple holds, hit an impact move, cover. Even in kicking out, Kaz is expending precious energy, and that's important at this late stage in the game!
