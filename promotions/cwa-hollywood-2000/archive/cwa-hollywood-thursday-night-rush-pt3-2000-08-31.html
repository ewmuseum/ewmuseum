<Riker> Actually... I hope you don't take it personally if I have to kick your butt too.
{smiling sweetly} Cause, I mean, you are one of my heroes, but only one of us can be champ, so let's go have fun while we can, and may the best
person win! {looks at Riker} Now...umm...I have to change...and although you are cute in that married man kinda way... {whispers} I can't do it with you here.
<Riker> I told you I was single but... nevermind. I'll just step outside. {the camera follows Riker out the door} Cute kid. I think she'll be fine. I just feel bad for Jimmy and Chris.
