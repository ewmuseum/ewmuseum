CWA Thursday Night Rush 08/31/00 Pt. II

7 September 2000

{ The arena goes black and red laser light Superman symbols as "Kryptonite" by Three Doors Down blasts over the PA system. The crowd isn't quite sure how to react until Riker Showtime power walks through the curtains. The fans start to cheer as
Riker pauses and looks over the crowd, then high five his way down the isle. When he gets to ringside, Riker slides under the bottom rope and quickly pop up with a mic in his hand. After a brief "Showtime" chant, the crowd quiets down.)
<TaliaYamahara> And we have a future CWA SUPERSTAR out right now in
Riker Showtime! <JackieDelShannon> And wouldn't ya know, it wouldn't be a wrestling federation without a Showtime in it!
<TaliaYamahara> Hey... we aim to please in the CWA! [Riker] I'm sure most of you people are familiar with the work of my brothers,
Roker and Rictor... (huge pop for the mention of the Showtime clan) (huge pop for the mention of the Showtime clan) [Riker] ... and I'm equally sure you're wondering why a Showtime is out here tonight, but not in the Hollywood Title Tournament. The reason is quite simple... I didn't feel I deserved to be in the tournament.
(A low murmur starts rolling through the crowd) <ScottMuir> Woo hoo! Nepotism is dead! [Riker] There are a lot of guys in the back who have made names for themselves in the States over the last few years. Guys like Chris
Hunter and Jimmy Rage deserve to be champ... and I'm not just saying that because they happen to be friends of mine. Heck...
Jeremy Byron and Sebastian Sloan have been busting their butts on American TV for a while, and they deserve a shot at wearing gold too. Me... I've been wrestling all over the world in order to become the best wrestler I can be... worthy of the name
Showtime.

[Riker] When I signed with the CWA, all I asked for was to be here tonight so that I could start from square one and work my way to the top of the CWA. So tonight... I'm making a challenge to anyone in the back
who wants the same thing I do... a chance to succeed. There's an open contract with my name on it and I'm willing to give anyone in the back who wants it, a good, clean match.

<TaliaYamahara> This doesn't bode well for Riker... at ALL. <JackieDelShannon> Somehow, I don't think Jahmonster is here to take
<ScottMuir> BOO! nepotism is alive again.

See, Riker... there's a couple of things you're not factoring in. One - the name "Showtime" means a bit too much on the marquee... even if it is only YOU... for me not to at least put you into my tournament. More importantly... there is that whole family feud thing, with your brother putting my pops in the hospital and all. Not that I'm mad,
'cause hey, I wish he could do it again. However, I still need to go home and deal with the aunts and uncles and such, so SOME kind of punishment is in order.
<TaliaYamahara> "Punshment"? Riker hasn't done a THING to Jamahn, or ANY
Chamberlin! <ScottMuir> Yeah, but he looks like a guy who did. [Jamahn] Hmmm... tell ya what. You like Chris Hunter and Jimmy Rage so much? I think you should wrestle against THEM in the tournament... and maybe you can all beat the snot out of each other for my amusement... oh, yeah, and the amusement of these paying fans. {smiles} What do you say?
[Jamahn] <JackieDelShannon> I did *NOT* just hear that! He wants Riker to face
Crash

Now get ready to make me some money! {"The Man Right Chea" keys back up again, as both make their way to the back...} <JackieDelShannon> OK, I'll bite. Who's Willow Moongarden? The name wasn't on these here prep sheets ya gave me....
<TaliaYamahara> I think we may have a chance to introduce her to everyone... I'm being told we have a videotape here, of "The Natural One" Willow

So I invited this nice guy who's holding the camera for a walk on the beach...I love the beach, there's a sense of peacefulness here that's amazing...well, there is when the local Hells Angels aren't having a barbecue 300 yards away from a group of transvestites getting some sun...not that there's ANYTHING wrong with that... I met Mistress Bunny the other day and had a GREAT talk with her about where to find some good deals on some of the cutest tiedye scarves...and her and Terrence make such a cute couple..what was that...talk about CWA. Right.
Anyway, I'm really glad to be here - they tell me they may not have too many other women right away for me to fight, and that's fine, cause I used to beat up my brothers and make them say "uncle" a lot as a teenager...and they were bigger too! And they had wrestling experience!! I felt important.
Anyways, I can hold my own with the guys...I'm a big girl! Literally! But you know, that's OK. Too many people place too much emphasis on body image, anyways, and there's enough model-types in wrestling anyways. So I have what my mom calls "peasant stock" - no big deal. Just more to lo... oh,
CWA. Sorry, my mind wonders.
Anyways, I'm coming soon, and I can't wait to meet all the fans and find out who I get to beat up first. Maybe after the first show some of the fans might want to tailgate. I make great hummus! {looks at cameraman}
Oh, right. So, anyways, I'm "The Natural One", Willow Moongarden, and you better be careful, or I'm gonna beat you up! {looks at cameraman} Was that
