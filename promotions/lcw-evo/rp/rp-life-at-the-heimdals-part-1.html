Life at the Heimdals Part 1

June 2002

LCW Evo - The Evolution Continues Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Entitled: Life at the Heimdals Part 1 Written: 6/02 Location: Liv Heimdal's House Note: Of course this happened many many weeks from the present date; just after Doc landed on Liv's porch steps. Sorry for the delay, folks. Now, take the journey with me...that's it. -------- We join David Chambers and Olivia Heimdal in her kitchen, the room which she had only recently (at the time of these events) dragged him through in his state of unconsciousness after three days of torture. The scene is considerably more pleasant now. David is almost completely physically recovered from the ordeal, having gotten much rest and foodstuffs in the care of Livvie and Nikki. Doing much better by this time, David's mind has cleared up enough for him to start to feel guilty. And concerned about certain other things as well. But let's watch, shall we? David: I really shouldn't stay here, Liv. It's not a good idea for anyone involved. Liv: Oh no? So where exactly were you PLANNING on staying? Gonna march back to the Asylum and say 'Ok, just let me get into my room; PLEASE don't tie me to a chair in the basement and make me watch home movies this time?' David smiled, but only slightly. He had told her everything that had happened in those last days in the Asylum right after regaining full consciousness. The memories were starting to become less distinct, less painful; he took Livvie's joke as best he could. He knew she meant no ill by it. David: It shouldn't matter where I go, Livvie. It should only matter that I'm not HERE. It's bad enough that there's bad blood between you and Frank and them. Putting me here...it'll attract them like flies to a pile of...well, a pile of something unpleasant, at any rate. Liv: Speaking of piles, that's a pretty big one that just came spilling out of your mouth, I'd say. It's not like they know you're here, is it? Unless they've got some sort of weird, super-secret spy satellite or something, and I'm pretty sure they haven't got that. Actually, I'm positive. They don't give those out to wrestlers. Only the commentators. Liv smiled again, and this time David smiled back fully. Her smile was infectious, and as he looked back at her he thought, and not for the first time, how easy it must have been for Lex to fall for her. David: Ok, fair enough, no super-spy satellites. But all the same, I'm not comfortable with it. Besides...I feel guilty just sitting around your house like this. You took me in; I passed out right on top of your door... Liv gave an overly-dramatic exasperated sigh. Liv: Didn't even ask if you could come in, either. Exhaustion makes you rude, David. David: Oh, hush up, you; I'm not finished yet. Liv: Hey, be nice! You said it yourself, 'I took you in; you passed out right on top of my door...' David chuckled. David: And then you said 'Didn't even ask if I could come in, either. Exhaustion makes me rude, Livvie.' And now look where we are. Anyway...I mean, I don't want to be a freeloader or anything like that. I'm not that kind of a guy. Livvie got up from her chair and went to the fridge. Liv: It's nice that you feel that way, Dave, it really is. But you haven't even been here a week yet. Why don't you give yourself a little more time? You're tired. You've been through the ringer. You need a break. Don't worry about it, okay? David remained silent. Olivia regarded him, noticing that he was still very uncomfortable. She pulled a can of Diet Coke out of the fridge, closed the door and walked around so she was behind David. Liv: I guess you could chip in with some of those big, fat, I'm-a-title-holding-professional-wrestler checks you get. I mean, if it really bothers you. Not that I need your stinkin' money anyway... David made a feigned hurt statement. David: What? My wrasslin' money's not good enough for you? Liv: Since you asked; no. No it's not. There was a brief but not uncomfortable pause. Still, this made Livvie feel a little guilty. She took a minor risk and invaded David's personal body space in the form of a little hug from behind. Liv: Dave...there's absolutely no reason in the world for you to not stay here. You're being silly. Mike and Aaron and their rat-bastard buddy...they're not going to find you here. Besides; they've got other stuff to worry about, don't they? What makes you think they're even actively looking? David: I got away, Liv. And they're already displeased with you. They might be planning on paying you a visit ANYWAY. Livvie sighed. Liv: Then let them. All the more reason for you to be here, no? To look after little ol' me? Livvie released her grip on The Doctor and took her seat at the kitchen table again. Livvie was joking, of course. But David would find himself bitterly and regretfully remembering this moment after Lex made his prediction come true days later. He had been out of the house when Duel arrived; one of the first times since he had arrived at Olivia's, in fact. His timing, he felt, couldn't have been worse. To say the least. At the moment, however, David only laughed. David: You've got Frank, don't you? ...and...that's something else in itself, isn't it? Liv: Why? What's wrong with Frank? David: Nothing! Don't get me wrong, Frank's a great guy. ...but... Liv: ...it's the thing with Dean, isn't it? David nodded. Things were not going well between him and Dean Wallace as of late; it was unfortunate, but it also couldn't be helped. And his association with Frank was something that had weighed on David's mind while he had been with Livvie. He hadn't encountered Frank at the house yet; David was more than a little nervous about doing so. Liv: Well, I don't think it's going to be that much of a problem. We both love Dean, but Frank ISN'T Dean, is he? You're not fighting with Frank. But...I guess we'll just wait and see on that one, huh? David remained maddeningly silent. Liv: Okay, listen. You stay here until you're done. If you're here any more than two weeks, you start paying 'rent' or doing whatever you can to make yourself feel better about being here. And when you're ready to leave, leave. But I want you to know... Livvie pushed out her lower lip and fluttered her eyes as if to blink back tears. Liv: (in an exaggerated act of being hurt) ...I'll be really sad when you go. Everyone else gets to see the The Bad-Ass Doctor, but I get to hang out with him while he's in his jammies reading the paper and taking naps on my couch. She smiled her infectious smile again, and David could only mirror it with his. The discussion wavered after that point, going on and off of a number of different topics. It wasn't the last time he'd express guilt and an uncomfortable feeling about staying with Livvie...but it was the last time his heart was really in it when he did so. The concern for her well-being was always there, but the desire to stay with her would win out every time. And not with much difficulty, either. -------- Stay tuned, kids.
