A Brief History of Time (featuring Lunacy)

LCW Evo: The Evolution of Lethal! Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Lost Entitled: A Brief History of Time (featuring Lunacy) Written: 01/11/2002 Location: The Asylum Lost, a man in the process of reinventing himself and his career, both willingly and subconsciously, it would appear, sits in front of the television set in the den of the Asylum. The TV shows footage from a match featuring Divine Intervention and Mike Static, namely when the then-Apostle lost the Tag Team Titles to same. In Lost's mind, this stands as an unavenged loss. Even though DI won the titles back, thank to some clever booking stipulations, they did not get the win over Mike Static. That's the way it goes in this business, thought Lost, always something there to monkey wrench the whole plan. Lost looks on intently, studying the tape for any and all mistakes, miscues and weaknesses that hdid notcover the first time around. Lunacy, sitting patiently by, chimes in. 'Why are you even bothering to watch this?' 'Studying. Forewarned is forearmed, or something. Besides, with this change of heart he seems to have had with Lex, he may be a different guy now, but he wouldn't change his offense that much.' Lunacy rubs his temples. He loves Aaron, a friend, a partner, a cousin, a brother. Whatever success he had in the past is thanks, in no small part, to this man-child. With Lost's support, it seemed as though the former Archangel could overcome the crippling injury to his vertebrae. A few short months ago, Lunacy was up and about with the help of a walker, and from time to time a cane. Little by little, the ever-logical Michael Gabrielle told himself, you must lierally take things step by step. He yearned for another shot in the ring, just one last match, he seemed to be on the road there, actually. But when his inspiration disappeared mysteriously, Lunacy appeared to have a relapse, putting him back at square one. As all these thoughts bombarded Lunacy's mind, he fought for conrol of his thoughts, some inner clarity. 'Aaron, I don't believe it for a second. The things he did to Lex go beyond forgiveness and redemption. He held a gun to the heads of two people whose only crime was to pick bad ime to walk through a park when two homicidal maniacs decided to take a stroll.' 'I'm not above violence. Matter of fact, I'm all for it. But Static has gone above and beyond, and objects that are in motion tend to stay in motion.' Lost continued to stare at the TV. Lunacy, sometimes, just didn't know how to communicate with him without being forceful. Given his cousin's recent bout of violent behavior, even this became a less-enticing proposition. Lunacy felt, though, that even in a blind rage, few men would assault someone in a wheelchair, especially knowing that said man is your cousin. So Lunacy approched Lost, leaning into his line to sight. Lost knew what was coming. It worked. Mike always knew how to give him that kick in the ass he needed from time to time, to focus him on where he needed to be. 'Aaron, knowing your enemy is all well and good, but this isn't a chess tournament. It will require endurance and strength to wn three matches in one night.' Lost stopped the tape. He knew intuitively that he had probably watched the last piece of footage for the next few days. 'Go to the gym.' Lunacy ordered. 'Go to the gym.' Lost parroted back, not in mocking, but in acceptance. *** Lunacy hits play on a tape recorder, and the stereo blasts 'Welcome To The Jungle' by Guns 'N' Roses. 'Haven't heard this song since I was about 7.' says Lost, a pleasent smile on his face. Lunacy says nothing. Silence is golden, he thought, and it can say alot. The message was clear to Lost, who immediately goes to the treadmill and built up to an almost full-out run. Then the dumbbells, low weight, high reps, tone muscle (Duel had taught him that one). On to crunches, then to push-ups. Aaron's high-school gym teacher was one of those old types too set in his ways to change with the times. He always liked to say hat former NFL running back Herschel Walker did nothing but sit-ups, push-ups, pull-ups and running. Herschel Walker was also built like a tank with legs. Lost didn't imagine he would ever get to that size, but there's nothing wrong with trying. Any training session run by Lunacy always ended with a Murder Set. 10 minutes, in the ring, alternating between running back and forth across the ring and dropping down for a quick set of 10 push-ups. Every time, without fail it made Lost's body feel completely spent, but in a productive manner. Same this time. With endorphins running high, Lost felt right again, as though nothing could get him down or break his focus. As far as Lunacy was concerned, tat was a mission accomplished. As Lost sat on the apron of The Asylum's makeshift ring, toweling himself off and chugging down water, Lunacy sat in front of him, staring, smiling. 'You've got two goals, Aaron,' said Lunacy, back to his authoritative tone. 'First is to win the tournament, that's the most important. No one can ignore you when you hold the gold.' 'Second, and this is key, regardless of hos far you get in the tournament, make the first show of evo, make the first Impulse of 2002 a night that will stand out as Lost's night.' Lost nodded to this, solemnly, as one might agree with a proverb from the Bible read by the pastor of your church. Lunacy left Lost to himself, and sitting on the apron of the ring, Lost closed is eyes, took one deep breath, opened his eyes and spiked his towel. 'It's my time now.'
