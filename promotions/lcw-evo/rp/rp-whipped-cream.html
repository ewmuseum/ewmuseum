Whipped Cream

22 January 2002

LCW Evo: The Evolution of Lethal! Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Silence Jake Julius Entitled: Whipped Cream Written: 01/22/2002 Location: Jake's House Impulse 1.3... The night that will change the life of 'Silence' Jake Julius. The night he has dreamed of. To know he will be there is one thing, but to preform there... That's a whole other ordeal among itself. In fades the camera to the front of a white, two story house. In front of it sit a green-colored Honda electric car, and a white convertible. We see the inside of the house, nicely decorated, and obviously very costly. We then cut to the living room, where in front of a big screen television are two black leather couches, a black leather chair, and a wooden table, all nicely placed in the room. On the black leather couch in front of the television sit Jake, and a beautiful blonde who's head leans on his shoulder. Jake is wearing a green turtleneck sweater, a black vest, and khaki pants. The woman is wearing a white tee-shirt and blue jeans. 'Aah, at Impulse this Wednesday, I will finally make it. I will finally have my dream come true,' Jake says with happiness. 'It's too bad Amy, that you wont be there to enjoy it with me.' Amy looks up at Jake. 'I know... But business is business,' Amy says as she reaches to kiss Jake on the cheek. Jake picks up an un-opened squirt can of whipped-cream. 'Yup... A glorious day. Nothing will ever top Wednesday for a long time,' Jake says as he opens the squirt can of whipped-cream. Amy sits up and looks at Jake in confusion. Jake takes the lid off, and squirts whipped-cream into his mouth. 'I'd uhh... Better be off now. My uhh... Flight leaves in an hour,' Amy says with a hint of fright in her voice as she walks out the door. 'But your flight doesn't leave until tomorrow morning!' Jake yells as the door closes. Jake squirts more whipped-cream into his mouth. 'Damnit. Everytime I do that, she leaves. What is wrong with whipped cream?' Jake questions into the empty house. Jake sets the whipped cream can on the table, and picks up a bag of shredded cheddar and mozarella cheese. He opens the bag, grabs a handful of cheese, and eats it. 'Mmm. This tastes good. But not as good as victory,' Jake says as he wipes his hands on a napkin. Jake leans forward towards the camera. 'You see Douglas, I have nothing against you. I mean, your a nice guy and all,' Jake says, 'But I'm afraid I will beat you. I mean, not in a bad way, but just... Aah heck. What do I mean 'not in a bad way?'' Jake questions towards the camera. Jake picks up the can of whipped-cream. Jake squirts the whipped-cream into his mouth. Suddenly, the top breaks and whipped-cream spills out onto Jake's mouth and shirt. 'NO! This is my best shirt!' Jake yells in anger as he throws the whipped-cream can onto the floor. 'Absolutely not! This is NOT happening!' Jake stands up and storms upstairs as the scene fades out. Only one man will come out on top in the debuting show... One man.
