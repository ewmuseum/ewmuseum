Amid certainty, controversy.

21 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo: The Evolution of Lethal! Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Rook Black Entitled: Amid certainty, controversy. Written: 1/21/02 Location: Ford Mustang GT 'I AM', interior. Rook merges into highway traffic. Shifting up, he speeds into the far left traffic lane and begins cruising. Rook: Something has come up. Rook stares ahead as he pilots the vehical around a Camaro. Rook: That's not alltogether unusual. Not really. Rook: My desires have a way of getting disrupted. You cannot always get what you want. But usually, you get what you deserve. Rook drives for a moment in silence. His eyes narrow down to slits. Then his expression narrowly shifts into anger, his sneer pulling back and his brow tightening. He takes a deep breath. Rook: Oh poisonous world, wrought with scorn and misery, I look upon thee with naught but mistrust and fury. I tread this narrow path, descending on the, irony of ironies, city of brotherly love. I come upon thee, approaching thy gates. All alone, a single solitary speck, whose struggles and desires are magnified ten thousand fold and displayed among the masses. Rook: I inspire both hatred and love. The hatred I embrace among the two for it is hatred that I am most familiar with. Her whispered words and cold hands are my most devoted company. Love, on the other hand, remains aloof. A stranger cast in shadow, often pursued by never caught. I've turned my eye away from her more times than I can recall, but yet in the corner of my vision her presence remains, indestinct, yet certainly a sentinel of false hope and dissapointment. Rook: I do not know her. I do not understand her. A closer inspection might reveal more of this love; however, as in many times before, I'm much more accustomed to discover that what appears as love in only something foul underneath a disguise. Rook: So I remain seething, unsoothed. And what is worse, is that I cannot ever forsee any circumstances that would arise to change this. Rook: I've taken a knife to this flesh of mine so that my interior and exterior make a sort of a match. And then later I can't help but look upon my action as indescribably overdramatic. Psycho freak in his psychodrama slits up his psycho face, ruining no beauty, achieving nothing of worth, and impressing all save his own psycho psyche. Rook drives for a bit. The snorts. Rook: Heh. Rook: As if this dramatic talk was more than b.s. itself. More driving. You get the idea. Suddenly, as an internal discussion in his head burst into sound. Rook: What I really want is to fight Doctor Chambers, but Docter Chambers doesn't seem to want to fight! And I don't want to fight the shell of the man caught up in his insecure worries over the nonsense around him, I want to fight the warrior that sleeps inside his stupid ass frame. Rook: Doctor Chambers. Yet another path of least resistence fellow. It's certainly easier to be nice to everybody than to challenge them. It's certainly easier to never try so one doesn't fail. It's certainly easier to tell someone 'you know what's best' and let sleeping dogs lie. Rook: Well, WAKE UP! Finish with your crap already! Let the stuffed animal GO! You haven't needed that crap for ages now, why is it lingering on! I grow so damn impatient waiting for him to shine. Oh well. Short of provoking him, I don't see him rising to meet me. Thanks a lot, Chambers, your mediocrity may please you, but it'll drive me to drink. Rook sighs. He notices that he'd exceeded 100mph during his rant, begging for police action. He slows. Rook runs his right hand over his hair. He flexes his fingers and crackes the knuckles in a fist. He isometrically flexes each finger in turn followed by his thumb. Rook: Something is wrong. Rook merges into the right lane in anticipation of his exit ramp. Rook: I don't know what. I'm consistenly recalling that tools can often become traps. Something is happening. Rook: You get what you deserve.
