Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Grfyon Entitled: Window to the Past part 3 (sort of) Written: 1/21/01 Location: Gryfon's Apartment Gryfon is asleep in bed, his face twisted into an expression of what seems to be fear. These flashes to the past have been destroying his grip on reality, and his control over himself. He can feel himself slowly spiraling back to what he used to be. What he hoped never to be again. But that hope was seemingly futile, as it appears to be only a matter of time before he will revert. A significantly younger Gryfon is standing in a poorly lit, shit hole of a hotel room in southern Los Angeles. Across from him, looking disdainfully at his meager surroundings, is a short and stocky bald man wearing a suit fit for a pimp, his light green clothing in direct contrast with the plain black shirt and jeans that Gryfon is wearing. The out of place man looks around the room, unconscious of the eyes teeming with hatred staring at him from a mere ten feet away. Gryfon’s eyes, seemingly engulfed in flames, are the only feature on Gryfon’s face showing any emotion at all. As the lips of the pimp looking man move, Gryfon’s eyes seem to get darker and, if possible, even more full of anger. Man: Heath, it’s me… your father. It takes a few seconds for Gryfon’s clustered mind to process what the man said, and when he does the emotion thus far contained to only the eyes explodes into his whole body and he lets out a cry of rage as he charges the man and thrusts his hand up to the man’s throat and grabs hold of it. The man struggles for air as his whole body comes up off the floor and is hurled through the air, crashing through a coffee table and onto the floor. The front door swings open and Shawn Merrit runs into the room and sprints over to Gryfon and attempts to calm him down. His failing efforts delay Gryfon just long enough for Gryfon’s biological father to scramble to the corner of the room and cower away from his enraged son. Ignoring the pleas from his then tag team partner, Gryfon moves toward the terrified man, but instead of striking him again, he reaches down and violently pulls him to his feet. Gryfon: You are not my father! My father is dead. You are nothing to me you gutless piece of shit! The last time I saw you, you were stuffing a duffel bag in a taxi and running off to Las Vegas with some prostitute. I had to live in a fucking alley because you owed all your damn money to a loan shark! And now, you want back into my life; now that I am starting to make a name for myself? All you see in me is a goddamn paycheck. What did you do this time? Gambling… drugs? With his hands still up to protect his face, the trembling man attempts to back away from his son but is soon stopped by a wall. Struggling to hide the fear in his voice, the man tries to respond to Gryfon. Gryfon’s father: No, no… it isn’t that at all. I just want to get to know my son, it ain’t about money. I… Before he can finish his thought, Gryfon cuts him off with a hard right hand that sent a blinding shot of pain up the bones in his nose. Merrit, still trying to restrain his friend and tag team partner, finally gets Gryfon’s attention, but before he can say anything is shoved out of the way and falls to the floor. He looks up at Gryfon and sees the fire in his eyes. Swallowing hard, he begins to fear that Gryfon has gone totally over the edge and may not ever come back. Staying on the floor in hopes of not provoking Gryfon to unleash the anger on him, Merrit decides to let this play out… as if he has a choice anyway. Turning back to his silenced father Gryfon manages to regain a sliver of control over himself, and just in time. Gryfon: You expect me to believe that bullshit? Get the hell out of my damn apartment! If I ever see you again, you won’t live to remember it. The man babbles out a few incoherent words of protest, but Gryfon advances toward him and brings his arm up to strike him, and the man turns and runs out the door. As Gryfon slams the door, he turns around and sees Merrit, still on the floor, looking frightened and concerned. Gryfon pauses, looks down at him awkwardly. He opens his mouth to apologize, but instead closes it, shakes his head and walks out of the room. Gryfon’s eyes open suddenly and he is lying in bed in his apartment just outside Philadelphia. Shaken by the memory that he had locked away, he stands and goes to get a drink of water. Standing in front of the mirror in the bathroom, he stares into his own reflection, looking himself dead in the eye. Searching for the shadows that have been so commonly clouding his eyes of late, he finds none. Relieved, he walks out of the bathroom and sits down on the couch in front of the TV. Surfing through channels, he lands on the Jackie Chan movie, Mr. Nice Guy. Thoughts of his next match run through his head and he can’t help but smile about the humor in his selection of movies. Muttering to himself, the smile fades. Gryfon: I am not facing just any ordinary chef. And thus endeth my third attempt at a flashback type thingy
