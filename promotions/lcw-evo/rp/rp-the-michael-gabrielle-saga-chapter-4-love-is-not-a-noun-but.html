Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Angel of Death Michael Gabrielle Entitled: The Michael Gabrielle Saga: Chapter 4 - Love Is Not A Noun, But A Verb Written: 1-30-02 Location: University of Texas at Austin With Aaron's age (14) an issue in Michael Gabrielle's mind, he still tried to get in contact with his younger cousin. Gabriele had been told many years ago thru the family grapevine of the murder of Aaron's mother by his father, and this made it hard for him to find Aaron. Another thing standing in Gabrielle's way was that the grapevine worked both ways, and he was certain that his family members didn't want a cocaine addict influencing a teenage orphan. Gabrielle knew better, however. FOr one, he was no longer an addict. Two, he had to get to Aaron while he was still young, train him well while his body was still in its prime. Third, nothing would stop them from being a team. It was destiny. But Gabrielle was smart enough to be patient. Aaron wouldn't be cleared to wrestle by anyone with half a brain for a few years yet. Gabrielle was faced with a unique opportunity: a man with a clean slate and a few years before he could set his plan into motion. Before turning to wrestling, Gabrielle had spent two years working towards an English degree at University of Texas. Before his passing, Gabrielle's father told his son the one thing he didn't want Michael to do was follow in his father's footsteps and become a police officer. His secondary goal was for Michael to get through college, to be the first Gabrielle to graduate college. When the lure of the father and son's main binding element - wrestling - came to call, Gabrielle could think of no better way of honoring his father. Now he realized that a college degree would not only respect his father's wishes, but it would make a nice parachute if his plans failed. But they wouldn't fail. Gabrielle got an apartment a short distance from the main campus. It was basically just a bed, a stove, a refrigerator and a television. Good enough for him. He took classes five days a week, even one on Thursday night so he could resist the call of frat parties and general drunken stupidity. Tuesday nights there was a Bible study group that met in the student center. It was headed by a student named Ben who used to use angeldust before finding God. Gabrielle immediately felt a kinship with him. One such Tuesday as Gabrielle was leaving the study, he heard footsteps running to catch up with him. It was dark, but this was a good well-patroled area, and besides, Gabrielle had been trained and payed to fight, so no fear. And those footsteps...they sounded like high heels. 'Hey, hold up a second!' screamed an out-of-breath female voice. Gabrielle stopped dead in his tracks. No sense in disrespecting a lady. Gabrielle turned around and was nearly bowled over by a blur of slightly-longer-than-shoulder-length brown hair. Gabrielle's strength kept them upright. 'Are you alright?' asked Gabrielle. 'Yep. I guess I'm lucky you are who you are.' The woman stood up and brushed her hair out of her face. Gabrielle remembered her as...what was her name? Virginia? No, close. Victoria. That's it...a beautiful girl from the Bible study sessions. Gabrielle had to catch himself more than once from looking at her too much and not paying attention to the Word. 'Who I am?' Gabrielle asked. 'My father and my brother were really big wrestling fans. They dragged me to see it alot when WWF came to Houston, or when TCW did.' 'Oh yeah, that.' Gabrielle said, now even more reserved with his religion taking a greater hold on his life. 'I heard what happened to you and Kirkpatrick, I'm very sorry for your loss, but, at least you came to the Lord.' continued Victoria. 'Lord works in mysterious ways.' said Gabrielle in a monotone. He didn't really want to walk down that path again. Gabrielle realized that he wasn't being much good company to this young lady, who looked very pleased to meet Gabrielle, but that was fading fast as Gabrielle wasn't exactly keeping up his end of the conversation. 'So what brought you to the fold?' Victoria smiled, glad that Gabrielle was at least showing some interest. She moved gracefully over to a nearby bench, sitting off to one side, leaving enough room for Gabrielle, who took the hint and sat down next to her, close enough to be friendly, far enough away so as not to seem too forthcoming. Besides, she had a story to tell. 'My parents decided to get divroced when I was 15, pretty rough. I was an only child, so I became the ping-pong ball. I was the defiant rebel back then and resented both of my parents for not staying together for me. Selfish, huh? So I left home. I slept at friends houses when I could, a park when I couldn't. Lots of homeless people in the park, though I never really considered myself homeless. I had a home, I just chose not to live there. One day these people from the church come down with some hot meals and open mouths more than willing to talk about salvation. At that point, I'd have bought a VCR if that's what they were selling. I made amends with my parents, went back to high school, and the rest as they say is history.' Gabrielle nodded. 'Glad to have you as a sis...friend in Christ.' Victoria shot him a beaming smile. Gabrielle realized that he wasn't so much being shy as he was awkward. She wasn't just beautiful, she was stunning. 'You know,' she said 'this isn't the best neighborhood for a lady to walk home by herself. Do you know where I might find an escort?' Victoria smiled playfully at Gabrielle, who more than got the joke. College campuses on average have more police per square foot than practically anywhere in the free world. Plus this was a safe campus. Other than a few robberies and drunken fights, the worse thing that could happen would be to run into some frat pledge running naked through the campus as initiation. Victoria walked along side Gabrielle as they walked, and calmly slipped an arm through his, combining them.
