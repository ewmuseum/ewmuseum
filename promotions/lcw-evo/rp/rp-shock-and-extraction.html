LCW Evo: The Evolution of Lethal! Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Rook Black Entitled: shock & extraction Written: 1/26/02 Location: Impulse 1.3 aftermath A tiny dot in the distance. Rook is hurtling toward it. Or maybe it's charging toward him? Nevermind. As it gets bigger, he can start to make out shapes...chessboard. It's a giant chessboard with somebody standing in the middle. Maybe they're rushing toward each other? Who's that? Suddenly, he's nose to nose with a little girl. She's wearing over sized pajamas and the same scars as Rook. After a few seconds of silence, Rook asks the question that anybody else would ask. The one that makes the most sense. 'Who did this to you?' He reaches a hand toward he face, the scar down the cheek opens up at his touch, blood begins to ooze over his fingers. She puts her hand over his. 'Don't you know who I am?' 'No.' She shrugs her shoulders, and her pajama top slides down a little, showing a seeping wound across her collar bone. Then she pulls his hand off and turns away from him. 'Would you like me to tell you?' Rook looks down at her. How could anyone do this to a little girl? 'Please.' 'I'm an aspect of you. I personify your sensitive nature. You tend to percieve me as a weakness.' 'Oh.' 'So when you feel weak, you desire to feel stronger. So you hurt me. You punish me for feeling.' 'I suppose I've treated you pretty badly.' It's not an admission of guilt so much as a fact stated. 'Yes. But I forgive you. That's why I'm here. No matter what you do, I'll always support you. And no matter how hard you try, I won't go away. You should really learn to deal with me better.' 'I will.' She laughs. 'That's nice of you to say I suppose. However, you really don't know how to deal with me any differently than how you have. And what's worse, is that you can't seem to learn how on your own. You'll need help with it. Do you want to hear what I have to say?' 'Yes.' 'Are you sure?' 'I'm sure.' 'You need to trust people more.' 'I can't allow-' 'Shhh. You said you'd listen.' 'Okay.' 'You do trust people, but you only trust aspects of them that you feel you can control. When people hate you, you know how they feel and you can forsee their actions and behavior. So, in questionable circumstances, where you don't know how someone feels, you inspire their hatred in order to gain control over them.' 'I understand.' 'Too well. You need to stop doing this. I mean, really, stop it. Right now, you've been hurt really badly. And in order to recover, you're going to need to trust people's good intentions. And you're going to have to treat them better than you have because you'll need their help.' 'I don't see who would help me really.' 'You're actually surrounded by people who care about you and would love to help you. You just need to open your eyes and accept their help when it's being offered.' The little girl turned toward him. All of her cuts have opened up and are bleeding now; blood pooled on the floor around her feet. She put her arms around his waist. 'I-' 'You're going to go away soon. But first, I have to tell you this. You are very vulnerable. You pushed your luck and skill too far this time and you're going to pay dearly for it. Your recovery isn't very certain in spite of how invincible you feel. You're not going to be whole for a while.' 'I imagine.' 'Actually, you never were.' Then the light came and Rook fell back into his body. Pain emenated from his hand, up his arm to his shoulder. He blinked, looking up at the underside of a barstool. Some pink chewing gum had been left there. The scent of alcohol from a spilled drink would accompany every inhale of breath. It was distinctly uncomfortable, the body aching severely, laying on the floor, the scent, the sounds. Rook tried to move but that sent a tidal wave of pain up his arm as well as making him breifly nauseous. The noise was a dull roar of voices. Panic, fear, anger carried through to Rook. He wanted it all to disappear. Thomas Brown didn't shove as he made his way through the crowd. The first disaster had struck as the people in the club were acting in a mob mind. Fortunately, several of the most violently inclined had left, presumably chasing after Duel. George and Henry were struggling to hold back the people crowding on Rook. One fan kept trying to jump over George's big frame to taunt Rook. 'You punk! I could kick Duel's ass! You're a (censor) wimp! Gravity was right about you! Get up so I can pound on you some, you piece of (censor)!' Patrick Arnold was in the crowd further, clinging to the back of a large angry bald man. Patrick had a sleeperhold locked on, and the man was more or less out on his feet. However, Patrick, in his nervous excitement had the hold tight enough to choke and wasn't letting go. The bald man was about to turn blue. Thomas Brown calmly shouted out orders. 'George, put that guy down. Patrick, let him go before you kill him, you idiot.' As the man jumped once more on George, George grabbed a hold of his shoulder and leg, and violently threw him down to the floor slamming his head violently into the unforgiving surface. Thomas looked around and couldn't see Charles Radding anywhere. So he had to work with what he had. 'Henry, Patrick, start escorting people out. George, back me up.' Henry moved into the crowd and started physically moving people toward the exit. Patrick moved to the exit and started sending people out. He shouted at the bottleneck at the coatcheck station. 'People! Keep your claim tickets and come for them in the AM! You have to leave NOW! Get moving!' Thomas Brown knelt down by Rook. 'Rook look at me.' Rook's eyes focused on Thomas. 'You look like you're going into shock. Don't try to move. An ambulance is on the way. Just stay calm for now, and we'll get you out of here as soon as we can.' 'Bring me tha meat wagon. Ahm not goin anyplace.' 'Heh.' Thomas stood back up and helped George with the press of people. After a few eternities, the sound of the ambulance siren neared. Thomas touched George's shoulder, 'Stay put. We need to clear a path.' George nodded, he looked over his shoulder at Rook. 'You'll be okay Rook. The ambulance is here.' Thomas met up with Patrick who was still trying to get people out. 'Patrick, we need to clear a path.' Patrick pushed between some people at the door and began waving the paramedics over to him. Patrick and Thomas began to push their way through people dividing them enough for the two paramedics could pass through towards Rook. At that moment, Charles Radding threw a guy down the stairs. Thomas cursed, 'That moron is useless.' The paramedics needed room. So Patrick, George, Thomas made a barrier with their bodies. One of the paramedics knelt down next to Rook. 'Hey, man, you look roughed up but you'll be okay. We gotta stabalise that hand as best we can, and get you on a stretcher, and the hell outta here, okay?' The other paramedic said, 'He's going into shock.' 'Let's move fast then.' The put the stretcher down next to Rook. 'Ready, 1, 2, 3, lift!' 'Errrarrrgh,' came out of Rook's mouth. Rook gritted his teeth as they moved his hand over his chest, and then secured him to the stretcher. Thomas Brown shouted to Henry, 'Find his Dad!' Henry nodded and spun around. Seeing a blur of faces and finding none that matched up to Joe Black. Fortunately, Joe found him. And Henry said, 'This way.' He led Joe over just as Rook was being lifted in the stretcher. The elevation brought the noise. The words were what people were shouting. 'You'll be okay, Rook.' was repeated countless times along with, 'You suck, Rook!' Joe resisted the urge to start punching people. He followed along after Rook's stretcher and climbed into the back of the ambulance after it. -- more later
