LCW Evo: The Evolution of Lethal! Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Grfyon Entitled: Suspicions Written: 11/12 Location: Johnny Steele's Gym and Exercise Club The scene opens with Gryfon in the gym, preparing for the upcoming tournament. Across the expansive room, the only other person ever in the gym this late at night, Shawn Merrit is working with some freeweights. After finishing on a treadmill, Gryfon pauses and turns to the television on the wall a few yards away. His face has no real expression as he stares at a tape of the last Impulse, which is nearing the end of his match against Dean Wallace. His dark green eyes narrow slightly as the screen shows Wallace connecting with the chair shot to the head, but his face remains expressionless. Gryfon: I suppose I could be angry about the way my match went, about the chair that opened up a cut on my forehead. But I’m not. Dean, you were just doing your best to win the match, and you caught me off guard. But I will remember, and next time you won’t get the chance. As Gryfon skips to the tag match, Merrit gets onto the treadmill a few feet away from his long time friend. Gryfon nods to Merrit and then returns his attention to his next opponent. Merrit jumps right into his unsolicited advice, but Gryfon’s eyes don’t stray from the man he is set to face on Impulse. Merrit: Used is a damn good singles wrestler, much better than Wallace. You’d better really push yourself or you’re going to get embarrassed. Gryfon finally turns his head to glare at Merrit, who responds with a chuckle. Gryfon turns back to the TV. Gryfon: First thing, Wallace is no slouch. He may not be quite as accomplished as Used, but he’s pretty damn tough. I know that I appear to be extremely overmatched, and probably am, but this wouldn’t be the first time I have beaten the odds. All I can do is give him everything I’ve got, and hope to catch a few brakes here and there. Merrit: Oh, you mean like people actually staying seated in their chairs rather than volunteering them to your opponent? Ignoring the interruption, Gryfon runs a hand through his slightly dampened hair before speaking again. Gryfon: If I’m going to have a shot in this match, I need to actually wrestle. I can get away with brawling Wallace, but Used is a different story. Merrit: Whoa, whoa, whoa! You mean you’re gonna try to beat Used with your limited technical wrestling skills? HA! This is going too be worse than I thought. Gryfon: I’m not just a brawler, I can wrestle too. And if you don’t pipe down, I’m going to use my limited technical wrestling skills to make you squeal for mercy. Merrit opens his mouth to respond, but is cut off before he can say anything, so he just kind of sits there with his mouth hanging open Gryfon: One more word, just one, and I will use your nut sack as a speed-bag. Merrit winces and closes his mouth. Gryfon turns back to the TV and stops the tape. He ejects it and puts in another one. After a few moments, Duel vs. Static from the first Impulse lights up the screen. Confident that he is safe to speak again, Merrit decides he’d better be serious for the moment. Merrit: Have you called Sam since she left? She wanted to talk to you to make sure you hadn’t gone completely out of your mind. Gryfon: No, I haven’t talked to her. I don’t think I will, at least not yet. Too many memories… painful memories. Merrit: You have to deal with it eventually. I mean, you can’t just shut out three years of your life. Gryfon sighs heavily and walks to a window a few feet away. Looking out, he leans against the wall beside the window. Deciding not to push this too far, Merrit changes the subject. Merrit: So, what are you going to do to your attacker? I mean, you do plan to respond, right? Gryfon’s lips move as if he is about to answer, but he remains silent for a few moments. Coming back to stand in front of the TV again, he pauses the tape and stares intently at Mike Static for a few moments. Slowly shifting his eyes to focus on Duel, Gryfon mutters just loud enough to be audible. Gryfon: Yes, my attacker… Camera fades
