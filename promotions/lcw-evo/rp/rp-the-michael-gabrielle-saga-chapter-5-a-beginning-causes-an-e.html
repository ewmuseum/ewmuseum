The Michael Gabrielle Saga: Chapter 5 - A Beginning Causes An Ending

2 February 2002

Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Angel of Death Michael Gabrielle Entitled: The Michael Gabrielle Saga: Chapter 5 - A Beginning Causes An Ending Written: 02-02-02 Location: University of Texas at Austin After the chance encounter with the beautiful Victoria, life was once again good for Michael Gabrielle. He was clean, happy, learning, and in love. After about 5 months of dating, the couple moved in together. Other than classes, jobs, and the weekly Bible study, they were each other's lives. They lived together, prayed together, helped keep each other on the right track, and learned about each other. What Gabrielle learned probably could have filled a book or two, but a good amount of that was just quirks and a basic knowledge of women in general. Victoria Marie Brice, Vicky for short. 5'6', 134 lbs., brown hair, brown eyes, favorite color: pink, favorite food: General Tso's chicken from a nearby Chinese restaurant. Gabrielle was her fourth boyfriend in her twenty-plus years. One she called puppy love, one was simply physical, and the third was abusive, 'so I guess this love thing is new to us both,' she said one night with a smile. Despite her best efforts to disappear and teach them both a lesson, her parents still divorced. Her mother won custody, as is the usual case in divorces. Her father got visitation rights every Saturday morning until she was 18. She kept in close contact with both. While neither was well-off (and the divorce had only insured that), they still tried to help their daughter through school as much as possible. There was, of course, more. There is always mroe to a person than their history and their likes or dislikes. There was the way she laughed when things weren't right, the way she smiled when Gabrielle was just the slightest bit nervous about meeting her parents, the way the hugged when she needed to feel loved, the way she would squeeze Gabrielle's hand just because. Valentine's Day had really snuck up on Gabrielle. He had been in such a daze of classes and Vicky that February simply jumped out in front of him. One night, after Bible study, Gabrielle asked Vicky to give him a few minutes while he spoke to angeldust-addict-turned-Bible-study-coordinator Ben. 'Ben,' Gabrielle said with chagrin, 'this is the first year I had a reason to buy anyone anything for Valentine's Day, and to be perfectly honest, I'm stumped. I know it's not your area of expertise, but you're the best friend I've got.' 'Mike, I understand your situation,' Ben said, motioning for Gabrielle to sit down. As he did, Ben could see out the window Vicky speaking with some of the other group members. 'She's special, isn't she?' 'More than you know, Ben.' 'Well, let's see. Flowers are always a nice touch, that never goes out of style.You could take her out for dinner, somewhere nice. I've never been one for big gifts myself, so, just give her YOU. Seems to have worked just fine so far.' Gabrielle nodded. He liked the idea, but how that would translate into a reality was beyond him. 'Give her you? How do you give yourself to someone?' And in that moment a light bulb went off inside Gabrielle's head. 'Do you mean I should pro-' 'Do what the Lord and your heart tell you to do, Michael.' ***** Valentine's Day came, and Gabrielle was right on time with the flowers - pink roses - in a vase on the tray that also carried breakfast in bed for Vicky. She, of course, found it very sweet, and reached under the bed to get her gifts for him: a black trenchcoat and matching cowboy hat. 'Figure it'll look good if you ever get back in the ring.' she said. On the subject of rings, Gabrielle planned to surprise her the next day (Monday) and take her out to get her finger sized for a ring. Dinner was great, a nice Italian restaurant on the outskirts of the city, and as they fell asleep in each other's arms, Gabrielle thought 'Today was great, but tomorrow will be perfect.' ***** Before Gabrielle could, in essence, kidnap his would-be-fiancee, both had 10:30 classes that would let out around noon. As Gabrielle walked into their apartment, he picked up the mail and began to leaf through it, anything to keep his mind off of what he was about to do. Bill, bill, letter from Vicky's mom, bill, letter from Aaron... Gabrielle couldn't even open the envelope through his excitement, actually having to pause and collect himself. Once open, Gabrielle was glad he hadn't ripped the letter in two by accident. 'Michael, I heard that you've been looking for me. I managed to get your address from a chain of relatives. I've got a pretty good idea why you're trying to get in touch with me; it just makes sense. I know I can't wrestle until I'm 18, but I'm willing to wait. By the way, what do you think of the name 'Apostle'? God bless, Matthew Green, aka, Aaron Antioch' Michael dropped to a knee right there in the kitchen and thanked God for letting the first step in his plan work. As he stood back up, there was Vicky standing in the doorway with an eyebrow raised and a smile. 'You don't have to bow every time I enter the house, Mikey.' she said laughing. Gabrielle tripped over his words more times than either of them could count as he tried to explain his plan: go to New Jersey, train Aaron, wait until he was 18, enter wrestling as a team, and let the rest write itself. 'Whoa, hold on there cowboy. New Jersey is a long way away from Austin, Texas, what am I going to do?' questioned Vicky, happy for Gabrielle but beginning to wonder about how all of this would affect them. 'Well, you're coming with me.' Gabrielle said as if this was the most obvious thing on Earth. Vicky dropped her head, and it was then that Gabrielle realized that he forgot to change the plan to suit the current situation. 'Mike, I love you, you know I do...' 'I love you too, that's why I...' Vicky waved him off, wanting to finish. 'I love you, but this is my home, this is where my family is, this is where I go to school. I can't just throw all of that away to follow you while you chase your dreams. Maybe it's time you settled down, you're not getting any younger, and by the time Aaron is cleared to wrestle, you'll be even older still. Get your degree, get a real job, and stay here, in your home, with me.' Gabrielle was taken aback. Up until now, everything was so perfect. They hadn't even argued once, not even the slightest disagreement. Had the situation been different, Gabrielle probably would have laughed at the irony of his thought: The honeymoon was over. 'Vicky, I'm not asking you to give up who you are. You can transfer to a college out there, we can visit your family and they can come visit us...' Even as the words came out of his lips, Gabrielle was sure they'd have no impact. It was more than seeing her family and getting an education, it's just roots. 'Mike, maybe Aaron can come out here, stay with us, you can train him out here.' 'No, his aunt would never stand for that.' A silence fell across the room. Gabrielle had a decision to make: the wrestling business and an uncertain future, or officially hanging them up for good and a relatively certain future. Most of his mind was in upheaval about this choice, but apparently the part controlling his voice wasn't involved in the battle. 'You say you love me, yet you won't stand by me?' The words didn't sound like Gabrielle. Well, they did sound like his voice, but like nothing he was capable of saying. Vicky felt it too. Gabrielle could tell as her jaw dropped. She tried to speak, but couldn't form the words. She stood up, grabbed her coat, and walked out the door. Gabrielle sat stunned. He wanted to call out for her, tell her that they could come up with some compromise, but he simply couldn't compel his body to do so. And somewhere, deep within Gabrielle's mind, a voice came through crystal clear: 'She's gone. Now we can get down to business.' Whatever that voice was, and whatever emotions it was tied to, Gabrielle wanted no part of them. The shock was too great; Gabrielle went into robotic action and began packing his belongings, including his black trenchcoat and cowboy hat. Gabrielle couldn't tell why he would bring these things along. Maybe to show her he still cared? Maybe to rub it in? No time to think of that now, time to get moving, time to make the next part of the plan come to fruition.
