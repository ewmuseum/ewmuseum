Angles and Visitations

27 January 2002

LCW Evo: The Evolution of Lethal! Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Frank Heimdal Entitled: Angles and Visitations Written: 01/ 27/ 02 Location: Philadelphia General Hospital The day after Impulse. Nobody likes hospitals. And these kids are about sick of them. Frank and Livvie stood outside of room number 423. 'Do you think he's gonna like what we got him?' 'I don't know. But he just doesn't seem like the flowers and get-well card kind of guy. ' Frank peered into the brown paper bag. 'I know this is what I'd like to get if I was laid up, though.' Livvie didn't say anything. She chewed her lower lip. 'You're going to chew that off.' Livvie smiled. 'Let's go in.' Frank took a step forward, and Livvie caught his jacket sleeve in her hand, pulled him back. 'Do you think he's going to tell me to fuck off?' 'Well, if he does, we'll just have to fuck off then, won't we?' 'Would you please stop making so much sense?' 'If we aren't wanted, we aren't wanted. That's that. Let's go in.' They entered the room, Frank in front, Livvie trailing behind. Rook was lying in his hospital bed, head to the side, eyes half closed due to the painkillers he was on, swathed in a white standard issue hospital blanket, with a minor case of bed head. Then there was the hand. The hand had been operated on the night before. It was in a white cast, with what appeared to be two bolts sticking out of the top. It laid across his chest. His left hand was at his side. Frank and Livvie stared. An elderly, balding gentleman noticed them. He raised his head off of his pillow slightly and aknowledged them with a polite nod. 'Are you here to see him?' The old guy pointed a thumb toward Rook. Frank looked over at him and nodded. 'He's a bit out of it right now. I'll get him up for ya.' He sat up a little more. 'Hey dummy! Wake up. You got visitors.' Having said that, he relaxed back into his pillow. Rook twitched almost imperceptibly, and his eyes opened a little more. 'Thanks Arnie.' 'No problem.' Arnie shifted to get more comfortable and closed his eyes. Rook's head slowly rolled over in dugged-up slow-motion, and he tried to focus on the people at the end of his bed. He squinted a bit. 'Oh. Hello there.' Rook had to go to the bathroom, and he had a nasty case of dry mouth due to the painkillers. But, out of pride, he wasn't going to ask for water, or the pee-thingie (whatever the hell it was), while his visitors were present. After a few moments, he said 'Well? What?' This snapped them out of it. Frank was first to approach the hospital bed, being sure to go to the side with the good hand. 'Sorry, sorry.' Frank didn't want to sound dumb, but he realised that there was nothing he could say to this person that wasn't going to come off that way. 'So, what's goin' on?' Frank said it in a slightly too loud voice on purpose, a goofy grin on his face. 'Well,' Rook relaxed his neck and let his head roll toward Frank 'surgery was pretty cool. I missed most of it. I didn't get to ask for a see-through cast.' Rook looked down at his right hand. 'Somewhere underneath this, my hand is in here.' 'Still looks pretty good though.' Even in his drug-induced haze, Rook knew that Frank meant that the Frankenstein-esque cast had it's own aesthetic appeal, and not that the wounded hand itself looked good considering the damage. Livvie finally decided it was safe to go to Rook's bedside. Her eyes avoided his hand, and she looked at his face. 'Hi, Rook.' Rook's eyes travelled over to Olivia. 'Hello.' Livvie looked down at her shoes. They were suddenly fascinating. Rook's eyes traveled over to Frank, who responded with a telling expression suggesting, 'Go on.' Rook looked back over at Livvie and took a deep breath. 'Duel is a loser, but not because of you, okay?' Livvie looked back up at Rook. Her expression registered a little surprise at his bluntness. 'Okay.' She said in a small voice. She wasn't going to argue with him on the 'loser' comment while Rook was in this state. She grabbed a hold of Rook's good hand and squeezed. 'But I'm still sorry.' Rook said, 'Okay.' Livvie changed the subject, 'We got you some presents.' Frank revealed the brown paper bag under his arm. 'We searched high and low for the perfect brown for the bag to put it in. We had to skin some well tanned old dude. Though if we knew you had one as a roommate, we would have used him. But anyway, here you are.' Frank opened said bag of alleged human skin, and pulled out some comics, trade paperback format. Frank dissertated, 'This is Johnny the Homicidal Maniac: The Director's Cut. And this is Squee: With Meanwhiles. And this is The Weekly World News. You made the cover.' Frank held out the rag sheet for Rook to see. It read, 'Rook Black's Hand Amputated. Cybernetics To Be Applied.' There was a picture of Rook lying on the ground, and his hand lying a few inches away from his wrist. It had clearly been airbrushed and re-touched. Rook squinted and blinked. 'Hold that away further.' Frank did. Rook said, 'Oh.' He looked down at his cast and poked at one of the screws. 'Ow. No, my hand's still in there.' 'Yep. Sure is.' Frank's face turned blank for a split second, then he tried to cover it over by smiling again. 'Well, Liv and I shouldn't keep you. We'll let you rest.' Frank put Rook's books back in the bag and laid the package on his bedside table. 'You...take care. Hope we see you soon.' Frank stepped back. Livvie hadn't realised she was still holding on to Rook. She gently released her grip. What could she possibly say to him? 'Yeah. Take care.' 'Thank you.' Rook's eyelids were beginning to droop again. Frank and Livvie decided it was time to make their exit. They turned and walked out. When they reached the hallway, they matched each other's stride, Franklin guiding Olivia by the elbow. Both of their faces had the same expression; so distraught they were blank. But one would only know they were upset if one knew them well. To the rest of the world, they merely looked like a pair of intimidating siblings. A dynamic duo in their own right, as they had always been growing up. Livvie was glad she made it to the rental car before the tears began. No sobbing, just waterworks. Frank turned it over, and they drove to the airport in silence. +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ It was a Ben and Adrienne joint this time.
