Apology Accepted... Or Not

26 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo: The Evolution of Lethal! Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Silence Jake Julius Entitled: Apology Accepted... Or Not Written: 1/26/2002 Location: Jake's Philadelphia Residence Is a person in the wrong for apologizing? If he offers help, shouldn't it be accepted? At least let the person make up for what he did. We see Jake sitting in his black leather Lay-Z-Boy chair with a squirt can of whipped cream in his right hand, and a television remote in his left hand. He's wearing yet another green shirt, and another pair of khaki pants. Jake squirts some whipped cream into his mouth, puts the cap on the can, and sets it on the wood table next to him. 'I had fun last night,' Jake says happily. 'Well... It wasn't so fun after that no contest match.' Jake picks up a can of cream soda that is next to the whipped cream can, and takes a sip. 'You see, Nate, I accidentaly ran into you guys. It was an accident that you got blown up over,' Jake says as he takes another sip of cream soda. 'I even apologized to you! And in case you didn't notice, I was trying to help your one-nighter up. She reached for my hand when I offered it, but you stopped it!' Jake trades the cream soda for the whipped cream and takes a squirt of whipped cream. 'What is so bad about me offering to help you? Is it wrong?' Jake questions with confusion. 'Is it wrong to make up for what a person has done? If so, what is wrong about it, because I fail to see anything wrong with it.' Jake takes yet another squirt of whipped cream. 'You see, if you did what I did to me and my fiance, and you offered an apology and to pick Amy up, I would let you. How would you like it if I told you to go away after you apologized to me?' Jake questions. 'Oh wait... You're to good to apologize. I forgot.' Jake swaps the whipped cream for the cream soda again, and takes a sip of the cream soda. 'I can't believe that you don't have the heart to accept an apology, and let a person make it up to you,' Jake says in shock. 'It's disgusting. Just disgusting.' The door bell rings, causing Jake to stand up and open the door. In the doorway stands Jake's blonde fiance, Amy, who is wearing a black skirt, and black boots. 'Hey, I'll be out in a second,' Jake says as he rushes back to his Lay-Z-Boy to finish off his cream soda. Jake then grabs his green jacket, slips it on, and walks over to the door, where he is met with a kiss from Amy.
