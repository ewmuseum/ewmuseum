Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Pheonyx Entitled: Consequences Written: 1-18-2002, 8:23 PM Location: Club Evo Pheonyx is sitting at one of the corner tables on Club Evo's second floor, nursing a bottle of Molsen Ice, staring down dejectedly. Alexis Fairchild, wearing a tight pink mini-dress and matching heels, sits down next to her husband-to-be, and looks at him with a concerned look on her face. 'I appologized to him...' 'I know, Dante...', Alexis replies as she takes his hand into hers. 'He just needs more time, that's all.' Pheonyx sighs, disheartened at this turn of events. 'I wish he was as forgiving as you, Alexis'. 'Everything isn't completely fine with us, Dante... but I can see that you are willing to change... and that is why I am sticking by you. Randall is trying to give you the benefit of the doubt, too.' 'What about Rook?', queried Dante. 'Rook... well, he's... he's going to take a long time. He's sort of stubborn like that. It will take a long time for him to deal with this... and I guess the same can be said for Michael. For God's sake, Dante... you basically stalked his fiance, showed pleasure in his regretful disability, a disability that you accidentally caused, but then claimed that you did on purpose. Do you really think that he will just accept your apology with open arms?' Dante looks hurtfully at Alexis. 'I thought that you were trying to make me feel better?' Alexis sighs hard, then continues, 'Dante... you did a lot of terrible things... caused wounds that will take a long time to heal, or maybe even not heal at all. You have to accept that, show them that you are trying to change, and maybe then they will see that you are really sincere about this change. It's going to be very hard, and there are going to be HUGE setbacks... but if you are truly serious, you have to see things from their perspective as well, and understand their reluctance.' Dante finally looks up and into Alexis' sparkling blue eyes, letting a small smile cross his face. 'How am I so lucky to have you in my life?' 'Don't know... just be happy that you do.' 'What bothers me about this is why he was out there in my match with Wallace?' 'I don't know, Dante... that is something that you will have to ask him for yourself.' Alexis stands up, looking at her man with a look of sweetness on her face. 'Come on, Dante... let's go home. If we're lucky, we can get home before the beginning of the encore showing of Tuesday's episode of 24.' Dante gets up slowly, takes Alexis' hand, and the two of them make their way through the Friday night crowd that is really starting to file into the club in droves. After a few recognitions and autographs later, they finally get into Alexis' red 1999 Saleen Mustang convertible, the top being up due to the cold Philadelphia winter weather, and they drive back to their new apartment a few miles away.
