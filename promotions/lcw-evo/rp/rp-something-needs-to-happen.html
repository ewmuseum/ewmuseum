Something Needs To Happen

2 February 2002

LCW Evo: The Evolution of Lethal! Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Silence Jake Julius Entitled: Something Needs To Happen Written: 2/2/2002 Location: Streets Of Philadelphia We see Jake driving his light green electric Honda Insight, with his fiance Amy in shotgun. Jake is wearing a green tee-shirt, and khaki pants with black sunglasses, while Amy is wearing a white tee-shirt and blue jeans. Jake drives the car through the streets with a dissapointing look on his face. As he turns a corner, Amy sees his look. 'What's wrong?' Amy questions Jake. 'Do you not want to get married?' Jake looks at Amy in shock as he pulls up to a stoplight. 'How can you say that!?' Jake yells in confusion. 'I love you.' Amy begins to look relieved. 'Well then, what's wrong?' Amy questions Jake yet again. Jake sighs. 'You see, I've been on two cards, and nothing has happened to me,' Jake says with dissapointment. 'All I have had were some matches. No argument, no challenges. Just matches.' 'Oh,' Amy says as Jake begins to turn the corner. They drive on for a few miles in silence, Jake still with a dissapointed look. 'Well, what are you going to do?' Amy questions Jake curiously. 'I don't know. I just don't know,' Jake says sadly. 'Well you should. Because right now, we need money for the new house, possibly a kid, a lot of yard space, the list goes on,' Amy says as she looks at Jake and pulls his head so he faces him. 'Remember we both wanted those things?' Jake turns his head away. 'Yes, I do. But I don't know if I do now,' Jake says sadly. They drive on for another few miles without saying a word. All the while, Jake ponders whether or not he should stay in LCW Evo. 'If I stayed, there is the possibility I could get in some kind of feud,' Jake thinks to himself. 'But if I left, I could join another fed. Why must there be a good side to both?' Jake questions himself as he slams his fist on the steering wheel. 'Aah!' Amy screams in shock. Jake pulls the car over to the side of the road and gets out. He walks over to the wall of a small shop, and begins to kick it. Amy gets out of the car and pulls him back. 'What is wrong!?' Amy yells in anger. 'What!?' Jake looks at the wall, and then looks at Amy. Jake sighs as he begins to speak. 'Listen. I have to make a decision. I have to decide whether or not I truly want to stay in LCW,' Jake says sadly. 'I haven't even feuded with anybody. Not a single person.' Amy looks up at Jake. 'Remember when you were unemployed a few months back? You said that you didn't care who you fought with. You didn't care if it was a freak or a normal person. A person you hated or a person you liked. All you wanted to do was simply wrestle,' Amy says as she recalls what Jake had told to her over the phone while he awaited to sign his LCW Evo contract. Jake looks down at Amy and smiles. 'You're right. I don't know what I was thinking,' Jake says as he smiles. 'I think I will stay in LCW Evo. It's fun. Thank you.' Amy kisses Jake. 'No problem.' Jake and Amy begin to get into the car. 'So, still on for a night of partying over at Evo?' Jake questions Amy. 'Oh yeah!' Amy replies. Jake drives off towards Club Evo, happy to be keeping his job, happy to not quit just because he hasn't had a single scuffle with anybody.
