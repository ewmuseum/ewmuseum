A simple request

A simple request A simple request [ LCW neo Legends Board ] Posted by Gryfon on December 05, 2001 at 02:46:58: The screen comes to life to show Gryfon in his locker room, moments after the Impulse show. The locker room already in shambles around him, he kicks a trash can across the room, obviously furious. He slams his forearm against the locker and rips it open and pulls his bag out, taking a seat on the bench. He is silent for a few moments before he stands up and walks toward the door. As he tears open the door to leave, he stops and looks at the mirror on the wall beside him. He stops dead in his tracks as he stares into the face full of hatred, sees his eyes glazed over with the shadows he has seen many times before. He stands still for a moment, and the expression, though still one of anger, softens a bit, and the light returns to his eyes. He stays another moment, now looking down at the ground. As he looks up, he can see the same shadows returning over his eyes and he slams his palm into the mirror and walks away. The next day Gryfon is shown sitting on the bed of a hotel room just outside of Philadelphia, and the camera pans out a little, showing a man and a woman talking to each other in the bathroom, occasionally sending concerned glances in the direction of Gryfon. Gryfon, looking directly into the camera, begins very straight-forward and to the point. Gryfon: I want my shot at Duel. He pauses for a few moments before combing his hand through his hair. He is much calmer now than when we last saw him, though still obviously affected by the events of the previous day. Gryfon: I don’t care about the damn title, I don’t give a sh*t about what kind of match. I just want what was promised to me. Duel, unless Static was right in what he said, you will give me my match. Unless you think you’re too good to fight me, you have no reason not to give me my match. And believe me, this time I’ll make sure to take the f*cking elevator. He takes another pause, and drinks from a bottle of water. As he sets the bottle down he looks back up at the camera; his expression looks as if he is somewhat removed from the situation now. Gryfon: To my attacker… to whoever didn’t want me in that damn match, I give you a warning. Next time, I will be ready. I hope you try it again. If it was in fact you Static, I want you to know that I am not intimidated by your past accomplishments. Hell, for all I know that’s all they are, things of the past. My point is, I just don’t give a rat’s ass if you have been the champ in LCW before, or who you’ve beaten that I haven’t even heard of. It doesn’t matter to me. All I know is that someone didn’t want me to fight Duel and you seem to be the most likely possibility. But then again, Mike, it couldn’t have been you alone. Unless you had someone helping you out… after all, you don’t have access to the locker room level. The bedside phone rings and Gryfon turns to answer it. After a few seconds, he turns to the camera and tells them the interview is over. The camera doesn’t turn off, however. The camera moves across the room and listens in on the conversation between Gryfon’s two friends. Samantha Paris: How bad is he? I saw what happened. Have you talked to him? Shawn Merrit: Well, he’s calm now. But when I got here… I hadn’t seen him this bad since he last tried to contact Tyler. Sam, he’s not in control right now. Sam: He tried to contact Hyatt again? Why didn’t you tell me? Shawn: I thought you knew. I suppose he didn’t tell you that Kabel is back either? Sam: What?! What the f*ck is going on? Shawn: I don’t know, but… Shawn Merrit pauses as he turns and sees the camera. He closes the door to the bathroom, shutting out the camera and we can hear the lock turn. The camera turns around to show Gryfon hanging up the phone. He finishes writing something on a piece of paper. As the camera moves closer, we see a name and an address. Gryfon turns his head toward the bathroom door as he puts on his worn out leather jacket. Gryfon: We’re going to Washington.
