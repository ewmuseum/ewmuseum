Happy Birthday, Dean! (Parte Dos)

LCW Evo: The Evolution of Lethal! Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Dean Wallace Entitled: Happy Birthday, Dean! (Parte Dos) Written: 1/26/02 Location: Dean's Apartment: Tempe, AZ When we last left Dean Wallace’s birthday psuedo-party, he was dragging his brother Ryan by his arm into his bedroom, angered at the situation he is currently in. Dean opens the door to his bedroom and whips Ryan spine-first into the bed. DW: I’m ready. Talk. RW: What do you mean, talk? DW: Talk. Tell me what the hell is going on. RW: OK. (deep breath) I called Gentleman’s World… Dean applies a standing headscissors to his younger brother. Dean outweighs Ryan by a good 45 pounds, and is taller by 4 inches. Dean flips his brother up, and powerbombs him onto his bed, right onto the remote control. RW: Ow… the hell was that! DW: Tell the f****** truth, dipshit. RW: OK, OK. Remember that night at Club Neo, the night of the tournament? Dean rams an elbow hard into Ryan’s sternum. DW: Yeah. RW: Well, that night, I was trying to hit on women, and failing… Dean picks Ryan up and applies a headlock. Seconds later, Ryan goes flipping, and Dean slams him with a snap suplex. RW: Cut that out, motherf*****! DW: For that, it gets worse. Dean absolutely lays into Ryan with a chop. RW: Shit, Dean! DW: Keep talking. RW: Well, you and Frank sent me over to go hit on the coat check girl. DW: Shit. Dean opens his closet and removes his Club Evo brochure/program. He turns to the staff page. Staring out at him is one Tina Strauss, coat check girl. 5’7”, 115 pounds. DW: Keep talking. Ryan gets to his feet. RW: Anyway, I tried to hit on her, too, but she was f****** psycho! So, for revenge… ow! The ow is courtesy a release German Suplex by Dean. RW: For revenge, I told her you thought she was hot. DW: You whaaaaaat? RW: I told her you were digging on her, and that’s why you sent me over, to invite her out to Arizona, to spend time with you. DW: Shouldn’t have done that. Dean belly-to-bellies Ryan, and Ryan goes flying over the bed, landing hard on the floor. RW: I think I broke a rib! DW: Consider yourself lucky. Now, stay in here while I go give her the boot. Dean exits, as Ryan clutches at his back in the background. He walks into his great room, where Tina occupies the sofa, petting Goofy and wearing Dean’s party hat. TS: Happy birthday! DW: Heh, thanks. Hey, uh, listen… TS: Oh, no. DW: My brother was just… messing with you… TS: Not again. DW: What you mean? TS: This is what always happens. Tina starts to cry. DW: Aw, shit, Tina, I’m sorry. But you should have come to me before you did anything like this. TS: (sobbing) It always ends this way! DW: (shaking his head) Look, maybe I could… put you up for the night. I’ll pay for the hotel room, and when I leave for Philly tomorrow, I’ll buy you a ticket, and it’s over. TS: (a little more composed) I don’t know. Doesn’t your brother know that misleading women can be hazardous to your health? DW: (chuckles) He does now. Both Evo employees sit in silence for a bit. DW: I tell you what. Stick around a bit. I’ll get you something to eat tonight, and I’ll set you up with a room at the Super 8. TS: Thanks, Dean. DW: Yeah. Dean walks over to his room again. Ryan still holds a hand to his back. DW: Let’s go. Get out. RW: So you two are going to do that thing. Alright. I’ll stand out in the hall. You got a glass? DW: Don’t provoke me any futher, Ryan. It isn’t smart to provoke me. Dean leads Ryan towards the door. He opens it, shoves Ryan out, and follows, closing the door behind him. DW: OK, I have to admit, that was a good one. But don’t EVER do it again. RW: I won’t. I promise. Dean turns to go back in. RW: So, what are you two having for dinner? A little macaro… Dean lunges for Ryan and drops him with a DDT on the carpet. DW: Out! RW: (whimpering) My brain… DW: See you at 5. RW: (still whimpering) What you mean? DW: You’ll be back at 5 for dinner. See you then. RW: (still whimpering)I don’t need your food, I have money. (beat) RW: (still whimpering)What are you having? DW: Clam chowder. RW: (in a pathetic voice) I love clam chowder. DW: See you at five, Ryan. Dean enters his apartment. We fade to black.
