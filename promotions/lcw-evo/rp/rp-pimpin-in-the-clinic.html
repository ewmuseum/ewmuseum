Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Nathaniel Kirbinati Entitled: Pimpin' in the Clinic Written: 1-10-2002 Location: Betty Ford Clinic September 27th, 2001 “I’ve been in rehab for about a month now, it’s been pretty rough on me so far. Dr. Jonnson asked me to keep a journal to free my mind of stress and anxiety yesterday. I’ve never really done this before, so we’ll see how it goes. The withdrawals are getting worse and worse by the day. The heroin injections they are giving me are depleting, and the wanting of the drug is becoming too much. I never thought I would get a dependency like this, never in my wildest dreams. I never considered myself an addict. I never thought I was hooked on the shit until now. I always considered myself a ‘social user,’ even when it was just me by myself. I’ve got 2 more months of this shit left in Betty Ford, and I don’t know how long I can last. I feel like jumping out, slitting everyone’s throats and running away to someplace else, but I know I wouldn’t do that. That would be the heroin acting, not me, and I can’t let my addiction beat me. I can’t lose...I can’t let Wes down...he’s counting on me to get him out of jail....” There’s a knock at the door, and a set of keys are dangling outside. Nathaniel puts his pen and tablet down and looks towards the door. As it opens, a beautiful brunette nurse walks in, seemingly holding his freedom in her hands: a syringe. Kirbinati looks at the nurse kind of funny, “What’s that for, beautiful?” “It’s just a little something to take the withdrawals away. Dr. Jonnson said you might appreciate it.” “I do, sweetness, I do...” Kirbinati replies, looking at the nurse as if she was his true love, “What’s your name?” “You don’t really need to know my name, I’m just a nurse you’ll probably never see.” The nurse turns her head and brushes her hair from the side of her face to the back. Nathaniel sees this as some sort of signal. “Come on, beautiful, I need to know the name if the one that brings me such happiness...” “You’re a real sweet-talker, aren’t ya?” “Nah, not really, I just speak the truth..” The nurse blushes and giggles in a soft manner, so soft that Nathaniel barely heard it. She then pulls out a thick rubber band and places it over his biceps of his left arm. When she finds a vein, she begins to stick in the needle. “Come on, sweetness. You’re just gonna stick me with some liquid and leave without even telling me your name?” Nathaniel says, looking at the nurse in a very confused manner. “Well, Nathaniel, I’ve got to do my job. I was told to come in here and give you a shot of this, not to get picked up by the hot looking Italian guy..I mean..” The nurse stops, mid sentence, and blushes even more. “Ah, so you think I’m sexy, huh? You think I’m hot, you wanna date me, you think I’m sexy, you wanna kiss me...” Nathaniel says, laughing pretty loudly. The nurse simply cuts him a ‘Oh, please shut up before you get in deep trouble’ look, so he quits it right where he was. “Did I strike a nerve with that, sweetness?” “Yes, you did Nate, now can you quit it?” Nathaniel kinds shrugs a bit, “Sure, no sweat, my pet, hehe” The nurse cuts him a semi-evil look, “I’m not your pet. You don’t even know my name..” Nathaniel stands up and walks right up to the nurse, “Well, how about you tell me so I can know for sure.” “Well....okay....” The nurse looks up at Nathaniel, smiling “It’s Me..” The door opens again, and Dr. Jonnson walks into the room. The nurse turns around, and notices the doctor. “Nurse, you’re needed in the rec hall. Old James fell down again and needs some help.” The nurse nods at the doctor, “Yes sir.” “But..nurse..you’re name..” Nathaniel asks, but it’s too late. The nurse had already left the room and shut the door. “Nathaniel, are you flirting with the nurse again?” Dr. Jonnson says, cocking a smile. “What do you think? I’m Italian, that’s what I do.”
