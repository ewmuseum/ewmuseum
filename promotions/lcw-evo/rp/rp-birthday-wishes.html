Birthday Wishes

10 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo: The Evolution of Lethal! Legend Board | Back to Legends Main Dean Wallace Entitled: Birthday Wishes Written: 1/10/01 Location: Tempe, Arizona, Dean's Apartment Dean Wallace sits in his apartment, Playstation 2 controller in his hand. The LCW video game is on screen, and Dean is in the midst of selecting his character. He, of course, stops at his name, and opts to make Rook Black his opponent. DW: In a feeble attempt to control my raging ego, I have decided that it is not a mere formality that I will win the 8-man tournament at Impulse at go on to face Rook Black for the title. DW: But it might as well be. Dean chuckles. He skips through the entrances, because they’re outdated now anyway. The cyber-bell rings, and cyber-Dean charges his opponent, hitting Rook with a jumping knee to the gut. DW: So when I do face Rook Black, God and myself willing, this is what I’m going to do to him. I’m going to… A knock at the door is heard. In the time it takes for Dean to swivel his head towards the door, cyber-Rook trips up the compu-Closer from behind, and quickly applies the Stalemate! Cyber-Dean taps like the proverbial bitch, and the match ends, just as Dean turns his attention back to the machine and Ryan Wallace enters the room. DW: Actually, yeah, that’s probably pretty accurate. ‘Sup, R-Dub? RW: Dude, I need to know what to get you for your birthday. DW: That is coming up, isn’t it? In, what, 16 days? RW: Yeah. I have no clue what to get you. DW: Get me that LTD PS2 game. RW: Anything else? DW: I need some cheap heat to pop the Philly crowd. RW: Guy on the corner’s selling an authentic Richie Ashburn jersey for a buck-fifty. DW: Get on it. RW: (laughs) Actually, I do have a hidden agenda, kinda. DW: Out with it. RW: When was the last time you heard from Dad? A serious subject. Dean has not seen his father since ’97. They kind of had a falling out, and Dean left home. Only Dean and his dad know for sure what went on, and neither is telling. DW: Maybe you should sit down. RW: Yeah. Ryan takes a seat on the opposite end of the sofa. Dean’s Australian Shepherd, Goofy, joins them in the middle. He’s looking for a butt-rub, but no takers so far. DW: 4 years. (Pause) DW: He sent me a card and some money on my birthday. RW: He leave a note? DW: Yeah. Something like, “Come back soon!” Sappy, sentimentalist crap. RW: When was Dad ever a sappy sentimentalist? DW: Then, and never aside from that. RW: You’re missing the point, Dean. Do you know where your father is now? DW: No clue. No real interest, either. We had our differences. He’s a stubborn asshole. I figured that out too late. Look, if you’re trying to salvage… He’s cut off. RW: He’s in SCOTTSDALE, playing F*%$ing BINGO all damn day. And do you know why? DW: Because somehow, he still thinks that he was in the right, and it’s made him into a bitter old coot with nothing better to do than wait for that ever-elusive B-6. RW: Try that he’s disappointed that the one thing that produced him with any pride in his life doesn’t want anything to do with him anymore. DW: And you know this how? Ryan pauses, and takes a breath. Goofy’s still looking to have his hind scratched. RW: Like it wasn’t obvious that Dad went all in with you? Look at me. I work retail at Gart’s. My athletic career consisted of getting in one JV football game on special teams and getting hit in the ass with the onside kick. My grades were OK, but not on your level. When people talk about the Lucky Sperm Club, you have to be chairman or something. RW: My point is, he was proud of you, Dean. He loved watching you play. He worked harder than just about anybody in the Arizona Tri-City area, but he would always get off work in time for tip, kick, or first pitch. He cast his lot with the smarter, more athletic Wallace. Dean jumps out of his seat. DW: And look where that got him! I’m a pro wrestler! Not exactly one of the most well-respected professions in the world. RW: You’re making money, you represent the Wallace family well every time you do a promotional appearance, and you could be the World Champ within a month. That’s not too shabby. Sit down, and listen to this. Dean does, indeed, sit down. RW: When you left, it destroyed him. He’s still not over it… DW: His problem. I’m over it. RW: Are you, Dean? Are you sure? Long pause here. It lasts about a minute. DW: Yeah, I’m sure. RW: Bullshit. Look… Ryan heads for the door, rubbing Goofy’s rear slightly as he goes, which pacifies the dog. RW: This tournament coming up. You can win. You have talent, and the good majority of these guys have no idea what they’ll be facing when they get in the ring with you. RW: Bottom line is, there’s only 2 people that believe you can win. Me… RW: And Dad. He’s still rooting for you. He misses you. RW: And you have to make it right before that tears you down from the inside. I know you. You internalize. RW: Go see him. You don’t have to talk to him, or even approach him. Just find him. Know that he’s still there. For you. For your own good. DW: I’ll think about it. RW: You’re a smart guy. You’ll make the right decision. And you’ll benefit from it. Ryan exits. Dean sits on the couch, look on his face that’s part anger, part pensive, all confusion. Fade to black.
