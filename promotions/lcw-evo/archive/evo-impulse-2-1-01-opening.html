Impulse 2.1: Opening

LCW Evo Impulse Opening Rook Black, Duel, Pheonyx, and Used stood in the ring at Torn's conclusion (When you're ripe you'll) The Club Evo neon sign flashed brightly amidst falling snow (bleed out of control) Several rapid-fire shots of the club's various locales played on various monitors (you'll bleed out of control) Pheonyx made Rook Black tap in Wingspan (you like attention) Duel choked Apex out with Unity (stop parading your angles) A moving shot followed a glass of beer as it slides along the first-floor bar (confused? ) Ruthless John Righteous dropped Steve Anderson to the mat with the Lock and Load (you'll know when you're ripe) Eric Recluse emerged triumphant from a pit fight (it's proof to you you're alive) A dizzying shot of the illuminated dance floor was shown (when you're ripe you'll) Used nailed TR Saxton with How's Your Neighbor (bleed out of control) Edward Olmstead quit LCW (you'll bleed out of control) An overhead shot of the Club's roof played (you're pregnant with all this) Frank Heimdal dropped Jason Bird with the Nosebleed (space thick with honey) Gryfon made his entrance into the Club for the first time (but I lost my taste) Dean Wallace delivered the Closer to Lost (you're into depression cause) Pariah passed out in the grasp of Doctor's Anesthesia (it matches your eyes) Rook Black forced Pheonyx to tap out using Stalemate (stop this faux to be famous) Lex Laww dropped through the top of the cell at the conclusion of the Lethal Combination match (confused?) (you'll know when you're ripe) The elevator doors at the top of the vertical transit area opened, and The Beastie Boy's "Remote Control" played loudly over Club Evo's P.A. Joe Black stepped out of the open door wearing a pair of charcoal gray dress pants, a French blue dress shirt and a silver bola tie. He regarded the crowd sternly, nodding his head with the beat. Just as the vocals to the music began, he crooked his right arm and Olivia Heimdal, his better half (as far as broadcast partners went) stepped out, looking very pretty in a long denim skirt and one-strapped black halter. She took hold of the offered arm, and together they walked down the ramp. From the outstretched hands of production crew members, Jimmy Epps and Sonya Shroeder, Joe and Livvie each take a microphone. "Legendary" Joe Black: Welcome to Impulse 2.1. Livvie "Ima gonna killa ya~a" Heimdal: Tonight we will slay you with our fabulousness. LJB: That is, not necessarily just that of Ms. Heimdal and myself. The fab- I won't say that word, belongs to LCW-evo as we broadcast our absolute supremacy. LH: More supreme than most machines.
