LCW Evo - The Evolution Continues Real Name: Dante James Anderson Birthdate: December 27th, 1974 Hometown: Toronto, Ontario, Canada Height: 6'3' Weight: 287 lbs. Nicknames: Status: Neutral Manager(s): Alexis Fairchild Current Teams: None Entrance Music: Instrumental opening to Rage Against The Machine's 'Ashes in The Fall', blending into God Live's Underwater's 'Drag Me Down' Wrestling Style: Well-Rounded High Flyer Signature Moves: Searing Flames of Retribution (Tope Con Hilo) Set Up Move: Rise From the Ashes (top rope flipping neckbreaker ) Finisher: Wingspan (Modified Inverted Mexican Surfboard that torques on the rotator cuffs of the opponent more than usual, while both men are on their knees, their backs facing each other) LCW Title History: LCW Award History: Other Federations: EHW Other Federation Awards: Bio Dante James Andersen was lucky enough to be blessed with athletic skill and remarkable intelligence. While in high school, he played football and excelled in his studies, earning him a full football scholarship to the University of Massachussetts and a full academic scholarship at MIT. Due to a clerical error, Dante's UMass scholarship was meant for another student and was revoked from him, so he took the MIT scholarship. It was there that he met Randall Leary, better known to LCW fans as Used. They were roommates for one semester until Randall was expelled for urinating on the dean of students' dog. Randall introduce Dante to pro wrestling as the two of them watched the 'Monday Night Wars' between WWF and WCW. After Dante graduated near the very top of his class,with degrees in both mechanical and electrical engineering, he returned to Toronto, longing to get back into physical competition. He played in the CFL for one year, but never saw much game time. During that time he was approached by Jimmy Grant, a local wrestling federation promoter, and offered Dante a shot. He learned very quickly, and quickly became a top draw for Grant's promotion as 'Pheonyx', a stylized version of the word 'phoenix', of which Dante has a tattoo of on his right arm. Everything went well for a while, until Dante started having strange dreams about a woman in a pink dress. These dreams began to throw off Dante's ability in the ring and earning the mocking nickname 'Nightmare Boy' from Grant and several others in the fed. Unbeknownst to him, a young female wrestler in the United States by the name of Alexis Fairchild had been having similar dreams about a mysterious man. Alexis and her friends in the group Pawn Shop, which consisted of Rook Black, Used (Dante's friend, which nether one knew the other was a pro wrestler at the time), Rook's then-girlfriend Sickness, Used's fiancee (now wife) Ophelia, travelled to Toronto. Alexis was drawn to a pink dress (the same one she wore in her dreams) and bought it, then was attracted by a sign for a local wrestling show. Sickness and Ophy went off to try and 'find a man for Alexis', and the two men accompanied Alexis to the show, where Pheonyx was performing. That night, Dante was at the top of his game, then after the match he saw her... the woman in his dreams... Alexis Fairchild. He didn't get to meet her that night, and Alexis was transfixed by him. The next day, Dante was fired by Grant, and a few short minutes after he left the gym, Rook and Used came to look for him, after Used realized that he knew Pheonyx and they decided to try to bring him into Pawn Shop. Pheonyx came back later, finding out that there were some guys who were asking for him, men who were with the woman in the pink dress. All that he knew was that they were from a big fed in the US and that they wanted to talk to him. With that, Pheonyx packed up what little belongings he had and staarted to hitch hike through the US in search of this girl. They eventually met at the Autumn Anarchy PPV for the EHW (predecessor to LCW), when he saved her from disgruntled former EHW employees, just like in Alexis' dream. They realized that they were soulmates, and meant to be together. Rook and Used asked him to join Pawn Shop, and he accepted. A few months later, at the Silent Nightmare PPV, Dante's career almost ended during a match with Hector Carreras, when his left knee was all bt shattered after it caught the security railing when Dante missed his Searing Flames tope con hilo, and Hector then hitting it with a steel chair. He returned months later, after an experimental knee replacement surgery and the wearing of a special knee brace created by himself and Dr. Soesbee, the doctor who performed the knee surgery, a brace that is lighter, doesn't inhibit any range of motion, and protects the knee better than the braces in the market. He then returned, and did not blame Hector because he remembered that when Hector hit him with the chair, it was to reset the broken bones back into place, not out of malice like Used thought for a while. After losing a very close fight with Apex in a steel cage after Apex almost destroyed Alexis' wrestling career, something inside Dante snapped, making him side with the man who he had once despised, and vowing to destroy Pawn Shop any way possible. Pheonyx's rage then began to spill into his relationship with Alexis, and started to harm and control her life. He even went so far as toe secure a World title shot at the Inferno PPV by making Rook give up his hard earned spot, threatening Rook that he would hurt Alexis if Rook did not give up the shot to him. Pheonyx then won the World title in a three-way match between then LCW champion Gravity and Hector Carreras. At the Torn PPV, He lost the title to Rook Black, and the formation of the neo began. Pheonyx was then fired by Dave Beaumont for actions that Pheonyx did to Beaumont during his stint with Apex. His firing and subsequent joining neo made Dante think about all that he had done, and is trying to make amends for what he did.
