Dawning: A Promise

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse A Promise David Chambers, Evo Champion, sat again at his bench near his locker, thinking. It had become a pattern that was more than a little distinct with him when matches came around; he would retreat from the noise and general confusion of the club and go to the locker room, which was generally empty by this time. He would sit on the bench near his locker, Bear at his side, and think. David sat with his hands at his sides and his eyes on the ground. He looked down at the floor, his head hanging downward in a way that would've once brought piles of greasy, unkempt hair cascading down into his face, before he cleaned up his act a bit. "Thought I'd come up and see if you had anything you wanted to say to a camera before you went on." The voice that interrupted David's admittedly aimless thoughts belonged to Alan Doyle, "head" of the digital camera crew. David knew Alan by this time; regarded him as a decent sort despite the initial off-puttingness of the demon tattooed on his neck. It left the viewer (or at least David) with an unsettling feeling; as though perhaps someone had just dragged something recently dead through the room. David looked up at Doyle, who held a camera in his hands. "You want me to talk to Wallace, you mean." Doyle nodded in response, and David shrugged. "Sure, I've got a few choice words for Wallace." Doyle switched on the camera. David looked into it and pictured Wallace. He started talking. "You wanted me, Dean. And I'm here. You've got your title match. Here's your chance to take the title you say you gave to me and make it your own. You've got your opportunity tonight." David paused and his eyes narrowed into slits. "It's just too bad nothing will come of it." David stood up from the bench and Doyle followed his movements with the camera. "The past couple of Impulses, you've approached the ring either DURING or AFTER one of my matches and attacked me with a baseball bat. As much as I applaud you for the honor you convey, and as much as it goes a long way to proving exactly how deserving of the Evo title you are...that's not going to work this time." David paused for a moment, thinking to himself that Bear used to sound like this, back when he couldn't. But only for a moment. "Do you have any idea how BORING it is to hear you whine about how you gave me this title? To have you come in and f(censored) up matches that we all know I would've won anyway? Do you realize how SAD it is, that you, so obviously unable to handle me by WRESTLING, have resorted to clubbing at me like some goddamned Neanderthal?" "I am a better fighter than you, Wallace. And I am going to prove it. I'm through taking your cowardly sneak-attack beatings. And I'm through listening to your self-important bitching. I've got far more than enough on my mind right now without having to deal with your piddling crap. This ends tonight. And I promise; it ends badly for you. You're gonna have to wait a long time to see this title leave my hands. And when it does, it won't be going to the likes of you." "I...f(censored)...PROMISE." (The very notion of David Chambers using the f word...TWICE...struck fear into the heart of even the author, who paused to gather his thoughts before resuming. Pause with me now...) Doyle smiled from behind the camera. "Wow. Not bad, Dave. Not bad at all." He shut the camera off. David smiled back, almost sheepishly. "What can I say? He gets me mad." Doyle laughed out loud at that. "No two ways about that. But I gotta get back down there. Kick some ass, champ." David's eyes narrowed at the use of the word before he realized the complete lack of sarcasm in Doyle's voice. "Thanks, Alan." He replied. Doyle walked back to the elevators.
