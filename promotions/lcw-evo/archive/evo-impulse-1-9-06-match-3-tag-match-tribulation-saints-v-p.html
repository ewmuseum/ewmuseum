Impulse 1.9: Match 3, Tag Match: Tribulation Saints v Pheonyx/Gryfon

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Match 3, Tag Match: Tribulation Saints v Pheonyx/Gryfon LH: We gotta tag match! And it's all about hate! I LOVE that! LJB: You love hate? LH: YEAH! Club Evo went dark, including the monitor wall. Against the black background, bold white words flashed: “Nothing Stays Divine Forever”, followed by a symbol vaguely resembling an interwoven letters T and S. “New Noise” by Refused played as the lights in the Club came back on with white spotlights scanning the ring and crowd. The monitor wall switched to shots of Lost and Angel of Death in action, including scenes of their two previous LCW World Tag Team Title victories, now cut with the words Tribulation Saints. From the vertical transit area, the men in question appeared from one of the elevators to a less-than-warm welcome. Both men hit the ring, with Lost dropping to a kneeling position facing the elevators with the Angel of Death standing behind him, stretching out and looking in the same direction. LH: Here we have the dynamic duo known as The Tribulation Saints, which consists of the formerly cute and cuddly Lost, who is now NOT very cute, nor do I think cuddling him would be a good idea, and the lllllllllllovely Michael Gabrielle, Angel of Death. What say you, cutie-wootie gangrene-bootie? LJB: Well, Peaches, Goddess of Evil, I say...bleah. The lights cut out and the room was suddenly pitched into blackness. The sound of a heartbeat penetrated the first floor of the Club, accompanied by a brief flash of dim red light. A few seconds later, the camera centered on the elevator at the sound of another heartbeat, once again accompanied by a red flash. Just after a third heartbeat, and a third red flash, red strobe lighting filled the ground floor and the elevator doors opened. Gryfon walked out, unfazed by the mixture of cheers and boos, and slid into the ring. He walked to one corner of the ring and stood on the middle turnbuckle, waiting for his partner, and keeping a steady eye on his opponents. LH: Gah! The lights! I'm gonna have a seizure! And it'll be all Gryfon's fault! LJB: Really? Great. That'll be my entertainment for tonight, seeing as how this all FAILS miserably. The Club filled with Rage Against the Machine's "Ashes in the Fall" instrumental opening, which then blended right into God Lives Underwater's "Drag Me Down". The wall monitors lit up with a fire that formed into a bird, then morphed into the name Pheonyx, the letters flaming. Pheonyx emerged from the crowd and stood on top of the black retaining wall around the ring, head bowed down and arms outstretched as his red, yellow, and orange laser lights played all around the first floor of the Club. He then leapt down to the floor and rolled into the ring, where a pattern of laser lights had formed a glowing bird shape on the center of the mat. LH: There's Pheonyx, who is NOT a dick. Nope. LJB: No. He's just kinda creepy and has spooky mind powers. LH: Yeah. That's all. Just mind powers. The Tribulation Saints conferred in the corner as across the ring Gryfon volunteered to start the match for his squad. Seeing this, Angel of Death stepped forward as Lost took to the apron. Gryfon circled his opponent uneasily, unsure how his big move oriented, power move offense would stack up against the aggravating agonizingly technical offense of Michael Gabrielle. LH: Gryfon and Gabrielle circling...the tension's building... LJB: Hmph. Looks like they're flirting. LH: Oh for the love of Pete, Joe. Both men went for a neutral collar-and-elbow tieup, with Gryfon taking the surprising advantage with an armwringer. Gabrielle however quickly ducked down to his knees and rid himself of the situation with a fireman’s carry takedown, one of the simpler moves in any wrestler’s arsenal. Gabrielle calmly, but quickly, returned to a vertical base before Gryfon could get up, and Gabrielle helped that along by placing a soccer style kick to the crown of the rookie’s head. Gabrielle strolled over to the opposition’s corner and got locked in a staredown with Pheonyx, who wisely avoided entering the ring and leaving his partner open for the double team. He was certain he would get his chance. LH: Ow. Boot for Gryfon. By then, Gryfon was coming back to his feet, prompting Gabrielle to charge him for a clothesline. Gryfon deftly ducked Gabrielle’s arm and hooked it with his own in the process. Before Gabrielle knew what was happening, he was in a full nelson and halfway towards a dragon suplex. Gabrielle instinctively hooked his legs around Gryfon’s waist to prevent himself from being lifted further. Gabrielle’s arms slipped out from Gryfon’s grasp, freeing the former Archangel to go into a modified victory roll. LJB: Wow. You're really behaving yourself tonight, Liv. LH: I have elsewhere to direct my hatred and anger, Joe. AoD gets none of it right now. Unless he wants it, anyway. Gabrielle sprung back up with Gryfon on the mat and tried to apply an Indian deathlock. Gryfon desperately crawled to the ropes and made it, thanks to the relatively short distance he had to travel in the first place. Before a five count could be administered, Gabrielle reached up and tagged Lost, who went into action quickly, legdropping Gryfon’s head, sandwiching it into the apron. LH: Does this seem at all fair to Gryfon, Joe? I mean, look at this. LJB: Oh, I'm sure Dante has something planned. Or not. Lost repeatedly slammed Gryfon’s head into the harder canvas of the apron as Gabrielle exited the ring, but not before shooting a hard glare across the ring to his opponent for Dawning. Speaking of Dawning opponents, Lost dragged his by the ankles back into the center of the ring and tried for a quick cover. LH: We gotta cover... 1... LJB: And a resounding "no." But it was still rather early in the contest, and Lost was certainly not above adding a little punishment to his main nemesis. To do so, Lost wanted to pull out something he had been working on privately. Lost helped Gryfon to his feet and applied an armwringer. Lost dragged Gryfon over to a neutral corner and stepped up to the middle turnbuckle. With Gryfon bent over to react to the armwringer, Lost sprang off the turnbuckle and connected with a somersault leg drop to the back of Gryfon’s head a la the Rocker Dropper in a move Lost named after the angel of the bottomless pit from the book of Revelations, and a word from Greek that means “destroyer”: Apollyon. LH: Wow, That was...new. LJB: And not bad either, considering it was Lost . Lost went for a cover. 1... 2... LH: This is it? Pheonyx, tired of being dormant for this match, came in and delivered a stiff kick to Lost’s head, glazing the eyes of the young star. Before Pheonyx could even be told to return to his corner, the Canadian was knocked to the mat courtesy of a springboard dropkick from AoD. Pheonyx rolled out of the ring with the momentum of the shot, and Gabrielle followed. Inside, both Gryfon and Lost stirred, while outside Pheonyx played possum for Gabrielle waiting until he was in plain sight before attacking and scoring a stiff kick to the jaw from the prostrate position. Gabrielle staggered away around the corner of the ring as Pheonyx got up to follow. As Pheonyx turned to corner, he was met rather violently by a foreign object to the forehead. Namely, a metal crucifix. LH: ...y'know, I imagine the charm of head trauma wears thin for these guys after a while. LJB: Naw. They like it. Meanwhile, in the ring, Gryfon tried to Irish whip Lost, who summarily reversed. AoD climbed up on the apron and delivered a knee to Gryfon’s back, destroying Gryfon’s stride off the ropes and leaving him victim to a Lost spear. Lost and AoD looked at each other briefly, then Lost took off and ascended the turnbuckle. Inside, AoD helped Gryfon to his feet and put him in a seated position on his (AoD’s) shoulders. Gabrielle backed up to the corner, where Lost did all the work, springing off with a flashbulb-worthy diamond dust. Lost made the cover. 1... 2... 3! Winners after The Move Formerly Known As The Act Of God: The Tribulation Saints Lost and AoD rolled out of the ring and made their way to the back, both coolly stepping over the unconscious body of Pheonyx on the way. LH: Well, looks like Gryfon's not gonna be happy. Again. LJB: Looks that way. LH: We have more people getting hurt for YOUR entertainment up next! LJB: So stay tuned. Click Here for the Next Segment
