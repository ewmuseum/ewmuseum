Impulse 1.9: Stipulations

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Stipulations Gryfon stood silently in solitude on the Club Evo rooftop. The wind picked up slightly and blew through Gryfon’s already ruffled hair. His back to the camera, he reached up his hand and locked his fingers around the chain-link fence in front of him. Staring through cold, uneasy eyes, he seemed unaware of the camera that had followed him up, or maybe he just didn’t care. After a few immeasurably long seconds, Gryfon removed his hand from the fence and took a deliberate step back. Finally turning to face the camera, he did not make eye contact, instead looking off in the distance while he spoke. “Sometimes the only way to think is to remove yourself from the insanity that we call life.” Gryfon paused, staring in the direction of the empty night sky. “You see, Lost, deep down I knew this all along. From the beginning I had doubts that it was Static, but I couldn’t be sure at first. After a little bit of investigating, my doubts were finally confirmed when a member of the security team told me that Static was spotted, on camera, in the crowd surrounding the ring at the time of the attack. I knew I had to look elsewhere for answers. I knew that if it wasn’t Static, then it was either Duel or one of his friends. Asking you to help me with the attack was no accident. And it only fueled my suspicions that you made your dramatic reappearance on the same night.” Pausing, Gryfon turned back around to the fence and looked down at the massive line to get into the club. As he spoke his voice grew in intensity and his words were backed by more force and anger. “Lost, I want you to fight me here, on this roof. If you aren’t scared of me then prove it! You and I get locked up here and neither one of us can go down until the other gives up. No rules, no interference, and no escape!” Gryfon took another pause to calm the thoughts raging through his head. Gryfon eased back from the fence, and sneered as he turned to face the camera. “What’s the matter Lost? You scared?” The sneer lingered for a few moments before fading to a cold stare, his eyes narrowing and focusing in on the camera for the first time. “That must be it. You are scared of the thought of having to face me all by yourself, without the reassurance that help is on the way if I hurt you. The only reason why I haven’t gotten to you yet is because I know that when I do, your two friends won’t be far away. No, I have no interest in attempting to take on three men at once.” Gryfon’s cold demeanor began to lose the calmness that it had maintained fairly well up to this point. His anger became apparent once again as he stared directly into the camera. “Everything you have done has been for someone else’s benefit, and you are just blind to reality. You have been since the day I got here and you kicked me down the stairs. And it didn’t benefit you at all. All it did was save Duel from the embarrassment of having to defend his title against a rookie.” “You’re nothing but a goddamn puppet! Come Dawning, I will cut your strings.” LH: Gryfon, so angry, so tense, so...Gryfon-ie. Has life dealt him a bad hand? Are the powers that be out to get him? Or...does he just need a piece?
