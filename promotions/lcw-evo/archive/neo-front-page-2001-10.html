The matches announced are: Rook Black v Pheonyx v Used for the neo Title, Duel v Gryfon for the newly-renamed Madhouse Title, and former LCW Tag Team Champions Frank Heimdal and Dean Wallace facing the Doctor and a mystery partner. Also scheduled to be on the show are LCW neo President Edward Olmstead and the return of an LCW superstar who's been out of action for months now! October 21, 2001: LCW neo Opens - Posted by Shooter LCW neo opened today, October 21st, 2001, with the first ever LCW neo Impulse! Rather than having matches, Impulse served as a press conference of sorts for Edward Olmstead to unveil Club neo, the idea that he believes will revolutionize the wrestling world!
