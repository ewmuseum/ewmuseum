Impulse 2.1: I always hated the name "Rat Pack 2.0"

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse I always hated the name "Rat Pack 2.0" Dean Wallace paced the backstage area. He wore black jeans and an old Arizona Cardinals t-shirt. He was looking for David Chambers. True, David had ripped Dean's arm from its socket at Dawning. True, Doc was now 3-0 against Dean. True, Dean had been a complete and total dick to David, and deserved not only to lose at Dawning, but to... well, have his arm ripped from its socket. So Dean paced the halls, looking for Chambers. Who he found was a face slightly more familiar to him. Frank Heimdal. Frank wore a black Sandman t-shirt, and pair of well worn comfortable jeans with deck shoes. Frank stopped and stared and Dean. He hadn't even talked to Dean since the nonsense with David had really begun to get bad. It wasn't as if Frank didn't want to talk to Dean; Dean had just made a point of not being very approachable to anyone. Frank still felt bad about not going to Dean and trying to talk some sense into him, but then again, Frank had had his own personal bullshit regarding his sister's ex-boyfriend to deal with. That, compounded with just trying to get to work on time, had made Frank more than a little pre-occupied. "Hey man," Dean said, happy to see his tag-team partner. "Dean? You aren't working tonight, are you?" Frank asked, remembering that Dean's arm was VERY out of commission. "No. Just looking for David." Frank looked concerned. He knew that Dean was the kind of person who harbored anger at people who had done things as Dean felt David had done. Dean felt embarrassed, humiliated, and would be on the warpath, figured Frank. "Well, I don't know where he is..." Frank trailed off. Frank wasn't lying, and was happy not to do so. While he was hardly David's keeper, (that was Livvie's job) Frank was also getting a bit attached to Doc, to say the least. He wanted to see more harm come to Doc as much as Livvie did. "Hey, don't worry. I'm not going to kick his ass. I'm just going to congratulate him." Dean smiled reassuringly. Frank nodded. Frank was VERY relieved at this point. He was seeing a bit of the Dean he knew prior to the title bloodlust. "Well, I still don't know where he'd be." "Eh, I'll look again later. I need a drink." Dean started to walk in the direction to the elevator that would take him downstairs to the bar to quench his thirst. Before he pushed the button, Frank called after him. "How's the arm?" Dean stopped and walked back over to his former tag-team partner. "Not bad. Can't go tonight, though. How's your back?" Frank stared quizzically. He had experienced no back trouble, and couldn't remember taking a particularly nasty bump. Dean couldn't be referring to the last match Frank had had… Frank's brows knit, and he smiled quizzically. "What do you mean?" "Nothing. Just that my back always hurt lugging 20 pounds of gold around it all night." Frank smiled and looked down at the Madhouse Title, slung over his shoulder. "This? Naw, this isn't heavy. I think it's made out of pop cans or something." Dean smirked. "You know, I was too busy being mad at myself that I never did get around to congratulating you." Frank wasn't feeling that he deserved to be congratulated over this particular win anyway. He'd won it for the wrong reasons, in his eyes. "Ah, don't worry about it..." "No, dude, I am worried about it. I mean, you're my buddy and all, and I should have been happy for you. I mean, I'll get another chance sometime." "'Course ya will." Frank said, not knowing when or where it would happen. But Frank was sure that it would. Dean smiled, genuinely, for the first time in weeks. Frank was reassured. "Well, thanks." "Consider yourself congratulated, m'man." Dean offered a hand. Frank took it. They shook, and Dean drew him in for one of those manly half-hugs, which Frank returned. "Thanks, Dean." Dean walked off in search of liquid refreshment. Frank smiled, happy that, at least for that moment, the old Dean was back. Click Here for the Next Segment
