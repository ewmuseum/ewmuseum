Dawning: Match 4, Submission Match: Gryfon v Lost

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Match 4, Submission Match: Gryfon v Lost LH: Well, I've always thought that "Lost" moniker was pretty nice. And I'll forgive Gryfon. LJB: Why? You like him because he's all moody and depressed, don't you? You chicks really dig that crap... LH: Actually, no Joe. Moody and depressed don't do it for me much anymore. I just think Gryfon's hot. My forgiving him is purely for shallow and aesthetic reasons. LJB: Heh. It would be hypocrisy for me to deny you that point. LH: Thank you, Joe. Around the roof of Club Evo was erected a large structure, one that Edward Olmstead had stressed be included in the construction of the building, but one which, to this point, had done next to nothing aside from sitting atop the building. Atop a three foot high half-wall that defined the edges of the roof sat a fifteen foot high reinforced chain link fence. Olmstead had wanted the cage to prevent any kind of accident from occurring due to the location of the locker room area. Tonight however, it would serve a different purpose. Lost and Gryfon would compete in a grueling match, where the finish could only be decided by a submission maneuver. There were no pads on the roof of Club Evo, meaning the two would go at it on rubberized concrete surrounded only by the steel mesh. The temperatures in the Philadelphia area were currently around 39 degrees, with the skies filled completely with dull clouds. LH: The first ever Evo Roof Match. What say you, Joe? LJB: It's a long way down from the top of the building. LH: Actually, there's chicken wire up there, Joe. LJB: Oh. Really? That's a shame. Inside Club Evo, the lights cut out and the room was suddenly pitched into blackness. The sound of a heartbeat penetrated the first floor of the Club, accompanied by a brief flash of dim red light. A few seconds later, the camera centered on the elevator at the sound of another heartbeat, once again accompanied by a red flash. Just after a third heartbeat, and a third red flash, red strobe lighting filled the ground floor. As the cameras reverted back to the roof, Gryfon appeared from the entrance to the locker rooms, the single door to the outside slamming shut behind him as he walked into the cool evening air. LH: Ooooooooh, spooky! The arena lights dimmed, giving way to green lights spiraling around the arena. On the monitor wall, a green spiral appeared, interspersed with shots of the rundown building known as the Asylum. Finally, the word Lost flew over the spiral as "Slow" by Professional Murder Music hit the speakers. Lost then came into the scene on top of the building, eyeing Gryfon from the moment he stepped outside. Referee Paul O’Sullivan applied a padlock to the door behind Lost (due to the match’s lack of rules, O’Sullivan would remain out of the competitor’s way), before signaling a bell inside the area to be sounded. LJB: Great. Now we'll leave them up there and cut back to the rest of the show. Fans inside the Club watched via the monitor wall as Lost advanced towards Gryfon, meeting him at the dead center of the roof with an elbow-and-collar tie up. They struggled for a moment, before Gryfon used his size advantage to back Lost against the cage wall. Lost attempted to break free, but Gryfon continued pushing backwards on Lost, crushing him against the steel. Finally, Gryfon lunged forward with a knee to Lost’s gut, causing the former tag champ to fall chest first to the concrete. Gryfon, thoughts of hate filling his head, knelt down beside Lost, and let him have it with several closed fists to the face. Lost covered his head, only to be pelted in the ribs with a forearm. LJB: Y'know what we need here, Livvie? LH: What Joe? LJB: John Madden's Magic Pen. LH: Yeah! Gryfon then stood, bringing one of his opponent’s legs with him. Moving quickly, he started to lock on a figure four, but Lost was quick to use his free leg to shove Gryfon into the cage. Lost rolled to his feet as Gryfon turned back to face him. Both men charged, full speed, towards each other. Lost met Gryfon with a Japanese arm drag, which sent him right down to the rough surface they fought on. Hardly fazed, Gryfon kipped right back up and turned back to Lost, intent on paying him back for the arm drag. However, Lost countered Gryfon’s clothesline with a pick-up and what looked to be a set up for a spinebuster. Before Gryfon could be raised, though, he ripped into Lost’s side with a hard soccer kick, doubling Lost over. Gryfon capitalized with a stellar DDT to follow. LJB: For instance, if we had Madden's Magic pen, we could stop all of this and draw those little cuss-word symbol thingies over Lost's head. LH: And maybe you'd write "Wheeeee!" over Gryfon's head. Lost slowly rolled onto his stomach, which proved to be a bad move as Gryfon, who had just reached his feet again, performed a leg drop across Lost’s chest. After a moment of grabbing his tailbone in pain, Gryfon snatched up Lost’s nearest arm, and fell back, locking on an arm bar. Lost let out a few wails of agony, reaching out for the ropes, only to realize there were none. As Gryfon wrenched back harder, Lost threw his free arm across himself, catching Gryfon in the stomach. Gryfon loosened his grip for a moment, allowing Lost to roll free. Before Gryfon could stand from his seated position, Lost smacked him across the face with a horribly loud kick, before performing a roll over neck whip from behind. LH: And you'd write "Boing!" right there. LJB: Yeah. And then I would write at length in regards to my feelings on that move. The words [censor] and [censor] would be involved much. Lost grabbed Gryfon by the hair and yanked him to his feet, where Lost applied a standing abdominal stretch. Lost twisted Gryfon’s body violently, bringing Gryfon to grunt in sufferance. After a moment or two, Gryfon noticed his closeness to the cage, and grabbed hold the wall with his free hand. He then tugged Lost off of him, and into a hiptoss, which send Lost backfirst and upside-down into the cage! Lost fell back onto his stomach, as Gryfon staggered away, holding his ribs in pain. LH: These guys are so PRETTY when they're in PAIN!!! Whoooooo! Gryfon then stalked back over to Lost, pulling him up by his blond, spiky hair. After a pair of kicks to the gut, Gryfon wrapped his arms around Lost, and pulled off a dangerous belly-to-belly suplex out of nowhere! Both men hit the roof hard (though Lost hit MUCH harder), and let out individual shouts of pain and frustration. LJB: I think the regular mat should be made out of the same thing as the roof. LH: Joe! That'd be brutal. LJB: So!?! Both men had expected a grueling encounter, but neither looked truly prepared for this. Though the concrete of the roof was rubberized, that only prevented them from getting abrasions when they hit it - it did nothing to soften the impact when they landed. LJB: Were the mats like the roof, these schmucks would beat the crap out of the opponent AND themselves. That makes for shorter matches, quicker turn-around times, more injuries, more new people, more laughter, more poetic justice... LH: Like... a carnage factory? LJB: YEAH! As they slowly got back to their feet, Gryfon mere seconds faster than Lost, the former Apostle found himself pulled into an Irish whip and then sent charging towards the wall. Lost put on the brakes moments before slamming into the wall, but before he could turn around, he found himself the victim of a clothesline from Gryfon that sandwiched him into the wall of the cage. With a triumphant shout, Gryfon pulled Lost into an inverted front facelock before quickly dropping him to the roof with a falling inverted DDT. Confident that victory was his, Gryfon moved in to apply the Cry for Mercy dragon sleeper hold with leg scissors, only to have Lost shoot a thumb into his eye and then quickly spin free. Lost rolled the blinded Gryfon to his stomach and then locked in the Indian deathlock that was the start of the Revelation hold that had been taught to him by his cousin Michael Gabrielle. LH: Looks like we got a submission hold... LJB: I wish there wasn't chicken wire keeping them from pushing each other off the roof... LH: Joe! That's horrible! Before Lost could complete the hold with an inverted front facelock, Gryfon turned his shoulders and fired a hard elbow into Lost’s stomach area, catching the former LCW tag champion in the solar plexus and driving the wind from his lungs. As Lost quickly tried to suck air back in, Gryfon disentangled himself and connected with a particularly brutal clothesline to the throat that further prevented Lost from regaining his wind. As Gryfon moved around behind him to once again apply the Cry for Mercy, Lost happened to look up and he saw a pair of shapes on the roof, mostly hidden in the shadows but still visible enough to Lost, who was too stunned to fight out of Gryfon’s hold. Gryfon fell backwards as he applied the leg scissors that completed the hold, and though he experienced a shooting pain in his back, he smiled with satisfaction at the dull thud that had accompanied Lost’s head impacted the roof. For the first time in the match, Paul O’Sullivan’s services were necessary, and he quickly moved into position to check on Lost. LH: Oh! Ow! LJB: Gryfon looks pretty pleased with himself. He also looks like an outrageous dork, but a very pleased dork. O’Sullivan was startled to see that Lost had been knocked unconscious by the impact, and he immediately called for the bell to be rung once more, giving the victory to Gryfon. The exhausted winner, fresh off of arguably the biggest win of his LCW career, released his hold on Lost and got to his feet, where he staggered to the still-padlocked door to the locker room area. As O’Sullivan opened the door, Gryfon fell through it and crawled down the hallway to his locker room, clearly feeling the effects of the match much more so now that the adrenaline was starting to wear off. LH: Wow! That was great. LJB: Heh. Love it when they crawl away instead of walkin'. Winner by Submission: Gryfon (Match Time: 8:14) Click Here for the Next Segment
