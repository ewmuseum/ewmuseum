Impulse 2.1: Legend?

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Legend? The lights went out in the club, except for two spotlights on opposite ends of the far wall. They shone on TR Saxton, who emerged from the first floor elevator and strode out in a thick black leather jacket to the cadence of Diamond Head's "Am I Evil?". On the monitors, video played of him in the more momentous hardcore matches of his career, one screen at a time, while cycling to the right and down. Then, the screen became a single image, one of a bloodied TR holding up the LCW hardcore title. The spotlights went off along with the leather jacket, and the house lights returned to normal. TR moves the microphone to his lips, which curl into a derisive smile. "Now, typically when a guy moves over to a rival, he goes and cuts a 'shoot' promo. Screw that, I'm a professional, I'm not ragging on Mercury just because the guy running it is fifteen pounds of crap in a five ounce zip-lock baggie. I'm here tonight to tell all of you paying customers, and all you guys in the lockers, what to expect out of TR Saxton." After a pause to allow the crowd to die down slightly. In spite of his history, TR was surprisingly popular with these fans, who had long been known for their thirst for blood and violence, two things Saxton had built his reputation on. "See, Evo likes to refer to its stars as 'legends'; yet, I don't even see five guys who really fit the bill. For instance, hardly anybody ever cuts a promo in this place. For another thing, you've either got rookies who are about ten months away from stardom, or guys who have seen better days, and the only three who don't fit in those categories are Heimdal, Wallace and Chambers, all of whom I could mop the ring with any time I want to." While this drew a slight boo, Saxton didn't seemed phased and continued unabated. "None of them are legendary." Saxton smirked at LJB. "None of them, at least to me, seem to be leaving a mark on this industry. So, it's come to this man to attempt to form some sort of legacy for himself, and in the process do something memorable. However, I'm not going to start at the top." Without an entrance, because he didn't warn the tech crew that he would be coming out, Used walked to the ring. TR was facing away from him, and didn't turn around despite the crowd's reaction to Used. "I'm not going to work my way up through the Cases and Devolas and Gryfons, either. I'm going to start right in the middle, with a guy who is everything that a legend should NOT be." As Saxton continued, the target of his tirade very clear, Used stepped between the ropes, mic in his hand. "I'm going to begin the legend of TR Saxton in Evo by demolishing none other than -" Saxton turned around to find Used three inches from his face. "- You." Used smacked TR in the face with his mic! TR tried the same, but Used parried TR's mic with his own such that TR's flew into the crowd, allowing Used to waffle TR with his mic repeatedly, until TR was sent tumbling out of the ring over the top rope! Used went out after him, only to be caught off guard by a wild lariat from the former Mercury star. TR grabbed Used by the neck and the seat of his pants, pivoted, and sent him into the crowd none too gently. Before security could step in, Saxton and Used fought their way through the fans and into a mass of humanity, at which point they were surrounded by too many fans for the cameras to keep following them!
