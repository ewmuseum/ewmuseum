LCW Evo Impulse Reckoning Alexander Lawson walked down the plush hallway that led from his locker room to the elevator that would carry him to the first floor of the club. He was slated to take on Doctor David Chambers in just a few short minutes, and that left him with little time to formulate his game plan. He had hoped to begin making reparations when he had resumed day-to-day control of his body again, but hadn't had the courage to approach Doc since that day, and besides, he wasn't sure if he'd be able to find him. Deep down, Alex knew he was lying to himself, as he knew that Chambers was exactly where he had himself told Chambers to go to escape Felony. Livvie's house. And Alex knew that he couldn't yet stand to be there, to see the damage that he had wrought, not without being able to ensure that it wouldn't happen again. That was why he needed to get through to Doc tonight, so that he could resume his therapy. He was tired of spending every moment in constant fear of what would happen if Felony were to retake control. He was tired of worrying about what the dark side of his consciousness had done while he was its prisoner. And most of all, he was tired of living the dual life that had been forced so violently upon him nearly a year ago. Over the summer, the warring personalities had nearly driven him to the point of true insanity, but he had managed to take control and get things in order. Then everything had come crashing down, and here he was, left in the ruins of his life, praying that the foundation that his happiness had been built upon hadn't been damaged in the violent maelstrom that was his Disassociative Identity Disorder.
