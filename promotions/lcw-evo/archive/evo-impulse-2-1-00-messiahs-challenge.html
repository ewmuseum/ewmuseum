Impulse 2.1: Messiah's Challenge

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Messiah's Challenge The lights in Club Evo began to flash rapidly, as "Chop Suey!" by System of a Down hit the speakers. The crowd launched into loud and enthusiastic cheering before the man behind that music was even announced: "Ladies and Gentlemen, your Evo Champion, DOCTOR...DAVID...CHAMBERS!" The elevator doors opened after descending from the locker room and David came walking out in his black leather jacket and hospital scrubs, Bear in tow as always. He entered the ring and raised his arms to the still-cheering crowd, smiling and intensely pleased as he stood in the center of the ring. James Cheong, looking bored and rather detached, approached David with a microphone. He waited for the applause to die down before addressing Chambers. "David, we've had you come out so we can talk to you a bit about how you feel after defending the title following the PPV match against Dean Wallace. How does it feel to still be the champ?" The crowd erupted again in various expressions of approval and congratulation. David smiled again, looking actually a bit embarrassed. "Thanks, guys." He said into the microphone that Cheong held up for him. The crowd cheered a bit more, and James rolled his eyes. (We can't, of course, know what Cheong was thinking at the time, but one could guess it was something akin to, "All right, enough already. I've got somewhere to be and I don't have time for this crowd-friendly crap!") When the cheering again died down for the champ, he spoke into the mic once more. "Well what can I say? I just wanted to prove that I still had it, that I was the champ for a REASON. That I deserved the gold; I'd earned it. And I did. I've beaten Wallace; he had his shot at the title and he couldn't best me in the ring. Just like the others who tried to take the gold from me. Who will try? I've proven I deserve to be here, and nobody...NOBODY is going to change it." At this the fans once again cheered for the Doc (he really is quite staggeringly popular; I guess people have a certain compassion for fallen psychiatrists), but the cheering was suddenly interrupted as "New Noise" by Refused hit the PA, heralding the entrance of the Lost and "The Angel of Death" Michael Gabrielle, communally known as the Tribulation Saints. Both men walked just far enough away from the elevator to be picked up by a spotlight, and Gabrielle produced a microphone of his own. "Well, if it isn't my old friend and current champion Doctor David Chambers," said Gabrielle with a sarcastic, caustic tone. "And if it isn't my old friend Michael Gabrielle? How've you been, old chum?" replied Chambers, throwing the sarcasm back at Gabrielle. "David, I've come out here with a very simple message tonight. We, the remaining members of the Asylum, have done away with our personal feuds, and now have a new focus. It's that gold belt in the ring, and the man it is attached to." The camera crew around ringside followed Gabrielle's lead and showed Chambers mid-ring with the Evo Title around his waist. "Now, we all sat down and had a nice long talk about who among us should go after you, didn't we Aaron?" Gabrielle shoved the microphone in his cousin's face, although it's clear Lost had no intention on being a part of the conversation, nor any idea what Michael is talking about. "Uhh...yeah, yeah we did." "And we came to a unanimous decision, didn't we?" "Oh yeah, nice and unanimous," replied Lost, still very clearly living up to his name on this topic. "And that unanimous decision was this David: the number one contender for the Evo Title is none other than..." Club Evo held its collective breath, David Chambers steeled himself for the announcement, and even Lost perks up to hear the name. "Yours truly, Michael Gabrielle. And David, when that time comes, and I show you what true wrestling is all about, I will not be your angel of death, I will be your unquestioned Messiah." Finished, AoD turned abruptly and walked out of sight of the spectators at Club Evo, who booed loudly as he exited; Lost following him quickly, perhaps a mite confused. Cheong, silent and a little frightened during this encounter, turned back to Chambers, who only smiled slightly. "Pretty strong words, David. Anything to say?" David shrugged and spoke simply into the microphone: "If that's what he wants…I'd be glad to prove myself yet again."
