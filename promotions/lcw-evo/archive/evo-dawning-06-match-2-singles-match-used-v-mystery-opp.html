Dawning: Match 2, Singles Match: Used v Mystery Opponent

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Match 2, Singles Match: Used v Mystery Opponent LJB: I know who the Mystery Opponent is, Livvie. Would you like me to tell you? LH: Enlighten me, Joe. LJB: It's Used v Used in an amazing display of his inherent laziness! He has to motivate himself! Let's see how this goes... Club Evo went completely dark except for the video monitors. On these screens, a close-up of a cigarette burning in a man's mouth was shown. The mouth inhaled, then coughed wildly, as the words "How's Your Neighbor" scrolled across the screen, surrounded by smoke. Video clips of Used's career highlights could be made out through the "smoke" on the screens, and the opening chords to Alice in Chains' "Again" remix pumped through the Club sound system. A single floodlight appeared from the wall behind the bar, directly in the center of it. Slowly, Used appeared from the ground behind the bar, his darkened outline the only thing visible due to the blindingly bright white light behind him. He rose all the way up until his feet were level with the bar, and he then walked out and stood at its edge. With his face to the ring, he put his head down and extended both hands out at a 45 degree downward angle, his index finger and thumb sticking out like a kid's imaginary finger-gun. He held the pose for a moment, then dropped his arms and crouched on the edge of the bar for a moment before launching himself into the air. He landed in the arms of the crowd at ringside, which passed him like a body surfer, face down, to the edge of the ring, where he landed on his feet. At the moment he landed, a green laser shot straight up from each turnbuckle before spinning around the room randomly as the floodlight at the bar died and Used took to his corner, looking around for his opponent for the night, a man whose identity was still unknown to him, now just minutes before his match. Used sighed, but he was resigned to his fate and was more than ready to face anyone. LH: And now...The Other Guy! LJB: It's Used I say! Used! I wish it weren't true, but it is! The music that filled the Club next was new to Used and the crowd in attendance, but the face that appeared on the video wall was not. The lights went out in the club, except for two spotlights on opposite ends of the far wall. They shone on TR Saxton, who emerged from the first floor elevator and strode out in a thick black leather jacket to the cadence of Diamond Head's "Am I Evil?". On the monitors, video played of him in the more momentous hardcore matches of his career, one screen at a time, while cycling to the right and down. Then, the screen became a single image, one of a bloodied TR holding up the LCW hardcore title. The spotlights went off along with the leather jacket, and the house lights returned to normal. LH: That sure don't look like another Used, Joe. That looks more like a TR Saxton...it is! It is a TR Saxton! The club's patrons, realizing that this was the first wrestler to jump between halves of what was once whole, gave a standing ovation. Used groaned inwardly, but not so much in front of the camera. LJB: Maybe Used v Used wouldn't be so bad after all... Referee Jason Sanders took his dear sweet time in patting down the combatants and explaining the rules clearly, as he remembered what happened the last time this pair of rulebreakers went at it. Once they had both checked out as being clean, he backed away and signaled for the bell. TR Saxton and Used began the fight by fighting. They threw punches at each other for a good forty seconds (to no visible effect), until Saxton grabbed Used's head and left arm for an amateur-style takedown, leading to more punching. Used reversed that by kneeing Saxton in the groinus maximus, drawing an enormous amount of complaint from Sanders. Used simply shrugged in reply. LH: TR gets it in the peepee! Ow! LJB: You never tire of that word, do you? LH: No! I don't! Say it Joe! LJB: ...peepee? LH: Hee! Used gave Saxton an Irish whip to a corner, then when TR stumbled out, he followed up with a boot to the gut and a double-axehandle between the shoulderblades. Used planted Saxton with a DDT and rolled him over to cover, but before Sanders even got in position to count, Saxton threw Used off and got to his feet. Saxton screamed, "Is that it?" at the top of his lungs, while the tension in his body was clearly rising by the second. Used shrugged again and went to work with punches, then a knee lift segued into a Russian legsweep. A cover, but again, not even a one-count when Saxton kicked out violently and got to his feet. Used...shrugged. LH: Used doesn't care! LJB: He's really putting what's-his-name off, don't ya think? The third shrug set TR off completely, as he decked Used with a tremendously hard diving lariat, then grabbed his opponent’s head and started slamming it on the mat as hard as he could. Jason Sanders considered the "head slam of doom" off-limits, but when he moved to enforce said belief, Saxton shoved him onto his backside. If you thought Used's low blow got him steamed, this really put Sanders in a foul mood, and he came close to ending the match right there. By the time he came to the decision to continue the match, Saxton had already Irish whipped Used and executed a spear hard enough to send both men between the second and third ropes, to the outside! LH: Now THAT'S what I'm talkin' about! Check that out! LJB: They're trying to kill each other. Excellent. As much as both men are able to absorb pain and look cool, they still needed time to catch their breaths after a stunt like that. TR recovered first (naturally), and he propped Used up on the apron. Saxton ran to the barricade and back, delivering a nasty spear such that Saxton wound up going under the apron, and Used wound up clutching his ribs in pain. TR climbed out from beneath the ring shortly thereafter, and he tossed Used back in to the ring to beat the 20-count. Sanders gave Saxton a lecture about staying in the ring, only to shut up with a single steely glare from TR. Meanwhile, Used crawled to a corner and pulled himself up such that he faced the turnbuckles. This turned out to be a bad choice, especially when Saxton sprinted to the far corner and built up momentum for the most brutal spear yet, this one to the small of Used's back such that it nearly popped his vertebrae out through his chest! LH: Well, Saxton's certainly got the 'You're a worm" look down pat. LJB: Who? LH: Saxton. The guy that's presently duking it out with Used. LJB: Oh yeah. Saxton. I forgot. The crowd gave a "holy s**t!" chant for the impact of the move, only to quiet down when Ophy waddled to ringside, looking for all the world to be extremely pregnant. Sanders told her that it would be best if she left, but her only concern was her husband's safety. Saxton, feeling exactly the opposite about Used's welfare, put him on the top turnbuckle facing the crowd. Saxton followed, and once on the second rope, he slapped on a cobra clutch. Used started thrashing the instant that happened, and he violently threw himself into a back headbutt which jarred Saxton loose. Three more sent TR to the canvas, allowing Used to stand up and turn around, then leap off for a gorgeously painful senton bomb to TR's ribcage! LH: Oooh, TR's gonna be a bag of broken glass! Yow! LJB: Who? LH: TR SAXTON. Got hit. By Used. LJB: I KNOW who Used is. He's the red-headed slacker in the fruity net shirt. Though he was gladly covering Saxton, Used suddenly found himself in far too much discomfort to hold TR down due to the repeated jamming of Saxton's shoulder into the middle of his body. Ophy, ever the diligent wife, crawled into the ring to check on her sweetie. Used whispered something to her, and she nodded and went back to ringside. Jason Sanders was by this time having an absolute fit, convinced that Used and TR had bent the rules as far as they could without breaking them. Used Irish whipped Saxton to the ropes, and as he came off, Used executed a textbook Thesz press in honor of the recovering legend. In mid-press, Used caught something thrown to him from Ophy. Mere seconds later, he drove said object into TR Saxton's head repeatedly... The Shoot! By this point, he had had enough, and Jason Sanders gave up and finally called for the bell. LH: Ophy! Why'd ya go and do that?!? Winner by Disqualification: TR Saxton (Match Time: 7:49) LJB: That was very very painful to watch. LH: What was? TR getting hit in the head with a mike? LJB: No. That match. It was painful to watch. Like all the other ones. I'm in hell. Used kept jamming the mic into Saxton's head until a bit of nonwhite color (re: red) could be seen, and he then did it a few more times after that before using it for its intended purpose. Used: Is that it, TiaRa? Huh? Used gave TR a Greco-Roman pimp slap for emphasis, but then his eyes went wide and he slowly fell down; Saxton had returned the favor and kneed him in the junk. Ophy yelled "not the taint!", and TR Saxton called for a ring monkey to hand him his leather jacket. Rather than put it on, he merely fished around in the pockets for his faithful brass knuckles. Used slowly got to his feet, TR pulled his right hand back, and Jason Sanders blocked the punch! Saxton, ever resourceful, simply used brute strength to toss Sanders into Used, leaving Used wide open for a brass knuckle sandwich to the nose! Used stumbled back against the ropes, and Saxton walloped him with another haymaker to send him soaring over the top and to the outside. LJB: Hey. Guys. The bell rang. Get the [censor] out of the ring! Used, oddly enough, popped right back up and rolled into the ring. He motioned for TR to fight him without the 'punch aids', so Saxton took them off... and threw them as hard as he could at Ophy's head! Well, that certainly wasn’t going to stand, and Used speared Saxton down en route to the land of punchtopia, since there hadn't been much punching yet. Ophy managed to block with her forearms, but it would still leave a bruise later, and that was all it took to draw Randall Leary's ire. Twenty seconds later, Saxton wrapped his arms around Used's waist and used them to roll over, reversing the situation so that it was his turn to do the hitting. Ophy reentered the ring with all the grace she could muster and tried to stop Saxton's right hand from going into her hubby's face any more, only to be roughly thrown to the ground! LH: Okay. I know Ophy handed an international object to her husband, and I know she really shouldn't be there at all, but I think that TR should know better than to rough up a pregnant woman. LJB: Yeah, Livvie. That guy's pretty mean. Whoever he is... A slew of Evo officials, a mix of retired wrestlers and referees, ran out to stop the madness. By the time they got there, Used was back on the offense, and Saxton's minor forehead cut had been opened up quite nicely. The combatants were restrained, and the crowd chanted "Let them go! Let them go!", not wanting to wait to see this conflict resolved. Sadly, they did have to wait, as the two were dragged backstage by force. LJB: Well okay then. That's that. I hope. Please god, let that be it. LH: No. This is when the elevator doors open, and TR and Used spill out into the fans, riding in on waves of mutilation and Evo fans, leaving a massive trail of destruction, carnage and cannibalism in their wake!
