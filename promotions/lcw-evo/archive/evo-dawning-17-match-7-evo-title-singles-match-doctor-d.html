Dawning: Match 7, Evo Title Singles Match: Doctor David Chambers (c) v Dean Wallace

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Match 7, Evo Title Singles Match: Doctor David Chambers (c) v Dean Wallace LH: And now, the long awaited main event. The crowd's gettin' all antsy. LJB: Why? LH: Joe, if you don't know why, I'm not going to tell you. The tension in the arena was palpable. The buzz in the crowd became a dull roar as ring announcer Neel Shah stepped into the ring. Shah: Ladies and Gentlemen, the next match is scheduled for one fall and is for the LCW Evo WORLD Championship! The crowd began to rock Club Evo in anticipation. Those cheers, however, quickly turned to boos. The lights at Club Evo dimmed to a deep gold, and trumpets blared with a bass line in the back. On the screen, shots of a man obscured by shadows were shown, performing moves. As the music kicked in, these words were rapped over the PA system… “I am what you want to be, but you can’t though, prime example…” With the last two words, the words “PRIME EXAMPLE” appeared on the screen, followed by the logo of Dean Wallace. The man in the video was then illuminated, performing moves and destroying opponents as Talib Kweli’s “Prime Example” played over the loudspeaker. Smoke engulfed the entrance area, and out of it stepped Dean Wallace, black baseball bat in hand. Wallace, his eyes obscured by his wrap-around sunglasses, surveyed the crowd, who was not giving him a favorable reaction. He strode out to the ring in a calm manner. Behind him, his video alternated from action shots to stills, from profiles to the four words “PRIME EXAMPLE DEAN WALLACE”. He rolled into the ring, removed his glasses, cap, and ASU jersey, and handed them to a ring attendant. He then began to stretch himself in the ring ropes. LJB: Prime example of what? How to be a loser and a tool? Shah: Introducing first, the challenger, from Tempe, Arizona, weighing in at 230 pounds… DEEEEEAN WALLACE! The boos from the crowd resounded, but Dean refused to acknowledge them. He simply stretched himself out, trying to keep himself loose. Then, the lights in Club Evo went out, "Chop Suey" by System of a Down hit and the crowd noise got as loud as it had ever been inside the Club. The laser lights came on, spinning around the room in a multicolored splendor. When the drums came in, the monitors started turning on, one at a time at random, showing piece-by-piece a dark green swirling spiral with Bear the Bear in the center. "The Doctor" was written in blood-red block letters just beneath Bear's shape on the screen. Just as the main riff started, the monitors went black and the words "I Have Awakened" appeared in big white letters. Bright lights hit the entrance and Doctor David Chambers came walking confidently out towards the ring in a black leather jacket, Bear clutched in one hand. His light green hospital scrubs were spotlessly clean, and his formerly long black hair was short around his head; his feet were clad in bright white sneakers. The lights followed him up into the ring as the monitors showed various shots of Doc in action. He removed the jacket, revealing the writing still scribbled along his arms: "I Am Everything." He put Bear off to the side of the ring, in easy reach for a Steelhead, and walked to the corner opposite his opponent before raising his arms to the crowd. Shah: And now in the ring, the LCW Evo WORLD Champion, weighing in at 200 pounds… The Doctor… DAAAAVID…. CHAAAAAMBERS! LJB: There are some perfectly normal guys out there waiting for their shot in this line of work, and look at what gets the airtime. Man, where the HELL do they find these guys? LH: Oh, it's amazing what you find when you don't open your fridge for five months. And sometimes you find the damdest things hanging out on your front porch. Both men approached the center of the ring. A chant of “Cham-bers!” seemed set to blow the roof off of the Club. The two competitors grew closer and closer together, Dean and David exchanging harsh words. Referee Bobby Borashi attempted to explain the rules of the match, but the two ignored him and continued to jaw at each other. LJB: What do you suppose they're saying to each other, Livvie? LH: I think they're exchanging recipes. Borashi finished talking, and so did Dean and David. They simply stared at each other, breathing hard, waiting for the other to move first. It was Dean who broke the ice, firing off a left. But Chambers saw it coming, blocked it, and caught him flush with a hard right. Dean swayed, and Doc took the opportunity to charge him hard and tackle him, pounding away at his face in retaliation for the post-match attacks Wallace had dealt out the past two weeks. LH: David's punchin.' This is sometimes followed up by more punchin', and, on the rare occasion, The Kick. Dean wouldn’t allow himself to be a pushover, and after a few rights and lefts glanced off his forehead, overpowered Chambers and rolled him over. Now on top, Wallace fired off hard lefts, and the crowd noise shifted from cheers to jeers. LJB: And Dean is also punchin.' Look at him go. He sure is punchin.' Yep. Chambers shoved Dean off of him and quickly got to his feet. The two men stared each other down, and the “Cham-bers!” chant fired up again, even louder. Chambers charged with a clothesline, but Wallace ducked. Wallace turned and flew towards Chambers with a leg lariat, but David caught it and rolled Dean through with a dragon screw, wrenching Dean’s right knee. Dean hurried up as quick as he could, only to eat a boot to the chest. David grabbed a bent-over Wallace by the hand, and whipped him towards the ropes, but Dean reversed. Chambers was sent into the ropes, and was sent down hard on his way back, courtesy a spinebuster that shook the ring and quieted the crowd. Dean held on to David’s legs and bridged. LJB: Wouldn't it be nice if this match was over? 1… 2… LJB: But alas, I am in hell. Joe gets no break. Doc bridged out, using his hands to complete a handstand. However, he had had the presence of mind to hold on to Wallace’s head with his legs, and, as he stood on his hands, Wallace was forced to his feet. Chambers fired forwards, sending Dean flying and scoring with a headscissors takedown! Again, Doc managed to hang on to Dean’s head, and rolled himself on top of Dean, sitting on top of him with his legs hooked. LH: Wow! LJB: Eh. It would have been better if he'd thrown Wallace out of the ring. 1… 2… Dean rocked forwards and into a pin of his own! LJB: But Dean could throw Doc out of the ring. I don't have a problem with that. 1… 2… Doc powered out! Both men kipped up to their feet, and renewed their seemed staring contest, as the crowd showed its appreciation with a standing ovation. That ovation quickly turned into a standing boo as Dean rushed Doc and dropkicked him out of the ring and on to the floor! LJB: There. That'll work. Dean followed the champion outside of the ring, and laid into Doc with hard stomps, not giving Chambers any leeway to get to his feet. Dean grabbed Chambers’ hand and whipped him towards the corner. Seconds later, David went plowing into the steel steps, dislodging them. David lay crumpled in a heap, but Dean wasn’t done yet. He approached a prone Chambers, picked him up into a headlock, and snap suplexed Chambers onto the floor mats. Doc writhed in pain, clutching at his back, which was now red and bruising. LJB: Well, Chambers should be used to this kind of treatment from Dean by now, huh? LH: *sigh* Yes, Joe. He should be. Dean went to pound away at Chambers, but David was ready to fight back. He kneed Wallace hard in the gut as Wallace knelt to hammer away at him, and the blow caused Wallace to back off. Chambers attempted to use the side of the ring to prop himself up, but all he did was leave himself a prime target for a vicious knife-edged chop from Wallace. Wallace then went for another chop, but this time Chambers dropped down and rolled himself into Wallace’s knee, causing Dean to fall forward and bash his face against the apron! LH: Whoops! Not the face! Not the face! LJB: YES the face! YES the face! With Wallace clutching at his nose to check for blood, Chambers threw him into the retaining wall and began to lay into him with hard chops of his own. The blows rained off of Dean, and his chest began to redden. Finally, Chambers whipped Dean back into the ring, and followed suit, stomping away at the challenger. Chambers charitably allowed Wallace a moment to stand up before locking Dean’s head and dropping him with a DDT! He floated over to cover, and Borashi slid over to make the count. LJB: We gotta DDT... 1… 2… LH: And Doc's got nothin.' No dice. But Chambers, undeterred, covered again. 1… Kickout by Wallace. LJB: And there's some more nothin.' All at once, all for him. Chambers picked Wallace up by his head and whipped him to the ropes. On his way back, Dean saw a bent over David Chambers, seemingly telegraphing his move. Dean stopped, looking for a pump kick, but he was stopped short by an elbow to the gut! Dean keeled over, and Doc hoisted the Closer up onto his shoulders. He spun around and dropped him neck-first with a Death Valley Driver! Cover… 1… 2… Wallace thrust a shoulder up. LH: Kick-out of a Death Valley Driver... LJB: Y'know, I'm very dissappointed about the name of that move. "Death Valley" implies that something would die there. However, you'll notice that that move was hardly the end of Wallace. Again, David picked Dean up by the head and applied a headlock. He lifted Dean straight up in the air and held him there for a good 5 seconds before falling backwards, slamming Dean, and floating over for another cover. 1… 2… LJB: I am in so much pain right now. Really. LH: Wanna kiss? Kickout! Wallace powered out of the pin. This time, along with the crowd, David verbalized his disagreement, discussing what he believed to be a slow count with the official Borashi. This brief moment of bickering allowed Dean time to get to his feet. As Doc turned around, Dean kneed him in the gut and applied a standing headscissors. He hooked both of Doc’s arms, and the ShutOut looked imminent. What Dean had not counted on was Doc being able to somehow twist his arms free, and end up back to back with Wallace, each wrestler jockeying for a backslide rollup. It was Dean that had the weight advantage, and he thus was able to gain the necessary leverage to roll David up. 1… 2… LH: That looks vaguely pornographic... LJB: Hey! You and I never watched porn! LH: Oh, I got something great in mind. It's called "Boys on Mars." Not this time! David powered out, and the crowd loved it. Dean, of course, did not. He allowed Doc a moment to regain his footing before unleashing a hard pump kick right to Chambers’ face. The impact sent sweat and a trickle of blood flying from David’s dome, and it left him bent over. This time, Dean was quick to apply another standing headscissors, and hook the arms. This time, he hoisted David up vertically, held him there… and dropped him, hard, head first. LJB: Looks like that'll be the match. Doc had been Shutout. The crowd grew deathly silent. Dean smiled for the first time all night. He stood just above David’s head. To throw salt in the wound, Dean got in a crouch, and, much like an umpire would, signaled David Chambers out on strikes, punching the air with his right hand first, then his left. This action did not endear Dean any further to the Club Evo crowd, and there were scattered boos, but mostly a sense of hopelessness filled the Club. Dean dropped down, and covered. Borashi started the count. 1… 2… … LJB: ... LH: ...uh...kick out? The silence in Club Evo was due to the shock. For the first time, somebody had kicked out of the ShutOut. That man was Doctor David Chambers. After the shock value wore off, the crowd erupted. So did Dean, but in a different way altogether. He screamed at Borashi, flailed his arms, pounded the mat, stomped his feet. All of his actions seemed to only serve to make the crowd louder. LJB: Aw, come on Dean. What would Jesus do? All that was left for Dean to do was haul Doc up and continue the assault. He fired several hard lefts at Doc’s forehead, then grabbed his hand and whipped him to the ropes. On the way back, Doc flew at Dean, but Dean sidestepped so that Doc was perpendicular to him sideways. Dean grabbed the backs of Doc’s legs on the way by and hooked them between his armpits. As David’s head dangled, Dean attempted to force David’s head between his legs. The attempt was successful. LJB: All, pinfall attempt- LH: Buuuuuut! Wallace now had Chambers right where he wanted him. Trouble was, Chambers also has Wallace in a perfect position. Before Dean could sit out, Chambers rocked forwards and into a bridge! 1… 2… Wallace powered out! Both men kipped up quickly, and rushed each other, peppering each other with hard shots. They traded punches in the center of the ring until Wallace saw a big right coming and ducked it. What he failed to see was a stiff side kick headed towards his temple. The kick met its mark, and Wallace dropped like a sack of potatoes. LJB: A nice face rearanger. LH: What's so nice about it? LJB: It might be the last one we see tonight. As Dean crawled to a corner to help himself over, David was also headed towards a corner. But his purpose was different. His intention had fur, one eye, and a steel ball in its chest. Dean hauled himself up in the corner just as Chambers held Bear up for everyone to see. Dean made a hard blind charge, and he never saw the bear coming. The dull thud echoed throughout Club Evo but was soon drowned out by the roar of the crowd. Doc had connected with Steelhead, and the outcome was all but assured. Doc covered. 1… 2… 2 and a half… 2 and 3 quarters… No! No! No! Wallace thrust a shoulder up at the last possible instant! The crowd sucked in its collective breath, and all Chambers could do was shake his head in disbelief. LJB: Bear says, "Oh drat and double drat. Hit him again." Dean writhed in pain on the mat, clutching at the brand new lump on his head. Doc didn’t let up, stomping at his downed opponent. When Wallace was face down, he slid by him and hooked an arm, and looked to grab the other just as quickly. The fans rose to their feet in anticipation for Anesthesia, but Dean scrambled to the ropes. Having his legs under him helped him gain enough leverage to reach the ropes and grab hold. Borashi counted to four, then forcibly removed Chambers from Wallace. LJB: Borashi does things like punches wrestlers. I think that's great. LH: Why? LJB: Because, wrestlers deserve to be hit. LH: More than usually? LJB: Yep. As Doc got up, Dean was quick with a swipe to the knee with his leg. David stumbled, and Dean was up in a hurry. He went behind Chambers and hooked his legs, leg-sweeping the champ down. Chambers kipped up quickly, and charged with a clothesline. Wallace ducked, then took the opportunity to apply a rear waistlock and send Chambers over his head with a release German suplex. Chambers landed hard on his neck, and lay still. LH: Some suplex action, imported with some St. Pauli Girl. Dean stayed on the warpath, grabbing David’s right leg and picking him up by it. Dean then slammed David knee-first into the mat. He repeated this twice more, and each time emitted a groan from Chambers and the crowd. Finally, Dean hooked that right leg with his own, and fell to the side. It was Seventh Inning Stretch time at Club Neo, and the fans indeed rose to their feet, but instead of singing, they hollered their support to Chambers. There was one problem. Doc was facing in the opposite direction of the ropes. LJB: Uh oh. LH: I figure Doc could use a cigarette right about now. LJB: He doesn't smoke. LH: Oh, moments like these are reasons to start. David tried desperately to wriggle free, but did not have an easy time of it. Bobby Borashi knelt beside him, checking on Chambers’ condition. This, of course, meant he did not see Dean Wallace holding onto the bottom rope for dear life. LJB: And Borashi catches Dean in the act, and decides to break it up with a second rope elbow drop. LH: He wouldn't do that. Would he? The added leverage was putting intense pressure on Doc’s right leg and knee, and he tried even harder to get free. His face was a mask of pain. He strained for the ropes, not knowing that Dean sat on the mat holding the ropes. Finally, Doc made one last desperate grab for the bottom rope, and missed. As soon as his hand hit the mat, he began to signal his submission. However, this brought a favorable crowd reaction, because Borashi didn’t see it. He was too busy kicking Dean’s hand off of the ropes and forcing Wallace to break the hold. Wallace reluctantly released David’s leg, and David crawled over to the ropes, trying to help himself up. LH: See! He just kicked him a lot. Chambers hauled himself up not knowing that behind him Dean was springing off the ropes. Wallace ran up from behind, grabbing Chambers’ neck as he passed and hitting an ace crusher. The Cut Above. Wallace covered. 1… 2… Doc kicked out. Dean was incredulous. LH: And in the moring that came after, there will be advil, and much advil will there be. Dean mounted David and looked to work him over with punches, but Doc rolled over and returned fire. David stood up directly above him, and dropped a knee right in Dean’s chest, drawing a nostalgic “Hooo!” from the crowd. Wallace tried to scramble away, but Chambers picked him up by his hair. David whipped him into the ropes and caught him with a knee to the gut on his way back. Just as quickly as the knee to the gut had unfolded, Dean was down, dropped on his head courtesy a stump-pulling piledriver from Chambers! He flopped on top for the cover and hooked a leg. 1… 2… Dean-ied (pardon the pun)! Wallace kicked out of the pin. Chambers signaled his disagreement, but quickly returned to work. As Dean was getting up, he wrenched his arm behind him. Dean dropped to the ground, rolled forward, and flipped out, straightening his arm. Dean then returned the favor to David’s left arm. When Chambers tried to reverse, Dean pulled him into him and launched him with a belly-to-belly suplex! LJB: I have this strange urge to puke. It's like someone said something really disgusting just a few moments ago during the kick out, but I didn't hear a thing. LH: Well whoever it was, you should be ashamed. With Chambers down, Wallace headed up top. He one-hopped the turnbuckle, and was ready to fly. But Chambers had recovered sufficiently to stumble forwards and bump the corner, crotching Wallace on the top buckle! Wallace clutched at his unit in pain, and Chambers decided to follow Dean to the top. LH: Oooaaaoh! The pee-pee! The pee-pee! My God, the pee-pee! Chambers sat Dean on the buckle and fired off a few short rights to Dean’s head to keep him off balance. He then leaped onto Dean’s shoulders, and began his descent, flipping backwards and looking to score with a frankensteiner. But Dean, in midair, grabbed Doc’s tights, and Wallace ended up powerbombing Chambers from the top! LJB: Yeah! Now that's something I like to see! Dean hopped right back up, staggered, and flopped backwards on top of Chambers. Borashi slid over for the cover. 1… 2… Reversed! Dean had left his arms in such a position that Chambers hooked them and rolled Wallace up with a crucifix pin! 1… 2… Dean kicked out of the pinning predicament the only way he could: by rolling backwards. But Doc rolled with him. Wallace ended up face down, with Chambers on top… and both of his arms hooked. One leg-scissor later, and Dean was being Anethesized. Dean screamed in pain, but he had nowhere to go. Doc wrenched back harder on Dean’s arms. Dean tried everything short of signaling his submission to get free of the hold. Nothing was working. Chambers cinched in the leg-scissor tighter. Wallace refused to tap. Chambers pulled back harder on Dean’s back. Still, Wallace would not give up. Convinced there had to be a way out, Dean kept looking for it, seemingly ignoring the pain for the time being. Finally, Chambers pulled back as far as he could. LJB: Choices choices choices. Oftentimes, these are the moments that people regret for the rest of their lives. The crowd, which had been getting louder with every passing second, drowned out the ‘pop’ in Dean Wallace’s right shoulder, but only slightly. Chambers withdrew slightly, startled. The look on Dean’s face slowly went to one of a resigned look - a mixture of pain and dread. With his still-together left arm, Dean reached back and tapped his left shoulder three times. Borashi signaled for the bell to be rung. LH: Oops! LJB: Oh dear, I believe Dean won't be throwing fastballs with that arm anytime soon. Neel Shah: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner by submission, and STILL LCW Evo Champion… DAAAAAAVID CHAMMMMMBERS! Winner by Submission: David Chambers (Time: 41:02) The crowd made as much noise as was humanly possible. Chambers released his hold on Wallace and thrust his arms into the air, victorious. Borashi retrieved his belt from ringside and handed it to him, and Chambers mounted the turnbuckles, raising his belt, soaking in the cheers from the crowd. LH: And David wins! That was an impressive title defense. LJB: And it leaves one to wonder who could remove that title from the Doc. On the other end of the spectrum was a broken, battered and beaten Dean Wallace. He took little solace in the fact that he had not choked - it had simply got away from him. He, too, retrieved his belongings from ringside, and prepared to carry them to the back. At the top of the ramp, Dean stopped and stared at Chambers. Chambers returned the stare. Wallace took his unused baseball bat in his left hand, pointed at Chambers with it, and used it to knock his shoulder back into joint. Then, he donned his baseball cap, and tipped it, ever so slightly, in homage to the man who had retained his championship. LJB: And Dean Wallace retains some pride with that gutsie display. On the turnbuckle, David Chambers simply nodded, and continued the celebration. He had overcome a lot in the past half-year, and he had finally reached the top of his profession. As “Chop Suey” blared through Club Evo along with the cheers of the adoring crowd, Chambers raised his belt repeatedly in victory as the Dawning came to an end. LH: And the champion retains. Victory for the Doctor. LJB: Bear says, "Not bad, young man. But you still have a long road ahead of you. Now take the ball bearing out of my belly and put me in the washing machine."
