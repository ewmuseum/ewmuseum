Impulse 1.9: From Employer to Partner

2002

LCW Evo Impulse From Employer to Partner Brandon Devola was sitting once again at the Club Evo bar. Since the last show, he had been eagerly awaiting news on his LCW contract from his current employer, Tommy G. Despite the fact that Tommy had come through for him several times before, he couldn't shake the shadow of doubt that clouded his mind. Sure, Tommy was one of the most verbally persuasive individuals he'd ever known, but how could he cut through the wall of dislike Gravity had built up towards Devola? Moments later, as Tommy G burst through the door of the bar, Devola felt an answer coming on. Tommy immediately jumped up onto a barstool and clinked his martini glass to get the attention of everyone in the bar. The whole room, especially Devola, turned to listen closely to the words of the undefeated superstar. TOMMY G: Laaaddddiiieeesss and gentlemen! I have just returned from the office of my dear friend Gravity. As many of you know, I have been working with Gravity for about a week now, trying to negotiate a little present for my current towelboy and very good friend, Mr. Brandon Devola. As a result of some fast talking and various compromises arranged by myself and my own manager, Mr. Antony "Cash" Monet, I am very happy to inform you that Brandon Devola is now an official LCW Evo superstar! The bar erupted with cheers in support of Devola, who was now wide-eyed with shock. His jaw seemed as if it were going to fall straight into his beer mug. TOMMY G: Now, this contract begins next week at Dawning, when I will team up with Mr. Devola to face Live and Brad Case. Wish us the best of luck...and till then, the drinks are on me! Tommy looked down and Devola with a knowing smile and winked before jumping down from his stool and heading to the dance floor. The other patrons of the bar had already gathered around Devola and broken into a chorus of "For He's A Jolly Good Fellow," toasting the new LCW wrestler and patting him on the back. It was like the ultimate dream come true. He had finally made it. And for the first time in his LCW career...for that matter, the first time in a long, long while... Brandon Devola smiled.
