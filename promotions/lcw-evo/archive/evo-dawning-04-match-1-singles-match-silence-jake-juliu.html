Dawning: Match 1, Singles Match: “Silence” Jake Julius v Angel Dust

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Match 1, Singles Match: “Silence” Jake Julius v Angel Dust LJB: Those stupid stage names. I'm still trying to get over how dumb they are. These guys are almost as bad as "Rook," or "Angel of Death,"-- LH: I always thought "Legendary" was kinda dumb...HEY! Cut it out! Gimme my beer back! "Sweet Mother of Pearl!" echoed deep into the halls of Club Evo as the lights dimmed. Moby's "Go (Rainforest Mix)" played loudly through the P.A. system as green and blue lights shone throughout each room on the first floor of the club, and Jake's face was shown on the screen. Each time the word "Go!" was heard in the song, the screen showed quick clips of Jake hitting a move, but it then immediately went back to Jake's face. Jake slowly walked out from the vertical transit area and into the ring. When he got in, he simply sat on a turnbuckle like a care-free man, awaiting his opponent for the night, the magnitude of the show seeming not to bother him in the least. LJB: Ah, to be that carefree. I want some of the drugs Silence is on. LH: Jake reminds one of a Hindu cow. LJB: On morphine. With a lobotomy. “Bull God”, by Kid Rock, played as the lights dimmed and the monitor wall showed the words “Angel Dust”, letter-by-letter. After each letter was shown, the lights came back and Angel Dust made his entrance. On the way to the ring he paid little attention to the fans. Angel Dust rolled inside the ring and bounced off the ropes a few times before resting in the corner and awaiting instruction from the match official, Chris Fisher. He called them to the center of the ring and gave them the rules for the contest, which was no different from a normal LCW match. The two competitors stared at one another before backing away a step and circling each other. LJB: (singing along) To the P-O-O, to the P-O-O, to the O-O-P, to the O-O-P! And I'm feces! LH: *Snort* LJB: You just snorted! LH: (laughing) Forgive me. I'm human! They eventually tied up, but neither man seemed able to take the advantage. As they struggled back and forth, neither man noticed a single fan leap over the safety barricade. The ringside security team did, however, and they charged at the newcomer, a lean but well-muscled Asian man. The stranger saw them coming, however, and he managed to backflip onto the safety barricade, causing the two security guards to collide and then slump to the floor. Stepping over them, the young man slid into the ring and got to his feet. He was approached by Fisher, and the two men exchanged brief words, ending when Fisher shrugged his shoulders and gestured at Silence and Angel Dust. LH: Um...uh...why's the ref letting that guy who just Stooged the ringside security? LJB: He gave him a dollar. And no one likes ringside security. LH: True. By this time, they had given up their struggle for dominance of the tie-up and had dropped arms to turn to look at the other man who was currently sharing the ring with them. Sizing him up, they saw first his black tights, which had the word “Xian” in flames on the left leg, as well as the word “Dragon” on the waistband. His bare chest was scarred and tattooed, and his entire right arm was engulfed with a dragon tattoo. He bowed to the two men and then turned his head to each side at an angle, so as to crack his neck. Smiling, the stranger beckoned for them to come at him, and both men obliged. LJB: Isn't this guy dead?!? LH: That's not the same guy, Joe. LJB: Is it his dead son?!? LH: No, not him either. Moving with a speed that seemed blurred, the newcomer fired a hard roundhouse kick into Silence’s ribs, driving the wind from the Evo competitor and sending him crashing to the mat. Without pausing, the Asian spun the opposite direction, staggering Angel Dust with a back kick directly to the stomach. As the rookie doubled over, the scarred stranger ducked behind him and applied a rear waistlock, bending his knees slightly so as to get an even lower grip on his victim. With a powerful grunt, the cords in the stranger’s arms stood out as he hefted Angel Dust into the air. As he picked Angel Dust up progressively higher, the enigma jerked his entire body backwards, arching his back and sending Angel Dust to the mat with a sickening back drop driver. Angel Dust twitched on the mat momentarily, and then he lay still. LH: Wow. He's pretty fast. LJB: How fast is he, Livvie? LH: He's as fast as...as...ten fast men! The stranger released his hold on Angel Dust’s waist and got back to his feet, just in time to catch a charging Silence with a stiff chop to the throat. As the smaller man recoiled, the stranger sent a superkick into the point of his chin that caused him to stagger backwards and into the ropes. As Silence rebounded, still groggy, from the ropes, the stranger hooked him in a front chancery and then hoisted him into the air, setting Julius up for what appeared to be a textbook stalling vertical suplex. What it became was something much different, however, as the Asian literally threw himself backwards, bending his knees and springing upwards while doing so, and in doing so, he executed a devastating brainbuster on Silence. LJB: Hmm, does this guy have a phone number? I'm sure there are plenty of matches that need this kind of conclusion rather than being drawn out for another half hour. Seemingly satisfied, the stranger stood up and raised his hands, receiving a loud cheer from the frenzied crowd, who truly loved their violence, especially when good wrestling was involved with it. As he turned back towards the area that he had came out of the crowd from, though, he was met with a stunning surprise. LH: Uh oh. Silence was back up. Even after such a severe drop, the youngster had managed to clamor back to his feet, and he was standing and staring at his attacker. However, as said attacker approached, he quickly realized that Julius was merely reacting and not acting, as the youngster’s glazed-over eyes told the stranger more than his body language ever could. With a smirk, the Asian pointed at Julius and turned his back, just as Silence crashed to the mat once more. LJB: Shhhh. Silence is sleeping. And then, just as he had entered the ring, the mysterious man exited, sliding beneath the bottom rope and then making his way between the recovering ringside security members before disappearing into the crowd. In the ring, paramedics had arrived and they were taking care to stabilize both men’s necks as they placed them on stretchers and carried them to a waiting ambulance. LH: Byebye, mysterious match-ending, kung fu guy. We'll miss you. Result: No Contest (Match Time: 3:17) LH: So, why'd you request that match, Joe? I don't understand it... LJB: Eh. Just wanted to see if they'd take my request. Now that I know the higher ups will, there's no limit to what I can do! HAHAHAHAHA. Ahem. Anyway. Click Here for the Next Segment
