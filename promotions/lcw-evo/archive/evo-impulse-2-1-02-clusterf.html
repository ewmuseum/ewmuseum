Impulse 2.1: Clusterf***

LCW Evo Impulse Clusterf*** As the Tribulation Saints took their leave from the ring, the co-owners of Evo simultaneously shook their heads. Olmstead turned from the monitor they had been watching the show on and looked gravely at his co-owner and business partner, Tim Hall, known worldwide as LCW Hall of Famer Gravity. Sure, Gravity wasn't in the Hall of Fame yet, but it was only a matter of time, considering he remained one of the most decorated champions in all of LCW's history. Olmstead was relieved to see that Gravity too shared his look of concern, and that neither man really favored the idea of Michael Gabrielle as the Number One Contender to their most prized championship. "Sure, maybe a year and change ago, when he had been the Archangel," Olmstead thought, "But now? The Angel of Death? There's no way I want that nut within fifty feet of the title." "I think we learned from Duel that mental stability is a must in a title holder or contender," Olmstead said, doing his best to remain calm and emotionless in spite of the overwhelming desire to scream out "No!" Across the room, no more than six feet away, Gravity looked every bit as calm as Olmstead was striving to be. He simply sat back and smiled before answering Olmstead. "Well, Ed, I can see where you're coming from, but I can certainly admire his go-getter attitude. I mean, like you said, we did let Duel go after the belt, didn't we?" Olmstead didn't answer immediately, mulling over what his partner had just said to him. In the four months since Gravity had come to Evo, they had come to respect one another, allowing the other's strong points to carry the company through the turbulence that it had been in. Now, with Dawning under their belt and blue skies ahead, Olmstead wondered if perhaps Gravity wasn't right. He was the wrestling-minded one, after all. Olmstead had always been the financier of the operation, and he made no bones about deferring to Gravity's wisdom a great deal of the time decisions were forced upon them. However, this time was different. "I don't know, Tim; I just don't know…" "Well, to be quite honest, Ed; I don't either. But being successful is all about taking risks…" "…calculated risks," Olmstead chimed in. "Fair enough. It's about taking calculated risks, and I think we can assume that Gabrielle isn't going to self-destruct like Duel did." This mention of the former champion's name sent Olmstead's mind reeling…that was it! Alex had called him several times since Dawning, insisting that he had changed and that "the other" was no longer in control. Olmstead, having been the backstage pull of the Black Dogs when Static and Felony had terrorized LCW, knew all too well what Duel had meant, but he wasn't sure if this was another trick of Duel's to try to gain another shot at the big prize. Either way, Olmstead felt that Duel was more suited to contest Doc than Gabrielle was, so he ventured the proposition to Gravity. "What about Duel?" "What about him?" Gravity countered. "Let's give him the shot," he hesitantly began, but then finished strongly with, "The last time they fought, it was the highest rated segment in the show, and the only ratings that have been higher were for Dawning…" When Gravity didn't look convinced, Olmstead continued, "Plus, it'll give us a chance to measure how far Gabrielle is willing to go to get a title shot." Gravity immediately smiled and nodded his head. "Good call, Ed. Let's do it. Also, let's throw RJR into the four-way for Madhouse contendership."
