Dawning: Preparations

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Preparations Tommy G and Brandon Devola were shown in the Club Evo bar, decked out in their in-ring duds. While Tommy was lazily throwing back his customary pre-match martini, Devola was nervously wringing his hands. Tommy turned and put a hand on his partner's shoulder. “Hey, man, what's wrong?” Tommy inquired, “You're shaking like crazy.” Devola looked at his partner and shrugged his shoulders, “It's just....I'm nervous. I've waited so long for this and now...I'm scared I'll screw it up. I'm scared I'll make a fool of myself in front of all the fans and....” Tommy threw his arm around Devola’s shoulders and shook the big man slightly. “Whoa, whoa, whoa, that's no problem, man. You've just got some pre-match jitters. Just have a quick drink and loosen up.” “No, no drinks this time. I'll lose my focus,” Devola said, looking solemnly at Tommy. Tommy looked back at his protégé of sorts, a look of incredulous wonder on his face. That was quickly replaced by one of skepticism, however, “Judging from the way you're talking and acting, you don't have much focus to speak of right now. You've just gotta calm yourself down. The fans know it's your first match, and they don't care if it's not a perfect performance. People are generally quite forgiving of you your first time out there; they know it's driving you nuts. And then, by the third or fourth time, when they DO start caring how well you're doing, you've gotten into your groove and you're in good shape. But the first time? Nah. No sweat.” Devola was obviously starting to come around, but he was still clearly nervous. “But what if they don't like what I do?” Tommy nearly laughed at Devola’s statement before boisterously replying. “They ALREADY like what you do, man! You're associated with Tommy G! What else do you need to make your career skyrocket, eh?” A very attractive young blonde in a tight tube top and leather pants walked over to the two men, who were instantly spellbound. She walked not to Tommy, but to Devola. “Hey there, Brandon....can I call you Brandon?” Devola, awestruck, simply nodded as he mumbled, “Um...uh....yeah...um....sure...” The young girl continued to look Brandon directly in the eyes as she talked. “I just wanted to wish you good luck against Live and Brad Case tonight. Looking forward to watching you strut your stuff...during AND after the match...” The girl kissed Devola gently on the cheek, turned, and waved as she walked off. Devola was frozen with amazement. Tommy delivered another slap to his back to wake him up. Tommy, smiling, raised his martini glass to Devola. “See what I mean, man? You're in good shape.” Devola rubbed his cheek and smiled. “Yeah...I guess I am...” Antony "Cash" Monet, Tommy's manager and now Devola's as well, entered the bar and approached the two men. Monet looked at his charges and smiled, putting an arm around each one. “It's about go time, guys. Better head back to the locker room and warm up.” Tommy smiled at Monet and winked before cocking his head at Devola. He punched Devola gently in the arm as he swiveled on his barstool. “Sounds good. Let's hit it, loverboy!” The three men exited the bar together, and the scene faded out to the main bank of monitor walls on the first floor. Click Here for the Next Segment
