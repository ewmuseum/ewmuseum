Dawning: Match 3, Tag Match: Tommy G/Brandon Devola v Brad Case/Live

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Match 3, Tag Match: Tommy G/Brandon Devola v Brad Case/Live LH: On to more guys kicking each other's asses! The shot switched to the locker room area, where Brad Case and Live had started attacking Tommy G and Brandon Devola. Live tossed Tommy into a wall, nearly knocking him unconscious, while Case grabbed a chair from across the room. He rushed at Devola with it, but lost his balance in a very awkward way and fell face-first onto the chair. This opened up Devola to attack Live from behind and help Tommy G up. The two grabbed Live and started dragging him towards the ring. LH: Whoa! Play nice! Tommy and Devola dragged Live through the crowd and up over the guardrail. Devola gave him one last smack to the head before tossing him into the ring. G and Devola waited on the outside, trying to regain their bearings, but Brad Case staggered in and tried to jump them. Again, he couldn't quite time his actions properly, and instead fell over the guardrail. G and Devola ignored him, and G entered the ring, with Devola heading to the apron. Once the two superstars were in the ring, the ref called for the bell, and the match officially started. As Tommy started pounding on Live again, Brad Case pulled himself up on the apron, staggering as he tried to keep from falling. LH: Why is it that Case always does more damage to himself than his opponents? LJB: I wonder if Brad's been on the same drinking binge he's been on since his first appearance in LCW Evo? LH: Now that's a scary thought...I don't even drink that much. LJB: You and I put together don't drink that much. Live began to battle back temporarily, sweeping Tommy's feet out from under him, but Tommy leaped back up quickly and caught Live with a hard right. Tommy tried for a whip to the ropes, but Live countered and bent down for a backdrop attempt. Tommy saw the maneuver coming and instead caught Live with a facebuster to the knee, sending Live back into the ropes with the impact. Tommy G ran to knock Live all the way over with a clothesline, but Live ducked and Tommy flew out of the ring; however, Tommy used his prowess to land on the apron, and hit Live with a guillotine drop before sliding his way back into the ring. Devola motioned for a tag, but Tommy shook his head no, as if saying "not yet." LH: Your TAG is DENIED, newbie. Step back. Live used Devola's distraction to battle back with a hard kick to the stomach. He used the advantage to execute a snap suplex, then bounced off the ropes to deliver a hard elbow drop to Tommy. He went for the cover, but only got a one-count. LJB: Your PINFALL is DENIED, newbie. Die! LH: Joe, imagine how bad you'd feel if Live really did drop dead. LJB: I'd feel bad. For about a day. Live started to pick Tommy up, but Tommy instead grabbed his leg and executed a dragon screw takedown maneuver. However, he held onto Live's leg a little too long, allowing Live to kick him hard in the face from the ground. Live started to get up, but was hindered by the left leg Tommy had just twisted so awkwardly, allowing Tommy to club him from behind with both hands. LH: Fascinating. One move cancelled the other. Kinda like the snake eating itself. LJB: Huh? LH: Nothing. Tommy whipped Live into the ropes and caught him with a Superkick to the jaw when he rebounded. He climbed on top of the fallen superstar and started delivering rapid punches to his head. Devola held his hand out over the ropes, desperately awaiting the tag, but instead, Tommy picked Live up and took him to the opposite corner. LJB: Another point of interest would have to be Tommy G's obvious desire to hog all the glory in this particular match. Tommy climbed up onto the second rope to deliver some punches to Live's head, but was instead greeted with a Spinebuster-type maneuver that laid him out on the mat. Both men began lethargically crawling to their corners. Live reached his first, and Brad Case immediately leaped into the ring. However, as soon as he got in, he became disoriented and dizzy, spinning around several times before falling to the mat. He looked up at Live, who had just pulled himself to his feet, and helplessly looked for a chance to tag back out. Instead, Live looked down at him in disgust, spat at him, and left the ring. LH: Ya think Live's a little disgusted? LJB: I know I am. Tommy G reached his corner and tagged in an ecstatic Brandon Devola, who immediately went to work on Case with a flurry of jabs. He whipped Case into his and Tommy G's corner and flew across the ring to meet him with a hard clothesline. As Case staggered out of the corner, Devola grabbed him and stuffed Case's head between his legs, then lifted him up for a hard powerbomb. LJB: Devola finally gets the tag. Lucky boy gets to work over someone who's already half-comatose. LH: Yeah. That's not going to make him feel bad about himself or anything. With Devola up on the opposite turnbuckle celebrating with the fans, Tommy G had ascended the turnbuckle nearest Case. He flew off and performed a perfect Shooting Star Press, hooking Case's leg as he landed. The ref fell to the mat for the count. LH: And a one and a two and a three and it's allllll done. Winners by Pinfall: Tommy G and Brandon Devola (Match Time: 7:38) LJB: That was kinda dirty of Tommy G, doncha think Livvie? LH: Well...yeah. Case refused EMT help and instead tried to walk out under his own power. He started to exit through the crowd, but threw up on a fan and collapsed instead. The EMTs rushed to get him onto a stretcher and safely out. LJB: So Case feels the same way I do about this match. Who would have thought? Back in the ring, Tommy G patted Devola on the back for a job well done. Devola didn't accept the congratulatory gesture at first, but gradually lightened up and walked to the back with Tommy.
