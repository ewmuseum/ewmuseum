Impulse 2.1: Lifestyle

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Lifestyle "Ruthless" Johnny Righteous approached Club Evo from the outside, closely tailed by an official LCW cameraman. A heavily packed duffle bag hung from one shoulder, a tightly gripped crowbar rested against the other, held in his hand as if it were his very reason for being. He could see the flashy lights, hear the bouncy music and the new-age atmosphere. He paused, perhaps twenty feet from the building, on the sidewalk. "This way, Mr. Righteous," an LCW crewman directing people outside said, pointing toward an entrance. "Yeah, yeah, just a second," Johnny replied, more astonished than defiant. Johnny stopped a moment to soak it all in. The last time he'd seen a wrestling ring was a packed arena amidst tens of thousands of screaming fans. The sight before his eyes was more like a disco-themed strip club than any wrestling spectacle he'd participated in previously. "So this is what we've become? A cheap dog and pony show? A giddy little thrill for dancers and clubbers? This is what I am?" He brushed a strand of long black hair from his eyes, and pulled it back behind his ear beside the tautly hanging ponytail. A long sigh escaped his lips. "If my old friends could see me here. God only knows what they'd think. I'm a fish out of water. A former convict making an ass of himself in a hip rave joint. If the old guys saw me here. I'd be a complete laughing stock." In the far distance, a police siren blared, and rubber tires squealed shrilly like a banshee into the brisk night air. Johnny looked up into the sky, at the moon and the stars and the hovering blanket of clouds above. "This is the price I pay. It's this, or the streets. It's this, or face the sorry end of the barrel of a loaded gun, staring me down night and day without relent. This, or day after another damn day, risking my ass just to earn another day of violence, murder, and hate." A rousing cheer was heard from inside Club Evo. "This isn't for me." "Nothing ever is." "Mr. Righteous?" the LCW crewman grew impatient, tugging on Johnny's arm. "Coming," RJR replied. Johnny was led through the entrance and throughout the various checks and levels of the building toward his locker room. As they went, he met each hall and door with a certain look of uneasiness, of discomfort, like a lowly peasant taken from his homeland and thrown haphazardly into a foreign kingdom. The flashy lights, the music, the atmosphere. It was all wrong, all wrong. Finally, they reached the locker room. A crewmember opened the door, and Johnny stepped inside and set his bag and crowbar down on a bench, looking up at the ceiling and around the room. "Mr. Righteous?" the crewmember said with a courteous smile. "I think you'll get along just fine here at Club Evo." "Bulls---." Johnny sat down on the bench, the camera still watching from through the doorway, and dropped his head to shuffle through a few things in the duffle bag.
