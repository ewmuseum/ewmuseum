Dawning: Carte Blanche

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Carte Blanche Frank Heimdal sat on the bench in the locker room. He had already stretched, he had already warmed up. He was in his ring attire, with a black hooded sweatshirt over it. He stared at the antiseptic white concrete wall. Once in a while, he glanced at his watch, and then went back to staring. Frank ruminated, his tired, almost febrile mind working along steadily. To go in with expectations and goals, whether they were of himself or his opponent for the night, seemed almost pointless. Preconceived notions about anyone or anything, people and situations, never really prepared you. They just made your point of view all the more narrow. But to go in without. Without any preconceived notions of how he should or shouldn't act, or how Duel would or wouldn't act. In expecting nothing and just going with it. A zen approach, if you will. That actually seemed like a good idea to him. Almost logical, really. The concept of the cage came into his mind after a while. There was more to it than reinforced chickenwire. There had to be... Frank rolled with his thoughts until they were interrupted by a feminine voice behind him. Frank turned around to see Eilene Hadden, camera in hand, smiling at him in an amused fashion. "Pardon?" Frank asked. Eilene laughed before speaking again. "I asked what's on your mind, chief." Frank smiled at Evo's resident goth girl, then looked back at the wall. "Oh, this is gonna be great. Eddie O's gonna ask what I came back with after taping you, and I'll tell him I got a fantastic shot of Frank Heimdal's back. He'll be fuckin' thrilled with me." Eilene said sarcastically. Upon hearing this, Frank swung his legs over the bench and turned around so he was facing Eilene. "Much much better. Now, can we talk? The camera's our friend. Our very, very good friend. C'mon cutie. Smile for me. You can do it." Frank half-smiled and flipped her off simultaneously. "Well, aren't WE in a mood?" Eilene said, knowing full well that Frank meant no malcontent by the vulgar hand signal he was flashing her. Besides, this was a mild reaction compared to the other wrestlers she had dealt with in her Evo career so far. Still, she couldn't complain about getting paid to tape good-looking men. "Good thing you're cute, hon. Otherwise, that smile wouldn't be so pretty once I was done with you." Frank smiled a little more broadly at this lady's bravado and brought his hand down. "So, we gonna do this or what?" she asked in a playfully gruff manner. "Yeah. Let's get this over with." Frank said a little grimly, his smirk dissipating. He waited until the red light on the hand-held camcorder flashed, then...rolled with it. "I guess this is the part of the show where I talk about how I'm gonna kick Duel's ass. I guess this is the part of the show where I tell Duel 'I'm not just here for my shot at the Madhouse Title, I'm here to defend Livvie's honor' or vice-versa. And I guess this is the part of the show where I get to talk about how bad-ass I am." Frank took in a breath then released it. "I'm not going to do that though. I think what I'm going to talk about is that this has gone well beyond anything that has transpired between you and Olivia. This isn't about me proving that I'm destined to become the Alpha Male around here, and," Frank looked into the camera meaningfully, "this isn't about how weak I think you are, Duel. "I'm pretty tired of "honor" and "duty." I think we're all a little tired of that at this point. I want this to be what it is," Frank looked at a point just over the camera-woman's shoulder, "And what it is, is pretty simple, really. Two men, fighting. In a cage. For all the adoring crowd to watch. This is all about love and hate and all the feelings that happen in between. Nothing wishy-washy or lukewarm here. Hot and cold. There's almost a purity to this. There's a screwed-up beauty to it. I see it. I don't care if you do. Because I see it. I see it all." Frank looked directly into the camera again, his features changing. The worry between his eyebrows left his face, and he visage became nearly blank. He paused for almost too long a time. "I want to talk about the cage," Frank finally continued, much to Eilene's relief. "The cage is an interesting concept. The cage is designed to keep you and I inside, where we'll fight until we're done. Frank's dry-mouth was beginning to get to him. He licked his lips, much to Eilene's subtle delight, then went on. "But the cage also keeps what's outside away from us. So, during the match, you can't get to Olivia." Frank looked down at the floor, then back up into the lens of the camera. "Olivia becomes a moot point. As do the fans, the staff, your friends, your stablemates. Everything but us. It's all about you and I. And that's it. "Now, keeping that in mind, we're in there, doing our thing. I can bring a lot into the cage. And I can leave a lot behind. So, to me, the concepts of ‘good’ and ‘bad’ become moot also. I've always tried to be generally good. I'm not a saint, I'm hardly the Dalai Lama, but I try." Frank chuckled despite himself at that comparison. He got over it and went on. "This time, I don't have to be good. I don't have to be bad. I just have to be. And I'm looking forward to it. Regardless of whether I wind up broken in two on the mat, or whether I'm climbing out of the cage. This will not be a show of might verses right. This won't be about morals. This will be the perfect match. This will be just us. I need this. I'm going to be selfish about this. I f****** deserve this. And so do you, Duel. You deserve it too. You could not have become what you are without your pain. I could not be what I am without my pain. We are what we are. You're entitled to this as much as I am." Frank gritted his teeth. Realizing that he was freezing up, he made a point of finishing up his dissertation. "This is something I have to do for myself. And anybody who knows me knows that what I say is true. So let's see about you, Duel. "Duel, I did this. To you, to them, to everybody involved. And I'm going to undo it," Frank paused, "One way or another." Frank made a slight slashing motion across his neck, signaling that he wanted the camera off. "Guess we're done here, huh?" Eilene asked, bringing down her camera. Eilene looked over her shoulder, then, to Frank's surprise, she leaned in close enough so Frank could feel her breathing on him. She stooped over, right in his face, and whispered fiercely "Did you hear what he did to Steve?" "What who did to Steve?" Frank asked, slightly louder than her. "What Duel did to Steve. Broke his nose. And Steve won't tell what or why. None of us on the staff like Steve all that much, but he's ours. Y'know?" Frank nodded. Frank knew about caring about your own and disapproving about them at the same time. "We don't like that, Frank. I was sent to you with a message, We ALL want you to kick that motherfucker's ASS. We don't care if you win. We just wanna see somethin' good. Somethin' to make us all feel better. It's bad enough we gotta put up with the fans, but to get flack from the staff too is getting a little old. Y'know?" Again, Frank nodded. Eilene straightened back up and glanced behind her again, then gave Frank an almost brutal kiss on the cheek. "I'm outta here, Frank. It was nice to finally meet you." Frank nodded, in thought once again. He wouldn't let what the bizarre female who (almost) reminded him of Nikki affect him too much. He'd let her affect him just enough, though.
