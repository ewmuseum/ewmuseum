Impulse 2.1: Joe and Livvie on The Four Way

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Joe and Livvie on The Four Way The camera focused on that of our fab- impeccable announcers. The back of them anyway. Joe Black casually reached for a rocks glass to his left and managed to catch sight of the camera over his shoulder. He blinked and said, "Oh crap." Livvie Heimdal gave Joe her dirty, "what the hell is wrong with you" look, before noticing what he had previously noticed. Livvie smiled reflexively, and said, "Ah yes." They spun around in their seats, serious anchorperson expressions on their faces. For about a second and a half. Joe Black spoke, "Up next we're looking at a four way dance to determine who will be challenging Frank Heimdal for the Madhouse Title at Impulse 2.2." Livvie nodded with professionalism leaking out of her brain, "Indeed, Joe, we're looking at Pheonyx, Brad Case, Gryfon, and the newly added, Ruthless Johnny Righteous, who will be henceforth referred to as "RJR" because it doesn't take as long." Joe Black does that head rolling thing that anchorpersons do before he continued, "Without a doubt, Pheonyx, who will be referred to as Dante Anderson because it's less pretentious, has held the coveted highest title of the former LCW." Livvie inclined her head toward Joe and crossed her legs demurely, "A phenomenal achievement, but one that occurred ages ago. Also, it would seem that Dante does NOT anticipate facing Frank Heimdal for the Title. As a matter of fact, he thinks that the Madhouse Title is some kind of glorified hardcore thing. And consequently, has expressed grave concerns that this match threatens his health." Joe Black snorted, "Well, if someone wanted Dante hurt, wouldn't that be too damn bad?" Livvie smiled brightly, "Moving on, let's see what Brad Case is up to shall we?" The image displayed cut to a muted shot of the Club Evo bar. Where Brad Case sat. A squiggly line appeared over Brad Case's head, clearly a televised effect superimposed. A voice over of Livvie commented, "Here is Brad Case." The squiggly line became an arrow pointing down to Brad Case's head. Joe voiceover added, "Looking less than sober to my trained eye." Brad Case lifted what appeared to be a drink up to his mouth, before he could take a drink, the picture froze. Livvie voice-overed, "THERE!" Several circles were suddenly drawn on the screen around Brad's Drink-in-hand. "If one looks very carefully, one will notice that there's a beverage in Brad's hand of some kind. And it could be BOOZE." The picture returned to Joe and Livvie. Joe smiled, "And that's all I have to say about that." Livvie raised an eyebrow, "Thank you, Forest. Moving on, we have the enigmatic Gryfon." Joe scowled, "One of the drawbacks of being enigmatic is that people don't have much to say about you. Because you GIVE THEM NOTHING TO WORK WITH. However, in recently, I think we're beginning to witness Gryfon's character." Livvie frowned at Joe, "Well, Joe, if you do research occasionally, you don't necessarily have to wait for wrestlers to hand it all to you. As it turns out Gryfon has a distinctly violent past, which lead to a lengthy prison term. Ahhhhhhh. And his "rehabilitation and release" have brought us to the Gryfon that we've seen in LCW. And I'm beginning to wonder, if he's been rehabilitated, or has just been kind of faking it for the parole people." Joe blinked, "Oh." Joe coughed, "And RJR is this guy who walked right in off the street after not competing for a while. But, hey, he's here at evo and he's clearly excited to be here, right? Right? RIGHT?!?" We cut to still frame of RJR sulking in his locker. RJR is drawn on the screen, with an arrow pointing down to … RJR. Livvie smiled, "There's nothing quite like a positive attitude!"
