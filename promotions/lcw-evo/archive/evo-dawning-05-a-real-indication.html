Dawning: A Real Indication

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse A Real Indication Amid the noise of Club Evo, in the middle of the cheers of the crowd, the chants of the fans, and the chicanery of the wrestlers in the ring and the locker rooms, a single camera ran across the length of the bar on the main floor. At the end of the bar, the camera dropped and landed behind the bar, catching the black shoes of three bartenders in its sight. Then, the camera turned and focused on a pair of ratty, faded white tennis shoes. The sneakers looked like the kind of thing you might find on the garage sale of somebody who owned four rottweilers who were never fed anything but old footwear. The laces on the shoe were tied, and a pale peach hand reached out and grabbed the lace by its end, and a familiar voice spoke. "A year ago, I was on top of this world. Things move so quickly around here that some of you might not remember that. Allow me to get you up to speed, by the numbers." The hand began twirling the shoelace around in an almost ritualistic manner, wrapping it around its owner's middle finger then slowly looping it in circles in the air. "Two LCW World Heavyweight titles. The single most powerful stable in the history of professional wrestling. And if I may digress... no, I'm not exaggerating. One whirlwind romance capped off with quite possibly the most memorable televised wedding since Luke and Laura. Three hundred and eighty-six cigarettes smoked on camera alone. Lots of cooing and cuddling interspersed with razor blade scars and cheese graters. One lesbian sister. One baby girl on the way. Three mad-as-hell, not-going-to-take-it-anymore employees, and the birth of what you've been witnessing here tonight. LCW Evo." The hand stopped. "You'd have thought I had it all. And you wouldn't be that far from the truth." The hand began tugging at the shoelace, as if trying to untie it, but never doing so. Only playing, and teasing an unlacing. "Then six months passed and you barely heard a word out of me. I started losing matches I should have won, and barely eking by opponents I should have squashed. At first, people missed me, I'm sure. They missed my witty monologues and my off-beat shenanigans. Then, after awhile, I just became another guy on the card. People stopped watching me so closely because they stopped caring about me. I became flat. I became worse than flat, I became practically non-existent. I fell farther off the face of the earth than Hugh Grant's call girl. Hell, another six months and I'd be a prime cut for Andy Warhol." In one move, the hand flipped at the shoelace with its middle finger and the knot came undone, the laces flipping into the air before falling uneventfully to the ash-stained floor behind the bar. "Look at me." The camera slowly panned up from the shoe, over a pair of baggy blue jeans, to a green flannel shirt tied around its owner's waist, up to a black mesh shirt revealing fourteen minute scars and a pair of nipples that really need a Bic, and finally set on the man's head, his face buried behind a mound of shoulder-length, cherry-red hair. He brought his hand up and, with his thumb and pinky finger, parted the hair from his face and tucked it behind six piercings on his left ear, and seven on his right. "Hi," said Used, flashing his trademark grin. "You'll have to excuse me if it seems like I'm getting corny and prophetic. It's just that I haven't talked to you in awhile, and I wanted to grab your attention. I haven't really gone anywhere, but I still feel like this is a sort of rebirth for me. A wife who’s two months from having your first child puts birth on the brain." Used paused for a moment and scratched the side of his head in thought. "I just hope that, after the birth, I don't have afterbirth on the brain. Because that would just be rank." Used smiled again and poked his finger into the lens of the camera. "In a perfect world, I would have been able to balance the pressures of preparing for a child with my business life. But this is not a perfect world, this is not a perfect business, and this is not a perfect life. The fans of LCW have given me the best times of my life. Everything I've done in this business has been because of them. Because of you. The ten of you watching at home who still remember what I used to be like. Back when you used to give a s(censored) about me. “Maybe you can give a s(censored) about me again. Maybe you can't, maybe you're hearing me right now thinking about what an ass(censored) I am for even wasting your time right now, and if that's the case, then thanks for the memories, but if it comes down to you and her, I'm choosing her. “Then again, maybe this is some kind of dream. Or maybe the last three years were. Maybe this washed-up, barely pulsating thing you see before you is the way I've always been. I've always known that, in the beginning, it wasn't that I was particularly good, it's just that everybody around me was so bad. This is a different LCW. But until I see red curtains and a midget dancing and talking backwards, I'm going to assume that this is not a dream, that this is reality, and that I've got to keep up with it." Used reached his right arm over his left shoulder and pulled a stock bottle of the bar's vodka from under the counter. He uncapped the bottle with his teeth, spit the topper directly into the camera, and pulled a quick gulp before replacing the open bottle right where he found it. "We're not f(censored) around here tonight. It's Dawning, and it's a dawning. Olmguy has promised me an opponent who's taken me to the limit before, someone who's pulled the best matches of my career out of me, and someone who's going to either remake or break me. And I think it's his last straw, too. He hasn't exactly been pleased with my performances as of late, and I can't say I blame him. But I look forward to it. For the first time in six months, I look forward to this. Just don't think, anyone who has any bit of doubt, don't you dare think that there's even the slightest bit of fear within me right now. I want to do this, and I CAN do this. And if you don't believe me, just keep watching the show." Used reached down and began retying his shoelace. "I've got a real indication that I'm in for twenty minutes of hell tonight." He finished tying his lace, slapped the side of his sneaker twice, and flicked his temple, shocking his eyebrows in, his head crooked forward, and his lips into a closed smile.
