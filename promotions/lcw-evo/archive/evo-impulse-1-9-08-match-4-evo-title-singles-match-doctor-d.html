Impulse 1.9: Match 4, Evo Title Singles Match: Doctor David Chambers (c) v Brad Case

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW Evo Impulse Match 4, Evo Title Singles Match: Doctor David Chambers (c) v Brad Case LJB: The Alkie and Dr. Dummy. I look forward to this not at all. Crashing thunder boomed in the distance. Slowly, but surely, it got louder and louder, until flashes of green and blue lasers fired all across the club. Out of one of the elevators stepped Brad Case, caught in the midst of a beaming spotlight. At the same time, a shot of Brad Case in the midst of hitting an opponent with a lariat appeared on the monitor. The fans roared at the sight of Brad. The tall Canadian didn't seem to realize this, however, as he walked slowly, stumbling to the ring. More shots of Brad hitting opponents faded in, including a shot of Sweet Sorrow destroying an enemy. Another loud roar echoed from the crowd as Case reached the ring, and began to mount its steel steps. He had some difficulty climbing the stairs, but finally managed to make his way into the ring. A shot of Case's standing sidekick proved the man's tremendous agility. Still oblivious to the thousands of fans watching and cheering him on, Brad wandered around the ring with a sullen look on his face. The screen morphed into fifteen different shots of Brad Case hitting Sweet Sorrow, while a blitz of flashing lights covered the first floor area of the club. LJB: That's an impressive entrance. Maybe it's to distract us from the fact that the guy can barely walk into the ring... LH: That wasn't very nice, Joe. I mean, I was expecting you to say something like that, but still. The lights in Club Evo went out. "Chop Suey" by System of a Down hit and the laser lights came on, spinning around the room in a multicolored splendor. When the drums came in, the monitors started turning on, one at a time at random, showing piece-by-piece a dark green swirling spiral with Bear the Bear in the center. "The Doctor" was written in blood-red block letters just beneath Bear's shape on the screen. Just as the main riff started, the monitors went black and the words "I Have Awakened" appeared in big white letters. Bright lights hit the entrance and Doctor David Chambers came walking confidently out towards the ring in a black leather jacket, Bear clutched in one hand. His light green hospital scrubs were spotlessly clean, and his formerly long black hair was short around his head; his feet were clad in bright white sneakers. The lights followed him up into the ring as the monitors showed various shots of Doc in action. He removed the jacket, revealing the writing still scribbled along his arms: "I Am Everything." He put Bear off to the side of the ring, in easy reach for a Steelhead, and walked to the corner opposite his opponent before raising his arms to the crowd. LH: Darn. I was kinda hoping he would throw Bear into the commentary booth again. LJB: Oh darn. I guess Bear says, "Livvie, you're a nice girl and all. But I'm a stuffed animal and furthermore my ability to speak is the result of a lingering psychosis in Dr. Chamber's head. Things just wouldn't work out considering the fact that I hope I'm not going to be talking for the rest of Dr. Chamber's life." LH: (censor) you, Joe. LJB: You like the bear more than me, don't you? LH: No, he's more polite than you. I never said I liked him better. LJB: You hear that bear!? HA! LH: Maybe I'll start liking him better, though. LJB: Nuts. Case didn’t waste any time charging at the Evo champ, but Doc was ready, and hit the running Case with a dropkick. Case rebounded, and again Doc dropkicked the challenger. As Case got up again, Doc applied a side headlock, and hoisted Brad up vertically. However, Case wriggled free of the suplex attempt and slid down Doc’s back. As Chambers turned around, Case caught him with a thrust kick that floored the champ. LJB: And bear says, "What the hell are you thinking, Doc?" LH: He does not. Case hovered, waiting for Chambers to regain his footing. When he did, he applied a hammerlock, and hit a hard jawbreaker that sent Chambers falling backwards to the mat. Case, seeing his opponent downed, attempted a standing senton. It missed, as Doc rolled out of the way just in time. LJB: Okay, he's still functional. LH: Maybe Case is a talented wrestler and is just taking that match to Doc. LJB: Whatever. Both men kipped up quickly, and it was Case who gained the advantage, locking in a headlock and snap suplexing Chambers to the mat. He floated over for a cover. 1… kickout. LJB: Nothing. I hope Case can write to his grandkids someday and say, "Hey, I got a 1 count on Doc Chambers, back in my prime." LH: Hey, Joe, how are your grandkids? LJB: I don't have any grandkids. Just one psychotic son. Case wasn’t shocked that Doc had kicked out: after all, it was early in the match. He was shocked, however, when Doc caught his knife-edged chop, twisted his arm around, and slammed him down face first to the mat. LH: And Doc unloads. Case took his time getting to his feet, but it wasn’t by choice. It was, rather, because Chambers was laying into him with stomps to the back. When Case finally did get to his feet, he didn’t stay for long. Chambers applied a headlock and nailed a DDT on the challenger. Cover. 1… 2… kickout. LJB: I really hope Brad feels that, down deep in his soul. With Case still down, Chambers looked to end it. He applied an armbar, but Case reversed into one of his own. Doc lay face down, and Case looked to apply a crossface hold! But he couldn’t get it locked in. Chambers slithered his way to the ropes and clutched the bottom strand just as Case was getting the crossface locked in. LH: Grrrrr. Two men rolling on the ground. I am pleased. LJB: In the interest of equality, can we watch porn later and we do commentary for that? LH: ...sure. Why not? Case went to his corner to take a breather while he waited for Doc to get up. Chambers was up quicker than he expected, so Case was plenty surprised to take a hard running clothesline from a charging Chambers. Case rebounded, and fired off a hard spinning heel kick. Doc ducked. Case fired a punch. It landed. The next one didn’t, as Chambers bobbed and weaved like a boxer to avoid it and Case’s next two shots. When the window of opportunity opened for the Doctor, he charged and tackled Case down to the mat, where he began to pummel him. The crowd grew louder with every shot to the face that Doc connected on. LJB: How about porn now? LH: You gotta wait. After a while, official Bobby Borashi separated Doc from Case to inquire about Case’s well being. Case acknowledged that he was OK, at which time Borashi stepped away from Case. LJB: Case, the trooper stays in until his impending dismemberment by Ku-Ku, the Wrestling Clown. Case reached out to the ropes and hauled himself up. As soon as he did, he turned around to look for Chambers. But it was Chambers who found him. He charged and flew at Case, leaving Case with no alternative but to catch Doc and collapse with the Evo Champ on top of him. Borashi dropped and counted. 1… 2… Case rolled it through! 1… 2… Oh, so close! But Doc kicked out! LH: Whoa! That would have been an amazing upset! LJB: More stories for the grandkids. LH: And how many grandkids did you have again, Joe? How old are you? Have any of your offspring married? Yet? LJB: I will puke soon if you keep this up. Case smacked the mat once in frustration. He pulled Doc to his feet and hooked both of Doc’s arms. But Chambers had been in this predicament before. He simply pushed forwards until Case’s back hit the turnbuckle. Case staggered forward right into Doc’s arms. He applied a standing headscissors, picked Case up, and fell back, piledriving the rookie! Borashi started the count as Chambers covered. LJB: PILEDRIVER! 1… 2… LJB: Damn. Must've not done it right. LH: Joe, piledrivers are weak. End of story. Accept and adjust. Not this time! With Case down, Doc headed towards the corner and grabbed Bear, his trusted companion and lethal weapon. The crowd roared as Doc raised the Bear high above his head. LH: BEAR! Suddenly, the camera went out. The viewers at home could see nothing. They could hear something, but it was hard to discern. Mostly, it was a mixture of crowd noise. It was hard to gage a crowd reaction. LJB: This is the part of the show where we make sex noises in the dark. LH: No. Then, the crack LCW Evo production team switched to an alternate camera. What viewers saw as the feed restored was David Chambers lying in the middle of the ring, with a groggy Brad Case ascending the top rope! Case gauged the distance, and flew off with a high-flying frog splash. Sweet Sorrow. LH: He connected! LJB: Holy crap. Cover. 1… 2… Moments before Borashi was to count the 3, he stopped. He then pointed at the timekeeper, signaling that the bell be rung. No Contest (Time: 10:12) Case got up, confused, and demanded to know the reason for the DQ. Borashi merely gestured in the direction of the crowd. Standing at the forefront of the people, holding a baseball bat and shrugging his shoulders, was Dean Wallace. LJB: My god, Dean, you are a complete and utter tool. If I may use such language. LH: You may. LJB: Then I shall begin. Dean, you are a complete and utter jackass. You're a loser. What the hell are you doing? You want something from Doc? Well, you won't get it, you moron. It's not Doc's fault that you're a chump and you choked when your opportunity came. Drop dead. Why don't you face up to the fact that you are your own worst enemy? LH: Uhm. Click Here for the Next Segment
