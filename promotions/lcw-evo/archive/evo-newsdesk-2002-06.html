LCW Evo - The Evolution Continues Saturday, May 25, 2002 Impulse 2.2 Preview - ImpulsiveProductions @ 21:01 EST The camera faded in, and it was a breathtaking shot of…a pair of unbelievably crazy high heels? The man behind the camera slowly moves up a pair of fishnet-clad legs, over a little black skirt that’s only other practical purpose could be a potholder, past a red and black corset and to the pale, pretty, face of the owner of the shoes, legs, and corset which contained the out of SIGHT little torso. “Hi,” said the lovely (yet scary) lady. “My name’s Eilene Hadden, and I am one of four of the Digital Camera Crew of…” Eilene paused dramatically before shouting, “CLUB EVO!!!” The crowd, who was separated from Ms. Hadden by an expanse of chain link fencing, erupted in a cacophony of cheers. Eilene pushed a few dreadlocks out of her face and waited until they calmed down a bit before continuing. “Well, 2.1 certainly left all of us hanging, didn’t it? We had Duel trying to make nice with Doctor David Chambers,” Eilene waited for the pop that ensued after the mention of the good Doctor before she continued, “and then everything went black. Not as in “Joe Black” or “Rook Black,” but black like the non-color. Will all be revealed at Impulse 2.2? Or shall we be left in…” Eilene dramatically put her hand up to her forehead, “the dark?” Eilene snapped out of it. “Anyway, we got a couple of singles matches going on tonight. First off, we have Tommy G, a very popular newcomer, taking on Brad Case, Evo’s favorite barfly. He has much in common with Mr. Charles Bukowski as far as the alcoholism goes, but were writing skills in-ring skills, Bukowski would kick Case’s ass. As for the fabulous Mr. G, well…his reputation as an attention hog proceeds him. With no Brandon Devola to oppress him, let’s see what happens. “ The crowd cheers for the upcoming match by shaking the chain link fence and jeering “Drunk Ass, Brad Case!” “Moving right along, we have Devola going up against the almighty Used. Used, who has held many a lofty title in his day, is now being slated to go toe-to-toe with a relative newbie. How DO you suppose Used feels about this? I know I‘d feel a little insecure. “ Eilene turns around and gives the fence a shake. “What do YOU guys think?” The crowd responds to Eilene’s attention by shouting out a mix of boos and cheers. “That’s what I thought. After that, we got the battle of the initials going on. RJR vs. TR Saxton. They share an ‘R’ in their initials, but other than that, they have little in common. Is it better to have two ‘R’s in your name, or just one really BIG one? What do the ladies in the house think?” Much high-pitched squealing ensued after Eilene’s comment. “Just what I thought. My GOD, you fan girls are irritating. However, I have no problem making cute little girly noises in regards to what I’m gonna talk about next. Gryfon vs. Heimdal in a Madhouse Title Match! How cool is that?” The fans don‘t respond much at all, seeing as how they don’t know what the stip for the match is. However, Eilene is more than ready to enlighten the clueless crowd. “I have, tucked away here in between my…” Eilene reaches down into her corset and pulls out a folded piece of paper, “brain-meats, the Madhouse Stipulation declared by Frank Heimdal. And it is…”she made a big production out of unfolding the paper before reading it, “A…LADDER MATCH!!! We’re kickin’ it old school!” The hardcore (and mostly Philly-native) crowd erupts with glee. A nice bloody, violent ladder match had that effect on this particular crowd. “That‘s right, you animals. Heimdal and Gryfon doing what they do best with ladders involved! Grr.” “And lastly, the most important match of the night. The Main Event. We have The Tribulation Saints up against The Doctor and Livvie Heimdal’s ex-boyfriend.” Eilene put her hand up to her mouth, “Whoops! I meant The Tribulation Saints up against Doctor David Chambers and Duel! Who’ll win?” Who’ll lose? Who’ll make Mistress Eilene hap-hap-HAPPY by sweating and bleeding? The only way you’ll find out is by tuning in to Impulse 2.2.” Eilene turned around and gave the chain link fence another good hard shake, encouraging the rabid fans to shake it back at her. She turned around and smiled and extended two fingers, then punched the air with her fist, the extended two fingers again, saying “two point two” as she did it. Thursday, May 16, 2002 Impulse 2.1 - JamesCheong @ 03:12 EST In their much-anticipated return to television, the superstars of Evo succeeded in living up to the expectations that had been placed on them following the stellar Dawning show. The show started off hot with a confrontation between Doctor David Chambers and his former comrades-in-arms, Michael Gabrielle and Lost, known collectively as the Tribulation Saints. From there, fans at Club Evo were treated to a fast-paced and action-packed match between newcomer Xian and Silence Jake Julius. After deciding on the number one contender for the night's match with Chambers, Evo co-owners Gravity and Edward Olmstead agreed to put Ruthless John Righteous into the four-way Madhouse Contendership match, which would serve as his in-ring return after months of time away from LCW rings. Righteous put on a strong showing against Brad Case, Gryfon, and the ever-dangerous veteran Pheonyx, but would he be able to overcome the ring rust and pick up a shot at Frank Heimdal's Madhouse Title? Also, the Tribulation Saints took on the somewhat shaky team of Brandon Devola and Tommy G, and in spite of their closeness, it seems as though the two young superstars aren't seeing eye-to-eye at the moment. In the final match of the night, David Chambers took on the number one contender in a gruelingly physical match. For all this and more, check out Evo's Impulse 2.1! Link: Impulse 2.1 Saturday, May 11, 2002 Impulse 2.1 Preview - ImpulsiveProductions @ 16:30 EST A tall, thin, redheaded man stands in front of a crowd of people inside Club evo. He’s safely separated from the fans by chain link fencing over the standard security barricade; Josh smiles as the people begin to throw themselves against the fencing and says, “Hello everyone, my name is Josh McDaniel, and really quickly I’m the production head of the broadcast camera crew here on LCW-evo.” “I want you all to know that we have an absolutely awesome Impulse 2.1 show coming up for you. In addition to our standard psychodrama, we’ve got our heinous remnant of the evil asylum, the Tribulation Saints, taking on the team of Brandon Devola and Tommy G. I’ve ordered the crew to keep their eyes peeled on that match since I desperately want some explosive footage of the double-team move `The Act of God’ by the Tribulation Saints.” The mob behind him boo strongly regarding the tribulation saints though one loud voice breaks out with, “Gabrielle is GOD!” “At least one guy, agrees. We’re also going to be looking at a singles match with “Silence” Jake Julius taking on Xian. Xian made his LCW-evo debut at the Dawning show by interfering in Silence’s match that night. I imagine Silence is looking for a little revenge here, but Xian with his ass-kicking, kung-fu action is probably ready for him. The move to watch for is was Xian’s finisher called Toryu, the stiff-stiff-stiff brain buster that Silence felt at Dawning. Xian hauls his opponent up in a standard stalling vertical suplex, and then kick his legs out as hard as he can. Regardless of whether or not we get to see that move from Xian, I assure you the violence will all be on film.” The mob lets their approval known regarding the violence, with a number of fans in front striking kung-fu poses and letting out a Bruce Lee yalp. “Later that night we’ll get see a triple threat match to determine the number one contender for the Madhouse title. The participants are Brad Case, Former LCW champion Pheonyx, and Gryfon. While it’s hard to speculate on what could happen here, Pheonyx definitely has the most momentum going in with his ring experience, and Brad Case won’t be winning crap if he enters the ring for this match drunk. Which oddly enough could make for an interesting contest between essentially Pheonyx and Gryfon as the first fall will decide the match, meaning that it could come down to who can get a cover on the passed out corpse of Brad Case. Which I suppose is only appropriate circumstances for a match to determine who will be facing Frank Heimdal for the Madhouse Title.” Oddly enough, a “Drunk Ass, Brad Case” chant begins behind Josh who turns to regard to mob with no small amount of shock. “Okay,” says Josh with a puzzled expression before continuing, “Now as of this preliminary report, I have been informed that Evo Champion, Doctor David Chambers, will definitely be defending his title tonight. However, I’ve been provided with absolutely no information regarding who his challenger is. In fact, it’s unclear whether Edward Olmstead and Tim Hall have decided to keep the challenger a secret up until the show is underway, or even if they have come to a conclusion yet on who the challenger should be. However, I can assure you as always, that any match with the Doctor will be one that you won’t want to miss, and since you all will be in the hands of the most talented and professional camera crew to broadcast professional wrestling, you won’t miss a damn thing.” The fans begin to shout, “BEAR! BEAR! BEAR!” over and over again, far louder than any other noise they’ve made so far. Josh smiles, “And the bear will be there too.” Josh McDaniel takes a deep breath and shouts, “Let’s get ready for Impulse Two~Point~One!” He turns around and the mob of overzealous fanatics raise their fists in the air and begin chanting “Two point one! Two point one!” pumping their fists with each syllable. Josh pumps his hand for “Two point one!” one last time before leaving camera shot, just before the picture and sound fade out. Thursday, April 18, 2002 The Dawning. - JamesCheong @ 19:48 EST The drama... the anticipation... the excitement... it has all lead up to this. LCW Evo's Dawning has aired. The show featured Brandon Devola and Tommy G facing Brad Case and Live. Used finding out who is mystery opponent is, and fighting him in an intense battle. Gryfon and Lost doing battle high atop the roof of Club Evo. Pheonyx and Michael Gabrielle duking it out, as well as Duel's Madhouse Title defense against Frank Heimdal. And, of course, the main event, featuring challenger Dean Wallace, and champion 'Doctor' David Chambers. The fourty minute battle proved to be one of the greatest in LCW's grand history.
