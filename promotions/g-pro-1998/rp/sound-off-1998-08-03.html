A narrator's voice speaks in a deep, stentorian male voice.) NARRATOR: When a man seeks to attain the greatest heights he can imagine... (The narrator pauses and on screen, the scene changes to slow motion colour footage of The Banshee in one of his television matches. In the bottom right corner of the screen, tiny lettering appears which reads: "Footage provided courtesy of Pro Japan Wrestling, June 14th, 1998". In the middle of the screen, The Banshee is defending his PJW International Title against Frazer Fury and has just dropped him with a jumping elbow thrust. Fury struggles to his feet as The Banshee climbs up onto the top turnbuckle. As Fury stands, The Banshee leaps from the top rope, nailing Fury with a flying somersault bodyblock.) NARRATOR: ...he must fly. (The scene changes to another match. In the bottom corner, the text reads: "Footage provided courtesy of Australian Wrestling Extreme, April 9th, 1998". The Banshee is wrestling Rob Thorne who he has just thrown to the mat with an armdrag takedown.) NARRATOR: When a man seeks to follow in the footsteps of giants... (The scene switches to black and white footage from wrestling matches in the 1950's and 60's. The footage depicts a stocky, muscular man with blonde hair and dark eyebrows who uses a variety of aerial moves, cartwheels and tumbling to confuse and overwhelm his opponents. Text appears below the man which reads: "THE FLYING FRENCHMAN" EDOUARD CARPENTIER.) NARRATOR: ...he must leap. (The scene switches back to the AWE match between The Banshee and Rob Thorne. Thorne is down on the mat and The Banshee quickly steps out between the ropes, turning around to face him on the ring apron. As Thorne staggers to his feet, The Banshee uses the top rope to fling himself up and over, nailing Thorne with a slingshot bodyblock.) NARRATOR: When a man seeks to bind himself to the rigours of honour and tradition... (The scene changes to yet another match. In the bottom corner, the text reads: "Footage provided courtesy of PJW, May 9th, 1998". The Banshee is about to defend his International title against Kim Chee but before the match starts, the two bow to each other as a sign of mutual respect.) NARRATOR: ...he must struggle. (On screen, Kim Chee has just levelled The Banshee with a devastating clothesline. He pulls The Banshee to his feet and tries to set him up for a piledriver but The Banshee ducks out of it, scoops up Kim Chee and throws him down with a fast bodyslam.) NARRATOR: And when he seeks to better himself through trial by combat... (The scene changes once again. In the bottom corner, the text reads: "Footage provided courtesy of AWE, March 24th, 1998". A cage match is already in progress and The Banshee is facing a sinister-looking wrestler who calls himself Lusipher.) NARRATOR: ...he must fight! (Lusipher has The Banshee pushed against the side of the ring and he attempts to grab The Banshee's head and ram it into the steel bars of the cage. The Banshee blocks the move and counters by grabbing Lusipher's head and driving it into the cage. Lusipher recoils and falls backward into the center of the ring.) NARRATOR: That man is... (The scene changes once again to the interior of a darkened arena, the stands empty and the house lights out. In the middle of a wrestling ring set in the centre of the arena, a lone spotlight shines down onto the same slender, muscular figure that has been featured in the previous match highlights. He is wearing his trademark green-and-white singlet and green-and-white wrestling mask.) NARRATOR: ...THE BANSHEE! (The scene switches to a solid green field with "THE BANSHEE" written in white across it. Taking a line from one of my favorite American actors, Jason-kun is a legend in his own mind. Domo Arigato gozaimashita! (Sho bows and then sits down next to Agito.) SSS: They yelled my name louder than yours. AN: Yeah, whatever. SSS: They did.
