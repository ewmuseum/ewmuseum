Gunryo Pro — This Week

G-Pro This Week, Issue No. 1

12 August 1998

The next few weeks will be vital for the forming of the league, because this is the time when "the ladder" sorts itself out. Not only are the inaugural title belts in each division up for grabs, but so are placings in the rankings. The big news this week is that the G-Pro website is complete!! It hasn't got all of the bits and pieces quite yet, but you'll find stuff such as the roster, rankings, news, the FAQ and application form etc at....... http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/2059/main.html Just take note that the profiles aren't uploaded on the roster page yet, and take no notice of the rankings. It's impossible to have proper rankings after one card, and I expect it'll be another couple of cards before anything should be read into the rankings. As you'll see below, the roster is filling up quickly. Newcomers since the last newsletter include Heavyweights Gaijin, Sean Sabre and Marc Thomas, Super-Middleweights Vinny Vitrone and Kutoh Ijema, and Jisatsus Brass Knuckles, Ketsueki Ikeda, Demon Boy Ishrinku, Dr Pirateo K and Kaijuu. On the other side of the ledger, the Down Boys have been released from their tour contracts. Plans are already being put together for the first Gunryo Pro supercard, to be held in the Osaka Dome at the end of August. We'll be keeping you up to date as more news comes to hand, but its fair to say we're aiming for an action-packed night. ---------- 2) Roster ---------- As at August 12 1998: HEAVYWEIGHT DIVISION: -------------------------------- 1) "Flyer" Jinsei Shakanuzi 2) Frazer Fury 3) Agito Nakajima (Fab Ones) 4) Simon "Thunderstryke" McQuaid 5) Sho Satsuma (Fab Ones) 6) "Thunda from Down Unda" Jason Storm 7) "Wildcat" Jim Lewis jnr 8) Ozeki Shumasita 9) Gaijin 10) Sean Sabre 11) Marc Thomas SUPER-MIDDLEWEIGHT DIVISION: ------------------------------------------ 1) Kabuki Kid 2) Kashan Akuma 3) The Banshee 4) Hakishi Takahashi 5) Kabuki Master 6) Chakushi Tenno 7) "Uncensored" Vinny Vitrone 8) Kutoh Ijema JISATSU (HARDCORE) DIVISION: ---------------------------------------- 1) "Suicide" Sid Snow 2) Hiroyoshi "Flamehand" Hamada 3) Brass Knuckles 4) Ketsueki Ikeda 5) Demon Boy Ishrinku 6) Dr Pirateo K 7) Kaijuu TRIOS TEAMS: ------------------ Daimyo - Kabuki Kid and the Fab Ones Zokugun Sangai - Frazer Fury, Kashan Akuma and Hiroyoshi Hamada Hiryuu Batsu - Kabuki Master, The Banshee, Ozeki Shumasita International Brotherhood - Jinsei Shakanuzi, Wildcat Jim Lewis jnr, Simon McQuaid That's it at this stage - if you're not in a trios already, start thinking about who you'd like to team up with. Also, the two teams above need to think about a name. ------------------------- 3) Line-up for next card: ------------------------- Battlefield, Saturday August 15 1) Trios cage match main event: International Brotherhood (Shakanuzi/Lewis jnr/McQuaid) vs Hiryuu Batsu (Banshee/K.Master/Shumasita) 2) First round Heavyweight title tourney: Frazer Fury vs Gaijin 3) Super-Middleweight match: Kabuki Kid vs Chakushi Tenno 4) Heavyweight match: "Thunda from Down Unda" Jason Storm vs Sean Sabre 5) Jisatsu match (electric barbwire, exploding barricades, glass): "Suicide" Sid Snow vs Brass Knuckles 6) Jisatsu match (electric barbwire, glass, bin of weapons): Ketsueki Ikeda vs Hiroyoshi "Flamehand" Hamada 7) Super-Middleweight match: Hakishi Takahashi vs "Uncensored" Vinny Vitrone 8) Tagteam match: Fabulous Ones vs Tobu Toratenki/Joe Kamikaze (j) 9) Super-Middleweight match: Kashan Akuma vs Masuruo Choutenka (j) Dark matches: Marc Thomas vs Kuro Bakemono (j) Ketoh Ojima vs Supero Makeinu (j) ------------------------ 4) Rumours: ------------------------ * Rumour has it that G-Pro is in negotiations with one of the leading US-based leagues, for some cross-promotional matches during the month of September. * According to sources, a two-time SJPW Battle Arts champion is set to sign for the G-Pro Jisatsu division. His history with Demon Boy Ishrinku could make for some interesting times indeed. ------------------- 5) Weekly awards: ------------------- Each week we'll be presenting a set of fun awards, each taken from the preceeding Saturday's Battlefield......... * Wrestler of the week - The Daimyo for their main event win over Zokugun Sangai in the first cage trios match of Gunryo Pro. * Match of the week - The main event - Daimyo vs Zokugun Sangai * Spot of the week - Frazer Fury slamming Kabuki Kid with Kashan Akuma's I-Crown Asia belt, signalling the continuation of bitter feelings between the two threesomes. Following close behind was the Daimyo's Dai Itai and Kamikaze Drop which won the main event. * Funniest moment of the week - Billy Thomas' continued mis-calling of moves. If you have any suggestions for awards, or votes for any of these, email them to me after Battlefield. --------------------- 6) Application form: --------------------- OFFICIAL GUNRYO PRO APPLICATION FORM: ===================================== PERSONAL INFO: --------------- Your real name: Your email address: ICQ?: AOLIM?: IRC nickname?: WRESTLER INFO: -------------- Wrestler's Name: Height: Weight: Orientation (face, heel, tweener): Manager/Valet (Optional): Describe manager/valet's appearance and attitude: Hometown and origin country: Appearance and ring attire [please be descriptive]: Wrestler's personality [again should be detailed]: Entrance (theme music, lights etc) - Please write this out as it would appear on the card, except for the ring announcer's words.
