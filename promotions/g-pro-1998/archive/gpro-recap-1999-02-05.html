The Avatar vs Jun Fuchiyama =========================== Division: Super-middleweight Winner: The Avatar Time: 4:26 Highlights: The G-Pro fans' first chance to have a look at newcomer The Avatar, who impressed in disposing of local youngster Jun Fuchiyama. The Avatar, who has wrestled in leagues such as the CIWF in the past, came to the ring dressed all in white, with the words "Avenging Angel" on his tights, and accompanied by his bodyguard/valet Angel of Darkness. The fans were a little slow to get into this match, but once The Avatar started showing some technical prowess, the crowd got right in behind him. Fuchiyama showed he wasn't about to lie down, taking the match right to The Avatar in the early stages. A nice hurricanrana by Fuchiyama put the man in white on the backfoot, but after a small period of offence, Avatar took the match to the mat, where he clearly dominated. A beautiful overhead belly-to-belly suplex by The Avatar was followed by the newcomer applying his coveted inverted figure-four leglock, the Cry for Vengeance. Fuchiyama tapped out immediately. "Cataclysmic" Chris Johnson vs Rakurai ====================================== Division: Jisatsu Winner: Chris Johnson Time: 6:48 Highlights: Another making his debut, Johnson locked horns with local hardcore wrestler Rakurai. Johnson glided to the ring on a zipchord attached to the Jumbotron big screen, which didn't get the reaction he was probably hoping for. In fact, the fans were fairly silent for most of this match, only the extreme-loving ones making much noise. This match was fought under no-disqualification rules, with Johnson giving up a good 50 or so pounds to Rakurai. This didn't matter too much though, because there was little actual wrestling in the match. The action stayed in the ring for less than a minute, with both wrestlers keen to take it to the outside. Once it got there, Rakurai got the advantage through a stiff chairshot, before both wrestlers used the ring railing, ring posts, and chairs to inflict severe punishment on each other. One spot which got the fans cheering was Johnson DDTing Rakurai through a set-up folding chair. The action spilled into the crowd for a bit, but soon got back to the ringside area, where Johnson set up a ringside table, and put Rakurai through it with a senton backsplash from the apron. A powerbomb on the floor later, and Johnson climbed to the top turnbuckle. As Rakurai lay on the floor, Johnson executed a backflip, into two full turns and ended by an open body splash. This brought a huge pop from the fans, and after Johnson rolled Rakurai back into the ring, soon brought about the end of the match. Akujin vs Serei Ketsueki ======================== Division: Jisatsu Winner: No match Time: 0:00 Highlights: Soon after the introductions were made for this match, Demon Boy Ishrinku stormed out and laid both wrestlers out with two of his patented Northern Lights Bombs.
