Satsuma sends Storm over with another snap suplex and then tags Nakajima back in. Nakajima comes running in, but instead of grabbing Storm he hooks Satsuma's head! Satsuma looks at the referee with an irritated look on his face, before leaving the ring, as Nakajima drags Storm to his feet. Nakajima tries again, but the same outcome. The fans pop as Storm shrugs Nakajima's arm away and grabs his head, ramming it extra-hard into the turnbuckle. Agito looks around in surprise, just in time to get a boot right in the face. The blow sends him flying off the turnbuckle. Satsuma, who is strutting for the fans, looks around as he hears the THUD!, thinking it is his partner executing the move. Jason Storm got out of the Dai Itai, and both members of the Fabulous Ones are down! BT: Yeah, but so's Storm. He's taken a hell of a beating. That reminds me kids, if you happen to come to Australia on holiday, watch out for the animals. In the few days we've been here I've been chased by a lizard with a big puffed-out neck, nearly been drained of blood by mosquitos the size of small birds, and just about had the crap kicked out of me by a bunch of guys in football jerseys who didn't take too kindly to my sheep jokes. What a terrible outcome for this match! The Australian fans are unhappy at seeing their fellow countrymen cheated out of a match!
