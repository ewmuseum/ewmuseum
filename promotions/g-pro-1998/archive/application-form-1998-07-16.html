OFFICIAL GUNRYO PRO APPLICATION FORM: ===================================== PERSONAL INFO: --------------- Your real name: Your email address: ICQ?: AOLIM?: IRC nickname?: WRESTLER INFO: -------------- Wrestler's Name: Height: Weight: Orientation (face, heel, tweener): Manager/Valet (Optional): Describe manager/valet's appearance and attitude: Hometown and origin country: Which division you would like your wrestler to participate in G-Pro: Heavyweight [220 pds +] Super-middleweight [219 pounds and under] Jisatsu [hardcore division] Tour [up to five weeks touring Nippon Pro without wrestling anywhere else] Appearance and ring attire [please be descriptive]: Wrestler's personality [again should be detailed]: Entrance (theme music, lights etc) - Please write this out as it would appear on the card, except for the ring announcer's words. In other words, the description of what the wrestler is doing as they come to the ring. 10 favourite moves: Please describe each move. Finishing move: Please describe in full, don't just give a name. Strengths (Choose three of the following or make up your own): 1) Aerial 2) Technical 3) Toughness 4) Extreme 5) Cheating 6) Submission 7) Agility 8) Fan support 9) Cockiness 10) Stamina 11) Teamwork 12) Strength Weaknesses (give three weaknesses your wrestler suffers from): Be honest guys - no wrestler is perfect. Profile: (wrestler's personal and wrestling background - make it long and descriptive) [It is important you answer the following by marking an X in the box to the left if you agree with the statement] [ ] I would you be willing to do some match writing for G-Pro [if so, how much could you do?] [ ] I could help out in another way [if so, explain how]. [ ] I agree that I am joining G-Pro for fun, and will not put my own ego ahead of the enjoyment of others? I also agree that I will accept losing if it means advancing an angle. [ ] I don't mind having my name and email address put on a list which will be sent out to the handlers for G-Pro purposes (angles etc). Please attach a sample roleplay and send this form to Fletch at this address. BTW - Japanese wrestlers have more chance of being accepted.
