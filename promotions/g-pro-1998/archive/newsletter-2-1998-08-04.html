GUNRYO PRO NEWSLETTER [Edition 2, 3/8/98] ===================================== WRITTEN BY: G-Pro chairman Fletch In this newsletter...... 1) News 2) Roster as at 3/8/98 3) Line-up for debut Battlefield on Saturday August 8 4) Upcoming deadlines 5) Application form ---------- 1) News ---------- Hi everyone, Well, after much pondering, some advice-seeking and finally some hard thinking [my head still hurts], I've decided to change the G-Pro weekly schedule to better suit handlers, bookers and writers. Everything will basically be the same, but with some minor changes. Now the schedule will looks like this...... Saturdays - Battlefield (the weekly live G-Pro show) Mondays - Recap (a RP packet featuring interviews about the previous Battlefield). This will include the lineup for the coming Saturday's Battlefield. Wednesdays - G-Pro This Week (this newsletter, on a new day, with news, rumours, surveys, handler interviews, sometimes the results of house shows etc etc) Fridays - Battle Stations (the preview show of Battlefield, in which a G-Pro presenter will talk about the following night's show, and RPs from all of the wrestlers will be shown, split into their matches. If you've ever seen IIWF Countdown, you'll know what I'm talking about). I think you'll agree that makes for a pretty exciting week's reading, and it also helps our booking committee to no end. It basically means you should RP twice a week, with the Battle Stations show the most important of the two. To find out the deadlines for this new schedule, read on in part 4). Apart from that, there's not much to report. I think everyone's excited with anticipation as we await the very first Battlefield. ---------- 2) Roster ---------- As at August 4 1998: HEAVYWEIGHT DIVISION: -------------------------------- 1) "Flyer" Jinsei Shakanuzi 2) Frazer Fury 3) Agito Nakajima (Fab Ones) 4) Simon "Thunderstryke" McQuaid 5) Sho Satsuma (Fab Ones) 6) "Thunda from Down Unda" Jason Storm 7) "Wildcat" Jim Lewis jnr 8) Ozeki Shumasita 9) VACANT 10) VACANT SUPER-MIDDLEWEIGHT DIVISION: ------------------------------------------ 1) Kabuki Kid 2) Kashan Akuma 3) The Banshee 4) Hakishi Takahashi 5) Kabuki Master 6) Chakushi Tenno 7) VACANT 8) VACANT JISATSU (HARDCORE) DIVISION: ---------------------------------------- 1) "Suicide" Sid Snow 2) Hiroyoshi "Flamehand" Hamada 3) VACANT 4) VACANT 5) VACANT 6) VACANT 7) VACANT TOUR: -------- 1) Down Boys 2) VACANT 3) VACANT 4) VACANT 5) VACANT TRIOS TEAMS: ------------------ Daimyo - Kabuki Kid and the Fab Ones ???? - Frazer Fury, Kashan Akuma and Hiroyoshi Hamada ???? - Kabuki Master, The Banshee, Ozeki Shumasita That's it at this stage - if you're not in a trios already, start thinking about who you'd like to team up with. Also, the two teams above need to think about a name. ------------------------- 3) Line-up for next card: ------------------------- Battlefield, Saturday August 8 - G-Pro's debut card...... 1) Trios cage match main event: Kabuki Kid/Fab Ones vs Akuma/Fury/Hamada 2) First round Heavyweight Title tournament: Jinsei Shakanuzi vs Jason Storm 3) First round Heavyweight Title tournament: Simon McQuaid vs Wildcat Jim Lewis jnr 4) First round Super-Middleweight Title tournament: Kabuki Master vs Hakishi Takahashi 5) First round Super-Middleweight Title tournament: The Banshee vs Chakushi Tenno 6) Tagteam match: Down Boys vs jobber team (TBA) 7) Jisatsu match (stips to be added): Sid Snow vs jobber (TBA) 8) Heavyweight match: Ozeki Shumasita vs jobber (TBA) ------------------------ 4) Upcoming deadlines: ------------------------ * All deadlines are Midnight US EST Wednesday August 5 - deadline for RPs for Battle Stations (Friday August 7) Sunday August 9 - deadline for RPs for Recap (Monday August 10) Wednesday August 12 - deadline for RPs for Battle Stations (Friday August 14) AND match strats for Battlefield (Saturday August 15) Strats are vitally important in G-Pro, because wrestling style and innovation is a big part of Japanese wrestling. Coming up with good "spots" (innovative moves or sequences of moves) and a good overall strat will be taken into account when deciding matches. --------------------- 5) Application form: --------------------- OFFICIAL GUNRYO PRO APPLICATION FORM: ===================================== PERSONAL INFO: --------------- Your real name: Your email address: ICQ?: AOLIM?: IRC nickname?: WRESTLER INFO: -------------- Wrestler's Name: Height: Weight: Orientation (face, heel, tweener): Manager/Valet (Optional): Describe manager/valet's appearance and attitude: Hometown and origin country: Appearance and ring attire [please be descriptive]: Wrestler's personality [again should be detailed]: Entrance (theme music, lights etc) - Please write this out as it would appear on the card, except for the ring announcer's words.
