[Kabuki Master scoops Kabuki Kid up once more, and drops him throatfirst across the top rope, snapping Kid's head back and sending him crashing to the mat. The fans start booing as Master sends a couple of hard stomps down onto the side of Kid's head.] BT: Wow, the fans are actually booing Kabuki Master. I'm sure goody-goody Banshee doesn't like to see his teammates get booed like this. JY: I'm sure he doesn't. But Kabuki Master does seem more aggressive than usual. [Again Kabuki Master gets Kabuki Kid to his feet, but this time Kid fights back with a couple of chops to Master's chest, which bring a pop from the crowd. Just when it looks like Kabuki Kid is coming back though, he gets a thumb in his eye, which halts his momentum. Kabuki Master follows up by nailing Kid with a kneelift right into the side of the cheek, and then doubling around behind Kid.....] JY: GERMAN SUPLEX BY KABUKI MASTER!!! He's bridging..... ONE!! TWO!! TH - Kickout by Kabuki Kid at two and a bit. BT: It's been all Kabuki Master so far, apart from that maniacal dive out of the ring by Kabuki Kid. JY: Hai. Which may have done Kabuki Kid more harm than his opponent. [Kabuki Kid is sent into the ropes by Kabuki Master once more, and Master lines up for a backdrop, sending Kid flying over and to the mat. But somehow Kabuki Kid manages to land on his feet, and he runs into the opposite ropes, jumping up and springing off the ropes.....] JY: OH!! MOONSAULT BODYBLOCK BY KABUKI KID!! BT: Maybe we shouldn't write him off just yet. [Kabuki Kid seems to have gotten a new burst of energy, as he rockets to his feet and climbs to the top rope. As Kabuki Master gets to his feet, Kabuki Kid leaps off....] BT: OH!!! JUMPING LEG SPEAR!! JY: MISSILE DROPKICK!! Kabuki Kid hooks the leg...... ONE!! TWO!! Kickout by Kabuki Master!! What a great comeback by Kabuki Kid, who seems to have shaken off some of the ringrust he was showing just moments ago. [Both men are back to their feet, and Kabuki Kid nails his opponent with a couple of European uppercuts, before grabbing him by the hair and running across the ring. Kid leaps over the top rope while keeping hold of the head, dropping Kabuki Master's throat across the top rope.] BT: HOLY COW!!! JY: Kabuki Kid using some of Kabuki Master's own tactics against him, by going for the neck!! Both men are down.... [The camera shows Kabuki Master squirming on the mat, holding his throat, and then pans to show Kabuki Kid climbing to his feet at ringside, holding his neck with a grimace on his face.] JY: It looks like Kabuki Kid may have injured the neck once more, which would be a great shame, because he was just starting to get back into this match. [Kabuki Master rolls out of the ring, and catches Kabuki Kid with a kick in the ribs, followed by a palm blow right on the side of the injured neck. Kabuki Kid falls to one knee. Master then reaches into his trunks, and as this happens, Kid's fan towel man sees this happening and runs into the ring to tell the ref that something's not right. The referee turns his back on the two Kabukis to get the towel man out of the ring, which gives Kabuki Master the opening he was looking for. He pops something into his mouth, and then as Kabuki Kid gets back to his feet, he unleashes a big cloud of green spray into Kid's face, which brings a loud reaction from the fans.] JY: OHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!! GREEN MIST BY KABUKI MASTER!! BT: I like it! JY: The referee didn't see it! If he had of, it would surely have been a disqualification. BT: But this is a towel match. JY: So? It's not a no-disqualification match. [Kabuki Master rolls Kabuki Kid back into the ring, and makes the cover, as the referee is successful in getting the fan from the ring, and turns to make the count.] JY: COVER! ONE!!! TWO!!! THR - NOOOOO!!! KICKOUT BY KABUKI KID!! AMAZING! BT: It just keeps getting more incredible as the night goes on Jin - supercards sure to bring out the best in wrestlers. [Kabuki Master starts arguing with the referee, before turning back to Kabuki Kid and stomping on his head a couple of times, which brings a big chorus of boos from the crowd. Master then gets Kid to his feet, the Daimyo member still blinded by the green substance. Master moves around behind Kid and hooks him around the waist. He then thrusts back.....] JY: RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX RIGHT INTO THE TURNBUCKLE!!! KABUKI KID'S HEAD HIT THE TURNBUCKLE EXTRA-HARD!!! BT: He's done now. [Rather than go for the pin, Kabuki Master gets Kabuki Kid up once more and drags him to the nearest corner. Master then climbs onto the second turnbuckle and spins Kid up into a position for a Tombstone....] BT: OW! OW! OW! RIGHT ON THE TOP OF HIS HEAD!!! JY: Pegasus Driver by Kabuki Master, and that must surely be it! Kabuki Master going for the lazy cover now..... ONE!! TWO!!! THREEEEEEEE!!!! BT: NO!! [The referee thrusts two fingers into the air.] BT: Kabuki Master didn't hook the leg, and that might have been the difference between two and three! JY: Kabuki Kid kicked out, but how much more of this can he take? [Kabuki Master whips Kabuki Kid into the ropes, but Kid reverses it, dropping to the mat as Master crosses over on the rebound. On the second pass, Kabuki Master runs straight into a kneelift from Kid, which hits him in the midsection and sends him flipping over and to the mat. Kabuki Kid runs into the nearby ropes and springs off the second rope.......] JY: LA QUEBRADA!!!!! KID HOOKS THE LEG...... ONE!!! TWO!!! TH - Kickout by Kabuki Master, but another close call, and yet another example of the great heart which exists inside Kabuki Kid! [Kabuki Master looks stunned after that last move, and is unsure on his feet as Kabuki Kid gets him up. A couple of European uppercuts help keep Kabuki Master off-kilter, and the Kid then quickly snaps Master over in a suplex, one of his trademark moves. Kid then puts Master in position for a powerbomb/piledriver.....] JY: RUNNING LIGER BOMMMMMMMBBBBBBB!!!! BT: Man did he nail that.....but he's not going for the pin? He could have him right there! JY: He's signalling for the Kamikaze Drop!! [The fans roar as Kabuki Kid signals for his signature finisher, and then scurries to the top rope. He holds both arms out to his side as the fans cheer like mad, and then leaps off, soaring through the air with a beautiful swandive before slamming into the mat with a headbutt. It is the mat, because Kabuki Master has rolled out of harm's way. A few screams and a general sound of surprise emanates from the crowd.] BT: HE MISSED!! HE MISSED!! JY: KABUKI MASTER MANAGED TO ROLL OUT OF THE WAY!! [Kabuki Kid lies on his stomach, not moving.] JY: OH NO! Kabuki Kid may have seriously injured his already injured neck with that move!! This is terrible! BT: He knew the risks involved, but he still went for it. It's his own fault if he's out cold right now. [Kabuki Master rolls to his feet and gets a big grin on his face, as he taps the side of his head as if to say "I was too smart for that". He then signals that he is going for his finisher, the Stardust Press.] BT: OH YEAH!!! STARDUST PRESS COMING UP!!! [As Kabuki Master climbs to the top turnbuckle, something unexpected happens.....The Banshee jumps onto the apron and throws his towel into the ring. This brings a pop of surprise from the fans, and the referee looks down at the towel, up at The Banshee, and back down at the towel, before signalling for the bell.] DING DING DING! JY: WHAT? BT: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON? [Kabuki Master drops to the mat on his feet, a look of both shock and anger on his face. Suddenly Frazer Fury comes running down the aisle, holding something in his left hand, followed closely by Ozeki Shumasita, and....] JY: THE BANSHEE!!! WHAT ON EARTH IS HAPPENING? [Shumasita and this second Banshee look highly angry as they storm the ring. Meanwhile, the original Banshee quickly unmasks, revealing....] BT: AKUMA!! KASHAN AKUMA WAS IN THE BANSHEE MASK!!! JY: I don't believe this! [Fury and Akuma greet Shumasita and The Banshee with their championship belts, flattening the two fan favourites. Meanwhile, Kabuki Master grabs what Fury has in his hand and climbs to the top turnbuckle with it. He slips whatever it is over his head......] BT: OH MY GOD!!! JY: TORA WANIZAME!!! IT WAS KABUKI MASTER ALL ALONG!! THIS IS SIMPLY MIND-BOGGLING!!! BT: Kabuki Master was Zokugun Sangai all along? Even I didn't know that! [The fans boo as Tora Wanizame climbs back down to the ring, where Fury and Akuma are beating on Kabuki Kid, who looks pretty badly beaten. Suddenly the Fabulous Ones storm from the back.] JY: ALL HELL HAS BROKEN LOOSE!! THE DAIMYO, HIRYUU BATSU AND ZOKUGUN SANGAI ARE ALL IN THE RING, WHICH NOW RESEMBLES A WAR-ZONE!!! BT: This is GREAT!! [The fans erupt as Jason Storm suddenly sprints from backstage, joining the action. Now The Banshee and Kashan Akuma are battling, resuming their war from earlier in the evening, while Sho Satsuma and Jason Storm trade blows, and Agito Nakajima and Ozeki Shumasita go at it. Tora Wanizame picks up the beaten Kabuki Kid and piledrives him in the middle of the ring, bringing more boos.] JY: NOOOOOO!!!! SOMEBODY HELP KABUKI KID!! BT: YEAH! ZOKUGUN! ZOKUGUN!! YEE-HA!! JY: Stop that Billy-san. This is not funny! [The Banshee and Kashan Akuma are now battling at ringside, as Storm and Satsuma go tumbling through the ropes and continue beating on each other on the floor. Nakajima sees Kabuki Kid getting beaten up by Tora Wanizame, so leaves Shumasita and saves his teammate, scaring off Wanizame before dragging his unconscious stablemate out of the ring. That leaves two men face to face in the ring....Frazer Fury with a chair, and Ozeki Shumasita.] BT: FRAZ-MAN!!! JY: HE'S GOT A CHAIR!! LOOKOUT!!!! [Fury swings the chair hard, bringing it down on Shumasita's head with a loud noise.] CLANG!!! [But Shumasita takes the blow and carries on standing, a look of fire in his eyes. The fans pop BIGTIME at this, as Fury gets a sudden look of fear on his face.] JY: OH MY GOD! BT: I DON'T BELIEVE IT!! HE'S STILL STANDING!! [Fury swings again, but this time Shumasita catches the chair in his hands, and rips it free of Fury's grasp. He then spins it around in his hands and crowns Fury with it, sending the Zokugun Sangai member sprawling from the ring. The fans POP!!] JY: FURY TASTES THE CHAIR!!! INCREDIBLE!!
