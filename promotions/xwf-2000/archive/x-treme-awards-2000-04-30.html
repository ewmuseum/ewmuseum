MAIN EVENT
AHALA vs. THE JACKAL ( Outdoor Buried Alive Title Match ) No Time Limit XWF Extreme Championship Title Match Outside on a small hill, with a freshly dug grave and blank headstone ( one shovel stuck in a mound of dirt) ready to fill the grave, the storm is a bit calmer, it is dark, very wet, lightning and thunder echo and crash, as The Jackal is seen making his way to the top of the hill.
Ahala is seen on the other side, as the two walk to the top, Ahala drapes the EC title over the head stone, the two hover near the grave plot, as
Ahala tells The Jackal not to fear, in no way, shape or form, will Damona or the SUPER FIEND play a part in this match.
The one referee is in his parka rain jacket, and signals to start, Jackal and Ahala lock up, both try to push the other in the grave, Ahala goes first, quickly stepping into the grave, then out, and into A DDT, Ahala is planted, then pushed back into the grave, but Jackal knows it won't be that easy, as he leap into the grave with a double knee drop.
Jackal climbs out of the grave, as Ahala reaches up and pulls him back in, the two fist fight, then stand, locked in battle, they twist around and both climbs out of the grave, The Jackal nails another move, a swinging neck breaker, and both go down, Jackal is back up and stomps Ahala, then lifts her up into his Power of the Beast (Fisherman's Suplex), Ahala slams the ground, then bounces almost full into the grave, as Jackal closes in,
Ahala springs to life, grabbing the shovel and whacking Jackal's ankle with it. Ahala is out of the grave, and takes a full swing at The Jackal with the shovel, he takes the impact on his ribs, lifting his arm out of the way, then down again, catching the shovel, then tries a face kick, but Ahala catches it, dropping her hold on the shovel, The Jackal tries a counter move, but Ahala uses her strength and flips The Jackal into the grave,
Ahala then quickly picks up the shovel, and in a stabbing motion, tries to impale The Jackal in the grave, but he leaps out and avoids the shovel slash.
Ahala uses the shovel like a pole vault, and diving heel kicks The Jackal, Ahala then nails a spinning heel kick and jackknife power bomb, slamming The Jackal into the head stone, his is knocked out, as Ahala then gorilla press slams him into the grave, she then starts to shovel the dirt into the grave.
The Jackal comes to and sits up, grabbing Ahala by the leg, and pulling her into the grave, the two are in a fist fight, both in the grave and start to stand, finally, while both are stepping out of the grave, The
Jackal delivers his The Decimator (Double Underhook driver), and Ahala is planted back into the grave, this time she is knocked out, The Jackal begins to fill in the grave.
The scene is perfect, semi-dark, The Jackal is at the base of the grave, as he shovels in loads of dirt over Ahala, the lightning flashes behind him, highlighting his features, as he shovels, then during a dark period after a lightning blast, a female figure, with a shovel, can be seen creeping up on the unsuspecting Jackal, when the lightning crashes again, the women, lifts the shovel over her head and whack's The Jackal in the back of his head, he does not fall down however.
With lightning crashing, the female becomes visible, it is Sweet Sensual, The Jackal's own manager, she is stunned that he still stands, he looks at her, his eye's show that he is not up to taking her down too, he has had enough blood letting for the day, he just glares at her, as if he doesn't even care, Sweet Sensual drops the shovel and slap's The Jackal across his face.
While this is happening Ahala is seen climbing out of the grave, she is dirty, but none the less, out of the grave, the referee is freaking out, as The Jackal still is unmoved, then as lightning crashes again, another figure, this time a tall slim man, is seen, approaching the grave and The
Jackal, at the same angle Sweet Sensual did, as the lightning flashes for a long time, you fully see....
The Red Raven run up the hill, leap into the air, and at the same time grabs The Jackal by the side of his head and slams him into the grave with his The Raven Drop (Sitting Bulldog), The Jackal is out again, he is in the grave, TRR has just betrayed his team, as he turns to Ahala.
Ahala has both shovels in her hands ..... she then hold one out to The Red Raven, and together they fill in the dirt to the grave, with two of them, The Jackal is buried alive in no time flat, the referee clarifies, that Ahala is the winner. TRR and Ahala both sling there title over their shoulders and walk back to the XWF Party Hall, Sweet Sensual is stuck to Raven like glue, the storm calms, the thunder crashes, the grave is matted with rain, the camera is about to loose focus, when a single hand is seen, breaking through the ground and out of the grave right as a white bright shot of lightning hits the grave stone, The Jackal will fight again ........
