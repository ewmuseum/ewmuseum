TIMMY McREADY vs. KAMIKAZE ( Standard Match ) 15 minute time limit Tyler - "Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is set for one fall, with a 10 minute time limit." There is the sound of a W.W.II fighter diving, then a loud explosion with flash effects and pyros. "Danger Zone" starts playing and Kamikaze walks to the ring, enters, and bows to ring announcer.
Tyler - " Now in the ring from Japan, he is the mysterious and honorable ..... KAMIKAZE !!!!!!!!! " Tyler - "And his opponent, from Wendover, Nevada Timmy McReady!!" The trumpets open up "Ring of Fire" and Timmy McReady steps out from behind the curtain. He wears a pair of heavily tinted out-of-style Top
Gun or State Trooper style glasses to the ring. He struts to the ring as if all the fans were cheering for him.
***The bell rings*** Timmy bows to Kamikaze, and Kamikaze returns the salute. Timmy McReady puts a boot in Kamikaze's midsection, then connects with an Asai moonsault! Kamikaze gets to his feet and hits a good missile dropkick, followed by a neckbreaker. When Timmy gets to his feet, Kamikaze does a solid enzuguri, and follows with a moonsault. Timmy McReady whips Kamikaze to the ropes, and follows with a boot to the face, and follows up with a drop toe hold.
Kamikaze connects with a flying clothesline, and goes to the ropes. Kamikaze hits with a good shooting star press. When Timmy gets up, he gets hit with a tornado DDT. Timmy begs off, asking for mercy. Kamikaze backs away. Timmy blindsides him, and knocks him down.
Timmy McReady hits with a hard Asai moonsault. But Kamikaze smashes Timmy McReady into the mat with a flying neckbreaker. Timmy rolls away, and out to the floor. Kamikaze watches him.
Timmy gets back in, and plants Kamikaze with his Double Face Drop-kick. Timmy McReady covers Kamikaze. Referee counts 'ONE!' Kamikaze kicks out with authority! Timmy McReady lands with a solid back rake, but Kamikaze turns around and hits with a good hurricane DDT. Kamikaze does a German suplex, followed by a hard tornado DDT. Then he goes up to the ropes and hits with a frogsplash. Then he goes for a moonsault and that hits too. Timmy struggles to his feet, and
Kamikaze lands with a solid spinning heel kick. Kamikaze covers Timmy McReady Referee counts 'ONE!' Timmy McReady kicks out with authority! Kamikaze goes back up to the top and does another frogsplash! Kamikaze covers Timmy McReady. Referee counts 'ONE!' Timmy McReady kicks out with authority! Timmy McReady once again rolls to the outside, begging for mercy. On the referee's count of 9 he rolls back into the ring, Kamikaze allows him to get in unharmed. Timmy looks exhausted, and walks slowly to
Kamikaze. He offers his hand for a handshake, saying that Kamikaze is much better then he thought. Kamikaze reaches out to accept, but McReady lands a Double Face Drop-kick! Timmy McReady then hits his finisher, the Wendover Bendover (double underhook driver) Timmy McReady covers Kamikaze. Referee counts 'ONE!' Referee counts 'TWO!' Referee counts 'THREE!' Timmy McReady wins by pinfall! Time: 5 minutes 33 seconds *** The bell rings*** After the match Timmy dances around the ring, celebrating like he just won the world title. He points and trash talks Kamikaze. Not at all like lovable guy we have seen in his interviews.
Crazy Noodle Boy's "music" hits and he comes strutting down to the ring holding his Extreme title belt proudly over his head. He grabs a mike once in ring.
CNB: Peas be with you mold! I am your leader and champion! It doesn't mean I am better than you!
Not until Sunday when I beat the Hardcore Champ and then I shall be SUPER DOUBLE CHAMP! And speaking of champs, here comes, the HARDCORE CHAMP!! Ahala's music hits, but instead of Ahala it is Burger Baby wearing a bathrobe. He walks into the ring. He stands in the middle for a moment looking confused. Crazy
Noodle Boy walks over and removes the robe to reveal the XWF HARD-CORE TITLE BELT ! CNB: LA LA LA! I agreed to your straight story! Now you MUST agree to my treasure hunt. See, what kind of Hardcore champ would I be if I beat you with out really BEATING
YOU! So after the match we will have BIG FUN trying to find these belts. I have instructed Burger Baby Buggy here to go and hide these straps o' gold somewhere in that arena. Crazy Noodle Boy takes his belt and straps it around Burger Baby's waist. CNB: Too bad you will NEVER see your belt again, AR lar lar! HAHAHAHA! (the next part is up to you) At this point Ahala's music hits again. Crazy Noodle Boy looks surprised. He scoots Burger Baby out of the ring as Ahala shoots to the ring like a bat out of hell. In seconds CNB and Ahala are exchanging rights and lefts as Burger Baby runs through the crowd and out of sight. Referees hit the ring to break up the fight. Ahala slides out of the ring and grabs a chair the same time CNB hops over the top rope and grabs a chair as well. They circle around the ring and meet each other with a flurry of chair shots. The refs hop out of the ring as CNB gets one last shot in the head that sends him into the crowd. Ahala is on the top turnbuckle in an instant with a chair. She flies off and lands on CNB with the chair, as the fans disperse that area. The fight continues on through the crowd as the next match begins....

THE GLACIER vs. QUEZZY ( Standard Match ) 15 minute time limit
Tyler - " The following match is the standard XWF non title match, with a 15 minute, time limit ! " Hero began to play and the fans started to pop huge as Quezzy and Krish hit the arena, they then got in the ring as The World of Sleaze then began to play and the XWF
Super Heavy Weight Champion came out, he got A huge pop, then spoke some words of Quezzy been one of the XWF upstarts, and that he would add the 2nd loss to his record.
Once in the ring, The Glacier clearly took it to Quezzy nailing him with everything in his bio, from the flying neck breaker, to the amazing 575 pound running drop kick, even his finisher, the
Titanic (Inverted tombstone pile driver), but pin after pin, without assist from Krish, this
Quezzy would kick out or get his shoulder up. The Glacier was beyond stressed as he lifted up Quezzy into A shoulder breaker, set up, leading to his Ice Ages (people's elbow), but upon delivery, Queezy sat right up and Glacier pounded the unforgiving mat, Quezzy then capitalized and with knee drops, leg drop and finally taking the
Glacier to his feet and then nailing A top rope DDT, but Glacier kicked out of that pin with authority, sending Quezzy out of the ring, through the ropes.
The Glacier again seemed to have it under control, but Quezzy snuck in a low blow, then nailed his The Death Roll (Inverted swinging neck breaker), he applied the
Quezzy Clutch (tazz mission), but it was only to get Glacier tired out so Quezzy could land an incredible sit-down pile driver and leg hook pin, but again The Glacier kicked out, so Quezzy applied the the Boston crab, it was amazing, but The Glacier would not give up.
But just then Damona was seen, running to the ring, she had A steel chair, and laid out C.C. on the way, then slid in the ring and forehead smashed Quezzy while he had the submission hold on still, this knocked him out and split him open, the referee then called for the bell and gave The
Glacier the DQ for interference, as Damona clearly helped him to his feet, the lights went out and she was gone once they returned, The Glacier was screwed !
Tyler - " Winner via pin disqualification ....... QUEZZY !!!!!!!! " ( 8:09 minutes ) After the match Quezzy made a quick exit to help Krish, Glacier was livid, and could not find his
XWF Super Heavy Weight title belt ! Tely - " What else can happen tonight, this is great card, upsets and high action all around ! "
Trent - " I don't know how Quezzy pulled that one off, but The Glacier, has bigger fish to fry, namely Cyric ! "
Sheila - " Next are to nasty SOB's and this is a prelude as in 2 days Jake goes after Lugg-Nutt to see who is the head Villain around here !? " Trent - " Legion ! " Sheila - " That was not a question, dumb ass ! Before the bell rings Ugg-Lee sneak attacks Jake King, nailing him with a off the ropes choke slam, then A suplex face slam, but Jake hit a jackknife power bomb, then running clothesline, set up for the King's Drop (Elbow to the heart, off the top rope), after he then made the pin.
Ugg-Lee kicked out and slowly got to his feet, countering A spear, by moving and pushing Jake
King out of the ring, Jake was stomped upon re-entering the ring, and hit with the Doomsday
(Opponent into a suplex, then drops them in a Spinning Pile driver) and pin. THE JACKAL vs. JUVENILE vs. AVALANCHE
( No DQ / Triple Threat Elimination Match) 20 minute time limit Tyler - " The following match is an XWF no DQ, triple threat, elimination style, non title match, with a 20minute, time limit ! "
Rock and Roll begins to play as Avalanche hits the arena and gets a nice pop from the fans, next is Back That Ass Up as Juvenile and Sweet Sensual hit the arena and the fans pop huge for them, then the XWF Rumble Champion, The Jackal hits the arena and the fans pop huge.
As all three are in the ring, Maxx Fitzgerald hits the arena, and tells The Jackal that this will be his 1st Rumble title defense as the match is also included as A over the top rope mini battle royal, thus making the winner, the next XWF Rumble champion and that person would spotlight the battle royal at XM2000 as well, this was to see if The Jackal was the right choice in Maxx's quest to find a real champion !
Right of Juvenile and The Jackal double teamed the new player, Avalanche hitting him with double clothesline take down, then A double suplex, before Avalanche could even get rolling he was then scoop slammed and tossed out of the ring by both men, and eliminated !
Juvenile and The Jackal then lit it up as they fired fist and boots, left and rights, then Juvenile got in a tiger driver and then a 3/4" neck breaker, but The Jackal turned it around with a swinging neck breaker and then his Flying Dragon Attack (Jumps from turn buckle and flip spin before landing on opponents back) he picked up Juvenile and tried to toss him out of the ring, but Juvenile fought out of the carrying hold.
Juvenile landed his Roll'N (Even flow DDT) and the Jackal was down, but as Juvenile lifted Jackal up and into A press slam to send him over the ropes, Juvenile own manager Sweet Sensual entered the ring, and from behind did the classic on knee, and full arm low blow, causing Juvenile to drop Jackal, who landed on top of him.
Sweet Sensual, then went as far as to help the Jackal to his feet, where he then nailed Juvenile with A pump handle slam, then his new and improved The Decimator
(Double Underhook driver), Jackal then tossed juvenile over the top ropes and retained his title ! Tyler - " Winner via final top rope elimination and still XWF Rumble Champion ....... THE JACKAL !!!!!!!! " ( 5:41 minutes ) After the match, Sweet Sensual raises The Jackal's hand in victory and the two left the arena together !

PHOENIX vs. MACHINEHEAD ( Hard-core Match ) 15 minute time limit Tyler - " The following match is a XWF hard-core, non title match, with a 15 minute, time limit ! " Machinehead plays as he also steps into the arena, fans give a nice pop as he walks to the ring, then Bawitdabe hit the PA system and the fans gave out another huge pop, as Phoenix hit the arena he was with Miss Victoria, Phoenix then entered the ring and the match was on.
It was pretty even each star dealt some key moves, including Machinehead with a tiger suplex and jackknife power bomb, then pin, Phoenix kicked out and then got his round with A shooting star press, then A series of side suplex's leading to the super kick and pin, but Machinehead kicked out.
The fight then led outside the ring, where each was slammed into the ring stair and posts, then
Phoenix smashed A trash can over Machinehead's head, and tried A Blazer (Diamond Cutter off top rope) off A chair, but Machinehead reversed it into A power bomb and slammed Phoenix into the chair, then tried to cover him, but Phoenix kicked out.
Miss Victoria stayed out of it as Machinehead then took, Phoenix over to the Spanish announcers table and set up for his Machinehead Megabomb (Pearl River Plunge), but
Phoenix in mid move, turned it into his own From the Ashes (x-factor), both men slammed through the Spanish announcers table and there was a pile of rubble.
After much time with neither moving much, Phoenix finally rolled onto Machinehead for the pin, only Machinehead kicked out, Phoenix then nailed a splash off the crowd barrier, then a frog splash from the ring apron, leg hook and pin.

BlackJack gets low blowed from outside the ring by Over Fiend, and stumbles right into the Super
