Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Fantasy Wrestling Organazation relaunched on 9 November 1996 after a hiatus. Its own Vice President of Booking and Angles announced it alongside its president, billed as StnCldCow: “FWO recently went on hiatus. But after I volunteered to help the prez... we decided to get this thing back up and running. Champions will be the same.” All five titles carried over unchanged: the World Heavyweight (J.P. Steele), United States (Stero), Television (Storm Shadow), Light Heavyweight (UPS), and World Tag Team (the Knight Foundation) championships.

Its roster was capped at 55, with returning members invited back and only limited new spots open, running on two weekly cards — Monday Night Massacre and Friday Night Extreme — plus a weekly magazine, restarting 1 January 1997. “ROLEPLAYING is the very core of FWO,” its own relaunch post declared, determining whether a wrestler ranked as a jobber, mid-carder, star, or icon; only invented wrestlers were accepted.

No document survives past that relaunch announcement. Whether the January restart produced a single card, or how its five title lineages continued, is not in the surviving record.
