Fantasy Wrestling Organazation, for several months it was a darn good league. but during it's end, it started falling apart sadly. But in a effeort to end the hiatus...I the noble e-wrestling person I am, joined on as Vice President of Bookings,Angles...while maintaing my FWO World
Champion J.P. Steele. January 1st, we kick things of with a bang. All of the champions will stay the same, and all the previosu ones invited to return. But in an effort to get this league better, FWO is open to new wrestlers, and looking for some good ones. Here are the champions-
FWO World Heavyweight Title~JP Steele FWO United States Title~Stero FWO Television Title~Storm Shadow FWO Light Heavyweight Title~UPS FWO World Tag Team Title~The Knight Foundation I've discussed it with the Prez, and we figured to start off one weekly card, and a weekly magazine/report to get things rolling.
And here's what the app. looks like. Simple enough... Name: Height/Weight: Hometown: Music: 10 (or 5) Fav. Moves: No real wrestlers, of course. I don't think there's a limit to how many you may have. But, ROLEPLAYING is the very core of FWO...and it determined if your wrestler is a jobber, mid-carder, star,icon,etc. Good roleplays, this could be a fed where you dominate. If we can get a filled roster, I just might be able to convie the Prez to start of earlier. So, send those apps in to:
