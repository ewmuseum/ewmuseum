An Australian-hosted federation founded in October 1997, its own founder recruiting staff through a run of posts into December, going silent after a community 'what happened' thread with no closure statement of its own. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Australian Baced Wrestling Federation opened on 24 October 1997 with a bare announcement: “A new fed has just started called ABWF Wreslers and writers needed. Join now and you may be involved in a tournement for the world title,” self-hosted at web.access.net.au/abwf.

Over the following month its president posted a string of staffing appeals, each signed only by role: a writer for its weekly Sunday card, a commissioner, and then, in mid-November, someone to keep the interviews page updated and a vice-president “who will help decide the outcome of matches.”

Its most ambitious plan survives only as a pitch. On 15 December 1997 the fed's own cardwriter announced “Promoter Wars,” a strategy tournament open to up to 600 entrants and decided by numbered strategy picks rather than roleplay, expected to run three to five months once it began in January.

A week later, a community member asked what had happened to the ABWF, “waiting to see the results of the Thursday Night House Party,” when it “disappears without a trace” — met by a reply that didn't even remember what it was: “Isn't the ABWF a women's basketball league?” That exchange is the last word on it in the surviving record. Whether Promoter Wars ever ran, and how the promotion ended, isn't in the surviving record.
