Where can I help? 9. I gotta a few questions... 10. Okay I wanna join. What should I do? ======================================================================= 1. What are the purpose of this fed? Well, the ISSW stands for International Super Stars of Wrestling (tks for the suggestion, Beebz).
ISSW is mainly a new idea for e-wrestling. It will work more in the indies format. (Tks Nick for the idea...) Small shows, small roster, not big special effects, either airtime. To be honest, it's a fed purely for fun. So, bitching and complaining will be immediately treated with the "kick in the ass" medicine. 8)
ISSW will be based on Florida, mainly because it's the only US state that I know personally. 8) BUT it may be touring through Caribbean and
Central America, as well as North and South America. ISSW is open to any kind of wrestler, altough I plan to have a ratio of US/Canadian to other countries' wrestlers in 1-1. The roster size will be at the rate of 20 wrestlers, prolly 10 US/Canadian ones and
10 International ones, with the US/Canadian wrestlers never becoming more numerous than the International wrestlers.
But, most important, ISSW is a fed for you and me to have FUN! If I wanted to have a big fed, I would have taken UWF or EMWC over...
Err... I mean... Nevermind. 2. Who run this thing? (Fantasy) ISSW is founded by Mr. John Longweiner, who currently is the President and CEO. Mr. Longweiner is a wrestling promoter since 1975, and is looking for some International "flavor" to US wrestling.
Mr. Longweiner is a man in his 60's, always elegantly dressed, and is very charismatic. His white hair is a tip about his age, altough his looks are still young.
3. Who run this thing? (IRL) Okay, you won... Here's the crazy... I mean the hard workers! 3.1. The Pres. Well, ISSW is founded and it's currently run by the Hardcore Brazilian himself, a.k.a. [name withheld] a.k.a me. 8) I'm the pres, matchwriter, booker... I have the power! BWAHAHAHA!
3.1.1. How can I reach you? Well, I am a man of many contact forms. 8) If you wanna find me easily, the two main ways is to go to IRC or mailing me. Here's my nicks, addys, UIN numbers...
E-MAIL: [detail withheld] IRC: ||Scorpio|| (you can find me in #issw room) [detail withheld] (not very often, altough) ICQ: [detail withheld] (this only available at weekends) 3.2. Match-writers Right now, ISSW has a staff of four matchwriters... - Me - [name withheld] - [name withheld] - [name withheld]. My idea is to have 1 more guy, so we can have the amount of 1 writer per MA's match and to have a different Main Eventer Writer per card. Anyone interested (hey, it's just a bi-weekly job!), can contact me.
4. What are the titles here? ISSW currently have only two belts. The International and the Floridian belt. 4.1. International belt The International belt is DABELT~!. The man who gets it can call himself the "King of the ISSW". This belt is the main belt in
ISSW, and it MUST be defended monthly. Failure to do so means a instantly job on your next match. The champ however can choose to defend it more than once in the same month.
4.2. Floridian belt. The Floridian belt is the other belt here. Not so glamourous as the International, but... It must be defended every Monday
Action, and if the champ chooses to, he can defend it at Report cards too. 4.3. International Tag Teams belts Self-explanatory... Must be defended monthly, like the singles belt. And champions can choose to defend it more than once, if they like to.
5. What are the cards? 5.1. ISSW Monday Action This is the main card! Hosted by Roger Fury and John Beaver Sr., this card shows the ISSW Action in his full intent. The big matches will be always broadcasted here. The main idea for the card line-up is:
. 2 jobber matches . 4 matches involving superstars . 1 main event 5.2. ISSW Prelude to Action This is the "flash show", where there's space for everybody in the roster to speak. Hosted by Sarah Johnson, it can shows live interviews (by Sarah himself), post-MA action, backstage flashes, promos and such.
5.3. ISSW Report This show reports the last happenings in ISSW, such as MA results, info about next cards, signings, departures, the rankings... And also House Shows results. This is the only weekly show at ISSW.
Flashes can also be broadcasted here. Hosted by Roger Fury. 5.4. Supercards Well, right now I don't plan many supercards... We're an indy fed, for God's sake! 8) My plan is to have one as soon as the fed starts, with two tourneys to determine the champions and the rankings. It's temporary scheduled for June 26th and it's called
ISSW Discovery. No more Supercards are planned... yet. 8)
5.5. Normal Schedule Okay, here's some info... As a bi-weekly fed, the schedule here permits more time for me to writing and for you to roleplay.
A normal schedule would be like... 1st week's Mon. - ISSW Monday Action 1st week's Thu. - ISSW Report 2nd week's Mon. - ISSW Prelude to Action 2nd week's Thu. - ISSW Report 3rd week's Mon. - ISSW Monday Action ... And that goes on. 6. Who are the staff members? The guys you won't see nailing each other in the ring... Well, not quite. 6.1. Announcers The voices behind ISSW... Our beloved announcers. 6.1.1. Roger Fury Roger is the man who always look for the right way to call a match. He normally doean't take any sides, altough he condemn cheating. He has a lot of patience, dealing with
Beaver. 6.1.2. John Beaver Sr. The man you wouldn't ever want at your side... Or as you chief. Arrogant, full of himself... He normally likes guys like him (a.k.a. heels), and always tries to take some benefit of all situations... Hey, he put his two kids on the fed just to get more checks at the end of the month.
He also sometimes abuses of the expletive terms, so take care of the children while he's speaking.
6.1.3. Sarah Johnson Sarah is the "okay" girl that every guy admires. Altough not the gorgeous kind of woman, she is kinda attractive.
She is the perfect reporter, always looking for the correct info, no matter if it comes from a face or a heel.
6.2. Interviewers The guys you gotta (if ya want) use in your roleplays. 6.2.1. Sarah Johnson When she's not hosting the Report or the Prelude, she is always backstage searching the truth. Always looking for the correct info, she isn't afraid of facing anyone...
Even the high offices or the biggest heels. 6.2.2. Peter Major Peter is a junior who needs to pay his expenses... And as a pro wrestling fan, he got this job. I personally thinks that Mr. Longweiner has more heart than brains, as Major normally messes the questions, change names and asks things that you shouldn't ask...
6.3. Referees The men in the charge of the rules... Weel, maybe. 6.3.1. Paul Beaver Huh? Yes, that's one of John's kids. But don't try to wonder how he got that job, knowing few or none wrestling rules. Normally John has to shout instructions for him during the matches... And he tends to misunderstand his daddy's instructions. Expect him to give someone a countout while his daddy is asking him to DQ the other wrestler.
6.3.2. Martin Rudolph Martin is a TRUE ref. He knows the job, he calls match right in the middle and allows no cheating. He isn't afraid of calling a DQ even beacuse of a spit. You touch him, you lose!
6.4. Ringside Crew You see them in the ring... Not fighting, not commenting... But they are there! You better believe it!
6.4.1. Linda Woodroe The more-then-gorgeous ringside girl... The one who would you take your eyes out of the match anytime. Luckly for
ISSW, she normally is dressed to kill... And unluckly for the fans, she is happily married. She normally introduces every ISSW Monday Action card.
6.4.2. Charles "The Voice" Piper No relation to a certain wrestler. He's the Ring Announcer for ISSW. He does the job with passion, sometimes even going to far... His only problem is that he loves the introduction part so much. Expect him to shout "AND
HIIIIIIIIIIIIS OPPOOOOOOOONEEEEEEEEEENNNNNNNNNTTTTTT..." while the crowd is already sleeping. 6.4.3. James "Achilles" Wolferson Retired wrestler, "Achilles" is the ISSW Security. His duty is to prevent the fans to invade the ring, the wrestlers to brawl too much and that everything goes according to the schedule. He's a massive man, so don't mess with him or you'll take it!
6.5. Da Jobbers! The guys everybody love to see... Getting beaten. 6.5.1. Johnny Beaver Beaver is the son of John Beaver Sr.... And that can explain how he got that job, after all, his wrestling knowledge is based purely on the hit-and-run strategy...
Better saying, the "run before he hits you" strategy. 6.5.2. Ray Masters Ray likes to call himself "king of hardcore"... Well, for someone who already took EVERY kind of bump and received EVERY type of finishing move... He may be right.
6.5.3. Paul "No Fear" Crip Paul is the insane job... I mean, "employee" that always is looking for a match. He doesn't back up a challenge, altough he should sometimes. Handicap match? He is in.
Explosive cage match against a stable? Sure! 6.5.4. The Blue Bunny Don't ask. I won't answer. 6.5.5. Metal Warriors (Tag Team) Crasher & Destroyer... Two KISS-alike wrestlers... Who signs better than wrestle. They made a special show each
Thursday at the Johnny's Metal Bar at Miami, Florida. 7. What I gotta do in this fed? 7.1. Roleplays Roleplays aren't REALLY necessary in this fed... Unless you want to win your matches. 8) RPs should obey the following rules...
- NO SWEARING: Yeah, I know we all say shit, ass, fuck without felling bad... But you know, we gotta look a decent fed (yeah, right). So it's preferable that you put a [bleeeep] or any kind of censorship you want. @ss, a$$ and @$$ are allowed forms, for example.
- NO ATTACKS: No physical attacks on announcers/reporters/staff are allowed without the permission of the powers-that-be... A.k.a. me. Do that and we will sue. And we will win, I promise you. 8) - KAYFABE: No mention of real wrestlers will be allowed... But other e-wrestlers and feds may be mentioned in your RPs IF they allow you too. Also, please keep the mention of "outside-ISSW"
things to a minimum. It's fine to know that you gained the XYZ World belt, but we shouldn't remember it at every RP! I wouldn't want any of you saying that "EMWC sucks" or "Annis is a coward"
in ISSW cards. Go say that in THEIR cards, if you have guts. 8) - FORMAT: There's no "official" format in ISSW. We aren't SCRA or UKCW... But of course, some good sense is always welcomed. Excess of punctuation, swearing, excess of typos and/or terrible format WILL cause your rp to be ignored. Things like...
GENERIC GUY: "Hey!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! You shuld know tat I'll ripyour a ss out, yu stupid mutherfucker, yu hgearing me?????????
And don't yu trye to mess wirh me, cause i'm the biggest sunoabitch in the ISSW!!!!!!!!!" ...will prolly make your rp to be deleted without remorse. So try to keep some sense. We may correct some problems for you
(like typos), but we won't be redoing your RPs all the time. Also, please keep a minimum and a maximum of RPs/RPs-lenght. 1 RP each 2 weeks seems fines, as well as 1 RP each card. But no 4 RPs for a Report card, nor 40k RPS. 7.2. Strats Strats, if you don't know it, are strategies you should do for a certain match. Something in the way of "what I would like to happen during the match". It always helps the writers and give you credit with the booking comitee.
8. Do you need help? Where can I help? ALWAYS! Right now, if I got some match writers, would be the perfect thing. But any kind of suggestion/help will be welcomed... I am also planning to put a small page for ISSW soon. Any contributions will be fine.
9. I gotta a few questions... Well, I will try to resume the most frequent questions people have done to me after I launched ISSW... If you have a question that is not listed here, feel free to mail me.
9.1. What kind of wrestlers can I enter at ISSW? A single or a Tag Team. If it's a single, I prefer that you enter a International wrestler (a.k.a. non-American/Canadian) as there's a lot of them already.
9.2. Can I enter a single and Tag Team? Or two singles and them combine them as a Tag Team?
Nope. ISSW is restricted to one "personality" (single/Tag Team). per handler. managers do not count. 9.3. Why there is no weight divisions? I believe that in an indy fed, you gotta show you can deal with any challenge. Including fighting a guy much bigger than you. I _MAY_ put a Cruiser division soon...
9.4. Why this fed has only a bi-weekly card? That sucks! Sucks more than a fed that has 2 cards per week... But never send them on time? Of course, there will be less exposure of wrestlers in ISSW than in other feds... But hey, aren't indy feds that way?
9.5. Can I enter my big guy, 10 times world champion here? I prefer not. Remember that this is an indy fed... And a BIG name would prolly make booking decisions more difficult.
10. Okay I wanna join. What should I do? Get the app (go for the page to get them), fill it and send it to me. Don't forget to send a sample rp from your entrance. In a couple of days, you may start receiving ISSW mails... Indicating you´re on.
