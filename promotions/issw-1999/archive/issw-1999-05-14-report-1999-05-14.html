Welcome to another ISSW Report. We had our first card broadcasted this week, and the feedback was TREMENDOUS! We have some news for you... And also tonight, later in the show, Mr. Longweiner will announce the 8 man who will compete in the Seeding Battle Royale next Monday Action! ====== ROSTER ====== Christopher "El Shogun" Estavon Cruz Euronymous "Everglades" Al E. Gator "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh Jamie Kidd "Keen" Kurt Orr Mark Marleau Mascara del Muerte Mitsunori Takizawa "No Remorse" John Davies Shayne Bryant Jr. "Ultimate Fighting Machine" Richard Gracie COMINGS: "El Shogun" Estavon Cruz/Mascara del Muerte GOINGS: "Cataclysmic" Chris Johnson ========= RF: Surprisingly, "Cataclysmic" Chris Johnson asked for being released of his contract. We wish Johnson the best luck in his future... [VOICE: You told he should burn in hell yesterday!] RF: Err... But with the newcomers Mascara del Muerte and Estavon Cruz, ISSW is filling his roster! More wrestlers are in contact with ISSW and we can see a full roster very soon... As we're saying, last Monday marked the debut of the first Monday Action card. The show was full of action... And the following day, ISSW wrestlers were back to action. Let's recap the past week! ================== PAST SHOWS RESULTS ================== 05-10: ISSW Monday Action at the Orlando Arena, Florida - "U. F. M." Richard Gracie defeated The Blue Bunny after locking him in an ankle lock submission hold. - "Cataclysmic" Chris Johnson defeated Paul "No Fear" Crip with a Scattered Ashes Moonsault under two minutes. - Christopher beat Mark Marleau by making him submit to the Hangman. - "Keen" Kurt Orr won his match after "Everglades" Al E. Gator was counted out due to a chairshot by Christopher. - Shane Bryant Jr. and Mitsunori Takizawa went to a time-limit draw. - MAIN EVENT: "No Remorse" John Davies and Jamie Kidd went to a draw as both men were counted out. Davies had to be restrained by ISSW Security Chief James "Achilles" Wolferson. 05-11: ISSW House Show at Universal Studios, Florida - "No Remorse" John Davies beat Johnny Beaver after a nasty chokeslam. - Euronymous debuted beating Ray Masters with a powerslam. - "Keen" Kurt Orr beat The Blue Bunny easily as he nailed a double underhook facedriver. - "El Shogun" Estavon Cruz showed the crowd a great match as he beat Paul "No Fear" Crip after a moonsault off tope - "U. F. M." Richard Gracie and Shayne Bryant Jr. battled a great match, with Bryant ending victorious as he nailed the Senton Splash on him. - Mitsunori Takizawa beat "Everglades" Al E. Gator after Christopher appeared on the ringside and Gator went after him. Gator was counted out as he chased Christopher through the building. ========= RF: ISSW is heating up... Wrestlers are looking for each other's throats already! And while that, our rankings keep changing! Anyway, we're on the phone with the President, Mr. Longweiner... John, can you you hear me? JL: [Phone voice] Very well, Roger. I wanna say you're doing a pretty job and that I am happy with the way ISSW is going. RF: Thanks, John... Before we go to the announcement of the 8 wrestlers that will compete in the Seeding Battle Royale, I think I gotta ask... What the status of the Tag Team Division? JL: [Phone voice] Well, for now, we're trying to contact teams that may join... We're in negotiations with some. But, for now, ISSW won't open the Tag Division until we got a amount of teams. RF: Okay... Now, the question everybody wants to hear... Who will be at the Seeding Battle Royale? JL: [Phone voice] Well, I was the lineup ready... When Kurt Orr phoned me and asked for a two-weeks vacation, as he will compete in a tournament on Italy. I decided to gave him the vacation, and it made my choices pretty easy. RF: Do you mean...? JL: [Phone voice] Well, your roster has now 8 wrestlers that wrestled twice for ISSW... And I think it's fair to give them the spots. For the newcomers, they will be on the Title Shots Battle Royale at ISSW Discovery. RF: So this means Christopher, Gator, Kidd, Takizawa, Davies, Bryant Jr. and Gracie are the chosen? JL: [Phone voice] Yes, indeed. RF: Well, thank you, Mr. Longweiner... JL: [Phone voice] Anytime. [click] RF: That's it fans... We have our nominees for the titles! Don't forget to check Monday Action to see who will be in each tournament!
