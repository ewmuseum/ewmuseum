An internationally-themed roster fed whose weekly newscasts named its full membership and recapped its debut week of shows. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

International Super Stars of Wrestling opened with a debut card at the Orlando Arena on 10 May 1999, and its own newscast the following Thursday still called that show “our first card broadcasted this week”. It billed itself as the invention of John Longweiner, a wrestling promoter since 1975 and, in its own words, “a man in his 60's, always elegantly dressed” and looking for international flavor in American wrestling. Based out of Florida, it planned to touch Caribbean, Central and South American cities as it grew, and its own FAQ was frank about scale: “small shows, small roster, not big special effects, either airtime. To be honest, it's a fed purely for fun.”

Its roster ran an explicit international quota, aiming for an even split of North American and international wrestlers never letting the former outnumber the latter, and fielded twelve wrestlers across its first week of shows — Monday Action, the bi-weekly flagship, Prelude to Action, its flash show, and the newscast Report, which tracked win-loss rankings after every card. “Keen” Kurt Orr topped that first ranking board undefeated in three, and the newcomers Mascara del Muerte and “El Shogun” Estavon Cruz were signed the same week “Cataclysmic” Chris Johnson asked for his release.

Both of its own championships — an International belt and a Floridian belt, plus unclaimed International Tag Team belts — sat vacant through this stretch, with a Seeding Battle Royale on Monday Action choosing the eight men who would fight for them at a planned supercard, ISSW Discovery, on 26 June at the Miami Arena: separate tournaments for each singles title and a Title Shot Battle Royale for the wrestlers left out of both.

How Discovery played out isn't in the surviving record; its own last surviving document, the 28 May FAQ, still describes both belts as unclaimed and the tag division as unopened, “until we got a amount of teams.”
