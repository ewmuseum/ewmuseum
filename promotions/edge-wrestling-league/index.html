Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Edge Wrestling League’s own record survives as a single page — its closing letter, dated 22 July 2004 and headed “The Day that the EWL Closes” — written by its operator to announce the promotion’s own end. By his own account EWL had run for “roughly seven months or so,” putting its unrecorded founding in late 2003 or early 2004.

The letter traces the decline to a change in leadership on its own boards, which it says the promotion never recovered from — “this place sucked completely. Lots of wrestlers left, and this place hit Rock Bottom” — compounding with the closing of its out-of-character board and, in the letter’s own words, “tons of inactivity, no one caring.”

Despite the ending, the letter frames EWL’s own run as a success on its own terms: “A lot of you may think EWL failed, however, in my eyes it didn’t. EWL succeeded beyond my imaginations. I never thought EWL would reach where it is right now.” The operator closed by thanking those who had kept the promotion going and saying he held “no grudges or anything towards anyone.”

No founding document, roster, card or title record survives; only this one closing letter and two bare navigational shells remain of EWL’s own site, so what EWL actually staged during its seven months is not in the surviving record.
