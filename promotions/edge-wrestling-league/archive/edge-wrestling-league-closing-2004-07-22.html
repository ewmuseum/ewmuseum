EDGE Wrestling League: It's All Over...

22 July [2004]
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EDGE Wrestling League : It's All Over... July 22: The Day that the EWL Closes. Well guys, a lot of you saw this coming. EWL hasn't been the same. Ever since Larry and Jay took over, no offense to them, but this place sucked completely. Lots of wrestlers left, and this place hit Rock Bottom. Because of the late circumstances, OOC Board closing, tons of inactivity, no one caring... I've decided EWL should close... Anything you have to say, hit me on AIM [detail withheld]. Also, if you're still interested in something else and looking for a home, contact me.
For Thank Yous, I like to send them to Kamlesh, Justin Sane, Jay LeCage, [name withheld], Johnny Olympus, good ol' [name withheld], and Brad from fWrestling. Also, [name withheld]. You guys helped me a lot, and kept EWL alive longer.
I think the handlers here know why EWL closed, if not, look at the boards. A lot of you may think EWL failed, however, in my eyes it didn't. EWL succeeded beyond my imaginations. I never thought EWL would reach where it is right now. Thankfully, I went through this experience once. EWL wasn't the fed that would come and go, instead, we stayed up for roughly seven months or so. I really liked meeting all the new people I did. I enjoyed every minute of it, however there were some harsh times, but luckily not too many. There were problems with some people, and why I don't know, I have no regrets on whatever I said, but it happens.
In closing EWL, I have no grudges or anything towards anyone. I just hope you all had a great time. Thank you all...
EDGE Wrestling League
