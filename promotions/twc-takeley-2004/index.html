Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

TWC kept a website e-fed on its own domain, with pages to join, the rules, a roster, the champions, a title history and staff, a roleplay board and an out-of-character board, and previews and results for its weekly show and its pay-per-views. In the autumn of 2004 the weekly show was Saturday Night Stateside. Its news told readers when each preview and set of results was up, that the Starcade pay-per-view went up on 31 October 2004, and on 7 November what happened on “Big Daddy Jason Berodt's first card as one of the bosses”, who had promised that heads would roll. Its news of 15 November said the next Saturday's coverage would come from Dallas, and Evil Incorporated was announced for the Saturday after 24 November.

By September 2005 the weekly show was Monday Night Stateside, with a roleplay deadline given as “1pm-P, 2pm-M, 3pm-C, 4pm-E and 9pm-UK”. On 1 September 2005 the home page wished itself a happy fourteenth birthday and said that “we really did start up as a backyard fed back in 1991”. The update of 10 September 2005 announced a massive update to the roster and a late match added to that Monday’s preview. Its welcome line called TWC the best e-fed experience in the world.
