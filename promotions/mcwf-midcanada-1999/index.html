Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Mid Canada Wrestling Federation traced its own history to August 1999, reopening in January 2000 after a short closure with a roster its own recruitment posts described as already thirty members strong within its first week back.

It ran an e-mail-based operation, roleplays distributed daily in packets, cards staged every Wednesday and a pay-per-view once a month, with a second weekly show added as the roster grew.

Its return kicked off with Back In Business, a pay-per-view staged from a named arena, before the federation settled into a regular rhythm of Wednesday Warfare and a monthly Battle Zone card.

The promotion styled itself a fun, suggestion-driven environment, its own bulletins crediting its handlers directly for angle and feud ideas worked into the ongoing shows.
