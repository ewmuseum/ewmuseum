Power & Glory --------------------- Scene: The scene is set in the DogMen Lair in St. John's, Newfoundland. Brad "IceDog" Doyle can be seen sitting on the leather couch in what is left of the entertainment centre with an icepack on his left ankle. Paddy "X-Dog" Doyle Is standing behind the couch watching as a crew of men and women continue with the clean up of the Lair after the deviastation
that was the IceDog vs. Big Dog bout in the final Friday Fury of the ACeWF. IceDog watches as a woman pushes a broom through the remnants of the television screen that lay scattered on the floor at his feet.
IceDog: Having those people come in to clean up the place was a great idea. I couldn't see any of us doing this...
X-Dog: Thats sure as hell true. What the hell did you two do to this place? It's like a monster truck rally came through!!!!
ID: Well that's just about what happened.... But it was all worth it. Now that Jaye and the Crazy Dog are back in the ranks of the DogMen, we are more powerful then ever.... Nothing is gonna stop us now.... The destruction and the pain is well worth that.
XD: You got it brother.... Now that the CWF is up and running there is plenty of new faces to slap silly....
ID: But a few farmiliar ones as well.... Man, I don't want to have to face the Shining Force for a while.... the Dynamic Duo is here as well. We never got our shots at them back in the ACeWF..... We deserved to retire those belts, not those guys. We never did get to see who truly was the best of the lot back there. But new challenges mean new truimphs, new championships, new power --
XD: -- and glory.... ID: .... and a new beginning. Now from what I can see, they got us debuting in the CWF against the motliest bunch in the lot.... Pimpin' Inc.... Like that's original.... *Brad smirks rather obviously*
XD: But I bet that they got the Ho's.... ID: Shut up Paddy!!! Who gives a da--well... *Spoken half jokingly* In all honesty, I hope that they do as
well, but that's besides the point.... I mean I like a jiggly rack as much as the next guy.... but it doesn't change the fact that they have to face us this sunday. It's time to make a statement.... It's time to show the world who we are.... It's time that we take the glory and the power and put it back around our waists....
XD: First it's gonna be a 291 pound Pimp Daddy being X-Picked and unconsious in the middle of the ring, just primed for the pin. Then it's gonna be whomever else and however many Derrick Hall wants to throw at us.....
ID: Then, it's the Rejects.... Mark my words CWF. The DogMen and Power and Glory will capture the tag gold round these here parts. It'll be ours for a very long time.....
XD: You see, it doesn't matter how big and strong you are. ID: As a team, P&G are bigger and stronger. XD: It doesn't matter how quick and agile you are. ID: P&G are quicker and more agile. XD: It doesn't matter how well a tag team works together. ID: P&G work better together. XD: It doesn't matter how much heart you have. ID: P&G, and the rest of the DogMen for that matter, simply have all the more heart and the determination to get the job done, and come out on top in the end.
XD: It's only a matter of time folks. You better get used to this cause 'To be the men, you gotta beat the men....
ID: ....And to be the men, you gotta face Power and Glory.' Pimp Daddy, Biggie Styles..... You two men are the first to fall at the hands of Power and Glory.
Then you two rejects had better keep your noggins held high in the air cause Power and Glory are out to claim what we are deserving of, the tag belts.....
XD: The DogMen and Power n' Glory are here to stay folks.... And we ain't stopin till we reach the top.... NOT!!!--till we reach the top.....
*And with that, the camera pulls away from the two DogMen as the clean up and renovations to the lair continue. The screen fades to black only to reveal a
Power and Glory Logo in deep neon green flashed across the screen.* Dancin' Cowboys ------------------------- (The scene opens up outside the dressing room of the Dancin' Cowboys. There an interviewer is standing.) -Interviewer knocks on the door and waits for them to come out while their manager stays in the dresser room. (Their manager is never involved in any interviews with them.)
Interviewer: So guys what brought you here to the CWF? Jim: Well we came here because we were just brought into the ACeWF and it joined up with this fed so we came along for the ride.
Joe: You heard him! Interviewer: When do you guys hope to début? Jim: We hope to début this Sunday, so they tell us. Joe: You heard him! Interviewer: Joe do you ever talk? Joe: No, I usually let Jim do all of the talking because when we are in the ring I do all of the f***ing work. Jim: That is not right. Anyway we will settle that later. Get on with the interview. Interviewer: OK. Who will you guys be facing this Sunday? Jim: We are not sure as of yet. We hope it is one of the best teams oh because we know we can kick their @$$ with our patenent "Heartbreaker".
Really it doesn't matter who we face because before you know it we will be the number one contender's for the Tag Team Titles and then when you blink again we will be the Tag Team Champs. No one can beat us and if they do it will be by fluke because we the Famous Armstrong Brothers down in Dallas,
Texas, or as you know us the Dancin' Cowboys we are the best damn Tag Team in the fed.
Joe: He got that last comment right! Interviewer: What makes you feel so confident? Joe: He already told you. Would you please listen for once. It is our famous 'Heartbreaker' that will beat everyone. When we give them a size 12 and a size 13 cowboy boot in the chest don't you think we will win the match? Stop and think about it. Jesus!
Jim: Any other questions you would like to ask us before we shove you out of our way so we can go back into our dressing room to practise some more wrestling moves before our ébut and maybe work out a little bit more?
Interviewer: No. not really. Joe & Jim: Well then get the F*** out of our way!!! -They both shove the interviewer out of the way. Jim: Oh and by the way you wanted to know why we are the best Tag Team here. Well then just let us show you. Get ready Joe. 1 2 3 go.
-They give the interviewer their patenent "Heartbreaker". The interviewer is down. EMT's are rushing there with stretchers. Joe and Jim go back into their dressing room and slam the door. (The scene closes and goes to an ad for Battle Zone.) Jaye "Big Dog" Doyle ------------------------------- (The scene opens up to a pitch black room that is broken by the light of a far away door opening up and letting the days light in. The flick of a switch echoes throughout the area and white light floods the tattered warehouse. The entire warehouse is in shambles; weights are scattered around in a mess, a large television lays face down and smashed on the concrete floor, the remnants of a chair is now splintered and scattered about the place, and red trails of splattered blood line up and down the warehouse. The silhouette of a man walks into the doorway and just stands there. Looking at the man from the bottom up he's wearing a pair of blue, Adidas running shoes, a pair of faded blue jeans, and a black t-shirt with "DOGMEN" written on it in big, neon green letters. His long, dark, wavy hair is tied back in a pony tail, and a line of stitches disappear into his hairline. The man is... Jaye Doyle. Doyle walks into the warehouse and mutters to himself.)
Jaye Doyle: Just look at this place. How did I let my foolish pride persuade me to do this? I'm surprised that Ice will even talk to me. I owe those guys an apology and then some... and to take me back with open arms after all I've done too.
(Doyle picks up a piece of the splintered chair.) Jaye Doyle: This is appalling. I can't believe I let the green cloud my judgement so. I've let my fans down, I've let my family down, but most importantly,
I've let myself down. (Doyle picks up the monstrous television set and pushes it upright.) Jaye Doyle: This is going to take some time to fix. What I can fix right now, though, is the down slide I've been on lately. It's time to get back to what made me in the first place, and that's honor, honesty, and hard work. I didn't get to where I am today by taking the easy way out, or by being underhanded and deceitful. I got here by my own blood, sweat, and tears. I got here by grabbing the bull by the horns and wrestling that sucker to the ground with my own bare hands.
(Doyle heads for the corner of the warehouse and grabs a broom and a dustpan.) Jaye Doyle: This Sunday, I get to begin to atone for my mistakes over the past few months, and against the former MCWF World Champ no less. A man who claims to be what I once was. A man who's inner hatred has consumed him to the point that it's cost him his title. I know what it's like to be angry at the world and yet not know why. The difference between you and
I, the very difference between men and boys, is that I've realized my mistake and now I'm back on the right track. (Doyle begins to sweep up the wood splinters from the chair and the broken glass from the television up off the floor.)
Jaye Doyle: Now Rice, as far as I'm concerned, you got the short end of the stick last night, but that match is over and done with and no amount of complaining will change that fact. What you've got to worry about is your match with me on Sunday. I've got a lot to prove my man and if you think that Rizzo was tough, you're in for a real awakening.
(Doyle sweeps the debris into the dust pan then heads off with the full dust pan in hand.) Jaye Doyle: You may already have heard about me, and then again, you may not have. Whatever the case, here's the deal; You're my ticket to get back on track and no amount of these intimidation tactics that you get on with is going to change the fact that Sunday, you're going face to face with the best technical wrestler that you've ever laid eyes on. You are going head to head with the living legend himself, "The Big
Dog" Jaye Doyle. (Doyle reaches an old tin garbage pail and dumps the contents of the dust pan inside.) Jaye Doyle: Get some rest Rice and lick your wounds because, come Sunday, you get a first hand example of why I am respected the way I am and of why so many wrestlers tremble at the thought of having to face me.
(Doyle lays the pan on the floor then leans up on the broom.) Jaye Doyle: You'll be just one more name on the long list of people who have found that, (now raising his voice a little) IF YA WANNA RUN WITH THE BIG DOG, YA
CAN'T PISS LIKE A PUP! (The scene fades out as Doyle lays the broom down and walks away.)
