Sir Ulric Everest ------------------------ (The camera is set up in a gym. Different training apparatuses, treadmills, bicycles and weight stands fill the room, but no people can be seen. The walls have been painted light green and large windows greatly brightens the room, letting in as much daylight as possible. There's a faint echo of footsteps as Ulric Everest steps into view. He almost beams at the camera.)
UE: Well. It's finished. It took an eternity and what seems like every dime
I could scrap together, but it has been taken care of. And I must say I could not have wished for a better result. Shortly, this gym... MY gym will be the trendiest place to work out in entire Boston! Oh, yes, it will.
(Ulric Everest turns around, puts his hands on his hips and admires the room.) UE: I've got everything you could ever want! It's the most complete training facility ever!
(Dan Davidson walks into view. He glances around and turns to Ulric Everest.) Davidson: This looks really nice. How could you afford it? UE: Come on, Dan! Surely you've figured out that I am, by now, quite the millionaire? Davidson: No. This doesn't add up... UE: Ah... Okay, I took up a loan. A mortgage, actually. If the gym doesn't succeed, the bank will take it away.
Davidson: Well, I hope for your sake this doesn't prove to be a dead end. UE: No, wait... I still haven't showed you the best part! (Ulric Everest puts his arm over Dan Davidson's shoulders and gently forces him along. They enter another room. Curtains are covering the windows and the lights are off. A thin strip of sunlight steals its way though the doorway as Ulric Everest opens the door. He shoves Dan Davidson inside in front of him and closes the door behind them. Total darkness.)
UE: Now, Dan. Check this out... (Lights suddenly illumine. In the centre of the room, there's a brand new wrestling ring. Ulric Everest climbs the stairs, grabs hold of the top rope and slingshots over it.)
UE: It's beautiful, wouldn't you agree? Davidson: Yes, of course. It's nothing short of stunning. UE: It's the pride of this facility. I could just barely afford one of these, but it was a purchase I will not regret making.
Davidson: This gym looks fantastic, it really does. However, since I'm here anyway, mind if I ask you a couple of questions?
UE: Oh, no, not at all. Please go ahead. Davidson: You haven't wrestled in nearly two weeks. Why? UE: Well, I thought I deserved a break, and I got one. Besides, my doctor advised me to take it easy a couple of days. I put my heart to what I do and it has almost cost me several weariness injuries. Now, however, I'm only waiting for that phone-call. Just tell me where and when, and I'll be there! I'm still one of Derrick Hall's shrewdest signings, you know.
Davidson: Wait a minute... I forgot to tell you right away! You're scheduled to go up against "Wild Child" Jimmy Sexton! On Wednesday Warfare!
UE: Wild Child who? (Ulric Everest jumps down to ringside. He lifts his arm to high-five Dan
Davidson, but Davidson ignores him, pulling out a notebook from his pocket.)
Davidson: Let's see... I wrote it all down... He's a wrestler from the
ACeWF. UE: Hmm... Can't say I've heard about him. Was he successful? Davidson: You really shouldn't ask me these things. I never had anything to do with the ACeWF, and I still haven't got to know the new names on the roster. Jimmy Sexton is about 6 feet 8 inches and weighing around 295 pounds. What status he had in the ACeWF, you'll have to ask "Wild Child"
himself! Or Frank Taylor. UE: I'm sure I can get hold of a videotape or something. Davidson: *checks his watch and freezes* Oh, no! I'm late! Sorry, Mr. Everest, I have to go. I've got an appointment. I'll come by later or tomorrow, if that's ok. I'll try to help you on this Sexton guy. Until then, have a nice day!
(Dan Davidson rushes out of camera view. Ulric Everest shrugs his shoulders and looks towards the camera.)
UE: If you live around Boston, be sure to check out Sir Everest Fitness
Club! We'll get you in top shape in no time! Don't miss the grand opening!

Is this speech some sort of farewell speech?" -The Viking: *smiles* "You must not misunderstand. I am not leaving... No! This is just the beginning of a new era, and a good-bye to the ACeWF. I haven't talked to him in a long time, and as of NOW I have no plans of teaming up with anybody!" (Another reporter asks a question..) -Reporter #3: "And what about Sir Ulric Everest?" -The Viking: "I haven't talked to HIM since about six months or so. I guess this has come to an end. That's it for today. Thanks for taking time to listen to me! I'll see you all later! Thanks!" (The Viking grabs his TV title and leaves to a standing ovation. The camera slowly fades out, as The Viking leaves the cameras view. The screen goes black...)

Titan -------- (In a tiny, dimly lit room, Tom Wilson sits in a big, red arm chair shuffling through some notes as he prepares for an interview. He is dressed sharply in black dress pants, an electric blue dress shirt, and a tie. Tom here's a knock and looks up.)
Tom Wilson: Come in. (A large shadow blocks out the light momentarily and Tom looks up to see The Titan, dressed in a black, silk shirt and beige khakis. Titan smiles at Tom as the man gets up to shake hands. The two men then take their seats, Titan in an identical chair across from
Tom. Titan looks to have lost a substantial amount of weight.) Tom Wilson: It's good to see that you're alive and well Titan. You had us all worried there for a while.
(The Titan just smiles modestly as he attempts to make himself comfortable in the comparatively tiny chair.)
Titan: I was scared a little too at first but I think that my injury may have been a blessing in disguise.
The physiotherapists back in Regina have whipped me into the best shape of my life. I'm down to 402 and I just feel invigorated.
Tom Wilson: You do look a lot thinner now but how will that weight loss affect the power that The Titan has exhibited time and time again within the squared circle.
Titan: I'm not exactly sure but I know this; I don't get tired as quickly and I'm a little bit quicker than before. If I have lost anything in the strength department, hopefully the added endurance and speed will balance it out. Besides, I've been training hard the last few days to try and get back to where I left off. I know that I'm not there yet but I feel that if
I can keep the weight off, the strength I once had will come back to me eventually. Tom Wilson: Well, I know that if anyone can recuperate from a blow like this, it's you Titan. There were rumors that complications from a previous injury were going to keep you out of action longer than expected.
Was there any truth to that? (Is still squirming in his chair and is now looking a little annoyed at the matter.) Titan: Well, yes and no. A while back in the ACeWF, I was in a big car accident and was forced to temporarily retire from wrestling due to injuries to my lower back. What had happened last week wasn't really due to the gauntlet match, but more to my old injury not heeling properly. Thanks to some ultrasound and differential treatment, the scare tissue that was giving me the problems has been blasted away and I'm as good as new. (Staring down at one of the chair's armrests.) Titan: What? (Looking innocent to his crime.) You said to make myself comfortable and this chair was far to small. You should have prepared better you know.
