Roleplays

The Rejects ----------------- *Sean, Tom and Coach B are reading the roster for the CWF. They look at the tag division and they just look in awe. They notices the teams from the now defunct ACeWF and just stare at each other. Jake Jones enters the room with a hand held camera. They see him and invite him over to the bench. When he gets there they put the pieces of paper away.*
JJ: I see you were reading the info packet on the merger. What are your thoughts on the merge?
Tom: Well, it's going to bring some real competition into the CWF in the tag division. No more easy Ryders, no more Houses of Pain. now, we have to face "Dynamic Duo." Wait...did I say competition?
*All three men start laughing.* Coach: Oh man. Hey boys...in unison! All three: NANANANANANANANA BATMAN!!! *They laugh it up some more.* Sean: Well, I see we may have to face them pretty soon. I know they're the champs, but I think they got help winning the ACeWF tag titles. We did it on our own with hard work and training. These guy...True Grits....that reminds me....my mom said she was going to make me grits for breakfast....
Coach: DAMNIT SEAN. Keep your concentration. Sean: Sorry Coach, I'm hungry and his name makes me want grits. Tom: Ok ok Sean, we understand. Sean: To answer you question JJ, we think it's good and bad. Some tag teams like the Archangels will give us, the true champs, a good run for the money. However, Dynamic Duo, time to pack your bags. Your belts, they mean nothing. Our belts, well, they mean nothing. Putt hem together, they are the true tag titles, and they WILL be around OUR waists.
Tom: World Renowned?? These guys must be from Wisconsin because they are pure CHEESE! I mean, come on. What the hell kind of "Dynamic" names are these? At least Batman was a cool name. Robin.....well, the tights speak for themselves.
Coach: now boys, it's not time to get too cocky. True enough they're names are crap, but they are tag champs. That shows they have talent. However,
I think you guys may be able to pull off the win.
Sean: Well, I don't know coach. They couldn't get anywhere without help in the place. They're loners now, so it's time for hard workers to shine.
Coach: I agree, but still, do not underestimate them. Tom: Yeah, he is right Sean. We need to watch what we say for we may eat our words. JJ: Well, Ok. Good lengthy answer there. Ok, so, Sean, what about the new influx of middleweights into the CWF?
Sean: Well, this is going to be very tough. See, I'm a dual champ. That means two matches back to back. I'll need to build up my stamina so I can be able to compete so much. I know I have a good chance to keep my belts, but it'll be difficult to do so.
Coach: Which means I won't make it easy on you Sean. You need to learn how to pace yourself. you're too crazy.
Sean; Coach, don't call me CRAZY! My mom calls me crazy. I am not crazy! I'm just misunderstood. Tom: And Ted Bundy was just a good-hearted man. Sean: Who's that? Coach: Shut up Sean. You know what I meant. You take too many risks. Sean: yeah, but they pay off. So what? Tom: I think he's trying to say someday they'll backfire. Coach: Thank you Tom. Sean; Whatever. Middleweights don't win with suplexes and crap like that. We're high flyers. To be middleweight champ means to be the best high flyer there is. It's a necessary evil Coach. Now, to answer your question
JJ, I think I'll be holding onto my belt for a while. I went through the best of the MCWF and will do so with the best of the ACeWF...now CWF. I'm not being overconfident. I just know I have the talent and Coach will help me use it to the best of my abilities. That's why we're tag champs and why
I'm middleweight champ. Coach has taught us well.
JJ: Anybody you looking forward to wrestling? Tom: Well, as tag champs, I'd like to defend against the Archangels. They seem to have it together. Like I said before, they'd be the best matchup for us.
JJ: Sean? Sean; Well, I don't know. Certainly not the Static Duo. let me see *pulls out the roster, running down it with his finger.* No...no...no....AH HA! Here we goes. The EGG. JJ: That dude is NUTS! He specializes in hardcore. Sean; Good, which means I can inflict more pain on him. I think Derrick should put us in a match for my belt next Sunday!
Coach: Sean. I told you, taking risks is going to cost you. He's a hardcore wrestler. He'll kill you.
Sean: This will help me build my stamina! Tom: He has a point Coach. Coach: DAMN IT! *Pulls out a bottle of whiskey and takes a swig* Tom: Is that healthy Coach? Coach: I care less. Sean being right frightens me enough to make me drink. Sean; I'm sorry Coach. JJ: Well, I see you guys need to discuss stuff amongst yourselves. I'll see you later. Sean: Nice meeting you man. Tom: come back soon and tell Danny we say hi. JJ: Will do. *The camera cuts to a CWF logo.* Inka Tupac ---------------- Washington D. C. Crystal is walking looking for someone, the camera moves thru the crowds, near the capitolium
Crystal: There, There he is She signalls at Inka, who is sitting down, his head resting in his handsin the stairs that lead to the Lincoln Memorial.
Crystal: Inka, Hi Inka looks up at Crystal then puts his hands in his head again Crystal: It's me Crystal from CWF Inka: What, you mean MCWF, oh right I heard the news now it is CWf Crystal: I see you are upset but come on you'll have your revenge. Inka: It's not that, I just made a decission that will change my whole career Crystal: What decission? Inka: I just fired Kalipe Rosas as my manager. Crystal: what, come again? Inka: You heard me, Kal is my friend and always be but actions like those on Warfare..., Maybe I am just dissapointed but... I had to make a decision so
.... I made it.
Crystal: what are you going to do now? Inka: First I gotte a Battle Royal to win on IEA, then I don't know the only things I have in my mind is to give Del Carver, Machine and Styx what they deserve and that is THE DOMINATOR.
Crystal: Now that you mention Styx, I suppose that you had heard his challenge, or I may say the answer to your challenge?
Inka: Yes, and the answer is Yes, Styx I'll meet you anywhere anytime, but let's give a twist you get two partners and I'll fight with mistery partner of my own and Knight in a six man tag team math in the INKA DEATH CAGE
MATCH, only that this time the team that gets out first win the match.
Maybe you will like Machine and Del Carver as your partners, don't know is up to you and them. Crystal: The Cavalier hasn't respond yet, if he wants to team up with you. Inka: I know if he wants to team up with me it's OK, he is a good wrestler, if not I'll fight handicap with my mistery partner agains Styx and his
"friends" in the Inka Death Cage Match. And one thing to the prez book this match wherever you want, maybe next Warfare, or in International Incident, do not care just make sure that I get my chance against those 3 punks.
Inka stands up and walks away Crystal: We have a few questions in the air, will STYX accept the 6 man tag team, and better yet will The Cavalier Tom Knight team with Inka and his mistery partner, see ya next time.
Slick Sosna ------------------ *Scene opens up in Slick's new house. They just bought a brand new house up in Quebec because they are really starting to hate it up in this hot Arizona desert, they love the cold snow days.So they have been working a lot to put all there new furniture Electronics, etc.. After they worked for about 4 Hours straight sweating like hell they bring out a couple of glasses and a couple of Beers.*
Wife: Man we sure did work a lot today we had to drive up here which took about 3 days. Had to take all the stuff we could with the U-Haul truck then unpack today. I'll I need is a ice cold beer then I will be ready to go. How about you Slick?
Slick: Same here that drive from Phoenix really burned me out but not as bad as my Wednesday match with
Biggie Styles. *Wife Chuckles* Slick: I mean it looks like he didn't even show up for the match. I beat his ass just like I told everyone.
Now that I am kicking ass and taking names there's no limit. Bob Jones=win Biggie Styles=win, then my upcoming Sunday Battle Zone Copycat=win. So no one can stop me right now and I mean no one. Speaking of that what the hell does Python have the right to say that he beat my ass? What the hell were you thinking. You said that you could be me at a Hardcore Match? Is that all you are? Well unlike you I am great and everything. Techincal, Brawler, extremist, hardcore you name. Python all you know is Hardcore. That's why you go nowhere in this business. I suggust you go to the gym's work on your wrestling skills a little more.
Trust me I have when I was in the dumps'. But look at me now, I suggest you take my hints and do what I say or else you are going to get in alot of trouble with wrestlers in the CWF because just like A-bomb and a lot more wrestlers they are going to whipp you like a mop, up and down the ring. Speaking of whipping, my upcoming match with Copycat. Damn now that's what I call a name. What is your record in the last 5 matches? 0-5 I think. I don't know but it's pretty close to that. Just like Bob "White Trash" Jones which in my mind has the best damn name in the business and
Biggie Styles, Copycat, you're no match for the Slickster. I can't wait again for this upcoming Battle Zone. My new beginning is in process and I won't stop until I get the gold. Brad's Rice title that is. Plus
I got some other stuff I got to take care of business, but nothing big. Damn look at the time Catherine our thirty minutes all already up
*Chuggs his beer down* *Catherine snoozy while Slick talks Slick wakes her up. She mumbles somestuff together* Catherine: I'm sorry I guess that since we have been working, I have been a little tired. Well I think we got just a little way to go before we are done and now that you are concentrated on beat Copycat there is no stoping you. Now I got some news that will be good to here. I quit my job since we moved up here. There's no sense because how would I keep a job in Arizona when we live in Quebec.
*Slick's mouth drops* Slick: What? You better start finding yourself a job missy. Because my money from the CWF won't cut it until I start making the big matches, but that won't be long I promise. Now let's start picking up what we started.. moving in to our house.
*Scene fades and shows a ad for Sunday Night Battle Zone commercial for Copycat Vs The Sex Symbol of the 90's Slick Sosna* Jake "Slasher" Lowe ------------------------------ (Jake Lowe is seen walking the halls of MCWF head offices. He stops in front of a bulletin board where the matches for Sunday night are posted. He scrolls through them with his finger.)
Jake Lowe - Yeah its right here just like Crystal told me, Sunday night Jake Lowe verses The Grim Reaper.
Yet from the interview I saw with him yesterday he didn't even know he was facing me, he thought he was going up against some loser. Well Grim Reaper I am no loser and if you don't soon get in the ring and prepare to face me or you will be the loser.
(A short man in his early 50's walks by wearing a suit and carrying his resume.) Jake Lowe - Hey aren't you Jake Jones from the ACeWF. Jake Jones - Yes I am. Jake Lowe - Well what are you doing here. Jake Jones - With the ACeWF and MCWF merging I'm here to meet with Derrick Hall about whether or not I have a job with the CWF.
Jake Lowe - Oh well I hadn't heard about the merger yet welcome aboard. Jake Jones - Thanks, well I've got to go see Mr. Hall now but hopefully I'll be talking to you again.
(Jake Jones heads down the hall, as he passes Jake Lowe, Lowe grabs Jake Jones' rug without him realizing. Jake Jones continues on his way.) Jake Lowe - Poor sap he never saw it coming. (looking at the rug) Maybe I can use this in my match against
The Grim Reaper and then he won't recognize me, oh wait he doesn't even know he's facing me yet. I guess he's just another poor sap for me to pull one over on.
(Jake Lowe heads out of the office throwing the rug in the garbage as he passes by a can. The scene fades to black.)

He looks through his high powered binoculars into Atomic Jaguar's room. He watches Susan cross infront of the window. He watches the camera crew walk in. He smiles. Picks up a hand held radio and listens to Atomic Jaguar's interview. )
Poor little kitty cat gotta cold? Another excuse from the Jaguar. He can not just take a beating like a man and admit it? Must have issues....Sunday is a big day. He gets a chance at vindication. The Jaguar gets back in the ring and try to end the losing streak. After a complete beating from
Dubose
and Copycat you would think he look into another line of work. Maybe he should go down to Sears and try to get a job selling ladies underwear(Snipe laughs at his own joke.)
The girl looks nice though. Lil' Susie-Q the Ring Rat. I wonder what strip club he found her at? Well...maybe he found her at a biker rally? I guess it doesnt matter. The two little love birds are tying the knot. How patheticly sweet. Make ole' Snipe want to throw up his lunch. Keep your focus
Jaguar. Keep your focus on Snipe Johnson or you may not make it to your little wedding day.
Nike shoe ad? (Snipe begins to laugh...) I find it hard to bieleve that a company like Nike would give a shoe deal to a loser like the Jaguar...I guess Nike is branching out to loser wear...Oh well. Sunday is right around the corner. Sunday I get back in the ring...a CWF ring....this should be fun.
An new surge of talent...more opponents...more allies....more enimies....oh yes....oh the fun. (Snipe looks back through the binoculars into Atomic
Jaguars room.)
Her breasts can not be real....(Snipe notices a police car pull into the parking lot. He quickly puts the binoculars down and begins to drive off.) Sunday is just around the corner.... (FADE TO BLACK) A-Bomb ------------ [The screen opens up to show a dark and dim alley. There are walls on both sides, and they are almost ruined. In the middle, on the right side, there's
a concrete building with a big sign. It says "Gym" with big red letters. You can also see a wooden door at the house. The wooden door opens, and a big figure steps out. Tom Wilson, the MCWF reporter, walks forwards from behind the camera, and looks towards the big figure. The man is standing in the dark, so that his face can't be seen.]
T. Wilson – *says with a frightened voice* A-Bomb? [The figure walks into the light from a street lamp, and reveals himself as
The A-Bomb. He has a blue bag in his hand, dark clothes and a pair of Ray
Bans, which he took of just after he left the gym.]
The A-Bomb – What do you want? T. Wilson – I was hoping to ask some questions. The A-Bomb – Just hurry will you? T. Wilson – Sure. You know you're up against Goliath. Any comments for the match? The A-Bomb – That sure will be a tough one. Titan showed me what he could do. I'm sure Goliath can do the same, if not better. But of course, I've not
given up, and I will never give up. He must really knock me out if he wants the pin. T. Wilson – What is this? You must have changed. The A-Bomb – What are you talking about? T. Wilson – Just a couple of days ago you was so sure you could wipe out all of your opponents, but now your having doubts? The A-Bomb – That's not doubt. It's called thoughts. I'm only saying it will be a tough one. Nothing else. T. Wilson – So you think you can defeat him? The A-Bomb – Of course I do. If you don't believe in yourself you can just as well retire before you have began. But just as I've said, it won't be easy to pull out a win.
T. Wilson – Have you ever considered getting backup? The A-Bomb – I must admit I have. But, really, you tell me. What will that prove? That the Norsemen is experts in interference? I want to prove myself good enough to defeat Goliath, and that without any help!
T. Wilson – So you can guarantee that the Norsemen won't interfere? The A-Bomb – I can't guarantee anything. But as I said, I don't want them to interfere. I want to take this guy down by myself, even though it will be the toughest match so far in my career.
T. Wilson – All right! Do you fear interference from their side? The A-Bomb – No, not even a bit! Because if they interfere, it will only show that Goliath fears me, and that he knows he can't defeat my by himself.
It will only be a negative thing for Goliath if they interfere against me. T. Wilson – Who do you think will win the match? The A-Bomb – The best man! Who that is, is still not revealed yet. It only remains to see. T. Wilson – Are you looking forward to the match? The A-Bomb – Yes. Beside my title matches earlier in the MCWF, this is the highlight of my career. I'm sure it will be one heck of a match. The roof will blow off!
T. Wilson – Any other comments? [Just as The A-Bomb is about to answer the question his mobile phone rings, and he picks it up from his inner pocket. He answers. The voice from the other side can be heard, but not recognized.]
The A-Bomb – Yes? Voice – Where have you been? The A-Bomb – Tom Wilson wanted to ask me some questions. Voice – Get over here will you? The A-Bomb – OK. I'm already on my way! [The A-Bomb puts his mobile phone back into his pocket, grabs Tom Wilson's mic and shouts out.]
The A-Bomb – LET'S TURN UP THE HEAT! [The A-Bomb tosses the mic back to Wilson, and the camera fades slowly to black just as The A-Bomb starts walking, and gets out of the lightened area.
Tom Wilson walks behind the camera.]
