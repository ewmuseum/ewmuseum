Sir Ulric Everest ------------------------ (The camera views a locker-room backstage after the Wednesday Warfare. The walls as well as the floor are tiled with white porcelain tiles. The room is sparsely furnished with a couple of wooden benches as the only furniture. A large, dark blue towel is lying, spread out across one of the benches. A white shirt, a pair of beige khaki pants and a pair of black boxershorts is hanging on knobs on the nearest wall. A green bag lies on the floor, stuffed with wrestling equipment. The air is thick with damp and a soft humming can be heard from a doorway leading to the showers. The humming comes closer... the camera zooms in on the blue towel. There's the sound of someone walking barefoot towards the camera and a dripping, wet hand appears and grabs the towel. Some moments pass and the camera finally zooms out. Ulric Everest is standing next to a bench with the blue towel wrapped nicely around his waist. He continues to hum until he suddenly spots the camera and gasps.)
UE: Wha... you could have knocked! (Ulric Everest frowns to the camera. He makes sure the towel is wrapped very tightly around his waist and once again frowns at the camera.)
UE: No harm done, I guess... (Ulric Everest sits down on a bench and sighs.) UE: I think I've recovered my will to wrestle! It seemed lost, so far away, and then suddenly... BANG... I'm back. I feel better than I have in months!
(Ulric Everest bends down to pull out something out of his bag and proceeds to tie his hair in a ponytail.]
UE: Oh, and Jimmy Sexton? "Wild Child"? You flunked. I wouldn't go as far as saying you're not qualified to be here, because really, you weren't that bad, but you still need more skill if you're going to go one on one with me and come out on top. You'll get another chance someday, Jimmy. Not everyone passes the exam the first time they take it. I'm willing to bet that brutes like The Titan and Goliath and schizophrenics like Brad Rice and Kyle Styx didn't even attend college! They were either judged too stupid to do so, or kicked out because of their miserable childhood. Anyway, tonight, I was the stronger one and I'm sure there will be plenty more nights like this one.
(Ulric Everest gets up from the bench. He points at the camera and then to the floor. The camera drops, viewing nothing but floor tiles. A blue towel drops to the floor as Ulric Everest continues talking.)
UE: In fact, I feel one coming up this Sunday! It will be The A-Bomb,
Morgan "Death" Adams and me, against The Titan, Goliath, Grim Reaper and
Slick Sosna. Mayhem. We're outnumbered but I don't care. I WANT to wrestle.
I'm sure The A-Bomb and Morgan Adams feel the same. My new policy has changed the way I look on things. No titles to worry about - no worries at all. This way I can forever be the challenger, the outsider, and the wildcard I prefer to be. Due time, I'm sure titles will come along, but right now... nah... no titles for me.
(The camera once again views Ulric Everest. He is now wearing his khaki pants and is about to pull his shirt over the head. He picks a wristwatch out of the bag and straps it around his wrist.)
UE: I shall have to visit Silverthorn at the hospital, too. The paramedics took him out on the stretcher, and that was the last time he was seen. We have no idea how he's doing. The bump he took was an ugly one, but on the other hand, maybe it looked worse than it was. I hope so. Really. Morgan
Adams will be covering for him while he's away, but we do hope that won't be for too long. There won't be a blood vengeance. We're not like the Titan and his fellow, Goliath. We're not like Brad Rice and we're not like Kyle
Styx. We're reasonable human beings, we've got our common sense intact.
Yeah, we're going to win on Sunday, but without that kind of means. I'm sure... Silverthorn... would want that...?
(Ulric Everest scratches his head and shrugs.) UE: Well, anyway, there are people queuing to pass the test, including
Titan and Goliath and Ray "Retch" Rancid further up ahead. That he's a legend and a superstar doesn't bother me, because I do not have anything to lose. Rancid is getting old and whether he has maintained his ability is yet to be found out. And I look forward to it. It's back to school, Rancid, and you too, Titan. Your assessment -and this is neither ridiculous
Archangel talk nor feverish Brad Rice babble- is up at hand, quite literally. And whether or not you pass the test? Let me be the judge of that. I'll know as soon as you pin me for the win. If you can do it, that
is - and somehow, I strongly doubt that. (Ulric Everest grins broadly. Suddenly, there's the muffled sound of a cellular phone ringing. Ulric Everest hears it and starts to desperately search the bag to find it.)
UE: No, no... hold on... (He pulls his hand out of the bag, with the phone in his hand. Ulric Everest answers it.) UE: Yeah, hello? Who? Oh, that's right, he told me... Well of course I am... I can't let my fans down... May I have your number, miss...?
Sherwood? Sarah Sherwood... Oh, hold on... but that's a Florida number! You saw me on TV? Well, then you know I'm quite the number of miles away from you... No, no, don't be. Look, I'll do my very best... my very, very best.
Well, I can do anything, so it shouldn't be much of a problem... Okay, then. Dave's Diner? I'll remember that. Okay, take care. Bye.
(Ulric Everest looks puzzled. He puts the phone back into the bag.) UE: Miami, Florida... I should have figured. That's where The A-Bomb lives nowadays... Maybe... I think I'd better concentrate on Sunday, Battle Zone.
I'll see you on Sunday, folks, in Molson Centre, Montreal. Brace yourselves... The Norsemen are on their way, en route for Battle Zone!
(Ulric Everest grabs a jacket hanging outside of camera view and picks up his bag to leave. He smiles a brief smile at the camera and disappears. The camera fades to black.)

And it's about to be my
