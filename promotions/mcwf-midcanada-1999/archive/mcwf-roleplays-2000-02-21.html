Roleplays

21 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kyle Styx --------------- ((The scene opens with Styx sitting on the steps to some old church in
Philadelphia, a light rain has begun to fall and his blonde/black hair is begining to cling to his face and mask his eyes. He is dressed in a pair of ripped black jeans, black "Type O Negative" t-shirt and a tattered army jacket with various patches along it.))
Kyle Styx: You know, its intresting....After all my challenges and all my comments Brad Rice wont even respond to me, its sad really...To see someone who calls himself a champion, to see someone who dares to claim they know what Insanity is be so afraid and full of cowardice...What can I expect from such a sinner though? All I can say is that Rice...You speak of judgement days, you say that people are going to be a sacrificial lamb....Well Rice, heres the kicker for you, I have never accepted such fate, be it given to me from a lowly sinner like you or a god...You should watch yourself "Maniac"
or it is you who shall be sacrificed............. ((Styx quietly reaches into his jacket and removes a pack of ciggarettes, casually he draws one from the pack and places it between his lips, lighting it with a scraped up zippo.))
Kyle Styx: Now onto other news...Inka Tupac wants to throw down another challenge to me...Well, I have a problem with this mystery partners bullsh*t you've been spewing...I'll tell you who I'm bringing right now, just myself...If your in there with whoever the hell you want, be it Tom Knight or who the hell ever I'm still going to make you pay, all of you...You drew me into this confrontation, you put my back against the wall in hopes that you'd be able to dominate me? Well heres a little lesson your soon going to learn, although I'm no monster like Titan, no powerhouse and perhaps I don't have the guys upstairs backing me.....I'm still alot more dangerous when the pressure is on, thats why I like the idea of this match so much..Because
I'm
going to leave your bloody beaten bodies in heap in the center of that ring..I don't care what the odds are, who I have to fight or how many I have to go through....Tom Knight, Inka Tupac....I've beaten both of you, now its time to simply PURIFY you of the taint you've created.
((Styx takes a long draw off his ciggarette, blowing the smoke upwards towards the grey sky.))
Kyle Styx: Now, onto the final little issue for today kids...The fates have decided to place a new sinner before me...One who I have never seen, never heard of and probably never will hear of...Earl "Frenzy" Mace...Nice name, perhaps you know what it is to frenzy then? What it means to have the beast inside you take full control, blocking out all rational human thought as you succumb to the most brutal and animalistic instincts.....Quite the sensation
I assure you sinner. This could be intresting, if you live up to your name that is, you see....You are what they are giving me to slack my thirst, to keep my hunger for destruction dulled until I can get my hands on Knight or
Rice...I don't know what your sins are, I know your actions before you take them...When you step into that ring with me, you will understand what chaos truely means.
((Scene fades to black.)) Copycat ------------- (As Copycat walks through to the backstage area, it is obvious he is furious about not beating Slick Sosna. He is breathing hard and is ferociously angry. He punches the first wall he comes to...)
Copycat: Ow! What the hell's wrong with me?!? That hurt... there's a lesson, don't punch the damn wall! Another lesson, don't get your dumb ass
COUNTED OUT!!!! (Copycat is yelling, and he throws a chair. Suddenly he sees the camera, stops, and looks directly into it. He grabs the camera from the cameraman and holds it right up to his face...)
Copycat: Sosna... listen up... when you wake up and hear this interview... I just want you to know that any time you want a piece of me, just bring it. Although the match ended in a draw, we both know I WON. I had you beaten through the whole match, and I beat you some more after the match. Hell, I had you beat before the match even started. How did it feel, Slick? How did it feel to put the only blemish on my record, a draw? To make me 4-0-1?
Oh... and how did it feel to be beaten with a chair? And to have your own
Sosna Suicide done to you on the hard concrete floor? YOU SEE, I WON!!!!!!
It doesn't matter that it goes down in the records as a draw, you didn't beat me. And I can beat you any day of the week, and I'm very willing to prove it.
But this Wednesday, I have a brand new challenge to get me back on track, a man by the name of the Egg. He's one of them there new boys from the
ACeWF.
It's too bad that in his very first showing in this fed, he's got to deal with the future of middleweights, Copycat. Listen up, Egg... I haven't had a loss. EVER. Period. And this was my first draw. And I am pissed. Sosna was rightfully mine, he was a dead man through the whole match. But I made a mistake, and I payed for it. NEVER AGAIN. There will be no mistakes with you, Mr. Egg. I don't know too much about you. You probably don't know all that much about me. So here's my gimmick... I copy you. Moves, style,
quotes, everything. I do it all, and I do it all better. And your finisher... the eggsault... it's just begging me to do it. A moonsault? Sorry, Egg, but with a finisher like that, I can pull it off perfectly. Hell, I do the damn thing every frickin match anyways, why not end ours with it. I can't say too much about you... I don't know too much about you...
but I can say a few things. Number one, if you are expecting to walk in here and walk out with an easy win, just forget about it. No way, Copycat puts up a fight, and puts you down for the count, every time... except this one,
Sosna... Number two, if you are expecting to walk out of here with any type of win, just forget about that too. Everyone knows that when a wildcat is cornered, they get violent. And I just got a draw. I feel I've been cornered, and it's up to me to show my loyal fans I can still wrestle with the best of them. Wait a minute... I hate these fans. Never mind. But
I'll
still beat the crap outta ya. Number three, if you are expecting here to walk out of here at all... FORGET ABOUT IT. I'll put you down with the eggsault. Or hey, maybe I'll even "crack an egg" with my wildcat.
HAHAHA!!! You get it? Uh... sorry, it wasnt funny. So anyways... I need a victory to get back on the right road to the top, and it will be this Wednesday night!
Welcome to the CWF, Egg, and welcome to a nightmare come true. Because the
CWF is where only the best will survive, and the rest will be destroyed. I am the best, Egg. You are the rest, prepare to be destroyed.
(Copycat looks on a TV monitor and sees Sosna begin to stand up and run to the back. Copycat screams like a little girl, drops the camera to the ground, and runs to his locker room to hide.)
Paul "The Crisis" Anderson ---------------------------------------- *The camera switches on, shortly after Sundays Battle Zone. Paul "The Crisis" Anderson is brought into view as he is walking down the corridor. He stops at a drinks machine and looks at what to get.* Dan Davidson: "Look guys, there he is! Let's get some info on this guy." *Paul slots his money in the machine and hits a button. He waits for the can to pop out but nothing happens. Paul gives the machine a kick.......nothing happens. Paul takes a few steps back then shoulder barges it.*
Paul: "God damn piece of crap!" Dan Davidson: "Er, Paul, could we get a few words?" Paul: "What? Who are you?" Dan Davidson: "I'm an interviewer for the CWF. I seen you trying to get your drink and I thought; that's the guy from earlier!"
Paul: "Well it doesn't really matter who you are. If I want your crappy services then I'll ask for them."
Dan Davidson: "But Paul..." Paul: "That's sir to you." Dan Davidson: "Ok.....sir. Well if you don't let any of the staff interview you, then how are the fans gonna find out about you and you feelings towards matches and wrestlers?"
Paul: "Well David you have made a good point there. So you can fulfil your little dream of interviewing me for only....$10.
Dan Davidson: "But.....but....ten.....oh, ok." *David hands over the $10 note to Paul. Paul takes it, smiles, then crams it into his pocket.* Paul: "So fire away with your first question." Dan Davidson: "Ok......What has brought you to the CWF? The fame? The cash? What?" Paul: "I came here to wrestle. The fame and cash is just one of the other good points. I came here when the CWF really needed someone to boost those ratings. Me and the CWF equal ratings. Very big ratings at that. And you can also expect to see the PPV buy rates to be higher as well. I've watched the CWF on TV before and I really must admit that the quality of the roster, well is pretty much trailer park trash."
Dan Davidson: "My mother lives in a trailer." Paul: "Well.....good for her. But did you see the fans all chanting my name? This is the first ever time I've been on television. And what happens after about 5 minutes? They all love me. Do you see losers like the A-Bomb get those sort of ovations? I got a bigger applaud then Brad Rice has ever gotten before."
Dan Davidson: "But sir, he is the World Champion. He represents this company in the world of wrestling. I wouldn't bad mouth him if I was you."
Paul: "Well David you wouldn't because he would slap the crap out of you. But the fans will get their moneys worth next week when I face that guy with the weird name. After they see my showcase of wrestling skills, Brad
Rice will be known as a title warmer for me."
Dan Davidson: "So I take it your pretty confident of having a debut win?" Paul: "Finally you say something good. You and all the fans know I ca beat that little "Crisis" wannabe. I mean look at him, I've never heard of him.
I doubt that the fans have ever heard of him. Have you?" Dan Davidson: ".........No. I haven't actually!" Paul: "See. Now as I go in on my debut, people will think I'm the underdog as no one has ever seen me wrestle. But I can guarantee right now that I will make myself heard all over the wrestling world. That I go in and rip that loser apart, bit by bit. A debut match that all the little kiddies can treasure for ever and tell their grandchildren about one day, that they witnessed the debut of the wrestler who will be the biggest name in the wrestling business."
Dan Davidson: "Well sir I think you have summed up everything right there." *Paul looks at Dan, then throws him out of the cameras view. He then takes the microphone off him.* Paul: "Inky Winky, you are a few days away from having your fat ass beaten like it has never been before. You go into the match, acting all tough, and saying I'm gonna beat this guy on his first match and stop him running his mouth. Well I'm gonna run my mouth forever and ever because the fans need me. The CWF needs me. And you, well nobody even knows you. And before I leave you all I say to you; do not worry. I will be back soon. And I will once again and forever entertain you!"
