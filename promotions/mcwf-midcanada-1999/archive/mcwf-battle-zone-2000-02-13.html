[MCWF] Battle Zone 02/13/00

2000-02-13
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ray Williams: Welcome to the Icelandic Pavilion in very cold Gimli, Manitoba for MCWF Battle Zone. We have a very good card tonight which will include two title matches plus a Hell in the Cell match between Python and
A-Bomb. Curt Thomas: Well it will be quite an action packed night, and I'm sure
Kyle Styx and Tom Knight will get bloodied up a bit, as Styx has been looking forward to really putting a hurting on Knight.
Ray Williams: Well we are ready at ringside, so let's go down to Brian
Johnson Copycat vs. Shawn Watt Brian Johnson: This is a standard match scheduled for a one fall. The referee will be Dan George. Coming to the ring first... weighing in at 220 pounds... Copycat! (crowd boos ******).
["Superbeast" by Rob Zombie starts playing as Copycat makes his way to the ring.] Brian Johnson: His opponent... accompanied by Tim Tirpak... weighing in at
265 pounds... a member of the Ruff Ryders... Shawn Watt! (crowd boos *****)
[The lights dim as The Ruff Ryders Anthem By Dmx plays over the audio. Shawn Watt walks to the ring. Tim Tirpak follows him closely, carrying a wooden table with the Ruff Ryders logo on it. Shawn lifts up the ropes, and climbs into the ring. The bell rings. Copycat rushes towards Watt, and nails him with a clothesline. Watt gets back up, but is hit by a dropkick.
Copycat with a cover. (...1...2... Shawn kicks out.) Copycat grabs Watt and pulls him up to his feet. Watt slaps Copycat across his jaw, and delivers him a knee to the head. Shawn Watt body-slams Copycat to the mat. Tim
Tirpak sends in table under the ropes. He grabs a folding chair, and throws it inside as well. Watt lifts up the table, and places it at the centre of the ring. He starts taunting the crowd. Copycat picks up the chair. He rushes towards Shawn Watt and hits him in the back of his head with the chair.]
Ray Williams: He knocked him out cold! [Copycat lays Shawn on top of the table. He climbs to the top rope, jumps off, and attempts a moonsault. Watt rolls out of the way.]
Curt Thomas: Copycat crashes right through the table! Ray Williams: Watt won't be able to execute the RR Power Bomb! There's no tables left! Curt Thomas: I'm sure he'll think of something. Ray Williams: He is not MacGyver you know... Curt Thomas: *sarcastic* Very funny. [Shawn Watt staggers up. He moves over to Copycat, and pins him. (...1...2... Copycat gets a shoulder up.) Watt grabs Copycat by his hair, and pulls him up. He unfolds the chair, and DDT's Copycat's head through it. He goes for another cover. (...1...2... Copycat gets hold of the ropes.)]
Curt Thomas: How did he manage that? Copycat ought to be dead by now! [Watt creams at Tim Tirpak to find him a table. Tirpak starts checking under the ring, but finds nothing. Copycat pulls himself up via the ropes.
Watt has got his back turned on him. Copycat springboards off the second rope, and delivers him a missile dropkick to the back. He grabs Shawn Watt, and executes a swinging neckbreaker. He goes for an elbowdrop, but Watt rolls out of the ring. Dan George counts him out. (...1) (...2) Shawn climbs up to the ropes. Copycat bounces off the ropes, and sends him back to ringside with a bodyblock. Copycat climbs the turnbuckle. Tim Tirpak helps Watt up to his feet. Copycat jumps off the apron...]
Ray Williams: Suicide dive! Curt Thomas: He hit both men! [George restarts the count out. (...1) Copycat picks up Shawn Watt, and brings him over to the announcer table.(...2) ]
Curt Thomas: Hey, get back into the ring! Ray Williams: I think I know what he's up to... [(...3) Copycat lifts him up, and powerbombs him onto the table.] Ray Williams: ...RR Powerbomb! Watt's finisher! [Copycat drags the unconscious Shawn Watt back into the ring. He pins him. (...1...2...3... The bell rings.) Brian Johnson: The winner of this match... Copycat! (crowd boos*******) Ray Williams: Well Copycat picks up the win, but we're going to get our table fixed here. Curt Thomas: I didn't think Copycat was that crazy, but he sure does know what it takes to win. And poor Shawn Watt got embarrrassed by his own move.
Ray Williams: But I think he put up one hell of a fight. I actually thought he had that one in the bag. But Copycat pulled it off with a lot of guts.
But now it's time for our next match. Curt Thomas: Inka Tupac is just getting over his injury and will be in tough against the hometown favourite, Del Carver.
Ray Williams: But if anyone is skilled enough to win against the odds, I'd put my money on Inka.
Inka Tupac vs. "Sinister" Del Carver Brian Johnson: This is a match scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Machu Pichu, Peru... weighing in at 335 pounds and accompanied by
Kalipe Rosas... Inka Tupac! (crowd cheers ****)
[Inka Tupac appears at the entrance, carrying his ceremonial mace. He walks down the aisle and climbs into the ring.]
Brian Johnson: And his opponent, from Winnipeg, Manitoba... weighing in at
265 pounds... The "Sinister" one, Del Carver! (crowd boos *****)
["Sinister Rulez" blares over the audio as Del Carver makes his way to the ring.] Brian Johnson: Referee for this match will be Peter Fast. Ray Williams: This will be a very even match between two skilled and talented wrestlers. I think this match can go both ways.
Curt Thomas: You're eyes are clouded as usual. Del Carver still hasn't been defeated in the MCWF and Inka Tupac certainly won't be the man to hand him his first loss!
Ray Williams: Due time, we'll know. Nevertheless, this is bound to get entertaining! [The bell rings. Inka Tupac and Del Carver circle around the ring. Del
Carver leaps forward and thrusts a kick to Inka's midsection. Inka Tupac grabs the leg, spins Del Carver around and levels him with a mighty clothesline. Inka Tupac covers Del Carver. (...1...2... Del Carver kicks out.)]
Curt Thomas: Del Carver easily kicks out! Ray Williams: Ridiculously early to go for the pin! This match won't be over so soon! [Del Carver gets to his feet. Del Carver tries to kick Inka in the stomach. Inka Tupac shrugs it off and grabs the leg. Del Carver is balancing on one foot.] Ray Williams: Making a mistake is human, -making the same mistake twice is stupid! [Del Carver reverses it with an enzuigiri kick to Inka's head, staggering him. Del Carver runs into the ropes and connects a spinkick to Inka Tupac's chest. Inka Tupac roars and charges at "Sinister" Del Carver. Del Carver takes him down with a toe drop hold.]
Curt Thomas: That's more like it! David levels Goliath! Umm... no, not THE
MCWF-North-American-substitute-champion Goliath... I'm talking Biblical myths here...
Ray Willaims: I would never have guessed... [Inka Tupac gets up. Inka Tupac sends Del Carver to the mat with a fierce right hand. Inka Tupac sits down on top of Del Carver and punches him and pounds him with series of punches. Inka Tupac drops an elbow onto
"Sinister" Del Carver. Inka Tupac signals for the Dominator. The crowd pops. Inka Tupac grabs one of Del Carver's legs and bends down to grab the other one. Del Carver kicks Inka Tupac in the face. Del Carver rolls backwards and is back on his feet.]
Curt Thomas: Inka Tupac fails to put on his move. Del Carver has got the upper hand! [Del Carver climbs the turnbuckle. Del Carver is ready to jump. Inka Tupac rushes over to the corner. Del Carver hits him with a twisting cross body block. Referee Peter Fast gets down to make the count, but Del Carver jumps up, breaking the pin.]
Ray Williams: Wonderful manoeuvre by the Sinister one! [Del Carver climbs the ropes. "Sinister" Del Carver goes for a legdrop off the top. Inka Tupac moves just in time and Del Carver hits nothing but air.
Inka Tupac picks up Del Carver and locks him in a side headlock. Del Carver struggles to break free. Inka Tupac tightens the hold. Del Carver gives
Inka an elbowshot to the ribs. Inka Tupac tightens the hold. Del Carver with another elbowshot. Del Carver slips out of Inka Tupac's grip and bouces off the ropes. Del Carver connects with a dropkick.]
Curt Thomas: Neither Inka Tupac, nor Del Carver has been able to take control of the match!
Ray Williams: But I've told you, in the end, Del Carver will win! [Del Carver shoves Inka Tupac. It has no effect. Inka Tupac shoves Del
Carver, sending him into a corner. Inka Tupac traps Carver in the corner and lands series of biting chops to the chest of Del Carver. Peter Fast gives Inka a four count to release Del Carver. (...1...2...3...4... Inka
Tupac backs out of the corner.) Inka Tupac roars and throws his weight into
Del Carver, tying him up in the ropes. Inka picks up Del Carver and elevates him with a suplex. Inka Tupac brings Del Carver to his feet. Inka
Tupac bends down to scoop up Carver. "Sinister" Del Carver grabs a firm hold of Inka's trousers and rolls him into an inside cradle.
(...1...2...3... The bell rings, Inka Tupac kicks out.)] Curt Thomas: Del Carver pulled that move right out of the textbook! Ray Williams: "Sinister" got the pinfall! Brian Johnson: The winner of this match, and still undefeated in the MCWF... "Sinister" Del Carver! (crowd boos ******) Ray Williams: Well carver got the win, but I think Inka was robbed. Curt Thomas: Who cares? A win's a win. Inka should consider not wearing something so easy to grab. It's nobody's fault but his own.
Ray Williams: Only in your twisted world. Curt Thomas: You better believe it. Now let's go to our Middleweight Title match, Atomic Jaguar taking on one half of the Tag Champions, Sean DuBose.
Ray Williams: Atomic Jaguar has been quite impressive lately, and is still undefeated, but so is DuBose so I personally think he is going to keep his title here tonight.
Curt Thomas: How can you say that? Jaguar has a Nike deal. That means he can't lose. Ray Williams: Give me a break. Middleweight Title Match Atomic Jaguar (challenger) vs. Sean DuBose (champion) Brian Johnson: This match is for the MCWF Middleweight Title, with referee
Jim Spencer. Coming to the ring first... weighing in at 214 pounds...
Atomic Jaguar! (crowd cheers ****). [The crowd cheers as all the lights go out. Then cherry bombs fall from the rafters to the ring as Atomic Jaguar makes his appearance. He walks to the ring.]
Brian Johnson: His opponent... accompanied by Tom Secu and Coach Brownoski... weighing in at 203 pounds... a member of The Rejects... current MCWF Middleweight and Tag Team Champion... Sean DuBose! (crowd cheers ******) [Blue and Yellow lights light the aisle. "Why Can't We Be Friends" by War plays through the speakers. The Rejects walks down the aisle followed by a green spotlight. Sean DuBose steps into the ring, and asks Atomic Jaguar to shake hands. Jaguar extends his hand, and kicks DuBose in the guts. The bell rings. Jaguar hits DuBose with an uppercut. DuBose screams, and moves backwards, holding a hand on his wounded jaw. Jaguar charges for him, and sends DuBose to the corner with a running shoulder-block. He garbs DuBose, and rams his head into the turnbuckle repeatedly. The crowd counts.
(...1...2...3...4...5...6...) Jaguar lets go of him. Sean DuBose sways back and forth for a couple of seconds, and falls heavily to the mat.]
Curt Thomas: Well, that's it for DuBose. Ray Williams: Come on! Curt Thomas: No, I'm serious! [Jaguar covers him. (...1...2... DuBose kicks out.)] Ray Williams: See? Curt Thomas: Pah... He was lucky. Barely kicked out of that one. [Atomic Jaguar stomps DuBose. He grabs his head, and chokes him over the ropes. Spencer gives him a four count to release. (...1) (...2) (...3)
(...4) Jaguar lets go of him. Coach Brownoski and Tom Secu yells at DuBose to get up. Jaguar picks him up, and sends whips him against the ropes.
DuBose ducks under a clothesline, rebounds off the ropes at the other end, and hits Atomic Jaguar with a dropkick. The coach applauds him. DuBose grabs jaguar, but is given a quick low blow without the referee noticing.
Tom Secu climbs into the ring, but Spencer stops him. As the ref has got his back turned, Jaguar seizes the chance to choke DuBose on the ropes again. Secu yells at Spencer, and points at Jaguar, but the ref shoves him away.]
Curt Thomas: Way to go, Jimmy! [Tom Secu goes back down to ringside, and Jaguar breaks the hold. Sean
DuBose crawls over to the corner, and slides out of the ring. Jim Spencer starts the count out. (...1) (...2) Coach Brownoski rushes to DuBose's assistance, while Secu shouts something to Atomic Jaguar. (...3) (...4) The
Coach pats Sean on his shoulder, and helps him back into the ring. Jaguar moves towards him, and slaps him across the chest. DuBose gives him a kick to the midsection. Sean climbs to the second rope, and goes for a bulldog.]
Ray Williams: Sean DuBose is back! Curt Thomas: It won't last for long, though... Ray Williams: ...DuBose with a cover! [(...1...2... Jaguar pushes him off.) DuBose jumps back up, and delivers
Jaguar a standing dropkick. Jaguar staggers to the corner. DuBose climbs up, and starts punching him. The crowd cheers, and starts counting.
(...1...2...3...4...) Atomic Jaguar gives him a DDT. DuBose gets up on his feet, and attempts a roundhouse kick. Jaguar catches his leg. DuBose spins around, and hits an enzuigiri. Jaguar is down. Sean DuBose runs to the turnbuckle, and climbs up. He lands a top rope moonsault onto Atomic
Jaguar.] Ray Williams: This might be it! Curt Thomas: No way! [Jim Spencer counts. (...1...2... Jaguar pulls a shoulder up.) Brownoski tells DuBose to finish him off. Sean DuBose picks Jaguar up, and executes an inverted DDT. He puts on the dragon sleeper.]
Ray Williams: DuBose with The Slap Shot! Curt Thomas: Fight it Jaguar... fight it... No! He tapped out! [The referee calls for the bell.] Brian Johnson: The winner of this match, and still MCWF Middleweight Champion... Sean DuBose! (crowd cheers******) Ray Williams: Another impressive match for Sean DuBose. It looks as if that belt will be around his waist for a long time to come.
Curt Thomas: Hey check this out. Here comes Snipe Johnson. [Snipe Johnson runs out from the wrestler's entrance with a chair in hand and slides into the ring. He stands over Atomic Jaguar as he writes in pain from the Slap Shot. Snipe Johnson holds the chair high above his head and slams it down on the ribs of Atomic Jaguar.]
Ray Williams: This is uncalled for. Break it up ref! Curt Thomas: Well Snipe has reason to be mad. Atomic Jaguar has been gretting increasingly rude to him over the past week.
[Snipe Johnson leaves the ring to a chorus of boos as Atomic Jaguar is helped out of the ring clutching his ribs.]
Ray Williams: Well fi you thought that was rough, then just wait until these next two get into the ring.
Curt Thomas: Well if the Norsemen have anything to do with this match, they'll be in Derrick Hall's doghouse bigtime.
Ray Williams: Well let's see if they get involved in this one. Python Rules Match Python vs. The A-Bomb Brian Johnson: This is a special "Python Rules" hell in a cell match. Introducing first, from Oklahoma City, Oklahoma... weighing in at 217 pounds... the "Prime Minister of Hardcore", Python! (crowd boos *****)
["The Dope Show" by Manson plays over the PA. Python walks out with his wet hair hanging in his face. He walks down the aisle, slowly snapping his head back and forth as the fans boo and shout their vulgar words at him. As he gets to the stairs he stands on the top step and spits water that has been in his mouth the entire entrance, onto the ringside fans.]
Brian Johnson: And his opponent, hailing from Miami, Florida... weighing in at 367 pound, standing 7'2" tall... a member of The Norsemen and the master of the Nuclear Powerbomb... The A-Bomb! (crowd cheers ******)
["Crush 'em" by Megadeath starts playing and The A-Bomb enters after a while. Pyroes goes of as he passes the MCWF logo, and continues down the aisle with quick steps, ignoring the fans. He walks over the top rope, and stops in the middle of the ring as fire rises from all the turnbuckles. The
A-Bomb steals Brian Johnson's microphone.] A-Bomb: Gimli, Manitoba, ARE YOU READY TO TURN UP THE HEAT!? (crowd cheers ****) Brian Johnson: Referee for this match will be Dan George. [The two wrestlers glare at each other. The cell is lowered from above.] Ray Williams: Behold, the lowering of the cell! Two man enter, one man... Curt Thomas: ...actually, in this case, nobody enters and one man leaves. David Copperfield wouldn't happen to be in the building? Ray Williams: Gimme a break. [The bell rings. Python dares The A-Bomb to come at him. The A-Bomb turns around to taunt the crowd. Python sees his opportunity and charges at The
A-Bomb. The A-Bomb spins around, planting a powerful elbow to the temple of
Python. Python sprawls backwards, crashing to the canvas.]
Ray Williams: Oh, my! The A-Bomb saw it coming! Curt Thomas: Python didn't hesitate to exploit the situation, I'll have to hand him that one.
Ray Williams: Attacking someone whose back is turned on you is a bad thing! Curt Thomas: And we like it! It certainly shows initiative on Python's behalf! Keep it going!
Ray Williams: He received quite a stunner there, but look at him! He's back up as if nothing ever happened!
[The A-Bomb grabs Python's arm and sends him into the cell wall. The A-Bomb clotheslines Python to the mat. The A-Bomb chokes Python with his boot. Dan
George waits for a while before giving The A-Bomb a four count to release
Python. He makes a very slow count. (...1...2...3...4... The A-Bomb backs off.)]
Curt Thomas: Hey, hey, Dan George! Calm down! We've got all day, y'know! Take your time, man... Ray Williams: I'm not sure Dan George was the right referee for this match. Curt Thomas: What? He fits it like hand in a glove. [The A-Bomb picks Python up and gives him a sidewalk slam. The A-Bomb tries a standing splash. Python manages to lift his knees, stopping him in mid-air. Both competitors get back up. Python kicks The A-Bomb in the abdomen and delivers a swinging neckbreaker. Python applies a cross-armbreaker. The A-Bomb is in bad pain.]
Ray Williams: Python has The A-Bomb in a deadlock! Curt Thomas: Keep him on the mat. That'll improve the odds a great deal! [The A-Bomb reaches for the ropes... Python tightens the hold. The A-Bomb gets hold of the bottom rope. Dan George looks bored.]
Ray Williams: Do something, ref! Curt Thomas: Give it a rest, Ray! Dan George is the man in charge. Give the poor guy a break!
Ray Williams: Do your job, Dan! [Dan George glances over at the commentator's table where Ray Williams is frantically trying to signal him to stop Python. Dan George gives Python a four count to release The A-Bomb. (...1...2... The A-Bomb breaks free with a surge of energy.)]
Ray Williams: The A-Bomb had to take the matter in own hands! Curt Thomas: Well, you can't complain. Python was in the exact same situation a while ago. [The A-Bomb gets up, clutching his arm. Python armdrags The A-Bomb to the mat. Python climbs to the top. Python goes for the Python's Point, but The
A-Bomb avoids it.] Ray Williams: He got away in the eleventh hour! Curt Thomas: And Python goes down hard! [The A-Bomb signals for the Nuclear Powerbomb! The crowd goes wild with a mixture of boos and cheers. The A-Bomb lifts up Python. Python summersaults forward to reverse it with a face slam.]
Ray Williams: Python with the quick reversal! [Python covers The A-Bomb. (...1...2... The A-Bomb lifts a shoulder.)] Curt Thomas: The A-Bomb only just managed to kick out! Ray Williams: Python couldn't get the three-count. [Python bounces off the ropes. Python tries a double axehandle smash. The
A-Bomb catches him in mid-air and drives him to the mat with a massive chokeslam. The A-Bomb covers Python. (...1...2...3... The bell rings.)]
Curt Thomas: The A-Bomb gets the pinfall! Brian Johnson: The winner of this match, The A-Bomb! Ray Williams: Very impressive match. I'm sure Python will be dissappointed in this result. Curt Thomas: He should have won. If I were him, I'd be looking for revenge. Now for more bloody action, it's time to raise the cell and lower the cage. Honestly, I was worried that the ceiling would collapse in this barn, but I guess I was wrong.
Ray Williams: How can you say that? This place is full of history and heritage. You're lucky someone in the crowd doesn't kick you around afer the show.
Curt Thomas: I'd like to see them try. Canadian Title Cage Match "The Cavalier" Tom Knight (challenger) vs. Kyle
Styx (champion) [The cage is lowered over the ring.] Brian Johnson: This is a cage match for the MCWF Canadian Title. Coming to the ring first, from Toronto, Ontario... weighing in at 243 pounds...
accompanied by fair lady Guinevere... "The Cavalier" Tom Knight! (crowd cheers ******) ["Ride of the Valkyries" by Richard Wagner plays as Tom Knight and Guinevere come to the ring. Tom Knight enters the cage.] Brian Johnson: And his opponent, from Philadelphia, PA... weighing in at
228 pounds... the current holder of the MCWF Canadian Title... Kyle Styx!
(crowd boos *****) ["War Pigs" by Black Sabbath booms from the loudspeakers. The lights dim and a large burning cross appears at the end of the entryway, Styx appears kneeling before it. He stands up and walks towards the ring, occasionally slapping the hands of a few fans on his way.]
Brian Johnson: Referee for this match will be Ted Jones. [Kyle Styx opens the cage door to enter. Suddenly, he grabs Guinevere by the wrist and forces her inside. The door locks behind them.]
Ray Williams: Oh, no! Guinevere is trapped with those two brutes! Curt Thomas: Good. A damsel in distress! Let's see if The Cavalier can earn his title! [The bell rings. Kyle Styx shoves Guinevere to the floor. Tom Knight charges at him, filled with rage. Tom Knight corners Kyle Styx. Tom Knight hammers Kyle Styx with series of punches, kicks and chops. Tom Knight climbs the cage wall. Tom Knight bulldogs Kyle Styx to the mat.]
Ray Williams: Tom Knight is on fire tonight, and Kyle Styx has to take credit for that! Curt Thomas: This is wrestling the way we like it! [Tom Knight helps Guinevere to her feet. Tom Knight guides Guinevere to a corner and tells her to stay. Kyle Styx is back up. Kyle Styx ambushes Tom
Knight from behind. Kyle Styx dropkicks Tom Knight in the back. Kyle Styx sends "The Cavalier" into the steel cage. Kyle Styx grabs Tom Knight by the collar and slams the back of his head into the cage wall. Tom Knight collapses to the mat.]
Ray Williams: My God! I can't watch this! Curt Thomas: This might get interesting. I wonder how Guinevere is part of
Kyle Styx' plan!
Ray Williams: Let's hope we never find out! [Kyle Styx furiously stomps Tom Knight. Then, he turns to Guinevere. Kyle
Styx laughs and makes a cutting motion towards her. She tries to climb the cage wall, but can't do it. Kyle Styx closes in on her, slowly, enjoying her fear.]
Curt Thomas: Ok, this might get somewhat out of hand... Ray Williams: Tom Knight, GET UP!!! [Tom Knight is back on his feet, a bit dazzled. Tom Knight pulls Kyle Styx around and levels him with a short-arm clothesline. Tom Knight puts Kyle
Styx in a camel clutch. Tom Knight tightens the hold, using all the strength he's got.]
Curt Thomas: Kyle Styx DID make this personal, but... Ray Williams: Tom Knight's anger is in control! No one messes with lady
Guinevere and gets away with it!
[Guinevere begs Tom Knight to let go of Kyle Styx. Tom Knight shakes his head and puts more pressure to the hold. Guinevere tries to calm down "The
Cavalier" signalising she's ok. Tom Knight breaks the hold. Tom Knight picks up Kyle Styx. Knight gives Kyle Styx Russian Legsweep. Tom Knight climbs the cage and waits for Kyle Styx to get up. Styx gets to his feet.
"The Cavalier" leaps off the cage with a missile dropkick. Kyle Styx falls to the mat and Tom Knight hits the canvas, hurting his leg. Both men remain on the mat. Ted Jones starts a knockout-count.
(...1...2...3...4...5...6...7... Kyle Styx grabs hold of the cage and pulls himself up... 8... Tom Knight gets up, limping slightly.)]
Curt Thomas: Both men are still going! Ray Williams: I bet they'll feel the bumps they've taken tonight for a long time to come...
[Kyle Styx sends Tom Knight into the cage wall. Kyle Styx slowly climbs the cage. Tom Knight grabs his foot, stopping him from leaving. Kyle Styx kicks at Tom Knight, but "The Cavalier" refuses to let go. Kyle Styx jumps at
Knight with a flying somersault neckbreaker. Kyle Styx nails Tom Knight with his Implant DDT.]
Curt Thomas: They've been going at it, but so far, none of them have attempted to get the pinfall!
Ray Williams: It's more than win/loss records at stake here, Curt. [Kyle Styx legdrops Tom Knight. Kyle Styx grabs both of "the Cavalier's" legs and headbutts him in the groin.] Curt Thomas: Oww... [Kyle Styx pulls something out of his tights.] Ray Williams: It's a pair of handcuffs! [Kyle Styx puts one of the cuffs around Knight's wrist and locks it together with the cage wall. Kyle Styx starts to stomp Tom Knight. Kyle
Styx turns to Guinevere, points at Tom Knight and laughs. "The Cavalier" is desperate to get out of the handcuffs. Kyle Styx backs away and hits a superkick to the chin of Tom Knight.]
Curt Thomas: Tom Knight is absolutely helpless at the hands of Styx! [Kyle Styx walks towards Guinevere. Kyle Styx grabs her arm.] Ray Williams: Styx, no! [Guinevere gives Kyle Styx a horrible high-heeled kick to his knee. Kyle
Styx falls to the match, roaring in pain. Blood drips from a gash on his knee. Guinevere covers her mouth in shock.]
Curt Thomas: I never, ever would have guessed! In a violent match like this, Guinevere was the first person to draw blood!
[Kyle Styx howls with fury and grabs Guinevere to put her in the River of
Styx.] Ray Williams: He'll break her arm! [Tom Knight tries to get out of the handcuffs. Tom Knight pulls madly in the cuffs. Kyle Styx laughs at him. Tom Knight pulls...]
Ray Williams: You can do it! Pull, dammit! [Tom Knight hauls at the handcuffs... and they're torn apart! The crowd goes wild.] Ray Williams: Yes, yes! [Tom Knight sends Kyle Styx into the cage. Tom Knight gives Styx three quick elbowdrops and starts climbing the cage. Tom Knight nails Kyle Styx with the Excalibur. Tom Knight climbs the cage again.]
Curt Thomas: He's going to escape the cage! Ray Williams: And I doubt Kyle Styx is in any shape to stop him! [Tom Knight is standing on top of the cage. "The Cavalier" hesitates... he glances down to ringside. Tom Knight clenches his teeth together and leaps off the cage. The crowd gasps in unison. Tom Knight hits Kyle Styx with an elbowdrop off the cage.]
Curt Williams: He jumped! Insanity! [Both men are down. Ted Jones starts a knockout count. (...1...2...3...4...5...6...7...8...9... Tom Knight lays an arm over the chest of Kyle Styx.) Ted Fast makes the pinfall count. (...1...2...3... The bell rings.)]
Curt Thomas: We've got a winner!! Brian Johnson: The winner of this match and new MCWF Canadian champion... Tom Knight! (crowd cheers *******) Ray Williams: Tom Knight did it! He overcame all the odds and is our new
Canadian Champion!
Curt Thomas: I'm sure Kyle Styx will get a rematch. Knight was just lucky he rolled over. I don't think he even knows he pinned him.
Ray Williams: No luck there. Juts a lot of heart and skill. Curt Thomas: You are most surely mistaken. Knight is one lucky soul today but he will most definitely see Styx again in the near future.
Ray Williams: Well that's all the time we have for today. We'll see you all next Sunday on Battle Zone!
