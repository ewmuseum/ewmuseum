APPLICATION TO JOIN AN INTRODUCTION Company Objectives - The CWF's main goal is to create an e-wrestling environment where handlers can enjoy themselves, and maintain a high standard of talent. We emphasize good feuds and angles and work to get everyone involved. This federation was created so the handlers can enjoy their e-wrestling experience.
CWF History - On August 15, 1999, the Mid Canada Wrestling Federation (MCWF) was created with its head office in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. It began with a tiny roster of only eight wrestlers, but grew rapidly until it was forced to close temporarily on September 30, 1999. It stayed closed until December
28, 1999 when President Derrick Hall recruited a talented staff to aid him in his mission to create the most enjoyable fed around. The roster grew to over thirty members, and on February 17, 2000, the announcement was made that the MCWF was taking over the roster of the Atlantic Canadian eWrestling Federation (ACeWF) and the name was changed to the Canadian
Wrestling Federation (CWF) and the roster was boosted to over sixty members, and is believed to be the largest roster in any fed, anywhere.
The CWF has now started a new era in ewrestling and looks to be at it's highest point ever and is still growing and getting better.

RULES Rules when Joining the CWF - You must submit a sample roleplay before you are allowed to join. This is very important as we want handlers who will be able to be competitive in the CWF.
Each handler is allowed two wrestlers. No real wrestlers and no ripoffs are allowed. Do's and Don'ts of Roleplaying - DOs Roleplay at least once a week if you are scheduled to wrestle. Write more than ten lines. Send your roleplays to me (email address given when you join). DON'Ts Swear more than what is said in the WWF. Use sexual content in your roleplays. Mention "real wrestling federations" such as the WWF, WCW, or ECW in your roleplays Mention other e-federations in your roleplays. Mention past titles you've won in other federations. They have no bearing on your ranking here. You start out new and you have to work your way up.
Use e-wrestling or insider terms in your roleplays such as, "jobber, e-fed, roleplay, angle, feud," etc.
Don't use common catch phrases found in the WWF, WCW, or ECW. Such catchphrases include Stone Cold Steve Austin's "Cuz Stone Cold Said So"
Rob Van Dam's "I'm the whole F'N Show " and Scott Halls "I'm just TOO SWEET!" and the like. You'll lose points for originality Tips - Be creative and be sure to set the scene in your roleplay. Tell us where your wrestler is. What he is doing. What's going on around him. Things such as these will make your roleplay more realistic and believable.
Creativity and Originality are two major factors in judging roleplays. Miscellaneous - Roleplays are sent out in packets every day, but will not be sent out on Mondays. Occasionally we'll make an exception to this. You may find announcements at the beginning or end of a packet sometimes as well.
CWF EVENTS Interview Show - We are planning an interview show on Tuesdays, which will feature approximately 5 interviews, a recap of the weeks cards, rankings, and possibly one match which will be a TV title match and possibly other features to add interest.
Weekly Cards - We have two cards each week. The main show is called Warfare. It is held on Wednesdays and is written in a full play-by-play format. The other,
Battle Zone, which is held on Sunday, is also written in play-by-play format. Pay-Per-Views - We hold a Pay-Per-View once every month or two. This is to help us work on storylines, feuds, and angles better. It also helps us build up to a climate for the PPV show. PPVs are held on a Sunday and take the place of the Battle Zone card for that week. The name of the PPV is announced shortly after the previous Pay-Per-View ends.
CWF TITLES Titles Available - There are currently five titles in the CWF. Here is a list of them. They are in order of how valuable they are to be considered.
CWF World Heavyweight Championship CWF Tag Team titles CWF Canadian title CWF Middleweight title CWF Television title Two more titles will be introduced in a short time. These are the Hardcore title and the Canadian Tag Team titles.
Defending Titles - This section details how often titles should be defended. CWF World Heavyweight title - Must be defended at least one Warfare a month plus at the PPV.
CWF Tag Team titles - Must be defended at least every second week plus on the PPVs. CWF Canadian title - Must be defended at least once every two weeks plus at the PPVs. CWF Television title - Must be defended on every Battle Zone, Interview Show, plus at the PPVs. CWF Middleweight title - Must be defended once a week plus on PPVs. If any of these titles are not defended within the specified time given, then it will be forfeitted.
ANGLES / FEUDS We value angles and feuds highly in the CWF. If you have a suggestion for a feud or angle, make sure to email me and the other handler in question about it. We'll go over it and if it sounds good, I'll incorporate it into the cards. Be original with your feuds and angles. Don't rip off other feds ideas. And, if you're in a feud or angle with somebody, and they win.
But you roleplayed better, don't complain about it. Your loss furthered the angle and you'll usually get payback somewhere in the future against that same person.
