MCWF - Seeking talent

2000-01-21
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone. My name is [name withheld] and I would like to inform you all about the Mid Canada Wrestling Federation. It has been reopened after a short closure. It was started in August of 1999 and is stronger than ever. We currently have a good sized roster but are looking for some new talent to fill the top spots in the fed. There is a card every Wednesday and a Pay Per View every month. It is an email based fed in which roleplays are sent out in packets each day. It is not a hardcore fed although there will be gimmick matches occur from time to time. I pride myself on using suggestions for angles and feuds from the handlers and I work to make an enjoyable e-wrestling environment for everyone. The homepage has just been redesigned and is looking better than ever. So if anyone is interested in checking out or joining MCWF, you can check out the webpage at
http://www.angelfire.com/mb/MCWF or you can email me at [detail withheld] with any questions that you might have. I look forward to hearing from all of those interested.
