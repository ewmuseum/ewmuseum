MCWF — Roleplay

Roleplays

20 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rizzo --------- *Rizzo and his manager Heidi are in their lockerroom after his victory. He and Heidi are just about to leave when Dan Davidson from the newly merged
CWF
enters the lockerroom. Rizzo looks at him with an evil look on his face.
The camera gets turned on and the interview begins. * DD: Well, Mr. Rizzo. I'd like to say job well done out there. Rizzo: Who in the name of all that is holy are you? DD: I'm Dan Davidson. I'm one of the reporters for CF. Heidi: Oh yeah. I guess zat was what Taylor was talking about. Ze ACeWF and MCWF merged. Rizzo: HAHAHAHAHA WE CRUMBLED THE FOUNDATION OF THE ACeWF!!!!!! Heidi; Now now. We have much more to do now that we're headed to ze CWF. DD: She's right. You're in a match Wednesday against the MCWF World
Champion Brad "Maniac" Rice. Rizzo; I think I remember that cat. oh yeah, he's a little punk ass who thinks he's from some other planet. Hey, listen 3rd rock boy, it's time for you to get some real competition. You see, I've beaten the best of the best out there. You, well, you had it easy.
DD: Silverthorn was no pushover for him. Rizzo: Whatever you say. I can take any of them suckers out. Be they big or small, fat or skinny, slow or fats. No one is a match for my in ring ability or my intelligence. Rice, be prepared to get cooked in 5 minutes. No one, not even your Uncle Ben can save you now!
Heidi: When we get to ze CWF, only one man will remain the World Champ. Rizzo will stand tall. Rizzo: Fist thing you need to do is get your head checked. If you think you can beat me you're down right crazy.
DD: Well, his nickname is maniac. Rizzo; Wow, ohh, that strikes fear into my heart! AAHH OOH!! *Rizzo laughs* Well, "Maniac"...OH...hey...is his entrance theme form Flashdance? DD: Uh, no, I'm pretty sure it's not. Rizzo; Oh, Heidi loves that song. DD: You seem to be very confident that you will come out on top. Rizzo: I have every goddamn right to be. You see, I've beaten greats such as Ray "Retch" Rancid and Dow Jones. Now, it's time for me to show these little punks in the CWF what true defeat is like. I have everything it takes to win...speed, intelligence, ingenuity, experience AND youth. Most of which that wannabe Rice doesn't have. Hey, Danny boy, is JJ still employed?
DD: I assume you mean Jake Jones. Yes he is. Rizzo; Good. I like him. He kisses my ass alot. DD: Cute, very cute. Well, any last thoughts? Rizzo; Of course. I'd like to say that it's about time that prick Taylor got popped. Also, CWF, watch out, Rizzo is not a friendly type of guy. If you cross my path, you'll be guaranteed to receive a RimJob. So everyone, take a good look at your next CWF champ. You'll bee seeing that gold around my waist for a long time!
*Camera cuts to a Warfare promo* Brad "Maniac" Rice ----------------------------- (The scene opens up inside of the CWF's headquarters, there is murmuring throughout the building and all the CWF writers and producers are outside of a room with a gold plate on it reading, "CWF President Derrick Hall" The door abruptly opens and the office is completely silent as Brad Rice walks out of the door and slams it close angrily. Brad is wearing his normal attire, which doesn't seem to suit the CWF offices, his attire consist of an
Alice in Chains shirt, and torn jeans. His hair is in dreadlocks which have been dyed purple.)
(Brad has his MCWF World Title across his shoulder and looks at it with his astounding, blue eyes. He walks down a flight of stairs and a man in a suit looks down at Brad worryingly, and then knocks and enters Derrick Hall's office. Brad stops when he reaches the bottom of the stairs and makes his way to a bench. He sits down and begins scratching at his arms and rocks back in forth insanely on the bench.)
Brad: Why….. (Brad's eyes begin to seem to grow darker and his face turns from a pale white to an enraged red. He begins to scream out his words.)
Brad: CWF! I AM THE MCWF CHAMPION! (Brad tries to calm himself, he accomplishes one goal by stop screaming, but his words are quiet and you can tell he is having a hard time holding back his frustration.)
Brad: Now what? I have to face Rizzo? I have to once again defend my belt? I would hate to be my opposition this Wednesday…Rizzo, we have been in the same federation before, we have had a few quarrels if my memory if correct.
You didn't seem much of a threat however, and you still pose no threat to me….WHY MUST I DEFEND IT AGAIN!?!?
(Brad looks back and sees a worried looking secretary standing at the flight of the stairs. Brad gives her a look that could kill, she gasps and hastily runs back to the other workers. Brad stands up and kicks the bench, which slides a few feet across the floor, making a horrendous screech as it slides. Brad fastens the MCWF World Title across his waist. Brad puts his hand on top of his head and begins pulling at his purple dreadlocks. He yells in frustration and walks to a door. A set of security guards begin to talk to Brad, Brad punches them both, they fall to the ground, and Brad exits the CWF headquarters.
(Brad steps outside and begins to walk and stops as he sees a limousine stop at the entrance of the CWF headquarters. A man with a World Title looks at
Brad, Brad returns the look. The man's face is not visible but Brad stares at him until he disappears through the entrance.)
Brad: WHY!?!? This is mine. (Brad points to his waist which holds the MCWF World Title.) Brad: This is not going to happen...All of you listen to me, and listen to every word I speak. I..am..not..going..to..fight.
(Brad's face begins to shake violently and he holds his hands, which tremble as well.) Brad: I..am..going..to..DESTROY!!! Rizzo…Prepare for devastation, prepare to face Brad Rice, Prepare…to be Crucified!
(The scene fades to black, showing the MCWF World Title and the ACeWF World
Title laying next to each other. Fade to black.)
Copycat ------------- (Superbeast by Rob Zombie begins to play... and immediately the crowd lets out a chorus of boos. Copycat steps out from the back, wearing hiking boots, jeans, and a black t-shirt. The shirt has a picture of a maple leaf, with
MCWF written on it. The M is crossed out in red, and below the leaf it says
"BIGGER AND BETTER". Copycat laughs and enjoys the booing as he casually strolls down to the ring. Along the way, he reaches out to shake a fans hand, and as the fan reaches out, Copycat grabs the fan's beer and runs to the ring. He drinks it, throw the cup on the ground, and jumps through the ropes, into the ring. Copycat grabs a microphone from ring announcer Brian
Johnson............)
Copycat: Ah... the more you fans boo me, the more it inspires me to beat the crap out of anyone in the ring with me. So thank you sooooo much for your support! HA!
In case you are wondering, yes, I did hear the comments from my opponent for this Sunday, Slick Sosna. And I just have to say... you are going to be very unpleasantly surprised come this Sunday night. Now Sosna... get one thing straight... I know you were only attempting to make fun of me here... and a very bad attempt it was... but my record is definitely not 0-5. I'm 4-0.
And if you bother to show up for our match, I'll be 5-0. Y'see, we both say that we aren't going to stop until we get gold. Well that doesn't make much sense. One of us has got to be stopped in this match. And I believe with
4
straight wins and not a damn blemish on my record, I have the momentum.
Not to mention ability, speed, intelligence, looks, charm, and about any other category you want to throw in there. But don't pay attention, none of that stuff matters. I will win this match, mainly because I want it more. I am a predator, and you are my prey, Sosna. And there's a few other guys who can tell you what it's like to be predator to Copycat. Hmm... I'm trying to think of someone who can tell you what it's like to beat me... but... there isn't anyone!!! So "Sex Symbol of the 90's", and by the way, you are a very sexy man indeed... just a joke, but I hope that scares you a little... if you really want to mess with a man that has defeated every giant that has come his way... bring it. I have you outmatched, and definitely outclassed.
You know you have an opponent beat when they start making fun of your name because they have no other traits to be proud of. So Sosna, what do you think about the name Copycat, hmm? It doesn't matter what the name is, smart guy, all that matter is how you fight. But I don't think you are prepared to fight. You are too busy moving crap in Arizona to pay attention to the little old 220 pounder who can deliver a beating to any man in the sport.
And I don't even think you are concentrating on me either... Python? You want him so bad. Well don't look past the challenge right in front of you.
No matter what you say, you know that Copycat is a damn good competitor, and if you want to look past me right to Python, I hope you've got some medical insurance. Just be ready for me at Battle Zone, and I'll give you a fight to remember... you'll remember your first loss.
I'd like to welcome all the new ACeWF members into the CWF. Next Wednesday, I have a match against a man called the Egg. Now I don't know too much about this guy, and I'm not going to pretend to. I'll trash talk to him as soon as
I'm done beating the fat bastard, Slick. But Egg, if you are anywhere near as dumb as Sosna... then I'd just back off. I'm going places in this fed...
and sending people places... like the hospital. So... we all know it's true... win number 5, Slick Sosna; number 6, the Egg.
(Copycat throws his microphone to the floor, and walks over to the crowd to make his exit. They start swinging at him, and he remembers just how hated he is. Copycat laughs, and spits at a fan, then hurries to the back before the fan can do anything about it)
Atomic Jaguar --------------------- (Jaguar walks into a small bar inside the arena that Battle Zone will take place in. He takes off his coat and hangs it on a hook on the wall. The bartender and a couple guys instantly recognize him)
Bartender: Hey, Jag! What can I get ya? AJ: Hmm, how 'bout a martini Bartender: You've got it Guy: (drunk) hey... I know you... you're that fighting guy! (AJ gives him a glance and turns away. He picks up a newspaper and flips a couple pages) AJ: The CWF's got their own section in this thing? Bartender: Yeah, you guys are pretty famous around here AJ: Hmm... (looks at a column on the page with the Warfare lineup) Well, at least I know half the guys on Warfare
Bartender: This Warfare's got ACEWF guys facing MCWF guys of the same ranking. We don't care much about ACEWF around here, so we know these idiots as well as you do
AJ: Speaking of idiots, Snipe Johnson's facing me tomorrow. I've been looking forward to this for a week
Bartender: Isn't that the guy that lives in his parent's basement? AJ: His mom's a prostitute in Germany, and his dad got a life sentence in jail for stealing sheep from farmers and doing... you don't want to know... to them
Bartender: Well, isn't that disturbing. Your drink's ready (Jag gulps it down and asks for another) AJ: You guys get any TV stations all the way out here? Bartender: Well, the one that shows CWF on it at least (The bartender clicks a button on a small TV in the corner of the room, and
Snipe's ugly little
face slowly comes into view)
Snipe: I think- (the screen fades away) Bartender: Must be another storm outside. If there's a storm here and the interview is broadcast from here, looks like no one in the country can see Snipe's interview. What a shame...
(the screen is all blurred, and the bartender turns the TV off) Bartender: Here's your drink (AJ takes a sip of it) AJ: Thanks. Hey, look at this thing in the paper (AJ points to a large article. The title is "CWF star will have sex for money". There's a picture of Snipe. The article's about how Snipe needs some money to buy new pants for all the times he wets his since he's so scared of AJ, and about how he's willing to have sex with other men to get some cash)
AJ: Geez, that guy's desperate. Oh well, not my problem. I'm gonna go now Bartender: How do you want to pay? (AJ grabs a large roll of bills from his wallet and hands it to the man) AJ: Keep the change (The bartender smiles, and AJ takes his coat off the hook and walks out of the bar into the backstage area of the arena)
(Fade to the MCWF logo and the ACEWF logo on each side of the screen, and the two smash together to make the CWF logo)
"Wild Child" Jimmy Sexton ---------------------------------------- (As the stereo plays in the background, "Wild Child" Jimmy Sexton sits in the living room of his apartment drinking a glass of iced tea and looking over papers from MCWF, now known as CWF. He looks over the roster as well as others who are involved in the league)
Jimmy Sexton: Just a few days away from the "grand re-opening". Hey, people that know me know that i'm an outspoken person. BUT until I go through everything and see what goes on around here i'm gonna hold off on criticism of events and people. I got an interview later in the week with some chick named Crystal McKay so things will get going then leading up to Wednesday
Warfare.
(Sexton gets up and goes over and turns off the stereo. He then returns to his chair and grabs the remote to the tv and flips through the channels)
Jimmy Sexton: What I WILL say right now is this. From what I hear this guy
Derrick Hall is something else. But it seems he has some of the same attributes as Frank Taylor of ACeWF had. He has his "bodyguards" so to speak. But at least he isn't hiding behind some ladies skirt like Frank did with that bitch Carmen Simmons.
As far as wrestlers go, some names i've heard of. Knight, Predator, Manipulator, Lowe, just to name a few. And with the influx of wrestlers from ACeWF this is turning into one hell of a lineup.
(Sexton turns off the tv and tosses the remote onto a nearby chair) Jimmy Sexton: I'll be taking off for Halifax in a few days. But don't think i'll be just sitting on my ass between now and then. I didn't come into this business to watch from the sidelines while everyone else rises to the top. I-M-P-A-C-T, plain and simple. Look for it. It WILL happen.
A-Bomb ------------ [The screen fades in to show a big dark and damp room. Some areas in the room is lightened up by some lamps, but besides that the room is totally dark. A wooden bench in the left corner and a table in front of it is all the furniture in the room. The A-Bomb can be seen sitting on the bench, drinking something from a big glass. Tom Wilson, the CWF reporter, walks up to him.]
T. Wilson - Can I sit down? The A-Bomb - I don't mind. T. Wilson - I wondered if you have any comments about the ACeWF and MCWF merging together to form the CWF... The A-Bomb - It shall be fun! We have a great roster! Something like 50 wrestlers. It's the most beautiful thing I've ever seen. The cards will be much bigger, and there is nothing bad about it.
T. Wilson - But the competiotion for the belts will be much harder. The A-Bomb - I know. That's what makes it so fun! T. Wilson - Alright! What about your match today? The A-Bomb - Against Goliath! You interviewed me yesterday, so there is no point for me to sit here and repeat everything one more time. I've said what
I had to say about that match. T. Wilson - But I want to know what your strategy is for that match. The A-Bomb - Do you really think I'm going to tell them to you in front of the camera? If I reveal my plans Goliath can sit down and think out a way to avoid them. You know what I'm saying? He's not as dumb as he looks like. He has enough experience to know how to handle different wrestlers.
T. Wilson - Any hints? The A-Bomb - No, no, no! T. Wilson - OK, I get it! What about your match at the first official CWF card at Wednesday. You're up against Retch Rancid. The A-Bomb - I know. That's yet another tough one. He may be smaller than
Goliath in size, but he's just as much experienced, and if not more. He's a living legend. He has held more titles than you can count. He knows more about wrestling than everybody around here.
T. Wilson - Even more than you? The A-Bomb - Are you kidding? I have been a pro wrestler for almost a year now... he started a long time before I entered this business. It sure will be interesting to face him in the ring.
T. Wilson - Do you have any other comments? The A-Bomb - No not really. Just... LET'S TURN UP THE HEAT! [The camera fades slowly to black.]
