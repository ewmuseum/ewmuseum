Crazy Dog ---------------- (Crazy Dog is seen putting a tape into his vcr he then returns to sit on his sofa and the tape starts) [(The screen is dark, the low hum of the television clearly audible. The hum rises to a garbled wine, slowly fades and rises into a high pitched screech. As the wining of the speaker diminishes, the image reacts as thick vertical lines flicker across the darkness. The image focuses: a dark green, nothing clear, nothing distinguishable. Still, the steady drone of the television continues for another 6 or 8 seconds, until it is finally interrupted by an unearthly deep voice quietly singing)
"This little light of mine, I'm going to let it shine..." (The camera swings to the right and then jerks up and down until it is facing a concrete floor: a long crack streaking from the left side of the image to the upper right hand corner. A light bulb dangling from a chord is knocked, twisting shadows, turning the screen from black to green, and black to green. The singing has turned to humming, and the shuffle of feet is heard, but beyond this there are other sounds.
Unrecognizable sounds. Sounds that are too muffled, too quiet to distinguish. Jake reaches for the television with a trembling hand, and turns up the volume, trying desperately to figure out what's going on. Now the volume is loud, so loud in fact that the television stand is vibrating. The humming slows briefly and a few short grunts are made.
At the instant the last grunt sounds, someone, or something utters a tiny yelp. A bright, black laced, blue boot comes into view: suddenly vanishing as the camera starts to pan up and to the left, centering on the green wall. The humming begins again, and the sound of footsteps fill the room, echoing off the walls. Into view comes a ...yes, a man.
His eyes burn with fury. His cleanly shaven head glistens as his shadow dances on the wall behind him. Abruptly the humming stops).
Crazydog: Hey, there partners. Just wanted to say Hi to my fellow Dogmen, and to whoever else will kneel before us. For the rest of you
I just want you to know that I'm going to tear each and every one of you a new asshole. But, you, Nasty Ned, I've got something else in mind for
you...." (The Crazydog charges around behind the camera. The singing starts again "This little light of mine, shine on the Crazydog, I'm gunna let shine...". The camera swivels around and stops, and the auto-focus adjusts the picture. A splintered wood post extends up from a wheel filled with hardened concrete. Around it, lay a cardboard box, and various lengths of string. Centered on the post is an animal, a kitten to be precise. Hardly breathing the kitten struggles for life, or maybe hoping for death. It's fur is mottled with burns and patches of bare skin. It's one remaining ear singed at the top, hangs limp).
Crazydog: You see Nasty Ned, you don't have much to look forward to in life, not unlike this poor little Kitty-cat. I fear your, and this pussys fate, are one and the same....
(The Crazydog approaches the kitten, anger and hate filling his bright blue eyes). Crazy: Go to the light kitty-cat, go to the light. (Without another word the Crazydog guts the cat, ripping its insides free with his bare hands. Laughter reverberates off the walls and the
Crazydog is dancing. Prancing around the room he knocks the camera over and the screen goes blank).]
(Crazy Dog stops the tape.) Crazy: God I was one sick puppy back then. I can't imagine why the doctors would want me to go back and watch all these old tapes but if it means I can
get back in the ring then that is what I'll do. (Crazy Dog gets up and returns to the vcr to remove the tape from the vcr and replaces it on the shelf. He then glares into the camera.) Crazy Dog: CWF... FEE, FIE, FOE, FUM. WHATCHA GUNNA DO WHEN THE DOGMEN
COME? FEE, FIE, FOE, FUM. WHATCHA GUNNA DO WHEN THE DOGMEN COME? FEE,
FIE, FOE, FUM. WHATCHA GUNNA DO WHEN THE DOGMEN COME? (Crazy continues to chant as the scene fades to black.) A-Bomb ------------ [The camera is set up inside a cafeteria. The room's lights are dimmed a little, and slow music is played in the background. The furniture is made out of wood, and so are the walls. Beside every wooden table, are two wooden benches. The A-Bomb is sitting in a corner, seen as the upper left corner of your screen. He's wearing a pair of jeans, a black silk shirt, and his Ray Bans. A waitress approaches him. She's wearing the small working dress she is told to wear, and she doesn't recognize The A-Bomb immediately. She has a notebook and a pen in her hand.]
Waitress - Can I take your order sir? The A-Bomb - Sure bring me a sandwich and a cup of coffee please. I need that. [After The A-Bomb answer, she looks up, wondering why he said that. Some seconds later she remembers him.]
Waitress - Hey! You're The A-Bomb. One of the top wrestlers in the MCWF! The A-Bomb - Keep it down will you? I don't want the whole café rolling over me! Waitress - I can understand that! I saw you at the Battle Zone. The A-Bomb - Great. Are you a fan? Waitress - Well, not only your fan. I like wrestling in general. The A-Bomb - *looks disappointed* Well then, could you please bring me the food? [The waitress walks away, and the screen fades away. It fades back in to show the waitress coming back from the kitchen with the food. She puts the food on the table, and sits down.]
Waitress - Do you mind? The A-Bomb - No, of course not. Always good with a little chat with the fans. You know, the motto is. Everything for the fans.
Waitress - So, I must say I was impressed by your win at the Battle Zone. The A-Bomb - And why is that? Waitress - Well let me see. Goliath is much bigger than you, and he had two guys following him. You beat the odds.
The A-Bomb - I wouldn't say that. The two guys didn't interfere, and I had to take care of Goliath.
Waitress - And what a show afterwards. The Chaos Elite is reunited. The A-Bomb - I'm not sure about that. We'll have to wait and see. Morgan is back, better than ever, but this time we've got Silverthorn with us. He's currently injured, but he's still in the force.
Waitress - Yes you're right. But anyway. I think you guys are great! Especially that guy. what is his name again. Everest? Ulric Everest? The A-Bomb - *coughs* Please. Do not disrespect him. Waitress - Oh, I'm sorry. Sir Ulric Everest. Is that sir thing that important to him? The A-Bomb - I don't know, but when he wants to be called Sir Ulric Everest, I think we should call him Sir Ulric Everest. Waitress - You're absolutely right! Could you arrange a meeting with him? The A-Bomb - I guess I could. When are you finished here? I'll send him over! Waitress - Oh my God! Do you really mean that? Or are you just playing with my feelings? The A-Bomb - No kidding. Is 5 pm all right with you? Waitress - Of course. Just send him over, and I can take the whole day off. The A-Bomb - *smiles* Cute, but I really don't think that's necessary. Waitress - Of course I wouldn't do that. As I said, I'm just a fan of wrestling in general.
The A-Bomb - No favourite? Waitress - No comment. The A-Bomb - Well then. I guess I'll see you! [The A-Bomb is just about to leave, when Tom Wilson enters the café. He's wearing his light blue suit, and has the MCWF camera crew ready in the café.]
The A-Bomb - What a surprise! I should have understood it when the camera was set up in here.
Tom Wilson - *looks at the waitress* Would you please excuse us? [The waitress nods, and walks away, but looks back at The A-Bomb and says while leaving.] Waitress - Don't forget our little deal. The A-Bomb - Of course not my dear. Tom Wilson - I just wondered if you had any comments for your match against
Retch Rancid. The A-Bomb - Well, of course it will be hard. It will absolutely not be easy. He's had more titles than you can count. He's one of the greatest.
Tom Wilson - He sure is. A living legend. The A-Bomb - Yes, that's what you can call him. At this Warfare I'll once again have to try to prove myself one of the best. It sure will be one of the trickiest matches so far in my career.
Tom Wilson - By the way. I saw you at the Battle Zone. Great win! The A-Bomb - Please. You have it all on tape. Just review it when I'm gone. I talked about it with that beautiful waitress. Now, if you would excuse me. I have to go. [The camera fades slowly to black while The A-Bomb stands up and leaves the café.] The Viking ---------------- (The camera slowly fades in. And especially when you're meeting someone, as good as Tom

Rizzo --------- *Rizzo is seen in the comfort of his hotel room in Halifax. He's watching videotape of Brad Rice's latest spiel. He laughs every time he sees that tear flow from his eye. Rizzo shuts it off and shakes his head. Heidi answers a knock at the door. Jake Jones is standing there with a camera already on. Rizzo tells Heidi to let them in. JJ pulls a mic from his inside pocket and hence the interview begins.*
JJ: I heard through the door you were listening to Rice's latest speech. What do you think of it. Rizzo: *Makes a violin motion while whining like a violin* Ya know what, a loser only tries to get pity from others. That whole spiel was just so people would pity him for getting his ass kicked this Wednesday.
JJ: It was some pretty emotional stuff. Rizzo: That's all it was. It was 100% pure emotion. It had no substance. It was just to make me scared because of "all his rage" He's the BRINGER
OF DEATH OOOHH AAAHHH!! What a crock. That guy had a rough life? join the club and get a helmet. Life isn't fair. Life is a series of letdown.
DEAL WITH IT! Oh, look at me mourn....oh look at my wrists. Too bad the bleeding stopped. We'd have been spared the pain of listening to your whining. Hopefully Wednesday I'll help shut your wannabe morbid ass up.
I'll start by taking you belt away from you. Then I'll RimJob ya until you turn into a catatonic mess.
JJ: You don't seem to care about any of the pain he went through. Rizzo: That's because I don't. All he does is bitch and moan about losing the love of his life, his dad blah blah blah *makes the violin motion and whining again.* Here's some advice slatch. Go to a bar. Meet a hot chick. F**k her brains out and forget about that slut who died three years
ago. She's dead...time to move on! Your father cut your wrists? NO! Your weak will power and YOU were the cause of your wrists being slit...Ass. Next time, cut deeper and put 'em in water. You'll be doing the world a favor. JJ: That has got to be some of the most cold hearted stuff I've ever heard in my life. Rizzo: I'm sorry if I don't pity stupid people. If they want to let shit get to them, let them. I've had it bad. I shrug it off and move on. I'm
better because of it. Rice on the other hand just brood and broods and then tries to make people feel bad for him. It ain't working here bub. I'll feel better putting you out of everyone's misery. This Wednesday, it's time for you to suffer emotional and physical defeat.
JJ: He said that he feels bad for you. He said he's going to crucify you. *Rizzo bursts out laughing.* Heidi: I am not amused. Zat Rice seems like he's a scary man. Rizzo, be careful. A wacko like that can just snap and injure you badly.
Rizzo: Heidi, don't worry. How can the dead injure the living. And for this crucifixion crap? Wow, you're original. I think you better call the coroner ahead of time President Hall. Rice will be leaving in a ready-to-cook body back. Rice, if I see a cross near ringside, I'll snap it in half. I'll burn it, then I'll stab you through the heart with one of its jagged pieces.
JJ: Well, looks like you're ready for a fight tomorrow night. Rizzo: I always like to say don't sing it, bring it. Tomorrow night, the fat lady will be singing for Rice after he gets that oh so dreaded RimJob.
I will be the CWF Champ, and Uncle Ben's will be leaving either dead or with a severe limp. He'll also be leaving 10 lbs of gold lighter! Now JJ,
I need to laugh some more. You're welcome to stay and watch Rice's interview, but the camera must be off. Some things I may say might be too much for TV.
JJ: Ok boys, you heard the man. Folks...we'll see you tomorrow at Warfare! *Camera cuts to Uncle Ben's Puffed Rice commercial...how ironic!* Sir Ulric Everest ------------------------ (The camera views the receptionist area in Sir Everest Fitness Club. There are two dark blue sofas standing in a corner of the room as well as a low, wooden table. Ulric "The Wizard" Everest is lying, casually outstretched across one of the sofas, reading a newspaper. The camera pans around the room. The floor is covered with light, wooden tiles. There's and a counter with a man standing behind it. There's a doorway leading to the training facilities an exit door leading out in the streets. The camera finishes its
360-degree spin and once again views Ulric Everest. He glances over his newspaper and arches an eyebrow at the camera. Crystal McKay steps forward from behind it. She's wearing a black, fairly long skirt, a white blouse and red shoes.)
UE: Well, hello there... What happened to Dan Davidson? I thought he'd be the one coming by today.
(Dan Davidson enters the room, walks over to Crystal McKay and stands besides her.) Davidson: Hey, I made it. I'm always in a rush. So much to do, so little time... Everest throws a look of contempt at Dan Davidson.)
UE: Anyway... please have a seat. (Dan Davidson and Crystal McKay sit down in the other sofa.) Crystal: How's it going? UE: Fine, I suppose. About the gym, I'm not sure. Less people than I expected showed up for the opening. I'm still confident the masses will start coming, I've simply realised I'm going to have to give them a little time. As for my career, I've done some thinking. I came to a conclusion, too. *declares loudly* I, Ulric Everest, am better without a title belt.
Titles distract me. Davidson: I'm not sure I'm catching your drift... UE: When you've got a belt, people swarm around you, everyone trying to get their claws on it. Vultures... Most of them tend to be wannabe champions, but among the crowds, there are always individuals capable of brewing just the kind of conspiracy needed to dethrone me. I'm not going to deliberately lose eventual title-shots; I'm just going to make sure I do not receive any. For a while, that is.

Crystal: And the first man to take the test will be "Wild Child" Jimmy
Sexton, right? UE: Yup. Jimmy Sexton. To pass the exam, he'll have to beat me. Dan, do you have any info on him?

UE: I did. He didn't have very much to say to me, did he? Nothing that I haven't heard before, at least... He heralded his appearance in the CWF like most newcomers do. I'm not THAT impressed, but he seemed experienced enough. Crystal, you interviewed him. What's your impression?
Crystal: Useful information, you mean? UE: Yeah. Crystal: *giggles* Well, I think he's single... (Dan Davidson snickers. Ulric Everest smiles, but suddenly goes dead serious, grasping Crystal's joke.)
UE: ...hey! Crystal: Sorry, I couldn't help myself. Seriously. You'll have to wrestle him to be able to evaluate his ability.
UE: Very well. A mole hill out of a mountain, huh? I'll take that as a compliment. I am quite the barrier, you know. You can't climb over and there's no way around. I guess we'll just have to wait and see. Although you're a little pretentious, I can't see you harming anyone. You want to fly at the heels of the champions? You're obsessed with the thought of draping gold 'round your waist? Go ahead. By all means, make a run for it!
You've made it here, Sexton. You only have to pass this final test. I, on the other hand, will be content with beating you 1, 2, 3. I'm reclaiming my throne as the King of Outsiders.
Davidson: When were you the k... UE: I was a rookie once. In fact, it isn't that long ago. There were no expectations. There were no predictions. Right now, I'm taking one match at the time and later... well, I'm sure I'll encounter one or two familiar faces... *grins* And I'll be swift and they'll be unaware that I have returned. But as I said, for now, I'll be happy with picking up the win on
Wednesday. "Wild Child", when you mess with me, you mess with a slumbering giant. I've got backup, I've got connections and I've got powerful friends.
That pretty much sums it up, wouldn't you say? Crystal: Would you care to send message to the new members of the CWF? UE: Uh, welcome to the CWF, everybody. If you're watching there, Viking,
I'm sure you remember me. I hope you bring home that title of yours, I know you can do it. As for now, I'm sure we'll meet again...
Davidson: Thank you very much. Unless you've got something to add, we don't have any further questions.
UE: All you folks at home be sure to catch Warfare tomorrow. We'll put on a show. (Ulric Everest gives the thumbs up to the camera. The screen fades to black as Crystal McKay and Dan Davidson get up to leave.)
