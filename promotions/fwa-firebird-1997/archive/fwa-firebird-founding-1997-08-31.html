"(FWA) FIREBIRD WRESTLING ALLIENCE!"

31 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I am starting a fed! It's called the Firebird Wrestling Allience!
The freshest and newest fed in e-wrestling, today! I know many people want to bye for titles, and in the FWA, we have four titles,
FWA World Title
FWA Global Title
FWA Television Title
FWA Tag Team Title
Each will have an 8-man tournement that will be on ONE NIGHT in Milluwukee, WI at The Bradley Center, for Massacare in Milluwukee!
Every 3 or 4 days, you will get a report with rumors, roleplays, arena reports and future cards! And, on Friday Night, things will REALLY heat up with Friday Night Fire!
Before you enter you wrestler, there are just TWO main rules yu must follow:
ABSOLUTELY NO REAL WRESTLERS!!!!!!!!!
IF DON NOT ROLEPLAY FOR 2 WEEKS, AND DON'T TELL ME ABOUT IT BEFOREHAND, YOU WILL BE KICKED OUT OF THE FED! NO EXCUSES!!!!!!
So, what are you waiting for? Fill out an applacation for singles or tag team TODAY! (P.S.:PASS THIS ON!!!!!!!)
