Looking for an established fed, one in which there will be no closing any time soon? Looking for a chance to battle other great RP'ers, and have your Roleplay judged fairly? Looking for friendly staff, great write-ups and tips on improving your
RP's? Look no further.. Your saviour is here, and it has five large initials.. N Y S W F "New York State Wrestling Federation" We are a booked fed, no sims have ever been used and never will be used. Your match will be written fairly and to the best of our abilities. No unfair or special treatment will be given to anyone for any reason, so know that we will never treat you badly.
The NYSWF is the place where stars can shine, legends can be made, and new RP'ers can be made great.
