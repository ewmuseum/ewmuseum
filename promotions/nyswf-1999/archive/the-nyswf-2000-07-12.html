Hello. I'm [name withheld]. I run the NYSWF -the New York State Wrestling Federation-, which has been operating since August of 1998.
I'm posting here today to, perhaps, gain new handlers to join the ranks of the NYSWF. Let me rundown what we have to offer- - Four singles titles : World Title, TV Title, Lightweight Title, and Hardcore Title - Of course, Tag Team Titles - The NYSWF Academy of Wrestling : A training ground for new, undeveloped handlers to gain experience and knowledge before joining the ranks of the NYSWF.
- A friendly environment with great competition - You can expect cards to not be late (I've been late, MAYBE, four times since the start of the fed) ever, and if they are, the write-ups themselves make up for it.
- Involvement for everyone. Whether you are a main eventer, mid-carder, or a "curtain jerker" we always look to put everyone into a productive and fun angle.
What else is there? We're reliable, fun, friendly, fair, and overall we are just a great fed to be in and around.
