Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Pioneer Martialarts Wrestling (PMW) announced itself on 17 May 1997 as the same-day rename of an existing promotion, Atlantic Coast E-Wrestling — “PMW was formerly known as ACEW,” its president wrote, pointing new and old members alike to a homepage at a personal account address.

The post's only house rule was a standing one: flash and roleplay more than once a week, “because we don't like to play around,” regardless of whether a wrestler was face, heel, tweener, or part of a tag team.

No card, roster, or title of PMW's own survives beyond that renaming announcement. A second federation's own head, writing six weeks later, recorded that PMW's wrestlers were being offered elsewhere and that its president could no longer be reached — consistent with the promotion going quiet sometime after its own founding post, though no closing statement of its own survives.
