From Chapel Hill, North Carolina ~ The Dean Smith Center 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night April 7th, 2004 Backstage ~ Exodus Featuring Tyler Davidson Legends opened with a shot of Tyler Davidson seated quietly behind a giant oak wood desk in his full business attire looking nothing at all like the wrestler he was more commonly as lately. “This won’t take long at all but there are some big things that need to be revealed here tonight,” Tyler began. “Now as most of you know eVolution is owned by my corporation Davidson Enterprises. Now that corporation like any other is primarily run through a Board of Directors who votes to make all executive decisions. It was that board who last week voted to drastically cut the funding for eVolution despite mine and others best efforts. With that cutting of funding we obviously had to cut quite a few talents. Those talents are as follows. James Kimson, Sovereign, Thug roC, Lance Jackson, Tyler Diggs, The Olympic Union, The Ace of Spades, The Dark Dragons, Ethan M. Coles, Shock and Awe, Memphis Cage, Machine, Psymon, Ricky Thunder, Scott Hunta, Shaft-Man, The HeartBreakers, Joshua Northstar, Troy Chryst, and WeedSmoker. Obviously that is a great deal of talents that have been lost and some of them were in the midst of major storylines. That will result in a great many changes hopefully for the better as on Monday night there will be yet another vote by the Board of Directors that will determine the further future of eVolution or the lack thereof. In addition Malcolm King and The Brotherhood of X are not here tonight as a result of a vicious assault upon King at a house show this past weekend by Asis that we will show to you later tonight.” With that said the shot faded to black for Legends to continue on in some form. Elsewhere ~ Hard To Stomach Featuring Damon O’Halloran "Mister O'Halloran?” For the past five minutes, Dr. Smith had tried to get Damon O'Halloran to answer her questions. In fact, he had yet to say a single word since he set foot into her office. He just sat in his chair, shifting uncomfortably. “Are you alright? You look like you’re in a bit of discomfort.” O'Halloran finally spoke, answering her question with a question of his own. “Ever been thrown off the roof of a three story building?” She brushed the end of her pen through her hair, dismissing the question. “No, I can’t say that I have.” “Well it felt like I did. I’ve got the bone chips and cracks, deep muscle bruising, and a couple of bad sprains to prove it. On top of that, I’ve got a knee that’s one detached ligament away from needing reconstructive surgery.” He leaned back in his chair. “Yes, I guess you could say I’m in a little bit of discomfort. Every waking moment is filled with pain. So are we done here?” “This would go much quicker if you’d just answer my questions.” Damon looked over and watched Dr. Smith flip through a rather thick folder. “How have you been coping with the loss of -” “My home?” Dr. Smith set her clipboard down in her lap. “Oh.” Damon pulled out his sunglasses and slid them onto his face. “Please remove your sunglasses.” “Please come here and make me,” he muttered under his breath. The sunglasses remained in place. Dr. Smith leaned forward. “Let go. You’re not a wrestler here. You’re just a human being, no different than anyone else. Let’s break down the walls you’ve built up around your feelings.” Damon looked up at the ceiling. “Let’s not. I like walls.” Dr. Smith put pen to paper and decided not to press the issue. “Fine. Let’s talk about your...” O'Halloran had tuned out the doctor as he stared upward. Why does eVo insist on putting me through this garbage? he asked himself. I’m above all of this. I mean, I’m the eVo United Champion. No you’re not. Not anymore. Not after this week, remember? I’m Damon O'Halloran. Nobody’s stronger than me. Nobody. I held that belt like nobody else ever had. But you won’t have that belt after Sunday, will you? And what did it cost you? Your knee, your health, and your own life. How does that make you feel? Shut up. You sound like Doctor Tits here. The company's dead. You put your pride before it and died for it. You killed yourself for this place and look where it got you. Bull. I never thought it’d be in any real danger. Tyler Davidson killed it. It didn’t have to die. You could have dropped the belt and won it back later. You're the cause it's all crumbling. Nobody wanted to work with you. You alienated yourself from everyone. Is that what you're thinking? I’m going to do something with myself. You havent even heard the last of Damon O'Halloran, I dont give a shit of any of those guys in the back. That’s not even an issue. So what is the issue? You’re miserable. The pain won’t go away, and you refuse to stop to let yourself heal. Don’t even start on that Act of God crap either. Just say it – you want to die. Shut up. If I stop now, it’ll pass me by and I’ll lose my shot at another go at things. Right. You just want someone to put you out of your misery. Shut up. Hey, I don’t blame you. If I killed the only good thing in my life, I’d want to die as well. I couldn’t live with myself. I can live with a lot. Maybe that’s why you don’t want to cure the pain. It’s your way of punishing yourself. Brilliant, Dr. Freud. Bite me. Truth hurts. Dr. Smith didn’t rise from her seat as Damon O'Halloran walked out of her office. As the door closed behind him, she looked at her evaluation form and started to write. “That poor man,” she said softly. Thirty minutes wasn’t nearly enough time to work on his problems. But as long as he wasn’t a danger to himself or others it couldn’t hurt to give him a pass on the evaluation... could it." Ringside ~ The Answer Featuring Asis and Malcolm King It was time now. He could finally get him alone (as alone as you could with 20,000 fans watching and another god knows how many on national television) and the night could begin. His night could begin. He forgot about other people sometimes, forgot that their night may have already started. He’d been there since the beginning although he had been a little quiet. Now it was time to make his announcement with a deafening roar. He was still in the ring, only now he was by himself, he had a microphone in his hand but he couldn’t hear what he was saying. It was probably some propaganda drivel, but it was nothing but inaudible to him. Even he could have heard it, he wouldn’t have paid attention. It was amazing he hadn’t turned and noticed him yet, he was only ten yards from the ring. Then again, when you’re than arrogant, ignorance comes in the package and that was going to be his downfall. Asis slid into the ring behind King and despite the noise the fans were making to try and make King notice, he didn’t. With a hard straight fist Asis punched King right in the back of the neck, dropping him to his knees. The impact down King’s spine must’ve been immense, as he could do nothing but stay on his knees grasping his neck in pain as Asis walked round in front of him. He measured him up, holding his head with one hand, before reaching back and again smoking King with a right hand, this time straight to the face. King fell backwards on the mat, dropping his microphone on the canvas as he did so. He was enjoying himself so far and by the sound of the crowd they weren’t. So all in all, things had started well. He looked down at King, grinning as he did so, before then kneeling over him and repeatedly unleashing punch after punch, each time rattling King’s head backwards, picking it up and knocking it down again each time. By the time he’d finished and come back to his senses, all he could see in front of him was crimson and all he could hear around him was boos. He looked up in a fit of rage, staring at the crowd and then looking down at the bloody King lying half-unconscious on the mat in front of him. He picked up the microphone that lay next to King’s still body and crouched down next to him. “So King. Do you have the answers? What meaningless propaganda did you feed these gullible fans this time? Well, it doesn’t matter does it, I showed them what your mouth is worth. But this is not a direct act of retribution against anything you’ve said against me, nope not at all. I did this, purely because I can King. You can take my title away from me, melt it down, try and discredit it, but what you forgot King, is that I don’t give a shit in the first place. You wouldn’t have done it if you hadn’t somehow felt threatened by it anyway. I did this to show to you that you can’t stop me with cheap tricks and “political speeches”. You can’t win this with a war of words. The only way you’re going to stop me, is by getting me in this ring and making me bleed more than you. And right now King, it doesn’t look like you can do that.” King tries to move and reach up at Asis, only to have Asis stand up and backwards, before viciously kicking King in the face leaving him unconscious, blood steadily flowing out of his head. He stood up smiling, thoroughly pleased with himself. Also he had later on to look forward to, whatever that was about… Backstage ~ Pick The Time Featuring Xander and Daniel Somers The customary bottle of Jack Daniels sat on the desk of Daniel Somers who of course had a shot glass ready for the liquor to be poured into as he watched the proceedings of Legends this week. So far things had not been going all that great on the night and they no doubt would be getting much worse as the night went by. Cue the knock on the door. Somers locked up at the door with an annoyed and yet concerned expression as it began to open and in walked Xander, a man that Somers had plenty of bad and good history with over the years. Tonight Somers clearly wasn’t sure in the least as to what kind of history would be played out here tonight. Xander approached the desk with a less than happy expression on his face as he looked down at Somers who returned the stare and expression. “Can I help you with something Xander?” Somers asked breaking the silence. ”Yeah I’m sure you can actually,” Xander told him. “I’d love to know why people are getting Universal Title shots before me when I’m the damn number one contender!” “If you’re referring to Tyler I had nothing to do with it,” Somers told him. “You can thank Thug roC-a-Cock for that.” “You might not have had anything to do with but you CAN do something about it,” Xander replied. ”Like what?” Somers asked. “Cancel the match? Can’t do that now.” “Then give me my match,” Xander responded stepping closer to the desk. Somers made a mental note of Xander with one hand on the table already and remembering all to well one of the past incidents between the two men. It was with that memory in mind that a thought came into the head of Somers’. ”Tell you what I can do Xander,” Somers began as he stood up from the desk. “You are number one contender so you by all rights should get a shot against Asis before anyone else.” ”Damn right I should,” Xander agreed. “That’s why I’m here.” “So all you have to do from this point on Xander is pick the time and the place and you got the match of your choosing with Xander,” Somers told him. ”Just like that?” Xander asked not quite sure of the reality of the deal. ”Just like that,” Somers told him. “May I make one suggestion however?” “Go ahead,” Xander told him. ”Wait for the opportune time,” Somers replied. “Wait until you have the advantage.” Xander thought about what Somers could have meant by that statement when it seemed a light bulb lit over his head as it hit him and a smile crossed his face. “Pleasure doing business with you again Somers,” Xander finished before he turned and walked out of the office. Somers watched him leave and then breathed a sigh of relief as he sat back down and poured himself a shot of JD and downed it quickly. Somers got up from his seat and left the office himself now as the scene faded to black. Backstage ~ Unbreakable Featuring Malcolm King and Daniel Somers The bottle of Jack still sat on the desk of Daniel Somers but Somers was not behind the desk. Instead the seat was occupied by one Malcolm King instead as he quickly helped himself to a glass and sat back relaxing in the leather chair. The door then swung open and Daniel Somers walked into his office only to stop short upon seeing King in his chair. ”Damn it now what?!” Somers shouted. “Nice to see you too Dan,” King replied with a laugh. ”What do you want King?” Somers repeated. “We never get to spend any more time talking like we used to Dan so I thought I’d pay you a visit tonight since it seems to be the popular thing to do,” King answered. ”Ok you visited now get the hell out,” Somers demanded. ”We need to talk Dan,” King continued as he paid no attention to the demands. “Take a seat.” ”As soon as you get out of my damn chair I will,” Somers told him. ”Fine stand then,” King replied. “I saw the little conversation you had with Xander and I do have a bit of a problem with that.” ”What a shock,” Somers responded sarcastically. “What does this have to do with me?” “I have a problem with not getting a title shot myself,” King told him. “You know as well as I do that there are issues between Asis and I and we need to have those settled so I have a proposal for you.” “Sorry King you’re not my type,” Somers replied quickly. “Now feel free to leave.” “Not that kind of proposal Dan and rest assured you’re not my type either,” King replied not at al amused. “I have a proposal concerning Unbreakable and the lack of a main event at the current time.” ”Really?” Somers asked slightly curious now. “Let’s hear it then.” “I want Asis at Unbreakable,” King told him. “Now obviously that would require him holding onto the Universal Title past tonight. If that doesn’t happen we’d have to work out a separate plan but if Asis defeats Tyler Davidson here tonight I want a title shot at Unbreakable.” ”If Asis beats Tyler tonight you got it,” Somers agreed. “Just don’t get your hopes up King.” “Yes I have no doubt you have a plan in mind especially if you agreed so easily,” King told him. “Just be sure that you don’t underestimate what Asis is indeed capable of here tonight.” With that King finished off his own glass of JD and got up out of Somers’ chair before he left the office and Somers alone. Backstage ~ Manager For A Night Featuring Tyler Davidson and Daniel Somers Tyler had just finished taping up his fists for the match that he would be put through next against the extremely violent Universal Champion all thanks to Thug roC. Once the taping was finished Tyler continued his walk down the hallways before being cut off by Daniel Somers. ”Wait right there,” Somers told him. “There is……..” ”No time,” Tyler cut him off. “In case you missed it that no good bitch in his last act as President booked me against Asis tonight in a no holds barred match. I have to go take care of that right now.” “I know,” Somers told him. “That’s why I made some last minute changes to the match.” ”What do you mean?” Tyler asked. ”Well one change really,” Somers told him as he pulled something out of his pocket. “I still have this little Manager License thing in my wallet here so I made myself your manager for the night.” “So you’re going to be in my corner against Asis?” Tyler laughed. “What good do you think that is going to do?” ”Probably not a lot but that isn’t important right now,” Somers answered. “I still have some tricks up my sleeve for Asis tonight though. Now let’s get out there.” With that said Somers led Tyler down the rest of the hallway as the show went to a commercial break. The two men stood at the top of the ramp and looked around at the crowd who continued to jeer both men. Tyler looked on with an arrogant look on his face before he slowly began to make his way down the ramp ignoring the calls and insults thrown his way by the fans. Tyler got to the bottom of the ramp and he quickly slid under the bottom rope as Somers climbed up the steps. Tyler walked to the corner nearest to him and hopped right up onto the middle rope with both his arms raised above his head to more jeers from the crowd. Tyler shook his head at the reaction he was receiving from the fans before he hopped down off the ropes and got himself ready for the coming match. A lone guitar plays quietly over a dark arena, the fans know who is coming out and the flashing pictures of Asis’ face on the eVotron, red eyes glowing beneath his hair, confirms it for them. The guitar continues to build up as the bass joins it and the lights begin to flicker as if someone is turning the power on and off and as the first heavy riff an explosion erupts on stage and the lights burn a bloody red. Drop addicts in the mixture, falcons have blown the fixture. From Autumn To Ashes’ “Alive Out Of Habit” blasts through the arena as Asis walks out onto the stage and starts to slowly make his way down to the ring, dragging the Universal Title along the floor by it's strap. He cracks his neck from side to side as he stares intently at the ring, ignoring the taunts of all those around him. Think of me as days pass us by. Shards of glass. Skin of gold. Steal my breath. Blood runs cold. Violet waves. Oceans blue. All my love. Lost in you. In you I see further. Can it last forever? Asis gets down to the ring and stands at the apron briefly, his head down, face masked by his hair. He then rolls into the ring and paces around as the lights return to normal, before stopping and sarcastically holding the Universal Title in the air and then throwing it down on the mat. Tyler made the first move as he charged right at Asis and apparently caught him by surprise as he took Asis down to the mat with a double leg takedown and quickly climbed on top of him before he began to wail away at him with rights and lefts to the head. The crowd loved it as they watched Tyler pound Asis with right hands while Asis tried his best to cover up from the blows until Tyler relented and got back to his feet. He grabbed hold of Asis immediately though and picked him right back up with him before he let have feel a quick succession of knife edge chops right across the chest as Tyler backed Asis up against the ropes. Tyler grabbed hold of Asis’ arm and went for an irish whip but Asis was quick to reverse the move and sent Tyler into the ropes before he came charging back and Asis took him quickly over with an arm drag takedown and he immediately locked Tyler’s arm in an armbar submission. Tyler immediately tried to get back on his feet and alleviate some of the pressure that Asis had on his arm but Asis managed to use his own leverage and forced Tyler right back down onto the mat again but the move had positioned them close enough that Tyler was able to get both of his legs locked around the bottom rope after he hit the mat and forced the break of the hold. Asis however knew full well that he couldn’t be disqualified and was in no real hurry to release the hold as he continued to twist the arm of Tyler and apply the pressure. With a bit of help from his manager, Daniel Somers, however, Tyler managed to get free of the hold as Somers slipped into the ring in plain sight of Asis as he knew that Asis would relax if not release the hold on Tyler upon seeing him enter the ring and that was exactly what Asis did. His grip loosened on the arm of Tyler who felt it before he again got back onto his feet still somewhat locked into the armbar but with the ropes to assist him this time Asis couldn’t force him back down again and Tyler caught him with a right hand straight to the face to break the hold and Somers again slipped back out of the ring having already paid a dividends for Tyler in this match. Asis was still down on the mat from the hard right hand by Tyler and Tyler followed it quickly up as he sprung onto the middle rope and spun in mid air before he brought a leg right down across the throat of Asis and made the cover. Asis with a handful of Tyler’s long hair dragged him out under the bottom rope and left him lying on the mat face up before he drove an elbow right down across Tyler’s throat to continue his breathing issues. Asis walked back to Tyler with the chair raised above his head before he brought it right down across the back of Tyler. WHACK! WHACK! WHACK! Asis was about to go for the cover but Somers quickly came up behind him and jumped Asis from behind with a forearm shot but Asis turned quickly and ducked the attempt at a clothesline by Somers before he returned the favor with a huge gore that drove Somers into the ground before he turned back to Tyler and made the cover. One……………………… Two……………………………. Three! And finally the match was over! Asis had to go through hell fighting off two men but he had successfully defended his title here tonight against Tyler Davidson! Ringside ~ Any Time, Any Place Featuring Asis and Xander Asis couldn’t even begin to celebrate the hard fought victory he had just achieved as “Silver and Cold” by AFI began to blast over the sound system. A cheer went up through the crowd as Xander quickly emerged with a microphone in his hand and stared down at Asis who returned the stare with his Universal Title in hand. “Well Asis it looks like you’ve had quite a successful night tonight,” Xander began with a weird grin on his face. “I do however regret to inform you that your night isn’t quite finished yet.” Asis looked at Xander confused as to what he meant and began to walk slowly towards the ramp as Xander hurriedly finished what he was saying. ”You might want to stay there for a minute or two longer Asis,” Xander told him. “You see that guy lying on the ground that you gored? Yeah Daniel Somers, one of the three bosses around here now. You might not have heard yet but he told me I could have my title shot against you at any time or any place of my choosing. I choose right here, right now, tonight! No holds barred!” The fans popped at the announcement whether it was official or not and Xander tossed his microphone down and headed down the ramp towards Asis. Universal Title ~ No Holds Barred Match Xander vs. Asis© Asis turned his attention back to Somers briefly who was still hurt on the ground and gave him one last kick to the midsection for giving Xander this opportunity before he tossed the Universal Title back into the ring and went to meet Xander head on. The two men met on the ramp and immediately Asis began to fire right hands to the head of Xander but Xander was able to fight back and with Asis already worn from a previous match, took control of the situation and battered Asis back with his own series of right hands. Xander grabbed Asis quickly when he had control and lifted him right into the air with a suplex before he dropped him right across the steel rampway. Xander got quickly back to his feet and drove a boot right down into the face of Asis before he reached down and began to drag Asis further up the ramp. Asis got back to his feet during the process and caught Xander with a right hand to the midsection that stopped the movement completely as Asis stood back up and lifted Xander right into the air and slammed him right down on the top of the stage. Xander reached a hand back and grabbed at the swell of his back in pain before he tried to push his way up only to be met with a stiff kick right to the face by Asis. Asis grabbed Xander by the hair and lifted him quickly up to his feet before he slammed him right into the eVotron steel supports right next to the eVolution logo screen. Asis slammed his forearm right into the throat of Xander and began to choke him with it as he held him back against the supports while with his free hand pounding Xander in the side of the head. Xander still had his legs free however and in a last ditch effort he brought a knee right into the groin of Asis and stopped the attack momentarily at least while Asis fell backwards in pain. Xander slowly came up behind Asis and tried to lock him into a cobra clutch but Asis reacted quickly and slammed the back of his own head right into the face of Xander and Xander stumbled right back into the logo screen as Asis turned and saw him. Xander was dazed in front of the logo screen and Asis took the chance to try and put the match away as he charged Xander with a massive gore like he hit Somers with earlier. CRASH! And missed the gore. Xander sidestepped it at the last second and Asis gored himself right through the eVolution logo screen and remained hanging over the supports of the screens unmoving as Xander got back to his feet. Xander shoved Asis from behind and saw little resistance from the champion before he turned him around and quickly lifted him onto his shoulders before nailing him with Chaos Reigns and made the cover while the referee looked unsure of whether this was even an official match before he slowly got down and made the count. One………………. Two………………….. Three! And the match was over! Xander was apparently the new Universal Champion once again after Asis knocked himself out with the missed gore.
