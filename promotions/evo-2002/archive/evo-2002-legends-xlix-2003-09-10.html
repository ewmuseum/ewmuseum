Legends XLIX Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From Walsh Park ~ Waterford, Ireland 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night September 10th, 2003 In The Ring ~ A Championship Curve-Ball Featuring James Kimson and ???? The Pyro stops as the camera spins around the arena. Legends, the 49th installment of the show has just started. The crowd is pumped as this would be the 1st show since X-rated, one hell of a Pay Per View and the fans are excited for what is about to happen here tonight. Libby Bowman stands in the middle of a red carpeted ring. “Ladies and Gentlemen. It gives me great pleasure to announce your. NEW UNIVERSAL CHAMPION!” It’s Time... Let the Hype Begin “What a way to kick off Legends!” screamed Fritz over the insanity of cheers. Stupify blasted over the speakers cutting off Bowman and the fans remain on their feet, deafening cheers as it appears as if the New Universal Champion is about to make his way out here. The Ring mat is covered in a red carpet, in the middle of the ring is a glass case with the Title inside of it, dressed up with the new James Kimson name plate. Kimson appears through the curtains wearing blue jeans a Kimson shirt under a Leather jacket and pauses at the top of the ramp. His forehead is bandaged up from his war with Xander and Asis a week ago, fans call it one of the most impressive matches of all time. So close down to the very last second. Kimson walks down the ramp towards the ring and takes the ring steps to the apron. He steps between the ropes 1 leg at a time and then walks over to the nearest turnbuckle and mounts it, thrusting his arms into the air in celebration. The Camera flashes pop all over that side of the arena as the fans get a shot of the new Champion about to receive his title. Kimson dismounts and walks past the title on its casing and repeats the mounting at the next turnbuckle. After his entrance is complete he walks over to the ring ropes and bends down to grab a mic that was left there. The fans refuse to relent their cheers as Kimson looks up to the fans and smiles a big white smile. He’s filled with happiness right now as the fans start a “Kimson! Kimson!’ chant that echoes from one end to the other. Kimson paces around the ring, soaking up the moment as he stars at the capacity crowd giving him an ovation that people in this business dream of getting at least once in their career. Kimson holds up a hand to silence the crowd, they follow suit, not willingly but they quite down. Kimson brings the microphone to his lips. “I want to start off tonight by saying how special it is that I get to come out here and have the Universal Title given to me here tonight by someone very special” said Kimson. “Indeed fans tonight James Kimson will be presented the title by his youngest Son Logan as was the request of the family.” said Fritz. “It makes me sick, seeing some little baby kid come out here, he doesn’t even weigh as much as the title” said Tyler. “That’s not important Jack, it’s the sentiment, it shows how much this means to Kimson and symbolizes a new era for our company.” said Fritz. “Some people will call it sappy and that’s fine. But in the end I’m out here tonight because we have something to celebrate. Last week at X-rated for 37 minutes in the greatest 3 way match probably ever I won this title fair and square when I pinned Xander 1..2...3!” said Kimson. The fans burst into cheers. “It was probably the hardest, most challenging match of my career. Every time one of us had the match won something happened to change the momentum. Every time you could feel your last breath something happened that let you move on. I felt like a million bucks after that match on a mental and emotional level but right now, to be honest as I stand here I feel like crap physically. And Asis and Xander deserve to be credited for that match because they put everything on the line in this ring a week ago. And they just simply couldn’t stand up against The Hype!” said Kimson to a swell of cheers from the fans. “I am going to try my best to give you a Champion that you can be proud of. A Fighting Champion that won’t back down. That will be at each and every eVo event. Because you supported me, because you believe in me that means that I’m going to be the best champion that I can be here in Evolution. So with that I’d like to bring out my little buddy.” Stupify hits and out from the back walks out Victory Kimson, the wife of the New Universal Champion with their youngest child in her arms. Logan is around 3 years old perhaps and the two walk down to ring side and walk up the steps. Victory hands over the child to Kimson who holds him up over the top rope and gives him a kiss on the cheek. Little Logan is embarrassed and nervous about the entire situation as Victory steps between the ropes and embraces her husband. They walk over to the Title as the music dies off and Kimson holds his child to the glass as he opens it up slowly and carefully. The Belt is to heavy for the kid to hold but his Mom helps him take the belt and place it over the shoulder of Kimson and the fans start to cheer and applaud as Kimson circles around the ring with child and title one on each shoulder, a smile gleams across his face as he looks like a champion, a triumphant champion then interruption, a shocking one. Shoots and Ladders by Korn hits the speakers. The entire arena is totally shocked as the music plays for a few seconds before anything happens. “What the hell?!” said Tyler. “That’s.... that’s Sovereign’s music!!” said Fritz. “What? He’s here, oh my God Sovereign is here tonight?” said Tyler shocked. “He warned us at eVo1 that he was not gone forever, could he come back tonight to challenge the man that put him out of action for the last, what 5 or 6 months?!” said Fritz. Kimson has put little Logan back into his Mothers arms and is telling them both to get out of ringside incase things get violent as Kimson no doubt suspects what everyone else is suspecting. Then, as the music continues out from the back doesn’t walk Sovereign, but Leya Forth, the wife of Sovereign and estranged sister of James Kimson. Kimson looks up at her shocked, his jaw is open dumbfounded and the fans in the arena have no idea what to think about it. Leya’s beautiful figure is clad in a business suit outfit as he holds in her hand a pad with paper and makes her way to the ring. “What in the name of God is she doing here?” said Fritz. “That’s a good question, I for once have no idea what to say!” said Tyler. “Well I guess that means this isn’t all bad news then” mocked Fritz. Leya now is in the ring and Kimson just looks at her confused. She smiles and walks over to him and holds a hand out asking for his Microphone. Kimson thinks about it for a moment and decides that he does what to hear what she has to say and hands it to her. The fans are in a mix of boos and cheers, not really sure what to think and she brings the mic to her lips as the music is all gone. “First off James I know that you are probably very surprised. Hell I can see it in your face, and I know that you are seldom shocked by everything right? You’ve seen and done it all in this business and tonight you just were crowned the Champion. As my Brother James I wanted you to have that moment. I wanted you to have that belt draped over those big shoulders because that’s just 30lbs of gold and Leather and it’s going to be nothing compared to the weight of the world!” said Leya. “Weight of the world?” said Fritz confused. “See James, I didn’t fly all the way here from home in Chicago to congratulate you. Although I must admit a certain family pride. You’ve always made us all very happy with your success James and I want to let you know that I was speaking with your other sisters last night and they are all very proud of you. I mean, Wow Universal Champion of Evolution. That’s an amazing accomplishment, not to many else have been able to say that James. But I can think of one person. One person who was the 1st champion of Evolution, sitting at home injured, crying at night trying to hold back pure hatred for you! That’s Sovereign, my Husband, and your Brother in Law!” said Leya. Boos echo around the arena. The Fans remember Sovereign’s reign as champion lasting almost 4 months when no one was safe, so many careers ended. “But I’ll break it to you James and to the fans that no, Sovereign is not here tonight. What James did to him at Unbreakable was very thorough, and he won’t be seen on this program for a while to come. But James, you need to understand that I don’t hold a full grudge against you because I know that Will had a far worse future in store for you then just a torn rotator cuff and a 12 inch gash along the arm. He wanted to end your career and everything that you knew was good and right. Sovereign will return James and when he does he’s coming for you and you alone. But enough unhappy news, because I’m not here to give you rumors of when he’ll come back because you are in for a special treat.” said Leya. “Special Treat?” said Tyler. “At X-rated Tyler Davidson appointed 3 individuals to run the day to day operation of Evolution Wrestling. He picked 3 people of different backgrounds as long as they knew the company and it’s roster. When I got the phone call and the offer from Tyler I was admittedly shocked. I mean, he had to know that our past James would cause some friction. Unless he heard from somewhere he’d made up and all was well. But it’s not all well. I haven’t spoken to you since March 30 the day you put my Husband in a hospital. You know what happened to him in that hospital? What happened was what happens every time. He was put in a straight jacket and locked away for 2 months and I didn’t even get to see him once! Do you know what that does to a man? To a woman? So Yes James, I am part of that committee that runs eVo. I am the woman that hired your greatest nemesis to eVo in Matt Hunt. And I’m the one that is going to make your life a living hell until someone comes that can do a better job, and the only man on the face of this earth that can do that is Sovereign!” “My God, Leya Forth is one of the 3 committee members? This isn’t right!” said Fritz. “Why isn’t it? She knows our Universal Champion better then anyone on the planet, and she’s already signed a huge star in Matt Hunt!” said Tyler. “She’s going to screw Kimson out of that title the first chance she gets!” said Fritz. “Now James, I know that you are probably really sore from that match with Asis and Xander at X-rated. And to show some compassion I’m giving you the night off from the ring. You can stick around if you want to, hell you’re the Champion right, that means you’ve got leeway in what you want to do around these parts. You’ve got some stroke I guess. But James, tonight is your last night off. I hope you expect to wrestle every single week and be that fighting champion that you said you wanted to be. Because next week at Legends 50 you will be defending that Universal title of yours!” said Leya. “What?!” said Fritz. “He just won it and now he’ll have to defend it, that’s great!” said Tyler. “That’s right James, and you’ll be defending it against your best friend and already 2 time Universal Champion, Scott Frost!” said Leya. “Oh my God!” said Fritz. “Wow what a match that’ll be!” said Tyler. Leya smiles an evil grin as she drops the mic and laughs and exits the ring, Kimson is still in awe of what happened and so are the fans as Leya walks up the ramp looking very proud of herself. Hunt’s eyes remain closed as he continues to speak… “This is fantastic, the best one I’ve had in years” he says in a soft tone. “You really should be charging folk for this, you’d rake in loads of cash…” Our minds start to work as the silence continues for just a few moments before Hunt, eyes still closed, speaks again “So…” he begins, adding a little pause while enjoying whatever it is. “Did you happen to see X-Rated last week?” he asks, presumably in the direction of who the blonde hair belongs to. “Uh-uh” comes the reply, an agreeable female tone. “Yeah, I bet everybody was watching when my debut match was on, eh?” he wonders while his right hand itches his chin before returning back to behind his head. “As great as feeling it was giving the slap to that Evans fella, nothing compares to this!” he states as a big grin appears on his face. His clean-shaven leg, reflects the light from the skylight in the room like the moon bounces off a bald man. “Yeah, you might as well…” Hunt replies, answering the female’s earlier question. The woman has an ordinary standard shaver in one hand and a towel in the other. A large can of shaving foam sits on the floor next to her, as does a large bowl of what seems to be warm water. “You make shaving legs an absolute pleasure, Sharon” Hunt says, commenting on (who we know now to be) Sharon’s handiwork on his left leg. Oh no” he says, getting all defensive. “Not that I have anything against back door merchants…well I would if one of them tried to chat me up….but generally I have no problem…that’s not say that I am a back door merchant…but just to say…” Sharon interrupts Hunt with a giggle “It’s fine Matt. I totally understand…” That seemingly calmed Hunt down as he leans back and watches Sharon soak his leg with dabs of warm water, from the groin down to the ankle. “So anyway, purposely changing subject…are you going to watch my match later?” he asks, with a thoughtful expression. “Maybe…” Sharon responds while glancing up at Hunt with a smile.
