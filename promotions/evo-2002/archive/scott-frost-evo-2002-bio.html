Scott Frost Biography Real Name Scott Michael Frost Nicknames Frost Hometown Detroit, Michigan Birthday March 31, 1980 Height 6'1 Weight 235 Interests Writing, music, and wrestling Favorite Pro Wrestlers Randy Orton, HBK, Chris Jericho, Chris Benoit, Eddie Geurerro Fed Titles High Impact (1), Universal Champion (2) Fed Awards Best Face of the Year 2002, First ever Multiple time Universal champion Biography In eVolution Before his injury: Scott debutes at Digital Destruction in 2002. Scott Becomes runner up in the Road To Glory Tournament of 2002 January of 2003, Scott becomes number one contender for the Universal Championship to face Shocka. February of 2003, Scott wins Universal Championship March of 2003, Scott loses Universal Championship to friend Jason Jameson End of March 2003, Scott defeats Jameson to become Universal champion once again April of 2003, Scott retains championship against James Kimson and Erin Christoph May of 2003, Scott loses championship to Xander June of 2003, Scott took a hiatus from the ring to concentrate on his personal life with his new wife Seara July of 2003, Scott is unmasked and revealed as masked wrestler Greatness August of 2003, Scott and wife Seara defeated The Heartbreakers and The Bodysnatchers in tag action September of 2003, Scott faces Jay Jay Jameson October of 2003, Scott is injured in a triple threat match against Jay Jay Jameson and Johnny Charisma. Scott leaves eVolution to heal. November of 2003, Scott finds out that wife Seara aka Katherine Frost dies in car wreck. --------------------------------------------- Year of 2004 June of 2004, Scott returns and attacks Erin Christoph and Tyler Davidson and blames Davidson for his wifes death. --------- Entrance: 'So you want to be a champion!' He then begins to walk down the entrance ramp, and he stares at everyone as he walks to the ring apron.
