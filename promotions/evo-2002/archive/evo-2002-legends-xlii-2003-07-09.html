Legends XLII Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The First Union Center ~ Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night July 9th, 2003 It has been quite a few crazy weeks for eVolution with A New Dawn being almost two weeks ago and the big one year anniversary show that followed it up in place of Legends last week. This week however things will be returning to normal in eVolution, well as normal as it ever gets anyway. Tonight will see a whole lot more action with a big tag team handicap main event as Shocka, James Kimson, and Seara will find themselves facing off against Greatness and Xander courtesy of Tyler Davidson with one last gift for Xander following their New Dawn match. Also Trevor Norman will defend his Cell Championship for the first time as he goes against the man who claims to still be champion in Jinx. All this and a whole lot more action can be expected for Legends this week live from Philadelphia. Last week Tyler Davidson threw a bit of a monkey wrench into the plans of most of eVolution’s tag teams as he took three existing teams and split them up into three new teams for this match. Even the rules themselves have been mixed up for this match as it will be a Captain Falls Finisher match where the only way to win is by using a tag team finishing maneuver before the captain of the team is able to make the cover. The three team captains are Jason Relms who teams with his mystery partner, Luther Wisdom who will team with The British Bomber, and Molotov who will team with Perry Quinn. This match can best be classified as a Cell vs. Cell Title match as the first and until New Dawn only Cell Champion Jinx made his surprising return to eVolution last week during the coronation of Trevor Norman as the new champion and demanded he be given a match with Trevor Norman as it was still his title to lose. Trevor of course was more than willing to get this match started at any time but it was Cameron Griffin and Joshua Krowe who made them wait for this week. Now the only two men to have ever held the eVolution Cell Championship will go one on one with only one man being able to walk out and be named the true Cell Champion. The Syndicate made their huge return last week with a victory over St. Violence to finally capture the eVolution Tag Team Championships. The two of them didn’t get any rest however as Negro Story once again made their presence felt after the match and had their sights set on attacking HBC while Dreamer was laid out in the ring. Unfortunately for them their plans were spoiled by The BodySnatchers, who have had plenty of problems with The Syndicate in the past but apparently put them aside as their current issues with Negro Story is more pressing in their minds apparently. Now this week Negro Story will bring Daddy’s Dirty Little Secret into the picture and the two teams will battle against The BodySnatchers and The Syndicate in an eight person tag team battle. Who’s bright idea was this match? Tyler’s of course as he thought it would be something interesting to see as he pairs Jason Jameson and Morpheus Omega together who obviously have some very hostile issues with one another now against Thug roC and Sean Mason who have had a very long history since Thug roC’s days as President of eVolution. This match isn’t going to be a very pretty sight more than likely as it will almost definitely end in one massive brawl as it’s doubtful that these four men can keep their tempers down long enough to finish this one. Last week James Kimson managed to barely win the number one contendership to the Universal Title and that put him on a one way course against Xander. Shocka and Seara meanwhile both have some issues that need to be settled with Greatness and just because Tyler Davidson can do it he’s signed this match to be a three on two handicap match with Kimson, Shocka, and Seara on one side and on the other side would be Xander and Greatness who do apparently have a rather mysterious relationship together. Just what their connection is has caused quite a stir throughout eVolution but no one knows for sure. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Jason Jameson, Thug roC, and many of eVolution’s superstars!
