The Genesis Pit 2, 21 June 2003

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Latest Columns The Genesis Pit #2 "Hello welcome to the new format for The Genesis Pit. The very foundation for professional Wrestling has been the house show business. A lot of things happen on these shows...and future stars are often introduced in this environment. Tyler Davidson has decided to use this show as a showcase Evolution talent as well as talent of our developmental territory the Urban Legend Wrestling Nation. I am your host Monica Angel and welcome to The Genesis Pit: The Ring Twilight Tour." "Tonight we have Yoshii Tanaka and Drake Nathaniel Dukes in match-ups. As well as a matchup between The British Bomber and 1001 Holds. Finally our main event for this week will feature The Evolution Tag Team Championship as well as The Evolved Championship in the balance as the champions Hedges and Luther Wisdom take on St. Violence." "Our first matchup of the night features Yoshii Tanaka against ULWN superstar Damien Legend in a ladder match." Dateline: New Haven, Connecticut 6/19/03 Yoshii Tanaka vs. Damien Legend (Ladder match): Legend is on the receiving end of some punishment throughout the whole matchup. Tanaka rams Legend into the ladder. Blinding mist directly into the eyes of Legend sends the ULWN superstar reeling. Back elbow connects, Legend staggers backward. He recovers enough to surprise Tanaka with a low blow out of nowhere. Tanaka gets hip-tossed onto the ladder. Spinning bulldog in the corner, sends the eVo superstar down. Tanaka recovers to catch Legend with a big clothesline. Tanaka DDTs Damien Legend. Legend kicks Tanaka in the gut to reverse the momentum. Tanaka avoids an avalanche, as Legend hits the turnbuckles hard. Big clothesline on Legend. Here it comes - RISING SUN. Tanaka climbs the ladder and grabs the suitcase filled with money for the win. As he descends down the ladder he is attacked by Matsuda Sun Tsai of Crimson Sun...who nails him with an inverted DDT from the middle rungs of the ladder. Sun Tsai grabs a mic..... "You are geijin.....and you disgust me." Back in the studio: "Wow what was that all about? Well Tanaka and Sun Tsai may get a chance to continue this on an Evolution stage as Matsuda Sun Tsai and his partner Hiro Iishi form the tag team The Crimson Sun. They are one of the 8 tag teams in the Evolution Invitational. Another The Fierceharts are represented in the next match by Kevin Fiercehart. He takes on the all ULWN triple threat elimination matchup which features Hiro Iishi of Crimson Sun as well as Vagabond a rising star of the ULWN." Dateline: New Haven, Connecticut 6/19/03 Kevin Fiercehart vs. Hiro Iishi vs. Vagabond: Fiercehart bodyslams Iishi. He then does the same to Vagabond. He turns around into a dropkick by Hiro Iishi, who got good elevation. Iishi however was not paying attention as he himself walks into a spike slam by Vagabond. Pin : 1....2....no! Shoulder up at the last second. Back elbow connects, Vagabond staggers backward. Iishi and Vagabond whip Fiercehart into the ropes and hit a double backdrop. Burning hammer drop from Iishi. Hooks the leg : 1....2....3! Kevin Fiercehart is eliminated. Massive backbreaker, Vagabond got planted. Vagabond counters a sleeper hold by turning it into a jaw breaker. Vagabond turns Iishi inside-out with a clothesline. Hooks the leg : 1....2....shoulder up. Iishi slips out the back of a Vagabond bodyslam. Suplex into a front slam from Iishi. ...Crippler Crossface! Vagabond taps! Promo: Matsuda Sun Tsai and Hiro Iishi are seen in the back locker room area..... "New Dawn....the PPV, the reunited team of The Crimson Sun has a chance to once again make it back to The Show. But this time will be far different from last time. I will not be lured by the false gods and wannabe deities of Evolution Wrestling. This time the tag title is coming home...to the land of the rising sun." "Beware Dan Somers...BodySnatchers.....Yoshii Tanaka. They say payback is a bitch, then we are much worse. New Dawn we get our foot in the door and at EVO1 we will kick it in." Back to studio: Monica Angel starts..."Next we have a match involving Evolution newcomer Drake Nathaniel Dukes. He faces the tag team of Wideload and Thinline in a handicapped match. Evolution may remember Wideload as a member of the tag team Syndicate as well as BodySnatchers INC. he is hoping to work his way back to EVo to settle some unfinished business." Dateline: Albany, New York 6/20/03 Drake Nathaniel Dukes vs. Wideload \ Thinline : Uninspiring brawling from Thinline only serves to irritate DND. Wideload hits a dropkick on DND. Wideload \ Thinline whip DND into the corner. Wideload whips Thinline in for a hard clothesline to follow-up. Hooks the leg : 1....2....shoulder up. Thinline turns DND inside-out with a clothesline. Pin : 1....2....no! Shoulder up at the last second. Thinline scoops up DND. Wideload bounces off the ropes and hits a flying Hart Attack clothesline. Thinline avoids an avalanche, DND hits the turnbuckles hard. DND hits a missile dropkick on Wideload. Cover! 1....2....kick out at the last second! DND looks shocked. Thinline reverses a DND hammerlock. DND gets squashed in the corner with a clothesline. Thinline \ Wideload hook up DND, then hit a double suplex. Hooks the leg : 1....2....shoulder up. DND ducks a clothesline attempt. DND floors Wideload He locks in The Stranglehold as Thinline tries to break the hold by repeatedly striking him in the back....but DND only locks it in tighter as Wideload the 600 pounder taps out!!!!! "Our next match features 1001 Holds of The Body Snatchers and British Bomber of America's Guardians. Both teams are embroiled in a bitter battle for the number one contendership for the tag team titles. The Body Snatchers are one up on the Guardians, but this singles matchup could sway the momentum heading into next weeks Legends." Dateline: Albany, New York 6/20/03 British Bomber vs. 1001 Holds : 1001 takes a kick. Weak headbutt on 1001 by Bomber. 1001 Holds takes a right hand to the temple from British Bomber. 1001 gets squashed in the corner with a clothesline. 1001 Holds elbows Bomber in the face to break a hammerlock. Spin kick by 1001 Holds to the face. 1001 hits a high kick on British Bomber. Cover! 1....2...kick out. Bomber counters a backdrop attempt with a kick to the face. Bomber hits a bulldog off the ropes. Hooks the leg : 1....2....shoulder up. Bomber DDTs 1001 Holds. Hooks the leg : 1....2....shoulder up! That was close. 1001 Holds ducks a clothesline attempt. Dropkick connects, and British Bomber goes down. Big kick from Bomber. 1001 Holds gets knocked to the ground by British Bomber, who is already climbing the turnbuckle. Flying Splash!!! 1....2....3!! It's all over. "Next we bring you our main event....as the dysfunctional tag and Evolved champions Hedges and Luther Wisdom places both belts on the line against the team of St. Violence. Tensions between the champions build on a weekly basis and are almost assured to boil over soon. St. Violence has orchestrated a few things behind the scenes to shake up the tag division most recently with High Impact of Team Network. They now find themselves on the front lines and not in the role of puppet masters as they take on the champs for both sets of gold. We now join this match in progress live....." Dateline: Hartford, Connecticut 6/21/03 Deacon punches Luther Wisdom repeatedly. Deacon tags in Eric Knight. Eric Knight gets back to his feet. Eric Knight gets back into the match with a jumping neck snap on Luther Wisdom. Eric Knight hiptosses Luther Wisdom. Luther Wisdom gets up. Luther Wisdom grabs Eric Knight's leg and takes him down. Eric Knight gets back to his feet. Luther Wisdom makes the tag to Hedges. They lockup. Hedges sends Eric Knight to the corner of the ring. Eric Knight measures Hedges up and drops a closed fist. Eric Knight climbs to his feet. Hedges climbs to his feet. Hedges comes from behind and bulldogs Eric Knight. Eric Knight brings in Deacon for St. Violence. Deacon gets hit with a dragon screw from Hedges. Hedges is back on his feet. Hedges executes a corkscrew legdrop on Deacon. Eric Knight tagged in by Deacon. Deacon piledrives Hedges. They lockup. Eric Knight sends Hedges to the corner of the ring. Hedges applies an arm wrench to Eric Knight. Hedges executes a ropeflip hiptoss on Eric Knight. Deacon tagged in by Eric Knight. Deacon is up again. Deacon throws Hedges off the ropes and hits him with a diving shoulder block. Deacon tags in Eric Knight. They lockup. Hedges sends Eric Knight to the corner of the ring. Hedges gouges Eric Knight's eyes out. Eric Knight brings in Deacon for St. Violence. Eric Knight with a high crossbody on Hedges. Eric Knight gives Hedges a reverse neckbreaker. Deacon hits Hedges with a rolling elbow smash to the face. Hedges delivers a spinning backbreaker to Deacon. Eric Knight tagged in by Deacon. Hedges with a suplex on Eric Knight sends him to the mat. Hedges is up again. Deacon tagged in by Eric Knight. Deacon piledrives Hedges. Eric Knight punches Hedges repeatedly. Eric Knight sets Hedges up DDTs him into the mat. Eric Knight kicks Hedges in the stomach and executes the sitdown face slam. Eric Knight climbs to his feet. Hedges puts Eric Knight on the top rope and executes a superplex. Hedges gets up. Eric Knight is back on his feet. Eric Knight throws Hedges off the ropes and hits him with a diving shoulder block. Deacon makes the tag to Eric Knight. Deacon punches Hedges repeatedly. Deacon suplexes Hedges. Eric Knight hits the handspring moonsault on Hedges. Hedges punches Eric Knight repeatedly. Eric Knight tags Deacon. Eric Knight hits a dragon suplex on Hedges. Deacon suplexes Hedges. Hedges knees Deacon and rolls back to his feet. Deacon tries for a fisherman buster but is not strong enough to lift Hedges. Hedges puts Deacon in an arm grapevine submission. Deacon is up again. Hedges gets taken down with a corkscrew armdrag. Now Hedges standing. Deacon tries for a spinning backbreaker but is unable to lift Hedges. Deacon hits a koppo kick on Hedges. Hedges gets up. Deacon makes the tag to Eric Knight. Eric Knight lifts Hedges into the air and delivers a spine buster. Deacon rakes his fingers across Hedges' back. Hedges gets thrown by his partner, and hits a plancha onto Deacon! Hedges climbs to his feet. Eric Knight tags Deacon. Hedges hits a spinning leg lariat on Deacon sending him to the mat. Deacon gets back to his feet. Deacon rakes the face of Hedges in attempt to make a come back. Deacon pins Hedges against the ropes and chokes him with his forearm. Deacon brings in Eric Knight for St. Violence. Eric Knight knifehand chops Hedges. Eric Knight kicks Hedges in the groin. Hedges brings in Luther Wisdom for Hedges/Wisdom. Hedges with an illegal chokehold on Eric Knight. Luther Wisdom hits Eric Knight with the double arm DDT into the mat. Hedges executes the slingshot facebuster, slamming Eric Knight's face to the mat. Eric Knight short lariats Hedges. Now Hedges standing. Eric Knight makes the tag to Deacon. Deacon grabs Luther Wisdom's leg and takes him down. Luther Wisdom with spinning headscissors on Deacon send him hard to the mat. Deacon stands up. Deacon goes for a hotshot but Luther Wisdom dodges the attack. Deacon tries for a gut-wrench suplex but Luther Wisdom avoids it. Luther Wisdom is nailed with a waistlock suplex from Deacon. Wisdom gets up. Deacon tags Eric Knight. Luther Wisdom tackles Eric Knight. Eric Knight makes the tag to Deacon. Deacon executes a ropeflip hiptoss on Luther Wisdom. Deacon punches Luther Wisdom repeatedly. Deacon gives Luther Wisdom a reverse neckbreaker. Deacon stands up. Luther Wisdom moves back to his feet. Luther Wisdom with a takeover suplex on Deacon. Now Luther Wisdom standing. Deacon gets back to his feet. Deacon gets thrown into the turnbuckle. Luther Wisdom comes over and smashes Deacon's head into it. Luther Wisdom hits Deacon with an earringer. Deacon hits a dragon suplex on Luther Wisdom. Deacon gets up. Eric Knight tagged in by Deacon. They lockup. Eric Knight sends Luther Wisdom to the corner of the ring. Deacon uses a swinging DDT to plant Luther Wisdom's head into the mat. Luther Wisdom superkicks Deacon. Deacon gets back to his feet. Luther Wisdom makes the tag to Hedges. Eric Knight is back on his feet. Eric Knight executes a huge gutbuster on Hedges. Hedges moves back to his feet. Hedges hits Eric Knight with a headbutt to the mid-section. Hedges brings in Luther Wisdom for Hedges/Wisdom. Luther Wisdom comes from behind and bulldogs Eric Knight. Luther Wisdom gets back to his feet. Luther Wisdom puts Eric Knight in an arm grapevine submission. Luther Wisdom forearm smashes Eric Knight. Eric Knight brings in Deacon for St. Violence. Luther Wisdom hits the mat. Deacon punches Luther Wisdom repeatedly. Deacon puts Luther Wisdom on the top rope and executes a superplex. Deacon stands up. and executes the Supper DDT on Luther Wisdom! Hedges shakes his head and abandons his tag team partner; Deacon tags in Eric Knight as he pins Luther Wisdom with a rolling cradle. The ref starts the count. ...1 ...2 ...3! Winner and NEW Evolution Tag Team Champions........St. Violence! And your new Evolved champion Eric Knight!! "Ohhh my god.....St. Violence are the new tag team champions, and Eric Knight the new Evolved champion. How does this effect Legends, the PPV as well as EVO1? Well...I'm sure we will find out on Legends. Next week we are off due to the PPV, see you after Evolution's One year anniversary as the house show circuit counts down to REVELATIONS. Good night all from The Genesis Pit."
