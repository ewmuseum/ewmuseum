X-Rated Transcript Live on Pay Per View! From Twickenham ~ London, England 7:59PM EST Sunday Night August 31st, 2003 Pre-Taped ~ Climbing The Mountain Featuring Damon O’Halloran Earlier in the day... The sun shone down on the muscular back of Damon O'Halloran as he held himself against the steep rock face. His thighs flexed as he slowly adjusted his weight from side to side. With his chalked hands searching for another hand hold, O'Halloran maintained his precarious footing on the small fragment of rock jutting out just far enough for his toes to rest on. Sweat trickled down his face and dripped off the end of his nose, falling hundreds of feet down to the desert floor below. For the past few hours, the Cell Champion had slowly made his way up the corkscrew summit of the sandstone formation known as Ancient Art. The climb had been challenging, both physically and mentally. There were quite a few climbers waiting behind him to complete his ascent, but the Irishman wasn't about to be rushed. If they didn't like the wait, they could go to the parking lot and climb each other for all he cared. As he dug his fingers into the crevice, O'Halloran regretted bringing his wireless headset with him. His road agent's voice had been in his ear for most of the climb. It was getting very annoying, not to mention quite distracting. "It's not too late to come down," Patrick said with great concern. "It will still be here tomorrow if you still want to do some climbing." "But I won't be here," Damon grunted as he pulled himself up. "After the show tonight, I'm a vapor trail." "I still don't understand why you're doing this today. I mean, today of all days." Looking through his tinted lenses, O'Halloran spied his next handhold. It was just out of reach, so he'd have to swing towards it. He tightened his grip on the crumbling muddy stone and propelled himself through the air, quickly grabbing the ledge above him. "Shut up. I'm trying to concentrate." "Sorry Champ." The silence didn't last long. "Are you going to be ready for your match against Terlayne and Sean?" Damon's feet dangled in the air as he inched his way across the ledge. "I'll show up. That's all I'll need to do." "But –" "Damn it already!" O'Halloran banged the side of his head against the stone beside him, scuffing his temple and knocking out his ear piece. He paused, waiting for the stars to subside. Asis didn’t get a chance to block or deflect the blow in any way as he was heart set on nailing Kimson with his move and forgot for a moment about Xander and now Asis is on the canvas with Kimson and Xander is on all 4's as he used all his strength to scramble Asis’ brains. Asis has rolled onto the ring apron at this point to try and absorb the blow, blood trickles down his face from the impact of Iron on skull as Xander makes ready to go out after him. But Asis stands up on the apron, Xander now has stepped through the ropes and both stand on the ropes. Xander throws an elbow to the face of Asis and Asis makes ready to fall but holds onto the top rope. Asis fires with an elbow to the ribs of Xander and then both men get all tied up in each other. They come flying off the apron and then dramatically crash through the table that Asis had discarded earlier when Kimson kicked it into him minutes ago. All 3 wrestlers are laying on their backs, bloody and broken. The only men sitting down in the arena are the announcers as the fans haven’t sat since the bell rang and inside the ring the Ref is shaking his head in disbelief. At this point it’s Kimson that gets to his feet first. He wipes the hair out of his face sporting the crimson mask that has dried up considerably. He was the 1st to lose blood as he staggers around and leans against the ropes. Kimson looks dazed out to the fans who try and cheer him on. Meanwhile out of the ring Asis has crawled out of the broken table and is rolling into the ring. Kimson is unaware of Asis who is getting to his feet and staggering over to Kimson. Kimson turns around to meet Asis with a right hand but Asis blocks it, boots Kimson in the gut and then slowly places Kimson’s head between Asis’ legs and hooks up his arms. It seems like Asis is going to try and finish off Kimson with his own finishing move History in the Making! Kimson can’t seem to reverse the move after the initial struggle and Asis plants Kimson with his own move! Asis makes the cover. 1………………….. 2…………………… Somehow Xander had rolled into the ring and pulls Asis off of Kimson and throws a right hand. Asis throws one in return and then hooks Xander up in a leg trap suplex throwing Xander to the canvas over his head. That move took what was left of Asis’ strength and most of what Xander had to take. Asis crawls over to Xander and drapes an arm across his chest. 1………………….. 2…………………………. Xander kicks out just before 3 and crawls to the ropes. Asis gets up slowly as well but Xander with 1 last ditched effort kicks Asis and twists him around in Asis’ finishing move the Asisination 2K3! Asis gets twisted around and goes for the ride, Xander slams him to the mat and quickly covers Asis in desperation of keeping his beloved title. 1……………………….. 2………………………….. 3 No... Kimson broke up the pin and pull Xander to his feet. Xander is stunned and throws a right hand but Kimson ducks and in his hands he holds the key, what started everything that had Xander involved for the last 3 months in that Monkey Wrench and he smokes Xander in the face with it! Xander teeters over to the canvas. Kimson throws the wrench down and grabs Xander, barely able to stand on his own feet and hooks Xander up for History in the Making, he nails the move, he hooks the leg! 1…………………………. 2………………………….. 3…………………………….. It’s all over. After everything that happened in the match finally the straw broke and no one could give a response this time. The Ref signals for the bell and the arena bursts into an ovation that is seldom rivaled. Libby Bowman gets on the mic and announces the “New Evolution Universal Champion, James Kimson” and that sparks a second pop. Kimson gets his arm raised as Asis rolls out of the ring to the floor still basically out cold. Xander lays motionless in the ring as Kimson is helped up by the Ref and is handed the title belt. Kimson takes the belt and slowly mounts the nearest turnbuckle and holds the belt up in the air in triumph, after all the circles he had to jump through it took this war for him to finally earn the Universal Title, and the fans couldn’t be happier.
