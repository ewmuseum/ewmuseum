"Platinum" Brad Kelley Real Name: Brad Kelley Also Known As: Platinum Date of Birth: November 29th, 1976 Hometown: Allentown, Pennsylvania Height: 6'3 Weight: 221lbs Alignment: Heel Theme Music: "Teething" by The Deftones Manager: None Wrestling Style: Technical Favorite Moves: Snap Suplex, German Suplex, Standing Dropkick, Swinging Neckbreaker, Tornado DDT, Piledriver, Flying Cross Body Block, Death Valley Driver, Inverted 3/4 Swinging Neckbreaker, Sitout Suplex, Double Underhook Powerbomb. Signature Move: Platinum Plant: Cobra Clutch Slam with Brad landing in a sitout position. Other Info Current Teams: None Past Teams: Strictly Business, Perfectly Platinum eVo Title History: None eVo Award History: None Other Titles: EdW Platinum Champion(x1) Other Awards: None Biography Wrestler Entrance: The Deftones' "Teething" rings out over the arena as men, women and children alike bellow out a chorus of jeers all in unison. The lights dim and multicolored strobe lights flicker all over the arena as Brad Kelley steps out onto the entrance way, prompting an even louder showing of disappreciation toward the former EDW Platinum Champion. With smug look on his face, mixed with that of ill will toward the fans, Brad begins to descend down the aisle. As he nears the apron, he slides in under the bottom rope and props himself up on one knee for a moment, before using the top rope to pull himself up. He then jumps to the second rope with his hands to his sides, peering out into the ongoing sea of boos that have followed him down to the ring. Appearance: Brad has blond hair, dyed of course, that reaches down to about the bottom of his ear. His blue eyes resemble that of the ocean that is projected with a hard, unforgiving stare. A medium sized caucasian grappler, Brad completes his look with a grown in beard and white medium length wrestling tights, black knee and elbow pads and black boots. Biography: For the past year or so, where you found Daniel Somers, you found Brad Kelley. Prior to his stint in Era of Destruction Wrestling, Brad Kelley had competed for a number of unknown organizations that failed to flourish. After speding time in these such places, Brad had made his way to the Pro Wrestling Dynasty. There, he became an International Champion as well as a Tag Team Champion as one half of the team known as Perfectly Platinum. Following the demis of PWD, Brad had put out an offer, stating himself as a free agent. It was then that he was recruited by Brandon Kelley, and employed by EDW. Following some months gaining notorioty there, Brad upset Big Daddy Dreamer and became the EDW Platinum Champion. He later lost the title to Shodan in a triple threat match, also involving Jason Jameson. After his devistating title loss, Brad just wasn't the same. He was no longer the man who was eager and ready to take on all challengers.. he had become a one-man pity party. After floundering around in nEw and Soldout Wrestling, Brad has once again found himself in an organization run by Daniel Somers. However, Brad now has a new attitude, and he is hungry for the one thing that has eluded him his whole career.. a Universal Championship.
