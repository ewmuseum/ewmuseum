The 2004 Revival

Here is a quick rundown of some of the main information on the new eVolution. I’m not posting this to give away any kind of storyline ideas, just the basic information. For what the returning storyline is all you need to do is check out the current Resurrection storyline that is being posted on the main page and updated each week until the reopening. The Basics In order to be successful here in eVolution you are going to have to be active on a regular basis. eVolution is a 100% angle fed that is booked by the handlers themselves. What does this mean? It means that each of the roster members are responsible for planning out all the details of their feuds including match schedules and winners for each story arc. This obviously involves a good deal of communication with other members of the roster. What this also entails however is that the handlers are responsible for the writing of their own segments and their own matches as well. So if you are working with someone else for one week of the shows then it would be up to the two of you to decide who will write the match, or whether or not you will work together to write it. If you need a match then all you need to do is post on the members board stating so. Hopefully someone will be man enough to step up and do what needs to be done whether it's to job or to win. If for whatever reason no one does I'll assign one of the NPC's to the spot instead. That is why they are here now after all. Angle Outlines In order for things to really run smoothly I need each and every one of you to do what you can to give me angle outlines for each arc. This doesn't have to be anything major at all. Just basic information like match schedules if possible and what MAJOR plans if any that you think I should know about. This will help me in a few ways by basically giving me some idea as to where things are going in the near future which can help in making decisions about titles, etc. Matches Now in the previous eVolution run the handlers were not responsible for the writing of their own matches, only their segments. That will no longer be the case. You are being given pretty much 100% control over your characters and that includes match writing. If you're scheduled for a match against another handler than it is up to the two of you to decide who will write the match. One of you or even both of you can write the match working together. Also all matches are to be written in the past tense without commentary. Segments Nothing really has changed here besides the requirement that all segments be written in the past tense. However if you wish to use any of the staff members in a manner outside of their listed function you MUST first contact me and get it approved. Don't want a bunch of people sending in segments where the same person gets the shit beat out of them twenty times on the same show. This also applies to the use of wrestler NPC's as well. Story Arcs Each story arc, or angle, lasts approximately two months culminating at the pay per views. As previously stated, each of you are responsible for the running and scheduling of your own story arcs. I will try and have a calendar of events post made back here after each supercard so that you can have an idea of how many shows there are for each story arc which should let you plan your own angles accordingly. Of course this also leaves you all the option of running mini-arcs as well. Basically meaning you can run short minor feuds with other handlers that really only lasts a couple of weeks at first but may lead to something a bit more major later on. This can also be done to cross several different angles/feuds together at some points for major shows and big tag team matches every now and then. Titles: There will only be three singles titles at the present time in eVolution. After we are able to get a stable singles roster going I will look into the possibility of adding on a tag team division as well. As of right now the three titles are: Universal Title - As before this is the top belt in eVolution. No explanation needed. :p Continental Title - Originally the Continental Title then changed to United Title is once again the Continental Title. Second highest belt in eVolution. eVolved Title - My basic idea for it is to make it as old school ECW a title as possible. So any suggestions on a better, more original, and unlame name feel free to throw it out there. Might make a poll on it later for the roster to vote on. Supercards Unlike in the past eVolution now will only have six super card events a year. The shows at first won't be actual pay per views shows as they'd be more along the lines of a televised special perhaps on HBO or a channel along those lines. Now though I'll just get down to listing each of the six pay per views for you by month. Subject to change January: Rise of the Legends - This is the show that features the annual Road to Glory tournament. March: Unbreakable May: Digital Destruction July: Revelations - This one will feature a new sort of special match that will involve ten people total including all three singles champions. September: X-Rated November: Faith Now the return show will be called From the Ashes for obvious reasons which if things go according to schedule will for this time only replace Faith on the show schedule. Legends Legends returns as the one weekly show for eVolution but this time the show will fall on a Sunday night.
