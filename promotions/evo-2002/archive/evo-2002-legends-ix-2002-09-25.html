Legends IX Transcript Live on OSE! From The American Airlines Arena ~ Miami, Florida 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night September 25th, 2002 The fallout from Digital Destruction continues as eVolution brings forward the next show Legends IX! Little information has really been revealed about this show including just what station it will air on. The Universal Title, Rising Star Title will both be on the line during the night. In addition the Tag Team Title tournament will also kick off for the night as more personal vendettas look to continue as a result of Digital Destruction! Gerico made his eVolution debut in a dark match at Digital Destruction. Now he makes it onto live television with a match against the man who interrupted his single greatest moment. Eric Knight chose Gerico’s match to make his return to eVolution and now the former Cell fighter is looking to establish himself in a new niche here in eVolution as he battles perhaps the hottest new cruiserweight around. The heated rivalry continues this week as Sean Mason looks to finally get his revenge against Quasar the Great. For week after week now Mason has been screwed out of victories by Quasar and Thug roC as well. Now he has a one on one chance with Quasar but always lurking in the shadows will be Thug roC who is sure to have a plan or two up his sleeve. The greatest tag team in the history of EdW reforms for another chance at the tag team titles here in eVolution. Their adversaries however are one of the most dangerous teams to exist inside of both EdW and eVolution. The Unlimited standing at the moment as Jinx and Xander look for a chance to face off for the Tag Team Titles. A promise still hangs in the air however as they will reveal just who the third member of their group will be. What kind of effect will this have on the match? And just who will represent The Unlimited? Not a part of the Tag Team Title Tournament these four men just have some personal issues which must be remedied. For Jameson and Shodan this is a trip back to their EdW days where the two of them had teamed on a regular basis but it’s also a chance for them both to get some revenge against their adversaries. It was Eric Badger who screwed Jameson out of his title victory against Asis at Digital Destruction and it was later revealed Badger was on the direct payroll of Daniel Somers to do it. There’s no telling just what plans they might have for Jameson here tonight and how does Shocka fit into the mix? The young rookie Matt Boone had been on a roll in recent weeks picking up two straight victories though the methods he used were not appreciated by his mentor Troy Pierce. At Digital Destruction, Boone made it a point to teach Pierce a lesson and with the aid of Cori Simmons he did just that. Now Boone, who failed to capture the Universal Title at the pay per view, will receive a shot at Rising Star gold against the champion Asis. It was Simmons who demanded this match be made into a submission match hoping perhaps that it will give Boone the advantage. Alias gets the chance of a lifetime this week as he battles for the one prize everyone wants and the chance to avenge his friend, Jamal Kage, beating at the hands of the champion. Unfortunately for him the chance to do all of this comes against the Human Monster Sovereign in a hardcore match. Sovereign has gone on a tear through eVolution and has yet to be stopped by anyone as he moves one step closer to complete and total dominance.
