Legends LVI Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From Tokyo, Japan ~ Olympic Stadium 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night November 5th, 2003 Backstage ~ Someone’s Missing Featuring Thug roC and Machine We see a very happy Thug roC walking into the arena followed by his bodyguard, Machine. Both men are almost too happy so early in the evening. With the United Title over his shoulder and a bag being rolled behind him, roC struts his way into the arena. He is then approached by a security guard at the entrance. “Have you guys sen…uh…Robet Brooks?” The guard says in his horrible Japanese accent. Thug roC just laughs. “No my good friend, I heard he was having car troubles.” Thug roC and Machine just walk away with huge smiles on their faces. Then roC starts talking to his bodyguard. “With Brooks not here tonight, our full plan goes into action. And won’t nobody stop us.” Machine pounds his right fist into his left hand. “Who’s first on the list?” Machine asks with a serious look on his face. “As I look outside, the Wether forecast isn’t looking good.” Thug roC smiles after his statement. “What about Evans? Oh yea, and we got Kimson tonight as well.” Machine asks. Both men walk through the arena doors, passing eVo officials and superstars on their way in. “We don’t want to into Faith with key injuries. Those two can become problems.” “Don’t worry, a plan has already been devised. Evans isn’t a threat to me, and soon, Kimson won’t be either.” RoC says confidently. “Kimson not a threat? How so?” Machine asks curiously. Thug roC just smiles and looks at Machine. “With a little help from you, we have a favor to do for Leya. Which promises that Kimson won’t be ‘making history’ anytime soon.” Thug roC laughs loudly while the scene fades away. Backstage ~ Medical Update Part 1 Featuring Danielle Spears Backstage we pan to find Evolution reporter Danielle Spears standing with a mic in hand as we look to get an update on Cameron Griffins condition. Question is, are the rumors true? "News has just reached Evolution HQ that this weeks Cell Matches have once again been cancelled" opened by saying. The reasoning behind the cancellation once again stems from the position of Cameron Griffins health, although nobody is forthcoming on specifics. It is however said that an announcement will be made in the coming hour" "Announcement?" Justin’s voice could be heard saying. "This is starting to sound very serious" Jack said with a weary tone. "Ever since the reported accident on the highways everything has been hush hush, although it had been said his condition was on the mend. Has that changed?" Justin asked. "Don't look at me" Jack shrugged, "though I get the impression that no news was when there was better news." Backstage ~ Breaking the Eternal Punk Featuring Thug roC, Shane Wethers and Machine We are backstage where Shane Wethers is just walking into the arena. He is greeted by a few eVo officials until he is blindsided from behind. It’s big Machine, taking Shane Wethers down with a massive clothesline, followed by a series of kicks to his back. Shane slowly gets up as Machine walks off of camera. Just as Wethers gets up, Machine comes back with a lead pipe and strikes Shane right in his side. Shane staggers sideways into a wall, giving Machine another clean shot to Shane’s ribs. Wethers screams out in pain as he drops to the ground. Machine picks him up bys his hair and puts him into a bearhug. Referees and officials hurry to the scene, but can’t stop the big man. “Come on Machine, finish him!” Thug roC yells, as he has arrived to the area. Thug roC is directing the big man, and Machine rams Shane into the concrete wall. Wethers puts out another scream and Machine won’t let go of the bearhug. Machine puts Shane on his feet for a moment, before delivering a hard belly to belly suplex on the hard ground. Thug roC is there to stomp Shane right into his sides while refs try to back them away from the scene. “Well, that’s one down.” Thug roC says with a smile. The officials are very upset at the two men and order them to go back to their dressing room. “What’s wrong Wetherman? Think you’ll be able to compete at Faith? Cause I don’t!” Thug roC is pulled away by an official. The camera zooms into Shane’s face, which shows nothing but pain. In The Ring ~ Greetings Champ Featuring James Kimson, Leya Forth, and Machine It’s Time... Let the Hype Begin. The Lights dim down to near darkness as the Fans burst into cheers as Stupify by Disturbed hits the PA speakers signaling the arrival of the Universal Champion, The Hype James Kimson. Known world wide as a Hero to the fans Kimson appears at the entrance way in street clothes with his Leather Jacket and the Title belt slung over his shoulder. He pauses at the top of the V shaped Evolution set ramp and looks out to the fans with a bit of a grin on his face after Last week where he got a small measure of Pay Back against Shocka. Kimson can’t hold up the show all night however as he marches down to the ring and then after walking up the ring steps he slips between the ropes and mounts the nearest turnbuckle to pose for the many flash shots that he can. Kimson dismounts as the music dies off and the lights come back up to their normal level. Kimson walks over to Libby Bowman and takes a microphone from the ring announcer and walks to the center of the ring, taps the mic as the fans even here in Japan love Kimson, chanting his name in a heavy Japanese accent. “Okay so lets review.” says the Champion. “August 31st 2003 I engaged in a battle with Xander and Asis for the Universal Title. Many are calling it the Match of the Year, some of the decade. While I’m not here to debate that claim the fact of the matter is that I won the Universal Title that night realizing a dream. But then, a week afterwards I was out here in this ring celebrating that accomplishment when before my eyes comes out Leya Forth... a member of the ruling body of this company it seems and with one goal in her mind and that was to make my life a living hell. Since then she’s booked in everything she could. I’ve been running around like a chicken without a head defending this title all over the planet no less and of course then she throws me into matches with dangerous people like Shocka. Yeah, Shocka who I faced at Digital Destruction, and despite what he wants to tell you about “letting me win” or “testing the waters” it’s all just a huge smoking steaming pile or horse shit because on Shocka’s best day, and me in the hospital in a wheel chair everyone knows that the wheel chair would whip Shocka’s ass!” Kimson paces around for a bit of a pause. “Now, after that you’d think that Shocka would be out of the picture.. But oh no, never out of the picture because it gets bigger for the Shock-Daddy or whatever that little bitch calls himself every 30 seconds. Anyway her he gets an undeserved title match at Faith, the biggest show of the year with Leya it seems so hell bent on getting this belt off me that she herself is going to Ref the match. Well, I’d have to say that it doesn’t look good for me does it? I’d even go so far to say that if I was a betting man the odds against me would be pretty bad. I mean, beating Shocka isn’t the hard part. I’ve already done that. Even with my ribs cracked I still beat him at Digital Destruction. But now with the Referee hell bent on me not leaving with the Title.... wow pretty rough seas ahead don’t you think? But... that being said Leya, I know that you are so hell bent on this going down at Faith with me laying down on this mat and you making that 3 count on me and giving that belt to Shocka and then probably keeping it on his worthless ass until your little hubby is all healed and ready to return from the ass kicking I gave him at Unbreakable what... 8 months ago that you can basically feed Shocka to him for the title you still have to realize something. That you haven’t won yet. And while I scrambled Shocka’s brains last week, and by the way that was a lot of fun, thanks for the chair Shocka. Goes to show how’s really that brilliant and on top of everything huh. Anyway Leya, you still have to win the match at Faith for Shocka. Because he can’t do it himself. It’s going to take basically an act of God to take this belt off of me at Faith, and I know you might look great, you might have a lot of power here, but you sure as hell are not a God.” Just then My Way by Limp Bizkit hits the speakers prompting boos from the fans in the arena. After a slight pause out onto the stage walks the devilishly beautiful Leya Forth in a business suit showing off her tight figure. She hasn’t got the regular cocky grin on her face anymore, more of a scowl after last week’s plan backfired. “You know James, the only reason why you are standing here tonight is because of that no good Brooks and you owe him a lot of thanks because there’s no way on earth you would have survived Charisma and Shocka at the same time. But you know, besides all that I am here tonight for one reason. You think that the deck is stacked against you, well that might be so. But it’s going to get worse tonight because besides your Tag Team match tonight, you’ve got yourself a Universal Title defense right now!” Kimson looked up to her confused as she smiled and then Machine’s music hit and he came running out with Thug roC to give him some extra support. Apparently the Ref at ringside jumps into the ring as the bell rings. Machine slides under the bottom rope as Leya and roC arrive at ringside to watch up close. Universal Title ~ Standard Match Machine vs. James Kimson© Machine is throwing right hands rocking the unsuspected Champion back to the ropes. Kimson gets whipped across the ring but comes back on the rebound and ducks a clothesline and turns around and fires away with a fury of punches with the 3rd one knocking Machine to the canvas. Kimson removes his jacket as the fans are getting pumped about this impromptu match. Machine gets to his feet and Kimson smokes him with a right hand and places him in the corner and starts to work him over with stomps beating down Machine to the canvas. Kimson walks out of the corner and rips off his shirt exposing his muscles and his power. Machine gets up out of the corner and comes out after Kimson who ducks his clothesline boots Machine in the gut and then hooks him up in History in the Making. RoC and Leya are shocked as to how this could happen as Machine just is planted to the canvas with huge force and huge height. Kimson rolls him over and hooks the leg. 1………………….. 2………………. 3! That was all it took, Kimson dominated the big man and is handed his title belt as his music hit. Leya and roC can’t believe it as Machine lays there on the canvas not moving as Kimson hoists the belt into the air as the segment closes to commercial. "I believe he is in his office Miss HeartBreaker.." "Thank you sir, you see D, you get more with a kind word and actions instead of threatening them from the get go." HBC looks back at the roadie and smiles to appreciation to him, before snapping his head back with 'The Kiss of Death' sending him crashing to the floor, unconscious. Backstage ~ Have A Little Faith Featuring Leya Forth The camera comes back to Leya’s office as she sits on the corner of her desk. This prompts a mixed reaction, cheers for her looks, and boos for her person. She has a cocky grin back on her face as she parts her lips. “Well what an interesting turn of events tonight. First off James, congratulations for beating Machine in the fastest Title match in the history of eVolution. But the problem is that the match has some consequences for you. I just got off the phone with our Doctor and it appears that you have caused some serious damage to Machine’s neck after the History in the Making you gave him forcing me to cancel his match tonight with Aaron Justice and probably remove him from the active roster for sometime. As unfortunate as that is, I have to make it very clear that we cannot endanger our wrestlers with dangerous moves like History in the Making. Therefore from now on I am officially suspending the move from eVolution. Any wrestler who attempts that move, at all will be by default Disqualified and suspended for 1 week without pay.” “What? That’s totally unfair” screamed Fritz over the headset behind the boos. “Now, that being said after LAST week’s altercation with Shocka and the chair I’ve also been forced James to rule that you would no doubt use that chair against Shocka at Faith again to purposely get Disqualified in the match thus retaining your title. I mean, it’s a crafty plot James but I can see right through you. Therefore if you get disqualified via a chair, or any other foreign objects including History in the Making you will not only loss the match but the Title will go to Shocka as a result of those underhanded tactics!” “What the hell?” said Fritz. “So James that means that the only way you can win the match is Submission or Pinfall. If you screw up and get Dqed then you loss that precious title of yours, and Shocka will be a 2 time Universal Champion in eVolution. That should teach you to be so reckless and endangering your fellow eVolution superstars” Backstage ~ Breaking The Ice Featuring Kramer Wild Wing We cut to backstage where a buffet table has elaborately been placed for hungry eVo wrestlers and crewmembers overtaken by the munchies. A brand new face to the Evolution scene walks through the doorway which gets no ones attention. “Hey could I interest you in some fine KFC chicken? Full of crispy fingerlickin goodness.” He asks an oversized cameraman as he pushes the bucket of chicken in his face. “What? Who the hell are you?” The cameraman asks with a full mouth, spattering food all over the place. “New signee to Evolution, Kramer Wild Wing of course.” “If you’re a new wrestler what’s the chicken for?” He asks crossly, not liking the idea of being pestered during his eating frenzy. “Sort of like a peace offering for everyone.” Kramer says slowly backing away from the man not starting off as well as he had hoped. “Just wanted to come in and get to know everyone seeing as how I’m new around here, but I guess that was a big mistake.” The cameraman has lost all interest and rushes away leaving Kramer there feeling pretty stupid. “What a nice welcome from Evolution!” Kramer says to himself sarcastically. He puts the chicken on the buffet table and leaves the room ready to scope out his future competition on tonight’s Legends. Non-Title ~ Standard Match Xander vs. Outblaze The entire arena is plunged into darkness as the beginning of “Tomorrow” by SR-71 is heard. As the toggling guitar effects come in and out the arena is struck by the strobe light going with the beat. Then, as the main guitar cuts through the toggling lead a huge explosion of pyrotechnics goes off and Xander comes out from the back and out to the staging. Surprising to Xander no doubt was that once he made his appearance he wasn’t greeted by boos that he had become so accustomed to in the past. Instead the crowd began to cheer for the arrival of Xander as he stood at the top of the ramp and took a moment to look around at the capacity crowd before he made his way quickly down the ramp. Xander got to the bottom of the ramp and rolled quickly into the ring before he shot back up to his feet and turned to the entrance to wait on the arrival of Outblaze. The arena lights grow dim slowly. The crowd falls to an eerie calm. Suddenly up on the entrance screen a green line can be seen pulsing, like that of a heart monitor. In the background a low bass beat thumps away. Silence again. Suddenly thunderous bang comes from nowhere as red and yellow pyros shoot from the stage and walkway. As the pyros calm and the smoke clears, Lonely World by Limp Bizkit blares across the arena system. From within the clearing smoke stands Outblaze. He pauses and watches the crowd briefly before throwing him arms into the arm. He lowers his arms and makes his way to the ring.
