House Show I, 1 June 2003

House Show Results eVolution House Show Results For June 1st, 2003 From The True North Centre in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada For the first time in a long while eVolution came around with another house show. This was the first house show the company has ran in about two months time and it was the first time they also came to the Winnipeg area. Most of the top stars were in attendance for the show and it was a huge show to follow up the From The Ashes pay per view last weekend. It was certainly a great show for the company’s first trip to Winnipeg and hopefully it’ll be back again soon for a televised event. Cell Match Eric Knight vs. Ali Khadafi The return of the Cell has been a breath of fresh air so far for eVolution and it continued with this show. The Cell veteran Eric Knight made his return to the steel structure in a battle against the very impressive newcomer, Ali Khadafi. This fight was brutal from the very start but it was Ali who took the victory in this one. He demonstrated his incredible kickboxing skills from the very start and in the end it was a sharp kick right to Eric Knight’s temple that put him down and out for the ten count and gave Ali the victory. Winner by knockout: Ali Khadafi Cell Match Josh Horroway vs. Othinel The Cell Fights continued with the second match as two newcomers Josh “Shockwave” Horroway and Othinel battled it out. Othinel seemed to be quite an odd person from the start of it but he demonstrated quite a lot of ability in the Cell as he battled back against Horroway’s initial attack. Just when it seemed like Othinel would be the one picking up the victory though Horroway countered a right hand from Othinel and took him straight down into a Crossface submission hold and Othinel was left with no choice but to tap out from the pain. Winner by submission: Josh “Shockwave” Horroway Tag Team Match Savage Society vs. The Syndicate The Syndicate versus Savage Society was the next match on the card despite the current situation going on in the eVolution tag team division. Savage Society had the chance to make a huge impact with a victory in this match and from the start of it, it looked like they just might be able to pull it off. One mistake was all it took and following a missed double clothesline attempt on Big Daddy Dreamer, Carnivore had his clocked cleaned by an HBC superkick and The Syndicate quickly followed up on Cannibal with The Syndicator and the victory. Winners by pinfall: The Syndicate Handicap Match Dane vs. Hate and Homicide It was announced prior to this match that if Hate and Homicide defeated Dane the two of them would get their contracts back in eVolution. Unfortunately for them that just wasn’t going to happen as Dane dominated the two of them. It wasn’t long at all before Dane caught both men with his Panic Breaker set up move and then drilled Hate with Dead-Irony Ender and the cover for the victory. Winner by pinfall: Dane Number One Contender To eVolved Title Match Xero vs. Malcolm Luther Xavier Xero and the former eVolved Champion MLX had an awesome battle in this one. Xero was obviously not at one hundred percent following his brutal match with Trevor Norman at From The Ashes and I’m not to certain MLX was any better off. In the end though MLX was still able to pull off the victory and earned a rematch against Hedges for the eVolved Title. Winner by pinfall: Malcolm Luther Xavier Continental Title Match Yoshii Tanaka vs. Sean Mason© This was Sean Mason’s first title defense as the new Continental Champion and it was against the man he had defeated to again earn the shot and for some reason he was wearing a mask. Yoshii continued his newfound heel tactics with a sneak attack on Mason before the bell even started and Yoshii it seemed was in firm control of the match until the referee forced him to break a submission hold on Mason in the corner. That wasn’t going to slow Yoshii down though as he went for his blinding mist on Mason and it went right into his face. The mist however didn’t seem to have any affect on Mason thanks to the protection of his new mask and the confused Yoshii went for a clothesline only to miss as Mason turned and caught him with a superkick right to the side of his head and covered him for the win. Winner by pinfall: “The Prodigal” Sean Mason Tag Team Match Thug roC and Greatness vs. Shocka and Jason Jameson This match was odd as for some reason Thug roC was teaming up with that new guy who called himself Greatness. The two of them didn’t seem to get along to well during the match but they still somehow managed to pick up the victory in a rather tainted fashion. Jameson and Shocka controlled the match as they took turns tagging in and out and had Greatness in trouble when the referee got knocked out in the midst of a double team maneuver by Jameson and Shocka. This gave Thug roC all the opening he needed as he caught Jameson from behind with a steel chair shot. The shot dropped Jameson like a ton of bricks and before Shocka knew what was happening roC had taken him up and around into the roCing Horse which allowed Greatness to climb on top and make the cover on Jameson for the win as Thug roC revived the referee. Winners by pinfall: Thug roC and Greatness Tag Team Main Event Xander and Asis vs. Tyler Davidson and James Kimson Xander and Asis displayed quite a bit of teamwork throughout this match and the two of them together dominated Tyler Davidson. Nothing lasts forever however and Tyler made a desperate tag out to James Kimson and he stormed into the ring and went straight after both men. All it took was a flurry of punches from Kimson to send Xander out of the ring and that was the last he was seen for the match. Xander grabbed his title and chose to leave his partner hanging against both men as he walked to the back to let Asis handle the rest. In the end it was Kimson who hit Asis with History In The Making to pick up the victory for their team. Winners by pinfall: James Kimson and Tyler Davidson Biggest Pops: 1) James Kimson 2) Shocka and Jameson team 3) Sean Mason 4) The Syndicate 5) Tyler Davidson Most Heat 1) Xander 2) Asis 3) Thug roC 4) Ali Khadafi 5) Greatness (c) eWentertainment 2003
