Legends 2 Transcript Live on CBN! From The Continental Airlines Arena ~ East Rutherford, New Jersey 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night July 17th, 2002 American Nightmare, Legends 1. Both are now in the books and ancient history as far as the wrestlers are concerned. The first champion had been crowned as Erin Christoph captured the Continental Title at Legends 1. Destruction had reigned supreme on that night and the aftermath is still being felt throughout eVolution. What will the repercussions be at Legends 2? Will Malcolm King finally make his appearance? Why is Eric Graves in court? In a matter of moments the answers will begin to unfold. Parking Lot ~ It’s Over Featuring The Syndicate "It's Over!" a voice says as the screen powers up and standing up in front of us are both members of the exclusive Syndicate organization, HbC and Big Daddy Dreamer. While the two stand in the back of the Continental Airlines Arena both dressed in their wrestling gear as the two team up with Barbatos Bronx in just a few moments from now. "Just relax, we got more important business to take care of tonight." Hbc replies, jumping off the subject and quickly catching the full attention of BDD. "You know what we spoke about earlier in the pre match briefing." Hbc says staring across at BDD who nods back in return. "Well it's a go." Hbc finishes bring a smile to the face BDD. "Great, who would ever wanna team up with anyone named Barbatos Bronx." BDD comments in a cocky tone as HbC shrugs in response. Just as HbC come to a stand still the sound of an engine can be heard in the distance. As the camera swings around it catches a yellow taxi cab pulling into the back of the arena. Both BDD and HbC look at one another obviously knowing whose inside the vehicle before BDD looks down at his watch growing a frown across his face. Just then the taxi pulls up in front of HbC and BDD before the back door slowly swings open and the seductive, bad ass, Katrina steps out decked in a red spaghetti string dress. "Why did you leave me...." Katrina begins to say but before she can continue on any further BDD cuts her off as the taxi pulls off. "Look at you, what in the hell do you have to say for yourself this week?" BDD asks as Katrina looks back in a shocked but disbelief state. "I...I..." Katrina tries to get out but nothing seems to come out right. "Exactly, you have nothing to say for you action. So like I was saying Monday before you ran off. Katrina I don't think this is going to work anymore. I see you have plans of your own and well, so do I. And to be fair to the both of us, I have to say. Katrina. IT"S OVER!" BDD says not waiting to see her reaction but spinning around and heading back into the arena as HbC decides whether to stay making up her mind and slowly walking over to Katrina who holds her mouth wide open and kissing her on the cheek. "It'll be alright." HbC whispers into Katrina ear before walking back into the arena. Not to say we didn't see this coming but damn it's surely bad things had to end this way. But I guess when the time comes the time comes and it seems time is up for Katrina and BDD. Continually to stand shocked Katarina eyes begin to fill up before tear after tear begin to roll out her eyes and down her cheeks. "Why me?" she motions, stepping off the curb and onto the concrete pavement making her way up the ramp way that leads out the building. Once making it up and out the parking lot area, a few fans that are yet to make it inside the arena wave at Katrina unknowing of what has just taken place. Katrina stares back up towards them nonchalantly throwing up her arms and waving back causing even more tears to come down her face as she knows this maybe her last time seen in front of a national audience. Backstage ~ Madhouse Featuring Sovereign, Tom the Bomb, and Barbatos Bronx Backstage several eVo staff members are fixing up the buffet area for the wrestlers so that they may get fresh fruit and sandwiches if they need something during the event. One man is filling the coffee decanter up as the two individuals are having a friendly conversation, you’d guess them to be friends. "I can’t believe she’d do something like that, I mean how long have you guys been together Ray?" said the man filling the coffee up. "Man, 3 months. I thought she might have been... well you know the one!” said Ray in response. "I guess you just never can tell when they’re out for your money." he sighed. Ray finishes uncovering the fruit trays when then walking into the area is Sovereign. Both Ray and his friend look at Sovereign and quickly hurry up their business. Sovereign walks up to them, grabs several pieces of fruit and then grabs a cup and goes to fill it with coffee, however the man that just filled it speaks to Sovereign with a timid voice. "Uh... Mr. Sovereign sir that coffee is really hot I just... I ..I just filled it" he said quickly hoping not to anger Sovereign. Sovereign just looked at the man blankly and filled his cup to the brim. Steam is flying out of the cup as Sovereign takes sip and pulls the cup away, obviously to hot to drink. Sovereign licks his lips and smiles as if he enjoys it. "Umm, not to hot is it?" said Ray. "I guess that’s right, you are that sort of guy that likes pain right" Ray added. Sovereign turned his attention to Ray and jerked his head to one side. "No" said Sovereign "I don’t like Pain, Pain hurts! Do you like Pain?” Sovereign quickly drops the cup and lashes his hands around Ray’s jaw in The Last Breath. Ray screams in pain as his comrade drops the tray of sandwiches he was holding and panics not sure what to do. Sovereign then picks up the man and with the death lock still applied hoists him up and then crashes him through the table! The table shatters and the other man makes ready to run, but Sovereign grabs him by the shirt and screams something at him, no real words just screaming, like a maniac and grabs him and throws him into the nearby soda machine! The poor soul bounces off the Pepsi logo and hits the floor like a sack of bricks and Sovereign stands over him and Ray with a smile from ear to ear and giggling, laughing out loud to himself. "Pain... maybe I do like pain" mumbled Sovereign as he grabs a sandwich and walks off taking a bite. As he does though two more people enter the picture as Tom the Bomb can be seen in the distance. Sovereign hurries to catch up to Tom for some reason when he is suddenly slammed into from behind by Barbatos Bronx who was also moving in on Tom. Sovereign barely feeling the blow turns slowly and with a confused look glances at Barbatos who just realizes what he’s done. “Oh crap,” is all Barbatos can manage to spit out as the monster Sovereign stares him down. “I…I.” Before he can finish the sentence Sovereigns look of confusion turns to one of rage as he lunges at Barbatos grabbing him by the throat and slamming him into the wall. Tom hears all of the commotion behind him and turns to look behind him and see the ruckus. Along with several other backstage workers, Tom runs to the scene in hopes of breaking the fight up before serious injury could be inflicted. As soon as he grabs onto the back of Sovereign though the monster spins and locks in the Last Breath on Tom. He quickly begins taking Tom down who struggles with the hold as the other workers keep back a safe distance not wanting the same thing to happen to them. After a few minutes Sovereign apparently calms down again and releases the hold on an unconscious Tom before looking around the room and walking off into the distance as the scene fades to black. Office ~ Eye of the Beast Featuring Daniel Somers, Justin Famous, The Syndicate and Erin Christoph Somers and Famous can both now be seen sitting backstage in the office of Somers. On the television in front of them the scene just playing itself out can be seen as Sovereign lays waste to both workers backstage and moves on to Tom the Bomb, Barbatos Bronx and perhaps in search of more victims for his brand of punishment. “God damn,” Famous said breaking the silence. “What in the hell were you thinking getting that guy out of the loony bin?” “Just think of the damage that could be done,” Somers replied calmly. “All I have to do is make sure he goes after my enemies and he’ll do all my dirty work for me now.” “That’s some dangerous games you’re playing,” Famous responded not at all convinced. ”Nobody knows Sovereign better than I do Justin,” Somers advised him. “I know exactly what I’m doing.” Before Famous can even respond the sound of the door being slammed open is heard and storming into the room is Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick. Both of them come to a stop in front of Somers looking not at all happy. ”What is this crap Somers?” Dreamer demanded as Somers stood up to stand before the two stars. “You got me and HBC booked to compete in the opening match despite half the competitors for that match not even being here now?” “We already lost our one partner and Badger isn’t here for the other team,” HBC put in. “He’s left to go on some ridiculous modeling job.” ”Yes those were some unforeseen circumstances,” Somers agreed. “I did make this match before all of that happened. Now though I agree there must be some changes made to the card though I don’t see what you two are so upset about. All this does is leave your opponent Boone alone for this match.” “We don’t want Boone,” Dreamer replied. “I want to get my hands on that lucky punk Pierce.” “Make that we,” HBC corrected. “Pierce already has a match tonight with Impact,” Somers began explaining before being cutoff. “We don’t care about that,” Dreamer responded. “Add us into that match or watch us crash it. Either way the match isn’t going to happen like that.” “I think we had enough match interference last week actually,” Somers answered stroking his chin trying to think up another idea. “I got someone I can put out there for the first match actually to go against Boone since he’s apparently the only one left now thanks to Sovereign. As for you two I’m going to give Pierce the rest of the show to find himself two partners to team with him in the main event against you and Impact. And to make things that much more difficult for Troy, Matt Boone cannot be one of the two partners.” “That works just fine for us,” HBC answered before Dreamer had a chance. “We’ll get our payback tonight.” Without waiting for another word to be said the two of them leave the room and slam the door shut as Somers goes back to sit in his chair again. Before he can even sit down though the door can again be heard slamming open but this time walking into the room is the new eVolution Continental Champion Erin Christoph. “I don’t appreciate this Somers,” Christoph shouted as he walked into the room. “Why am I facing Xander again this week in a simplistic match such as this?” “Because I told you too perhaps?” Somers replied sarcastically. “I’m busy Christoph so what do you want?” ”I want to know if my Tiers of eVolution match is going to happen,” Christoph demanded. ”Yes it is,” Somers answered. “On August 7th at X-Rated.” “What?” Christoph said taken by surprise. “What about tonight?” ”Tonight you have a cage match against Xander with the title on the line,” Somers replied. “Your match request is being withheld by me for use at X-Rated when you defend your title against the number one contender who will be decided next week. That is all of course dependent upon you getting by Xander here tonight.” “That won’t be a problem I can promise you that,” Christoph fired back. “You might think this is funny but you’ll pay before it’s all done.” ”I think I’ve done enough of that at your hands Christoph,” Somers answered cryptically. “Do you think I’ve forgotten you? Remember I was a guest of The Unlimited for over a month and I remember exactly who you are. We are far from through my friend. Now get the fuck out.” Christoph taken back again by the harshness of Somers comments takes a step forward but stops short when he notices Famous rising from his seat and stepping over to the two men. Christoph takes a look back at him and then at Somers who has a smug grin on his face before turning and walking out of the room as the scene fades to black and changes to ringside. Ringside ~ The Beginning Featuring Justin Fritz, Jack Tyler, Matt Boone, and ????? The scene fades in from the blackness in front of you to an image of the eVolution logo. The logo slowly fades away into darkness and is replaced with the sounds of explosions blasting throughout the Mellon Arena as “Voices” by Disturbed begins playing through the arena. Six man tags, tag team matches, cell matches, and maybe even a one on one match to boot.” ”Well as we just heard unexpectedly the main event for the evening has already been changed and the Continental Title match has also been changed into a steel cage match between Erin Christoph the champion, and Xander,” Fritz spouted off recapping the first few segments. ”Yes and don’t forget the obvious facts that Eric Graves is not here tonight,” continued Tyler. “And the fact that Sovereign is here and already on a rampage. What the hell is he going to do to Asis later tonight?” “It’s not going to be pretty to say the least,” Fritz agreed. “Sovereign versus Asis with no disqualification? Someone’s going to get hurt. Than also for the first time ever tonight we will see a tag team match take place inside of The Cell! Not only that but it is fought under Mayhem rules which means this fight can legally leave the confines of The Cell!” ”Another one of Somers’s brilliant ideas apparently as he tries to kill off the active roster,” Tyler responded sarcastically. “All I want to see is someone, preferably Eric Knight, get hurt inside that Cell tonight.” “That’s all you ever want to see for some reason,” Fritz replied. “Now last week we saw the eVolution debuts of Shodan and Brad Kelley, two former EdW Platinum Champions. Tonight in a battle of EdW it seems Shodan will team with former EdW Karnage Champion L2J and the two will battle against Brad Kelley and Tommy Michaels also formerly of EdW.” “I don’t like Shodan or L2J but that one should be a good match,” Tyler put in surprisingly. “Kelley and Michaels is one of my dream teams to say the least and it becomes a reality tonight when they destroy L2J and Shodan.” “Easier said than done,” Fritz argued back. “The main event has already taken a whole other form here tonight as well with the addition of The Syndicate as the partners of Impact. Together they take on Troy Pierce and two partners of his own choosing.” ”That’s if he can find two partners dumb enough to go against a team like that,” Tyler added. “Pierce is a walking dead man here tonight and he knows it.” “I’m sure he does,” Fritz replied with sarcasm. “Right now we were scheduled for a six person tag team match but Somers has already cancelled that one on us so I don’t know what we’re going to be doing.” Before Tyler can even respond to Fritz he is cut off by the sounds of “Beat the World” by Pressure 4-5 as “The Natural” Matt Boone makes an unexpected appearance since his match has been cancelled. Boone makes his way quickly down the ramp showing little glitz before sliding right in under the ring ropes and grabbing the mic from Libby Bowman. Justin Fritz: I don’t know what Boone is doing out here without a match now but I guess he is going to tell us. Jack Tyler: He sucks. Matt Boone: You know I came here tonight with the plan of teaching Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick a lesson. Little did I know the two of them would be weaseling their way out of their match with a green rookie who shouldn’t pose any threat to such a dominant duo as The Syndicate. Jack Tyler: Damn right. At least Boone can admit he’s not on their level. Justin Fritz: I don’t think anyone doubts that bit at all. Matt Boone: Now though they aren’t here for this match and that leaves me without one. That doesn’t sit well with me as in order to improve I need matches. Without one tonight that is one week I lose out on working to better myself so right now I’m calling out anyone in the back to come out here and face me one on one. Boone stands in the ring waiting for any sign of life at the top of the ramp. As he waits and drops the mic finally a lone figure can be seen stepping out from behind the curtain. As the camera closes in on the figure you can see the features of former OSW Intercontinental and Tag Team Champion, “The Impact Player” Drake. Drake stands at the top of the ramp and watches Boone down in the ring with a cocky smile as he raises a mic to speak. Drake: Now I’m sure most of you people will remember just who the hell I am but for those few idiots that don’t, my name is Drake and I’m the best damn wrestler you people will ever see. The crowd boos the arrival of Drake as Boone stands in the ring not sure of what Drake’s intentions are out here. Drake: Now for you Boone, I hear you call yourself “The Natural”. That is the biggest insult to every real wrestler out there that I can think of. You’re nothing more than a little punk that has gotten this far my kissing everyone’s ass along the way. You want a fight here tonight than I’d be more than happy to step down to that ring and give you a free wrestling lesson here tonight. Justin Fritz: What the hell? It looks like we have an impromptu match coming forward right here now! Jack Tyler: I can’t believe Drake is here! This is going to be great. We can finally stop listening to this little brat Boone claim to be a natural. You see, the thing that ties them together is that they worked together to take away my title, they took away what I worked for. I’ll see you out there… partner” Brad belted as he patted the cautious Michaels on the back, heading toward the ring. Both men got up from their seats and as Jinx and Black left and as they left the room and closed the door stepping out from around the corner hallway can be seen Eric Knight still wearing a brace around his right arm from Black’s attack a week ago as the scene fades to black.
