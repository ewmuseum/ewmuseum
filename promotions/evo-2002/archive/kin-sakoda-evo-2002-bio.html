Kin Sakoda Biography Real Name Kin Sakoda Hometown Tokyo, Japan Height 6'3 Weight 247 Biography Like Yoshii there is little to know about Kin’s life prior wrestling. Like many Japanese men he was rather in a strict family that put much emphasis on education and bettering oneself. It wasn’t a very popular decision within the Sakoda household when Kin had decided what he would do with his life. The first choice he had made after school was to train and become a professional wrestler. It was at the Ultimo Dragon Gym that Kin had actually begun his training in professional wrestling at the age of 22 and about one year later he had made his pro wrestling debut in Toryumon. He spent several months competing there before he began to move around more often garnering appearances in Zero-One and Pro Wrestling NOAH where he enjoyed a good deal of success. It was that success that had begun to lead to the rumored second life of Kin Sakoda. He had begun to garner more and more attention in Japanese society when he found himself enthralled in the lower echelons of the Yakuza organization through an unknown contact. From that time on the connection continued to live as everyone knew full well that the Yakuza would never let anyone walk away from its organization intact. For Kin though it didn’t seem he was very much against the idea of the rumored connection as he knew full well that it would to his advantage in Japan as no one would be foolish enough to cross him now. How it was he and Yoshii crossed paths is still a mystery but somehow Yoshii, a man who had a bad history with the Yakuza, and Kin had formed an alliance. Was Kin trying to use Yoshii to get himself out of the Yakuza or was Yoshii back in the Yakuza fold willingly?
