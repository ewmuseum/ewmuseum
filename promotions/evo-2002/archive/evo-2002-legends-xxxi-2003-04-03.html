Legends XXXI Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The America West Arena ~ Phoenix, Arizona 7:59PM EST Thursday Night April 3rd, 2003 Unbreakable is now history and many of the superstars in eVolution are feeling the effects of it still as they head into the first Legends following it. All three of the new champions will be in attendance at the show this week and it will feature several huge tag team matches and updates on the conditions of the stars who suffered what appeared at least to be serious injuries at Unbreakable. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Nik VanCore just went through a hellacious battle Sunday night against Xander and now will go one on one with one half of the Tag Team Champions in an effort to try and earn Strictly X a shot at the gold. Jason Relms along with his partner High Impact has been on quite a roll since they arrived in eVolution and it all culminated Sunday night when they won the gold. Now he will have to extend that roll at least one more show if he hopes to overcome the unorthodox style of Nik VanCore. The Syndicate came just shy of capturing the eVolution Tag Team Titles at Unbreakable and now they have to go through the erratic team, to say the least, of Luther Wisdom and the new eVolved Champion Xavier Citizen. Just why these two are teaming up again for this show is still unknown but they are going to have to work together as a unit if they hope to survive this encounter against Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick. At the end of their battle at Unbreakable, New-TV abducted Shocka after Morpheus Omega had stuffed him in the back of an ambulance but all signs point to his being ready and able to compete on Legends this week and he will find himself teaming with two of his Team eVolution partners in Seara and Lee eXodus as they battle Omega, Thug roC, and the newest member of eVolution and New-TV Hedges! These six stars in one match will certainly be volatile and it goes without saying that Unbreakable did not settle any issues that these groups had with one another. Less than a week removed from capturing the Universal Title for the second time, Scott Frost will team up with the returning Sean Mason as the two of them will do battle against The Unlimited’s Asis and Xander in a tag team match. Mason made quite an impact in his return alongside Cameron Griffin at Unbreakable and had an assist in Tyler Davidson’s second victory over Erin Christoph and also made certain Asis didn’t have as eventful a night as he no doubt planned going into the show. Now Asis has his chance along with his volatile partner Xander to get some payback and right the course which The Unlimited have suddenly found themselves going. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Jason Jameson, James Kimson and many of eVolution’s superstars!
