El Agave Diablo Biography Real Name El Agave Diablo Birthday 5.29.84 Height 6'0' Weight 225 Biography Favorite Moves: Broken Down by Style: Capoeira: Au- A cartwheel used to avoid high kicks, strikes with the fists and knees. It can also be used to put distance between himself and his opponent. Folha Seca- a backsault that is done while running forward. It's used to close distances quickly and avoid opponents offensive techniques due to the fact that the peak of backsault is about three quarters of the distance of an average NBA player's vertical jump. A twist can be added to further avoid attacks. Whirling Handstand- Leans back into a handstand and then swings his left leg up to complete the move thus causing him to spin- or whirl- away from his opponent. The move is finished with the Au to further the gap between Agave and his opponents. Switchover- A no hands cartwheel, with one hand gripping below the knee for support. It's used to dodge attacks or maneuver to the side or back of the opponent. Hurricane Kick Reverse Hurricane Kick Rabo de Arraia- High half-circle kick, with both hands placed on the ground and one foot planted for support. Often strikes the temple or the shoulder with great force. Jeet-Kune-Do: Flying Knee Thrust Foot Sweep Scissors Kick Flying Scissors Kick Backflip Kick Spinning Elbow Smash Stepping Front Kick Burmese Boxe: Spinning Elbow Strike Roundhouse Elbow Knee Strikes Explosive Punching combos Judo: Uki Goshi-floating hip throw Kibisu Gaeshi- heel trip Tomoe Nage- Circle Throw Kani Basami- Scissors Throw Sankaku Jime- Triangle Choke from the front position utilizing the a figure four with the legs to choke via head and arm Hadaka Jime- rear naked choke with the forearm across the throat. Katah Jime- one arm chicken wing choke Wrestling Style El Agave Diablo is a practioner of Jeet Kune Do, Capoeira,Judo, and Burmese Boxe. Agave is not very methodical in his approach to fighting. He prefers to fly at his opponents and try to overwhelm them with his eclectic mixture of kicks, punches, and takedowns. However, he does like to work his opponents over with strikes to the head. He prefers to aim his strikes at the nose, cheek-bones, and eyes. Finisher Move El Agave Finisher Description Agave places his opponent, who is still groggy from the Termination Point, so that their lbows touch their knees. He then underhooks both of their arms and lifts them up so that they are completely upside down. He holds them like that for a few seconds and then does a sit out, driving their head/neck into the mat. Finisher Setup Move Termination Point Setup Description this is usually done after a particularly potent flurry of strikes. It's a high cartwheel kick transforming into an axe kick at the apex of the maneuver It's made to take the force of the cartwheel kick at it's peak and add the power of the axe kick.
