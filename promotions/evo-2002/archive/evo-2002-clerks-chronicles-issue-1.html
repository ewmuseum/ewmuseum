Well since I am listed as a column writer I figured that it might be a good time to write up my first column. As I sit here looking at Evolution and the stars that have signed to the fed I can only wonder what is next for Evolution. Some of the feuds carry over from EDW, which eVo has many ties to. Xander and Malcolm King trade words as the build up for a show down between the two is near. Matt Boone and Troy Pierce have teamed up, with Pierce pushing Boone to take over the place that Pierce once held. But many sources have told me that while Pierce is helping Boone, he isn't going to be complacent over just giving up his spot in eVo. But as a former Universal Champion I don't say that I can blame him. Others that were in EDW (which are most of the wrestlers that I have crossed paths with) are also present, BDD and HBC are making their presence felt, alongside Asis and L2J, who seemingly have also teamed up much like Boone and Pierce. The connection between the two should be cleared up as soon as Asis gets out of jail. The man known only as Sovereirgn has also made his impact felt. With his interference in the American Nightmare main event many are left to wonder what if any connection is made between [name withheld], Sovereign, and Pierce. Is he here to punish [name withheld] or to protect him? Shodan is also listed as a wrestler in eVo, he made quite an impact in one of the royal rumbles that EDW held towards the end of its run, will he once again do the same in eVo? One of the major wildcards in eVo is Tommy Michaels. A man who has crossed paths with many superstars in eVo. He may have a bigger target on his head than even "The DiscoKid". Well as for what is to come, rumors run rampant that one man with the initials BK wll arrive in eVvo soon, and another with tthe initials KN, will also show up, but this time he won't be a wrestler but a "fighter." With these two one man should definelty watch his back because everything he has worked for may soon come tumbling down around him. But that man may have a spectacular card up his sleeve as a man who has been out of the ring for almost a year may be poised to return soon. Dante Hicks
