Hate Biography Real Name Shaun Brooks Hometown Compton, California Birthday 02.09.1977 Height 6'4' Weight 252 Biography Shaun Brooks, AKA Hate, was born on February Ninth, 1977, in Compton, California. While most children his age went to sleep with a soothing bedtime story from there mom or dad, Shaun was forced asleep with the not so soothing sounds of police sirens and gunshots. Life isn't always a fair place, and Shaun Brooks knows this better than the mass majority. Although, not much is known about the life and times of Shaun Brooks, enough is known to the point where it's obvious this guy isn't your average, friendly white guy. It's known that Hate never lived a healthy lifestyle, and never made friends or got a long with people. But if you were to ask many people about Shaun, you would be told that he is a hell of a lot smarter than he appears. Being locked in a basement for days on end, being attacked at any random time by groups of people, and being told he was mentally ill were just some of the distorted elements that made up Shaun's childhood. Shaun was banned from highschool after assaulting several teachers and students through out many different school districts. Local authorities state that he is better behind bars, which is a rather strong opinion. Granted, he spent five years of his young life in a maximum security prison, he was never honestly given a chance in life. Alcoholism basically consumed a large percentage of his freetime over the years, but now he's gotten into working out and wrestling. Evo is likely to be this madman's next major promotion he's worked for. Don't look past the fact, he is still an alcoholic and seems to be on the edge of bi-polar disorder. More is being learned about this monster of a man as the days go on... Maybe you should just take a look for yourself?
