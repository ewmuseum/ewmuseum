Legends XLI Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Nassau Coliseum ~ Uniondale, New York 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night June 25th, 2003 The final show before A New Dawn is set to go down live from The Nassau Coliseum in Uniondale, New York where the Universal Title will be on the line. Tyler Davidson made certain last week that Xander would not have an easy road to A New Dawn on Sunday night and the final task set ahead of Xander is the returning James Kimson with the Universal Title hanging in the balance. As though that wasn’t enough the Cell Division will go up to standards never before seen as there will be two previews of sorts happening throughout the night for the Chambers of Survival with one triple threat match and another tornado tag team match both taking place inside of the Cell. Also this week will be the debut of Drake Nathanial Dukes in a handicap match as he battles Ryan Justice and Scott Evans. What will happen at Legends? No one knows, watch or be eVolved! This will be the second match in a best of three series between The BodySnatchers and America’s Guardians with Tag Team Title shots hanging in the balance. Last week it was The BodySnatchers who managed to steal a victory away from America’s Guardians and put the British/Russian duo at a serious disadvantage now for the series as they know full well that one more loss and they can kiss their title shot goodbye. Last week Justice and Evans were busy beating the hell out of each other and now this week the two of them will find themselves as a tag team against the newcomer Drake Nathanial Dukes, the former UFC and PRIDE fighter now turned wrestler in eVolution. Drake made an impression last week when he showed up at the end of the Justice and Evans match but now it’s likely to be Justice and Evans who make quite a painful impression on Drake. Last week saw very impressive debuts from both Machine and Terlayne Sterling in Cell competition as they each made short work of their respective opponents. Now the two of them will get a chance to square off with Trevor Norman also thrown into the mix provided he can keep himself out of the clutches of Marcus Escobar and the local authorities long enough. This match will certainly be a brutal affair and just a small preview of what A New Dawn will have in store for everyone as The Cell Championship will be decided in the Chambers of Survival! Last week Malcolm King got a very rude surprise courtesy of Tyler Davidson. He was now a fighter in the Cell Division and his first duty as one of course will be to go into this match against two of the top performers in the division to date as he teams with his protégé Question Beyond X to battle against Damon O’Halloran and Ali Khadafi. The relationship between Khadafi and O’Halloran is still a bit of a mystery at this point but it seems to have worked out well for both men thus far at the very painful expense of QBX who has been on the receiving end of both men’s anger management. Of course being a tag team match inside of The Cell and with no ropes there are no tags to be made as all four men will be going at it in this match at one time which is certain to be quite chaotic! Siege has been on a warpath since his arrival in eVolution and has yet to lose a match but this week things could come to an end for him as he must battle against not just Yoshii Tanaka this time but Deacon as well. Yoshii had almost managed to pull off a victory against Siege a few weeks ago by himself but now with the help of Deacon his chances are no doubt a whole lot better. James Kimson made his return to eVolution last week during an interview making it know to everyone that he is entering himself into the Caged Rumble at A New Dawn for a shot at the Universal Title. Little did Kimson know at the time that the current champion, Xander would take offense at Kimson’s claims of being the next champion this early and confront him about it. After a brief battle Kimson had Xander on the ropes when he made his way out of the ring only to be stopped short by Tyler Davidson. Despite having his own shot at the gold at A New Dawn, Tyler once again decided that Xander would have to defend his title in a match against James Kimson this week and during that match Tyler himself would be the one that does the refereeing. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Jason Jameson, Thug roC, and many of eVolution’s superstars!
