Legends LIV Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From Osaka, Japan ~ Osaka Dome 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night October 15th, 2003 In The Ring ~ Special Faith Featuring James Kimson and Leya Forth It’s Time.... Let the Hype Begin. Legends kicks off after the pyro and music with the sounds of Stupify and the Universal Champion appearing at the top of the sound stage. The fans have blown the roof off the building with their cheers and support for the man who at Digital Destruction with cracked ribs defeated Shocka to continue his reign as champion. James Kimson walks down the ramp, leather jacket, blue Jeans and a plain white T-shirt and a baseball cap on backwards with Hype written across it. Over his shoulder is the beauty that is the eVo Universal Title, the lights dance off its golden brilliance as he steps up to the ring apron from the stairs and in between the ropes into the ring. The fans don’t discontinue the cheering. They in fact grow louder as Kimson mounts the turnbuckles showing the belt to the fans, the flash bulbs popping across the arena in unison as Kimson poses. After he reaches the final corner and shows the belt to the fans at the end he dismounts, and reaches behind his back and pulls out a mic that was secured to the small of his back. The music dies, but Kimson stands in the center of the ring, it’s time to speak, the first chance to since Digital Destruction, his fans wanted to hear his voice. He scratches his unshaven beard as the fans chant his name, cheer him for just standing there. Something he never really gets used to. But here is, in the center of a wrestling ring, surrounded but 20,000 people who paid to see him. “Ok now calm down people. I came out here to get a few things off my chest here and talk about a few ‘issues at hand’” said Kimson as the fans quieted down. Kimson paused, he began to pace slowly. “Now Last week, Shocka had some interesting things to say about Digital Destruction. It was short... it was to the point and it was basically a lot of sore loser talk about how I whipped his ass at the Show! Now I was more then willing to come out here last week. But strange things happen and Leya Forth, and I guess the entire committee saw it fit to give the Universal Champ a day off.” Boos, yeah Boos they happen. Kimson’s control over the crowd as pretty complete, he never liked to hear people boo his sister, but it was deserved. “I don’t mind a day off, but only when it’s the right time. Because Legends is my time and I am supposed to be here, in this ring doing what I do best and that’s wrestle, that’s entertain and do my damn Job. Leya might not like that my job is be the Champion. She might not like the fact that my job was to whip Shocka’s ass. But I did it, and I will continue to do my job every day of the week, and any time of the day.” said Kimson. “Now, Faith is coming up. It’s the biggest Show of the year and James Kimson is the Champion, so that means he’s going to headline the show. So what do you people think? Who should be the Number One contender to the Title?” asked Kimson then looked out to the crowd. Names were showed out, names like Asis, Jameson, Scott Frost, name after name. “Yeah there are a lot of worthy contenders out there.” Said Kimson. “But I’ve beaten all those Curtain Jerkers!” said Kimson. “I’d love to wrestle anyone in that locker room at Faith but I think that the world deserves to know who it’s gonna be.” Kimson paused, looked up to the entrance when pretty much on cue “My Way” by Limp Bizkit hit and out walked Leya Forth to a huge round of boos. The Beauty in black she was tonight, black business suit with a skirt tonight instead of pants. She glared towards Kimson with an intense and hateful look as Kimson smiled back in the face of the Devil. “Okay James, I’m going to give you credit when it’s deserved. Yeah you beat Shocka at Digital Destruction and you did it with cracked ribs after the beating he gave you the week before. But James, you have to understand one thing. That’s that your ass is mine regardless. Regardless of if you beat Shocka, Asis or anyone else you’ve beaten some day your going to drop the ball and I’m going to the one that smacks it right out of your hands!” said Leya to huge heat. “But James the problem is that I gave you last week to rest up, I gave it to you to celebrate, to enjoy life. Because in the end you are my Brother and I love you. But now all that is over starting right here tonight. Because you want your Number 1 contender picked so badly I can tell you who you’ll be facing at Faith and even the match stipulation! Because as you assumed James, I am one step ahead of you. I knew that there was a good chance you could beat Shocka at Digital Destruction. But I’m going to tell you now James, there’s no way on God’s Green Earth that you will beat Shocka at Faith!” “Shocka is still the Number One Contender?” said Fritz over the headset for the 1st time, allowing the action to happen uninterrupted.” “Yes James, Shocka isn’t done with you yet. I know you’re banged up pretty good from your match at Digital Destruction. I know your ribs hurt, your body aches, your neck isn’t %100 and never will be” said Leya. “I know though James that never once have you backed down from a challenger at any time. That’s why not only will you defend the title at Faith, but you have to work to get there! Because tonight you will defend that Title in the middle of that ring tonight against... Jason Jameson!” said Leya. The fans boo, Kimson forced to defend against one of the best wrestlers in the world on 2 hours notice, and injured at that. Kimson looked down to his feet for a moment, trying to contain his anger at the unfairness of this. How long ago did she tell Jameson he’d have this match he thought. “But that’s tonight. You want to know about Faith don’t you. Yeah, well the Match will be very simple James. It’ll be a wrestling match, but with 1 special stipulation. That will be that there’s going to be a special Guest Referee for the match.” said Leya. “A Special Ref?” said Tyler. “Who could that be?” said Fritz. “Yeah, a special official to call the match right down the middle. And that special ref is going to be...” Leya reached behind her back and produced a Ref shirt and slung it over her shoulder. “ME!” screamed Leya into the microphone. The fans were shocked. “So James, I’m hoping to God that you make it to Faith with that Title on your shoulder because I want to be the one that makes the 3 count on you and watch your dreams slip away like you did to me and my Husband. I want you to know that it was ME that finished your title reign. I want you to know and to feel my satisfaction when I ruin your career at the biggest wrestling event of your career. Making you a loser in the biggest match of your life! I hope it was all worth it James! Because it will be for me!” Leya threw the mic down and walked off the set, the fans booing, in total shock. Hunt clamps his right hand around Tok’n’s face and whispers with a deep snarl into his right ear… “Greetings…tart” are the words that echo around Tok’n’s head as he grimaces. Hunt then lands a vicious slap right across the left cheek of Tok’n, which seems to reverberate throughout the hallway. Tok’n doesn’t even have a chance to reach for his face when Hunt grabs his arm and whips him into an opposite wall. THUD~! Tok’n reaches for his back as Hunt strolls over and proceeds to unleash a stomping marathon, forcing Tok’n to slide down the wall. By the time Hunt has finished, Tok’n is slumped up against the wall, with a red hand imprint on his left cheek. Hunt then turns to the corner and points at the dazed Tok’n, he speaks softly…trying not to draw any attention to himself. “Hey Sid, you like what I’m doing here, camache?” he snarls as he lands another stomp to Tok’n’s chest. “Well sit back and enjoy Stage Two of Operation Slap, mooch!” he continues before reaching down and pulling Tok’n up by the throat. He then grabs Tok’n by the hair on the back of the head and leads him down a hallway, ramming his head off any item that come into view…a transportation trunk, a door and a few tables with equipment. The camera attempts to keep up as each impact is felt with a sickening and vicious thud. Tok’n grimaces and groans with each headshot and before time, some small trickles of blood start to drip from his forehead. Hunt is surprisingly quiet and doesn’t utter a word as he drags a beaten Tok’n down the hallway, eventually pausing and stopping with a sick smirk on his face. He glances back at Tok’n, whose face is slowly being covered with blood, and starts to laugh manically. The camera manages to spin around to notice a collection of large pans of glass, collectively upright and organised in the corner of a small lobby way. There are a few backstage workers, who scurry away upon spotting the commotion as Hunt’s laughing suddenly stops. He glances between the glass panes and Tok’n, who is too out of it to realise what is coming up next. “Ladies and Gentlemen, we are ready for take off…please fasten your seat belt as we are in for a bumpy ride!” Hunt hollers prior to charging at the glass, dragging Tok’n behind him and eventually hurling the dazed foe into the glass and creating a huge crash upon impact. CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAASH!!!!!!!!!!!!! Glass shatters everywhere, as Tok’n is in the middle of the mess and blood surrounds the area. A few of the backstage workers wince in amazement of the impact. “Tok on that, camache…and tell QBX that he’s next!” hollers Hunt as he stares down at his handy work before backing off with a twisted smirk on his face. The camera follows Hunt, who then turns and storms away before panning back to the chaotic scene with a motionless Tok’n covered in glass and blood.
