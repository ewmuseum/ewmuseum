Legends XLIV Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Anfield ~ Liverpool, England 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night July 23rd, 2003 To the shock of a great many people eVolution managed to pull out another show though a great deal of people were not present for the show both in the production truck and even several of the wrestlers. The show had to go on however and go on it did live from The United Kingdom where Legends will now settle into The Anfield in Liverpool, England with only one week remaining before ReVelations which will go down live from Old Trafford in Manchester. There should be quite a show for all the fans in attendance as those who did not make the original trip overseas from the United States will catch up with the rest of the eVolution crew for this show as they all prepare for ReVelations on Sunday night July 27th. Malcolm King found out well over a month ago that he would now called upon to do a good deal of fighting inside of the eVolution’s Cell and now he will have a one on one fight where Question Beyond X and the rest of King’s Army will not be around to give him a helping hand. King’s opponent however will be the lackluster Othinel who has had no success whatsoever since arriving in eVolution and his only career highlight thus far has been an unwanted run-in with Terlayne Sterling a few weeks back. Speaking of Terlayne Sterling he will also see some further action as he goes one on one with the aforementioned Question Beyond X. Both men have found themselves a good deal of success thus far in eVolution since their arrival but it seems that the advantage might sit with QBX in this one as Terlayne could perhaps be having a bit to worry about from outside of the Cell in this encounter as Marcus Escobar and Geneva are still quite intent on getting their hands on Terlayne. Last week Siege teamed with Sean Mason against Thug roC and Carl Cash and it was Siege who picked up the pinfall over Carl Cash after Thug roC all but abandoned his partner in the ring to a vicious double team to end the match. Now Thug roC can get a little bit of redemption in this battle with Siege and also send a message to Sean Mason who no doubt will be paying close attention to this match as he and Thug roC are scheduled for a battle at ReVelations as well with the Continental Title on the line. Two more bitter battles are thrown into one match here as Jameson will team with Hedges against Morpheus Omega and the eVolved Champion, Scott Evans. Hedges and Omega are no strangers to one another as the two men used to be close friends but with the destruction of New-TV neither of the men have been on friendly terms as of late as Omega even went so far to cost Hedges the eVolved Title a few weeks back. Of course Jameson and Omega still have some very serious issues brewing between the two of them as they have come to blows every time they are in the same vicinity even when they are supposed to be tag team partners. Hedges and Scott Evans will also of course have a match coming up at ReVelations as Scott Evans will again defend his eVolved Title against Hedges at the big pay per view event if he’s able to survive this match. Sean Mason has been quite a busy Continental Champion as he has defended the title against just about all comers but this match could be one of his toughest challenges to date. Carl Cash has held many titles in the past and has been a star in a great deal of promotions. Mason will no doubt be put to the test in this battle for his Continental Title as Cash would love nothing more than to pick up his first victory in eVolution and also capture the Continental Title as he heads into ReVelations and a match with Siege. Last week Seara again tried to get her hands on Xander during his hardcore match against Bob but she ran into something that she didn’t expect, Dilyah Love. What exactly the newcomer’s intentions were last week they did not sit well with Seara in the least and she immediately wanted a chance to get her hands on the newcomer but the only catch was she would have to find a partner as Dilyah would have Greatness on her side. James Kimson was the man to step up and team with Seara for this match but the question also remains just what is the deal between Greatness and Seara? Everyone saw Greatness reveal himself to Seara a few weeks ago but there has been no mention of it since then by either of the two. Xander meanwhile will no doubt be present in some form for this match as he has some apparent link with both Greatness and Dilyah Love now though he might have other things to worry including James Kimson and his opponent for later in the night. Shocka was attacked last week by Greatness and Xander and he demanded a match against either one of the two men. To the shock of Shocka he found out that it was Xander who accepted the challenge and even went so far as to agree to put his Universal Title on the line against him. This was the opportunity that Shocka has long been waiting for as he could finally have the chance to recapture the Universal Title that he lost back in February. For Xander it will be his last match before his huge battle with James Kimson at ReVelations and Shocka will certainly take Xander to his very limits and back in an attempt to recapture the gold. Also you can expect to see a very important announcement from Cameron Griffin concerning ReVelations for the Cell Division and also the contract signing between Damon O’Halloran and Ali Khadafi. As Kimson turns to reorient himself Xander's right back near him and reaches out and Xander grabs Kimson by the arm, swinging him around, and driving him down to the mat. Xander held on and now maneuvers himself into some variation of a cross arm breaker. The same arm that was damaged not too long ago by Xander himself. As Xander wrenches the arm ways that it was never meant to bend some referees and agents come out from the back to try and separate the pair. After a minute or two of pulling, and pleading Xander finally lets go. The agents push Xander to the ropes, and get him to leave the ring as the rest of the crew in the ring check on Kimson. Outside the ring Xander picks up his wrench and holds it high in triumph as the crowd lets him have it.
