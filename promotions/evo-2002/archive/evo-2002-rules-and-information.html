Rules and Information

In order to be successful here in eVolution you are going to have to be active on a regular basis. eVolution is now a 100% angle fed. What does this mean for you? Simply put any angles or segments you submit for the shows and your character can at times help you in winning the match. This also means that you don't always have to sit here and write out 300 line roleplays in order to try and win a match. I do fully encourage the wrestlers to take a more active role in developing their own angles rather than relying on the staff to do everything. If you simply sit back and give no suggestions or feedback to us than you aren't likely to get what it is you want. The more feedback we get, the better we can make things for everyone. Roleplay Rules 1) There is no set roleplay limit. Since the weekly shows are based mostly on angles you are free to roleplay as much as you want. 2) The RP deadline will be 12 midnight EST the day prior to the show. 3) Do not use other handlers wrestlers in your roleplays without their expressed consent. 4) You have two weeks from the time of your acceptance to roleplay for the first time. If you don't I will take you off the roster no questions asked. I have no time or desire to deal with inactive handlers. 5) Roleplays will be called Timeline's in this fed, and though I probably don't have to mention this, they are never to be mentioned in one your writings. Always refer to them as interviews when you are in character. The Rules 1) Do not bug me asking when the results will be up. The more you ask the longer it will take for them to get finished. When the results are done they will be posted on the site and a message will be on the main page. Check the main page instead of asking me. 2) If I hear of anyone in eVolution causing any problems with other members OOC I will be quick to act. I will not allow people to start OOC wars here and if it is a regular occurrence from someone they will be fired real quickly. This also goes for anyone who complains to me on a regular basis about the outcomes of their matches. If you keep complaining you won't be here long because I won't be dealing with the crap. 3) eVolution will be for fake wrestlers only. If you got a cheap rip off of the Rock or Stone Cold you want to use then take them somewhere else. 4) Wrestlers must be kept realistic. No one is to be of insane size or with insane abilities. This means no 10 foot tall giants and no alien wrestlers or anything like that. 5) The most important rule about eVolution though is to just sit back and have fun. That's what all of this is meant to be about so try not to forget that. The Wrestler Titles 1) The eVolution Universal Championship has been one of the titles carried over from the old EdW. The lineage of the EdW Universal Title still lives on in eVolution with the title held by such stars as Malcolm King, Cori Simmons, Troy Pierce, and Justin Famous. 2) The eVolution Continental Title is another title carried over, this time from nEw. The title had never been won by anyone in the short tenure of the company. 3) The eVolution eVolved Title is the newest title to be brought into eVolution. The title is up for all competitors of any gender or weight class and is fought with random stipulations. 4) The eVolution Tag Team Titles have also been carried over from EdW where such teams as Total Chaos, The Syndicate, and Domestic Violence had battled constantly for the titles. Television 1) The one and only weekly show eVolution offers at the moment is Legends on Wednesday nights. Legends will for the time being feature both wrestlers and fighters. There is the possibility of another show being added but it would mean an increase in the staff to do it. I'm always willing to take on more staff so anyone interested in a staff position need do nothing more than contact me. 2) Pay Per Views: As always we will have one monthly pay per view. Unlike most feds though our pay per views won't fall on Sundays. The pay per views do take place on Sunday nights and usually fall on the last Sunday of the month. a) January: New Year's eVolution b) February: Tainted Love-Includes Hell’s Fury match. c) March: Unbreakable d) April: End of Days e) May: From The Ashes f) June: A New Dawn-Caged Rumble match for Universal Title number one contendership. g) July: Revelations h) August: X-Rated i) September: Digital Destruction j) October: Faith k) November: Rise of the Legends-Features the Road To Glory tournament matches and finals. Winner will receive a shot at the title of his choice before or at the next pay per view. l) December: Nightmare Before Christmas Staff Positions 1) eVolution is always looking for new people to join the staff. The more members we can have for staff the better we can things for all of you. Staff positions are open to people on the roster or not. It doesn't matter to me so long as you are going to be reliable and able to do your work on a weekly basis. The following are the positions currently open and who holds them if anyone. All positions are open to one person at a time unless otherwise listed. a) The CEO of eVolution. Held by: [name withheld] Description: He will do anything that needs to be done around the site including writing results, occasional columns, news reporting, and booking shows. Any other work that needs to be done will also be his responsibility. The CEO is also responsible for the development and booking of the Universal and Continental Divisions. He also has final say in all new hirings and assists the other division directors when help is needed. b) The Tag Team Division Director. Held by: Malcolm King Description: The Tag Team Director is currently in charge of the booking and development of the tag team division. All new hires are done through the Tag Team Director c) The Cell Division Director. Held by: [name withheld] Description: The Cell Division Director is responsible for the booking and development of the Cell Division. All new hires for the division are done through the director. d) News Reporters. Held by: [name withheld] Description: All news reporters would be responsible for posting news about the fed on various websites. This includes any interfeds eVolution may be part of at the time. News reporter jobs are open to more than one person at any particular time. e) Columnists. Held by: Jeremy Hunter, Max Dickens, and various roster members Description: They will write weekly or monthly columns for eVolution relating to any topic they choose. Also will be looking for one person to write a detailed preview and review of each show held. Also open to more than one person. f) Designers Held by: Rich V. (web designer), Josh S.(graphics) Description: They will be in charge of making the graphics for eVolution that you will be seeing on the site. This includes PPV images, TV logos, and splash pages. Open to more than one person. g) Poser Artist Held by: Jeremy C. Description: They will be in charge of making posers for eVolution. This is open to more than one as well. If we have more than one poser artist the wrestlers will choose which one they want to do their poser. h) Writers Held by: [name withheld], Malcolm King, [name withheld], Jake H., Rob B., and various members of the roster. Description: These people will be responsible for helping to write the shows. This includes show segments, and matches. They are expected to write at least one match a week, sometimes but rarely more. Open to as many people as possible. 2) If you are interested in applying for any of these positions please email me at [detail withheld] Roster 1) Currently the eVolution roster is going to be open to only 20 singles wrestler. Anyone applying as a tag team is not subject to this limit. If you check the roster and see that we have twenty people already it's a safe bet that we are not accepting any new talent. 2) There are a few recognitions for accomplishments in eVolution. a) Grand Slam Champion: Win Universal, Continental, eVolved, and Tag Team Titles. b) Road To Glory Champion: Win the Road To Glory Tournament. c) Ultimate Champion: Win every Singles Title, the Tag Team Titles, and the Road To Glory Tournament. 3) Anytime a handler needs time off for any reason please contact me. I have no problems at all with giving someone time off. Just let me know about it to the best of your ability and not just disappear. 4) Stables are of course allowed and at the time have a limit of five members. That can be changed to include more in certain circumstances at the discretion of the CEO or President. 5) When it comes to angles you can use other wrestlers in them but they first must be approved by the CEO or President who will then discuss it with the other wrestler if they see the need too.
