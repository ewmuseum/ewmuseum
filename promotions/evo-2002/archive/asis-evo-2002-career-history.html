Asis Career History Wrestler Entrance: A lone guitar plays quietly over a dark arena, the fans know who is coming out and the flashing pictures of Asis’ face on the titantron, red eyes glowing beneath his hair, confirms it for them. The guitar continues to build up as the bass joins it and the lights begin to flicker as if someone is turning the power on and off and as the first heavy riff an explosion erupts on stage and the lights burn a bloody red. In you I see further. Can it last forever? Identify your patients, XY configurations. Words are lost in your eyes. One thought inside my heart. Drop addicts in the mixture, falcons have blown the fixture. From Autumn To Ashes’ “Alive Out Of Habit” blasts through the arena as Asis walks out onto the stage and starts to slowly make his way down to the ring, dragging the Universal Title along the floor by it's strap. He cracks his neck from side to side as he stares intently at the ring, ignoring the taunts of all those around him. Think of me as days pass us by. Shards of glass. Skin of gold. Steal my breath. Blood runs cold. Violet waves. Oceans blue. All my love. Lost in you. In you I see further. Can it last forever? Asis gets down to the ring and stands at the apron briefly, his head down, face masked by his hair. He then rolls into the ring and paces around as the lights return to normal, before stopping and sarcastically holding the Universal Title in the air and then throwing it down on the mat. Appearance: White and fairly pale from years indoors and in the shadows, massively built like Batista. Natural silver hair, black at the roots that at the front reaches just below his chin and at the back reaches the bottom of his neck. His hair is always messy and raggedy looking, never any recognisible style about it. Has a strange eye disorder which makes the pigment of his eyes red with almost no pupils. Has a scar running from the corner of his right eye to the bottom of his jaw. Normally stares at people from underneath his hair and this accompanied with his red eyes creates an eerie and intimidating effect. In ring attire is black baggy trousers with fire on both legs. On the left leg it stretches all the way up to the top, but wraps round the leg, in certain places covering more of the trousers than others. On the right leg it reaches just below the knee. Plain black boots underneath. He has black tape wrapped round his hands in a similar style to boxers, with the tape reaching about 1/4 of the way up his forearms. He has one black elbow support on his right arm. Out of the ring he wears a pair of baggy black trousers, a tight black top, a pair of black boots and a black leather trenchcoat over the top. Biography: Zak Samson was born with natural silver hair and also an eye disorder which made the pigment of his eyes blood red. This did not mean his parents loved him any less, but kids at school didn't understand and teased Zak from an early age. Because of this, Zak gained a very short temper and was prone to lashing violently at people, which resulted in him having to move schools a lot. Constantly having to move put a strain on his parent's marriage, and at 13 years old Zak's mother walked out on him and his father. Zak's father became an alcoholic and started to beat him. After getting kicked out of school for the last time and having had enough of his dad beating him, Zak left home at 18 and started living on the streets suriving the best he could, even if it meant stealing or worse... So, at 6'5, 290lbs and the age of 21, Zak decided to get into wrestling, to 'express' himself. This basically meant to cause as many people pain as possible to make up for all the hurt he'd felt in his life. Using the name Asis (he wouldn't respond if you called him Zak now) he started going through the indy feds like a knife through hot butter and ended up in Daniel Somers' EdW. However he was only there for a few months before it closed down, but through his contact with Somers he secured himself a place in eVolution. In his personal life over the last year and a half Asis learned that his mother had died in a car accident and found the man responsible to be the very man she'd left him and his father for. Asis got in a lot of trouble with the police when he found where the man lived, but the man himself, one James Orcs, is now in prison. Since being in the company itself, he's been a Rising Star, Continental and Tag Team Champion, turned on supposed friends, retired legends, caused more pain and suffering than thought imaginable and been a part of the most powerful stable in wrestling history. After beating all competition on the singles roster, he gained a unrelenting contempt for the business itself and set out on a quest to take the respect and the magic of wrestling away from all those that held it dear. He beat James Kimson at Tainted Love. He became Universal Champion. At the age of 23, Asis has accomplished all that a professional wrestler can in eVolution, but he's not done yet. He won't be happy until he's destroyed the business for everyone. And he's not even started yet.
