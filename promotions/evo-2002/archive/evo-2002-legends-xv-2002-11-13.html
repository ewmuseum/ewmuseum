Legends XV Transcript Live on OSE! From The Pepsi Center ~ Denver, Colorado 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night November 13th, 2002 The Road to Glory continues this week with another action packed edition of Legends live from Denver, Colorado. After the events of last week there is a lot left in question including whether or not some superstars still have a career left ahead of them. The stable wars have erupted throughout eVolution with the Notorious, Unlimited, Omen Brotherhood, and New-TV all trying to win over their supremacy. Tonight The Notorious gets the biggest chance to do that as one of their members gets a shot at some Universal gold and the chance to become the sole holder for the title. Last week it was Jay Stylez Junior with a baseball bat that did some serious damage to Sean Mason and his chances in the Road to Glory Tournament. This week Mason is going to look for a chance to extract some revenge against Stylez. Mason went to Thug roC of all people last week and demanded this match be signed and for him to be given the opportunity of some payback. Initially reluctant to sign the match, Thug roC eventually did with some encouragement from the new commissioner Bobby Stormbringer. Hillard came off a great performance two weeks ago against Shocka which fell just a bit short. Now this week he must take on a very dangerous man in the form of Jinosyde. Jinosyde was less than happy after he was screwed out of the Road to Glory against HBC last week by Dreamer. This week he looks to take some frustrations out on Hillard. One match is all that stands between a team and the eVolution Tag Team Titles. The Unlimited have already advanced to the finals with a controversial victory last week over Sovereign and L2J. Now this week Jason Jameson and Seara will team together against Shocka and Jason Steele of New-TV. As the rules state concerning tag teams any members of a group can compete in the match so the listing of Jason Steele and Shocka could very well be different come show time if Thug roC chooses. Jameson and Seara however are locked into their spots in this one. The wildcard remains just what is Jason Jameson’s answer to the offers of The Notorious? Big Daddy Dreamer returns to action this week in the Road to Glory tournament with a match against Ben Juventud. Juventud is another newcomer being given a chance in this one against the always dangerous Dreamer. The question is just how close to one hundred percent is Dreamer? All saw last week that his right eye was heavily bandaged from the assault at the hands of HBC and Jinx at Faith and that could very well be his undoing in the end. Newcomer Kai Kua gets a huge opportunity this week thanks to his victory over Mark Steele last week. He finds himself entered into the Road to Glory for a one on one encounter against the mysterious Strictly Prohibited. Prohibited had a hand in Kai’s victory last week but his motives were certainly some of his own and no one has yet to be able to get a straight answer from the mysterious masked man. Kai isn’t looking for any answers however as the only thing on his mind is victory and moving on in the tournament. Under the orders of Xander, Asis made quite a mess of a few people one week ago. This week he looks to continue that on his way through the Road to Glory against Xavier Citizen. This one certainly isn’t just Citizen versus Asis however as it’s a one on one battle between The Unlimited and The Omen Brotherhood. A battle which has been brewing for a while now. Both men are looking for their chance to continue on in the Road to Glory but only one man can make that leap. After Sovereign’s actions last week against L2J, it was Scott Frost who came down to the rescue. It was a rescue that Sovereign certainly did not appreciate as he had every intention of ending L2J’s career. Whether or not he succeeded is still in question but tonight Scott Frost will look to avenge his fallen friend and bring the Universal Title home to Notorious. A Frost win however, will even cost L2J his share of that title if he’s still able to defend it and that is something that could cause some trouble in the very near future. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Xander, The HeartBreak Chick, Jinosyde, and many more! Jack Tyler: Smartest thing I’ve seen L2J do. Would you risk your ass to help the man that’s trying to get your universal title.
