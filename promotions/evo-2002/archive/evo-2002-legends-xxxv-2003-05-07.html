Legends XXXV Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Aloha Stadium ~ Honolulu, Hawaii 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night May 7th, 2003 eVolution returns to action fresh off of End of Days last weekend with an explosive show where the fallout will no doubt continue to be felt. The battles at End of Days had wrapped up some massive wars but just as many had begun as will be evident on Legends. We’ll see the debut of a new tag team in America’s Guardians as well as a huge main event match between two old adversaries in Shocka and Erin Christoph. Also expected to be in the house will of course be the Universal Champion Scott Frost, Xander, and James Kimson all with something to say about the events of End of Days. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Two former tag team partners battle in the first of two matches to determine a new number one contender to the Continental Title. At End of Days Yoshii Tanaka turned on his former ally Tyler Davidson and now this week he battles his other partner in Nik VanCore. What the reasons were behind Yoshii’s recent decision to turn his back on his friends still remains a mystery but with a title shot at stake he’s certainly not going to let any old feelings get in the way of what he has to do. The former Tag Team Champions have hit a bit of a speed bump in their eVolution career following their near destruction at the hands of Twisted Reality a couple of weeks ago. This week they find themselves the first test for a highly regarded new team in eVolution America’s Guardians. The British Bomber and his Russian tag team partner, Molotov will make their debut and try to get off to an immediate start in eVolution at the expense of Team Network. The hostilities between The Syndicate and Twisted Reality picked up at End of Days and continue this week but in a much more different sort of contest. SkyNight and The HeartBreak Chick will compete against one another with the fans as the judge in a lingerie contest to determine just which of the woman is truly the hottest and has the fans most “support”. Morpheus Omega and Big Daddy Dreamer will no doubt have some sort of presence at ringside for this one though so long as they aren’t in lingerie too the fans probably won’t care. The only thing known about this one is that Malcolm Luther Xavier will once again defend his eVolved Title. The only question is who he’s going to defend against. Shocka has finally finished his battle with New-TV and now it seems he has found himself thrust into a battle with an old nemesis of his in Erin Christoph. The two of them had some epic battles in the past over the Continental Title and now they both find themselves in top contention for the Universal Title, a title that Christoph has never held but was only inches away from capturing at End of Days. In the past it was Shocka who defeated Christoph to capture the Continental Title but now Erin has a lot more friends in a lot higher places to aid him in his battle. The Unlimited and NTICW will certainly be keeping a close eye on this battle as Team eVolution members will do the same to watch the back of their partner, Shocka. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Scott Frost, Jason Jameson, Thug roC, and many of eVolution’s superstars! Backstage ~ A Painful Event Featuring “The Prodigal” Sean Mason End of Days was supposed to be his night of triumph. Instead it had been a night full of nothing more than pain for Sean Mason. He came through the doors of the arena and under each arm he had a crutch to assist him in his walking thanks to the injuries he had suffered at the hands of Asis. It was obvious by the expression on Sean’s face that tonight was not going to be a very good night for him. Instead of being in the ring competing like he wanted to be he was forced to rely on crutches just to stand up. Somehow he was going to make sure that it was Asis that paid for that. He looked quickly around the backstage area upon entering the room and as quickly as he could with the crutches he made his way further and further into the arena.
