Legends XXXII Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The ALLTEL Arena ~ Little Rock, Arkansas 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night April 9th, 2003 Last week it became obvious that Davidson and Somers were going to be using eVolution for quite a while now to wage their own war against one another until only one of them was left with any power in the company. It all kicks off this week as we’ll see all of the NTICW signed wrestlers currently in eVolution heading into some singles action and Christoph teaming with Xander once again in a big tag team main event. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! On Legends this past week Daniel Somers went through and made this match that will pit four men who have let him down time and time again lately with the stipulation that only the man who wins that match will still have a job left in eVolution. The other three men will be fired outright while the winner will begin teaming with 1001 Holds and will have to fight for their right to remain in eVolution as well. Last week Daniel Somers revealed that he was the mystery owner behind the purchase of NTICW and to make matters even worse Somers has now brought the two men he considered top talents in NTICW over to eVolution to help him in his war against Davidson. Now he gives Homicide the chance to show the fans in eVolution just what it is he’s capable of doing against “Blazing” Fiona Preston who has picked up a couple of big victories already since her debut. Playaz Inc make their return to in ring action after several weeks off and look to kick things back off where they had started from with a victory but the only problem is they don’t know just who they will be facing yet. A new mystery team has been brought into eVolution and they will have their first test against a high caliber team like Statuz Quo and Duzza. Just like in the previous match between Fiona and Homicide, Hate another member of NTICW now finds himself paired in a match against Chuck. Chuck came in with a lot of fire going for him but since then he has yet to pick up a victory and will now have to face off against a man who was on the fast track to the NTICW Global Title, Hate. After Team Network captured the sanctioned eVolution Tag Team Titles at Unbreakable they found themselves with large targets on their back courtesy of not only Playaz Inc. but of Twisted Reality as well. The team of Morpheus Omega and Sky Night are always quick to point out that they have never lost the Tag Team Titles and should still be the rightful champions so now this best of three series has been set up for the next three Legends. The team that reaches two wins first will be the team that becomes the Unified Tag Team Champions! Despite being fired at Unbreakable by Tyler Davidson, Erin Christoph still managed to make it to Legends last week with an assist from Daniel Somers and Christoph’s newly signed NTICW contract. This week on Legends he’ll once again team with his cousin Xander and fellow Unlimited member as they battle against Tyler Davidson and the eVolution Universal Champion, Scott Frost. This will be the first time Davidson and Frost will have been in the ring together since Frost attacked Davidson during his debut at Digital Destruction. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Jason Jameson, James Kimson, Morpheus Omega and many of eVolution’s superstars!
