Legends XXV Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Compaq Center ~ San Jose, California 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night February 12th, 2003 Chaos continued to reign supreme in eVolution under the control of Daniel Somers and The Unlimited which seems to grow more and more each work. Legends XXV will now come to you live from the Compaq Center in San Jose, California with less than two weeks before Tainted Love. This week the tag team chaos will continue as several teams try and battle it out for a chance at the titles once again being held by The Unlimited. More fireworks are expected to go down also as things heated up in the Universal Title scene between Frost and Shocka. Frost is rumored to be looking for a chance to get some payback for Shocka and Omega’s actions one week ago. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Last week Oz and his brother Angel both turned their backs on The Unlimited and this week the group looks to get a small taste of revenge against them. Xander is certain to be representing The Unlimited in this match and it’s highly rumored that the second man for The Unlimited would be his cousin Erin Christoph as they take time off from their other issues to deal with the turncoats. Lee eXodus hasn’t seen a lot of action since his pay per view match against London Rockwell who has gone missing but this week he returns to Legends with a shot at the eVolved Championship and Seara. The stipulations for the match have yet to be disclosed but they are left to the decision of Seara herself as the champion. Seara had just one week ago teamed with Sean Mason trying to get a little payback against Xander which is something that you know has got to be lurking in the back of her mind as she heads into this title defense. Big Daddy Dreamer gets a mystery partner for this one to take on the two men who claim to have exclusive rights to the Syndicate name. The two men viciously attacked the time keeper last week at Legends and this week they will look to do the same to Dreamer and any partner he can find for the match. Likely scenarios point towards HBC being the partner but it would be at the expense of a future title shot her and Luther Wisdom have coming to them under the orders of Daniel Somers. The team of Strictly Prohibited and Yoshii Tanaka now team together once again against another mysterious duo in The Crimson Sun. The group had a message for Yoshii Tanaka last week that left the Japanese superstar rather confused by it all. If that wasn’t enough Strictly Prohibited has now fallen under the watchful eyes of Daniel Somers and Erin Christoph as the two men believe they now know the identity of the mysterious star. If Somers and Christoph are to be believed then Strictly Prohibited will soon find himself unmasked and revealed as Tyler Davidson. How in the world this match will actually survive with a winner coming out is beyond anyone. No doubt the team of Shocka, Omega, and Sky Night will be able to successfully work together but can Jameson and Frost possibly coexist with the human monster Sovereign? Common sense would say no but all three men have had problems with Shocka, and Omega in recent weeks. If they can survive each other than winning this match might not be a problem for the three of them as James Kimson is also certain to be looking on. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Troy Pierce, James Kimson, Luther Wisdom, The HeartBreak Chick and many of eVolution’s superstars!
