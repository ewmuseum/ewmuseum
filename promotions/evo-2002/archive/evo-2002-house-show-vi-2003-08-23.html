House Show VI, 23 August 2003

23 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
House Show Results Non-Title ~ Singles Match British Bomber Vs. Yoshii Tanaka The opening match of the night showed a crowd favorite in Yoshii Tanaka. Although the crowd wasn’t behind him at first, his highflying moves brought them to their feet. Almost an instant pop was when Yoshii hit his green mist in the eyes of the Bomber before picking up the win with an asai moonsault. Squash match, but we enjoyed it. Winner via pinfall: Yoshii Tanaka Non-Title ~ Singles Match Matt Hunt Vs. Shaft-Man A see saw battle between these two. Hunt showed his overpowering strength over Shaft-Man multiple times during the match. Hitting numerous power moves. Shaft-Man looked to have lost the match when Hunt hit a Crucifix Powerbomb, which Hunt calls the Postbox, but Shaft-Man somehow got a shoulder up. Shaft-Man showed a comeback though, hitting a lot of speed and cruiserweight moves. We saw an amazing moonsault from the ring apron to the outside during the match that got the crowd on their feet. But in the end, it was Hunt who hit The Last Post to pin Shaft-Man. Winner via pinfall: Matt Hunt Non-Title ~ Standard Match Scott Frost vs. Big Daddy Dreamer Both Seara and HBC were ejected from ringside before this match even started. The match went back and forth for the majority of the match, with no clear leader in the match. Frost got a nice sized pop with a Lou Thez Press. Dreamer would show more offense with a TKO, but only got a two count. Dreamer whipped Frost into the corner, but then ran into his boot when he tried to splash him. Frost got on top of Dreamer’s shoulders and rolled through with a victory roll for the three count. Dreamer looked to have kicked out, but the ref didn’t agree. Frost exited the ring and the ref chased him down to raise his arm in victory. BDD looked upset in the ring as he watched Frost celebrate. Winner via pinfall: Scott Frost Universal Title ~ Standard Match James Kimson vs. Thug roC Champion against champion as roC challenged Kimson for his Universal Title. The crowd was behind roC for the entire match as he was trying to pull what could be looked at as an upset victory. The two continued to go at it back and forth, but Kimson domination the majority of the match. Kimson showed a lot of frustration when roC kicked out of both the Kimson Drop and Kimson Crush (damn this guy loves his name). And roC looked like he was suffering more then surviving this match. Thug roC started to comeback after he reversed a sleeper hold into a back body drop. The two laid on the mat and roC was able to get on the offensive after that. Kimson seemed to absorb most of roC’s punches, then he sent roC to the turnbuckles. When Kimson missed a huge splash, roC was able to dropkick him down into a sitting position in the corner. Thug roC got the crowd behind him more as the ref tried to stop him. But Thug roC went ahead anyways and hit his Lethal Injection which is an illegal move. After Thug roC got the blatant DQ, we saw him celebrate his somewhat victory. That was until the angry James Kimson attacked him after the match. He laid roC out with a vicious running lariat that turned roC inside out. After a beatdown, Kimson hit his History in the Making and left Thug roC laid out in the ring with EMTs on their way. Winner via DQ: James Kimson Evolved Title ~ Final Four Match Siege vs. Aaron Justice vs. Brianna Washington vs. Scott Evans © A great match that showed Scott Evans defending his title against a bunch of great wrestlers. We saw a new stipulation to this match, as you could also win the match by throwing your opponent over the top rope as well as pinfall. The four battled in and out the ring though, exiting through the ropes each time. We saw Brianna going at it with Siege, with Siege taking a lot of punishment before overpowering Brianna. Scott Evans and Aaron Justice went at it intensely, and we saw Justice hit a huge sambo suplex on Evans, but Evans got his foot on the bottom rope. The first elimination of the night was when Siege went for a powerbomb, and Brianna Washington reversed it, and body tossed Siege over the top rope and to the outside. The crowd cheered loudly as Siege was obviously pissed. The celebration was short though, after Justice came over and speared Brianna to the mat hard. Aaron then hit a Final Justice to pick up the pinfall for the second elimination. It was then down to Aaron Justice and Scott Evans, what the fans wanted to see. Evans would go to the top rope and try a 450 splash, but only hit the knees of Justice. Justice would then pick Evans up and drill him with a jack hammer, but no luck with a three count. The two continued for a while until Justice had Evans against the ropes. Justice went and clotheslined him over the top rope, but Justice’s momentum took him over as well. Evans was able to hold on and land on the apron, but not Justice who was then eliminated. Winner via over the top: Scott Evans. (c) eWentertainment 2003
