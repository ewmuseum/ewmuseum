Legends XLVI Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From St. James, Park ~ Newcastle, England 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night August 13th, 2003 Legends under the demand disguised as a request by Cameron Griffin comes along to St. James Park in Newcastle, England for it’s latest stop on its current tour of the United Kingdom. The show is slated to be a big one with several rematches from the past pay per views taking place as well as a huge number one contender’s match for the Universal Title that is certainly going to be one massive battle. Also the battle for the Cell Championship will also continue as a big four man brawl will go down inside of the Cell with the winner receiving a shot at Damon O’Halloran and the Cell Championship next week on Legends. Last week both of these men came up short in their fights for victory with Machine falling victim to Sean Sterling, and Drake falling to Priest Callaghan, both men who were newcomers to eVolution and the Cell Division. Drake is only in his second week as an active competitor within the Cell while Machine has been within the confines several times before and knows exactly what to expect now when he steps through the door. Both men will be ready for a hard fight and the chance they are both waiting for to try and finally snap their losing streaks. Four men all have one chance to earn a shot against Damon O’Halloran and the Cell Championship. In order to do that however they must first survive this match which will no doubt be quite a brutal battle as Question Beyond X, Marcus Escobar, and newcomers “Priest” Callaghan, and Sean Sterling will battle it out. Whoever comes out of this match will have the victory but whether they can truly be prepared for what would come next is the question as Damon demonstrated last week that he will not tolerate any challengers to his throne at the top of the Cell Division regardless of who they are. One of these four men will have to try their best to overthrow the violent king. The leader of The World’s Finest will have his biggest test in eVolution to date this week as he goes one on one against the eVolved Champion, Scott Evans. Evans has been on quite a roll since he captured the eVolved Title with convincing victories over Eric Knight, Drake Nathanial Dukes, and of course Hedges at ReVelations. Through that all there has apparently been some changes going on inside the head of Evans as he is slowly becoming more and more confident in his abilities and no doubt will be more than ready for this match against Valmonté whether the rest of World’s Finest decide to get involved or not. Siege and Carl Cash will battle it out in a rematch from their big battle at ReVelations that was crashed by Sean Valmonté and the rest of the World’s Finest. Though both Siege and Cash have apparently moved onto other matters at the current time they will no doubt love the chance to get their hands on one another and Cash will of course have some tricks up his sleeve as well for Siege including possibly, The World’s Finest. On that note just what will Shocka have in store as payback for Carl Cash after he was put through the table by him last week? Another pay per view rematch, this one dates back to A New Dawn and the Caged Rumble where these two men lasted almost longer than anyone else until roC was betrayed and eliminated by Morpheus Omega. Before then it was Thug roC who managed to pick up a victory over Jameson at From The Ashes and Jameson only now finally gets the chance to avenge that loss. What might play further in his favor now is that this most certainly is not the same Jason Jameson that Thug roC beat on that night. Something inside of Jameson has changed him whether it be better or worse than he once was is debatable but he has certainly demonstrated a much more aggressive nature in the ring that Thug roC will have to watch out for if he wishes to repeat the outcome of From The Ashes. This tag team battle between the newlyweds Scott Frost and Seara against the team who crashed their wedding last week will be a bit of a rematch as well while Luther and Xian both hope that they have a better outing than they did last week. Luther has been on a bit of a struggle over the past few weeks and is now looking towards his partner Xian, to help him break out of his current slump but the two of them might have picked the wrong duo to mess with for their current mission. James Kimson and Asis both have one thing, and one thing only in common. They both want to get their hands on Xander but according to Tyler Davidson only one of them will be able to. Kimson could care less about Asis’ desire for revenge against Xander and simply wants the chance at him to finally capture the eVolution Universal Championship. Asis on the other hand could also care less for just about anything that Kimson or anyone else wants for that matter and wants to get his hands on Xander and get revenge in the best way possible, by taking the one thing that Xander truly cares about, the Universal Title.
