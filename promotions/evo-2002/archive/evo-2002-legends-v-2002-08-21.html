Legends V Transcript Live on Splink l Parp! From The Charlotte Coliseum ~ Charlotte, North Carolina 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night August 21st, 2002 Fresh off of X-Rated, eVolution heads south to North Carolina to bring you Legends V! X-Rated had crowned four new champions in eVolution with a night filled with pain and agony for all that were involved. Revelations were made as secret alliances were revealed and some that were not so secret were broken. What sort of impact will X-Rated have on the future of eVolution? Legends V is where the questions begin to be answered! The true members of The Unlimited were revealed at X-Rated when Christoph and Xander joined together in an effort to destroy Malcolm King. They didn’t get the job done though as King still walks among us in eVolution with an apparent deal made with Tyler Davidson. At Legends V, King gets a chance for revenge as he teams with the Cell Champion Jinx against Xander and Christoph. The question still remains just who is The One? Sean Mason had no doubts about it. He was screwed out of the High Impact Title by Thug RoC at X-Rated. Now he goes for a little redemption as he’ll go one on one with Python in a match set up by Thug RoC. Python wasn’t happy about being left out of the High Impact Title match to begin with and looks to make sure Thug RoC doesn’t repeat that mistake a second time. On a related note the High Impact Champion himself, Quasar the Great will be at ringside doing guest commentary for the match. Two women going one on one in this contest. One is already a firmly established superstar in eVolution while the other is a newcomer trying to make a name for herself. Can she do it at the expense of The HeartBreaker? Not if HBC has anything to say about. She plans on showing Seara that there is only one dominant female in eVolution. Shocka, the number one contender to the Continental Title battles Shodan in a hardcore match. Shodan has been on a bit of a losing streak since arriving in eVolution and looks now for a chance to break out of his slump and begin climbing back up the ladder of contention. With no rules going in this one all hell is sure to break loose as Shocka looks set to prepare himself for his inevitable encounter with Erin Christoph and the Continental Championship. Lawless made his presence in eVolution felt in a big way at the expense of L2J during X-Rated. Now he must compete in the ring head to head against “The Natural” Matt Boone. Boone still grief stricken over the death of his grandfather feels even worse upon not being able to live up to his promise and defeat Sovereign at X-Rated. Now a small taste of retribution can be his if he’s able to defeat the last $W Global Champion! Two bitter old rivals go one on one in an eVolution ring. A feud which had begun in EdW over the Platinum Title continues as two of the former champions battle it out. Brad Kelley faces the last ever Platinum Champion Jason Jameson. Jameson made his debut at X-Rated revealing himself to be The Voice which had interrupted previous shows and spoiling Somers and Famous’ fun at the expense of Troy Pierce. Now Somers pits Jameson against his old rival in hopes that Brad Kelley can eliminate Jameson before he becomes too troublesome. A massive tag team battle should close the show out as Impact teams against with Dreamer and Kage to take on the EdW contingent of L2J, Asis, and Troy Pierce. The problems have been persisting for weeks now between L2J and Asis but now they are forced to work together if they want to keep the EdW spirit alive. The other team is also questionable as how well can Impact work together with the man that had eliminated him from the Universal Title tournament in Jamal Kage? Explosive agendas exist on both sides of the ring as an all out war gets ready to erupt inside of eVolution. Courthouse ~ The Witness Revealed Featuring Eric Graves, Josh Matthews, Jeremy Hunter, and others The scene opens inside of the Philadelphia District Courthouse where the case against Eric Graves for the rape and murder of Tina Wilcox is being held. The judge for the case can be seen slamming his gavel down trying to restore some order inside of the courtroom following Josh Matthew’s taking the stand and directly accusing Eric Graves of the crime. The prosecution has already asked their fill of questions of the witness and eVolution attorney Jeremy Hunter was left to take care of his. “I take it Mr. Matthews that you can without any doubt in your mind state that Eric Graves was the murderer,” Hunter asked eyeing Matthews. ”Yes I can,” he replied calmly. ”How so?” Hunter fired quickly back trying to catch him in a lie. ”I saw him do it jackass,” Matthews responded annoyingly. ”You saw him do it?” Hunter repeated doubtfully. “Why didn’t you try and help then? Why didn’t you try and stop Mr. Graves from raping and murdering Tina Wilcox? Surely that was in your power was it not?” ”No it wasn’t,” Matthews answered with a pissed off stare. “I was piss drunk and could barely walk straight.” ”So you were intoxicated?” Hunter asked. ”Didn’t I just say that?” Matthews sarcastically replied. ”Yes you did,” Hunter commented. “This makes things quite interesting. Just how drunk were you? Could it be that the man you saw wasn’t really Eric Graves?” “No way,” Matthews answered. “I’d recognize that ugly mug anywhere.” “I’m sure you would,” Hunter agreed sarcastically thinking for something to help his case. “Why did it take you so long to come forward Mr. Matthews? I mean it’s been over a month since it happened and you are only just now coming forward. What took so long?” ”I had to make sure those asses Somers and Davidson wouldn’t fire me for going against Graves,” Matthews answered. “In the end though I had to make sure the truth was told.” ”I’m sure you did,” Hunter replied doubtfully again not believing the story of Matthews. He stands pacing back and forth before the judge speaks up. ”Any further questions?” the judge asks him directly. ”I’d like to request a short recess your honor,” Hunter suddenly answered looking back at Graves who was looking even more concerned. ”On what grounds?” the judge asked surprised at the sudden request. ”There are a few things I would like to discuss with my client,” Hunter answered. “I think it could be imperative to the course of this case.” The judge sat silent in his chair for a while watching Graves and Hunter before looking at the prosecutor who showed no signs of protest. ”Very well,” the judge answered. “You have thirty minutes. I expect to see you back in the court room in twenty.” ”Thank you your honor,” Hunter said before heading back to his table and grabbing his briefcase before leading Graves out of the court as the scene fades to black.
