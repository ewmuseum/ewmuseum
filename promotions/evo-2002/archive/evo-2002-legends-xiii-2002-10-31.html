Legends XIII Transcript Live on OSE! From The Memphis Pyramid ~ Memphis, Tennessee 7:59PM EST Thursday Night October 31st, 2002 After the explosive events of Faith this past weekend, many questions were left unanswered. Among them was just who is the current Universal Champion? All of these questions look to be answered this week during Legends XIII on the special Thursday edition of the show. The Rising Star Title Tournament will continue this night and the Road to Glory tournament also begins as Rise of the Legends is the next pay per view on the eVolution agenda. Two newcomers to eVolution will kick off Legends XIII in a dark match. Ben Juventud and Kai Kua, both men are newly signed cruiserweight competitors and will look to make an immediate impact upon their debut this week. Legends XIII kicks off with the third of four Rising Star Title tournament matches. This one a sort of return match from Faith will feature Sean Mason taking on Jay Stylez Junior. It was during the four corners match at Faith that Mason had made Stylez tap out to win the match and now it's Stylez turn to try and get some revenge. On the horizon for both men looms the title which Sean Mason already believes is his in the Rising Star Championship. The Road to Glory tournament kicks off this week as the now former Continental Champion, Shocka goes one on one with the newcomer Shane Hillard. Hillard is no stranger to a wrestling ring with past employment in Cameron Griffin’s OSW, and AIW but now he steps into his first eVolution ring. Only time will tell if he has what it takes and if Chris Osbourne is enough of an edge to push the young man past the former champion Shocka. Shocka certainly won’t be to warm a welcome for the young man as reports state he is quite upset about losing his Continental Title to Shodan in controversial fashion. Hillard had better come prepared or he will be in for a very painful first night. Scott Frost continues his battles against the Unlimited this week as he now battles Jinx in a hardcore match with a whole lot on the line. The Road to Glory is the one chance that most of the roster has been looking for to raise the bar another notch and take that last step up to greatness. In order for Frost to do that he must survive Jinx in his home environment and with the rest of The Unlimited in attendance, Asis in particular. With one eye certainly looking over his shoulder Frost looks for his chance to make an impact and take that next step. After the controversies in Faith’s Continental Title match a four corners match has been booked to officially determine the new champion. Curious by his absence in the match of course is Shocka, but the most recent champion Shodan is in this one along with Jason Jameson, Tommy Michaels, and The HeartBreak Chick. This one should certainly be full of explosives with these four competitors all going after the title. All four of these stars are former EdW competitors and there is quite a bit of history among them. Now all four get the chance for their biggest victory in eVolution yet but only one can walk away with the gold! A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including L2J, Sovereign, Xander, Asis, and many more!
