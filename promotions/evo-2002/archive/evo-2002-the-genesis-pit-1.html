The Genesis Pit 1

circa May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Hello this is Monica Angel welcoming you to the premiere edition of The Genesis Pit; a bi-weekly look at Evolution Wrestling through the eyes of our wrestling insiders, Cassius Owens who was last seen getting his ass handed to him by Sovereign and Satchmo Miles Coltrane senior writer for Wrestling Society magazine. Remember the opinions expressed here do not necessarily reflect those of Evolution Wrestling. The Pit has the flavor of its major influence ESPN's Pardon The Interruption and is an ever evolving piece and now the rundown......" Asis-- Profusion-- James Kimson-- The Tag Team Division-- Xander-- Fiona Preston-- Scott Frost. "The show works in three segments, News/Views...Wrestler Breakdown...and viewer mail. Our panel doesn't have a clue who will be introduced for what segment but will have only a couple of minutes to give their opinion before moving on. Finally we like to have a revolving panel so anyone who is interested please feel free to get in touch with me at Evolution Wrestling Multimedia. And now THE GENESIS PIT....." NEWS/VIEWS: SCOTT FROST "Well those who remember me might know my views on this so called sport.” Cassius Owens began to start things off. “From an entertainment aspect I find Scott Frost an entertaining fellow. Now the question is would I go see him in all these rumored movies that's been proposed to him....most likely not. In my opinion Frost has done more with the tools he is given than any other Champion currently on the roster....that said I have serious questions on the long term viability of this company if Frost is the man representing it." "I could not agree and disagree with you more...” Satchmo Miles Contrane argued. “Scott Frost is the complete package. Out of anyone on the roster is the one who can work any style of match with anyone. He adds to the credibility of the Universal title and basically what it comes down to is the man Scott Frost has shown the most well rounded development of anyone else on the roster...." "So what you are saying in the land of schitzo's he's the most adjusted?" "What I'm saying is that I'll put our current Universal Champ against any other feds champion any day...." TAG TEAM DIVISON "Our tag team division is the strongest I've seen in any federation. With that said I'm not sure the man booking the division is booking to our strengths as a federation. Don't get me wrong Twisted Reality and The Syndicate are excellent teams....but the four members involved are also strong singles competitors and in the long run that might hurt the division as Morpheus Omega, Big Daddy Dreamer, HeartBreak Chick, and SkyNight are viable singles competitors and have had great success as such. I think the foundation might not be there with the main focus on these two teams....." "Normally I would agree with you but in recent weeks I've seen a shift in philosophy. Early on the tag division was about the pursuit of the belt....the title as the holy grail. However after End of Days I think the division is in even better hands because the shift in focus of building the rivalries between teams and not per se making the division all about the belt. You have America's Guardian's vs. Savage Society, The Body Snatchers vs. Tribute, as well as the burning issue with Team Network and St. Violence...what you have is that very foundation that will make a title feud with Twisted Reality and or The Syndicate that much more meaningful. The Foundation is there." "Yes you are right as well as the rumored new OMEN Brotherhood team managed by Malcolm King and the tag team division's open door policy of eVo or non EVo teams should make it interesting for years to come." FIONA PRESTON "It must be said and many aren't going to like it but here it goes. While tragic...that's what happens when a woman tries to play a man's game." "Wait...wait ...wait...there have been many successful women in this very fed Seara, the Heart Break Chick, SkyNight...." "And you'll never have one as Universal Champion....they should know their role in this company and be happy they are getting the exposure they are. But that's another topic for another time. Preston could have avoided all of this by simply having common sense and seeking medical attention...." "I'm not going to sit here and let you belittle the career of a great woman as well as a competitor for EVo. What I hate is that what has been lost outside of a life too soon is the art of wrestling. Fans today are conditioned to get excited for these hard core , high risk matches.....what happened to wrestling; what happened to the art?" BREAKDOWN.... "This segment is reserved not for really an informed critique on a wrestler in eVo or a situation but a rant warranted or not about any thing you want to say. Just so happens I won the coin flip in back and my rant is on none other than the wrestler known as Xander." "Those of you who know me know , know I'm not convinced on this whole wrestling thing. But it amazes me that Xander has been handed the keys to the kingdom time and time again yet fails to get over the hump. Many forget he was one of the founding fathers of Evolution so to speak. His feud with Malcolm King began in EDW and was one of the driving forces that lead to the formation of EVo, and yet I find myself wondering where is Xander? A man with his obvious talents and gifts SHOULD be the Universal Champion. I mean who else does he have to beat? When will Xander stop being a facilitator to others careers and takes charge of his own? He is the most underappreciated superstar on this roster and it's about time Evolution stand up and take notice. He seems to finally be heading in the direction he should have been headed months ago....I fear he only has one shot, and in my opinion the office should stop playing these political games and give this man a legitimate shot at the belt. This week we take a look back at Legends number one and the four corners match featuring Xander among others for the Continental Title: Location: The Mellon Arena ~ Pittsburg, Pennsylvania Date: July 10th, 2002 Continental Title ~ Anything Goes Match Xander vs. Erin Christoph vs. HeartBreak Chick vs. Eric Badger Libby Bowman: This match is for the Continental Championship it is a four corners match. Introducing first from Los Angeles, California at a height of six two and a weight of two hundred and fifty two pounds he is Xander! The entire arena is plunged into darkness as “Sweetness” by Jimmy Eat World starts up. Then multiple strobe lights go off on the stage in time with the music. The lights circle the arena as Xander comes out from the curtain. Xander looks to the ring, and then the crowd, then makes his way to the ring. Circling it once, then stepping up onto the apron. Before entering, Xander gives one last look out to the crowd as all the strobes center on Xander entering through the ropes. Jack Tyler: This guy as the Continental champion? I don’t see it but hey he might just be able to prove me wrong and come out victorious here tonight. Justin Fritz: It wouldn’t take much to prove you wrong now would it Jack. I do think that Xander has a pretty decent chance here tonight. Libby Bowman: Coming out next the first opponent hailing from Moscow, Russia at a height of six two and a weight of two hundred and fifty six he is Erin Christoph! The lights go dim throughout arena; and a sinister whisper is heard over the PA system and it says, "Enter, Erin Christoph". "It's going down" by Executioners begins playing and red strobe lights slowly flash back and forth. Christoph comes walking out with manager Ricky Fury. Christoph is wearing a hooded robe hiding his face but it is open and revealing his muscular body. His baggy jeans and black boots make him seem more intimidating as he and his manager strut down to the ring. Justin Fritz: Erin here defiantly stands a chance face it he’s probably the best in this whole match up. This will be one great match up tonight. Jack Tyler: Well since we don’t know who and all are in this match up yet yeah I guess it would be pretty easy to say he is the best wouldn’t it. Libby Bowman: Now coming to the ring from Toronto Ontario, Canada at a height of six two and a weight of two hundred and twenty pounds he is “The Prophecy” Eric Badger! The lights in the arena go down, and a message is relayed across the screen "The prodigy of professional wrestling is here" Pyro of red and white explode out of the stage as Eric Badger appears at the stage to boo's from the crowd. He makes his was yelling at the fans and eventually dives into the ring, gets on the turnbuckle and raises his arms in the air, to even more booing. Jack Tyler: Here he is the third contestant in this four corner’s match. I don’t know to much about Eric Badger but what a name he has. Justin Fritz: What’s the matter Jack your source’s let you down for once? Besides with a last name like Badger how good can he be? Libby Bowman: The next and last opponent coming from Toronto, Canada at a height of five eleven and a weight of one hundred and forty pounds she is The Heartbreak Chick! Little hearts fall from the top of the arena as "Lie to Me" by Disturbed echoes through the arena as HBC comes out from behind the curtain. HBC slowly walks to the ring in a very seductive manner before stepping through the ropes, climbing up to the top and backflipping off into the middle of the ring with her arms outstretched and her prancing around. Justin Fritz: I always enjoy watching her wrestling. I completely enjoy watching her come out just because I like to see what she is wearing this day. Jack Tyler: Yes she is a great competitor not to mention a bit on the beautiful side. Only thing is the possibility that she will be thrown around like a rag doll. HBC has a terrible disadvantage in this match-up. The match up begins rather quickly with Xander and Erin bouncing on each other. Xander with a right after right and another right hand.. Eric and HBC are also going at it as Eric has HBC lifted up in a choke hold. HBC counters by lifting her foot up and smacking Eric in the crotch. Eric lets go of HBC and drops to his knees. Mean while Erin has gotten the advantage over Xander and he whips him. Xander’s knee dives into the back of the head of Eric as Xander trips and lands face first into the mat. Erin then walks over to Xander and lifts him up. Erin gets Xander up in a guerilla press slam but before he can drop him HBC comes around and dropkicks Erin. Erin falls back and Xander lands on top of Erin. The referee begins to count 1…kick out. Jack Tyler: Only a one count there and we just began the match up. What was he thinking going for a pin so early? Justin Fritz: Maybe he was thinking that he just got saved by HBC and he’s very thankful for it or maybe just maybe he was thinking damn I might be able to win. HBC is back on Eric as she is stomping him. Her stomping doesn’t seem to be to effective as Eric is slowly getting back up. HBC goes for another stomp but Eric predicts it and grabs her foot. Eric then stands up as HBC hops for a couple seconds. HBC then jumps up and delivers a devastating enziguri kick which sends Eric into a flip and onto his back. Erin is now to his feet and Xander has just reached his. Erin walks to Xander and grabs Xander by his throat. Erin then throws Xander almost to the other side of the ring in a choke toss. Xander lays there for awhile and looks to be knocked out. Erin follows over there but Xander springs up and clothesline Erin. On the other side of the ring HBC has done climbed the turnbuckle and patiently waits on Eric to get up. Eric slowly get up and HBC flies off going for a missile dropkick. Eric sea’s it coming though and falls back down on the ground. HBC lands right on top of Eric as Eric lifts up his legs slingshotting HBC into the ropes. Justin Fritz: Now this is what I’m talking about! This is actually starting to look like a title match now! With all these bodies flying around it’s hard to keep up with who’s beating who! Jack Tyler: Tell me about it and the referee must be at least mildly confused with the action as well. That’s just one of the things about one of these four corners matches it’s so hard to keep up with all the talent inside of the ring. Xander takes advantage of Erin and grabs Erin’s arms. Xander wraps Erin’s arm’s up in the ropes as Erin can’t get free. Xander slides out of the ring and looks under the apron. Xander then starts to bring out table’s, ladder’s, chair’s, any weapon he can find under there. Xander slides in the ring with a bat in hand. Xander smacks Erin in the head with the bat before working on his ribs. Meanwhile Eric walks over to the fallen HBC and lifts her up. Eric goes to throw HBC over the top rope but HBC clings on and lands on the apron. HBC then jumps up over the top rope and wraps her legs around Eric’s head. HBC then delivers a frankensteiner that throws Eric over the top as HBC still clings on. HBC then clings onto the top rope bounces her feet off the second rope and then puts her legs on the top rope and springs off doing a modified star press shooter. Erin has finally broken free of the ropes hold as Xander lifts Erin up and throws him outside of the ring as well. Justin Fritz: Well it seems as if were going to take this to the outside of the ring now. I just hope we can still stay with this action. I do admit though that frankensteiner HBC did to Eric was simple extravagant! I know one thing she could wrap her leg around my head like that anytime! Jack Tyler: Yeah hopefully she’ll kick your ass while she’s at it. There hasn’t been very many pin falls as of yet it seems like there just wanting to beat the living hell out of each other rather then actually go for a quick pin fall and get it over with! HBC lifts Eric up but Eric delivers a shoulder thrust into the gut of HBC sending her back. Eric takes advantage of this and whips HBC head first into the steel steps at ring side. Eric then lifts HBC up putting her on his shoulder. Eric starts charging at the turnbuckle but HBC slides out of his arms behind him and dropkicks him into the turnbuckle. Eric drops back and HBC sends him back into the ring as she slides in after him. Outside the ring Xander has set up a table and a ladder. Erin is starting to get on his feet as Xander notices this. Xander grabs a broom and breaks the handle against Erin’s knee as you can hear Erin scream out in pain. Xander lifts Erin up and puts Erin up on the table as Xander delivers a few hard punches on the forehead of Erin. Xander starts climbing up the ladder taking his time as he taunts the crowd slightly but not enough to get a response. Jack Tyler: I know he isn’t going to try this! We don’t have any of those pussy ass mat’s like some other federation’s use all we got for them to land on is straight concrete! That will hurt both Xander and Erin right there! Justin Fritz: Shut up Jack! This is Evolution were starting our own damn category you’ll just have to learn to live with it. We are uncensored and these fans came to see blood so let them see it! Xander is about to jump when we see the ladder fall over! Xander falls fast first through that table as Erin barely managed to get out of the way. Xander’s head busting open on impact as we see the blood pouring from his face. We then notice a man wearing black and red robes as he helps Erin up to his feet. Erin and the man in the robes begin to pound away on the fallen Xander! Before long we can see Jinx running down the ramp as he jumps on both men knocking them down! Jinx grabs a sledge hammer and smashes it across the back of Erin as the man in black and red takes off through the crowd! Jinx takes off after the man in robes. In the ring HBC has done delivered The Equalizer onto Eric sending him onto his knees. HBC gets a couple feet away and waits for Eric to get up. Eric drowsy slowly begins to get up as HBC leaps at him delivering HBC’s Sweet BadAss Superkick! HBC drops to her knee’s and quickly goes for the pin fall on Eric as the ref falls and begins the count. 1….2….3! Justin Fritz: There you have it! HBC was the first person to eliminate someone and it just so happened that Eric was the poor sap to be the first one out. I told you with a name like Eric Badger how good could his ass be!? Jack Tyler: Yeah, yeah she just got lucky that’s all that was! What I want to know is who was that man in the black and the red robes? Xander seems to be really hurting right now and that’s just terrible to see something like that happen to a man of his expertise! Erin lifts up Xander and there both knocked down as HBC jumps over the ropes with a corkscrew moonsault! HBC lifts up Erin and rushes him into the ring. HBC then sets up Erin for her finisher as well as Erin stands up. HBC goes for it but Erin catches her foot and throws her over his head by just her leg! Erin picks HBC up and there it is Erin’s trademark move the Trinity Bomb! Erin goes for the pin and the ref drops to his knees and begins the count 1….2…….3! Erin then rolls HBC out of the ring as he now eye’s the broken down Xander that is laying down at the outside. Erin goes to the outside to Xander and throws him into the ring. Erin climbs into the ring and grabs Xander up. Erin then delivers the Trinity Bomb and goes for the pin fall 1…2..kick out! Jack Tyler: Holy shit! I thought Erin had it won right there but Xander refuses to give up! You have to give credit where it’s due Xander has heart and plenty of spirit! Justin Fritz: He’s still a dumbass! He should’ve just went ahead and laid down before he got more of an ass whooping delivered to him! Erin then lifts Xander up and does a powerbomb onto the top part of the turnbuckle as Xander lays there in pain. Erin walks over and sticks his foot in between Xander’s legs delivering a low blow. Xander holds in between his legs in pain as Erin is loving it! Erin lifts Xander up and delivers a devastating Falcon Arrow Suplex! Erin goes for the pin and the ref drops to his knee’s 1….2..kick out! Erin looks a bit pissed off as Erin lifts Xander up. Erin goes for a clothesline but Xander manages to duck it. Xander then turns around and delivers a roll up pin on Erin! The ref drops to his knee’s 1….2…but Erin reverses it! Erin his holding onto Xander’s tights and there’s the 1….2….3! Justin Fritz: There you have it! Christoph walks out the winner in this four corner’s match up! This match was simply amazing and it turned out a good idea to just whoop each other’s asses until the end so it made for an easier and quicker together match up. Jack Tyler: Indeed it did turn out to be good for Erin as he is now the new Continental champion! We hope to see more match up’s like this in the future! Libby Bowman: These people were eliminated in this order. First was Eric Badger, second was Heart Break Chick and the last person eliminated was Xander! Here is our winner and the first ever Continental champion he is Erin Christoph! MAIL TIME 1) When will Asis claim his rightful place among the Legends of Evolution? "I think once he realizes that maybe that sometimes the individual strength outweighs that of numbers.” Satchmo answered. “Much like Xander I think the whole Unlimited thing has been a giant albatross." 2) Who impresses you the most on the Evolution roster? "James Kimson without a doubt.” Cassius took his turn to answer. “He has it all in my opinion and then some. Most importantly he uses his head when he's in the ring. This man is destined for greatness." 3) Who do you see as Evolution's next breakout star? "Profusion...remember you heard it here first.” Satchmo answered. “However he shouldn't go through normal channels, as stated before the tag division has a solid but still new foundation. He maybe able to parlay any success there into a single run." "Well ladies and gentlemen that is our show for tonight. Our next edition takes place after From the Ashes...until then this is Monica Angel saying goodnight."
