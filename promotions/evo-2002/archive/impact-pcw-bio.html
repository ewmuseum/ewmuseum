'The Hollywood Monster' Impact Biography Real Name Michael Scopes Nicknames 'The Hollywood Monster' Hometown Hollywood, California Birthday 5/4/75 Height 6'6' Weight 324 Biography Ring Entrance: The lights turn off... blue and white smoke start to hiss from the sides of the ramp… a guitar introduction blares through the arena… ‘Conceived in a hell beyond, your depth of perception, chaotic case of conquering domination, psychopath snaps, fired chains of imprisonment a bludgeoning force that's undermining the government!‘ Edgecrusher by Fear Factory blasts throughout the as light blue and white lights start to flicker and move around the fans.... Impact is standing in the middle of the ramp into the immense boos and a few cheers from the fans. He walks down arrogantly, looking at people in disgust before setting his sights on the ring and the man already waiting for him inside of it. Sovereign stands in the ring and as Impact is on the outside the two men stare back and forth with their eyes locked. He was born. Your savior was born on May Fourth of the year Nineteen Seventy-Five O so HE says. “The Hollywood Monster” Impact, was actually born as one, Michael Scopes, into a urban upper-class family. His mother, Martha Scopes and his father Michael Scopes Sr., loved their handsome and undeniably adorable son with all their heart and with all their mind. Michael did not fret or whine from this over excessive expression of love... no... he loved it. He soaked up every bit of it. In reality, it were his parents, not his character that caused Michael Scopes to be the egotistical monster you see now. Here is a prime example. When Michael was growing up, although his size was astronomically large compared to his peers, many bullies would pick on him because of the fact he had much money. One night, Michael was jogging along his neighborhood in his religiously followed exercise. He was mugged. He beat the living piss out of the mugger. And literally, their piss was beaten out. However, he was not faced with the least bit of disciplinary action or a talk about the violence that he unleashed onto the mugger. Michael's parents complimented him. They boasted about his impressive strength to their friends and family. Michael felt proud… Michael felt healthy about himself. He saw the other kids in his neighborhood and school. No one was as big as him. There were kids as tall as he was, but those kids were usually too lanky or too fat. “I am perfect!” he boasted one night while he vanquished Bowser in Super Mario on Nintendo. Seeking wealth from Michael’s unique look for a teen, his parents sought him into the business of Hollywood… the business of legends and glorified men and women… acting. Not surprisingly, many people immediately jumped on Michael’s handsome face and impressive physique even one time putting him as a boyfriend with now famous Jennifer Love Hewitt in several movies. “Good job Michael.” Said a director as they finished a shot. “I did good huh?” questioned Impact as he waved his hand good bye to the gorgeous teen in Ms. Hewitt. “Just perfect.” Said the director. He felt the healthy sense of self-pride again. That is not a terrible thing… that is not something to be scored or put down… it is perfectly normal! But… this is Michael… normal is not what you can call a teen with a physique of an Adonis and a look of a model. This leads us to this year. Now 27, and fully aged and not even in his prime, Impact has now grown and nurtured his ego into a fine… boast. Because that is what is ego is fueled upon… his uncanny ability to boast and do it well. If he goes into a super market, he will boast that his grown vegetables are of better quality to a near-by person, if it is a baby giggling on the cart seat or an old grandmother barely able to walk, it doesn’t matter to him… not at all. Impact continued his career in Hollywood as he starred in many action movies. He was adored by millions of fans and had a huge cult following. That was another something to fuel the fire. He earned huge paychecks… he had women screaming at the top of their lungs at the sight of the marvelous marvel. Another something. However, he became bored. Very bored. Here was an actor with millions of dollars… too many girls and too much free time (He was a very quick man also…). That is when remembered something he had not thought about in a long while. Wrestling. As a young child, he admired greats like Hulk Hogan, The Ultimate Warrior, Andre the Giant and Ric Flair. Some of those wrestlers were the people to inspire to be healthy and have impressive physiques. He grew! He dominated! He physically and mentally blew out his competition. This man was unstoppable! He was now “The Hollywood Monster” Impact! He was the man to beat! Great... one more thing to add to the fire.
