From Brisbane, Australia ~ Brisbane Entertainment Centre 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night December 17th, 2003 In The Ring ~ Setting the Standard Featuring Matt Hunt and Damon O’Halloran “Po-po-po-postman!” A booming deep voice that echos throughout the arena and a large pop from the locals. There’s a brief silence before that rhytmatic sound echos through the sound system and after a few moments of this quiet entrance, the following is heard. "Forfeit the game before somebody else takes you out of the frame and puts your name to shame. Cover up your face you can't run the race, the pace is too fast you just won't last." Mike Chioda's rap entrance ends and "Points of Authority" by Linkin Park blasts into full song while the fans attention is diverted to the entrance way. A collection of multi coloured fireworks and pyros explode around the entrance curtain. “Last week, Matt Hunt defeated Thug roC and unified his eVolved Title to become the new eVolution United Champion” commented Justin. “Not to mention that he’s in the next stage of the Road to Glory tournament” said Jack. Moments later, as Chester Bennington's lyrics burst throughout the arena, Matt Hunt appears at the entranceway with a focused facial expression and the United Title wrapped firmly around his waist. The smoke from the fireworks and pyros slowly drifts up to the rafters as he stands at the top of the aisleway and scans the crowd in front of him. The Postman then makes his way down the aisleway, slowly strolling to the ringside area. As the chorus hits, The Postman glides up the steps and walks across the apron before to have one last glance to the people with a smug grin. He then steps through the ropes and enters the squared circle, marching towards the opposite corner and grabbing a microphone. The place is filled full of thunderous cheering as Hunt strolls around the ring, glancing into the audience. “It’s going to be a great night, Jack. I’ve just got this feeling” said Justin. “Yeah? Well I just want these people to shut up so Hunt can finally talk” responded Jack. The fans noise eventually dies down a few seconds later as Hunt pauses in the middle of the ring and puts the mic to his mouth. “Greetings, camaches…” he says to another big cheer from the people. “I killed two birds with one stone last week. Whilst defeating Thug roC and finally getting my hands on this little beauty…” he says as he glances down to his waist and the United Title belt. “…I also progressed into the next round of the prestigious Road to Glory tournament” he states as the fans cheer once again. “Now that I am the new United Champion, I plan to add some credibility to this title. I’m not saying that Thug roc wasn’t a great champion, despite the person that he is, but this strap has been unified twice and in my book, that has devalued it. I guess you could call me the eVolution ULWN Heavyweight Continental eVolved United Champion, eh?” he says as he pauses. “roC defeated Ryan Mason back in August to unify the ULWN World Title and the eVolution Continental Title and held the United Title for four months” stated Justin. “Sure, Thug roC was the United Champion for four months…” “You were right, Fritz. I’m impressed…” said Jack as he spoke over the headset. “…but his way and style of winning matches hasn’t exactly helped establish this title as the secondary title of eVolution but that’s what I’m going to do. It isn’t going to happen over night and it’s not going to be easy, but rest assured I will eventually add the prestige and pride that this title deserves” he states as he strolls around the ring. “Hunt is talking as if he’s going to hold that title for months and months!” said Jack in a tone of disbelief. “He’s set his goals and ambitions and it seems like he’s intent on reaching them” responded Justin. “Now I know this is sounding like a Bret Hart 1994 speech, but I will be a fighting champion and I will put my title on the line against anybody who steps up. It doesn’t matter who it is. Even if roC wants a rematch, I’ll be waiting” he states as he holds his arms out and receives another cheer from the people. At that moment, the Irish National Anthem blares through the arena and Hunt turns to look up at the entrance way with a scowl to see Damon O’Halloran stood at the top of the entrance way with a mic in his hand. “Oh what the hell does he want?” said Justin. “Before anything is said, may I just congratulate you on winning the United Championship” he says as he places the mic underneath his armpit and slow claps to a negative reaction from the fans. Hunt simply shakes his head as he leans on the top rope. “I’d also like to commend you on a great speech as well. It’s a shame you mentioned how it was like a Bret Hart parade, because I was looking forward to reminding you that we’re in 2003 and not 1994 and…” “Hey mooch…” Hunt interrupts with a smirk on his face, which irritates his Irish foe. “Is there any point to this little act of yours or are you nearly done?” he asks as Damon shakes his head with a smile of his own. “I was about to comment on your “I’ll put my title on the line against anybody” statement…” “Look…” Hunt interrupts again, causing O’Halloran to grit his teeth. “All we ever do is talk…well, except for last week when I introduced your head to a window!!” the fans cheer as Hunt strolls around the ring with his arms held out. This provokes Damon to take a few steps down the aisleway. “So instead of waiting for tonight’s tag match, why don’t we just…” Hunt drops his mic in preparation for Damon, who charges into the ring by sliding underneath the bottom rope. Hunt meets him in the middle of the ring and they start to exchange hard rights, with neither man managing to get an upperhand. “Hunt and Damon going at it!!” hollered Justin. “I knew they couldn’t wait, this has exploded…and look at the refs!” Within seconds they are entangled in a brawl, as the fans go nuts, and a swarm of officials storm into the ring and attempt to separate the fighting pair. Boo’s break out as Hunt and Damon are eventually separated, with the Irishman being escorted out of the ring and up the aisleway. Hunt is in the ring, also surrounded by a host of referees. “Well they’ve been separated for now, but I expect this to continue in the tag match later tonight” commented Justin. Instead of both men snarling at each other at a distance, they share a slight smirk as Damon is backing up the aisleway and The Postman is backed up in the corner. “What the hell are they both smiling at?” wondered Jack as the scene cut to a commercial. In The Ring ~ Deal Time Featuring Leya Forth and James Kimson “My Way” by Limp Bizkit hit the Speakers to the surprise of many fans in attendance. It had been nearly a month since this song played at Faith when Leya Forth, Committee member and arch nemesis of Universal Champion (and Brother no less) James Kimson. It was at Faith when her ‘master plan’ was foiled by Robert B. and James Kimson’s undying resolve to never give up. It was then as well when an errant chair shot from Shocka, her muscular hope for victory smashed her in the head putting her in hospital. But here she was, walking down the ramp way to boos from the crowd. She looked stunning, in a black tight business suit that showed off her legs and her behind. She got into the ring and was not looking all that pleased, perhaps even embarrassed, ashamed to be out there. But when the music died off she took the microphone and began to speak. “I know what you are all thinking” said Leya. “Everyone wants to get their laughs out at me for not getting the job done. Despite all the odds that I stacked against James Kimson he came out on top at Faith, and not only that but he managed to somehow get me off TV for almost a Month. That left Brooks in charge, and well he’s made some calls that I most certainly am not happy about. First of all starting the Road to Glory tournament without my consent. Picking the match up’s the dates and stipulations like uniting the United and Evolved titles and basically handed it to his new found friend in Matt Hunt, the man I BROUGHT to eVo to deal with Kimson. So he sets up his own little match between the two. Well, let’s put it this way. Hunt’s days are numbered thanks to my little deal with Damon O’Halloran. But that’s the least of what’s on my mind. Because you all know that I can’t stand the fact that after all I’ve done that James Kimson is still Universal Champion.” The fans cheered. “Kimson was not at Legends last week due to a sever Flu, but he is apparently here tonight” said Fritz. “James, I must admit that you got guts and that you surely are determined to never die.” she said. “I thought I had your number at Faith, I really honestly did. I had back up plan after back up plan but I didn’t know that Brooks would come down and count the 3 count. I guess the good guy sometimes has to cheat himself and if that’s the case then here we are. I stand here in this ring and you are backstage with the Title over your shoulder probably laughing it up with the ‘boys’ in the back over poor little Leya with egg on her face. The Problem is James that it took me a month to come back, to get over the defeat of seeing everything fail and everything fall apart. Shocka screwed up, twice and for that I won’t forgive him. But if he wants to go and earn his right to the Title by winning the Road to Glory then so be it. But as for that night, that very special night James you forget one thing. That I still am in charge here. And I make the matches revolving around you and your title.” With that Stupify hit and the crowd erupted in cheers. The Universal Champion arrived on the stage, blue jeans and a University of Hype T-shirt on, no sleeves to expose his muscular arms. The Title was draped over his shoulder, hair tied back and he looked out to the ring. Leya stood still as the ovation continued she could only look up at her Brother and protagonist standing there looking down to the ring with a slight grin on his face. “Hey Leya I don’t know if that’s egg on your face of some odd bruise left by Shocka from faith. But either way I think that it suits you!” the fans popped with the insult. “But either way Leya it’s not important. And while I am sure you feel embarrassed and defeated I know who makes the rules around here. And I know who’s in charge. And by title and name it sure as hell isn’t me. I’m just the Champion and yeah, regardless of if I want to or not you are the Boss around here.” said the Champion as he paced on the ramp way. “But that aside Leya you can dance around the issues all you want. You want the title off me. And if your master plan at Faith fell flat on your pretty little face then everything else, down the line is going to make you look worse and worse. So basically Leya, I’m inviting you to try me. I want to see your baddest, most devious plan. I want to cut to the chase and I want to see what Leya Forth is made of.” said Kimson. “Because I’m sick of waiting for this bullshit to end. If I loose the title because of you I’d rather do it tonight and get it over with then deal with this bullshit anymore.” Leya smiled. “Oh but isn’t that half the fun? Well maybe not for you James. I can understand your desire to finish this all. It sounds like the talk of a beaten man. Because it is unavoidable. I failed at Revolutions and I failed at faith.” she paused. “Perhaps that was Shocka, perhaps it was my fault for not dotting all the i’s and crossing all the t’s. But the only thing you need to worry about James is not how but when. I don’t mind drawing this out, to make you suffer for as long as I can. Maybe next year? Maybe next week. But it won’t happen tonight, tonight the match against roC and O’Halloran will go on, and Matt Hunt will be your partner, and I do know that you both were Tag Team Champions together before. But I doubt severely that you will manage to walk out of the ring tonight a winner.” “Well you know I can remember you saying that about the last two times you put me into the ring against all odds. I whipped all their asses. But you know, I never take your words lightly Leya. But you know what, I want to make a deal with you tonight regarding some stipulations.” said Kimson. Leya seemed interested wondering what was on Kimson’s mind. “Since October you have had my Finishing Move banned. The fans want to see it, I want to see it. The only people who don’t are your bitches and you the bitch.” said Kimson bluntly to the joy of the fans. “Here’s the deal. If I get the pinfall or submission in tonight’s match, you lift the suspension on my move.” he said. Leya mulled over the thought. “Well you know James, that’s not much of a deal. Because it seems to me like you haven’t put anything on the line.” she smirked. “How about this James. If you get the decision tonight then I will lift the suspension on History in the Making. But if you should happen to be pinned or if you Tap out tonight, then the man who does it. He gets a Title shot at you. And not only that but he gets to choose whatever stipulation that he wants for the match.” she said with an evil grin. Kimson was not afraid to defend the belt, but in any match someone would come up with surely wasn’t a good plan. But he bit his lip. “Your on.” he said. “Good, then it’s a deal. Good luck tonight James, because I promise that you’re going to need it.” “My way” hit again and The segment drew to a close with a very important stipulation announced. It didn’t bother him that Xander had attacked him from behind with a steel chair and tried to break his neck. His own personal pain was irrelevant now. Of course, there was that part of his mind that was pissed off at having been drawn into that kind of situation, but once again that internal voice was being flat out ignored. The ones that told him to kill him, to spill his blood and bathe in it. He didn’t care about the voice that was telling him to be rational, to manipulate Xander the way Xander had manipulated him. The voice that told him to be cautious, to not let the same thing happen again. No, he’d listened to that voice far too much for far too long. He knew what he needed to do, he knew what he was going to do. There was no plan this time; no patience, no waiting. He jumps down and walks to a side of the ring where he throws his arms out sideways and trash talk a bit. His music fades away, and Thug roC is still showing that huge smile. He approaches his partner and they share a few words and a high-five until the following distracts them to the entrance way. “Po-po-po-postman!” That infamous deep voice echoes throughout the arena as Linkin Park’s ‘Points of Authority’ skips the chorus and bursts straight into the main beat of the song. Damon and roC take a stance and prepare themselves. More moments passed when suddenly Matt Hunt appeared at ringside, but from through the crowd and behind his two foes! The people erupted as the United Champion reaching into the ring and pulled at roC’s ankle, pulling him to the outside. As Damon was busy staring up at the entranceway, he had no idea that Hunt had just hurled his tag partner into the ring post, before sliding into the ring himself. “Look! Hunt came through the crowd and has just taken out Thug!!” hollered Justin with excitement. “Damn, what a cheap shot Fritz. Can’t he face these two man to man, for crying out loud?” responded Jack. Hunt takes a stance of his own and waits as Damon is still facing the entranceway, preparing to strike his foe at any moment. However, The Postman grows impatient and storms up to Damon, spinning him around and nailing some hard rights on the bewildered Irishman. He’s backed up into the corner as Hunt unleashes a flurry of hammers, ignoring the pleading referee who doesn’t seem to be stating any order. roC recovers on the outside as he shakes his head and gingerly crawls into the ring. By this time, Hunt has pulled Damon up and nails a tap to the stomach, seemingly going for the Last Post. However, as Hunt spins around to drop the shoulder, O’Halloran blocks him and shoves him away and inadvertently into Thug roC, who lands a devastating clothesline. Both Damon and roC shake the cobwebs as they pass instructions to each other. “roC almost took Hunt’s damn head off right there, good god!” said Justin in amazement. “Isn’t it a coincidence that Kimson hasn’t appeared yet?” said Jack in a coy tone. “Oh cut that crap already, Jack” snapped his colleague. As Damon pulled a groggy Hunt up… “It’s time…let the hype begin…” A huge roar from the crowd shakes the foundations as the opening riff of Disturbed’s eVo remix of “Stupify” blasts around the arena. Seconds later, the Universal Champion charges down the aisle way and slides into the ring, taking down Damon immediately with a harsh clothesline. Thug storms over, only to be nailed with another clothesline. Damon is already back to his feet, but Kimson lands some hard rights before grabbing him by the hair and hurling him over the top rope. “There’s Kimson dammit and he’s cleaning house here!!” hollered Justin over the noise. Thug is also back to his feet and motions to go after Kimson, but Hunt cuts him off and charges at him, taking him down and landing some mounted punches. The Postman eventually gets to his feet and stomps on roC, who is stomped to the outside. Kimson and Hunt then storm around the ring, with their adrenaline pumping and the noisy fans as Damon and roC group together on the outside. “Take your time boys, get the plan sorted out” commented Jack. The official motions for Damon and roC to re-enter the ring as he orders Kimson and Hunt back towards their corner. Hunt steps through the ropes and stands on the apron as Kimson warms up, using the top rope to stretch. Damon slides into the ring as roC takes his position on his apron as the official motions for the bell to get the match underway. “Finally, we’re off” said Jack. Kimson and Damon lock up, two big men grappling with each other for what seems to be an eternity. Damon somehow wriggles out of the lock and spins around Kimson, landing a clubbing blow to the spine. The Hype staggers forward as reaches for his back as Damon taunts Hunt, who threatens to step through the ropes but the official orders him to stay on the apron. “Kimson attempting a technical approach to this match, but Damon is having none of it!” said Justin. “Damon is a fighter, Fritz. He don’t do those quick submission moves and he’s got those soup bowls at the ready!” said Jack. Damon backs off as Kimson motions for another lock-up, but the Irishman ducks and spins Kimson around, landing some stiff rights and backing him up into the corner. O’Halloran then changes to nailing some stomps to Kimson’s mid section as he slides down, slouching against the bottom turnbuckle. The Irishman then backs off and raises his arms, provoking thunderous booing from the locals. He then reaches down to Kimson, who bursts out of the corner with a flurry of his own rights and a kick to the stomach, hitting a DDT which drives Damon’s head straight into the canvas. “Impact from Kimson there, fighting out of the corner” remarked Justin. Kimson then pulls Damon to his feet and clamps on a headlock, tightening the grip and allowing the Irishman no room for any counters. With Kimson’s grip like a vice, O’Halloran maneuvers his foe to the ropes in attempt to break the hold and whip his opponent into the ropes at the same time. However, Kimson keeps the lock in tact and doesn’t go anywhere, only squeezing more and more of Damon’s neck. After a few more seconds of Damon wriggling and groaning, he manages to hoist Kimson up and fall backwards, hitting a high back drop. The hold is broken as Kimson reaches for his back and Damon uses the ropes to get to his feet. “Desperate move from O’Halloran there, much needed” said Justin. O’Halloran staggers to Kimson and lands a stomp on his chest before pulling him up and whipping him into the opposite corner, with Kimson’s lower back hitting the middle turnbuckle hard. Damon follows up and charges into Kimson, landing a clothesline, which forces The Hype to stagger out of the corner. O’Halloran locks in a front face lock and tightens the grip, before throwing Kimson’s arm around his own neck and hoisting the Universal Champion up and over hitting a harsh suplex. “O’Halloran slowly taking control of this match, Fritz. Measuring up Kimson and finishing him” noted Jack. Damon hooks the leg and goes for the pin. One… Two… Kickout! The kickout provokes a “tooooooooooooooooo” shout from the locals. The Irishman is back to vertical base and pulls Kimson by the feet and back to his corner, where he tags in Thug roc. Damon steps through the ropes as Hunt grows impatient from the opposite side of the ring, gritting his teeth and watching the action. roC jumps in and immediately lands a some hard stomps on Kimson before pulling him up and whipping him into the ropes. On the return, Kimson ducks an attempted clothesline and maneuvers behind Thug, spinning him around and scooping him up into a scoop slam. roC hits the mat hard as Kimson pulls him to his feet and hoists him up again, hitting the Kimson Krush – the Canadian Backbreaker and then goes for a pin. One… Two… Kickout…and another “tooooooooooooooooo” cry from the people. Kimson motions to reach down to the groggy Thug roC, but notices that Hunt has his arm out and signals for a tag. He walks over to Hunt and tags him in as the fans cheer. Hunt calmly strolls to Thug, who has used the ropes to get back to his feet, and lands a clubbing forearm to his lower back. Hunt lands another forearm before spinning roC around and whipping him into the ropes, hitting a Stamp of Approval big boot as Thug bounces off the ropes. Hunt drops to his knees and makes the cover. One… Two… Kickout! “Tooooooooooooooooo!” Hunt pulls Thug up and hurls him into his own turnbuckle, inadvertently striking his partner with a flying elbow. Damon drops to one knee on the apron as roC slides down the turnbuckle. Hunt then storms over and grabs roC’s arm and slaps O’Halloran on the arm. The official signals this as a legal tag and the fans cheer as Hunt reaches through the ropes and pulls Damon into the ring. The Postman nails some hard rights before aggressively whipping his enemy into the ropes, hitting a devastating spinebuster when Damon returns. Hunt goes for another pin… One… Two… Kickout! Without so much as a word to the official, Hunt pulls O’Halloran up and executes a Russian Legsweep within seconds. The Englishman hooks the leg once again… One… Two… Kickout! Damon managed to get the shoulder up and starts to crawl towards his own, where the dazed Thug roc motions to reach out but Hunt is also back to his feet and pulls Damon back by the feet. He then grabs one leg and starts to go to work on it, stomping on it relentlessly as the Irishman wriggles around the canvas. Hunt then attempts to lock in a figure of four leglock, but O’Halloran telegraphs the move and as Hunt turns around, he lashes out and shoves Hunt into his own corner, with his shoulder hitting the ring post. As Damon reaches up to tag in roc, Kimson slaps Hunt on the back and charges into the ring, exchanging right hands with the cruiserweight. Both men reach back and land stiff punches before Thug gains a slight advantage and lands a knee to the stomach and hits a dropkick to Kimson’s legs, forcing the Universal Champion to drop to one knee. Thug then backs up towards the ropes and bounces off them, hitting another dropkick and catching Kimson square in the face. As he drops to the canvas, roC hops to the top turnbuckle and soars off it within seconds, hitting the Nail In The Coffin top rope elbow. Huge impact as the elbow hits the mark, roC positions himself and hooks the leg… One… Two… Kickout!! Thug shakes his head and shows slight frustration as he gets to his feet, tagging in Damon who storms over to Kimson and nails an elbow. He jumps to his feet and hits another elbow, repeating this maneuver one more time before finally hooking the leg immediately after the third elbow… One… Two… Kickout!! Damon jumps to his feet and questions the officials count, as Kimson shakes the cobwebs and gets to his feet. The Irishman turns back to Kimson who is staggering away from him, spinning him around and landing a stiff right. However, The Hype blocks it and retaliates with a thumping blow of his own, which rocks O’Halloran. Kimson reaches for his stomach and winds up, nailing a clothesline that takes Damon down. He gets back to his feet and Kimson hits another clothesline, sending the Irishman down again. Thug attempts to scramble through the ropes to assist his partner, but Kimson cuts him off and lands a knee to his stomach, sending roC to the outside. “Kimson getting the adrenaline going here” noted Justin. As Kimson turns back to Damon, the Irishman is suddenly back to his feet and nails a high-impact DDT, driving Kimson’s head into the canvas. The official slides into position as Hunt dropped off the apron… One... Two… Th… Hunt broke the count up as he pulled the referee to the outside!! The bemused official barks at Hunt, who simply responds with a hard right and hurling the referee into the steel steps!! The fans roar as Hunt slides into the ring. “What the hell? He can’t do that! How dare he strike an official!!” barks Jack. “You better cool it Jack, he can hear you from where he is!” responded Justin, warning his colleague. Hunt is now back in the ring, but Damon charges at him, spotting The Postman’s antics with the referee on the outside. However, Hunt dodges the incoming clothesline and locks arms with his Irish foe, executing a huge Postman’s Knock!!! The fans roar again as Hunt jumps to his feet and stomps on Damon, who rolls underneath the bottom rope and to the outside. But just as Hunt is about to follow him, he spots Thug roC charging at him from out the corner of his eye. Hunt sidesteps and roC flies off the ropes, receiving a stiff kick from Hunt on the return. He then motions to hit the Last Post, but Thug blocks it and shoves Hunt away and into the referee, who had managed to climb onto the apron and scramble on the ropes!! The zebra is sent flying again and bounces onto the arena floor with a sickening thud! Hunt turns back to roC and attempts a right, but Thug dodges it and somehow hoists Hunt on to his shoulders and hits the roCing Horse!! There’s a mixed reaction from the people as Hunt is laid flat out on the canvas! But as Thug turns around, he’s met by a stiff kick to the midsection by Kimson, who then hooks the arms and motions to hit the History in the Making!! “Woah, woah, woah…that move is banned! He can’t use it!” screamed Jack. “Damn, I had forgotten all about it! Damn that Leya Forth! Kimson can only use the History in the Making if he wins this match!” said Justin. “And if he doesn’t win, then whoever pins him is the number one contender to his title!! C’mon roC!!” hollered Jack. A thunderous roar from the fans meets the impact as Kimson slowly gets to his feet. Hunt is showing signs of movement as he uses the ropes on the far side to get back to his feet, but as Kimson reaches down to the motionless roC, Damon appears from nowhere and spins Kimson around, executing The Plunge, a devastating neckbreaker!!! Booing and whistling respond as Damon scrambles to his feet. As he turns around, Hunt charges in with a kick to the stomach and then hits the Last Post!! There’s another huge roar from the crowd as Hunt storms around the ring with an irate expression, but is cut off by roC who is also somehow back to his feet and hits another roCing Horse on The Postman!! I have no idea what’s going to come next!” shouted Jack. Kimson is back to his feet as roC charges to him, both men exchanging hard rights as the brawling returns. With Damon and Hunt recovering from their blows respectively and Kimson still sat up in the center of the ring, the victorious Thug roC stumbled back up the aisle way with his arm raised high and big grin across his face.
