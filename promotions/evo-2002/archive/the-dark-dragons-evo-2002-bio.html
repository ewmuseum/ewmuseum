The Dark Dragons Biography Biography Yoshii was known in the past to have had some bad dealings with the Yakuza, Japanese Mafia. After his commissionership with eVolution’s tag team division fell through he returned to Japan along with the rest of eVolution. Apparently while there he made a new friend as well. Kin Sakoda is a man that wrestled quite extensively throughout Japan focusing most of his time on Pro Wrestling NOAH, Toryumon, and Zero-One. There have always been questions however about the legalities of his presence as he was long rumored to have ties with the Yakuza organization. If that is indeed true then it becomes obvious that Yoshii has worked out some deal with the organization to maintain his freedom and perhaps make up for past deeds by taking on a new partner. What kind of ties either man now has with the Yakuza in question but it’s apparent that they both have one goal in mind, domination. Wrestling Style Wrestling Style: Puroresu/Aerial and Technical. Lots and lots of kicks and martial arts from both men though Kin relies more on the technical wrestling side of things while Yoshii prefers to go to the air. Both men are considered to be quite stiff in the ring especially Kin due to his long stay in Japanese wrestling promotions where the workers tend to be a good deal stiffer in the ring. Together the two men also use tag team wrestling to the fullest with lots of double team moves.
