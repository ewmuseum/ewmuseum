Shocka Biography Real Name Jonathan McCade Nicknames The #1 Stunna Hometown St. Louis Missouri Birthday 07.04.1976 Height 6'2 Weight 270 Interests Killing stuff Favorite Pro Wrestlers RVD, HBK, Edge, Goldberg, Ultimate Warrior Fed Titles 1x Continetnal champion, 1x Universal champion Fed Awards Road to glory champion Biography Jonthan McCade grew up watching wrestling when he was 15 he became interested in the field of law. He took classes such as Business Law and other Law classes. He graduated high school and went into Law school he dropped out after a short one year of bleak attendance. He started training because he had nothing better to do, one day a wrestling promoter saw him lifting weights and saw something in him. He brought him to a small federation where he taught Jonathan the basic moves. Upon his first match the same man that taught him and found him snapped his leg. After this Jonathan was not sure if he wanted to return to the ring and bounces back and forth between meaningless desk jobs until his leg completely healed and he could gain the muscle back in it. When he came back the trainer got what he had coming as Jonathan broke his nose and left arm. The trainer banned Jonatahn from wresting in his organization ever again. Jonathan snapped and again beat the man down this resulted in him pressing charges and Jonathan being sent to prison for 6 months on assualt charges. When he got out he had beefed up by 20 pounds and was even more physically impressive than before. By this time he had gained ring rust and went back to the local federation the man let him back in because of his size. They again went through all of the training movement and Jonathan gained a whole new persona, a new name, and a new attitude. He was now known as Shocka and was ready to destroy anything in his way. This is what led him to the BWF where he gained the world title twice in 6 months defeating Blazin Spyder and Sabin. Shocka was noticed by Michael Parker who took him under his win and taught him about the backstage aspect of the business. This led Shocka to the PWF where he had a short reign as US champion beating Thug roC body guard Machine. Shocka went from there to a federation that Michael Parker himself created the WiW. Many famous names came from this federation its where Assassin made his name. Shocka never gained glory in the WiW as Michael Parker held him down intentionally. From here Shocka left to Jew 3 where he had the chance to win the Hi-Octane title but was pushed from a 3 tier cage. The recope from this injury came quickly as the announce table broke the fall and his hand. Shocka came to the EDW after this and went on a 4 match winning streak before loosing to Too Xtreme and then leaving the federation. He left to go to the restarting of the NRCW, he went back won the world title lost it and then the federation closed nothing really important. He was welcomed back to the EdW where he beat the Universal Champion Josh Sutton in a non-title match and a few others before losing to Shane Lawless in a questionable match as Big Daddy Dreamer interefered never clearly detrimining a winner. After the EdW eventually died Shocka went to ReAcT wrestling and did nothing but invade Soldout Wrestling he may have had one match but he doesn't remember. He ended up in Soldout Wrestling and shot to the top...or as high as he could without winning a title. He was in line for a National Title shot but Soldout changed ownership causing him to leave to eVolution after American Nightmare. He started out his career in eVolution taunting a 'big star' in PPC they traded blows and wins but Shocka ended up winning the feud at X-Rated and gaining the #1 Contendership to the Continental title. Before he got the shot tho he had to face Shodan and he came out on the lesser end as Erin Christoph ran down to the ring and hit Shocka in the face with a dildo. Distracting Shocka just long enough for Shodan to put him thru a table and get the victory. The next week Shocka went on to beat Erin Christoph for the title he loves so much with the help of Tyler Davidson. Shocka went onto lose because of Asis to Jason Jameson this week the most current as of this writing.
