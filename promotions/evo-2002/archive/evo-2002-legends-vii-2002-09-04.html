Legends VII Transcript Live on Splink Parp! From The Bi-Lo Center ~ Greenville, South Carolina 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night September 4th, 2002 Legends VII comes to you live from the Bi-Lo Center in Greenville, South Carolina. Following the most controversial episode of Legends to date the executives of Splink Parp were less than thrilled with eVolution and demanded a toned down show this week. With eVolution’s promise of being completely uncensored will they adhere to the demands of the network which carries them? Judging by the proposed line up for the show it doesn’t look good for eVolution/Splink Parp relations. A Cell match, a hardcore match and just some matches that scream brutality are lined up for the show. The show will kick off with Sean Mason and Seara teaming to battle The HeartBreak Chick and her partner for the evening Quasar the Great. This tag match comes to us straight from Legends VI and the culmination of the Python-Quasar singles match. Seara and Sean Mason had squared off last week but following the match joined together to battle back against a common enemy in Thug roC and Quasar the Great. Now the HeartBreaker finds herself teamed with Quasar who has a title she wants in order to battle against the team of Mason and Seara. The final televised Cell match on Legends features Alias against LeRoy Mohammed and the Cell Champion Jinx though the title has been disbanded. Jinx is not at all happy with the decision by Daniel Somers and Tyler Davidson to disband the division and plans to make an impact at all costs during this match. Alias meanwhile is a man bred to fight and is certainly in his element this week as he looks to make an impact of his own at the expense of the only man to hold the Cell Championship. Shodan gets his long awaited rematch against Asis with the Rising Star Title on the line. It was Asis who had defeated Shodan and Brad Kelley in a triple threat match at X-Rated in order to win the title and now he must defend it against a man he barely beat only weeks ago. Shodan a one time member of EdW looks to continue his winning streak this week against Asis and capture his first taste of eVolution gold but after last week he must be keeping an eye out for The Unlimited and The HeartBreak Chick. Shocka is the new Continental Champion and now the first person in his way is Jason Jameson, a former EdW Platinum Champion. Shocka must now face an even bigger challenge on his way to Digital Destruction and defeat the red hot Jason Jameson. Jameson has been seemingly unstoppable since arriving in eVolution and a victory over Shocka could propel him right to the top of the rankings. Big Daddy Dreamer goes one on one with L2J, provided he is able to find his way out of jail before the show can start. Big Daddy Dreamer has had some trouble of his own lately and continues struggling in singles competition as he tries to rebound here against L2J. L2J meanwhile has his sights set squarely on Impact and Digital Destruction. Can he stay focused enough to survive in the ring against an athlete the caliber of Big Daddy Dreamer? Or will L2J fall to the Dreamer Crossface? Troy Pierce and Impact, the monster who is apparently in the pocket of Daniel Somers for his war against the EdW loyalists. This week he gets his hands on one of those men in a one on one hardcore match. Troy Pierce has scored recent victories over the team of Big Daddy Dreamer and Xander and this week he looks to upsetting the Hollywood Monster and possible number one contender to the Universal Title. Impact however has his sights set solely on destroying Troy Pierce and anyone else who gets in his way of the Universal Title. King and Boone perhaps one of the unlikeliest teams in eVolution goes against a team that can also lay claim to that title. Xander and Sovereign both men former OSW superstars team up for the first time in this battle. In recent weeks Jamal Kage has always found a way to work himself into matches involving Sovereign and it remains to be seen if he can do it again this week. After the vicious battle they had last week there is no telling what Boone and Sovereign could be set to do this week. Xander and King meanwhile continue their bitter rivalry, which will one way or another come to an end at Digital Destruction inside of the Cell.
