In Your Face with Tyler Davidson

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pre-recorded footage "Welcome fans today in a special interview I have here with me former $W superstar and CEO of the multi-million dollar wrestling promotion, eVolutin, Tyler Davidson" Danny smiled with a huge grin on his face while waiting for Tyler to respond back. "Great to be here," Tyler responded. "It's about time I get some things cleared up around the Fedwars world." "And today you will get your chance as we will discuss a variety issuse as well clearing the air up on a few other minor details as well." Danny stated. Setting the tone for converstation. Danny turned away from the camera and now towards Tyler, smiling before starting with his first question of the day. "As we all know Mr. Davidson before stepping away from the ring to take your turn in the executive chair, you were quite a hit." Danny said, obvisously not knowing about Mr. Davidson past. "Is that correct?" "Was quite a hit?" Tyler replied. "What is this past tense crap? I'm still a hit no matter where I go." "Well would you mind explaining your departure from $W? What caused this sudden change in heart as you were a member for quite sometime?" Danny asked quickly changing the subject to something worth talking about. "Really isn't a whole lot to it," Tyler began. "I've been in $W since day one. I was one of the first members announced during their press conference and quite frankly I did everything I could there. I even took Fedwars.org number one poster boy Justin Pearce and turned him into a joke. There wasn't anything for me to accomplish in $W and I decided that eVolution deserved my full concentration at this time. Perhaps someday I may decide to make my return to $W." Tyler continued. "Honestly though I don't see that happening anytime soon. The company has gone through a lot of changes in the recent weeks and I can't honestly say that I feel they were for the better so right now as far as I'm concerned eVo is where it's at." "So basically your saying from being in both promotions at times, eVolution blows the water out of $W when it comes to in ring wrestling and pure sports entertainment at it's best?" Danny asked opening the envelope for Tyler to come out his mouth in a vulgar way about $W. "Damn right that's what I'm saying," Tyler answered. "Nothing against $W but the group of talent they have there now cannot even hope to compete with the talent that we have signed to contracts in eVolution. In the end it will be only a matter of time before eVolution buries $W under a pile of rubble just like everyone else that gets in our way." "It's funny you say that Mr. Davidson." Danny says with a huge grin on his face. "Because since aquiring my job here with eVolution and during my journalist duties, I've noticed alot of fans speaking upon $W, but from thier lastest show Gensis 14 how would you rate it as a wrestler in the show and as a viewer on the outside?" Danny asked. "It was a good effort put forth by the talent but not many other things managed to click on that night," Tyler answered. "They have lost some key talents with myself, Thug Roc, and Tony Rolo having departed recently so it leaves quite a bit of a void at the top of the card there. The key they need to remember is to get the reliable and popular stars brought up there to fill that void. Going by the Genesis 14 show they aren't really doing that to well. Why else would a jackass like Josh Sutton be main eventing already? This is a guy that has gone from fed to fed and obviously doesn't have a good reputation but that doesn't seem to matter there right now. It's going to be one of many things that I think could contribute to an untimely death for $W if they're not very careful." "So in regards of feeling this way, Mr. Davidson. Do you feel by the draft, that it was merly name recogintion on determining which feds got picked and where they went." Danny asked rather a interesting question staring back at Tyler for a response. "Yes I think that had quite a bit to do with it." Tyler answered."Probably quite a bit of money involved in it too. $W went first, BMW second, and then some fed no one, including Dave Jacobs, has ever heard of went third? Something doesn't seem quite right to me there." "Well has anything ever seemed right in Fedwars." Danny answered "Politics has ruled fedwars from day one and now is knowing too different." Danny stated in a stern tone. "What will be your comback to such disrespect by the other networks that let you slip by.?" Danny questioned. "I'll just have to make it my mission to rip through each and every single one of them in the ratings." Tyler replied. "A guy like Mr. Batee wants to pass eVolution up for a washed up $W that doesn't even have their best star anymore than that's fine. He'll have to learn to regret it at the end of each ratings week when eVolution is standin on the top of the charts." "With the word ratings that brings me to another topic I was wanting to discuss with you." Danny said very eagerly. "Being drafted number two by the Hogans Brief network you must be impressed with the feds accompanying you or shall I say engaging in battle with eVo throughout the fedwars season." asked Danny. "Impressed?" Tyler repeated. "Not in the least quite honestly. The Warehouse is nothing more than a talent raiding organization as we've already seen. They've stolen talent already from $W and now they stole talent from eVolution. That doesn't sit well with me and unlike $W I fight back. That's the reason as most people know, we have already signed a TW main eventer to join eVolution in the coming weeks." "Yes, from what I heard, that is in fact true. Evolution has purchased the rights of a certain, TW talent. Would you mind dropping us any clues as to who it maybe?" Danny asked. "Normally I would love to come out here and drop a name or some hints," Tyler began. "But I really don't want to give away to much to the people at home and especially to that drunken idiot Justin Williams. So for right now all you will know is that it is fact that a TW star is making the jump to eVolution very soon." "I must say that is shocking news Mr. Davidson to hear come from your mouth." Danny added. "As you heard right here fans, Tyler Davidson, CEO of Evolution has offical signed a TW wrestler to a contract. If you want to know who check out Legends III, this Wednesday at 8:00 on your favoriite wrestling network, CBN ." Danny smiled before the cameracut to commerical. But before the scene was offically threw we can hear someone shouting. "Your favorite wrestling network, Hogans Brief." then Danny realizing he missed a line before the scene fades totally to black. Danny Ruble
