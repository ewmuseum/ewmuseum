Legends XIV Transcript Live on OSE! From The Gaylord Entertainment Center ~ Nashville, Tennessee 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night November 6th, 2002 With only a few weeks until Rise of the Legends the tension begins to mount throughout eVolution. Last week everyone saw a rough week occur for Daniel Somers with an attack from Tommy Michaels, an attack from Jason Jameson, and the apparent appointment of Bobby Stormbringer as the new commissioner while Tyler Davidson is recuperating from injuries. This week the tournaments will continue as all three have matches taking place on the card with a main event which is sure to be explosive. Kai Kua comes off an impressive debut victory one week ago as he defeated Ben Juventud in a dark match prior to Legends. This week he has a much tougher challenge left ahead of him as he battles against New-TV’s very own Mark Steele. This will be the debut match for young Mark as he tries to stake a claim in eVolution with a victory against Kai Kua who promises much success in eVolution. Another in ring debut for a superstar happens when Lee eXodus steps into the ring against Seara. One can only think that his good friend Thug roC has a bit to do with this match be part of the Rising Star Title tournament. None of it will matter however if Seara gets things going her way this time. She is still coming off the brutal table match at Faith so she can be expected to be at less than one hundred percent as she presents the first challenge to eXodus. The Road to Glory continues for either HBC or Jinosyde while one will go home bitterly disappointed. It was two weeks ago at Faith that both HBC and Jinosyde were victorious in their respected match ups but tonight only one can leave with the feeling of success. HBC will have to keep a look over her shoulder however as Big Daddy Dreamer can be coming from any direction, as evidenced by last week. Despite suffering a loss at Faith, Tommy Michaels had a pretty damn good week last week at the expense of Somers. This week he looks to continue his streak with a one on match against Jason Steele in the Road to Glory. Steele fell a bit short in the Rising Star tournament but has continued along with Shocka in the Tag Team Tournament. With the backing of Thug roC no doubt, Steele will look to derail Michaels and his run for some gold in eVolution before he can get started. The new Continental Champion goes head to head with a man who is one of the favorites to win not only the Rising Star Title but the High Impact Championship as well in Sean Mason. Mason picked up a victory last week against Jay Stylez to move on in one tournament and this week will try and continue forward in the next. This week he gets a chance to move on in the Road to Glory but in order to do that he must pull off a victory against the newly crowned Continental Champion, Jason Jameson who won’t go down lightly. The Tag Team Title Tournament continues with this one explosive match up. Sovereign and L2J, the Universal Champions, must find a way to coexist this show in order to continue a quest for more gold. The team standing in their way however, has proven to be no pushover as the ever dangerous Unlimited continues to grow in members and strength. With the recent addition of Asis to the group they have gotten even more dangerous as evidenced the past two weeks with his attacks on Scott Frost. This week the match should be a brutal one as these two teams vie for the tag team titles but can Sovereign and L2J exist as a team long enough to win could be the deciding x-factor. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Scott Frost, Big Daddy Dreamer, The Omen Brotherhood, and many more!
