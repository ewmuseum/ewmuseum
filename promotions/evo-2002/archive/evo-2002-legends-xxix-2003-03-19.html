Legends XXIX Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Safeco Field ~ Seattle, Washington 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night March 19th, 2003 Legends had an explosive show last week that looks like it will no doubt be easily topped by the line up that has been prepared for the show this week. The Universal and Continental Titles will both be on the line and we will see the Tiers of eVolution on Legends for the first time ever. In addition to that the tag team battles will continue tonight with three more matches that can have an effect on Unbreakable’s WarGames match. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Last week Fiona made her in ring debut and picked up an upset victory over Nik VanCore with a huge assist from Xander and Asis of The Unlimited. This week now she finds herself paired off against the massive Chuck where The Unlimited isn’t likely to be much of a help to her. Chuck on the other hand is going to be looking for a little redemption as he suffered a stunning loss against Troy Pierce two weeks ago and he is now intent on establishing himself as one of the future stars in eVolution. This battle has been building for weeks now and it will finally go down on Legends as Xavier Citizen and Luther Wisdom will battle with the ULWN Legacy hanging in the balance. Both men have been long time friends and allies of Malcolm King, the originator of the ULWN but only one of them can continue on with the tradition. This match will decide which of the two raving lunatics will do it. The obvious feud between The BodySnatchers and The Syndicate continue this week as HBC battles against Dolo Bentswinger in a body bag match that also has Luther Wisdom and Big Daddy Dreamer both barred from ringside. This leaves one to believe that HBC is likely to find herself at a very serious disadvantage as she tries and battles against Dolo and also knowing that the rest of The BodySnatchers will no doubt be lurking near by ready to strike if they are needed. Last week Playaz Inc managed to defeat The Crimson Sun to pick up their second victory since arriving in eVolution but their win was short lived when they found themselves attacked by Team Network, Jason Relms and High Impact along with their managed Ms. Network. This week the two teams will clash in battle as Playaz Inc look to teach the newcomers a lesson in their own way. Can Team Network survive against the former PCW Tag Team Champions or will their eVolution career’s be short lived? This was a match that was originally scheduled to go down at Unbreakable but thanks to Daniel Somers and an attack against Pierce last week by The Unlimited it has instead been pushed up to Legends. As if the assault against Pierce last week wasn’t enough Somers added insult to injury when he gave Pierce exactly what he wanted but he forced Pierce to put his own career on the line in order to get it. And just to prevent any interference from anyone the two men have been placed inside the Tiers of eVolution and will battle until only one of them is left standing. It will be Asis defending his Continental Title against Troy Pierce who has to put his career on the line in what will surely be an epic battle. Last week Frost came out and successfully defended his Universal Title against Erin Christoph despite the efforts of The Unlimited and later found himself having to deal with the monster Sovereign until James Kimson made his stunning return to Legends and went after the man that tried to end his career. Frost has agreed to defend his title against Jason Jameson and it will now go down on Legends XXIX as Jason receives his first ever shot at the Universal Title and it will be against a man he has long considered a friend. The two of them are sure to put on an incredible contest and hope that there won’t be any interference to taint the match itself. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Sovereign, Lee eXodus, James Kimson and many of eVolution’s superstars!
