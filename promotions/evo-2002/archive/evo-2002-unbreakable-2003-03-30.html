Unbreakable Transcript Live on Pay Per View! From The MGM Grand ~ Las Vegas, Nevada 7:59PM EST Sunday Night March 30th, 2003 The raving lunatic himself, Xavier Citizen, takes a break from his battles with Luther Wisdom and The Syndicate to challenge the longest reigning champion currently in eVolution, Seara, for the eVolved Title. With the random stipulations for each match always in effect at the request of the champion this one has been made into a submissions only match which curiously favors Xavier Citizen more than it would Seara the person that actually determined the stipulations for the match. Last month it was Nik VanCore that screwed Xander out of winning the eVolved Title from Seara and now the two of them will finally settle their differences in an environment that both men have always found to be a home away from home for them. Xander has said he has a lot to prove to people now that seem to think he has fallen off from the status that he once found himself positioned at in the earlier days of eVolution and with a young tiger in the form of VanCore nipping at his heels now would be the time for him to do just that. Three teams will fight on the inside of the War Games structure and the Tag Team Titles will be hanging in the balance. The Syndicate and The BodySnatchers are two teams that have been around eVolution for quite some time now and they find themselves caged in with a team that has exploded onto the scene in Team Network, Jason Relms and High Impact along with their manager Miranda. In only two weeks time Team Network has already worked themselves into a title shot and are determined to make the most of it in War Games against two teams that have held those very same Tag Team Titles before. Both Yoshii Tanaka’s have been stalking Erin Christoph for the past several weeks now and at the request of Christoph this match will now go down but there is no telling just which Yoshii he will get or even who the hell Yoshii is! Christoph claims to have an idea as to the identity of the man but Yoshii himself has already promised that before this match will even start all the answers everyone wants will be answered and Christoph himself will see the end of his days in eVolution. Erin Christoph will apparently pull double duty this show as right after his No DQ match with Yoshii Tanaka he will go on to face his fellow Unlimited member and the Continental Champion, Asis. This match was originally scheduled to be Asis defending against Troy Pierce but with the injury Pierce suffered at the hands of Asis two weeks ago he found his career come to a screeching halt once again. Asis issued an open challenge a week later and it was Daniel Somers that decided for the benefit of The Unlimited that it would be Erin Christoph who takes the spot in that match and no matter what would happen the title would be kept within The Unlimited. But would Christoph even be able to make it to the match after his battle against Yoshii?! For the longest time Shocka and Morpheus Omega were the closest of friends and allies but all of that is now in the past as the two men have set forth on a war that has left Sky Night lying dead in its wake so far. A murder that Morpheus Omega was actually arrested for this past week on Legends that also threw his very presence at Unbreakable into doubt. If he is in Las Vegas for this battle it is certain to be a very violent encounter as these two men must destroy one another until they are able to stuff their opponent into the back of an ambulance and slam the doors shut. James Kimson and Sovereign have for over two months now been at each other’s throats with the wife of Sovereign, the sister of Jameson Kimson, Leya Forth caught right in the middle of it all. Apparently she has been firmly on the side of her husband but with his erratic behavior as of late there is no telling where her true loyalties might lie but one thing is for certain. If the Omega/Shocka battle isn’t violent enough for you then this match certainly will be as the only way to win is to strap your opponent down onto a stretcher after you are satisfied with the beating that you have dished out. Two weeks ago Jason Jameson stunned the eVolution world when he pulled off a shocking upset on Legends to capture the Universal Title from Scott Frost in what many people had considered the best match to be aired on Legends to this date. Now the two men will step back into the ring together once again for what is certain to be an incredible rematch. Jameson had barely managed to pull out the victory two weeks ago and now he must find a way to do it again. Was the first time just a fluke or is Jameson really the better man of the two? Even Frost and Jameson are unsure of that but their long friendship has survived a lot so far and they are certain it will survive this as they battle again for the Universal Title and to see just who the better man will be at Unbreakable. As soon as Frost made it back to his feet Jameson had moved right back in and grabbed him from behind before he lifted him up onto his shoulders in a torture rack position but the bottom dropped out quickly as Jameson let Frost come slamming face first down to the mat with a reverse death valley driver and again made the cover. One Two And again Frost got his shoulder up just before the three count! Jameson sat up with another shocked look on his face and he shook his head as he got back to his feet. Jameson quickly grabbed hold of Frost and began to lift him up also as he set Frost up for the second time this match with Perfection but for the second time Frost caught him with an elbow right to the side of the head. The shot connected and Jameson staggered away as Frost grabbed him and quickly delivered his own version of the move as he brought Jameson down into the mat with the Cold Front and went for the cover himself. One Two Three…..NO! Jameson again got the shoulder up! “Frost can’t believe it and I can’t even believe Frost was in position for the win just then!” Justin said. ”How does he keep doing that?!” Jack asked sounding a bit annoyed by it all. ”He’s just good,” Justin told him. “The only question is who is better here tonight? Frost or Jameson?” “They both…..” ”Yeah I know already,” Justin said as he cut off Jack before he could finish his statement. ”I wasn’t even going to say that!” Jack protested. “I was going to say they have both beaten the hell out of each other already and neither one of them have any business still standing up with some of the moves that have been hit.” Frost and Jameson were both slowly getting back to their feet again and it was obvious that both men had been worn down by the battle they have gone through so far but neither one had a clear advantage as Frost landed the first shot upon getting back to their feet and Jameson came back with one of his own. Jameson fired off a second one and caught Frost who came back with another one but Jameson ducked under it and went behind Frost before he put him into a full nelson and took him over with a full nelson suplex and a bridge for the cover. One Two Frost again just barely got his shoulder up! Jameson got back to his feet quickly and grabbed onto the leg of Frost and he began to look for the single leg crab but Frost was to close to the ropes and he quickly managed to grab onto them and forced the break. Jameson let go of Frost’s leg but he reached right back down and picked Frost up again before he went for an irish whip. Frost countered it though and pulled Jameson forward right into a kick to the midsection and he followed it with a vicious Frost Effect that drove Jameson head first into the mat in a very awkward position and Frost made the cover. One Two And it was over as Frost had survived the battle with the third slap of the mat by the referee and the sound of the bell ringing and the fans cheering not just Frost’s victory but the effort that both men had put forth into the match. “Your winner by pinfall and once again the NEW eVolution Universal Champion……..Scott Frost!” “After a grueling, grueling battle Scott Frost has for the second time become eVolution Universal Champion and he is the first man to accomplish that feat!” Justin said. ”Jameson looks like he might be hurt!” Jack pointed out. ”His head struck the mat in a rather awkward position but hopefully he’s just exhausted from the match,” Justin suggested. “To be on the safe side though the medics are already in the ring and checking up on him and Frost as well who is no doubt exhausted from this battle.” “I hate to say it but I can’t complain to much about that match as the two of them kept beating the hell out of each other every which way possible,” Jack said surprising Justin. ”That they did and tonight it seems as though Frost was the better man,” Justin put forth. “I don’t think we have seen the last of these two just yet. We’re just about out of time fans so as the medics check on Jameson in the ring we will have to leave you but we will have an update on him Thursday night at Legends to be certain!” The show began to come to an end with Scott Frost on his feet and the Universal Title once again in his hands as he too watched on as the medics checked on Jameson who was showing no signs of movement as the scene faded to black.
