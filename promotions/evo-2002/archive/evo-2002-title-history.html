Universal Championship: Unbreakable October 21st, 2001: Cori Simmons won ten man triple cage for vacant title. Turmoil 9 December 14th, 2001: Urban Legend defeated Troy Pierce, Justin Famous, and Cori Simmons. When Worlds Collide January 6th, 2002: Justin Famous defeated Urban Legend with Troy Pierce as guest referee. Turmoil 12 January 11th, 2002: Troy Pierce defeats Justin Famous. Pierce is stripped of title by [name withheld] due to interference in the match and a rematch is signed for a week later including former champion Urban Legend. Turmoil 13 January 18th, 2002: Troy Pierce defeats Justin Famous and Urban Legend. Tainted Love February 10th, 2002: Xavier Citizen defeats, Troy Pierce, Justin Famous, and Cori Simmons to unify the Universal Title with his Urban Legend Wrestling Nation Title. End of Days March 10th, 2002: Big Daddy Dreamer defeats Xavier Citizen. Turmoil 20 March 22nd, 2002: Cori Simmons defeats Big Daddy Dreamer. Lethal Injection April 7th, 2002: Josh Sutton defeats Cori Simmons and Big Daddy Dreamer. Turmoil 21 April 12th, 2002: Malcolm King defeats Josh Sutton. EdW closes down following the departure of talents to [name withheld] rival New Era Wrestling which closes a week later. All titles were then made vacant after the purchase of EdW by Tyler Davidson and [name withheld] The Universal Title was the one title brought over to eVolution from EdW. X-Rated August 14th, 2002: Sovereign wins an eight man tournament to become first champion of eVolution. Faith October 27th, 2002: Sovereign and L2J battle to a double knockout in a House of Pain match. The title is then ruled to be held by both men as a result of the draw. L2J and Sovereign can both defend the title but if one loses it then they both lose it. Rise of the Legends November 24th, 2002: L2J defeats Sovereign to become sole champion. Nightmare Before Christmas December 22nd, 2002: Shocka defeats L2J. Tainted Love February 23rd, 2003: Scott Frost defeats Shocka. Legends XXIX March 19th, 2003: Jason Jameson defeats Scott Frost. Unbreakable March 30th, 2003: Scott Frost defeats Jason Jameson. From The Ashes May 25th, 2003: Xander defeats Scott Frost. X-Rated August 31st, 2003: James Kimson defeats Xander. Continental Championship: Legends 1 July 10th, 2002: Erin Christoph defeats Xander, Eric Badger, and HeartBreak Chick to win vacant title. Legends VII August 28th, 2002: Shocka defeats Erin Christoph. Faith October 27th, 2002: Shodan defeats Shocka. Shodan vacates title following the match due to the circumstances he won it under. Legends XIII October 31st, 2002: Jason Jameson defeats Tommy Michaels and The HeartBreak Chick to win vacant title. Legends XVI November 28th, 2002: Shodan defeats Jason Jameson. Legends XXII January 22nd, 2003: Big Daddy Dreamer defeats Asis in a tournament final for vacant title. Legends XXIV February 5th, 2003: Asis defeated Big Daddy Dreamer. From The Ashes May 25th, 2003: Sean Mason defeated Asis. ReVelations July 27th, 2003: Ryan Mason defeated Thug roC. X-Rated August 31st, 2003: Thug roC defeated Ryan Mason. Title was unified with the ULWN Heavyweight Title and renamed The United Title. United Championship: X-Rated August 31, 2003: Thug roC defeated Ryan Mason. Rising Star Championship: X-Rated August 14th 2002: Asis defeats Shodan and Brad Kelley. Legends IX September 25th 2002: Matt Boone defeats Asis. Matt Boone is fired for attacking Tyler Davidson and the title is vacated. Rise of the Legends November 24th, 2002: Sean Mason defeats Seara in a tournament final to win vacant title. Title becomes unified with the High Impact Championship to form the eVolved Championship. High Impact Championship: X-Rated August 14th, 2002: Quasar the Great defeats Sean Mason, HeartBreak Chick, and Kin Ryo. Digital Destruction September 18th, 2002: The HeartBreak Chick defeated Sean Mason, Quasar the Great, and Seara. Legends XI October 9th, 2002: Thug roC defeated The HeartBreak Chick and Quasar the Great. Legends XVIII December 11th, 2002: Scott Frost defeated Thug roC. Thug roC strips Scott Frost of the title before booking a title match between Seara and Lee eXodus. Legends XX January 8th, 2003: Seara defeated Lee eXodus to win the vacant title. Title becomes unified with the Rising Star Championship to form the eVolved Championship. The eVolved Championship: Legends XXI January 15th, 2003: Seara defeats Sean Mason to unify the High Impact and Rising Star Titles to create the eVolved Championship. Unbreakable March 30th, 2003: Xavier Citizen defeats Seara. From The Ashes May 25th, 2003: Hedges defeated Malcolm Luther Xavier. Legends XXXVIII June 5th, 2003: Hedges and Luther Wisdom gained a double fall in a double championship match making both men Co-eVolved Champions. Genesis Pit #2 June 21st, 2003: Eric Knight defeated Luther Wisdom. A New Dawn June 29th, 2003: Scott Evans defeated Eric Knight. Digital Destruction October 5th, 2003: Aaron Justice defeated Scott Evans and Matt Hunt. Faith November 9th, 2003: Matt Hunt defeated Aaron Justice. The Cell Championship: X-Rated August 14th, 2002: Jinx defeats Eric Knight, Marcus Escobar, and LeRoy Mohammed. Title was later suspended by Tyler Davidson. From The Ashes May 25th, 2003: Title is reinstated by Tyler Davidson and appointed Cameron Griffin as Director of The Cell. New champion will be determined at A New Dawn. A New Dawn June 29th, 2003: Trevor Norman won the Chambers of Survival Match to become new champion. Legends XLIII July 16th, 2003: Jinx defeated Trevor Norman. ReVelations July 27th, 2003: Trevor Norman defeated Jinx. Legends XLV August 7th, 2003: Damon O'Halloran defeated Trevor Norman. Digital Destruction October 5th, 2003: Cameron Griffin defeated Damon O'Halloran. Tag Team Championship: Rise of the Legends November 24th, 2002: The Unlimited defeat Shocka and Xavier Citizen in a tournament final to win the vacant titles. New Year's eVolution January 26th, 2003: The HeartBreak Chick and Luther Wisdom defeated The Unlimited, Big Daddy Dreamer and Malcolm King, and Strictly Prohibited and Yoshii Tanaka to win titles. Legends XXIV February 5th, 2003: The Unlimited defeated The HeartBreak Chick and Luther Wisdom. Legends XXVI Februrary 19th, 2003: Luther Wisdom and The HeartBreak Chick defeated [name withheld] and Malcolm King. Tainted Love February 23rd, 2003: The Body Snatchers won a six team Tag Gauntlet. Tainted Love February 23rd, 2003: Twisted Reality defeats The Body Snatchers in a match that didn't air on the pay per view. Twisted Reality refuses to defend titles in the thirty day period but holds titles hostage. [name withheld] unsanctions the belts and brings in a new set of Tag Team Titles to be defended at Unbreakable in A Match Beyond. Unbreakable March 30th, 2003: Team Network defeats The Syndicate and The BodySnatchers to capture vacant titles. Legends XXXIV April 23rd, 2003: Twisted Reality defeats Team Network. Legends XXXVIII June 5th, 2003: Luther Wisdom and Hedges defeated Twisted Reality and The Syndicate. Genesis Pit #2 June 21st, 2003: St. Violence defeated Luther Wisdom and Hedges. eVo1 July 3rd, 2003: The Syndicate defeated St. Violence. Legends L September 17th, 2003: Morpheus defeated The Syndicate. Legends L September 17th, 2003: Tok'n and QBX defeated Morpheus Omega. Digital Destruction October 5th, 2003: Thug roC and Shaft-Man defeated Tok'n and QBX. Digital Destruction October 5th, 2003: Tok'n and QBX defeated Thug roC and Shaft-Man. X-Rated November 9th, 2003: St. Violence defeated The Syndicate, The Industry and Astonishing Entertainment.
