Seara Biography Real Name Katherine Frost Hometown Chicago, Illinois Birthday January 17th 1980 Height 6' Weight 140 Interests Music, writing and my boyfriend Fed Titles First eVolved champion Fed Awards First ever eVolved Champion. Longest running title holder in eVolution(eVolved title) Biography In Ring Attire: In the Ring, Seara wears a pair of baggy black cargo pants, down the right leg of the pants, it says 'Seara' in large purple letters with a silver outline, down the left leg it has Frost written in large ice blue letters with a white outline. Seara wears high boots to wrestle in, the right of which has a purple and silver design on it to match the colours of the leg of her pants, while the left boot has the same design but in ice blue and white to match the colour of the left pant leg. The female's top is a low cut black top that stops just above her naval, on the front it has a logo of a large 'F' in a white design, and on the back in plain white letters, it says 'The New Era'. Seara has shoulder lenght Blonde hair that sits around her face in a sensible yet tempting fashion. Entrance: In Memory by Shinedown begins and the arena is plunged into complete darkness as the guitar fades in, as the music kicks in, the arena starts to flash in a frenzy of coloured lights. 'Some of the ugliest things took the longest time to make. Some of the easiest habits Are the hardest to break' As the vocals come in, a spotlight comes up on Seara standing in her black outfit at the top of the ramp, her arms high above her head, as the vocals build up, the female brings her arms down, the arena erupts into a display of pyrotechniques. 'And I can't wait for you To catch up with me... And I cant live in the past' With the start of the line 'And drown myself', Seara makes her way down to the ring at a run and slides in under the bottom rope, jumping to her feet, she raises her hands high again and acts up to the huge cheer coming from the crowd who chant her name. Bio:Born in Fort Worth Texas, Seara has an awkward childhood, she is the middle child of three, and has the constant worry that her dad will end his career and life in the Wrestling ring. At the age of three, Seara is on her way to the Chicago arena with her older brother, her baby brother and her mother, where they will watch the biggest match of her fathers career, when a drunk driver causes a large car pile up on the highway. Seara and her brothers survive with a few broken bones, cuts and bruises, however, Seara watches as her mother dies next to her, holding her hand. For the next year, she is closer to her father than ever before, and begins to train with him as a wrestler. When she reaches the age of 5, her father has a new girlfriend with a son she hates, proposing to her, Seara's father agrees to move to Chicago, Illinois, his fiancés hometown. No wedding date is set because Seara begins to become a troublesome child. By the age of seven, she has been expelled from her school, and is starting to disappear at nights, on three occasions she is escorted home by the police, and is put on a missing children’s list after going missing for two weeks. She is found on her grandfather’s ranch in Fort Worth, but not even he knows she is there. Once again she is escorted home, the story hits national headlines. Putting her anger to good use she once again begins to train with her father with the intent of becoming a great wrestler. But continues to disappear at nights, sometimes going missing for a few days. By the age of 15 she is 5'11 and 120lb, and she has been trialed as a minor for theft, burglary, and grand theft auto, and is put under strict probation rules, which state she must spend all her time at school and at home helping her fathers still fiancé Gail Pearson. The only time she is allowed out of the house is to accompany her father to wrestling matches. Eventually her probation is stopped, and Seara goes back to the streets and the one friend she has. When she is 17, she takes her newly acquired pick up truck and runs into Gail’s son Vinnie. Seara's midnight excursions are at a point where they become trips to another country, so that she can visit her only friend, who was moved there by his parents. In July of 1997, aged 17, she takes enough money with her to support herself in a youth hostile, and leaves the country for just over a year. On her return, she decides to knuckle down, stay focused and use her anger and hating for Gail in the wrestling ring, she joins the local wrestling fed, and is coached by her father until Vinnie crashes a car, and leaves her father paralyzed from the waist down, never to wrestle again. Seara promises herself revenge on Vinnie, no matter how long it takes, and begins to train him to join her in the ring. Aged 22, only four years later, Seara introduces Vinnie into her fed as a new up and coming star, but with her two tag team partners in this fed, she is the most powerful wrestler, and is also the only female there, as a death match champion, she vows to her friends she will end him in a death match, and vows to herself that after Vinnie is out of her way, she will leave her tag team partners where they are to join a newer and professional fed, eVolution. Seara begins to make a big impression on eVolution, and before her first fight, she has already established her attitude towards life and other people. It is not long before her constantly hidden friend is unveiled as Asis, another wrestler from the depths of Chicago. The pair seem to have a great friendship or respect for each other until Seara's first PPV when she is viciously attacked in the ring by Asis and put out of action for a couple of weeks. Seara soon begins to get more agitated by her situation and it seems that her personality is starting to change as she becomes more unpredictable and wild. In the run up to Faith, her second PPV, Seara and Asis' feud continues, and the pair become more violent towards each other in the ring, although Asis begins to start to show a territorial ownership over Seara, and begins to attack people to keep her for himself. His first is ECHO Blackwhisper who the week before had showed an interest in the female wrestler. Now in the last Legends before Faith, Seara watches her older brother Jake being brutally attacked by Asis, who leaves her brother in the parking lot with a broken neck after Seara dropkicked a chair into the face of Asis at the end of his match. Seara will calm down a bit after the death of her older brother. She becomes more caring and involved with her family, and seems to want to keep life going on more now than she ever had since the death of her mother. It becomes clear that something in Seara is changing, and within this change comes Scott Frost a wrestler from Detroit. Frost at first comes across to Seara in a looming way which gives her a fear of him, but her mind is soon changed and she finds in him something she has never had...a true friend. Frost is quick to become a major part of the females life both inside and outside the ring. Inside the ring he is Scott Frost, Notorious leader, and team member. With wrestlers Jason Jameson L2J, Frost and Seara, Notorious try to make their team work, but Frost becomes the first to leave, not only leaving his friends without a leader, but leaving his girlfriend without her safety line. After the departure of Frost from Notorious, things looked to slip even more for the stable, and once again after a long run with a stable attitude, something seemed to begin to tick once more inside the female, and in her last 2 commercial airtime appearances between Nightmare before Christmas and Legends XX, the female seems to take a dramatic and apparently instantanious turn back to her old attitudes about the sport and career she has chosen. One thing is not changing though, but rather getting stronger, and that is her feelings for Scott Frost. As Legends XX draws near, the female can feel herself become more hate filled toward a large amount of the eVolution roster. Legends XX arrived with a bang, Seara was the winner of her match against Lee eXodus, and gained the High Impact Title, her first piece of eVolution gold. In the same night, a huge blow is felt throughout eVo as superstar wrestler and close friend of Seara's L2J dies in the ring while giving a speech to the crowds. A week later Seara appears to be in fine in ring form as she goes up against Sean Mason, holder of the Rising Star Champion, the winner of the match taking the two belts (High Impact - Seara's and Rising Star - Mason's), and unifying them to become the first holder of the all new eVolved Championship belt. With the death of L2J still on her mind, Seara faces the fight and once again proves that she has something to offer eVo by winning the fight, and becoming not only the first eVolved champion, but the first female holder of the belt. PPV time will bring Seara and Xander together, one man who Seara wants to prove herself to, so far she has proved herself, but with the title on the line, Seara is preparing for Xander to be backed up with help so he can cheat the win. Seara is preparing to do anything to keep the belt. Seara has defended the eVolved title more than once now, and going into Tainted Love, Febuarys PPV, she still has it, but with a title fight against Xander, things could change. This Three Falls Cell Match chosen by Seara will be the end of a long testing feud between the two powerful wrestlers, and will for once prove which of them is better and deserving of the title. Seara has defended it before against Xander and kept it, and if Tainted Love goes her way, the female will still have it firmly in her grasp. Tainted Love proves the evening that Seara has waited for as she once and for all ends her feud with Xander that started when the female first joined the fed, winning the match Seara leaves the arena that night with her title still hanging over her shoulder and the knowledge that she defeated Xander. The female also leaves the arena with her best success of the evening, a change in Frost. A change that has brought the Universal title to his waist. Two title holders, the eVolved Champion and the Universal Champion, their lives effected that little bit extra, but will the close future bring greater changes to the lives of these two young talented fighters.
