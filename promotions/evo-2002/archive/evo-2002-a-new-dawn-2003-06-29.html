A New Dawn Transcript Live on Pay Per View! From Madison Square Garden ~ New York City, New York 7:59PM EST Sunday Night June 29th, 2003 At From The Ashes things had begun to change throughout all of eVolution. Now there will be A New Dawn rising for eVolution with new faces coming in from all corners of the world but as always there are those who fight against change. There are those who still liked what the regime of Daniel Somers had done and they fight back now against the new regime of Tyler Davidson. The fight continues for everyone regardless of what they feel about the situation now and there will be one match that will make some stars while it will break others. One way or another tonight marks the beginning of something brand new for eVolution as it looks to the future with some new faces and whole lot more attitude then ever before. For the past few weeks America’s Guardians and The BodySnatchers have been battling it out for the chance to be called the number one contenders to the Tag Team Titles currently held by St. Violence. This best of three series is currently tied up at one match a piece and now the third and deciding match will go down with some tables, some ladders, a little bit of chairs, and an added touch of flags as well. The team who captures their opponents flag first will of course be the winner and both teams are able to use anything at their exposal to make that task as easy or as hard as possible. At a house show last weekend Eric Knight surprised everyone when he not only won the eVolution Tag Team Titles with his partner Deacon, but he also captured the eVolved Title when he pinned Luther Wisdom in that match. Now at A New Dawn, Eric Knight must be prepared for his first title defense as he will be challenged by the relative newcomer to eVolution in Scott Evans. Evans has already picked up victories over Dane Ventrella and Ryan Justice over the past few weeks and that hard work has earned him this big opportunity at the eVolved Title. Can he make the most of it or will the long overlooked Eric Knight prove his worth finally with some gold around his waist? This match was originally supposed to take place a few weeks ago on Legends but has been pushed back to A New Dawn. Heavily rumored to be involved has been The Syndicate who are already confirmed for the One Year Anniversary show, eVo1. The chance could be there where we just might see one of the most dominant teams in eVolution and EdW history show up at A New Dawn and throw themselves into the Tag Team Invitational but the only way we’ll know is for the match to happen as planned. The Cell Championship will finally see the light of day again in eVolution but the only question left to answer now is just who will be the man who gets the honor of bringing the title back into reality? Eight men will fight for that chance in a match that is only being called now the Chamber of Survival match. The match itself is so massive that it cannot even be held in the same arena as the rest of the show and eVolution officials were forced to rent out a Manhattan warehouse five minutes away from Madison Square Garden in order to hold the match. There will be five different Cell cages connected together with one larger Cell right in the middle and the other four connected to it on each side. In each of the four smaller Cells there will be two men who must go one on one until only one remains in each Cell. When that happens the doors to the large Cell will be opened and a four way Cell fight will be underway with the Cell Championship hanging in the balance. For the past few weeks now Shocka and the newcomer Greatness have been at each others throats but no matter what Shocka has tried to do it always seems that Greatness has some trick up his sleeve to still escape with a victory. Now at A New Dawn, Shocka will get one last chance to redeem himself against Greatness and pull out a victory but he will no doubt have a huge battle ahead of him if he hopes to succeed because Greatness will not go down without a fight. The wrestling ring is surrounded by the hell in a cell cage as up to twenty men can be on the inside battling it out for the chance to be number one contender to the Universal Title. If the ring is surrounded by the cage then how is someone eliminated? The only way to do that is to find a way to throw them out of the cage itself whether it be through the door or through the cage itself. There is a one hour time limit set for the match with a new competitor entering the match at every two minute interval. If there is still more than one person remaining inside of the cage by the time the one hour limit is up the man with the most pinfalls will be declared the victor. Inside of this cage no friendship will be able to survive as there can be only one man who walks out of this war with a victory. A match that was originally scheduled to be a triple threat match with the involvement of Asis has since been changed to a one on one encounter thanks to Xander turning on his fellow Unlimited member shortly after the match was announced and injuring the shoulder of Asis. Those actions turned this match into a rematch from End of Days where Xander had defeated Tyler Davidson to become number one contender to the Universal Title. Now there is even more on the line as Xander looks to successfully defend his title against Davidson which was the only black mark on the events of From The Ashes for Tyler Davidson and in his opinion eVolution as a whole. Backstage ~ Two Options Featuring Damon O’Halloran and Ali Khadafi Working out in his room, O'Halloran was sweating hard. He was throwing lefts and rights hard at his shadow. His monstrous physique and tattoos were shown as he warmed up in his jeans. Everything was blurred together with a ring of black partially blinding him. Every touch seemed dull; every breath quicker and less satisfying. And he left, leaving behind that strange aura as Khadafi looks on as he continues his preparation for the traumatic night ahead.
