Travis Kyle Biography Real Name Travis Neal Kyle Nicknames The Dark Magician, The Messiah of Magic, Ayatollah of Illusions, The Astonishing One, The Evil Magician, The American Nightmare Hometown Las Vegas, NV Birthday April 9, 1983 Height 6'0' Weight 217 Interests Wrestling, Reading, Writing, Hanging Out Favorite Pro Wrestlers Chris Jericho, Shawn Michaels, Eddie Guerrero, Batista, Chris Benoit, Kurt Angle Biography Travis Kyle was born and raised in Las Vegas Nevada to Anthony and Tonya Kyle. On his 6th birthday, Travis' parents bought him a magic set and Travis was instantly hooked. His love for magic grew as he got older and he became more and more obsessed with the craft. When Travis turned 15, he got a job working as an assistant to famous magician Derek Marshall. Derek taught Travis all about magic and illusions and even taught him that magicians were indeed real and not just weird people with no life. Travis and Derek formed a bond that resembled one of a father and son relationship until one fateful day, Travis botched a trick in front of a world-wide audience and horribly embarassed Derek. Derek belittled Travis and tore down his self-esteem,sending Travis into isolation for several years. During those years, Travis' attitude towards life began to shift for the worse as he grew a hatred for Derek that stemmed into a hatred for everyone. Travis wanted to be back in the spotlight again and eliminate anyone who was already in it. Upon his return from isolation, Travis murdered his parents, blaming them for his sorrows. Travis quickly dominated the magic world once again, nearly killing another famous magician David Houston in order to get a contract with MGM Grand Entertainment to have his own show at that luxurious hotel in Las Vegas. He even discovered that he had a twin sister that was given away at birth, whom he was extremely jealous of and vanished her tp parts unknown. Then Travis decided to focus on the wrestling world as it's popularity was growing and he intended to use that for his own personal fame, showing off and tormenting others with his magic.
