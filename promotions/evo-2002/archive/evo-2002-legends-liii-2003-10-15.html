Shane is going to try and end roC!” Shane has stripped the announce table and is going after Thug roC now, but Thug was waiting and connects with a cheap right to the kisser of Wethers. Thug roC scoops Wethers up for a body slam, but Shane slides down the back of roC. He then grabs roC by the head and hits a devastating reverse DDT right on the hard mats. The ref yells at the two, but doesn’t use his ten count. Shane picks Thug roC up, and rolls him up on the table, then rolls back into the ring. “What’s Shane doing?” “I have no clue.” Shane paces around the ring, checking on roC every few seconds to make sure he’s still on the table. Shane Wethers sprints across the ring, and hits the far ropes. Shane leaps up onto the top rope and springboards off before flipping forwards and nailing a huge swanton bomb off the top rope! The crowd all hit their feet and cheer as both roC and Shane Wethers are laid out in pieces of what use to be an announce table. The crowd is ecstatic, chanting ‘holy shit’ loudly. The replay is shown while roC and Shane try to get to their feet, roC moving a lot slowly then Shane. “Are we back?” Scott asks, putting his headset back on. “Shane Wethers, pulling out all the stops to win this match!” “I can’t believe what I just saw. Did you see that replay?” “Of course not, we don’t have any monitors.” The crowd continues to be in a loud chant, but suddenly the chant changes. The blood thirsty crowd chants “One more time! One more time!” The chant only gets louder and louder as Thug roC and Shane get to their feet. Shane with a hard right, and Thug roC returns with one of his own. Thug roC goes for a second, but it’s blocked and Shane quickly nails a downward spiral, sending roC face-first into the ground. Shane slowly gets up and starts going for the Spanish announce table, which pleases the crowd. “Again?” “I guess so!” Shane strips the monitors off the second table, getting ready for another stunt. He grabs the steel steps this time, and drags them to that they are right next to the table. Thug roC from behind clotheslines Wethers down, taking some air out the crowd. Exhaustion is now a factor, both men are slow to rise to their feet. Thug roC goes to grab Shane, but Shane quickly hits a drop toe hold, dropping Thug roC onto the steel steps. The ref is trying to get both men in the ring, but Shane shoves the ref to the ground. He pulls roC up to the top of the steel steps, and prepares for a powerbomb, or maybe a piledriver. With the ref down momentarily, Thug roC hits a desperation low blow, causing Shane to fold up. Thug throws Shane’s arm up around his neck, and hits the roCing Horse through the Spanish announce table! “Oh my!” Scott exclaims. “What a move by Thug roC! I don’t think Shane saw that coming!” “I don’t like the guy, but that was one hell of a move.” Both men lay in the wreck once again, neither man moving. The ref checks on both men to make sure they are both able to continue the match. Thug roC pulls himself up to his feet as Shane struggles to do the same. The attacks don’t stop though, with Shane striking him hard with a right hand. Thug roC staggers back, right into Scott Evans. Evans catches him before he falls on top of him. Thug roC turns and nails Evans with a hard right. Knocking Evans right out of his chair. “Evans? You ok?” Jack asks. But Evans doesn’t have his headset on anymore. Thug roC is grabbed by Shane and rolled into the ring. Shane is about to enter the ring, but Scott Evans runs and shoves him out the way. Thug roC gets up and Evans knocks him right back down with a hard right. The ref calls for the bell as Evans chases roC out the ring, and roC is quick to pick up his title and vacate. Evans stares up the ramp as Thug roC retreats up the ramp. “Here is your winner by a disqualification….” Libby Bowman starts to announce. On the outside you see Shane Wethers obviously pissed off, staring up at Evans and then up the ramp at Thug roC. “Thug roC will retreat with his title tonight thanks due to Scott Evans getting involved with the match. I bet this doesn’t sit well with Shane, he had the match in his hands. On behalf of Scott Evans, Libby Bowman and the rest of eVo, this is Jack Tyler saying goodnight!” But no, it’s not over quite yet! Shane Wethers with a cheap shot, hitting Evans in the back of his head with a steel chair! “Oh my! Shane is attacking Scott Evans now!” Shane then stomps Evans down as he is barely moving. “He must have realized that Evans just cost him the United Title. He can’t be to glad about that.” Shane exits the ring and goes under the ring to look for something. A rare Shane Wethers pop is heard as he enters the ring with a steel ladder. Scott Evans, lying on his back, is struck in his sternum with the ladder by Wethers. He constantly slams the ladder repeatedly into Evans’ gut. Shane Wethers then goes and sets the ladder up, unfolding it, and then starts climbing the tall ladder. Scott Evans has rolled over on his side, holding his stomach in pain. Shane Wethers throws his arms up with a mean look on his face. He leaps off and nails a huge frog splash onto the side of Scott Evans. Scott screams in pain as Shane took a little punishment as well. Scott Evans holds his sides and stomach in pain. “My god!” Shane laughs before he exits the ring and starts walking up the ramp. Scott Evans is in the ring and is coughing up blood. He is in obvious pain as he hasn’t even attempted to get back up. “Scott Evans is bleeding eternally thanks to Shane Wethers!” Legends finally closes out, the camera zoomed in on the fallen Scott Evans.
