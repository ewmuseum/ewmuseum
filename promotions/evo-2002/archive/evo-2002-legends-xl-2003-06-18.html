Legends XL Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The HSBC Arena ~ Buffalo, New York 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night June 18th, 2003 The action continues to heat up on Legends as we are only two weeks away from the first pay per view event of the Tyler Davidson regime in eVolution and the show is shaping up to be quite an explosive event. The Cell Division continues to kick off with explosive action and now with the addition of Joshua Krowe to the directors board along with Cameron Griffin things are likely to get a hell of a lot more unpredictable as well. Tyler Davidson will be at the show in some form but after Xander’s attack against him last week there is no telling what kind of condition he will really be in at all if Xander decides once again to make his move against him prior to A New Dawn. What will happen at Legends? No one knows, watch or be eVolved! This match will be the debut for two more members of the Cell Division as Terlayne Sterling will go one on one with the extremely odd Othinel. Othinel has seen some action so far on the house show circuit but has yet to find his grove in eVolution apparently and this doesn’t seem to be the kind of match to do it in as Terlayne is reportedly quite a formidable opponent. Last week we saw the former bodyguard of Thug roC, Machine make his arrival and announce that he would indeed be making his debut soon inside of The Cell. One person that apparently didn’t go over very well with was Josh Horroway as Machine had some very unflattering comments for Horroway following his loss inside of The Cell last week. Now this week Horroway wants to try and teach the newcomer a lesson but it won’t be easy as the massive Machine has proven on more than one occasion that he can certainly take care of himself in any situation. Question Beyond X tried getting himself some revenge last week against Ali Khadafi for his loss two weeks ago but despite his best made plans they were foiled by the unexpected arrival of Damon O’Halloran. The Irishmen has come to eVolution for a chance to prove his worth against some of the toughest men alive and QBX is out to make sure that doesn’t happen as he and his apparent mentor, Malcolm King, look for some payback against the man that foiled their plans. Both The BodySnatchers and America’s Guardians pulled off huge victories for their respective eVolution careers last week and now the two teams begin a best of three series that will certainly propel the victors right up to the very top of the tag team rankings in eVolution. The Tone has been one half of the team known as Tribute until last week when his partner was kicked to the curb rather violently by the rest of Malcolm King’s army. Now the former Tribute members tries his luck in singles action as he’ll start things off against Dane Ventrella who has been a highly impressive newcomer since his arrival at From The Ashes. Dane’s in ring work has demonstrated he would be a force to be reckoned with but a match with Tone will be no easy task even for him. Ryan Justice and Scott Evans are two of the newest faces to eVolution and will square off one on one this week. Both men made their debuts last week on Legends and they will look to continue on the path that they had started this week with the dreams of gold definitely in their sights. While his partner will defend the eVolved Title later in the night, Hedges will be on a different mission altogether. That mission whether he knows it or not is more than likely to rid Thug roC of the thorn in his side known as Jason Jameson. The former Universal Champion has been trying since From The Ashes to get a rematch against Thug roC and this now is yet another test to be placed in front of him before that match can ever happen. Even a win here this week won’t guarantee Jameson the shot he wants but it would no doubt feel quite good for him as he can take some form of revenge against Hedges for the handicap match he was placed in last week. Last week Thug roC was the referee for a handicap match that pitted the Tag Team Champions, Luther Wisdom and Hedges, against Jason Jameson, a man that Thug roC is doing everything in his power to avoid a rematch against. Thug roC’s actions during that match didn’t seem to go over with Luther Wisdom very well and as a result of that and their own differences the Champions almost lost the match to Jameson but the quick thinking and interference of Thug roC bailed them out. Luther Wisdom now has issued a challenge out to Thug roC for this week with the eVolved Title on the line but the question is just what will Hedges when his New-TV partner faces his tag team champion partner? Last week Sean Mason came within inches of capturing the eVolution Universal Title and being the first man to ever hold the Universal and Continental Titles simultaneously. Xander had other plans however and made certain that didn’t happen. The two men meet up once again this week but it will be in tag team action as Mason teams with former Universal and Continental Champion Shocka against Xander and Greatness, a mysterious masked man who Shocka has had problems with as evident over the past two weeks. Nothing was settled between the two of them last week and it’s not likely to be settled this week even though they will certainly try to end one another once again. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including James Kimson, Thug roC, and many of eVolution’s superstars! Reaching across he takes his much adorned title in his hands and sits it upon his lap gently running his finger across the engraved surface. It had been a long hard road to capture that very title, but it proved one thing that Sean was certainly not a quitter.
