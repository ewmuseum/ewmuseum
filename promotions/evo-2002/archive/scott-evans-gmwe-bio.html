Scott Evans Biography Real Name Scott Evans Nicknames 'The Firebird','Nachezer' Hometown Chicago, Illinois Birthday 01/02/76 Height 6'5' Weight 235 Interests e-wrestling, football Favorite Pro Wrestlers Christopher Daniels, Hayabusa Biography The life of 'The Firebird' Scott Evans began one day in Las Vegas, Nevada. His father met a stripper who became Scott and his brother Adam’s mother. Scott and Adam weren’t conceived until later that night. 9 months later and he sat pacing back and forth in his wife’s hospital room where soon enough Scott and Adam would be born. But something went wrong whilst she was giving birth, she pushed to hard and her heart that had a clogged artery gave out. Adam and Scott came out fine, but motherless. Soon after birth their father took them with him to live with his mother. Their father’s apartment wasn’t compatible for 2 children and their mother lived with their father. So they stayed at their grandmother’s house for 3 years where they grew up and learned to love their grandparents, Adam nearly broke his arm one morning giving their grandfather a near fatal heart attack, he stayed in the hospital for 2 weeks before he passed on, their grandmother was in bad condition afterward and when Scott almost fell on his head she had a stroke and a heart attack at the same time and died immediately. Their father was stricken hard but he and his children kept on living in the house for 6 more years before the pressure collapsed on their father, it was their ninth birthday and they couldn’t be happier with all their friend’s there. Scott and Adam were not normal children, they were declared co-MVP’s of every football championship they helped their Junior Pee Wee team win, this day turned that around on them though, their father went out to get them some candy and pizza, he left them and all the kids there with his neighbor. He went and was gone until 9:00 that night before they went looking for him it took a few minutes to find what had happened, their father had committed suicide when he received a call saying he was fired. He hung himself in their backyard and left a note saying that they should stay with their mother’s sister. She was never found alive after this event, after a 3-week search they found that she had evidently shot herself in the head when she lost her money gambling. So Scott and Adam went to an orphanage. Their first few months were fun they were making friends but then a monster showed up that was shy towards all the kids except Scott and Adam, he was 9 and he stood at 5’5” already. He held some grudge against Adam and Scott and the 3 had many battles, one day it went to far and Adam was hit upside the head with a baseball bat, Adam was knocked unconscious and Scott went crazy, in the next few moments Scott had token the baseball bat and begun to brutally beat the monster non-stop until he saw his was still alive. The monster as everyone referred him to had a fractured skull, a broken arm, the inability to walk on one leg, and broken ribs. Scott had come to his senses inside of the hospital where his brother was being treated; The Monster was in critical condition for 4 months before he finally was able to come back to the orphanage. After nearly a year everything went back to normal and Scott and Adam retook their former positions. Up until the time they were 12, then something happened, The Monster having fully recovered from everything decided to have a re-match between him and the twins. For some reason he could barely lay a finger on them, up until that time they had been watching that spectacle that was pro wrestling learning the maneuvers from the greats of that time, Jimmy “The Superfly” Snuka, Hulk Hogan, Dory Funk Jr., The Freebirds, Andre the Giant, and many others. After a while they got bored of being on defense and tried some moves on him, they eventually started using tag team methods and they gave him a beating he would never forget, Scott and Adam soon came up with the aspirations to be pro wrestlers. A few months later Scott and Adam were given notice that they had an uncle that no one had ever known about. It seems that he was their grandfather’s son with a different mother. He had won the lotto and went on search to find his family members on his tree; he came across Scott and Adam and brought them into his life. He adopted the two and became their Legal Guardians. Soon after Scott and Adam moved to his stately home outside of Chicago, Illinois. After they moved in they had to go to their first day of normal school in years. They had forgotten what it was like to be in a school where there were cliques of kids, they hadn’t been in a school where everyone was friends with everyone since they were 9, so they didn’t think that the incident that did happen would. They walked into the school holding their backpacks over their shoulders walking in step like they were in the military and the 'popular' kids as I’ll call them for our purposes didn’t like the way the looked so they went up to them and told them their opinions, Scott and Adam went insane and began wrestling with the kids, they were out numbered 2 to 5 but when you have two kids that are 6’0' at their age it is hard to not get knocked around. Scott and Adam used their environment as an advantage and they jumped off trees and did high-flying moves mimicking Jimmy 'Superfly' Snuka in a big way. They finished the fight by hitting the kids with Leg Drops. Luckily no teacher saw the fight or the two would have been in big trouble. They went through the rest of the school without trouble until an incident late in the year, the incident that helped form Scott Evan’s crazy attitude. Scott and Adam were walking back to their home when a few kids with wooden bats ambushed them, Adam was knocked out in a few minutes and Scott went solo for the first time ever. He ripped of his shirt and began to attack the other people as if he was possessed by demons that were telling him what to do, he did everything so perfectly that it seemed no one could stop him. After a few minutes he had all the ambushers running, but one problem, cops had seen what had happened. So they went right for Scott, they arrested him and he was sent to Juvenile Court, they ruled that he had psychological problems and sent him to Juvenile Hall and he was given medical care and treated for his mental illness. He spent a year there leaving Adam and his Uncle to sit at home work and go to school. He came back fulfilled as the psychologists call it and he returned, he and Adam finished school with no other big problems or incidents except for winning their high school a state championship for football and getting scholarships to Florida State. So College came along, Scott went for mainly football while Adam focused on football and academics. The first year came and went Scott was on his way to a degree in Criminology and Adam was going towards being an engineer. The two went back home and Scott met a girl on their flight home. Somehow the two were going to nearly the same place, she was the girl next door that went to private schools. The two were going to the same College and taking the same classes and by the end of the summer they were dating whilst Adam caught up on some sleep and worked out. The next year they went back to school and during a game in mid-season Scott’s throwing arm was broken. He couldn’t compete until next year but he lost some of his former magic. Adam on the other hand was becoming an all star Linebacker. After a few years college was over and both Scott and Adam had chances at the NFL both for the same team, the NY Giants, they turned the team down and Scott went into law enforcement whilst Adam began working towards the childhood dream, becoming a Pro Wrestler. In the fall of 1994 Scott was married to the girl he had met on the plane, 9 months later their child was born, fast forward 3 yeara and Scott and Adam both have situated themselves in the Chicago Police Department, Adam is undercover in a case against the mafia at the time, christmas rolls around their raid begins on the mafia but it backfires when Adam's partner betrays him, Adam is fatally shot and his last words to Scott are to go into wrestling, Adam died moments later and Scott went home mentally unstable, in the morning he awoke to screams he runs out into the yard his wife and child are both lieing their blood splattered across the lawn Scott pulls out his gun and shoots after the assasins. That night he began an attack against the mafia fatally shooting 17 people in 3 days, the night of the 3rd day the fight takes him to his brother's former partner and Scott is jailed the next day for murder, but the case is argued that he is mentally unstable so he spends until 1998 in a home for insane people, he was released the winter of 1998 where his life goes into basically darkness until late 2002 to early 2003 when he began to live his dream.
