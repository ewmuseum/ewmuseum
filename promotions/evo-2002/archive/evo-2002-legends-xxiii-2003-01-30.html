Legends XXIII Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Staples Center ~ Los Angeles, California 7:59PM EST Thursday Night January 30th, 2003 The new year is now officially upon eVolution and on Sunday night it had kicked off to a very explosive start. The One was revealed at the pay per view to be Daniel Somers himself and some explanation behind that is expected to be made this week on Legends. In addition we will certainly be hearing from the newest signing in eVolution, former 8 time World Champion, James Kimson! With the chaos that surrounded the Tag Team Titles match at New Year’s eVolution, this show is largely going to determine just who the top contenders to that title will be with a three team round robin tournament taking place. Prohibited and Tanaka have formed an obvious alliance against The Unlimited in recent weeks but this week they will find themselves placed against Dreamer and King who found themselves the two odd men out as their respective partners walked out of the pay per view with the Tag Team Titles. In the first of the round robin tournament matches these two teams will battle to find out just who will move on to the following round with the advantage and who will be fighting from behind. Two weeks ago it was Sean Mason who cost Asis his chance at the Continental Title against Big Daddy Dreamer. At the pay per view it was Asis who retaliated and cost Mason his chance at the gold leaving the young star lying. Now the two former Rising Star Champions will battle it out with the winner getting a chance to move on and face Big Daddy Dreamer for the Continental Title at a later date. The Unlimited are now the former Tag Team Champions and despite their obvious power in the front office they still have to earn their shot at a rematch for the gold. They will face the team that loses in the first match of the round robin before moving on to face the winner in the main event with the Tag Team Title shot on the line. As punishment for her betrayal of The Unlimited, Daniel Somers has booked The HeartBreak Chick into a handicap match against a new mystery team. There have been no hints of just who the team will be but HBC is without a doubt going to have to be at the top of her game in order to survive this one. Second match of their night and win or lose in their first one The Unlimited will have to try and knock off the winner of the first round robin match to have any hopes of gaining their Tag Team Titles rematch. If the winners of the first match can pull off the victory they will ensure their victory in the tournament. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Troy Pierce, Seara, Sovereign, Shocka, Scott Frost, James Kimson, and many of eVolution’s superstars!
