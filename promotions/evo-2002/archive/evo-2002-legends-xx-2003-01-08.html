Live on OSE! From The Kemp Arena ~ Kansas City, Missouri 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night January 8th, 2003 With Nightmare Before Christmas now behind us and 2002 also gone, eVolution prepares to move forward with the new year in an explosive way with Legends XX! After the violent action and shocking revelation at Nightmare Before Christmas many superstars are in no condition to go into action this week. That doesn’t stop eVolution from pushing on however with two title matches, a loser leaves eVolution match, and of course the speculation of just what is going to happen now that The One has returned to plague the life of eVolution like he had done EdW! Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Old School Entertainment! Steele and Flesco have had a bit of a feud building the past few weeks and now it’s set to come to an end one way or another. Daniel Somers booked this one and ruled that the loser of this match would never again be seen in eVolution. Now both men are not just fighting for bragging rights but for their entire careers here in eVolution and only one of them will have one to continue after Legends. Thug roC stripped Scott Frost of the High Impact Title last week and immediately signed this match. Seara who has been one of the hottest and most underrated stars in eVolution gets a shot to capture some gold finally in eVolution as she battles Lee eXodus for the High Impact Title. Lee of course is a good friend of Thug roC and there is no telling what kind of plan the two of them might have if Thug roC is even in any condition to do it after Shocka’s brutal assault. What Thug roC didn’t mention however was that the winner of this match will head to Legends next week for a one on one unification bout with the Rising Star Champion, Sean Mason! A rematch from Nightmare Before Christmas, the two monsters, Sovereign and Impact will go head to head once again. It was Leya’s betrayal of Impact at Nightmare Before Christmas that cost him his chance for revenge against Sovereign but now he’s even more determined to get some payback. Sovereign however is determined to put an end to The Hollywood Monster once and for all and there isn’t anyone he will let stand in his way. After his victory at Nightmare Before Christmas, Shocka was the happiest man on earth. Now he finds his emotions conflicted as he’s forced to defend the title on Legends this week against the man he barely defeated to win Road to Glory, Scott Frost! Frost came off a tough victory against Asis last week and isn’t at one hundred percent but he’s determined to make the most of this opportunity here at Legends and make Shocka’s title reign the shortest in eVolution history. Shocka won’t sit by and let that happen as he is determined to send Frost the way of L2J and Thug roC now. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including The Unlimited, Big Daddy Dreamer, Troy Pierce and many more!
