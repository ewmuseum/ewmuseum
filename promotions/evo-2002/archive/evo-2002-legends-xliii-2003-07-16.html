Legends XLIII Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Auto Quest Stadium ~ Widnes, England 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night July 16th, 2003 Just when you thought things couldn’t get any crazier they just take one hell of a turn off course. After finding out that their license to promote shows within the United States was revoked at the show last week, Tyler Davidson was forced to find an alternative method to keep eVolution running. The first international show in eVolution’s history will now take place outside of North America as eVolution invades the United Kingdom in order to keep their show going. Pretty much all of eVolution’s stars are expected to make the trip overseas in time for this show though that is by no means a certainty. However the show is certain to be an action packed show as eVolution comes closer and closer each week to ReVelations. eVotron ~ Disease Featuring ???? eVolution’s Legends did not open with a token fireworks display. Instead a single word written out in plain white text sat ominously on the pitch-black video wall. Disease. Slowly but surely the letters bled into the next word. AIDS. The word remained only for a few seconds before fading away to be replaced by the armed individuals standing next to one another in a line, each clinching their rifles one at a time until the third did so and dropped dead. And then the deep voice that wouldn't have been out of place in a movie trailer spoke up. "Imagine a world where the common cold can kill you and you have an insight as to what AIDS can do. Thought to have spread from monkeys to humans between 1926 and 1946 although recent research indicates that it could have spread from chimpanzees to humans as early as 1675. AIDS breaks down the immune system and as yet there is no cure, it killed 3 million people last year and the numbers continue to grow. From this we learn that diseases can develop in any time or place under any circumstance leaving nothing immune. Not even evolution. Soon, a new disease will develop and the epidemic will begin. The Cell will rise again." The footage faded to black and Legends returned to regular programming. Backstage ~ The Epitome Featuring Damon O’Halloran O'Halloran grinned and rubbed his knuckles. He couldn't wait. In just a few minutes he would have Ali Khadafi in the cell. I go into that ring with nothing but hatred. But what if he beats me? But the odds of that... well... Damon closed his eyes and breathed in deeply as he remembered the moment of cracking his across the face with one hard blow. The feeling, as they say, was nearly better than crack. Nearly. Standing slowly, still feeling the rush of pleasure at the memory, O'Halloran strode over to the dressing room door and glanced out of it. "Why did I do it?" he pondered to himself. Revenge. Jealousy. Hatred. Power. Fame. "All of the above, actually." he said out loud as he made his way through the sterile hallway. "Such a fool for trusting me, in his hour of need he looked towards me. Always waiting for me to make the first move. Ali Khadafi, the epitome of the Cell Division?! I think not." A cold sweat overcomes the Irish native as he leans up against the wall, he slowly slides down as he rests on the floor; his hand outstretched before his very eyes, almost trance-like. "It's one thing to say you are going to kill a man." he muttered. He paused. "... it's a completely different proposition, when you actually have to do it." The camera fades to black as O'Halloran contemplates... and waits. Almost if he was in a void he could not escape from, his only release, his only way out... to finish Ali Khadafi permanently.
