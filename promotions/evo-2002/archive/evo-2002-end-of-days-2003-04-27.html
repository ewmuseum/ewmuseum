Tens of thousands of fans are on their feet cheering for the start of the show as the usual blue and red pyro display blasts off from the ramp into the air. He got to the bottom of the ramp and slid in under the bottom rope before he got back to his feet and walked over to the corner where he began to do some final stretching for the match. “Ronan Pierce picked up a victory last week against Seara and he’ll try to continue that here tonight,” Justin said. “So far he seems to be one of the only members of NTICW aside from Christoph that is actually capable of winning.” ”Shaun Brooks and Homicide are both just getting warmed up here,” Jack defended. “Sooner or later they’ll light the place up with their victories.” ”I’m pretty sure they’ve been lighting something up alright,” Justin replied. “And introducing next his opponent hailing from Chicago, Illinois and weighing in at 123lbs……Seara!” The beginning to Replica can be heard as the music fades in, and the crowd begin to cheer.
