The Genesis Pit 3, 5 July 2003

Latest Columns The Genesis Pit #3 Taped July 5th, 2003 "Welcome to the post EVO1 edition of the Genesis Pit. Tonight our action all takes place at the special July 5th house show at the fleet Center in Boston, MA. Scheduled to compete here tonight you have ULWN's Damien Legend as he takes on ULWN roster mate Vagabond. Perry Quinn of The Industry takes on Luther Wisdom in singles action as well as Yoshii Tanaka taking on Matsuda Sun Tsai in a response to being attacked two weeks ago here on The Genesis Pit. Finally our main event features 6 man tag action as Evolution's own....The BodySnatchers and HeartBreak Chick take on ULWN's Negro Story and a partner to be named later...all apart of eVo's PRIVATE AGENDA house show tour leading up to Revelations, so now let us go to our first event....." Before any match can begin.....the ULWN Champion C.O.D.E. is standing in the middle of the ring with mic in hand.... "On July 3rd...some of my ULWN brethren went to the Evolution Wrestling show and decided to make a point about who gets the call up to the quote unquote show...and who is deemed worthy to spend time in developmental territories. While I may not agree with their actions...their message has a ring of validity to it. I am the ULWN Champion can I not aspire to be something greater? Why is it after a year here I have yet to even be featured on Legends? Since when is god spelled Tyler Davidson? It seems that Evolution can use us to fill out the house show circuit when someone like Kimson, or Frost decide they are too good to work an extra day or two....but yet can't respect us enough to give us primetime expos......" TOMORROW by SR-71 starts to play as the crowd surprisingly erupts in cheers...amongst the people stand the Evolution Wrestling's Universal Champion..... "Respect is something that is earned...." Xander starts. But before he can continue......THE THEME FROM SHAFT PLAYS as THE TONE a member of King's Army emerges from the backstage area..... "And you Xander should learn a little more respect...." The Tone starts... "King sent me here to deal with this problem you're being here is not requi...." "Problem....it seems like the Universal Champion of evolution can't even keep his own house in order..." C.O.D.E. begins " If he let others steer the federation he's the supposed leader of..." "King...is as much a non factor as this jabronie..." Xander states pointing towards The Tone... "Jabronie?!?!?"...The Tone says insulted...."Why don't we put that to the test, tonight..." Xander starts to smile that turns into a little smirk..."Okay...I feel like a workout, you and me tonight. Just for kicks I'll put the title on the line so King will know that through you will be the closest he will ever get to being Universal Champion again." Xander's music starts to play as The Tone has a surprise shot at the Universal Title and C.O.D.E. much to his delight has a ringside seat to watch the Universal Champion in action. Match One Damien Legend vs. Vagabond: Running powerslam from Vagabond he goes for the cover! 1....2...kick out. He slaps on a headlock Legend powers out of the headlock. Legend hits a right hand. Flying shoulder tackle by Legend sends Vagabond to the mat. Pin : 1....2..no. The match continues. Flapjack from Legend. Pin : 1....2....no! Shoulder up at the last second. Full nelson slam by Vagabond, he goes for the cover and hooks the leg : 1....2....shoulder up. Vagabond DDTs Damien Legend. Cover! 1....2....kick out at the last second! Vagabond looks shocked at the resiliency of Damien Legend. Back elbow connects, Legend staggers backwards and is met with a vicious clothesline via Vagabond. Vagabond tries to follow it with a leg drop but misses. Running knee lift from Damien Legend stuns Vagabond. Legend nails the LEGEND MAKER (spinning Samoan Driver) he goes for the cover 1....2....3. "Our next matchup was actually set up a couple of weeks ago on The Genesis Pit as after a grueling matchup Yoshii Tanaka was attacked by 1/2 of The Crimson Sun....Matsuda Sun Tsai. Tanaka has gone to management and demanded a match that he hopes will settle this issue. However before we start the match we have an important announcement to make......as of July 3rd a deal was finalized between Evolution Wrestling and Malcolm King's RoguePatriot Entertainment to carry Evolution programming as well as its multimedia division. However this deal does not include LEGENDS as its still being broadcast on Tru-TV at least for the 30 day negotiation window. However Genesis Pit and the PPV X-RATED will be RogueTV....with negotiations still continuing for Legends and subsequent PPV's....I'm told more details will be revealed before Legends as Tru-TV has denied King commercial time to promote." Match Two Yoshii Tanaka vs. Matsuda Sun Tsai : Stiff high kick on MST by Tanaka. Tanaka hits an irish whip into a shopping cart as the two have yet to make it into the ring. Spin kick by Tanaka to the face of MST. Driven DDT by Tanaka establishes the eVo superstar as being firmly in control. MST tries to get back in as he reverses a Tanaka hammerlock. Flying trash can shot from the top rope! Back heel kick from MST. Cover! 1....2...kick out. Tanaka ducks a wild right hand. Second rope splash by Tanaka. Cover! 1....2...kick out. MST walks into a face crusher variation. Pin : 1....2....no! They cut that one close. Tanaka picks him up and irish whips MST to the corner; MST counters an avalanche with a raised foot to the face. MST scores with a back heel kick on Tanaka. They brawl to the outside and into the crowd. Open-handed chop from MST. Hooks the leg : 1....2....shoulder up. MST snap mares Tanaka. They wind up back in the squared circle. Tanaka counters an arm wringer with an elbow to the side of the head. Tanaka hits a dropkick on MST. MST is in trouble. Here it comes - He goes for THE RISING SUN...but as he springboards off the rope...Hiro Iishi, MST's partner comes out of the crowd and throws a fireball in Tanaka's face causing him to land awkwardly. MST quickly covers the fallen Evolution Wrestling star for the 1.....2.....3!!!! "This is not over as MST or most importantly The Urban Legend Wrestling Nation has stolen a victory over Evolution Wrestling. Well before we move onto our next match with Perry Quinn of The Industry and Luther Wisdom of The OMEN Brotherhood...we have a few words from one...Luther Wisdom..." PROMO: "It seems to me boys and girls that Evolution Wrestling have decided to punish me for asking Xander his opinion on my main event worthiness up close and personal. They tell me to help out house show attendance by appearing bringing a name to these events...to increase my profile and i will get my shot. I asked Davidson what about Kimson, King, or Morpheus Omega...why must I split my time between Legends and an increased house show appearances...yet they do not? Davidson said he'll take care of me....but is it me he's really taking care of? So fans....I figure I'd use this forum to make a point about me being Universal Champion and to make an impression management would never forget....courtesy of THE HOUSESHOW HIGHLIGHT...LUTHER WISDOM, hey I kinda like that." Match Three: Perry Quinn vs. Luther Wisdom: Ouch! Wisdom gets slammed onto a trash can, squashing it. Big clothesline from Quinn. Pin : 1....2..no. The match continues. Wisdom avoids an avalanche, Quinn hits the turnbuckles hard. Quinn takes a headbutt from Luther Wisdom. Running knee lift from Wisdom results in a cover! 1....2...kick out. Rude Awakening on Quinn by Wisdom. Quinn counters a sleeper hold by turning it into a jaw breaker. Quinn hits a bulldog off the ropes. Hooks the leg : 1....2....shoulder up. Flapjack from Quinn. Hooks the leg : 1....2....shoulder up! That was close. Quinn with a spinning neckbreaker on Wisdom. Wisdom with a standing spinebuster. Quinn can barely stand. A SLAVE TO NO MAN.....as Quinn taps out!!!!! Wisdom grabs a mic..... "Next week Evolution Wrestling on Saturdays house show I am issuing an open challenge to Xander, Kimson, Frost, Morpheus Omega, or my personal bitch Malcolm King....come to my playground see how well I play with others!" MATCH FOUR "This matchup was set up earlier tonight as The Tone representing King's Army goes one on one with Xander for the Universal Title. With ULWN's own champion C.O.D.E. watching from ringside...." Xander vs. Tone : Big clothesline on Xander catches the Universal Champion by surprise . Full nelson slam! Hooks the leg : 1....2....Xander kicks out. Xander ducks a clothesline attempt. Xander catches Tone and bodyslams him. he hooks the leg 1....2....shoulder up. Tornado punch from Xander sends Tone crashing down to the mat again. Hooks the leg : 1....2....foot on the bottom rope! That was close. Massive backbreaker, Tone gets planted. Pin : 1....2..no. The match continues. Xander whips Tone to the ropes and catches the returning superstar with a Spear! He picks up Tone and plays to the crowd CHAOS REIGNS!!!!!! For the 1...2....3! Xander destroys a member of King's Army wait what's this LUTHER WISDOM comes out from the crowd and slaps on A SLAVE TO NO MAN....as the bell just keeps on ringing!!!!! Wisdom releases the hold and allows Xander to drop as both he and Tone leave the ring. C.O.D.E. rolls in with mic in hand..... "Like I said Xander....put your own house in order, and let me worry about mine." The ULWN champion throws down the mic and walks off with a smile on his face. "Well I'm sure Xander will have something for the self proclaimed House show Highlight." Monica Angel begins.... "however now it's time for our main event. Negro Story crashed the party at the EVo PPV A new Dawn but got more than they bargained for as they not only wasn't in the Tag Team invitational but lost their ULWN tag team titles to The BodySnatcher's. However not to let that stop them...they crashed the party yet again and made a statement after The Syndicate's Evolution title win. With Big Daddy Dreamer still recovering Heartbreak Chick is teaming with their nemesis The BodySnatchers to take on ULWN's own Negro Story and a man who they say is equally oppressed by the Evolution slave masters....one half of Crimson Sun, Hiro Iishi." The BodySnatchers\HeartBreak Chick vs. Negro Story\Hiro Iishi: Iishi receives some punishment. Running knee lift from 1001 Holds. Tag to HBC. Hard slam by HBC on Iishi Cover! 1....2...kick out. Tag between HBC and Dolo Bentswinger. HBC \ Dolo whip Iishi into the ropes and hit a double clothesline. Dolo strikes Hiro Iishi with a knifehand chop. Iishi blocks a suplex attempt and nails a legsweep out of nowhere. Iishi tags out to BlackMan#2. BM2 with a spear! Dolo is down and hurt. Cover! 1....2....kick out at the last second! Big clothesline on Dolo. BM2 tags out to BM1. BM1 hits some punches. Big clothesline on Dolo. Dolo powers out of a BM1 headlock. Tag to 1001. BM1 tags out to Hiro Iishi. Kick from Iishi to the leg.1001 blocks a suplex attempt. MOVE 1002 hits.... 1....2.... BM2 breaks the pin attempt. The tag is made to Dolo Bentswinger as BM1 makes the tag to his tag team partner. Flying shoulder tackle by BM1 sends Dolo to the mat. Tag to Hiro Iishi. Hard back suplex on Dolo. Back heel kick from Iishi. Cover! 1....2...kick out. Tag between Iishi and BM1. They whip Dolo into the corner. Iishi whips BM1 in for a hard clothesline to follow-up. Gut buster, Dolo hits hard. Dolo Bentswinger pulls a mule kick out of nowhere. Dolo strikes BM1. Tag to 1001. BM1 walks into a side choke slam. Pin : 1....2....no! They cut that one close. Bodyslam by 1001. Tag between 1001 and HBC. HBC with an arm drag. BM1 slips out the back of a HBC bodyslam. BM1 tags out to BM2.Tag between HBC and Dolo .Dolo hits a bulldog off the ropes. The pin 1....2...Kickout at the last second. HBC makes the blind tag. Dropkick connects, as BM2 goes down...cover 1...BM2 powers out and makes the hot tag to Hiro Iishi. Flying elbow from Iishi sends HBC down. Iishi tags out to BM1. Back elbow connects, HBC staggers backward. Full nelson slam! Hooks the leg : 1....2....shoulder up. Tag to BM2.Negro Story hook up HBC, then hit a double suplex. Tag to 1001 Holds.1001 turns BM2 inside-out with a clothesline. Pin : 1....2....kick out! A split-second away from a three. Tag to Dolo. Big kick from Dolo.BM2 tags out to Hiro Iishi. Dolo tags out to HBC. CHICK KICK 1....2...Oh no BM1 nailed the referee causing him to end the count. BM1 bodyslams HBC. Running knee lift from BM2.Hooks the leg 1....2....the ref is still trying to stir as BM2 releases the hold.BM2 tags out to Hiro Iishi. Hard back suplex on HBC. Dolo enters the ring illegally. Iishi takes a vertical suplex. He floats over for the pin as he gets a 1....2....shoulder up! That was close. The referee realizes HBC was the legal person in the ring and as he's escorting Dolo out of the ring...Daddy's Dirty Little Secret attacks 1001 Holds...as both Dolo and the referee's attention are on the attack Matsuda Sun Tsai emerges from under the ring as Hiro Iishi whips HBC to the ring corner. A Diamond dust by MST floors one half of the Evolution Tag Team Champions. Hiro Iishi goes for the pin as his partner rolls out a ring, and with a handful of tights the ULWN contingent pulled off the upset victory. "It looks like the Urban Legend Wrestling Nation are pulling out all the stops as they feel they have been considered developmental talent for too long..." Monica Angel begins..."Will this carry over to this weeks Legends? I know one thing for sure Evolution Wrestling will not sit still after being smacked in the face to what amount to their little brother.....as I just got word that none other than Tyler Davidson will be here next week during our Saturday house show...the one that will give us our main event concerning Luther wisdom. Well that is all from tonight on The Genesis Pit" ROGUE-TV: Channel 89 **The Anti-Spike**
