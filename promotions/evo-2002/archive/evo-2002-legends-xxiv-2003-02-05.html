Legends XXIV Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The L.A.Sports Arena ~ Los Angeles, California 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night February 5th, 2003 Last week was a hectic show for just about anyone that has ever crossed the paths of The Unlimited. With Daniel Somers now firmly entrenched in their corner the group has show just what it is they can do with their newfound power and this week’s show doesn’t look like they’ll be calming down any. The Unlimited are given the chance to capture the Tag Team Titles and the Continental Title both in one night as they also get the opportunity to try and eliminate Sean Mason and Seara once and for all in tag team action. In the main event the Universal Title will also be on the line as four of the top guns will battle as James Kimson looks on as the referee. Mason and Seara are teaming up this week for the same purpose, to get a little bit of payback against Xander for his attacks on them last week. Xander left both Seara and Mason lying at his feet at separate points of last weeks show just as a little message for the two young stars. And in the corner of Xander as his partner for this one will be the always dangerous Angel who would love the simple chance to make an impact alongside his leader for this one in Xander. Asis has once again earned another shot at the Continental Title this time at the expense of Sean Mason. While Mason is sure to be keeping an interested eye on this match he’ll have to do it from the locker room as Dreamer must defend his title this week. Asis has been on a rampage thus far and is determined to finally capture the elusive Continental Title gold. The Unlimited are the only other tag team in eVolution to have held the titles so far and now they get a chance to once again regain them as they battle Luther Wisdom and The Heartbreak Chick. Despite their obvious difference in opinions, HBC and Wisdom are going to have to find some way to defeat a very dangerous Unlimited team that also has the backing of the man currently running the show in eVolution. Needless to say the deck will definitely be stacked against the champions in this one. Last week the team of Morpheus Omega and Sky Night made an impact as the mystery team against The HeartBreak Chick. The two of them were mere matters of seconds away from winning the Tag Team Titles when an impromptu title match broke out. This week they again find themselves placed in a handicap match this time against Malcolm King as they try and prevent him from gaining a shot at Shocka for the Universal Title. Shocka is in action again this week as he finds himself in the unenviable position of having to defend his coveted Universal Title against three other men. Jason Jameson wanted Sovereign one on one again, and Frost wanted Shocka but they both got what they wanted with a little twist thrown into the mix thanks to Daniel Somers. Now all four men will collide in what is certain to be an incredible battle as newcomer James Kimson will be the fifth man in the ring with the sole purpose of counting the one, two, three and declaring the winner at the end of the match. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Troy Pierce, James Kimson, Strictly Prohibited, Yoshii Tanaka and many of eVolution’s superstars!
