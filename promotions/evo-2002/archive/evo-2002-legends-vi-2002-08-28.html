From The Raleigh Sports Arena ~ Raleigh, North Carolina 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night August 28th, 2002 Legends V was the expected follow-up to the events at X-Rated. The Universal Champion had arrived and gone a violence spree and the first two matches for Digital Destruction had been announced. What could possibly be done to make things even more hectic around eVolution? For starters we will find out just what was on that tape received by Jeremy Hunter and Eric Graves and two titles will be on the line. Lightning has struck twice in two weeks for young Sean Mason. First at X-Rated and now at Legends V he has been screwed out a victory by the pair of Thug roC and Quasar the Great. Now he will try and get some redemption for himself as he takes on the newcomer, Seara. Seara had problems of her own on Legends V with some sort of deal being struck and a questionable loss against HBC. We’ll see if she can rebound this week against Sean Mason. Python gets his shot promised him by Thug roC against Quasar this week but much to his regret the High Impact Title won’t be on the line. Quasar has been getting himself into some trouble as of late with only the authority of Thug roC to save him. It’s now time to see whether or not he can stand on his own in the ring when that trouble catches up to him. Three men all are battling it out in this one trying to prove themselves in the eyes of eVolution front office members. Alias has come in with a message to prove to everyone inside the Cell but with the injuries having stacked up to the roster he’s being forced into the squared circle instead. Opposing will be Platinum Squared as Platinum Cassidy and Mr. Platinum himself Brad Kelley partake in the festivities. Both looking for some redemption following disappointing losses in the past two weeks. Several battles come together in this one as Pierce and L2J team with another former EdW superstar in Malcolm King to take on three other EdW stars who don’t share their opinions apparently. With Xander and The Syndicate on one side of the ring you know the rest of The Unlimited can’t be too far behind in this one. Throw in Jamal Kage on the side of his apparent friend Big Daddy Dreamer and you have a team to beat which will be tough for many to overcome. Can the EdW team of Pierce, King, Shodan, and L2J do it? And how closely will The Hollywood Monster be watching his Digital Destruction adversary this week? Shocka earned the right to this title shot two weeks ago at X-Rated and now on Legends VI he gets his chance at gold. Christoph has been dominant in his reign as champion defeating everyone to step in the ring with him. Can his streak continue against Shocka? A man who shocked everyone with a victory over Platinum Cassidy at X-Rated to earn the shot? The actions of Asis at Legends V left many people wondering just what was going through the head of the popular young star. One of those men is Jason Jameson who apparently did not like the way Asis handled himself with his fellow EdW alumni. Jameson a former Platinum Champion in EdW looks to teach Asis, the current Rising Star Champion, a lesson in respect. The first Universal Title defense of the monster called Sovereign. His first adversary is none other than the rookie Matt Boone. Perhaps it’s his rookie stubbornness that keeps him coming back time after time but once again Boone wants a chance to defeat Sovereign. This time all the action will take place in the confines of a fifteen foot high steel cage. The brutality is certain to be unspeakable but can Boone survive the monster Sovereign yet again? Or will he be another victim to fall before his onslaught? Stay tuned to Splink Parp tonight to find out the answer!
