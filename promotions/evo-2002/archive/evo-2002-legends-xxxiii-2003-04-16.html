Legends XXXIII Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Bradley Center ~ Milwaukee, Wisconsin 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night April 16th, 2003 eVolution continues on its trek towards End of Days as the battle between Somers and Davidson continues to heat up with the sides being quickly drawn. With each passing week The Unlimited seems to gather even more power with the addition of the NTICW branch to its number but this week they have more trouble ahead for them. The main event will feature two of the top Unlimited members and two of eVolution’s top stars as they all battle it out in a four corners match. The battle between New-TV and members of Team eVolution, Shocka and Lee eXodus also continues this week in two separate matches as some old adversaries will cross paths once again. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Last week the team known as Tribute arrived in eVolution in a most peculiar way and now this week they get straight to work with a battle against one of the top teams in eVolution in Strictly X. Yoshii Tanaka and Nik VanCore are both coming off some minor injuries sustained at Unbreakable and the Legends after but now they are both ready for action as they tangle with this new team that as their name implies likes to pay Tribute to their adversaries among others. Fiona picked up a huge victory last week over NTICW’s Homicide and now she tries her luck again this time as she battles against Hate. Hate was one of the top stars in NTICW before Somers brought him to eVolution to aid in his battle and now after coming off a victory last week against Chuck he’ll try and continue his win streak here this week. Fiona meanwhile has set her sights on bigger goals and views Hate as nothing more than a bump on her road towards them. Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick return to action again this week against their adversaries The BodySnatchers who last week surprised eVolution when they changed their allegiances from Daniel Somers over to Tyler Davidson. With the history between these two teams it makes little difference whose side they are on as far as The Syndicate is concerned as they intend to put an end to their feud with The BodySnatchers once and for all. Jason Jameson made his return last week and saved Chuck from what looked like it would be a brutal beating at the hands of NTICW’s Hate and Ronan Pierce. The help however wasn’t appreciated by Chuck it seems as he responded with an attack of his own against Jason Jameson and as a result the two of them will go head to head this week. The question on everyone’s mind however is whether or not Jameson is really at one hundred percent following his injury against Scott Frost at Unbreakable. Chuck will be his first test on his road to recovery but if last week is any indication it certainly will not be his last. Thug roC and Lee eXodus were at one time the best of friends but that was before Lee eXodus has made the decision to become the number one Frostcicle of Scott Frost. Now Thug roC will go one on one against his former friend and ally and current Team eVolution member as the two of them will continue the Team eVolution/New-TV battle that has sparked up in recent weeks thanks to Shocka’s antics. Shocka will no doubt be watching this match but he also must make sure he doesn’t touch Thug roC in any form thanks to the restraining order roC has placed on him. Shocka and Hedges are two men that have had a long history together. It’s a history that has left Hedges with an undefeated record in one on one matches against Shocka. This could Shocka’s final chance at defeating Hedges and he’s going to go all out and do it but there can be no doubt that New-TV certainly has some tricks still left up their sleeves in time for this match and Shocka had better be ready for anything. Match two is on its way and everyone was left surprised with the outcome of the first match as it ended in a time limit draw. Now Twisted Reality and Team Network will have to turn up the heat on one another in their efforts to claim the official eVolution Tag Team Championships! With both teams now at a disadvantage of not having a win yet going into the second match the importance of this one has certainly picked up and the winner will find themselves in a perfect position to capture the gold. The team that reaches two wins first will be the team that becomes the Unified Tag Team Champions! Asis has been tearing through the competition it seems since he captured the Continental Title and while the title certainly isn’t on the line in this match he would no doubt love the opportunity to tear Tyler Davidson apart and perhaps put an end once and for all to the battle that is currently going on between Tyler and Somers. Asis is still currently part of The Unlimited though in recent weeks there has been some obvious friction between him and Xander. If this match goes the way The Unlimited want it too it could do a lot to end that friction or it could make matters even worse for the group and the victory of Daniel Somers over Tyler Davidson. After the events of last weeks main event match that saw The Unlimited and NTICW try to outnumber Team eVolution’s side James Kimson came to the rescue of Team eVolution with a steel chair in his hands for his equalizer. Now Kimson, Frost, Xander, and Christoph will all be in the same ring again tonight in a huge four corners brawl where the Universal Title will not be on the line. Kimson is also just coming off some injuries as a result of his brutal match with Sovereign at Unbreakable and will have to try and find a way to overcome any lingering effects if he hopes to survive against these three men and also answer the challenge that was laid down last week by Erin Christoph. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Sean Mason, Seara and many of eVolution’s superstars!
