Legends XXVIII Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Rose Garden ~ Portland, Oregon 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night March 12th, 2003 Last week Legends saw the reformation of a stable that was thought dead and the open challenge being issued by Scott Frost for anyone in eVolution to come and challenge him for the Universal Title. That challenge was accepted but Frost has gone so far to say that after he defends his title this week he will once again be looking for another challenger as he wants to prove himself to be a fighting champion. The tag team action will also continue to heat up this week as the battle for the newly sanctioned Tag Team Titles will began and possibly even the battle for the unsanctioned titles against Morpheus Omega and Sky Night whether they like it or not. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Fiona Preston was promised a match last week by Daniel Somers against one of the eVolution superstars and the man she found herself paired against was one half of Strictly X, Nik VanCore. Fiona will be put to the test in her very first match here against a talent like nVc. Nik on the other hand must be keeping an eye over his shoulder and a close watch for Xander who is certainly plotting some form of payback for VanCore’s interference in his match against Seara at Tainted Love. Last week we saw the formation of the brand new BodySnatchers team with the return of Wideload and BlackHavoc to bolster the team following the injury to 1001 Holds. Last week the new group made an impactful debut with a vicious attack against Sky Night but now they find three people who are more than ready to fight standing across from them. The trio of Big Daddy Dreamer, HeartBreak Chick, and Luther Wisdom will join forces as they try to stop The BodySnatchers cold before they can even get started. The Unlimited have continued their dangerous ways the past few weeks and they now find themselves paired off against Jason Jameson and “The DiscoKid” Troy Pierce. Pierce has set his sights upon Asis since his recent return to action in eVolution and this will be his first actual chance to get his hands on him provided things go as planned. Jameson on the other hand as seen last week seems to be struggling for a space to fit in and also might have an invitation on the back of his mind that was extended by his old allies Scott Frost and Seara last week. An invitation that was also sent out towards Troy Pierce. The Crimson Sun returns to action in eVolution with a new mission but before they can even think of getting that mission started they must try and find a way to get by the team of Statuz Quo and Duzza. Finally, after weeks of run-ins, the Playaz Inc. team makes their official eVolution in ring debut with this match against The Crimson Sun who have since set their sights on Daniel Somers. Is it just coincidence in their first match back that they find themselves paired against one of the most dominant tag teams to ever step foot in PCW? Or did Somers know exactly what he was doing when he put this one together? Thug roC and Morpheus Omega had a joyous reunion last week as they reformed the nearly dead stable of New-TV along with Sky Night the other half of the unsanctioned eVolution Tag Team Champions. This week the two men of the group team together to battle against the unlikely duo of Seara and Shocka. Whether the two of them can possibly survive teaming together after all the past history between them remains to be seen but it will certainly be a huge battle and Shocka is definitely out for revenge after what happened last week. Last week Frost came out and made an open challenge to anyone interested to challenge for his newly won Universal Title. Erin Christoph was the first man to accept and with the unresolved history between the two men you really can’t find it that hard to believe. It was Frost who debuted at Digital Destruction and cost Christoph his match against Tyler Davidson that also cost him his job in eVolution for a couple of months until Somers managed to take control and rehired him. Now for the first time these two men will step into the ring and go one on one with the Universal Title hanging in the balance. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Sovereign, Lee eXodus, Chuck and many of eVolution’s superstars! Parking Lot ~ Get In….. Kimson stands behind Sovereign who’s unaware, the voices inside his head probably blocking out the crowd as he looks down at Frost, who’s obviously in no condition to resist anyone. Kimson, wearing his leather jacket, jeans and more obvious is a neck brace. He then rips off the brace and throws it to the ground to a huge pop from the fans. Sovereign then turns around and spots Kimson who promptly fires a kick to the mid-section and then flattens Sovereign with a huge History in the Making! Sovereign sells huge as he bounces clear off the mat! Kimson pops up and removes his jacket and rips off his T-shirt. His arms pumping back and forth as the crowd is blowing the roof off the rafters. You can faintly hear Justin Fritz going crazy over the mic, but the crowd is to loud for you to make out what he’s saying! You can hear Jack Tyler saying something to his dislike of the situation but still to loud. Sovereign slowly starts to move as Kimson positions himself up for a second move, but surprisingly Erin Christoph gets up, sizing up the situation things that taking out Kimson would do him some good. He moves to attack, but Kimson must have eyes behind his back, he spins, boots Christoph in the ribs and then delivers a second History in the Making, Christoph slammed face first onto the mat rolls out of the ring on sheer momentum and Kimson gets up, then turns quickly, boots Sovereign in the ribs and then takes him, head first and throws him over the top rope! Sovereign lands on the cold floor with a huge smack and rolls a few times as Kimson follows up to the ropes, one foot on the bottom, leaning over the top rope taunting Sovereign to come and get some more! Sovereign looks up at Kimson with a look of pure amazement and shock as Kimson then flexes his impressive body as what could be an intimidation plot, Sovereign still totally shocked. "MY GOD KIMSON ISN’T FINISHED!!" Screams Fritz over the crowd "AND HE’S GOT A DATE WITH SOVEREIGN AT UNBREAKABLE!!" he adds. "Nooooo! Damn it! God Damn it!" says Tyler, shock and disappointment in his voice. Sovereign is now exiting the arena, not taking his eyes off of Kimson, his jaw open almost like it hit the floor. Kimson backs off and smiles a cocky grin and then turns. Scott Frost getting up, very slowly. Kimson slowly walks up to him, then bends down and picks up the Universal Title, dropped in the melee. Frost now, standing still effected by the match, and the beating sees Kimson with the title, look at it. Frost, unsure what to do about the situation stares at Kimson. "Uh-oh what’s going to happen here?" said Fritz, somewhat concerned. Kimson then smiles and offers the belt to Frost, who accepts it. Kimson then hoists Frost’s hand as Champion! The fans burst into move cheers as Stupify blasts over the speakers. Kimson pats Frost on the back and then marches his way to the turnbuckle and raises his arms in glory as Legends goes off the air to the sight of Seara, Frost and Kimson celebrating together.
