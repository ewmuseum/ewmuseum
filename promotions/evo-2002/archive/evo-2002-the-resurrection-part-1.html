The Resurrection, Part 1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He had lost it all. All the money, all the fame, all the power. It had all been taken away from him again with but a moments notice. eVolution had been ripped from their hands and put into an early grave. Tyler had already all but given up on the promotion now and focused instead of his own wrestling career in LoC. That was something he couldn’t do however. He could never do that again and as such there were only two things left to him. Dreams and the drink. The first one always seemed to be just out of his reach but the second one however was always close at hand nowadays. That is why [name withheld] now sat at McGuire’s Irish Pub on the lower east side of Manhattan watching the New York Jets football game and downing drink after drink. There seemed at least to be nothing else for him to do at this point. He had made several attempts over the past several months to convince the Davidson Enterprises Board of Directors to allow him to reopen the promotion but he never got anywhere with it except for another meeting being scheduled down the road. Next week would be another such meeting and he had no doubt that the results would be the same. It was clear to him that out of the nine member board there were only three people that supported him fully. Robert Brooks, Leya Forth, and Jeremy Hunter however were simply not enough to turn the board against Justin Roberts. [name withheld] was convinced that Roberts had been dragging this whole ordeal out for the past several months for the sole purpose of tormenting him. At that thought [name withheld] slammed his empty glass down onto the bar top in frustration drawing the attention of the bartender and several of the other patrons. “Ready for another I take it?” the bartender asked dryly as it was clear he had seen this occur many times before. “What the fuck do you think?” [name withheld] replied bitterly as he stared up at the bartender who seemed about ready to tell him exactly what he thought but a third party chimed in from behind [name withheld] before anything else could be said between the two men. “I think he’s had more than enough personally,” the person said in a voice that was all too familiar to [name withheld] “Get lost Hunter,” [name withheld] ordered him without even turning around to look at Jeremy Hunter as he stood behind him shaking his head. “I’m in no mood to listen to you about giving up on my dreams. I already know how unattainable they are thanks to you bastards selling EdW back to that scumbag Brandon Kelley.” Jeremy continued to shake his head at [name withheld] unseen before he took a seat next to him at the bat. He ordered a beer for himself and paid the bartender a bit extra for the trouble before he turned back to [name withheld] “Then why do you keep fighting for it [name withheld]?” Jeremy finally asked him. “Every single month we go through the board meetings with you only for you to be told to come back next month by that jackass Roberts. Why keep going?” “Because it’s all that I have left damn it!” [name withheld] shouted again drawing more attention to himself. He reached for his glass again before he realized it was empty and then shot an angry glare at the bartender who turned his back on him. “Then believe in it!” Jeremy told him sternly. “eVolution existed for two years on its own merits with men and women who poured their heart and soul into the company. Do you really think that the loss of the EdW trademark nonsense would hurt that in the slightest bit?” “No, no it wouldn’t,” [name withheld] agreed with a deep sigh. “But we both know that Roberts won’t ever let it happen. Especially not with me running the whole thing. He just has too much influence over the rest of the board. Next week will just be more of the same and we both know it.” “Actually next week isn’t happening,” Jeremy told him. “That’s why Jess told me where you were.” “What do you mean next week isn’t happening?!” [name withheld] asked suddenly taken aback. “It’s happening tomorrow,” Jeremy told him without looking at him before he took a gulp of his beer. “Roberts realized that with the appointment of a new board member recently that he no longer has the strong support on the matter. As a result he decided to make his final move against you and eVolution sooner to try and prevent any turning of the tide against him. Tomorrow is going to be the final vote on the fate of eVolution.” “What?!” [name withheld] shouted in clear disbelief. “He can’t do that!” “Yes he can,” Jeremy told him. “But if you listened you would realize that this is a good thing. He’s losing support and there is a very good chance the vote will go in your favor. But to do that you have to do on top of your game tomorrow. So as I said you’ve had enough to drink and had better head home to Jess. She’s waiting for you.” [name withheld] took a few moments to turn his stare between the empty glass on the bar and then back to Jeremy before he slowly nodded his head and stood back up with a bit of a stumble before he regained his balance. “Fine, fine,” he said finally. “I’ll head home then since it seems I have a lot to do before tomorrow.” “Yes you do,” Jeremy agreed. “Now move along.” “You’re staying?” [name withheld] asked as he looked at Jeremy quizzically. “Sure why not?” Jeremy responded. “I ain’t got shit to do but say yes or no during the vote tomorrow. Now get!” That brought a bit of a laugh from [name withheld] who hadn’t done so in quite some time before he turned and left the bar. Tomorrow would be one of the biggest days of his life.
