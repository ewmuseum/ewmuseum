Legends 1 Transcript Live on CBN! From The Mellon Arena ~ Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night July 10th, 2002 eVolution exploded onto the scene July 3rd with the American Nightmare pay per view. The effects of that show are still being felt by the roster and fans alike. What kind of effects did the show have on eVolution itself? The answers too many of those questions can be expected to be answered here tonight. All of the stars will be out in full force as Legends invades the Mellon Arena in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. The quest to become the best in the business will continue as Troy Pierce again teams with his young protégé Matt Boone to battle Jamal Kage and Big Daddy Dreamer. All four men are already in the X-Rated Universal Title Tournament on August the 10th. Both Pierce and Boone however must be weary of the presence of Sovereign. The same man who had struck out against them at the end of American Nightmare. Sovereign by himself managed to lay waste to both men following their grueling tag team battle. His reasons behind it were still unclear to everyone but himself but his attention was definitely focused on his old adversary Daniel Somers. Just what are the plans of Sovereign’s for Somers? Tonight the answers can be perhaps be revealed. Sovereign himself will find his hands full as he must battle a ghost from his past. The history between L2J and Sovereign goes back over a year to the now defunct Old School Wrestling. L2J the partner of Daniel Somers had paid for it dearly at the hands of Sovereign in an attempt to hurt his hated rival. L2J, young and inexperienced at the time had suffered for his alliance at the hands of Sovereign with little hope of retribution. Now further into his career and more matured as an athlete the opportunity has presented itself. L2J must strike out for his chance of victory and he must defeat the monster Sovereign. If the odds were not already stacked against the fearless superstar, Tommy Michaels had also been named the guest referee for the match. Michaels and L2J teamed at American Nightmare to defeat Shifty and Barbatos Bronx but did not end the night seeing eye to eye. What kind of ulterior motive could Michaels have in this one? Only Michaels and the man who made him referee, Eric Graves knows for sure. The success of the Cell at American Nightmare was obvious from the reaction of the beating which Eric Knight and Marcus Escobar gave one another. The Cell returns tonight on Legends with not one, but two separate battles featuring three newcomers and one we have seen before. Eric Knight battles newcomer and former HLW star Jinx who had observed the first battle at American Nightmare. Jinx obviously has the upper hand knowing exactly what is in store for him but Knight has no clue over just what Jinx is capable of. In the second bout Jayme Black battle LeRoy Mohammed to see just who the superior newcomer is. Black seems to have a bit of a secret past about him keeping many people in the dark though a connection to Jinx and other former HWL stars is apparent. Four lucky, or unlucky depending on your point of view, individuals will be selected at the start of the show to compete in a Continental Title match. The only catch for all four is that anything can and will go in this match up. When all the dust is settled and cleared the only certainty is one of the four will be weighing an extra fifteen pounds thanks to the addition of a new gold belt to their wardrobe. One of the literally biggest stars in eVolution Impact will get a chance to make another impression on the fans and staff alike as he battles newcomer Luigi Lombardi. Luigi also made an impression on the people but not much in the same way. Luigi got himself under the skin of Quasar the Great at American Nightmare and had better be keeping a close look over his shoulder for any unwanted visitors during the course of the night. Then in the opener four corner cruiserweight action will kick things off as Quasar the Great battles against Barbatos Bronx, Archangel and Dave Hurst. Bronx and Quasar are both coming off of failed efforts at American Nightmare and looking for a little redemption. Hurst and Archangel however were left out of the festivities of American Nightmare and will now be looking for the chance to prove themselves against two that were and make people sit up and take notice.
