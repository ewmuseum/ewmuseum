From Meadow Lane ~ Nottingham, England 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night August 20th, 2003 Backstage ~ Size of the Dog Featuring Ryan Mason "Name sir" the guard demanded as the scene opened backstage at Legends. At this point we find ourselves outside in the car park where security is conducting their standard checks. Ryan Mason stands with his gym bag upon his shoulder going through the spot checks prior to admission; only you can tell he is a man in a hurry and with a degree of nervous tension as he stands tapping his foot on the concrete over and over again. "I asked you for your name" the guard shouted trying to bring Ryan out of his trance like stare. "Sean Mason" Ryan said before correcting himself, "I mean Ryan Mason" "You sure about that?" the guard asked in a mocking tone. "Yes thank you" Ryan said somewhat agitated. "Well that’s lucky" the guard replied, "had you said Sean" he paused, "I wouldn't have been able to let you in" "Like you say, lucky I aint really isn't it" Ryan snapped back as he marched past. Just then he stopped in his tracks. "Wait a second" "Yes junior?!" the guard said with a sigh. "What did you mean if I was Sean Mason I wouldn't be allowed in?" Ryan asked. "I mean what I said" the guard retorted, "Sean Mason is not on the list" "How can that be so?" "Well obviously he aint meant to be on it" the guard replied somewhat agitated now also. "I'd appreciate it if you dropped that tone" Ryan said biting his lip once more. "And I'd appreciate it if you got your little white college boy ass inside before I change my mind about admitting you into this building. I mean isn't it past your bedtime?" Just then Ryan Mason snapped. It seemed the toll had become too much and finally the strain had overcome him. As the guard leaned over him, Ryan rolled through and applied a variation of a hammerlock into a cross chicken wing. The guard began to scream and by passers looked on wearily. "Next time leave the one liners at home" Ryan said before pushing down harder. "Oh and should Sean arrive, what will you do?" "I don't understand" the guard screamed in pain "Lets see if this helps" Ryan said before wrenching down harder. "Ok ok" the guard screamed. "Ok what?" "He comes in" the guard said as his eyes began to fill up. "Thank you" Ryan said with a smug grin before tossing the guard to the ground. "That wasn't so hard was it? Now go order me some milk and cookies, before I go all Jackie Chain on your ass for a second time" Ryan adjusted the bag upon his shoulder and headed off into the distance. The guard stood up shaking the dust from his attire; embarrassed and shocked. As the saying goes; it’s not the size of the dog, but the fight of the dog. Backstage ~ Disrespect Featuring Damon O’Halloran and Cameron Griffin Cameron Griffin sipped his coffee, setting the cup down besides the slowly shrinking pile of paperwork on his desk. His job was not an easy one, but it was fulfilling. However, some things were less pleasant than others were. “Your guy out there told me you wanted to see me,” Damon O'Halloran said as he walked into Griffin's office. “I wanted to see you days ago. Thanks for being so prompt,” said Griffin, the sarcasm heavy in his voice. “I’m a busy man. What do you want?” Despite their hatred for each other, Cameron was impressed with the size of the big Irishman. When O'Halloran walked into a room, people couldn’t help but notice. The fans hated him, yet he was still successful from a marketing standpoint. “There are a couple things I wanted to discuss with you. Have a seat.” O'Halloran folded his huge arms in front of him, almost with difficulty. “I’ll stand.” “Stop the attitude. The cameras aren’t rolling.” Trying to get even a hint of respect out of O'Halloran was like getting blood from a stone. “I wanted to talk to you about the incident last week.” “You fining me?” O'Halloran asked as he pulled out a checkbook from his back pocket. “Tell me how much and I’ll take care of it.” “That’s not the point Damon. You can’t just buy yourself off the hook.” “Funny, coming from a guy like you,” smirked O'Halloran. “That’s a gimmick. I’d think you’d be able to tell the difference.” Cameron shuffled the papers on his desk and set them aside. “You stepped over the line. In fact, you’ve been doing that since you got here. I let you in as a favor to Mason, and you start throwing your weight around like you own the place. I’ll be frank here: your attitude has pissed off a number of people, and I won’t have one man disrupting my locker room.” “Save the speeches for the marks. You signed me because you knew I’d make you money. It just happens that Adam and I are good friends. I’ve been putting asses in seats everywhere I go, from Bumfuck Tennessee to the Egg Dome in Tokyo. People boo the Hell out of them when I walk down the aisle, but as soon as the match ends they’re at the souvenir stand buying my shit. It’s about dollars, and I bring in plenty of them. If some people in the back don’t like my attitude, they can piss up a rope for all I care.” Cameron couldn’t believe the gall. “Let me tell you something, O'Halloran. eVolution doesn’t revolve around one man. It certainly doesn’t revolve around you. You’ve been pushing me, Damon. Don’t think I won’t push back. With all the new talent coming in, you can’t afford to be getting into trouble.” Damon smiled. “Are you threatening me?” There was something in the look Damon gave him that made Cameron a little wary. “I’m simply giving you a reality check. You’re not untouchable, so don’t walk around here like you are. I won’t tolerate any kind of ‘clique’ behavior destroying morale and eroding the respect of the other guys for authority. It stops here.” Cameron waited to see if his words were getting through. Either they weren’t, or O'Halloran simply didn’t care. “I’m fining you for your actions towards me last week. I’m also giving you a warning, Damon. I don’t want this kind of behavior happening again. I’ve been letting you slide for quite awhile now, but no more. You know when you came here first, you told me you hada dream: to be the best. I have a dream too, Damon. Can't I have a dream?! My dream is to be in the Cell Division without you as my Champion, starting tonight with Sean Sterling, your life just got a whole lot worse.” “Are we finished?” “That’s it,” Cameron said. Damon wasted no time in leaving the office. Once in the hallway, Damon struggled to control his anger, physically trembling from the effort. A cool, wet sensation covered his lips. His nose had started bleeding, and bleeding heavily. He pinched his nostrils and tiled his head back. “Great.” O'Halloran spat. “If it’s not one thing it’s another.” Still, he thought to himself. Was he pushing the envelope too hard? More importantly, what would happen if he did go too far?
