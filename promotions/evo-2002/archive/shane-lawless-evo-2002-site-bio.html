Shane Lawless Real Name: Shane Lawson Date of Birth: November 23rd, 1974 Hometown: Los Angeles, California Height: 6'3 Weight: 255lbs Alignment: Neutral Theme Music: Ronnie by Metallica Manager: None Wrestling Style: Martial Arts/Technical Favorite Moves: Michinoku Driver Slam piledriver Fujiwara armbar (many variations) Evenflow DDT Spinning DDT Rolling DDT Martial Arts kicks Cartwheel kick Superkick Crecent kick from top rope Rolling Splash Ankle pick w/ palm to the chest Wrist-lock throw Japanese armdrag Monkey flip Split-legged Moonsault Single-arm bulldog Knee-to-face facebuster "Above the Law" facebuster (Drives a chair into opponent's face with guillotine legdrop) Kata-hajime Crossface Crucifix armbar Twisting Crucifix armbar slam Signature Move: Set-Up: A Touch of Insanity-Shane kicks his opponent in the gut and grabs the back of his or head, jumping and sitting-out, driving his opponent's face viciously hard into the mat. (aka "The X-Factor") This is meant to be used as a surprise move and can be hit from any position or situation (ie. Irish Whip, Springboard, Running, etc.) and is often used as a set-up for "Brain Dead." Finisher: Brain Dead-Shane grabs his opponent's wrist from behind and places his head in the middle of their shoulder blades. He then spins, twisting his opponent around into a similar position, with his opponent's head held firmly between his own shoulder blades. He then sits out and throws himself back, spiking his opponent's head and neck with brutal force. (aka "The Unprettier" or "The Tomakazie") Other Info Current Teams: None Past Teams: None eVo Title History: None eVo Award History: None Other Titles: $W Global Champion(x1) Other Awards: None Biography Entrance: The lights in the arena mysteirously go out. There is a long, tense pause. Suddenly, the opening chords to Metallica's "Ronnie" begin to play. As the drum beat kicks in, a spot flashes in time, illuminating the top of the ramp like a giant strobe light. As the music crashes in, dark blue and white strobes begin to flash and a giant spotlight at the back of the ramp illuminates the silhouette of Shane Lawless as he emerges from behind the curtain. He pauses for a moment, ringing his wrists and smoothing down the tape wrapped around his knuckles. He walks calmly down to the ring, an ominous smirk on his face as he rolls in from under the bottom rope. He throws a jump, spinning scissor kick and stretches his arms, leaning backwards over the top rope and cracking his vertebrae. Finally, he moves to the corner and leans against the turnbuckle, his elbows resting on the ropes,one leg hitched up backwards and resting on the bottom turnbuckle as his eyes watch the top of the ramp, waiting for his opponent (or watching his opponent if he is already in the ring). Appearance: He has a strong, athletic build, but is not ridiculously over-muscled. He has shorter, jet black hair that was at one time dark blonde (not short but not long). He has trademark steel-gray eyes and has a silver eyebrow bar in his left eyebrow. He is clean shaven with just a hint of a goatee. He wears a plain black, long-sleeve tee shirt and a pair of long, blue denim jean shorts. He wears black wrestling boots with a red trim, similar to Tommy Dreamer's, but he has shin protectors like RVD. His hands are wrapped in black tape. When not in the ring, he prefers to wear casual shoes and khaki pants with a short-sleeve, black button-up shirt. Biography: Born Shane Lawson, he was fathered by the CEO of a world-renowned law firm and mothered by a sadistic, greedy woman whose sole purpose of having Shane was to tie down his father so she could suck the life from his bank account. Often, when his mother was not spending his father's money, she was dragging to the strip and to all-night parties to mingle with the latest and greatest of stage and screen. Shane, left alone for most of the day, taught himself to read by watching television and reading his father's newspapers. When he was old enough, he was enrolled into the most expensive private schools money could buy. But Shane was a sad child; many nights were spent laying awake in bed, waiting for the crunch of gravel outside that would signal his parent's return, and many nights were spenting crying himself to sleep when it never came. Then...one day...Shane was studying alone in the family's garden when he was approached by a man, the family's new caretaker, Bob Grayson. Bob and Shane became instant friends. They did almost everything together; Shane would often prefer to spend the night the small shack that Bob slept in. It was through Bob that Shane fell in love with professional wrestling. He idolized such greats as Andre the Giant, Hulk Hogan, and Jimmy Snuka. Several time, Bob would take Shane to the local wrestling shows, and every suplex, every wristlock, every punch would mesmerize Shane - and he loved it. His relationship with Bob was a special one to Shane; Bob was almost the father that Shane always wished for but never had. Finally, one fateful night...it all came crashing down. Shane was sitting in the living room reading when he heard shouting. He went to investigate and saw his father and his mother engaged in a terrible shouting war. Finally, it escalated and his father stormed out. His mother stormed back to her room and Shane shrugged, returning to his reading. Then he heard Bob enter the front door. He had come to check to see if everyhting was alright. Shane jumped up to run and see his best friend. However, as he reached the foyer, he looked up to see Bob at the top of the steps; his mother was there too, he teeth bared, her rage flashing. In a moment it was over. Bob lay at the bottom of the steps, his neck broken in several places and his head twisted around on his shoulders. Shane felt something explode inside of him as he looked as his mother. His father returned just in time to see Shane, strangling his mother, throw her over the banister. She survived the fall with critical head injuries and to this day, Shane has very little memory of that night. His father's influence kept his mother from prison, but Shane was not so lucky. He was sent for an extended stay to Dr. Joseph Benton's Hospital for the Clinically Insane. Shane then underwent four years of intense psychological counseling that left him cold and emotionless, if somewhat sane. His father, eager to have him out of his life, signed into his name a large bank account and sent him away. Shane used his fortune to put himself through Harvard, where he defied all odds to become one of the youngest graduates ever, receiving a doctorate in Psychology. While in college, he studied a variety of martial arts, like Jujitsu, Ninjitsu, Karate, Tae Kwon Do, Aikijutsu, and Muay Thai Kicboxing. It was in college that he rediscovered his love for professional wrestling. He put himself into a wrestling academy and. using his martial arts knowledge, excelled quickly. Finally, at the tender age of 23, he entered his first wrestling federation and shot to the top instantly. The rest, as they say, is history.
