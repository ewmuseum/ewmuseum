Shocka lifts the steps up and brings them slamming down hard across the midsection of Frost. Shocka leaves the steps lying across and once again grabs the book of matches. He lights another match up and this time tosses it down onto the table causing the whole thing to burst into flames as Shocka and the referee both take a few quick steps back to avoid the burn. Justin Fritz: My god Shocka is actually going to try and do it! That sick son of a bitch! Jack Tyler: He’s trying to win the match and hold onto his Universal Title. Justin Fritz: No he’s trying to end the career of Frost first chance he gets! Jack Tyler: Yeah that too. Shocka has a big smile on his face as he watches the table burn before he turns his attention back to Frost who is still pinned under the steps. Shocka shoves the steps off to the side and picks Frost back up to his feet. With Shocka’s back facing the table he begins setting Frost up for a powerbomb and he lifts him into the air before spinning around but before he can drive Frost through the burning table Frost fires back with a quick flurry of right hands until Shocka falls backwards with Frost right on top of him. Frost lands almost right on his feet and turns quickly around as Shocka gets up and catches him right in the jaw with a quick super kick. Justin Fritz: Frost is fighting back again now! Jack Tyler: Why won’t he just stay down and accept the inevitable? Justin Fritz: Because he’s not a quitter. Frost slowly gets back to his feet and lifts Shocka up with him again. He rolls Shocka onto the apron so that he’s still lying on the outside of the ring and he then hops up onto the apron himself. Frost gets to his feet on the apron and lifts Shocka up. He begins setting him up for a powerbomb off the apron and through the flaming table but Shocka drops down before Frost can lift him and nails him with the low blow. He grabs Frost and setting him up quickly Shocka takes Frost off the apron and sends them both slamming through the flames and table with the #1 Stunna. Justin Fritz: Shocka actually did it! Jack Tyler: I can’t believe it! It’s over Fritz! Justin Fritz: After that I would sincerely hope so! Who knows what other lengths Shocka would go to in order to win here tonight. Jack Tyler: That would be really bad probably. Frost obviously got the worst of the move and is left lying amidst the flames and shards of the table as some ring attendants come rushing in with fire extinguishers. Shocka rolled out of the pile and also got sprayed down as the fire had burnt into his tights, skin and hair. Shocka already began making his way up as the ref began counting after the shock wore off. One Two Three Four Five Six Seven Shocka is barely back on his feet and clutching his ribs and right arm in pain. Eight Nine Ten! Libby Bowman: Your winner and STILL Universal Champion……..Shocka! Justin Fritz: It’s finally and mercifully over! Jack Tyler: And Shocka is still the champion Fritz! Just like I told you would happen. Justin Fritz: It happened but it was a hellacious fight between the two men and it took Shocka and a flaming table to win this one. Jack Tyler: Medics are already tending to both men. I haven’t even seen Frost move after the #1 Stunna off the apron and through the table. Justin Fritz: Frost definitely got the worst of that but he might have some serious burns and injuries to be tended to after the show. Shocka is handed his Universal Title and he stands tall now trying to raise the title in the air but he tries it with his right arm first and can’t do it from the pain. He switches hands and raises the title all the way up this time drawing the boos from the crowd before he walks over the body of Frost and poses again. Shocka looks down at Frost with a look of disgust and then walks off heading backstage leaving the medics to tend to Frost. Justin Fritz: Shocka is just loving this. We’re out of time here tonight fans. We’ll be back with Legends next week!
