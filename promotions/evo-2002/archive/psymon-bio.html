Psymon Biography Real Name Simon Kreed Nicknames 'The Demented One;' 'eVo's Resident Psycho' Hometown Springfield, Illinois Birthday July 5, 1980 Height 6'0' Weight 235 Interests Stuff Favorite Pro Wrestlers There isn't really a favorite in my eyes. It wouldn't be fair to the rest of the superstars that I like if I picked favorites :-P Fed Titles None at the moment Fed Awards None at the moment Biography There's not much known about this poor, demented soul, but this is what we do know... Before Psymon was born, his mother, 18-year-old Alexis Falco, was raped in a dark alley. The next day, she was pregnant. She has thought about an abortion but thought against it, so she decided to put her unborn baby and put him or her up for adoption She called up the Kreed family, who have helped her out for living on her own and independence. She told them that she was pregnant and asked them if they can have their child. Steven and Rachel Kreed were delighted to have her child, Steven the most. Why was he so excited to have Alexis' child? Finally, on July 5, 1980, the time has come. Around 1:15 am, she called up the Kreed family telling them that she's in labor. They rushed over to take Alexis to the hospital. Hours have passed and she was supplied with a safe dosage of drugs to block out the pain she had endured. Finally, 3:00 AM was time when the drugged-up Alexis gave birth to 'Simon', named after her dead brother, was born. He wasn't born as Simon Falco, no, the Kreed family signed over the rights to claim Simon, so he was born as Simon Kreed. Isn't that sweet? A family to take in a newborn child. Sort of a 'facism you can vote for' type deal. Wait, that didn't make any damn sense. Oh, well, let's get back on track, shall we? During his infant and toddler years, Simon was treated well, like he was apart of the family, and he had a sister to play with. Her name was Emily and she was 7 years older than he was. Everything was going just fine until Simon turned 7, that was when all the pain and torment happened. His adopted father constantly beat on him. His mother was no help, she would watch on in her self-amusement, fingering herself. After the pummeling that Simon would get, the adopted parents would fuck right in front of the haggard and beaten Simon. Steven and Rachel would neglect Simon weeks apon weeks, but where was his sister in all of this? Emily wasn't much help, she couldn't protect him, she would get a beating as well from her father if she tried to. After each beating Simon would get, voices would manifest themselves in his head, telling him to kill his parents and his sister. He ignored them. As Simon got older, his beatens became more severe; broken bones and several trips to the hospital. Still, Simon couldn't defend himself, he can't defend himself against a 6'6', 290-pound father. He has tried, but that got him nowhere, just more broken bones and trips to hospital. He could barely attend school after all the beatings he gets. He couldn't go 5 seconds without puking up blood in class, because the beatings are that bad. One day, as Steven was beating the fucking piss out of Simon, he admitted that he raped his mother. That's why he wanted to have Simon so badly. That enraged Simon and he punched his father hard in the jaw. That punch almost costed Simon's life...As a result of that beating (half his ripcage broken, punctured lung, grade three concussion and internal bleeding) Simon was laid away in the hospital for about...6 months, Psymon believes, looking back at that specific beating. By the time he was 15, he had fucking enough! He came home from school one day, went to the kitchen, pulled out a butcher knife and hacked up his father with it. Simon did have any problems since he split Steven's skull in two when Steven was sitting in his chair. Simon put his father's remains in the middle of the room and waited for his mother. Around 5 PM, Rachel came home and was greeted with a plastic bag over her head and three rapings before he broke her neck. She enjoyed the third rape before she died. He picked up his 5'8', 210-pound cunt of a mother and threw her on top of his father's butcher body. Then he realized that his 22-year-old sister is visiting the next. So, he removed his bloody clothes, showered and gets some rest before he can rape can kill his so-called 'sister.' Simon was up at 6 AM waiting for his sister's arrival. About 10 AM, Emily stepped inside and saw her parents' dead body in the middle of the house. Before she can scream, a butcher knife goes in her back. Afterwards, he raped her and decapitated her. After all was that done, he called the police, telling them that his voices told him to kill his family. A few hours later, he was sent to a mental institution for eighteen months. In his records, it shows that he was diagnosed with an acute case of psychosis and schizophrenia. When he was 16, he was released from the mental institution for being 'cured.' He decided to take up wrestling to work out his schizophrenic and psychotic tendencies on people. He trained for 9 months, doing everything he was told to do. Finally, on August 10, 1997 he has his first official match and almost killed his opponent, because of his schizophrenic and psychotic episodes. He was immediately fired after that match. He was enrolled in a few classes to control his schizophrenic and psychotic episodes. The classes worked rather well for him, and hasn't had an episode since. On his 20th b-day, July 5, 2000 (July 6 in Japan), was in Japan to face Kensuke Sasaki, one of the greatest wrestlers in NJPW (New Japan Pro Wrestling). Before his match started, he met Uriko Yakkama in the backstage area. They talked for awhile, never really realized that they are falling in love just by talking to each other. Finally, the biggest match of his match is up, against Kensuke Sasaki. It was 54+ minutes of pure skill and pain. Back then, he was wearing faded blue denim shorts with an inseam 5' below his knees, black wrestling boots cut off below the calves, a black muscle shirt and a long-sleeve red flannel shirt, sort of a grungy, trailer park trashy look. After minutes passed, he was wrestling topless. Kensuke accidentally got a hold of his left nipple ring and ripped it off. Psymon (he calls himself), didn't feel anything until he looked at his left pec, which was bleeding. He eventually lost the match and Kensuke respected his talent and him. Sasaki soon started an 'Ishiban' chant. 'Ishiban' is 'number one' in Japanese. This was, perhaps, the greatest event of his life. Now, Psymon makes it into the eVo for one purpose: competition and winning title belts. He will stop at nothing to get what he wants... Even if it means killing again! Other Feds EWP BWA ISWA 4WW Other Fed Awards EWP: Immediately fired after first match BWA: First and longest Television Champion (18 months) ISWA: Heavyweight Champion 4WW: Best Feud of the Year (Psymon VS Chris West); Best Match of the Year (Japanese Death match---Psymon VS Chris West---Maelstrom I); Most Hardcore Runner-Up; blacklisted from the 4WW Wrestling Style Technical mixed with Submissions, executed with a severe, aggressive style and has the toughness of any Hardcore/Extreme legend. He also has almost 19 years of experience in Capoeira. Finisher Move Possessed Finisher Description Queen Victoria Stretch; Psymon executes a Cross Indian Death Lock (or a Sharpshooter to you lamens). Afterwards, he applies a Full Nelson and pulls back as far as he can. This move has been known to break backs. Finisher Setup Move Tormented Soul Setup Description A violent, sit-out Square Driver; Psymon sets up his opponent up for the move. Next, he sweeps his feet from underneath him and violently sits down, giving the move more damage to his opponent's neck. If performed correctly, it'll break his opponent's neck. Afterwards, he finishes off his opponent with his finishing move, Possessed.
