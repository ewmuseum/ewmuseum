October 2000-March 2001, The Beginning of an Era of Destruction The beginning of Evolution started two years ago in Era of Destruction Wrestling. [name withheld] and [name withheld] together opened EdW for the very first time and ran the fed together for one month before the two had a falling out. [name withheld] up and left EdW taking some of the wrestlers with him and leaving Winters to run EdW himself. From November to the middle of December, Winters under the name [name withheld], continued to run EdW before time began working against him. [name withheld] facing less and less time was forced to make a decision as to whether he should shut EdW down or find someone else to take control. Big Daveylicious then stepped forward and took control of EdW from [name withheld] before trying to run things himself. He also though did not have the time it took to run things and EdW soon after closed down until January. [name withheld] again reappeared on the scene and tried regaining control of EdW from Big Davey but it was Kris Nightmare who in the end walked away with the company. Nightmare had as much luck as the previous owners however and EdW soon collapsed for what seemed to be the final time in March of 2001. August 2001-October 2001, An Era Reborn It was not to be the case though. Kris Nightmare after his failure sold the company to a young man he had known for a while named Brandon Kelley. Unlike the previous owners Brandon decided that before reopening EdW he would acquire the staff needed in order to help run the company successfully. At this point he had recruited former wrestler Daniel Somers to join him as Vice President of EdW. Together the two men worked day in and day out and began the preparations which would take EdW far into the future. Things got off to quite a slow start for the two men however and it seemed that EdW would never be destined for an opening again. It did happen however and despite some early problems for the company they eventually gathered together some of the top wrestlers in the world and all of them competed on EdW Turmoil. Men such as Malcolm King, Cori Simmons, L2J, Xander, Big Daddy Dreamer, Justin Famous, and Troy Pierce had all competed at one time or another under the EdW banner and made perhaps the biggest impacts of their career in EdW. Many other established stars from other organizations had also found their way into EdW with the majority of them coming from the former JEW wrestling franchises. The Assassin, Snoops, Thug Roc, Shocka, Sith, and Jackson Powers had all at one time or another competed for EdW. October 2001-January 2002, The Curse The EdW lifestyle however had always been a tumultuous one for all involved. Perhaps none more so however than the owners of the company. From [name withheld] to [name withheld] to Brandon Kelley it seemed that being owner of EdW would always lead to some trouble down the line. It didn't take long for that trouble to manifest itself for Brandon Kelley. In October of 2001 it was the invasion of the AWF which had began the streak for Brandon Kelley. Wrestler and AWF owner Chris Osbourne had returned to EdW during the premiere Turmoil and when he did he didn't come alone. Along with Chris Osbourne he brought forth an army of his former AWF wrestlers to lead an invasion against EdW and Brandon Kelley. It seemed that together Kelley and Daniel Somers would be able to lead EdW to a victory against the AWF and at Unbreakable they had set the first stage for that victory. Osbourne along with Too Xtreme, Big Daddy Dreamer, HeartBreak Chick and others were all present as Osbourne would battle former EdW owner Kris Nightmare for the Platinum Title. Daniel Somers and Brandon Kelley to bolster their chances appointed themselves as the referee's for the match. Osbourne though always thinking had his AWF eliminate Kelley before the match leaving Somers alone to officiate the match. It didn't take long for Somers to shock the world, including his employer, and turn on EdW to join with Osbourne and assist him in the victory for that match. Being Vice President of EdW it gave Somers and the AWF an added boost of power in the company and it was revealed shortly later that Somers was in fact the mind behind it all to begin with. A former AWF wrestler himself Somers enlisted the aid of Osbourne for an attempt at a hostile takeover of EdW. The plan did not last long however as Osbourne was injured shortly after Unbreakable and it left Justin Famous along with Somers in control of the renegade group. In the meantime Somers and Kelley signed on the dotted line to face at the second EdW pay per view, Vengeance in December with complete control of EdW on the line. It was Brandon Kelley who came out with the victory and complete control of his company once again. It wasn't long before Somers powerless in EdW was then turned on by his cohorts in the AWF and left laying as Justin Famous took control of the AWF from him. Famous followed his actions up by capturing the EdW Universal Title not to long after and leaving the AWF as they were set to take part in their biggest match to date. At When Worlds Collide the final battle had been signed and it was to be the EdW four vs. AWF four in a Wargames match. To the puzzlement of everyone the stipulations for the match had been signed that an EdW victory would mean the end of the AWF but the AWF on the other hand would receive nothing in return for their victory other than their continued existence. It was Famous who agreed to the stipulations but as everyone would find out it was Somers once again returning, this time alongside Brandon Kelley, who arranged the match and using his 50% ownership in AWF made the match official whether they liked it or not. The show came about and it was Big Daddy Dreamer, HeartBreak Chick, and Sabre who would battle Xander, Tru, C-Murda, and Troy Pierce for EdW. Billy Dreamer had been injured earlier in the night and left the deck stacked completely against the AWF as they were down one member in the match. Despite the disadvantage the trio did quite well for themselves keeping the EdW team off guard. As it often would throughout the course of EdW betrayal would again strike however. This time it was Big Daddy Dreamer and HeartBreak Chick that would betray the AWF and Sabre when they turned on him during the course of the match and left him alone against the EdW Four. The match didn't last another five minutes following the betrayal and Tru along with Xander put a final end to the AWF when they knocked Sabre out inside the cage. The AWF came to an end inside that cage only to find out that Somers in exchange for part ownership in EdW gave his ownership of the AWF to Brandon Kelley who then made it official and closed the AWF down in order to bolster the roster of EdW. It again left Somers and Kelley together to run EdW but now with Somers being part owner the power was equal in the company and it left Somers open to what many believed a curse running through EdW. February 2002-April 2002, The Beginning of the End On Friday night March 1st, 2002 the beginning of the end had begun. It was on this day in EdW history that Daniel Somers was viciously assaulted and abducted by a group that at the time were being called The Unlimited. What the repercussions of this move would be no one in the wrestling industry had any idea. There was one thing that was certain though, with Daniel Somers no longer in control of EdW it would give Brandon Kelley the opportunity he needed to take back control of the company that he originally was sole owner of. In the absence of Daniel Somers, Brandon Kelley and his new associates ran the EdW with an iron fist. They formed their own little group to help them run the company and they continued their assault against anyone that would stand in their way all the time ignoring the threat of The One and The Unlimited. What should have been one of the brightest times in the company’s history had rapidly turned into one of its darkest hours. In the midst of their world tour the inmates were literally running the asylum. The true talents and stars of the company such as Troy Pierce, L2J, Ryan Mason, Xander, Shodan, and Sabre were being pushed to the side in favor of the Brandon Kelley loyalists. Who was the man behind it all? That was the question being asked by everyone. First Daniel Somers had fallen and then it was Xavier Citizen that had fallen at the hands of The Unlimited. Many other lower wrestlers in the company had also made some mysterious disappearances in that time period as well. No one ever knew what had happened to them nor did they know where Somers or Citizen were located. Both were missing and assumed to be in the clutches of The Unlimited. Who was their leader? Who was leading this group on its violent path through EdW? Many people eventually began to believe that it was Brandon Kelley himself that had been secretly leading The Unlimited. Choosing when and where they would strike out. The list of victims at the time certainly lent credence to the theory. Daniel Somers, Xavier Citizen, Josh Sutton, Cori Simmons, and Xander had all been some of the earlier victims of The Unlimited. None of these men were in the good graces of Brandon Kelley and he would love nothing more than to have seen them taken out of action for good. The main target was obviously Daniel Somers. He had secretly become Co-Owner of EdW along with Brandon and that as a result gave him unlimited power in EdW. It was a power that he used time and time again to thwart the plans of Brandon and his associates. It’s no secret that the two never saw eye to eye on things and that is what had led to Somers earlier attempt at a take over using the AWF as a weapon. He failed then but after rejoining EdW things began going his way. Brandon obviously saw this happening and knew that he had to find some way to put a stop to it. That was where The Unlimited came into the picture thought the people, a secret organization with a secret leader that no one knew. Yet despite no one knowing who the leader was he certainly displayed the knowledge of just who the people were. Who else could have possessed this knowledge? Many people had claimed that it was Somers himself that was behind it in another attempt to take the company over in a more secretive way this time so that he would not be putting his own career at risk. This was mostly just talks of the people however and there was never any evidence to even lend a passing bit of credibility to the theory. All the evidence however certainly pointed in the direction of Brandon Kelley. Until the night of March 22nd it did. On that night the third victim of The Unlimited had been chosen. His name was Brandon Kelley. Face to face they stood Brandon Kelley and the masked visage of The One. That was the last that anyone had seen of Brandon that night. Where was he? Was he with Somers and Citizen or was it all a ruse to throw off the suspects? No one knew for certain but the truth was that there were now three victims that were taken prisoner. Daniel Somers, Xavier Citizen, and Brandon Kelley were those three men. What did they have in common if anything? Was there some hidden connection that linked all three of these men together? No one knew and it didn’t seem as though we would be finding out either. That is until the 29th of March came. For some reasons unknown to us all Brandon Kelley was released by his captors and returned to EdW. Why Brandon? Was there some sort of agreement reached between the two or was he the one behind it to begin with like so many people believed? To add even more puzzlement into the situation Xavier Citizen would disappear from the confines of The Unlimited’s stronghold as well shortly after that leaving Somers the only one still in their clutches that we knew of. Brandon Kelley returned to EdW on the 29th and picked up where he left off taking back the company that had been run by his associates for the one week he had been gone. Nothing at all had changed. He continued to ignore the actions of The Unlimited and focused all his anger on the trio of Troy Pierce, L2J, and Ryan Mason. They waged war against the three of them at the Lethal Injection pay per view but things were not all well in the camp of Brandon any longer. Problems had arisen between Alex Kayman and Brad Kelley. The two came to blows at the end of the match which had resulted in their eliminations and the victory of the Pierce, L2J, Mason trio. That wasn’t the end of the festivities however on that night. Xander had been the chosen one to do battle against The One in a career vs. mask match. If Xander won The One would reveal his identity to the world. However, if Xander were to lose that match it would mean that his career would be coming to an end in London. It was a hard fought battle with Xander’s newfound allies helping to fight off the attempted interference of The Unlimited before Xander managed to pull off the victory and forced The One to reveal himself at last. Who was The One? The One was not anyone that the people had guessed. He was not Daniel Somers nor was he Brandon Kelley. No, The One had been someone else altogether. He was someone from the past that the whole world had thought long gone. Forgotten he was but certainly not gone. The One was Malcolm King aka The Urban Legend, longtime friend and ally of Daniel Somers. The news sent shockwaves through EdW including the man he had just beaten in Xander. Xander and King were no strangers to one another either as they had battled in the past in OSW but even Xander didn’t expect to see his face on the opposite side of the mask. As quickly as he had appeared though, he had just as soon disappeared again into the darkness of the arena. That was when officials were sent to where Daniel Somers was supposedly being held. When they got there however there was no sign of Daniel Somers. He was gone again. Still in the clutches of The Unlimited? Or was he never in there hands to begin with? No one knew and Malcolm King was in no mood to reveal his intentions to anyone let alone the truth of the situation. He had disappeared from where he came and was unseen by all the people again. One thing was certain and that was he was waiting and watching, plotting his next move once again. It was obvious for reasons of his own that Brandon Kelley was not pleased with the events of the night. The battle between Brad and Alex had not gone over well. The revelation of Malcolm King and the mysterious disappearance of Daniel Somers again did not help matters at all. Did he have something to hide? Even his own ally Alex Kayman was not safe from the repercussions of Brandon’s wrath. It was Friday Turmoil on the 12th of April that Alex and Brandon had gotten into a heated argument which actually resulted in Alex coming to blows with teammate Brad Kelley. By the end of the night things seemed to have calmed down between the two but what could have been expected considering what had happened at the end of the show? Daniel Somers had made his presence felt. He shocked the EdW with his return at the end of Turmoil that night with something big to say. It wasn’t a return that went over well with Brandon Kelley and his associates at all. The other Co-Owner of EdW had returned and he announced that he had sold his half of EdW. Somers then proceeded to introduce who he sold it to when he called out the ex-wife of Brandon Kelley and introducing her as the new Co-Owner of EdW. What did Somers have to gain by this move? He loses all power that he had in EdW and is left with nothing for himself. No one knows but on that night he had left the EdW with a very cryptic message. On Wednesday night Brandon, the era of destruction will be coming to an end, and a new era will begin. What does this have to do with the present time? What did that message mean? That is uncertain but the one thing that everyone has agreed upon was that since this incident Daniel Somers has returned a changed man. For someone that had poured his heart and soul into the company that is EdW he had found himself betrayed. It was eventually revealed that the man behind the mask of The Unlimited was none other than longtime friend of Daniel Somers, Malcolm King. The man who had helped Somers in his quest to become OSW World Heavyweight Champion a few years back. It was Malcolm King that had taken Daniel Somers under his wing then and it was Malcolm King that had betrayed Daniel Somers now. Was he even justifiably sane now? Some people had their doubts but Daniel Somers had left EdW with a promise. It was a promise of a new era beginning. The era had begun on Wednesday night April 16th but it also had ended on that very same night. The reasons behind the sudden ending were and still are unknown to everyone. To the shock of everyone though it wasn't just New Era Wrestling which had ended. Brandon Kelley after being declared legally insane was institutionalized leaving his estranged wife Nichole as the full owner of the company. Perhaps as a final blow to her husband Nichole would then close the company down and sell it to an unknown bidder. One week later it would be revealed just who that bidder was. It was Daniel Somers with the backing of Davidson Enterprises that had purchased EdW and combined the company with his own nEw. Instead of reopening one of the two however Somers and his partners made plans for another idea. They began preparations to take the next step towards Evolution.
