Legends XLV Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From Elland Road ~ Leeds, England 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night August 7th, 2003 ReVelations has come and gone now and eVolution looks to get back into the swing of things with Legends coming live from Leeds, England. This week’s show is certainly going to be filled from top to bottom with non-stop action as we will see the debut of Shane Wethers in eVolution as well as the return to action of Asis who surprised everyone when he showed up at ReVelations on Sunday night and had his sights set on one man only. Tonight he returns to action with no doubt a lot of pent up frustration boiling inside of him and the rest of the fallout from ReVelations will continue as the night goes on. Also announced was that Scott Frost and Seara will finally tie the knot with a live wedding ceremony to take place during Legends this week with all of the fans and superstars of eVolution invited to take part. Machine takes another go at things inside of the Cell in between his rapping battles with the man he used to bodyguard, Thug roC. Now his opposition this time will be the mysterious newcomer to eVolution, Sean Sterling. There is very little that is known about Sean Sterling but there does seem to be some sort of connection between him and another member of the Cell Division. For any of that to matter however he will have to find a way of surviving the massive Machine in his debut match. Drake Nathanial Dukes is a man who made quite an impact fighting for the eVolved Title. As a former PRIDE and UFC Fighter however, Drake has decided to go back to his fighting roots and has now signed on to compete inside of The Cell. This week will be his first battles inside of the Cell and standing in front of him will be “Priest” Callaghan. Callaghan is another newcomer to eVolution and The Cell Division but he comes with some very high credentials. At least in the Priesthood anyway and we’ll find out for sure just how tough he can be in the fighting world this week against a very established opponent in Drake. This will be one fight that has the potential to put both of these men on the map in the Cell Division. After ReVelations, Trevor Norman became a two time Cell Champion with a huge and impressive victory over Jinx that rid eVolution of Jinx and Joshua Krowe both. Now though Trevor will face what will no doubt be the biggest test of his eVolution career as he must defend the title against the number one contender. That number one contender following ReVelations turned out to be the vicious Irishman Damon O’Halloran. O’Halloran and Ali finished off their feud in a big way at ReVelations and now Damon is the only man left standing as he moves on to the fight for the title here at Legends in a match that will certainly be one of the biggest in the Cell’s history. Right in his very own hometown, Shane Wethers will make his official in-ring debut for eVolution against Yoshii Tanaka. Shane has been a superstar everywhere he has gone and has even had quite a heated rivalry with Tyler Davidson during their days together in $oldout Wrestling but now Shane finds himself on a new venture to try and re-establish himself as a top performer after being forced to take several months off due to an injury. Yoshii Tanaka will be his first test in eVolution and where Yoshii goes there is always the possibility of The World’s Finest being not to far away and Shane will definitely have to keep one eye open in case they decide to have an influence. Asis made his return at ReVelations with a fury and it was Xander who felt it the most. Now it will be Asis’ turn to show to everyone that he is indeed at one hundred percent in a battle against the British Bomber who is also coming off of a huge victory at ReVelations against Malik Allah Roland. Bomber’s new singles career in eVolution got off to a good start but he will find himself hard pressed to continue it now with Asis standing across the ring from him. Asis has certainly got a whole lot of violence and frustration pent up inside of him that is just waiting for the chance to be released. Sean Valmonté made his debut at ReVelations as a rather unpleasant surprise for Siege during his match with Carl Cash that was more like a handicap match then anything else. Now Sean and Carl will both take part as The World’s Finest representatives in their on going battle against Siege who will be joined by Shocka. Shocka is of course no stranger to Carl Cash as the two have been in many different federations together in the past. Shocka and Siege have also crossed paths in the past and there is no telling whether or not the two of them will be able to get along very well in this match. The Syndicate by hook or by crook managed to get out of ReVelations with the Tag Team Titles still in their possession and now they head right back into the fire with a title defense against one of two teams. Luther Wisdom and Jason Relms both fought for fifteen minutes at ReVelations and it would be up to the fans to decide who won the match and also determine just which tag team which get the shot at The Syndicate’s Tag Team Titles on Legends. For Jason Relms and The Industry it is the chance that they have been waiting for since arriving in eVolution and for Luther Wisdom it’s his chance to finally emerge from the shadow of Malcolm King. In The Ring ~ Xander’s Celebration Featuring Xander and Asis The entire arena is plunged into darkness as the beginning of “Tomorrow” by SR-71 is heard. As the toggling guitar effects come in and out the arena is struck by the strobe light going with the beat. Then, as the main guitar cuts through the toggling lead a huge explosion of pyrotechnics goes off and Xander comes out from the back and out to the staging. Still battered, bruised, and bandaged from the hell he went through at ReVelations, the champ makes his way down to the ring, slowly. As Xander steps onto the apron, it takes him a second to actually get in between the ropes, and stand back up. The lights slowly come back on as Tomorrow fades out. As Phil Anselmo’s voice roars above the noise of the crowd Asis steps out onto the stage. He runs down towards the ring as you hear Xander's mic his the mat as he prepares himself for a fight. Asis slides in under the bottom rope, and trips up Xander before Xander can get an upper hand. Asis slides over the Universal champion and starts pummeling him. Xander pushes Asis off of him, and slides out of the ring. Asis quick to attack dives out onto Xander, crushing his body to the floor. Continuing the beating, Asis stands up, pulling Xander up as well. Asis lifts Xander in a bear hug, and slams him spine first into the steel post. Asis releases the hold as the limp body of the Universal champion crumples to the floor. Asis looks under the ring, and pulls out a table. He sets it up in between the guard railing at the end of the entrance area nearest the ring. Asis goes over to the ring steps, and tears them apart. He grabs the lower, bigger, half of the steps, and brings it over towards the table he just set up. Asis sets the steps up facing the table, and goes back over to Xander, who has managed to get himself back up to his feet. As Asis closed in, Xander attacked, hitting Asis with a pair of rights. Just as Xander was gaining some ground, Asis sends a knee into the champ’s rib cage, sending Xander back down to his knees. Asis grabs Xander by the hair and lifts him back up, dragging his almost limp body over towards the steps he had just set up. Asis lifts Xander up onto his shoulders into a fireman’s carry. He climbs the steps, and as the crowd pops for the spot, Asis sends Xander through the table with a variation of Xander's Chaos Reigns. Xander's body hits the table full on, shattering it into a million pieces, as goes Xander's carcass. As Asis looks on with pride, the trainers and medics, accompanied by the fearsome group of referees comes down to Xander's aid. A couple trainers, and the pack of wily ref’s coral Asis and get him to leave the area as a stretcher is brought down to haul off the fallen champion. Backstage ~ Life Is A Bet Featuring Sean Sterling The scene opened in the back just as the two swinging doors that led into the parking lot were flung open and the newcomer, Sean Sterling, stepped in. That soon-to-become familiar grin was glued on his face as he walked along the concrete floor. He was a few minutes late. Definitely not a good first impression, but that was beside the point. At least he had made it. To be honest? Sean didn't even know who he was up to wrestle tonight. But, that too was beside the point. He passed by a eVolution Official. The Official was rather... nervous looking. Even more so once Sean stopped him. Sean held a hand out in front of the eVolution Official. When Sean spoke...? He seemed quite calm. Serene, almost. "You," Sterling said, watching the Official for a moment. "Who's my opponent tonight?" "Um..." The Official hesitantly began to go through a few papers attached to a clipboard he was holding. After looking for a few seconds, he sputtered out, "Machine. Um... You're facing Machine!" "Meh," Sterling let out a long sigh as he dropped his hand from stopping the Official. "He's the big oaf ain't he? The one with the whole rapping shit or whatever?" "Um... t-that's him." The Official sputtered out once more, nodding his head. "Right... You a betting man?" Sean asked the official as he took a cigarette out of his pocket. He slid it into his mouth and lit it. It hung limply from his lips as he offered the Official one. The Official didn't take the cigarette offer, but nodded. "Y-Yes. I'm a betting man." "Who do you got your money on to win my match?" Sean asked him, tucking the box of cigarette's back into his pocket. "I... no offense... but... but I got my money on Machine." The Official managed to mutter. Sean grinned. There was something to that grin that simply wasn't right. It was enough to send chills up your back. "Is that so?" Sean said softly, laughing and taking a puff of the cigarette. Then exhaled the smoke, right into the Official's face. "Well then, watch the monitor. I'm sure you're going to be impressed. The Official nodded dumbly. Up and down went his head as Sean watched him for a moment. Only for a moment. And then he said, "Now what are you waiting for? Fuck off." "California Love" blares loudly throughout the jam-packed arena as Machine makes his way from backstage. He throws his arms up in the air, getting a great reaction from the fans. He paces around the cell one time before throwing his arms up again while his music fades away. The two men circle the each other in the cell as the weight advantage obviously goes to Machine. The big man stands over Sterling, along with a 150+ pound advantage over eVo’s newcomer. The two lock up and Machine shoves Sterling across the cell. Sterling gets up, showing no intimidation at all. Sterling goes for another lock up, and quickly goes behind and applies a waistlock. Machine struggles to reach behind him to grab his opponent. He instead just runs backwards and squashes Sterling into the cell. Sterling leans against the cell wall as Machine starts delivering hard rights and lefts to the body of Sterling. Sterling response with a hard kick to the knee of the big man, that causes Machine to stumble backwards. Sterling, with a rage of speed, connects with constant jabs and stiff kicks to the thighs of Machine. The strikes go by so fast that Machine can’t seem to respond and the crowd is starting to approve the newcomer. Sean Sterling finishes off with a dropkick to the knee of Machine. Machine drops to one knee and Sterling DDTs Machine to the ground, he holds on to Machine and applies a chokehold that Machine is struggling to release from. The referee is right there to see if Machine will quit, but no doing. Sean Sterling proceeds to wrap his legs around the waist of Machine, holding on for dear life. Machine uses his strength and power and stands to his feet, with Sterling still around his neck. Machine parades around the cell with Sterling around his neck and tries to ram him into the cell wall, but Sterling puts up his feet to block. Sterling pushes off and hits a tornado DDT on the big man. The crowd cheers loudly as Machine gets to his feet. Sterling goes at Machine again with his fury of rights and stiff kicks. The kick boxer techniques in Sterling show nicely until Machine knees Sterling in the gut as hard as he could. He grabs Sterling by his waistband and head before ramming him face first into the cell wall. Sterling drops to the ground hard and Machine drops to one knee to deliver hard mounted punches. Sterling can’t do anything but block the blows with his jaw as the crowd watches. Machine gets up and puts his arm in the air, signaling for his finisher. Sean Sterling gets up, and is doubled over. Machine goes for his Rabbit Punch, but Sterling moves out of the way and kicks Machine with his Birth Control Method! Machine crumbles and drops to the ground as the fans ohhh and ahhh. The replay is shown on the eVo-Tron while both Machine and Sterling rest a bit. Machine has to use the cell to pull himself up. He turns and is struck right in the mouth by a stiff kick from Sean Sterling. Machine stays on his feet and Sterling hits another one to the jaw. Machine teeter totters backwards and Sterling takes a step back before lunging forward with another kick. Machine catches the foot and clotheslines Sterling down to the ground. Machine then hits a double stomp on the chest of Sterling, taking all the wind out of his opponent. The ref is forced to apply his ten count. One… Two… Three… Four… Five… Six…Sterling gets to one knee Seven…Sterling is up! Machine is still holding his groin in pain while Sean Sterling reaches his feet. Machine goes for a hard right hook, and Sterling ducks it, having Machine strike nothing but the cell wall. Machine holds his hand in pain. He turns around and Sean Sterling does a back flip, kicking Machine right in his jaw! Machine drops to the mat with a hard thud as the ref makes a ten count. One… Two… Three… Four… Five… Six… Seven… Eight…Machine starts to roll over Nine… Machine gets to one knee Ten!!! Machine couldn’t get up in time! The ref signals for the bell as Sean Sterling accepts his first eVo victory. Machine seems a little irate, but holds his temper down as he leaves the cell. There in all its glory sat the deeds to the Cell Division, a division which by the command of one pen stroke would be Cameron Griffins and Cameron Griffins only. Evolution had become little more than a stage for the Cell, now it was time to firmly make it a distinct entity. "Black or blue?" Cameron said looking up from the papers for a second. Drake had been out of action for the past few weeks following a brutal table match against Hedges and Scott Evans for the eVolved Title but he put his time off to very good use as he got himself prepared for this chance. The chance to return to the style of action that he had made a name for himself in, the world of fighting. Drake made his way down towards the Cell and climbed to the inside of it for his first venture into this world of eVolution and against another newcomer to this atmosphere. A flashing image of a shamrock appears on the eVotron as the Pogues' "Irish Rover" blares out over the PA. Priest Callaghan appears at the top of the ramp along with his manager, Father Jack Keane and walks down slowly, acknowledging the crowd's reaction with a nod of his head. Priest made his way through the door of the Cell and stood near the entrance as he stared across at Drake. Callaghan drove another hard right into the jaw of Drake and again wrapped his arms around Drake’s waist before he flipped him over with a belly to belly suplex. Callaghan got back to his feet quickly and drove a series of kicks right into the side of Drake as he continued to try and pull himself up with the assistance of the Cell wall but he was barely able to stand long enough to mount any offense thanks to the damage done to his ankle. Callaghan drove a hard boot right down towards the ankle of Drake and he fell right back down to the mat again while clutching his ankle in pain but he tried still to get back onto his feet and even caught a surprised Callaghan with a hard right hand straight to the midsection. Drake stood right back up and caught Callaghan with a hard uppercut to the jaw that knocked him down to the mat before Drake fell right beside him still unable to stand firmly on his feet.
