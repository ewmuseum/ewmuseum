House Show II, 8 June 2003

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
House Show Results Singles Match British Bomber vs. Chad "C-Dub" Winters In a return to singles matches we saw Chad Winters, or, C-Dub face off against the British Bomber. In what was an almost embarrassing display, Winters came out and try to show Bomber that it's all about the old school. Bomber laid into Winters for a good few minutes, hitting a variety of kicks, and submission holds. Winters gained the upper hand after a low blow took the wind out of the British superstar. With a flurry of suplexes, and a top rope splash Winters pinned Bomber for the 2 count. The match turned around after a reversal of Winters' finisher, which saw Bomber float over into a single leg takedown and locking on the Englishlock for the submission victory. Winner via submission: British Bomber Tag Team Match The BodySnatchers vs. The Industry A heated spot fest of a match saw the pendulum swing constantly between these two superb teams. The Industry keeping Dolo tied up in their corner for the majority of the match. 1001 Holds got the hot tag, and took down both BodySnatchers by himself. As 1001 readied himself to take Quinn down with a spear, he got blindsided by Relms as Realms hit a modified version of his Whole FN Show. As the ref turned, Quinn made the cover, and Relms took down Bentswinger before he could interrupt. Winner via pinfall: The Industry Cell Championship match Drake Nathaniel Dukes vs. Damon O'Halloran In the first of a double main even tonight we see the Cell championship on the line as the champ, Damon O'Halloran faces with Drake Dukes. A flurry of offensive moves started the contest as Dukes took it to the cell champion. After a beautifully places Samoan drop, Dukes took too much time getting back on the attack, as it left a door open for O'Halloran to attack, and attack he did. With some vicious blows landed by the champ, out of nowhere he hit The Struggle Within, and picked up a victory over Dukes in this contest. Winner via knockout: Damon O'Halloran Singles Match Shane Wethers vs. Erick Mathews In an oddly quick match, quickest of the night, Shane Wethers met another newcomer to eVolution by the name of Erick Mathews. A few fans may recognize Mathews as known as the Defend of Foo from the old HWL federation of years passed. Mathews started with a good attempt at a takedown, but was unsuccessful at doing much to the man known as Wethers. After getting in a few good body shots, Mathews went for a Northern Lights Suplex, but Wethers blocked, and reversed it hitting The Avalanche on the unsuspecting Mathews. Wethers covered, and got a victory is this great showing for this young superstar. Winner via pinfall: Shane Wethers Tag Team Title Match The Syndicate vs. St. Violence In the main event of the night, we saw the tag team titles defended. This match was a mess of interference from St. Violence, as well as backhanded tactics. All of their attempts to steal away the gold were unsuccessful as the bout ended in a no contest at the 5:34 mark after Eric Knight hit Dreamer with a stiff chair shot that left the Big Daddy downed and busted open.
