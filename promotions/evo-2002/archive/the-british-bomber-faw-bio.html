The British Bomber Biography Real Name Steven Smith Hometown Birmingham, England Birthday 13th November 1974 Height 6'0' Weight 230 Biography (Note - This is a bio that only dates back to January 2001) Bomber gets his big break into the wrestling world as The Birmingham Bomber, the name only lasted days though and soon changed to The British Bomber. Not long after joining UCW he got a Intercontinental Title shot and defeated Justin Williams for his first title. He granted Williams a re-match at the UCW PPV entitled Rumble but was defeated but then went on to win the 30-man Rumble to earn a title shot at the next PPV. Bomber's win-loss record was impressive, he was 7-2 up, with his only defeats coming at the hands of Mike Jarrett on his debut and Justin Williams at the PPV. The February PPV came round slow but the PPV was huge, Bomber won his first World Title but lost it the following night due to the owner saying the win was consired 'complete and utter rubbish' saying the referee had cheated. He announced the title would be on the line at the show with him as the referee this time. Bomber went into the match knowing he would lose and did, but that didnt stop him, Bomber issued the challenge days later saying if he didnt win the title at the next show then he would quit, and sure enough Bomber won. But the owner came out saying if Bomber loses the gold at the PPV he will be fired...... He went the the PPV with all the confidence in the world but it wasnt enough, Bomber lost and was un-employed but only for a week, he later went to XCW. But that was also short lived due to Primetime Championship Wrestling taking over. He left in search for work again and stumbled across a small federation called FAW. He applied for a job and got it, he started in April 2001 and this was the start of a fairytail........ On his debut he won the European Title but went onto lose it at April Avalanche to the great JLC. Bomber went on the win many titles in FAW, he captured Tag Team gold 2 times with Spike, with the tag team name the Power Of Two. Which argueably went on to be the best tag team ever in FAW history. Bomber and Spike split, with both going in search of singles gold with Bomber coming off better, he went on to be 2 time European champion, won the Tag Titles again with Chris Thunder, 3 time International Champion, won the XWW Intercontinental Title on FAW TV during the Invasion of which Bomber defected to XWW. This is where he re-kindled his relationship with childhood sweetheart Kathryn Velmont-Thomas. But that turned sour when she tricked him, and formed a alliance with arch-enemy Chris Thunder. The whole situation turned sour though at the FAW/XWW PPV entitled Entrapment, the final score was 6-5 to FAW, but it would have been 6-6 if it wasnt for Bomber screwing XWW over and turning his back on XWW. Bomber returned to FAW along with KVT, which they was back together but in the stormy relationship in which November KVT turned her back on Bomber yet again.... Although she came back again, much nearer to Evil Intentions, along with leading the xCw invasion, a match was announced that included 9 men for the FAW World championship. Bomber went into Evil Intentions in that match along with xCw collegues Jeff Harris, Stunning Stan and also facing Chris Thunder, Spawn, Pyro, Skipster and Spider. The match turned out to be huge, broken bodies, The British Bomber won the World title thanks to help from Tom The Bomb but at a cost, half of xCw! January wasnt a good month for Bomber, losing the World title, xCw died, losing his re-match for the World title as well as KVT faking her death then re-appearing to announce she was married to Jeff Harris! Things picked up, Bomber turned face, Tom The Bomb, Tony Vigilante and KVT formed a alliance to take out Bomber but Bomber put out a challenge, he told Tony V to put his World title number 1 contendership on the line which he had won at When Worlds Collide which Tony V accepted and the match was made for Vendetta. Vendetta came around quick and Bomber and Tony V went at it, the match was great in which Bomber won and also broke Tony Vigilante's ankle. Bomber was going to Extreme Beating 2 to face Spawn! Or was he? Weeks later Bomber was forced to defend his number 1 contendership against The Omega Fro which he lost, forcing him out the Extreme Beating 2 main event, Bomber went on to challenge Tom The Bomb at Extreme Beating 2. At Extreme Beating 2 he was fired and left the company, but on May 3rd The British Bomber made his return to FAW with a match against Joe La Fontaine.....Old School lives on! One month after returning Bomber once again turned on the fans and aligned himself with Tony Vigilante to take out Old School, and ultimatly costing 'Old School' to die and help Tony vigilante retain his title. But not so long after New School aplit up, Bomber turned face and got the fans back on his side, then at Diabolical Day the final legacy of the book was complete. Bomber defeated Chris Thunder to win the XWW World Title and progress to the final of the annual Diabolical Day knockout tournament to face Xander whom he defeated and became two time FAW World Champion. But the PPV after winning the title he lost to JLC, then reported arguements with the former owners regarding a contract made Bomber leave FAW. He then signed with the DWO until January 2003 but on September 29th 2002 Bomber, FAW and the DWO came to a agreement where Bomber would wrestle for one night only...... only to be screwed over by former fellow Dark Generation member Chris Thunder but winning via DQ. Other Feds FAW, DWO, xCw, XWW Other Fed Awards FAW: World Champ x2, International Champ x3, European Champ x2, Tag Champ x4. xCw Hardcore Champ, XWW Intercontinenal Champ, FAW: Diabolical Day Winner, Hall Of Famer, Holds Record For Most Titles Held.
