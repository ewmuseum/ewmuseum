'The Outkast' Eric Knight Biography Real Name Eric Knight Nicknames The Outkast Hometown New York City, New York Birthday August 29, 1980 Height 6' Weight 205 Fed Awards First Cell Fighter In eVolution Biography Entrance: ”Freaking Out” by Adema begins playing over the sound system and Eric Knight wearing his blue and black hooded robe walks out onto the ramp. He looks around at the crowd with his silver hair falling over his face and the hood covering most of his features before he slowly walks down the ramp. Knight climbs the ring steps while lowering his hood and taking his robe off. Appearance: Has platinum silver hair just long enough to come down and cover his eyes. Wears knee length black trunks with the word Blackout going across the front of them. Wears a black and dark blue robe out to his fights. Eric has a small scar just under his left eye and another one across his right shoulder. Both of his eyes are discolored from something in the past as they both have an icy blue gleam to them enough to frighten small children. Biography: Eric grew up in New York City, New York with his parents and two older brothers. The family had little money and could barely afford to get by with the basic necessities of life. As a result growing up tended to be quite hard for Eric. Things never seemed to go his way at home and the trend continued when he got older and began high school. Constantly in fights he was kicked out at the age of sixteen before falling in with the wrong crowd. He began dealing drugs until one day when a deal went wrong. Eric was shot and left for dead by a prospective customer who decided not to pay. Medics arrived in time to save him but it left him scarred for life. He turned his attention back to the one thing he loved in life before, fighting. Eric at the age of nineteen joined the Underground Fight League. For two years he fought with some of the best and deadliest men alive before calling it quits and deciding to try and go legit. That brought Eric to eVolution where he sought to become the toughest fighter inside the Cell. That dream was cut short however by the suspension of the match and the all out assault by Sovereign which followed it. A month later Eric has returned with a new goal in mind which only he knows and the promise of making everyone in his path feel his pain.
