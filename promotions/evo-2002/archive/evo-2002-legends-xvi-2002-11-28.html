Legends XVI Transcript Live on OSE! From The Skydome ~ Toronto, Ontario, Canada 7:59PM EST Thursday Night November 28th, 2002 Rise of the Legends came to an explosive end with some stars crowned champions and others leaving with a disappointed experience. Now all the superstars must put Rise of the Legends behind them and prepare for Legends XVI coming to you live from the Skydome in Toronto! The Continental Title will be on the line as we know already but more repercussions from Rise of the Legends will certainly be felt throughout the course of the night. Catch it all on a special Thanksgiving Thursday edition of eVolution Legends! The God of Sleep and Dreams, Morpheus Omega made an impact on Sean Mason at Rise of the Legends and now for his first taste of single action in eVolution he faces another newcomer in Ryan Flesco. A man who was at one time part of the original JEW and GsE now looks to make a fresh start in eVolution at the expense of one Omega superstar who has some questionable motives to say the least. Judging by their appearance together on Sunday night, Thug roC and Morpheus look to be anything but friends so why is Morpheus helping him? The answers can't be to far behind if the past is any indication. At Rise of the Legends it was The Omen Brotherhood who just about handed the Tag Team Titles over to The Unlimited. Now the team they screwed, New-TV will be trying to extract some revenge and walk out with maybe a little bit of blood on their hands. So long as it's the blood of The Brotherhood they could really care less. The Omen Brotherhood for their part have made quite a few enemies and seemingly sparked what is certain to be a very violent stable war in the coming months. Not only do they have New-TV after them but they have The Unlimited breathing down their necks as well. The wildcards of course would be The Notorious and the apparently back from the dead Xian Echo. At Rise of the Legends, Jameson received a video tape and later in the evening found out he would have to defend his title against the man who was apparently on that tape. The question remains, who was on it? No one but Jameson, Somers, and obviously the man knows who it is and none of them are giving up a damn thing this time around. Is this just another ploy by Somers to try and get rid of Jameson or is this something that goes a lot deeper? Jameson has a mysterious past for most people and one can only wonder if this has anything to do with it. After what happened at Rise of the Legends how on earth can anyone possibly expect Sovereign and the returning Impact to team together? Someone should ask Bobby Stormbringer because that is exactly what he expects to happen as he's booked the two of them to team up and go against L2J and Scott Frost of The Notorious! Without a doubt it's going to be a blockbuster main event but the fireworks are sure to far exceed the battle between two teams as the two monsters of eVolution are at one another's throats with L2J and Scott Frost now caught in the middle. Parking Lot ~ Waiting Featuring Scott Frost and Seara Standing in the parking lot, Frost waits too see if Seara will turn up tonight before his match begins. It has been hazy as to whether she will be there, and even Frost is unaware, but unlike everyone else, he is standing in the freezing cold, waiting to see if the female puts in an appearance. A car door can be heard slamming in the distance and Frost jumps around in a quick shocked motion to see whom it is, but all he manages to see is the bottom of a sports bag as it thrown on the shoulder of the figure who then disappears through the back arena doors out of sight. Waiting for anyone special? Seara’s voice comes from behind Scott Frost, startling the male, but he turns around with a smile on his face, replacing the look of anger and boredom he had gotten standing out in the cold unforgiving wind. Seara – Hey you look freezing, best get you warmed up, you’ve got a fight tonight. Frost – I didn’t think you’d show up, why did you come? Seara – Right, you didn’t think I was going to leave a fellow team-mate alone tonight, come on, this is a big fight, and I wanted to see how you were after the PPV. Frost puts his arm around Seara’s shoulder and turns her to walk indoors out of the extreme cold night air. VIP Office ~ The Ever Caring Cameron Featuring Cameron Griffin The camera fades in as we see Cameron Griffin walking backstage. He looks a happy figure as he whistles aloud and smiles for all to see, but what is more noticeable is in his hand he holds a white carrier bag of which at this point the contents are unknown. Continuing through the maze of corridors, finally he arrives in a room marked Dan Sommers. Grinning to himself he knocks once, before then proceeding not caring to wait for any sort of response. As the camera follows Cameron in we see that Daniel is not yet in his office and infact the room is empty of any inhabitants. Moving towards what will be Daniel Sommers desk for the evening he reaches inside the carrier bag and pauses. Out of the carrier bag he pulls out what is a neatly folded up is a referees shirt, seemingly the one from the PPV he wore this Sunday. Placing it down he looks up and ponders for a second. Next step it then seems, is to leave a brief note. Taking what could well be an important document from a folder which lays open he smiles, before then taking a pen from inside his shirt pocket and proceeding to write a brief note... "Daniel ol'buddy, It’s been washed and ironed and now you can have it back. Hope you enjoyed my performance on Sunday, perhaps maybe I might don the black and white colors again some day but it’s just not for me full time." With that Cameron pauses again, sitting in the open folder where he found the paper he is writing upon sit two passes for the VIP Sky Box suite for tonight’s show. A broad grin emerges across Cameron’s face once more as he takes one of the passes and places it on the pocket of his shirt using the clip attached. "PS: Have taken a pass which you so kindly left for me, but don't worry I left one for you also, so there’s always a space next to me if you feel the need for a break from it all. Otherwise I'll just sit and enjoy the show. Your good'ol pal Cameron" Dropping the pen down on the desk, he carefully positions the note on top of the neatly pressed shirt he leaves behind before then looking down at his newly acquired pass again. "I always knew he appreciated what I did for him in OSW" he mutters to himself as he adjusts the pass slightly. Just then his cell-phone begins to ring and after briefly checking the callers ID he opts to retreat to a more private area. Cameron leaves the office and thus the scene fades to black. As Frost walks down to the ring apron, the crowd is cheering on for him as he comes out with Kendo stick raised high into the air. He climbs into the ring, and then turns around looking around the ring and the arena before the match begins. Justin Fritz: L2J and Frost are both members of The Notorious and came within inches of having to face one another next month at Nightmare Before Christmas. Jack Tyler: Yeah but that was before Frost failed to get the job done and Shocka won the Road to Glory. Justin Fritz: Shocka did win the match but as stated earlier he had a lot more help than you would like to admit and you can be sure he hasn’t seen the last of Frost just yet. Jack Tyler: I’m sure Shocka is terrified. He’ll just beat him yet again. Libby Bowman: And their opponents, introducing first from Chicago, Illinois and weighing in at 280lbs……..Sovereign! The lights in the arena then dim to a near black as the fans all hush hoping to hear the music of whomever is about to come to the ring. Then the sound of children’s laughter with singing starts to float into the PA system as the fans then recognize that it’s not someone to cheer, it’s time to boo. The booing begins just as Shoots and Ladders by Korn begins to play, a light show erupts around the arena as the guitar rift kicks in and then from out side the entrance way walks Sovereign. The fans react with massive boos and jeers as Sovereign walks down to the ring, eyes fixed on his target. Sovereign hops up onto the apron as a burst of Pyro from the corners of the ring. Sovereign enters the ring as the lights in the arena come back up, smoke from the Pyro floats through the air as Sovereign is ready for what will come next. Justin Fritz: The monster himself is in the ring and just waiting now it seems for the chance to tear someone else apart. Jack Tyler: We’ve already seen what he’s done to Stormbringer here tonight. Why not L2J or Frost too? Justin Fritz: Why not Impact? Jack Tyler: Because I said no Impact damn it! Stop encouraging it. Justin Fritz: You know as well as I do that Sovereign has been searching for Impact all night. Jack Tyler: He just wanted to go over their plan for the match. Justin Fritz: Yeah I’m sure that’s it. Libby Bowman: And his partner from Hollywood, California and weighing in at 324lbs……”The Hollywood Monster” Impact! The lights turn off... blue and white smoke start to hiss from the sides of the ramp… a guitar introduction blares through the arena… ‘Conceived in a hell beyond, your depth of perception, chaotic case of conquering domination, psychopath snaps, fired chains of imprisonment a bludgeoning force that's undermining the government!‘ Edgecrusher by Fear Factory blasts throughout the as light blue and white lights start to flicker and move around the fans.... Impact is standing in the middle of the ramp into the immense boos and a few cheers from the fans. He walks down arrogantly, looking at people in disgust before setting his sights on the ring and the man already waiting for him inside of it. Sovereign stands in the ring and as Impact is on the outside the two men stare back and forth with their eyes locked. Justin Fritz: This isn’t going to be pretty Jack. Jack Tyler: Shut up. They’ll be fine. Justin Fritz: You’re dreaming. Sovereign and Impact continue staring at one another and when it becomes obvious Impact isn’t entering the ring Sovereign begins making his way out after him. Before Sovereign can make it through the ropes however both Frost and L2J leap into action attacking him from behind with forearm shots and kicks into the midsection causing Sovereign to grab onto his stomach in pain from his two broken ribs. The two of them grab onto Sovereign and whip him into the ropes and as he comes back at them they both leap and nail him with a double dropkick flooring Sovereign. L2J quickly gets back to his feet and he runs into the ropes before coming down across the midsection of Sovereign again with a senton splash causing another wave of pain to go through the ribs of Sovereign as L2J makes the cover. Almost immediately though Sovereign powers out of it with a big kickout and Impact finally comes into the ring and nails Frost from behind with a clothesline. Jack Tyler: You see Justin things are working out fine as it is. Justin Fritz: I don’t expect that to last one bit. Jack Tyler: Stop trying to jinx things damn it. Justin Fritz: I’m just stating the realistic facts Jack. L2J gets back to his feet and goes right after Impact but the much more powerful Impact simply scoops L2J into the air with a gorilla press before tossing him right at Frost as the two collide and roll out of the ring. Impact stands on his feet in the ring looking out at Frost and L2J when he turns around and is greeted suddenly with a clothesline from Sovereign. The shot doesn’t knock him down but wobbles him a bit as Sovereign then takes a few steps back and goes for a spear but Impact side steps it and Sovereign lands on the mat. He gets quickly back to his feet and turns around being met with a right hand from Impact but Sovereign doesn’t back off and fires away with one of his own. The two of them battle back and forth with punches until Impact suddenly drives a knee right into the gut of Sovereign. Impact sets Sovereign up in a pumphandle and then lifts him into the air for a powerslam but Sovereign slides out of his grip and comes up behind him before catching Impact in the Last Breath as he turns around. Jack Tyler: No damn it no! Justin Fritz: I told you it was going to happen Jack. There was no way around it! Jack Tyler: This has got to stop! As Sovereign locks the move in L2J and Frost both make their moves again sliding back into the ring this time with steel chairs in their hands. They each line up behind Sovereign and slam the two chairs right across his back causing him to release his hold on Impact. Frost then quickly turns and with the chair still in hand he smashes it over the head of Impact dazing the big man until L2J comes off the ropes and leaps into the air and swings another chair shot cracking on the head of Impact and sending him down to the mat. They turn towards Sovereign again and wait as he slowly begins pulling his way back to his feet but before they can do anything Frost is grabbed from the outside by Asis who yanks him down to the mat and out of the ring. Asis looking for some revenge for earlier nails Frost with a hard right hand but his attack is cut short by L2J who is quick to come and help his friend out. Justin Fritz: This one has already broken down into pure hell! Both Sovereign and Impact are hurting in the ring from those chair shots and now Asis is out here attacking Frost and L2J! Jack Tyler: That’s because Frost attacked Asis earlier in the day. Justin Fritz: He’s trying to get some payback now I guess but he didn’t count on L2J getting in the way. Jack Tyler: Or maybe he did. As L2J begins attacking Asis along with Frost his sudden attack is also cut off as a vicious chair to the back drops him and Shocka stands over the current Universal Champion. Shocka begins stomping away on L2J before raising the chair above his head and slamming it down across his back. Frost looks to come help L2J but he’s to busy with Asis as the two of them battle back and forth against the guard rail. In the ring Sovereign is back on his feet and looking down now on the form of Impact still down from the two chair shots. Sovereign picks Impact up and slowly sets him up before driving him back down into the mat with Sovereignity to make sure he’s down. Sovereign then rolls out of the ring and heads to the announce position where he grabs two more steel chairs and rolls back into the ring with them. Justin Fritz: This isn’t looking good at all now! Jack Tyler: Sovereign’s going to try and put Impact out again! Justin Fritz: It certainly looks like it now don’t it? Jack Tyler: He can’t do that again can he? Justin Fritz: Unless someone stops him he can. Sovereign tosses one of the two chairs onto the mat and he positions it right under the head of Impact who is lying on his stomach face down on the chair. Sovereign then takes a few steps back with one hand placed over his ribs and has the chair raised above his head with the other. He grabs the chair now with both hands and lifts it into the air for a one man conchairto but before he brings the chair down Sovereign stops noticing something just outside the ring. Sovereign looks to the side just in time to see a familiar looking woman hopping the guard rail and climbing onto the apron where she stands staring back at the obviously surprised Sovereign. Justin Fritz: That’s Leya! Jack Tyler: What the hell is she doing here?! Justin Fritz: I don’t know but that is Sovereign’s wife! She has to be insane to be here and that close to him right now. Leya stands right on the apron staring in at Sovereign who lowers the chair and slowly begins walking over towards his wife. Before Sovereign can get to close to her however, Impact is somehow back on his feet and with the second chair in his hands. He winds it back and delivers a deafening shot right into the back of Sovereign’s head flooring him as Leya then climbs into the ring. She stands over Sovereign, yelling at him calling him a bastard among other colorful names before actually kicking him right in the side. Impact looks down at Sovereign before looking over at Leya and throwing the chair already dented by Sovereign’s head down to the mat. He reaches a massive hand down and clasps it around the throat of Sovereign before pulling him back to his feet. Impact stands there with his hand wrapped around the throat of Sovereign before he lifts him into the air and brings him slamming down on the steel chair with a choke slam. Impact gets back to his feet and looks down at Sovereign with a smile as Leya stands alongside him. Justin Fritz: Impact has left Sovereign lying again! Jack Tyler: What the hell is he doing with Leya?! Are the two of them together now? Justin Fritz: I have no idea but it certainly made an impact on Sovereign! Jack Tyler: This isn’t good! Justin Fritz: Watch out! Just as Justin yells out to Jack the two of them quickly scatter as L2J and Shocka both come fighting into the scene and Shocka is backdropped by L2J right onto the announce table. L2J turns and quickly hops onto the table trying to pick Shocka up with him but Shocka counters it and nails L2J with a low blow. He gets back to a standing position with L2J and delivers the #1 Stunna slamming L2J right through the announcer’s table. Both of them remain lying in a heap in the wreckage of the table as the show fades to black.
