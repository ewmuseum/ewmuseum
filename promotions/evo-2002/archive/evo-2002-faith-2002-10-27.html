Faith Transcript Live on Pay Per View! From Louisiana Superdome ~ New Orleans, Louisiana 7:59PM EST Sunday Night October 27th, 2002 Do you have Faith? Many of the superstars in eVolution are being asked that question as the time until Faith grows closer with each passing second. The Universal Title, and the Continental Title will both be on the line as a tournament for the Rising Star Title also begins. All the past issues for the past month will come to an explosive conclusion at Faith. So much action will be scheduled to take place at Faith that will leave most of eVolution spinning from the repercussions. The only way to survive this explosive outcome is by having Faith! Jinosyde and Steele were teammates just one week ago with their sights set on the eVolution Tag Team Titles. The team did not make it through but Steele did at the expense of his partner, Jinosyde. Now the two men step into the ring once again with each other but the stakes are higher in this one on one battle. The first match in a Rising Star Title tournament pits the two Canadian superstars against one another to advance to round two. Will Jinosyde get some revenge against his partner or will Jason steal another one? Immediately upon his arrival Strictly Prohibited had for some reason picked out Jinx as his first target. Now both of these men will step into the ring for a hardcore match where Jinx has done nothing but excel in the past. The mystery behind just who Prohibited really is continues to deepen as his own comments indicate a past between him and several stars on the current eVolution roster including Jinx’s own ally The HeartBreak Chick. Jinx just as many others is eager to answer the question behind Prohibited’s identity while he for some reason tries to keep it secret. Originally a six pack match but with the releases of Quasar and Andrew Martin this one has become a four corners match. Sean Mason feels robbed of not only the Rising Star Title but the High Impact Title as well as he tries and makes himself noticed in this match. Standing in his way is Jay Stylez Junior who pulled off a huge upset win over Sgt. Jack O’Hara. Ever since Digital Destruction the battle between the former friends, Seara and Asis has intensified. Asis has gone on a destructive path of his own terrorizing Seara in any way possible. Seara has fought back as much as possible including picking up two stunning victories over Asis. After this past week and Asis’s vicious assault of Seara’s brother the personal issues have only intensified between these two and their final showdown is just about upon us. Eric Badger, the newest personal hitman hired by Daniel Somers in an effort to eliminate Jason Jameson. The efforts so far have been a downright failure for Badger as no matter what he’s done Jameson has been able to counteract and defeat him in tag team action. Now the two men will finally get a one on one battle with this perhaps being Badger’s last chance at gaining any form of success before losing his high standing in Somers’s view. For Jameson, eliminating the thorn that is Eric Badger will bring him just one step closer to Somers himself. Tommy Michaels has a secret. It’s one that he has gone to all sorts of lengths to keep from revealing to Somers. He even made the deal that if Somers can find one man to defeat Michaels then Tommy would reveal his secret. After lining up Deacon, Molten, Jinx, and even Big Daddy Dreamer no one has been able to finish the job on Michaels. Now this week Somers up the odds even more and signs new recruit Sgt. Jack O’Hara to take on Tommy Michaels and finish the job the others couldn’t. For the massive O’Hara this is a chance to make an immediate impact in eVolution and to even get in the good graces of Daniel Somers. Shocka and Shodan have been feuding for well over a month now and finally, things are set to come to an end. Twice now Shodan has managed to pick up a victory over Shocka but neither time has the Continental Title been on the line. The one title shot he has gotten he came up short due to the antics of Thug roC and now is his one and perhaps last chance to capture the championship. Shocka tries to hold onto the belt for a bit longer before setting his sights a bit higher towards the Universal gold. The greatest team in the history of EdW explodes right in front of everyone’s eyes. HBC was the one to strike first delivering the kick heard round the world when she turned her back on Dreamer and joined The Unlimited. For weeks Dreamer has tried getting his revenge but she has managed to avoid it using her feminine wiles and the help of The Unlimited. The wild card of course thrown into the mix is the mysteries surrounding the woman we have come to know as Magdalena the past few weeks. According to HBC, Magdalena also has a secret which she has figured out but Dreamer has no clue what it is. Will she have an effect on the outcome of this match or is she just what she says she is? A loyal supporter of Big Daddy Dreamer. Scott Frost has betrayed The Unlimited and joined the side of L2J. Why he did this no one knows but Xander has every intention of beating the answer out of him. Xander has promised nothing but pain will be in the future of Frost and with this match now a dueling canes match there is no doubt in anyone’s mind that it could very well happen. Frost and Xander will battle no longer as allies but as two very bitter enemies with each one having something to prove to the other one. Even with the knowledge of this match happening L2J and William have formed somewhat of a bond over the past few weeks. Despite all the past history these two men have managed to mold themselves into a working team and now they must throw it all out the window with the intentions of then beating the hell out of each other with the Universal Title hanging in the balance. L2J feels it’s his destiny to finally capture the one title which has eluded him his entire career. William will do anything in his power to keep the title which he has come to covet but can he do it while also keeping the personality of Sovereign at bay? Or will the monster break free of his prison and once again wreak havoc on eVolution? Tune in to Faith on October 27th to find out the answer! Ringside ~ Got To Have Faith Featuring Justin Fritz and Jack Tyler The show opens with a shot inside of the massive Louisiana Superdome with “Faith” by Limp Bizkit blasting over the sound system. Tens of thousands of fans are on their feet cheering for the start of the show as the usual blue and red pyro display blasts off from the ramp into the air. On the eVotron footage of the past events and superstars of eVolution plays over and over again as the music continues. The camera pans around the arena before taking a long shot of the V-shaped ramp eVolution uses for its show and coming to a stop finally at the ringside area where you can see Justin Fritz and Jack Tyler seated in their positions and ready to call all the action as the music and pyro slowly die down.. “Welcome fans to Faith!” Justin begins screaming into the headset. “Our fourth pay per view event and it’s without a doubt the biggest one of them all here tonight.” ”The only thing that will make this show worthwhile for me is if Sovereign finally comes back and beats the hell out of L2J tonight,” Jack put in. “If that doesn’t happen then this show sucks for me.” “We got a long way to go before that can possibly happen,” Justin responded. “This show is personal from the top to the bottom and because of that Daniel Somers and Thug roC have made a new ruling about this show.” ”Huh?” Jack replied feeling left out. “What are you talking about? I haven’t heard anything about a new announcement.” “The two of them have instructed all the referee’s and officials of eVolution tonight to basically throw the rule book out the window,” Justin answered. “They want this one to go as far as it possibly can and for all the personal issues to come to one final end here tonight!” “Really?” Jack responded. “This show might not be too bad after all then. Let’s see some violence!” “It just adds even more to an already explosive card,” Justin agreed. ”Why don’t we get to it and stop all the talking?” Jack recommended. “Sounds good to me,” Justin answered.
