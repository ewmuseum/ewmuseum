Welcome everyone to the first entry in what I hope to be a very interesting column. I'm calling it the State of Blaze. If you can’t figure out why, don’t bother. Your few brain cells are too precious. Anyways, for the first topic I decided it should be something different. A view on something popular, but yet a view abstract from the rest. Marijuana. First I would like to state that in no way, shape or form is this column condoning the use of the magic herb. I, myself, do not use or have ever used the mentioned. Now without any further delay... The other day I was sitting amongst some friends and overheard one of them ask the other if they had "any". I turned and asked what they were talking about and they answered. After hearing their response another friend said something along the lines of, "Ya' know that shit kills your brain cells, right?" After this, I sat and realized that that seems to be a popular comment about marijuana. Of all the other harm it does there is one thing people mention more than any other. Brain cells. I sat and listened to them try and make a defensive stance on the said subject. It was sad. So I decided to help out. Here is my theory. I'll call it, Survival, after the old Survival of the Fittest idea. You see, when I think of brain cells and dope, it reminds me of...say...i dunno...we'll say buffalo. Yes, you read that right. Brain cells and weed are like herds of buffalo. When traveling in a herd the slowest of all buffalo are the herd's weakness. Because of how slow and vulnerable the slow one is, the rest of the herd falls back and slows the pace to protect them. By doing this they are risking the whole herd. However when a predator comes, the slow one is going to be the first to go. In return, the buffalo lose a member, but they are now able to hasten themselves. Now, over time, the herd rids themselves of the weak. This eventually works its way down to a slight few. The strongest of the entire herd. By losing the weak and slow, the herd is able to assure their strength, safety, and cut on travel time. Now if you apply this theory to brain cells it is much in the same. Yes, it is true that smoking pot WILL kill brain cells, amongst other things...like impotence...but let’s stick to one subject for now. According to the Survival theory, this might not be such a bad thing. The first brain cell to go will be the slowest and weakest of all cells. Over time you will have few cells left, but they will be the fastest and wiser of them all. This would theoretically strengthen your brain power. Allowing you to think faster and more clearly. Just as with any other living thing on this planet, the theory of Survival plays role in keeping the weak and fragile where they belong. Out of the way. Now with this bit of theory and creativity, use the few brain cells you may or may not have left to think about the other effects of smoking weed. And the next time you are criticized for your smoking, remember, you are not merely doing some dumb act...you are strengthening your mind. .::d.a.r.k::. (Donovan Avarus-Rey Kilner)
