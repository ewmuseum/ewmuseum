"Punisher" Platinum Cassidy Real Name: Platinum Cassidy Also Known As: PPC, Punisher Date of Birth: March 24th, 1976 Hometown: Chaos Creek, Connecticut Height: 6'4 Weight: 256lbs Alignment: Neutral Theme Music: "Know Your Enemy" by Rage Against The Machine Manager: None Wrestling Style: Technical Innovator Favorite Moves: Platinum-Plex - (Inverted brainbuster) Aftershot (Superkick) Kiss of Death (Unprettier) Signature Move: Set-Up: Swinging neck and arm vertebreaker- Fundamentally, a neckbreaker but with extended force on the lower body, so the spine, or vertebrae takes the brunt of the force, with neck punishement the by-product. Finisher: The Victimizer-In order for the Victimizer to be achieved, Cassidy's opponent must be bent over. The best way to achieve this would be for Cassidy to kick his opponent in the lower abdomen or gut thus doubling them over. With opponent over, whilst being in front of him, Cassidy then proceeds to lock the opponents head between his thighs. He then vines his opponents arms in a double underhook position (similar to Triple H's pedigree) and jumps into the air with opponent still hooked. In mid-air, Platinum uses his right foot as a pivot and swings his opponent 90 degrees to the right in flight. Whilst elevated, Cassidy puts his legs out in front of him, either side of his opponent's body and forces his head hard into the mat. When manoeuvre finishes, Cassidy should be in a sitting position on the mat with opponent prone in front of him, face down. This move is otherwise known as the sitdown double-underhook faceslam. Other Info Current Teams: None Past Teams: None eVo Title History: None eVo Award History: None Other Titles: None Other Awards: None Biography Wrestler Entrance: "Know your Enemy" brings the crowd to their feet, with PPC entering approximately 40 seconds into the track, allowing the crowd to antipate his arrival. He cockily swaggers to the ring, but focussed. He stands on the apron of the ring as fireworks colour the air electric blue. Is known to badmouth his opponent prior to the match beginning. Appearance: Cassidy has short, ruffled spikey hair, with blond highlighted tips. He has dyed jet black chin-length side burns. He is athletically built, and is lightly tanned. He is what would be considered 'good-looking', with blue eyes that have an almost innocent look about them. Cassidy is well built for a man of his stature with a chiselled physique and well toned body complete with washboard stomach and differentiable pectorals. He sports a waxed chest and large biceps, as well as a hugely muscular neck and broad shoulders. Platinum's legs are not shaven, but the hair is hard to see due to his fair natural colouring. He is not a big man, and only takes a size 12 shoe. Cassidy wears a double strapped all-in-one lycra suit with knee length shorts. It has a red background design on the front and back, with black and red intertwined barbed wire in the foreground. He wears long lucha-style black boots with 'platinum' in white on the outer length of each boot. The boots come up to where his all in one shorts finish. He wears a brace on his left knee, and an elbow pad on his right arm and also sports white taped fists, secured with black hockey tape at the wrists. The Punisher does not wear any kind of robe to the ring. Biography: When Cameron and Martha Cassidy gave birth to baby Platinum 23 years ago, the Cassidy Steel Company was in its third generation of inherited family leadership. His father and grandfather before him had run what now belonged to Cameron Cassidy, and Cameron was a dedicated worker, perhaps too much so. His wife Martha was an actress and was always in America, the couple did not see too much of each other, only usually at gala evenings and ceremonies in the eye of the Media. Based in Connecticut, Cameron left his son, christened Platinum Cameron Cassidy in their Glasgow residence in Scotland to be raised by loyal butler Jeeves. Platinum was brought up with a silver spoon in his mouth by the maintenance his parents sent back to b brought up with and so Platinum never worked for a thing in his life, getting a wrestling tutor at the age of eight under Jeeves' influence, as he was once a professional on the British circuit himself. As he grew older, Platinum deterred from schoolwork and watched wrestling obsessively until the age of 16 where he dropped out of a fee-paying private school to tour the US independent wrestling circuit, enraging his father by showing no interest in continuing the family business. Cassidy made his professional debut under the name of Master Cassidy, a gimmick which involved no real effort as Cassidy lived his gimmick with the blue-blood and rich, large-living lifestyle. On his travels, mainly of the Mid-Atlantic and Midwestern territories Cassidy competed under the characters The Masked Johoba, Reckless Rage and Platinum Cassidy and claimed the mWo United States and Hardcore titles as well as the DWO Illinois State and Midwest championships. After a brief sabbatical, Cassidy returned to the mWo in its final lap of glory, and retired the promotion as World Heavyweight Champion. Since then, Cassidy has guest refereed for EPW and appeared at various wrestling conventions, as well as making a Hollywood movie "In Technicolor". After travelling the world, Cassidy has learned new disciplines, making him even deadlier than ever. With an even more inflated ego, and skills to back it up, can anybody stop this man?
