Ricky Thunder Biography Real Name Ricky Thunder Nicknames Ricky T Hometown Perth, Western Australia Birthday August 11, 1982 Height 5'11 Weight 219 Interests Work to much to be interested in anything else lol Favorite Pro Wrestlers Hyabusa Biography March 17, 1994: 3pm Tonight, Ricky is going to his very first CCW Pay per View, to see his brother Shane take on the Supreme Machine, also known as SuMa to those around him. Tonight, the Heavyweight title is at stake. Tonight, Shane could possibly break the friendship that him and SuMa has held for so many years, the bond that formed the incomparable Supreme Team. The unbeatable tag team champions. The two men, now split, and facing each other for that precious title. Shane Thunder: Hey Ricky you ready? Ricky: Yeah! I'm coming. Where's mom and dad? Shane: They will be there. They had a few stops to make. Ricky: That's cool. So this is the night eh? You finally get your shot at CCW gold! Shane: Against SuMa. Good ole' SuMa. Ricky: You can beat him Shane! I know you can. Shane: It's not about beating him, it's about the friendship that is possibly at stake. Ricky: pay no attention to that stuff. If he is a poor sport, then that is his loss! Shane: That's not quite what I meant but I like your spirit. Let's go Ricky. Ricky heads out the front door and hops into the Toyota Tundra sitting in the driveway. He gets in on the drivers side, as Shane hops in the passenger side. Ricky looks over at him and laughs nervously. Ricky: You're gonna let me drive? Shane: Do I look dumb? Trade me. The two share a laugh, as Shane pulls the truck out of the driveway and onto the main road, heading south to the arena. March 17, 1994: 9pm Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 298 pounds, and standing 6 feet 9 inches tall, hailing from Perth Western Australia, he is one half of the CCW tag team champions, Thunder! His opponent standing across the ring, and standing 6'10 and weighing an impressive 325 pounds, he is the current Heavyweight champion and the other half of the CCW tag Team Champions, hailing also, from Perth, he is The Supreme Machine! March 17, 1994: 9:27pm Your winner, by way of pin fall, and NEW CCW Heavyweight Champion, he is Thunder!!!! June 29, 2001: 7:16pm The chopper lands as Ricky exits the chopper. He is met on the landing pad by a large gentleman in a rain parka. He shields himself from the blasting water from the helicopter's rotors, as he motions for Ricky to follow him. Ricky heads inside the building where he follows the man down the stairwell. They enter a corridor, which is soon revealed to be a hospital. Ricky asks the man: Ricky: So how is he? The man removes the parka to reveal himself as SuMa. SuMa: Not good. Ricky: What happened? SuMa: He has been battling cancer for quite some time. It has finally caught up to him. Ricky: CANCER? Why wasn't I told?! SuMa: He didn't want anyone to know.. Ricky: I need to go see him. Ricky follows the man down the long corridor and into a small room at the end of the hall. As he enters the hallway there in the bed, lies a skeleton of a man he once knew as his brother. He looks down at him as his eyes fill with tears. He moves to his bedside and grabs his brothers hand. Ricky: Shane.. Shane: Ricky, listen. I am sorry I never told you however, I didn't want you to worry. I need you to promise me something. Ricky: Anything .. Shane: I want you to follow your dreams. I know you want to be a wrestler, and it is something you need to do..promise me you will? Ricky: I will try Shane. Shane: Ricky, you have a tryout in CCW. I have made the arrangements, so all you need to do is.. Shane suddenly convulses in a seizure like fit and collapses backwards on the bed. Ricky rushes into the hallway screaming for a nurse, but nobody can be seen. SuMa goes to restrain the smaller Ricky. SuMa: Ricky, he is gone. Show him honor in fulfilling his request. September 17, 2001: 8:17pm The arena goes black and the song fills the air: 'Thunder, Thunder, Thunder, Thunder.....' Ricky Thunder appears from behind the screen standing on the stage looking down at his feet as the pyros rain down on his shoulders from above. 'I was caught In the middle of a lightning attack' Ricky raises his eyes to meet the ring. He takes a step towards the ramp and raises his arms into the air. 'I looked round And I knew there was no turning back' Ricky takes a few steps down the ramp then stops and looks to the ring. He then looks out over the crowd as he stops in his position. 'My mind raced And I thought what could I do .....Thunder.... And I knew There was no help, no help from you .....Thunder' In an instant the ring poles erupt with a loud bang and pyros fill the darkend arena as lighting up everything. As the pyros disappear, the lights come up as Ricky is now in the ring. RA: Making his debut tonight, weighing in at 219 pounds, and standing 5'11, he is the younger brother of the former CCW legend, Thunder, he is Ricky Thunnnnder! The crowd explodes in unison as Ricky stands there in the ring. Though it is only his first match he already feels at home in this arena. Thus the story of Ricky begun. He has since held the tag team and cruiserweight titles in CCW, as well as the Hardcore title. He outlived his fame in CCW and his brothers reputation overshadowed him in his opinion. He then took to the streets looking for work and wound up in PCW, where in his very first match, he finished a resounding 3rd in the schoolyard brawl, losing only to Xavier Kannon and Miyagawa, both former PCW champions.
