Legends XXX Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Thomas and Mack Center ~ Las Vegas, Nevada 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night March 26th, 2003 Only one show remains before Unbreakable goes down and this one is the thirtieth edition of Legends, being referred to currently as Legends Triple X this week. No titles will be on the line through the course of the night but all the questions remaining headed into Unbreakable are certain to be answered when the show comes to an end. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Two of the top women performers in eVolution goes head to head in this contest as Fiona takes on the eVolved Champion, Seara, in a non title match. Fiona has had two matches since arriving in eVolution and has been impressive in both encounters so far though only picking up one victory with an assist from The Unlimited. This week she faces a true test against Seara who has proven herself time and time again in eVolution to be one of the toughest and most determined competitors around. She might however still be feeling the effects of the two on one beating she received at the hands of Morpheus Omega and Thug roC two weeks ago. Upon hearing this Xander almost demanded a match at Legends and has been added straight into this contest against Seara and Fiona Preston who Xander promised was safe from The Unlimited last week ten minutes before she was assaulted by Asis during her match. With only one show left until the match beyond at Unbreakable, Daniel Somers has put every tag team in eVolution into one final match with battle royal rules. At stake in this one match are the points needed to put certain teams over and into the Tag Team Title match at Unbreakable. Not all of the teams can survive however and this match will be the final determination of who it is. Last week Shocka apparently didn’t make friends with Somers like he should have and now he finds himself signed to go against Morpheus Omega and Thug roC in a handicap match. Both Omega and roC no doubt will love every second of this match as they get to take their time against Shocka the way they did Seara two weeks ago. Shocka and Omega are still signed to battle at Unbreakable and this match will be a preview of sorts of what might happen if Shocka is able to survive long enough for the contest. Two weeks ago Christoph found himself assaulted by James Kimson when he made his shocking return to Legends and now he’s out for a chance at some payback. Kimson on the other hand has his sights locked firmly on his opponent at Unbreakable, the monster Sovereign in what is certain to be a very violent encounter between the two of them. He just might however be making a big mistake if he doesn’t keep himself focused on Erin Christoph who is perhaps the most dangerous opponent Kimson has faced in a one on one encounter since arriving in eVolution. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including the new Universal Champion Jason Jameson, Scott Frost, Sovereign and many of eVolution’s superstars! Arena Entrance ~ Investigations Featuring Daniel Somers and The Las Vegas Police Department The show opened with a shot of Daniel Somers as he stepped out of his long black limo and began to make his way towards the arena with a huge grin plastered on his face. As he walked closer to the entrance of the arena itself that smile quickly began to disappear as he saw standing in the doorway a line of Las Vegas Police Officers. Somers walked right up to them and stopped short when he noticed that they showed no signs of moving. “What do you people want now?” Somers asked obviously annoyed. ”We aren’t here for you if that’s what you’re worried about,” the lead officer assured him. ”I’m not worried in the least,” Somers told him. “I would have to have done something wrong to be worried about it.” ”Whatever you say,” the officer replied. “For the record though consider this a warning. With the recent murder of Sky Night and the reports of rampant drug use by some former wrestlers for eVolution it’s been said that the United States Sports Commission has begun an investigation of it’s own on this matter as well.” ”Is that a threat of some sorts for what you conceive as a lack of cooperation?” Somers asked. ”Not at all,” the officer replied. “The murder was in Seattle so I’m just here doing my job and helping out the best I can. You on the other hand might want to make sure your hands are clean before it’s too late.” ”Thanks I’ll keep that in mind,” Somers told him before he finally brushed by them and entered the arena.
