Legends XXXVI Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The General Motors Place ~ Vancouver, British Columbia 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night May 14th, 2003 Last week the changes throughout eVolution began to kick into full swing with several new faces appearing throughout the course of the night and it looks to continue this week with the in-ring debuts of several new stars to the eVolution fold. The four person tournament for contendership to the Continental Title will continue this week as well in its second match with the winner still having the chance to look forward to yet another brutal match with Asis for the Continental Title. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Even though it wasn’t known at the time it was the man called The Siege that made his debut last week in an effort to help out Shocka while he was being double teamed by Ronan Pierce and Erin Christoph. This week The Siege is placed into his first action in eVolution as he will battle against another member of NTICW in Homicide. Since coming over to eVolution, Homicide hasn’t had the greatest deal of luck in singles competition and to have a man like The Siege set in front of him will certainly be a tough test to handle. Nik VanCore came up short in his quest last week to get revenge against his former tag team partner Yoshii Tanaka and advance in the Continental Contenders tournament but this week he has another chance set in front of him to turn some heads as he faces off against newcomer Trevor Norman. Not much is known about the past of Trevor Norman but early reports about his in ring style have stated a rather violent past for the young man. Whether he can strive in a match where the rules are enforced remains to be seen but with an opponent like Nik VanCore this one can certainly get out of control in a hurry. One last debut to be made for the night involves a former star over in OSW, PCW, and FAW in Profusion. Profusion will go one on one against NTICW star Hate who’s career has hit a bit of a wall since showing up in eVolution as part of Daniel Somers own personal army against Tyler Davidson. Profusion will get the chance he needs to prove just what he has in him with Hate as an opponent which will be no small challenge. Sean Mason and Seara will both battle it out in the second of two matches to determine the Continental Title Number One Contender. Mason and Seara both have had a long history and battled numerous times for both the Rising Star and eVolved Titles in the past. Now they both get another chance to earn a shot at the Continental gold and also another match against a man who both of them have had many issues with in the past, Asis. Last week America’s Guardians made their in ring debuts and picked up a huge victory over the former Tag Team Champions, Team Network who seemed to have had some troubles of their own. This week Jason Relms will now team up with the new team of Cannibal and Carnivore, the Savage Society and will battle against his former partner High Impact and America’s Guardians who want some revenge against Savage Society for their brutal attack against America’s Guardians after the match last week. Three of the top competitors in the eVolved Title hunt right now will go at it in this handicap match as Hedges finds himself at a distinct disadvantage against two men. Xero is another NTICW wrestler but unlike the others he has made it a point to fight against the wishes of Daniel Somers. Now he’ll team with Lee eXodus of Team eVolution to battle against another former NTICW superstar and member of New-TV Hedges who hasn’t been the same it seems since his defeat at the hands of Malcolm Luther Xavier a few weeks ago. It was a loss that Xero also played a huge part in. Last week Malcolm King managed to coerce his former stand-in to accept an eVolved Title match for this week’s show. What happened instead was MLX pulled a trick of his own and arranged that the match would end up being a handicap match and the only way he could lose the title is by being pinned himself by either member of Tribute. On his side of the ring MLX will be standing alongside Dolo Bentswinger and 1001 Holds of The BodySnatchers to help ensure that he doesn’t lose the eVolved Title to anyone aligned with Malcolm King. Last week after a brief confrontation with Thug roC, Jason Jameson found himself on the receiving end of a New-TV beat down. This week Jason has found a bit of help to back him up as he teams with Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick who are also looking for any chance possible to get their hands on the Tag Team Champions, Twisted Reality. Jameson meanwhile will be looking for a shot of his own against Thug roC who despite what he says might be a little more than intent on proving to Jameson that he truly is just what he says he is, the greatest cruiserweight in the business. Shocka has some unfinished business with both Christoph and Ronan this week after their double team attack against him last week. Had it not been for the intervention of The Siege and James Kimson we might possibly be talking about Shocka in the past tense right now had Christoph had his way. Now Kimson and Shocka will both team up with identical agendas as they try and do what they can to teach both of the NTICW members a lesson while no doubt keeping a close eye out for the impending interference that Hate or Homicide would no doubt attempt. Asis and Scott Frost are two former adversaries but that was before Frost ever captured the Universal Title for the first time. Now the two of them will perhaps renew their intense rivalry as they go one on one in a match that Xander will no doubt be watching very closely either from the back or at ringside itself and plotting his own plans to take the Universal Title away from Frost’s grasps. The relationship between Xander and Asis has obviously soured over the past few weeks and that alone could be just the edge that Frost needs to pick up another victory and send a message out to his other old adversary, Xander before their big Universal Title match. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Xander, and many of eVolution’s superstars!
