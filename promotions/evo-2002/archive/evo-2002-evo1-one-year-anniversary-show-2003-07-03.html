eVo1 Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Continental Airlines Arena ~ East Rutherford, New Jersey 7:59PM EST Thursday Night July 3rd, 2003 One year ago on July 3rd, 2002 eVolution had kicked off it’s long and hard journey with the American Nightmare pay per view event. Now it’s July 3rd, 2003, a full year later and eVolution is still going strong but with a lot more surprises waiting around ever corner as it presents the one year anniversary show eVo1 that will feature an explosive card with the Universal, Continental, eVolved, and Tag Team Titles all on the line as well as the number one contendership to the Universal Title as Jason Jameson and James Kimson finish the battle that they started inside of the caged rumble on Sunday night at a New Dawn. Several other major matches have been signed as a result of the Caged Rumble match as well which is certainly going to make eVo1 one of the most explosive shows in eVolution history and quite a way to kick off year number two! The Omen Brotherhood has been resurrected again this time in the form of Luther Wisdom and Malcolm King, who has apparently found some sort of loophole in his contract to allow him to compete in this match. The two men will have quite a battle on their hands at eVo1 though as they try to battle back against America’s Guardians who came up short in their series of battles against The BodySnatchers and also against the new team of The Industry who pulled out a victory in the tag team invitational perhaps with a bit of an assist from the returned Syndicate team of Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick. Scott Evans walked into A New Dawn with the chance to make himself a star in eVolution with a victory over Eric Knight for the eVolved Title. Knight had shocked the world just days earlier by winning the title and Evans returned the favor with a very convincing victory over Knight to become the new eVolved Champion. During th Caged Rumble match later in the night Drake Nathanial Dukes made it a point to go after Scott Evans and attempt to send a message to the newly crowned champion that he had some challengers waiting for him. Scott Evans returned the favor by eliminating Drake from the rumble match but now they come head to head again this time in a table match and with the former eVolved and Tag Team Champion, Hedges also put into the mix in his quest to regain the title that he feels Luther Wisdom robbed from him. Much like his shocking victory last week for the eVolved Title, Eric Knight along with his partner Deacon, surprised everyone when they captured the Tag Team Titles. The two men have competed in tag team competition for months now and finally hit the top of the mountain and in their first defense they suddenly find themselves place into a match against one of the greatest teams in eVolution history, The Syndicate. Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick made their actual return at A New Dawn but now it’s time for their official in-ring return with a shot at the Tag Team Titles which have eluded them for the better part of a year now. At A New Dawn, Trevor Norman was crowned the new Cell Champion and he now has about eight different men looking to take that title away from him. Despite all of that however there will be a four team cell match with the teams to be announced later at Legends that Trevor just might find himself in. If that is the case he had better hope that there is at least one man in the Cell Division that isn’t out to take the title away from him. At least not just yet. It would seem to most that Ali Khadafi and Damon O’Halloran would be a logical team for this match along with Malcolm King and QBX but King’s status remains up in the air as he is competing earlier in the night alongside Luther Wisdom. Carl Cash surprised everyone when he showed up during the Caged Rumble at A New Dawn. No one was more surprised by that than Siege obviously who apparently has quite a long and storied history with Cash but what that history includes neither man is telling. It was Cash’s arrival that lured Siege out of the cage to eliminate himself from the match until a brawl broke out between both men that they look to finish here in this match. It will be Cash’s second match in eVolution and his first in quite a few months since he had quite a violent battle a while back with Morpheus Omega. Malik Allah Roland made his eVolution debut this Sunday at A New Dawn as one of the entrants into the Caged Rumble. He was a former thReat and SE superstar and looked to make quite an impact in eVolution on his first appearance but it was all ruined by Sean Mason. The Continental Champion hit the ring and Malik tried to eliminate him quickly but the plan back fired and it was Mason who ended up eliminating Malik and putting a stop to his plans of dominance in the Caged Rumble and eVolution. As a result Malik demanded he get a chance at Sean Mason and as an added bonus he got the match and a shot at Mason’s Continental Title. It’s official. New-TV has imploded and this match is just part of the fallout that is expected from it. Over the past few weeks Thug roC has made it known to anyone that would listen that he was sick and tired of dealing with the crap that came from New-TV, a group that he originally formed. As a result he made the choice to walk out on the group and did what he could to eliminate both Hedges and Morpheus Omega from the Caged Rumble at A New Dawn. He paid for his elimination of Morpheus however, as he stormed back into the cage and using anything he could get his hands on he assaulted Thug roC before he drove him out of the cage to be eliminated. Thug roC was said to be furious about it being allowed to happen and he demanded that this match be set for eVo1 so that he could get a chance to teach Morpheus a little something about respect. Over the past few weeks Jason Jameson has demonstrated a much more aggressive and violent version of himself most notably last week during Legends with his kidnapping of Sky Night and holding her at what many originally believed to be knife point until it was found out the knife was a fake bought in a Halloween store. He is also no stranger to James Kimson however as the two men battled in the past with Kimson coming out on top in the three way battle between them and Sovereign and they also continued that battle in brutal fashion during the Caged Rumble that saw the two men come down to the wire tied and as a result this match was signed with Jameson being given the chance to pick the stipulations for the match. What he will decide is still up in the air but we expect to find out for certain at eVo1. Seara made her official in-ring return during the Caged Rumble on Sunday night and had quite a good showing for her in the process while she also assisted Shocka in his victory over Greatness in their match. There is one thing that Seara had her sights set on the whole time however and somehow she managed to get it. A Universal Title shot from Xander, a man who she has beaten in the past when the two of them feuded over the eVolved Title for several months. Now though Xander has re-established himself as one of the toughest men in eVolution and he’ll look now to put a violent end to Seara’s wishful return to prominence. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Jason Jameson, Thug roC, and many of eVolution’s superstars!
