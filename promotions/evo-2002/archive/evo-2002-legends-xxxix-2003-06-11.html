Then a few weeks after that Siege shocked everyone by turning on Shocka and joining Daniel Somers. Apparently that too was another swerve courtesy of Siege as at From The Ashes he turned on Daniel Somers and helped Tyler Davidson defeat him once and for all. Now one of the last remnants of Daniel Somers army in eVolution stands in front of Siege as his opposition in Yoshii Tanaka. Whatever they are Shocka is the one man who doesn’t care and is out now to teach Greatness a lesson in respect. All he has to do is get by the newly dysfunctional Tag Team Champions in Luther Wisdom, formerly Malcolm Luther Xavier among other things, and Hedges. The two men also just happen to be co-eVolved Champions at the moment now as well to add to matters. It seems that the only chance Jameson might have in pulling this one off is if the differences between Wisdom and Hedges prove too much for the two men to work together through. Oh and don’t forget that Thug roC also just happens to be the special guest referee for this match as well. Hedges made it down the ramp and he leapt onto the apron before he climbed into the ring with the Tag Title still in his hands and his sights set on Luther Wisdom instead of his opponent Jason Jameson. That was when Jameson made his move. Luther and Hedges immediately began to argue over who would start the match for their team and Jameson came right up behind Hedges and with a forearm to his back sent him right back through the ropes and crashing down to the outside of the ring. Jameson quickly turned around and caught the surprised Luther with a quick flurry of right hands that kept him off balance and Thug roC tried himself to get in the way of Jameson’s quick assault. Jameson turned and took a quick swing at Thug roC but roC ducked out of the way and with Jameson turned around from taking the shot Wisdom reacted quickly and leveled him from behind with a clothesline to the back of his head. Jameson fell face first to the mat and Wisdom kept right on top of him as he drove kick after kick right down into the back of Jameson’s head until he was joined in on the attack by Thug roC. Wisdom stopped his attack on Jameson and just watched now as Thug roC was the one doing all the damage to Jameson. Wisdom suddenly grabbed Thug roC and spun him around about to plant him with a big right hand when Hedges caught Wisdom and yanked him away from Thug roC. Hedges and Wisdom stood face to face momentarily as Thug roC watched them and began to yell at both men to get back on their target. Wisdom looked at Thug roC and stepped through the ropes to stand on the apron and watch the action from their corner where one of them was supposed to stay with the rules enforced. Thug roC watched Wisdom with a surprised look on his face before he turned back to actually officiating the action while Hedges went right to work on Jameson with right hands as he tried to get back on his feet. Hedges drove right hand after right hand straight into the face of Jameson before he pulled him back up to his feet. Hedges drove another big forearm right into the face of Jameson before he grabbed him around the waist and took him quickly over with a belly to belly suplex. Hedges got back on his feet quickly and bounced off of the ropes before he went for a leg drop right across the throat of Jameson but Jameson rolled out of the way just in time. He got back on his feet quickly but the second he did the foot of Thug roC connected with his jaw in a big super kick and Jameson was sent right back down to the mat as Hedges climbed right on top of him for the cover. One………. Two………… Jameson kicked out! Hedges and Thug roC were both surprised by the kickout but Hedges didn’t stop anyway as he got back on his feet and picked Jameson right back up with him. Hedges scooped Jameson up onto his shoulder and walked towards their corner with his hand out to Luther but before he could make the tag Jameson slid down his back and shoved Hedges forward right into a head on collision with Luther that sent him flying off the apron and into the guard rail. Hedges staggered back after the collision and Jameson quickly rolled him up with a school boy as Thug roC went down and made a very slow count. One………. Hedges kicked out! Jameson got back on his feet as quickly as he could but before he could do anything else Wisdom was back in the ring and brought a clubbing forearm right down across the back of Jameson’s head that knocked him down to both knees as Wisdom grabbed him and quickly locked him into A Slave To No MAN. Wisdom pulled back on the hold and kept it locked in tightly as Thug roC watched with a smile on his face at the obvious pain that Jameson was in. Hedges got back to his feet and saw what was happening and he just stood by and watched as Wisdom kept the hold locked in. Jameson never showed any signs of giving the match up however and Wisdom finally released the hold and let Jameson fall face first to the mat. Wisdom and Hedges both began now to stomp on the downed Jameson as Thug roC looked on with a satisfied look on his face. Hedges grabbed hold of Jameson and lifted him back up to his feet before he held him in a full nelson as Thug roC took his own turn at taking shots against Jameson as he pounded him with rights and lefts right to the midsection. Thug roC backed off and now it was Luther Wisdom’s turn as he bounced off of the ropes and came running back at Jameson with a right hand but he was no longer there and Wisdom instead slammed a big right hand straight into the chest of Hedges which knocked him staggering backwards in pain. Hedges stopped his movement and turned to look at the unconcerned Wisdom before he planted him with a big right hand that knocked him down to the mat. All their teamwork had obviously come to a failure as their hostilities could not be tempered any longer. Wisdom fired back with a right hand of his own only to receive a return shot from Hedges as the two battled back and forth as Thug roC looked on in horror. Thug roC tried getting in between the two men to put them back on their assigned task but it was no good as they battled throughout the ring. Jameson began to get back onto his feet and watched as Hedges drove a big boot right into the side of Wisdom’s head and knocked him down to the mat. Hedges grabbed right back onto Wisdom and lifted him right into the air before he brought him slamming down to the mat again with a powerbomb. The second Hedges released him with the powerbomb however Jameson pounced on him and took him quickly down with Perfection. Jameson went straight for the cover but Thug roC showed no signs at all of making the count for him. Jameson looked up at Thug roC and saw that he was doing nothing and got back on his feet. Thug roC immediately began to back away but Jameson continued to stalk him and paid no attention to anything else including Luther Wisdom getting back on his feet. Wisdom got back on his feet and saw that Hedges was down and out and he also saw that Jameson was paying no attention to him as he stalked Thug roC. Wisdom came right up in pursuit of Jameson now and was about to nail him from behind when Jameson suddenly swung around and caught Wisdom with a quick series of right hands. Wisdom didn’t see them coming at all and was dropped by the last right hand but that was when Thug roC made his move. With Jameson’s back turned Thug roC grabbed him from behind and quickly spun him around and back down to the mat with the roCing Horse. Thug roC hit the move and he quickly grabbed onto Hedges who was barely coherent and dragged him right on top of Jameson for the cover as Thug roC made the quickest count he possibly could. One………. Two…………. Three! Jameson wanted the shot but even he as good as he is couldn’t overcome the odds that he was placed against tonight. And despite the hostilities between Hedges and Wisdom the team survived, barely, this encounter thanks in part to the assistance of Thug roC himself who obviously had quite a bit to lose. Universal Title ~ Standard Match “The Prodigal” Sean Mason vs. Xander© Up next was the main event for the night and the newly crowned Continental Champion got the chance of a lifetime as he now also had the chance to capture the eVolution Universal Title as well if he could come out with a victory over Xander here tonight. The arena was plunged into complete darkness as the dramatic intro to “Smash Sumthin” by Redman began and strobes raced back and forth across the arena in both a random and spontaneous manner. As the instrumental fanfare came to a climax a pyro exploded from the stage area… ”They going to let me out,” The fans screamed with excitement. “They going to call my name,” A black and white photo of Sean Mason flashed back and the name “Prodigal Son” flashed back and forth one after another on the screen. As the music quickened the faster the eVotron changed its appearance. Suddenly the masked Sean Mason stepped through the curtain and on the cue of the lyrics…… “Turn the lights on!” Pyro’s exploded and Sean Mason is seen under a spotlight with his arms raised and the Continental Title strapped about his waist as he stepped before the fans to a huge ovation from the crowd packed into the arena. Thrusting his arms side to side the masked Sean Mason began a purposeful walk toward the ring not once taking his eyes off the ring before he slid in under the bottom rope and got quickly back to his feet and awaited the arrival of the Universal Champion, Xander. ”Smash Sumthin” by Redman finally died out and with it went the cheers of the crowd as they were quickly replaced by boos as “Tomorrow” by SR-71 began to play throughout the arena. As the toggling guitar effects came in and out the arena is struck by the strobe light going with the beat. Then, as the main guitar cuts through the toggling lead a huge explosion of pyrotechnics went off and Xander came out from backstage area with the Universal Title firmly in his hands and he quickly raised it above his head with a sick and sadistic grin on his face as in his other hand was the monkey wrench which he had assaulted Asis with one week ago. As the crowd booed the leader of The Unlimited, Xander shrugged them all off and made his way quickly down the ramp with his eyes locked on the man that would be his prey this evening. Xander rolled right into the ring and dropped the Universal Title but he kept the monkey wrench firmly in his grasps as he came right at Mason and took a big swing at him. Mason however ducked out of the way just in time and turned quickly around before he caught Xander with a quick succession of right hands straight to the head that kept Xander rocked and gave the referee a chance to react as he grabbed the monkey wrench and yanked it right out of Xander’s hand and put it down and out of the way of the action. Mason kept right on top of Xander and pounded him with right hands before he backed Xander against the ropes and whipped him across the ring. Xander came flying back off the ropes and Mason leapt into the air and caught him with a perfect dropkick right to the jaw before he quickly made the cover in an attempt to pick up a quick victory. One…….. Two………… And Xander kicked out easily! Mason got quickly back on his feet and as Xander tried to rise he was caught with a quick kick right to his ribs but Xander still powered his way back up. Mason kept right on top of Xander though and now began to deliver a quick series of kicks right to the thigh of Xander until he tried to go up high with a spinning heel kick but Xander caught him in mid-turn and lifted Mason right into the air before he brought him slamming down onto the mat back first with a huge powerbomb. Xander staggered backwards for a second still shaking off the initial attack by Mason before he pounced right on top of the downed Mason and began to pound at him with right hands. With Mason down and under Xander, Xander began to try and untie the back of Mason’s mask and yanked it off his head but Mason prevented him from doing it and Xander got fed up and got back to his feet before he drove a boot right down into Mason’s midsection. Xander grabbed Mason by the mask again and yanked him back up to his feet before he grabbed him by the arm and pulled him right into a short arm clothesline but Mason ducked it again and caught Xander from behind now with a bulldog and brought him face first into the mat! Mason now quickly tried to apply the Enigma on Xander with him face down on the mat but Xander fought back against it and quickly made it to the ropes to prevent the hold from ever being locked in securely. Mason quickly got back on his feet and waited now as Xander pushed himself up again as well. As soon as Xander got back on his feet Mason came charging at him with a clothesline attempt but Xander caught him again and scooped Mason right up into the air before he turned around and sent Mason flying. This time however Xander sent Mason flying right over the top rope and he sailed right into the steel guard rail that found itself shoved back a few feet right into the first row of fans who probably just got themselves a little more than they expected with their ticket purchase as they nearly had a lap full of Continental Champion. Xander quickly climbed out of the ring and went right back after Mason who despite the pain he had to be in following that move was right back on his feet and threw himself right at Xander as he slammed his head and shoulder right into Xander’s midsection and stopped him in his tracks. Mason remained on his knees and looked up at Xander before he drove a right hand right into his midsection but Xander fired back quickly and drove his knee right up and into the face of Mason and knocked him back down again. Xander grabbed Mason by the back of his mask and pulled him back up to his feet again before he swung them both around and sent Mason running straight towards the steel ring steps and Mason flipped right over the steps upon impact before he landed flat on his back on the other side and Xander continued right after him again. Xander grabbed Mason and again lifted him back up to his feet before he clocked him with a big right hand and rolled him back into the ring before he followed right back in after him. Xander quickly rolled Mason over onto his back and covered him. One…….. Two………. And Mason kicked out now! Xander kept right on top of Mason as he pounded him with a right hand before he wrapped both his hands around the throat of Mason and began to pick him up. Xander stood Mason up on both feet with his hands still wrapped around Mason’s throat before he lifted him right into the air for the Victimizer but Mason countered it and used Xander’s own strength against him as he used all the momentum of Xander’s lifting him to propel himself further up and free of Xander’s grip before he drove both of his feet right into Xander’s chest and knocked him backwards. Mason landed on all fours and quickly got back on his feet again before he saw Xander already trying to get up and drove both his feet again right into the side of Xander’s head and knocked him right back down and Xander rolled out of the ring to safety. Outside of the ring was not a safe place for Xander to be however against a competitor like Sean Mason as he sprung onto the top rope quickly and came right down across Xander with a flying bodypress that brought him slamming right down to the ground again and Mason quickly followed up as he pounded on Xander with rights and lefts as he kept him on the ground. Mason got back on his feet and brought his foot right down into the face of Xander before he slid back into the ring and waited for Xander as he slowly got up on the outside. Xander began to roll back into the ring and Mason sprung into action again with a baseball slide this time as he slid right into Xander and knocked him backwards and right into the guard rail. Mason went right back to work as Xander leaned up against the guard rail as he was pounded on with right hands by Mason before he began to rip into the chest of Xander with knife edge chops. Mason wailed away on Xander with chop after chop before he finally rolled Xander back into the ring and then hopped onto the apron. Instead of climbing into the ring and going right back after Xander, Mason instead began to climb the ropes. Mason made it up to the top rope but he was a little slow in doing so and it gave Xander all the time he needed as he threw his weight into the ropes to shake them and it caused Mason to lose his balance and he crotched himself right on the top rope. Xander knew he had Mason in trouble now and took his time to recover from Mason’s unexpected assault on him before he began to climb the ropes himself and get onto the top rope with Mason. Xander pounded Mason with right hand after right hand as he tried to balance himself on the top rope at the same time as he pounded Mason and looked to put an end to this match and look towards the future once again. Xander began to set Mason up for a superplex but it seemed that Mason himself wasn’t quite ready yet to let this match come to an end. He drove a right hand straight into the midsection of Xander and followed it quickly up with a second that slowed Xander just enough for Mason to stand up on the ropes now as well. The two of them stood face to face and they battled back and forth as they exchanged right hands but it was Mason who got the advantage when he drove his head right into the face of Xander and stunned him long enough for a right hand to knock Xander off the top rope and send him crashing down to the mat. Mason slowly now climbed back to his feet on the top rope and watched as Xander got back on his feet as well. As soon as Xander stood up Mason came off the top rope and caught him with a flying clothesline that brought him right back down to the mat and Mason again went for the cover. One……… Two………. Xander just barely kicked out! Mason was only inches away from pulling off what many would consider an upset and capturing the Universal Title but he didn’t let it slow him down. Mason got right back on his feet and retreated back to the corner again before he stood there and waited for Xander once again. The second Xander stood upright Mason went for The Midas Touch but Xander caught his foot at the last possible second and spun Mason around before he shoved him from behind and right into the referee. The referee was knocked down but not out despite the head on collision he had with Mason. Xander reacted as quickly as he could and kneeled down near the corner in order to lure Mason in and the second Mason came close enough, Xander was up and swinging as he drove the monkey wrench right in under the jaw of Mason and floored him with the shot as he dropped the wrench and got quickly back on his feet. Xander stood over the knocked out Mason and lifted him slowly back up to his feet again before he hoisted Mason up and onto his shoulders. The referee got back up just in time to see Xander swing Mason around and into the Chaos Reign before he quickly went for the cover. One……… Two………… Three! The match was over and despite an incredible fight being put up by Sean Mason, Xander was still the eVolution Universal Champion but this one wasn’t over just yet. Xander got back to his feet and waited for the referee to raise his arm and give him the Universal Title but it wasn’t the referee who grabbed him by the arm. It was Tyler Davidson. Tyler spun Xander around and began to wail away on him with right hand after right hand as he backed Xander away from Mason and up against the corner. With Xander backed against the corner now Tyler drove an elbow right up and into the jaw of Xander before he took a few steps back and charged at Xander but Xander got his foot up and slammed it right into the face of Tyler to send him staggering backwards. Xander reached down and grabbed his monkey wrench again and the second Tyler turned around the wrench was slammed right across the top of his head and Tyler dropped to the mat like a ton of bricks. Xander stood over Tyler and looked tempted to continue the attack with the monkey wrench but he restrained himself for a few seconds and by the time he was about to attack Sean Mason had seen what was going on and caught Xander from behind with a flying forearm shot to the back of his head. Mason went for the super kick again but Xander ducked under it this time and sent Mason right over the top rope with a big back body drop. Xander didn’t even watch as Mason crashed to the ground but he saw Tyler bleeding from the head as he tried getting back on his feet again only to be knocked right back down again by Xander with the monkey wrench shot a second time. Xander didn’t deliver a third shot though as he walked back to the corner and called for a microphone. He was given the mic and walked back to stand over Tyler as he looked down at the blood soaked unconscious face of Tyler. ”I could finish you right here once and for all Tyler,” Xander warned him. “But I’m not going to do that. I’m enjoying this far, far too much! Come A New Dawn whether Asis is there or not I will take great pleasure in breaking you once again and this time I’ll be sure to finish the damn job! Enjoy the time that you have Tyler for it is running short for you.” Xander threw the mic down to the mat and remained in the ring with his arms raised as he stood over Tyler and on the outside of the ring Mason was getting back to his feet as the show faded away to blackness.
