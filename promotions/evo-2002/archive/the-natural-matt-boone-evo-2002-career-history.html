“The Natural” Matt Boone — eVo career history

History Wrestler Entrance: 'Beat The World' by Pressure 4-5 begins playing throughout the arena as Matt Boone makes his way out from backstage with little glitz or glamour in his entrance. He makes his way quickly down the ramp and slides right into the ring before doing some last minute stretching for his match. Appearance: He's a skinny muscular kid with medium-length blonde hair. In the ring he wears very traditional clothing, a simple pair of black trunks, knee pads and short boots. He tapes his wrist. Biography: Matt Boone was a high school standout athlete who was unsure of what he would do after leaving school. With no plan of college, he worked construction when one of his buddies, dared him to try out at a wrestling school. For eight months he trained, growing to love the sports entertainment world. Because of a connection between his trainer and 'The DiscoKid' Troy Pierce, Boone got a shot in the now defunct nEw promotion. Two weeks before his debut match, the federation closed. Lost without a place to call home thanks to the dwindling indy scene, Boone was saved by another call from Pierce, this time for eVolution. The two now team regularly for the promotion, and Boone recently had his first match, getting the victory for him and Pierce in a Lethal Lottery Matchup.
