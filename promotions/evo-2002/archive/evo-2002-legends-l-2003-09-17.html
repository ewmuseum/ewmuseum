From Fitzgerald Stadium ~ Killarney, Ireland 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night September 17th, 2003 Parking Lot ~ Well Wishes Featuring Thug roC and Shaft-Man At the bottom of the screen, we see the words ‘earlier today’ placed to allow us to know when this took place. The bright sun gleams outside the arena, while we watch a S.U.V pull up. The tinted vehicle is too dark to tell right away who is inside, but it’s only moments before Thug roC makes his way out of the driver’s side. He opens the back seat to his vehicle and grabs a bag along with his United Title. He makes his way up the parking low, rolling his bag behind him. Shaft-Man appears out of nowhere and embraces Thug roC with an unwelcomed hug. Thug roC pushes Shaft-Man away and sticks out his hand. “For now on, whenever you want to say hi, just shake my hand.” Thug roC says in a stern voice. Shaft-Man shakes roC’s hand, putting a smile across roC’s face. “See, that’s better.” “But friends don’t shake…” Shaft-Man pauses, only making Thug roC frown immediately. Without notice, Shaft-Man proceeds to hug roC. “THEY HUG!” Thug roC shoves Shaft-Man off of him again. “Damnit, Shaft, what do you want now?” “Well Thugster, you let me come down to the ring with you last week. So I’m going to extend the invitation back to you to come to ringside during my debut match.” Shaft-Man ends his sentence with a huge smile on his face, anticipating an answer from Thug roC. “Look Shaft, tonight is a big night. And trust me, I would love to come to the ring with you, but I have other things on my mind. I mean, this being Legends’ fiftieth show, it’s going to be a big one. We have a big double main event. First I defend against Evans, then Shocka and Jameson go at it. So, unfortunately, I need to keep my mind on my match.” “Aight roC, but this is a big match too. Canny and I were talking….” Shaft-Man is then cut off. “Hey, good luck.” Thug roC just walks off, leaving Shaft-Man in middle sentence. Shaft-man hiptosses Tok'n as it seems like he is the aggressor. Tok'n sends Shaft-man to the corner of the ring. Tok'n follows it up with a dropkick. Tok'n finishes off this sequence of moves with a bulldog from behind. Shaft-Man surprises Tok'n with a dragon screw legwhip. Shaft-man follows it with a piledriver. Tok'n soon reestablishes himself in this match with an arm wrench. Shaft-Man throws Tok'n off the ropes and hits him with a diving shoulder block. Shaft-man gives Tok'n a reverse neckbreaker. He goes for the cover 1..... Tok'n kicks out! Tok'n delivers a spinning backbreaker to Shaft-Man. Tok'n with a suplex on Shaft-man sends him to the mat. Shaft-man with a leg trip sending Tok'n to the mat; Shaft-man tries for an anklelock submission but Tok'n reverses it into a small package.....1......Shaft-man kicks out! Shaft-man sets Tok'n up DDTs him into the mat. Shaft-Man then sets him up in a dragon sleeper. Tok'n fights it and is able to reverse it into a test drive maneuver taking out both men. A man in a full black body suit and mask makes his way down to ringside. Shaft-man tries for a fisherman buster but is blocked by Tok'n. However Shaft-man nails a spinning backbreaker on Tok'n. Shaft-Man whips Tok'n into the ropes and catches him with a flapjack. Shaft-Man delivers a double armed DDT on Tok'n as he goes for the cover..... The masked man enters the ring and nails Shaft-man in the back of the head with a lead pipe. Tok'n recovers and nails The Mindfucking DDT onto Shaft-Man knocking him out cold. The referee rings the bell as he has officially disqualified Tok'n. The winner of the bout by Disqualification......Shaft-Man. However Tok'n and his masked partner does not stop the attack as the masked man repeatedly strikes Shaft-man in the back of his body with lead pipe. Tok'n places boots to the back of Shaft-Man trying to break it. Tok'n picks up Shaft-Man and double arm DDT's him as the masked man throws the seemingly lifeless body over the top rope. Shaftman's head gets caught between the top and middle ropes effectively cutting off his oxygen and hanging Shaft-Man. The masked man pulls on the legs of Shaft-Man hoping to speed up the dying process...as Tok'N nails repeated shots to the head of Shaft-man with a chair...busting him open. The crowd erupts as Thug Roc slides into the ring and catches Tok'n from behind nailing the roCing HORSE! As the masked man pulls Tok'N's body out of the ring. Thug Roc releases the almost lifeless body of Shaft-Man from the ropes. Covered in blood Thug Roc looks at Tok'n and the masked man in disgust as they make their way up the aisle to the song ROCK SUPERSTAR!!!!! Possibly the biggest Main Event in eVo Televised history is tonight as James Kimson sets to defend his title against friend and former 2 time Champion Scott Frost. The camera is backstage, in the parking lot as a Limo drives up to the doors of the arena. It’s a cool evening as the limo glides gently to a halt. The back door swings open and one leg at a time out walks James Kimson to a pop from the fans in the arena. Kimson’s wearing street clothes with his leather jacket but most notably is the Universal Title with the new nameplate and all draped over his impressive shoulders. Kimson takes off his shades and pockets them listening to a “Kimson! Kimson!” chant growing in the arena. “Well it’s about time I arrived here!” said Kimson to a pop. “It’s been to damn long. But most importantly tonight is a huge night! 49 episodes of Legends have left us and now it’s time for a new era. A new era with a new Champion. And a with the Title on the line I am going up against possibly the best Universal Champion of all time in my good friend Scott Frost.” said Kimson. The fans cheer at the mention of Frost, 2 time Universal Champion. “It’s going to be one hell of a night. And I can’t wait to get it on with one of the best ever. But that beings said. Backstage ~ Backstage Ramblings Featuring Matt Hunt The shot fades in to the busy backstage area. Various roster members are seen strolling around or limbering up for their respective match as well as half a dozen crew members who carry out their usual work routine…whatever that may be. Thousands of cables run along the floor, scattered around the place but not too much that an accident can be caused. Empty transportation trunks are also seen as they are neatly piled up in certain sporadic places. Of course, a backstage area wouldn’t be complete without the stack of tables and pallets as well as the array of fold-up metal chairs that are either being used or just stored in a corner or out of harms way. A figure sat on top of the chest-height stack of pallets is spotted as the camera glides past. The shot almost does a double take, as it seems strange to see somebody actually stood - or in this case, sat - still. Upon the pinpoint of the focus, this person seems relaxed and calm as he glances around with a motionless expression. With the short shaved black hair and the stubble that is scattered around his mouth, the facial features are identified as one of eVo’s new blood - Matt Hunt aka The Postman. There’s a mixed reaction from the people who watch on the large video screen inside the area floor. For a few moments, we watch Hunt who watches the busy scene before him. It seems like an entirety flies by when Hunt notices the camera while scanning around. He frowns, then raises an eyebrow before motioning to the cameraman with his head to come over to him. There’s no reaction as Hunt rolls his eyes and slightly shakes his head and after a few moments, he jerks his head back with is eyes budging out which eventually draws the cameraman to him. The picture bobbles up and down as the cameraman jogs over, his heavy breathing heard over the small camera mic, and pauses just before Hunt who doesn’t move from his seated position. ”I’ve noticed that people tend to think these cameras that lurk around backstage are invisible” he begins with a carefree expression. “I mean, isn’t it a bit of a coincidence that the camera appears just as some little “skit” takes place in the first place? And you’d think they’d at least have the courtesy to acknowledge the camera…ignorant sods.” Hunt tuts and shakes his head as he finishes the last sentence before shrugging with a puzzled expression. He glances around for no apparent reason before returning to talk to the camera. “Heh” he chuckles “This is like that Malcolm kid in that program. You know when he’s the only one that can see the camera and acknowledges it by yapping his little head off about this and that every five minutes. Well, I guess that’s me…” he finishes with he suspiciously shifts his eyes from left to right. He then holds his arm out in amazement with the bemused facial expression, causing the camera to turn around and aimlessly pan around. “Look at them…sheesh!” he says while shaking his head “you’d think somebody would notice a camera.” More moments pass before Hunt continues. “Oh…maybe they’re being ‘professional’…” he starts with a thoughtful expression. “Pfft, bollocks to that. If I see a camera pointing at me, I’m not going to act like I’m doing something.” He concludes. “So anyway, tonight is my night off. In a way I’m a little pissed off that I haven’t been booked, but then again…I have been working my arse off since I’ve been here.” He begins with a determined expression “Suppose you can’t blame the eVo booking team, they’re just resting one of the future stars of this business…either that or they forgot all about me and when they finally realized, they thought ‘meh, he’ll get over it’…and that I will” he finished. “Yep, that I will” he sighs “I’ve only been here a matter of weeks, no need to rush…just sit back and relax, take your time…and when some little runt like Sid or Evans pokes their nose in…KERPOW!! WAMMO!! and various other old Batman-related visuals…ah, good old Adam. He was the best Batman of them all…” He looks up to the ceiling to give the impression that he’s reminiscing before remembering that the camera is on him… “Anyway!! I’ve been sat here doing bugger all for long enough…and now it’s time to have a personal conference with Mustafa Sheet. So long, suckers…” Hunt hops off the stack of pallets before strolling down the hallway, after a few moments he starts to quicken the pace before starting to jog…which then turns into a sprint and disappearing around a corner.
