Legends XXXIV Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Conseco Field House ~ Indianapolis, Indiana 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night April 23rd, 2003 eVolution continues on its trek towards End of Days as the battle between Somers and Davidson continues to heat up with the sides being quickly drawn. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Ronan Pierce made his debut two weeks ago but now he gets to make his in ring debut with a match against Nik VanCore. Ronan has a long history of knowledge backing him up in this battle of a veteran against a relative rookie in the business. Fiona Preston and Seara will go one on one this week in the first battle between two of the top female performers in eVolution. Last week Tribute defeated Strictly X and won the rights to the number one contendership against the eVolution Tag Team Champions. It took an assist however from The HeartBreak Chick for Tribute to win that spot and now they are forced to put it on the line against The Syndicate, a team who is no stranger to Tag Team Gold in this business. The winner however will first have to defeat The BodySnatchers at End of Days in order to hold onto the number one contendership. After accepting the challenge of MLX two weeks ago for last week’s escapades Hedges managed to earn himself a shot at the eVolved Title here this week. Hedges did pick up a rather tough pinfall last week on MLX but this time he’ll have to do it again with the eVolved Title hanging in the balance. Meanwhile there is absolutely no way of telling just what MLX could have in store for them. This week there will be the third and perhaps final match between these two teams with the Tag Team Titles hanging in the balance. Twisted Reality could put one final end to it all with a victory here that would give them the required two wins meanwhile Team Network has yet to win a match and must somehow pull off a victory here this week if they want to keep their title dreams alive which Twisted Reality is certain to be looking to smash to pieces once and for all. With the complete breakdown of last week’s main event match this week the number has been reduced to two as James Kimson will battle Xander. Xander was the recipient of some bad luck last week as his own cousin took advantage of him to steal a victory away from Scott Frost who in some bad luck of his own had knocked Kimson out with a vicious chair shot that the Hype certainly has not forgotten about. Frost and Christoph are both no doubt going to have close eyes on this match but Kimson and Xander are the two that have the most to worry about with them here as they will both have to keep a watch out for unwanted entrants. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Scott Frost, Jason Jameson, Erin Christoph, Thug roC, and many of eVolution’s superstars!
