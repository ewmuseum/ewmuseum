House Show V, 16 August 2003

16 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
House Show Results A massive crowd for this house show as eVo came to London for the last time of this month. Standard Match Chad Winters vs. Aaron Justice A back and forth match that the crowd loved. Multiple spots in the match that caused us to rise to our feet and anticipate the match’s end. Winters flew off the top with a cross body, and Justice caught him in mid air. That’s before throwing him onto his shoulders and nailing his Final Justice for the impressive win. Winner via pinfall: Aaron Justice. Cell Match Aaron Frost Vs. Machine Another great cell showing, seems like the London crowd loves the cell more then the ring. Machine obviously the powerhouse in this fight, tried over powering his opponent, but Aaron’s size and speed were the perfect combination to take out Machine. Frost with a shocking right left combo knocked out Machine, followed by a sharpshooter, and a tap out. Winner via submission: Aaron Frost Standard Match w/managers HBC w/ Big Daddy Dreamer vs. Seara w/ Scott Frost. A classic battle to say the least that really got the crowd on their feet. A lot of technical holds from Seara, grounding HBC most of the match, but HBC would come back. HBC got a few shots in, and at one point went for her Kiss of Death, but Seara was able to catch the foot and clothesline her down. Scott and BDD were cheering loudly for their girls as HBC went for another kick, and Seara ducked it and gave HBC a german suplex. The two went back and forth until they both tumbled to the outside. The match got out of hand out there, and both girls were counted out. As the bell rang, the two turned this match into a catfight, causing BDD and Scott Frost to have to separate the two of them. Good match. Winners: Double count out. As the intermission was announced, we saw Scott Frost and BDD arguing on the outside. It was then announced that the two would compete against each other later in the night. Of course, this stopped a lot of people from going to the bathroom, as we thought the match was next, but that wasn’t the case. Standard Tag Match Thug roC and Shaft-Man vs. Sean Valmonté` and Yoshii Tanaka Weird seeing Thug roC and Shaft-Man in the same ring ever since their old JeW days. But they got a mixed reaction. The match primarily consisted of Valmonté` and Shaft-Man, rumors say both Yoshii and Thug roC were under the weather. Thug roC got tagged in as Shaft-Man was getting a beating. He cleared the ring as if he was a face, kinda weird cheering for him Thug roC nailed a roCing Horse on Yoshii as Shaft-Man was setting up on the top rope. Shaft-Man came down with a 450 splash, but got a face full of green mist on the way down. Valmonté` put up a fight with Thug roC, taking him into the turnbuckle, but Valmonté` would run into a big boot. Just as Yoshii and Valmonté` went to double team roC, Shaft-Man came back and superkicked Valmonté` out of the picture. Thuggy would give Yoshii another roCing Horse and the pin. Winners via pinfall: Thug roC and Shaft-Man Standard Match: Main Event Big Daddy Dreamer Vs. Scott Frost Both Seara and HBC were sent backstage before this one started. Big Daddy Dreamer and Scott Frost went toe to toe in a great battle. I was kinda upset that I didn’t have my camera with me for this one. BDD hit a vicious running lariat, that was thought to take Frost out, but Frost was able to get his foot on the rope. The two continued to battle until Frost hit a corkscrew off the top, taking the standing BDD down. But that pinfall would only get a 2. BDD went for his submission finisher, but Frost was able to break free. Once BDD got back up, he swung with a wild right that Frost ducked, and Frost hooked him up around his waist. Frost hoped on his shoulder and went for the victory roll, but BDD rolled through it and got the quick three count. Frost seemed to have kicked out in the end, but the ref counted three anyways. Frost was obviously upset, as BDD took his tag title belt and made his way up the ramp. The fans booed as Frost was ready to go at it again. Winner via pinfall: Big Daddy Dreamer Overall, a great house show, a lot of people were disappointed that Shane Wethers wasn’t there, a few other names were mentioned, but London was happy with what they got.
