Legends XVIII Transcript Live on OSE! From The General Motors Place~ Vancouver, British Columbia 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night December 11th, 2002 Will chaos reign one more time? What is in store for eVolution now that Cameron Griffin is the new Commissioner? Will Bobby Stormbringer make his presence known after losing his job this past week? What is in store with the ongoing stable wars plaguing eVolution? Who will take the advantage away from the now growing New-TV stable led by eVolution President Thug roC? Daniel Somers made some impact last week as he tried to stop the attacks that The Notorious had orchestrated against everyone, mainly New-TV as of late since Shocka defeated Scott Frost at Rise of the Legends for the Road to Glory Championship and also cost Frost his chance at the eVolution Universal Championship. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Old School Entertainment! For the past few weeks Jackson Powers has been making his presence felt in eVolution matches. This week he’s thrown into a match with the high flying and always dangerous Jay Stylez Junior. Powers despite not having a single match or even being officially signed to eVolution has already made himself an enemy in the mysterious Strictly Prohibited. Twice Powers has attacked and both times now Prohibited has been caught in the middle. It’s safe to say he will likely be keeping a very close eye on this one. If that wasn’t enough to make this one an explosive battle Daniel Somers did some last minute card changing on Cameron Griffin to add his new recruit Angel into the mix. Angel was the man we saw last week that viciously assaulted Jinosyde after talking with Daniel Somers and left the former Rising Star Title contender lying hurt and out of action. Morpheus Omega has had issues with Sean Mason since he arrived in eVolution at Rise of the Legends. Now he gets his first chance to get his hands on him at Legends this week as New-TV and The Unlimited join forces for this tag team match. The HeartBreak Chick will join Omega for this match meanwhile her former partner, Big Daddy Dreamer joins the side of The Rising Star Champion Sean Mason. Whether or not The Unlimited, and New-TV members can get along long enough could very well determine the winner of this contest if Dreamer and Mason let it last long enough to come down to that. The eVolution Tag Team Titles belong to The Unlimited right now despite their obviousness of being on a rather violent downsizing of their own group. Cameron Griffin has arranged this week for Seara of the Notorious to face Luther Wisdom of The Omen Brotherhood with a Tag Team Title shot hanging in the balance for Nightmare Before Christmas. If Seara wins, she and a partner of her choosing will receive the shot but if Wisdom wins he and a partner of his choosing will get the shot. Both choices should be quite obvious for either team as Seara has The Notorious firmly behind her in this one as Xavier Citizen will certainly be watching Wisdom’s performance as well. Some issues have obviously been existing between The Notorious and New-TV. This week the two “leaders” of the group will come head to head in the ring and the all but forgotten High Impact Title will be placed on the line. Scott Frost through out a challenge to Thug roC himself after his constant interference in Notorious affairs this past week and he has been granted the shot against Thug roC’s wishes thanks to Cameron Griffin. Griffin ruled the match would take place and with Tyler Davidson appointing Cameron to organize this show there wasn’t anything he could do about it. Thug roC will for the first time be forced to defend his High Impact Title and it’s going to be against Scott Frost. Last week in one of the latest returns to eVolution, the bigger and stronger “DiscoKid” Troy Pierce made his return from injury. During his absence Pierce had obviously been spending some time in the gym and now in the best condition of his life he gets right back to work. Issuing an open challenge to anyone an unfamiliar man with a very familiar name accepted the challenge. The history between Troy Pierce and Malcolm King who at one time went by the name of Xavier Citizen in EdW is well documented. Now Pierce will face the real Xavier Citizen in a one on one battle. The question certainly lies in the motives of Xavier Citizen. He has never been a man to do something without a plan and the unexpected acceptance of Pierce’s challenge has left many wondering just what his agenda is. Two weeks ago Jason Jameson was the Continental Champion. Shodan then returned and with a title shot still coming to him he defeated his one time friend to capture the title. Now Jameson wants the title back in his possession and he faces a mystery opponent in a mystery match this week in order to earn a rematch. Yet another plan put forth by the champion Shodan that Jason must overcome for a shot at the gold again. After all hell broke loose last week during their tag team match the new Commissioner Cameron Griffin made the stipulation that if either Impact or Sovereign came to blows outside of a sanctioned match then both men would be suspended. He then took it upon himself to book this four corners match as he combined the two announced matches for Nightmare Before Christmas so far into one massive four corners battle.
