Digital Destruction Transcript Live on Pay Per View! From TD Waterhouse Center ~ Orlando, Florida 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night September 18th, 2002 eVolution returns to pay per view for the third time as it presents Digital Destruction, live from the TD Waterhouse Center in Orlando, Florida! No pay per view has been as personal as this one has become for so many people. After being thrown off the air at the end of Legends there is no telling just what might happen here tonight as all the feuds and personal agendas will come to a climax in nine exciting matches! Two cruiserweights go at it in this contest as Python will take on Gerico in a dark match for Digital Destruction. Both men look to use the match as a stepping stone back to greater things in eVolution. A match that was originally scheduled to happen on Legends last week but was cancelled due to Splink Parp executives pressuring eVolution to tone down the already violent product. Obviously that didn’t work very well this week so the match had been rescheduled to occur at Digital Destruction. All three men will step into the ring for this battle and being on pay per view there isn’t a damn thing anyone can do to stop them. Jameson and Asis have been battling it out for weeks with Asis seeming to keep the upper hand with sneak attacks. Jameson got a small taste of revenge last week costing Asis a match but now he gets an even bigger opportunity presented to him. Asis will try to keep his Rising Star Title around his waist with the biggest challenge presented to him to date. The two former EdW members will take it to one another with only one of them being able to capture the title as Jameson also tries to teach Asis a bit of respect for those that helped make him, the EdW. The four top cruiserweights in eVolution bring their own brand of entertainment to Digital Destruction in this match which is almost certain to be an instant classic. Quasar will defend the High Impact Title against three other stars who all have come to hate him. Will his alliance with Thug roC be enough to help him retain the title or will one of his rivals finally avoid the Thug roC screw job to pick up the victory and walk out the champion? Shocka is the Continental Champion following a victory over Erin Christoph to capture the belt. Shodan believes he should be the champion as a week before Shocka’s title victory he had defeated him in a one on one match. Shodan tries to repeat his past feat and this time walk out with some gold to put around his waist while Shocka will be out to prove the first match a fluke. A match can’t get much more personal than this one has become. Months ago Davidson’s fiancée was murdered while he was off at a wrestling show. Two weeks ago Tyler found out just who the murderer was. To the shock of everyone it turned out to be his current employee, Erin Christoph. Now he gets a chance for revenge while Christoph will try and finish the job he started over six months ago as Davidson ordered the match to be fought with no rules there’s no telling just what the Unlimited may have planned to help one of their own. A rematch from the X-Rated Universal Title tournament comes about following Legends and Big Daddy Dreamer’s interference in Pierce’s title shot against Sovereign. Dreamer could be largely considered the reason as to why Pierce was not able to defeat Sovereign or continue on in the X-Rated Tournament to win the title. Twice Dreamer has screwed Pierce out of the Universal Title and now Pierce is looking for some payback of his own. Malcolm King and Xander, two men who hate each other with a passion battle in one violent match. The history has been well documented back to the days of OSW and EdW and now in eVolution it can all come to one final end. One of these men's careers will come to an end inside of the Cell structure normally reserved for the fighters of eVolution. These two find themselves pitted against each other one last time and only one will walk away. A match which had originally been scheduled as just Sovereign defending against Jamal Kage had been thrown into complete chaos over the course of the past two weeks. L2J and Matt Boone have both earned themselves shots at the Universal Title with victories over Jamal Kage and Sovereign respectively. As a result Daniel Somers was forced to add both men into the match making it a four corners match where the person to score the first pinfall will walk out with the most prized possession in the business.
