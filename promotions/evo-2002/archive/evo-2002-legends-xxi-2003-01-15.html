Live on Tru-TV! From The Savvis Center ~ St. Louis, Missouri 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night January 15th, 2003 eVolution was left stunned and saddened by the closing events of last week’s program and this week the company will push forward with its next show in St. Louis. In addition to the five booked matches the superstars of eVolution will take time to reflect back on the life of the fallen superstar, L2J. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! We saw both these men for the first time last week in separate points of the show. The mysterious Yoshii Tanaka had a battle with Marcus Escobar that was rudely interrupted by Luther Wisdom meanwhile London Rockwell comes to eVolution with quite a history and he has apparently already made a choice concerning the well being of Lee eXodus. Just what that choice is remains to be seen. Also thrown into the mix is another newcomer in Colombian, Vaikona de Volombo. Not a lot is known about this international star but he’s here in eVolution to try and make an impact. Last week Seara pulled off what many considered to be an upset victory as she defeated Lee eXodus and captured her first taste of eVolution gold by becoming the High Impact Champion. Now this week she goes against Sean Mason will the opportunity to unify the High Impact and Rising Star Titles into one championship. Mason is just coming off a huge and violent battle against Morpheus Omega which he pulled out a huge victory to retain the title. Now he faces the one woman who took him to his limits in the past. Shodan has once again disappeared into the darkness and as a result Daniel Somers vacated the Continental Title once again in place of a four man tournament to take place. In the first of the two matches, Asis makes his return to action with a one on one battle against Strictly Prohibited who has been stalking the entire Unlimited over the past several weeks. This will be his first chance at some gold in eVolution if he can find a way to get by the always violent Asis. In the second of the two tournament matches another member of the Unlimited finds himself granted a chance at the Continental Title as Angel is placed in a one on one battle with Big Daddy Dreamer. Dreamer makes his first appearance since Nightmare Before Christmas and must be keeping an eye over his shoulder with the returns of Katarina and Thomas Draven. Last week Frost was robbed of his chance at the Universal Title by Morpheus Omega but this week he gets a small chance at revenge as he finds himself teamed with Jason Jameson against Shocka and Sovereign. Jameson was the one man last week who stepped forward and stood his ground against Sovereign’s rampage and all out assault against Impact during their Cell match but the sanity of that move is definitely questionable. This week the two former Notorious members will team up against the current Universal Champion, and arguably the man who was the best Universal Champion in eVolution, Sovereign. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Troy Pierce, Xander, The HeartBreak Chick, and reflections from many of eVolution’s superstars on the life and times of L2J!
