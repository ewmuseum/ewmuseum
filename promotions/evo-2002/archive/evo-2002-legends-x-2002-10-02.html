Legends X Transcript Live on OSE! From The Lockhart Arena ~ Miami, Florida 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night October 2nd, 2002 Many shocks had occurred on Legends IX but X promises just as many if not more. The lucky five will be announced as five new superstars will officially be announced during the course of the show and might even be in action. The Tag Team Title Tournament will continue with a huge tag team match capping off the show in addition to the Continental Title being on the line. The man formerly known as Xavier Citizen makes his eVolution debut in a dark match. Going under the name ECHO BlackWhisper he will do battle with former Cell fighter Marcus Escobar in his first battle. This will be the first in a long road as ECHO tries and establishes in eVolution as a future star before his time runs out. The former High Impact Champion, Quasar the Great gets a shot to regain his title. If he wins this match against the hot newcomer, Gerico then next week he’ll get his rematch against HeartBreak Chick. If not then the title shot will go to Sean Mason. Gerico on the other hand will have the chance to make a huge impact in eVolution if he’s able to knock off the former champion in this huge match. A triple threat match signed after a demand from Asis to get his hands on the two people who have recently found themselves interfering in his matches. Both Eric Badger and the Masked Attacker have done so in the past two weeks but it was the masked one who had cost Asis the Rising Star Title against Matt Boone and you can be sure he’ll be gunning for some sort of revenge while Eric Badger continues his quest to try and eliminate Jason Jameson once and for all. Last week the world was shocked when the greatest team in EdW history came to a violent split when HeartBreak Chick turned on her long time partner and friend Big Daddy Dreamer to join the Unlimited. Now she teams with her newfound friends against Dreamer and three other men he has managed to recruit for this battle. Jason Jameson, Shodan, and Sean Mason will all stand by the side of Dreamer in this battle as all three men are well aware of just what The Unlimited can be capable of in the long run and look to stop them before it gets started. Tommy Michaels has now found himself placed on the hit list of Daniel Somers and the next man to try and take him out is Molten in his own environment. Michaels has never been a fan of hardcore action but this week he must find a way to survive in it against Molten or he will have to reveal all his plans to Daniel Somers. As an added bonus “The Natural” Matt Boone will also be at ringside commentating for this match as it was Michaels who cut short his attack on Troy Pierce at Digital Destruction. Shocka takes the chance to dodge Shodan and defend his Continental Title against a “worthy” opponent this week as he goes one on one with Python. A match which was set up by Thug roC mostly in order to keep the title and Shocka as far away from Shodan as they possibly can. Shodan isn’t a man to be deterred for very long however and will be on the lookout for Shocka. The unlikely duo of Sovereign and L2J team together in the main event as they find themselves entered into the Tag Team Title Tournament. On the opposite side of the ring opposing both men are Deacon and Eric Knight as determined by Daniel Somers. Knight has already been annihilated once by Sovereign and many believe a second time can’t be to far behind. Non Title ~ Dark Match ECHO BlackWhisper vs. Marcus Escobar Both Echo and Escobar are already in the ring for this starting match as the bell rings. The fans are on their feet already and cheering for the man now called Echo and formerly Xavier Citizen. He quickly starts the match off coming out after Escobar and catching him with a stiff right hand to the head. Echo backs off and comes charging in at Escobar and goes for a clothesline but it’s ducked by Marcus and he takes him over with a quick belly to belly suplex. Escobar gets back to his feet and catches the groggy Echo with a clothesline taking him out and over the top rope. Escobar quickly follows Echo out of the ring and drives a double axehandle down into his back. He reaches down and grabs hold of Echo lifting him back to his feet and then slamming him back first into the side of the ring as he begins working on the back. Escobar turns Echo around and holds him against the ring with his body weight before slamming a series of right hands into the lower back of Echo. He scoops Echo up into a back suplex and running forward he crotches him right into the steel ring post before letting Echo drop to the ground. Escobar picks him right back up and rolls him into the ring before following quickly in after him. Escobar climbs into the ring and quickly covers Echo as the referee makes the count. One……..two………kickout by Echo! Escobar gets back to his feet and lifts Echo up with him again. He sends Echo into the ropes and as he comes back he takes him over with a quick power slam and makes the cover again. One……..two………another kickout by Echo! Escobar looks up with a look of disgust and impatience growing on his face but quickly gets back to his feet. He grabs hold of both of Echo’s legs and begins turning him over into a Boston crab to try and finish the match off with a bit of extra pain and damage to the back. Escobar tries turning Echo but he keeps fighting the hold off trying his best to avoid being locked into it but Escobar finally manages to turn him. He turns Echo and applies the Boston crab but it’s to close to the ropes and Echo quickly grabs onto the bottom rope forcing the hold to be broken. Escobar breaks off of Echo and turns around driving another hard boot down into the back of Echo. He picks him up and sets Echo up for a suplex but it’s blocked by Echo who quickly manages to counter the move with a knee to the midsection and follows it quickly with a twist of fate and making the cover. One……..two……..Escobar just barely kicks out! Echo slowly gets back to his feet still feeling the effects of Escobar’s assault and he stumbles back into the ropes. Escobar gets back to his feet and he’s quickly taken right back down with a big clothesline from Echo. Echo picks Escobar right back up to his feet and hooks him up before driving him down to the mat with an uranage slam. Echo gets back to his feet quickly and lifts Escobar into a seated position. He steps around Escobar and locks in a modified Dragon Sleeper on him that he calls the Questionable Sanity. With the hold locked in tightly Escobar wastes no time in tapping out from the pain. Libby Bowman: Your winner by submission…….Echo BlackWhisper!
