From Perth, Australia ~ Burswood Dome 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night November 26th, 2003 In The Ring ~ Opening Statement Featuring Thug roC and Matt Hunt As the weekly Legends fireworks explode into the air, the crowd is going wild for the return of eVolution Wrestling. They are all on their feet when “Kickass Anthem” by Thug roC hits and they are slow to react to the new theme song. But the second Thug roC walks out the black curtain, the fans immediately boo him heavily. Thug roC, with his United Title over his shoulder, smiles as he looks out into the crowd. Thug roC marches down the ramp, huge smile across his face and even a new roC-TV shirt across his chest. He enters the ring through the ropes and gets up on the turnbuckles. He throws both his arms in the air, only getting boos from the crowd. When he gets down, he is given the microphone from Libby Bowman. “What the hell is the matter with you people?” Thug roC asks, only getting more boos. “I am your United Champion, you all should be giving me a standing ovation every time I come out, every time you hear my voice…hell, if you see me picture you should be bowing in silence.” The crowd doesn’t take to this well, just getting louder, trying to drown Thug roC’s mic out. “But I bet that there are some of you that didn’t even think I’d make it this far with the United Title. You probably thought that since Scott Evans is quote, ‘more athletic, and more talented’ that he was going to come out the match with MY United Title. Hold on, let me make sure you heard that Because it’s MY UNITED TITLE.” Thug roC pauses and the crowd is indeed chanting “Thug roC Sucks!” loudly. “I understand why you all are so upset.” Thug roC states. “I mean, you were all drastically wrong with the outcome of my match. Bookers around the world, placing bets on some washed up former cop like Scott Evans, thinking that he was better for the title then I am. Well guess what? You all were WRONG. I’m the best damn United Champion ever, without a shadow of a doubt. I did what everybody else in that locker room does every night.” Thug roC continues to pace around the ring. “I sat down, I thought about it, and I came up with a plan. And if you think that my plan was cheating, then you can take your ass somewhere else. My plan was 100% pure genius.” Thug roC says mockingly, “Oh, but…but…Evans hit you with a cross body. But didn’t that DVD off the ladder hurt.” At the mention of the DVD, the crowd gets excited. Thug roC looks around the arena to the fans who obviously loved the moves. “Let me tell you all something. Nowhere in there did I say the ‘almost won column’ or the ‘I tried my best column’ NO. So you fools can cheer all you want about how Scott Evans threw some dirt in my eyes to complete a move like that, I don’t care. As Matt Hunt emerges from the beyond the curtain, the arena erupts into a cheering frenzy as the focused The Postman stands at the top of the aisle way with a mic in hand. “Greetings camache…” Hunt starts, which draws a reaction from the crowd. “Yes I was sitting in the back, yes I was hearing you go on and on and on and yes, I got sick and tired and decided to show my face” Hunt starts with a determined expression. “Wh…” roC attempts to say. “Hold your noise, mooch!” but Hunt cuts him off, which sends the fans into another cheering frenzy. The United Champion is beside himself in the ring and grits his teeth in disbelief. “So you’re still the United Champion after defending it in one hell of a Ladder Match…” Hunt pauses and places the mic underneath his arm so he can mock roC and clap. He also starts to make his way down the aisle and towards the ring as an irate Thug roC prepares himself, Hunt resuming the talk. “That’s what you wanted isn’t it, a standing ovation? But he didn’t. Jason Jameson looked around, his body aching from wounds he never took in battles he never fought. His head throbbed and his sight was a bit hazy, but he knew where he was. He knew where he was going. He knew what he wanted to say. And then that would be it. He walked down the ramp, limping slightly as his feet shot sharp points of pain through them violently. Jason looked up at the ring, the haven he had not seen in months, and rolled under the bottom rope. He looked down at stains of blood he didn’t know was his own. His music died down, and his hands gripped the microphone despite the soreness within his fingers. The fans still booed. “Listen. “Listen to me. “Because I have one thing to say and then you will never see me again.” Some of the fans cheered, while the majority took on a feeling of shock. Eventually all of their voices calmed into a faint grumble. His lights strained and winced under the lights, but he never stopped scanning the crowd. “Whether you believe me or not, I have not been here for awhile.” Fans booed out loud, remembering quite well that he had been there to cause many of their beloved idols pain, both physical and emotional. But others watched, confused and waiting for his follow up comment. “The doctor’s believe that something happened to me that may or may not have happened to anyone before. Something with multiple personalities conflicting with one another, where one lay dormant while the other… well, while the other lived and controlled everything. “And from the response I have just received, that one personality wasn’t taken very nicely. “I’m sorry for anything that ‘he’ did while I was gone. I wish I could take it all back.” The fans didn’t respond. A sick mix of appall and past admiration came together, and they waited to see if this was in fact the truth. “Because of this, I have been ordered to recover and take it easy. I figured that I could have come back after awhile, when I was ready to face this again. I could come back and make up for what has happened in the passed few weeks. “But I have decided that I am going to leave wrestling behind for good.” Still, a shock of disbelief filled the crowd. They didn’t know what to make of the situation, and even more importantly didn’t realize the validity of what he was saying. But they all paid attention, for this was either an elaborate trick taking place before their very eyes or it was just that. The final words of Jason Jameson. “You know, I can still remember that feeling I had when I stepped into this ring for the first time.” His head hanged low, and his eyes began to glimmer as tears began to build up. “EdW was a big step forward for me, and I was glad to be there. I came down into this ring, and took on such men as Cori Simmons, Brad Kelley, Josh Sutton, Shodan and others. Before it was over, I wore the Platinum Championship around my waste, and was fortunate enough to be Era’s last Platinum champion. “I never told anyone this before – the last thing I did with that belt was laid it down in the middle of this ring, thinking I would never step foot inside again. I didn’t think I would ever wrestle inside these ropes again. “I didn’t realize that this ring would evolve.” Jason quickly wiped away a single tear before any of the fans could see it. The camera caught the moment anyway. He looked up at the fans, who were now listening, hatred slowly diminishing. At least he hoped so. That was the first time he realized that he loved all of them. Before this moment he would look at them and see nothing but pitiful lives, wasting away for no reason at all. Now he knew that a piece of them was going to die in that ring that very evening. A part of him wasn’t going to leave that arena. “I came here, and once again I felt at home. I had incredible matches with incredible men – Scott Frost, Shodan, Sovereign, James Kimson, Asis, Shocka, Xander, Morpheus Omega, Troy Pierce and countless others. I made friendships that I hope will never end. I can happily say that Loudon St. Hill was one of my best friends. “Evolution, for reasons beyond me, was not just a job. This federation was something more then that to me, and I’m just beginning to realize this now. After a hard life, filled with what I thought was completely deprived of even the slightest of hope, Evolution was a piece of me that I will carry with me forever. “It will always be apart of me. “Thank you. “For everything.” Jason dropped down the microphone, and looked around the arena as a million hands came together and a million voices cheered for him. A smile went across his face, and another tear inched out of his eye. He didn’t bother to wipe this one away. Instead of the sounds of Metallica, another song took its place. Jason climbed down the steps. He began to walk up the ramp as a young, attractive young woman walked down to take his hand and walk beside him. The fans didn’t know who she was, but Jason knew her as Marilee Ambrose. Soon to be Marilee Jameson. An arena cheered for him – they had just seen one of the greatest men they would ever know for the last time. A beautiful woman held his arm – she would love him for the rest of her life. An enemy was buried away – he would never interfere in his life again. A federation was saying goodbye – it would never forget the impact he had. And as for Jason Jameson? He found the happy ending he was always looking for. ----- Breeze still carries the sound Maybe I'll disappear Try to fade in the snow You won't find me here Ice is starting to form Ending what had begun I am locked in my head With what I've done I know you tried to rescue me Didn't let anyone get in Left with a trace of all that was And all that could have been Please Take this And run far away Far away from me I am tainted The two of us Were never meant to be All these pieces And promises and left behinds If only I could see In my nothing You meant everything Everything to me Gone Fading Everything And all that could have been Could have been Please Take this And run far away Far as you can see I am tainted And happiness and peace of mind Were never meant for me All these pieces And promises and left behinds If only I could see In my nothing You meant everything Everything to me “And All That Could Have Been” by Nine Inch Nails Credits: Opening Statement: Matt and Rob All Gone Part 1: Eoin All Gone Part 2: Eoin Welcome Back: Rob Reflection: Matt All Gone Part 3: Eoin Meeting of the Minds: Jake All Gone Part 4: Eoin Reaction: Matt All Gone Part 5: Eoin Reunion: Jake Kramer Wild Wing vs. WeedSmoker: S.
