"If I could get away with it I'd send him to his Grandfather. But I'm afraid I'd get fired or something like that. Kage, you have become an interest of mine... Jamal Kage is going to pretty soon find out what I believe... and that might not be something that HE'S looking forward to discovering!" Monica Angel sits on the set of 10 count after that promo footage airs of current Universal Champion Sovereign.... 'Hello this is Monica Angel and welcome to our second addition of Answer the 10 Count. I am joined here today by noted Dr. Cassius Owens. Dr. Owens called me because he was intrigued the mental state of Our Current Universal Champion....Dr. welcome." Dr.CO: "Thank you Monica for having me...." MA: "Well based on the first clip what is your opinion on Sovereign?" Dr.CO: "Actually Mr. Sovereign's condition if it is a condition at all.....is not that uncommon." MA: "What are you saying Dr. do you think this is an act?" Dr.CO: "Well isn't everything in your line of work? I mean come on.....what makes people want to get into the ring with someone that is that unstable...that bloodthirsty." MA: "Dr. When we booked you for this show it wasn't to defend Pro Wrestling but to provide analysis on the metal condition of our Universal Champion.....I quite frankly am insulted that you would use this oppr...." Dr.CO: "Then why have I been denied one on one access with this so called monster? What does your company fear it being exposed for the sham that it is?" MA: "Actually sir it was for your own protection...." Monica visibly annoyed motions for the booth to role the other footage...... " Who says I want to be any different!! I have everything I’ve dreamed of! I have money... I am the Universal Champion... something that an entire group of men would die to get that chance... and it’s mine. I have it all and you think that I should open my eyes to what? So that I can be healthy? So that I can live a 9 to 5 kiss the wife and kids life! I’m sorry Max Dickens... but the world that I live in is far more entertaining then asking other people questions... driving around on a Sunday morning to church. Picnics on Saturday Afternoon with the rest of your life either sleeping or working! Why would I want that...? Why would I want to compromise all I’ve gained because why? Because I’m insane? You judge me so quickly don’t you Dickens. Because I’m crazy, I’m an instant push aside... keep Sovereign out of reach... don’t let him talk to you. Have I hurt you Max? Have I tried to hurt you? Your afraid that I’m going to, I can feel it, I can see it in your eyes that you think that at any moment I might lash out at you. But have I? I haven’t because I look at you Dickens and I see someone that you... your not even worth it. Sitting there, you might even just piss your pants if I made any sudden movements. It’s all about control and you... I own you right now I can tell just by the smell of you." MA: "Dr. certainly that could at least provide you with a little more objective insight to our champion?" Dr.CO: "All it proves to me is that the man/character Sovereign is playing his role to a T. He seems to be a well versed and educated man. Yet he reverts to the testosterone driven, school yard bully mentality. He is right it's all about control, he gives you the impression that he's in control...but really is nothing more than a Pavlovian dog reacting to the proper stimuli." MA: "Are you saying that he should be more destructive than in weeks past? You've seen the tape on him....how can you condone some of his actions?" Dr.CO: "And the Production on those tapes were top notch I might add, it almost had me convinced..." MA: "You are not a wrestling fan are you Dr.?" Dr.CO: "Most certainly not I find this whole exercise trivial and barbaric.....trailer park in it's appeal, and it's individuals as your Sovereign who do your alleged sport more harm than good." Monica is fuming as she barely gets out..... MA: "Roll the final cut..." " And to be perfectly honest, as much as it pains me to have this title, here in front of me. Even though it’s Sovereign’s it is mine also. It hurts that I did what I did, but at the same time, the conflict inside of me is stronger then it ever has been before. Sovereign grows stronger, his will is making me quake in fear. And while many don’t fear Sovereign as much as they rather avoid him, I know him and I fear him more then anyone else in the world. Do you people, my friends have any idea what this has done to him? What it’s done to me? Here’s the problem. Sovereign’s world is very simple, and with this Title everything has been blown wide open. He’s the Universal Champion, the most important man in Evolution Wrestling and all eyes will be upon him. He isn’t the hunter anymore, he’s the hunted. His paranoia will start to take new manifestations. If anyone thought that you were safe before, then you should all, please listen to me when I say that Sovereign will now be more dangerous then ever before. I’m not saying that because [name withheld] will back him up, I’m not saying that just because he has renewed confidence. He is now willing to take back everything that he wants; his crusade will enter a new era. Sovereign, the Human monster will be more then that from now on, and I can feel him under my skin, crawling, digging trying to get out and move on his ambitions. And even though he isn’t scheduled to be there on Wednesday, he will be at Legends, and not to make a speech, not to be crowned but to continue his work. He’s truly a monster now, a golden monster with an agenda to continue his reign. His reign of terror and I have pity on everyone that isn’t smart enough to get the hell out of his way!" Dr.CO: "Classic.....this Sovereign, an apparent conflicted soul offers you an alter ego that begs to be sympathetic. He screams to not be blamed for his actions. Yet feels the need to offer apologies as if remorseful." MA: "Look I can't take this anymore...this interview is over...." Dr.CO: "Are you upset that I exposed your business for what it really is....." MA: "No I just hate talking to ignorant assholes as yourself. We book you on this show for you to provide some sort of insight into our Universal Champion. Instead I get some condescending bullshit on my profession in general. For all your degrees and dissertations the best you can tell me is that Sovereign is not acting violent enough? So you tell me doctor who exactly is earning their money here? Those men who put their lives on the line to entertain the fans night in and night out? Or you who sit behind a desk and computer and type out bullshit that gets disproven two years later?" Suddenly a stage hand gives Monica a piece of paper. She smiles as she turns to the Dr. with a smirk on her face. She hands him the paper and settles back in her chair.... MA: "Be careful Dr. of what you wish for....you may get it. What you hold in your hands is a waiver. You want to interview and evaluate Sovereign.....sign that and you get to see the man up close and personal. If you survive I hope to have you back on the show to discuss your findings." Dr. Owens face turns blank as he seems like he just seen his life flash before his eyes..... MA: "Well tonight’s show was about Examining Sovereign. Although we were not able to delve deeply into the psyche of the man who is our champion...we know this he's a conflicted and tortured soul; a contradiction. So maybe the best way to understand him......is to accept that we may never understand him. I'm sure the good Dr. here may agree after a one on one session with Sovereign. We close tonight with the 10 count....he asked the fans who would be the next Universal Champion?" 1.Ron from GA:"I think either Malcolm King or Jamal Kage; because wrasslin has always found a way to hold the black man down; so it's time for these brothers to take matters into their own hands." 2.Adrian from SF:" Quasar the Great; I mean look at him he's highly underrated. Those eyes...that physique, he needs a belt around his waist in my opinion." 3.Sean form MN: "The King of the Dildo Smack, Shocka....what more reason do you need?" 4.Amy from NC: "Shocka, he's really the only reason why I watch Evolution. In fact when they come to Carolina I have a special surprise for him; you see a court order prevents me from leaving state which is why when they come here I can show my favorite wrestler how much I love him...and keep him safe from all those other men trying to hurt my Shocka...." 5.Booker from TX: "Jamal Kage....no doubt. He has the skill and the determination it takes to bring it to the next level. Plus it'll show my boss it's okay to take a chance on a black man in a position of power, if not once maybe 5 times!" 6.Tina from CA: "Heartbreak Chick...what a woman can't win the title? I find that highly discriminatory and insulting." 7.Kurt from PA:"I think Matt Boone will win the belt. He's an exceptionally gifted athlete whose star is rising...." 8.Khris Kougar from TX:"L2J.....because that bastard owes me money." 9.unidentified woman: “I think Malcolm King.....now are you sure the fact that I'm his wife won't come out on camera?" 10. Erin Christoph: “I think Erin Christoph will be the next Universal Champion...why? Don't ask stupid questions like why....just accept my answer and know it'll happen sooner rather than later."
