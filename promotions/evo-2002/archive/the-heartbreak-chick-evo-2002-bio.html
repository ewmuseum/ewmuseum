The HeartBreak Chick Biography Real Name Ashley [name withheld] Nicknames The Heartbreaker, HBC, First Lady of Evolution Hometown Toronto, Canada Height 5-11 Weight 140 Fed Titles eVo High-Impact Champion (x1), eVo Tag Team Champion (x4) Fed Awards Winner of the Crusierweight Battleroyal Biography The Heart Break Chick (HBC) was born 1978 in Toronto, Ontario, Canada to a wealthy international shipping tycoon and a former vogue Covergirl model. During her formative years, HBC went to prestigious private schools in Toronto, and Oxford, England where she excelled in athletics, languages and history. At her time in Oxford, HBC passionately took up sports, especially gymnastics, swimming, and martial arts. It was during this time, she competitively competed in those sports and became somewhat of a national sports figure becoming one of the youngest people ever to win major national titles on two continents. HBC sporting endeavors continued to blossom by becoming proficient in every sport she tried. No matter the sport, she become nationally ranked within a year. Several major sporting academies were eyeing her progress as well as her home country of Canada. In 1995, at the age of 17, Canada proclaimed her the best all-around athlete under the age of eighteen and was heavily recruited by major U.S. Universities as well as the Canadian Olympic Team. Her parents were definitely proud of their only daughter. At the age of 18, HBC took a sudden career step at the University of Toronto. On one fateful day while having lunch and studying at the base of the CN Tower, a photographer introduced himself and immediately signed her to a modeling contract. HBC, always up for a new adventure, decided to put her schooling on hold and jumped at the chance. After all her mom was a highly successful model and followed in her foot steps. HBC’s whirl-wind modeling tour placed her on every continent with shows all across the globe, thousands of magazine covers, including her mother’s favorite, Vogue. However, on one spiteful day in Paris, France, the photographer who she’d had been working with, decided to turn the tables against HBC and tried to rape her during a shoot. HBC, though proficient in martial arts and several other forms of combat, tried to reason with the photographer. But at the age of 19, models were expendable and tried to reason with him. Unfortunately, he wasn’t having anything of it. With no choice but to defend herself, HBC attacked and restrained the photographer. Then it happened, she snapped… HBC beat the living hell out of her photographer, who in turned was rushed to the hospital. At the hospital, the photographer died from internal injuries, and the hunt began for HBC with Parisian authorities in hot pursuit. At her parents bequest, she turned herself in and stood trial for the beating death. Although she was cleared of all charges as it was ruled in self-defense. The experience haunted her for some time. Face with a internal dilemma, HBC went to Sweden to find solace with some friends she met during her sporting and modeling career. While in Sweden, she would find her life radically changed on two occasions. The first life altering experience was the news she received while skiing outside the slopes of Stockholm. HBC received an international telex informing her that her parents died in the South China Sea on a shipping accident. Distraught, HBC immediately flew to Hong Kong, to grieve for her parents. Now HBC was really confused. As the solo heiress of a multi-billion dollar international company, and still grieving over her fallen parents, she did what most, ok, some people would do…. she went to a Kickboxing exhibition at the New China Tower. It was here, HBC met a competitor calling himself AKage. AKage pulled some strings and got HBC entered in the tournament. For several grueling rounds, HBC kicked and punched her way to the ladies tournament championship, releasing all the anger and frustration over her modeling career, the death of parents, and the new found responsibility over TransGlobal Shipping, her father’s company. All the lawyers wrangling came out in the finals as with one superkick, her opponent was leveled unconscious. No one in the history of the tournament had ever knocked out the 4-time defending champion, until now. AKage was very impressed and pulled HBC aside. Though excited by the victory, more pressing matters remained. She did not want the company right now. After all, she was only 20 and though with a worldly sense about her, she was unsure of her business sense, but she kept it to herself. AKage impressed by what he had seen. A beautiful cover model with a vicious, very proficient martial arts background, he asked her if she ever considered being a pro wrestler, after all, that is what he was training to be. She causally replied, I’ve watched but I don’t think its me, I have other things on my mind. AKage, determined, told her of a training school in her hometown of Toronto and that she should go check it out. It was at this school, her life would change for a second time. Flying home to Toronto, the estate seemed very empty as HBC spent much a night crying over her loss. With business matter’s pressing, she halted the company on a four-month freeze to give her time to think. With the constant barrage of phonecalls, letters and contracts coming into the place, HBC left one night and began walking along the shores of Lake Ontario. After coming around a corner in the warehouse district, she came upon an old warehouse with lights and ruckious good time happening. She peered through the door and much to her surprise, it was a wrestling school where everyone seem to be having a great time. She looked around and saw a lot of men and women competing with each other, against each other and having a good time afterwards. It was the comaradary she was missing and missing desperately. She cautiously entered and was immediately spotted. As she was about to leave, the door was slammed behind her. Nervously, she asked if this was the Big Time Wrestling School. They replied that it was and wondered who she was and how’d did she know. Before she could answer, the person in the ring climbed out and stood before HBC, recognizing her. The man was Big Daddy Dreamer, their finest student and one of the instructors. Dreamer informed the group that this was the person with a kick from hell that AKage told us about. HBC seemed to be relived of hearing the name of AKage for a second. HBC paraphrased her story to the group, and then they welcomed her to the school. HBC left out the financial part of her history though preferring to remain quiet about that. Dreamer put her in the ring with another student calling herself Miss Extreme: Katarina. Katarina competed on the indy circuit, winning several titles but was after awhile floored by HBC’s Superkick. Dreamer was very impressed and decided to take HBC upon his wing personally. Two-month’s had past as HBC and Big Daddy Dreamer were becoming the best of friends. On a cold wintery night, HBC confessed to Dreamer her life story and broke down on his shoulder. It was after this moment, Dreamer finally decided to tell her a hidden story of her father, that it was indeed her father who was financing the wrestling school. HBC couldn’t believe it and was very shocked! But it was true, Dreamer pulled out his laptop showing the reseats of the construction, building and equipment cost, all paid for by TransGlobal. She was speechless, but even more so when Dreamer handed her a sealed letter from her father addressed to her. Dreamer went into the next room to give HBC some privacy. The letter stated how proud he had been of her, her accomplishments, and that one day he knew he’d be handing over the company to her, but wasn’t sure if she would accept it. The letter listed several pet projects of his that he was funding and the school was indeed one of them. HBC in a way, felt home again, a sense of purpose that was lacking. HBC continued reading the letter and a line struck her hard: the company is yours, to do whatever you see fit with it, keep it, sell it, do whatever it is that will make you the most happiest because that is how I feel about you. But keep the projects listed afloat always, they mean a lot to me. HBC promised she would and called Dreamer back in the room, and gave him a big hug and kiss. The next day, HBC called the TransGlobal board of directors together, who were expecting to take over, after all how many now 21 year old can run a multi-national billion dollar company. Much to their surprise and shock, HBC fired them all and renamed TransGlobal into the ‘Syndicate’. Now with complete power and control over the company, and most importantly confidence, HBC named Big Daddy Dreamer, president of an affiliate within the company, ‘Syndicate International’. HBC, for the first time in two years, smiled like a giddy school-girl and pumped $50 million dollars into the Big Time Wrestling School and renamed it, Syndicate International Training Haven under the direct tutelage of Miss Extreme: Katarina. With the company now in safe direct control, the Syndicate has been continuely branching out into various global outlets whose empire now rivals her father’s at his height. But most importantly, HBC found solace in herself again with her friends, and though president of a very successful company, HBC finds the most happiest competing in the middle of the ring with Big Daddy Dreamer and all her old school buddies.
