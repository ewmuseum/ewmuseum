Legends LXI, 1 January 2004

1 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live on Tru-TV! From Melbourne, Australia ~ Telstra Dome 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night January 1st, 2004 Non-Title ~ Standard Match Chris Jeopardy vs. Ethan M. Coles Non-Title ~ Standard Match Shocka vs. Johnny Charisma Non-Title ~ Eight Person Tag Team Match The Syndicate and The Industry vs. St. Violence and The Faithless All eight people were already introduced for the match and everyone left the ring except for Big Daddy Dreamer and Marcus Escobar who decide to start things off for their own teams. The bell rings and the two men lock up in the ring before Marcus quickly tosses the Dreamer off and down to the mat giving an impressive display of strength. Dreamer smiles and nods at Marcus before slowly getting back to his feet and trying for the lock up again. This time though Dreamer strikes first lifting a knee into the midsection of Marcus. He follows it up slamming an elbow down right across the back of Marcus’ head and taking him down with a quick spinning neckbreaker after it. Dreamer rolls Marcus over onto his back and begins trying to apply an armbreaker submission to him but Marcus is able to overpower him and keep the hold from being locked in. Both men get back to their feet in the ring and Dreamer charges at Marcus with a clothesline but he ducks it and turns around catching Dreamer with a right hand. He rocks him back and then whips him into the ropes before ducking down for a back body drop. Before Marcus can execute the move though, Dreamer counters and grabs Marcus before driving him down jaw first on his knee. Marcus bounces back up in pain before being taken down with a big clothesline by Dreamer who quickly goes for the cover. One….. Two…… Kickout by Marcus! Dreamer gets back to his feet quickly and kicks Marcus while he’s down before going to the corner and tagging in QBX. QBX steps into the ring as Marcus is getting back to his feet and drives a hard stomp down onto him. QBX reaches down and picks Marcus back to his feet before setting him up for a suplex. QBX goes to lift him but Marcus blocks the move and counters with a suplex of his own taking QBX over. Marcus floats over and immediately follows up firing a right hand into the skull of QBX before getting back to his feet. Marcus waits behind QBX as he begins getting up and quickly moves in on him setting him up and taking him over with a back suplex. Marcus grabs hold of the leg of QBX and begins going for a figure four but before he can lock the move in QBX kicks him off and sends him right into the opposing corner where Dreamer is waiting and nails him with a right hand. QBX gets back to his feet and catches the staggering Marcus with a kick to the midsection before planting him in the ring with a DDT. QBX rolls quickly over and immediately goes for a Crossface submission but Marcus counters and hits a series of back elbows on QBX setting himself loose. Marcus quickly begins crawling towards his corner trying to make the tag but his leg is caught by QBX. Before QBX can pull him back at all though he is suddenly taken down by a furious clothesline from Deacon illegally entering the ring. The ref quickly tries getting in the way and getting Deacon out of the ring until a tag can be made. Marcus makes it to the corner finally and he makes the tag bringing Knight into the ring. Knight comes in and meets head on with QBX nailing him with a right hand. Knight backs QBX into the ropes and whips him in before catching him with a dropkick to the jaw as he comes back. Knight gets back to his feet and turns away from QBX nailing Dreamer with a right hand knocking him off the apron before turning to WeedSmoker who puts his hands up and backs away giving Knight a clear way. He turns back and drives a boot into the rising QBX before picking him up and going for a body slam but QBX floats down and grabs Knight slamming him to the mat with a full nelson slam. QBX falls back against the ropes and rests for a moment against them as Knight is laid out on the mat. He comes back off the ropes and drives a knee down onto the throat of Knight. QBX goes to his corner and tags in WeedSmoker before picking Knight back up to his feet as WeedSmoker enters. QBX holds Knight leaving him wide open for WeedSmoker who winds back for a shot but misses Knight and nails QBX instead. WeedSmoker looks at Knight and then at QBX and Dreamer on the outside before locking up with Knight. The two lock up before Knight takes WeedSmoker over with an armdrag takedown and follows it with a running clothesline knocking WeedSmoker into the corner. He drives a boot into his midsection before WeedSmoker suddenly counters grabbing Knight and tossing him into the corner. WeedSmoker fires away with a series of rights knocking Knight down to the mat before lifting him up and placing him on the top rope. WeedSmoker climbs up after him and begins to set him up for a top rope hurricanrana but as he does he doesn’t see Deacon make the tag to Knight and come into the ring. WeedSmoker takes Knight off the top rope with the hurricanrana but as soon as he gets back to his feet he is met with a big boot right to the head. Deacon immediately begins pounding away on WeedSmoker not giving him a chance to get back to his feet. Deacon reaches down and lifts him back up before pressing WeedSmoker over his head. He lets WeedSmoker go and he falls right into position for a front slam as Deacon drives him down to the mat hard and goes for the cover. One….. Two….. WeedSmoker just barely gets a shoulder up! Deacon gets back to his feet shooting a harsh glare at the referee before turning his attention back to WeedSmoker. He lifts him up and whips him into the ropes before going for a powerslam but WeedSmoker manages to counter it and using his momentum flips over Deacon and goes for a neckbreaker. Deacon powers out of it though and sends WeedSmoker into the ropes again and goes for a clothesline this time but WeedSmoker ducks it though and takes Deacon down from behind with a neckbreaker. WeedSmoker gets back to his feet and runs into the ropes and comes down onto Deacon with a somersault splash and goes for the cover. One….. Two……. Kickout by Deacon! WeedSmoker gets back to his feet quickly and continues stomping on Deacon trying to keep him from getting up. Deacon still powers his way up though and takes a swing at WeedSmoker but he ducks and quickly rolls under his arm to come out behind him and make the tag to HBC. HBC comes into the ring but runs right into the outstretched arm of Deacon. HBC falls to the mat hard as Deacon begins catching his breath and going back to work on her. Deacon picks HBC up and goes for a suplex but HBC floats over behind him and catches him with a low blow from behind. HBC gets back to her feet and continues stomping on Deacon as he slowly powers himself back to his feet. HBC drives another fist down into the skull of Deacon as he gets back up. Coming into the ring Dreamer slams a forearm into the back of Deacon’s head. Together Dreamer and HBC begin double teaming Deacon with rights and lefts before whipping him into the ropes and driving him to the mat with a double spinebuster. As soon as the move is hit though Marcus and Geneva both come into the ring and go right after Dreamer and HBC. Knight remains the only one not involved in the fight until he suddenly hops down off of the apron and grabs a steel chair from ringside. He slides into the ring and just as WeedSmoker turns around he slams the chair down across the skull of WeedSmoker dropping him to the mat. Deacon takes QBX out as everything breaks down completely. Knight with the chair still in hand slams it down across the head of Dreamer next knocking him from the ring before tossing the chair down and turning his sights on HBC. HBC was preoccupied with Geneva who is against the ropes but is caught by Knight who quickly locked her into The Blackout. Knight keeps the hold locked on before the chair finally finds a spot across his back thanks to Dreamer. Dreamer slams the chair down on Knight again before throwing it at him and then dropkicking him in the head. Knight rolls out of the ring in pain as Dreamer locks down on the already hurt Geneva. Dreamer grabs hold of Geneva and suddenly locks her into the Dreamer Crossface as he takes her down onto the mat with it. Geneva quickly begins tapping out from the pain as Dreamer pulls back with the hold locked in tight. The Syndicate had just picked up a hectic victory for themselves and The Industry in this big eight person tag team battle.
