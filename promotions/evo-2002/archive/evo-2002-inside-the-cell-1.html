Inside The Cell #1 by Jeremy Hunter This is Jeremy Hunter here with my first official piece of writing for the eVolution company in addition to my duties as company spokesman. For my first duty I’m going to sit back and take a look now at the newly reinstated Cell Division of eVolution. In this article I’ll take a look at each of the men currently slated to take part in the division as well as the possibility of other faces that just might enter. I’ll start from the first people signed and work my way down. Eric Knight Height: 6’ Weight: 205lbs Eric was the first ever fighter signed to eVolution and has been with the company since day one. Despite that he has never been able to truly succeed within the company. There were a few times where it looked like he might be on the edge of winning a big one but in the end he came falling right back down again. With that said Eric has always been at his best when placed inside of the confines of the Cell. With the return of the Cell Division I personally think we will see bigger things from Eric in the very near future. Style Eric relies primarily on his submission skills in the Cell and uses a modified dragon sleeper as his finisher. He is also quite an intelligent man inside and outside of the Cell and has demonstrated many times in the past just how sneaky he can really be. With his light size Eric is also extremely quick and can use it to his advantage in many situations he finds himself in. Strengths Deacon: The past relationship between these two men has been well known as they were both former members of [name withheld] crack security team. Since then the two have formed an alliance of their own and if he can find a way Deacon will most certainly do what he can to aid Eric Knight in achieving his goals. Hunger: After months and months of either inactivity or disappointment Eric Knight is ready to get back into action and I’m certain he’s also dying to finally prove himself to everyone once and for all and most importantly to finally prove himself to himself. Weaknesses Lack of size: As mentioned earlier Eric Knight is not a large guy at all and as a result he has to rely on his speed to get by most of the time. While he still packs some power in his punches and kicks you won’t see him get many knockout victories and with the lack of room for maneuvering around inside the Cell he will have a hard time surviving against some of the stronger men. Past Failures: Eric has failed so many times in the past now that it might be a habit he simply can’t break any longer. With any luck Eric will be able to put this all behind him but there are certainly no guarantees when it comes to that. Trevor Norman Height: 5’11 Weight: 244lbs Trevor is one of the more recent signings to eVolution and was the first to sign on for the Cell Division once it was reinstated. So far he has shown himself to be a perfect fit for the division as in each of his matches he has demonstrated a ruthlessness that will take him a very long way in the Cell. Nik VanCore, Lee eXodus, and Xero have all already felt just how vicious Trevor Norman can be and I’m sure there will be quite a few more in the coming weeks and months that will also find out the hard way. Style Trevor mixes together several different fighting styles including kick boxing and street fighting. Add onto that he has also proven a good ability at fighting in the ring as well as the Cell which makes him a threat in more than one way. He’s larger than most of the other Cell fighters and as a result packs a hell of a punch which he has already demonstrated in a knockout victory over Xero. Strengths Vicious: As mentioned previously Trevor has a knack for being vicious. With the way Cell Fights are fought that viciousness is going to take him a very long way. If for some reason he finds himself in a tough position he can easily fight his way out of it and would more than likely take the first chance afforded to him to injure any opponent. Confident: If you ask Trevor if there is anyone out there that can beat him he’ll be quick to answer no. After he gives you that answer he would more than likely kick the shit out of you as well however. If he is that confident that he can beat someone than who is going to be able to tell him otherwise? Not very many people will care to take that challenge. Weaknesses Status: Before the Cell Division was ever reinstated Trevor had already been making a name for himself in eVolution. Normally that wouldn’t be to bad a thing but with a bunch of new faces coming in now to compete against him in the Cell he is going to have quite a large target on his back. As good as Trevor is sooner or later someone is going to come up with the plan needed to take him out. Whether or not it will be for good or not is the question. Question Beyond X Height: 6’4 Weight: 266 There is very little known about QBX so far other than there seems to be some sort of relationship between him and Malcolm King. Just what this relationship is all about remains a mystery but it certainly can’t do anything to hurt the career of QBX in the Cell Division and it’s something he’s going to need. He has apparently already found himself on the bad side of Ali Khadafi and Malcolm King can more than likely offer some form of protection that QBX could definitely benefit from. Style QBX isn’t the typical fighter you would expect to see on the inside of the Cell. While most of the men who fight inside of it have experienced the world of no holds barred fighting in the past and use their striking techniques or submission holds to pick up victories the same really can’t be said for QBX. QBX is better defined as a wrestler than as a fighter so what he is doing stepping into The Cell is a question to be asked at a later date. Strengths Unorthodox: As previously mentioned QBX is more of a wrestler than of a fighter. In some cases this could prove to be quite a disadvantage for the man but it seems more likely that that difference in his style compared to the others could be quite an asset for him and it would be something that the other fighters might not be prepared for. Malcolm King: There is no one man who has enjoyed more success in this business than Malcolm King. The man has been a mainstay in the business for years and in eVolution despite [name withheld] best attempts to get rid of him. Malcolm can probably offer quite a lot to a young rising star like QBX and provided the young man is willing to listen to him it will take him a very long way. Weaknesses Sanity: I’m not quite convinced that QBX is all there in the head. This is only a problem for him because I don’t think he’ll truly know when to say enough is enough and give up. If that ends up being the case his career could easily be one of the shortest lived ones in the history of eVolution if he crosses paths with the wrong person.
