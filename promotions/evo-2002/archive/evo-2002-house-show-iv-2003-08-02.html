House Show IV, 2 August 2003

2 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
House Show Results Standard Match Aaron Justice Vs. Yoshii Tanaka A great battle to start the night off where two great athletes went at it full-steam. Yoshii showed his normal highflying moves to the England crowd, but it didn’t take long for Aaron Justice to use his power to ground the highflying attack. The match went on for a little while before Aaron Justice got the 3-count with the Final Justice for the win. Winner via pinfall: Aaron Justice. Standard Match Carl Ca$h vs. Shawn Shafton In a grueling battle with a lot of hard shots and kicks, Carl Ca$h seemed to be a dud in this competition. Shawn Shafton aka Shaft-Man, showed his talents very well in his second house show match. Shafton pulled out the win with a hard superkick followed by a 450-leg drop from the top rope. Shaft-Man got a warm welcome as he watched the defeated Carl Ca$h walk up the ramp. Winner via pinfall: Shaft-Man Standard Tag Match Non-Title The Syndicate Vs. Sean Mason and Ryan Mason A great match that had the fans behind it all the way. Both teams had nothing but cheers from the crowd in this non-title tag team match. Sean Mason though, showed some ring rust, but still had a great showing. The match was back and forth with quick tags on both sides. HBC connected with a hard superkick to Continental Champion Ryan Mason, but the pin was broken up by Sean Mason. Big Daddy fell victim to a top rope downward spiral from Ryan Mason, and it was HBC who was there to break it up. A big spot was seen when both Sean and Ryan Mason hit a double superplex on Big Daddy Dreamer while HBC was being detained by the referee. HBC and Ryan Mason got tangled up in a battle and found themselves toppling over the top rope, leaving BDD and Sean Mason in the ring. Mason went for a sunset flip on BDD but BDD sat down on him and hooked both legs to get the quick 3-count. The fans still loved the match though, giving all 4 wrestlers a standing ovation at the end of the match. Winner via pinfall: The Syndicate At this point, Libby Bowman announced intermission and gave the fans a chance to take a break and get some food or use the bathroom. The crowd didn’t like this break as half of us didn’t even need to use the bathroom. Standard match Shane Wethers Vs. Siege A good match as the crowd was rushing back from intermission. Shane controlled most of the match, with Siege having a few moves in there every now and then. Shane Wethers made quick work of Siege though, winning with his Avalanche that seems to almost decapitate Siege. Not a pretty ending, but a good match. Winner via pinfall: Shane Wethers ULWN Title Match Thug roC © vs. Shocka In a match that very well could’ve main evented the show, Shocka went toe to toe with Thug roC. A lot of excitement from the crowd in this one, we all still remember their old feud. We saw a lot of power from Shocka in this one, to a point where it looked like a total squash. Thug roC came back though, a few eye racks and low blows put him back in this one. Thug roC hit his roCing Horse…but Shocka got his foot on the rope! The whole crowd was shocked as everyone was ready to count three, but I guess not. Shocka would then make a comeback in this one, and finally signaled for his finisher. This was all halted when Carl Ca$h came out and cleaned his clock with a chair, giving Shocka the win by DQ. Afterwards, we saw Cal Ca$h hit a nice splash off the top rope. Not to shabby from Cash. Winner via DQ: Shocka Non-title Lumberjack match Asis Vs. Xander Probably the most anticipated match all night, Asis and Xander were to face off. Before the match, Asis cut an in ring promo about how Kimson wasn’t at the show tonight, of course we booed the hell out of him. A lot of eVo stars were all around the ring including guys like Morpheus Omega and Hedges, people we haven’t seen on TV in a while. The match was back and forth the whole way. Xander was the first to throw Asis over the top. But Asis held his own against Thug roC and Carl Ca$h who he met on the outside. Asis would gain control of the match and throw Xander over the top later on. Xander wasn’t as lucky, as he had to defend against Machine and Big Daddy Dreamer. The match continued like this for a little while longer until the music hit and James Kimson came flying out from backstage. The crowd loved it as he slid into the ring with a chair in hand and cleared the ring. The ref quickly called for the bell as the lumberjacks started fighting amongst themselves all over the arena. Asis would get up and the three men would fight until they were broken up by officials and referees. Winner: Draw Overall a great show once again, seems like eVo doesn’t disappoint when it comes to England. Felt like a televised show. The only disappointment was that there was no cell match scheduled, but oh well, maybe next time. Biggest pops: 1. James Kimson 2. The Syndicate 3. Ryan and Sean Mason Most Heat: 1. Xander 2. Thug roC 3. Carl Ca$h (c) eWentertainment 2003
