Legends VIII Transcript Live on Splink Parp! From The National Car Rental Center ~ Ft. Lauderdale, Florida 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night September 11th, 2002 Another night of action and incredible violence will come to you live from Ft. Lauderdale as eVolution presents Legends VIII! Shocking revelations at Legends VII piled up and continued to affect the outlook of Digital Destruction which is only two weeks away. One of the number one contenders to the Universal Title, Impact had been taken out of action and severely injured at the hands of Sovereign which leaves L2J the lone man left. Erin Christoph revealed a shocking secret of his own which brought him to blows with the owner of eVolution and the promise of a one on one encounter between the two at Digital Destruction. While Sovereign continued his violent rampage and prepared for a showdown against Jamal Kage but could he contain the essence of William long enough or will it have cost him once again? Python makes his return to action here in this match up following his stunning upset over Quasar the Great a few weeks ago. His competition in this match will be the up and coming Seara as she looks to solidify herself further in the High Impact division of eVolution. Both of these competitors have already crossed the path of Thug roC so there is no telling just what he could have in store for the two of them. Alias, the man who inadvertently took the life of LeRoy Mohammed inside the Cell one week ago, will once again step inside the ring. This week he must not only deal with the pressure and the guilt of having taken one man’s life but he must also battle back against two other men who have some unresolved issues from last week. Molten and Jinx will look to continue their battle where they had left off last week with only one man being able to walk off the winner. An uneasy alliance over the past few weeks comes to blows this week as The HeartBreak Chick finally gets the title shot she had wanted for weeks. A one on one encounter with Quasar the Great and the High Impact Title riding in the balance. Quasar and Thug roC however will do whatever they need too in order to make sure that the High Impact Title stays with NEW-TV. King and Christoph, a battle which has been brewing since the day Christoph stepped foot in eVolution finally comes to fruition. Malcolm King was at one time The One and the leader of The Unlimited but since Christoph’s arrival there has been a new leader in the group. Christoph, was once just a number in the group of The Unlimited but in King’s absence he seized the power of it all for himself. He took control of The Unlimited and a controversy ensued mired with deaths along the way. Now King looks for a small taste of retribution against the usurper of his power and what could be the wild card in the match is just who would Tyler Davidson side with? The man who killed his fiancée or the one that was ultimately responsible for that chain of events ever coming around? Matt Boone picked up what was without a doubt the biggest win of his career just one week ago. He pinned the Universal Champion, Sovereign in a tag team match. Now he gets a shot at the other two singles champions in eVolution as he battles the Continental Champion, Shocka and the Rising Star Champion, Asis in a triple threat match. Asis has been on a violence streak of his own leaving more battered bodies in his wake with his latest victims having been Jason Jameson and Shodan. Both men will be in the arena this week and keeping a close eye on this one. Shocka will just be trying to rebound from his match against Jameson and get a little measure of revenge himself for Asis costing him the match. Jamal Kage is the one man who has been lucky enough so far to get a shot at Sovereign and the Universal Title. After the events of last week however, that has possibly been changed. With Impact being taken out of action by Sovereign, L2J has been given this chance to earn himself a spot in the Universal Title match at Digital Destruction. A victory over Kage will put L2J right into place for the match and forcing it into being a triple threat. “The DiscoKid” Troy Pierce, a former EdW Universal Champion gets another shot at regaining the gold he once held. In order to do it he must somehow find a way to defeat the monster Sovereign, a man who has been nearly unbeatable since arriving in eVolution. Pierce though is coming off a huge win over Impact last week and with the apparent help of Tommy Michaels on a weekly basis he could have all the ammo he needs in order to slay the monster that blocks his path to the gold.
