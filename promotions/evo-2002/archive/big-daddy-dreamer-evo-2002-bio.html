Big Daddy Dreamer Biography Real Name Big Daddy Dreamer Nicknames BDD Hometown Toronto, Ontario, Canada Birthday November 13th, 1974 Height 6'5 Weight 252 Fed Titles Continental Champion (x1) Fed Awards ULWN Champion (x1) Biography Entrance: ”Seizure of Power” by Marilyn Manson begins playing over the sound system and a mixture of cheers and boos go up from the crowd as you can see Dreamer walk out from behind the curtain with a look of intensity on his face. Dreamer stops at the top of the ramp and looks around at the arena full of fans before beginning his walk down the ramp. Dreamer gets to the bottom of the ramp and quickly hops onto the apron before stepping through the ropes and raising his arms in the air at center ring. History: Big Daddy Dreamer's illustrious story begins 28 years ago in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Born to wealth and privilege, Dreamer had it all. The fancy clothes, cars, exotic locals, women, everything. He lived in the excess and partied hard, very ha rd. But for all his excesses, he did have two things going for him, he was a physical specimen, and board certified genius. It is those two things that will and have made him a very determined and dangerous individual. Through his formulative years, he st udied in Canada and the United States. He excelled in athletics, business, marketing, acting, psychology, history and political science. Whatever he studied, he learned it with such voracity, it could sometimes be frightening. During the secondary year's Dreamer's education and athletic prowess continued to improve exponentially. He competed in American rules football, wrestling, baseball, boxing, tennis, basketball, and gymnastics. After scoring a perfect 1,600 on the American standardize test, college's and universities from around the globe came calling to Big Daddy. Weighted by a big decision, he deferred and spent six-months backpacking through Europe and pulling up a residence in the tiny country of Luxembourg. It was here, he met Katarina and Lexi, two people like himself traveling through Europe and Asia and sharing the Luxembourg flat with him. After a hot passionate friendship, Katarina and Lexi went home and Dreamer made up his mind and accepted a three-sport scholarship offer to Stanford University in California. Dreamer traded in cold winter days, for hot and fun filled nights with all that California has to offer. He played Football, Wrestled, and played tennis for the Cardinal's. In 1996, the past came back into forefront at the Orange Bowl in Miami, Florida. The Stanford Cardinals were playing the Syracuse Orangemen in the Orange Bowl. Dreamer was the star quarterback, and out of the blue, two Syracuse University Cheerleader's ran up and tackled Dreamer. It made for great NBC television. Dreamer puzzled for a second, recognized the two cheerleaders as Lexi and Katarina. They had indeed went back to school and were cheering the Orangemen against his team, though when pressed, they also cheered for Dreamer to do well. Dreamer led the team to a 38-31 victory and partied with the entire cheerleading squad from both teams, but paid special attention Katarina and Lexi throughout the night. The Orange Bowl was over, and the three of them kept in constant touch the remainder of the year. Dreamer wen t on and won the heavyweight class at the NCAA Wrestling Tournament and became an All-American in tennis. The three of them got together again when they culminated at each other's graduation. Now finished, the three wondered what to do next. Dreamer was drafted in the second-round of the NFL draft by the Oakland Raiders, but he no longer had any desire to compete at that level. So Dreamer with his friends in tow, went back home to Toronto. The WWF was at the Skydome hyping one of their great many events and with Lexi's help, secured front row tickets and back stage passes. It was at this event, he decided what to do with his life. Become a professional wrestler. His parents thought he was crazy, but before they could object any true feelings, they were cut dow n by a drunken driver on a cold rainy night. Dreamer took the loss hard, and found solace in Katarina and Lexi, who encouraged him to try pro wrestling. Hell they would even join him. After scouring for the best school in Toronto, he settled at Big Time Wrestling School. Under the watchful tutelage Owen Hart, Dreamer's skill progressed to stratospheric levels. Indeed, Dreamer had become the finest student to ever graduate from their. Katarina and Lexi also graduated their training and competed in some minor indy federations and winning championships, bringing their talent back to the school. Dreamer on the other hand, stayed, and instructed for a time being until the fateful day Owen was tragedly taken. Owen had left the school to Big Daddy Dreamer but it had a hardtime maintaining funds. Dreamer dipped into his own vast deep pockets and with some promotional help, secured funding from TransGlobal Shipping to maintain the school, which would lead to an unexpected and unforseen change in his life. The unexpected change was the day another new student entered the school, calling herself The HeartBreak Chick, HBC. Dreamer had indeed heard of her, for she was the daughter of the benefactor supplying the school. And coming from similar backgrounds, they had a lot in common. Though she was rough around the edges, HBC and Dreamer soon fostered into a very tight friendship in which Dreamer took her under his wing and taught her the business. Upon her graduation. Dreamer introduced her to the wrestling world and eventually began teaming with her, winning several titles in the process. But Dreamer would yet be surprised again as HBC, the Billion dollar heiress to a multi-national shipping company, gave him part of her business in a restructuring and naming it 'Syndicate International'. HBC also pumped in millions of dollars to Big Time Wrestling School and together, they renamed it 'Syndicate International Training Center'. Soon hundred of students began flocking to the school. Faced with a tough decision both personally and professionally, they handed over the school to their most trusted trainers and advisors and began wrestling on the circuit full-time. After six-years in the business, BDD has racked up countless accolades, inducted into several league Hall's of Fame, and set numerious records that are unlikely to ever be broken.
