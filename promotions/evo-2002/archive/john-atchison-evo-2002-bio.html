John Atchison Biography Real Name John Atchison Nicknames Phantom Hometown Aberdeen, Scotland Height 6'2' Weight 239 Interests Wrestling, Women, (My Gurlfriend), TV :), eW Favorite Pro Wrestlers Chris Jericho, Trish Stratus Biography Born in Aberdeen Scotland, no date was recorded leaving many to believe that he was not born into the hands of a doctor, there is no real proof that he was even born as he has no birth certificate, indicating that who ever had the child didnt want him alive. He has a scar on the top of his head, that would make most think someone tried to kill him while he was very young, and what would have stopped the young child from dying is a mystery all to itself. There were no records on his parents or even anyone named Atchison in Scotland, the one Atchison that lived in Scotland had dies 100 years ago in a freak accident when a hay bail snapped from its supports and crushed the man. The problems first started when a tour went through the castle, of course the tour guides had no idea that anyone was born there didnt think of looking through the castle first. When the tour reached the room at which John was staying weird things happened like the book shelf falling on its own, books being picked up from the ground and thrown at the visitors, all eye witness reports had the same thing... Nothing Was Throwing The Books, At Least What They Could See. The problems continued always in the same room which indicated that whatever was there didnt want anyone coming into the room. Finally 20 years later a man with extremely pale skin emerged from the castle, went to teh town and was arrested on site and charged with assualt, something he did not do. 5 years later he was released even more crazy than before. They shipped him out of Scotland and found himself in America where he got into nothing but fights with people. One night a wrestling promotor saw him and hired him for a trial match where if he won, he would get a contract. He won the match and nearly killed his opponent. Since then this thing has been wrestling, doing the only thing that seems to keep him half-sane.
