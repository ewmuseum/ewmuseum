The Ten Count #1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Hello I'm Monica Angel of Evolution, and this is Answer the Ten Count. A magazine/interview show that will try to give a little insight into The Wrestlers of Evolution. The wrestlers and personalities are totally free to answer how they see fit. The Show is divided into three sections; Count One offers a little insight into the wrestler where he/she is coming from. Count two involves their take on their present situation here in Evolution. Both sections will achieve this by asking three questions. The third section will also be divided into three questions one will examine any future plans here in Evolution, The second is the 10 count which Is word association one word first one that comes to mind. And finally the Answer, which allows the talent to talk about anything they want to close. Now that that is established I want to introduce you to my first guest.... "The Urban Legend" Malcolm King. MA:"Thank You Malcolm for coming..." MK:"Thank you for having me....." COUNT ONE. MA:"So how did the Urban Legend come about....the name I mean you use as a moniker?" MK:"LOL....actually it's a parody that has developed over time. The definition of an Urban Legend is a story that is not really supported by truth or fact. People are so quick to believe the hype about anything. I've always said I'm not the greatest wrestler in the world, maybe more technically sound than most, but in this business you are always learning . I owe a great deal of my success to the talent I face...it's a credit to them that I say they are as much apart of what made Malcolm King than the man known simply as D. I chose the name Malcolm King as a homage to Malcolm X and Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. So people naturally feel the Urban Legend nickname is street related; it's not it's an inside joke meaning don't believe the hype....or is it hype?" MA:"I see....so where have you had you most successful run as a professional wrestler?" MK:"That would be in the OWA. It really allowed me to expand my horizon's as a wrestler. I entered primarily a tag team wrestler with Xavier Citizen, I left holding every single belt at least once and with the experience I needed to go it alone. The OWA showed me that I can be a singles wrestler and I attribute that to one man......Big Grumpy; one of my better foes as well as friends." MA:"So you have had some pretty intense fueds in your career which ones stand out?" MK:"Well the ones with Big Grumpy first and foremost. Then the tag team wars with Maximum Carnage...LOL; The Street Squad. [name withheld] and Tyler Davidson, Troy Pierce was one of my favorites for many reasons. My most intense on however has been with Xander. Howevered I'm bothered by the fueds that never quite plan out like the one I had with Chris Boswell over at Dynasty Wrestling." COUNT TWO.. MA:"Speaking of Xander...do you have any idea how this fued is gonna end?" MK:"With him saying I quit of course...LOL. I see it ending because it has to end; it needs closure. His connection to The Unlimited makes him more dangerous than anyone realizes. luckily I have Christoph on my side......he'll serve as a distraction for him, Xander has to figure out whats more important to him The Unlimited or ending the threat of me for himself.....as long as I'm around, and as long as he's closely associated with Christoph, He will never establish himself as his own man." MA:"You have been called the master of deception....what if this is all a game you are playing how are we to believe anything that goes on with you?" MK:"I'm not asking you to believe me.....in fact I hope you do not. You see a deception is mainly a truth submerged in lies. For you to be fooled by me you must be open to the possibility that what I am saying has a grain of truth in it." MA:"That tells me nothing...so maybe a more direct question; are you or have you been THE ONE?" MK:"We are all The One to someone, as far as me being your one dear Monica I'm sorry but I'm a married man." COUNT THREE... MA:"So what about Sovereign and The Universal Championship?" MK:"That title in it's former form was a belt I held on three ocassions. I am the last EDW Universal Champion and no one can take that away from me. That said Evolution to many is considered the next step; to me it's unfinished business....that business is with Xander which to me is much bigger than the Universal title. So as much as I would like to face Sovereign for the title...it's not going to happen because I have another agenda as far as evolution goes...." MA:"Fair enough...ready for the 10 count?" MK:"As ready as I'll ever be...." 1.Halle Berry "My wife would kill me if I said what I'm thinking" 2.[name withheld]/Tyler Davidson "Coin Flip" 3.Troy Pierce "Respect" 4.Big Daddy Dreamer "Right place, Right time" 5.Erin Christoph "Leech" 6.Xander "The ONE" 7.WWE "Afraid of Evolution, both figuratively and literally." 8.ENRON "20 cents a share" 9.Evolution Wrestling "Home" 10.Malcolm King "The Urban Legend" MA:"In closing......" MK:".....I've had a full career. Made some friends, made some enemies. This business has given me more than I can ever repay. To the boys I don't care if you like me or hate me, just hoped that in the end you respected me as I respect each and everyone of you." MA:"Thank you Malcolm" MK:"You're welcome" MA:"Well this is Monica Angel I hope you enjoyed this first edition of Answer the 10 count....hopefully I will be back next week if you are interested let management know and I will set up...." Off camera Xander makes his way to the set. King stands up as Both He and Xander are looking eye to eye. King cracks a smile as he moves out of Xander's way and offers him his chair.... MK:"Go ahead...I'm done here; I have no reason to fight you now I have you Sept 18th." Xander:"LOL.....I didn't come here to fight you only to look you directly in the eyes and see..." MK:"See what?" Xander:"Don't worry; I've already got what I came for....lol" Xander laughs as he just walks off totally disrespecting Malcolm King.
