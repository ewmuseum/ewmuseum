'The Perfect Design' Aaron Justice Biography Real Name Aaron Justice Nicknames 'The Perfect Design' Hometown Charlotte, NC Birthday March 16th, 1981 Height 6'9' Weight 285 Interests Wrestling, e-feds, web design, poser, graphics, fast cars, racing, music, movies, anything fun, and spending time with my wife and daughter. Favorite Pro Wrestlers Chris Jericho Fed Titles eVolved Champion Biography Aaron Justice is a tattoo artist turned pro wrestler. Aaron owned a tattoo shop in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. One day he got his break when the owner of Global Championship Wrestling stopped in while on tour in Pittsburgh. Talent scouts always talked about this guy, when they seen him training in the gym. No one knew if he would be a star or not, but it was worth the chance to GCW, seeing as his brother in law Ryp Fandango was probablly the greatest thing to hit GCW ever. Aaron was quickly signed to GCW. He was trained for a few short months while GCW was in the middle of it's restart. GCW was bringing in all fresh faces, but would this monster be ready in time. It was time for the re-opening of GCW and the higher ups asked if Aaron was ready. The training staff stated, he is more than ready, this man is like a veteran. Justice quickly proved to GCW that he was the man to beat. Justice captured every title in GCW and was crowned a Grand Slam Champion, since he won every title. In the summer of 2002 GCW closed it's door and Justice took a break. At this point Justice accomplished everything his brother in law Ryp Fandango has done, except on thing and that was making it to the CSWA. In the begining of 2003 Justice was contacted by CSWA. Justice quickly jumped to the offer and signed a contract. A feud was started between him and his brother in law, but it was never finished. Justice walked away from CSWA and didn't look back. Since then Justice has just been enjoying life with the money he made in professional wrestling. Other Feds GCW.cc and CSWA Other Fed Awards GCW Grand Slam Champion (held every title once. only 1 other person has done this.)
