The Kysis

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well as we saw last week, the newly placed stable, The Kysis, has made their call out in the eVolution megaphone and it was yelled at the leader of the Unlimited, Xander. The match with Oz, the older brother of Angel, and Xander had turned out to be the first manuever in this game of human chess. Not being the first time that Xander has been double crossed before, this was the main thinking of his former friend and former Unlimited companion, Angel. Let's go back and see the footage of the tradgedy known as, The Kysis, began. Xander slid right back into the ring as Oz was again in control of Seara sending her into the ropes right towards Xander. Seara managed to duck at the last possible second under a Xander clothesline and ducked a second one before she slid right in between the legs of the surprised Oz and to the outside of the ring. From the outside Seara grabbed the ankles of Oz and tripped him up before she grabbed him and dragged him along towards the corner and yanked him crotch first right into the ring post. Xander again came sliding to the outside as he tried going after Seara but she kept herself several steps ahead of him as she hopped onto the ring apron and sprang over the top rope before coming down across the back of Oz’s head with a leg drop just as Xander slid back into the ring once again and finally caught her this time with a handful of hair. Justin Fritz: Seara was keeping herself a step or two ahead of Xander but she just got caught. Jack Tyler: And now she’s in trouble. You can count on that. Justin Fritz: She isn’t done just yet though Jack. I think there still might be a few surprises left in store for Xander before this one is over and done with. Jack Tyler: You know something! What the hell have you heard? Justin Fritz: Not a thing. Jack Tyler: LIAR! A second referee finally came down the ramp and slid into the ring as Xander turned Seara quickly around and had scooped her straight up onto his shoulder’s in a standing fireman’s carry. He held her up there for a moment before taking a few steps to the side and flipping her over right down into a death valley driver before going for the cover. One Two The pin was broken up by Oz! Justin Fritz: Oz just made the save for Seara! Jack Tyler: What the hell is he doing?! Justin Fritz: I don’t know but it looks like Xander has just been set up! Jack Tyler: Oz has a death wish. Oz drove a double axehandle shot right down across the back of Xander’s head as he still covered Seara. He got back to his feet and reached down to pick Xander up before he sent Xander right into the ropes and nearly ran right through him with a massive spear that left Xander lying on the mat holding his ribs. Oz grabbed Xander again and began lifting him to his feet but before he could do anything else out of the crowd, his brother Angel came sliding into the ring. Jack Tyler: Here comes the retired Angel to talk some sense into his maniac brother! Justin Fritz: I don’t know if that is even possible. Jack Tyler: Someone better do it or he’s a dead man. Angel, wearing a pair of blue jeans and his leather jacket, ran right up to his brother and shoved him back a few steps and away from Xander. Angel took a moment to lean over Xander and check on his condition before he got back to his feet and stared across at his brother. Angel slowly began taking off his leather jacket and then yanked his shirt off over his head and threw it at his brother. Underneath the shirt Angel had just taken off he had an identical black shirt with the word “Kysis” in green grunge letters. Oz took the shirt his brother had thrown to him and put it on so they were both wearing matching shirts as the two men lifted their arms into the air as the fans were unsure of just how to act. Jack Tyler: Now what is he doing?! Justin Fritz: I think Angel was in on it! He swerved Xander and The Unlimited! Jack Tyler: He’s supposed to be retired damn it! Justin Fritz: He might still be but he’s here with his brother obviously. The two men stood over both Xander and Seara as suddenly swarming down the ramp came both Erin Christoph and Asis. Angel and Oz saw them as the came out the backstage area and after a quick kick to Xander by Angel the two men scattered out of the ring and disappeared into the crowd as Christoph and Asis slid into the ring. Justin Fritz: I don’t know what we just saw happen but Asis and Christoph have just run off Oz and the traitor Angel. Jack Tyler: This isn’t going to go over well with The Unlimited. There will be hell to pay. Mm. Mm. Mm. Mm. Mm. That's exactly what it's going to be, and knowing of Angel's sick way to twisting his matches into his own little deformities, there's no doubt in anyone's mind that if he does indeed go against his former friend, Xander, that there will be alot of drama as well as destruction. The Kysis is full of two very special and powerful men, Angel with the smarts of a genius and Oz with the body and power of a god. This'll only errupt into more chaos, and I don't think that's a very good thing. We'll just have to wait and see what'll happen between the two former friends. This might be eVo's first family match. The brothers, Oz and Angel versus the cousins Erin Christoph and Xander. We'll just have to see what this will morph into in the future of eVolution. This is Hugh G.
