From The Nassau Coliseum ~ Uniondale, New York 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night August 7th, 2002 After the stunning events of Legends III the heat continues to pick up with Legends IV at the Nassau Coliseum. With only one week remaining before the X-Rated pay per view event all the stops will be getting pulled out at Legends to make it a memorable one. Daniel Somers will be back in the house this week and reportedly will have some news on the Eric Graves situation. Rumors have it a possible shake up in the chain of command can be in store for eVolution. Tyler Davidson will also be attending this week following the promise of one Malcolm King at Legends III. The only problem so far Legends IV has demonstrated is the apparent lack of a television deal to air the show. Legends 3 was apparently the final show to air on CBN and now the contract with Hogan's Briefs does not start until Legends V, one week after X-Rated. Where this leaves the status of the show is still uncertain but it will go off one way or another as scheduled. For months these two men have been battling across the wrestling world. The action started in the now defunct EdW where Shodan pulled off an upset victory over Brad Kelley capturing the EdW Platinum Title from him in a match which also featured Jason Jameson. Both men made their eVolution debuts on Legends I and have picked up right where they left off with each man one upping the other in the past two shows. Now they get a chance to settle their differences inside the ring. A late addition to the match Calvin Brooks could play the part of the wild card. A former World Champion in IW, Brooks is certainly longing for the taste of gold once again and will do anything to make it a reality. Time and time again now Asis has been screwed over by Daniel Somers and Tyler Davidson. Now as a bit of a courtesy to Asis, Nichole Kelley has placed him into this match giving him a chance to earn a spot at X-Rated. Drake made his debut two weeks ago and has since set his sights on the young rookie Matt Boone. He won't be able to get away with his actions for long against a man like Asis. Asis is out to prove a point and he will do so at the expense of Drake. Four men, two teams and a very violent situation. Neither of these two teams are destined to be together long as the bad blood between the partners is so thick you can cut it with a knife. Shocka and Cassidy have been battling for two weeks and are now forced to team up against a team that didn't work in the least at American Nightmare. It was Erin Christoph that screwed Xander out of his chance to be in the X-Rated Tournament and now they team for a second time one week before their Tiers of eVolution match at X-Rated for the Continental Title. Chaos will certainly rein supreme here. Last week saw the official start of the eVolution High Impact division with the shocking debuts of former PCW Cruiserweight Champion Jake Keeton, Kin Ryo, and the return of Quasar the Great. Now Legends IV marks the big battle royal match between seven of the top cruiserweights in the business today. Python and Sean Mason will make their eVolution debuts in this match as Barbatos Bronx will return to action as well. The possible sleeper of the match of course is The HeartBreaker herself as HeartBreak Chick enters herself in the match as well to try and prove once again that she is the best cruiserweight in the business today. A preview of things to come at X-Rated as the eight men in the tournament will square off in eight man tag team action. The teams were randomly picked by Tyler Davidson and Daniel Somers's mysterious friend to create quite an eventful pairing of teams. Sovereign, Impact, Troy Pierce, and Matt Boone will team against L2J, Tommy Michaels, Jamal Kage, and Big Daddy Dreamer. Pierce and Boone have already had their fair share of trouble with both Impact and Sovereign and now must team with them if they hope to survive this battle. Meanwhile L2J finds himself surrounded in very hostile territory as he teams with Tommy Michaels, Dreamer and Kage. Things don't look good going into Legends IV for the fearless young star as there is an obvious connection between Dreamer and Kage and a strong dislike for L2J on the part of Tommy Michaels. Expect all hell to break loose before this one is said and done.
