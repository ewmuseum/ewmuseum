Legends XVII Transcript Live on OSE! From The Saddledome ~ Calgary, Alberta, Canada 7:59PM EST Thursday Night November 28th, 2002 The mayhem continued last week on Legends XVI and if the preview for XVII is any indication nothing will be changing. The stable war between The Notorious and New-TV will heat up this week as The Omen Brotherhood and tag team champions The Unlimited will simply look on and wait for the smoke to clear to see who is still standing. The war between Sovereign and Impact also took a strange twist last week and the repercussions are sure to be felt in Calgary for Legends XVII! If that wasn’t enough with the elimination of Bobby Stormbringer by Sovereign last week, Daniel Somers and Thug roC have gotten full control of eVolution back and this show is certainly going to make them happy if nothing else. Two of the very promising young cruiserweights in eVolution kick the show off with as Ben Juventud battles against the mysterious Strictly Prohibited. Both men have hit some roadblocks so far in their eVolution career’s but now could be the time for one of them at least to break through and begin their path to greatness. Only one will be able to survive this one though. That is if either of them is able to. Ryan Flesco made his debut last week and despite having an impressive performance he fell a bit short thanks to some New-TV antics in his battle against Morpheus Omega. He gets another chance this week however to regain his footing and impress the brass of eVolution. This time his challenger is a much different performer as he battles against Jay Stylez Junior in which he must find a way to counteract the high flying and quick offense Stylez likes to use against his opponents. The Unlimited and The Omen Brotherhood are obviously no strangers to one another but now there is some more gold in the equation. This match is a non title match but if the Omen Brotherhood manages to pick up the victory it would certainly put them in some very good standing for the Tag Team Titles in the near future. The only problem they may have is just which members of the group will they have to deal with this week and what will the others be up to as a result? The former Cell fighter takes a break from his teaming with Deacon this week to try and capture his first taste of gold in eVolution as he battles the new Rising Star Champion, Sean Mason. Mason pulled off the huge and hard fought victory against Seara at the pay per view and will now make his first defense all the while looking over his shoulder no doubt for any presence of Morpheus Omega who has apparently singled the young star out for his first victim thanks to the encouragement of Thug roC. Both Impact and Sovereign will be in the same ring at the same time this week but now they are on opposite sides of the ring with partners added into the mix. The new Continental Champion, Shodan is paired up with the Hollywood Monster as they battle against Sovereign and The Unlimited’s own HeartBreak Chick. Fresh off a brutal battle of her own against Big Daddy Dreamer, HBC makes her first appearance since that battle tonight. Dreamer has for the second straight time been left feeling a bit injured as a result of his battles with HBC but this time it was a car that did the damage and a driver that no one saw. He is expected however to be in the house this week for a first class seat during this one. Last week it was Shocka and Morpheus Omega who kidnapped Seara of The Notorious and sparked a battle between the two groups which lasted through the main event. Now this week it was Somers himself finally back in full control along with Thug roC that set the match up with an eight person tag team lumberjack match. Where lumberjacks go, chaos usually tends to follow and when you have lumberjacks like The Unlimited, The Omen Brotherhood, Sovereign, and Impact you know things will get out of hand real quickly. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Xander, The HeartBreak Chick, Big Daddy Dreamer, and many more!
