Damon O'Halloran Biography Real Name Damon O'Halloran Nicknames 'Act of God' Hometown Coleraine, Northern Ireland. Birthday 13/08/1981 Height 6'5'' Weight 275 Interests Soccer, Wrestling, every f'n sport there is. Favorite Pro Wrestlers Shane Douglas, Jeff Jarrett, Steve Austin Fed Titles (1) Cell Championship (1) United Championship Fed Awards 2003 eVo Fighter of the Year Biography Born at the turn of the 1980's, Damon O'Halloran grew up a typical Roman Catholic family. His father worked in the mines of Shanagolds in West Belfast as his mother stayed at home to rare the six children. Damon was the second oldest in the family; three boys and three girls. Damon was a regular kid, as regular a kid can be being a Catholic growing up in a predominantly Protestant area. But like most kids growing up in the eighties, Damon was not that bothered about religion and was more interested in how Manchester United were doing on the television. That all changed in a blink of an eye. Damon had idolized his brother Tom, six years his elder, Tom embodied everything a man should be, according to Damon at least. On the 20th July 1991, as Damon was out playing a little footie with the lads from Scahill Road, he witnessed in front of his very eyes his brother being shot down in the middle of the street by two armed gunmen. From that moment on, Damon changed forever. He was not the sweet little boy playing footie with the lads down in the park. He rarely left his room. In school, he was a avid history student and indulged himself in reading up on the history of Modern Ireland. He begins to become obsessed with every aspect of his brother's life. He reads only a book in the bible every night... the same book over and over: Redemption. As he reaches his late teens, he becomes a staunch nationalist, hating everything British... everything unionist. On July 20th 1995, 4 years to the day that Tom died, Damon joins the Irish Republican Army. By the time he was fifteen, he was 6'5'' and was seen by many in the Nationalist community as one of the leaders of the new generation in the fight for equality. Damon, prepared to do anything to further the cause of Nationalism was an accomplice in the Omagh bombings of 1998, although, he wasnt directly involved in the plan, the Stormont Government came down on him hard. They offered him a way out: to exile Northern Ireland forever or face life imprisonment. Knowing his life was in danger and knowing that many IRA 'martyrs' still did alot of work gathering funds and arms in America to further the cause, O'Halloran left Ireland for the last time, on a chartered plan straight to New York, as he touched down in JFK Airport, accompanied by two armed guards, the people in the terminal that day knew from his skinhead and tatoos who he was. It was not long until O'Halloran incorporated himself into American society in an effort to further his cause in the long term by the only way he knew how... by fighting. Almost immediately after setting up in America, O'Halloran aligned himself with a bunch of skinheads. Neo-Nazi's by the markings on thier bodies, but O'Halloran didnt care. All he wanted to do was further the cause and if that was by fighting and taking out his pent up frustration on others then so be it. He began fighting in Smilthy's, the Asylum's subsidary federation, in late 1999, while all the time, making more and more Nationalist contacts. He never questioned his own ability, trained for years by the para-military IRA, O'Halloran knew how to kill a man in numerous ways, with a avid hunger for growing as a fighter, he had a black belt in Taekwon Do and Martial Arts. In the ring, he anniliated one after another of Smilthy's best. Soon Joe Campbell, owner of the Asylum, was invited to check out the insatiable abilities of O'Halloran. As he continued to dominate the pit. O'Halloran never looked upon himself as becoming a 'star', his goal was to fight and futher his cause. He knows no other way. And now, with the unstable situation in the Asylum, he is going to bring his extreme Catholic and Nationalist ideas to eVolution ... and he will not stop until he converts each and every single fighter until there is no one left!!
