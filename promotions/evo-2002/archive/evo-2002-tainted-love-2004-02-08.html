Live on Pay Per View! From Tropicana Field ~ St. Petersburg, Florida 7:59PM EST Sunday Night February 8th, 2004 Backstage ~ Welcome To Tainted Love Featuring Matt Hunt Tainted Love was on the air. The fans screamed in delight as "The Postman" Matt Hunt showed up on the eVotron. Tonight was the night. Tonight was the night to settle old scores. To put his demons to be once and for all. Tonight was the night to rid eVolution of that parasite.... Damon O'Halloran. "Well, you got what you wanted. Now what?” Now what? Yeah……….now what? I finish this……….once and for all. Then what? Then………..life goes on. You mean to tell me you have reached a level of madness so complete that you act nonchalantly about all of this? It’s not madness. It’s justice. Revenge is more like it. When dealing with someone like Damon O'Halloran it’s splitting hairs to try and define the difference. I’ll worry about everything else later. “After you’ve become the monster you’ve fought so long against? You realize this has stopped making sense a long time ago? I defeat O'Halloran. I end his run in eVolution, cripple him whatever else needs to make sense? What do you think crippling him is going to do, that needs to make sense. You hurt Damon bad enough that he’s never heard from again and the last months never happened? That doesn’t erase what he has done to me! The strain he has put on my family. Of course not. But then, then maybe he’ll understand that his actions all these months have had consequences. Maybe then he’ll never have a chance to hurt anyone ever again. And……if it means that I’m no better than him then let me burn in Hell like he’s going to. Damon O'Halloran is not the only one out there. You bury him there’ll be another, or two, to rise and take his place. It’s the never-ending battle after all isn’t it? I’ll fight till I can’t." Matt Hunt looked up into the mirror. If only those watching and cheering his name could hear the turmoil of the Postman they may have an inkling of an idea for the pain Hunt lived with. He pulled on his elbow pad. His left fist taped heavily, cushioning it. The head bandage he had worn since the incident two weeks ago on Legends was now gone, both his steely eyes penetrating to the heart of the madness that had gripped eVolution, and his heart for the past year. He breathed a heavy sigh; carrying with it all the burdens he had saddled himself with over the months against O'Halloran. It had worn him down. This was the night, his chance to walk away unburdened, ready to finally move on. There was only the Act of God standing in his way. Hunt looked over his shoulder and into the camera lens, his pupils shrinking. “Damon O'Halloran…..boy….you’ll bow down to the Postman tonight, against me you have no chance. You fight for everything that’s wrong in this place. Any ideal you could have that would save your rotting soul has been corrupted. To the lowest plane of Hell with you, Damon……..and if I have to take you there myself………..I’ll do it.” Tainted Love had begun. Pyros. You can still hear them murmur throughout the arena over Psymon's presence here at Tainted Love. He shifts his weight and brings the microphone to his lips. "You're all probably wondering why I'm here in the eVo," he began, then pauses for a moment to hear a cheap pop after saying 'eVo.' Then he comes to a decision: "Evidently so," one of his voices said. Psymon nods his head in agreement. "What the hell is he doing in there?" one of the announcers said. "I guess we'll find out," the other replies. Psymon comes back to reality. "It's kind of hard to be in the West Wind Division when you're fired from the 4WW altogether." The fans boo at the fact that Psymon has been fired from the 4WW, Ian and Fey Childress' federation. "I make one 'comment'----if I can be so blunt----about some soap-opera-looking angle, it pisses three people off and it gets reported to the bosses. I get a call after sending a one-week honeymoon with my wife and was fired over the phone by Fey, saying that I'm not a team player that I should find work elsewhere." He pauses for a moment, his eyes scans over the sea of fans again, loud boos emanate from them. "They could have solved this in a more professional manner by giving me a slap on the wrist and a one-month suspension, but no, they terminated my ass!" The same result as last time: more boos. This is something Psymon isn't use to. He's used to having people boo him, not have the fans boo at the federation he once was in. But it doesn't matter to him. The fans have their right to cheer or jeer just about anything they want. It's their first amendment right to do so. "Everything was blown way out of proportion when that whole mess started, because of some comments I made about an angle----which is doing better than I thought it would----I get fired. Apparently, I wasn't entitled to my opinions about something and this is what I get, a termination. Aren't bosses grand nowadays?" He makes a mock smile, then instantly has his expression harden back into a serious look. "Fuck Fey, fuck her 'darling' husband, Ian, and fuck the so-called 'good name' of the 4WW!" He pauses for a moment, then slips the object off his left shoulder and unfolds it, holding it up in the air. It's a black 4WW T-shirt. "This is the fed I was once in." He pauses, and pulls out a gold Zippo lighter from one of his front pocket. "This is the fed after I was fired." He pauses yet again, looking at the fans for a moment. He opens up the lighter and sets the shirt ablaze, dropping it to the mat. The fans cheer, chanting 'PSYMON! PSYMON!' He looks over at the stage, a smirk curls the ends of his mouth. "Since I have match starting now, send me my first victim."
