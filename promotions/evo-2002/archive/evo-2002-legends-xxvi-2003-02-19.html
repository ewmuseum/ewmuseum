Legends XXVI Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Cow Palace ~ San Francisco, California 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night February 19th, 2003 We are less than two weeks away now from Tainted Love and that means there is only one episode of Legends left before the explosive pay per view event is set to kick off. There is no telling what you can possibly see this coming week as Tainted Love finishes setting up the remainder of matches. This Legends will feature a series of matches that will determine just who will move along to the Tag Team Title gauntlet match as well as the debut of several newcomers to the scene. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! This week we’ll get to see our first look of a newcomer to eVolution in the form of Chuck. Not a lot is known as of yet about the newcomer but rumor has it that he is one of the top competitors from gWa and has some past history with several people in eVolution. The first person that he finds himself confronted with this week is Yoshii Tanaka who still has a target on his back courtesy of The Yakuza thanks to Daniel Somers. If that wasn’t enough after spoiling the plans of The Unlimited last week they are certain to be out for some payback as well. Twisted Reality and The BodySnatchers came to blow last week on Legends and now this week the two teams come head to head with the winners moving on to the pay per view. The BodySnatchers two weeks ago had captured the Tag Team Titles on behalf of The Unlimited but find themselves in a match without the belts which will be defended later in the night. Both teams have a lot to lose in this match and neither one should be settling for anything short of a victory. Last week Big Daddy Dreamer managed to defeat both Wideload and BlackHavok but now this week he is once again finding himself in a very difficult situation. He must once again go into battle alone as his partner HBC will be busy trying to regain the Tag Team Titles along with Luther Wisdom. Dreamer must battle against the new tag team of Arty Apple and The Karma Dragon, collectively known as The Cogs of War. The team made their first appearance last week in a brief meeting with Daniel Somers who was eager to give them this chance and see what they are worth. Luther Wisdom and The HeartBreak Chick get the chance to regain their Tag Team Titles in a battle against Daniel Somers and a mystery partner of his own. The question left was would Somers choose Malcolm King or Big Daddy Dreamer to be his partner like he stated last week with the added stipulations as it results to who gets pinned to end the match. How could either Dreamer or King trust Somers enough to team with him knowing that no matter what happens he’s still certain to win in the end? A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Troy Pierce, James Kimson, Scott Frost, Shocka and many of eVolution’s superstars! VIP Office ~ Christoph Questions Featuring The Unlimited The scene opens up and Erin Christoph is seen looking at Xander, Asis, and Daniel Somers. He looks to be very upset about something, and the rest of the Unlimited members are staring at him with concern. "...I mean, I can't believe we still haven't done anything about Prohibited. I mean. It is obviously Tyler Davidson, and he is obviously after us because of me, and Somers. I mean, look. Who runs this operation. The One. Who is The One? Somers. Who ordered me to kill Davidson's fiancée? Somers. Who killed his fiancée? Me! Just look at all the facts, I have been pondering this for a very long time. One reason why I have kept my silence for the most part. But it all comes down to Tyler Davidson." Christoph says to the group. Everyone looks around at everyone else. "We can't be too sure though..." Somers says. Christoph looks amazed. "You're not scared?! If it's Davidson, he could possibly fire you! He can fire me too!" Christoph says. "Exactly, if it was Davidson. Don't you think that he would want to get rid of you as quick as possible?" Somers retorts. Christoph stops for a moment and then scratches his head. "Good point." Christoph remarks. Xander stands up. "Besides! You need to worry about King. You're going to be facing him at Tainted Love." Xander says. Christoph looks at his cousin and smiles. "What do I care? I proved that the Unlimited is much quicker than the very man who started it. And I represent the Unlimited in that match, therefore. I am much quicker than him." Christoph says. "All I have to do is hit the U-Factor, and end of King." Christoph continues with a smile. Ringside ~ Legends XXVI Featuring Justin Fritz and Jack Tyler The show comes back with a shot inside of the Cow Palace as “Prayer” by Disturbed blasted over the sound system. Tens of thousands of fans are on their feet cheering for the start of the show as the usual blue and red pyro display blasts off from the ramp into the air. On the eVotron footage of the past events and superstars of eVolution plays over and over again as the music continues to blast over the sound system with the crowd screaming at the top of their lungs. The camera pans around the arena before taking a long shot of the V-shaped ramp eVolution uses for its show and coming to a stop finally at the ringside area where you can see Justin Fritz and Jack Tyler seated in their positions and ready to call all the action as the music and pyro slowly die down.. “Welcome everyone back to another great edition of Legends!” Justin Fritz began saying. “Tonight’s show should be explosive as we are only days away from Tainted Love!” ”We’re going to find out the truth here tonight Fritz,” Jack butted in. “The Unlimited have told us that they will unmask Strictly Prohibited one way or another tonight.” “They have made plans for doing that from what I heard,” Justin agreed. “Also here tonight though, Daniel Somers and a partner will be defending those Tag Team Titles currently belonging to The Unlimited against Luther Wisdom and The HeartBreak Chick.” “And when it’s done either HBC or Wisdom will be The Unlimited’s own personal slave,” Jack added. “I love it.” ”That’s only if they lose the match,” Justin corrected. “We’ll also find out who the other two teams in the four team gauntlet match at Tainted Love will be. Strictly X is already in there and whoever the Tag Team Champions are at the end of the night will also be in.” ”That leaves two spots to be filled here tonight,” Jack continued. ”At least you can add,” Justin mocked him. “Right now we’re going to head to the ring and get things started as Yoshii Tanaka takes on a newcomer, Chuck!” Non Title ~ Standard Match Chuck vs. “The Immortal” Yoshii Tanaka The arena darkens to pitch black. Unanticipated pyros erupt from both sides of the ramp atop of the stage. As the eruption occurs, the crowd reacts in excitement. With that brief instance, the arena became lightened, however the only thing visible to the eye due to all the darkness were the video clips fading in on the eVo-tron as the music reached higher tunes. A man who is quite tall and well fit makes his way through the curtains of darkness at the very same moment the fans witness this and their cheering soon becomes silent. Their faces, if at all possible were visible would show the exact fear they felt at that moment. Their hearts can be heard pounding and their voices panting throughout the entire arena. The little pounding that was noticeable as the man began making his way out has now reached louder tunes. The tune that fills the arena seems to be none other than "Slow Chemical" by Fingers Eleven. Libby Bowman: On his way to the ring, weighing 335 pounds at an even 7 foot, from Charlotte, North Carolina.......the newest newcomer to eVo..........CCHHHHHHUUUUUCCCCCKKKKKKK!!!!!!!! The crowd reacts in mixed emotions. Some of the wild people in attendance boo him as the others remain cheering and or either silent. Chuck just simply ignores any recollection after hearing his name announced over the sound system. From the eVo-tron, a phrase enlightens the entire arena, as Chuck makes his way down the ramp. The phrase expresses...... I'm as dirty as they come....and in some cases as they go! Chuck now still in the same manner as he entered the arena, enters the ring with great reign of power and force. “Why does Yoshii always get stuck against the big monsters?” Jack asked. ”Apparently he has bad luck,” Justin answered. “Personally I think it has more to do with the fact that he and Strictly Prohibited have found themselves on the bad side of Daniel Somers and The Unlimited in recent weeks.” ”Sort of a punishment type deal,” Jack pondered. “Sounds fair to me.” ”Of course it would,” Justin commented. Libby Bowman: And his opponent hailing from Osaka, Japan and weighing in at 219lbs……”The Immortal” Yoshii Tanaka! ”Immortal” by Adema begins playing over the sound system and the lights dim down to a low setting as red mist begins filling the entrance and the lyrics of the song kick in loudly over the sound system. Let's fight! We're face to face Loyalty is what I need to see from you Your insecure I can see the fear that breaths in your heart Where will you run, where will you hide I see the blood drip from your eyes Who will survive let's get it on and we'll find I know who you are The leader of lost souls You can't kill me, I'm immortal I'm not afraid to die My soul will travel on You can't kill me I’m immortal, immortal, immortal As the smoke flows through the arena and the first chorus comes to an end, the figure of a man with long dark hair and a masked face can be seen standing at the very top of the ramp with his hands folded in front of him. The lights turn on and the multicolored mask of Yoshii Tanaka can be seen clearly helping to disguise his features from the fans as does his hair hanging down in front of his face. He stands at the top of the ramp still before suddenly darting down the ramp and sliding in under the bottom rope as “Immortal” continues to play. Once in the ring Yoshii grabs hold of the bottom of his mask and yanks it off revealing underneath it an identical design in face paint. “This guy has more targets currently set on him than Saddam Hussein,” Jack laughed as Yoshii stood in the ring staring at his opponent. ”The scary part is I don’t think that’s much of an exaggeration,” Justin agreed. ”Of course it’s not,” Jack told him. “He has everyone in The Unlimited pissed off at him. The Japanese Yakuza are apparently pissed at him for something he did in the past and Chuck probably just wants to kick the guys’ ass for no apparent reason here tonight.” ”I think that about sums it all up pretty much,” Justin agreed again. “Is it just me or does he look a bit different tonight?” Jack asked. “He seems a little smaller to me.” “I think your eyes are just playing tricks on you,” Justin answered. The bell rang to signal the start of the match and the newcomer, Chuck, made the first move as he came charging forward with a clothesline only to be ducked by the much smaller and quicker Yoshii. Yoshii turned around and immediately began letting loose on the legs of Chuck with a series of stiff kicks as he tried to keep the big man off balance until he ran into the ropes. Yoshii came running right back at Chuck and went for a leaping spinning heel kick but he was stopped in midair as Chuck caught him and then lifted him high into the air before he drove Yoshii right down into the mat with a massive powerbomb. Chuck staggered back a few steps stretching his leg to try and work out the pain from Yoshii’s kicks before he reached down and grabbed his adversary before he yanked him right back to a standing base and leveled him with a massive clothesline before going for the cover. One Two Yoshii kicked out! “Chuck almost put this one away with his obvious power against Yoshii,” Justin stated. ”Did you see that powerbomb?” Jack asked. “He damn near put Yoshii through the mat.” “I saw it and I think I felt one of the after shocks from it,” Justin answered. Chuck looked up and got right back to his feet before he took a few menacing steps towards the referee. He turned right back though when he noticed Yoshii slowly trying to struggle back to his feet and caught him with a boot right into the side of his head. Chuck grabbed Yoshii again and lifted him back up to his feet a second time before he scooped Yoshii right onto his shoulder. Chuck paced around the ring slowly with Yoshii draped over his shoulder before he ran towards the corner and was about to snake eyes Yoshii only for him to slip out the back door and almost cause Chuck to collide with the turnbuckles. Chuck turned around and Yoshii quickly went for chop right across the chest of Chuck. He hit one and then delivered a second before he followed it with a quick kick to the side of Chuck. With Chuck in a dangerous position Yoshii quickly went for The Blinding Mist but Chuck managed to step to the side just before any of the mist could get in his eyes and he grabbed Yoshii around the waist before driving him down and into the mat with a spinebuster. ”Yoshii is doing his best to avoid Chuck in there but he just hit a bit of a roadblock,” Justin continued. ”That’s one big damn roadblock,” Jack laughed. ”He’s rather quick for a man his size too obviously,” Justin stated. “I thought for certain he would be in trouble following Yoshii’s mist attack.” ”He would have been if the illegal attack worked,” Jack protested. Chuck got back to his feet again and stared down at the body of Yoshii lying hurt on the mat as Chuck slowly raised his arms to draw the boos from the crowd. Chuck reached down and grabbed a hold of Yoshii again before he lifted him back up to his feet and this time placed a hand firmly around the throat of Yoshii. Chuck lifted Yoshii straight into the air and brought him crashing down to the mat with a huge choke slam. Despite the damage that was obviously done Chuck was about to reach down and grab hold of Yoshii once again but before he could do anything Erin Christoph, Xander, and Asis had stormed the ring each man armed with a steel chair and with Daniel Somers pulling up the rear, a pair of handcuffs in one hand and a microphone in his other as he immediately began to speak. In The Ring ~ The Revelation Featuring Daniel Somers, Erin Christoph, Xander, Asis, Yoshii Tanaka, Strictly Prohibited, Big Daddy Dreamer, and The HeartBreak Chick ”Calm down guys,” Somers called to Xander, Christoph, and Asis. “Chuck you got five seconds to get out of the ring now and let The Unlimited handle their own business because this match is over one way or another. I’m just being nice and giving you the chance to leave on your own right now because you’re new around here.” “We knew something like this was coming this week,” Justin continued. “I don’t know if Chuck is going to leave on his own however.” “I don’t think he’s about to do that though,” Jack argued. “He doesn’t look like he’s ready to stop fighting just yet.” “If he knew what was good for him he would move real quickly,” Justin added. In the ring Chuck showed no signs of leaving despite the five seconds he was offered being up. Noticing the slow approach of Erin Christoph, Chuck quickly leapt into action going right after him with a right hand but his assault was short lived as both Asis and Xander came in for the save each one slamming their steel chair right down across the back of Chuck and then right into the back of his knees taking his legs out from under him. Christoph rubbed the side of his face where Chuck had punched him before he lifted his chair up and brought it down across the midsection of Chuck over and over again until Somers came into the ring and with the help of Xander began to restrain him. “Chuck made a big mistake there,” Justin went on. “He might not be a rookie but he is new to eVolution and anyone that’s been here knows that what he just did is never a good idea.” ”Yeah especially when Christoph is obviously in a pissed off mood,” Jack added. “Plus we all know Asis is a twenty four seven PMSer.” “That’s one way of putting it,” Justin agreed. “Now we got Somers and Xander both restraining Christoph and that chair while Yoshii and Chuck are both down and out. I guess it’s safe to say this match is over.” Somers and Xander finally released their hold on the calmed down Christoph as Asis picked Chuck back up to his feet and sent him through the ropes and to the outside before they turned their attention now to Yoshii. “This isn’t going to be good for Yoshii,” Justin said. ”This was coming and you know it,” Jack argued. “He’s constantly got himself into Unlimited business and now it’s time for him to pay. You can just consider this a message for his buddy Strictly Prohibited.” Asis rolled Yoshii right over onto his back and grabbed both his arms before placing his hands palms up against his back as Somers leaned down over him and fasten the handcuffs into place on him to hold him hostage. With that done Asis grabbed Yoshii by the hair and dragged him up to his feet and held him as Christoph and Xander took turns with their chairs. One after another the two of them began to take turns as they nailed him in the head and in the midsection separately with Yoshii having no way to possibly defend himself. After a few shots Asis handed the body of Yoshii off to Christoph who then held him allowing Xander and Asis to now continue the attack on Yoshii. “This is getting brutal!” Justin yelled! “They need to stop this already! The man is busted wide open already and no doubt is in incredible pain!” ”Even I got to admit this is getting to be a bit much,” Jack agreed. “He deserved some payback for interference but this is going a bit far.” Finally in the ring the assault stopped and Christoph let the bloodied Yoshii fall face first and motionless to the mat. Somers stepped up and nudged him with his foot and still no movement came from Yoshii as Somers grinned and began to speak. ”First things first,” Somers said looking around at the crowd. “Troy Pierce I am giving you until this Sunday night at Tainted Love to start talking. You and I have gone through a lot over the years and it’s time for this game of yours to end. This Sunday night it will or you will suddenly find yourself standing on the unemployment line Monday morning. Of course if you fans notice there is no Troy Pierce sitting in the front row tonight and I guarantee you that won’t be happening at all here in San Francisco. ”Are you kidding me?” Justin asked. ”It think it’s fair,” Jack answered. “Maybe so but how can this even be the time for it right now?” Justin asked. “They have a gang assault going against Yoshii and he’s talking about Troy Pierce.” ”Hey we got two hours to kill,” Jack argued. “Might as well do it somehow.” “I suppose you all want this to stop don’t you?” Somers asked the crowd who gave him a cheer in response. “Well there is only one way that’s going to happen and that is if Strictly Prohibited marches his Jason Voorhees wannabe ass down to this ring right now and show the world what we in The Unlimited already know. We want him in this ring and unmasking himself to reveal once and for all that he is and always has been Tyler Davidson!” “This has gone too far!” Justin shouted. “They did all of this just to try and force Strictly Prohibited to unmask?” ”Well not for nothing but if Somers tells the guy to unmask he should listen,” Jack argued. “Somers is the boss after all.” ”Maybe so but there’s one problem with that,” Justin corrected. “Tyler Davidson is Somers’s boss so if it is Tyler Davidson under that mask he has no reason to do anything Somers tells him to do.” ”Damn logic,” Jack mumbled defeated. “Let’s go Davidson,” Somers yelled again. “We don’t have all day and if you don’t get out here we might have to do a bit more damage to your pal here.” Christoph and Asis again picked up Yoshii but instead of going for the chair Xander slid out of the ring and flipped over the apron. He searched under the ring briefly and then pulled out a baseball bat that was almost completely covered in barbwire. Xander slid into the ring with the weapon in his hands and walked right over to Yoshii who was held tight by Asis and Christoph and Xander brought the barbwire bat slamming right down into the skull of Yoshii as the barbs pierced the flesh of his face and drew even more blood to add to that which already soaked over and covered his face. “I don’t want to see this happen any longer Prohibited needs to come out here damn it!” Justin yelled. ”He could have saved a lot of trouble in there,” Jack added. “Yoshii’s blood is covering the ring mat now and he’s not done bleeding yet.” “Let’s go Davidson,” Somers continued. “If you don’t come out here we’re going to put an end to your little friend once and for all and then we’re going to come for you. Give him another one guys.” Xander handed the weapon over to Christoph and along with Asis the two of them again picked the limp body of Yoshii up and held him for Christoph who wound up. Just as Christoph brought the bat and barbwire slamming down into the skull of Yoshii, “Do You Call My Name” by RA began playing and a roar of approval went through the crowd as the black mask of Strictly Prohibited appeared on the eVotron followed by the words “Endeavor my curse! Endeavor my soul! Endeavor my Past!” “Thank god Strictly Prohibited is finally coming out here to end this insanity,” Justin said breathing a sigh of relief. ”It took him long enough,” Jack complained. “Apparently he doesn’t care too much for Yoshii after all.” ”I hardly think that’s fair,” Justin argued. “There’s no telling if Prohibited was even in the arena yet.” “Always making excuses,” Jack said shaking his head. After a few seconds Strictly Prohibited again appeared at the top of the ramp with his singapore cane in hand and he raised his arms to the cheers of the crowd before he looked down towards the ring. ”Get your ass down here now Davidson!” Somers called to him as he sat on the middle rope and held the ropes open for him. Prohibited just stood at the top of the ramp before slowly making his way down the ramp knowing it was the only way to end this vicious assault against his partner. Prohibited gets to the bottom of the ramp and quickly swings at Somers with his singapore cane as he sat on the ropes causing Somers to quickly back away to safety and drawing more attention from Christoph more than Xander or Asis. ”Christoph is just waiting for Prohibited to get in the ring,” Justin said. ”We all know the history between him and Davidson,” Jack replied. “I’m shocked Prohibited hasn’t already tried attacking Christoph since he returned.” ”Maybe that should tell you something,” Justin responded. “Christoph murdered Tyler’s fiancée so it would give him damn good reason to go after Christoph.” Prohibited finally hopped onto the ring apron and stared in at all of The Unlimited and the prone handcuffed body of his partner with blood still gushing out of his head and onto the now red colored ring mat. Prohibited watched them all before he slowly stepped into the ring and armed himself closely with the singapore cane. ”Don’t worry Tyler,” Somers said. “We’re not going to kick your ass just yet. First we’re going to give you the chance to unmask yourself. If you don’t then we WILL kick your ass and leave you here with Yoshii the bloody.” “Somers is a smug guy when he has the whole damn Unlimited there to back him up,” Justin said. ”Who wouldn’t be?” Jack asked. ”I’m sure plenty of people would be,” Justin answered. “It would be a bit more respectable if he was like that when they weren’t around too.” “Come on Tyler just take the mask off and save yourself a beating,” Somers said again taking a step closer to Prohibited. Prohibited obviously loosened up after Somers said that and he lowered the singapore cane down to his side as he stared at them all. Slowly Prohibited raised his other hand up towards his face and gripped the bottom of his black hockey mask as a smile crossed the face of Christoph. Inch by inch Prohibited began raising the mask off of his face before he finally ripped it clear off his face revealing someone that certainly wasn’t Tyler Davidson as Somers and Christoph both looked on with shocked looks on their faces. “What the hell?!” Jack yelled. “That’s not Davidson!” “No it’s not,” Justin agreed. “I know who it is though and so do you even if he does look a bit different from his EdW days as Suicidal Champion!” ”Huh?” Jack asked still confused as all hell. “It’s supposed to be Davidson!” ”But it’s not,” Justin answered. “That’s Nik VanCore!” Nik VanCore took advantage of The Unlimited’s shock at his appearance and suddenly sprung forward and drilled Somers with a sickening cane shot right across his face that dropped the eVolution Director of Operations and brought a huge roar from the crowd. Just as VanCore did that he was about to turn and nail Christoph but he was caught before he could by a chair being rammed right into his midsection by Asis. Christoph then ran forward and drilled him with a clothesline that dropped him to the mat. Xander, Christoph, and Asis all suddenly converged on the down nVc and began stomping him down into the mat. ”It looks like they got over their shock pretty quickly!” Justin said. “And now they’re ready to beat the hell out of VanCore too and he deserves it for tricking them damn it!” Jack continued complaining. The beating continued in the ring as VanCore tried covering up from their blows but the three of them were to much for him until another cheer went up from the crowd and coming down the ramp quickly were The HeartBreak Chick and Big Daddy Dreamer. Coming up right behind them was another man that was wearing an identical mask to the one that Yoshii wore out to the ring and he slid right in behind The Syndicate and came to stand side by side with them as they held off any further Unlimited attack. ”It looks like we got a bit of a standoff in the ring,” Justin said. ”More people sticking their noses in where they don’t belong,” Jack responded. “And who the hell is that masked guy? Isn’t that Yoshii handcuffed in the ring?” ”I thought it was but that masked guy is dressed exactly like Yoshii is too,” Justin answered. “I think we’re seeing double or something.” “Time to schedule that appointment with the eye doctor I guess,” Jack commented. Both sides stood on opposite sides of the ring as the standoff continued. Somers slowly got back to his feet and turned around for the first thing for him to see again was the image of their three adversaries standing there and he fell backwards a look of shock on his face until he backed up enough to bump into Xander who grabbed him by the shirt and lifted him back up. With that done The Unlimited slowly stepped in front of Somers who quickly took the chance to get out of the ring as both sides suddenly converged into a massive brawl in the ring. ”I don’t think The Syndicate really give a damn about Yoshii, VanCore, or even the other Yoshii but they have their own issues right now with The Unlimited and it’s apparently aligned all of them here tonight,” Justin continued. ”They are all trying to gang up on them damn it,” Jack yelled. “That’s not fair.” ”It’s no different than anything The Unlimited have done in the past,” Justin pointed out. “This time though they are on the receiving end. After all this we need to go to a commercial break though and hopefully get some order restored.” The brawl in the ring continued and the anti-Unlimited group was soon joined in the ring by the late arriving Luther Wisdom and Malcolm King who came from the back to help in the battle as the show went to a commercial break.
