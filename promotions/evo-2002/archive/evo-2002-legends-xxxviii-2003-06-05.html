Legends XXXVIII Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Air Canada Centre ~ Toronto, Ontario, Canada 7:59PM EST Thursday Night June 5th, 2003 After an awe struck Pay Per View, Legends comes to you in the Air Canada Centre in Toronto. The aftermath of what happened to the history of eVolution will no doubt be shocking. As we continue on with Xander as our new Universal champion, Sean Mason now holds the Continental strap, and Hedges has obtained the eVolved gold. Meanwhile Cameron Griffin will have a huge announcement concerning the newly reinstated Cell Division of eVolution and Tyler Davidson following his victory over Daniel Somers at From The Ashes will also be in the house and likely to have some things to say concerning the future of eVolution. What will happen at Legends? No one knows, watch or be eVolved! The first match up of the night, will most definitely take place inside of the newly built Cell. Question Beyond X will face off against a man known as Ali Khadafi to the end. Khadafi lately has been sparking interest with his promo's and is more than likely hoping for a big win over the man known as X. Last week at From The Ashes, Trevor Norman once again added another win to his résumé as he defeated Xero inside of the Cell. Trevor Norman has been on winning streak since his arrival, and new to eVolution Fighting: Josh Horroway will want to end that winning streak and prove to the world that he is the ultimate fighter. In the past two months, we've seen eVolution boost up the vignette's about Greatness, and last week at From The Ashes he announced that he will be debuting at the next Legends instead of July 3rd. Greatness has made such claims as to being the best in the business, but we in eVolution have yet to be proven that. Eric Knight quickly stepped up to the plate to prove him wrong. Knight wants to defeat this masked wrestler, and prove to the world how bad he truly is. Dane and Lee Exodus will be competing in singles action. Dane debuting at From The Ashes in the Evolved championship - Ladder match. Dane almost walking away the Evolved Champion, he competed very well with hardcore tactics and brawling skills. Dane signed to compete against someone well known around here, Team Evolution's very own Lee Exodus. Lee has competed against some of the toughest in the sport and has proved to be an excellent athlete... This will be action packed! This match, victory can be achieved via pinfall or submission. The first two falls/tap out will become the Unified tag champions and tag match rules apply. The third and final will become the Evolved Champion and the remainder of the match will be a free for all brawl. There will be no elimination, but standard rules will still be in effect. After weeks of Asis and Xander fighting inside the Unlimited faction, they have been giving the chance to prove that they are still both willing to keep the Unlimited name a fierce name, with Xander as eVolution's new Universal champion the standards are high and only they can hope to co-exist against the mammoth monsters of The BodySnatchers. Will Xander and Asis co-exist to win this for Unlimited, or will Unlimited fall hopelessly to the ground? A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Jason Jameson, James Kimson, Thug roC, Shocka and many of eVolution’s superstars! Backstage ~ And So It Begins….. Featuring Cameron Griffin Not normally does one man attempt to make such a dramatic entrance, but with Cameron Griffin the minute you saw he was set to make an announcement it was certain it was not going to be a low key affair. With the various camera and newspaper reporters on hand the stage was set for his ever imminent arrival. Positioned before a spectacular "Cell Division" backdrop logo stood a stand ready for Evolutions Commissioner to make his announcement, now all we needed was the show man himself to take centre stage. After a few minutes of waiting a shuffling sound can be heard behind the audience and making his way along the narrow aisle between the various rows of chairs was Mr. Griffin. It was of no surprise to see him in a silk shirt and jeans; his silk shirt having long been mocked back in his days within OSW by Dan Somers. In his right hand he carried a briefcase and in his left it appeared he had the audience. Like any great leader the excitement built around this announcement had been carefully planned out and ensured to have maximum impact. Ushering away his entourage he took the bold step into the limelight and with that fans, wrestlers and those on hand were all ears. "Ladies and gentlemen" Cameron said into the mic as he dropped his briefcase to one side. "Tonight marks a night in Evolution which will long be remembered as the night Evolution was taken in one hand" Cameron said raising his hand before the crowd, "and tested to the very extreme." As the words came out of Cameron’s mouth he clenched his fist and smiled. Camera flashes went off all around as he posed, but it was clear this was about a lot more than just him posing, this was about things being turned on their head here in Evolution. "You know in my many years in and around wrestling I have grown sick and tired of listening to one person after another announcing themselves as the most extreme athlete of his generation; or even the most extreme athlete period. Of those people making those brash statements, it tends not to be that long before their very reputation is left shattered as a man decimates the very livelihood they depend upon. I'm not a man who enjoys watching others suffer, but I am a man who enjoys those people around him overcome all odds to prove without a shadow of a doubt they are worthy of such recognition. 8 foot high" Cameron smiled, "eight foots worth of cold, hard and unforgiving steel. Imagine that" Cameron said leaning toward his mic, "imagine how it must feel to have your face smashed open against steel. Imagine the pain which must ravage your body upon impact and the bruises that remain long after; then imagine the courage of any man who fights through all of that!". "Over the past few weeks I have scoured the globe" Cameron said shaking his fist "and in that time I have found but a few men who I believe capable of surviving in such an environment. A few men who I believe both demand and are capable of existing within such a structure. I didn't guarantee these guys that they would see the light of day after a grueling match in the Cell, but I certainly was able to guarantee them the recognition as the hardcore fighters they truly dream of becoming. Imagine the belief and courage a man has to be able to take a pen and paper and potential condemn himself to potentially an early grave. The Cell is not for the faint of heart, its for the strong of mind and it promises to eclipse even those nightmares which wrestlers around the globe have long been haunted by and turn those nightmares into a leaving reality!" Taking his briefcase Cameron opted to pause and delve into the leather interior searching for a folder he then extracted and placed before him. As he flicked through the document the audience remained silent and in awe. "Ali Khadafi, Josh Horroway and Question Beyond X your time is tonight" Cameron announced only to be greeted by confusion. "Those three men will tonight take a firm step into a career in Evolution we hope will not come to an abrupt end tonight. I can not guarantee that will be the case, but there will be those with their hands clasped living in hope. Tonight along with Trevor Norman these men will announce their intentions and determine whether they wake to see their loved ones tomorrow. To some extent Trevor Norman proved he was capable at the PPV, but that was no more than a test, a test against a man who had not been chosen shall we say" Cameron smiled. "Xero tried and he failed. I expected it for during my travels I had seen the passion in Trevor Normans eyes and it was for that reason I offered him the chance to put his survival on the line; the chance to step into a domain not known by man before. Tonight though the PPV is forgotten and as I said these four men will step into the Cell in their respective matches. First Question Beyond X vs. Ali Khadafi. Then Trevor Norman vs. Josh 'Shockwave' Horroway". Cameron again paused, "for these men I suggest you do not hold back with your praise, nor leave anything un-said. No man is immortal and no man is without their failings. The Cell will not only test a mans mortality, but it will expose his failures in the most cold and callous way imaginable". "Now" Cameron said pausing again, "I could invite questions or I could even continue to depict graphically the task these four men face tonight in The Cell. I'm not though, why?? Simply because every question will be answered tonight and every picture I paint will be painted that much worse here tonight. Time counts down not only till the first Cell matches on Legends; but the last match on Legends perhaps for those four men. With that thought I bid you farewell". A huge explosion of chatter began and some tried to catch Cameron before he left the building. Brushing them to one side though Cameron left regardless. The bombshell had been dropped, but the aftermath awaited us here tonight.
