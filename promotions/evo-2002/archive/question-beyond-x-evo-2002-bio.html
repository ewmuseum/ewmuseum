The young black man has at a up down life. He was top of his class at a local High school in Queens.But he lived in Queens Bridge the aparments of what most would call the 'projects' of America. His has never known his father but his Mom has been with died just a year ago. She raised him all by hiself. She was a strong black woman who believed in many different things but one thing was for certain is she was a strong believer in the 'movemant' for a equal america. Ever since the age of six his mother,Tracy Braggs, would read him stories about the great Martin Luther King. Would read him several poems and even would read him some of his services he had when he preached at the churches. Of course young Malcom Braggs had memorized by the age of 12 the most famous of all Luther's speaches I have a dream.Also young Malcom found out he was named after the black leader Malcom X. Being named after a man kind of makes you look up to him an this was true with Malcom Braggs. At 16 his Junior year of High School he was reconized as one of the best writers in the state of New York with his report of X on a America.Now through out his high school career he played two sports. His favorite sport to play was Basketball. The one that brought him the most sucess was Football. His senior year in football he lead his high school to the state championship as they where victorious because of a 45 yard dash by Braggs.When he graduated high school he looked at his mom and said he wanted to go down in history. He looked all around him and saw the place he has lived his whole life, Queens, He looked at his mom and said he too was going to make a impact for him and all the people like him. So then he moved out the house not going to college. He believed he knew all he had to, so at the age of 18 he picked up a job at a Burger King and begin to work out in the gym at nights. He still stood there a year later at the age 19 with the question in his mind how could he make a impact. He went home to watch TV as he turned on an saw WWF now (WWE) and looked an noticed it had always been in front of his eyes. He has always watched wrestling since he could remember an that was the key. He had the body to survive against any man and his knowledge in the street with fighting could carry him as far as he wanted. From that moment on he has looked at fighting for his quest to answer everyone of his people s questions, to give America the free equal oppertunity for all, now at 20 he is here, he is at the Cell...CONTRARY to what people have assumed about X..he is a fighter..never as he wrestled just watched it on TV..he has fighten history from his neighborhood..he is a Fighter in Cell Divison.
