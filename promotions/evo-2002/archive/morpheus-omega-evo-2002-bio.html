Morpheus Omega Biography Nicknames The deadliest athlete in the game, God of Sleep and Dreams, Infinite of evil, Satan's Assassin... Height 6'10 Weight 323 Interests billiards, raves, golf, electronic music. women, working = money, patrying as much possible... Favorite Pro Wrestlers Kevin Nash, Chris Benoit, Kurt Angle, Rey Mysterio Fed Titles Twisted Reality - eVolution tag champions, Unified eVolution tag champions (3month + reign) Fed Awards 2002 eVo best new comer Biography soon to come Other Feds FDW - XWO - NrCw - RDWF Other Fed Awards FDW- Only Unanimous Champion, Hall of Famer... XWO- Only World Champion, Undefeated Streak, Hall of Famer... NrCw- 2001 Most Improved Wrestler (roleplayer), 2001 Deadliest Stable, 2001 Best Finisher, No Rules Champion (longest reign)... RDWF- Silver Champion... Wrestling Style Powerhouse Grappling/ technical & submission Finisher Move Code Omega or Code Omega from HELL!!! Finisher Description Reverse Diving Brainbuster ... Dives off one of two turnbuckles which faces the announce tables and hits the reverse brainbuster directly through the table... Finisher Setup Move Eternal Darkness Setup Description Standing at the top of the turnbuckle with the opponent and thrusts 180 degrees grappling the opponent and spinebusts them into the ring Other Moves Submissions: Distorted dreams as below, Front and Reverse Guillotine, Drop toe hold into an ankle lock, Ungodly Ruins as below, Infinite Grip as below, Escape from Reality as below, Insanity as below, Unforgiven as below, knee wrench lock, various choke locks, Camel Clutch, throat choke hold arching the opponents back over Omegas knee... Aerials: High leaping cannonball splash landing with his knees locked down on the opponents chest -also can pin following move, flying head butt, missile drop kick... Huge knee drop from an elevated level... Counters: Morpheus counters with offense, hes a rush figher... Favourites: Iron Fist (Morpheus occasionally carries around a six foot heavy linked iron chain and carries it with him ring side. Morpheus wraps the middle portion around his hand a few times and right hooks the opponent) ..... Falling Skies (Vertical Suplexe into Tornado DDT) ..... Downfall of Life (Military pressed high into the air and on the downfall hits a falling reverse ddt) ..... Ungodly Ruins (Morpheus triple powerbombs the opponent, snapping their body back and forth and crushing their backs off the mat and on the final one grabs the opponents legs and locks on a mean Boston crab) ..... Satan’s Sin (Morpheus runs up the second turnbuckle and leaps backwards and lands with two knees across the opponents chest) ..... Black Out (Jumping Knee thrust to the opponents chin) ..... Infinite Grip (Inverted Piledriver into front face lock) ..... Fading Memories (Spinning back elbow in the mouth) ..... Escape from Reality (Morpheus wraps the long iron chain around the opponents throat and monkey flips them over the ropes to choke and hang themselves) ..... Insanity (Reverse throat choke hold with opponent choking themselves with their own weight hoisted on Omegas back) ..... Distorted dreams (Fireman's carry into triangle choke hold) ..... Unforgiven (ankle lock but with his legs wrapped around the opponents hyper extending the knee at the same time) ..... Falling Sun (High leaping cannonball splash landing with his knees locked down on the opponents chest -also can pin following move) ..... Face of Death 'One' (Steps back and punts into the mouth of his opponent football style wearing doc marten boots) ..... Face of Death 'Two' (Tears the metal stairs and throws them face down, Morpheus front face slams the opponent on the steps and quickly stomps with impact on the back of their head while mouth down to the metal stairs) ..... Face of Death 'Three' (Usually following running turnbuckle bombs, while in tree of woe position. -Following shoulder thrusts can be used to set up the move as well- Chains the opponents feet to the turnbuckle and from the opposite turnbuckle runs and baseball slides into the face of the opponent) Entrance Music 'Sanitarium' by Metalica
