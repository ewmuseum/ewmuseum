Legends XXVII Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Arco Arena ~ Sacramento, California 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night March 5th, 2003 Tainted Love is now in the past and eVolution will move onto Unbreakable with Legends XXVII in Sacramento. We will certainly be hearing from Scott Frost at some point in the night as he will address the crowd concerning his newly won Universal Title and is also expected to make an announcement. Daniel Somers has also promised some news of his own concerning the Tag Team division and we will get to see first hand just what happened after Tainted Love went off the air. Add onto that we will also see a familiar face make his return to eVolution after a rather long absence. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Two teams that came up just short of capturing the Tag Team titles at Tainted Love due to outside influences will go head to head in a match that is certain to have an effect on the standings in the tag team division. The Cogs of War for the second straight week had been deprived of getting their business done by the team of Statuz Quo and Duzza, Playaz Inc. The two of them will certainly be keeping an eye out for any third appearance of the duo on this night as they try and topple the team of Nik VanCore and Yoshii Tanaka. Last week at Tainted Love, Troy Pierce finally broke his silence after several months and he did it with a challenge in mind. He issued a challenge to Daniel Somers to give him a match on Legends against anyone and if Pierce won he would get the chance to go one on one with Asis. Somers has accepted that challenge by Pierce but he refused to reveal the identity of Pierce’s opponent until “he was damn good and ready to reveal it.” On one side of the ring stands Big Daddy Dreamer, her partner in The Syndicate. On the other side of the ring is Luther Wisdom, the man she has held the eVolution Tag Team Titles with twice now. Stuck in the middle of it all of course is The HeartBreak Chick as she must officiate this match between the two men she has recently known as partners. Dreamer and Wisdom both are no doubt feeling a bit deprived coming out of Tainted Love and will use this match as a starting point for Unbreakable. The question remains though just what will HBC do as the referee? With the events of two of the top matches at Tainted Love a tag team match has been booked for Legends pitting the unlikely team of Shocka and James Kimson against Sovereign and Morpheus Omega. Shocka and Kimson are by no means friends in the slightest way but both men would no doubt love the opportunity to get their hands on their opponents for the night. It was Omega who double crossed Shocka and attacked him at Tainted Love that might have contributed to his loss of the match and it was obvious to anyone that saw it that nothing had been settled between Kimson and Sovereign. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Scott Frost, Xander, Seara, Asis and many of eVolution’s superstars!
