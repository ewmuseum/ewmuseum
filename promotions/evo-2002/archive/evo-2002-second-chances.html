Second Chances

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestling has been going through a tough time as of late, and the most talked about items on everyone's mind has been what will be the next big federation. Everytime a new corporation has opened up in the past year, the big talk has been about names from the past being given a new chance, and eVolution has been no different. The closing of nEw and EdW left many men out in the cold, and the new hybrid, eVolution has given many men a new shot at the greatness that a career in wrestling can provide. Thus for the first feature article for eVolution, I thought I would write a tidbit about several wrestlers who are getting another chance to leave on a higher note. Certainly "The Natural" Matt Boone is thanking his lucky stars about the opening of eVolution. Weeks before his debut in nEw, the federation closed its doors. Hopes of an EdW launch were shut down with EdW's quick end. Without another connection in the business, and with the evaporation of indy feds around the country, Boone looked to be doomed to regional federations, far from the bright lights. Even worse he could have headed back to construction, or whatever other job he could muster. With eVolution up and running, Matt works for a well-known company in which he can build himself a name. Though his humility doesn't allow him to admit it, the kid has all the tools to become one of the biggest names to offer for the company, and thankfully he's getting the chance to show it via the opening of eVolution. Shifting away from Boone, we look at a man that appears to be an antagonist to him, Sovereign. The last time anyone in wrestling saw Sovereign was Old School Wrestling, the fledgling company ran by Cameron Griffin. No one is quite sure what happened to him after that, but his descriptions of the hell he's been through since doesn't make one envision a perfect life. Somehow he's become free and "Set loose" throughout eVolution, and no one has gotten an explanation as to how and why. There are more questions than answers in regards to Sovereign, but now he gets another chance to make a huge impact, and if his performance at American Nightmare is any indication, you can be sure he will. While Sovereign hasn't been seen since the closing of his last federation, it wasn't a federation that forced Justin Famous from the ring. Personal problems and a knee injury at the hands of Cori Simmons forced him away from the business he loved. At the height of his popularity, Famous began feuding with Cori Simmons. Simmons asked him to come along with him in his "Marxist quest". When Famous refused, Simmons threatened that he would pay the ultimate price. First he took his wife, and after that he took away his knee in one of the more memorable contests in EdW history. The feud, credited by Simmons as the best he ever had, ended the EdW run for Justin Famous. Now rumor has it the knee is healed and there are many potential suitors outsearching for him every night. Participation in eVolution will only help the story of Justin Famous grow larger. Perhaps the most troubling aspect for many involved with the conclusion of EdW was the knowledge that Xander, perhaps the most respected man backstage, was never going to hold the EdW Universal Championship. Many of the wrestlers in the back constantly commented on the lack of title shots that Xander received. It looked though, that after Malcolm King grabbed the championship from Shane Lawless, that Xander's Universal Championship chances were higher, as the feud that the two men were embroiled in was certain to lead to a title shot or two for Xander. The feud and the federation were cut short. Here in eVolution though, Xander will have the opportunity to hold the same Universal Championship with the same legacy. How he and the other four men will fare here in eVolution is unknown, but one thing is certain, eVolution will continue to provide chances for a multitude of talent to put on the greatest show that they can provide. Gabriel Gordon
