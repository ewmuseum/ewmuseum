Legends XXII Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Delta Center ~ Salt Lake City, Utah 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night January 22nd, 2003 Last week was the memorial show for L2J but this week eVolution will push on to New Year’s eVolution. Shocka had turned the memorial for L2J into a complete mockery and this week gets some time off for his troubles as he’ll sit back and wait to see who it is he’ll be facing next Sunday night. The Continental Title tournament will also be wrapped up during the course of the show. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! Last week Rockwell and Tanaka were on opposite sides of the ring in a triple threat match but this week it’s down to the two of them teaming together to battle against Angel and Lee eXodus. Since arriving in eVolution, Rockwell has apparently been stalking eXodus and now the two of them will be in the same ring while Angel will be looking to get some revenge for The Unlimited against Yoshii Tanaka for his interference in their affairs last week. Last week both Asis and Big Daddy Dreamer walked out with victories in their prospective matches in the Continental Title tournament. This week it will be the two of them going one on one with the title hanging in the balance. Dreamer last week got an unlikely and probably unwanted assist from The HeartBreak Chick as an errant super kick cost Angel the chance to battle Asis. Asis still bent out of shape over his injury at Nightmare Before Christmas and even more so following the interference of Yoshii Tanaka in his plans last week and he looks to make Dreamer pay for it now. Luther Wisdom had his plans to eliminate Malcolm King foiled last week thanks to Xander of all people. This week Wisdom will try and extract some revenge against the man who foiled his plans while also plotting some other plans to rid himself of the specter of Malcolm King and discover the answers concerning his former partner, Xavier Citizen. Xander meanwhile continues to play his trump card with The One in his corner and also the mystery surrounding his new friend which the ultimatum was issued to Somers over. Frost, Jameson, and Sovereign all have one thing in common. They were all left lying on the mat courtesy of Shocka and Morpheus Omega last week following the big tag team match. Now the three of them all get the chance to earn a shot at Shocka for New Year’s eVolution with Malcolm King thrown into the mix by Daniel Somers. King’s threat to get the title off Shocka for him seemed to have worked a bit better than he expected or perhaps even wanted to this time around. It seems that King might have actually played himself right into the hands of Somers and will now have to content with three of the top stars in eVolution. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Troy Pierce, Seara, Strictly Prohibited, The HeartBreak Chick, and many of eVolution’s superstars! Backstage ~ The Angel In Me Featuring Angel We see Angel there, with his arms grasped across a bar pulling himself up, then back down in a repetitive process. He continues this form of motion until Monica Angel slowly throws herself into the locker room area behind Angel. Angel obviously heard her enter since he turned his head and looked at her as she stood their, but he acted as if he paid her no attention. He continued to pull himself, up, then down again in that same repetitive motion. The silence in the room, besides Angel's grunting was broken by Monica when she spoke... "Um...Angel?" He continued his exercise, as if he were ignoring her, in fact, that's exactly what he was doing. He continued to act as if he wasn't listening but Monica continued to gain his undivided attention. "..Angel?" Angel finally gave her recognition and stopped. He lowered himself from the metal bar and turned his body toward her, shirtless with his muscles bulging out toward her he stood there stiffly and awaited for the reason he was interrupted. He tilted his head some and lowered his head for about a second before throwing it back upward into regular position, his hair cascading behind and out of his face. "I've got a few questions for you, if you'd like to answer them..." He uttered toward him. She held a microphone in front of her chin, staring at him awaiting his answer. "...Alright. What are they?" He stood their and folded his arms across each other, but his attention was divided once more. He turned his body toward his bag and headed over toward it. Monica then began to ask her questions. "You lost to Big Daddy Dreamer last week due to HBC's interference, and HBC is an ally of yours. How do you feel about that?" She extended her hand with the microphone still in it but Angel was speaking loud enough to be heard regardless. Angel pulled out some Old Spice from his duffle bag, pulled off the cap, and through it into the bag. As he started applying it under his arms, he began speaking, "It's cool. Everything’s JUST peachy. Normally, I’d be looking for her so I could stomp her, but she's okay. It was obviously a mistake and I'm all cool with it. It's not the first time I’ve been screwed before." He walked back over to the bag and keeled over, rummaging for something. "Ah, I see. What do you feel about this whole L2J dilemma and everything that's going on as of right now?" Angel stood up and in his hand was a can of AXE deodorant body spray, Phoenix was the scent. He slowly extended his hand about 6 inches away from him and squeezed the top. A scented mist erupted from the little can and attached itself onto Angel's flesh. He looked over toward Monica and through the AXE back into the bag. "Not a thing to do with me, Not my dilemma, not my problem..." "Well...that was pretty straight forward...anyway. Last question. You're about to go out there against a guy who attacked at you last week, a guy whom you've faced before, and a guy you know nothing about. Surely you have mixed emotions about this dilemma." Angel folded his arms across his chest and tilted his head, and with a sarcastic worried feeling he smiled some. "All I have to say about that is I’m going to smack down on anyone who gets in my way. Basically, I don't care if I have to do the job myself. I'd rather have it that way, but Lee Excellent or whatever the hell is name is had better get his shit right. He never had the one up on me, I was sick that week. He'll see how well I am now if he gets in my way. Justin Fritz: King and Sovereign are the only two men to have ever held the Universal Title before and it’s a place that both men would like to make it to again. Jack Tyler: King might want it but he’s not going to be able to manipulate his way into it this time around. Justin Fritz: You can never be to certain with that guy. He’s a sneaky son of a bitch to say the least. Jack Tyler: Now that I agree with. If he wasn’t a wrestler he’d probably be conning some people out of money in the New York subways with his card tricks. The bell rings to start the match and all four men stand in their respective corners plotting their first move. Jameson and Frost slowly begin inching their way towards one another and it looks like a plan is being formed between the two but Sovereign and King cut off all discussions before they can happen and attack each man. King is on Jameson and pounding him with right hands as Sovereign takes a similar approach against Frost backing him away and into the corner. Sovereign drives a hard elbow right up and into the face of Frost before taking him out of the corner and slamming down hard to the mat. Sovereign turns from the corner just in time as Jameson begins overtaking King with rights hands and follows it with a knee to the midsection but before he can follow through with anything else Jameson is leveled from behind with a big clothesline by Sovereign. Justin Fritz: Sovereign just saved King from a little bit of trouble there. Jack Tyler: That’s a surprise considering the history between the two of them. Justin Fritz: Sovereign more than likely just wants to get his hands on Jameson at this point. Sovereign and King both begin stomping down on Jameson as he lies on the mat but in the midst of it all Sovereign winds back and nails King with a right hand knocking him back into the corner. Sovereign reaches down and grabs hold of Jameson lifting him back to his feet before turning quickly around and whipping Jameson right into King in the corner. Sovereign follows it quickly and splashes both men as they are pressed into the corner. He turns slowly around and just at that moment he gets nailed with a super kick from Frost. Frost falls on top and makes the cover. One……two………Sovereign kicks out! Justin Fritz: Frost tried taking advantage of Sovereign’s putting both King and Jameson out temporarily and almost got the win. Jack Tyler: He wasn’t close at all. Frost just got a lucky shot in. Justin Fritz: That lucky shot almost got him a three count. Frost gets back to his feet and looks to continue his attack on Sovereign but he turns instead to King who is slowly coming out of the corner. Frost comes charging at him but King moves out of the way and Frost just manages to stop himself from colliding with the turnbuckles. He turns around and blocks a right hand from King before firing off one of his own and knocking King back a step or two. Frost comes out of the corner and goes for a clothesline but King ducks quickly under and grabbing Frost from behind he brings him down with a neckbreaker. King rolls quickly over and grabs the arm of Frost as he tries getting him into position for the Questionable Sanity but Frost begins making his way to the ropes and before he can even do that Jameson drives a kick right into the back of King’s head. Justin Fritz: Jameson might have just made the save for Frost there. Jack Tyler: There’s only one winner so he better make the save as often as possible. Justin Fritz: Unless of course he’s the one making the cover or going for the submission. Jack Tyler: Which won’t be happening of course. Justin Fritz: Says you. Jameson stomps a foot down across King and then turns before giving one to Frost for good measure. Jameson turns around and is caught with a kick to the midsection by Sovereign who pulls Jameson in for a spinebuster as he drives him down into the mat and goes for the cover. One……..two……..kickout by Jameson! Sovereign gets back to his feet quickly and lifts Jameson up with him again before setting him up in a suplex position. Sovereign lifts him up and goes for the Pure Insanity but Jameson uses the momentum and floats over behind Sovereign before taking him over with a german suplex and a bridge. One………two……….Sovereign kicks out! Jack Tyler: More luck by Jameson. Justin Fritz: Looked more like a great counter move there by Jameson to me. Jack Tyler: You’re a fool. King and Frost are back on their feet and trading right hands back and forth again as Frost drives a knee into King’s midsection and sets him up for the Frost Effect but before Frost can drive him down King grabs onto the top rope and Frost drives himself back first into the mat. King grabs both of Frost’s legs and folds him up for a cover but Frost kicks out before the referee even realizes what is happening. King runs into the ropes and goes for a springboard moonsault onto Frost but Frost gets his knees up just in time and King lands down hard on them. Frost grabs onto King and quickly rolls him into a small package. One……..two………Jameson makes the save! Justin Fritz: Jameson makes another save and I don’t think King would have kicked out after that. Jack Tyler: He would have somehow or another. Jameson drives another kick down into Frost before picking him up to his feet and driving him with a right hand. Jameson scooped Frost up and went for his Taste of Reality but Frost slipped free and behind Jameson he drove him face first into the mat with the Cold Front as he goes for the quick cover. One……….two…………Sovereign just barely makes the save! Sovereign pulls Frost up off of Jameson allowing Jameson to roll out of the ring and to relative safety. Sovereign picks Frost up to his feet and measures him for a big right hand but before the blow can be landed Frost is tripped up and dragged to the outside by Xander and his masked accomplice. Justin Fritz: Again?! Now what the hell are the two of them doing out here?! Jack Tyler: It’s time! We’re going to find out just who the hell he is! Justin Fritz: Couldn’t they pick something OTHER than the main event?! Jack Tyler: Guess not. Xander pulls Frost around and the masked man drives a chair down across his skull dropping Frost to the mat. As that happens Sovereign ducks a right hand from King inside the ring and turns it quickly into the Sovereignity and goes for the cover. One…………two……………Jameson yanks Sovereign out of the ring and outside the ring before the three can be counted. Jameson and Sovereign begin their own battle on the outside of the ring showing little interest in what’s now happening in the ring as the masked man and Xander both slide into the ring. Justin Fritz: Sovereign and Jameson have apparently forgotten all about the damn match now! Jack Tyler: That won’t last. It would be over if not for that blasted Jameson! Justin Fritz: He was trying to win this one for himself but now they’ve got a bit more to worry about. Xander gets back to his feet in the ring and waits patiently as King slowly pushes his way back up as well. King turns and Xander kicks him in the midsection before pulling him out in front of the masked man and he slams the chair down across the skull of King. King falls to the mat and the masked man slams the chair down across his shoulder and head again trying to continue the damage as Xander keeps pounding down on him with kicks. Justin Fritz: Why are they focusing on King?! Jack Tyler: Why not? He stuck his nose in their business earlier. Justin Fritz: I think that might have a lot to do with it actually. King tries getting back up but he’s put down again with another chair shot down across his back until Xander steps in between the masked man and stops him. Xander walks to the ropes and calls for the mic from Libby. Xander: The nightmare never does end with you does it King?! You thought that you could come back and cause more trouble. You thought that you could try and infringe on the greatness that WE have made The Unlimited! King tries getting back to his feet again but is knocked right back down with another chair shot from the masked man as Xander continues to speak. Xander: The problem with that King is I want nothing from you. I don’t even want to kick your ass any longer! I have been there and done that more times than I can count. This man standing right here doesn’t even want your ass kicked though King. So you must be asking yourself now just WHY we are singling you out. Would you like to know that? King can’t do anything to form any kind of answer to Xander other than try and get to his feet again only to be knocked right back down. Xander: It’s your creation King. You created it and your very own creation Malcolm is now coming back to finish you! The One has singled you out for extinction and that’s exactly what we are going to do. The One wants the honor for himself however and on Sunday night he will have it…….but first this man will get his own chance for some payback! Xander stepped to the side and the man drove the steel chair down across King again before dropping it to the mat and bringing his hands up to the mask. He unties the strings at the back of his mask and then rips the mask off allowing his hair to finally fall free. Before you can see the face though he quickly grabs King and like he did Wisdom he picks him up into an implant DDT and brings him face first down across his knee. The man then turns and gets back to his feet again and the cameras focus in on his face. Jack Tyler: He’s back?! Justin Fritz: I don’t believe it! That no good murdering son of a bitch is back?! Jack Tyler: You’re damn right! Erin Christoph is back! Sure enough the camera focuses on the features of Christoph standing alongside Xander with sinister smiles on their face as they stand over King. Both men turn their sights and see Frost climbing back into the ring and Christoph’s eyes go ablaze as he lunges forward at the man who had helped eliminate him from eVolution. He grabs Frost and begins pounding him with rights and lefts as he picks him up to his feet. Christoph grabs Frost around the throat with both hands and lifts him into the air before driving him down into the mat with a baldo bomb. Justin Fritz: And now it’s Frost the two of them are attacking! Jack Tyler: Xander’s not doing anything! He’s trying to stop him actually. Xander is trying to restrain Christoph yelling at him to focus before reaching down and grabbing Frost before he drags him over and puts him down on top of King for the cover and the referee has no choice other than to count. One………..two…………three! Frost won the match but was obviously not safe yet as Christoph didn’t look to be at all finished. He grabbed a handful of Frost’s hair again and picked him back up to his feet but before he can do anything a roar goes through the crowd. Justin Fritz: Christoph is looking for some payback but it might have to wait! Jack Tyler: Jameson again?! Damn him! Justin Fritz: It’s not just him! Jameson had momentarily at least dispatched Sovereign and was sliding back into the ring to try and help his friend but that wasn’t the source of the crowd cheering as Seara also came flying down the ramp with a lead pipe in her hand. The two of them slid into the ring and seeing the weapons in her hand both Xander and Christoph made their retreat to safety before disappearing into the crowd. Seara drops the pipe to the mat and quickly goes to check on Frost as Jameson is on his feet and keeping an eye out for Xander and Christoph but he doesn’t see Sovereign on his feet. Sovereign grabs Jameson and yanks him from the ring before anyone realizes what is happening and has the Last Breath locked in tight. Jameson fights and struggles but he’s quickly overpowered and falls down to the ground. Justin Fritz: This is getting way to far out of hand. Jack Tyler: Could be worse. Justin Fritz: How? Jack Tyler: Like that. Justin Fritz: They’re just watching but we’re out of time. We’ll see everyone on Sunday! Seara tends to Frost in the ring and neither of them knows what had just happened to Jason but as Frost gets back to his feet he notices standing at the top of the ramp pointing and laughing are Morpheus Omega and the Universal Champion Shocka.
