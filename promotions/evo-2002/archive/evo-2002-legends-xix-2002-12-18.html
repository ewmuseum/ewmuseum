Legends XIX Transcript Live on OSE! From The XCel Energy Center~ St. Paul, Minnesota 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night December 18th, 2002 Last week more plans began to unravel in the stable war and something that perhaps no one saw coming might have begun. Xander has promised everyone an early Christmas present and the rumors have stated that at Nightmare Before Christmas, the nightmare for everyone will begin once again. This week on Legends more battles for the pay per view continue to heat up as Sovereign looks to send a message to Impact in a battle against Luther Wisdom. Wisdom must have his focus put on The Unlimited and the Tag Team Title shot against them but that’s only if he’s able to survive this match against Sovereign. His partner Xavier Citizen continues his battle against Troy Pierce and Morpheus Omega and Carl Ca$h look to settle an old score between the two of them. All this and much more will lead us on the final turn until Nightmare Before Christmas! Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Old School Entertainment! Jason Steele made his return two weeks ago and after watching the success of Shocka and Morpheus Omega the past few weeks and being screwed by Shodan last week he tries grabbing hold of some of the New-TV momentum against Ryan Flesco. Flesco is an experienced wrestler though new to eVolution and looks to continue his quick start with a victory over an already established member of the company. Flesco has already had some dealings with New-TV which have left a sour taste in his mouth and he’ll be sure to keep an eye over his shoulder this time around. After last week’s encounter between these two men this match was quickly signed pitting Omega against the newly arrived Carl Ca$h. Carl made his debut last week during Omega’s match with HBC taking on Dreamer and Sean Mason. Carl and Omega got into a slight scuffle which eventually led to all hell breaking loose and Omega losing his temper costing his team the victory by disqualification. Now he gets a chance to take out some of his aggression against Carl Ca$h, a man who Omega is certainly not strangers with. Two weeks ago Pierce returned, and this past week he returned to his winning ways with a hard fought victory against Xavier Citizen in a ULWN Rules Match. That didn’t sit well with Xavier who immediately challenged Pierce to a rematch but this time it would be fought under last man standing rules. Despite his own health problems and pending battle against The Unlimited, Citizen still pushes on and will try his luck to avenge last week’s defeat against the new and improved DiscoKid. While his partner finds himself paired off against Troy Pierce once again, Luther Wisdom finds himself being fed to wolves so to speak as he battles against Sovereign in a falls count anywhere match. Sovereign has long claimed to be a master of falls count anywhere matches and is apparently using this match as a way to send a message to “The Hollywood Monster” Impact. The two men are now slated to go head to head in just over a week at Nightmare Before Christmas and thanks to the deal with his estranged wife Leya, Sovereign has managed to make it into a falls count anywhere match which could mean some serious trouble for Impact. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Xander, Asis, Tommy Michaels, The Notorious and many more! Carl can be heard mumbling "Omega you are going to pay. Rest assure that you have not heard the last of me!" he pauses trying to fight the pain and arch his back but the paramedics hold him down. Carl screams out of half rage and from the pain he has endured, the sound is drowned out by the war cries of another. Almost like he appears from the shadows. Morpheus Omega can be seen running full tilt with the iron chain wrapped around his hand. The paramedics noticing Morpheus at the last second as he cracks the one in the jaw and knocks him off his feet, he flies face down into the asphalt. The second one holds his hands up, clearly showing he is not trying to impose threat, but merciless Morpheus hooks him with the left and thrusts into an uppercut rotating his body and almost takes the paramedics head off; he cracks the back of his head of the ground. Both paramedics lying on the ground unconscious and no one to help the injured Carl Cash as he stares with his eyes wide open. Carl struggles being strapped down and all, he tries to loosen his hands that are conveniently unable to be used. Morpheus drapes the iron chain around his neck and stares at Carl with a sick look in his eyes. Morpheus starts running with the stretcher as fast as he possibly can, he heads right towards the ambulance directing the stretcher straight for it. Nearing the ambulance now with a burst of power, Morpheus rams the stretcher while flipping it at the same time. Cash hits with hard impact off the ambulance; the metal of the stretcher heard loud off the side of the ambulance. The body of cash connects like a pancake effect. The stretcher lands upside down and Morpheus throws the chain down in the surrounding area. He flips the stretcher back on its wheels to view the disorientated Carl Cash. Morpheus mounts punches to the face of the helpless Carl Cash, he steps back and breathes heavily for a moment. He then takes one last driving downward; with an elbow shot that flips the stretcher on its side. Morpheus forcefully tears the straps breaking every last one of them to free his victim. The face of Cash now a sea of blood. Morpheus pulls the arm of Cash while he falls downward and launches of the body of Carl Cash high into the air and colliding off the ambulance once again. Carl falls straight backwards and directly on to his weakened and injured back. Morpheus scrambles, he lifts the stretcher back on its wheels again and looks back and forth at the helpless Cash and the stretcher. Morpheus pulls cash to his feet once again. Cash stumbles and almost falling back down, but is caught in the wrong hands. This situation clearly not well for Cash. Morpheus grapples and lifts him high into the air in the reverse brainbuster position and dives. Cash feels the pain of another Code Omega this time, the stretcher folds upon immediate impact. Morpheus nods with that sick grin again, he stares at Carl like he is absolutely amazed by this carnage. Morpheus picks him up by the scruff of his neck and drags him over to the ambulance and opens the back door. Morpheus throws him into the back and slams the door back shut. Morpheus walks to the front of the ambulance and approaches to open the driver's side door. Where is Morpheus taking Carl Cash??? Morpheus looks like he has seen a ghost. Sean Mason leaps off the top of the ambulance screaming out of rage, he lands on his shoulders and attempts to hurricanrana the six-foot ten giant. Unsuccessful, Morpheus thrusts one hundred and eighty degrees and completes a powerbomb, he turns again and powerbombs him even harder. Morpheus turns once again and hits a free falling powerbomb. Morpheus quickly locks on a tight boston crab. Mason screams but shows no sign of wanting to give up and still looking rough from the previous week of his and Omega's confrontation with a windshield. Mason with his eyes wide open, he fights the hold. Mason as a last effort, he stretches out toward the wall. Digging his nails into its structure he uses it to turn and gain some height, but in the process putting himself under more of a strain. Even Morpheus looks puzzled as to his intentions as he looks down in pity at his prey. Suddenly though pushing off against the wall he uses the spring to get within touching distance. In a move that is a mixture of luck and skill he is able to outstretch his right hand as he falls to the floor and rake the eyes of Morpheus, this breaking the hold. Morpheus stumbles back rubbing his eyes from the stinging sensation. Mason staggers to his feet and hits the Midas touch, he sends Morpheus stumbling. Capitalizing on the situation he spears the knee of Morpheus Omega, he takes him down hard. Mason back to his feet stomps the face of Morpheus with fierce shots and major aggression; he puts more into each and every blow. Sean screams at Morpheus, "I got your message! Now I hope your getting mine you sick son of a b***h" with that Sean leans down, cocks his fist back for a final time and lands a haymaker with power and fury like we have never seen from him before. Mason drops and locks down his arms around the face of Morpheus; he leans back into the Enigma. The hold looking like it couldn't be broken if anyone wanted to attempt it. The bottled up rage inside Mason is coming out all at once. Morpheus screams from the painful submission with the tables turned at this point. Moments later with time passing, Mason keeps Morpheus in this locked down position. Morpheus screams and looks physically exhausted from his long night. Morpheus refuses to tap. "You want to leave so soon!?!" Sean Mason screamed as his eyes begin to burst bloodshot with rage looking down at his victim beneath him. Looking up toward the heavens a grin covers Sean's face. It's a sickening grin, as he seemingly reveals in the delight that he is causing another human pain, but regardless he shows no sign of ceasing; Sean feels vindicated. Sean having been embarrassed dismantled and tortured on Legends; this had been the only revenge, which would have sufficed. Morpheus was now on the back foot, sure Sean is still the underdog; but this attack could act as the catalyst. Multiple officials and security rush the scene. They break apart Sean Mason and Morpheus Omega and separate them. Morpheus panting still, like he is having difficulty breathing. Mason holds the back of his neck. He still looks rough from the previous legends as well with the scrapes and road rash now semi-healed. Sean suddenly unleashes a verbal rant as he yells "Nightmare before Christmas you will play my game. I will make you tap like the oversized bitch you are! This is what I propose, Submission match for my Rising Star Championship!!! This isn't about a petty fall anymore, last Legends you made things so personal. I don't want a fall; I want you to scream I QUIT!! I want to make you look me in the eyes and beg for mercy..." Morpheus struggles with the security holding his arms back. Normally he would be able to break free but his body has just been drained of all energy. Morpheus breathing heavy, he replies screaming "Pick your own funeral Mason, but why don't we make this match even more interesting by incorporating no disqualification. NO DQ Submission match for your championship! I am your nightmare Mason and I think its time to exchange gifts for Christmas a little early. You have something that I will take and I will leave you with the most life threatening injuries this industry has ever imagined!" he retorts and following with another scream of rage. Scene fades.
