Legends XXXVII Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From The Halifax Metro Centre ~ Halifax, Nova Scotia 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night May 21, 2003 There is only one last show before the huge From The Ashes pay per view hits the airwaves and it comes to you live from The Halifax Metro Centre in Halifax, Nova Scotia. The night will be full of excitement with two titles being on the line and no doubt one last encounter between Scott Frost and Xander as they get prepared for their big Universal Title match at From The Ashes. And the final battle lines will be drawn as Legends come to a close and a new day prepares to dawn upon eVolution following From The Ashes. Legends is the only place you can find out all the answers and catch all the battles, Wednesday night only on the network that cares about what the people want; Tru-TV! There was another successful debut last week for Profusion and now this week he will battle against NTICW’s very own Xero even if Daniel Somers and the rest of NTICW would love to disown him. Both of these men are in line for a spot in From The Ashes eVolved Title Ladder match but only one of them will actually make it there and it will be the man who makes the best showing in this battle. Profusion comes through with a lot of experience in big matches while Xero is a relative rookie and will be hard pressed against a man like Profusion. Trevor Norman made quite a statement in his debut match last week and in the end left Nik VanCore with a severely sprained ankle and out of action for an undisclosed period of time. This week he goes up against another member of Team eVolution and the number one Frostcicle, Lee eXodus. Many people could perhaps be a bit upset with the actions of Trevor Norman last week and that could even include the referee for this match but if he has his way Lee eXodus will no doubt be joining Nik VanCore on the injured list very quickly. Last week Siege made some quick work of Hate’s tag team partner Homicide and the end result was Homicide being fired for his disgraceful performance in the ring. Now this week Hate finds himself forced into a must win situation if he wants to keep his job and in order to do that he has to survive an encounter with Siege who seems to have quite a fire burning within him and has even caught the attention of Daniel Somers very quickly. Ever since winning the eVolved Title, Malcolm Luther Xavier has been on a roll by hook or by crook and now he finds himself challenged by what could without a doubt be his biggest test to date as champion. Not only does he have to face Hedges, a man he barely managed to defeat in the past and only did thanks to the assistance of Xero, but he must also now contend with the person he defeated for the eVolved Title, Seara. This will be Seara’s first shot at the gold since she lost it to Malcolm at Unbreakable and no doubt she would love the chance to recapture that gold. This tag team match is just a preview of things to come at From The Ashes as The Syndicate and Twisted Reality will finally do battle but this time in a non title encounter and the ring will be surrounded by America’s Guardians, Savage Society, The BodySnatchers, Tribute, and St. Violence as lumberjacks to make sure there is no funny business. Unless of course they are the ones initiating it. Sean Mason defeated Seara last week to earn this shot and the week before Yoshii Tanaka robbed his former partner Nik VanCore of a shot at this match with his mist induced victory. Now this week Sean Mason and Yoshii will go one on one with the winner receiving a title shot for the Continental Title on Sunday night at From The Ashes. It will be Yoshii’s first shot at the gold if he’s victorious but for Mason it will be perhaps one final chance to finally capture the gold that has eluded him for so long now in eVolution. These two men are scheduled to go one on one at From The Ashes but after a demand from Ronan Pierce he gets Shocka a few days early as well and made the promise that when all is said and done Shocka won’t even make it to From The Ashes. Shocka is still looking for some payback as a result of Ronan’s sneak attack on him two weeks ago that left Shocka lying and double teamed by Ronan and Erin Christoph. After months of work and a lot of underhanded tactics Thug roC has finally gotten himself a shot at some gold as he’ll challenge Asis in the main event with the Continental Title on the line. Thug roC is now coming off a big victory over former Universal Champion Jason Jameson last week in six person tag team action meanwhile Asis stunned the world when he pinned Scott Frost in the center of the ring last week in a non title match. Now with the winner of Sean Mason and Yoshii Tanaka waiting in the wings one of these two men will have to survive this match and then look forward to From The Ashes on Sunday night. A lot more action is expected to take place as all the stars of eVolution will be in attendance including Xander, Scott Frost, Jason Jameson, James Kimson and many of eVolution’s superstars!
