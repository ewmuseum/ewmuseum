Legends LV Transcript Live on Tru-TV! From Saporro, Japan ~ Saporro Dome 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night October 29th, 2003 In The Ring ~ Match Making Featuring James Kimson and Leya Forth It’s Time... Let the Hype Begin. The arena filled with fans is now on its feet as Stupify hits the speakers in the Saporro Dome. The light show dances around as the curtains part and out walks James Kimson, The Hype, and the Universal Champion in eVolution. Wearing a muscle “Believe The Hype T-shirt” not sporting his leather jacket this week wearing trak pants and the Title belt slung over his shoulder the champion makes his way down to the ring and up the ring steps as the fans continue to give him a huge pop. Kimson steps between the ropes and into the ring, mounting the nearest corner hoisting the Belt into the air for the fans to see, the flash bulbs from the digital cameras sparkle through the capacity crowd on hand as Kimson looks out to them, not with his usual smile, it appears that this time Kimson really means business. He dismounts and walks to the far side of the ring where Libby Bowman is waiting, holding a microphone for the Champion and hands it to him. Kimson nods to Bowman as the lights come back up, the music starts to die off, Kimson doesn’t want to wait for chants, or cheers. He’s not in a good mood. “So Last week we went off the air and it seemed to me like things were not going my way” said the Champion. “Basically, we all know what happened. I was wrestling Jay Jay Jameson for this title here when Shocka decided to come on down to this ring here and not only cost me the match via count out but beat the crap out of me with a steel chair.” Boos echo through out the arena. Kimson looks up and waits for a pause, then continues. “Now, Shocka. Let’s get one thing straight, as you seem to have a selective memory about a lot of things. First of all, I don’t have a problem with admitting I got my ass kicked last week. That wasn’t the first time it’s happened and damn well won’t be the last time. I plan on taking a good number of lickings before I hang up these boots and hell, before I even let this title go. So what’s to gloat about Shocka? I bet I could break every one of your ribs if I wanted to attack you from behind with a chair like a coward. But instead I’d much rather do it with my own bare hands with you looking me in the eye at least trying to be a man” Kimson has promoted a cheer from the fans. He looks into the camera, intensity in his eyes. “So you kicked my ass last week. You claim it like it was Faith itself. Coming up with stories and excuses about your little plan, your little ‘I’m Shocka and I’m a monster, I’m dangerous, I’m the man that’s going to take your title’ bull shit. You know Shocka, I’ve seen a million nickel and dime curtain jerkers just like you come and go in my career. Every one of them saying that they were just playing with me. That they were going to end my career. That they would take the title off me. That they had a problem with wetting the bed on occasion just like you.” A laugh and a cheer mixed together from the fans. “Shocka, I buried them all. I stand here right now in this ring looking out into a sea of fans with this belt on my shoulder. It’s a symbol of one thing Shocka. It shows the world that I am the best that there is. I’m better then all the others that held it before, because if they were better then me they wouldn’t have ever lost it. And for some reason Shocka you think that doesn’t apply to you. You lost this title what.... 8, 9 months ago? Then what did you do after that? Not a whole lot Shocka, drifted and moaned trying to figure out what the hell your problem was. Well, your problem was and is that you just plain simply suck! Shocka, Faith is coming up in 3 weeks time. You’ve got the deck stacked in your favor. Saying bull shit about how you hope I’m at %100 so I won’t complain about losing. Well, I don’t complain about losing Shocka, because it doesn’t happen all that often and when it does it’s because someone has gotten the best of me. Shocka, you got the best of me last week. But I’m going to give you a chance, an opportunity to get the best of me again.” Kimson pauses. “So Shocka, if you are better then me. And if you aren’t afraid of me as you say you are then you’ll get your little theme music played, have your little pyro show or whatever you do and come down here and face me man to man right now. No titles, no rules just a fight.” Kimson paced around the ring as he waited for Shocka to make an entrance. After about 15 seconds he was going impatient. “C’mon Shocka? What’s the matter? Your not afraid of me right?” Then My Way by Limp Bizkit hit. The fans started to boo as they knew Leya Forth was about to make herself an entrance of some sort. The eVo-Tron flashed to live as Mrs. Forth sat on her desk showing lots of beautiful leg and smiled, boos and cheers mixed together, mostly boos. “Well, well, well, James. I’m glad that you made it here tonight. We were all worried about you stuck up in the Hospital last week after the unfortunate incidents that took place. You know James, I might be part of the committee and all, but I can’t be everywhere at once, so I’m deeply, truly sorry about what happened last week during your Title match with Jay Jay Jameson. But that being said James. I want you to know that tonight you will get your chance at some redemption. Because I’m putting you in a match.” Kimson looked unimpressed. Broken ribs weren’t something he relished a match with. However he couldn’t do a damn thing about it. “That’s right James, you will get your hands on Shocka tonight.” Kimson smiled. “It will be James Kimson in one corner, and in the other corner... the tag team combination of Shocka, and Johnny Charisma.” Kimson’s frown turned upside down. Leya was going to make sure that James Kimson was nice and soft for Faith. 3 weeks to go, 3 broken ribs and now a handicap match against two very dangerous men. “Okay James? Isn’t that what you want, just a chance to get your hands on Shocka? Well, don’t say I never do anything for you. Because I’m going to add a special stipulation to this match James. If you manage to win the match I’m going to allow you to add any stipulation you want to your match at Faith With Shocka. Any stipulation at all. IF you manage to win the match.” Kimson didn’t move, he didn’t care about stipulations. He was just looking forward to, in a masochistic way to getting his hands on Shocka tonight. “So good luck tonight James, you will most certainly need it!” My Way played again as the cameras faded out and the segment ended. Backstage ~ Return of the Crabman Featuring Shane Wethers It’s a busy night once again at Sapporo Dome as the masses of fans get ready for the night atmosphere to rise beyond levels of expectation, rise so high that the night will remain in the minds of wrestling historians for the next several decades. The long hallways are cluttered with P.A equipment and other large boxes that have previously contained items used for the night’s event. The walls are coated in a thin cover of cream-colored paint and the floor is simply concrete. All of a sudden Shane Wethers walks through a set of double doors at the far end of the corridor, wearing dark blue baggy jeans, a blue T-Shirt with the words ‘Wang Kurd’ written in a bold yellow Japanese-style font. Underneath he is wearing a long-sleeved white t-shirt and black leather gloves on his hands, cut-off at the fingers. The arena is filled with an intense auction of cheers at the return of the indispensable wrestling legend. The chunky silver chain hanging from his pocket clinks against his thigh as he walks down the hallway calmly, acknowledging the smiles, waves and greetings given to him by staff officials stood around like spare parts. He approaches another set of blue double doors and then turns right straight after them, walking over to a large desk where a smartly dressed brunette is sat. She is wearing a red dress suit and a white blouse, her hair is tied back neatly with minimum effort. Shane leans onto the desk with a cocky smile, followed by an arrogant wink. The lady looks up at him. “I’m baa-aack” Wethers announces with a Cheshire cat grin. “Mr. Wethers?” She replies, “It’s good to see you have returned, have you signed in yet?” “That’s why I’m babe” Wethers informs with another wink, “You don’t actually think I’m her to chat to you, do ya?” Shane begins to laugh and shake his head with disbelief as the woman tries her best to control her disgust and ignore him. “So… I see you had the crabs?” Shane’s face fills with pure shock as the lady looks up at him. “Wh-what???” Shane asks with a dry throat. “The reason you’ve been unwell in the last few weeks??” She replies. Shane brings his hand to his forehead and ponders in a surge of panic. “What are you talking about? I was out with CRAMPS!” Shane exclaims, “Knee CRAMPS!” The lady looks surprised and embarrassed at her mistake. “Oops, I’m sorry Mr. Wethers… I better call our headquarters in New York and hope they have sent the injury list to the press Already” She says apologetically. Shane stomps his feet in anger. “You mean to tell me that the world may already think I have the crabs” Shane continues to bellow. “Er…there’s only a small possibility!” The lady continues to reassure him. “Small!?!?” he roars “S-s-small!?!?!” “I Think you should go to the lobby and relax Mr. Wethers, Tom will escort you” The lady informs him. A man walks over to Shane with a friendly smile and puts an arm around Wethers. “It’s ok” he says with a sincere smile. “GET OFF ME YOU FAT FREAK! WHAT ARE YOU? AN OOMPA LOOMPA REJECT OR SOMETHING?” Shane detests. He continues to walk down the hall, booting a trash can on the way down and mutters under his breath, whilst Tom and the lady begin to laugh discreetly. Non-Title ~ Tag Team Match Outblaze and Patrick Powers vs. Kincade and Simon Krask Outblaze, Patrick Powers, and Simon Krask all stood in the ring already and Krask was waiting for the arrival of his partner so that they could get this match started. The music that began to play however was not the music of Kincade. It was “Synthetic” by Spineshank and that meant it could only be one person; Shocka. Shocka wasted no time as he quickly emerged from the backstage area with a steel chair in hand and made his way quickly down the ramp ignoring the boos from the crowd. Shocka slid right into the ring as all three men stood in the ring confused as to the presence of Shocka before Powers was the first one to rush him. Shocka ducked quickly under a clothesline attempt from Powers and turned around swinging before he slammed the steel chair right across the back of Powers’ head and dropped him to the mat. Shocka raised the chair above his head and delivered a second shot right down across the back of Powers before he rolled out under the bottom rope. Outblaze had plans not to be Shocka’s next victim but Krask wanted to make sure he wasn’t next and after quietly slipping behind Outblaze he shoved him right at Shocka. Luckily for Outblaze, Shocka was a bit slow to turn around as Outblaze rushed him from behind and drilled him with a hard clothesline to the back of the head that knocked Shocka face first down to the mat. Outblaze drove a quick kick down into the back of Shocka before he reached down and picked Shocka back up to his feet. Outblaze grabbed Shocka by the arm and went for an irish whip but it was reversed by Shocka who pulls Outblaze right into his arms and with a quick lift off the mat drove him right into the mat with a variation of the #1 Stunna. Shocka was getting back onto his feet and Krask knew it was his turn next and quickly rushed Shocka before he proceeded to pound him with a series of forearms across the back. The blows had little if any effect on Shocka however as he continued to rise and again had the chair in his hand before he turned around and drove a kick into the midsection of Krask to stop him in mid movement before Shocka wound back with the chair and slammed it right across the top of Krask’s head. Krask was about to fall to the mat but Shocka grabbed him and held him up with one arm before he pressed the top of the chair up against Krask’s throat and then shoved him down towards the mat until the legs of the chair hit the mat and the top part slammed up against the throat of Krask who collapsed in pain to the mat coughing and hacking as Shocka with a sick smile on his face slowly backpedaled towards the ropes and climbed back out of the ring. Without saying a single word Shocka had made himself quite an impact tonight and perhaps sent a bit of a message out to James Kimson. Backstage ~ Final Warning Featuring Asis and Leya Forth He’d had enough. He’d been waiting too long with no sign of anything being done about his simple request. In short, he was now pissed off. Leya Forth was going to get a little visit. The walk to her office didn’t take long, he saw the usual backstage crew on the way there, all of them gladly moving out his way as he mowed down the hallways. He got to the office, barging through the door and slamming it behind him. He was greeted by the sight of two shocked looking faces, one being Leya Forth, sitting behind her desk, the other a middle aged man, dressed in a rather business looking suit. His eyes met the red ones of the silver haired monster in front of him and he realized he was in entirely the wrong place at the wrong time. Employee? Asis casually asks the suit, who slowly shakes his head. Asis swiftly moves over to him, grabs him by the collar of his suit and hoists him out of his seat with ease, before proceeding to throw him through the air and into the door, which comes off its hinges on impact, leaving the suited man sprawled on the floor, most likely nursing a very nasty headache. Asis then very calmly sits down in the empty chair and looks at Leya who is outraged. How dare you come into my office and disrupt it like this!Leya screams at Asis, absolutely raging. I wanted us to be alone. Asis replies in a very calm manner. I’m sorry, I couldn’t think of an easier way to achieve it. Next time, if you can, you should suggest something. I tend to act on impulse. Don’t try and be smart with me you son of a bitch! I should fire you for this! He was a very important prospective client! Leya screams, standing up and leaning over the desk at Asis who remains sitting very calmly. He wasn’t an employee Leya. Your “rules” don’t apply to him. Asis replies, somewhat of a sarcastic tone in his voice, the tiniest evidence of a smile on his lips. Who the hell do you think you are! Listen here you- No! You fucking listen to me bitch! Asis roars, flying up out of his seat and slamming his fist down hard on the table, scaring Leya into sitting back down in her chair quickly. I’m the man who is going to tear this fucking company apart if you don’t pay attention to me! I ask for one thing, one tiny little thing and I was crazy enough to buy into your games thinking you might actually deliver. There’s no get out clause for me in this agreement- That’s right there isn’t Asis. What are you going to do if I don’t bring Xander back? Leya defiantly and somewhat triumphantly says to Asis. I don’t care if you fire me or not, I swear if you interrupt me again you will never walk again in your life. Leya finally sits quiet and still, looking quite afraid at the seriousness with which Asis is talking to her. But then he never was one to joke. Now, as I was saying, there’s no get out clause in our agreement for me. But if you don’t get Xander back here, that’s not going to stop me. You can only fire me once and if you do, it doesn’t mean I’m going to leave you and this company alone. Once I’m through, there won’t be a company left. And not being an employee means you won’t have any power over me. You’ll just be another person in this world to me Leya. So you’d better prey Xander is here by Faith, because I promise your life and the life of many others around here will change forever if not. There’s not a force in this world that can stop me Leya. Now you think about it and have a nice day. Asis finishes with a snarl as he walks out of the room, leaving Leya in a state of shock, with a lot to think about. Backstage ~ Softening Up Featuring Matt Hunt and The Select The feed cuts to Matt Hunt walking through the parking lot, with his kitbag hanging off his right shoulder. As the fresh cold air swirls around him, he wears a black woolly hat and thick black jacket to keep warm and the cold air is displayed as a slight catch of breath exits his mouth as he exhales. The fans react positively to Hunt, who watch this footage on the large video screen inside the arena. As Hunt approaches the rear entrance, reaching his arm out for the large handle, he pauses as he notices a collection of footsteps from behind him. He turns his head slightly as the footsteps get louder with every second. Eventually, the sound of the hard sole against the concrete suddenly stops and Hunt slowly turns around to find QBX standing in his face. Hunt lets his kitbag drop off his shoulder and thump to the floor. He and QBX share a stare down as there’s a momentary pause “Mr. Postman…” starts QBX, as if it was a greeting of some sort and with a smirk on his face. Hunt takes his time to react verbally as he glances to his left, to his right and looking over QBX’s shoulder. “Where’s your gang, homie?” Hunt says with a grin and forcing QBX to respond with a scowl. “Don’t me playing me foo’, I’ll knock ya down on yo ass, bitch!” he snaps as Hunt simply stares with a blank expression. “Well…what can I do for you, camache” Hunt asks after a few more minutes’ silence. “You can stop getting uppity wit’ me before I…” “…bust a cap in yo ass?” Hunt interrupts with another grin. “I’m warning you, punk…quite playin’ me…” QBX barks “I’m here for this plan of yours, you know…this mission” he continues. “Oh I see, that…you’re on about Operation Slap” Hunt responds with a calm manner. “Yeah. Now all I been hearing is that QBX is next…” he asks Hunt as he peers in. “Well, that is the schedule” responds Hunt “But all in good time, camache…all in good time.” Hunt picks up his bag and motions to turn away, but QBX tugs at his arm. “No, you don’t get it…you want me, well here I am. You ain’t gonna attack me like a little candy ass like you did with Rimian and Tok’n” he says. Hunt shrugs off QBX and pulls down on the handle, opening the door but before he can even turn to look into the arena, QBX gets his attention again as two figures are seen appearing from nowhere in the doorway. “What is it you say? Back door merchant” he beams as he laughs. “Excuse me?” Hunt responds. “Yo’ heard, bitch!” snaps QBX and before Hunt has time to prepare, the two figures – now identified as Tok’n and Rimian – burst out the doorway and jump on Hunt, taking him down to the cold, hard concrete. As QBX joins in the attack, Aaron Justice appears from the doorway and mockingly shines his eVolved title as his three stable mates put the boots to The Postman. “Postman…” Justice “You just haven’t gotta clue, have you?…” he says as QBX picks up Hunt by the hair and hurls him into the concrete wall. As he slides down the wall, Justice gets into his face and barks “WE ARE THE SELECT AND NOBODY SCREWS WITH US!!” which echoes throughout the empty parking lot. Rimian and Tok’n continue with the stomps as Hunt’s forehead is busted open, immediate impact from the shot to the wall. Justice takes a few steps back as do Rimian and Tok’n, allowing QBX to get some shots to the chest before pulling Hunt up by the hair. “Listen up, punk…maybe you’ll think again about having The Select on your radar screen” barks QBX as he locks his right up underneath Hunt’s left arm. QBX then roars and he charges into the parking lot, pulling Hunt with him, before hurling The Postman up and onto the bonnet on a nearby car with a harrowing hip-toss. The headlights flash and a siren is set off upon impact as is a sickening thud. CLUNK~! The Select member’s high-five each other and stare back at Hunt with a menacing scowl as they re-enter the building. The camera shot pans back to Hunt, who remains on sprawled across the bonnet of the car…lights still flashing as well as the seemingly never ending ear piercing siren. The bastard!” Xander stand over Asis absolutely fuming and starts wailing on Asis with the chair, repeatedly hammering him while he’s on the floor with the chair, bending it all out of shape and proportion. When the chair doesn’t seem to be of any use any more he throws it on the canvas, and then bends down, dragging Asis to his feet. In a surprising display of strength, he hauls Asis onto his shoulders and delivers a Chaos Reigns onto the chair, driving Asis’ head and neck down onto the chair. “Xander is back and he’s back with authority! We’ve not seen anyone man handle Asis like that before!” Xander stand over Asis screaming at him, the words almost inaudible, but what you can hear is the word “family”. He turns round to see to his amazement, a bloody Asis propping himself up against the ropes, a microphone in his hands, breathing heavily. “Xander. It’s good to have you back. And I’m glad you’re in the mood for a fight, can’t imagine what has provoked you so much though. Nether the less, I’ll see you at Faith. Take care.” Asis drops the microphone and stands smiling at Xander, blood running down his face and into his mouth, whilst Xander stares right back in the eyes of Asis, the hatred and malice almost felt by the audience through the eyes of the two men.
