House Show III, 15 June 2003

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
House Show Results Standard Singles Match Shawn Shafton Vs. Memphis Cage While the fans were still finding their seats, eVo ran a test run on two of their newest signees. It seemed neither man got the crowd behind them noticeably until the end of the quick match when Shafton beat Cage with a jaw-rocking superkick taking cage out easily. Shafton didn't pin him though until going to the top rope and hitting a 450 leg drop on Memphis followed by the 3-count. Winner via pinfall: Shawn Shafton Standard Singles Match British Bomber Vs. Chad Winters In a rematch from last week, Chad Winters showed a lot better of a display then he did last time. Winters stunned the crowd with his high flying ability, with various leaps over the top rope and to the outside. Winters hit a few springboards to dazzle the crowd and British Bomber seemed to be at the hands of a defeat. It wasn’t long though until Bomber was able to turn the tables and capitalize on a missed Phoenix Splash by Winters. Bomber soon locked in his anklelock, but Winters was able to get to the ropes twice! The crowd was enjoying themselves in this one until Bomber locked in the anklelock for a third time, forcing Winters to tap out. Great match and a great showing by Winters. Winner via submission: British Bomber. Non-Title Cell Match Damon O’Halloran vs. Machine Machine with his first house show appearance showed great strength and power in this non-title cell match. Machine tried using his power the whole match, but sooner or later it caught up with him, showing that the big man was getting tired in the cell. Just as Machine was feeling sluggish, Damon capitalized, hitting furies of rights, lefts and stiff kicks to the big man. Machine, with a last ditch effort, went for his rabbit punch, only to have Damon dodge it and connect with two kicks. The first kick to his gut, doubling him over, then a straight shot to his head, knocking the big man out. The ref counted to ten and the champion wins. Winner via knockout: Damon O’Halloran Double Title Match: Winner takes all! Scott Evans © Vs. Yoshii Tanaka © In a match where both the evolved and ULWN titles were on the line, Scott Evan and Yoshii Tanaka truly pleased the crowd. As soon as the match was announced to be double title, everyone went wild; and they weren’t disappointed. Yoshii Tanaka proved to be a bird without wings, flying through the air at any moment. Flying clotheslines, suicide dives, spinning heel kicks and others gave Yoshii the early advantage. Both one missed moonsault was all it took to put the match back in Scott Evans side. Scott Evans brought a different plate to the table with a series of suplexes and slams, grounding Yoshii to the mat. Yoshii Tanaka hurt his knee when he was thrown over the top rope and to the outside. But Evans didn’t seem to stop the attack. Yoshii limped for the rest of the night, and at one point spit out green mist, only to have Evans duck and the referee to get hit. The match continued without the ref for a while, both men still fighting without him. Evans grabbed Yoshii and hit 3 straight german suplexes, and pinned with the third, but the ref was slow to get up. The bridge was sloppy though, Evans shoulders were down as well. A 2nd ref slid into the ring as the 1st one came to. Both referees counted the pin, and the crowd was stunned on who won. One referee pinned Evans and one pinned Yoshii. To the crowd’s disappointment, the match was declared a draw and no title changed hands. Winner: Draw The show broke for a 15 minute intermission at this time, Libby Bowman was in the ring, plugging the vendors and merchandise. She was wearing an End of Days shirt that she said was on a very affordable price now. Standard Singles Match Eric Knight vs. Shane Wethers Shane brought the crowd to their feet in this contest. Eric showed a lot of talent in this one, putting Wethers on his back multiple times. But this would turn to be a back and forth match, Wethers returning the blows and slams that he received. You can see Shane Wethers adjusting to eVo’s wrestling style very well, along with getting the crowd excited. In the end, Shane won with The Avalanche and a 3-count. Winner: Shane Wethers. Standard Tag Match Sean Valmonté` and Carl Ca$h vs. Scott and Seara Frost. A great tag match here, main event caliber. Scott Frost brought excitement along with Cash’s high flying ability. Frost was in the ring the most for his team, being double teamed by The World’s Finest. Cash and Valmonté` showed incredible teamwork throughout the match. Finally, Scott Frost got the tag and Seara cleared the ring in a cliché fashion. In the end, we saw Seara with a low blow, taking Valmonté` out, and Frost pinning Cash after the Frost Effect. Winner via pinfall: Scott Frost and Seara Main Event: 6-man tag The Industry and Thug roC Vs. The Syndicate and Ryan Mason A lot of titles in this match that showed the champions taking on their number one contenders. Thug roC and Ryan Mason got some boring chants during their few minutes in the ring, seems like it was too early for Ryan to get a main event match without his mask. The pace of the match picked up quickly though for the two and Ryan showed a lot more speed the aging Thug roC. Relms and BDD also gave the crowd what they enjoyed, taking their battle from ringpost to ringpost all the way. Perry Quinn didn’t do as well though, but with HBC in there, any fan would watch. Perry sent HBC to the outside, and that caused an immediate breakout of all 6 men and women. In the ring, Big Daddy Dreamer nailed Relms with his Dreamanator, only to be hit from behind by Thug roC. Thuggy then lifted BDD and hit his roCing Horse before going to the top rope. Ryan Mason slid back into the ring and with great speed, ran up the turnbuckle and grabbed a shocked Thug roC. He then hit a downward spiral off the top rope. Ryan pinned Thug roC for the 3-count. Winner via pinfall: The Syndicate and Ryan Mason. Biggest Pop: 1. Shane Wethers 2. Yoshii Tanaka and Scott Evans match 3. Scott Frost and Seara Biggest heat: 1. Double Title Match’s Ending 2. Thug roC 3. Announcement of Intermission (c) eWentertainment 2003
