Quasar the Great Real Name: Quentin Rodgers Also Known As: Quasar the Great, King of Strainobi Date of Birth: June 22nd, 1974 Hometown: Las Vegas, Nevada Height: 5'11 Weight: 201lbs Alignment: Neutral Manager: None Wrestling Style: All Rounder Favorite Moves: Monkey Flip, Spinning NeckBreaker, Flying Body Press, Camel Clutch, Top Rope Bulldog, Full Nelson Slam, German Suplex, Frog Splash from top rope, Ankle Lock, Back Body Drops. Signature Move: Set-Up: Trained and Set-DDT to the mat in the center of the ring. Finisher: Feel The Greatness-Rolling Thunder. Other Info Current Teams: None Past Teams: None eVo Title History: None eVo Award History: None Other Titles: EdW Suicidal Champion(x1), EdW Karnage Champion(x1) Other Awards: None Biography Wrestler Entrance: The lights dim low as "Epiphany" by Staind starts to play lightly on the speakers. Then red spotlights switch on and align down the ramp creating a red carpet type effect. Suddenly the music switches from very low to loud! Out comes Quasar who stops on the top of the ramp wearing his robe and crown. He walks down the ramp slowly, yelling things at the crowd. Finally he makes it to the ring. Before he gets in he takes off his robe and crown and gets in the ring Appearance: Quasar has long silky brown hair with dirty blond streaks that is normally slicked back with gel. He has a thin mustache growing in that is barely noticeable. He is kind of scrawny looking but still has some muscles. He has barbed wire tattooed around his waist. To the ring he usually wears gold colored pants, a gold net type shirt that he takes off as he enters the ring, and a crown. Biography: He grew up in a normal subarban family. He went to public schools and managed mediocre grades. But being that he didn't like school he refused to go to college and instead went into the job market early. Job after job he jumped to, but none kept him entertained and he grew bored easy so he always quit within the first week. One night he sat in his apartment and thought about future goals. And two of them made the top of his list, become a wrestler and to become a ruler, but more specifically a king! From age twenty to twenty to twenty-five he trained at a few wrestling schools where he became the top five percent of his class. One day his teacher, Runk Taens, suggested that he go for a big wig federation. It was then that Quentin knew that he was going to try to get into the only federation he cared about, EDW. Shortly after joining EdW he captured both the Suicidal and Karnage Titles before jumping ship to Daniel Somers rival nEw. Following the closing of both feds Quasar disappeared from the public eye before resurfacing in eVolution.
