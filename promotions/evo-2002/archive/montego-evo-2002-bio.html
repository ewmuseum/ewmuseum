Montego Biography Real Name Joseph [name withheld] Nicknames The Mont/Monty Hometown San Antonio Texas Birthday 30/4/1978 Height 6ft2incs Weight 259 Interests Football (Soccer) Wrestling Favorite Pro Wrestlers HBK Shawn Micheals Biography Montego started his Career in the ICW (International Championship Wrestling) Where he started his fame by beeting James Lord for the Intercontinental Championship in what was Probably Montego's best ever Moment then. Montego with his Sarcastic Attitude, his Uncaring Actions made him one of the most Dangerous men in the E-fed World. After holding the IC Belt in ICW for nearly 4 Months, He eventually lost it to non other then James Lord himself, it didnt take Montego long to get some kind of Gold back, Two Weeks later Montego entered himself into a 10 man battle Royal for the Hardcore Belt, He cheeted his way to Victory after the Ref was blinded when his two feet obviously touched the Floor, The Next night on Breakthrough, a Television show, Montego Challenged Hardcore Mack to his Hardcore title, He Breezed through that meaning that Montego held two titles from two nights of Action. Montego eventually lost both belts, but just before the ICW Was Shut down he beet James Lord for the IC belt. It didnt take much longer for Montego to find more fame as he joined the ICWF and within his first two weeks won the Television Championship, and held that for nearly a year winning the Intercontinental Championship in that time too. Montego's Most Succesful Federation was the UKWF, in which Legends such as James Kimson, Matt Hunt and LoZ were a part off. Montego Still Barks on at the fact he beet James Kimson in a Hell in a cell Match, He knows that the Moment he pinned Kimson 1-2-3 was the greatest moment of his great Career which is only at the beginning.
