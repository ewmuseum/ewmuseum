Kimson staggers around holding his ribs as Shocka pulls himself out of the corner grasping his shoulder turning around. Kimson unloads with a big right hand shaking Shocka’s foundation and falls back into the corner. Kimson still holds his ribs but he unloads with a chop that echoes around the entire arena.
