Shane Wethers Biography Real Name Zak Nicknames 'The Eternal Punk' Hometown Leeds, ENG Birthday 01/09/75 Height 6'3 Weight 277 Interests Music, Skating, drinking, soccer/football, women~ Favorite Pro Wrestlers RVD, Rey Mysterio JR, Steve Austin, Mick Foley Biography Shane grew up on the streets of leeds at the centre of a family engaged in organised crime.. most of the relations being american/italian and alot of them being british. His real passion was always punk-rock music and he'd spend hours in his room just listening to the sounds of Rancid and the Sex Pistols. Shane avoided crime at all costs after witnessing the faked death of his brother, Silus when Shane was only 11. 15 years later Silus returned and tried to kill his brother out of spite from their childhood. Silus failed and has been locked up in the New Jersey county jail for the last three years. Shane grew up with a lot of hate inside of him, spending his days learning kick boxing and karate, his educated fighting style gained him many attributes along with many enemies. He continued with his music, playing the guitar and singing in the that recently became infamous, called 'Sanity'. He was discharged from the airforce when he was 22 due to rebellious acts, he then took up professional Wrestling and his music on a full time basis, making major league with both of them. After wrestling across the nation in many large industries Shane is one of the most loved competitors in the business but has a well-known viscous streak, which many of his past enemies never forget.
