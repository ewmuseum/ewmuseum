Faith Transcript Live on Pay Per View! From Tokyo Dome ~ Tokyo, Japan 7:59PM EST Sunday Night November 9th, 2003 In The Ring ~ You Got To Have Faith Featuring Damon O’Halloran ”Jeremy” by Pearl Jam. Damon O'Halloran stepped through the curtain. Wearing a black track suit, O'Halloran slowly made his way to ringside. As he passed by an eVo cameraman, his reddened eyes were quite evident. The fans, however, seemed almost sympathetic to Damon... almost. There was no love for the Act of God, even in this situation. O'Halloran didn’t care what the fans said or thought; his expression hadn’t changed since he walked through the curtains: one of both dejection and anger. A ringside attendant offered O'Halloran a microphone, which he quickly snatched away like a bear snatching a trout from a stream. “Goddamn it! And I thought it was just the United States that had all the inbred, redneck hicks. Look at you idiots with your cowboy hats on. I’ll let you in on a secret: just because you twits have cable TV with all your American channels, watching Dallas 24/7, it doesn’t make you cowboys. It makes you wanna-be cowboys, just like all of you are O'Halloran wannabes.” Damon ignored the “Damon Sucks” chant. “I think you people better get it straight. This whole place sucks. Hell, even Jackie Chan knew that. That’s why he left.” The chanting dissolved into a loud thunder of booing. The corner of O'Halloran’s mouth lifted into a hint of a smile. O'Halloran then turned to address the camera positioned at ringside. “But that's not what I’m out here to talk to you fine people here tonight. This past week has seen the wrestling business in mourning, we have lost another one of our sons. Cameron Griffin.” “Now I know that I’m supposed to be fighting tonight.” O'Halloran paused and looked down at the ring. “It’s not like I can’t beat them all on my worst day, but I seem to be going through a bit of a crisis, with my... with my... conscience?!” The crowd began booing again, which only seemed to infuriate the Act of God even more. He lunged forward, grabbing the top rope with one hand. “It’s not like you damn hicks even deserve to see me fight! I don’t fight in front of losers! Truth is I'm sad. To be quite honest, I'm devastated.” The fans seemed quite bemused. Again, O'Halloran ignored them and continued. “... I'm devastated that a high speed vehicle got to Griffin first! You know, he didn't let me down though. I knew he would find some way of getting out of facing me again. Knowing that there was a fate worse than death awaiting him. I knew he didn’t have the balls to face me again.” The chorus of boos grew louder. "Make no mistake about it, folks. Cameron Griffin was in no accident. He committed suicide!" O'Halloran halted right there as the horrified crowd tore the roof off the arena with their disapproval. O'Halloran, being the brazen son of a bitch that he is, ignored them and continued. "And he took the one thing that had significance to me - the Cell Title. He ripped that away from me; and by god, a match tonight wont rectify that! I was supposed to finish him off. I was supposed to look into his eyes during the last few seconds of his pathetic life. He knew what he was doing - he has tried to kill the Cell Division; and everybody in it. He was trying to kill... his own creation. This is not the end... but the beginning of his purgatory... the Cell will rise again! And I would rather piss on his grave and give one to his old lady than to ever honor that fruitless belt of his. This is just the beginning." Scene fades to black and Faith gets underway with the opening credits. His silence was sweeter than his words had ever sounded. While looking down at him in his melancholy state I couldn't help but grin at his woes, laugh at his family's tears and hoped that their rivers would drown his soul. When Cameron Griffin's soul was taken the heaven's shone brighter than ever before whilst the angels sang songs of the devil. I not only wished for Cameron Griffin to die, but also for his soul to be left desolate, excluded, the hobo of Purgatory, waiting half-erect for Jesus to wave his guiding hand toward heaven. While he waits and waits a little bit gets chipped away from his soul until he's nothing. No soul. No home. No fancy funeral. I often lay in bed unable to sleep, thinking of Cameron Griffin suffering, hoping that he would rot like the decaying matter he is. The decaying, insignificant centre of this world. Two days ago, he turned to cow-dung. His body trampled over while I whispered not only senseless nothings but words of disdain, telling Jesus to have no mercy on his soul but to treat him no better than he's treated us. To pick and pull away from him like a puppeteer perfecting the puppets every move to his desire. "Rot Cameron Griffin, rot," I told him. After I left the funeral home the aroma of cigarette smoke smelt as if it had been dropped down from heaven by an angel, dropped purposely into my hands. And so I pray that Cameron Griffin's soul be trampled upon like cow-dung. That what remains on the shoe to be stamped onto the curb. I was strung along by Cameron and now I pull the strings as God carries out my very orders and gives an obligatory wink and tells me that my wish is his command. The chair connected smack on the head of her henchman Shocka! The boos turned tin cheers in the Blink of an eye as Kimson saw him fall and turned, looking at Leya with a bit of a smile on his face. Leya went to swing again at Kimson but he caught the chair and threw it out of the ring, almost stalking Leya now, telling her he she just made a mistake touching him. The fans are going nuts, not caring anymore about the title but the chance that maybe Leya would get her just reward for all she’s done to Kimson, not just in the match but throughout everything. But then, to much of the surprise of everyone, Thug roC slid in the ring behind everyone and grabbed the Champion up in the roCing Horse, planting the Universal Champion with skill and surprise! Leya smiled an evil Grin looking at roC who stood there smiling and nodded to her before walking over to him going to hold him up as Shocka was getting to his feet as well, Shocka grabbed the chair used before and goes to line up Kimson and drills it over his head bending the seat in over the head of the Champion. Shocka and roC and Leya all look at each other as Kimson lays at their feet, blood starting to trickle down his face. Shocka says that he wants to end it right now but Leya says she wants to make absolutely sure that he’s finished and orders roC out of the ring to grab something. Thug roC exits the ring and flips up the apron and slides in a table. Shocka goes over to it and starts to set it up as roC walks over to Kimson after sliding in under the bottom rope and starts to lay the boots to him, then mounting him and goes to work on the cut busting him open good. Shocka has the table all ready as he sets it up on an angle with the turnbuckles probably for the real deal # 1 Stunna but Leya points to roC to finish the job. Shocka has no objection, they drag Kimson to the table and set him up on it and Thug roC headed upstairs, despite his Ladder Match before he wasn’t afraid. Kimson was half way across the ring when roC dove off for a Diving Elbow. Moving out of the way wasn’t ever more dramatic as Kimson rolled off the Table. Thug roC crashed through it as the table basically exploded under the impact. Everyone again couldn’t believe it. But believing is seeing as Shocka couldn’t afford to wait much longer, he grabbed the chair, bent slightly and all and went after Kimson who got up, but he ducked, the chair landed square onto of the pretty little head of Leya Forth, the Ref and his meal ticket. Shocka dropped the chair in disbelief how he could have possibly done it. Kimson turned to face him and booted him in the ribs and hooked him up in History in The Making, planting Shocka onto the canvas with a huge move! Kimson looked around, a table trashed in the ring, Thug roC laying in the rubble, the Ref, Committee Member and his Sister laid out from an errand chair shot and Shocka laying on the canvas after taking the most effective move in wrestling. It looked good to Kimson, problem being no one to make the cover. He looked around, the fans going nuts, blood running down his face as he points to Shocka and scoops him up for a second run at History. He turned around with Shocka in the double hook but it was showboating. Thug roC drop kicked Kimson from behind, sending everyone down in a heap. Thug was hurt bad, after all a Ladder match featuring a DVD and Cross Bodies off ladders hurts but he knew that if he got Leya what she wanted it was only going to help him. He grabbed Kimson for a second roCing horse but Kimson slipped down off the back of roC and booted him, History in the Making for Thug roC was in order, Thug was planted. Kimson got up and he was pumped, not as much as the crowd as he walked over to Shocka, grabbed him, hooked him up and planted him with History in the Making. Kimson covered Shocka, maybe hoping for a miracle, but they do happen. After what seemed like an eternity someone came running down, Robert Brooks charging to the ring down the long ramp way as he slid into the ring. The new Nemesis of Leya and perhaps Ally to Kimson slammed his hand on the mat. 1…………………………. 2…………………………. 3! Brooks signaled to the bell. The fans blew the roof off cheering. No one could believe that Kimson overcame the odds, that no matter how bad it looked he refused to give up. He refused to loose. He refused to let Leya and Shocka have the satisfaction of victory. Brooks helped a tired, beaten and bloody Kimson up to his feet as the Time Keeper slid the Belt over to them as Brooks handed it to the Champion as Libby Bowman announced James Kimson the still reigning Universal Champion of eVolution.
