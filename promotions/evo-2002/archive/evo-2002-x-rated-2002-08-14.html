X-Rated Transcript Live on Pay Per View! From Madison Square Garden ~ New York City, New York 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night August 14th, 2002 American Nightmare was the first step to glory for eVolution. A blockbuster event had kicked off the start of the new up and coming promotion and now one month later they return to pay per view. X-Rated will be the second pay per view installment for eVolution and promises to crown a new Universal Champion with an eight man tournament for the title. Feuds have sparked up throughout the month on Legends and many of them will be brought to a very violent end in the Mecca of professional wrestling, Madison Square Garden! Three men who have all earned a shot at gold in eVolution's second pay per view endeavor. Asis has been on a warpath as of late with the constant presence of Somers and Davidson in his way. The owner Tyler Davidson and his right hand man Daniel Somers have been putting obstacle after obstacle in his path since day one. Next up are Brad Kelley and Shodan, two men that have some serious unfinished business from back in the days of EdW. Now the two must settle up and fight through the always dangerous Asis if they hope to survive. Four men inside of one jail house with the Cell championship on the line. The most violent matches in EdW have come as a result of the Cell and now everything these men have fought for comes back once again with the stakes raised higher than ever before. A late addition to the card a mystery staff member for eVolution announced the signing of a four corner High Impact title match. The man refused to give his name but said that The HeartBreak Chick, winner of Legends IV battle royal will be in action during it along with Sean Mason, Kin Ryo, and Quasar the Great. Both of these men have been tearing through the competition in eVolution with only one thing on their minds, destroying each other. The violence had begun on the day of their signings and has continued ever since week in and week out with them trying to one up one another. Now with a shot at the Continental Champion on the line the feud looks set to end in a hardcore match. The Continental Title on the line as Xander and Erin Christoph finally do battle in a triple cage match. The wild card factor to be played into the mix of course is the presence of Malcolm King. King showed up at Legends IV with a proposition for Tyler Davidson concerning Xander and Erin Christoph. The only thing certain at this point is the violence is certain to get out of hand. Eight men will fight in the course of one night for the chance to be named the best in eVolution with the Universal Title hanging in the balance. Match one of seven in the eVolution Universal Title tournament. Sovereign has destroyed everyone in his path so far since his shocking arrival in eVolution nearly taking the life of James Horton in a Cell match two weeks ago. Now he must face the rookie sensation Matt Boone who looks to prove himself as one of the young and rising stars in eVolution. Tommy Michaels and L2J are both probably the most underrated stars in the tournament. Michaels has been troubled as of late with some personal demons so it will be interesting to see if he can overcome both them and L2J. L2J on the other hand is on a mission to silence all the critics and finally win the big one. Jamal Kage and Impact battle in the third tournament match between two of the possible wild cards in the tournament. Kage has seemingly been everywhere as of late in eVolution while Impact has apparently sided with Davidson and Somers in their war against the EdW loyalists. Just what did Davidson and Somers purchase his services with and what could it have in store for Kage? Two former EdW Universal Champions lock up in battle in this final first round match. Troy Pierce a two time champion will battle Big Daddy Dreamer in a match which has been building since day one at American Nightmare. The two men considered by many to be favorites for the title must battle until only one of the two is left. Whichever combination of the four men make it out of the first round will then continue on to the second round and then the finals. In the final match of the tournament, the Universal Title will be hung above the ring in a ladder match with the two finalists going head on for the right to claim themselves the best. Who will be champion? Presidential Skybox ~ Walkout Featuring Tyler Davidson and “The Awesome Superstar” Kris Nightmare The show opens with a shot of Tyler Davidson’s skybox office. Sitting inside is Tyler behind on a plush couch and in front of him is “The Awesome Superstar” Kris Nightmare. The two men are in silence as Tyler looks over some papers with an agitated Kris Nightmare looking on. “I don’t see what it is you’re so upset about Kris,” Tyler began responding to an unheard comment from Nightmare obviously. ”I’m upset about the fact that you seem to think I’m willing to work with an asshole who fired me a few months ago,” Nightmare fired back looking furious. “Well you do have a contract here Kris,” Tyler replied losing his patience. “It’s a contract which says you will do what you’re told or you’re fired.” ”Is that so?” Nightmare asked. “Mind if I see this contract?” “Of course not,” Tyler answered as he hands him the piece of paper in his hands. “So it does say that,” Nightmare said as he looked the paper over. “I wonder what this means then?” Nightmare takes the paper in both hands and proceeds to rip it into pieces before throwing them back at Tyler who simply sits with a smile on his face as Nightmare turns and walks out of the room. ”That’s one down,” Tyler muttered as the scene faded to black.
