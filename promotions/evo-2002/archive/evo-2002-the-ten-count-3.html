Malcolm King Xander Erin Christoph Troy Pierce Big Daddy Dreamer What common bond do these men share? They have all in some form or another been touched by the hand of The Unlimited. Tonight we take a look at The Unlimited on..... THE TEN COUNT! "Hello I'm Monica Angel, and welcome to Ten Count. An Evolution production that looks into Evolution and her wrestlers.Tonight we take a look at The Unlimited." Video footage starts rolling..... Just who is The One? The seeds for the current incarnation of The Unlimited was planted long ago during The first EDW Ground Zero (Turmoil). Malcolm King or as he was known by then Xavier Citizen. Was brutally assaulted by a rival wrestling organization known as The AWF; whose membership included Big Daddy Dreamer and [name withheld] among other. Xander led the EDW lockerroom in clearing out the AWF, but a little too late for the likes of Citizen. Fast Forward..... Attacks on EDW wrestlers and crpytic messages left by someone refering to themselves as 'The One'. His goal it seemed was to exact revenge on EDW or specifically Brandon Kelley and [name withheld] for an unspecified crime. The Unlimited were considerd a wrestling cult; who believed in domination through assimilation. There goal was to destroy EDW by asorbing it into their own organization like a parasite or a disease. The cure apparently was forever midcarder Xander. The Unlimited wanted to destroy everything EDW which included their main event.....and felt the forced elevation of roster underacheivers would be good for their cause. But why Xander? The first major move by the Unlimited was the abduction of [name withheld] Then they helped Big Daddy Dreamer twice in one night against Xavier Citizen. First by keeping certain EDW wrestlers occupied while Citizen fought for the rights of his name...and secondly by causing the former Citizen to lose the Universal title to Big Daddy Dreamer later on that night. The Unlimited then abducted the former Citizen and carried him to parts unknown. Having abducted EDW's cheif of wrestling operations and World Champion...where else could they strike? The following week provided an answer as Brandon Kelley was the third abductee of the Unlimited. The three most powerful figures in any wrestling organization were now gone from EDW and the question on everyone's lips....just who was THE ONE? Dave Jacobs of FedWars was one of the first to bring out that it had to have been an inside job. He accused Brandon Kelley of intentionally trying to sabatoge his own company so he can regain complete control of EDW himself? Names were being thrown around.....[name withheld], Josh Sutton, Vince McMahon, hell someone had even suggested that Dave Jacobs of FedWars was The One as an attempt to micromanage the federations under his banner. Just who was The One? Ironically a newly elevated Xander would reveal the answer. Xander who a week before was carrying a picket sign screaming Xander=Ratings! A man who could not get TV time while [name withheld] was in control now found himself headlining Turmoil as well as PPV's. When the question of the one came time to revealed it was Xander who ended months of speculation and revealed Xavier Citizen as The ONE! But why? why would he intentionally lose his title and kill his credibility...but more importantly why would he risk the wrath of Dave Jacobs by proving him wrong? Under his first wrestling name Malcolm King he captured The EDW Universal title for an unprecedented third time. His number one contender.....Xander. King the supposed head of The Unlimited had set into motion events that would lead to the death of Tyler Davidson's fiancee (Davidson being a longtime adversary of King dating back to MCW) and [name withheld] breaking off from EDW to form a rival promotion. Under King The Unlimited had accomplished their goal and EDW closed, alas before Xander could win the Universal belt...but not before establishing him as a viable Champion which was unheard of a few months prior. FastForward....... The opening of Evolution, King returns with shocking revelation. He is NOT The One! He contends that a look a like actor was brought in to portray him for the unmasking and that he was detained until well after EDW's closing when he signed up for Dynasty Wrestling. King furthur states that Xander not him was The One and that The Unlimited was used by Xander as a way to elevate him...by using an artificial crisis to push the traditionally underpushed Xander. If what King says is true then that brings into question his histroy making third EDW Universal title reign as well as questions on who really is THE ONE. MA:"and that is why we are here today to answer that question Who is The One? sure you know about Malcolm King and Xander. You also know about Erin Christoph. But what about the lost suspects? What about Troy Pierce a man who is in some respect an ally of King's who was injured/retired around the initial run of The Unlimited and then there is Big Daddy Dreamer who inadvertantly benefitted from The Unlimited wit a Universal title reign as well as one of the principles at it's alleged Genesis on EDW GroundZero (Turmoil) those many months ago. Well who better to answer that question than ......THE ONE who joins me live via digital feed from an undisclosed location." On the screen the Infamous One appears...behind him is an army of individuals all dressed in Unlimited colors... 1:"Thank you for having me Monica." MA:"I guess the best thing to do is cut to the chase...who are you?" 1:"I am Malcolm King....I am Xander.....I am Erin Christoph....I am Heartbreak Chick.....I'm Monica Angel....I am Dave Jacobs.....I am Hillary Rodahm Clinton....I am who I am. I can be everyone and no one at the same time. You see the question is not who I am the question should be why do you want to be me. In my position....weild my power. EDW was a testing ground....here in Evolution we see how our test subjects have progressed. If you ask me who I am the answer is simple I am your father, husband, lover, brother, sister, wife, mother, and worse enemy all rolled into one. I am in some respects a god....in some respects I am an emmissarry of hate. When you ask who is the One...look into the mirror and ask yourself another question; Am I the One?" MA:"So what is the purpose of The Unlimited?" 1:"Global Domination is really a high priority. However my personal goal is to marry Heartbreak Chick. We serve to eliminate the weak. We feel as a collective whole we can overcome the needs an individual might have. We are supposed to be working for the same goal...making whatever company we work for the best. How can we do that if there are those who are only in it for themselves. Their egos....thei ambition is the weak link that destroys the federation, not us. EDW was weak.....Evolution is weak. EDW was the testing ground, Evolution has become the battlefield. Our army is ready....is yours?" MA:"So you are saying you want to wage war on Evolution?" 1:"No I'm saying we are waging war on wrestling in general for starters. Evolution, FedWars, whatever if you are a so called World Champion...if you are a so called World class organization put it to the test and stop Us; plain and simple...any organization out there just stop us. I doubt anyone can...because they think we are your typical stable; and that ignorance would be their downfall. Every fed...every stable claims they are the best. Unlimited never claims that because it's common knowlege it's assumed and rightfully so. We don't have to convince anyone of our dominance...because quite frankly their opinion does not matter. Facts speak louder than words and what we have managed to do is nothing compared to what we will do." MA:"So you are saying that with Xander, Christoph, and Jinx as your generals you can take over the wrestling world as we know it?" 1:"As I stated before Evolution is our battle field, they have the audacity to say they have evolved when in reality this federation is far from the next level. The wrestlers here are mere fodder in our war, if anyone thinks they can stop us they are welcome to enter Evolution and try. Stop us...before we spread into your home and 'cure' it as we have done EDW and as we are in the process of doing to Evolution." MA:"Okay ONE we are running out of time so I'll go to the Answer the 10 count portion of our show with the first name being Tyler Davidson...." 1:"Davidson....LOL. I can't wait to tell him what his fiancees' last words were as the life was being choked out of her. Quite romantic in my opinion....a shame really she had to have been made an example of." MA:"Malcolm King, Xander, Troy Pierce, Big Daddy Dreamer, Erin Christoph, or anyone accused of being you?" 1:"No comment" MA:"Fedwars?" 1:"Prime for a hostile takeover." MA:"Heartbreak Chick?" 1:"She has a choice...rule by my side or die by my hand." MA:"EDW" 1:"A waste in my opinion....." MA:"Monday Night Football" 1:"John Madden has one season.....before The Unlimited expands their powerbase." MA:"Our Universal Champion" 1:"Sovereign would be a great soldier for our cause...if he wasn't so weak." MA:"The Future of wrestlin......" Suddenly a comotion off stage prompts both host and guest to turn their heads. A man wearing a black mask with bronze trim enters the stage area and grabs the microphone from Monica...... 1:"Well apparently you have a developing situation there, maybe you should get your own house in order before examining Unlimited business." With that The ONE cuts the video feed. Monica enraged looks at the intruder..... "WHO IN THE HELL DO YOU THINK YOU ARE?!?!?!?" man:"It's quite simple I'm Demetrius Echo, the next Universal Champion!" MA:"Echo....Echo....hey wait wasn't that the identity used by...." DE:"Shut your mouth woman, and listen to greatness. You see Tyler Davidson has denied me a chance to appear on Evolution TV he states he doesn't have the room on the roster....well listen here Tyler my boy on Sept 18th you will have an opening...maybe two. One of those spots belongs to me. I know what you are thinking...and no I'm not him; just adopted the gimmick to remind you of the time during your independant wrestling days in the midwest...to remind you on why you are holding a talent like me down." Security rushes the stage and carries Echo off he can be heard screaming..... DE:"This ain't over.....ohhh hell no it ain't over it's just begun; I am the past, present , and future.....Davidson your goons hurt me I'll see you in court!" MA:"I know I have an open door policy.....but damn. Can I have one show that doesn't end in a violent confrontation? Well that was my talk with The One...which in all honesty served as an advertisement for The Unlimited than answering any questions. Next Week is PPV hype Week and I'm hoping to do three shows....one featuring Matt Boone, the rookie sensation who just pinned Sovereign. I am also efforting to have a sitdown with Xander and Christoph, as well as The first lady of Evolution Heartbreak Chick and hopefully Jamal Kage. Then before the PPV a special PPV run down show. For this edition of Ten Count I'm Monica Angel.....good night."
