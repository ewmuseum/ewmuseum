Homicide Biography Real Name Eric Corven Hometown Los Angeles, CA Birthday 12/03/78 Height 6'6' Weight 264 Biography Eric Corven was born on December 3rd, 1978 in Los Angeles, California. He was raised with the basic life of anyone in Los Angeles, listening to gun shots and people being killed around him. His parents were both trying to raise Eric up right, so that he wouldn’t end up being a killer or a drug dealer. They failed. Eric’s friends got him into drugs, because they told him that it was the “cool” thing to do. Eric wasn’t the typical druggie though. He knew what he was doing, unlike most of his friends, who got shot and killed because of their stupidity. Eric was a kid that knew that he would end up turning into a bad person when he grew up, so he didn’t really fight it. His parents knew of the bad stuff he was into, and they tried to stop him, but again, they failed. Eric ignored his parents’ warnings and continued on his path to failure. When Eric was 14, he was put into juvenile lockup for the first time. When there, he faced kids that were bigger than him. Most of the people there were for crimes like stealing cars, beating people on the street, and other things of that sort. Eric was in there for selling drugs and jumping a person with a pipe. Eric didn’t really care about what the other people thought of him, as long as they stayed out of his way. Unfortunately, that’s not the way it was there. The kids there didn’t like him, and they let him know it by jumping him on a regular basis. The kids would beat him, and the police officers there would do nothing about it. They just watched and let it happened. Eric then decided that he was sick of the beatings he was receiving. He started working out with the weights that they had there, and he also started boxing and other forms of fighting techniques. He received his daily beatings, but each time he put up more of a fight. After about a year of training, he began dishing out the beatings, and all the people became afraid of him. He also had a growth spurt in juvi. When he was put there, about a year before, he was about 5’10”. Now, at 15, he reached a height of about 6’3”. He began being the aggressor, and he was so bad that they had to transfer him so the other kids could heal up from the beatings he gave them. At his confinement, Eric started off with the kids being afraid of him. He ruled the place, and continued with his training. He was well built at 15. He was probably about 220 pounds. After a year and a half of confinement, Eric was released from the juvenile imprisonment, and put back on the streets. When he got to his house, his parents were shocked to see the changes in Eric, and they didn’t want anything to do with him. Eric, being the pissed off person that he had become, didn’t take that lightly. He went through the house, causing destruction. His parents didn’t want him, so he didn’t want anything to do with them either. He went through his parents house with a baseball bat, and destroyed pretty much everything. He even hit his dad with it, after his dad tried to restrain him. His dad was a big man himself, about 6’4”, and Eric couldn’t take him on by himself, so he beat him unconscious with the baseball bat. Eric then left the house, leaving broken objects, and unconscious dad, and a crying mom behind him. He went onto the streets and basically toughed it out. He had to get a job, so he didn’t live in a corner. He went back to his old ways, selling drugs, which got him enough money to buy a cheap apartment. He also met some new friends, which were bad news. They were drug dealers, and they were also involved with gangs and murder. Eric fit in well with these people. In his spare time, Eric continued to work out and get stronger, and train in different styles of fighting as well. He boxed, practiced street fighting, and also picked up wrestling. He watched wrestling in his spare time, and he liked the sport. He liked the physicality of it, so he learned how to wrestle, with his boxing and street fighting. With those three styles under his belt, Eric was a person to be reckoned with. Eric was still growing as well, and his workouts made him even bigger and badder. By the time he was 18, he was 6’6”, and he weighed around 240 pounds. One night, Eric and his friends were at a drug deal, when one of the thugs they were dealing the drugs to got angry with them, and shot one of them to death. Eric was pissed off, because that person was his friend. He chased after the guy, but the man had a gun. He shot Eric in the left shoulder. Eric went down. The guy tried to run away, but Eric through a nearby pipe at the guy with his right arm (his good arm, he’s right handed) and hit the man in the legs, knocking him down. The man’s gun dropped, and landed near Eric. Eric instinctively grabbed the gun. He walked over to the man, who was on the ground, and proceeded to put eight bullets in his head. Eric then walked away with a grin on his face, happy with what he just did. He then proceeded to the nearest hospital to get the bullet removed. While there, they asked him what happened, and he told them, “Some maniac shot me, but he didn’t get away, because I killed him in self-defense.” They immediately called the police, who arrested Eric. Eric was then put in jail for the murder of the man. While in jail, he was with many people who were as big as him. They didn’t like him. Again, they tried to jump him, but Eric, who was trained in many fighting styles, handled them just fine. He also made friends in jail. He had buddies that he worked out with, and people that would train with him, and teach him new things about fighting. They also gave him the nickname, “Homicide”, because he killed the man, and even gave him a tattoo that said, “Homicide” on his right breast. He was put on trial for manslaughter. While in jail, his training increased, and he became stronger than he was before, weighing in at a 260 pounds. His physique was amazing, and the others in the jail feared him. At the trial, they determined that the murder was self-defense, believing Eric’s lie, and he was let go. Eric decided that he was done with drug dealing, the he would move on to even WORSE things, like murder. He felt good about killing that person, and he wanted that feeling again. Eric became effective at killing people, and killed many innocent people just walking down the streets. He became a contract killer, employed by a well known drug dealer by the name of Lexu$ Lincoln. Eric thought it was fun to kill people, and became VERY efficient at what he liked to do. Eric realized that he needed a job to a job that would make him look innocent. At the time, all he could think about doing was wrestling, which was one of his favorite things to watch on TV. He had the physique to be a wrestler, and he trained for it some time ago. He decided to give it a shot. He trained even harder, and got VERY good at it. He knew that he could easily beat the performances on TV, so he decided to try out for some local promotions. After wrestling, people realized that he was good enough for the big times. Scouts of wrestling promotions saw the potential in the young man, and decided that it was time to hire him. He got many contract offers, but decided that he wanted to find one of the biggest companies in the wrestling business. He decided that he would modify his look a bit. He liked the tattoo on his chest, and decided to get more. He also decided to grow a goatee. He went and got five more tattoos. He got a tattoo of a skull on fire on his left bicep, a flame on his left forearm, a big black Aztec symbol on his right bicep, barbed wire twisting from his elbow to his write on his right arm, and a skull with a fist through it on the back of his neck. At the age of 24, Eric has decided to tryout for NTICW. He’s hoping that he can get a contract with enough money and exposure to make people suspect that he's not a killer, and keep him with enough money to support himself for the rest of his life. Homicide will spread his terror through NTICW, and take out anyone in his path.
