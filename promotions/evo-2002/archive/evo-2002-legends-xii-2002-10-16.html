Legends XII Transcript Live on OSE! From Jeff Municipal Auditorium ~ New Orleans, Louisiana 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night October 16th, 2002 Legends XII is about set to kick off from New Orleans, Louisiana as the road to Faith continues onward. Splinters had already begun to form in the grouping of The Unlimited last week and with the defection of Scott Frost their enemies have just gotten themselves a little stronger for the oncoming war. Tyler Davidson was assaulted by Cori Simmons and Matt Boone who this week are expected to pay the price as a result of the orders by Daniel Somers and Frost will team with an unlikely ally in a battle against The Unlimited while L2J and William continue their uneasy alliance on the road to Faith. The tag team of Asis and Eric Badger didn’t survive for very long after last week’s match. Badger had taken the loss against Jameson and Seara allowing the two of them to advance and costing Asis his chance at some tag team gold. Asis didn’t appreciate that fact to much and after the match he made certain that Badger was well aware of it with an attack of his own on the already beaten Badger. This week both men will go one on one as Badger looks for a little payback or Asis tries finishing the job he started one week ago. One week ago HeartBreak Chick was the High Impact Champion until Thug roC pulled off the screwjob victory to steal the title for himself. If that wasn’t enough he then put her into a match against two other men to try and keep her away from him and the title. Strictly Prohibited made his debut last week and Gerico has been on the path to gold himself now with a former champion in his path. No one knows what is on the mind of Prohibited but the hints to his past dropped by him reveal that he and the HeartBreaker might not be strangers to one another after all. Jack O’Hara made an impact in eVolution last week with his debut victory in the four corners match and now he’s set to battle against Jay Stylez Junior once again. Stylez had nearly managed to pull off the victory last week but the numbers had cost him and O’Hara used his size to overcome the odds. This week both men look to repeat their previous performances with Stylez trying to change the history of last week with a victory over O’Hara. Two more newcomers to eVolution take part in this match and despite being new the two men find themselves paired up as a tag team. Jason Steele and the man called Jinosyde will battle against the Continental Champion Shocka and the former High Impact Champion Quasar the Great. Thug roC, the current High Impact Champion, will lead both men into this battle with their sights set on adding some more gold to their group. Jinosyde and Steele both look to get themselves off to a good start in eVolution with victories and advancing one step closer to tag team gold. After his stunt last week Shodan made Thug roC even madder than he already was at the young star. This week Shodan is put into a bit of a punishment match as he’s forced to go against Andrew Martin and Eric Knight in a handicap match. Martin still a newcomer to eVolution looks at this as a chance to not only make an impact and pick up a victory but also a chance to get into the good graces of Thug roC by taking care of a constant thorn in his side. Shodan on the other hand is doing anything possible to get his rematch against Shocka for the Continental Title and a win here could bring him a whole lot closer. Tommy Michaels is certainly pissing Daniel Somers off with each victory he gets. This week Somers ups the odds a bit on Michaels as he places him in a match against Big Daddy Dreamer, a former EdW Universal, Platinum, and Tag Team Champion among other titles in his career. Michaels is trying to keep his plans secret from Somers even longer but in order to do that he must pull through with the victory against Dreamer, his biggest challenge to date. The unlikely duo of William and Scott Frost take on the Unlimited’s own Xander and Jinx. Last week it was Frost who turned his back on The Unlimited only weeks after joining them and aligned himself with L2J. This week he must team with L2J’s ally and foe William to take on the men he had betrayed last week. Xander was very verbal in his desires to get his hands on Frost and perhaps even more importantly against the Universal Champion, William. Backstage ~ Unusual Face Featuring Seara Backstage a strange figure can be seen lurking around in the corridor. A familiar sight is seen as Seara walks around the corner, banging straight into the stranger in front of her. From under her breathe the word Jake can be heard, her older brother.
