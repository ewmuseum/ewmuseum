Prologue ~ The eVolution Begins Either way you decide to use the word, it would still be a rather fitting description of what is about to come. For weeks now the whisperings and rumors have been passing through the internet “news” reporters and the fans alike. Now is the time for all the rumors to be put to rest. Now is the time for all the answers to be put forth into the light. Well, maybe not all of them but the ones which have been on the minds of the fans and wrestlers alike certainly will be. Who is the man or woman behind this event here tonight? The question has remained a mystery to everyone despite the beliefs of some wrestlers. It started back on April the 27th when the Era of Destruction Wrestling had come to a very untimely end. Despite the dominance which the organization showed in the ratings wars of Fedwars it still managed to fall on rough times. These rough times would eventually lead to the end of the company’s nine month strong run. Many people cast the blame for it all on the shoulders of Nichole Kelley, the estranged then wife of former EdW owner Brandon Kelley. Coincidentally enough however less than one week after the closing of EdW, New Era Wrestling owned by Brandon’s former EdW business partner Daniel Somers, had also closed it’s doors. Less than one week later the rumors began circulating. Nichole having full possession of EdW following her husbands apparent mental breakdown, took the company her husband spent so much time building and put together a deal to sell the company. Rumors included buyers such as Daniel Somers, Justin Famous, Chris Osbourne, Cecil Cassidy, and many others. Who the real buyer is remains a mystery as another revelation is made. The man or woman that bought EdW had somehow managed to get their hands on the rights to the defunct nEw and decided to merge the two feds back into one seeing as how nEw had been an off shoot of EdW to begin with. This news put even more people into a frenzy as the finger pointed obviously at Daniel Somers being the new sole owner of EdW. Somers for the most part kept himself out of the spotlight of the debates and would remain uncooperative whenever the topic would be brought up declaring that he was through with the business and would be concentrating on other endeavors. Whoever the man was however he came prepared. The roster quickly began to grow including former members of EdW and nEw to the starting roster with the addition of some fresh new faces. Along with that eVolution also took back the spot which EdW and nEw had vacated in Fedwars itself as they returned to the fold which EdW had dominated for months. Everything was seemingly set for the big debut, the only question left was when would that be? The date is July the third and it’s a Wednesday night as eVolution now prepares itself. What it’s preparing itself for is unknown even to the wrestlers themselves. All any of them received was a cryptic message from Jeremy Hunter, the spokesman for eVolution, informing everyone signed to a contract to show up at the First Union Center in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. For their part they have shown up and now it is only a matter of time before they get the answers which they crave. Now is the time for the eVolution of professional wrestling to begin. eVolution Headquarters ~ Final Discussions ~ Featuring Josh Matthews, Eric Graves, Nichole Kelley, and the Owner~ The show opens with a shot of three men standing in one office as they are apparently discussing plans a few days prior to American Nightmare. One has his back turned towards the camera as he sits in his plush office chair looking across his desk at the two men in front of him. One of the two men is former co-owner of VPW Eric Graves, the recently announced Vice President of eVolution. The other is the assigned President of eVolution Josh Matthews. Together they are two of the men who can be considered the brain trust of eVolution and ultimately decide the fate of the wrestlers and the company itself. Who the third man is, is still unknown at the moment but before the meeting can officially start they seemingly await the arrival of a fourth person. As though on cue the door to the office suddenly swings open and stepping into the room is the former owner of EdW and ex-wife of Brandon Kelley, Nichole. Nichole slowly strolls into the room and looks between Graves and Matthews before setting her sights upon the third man in the chair and cracking a smile upon seeing him. “Funny meeting you here now isn’t it?” she asked sarcastically as she smiled. “Not at all,” the man quietly replied his face still hidden from the camera’s view. “We’ve been waiting here for a while now just for you. The three of us have some very important business to get out of the way after you and I are through.” “Well I had good reason to be late,” Nichole responded. “I received a very important phone call just before leaving. It turns out that my dear old ex-husband didn’t have the funds he thought he did.” “What do you mean?” the man asked. “It turns out that he has already been forced to put an end to his new Cursed venture,” Nichole answered. “Setting all his contracted wrestlers free including a couple that I believe you wanted for eVolution.” “Interesting,” the man said as he thought aloud to himself. “I didn’t believe it would last very long but to die this quickly is just sad. Oh well one less thorn I will have in my side during the coming months.” “It seems that way now don’t it?” Nichole asked somewhat hypothetically. “All you need do now is make things official with me and it will all be taken care of.” “Yes and that will be done on Wednesday night,” the man replied. “You have the papers now don’t you?” “Yes I do,” Nichole answered as she pulled an EdW folder out of her bag and slid it across the desk to the man. “All you need to do is sign them and EdW is yours.” “Yeah that’s all well and good but can’t we get down to business here already?” Matthews yelled out impatiently interrupting Nichole and the other man. “Me and probably Graves even do have better things to do than sit here and listen to this crap.” “Yeah we do,” Graves agreed. “So if you can hurry this up it would be most appreciated.” “Yeah, yeah don’t get your panties in a wad,” the man responded casting a glance at Matthews more so than Graves. “I’ll sign them now and we can save the official announcement for American Nightmare.” The man grabs a pen from on top of the desk in front of him and quickly turns through the papers to the final page before putting the pen to the paper and signing his name which has a signature to illegible to read. He finishes the signing and then slides the papers back over to Nichole. “It’s done,” he said as he slid the papers closer to her. “All we need do is wait for Wednesday.” “I’ll be there,” Nichole said as she picked the papers up. “Until then I will let you boys get back to work.” Nichole quickly gets the papers gathered together and turns leaving the room and allowing the three men to get back to work. “It’s about time that bitch is gone,” Matthews said as soon as she left the room. “Why the hell do we have to deal with her?” “Are you really that stupid Matthews?” Graves interrupted. “Until EdW is officially sold we need to keep her around. Once that happens than the hell with her, right?” “Couldn’t have said it better myself,” the man agreed. “After Wednesday night Nichole will no longer be needed and I will be in complete control of EdW finally. Now though we must decide what the plan is for this first show? We have to decide how to determine the Universal Title.” “Why don’t we just have a tournament?” Matthews suggested. “It’s the easiest way to do things.” “And the most boring,” Graves again interrupted. “Why the hell are we going to do a tournament? We already know the title won’t be decided until August. That will just drag things out longer than needed.” “I agree with Graves,” he replied. “A tournament may work in some form but not the usual way.” “Yeah we need something different,” Graves agreed. “A one night eight man tournament then at X-Rated,” Matthews suggested again. “Then we just need to decide how to determine the eight men.” “That’s the tough part,” the man commented. “How can we do that now and do it fairly?” “The hell with fairness,” Graves responded. “I say you just let me pick eight guys and they do their thing.” ”Not a chance,” Matthews butted in. “I don’t trust you as far as I can throw you Graves. You’d rig this thing to work to your advantage somehow.” As Matthews and Graves begin arguing over the details of everything the other man simply sits silent in his seat staring at the two men. Suddenly a ringing noise can be heard and the man reaches to his side and picks up the phone sitting on his desk. “Hello?” he asked into the receiver before going wide eyed at the response on the other end. “Who is this?” “None of your concern,” the voice came back through the receiver. “Just consider me a helping hand. Take my advice and I’ll be in touch again.” Before anything else can be said the sound of the other man hanging up his end of the phone can be heard leaving the man there with the phone to his ear and no one on the other side. He places the phone back down and slowly rises from his seat staring at the two partners he has that are still arguing. “Now, my friends please calm yourselves and have a seat again,” he said interrupting their back and forth banter. “I have just been given a solution by a very odd individual I would say.” “What is it then?” Matthews asked impatiently and still obviously irritated with Graves. “I’ll save that for later as well,” the man responded. “I think it will work quite nicely.” The scene slowly faded out to black as the three men continued on with their conversation moving to a different topic finally. When they come on however there is now one more man standing in the ring, a man that most fans haven’t seen in nearly a year since his last appearance in Cameron Griffin’s Old School Wrestling. Jack Tyler: Oh my god! I know him! Justin Fritz: Who the hell is that monster? Jack Tyler: That’s Sovereign! Last we seen him he was locked up in the nuthouse following his OSW war against Somers of all people. It’s been damn near a year but he must be here to finish the job finally. Pierce surprised by the sudden appearance of the man releases his hold on Somers while Boone foolishly rushes in at Sovereign. He goes for a clothesline but Sovereign ducks up and as quickly as he can launches his hand at Pierce and locks in a tongan death grip. Pierce tries fighting the move off but Sovereign applies the pressure and forces Pierce down to his knees as Boone comes back up behind him. Boone drives a series of forearms into the back of Sovereign trying to break the hold but seemingly having no effect. Without warning though Sovereign releases the hold on Pierce allowing him to fall to the mat and quickly turns his attention towards Boone locking the same hold in on him and quickly dropping him to the mat with it. Justin Fritz: This beast has just laid out Pierce and Boone! He just saved Somers! Jack Tyler: Well that I don’t understand. Then again no one ever knew what was going through that man’s mind. He is a certifiable lunatic which is why he hasn’t been seen in a year. Justin Fritz: Here comes Famous now. The bodyguard of Daniel Somers is on his way out here to do his job possibly. Jack Tyler: I think Famous might need some help if Sovereign wants Somers. Perhaps the US Army would be a good idea. Sovereign apparently satisfied with the pain inflicted on Boone releases the hold and then turns his attention and locks eyes with Somers for the first time in nearly a year. Sovereign makes no signs of moving in on Somers and Famous quickly gets down the ramp and grabs hold of Somers from the outside pulling him out of the ring. Sovereign simply watches as Famous and Somers backpedal up the ramp with a look of confusion mixed with fear and shock on the face of Somers. For the first time in nearly a year he came face to face with the one man who nearly ended his career. Pierce and Boone remain laid out in the ring hurting from the sudden attack of Sovereign as the closing credits appear on screen. Justin Fritz: I have no idea what the story is here but we are out of time tonight. We’ll be back next week fans with the premiere broadcast of eVolution Legends!
