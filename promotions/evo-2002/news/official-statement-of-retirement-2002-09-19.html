Official statement of Retirement......

19 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A bruised, bloodied, and defeated Malcolm King was interviewed by Monica Angel shortly after his defeat at the hands of Xander.... "The better man tonight won, it's that simple." When pressed about if this was indeed his last match..... "As an in-ring competitor this was it. I want to thank Tyler Davidson and the Evolution staff for giving me the chance to vindicate myself in the eyes of my fans. The next logical question would be has Evolution seen the last of Malcolm King? I would say No.....I may no longer be in the ring but this business is still in my blood. Before the PPV I was discussing the possibility of ULWN being a feeder network for Evolution as well as some responsibilities as an agent for Evo and maybe working on the creative/TV side. I'll be honest though, the fire still burns....but Xander beat me up pretty good. I need to stay away for awhile heal uo from my injuries spend time with my family." So upon your return in whatever capacity will Xander be a target..... "This fed is about Evolving and moving on. WHEN Malcolm King makes his return I will not pick up where I left off. Xander has earned the right to move on to bigger and better things, and so have I. Ironically this all started as a way to bring Xander to the next level......through his betrayal of me it seems he didn't need as much help as we were led to believe. He beat me plain and simple, that chapter in both our books is closed." finally anything else you wish to say...... "Just a thank you to all the fans of Malcolm King for supporting me for all these years. Thanks to the boys in the back, past and present; for providing me the highest level of competition I could ever imagine. And my wife and kids as well as my family for supporting and understanding. Love me or hate me I just hoped in the end you all respected me. Thank you all for allowing me the honor of entertaining you." So Malcolm King closes the book on what some may consider a hall of fame career....what the future holds one can only wonder but for now today seems like it's a good day.
