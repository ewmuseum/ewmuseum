More On eVolution's Shut Down

April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
by Jeremy Hunter One last bit of news was released today by Davidson Enterprises Board of Directors member Justin Roberts concerning the shut down of eVolution. It turns out Friday before Unbreakable that an unnamed source had approached the Board of Directors about purchasing the rights to EdW which was one of the two federations that originally spawned eVolution. After the shutdown was made official the sale of EdW was also made official to the buyer who is now known to be Nichole Kelley the ex-wife of Brandon Kelley and the woman who originally sold EdW to Davidson Enterprises.
