Legends LIX

10 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Legends came to you live tonight from the Adelaide Entertainment Center as the Road to Glory tournament continued with two explosive matches as well as a Tag Team Title match. We also saw the debut of newcomer Felony as he took on Machine and Scott Evans and Troy Chryst battled in a hardcore contest as Evans tried to redeem himself following his defeat last week at the hands of Damon O’Halloran. In the main event we saw Matt Hunt and Thug roC battle in a first round Road to Glory match up with the added incentive of both the United and eVolved Titles being on the line in a unification bout thanks to Robert Brooks with one man guaranteed to walk out a dual champion. Asis also had his first round match as he battled against former eVolved Champion Aaron Justice and Shocka made his return to television with the promise of competing next week in the Road to Glory in order to defend his championship as last year’s winner.
