Legends 3 Goes Down and 4 Card Announced

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Legends 3 has just come to a conclusion live from the Mid-Hudson Civic Center and Tyler Davidson followed the show up by giving me the listing for next week's show which is below. Rising Star Tournament ~ Standard Match Shodan vs. "Platinum" Brad Kelley Rising Star Tournament ~ Standard Match Asis vs. Drake Non-Title ~ Tag Team Match Shocka/"Punisher" Platinum Cassidy vs. Xander/Erin Christoph Non-Title ~ Cruiserweight Invitational Battle Royal "The Future" Jake Keeton vs. Quasar the Great vs. Kin Ryo vs. HeartBreak Chick vs. Barbatos Bronx vs. Python vs. Sean Mason Non-Title ~ Eight Man Tag Team Match Sovereign/"The Hollywood Monster" Impact/"The DiscoKid" Troy Pierce/"The Natural" Matt Boone vs. L2J/"Mr. Egoist" Tommy Michaels/"Black Jesus" Jamal Kage/Big Daddy Dreamer All eVolution stars will be in attendance as the countdown to X-Rated commences live from the Nassau Coliseum in Uniondale, New York. OOC: Thanks to Shocka and Cyrus for helping out with the results last night so I could get them up early as possible. It damn near 6AM now though so I will post card on site with match previews tomorrow night more than likely along with various other updating.
