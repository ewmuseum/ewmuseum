DD Main Event News

15 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The HUGE Main Event at Digital Destruction promises to be a Match of The Year Candidate as Matt Boone, Jamal Kage, L2J all compete for Sovereign's eVo Universal Title. At this point the match is "Officially" a 4 corner match, now this is subject to change as eVo spokespeople have hinted that this match could have another interesting twist, falls anywhere with no holds bared. Mix that with the ruling that Sovereign doesn't even need to be counted in the fall the chances that Sovereign retaining his title are growing slimmer by the day. It seems that S. is serious about removing Sovereign from his position of power while not directly doing it himself knowing Sovereign would probably go directly after S. in a fit of rage. Isn't it interesting that the "Heel" owner is trying to get the title off his "Heel" champion, and the man that he has allied himself with over the past 3 months. Sovereign suffered some cuts and burns last week due to L2J's attack that was cut by the network, the damage isn't significant, he is expected to be at Digital Destruction with all guns firing.
