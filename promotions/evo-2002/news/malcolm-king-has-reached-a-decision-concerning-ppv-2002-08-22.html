Malcolm King has reached a decision concerning PPV

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After Legends the announcement came down that Malcolm King was not only granted a PPV matchup with Xander, but now he's able to pick the stipulations. Malcolm wasted no time as he spoke with Monica Angel after Legends in the lobby of his hotel..... "Well Monica,after my encounter with Jinx; I guess I was supposed to be shocked at his turn but I really could have cared less because my focus win or lose is on one man. Like I said before this is even greater than the Univeral title....for I would have been the first one to accept Sovereign's challenege, especially after that incident with BOB a few years ago." he pauses as he looks at Monica before continuing...... "I had already announced my desire to have an I QUIT MATCH in THE CELL; I figured now that he has the Cell champion on his side he can get a few pointers on how to survive in that environment. So that will be our match for the PPV. But then it was announced I can pick any....and I mean any stipulation, it was something I could not pass up......" he pauses for a minute, collecting his thoughts before continuing.... "Most people probably know this, but now I'm making it official Sept 18th at the PPV will be my last match as an in ring performer. I plan to retire that night. I don't plan to go alone however. So Xander you and The Unlimited can spend the next few weeks making sure I don't make it to the PPV; because this stipulation should keep them busy on the night of Sept 18th. I WANT A DQ STIPULATION...that states if ANYONE interferes for EITHER MAN, XANDER LOSES HIS CAREER. LOL....you see Xander you only now have to worry about me one on one; in addition to Christoph's ambition, in addition to whoever you manage to piss off from now until then. I'm sure The Unlimited will be busy keeping everyone away from us....so that I can FINALLY have a one on one confrontation without the BS! We know that on September 18th one career will be over; it's up to you Xander to make sure that count doesn't escalate. Get your house in order Xander.....then come into mine for what may be the last time, as I make you say I QUIT for all the world to hear." Malcolm thanks Monica for tracking him down as he turns and leaves to head up to his hotel room....
