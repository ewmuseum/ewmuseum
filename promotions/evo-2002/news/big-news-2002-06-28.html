Big News!

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Today, I spoke with "The Tru One" Joshua Krowe. Former EdW wrestler, and Karnage and Suicidal champion of the no longer open federation EdW. Krowe spoke about his, wanting to return to wrestling. But he is tired of the bad bumps, and the bruises that came along. He stated, "I know i've bled for a good of wrestling, and i've almost lost my life. I think that i've bled enough to make the hardcore division in any federation a suitable place to be. I've proven that although I am hardcore, that I do just as well in the normal division as I have became a multiple time tag team champion with my long time friend Xander, and others that i've befriended on that road. But now im looking for a more "suitable" role. Probably as commissioner of a federation, or even the times working in the offices of an interfed." I know that people in eVolution that know "The Tru One" will join me in a warm and hearty good luck. In other news. Erin Christoph was seen at a Jimmy Eat World concert, funny thing is that even though they weren't aware of it. Christoph and fellow roster mate and former EdW wrestler, Xander were both at the same concert. Also, at the concert was sighted many a time. Malcolm King, or atleast people that looked like him. King is doing a good job getting his face scene around the world to, what we think, scare the hell out Daniel Somers. On the protection part of this conversation. Justin Famous. Said to be security leader of the eVolution security team has been talking with yet another former wrestler to help him. It is also known that Famous quit his job as bouncer at hot/violent dance club "The Groove". Famous has been steady at the offices working on security plans for each and every show.
