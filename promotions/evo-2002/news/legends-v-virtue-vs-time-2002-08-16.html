Legends V: Virtue vs. Time

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Virtue; The high and might morale of the person used in the perplexity of reason or action. The opening tide of revelation has finally began, a river of destruction now floods the gates of eVolution Wrestling as the team of Xander and Christoph take charge of the infamous stable, Unlimited. Unlimited. An organization. That grows by the day. More and more men, secretly work under their code number. Each who joins is given a number. Each who works to make an active difference is given the chance. The chance to prove their worth to Unlimited. Their chance to come out, and lay claim to the rebirth of something new. Number 867, was revealed July 7th, 2002. A man, who helped the near destruction of Daniel Somers. A man, who debuted and showed the world his true nature; Destruction. This man is known as, Erin Christoph. Alone. In his quest to continue the life of the Unlimited being the only admitable member. Erin Christophs' quest for Unlimited glory lead him to the path of the Continental Championship, inwhich he has obtained and retained for the longest period of time in eVolution Wrestling history. But now, with his cousin Xander by his side. The possibilities are endless. The quickest rising rookie, made championship history and the hardcore Icon himself made eVolution Wrestling history by being apart of Unlimited and taking his time to destroy the man who seems to have his whole life planned out, Malcolm King. Malcolm King is now supposed to stand, toe to toe with the new team of Xander and Christoph along side with Cell Champion, Jinx. Can the never before formed team of "The Urban Legend" Malcolm King and the "Core of Hardcore" Jinx take out "The New Threat" Erin Christoph and his partner "The Extremist" Xander? And what game plans does Malcolm King speak of? Is Davidson involved or Tory Pierce? Only time will tell.
