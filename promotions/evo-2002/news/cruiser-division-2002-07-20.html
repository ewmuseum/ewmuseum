Cruiser division.

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Around the wrestling world, Daniel Somers has went. Already signing some hopefulls and some definate cruiserweight superstars. Daniel Somers has signed several cruiser weights already. I was assigned to interview one of the several, Kin Ryo. Ryo is a member of the eVolution staff of wrestlers but contract negotiations are still in talks. This young wrestler at the age of twenty has been trained by the "hardcore legend" and long time friend and foe of both Xander and Jinx, "The Tru One". I got in touch with "The Tru One" and this is what was said. "The Tru One": I remember hardcore wrestling being innovative to a whole new level. But when i signed this kid to become a cruiser weight. I knew there was that "potential spark" in him. He is 5'10" and 215 lbs. he is a small kid. Twenty years old, just graduated college and my wrestling school "Wikid Ink". He tried to be apart of the PTC promotions but that didn't fly to well with them since he was trained by a former Fedwars.org top star. But with me still being in good connections with the people from EdW even though the closure. I gave Ryo some advice, i said. "Look kid. This will be the last thing that I tell you. Call up Daniel Somers. Tell him you have professional wrestling training, and that you heard about his Cruiserweight search. If Somers finds out that I taught you, it will more than likely help a lot." All I can say now, that Ryo is hired and in the division. Watch out for Kid Ryo....this boy has developed awesome skill. Afterwards, "The Tru One" made some comments about the happenings with eVolution and is pleased to see that Somers is running a very successful organization.
