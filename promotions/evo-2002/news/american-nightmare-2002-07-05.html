American Nightmare

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello ladies and gentlemen. My name is Josh S.. Former columnist for the EdW news transcript, Blacklabel. Now. As I continue to do the news, and handle the truth behind the scenes of eVolution. Daniel Somers has rewarded me, and given me the job of writing the "hype" of American Nightmare. American Nightmare, was very impressive. The ultimate heel, Daniel Somers was scared out of his mind. With the attack on Xander by a member of the infamous stable "Unlimited" and then the phone. Somers and his bodyguard Justin Famous, former EdW Universal Champion. They have no idea what they are going to do to protect the best interests in a federation that has just begun. But when i witnessed the unbelievable turn of events of Tyer Davidson being the true owner of the eVolution banner and not his long time friend Daniel Somers, our CEO. I was stunned. It was a very smart move, in their friendship. But i feel that Davidson is planning something. Something "brood" i mean Rude. Nichole Kelley. WOW! What can i say? Brandon Kelleys' ex-wife, former owner of EdW. AND HOT AS HELL! I would like to see Nichole and HBC in a "Diva's" photoshoot. Maybe i should try to pull some strings. Also, it is known that Erin Christoph is a member of the stable, Unlimited. After the post-pay per view promo that was conducted with Christoph. It is well known that he is out to end the career of someone, and that someone looks to be Xander. Asis. Arrested, for what seems to be the brutal torture of another man before eVolution has started. Is as far as we know, still in custody. More news on that later. (To be continued)
