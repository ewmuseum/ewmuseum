Security heightened.

22 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the recent remarks of: "The Disco Kid" Troy Pierce, and the apparent aligation that one, Malcolm King; will be looking for the CEO of eVolution wrestling, "Suicidal" Daniel Somers. No longer a wrestler, Somers has made it a point that he will not allow himself into physical conforntation unless one hundred percent needed. Somers has hired one man to lead the staff of security. A former wrestler, who has his named on the great champions of the EdW Universal title. Justin Famous. Famous has had bad blood with both, King and Pierce. This will hopefully solve Somers distress because it is a known fact that the three of them don't like each other. Famous has taken his oath into security guard-ship. He has promised to set aside any bad blood him and Somers might of had and protect him to the best of his ability. BUT, thing is. Justin Famous has a security contract at another place right now and has a three month waiting period before he can offically be the chief security gaurd for eVolutoin. Currently, Justin Famous is a security guard at highly violent dance club in Chattanooga, TN called "The Groove".
