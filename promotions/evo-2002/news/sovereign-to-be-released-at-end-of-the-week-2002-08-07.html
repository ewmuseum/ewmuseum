Sovereign to be released at end of the week

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Doctors met with eVo superstar Sovereign today at the arena where Legends IV is to take place and after a lengthy interview process have decided that Sovereign no longer requires permanent care, or to be confined due to the recent positive effects of his new medication. Sovereign, aka William Forth has had a long history of mental Illness that includes Psychotic Behavior that manifests itself violently as many staff members of Evolution discovered over the past month and a half, Sovereign was then put back into a mental hospital until he was released to the care of eVo CEO Daniel Somers, since then Sovereign has been secluded only to be allowed to go from arena to hotel. Sovereign has been showing signs of a less dominant, however less dangerous personality, what Doctors believe to be his true self and not the "Human Monster" persona that we have come to know as violent, unpredictable and uncontrollable. Sovereign wrestles tonight in Legends IV main event 8 man tag match and is considered to be one of the favorites to win the Universal Title next Wednesday at X-rated. Also in other Sovereign news it seems that CEO Somers has chosen Sovereign to represent eVo in the upcoming Fedwars IPPV coming the middle of the Month. It's no secret that Sovereign and Somers have a rather unique relationship as it seems Sovereign is content to help Somers in any way possible as it seems his demented mind is trying to "buy back" the respect he once had as a destructive force in the wrestling world. Sovereign and Somers are no strangers as they once almost ended each other’s careers just over 1 year ago in a brutal 2 out of 3 falls match that ended in a draw. It’s no doubt that sovereign will be a good choice for eVo to place in the tournament as Sovereign is most definatley a dangerous man, regardless of if he’s been released or not.
