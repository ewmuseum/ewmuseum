The Ruble Injury Report

18 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the recent opening of eVolution many stars whose names weren't known to the public from past accomplishments knew for the first couple shows they would need to perform at a rate like nonother in thier career, which possibly could lead to career ending inury. As of late many men, including the likes of Quasar the Great, Luigi Lombardi, Anarchy have all fell victim to the injury bug whether it been in ring or out, neither of these three men look to be stepping inside a sqaured cicrle no time soon. Which brings me to another individual who won't be stepping inside a ring nevertheless be walking for the next few years of his life, Teddy Rosenhowell. The ref for the No DQ match between Asis and Sov which saw him slammed inbetween the tractor trailer and the ring apron. Paralyzing him from the waist down. There as been no word if the family of the Rosenhowell's will be pressing any charges against the eVolution coporation, however thier must be some action taken with Sov who has caused more trouble than he's worth in the last three weeks. Leaves me to wonder if he's going to be the downfall of Evolution. I guess that a question we'll have to wait and see. On the flip side of things Sov hasn't been the only person on a lunatic vengeance as of late. During the main event on Legends II, The Synidcate duo did a number on former acquaintance, Katrina when she was named by Troy Pierce as one of her partners for the six man intergender tag match. Results of the match left Katrina wheeled out on a strecher after being dropped on her neck several times by HbC before BDD locking in his patent Dreamer Crossface. Once arriving to the hositpal Katrina was already passed out and thats the way she remains at the time being. Dr. Timberlake had these words to say. "At this time Katrina is in a very bad state. We know the dics she had reputured a few years back have now fallen back out of place and that maybe the reason for her being in the coma like state right now." How could a friend do this to another friend you ask? Well if your in the wrong place at the wrong time I guess anything can happen. I wonder if The Syndicate members have any bad feelings for thier action. Knowing them probably not but we will find out once they realease a promo for this upcoming Legends show, which will be the third installment and prolly the best one yet. As for the eVolution reporting team my name is Danny Ruble, so long and goodbye.
