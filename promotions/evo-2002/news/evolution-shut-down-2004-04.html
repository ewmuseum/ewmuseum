eVolution Shut Down

April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
by Jeremy Hunter The vote mentioned by Tyler Davidson on Legends this past week went through and following the untelevised events of Unbreakable last night the Board of Directors had all the ammo they needed and officially pulled the plug on eVolution. During the Universal Title match that pitted Xander defending his newly won title against Asis and Malcolm King in The Hell's Fury match Asis powerbombed Xander off the top of the cage and through one of the exploding tables outside of the cage that resulted in Xander's immediate removal from the match. Many people backstage were extremly concerned about the well being of Xander following the literally explosive spot and it swung favor among the Board of Directors in the direction of those who were in favor of shutting the promotion down led by, Justin Roberts one of the majority shareholders in the company. As mentioned earlier Unbreakable ended up not being aired as the PPV airing was another casualty of the massive budget cut. Below are the final results of the three match PPV event. Tag Team Title Match The Brotherhood of X def. The Order United Title Match w/Matt Hunt as Special Guest Referee Damon O'Halloran def. Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick Universal Title Hell's Fury Match Asis def. Malcolm King and Xander Here also is a transcript of a speech given by Matt Hunt during the event as well following a Last Post on Damon O'Halloran. “Greetings, camaches…” Hunt spoke to a pop from the fans “I would say that’s great to be back, but in fact I’m not…” he starts, pausing “What?” enquired Jack “To wait all that time and to come back for just one night pisses me off. Some say that it can’t be helped and that these things happen, well that’s just bollocks. It could have been dealt with, had the “powers that be” pulled their fingers out…but instead, they pulled the plug on eVolution and thus, destroyed everything that you people helped build up and everything this company stood for” “Hunt’s outspoken views here, we should have expected something like this…” said Jack as a short burst of booing followed Hunt’s previous comment “With that said, I raise a big “up yours” to Davidson Enterprises…” Hunt states as there’s a pop from the fans “Bollocks to this gaping financial black hole – which was your responsibility in the first place - and bollocks to each and every single one of you. May you all kiss The Postman’s English Ass, you group of grade-A camaches!!!” he continued, with another pop “He’s laying on pretty thick, eh?” quipped Jack “Can’t say I blame him…” he continued “So I guess the only thing left to say is…goodbye” Hunt muttered as he lowered his head, forcing some boos from the crowd “Nah, that ain’t gonna happen. I’m not here to say goodbye, people, I’m simply here to say ‘see you later’. I’m not disappearing off the map, not after waiting all this time to return to a ring so I’m looking for a new home. Whether it be employed under eVolution and the bunch of ball-less tarts that run this place or somewhere else, guaranteed that The Postman will run his mouth and give some slap in the way that only I know how to” he says while strolling around the ring “Couldn’t have said it better…” said Justin “So, I’ll see you lot later and as always, let the games begin…” Points of Authority blasted into full swing as Hunt dropped the mic and calmly exited the ring. The fans’ roar echoed throughout the arena as The Postman strolled up the entrance way and soon disappeared through the curtain “See you later, Postman, see you later…” uttered Justin.
