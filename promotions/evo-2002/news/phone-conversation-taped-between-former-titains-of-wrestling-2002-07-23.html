Previously Recorded [Some parts have been edited out] "Hello." "How ya doin? Long time no here." "Yeah I know, I've been mantaining though." "And you?" "You already know how it is." "Yea, still being the most hated man on the planet?" "Well actually, no. I'm playing a bit of a different role." "Yeah you were telling me that." "Excatly, no need for me to really show up when I can just use the phone." "That does make better sense. Since in fact if anyone knew who you really were they would probably go haywire." "I could see that. But I didn't call to talk about me I called to talk about you." "Yeah...well what'd up?" "Are you currently fedding anywhere at the moment." "No, at the time I'm just sitting back and waying my options." the voice started. "You know for an former Titians of Wrestling/Edge, FSW and PCW star. You should know that." "That I do know and that's why I called. If you haven't heard eVo is now currently in looks for the top crusierweights in the world and well..." the other voice cut him off. "I am the best crusierweight in the world." "True indeed, thats why I want your talents in eVo." "Wait, are you asking me to join forces with you again?" "Well..actually...hell, yes thats what I'm saying." "You know I've never really been able to say no to you." "So is that yes?" "Yes, that is a Yes!" "Great! Have you ever been to Poughkeepsie, New York?" "Huh....poooo..Who?" "Nevermind, I'll email directions too you. The show is tommorrow, I would send plane ticket but then they would know who I was. But don't worry once you arrive in the fed you will have first class service all the way and that you can take to the bank." "Aight likes the sounds of that, just don't think Blair will like it too much." "Tell her don't worry about." "You know how she is." "Yeah I do." "Well I'll see you there tommorrow night." "No I'll see you there tommorrow." "Oh yeah...hehehe." The man laughed as the sounds of both hanging up cuts him off.
