Legends VIII

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another night of action and incredible violence will come to you live from Ft. Lauderdale as eVolution presents Legends VIII! Shocking revelations at Legends VII piled up and continued to effect the outlook of Digital Destruction which is only two weeks away. One of the number one contenders to the Universal Title, Impact had been taken out of action and severely injured at the hands of Sovereign which leaves L2J the lone man left. Erin Christoph revealed a shocking secret of his own which brought him to blows with the owner of eVolution and the promise of a one on one encounter between the two at Digital Destruction.
