Rumors, or truth?

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier, Jack Tyler announced that the recent decision by the Evolution Wrestling, and CBN entertainment department has allowed the uncencored styles of everything that Evolution has to offer. But the plan to put a "Parental Advisory" symbol in the Evolution "intro scene" a must that way, and i quote CBN president "These stupid people, won't sue my ass for actually allowing real entertainment." Also, after the announcement that July 3rd is the day of opening with Evolution first ever card "American Nightmare" the phone lines have been flooded with excitement from awaiting fans of such wrestlers as: Xander, Shifty, Asis, Tommy Michaels, Malcolm King, and even new comer Erin Christoph. Everyone that has been voiced on the news portion has some how made an impact already in the eVolution company. Fans trying to order life time tickets to shows and ppv's and even begging for the opening of a shopzone for eVolution. Erin Christoph, Xander, Malcolm King, and Eric Knight all have been keeping intouch with Daniel Somers and Jeremy Hunter to help increase their obviously already popular backstage politics. But since the death of Rick Fury former manager of Erin Christoph, Christoph has been acting strangely and has shown sides of "Anger" and has told people backstage that he has found just the place to help release his anger......
