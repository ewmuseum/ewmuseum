An angle federation on the eWe network that opened in July 2002 with American Nightmare and ran its weekly Legends sixty-one times to 1 January 2004, with a pay-per-view most months, tours of England, Ireland, Japan and Australia, and eight championships. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

eVolution began as a reveal. American Nightmare, on 3 July 2002, was billed as live on CBN from the First Union Center in Philadelphia, and its prologue set out the one question the fans had come with: who had bought Era of Destruction Wrestling and the defunct New Era Wrestling and merged the two. The answer came in two parts. Tyler Davidson, the head of Davidson Enterprises, had signed the papers and put up the money, and the former owner of New Era Wrestling would make the day-to-day decisions. Troy Pierce, the EdW loyalist, walked out to tell him, “I’m here for EdW, not you.”

The company ran on a Wednesday show, Legends, sixty-one times, and a pay-per-view most months from American Nightmare in July 2002 to Faith in November 2003, with house shows, a developmental territory and a run of columns beside them. Its rules page called it “a 100% angle fed” in which the angles the roster submitted could help win a match, and it staged a Cell of fighters beside the wrestlers from the first night.

The Universal Championship came over from EdW, and Sovereign won the vacant belt, the first held in eVolution, by unhooking it from the top of a ladder in an eight-man tournament at X-Rated on 14 August 2002. Eight championships passed through 59 reigns. The Rising Star and High Impact belts were unified into one eVolved title at Legends XXI on 15 January 2003, and the Continental belt was unified with the title of the developmental territory as the United Championship at X-Rated on 31 August 2003.

The Unlimited, the faction around its Director of Operations, were the first to hold its Tag Team Championship, from 24 November 2002. On 19 February 2003 at the Cow Palace the faction handcuffed Yoshii Tanaka and beat him with a barbed-wire bat to force the masked Strictly Prohibited out to unmask, having taken him for Tyler Davidson. The face under the mask was Nik VanCore’s.

The last write-up, Legends LXI on 1 January 2004, came live from the Telstra Dome in Melbourne, with an eight-person tag written out and a United Title match listed without a result; Rise of the Legends, a pay-per-view billed for three days later with nine matches, left no results. The last dated roleplay is from 9 March 2004. A page of its own, “The 2004 Revival,” lays out a relaunch with handlers writing their own matches, three singles titles and six supercards a year. Whether the relaunch aired, and why the weekly stopped, is not in the surviving record.
