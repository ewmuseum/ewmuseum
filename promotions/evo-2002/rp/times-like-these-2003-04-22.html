Times Like These

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/03 {The scene opens in the now-too-familiar Unlimited war room. As Xander sits at a small art style desk to the left of the room, you can see various pictures upon the wall of other Evolution wrestlers who have challenge the Unlimited, and have fallen. At the very end of the list, you see the pictures of James Kimson, and Tyler Davidson.} 'So Kimson' Xander begins once again. 'For some, odd reason you've decided to stick your nose into my business. I mean, I guess I can't blame you. After getting your ass molested by Sovereign, you need someone to try and make you feel better about yourself.. so why not go after the biggest dog in town? Smart, yet very naive move.' {Xander shuts off the desklamp, now being illuminated by the strategy table's light} 'If you have a beef with Christoph, S., whoever.. deal with that yourself. But, for some reason, you think you can take any of it out on me. That shows your gross incompetence. I don't know if you've noticed the streak I'm on, but I am undefeated in the last few weeks. Last time I won a match on Legends? You obviously don't even try to look, cause you only have to go back two weeks to find that. In a matter of fact, it was when I pinned the Universal champion, Seara's bitch.' 'No matter though. I'll dispose of you tomorrow, and once again show Davidson exactly what he's in store for at End of Days. Hey Ty-Lo, hope you're watching this week.. I mean, after getting your ass royally kicked last week, I wouldn't blame you for being too afraid to show up in the same building as I am in, but I know you own at least one television set. So go ahead, flip it to good ol' Legends, and watch a man handle business.' 'And Kimson. I'd offer you some advice, but being as low in the food chain as you are, even if I told you, you'd never be able to figure out what Xander would do.' {The scene fades to black}
