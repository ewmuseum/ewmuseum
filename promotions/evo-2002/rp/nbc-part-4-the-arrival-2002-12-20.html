NBC Part 4: The arrival

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.20.02 Courage {L2J wants to talk about courage…the courage that he has?! He wants to talk about the things that he has been through. Sure he was put through a table and left bloodied and beaten does that mean that he has courage?! No! It simply means that he got his ass beat and that he may have earned a little part of that title that he holds. But he gave up that little part when he got pinned by Shocka twice in two weeks. How can he go along thinking that he has courage? Shocka is the man that has fought his way to the top…I mean he started with the continental title and earned his keep there before being able to move on. He won the road to glory tournament at Rise of the Legends when he had 4 matches in one card…now that’s courage. He will do this on his own…the Universal title will have found its real home…a home worthy of carrying it not a trailer like L2J. Someone stable like Shocka should be carrying around that title…Shocka will make L2J respect him, no matter what he has to do. He will win at any cost and he will not hold back anything just for this match.} {With the exception of thug roC being banned from Ringside by Shocka himself…does that prove to you how much that he wants this title. The man that has brought him here is now going to be confined to the locker room area to watch on a monitor while Shocka fights this match truly 1 on 1. L2J is someone that should be contending for the high impact title not the Universal title…L2J does not realize that he is giving up many things. Height, weight, skill, level of intensity, and the mind for the game.} Championship material {the three things that L2J named as championship material are three things that describe Shocka perfectly. Three things that Shocka lives for, he is willing to fight for the Universal title and he is ready for it. He has beaten L2J and proved that he can pull off a win at any time! He has proven that he is no pushover and is truly the next huge thing in eVolution. Shocka will continue to prove night in and night out what he has been saying all along. That he does indeed rule, and when he pins L2J for the third time in as many weeks this Sunday you can be sure that those will be the first words out of his mouth.} ~Libby Bowman can be seen applying lipstick to her lips of course in front of a mirror getting ready for a big interview. Last time we saw her like this she was getting ready to interview Shocka before he won the road to glory tournament. Perhaps she brings a little good luck to Shocka or maybe it’s just a coincidence? Only time will tell as she is preparing for an interview with him indeed.~ ‘Listen guys, we have to make this good…I mean last time I underestimated him and didn’t think he would make it but he did…he is making a name for himself and I want him to come to me when he is the universal champion for the big interviews.’ Libby speaks in a slightly elevated tone. ‘Oh we’re on?’ She asks confused. ‘We are here at the HHH airport in Minneapolis Minnesota waiting for the arrival of Shocka. His plane comes in about 10 minutes and we are going to get the scope with him as soon as he gets off the plane.’ She continues. ~The scene slowly fades out and back into Shocka walking off the plane dragging his bag behind him murmuring something as he stops when he sees her. She runs up and begins speaking~ ‘Shocka, last time we spoke you said that your Legend would indeed begin to rise after rise of the legends and it has!’ She starts off. ‘You now face L2J at the upcoming PPV nightmare before Christmas for the Universal title. Is there anything you’d like to say before we get to in depth.’ She says. ‘Is there something I’d like to say?!’ Shocka says with a laugh. He drops his bag on the ground and stares at Libby. ‘You know I have been psyching myself up for this match for about a month now…and I am about as ready as I ever will be so I have some advice for L2J. He needs to shut his goddamn mouth and be ready for me. He needs to get ready for something that he has never faced; sure Sovereign and him have had some duels. But you know he has never been up against someone like me one on one.’ Shocka speaks with a harsh tone. ‘Well any comments on what L2J had to say earlier?’ Libby asks. ‘Of course there is…he doesn’t think I am sick enough?’ A sick smirk comes over Shocka’s face and he tilts his head back in laughter. ‘Well you seem like you have changed into something different…’ Libby says. ‘Sure I am the #1 Stunna now, but you need to realize that Jonathan could shine through at anytime.’ Shocka laughs. ‘He needs to become aware of the fact that I am not a man to mess with…that I am not someone that you can over look. I mean that would be one of the biggest mistakes that you could make…and your damn assumption that I am such a chump…well if that was the case then this chump holds two victories over you.’ Shocka says holding up two fingers. ‘You don’t think that I am willing to fight for the title? Then why did I go through the road to glory like it was nothing…and that was the time when Sovereign was the champion. Does that make you realize that I am willing to die for it? I was willing to take on Sovereign in any kind of match as long as I got my hands on that damn title. I am the most innovative man in hardcore matches. But I do realize that this match is far from hardcore, it favors you in the matter that it is a standard match. Shit if you wanted to you could simply walk away from the ring and get counted out like a pussy. And I wouldn’t put that past you, I think that you realize that your days are numbered so you are talking yourself up. You better take all the damn pictures you can with you and that title because once I get a hold of it will be nothing more than a memory for you. You can have a shot back but that doesn’t mean that you will actually succeed in beating me!’ Shocka screams as he looks down at Libby. ‘What are your feelings about L2J saying that chumps or you can’t take the pain?’ Libby asks seeming very interested. ‘Well you know what Libby, if I couldn’t take the pain I wouldn’t be willing to go into this match with a herniated disc in my fucking neck would I!?’ Shocka yells down at her. ‘Well…’ ‘Answer me!’ Shocka screams. ‘No!’ She starts. ‘I guess L2J is just trying to make you feel a little down on yourself.’ ‘Me feel down on myself?! Am I the one who has been pinned twice in two week…by the man that you are fighting none the less?’ Shocka questions in a sort of angry voice. ‘Well, no that’s what L2J did…’ Libby speaks. ‘Ahh,…yes the piece of shit overrated mother fucker. You know what L2J I hope that you bring you’re ‘A’ game to NBC because I want to beat you at your best.. I want you to have no regrets for pulling out the stops at the PPV. Put it all on the line just like I am going to…just like I always do night in night out.’ ‘Well I can’t wait can’t wait to see the match on Sunday night I am looking forward to it very much so. But can you tell us what to expect perhaps?’ Libby asks. ‘I can almost guarantee that you will see at least one #1 Stunna…maybe L2J will hit me with one of his piece of shit finishers like the teardrop or the device. You see he may have hit me with the device on Wednesday but that was because he hit me in the face with the Universal title first. And it was a cheap shot might I add. I mean I have hit the #1 Stunna on him in the ring during the course of a match and proved that it does him in pretty well. It left him laying on the mat for about 10 minutes each time as the show went off the air.’ Shocka speaks slowly calming down. ‘Well we have been hearing rumors of the Shocka roCka perhaps coming into play in this match could you elaborate on this and what it is.’ Libby asks. ‘Well ya know Libby I would love to do that for you…but I can’t. I can tell you this much you may see it, and it affects the lower part of your back. Maybe you will get to see it used for the first time on L2J at the PPV.’ ‘Come on please, just inform me of what it could be!’ Libby screams with anticipation. ‘It affects the lower part of your back and if you don’t shut the fuck up maybe I will apply it to you?’ Shocka asks with a sick look on his face. ‘Has Morpheus Omega been getting into your head?’ Libby asks slowly backing away. ‘Yeah, we have been talking lately… can you tell?!’ Shocka smirks evilly as he walks away and the scene fades.
