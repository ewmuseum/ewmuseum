ICE BOX!

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-30 [Recorded: 7-28-03, 3:00 PM] '...Welcome back to TRL. I am standing here with eVolution wrestling superstar, Scott Frost.' The voice of Carson Daly is heard. The scene fades in and Carson is standing next to Scott Frost, Seara, and Lee eXodus. 'Also with us is Scott Frosts fiancée and his wrestling protegé, Seara and Lee eXodus.' Carson announces. The fans start screaming and the three look around smiling. Lee eXodus raises his microphone to speak to Carson. 'Actually, I don't wrestle anymore.' Lee says before getting interrupted by the 'awes' of the auidence in attendence. eXodus smiles and nods his head. 'I know, I know. But I really feel that I have found my true calling and that is rapping. I started messing around in the studios a few months ago and it kinda just boomed from there on.' eXodus adds. Carson nods his head along with Scott and Seara. 'Yeah, Lee is a good friend to me and Scott and we are proud that he actually wrote a song about Scott and of course talked about me in there. I really like this song, and I hope that the fans do to.' Seara continues. Scott nods his head and smiles before starting to talk. 'You know. Lee got the most attention from the fans and everything when he announced himself as being my number one fan, but really Lee has also been a superstar and now I feel he will really bank out on his new career.' Scott adds to all the comments. 'Right.' Carson agrees. 'Well ladies and gentlemen, the world premiere of the Lee eXodus rap entitled, 'Ice Box'. Now there isn't a video made for this. So we will be showing random clips of Scott Frost's history as a wrestler but I am sure a video will be made soon.' Carson adds. Lee nods his head. 'Yes there will be, Davidson Enterprises which owns the franchise eVolution has agreed to let eVolution superstars come to the set and be apart of the song.' Lee announces to the cheers of fans. 'Well here it is....Lee eXodus....ICE BOX!' Carson announces and the scene fades away.
