What now?!?!

13 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-13 The scene opens as X is just standing around in a gym..He is standing in the middle of what seems to be a self made Cell..One That he has used for many times...X just stands there with no lookk on his face..He was not added on the last Legends as he was very upset with not being on the show..Now that the show has left the states he begins to wonder if this means he can take his wors world wide..Besides just dominateing his home town..He plans on makeing a take over WORLD WIDE now that eVo cant have another show in the USA..As he stands there..A question mark is raised on the camera crew..X seems not his typical self..HE is not full of hate..But maybe this time..Filled with confidence and cockyness..He begins some what talking to hiself..As when he speaks he just begins to pace around the Cell..Its funny though..He is just talking..he is trying to have everything ryhme with everything..as he says with a cocky voice... 'eVo has ended in this damn nation... Its now traveling the world... in all different places..' He then looks down at the bottom of the cell as he patiently walks around it..thinking about his past in it..Thinking about his future and all it has for him..The great matches..All of his chances to be the leader of the cell divison..as he says.. 'I have thought about clocking out.. Because there's been times I have been knockedout.. But now you better watch out.. The cats out the bag.. Machines time is running out.. Like and hour glass..Forget anything he said.. When we enter this cell.. Ill leave you for dead..Knock off your head.. And then it will be said..Whos the star of them all.. Fuck the mirror..hanging on the wall.. Ill answer the question myself.. The one when he speaks.. His words are felt..' He then stops walking as he stops talking..He begins to just stand there still looking at the bottom of the cell.He jerks his head back as he has a slight smile on his face..He begins to chuckle as he begins to move to the edge of Cell..The cameras get closer as the only thing thats seperate the face of X from the cameras is the fence of the cell..He looks at it with a cockyness..as he says.. 'Tick tock tick tock... the time begins to pass.. I promise when we come back to this place.. everyone will know my ass.. Everyone needs to wise up.. because im about to throw something.. That no man could pass up.. Its the offer to the man in charage of the cell.. kinda like its the start of a match.. so ring the bell..I got something to TELL.. Beause I wanna see if Cameron can spell this.. The word Champion..let me say it again.. Champion..quick man.. get a pen..Im a guarentee win..' He then jsut glares at the eVo cameras while they fade to black as he says.. 'Led it be known..because it was said..this has been X..Machine I will leave you covered in red...'
