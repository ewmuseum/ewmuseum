Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-19 The scene opens in a another cell.once again this Cell is self made..The type of fence you find around houses..Inside X is standing with a smile on his face..He looks around as he says.. 'The name game will never change.. Coming out here to make sure things remain the same.. Me on top of this game.. The only label worth anything... sence some hot bitch named Sabel.. These cats in eVo will never be on my level, IT seems the wrestlers will always have a score to settle.. They now know the best no longer stays in a sqaure... best means more not less.. Lucky for yall I got a few more things to get off my chest..' A pause as he keeps speaking... 'Some things may bother me...in this place.. Like we let a man named bob in.. does he even what a exits sign says.. I mean the dude is local.. so wierd like that kid named urkel.. Calls himself a hardcore legend... To bad the man still is under the title of Wrestling..' 'Lets keep it real though.. the HEDGES need to go.. might sound a little strange.. but you know what I mean.. More time on TV for people like me... the real muth Fucker in this whole damn thing.. I dont walk out here and try to sing.. I just make it real.. every time I speak you can feel what I say.. even if people leave we always know X is here to stay..' 'Machine knows the best.. I do what I say... Just on legends... I made him a new face.. he is nothing but a disgrace.. brings the cell down... makes me wanna frown.. I seen people like him before.. they call em clowns.. He go put down..Just like the rest.. cuz I do it the best..' 'I have to say..The cell got some freaks.. That Kid Damon..talks about a disease.. But I checked for ya.. doctor said you just got flees... I aint with ya like that chic named Terry.. wait he is guy?.. reminds me of a pink fairy.. his mom should of named him Larry... they think things are about to get scary.. trying to make the situation a little harry.. But dont worry...I want ruin your plans.. Ill be on top.. While u two just stand with your dick in your hands..' The camera guy tells him to hold down with the language.. 'You better step back.. I be damned if im ganna let a camera guy tell me were I need to be at... I say what I wish.. new flash man.. X dont give a shit.. remember this.. write it down... X is the new man in charge of this town..' He stops as he smiles to the cameras as the image freezes and the scene fades to black..
