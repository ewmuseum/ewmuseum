Eden's Gate :: Prologue :: II

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
London laid there for twelve hours. Twelve hours he laid on the ground, in a pool of blood. Motionless, not knowing what's going on. Half the time he wasn't even conscience, which probably was for the best. There was no chase of anything when London's land lord found him. Besides the torn up apartment and the body of London, nothing. By instinct Al, the land lord, went inside to London's apartment trying not to move anything or touch anything and used the phone. Which was back on, coincidence? Perhaps. '9-1-1, what's your emergency?' The operated said, in the calm tone they have been taught, 'There's been a shooting! One of my tenets, he's just laying there..' Al was frantic, he didn't know what to do. In all his years of owning public housing, he's never had this kind of problem. 'Sir, calm down, is the person still breathing?' Her tone, still calm, but concerned, 'I don't know, I just saw him laying there and came in and called the you! Get someone over here!' Almost yelling at the lady Al hung up the phone.. Good thing 9-1-1 has the address as soon as they call. Al went back to the body, he didn't touch London, who's eyes were open but he didn't seem to be responsive. The shots were in his upper torso, dead center, probably piercing the heart. 'Lance..LANCE!' Al tried calling his name, but it didn't come to any avail. Nothing, just him laying there, the blood stopped gushing hours ago, and was mostly dried and crusted over now. The floor was almost completely covered, the rug stained.. Meanwhile, the police only knowing the building number, they had to find the exact location. The building wasn't all that small, but it was hard to miss a floor. London being on the fifth, they had a bit of a run. Nothing they weren't trained for. Al heard them running up the steps, 'Fifth floor! Fifth floor!' Faintly the paramedics heard the cries and stopped checking each hall until they goto the fifth. London was at the end of the hall, and they raced towards where they saw feet sticking out of the doorway. 'Do you have any idea how long he's been here?' One of the medics asked quickly, Al just shook his head. They ripped off London's shirt and saw the three bullet holes. 'Oh man... What's his name?' The medic shook his head, 'Lance Rockwell.' The medic made a motion for Al to step back, they had to move London back to get him on the stretcher. London's chest was covered in blood and the holes looked dried up. It wasn't a very nice site. The bulletholes where larger than the bullet would have been because of the point black shots, they folded back the skin revealing deep, wide holes. 'Is he going to be alright?' Al asked, as the medics jogged down the hall with London on the stretcher.. 'You'll be the first to know..' One of them answered, probably thinking that Al was family. The ride to the hospital wasn't that long, London needed the medical attention pretty bad. Being left there for twelve or so hours with no attention, in his own blood. He needed to be treated or god only knew what could have happened. The medics that picked him up, got him to come to a bit more and tell them what happened.. There wasn't much that they could do for him in the ambulance, therefore they had to wait to get to the hospital and into the E.R. Rushed right into the E.R. The docters did everything they could. He was in Critical Condition, but they had the bullets out of him, and stitched up. His heart was hit twice, and the third bullet lodged in his lung. There was nothing else that they could do for him, except keep him in a critical yet stable condition. No one came to visit London, no family, nobody. They had nothing on him except his first and last name. No ID, no social security number, no past records of being in the hospital. There wasn't much else they could do for someone that apparently didn't exist to the records.
