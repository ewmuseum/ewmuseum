Homework... part three.

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens inside of the car with Morpheus driving with sudden road rage... Morpheus grinds his teeth back and forth, like he was high on cocaine and his left eye still twitching rampantly. The eyes of Morpheus are glued to the road as the car quickly rises in speed. Morpheus abruptly turns his head to Sky and goes to say something, but instead shakes his head like he felt sorry for her. 'What was that look for?' Sky questions with a concerned look on her face, she continues 'We accomplished our mission and by you killing that man, would not of benefited us in any way, shape or form!' Sky speaks like she is trying to talk sense into him. 'You don't fuck with a man's money, you don't fuck with his home, you sure the fuck don't fuck his wife and last and most importantly... You NEVER fuck with his car!' Morpheus screams in a fit of rage. 'You knocked his face in, the man was bleeding like a drunken whore being fucked on her period' Sky replies and continues... 'Why do you feel the need to kill people and not only torture them?' 'LOOK!' Morpheus screams... 'Its the whole idea of someone thinking why not buy my own ride when I can steal someone else's. You think he will stop at only trying to steal mine. There will be others and I could of prevented that from happening... Hear what Im saying?' Morpheus asks and sounds fully confident his thinking pattern makes sense. 'You are definitely spun on some kind of loop!' Sky night says nodding and smiles. 'Sometimes I really start wondering where these insane ideas of yours come from. Do you have a few loose screws in your head? Sky questions sarcastically and directs her attention to a file folder that reads 'Jason Relms'. Sky flips through and smiles approvingly. 'Sky... Did you find all the necessary files tonight?' Morpheus changes the subject by asking Sky about their secret mission. 'Operation Network completed?' he asks. 'Yeah and more... Team Network is covered and I also got the file of that whore HBC... I have grown this strong hatred for that trashy slut, she walks around eVolution like she is the hottest piece of ass in the locker room. When really, she is just the one who gives it up for entire roster and more... Heart Break Chick probably thinks she can spread her legs all the way to the top and win the Universal title!' Sky says and growls loudly thinking about it. 'You really don't like her, what has she ever done wrong to you?' Morpheus questions like he is trying to provoke the situation and push Sky far over the edge... 'Don't even start because I remember before I was in the eVolution picture, you and her kissed while we were dating... So press the envelope some more because I will beat you!' Sky dares him. 'I already told you last week, forget her for now because the only thing that matters is that we walk out of this three match series with those unified tag titles around our waists. You almost dropped the ball last Legends, don't drop it again!' Morpheus retorts. 'This time, no worries because I will pick apart everyone of their weaknesses with these files right here. See, HBC is only my pet project that I can always pursue later' Sky Night says intrigued. 'Remember, I can handle taking most of the heat inside the ring and can put Team Network under the table myself though I need the tactful and strategic thinking from you every time the opportunity rises. They compare nowhere close to the deadliest ever known!' Morpheus comments. 'Wait a minute...' Morpheus continues and has an interested look on his face. 'I never kissed HBC... Where the hell did you get that idea?' he asks and starts laughing. 'Just because it was never seen on camera, doesn't mean I don't know about it!' Sky says and has an angered look on her face and bats her eye lashes at him. 'Actually no, I never kissed her!' Morpheus states. 'and trust me, if I had kissed her I would be honest and at least tell you. You tell me that Im crazy? You are one manipulated bitch!' Morpheus points out the obvious with a wide grin on his face; like he had overcome and defeated Sky in her own mind game. Sky Night growls once again and speaks 'This legends, Team Network is only two stepping stones away from defeat because we will prove to be the strongest and deadliest tag team ever seen. You may care all about your money, home, wives and cars... But you never fuck with a woman's gold!' Sky says with a greedy, dirty look on her face as her eyes light up like the fourth of July. The scene fades.
