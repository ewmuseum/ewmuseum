Welcome to the greatest show on Earth!

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-28-03 Los Angeles, CA Evening Time Staples Center It has only been a few days since Evolution’s Pay Per View and the world is still talking about the numerous twists, turns, and transgressions that occurred. From the possible arrival of someone calling himself Kimson, to the shocking reformation of The Syndicate team of Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick. Everyone stood their, mouths open, in total disbelief as the greatest tag team in wrestling history, stood side by side, and technically won the tag team championships, and was screwed of the tag team championships on a technically due to S., bogus bullshit decision. None the less, The Syndicate is back in full force and will be leading the charge into summertime, burning all those in its wake. But for now, the current reigning Continental Champion, BDD, and the current reigning Tag Team Champion, HBC are enjoying some relaxing moments at a Lakers basketball game. Though the Lakers literally suck the big fatty this year, the Hollywood crowd and Los Angelenos are ecstatic to see them down courtside and erupt when their picture is shown on the jumbotron. Even Kobe and Shaq seem happy they are their to help disrupt their losing monotony. Around the fourth quarter the dynamic duo get up to leave and head to the cavernous underground of the Staples Center where the Evolution staff has already secured a small wing of the place. They go and check out their spacious locker room suite, satisfied, they toss their bags from the rental car into the lockers and begin a quick change because the entire world, wants to know, what is on their mind, especially after Salt Lake City. BDD puts on his usual black and red outfit while HBC sports a new more seductive and just as skimpy black/red outfit as well. After finally hiking up her skin-tight micro-mini skirt they high-five each other, grab their respective titles and head off to the small interview area where Jennifer Gardner is standing by. After shaking hands and doing the usual pleasantries, they pull up leather chairs and begin. JG: Welcome Evolution fans, Jennifer Gardner presenting another issue of Evolution Live, and today, I am joined by one of the most controversial teams in Evolution and perhaps the greatest team in wrestling history, Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick, collectively known as The Syndicate. Welcome to the show guys. Syn: Our pleasure to be here Jennifer. JG: So many questions we want to ask the both of you. Together you shocked the world numerous times these past few months, ranging from the so-called death of Miss Extreme: Katarina to HBC’s superkick of you and joining the Unlimited to the sudden reformation out of nowhere this past Sunday. Can you tell us what was fact, and what was fiction? BDD: Oh the tangled webs we weave Jennifer. I’m not sure we can really discuss all of those because certain things transcend from one to the other to events that haven’t happened yet. But I will say one thing though, Katarina was a surprise to the both of us and we really did go through a grieving process. Some of the animosity you saw between HBC and myself was real because we grieve in different ways. However at NBC, we were just as confused, surprised as everyone else in the arena that night. Jennifer: And as a result of that shocking event, you superkicked your long time partner? HBC: Boy you don’t waste anytime do you? As much as I love and respect my partner over here, I did want to strike out on my own for awhile even if nothing more just to test the waters perhaps. Of course BDD was shocked when it occurred because I didn’t tell him it was going to happen. It was an idea that we’ve bounced around for sometime since we joined eVo. I surveyed the scene, knowing that I’d be a hot commodity and did it. Of course after that night, we privately sat down and talked about how we wanted to proceed with it. It was then we came up with this two-fold plan. One, to bolster my single status and international status as a superstar, because I was tired of people thinking that I needed BDD to help my career, which isn’t the case at all. Sure, he trained me, but everything I’ve gotten, I’ve done on my own. BDD: That’s the truth, pound for pound, male or female, HBC is the greatest all-around athlete and wrestler on the planet, and probably will go down being even better than I was which is saying a lot. HBC: As everyone knows, it was during this time that my Playboy pictorial came out, my winning of the High-Impact Championship and my legendary best of three series against BDD. The Playboy issue I’m very proud of because now people know why I am called ‘The Heartbreaker’ and that it was the top selling Playboy of all-time. Well perhaps at least until the Torrie Wilson pictorial comes out sometime later this year. It’ll be close, but none the less I’m very proud of it. I’m also pleased that the dirtsheet writers finally got off my back. Sometimes I feel like there is no pleasing those people, but you know what Jennifer, truth be told, while they were a part of it, it was the fans though. I wanted to show the fans, that a female, not some steroid freakzilla like Chyna, but a true female could compete with the guys on top level. Sure Trish Stratus has had a few mix matches, Jackie occasionally competes with the cruiserweights and Victoria looks like she could run with the guys but probably won’t. There are a few independents trying to emulate me, but can’t. I am without question, THE dominate female, and I stress female on the planet today. I’m not 6-2, 200 pounds like Chyna. I don’t bench press 400 pounds like she did, but I don’t have too either. She was too one dimensional. Also I would add, people went easy on her because she was boning HHH. In my case, I had to prove myself from day one. BDD didn’t go easy on me, he trained me like he trained all his students and I respect him for that. Hell, he’s the one who I have the most respect and love for. But no, I’m, 5-11, 140 pounds. I’m very toned and fit. I have a model-type body but can toss around these heavyweights with ease. Why? Because I learned properly. People were shocked back in FWA when I belly-to-bellied 500 pounds of Bubba McDowell. Damn, near killed me too, but its something I can do with ease and naturally. Would I want to do that all the time…no. I rely on my quickness, my martial arts training, and I can throw and mat wrestle with the best of em. JG: Now HBC you joined the Unlimited and you BDD have had a long history against the Unlimited, did you two devise some kind of conspiracy plot to destroy them both from within and from the outside? BDD: Us? You must be joking Jennifer. Laughs It was part of the plan from the get go. That night when she superkicked me, we talked for hours coming up with a plan to divide and conquer them, and you know what, its been working. You look at The Unlimited history during their tenure within Evo. People have come in and left, disorienting them. They were all excited when HBC joined their ranks, thinking they had the final piece of the puzzle only to find out, she was a mole. A back stabbing bitch as Xander recently called her as of late. HBC: Oh, Xander…I’m crushed, after all I’ve done for you. Sniff, sniff. HA!! HA!! HA!! BDD: Exactly. Awhile I was systematically destroying each and every Unlimited member, from Asis, Jinx, Xander, to the rest of the lot, HBC was working in the shadows, and though they didn’t know it at the time, HBC has completed depleted the entire Unlimited bank accounts. Meaning that they are physically, emotionally, AND financially BROKE!! HBC: I’m such a sneaky bitch! Hehehehehe!!! BDD: In a nutshell, The Syndicate has owned The Unlimited time and time again. None of them have ever defeated us, are the pissed, especially after our little revelation, probably, but hey, too bad, they have bigger problems than us. The whole ONE thing. Whuppy do. I’ll knock his bitch ass out as well. HBC: Likewise. Don’t think I’ve forgotten about the One either from back in EDW when bitchboy espoused he wanted an heir from me. Please, that’s never gonna happen except in some deluded wet dream fantasy! You get near me with some schlong, and I’m taking it to the chop shop like an ex-partner of mine. You all know the story, yes it really happened and no its not an urban myth or wrestling folklore. JG: Getting to this week, you both are in a unique situation. At the PPV, HBC got the victory for The Syndicate team while Luther Wisdom garnered the win for his team. With S. playing his usual tricks, you both are split up once again and both having a partner neither of you like or want. What are your thoughts on this. BDD: Good question. Well Jennifer, its weird because HBC holds the Tag Team Championship, and though I’m very pleased for her we should be holding it together because of what happen. Because of S., that isn’t the case and now I’m forced to team with someone who I have a lot of, how do I put this delicately, a difficult history with in Malcom King. Its true, King doesn’t get along with anyone in this business. Its fact. Its also fact that King is perhaps one of the best wrestlers this industry has ever seen. Its also true, he’s still probably pissed off at me, again no big shocker because of his mentality, that I defeated him for the EDW Universal Championship. I knocked him off his high horse. I’m undefeated against him. It can be a hard pill to swallow. Do I trust him? HELL NO!! However, King is also not a stupid individual either. If he wants the title, and more importantly if he wants to get his hands on Xander and S., he knows deep down, he’s going to have to work with me to defeat the Unlimited team and Strictly Prohibited/Yoshii Tanaka. Let me put it this way, I’m down on his hit-list for now, he needs me because he has bigger fish to fry. I can deliver that to him. He knows it. Of course, difficulties may occur, but, as I said, King is no dummy. You don’t get to where he was by being stupid. You pick your shots, and go from their. That’s exactly what’s happening. He knows fully well that S. would be pissed all to hell to see King hold anykind of title. That is why we were going to work this week, putting aside all differences, because we both want to rip S. a new one. JG: And HBC, you are in a most difficult position this week for on Legends, you are booked in a handicapped match against two people who you don’t know and probably never seen before. Your thoughts? HBC: Well…..your right. Xander and the rest of the Unlimited couldn’t believe they got hoodwinked by a girl. Newsflash people, it probably wasn’t and won’t be the first time. It will happen again and again. Why because beautiful people like myself can get away with it. From getting out of speeding ticket to getting free stuff by licking my lips, its just the way it is. But in regards to this match. Sure, I’d be lying if I said I wasn’t concerned about it. I am. Its hard to get ready for someone who you may or may not know, and being that it’s a handicap match, a two-on-one kinda match, well shit Jennifer, I love those (with a sly little wink to the camera). Its kinda like a kinky threesome, except in this case, I won’t be sexually satisfied, just physically satisfied as I get to Superkick to unlucky pieces of ghetto trash trying to beat down me, the HBC. But unfortunately for whoever it ends up to be, they will know why I am known throughout the world, as ‘The Heartbreaker’, breaking hearts and careers, worldwide. So bring them on, so I can do my thing, and then go home to have a REAL threesome! Would you like to join me Jennifer? Jennifer, obviously blushing and contemplating it looks to the camera and announces that we are out of time. The camera stops as Miss Gardner gives HBC her personal number and smiles as The Syndicate team walks away
