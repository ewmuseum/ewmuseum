Reaper of sorrow.

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.1.03 ~eVolution has never reached higher times than right now. Shocka is the Universal champion and he is going to prove to be the greatest champion. He will show that he is willing to be the fight ingest champion of them all. He will defend his title the show after and then whenever he feels necessary for him to defend it. He proved that he is a force to be reckoned with, without the help that thug roC has provided over the last few months. But he still does not stand alone in this world…he still needs some people to watch his back and make sure that no one gets any kind of advantage on him. Now we catch up to him on his way to Kansas City Missouri for Legends XX.~ ‘You know what?’ Shocka can be heard talking into a cell phone. ‘You tell her that I don’t have time to deal with her shit and this god damned divorce.’ He pauses for a response. ‘I have more important things to focus on now…I’m the fucking Universal Champion…you make something happen Patrick or you will be unemployed.’ Shocka says before he throws his phone onto the passenger seat of his car. ~He travels down a lonesome highway in yet another new car, this time it’s a red BMW Roadster with yellow flames down the side. He studies the road as he prepares to make a pass on a slow moving farm truck. He flies around it and scoots back into his lane still in deep thought. He picks up a tape recorder and begins speaking into it.~ ‘You know…I have thought over every move that I made in the match against L2J…and I can’t find a flaw throughout the whole thing. One of the only mistakes I made was allowing thug roC to come to the ring with me. He almost cost me the damn match when he bashed that steel chair over my head.’ He shakes his head a minute and pauses. ‘I have been asking myself if what I did was truly the right thing…I have been doubting that it truly is. I mean ending the careers of two men in one night with one move? I was out of control…I was not able to handle my emotions…it took control of me. I let it though I didn’t take any steps to stop it from happening.’ He speaks with what seems to be deep sorrow in his voice. ‘Now that I think about it more and more…I don’t regret shit!’ He screams into the recorder. ‘Isn’t that what life is about? NO regrets?! I look back at the path I took to get to this title and I remember that I did what I had to do. Sure it took some underhanded measures to get what I wanted. But when it comes down to it…I won the Universal title fair and square…with the help of no one. And that’s how I will live defending it night in and night out against the greatest competitors that eVolution has to offer. Whether it be the likes of Scott Frost or even Sovereign. I will show this entire company that I have the will power and the desire to fight for this belt win or lose.’ Shocka says now going back into the same brooding fashion he was in just moments ago. ~He looks over the road and then to his right and to his left. He now again picks up the recorder and speaks still studying the road.~ ‘I have respect for Scott Frost; he has proved that he is a contender for my belt. He proved it at Rise of the Legends when he came within an eyelash of beating me. But you know what he was the first one to make that big mistake the other could capitalize on. I don’t care what excuse he comes up with the fact of the matter is I beat him in that ring fair and square. He can say that he will not falter for that one second in the ring this time but he is bound to as he is to wrapped up with Seara. When he thinks of me I want him to think of the time I kept Seara captive! I want him to think of the man that destroyed him 1…2…3 in the middle of the ring at ROTL. And when he thinks of these two things I want the anger he has for me to consume him! I want him to have ultimate hate for me…just like I do him. He has something I want…but I will do anything it takes to get her from him.’ He throws his head back in laughter as he smiles sinisterly. ~He pulls over to the side of the road in the parking lot of gas station he walks in and leaves the tape recorder in the car playing back everything that he has just said as the scene slowly fades.~
