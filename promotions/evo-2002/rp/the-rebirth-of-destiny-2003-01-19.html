The rebirth of destiny

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1=18=03 Skopje, Macedonia The Highplains Daytime It is a bright day, the unrelenting heat shining down on the man they call Big Daddy Dreamer. A bit more ruthless, more fitter than ever before, even more ripped. But unlike Freakzilla, it is all natural. He is alone standing on the long forgotten plains of Macedonia, home to one of the greatest rulers, and conquers of all-time. The man they call Alexander. So as BDD surveys the scene unfolding before him, he thinks ahead to this week and his opponent. A man whom he has faced before. A man who coincidentally is coming off a potential career ending injury in Asis. A formidable foe? Or formidable joke? One can never tell with the company he keeps. A ruthless band of backstabbing thugs. However, Wednesday night, it will be different. Something will actually be on the line when Big Daddy Dreamer once again rips The Unlimited a new a-hole. The Continental Championship. A prize worthy, a prize on the rode to greatness again. A prize, BDD must have…… “635 B.C. An Empire was shaped by one man with an unrelenting vision to do the impossible. Conquer the world before he was 30 years of age. A highly unlikely feat given the consequential feudalism, warlords, factions, but none the less, he forged his empire from flesh and iron, tossing aside this faction, that faction, seizing this city with words of his coming, local citizens awestruck and fearful at the same time. Those who opposed him were ruthlessly destroyed. That man, Alexander of Macedonia is a direct descendant of mine. And just like my forebearers, my family history has been devoted to greatness. I descend from royalty, rulers, emperors, conquers, even a few notorious villains that all made their mark in the history books. To some extent, I have lived up to my family history. I’ve conquered the business world, buying, seizing, acquiring lesser unworthy companys. I’ve won three dozen titles in my career, and though I should be satisfied, I am not. One last prize remains. Controlling Evolution. For a time being, I indirectly controlled its predecessor, EDW through Nicole Kelly. But here, no, things are just alittle different. Sure certain talent remains, but none the less, they are de-evolving instead of evolving to the next level.” “People ask me, am I going to compete in FedWars again? Noble question, but the answer is no. There is no reason for me too. No competition. I’ve already dominated it in the past, now would be no different. In fact, the day I swept them all aside, I did nearly cry a single tear because for a time, I thought that was the pinnacle of my career. Alas, it is not. As the other day, I was watching some old matches of mine, brutal affairs, sometimes I wonder what the hell I was thinking doing some of those retarded high flying moves thirty feet up. None the less though, it furthered my career. However, as I was reminiscing of the past, and present for that matter. I thought about the direction I should go in. End my career with the finality with Miss Heartbreaker? Please. I’m only 28, I have at least 10 more good years in me. No, as I sat back, I noticed something, and frankly it kind of disturbed me. I’ve been in Evolution since the beginning, and low and behold, I haven’t held a title here yet!” “Disgraceful! That mark is unworthy of me. A wrong to be righted persay. So Angel stepped up to the plate, another Unlimited flunky with a big mouth to compensate for his sheer lack of talent. Poof! Down he goes faster than a roofie! And now, its time. The time I’ve been waiting for. At last, a title shot. And surprise, surprise, it once again comes at the hand of an Unlimited opponent. When will you guys learn, you simply cannot defeat the D-Man! Well one can, but she doesn’t count since she has more talent than most of this fed combined. But none the less, Asis is standing in my way of succeeding something I should have done a long time ago. One time HBC and I used to bet who would win a title within 4 weeks. She won that one, but this isn’t about her. Its about me, and my destiny. I don’t give a damn what the locker room says, the critics, the fans, even management, they all know deep down, I’m one of THE top guys in the business. One of the best consistent all-around performers. One of the best technically sound who can move like a bug weight when I chose.” “And I chose……….NOW……..Asis. Opponent, Unlimited crony, competitor, enemy. Frankly, it doesn’t mean anything to me anymore. Just victory. Just gold. A title around my waist to make my ancestors happy once again, knowing that I’ve lived up to my destiny. At your expense? So what! Think I care? No. Much like Alexander who didn’t care except winning at all cost. So shall this. So shall it be……from sands of the ashes…. The phoenix is reborn!
