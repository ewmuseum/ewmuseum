The hit (Part 3)

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/20/02 {The scene again picks up on a dim and sort of scary New York City type street. A car rolls into view its headlights turned off not allowing any further illumination rather than the sparce street lights. Out of the car steps a man we have become familar with lately. His name is Jonny as his uncle the red dragon called him. He wears the same gray suit and moves toward a door. He opens and and walks into a room. A quick scene change brings us inside of a house. The man known as the red dragon he sits calmly on a couch looking forward when he sees his nephew he greets him with a brief smile. The smile soon turns to a scowl as he looks over the demeanor of his nephew.} 'What now?' Uncle Tone asks with a harsh and uncaring voice. 'Well...I can't get you the money..' Jonny speaks with a sad tone to his voice. 'Whata mean?! I need this job and you know that...why can't you get the damn money?!' {A ringing cell phone interrupts the conversation and it draws uncle tony to a different room. He begins speaking but not loud enough for Jonny to hear. Jonny sits on the couch and reaches into his coat pocket and pull out a 9 MM gun. He looks it over before carefully placing it back into his pocket.} 'You know what?' The uncle says breaking the silence. 'What tony?' Jonny asks in a rude manner. 'I don't think that you belong in this family...I mean what have you done for us?' Uncle Tony speaks with a growing anger in his voice. 'What have I done for you?!' Jonny seems to think for a moment before again speaking. 'Gee ya know what Tone i don't think that I have done that much...ever since you and ma talked me out of becoming what I wanted to be I hadn't really thought of what i could do for you...' 'You know what you can do for me?!' Uncle Tony pauses and pulls a gun from his coat he raises it at Jonny and smiles. 'You can take a piece of lead straight through the heart.'
