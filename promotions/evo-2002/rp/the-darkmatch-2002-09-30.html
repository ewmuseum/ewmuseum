The DarkMatch

30 September 2002

Date: 9/30/02 March 30, 1989 Xavier Citizen is nailed with a waistlock suplex from Demetrius Echo. DE grabs XC head and arm and delivers a head and arm suplex. XC tackles and begins punching DE. XC clotheslines DE.DE with a takeover suplex on XC.DE puts XC in an arm grapevine submission. Citizen taps Citizen taps!!!!! Off in the distance William Louise both Demetrius Echo (Malcolm King) and Xavier Citizen mentor stands watching his pupils closely. He is joined by a figure who identity is in the shadows. man:'I need a tag team to face Jim and Bret tonight at the houseshow...some local talent.' WL:'These two Demetrius Echo and Xavier Citizen are my best students thus far, do you think they are ready Mac?' man:'The one Echo....looks to be ready, Citizen though is undisciplined. They work well together however maybe tag teaming them would be a way to cover up the recklessness of Citizen...while allowing Echo the chance to hone his craft.' WL:'I see your point Mac....I'll let them know about tonights show.' man:'Ohhhh yeah...and give them some new names while you are at it...both names sound too Indy for tonights TV taping. Granted they are going to be caught in a squash between The Foundation and Demolition....but I still have a product to sell.' WL:'Don't worry Mac....they will be ready.' SEPT. 30, 2002 'Everywhere I go....the spector of Malcolm King still lingers. The Unlimited in it's newest incarnation of misfits are running roughshod over this place. Apparently they forgot I too was a member of the organization before...the illness. Maybe I should offer a reminder; but then again that was Malcolm's fight, not mine.' 'No Tyler Davidson has relegated me in a dark match situation. I have to earn my spot on the roster against Escobar, in what should be a relatively easy match. Then what? I have to earn what others here take for granted....starting next week as I open the show with no music, pyro and video. Hell how am I supposed to be recognized by the fans if they don't know when I'm coming? But that is for the future...first things first and that is defeating Escobar in the middle of the ring because this match....much like every other day is a situation that my very life depends upon.' 'Then next week I take my own career in my hand and start to make a name for myself. Starting first with my name. Changing that name given to me by Tyler Davidson (OOC:Dan altering my name to pay tribute to my deceased Grandmother who started me on this whole wrestling thing. Will forward you the revised info of changes if/when I win the match where this is a stipulation) That too me represent a slave name.....a slave to Davidson; as well as a slave to the legacy of Malcolm King. This Wed is the most important day of my as of now limited life. Not only do I begin a quest to establish my legacy, to claim the Evo Universal title, and to take revenge upon those who have committed grave injustices towards me. I also see this as my one chance....to put Malcolm King to rest once and for all. Everyone here....will soon forget him, he was weak while I.....I am destined for greatness or will die trying.' AUG 10 2004 Bianca Dare is at the ULWN show as she is taking a look at the talent for Evolution. She kinda feels torn because of her relationship with Malcolm King her close personal friend and her business relationship with Tyler Davidson. Malcolm is about to crown the First ULWN Champion and is giving a speech about how this night is dedicated to Xavier Citizen, friend and tag team partner. Out of the corner of her eye she sees The wheelchair bound William Louise Jr. and shakes her head.... BD:'Tyler...Tyler...Tyler, how can Malcolm ever forgive you for that one on top of what happend to X?' she says to herself. She turns away for a minute and notices a woman leaving the event. She's a bit elderly. Bianca runs after her... BD:'Excuse me miss I think I know you....' woman:'sorry dear...I can't say we have met.' BD:'Ohhhh you are probably right you look a lot like my friend Xavier Citizen...I thought....' woman:'...I forgot he believed that to be his name, you are right I was his mother.' Bianca shocked could only muster up the words..... BD:'Mother?!?!?!? I thought you were dead????' woman:'....I think he honestly believed that too. Excuse me I've said too much already.' Before Bianca could react the woman gets into her cab and speeds off. Bianca still in a little shock thinks to herself.... 'What does she mean he believed that to be his name?'
