Engagment of the Past

10 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct. 10, 2002 'When I was a kid. The domain that was raised in, was the streets of Detroit. The eight mile was the only road seperating myself, from my friends. The eight mile is a road between the white and black communities. I lived on the eight mile. My poor, white trash trailor home was inbetween the suttle and decrepied buildings once used in the early years before the great depression. Now the buildings store people: people who have no lives and live life on the street, gang members, prostitutes, drug addicts; my whole life was in those buildings. I will never forget the day that my destiny was shown to me. I was destined to become someone, someone that everyone knew and respected in the long run. Someone with goals, no, with rules to live by. Some say I found a new religion, others say that I found something evil. But to me, I found my life...' says the voice of Scott Frost. The scene opens up in the very spot that Scott Frost described. White people, Black people, and even the occassional Hispanic would walk in, out or by the two huge buildings that stood beside a double-wide trailor. A little box is sitting infront of the trailor playing with a basketball. As the camera pans in and you see the clearer and on the name of the trailer says 'Frost Family'. The little boy stands up, and tries dribbling the basketball between his legs but because of his size he has to lift his left leg up over the ball as he bounces it. Then the little boy turns around and looks at the trailor door. 'Scottie!!!' yells the boy. Quickly a boy that seems to be about fifteen years old and resembling that of Scott Frost. Walks out of the door, and he walks over to the little boy and picks him up and the boy rests in one arm as the other grips to keep his hold so the little boy don't fall. 'Scottie! Scottie!' the boy yells. 'I can bounce the ball between my legs now!' exclaims the boy. 'Really?' Frost questions. 'Uh huh, watch!' the little boy tells Frost as Frost sets the little boy down on ground. The little boy picks up the basketball, and looks at Frost with a confident smile. He looks back down at his feet and then smiles. Frost smiles back, and then the boy lifts the ball up to about his chest and he bounces the ball through his leg after lifting it like he did the first time. 'See!! I didn't all by myself!' the little boy sayins joyfully. 'Yep. Hey, why don't I take you home to your mommy? It's starting to get dark.' Frost says with a little worry in his voice. The little boy looks up at Frost and smiles. 'Mommy is here.' says the boy. 'Wha...?' Frost says in a state of confusion. 'Mommy is uummm.....' The little boy looks at both buildings and then points to the one on the left of the Frost Family trailor. 'She's in that one.' the boy again replies with joy. Frost looks at the boy, and then at the building. Frost knowing that, that building is the building that men and women go to, to sell their bodies for money and or drugs. 'Okay, how bout I take you to your daddies. I need to pick up my check from there anyway. I will tell your mommy where I left you.' Frost says. 'Can't.' The boy quickly responds. Frost again looks at the boy with confusion. 'Why...?' Frost asks. 'Daddy was shot a couple days ago. Mommy has been in that building for two days. I have been sneaking into your room and sleeping under your bed every night...' The boy says, not to thrilled now. As his head looks down at the ground with shame. 'Look.' Frost says. 'Your sleeping on my bed tonight. I will sleep on the couch. So go lay down, I will be back in a little bit.' Frost says to the little boy. 'Okay. Talk to you later.' The says, with joy in his voice again. The boy walks into the trailor to be greeted by a very skinny looking woman and a very plump man. They seem to be happy with the little boys entrance and they shut the door behind the little boy. Frost, looks at the building once more and then at the entrance. And he decides to walk in. As Frost slowly makes his way up a stair-well. He is meet by a teenage girl who looks to have been beaten and to be about his age. 'FROST!' The young woman exclaims. 'You gotta help me! My dad beat me up. I have no where else to go. I have been here for a few hours. But the sex noises and gun shots are driving me even more insane!' The young woman again exclaims. 'Kelsey? Listen you gotta get out of here. Tell me where the gun fires happened! And go to my place, Bobby is staying over there. Tell my parents your staying too.' Frost says quickly. Frost takes off up the stairs and is stopped by Kelsey. 'The gun fire happened on the third floor.' says Kelsey before she takes off out of the building. The scene slowly fades away as the young Scott Frost takes off up the stairs. 'That night. I found Bobbys' mom. She was shot three times in the head. He lost his mother and father all in less than a week. So, social services were called and me and my family raised him as my brother. Robert Joshua Frost is his name now. He used to be a Berkly. But he doesn't remember his real parents, he knows who they were. But he can't remember what they looked like. To me, that is rule one of being ruthless. Forget who created you, and move on. On the wall, where I found Roberts mom. There were words written on the wall that have become all to well known for me. 'You gotta be ruthless to be recognized.' First I find these words on a cement inclince under the jackson bridge where a dead man rests his final rest. Now, I see those words were a woman rests her final rests. Only this time it's different. This time. My heart is cold. I feel something take over me, my body goes numb. And then, I wake up. My life is back to normal. I lay beside my beautiful wife Kelsey. I still get random phone calls from my adopted brother Robert. The only thing that has came back from my past is, I forgot about the one that created me.....'The Unlimited', and I have moved on.' says the fading voice of Scott Frost.
