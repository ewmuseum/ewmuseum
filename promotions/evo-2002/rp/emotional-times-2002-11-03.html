Emotional Times

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd November 2002 Since Faith, female wrestler Seara has been seen twice, once in the same hospital as Frost as she past his room to leave, and once when she made and appearance to talk about what seemed to her to be an ending. At legends this past week, although planning with friend Scott Frost to make an appearance to talk to the public about a new situation, the female never put in an appearance at the show, and stayed clear of the state, heading back home to Chicago to attend the funeral of her older brother. She never told Frost, and he never made it to her dressing room to talk to the female. But since then she has contacted him to talk about his approach to Jason Jameson who he has asked to join The Notorious. Now she sits in her apartment waiting for her friend to join her. “I have tried to make it better but I realise I am not her and I will never replace her, she was your love, and although I can never take her place in your heart, I wish to tell you that I now realise we have been wrong. I realise I have tried to separate you from your father because I could never have him with you in his life, he would have given me up for you, but I made him choose me, and seeing you there, looking like your mother, standing the way she did, looking down, the look on your face when your eyes meet your fathers, I realise I may have destroyed part of you.” Seara’s voice begins to quiver as she reads the letter in her hand. Only hours before she had entered her apartment, sure that nothing would be awaiting her except the musty smell that forms over a period of time when human life forms don’t enter a building. “I know how much your father loves you, and how much he would love to be able to walk down to the ring with his little girl and say he was her manager. You have always made your father proud of you, and I hate to admit it, but you made me jealous, made me want to get rid of you, and I tried, but everywhere we went and everywhere you go, your father always tells people, that’s my little girl, my little Katherine, he would never introduce me until someone would ask who the woman behind him was. Then he would say, my wife Gail, but he would never say this is my little girls step mom, I could never live up to you. You were daddy’s little angel, you still are. I found it touching that you showed up to your brothers funeral, but I see no reason for you to have bothered, you were not needed, you were just another face, but daddy noticed you, he wanted to ask you so many things, check you are safe that the monstrous Asis hadn’t hurt his poor innocent little girl.” Seara laughs briefly at the amount of jealousy she had just read and took to her feet to open the door to the banging on it. On the other side Notorious member and friend Scott Frost stands hands in pockets, he looks at the female and steps past her into the room. He looks around at the destruction that she has caused over numerous occasions and visits to the apartment. Stepping back in she closes the door and walks back to her seat and sits back down in the same position as before. She hands frost the letter, but he seems to know already why she is passing it his direction. An explanation for not appearing at the past weeks Legends. It was all Frost needed to secure Seara’s faith and trust in him, she had not shown up for the reasons he had first presumed, but rather for much different reasons which she now seemed to have some amount of regret about. Seara – How is L, I heard he was having stitches removed? Frost – He is good, L bounces back, he is a strong guy, and he will be fit and ready as always. But what about you, that is after all why I am here. Seara – What do you mean, there is nothing wrong with me. I just don’t like being surrounded by people all the time. Being here is like a hiding place, people never know where to find me, so I get left, but you, you Frost are a devil, you know where I am and when, is there nothing you don’t know? Frost shrugs his shoulders to his friends question and laughs at the stranger dazed look in her eyes, it is the same look she had after Asis last attacked her, but this time she does not have it because she is in pain, she seems confused, somehow worried about the presence of Frost. A lack of trust in humanity has left her suspicious of any one who shows friendship or caring towards her. Frost – You know what I mean Seara, you never turned up, and I know why, but we were going to announce that you were joining me and L2J, The Notorious. I even asked Jason Jameson if he wanted to join us, he is a good tag partner for you, and with you in the running for more than one gold, you will need all the help you could get. Seara looks at Frost and thinks about carrying gold in the presence of The Notorious. Tag Team gold with Jason Jameson, the possibility to have the Rising Star Title around her waist, the same title that she cheated her old friend Asis out of, and also the High Impact Title. Three possible belts that she could wear, all with help from her new friends. She did not see a great need for help to gain these titles, but having back up, someone to look over her back would be helpful, security of someone keeping their eyes on Asis, who she knew would be watching all the time, waiting to end her life, to make up for Faith where he failed in himself, failed to end her life when he knew all it would take was a swing of the sledgehammer. He had admitted that for his own reasons he needed Seara, and she had revelled in the fact that she had always known this. Now she sat with Frost standing looking down at her and the camera focusing on the facial expressions of the pair as they talked in a calm trusting fashion. And her only thoughts were that of Asis and his reasoning, maybe he would turn something around, but she had no idea of how he could react or what his reasons could be. She seemed defenceless against the thoughts of Asis and his possible actions and reasons, but she knew that being with The Notorious offered her some safety over him. She shared common pasts and enemies with both L2J and Frost. L2J, a man who had once been friends with Asis, but Asis had turned his back to L2J, and the male wrestler now knew what Seara could expect, he shared the knowledge of Asis. They had both known him before he had ever been in a ring to fight, and they had both foolishly become his friend within eVolution, only to have it backfire. Frost a now ex member of Unlimited shared a common enemy in Xander, a man who had hated Seara since she had first walked through the doors and into eVolution. A man who had resented Frost ever since the male wrestler had betrayed him and left Unlimited to start a stable with highflying wrestler L2J, and make friends with Xander’s only female enemy Seara. Frost now also had Asis to deal with, and all three had gained experience to beat the large destructive male. Seara – The freak is nature, nature is the freak. Seara’s words were quiet, but seemed to refer to Asis, the boy who had been called a freak, the man who is called a freak. Seara- L2J saved me from Asis before, and he had no reason to, he is willing of defence and I am willing to back him up no matter who is after him. If Sovereign turns on L2J this week, I will be out to the ring to help him, and I am sure that you Frost will join me. After all we are a team, and a team is connected, whatever one of us goes through, the others must be there and go through the same, and I will still join the pair of you, become the third member of The Notorious, that is if you are still interested. Seara looks at Frost, waiting for the answer, and he gives it with a simple smile and nod. Lighting a cigarette she walks to the window and looks out over Chicago. Frost joins her and gently places a hand on her shoulder to signal his gratitude for her words. As the scene fades out the female turns to Frost and in a calm and knowing voice tells him that she will show the rookie that no one messes with The Notorious or with the Queen. The last thing we hear as the screen goes black is the sound of footsteps and the banging of a door closing. Another set of footsteps, someone else entering the room.
