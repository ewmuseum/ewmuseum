Changing Times

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th October 2002 Last week female wrestler Seara teamed up with Jason Jameson against the team of Asis and Eric Badger as an ongoing part of the tag team title matches. After what seemed like an easy fight for Seara, she left the ring celebrating while opponent and occasional friend Asis stayed in the ring to teach Badger a lesson. Shouting and hitting his unsuccessful partner, Asis took out some of his anger on yet another unsuspecting wrestler. It was the second time that night Seara had witnessed it. Earlier in the night, she had witnessed him attack ECHO Blackwhisper on a backstage screen while shouting words of territorial ownership. This week although not fighting, Seara is set on being at Legends, but her plan is unclear. Will the female follow the patterns of fellow Chicago wrestler Asis and go to intentionally hurt unsuspecting wrestlers, or does she have something else planned for the night. We find Seara sitting in the pouring rain, her hair matted to the sides of her face and a look of disbelief in her eyes. Placing the cell phone in her trench coat pocket she looks at the camera as though she wasn’t expecting it to be there. As I have said before, I have no reason to brag about a win over Asis. He is someone I have known for a long time, and he is a great wrestler, I could almost say I respect him in the ring. I do not fear him, and I do not see any reason to avoid him or to try not to annoy him. When it comes to my career, he is just a wrestler, someone for me to be put in the ring with, when it comes down to the fight, I am there for a few reasons, one is to inflict pain on whoever I see fit to feel what I have to offer, another is to carry on the only thing I have ever been good at, and finally I am here out of a passion for the game. This is my life; I know only how to fight and how to struggle to survive. I am not looking for an easy way out of anything. If I get anywhere I want to know that I can get there and earn it on my own. And this is the best way to test and push myself. To punish myself week in week out is to truly know what it is to be alive, to appreciate the one skill I have been given. Looking at the camera, Seara sparks up a cigarette and begins to hum her entrance theme. There is a look of passion in her eyes, but not one of love, one of pain. This is a female who sees the pain she suffers as a blessing, a way of proving she is truly alive, that her nightmare is reality. The cameraman hands her a magazine with pictures of all the matches from last weeks Legends, including a few backstage pictures. Flipping open the magazine she instantly spots a picture of her brief encounter with Frost. She stares at it hard, reads the small article below and begins to laugh. Fucking reporters. I would love to know where they get all this bullshit from, it really is hilarious. I speak to someone for 30 fucking seconds and these reporters start assuming that I have dumped Asis to be with them. FUCK IT. Listen carefully, one, I have NEVER been with Asis and I pretty much doubt I ever will, and secondly there is no one here that I find myself interested in. And to clear up for any reporter watching, before you instantly jump to the conclusion that I’m with Frost, think about his wife. Damn I’m not one to give a shit if his marriage fails, it is none of my fucking business unless he chooses to tell me and make it my business, but I do have enough respect for others to realise that this kind of bullshit can really kill someone emotionally. This is why I have fucking refused to do any magazine interviews, those little idiots always change what you say to suite themselves, its all bullshit, a way for them to manipulate dim-witted assholes like you twats who are idiotic enough to believe everything you read on these white pages. Frost is a fellow wrestler, someone who I happen to see in the arenas, and yes he seems friendly enough, but whether I chose to let him become my friend is another matter. Seara continues to flick through the magazine until she comes across pictures of her fight, yet again she begins to laugh at the sight of Eric Badger pinned down to the matt. Taking a last drag of her cigarette she flicks it away, realising the rain has finally stopped. Water drips down her face onto the magazine which is reasonably dry considering how hard the rain has been falling the past few hours. So you wanted to know about my fight, and what I thought about it. I think I had every right to win. I am not being big headed but Badger seemed too easy for me to beat. If I had been against Asis, I may have found it harder, but that is not the way it went, and I don’t have any complaints about the way it finally went. Getting to her feet Seara turns and walks away, leaving the magazine open at a large picture of Asis assaulting his tag partner from the week before. As she disappears around the corner she can be heard saying “It is all mine, all my time, now today, this is it, the time, I will take them, take what I want, I will love it all, I will love them and I will love their pain”.
