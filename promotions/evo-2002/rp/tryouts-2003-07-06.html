Tryouts

6 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-06 A tall black haired lady walks into the training room of eVolution and sets her bag down, looking around. Her blue eyes scan the room as she scratches the pale skin of her left arm. Deloris Breedlove had been recruited from the Otterman School of Wrestling a few short weeks ago, where she had been wrestling some house shows, and was looking to make an impact. First, she had to make it through her tryouts. She was actually doing something pretty risky as far as her character went. She was going to rejuvinate the character her friend Francine West had used in the XWF some months ago. Apparently, Frankie couldn't quite get the niche this character worked in. Deloris smiled. Frankie had always struggled with the Dilyah Love character. She could work the dance moves, and she could do all of the facial expressions, but she just couldn't quite get into the character. However, Dilyah was Deloris' creation, and she knew the character better than she knew herself. If anyone could make this work, it would be her. She just had to convince these people that she was worth the effort. She walked up to the ring, where two men were working out. One, obviously the instructor, had graying short brown hair, and a mustache that went all the way down his chin, and on down to his neck. This was the discription Ernie Otterman, her trainer, had given to her. The man stopped and looked down at her as he finished a move with the student. 'Can I help you,' he asked. 'I'm Deloris Breedlove,' she said. 'I'm here for tryouts.' The man thought for a moment, then smiled down at her. 'You're the dancer with the wierd character, right?' Deloris nodded. 'Alright, then,' he said. 'You go in the women's locker room and get into charcter, then we'll have our tryout. Oh, and when you come out here, I want to see the character, not you.' 'Will do,' said Deloris, picking up her bag and heading towards the locker room. As she entered, she heard the instructor pick up where he had left off with the student. She pulled out her makeup kit and began applying the white mime makeup to her face, leaving her lips and eyes out. She then took out her black mascara and applied it to her eyelashes, followed by black eye shadow, which she brought to a point at the angle of her eye socket. She then pulled out her red lipstick and finished the job, puckering up and kissing at the mirror with a giggle. To finish the whole effect off, she reached into her bag and brought out a pair of contacts, putting them and and changing her eyes from a deep blue, to white cat eyes. The look was wierd, but effective. She smiled. This was Dilyah Love. After changing into her skimpy outfit, which was made up of a black, low-cut camisole, and tight black leather pants, that run into knee high wrestling boots, she took a deep breath. This was it. Do or die time. She stepped out into the room, to find it filled with several other people. Some she recognized from watching some tapes of eVolution, others she didn't know. Perhaps they were behind the scenes people, she didn't know. What she did know was that if she didn't impress them, she was gone. She did recognize one person, Ernie Otterman. She was happy that he was there to see her tryout. It gave her confidence to have him there. As she approached the ring, the guy with he mustache looked at her and shook his head. 'What a freak,' he said with a smile. 'Get up there and show me what you're made of. I want to see a promo, then we'll put someone up there to wrestle you.' Great, thought Dilyah, she would have to work with someone she's never worked with before. As she got into the ring, she looked around, then walked over and took a microphone. When she turned back, there was something missing. No, there was someone missing. Ernie had dissappeared. Get a grip, she thought. You can get through this, even if Ernie's played a dissappearing act. She put the mic to her lips and was instantly in character. 'Sluts and germs! Rugrats of all ages! Bow down before me, for your queen has arrived!' Well, that was a start, she thought, as she got a little reaction from some of the people, but others frowned. Why were they frowning, damn them! She used this to fuel her next rant. 'I see some of you don't take to kindly to the idea that I'm your queen,' she said. 'I see some of you don't think I have dominion over you! Well I beg to differ!.' She drops and rolls out of the ring, walking over to a man and circling him. 'Look at you,' she said. 'You're all smug in that nice suit and tie, but in your head, you're thinking... damn, what a woman! I wonder who she's blowing? Well, I'll tell ya buddy, I blow who I please to blow.' She ran her finger across his lips as she sashays back over to the ring, this time taking the steps to enter. 'I am the Divine Dominatrix, the Goddess of Lust,' she said, as she leaned over and stepped into the ring, 'and I will worm my way straight into your head. Oppose me, and you will pay the consequences. Worship me, and you may be granted an audience in my throne room... my... chamber of horrors! Now, before I start getting bored, send me someone to trounce!' She walked over and handed the mic back to the ring attendant as she got a little applause from some of the gathered people. She then saw Ernie approaching the ring, in full wrestling attire. Damn, she thought to herself. They're gonna make me wrestling him? Ernie rolled into the ring and walked right up to her. 'Good job,' he said, as he pushed her. 'Go with me.' They then launched into an assault of one another that lasted the better part of 15 minutes, before they ended the with Dilyah the winner. As she walked back towards the locker room, she felt sure she had secured herself a contract. If the promo hadn't done it, the wrestling surely had to. She would know soon enough.
