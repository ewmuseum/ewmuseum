deadly sins 'Envy'

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-17 envy is the desire for others' traits, status, abilities, or situation. We can only expect the furious anger and rage that flows through the mind, body and soul of the deadliest athlete in the game, after being screwed out of eVolution tag championships. For every action there is a repercussion and the outcome after two Legends ago, is terrifying to the point it sickens me to my stomach. Morpheus Omega has lost his partner to a brutal neck injury after an airplane spin toss off the top turnbuckle, he has been suspended from eVolution with no specified amount of time, and now torn apart inside about how he should react towards Tyler Davidson; who carelessly holds his career inside of his hands... The scene opens inside a hospital room with bright white walls, a bed positioned in the middle of the room and an end table beside it. Sky Night lies asleep with a disturbed yet peaceful look on her face. Sky breathes deeply and motions to turn her head and lets out a loud cry, she holds her neck and lies motionless once again. A loud-sickening growl can be heard and the scene pans to find Morpheus with his back arched against the wall, he shakes his head out of anger and despair. A snarling look can be seen on Morpheus face. 'The last few weeks have been very rough ones for Twisted Reality, Sky Night and myself. I think to myself who I should hold responsible and only one name repeats over and over, inside of my head. The name Tyler Davidson rings inside my head with utter hatred, but I am stuck in an intense moral dilemma. The man who has full control over my career, but has also ended the career of Sky. The choices and decisions made by Davidson are a direct reflection on everything that has happened in the last two weeks. This is now a personal issue between Tyler and myself, he will feel my wrath in the most horrible ways possible' Morpheus pauses with an evil grin sketched on his face, he shakes his head side to side and shows his clenched teeth. Morpheus is evidently envious over the fact Davidson has screwed him over, his title within eVolution and the poor decision making that he has made. 'Twisted Reality proved to be the deadliest force known to the wrestling industry and reminded eVolution night after night, no one could compare. The stunning ability of the deadliest athlete in the game, with the physical abuse and torture I would put our opponents through to the smart, cunning ability that Sky had to orchestrate the perfect game plans. Together we were the Bonnie and Clyde of this sport, nothing could stop us! I have thought to myself that maybe we finally received a taste of our own medicine, I just feel sorry for the bastard who I point the finger of blame towards! Correct me If I am wrong, but the duration of our tag championship is the longest title reign in eVolution history, for all divisions!' Morpheus elbows backwards and puts a hole into the drywall, he breathes deeply and barely controls his temper. The feeling of envy destroying and tearing Morpheus apart. 'I heard a loud bang, what happened?' A nurse questions, who walks inside the room. 'My arm slipped and then my elbow crashed through the wall, you would think the medical funding would cover a good, sturdy wall!' Morpheus replies with a smart-ass look. 'Funding?' The nurse questions and bursts out laughing, like something amused her. The nurse continues speaking... 'You have been here for days, I think you need rest and fresh air. This cannot be healthy for you!' 'I was gone for about thirty-six hours on Wednesday when I left for Legends. I have been suspended for the next while from eVolution, so you may see a lot more of me!' Morpheus answers with a broken look on his face and an insane look in his eyes. 'I don't plan on being here longer then another day!' Sky says with a weak, raspy voice with a following loud cry, she leans over and makes eye contact with Morpheus. 'You are in no condition to leave here Ms. Night, you could possibly be here for months little lone another day. Lay back down, you need rest!' The nurse says with a concerned voice. 'Listen if I wanted a counselor, I would of called one and I specially don't need medical advice from you... Get the hell out of my room!' Sky screams and throws a drinking glass, it smashes off the door into pieces. The nurse leaves the room following... Morpheus kneels down and lowers his head. 'I will avenge my Queen!' Morpheus says full of despite. The envy eating away at Morpheus like a flesh eating disease, this could either be the final straw that breaks the God of Sleep and Dreams back or the true beginning of the end!
