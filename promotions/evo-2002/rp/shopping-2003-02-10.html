Shopping!

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.10.03 ~Reality, reality is something that you can have an altered perception on a sort of Twisted Reality. A spin put on things so sick, so violent that no one could comprehend what was about to go down. This twisted Reality will take down eVolution by eliminating the three stars that are below Twisted Reality! Jameson, Sovereign, and Frost. Shocka, Omega and Night have never had a better opportunity to make a name for themselves than they have this coming Legends. Shocka and Omega have a storied history of being a tag team and it seems that Sky Night and Omega work well together. Hell you could go as far as to say that they are a well oiled machine ready to take out anyone that gets in there way.~ ~Some people are stuck in there past and lost in there own world struggling for breath in a world filled with oxygen plush air. Struggling to make a name for themselves while other do it so easily. People like Shocka, Omega and even Sky Night. But People like Jason Jameson strive for all of the glory, they strive for everything that they can but they end up with nothing. These people we’d like to call failures trapped in there own pain not strong enough to overcome to put it into the back of there heads. ~ {Lying in his own world of glory striving to be that much better and proving week in and week out that he is, is Shocka. He lies halfway back in his BMW roadster with his hands on each side of his head and a large smile on his face. As the scene slowly gets wired you see a woman’s head bobbing up and down as the smile on Shocka’s face grows wider and then at a climax he laughs and pulls his seat up the chick goes up blocking the shot. He finishes and takes off as he speaks.} ‘You know what April I think that this little thing between me and you will work out after all..’ Shocka says with a smile. ‘Yeah Jonathan I really do like you!’ She says as she rubs his chest. ‘I bet you do…’ Shocka says as he shifts into 4th gear and takes off onto the interstate. {They arrive in a parking lot and Shocka parks and gets out the woman follows and Shocka sets his alarm with a beep. His red polo shirt is a little ruffled as they walk in. Shocka takes a sharp turn trying to loose the woman into a sporting goods store. Shocka moves right toward the wrestling gear he looks over some of the shirts studying Scott Frost, Jameson, and Seara’s shirts littering the whole place. Shocka begins pull shirts off and flooring them toward the floor in the back he finds one of his it says #1 Stunna on the front and has a picture of Shocka on the back. The shirt is snatched by someone who is running by and Shocka turns violently and grabs one of there arms he throws them into the rack of shirts with a thud.} ‘Lee what in the hell are you doing?!’ Shocka yells with fury. ‘You know what I am doing…imma by this damn shirt before you do you are my new hero!’ Lee exclaims with excitement. ‘NO bullshit I am going to buy this shirt!’ Shocka says. ‘Shouldn’t you be getting ready for your match with Seara I mean it is for the evolved title damnit!’ ‘Shit…I got that bitch under control she thinks that she can beat up Brittney’s boy friend?! She is way out of her league in this match I don’t even care what stipulation we have imma be like this!’ Lee throws a shadow punch. ‘And then she is gonna be all like this!’ He falls simulating what Seara would do.. ‘Riiiiiiiiiight’ Shocka says. ‘Dude who is that chick she looks like she is going to eat your brain!’ Lee screams. {Shocka turns slightly terrified and sees April he turns and covers his face before speaking to Lee.} ‘Dude…get her off of my tail go hit on her or something she gives a pretty good BJ…’ Shocka says. {Lee darts his head and then struts over to the woman he puts his arm around her and turns her around. She goes with him and Shocka shakes his head with a look of relief.} ‘Now I can get back to shopping damnit!’ He says in a slightly mellow tone. {Shocka looks over a few more shirts and picks out a Omega and Shocka one he takes them up to counter and buys it. He walks out with a bag to see April and Lee. He covers his face with the bag and sort of jogs out to his car.} F.A.D.E
