A hero's welcome

12 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-01-12 Toronto, Canada It has been nearly five months of constant overseas travel within Evolution and now, The Syndicate has finally returned home to North America……as champions once again. But now, an old entity, assumed to be long gone has finally risen from the ashes once again to try and make HBC’s and BDD’s life a living hell. It seems they cannot rid themselves of this scourge. Whether it was in AWF, the EDW, and once again here in Evolution, Malcom King has returned and once again…… nothing is safe……. For him….. While it should be a joyous time for The Syndicate, The Heartbreak Chick capturing the tag title gold for a record setting fourth time in addition to picking up three year end awards, (Tag team of the year with BDD, woman of the year and moment of the year with Seara), and Big Daddy Dreamer, holding the tag gold for the second time and winning tag team of the year with HBC, the mood is anything but. As they return to the famous Syndicate International towers, the discussion is all about King, the Bodysnatchers and Faithless. Even the staff of tireless workers applauding their return is not enough to change their mood. BDD and HBC fire off a smile to Alexis who tries to inform them the city has planned a homecoming parade for them, but Dreamer just holds up a finger telling her later, apparently they haven’t been keeping up on current events as of late. As the elevator whisks them up to the 110th floor, there private office, they open up the blinds and sigh……it’s good to be home…. HBC places her tag title belt on her desk along with her three awards, looks out to the cityscape around her and utters…. “‘I can’t believe that bastard is back! I had thought we had finally gotten rid of him once and for all this time. I mean, you defeated him…..Malcom King, one of the greatest of all-time, in a career versus career match. You defeated him and ended his career D, the only man in history able to accomplish that feat! So why the hell is he here again now!” “It must be Davidson…..no other person could have pulled this off. That son of a bitch! Look C, you and I both know we can handle Malcom King and his crony Bodysnatchers, especially with Draven looking out for us now… “I know that D, I know that…….I just hope to hell, King never finds out what we did to his wife and precious daughter, the same with SkyNight, too many sins of the past are coming out now and I truly thought this one was closed forever. “ “*Sigh*……I guess it isn’t. As truly nuts King is, just remember, we have the advantage, and if for some reason, some reason at all, our security measures failed, we still have the trump card to play with and he knows us well enough that we will use it and exploit it fully. He isn’t stupid, nuts yes, stupid, no….not the Malcom King I know. He’ll comeback with a vengeance sure, still bitter about me costing him the Universal Championship, still bitter about us destroying his precious Unlimited from within, still bitter and blaming us for his failures, bitter at me for ending his career, but he is a logical man and I don’t think he will play with his family’s life. If he does…… then, I will admit he wasn’t as smart as I give him credit for…” “A dangerous web we weave D……” “Indeed it is, but remember……we own him, we know him, and we know how to exploit and crush him and this time, he will stay gone once and for all!” “Well I will say one thing…..he must be getting desperate to enlist The Bodysnatchers into the King’s Army. Bloody mercenaries they are but all talk, no bite. I’m sure they would love to go into Syndication once again.” “Exactly…….Dolo and 1001 Holds have yet to learn they are at best a C+ team in a A+ league. Sure they can surprise anyone on a given day, but true greatness….no…..true greatness is this right here. The tag team championship gold and the 2003 Tag Team of the Year award. That, that right there is what it’s all about. Bodysnatchers, please…. I’ve seen better high-school wrestling teams than those two. Faithless is a better team than those two.” “Ah yes, I’m glad you mentioned them….we face them in Miami you know. I guess that was there reward for beating those two j-rones at Legends we helped out in.” “Figures….” “Anyways, Faithless in Miami…..mmmmm…..Miami, quite lovely this time of year, I’ll be able to work on my tan again and enjoy the South Beach nightlife….” “Not to mention all the hotties that adore us down their….” “Ah yes……its good to be back home, and not to good to be Faithless, because frankly, it took nearly six months to win these titles back, and I’ll be god damned if I wait that long for another crack at it. No, these titles will be around my sexy waist for quite some time! Faithless be damned! The Syndicate is back reigning hell in the tag division once again!!!
