Serious Thinking

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20-12-02 The scene opens outside of the Granada studios in London. Inside L2J has just finished an interview with Michael Parkinson to help promote the up and coming pay per view. Outside the studio the audience has gathered to see off the evolution champion and wait eagerly for the doors to open. The suddenly swings open and the fans are surprised to see Seara walk out and split the crowd as if they weren’t even there as they burst into cheers. Closely behind Seara, L2J is shaking hands with Michael and then heads towards his car. The crowd explode and L2J cant help but throw his weight and his title around and play up to the crowd. Seara sits on her bike and subtly laughs as she watches her friend ponce about infront of his fans. She patiently waits for L2J to finish with all his autographs before bringing her bike over to him. SEARA- Come on L we’ve a plane to catch and people to see. L2J- I’ll meet you at the airport. I’ve got a little sight seeing to do. Seara seems to know what L2J is talking about. SEARA- Are you sure that’s wise? She thinks better of what she just said as she tries to remember the last time L did anything even remotely wise. SEARA- Ok just be careful. I’ll see you at Heathrow. With that Searas bike takes flight and disappears through the London traffic. L2J gives one last salute to his fans before jumping in his car, throwing his belt onto the passenger seat and starting the engine. He looks out his window at the fans being held back by security and is amazed at how far his fan base has spread. If you can win over the high brow fans of Parkinson what cant you do? L2J spends an hour mindlessly driving up the A10 until he finds himself back home. And he instantly remembers how grateful he was when Dan S. got him out of this place. If you take L2J’s guided tour round his hometown the main points of reference are all the parts of the streets he got mugged in. He weaves through the bleak streets until he brings his car to a stop outside his now uninhabited place of birth. The paint is peeling from the exterior walls and the windows are now boarded up. He doesn’t know why he stopped here. The memories aren’t fond ones. A single bedroom house. His two less than law abiding older brothers, and a mother too proud to claim benefits from the state. Pride that she could obviously turn on and off as she saw no wrongs in letting her two youngest sons leave their home with a total stranger to make a living as bareknuckle boxers. L was too young to ask questions, nor to have any moral issues with what he was doing. Maybe that was for the best. It got him where he is today and he can now look at this house from the outside. He made the money to bring his mother out of this hole. And made more than enough money to ensure that she never had to work another day in her life again. L2J spends little time dwelling on the past and drives out of the town and towards the burbs. He has other people to see. Another person that came with him out of this city. She was desperate to get out of this city and used love as the ticket. But she hated to see what L had become. More than this damn place and she came back to start again. L2J pulls up to her house. He doesn’t really seem to know what hes doing or why he is here and he almost thinks better of getting out of his car. But he forces himself to his feet. He walks past the BMW in the driveway and comes to the conclusion that she has made good of her life. He walks up to the door and listens to the patronising lift music tune of the doorbell echo through the house. He waits for her to answer the door as he steps off the porch and looks down the street. There seems to be a lot of colour and tranquillity on this side of town. He hears the latch release on the door behind him and he turns sharply to greet her. But is set back to see a well dressed, prim and proper man answer the door. MAN- Hi there….Can I help you? L2J is clearly put out and doesn’t really know what to say. So he doesn’t say anything and the two men share an uncomfortable silence. The man uses his higher position on the porch to look down his nose at L2J’s blue hair and long black trenchcoat. The mans looks as if he is about to ask L2J what he’s selling but opts for a more polite approach. MAN- Are you looking for Claire. I’m afraid shes out doing some Christmas shopping right now…Are you a friend? L2J looks past the man to see a 2 year old girl peer round one of the doorways inset to the hall and forces himself to smile. L2J- Yeah, yeah I was just in the neighbourhood and thought I’d just stop by. MAN- Well do you want to come in she should be back in about half an hour. L2J- No that’s fine I wouldn’t want to trouble you. Thanks anyway. MAN-Ok then, well take care. L2J gives the girl a little wave as the door closes and she chuckles and waves back. L2J smiles and turns to head back to his car spinning his keys on his finger. It was naïve of him to think that she wouldn’t move on. Its been nearly three years since she left him in American and it’s clear to see that she has moved up in the world and has done a lot better for herself here. L2J gets into his car and looks down at his title. He thinks about the next place he should visit. But there isn’t a next place. He realises that there is nothing for him here anymore. The life left behind is now gone. All that his life is now is this title. And his home is evolution. His train of thought is interrupted by his cellphone. L2J-Hello? SEARA-L will hurry the fuck up our plane leaves in an hour. L2J-Yeah ok….I’m on my way. L2J closes up his cellphone. Takes one last look at his hometown. Then leaves it behind.
