Unlimited Prospects

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/06/03 {The scene opens up in the corporate office of Daniel Somers. Xander is seated, possibly awaiting the Suicidal one's presence.} 'You know Angel' The Unlimited coordinator begins. 'The only reason you were ever allowed into Unlimited, was as a favor, and part of the deal with Somers bringing him in. We have discussed what happened, and I hold him at no fault. He's as shocked as I am. Unfortunately for you though, you stupidly pissed off, not only the man who signs your paychecks, but a man who has bled with the best. Fought the greatest. And will soon show everyone why it is that Daniel Somers has joined forces with me.' 'Think about it Angel. What would Somers have to gain by joining forces with me, and Unlimited. Think, for just one moment. He has more power in eVolution then we do. If he needed hired help, he can, and has already done that in various forms. So why join us? What in the world can he be getting out of this relationship.' 'Well, luckily for you, only two men know the extent of the relationship. Somers, and myself. No offense to Asis, Christoph, or anyone else we are, and will associate with. But, it is none of their business. Unlimited, is a business. A conglomerate, if you will. Each member is hand picked for a specific job. Asis has his job, I have my job, Somers has his, Christoph, and so on. Once your job is completed, or not longer useful, we allow you to leave, or, we eliminate you from competition.' 'You two, on the other hand, were a fluke. As I stated, a part of a deal. You had no job. You were not enforcers of any kind, Asis has not failed at what he does. In fact, he is the Continental champion now. A belt I strived so hard for, but unfortunately never grasped. But that is behind me now. Now, my focus is firmly placed upon three objects.' 'This Wednesday.. Angel and Oz step into the line of sight.... and soon enough Seara will feel the pain of regret. Regretting that she ever stepped into eVolution.. and that she stands for what she does.' 'Soon enough, it will all be crystal clear. The planning.. the reasoning. The eVolution. We are Unlimited.. and you are next.' {The scene fades to black}
