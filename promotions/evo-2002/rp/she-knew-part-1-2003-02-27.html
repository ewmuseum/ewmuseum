She Knew part 1

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th Febuary 2003 ‘ It will only be for a couple of days, and you know where to find me if you need me.’ Frosts words rung through the females head, and as she hit the floor with full force she could picture the scene at the airport as she had got ready to board her plane for Chicago. ‘ Scott, I…. I could never cope with mom’s death as a kid, and for 10 years I skipped going to this dinner to celebrate her, and now I’m going, I have no idea what I’m doing.’ Seara had seemed lost when she talked to Scott about her family, for so long in her life she had let herself feel secure by telling herself her family were dead, it had been her way to cope, but now her family was minimal, and the female knew she was partly to blame. Her stepbrother’s death had been her fault, but she had wanted to kill him for so many years, and she brought herself to do it. It was something she never regretted, Vinnie had been the bain in her existence, but her stepmother was worse. She had eventually been dumped and left by Seara’s father who after having his baby girl returned to him by Asis actions, had seen his wife’s flaws and changed his life. Her older brother Jake, he had come to the arena to find Seara and Asis had found him, and beat him, leaving him within inches of his life, he became paralysed and was on life support after the attack, and Seara had stopped his pain by turning off the machines forcing his life to be prolonged. But had all started with a death that had affected the female worse than she had ever come to realize, her life had been her mother, but aged 3, Seara had watched that change. ‘Something is bound to go wrong if I go there.’ The words the female had uttered 2 days before coursed through her mind and she felt the sensation of blood flowing, but she was seemingly asleep, paralysed by the pain, something was wrong and she knew it, but she could do nothing. On the anniversary of her mother’s death every year, the females family headed out to the restaurant where her parents had got engaged to celebrate her life. At first the female had went, but she always ended up running out, scared and alone, hating her family. She had never understood how they could celebrate someone’s death. Ten years ago she told her family that she would never again sit with them in that room and do what they had found to be a tradition to them. The female had hated them all so much. Fear filled her mind and she became worried, she could not control what she was feeling seeing or hearing. The image of her mother dying next to her seeped into her thoughts and she knew she was crying, but she could not feel the tears or hear the sound of her sobs. Something was wrong and she knew it. The ring, her first fight, the faces if eVolution, Xander first, Asis, her enemies one by one invading her mind, then something different. L2J and Scott Frost. They were there, caring for her, leaning over her, but L2J was dead and she knew it. He faded away, his last words flooded her mind, that last moment in the ring, the last time she had seen him alive. She wanted to shout out his name, but she couldn’t. Extra pain seemed to seep into her mind, and once again she was at the airport with Scott holding her tightly in his arms. ‘ I want to go with you, but you need to do this alone, if you want me, I’ll be here, you know I’m not going anywhere without you. It’s only 3 days, I’ll wait right here for you.’ The female laughed and looked at the man next to her, a man she had once been scared of who now filled her every thought. She couldn’t breath, it was too hard for her, breathing seemed to let the blood flow faster, and the blood flowing smoothly to her head seemed to be causing immense pain, but she could not feel it. She could see herself walking onto the plane and sitting down, she fell asleep with a Cd playing and her headphones on, but she could not hear the music. A moment later she was in her fathers house, then in a car, the ever-changing scene was worrying the female, she did not understand why this was happening to her, or for that matter, what was happening to her. A restaurant, it seemed so close as though she were only just there. She got up to go to the toilet, she said she felt light headed and quite sick, she couldn’t hold her own weight. Collapsing she had fallen forward and hit her head off the corner of the table, splitting her eye open, the blood spitting everywhere, and she had slumped full force into the Greece flooring, the uneven tiles had crushed into her body as she hit it and she had not got back up. A feeling of immense pain flooded into her and the motion of falling only seconds before had rushed through her mind at a great speed, and she felt herself become aware that it was real and that she was on the floor. Her eyes opened slightly and she gained a blurred image, blood soaked crimson eyes and the sight of two males, her father and brother. She couldn’t make them out though. The pain seared through her skull and trying to get to her feet she pushed up off the floor. Her brother had grabbed her, pulled her up and sat her down again, then passed her a glass of water. ‘ You gave us a fright, are you alright?’ Bruce’s deep soothing voice was slightly too much for the female, and shaking her head she tried to stop herself shaking. The shock of the untimely collapse had frightened the female slightly and it could be seen in her eyes. ‘Come on, I’ll get you home, and you can call Scott.’ Putting his arm around the female, Bruce helped her to her feet and led the way out of the building to head home. The scene faded to black as the female was placed in the passenger’s seat of the car. To Be Continued.
