The Coronation Part IV

8 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-08 Flasback once again to the Coronation of Ethan Coles. Last time we were present we saw the arrival of Bob, the consequence of which I doubt even Ethan could have imagined in his wildest dreams as we now move forward in time to the second event on the agena; the concert and parade. 'Why did you invite him Ethan?' asked the function manager to Ethan as they quickly made their way to the stage situated on the front lawn. 'I didn't think I did' Ethan replied somewhat confused, 'just keep him out of the way' 'How?' asked the aide. 'Hes a drunk it shouldn't be too hard' Layton replied flippantly. At that moment Ethan and the aide look down across the lawn and notice a commotion which has the crowd on their feet; some of whom are cheerings, others: notably those English descendants of Ethan reaching for cover. Ethan turns away in absolute disgust holding his head in his hands, the man who was deemed to drunk to elude anyone had struggled past two guards and made his way onto the stage and was now attempting to gain control of the micophone. 'Hello ENGGGGERRLAND' Bob said somewhat dazed by the light shining into his eyes and the effect of the alcoholic content in the free champagne he had been pumping into his liver upon his arrival. 'Don't just stand there!' Ethan screamed across to his aide demanding action. 'Yes sir' the aide said frantically gesturing for more of his men to move over to the stage area and remove the problem. 'I...' Bob began before falling forward and to his knees drunkenly. 'Oh dear god' Ethan muttered to himself, 'a disaster' At this point though much to Ethans relief the guards were now on site and ready to remove the thorn in the side of master coles, only they couldn't, the fans wouldn't let them. Ethan looked on mortified, the guards stood bewildered. Cheering at the top of their voice, the fans stood in unison calling for Bobs stay to be extended; a shock to Ethans system and more importantly to the aide who knew not whether to act on Ethans advice and risk a crowd rebellion. 'What do I do?' the aide said turning to Ethan. Ethan looked like a deer in headlights he could not believe the response this drunken man was getting. 'Ethan' Bob slurred, 'come sing a song with me' The crowds cheered with delight, this was something they definately wanted to see. A slow clap then began as the crowd called the host to the stage. 'Mr Coles?' the aide again called out. Bob in the mean time had staggered over to the orchestral band on hand. There was some discussion between Bob and this group of musicians. If the unexpected hadn't happened it was about to, it appeared Bob had a rendition of the British national anthem in his locker. Would this bring the champion to the forefront?! 'God save our gracious queen' Bob slurring heavily but with the crowds voices for backing... 'Long live our...' Bob stuttered trying to remember the words; 'Nobel....' Bob fell forward at this point to his knees but not before winning the adoration of the crowd on hand. Ethan had a rival for the imaginations of his fans, or perhaps an ally in a mission to win them for himself? What else could happen at this coronation?!
