lies deception fianlly truth part 3

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-14-03 [Sometimes in a game you score more than one goal and in this game Hedges is out to score at least two. One against Shocka and one against Malcolm. A feat easy enough and soon Hedges will be recognized as a force instead of the push over people think he is] The scene opens back up with Hedges packing his things to head off to the airport. He’s got his bags and throws them at the front door to wait for him limo to come and pick him up. There’s a knock on the front door and Hedges opens it up. There’s an eVo cameraman outside. Hedges looks at his watch and indicates that he’s late. The man apologizes and starts filming… “You know I bet there’s a man sitting at home right now who thinks I’ve forgotten about him. He issued a challenge to me. Yeah you heard me right. I didn’t ask for a match with Malcolm Xavier. I asked to prove myself and I said to watch his back. Obviously the man knows I have what it takes. I don’t expect because of my past to be handed title shots and main event matches. Though you seem to think I ask for them. Not once since I’ve been here have I asked for shit. I earn everything that comes across my path. You are giving me a chance to skip a lot of the BS uncard matches and you’re damn right I’m going to take that. Pin you once anywhere today. It can be done and shall be. I shall strike when you least expect it…” Hedges steps outside on the bright sunny day. There’s a slight breeze as he lights up a cigarette. He looks up exhaling a drag threw his nose. “I wonder about all these misconstruing facts floating about…Omega’s Shadow? Last time I checked I co-founded the resurrection and lead it for a good while. Whatever not important. I don’t know what Malcolm has been doing in his room or what he’s smoking but he obviously has not a clue about what he’s talking about. I came back to help Omega out here and get my career back on track after a brief retirement. As far as being an NTICW wrestler…I WAS…If you notice I’m part of no NTICW team. I joined before they came in. I have a bonified evo contract. I have to earn nothing. Tyler could sign me and you threw evo if he wanted. I claim no glory to that fed. I had one win in the one fed…WHOOO lets celebrate…” He mumbles in a sarcastic tone while taking a long drag and sighing “As far as Shocka goes everyone knows how I feel about him. He’s lies, excuses, and pure bull will be simply ended tomorrow. I’ll punt that runt ass mini Shocka to the rafters if he touches me. New-TV won’t be needed to beat Shocka. I’ve done it alone countless times before and this time will be no different. Now I want you all to mark today’s date on your calendar people. Hedges breaks threw to a new crowd of people…I’ll beat the road to glory former blah blah blah and maybe gain some respect from the crowd and I’ll pin Xavier and take he chump ass title as well the next week before I move on to greater things. Let it be known an era of truth is upon us and Shocka if you say you’ve evolved then you where a lower life form to begin with than I thought”
