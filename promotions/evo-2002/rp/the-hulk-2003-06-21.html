The Hulk

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-21 With two cars traveling down the road, it seems that Machine and his gang are on their way to go somewhere. Of course, when you got a massive man like Machine, you won’t be able to fit 4 men in one car comfortably. Machine and Brett are in one vehicle as Cedric and Adam are in the other. We go inside Machine’s car to start off the day. “You ready to go see your movie?” Machine asks “My movie?” Brett responds. “Yea, don’t you remember?” ~Flashback~ Cedric is obviously on the other side with the 151 bottle. Adam continues to push, but can’t seem to open it. Brett walks downstairs and gives Adam a confused look. “Give me a hand, Ced took the damn bottle of liquor and has locked himself in.” Adam explains to Brett. “Come on Ced, open the damn door!” Brett yells. “You don’t want to take the liquor and get me angry!” “Who are you, the Hulk?” Adam asks ~End Flashback~ “That was right before you broke the door Hulk.” Machine says. You had to Hulk-Smash the door down, you muscle bond freak.” “You keep talking like that and we’re going to take sugar out of your diet.” Brett says in a threatening way. “Hey…that’s not even funny.” The two cars are seen entering the parking lot of a movie theater, where hundreds of little kids are all outside waiting to get inside the movies. The four men all walk to the front of the line, where all the little teenagers are all restless to get inside. Two security guards stand at the door where they’re suppose to take tickets and let the people in. “Hey Machine you ready?” Adam asks. “Yep.” The two biggest men of the four walk straight to the front of the line and greet the two guards that stand there. Machine greets the one on the right, whereas Adam greets the one on the left. “Ok, you guys can leave now.” Machine says to the guard. “Who are you?” The guard asks. “We’re TOP FLIGHT SECURITY!” Adam replies. “You guys must bet new. Go ask your boss, we’re suppose to be the ones that let you guys off your shift.” “Our shift ends at 10:15 now?” The second guard questions. “Indeed. So you can go ahead and leave.” Machine answers. “Sh*t, I won’t complain about an early release, you guys can have your shift.” Both guards then leave. Machine and Adam let Brett and Cedric in before they enter themselves. “ALL THE TICKETS ARE FREE!” Machine yells, causing a mass stampede of kids into the movie theater. The two guards watch this from about 20 feet away… “We’re going to be sooo fired.” We fast forward to in the movie where the group is indeed watching the debut of The Hulk. We find Adam sleeping as there is WAY to much talking in this movie. During the movie, The Hulk breaks down a door… “Remind you of something Brett?” Machine asks and laughs. “Fuck you!” It’s not too long before all 4 men fall asleep and the movie continues to run way too long….
