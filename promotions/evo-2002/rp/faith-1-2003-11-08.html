Faith 1

8 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-08 Tokoyo, Japan NHK 1 Station Noon With the United Kingdom leg of the EVO World Tour completed, everyone headed into Faith all pumped up. History has shown it is one of our top pay-per-view, but the overall general feeling is that this one is going to be something special. The matches better, told the story is better. And certainly with the sudden influx of new talent, many are primed and ready for the big time, however the two major players on the card,Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick are currently being prepped by one of the NHK technicians and putting their microphone on them as they take a seat. Sitting with them will be Isis Yomanoa, Japan's premier female interviewer, their version of Oprah persay. BDD and HBC take a seat, take a drink of bottled water as Isis announces to the world that they are on the air... Isis: Welcome Japanese viewers!! Today we have a some very special guests from Canada who literally just flew from England of all places. And why are they here, to help promote our Ministries of Sports cross-over exchange program....for the first time in Japanese history...The EVO is here for a series of wrestling shows and holding a major worldwide pay per view event at the Tokyo Dome. Now we've had WWF in the past, we've had WCW in the past, we've had individuals come over to make a name for themselves, but never in the history of the sport, have we had the premier federation all come over at once and honor us with their show. Today.......I am joined by Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick....collectively known as The Syndicate. Welcome to show! BDD/HBC: Thanks it's great to be back in Japan again. Isis: You've both have been on an exchange here before haven't you? BDD: Yes... I've personally have represented the RGWF, SWF, XWF, and EDW over here taking on some of the FHW guys like Muta, Masto and Chono. The Tokyo Dome is almost a second home to me. HBC: Unlike Dreamer here who came over on an exchange, I actually started out over here as a Gaijin in the F-1 promotion. I was just a rookie then, but I have to tell you, my time here was one of the greatest and most memorable ever. I learned so much from your fellow Japanese wrestlers, who took me under their wing and taught me alot. I still use alot of Japanese moves in my arsenal today. Isis: Indeed. We recently conducted a poll here on who everyone thought was their favorite female athlete, and suffice to say it HBC, you won it hands down. Everyone has been going crazy over here when it was announced you were coming and competing. Even Emperor Hiroito was excited, he is a big big fan of yours. HBC: That's very flattering. From the moment we landed last night, we have been besieged by fans. They are definately much more vocal over here and personally, I want to thank them and hope many of them can attend the matches and watch us in person. BDD: The Syndicate is even having a contest in which the winner gets two free front row tickets and dinner with us. How you win is simple, we want you to create a poser image of HBC and she will personally select the winner. Its that simple. Isis: Now it says you've just recently finished the second leg of the Evolution World Tour in the United Kingdom? HBC: That's correct, we were their a month performing shows in England, Wales and Scotland. Ireland was crazy. We won and lost the Evo tag titles, Dreamer became the tag team commissioner only to have our boss interfere with it, so yeah its been crazy lately for us. (The tape runs of The Syndicate and The Industry’s match...Dreamer acting referee tossing the match out due to interference) HBC: Needless to say we weren’t happy about it at all. I mean who the hell does Tyler Davidson thinks he is screwing around with the greatest team of all time and then having the gall to put our careers on the line AGAIN!! BDD: That's right C, Isis, on Sunday night, myself and HBC team up once again to dominate the tag team division, a division we've ruled here as where as everywhere else. Its no secret she and I were former tag team champions of the EVO, and now with the titles being quasi-held up the EVO decided to have a triple threat match with us, The Industry and Astonishing Entertainment with both the Industry’s and our’s career on the line so here we are. Back together, once again, the greatest tag team in wrestling history to lay claim to the EVO Tag Team titles for the second time and the Fourth for HBC Isis: Do you know anything about Astonishing Entertainment? HBC: They are a new team from Las Vegas, sort of a Sigfriend and Roy reject team trying to make a name for themselves at our expense. However, unlike Vegas were everything is smoke and mirrors, they will find Evolution a little different. Consider us the White Tigers ready to maul, chew up and spit out Astonishing Entertainment like that queerboy Roy their. This won’t be no protection excuse though, were here to injure, maim and even kill if need be against them. BDD: The Industry, it doesn't even matter. HBC can defeat them, that's no question. I've already beaten them, hell I've beaten the both of them myself, but point of fact is, no matter how good they are, how prepared they think they are, she and I dominated them individually, and together, when were clicking on all cyclinders, or operating at 75%, no team, I repeat no team in the world, can defeat us. Only one team in history has ever held an advantage over us, but never again, we ended their careers in a most violent fashion. Isis: OH MY! HBC: Its a dangerous business that were in. Sure the fans cheering for us are wonderful, the pay is great and you get to travel to places that most people only dream about, but it is a brutal, violent business. Friendships are virtually non-existant, except me and D, most are at best intangible alliances, something American philosopher Thomas Jefferson said to 'avoid at all cost'. The tag team division is the same way. Unless you and partner have teammed for years and years, most fail and predominately, they stab each other in the back to better their own agenda. Me and D, we complement each other like no one else. He's got the power, the strength, the solid mat-game, not to mention one of the most brillant minds in the industry and I have the speed, quickness, and high-flying aerial assault that makes it dam near difficult to prepare for us, let alone get an advantage on us. BDD: Exactly, she and I have won over 29 tag team championships together in nine different federations. That alone speaks volumes about our work, but their is more to it than that. We read other minds so well also. I know what she is thinking in the ring and vice versa. We never motion for a double team, we just do it, because we know when the timing is right, we know how to distract the ref. HBC: Yeah, show him my tits! BDD: *Laugh* Yeah, something like that! But when you face us, you face the complete package. Their is nothing we haven't seen, no match we haven't been in. Hell, HBC was recently in a glass coffin match. Isis: No shit! HBC: No shit! I learned it here, the guy needed a lesson in humility, and I think he learned finally after the Battle for Times Square. But I disgress back to D. BDD: In a nutshell, as good as the Industry are and these hacks Astonishing Entertainment, they are outclassed in this match up. We want the tag team gold, we want to dominate it once again. And guys, once we win the titles again, all of you are being putting on notice. The same goes for every player in the game. Your good, but your not in our level. We defined tag team wrestling in the 21st Century, and we still continue to do so. So in Tokoyo, HBC's homecoming of sorts, you guys will fall victim to the most devestating tag team finisher in the world today....The Syndicator. HBC: Yes, yes..... I am back home to the place where it all started, and their is no way, I'm leaving Japan without Championship gold, tag gold, singles gold, it doesn't matter. Its my time, and no more will I allow these little upstarts, upstage me, especially here....especially now.....Industry/AE get ready.....The Syndicate is back.......in business...
