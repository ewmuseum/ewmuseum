Counter Attack

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th November 2002 A stable war has erupted in eVo. And at the centre of that war, the man who seems to be fuelling it all, is Asis. He has attacked the two stables that are trying to eliminate Unlimited, The Omen Brotherhood and The Notorious, but more specifically he has attacked certain individuals within the groups, members who seem to be important to the groups themselves. At the command of Xander, Asis has attacked Scott Frost of The Notorious more than once now and the experiences have obviously left Frost less than happy with him. At Faith, Asis finally went one step too far, putting Frost through a window and leaving him needing sixteen stitches. This took Frost to the point where at Legends XIV he left Asis with an open challenge, any match, any time. And while Asis must be spending a lot of time thinking up deadly matches that he cannot expose Frost and himself to pain in, he has something more immediate on his mind. From the mind of one man, a lifetime ago Asis attacked him. This man was formally known as Echo BlackWhisper, although in his mind, Asis killed him. Now there is only Xavier Citizen, Omen Brotherhood member, whom Asis must face this week. But Asis is quite sure he can kill him to… To listen to Xavier Citizen moaning about things he has no comprehension of did not bother me at first. It was up until the point where he mentioned the name “Seara” that I did not listen. Xavier, you seem to have some kind of obsession with me and Seara, a lot of misguided conceptions and ideas about me and her. You know nothing, absolutely nothing about me and her. You see, I do not know why I attacked you originally, and yes it was because of her, maybe it was protection, but that does still not change the fact that splattering your blood up the walls made me feel damn good. I was going to kill Seara once and the fact that I didn’t did raise a question in my mind. But now I realise that I didn’t end her life for a reason and that reason will become clear. (Asis’ voice is quite clear, quite calm, it’s quite unusual for him. He is leaning on a handrail of a bridge above a freeway. The snow is falling thick and fast, like ash from a burning sky, carpeting everything in a sea of dirty white. Despite the obvious cold, Asis wears no more clothing than he usually does and shows no signs of discomfort. The time of day is unknown as the sky is blackened by cloud, but it is dark enough for Asis to be outside.) Xavier you think I joined Unlimited because I wanted to claim a legacy? To make a name for myself as a legendary member of The Unlimited? Again, you have no idea, my life, my reasoning for doing anything is never quite as black and white as that. Joining The Unlimited has given me a better chance to hurt people. The opportunity to attack and maim the same person on a regular basis. Now that person could be you, or Luther Wisdom, or any member of The Notorious, it could be anyone. You asked me what the point of the current incarnation of The Unlimited is? I don’t know and to be honest I don’t care. What the purpose of the stable itself is, is no matter to me the fact that I am part of Unlimited is that it gives me direction. It gives me a focus for my anger, Xander points I destroy. And unfortunately, he’s pointed at you. Being Unlimited means that people will hate me more, just because of the fact that I’m a part of a stable that likes to dominate, to control. And when people hate, they attack, and when they attack Unlimited, Asis is sent on the counter attack. (Asis is still leaning against the rail in the same spot and still seems relatively calm, although his steamy breath is now coming a bit quicker, a bit thicker.) So Unlimited attacking Notorious because they are attacking us for no reason is how you justify what’s going on? Do you not see this started as a personal war? Frost and Xander fighting because of Unlimited business in the first place. Seara and me having our own personal dispute. Me beating the living shit into you and “killing” you as you like to say. The stable war started as personal wars amongst individuals, but everyone have united against common enemies, whoever they may be. And as for taking away my one chance to prove myself? You really don’t understand what you’ve let yourself in for Xavier. You do not know much about me and for this reason you should fear this coming Wednesday. But I know you will keep up your façade, you wouldn’t admit to fear even if it did coarse through your veins. You see unlike most, I am not in the wrestling business for personal gain. Victories, titles, fame, fan appreciation, none of that matters to me. All that matters is that when I get in that ring, I make sure that the other guy knows that he has faced me. Pain is all that matters, whether the guy can walk out of the ring or whether he is carried out is inconsequential, but all opponents, win or lose, know that they have just faced Asis. Xavier, you will by no means be any different. Whether or not you deny me the opportunity of advancing in this tournament of puppets doesn’t matter, but you will know that you’ve just faced something in that ring that you will not want to face again for a long time. You said to me that you’re dying and that gives you a reason not to care whether you advance further in the tournament. A kamikaze mission is it Xavier? If I have my way you won’t live to reach the finals anyway. The jobber known as Echo BlackWhisper was killed by Asis. Come Legends Xavier Citizen will Taste The Pain, and will be another man that proved nothing while facing “The Demonic”…
