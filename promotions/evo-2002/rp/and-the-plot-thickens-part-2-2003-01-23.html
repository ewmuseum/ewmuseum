And the Plot thickens...(Part 2)

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.23.02 ~Shocka now stands still looking at the bullet hole he drops the Universal title on the ground and walks over to a bench. He sits down and rests his head on his hand. He begins speaking to himself.~ ‘You know…this prison means so much to me…maybe to much. Every time I have ever doubted my ability in a match I’ve come here to sort out my thoughts…one time I spent a whole week here before my match against Williams for my world title. It did more harm than good though…I got screwed out of my title by 5 cronies…and one conceited president. But not this time…oh no this time I am taking my fate into my own hands and I will lay it all on the line to show Scott Frost who the boss is…he’ll realize that he needs to train in the ring in the eVo lower leagues. He can barely hang with me as it is…but now that I am on top of my game he doesn’t stand a snowball’s chance in hell.’ Shocka speaks in a very serious tone to himself. ~It seems that he is trying to convince himself of something…BOOOM a shot fires through a window and glass shatters all over. Shocka ducks under the table and stands up yelling.~ ‘WHAT THE HELL?!’ ‘You want to try and comfort my nephew?!’ A voice yells. ‘You don’t have the right! I’m going to end you the way you did him!’ The uncle now walks into view as Shocka looks around sort of confused. ‘What are you talking about?!’ Shocka yells… ‘I’m the uncle…I shot the kid…I DIDN’T FUCKING MEAN TOO!’ He screams in a fit of pain. ‘I know…but why didn’t’ you just turn yourself in…’ ‘And go to jail?!’ The man yells. ‘You could get a plea bargain.’ Shocka says. ‘You know what would happen to a guy like me in prison?!’ The man again screams. ‘You’d get fucked in the ass for killing your nephew!’ Shocka screams in rage. ‘I got a gun and you have the nerve to yell at me…’ ‘Are you telling me you’re gonna kill me too?!’ Shocka questions the man. ‘Maybe I fucking will…it’ll be easier this time…I’m used to it!’ The man screams. ‘Shaddap!’
