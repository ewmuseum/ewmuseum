Legistics of Anger

3 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd December 2002 The Rise of the Legends was a time when Asis did not rise, but neither did he fall. He did not predict or boast that he was going to win The Road To Glory Tournament and he didn’t. He was beaten by Scott Frost in a hardcore rules match, although he did not go down without a fight and a brutal one at that. Later on in the night Asis did what he said he would do at the pay per view and that was win the Tag Team Titles, although all did not go according to plan. It was not quite Unlimited vs New-Tv, more Unlimited vs New-Tv and The Omen Brotherhood as Shocka’s partner, Thug roC was arrested and escorted from the ring and replaced by Xavier Citizen. Unlimited won the match and the titles, although in dubious fashion and after the match Xavier Citizen and Luther Wisdom attacked Asis and Jinx and stole the tag belts. Now this week The Unlimited is facing The Omen Brotherhood, but despite the fact that the members of The Unlimited in the match have yet to be confirmed there have been several rumours that Asis will be involved in the match… Things seem to have changed quite a lot in eVo within the last few weeks. There’s a new Universal Champion in L2J after he beat Sovereign and Shocka won the Road to Glory tournament, a tournament I myself was in. So Shocka now gets a shot at the title of his choice after I won the tournament for him by taking Scott Frost out in their final match. Undoubtedly Shocka will choose to fight L2J to prove who is “the best” in eVo. Impact made his return to eVo which surprised even me, as I thought that Sovereign had definitely ended another career. Now they are having a battle of the “monsters”. But despite all of the changes that are going on around me, none of it means shit to me. All I care about is The Omen Brotherhood. Unlimited proved that it is the most dominant force in eVo by winning the tag team titles at the pay per view. And despite all the shit that happened it that match, regardless of who was involved and what happened, Unlimited came out on top. I didn’t trust Citizen and Wisdom when they came up to us at the end of the match to “congratulate” us, but I had been through one of the best and most gruelling matches of my career earlier that night against Scott Frost. My reactions were slow, you caught me off guard. But this week at Legends, if I get my way, you will have to fight me face to face and I will be at 100%. And even if I don’t get put in the match, I will see you at some point during the night and I will have revenge. You see before I didn’t care about you guys whatsoever. You were just another two people who were going to be a focus for my rage, another two people that Xander had pointed out for me to lash out at, another group in this thing called stable wars. But when you assaulted me two weeks ago, you pissed me off. I was not in the greatest frame of mind, not because I lost to Frosty, but because even though I did loose I didn’t hurt him enough. I didn’t injure him, I didn’t cripple him, although I did cause him pain, it wasn’t enough. And you caught me at that bad time and made it worse, not for me but for yourselves. You were going to be victimised by me eventually, it was just a matter of time, a matter of time before Xander picked you the next to be eliminated. But now you’ve got my personal attention and also by stealing one of our tag belts you’ve got all of Unlimited’s attention, especially Xander’s. I’m not gonna warn you for Wednesday to watch your backs because hopefully you’re smart enough to do that anyway. And if I’m not in the match then I’m pretty sure we’ll see each other at some point anyway. See you around guys…
