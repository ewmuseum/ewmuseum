A Long Night Alone.

10 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/10/02 Alone sits eVolution wrestler Jason Steele with a friend on the phone who remains nameless, the man has a familiar voice to most of the former EdW followers. They begin discussing something as the cameras begin to roll. Steele looks up with an angry look in his eyes as he says goodbye and hangs up the phone. He stares at the camera and begins to speak Jason Steele: I look in the eyes of this beloved cameraman and I see fear, fear of the fact that this evil look is directed towards him. He may be smart for choosing the position with this company but he sure isn't smart enough to realize that this angry look is about the phone call that was so rudely interrupted by him. But I have a little message to send out. This company has been looking for talent from all over the globe. But they never came to Canada. Canada had to come to them. That very next day, with a very regretting look in their eyes they signed me. They don't know what the hell to expect because I am just a rookie. The future will show what talent I process. My career is now my future and they have both been placed on the line. My life and my family are the two most important things that I live for and now those too are placed on the line. I am here with this company because I see some potential of greatness. I brag on and on about how great I am and how great this company is but the truth is I know neither. The only thing I truly know is that this is my home. This is where I will spend my life. If my mind changes then my mind changes. Everyone goes through it at least a million times. For now this is my place of employment and my loyalty is worth more than my respect. As I sit here thinking of what I am going to say I feel my heart is racing. Racing to get a quick start in the ring to take on who ever my opponent is. At this point and time I have butterflies in my stomach but at the same time I have an adrenalin rush waiting patiently for a chance to hear that bell ring for me and then ring again with my arm raised as the victor. My life had come to a crashing holt last year when I was devastated after being turned down by the Blue Jays. Now the time has come that I try my other talent and take it to a level never seen before. I don't know how it will turn out but one thing is for sure and that is I will love it. From now on my past is my past and my future is my life. Nobody understands me because nobody knows me. My life is hell. My future was shot until I met one man that changed my life. Brandon Kelley turned me to eVolution; he wanted me to use it as a training facility until I got my skills up to his liking. He saw my potential. I never understood why. Why would someone want an average Joe like me to join a wrestling federation with nothing but great superstars? And then I remember following EdW and I looked around the eVolution Staff and Roster and it all came to me. All he wanted was to seek revenge. Or so I thought. With in months of training before going through the doors to eVolution, Brandon Kelley told me that he had something big and he wanted everyone to see what they missed after leaving EdW. I thought in a way that it was still a way to plot some revenge on someone. He told me he wanted to get back at his wife for everything she did to him. Taking EdW from him and making me file bankrupt. The man was richer than a mutha fucka until that April night. After months of mental difficulties and repairs Brandon Kelley was sent back to the real world. The first place he went was to his former friend Kris Nightmare but Nightmare told him he wasn't interested. His final stop was at my front door, he heard about me in a Canadian Wrestling Magazine he bought while visiting in Canada. It said 'Former baseball player becomes Canadian Hero'. The headline caught his attention as I was in a Canadian Indy federation based in Toronto. I couldn't believe my eyes when I had Brandon Kelley former owner of EdW scouting me to become my agent and trainer. As I finished my match he pulled me aside and we began to speak. After talking and training for four months I still wasn't sure what I wanted to do. I had so many plans and so many ideas. I thought it over and I contacted him last week and he gave me the way to contact Tyler Davidson the owner of eVolution. Well the rest of the story has yet to be finished, but we still have a while to go. The scene fades to black as his voice begins to fade away
