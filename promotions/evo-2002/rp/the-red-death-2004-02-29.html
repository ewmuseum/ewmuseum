The Red Death

29 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-02-29 The tedium wasn’t something he wanted to have to deal with, but he dealt with it week in and week out. King was just another character created by the company, someone they hoped could take the Universal title from him, someone that had been given the chance to stop him from taking away the fantasy. The contradictory nature of the character they’d recently created for King was indeed a clever way of trying to win over the fans, using his own beliefs in a way that the fans would favour rather than oppose. But this wasn’t about winning over the fans, this wasn’t some popularity contest between the two men. As far as the fans were concerned, they probably thought that if they screamed louder for King he’d have more of a chance. They’d already tried that. They tried it throughout the Road To Glory Tournament. They tried it in the final again Matt Hunt. They cheered louder for Hunt, it didn’t work, he’d gotten the Universal title shot. They tried cheering louder for James Kimson at Tainted Love, but it didn’t work then either. He won the Universal title, he was the first to beat James Kimson. And if it went that far, if it went all the way to Unbreakable, they could cheer louder for King then. It would not work. This was not about who had more support from the fans, this was about who was the better man, who was the stronger man. It was down to who wanted to destroy the other man more and Asis knew that he was not going to come off second best. He’d already shown everyone what he could do. He’d picked apart King’s little faction that had been put around him for more marketability. He’d already delivered his message to King personally as well. The little group that wanted to take wrestling back to its roots, make it purist again. Get rid of the story lines, get rid of the glitz and glamour, make it just a battle for who was the better man. It made him sick that something that should anger the majority of fans naïve enough to watch eVolution wrestling was making them cheer, purely because it was marketed in the right way. Because they were doing this whilst “feuding” with him, that somehow was more acceptable to the fans. Maybe they weren’t as naïve as he thought. Or maybe they were just more susceptible to the “good guy, bad guy” thing than having to think about politics. The fact that he wanted the same thing as the people he was fighting didn’t seem to register in the fan’s minds. Yeah he was doing it in a different way than them but the fans weren’t that smart. Besides, he was actually doing it, they were being told to do it… Fuck it! He was thinking about it too much. He didn’t need to analyse it to hell, let the smarks do that, let the fans that give a shit try and figure it out if they wanted to. Nobody else mattered in this, it made no difference if the fans knew what was happening, or the reasons for it happening. It was happening, all the time, every minute, ever second brought him closer to his ultimate goal. Every opponent that challenged him reinforced his message and ever opponent that he beat showed him on his way to that goal. King would be one of those opponents.
