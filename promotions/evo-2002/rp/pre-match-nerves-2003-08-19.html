Pre-Match Nerves

19 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-19 'I'm nervous' Ryan said turning toward his brother. It was the night after the recent house show in which Ryan had put in a jittery performance and having watched the video he could see clear signs of weakness. Now with the mask gone the pressure was firmly on the young mans shoulders. Despite having competed in UWF, Evolution was very much different, Ryan was no fighting in his elder brothers shadow and he had a point to prove having cost his brother so much. Sean had forfeited his status as a wrestling for Ryan, Sean had lost his fan base for Ryan and it didn't take a rocket scientist to realise that Thug Roc was slowly wearing Sean down with his little tirade. During Legends it had been a heart felt plea to the fans. No matter what Sean tried to make Ryan believe, Ryan knew he had to win by the fans for his brother, win back everything his brother had at the cost of any sacrifice. This had started about being all about Ryan, now it was firmly about the both of them. 'Don't be' Sean had replied, 'you'll do fine, you just have to translate your form in the gym through into the match situation. You've done it before and you'll do it again' he said almost stubbornly. 'Your right' Ryan said looking back down at his wrestling boots which he had on still after his gym workout. 'You've faced Bomber before haven't you?' Ryan said attempting to change the subject. 'A few times yea' Sean said with a rhye smile, 'the two of us have a history certainly. In FAW we fought a fair few times and like you will, I came out on top'. 'Your confident then' Ryan smiled. 'Like you should be' Sean snapped, before turning his back and heading off into the distance. Ryan at this point sat alone. Taking his left shoe off he throws it against the wall opposite in frustration. What had seemed such a simple plan, was now testing the bond between both men. Wiping the sweat from his brow he looked down at the Continental Title which sat upon his lap. Sean had given him this also, but three times Ryan had defended it. This was now also the fruit of Ryans labours, it wasn't like this week on Legends was going to be his first match in Evolution, so why the nerves? 'This is pathetic' Ryan muttered. And Ryan knew it only too well. Taking the Continental Title in his hand he held it up to his face. Ryan was now a champion. Ryan was now a member of Evolution. Evolution was not a day care centre, it was not a place for the weak of mind and as Ryan was finding out from the cruel review of the critics you stood out from the crowd if you were weak in any sense of the word. That time round it was a house show and the nerves stemmed from the fact that on Wednesday it was going to be a televised show with a much larger audience. Ryans head slumped into his hands. There he went again. There were those nerves.
