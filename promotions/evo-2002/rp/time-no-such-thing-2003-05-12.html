Time - No Such Thing.

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-12 And so we begin with the hypnotic sounds of Jimi Hendrix’s timeless classic 'Purple Haze'. (The closest we’ll probably ever get to hearing God himself play the Fender Stratocaster.) Why? Don’t question the f*cking champ! Just play the damn song. Ok... as the immortal Jimi Hendrix once said, 'Knowledge speaks, but wisdom listens.', we all are blessed with two essential gifts. One being the weight of expertise, which we project to each other everyday and the other being the safe of our psyche. If you are aware of the aforementioned then you can stop here. Don’t continue any further. All I would be doing is telling you something you already know. For those who weren’t aware of this fact, which there are many here that I know weren’t, this promo is dedicated entirely to you. You see right now we have a federation that is up to its balls in novice’s and newbies. And we all know with amateurs in anything sometimes they need that instructional manual or reference bible that they can depend on to guide them down the road to expertise. So allow me to do you all a favour, not to mention the federation, and take it upon myself to address you directly. In a business that seems to be in a time of renovation and rebuilding there has never been a greater call for practiced talent then now. But forget the industry for a moment, lets check the eVo for a moment. Yes it’s a great federation that actually has a rich past and has built its image on that, however no time has been harder for it then now. Blame it on a lethargic economy, or blame it on a lack of talent one thing is clearly to blame here in the eVo and it is the lack of commitment. The heart has been lost in one of the more depressing times in this game. The last hope is the guys who have recently joined the business in hopes that they can one day be the next Profusion. No one has greater determination than a man with nothing to lose. And I know this quality well. For now I can be the pillar for the business to build itself around. But I won’t always be here to be that pillar. And while I continue to question this business’s dedication to ME I don’t believe there has ever been a greater opportunity here for a new face then now. Because its obvious that even though sometimes a legend returns in this day in time we can’t find ourselves dependent on these people. The time to step up is now. If you are a rookie and you want to exceed expectations and make people turn their head then you better start working on some shit that is new and innovative. Otherwise you won’t get anywhere. Now when I talk about new people coming in I am always anticipating the next legend but let me remind you the road is long and it is difficult. If you plan on coming in to make some noise and bail out when the first speed bump seems a little to big for you to cross then get the hell out. I can’t and won’t tolerate people who complain. I didn’t do it when I was newbie I actually begged for people to hate me. People to hold contempt for me but I never complained because I knew I had to earn everyone’s respect. Firstly if you expect anyone to dignify your promos with look then you better improve with each promo. No one likes watching the same shit over and over again. And you would be doing your part for the federation’s sake if you got off your ass and made adjustments. Otherwise its just a waste of both parties time. And finally watch, learn and take your losses like a man. You won’t become the champ within a week. You can only know what the hell to do if you learn from other peoples promos. Craft a viable style to play the game. Don’t drop some shit time and time again like, ' and so uncle tom enters the scene and snatches the mic from Profusion... Uncle Tom - Now lemme tell you muthah whassahs somin.. (Uncle tom pauses for a moment then scratches his groin and ass. Then brings the mic back to his face..) Uncle Tom - Uncle Tom can tell ya right now he don’t mess around because I’m the toughest SOB in the business and I don’t trust anybody. So that’s the bottom line cause Uncle Tom said so!! Jabronays!!! (Uncle Tom throws the mic down on the mat then exits on his wild hog... Fadeout.) Uh, uh. That shit don’t fly anymore. If anyone can explain why I just dropped a class of wrestling 101 on ya then that would make one person. Now that we got the fundamentals down from the eVo’s one true wrestling Fundamentalist lets learn something even more focal... the smack. Cut the damn music. Now put the Jimi Hendrix away. Lets begin to kick this shit with some of that undying Profusion love he likes to drop on the opposition.. [We fade.. What? Cliché? Not imaginative enough? Well we fade in from the bottom. Aight muthah fuckah we fade in from the bottom as we stop and marvel at the man that has innovated the game. The man who has taken this another level. Improved upon what was handed to him and made it that much better. As we await another Profusion Picture we stop and be in awe as we recognize what this man has done and continues to persevere for the sake of the federation. We fade in from the bottom to find the cameras welcoming us to a men’s restroom. The bright lights compliment of the white tiled restroom. We take notice of two sinks with mirrors in front of a stall and us directly to our left, which sort of obscures the rest of the view, as it is very prominent in the foreground. But of course the one thing that sticks out the most is a tall dark man who is facing the camera. This man has appeared in only one other Profusion promo that wasn’t in the eVo but emerged as a cameo in one for Ryan Gibson’s Bar Cellar. He stands seemingly impatient in a black knit cap and large black Fubu jeans. He wears a pair of white Nike Air Force One’s with a complimentary a Cash Money Millionaire t-shirt and a dark blue School Of Hard Knocks denim jacket. He also sports a diamond ear ring on both his ear lobes. Yes the Libertine in all his malevolent glory. Again he seems to be waiting for something or someone when finally the sound of a stall or toilet is heard when a man enters the picture in the background and heads over to the sink to wash his hands. This man seems to be representing Dickies as he is largely dressed in the products merchandise. He is dressed in navy blue Dickies double knee work pants with a pair of all black and white old school Addidas with a black hooded fleece jacket. In the mirror you can see his reflection which you will find a hand stitched Houston Rockets cap. Before he turns on the water words are exchanged.] Libertine - You heard about the latest from the eVo grapevine? Profusion - What’s that? Libertine - Brotha it seems the federation ain’t too interested in catchin your promos. Profusion - Come again? Libertine - Man they putting words in your mouth, for real. Profusion - ..... Libertine - According to the eVo you were quoted as to bein very 'anxious' and 'very nervous.” Profusion - ..... Libertine - And check this brah.. your name ain’t even spelt right. [Profusion turns on the sink and begins to wash his hands.] Profusion - Tha hell?... How the hell you mistake my name? Somebody better bust out with a spell checker two years ago. Libertine - Brah I think eVo ain’t too excited about your last episode of 'Truth or Consequence'. Profusion - Apparently... Libertine - You ready to do this? [Profusion takes turns off the sink and reaches for a towel cleans his hands then disposes of the discarded paper towel. He then turns toward Libertine who is facing him then begins to walk in the direction of him and the exit.]
