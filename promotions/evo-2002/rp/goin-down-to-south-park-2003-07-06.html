Goin down...to south park...

6 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-06 ‘What’s the big fuckin’ deal bitch?’ Shocka could be heard erupting in laughter as he looked into the mirror. ~He had seemed out of it right off of the bat. 2 bottles of Skky vodka were spotted in the corner of the room. One empty and the other in Shocka’s hand half empty he laughed and took a sip. He gave a grimace and shook his head before speaking again.~ ‘That’s some good shit…’ Shocka dropped the bottle and you heard it shatter he laughed and smiled at himself in the mirror. He reached down to pick up the bottle and when he reached his hand back up blood ran from it. He looked down slightly shocked and sat back for a minute. ‘Holy shit…’ Shocka studied his hand before screaming. ‘NO! THE VODKA IS ALL OVER THE FLOOR! I better get the vacuum and suck that shit up…’ Shocka ran out of the room and began searching for his dust buster. ~Crash! The sound is the prelude to Shocka walking back into the room he smiles and just laughs. He begins speaking about Legends.~ ‘You know what…I got a few words for you Xander and you Greatness you are boner biting bastards uncle fuckers….you suck big floppy donkey balls…and I hate you.’ Shocka smiles and looks around the room. ~You can see Shocka collapsing and then you are whipped into a dreamscape the setting? Well its hard to explain it looks like choppy construction paper animation…kind of like South Park…oh my god what has happened! Shocka walks down the streets of South Park a small Colorado town. He his completely in character and you can see Cartman, Kyle, Stan and Kenny standing waiting for there bus to school.~ ‘You guys suck.’ Cartman spoke angrily. ‘Fuck you fat ass.’ Kyle replied. ‘Fuck you, you stupid jew.’ Cartman snarled back. ‘muffle, muffle muffle.’ Kenny. ‘Kenny’s right guys you two should stop fighting.’ Stan spoke as Shocka stopped and looked at the boys. ‘Holy shit!’ Kyle screamed. ‘What dude?’ Stan asked. ‘That’s…that’s that wrestler guy from eVolution! It’s…uh…’ Kyle was at a loss for words. ‘He looks like a big retard.’ Cartman spoke. ‘What the fuck?’ Shocka asked. ‘Where in the hell am I?’ ‘It’s…Christoph!’ Kyle shouted. ‘Hey! Its Erin Christoph everyone!’ Stan shouted. ‘Fuck you I’m Shocka.’ Shocka spoke. ‘Who?’ Stan asked. ‘Oh you mean like this…’ Cartman said doing the Shocker. ‘I bet your mom has had that done to her more times than you shit a day.’ Kyle spoke. ‘You fucking jew.’ Cartman spoke. ‘Why in the hell am I here?’ Shocka asked himself. ~The bus pulled up and Shocka boarded with the students because it was better than walking. He proceeded to lead a chant on the bus causing it to be pulled over resulting in everyone walking. Shocka took a beating and was thrown in a dumpster by the angry 4th graders.~ ‘You’re a boner biting bastard uncle fucker!’ All of the kids sang as they marched away like a little army. ‘God damnit!’ Shocka screamed as he climbed out of the dumpster. His cell phone rang and he answered it. ‘Hello? What in the hell do you want?’ Shocka spoke into his phone. ‘Well I don’t care if eVo is doing promotional shit here or not…’ Shocka slammed the phone and began walking as he spoke to himself. ‘That son of a bitch Greatness is going to pay with me Kimson and Seara all gunning for him and then you throw the Universal champion into the fray and you have all the ingredients for a blood bath.’ Shocka spoke with a sort of evil smile as he stopped and laughed. ‘Things are about to change.’ ~Dreamscape ends…Shocka sits up and looks around very confused as the scene fades.~
