Date: 3.16.03 'I can't believe you locked the keys in the damn car Buuday! I outa kick yo ass right now!' Furious reigned the present expression on Sheeca's face as she stared down Buuday with eyes of threat. 'I'ma choke you, stab you, then KICK YOUR ASS!' 'CALM DOWN! Damn, it wuz a motha fuckin' accident! We got a damn cell phone call lil' Thug to come pick our asses da fuck up.' | Sheeca brushes her silky brown hair out of her face while getting her cell phone, on the other hand, Buuday pulls weave from her purse and begins fixing her hair. All Sheeca can think while dailing the number is, 'I'ma whoop her ghetto ass!' Other, and other, and other again. | *ringing* *ringing* *ringing* *ringing* ............ *dail tone* 'That motha fucka ain't picking up the phone! BUUDAY! I'MA KICK YOUR ASS!' | White people walking by begin to stare and some even calling for the police on the sly. | 'Oh lord, now look wut ya did! Now you KNO' them police go be rollin' up any second now dat those white people callin' da police over YO loud ass!' Thinking of an idea, 'You kno' wut, they myte jus' been wut we needed Sheeca, nice lookin' out girrl.' In the motion of leaning against the car and flicker her horse hair out of her face. 'Yup, they comin' any fuckin' tyme now.' Still trying to convince myself they were coming... The sad part is I knew they weren't coming. 'You kno' wut be sum fuck'd up shit?' 'You mean more fucked up then locking us out the damn house, melting in the sun, and being concerned about Crips coming and shooting us the fuck up? What ever could that be?' Lipped with a tone of sarcastic remarks. 'Funny *fake laugh: ha-ha-ha-ha :straight face* No.' Continuing, 'Wut I wuz gonna say is dat it'll be fucked up if they locked they damn keys 'n they car.' Followed with small laughter. 'Don't even play like that, you know good and well they shopping, well Sheeca is... Buuday is a ho.' Thinking, 'Hey, didn't you give Ali a key to your apartment?' 'Hell motha fuckin' naw! They my doggs 'n everythin', but fuck around and come home to a house straight jacked!' I eye balled Duzza giving an assumetion. 'HhmmmUh, I kno' you stole my damn chain nigga!' 'You still on that shit?! I told you, this ain't yo chain! It may say Statuz Quo on it but still, it doesn't mean it's yours!' Not realizing how off that came out. Coming out of my ugly, perplexed look given to Duzza, 'Anyway. It's hot as FUCK out herre and we have to put Team Network 'n check.' 'Team Network?' Goes harder in thought, 'OH, you mean Team Crackaz; OH ok... We have to fight them?! Damn...' Trying to keep a serious face, we gave in and bursted out laughing.
