Getting A Contract - Part 1

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-14 It's a night I will never forget. It's the night I heard my name come across the pa system one more time. The pyros went off, the flames shot up, and I quickly made myself visible in the spotlight one more time. I love the buisness and I can't get enough of it. I go from fed to fed proving that I truly am the best man in the game. One by one they fall as I step my way up to greatness. The night I won't forget though is my tryout for Evolution. Like I said when I stood under the spotlight for one more time, it felt so good. The fans didn't know whether to cheer for me or to boo me. Either way I didn't care, because I was back in the spotlight. I posed for the fans as the camera flashes went off around the arena. People where shocked that I was here. I was just thinking, how long will I be here. I could start to smell the odor of the dirty people in the crowd. It was getting to me. Oh, that awful smell. I taunted them fans the whole way to the ring. I got in the ring and just waited for my opponent. Right then his music hit. I looked on as a very small man came out from behind the curtains. I thought to myself, this can't be my opponent. If it is, Evo has to be joking. The man charged the ring like he had something to prove. As he slid into the ring, I put my boot to his back and held him to the mat. The little guy couldn't move. Right then I felt myself getting hit in the back of the head. I crumbled to the ground. As I was falling I turned my head to look up and their I saw a monster of a man behind me. I thought to myself, I have been tricked. I am the guy that does this kind of stuff. My arms began to shake as I felt the adrenaline running through my body. I got up and we battled all night long, untill it was time for Final Justice. Yep, you know it. My arms where raised in victory. I felt I put on my best performance ever and I was pretty sure I was getting on a contract. Towards the end of the night, I searched the back for management, but I didn't have any such luck. I sat in the dressing room all night, just waiting to hear the good news. No word as of yet. I felt frustrated. This is the first place that kept me in the dark. I thought to myself, what is going on. I took my shower and got dressed. The dressing room was starting to fill up, because it was the end of the night. I quickly left in disgust. I woke up today not thinking much about last night. When all the sudden the phone rang. Am I getting a contract?
