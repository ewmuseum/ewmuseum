Fire.Laying The Flames To Rest

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The lifelessness paced slowly, as it gazed outside it's viewing screen. An environment of beauty surrounded him, it's tears streaking the ground all around. There was something in the air, which allowed this loss of care. For a time now the same actions were repeated. All in preparation for the defeated. Time had fore told this event long ago, and finally the time had come when it was time for it to end. As the heart quickens it's steady pace, it is only a short time longer before the race. The passages that fill us with hatred soon grow full. And it soon will be time to tell, who the victor is, and what his prize truly is. The mutual respected belief died long ago, tread with grief. Perhaps something greater has been in store, and time now to end this forlorn, game of chance began long ago, for what is written isn't so. Perhaps this is the true enemy which holds me in bondage, the feeling of knowing what lifelessness cannot accomplish. Weary one can slowly grow, with no place left to go. It's time I make my claim, but not for the prize of fame. Lifelessness now does want this story simply to end. Far too long has it grown to higher proportions, as clean conscience's grow heavy with contortions. ...It is here that I grow in anticipation Shocka. Our match will soon be upon us, and soon our decided fates will be decided. I've grown tired of this game, as I've let you know. But no more. The time has come for us to end this game. But a game is played for amusement. This has been a tale of pain. Your pestilence disgusts me. You simply can't go at it alone can you? There always has to be some insurance mechanism, someone always there watching your back to make sure the job gets done. Your failsafes seem to always work, right on time too. I walk in alone, you come in with your army. For the last time. This will be the final chapter. We began the tale so long ago it's beginning is a mere blur in the passages produced by us. The finale will certainly surpass that which was our introduction. I have longed for this day for many a time coming. You have surpassed me before, but those times were different. In front of thousands, calling in the assistance of your futile ally, another enemy into my foray. Your twisted mind games have more than filled me up. My saving throw was the distraction I need to show you that you are just as weak as I. One small plan that had enough power driven into it to make you question your ability. And now the time has come. We have risen, and shall fall together. But one will make it through. The victor has been chosen not, and when he stands atop we shall see which one of us he truly is. No matter what happens, the dance will have ended, and the children will return once again. Lifelessness dragged itself back again, as it's woven path seemed to never end. There was always a twist that one could take, giving just enough before his bitterness would truly break. And as it crumbled like the grains that were, it began to grow evident who would incur, the wrath of the hated, the pain of the jaded. As the flame which began long ago finally wore out. As the flames were laid to rest.
