Untitled

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'This is unbelievable, I cant believe everyone thinks that I killed the only woman I have ever loved and truly cared for...' Morpheus says with sound of weary concern in his voice. With an astonished look on his face, Morpheus sits with his back and head leaned against the wall and his eyes half shut. A small smirk shows and disappears again, he breathes deeply. 'Shocka had to of framed me... There is no way that he turned into some kind of hero or good guy over night. Shocka is nothing, but a crooked bastard!' Morpheus curses in spite of the emotional situation. 'Who in their right minds would murder someone with the most obvious, incriminating weapon they could possibly choose? A lot of this makes no bloody sense and I will get to the bottom of this...' Morpheus sounds fully confident in himself with a serious look on his face. Closing his eyes completely, Morpheus attempts to relax and not let this horrendous news about his girlfriend, tag team partner and one half of the eVolution rogue tag team champions eat away at himself to badly. Morpheus generally seems to be pushing away his anger, emotions and any tears that any normal person would express in this situation. Only showing his cold emotion. 'You think just because I am the deadliest athlete in the game that I would take things so far as killing Sky Night because she chose some tool like Shocka over myself?' Morpheus questions while staring in bewilderment. 'Huh?' he screams out more aggressively. The rage built up until the final point before melt down with Morpheus, he pants heavily and the left eye starts to twitch again. Without one sane or clear left thought in his head, he runs his hand over the thick stubble across his face and he hesitates a moment... 'Shocka has stepped far over the line with me and there is no other explanation for this... I will not rest until I find the truth and make the sorry son of a bitch beg for forgiveness before I torture and follow to deprive him of his live. Jonathon, you took the only person I have ever cared for and for some sweet icing on the cake you are pressing these charges on me for your actions. I look very lowly upon this and I will prove you dead wrong!' he shakes his head. The creaking of a door is heard and Morpheus shoots his head to the side and his eyes stare that direction wide. Sick laughter is heard and Morpheus is left speechless with an overcastted shadow falling upon him, like a blanket of darkness. A grin widens on the face of Morpheus quickly as he comprehends who is standing in front of him. 'It has been too long since the last time we have spoke!...' Morpheus nods his head like the presence of the person has enlightened his day. 'When I heard exactly what you were up to, I couldn't pass down an opportunity of the past coming back to haunt Shocka!' the man says while stepping forward; an unfamiliar man to eVolution. 'The all knowing, supreme being Hedges... That's one name and face I am definitely glad to see and hear of. The fallen path of destiny lets us encounter each other down our twisted journeys again. Who would of ever thought?' he shakes his head with an amazed, sick look on his face. Pushing himself up the wall and rising to his feet, Morpheus steps forward and both men lock hands tight and squeeze tightly. Both men look at each other snarling and have serious looks on their face. 'I send my condolences to the old lady, but in the end I guess she had what was coming to her...' Hedges states bravely. 'WHAT???' Morpheus screams loudly. 'You think I hung that whore?' 'You have me all wrong... I don't think that's your style, more like that little chump Shocka's. I heard how she turned her back on you for that little maggot. Teach your own Morpheus, just live the life of ups, downs and all around... You know what I mean?' questioning. 'So how goes the battle?' Morpheus changes the subject... 'Its treating me alright, what is there to complain about...' Hedges laughs and steps back and breaks the hand shake with Morpheus. 'The man who beat ol' shockalaka 6 times and only lost once to him in return... That's an impressive track record. You come back to take care of unfinished business?' Morpheus asks. 'Only to see Jon's flame finally die out... You know his run ends here and now with you. The punishment he will endure from stepping toe to toe with you will be the final straw that breaks his back and ends his career... Shocka is nothing, we both know this' Hedges laughs. 'The little punk thought he was squirming when he decided to face his limits and press the envelope with me... Wait until he finds out an old friend has come to pay him a visit. Hah ah ah!' Morpheus says with an evil chuckle. 'This week, he has pulled his last stunt with the obvious set up with killing sky night and taking the pussy way out of his beating, he will be crushed from the high horse he thinks he proudly sits on. I know that conniving little hoe, he would do something exactly like this... Cant face the music so he tries to have you put behind bars, Fuck!' Hedges says with sound of anger in his voice. 'Playtime is finally over and the living nightmares have begun, finally the end of one that will bring life to another... The end of one, will bring back life to something greater. I mean that in so many ways it makes me sick to my stomach. You don't have a bloody clue Hedges...' Morpheus shakes his head and has a disturbingly, odd voice. The scene fades to the odd turn in this unimagined, story of reality...
