The Mark of A Fighting Champion (Part I)

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-30 'Dean I need some tapes' I said as I stood in the production truck about two hours after the conclusion of New Dawn. The production truck as you can imagine was cluttered with tapes filling both the shelves, the desks and any other available space imaginable. Dressed in a pair of kharki's and a loose fitting shirt, this was my final stop before I head back to the motel. 'You need some tapes of what?' Dean said turning on his chair to look toward me. 'Of this Malik guy' I replied stepping over the threshold closing the door behind me, 'the only time I saw him was when I took his arm and tossed his ass out of that cage' I grinned. 'Right' Dean replied, 'congratulations by the way mate.' 'On what?' I asked. 'The caged rumble' he answered, 'third is something special and I gotta say don't look like a man who just went through hell and back.' 'Thanks' I said stuttering slightly, 'so the tapes' I said putting us back on topic. 'You know its weird seeing you out of your mask again mate' he said as he reached into a huge box of tapes sitting infront of him; 'I'm suprised it doesn't leave a mark'. 'I'm not' I replied dismissivly before pausing suddenly, 'I mean why should it?' 'Well I would imagine it puts a fair amount of pressure on you' he replied. 'I guess' I answered turning away as I glanced at my watch. 'Oh I'm sorry' Dean apoligised, 'heres me harping on about your match and your probably in a rush. The tapes are in here somewhere' he said continuing to dig deep in his box, 'what about if you leave me a contact address and I'll have them delivered to you before dawn'. 'I'd appreciate that if its not too much trouble' I smiled, 'you got a pen?' I asked digging deep into my pockets. 'Yea somewhere' Dean said reaching forward taking a pad and pen for me. 'Do you mind if I get an autograph from you at the same time?' 'Sure no problem' I said taking the pen in my hand, 'any particular message?' 'How about signing it as the number three man of the caged rumble?' Dean smiled, 'thats got to be something your proud of so why not start milking it.' 'Nah man I couldn't' I replied somewhat taken a back. 'Sure you could' Dean replied. 'Nah really' I said as I scribbled just my name no the second page, 'I've too many accomplishments I'm proud of can't single that one out' I said struggling for words. 'Oh yea, I understand' Dean replied without question. 'Anyway' I said handing back the pad and paper fully completed, 'I'd best be heading off'. 'Aight man' Dean said with a friendly wave, 'have a good one' he smiled. By this point I was ready to leave. For many reasons I just didn't feel in the mood for this barage of questions. I had to meet someone and congratulations were definately required when I did. Tonight had been much more about them than me.
