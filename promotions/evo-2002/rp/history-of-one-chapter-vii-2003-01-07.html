History of One :: Chapter VII

7 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/7/03 The food was great... I even had seconds.. And thirds.. I didn't know when the next time I would eat was, so I figured I'd get as much in me as I could.. Considering I was off the highway and in what looked to be a decent sized town, I didn't worry too much about shelter now.. The guy that helped me left.. He didn't say a word to me, just left.. Whata guy really. I didn't think anyone would ever do something like that for me. Seriously, who was I? Just some punk kid looking to get away from everything. He didn't have to do anything for me, but he did.. Back than, I didn't give a fuck what he did for me.. But now.. I appreciate it.. Without him, who knows when the last time I coulda ate was. After I ate, I didn't bother staying the truck stop much longer.. I started walking again.. I had no real direction or where I was going.. Still no one was looking for me and I didn't care.. Where was I going to end up? Thats really all I was worried about..
