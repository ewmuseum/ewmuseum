Suicide is Painless. 1.

31 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-31 Human beings stop being human beings after they live for a while. Human beings become machines. We all become machines sooner or later, spiritual machines if you will. School, it teaches us to be highly advanced machinery that of which are used in whatever job we choose to take. A lot of people ask, 'When will machines exceed human beings?' Well, they already have. And there's no one to blame but ourselves. A lot of circumcised men want to restore their foreskin. They want to be all natural again, or whatever. They feel like a piece of them has been stolen. So, if it's possible to restore our foreskin... is it possible to restore our humanity? --- I drive along the road with a somewhat glazed look in my eyes. The road, which is illuminated in neon light by the nearby casinos and restraunts, seems to be playing tricks on me. Making me see things that I don't really see. Making me hear things that I don't really hear. Making me think things that I don't really think. Suicide is painless, so says the voice in my head. Usually, I might stop at one of those neon lit casinos to do a bit of gambling with Lady Luck. Usually, I might stop at one of those neon lit restraunts to grab a bite to eat. Usually... but not tonight. Tonight, I'm more concerned with what the voice is telling me. Tonight, I'm going to go find the highest building in this town so I can jump off and die. Just like a lemming. Suicide is painless, so says the voice in my head. It's late. How late? I don't know and I don't care. Some old Elton John song plays through the radio. It's depressing and fits the moment well. You can practically feel the melodrama in the air. For whatever reason, Terlayne Sterling comes to mind and for whatever reason I wonder if he'll be the one to prevail in the Cell Title match at the pay-per-view. Not that I care. For whatever reason, Damon O'Halloran comes to mind and I wonder if he'll be the one to prevail in the festivities. Not that I care. For whatever reason, Judith comes to mind and I wonder if she'll cry when she wakes to the news that her brand new boy toy is now a splatter of blood on asphalt. Not that I care. Suicide is painless, so says the voice in my head. This is like a drug hallucination. The inside of the car is spinning and I'm beginning to think that the road isn't there. I begin to go into the wrong lane and a car swerves to not hit me. This is like a roller-coaster ride. Another swerves, but barely scratches my car's new paint job. Sooner or later, I get back into the right lane. The man in the moon is laughing right now. Laughing at me. Death seems nice.
