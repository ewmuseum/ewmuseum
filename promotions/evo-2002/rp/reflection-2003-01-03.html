Reflection

3 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.03.02 ~Reflection; if you were to look back at your life as whole what things would you want to change. What things would you want to rip out of place and disregard them for good? Good things, bad things. things that make you sick? Every person has there own fair share of these things but one man in particular is stacked up having to many sick things in his life. The man that is spoken of is the man Jonathan McCade.not the wrestler but the man. He watched his mother die on the street from a gun shot wound and he knew that there was nothing he could do to stop it. He vowed revenge on the people that did this and even though it took him 20 years to do it he did get it accomplished. He can never change the moment of watching his mother die. But he can sit in the calm reflection of his vengeance. Some people cannot hold grudges its simply not in them. But a man like Jonathan? If he sees something he wants he will stop at not length to get it. The Universal title for example; he saw the title. He knew from the moment he entered eVolution that he would make his way to the top no matter how long it took. If he takes the time to convince himself that he will be the champion then he will be. If he does the convincing that’s need to beat Frost…to not let up at any moment, to simply not care about the well being of Frost then he most certainly will. He has to be in the right frame of mind for these things of course, but how could he not be? He is the greatest wrestler in eVolution everyone looks up to him hoping and praying that they can some day live up to the standards that he has and will set. This is enough to make an already inflated head burst…what will the explosion bring?~ ~His rise in the wrestling world has been not so well documented before eVolution he floated around smaller federations, he let his anger go in small intervals. But now? Now he will let it go in leaps and bounds. He showed what he could do against L2J without the help of the man that is devoted to him. The man that brought ‘devotion’ into the life of Shocka. Now Shocka’s devotion lies deep within the history of holding such a title as the Universal title…he thinks back to the days of EdW where he accomplished nothing and watched the champions there. He watched them here in eVolution and now he gets to be a part of that heritage…that greatness.~ ‘Devotion?!’ A voice screams. ‘I’ll show him devotion!’ Shocka screams as he now slams his fist on a desk in a hotel room. He smiles for second before turning his head. ‘Scott wants to know what devotion is…I will show him devotion when I go all out at him…I will hold nothing back just because I feel sorry for him. I mean he is the under dog going into a match with me. Thug roC was not devotion for me…I simply used him to get into the position that I wanted and look it worked. My real devotion is to my mother and proving to her that I am something.’ He says thinking of his mother that has passed on. ‘Not many people can understand what I have gone through…not many kids have to watch there mother die right in front of there own eyes…and much less from a shot fired by her own brother!’ He screams as he stands from his chair. ‘No mission?!’ He speaks to himself. ‘What do he know?!’ ~Shocka pulls his wallet out of his back pocket of his pants and looks at a picture of his mother. He smiles and glares down at it before darting his head upwards and screaming into the mirror.~ ‘I am on a mission…a mission to the top of this federation…sure I have arrived but all of the glory that I want has not yet arrived. Scott Frost I am on a mission of destruction…I am going to destroy your life and make you wonder why you ever poked me with a stick! I will make you ponder from a hospital bed why you wanted to see if the anger in me good raise so high. Once I step through those ropes in 5 days you will see a completely different man from Rise of the Legends…and this time I will do it all on my own. I won’t have that annoying little fuck roC at my side shouting instructions. Half the time I just ignored him anyways. Frost as well as the rest of the superstars in eVolution will hope and pray that roC comes back to my corner because now that he’s gone I will focus all of my attention toward the outcome of a match. And you will see an even longer winning streak than I have now. It will continue on and on…’ He laughs. ~A look of hate comes over Shocka’s face as the scene slowly fades away.~
