TooLate - The Desolate Road

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/23/03 Settle down His body aching from the fight, Jason’s mind took complete control as he drove his vehicle towards the new evolution event – the one he had been waiting to come for over a month. The desolate road was all but dark, with a whistling desert all around, the silhouettes of mountains standing in the distance. A few cacti and desert wildlife stood like ghosts out in the sand. Only the glare of his headlights could lighten this eerie darkness basking in the desert evening. I won’t hesitate to hit the highway A never-ending row of lonesome power lines seemed to follow Jason on his way, each of them shooting by like a bat out of Hell. The sounds of small rocks being thrown against the car emanated from under him, the tires tossing a million little particles of earth through the air. All of this surrounded Jason. All of this took place, and entered his ears from waves flying around his head. All of them were heard. None of them were acknowledged. Before you lay me to waste The radio had played all the way since Boston, a song’s rhythm echoing through the car for thousands of miles. The song playing right then, on Jason’s drive down the desert highway, was “Getaway Car” by Audioslave. Through the darkness of the desert, Jason could stare into the eyes of a reflection. He could see everything. Not just the blue or the black hole within, but all the emptiness inside of his skin. All the crying that he pushed back. All the fear that he didn’t bother to show up on the surface anymore. There was nothing left to for him to care about. Except one last fight with the one man who was untouchable. Settle up But there was nothing for him to restore. His life was empty, as it had been for the past few years, but there had always been that glimmer of hope. Hanging in the darkness like a swinging light bulb, waiting for someone to walk by and turn it on. But now all of that was gone. No hope remained in his empty heart. Someone gripping a bat walked by, and smashed the bulb into shards to die inertly on the damp pavement of the room. Now he walked the corridors of fate, waiting for someone to come out from behind a corner, clutching a knife in their bloodied hands. Was there any way to escape this awful doom of his? If there was… And I’ll help you find …he couldn’t find it. The night was too thick for any soul searching. The car was moving toward destiny way to fast for sticking your hand out the window, seeing if you could catch the essence as it flies above your head. The only radiance, besides the headlights, was the twinkling stars in the heavens above, watching from their nightly haven. Watching the entire world make love to their desires. Watching a small, insignificant car driving down the road. Something to drive Jason’s ride dragged on for hours now. His hands were sweaty, staining the steering wheel with perspiration. All this time, a song played in his heart, loud enough for him to feel a strain from everything that happened before him. And as the words of Chris Cornell hit the air… Before you drive me insane … a beeping, red light shined through from the fuel gauge. The needle aimed itself at the E. The tires, once moving about ninety miles per hour, slowly died down, before stopping on the road. And the whole time the car came to a stop, Jason stared at the gas station that seemed to just miraculously appear to welcome him.
