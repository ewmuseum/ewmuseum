The winds of change......

19 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-19 'Taking evolution by storm......eroding it's very foundation one brick at a time, to establish a new foundation.' Malcolm King starts off as members of his army The BodySnatchers, QBX, Luther Wisdom, and The Tone look on..... 'Change my friends is inevitable....the very name of this federation begs of it. We spent months and months playing these political games using powerplay after powerplay, it is time for the wrestlers here in evolution to take back what's rightfully theirs. Someone had to be proactive....and as always that someone was me. The tag division, The Cell Division, Evolved as well as Continental division are all under lockdown with the necessary pieces in place....that leaves one, the Universal Championship....and my good friend Xander.' Luther Wisdom steps to the forefront as QBX answers a cell phone call....... LW:'Hedges...my proverbial partner in crime or should I say the bile that I will use to clean the Evolved division with? You were impressive winning both the tag belts as well as the Evolved title and look at what you get as a reward......ME. Now I know that you may share a certain envy of me that can easily be explained away as a genetic and racial endowment; however this is not the time nor the place for such pillow talk. You see you need me more than I need you. I have long ago been written off as a failure so the expectations going into my matches are understandably low...however now with your fate intertwined with mine like a cheap whore your barometer of success here in evolution coincides with my mood. To say I hold your career in my hands is an understatement because in all honesty I owned you from day one Hedges. Now that I am my own man it provides me with a new sense of power a new sense of purpose that suggests that maybe I'll start taking this wrestling thing seriously.....but then again how can one deny their very nature. I like to hurt people....it's almost orgasmic Hedges, ask Seara about our climax....ask Fiona....wait a minute is necrophillia your game because that is the only way you will be elicit any response from her. The bottom line is that I cross the line more often than not, pray my hatred for you gets the better of me because if I hate you I want to prove I am the better man.....the better champion. However if I'm indifferent towards you.....then you can say hello to fiona in the next life. The evolved and tag titles mean nothing to me......however now they have a purpose in my life. Now they are my instrument to get to you...they are my instrument that will allow me to destroy the one they call Hedges; they are my instrument......of hatred. They are also an albatross becuase they will forever link me to you....the bile beneath my wings.' QBX hangs up the phone and speaks to Malcolm King..... QBX:'King that was her......she's coming to Evo......' King:'Great.....Xander, my dear friend...the man who ended my wrestling career in the Cell....I hope you had time to think over my offer. We could be of mutual benefit to one another. However if you need a peace offering I will provide you with one at the Legends before a New Dawn......' Dolo Bentswinger:'America's Guardians, a crock if I ever heard one....what makes you think you are in the same division...the same league. You quite obviously impressed somebody...because now we must face you in a two out of two sweep for the Snatchers number one contenders series. The outcome is already a foregone conclusion as you face the epitome of the American Dream.....power and money. You should have slapped whoever got in your ear and told you you were great; because all they were doing was setting you up....for failure.' 1001 Holds:'Time after time we are in big pressure, big money situations...and yet your claim to date is that you were able to fend off a couple of savages. Your step up to the big leagues will be short lived as you will see in about two weeks. The snatchers are taking a clue from Twisted Reality....take them out, before they get you. That means you AmericanGuardians......that means you Heartbreak Chick and Big Daddy Dreamer. Yeah HBC/BDD...you have what rightfully should be OUR spot at EVO1. Relish it and enjoy it while it lasts because once we are done with America's Guardians....you are next.' The Tone steps up to the mic...... Tone:'I'm the best....thank you come again.' King:'Is that all?' Tone:'Why should I go into detail when they already know? D-lo Brown stated you better recognize; I will allow my actions to speak for me. I'm already bigger than this business; they are just too stupid to realize it yet. You know the tag line King.....I set the tone, all others just follow it.' King:'Confidence is the only way we will win this war, and believe me Evolution a war is coming. Never again will we play the role of the oppressed. It is time for us to rise up and take what is rightfully ours. Tyler davidson is a changed man.......but I've known him and S. a long time and the man I affectionately call 'shadow' may not be so different than the devil we all knew. The pieces are in place.....as my gambit, my final gambit is set into motion. Now is the time after one year to see who has evolved....and who prefers to die amongst the weak.' fade to black
