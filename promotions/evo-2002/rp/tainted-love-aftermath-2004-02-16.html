Tainted Love aftermath

16 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-02-16 There are some days that one remembers within history, events that are forever marked by the annuals of time, and occasionally certain events transcend no other to become legendary. Tainted Love was such an event of truly global historic proportions. On that single night, every match was historic in their own right. Asis became the Universal Champion, a quest he has been on for over a year. The monster within Sovereign returned, sending a shockwave through out the industry. Malcom King having internal problems again. Big Daddy Dreamer winning one for all the wrestlers who have ever been screwed over by Tyler Davidson, and lastly, the most historic of them all, The HeartBreak Chick toppled Thug Roc at his own game. The biggest shock on the night, Roc-TV is dead, the myth of Thugganomics crushed in his own match. The self-proclaimed best Cruiserweight in the industry kicked down a peg by another crusierweight, and a female one at that. At Tainted Love, the icon became a legend, transferring herself to immortal status, a monumental player and an unstoppable force to be reckon with. The crowd goes absolutely nuts when ‘Faceless’ by Godsmack erupts through the PA system as HBC and BDD decked out in their usual wrestling attire make their way to the ring, clearly with something to say. Together they slide into the ring and marvel at the tens of thousands of fans cheering in approval for their tireless efforts. HBC and BDD can only look on in amazement for this is a defining moment in their career. They look at each other and smile as HBC hands BDD the microphone and allows him to speak first. “Now I know all of you are dying to hear HBC speak, I don’t blame you and am looking forward to it myself, however I’d like a few moments of your time as well to address something’s today.” Slowly the crowd begins to settle down as Dreamer waits to speak again. “I want to poise a question to you, how many of you enjoyed watching Tyler Davidson tap out like a little bitch!” Of course the crowd goes nuts. “That’s what I thought, and I’m with you on that sentiment. Now we know at times we haven’t always been the fan favorite, that we’ve done things that are a bit controversial, but face it, deep down you wouldn’t want it any other way would you. No. Love us or hate us, you know we are simply the greatest team ever to happen. We bring ratings, we entertain all of you and we pack the house every single night which is why it was so bittersweet when I clamped the crossface on Davidson and made him tapout. Man that was a good feeling. A rush unlike anything else. Truth be told I think that match will be in my top three of all time because it was personal to me. I really felt like he was ruining not only the tag division but all of Evolution as well. That match was for me, for you, and all the men and women who Tyler Davidson has screwed over the years. He was destroying this establishment of superstars, so I had no choice to cut him down to size. Hopefully now Davidson has learned from mistakes and to fear his dreams because next time, he’ll be tapping from his hospital bed trying to reach his nurse after I get done with him again. Now, without further adeu, lets give it up for the one, the only, the HBC!” The crowd goes insane when HBC takes the microphone from BDD and kisses his cheek. With the biggest ovation ever heard, she tries to speak but soaks up the cheers as they call her name over and over again. Finally they die down long enough for HBC to speak. “Tell me, was that the greatest match ever or what!” Of course, they go nuts again! “I told Thug Roc not to mess with HBC. I told him that I wasn’t interested in him, well in a relationship sense, and how does he take it, not very well. It seems as though his little roc does his thinking for him. Little roc, well more like a teeny tiny pebble. In fact, I think that’s what I will call him, pebbles. Pebbles tries to thank me by taking a steel chair and hitting me over and over with it. Oh how manly, how chiverious, how pathetic! No wonder he has carpal tunnel syndrome in his wrists. Pebbles idea of a three-way is his left AND right hand. When he raps about sliding into home, that’s his codeword for needing more lube because he’s callousing. Seriously, what kind of retard thinks he can win me over by hitting me with a chair and then putting me in his kind of match, a streetfight of all things. It must have been the lack of bloodflow to the brain if you know what I mean. So Pebbles challenge me at Tainted Love thinking he’ll somehow prove his manhood to me. What happens? Pebbles gets rejected again, at his own game since he probably doesn’t remember the outcome, can we roll the footage please?” Tape rolls of HBC against Thug Roc match at Tainted Love, especially the ending from different angles. “So Pebbles, I’m going to put this in a language, a lingo even you understand….its not Roc-TV bitch! Its HBC IS A BITCH and as you people so eloquently…..YOU GOT KNOCKED THE HELL OUT!!! So its over, over between you and me and I officially declare myself as going after the Platinum Championship followed by becoming the first ever female Universal Champion! All hail the Queen of Evolution!!!”
