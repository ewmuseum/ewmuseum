The Return

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 22, 2002 ***The scene opens in a small Canadian Suburb just outside of Toronto, Ontario, where a community is in awe that another one of the many children have grown up, and moved on in their lives, doing something that they love. This week, Eric Badger has changed his career choices for the better, when he decided to come back, and become a wrestler again, instead of modeling for a living. He has gained 50 pounds in body mass over the past few months, after developing an eating disorder while he was in the Playgirl mansion. He got into a fresh mindset, and turn the excess fat into muscle, which convinced Daniel Somers to allow him entry back into Evolution. An alternate plan was though of by Somers, who paid Eric to attack Jason Jameson at the Pay Per View entitled, Digital Destruction. The plan was a great success, which allowed Asis to retain his title, and Jameson to suffer a defeat. This has started a new feud between the returning superstar and a current Evolution master. Only time will tell of what these two combatants will do to each other, starting off with a tag team matchup, scheduled for Wednesday September 25th, 2002. The next edition of Legends will feature Jameson and Badger, as they are on opposite teams with predetermined partners. Jameson teams with Shodan, as Badger teams with Shocka. This should turn out to be a classic slobberknocker of a tag team match, which will spark the madness of Jameson and Badger. We journey inside a house, where a family has sat down at the dining room table, and has commenced eating their dinner. Halfway through the meal, the mother begins to shed a tear from her right eye, which catches everyone at the table off guard, as they cease eating. As he is at the table, Eric Badger knows that his mom is crying because of him. She had a rough time getting through his modelling phase, and is happier with him following his dream to be a professional wrestler. He gets up from the table, and walks over to his mom, and gives her a hug. He clears the table, and takes all the dishes into the next room, the kitchen, and places them in the dishwasher. After the emotional dinner, Eric says one last goodbye to his family, before heading to Pearson International Airport in Toronto to catch his departing flight to Miami, Florida to wrestle in his tag team match at Legends IX. He grabs his bag off the bannister in the main hallway and opens the front door. He walks down the path, right next to the taxi which has been patiently waiting for the past ten minutes. He puts the bag in the trunk, and gets in the backseat of the cab. It takes off down the narrow, suburban street, and makes its way onto Highway 400, and heads South for the airport. Once the cabby and Eric arrive at the airport, the time is 1:30 am, and he checks in for his flight. Flight 743 is the one he wants, and it will be departing at 2:15 am. When this time rolls around, the plane has almost been booked full, and Eric enters the plane to see that his second class seat has been taken. He walks up to the front of the plane to tell the stuardess about the fixation that he has been placed into, and she gives him a first class seat instead, claiming the rich man that bought it had already called in sick for this particular flight and will be back tomorrow morning for the next one. Eric agrees and sits down as the plane gets ready for takeoff. Away they fly, as Eric Badger prepares for his Evolution re-debut, in the tag team match. The plane arrives in Miami the next morning, and Eric is still drowsy from the flight. He is escorted to a limosine located at the front of the airport, and he reluctantly gets inside. The limo takes him to a nearby Hotel, which is almost four blocks away from the American Airlines Arena. He grabs his bag, and walks into the hotel, to get himself a room. He is given a room on the ninth floor, and is taken up there with a bellboy. They take the elevator up to the room, and crack open the door. Inside is a full, decked out room, furnished with everything a Canadian man would want. There is free food, a king size bed, big screen television, and of course, Canadian Beer!! Eric tips the bellboy with a twenty from his pocket, which gives the boy an angry look on his face.*** EB - 'What's wrong kid? Don't you like money?' BB - 'What do you mean? I love money, but you gave me a Canadian twenty you asshole! That money is worthless here!' ***Eric takes the money back from the kid and kicks him in the ass until he is far enough out the door for Eric to slam it. The door closes and Eric runs and dives onto the bed. He notices that there is a laptop on the table for him as well. On top of it, there is a note. It reads, 'open me', so he does. This logs Eric into the Evolution database, where the Timelines are kept. The built in webcam starts rolling as Eric realizes what the hell is going on.*** EB - 'Well, I guess this is where I talk trash about this Jason Jameson guy. Well first things first, it's great to be back, but the times have changed, technological advances have been upgraded I notice. It's awesome to be back on the road with my former superstars, but once the show starts, its all business. Jason Jameson, you might think that I picked the wrong guy to mess with when I was paid to kick your ass, but you better realize that I'm more than just a bitch to do Daniel Somers bidding. I'm one tough mamma jamma, and once you piss me off, I'm indestructable, just like when you're pissed out of your mind on a weekend long bender, and you fall down the stairs, no pain what so ever. You and Shodan against myself, and my former enemy, Shocka. I haven't heard much from him in my travels over the past little while, and I hardly know anything about Shodan, except for the fact that you and him were good buddies back in the day of EDW. Being good buddies won't help you here though. The brutal fighting ability of Shocka, against my physical, technical and high flying skill, it's just boggle your mind, and hurt your body. When it's all said and done though, Jameson, you and I won't be finished, not for a long, long, long time. Asis owes me a debt of gratitude, for saving his ass from losing his title to you. That's right, you probably would have picked up a victory, but that's just too damn bad. Jameson, I've seen you prance your ass around here for quite sometime, and it's about time you were put in your place. I've been thinking on the flight over here to Miami, and I'm thinking, why don't we have some sort of special match for Legends X? The tenth one in the set, something big should be planned. So I was thinking, you, me, a shitload of weapons, and whatever else you can think of, and I'll prove my 'Too Xtreme' attitude as of late. The ball is in your court Jason Jameson, are you going to shoot? Or are you going to lose your shot at glory and pass the ball?' ***Eric then turns the laptop off, which locks and loads his Timeline into the Evolution Database, allowing all fans and wrestlers in the back to view what cards he has placed on the table. He lies down onto the extremely large bed and drifts off into sleep as the scene fades to black*** FTB
