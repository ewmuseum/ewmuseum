Dreamer

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.17.03 ‘Turn around and face me like a man.’ A voice said with very harmful intent behind it. ~A dreamscape now rises around you revealing two men trapped inside the dark place of one man’s head. A place where he stores the almost forgotten memories of his dead parents. People he barely remembers.~ ‘And what if I don’t?!’ The second voice questions. ‘You remember what happened to your daddy?!’ The voice calls. ~This makes the holder of the dream and the second voice spin hard with rage. It was Shocka he knew if he came back to this place in his mind that it would try and fight him off but he had to escape it all.~ ‘Listen here you sorry son of a bitch…’ Shocka demanded. ‘Calm down fella, I was only joking!’ ‘You’ve known me my entire life…do you think that I enjoy the humor in this whole situation now!?’ The voice of Shocka literally shakes the scene. ‘Why so angry Jonathan?’ A female voice speaks to him slowly. ‘Sky?!’ Shocka questions as the man fades away only leaving two. ‘We are the only one’s here what is going on inside of your head?’ the voice calls as the female body slowly comes to fade. ~Shocka sits up and darts his head back and forth listening quietly for any sign of life inside of his hotel room. He finds none and plops back down on his pillow before again sitting up. He moves toward the bathroom to turn on the light and only see himself. A voice is calling at him but he cannot seem to find it…perhaps it’s the guilt riding his soul to hell…or maybe its something more, someone calling from beyond the grave to inform him of plans.~
