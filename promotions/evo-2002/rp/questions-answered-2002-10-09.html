Questions Answered!

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-09-02 Faith No More! Jason Steele is seen sitting on the front steps of his home in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. He looks around as a man with a camera steps up and a beautiful woman begins walking his way. As they get closer he makes out that the woman is Jennifer Carter from eVolution. The first thing that comes to mind is that she wants an interview so he stands up but she tells him to sit back down for she wants him to be sitting for this interview. Jennifer sits down on the step in front of him. She begins to ask some questions about how he feels about being in eVolution. Jennifer Carter: Well Jason, how does it feel to be a part of this organization and having your life long dream come true? Jason Steele: Well to be honest being a wrestler was never a dream of mine. It was just a back up plan if being a baseball player didn't go through and well as you can see it didn't work out. See being from Toronto, I am a huge Blue Jays fan and as my life long dream was to play for them but when I went to training camp last year they released me saying I just didn't fit in their line of players so I pushed that aside and joined a wrestling school. In high school I was involved in only two sports, baseball and wrestling. Football just wasn't my thing. I began wrestling and playing baseball at the age of eight. I have been training since. Since a shoulder injury my baseball skills have been in a wreck. With wrestling they train you to heel that injury faster so I am hoping to make a name for myself in this sport being I have followed and been involved in it for most of my life. Jennifer Carter: Ok, next question. What is your goal here in eVo now that you have a signed contract? Jason Steele: My goal in eVo? Do you really need to ask that question? I am after what everyone is after these days. My goal is to take that Universal Championship and strap it around my waist making everyone bow down before me and call me The Champ. Jennifer Carter: With your dad being a penicostil priest you must be loyal to gods word, right? Jason Steele: That is an interesting question, now to be honest I was loyal to 'gods' word as a child but as I grew up I grew out of that. I finally figured out that in the realistic idea we have two choices during our life, and no it is not are we going to heaven or are we going to hell. The two choices are made later in life and those are whether or not we will become ashes or if we will be food to the worms. Seriously, we have all been to at least one funeral, do you see them releasing the person to heaven or hell? NO! because neither exist so to answer your question honestly, no I am not loyal to god’s words. I am only loyal to my own words. Jennifer Carter: Wow! Moving on, now in your first match is there anyone in this federation you would like to face more than anything to start off your career here in eVolution. Jason Steele: As a matter a fact there is and that would be fellow Toronto native, Eric Badger. Why? Because we are both Toronto Natives. A battle with in Toronto I guess it could be called. I just feel it would be great to show my parents how great I am by facing another Toronto native. Jennifer Carter: Ok final question and I will leave you with the camera man taping you for some final words. Jason Steele: Hit it with the final question, I am ready for anything. Jennifer Carter: There has been speculations or rumors you may call them stating that you are being aligned with former Era of Destruction Owner Brandon Kelley? Would this be true or would this just be a rumor that need to be put to an end. Jason Steele: Well, Ms. Carter all I can say at this point is that I am a follower of Brandon Kelley's work and I have had contact with him but I will not state whether or not he is my manager or my agent. Everyone will just have to wait and see. I hold a lot of loyalty to eVo, but I have told you all that I will. Jennifer Carter: Well thanks for your time Jason. Jason Steele: No problem, come again maybe next time it can be over dinner. Jennifer Carter: I highly doubt that. Jennifer Carter leaves his presence and enters the van as it drives off and he begins speaking to the camera man. Jason Steele: The future of this company is bright but the future of my career is even brighter. You all ask if my connection to Brandon Kelley is what brought me to this company. Well soon you will all find out that my connection with Kelley is purely business. We have been discussing some things for the past six months. Now that I have gotten my foot in the door of eVo those discussions may stop or they may get heavier. Only time will tell and only I will find out first. You think you know what I am about.... but you have no idea. My career is a long way away from being called legendary but soon I will reveal my talents to the world and you will know quickly how big of a star I shall be come. You wait and see. My talent is hard to find, you will watch and learn from me as I watched and learned from the Canadian greats. Wait and see, you will see my name in the Pro Wrestling Hall of Fame and your children will worship me as their King. A Canadian Universal Champion is what this company needs to become great and that is what they will get, sooner than you think. Now cut that camera off and get off my property... The camera fades to black as you see Steele yelling at the camera man.
