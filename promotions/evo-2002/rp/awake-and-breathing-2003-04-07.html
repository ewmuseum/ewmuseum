Awake and Breathing

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7/03 Now Here it goes So I opened my eyes for the first time, and what did I see? I saw that bitch that Jason has been crying over for years and years, no end in sight. There she was, standing before me, hear eyes watering and her smile forming, as her man came back to the land of the living. I really wonder if she even suspected, in those few minutes, just who I was or where Jason had gone. I guess I’ll let her know eventually. But for now… Sex is sex. “Jason!” she yelled. She then took her body and threw herself forward, lunging towards me with a hug only love could provide. Too bad I didn’t return that love for her. She meant absolutely nothing to me, and neither did that ring she held on her finger. Whatever love she held inside of her when Jason was alive was now buried with him. Weird thing was… well, I felt like I owed her my life. Shit, after all she was the one that got raped in the first place. Without her, I’d only be a thought running through Jason’s head every so often, and I’d never have the chance to exist today. In fact, Jason would be married right now, probably carrying a kid around in one of those little knapsacks and shopping at Banana Republic. Thank god she saved me from that. “I didn’t know what I would have done without you.” she said, a few tears falling down her face. A sick amount of pity came through me, but I dismissed it immediately. No time for shit like that, I’ll tell ya. That’s not me at all. Stupid Jason and his stupid emotions. Bastard. “Well you don’t have to worry about that babe. I’m all right. No harm done.” Well, despite the monster headache I woke up with. I suppose that was just poetic. I gave Jason his fair share of them. “You just went out in the middle of the ring, and they rushed you to the hospital. I ran to my car, and followed that ambulance, and the whole time I was just prayed that you were ok. That I would…” Shut up God damn it. You’re voice is so loud. “It’s ok, Marilee.” Saying her name to her like that was quite weird, but I guess that doesn’t matter by now. I’ll have to get use to it. “I’m perfectly all right, and nothing is wrong with me. Isn’t that right doc?” Some stupid, useless nurse looked over at me, and nodded her head, her lips covered with pink lipstick never breaking loose of its form. Stupid bitch. How I’d love to just get up and slap her around for awhile. “I had to go back to Boston a few days ago, but I called up and they gave me some extensions on my exams. I wanted to be with you and make sure…” Here’s my chance. Sex be damned. “Oh that’s ok. You should go back to Boston and take them. I’ll be all right for a few weeks.” “But… Are you sure? I would prefer to make sure that nothing happens to you.” “Yeah, that’s quite all right.” I said, thinking of Evolution quick. “I have some business to attend to anyway. Some stuff that I prefer you not see.” Nice touch, Jay. Nice touch. “Ok then. I’ll take the flight around eight tonight. But I’ll stay with you until then.” She leaned over and kissed me, definitely catching me off guard. First kiss, technically. But that didn’t mean that I was a stranger to it. She pulled away. And that’s when I got the look. A look that, in all the time I had watched her through his eyes, I had never seen before. Confusion mixed with worry. Marilee glanced at me for a few seconds, and I knew what she was wondering. Same body. Same eyes. Same mouth. Same saliva. But different kiss. “I’m gonna get us something to eat, Jason.” she said, the emotion leaving her face as she was sure that it was just a weird mind trick or an effect from my injury. Shit, I have to be a lot more careful. Marilee walked out of the room, leaving me alone in this new world of mine. I think it was then that I realized that I was alive. Alive for the first time. Seeing the sunlight. Feeling the warmth of the bed sheets. Smelling the… God damn, what’s that smell? Fucking hospitals. Today is Monday. Tomorrow is Tuesday. And then comes Legends. What I’ve been waiting so long to see first hand. And what will begin my conquest.
