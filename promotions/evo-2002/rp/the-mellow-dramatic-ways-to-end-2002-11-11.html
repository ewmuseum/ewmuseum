The Mellow dramatic ways to end

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 11, 2002 'Persecution.' calmy the voice of Scott Frost says. The scene opens up with Scott Frost standing in a dimly lit room, starring into a mirror. He looks to be very upset, and he runs the fingers of his right hand through his seemingly freshly breaded hair. 'The Omen Brotherhood. Unlimited.' again the calm tone of Frost says. 'It's funny. Unlimited hates Notorious, because the leader has single handedly taken out practically every member of Unlimited out.' Frost smirks. Frost shakes his head. 'First, Erin Christoph. Second, Xander. Third, Jinx. Next, Asis. The count is three and zero with the Frost verses Unlimited scoring. I challanged Asis to any match of any type at any time, to prove that even knowing that Unlimited will back him up, I am going to kick his mentally ill ass!' Frost says very enthused. Frost smirks. 'But the Omen Brotherhood. This is a trip. This is a tag team, managed by probably the weakest slub in the history of sports entertainment. I mean, they put their strategic plans in the hands of a man that lost to Xander? A man that couldn't keep poor and defenseless, Xian, alive. This man is nothing more than a pure one hundred precent fuck up. And Xavier Citizen, and Luther Wisdom want to follow his wishes? I mean, Citizen already thinks that Unlimited and Notorious are one in the same? Atleast Wisdom is wise. I mean, his meditation is prolly the only thing keeping him sane when he is around Citizen. They are trying to say that we aren't even a 'stable' to be entered into the 'stable wars'. Frost finishes snickering under his breath. Shaking his head, Frost straightens his composure and begins to finish. 'Do the math Citizen. How many wrestlers does the Omen Brotherhood have? And then Count how many that Notorious has? You have, yourself and Wisdom. Then you have, one half the Universal champion L2J and the queen of wrestling Seara and yours truly in Notorious. Three to two, you do the math. If we aren't a stable fit for the stable war. Then you sure the hell aren't a stable fit for the tag team division. So do yourself a favor, go fuck yourselves and stay out of Notorious territory.' Frost demands. The scene fades away as the cell phone of Scott Frosts' rings and he picks up. 'hello?......' To be continued....
