Train of thought

October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th March 1987 “O’Hara you can do ten times better than you maggot, you want to keep you title don’t you! Ten more reparations” Shouted Staff Sergent Miller. Staff Miller was the reason that Jack had reason from nothing to heavyweight champion in 3 seasons of boxing. His way of some how motivating, his recruits was next to none, most of the boxing squad, hated him, something rotten, his training schemes would involve running a 10 mile cross country run in the snow, then straight into the gym for a painful hour workout. Miller broke many, within the first 8 weeks of the training season, but whatever he through at O’Hara it didn’t seem to faze him. “Come on you, you good for nothing piece of shit you call those press ups!! I have seem your Mum do better press ups on my dick, O’Hara your nothing! I tell you your nothing! You call yourself a champion” Jack through his head back and looked up at Staff Miller, before knocking out another set of Press Ups, he wasn’t doing for Miller, he was doing it for him, Miller was just someone that he hated and wanted to prove wrong. Jack would hit the showers wanting to simple ripped the head of Staff Miller and shit down his neck, but this is what Miller wanted, he wanted Jack to hate him, he wasn’t in this game to make friends he wanted a monster to be dominate in the ring. In Jack O’Hara he had it, he had what he wanted, all he had to keep doing was feeding the fire of hatred in the boys body and we would keep winning, cause each time Jack went into the ring, he would envision his opponent to be Staff Miller. Slapping his hands together, “Get the hell outta my gym, not one of you have impressed me today, expect another mile added to your run tomorrow! What your still fucking here, Get the fuck outta here or I’ll add another mile” The gym quickly emptied, Jack was the last to leave, he collected his towel, and headed for the showers. “I’m better than you, Miller and you understand that” “You say something, you piece of shit?” “No Staff”
