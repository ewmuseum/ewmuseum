Number One!!!

9 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-09 The scene opens outside of a major mall near the up comming arena for Legends. Walking in the parking lot is QBX. He is wearing a black yankee's hat titled foward and up on his head. He also has on a white Sean Jean t-shirt with some baggy blue jeans. He walks over to a black explorer where he then looks around as he sees the eVo cameras every were. He then walks away from his door as he walks behind the vehicle over to the eVo cameras. QBX walks over as he smiles and begins to ryhme.. 'This Legends.. Ill send more than one.. to that place called heaven.. I have commited so many sins.. way more than just seven.. I see that numbers are the topic.. who's ganna be number one.. it want be any of yall.. little jerk offs need to visit the hun.. quick you need to run.. yall think its a fist fight.. while I hang with nas... I got myself a gun..' He pauses as he smiles in his gangsta mentality. He then takes a short break as he does nothing but smirks at the camera..as he cnotinues and says.. 'We got three guys.. who have obviously lost it mentaly.. if they ever believe they could beat me.. first we got a PRIEST... I guess he needs to be in the church.. comes legends he will be ridden in a hearse.. Ill go ahead and call the coroner.. start digging up the dirt.. You want be a problem.. this is ganna be easy work..' Takes a pause as he now begins on the other opponents. He then says.. 'Who we got next.. think its Sean Sterling.. funny thing is.. I got SEAN on my shirt.. bad thing is.. he aint one of Pdiddys kids.. he needs to go back to whatever he did .. Ill have him hit the matt so hard.. have him see shit he aint never seen before.. I dont care what you think of me.. Ill have you in a different state eXstacy.. the one you dont come back from.. Ill tell you about it.. You will feel all the pain in the world.. Its like your privates are broken off.. you cant even think of a girl.. make you want to leave this world.. so I am going to say good bye to you.. move on for now.. but dont feel blue.. late on Ill get back to you.' Another pause as he continues with the members of this match.. 'Last and the least.. Marcus Escobar must be in love.. cuz he is ganna be swept off his feet.. looking at the ceiling.. he trys to move his arms.. but he has no feeling.. he is motionless on the matt.. what to do.. just move your head.. let everyone know your not dead... better yet..look up at me.. see that ended it all.. the man they call..QBX.. come legends..everything will be put to rest.. not only will there be three lost souls.. after that..there will still be one KING.. left to GO!' QBX smiles as he walks back to his door and exits the parking lot in his car as the scene fades to black..
