First Blood

30 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-30 'First blood, the stipulation of all stipulations. The one to show the world, who has the weakest body. The most frail of them all. Who will it be? Will it be Jay Jay Jameson? A man, who has suffered a concusion through me. Or, could it possibly be the man who rose up quicker in eVolution than any wrestler to date, Scott Frost?' 'I've been doing my homework. I've studied eVolution since the day it debuted. I was just as shocked as anyone, when Joshua Krowe took me. Some rookie from the streets of Fort Wayne, Indiana and gave me an opportunity. So far, I am pitted against two former Universal champions. Two of which hate each other, and ironically used to be best friends with one another.' 'I've done my homework.' 'What i've realized is, the further Scott Frost goes. The more determined Jameson is to one up him. Even when they were friends, Jameson asked for a title shot. Got it, and won. One week later, Jameson lost the title again to Scott Frost in March.' 'I've done my homework.' 'I've noticed that, Scott Frost is a very emotionally open man. He loves this business, he loves his friends, he loves his wife, Seara. What he hates the most is being betrayed. His friend betrayed him, and an all out war broke out. Now, they fight for First Blood.' 'Why am I taking such a deep out look into this?' 'Because they are my enemies. Anyone, and everyone who thinks that the most charismatic man in the WORLD; can't cut it in the business, are my enemies. I chose these two, because as I was told, by Joshua Krowe. I needed to make a name for myself. I needed to set an example of what is instore for eVolution.' 'I've done my homework.' 'And the thing that I noticed most of all, is when two men are pitted against each other with the purest of anger. I have an opportunity. An opportunity to prevail. I've studied their behaviors. I've watched their actions. I've even hired people to watch them and let me know what they do in their free time.' 'What did I come up with?' 'I came up with a solution that will make everyone happy. Well, almost everyone. If I take out Scott Frost and Jameson. I take out, two people who have achieved something nobody else has. I took out two former Universal champions. Something that nobody has ever accomplished. By taking out Jameson, I clear the path for people with the same kind of ambition in this sport as me. By taking out Scott Frost. I set a path for pure destruction. Everyone guns after James Kimson now that he owns the titles. But nobody but myself and possibly Jameson really understands is, Frost has held the title more times than anyone in the business. He is bound to acquire that championship once, if not more in his future. Therefore, once he is gone. That worry will never have to be delt with again.' 'I did my homework.' 'Do you think they did theirs?' -Charisma
