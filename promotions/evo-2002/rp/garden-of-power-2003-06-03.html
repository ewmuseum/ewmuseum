garden of power

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-03 From the Ashes produced the most brutal action packed evolved title fight ever witnessed. Four men battling for one belt and only one walked out, that man was Hedges. For weeks he promised to bring the belt home to New-TV and he delivered. This time he was not screwed. He climbed the ladder to bigger and better things while three men lay at his feet… The scene opens up outside a large mansion in side a large garden. It’s filled with crimson colored roses and is fenced in by a large hedgerow. Butterfly bushed and brightly colored wild flowers grow in abundance. In the middle of it all sits a table covered by a green umbrella and sitting on a chair drinking a beer with his feet propped up on the table is Hedges. The evolved title lies near his feet on the table as the camera comes closer. “So glad to see you…” Hedges exclaims in surprisingly nice tone “You know it’s been about a week since I won the evolved title over MLX. What a great feeling to finally not be screwed out of my rightful gold. My time to step in evolution has come. I brought gold back to new-TV. I received a raise and claimed victory over the man who has slipped threw my grasp a few times here in eVo. Not this time I literally made him fall down the ladder of success crashing to the bottom” Laughs Hedges. “Profusion hurt his poor little neck when I shoved him off the ladder…Boo hoo hoo…Dane showed some surprising ability in his attempt to capture the title but I was far to focused and prepared to win this title than to lose it to a rookie. I’m sure I’ll meet him again one day in ring but first I have to defend my title in some unusual circumstances.” Hedges says rather annoyed. He takes a moment and swigs down a bit of his beer “I don’t just have to pin someone to keep my title I have to win the third fall. I could end up sole tag champions and lose my evolved belt. Some BS stips if you ask me because MLX wants a rematch for his belt. Sure I’ll give it to him but don’t think you’re going to have work to survive past the first two falls. As far as Big Daddy Dreamer and heartbreak Chic are concerned I have twisted reality behind me. Worried about this match hardly…Annoyed a bit” Hedges says as he sighs “Whatever if that’s what comes with being champion here then I’ll do everything in my power to retain my belt and keep gold in New-TV. Right now three battered and worn out men are about to step in with three fourths of the most powerful stable Evolution has ever witnessed. I should just say I’m going into this match full steam I’m on a power trip since winning this title knowing now I can’t be screwed out of it yet I have full power to screw others like MLX did so many times to me. Not that I need to screw anyone because I am the all knowing supreme being and that’s why I am that much better than all of evolution” Hedge says with a laugh… “Now get off my property!” He says angrily before leaning back and enjoying the rest of his beer
