Legends

30 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-30 Toronto, Canada Syndicate International Headquarters Early Evening The cold blustery air has finally swept through the city streets, and though the citizens are abuzz about Christmas and St. Nick coming to town, they know it’s minor. In fact more people are abuzz about their own hometown team The Syndicate going for the Evolution Tag Team Championship at ROTL. But in a strange ironic twist of fate, this week legends has them teaming with some rivals, leaving everyone to wonder, just what the hell is going on with this match. Can both teams co-exist eventhough the belts will be going to The Syndicate. Can they get along despite having a history with The Industry? Only time will tell. But one thing is for certain and that is The Syndicate are getting ready for this match as they have countless times over preparing for every contingency that the eVo might throw at them to derail them from their ultimate gameplan. As we enter the 102-story towers of Syndicate International, BDD and HBC are busily preparing for the four-man tag match this week. Not bad for the legendary team like them with a stellar reputation. But as HBC finishes her set on the butterfly machine and BDD finishes benching 425 twelve times, they both stop and relax for a few moments. A staffer runs in with some messages for them and as they flip through several are from St. Violence. They just roll their eyes and toss them in the trash can. BDD grabs a water bottle and downs it, parched from his workout. HBC on the other hand walks over to the kick boxing bag and fires off some final round house and straight kicks before ending her session. Together they head over to the locker room, take a quick shower and freshen up for the remainder of the evening, seeing that they have dinner plans at nine-ish. BDD comes out all ready to go in his Armani suit that fits him like a glove. HBC walks out in a stunning low cut blue dress. Together they sit down on the plush oversized leather sofa imported in from Luxembourg. They smile into the camera and begin to address the latest state of events eVo. ‘Hello fans, its everyone’s favorite dynamic duo, BDD and myself, The HBC, collectively know worldwide as The Syndicate. And before we begin this weeks latest monologue, I want to congratulate my partner here on hissoon to become two-time Tag Team Champion. Now its almost common knowledge that we had alittle history between all our teams. Truth is, were the best, no one else is in this Industry (Sorry Guys, but it’s true, its true). But that isn’t our problem, why should it be, after all were not the ones that made them suck, they did and just as my partner showed last week, Awol still sucks, which is why Evolution is questioning them about giving them a title shot in the near future. They aren’t good enough to be part of us. The most dominating group walking today. I guess they figure that if they could help save the eVo from us and the rest of our group when the fact remains, they can’t even save themselves and sucking up a ring royally. Quite sad really. But enough about him, lets address this week match that we have against the current Evo Tag Team Champions, St. Violence and two jobbers. ‘I’d love too C. As my long-time partner in crime in as already mentioned, yes we do have a history with St. Violence and hell it must go back about nearly a year by now. Back and forth, we’ve been upping each other, though we are better at it especially since they outrighted STOLE the most dramatic finish in wrestling, the collapsed cage trick. But hey, imitation is the sincerest form of flattery. You copying us every week and our antics just proves once again why we are the best in the world at what we do. And at ROTL, once again shall we hold the tag title gold. Its avoidable just as the whining around here from certain so-called management….eh hem,….Davidson… First things first though, the tag gold and then I shall put Davidson in his place once and for all. But this week really is just a routine warmup for ROTL. We go in, kick some ass, watch a botched finish because St. Violence does truly suck and make them pay for it at the PPV. What more is their to it….. other than perhaps Thomas Draven showing once again why we picked him to be our manager because he is more deviant than we are…….. either way it will be interesting and horrific to our opponent. All in all….DAMN GOOD TELEVISION!!!
