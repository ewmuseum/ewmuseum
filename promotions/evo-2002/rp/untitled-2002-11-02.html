Untitled

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 2nd I thought I would be happy. Honestly, after that night when I destroyed L2J, and I did that everything would finally work itself into place. I would have crushed all my past demons and now live for the future. For the chance to finally make some true history and stop living in the past. I suppose that in an odd way of dealing with things I can’t ever chase after the things I always wanted. Every time something came that I thought was needed someone always stuck their nose in my business. That’s what L2J is to me, someone poking their head into your office door when the Boss has come to ask you to “take care of so and so” or to make sure that “such and such gets done” and then L2J sticks his head in and says “I can do that boss, just give me the chance!” finally I guess someone took him for real. The blank screen snaps into view, standing there is Sovereign, still with bandaged arm and head wearing clothing that he’s probably been wearing since Legends went off the air last night. Sovereign and L2J as co-champions certainly has a lot of people confused and as the show went off air last night it was L2J and Sovereign brawling with each other until separated by eVo staff and arena security. With that being said we know that these two must continue in the tag tournament from before Faith, and added onto that we know that at Road to Glory on November 24th they will once again lock horns for the Universal Title, that being if they manage to hold onto it. I know what your thinking. How in the name of God will L2J and Sovereign exist together as champion. Hell, I don’t know. I doubt that we can, because even more then before the sight of that freeloading son of a bitch makes me wretch and want to slam him into a moving truck or something like that. L2J, I will be honest with you, you stood your ground at Faith. But you know just as well as I do that you are no match for Sovereign. I took everything you gave me and it was my own move that let you get wheeled out of that arena with your arm raised and then let you walk into the arena on Thursday and be handed my title. Sharing it with you L2J is worse then losing it to you simply because as I learned as a small boy I don’t share very well. I tend to grab and snatch, and then when little L2J cries to Mother I generally beat the hell out of him. Nothing new there I suppose L2J, you’ve been at the receiving ends of my assaults for sometime now and Faith took the cake. I make you bleed more then you thought you could bleed. I threw you off the top of that cage through the announcers table. I burned you, I beat you I made you go through hell for 37 minutes. It could go down as the greatest match of all time, and it was you that took the worst of it. I saw you out there on Legends L2J, your in pain. I have taken beatings worse then that and wrestled the next night out of the need to eat! And although I don’t doubt you’ve paid your dues I’m going to tell you this right now that at Road to Glory you and I will go once more. And if it’s for the Universal title you can bet your bottom dollar that it will be solely around my waist, with only my name on it after the most drawn out, bloody and brutal match that could ever happen. Because L2J, I will destroy you one way or the other at Road to Glory and it’s not going to be quick, it’s not going to be painless. Just when I think you can’t go anymore I’ll help you rediscover how far the human body can go before it just can’t take anymore. You will stand across the ring from me, a product of pure luck and you will leave that arena one step away from death. And if the match isn’t for the universal title then I gladly will say that your costing me of that title will warrant you an even worse beating. Because I know that no one in eVo can beat me. No one has beaten me one on one. I have yet to be pinned and I’ve beaten everyone in eVo worth beating, including you L2J. So I suggest that you don’t drop the ball. Not only for the fact that losing the title for yourself would be bad enough, but having to answer to me at Road to Glory will be a fate worse then death. I’m not going to make deals with you L2J, I won’t watch your back, I won’t help you in your matches in any way. The thought of saving L2J from the drama of a Universal Title match almost seems... wrong. Yes, losing the Universal Title on a technicality is amazingly unfair but then who said life is fair? I sure haven’t and I’m not going to anytime soon. I’m the product of unfairness in this world regardless of what topic you want to discuss. But L2J, I hope that you know what is coming for you. Because I want you to see me at Road To Glory, across the ring from you and know this time. I want you to be prepared this time, because this isn’t going to be some run of the mill match. No, it’s going to be something quite interesting, innovative and for myself a lot of fun. There will be no tricks, no fooling around this time L2J. You have to survive for 1 month with me some how or the other and then at Road to Glory it all comes to an end. Sovereign pauses and moves right up to the camera, he places his face in front so it’s a head shot only. L2J, you have been the proverbial thorn in my side for far to long now. And the wound your leaving is getting infected. It’s time to do something about it. And while you are going to live with me as a parasite for at least one more month I want you to know that after next month’s “really big show” you and I, and this little Co championship thing will all come to a violent and bloody end. Because I am standing here, in front of a camera and all I can think about is looking back and seeing that look on your face at Faith. Yeah, you know the one that I am talking about L2J, not your little smile, it wasn’t your “I am ready gosh darn it, I know I can do it” face. The Fearless one’s poker face broke, and I saw it in your eyes, you were afraid. Afraid of me and what I was about to do to you, you may very well have been prepared for a brutal contest but you are not fooling anyone out there when you say that you were ready, and looking forward to the House of Pain. You smiled when the cage went down, smiled a smile of if I’m going down I’m taking you with me. Because you knew L2J that you weren’t leaving that arena on your own. You knew that you weren’t going to be wrestling at Legends and you probably had a pretty good feeling that your career was in great danger. And L2J, I want you to remember that feeling, because no matter how much you prepare mentally, or physically for Road to Glory you will have to face me once more. And it’s going to be scary and you should be afraid. Because I’m coming to finish the job that I started at Faith. I would have done that this last week on Legends, but someone got the bright idea to make sure you survived again. Cameron Griffin has caused me more grief then anyone can understand. I’m going to spare everyone that story again but basically that man owes me one year of my life and he owes me that Universal Title that he is telling L2J and I to share. Personally, I don’t understand how this has happened, or who the hell gave him the power to do such a thing. But there’s no use in complaining because what’s done is done and there is no undoing it. But Cameron Griffin’s days are numbered and I’m counting down the days till the Legends after Road to Glory when I will have purified my Universal Title and then can give Cameron Griffin what he deserves for everything he has done to me. That isn’t an idol threat, it will happen and when it does Cameron Griffin will be wishing that he never stepped on my toes again. Sovereign’s face twitches violently as he’s been trying his best to keep a straight face. But it’s clear that as he’s been able to hide his intentions for over the last month that Sovereign is back and he’s even more unstable then he ever has been. As far as this week is concerned and the Tag title tournament. I could care less about the match, or who the other team is. L2J will be there, he might be “on my side” or fighting for the Tag team gold, but I think that being forced to share the Universal title is more then enough then having to go all the way and hold the tag titles. Legends is about the continuation of a false champion in L2J and me making sure that the Universal Title will stay around my waist for as long as possible. The Unlimited have their own problems and I have mine. I’ll be in that ring as a formality, but I don’t care about the outcome of the match. L2J, you’ve been given your chance. You now can call yourself a champion, although everyone in the world knows that it’s bullshit. I hope that you yourself realize that when you go to sleep at night. When you look your wife in the eye and she smiles back at you trying to make you feel good about yourself when you both know your nothing but a FRAUD! Sovereign’s arm lashes out knocking the camera to the ground and you hear his screams as a foot connects with the camera knocking across the room as the shot goes to snow with the words “A FRAUD” screaming in the background.
