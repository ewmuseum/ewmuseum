Roll Credits

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.04.03 ~Lost in his own mind look for a way out trapped by anger and fear…Shocka has once been in the same state as Morpheus Omega but he was strong enough to overcome it. Omega talks about the NRCW like he is important…Shocka was there 2 years before the Big MO had ever even heard about the organization…and as soon as Omega had joined he realized who was the big dog in the yard and he wanted to join with him. Omega came to Shocka… back then Shocka saw some potential in the big retard of a man.~ ‘Anger, rage, empowerment…things that once consumed my life but now I have overcome them with the power of my own fist. I fought my way out of the rage something that Morpheus Omega will never be able to do because of two reasons… he may be strong but he lacks psychological smarts something that is a must to be a proficient wrestler that’s why he relies on his power. And he lacks the strength of his mind…he can’t force himself to overcome what he must to make it to the other side…’ The voice of Shocka slowly trails off as you see him talking on the phone feet propped up on a table. ‘I know…who in the hell does he think he is talking about the NRCW like he ever accomplished anything there…but like he said lets not bring the past into this feud since I was the only one to beat him in about 20 matches…but that’s not the point here. I remember the time I brought that up on Fedwars radio…’ ~Shocka pauses to listen to what the person on the other end of the phone has to say nodding with a laugh.~ ‘Yeah…he went on a rant and sent me a bunch of shit in the mail, I dunno why he was so steamed I mean it will happen again if he gets into my way.’ ~Shocka laughs as he puts the call on speaker phone to listen to what the person has to say with more ease.~ ‘You know that he will get in the damn way…I mean he can’t not try and grab your spot light. He knows that you have garnered all this attention and he just wants to try and stick his nose in it and be something…he’s simply jealous.’ The voice at the other end speaks. ‘I know, its unbelievable how he just tries to steal my spotlight but you know what? I will let him in my spotlight and then absolutely destroy him.’ ‘I know man…just take care of him the way you did Ryan Justice in the 3 tier cage match…’ The voice speaks. ‘Dimitri…you are brilliant…’ Shocka speaks like a light bulb just went off. ‘Hey man I don’t go by Dimitri Williams any more its just plain old Dema…’ The man speaks. ‘I’m glad I got a hold of you before Omega did as you are the only one to take my title away in NRCW history…’ Shocka says with a smile. ‘With a small fee of course.’ A roar of laughter comes from the other end of the phone throwing Shocka into a little laugh of his own. ‘Its gonna be time for Morpheus to roll his own credits after I get through with him and he will be giving his special thanks to me for not letting him suffer…’ ~Shocka now lifts the phone and drops it back onto the receiver turning it off. He pulls his feet off of the desk and goes into a brooding position thinking about what he has coming tomorrow night.~ ‘Now that, that part of the plan is secured…I have to deal with the other side of the deal…’ he speaks in a sort of sly tone as the feed cuts.
