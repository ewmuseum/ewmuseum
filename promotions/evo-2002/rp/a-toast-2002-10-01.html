A Toast

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/1/02 Picture yourself, locked inside the confines of your own personal hell, waiting for someone to unlock the door and let you out. Can’t you feel the darkness staring at you? Can’t you see the briskness of your heart as you stare back? Can’t you feel your soul slowly leaking from you, and waiting for your turn to meet death? That is how I feel every second of every day. I can feel the world’s eyes on me. Examining me with their vengeful judgment. Hoping that I will fall into my own pit of despair, never to see the light of day again. They would all love to hurt me with their every breath. Despise me with their every step. Kill me with their every sense. Just like you all want to. But I don’t see you for that. I don’t see you as reluctant little killers, who remain silent and still, waiting for their prey to draw closer. I don’t look upon you as my executioners or as my assassins. I don’t see you as my predator. I see you as the poor little parasites that suck off the world tit, hoping to gain more then those who suckle with you. You think that I am afraid of you because of your horrifying disguises. I am not. You think you see me tremble whenever my eyes watch you glaring. I do not. You think I fear what you are capable of doing. I will never be. All I can do is watch you try to shadow yourselves in something, when all you are is nothing. Shit trying not to smell. Darkness trying to light a candle with no match. The scum of the world trying to make it clean. Basically, you are all lies trying to pass off as the truth. A toast to the shit of the world – may you all suffer in life as you have made the world suffer. The Unlimited is it? You seem to think you are special, when all I see is the same old stupidity that I see in every other corner of the world. You try to pass off as the world’s intelligent people, when all I see is ignorance and idiocy. You can go on with your false ideas that you are everything, and I’ll continue on knowing the truth, and watching the steam rise off your shitty bodies. You are the sins of the past. I am the judge that has damned you. And you will realize just what perfection is.
