The rage....

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.20.02 {For weeks Shocka has been the Continental champion and has put his title on the line a few times. But something is different this time, something has changed. When he went into the matches before he knew that he had the match in the bag but now? Well in the last couple weeks his confidence has been shattered by Showtime a phony wrestler that was actually Shodan. The man got lucky because Shocka smashed himself in the face with a steel chair laying himself out and allowing Shodan to get the pin over him. The second one on one victory that has been scored over Shocka by Shodan. Neither have his previous wins have had anything about them that is honorable. And you could also argue that Shodan has 3 wins over Shocka with one off the record. But what is so different now than before?} {Shocka has proved time in and time out that he can beat the best…that he can leave them lying in a pool of there own blood. Just ask Erin Christoph, just ask PPC…the line continues out the door on so called superstars that Shocka holds victories over. And now he very well may be defending his Continental title for the last time…sure he is ready for the jump to the Universal title division but he doesn’t want to end his feud with Shodan by Shodan winning that’s just not the way he wants thing to work out. Shocka has something very special planned for Shodan…planned to finish him off once and for all. Jason Jameson can come down to the ring if he wants but Shocka will be ready and willing to take out his rage on anyone who gets into his way.} {As the seconds tick by Shocka’s rage grows ever larger…with every breath he breathes in and exhales he gains confidence. Sure he may be left overzealous after all of this but he doesn’t mind. You could say that this is Shocka’s biggest match in his career to date and you wouldn’t be telling a lie. You could say that the butterflies he had when he first debuted in eVolution are back and again would pass a lie detector test. But if you said he is confident in him being able to win…well that’s a whole different story…he knows that he is not 100% as he carried Quasar through a tag match. He knows that he still is feeling the effects of a lingering concussion that he gave himself just a mere two weeks ago. But he also knows that Shodan holds 2 victories over him…both times that Continental title could have been on the line and both times Shocka could have lost his belt. But fortunately for him it wasn’t on the line.} {Shocka has proven to be a dominant Continental champion holding the belt for 7 weeks straight now after beating Erin Christoph. And he has taken on everyone that he thought had earned the shot. And this time has left him humbled instead of boosting his ego to another level…it has left him a torn and tattered man with plaguing injuries. It has also left him with a new perspective on what he wants and its no longer the Continental title oh no…its respect. From day one he said he would gain the respect of all in eVolution and he has not yet garnered it but he still feels that has the chance.} /Exhaling can be heard every 10 seconds as the scene opens to Shocka lifting about 100 pounds of weight on a bar curling it to his chest with both hands. He sets it down and looks around the empty gym. Its after midnight and he is the only one around…it seems that this is the only time he has to himself anymore. With reporters from eVolution running rampant about the upcoming PPV Faith he has finally gotten away from it all. And instead of using this time to rest and relax he is lifting weights…after all it is one of the most relaxing things that he does. It’s the only way that he can focus in and think to himself, and escape the every day worries we call life.\ ‘Jason Steele and Shocka…what in the hell kind of team is that?! Some washed up baseball player trying to make a name for himself in my business….’ Shocka mind rarely wanders from his matches but today is different he is stuck on how the match ended at Legends. ‘I should just get the win and be able to pick a damn partner…I mean I am the Continental champion…and I have raised the standards for the belt beyond anything Christoph did…’ Shocka shakes his head trying to get back to what is important. ‘I have to focus on Shodan…’ He shakes his head. ‘I have concentrate on him…this is how I lost the other two times lack of focus…thug roC has been trying to drive that into my head but for some reason I can’t get it…’ /Shocka looks up to notice the presence of an eVolution camera…Shocka looks back toward the ground and continues speaking to himself like the camera is not even there.\ ‘I’ll I have to do is ground him…I have to keep him out of the air and work a more technical match.’ Shocka begins thinking to himself now as the scene slowly fades.
