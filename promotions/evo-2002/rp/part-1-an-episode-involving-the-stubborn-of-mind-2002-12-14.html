Part 1: An Episode Involving the Stubborn of Mind...

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As my eyes opened my first thought was where was I. Prizing my eyes opened I was forced to remind myself that I was still being held captive in Vancouver medical centre and despite counting down the seconds it seemed that time was standing still at present. To open your eyes and see that your at the mercy of a number of men in white coats is a wrestlers biggest nightmare. Then to think that someone is revelling in the thought that this is the case, is perhaps more worrying. Looking down at my wounds I took comfort in the fact that the bandages were much fewer in number than yesterday, however those that were still present were stained with blood so it seemed better not to dwell on my appearance. Now was another test of my mental strength I thought and my focus had to be on the response; now was not the time of self pity. Taking a second to clear my thoughts I turned to see the time was 4pm. Four. Suddenly something clicked in my head. I didn't know why, but four had some significance. Thinking hard I tried to recall the importance, but in doing so my head began to pound with pain. Perhaps right now deep thought was not the best recreational activity, mental note, I was still ill else I wouldn't be in this prison like atmosphere. Sleep. Now that seemed like the best thing to do.. Sure I'd slept for the best part of the day already, but after the exertions of this past week who could blame me for wanting to take a bit of a break from the toils of work. Closing my eyes again, I slumped back down resting my head on the pillow once more. Just as I was set to drift off once more though, I was awoken from my slumber by the sounds of footsteps and the calling of my name. 'Sean?' said a slightly tentative voice. There was then a pause and I considered ignoring what I thought was my head playing tricks on me again; ignorance can be bliss. However that was not to be, 'Sean?' the voice spoke again, 'their here.' 'I didn't order anything' I groaned back sarcastically trying to get rid of what I thought was just another docter wanting to perform just another check! Having closed my eyes again and with my head still pounding I had no intention of going through the pain of opening my eyes without any real need. At that point everything went silent, until I heard footsteps seemingly dissapearing into the distance again. Guess they listened to me I thought, however as I relaxed once more I could here a group whispering. My eyes were still closed, but as I strained my ears I could just make out what the group was muttering about. 'He's not well' one of them muttered, 'and hes certainly not in any condition to be taking interviews!' Suddenly my curiousity was increased. Interviews? Who in this room was worth the time of a camera crew? Wait a second... Then it clicked. Four o'clock was the time I had told the crew to come. As I opened my eyes the crews were just leaving, I'd had just got in soon enough. 'Wait!' I screamed be it in a very husky and faint voice. The crew turned. Realising the scream was aimed at them they attempted to re-enter the room thinking this was not a wasted journey after all. A look of horrer though was apparent on the docters face. Trying his best, he tried to usher the crew out of the door regardless. Whatever he thought was best for me though meant nothing, I was not about to surrender all my rights. 'Let them back in!' I screamed.. 'But Sean..' the docter said in a pathetic but concerned manner. 'But nothing' I screamed back. My head was truely pounding now. I was determined I was going to cut this interview and if the Dr knew what was best for me he would grant my wishes. Struggling to an upright position I fought through the pain to my point be known, this interview was set to go ahead. 'No cameras allowed' the Dr said panicing. 'Its policy' he said, but however crap I felt, it was obvious he was lying through his teeth. 'Im sorry Sean, but I just can't let them in' the Dr said trying to place authority into his voice without much success. 'So you can't let them in?' I groaned back, trying to clear my thoughts. 'No' the docter replied. 'Well in that case I need ten minutes' I replied with a stubborn expression. If Mohammed couldn't come to the mountain, then it would seem the mountain would have to get to Mohammed; or in this instance Sean would need to get to the EVO camera crew. Digging deep I threw the bedsheets off my body and sat fully upright, slinging my legs over the side of the bed. The docter was horrified and it was very apparent. Racing over to my bedside he tried to usher me to lay down and rest once more. 'SEAN?!' he screamed, before trying to regain his composure. 'You can't! You need to rest for heavens sake, surely even you can realise that! Racing your recovery is one thing, but not having any recovery time is almost suicidal.' 'Look doc, I'll be back in ten, quit whinging you'll give yourself a hernia' I groaned back. Then reality hit me. As I struggled to my feet I suddenly found my vision was alot more blurry than I had thought and I fell straight back down on my ass. I was embaressed, but by no means beaten. 'You' I said pointing to one of the crew members, 'come give a guy a hand will you and don't you tell a damn soul that I needed your help!' Ever stubborn, I did not want the extent of my injuries to be blurted out for all and sundry to hear. At first the crew member seemed un-willing, but not realising it my scowl was enough to get the youngster on his feet. Pushing the Dr slightly to one side I used the youngster for leverage, before finding my feet for the first time in what had seemingly been a decade. 'Sean please!' the docter screamed in vain. 'Look conduct the interview here, just don't leave the room!' 'But thats not allowed' I replied raising an eyebrow sarcastically, 'and anyway I think I need some fresh air'. My vision at this point was still very much hazy, but it seemed those of stubborn minded nature didn't need 20-20 vision. Raising a slight grin I turned back toward the crew, 'now lets get this over and done with' I said pointing toward the door.. At that point with the aid of the crew member I shuffled out of the room and into the corridoor in search of a setting for the interview which was to follow... To Be Continued.... Scene Fades to Black...
