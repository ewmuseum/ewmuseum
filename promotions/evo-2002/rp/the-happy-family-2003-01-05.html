The Happy Family

5 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-01-03 We should all be used to it by now I guess. Over the life of Evolution wrestling there has been 1 man that has remained at the top. One man that has consistently had us fooled. Arriving out of the blue. Making us guess what side he was on. Creating an alter ego to get a rival into a match that will go down in history. Now he’s created something even just as shocking. The alliance between Impact and Leya was nothing but a plot, a sham and now we see Sovereign and Leya reunited. What does it mean? Well we saw at Nightmare Before Christmas Sovereign continue on his Crusade of Violence. Impact and Sovereign battled all over the building for what was just shy of 30 minutes. They spilt each other’s blood, they broke each other’s bodies and in the end it was the help of the woman we all thought hated Sovereign more then anyone else has ever that was the deciding factor in Sovereign’s victory over Impact. The blow, the handing of the weapon. It was shocking. The boos were thunderous. The hatred was real, the fans had been fooled once again by this mad man. Impact lay on the floor, his face the proverbial “crimson mask,” his eyes closed shut with Leya embracing Sovereign in a sick display of what might be considered “Love”. Now reunited no one can predict what comes next in Evolution. Sovereign once before reigned supreme over the entire roster, only to be dethroned because of Impact, and now L2J, the man who reeped the reward is out of action himself. Sovereign, yet to be defeated cleanly. Sovereign, yet to be pined. Now with his wife at his side, someone who no doubt is as ruthless as he is if she is willing to be back with him. Some might say they deserve each other. I personally try my best day in and day out to wish no ill will towards any of my fellow human beings. However I find it hard some times to deal with the likes of Sovereign and Leya. The Forth family, the 1st family of Evolution some might say, and that’s not a comforting thought. But all is not lost. This week at Legends Impact has requested a rematch, and it’s been given by Cameron Griffin. We all know friends that Cameron and Sovereign have a storied past, especially in the short time that Griffin has been Commissioner here in Evolution. Impact has sworn revenge, but isn’t that what he wanted in the first place? Revenge on Sovereign for his injury? He didn’t get it did he at Nightmare Before Christmas, and so who thinks he has a chance at getting it now? Maybe a run of the mill match would favor Impact, but who’s to say what is going to happen at Legends. S. hasn’t said a word about Impact’s revenge, and Sovereign has been silent. Up until now. I’ve been given a task my friends. I, Max Dickens took a camera crew and flew to the Forth home in Chicago to do an interview with Sovereign and Leya. I was promised that myself and my crew would be safe by Leya, and although I have no reason to trust her, in fact I have every reason to think that I’ll be leaving that place with something broken (bone wise) but a reporter must do what a reporter must do. The last time I did something like this with Sovereign I ended up with a brutal beating. The last time I did this with Leya, we were all heart broken. The story of their past, Leya’s story was so touching, but now we are going to find out why she’s done what she has done. And what is next for this duo! The voice over from Mr. Dickens ends as the camera fades in. The scene is set, it’s the living room of the Forth Home in Chicago, the place is rather nice actually as we’ve seen it prior to today in a promo with Sovereign, where he “broke” into the house and trashed it. However now we don’t know what was real anymore. There is a love seat, sitting in it are Sovereign and Leya. Leya is wearing fine clothing, but nothing flashy, her hair is down and looking rather attractive as we’ve come to know. Sovereign sits at her side, hands held together. He looks pretty much the same as always, a wild look in his eyes, hair straggeled, hooked behind his ears. A bandage graces his forehead, wounds suffered from Impact at the Pay Per View. Sitting across from the couple, separated by a coffee table with three cups of tea and a tea pot is Max Dickens, the camera rolls as you hear from the back, 5, 4, 3.... 2 seconds later Dickens speaks Dickens: I first off want to start by saying thank you for allowing me into your home this Sunday afternoon. I know that Sovereign you have kept to yourself a lot lately, and that you probably haven’t been here in a long time. But I want to thank you two for allowing us in here to film this interview. Leya: Your welcome Max. Just because Will hasn’t been around a film crew in 4 months doesn’t mean that he can’t control himself. He isn’t a concern of yours today. Sovereign: I promised I’d be good. Silence. Dickens: Okay, you will have to excuse me if that doesn’t make me feel any better. But how about we get right to the point. Evolution fans are foaming at the mouth after what you two did to Impact this past week at Nightmare before Christmas. We want to know what happened and why it happened. Leya: Well Max, it’s really not that difficult. I think that you’ll remember the interview you did with me before Faith. Don’t think for 1 second that every last word I said was the heart felt truth. But after what Sovereign did at Faith to his family, to L2J and of course to Me I had to do some serious soul searching. And it was your interview Max, I watched the replay of it just before I made my decision that I would do what I did. You see I said in that interview Max that I couldn’t stand to lose my man again. I said that I would stand at his side and support him. I know what he is. I look into his eyes and I see a monster, a man that is crazy, a mad man. Someone that has had everything ripped away from him before he even got a chance to really taste what it felt like to love, to be truly happy. All he’s known is pain and hatred. I loved this man Max. And the man that is within Sovereign in William. He’s not fake. He’s in there. But Sovereign and William have become so similar that you can’t tell the difference between them. I can. I know when it’s the loving husband standing beside me. And I said that I would support him. And if supporting Sovereign means supporting William then I’m going to do it. Dickens: Are you listening to yourself? You are saying you will sacrifice everything for William when he’s basically Sovereign. You know what this man can do! Sovereign: No one knows what I can do Dickens. No one has seen my full potential yet. Leya: That’s right Dickens. Sovereign is here, he’s everywhere. He is the most feared man in wrestling. Now he’s got the one person on earth that understands him, his only weakness is his ally now. Impact came to me the day after Faith and asked me if I would help him get revenge on Sovereign. I first thought that it was my chance at retribution and I stood at his side while he finished his recovery. But it was over that time frame my real feelings started to develop. The need for my man was starting to really burn inside of me. I missed him, uncontrollable heart ache and all I was hearing about him was from Impact, cursing him, plotting against him. It was painful to hear all the bad things said about him when I truly knew how good this man could be. Sure, no one would believe me if I told them that he’s a caring man. That he’s a tender man, a lover. All they know is this beast that walks to the ring and destroys without emotion. Yes, that is Sovereign. But what about the life away from the ring? He needs support. I’m not trying to change you, you know that. She says as she turns to Sovereign and grips his arm with emotion Sovereign: She came to me one night, it was a surprise and in secret and told me that Impact was coming back for me at Rise of the Legends. But that she didn’t know how or when at the show. But that I must be prepared for him. She said that Impact was going to ask her to join him on legends the next week and that if I wanted her then that I could have her back. She was rather upset, and I could tell that finally she had accepted me for what I am. That I am nothing more then a human being with some problems. And yes, I am a monster. Yes I am vicious but that no one ever, EVER gave me a chance in live. I looked at her face and knew she was honest. I don’t know why people are so upset Max. I mean it’s a storybook ending to a sad story! We’ve fought through all of this... this crap and now we’ve survived. We’re back together despite everything. I personally think it’s a great story. Dickens: Well I know Sovereign, that from the point of view you both have it’s good. I am not one to say that you aren’t supposed to look for a happy ending. But I want to be honest here Sovereign, Leya. There is a locker room full of wrestlers in Evolution that hate you Sovereign. Because of all the pain, and sorrow you caused them. And now, they looked to Impact, maybe the one man that could have given them some piece of mind, and you two both shattered him at the Pay Per View. I mean, you played with his mind for so long, how do you look yourself in the eye! Leya holds up a hand and pauses Dickens before he continues. She lowers the hand and speaks in a calm voice Leya: Lets be frank here Mr. Dickens. Impact is nothing more then someone who is a glory hog. He wanted to come back, use Sovereign here as a stepping stone to the Universal Title. A Title that Sovereign lost only because of Impact’s plans and we got our retribution on him for that. Now Impact sits in the back locker room and cries and bitches about getting screwed. That’s okay, and I don’t mind because yes we screwed Impact. We screwed him good, so good in fact that if he’s able to even go through with this match at Legends I’ll be personally shocked. And although I’m not as malicious as William here, I surely hope that we send him back to the hospital and that this time he truly learns his lesson not to get in Sovereign’s way. Impact is a dog, he should be treated as such. Nothing but a bitch as far as we’re concerned. Sovereign (interrupting Leya): My hatred for Impact runs just as deep as his for me Max. You see, for so long all I knew was this business. All I wanted to do was to succeed. I wanted to quench my thirst for inflicting pain, but when that got to easy. After so many had fallen in front of me Max I wanted more. I craved more, and if you speak to anyone in any company in any era of this business and ask them what it is like to be a champion and loose that title they’ll tell you it’s like a drug. I’m quite familiar with the effects of being taken off a drug cold turkey Max. You get the sweats. You shake, you can’t take your mind off of it. That 1st night I was alone without the Universal Title was so horrible that I didn’t even want to live. And regardless if I knew Impact was coming back or not I wasn’t prepared for that feeling. The feeling of having a child ripped from your grasp. I was devastated by the loss Max. And when I took Impact last week and beat him to within an inch of his life I took an unbelievable amount of pleasure in it. So much in fact that I never had ever felt so good in all my years. It was the greatest moment of my life. To split his egotistical head open, to watch the blood flow like water. To throw him over the railing and watch his body fall limp at the floor below me. To crack that 2 by 4 over his skull and watch his 6'6, 350lbs frame collapse to the ground like a mighty building falling into rubble. It was better than any sex, any drug, any emotion you could feel Max Dickens. Because you don’t truly know what it is to Hate someone like I hated Impact that night. It’s the sort of hate that ends cultures Dickens. I couldn’t sleep for weeks knowing the anticipation of my match with him. With the knowledge that Leya and I would finally become one again. The game was afoot and I was so excited, and when it all went down. When I heard his music hit and he headed towards the ring I had chills going up and down my spine. I could have cared less about the Universal Title. The first time since I lost it I didn’t care about it, only because I knew that the match with Impact was bigger than the Universal Title. The outcome is bigger then the Title Dickens. Because now it’s time to go back to work. It’s time to regain what I’ve lost, regardless of how I have to do it. Dickens: Your saying you will be going after the Universal Title then? Leya: Sovereign was never given a rematch. Silence. Dickens clears his throat, disturbed by the news Leya: It’s a fact. He lost the title, he is automatically supposed to receive a rematch. Let alone under the circumstances he lost the belt, and after a 3 month reign you would assume that the right thing to do would be have Shocka defend his title at New Years Evolution. Dickens: Leya, Frost and Shocka are set to wrestle this week for the Universal Title Sovereign: Do you think that makes any difference? I was the most dominant Universal Champion in history. I deserve that Title match more then anyone in Evolution. You can’t honestly sit there... there... and say that Scott Frost deserves a Title match over me? Who did he beat recently? Asis, yes he’s my brother in arms but he sure as hell is not Sovereign. Who else? Thug roC, someone who isn’t even an active wrestler let alone around much longer after Shocka beat him senseless. What did I do recently? I destroyed Impact, Luther Wisdom. I don’t think that there is a single man in Evolution that has a claim to the number one spot except for me, and yet it gets tossed to the dog Scott Frost just because why, the fans love him? Because when they hear his music they cheer. But when they hear my music they hide behind their programs, children cry as their mothers cover their eyes. There hasn’t been a man in Evolution that has stood up to Sovereign and survived. No one has pinned me. No one has made me submit, and for the sake of competition they toss me aside to wrestle Impact again? Where is my challenger? Who wants to face Sovereign.... No one. Because they know that I’ll rip them to shreds in front of the throngs of fans that watch each and every week. Watching for that 1 person that might be able to stop the Human Monster. They thought it was Jamal Kage... where is he? Traction. They hopped it was Matt Boone, he’s no where to be found. They prayed it was L2J, nothing but a waist of skin that got lucky because of someone else. They hoped, stayed up at night, lit candles for Impact... how he’s nothing but a poor man crying for a mulligan. I waited my turn for years to get my chance to be a Champion. I took that opportunity and I succeeded. I succeeded so well that Evolution has tried to distract me with meaningless opponents so that I might not head back and reclaim what is mine. The Universal Title is Sovereign’s title. No matter what anyone else says, no matter what anyone else does I am the Number One contender. If you want to be the top man in Evolution you must step into the ring with Sovereign. You have to at least try. But when they do step up to me, I’ll beat them so badly that they’ll wish they were still in wrestling school. Dickens: Sovereign, no one doubts your ability. You’ve had a hell of a year in Evolution, including your Title run. But things have changed, you aren’t the Number One contender. That is Scott Frost. Sovereign: You must not be listening. I don’t care about what Scott Frost is. He may very well be getting a title match this week. But win or loose he will eventually have to face me. And I’ll make it sooner then he or Shocka will want. They might welcome my challenge and that’s all well and good. But listen to me when I tell you Dickens. This time the Reign of Terror, the Crusade of Violence will not stop, no breaks for anything. It will start with this week’s Legends in Kansas City. I will be there, not just for Impact. He’s secondary. I’m coming back for what is mine. Evolution’s cream of the crop. To stand upon the top of the mountain once again, title or no title I am the most dangerous man in wrestling. Title or no title I am the best in Evolution. It might not be something that I have cared about in the past. But right now it is what I want. I want to stand over the people and look down on them and smile, ear to ear and laugh at them. The man that wasn’t ever given the chance Dickens. I wasn’t given anything all my life, and I showed them all wrong. I’ll show them all wrong again. Come hell or high water Max Dickens. I will be Universal Champion once more. And when I get that title back, I’ll never let it go. Leya: It’s a warning Dickens. To everyone in Evolution. That Sovereign isn’t going to be bother with petty things anymore like personal problems. He has a center to focus on. His mind is focused, his thoughts are clear, more clear then ever before and he knows what he wants now. In his professional life, things are now focused, at his finger tips. And that means trouble for Evolution. That means that The Human Monster is more dangerous then ever. More sadistic, more methodical. He won’t rest until he gets what he wants Dickens. And I will help him get there any way I know how. Dickens shakes his head as the camera fades to black.
