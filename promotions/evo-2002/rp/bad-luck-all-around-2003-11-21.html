Bad Luck All Around

21 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-21 The smell of the smoking car drifted in the air as Kramer exited his beat up red truck and went to the source of the problem located under the hood. He propped open the hood and was immediately engulfed in a rise of smoke drifting from the overheating engine. “Ah this is just great!” He gives the car a good swift kick which sends a throbbing pain through his foot. He hops around holding his foot while he mutters curses under his breath. The frustration is obvious as he slams the car hood down and walks away from the car nearly getting clipped by a passing Saturn. “Watch it!” He yells shaking his head in anger as he leans against the truck not sure what to do. Several minutes pass and he stands up straight from the car and opens it up to get his duffel bag out of the passenger seat, the only real valuable in the truck. He slams the door and starts walking down the pretty barren street. “I’m supposed to be enjoying the little vacation time from the Evolution hiatus and look where I’m at! Can’t even get to a simple college football game! This is just perfect. All I need is more frickin bad luck to tack onto this week.” This week has been pretty unbelievable for Kramer. On Tuesday he was nearly electrocuted by his toaster. On Wednesday a bird decided to use his brand new silk shirt as a toilet and the stains are still refusing to come out. On Thursday he was mistaken for a wanted man in the Tampa Bay area and was later released after it was discovered that they had the wrong person. It was the first time Kramer had ever been frisked like that by a man and he hopes it’s the last…..who would have thought they checked EVERYTHING!? And now today he is stranded. He continues to walk as the crease in his forehead gets bigger and bigger as his eyes become more and more arched with anger. He kicks a few pebbles every now and then sending them sailing across the street. Kramer is suddenly startled as a loud car horn blows behind him. He turns around not sure what to expect and sees a beautiful lady in her late twenties with flowing golden hair. “You need a ride?” She asks with a cheerleader like voice. Not believing his luck Kramer looks around to make sure she’s really talking to him. Getting picked up from a lady like this is not an every day occurrence. Just when he felt cursed with bad luck, the good has come back. “Of course I would, if you don’t mind of course.” The lady waves him over and he wastes no time sliding into the car. “So where to handsome?” She asks as Kramer wiggles around in his seat nervously. “I need to get to an auto shop place. My piece of crap truck overheated the engine and the whole things pretty much shot.” “I know a great place!” The woman replies perkily. “My boyfriend Bruno could hook you up and get your truck started in no time.” “Sounds great.” Kramer says with a sigh, hearing that she is taken. A few minutes pass and the car comes to a stop in the parking lot to the small auto body shop where cars are strewn all over the place. “There’s Bruno now!” The blond points out a six foot monster bent over the hood of a car. Kramer just stares in awe at his size, thinking how good a wrestler he would make. The two are about to exit the car to get Kramer some help for his truck. Kramer picks his hand up from where he had rested it and has a glob of sticky gum attached to it. ”Yuck!” He pulls his hand to the left as the blond is halfway out of the car and in doing so accidentally touches her rear end. His eyes light up in horror as she pulls away with a squeal. “It’s not what you think.” Kramer said totally embarrassed. “BRUNO!” The girl yelled which caused Kramer to freeze with fear. “No no I didn’t mean to touch you ma’am!! I just had gum on my hand and…” Before he could finish his sentence he heard Bruno come charging over as the blonde yelled at him what had just happened. “And here I thought it was over.” Kramer whispered to himself as he bolted out of the car and raced down the street with Bruno chasing behind him. So the bad luck really wasn’t over…. Scene fades out…
