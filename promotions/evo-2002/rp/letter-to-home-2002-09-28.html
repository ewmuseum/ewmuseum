Letter to Home

28 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/28/09 Dear Mother I don’t really expect that your reading this letter. Considering that I haven’t mailed you in over 5 years, and that last letter was from when I wasn’t having one of my better moments. I bet you don’t even recognize my hand writing but I feel it was time to come to you in some way, to reach out. I know that this might be a shock to you that I’d actually write you after the last time we saw each other. My face covered in blood, and I grabbed Father by the throat and threatened to kill him. Back then things were different, confusing. Now there is focus, a new light in my path that makes me see things for what they are. Not for what they seemed to be. Blurred by visions of things that were never there, masked by Insanity, and things I felt I couldn’t control. But now, it’s all different now Mother. I’ve changed, and changed for the better. There’s no more innocent victims in this man’s path anymore. I’ve cured myself of a sickness that has dominated my life for 17 years. Sovereign is dead, I beat him once and for all. It’s me here sitting at this table in Miami writing to you on a piece of hotel note paper and a pen I picked up at the information desk. I would have emailed you but I didn’t know if you have an email address, mine is [email removed]. I get a lot of hate mail there, that might not come as a surprise, and I really doubt that you even know anything about my career, simply because it mirrors everything that you hated about me. I know, that sounded cruel but it’s true, I know it is. Either way, I want you to know that last week, I managed to overcome everything and for the first time in my life I went out to the ring as me. Not as Sovereign, and I went out as Champion, and I returned to the back, the champion. I was a success, I was everything I’ve ever wanted to be in my entire life. It was closure on everything and when I came here today, with time on my hands I had to write to you. Just like I did when I was first away from home. I remember writing you once a week to tell you about my travels. Now, sometimes I forgot how fulfilling a pen and a pad was. My only hope is that you get this. I don’t even know if you live in Chicago anymore. This is the last address that I know of, I remember the house. I remember my bed room, I sort of miss it I guess, back when things were contained and easy to get through day by day. I wish I never left home, I wish sometimes that I never have done the things that I did in my days. The people I’ve hurt, the lives I’ve handed to destruction. All because I was to selfish to try and contain him. Because I was afraid of where I would be plunked down into. Now I’m here, without Sovereign whispering in my ear. I’m a success for the first time in my life Mother, I feel good. I feel happy, I even have a smile on my face these days. It’s an unbelievable feeling Mother to be happy again. I had forgotten what it felt like, it’s been oh so long! So how is Father? I know that’s probably something that he’s not wanting to hear, me asking him how he is. But I want to know. I felt guilty about Sovereign that day, and I cried myself to bed many a night for now I never tried to form any sort of bond with him. Even though he generally wanted nothing to do with me. I suppose I could blame him somewhat, but this letter isn’t about blame. It’s about me trying to save something. I miss him so much, just like I miss you Mother. He was and is always going to be my Father, and if he doesn’t want anything to do with me then that is alright. I will get over it and hope that maybe some day he’ll realize that I’m not the man I used to be. I’m not blood thirsty. I’m not a maniac. Yes, Mother I know what your thinking, I can’t be 100 percent cured and your right. I still have a lot of problems. But you see, there is this new pill I’m on, and it’s working. It’s really working well and it’s helped me so much Mother, it’s like a miracle drug or something. It’s held all the urges at bay, it’s helped me concentrate and appreciate everything that I see and do. Now, it is like I can slow down time and see things with clarity and certainty. So how is Father? Is he still smoking? I hope not because that is very bad for your health if you haven’t heard already, although I remember you used to read a lot so I figure you’d probably know by now. How’s Roger? Is he still in construction? Last time I saw him he was working for some company that was working on the Sear’s building on the upper floors. I hope that contract led to some other things because he never judged me and always would play with me. Probably because he was bigger then me (funny joke he used to say all the time, sort of an inside joke that I never got till I was older). Does he still play in the blues band? What was the name, I don’t recall. Hell, he probably doesn’t even live in Chicago anymore. How about Marge? I bet she’s a lawyer by now, she was so smart that I hated talking to her cuz she’d put me down. But then she’d come back and do the sweetest things for me. I remember she used to sneak desert up to my room whenever I would freak out at diner. I felt so bad after I did those sorts of things, she always was sympathetic towards me. I suppose Roy isn’t finished Highschool yet, he must only be 16 or so. I hope that he’s staying out of trouble and that he’s doing well in school. I really miss the city and the street we lived on. I remember Old Man MacInnis, he used to shake his cane at us when we ran down the street on the corner and always cut across his lawn. Man I remember those days back then before I really got sick. Not a care in the world, only you, your friends and the playground. I wish I could have those days back again. But now, as you already know I can’t go back there. Not with all the miracle drugs in the world. Because now, even though Sovereign is dead and gone, I have a responsibility to a lot of people. The responsibility that I am a wrestler. I’m strangely enough an entertainer, something I hated to think of myself as when Sovereign was here. I’m an athlete. I’m a powerful man, strong of mind and body. I’ve managed to hold on and for the first time, the first time in my life I have come out on top. But things aren’t coming up all roses Mother. Because of Sovereign I have a lot of enemies. I feel very lonely. It’s probably why I’m writing you this letter. Because no one will trust me. No one wants to come near me. I know that it’s probably natural, because of all the things I’ve done. Especially to people like L2J. I doubt you know who I mean Mother but Sovereign did some things to him that keep me up at night. Now he has to be my opponent next month at the big Pay Per View and I don’t know if I can go through with it. I don’t know if I can put myself through hurting him again. Even if it was Sovereign before, there will always be that link between the two of us. And I feel so guilty about it. And L2J doesn’t care who I am, he knows what this body has done to him and I have something he strives for in this damn title belt. Sometimes I don’t even think it’s worth it, and then I look at it, and I smile. It’s something I earned, and although I didn’t go through what Sovereign went through per say, I earned it last week when I defended it in his place. I am the Universal Champion Mother, and it’s the first thing I’ve ever done to say I’m a success in this industry, and in this entire world! I became a man last week, I stopped hiding and cowering behind the strong, and I stood up for myself and my believes just like you always told me to when I was a boy. I never forgot the things you taught me Mother. Your voice is imprinted on my soul. Your face on my eyes and your touch on my skin. I want you to know that Mother, that regardless of all the evil I’ve done, I love you just as much as you loved me when I was 3 feet high. So next week Mother I will be here in Miami, and probably be in the south for a little while longer. If you felt like it maybe you could write me back or something. I really miss all of you. I know that one letter can’t bridge the gap that’s grown between all of us. But maybe, just maybe I can start to form that bridge. I hope this laid down the ground work for a future. I do want to have a future with you all and not just sitting in some home somewhere after I’ve finished with this wrestling stuff. I love you all, bless you and I hope that I hear from you eventually. Will
