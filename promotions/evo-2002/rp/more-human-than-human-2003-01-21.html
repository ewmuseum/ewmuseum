More Human than Human

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/01/03 To be the Champion. I guess in and of itself it’s not so unselfish. Every man in this world wants to better himself in one way or the other. Men, as a gender in general want to prove to their fellow men that they are superior. In one way or the other it’s an underlaying goal. To be able to say to your friends that You beat them at that game of Nintendo. To turn to the guy you sit in front of in school and show him your mark on your exam knowing it was better then his, even if only by 1 damn point! You want the better, faster, more attractive card. You gloat about your woman, anything about here that you can find. Boasting is Human nature. But is it? Maybe not in the minds of some. I know that Sovereign’s drive to be Universal Champion for a second time is based off his need to have identity. He calls it his identity. He came to eVo for redemption upon Daniel Somers, and after he found that his goal was ill founded he turned to a new life, the Universal Championship. Then it was stolen from him. I know some would say that Sovereign is thinking in the past. In things that once were. To regain his past glory so to speak. And is that at all a wrong thing? Lets be honest, Sovereign isn’t exactly a model citizen. He is no role model. He is not a good person. But then again, isn’t he Human? A human deserves a second chance. Now, don’t get me wrong friends. I don’t want to see Sovereign as Universal Champion again on account of what happened the last time he held that prestigious title. The body count, the terror, the sorrow. That’s not something I particularly want revisited. I’m just trying to see it from his somewhat distorted and twisted point of view. It’s so hard to understand Sovereign sometimes. But in the end, he’s more human then we think. He has wants, desires. He has goals that he wants to reach. And who are we to deny him the chance to be happy, or at least as happy as someone like Sovereign can be. And if that happiness can only be attained by the Universal Championship then we should give him the chance. And if he happens to become Universal Champion for a second time... well we should deal with that bridge when we cross it. Who am I kidding? I don’t want Sovereign to be champion again. The only two people on earth that want that are Sovereign himself and his twisted wife Leya. Those two make quite a couple, and quite a dangerous team. We saw already what can happen when you put them together. Will we see them as a team again this week at Legends? Will Sovereign defeat King, Frost and Jameson to face Shocka at New Year’s Evolution? Or will the rivalry between Jameson and Sovereign continue to escalate and be to much of a distraction for both men, to loose sight of the big picture that is the Universal Championship. Oh how I want to celebrate the moment that Sovereign tastes that bitter defeat. How I want to get out of my chair and scream that the Human Monster finally isn’t just a monster anymore. That he’s really just Human. Like you and I. That he is beatable. And yes, I am well aware my friends that Sovereign’s record isn’t perfect. Matt Boone pinned him in a Tag match, but folks we all know that was part of Sovereign’s plan, no one counts it. And of course the late L2J’s victory at Rise of The Legends due to the aid of one Impact, none of those men who’ve claimed to have Sovereign’s number still exists in eVo. So who steps up to the plate next? Jason Jameson? No offense to you Jason, but it seems your mind is more bent on your lost love then that of dealing with the 6'3, 280lbs of pure rage that is Sovereign. Bent on the Universal Title. Bent on being a Monster. It seems your priorities as of late don’t lean towards the match of your career tomorrow night when you get a chance to face Shocka for the Universal Championship. I know what it’s like to loose your 1st love, I think that a lot of people do, and that only the very lucky end up never losing. Sovereign lost his only love. Now they are reunited. Maybe that will give you some comfort. Or maybe it will drive you deeper into your problems. Regardless of that Jason Jameson, I’m rooting for you. I really am. The Narrartor’s voice fades out and is replaced by the sounds of dripping water. It’s an odd sound, knowing that there’s a huge cold snap going through most of the mid-west these days. The sound of wind on the other side of a window rattles back and forth, an ominous sound it is. Almost as if it’s about to break through, but then subsides. Dripping of water, the camera shot is unclear. It’s Sovereign though, the Black and White shot is so classic. The Black and White nature of the man. Never to be trusted, yet at times the day light seeps through and you can even pity the man. To know that all of this is a result of a man not willing to conform through medication. Not thinking that he needs the help. His recent revelation that he’s More Human than anyone else is somewhat of a disturbing thought as you can hear a groan escape, the groan is that of Sovereign’s guttural voice. You see movement, he stands up, to the right of the screen out of the shadows. A footstep.... a drop of water and then his voice. I hate the winter. I’ve never liked it. It’s cold, dark and unforgiving. Some might say that I’d find those to be soothing qualities. I find that a broad generalization. I’m rather partial to the Fall actually. The color changes, almost like a new personality. The air is warm, not hot and not cold. People head back to school. Everything is settling down from the giant festival that is the 4 months of summer. No one generally says anything about the Fall. Back to school the kids say. Back to work the Parents say. But complain? I don’t know. My experience walking around in the Fall is limited. It seems that I’m stuck watching it from a window in a room off in some distant building locked away from the world. Now I’m a free man, and have been for some time and stuck here in the winter. A cold winter. A brutal Winter. Seasons have personalities. I would suggest I guess that Winter is very much like myself. All of those adjectives I used would probably describe myself to a “T”. The cold and callus Sovereign, not a shred of mercy for his fellow man. Yeah... that’s true isn’t it. But why have sympathy? Why have any of that when no one’s shown it towards me all my life. For 31 years that I’ve been on this planet every single man woman and child has shunned me in one way or the other. Only a few have come to terms with who I am. That is me and my wife Leya. And so people continue to push me aside without thinking about it. Then they wonder why they have to suffer the consequences that they do. I’m 6'3 and I’m 280lbs. I am in great shape. I am strong, durable and I’m fast. I am a machine as some would say, I am a Champion. So why would someone want to push me aside. They should take notice that I am here. That I stand in front of you baring my soul on my sleeve. Then they have the nerve to take from me all that I was. I wanted redemption and so that was taken away from me as well. Sovereign stops. You see the whipping motion of his neck throwing his hair out of his face. Sovereign steps into the light, he is wearing street clothing, the hair now out of his face. His scared face, teeth gritted, eyes wide open. What is so wrong with that! What do you want? Think to yourself, you the listener. The person watching this. Think about something that you have the urge for. Say it’s a house, that’s a good goal to have. And what if just as you are about to move into your dream home someone goes and burns it to the ground! How about a new car? Yes, a new car is a beautiful thing my friends. What if after you drove it home from the lot someone steals it from you? How would you feel? How would you react? You’d want revenge right? It’s the most Human of reactions. The most Human of things to want, in that blind moment of RAW emotion it’s what you want. You want to find the arson that burnt your home to the foundation and make him pay. You want to find that son of a bitch that took your new car and run him over. Is that such a wrong thing to think? Well then our society tells us, no wait you can’t think like that. Well what has society ever done for me? Locked me away? Taken away my life? It’s because of that, that I am more human than human. Down to the very fiber of myself, I am more then anyone. I’m better then anyone else. That isn’t something that I’ve ever thought of before. But I am natural. I am here, before you now the true essence of a man. Some call me crazy. Most call me a maniac and just about everyone says I’m a monster. So what. To them I say kiss my crazy, maniac, monsterous ass. Sovereign lowers his head, his eyes relax So now Jason Jameson my question to you is why? I’ve stated my case. You know why I do the things that I do. And what have I done to you to have my rights, my human right taken away from me? Do I care about your woman? Do I care about your friends in Boston, lost loves? No. But Jason, you and I are a like. You draw closer to me. You take what you want deep down inside of you. You took that woman into your bed that night, not out of hatred for yourself. But out of a love for yourself. You wanted to make yourself happy. Is that a bad thing? I sure as hell hope that it isn’t. Then why feel bad about it? I think it was the right thing to do Jason. I think you should have gone further. You should do the things you’ve always thought were wrong, but deep down you knew it was what you wanted. I’m sure that this fantasy about Sovereign and Impact was some sort of planted notion of fame, or of the love of the masses. Let’s be honest to each other Jason. No one cares about us. All they want is someone to cheer and someone to boo and then watch them rip into each other. The masses are just dogs. They’re vultures. Throw them a slam of meat and watch them knaw on it. That’s all we are to them Jason. Cheering and Booing is an afterthought. It’s not about love, or hate. To them it’s violence. For me it is dominance and what is it for you? Is it redemption for screwing up your life all those years ago with that whore of a woman who’s gone and left you for someone named William. Not that William is a bad name, but I assure you Jason not all people named William are as keen and thoughtful as yours truly. I’d have to say Jason that if you did not get involved with me against Impact that night you and I could have gotten a long well enough to avoid what is coming to you. And after I am finished with you, Frost and King tomorrow night and then after I regain my title from Shocka at New Year’s Evolution I will be looking and seeing if you still have the guts to be Evolution’s Knight in Shining Armor, or just another pretender to the throne. Sovereign takes a step back into the shadow Maybe you can learn something from me Jason Jameson. Maybe you can learn about yourself. Learn to stop living in the past. Your woman is gone Jason. You and your live that you once had is over with. It’s not coming back. And the only way that you will survive Evolution is if you come at it head on. If you take those emotions and find out how you really feel. Use that, become what you really should be. Be more than the sum of your parts. Be Human for a change Jason Jameson. Stop meeting it half way. I can see it in you Jason Jameson. The potential to be great. But you sit there, crying in your beer and not realizing the chance of a life time. Standing right here in front of you. You probably don’t even realize that you have the opportunity that only so few have gotten so far in Evolution. The chance to fight for the Universal Title is at your finger tips. Wake up Jason Jameson. Wake up and try your best to shake all of the negative feelings into something that will give you the strength to fight with a force that you’ve never felt before. That will be the only way you can beat me this Wednesday Night. I know that King and Frost don’t have that in them. King is an old man, washed up with nothing left. Looking for that one last chance to make the fans chant his name. It’s so pathetic I want to throw up all over the place. Scott Frost isn’t unlike King. He’s younger, fitter and more dangerous. But he’s still weak. He isn’t like you or I Jason. He left Notorious before you could take over and you know that is true. Your time is now Jason. Show us what you are made of. Because I don’t want this to be a waist of my time. I will punish you at Legends tomorrow night Jason. It’s up to you how to deal with it. Not just physically, but emotionally as well. Sovereign fades completely from site, the shot is much like the start, a drop of water hits the floor. I want to see what you are Jason. Are you human? Or are you weak like all the others. Or will you change like the seasons? I’ll see you in Salt Lake City. A chuckle ends the promo, the Legends banner fades onto the screen in black and white.
