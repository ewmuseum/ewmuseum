What Would You Do?

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-02-03 The camera fades in, the scene is that of a room, most likely some sort of study, there is a chair, back facing you as the lighting settles down. The chair slowly swings around to show James Kimson. His debut at New Year’s Evolution was a shocking event. Especially for the Duo of Sovereign and Leya, who with the actions of Kimson lost their match against Jason Jameson. Sovereign’s 1st pinfal loss in Evolution, and the revelation of Kimson’s agenda on Legends was just as shocking. The demand to leave Leya, his wife. Kimson standing up for Sovereign’s past crimes to his family member, sister Leya. Kimson sits there, dressed in street clothes, his powerful body is relaxed, hair tied back in a neat ponytail. He leans forward, clasps his hands together and rests his elbows on his knees as he speaks, a clear direct tone to his voice. It’s been a while. To long in fact since I was last before these people. The people who feed me. The people who make me a rich man. The people who let me afford 3 large houses all over the world. It’s these people that made me who I am. And for to long I avoided being with them. But things have changed. It’s time now for a return. It is now time for this man to come back in front of the people. To give them not only what he thinks they want. But also for himself. History has a way of making interesting stories. There are always two sides to everything, and they don’t always line up. They don’t always seem to be distinguishable from right and wrong. But a history lesson is what I have to give you. My family was always filled with love, and acceptance. From my very earliest memory of my loving Mother holding me to her heart to the last breaths of my father. However there are always exceptions. I myself am a father of 4, my sisters all have children with the exception of Leya, and her chances of conception are now zero because of what a certain maniac did to her. But we will have to hold on for just a few moments while I recap for your benefit. When I was 16 I left home with my Father to hit the road. Yes, a young buck out for fame and glory via the way of the wrestling ring. Of course, it wasn’t easy. I took my bumps and paid dues that not many superstars of today have paid. However I am not a gloater in those senses. I am proud of my rise to the “top” so to speak. But when I left home my Sisters all were weary of me leaving. Following in Father’s footsteps as they called it. And I was sure to not follow them but to jump past him by miles. I wanted to prove that I would be better then him. My father and I always had a competitive relationship right down to the very end. My sister Leya however, well she thought that it was endearing. Out of my 3 sisters she was the only one that enjoyed athletics. She was the only one that would come to see Father wrestle in the smoky bingo halls and high school gyms. She was a natural athlete much like myself and although she is my sister I knew that she was indeed a beautiful woman. Boys, well Men always paid close attention to her. And I was always very protective of her best interests. Sometimes my protection wasn’t wanted. But always Leya understood what I was trying to do. Because I know that when I was 16 or 18 all I wanted from women was to get in their pants. And excuse that sort of language but lets be honest about everything. Leya, had a nice set of pants to get into and I’m sure that when I wasn’t watching, or at least trying to get into some one else’s pants that a few guys slipped past my guard. One of them is a man that fans of wrestling will know, that being William Forth. I remember the day that Leya called me. It was maybe 7 years ago, 8 at the most and she told me that she found a man. Well, I was happy, ecstatic even! The problem with being very attractive is that it’s hard to fine a special someone that loves you for who you are and not what you look like. Leya was a prime example. So many broken hearts caused by men who only wanted a short term roll in the hay. However she said his name was William, and she met him at a wrestling event. Well he liked wrestling and that meant he was okay in my books. She went on about how she’d known him for about 6 or 7 months already, but just now they started to “see” each other regularily. She wanted me to meet him. The schedule of a full time wrestler is harsh. I was just starting to break out of the indy scene and into a bigger spot light. Titles, TV contracted feds, more money, a fan base. I was so excited about my sister and my career that I didn’t really know how to work it in. Our other two sisters were a few years older then the both of us (Leya is about 11 months my senior, thus you can tell we were really close) and both Jule and Marge were already married and heck already had children at that. Leya was getting frustrated and could hear her biological clock ticking down. I thought that she’d make a great wife, but I knew that she couldn’t support herself and a family on her pay as a wrestler herself (She mostly was a Valet but she did the odd woman’s match) So when it came time to meet William and everything was in order what happened? I got called away to wrestle in Africa for 3 weeks. That was my chance to meet William. But when I returned and she told me everything about him, how he has mental sicknesses and that he has the potential to be violent and his time in the Mental Institution for like 5 or 6 years for assault made me somewhat skeptical about their relationship. I mean, what would you do? Tell your sister that this guy isn’t safe, or maybe trust that love conquers all? I’m not a philosopher, but when she told me they were engaged to be married and that she really, really wanted my blessing well I said I couldn’t until I meet the man. Again, it was not possible, and William told her apparently that with or without my blessing they would marry. The only time I ever had a conversation with the man was on the telephone, the morning of their union that if he ever hurt her I would come and he would meet me. And that he’d regret it. If there’s anything that my Father every really taught me was that you always... always make good on your word. It’s the only thing that you really have as a man. So what would you do? What would you do if your sister called you up at 2 in the morning when you finally made it out of the hospital yourself to tell you that her husband had attacked her, causing a miscarriage and then left her to go off and wrestle in death matches in Japan? I almost freaked out, I first unfortunately told her that “I told you So” but then I reached for my hotel room phone (She was on my Cell) and was about to call my travel agent and head to Japan to make good on my word. Leya begged me to let him go. She knew that he’d be back, she knew he didn’t mean for what happened to happen. My sisters have a way of finding a weakness, and the sound of their sobs and crying will make me fold like a table under the weight of 300lbs man. William Forth killed my would be Nefieu. He broke my Sister’s heart but that since she loved him so much she wouldn’t let him go. So what would you do? Imagine the shock on my face when Marge called me on the phone and told me that she saw Leya on TV side by side with “Sovereign”. She told me I’d better tune into this program called Legends on Wednesday nights. At this point my career was on hiatus to spend time with my family and wrestling was not something I followed a whole heck of a lot. Next week I turned on the TV and sure enough, Leya on wrestling, it was a week before they had their Faith Pay Per View. Obviously I ordered it and watched. It was indeed an excellent event. I have to hand my hat to Sovereign. He sure can fight, he and L2J put on a match to remember that night. But in the end all I could see was Leya sitting at ringside with her family (who are great people mind you, I did meet Sovereign’s family on 2 different occasions and they were quite friendly and very warm) That only pained me more to know the damage that man had caused not only my loved ones but his own. I knew that Leya always held up hope, and at my Father’s wake I told her that she should give up. There was nothing left in the man but rage and a cloud of thoughts and feelings that should be left alone. All she listened to was the fact that I acknowledged that she loved her man. “How can you say that” she told me. “After all you and Vicky have been through she has still stood by you, so I’m going to do the same. I made a promise, in sickness and in health” said Leya. A valid point. But there comes a time when she needs to realize that what she’s been doing is wrong. Wrong for her and wrong for those who are around and care about her. But who cares about her now? No one in the world of Wrestling. She’s an evil bitch as most have called her. Turning her back on Impact, orchistrating his retirement. Trying to put Sovereign back into the reigns of power. Is it because she loves him? Perhaps some of the reason rests there. Maybe she’s finally decided that after all the world has taken from her she deserves to get a little payback. But pay back is a bitch. And if she won’t fight for what she’s been wronged then I by God am going to do it for her. As for Sovereign. I know this man is dangerous. And so am I. He’s big, strong. I am to. He’s fast, cunning. I am fast and cunning. He has won titles, I’ve won more. He has an impressive record of winning when it counts, mine is better. Some might look in the annals of history and say that Kimson vs Sovereign could be the greatest of all wrestling rivalries and they could be right. I’m not doing this for a rivalry. I am not doing this so Daniel Somers can make millions of dollars. I’m doing this for my sister. I’m doing this for the child she’ll never have. I’m doing this to prevent the world of wrestling suffer more and more loss. Not because people don’t want to watch Sovereign. Lets face it, as much as they hate him I am quite sure that if Sovereign did not appear on Legends this week that a lot of fans would be quite pissed off. The Human Monster, the icon of Evolution. The man that turned the world on it’s head with his antics, his cunning, his legendary Universal Title matches with every contender that stepped forward. Voted by the fans to be the best wrestler of 2002. Well here’s news. I wasn’t wrestling in 2002. Sovereign’s match has been met. James Kimson is returning, and one way or the other, even if he does leave Leya as I’ve called him to do I will kick the shit out of that crazy son of a bitch so that he can learn a lesson that no one has been able to teach him. Isn’t that what you would do? Sovereign, you and I will go down. There will be a battle that will leave Evolution clouded in dust and ash. I’m going to take out all the frustrations of my sister’s past pain. Of my family trying to convince her that she’s made a terrible mistake. To help your family get closure. To help the people of Evolution get some time to swallow that they’ve lost star after star after star to your whims and ravings. You’re a maniac, you’re a psychopathic monster. There’s no room for you anymore Sovereign, and I will see that not only your life is made a living hell, but that your time in Evolution is drawing to a close. The final battle of your crusade is going to be fought between us, and I will end what you started. And I know you will fight back. I know it won’t be easy. I know your tough, and I sure as hell know what you are capable of. But when it comes down to it, every bump. Every cut, every bruise, every broken bone. Every trip to the emergency room, every day in physio therapy. Every time that I came home after months on end of not seeing my wife and kids will all be worth it when I get to redeem my sister. Because it’s the right thing to do. And when it comes down to it, every man has to decide when it’s time to do the right thing. And after every wound you give me. After every week that you leave me laying in the center of the ring, or in the backstage area won’t matter when you final will have to stand in that ring and hear my music, and hear the fans swelling in support for me. Because it’s not just for me that they’re going to cheer. It’s because they want to see you get what you deserve Sovereign. I know you are going to hurt me. Hurt me bad. I know that you probably are sitting at home right now mumbling to yourself, enraged at what I demanded last week. I know that you probably want nothing more then to walk down to that ring and tell me to go to hell on Legends this week. And if that’s what you want to do Sovereign, then you go right ahead and do it. Sign your death warrent because if I have to I’ll kill you in that ring if it means you will stay out of my sister’s life. So what will you Do Sovereign? What is your plan William Forth? The Human Monster? The Greatest Universal Champion of all Evolution’s History? What’s next? The ball rests squarely in your hands. I’ve told you what I’m going to do. And I will make good on my word this time. No amount of begging or crying will save you from the wrath of my family. And if my father were still alive he’d probably have shot you by now. But you don’t deserve a quick end Sovereign. No, you will have to out last The Hype. And it will be epic. I’m sitting here in my home, in my chair and I will wait. I will be there in Los Angeles on Wednesday and I’ll be the official in your match. But trust me Sovereign, I’m not such a small man when it comes to these things. I know what it means to be a ref. I have a responsibilty and that match will be all business if you want it to be. I know that Shocka is bitching about it already. But Shocka need not worry, because if he’s supposed to be the “Champ” then he can prove it to me and everyone else. No I won’t Disqualify anyone. No I won’t count anyone out. But Sovereign, after that bell rings it will begin. So what will you do? Kimson slowly swives his chair around as the camera fades to black.
