HBC, its all you need to know

13 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-13-02 *HEE*HEE*HEE* Laughter is heard from behind a closed door only marked with a broken heart on it. This could only mean one thing. The HeartBreak Chick personal dressing room. As wayne and garth would call it……..SWING!! HBC has been in a very good mood as of late. If there was any doubt the Syndicate split was all a ruse designed to throw everyone off. Legends XI, answered that question once and for all. Though, HBC is pissed she isn’t the High-Impact Champion anymore, screwed over by BDD’s trick shoulder injury (dam faker), he certainly paid the price after the match didn’t he. I swear, it was better than girl-on-girl-on-girl-on-guy sex! Looking at him, all helpless, all handcuffed, unable to move or defend himself, the shock in his eyes as I hold the chair over him, calling out the Herculean Babe Ruth shot of 1922 and BAMMO!! Right between the eyes! Barry Bonds don’t have shit on me! And hell, why not unleash another surprise, BANG! Arabian Facebuster! Didn’t know I could do that did you D! You don’t know half the things I can do! Yet, you like to come out here, saying ‘oh I haven’t taught you everything I know’, well boo fricken hoo D. I know you didn’t. Don’t you really think I would seek out outside teachers, outside mentors. You remember that glass coffin match? You didn’t know shit about it until I EXPLAINED it to you. Please D, your rhetoric is getting old, just like your career. You are a legend. You were a champion. You are a Hall of Famer. I give you all that because it is rightly and truly deserved. You will never perhaps understand why I did what I did. And so what. Whuppy fricken do. Your face is broken I see. What was it you said, ‘orbital fracture’. Good! You had that coming a long time ago. Consider that payback for what the Hell Razors did to me back in SWF. Dam near broke my back and where were you? No where to be found again. Convenient. I don’t forget my history, something you instilled on me when we first trained together. But please D, this new found fire, this piss and vinegar attitude you suddenly have. Who are you trying to fool. No one is buying it. I know you better than you know yourself. Trying talking all you want to build your psyche up, it won’t matter anyways. Any doubt you may have thought with me, ended with that chairshot, along with all my feelings for you. Frankly, it felt really good, bashing your head in like that. Seeing that crimson spray from your forehead, the divot in your head. Hey I know, I’ll call you divothead from now on! Divothead! Divothead! Divothead! Ahhhhh, that’s much better. Of course, seeing the lineup this week, you somehow managed to avoid me again. How atypical of you. What’s matter D, afraid to come to grips with reality? Afraid to recognize that I’m lightyears better than you now. You couldn’t even defeat that rookie Matt Boone! Whereas I, The Heartbreaker, took him out, doing what you couldn’t. Its kind of been my career in a way, doing what you couldn’t, well except for one thing, and that blemish will be fixed before it is all said and done. But really D, calling my by my real name, how childish. The Ashley you knew doesn’t exist anymore. I am, and always will be, The Heartbreaker, better get used to it D. The best dam thing you ever had in your life is gone. That little tramp Magdalena or whatever her name is, is no where near my league. Everyone knows that. Still though, enjoy the little slut, it’s the only action you’ll be getting anymore. Just promise me one thing though, no excuses when I kick your ass all over that ring at Faith! Be a man about it. Admit it. Admit it to the world, that HBC is better than you, and always have been. If not, I’ll just have to break the other socket. That will be wicked fun! Now, this week, who the hell are these two retards! Gerico and Strictly Prohibited? Richard Gerico? Smoking boy? This bullshit is Thug Roc’s doing, I know it is. I mean really, someone of my stature shouldn’t be wasting their time with these two inferior bargain-basement bingo parlor wrestlers from the backwoods. I’m the goddam HeartBreak Chick for crying out loud! The industry top cruiserweight! I deserve better than this. I deserve a shot at Sovereign! Or I’ll even lower it, Thug Roc to get my title back, the title he STOLE from me! What the hell does a girl have to do around here to get some serious competition! Half the cruiserweight roster ran away instead of facing me. Not that I blame them! So yes, Legends XII, I get two dipsluts trying to be a wrestler and showing off for their little lady in the cheap seats. Strictly Prohibited thinks he knows me. Beating off to my Playboy pictorial doesn’t constitute as knowing me. Playing my character on Playstation 2 doesn’t constitute as knowing me. Being in my fanclub, watching my old wrestling videos, none of it matters. Boy, you don’t know shit about me. So keep your wet-dream fantasies to yourself. No one gives a rat’s ass about you anyway. After I’m done smacking you around, maybe I’ll smack around that girlfriend of yours as well. S&M is really hot!! Especially when I’m the dominatrix, and you, like everyone else, are my bitches! And Gerico. Somehow he gets one win and now as the pleasure of facing me, The HBC. Joy! Sucks to be you! Gone your whole life trying to make something of yourself and just when your on the brink of something, its derailed by me. Don’t take it too personal. I’ve better people way better than you. But at least you can say, yeah, I fought the very best their was, I fought HBC and though she kicked my ass all over the place, I was beaten by the best. Every fed needs a Brooklyn Brawler, guess you got the job. Legends XII, the Emerald City of Nawleans! (For those in the know), HBC is going to do what she does best, break two more people’s heart and then going to get so shit-faced on Bourbon Street with my comrades of the Unlimited! And if you don’t like it…….Blow me!
