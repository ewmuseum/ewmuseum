Thoughtless Thinking

13 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-13 You did it on MY time not yours… Those words had stuck with him all week. Out of all the words he’d heard James Kimson speak when they were opposite each other in the middle of the ring last week, these were the ones that had repeated over and over in his brain. You all thought it would have been the ones that revolved around brutality didn’t you? Asis is a bit misunderstood. As with most human beings he does have the ability to think, to rationalise, to think before his actions. He has the little voice inside his head telling him what to do. The thing most people call a conscience. He just doesn’t very often listen to it. He has been teaching himself though, things like restraint and patience, all so his revenge on Xander would be sweeter. Oh wait, you thought that beating he got at Legends was it? People have to wake up. Why would he spend the last month stalking him, watching his every movement, planning things in his head as he watched from the shadows, if he’d wanted it over and done with? If that were the case he would’ve done it a month ago. See when Asis is alone, when he’s away from eVo, that’s when he does his thinking. When he’s around the arenas, the people, the atmosphere, that’s when the instincts take over, the little bit of rationality is thrown out of the top window of a multi storey building and the fists come into play. The instinct of survival is most powerful. You did it on MY time not yours… Back to that though, he’d been thinking about that, what that really meant. Whilst Kimson probably sat in a luxury five star hotel somewhere, sipping champagne from crystal glasses, Asis sat on the side of a road somewhere, deep in thought, completely alone. He’d been thinking about the arrogance, the selfishness of what those words meant. The fact that Kimson seemed to think that he had “a time” within eVo. A time where he was supposed to be the one with the belt? The champion? The one all the fickle idiots in the crowd cheer mindlessly week in week out because that’s how it seems they should react? If that was the case, Asis didn’t want “his time”. But Kimson was right about a few things on Wednesday night. Yes he wanted revenge on Xander, but the two half hearted attacks on him so far were no where near qualified revenge. And he was right about Asis planning his return. He thought while he ruined one man’s night he might as well deprive the other of all that he deemed worthy in his life. All seemed a bit empty to him. If people thought all they were worth was a leather strap with a bit of tacky gold on it, then Asis thought they deserved to be punished. He didn’t interfere to get himself a title shot. He did it to hurt Xander and to piss off James. There was no real reason behind it, kind of a thoughtless thinking. Asis wasn’t scared of Kimson. He wasn’t scared of Xander, come to think of it he didn’t really know fear. And a man without fear is deadly.
