Irish..I see the corperate world is going national..

14 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-14 The scene opens as we see X standing in front of the eVo cameras. The location can not be seen as it is a very dull lighted place. X is wearing a black shirt with the words Black Label on the front in white. He has on some black baggy jeans with some brown timberland boots. He stands in front of the cameras looking at the cameras in a very angered manner.. 'I told them about my idenity before Legends. I warned them about my true background..Just to make sure my warnings went unnoticed..I felt the need on revisiting Ali.' He stands with a bit of a smile on his face as he still focused on the eVo cameras. 'The war has begun in the cell divison. The corperate world didnt see this comming. They didnt see the other side of the bottom dollar..They only looked at it one way..face up..or in their terms..face value.' He goes away from the camera as the eVo camera crew sets up one of there lights so they can see exactly where they are. It seems to be the edge of a old bridge. As the cameras scan the place they see off in the distance the newer bridge that has been built. They begin to follow X as he is walking up the old bridge. As he is walking he says.. 'Has you looked over to the right of you. You saw what our uncle sam thought would be better for the community. Let me break it down simple for ya. This bridge we stand on goes through a town the locals call Olie. Well that town Olie is more of the 'lower' class. Well the class that corperate america trys to label the black man. That bridge was built on one soul purpose. To avoid having to cross the water an going through Olie...' He stills keeps walking as you can see the angered look on his face. He then reaches the top of the bridge.. 'They dont want us here. No one wants X even in the cell divison. But thats what so great about the divison. Its not promotion value. Its not what you can or cant sell. Its all about the bottom dollar. The one people dont get to see when they use to turn on eVol. The one that has the rejects from corperate america..' Looks down over the water.. 'As I look out on top of this bridge, one thing comes to my mind. The movemant, the change of the face of corperate america. The 'typical' look you all no. The typical society that you live in..it all is changing. But why do you ask. How can the cell divison change all of that?..' He shakes his head no.. 'Those who ask that question are no other then the ignorant people who ride the coat tails of the corperate world. The ones who live up to the picture that has been painted far to many times. Looking over this water my peopel..It reminds me why I am the black label. Why I am here to change everything. Our whole world, no matter what 'level' of society you are placed on, it all revolves around one thing. The thing that has kept corperate america so powerful and untouchable for so long..The one thing that no matter where you are placed..you turn to it..the televison.' He smiles.. 'Are those of the ignorant starting to understand. Are you starting to get the just of things The Black Label is starting the takeover of the cell. All to gain the TV spotlight. You must takeover what you hate in order to win. Its like any old war that has ever been fought.Except this one has no treatys. This one only has two levels..two spots..the glory of victory..and the agony of defeat.' He looks down as he drops a rock down and waits to hear the small splash.. 'That sound..Its funny how you dont hear it in the air..only as it hits its destination can you hear the rock splash into the water..' Takes a pause as he does the same thing over by dropping another rock.. 'Damon, you dont see it comming. For god sakes you are from ireland...You like many others are blinded..The corperate world has brought you here to try and bring the face value to the cell. To try and bring the established image they have in the regular ring, to the cell. ITs not working like that.Fighters are the ones who are the real men..Kid, you have yet to step in the ring with a real man. Understand?...' 'So as you are home watching this. I want you to do me a favor. If there is one thing you understand out of my whole interview..Let this be the key. You see, most people come out in front of eVo an bash their opponents. Why doesnt The Black Label do it?..Why doesnt the mark of X tell the world his true feelings about Damon?..' 'Its simple Damon..this whole war..its not about you. You are no more then another fallen soldier. A piece of meat thrown to the dogs..When its said an done..Way beyond our match..as the time of war will be close to ending..You will see why the mark of X does it..you will see why X is the true untouchable....you will see exactly why... He pauses as he gets right up in the cameras face and says... 'I am...the black label..' He then pushes the cameras out of his face as he walks on by with a angered look. The cameras catch him walking off the bridge as they begin to try and follow him but he jumps in his car and leaves as his tail laights are the last thing we see as the scene turns black...
