Date: 01/05/03 When I got home, everything was just like it always is.. My mother in the kitchen cooking, my father watching TV in the den.. He must have been home early from work, past couple weeks thats just how its been. He's been getting back earlier and earlier.. My brothers were already home and upstairs doing they're homework.. Seemed like perfect harmony? Maybe. I'm sure they all thought so. I couldn't stand it though, I hated how everything was always ok. How a dispute was rare. It didn't seem real. That was the last thing at my mind at the time though.. I had Heather Lexington's phone number. That was the first phone number I have ever gotten from a girl.. Of course I was nervous to call, and I didn't wanna call as soon as I got home either. I woulda seemed a bit desperate if I did that, hell.. I don't think I could have done that, I was never so nervous in my life.. I sat in my room for awhile just starring at the peice of paper that the number was on.. I wanted to go into my parents room and dial the digits but I couldn't bring myself to it.. Not yet.. The way 'Heather' was writting on the paper just drug me in. Her hand writting was so perfect. The way she wrote the number, a bit of a foward slant, showing she was in a bitch of a rush.. But not sloppy at all. Amazing, thats the only way she could be explained.. I even smelled the paper a couple times.. A bit weird? Yeah, but still. I never had feelings like this before. I never wanted to be with someone, a girl, this bad.. I couldn't help but think of her. Being at the age where girls were supposed to be on my mind all the time, and they weren't.. I guess it classified as being weird to my friends.. I didn't have girls on my mind.. I have Heather on my mind.. I could careless about any of these other girls, it was just her that I wanted anything to do with. Minutes went by... Than hours.. A couple of hours.. I wanted, needed to call her.. Hmpf.. That was it.. I was going to do it.. Now..
