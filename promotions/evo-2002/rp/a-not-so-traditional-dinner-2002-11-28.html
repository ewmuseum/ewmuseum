A not so traditional dinner.

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.28.02 ~~The scene picks up inside of an old fashioned hotel room. A bed with a red comforter that has recently been slept in is dead ahead. The alarm clock to the right of the bed reads '7:40'. Its the beginning of another long day for a professional wrestler. Steam rolls from under the door of the bathroom indicating that the wrestler is indeed in the shower. You can hear some music playing in the bathroom as well. Some clothes are laid out on the bed ready to be worn. You can hear the water turn off and the bathroom door flings open. Shocka steps out with a towel wrapped around him and his hair wet still. His goatee is shaven down to just his face. He doesn't look like a very happy man at this point in time. He mumbles something to himself.~~ 'Why in the hell didn't we get Thanksgiving off?' He moves to the bed and looks over his clothing. A growl comes from his stomach and he looks down... 'Where in the hell could I get some turkey?' He questions himself as another growl comes... 'I know I'll call room service....' ~~He sits down by the phone on his bed and dials 7 for Room service but nothing happens it just rings and rings. He rolls his eyes and slams the phone down again speaking to himself.~~ 'The Kitchen doesn't open until 8 damnit...' Shocka speaks with a snarl in his voice. 'Guess...I'll just have to work on my game plan for tonight...I mean I have to team with Morpheus Omega...how can I trust him...I mean we have beaten the hell out of eachother many, many times before...and now he just shows up...something doesn't sound right about that to me...' ~~Shocka begins drying himself off and puts his clothes on, they seem to be some workout clothes. He wears a pair of sweat pants and an eVolution tank top. He grabs his key card and gym bag and heads out of his room. He gets into the elevator and heads to the lobby for some Continental breakfast. In the elevator he speaks to himself.~~ 'Continental breakfast for the best continental champion ever...that sounds about right...I wonder if they will name a breakfast after me when i am the greatest universal champion ever...I can see it now! The UNIVERSAL breakfast!' ~~A smirk comes over his face as the elevator slows to a stop to pick up someone else. It just happens to be Morpheus Omega himself. Shocka stands in the corner of the elevator just staring at Omega as he steps in.~~ 'How ya doin bro?' Omega speaks. 'I'm fine.' Shocka says. 'Come on...you do realize that I am going to prove to be the death of you if you do not follow what i say right?' 'What?' Shocka asks. 'You heard me...and if you want me to be a loyal partner then I am going to...but if you don't trust me...if you even don't have the slightest bit of faith in me...I will make your life a living hell!' ~~The elevator stops on the next floor and Omega walks away from the elevator. As the scene slowly fades to Shocka in the gym working out. He is on the tread mill running at full sprint he goes step for step for about 5 minutes before he slows it a little. He continues going and the machine beeps and comes to a stop as Shocka looks down at it.~~ 'That was five miles?' He shakes his head and jumps off grabbing a towel to wipe the sweat from his face. 'I guess next week I will have to start running 10 miles...' FADE.
