Life for Life... final part.

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up inside a limousine with very dim light. All corners of the limousine dark, except the corner the shadowed figure of Morpheus can be seen but barely. The limousine driving down the road because the figure can be seen barely jolting from bumps on the road or terrible driving but none the less the vehicle moving. Morpheus with his face half down but, a mean look and that trademark grin arching across his face can be seen. Morpheus speaks like he’s talking down to another individual, 'What are you really trying to prove with this whole act and charade of appearing out of ghetto hell and making these veil threats and accusations? You have weary feelings about tonight’s police act but I swear I’m innocent' Morpheus pauses taking a breath. Morpheus continues speaking, 'You say you have me locked down on charges on a crime that would put me away for twenty five to life but you will not tell me what this charge actually is? Something’s very shady here. If you don’t show me anything solid soon, you will feel my unholy wrath' Morpheus with that sick grin arching on his face like it hadn’t been seen in quite some time. Through the darkness with the shadow portrayed over his face, it resembles the face of death almost. There’s pause in the air, the other individual hasn’t spoke yet. Suspension building with no response from the man with what he claims to have is all control of this situation. A man laughs, a very familiar one. The unknown man speaks, 'Its simple what I want from you. I have heard sick story after sick story of your wrestling profession. Things never imagined to be seen or even heard of, almost like its all myth. Former Final Destiny Wrestling Unanimous Champion who held only reign of that title and recent announced news, first draft hall of famer. Impressive to some but never to me...' The man laughs with a sick tone in his voice, he continues speaking. 'Where we are going is somewhere you can finally prove yourself. Where we will contend against some of the toughest most violent, sickest of contenders yet. Like it or not we will be appearing at Evolution's, Rise of the Legends!' The man speaks louder finishing the last of his words. Morpheus leans forward with his hand pointing, like he’s threatening him at the same time. Morpheus is quick to respond speaking, 'What do you want from me. I gave up the profession not because I cant contend or decimate every opponent I face but because of boredom of history repeating itself to many times. With me destroying rosters, winning the top championship and the federation closing. What’s your purpose of black mailing me and bringing me to Rise of the Legends?' The man laughs loudly again and speaks, 'Because from what I hear, your the man who breaks legacies and best of all you kill legends, deprive them of the show light they were never meant to have. By the word of mouth, you are the deadliest athlete in the game but you haven’t proved nothing in my eyes, you have a lot to prove yet Omega!' Strong tension builds in the small area of the limousine. Morpheus through the dim light can be seen panting like rage is building inside him that he’s about to take out. Morpheus speaks words spoke with a lot of hatred towards this individual 'As the expression goes, don’t poke the bear because the bear will poke back. You think you can win these mind games your trying to play to force me back into the wrestling industry, like you are backing me into a corner. You have no clue who or what you are fucking with. When all said and done I will triumph and you will fall to your miserable end of existence' Morpheus loud, trying to show dominance and control. The unknown man replies, 'Morpheus what if someone were to say follow you around long enough and lets not even say this was in the last short while and almost document your move and study you like their prey, acknowledging your every step among the way. Do you think after long enough they would have something solid on you, enough to put you down for along time?” ... The man pauses. Morpheus replies quickly, “Impossible no one could complete such task because I’m the perfect soldier… I don’t know myself so how could someone else know my move or think they are close to knowing what I think and feel? How sometimes its like I step out of reality, like I’m not coming back. No one can stop, stalk or defeat me in the mind or physically for that matter. If someone was stalking and watching my move they would of been taken out in ways that you wouldn’t think to imagine or even dare to dream about' The man continues speaking, 'What if you were out smarted and thrown the test of life and death. We laid down the perfect opportunity for the moment of shock, and you lashed out in one of your episodes and killed someone and didn’t remember. I have reviewed your medical files and I know you are prone to these types of black outs' The man laughs this time in all control and speaks with a sick tone of voice filled with negative energy, 'Then you my friend would be absolutely fucked. You better listen to me, or you will have the book thrown at you openly. All this would take is one tape of evidence mailed to police head quarters and your life and career would be done. Check mate, Morpheus!' Morpheus breathing heavy and that sick crazed tendency is back flowing through his veins intensely, the adrenalin rushing through his entire body. Morpheus casually grins and responds, 'Check maybe but not Mate. You may have won this first of mind games but you surely have not one the war. You may think you have the upper hand on this situation but I assure that this high horse you seem to be sitting on. You can be knocked off quicker than you think. You really don’t know whom your fucking with son. You think for now you have control but in all reality it’s me who has control. The control of knowing what I know, the things I have done and what’s planed for you when this ends. I assure this will End!' Morpheus snarls with his eyes locked on the darkened opposite side of the limousine, staring the direction of the unknown man. Energy filled within this vehicle and strong hatred building within Morpheus towards the man of mystery identity. The face of Morpheus Omega can be seen with one of his sick grins that portrays in never ending nightmares. The scene ends...
