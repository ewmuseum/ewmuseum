The Book of Cold:Chapter Four:

29 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-29 The Book Of Cold:Chapter 4: The lost of a loved one Age:19 I remember it all to well. I was 19 and still trying to achieve my wrestling dream. By this time I had stopped with the backyard wrestlilng. I was being teached the art of the game by a man we called Solid Sean. He was a young man who left wrestling way before he should of. I loved training with him as he could always relate to things going on in my life. Anything that bothered me he would be there for me. Anyway, I would always go to the gym as we would meet five times a week. The rest of the time I was lifting weights. My whole life was dedicated to it. I will never forget that cold thursday. I got up from my apartment and went to the gym like always as Sean and I did our routine. Like always, Sean would make sure I was burnt at the end. He would always make me work harder and harder each day. By the time we were through I thought I was about to pass out. But like always, I jumped in the showers and refreshed myself..But this time..when I got out and was getting dressed..I got the call that you only have nightmares about.. It was my mother. When I answered the phone all I could hear was screaming. She was yelling in the phone.. 'Aaron..Aaron..Gavin is in the hospital..he has been in a accident..' When I herd those words I dropped the phone as everything was trying to set in. I quickly picked the phone back up..I began to talk to my mom..trying to calm her down so I could understand more of what she was saying.. 'Momma, shhh..Calm down..tell me momma..what happened?' She then tried to collect herself. But even when she tried it was hard not to stop the tears from coming. She then got on the phone and began to explain what had happend.. 'Well Aaron, your dad was coming home from work on the interstate. And you know him, not the patient type, passed these two big trucks an then saw he was about to pass the exit to he went to zoom over and the back end of the car was clipped by the truck as he then flipped and went smashing into the woods. ' I just didnt want to believe it. Everyone doesnt think of how common things like going home from work could be your last time on this earth. I had to ask the question that I didnt know if I wanted to hear the answer too..but never the less..I asked mom.. 'What are they saying?' That question took all the breath in me as I then felt like I was hit in the stomach where the wind was knocked out of me..I then just heard mom say the thing you never wanted to hear from anybody about anyone.. 'It doesnt look good..I think you need to come down here.' I then told here I would be there right away as I hung up and gathered my things and went straight to the hospital. On my way out I was stopped by Sean as he was wondering why I looked upset. I then told him about the phone call that I just had as he told me he would be at the hospital later and good luck... The whole way to the hospital I just listened to the radio as I was trying to get my mind off of things. But with anyone who has had this bad of news..its always on your mind. I then remember thinking that when I see my dad..he might not even be able to say anything to me..I then began to doubt everthing I have done up to this point. All the time I have spent away from my family because of the dedication I had put towards this business. All the sacrifices I have made. I then begun to think of what my dad thought of me being away..that maybe he thought I didnt like spending time with him..maybe he didnt know how much I cared for him.. With every thought that came into my head made me just press on the gas that much more. As I felt that this was going to be it and I wanted to see me father..at least one last time. I wanted to tell him everything that he might wonder..I wanted him to know that his only son would not let him down..I would not let his name go.. When I arrived to the hospital I was very scared. As when I got out of the car I could only see like all the negatives in the place. I saw the ambulances pulling in with bodies. I saw old people walking around with pain. I tried to just block everything out and keep on track of my dad. I went in the emergency room as I looked around and saw my mom. She then explained to me that dad was in room 123. I looked as behind her came out preacher John. John walked up an took my moms shoulder as they then began to pray. I left there side as I still wanted to see my father. I still wanted to tell him everything I felt.. I went running down the halls. Looking at every door to make sure I havent passed it yet. I then saw the number..123. I then ran and opened it as I saw a doctor leaving. The doctor came up to me as I could tell he was getting ready to tell me bad news..He walked up to me as he said.. 'I am sorry..he has only a few more minutes..but you can try an talk to him.' I ran by my dads side as now I was torn to shreads. I was still in my gym cloths as I begin to tell my dad everything so he would know how much I cared for him ..how much he ment to me. 'Dad,Im sorry..Ill do anything you want me to do. Dont leave me. Ill stop training an watch over you..Dad, I love you.' At that second..he rolled his head over and looked at me. It was like for a brief time all the pain had left his body. That he couldn't feel anything..As he told me in the calmest voice.. 'Aaron,be a wrestler..its in your heart..and know son..that I love you..' He then grabbed my hand as I knelt down at the bed as just grabbed him as hard as I could..All of a sudden his hand stopped moving..and I could hear nothing..I then herd something go off..but now everything was blurry..I just didnt want to admit it to myself..My eyes were filled with water.My heart had split open..As I just couldnt tell myself he is gone..my mom came in and placed her arms around me..As that rest of the night I didnt want to talk to anyone..I didnt want to discuss it.. About four days after his burial I still stayed in my room. Just watching TV and listening to music.Asking about a thousand questions that I had no answers to. I heard a knock on the door as I got up an answered it. It was Sean. He came to see when I wanted to start back with the training. I told him I didnt know. He then asked what did my dad tell me.. I then begin to process that night slowly in my head as I then remembered the first thing he told.. 'Be a wrestler..' As I said that Sean smiled and just noded his head yes..As I then told him I wanted to start tomorrow. Sean said great and walked out as I then just sat back down in my room thinking..I knew that dad knew everything all along. He knew how much I cared for him..He also knew how much I cared for wrestling..As I then said.. 'Dad..I will make you proud..'
