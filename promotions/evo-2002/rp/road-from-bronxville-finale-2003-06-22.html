Road from Bronxville: Finale

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-22 “What the fuck you looking at?” Terlayne sat recording the fat American’s who walked past him as he sat like a homeless person waiting for food. The bemusing American’s continued to bemuse him and he wasn’t prepared to get up. “Saggy tits, saggy tits. What are they feeding you?” A woman with relatively saggy tits came to a halt on the street and gazed into the camera’s screen, a look of annoyance on her face. “Saggy tits, saggy tits, it’s not your fault.” The woman dismissed Terlayne’s remarks and continued on her way to wherever it was she was going. “Fucking… saggy… titted… bitch.” Nowt was happening, much to Terlayne’s bemusement so he decided to go in search of some action of his own. On his way to… wherever he was on his way to he came to a halt at a middle aged American waving into the camera. “Oy, could you like NOT do that?!” Terlayne remarked as he continued on his way to wherever he was going. “Fucking yanks.” Terlayne had felt relatively baffled so to speak about the events of two nights previous. But nothing in the constitution says that there’s anything wrong with kicking a hermaphrodite’s head in, which pleased Terlayne. Later that day… A frustrated Terlayne stares into the screen of his camera. After a sub-standard, to say the least match Terlayne’s had much of an out and out nowt day. After failing to convince 1...2…3…4..5.…16 producers to take on his new film “Terlayne Scrappin’ Around Out World” he’s decided to put a lull in productions for quite some time. Now he could concentrate more on his main objective. That one objective that made his time in Evolution worth while. What the bloody hell was it? Ah bollocks, there goes. Wait for further updates to see what this was. But now, it had become apparent that some people in this country didn’t like Terlayne to say the least, maybe even hate him. Firstly there was that twat Daniel Somers. Now what’s the bloody twat gone and done? Well nothing yet actually but Terlayne had a hunch. Then there were the pigs, which brings us to Terlayne’s obsession. “Pig,” Terlayne muttered to a cop who stood across the pavement from him. The bobby, paying no attention to Terlayne who coughed and turned his head the opposite direction whilst whistling. “Pig,” Terlayne continued as the camera zoomed in on a nervous police officer who appeared to be losing his temper. “Eh you fat bastard.” Three others arrived opposite the other cop as Terlayne yelled once more… “PIGS.” Each of the cops having obviously lost the temper by now at Terlayne they approached Terlayne who pointed the camera at an innocent looking negro child. The cops were quick to take action against the kid as Terlayne shook his head in dismay. …And they say I’m prejudice
