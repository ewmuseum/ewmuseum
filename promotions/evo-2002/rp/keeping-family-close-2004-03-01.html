Keeping Family Close

1 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-01 “A Damn fool! That’s what you are!” Ricky slams down the cordless phone, as he leans back on his couch. His ribs bandaged up from Legacy, and his attack from Psymon. He lies back on the couch and stares at the ceiling, before reaching behind him and grabbing the cordless phone. He punches in a few numbers then stops, he looks outside through the partially pulled curtains then dials the remaining numbers. A few rings from the other end when a answering machine picks up. “Hi you’ve reached the home of Debbie, Thomas, Shane, and Ricky, leave a message and we’ll get back to you as soon a possible.” “Yo, pops. You and mom really need to change the recording. I miss him just as much as anyone else, but fact of the matter is….” A voice interrupts from the other end… “Ricky? Is that you?” Shocked and not expecting to hear anything he stutters over his words before muttering “y..y..yeah? Who is this?” “Ricky it’s your uncle Sean.” Ricky sits up on the couch and lowers his head the phone still pressed to his temple. “I didn’t want to answer the phone, in case it was a private call. But when I heard it was your voice, I really wanted to talk to you. I heard about your signing. It is huge news back home. How is everything there?” “Life is good” Ricky replies “I mean, everything is going great. Did you see my debut match?” “Unfortunately not. You see, we don’t get eVo here. Apparently it isn’t a big deal to the Aussie locals ya know? Ricky, can I ask you something…” “Sure thing Sean, as long as it wasn’t about the message…” comes Ricky’s reply. “Ricky you DO know…” Sean is cust off in mid-sentence as Ricky interrupts him “Yes, I know as well as you do, but I like to hear the voices at times. That is why it remains.” “Ricky, you were leaving a message” “I guess I was just hoping someone would pick up” Ricky answers. “I think we both know that wouldn’t happen.” Sean replies Ricky chuckles a bit as he says “But someone did answer, and it kind of scared the shit out of me” “So Sean when are you coming to America to see me wrestle? I mean you have yet to see me in action. You would never come see me when I was still independent in Aussie. So how about it? You wanna come out and see me wrestle?” “Aw Ricky I don’t know. I mean with everything going on here, I just don’t know if I could get away” “What else have you got to do? I mean it’s not like we have an abundance of family there to look after” Sean sighs a bit over the phone, yet does not reply. Ricky can hear there is something bothering him in his voice. “What is it Sean? Something is wrong.” “Ricky, I need to tell you….Mom passed away.” “What? When? I mean how? She was so healthy” Ricky raises his head still seated on his couch with a bewildered look on his face. “She passed away yesterday Ricky. Self inflicted gunshot wound.” “Yesterday? That was the anniversary….of it all….” “I know Ricky. Shane passed away, and your parent’s horrible accident, and now this. I just don’t think she could take it. Debbie was the only family she had left. After dad walked out on us, I mean it was just us 2, and your family. Then everything fell apart, and she just couldn’t take it…” Sean begins to sob a bit trying to control his emotions. Ricky lowers his head shaking it slowly side to side. “Sean, I need you to do me a favor.” “Anything I can Ricky” Ricky takes his time in replying “I need you to come to America” “What? Why?” Sean exclaims “Sean, we are the only family we have left. I don’t’ want to lose that. I would have never asked this of you before because of Jessie. I know how close you were, but now, there is nothing left for us in Aussie. Please Sean, come to America.” “Ricky, I can’t….” “Sean…” “Let me finish Ricky. As I was saying, I can’t afford it. I am busted right now. It will be months before there is any insurance money.” “Sean your ticket will be at the airport when you get there. I will go back with you when it is time to settle up with the insurance. You can stay with me, and we will get everything worked out.” Ricky explains. “Ricky, I can’t live off of my kid nephew” states Sean. “You asshole, you are 2 years older then me.” Both men share a laugh at the comment. “Seriously Sean, I got my signing bonus, so I have PLENTY of money. Get your ass to the airport, and I will get you a flight out ok?” “It’s a deal Ricky. I mean you are right, there is nothing left for us here. When should I go?? Sean asks. “Be there by 8pm your time tonight. I will have the flight sometime around then. I look forward to seeing you again Sean, oh and one more thing, my place is a shithole. I kinda got into a depressive state….you know what, there will be plenty of time to talk. I will see you in a few days.” Ricky hangs up the phone not giving his uncle any time to interrogate him. Ricky grabs the phone book and looks up the number to the different airlines. After a few calls he books a flight for his uncle, out of Aussie at 8:14 pm. Ricky again picks up the phone, and dials a few numbers, from the other end a voice can be heard answering the phone. “CCW Enterprises, Annette speaking.” “Annette, Ricky Thunder, patch me into Spyder’s office” After a brief pause, the phone extention rings once, then a voice is heard on the other end. “Spyder, Ricky again” “Ah, Ricky, so you have reconsidered?” “No, I still think you are a damn fool, but I need a favor” “Let me get this straight Ricky, I am a fool, yet you need a favor from me?” “No, you are a damn fool, and yes I need a favor from you” “What can I do for you Mr. Thunder?” “I need my brother’s records. I know CCW shut down, however, the corporation still stands and you have his files. I need copies or something. I need some hard copy stuff on him.” “No can do Ricky. This is all confidential information” Spyder replies “Spyder, come on! You know I am his only surviving family! Aside from his uncle, but I am the only immediate family he has left. Am I going to sue you? No, I just need this for emotional and personal reasons.” “Sorry Ricky, you know the rules. Anything else I can do for you?” “You are a god damn fool!” Ricky slams down the phone, as he flops back down on the couch. I gotta get those records somehow.”
