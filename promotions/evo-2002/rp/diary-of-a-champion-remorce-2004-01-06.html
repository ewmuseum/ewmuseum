Diary of a Champion: Remorce

6 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-01-06 How interesting things keep happening to me it seems. You know, almost 4 months ago I guess it would be, when I pinned Xander at X-rated for the Universal Championship in that historic, epic match between him, Asis and myself I had no idea that it was going to be like this. Don’t get me wrong, I knew that this wasn’t going to be easy. But who would have anticipated it to happen as it has. With Leya’s aquisition of the Committe’s Number 1 seat of power thanks to some interesting moves on her part it seemed like for certain that sooner, rather than later she would get what she wanted. Although I highly doubt that she’s compeletly sure she knows what she wants she at least has a good impression of it. She wants me laying at her feet begging for my career so that she can snatch it up in her hands laughing like the Bitch that she is. Yeah, I got involved with her personal life. Brother or not, I was not going to stand and let her throw away everything she worked for just because of who she was married to. And in all honesty again it’s gone so far past Sovereign that he isn’t even important to what’s going on. It went from being completely about him to now he’s just a shadow of the past and now all that matters is Leya, her revenge and me and my attempt to spoil her fun. I guess it’s my stubbornness that was a family trait. I worked my God damn ass off all my life to sit here at the top of the mountain in this industry. Title after title I fought, I cried and I hurt all over my body. Broken Bones and broken spirits are part of this business and I have seen and experienced it all. That goes with the job description. It’s like you sign up on an application and say “I am willing to completely destroy my body and mind and ego for next to no pay and no social life for the chance to even get a shot at the big time and maybe make some real money and perform in front of more then a couple dozen high school kids or drunks trying to get out of the cold.” I worked those gyms with those drunks and high school kids and I worked my God damn ass off for those people who paid to see me. And if they didn’t come specifically to see me I made sure that the next time they were coming to see me. I wasn’t a huge fan of failure, nor was I very much into not being the focus of any sort of event. James Kimson, born to be a star somewhere or another and now that I really felt that I reached the uptmost that I could reach here comes little ole Leya that is hell bent on destroying me. She tried at Faith with Shocka, failed and she paid the price. Now she’s tried at Rise of Legends with Thug roC and once again I turned her away with a victory over that little punk ass bitch. It was a hard match, how can you get a leg up on your opponent when you can’t dictate a pace with a dozen guys outside the ring all foaming at the mouth trying to get their paws on you. Either way, in the end I managed to get the win. A tainted win, perhaps. That’s all well and good because one good screwjob deserves another. And at this point I’m willing to do just about anything to keep this God damn title from anywhere near Leya or her hired muscle whomever that happens to be on a month to month basis. But the highlight, and I suppose low light at Rise of Legends was when I did something that perhaps I should not have done when I put my hands on Leya. Lets face it ladies and Gents, she deserved what she got that night, far more then what happened at Faith with Shocka’s misguided chair shot. I did it this time, completely intentional. Basically, I want to try and explain myself to all those out there that might be thinking ‘how could he to something like that to his own blood sister!’ Well I’m asking myself that right now as I’m sitting here on this bench in the arena afterwards. It’s 3 in the morning, and I haven’t left yet because I’ve been doing somethinking. I guess to that I wanted to avoid some fans who wanted autographs or whatnot. Hardly do I ever do things like that. But I have had to sit here and think. Was the Universal Title work putting my Sister in the hospital? No, I don’t think it was. But was it in the heat of the moment or did I think it over? And then, now I can’t help but think of the consequences of my actions. Not as much perfessional but personal. Maybe before tonight there was a way that we could mend the wounds that have been opened up in recent months between us, but now? It’s all very trying, and I honestly don’t know what to think about it. You know, I actually got a phone call there an hour ago from my oldest sister and she gave me a good toungue lashing over it all. I sat there and nodded because I agreed with it. I didn’t have to hurt her. In fact, she was trying to escape from me when I did it. I guess that goes to show that sometimes people do bad things regardless of how hard they try to do the right things. That sometimes you have to do something for yourself. Sometimes, emotion takes precidence over your common senses, and that happened. It pains me to think that if I even go to the hospital to try and visit her that there’d be no way in hell she’d even see me. Hell, even after all the grief she’s given me, things she’s been responsible for and taken full credit for I still feel guilty over all of this. I remember the beatings at the hands of Shocka and O’Halloran and all that bullshit. But I guess, I guess I just love my sister regardless of what kind of woman she has turned out to be. So now what’s next. I guess the answer is pretty simple, it’s Asis and Tainted Love. A lot of thoughts go through my head when I think about Asis. First and formost it’s quite clear that this man is a Monster. That doesn’t frighten me in the least because I’ve dealt with monsters before. That doesn’t make me rest easy though. Asis is a big, strong tough and determined wrestler with nothing to loose and everything to gain. Not to mention the fact that at Legends perhaps 4 or 5 months ago we fought to a draw in a match that could have been nominated for match of the year. I sit back and go over that match on tape sometimes because I thought it was a great match. Not to many times have I gone to a draw with someone and that forced me to respect Asis, for whatever reasons. The kid is a maniac, he’s a monster and he’s the Number 1 contender. And at X Rated we tied up again in that 3 way match with Xander where I won the Universal Championship. Since then Asis has not so quietly demanded attention. And even before he won last night he had mine. I watched that final match and knew, that regardless of how much I think Matt Hunt is a great talent that Asis would not be denied. I don’t know if on the inside he really honestly cares or not about the title. Somehow, like he mentioned it’s more about robbing it of someone who wants it more. But I think that Asis took this road because he wanted it to go just like this. He’s failed to beat me in the past. Now, he’s run through the entire company and climbed to the very peek again, looking across the summet of Mount Evolution at me with my flag burried deep into the hillside. And because Asis, you aren’t one of Leya’s little monkey’s I honestly can say that this is a match that I am looking forward to more then any other match I’ve had in eVo since my match at Unbreakable with Sovereign 10 months ago. That match was very personal, but that’s the past. And you and I, we’re going to have a match that will be epic and the winner of that match, weither it be you or I will be the most deserving Universal Champion that we’ve seen in the history of the belt. You for clawing your way from the lower card winning every single title in the company. Or for me, for holding on against all odds over and over again.
