History of One :: Chapter II

4 January 2003

Date: 01/04/03 Lexington.. That was Heather's last name.. After some thinking, it came to me.. For having such a refined last name you wouldn't think of her to be as she was. Its ahrd to explain it really, she wasn't really a fraky typem but she wasn't one to be a 'cool' kid. Neither of us were.. For some reason we just seemed to fit together. We seemed like two that would hang out. There wasn't many people like us at St. Mary's.. Yes, a catholic school.. There was no dress code or anything, but we did have strict rules and strict teachers.. I wasn't one to get along very well there.. Always some kind of problem, regardless of what it was. Whether it be a teacher on my case, me not doing my homework.. Anything, in a way I was trying to get kicked out.. I hated it there with a passion.. The stairs, the walls, the chalkboard. You name it, I hated it.. Most of the other kids pissed me off too.. All believing in some kind supior being and that they had to live by some book.. Nieve.. Only word to sum them up.. 'You live around here your whole life?' The conversation I wanted wasn't going as planned but I was trying.. Forcing myself really, any question that came to mind, I just blurted out.. 'Yeah, same house, and everything. Never moved.. We travel a little bit though.. During the summer and stuff... What about you?' I wasn't sure how to answer.. I was born in Germany, but I really didn't know much of it there, I wasn't even a year old when we moved here and this is where I have been my whole life.. I knew a bit about the country than though.. I figured I could strike up some sort of conversation, that being the point of intrest. 'No. I was born in Germany..' It seemed to catch her attention, she lifted an eye and smiled a bit.. Oh, her smile was one to remember.. Her face just glowed. I always loved her smile.. 'Germany eh? How long did you live there?' She seemed to actually think it was worth talking about.. 'Not long. I don't really remember it, only what I see in pictures..' 'I figured as much, no accent or anything you couldn't have lived there long.. Do you want to go back?' I liked knowing osmeone actually cared enough about my past to ask questions that could lead into the future about my past.. Ya know, it felt good.. She knew how to make me feel good... 'Yeah, kinda.. I want to see what I was born to ya know?.. I dunno, I doubt I ever will though..' Her smile went away a bit.. I was confused.. 'Why do you say that?' She seemed concerned.. 'I dunno. My family isn't exactly the traveling type.. And I don't know how much longer I'll be around them, so who knows.. Maybe one day when I'm older I'll have that kind of money..' I didn't wanna make it seem like I was in a rush to go back, but I wanted to make it clear that I wanted to go back... 'Yeah, I know what you mean.. I'd wanna go back there to.. My family normally stays within the country when they travel...' I just nodded. I didn't know what to say.. Class was about to let out. So we headed back to our lockers, luckily they were pretty close also.. We kept talking all the way to our lockers.. Eh, well more or less it was her talking and me just agreeing with it or nodding.. I was at a loss for words.. I would look at her and get nervous, or scared, or the words would be on the tip of my tongue but I couldn't say them.. The feelings inside me were so jumbled. She did give me her number though.. That never happen before that very moment...
