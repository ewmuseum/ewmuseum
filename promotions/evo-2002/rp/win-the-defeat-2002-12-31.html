Win the Defeat

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31st December 2002 The scene opens in Seara’s old Chicago apartment, it is a long time since she has been here, but she is not alone. Frost lays asleep in the next room, worn out from the long drive back here from the Target Centre in Minneapolis. Seara sits on the edge of a table looking out a window, the room that familiar darkness that had kept her hidden from the world until she had met Frost. Blood drips from her knuckles, and a few wooden chairs lay smashed into tiny pieces on the floor below her feet. Something seems to have snapped in Seara and she sits muttering to herself about the fight, what she say as those huge hooded figures appeared, but what she had seen had been something that had made her laugh deep inside, a hidden laugh, one that she had wanted to show Xander, one she had wanted Frost to witness, she was stronger now than she ever had been, and although she had lost to Unlimited’s leader, she was sure that she was stronger, and she seemingly had a plan to prove the new improved fighter in her. This was the remade, the new, the times had changed, Seara had been underrated in eVo for far too long, and Xander was the last shot, at Legends XX, she knew she would make Lee Exodus feel the mistakes Xander had made. His mistake to change her attitude, he had once again proved himself unworthy and unfair, a man who trumps himself up, yet cannot live up to his own reputations, a man who needed others to interfere every time he had business so that he came off on top, came out the winner. He had beat Seara, but she had won a war with herself, she had won the defeat. Xander, you think you were the smart one, you think that having huge men to come out during our match and secure you the win makes you better than I, you are sadly mistaken, and you Xander are quite pitiful. I was laughing as your little friends helped you beat me, why, I’ll let you in on a secret. At nightmare before Christmas when I seen your little friends, yes I knew they would obliterate me, wipe me out take me out of the equation, just so you could get the win over me, just because you were scared that a 123lb female would be able to beat you. But I was not laughing because I knew I might well be injured or dead by the time those huge men finished with me, I laughed because you proved what I suspected the whole time. Xander you are not a fighter, you are no good, you can now live knowing that you could not beat a little girl like me, you can think hard about that one, a man who thinks he is better than this federation, yet within that giant ego, you could not end me by yourself, you are nothing more than a pathetic cheating loser, and although I lost to you, although you managed to get that 3 count over me, you do not realise that I am the true winner, I never asked Scott to come out, I asked him to stay away from this match, and he obliged, that is until your goons came out just like you planned, Scott came out to stop you cheating, but unfortunately he could not completely stop you cheating your win. You Xander are a pathetic man, instead of personal strength and talent in the ring, you have the talent to manipulate people, Asis, HBC, everyone that has ever been under your command, all of them could do so much better without you involved in their careers. They are only under your command because you need them so you can look like a winner, it is sickening that you have to stoop so low to make yourself feel big and powerful and in control. Face the laughter yourself for once; actually do something as Xander, instead of Xander and his little gimp army. I’m sure after you felt a true defeat or a true win, something you accomplished yourself, you would realise it is a much more satisfying feeling to do something by yourself instead of relying on others. Become independent Xander, I know you would find it more liberating than having to baby-sit people like Asis, hoping that he will not realise your manipulation of him one day and destroy you. Seara pauses and rams her fist into the wall close by, smashing her knuckles against it with her full strength, you can hear a crack, and the female stops looking down at her hand and wrist. Pushing at her wrist she grits her teeth and with her left hand, she pushes her wrist full force and twists it, it jumps back into the socket and grinds loudly as it does, the cameraman cringes as the noise echoes throughout the empty room. Xander, you may have won the match, you may have been declared winner, but we both know that I truly won, I proved myself out there in that ring, which is more than you ever have or ever will be able to do. The female has barely finished her sentence when she suddenly begins to scream at the top of her voice, not words, just a loud yell of pain and anger, and as she screams she lunges her fists back and forth, hitting at the brick wall with more force than she has ever displayed before. The noise wakes the sleeping Frost, and he is quick to run to doorway to look in at Seara. As the male begins to shout over her screams, the scene fades to black, but all that can be heard from Seara now is the sounds of the female shouting Xander's name.
