Family Feud Part 1: Mom Vs. Dad

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/31/03 ~Ain’t revenge a bitch? It sure is. Back in December, instead of having christmas with my family and watching my kids open their presents. I was sent to the hospital thanks to Shocka himself. I bet he felt real good after doing that. I beat he thought he put me out of my career. But I bet he didn’t like Unbreakable. I bet he didn’t enjoy his unexpected ride out of the arena. Now, with New-TV one stronger, Shocka is on my hit list. But not just my hit list like before, but he’s on my personal hit list in which I keep on the top of my priority list. Shocka, this week will only be a sample of what’s to come for you in the near future…~ -=[We start off another day in the roC household with Thug roC sitting at the table with his wife Shannon. As Shannon eats her food, we see Thug roC going through bills and so on.]=- “What the hell?” Thug roC says with an almost angry voice. “How did the phone bill get so high?” “Those 1-900 numbers are on that list aren’t they?” Shannon asks with a smart tone to her question. “Uhhh….nevermind, I’ll pay it off.” Thug roC says trying to end the discussion quickly. “Well I have to go now…” “I thought we were going to spend some time together…” Shannon gives the innocent look to Thug roC, trying to appeal to roC’s sesitive side. “You spend all that time in the ring now, I thought we’d have some time to play some games.” “Sorry Shannon, but I told Mike I’d take him to the ring.” Thug roC says referring to their 17 year old son. “He wants to be in eVo some day, so I’m gonna train him a little bit.” Thug roC gets up, revealing that he had on some sweats and a sleeveless shirt, ready to work out. “Michael is going to be a star someday and I won’t have to be in the ring, instead I’ll have my son to look forward to watching.” “Yea cause we both know he got his brains from you.” Shannon says under her breath. “Usually,” Thug roC starts. “I’d take that as a compliment if the boy didn’t get a 520 on his SATs. But it doesn’t take brains to be a wrestler anyways. Look at Shocka, he was doing fine under my management. Michael will do the same.” “All I’m saying is that you spend a little more time with the family, instead of just Michael. You know, you do have two other kids besides him.” “Clare and Lisa are all for you, y9ou can spend as much time with them as you want.” Thug roC says. “That just prove how unselfish I am.” “Terrel, it’s either me or him!” Shannon states while crossing her arms. The two look at each other until a cell phone rings. “Shannon, if you want time, that’s all you had to say. I’ll give you as much time as you want.” Thug roC says, suddenly sounding senseitive. “Just let me run to the store real quick and get you something so that we can spend this ‘time’ that you want so much” “Really Terrel? You’re so sweet.” “I’ll be right back.” Thug roC gives Shannon a peck on the cheek and leaves the house for the store. Later that day… Thug roC is seen coming down the steps with Shannon sitting on the coach, waiting and anticipating. She doesn’t know what Thug roC has bought, nor what he has in mind. “Come on Shannon,” Thug roC says. “Come see what I got you.” “I’m so excited!” Shannon says, rushing up the steps and past Thug roC. The two show up in the master bedroom, but nothing seems out of the ordinary. “Where is it? I know I saw you come in here with a bunch of bags…” Shannon continues to look around. “Well…I know you say you want more time, so I got you soemthing. It’s in the closet.” Thug roC says with a smile on his face. “Matter of fact, let me take a picture, cause when you open that door, I don’t want to forget the look on your face.” Thug roC then pulls a camera out of a drawer in his dresser. Shannon creeps, step by step towards the door. She turns and looks at Thug roC with a smile right before she opens it. She opens the door, but nothing is there…Shannon looks around and sees a rope dangling down from the top shelf. Shannon is too short to see over the shelf so with curiousity, she pulls the rope. Thug roC is there with the picture as the shelf slants down and three bags full of watches and clocks come tumbling down ontop of Shannon. Multiple pictures are taken by roC as Shannon falls to the ground, being covered in watches off all different kinds. “That’s time for ya!” Thug roC says while laughing. “Terrel!” “Have a nice day sweets, I’ll be in the ring.” Thug roC leaves the room and the scene fades to black as Shannon isn’t looking happy at all.
