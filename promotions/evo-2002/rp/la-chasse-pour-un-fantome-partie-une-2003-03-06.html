La Chasse Pour Un Fantôme - Partie Une

6 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Translation: Hunting A Ghost - Part One 'Are you sure you want to help me on this little side jaunt?' 'It's no problem with me. I don't have any projects in my schedule. Just a few things that need to be taken care of at the Publisher's. That's about it.' 'Most of it will be driving. At least, for you it will be. I'll be on the road most of the time, so I won't be able to just roam away whenever I discover something.' 'Fine with me. So long as your paying me for gas and stuff.' '...Oh.' 'You weren't going to pay me?!' 'Well...I just didn't think you'd ask for money or anything.' 'You twisted little bitch.' 'Screw you slut.' 'Just get your shit so we can go.' 'I have to go back home first. Only for a couple days, and then I have to head to Portland.' 'To the Garden?' 'Yup.' 'Why?' 'Best way to face a ghost from the past, is to go there.' 'We don't have to visit your parents or anything, do we?' 'No. They won't even know I'm there.' 'Alright. Let's get going.' Fiona zipped her duffel bag. Jody had the things she needed in her car already. It was going to be quite a drive, actually. At least she had Jody there to accompany her. She could always keep things in perspective somehow. Helped to keep Fiona in line sometimes. She wouldn't be able to understand any of this, however. That, and Fiona didn't want to tell her anything anyways. She had twisted it into some kind of sick paranoia. Sometimes she felt as though she were exaggerating things. It wasn't a big deal. Let live, and let go. But then she'd come back down, and it'd seem that much more important a task. One which, she felt, had been put off for too long. It was alright though. She didn't waste the time she had. She didn't know what she was going to find back home, if anything. But as the car started, she knew it was a journey that wouldn't end until she accomplished her task. She tried before. Twice. But, they as they say, 'Best laid plans, laid to waste.' This was beginning to become a similar case. Maybe her third time would be the charm. The car pulled out of the driveway, and the hunt was on. The hunt to find a ghost.
