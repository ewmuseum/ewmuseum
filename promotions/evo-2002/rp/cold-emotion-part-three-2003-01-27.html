Cold Emotion... part three.

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in an undisclosed location with Morpheus resting his eyes shut, leaning back on a bar stool and only on the two legs. A glass half filled of alcohol with partially melted ice cubes sits in front of him, abandoned like. Carelessly he leans forwards and the bar stool slams back down on the four legs. Morpheus snarls while staring down at the glass, almost unimpressed. Morpheus mutters under his breathe, mentally unstable like and also due to the face he is intoxicated. Breathing heavily, he sighs... 'Something is definitely wrong with my life. Multiple things are wrong with my life actually, I have the largest identity crisis thought possible, my love life is slipping from my grasps and to boot my career is falling all to hell!' Morpheus complains to himself. Lifting his glass and swallowing the last of the remaining alcohol, he stands from his seat and turns. Out of nowhere, Sky Night is standing behind him with an awkward grin. The two of them remain staring at each other. The thoughts of them unknown at this time. 'I think everything is going to your head all in the wrong way Morpheus and personally I think this whole identity crisis is eating away at you like a flesh eating disease... You are the cruel and cold hearted, Infinite of Evil... Who cares about nothing. At least that is what I thought!' Sky demands, speaking with a dominant tone of voice. Morpheus stands with his fists slightly shaking and breathing heavily, he steps back and forth from foot to foot with that blank look on his face that carries no expression. Morpheus with the right side of his upper lip curling up, in a mean snarling look. Dead silence fills the air. Sky steps closer to Morpheus, almost making physical contact and standing chest high with him. Sky remains staring deep into the eyes of Morpheus. Sky tilts her head slightly, expecting reaction from him. Sky becomes angry and pushes the chest of Morpheus which causes him to fall into the bar, clearing out two of the bar stools. Morpheus pushes himself back to his feet and standing straight, he remains towering over and staring down at Sky. 'I don't think you have the slightest clue about me Sky. I don't think you know the first bloody thing about me actually. You want to get inside the head of the deadliest athlete in the game? Then take a seat, we will have some drinks and I will tell you about myself!' Morpheus retorts. Morpheus cracks his neck side to side and turns abruptly and lifts the bar stools back up and rests them on all four legs instead of lying on their sides. Morpheus motions for Sky Night to sit down with a cold, serious look on his face. Sky Night proceeds to sit down with a fearful look in her eyes, almost as if she cannot trust Morpheus or his deranged thought pattern. Morpheus sitting down sideways and facing Sky Night, he has that twisted look on his face and questionable look in his eyes. The scene fades to this intense situation.
