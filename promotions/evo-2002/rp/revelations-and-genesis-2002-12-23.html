Revelations, and Genesis

23 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-23-02 {The shot fades in inside of a room, very dark. The main source of light comes from a large, circular table that it internally lit. On the table you see a picture of Seara, Scott Frost, Luther Wisdom and Xavier Citizen. As the camera raises the shot, you see Xander's face illuminated, and you can see the sides of two grey and blood red robed Unlimited members behind him, to each side.} 'So' Xander begins smugly. 'The little girl doesn't know when to be silent.' 'For some reason, you go on.. and on about me not having the rest of Unlimited at ringside with me. Why, whoever said they would be?' Xander questions. 'I've never needed them for victories. No, that's not why they, or I've ever come out during other Unlimited members matches. That's not it. It's just to prove a point, to the offender, and usually to show the rest of eVolution that we are not a force to be taken lightly. But, they still do.. and that will lead to the unravelling of eVolution.' 'As far as Asis, HBC, and anyone else I may have at my beck and call... don't worry your pretty little head. Asis has your boy toy to warm up for, C's got Dreamer to finish off once and for all. And anyone else involved with Unlimited.... well, we'll just see what they are planned to do.' 'Our match... Seara versus Xander... will be just that.' Xander proclaims. 'You want to try and prove you're better then me... here's your chance. And when that minute fails... I will prove why the weak are taken out be the strong. It starts with you, then will go to Frosty the Ho-man, because I'm sure he's gonna get jealous of another man, such as my caliber, even laying a finger on his 'dame,' so that will throw him off his game, and leave him prone for Asis to attack. It will all start with you my sweet. The beginning of the end of the good life as you all know it.' {Xander stops and thinks for a moment} 'There is two ways of going about this match' he states as he scratches his chin. 'There will be the nice one on one battle to show who's better. I know that's what you want. Or, there will be the big guy beating the snot out of the little girl, kind. Which will most likely be what happens. But, rest assured, you will be the first to receive a Christmas present from me. No, unfortunately for you, it's not a night with a real man, instead of a boy like Frost. You will be the first casualty of the Genesis of Unlimited. The new era of a stable born out of spite has begun. No one can stop it... no one will stop it. There is not one person who can halt the process that two man have begun. Nightmare Before Christmas.... still as fitting as ever. Seara.... welcome to your nightmare.' {The scene fades out on one of the robed Unlimited members}
