Date: 2003-05-21 Cracked porcelain. Broken and battered bathroom tiles. A shower that wreeks of mildew and mold. Dirty towels and laundry litter the floor. The toilet seat is missing, and the basin of it is a rather unflattering palette of various brown hues. Above the sink, with encrusted white-stuff around every corner, a mirror reflects the image of a man. Two images actually, from the long crack up the center. He stares uncaringly with his cold gray eyes. His brown hair looking rough as he brushes out of his face. He opens the mirror to reveal a hidden cabinet with many bottles inside. He takes a few of them out and places them on the rim of the sink. He smiles as he then picks each one up and pops off the top, shuffling many small capsules out into his palm. He consumes them with a hollow smile. He continues until the last bottle and then neatly places them back into the cabinet. He moistens his lips in the mirror, which now also contains the innocent face of a young women. '..Dane.' Her voice is gentle and soft with a slight harmony to it. It slightly echoes throughout the small bathroom, prompting this man to turn to her. He gazes into her eyes and scowls. 'What.' Dane's voice contrasts very much from hers. It's rough, gritty and reminescent of the New Jersey streets. He rubs his hand over his chin stubble and he looks back into the mirror. 'For the love of God, Dane. Why are you doing this to yourself? To.. us?' She slowly steps in the doorway and a large stomach is revealed. Her face probes deep into Dane's mind wanting an answer. He sighs and looks to her, brushing a few piles of clothes aside and inviting her in, reluctantly. 'Us? Okay let's get one thing straight Katy. That child is yours. What's that feminist mantra you women have? 'Our bodies our selves'? Your body. Your uterus. Your child. I suggest you leave your domestic fantasies out of my living quarters - mind you - I'm allowing you to stay in. Just because you werent smart enough to use contracep-' Dane callously talks down to Katy, whom is now slumped on the wall. She grimaces at the words as they sink in. She puts her head down. She looks up in the dim lighting, a small tear stream now visible down her cheek. The room grows quiet as his words trail off. 'Katy.. I.. Look. I'm sorry. But you know I'm hooked on these things. I've always been. Painkillers are virtually making me function at this point.' She slightly nods as she wipes away her tears, sniffing. 'You know I love you. And I will love your child. I will. And.. I'll make things work. I know that bastard left you but you gotta see where I'm coming from. I wasn't ready for a child just yet. Then. But now I'm back on my feet with my new job at eVo.. Well.. Surely I'm not a criminal for this?' He walks to her and gently smiles. She gives a wry half-smile and he presses her to the wall, nugging her chin. He leans down and kisses her animalistically. She takes a deep breath afterwards and shakes her head, remaining quiet. Dane grins. 'Fine. Get to bed now..' She gently tugs his arm as she signals for the door, but Dane pulls back and snarls. 'Uhh.. I've got the couch tonight..' 'Dane.. please. I need yo-' Before she can finish, Dane silences her with a cold glare. He snaps back to the mirror as he quietly walks out of the shot. He splashes some water on his face and wipes it with a blood-stained rag nearby before he too, walks out of the door. He flicks the lightswitch and the shot turns black.
