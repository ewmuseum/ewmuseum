Championship Credibility

2 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 2nd Legends is over. The Main Event saw a lot go down. Mainly two things that are evolving out of the outcome. First of all, L2J vs William for the Universal Title at Faith. Secondly and on a personal level Sovereign’s name will be removed from the Universal Title, Williams will be in it’s place. As our camera starts to role you can tell it’s only been a very short time from Legends going off air. Sitting on a bench in a locker room is William Forth, the eVo Universal Champion. I guess now it’s official as he holds in his hand the gold plate that says Sovereign. William hasn’t gone through the showers yet, sweat still laces his long hair that’s been hooked behind his ears, although stray strands litter his face. The Title belt rests upon his wild, powerful shoulders, light dances off it as he takes in a deep breath, the name plate now reads William Forth. William isn’t out of his gear yet, although the straps on his singlit are down, showing his powerful upper body. William hasn’t yet looked up, still breathing and taking a good long stare at the former name plate in quite remembrance of Sovereign. He mumbles something to himself and then drops the plate, into a trash can that rests in between his legs. Thanks for the memories Sovereign. William looks up into the camera. His face is marked with emotion, powerful eyes beam towards you like lasers. He parts his lips and speaks. So you now know he’s gone. L2J, I told you that he was gone, and regardless of if you trust me or hate me it’s not important anymore. We both had agendas tonight, you wanted a Universal Title match, and I wanted my name on this piece of hardware. I earned it, I did last week and again this week I went out there and saved you on more then one occasion from losing everything you were putting yourself in to. You want to be Champion, well L2J there’s a long list of people that would love to be champion and lucky for you your first on that list. Your front and center and at Faith it’s now official that you and I will face each other, one on one for the Universal Title. You might have wanted Sovereign, but it’s not going to happen. Not what you know of Sovereign. That man is now gone, and I’m in his place. If you think for one second that I’m not as passionate about this title then he was then your wrong. Hell, I’m more involved with this belt than he ever was. He said once that the only thing belts are good for are holding your pants up. He may have had a good point. But Sovereign’s world didn’t revolve around titles or appreciation of what it meant. I sit here, in this locker room about 2 hours after Legends went off the air and I am as happy as I’ve maybe ever been. You have a hunger for championship Gold L2J, I know it. Everyone can tell when they look at you. And when you look at me it’s only hatred and the need to have what I’ve earned. Not to say L that you haven’t earned the right to be the number one contender, because you have. Personally you have pulled on the heart strings of a lot of people in your quest for this title. And perhaps at Faith your quest will come to an end. No one knows, because we can’t predict the future. We can give our best guess and stuff like that. But there’s no way to know what’s going to happen. All we know now L is that the bell will ring and two men will give everything they have plus a little bit more just to try and have their name on this. The problem is L. My name is already on it. And I’m not a quitter. I’ve proven that for sure. I doubt a quitter would have been able to over come everything that was Sovereign. Beating him in a wrestling match, and beating everything, EVERYTHING about him are totally different things. I don’t quit L2J. I don’t give up and even if I think you’re a very interesting and compelling individual I sit here and have the credibility of a Champion. You have a charisma about you that makes people want to see you win. Me, it’s unsure. No one trusts me yet. Hell, I wouldn’t trust me if I was a fan, or another wrestler. I have go EARN that trust. I’ve not been exactly the best citizen over my lifetime. Sovereign has done some wicket things and in an indirect way I’ve been involved with all of them. Now is the time to work my way back into some sort of public lifestyle. Doing it here in eVo is the best way that I can see. When I can show people that I’m nothing but a Human being, with a talent. I am a wrestler, I’m not some pushover like a lot of people thought I would be. I’m a champion, the Universal Champion. And L2J you will be the ultimate test at Faith. All your ambition bottled up all for one contest. And regardless of how far this little tag thing goes the bottom line of the entire thing is that we will wrestle. I don’t want to fight you l2J, I’m here to wrestle. I’m here to prove that I’m “the man” in eVo. And if that means that I have to team with you, be your partner for the next few weeks and hell months to come then I will. If it means I have to beat you bloody at Faith then I’ll have to do that as well. I would rather be civil about the entire thing. I’d rather shake your hand before and after the match. But I know that isn’t what is going to happen L2J. Because I know that your not that sort of person. Your to absorbed to have any sort of sportsmanship and that’s something that disappoints me when dealing with you. Not a single tag all match tonight at Legends and I hand fed you the victory. I hope this doesn’t turn into some sort of everything you can do I can do better thing L2J because I’m not interested in it. First and foremost is the Universal Title, secondly are the Tag Team Titles. You can make this as personal as you want to, I’m here to be professional and be a proper champion, not some egomaniac trying to slip his way into power. I’m a wrestler, I’m here to wrestle not to get involved in problems that you had with Sovereign. That’s the past now. Get over it! William looks down into the trash can, smiles to himself and speaks on still looking down into the trash. That was the end of what he had in this company. His name on a title, now it’s in the history books and we can almost say that I am the 2nd Universal Champion in eVo’s history. Of course, those are details that no one cares about folks. Here I am, sitting here today, with this title over my shoulder with everyone in the world watching me waiting for me to snap and put L2J through a fire table or something like that. Unfortunately that’s not going to happen. Sure some fans might hate that fact, some might realize that it’s a good thing. That I’m trying to fight for something that’s right. I never will or ever have asked for attention from Fans. If I get it I will be a happy man, maybe the happiest man on earth. If I don’t it won’t change my job one little bit. So now this week I must work as a Lumberjack. I personally don’t understand these matches, from what I gather there’s Scott Frost who wants to be the Number 1 contender, who apparently has some wild scheme to take this belt from me. And then there’s L2J, his opponent and the ring is surrounded by other wrestlers. There’s a good long list of people, most of whom would love to see me get ripped to shreds, because hell I’m the Champ and because I’m “Sovereign” to those who just don’t seem to understand yet. Well either way L2J, if your worried about getting a beating from me at Legends then don’t worry. I’d protect you more then anything for two very simple reasons. First, because like it or not we’re tag team partners. We’ve advanced in the tournament and if it gives both of us a chance at Tag Gold then I will work to have it happen. Second, and foremost is that I don’t want Faith to have any stigmas attached to it. I want L2J, healthy and ready to fight. I want L2J at his best. Because you have complained a lot about not getting a fair shake, and you L2J are more then right. You’ve been screwed around more then anyone in recent memory and that’s why I want to give you the Title match that you deserve. You will be as healthy as I we can manage so that when that bell rings in New Orleans. When the Ref signals us to go for it, that you and I will have the match of a lifetime. No excuses, not this time. No “I was screwed” segments, no “I was better but never won” stories. This will be the final straw between you and me, and who you see as “Sovereign”. But dead men can’t wrestle, Dead men can’t talk and so you better get it into your head L2J that Sovereign is a Dead man, he’s gone. He’s not coming back. L2J, you might have avoided the Monster... but you haven’t avoided the Champion. So why don’t you come out and prove to these people that your championship material. Why don’t you explain what happened in that Tag match. Why don’t you show me why I should be worried about you. Show me why you’re the Number One contender. I think that the evidence says that without me you would be just another guy on the roster L2J. I gave you that title match because I want to show everyone that William Forth is the Universal Champion for a reason and not by happenstance because I share the same body with Sovereign. William stands up, the title drops down to his side, caught by his strong hands and the camera zooms out to get a full shot of William. Legends is coming up again L2J. This time I might not try and shake your hand. I respect you. I want to be forgiven for everything that I’ve been a part of in your life. I don’t see it happening though L2J. And although I want nothing more then the earn the trust of the fans and fellow wrestlers, I will go to great lengths to remain the Champion in Evolution. At Legends I will be at ringside L2J, as a Lumberjack and I’ll perform my duties as well as I can. And when we meet at Faith, I will do my duty to the best of my ability. My duty to retain this title belt. My Duty is to entertain fans, my duty is to represent this company as the best they have to offer. And L2J, you may very well be the 2nd in command as it compares but L2J, you have to face ME at Faith. I’m not some crazed out Psychopathic monster. I’m the Universal Champion. And your just second best. William turns and exits the room on that final statement.
