Self-Destruct

2 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.02.03 ~When one person sets a standard for themselves and uses everything in there power to achieve the goal but fail…what can happen? A person can go into a mode of self-denial, a sort of self-destructive mode that could end in the devastation of one or of many. If he set his mind to bringing the pain to those who’ve hurt him, it’d be the many. But it could also be too much of a task for him and lead to his own demise. Morpheus Omega, he vowed that he would be the first to go down in flames, then anyone else who stepped in the #1 Stunna’s way on the road back to the Universal title would pay.~ ‘What else can be said?’ Shocka questioned himself. ‘I mean from now on I am going to have to let my actions speak louder than my words if I want to come across as a legit threat…’ Shocka speaks in a more upbeat tone. ~He lies face down on a massage table as a nurse rubs ointment on his back he cringes a little as she crosses some of the more burned portions of his back. It seems to be healing well and only a few parts are still looking bad. He sits up after the nurse finishes and he stands looking in the mirror he flexes his arms.~ ‘You know what…I have been working out like no other…just so I could take down Omega when the chance came but now…I am ready for anyone that can come…’ ‘You better be easy on that back there tiger…’ The nurse speaks seductively. ‘What?’ Shocka asks. ‘You know what I mean…I can help you cure and work out…’ the nurse speaks in a over bearing tone. ‘What in the hell are you talking about…do you want to have sex with me just because I am a fucking star…’ Shocka says ego raging. ‘What? NO! I have a remedy that involves stretching.’ The nurse says looking at Shocka. ‘Oh well if I were you I’d want to have sex with me…’ Shocka says with a sly smile as he walks out of the room grabbing his shirt on the way out. ‘Omega will go down no matter what I have to do…I still have fucking connections I hope that he realizes that…’
