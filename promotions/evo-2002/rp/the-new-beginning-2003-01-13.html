The new beginning

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On the Algerian-Libyan Border Oasis 436 Underground Top-Secret Training Facility It has been over a week since Big Daddy Dreamer’s abduction by The Collective. Dreamer slowly opens his eyes to the blinding light above him and tries to shield it but can’t. He tries to flex his muscles but finds that they are securely restrained as well as his legs. He slowly twist his head to the side and discovers he is in some kind of medical lab and is at that moment, notices a series of tubes and wires going into his massive frame. He scans around again, trying to see some resemblance of similarity or is this some kind of drugged induced nightmare. BDD exhales out slowly catching something from the corner of his eye. The SF-5 symbol of The Phoenix. Finally. Drugged induced nightmare my ass he thinks to himself. Well, at least its apart of our organization. Rogue operation though I cannot tell. Damn my letting Draven head up this group without full oversight from me. That bastard had better be careful. At the moment a nurse walks in holding a long syringe. Pretty one, about 5-7, blondish hair, European accent. Hungary maybe? Czech? She looks down at BDD, smiles knowing full well who he is. She gently strokes his hands and whispers slowly in his ear…… ‘Your among friends now…’ and slides the syringe into BDD bulging vein as BDD slips back into unconsciousness. Hours later, one can never really tell given the current state of affairs BDD is in. He wakes up inside a plush room with dark mirrored plexiglass all around. He scans his surrounding again, taps the wall, figuring he is being watched. ‘I look like shit!’ he thinks out to himself trying to piece together the last few cognitive memories of days gone by. Suddenly a voice booms out from nowhere telling him to sit down and recall the past few days as best as he can. By now BDD is getting somewhat annoyed at the entire situation but pulls up a chair, spins it backwards and sits down. Name? My name if you already don’t know and I suspect you do is Big Daddy Dreamer. Occupation? Again, I know you already know but Chief Executive Officer of The Syndicate International Conglomerate and current active superstar of Evolution. Are we going to continue this b.s getting to know you garbage knowing you have my dossier out in front of you and asking me rudimentary questions to get my brain going or you going to tell me why the hell I’m here in the first place? We will do the asking thank you very much. Not after I get done with you, you won’t. Do you know where you are by any chance? ‘What an idiot’ BDD thinks to himself. Should I play dumb or shall I crack his head open. I think I’ll crack his head open. Lets see, surveying the scene here, I’d say I’m in a room with dark mirrored plexiglass from floor to ceiling. Some of these things in here are mine, well a cheap replica anyways. Very good sir.. Now – Did I say I was done? No I didn’t. I would gather to say, that somehow, I ended up at The Syndicate Force – 5 training facility. Our top secret military project that no one on the outside knows about. Surprised? You shouldn’t be. I created this team, this project, but I may have put the wrong person in charge of it, and when I get done, his little experiences in The Underground won’t mean jack after I get through with him. Who are you in reference too? Thomas Draven and Katarina. You know mister dreads and the hot blonde with the ample chest with the phoenix on her arm. Yeah, I know all about it. Katarina supposedly back from the grave, but more than likely hiding out at perhaps this very facility here. Now bucko, you have two choices, let me out peacefully, or I’ll tear this facility apart and rip your head clean off your shoulders. You guys picked the wrong person to screw with. And though we have our differences, if you have HBC here, I suggest you let her out as well. She isn’t as forgiving as I am! All of a sudden the door opens and two burly operatives rush in charging BDD. BDD sidesteps one, catches the other with a quick stiff blow stunning him. The other tries to overpower him, BDD shakes him off and flips him through the plexiglass window, shattering it in a million pieces. The first one, quickly recovered catches BDD with a stiff sabot kick and tries another. BDD catches him and throws him down with a dragonscrew kneelock, ripping at the joint until it snaps. The man screams in pain as BDD grabs one of the shards of glass in his right hand and grabbing the technician with his left dragging him through the plexiglass. He holds up the shard to the man’s throat. ‘Now, like I was saying……she isn’t as forgiving as I am. Why am I here, tell me now, or tell St. Peter at the pearly gates yourself. The choice is yours.’ ‘Re…….re……..re-education sir! We …..need you……and HBC……to….to….fix up the mess caused in your absences…. Draven…….and…..Katarina…..really had good intentions…..just…..bad….methods….’ BDD drops the man, flings the shard into the wall as the technician passes out. From the distance, a clapping sound is heard. The buxom brunette beauty Natalia has a wide smile on her face. A look of distain comes across BDD as he walks up to her and stares into her baby brown lifeless eyes. Bad methods eh? Well I wouldn’t perhaps say that, your just about ready to rip everyone a new one, including in Evolution. Perhaps that was their plan all along. To turn up the hostility violence level up to max just as you two once were ages ago. What was it, breaking necks and taking names you once said back in RGWF? And your partner, her violence streak was legendary. By the way, you have a match this week with the newest member of HBC’s little group, The Unlimited. You remember them. You are their biggest threat because your undefeated against Xander and Malcom King. Well anyways, your facing Angel. Go get’em tiger! Roar! BDD laughs and suddenly gets very serious burning a hole in Natalia eyes with his gaze. Be careful of what you wish for, it just may come true………and one more thing…….. At the moment, Big Daddy Dreamer looks Natalia up and down, definitely pleased with what he see’s. Natalia smiles back, licking her pouty lips before falling unconscious to the floor thanks to the vicious left hand of Big Daddy Dreamer. He looks down on her, grins, and walks off as a few people rush over to help Natalia to the infirmary.
