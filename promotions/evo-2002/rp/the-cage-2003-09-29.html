The Cage

29 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-29 Inside cages society has been known to enforce animals into this steel structure. Not suitable for animals but mankind has brushed them away. A confined cage is no place for a ravenous animal... But it is a place for a gigantic, psycho path.. Humanity has not since been in the presence of such a .... a.... Monster. A Monster that has been unleashed since the day of his birth... confined in a white room with padding on the walls. The cage door is slightly opened as Hymm stands inside. What drives this monster to stay in there? It is his act of sympathy for the mankind. The mankind that turned their backs on a cold, possessed young boy. Hymm has evil intent in his eyes as once name races through his mind... “Sean Sterling.. Human filth, digging his own grave for myself to put him in....Sterling... Sterling... The name pulls the trigger as I feel I should...no I must put him to rest. To lay in his grave, going through only HALF of what I have been put through. Oh but it doesn’t stop there! The day of reckoning has come... Judgement Day... The day I am never confined in this cage ever again...” A deafning grunt is heard as Hymm shakes the cage. What curse has been bestowed on the human – if that is what you prefer to call him. The prophecy has come true for he has caused the apocalypse in the hearts of mankind. Time is slowly running out.. Running out on the miserable human beings such as Sean Sterling. A man that will be put through the torture of being inside a death ring with Hymm. Hymm tilts his head slightly as he has another thought race through his mind. “The day of reckoning is amongst them... and it is time to show Evolution that the Iniquity is tired of everything mankind as put them through...” Iniquity – A gross injustice ; a sin... Suits both men. The torment Synned and Hymm have gone through have not been suffered through the eyes of many human beings... pure filth. Humanity will be damned once Hymm exits the confined cage... and that is only a matter of time.. time is drawing to a end for Evolution. Hymm heavily breaths as he knows its almost time... the cage door is clearly seen open but why is the ravenous monster not outside of it? What can possible contain him? The heavy breathes slowly fade away as he moves around with complete rage inside of the cage. “I will tear Sean apart... Once I leave this cage, the world is in great danger. As I know as soon as I touch the ground under this cage I will bestow grave danger of me a ravenous monster. Ahhh... Prepare for the worse wrath you have ever felt humanity! The pain, the torture, everything I was put through has come back... and it’s seeking revenge. You might just be the one I unleash it on. Ahhhh” A eroding grunt comes from Hymm as he stays inside the cage for the time being. The confinement is only temporarily someone has to believe. But the pain he shall unleash will account for everything a human can ever suffer. Time is quickly fading.. fading away as it cannot reason. Carpe Diem... Seize the moment, for this time it is truly your last. The unbearable wrath of Hymm has started...
