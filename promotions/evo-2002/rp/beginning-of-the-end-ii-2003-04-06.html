Beginning of the End II

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The destructive path of the Infinite of evil swept over eVolution like a blanket of darkness... With hell and fury, Morpheus is one of the greatest warriors to ever contend against. The combined forces of the deadliest athlete in the game and the deceived, conniving queen of darkness is a prophecy on its own. Twisted Reality, the true and only real deal tag team that this federation has to offer. After orchestrating the sickest scheme in wrestling, who knows what is next of them? They will prove without a doubt, they are the true and only tag team champions. 'This is all business now Sky, you have to have full and undivided attention this week on Legends...' Morpheus states with a very serious look cast over his face, he shows no emotion. 'You think I am going to stress over team network?' Sky questions with a baffled look on her face and raises an eyebrow, she continues... 'Honestly, they are the least of my worries...' she smiles. 'All because I took my chance in hitting Shocka where it hurts, they made our championships unsanctioned rogue titles and now those chumps have belts of their own around their waists?' Morpheus questions. 'I really don't know who half these people think they are...' Morpheus continues and shakes his head. 'Team Network is about to be hung out to dry, they have nothing on us and come nowhere close in comparison. They have met their match and never have they seen an alliance like the two of us. I wonder how they feel about getting their asses beat by a woman' Sky wonders. 'They are going to find out on Legends...' Morpheus comments. 'With your strength and my smarts, nothing can stop Twisted Reality... Always thinking and stepping ahead of the rest, I welcome all challenges...' Sky says with an evil grin, she licks her lips... 'eVolution must want us to end Team Network, signing them with a consecutive three matches against us?! They have accomplished nothing in my eyes, I defeated a former universal champion in his own match and we won the true tag championships here... Jason Relms and High Impact, they will dance with the devil himself. This is their fate!' Morpheus says with a snarling look on his face, he smirks. 'Team evolution was eliminated last Legends and it will make for easy annihilation to take down the third world version of them, Team Network. What kind of maggots do they hand championships to these days? The unity between both of us, will bring home those unified championships. We are everything those fake champions dream to be, that much better in every way possible. I see a very shady future for them ahead...' Sky says confidently and brushes hair from in front of her beautiful, crystal clear green eyes. The two of them stand together inside Eternity, the nightclub that Morpheus owns. The light flashes bright of the name to the side of them. There looks to be no sign of life or like they had even been opening the club each night. Morpheus and Sky stare at each other with wicked, strange grins on their faces and eyes locked. 'There is no turning back from here, we will drive fear into our opposition and hit them where it hurts... If Network wanted a piece of the spotlight so badly, they should of challenged us for it but they went through the wrong connections to get where they are. New-TV steals the show and I will not stand for them trying to thief our spotlight. They have both found themselves in horrible ways and I will systematically destroy them and make them suffer every way possible' Morpheus retorts. 'We will convict and punish them for the childish, immature crimes they have committed... The seriousness does not matter because its like treason against a king!' Sky says with a wide grin on her face and continues... 'Or better yet, THE QUEEN!' 'TEAM NETWORK???! They wanna fuck with the bull, they will get the horns... Them stupid fucks can dare 2 dream about the future that lies ahead for them, but I will promise to make their lives living hell!' Morpheus screams loudly and the scene fades.
