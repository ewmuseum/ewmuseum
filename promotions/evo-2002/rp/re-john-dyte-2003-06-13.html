Date: 2003-06-13 John Dyte is a faggot. Literally. He is. John Dyte has H.I.V positive. No joke. He does. John Dyte is a mother fucker. Now make what you want out of that. His mother was a hoe. A prostitute. An insult to God’s green Earth. John Dyte thought it awfully strange, however, that Mrs. Dyte took a long time to do her shopping, and sometimes she would never even come back. Strange? She must have had so much on her mind and the kind people at Amnivac Corporation must have let her use their facilities for a quick nap. Now what John Dyte found very, very strange was the fact that he was getting private education, the best school in the state at the youthful age of eight and his mother was ‘unemployed’ on social welfare. Now John was not one to judge so he continued living his life of luxury, no questions asked. Therefore John didn’t ask any questions as to why his mother went shopping at approximately eleven o’clock every Friday and Saturday night and half twelve on the others when there wasn’t a twenty four hour store in the locality. Must have scraped one of the late night shops before closing time. One day John walked in on his mother when she was being helped by a middle aged man, who very peculiar happened to be naked, to find a ring that she believed she dropped underneath the bed. Pleased with this alibi, John continued to bed and let his naked mother and the naked middle aged man continue in their search for the ring that she had dropped underneath the bed. She bent over and the man began to help her search for it. After a good night’s sleep John awoke to find another naked, middle aged man helping his mother unblock a plug hole in the sink in her room. OK, fine, continue on your work. John Dyte was obviously not the curious type and believed that her mother’s ‘handy men’ just enjoyed working in the nip. That night John awoke to find his mother had been acting quite strange indeed. Now, for anyone else this behaviour would have been regarded as retarded, utterly disturbing, but John just enjoyed pleasing his mother and abiding by her every wish. Her EVERY wish. After several peculiar events that day she had asked John something that no mother should never, EVER ask her child. (And for those of you with an over-active imagination, I’mma let you come to your own conclusions on what that something was, catch my drift?) Weeks had now passed and his mother’s requests became more and more peculiar. And he met every single one of those demands and did it with great pleasure. The sick fucker, in his own opinion he was helping his mother through ‘a rough patch’. The intensity grew every night. Each night was more intense that the previous. Every night was magic for his fucked up, prostitute mother. John no longer enjoyed it and never really did but saw it more as helping his mother out. One day, after returning home from a day in his public school which he was now attending as his mother was no long racking in the big money because of the new girls in the district John was met be streaks of crimson blood on the white, tiled, porch of the house. He thought it strange as this had been fairly out of the ordinary. He walked in to the sitting room to see his mother’s dead body laid across the sofa, crimson streaks running from her mouth, her hand stretched down to the tiled floor where a silver pistol is lying. Her body motionless on the sofa he didn’t shed a tear, felt no emotion toward this appalling sight, no remorse, nothing. He just picked up the note that lay on her chest. “John, Got H.I.V positive. Took the easy way out.” He just dragged the body out the back and disposed of it in a nearby lake. He didn’t need any medical examination, didn’t need to pay for a high priced doctor, he already knew that he had the disease. John Dyte. You faggot. John Dyte. You mother fucker. I hate you; I hate you with a passion. Fuck you John Dyte. I stayed away from John Dyte, never had I spoken to him and never shall I speak to him. My father told me he had some disease and that he was a fucking faggot. Fuck you. Fuck you. Fuck you.
