Interactive Interview

16 November 2003

Date: 2003-11-16 Welcome..welcome....welcome. Radio 105.5 F..M!!!!!!!!!!!! Internet Radio, one of the underrated uses for the Internet is the focus of your ears today as you’ve been waiting for this chance to hear what you are about to hear since last week when thew news came over the web to “Net Factory Radio”. A Wrestling website run by Wrestling pundants, critics and collunmists. Every month they do a high quality radio broadcast where they touch on the subjects in the industry ranging from television ratings to injury updates to spoilers and rumours. The Hosts are Bill Humphry and Simon Wright. Often times they try their best to get guests to interview, but most wrestlers now a days who work for one of the “Big three” (WWE, TNA and eVo) don’t either have enough time, or are not huge fans of “Da Net”. It never stops them from trying as the show’s theme music plays before they open up the show. You yourself have been waiting for this show to happen for a long time, not nessesarily since it was announced, but being a fan, well sometimes it can be a full time job. “Alright!” said Ben’s voice, excitement graced his tone. “Well it’s another Month Simon, I can’t believe it’s November already eh” he added “Yeah that’s right Buddy, hard to believe that it’s almost the end of the year 2003. It’s been a great run so far. What a year for wrestling.” added the sidekick. “That’s right, you know a lot of changes have happened in the Industry this year with the emergence of NWA-TNA and eVo and really this has sort of breathed life back into the industry. Although they might not be the WWE at least we’ve got something else to watch. And that is the focus of our discussion today because fans, I know your listening because of our guest tonight” said Ben. “That’s right, we said we were going to get him and he’s going to be here joining us on the phone for a full interview, and what we’ve done as you all know is that on the Forums is that if you have a question for our guest you can email it to us and we’ll ask it to him if we have time.” said Simon. “Yup, damn right, you got a question for the Champ? We’ll ask him because today our guest is none other then James Kimson.” Said Ben almost like a building to a climax that he got to say it. “I’m real excited about it. You know Simon not only is he our biggest quest period but he’s easily besides Hogan the biggest name in wrestling that’s not working for Vince McMahon or that amazingly has never worked for him yet is maybe one of the most popular wrestlers in the world.” said Ben. “It’s true you know and that goes to show his talent. I think that a lot of fans, and we have the proof of it would love to see James in the WWE because of what he could do there. The matches he could have with some of the talent there. But it’s amazing that he’s never been there yet he has such a massive following. But today he is going to be here talking to us. James has said that no question is off bounds and that he’s looking forward to talking to us for the next hour or so.” said Simon. “So what we are going to do is get some Commercial breaks out of the way right at the start to avoid to many stalls in the show and then when we come back we’ll have James on the line” “This is going to kick ass!” said Ben. “I’m marking out” said Ben laughing as if he was joking, yet you could tell that he wasn’t joking in the least. There was a pause as the station went cold before the commercials rang through. Not much of a big deal to you, all you wanted to know is when the interview was going to start. After about a minute of ads and so on they came back on. “Okay, we’re back on the air fans.” said Simon. “And as we promised we’re being joined by James Kimson... James thanks so much for taking the time to speak with us today!” Simon continued “It’s my pleasure guys, I’m always happy to get a chance to communicate with the fans, so when I got asked to do this I thought that since we’ve been out of the States for so long that I couldn’t not do this interview.” said Kimson. “Well James” replied Ben. “As you may know we’ve really been pumped for this interview and on our forums and our email we’ve recieved literally hundreds of questions from fans all over North America. And it’s so hard to compile them and it’s impossible to ask them all. So what do you say we just head straight for the questions and at the end Simon and I will probably have a question or two personally we’d love to ask.” said Ben. “I think that’s a great idea.” said Kimson “Okay, our first question comes from MarkV on the forums and he is wondering what you thought about Faith and how did you like working that sort of a match.” said Simon. “Well, right away I think that the match at Faith was awesome. I had my worries about it on a personal level because it was going to have to be real physical for everyone that was to be involved including Leya. Shocka himself, he’s a real tough guy and working with him sometimes is like working with a man made of stone because he can be real stiff.” said Kimson. “We;ve been told he’s real stiff before.” said Ben. “Well, I never mind working with a Stiff worker. See it reminds you that this isn’t just some sort of soap opera, there’s physical contact and Shocka is a big strong man and he sometimes gets into his character that for points in time throughout the match you feel like you are in a real fight. I felt that way at Digital Destruction with him but at Faith it was much more controlled and I think it was a far better match. I think that it was a fitting main event and a contender for the best match of 2003 for sure. Added to that with Faith that there were other outstanding matches like the Asis vs Xander match and the eVolved Championship match that it was probably our best showing since A New Dawn.” said Kimson. “What about the comment about ‘That sort of a match’” said Ben. “Well in the end it was real rough on me because I got my ass kicked for 25 minutes. I took a hell of a whollop but it was a different sort of match, more old school. There wasn’t a part of that match that had more then 4 punches exchanged, there weren’t dozens of chair shots and every move meant something. IT was something I wanted to have and so did everyone else want. Simply because you know we had already had all these insane gimmick matches that night that this match couldn’t just copy them, it had to be different. We were concerned that after all the spots in the Ladder Match and the Cage match that the fans wouldn’t recognize what was going on, but they certainly did. The match was sort of old school in how it was worked. I love those kinds of matches and we’ll probably be seeing a few more of those style of matches in the future. The human body can only take so much punishment and in the end guys you don’t need to crash through tables to get a reaction from the crowd, we worked 20 minutes of a 30 minute match and there were no table spots no chairs for 2/3rd’s of the match, the fans didn’t know the difference. Then when the chairs came out it was all that much more important, that much bigger of a spot.” said Kimson. “Yeah, I have to agree, to many guys out there today all over the world are risking to much” said Simon. “Yeah exactly. Look at what happened to Shawn Michaels who was really an originator for main stream risk taking. He has an iron rod in his back and now he is working again but it’s not the HBK we all knew 10 years ago.” said Kimson. “You risk a lot when you go out there in the first place. I know I have and I’ve done some pretty stupid spots that in hinesight I should have not done. But I’ve come to see that you don’t need to do the 15 foot bump anymore. So that if you go for 8 months without taking a crazy bump that when that bump comes, and you are healthy that you won’t miss any time from it that when it happens the fans will go ape shit and it’ll be the biggest spot of the year instead of... oh wow yeah James Kimson fell off the wall of a cage again....wooo yeah great bump.” said Kimson sarcasically. “Evolution has always had an extreme side to it” said Simon. “Exactly, but I think times are changing. We won’t loose the extreme aspects, that has it’s place. But as for insanity in matches we’re going to avoid that. No one was better at being vicious yet safe like Xander or like Sovereign. They could do anything in that ring, I never felt like I was in danger.” said Kimson. “It’s just being a good worker”. “Yeah” said Simon. “Okay, lets move on.” said Ben “Our next Question is from eVofan316 and he asks what is your definition of a Universal Champion, and what does it mean to you?” “Well... hmmm. I don’t know if I can define it. I think that being the Champion really means not that you are the best worker, best mic guy or have the best look. But that really it’s that for the time being you are the man the company is looking towards. You are the man that everyone is looking to see, in the back, out in the ring, on the streets, at the events all over the world. Not everyone can be the Champion. You could, technically put the belt on anyone. But the nature of the business is that the belt goes to your top guy. I’ve had the belt for nearly 3 months now, it seems to me that I’ll have it for a while yet. Evolution has a lot of guys who can take the belt and a deep talent pool but right now I don’t know what it means to me really. I guess on a personal level it’s not far from the storyline we ran. This really is the pincale of all the things I’ve done. This it the biggest title I’ve won, it’s like getting a raise for a job well done. There’s a sense of pride. I worked my ass off since I was born to be a wrestler, and I have sacrificed a lot for it. It’s like a form of recognition that S. says that I’m the guy to carry the company. I’m the guy they want to focus on in the story lines, in the ring I have the big draw’s. I get the rewards, I get the recognition and of course money wise it helps me out a lot. But like Jake Roberts said, no one wins a title, it’s a gift. And I really believe that, and when it’s time for me to move on I will count my blessings and look back on this time with a smile. Because I really think that I’m having the time of my life right now. Said Kimson. “Lets talk about the Hiatus. We have a question here, the name I don’t have written down but the fan wants to know what you think of this break and what comes afterwards. He also said that he thinks the Hiatus is going to ruin any momentum coming out of Faith with 2 weeks off.” said Ben. “That’s a great point” said Kimson. “The Break, well on a business level it has it’s short points. First off, yeah I think it’s going to hurt the momentum out of Faith. Most times after a PPV you want to have a strong strong showing right away. Normally we have a week off, but now with 2 it’ll hurt us. But what has to happen is we need to come out of this thing with a show for the fans to really remember, for new fans to want to stay with us and it’s got to be like eVo1 or like Legends L. But the reason it’s happening, well there are so many things going on that it was decided at the beginning of October, at least that’s when the roster was notified that we’d be off for 2 weeks. I’ll tell you that it was a sweet sound to us. Basically if you think about it that we’ve been on the road for nearly 5 months of this tour. We’re used to being on the road. But what you normally deal with for us on a week would be Legends on Wednesday, House Show Thursday and on Saturday. The Friday in between normally you have autograph signings or whatever. That is 4 days on the road, add a day of travel and you work a normal persons 5 days a week. Here, on this tour it’s been 6 days a week if not 7 days. I have not been back home for 2 months personally. It’s hard, really hard to be away from home, family and friends for so long. And while you might get a long with everyone back stage they still aren’t your personal friends for the most part. And you miss the little things that you didn’t think you’d miss. They guys were getting real tired of it. Not getting enough sleep, time zone’s changing constantly, 500 dollar cell phone bills from calling home every day. You start to resent fans because they don’t get wrestlers from North America coming for such long periods of time sometimes so everywhere you go it’s an autograph and a picture and it’s fine but when you just wish you were back home and a normal person in society you start to get aggrivated. I’ve never turned down a photo or an autograph and I’ve never asked for money for it. But over the last 3 months I guess it’s been getting pretty rough on the locker room’s morale. But when show time starts everyone’s spirits lift because being in the show, that’s what it’s about After it’s over everyone was holding their chin to their chest again. No one was happy and when we got home afterwards, I know myself I was so happy to be home.” said Kimson. “But as for the reasons, not just a break but things are changing. We’re getting a new Web site, a new TV deal. Some guys need time to heal up and a few weeks will help that especially when someone’s not beating on you 4 days a week. I know myself I feel so much better already and it’s only been 1 weeks, not even yet really. But I’m sure that when it’s time to get to Austrailia for the rest of the tour that I’ll be ready to get back into that ring and do what I do best.” said Kimson. “I suppose that injuries would be a good reason for the break. From what we know several of your top guys are banged up.” said Simon. “Well you’re always banged up. But yeah the break will do all of us some physical good.” said Kimson. “I guess we’ll get to the next querry.” said Simon. “We have a question here asking what your thoughts are about Cameron Griffin’s passing, and it’s effect on the Hiatus.” said Ben. “Well, let me say that Cameron was a beautiful person.” said Kimson. “I think that if Heaven was perfect before it just got that much better with him there. He was a warm person, never made you feel out of place. He welcomed you into his home, he gave so many people their breaks in the business. As a man he was pretty much complete. He didn’t have any vices, he loved his family and it was... well it was very hard to hear about his passing. Leya called me on her cell and she was a complete mess. She and Cameron were very good friends, same with Sovereign, they were close as it was Cam that got Will broken into American wrestling. It was hard on them. I was to attend the Funeral but I wasn’t able to make it today to it because of personal commitments. But I most certainly wish his family all the best. Heh, I have some great stories about him, but I think nows not the time for them.” said Kimson. “As far as his effect on the hiatus, yeah it’s a big part of it. A lot of things were supposed to happen with the Cell and now they obviously won’t be happening, so everyone is about to change. It will be interesting to see what we’re going to do but yeah he’ll be missed a great deal.” added Kimson in a somber tone. “You mentioned Sovereign.” said Ben. “I am wondering if you’d like to say a few things, maybe bring us up to speed on him. Your relationship with him and talk about your on screen role with Leya.” said Ben. “Well about Leya, i have to say that it’s a great treat for me personally. I mean, we are real life brother and sister that much is very true so it’s real interesting to try and look at her with a lot of hate and disgust but you know, putting yourself in character isn’t hard because she’s so good at what she does. She plays the role so well and so I can’t help but get into it. But there’s no hatred between us at all. She and I were the closest of our siblings because we had that physical desire to be in the wrestling business. My other sisters wanted to do other things then follow our Father’s foot steps but Leya who was the one that decided 1st when she was 16 that she wanted to be in this business in one way or the other and I was right behind her. We’re only a year apart from each other and always had a big bond. I felt I was her protector, she’s a beautiful woman and that means she was prey for a lot of other guys with unholesom intentions. She wasn’t always happy that I would be there to scare them off but in the end she and I were always very close and love each other very much. So it’s really funny you know to go out there and call her a whore or a bitch or whatever because she’s probably the sweetest woman on the planet. You should see her with Sovereign when they’re home, she treats him like he’s the king of the world and she’s her queen. They’re probably the happiest couple I know of inspite of all the problems they’ve had in the real world.” said Kimson. “With Sovereign, well Will. I took a lot of those feelings I had when they first started and used that as fuel for the story. I wasn’t happy that my sister was dating a man who had mental health issues and who was known to act upon them physically. Regardless of if Leya knew how to take care of herself, Sovereign literally is a monster. It’s funny seeing Will in the locker room because he’s real quite and real soft spoken but people still give him a wide birth to walk down the hallyways, no one questions him, no one talks back to him and in the end he’s pretty harmless when he’s just there, one of the guys. But he is so amazingly convincing as a character that it translates into the real world. Sometimes he’ll give that classic crazy Sovereign look and you can’t help but feel uncomfortable and freaked out. You ask him not to do that and he’ll just say he’s ribbing you. I’ve seen a lot of great characters but no one in the history of wrestling lives for their character like Will does. That’s why he’s so good, he’s so convincing and that’s what it’s all about. That’s what makes a great character, no matter how over the top he might be, like Asis or like Sovereign for examples. If they make you believe in them. That they are what they say they are then they’ve done their job. It’s like a heel that wants you to boo him, they sound like cheers. For Sovereign, if you feared him and if you were so afraid of him you got scared when he came to the ring that you yourself could be in danger then it makes him so happy. You should see him after a match he’s like a child, smile from ear to ear even if blood’s pooring out of his face.” “What are your thoughts about your 1 match with him” said Ben. “I think that was probably the most physical match I’ve ever had.” said Kimson. “Will was so into the match, I mean he needed surgery on his arm bad and he told me the week before that he wanted me to work his arm the whole match one way or the other. Because he wanted it to feel real, he wanted it to be the most real match ever and I agreed. I never felt dangerous with him. He protects his opponents better then anyone and it’s funny because you know he’s been responsible on air for so many injuries and there all part of their character. I don’t think that he’s hurt anyone since he’s come to the States seriously. Even if it gets extremely violent like our match did at Unbreakable I was sore afterwards for days but I wasn’t hurt, I could have worked the next day if I wanted to. He’s the best worker, the best character that I’ve ever wrestled and would love to do it again.” said Kimson. “When is he supposed to return” asked Simon. “He’s supposed to mean with Dan S. today after the funeral” said Kimson. “He’s had two surgeries on his rotator cuff and from what I was told by Leya at Faith that he’s ready to start light work outs in the ring so maybe 2,. Perhaps 3 months, so with all that there it’s going to work smoothy into the storylines. In the end a lot of fans don’t remember Sovereign, maybe as a shadow of the past. But when he comes back it’ll be impossible to ignore him, and I know that I’ll be working a program with him right away. Details still are being developed so I can’t say much more but it’ll probably be the program to end all programs” said Kimson. “We’ve got a question about your health” said Ben. “Kimson01Fan is curious about your current health.” “Well, you know I’ve never been %100 in 5 years” said Kimson. “I think that right now we’re really hoping that certain pains are only temporary. My neck has been bothering me and I’ve been speaking to some doctors about surgery down the road because I really do need it.” said Kimson. “When you have a neck problem you have to live with pain, and I’ve gotten used to that pain.” said Kimson. “But I don’t want to live with that, and in 10 years when I’m retired and got a pot belly and playing with my kids I don’t want to have to take a pill so I don’t feel like ripping my spine out. We’ll see what’s going to happen but I am not missing any time.” said Kimson. “Other then the neck everyone’s doing well, holding up and all. No injuries to report” joked Kimson. “We have a fan wanting you to list who you think are the top 5 workers in eVo right now besides yourself.” said Ben. “Wow, uh top 5. Well I’ll stick with the wrestling division and I’ll have to think about that for a second. Guess the top guys would go Thug roC, Asis, Xander, Hunt and probably then Frost.” said Kimson. “Those 5 guys have great matches every night, and they’re all very different in how they work. I think that goes to show that eVo can give you different and high quality matches all the time.” “If you could wrestle anyone past, present, guys in their prime list your top 5 dream matches!” said Simon. “God, wow. Lemmie think about that one for a second.” said Kimson and paused. “Well I’d love to have gotten my hands on Hogan back when he was in his prime. Hogan vs Kimson yeah. Then Michaels when he was healthy, HBK vs Kimson I think the two of us would have a match to end all matches. I’d love to wrestle Kurt Angle, that would be so much fun, I love watching him wrestle, same with Benoit, they’re both so good that it’s not funny. Most fans would recognize that they’re good but when you are a wrestler and you watch a Kurt Angle or Chris Benoit, it’s like perfection you know. It gives you goose bumps.” said Kimson. “I think I’d have loved to work with Flair to, and I wouldn’t mind a match with someone like Triple H simply because he’s a great worker, Jarret to. As for eVolution I really am looking forward to getting in there with Asis again, we had that outstanding match on Legends a few months back that was a draw, and then X-rated. I want to wrestle Thug roC, I want to wrestle Sovereign again as well. I’d love to get another match with Hunt, I felt some of my best matches were with him.” said kimson. “Out of all your big moments in your career, what stands out the most in your mind” asked Ben. “Winning the Universal Title. It’s the newest big memory, I think that the storyline was so good and that everyone was into it. The match was simply outstanding and that everyone really came together for the match, the story and the title is a symbol of the hardwork.” said Kimson. “Here’s a funny one. If you were trapped on a deserted Island and could take 3 items with you what would you take.” asked Simon. “A photo of my family, a lighter and a hunting knife.” said Kimson. “Or if I could take one of those food replicators from Star Trek that’d be great” joked Kimson. “Well, I think that our time is almost up” said Simon. “Sadly.” he added. “Really?” asked Kimson “Yeah, we’ve only got time for our closing comercial and then we’re done” said Ben. “Well let me say that I want to give you my thanks. I love doing these things and that I want to thank everyone for watching eVo. I myself am not an anarchist or anything but not watching Vince on TV 4 hrs a week is going to help the industry, turn the channel to some wrestling and not his crap. Evolution might not have his pockets but we’ve got good wrestling, tune in, keep tuning in and thanks” said Kimson “No, thank you James, that was a great interview” said Simon. Kimson hung up the phone as the hosts gave a quick break before the commericals, you turned the site off, hearing what you wanted to hear
