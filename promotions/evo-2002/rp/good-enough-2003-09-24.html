Good Enough?

24 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-24 Brianna Washington’s record is on the line yet again. She has managed to maintain a perfect record thus far, but tonight may be an end to it. Not only does she have to rely on herself, but also her partner. What if he isn’t as prepared as she is? It’s been a very long time since she had last had to rely on someone else. But the fact that she has faced both of her opponents previously in single matches gives her a sort of edge. She knows what to expect, and has a good idea of their abilities. The fact that she doesn’t really know about Siege and if he is actually going to want to tag with her makes it hard to even come close to figure out the possible outcome. The scene opens up into a car. We can hear the low radio playing as two manicured hands are grasped onto the wheel. Brianna sits back and lets the music shift into her ears as she heads down the road, making her way to Gaelic Grounds, the site of tonight’s Legends. “I’m not crazy…I’m just a little unwell…” Brianna sings, trying hard to keep in tune, as she makes a left. “I’m about half hour away from the arena, and then I have a few hours to get ready for tonight.” She tells herself. “It would be awesome to walk out with yet another win to add to my record. I’m sure if I keep up my success rate, eVo personnel is bound to throw me into some big time matches. Who knows, in the next couple of weeks or months I could get a chance at a title. Since I’ve only had one title ever in my life, I wouldn’t mind getting another. Besides it’s been a long time….I need that reassurance. I don’t have the confidence level of some people. I need to know that I’m doing well and the only way to do that is to get put into title contention. So until I reach that point I’m going to go out thinking that maybe I’m not good enough. That will hopefully give me the push to keep on the winning track……just have to sit back and wait for things to come.” It seems that’s all as she continues to get back to singing along with the radio, drumming her fingers on the steering wheel. The scene fades out.
