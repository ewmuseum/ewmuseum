Doubt

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.01.03 ~What happens when a man is pushed beyond his limit? What happens when he cannot take it anymore…when it is to much for him to handle. Simple he snaps…he takes it over with a dominant battle. He shows everyone what he has in this case It was at New Year’s eVolution when Shocka showed Scott Frost and every other doubter that he had what it takes. Another example if you want is when Shocka ended the life of L2J with no remorse in his face with no feelings. But now he has to go into a match with 3 other men for the Universal title! What did he do to deserve this? Sovereign, Jameson and Frost all get there shot and Shocka doesn’t even have to loose the match to loose his belt. It’s ludicrous.~ ‘What the fuck man?!’ You hear a voice screaming. ‘Just calm down please Jonathan!’ The voice of S. his heard replying. ‘How in the hell can I calm down?!’ Shocka screams again. ‘I dunno…’ S. says starting… ‘I don’t even know why you are all pissed…’ ‘Think about it you stupid fucker! I am fighting 3 men for my title and I don’t even have to loose to loose my god damned belt…’ ‘Yeah’ S. says interrupting. ‘I wasn’t done yet damnit!’ Shocka screams. ‘James Kinson is the fucking referee in this match ‘ ‘His name is James Kimson…’ S. says. ‘Whatever…but I still wasn’t done god damnit! And I just came off of an injury my arm is still in pretty bad shape and now I have to fight in this match not even at 100 percent.’ ‘You’ll get over it!’ S. yells. ‘You are the one that wants to show everyone how damn great you are all the damn time!’ S. says. ‘Yeah well what if I do this week and then Malcom King wins?’ Shocka asks. ‘Then you have to face him next week…’ S. says. ‘So I don’t ever fucking get a break from defending this title DO I?!’ ‘You didn’t have to face anyone last week…’ S. says in a sort of sly tone. ‘Jesus, I can’t believe you are doing this to me S. just because you are on your little power trip.’ ‘Listen…I don’t have time for your bullshit you are not going to squirm out of this match and you are going to have to defend your title no matter what so your endless bitching what get you anywhere!’ S. yells and you hear a dial tone. ~You can see Shocka slowly rise from the chair he was sitting in and he pushes the end call button on his speaker phone. He shakes his head and looks into the mirror. He wears a tight black shirt and a pair of shiny silver pants. The Universal title is over his shoulder and he still has a sling on his right arm. He smiles evilly to himself as he is seemingly formulating a plan.~ ‘All I have to do is get myself disqualified!’ He screams to himself in a moment of brilliance! ‘oh shit….Kimson is the ref its not like that will happen…’ he says to himself as the scene fades.
