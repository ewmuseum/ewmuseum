Criminal Acts: Part 4, The Chase

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 9th, 2003 -=[We fast forward a day and show up at the Dullas International Airport. Cops are seen everywhere at various locations. Thug roC and company are there as well, searching for any signs of Vulgar of the blonde kid.]=- “ Man,” Vega says to break the silence. “I can’t believe we skipped breakfast to be here. I’m hungry” “You eat all the time, yet you’re skinny.” Adam Carr responds. “You must take after your mom.” “What’s that suppose to mean? Tryin to say my dad’s fat?” Vega asks, almost offended from Carr’s comment “YES!” Everyone shouts, thinking the same thing “We all remember that Easter where I had on all brown and he thought I was a giant chocolate easter bunny.” Adam Carr says “That’s not true.” Vega responds “He picked me up and put me on his plate!” Vega shouts “Yea I remember that. That was the year your dad got baptized….at Sea World.” Thug roC says before chuckling a little bit. “That was low roC, even for you.” Vega then crosses his arms -=[The group walks around till they walk over to the baggage check area. You see a line of people waiting. They then get to put their bags and boxes onto a scale to get weighed and then onto a conveyor belt. That belt then goes downa shoot to somewhere in the basement. And at the counter getting his box weighed is none other then….Greg Vulgar. Thug roC shouts out at him and he jets off]=- “Come on!” Thug roC shouts -=[The four runs after him and Hit-Kid was with him. Cops quickly tackle Hit-Kid down, but Vulgar slides down the shoot right after his box went down. Cameras switch to the airport basebment as we see Greg Vulgar come down the shoot and hops off and runs. Thug roC is right behind him along with Adam Carr and Machine. Vega is the last one, but isn’t smart enough to get off the slide. Instead he crashes right into the huge box that Vulgar put on the conveyor belt.]=- -=[Vulgar is quick to run around kids and women, almost knocking many of them over. Vulgar hurdles over a set of carts and the remaining three do the same. He runs up the escalators, sprinting up each step kowing they are behind him. Thug roC and Adam Carr do the same but a little girl cuts in front of Machine. So Machine takes the escalator right next to it. He sprints even faster but isn’t getting anywhere. That’s when he realized that he was running up the down escalator.]=- -=[Vulgar continue to run and dodge throughout the airport. It finally seems that Vulgar is running out of breath as he starts slowing down. He continues to run though, as fast as he can. Thug roC and Adam Carr are the two right behind. You can hear a beeping of some sort as they are running. Suddenly a motorized cart cuts Adam Carr off and he falls inside where two ladies were sitting. His face falls right into the lap of one and he looks up at the 20 year old. She proceeds to slap him and shove him off the motor vehicle.]=- -=[Thug roC is left chasing Vulgar. Vulgar runs out the airport and finds multiple cops staring at him. The cops don’t realize who he is at first, but when they do, Vulgar runs back into the building. Thug roc swings at him but misses. Vulgar runs out another door with Thug roC closer then ever. At the curb is a bus about to pull off. The doors of the bus are closing and Vulgar dives inside just as the doors close and the bus pulls off. Thug roC is left standing at the curb, watching the bus leave with Vulgar inside]=- -=[About 10 minutes later, the group of four are gathered together once again. Most obvious are Carr’s red face from the slap, and Vega being covered in bubble wrap from the box he fell in. They all look depressed as this was probably their one chance to catch him]=- “ Well at least they caught the blonde haired kid, that’s a good thing right?” Machine says, trying to bring the group morale up a little bit. “ Yea but we don’t have the jewlry.” Adam Carr says with an angry tone to his voice. Vega interupts, “Sure we do…” “What are you talking about?” Thug roC asks, drinking some water as he still seems a lil tired “That box I fell in…..look….” -=[Vega pulls out watches, leather wallets, and a lot of other jewlry. The group is all puzzled at what Vega pulls out]=- “Where did you get all that?” Adam asks “That box I fell in. It was like I ran into a treasure box.” Vega responds. “But what would Vulgar be doing with a purse?” Machine questions. -=[Indeed, along with the jewlry is a purse, with lipstick and a tag on it that says “C” on it. The group looks confused but Thug roC remebers something…]=- ~~~Flashback~~~ “ Today the release of Greg Vulgar is on everyone’s mind,” The news reporters starts. “Nine months ago, Vulgar was accused of mob criminal action but was later found innocent by a jury of his peers. Many speculate that the trial was very unfair due to the amount of evidence that was thrown out the courtroom. The streets are…” ~~End Flashback~~~ Thug roC suddenly looks as if there should be a light bulb above his head, “The amount of evidence thrown out…it wasn’t thrown out…it was stolen!” ”What are you talking about?” Carr asks “Vulgar was found innocent and they said that a lot of evidence was thrown out. He stole it back, that’s why they couldn’t find his ass guilty. That purse must’ve been evidence C or something like that!” Thug roC states “So what are we going to do now?” Vega asks, still confused about the whole situation “Well he is still in the area right? This time, he’ll come to us…” Thug roC concludes, before the camera fades to black.
