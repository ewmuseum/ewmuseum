Jinx and HBC Be Clubbin'

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-15-2002 d::The 'Nawlins' night is blasting with music coming from the Jester's Court night club. Jinx and HBC get out from the backseat of a hummer that stops at the front door. They move straight past the huge line and past the bouncer that is holding everyone back. They push through the trancing ravers that are illuminated by their glowsticks, and are directed to the MVP seating. They sit down and order up their drinks. They are surrounded by thumping music, only the best alcohol, and most importantly, many beautiful women that would give anything to be with either one of them.:: Jinx: Well it looks like we all were made a fool of on last night by the Notorious. However, let us not forget the beating that Frost got before his little buddies came out. Revenge is coming for each and everyone of you. EveryAs for me and C, we are in marvelous condition and very prepared for Rise of Legends. It is only 1 week away until Unlimited contains the Evolution Tag Team Champions. Me and Asis are more than ready to take out New-TV. You have Jason Steele coming out and basically saying the the win and the titles are in the bag. But I am right here to tell you that that boy does not know what he is talking about. I will just be adding that tag championship belt to my Cell Championship Belt. Not only that be we have the finest woman in not only the wrestling industry today, but in the entire world, HBC, who will become the winner of the Road to Glory Tournament. Tell 'em C. HBC: J, Rise of Legends is going to be the night of the Unlimited. There is not one person that is going to stand in HBC's way next week. When I am in the ring and Jinx is on the outside, there is no one that can beat me. We are unstoppable. That goes for Notorious, New-TV, and the Brotherhood. Unlimited was the first stable in Evolution and I guarentee that we will be the last. Notorious, you wanna play with fire? I just hope that you know when you play with fire long enough, you are bound to get burned. Trust me when I say, me, J, Xan, and Asis can and will put a definate burn on you for the stunt that you pulled on Legends. Dreamer, I tried to warn you and even your little lap dog, Maggie tried to keep you from your own ignorance. But would you listen to either of us? No. So now the match is set. In one week it will be me and you in Battle for Toronto Match in our Tournament Match. I hope you are prepared. But how could you possibly prepare to face perfection? That's right you can't. ::HBC slings back a Jagermeister and slams the glass back down on the table. Jinx motions for a tall, blonde, fine speciman of a woman to come around the table. She sits down in HBC's lap and lays back her upper body in Jinx's lap and his direction. HBC starts to carress the girl's beautiful, long legs as Jinx brushes her hair back. The girl looks as if she may be in total ecstasy.:: Jinx: My dear C, it does not get any better than this. We are past and future champions, we are members of the most elite group that wrestling have ever been faced with, and now we have what I have no doubt that most beautiful angel in heaven in our laps. Not a thing that we can complain about, C. I definately think that after me and Asis take the tag title belts in a shut-out victory, and you win your tournament, that we come back here to celebrate. Xan and Asis will love this place, especially Asis, this is his kinda environment. However I think it is time that we get these cameras out of here and get down to pleasure, if ya know what I mean. From me, C, and the rest of Unlimited to New-TV, Dreamer, the Notorious, and the rest of Evolution that wants to stand up in our way, WELCOME TO YOUR DESTINY!!!!!!!! ::Jinx looks at C who winks at him. He tells her,'You start on that end of her body, I will begin on this end, and then, we will meet in the middle.' Jinx smiles and leans down toward the awaiting girl in his lap as the camera cuts to the fuzzy snow.::
