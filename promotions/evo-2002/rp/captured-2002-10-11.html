Captured?

11 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.11.02 Scene 1: Debriefing {6 cops sit around a table one standing in front of a overhead speaking.} ‘Now I have gotten word from the police chief one county over that this man is headed our way so we have to be ready…’ The man speaks with a serious tone. ‘He has been seen throwing things and has beaten two men into a pulp in the last hour.’ ‘What do we have to do to be ready chief’ a voice cuts in. ‘Well be ready for anything basically we could get the call at anytime to be sent out to a location. I don’t want any of you going out there alone take another unit with you or just take a partner. We don’t want you to get in over your heads…’ ‘I don’t need the back up…’ A cocky rookie cop speaks. ‘Alright then we won’t give you any.’ What looks to be an older veteran cop chips in. {The young Caucasian male looks over at his elder a black man maybe in his later 30’s and gives him a look of pure and utter confidence.} ‘Back up will be given whether you like it or not Chuckie…even if it has to be me backing you up.’ ‘Ahhh chief just let me take this guy down I bet he is not as big and bad as he sounds…what’s his description 5’10 180 pounds?’ ‘Well according to this description more like 6’2 270 pounds or so.’ ‘Big deal he’s a fat ass…’ The rookie speaks. ‘Not according to this it says and I quote ‘he is very built, perhaps a professional body builder.’ The Chief speaks with a sick smile. ‘…’ Silence comes from the rookie as the elder man slaps him on the back and laughs. ‘He’s all yours buddy…’ {The 6 foot 170 Pound rookie cop stands up and parades around the room with his chin up speaking in a deep voice trying to imitate the chief.} ‘He’s a big bad body builder…let me see that damn report!’ The rookie snatches the report from the chief and he begins reading it over. ‘Wait…this is a phony report isn’t it?’ The man smiles and throws it on the ground before moving over to a pop machine and he pops in 50 cents and pushes a button receiving a pop. {The radios of all the men in the room create a PA type effect in the room as the dispatcher comes on the air.} ‘We have a silver Honda 2000 traveling approximately 60 MPH heading Northbound on Glen Mtn Road. Request back up.’ The voice cuts off and the rookie drops his pop and grabs his radio. ‘I’m on my way…’ The rookie runs out of the door and the chief rolls his eyes and speaks. ‘Charlie would you please follow him…this could be our guy.’ {Without hesitation the man in his late 30’s gets up and walks quickly following the rookie…} ‘I suggest the rest of you get out there in your cars and be ready to follow.’ {All the men jump up as the scene slowly fades.} Scene 2: The car. {The sounds of Spineshank can be heard as a car whips wildly down a residential street weaving in and out of traffic even passing someone on the shoulder. Sirens can be seen in the rear view mirror along with a familiar set of eyes. The car slows to a stop and the driver door opens and a man bolts from the car you can clearly see its Shocka. He runs into a field at full speed. The voice of the rookie cop can be heard.} ‘Get back here!’ The cop screams as he struggles to find his flashlight and moves into the field without any light. ‘The sound of screeching tires can be heard as a second car arrives with there brights. ‘These flashlights are brighter than I thought…’ the rookie speaks oblivious to the situation. {As the cop stands at the edge of the field Shocka’s lead on them grows greater in distance. Charlie the cop sent to back up the rookie speaks on his radio.} ‘Sure he can out run us but he can’t out run the damn radios!’ He turns on the radio and speaks to dispatch. ‘Suspect headed West on Palm Street request additional back up!’ He turns off the radio and looks at the rookie. ‘Get him!’ {The rookie sprints into the field with his flash light on after about 20 steps he falls flat on his face and the light again disappears. In the fair distance you see white shirt approaching it gets to where the rookie fell and the person pulls the rookie to his feet.} ‘You are supposed to try and catch me…’ The man utters in a disgusted tone. ‘What you want me to chase you?!’ Chucky speaks. ‘Of course I do…I need some training…’ By now you can tell its Shocka. ‘You are under arrest…’ ‘How much you wanna bet that I could break those damn cuffs that you are going to put on me?’ Shocka speaks cockily. ‘I’d be willing to bet my life that you couldn’t break the two sets that I am gonna put on ya…just to show you what I am made of…’ ‘That’s what your mom said last night right before she just about choked herself…’ {A look of rage comes over the face of the cop as he quickly cuffs the man and pushes him out of the field and into his cop car. He slams his door and Shocka looks at him smiling.} ‘You messed up bad…’ Charlie speaks with disappointment… ‘What are you talking about?’ The young cop speaks. ‘His rights…you didn’t read him his rights…’ The elder cop speaks laughing. ‘Why in the hell do you think he looks so happy?’ ‘…..’ Nothing comes from the mouth of the previously cocky cop who is now left dumbfounded. ‘Now we can’t hold him on anything…’ {Just as Charlie finishes speaking you can hear a window breaking they both turn quickly to look at the cop car but Jonathan is nowhere to be seen. You can hear the screeching tires of the silver Honda 2000 as it races at the cops before turning about 5 feet in front of them leaving a cloud of smoke.} {Scene fade.}
