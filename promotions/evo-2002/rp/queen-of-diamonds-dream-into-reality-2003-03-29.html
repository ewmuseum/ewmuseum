Queen of Diamonds - Dream Into Reality

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/29/03 She didn’t say a word, and he didn’t make a sound. He didn’t breathe. Jason didn’t do much of anything. If his heart was pumping blood, then it was doing it on its own. If his eyes blinked, it didn’t matter. His eyes never let her loose. Marilee hugged Jason almost immediately, and he puts his arms around her, impulsively and surely. He closed his eyes, and realized that she was in his arms once again, the dream that kept him awake so many nights previous. It was now a reality. Jason wasn’t sure how real it was or anything, but he judged by her smooth skin and her curly, brunette hair, everything he could remember about her, that it was as real as it could get. Everything else was locked away – his championship, his upcoming match, all his misery, all his happiness. They were all momentary memories. Marilee was the only truth that was important. “I missed you Jason.” she said into his ear, opening his eyes and making him cry his first tear of the moment. “Oh God I missed you. I’ve missed you for so long.” Jason didn’t want to let go of her. Ever. He didn’t care about life or death or anything anymore. All irrelevant. She was there with him again. She let go, and kissed Jason off guard. He wasn’t expecting it, but he unquestionably didn’t care. Jason closed his eyes, and smiled through it, knowing that everything was back to normal, and that life was all right once again. Nothing could go wrong, and everything would go his way once again. Life was grand and nothing was… William? What about William, Jason? Jason pulled away, even though she kept kissing his face and squeezing him against her. Lost in the moment, Jason had forgotten the reason why he was pining over Marilee once again. It was because of the man that had taken her from him, and all his hopes to have her back being shattered by his presence. When she stopped and looked into his eyes, she could see his thinking process, and the look of disappointment. “What’s wrong, Jason? I thought that…” “What are you really doing here anyway, Marilee?” Jason asked, a little bit of unwanted anger in his voice. “I’m here because I wanted to see you again, Jason. I happen to love you, remember? I haven’t seen you in years and then when I realized where you were, I came to see you.” “But you can’t be here. You can’t be with me, again.” “Why can’t I? I came here just to be with you again, Jason. Why do I have to leave now?” Jason looked at her, and into her eyes. He could see that she was serious about staying, but he knew that she couldn’t. “Because of him Marilee. Because of William.” Marilee look became one of confusion, as she looked down and thought for a second. Then she looked up at him, a smile still on her face. “He’s only a friend, Jason. We were together, but that ended awhile ago. Before Valentine’s Day, actually. How did you find out about William any…?” “But I saw you with him in Boston. Two weeks ago. You two were eating together. Francis even said that you two were…” “Francis? I haven’t seen Francis in months. We are really close, but we’re only friends, Jason. We went out to dinner. That’s it.” Jason, confused but slowly understanding the situation, laughed an untrue giggle and smiled. “We’re only friends. We’re only friends, Jason, and nothing…” Jason kissed her this time, a long and passionate kiss. And when it was over, a look of life flew into his eyes and shined through his tears of joy. “Ok, Marilee. Ok.” He said, nodding and smiling She hugged him as hard as she could, and all he could do was rest his head on her shoulder, staring down at the table with, ironically, the queen of diamonds and the king of diamonds sitting right next to each other. Another kiss and a few minutes later, Jason knew that he wouldn’t be sleeping anytime soon. But this time, for a different reason altogether.
