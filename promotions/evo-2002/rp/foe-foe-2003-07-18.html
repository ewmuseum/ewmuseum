Foe... Foe...

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-18 Young Sarah O'Halloran giggled as she watched QBX’s head being shoved into the cell on Legends. The 'New Dawn' PPV was airing in the home of Mary O'Halloran, sister of Damon. She also happened to be the legal guardian of Damon’s young daughter, Sarah. Mary fidgeted uncomfortably as she watched the images on the television. “If your father weren’t wrestling tonight, I wouldn’t let you watch this.” She picked up the remote. “With him being on here, I probably shouldn’t let her watch it anyway,” she thought to herself. There was no love lost between Damon and Mary. But for Sarah’s sake, she decided to be civil. The phone rang, and Mary picked it up as QBX stabbed Ali Khadafi in the chest with a pointy object. “Sarah, I don’t think you should watch this match,” she said. “This is for older people.” “Let the kid watch what she wants Mar,” the voice on the other end of the phone said. “Damon? Aren’t you wrestling tonight?” Mary asked. “Yeah. But I’m taking on King to start off with. It’s practically a night off for me.” “Is that why you’re calling? To gloat about an easy match?” Family or not, Mary often had a hard time dealing with Damon’s arrogance. “I just wanted to make sure Sarah was watching,” Damon snapped. “She’s my kid, and since you’ve got the damn courts convinced I can’t be anywhere near her, the least you can do is let her see her old man in action! Bitch!” Mary started to hang up the phone when Sarah turned around. “Is that Daddy?” Mary sighed. “Yes it is.” Sarah beamed as she snatched the phone away. “Daddy? Are you wrestling soon?” “Soon pumpkin.” Damon’s voice softened. “How are you doing? Are you finished school yet? I’m trying to get your aunt to bring you up to the cabin in a couple weeks. Wouldn’t that be nice?” “I like the horsies.” Sarah hopped up on the chair beside her aunt. “Daddy, are you fighting the man on the tv?” “Yeah baby, I'm facing him and a number of guys, but remember these guys are Daddy's friends, they would never do anything to hurt Daddy.” Damon smiled on the other end of the line. “Want me to throw him off someplace high for you? Or hit him with something really heavy?” “Get me a picture of Xander! He’s a cutie!” She handed the phone back to Mary and walked back to the television. Mary raised the phone to her ear. She could hear Damon still talking on the other end. “ - don’t know if I can. But what else have you been doing? I haven’t heard from you in so long, that -” “Damon, it’s me,” Mary said. “Sarah’s watching television again.” “But she didn’t say anything.” Damon’s disappointment was clear. “All she wanted was to ask for some stupid picture of some goddamn wrestler! What kind of crap are you feeding my daughter that she won’t even -” Mary whispered into the phone. “Damon, I don’t have to put up with your abuse. Your daughter likes the belt that he carries around, thats all. Maybe you should get her a replica one. Or maybe you should just spend some time with her. The courts said you could have supervised visits with her.” O'Halloran roared on the other end. “I’m not some goddamn pedophile that I need a fucking chaperone to visit my own child! I’ll visit her when I can, and I’m not about to ask for permission to do it! And if you try and stop me... Don't fuckin' go there.” “That’s why you don’t have custody, you maniac!” Mary hissed into the phone. “You’re out of control. You’re not safe to be around! I have to go now!” As she hung up the phone, she could hear Damon yelling on the other end. “DON’T YOU HANG UP ON ME YOU BIT-” Click.
