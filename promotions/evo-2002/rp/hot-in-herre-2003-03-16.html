| Hot in Herre |

16 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.16.03 | Humid hair felt heavy on the skin interludes sweet to my body. Scorching hot sun rays glistening off our skins as we sat out in ghetto smoking a blunt whole coming up with new ways to exert it from our bodies. My boy Dante sat to the side of me drinking back a forty, chilling for ironic, with me. Tall red brick buildings built around us in no certain fashion made up the projects of which I knew all to well. Buuday and Sheeca were out as usual since the sun first made its presence over the builds. Before long, I used my other lifeline of escaping the heat by taking off my wife beater and tossing it to the side. | 'It's hotter than a bitch out herre! Damn, Buuday and Sheeca betta get they asses back soon!' Squinted in eye, I looked as far as I could both ways for Sheeca and Buuday, then sitting back down and smoking my blunt again. 'Where da fuck Ali go?' 'He prolly took his black ass home! You know it's to hot out here for us black folks! Shit, as soon as we get inside we gone had changed five darker shades already! We gone be the crispy ass motha fuckas around here pretty soon!' Stating the obvious, Duzza continued, 'And if you didn't lock us out the house we wouldn't be going through this! I swear, black folks...' Mild tone irate, Duzza was through. 'It's not my fault! All I kno' is dat the house wuz all foggy 'n shit from da weed clouds and then when I went to bounce out the doo'... Hell, wut da fuck did happen?' Lost myself as to past reminicing. 'Whatever, when the fuck Buuday and Sheeca coming back?! They got spare keys.' 'They prolly on they way right now.'
