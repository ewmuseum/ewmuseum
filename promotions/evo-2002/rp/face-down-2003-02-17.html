Face Down

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/17/03 I looked out the window of my one bedroom apartment, staring down at the streets as if I was looking for something important. The streetlamps gave the expressions on the walking carcasses a shred of life as they roamed, hands in their pockets and eyes to the ground. My heart was beating fast, and my sweat was collecting in my palms. I knew, deep down, that this was truly it. I don’t want to get into a big explanation of all this, as you would probably see in a movie. All I can tell you is that I made a big mistake, and now I was going to pay for it. Sitting there on my windowsill, I wasn’t sure whether I was scared or calm. Probably both, I decided. Below me, the people all seemed just a little more hypnotic. Seemed to carry with them a sense of life that I had never seen before. I wished that I had seen it before, maybe when I might have actually had a chance to share this feeling with them. All I wanted to do was change my world around, and maybe have a life that I could enjoy. But now it was too late, and all I could do was sit, my envy eating away at me. The sound of my cheap cuckoo clock went off. The aged wooden bird sang me a message – the time was 9 P.M. After that, I sort of lost track of time. The world was just too big for me to care about something so little and insignificant. But the one thing that woke me from my daydreaming splendor was the knock at the door. Knock? That was such an understatement. It was more like a… “You fucking bitch! Open this fucking door! Now!” The sounds of the man who thought that I was his property echoed down the hall and into the streets. They were so loud, even the people walking by looked up into the window, seeing my face leaning up against the warm window. That’s one reason I love California. So warm. So very warm. More warming then the heart of a man who I thought loved me. “You don’t want me to fucking knock this door down. You better open it right fucking now!” When I found out he cheated on me, I felt no regret in sleeping with his best friend. Poor Leonard. His only crime was having sex with the girl who he had been after for God knows how long. I thought he was very charming the first time I met him, but I knew that it would never be allowed to happen. Not as long as I was his girl. But after I found out about that whore he slept with, I didn’t care. He was more equipped then this asshole was and I actually enjoyed the sex, and I was planning to runaway with Leonard where he could never find us. Him and his gun had different plans. “Fuck you! I’ll shot the door down. Is that what you want you whore?!” Leonard was shot and killed last night. When I found out, I ran and hid in this hotel. What was I going to do? Tell the cops? They would never help me. I knew that I was alone, and now I was going to die for what I had done. I heard the doorknob blow apart, the gun on the other side ripping it apart with three separate bullets. His foot kicked in the door, and I stared into his face, red with rage. Maybe if I quickly said sorry, he would let me live. But then again, I thought that my hope was over now. “Please. I’m sorry. It just sort of happened, and I…” A fist silenced my final chance at salvation. I guessed that there was nothing left for me to do but go along. “You are going to fucking pay you whore! I’m going to fucking kill you the way you killed me!” His gun’s barrel gazed into my face, and, in slow motion, I looked down at the rug, waiting for the searing kiss of a bullet to quickly take away my life. To tell you the truth, I didn’t really care either. My life sucked anyway. All I really had to look forward to death anyway. As I kneeled there, the pain in my jaw not being felt and the barrel pointing directly into my face being ignored, all I could think about was my older sister. The one that I had to see die from cancer, and that I loved with all of my heart. I tried to be the sister that she always wanted to be, but the drugs and the prostitution got in my way. I wished that I had been a better person. Maybe then I’d have something to live for. God, if I only had one more chance. Just one more chance… The sound of a bullet drowned out all my prayers, but the blackness wasn’t there. Instead, the sound of a window shattering took its place. I felt the glass fall against my back in tiny pieces, and roll off my skin, but I didn’t feel a bullet rip inside of me. I had lived, but I was sure that the next bullet would hit. “What… Fuck you, asshole! Now you’re going to fuckin die.” I looked up, and a man reached out and held the gun away from me. He forced it out of his hands, and tossed it to the ground. The two of them started fighting, with my ex punching my savior in the chest with jabs that I had seen almost kill other men. I could see pain in his eyes, but I could also see a resistance within that agony. As the fists went towards his face, he easily ducked under them and then punched him right in the face. “Oh my God, thank you.” I said, smiling and jumping up to hug my rescuer. Before I could though, he picked up the gun and jumped on the bed. He picked up the remains of the man he had just beaten to a pulp with one punch, and pointed the gun into his face. “You want to threaten her? Do you? Do you?!!” He clicked the safety off, and my ex’s eyes shook. I didn’t know this man, and I didn’t know what he was capable of, but I watched him hold the gun that had tried to take my life and point it into the face of the man I once thought loved me. I didn’t know, but part of me was afraid. “Fuck you!” he yelled, and then slammed the gun against his temple. He was knocked out cold. Knocked out? That was such an understatement. It was more like… “Are you all right?” he asked, his breath still coming back to him. “Fine. Thank you so much. I didn’t think that he was going to…” “Look, I’m sure someone called the police, and they’ll be on their way.” He pulled out a deck of cards and a bunch of bills, and counted a bunch of bills. “Here’s some money. Get yourself a plane ticket home or something, and don’t look back to all this shit.” He handed me over a thousand dollars, and all I could do was stare in shock as I counted it. “No. You helped me and I’m grateful, but I can’t take all this money from you. I don’t even know…” “No, it’s ok. Just take that money and get the hell out of here. That’s how you can pay me back.” He smiled, and I knew that he was serious. Something inside of me felt a little upset at me being unable to do anything. He had just saved my life and handed me a grand and all I could do was smile and say thank you. It didn’t feel right. “It’s not too late to change.” I looked up, and heard the preaching of a prophet right before me. After all the praying and all the wishing, this man just came out of nowhere and turned everything around. With all my fear, he came through and took it all away. In my hour of need, he took my hand and pulled me out of the bleakness. I had to know who he was. “What’s your name?” He looked up at me, as if he had been asked that question a thousand times before by a thousand different people. He chuckled, and opened his deck of cards. “Just a man who happened to be in the right place at the right time.” He said, picking out one card and handing it over to me. He walked out of the room, and into the hallway, where my eyes followed his shadow down the rug and into my past. I looked down, and saw the king of hearts. In black ink, a word was written diagonally along the back side, face down. Jason My knight in shining armor.
