All Good Things Must Come to an End...

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/2/03 {The scene opens up just outside, in the parking lot, of the Arrowhead Pond. The site of Tainted Love. This being Xander's hometown, victory is inevitable.} You know, this was fun before. It had that special little spark to it that made it all enjoyable. But now, I'm tired of you. Sick of looking at your face across the ring from me, and sick and damn tired of having to pound on you. Beating you is getting me nowhere fast. The only plus to this, is that the more I brutalize your carcass, Seara, the more it pisses Frost off.. and there's nothing I enjoy more then trying to get him off of his game, even a little bit. I am still in the dark as to what kind of match this will be little girl, but that doesn't amount to a hill of pig shit to me. I've been in them all. I've created some, and I've conquered all. This will be our last dance. Your infatuation with me, ends now. Tomorrow night, in front of all those screaming Xandarians, I will end this, once and by God for all. And Nik VanCore.. don't think that little stunt you pulled this week means anything to any of us. You were nothing in EDW, and you're even less to me now. Between me, Asis, S., Christoph, The Body Snatchers, and anyone else we add on... you have made a fatal mistake. We are tired of having to take this.. we are the only stable with any power left around here. The gold will be all of ours... and we will defeat all. Seara, Big Daddy Dreamer, King, Citizen, Wisdom, Frost, anyone.. everyone.. all encompassing defeat, and chaos will reign supreme. {The scene abruptly fades out to white noise, and snow before stopping.}
