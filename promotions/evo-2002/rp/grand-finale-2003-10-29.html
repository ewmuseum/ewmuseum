Grand Finale

Date: 2003-10-29 The all too familiar ash begins to blow across the screen and all that have seen it before know exactly what is coming next. Well, maybe not exactly, but a rough idea at least. They know that they’re not going to enjoy what they’re about to see, yet they feel compelled to watch, intrigued by what kind of maniacal scheme the mystery mad man is going to come up with next. The blood covered car was one thing, but what they were about to see tonight, not even the most imaginative of people could have dreamt up. The picture finally, comes into shot, but all there is, is blackness. It takes a while for the eye to register it, but it’s not just total blackness. There are twinkles of bright light, as it suddenly dawns that you are looking up at the night sky, the stars shining on a clear spatial horizon. The camera pans down to show the back yard from above, only visible through a combination of the moon’s ambient glow and warm lights shining through downstairs windows. The camera moves back to show that the operator of it is in fact standing on a balcony, overlooking most of the property he seems to have been stalking. There is a small amount of light coming from behind him and he turns round, revealing that the balcony is outside the master bedroom. The door is one large glass window that provides a perfect view into the sanctuary of the most private room in any house. Then she walks in. the beautiful wife of the man who’s attention he’s been trying to gain. If he didn’t have it by now, he certainly would after this night. The net curtain is transparent enough to view her perfectly, yet if she looks through it all she will see is darkness. She’s clothed in a pair of jeans and a white strappy top, her long dark hair tied up in a ponytail. Even when not dressed up and with little make up on she still looks stunning, the kind of natural beauty that many “models” lack. She moves out of the picture, but the camera remains in the same place, focusing on the spot where she once was. After a couple of seconds she walks back into shot wearing nothing but a matching set of red lingerie, a very attractive lacy bra and a matching thong. You really should take more care of this one. It would be a shame if something happened to her. The same edited voice is over the top of the footage, although it is highly unlikely that the words were spoken during filming, the glass not being that thick. She unties her hair, letting it fall around her perfect breasts and then moves her hands behind her back, unclipping her bra, letting it fall carelessly to the floor. Her breasts stay nice and firm, but are definitely not fake. She then moves her hands to her hips, holding the sides of her thong, and sliding it down her long legs before standing completely naked, her hour glass figure still very much alive, despite being a mother of two children. You really should take better care of her. She obviously takes care of herself. The woman then walks off camera and the night is so quiet that you can hear the shower turn on in the other room. A hand reaches out from behind the camera, taking hold of the door handle. The handle twists and the door slowly opens, the camera moving inside for the first time. It pans round to the left, where the bathroom is, and a shape of a woman in the shower can be seen behind the shower curtain. The door is closed behind him and the camera moves out of the room and into the hallway, heading for the first open door that has “Alex” inscribed on it. In the room a night-light gives some shine to the room, as the young boy lies in his bed, thumb in his mouth fast asleep. Where are you huh? Where are you when your family needs you? Your son lies here, in potential mortal danger and you’re nowhere to be seen. Some parent you are. I could slit his throat and bleed him dry and you wouldn’t know about it till the morning, when you found him in a pool of his own dried blood. The camera turns and moves out of the room and moves further down the hallway, moving into another room lit by the soothing warmth of a child’s night light, toys scattered around the room, a decoration of stuffed animals in every corner. In her crib, the little baby girl, Amy stands holding herself on her feet by the bars, staring innocently into the face of unknown danger. She coos sweetly and if you’re watching you feel your heart skip a beat as you’re overwhelmed by dread and fear for the child. It’s uncanny isn’t it? I bet this is exactly what you’re beautiful wife Adriana looked like as a baby. I can see her mother’s eyes in her already, that beautiful alluring smile starting to develop already. The innocence of childhood is such a gift isn’t it? I could snap your young daughter’s body in two right now and rip her childhood away from her, just like mine was taken from me. She sits there staring me in the eyes, something you yourself are incapable of and I see no fear in them. Just wonder and potential, but whether she lives up to that potential is another matter. You’ve alluded me for a while; you’ve hidden and refused to make yourself seen for far too long. Now, regardless of whether or not I rape your wife and kill your children, leaving your house covered in your own family’s blood, I hope you’ll return to me soon. We’ve certainly got unfinished business to attend to… The last image seen on the screen is that of young baby Amy, smiling an innocent smile, before for one last time, the screen fades to nothing but ash, blowing across the screen.
