Cold Reception-Part I

24 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-24 (10 Years Ago) The wind whistled threw the dark cold halls of the castle, the bricks starting to crumble apart. As the wind weaved threw the halls it slammed all the doors that wear open shut. The owls outside were hooting aloud and peaking their evil yellow eyes threw the windows. A man walked up and down the corridors of the castle, he held a candle in his hand, the wax dripping down to the floor leaving a dotted wax path to his room. The man slammed the door shut blew out the candle and went right to bed on the cold floor forgetting to pull up his blanket, which was pulled over him by something in the room. Next Morning “And that is the beautiful front of the castle.” A tour guide shouted out as John slowly started to wake from his deep sleep. He sat up on the cold damp floor and looked over by the door where he could see a faint man standing next to it. John began to mutter a few words, which were in audible for even the faint man to hear. John slowly got up and walked over to the door and began to whisper to the man. Meanwhile at the entrance to the castle the tour guide and the visitors were getting ready to enter the castle, the last man got inside the castle just as a huge hay bail came crashing down and slamming into the ground, right where the man stood not only 5 seconds before. “Now, don’t panic that sort of thing always happens here, no one knows for sure why but we have an urban legend that a young man with the name of Atchison died here over 100 years ago from a hay bail accident and his ghost still roams these halls. Most people think it is because he has unfinished business on the Earth but no one knows for sure if that is a true story.” The guide kept walking along the corridors of the castle pointing out some of the old paintings that lined the wall, some of the first owner of the castle Mr. Atchison. The bricks of the castle were starting to fall apart over the years of wind and rain erosion. The guide took the tour down to the basement of the castle first where all the battle strategy was thought of and where the leaders would have stayed incase of the enemy getting through the defense. The guide also pointed out that this was the believed spot of where the death of the young man occurred. Once again back upstairs where John and the man were you could still see them whispering to each other with the occasional chuckle of evil laughter thrown into the pot to really make you think twice of them. Finally the silence was broken with a hellish laugh that echoed through the castle and made everyone inside jump including John who was surprised at how the man laughed. But after awhile John joined in making for one hell of an evil laugh that made everyone want to leave the castle. They cut off the laugh when they thought they heard someone entering the room, which only turned out to be the wind blowing the old books around. Back downstairs with the tour, the men comforted all of the women who were almost in a breakdown stage after hearing the shrill laughs echo through the castle. The guide insisted that they continue with the tour, as it was nothing less then someone playing a mean trick on them. So believing in the guide the continued to follow him, with a woman on every arm of one guy. The guide started to take them up the stairs to make there way to the upstairs section of the castle. Once they reached the main hall, the doors slammed shut with a furious thud flinging a piece of wood right between to of the visitor’s head. “Oh My God! Get Me Out Of Here!” A Woman shouted out and ran to the doors, but froze in her footsteps once she got near the door. “Is everything ok miss?” The guide asked with a look of confusion on his face, as to just why the woman was just standing there seemingly looking into space. The woman slowly began to lift here arm before falling over, no one could reach her in time to catch him. They all stood in shock as they saw her head smash off of the stone and her eyes roll into the back of her head. The guide rushed over to make sure she was still alive. “She’s alive.” He said with a relieved look on his face. “Is there anyone who would like to stay with her so nothing else happens to her?” A man in the back raised his hand; the tour guide motioned him to come forward and gave him some instructions under his breath. The rest of the tour continued on leaving the man and the woman in the main hall. Meanwhile back upstairs in the room, the faint figure re-entered the room with a ghastly smile on his face. “HAHAHAH! You should have seen the look on her face, it was priceless! Then when a made her fall back that was even better!” The man said as he walked around the room. “But, I thought you said you weren’t going to hurt anyone.” John said with authority. “Bah! I won’t kill anyone, unless it is by accident of course, aren’t you the one who wants these people to stop coming to the castle?” The man fired back with an evil grin on his face. “I do but I don’t want you to kill them!” John said with a saddened look on his face. “Listen small fry, this is the only way they are going to leave us alone, now either you help or hide because I don’t want them to see you and ruin this whole thing.” The tour slowly went up the stairs, at the top of the stairs the guide turned around to address everyone and tell them that they are entering a very dangerous area of the castle where there has been huge amounts of ghost sightings.
