New-tv 4:20 International!

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4:20 International Time... What time could this be... The time for wild passionate sex? No, that would be hump-day. The time for off the wall, intense battle? That would be Legends. The time for smoking the finest herbs on all of God's green earth? Now you have the right idea... 4:20, international time and also one very special holiday. The scene opens with all of the current New-TV members sitting around a glass table with big grins on their faces. The group of them for the first time, they don't have snarling, sick looks on their faces. Sky Night can be seen cutting something into her hands with a pair of silver scissors. 'End Of Days is nearing quickly now, this Legends we will have to show our true colors and prove to everyone that New-TV always steals the show and just who's backyard they are roaming...' Morpheus says with a unique, strange smirk while nodding. 'Nothing can compare to us...' Thug roC retorts with a serious look on his face, he continues. 'The sudden downfall of Shocka's career has only begun... The annoying, high-strung bastard probably has no clue about this all being pre-meditated since before Tainted love, when you screwed him for the Universal title...' 'Him and that Xero punk, they will both have the same fate... Shocka has been beaten at the hands of the all knowing many times before... Though Xero on the other hand...' Hedges says and hesitates, he smiles 'He never made the mistake of crossing me until now!' 'You cant tell me that he never watched you humiliate and destroy Blitz on Immortal I... Xero has some kind of point to prove and feels it should be on your cost... You could end his chance to shine before the sun even rises if you know what I mean!' Morpheus says and breaks out into sick laughter... 'Almost ready for blast off?' Sky questions with a peculiar look. 'Time to celebrate my favorite sacrilegious holiday... You know what they say, go hard or go home!' she says smiling. Sky lifts her hand and a large pile of green herbs is scattered across the table, she pulls a large rolling paper from pack and rolls the paper back and forth in her hands a couple of times. 'New-TV kicking it old school...' Hedges says with a big grin on his face. 'They also say don't get high on your own supply...' 'That's alright because I stole this from Fiona Preston's locker room last week when she was competing...' Sky says with a raised eyebrow and smiling. 'They also say, with booze you lose and with dope there's hope! I think you must of tipped the bottle one to many times before last Legends, hah ah!' Morpheus replies staring at Hedges Hedges stands up with a crazed look in his eyes, he points in the face of Morpheus with a snarling look on his face. Hedges breathes heavily. 'Im only messing with you, sit down before I actually decide to hurt you' Morpheus says and Hedges remains staring at him the same way. 'Sarcasm, I would think the all knowing would now a little about it, Heh eh!' Morpheus says and extends his hand to Hedges. 'You are lucky that you are, who you are Morpheus... One of these days you will push the wrong button and I will have you for lunch... Just remember who brought you into this business with good faith and showed you the ropes!' Hedges says with sound of concern in his voice, he sits back down following. 'I was only provoking and aggravating anyways...' Morpheus replies. 'That way next time you see that circus freak Shocka, you will have some more built up rage inside of you. You know, if you wanted I could super glue a picture of his face to MLX's face for some initiative this Legends to win the Evolved championship' Morpheus mentions and then shakes Hedge's hand. 'Its alright, Im straight with Martin Luther Xavier... then when I am done with him, I will hunt and eliminate that undeserving, jealous Xero... I cant blame him though, If I was him, I would want to be me too!' Hedges says very conceitedly with a cocky smirk. 'Get bent, but anyways...' Sky changing the subject and pauses to lick the glue on the fat spliff she rolled up. 'Its 4:20 on the date 4/20/03 in less then a minute, so lets get ready to light this bad boy up...' Sky says with a twisted look on her face, she grins wide. 'Now you have the right idea, listen to these two over here... There all about work and no play... Lets smoke that fatty!' Thug roC says with a smile on his face, he nods to Sky. 'Someone who can relate where Im coming from...' Sky says to Thug and turns and gives Morpheus a jokingly-dirty look, she puts the blunt inside her mouth and lights a lighter that makes the end blaze strong. Sky takes a long haul, then another, then another and inhales a huge lungful. 'Like a champ!' Hedges says with a smile on his face. Sky passes the joint to Omega and exhales a big cloud of smoke into his face which billows and surrounds, making his face not visible. 'Ill show you how its done!' Morpheus says waving all the smoke from in front of his face, he holds the fatty like a cigarette between his fingers and like an amateur. Morpheus heaves off the end of it and takes huge, power hauls from it. Morpheus inhales and coughs hard, he heaves and coughs even harder with watery eyes. Morpheus gets a dizzy look on his face and hands the joint off to the side of him. Thug roC intercepts. 'Damn man, puff puff pass...' Thug roC says with an angered look on his face. 'You not know the basics of smoking tweeds 101? For real, I don't know what to do with you sometimes...' Thug shakes his head side to side and holds the joint and heaves back twice, really hard and lets smoke come out of his mouth and inhales some back through his nostrils. Letting the rest of the smoke elevate a moment, he sucks really hard and creates a vacuumed tunnel of smoke which all goes back into his mouth. Thug smiles and holds the smoke in his lungs. 'That's how its done junior!' he says while exhaling the rest of the smoke. 'Upside to things, cough to get off right Morpheus?' Hedges questions and awaits a response... No answer, 'Morpheus???' he questions again and receives no response. Hedges waves his hand in front of his eyes and Morpheus seems to be spaced right out. Hedges bitch smacks, which doesn't faze him the slightest. 'More for the rest of us, pass that before it burns away to nothing!' Sky says with an impatient look on her face. Thug passes the joint over to Hedges who takes some heavy pulls and exhales a huge cloud of smoke into the air. Hedges laughs and passes it on. 'Ohhhhhhhhh Sweet Mary Jane, the only woman I would abuse and take advantage of without having to kill after...' Sky says with a smirk on her face and fills her lungs once again with the sweet, tasting fine cannabis sativa smoke. Sky exhales with a calmed, peaceful look. 'You should wipe that drool from the corner of his mouth...' Thug roC says to Sky Night and shakes his head. 'He don't look to deadly now, does he?' Thug asks and looks back and forth from Hedges and Sky, he takes the joint and takes some more heavy pulls from it. Thug holds the smoke inside his lungs awhile and then exhales the largest cloud of smoke imagined. The entire room now filled with thick, heavy gray smoke and nothing can barely be seen. The scene fades during the chronic session, celebrating 4:20!
