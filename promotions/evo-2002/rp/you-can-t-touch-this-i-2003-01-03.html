.you.can't.touch.this.i.

3 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: .tenth.jan.03. It stood in front beckoning him closer, closer still, as the mist settled a grim shadow remained, it looked to be too small to be VDV, however, in this distorted world of the hidden imagination, big is small and small is big. His face was hidden by a set of mangy brown dreadlocks, covering his face with a passive dictatorship. As an invisible hand wrote these letters in the sky in front of his very face, conveying every deep thought and emotion… My pen staring at the note pad, due to my pondered thoughts being trapped And some how my ignorance remains intact, in fact it’s fighting back, and on the attack of my thoughts path If you could look me in the eyes you’d see why celestial angels cry And why Eternal Beings wish to die I’m sifting through the propaganda to see what lies I despise all poison going through my mind and causing it to get fatter But, the subject matter isn’t going to be about Mind Nor Matter, because Mind and Matter really doesn’t matter It’s all going to fade away Only my soul won’t see Decay But it can certainly sway, from good ~ to ~ evil ~ to ~ evil right back to good But why? Am I losing my mind? Is it because I’ve over stood... everything that was never meant to be revealed All this truth that’s been concealed, has me feeling like; everything I touch is dismantled So I’m stuck in a 'inner-turmoil battle' running from my own shadow I hear the wind calling me, telling me to rid my life of all unneeded property I open my mind and find that I’m a step closer to finding just what wakes me up every night in a cold sweat And what has me day dreaming about every little thing I regret The striving after respect and not realizing I’m just a spec that can’t detect my own flacks & flaws I hear the teardrops from heaven and I pause…. As thunderbolts strike out with a force Knocking my pondered thoughts off course I drift to a 'scene from a memory' in which I’ve never lived God forbid that I’m hallucinating about images with no root of truth My pilot directs me to the hidden proof to sooth my 'self-inflected' wounds I’m consumed by self-hatred and pity An outlaw to my own city, as I sit and stare The only soul that dares to repair the negative aspects of my life But I’m terrified by the words of which I write For I see truth in the eyes of a gawking reflections emptiness 'Everything is Meaningless' I cry out, I can’t stop pondering what this life is about I’m torn between two routes, that both lead to death I pause and take a deep breath…and proceed to walk blindly It is at this moment that I finally realize that my hand won’t let me stop writing 'All I’ve ever wanted to do is find myself through jotted thoughts seeing that I’ve ever sought has taught me that in essence I REALLY DO HATE THIS!' Live and let live, crush or be crushed, hate or be hated. Live if he lets you…
