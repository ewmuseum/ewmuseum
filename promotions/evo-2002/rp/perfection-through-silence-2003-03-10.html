Perfection Through Silence

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10th March 2003 Tainted Love was indeed a pay per view that lived up to its name. There were many battles where the combatants held ill feelings towards one another, not least of all being the main event. And many people thought that Scott Frost got what he deserved in winning the Universal Title, and Shocka got what he deserved in losing it the way he did. And at the same event, Asis did something that he has gotten pretty damn good at as of late; he fulfilled a promise. And that promise was to retain his Continental Title against Big Daddy Dreamer, and make sure that BDD couldn’t get up after it. This he did, by goring BDD through the wall of the Cell and then making him unconscious through his tazzmission style manoeuvre, Immune. Also in the same night, Troy Pierce asked Daniel Somers to put him in a match with Asis at Legends XXVIII, if he won any match that Somers picked for him at Legends XXVII. Somers granted Pierce's request, putting him in a match against Chuck which Asis watched Troy Pierce win from ringside. He has not spoken since before Tainted Love and it’s only now in Portland that he speaks his mind… It’s been quiet recently. Really quiet, silence kind of quiet. I don’t mind it, because it means that I’m alone and that is something that I’ve gotten used to and come to feel comfortable with during my lifetime. But never have I been truly alone, I have always had my thoughts with me, I have always had my mind to retreat into. My mind may not be the kind of place that most people would want to spend their time, but I’ve always felt welcome there. But now even that is gone, I can no longer use my mind as a safe haven, it feels as if it’s no longer there. Asis paces around, rubbing the top of his head and closing his eyes tightly, shaking his head from side to side and making strange unfamiliar noises. It’s as if there’s nothing up there any more. That my head is completely devoid of all thought and I can’t remember when it went silent. Maybe it was after I gored BDD through the cell wall and kept my “title”. Maybe it was when I tried to kill Jimmy Orcs. Maybe it was when L2J died. Who knows. I don’t that’s for sure. All I know is that I can’t hear anything anymore. There is nothing there and it scares me. Wait, maybe it excites me? I’ve always been branded “crazy” or “insane” whatever you want to call it, which I probably brought on myself. But now, that sickness is gone, I hear no voices, there is nothing influencing me but my own emotions. And as always my emotions are telling me to hurt, to maim, to destroy. That’s exactly what I wanted to do to BDD and that’s exactly what I did. I didn’t do it because I want to keep my Continental Title, if anything I didn’t want him to have it because he values things like these. You take a championship status away from someone like BDD and they’re left with nothing but bitterness. Asis is still pacing around the blank lifeless room that he is in. No furniture, no windows, nothing but a white room. He does not look at the camera, but stares at the floor, or the walls, or just shakes his head around. All in all, Asis looks to be disturbed, despite what he is saying. I took it away from BDD and despite his attempt to brush it away quickly, I know it burns him up that a comparative rookie like me walked all over him. But now, another veteran gets on my case. Another person gets my attention that will soon learn that it’s not something you should desire. A man that I crossed paths with briefly before, briefly enough to smash a steal chair over his head. Since then, we have kept clear of each other and now he wants to pick a fight with me. All over a spilt beer, and now you’ve gotten yourself in a situation where you are going to get very hurt. And much like I said to BDD, that is a promise. And recently I have become good at keeping my promises… And thrown innocently into the mix of this at Legends is Jason Jameson, a man who I have also crossed paths with in the past. A man that I feel I owe something as well. Some might say that he has moved up in the world since our minor feud a few months back and I would probably agree with them. However, looking at him now, he’s nothing special, I’ve never thought he is. I’m not putting myself above him, but he lacks something. It’s not anything that he couldn’t have, it’s just something he denies himself. If he were to let himself go, to let himself become true, he would be great. But he holds himself back; he never lets his full potential shine through, because he is afraid. You are Jason, you’re afraid of what you could do to people. I see it in your eyes, you know that you don’t just want to gain victories in that ring, you want to hurt people, make them pay for all the shit that you’ve had to put up with in your life. But you don’t release yourself from your torment. You keep it locked behind your façade of a life, letting it build up. One day though Jason, it will break through those locks, those safety measures you’ve put in place but until that day comes, you will just be Jason. Which is why at Legends, you and Pierce will fall to Unlimited, to Xander and I. For the first time now, Asis looks focused and stares into the camera. He just stares into the camera for a while, as if he’s trying to see who is looking back on the other side of it. Pierce, up until recently, you had not said a word for months. You’d walked around arenas trying to charm your way through things, without any actions or words to get by on. Well unfortunately for you Troy, I am not something that you can charm your way through. Now that you’ve gotten my attention, I am not going to let up, until you’re not there to have it any more. I will go through you just like I did BDD and you will never feel the same again. I will fulfil another promise to myself and to you by making sure that you never try anything as foolish as this again. My silence has lasted mere weeks compared to your months Pierce, but what do you think you achieved from it? Did you gain anything from it; did it serve you any purpose? Did you achieve perfection through silence? I don’t believe so and I will prove it to you at Legends Troy. Be careful what you wish for…
