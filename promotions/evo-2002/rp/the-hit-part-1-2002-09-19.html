The Hit (Part 1)

19 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19/02 {A half way burned streetlight illuminates a other wise dark and deserted street. Below the light is a sidewalk and a garbage can. To the left somewhat out of the light is a building where we can here a couple fighting. The gloomy and somewhat scary street is now greeted by the face of a man in a business suit he walks with a hurried step perhaps this isn’t where he is from. The neighborhood sure doesn’t fit him. Another face moves into the picture also wearing a business suit the two greet under the light and a conversation insues.} ‘Are you him?’ The man with the hurried step says in a dull tone. ‘Am I him?’ The other man says. ‘Yes…I heard about him.’ The first says with a slight smirk. ‘What’s funny?’ The other man says with a sort of frightening tone. ‘Uhh…nothing…I can see you want to get to business.’ The smirk on the man’s face turns to a very intense look he reaches into his pocket and grabs something as the other man speaks. ‘Not here…’ He says with a hand raised in the air. ‘And you can call me Dragon, the red dragon.’ The man we now know as dragon speaks. {A snicker comes from the man that does not fit in to well.} ‘Again I will ask you what you find funny?’ The red dragon speaks with a serious tone. ‘Not a joking man are we?’ The man says with a forced smile. ‘By the way you can call me Bear…a dancing bear.’ ‘How dare you mock me?! And you expect me to make you a deal?’ The man shakes his head and turns halfway before the other man grabs him by the arm and bring him facing him. Dragon looks at his arm then to the man brushing his arm off. ‘Don’t you ever touch me…or instead of me doing the hit on your friend I will be doing a hit on you!’ {The man steps back and looks Dragon in the eye. His intensity seems to penetrate through the man planning to have someone killed.} ‘You got what I want?’ Dragon speaks with a slightly angry tone.
