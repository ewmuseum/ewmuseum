The Underground Series: Episode 1

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-28 3rd November 1999 [SMACK] I laid in another punch to my opponent as he slowly and dazed tried to dodge, he was a black male, short hair and in red trunks, he seemed prepared for what was to come as I laid in another attack. [SMACK-SMACK-SMACK] Double punch-uppercut combo send his neck snapping back and to the front again, the crowd was baying for blood and I could feel my sweat drop to the gravel floor as I laid another beating in this underground lair. [SMACK-SMACK-THWAP] A thunderous kick that a normal man would never have taken, but that’s why we call this Ultimate Fighting… the black gravel floor the black walls and roof, only the lights were a different colour from the rest of the furniture, lighting our fight area with dim blood red colours. [SMACK] Another hit, this time he hit, I was distracted by more and more bets being placed on my side, nice to know they believed as I set up to end it. “Ayah!” I screamed as I dived in, I grabbed my opponents neck with my right arm and swept him off his feet. I had his head 180 degrees the way it should be upside down, I slammed him into the metal mesh that surrounded us before hitting a brain buster onto the gravel, the referee checked, I had landed him on his head and he wasn’t breathing, still the crowd cheered as I stood in shock, the referee got up and walked over to the boss of the underground brawlers, Don Carlucci who screamed… “WHAT?!” He was shocked, one of his prize fighters, killed. “Your winner and still undefeated in the Underground… Shockwave!” the referee shouted, evidently concerned for what had just gone on. The mesh was held up as two men in suits ran into the ring and pulled the man out from the ring… I was disturbed because I knew what would happen to him. He would be disposed of in the proper manner, in the Roman Catholic was of course. A man slowly walked up to me as I got out of the mesh cell. “Follow me please…” the man droned in a very boring voice, not at all like the rest of the mobsters who spoke with the Italian-American New York drole. The man walked through the crowd and took me to the back of the crowd. We walked through a corridor to the locker room where the fighters gathered. Standing in front of my locker was the boss’ right hand man Tommy ‘Gun’ Mariposa and two of his goons. The locker room was a small room, perhaps only the size of ten telephone boxes, the grey cement covered every part of the room, there was a small bench and some blue lockers, it was a pretty boring place but it was sanctuary before the battle. The chauffeur walked off as I stood, no fear toe-to-toe with Tommy. He was pissed, he was a little taller than me, lanky one might say, his cream suede suit was not ruffled at all, the man had strange features, aside from being thin and tall he had large ears and his nose bent to the right a little, I stared into his cold ice blue eyes as I looked at the bold bearded twins each side of him, they looked identical. “If you don’t mind you are in front of my locker…” I sneered up to his bent nose. He laughed and shook his head. He reached up and pulled out an Uzi, he placed it directly against my stomach, if he pulled the trigger it would cause a rapture through my right lung and stomach. “Listen you little shit,” he smiled cockily and spoke with that typical ‘New-Yawk’ drole I previously mentioned, “the boss wants me to ban you from The Underground, for killing Terry.” He gave me an evil glint in his eyes, something was wrong, something he wasn’t saying, but I wasn’t going to question that, it would leave more space for questions later when I needed them. “But you know what?” he smiled again, “I got a better idea. Before you leave, you are going to throw a match, put a great fighter to the top by beating the unbeatable ‘Shockwave’. Capiche?” Evidently this guy had watched too many Godfather movies. I laughed and looked up at his two bruisers “What if I don’t?” I questioned, not backing down from the bully. “Let’s just say umm…” he stuttered, he seemed to be nervous because I questioned him, “…let’s just say your infamous ‘Fists of Fury’ won’t be so angry no more…” I laughed off his comment, he shoved the gun further into my abdomen sneering. “If you know what’s good for you then you’ll throw the fight and get us our money…” he snarled. They walked off with that typical strut all bullies have. I watched them go when I noticed a shadow in the dark of the corridor, I looked out but no-one was there, I could hear footsteps going away, must just have been the guys. I turned to my locker and pulled out my stuff before heading away…
