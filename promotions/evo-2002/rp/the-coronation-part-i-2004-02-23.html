The Coronation: Part I

23 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-02-23 'Balloons?' 'Party streamers?' 'Ten foot cake?' 'What next? A set of dancing monkeys?!' the courier thought to himself as he tended to the final checks on his delivery sheet. 'No they were in the other lorry' a man said emerging from behind the check sheet. 'Tell me your joking' the courier replied. 'Nope they were shipped in from a zoo in the south I am told' the ginger bearded chap replied as he too glanced over the paperwork. 'What the hell is this all for?' the courier asked shaking his head in disbelief. 'Private party, did you not see?' the collegue said pointing to the top of the page. 'Ethan M Coles?' the courier said thinking aloud. 'More money than sense but funny as hell' the collegue smiled, 'just trying to make a name for himself within Evolution' 'The wrestling promotion?' the courier enquired. 'The one and only' the guy said turning away only to then be called back. 'Wait is Ethan related to Lord Coles the billionaire?' the courier said grabbing hold of his colleugue. 'Quite a break away from the family business' his new found friend nodded in agreement. 'I wonder if I will get a tip?' the courier said again thinking out loud. 'A war story more likely!' the friend replied as he bundled into his own van about five yards away from the couriers van which sat in the foreground. 'So long as I get a tip I really don't care' the courier grinned as he pulled too the door on the drivers side. 'Goodbye open road, hello retirement' the courier smiled to himself as he placed the van into first gear. This was something he could not be late for.
