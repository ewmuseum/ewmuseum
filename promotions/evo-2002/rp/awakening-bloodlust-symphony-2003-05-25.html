Awakening - Bloodlust Symphony

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-25 You ever have the taste of vengeance hanging off the tip of your tongue? I was sitting there, one evening when the wind was low and the temperature was high, drinking down a bottle of Heineken and staring down at the sidewalks of the city. That evening was this evening, if you really want to know or if you had any doubt, and it was the evening before the day I would take Thug roC’s pointless, little life. The beer, as strong and tasty as it had always been, was not enough to get rid of that need for vengeance. Sure, I had beaten him with a heavy pipe a few weeks ago, and even had the great thrill of costing him the Evolution Championship. I mean I didn’t just ruin his chances of being one of the few distinguished men to hold that title, including Jason, as ironic as that is, but I also took it from him. He had the opportunity to bathe in the glory of satisfaction before I stole it all away from him. Standing there, swallowing the brisk alcohol and reveling slowly, thinking about how I had just days before crushed one of many dreams, all I could do was smile and realize that I, after that short period of self-loathing, truly had a purpose. All thanks to the failings of Thug roC. With the bottle empty, my hands reached over the side of the porch, leaving the bottle to dangle over the edge. I stared into it for a few seconds, taking into consideration just what it was and what its meaning in this crazy world could be. My eyes looked through the green and saw an essence glowing in the still of the night. Somehow, as irrelevant as that God damn glass seemed to one side of my brain, the other side told me that it was so much more. Left side: It’s just a mixture of sand painted green used for the packaging and distribution of a product used to control the members of society who can’t control their addictions and give college students something to do in their off time. Nothing more. Right side: But look at it. Look at what you hold in your hand. The very spirit of the world, glimmering in the palm of your hand and sharing the warmth of your body. You carry apart of this world with you. Left side: It’s just a piece of freakin glass. A God damn bottle. What more do you want from it? Right side: A piece of the universe, as insignificant as any other spec of dust in the vast ocean of the sky, and you grip it sternly and honestly in your hands. Doesn’t that give you a taste of what you are apart of? Left side: And you expect to…? Wait a second. You do have a point there. I am holding a piece of the universe in my hand. I’m holding it in my hand. I have complete control over what I choose to do with it. I am the one that decides whether it goes on as being apart of the world, or transforms into pieces of rubble on the street. Right side: No, wait. That’s not what I meant. It’s its own separate entity. That piece of scrap you hold is apart of something far greater then any mortal can understand. It is so much more then just a… Left side: And I can choose to end it. Just like that. Well, my left side suddenly remembered that there was such a thing as gravity, and that it just happened to be on duty every moment of every day. The sweat in my hand stopped flowing, and the right side silenced, waiting for what it probably saw coming moments ago. My fingers loosened and my wrist’s muscle just let go. The weight of the bottle pulled the useless container falling to the ground, screaming through its neck in the wind. Seconds later, when there was no longer and room for a fall, the bottle hit the cement of the sidewalk, and shattered. Music to my ears. “Hey asshole!” some voice echoed up from the street below. “What the fuck?!” I didn’t pay any attention to him. None at all. He didn’t matter, just like that bottle didn’t matter. I had the power in my hands, so to speak. It was I who carried the choice of whether that bottle would live or die, and in mere seconds I could decide the fate of a piece of God’s kingdom. Dropping that bottle gave me one heck of a sensation. Like being able to choke the life out of a human being. Maybe that was how I would feel tomorrow, after my match. Cause it sure as hell reminded me of that bloodlust I held a few seconds ago, only the pleasure of fulfilling that desire for revenge. It’s one great feeling. Sort of like the taste of satisfaction hanging of the tip of your tongue.
