Alone And Surrounded

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th December 2002 Alone, always alone, but lately never without another human. Seara’s words open the scene as the camera fades into a partially light room. The female wrestler is sitting in the hotel room of Scott Frost, perched on a seat at the bottom of his bed, watching the male sleep; he is unaware of her presence in the room. In the background music can be heard quietly playing as Seara is seemingly mesmerised with “Stairway to Heaven” by Pink Floyd. She leans forward and reaches out, placing her hand on the bottom of the bed, moving her jacket off it and placing it on the floor in front of her. I took matters into my own hands at Legends. Shocka, Morpheus Omega, and with Luther Wisdom’s help, Xander. Shocka, you are one person that will remember me from when I joined, you Xander should also remember my early days, my apparent friendship with The Demonic Asis, a Unlimited member, I never hesitated once, I never paused and thought about the damage that would happen to me if I went after Asis, interfered in his matches, attacked him, I did it because I have the urge to hurt him, yes have, I still have that urge, but I have felt the enjoyment of Asis being put down by me, and I briefly began to experience what it would be like to take out you three. Xander you and me go back, you annoyed the fuck out of me when I came here, and you were in my face all the time, you couldn’t back off and let me breath, but now, I want you again. I want to feel your blood flow across my hands; I want to make you scream in pain, and I know that you will be more than happy to take me out. It is not new information that you hate me, and it is obviously clear to you that I have no want to be friends or too even be nice to you, so I say Xander, be weary that I am here, and don’t underestimate me, I may have a few surprises for you. Another thing, do not think that sending Asis after me will stop me or scare me away. Turning to the camera Seara gives a sadistic smile then turns back to look at Frost, getting to her feet she slowly paces back and forth across the room as though waiting for Frost to wake up, but is apparently thinking as she stops and looks at the ceiling in a moment of deep thought. Lighting a cigarette she sits back down and places an ashtray on her leg. She stares at Frost as he turns in his sleep and pushes the covers down to reveal the top half of his body naked. The female laughs slightly at the innocent look Frost portrays, as he is asleep, unknowingly being watched. Funny how people act so differently when they are not trapped in that ring like a caged animal, funny how they act when they don’t know they are being watched, to be honest, this is the only real time any of us are different, but no one can help that, there is an innocence about a sleeping human, one that even the most violent people have, Asis, Xander, Shocka, Omega, even Sovereign, all humans have the unique ability to look innocent and different as they pass their time thinking and dreaming about better things. We all dream about better things, well, at least once. Seara is quick to turn her head back to Frost as he seems to wake up, stretching the male yawns and opens his eyes, Seara makes no movement and waits as the male wrestler falls back to sleep, it seems to be a strange thing for her to do, not letting the person she loves know she is there. Let him rest. Her words are quiet and she moves in front of the camera, which had begun to move toward the bed and the calm male. I know it seems strange, me sitting here saying to let him sleep, why then would I come here and talk to you guys, well not even I know that. Maybe I wanted to be near the person I love, maybe I just wanted to watch him sleep, maybe I wanted you guys to know that you are all wrong. Frost leaving Notorious is not the end of the world, yes it is a shock to me, and to L2J and Jameson, but we will cope. I know that Frost thought he was the weak link, but Scott was stronger than I think we anticipated, I think we all thought about him as the leader, but certainly I did not expect him to harbour such violent thoughts. This brings me to another little rumour I have hearing floating around the sports radio stations and throughout the media. Yes, Scott may have darker thoughts than people may have cared to imagine or believe, and yes in some way he is dangerous, he may have hurt people, it may please him to do it, just like it pleases many of us, but no, for what your rumours have been saying, there is a huge NO. Never once has Scott Frost harmed me, never once has he raised his hand to me, and if Scott is the man I love and trust, then I know he will not hurt me physically. Getting to her feet, Seara takes a magazine in her hand and shows it to the camera. This magazine the biggest magazine for eVo fans, this is where I found the first rumour that Scott hit me and beats me, forcing me to stay with him. The females face turns from a calm collective face to one full of rage and a want for the pain. She is quick to grab a table, but it seems that Frost had not gone back to sleep, but had instead been awake listening to her words, jumping out of the bed he grabs her and makes her put the table back down. Frost – Katherine, it’s a magazine and a rumour, don’t let that shit get to you. It is more than that Scott, it is the principal of the truth, the concept of the lie, the death of the trust, too many times it has happened and now they will try it again, and I wont let them break us, I wont let them think they can get away with it. Frost begins to rub the female’s shoulders and tries to calm her as best as he can before asking the cameraman to leave, telling him that Seara is not in the perfect mood and that he could spark off a violent streak by being there. As Frost turns back to face Seara, the cameraman gets up to leave, but he continues to film as the male superstar sits down and kisses the female. The cameraman leaves the room, closing the door, and the camera fades to black. To be continued…
