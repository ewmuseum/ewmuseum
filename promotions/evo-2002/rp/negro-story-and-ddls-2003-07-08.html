Negro Story and DDLS

8 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-08 New York City Club Zero Late evening Ah what a fine night to be the new holders of the prestigious Evolution Tag Team gold. Granted for the Miss Heartbreaker, it is her third occasion, but the first gold for Big Daddy Dreamer and the first time as a team here in Evolution. It was real simple, put up or shut up and St. Violence couldn’t put up so, like many teams previously, they had to go. The world watched in shock and awe as BDD powerslammed Deacon off the ladder and through the ring. That was sure to be a moment in the second Evo-2 special anniversary. As we said, this isn’t a one time run, this is for the long haul baby. So as The Syndicate are relaxing and kicking back inside the cavernous Club Zero, thoughts dwell on their next upcoming match. An eight person tag match and their new found ‘friends’, the bodysnatchers. So let us enjoy greatness personified, as The Syndicate take the floor and address their new pretenders to the crown. ‘So let me get this straight D, this week were in a eight-person tag match. Evo versus ULWN kinda thing?’ ‘Yup, afraid so C. Some people think they’ve been held down by the man or something, not getting their chance at the big time.’ ‘Well boo fucking hoo. Get good then, instead of bitching and moaning about it. I swear if some teams actually committed as much time as they do bitching to training, then all of this would be a moot point. I’m tired of these rejects thinking they are the shit, but only to find out, they haven’t done squat. ULWN? Woohoo! Now that is something to be proud of! I guess not everyone can be, start and maintain the top rung like we have these past seven years.’ ‘True dat, preach on sista!’ ‘LOL. Don’t be all getting me started. You all best recognize! We got a bunch of playa hata wannabe’s disrespecting me, what up with dat! Make’s me want to represent ya know, like throw down a ho down, and make me pimpslap somebody! Hell people, we so good, make’s ya wanna slap yo momma!’ HBC looks at BDD, BDD looks at HBC, both snicker before busting out laughing! HBC grabs the microphone and heads over to the DJ area, flashes BDD the evil look before going off like top flight security! ‘Yo…yo…listen up all. This week we got to speak on two teams , coming in and hating me, yo. So, ho, I’m gonna drop it like this yo! We got Negro Story from the ULWN, coming in, trying to fit in, but hey, its more than that say, they simply don’t appreciate. Playa hata’s one and all, bitching and moaning, whining and groaning, bout how, they’ve been held down by the man. Truth is son, that’s the plan. See, teams like you, don’t have clue, bouncing from fed looking for something to do, but hey yo, this is the show, the big time, you can’t do jack or even rythme. So now you want the champs, the baddest team that went to hell and back. You want me, the HBC and her dynamic cohort Big Daddy D to step in the ring, give you shot because you think your deserving…pleeze! Bitch, you ain’t nothing to me and D. We THE team, 3-times running for me and once for my man D. So yo, bring this shit on and your gonna get stepped on cuz this is Evolution story, and its gonna get gory. Were gonna slash, gash, cut you in half, killin, chillen just like the villians we are, but before its done and through, you’ll know were the real deal, so take your uncle tom ass back to the redneck lands of the pathetic piss poor ULWN hack ass crack…peace! Everyone in the crowd goes nuts after HBC’s freestyle rap, chanting C over and over again. HBC flashes the double crossed peace sign and winks over to BDD before continuing. ‘So Negro Story in case your uneducated ebonic black ass doesn’t get what the hot white girl you wish you could with is saying, its like this. You didn’t have to whine and moan like alittle bitch, all you had to do was ask. But no, instead you pull this race crap, so fine. Have it your way ‘boy’. Since were the champions, and we make the rules, were going to give you a little added bonus, a little stipulation that you should be familiar with. How about we make this……. ‘A strap match!’ ‘After all you should be used to it. I mean hell you come out here and say you’ve been held down by the man, now get beaten down by the bitch circa 1930’s in the deep dirty south. Tying you up like the masta and whipping you for stealing my cornbread. But hey, don’t worry, after we brutally decimate you, we’ll give you a job you can pleased with. Picking cotton on one of my many plantations! How do like that, BOY!’ ‘Oh hey now, there is another team in the mix isn’t there, figures Story couldn’t do it by themselves because that would require some knowledge of education and so we have Daddy’s Dirty Little Secret. A bunch of child molesting freaks who get off playing with little boys and unichs. Hell son, you disgust us. Pieces of crap like you shouldn’t even be allowed in the ring. So after this match, and we get done with you, its off to ASH you go with the rest of the freaks. DDLS, shit, you’ll probably like get beaten, so we may just have to set you ablaze on fire just like ECW of old. Hey D, you want to represent!’ BDD takes the microphone. ‘Hey yo, is she hot or what! Damn straight! Why oh why are the Bodysnatchers involved with us? Loyalty really. While there is mutual hatred between us stemming from months and months of animosity. They are loyal Evolutionist through and through. So while we don’t trust them, we do know they will kick Negro Story and DDLS back to whatever backwater bingo hall carnival tent they came from. Sho’Nuff! But then again, if they cross us, they’ll end up like St.Violence as well. Hail to the King and Queen baby!!! We out! With that BDD and HBC head down to the dancefloor to get their grove on……..
