Faith in Life, and Friends

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct., 24, 2002 'Where is she?' the wondering voice Scott Frost is heard. The scene fades in with Scott Frost sitting outside of very quite motel. He is sitting in a lawn chair set out for the tenates to use during their stay. Frost is smoking a cigarette, and he takes a drag off of the cigarette. The ember glow of the tobacco slightly illuminates his face as he takes his drag and in exhales the smoke smoothly. 'She said she would be here at mid-night. Where the hell is she?' Frost again questions. Frost stands up slowly and looks around, some screaming echo's throughout the sky and he notices a couple outside in a heated arguement and then the woman storms off from her significant other and into a silver vehicle and quickly pulls away. The man looks at his other leave and he looks to be very upset, as he throws his arms in the air. 'What am I supposed to do?! WHAT?!' The man yells as he notices that Frost caught the end of their argument and the man walks back into his motel room slowly shutting his door behind him. Frost takes another drag from his cigarette. 'Seara. This is the second time you've bailed on me. I thought we were supposed to be friends....' Frost speaks to himself as he sits back down in his chair and looks into the sky. 'In mere days, I will have the chance to do what no man has yet to prove he can do. I get the chance to destroy the person the world has come to know as 'The One'. Xander and myself, we enter the ring. Dueling Canes match, his singapore cane against mine.' Frost says. 'I really need a friend to talk to.' Frost says, and then suddenly the all to known and semi-popular sound of a Nokia cell phone is heard and Frost looks around to answer his cell phone. Frost picks it up and he smiles as he see the number calling him, and then he answers it. 'Hey.' Frost says calmly. Frost pauses for a moment. 'It's cool. I thought you were bailing on me again, he need to hang out. I wanna prove to you that I am that friend that you need.' Frost says gently. Frost pauses yet again. 'Alright. Sunday. We will talk then.' Frost says. 'Alright. Talk to you later...' Frost says before seemingly getting interrupted. Frost again pauses. 'Seara....' Frost pauses his voice slightly. 'You can trust me.' Frost smiles and then hangs up his cell phone. Frost looks around at the darkened sky and then he pulls out his wallet, and reveals a picture of his wife Kelsey. She looks at it and runs his right thumb gently over the picture before putting it back into his wallet and smiling. 'Kelsey. I promise you. After Faith, I will be one step closer of ridding the world of Unlimited, and gaining Universal championship status.' Frost smoothly says. The scene fades away... (To Be Continued....)
