Fuhuck YEAH 1

31 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-31 It must have been Sunday, he’d remembered having something big on Sunday. His cigarette hung from his mouth dropping small ashes onto the sheets. He’d looked through his calendar and Sunday had a large circle scribbled around it, he must have forgotten to write the thing down. Everywhere he walked seemed to be a reminder of Sunday. Literally. To avoid the bother he had with Cameron Griffin at the last main event, he decided this time he’d break with tradition and remind himself, so his room had been practically painted with small stickers with SUNDAY written in block capitals. Come on now Terlayne, you have to remember what was THAT important. “Well I came in here… and…. I got a phone call… and….” And… Light bulb. “That’s right, Sunday’s the day I kick the shit into Damon O’Halloran and Sean Sterling.” He leaped up from his bed, the cigarette falling from his mouth. “And he told me I’d better train coz it was the last time I’d ever get a shot at the title.” Down to the gym. The gym was empty; Sunday was the day most took off, including the staff. He had more important things on his mind than the homos who attended the gym and focused his attention on the homos who he was going to have to kick their shit into. So there he was, training hard…. As usual when a hand grabbed his shoulder from behind. He rolled his eyes over and had expected to see another queer but instead saw an old man, must have been in his late seventies. A large scar stretched from his cheekbone to just below his bottom lip. His nose fell to the left and he had very little white hair remaining. “Jesus, you queers come in all shapes and sizes now…. Fuckin’ hell,” he yelled excitedly as he quickly sat up. The old man shrugged and forced a gummy grin as he took another pull of the cigarette, which was clasped, between his fingers. “Nah,” he replied. “I saw you comin’ in and thought you might want to, err… avail of my services.” This comment was met by hard right from Terlayne. Each time the old man opened his mouth he seemed to release a scour, fishy smell. This guy was scum but wasn’t quite as bad as Cameron Griffin. “I thought you looked like you needed my help,” the old man continued. Terlayne shrugged again and continued lifting the weights. “I give you special offer.” “Look Boris I’ve got to kick the shit into two fuckin’ queers tonight, but I can make it three,” Terlayne replied, sighing once again. “Oh, a fighter?” The old man questioned with a glint in his eye. “Well I have just the thing for you,” the old man said reaching for a small leather bag that lay on the gym’s shag carpet. Terlayne reached over and tried to catch a glimpse of the bag’s contents. Nothing. “Now this should save you some time and energy.” The old man put out his hand and dropped a small, white pill into his hand. “Now you take this, and you’ll be strong as an ox.” Terlayne looked down at the pill resting in the palm of his hand and looked back up at the old man, a sly grin slapped across his face. And without further ado, the pill was flushed down with a glass of water.
