Something to gain p1 - worn out

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-22 An old saying goes “What doesn’t kill you only makes you stronger”. Many beg to differ after Wednesday MLX escaped with the title only by the hair on his chinney chin chin. He took one hell of beating at the hands of Hedges who was once again screwed over by a woman who couldn’t hold her own. MLX was left a beaten, bloody, and broken man at the hands of Hedges but yet failed to win the title. He didn’t lose the match but he didn’t win it. MLX is playing a game of cat and mouse and this time the cat has the mouse cornered with his tail caught in a mousetrap. From the Ashes will find one man stepping out with a piece of gold…. One man will continue to show everyone he can’t be burned…and that one man will be Hedges and at his feet will be left three men; bloody, bruised, and confused in the middle of the ring. The scene opens up outside of and old warehouse. It’s run down with huge rows of windows around the side of it, with almost half being broken in some form or the other. Graffiti covers many of the other windows and grass and weeds grow tall from the cracks in the old worn out asphalt. The evening sun is beating down a glowing orange threw the clouds. All down the block the camera spans to shows nothing but old run down buildings and overgrown grass. The street running in front of the buildings is worn with two deeps treads in the asphalt from tire ruts and a gleaming black hummer sits alone in the parking lot. Hedges leans against the front of the building; one leg in front of the other; smoking. His hair shines in the sun and a glare reflects from his sunglasses. He steps forward flicking his cigarette on the ground as the cherry explodes and fades out… “Here we are Malcolm…a building much like yourself. Run down, looks tired and worn out. It’s been threw the weather but stands tall but yet a fragment of it’s former self…” Hedges says with a cocky grin on his face…. Picking up a rock from the ground… “Yet though it stands it is in a weakened state. I could cover it with paint and new windows but inside…the foundation…It’s weak…” Hedges flings a rock and it shatters a window “It’s fairly simply though to break it down. The flick of the wrist and something shatters and breaks. Much like you Malcolm. You will walk down to that ring Sunday a worn out man. Wednesday you pulled the final trick out of your bag. You’ve got nothing left to throw at me. You fear me…I’m one man that’s not just given you a run for your money but for your life and career as well. Though I’ll jump out say it…I’ve got a bit of respect for you. You gave me the first shot at the title here. Gave me the opportunity to jump into contendership when I was just proving myself. Then without Xero’s help you wouldn’t have kept the title. Among many other things you keep managing to slip out of my grasp and escape with the gold. Don’t take this as a weakness though. Respect is something I don’t give out often and it doesn’t mean I’m going to lighten up and have a good clean fight…fuck no…I’m going to do anything and everything to get that gold. It’s in my grasp and I can feel it. I simply have to climb the ladder and grab hold and this time it will keep a hold of it…” Hedges picks and flings another rock at a window and it shatters with a crash. He picks up a few more rocks and throws them at the same time…A few break in quick succession as Hedges walks over to the boarder up door… “Destruction…It’s so amusing…simple…but yet so fun…Remember when you used to be a kid and breaking things and bending the rules was just something you did to have more fun. When I was in the Army breaking rules wasn’t an option but the destruction we got to cause was great. I have never lost that love of being destructive. The sick nature of my mindset causes me to do some thing’s quote “out of the norm” but what the fuck is normal anyways…” Hedges pulls out a pack of camel lights and a Zippo. He flips it open and lights a cigarette up and leans back against the wall smoking and drifting into thought as the scene fades…
