The Path to Redemption...

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-18 Madison Square Garden, New York City. In one of the private dressing rooms deep in the heart of the colossal structure, Susan wraps another strip of tape around the thick wrist of the next eVolution Cell Champion, Damon O'Halloran. She gives his wrist a squeeze, making sure the tape is tight. Balanced on one knee, Susan looks up at the stoic O'Halloran who sits motionless on a hardwood bench. 'How's that?' she asks. O'Halloran clenches his fist, his knuckles cracking. 'Good,' he says simply and extends his other arm. Susan repeats the procedure on his other wrist. 'Pen,' Damon says as Susan completes her work. Susan stands up and walks over to her purse. After a quick search, she returns to the Irishman and hands him the writing utensil. Susan turns away, knowing Damon's pre-match ritual is one he prefers to keep to himself. Still, she knows that Damon writes the name of his number one fan on his wristband before applying the final strip of tape. The name is that of his daughter, Sarah. Rich Doyle, one of Damon's closest friends, slams the heavy metal plates onto the weight bar. He knows how much to put on, how many sets and reps Damon will do to warm his muscles before heading to the ring. No words need to be spoken between the two friends. Doyle knows his job: help prepare the huge Irishman for war. Damon wraps the final strand of tape around his wrist as the roar of the crowd echoes down the hallway beyond the dressing room door. He looks at the door, his skin tingling. There it was. The reason he defied doctor's orders after the beating he took at the hands of QBX and Malcolm King and returned to the ring after the incident. No drug in the world could duplicate the rush Damon felt when the crowd popped, especially when the crowd popped for him. It stoked his ego like nothing else could. Damon knew he was on of wrestling's hottest commodities, the man people throughout the industry talked about. What was even better was that he could cram his credentials down everyone's throat because he backed up everything he said. Love him or hate him; people had no choice but respect what Damon O'Halloran did in the ring. And God help the poor soul who crossed the Coleraine Native. In the small bathroom, Vic looks at the sculpted physique in front of him, his bodyfat had dropped dramatically, lean muscle mass increased, and jacked his power to the moon. The result was that Damon felt he was far more than any man could handle. Especially if that man pissed him off. Poor Malcolm. King, Damon thinks to himself. His arrogance had barely registered on Damon's radar. But when he dared to put his hands on his genetic superior... the thought brought Damon's blood to a slow boil. His hands tighten around the edge of the sink, his teeth gritting. Bruises cover his body as he looks at the scars left from that match on Wednesday. He smiled as a small trickle of blood began to run from Damon's nose. Tonight Malcolm would learn the ugly truth: that for all his previous title victories, for all his accomplishments, he was only human. Tonight, he would be stepping into the ring with the next step in human evolution. Tonight, he would be made an example of. And there was only two things he could do about it... Nothing and like it.
