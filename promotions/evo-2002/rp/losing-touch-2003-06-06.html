Losing Touch

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-06 “Aaaahhhhhhh!” screams Siege as he stands aloft a rooftop in the dark rainy night. The rain pours down as he looks towards the heavens screaming in agony, a kind which is not seen only felt through his mind. The cool spring rain provides a semi-soothing feeling as it runs down his body. The water drips down his bare chest providing a layer of soaking water. His long blonde hair is drenched and glued down onto his head. Small strands bead together and form small slightly curled locks. Although beaded and naturally braided his face still is hidden from everything. Panning over the city he watches, containing his anger, letting his hatred build until he can have a release. Lightning flashes and brightens the sky and Siege turns away from the ledge. Stepping down he flips his head back and his bangs spread to the back in a single swift motion. His blue eyes are ice cold as they pierce the very hearts of men. Not many have withstood the look which he now expresses. So much anger, hatred, and vengeance poured into one glare. A painful stare that has often befallen Siege’s enemies, and has become a final mark which remains etched in their minds. Everlasting is this look and those who gaze at it, having it, and pulling it from his very eyes, know the feelings Siege endures. Siege closes his eyes slowly and turns his head down toward the ground. With his eyes closed he turns to the sky letting the soothing rain trickle down his face. His eyes open and he stares in to endless cloudy night. “Why?” He asks. “Why did I have to get involved?” Siege looks straight ahead reflecting his thoughts and actions. “S., you made me do something that I regret. You made me give someone a part of my pain that didn’t deserve to feel it. Who gave you that right? The right to ravage others, the right to take, and the right to get away with it; all these are reasons I fooled you. No man should be given so much power.” Siege says as he turns toward the roof entrance. He pushes the old wooden door. It wails and cracks in pain as he passes by holding it open. Thunder and lightning roar and rage behind him and his footsteps echo down the hall. His boots chime the metal steps and if there where anyone in the building they could feel his presence just by the power in his walk. “I don’t know what your future holds S., but pray our paths do not cross. You told me that you could have saved and protected my brother. Thou shalt not dwell on my past, my losses, only on what is in store for thyself.” Siege stops rubs his hair back. Water drains and splashes about the floor as excess runs down his back. He shakes his head back and forth loosening it up. The small beaded locks return and his hair once again eclipses his face. “Do not aspire toward that which you can not reach upon your own two feet. ‘Tis folly, and you’re a fool to desire that which you can not posses and control.” Siege turns and heads down the hall.
