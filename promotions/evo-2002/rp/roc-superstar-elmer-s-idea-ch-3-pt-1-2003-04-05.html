roC Superstar: Elmer's Idea (Ch.3 Pt.1)

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.05.03 ~When we left Shocka was in desperate need of ideas on how to get thug roC back for the restraining order that roC had placed on him. He was beckoned to a diner where he met a midget who said that he had an idea on how Shocka could get thug roC. Shocka was in dire need of an idea so he decided to listen.~ ‘Well first I thought that I could…die my hair blue like yours…die my goatee and pretty much dress like you every night…’ Elmer starts. ‘Kind of like mini Sutton…’ Elmer says. ‘Don’t you dare bring up that little bastard he caused me hell for the better part of 4 months…’ Shocka says seemingly setting off a light bulb. ‘Holy shit!’ Shocka yells to himself. ‘Imagine if that was your enemies clone a mini Shocka would be absolutely amazing against roC you could cause all kinds of hell for him! With the help of me of course!’ Elmer says with a large smile on his face. ‘You are brilliant!’ Shocka says starting off. ‘The thing is everything comes with a price…’ Elmer says. ‘You think that I give a shit about money?’ Shocka questions the little man. ‘Well not really since you are a star! And that also reminds me you have to make me a star too…I’ve always wanted to be on TV and I figure this is probably my only shot…’ Elmer says with a serious tone. ‘Mini Shocka eh?’ Shocka says with a interested tone.. ‘Alright man I am down…but we got some work to do with you…’ Shocka says trailing off. ‘Really?!’ Elmer screams ecstatic. ‘Yeah man I think it will work out great!’ Shocka says as the two get up. ‘So what are we gonna do now?’ Elmer asks. ‘Well imma go to my hotel and get some sleep…’ ‘But what about me?’ Elmer asks. ‘Go home rest…I got your number.’ Shocka says kind of tired like. ‘I don’t have one…’ Elmer says. ‘Well…I guess you could come with me…you could sleep on the chair or the floor in my room…’ Shocka says showing some compassion. ‘Alright sweet man!’ Elmer screams happily. ‘This is going to be…grand.’ Shocka says in a very bland tone. ~Shocka and Elmer load up in Shocka’s car and take off as the scene now fades to darkness.~
