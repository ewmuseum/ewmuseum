Date: 15-10-02 The scene opens high up in the French alps. Seemingly the last shred of civilisation before an endless sprawl of snow and rocks. Amidst the white is enough flat land for a helicopter to have come to rest on the edge of a mountain town. Approaching the chopper are three men all wrapped up in warm and waterproof. One is slumped under a large hiking bag, another cradling a camera and the third man carrying a snowboard under his arm. The man with the bag takes to the drivers seat and the other two jump in the back and leave the doors open. They take to the air throwing up a cloud of snow from the floor. The camera man looks across the chopper to the snowboarder who seems totally switched off. Simply staring through the doorway as they leave civilisation behind. Cameraman- You look tense. Don’t tell me we’ve come this far and you’ve got cold feet. The snowboarder lifts the goggles away from his eyes and pulls his beanie off of his head. L2J’s long blue hair is violently blown around by the wind from the doorway. He doesn’t respond to cameraman. Just looks back at the rapidly disappearing village he has been hiding in for the past 2 weeks. And begins to wonder why he has left. Why he doesn’t just remain anonymous. His head slowly turns to take in the beautiful early morning scenery. He switches off again. Dead to the world. But he cant help thinking about what he has to go back to. The fine line between enemies and friends gets ever thinner as every day goes by. There are simply to many angles for L2J to cover. It may seem fate for L2J to never take the ultimate gold. He has fought impossible odds his entire career. Everything he does seems to be physically unachievable. But maybe that’s what appeals to superstars and fans alike. It is what has brought L2J to where he is today. He has come so far, he has defied and overcome. But this is the end. Faith will be the end. The end of L2Js desire? To finally, after 4 long years of torment, disappointment, injury after injury, could he finally claim the ultimate prize and rise above the rest. 4 years ago L2J’s own trainer Khris Kouger told him to never set a goal so high. That a mere 208lbs cruiserweight could never achieve such a feat. But after all that time, all the screwjobs, all the sneak attacks the opportunity has presented itself. An opportunity that L2J has fought hard for and earned. 4 long years brought to one final moment at faith. Three seconds. One, two, three. L2J stares into the endless nothing that can be seen out of the helicopter. The white snow only interrupted by occasional rocks and cliffs. Could it be done. Could L2J get the better of inhuman monster that has destroyed him so many times in the past. And violent beast that wrote the book called “the injured list”. A Behemoth that takes this game beyond business and pleasure. To a level beyond sanity. A man that has only one thing between him and another month of glory at the top of one of the greatest federations in the world. A fearless 208lb superstar with a deathwish. Could this superstar break the mould. Could he be the difference maker. The one that can finally end this human monsters reign of terror?....No. could this superstar be something else? Could this superstar bring the game up to his level? Could this superstar tip the balance and be the won to take the title from a mentally damaged schizophrenic who has a family back home? Cameraman-We’re here L2J snaps back into reality and grabs his goggles and beanie Cameraman- you ready to go? L2J nods. But out of the doorway there is still nothing. Nothing but a 35ft drop to the same endless white that has been seen for miles. L2J throws his snowboard to the floor and snaps his right boot into the front foot binder. He then shuffles over the doorway and clips in his back foot. Cameraman- God damn. You’re a braver man than me. L2J again doesn’t respond but this time for quite different reasons. Because this time he has a very heavy dose of reality as he grabs the handles on either side of the doorway and drops his feet onto the leg of the chopper. Cameraman-best of luck to ya kid. We’ll be seein whats left of you at the bottom. L2J may aswell have been alone in that chopper. He may aswell have just been floating. All he was thinking about was what is below him. The camera starts rolling. The chopper steadies. And L2J lets himself go. For that brief moment he is free. One…Two…Three. The ploom of snow upon contact is quickly left behind by L2J as he tears the mountain in half. He leaves the sound of the chopper behind as it nose dips and motors roar to try and keep up. Cameraman- Jeeesus this kid can sure as hell fly. Where you find this kid? The camera man watches the snow fly from the tiny spec on the landscape as L2J goes terminal. The scene fades.
