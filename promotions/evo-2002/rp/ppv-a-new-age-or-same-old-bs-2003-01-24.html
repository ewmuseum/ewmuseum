PPV a new age or same old BS?

24 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/24/03 'Hey Luther. I've been getting kinda bored over the past couple of weeks of what I'm getting so I've decided as apart of my having fun, and being dangerous.. we have what I call, Angel's Sadistic Showdown, or ASS. This match won't just be a single falls match, but a 3 way combo of matches. First to get two out of three falls wins. The first match being a Table Match, first to impale their opponent through the tables is victorious. The second match will be a Finisher First match. Then the third match, if there is one, will be a Heaven to Hell match. Your normal steel cage has been taken away and a steel octagon will be placed down. The only way to win this match is if you knock your opponent unconscious or make them submit. All of the matches will be No Disqualification. Come tame these broken wings... if you're man enough.' Luther Wisdom rewatches the challenge. Slightly amused slightly ticked off. He takes a look at his stock...it's trading at $11.57 a share, slightly up but not to the level he feels it should be. 'Angel...Angel....Angel....' He closes a portfolio that is resting on his desk.... 'Why is it individuals in this profession feel the need to issue mindless and somewhat admittedly inane challenges and expect the quote unquote challengee to accept it? And really the slight on the manhood in all reality was a very nice....if not predictable touch.' looks over a printout of the proposed challenge by Angel..... 'So let's see Angel I'm supposed to give a damn about YOUR boredom? I'm supposed to care that you chose to be Xander's and The Unlimited's pet and because Xander refuses to let you out to play you feel the need to piss in the yard of a dog bigger than yourself only to get a reaction? I mean take a look at this for a minute.....' reads the proposal..... 'a table match.....a finisher first match.....and a heaven to hell match...please Angel give me a break here. First off the table matches have been done to death as well as the gimmicky cage matches with the name change so I can protect the copyright for future usage. Although the finisher first match has yet to be done in Evolution and one I'm intrigued in doing it brings me to my next point...why?' takes a sip...... 'Why Angel would I face you on your terms? Tell me who in the hell are you to dictate the match I will have....you are nothing more than an Unlimited lackey that lucked onto an important angle. So I guess what I'm trying to say is what is in it for me?' closes the portfolio..... 'First and foremost I'm after the Tag Team Championship and in all honesty facing you in three matches is just not good for business. If it was Xander...then maybe I'd consider it, but you....please. However I won't deprive the audience of the Luther Wisdom Experience...so Angel you will get one of your matches.....and it'll be The Finisher First match.' rests back in his chair...... 'In all honesty Angel.....you are not a big enough contributer to Evolution to demand or dictate any match with me, much less a two out of three match PPV encounter. Seriously Angel if you could put Asses in the seats and by that I don't mean Angel's Sadistic Showdown; then I would consider it. But honestly the risk versus reward ratio is one that I really don't feel like doing. Or in terms you can understand....a three match 'feud' will only get one of us over, which based on the stipulations would be you.....so I ask you again Angel; what's in it for me? Answer that question to my satisfaction then maybe.....just maybe I'll entertain what you proposed. Becareful what you wish for...be mindfull in what you say, once I'm in your life not even death can erase the damage I will cause.' ELSEWHERE...... Malcolm King does a series of pull ups. With each and every pull up his hatred fro The One and Xander continues to grow and grow..... 'Same old story...next chapter....' Malcolm says without missing a beat... 'So again I find myself in a match to find out to determine once and for all Who is The One? First in EDW...then at the beginning of my Evo run and now it is 2003 and yet I find myself in the same situation.' Malcolm stops..... 'Only this time there will be a resolution, only this time my focus is on ending once and for all the shadow cast by the Unlimited. It's not about deception, it's not about the body of my ex-partner, nor is it about the returning Christoph. This is about restoring a legacy and ending a chapter.' Malcolm begins to kickbox a punching bag...... 'You have decided to play mindgames with the master Xander. Time and time again I have shown you that no matter how bad you can be.....I'm ten times worse. So surround yourself with an army...bring back Christoph, hell recruit Mary Poppins for all I care. I'm back for one reason and one reason only and at the PPV it begins.' he stops and takes a sip of Gatorade... 'The Unlimited by playing with me, by toying with my career have been placed on The endangered species list. I spent my return trying to recruit and Army....to make them mentally tough for the war ahead. But when it comes down to it....this is my fight and my fight alone. So Unlimited now instead of me stockpiling for warfare......I plan to strike at each of you one by one. You've tried to stop me in the past...and failed. I've taken your best shot and survived, so now it's time for me to fire back. Know thy enemy....which I do, strike from all sides....which I will....and never let them see you coming, Unlimited.....you don't know what I've already done within your organization and who I have working for me already. You'll find out when it's time......Xander and One, look around you and see who you can and can't trust; then look again.....and make sure the man sitting next to you, isn't an agent of Malcolm King.'
