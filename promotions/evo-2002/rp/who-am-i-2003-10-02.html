Who am I?

2 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-02 The storm has tempered down a bit, but the rain is still falling like a smaller of the great falls. A day or so has pased since Outblaze had sent in his first recorded promo for eVolution. Since, he's been merely roaming the streets. Watching the people of the small town wander around aimlessly. Off in the distance there is a small convience store when a couple are fighting out in the parking lot. The woman appears drunk or on something and the guy seems to be hiding something. Outblaze has been watching for about ten minutes or so. He cant really put his finger on it, but there is just something about observing society at its ugliest of times that makes Outblaze feel somewhat better about himself and his past. It's a general misconception that people wish each other to be happy. Natural laws states that a person can not be happy without knowing there is somebody doing worse than they. Its very rare for somebody to truely be happy doing nothing more than helping others. Even some who think they are happy, they are not. They go and do their good deeds and help the weak. But they do this in hope of redemption. Forgiveness for their own flaws and mistakes in life. Outblaze turns away to shield the unlit cigarette from the wind. A match flickers in the bit of wind and lights the tip. He waves out the match and tosses it on the ground. As he takes the first drag he starts to think to himself. 'How many people saw my promo and couldnt figure out who I was? How many people really care who I am? They all should. But then again, ignorance is bliss and people are about as ignorant as anything can get. Then again, I am finally just figuring out who I am, myself. Can I really expect others to know already? No, I cant.' Outblaze walks down the unlevel zig-zag of a sidewalk and turns down a dark alleyway. The walls of the surrounding building are covered in techno looking neon graffiti. He stops and studies what looks to be a giant question mark. 'Hmm...Who am I?' Outblaze continues walking. 'Who am I? Simply put, I am the future. I am a legend in the making. Soon the image of my face will be forever etched in the minds of everyone. My name will flow from their lips and my voice will resonate within their minds. I am a saint amongst sinners. A principle to adhere by. And an example to compare by. I am the new shit. The up and coming. A newcomer soon to be superior. I am Donovan Avarus-Rey Kilner. Outblaze. The d.a.r.k one. I am the fire big enough to be seen from the stars. I am everything within nothing. Like the air in an empty room, I am unavoidable, and my rise is inevitable.' Outblaze takes a last drag and tosses the cigarette into a pudle as he steps through. He stops for a moment and looks into the sky of the dusk. He turns to the camera, looks dead into the lense... 'For those of you wondering who I am? Just remeber this. Keep it well. Fore it wont belong before I am above you. Towering like a clocktower. I am coming and I am here. I just am.' Outblaze turns a corner and disappears into a thick cloud of rolling steam rising from the grates within the city streets. As the camera fades, a voice can be heard whispering... 'Remember me. Fear me.'
