Like A Canyon

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What had she been doing? Where had she been? Was she still alive? The answer to the last question was a positive yes. However, it might not be fully compliable in the entire sense of the word. For the past two weeks, she had covered a rectangular area of the Pacific Coast. Her search had begun in the Pacific Northwest, and started to follow her border. Occasionally she'd dip into the inner portion of her outlined path, but then she'd veer out again. All the while making sure she hit various key points. Her search came to a halt in the lower portion of California, near her hometown of Garden Grove. It halted, because she decided to stay with her parents for a couple nights. She had become ragged and fatigued throughout her constant travel, and a peaceful few days rest was welcomed. Her job became an issue, as Daniel Somers didn't want her missing too much time. He felt that she might be able to make it as a mid-carder, and that couldn't happen if she was constantly taking time off. This was alright with her, for now. She had her own agenda for being in eVolution, and it was one she wasn't altering for anyone. Well, maybe one person. And now, she had decided it was time to resume her wrestling career. Her hiatus was a simple one, not worth noting really. And now it was time to begin again. Time to signify that she was someone worth testing. Perhaps it was time to really break out. She'd been holding back for a while now. If she got too good, she'd get too busy. It'd ruin everything. Now, it was time to really try and shine. Her mind was a battlefield, and everything that crossed it was obliterated. She wasn't prepared to let years of waiting and planning go to waste. She had one goal in mind. Destruction. Of her very own saviour.
