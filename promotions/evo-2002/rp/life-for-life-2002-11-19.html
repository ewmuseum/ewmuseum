Life for Life...

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life for Life… Part One! The scene opens with a man walking his head hung low, his face not visible but the man wanders aimlessly without care in the world or thinking to look up and see what was in front of him. The man looks like he could be lost at mind, body and soul... Like the man has no thought of what was around him this very minute. The man keeping his face not visible, he's larger then the usual man. This figure strides quickly like he has somewhere to be but nowhere at the same time. The man snaps his head up and without the same evil and strange demonic grin but the blankest of expressions ever thought to be seen on his face. The man is the Infinite of evil himself, The God of Sleep and Dreams, Morpheus Omega. The scene surrounding are the back end streets of a neighborhood not journeyed by the average man of population. The darkness of the cold night thickens around as well. Crack dealers and addicts disappearing and reappearing from the shadows. Hookers lined at the end of the street like they are being displayed like steaks in the butcher shop window. The ladies ready to be used and abused like the cows themselves before being slaughtered to their deaths at the hands of man... Morpheus cares nothing of what's surrounding him. This man has never appeared this way before and something is definitely wrong with him like he's lost all sick and twisted hope that was once where he left off, like the man has lost the lustful hunger for evil, and the hatred that once drove himself over the edge and back time and time again. It seems like Morpheus has finally met his own demise. You know what they say... What goes around comes around and this time finally it might have all caught up with once who used to be the deadliest athlete in the game. Does this man still have the worthiness of the named titles he has received after slaughtering victim after victim, and innocent bi-standard after bi-standard? Morpheus fumbles through his pockets trying to find something in his three quarter length black leather jacket. Morpheus pulls a pack of players light regulars from his pocket and pulls out a cigarette from inside, he returns the pack of smokes where they came from and pulls out a lighter and it flicks with an immense glowing flame. The cigarette heater flashes bright as he pulls hard from the filter inhaling a large amount of smoke and exhaling. Morpheus continues walking down the street. Three men appear from the shadows black as night and dressed all gangsta and thuged out. The smallest man getting up in his face, or tries because the man stands a foot shy of Morpheus. The guys friends stand beside, one about the same height but stocky and built like a roid raged pit bull. The other stands close to Omega's height but probably weighs about half the amount total. The man stares up into the eyes of Morpheus with no fear or worries. The man speaks without hesitation... 'You look like your lost cracka. You mind telling me what a big stupid snow flake, mother fucker like you would be doing all up in the ghettos of my hood. I should cap your ass just for walking my streets little lone not bowing down before me in my presence. Bitch you will be lucky if you...' Morpheus cuts the man off and quickly grabs him by the throat and squeezes. The thug waves for his men to stand down. Morpheus speaks not like you would think in his sadistic, insane tone but one of a broken man. One that a man would speak when he knew nothing was left for him. Like it was the coming of his bitter end. 'I think you have me mistaken but your lives and fate rest in my hands, Its all matter of are you worthy of dying at the cause greater then you would ever know. One that you are to blind and ignorant to realize before its to late' Morpheus cracks his neck to the one side and then to the other, he breathes deeply. The man is now gasping for air. The two men from beside him at the quickness of the draw have two guns to the side of his head. The one a silver magnum with extended barrel and the other an Oozy black and stealth like. The man built like the pit with the magnum screams into the face of Morpheus. 'Let him go dawg. Your already dead, why don't you lie down and take your bullet like a bitch and die. Maybe if you cooperate we will let you choose where you take your chunk of lead' The man laughs all sick and the other man gets all hyped up. Morpheus calmly takes a long drag off his cigarette and awkwardly gets a unique smile on his face. The man who's tall with the Oozy in his hands grips the gun tighter and presses the cold metal end of the gun further into the side of Omegas head near his temple. The man screams. 'You don't know who you are fucking with. All you have to realize Cracka, Dude, what ever the fuck they call your kind, that we own you, when you step foot on these streets... Too bad white boy but you have to die!' Morpheus takes another long haul of the smoke and his facial expression still not changing. Clicking noises can be heard from both guns distinictly. Morpheus flicks his nearly finished cigarette butt to the side in the face of the man holding the Oozy and spins his body letting go of the first mans throat and a gun shot can be heard firing from the magnum ear deafening loud. The scene slows down into suspended animation while Morpheus ducking and turning at the same time. The bullets can be heard ripping from the end of the Oozy into the air, flames can be seen shooting from the end of the gun that is pointed straight to the sky. The gunshot with an echo through the streets. The sound of the empty bullet shells ting off the concrete below. The man with the Oozy slowly drops with his body turning. The man hits the cold street with an impact. Blood flows from his throat with a small amount of smoke billowing from the fresh gun shot wound. The scene returns to normal frame time recording... Morpheus thrusts back up and elbows the man with the magnum in the face and throws his fist upwards and back fist smashes him in the mouth. The man bleeding drops to the ground with force, his head cracking off the curb below. Morpheus lunges down and grabs the Oozy before its to late but the first thug had already been thinking ahead and had a gun in his hand pulled from places unknown. Both men hold the guns aimed steadily at each others faces. Morpheus is the first to respond speaking. 'Institutionalized little thugs who think they can hustle anyone they see. Never thinking the possibility one of your boys would end up dead on the street because of your own Dawg? Your man was just shot because you stupid fucks don't know when to give the fuck up. You pull that trigger because I can live a lodged bullet in my skull or torn through my body. Your so cracked out, You would probably die of blood loss from a bullet scraping your ear son. This is your end of eternity, you have all signed the death wish that will drag you into the sacrilegious ruins of hell, you can build your own fate like a make believe story but you build around the structure that has been left for or given to you. Hell son, This is my Reality!' Morpheus nods, the sick look on his face slowly regaining at times and fading away again. Morpheus mostly does this with no emotion at all. Like he's got nothing to lose if he had to hide and dispose of these worthless trash punks bodies in a dumpster, he would have no problem and do so with no remorse or guilt. The thug speaks all of a sudden with heart, like the cold hearted gangsta found heart after seeing his boy capped off in the face from another one of his best friends. The thug speaks with careful words. 'This is all your inbred fault, what should prevent me from pulling this trigger, Why should I spare someone like you who has just taken from another. You ever heard the expression Life for Life? Its time you found out about what goes around comes around and Life for Life and what the fuck I mean by this. You are owned, stamped and branded and there is absolutely nothing you can do about it. Little bitches trying to walk all the hell over me, Shit' Both men launch forward and have the guns pointed in each others faces barely from touching each others skin. centimeters apart and nothing stopping these man but the matter and molecules of air itself. The scene fades dark and two gun shots can be heard with a loud bang. The scene ends with the mystery eluding itself... To be continued...
