Hidden Action part 2

2 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-02 The scene opens in a hotel room in New Jersey, Seara sits with Ali Khadafi to one side of her and another figure, still hooded to her left, so far he has not been present with Seara in the ring or arena, and the only people who know the masked males name are the others in the room. On the table in front of the three is an old picture of the female wearing her wrestling attire and the eVolved title, behind her the camera can pick up the image of Scott Frost wearing the Universal title. At present, Scott Frost is in the bedroom sleeping, the door shut he does not know there are other wrestlers in the lounge area with Seara. “You didn’t come here to look at that” the female snaps, removing the picture from the hands of the masked wrestler. She rubs her temples and leans on her knees looking forward. Placing the picture down she stares at it, and focusing in looks at the reflections of the two men in it. “Scott back there?” asks Khadafi. He looks at the female who nods and looks back to the doorway in the right hand corner of the room, a small smile appeared on her face. And just as quickly disappeared. “Look, we aren’t here to talk about Scott, I wouldn’t have called you to my room, if it weren’t for something I heard you saying last night…. Seriously guys, if you are going to try and talk about me in private, do not do it when we are all training together, it is not very private anywhere outside your own rooms…or minds for that matter.” There was a long silence and Seara light a cigarette, she knew Scott had grown accustomed to her getting out of bed at night and sometimes sitting all night thinking about her next move or what she could have done to fix things earlier, she also knew he was aware of the smell of her cigarette smoke when she would sit up at night. “Tomorrow night is pretty goddamn important to most of the people of eVolution, and although you think it would be a good idea to create a great deal of havoc, I have something more planned for another night, tomorrow, I have a pretty important fight of my own, I will face Xander once again, this time for a different belt, the Universal title, something so important it seems to Xander, and although I have waited for a shot for that belt, it does not mean as much to me as it will for me to get in that ring and annihilate that piece of shit. Since my first day in eVo he has done nothing but torment me and get in my way, and why, because that little idiot thinks he’s better than everyone, better than me, and yes, he is better than some around here, but I will once again prove myself against him tomorrow night, and you boys will do one thing for me, you will stay away from that ring. I know Khadafi that you have a taste for Xander now, and I must admit, you did well, you proved yourself in his locker room. I could not have asked for anything more from someone as new around here as you. As for you, I still have to see what you can do for me, but I know your history around eVo, don’t think I haven’t watched you since day 1.I’ve been planning this for a very long time, and you, I’ve had my eye on you since you came here all those months ago, and I know creating havoc is your skill and your taster around eVo, but you will not make a move until I say that you can strike out, and when I am ready for you to strike, it will be a catastrophe beyond your limitless imaginations. “Last night I overheard you talking about jumping to my rescue if anything in that ring went wrong, but I assure you, no matter what happens, if I come out of there on stretcher or if I walk out the winner, no matter what happens, I will look after myself. I know we agreed that we would all look out for each other in the ring, that this was part of the deal of us working together for better something we all want, but not tomorrow night, tomorrow night I am asking…no, not asking, telling you, you will not come into that ring to help me, you will only come to the ramp, any further, and I will be forced to remind you why I am in charge here. I may not look like anything special, I may not look like I can handle myself in a fight or in that ring, but you off all people know what I am capable of, you have seen me around eVo, you have seen me fight and win. I have never faced a female since the day I last fought HBC, and I am still fighting, so do not think that just because I am up against Xander, a male, that I cannot take care of myself. “One more thing before I kick you guys out, tomorrow night at eVo1, if either of you see Greatness around and I am not with you, do not hesitate to hurt him, I have no objections to that little rookie bastard being destroyed, tear him limb from limb if you feel the need. However, if you feel you must find me to let me witness his demise from wrestling, then come and find me, you know where I will be. Now scatter and remember what I told you. Only Greatness if you feel the need, and stay away from the ring while I’m in it.” Seara watches as Ali Khadafi leaves and walks away down the hallway towards the elevator, grabbing the arm of the other male she looks at him and sighs deeply. “If for some reason you need to see me tomorrow night, come to, but show your face and there will be hell to pay, eVo must feel your fists before they know who you are, it will be a greater shock for them to find you working with me.” The male steps out the room and Seara slams the door behind him and turns to see Frost standing in the doorway. The scene fades out as the female slowly walks toward the male with a smile on her face.
