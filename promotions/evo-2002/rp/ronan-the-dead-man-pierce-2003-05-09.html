Ronan 'The dead man' Pierce

9 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.09.03 ~His eyes were focused, determined, studying something in the distance, it brought him to sort of a squint. His nose and mouth quickly came into view as a disturbed look was left on his face as he sat in a dark room just thinking. His eyes darted back and forth for a minute as he spoke in short sporadic motions.~ ‘Ronan Pierce…you think that you can get involved in my business? Just because you knew that I had the match with Christoph in the bag and you didn’t want the NTICW to look bad because of his lack of ability inside of the ring…Now Ronan since you came into my match and decided to try and take me out I am going to show you next week in the ring that I am not a fun man to mess with…and if you don’t believe me you can talk to your buddy Erin about that fact.’ Shocka said with a smile as the cameraman moved back for a minute. ‘Ronan you may out looking for glory or something like that…maybe a quick rise to the top in eVo. So let me get this straight you are standing in the mirror talking to yourself trying to get pumped up and you think…hmm lets mess with a man that weighs 270 pounds in a six foot 2 inch frame…what kind of concept is that…I’m a former Universal, Continental and the only road to glory champion here in eVolution…my path of domination is well documented all you have to do is look back at a Universal champion and chances are that I’ve pinned them…and then I am scanning again through fact sheets and you know what I come across? Your birthday I mean I’m surprised you are still walking and since I missed your birthday which was April 26th I’d like to wish you a happy birthday, and then after Legends I’ll through you a retirement party at 44 years of age buddy.’ Shocka says with a smile. ‘So not only did you want to mess with a man that was forty pounds heavier a former champion but also someone that is 18 years younger than you! I think that your brain has simply dissolved to something about the size of a peanut and you no longer understand what you are doing when you come down to the ring…’ Shocka says with a bit of a laugh. ‘I mean you can’t take yourself seriously when you step into the ring with a young talent like me can you?’ He asks with a look of disbelief on his face. ‘Oh well this week I can get my revenge on both you and Erin with the help of the Hype James Kimson in a match that will prove to be eVo vs. NTICW supremacy and when me and Kimson pull one for the home team we’ll make damn sure that you two won’t walk from the arena.’ ~The camera slowly backs again as the scene now fades on Shocka gazing out a window that rain drops beat down onto.~
