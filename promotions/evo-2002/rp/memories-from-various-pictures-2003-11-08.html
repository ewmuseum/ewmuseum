Memories from various pictures.

8 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-08 Samantha's asleep and I'm reviewing my life by looking through snapshots in a family album. This is something I'd never do while Samantha is awake. If she saw this side of me, she might see the real me and that's something I really don't feel like dealing with. You see, I've got this philosophy that I should never let anyone see the 'real' me. It's like a masquerade ball. I wear a mask and I can be anyone I want to be. To her? I'm the God of Fuck. The real me, however, is nothing more than a frail little boy in an adults body. Scared and timid and unsure of anything. That's the difference between myself and Sean Sterling. Flip a page and see my family's history drawn out for me in little pictures. Pictures from my father and mother's wedding, back before I was even a thought. Pictures of family reunions and cookouts that happened before I was born. My first birthday. My first Christmas. There I am, see? There I am under the Christmas tree crawling on my hands and knees like an animal. Like a stray puppy brought in from the cold. I used to be so cute back then. What the hell happened? Heh. Old faded pictures of me and my sisters on the first day of school. These are all memories. Forgotten times long before now. There I am in a ring for the first time, posing with some wrestler who no one knows now. I hate that picture the most. It's reminds me of the job I'm doing, this whole violent fighting sin thing. I hate that picture and I'd like nothing more than to burn it. But you really can't get rid of memories, so why bother? Burning it would only create a new memory of it. So fuck it. Samantha's awake.
