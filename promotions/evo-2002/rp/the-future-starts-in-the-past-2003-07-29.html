The Future Starts In The Past

29 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-29 “FUCK, ITS ABOUT MORE THAN JUST THAT. IF THAT WAS MY ONLY REASON FOR BEING PISSED OFF AT YOU, I WOULD HAVE FORGIVEN YOU SOONER.” The raised voice of Seara can be heard in the hallways of the large London hotel but the female is not in the hallways, rather she stands above Scott who sits on a sofa in their room, he looks down at the floor and seems to cringe away from the screaming Seara as though he is somewhat scared of her. She paces back and forth and cannot take her eyes from the male below her. “SCOTT” the female begins to shout again but stops when she sees the look on Frosts face and she knows he truly is sorry, her voice lowers and she sits down on the floor in front of him, her legs crossed and she puts one hand on his leg and lifts his head with her other hand so he is looking at her. “Scott, I am mad that you never told me you were going to pose as Greatness, but that is the least of my worries, you broke my trust, that is what pissed me off. My god, you knowingly attacked me.” “You were the first to step up and attack Katherine.” Remarks Scott Frost; it seems however he has said the wrong thing and the female is quick to pull away from him as she stares blankly horrified at her fiancée. “I…I…I can’t believe those words just came from your mouth, do you think I would have attacked if I knew it was you behind that mask, honestly Scott do you? “ “I don’t know Katherine” Pausing Scott looks at the female and slides down off the sofa to join her on the floor, he looks to be pondering a question, but puts off saying it for a while, before he finally breaks the silence. “Katherine, do, do, do you love me still?” “Scott you idiot, I would give up everything to be with you for the rest of my life, I would give up wrestling if that’s what it took, I hope it would never have to come to that, but I would honestly do anything to be with you forever. I have never loved anyone or anything the way I love you and that will never change” Frost smiles at the reply of the female to his question and begins to apologise the best way he can for what he did, eventually he leans forward and kisses the female deeply and passionately and she kisses back with all her love. “Anyway Katherine, lets talk about the future, because you are not quitting this game, not for me, not today anyway, we have work to do around eVo. I am eVolutions king and you my queen, this is a new era for Frost after all, and you are soon to be a Frost. And I don’t want us to just be a team here, this is important to me and I want us to once again rule eVo as a team. No longer will I sit back and watch you being hurt by the men in this roster, you are mine to protect and that is what I will do, but I will not just sit around and watch you waiting to come to your aid, I want us together in all we do” Scott smiles and looks deeply into Seara’s eyes, something which he has not done for a while now, not since he came back into eVolution posing as the rookie and enemy of the females Greatness. The scene fades out as the pair begins to plan their future of domination within eVolution.
