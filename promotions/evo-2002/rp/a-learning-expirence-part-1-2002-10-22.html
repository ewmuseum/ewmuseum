A learning expirence (Part 1)

22 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.22.02 Flashback ‘Ha ha! Look at Johnny!’ The scream of a boy can be heard. ‘Oh my god Johnny no! Stop it Johnny!’ The boys scream echoes throughout a wooded area that surrounds them. A young boy stands about 10 feet away from the one screaming, blood covers his shirt. More blood flows from his nose he stares straight ahead at one boy with rage in his eyes as he storms one of them. He begins pummeling him with clubbing fists. ‘Someone help me I think he snapped!’ The boy being attacked is able to scream before he hits the ground with a hard thud. Kids ranging in ages from what looks to be about 8-12 flood the scene and take “Johnny” down with punches and kicks. Out of all the screaming and thuds you can hear him crying loudly as they continue to punish him. ~~The scene fades to Shocka lying in a chair in a hotel room with his feet propped up on a stool. An eVolution camera is focused in on him as he sits with a sort of angry look on his face as he looks at the camera.~~ ‘Now this is a rare occurrence me, talking to an eVolution camera and now is my chance to spill my guts and maybe give you a little insight to how I think.’ He speaks quickly and to the point. ‘Now when I was a child I was beaten almost to death 4 times by my peers because they thought I was weird and that is the simple end of it. The first time it happened I was only 6 years old and they had taken me into a wooded area and broken my nose. My shirt was covered in blood but something inside of me snapped and I attacked one boy breaking his arm before 10 or so of them brought me back and did some more damage breaking a rib. They thought that they could finish me like that…’ He speaks with pain in his voice. ‘My mom took me to the hospital and they discovered I had a broken rib nose and a fractured arm. They gave me some mental evaluations and said that a physiatrist should take me in…but because of my moms income we couldn’t afford anything of the sort. The second time the boys beat me up was about 6 months later but this time I was ready I had my pocketknife and jabbed it into the lungs of one of them leaving them near death. But I didn’t stop there I stomped his face and beat him with a tree branch before one of them brought back some help.’ He shakes his head in slight disappointment. ‘It was a horrible scene…he bled to death on site but I didn’t’ suffer the consequences as the juvenile courts believe in rehabilitation. I was to smart for them…my “rage” disappeared after a year of treatment.’ ///Scene Fade\\\
