Cold Emotion

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Where do you define perfection and normality? Or does it really have existence? Its this made up image within your mind of what you dream and want for yourself. So this perfection you feel, everyone else around you feels greatly different and really only exists within yourself. Within limits of imagination. When one does not meet up to their own standards and strive to be the better or the best they find one of many escapes to life. Some choose to drink their problems away, others with narcotics from something as minor as smoking a joint to another stabbing heroine filled needles into their veins. Cigarettes and sex are temporary escapes to find their inner peace as it disappears like smoke into the air. With all the possible escapes, narcotics or alcohol abuse to various sports and hobbies. The choice of escape is what defines them and what and who they are. The choice that Morpheus Omega decides to take is continuing this brutal path of destruction, disassembling of and destroying contender after contender. MO lives for the adrenalin rush to escape the life he has never known. Without guilt or sorrow, the blood runs cold through this mans veins. Evil is an understatement when speaking of this cursed name, a BLOODY Understatement! The scene opens with Morpheus Omega reading something, lost in deep thought absorbing all of this information that he is reading off the page bright white page. Carefully examining and reviewing each word that is read. An Intrigued look bears on the face of Morpheus as he continues down the page further. 'This is unbelievable, who does this guy think he is?' Morpheus retorts with the sound of resentment. Morpheus continues reading the letter, judging by the thick white paper with vibrant black font its some kind of legal document. Morpheus licks his thumb and flips the page, he continues reading and his mood unknown of what he thinks about this document. Clearly like he is impressed but like he wants to snap at the same time. 'Who in their right mind, would send legal document claiming they know of my identity. They want me to arrange where I can speak with him and his lawyer present about large amount of money, my identity and my past. Who the hell is this guy?' Morpheus sounds very amazed but also confused... Information over flowing his mind. Finishing the last page, Morpheus continues speaking 'Nowhere on here is my actual name or any information that would lead me to believe he is speaking of the truth. The hell with him!' Morpheus barks out and crumples the document into a ball. Morpheus stares at the paper in his hand awhile and throws it behind his head.. The Infinite of evil lets out huge sigh and shakes his head back and forth. Morpheus speaks and his words filled with hatred 'Why the fuck do these tools have to fuck with me. FUCK!' the word trails and echoes. Breathing deeply trying to stay calm, cool and collective. Three words that are almost the exact opposite of 'The God of Sleep and Dreams'. Morpheus shuts his eyes deeply concentrating for a moment, he then opens his eyes wide as can be. The cold gray eyes of Morpheus clearly seen and more bloodshot then usual. The look on his face looks like he has lost hope and has become very raged. Clenching his teeth from anger he continues to breath deeply. The scene fades very dramatically with Morpheus showing his cold emotion...
