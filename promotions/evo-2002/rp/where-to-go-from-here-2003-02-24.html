Where to go from here

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/24/03 'What now....where do I go from here!?!? Ohhh I'm sorry let me introduce myself I'm Luther Wisdom Evolution Superstar and apparently Daniel Somers new whipping boy. I've decided instead of giving a promo I'll sit down from now on and talk to you...my fans about Evo and what I expect and feel will happen to yours truely.' 'As you probably saw by now HeartBreak Chick and myself lost the tag team titles. Somehow when Somers stacked the odds against us I was sort of expecting a screw job.....but Xavier? Never did I expect that...does his hatred run that deep that he would retard my career based on a notion that myself, Big Daddy Dreamer, among others stiffeled his? I mean what he did his violent actions against Heartbreak Chick prove anyway other than piss me off?' 'Which is another issue that's bothering me.....I saw myself throwing in the towel to prevent my partner from being used as an Xavier Citizen pawn. However did I betray my true feelings as part of my act? I mean HBC and my partnership was born out of Somers hatred of Dreamer and King, the fact that we became champions was used more as a tool for The Unlimited more than anything else. Simply put everyone expects her to renew her Syndicate ties.....however there is a part of me that can't allow that to happen. Afterall isn't this federation called Evolution? Our alliance was born out of sins of others past.....yet I found in the short time working with her that there is untapped potential in the Wisdom, Chick combination. I need to escape the shackles of my past and that includes the eventual confrontation with Xavier, my only fear that in doing so will I lose the window of opprotunity I have to convince HBC that maybe....just maybe her future lies with Luther Wisdom MultiNational and now with The Syndicate?' 'look at me sounding like a lovesick teenager when my interest is strictly business and in no way meant as disrespect towards Big Daddy Dreamer but now I am at a cross roads.....I came to Evolution with the single goal of capturing the Tag Team titles...something I've done twice now with an unexpected partner. Naturally a move to singles would be in order....but I feel a sense of unfinished business in the tag ranks. HBC and I were used as pawns the first time around......but now I feel it's time to turn the tables on Daniel Somers. Even if I have to go about it alone....because in all reality who can I trust?' ELSEWHERE....... Xavier Citizen sits in the bowels of an arena; deep inside of a boiler room. He's looking off into space, watching the water drip off the pipes. He's wearing a Syndicate t-shirt, a little bloody however one suspects from self mutilation.....There is a shard of glass on the floor with blood on it. XC:'Nick Van Core you have to understand I came to the PPV to send a message to the usurping bastard Luther Wisdom and the bastard Big Daddy Dreamer. I saw a chance to deliver a message and I did. I hope Heartbreak Chick was able to deliver what I said verbatum to Dreamer.' He takes off his shirt to reveal the word SLAVE etched in his chest still bleeding apparently from the shard of glass..... XC:'You see I am a slave to all those who take and take and take from me. Malcolm King took my friendship...my loyalty and used it for his own personal gain. But now he's out of Evolution and quite frankly at his age I have doubts he will wrestle again. Big Daddy Dreamer took my name; albeit from Malcolm, but the principle still applies. Dreamer wanted to become Universal Champion.....by any means necessary, I'm glad he attained his goal but in doing so allowed some ingrate to use my name and disrespect me in CEW. However I don't blame you and your ambition as the start of my downward spiral......ohhh no quite the contrary. There is someone else here I blame.' He stands up and sticks his tongue out....some water from the pipes drop in as he continues his rant..... XC:'The person I blame for my downward spiral is none other than that slut SEARA!!!!! It was you who didn't do the right thing as far as business and give up your roster spot when I asked you to do so. It was YOU Seara....who caused Asis to attack me to keep me from destroying you before he had a chance. You see Seara that simple act of you being you..caused me to deal with The Unlimited, which forced me to call Malcolm King in to help which saddled me with babysitting Luther Wisdom which basically provided me with the background role I'm ill suited for and yet strangely have grown accustomed to. But no longer my little bitch.....no longer....' He turns around his back to the camera. On his back the words: FREEDOM, JUSTICE , REDEMPTION are also carved in his back in similar fashion to the word slave on his chest. XC:'Seara I intend to finsih the job that Asis was ill equipt to do.' (fade to black)
