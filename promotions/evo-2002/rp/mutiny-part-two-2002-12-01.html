Mutiny... part two.

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens with two police officers bashing a man's head in with their billy clubs repeatedly over and over again, while the man winces in pain and lets out a screaming cry. The one officer kicks the man in the face and spits on him. The man lies wounded bleeding from the face profusely. Multiple police officers can be seen through the street and in the immediate location of the hotel and surrounding area. One of the two officers who were beating the man, drops down driving one of his knees into his back and forcefully tears the man’s first arm behind his back and locks down the first handcuff. The mechanical noise can be heard clicking. The officer tears the next arm behind his back and cuffs that hand as well. The mechanical noise heard once again. The officer rises back to his feet and pulls the man up by the short chain connecting the handcuffs together. The man disappears leading the man towards the patty wagon. The scene pans and Morpheus Omega is standing on the steps to the hotel with a huge grin on his face like he’s over whelmed by all of the action and excitement. The fire extinguisher, rests on the step down below Morpheus. Morpheus speaks to himself, muttering under his breathe. 'What morons, they actually fell for the old fire extinguisher routine. They must of been dropped on their heads or spoon fed bowls of ecstasy as children. Tricking them was even easier then defeating Ryan Flesco on Thursday night!' Morpheus smiles knowing how easy the victory over 'The True Blood' Ryan Flesco actually was. Morpheus walks down the stairs of the hotel to the sidewalk below and observes the bloody mess that the mob left themselves in, also with the help of the police. Morpheus picks up the white cross that reads ''Morpheus Omega, lies here today' and tears the sign into multiple pieces and lets it fall to the ground below like confetti at a new years party... One of the police officers approach Morpheus with a serious, stern look on his face. The officer stares down Morpheus like he doesn’t know if he should congratulate him or beat and treat him like the rest of the angry mob. The officer speaks 'State your business and what your doing standing here!' The officer’s expression does not change the slightest bit. Morpheus snarls, staring down at the police officer, showing his hatred for authority. Morpheus replies with a cocky tone 'Get with the times there tanto. I’m Morpheus Omega, the God of Sleep and Dreams, The Infinite of Evil. Do you not watch television?' The officer replies with 'Eh' and thinks to himself before replying again, 'I think I might have seen you on cops a few months back. You are that guy, who was busted with a kilo of heroine and attempted to murder another street thug for his drugs before you were circle stomped into your own ruins by multiple officers. Your coming with me for questioning buddy!' The officer looks very serious and approaches Morpheus even closer. Morpheus backs up and speaks with a weary tone of voice, 'Actually I’m a professional wrestler so back the fuck up. I can see and smell the bacon grease dripping from you already. How about I go back to my hotel room before you start thinking I’m some Columbian drug lord. Some people and especially you Canadians!' Morpheus has a disgusted look on his face. The officer glares very seriously at Morpheus and abruptly turns and walks away from his personal space. Morpheus climbs each step slowly and something catches his ear and he turns back quickly. In the short distance Morpheus can see two officers pushing a man. The man screaming into the face of the officers, 'Get your hands off me you filthy pigs. I’m here to see Morpheus Omega not kill him. What the hell is wrong with you Canadians?' The man retorts as he’s pushed back further. Morpheus stares at the man who’s half blocked by the officers almost off in a daze. Morpheus snaps out of it and goes running towards the scene like he recognizes the man. Morpheus screams at the police officers while running over there as quickly as he possibly can 'Let that man go... That man could buy out your precinct and fire you all if he wanted. Let him the fuck go!' Morpheus screams as he stops beside the officers and pushes both of them. The officers look very angry at this gesture. One screams into his face 'Toronto police, stand down now!' The officer huffs and puffs... Morpheus backs up from the demanding officer almost like he’s protecting the familiar man he seen from the distance. Both of the officers staring down Morpheus, they back away slowly and away from the scene. Morpheus standing guard with that sick look on his face. Morpheus turns suddenly with an excited look on his face due to the unexpected visitor. He waists no time speaking 'Robert Knight... What are you doing all the way up here in Toronto? I thought you hibernate down south all summer and winter long? Regardless Its damn good to see you!' Robert Knight with a huge smile on his face also very excited in return of seeing Morpheus Omega. The scene fades from the screen... Who is this man named Robert Knight, what past relation does he have with the sick, Infinite of evil? Time will only tell us these answers after all of this chaos and anarchy in the Toronto streets. To be continued…
