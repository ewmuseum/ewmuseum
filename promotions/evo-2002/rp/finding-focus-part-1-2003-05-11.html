finding focus part 1

11 May 2003

Date: 5-11-03 Sickening, screwed up, and confusing is the situation surrounding Hedges. Never has such a string of defeats ever happened in his illustrious career. Perhaps his long break away from the ring caused a little bit more rust than was visible to the naked eye. Coming on strong his rusted parts seemed to hold to gather fine in a string of early victories before finally giving way. Perhaps it is the fool Xero’s fault for he has cost Hedges more than one match. No matter what it is can Hedges bounce back and reclaim the limelight is the question everyone is asking themselves…There is only one answer and that is yes, simply because he is not a man to back away when the tides are against him. He will not back down from a fight or walk away from a company. A new chapter is being written inside the twisted head of Hedges and only time will be able to tell what is written in store for the rest of eVo. The scene opens up in the darkened streets of downtown St. Louis. In a run down part o the city there isn’t much life around. A few neon beer signs hang in the windows of closing bars. Most of the buildings are run down or boarded up as the last few customers come stumbling out of the bar. Hedges emerges from the door barely up on his own power. He’s with a beautiful young blond about 5’10. He has an angry scowl on his face as he stumbles down the street. Closer inspection reveals it is his old girlfriend and manager Meghan walking next to him, someone who knows Hedges better than he knows himself. “What the hell is wrong with me, Meghan? Why can’t I seem to do anything right lately. I f*cking lost to Shocka and MLX blowing my title shot I earned. Man I haven’t done this bad in the ring since I started out in HMW 7 years ago. You know me better than anyone. I need some help here, what the f*ck am I doing wrong.” Hedges exclaims She continues walking for a moment in silence before talking… “You seem like you’ve lost your focus. Where’s that rush of energy that used to pulse threw your veins every second? You where always ready to pounce on someone for the slightest mistake. Now you let people walk all over you. Instead of using that great intensity and skill you possess to kick someone’s ass. You use it to walk into the nearest bar and drown your sorrows. Sitting in your head getting angrier as your problems lose focus. You used to knock heads and go to the bar to cool that adrenaline rush you got from hearing your named announced as the victor so you wouldn’t go kill someone…Where’s that evil you used to have. I can remember when people where afraid to walk next to you for fear you’d kick their ass…where are the mind games you used to play so well…I think you need to find your focus and pull your head out of your ass” Meghan said to him in a stern voice. “You’re right, it’s time for me to pull my head out of my ass. What did I do? I turned soft. That’s not me…Tone my actions down I was told…I’m not going to have that shit anymore. I could care less if the front office doesn’t like what I do. It’s tough shit for them…My minds sick so my actions are sick. Slump or no slump I don’t lose to chumps like Shocka and fools in team eVo. There is going to be no turning over a new leaf, the one I’m on is fine. I’m simply going to go back to using it the way I did back in the day. The branch I’m on produces bad apples and there’s no more hiding it so I can look better. People are just going to have to deal with it and a few bad apples spoil the bunch and eVo is about to have a rude awaking…” “That’s what I like to here. So the supreme being is back” “No doubt about it” Hedges says with an intense look in his eyes With that scene fades to black in anticipation of what Hedges finally has in store for the evolution locker room….
