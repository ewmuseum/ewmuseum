T minus your life and counting

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th October 2002 “A vacation, too much time spent away from the ring is your countdown, t minus your life and counting. We will all die; it is just what we do on our way there that will make people remember us. You work your life in the ring and you die, and people will remember you as the kid who risked their life to do something important and worth while instead of being out there on the streets killing and raping and causing uncontrolled havoc. Havoc yes it is done here in the ring and out the ring, but there is more control, it may not seem that way from where you are sitting, but the truth is it is controlled.” The camera is sitting looking into a dark motel room, the female voice is heard, but so far no one can be seen. The scene has been this way now for 5 minutes, but the camera does not look away or leave the room, but waits instead until the female reveals herself to it. No lights are attempted to be turned on, but at this point the cameraman is not sure what kind of a mood the female is in, and whether he wants to risk making her mad. A small red and yellow flame appears from the corner of the room and the camera focuses on it, opening the curtains a few inches Seara lets light into the room. She stands where she can be seen. She is holding her ribs in an uncomfortable fashion, bruised and battered from Faith when Asis attacked her even after their match was over. Only a few days before we briefly seen the female wrestler in the hospital, looking at a man she is admitting to being friends with Scott Frost. She had taken herself to the hospital only days after Faith to be checked up, after she had been getting migraines for no reason that she knew and since she had been finding it hard and painful to breath. She had left the hospital the next day after choosing to spend the night sleeping a free bed, which she had noticed while being examined. Finding out her ribs were slightly bruised and inflamed was the least of her worries, she had recently heard of Asis join with Unlimited, a group Seara had some amount of history with. When she had first joined, she had been up against The HeartBreak Chick, and had never once beat her in a match, and Xander had been a bain for Seara, the pair had an instant disliking to each other, and now with Seara looking close to Notorious member, and ex Unlimited member, Scott Frost, she was sure that her meetings with Unlimited were about to grow regular and more painful. Stepping away from the window she places her cellular phone on the small bedside cabinet. This is only the second time we have seen Seara in a motel room, and this is the only of the times that she has not destroyed the room. Something is once again changing in the female, she seems more passive as of late, and she has no real care if she is alone, she seems interested in the friendship of Frost and the options that come with her newfound company. Asis and I have nothing to fight over anymore, I know he may well attack me again, I am not stupid, it would be the perfect way for Xander to get to me, I mean he never got to get his hands on me back when I joined EVolution, and he won’t, but by using Asis to hurt me, he will get the satisfaction without ever having to risk getting the shit kicked into him. I am no longer alone; there are more people around. I have Frost on my side, and I know Asis he will become an enemy of yours, but I will be there with Frost, he is my friend, and I will defend him, I will stick by his side, like he is sure he will do for me. Trust, the first time in a long time. Last time I trusted another human being was long before… I have no recognition of the last time I trusted another human being. When I came here people thought Asis was my friend, and thought I trusted him, but Asis and I only share a similar childhood in the same area, I have no trust in Asis, and I never have, the only thing I trust Asis with is the fact that one day he will end a life in that ring, and that one day he will end my life. I will never expect Asis to trust me or be a friend towards me, I also never expect him to protect me from an attack or someone who is bothering me. Asis and myself are more different now than we ever have been in our lives, and I am sure our differences will continue to grow and amount. An ending to end all endings, the fight to the death, that will see both wrestlers carried from the rings in pain and horror, to have one more chance after to step in the ring to once again face each other, and to once again leave the ring without the use of their limbs, for this time they will leave… dead. The camera shakily backs away from the female wrestler, and she seems to follow it, although she never gets close enough for them to see the large cut across her forehead and part way down one side of her face, there is another one on the underneath of her eye, and she looks to be in slight discomfort with her once again injured ribs. But injured ribs mean nothing to her, discomfort is something she has learnt to live with, and pain is like a normal everyday feeling for her. Her cell phone begins to ring and she reaches forward for it. Opening it she lifts it to her ear, smiling slightly at the sound of Frosts voice. “Yeah, I’m fine, but what about you, are you ok?” “Yeah sure. It was only a few weakened ribs, nothing much, some bruising, swelling, but I don’t care. The cuts on my head are just as bad as when you tried to give them a clean up…no frost…. no, its ok” Seara does not seem to be getting frustrated, normally, by now she would have slammed her cell shut and walked away. Leaving it where it was to ring as long as they let it. But this time, she keeps it to her ear and continues to smile. “Yeah, this week, you sure this week, I’m not complaining, I would rather we did it this week, but I didn’t think you would be ready for it” The female laughs for a moment, looks at the camera and hangs up her cell phone before sitting on the bed. “The end of all things to come. The end of your life, the end of hers, she is hidden; she will not spoil me, not now. You will all die, you will all suffer in hell, or domed to wander through purgatory forever, but I will never suffer the pain, nothing can worsen the hell that I am. The hell I suffer is greater than any you will ever suffer in you eternity of death. A vacation is your countdown, t minus your life and counting. We will die together, but what you suffer is what I live. Death, the only way out. The closest to this in life is friendship; in life friendship is the only way out. We will all know what it is like to be damned to hell, but you will already be free when your soul is tortured for all eternity, I am being tortured. Frost’s friendship is saving me. Friendship is lives only way out. A vacation is your countdown, t minus your life and counting.” As the scene fades in the motel room, the female continues to repeat the line “ A vacation is your countdown, t minus your life and counting” after repeating it a few times she is down to repeating the last part of it “t minus your life and counting” it is not known what the female means by this, but it is possibly the start of another change in her as she grows ever closer to male wrestler Scott Frost.
