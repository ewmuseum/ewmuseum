Crushed

29 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: september 29th Since digital destruction, Seara’s mood has become more agitated and she has found she has a shorter fuse. Talking her way out of hospital, the female wrestler has promised to let a female nurse stay with her until she is up to full health. The nurse chosen for the job had favoured Seara, and had been a fan of hers since her days in an amateur Chicago wrestling fed. Knowing Seara’s short temper, she checks her twice a day to make sure the female is coping ok, and leaves her for the rest of the time to deal with her own problems. During the day the female has been disappearing, and has been found in front of TV’s or magazines, keeping informed on the latest happenings within Evolution. On Wednesday night she disappeared before Legends, and reappeared a few days later. This Wednesday she knows she will be at Legends X, but will she be in the ring, or will she take a spectators seat? Seara – I am still thinking about what Asis did to me at Digital Destruction, and it would be expected, I’m back in my own hometown, and I still have a nurse following me around, and I’m still bandaged up, I have broken ribs, but they won’t stop me getting in that ring. As soon as Daniel Somers gives me a fight, I will be back in that ring as if this had never happened. I know I would probably still be in some discomfort, but pain is nothing, it is in the mind, pain is what I live for. Seara looks up at the camera from the floor of her apartment, she is sitting cross-legged with a candle light in front of her, occasionally she pours the hot wax onto a sheet of paper and places her entire hand into it palm first. She seems to ignore the fact that the wax is red hot, and continues to do it. As she looks down at the wax, she looks confused by her own actions, and she twists her head to get a different view of the molten liquid below her. Picking up the candle she blows it out, eliminating all light from the room and throws it across the room towards a small steal bin, the nose of it going in is a large thud, and we can make out Seara smiling to herself. She cocks her head again, and tries to make out the cameraman’s face, and then looks away in disgust, it is a cameraman she has never seen before. Seara – Some things have certainly changed since Digital Destruction. Erin Christoph is no longer with us, and in his place we have Scott Frost, who I have not had the pleasure of meeting face to face yet, but I plan to this week at Legends X, not to torture him, but to look upon him and see for myself just how he is going to cope within eVolution. I have looked upon him when he has appeared on the TV, and I must admit he is often there when I switch on or walk past a shop window. Normally with his wife and manager, but be careful Frost, you wouldn’t want to destroy your perfect little scene you have. You could very well upset your wife when you bring her to the ring, so just make sure you know what you are doing at all times. I let my private life interfere with my fighting, and I let my stepbrother Vinnie get in my way, he got out of control, he pushed me one step to far, and now he is dead, so don’t let your work and your family become the same thing, Vinnie was my family, and I let him become another victim of my work and my pain. He paid for what he did, and I would hate to see your potential be crushed by your little wife getting hurt or lost within this business. Don’t think I care about you or what you do, just take it as a warning from one eVo rookie to another. Be careful, be very careful. Going back to last weeks Legends, I know about Asis attack, and I could say he deserves it, and I could say he didn’t, but I have no real feelings about it, I’m sure it will not be the last time I see Asis being attacked, and I’m sure I will have something to do with it at one point, but right now, I would like to find out just who was behind that mask, many people have suggested it could have been me that attacked Asis, and yes, it could well have been me, it also could have been someone else, and even if I did know who it was, why would I tell anyone, its not as if I have a public duty to tell you anything. But just because I said that, doesn’t mean I do know anything about who attacked Asis. But this week we will find out at Legends X, and I will be there to see the reaction on Asis face when he sees the unmasked face of his attacker. Seara looks carefully at a cut on the palm of her hand, which now has solid cold wax filling the fresh gaping wound. The edges are red raw and it looks painful, reaching down she pulls a shard of glass out of the wound, pulling the lump of dry wax out with it as well. Blood begins to once again flow from it, wrapping it in a bandage she ignores it and continues to shake her legs like she has been doing now for 10 minutes. She smiles briefly and looks up to the ceiling; on it is a large poster with the word eVolution scrolled across it. She focuses hard on the blue background then spells out eVolution letter by letter; at this moment there is innocence in her that has never been seen before. Holding her stomach she lays back and looks at the poster, concentrating hard on the large rectangular piece of paper. Seara- they will never know. I will never show them. They will never learn it. Her words are quiet, but she repeats them over and over again, getting more frustrated with every word and thought. Without warning she leaps up to her feet in a single movement and turns toward the wall, swinging her fist at the same time, after the initial impact she grabs her ribs and folds her body in half. The sudden movement to her upper torso has frustrated the injuries Asis inflicted nearly 2 weeks ago. When she is back to her feet, she begins to throw punches at the wall until her knuckles become red and bruised, it is clear to the cameraman that the female wrestler is in pain from the injuries, and that she is not in full health yet. Seara – I’ve been crushed by Asis once now, and I have seen him be crushed twice, once when he took me out at Digital Destruction, and L2J came to the ring and took out “The Demonic”, I seen that many times on replay. I liked that, it was fun, it was scary, and it was different. I saw Asis crushed last week as well by a masked attacker who is going to fight him this week at Legends X along with Eric Badger in a Triple Threat Match. I will watch closely to see him crush people like he does each week, and I will watch his attacker unmasked, and I will pay close attention to the way they fight Asis. The female walks toward the camera, gets as close to it as possible and stares hard and cold into the lens as though she were trying to see through it to the people watching on their TV sets. She eventually backs away, but continues to give the camera that awkward confused stare that she is known for. Seara – Things have been weird, they have been different, and I have had time to think about a lot of stuff. I don’t like to think about everything days in advance, but right now I have nothing else to do. I have kept myself busy, tried to keep out of this place, but it’s hard, I have found myself her, getting away from the world, it’s a space where you are the only people that are not scared to be in with me. People have a problem with me, they are scared, they are worried about what I can do to them. They think I’ll kill them, and they are right, I would kill everyone if I could, but not now, now I need to rest, to focus, to push myself all at once. I need to heal, I need my health back, well what little health I did have. I’m not even allowed to smoke now, but they won’t stop me, they can rip up every cigarette I light up, they can put them out on my bare flesh, but they will not tell me what to do. She looks away from the camera, and takes off her trench coat, dropping it onto a pile on the floor, she then begins to remove her top that she wears under her coat, and drops that on top of her trench coat. A moment later she is rummaging through her pockets, pulling out a cigarette box and a silver lighter she lights it up and looks at the scares across her stomach. She is thinner than she has looked in previous weeks, and it is clear to see the punishment she took two weeks ago at Digital Destruction. Still bruised she shows the large white bandage wrapped tightly around her ribs, and waist, prodding at them she smiles as the pain courses through the small frame of her body. Standing in her bra and trousers she walks around casually, smoking her cigarette and singing to herself, it suddenly becomes evident there is music coming from somewhere, and Seara can clearly hear the guitar riffs of Jimi Hendrix’s Voodoo Child. The cameraman listens closely as Seara begins to mumble to words “I was crushed” to her self in a slow hypnotizing manner. Taking a deep breath on the cigarette she blows out the smoke and watches it as it circles up around her head, engulfing her in a white cloud. This is the slowest we have ever seen Seara smoke, and with every breath, she grips at her bandaged ribs, but she has no care that she is half naked with a camera following her every move. Seara – it will all change, I will change it, I will make it happen my way. I was crushed and now I will change it. Things are different, things are different, they are weird, I will change that, I will make sure things get better, I will, I know I will do it. I will do it, I will stop this happening to me again, this is frustrating for me, and he will not have the chance to do this to me again. I am prepared, I will train twice as hard, I will make sure I am ready to face “The Demonic” Asis. We will meet, and I will be ready for it. I am more than he thinks I am, I will kill him, prove myself to him, make myself known to all. Seara’s words make no sense, and as the camera fades to black, she continues to mutter the same thing over and over to herself, obsessed with her words and thoughts. It is not uncommon to leave Seara like this, but she has left the cameraman worried about what she is going to do to the people who get in her way, no matter how pathetic their involvement is with her. The last words we hear as the camera completely fades out are clear and devoid any of emotion. “This is the end of it, the end of life as we know it”.
