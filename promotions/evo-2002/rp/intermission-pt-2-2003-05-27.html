Intermission pt.2

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-27 I sat in the shadows watching, listening, fuming… ”Come on, he won’t even mind” That coming from the guy in the multicolored shirt, leather pants, and dorky looking shoes standing across from Khadafi’s girlfriend. ”No. I know Ali, Alex. He won’t like it if I just drop my plans with him to do something with you.” Deb was standing away from him, arms crossed against her chest, the pure white blouse she was wearing rippling in the light breeze. That’s a good girl, babe, just tell him to fuck off. ”We’re just friends though. He won’t mind you hanging out with your friends will he? He can’t be that big of a prick.” Strike one, anybody? Deb shifted her weight from foot to foot looking a bit uncomfortable. ”No Alex. I don’t think it’s a good idea.” Good girl, good girl. But Alex was putting up a protest, moving towards her, trying to get her to remember all the good times they had had together and assure her that nothing untoward was going on. Yeah, that’s about the time I stepped out of the bushes and fucked up Alex’s night pretty righteously.
