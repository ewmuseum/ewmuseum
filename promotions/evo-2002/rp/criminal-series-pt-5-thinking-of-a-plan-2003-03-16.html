Criminal Series Pt 5; Thinking of a plan

16 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 16, 2003 -=[We open the day off with Greg Vulgar for a change. Looking over his shoulder all the way, he leaves his rental car in the 7-11 parking lot. He pulls out some small bills along with some change from his pocket. Counting it to himself, he walks towards the 7-11 entrance but then decides to get something to eat first. He walks right next door to the Subway where he is ready to enter. He sees a wanted poster on the window of Subway, with a pencil sketch of his own face. Vulgar ignores it and continues. He enters and two old women are sitting at a table staring at him pretty hard. Both ladies looked to be in there 70s and with obvious wigs on. Vulgar acts like he doesn’t notice them as he goes to order a steak and cheese sub One of the ladies looks at Vulgar and reaches into her purse and pulls out a cell phone. Vulgar gets nervous and bolts out the store]=- “Whom are you calling?” Asked the first lady “The Veterinarian,” the second lady responded. “I think those weren’t Sparky’s teeth I gave him…” The old lady smiles and her teeth seem to be rather large and pointy. -=[We go to the Thug roC household where everyone is. Everyone, even the wives of Thug roC and Adam Carr, all are in attendance as Thug roC is putting some black gloves on. Shannon, Thug roC’s wife, and Tasha, Adam Carr’s wife, both have clothes on as if they are about to go jogging or do some sort of sporting activity. Vega, Machine, and the other guys have on the same]=- “I still don’t understand why you guys won’t let us go with you.” Shannon says with a whine to her voice. “He robbed us just as much as he robbed you two.” “This isn’t fair! I want to kick Garth’s ass too!” Tasha yells. “First of all, his name is Greg.” Vega points out “And secondly,” Thug roC starts. “you two aren’t coming, he can very well attack you two “ So? We can defend ourselves too!” Shannon says with a high pitched voice. “Please just spare us the whining. If we need something, we’ll call” Adam Carr states “That’s just like you Adam, you always call when you need ‘something’” Tasha says and looks at Adam. -=[The room goes quiet and looks at Adam]=- “She’s talking about gas money” Adam says, trying to defend himself -=[The room goes back to the normal status.]=- “We don’t need you two getting hurt. If we don’t come back within like 20 minutes, then you can get worried” Adam says, ignoring Tasha’s last comment “That’s a deal,” Tasha replies. “you always “come” within 3 minute anyways…” -=[The room goes silent and looks at Adam]=- “She’s talking about bathroom breaks between commercials” -=[Everyone in the room gives Adam a look like they don’t believe him]=- “Oh would you all stop!” Adam yells -=[Everyone in the room continues to get ready for their night out]=- “Before I go to the bathroom, what do I do again?” Machine asks “You take a sh*t, then you wipe; what’s so hard about that?” Vega replies with a smart comment “No! I mean what do I have to do tonight?” Machine gives Vega a dirty look “Ok, lets go over this one more time….” Thug roC states before the scene fades to black
