From the hospital

12 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: after Legends XI Tampa, Florida Hours after Legends XI Tampa General Hospital Legends XI, and the friction between BDD and HBC continues to grow by leaps and bounds. It all started with the self-professed ‘Kick heard around the world’ in which HBC decided to terminate their partnership, sealing the deal with a boot to the chiseled chin of BDD. She does pack quite a wallup with it. Many superstars have fallen from it. Now, I can understand why. Though she may be 5-11 and weigh 140, she kicks like a dam freight train hitting you full force. Taijari’s kick ain’t squat measured to it. But alas, BDD did have some measure of revenge, costing her what she held so dear, the High-Impact Championship. The gold she worked long and hard for, taken away by an old ‘throwing’ injury stemming back to my collegiate football days. But even then, it was Thug Roc who ultimately cost her. So much for her New-TV deal, BDD thinks with a chagrin on his bloodied face. Still though BDD paid a price for upsetting The First Lady of Evolution. The world watched in horror when a handcuffed and defenseless BDD took the most vile chairshot in the history of Evo. HBC took the chair and swung it like Barry Bonds, crashing it over BDD head, initially busting him open, and then following it with an Arabian Facebuster. Interesting though, I never taught her that one. None the less, BDD for the first time in his illustrious career, was busted open worse than anytime in his life. Which brings us to where we are now, Tampa General over by the University of Tampa. BDD is sitting in the emergency room with a blood soaked towel pressed tightly against his forehead. Several people already waiting, turn away from the blood soaked crimson mask wondering if a walking traffic accident victim staggered in. BDD face is discolored, a large purple knot forms just below his right eye socket where the chair was smashed in the second time. Dried blood has already crusted in his hair. Frankly, BDD looks like hell. Looks like he’s been to hell and back, and yet strangely, something seems eerily calm as well. BDD tosses the blood soaked towel on the check in desk and smiles at the attending nurse, who looks up at him and gets wide-eyed, not because of who he is, but because of how bad his face appears. She shouts for an emergency room physician to run over and attend to him. DR: Good lord man, what the hell happened to you! BDD: Two chairshots from my ex-partner. DR: It looks like you got propelled through a car windshield. Let me guess, your with Evo right, they had a show tonight didn’t they? BDD: Yeah. DR: I thought so, one of your colleagues came through earlier. He was a nut! Terrified everyone on staff! Well let me take a look at this. As the Dr. begins to examine BDD’s head and face, a stunning woman walks through the emergency room doors, he recognizes her on the spot. Magdalena looks around and finally see’s BDD sitting up on the table as the Dr. part’s BDD scalp seeing two large divots and whistles in amazement. He then presses the large purple knot. As he does BDD screams out in pain and grabs the Dr. by the throat and releases him. Magdalena takes BDD hand and starts to caress it which seems to calm the big man down as the physician grabs some tools to begin stitching up BDD’s forehead. BDD: What are you doing here? M: I wanted to check up on you. Everyone in the back couldn’t believe the monster chairshot you took. They compared it to Tommy Dreamer-Raven back in ECW. Then were horrified by the second one. Even those who don’t like you felt you didn’t deserve that. All were shocked when you walked out under your own power. They thought you were going to be carried out. BDD: I’ve never been carried out, not ever, and I’m not about to start now. Ashley got me good, that is a fact, but you know something Magdalena, in a strange, sick way, she did something beneficial for me. That was wake me up, get me out of my funk and re-ignite the fire that burns in me. The last time that happened as she’ll testify to it was against another partner of mine, Callous. Four years ago, Hell in the Cell. He did everything he could to get me, to hurt me, but in the end, I had enough and on top of that cage, I cinched him up, put him in a brainbuster position and leapt from the top of the cage, crashing the both of us through a table and snapping his dam neck in the process. It was the most gruesome thing ever recorded, and right about now, I feel like I’m their again. Ashley, I’m sorry, The HeartBreaker will rue the day she decided to cross me. Bill Cosby once said in a comedic monologue. ‘I brought you into this world, and I’ll take you out.’ Such truer words couldn’t be spoken. HBC is here in this business because of me. Because I saw something in her and she has never failed to live up to her hype. And though we’ve traveled the road for years, I didn’t teach her everything I know. One day, I knew we would somehow fight it out. The fans, the bookers would all want it, but now, it is a bit more interesting. I know how tough she is, how much pain she can absorb, how fast she is, I know all that she posseses. I know how devious her mind is. From the day she lopped off her rookie year tag partner manhood to all the little games she played with her opponents. She is good, but, I am by no means over and done yet. Me costing her the High-Impact Championship, that I did for me because it would wound her. A short title reign further adding to that one solo inadequacy she possesses that everyone harps on. Poor Heartbreaker can’t hold a singles title very long. The longest reign she’s had was three weeks as a singles champion. That blackmark on her stellar career growing bigger everyday. And added to the fact, she’s never won the big won. Well gee, I wonder why. Probably because Ashley, you can’t even hold onto a mid-tier title very long! So while she may have busted my face, my face will heal, but your mental conditioning and my dark secrets I know about you, they won’t heal. I didn’t start this war Ashley, but I’m dam sure going to end it and even if that means the impossible, doing something that many have tried before with you, ending your career, so be it. You and I both know many have tried and all have failed, but, know one in this world knows how you tick, better than I do. You know I’m a very dangerous man when it gets personal. You remember our friends Akage, and Kuda. Kuda still will never walk again, not even after his twentieth surgery on his legs, trying to repair when I ran them over with a forklift! And Akage, his spine still shot when I snapped it in two! The former members of The Syndicate, and yes, I nearly forgot one, Extreme Bruce, whose neck was snapped by my hands. As you can see Ashley, there is only one way to truly escape The Syndicate……being a cripple. You tried doing it to me but failed, now learn from the master Ashley, learn from your mentor, learn how to get it done properly when I send you, The HeartBreaker into the early wrestling retirement home. I’m glad you did your Playboy pictorial, it will serve as a reminder to you of what you used to be. I’m glad I fucked you when I did. I’m glad I had you every way possible when I did because after I’m through, no man, no woman, no farm animal will ever want to appear with you or bang you senseless again. You wanted it this way Ashley, fine, no rules, anything goes, just like old times. But when you look up at me with those baby blue eyes of yours, begging me for forgiveness, begging me to suck me off like old times with those pouty lips of yours. I’ll have a smile on my face before I drill yours repeatedly with my fist and snapping your neck with the hold that you fear…..the Dreamer Crossface. When you give and submit in the ring, I’ll carry you into the back, and you’ll give and submit to me once more…….in my bedroom! No more games. No more past. No more anything. Just you, learning once again, why I was the Enforcer and the most feared man in this industry. The twilight of your stellar career is nearly over, I hope, check that, I don’t give a dam if you can handle it or not! Get ready Ashley, to be Heartbroken, once and for all! Aren’t you done yet doc! Jesus Christ man! Our EMT’s would have been done already. Give me the dam thing, I’ll do it myself!!!
