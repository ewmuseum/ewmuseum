Memories III

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 14th, 2003 What happens when the queen is the first piece to be eliminated? He saw to make sure that was the case though he knew it not at the time. He saw to it to eliminate my queen before the rest of us even knew what was happening. The old rules of society have long ago been dispatched by all of us. They haven’t applied for quite a long time to the society that we all now live in. I am not coming out here to strike out against their queen. Little do they know that their queen is on the verge of betrayal. I wonder if any of them even see it coming. Or are they as blind to the obvious as I once was? Whether they know it or not my target is set and they have no clue that I will be coming. Do they perhaps think I’m dead? Do they think I’m as dead as she now is because of them? To have forgotten me so soon is but a game that fools play. I am far from dead and will soon be far from forgotten. The one thing that they can all count on is that someday soon I will be back. I will be on my course and hunting the would be King! The hunter is now the hunted and the hunted will always be the hunters. There is nothing that I will love more than the moment of revelation. The One, Xander, King, S., and most importantly him. They all haven’t even seen it coming! Now are the final hours of our time.
