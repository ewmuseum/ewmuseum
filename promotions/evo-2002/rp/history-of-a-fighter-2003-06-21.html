History of a Fighter

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-21 'Drake rides Kurt Winzor to the mat and drives several forearms into the back of his head. DND has really broken out of his shell here in PRIDE. He came from the gutter, working underground fight clubs to becoming a big name.' exclaims one of the announcers. Dukes picks the opponent up and sends him back to the mat with a release judo throw. With the opponent down, Drake grabs his arm and applies a modified cross armbreaker, sinching it in deeply. 'Drake has it! Drake is going to win his second match here in PRIDE!!!' Winzor taps the mat several times and the hold is released. Winzor gets back to his feet, and extends his hand. Drake reluctantly takes it, only to be kicked directly in the kneecap by the sore loser. Drake stumbles forward and Winzor steps in front, driving the back of his elbow directly into DND's left eye! A loud crunch is heard from the audio on camera 2. 'Kurt Winzor violates the code of honor here in UFC! That's going to cost him his purse here tonight, maybe even keep him out of future fights. Dukes looks hurt!' 'His left eye has been pushed out of it's socket, and he could have some snapped tendons in that knee from the impact of the kick. Those are career threatening injuries.' Drake gets to his feet, bracing his weight. As he exits the ring, paramedics flood the area. -One Year Later- 'As you know, just one year ago Drake Nathanial Dukes was brutally assaulted following a victory over Kurt Winzor, who now carries the PRIDE Heavyweight crown. Drake has agreed to make a final appearence, and it's set for next months PRIDE Pay-Per-View event. Dukes/Winzor: Round 2.' 'You know, this might end up coming back and haunting the officials here at PRIDE. Drake's obvious motive is going to be revenge.' 'But it will make for an interesting fight. Dukes is going to be as intense as ever, but what about the heavyweight champion, Kurt Winzor? He's changed a lot since the last meeting between the two. His mixed martial arts are spectacular.' -One Month Later- Referee: FIGHT! Round one starts with the two fighters simply feeling eachother out. A few kicks landed by Winzor, who suprisingly didn't go after the knee. Drake managed to land a crucial blow to Winzor's nose that busted him open near the closing seconds of the first round. Medics managed to stop the bleeding and allowed the fight to continue. Round two saw a lot more offensive strategy than the previous. Winzor came out strong, landing a brutal savate kick to Drake's chest that temporarily stunned him. Dukes stumbled forward, taking a brutal spin kick to the face. DND fell the ground but managed to get back to his feet. The fight continued and Winzor continued to pour on the punishment, taking Drake down with a judo legsweep at the closing bell of the second round. Round three: DND came out throwing spitfire rights and lefts, once again busting Winzor's nose open. He then managed a double leg pickup and tackled Kurt to the ground, pummeling the hell out of him. All Winzor could do was wear down the clock as he provided minor cover to his face with his hands. Drake got a sick forearm in at the bell, busting Winzor open above the eyebrow. Round four: Winzor, sensing his time in need came out on the offensive again. Drake was down early after a kick to the stomach doubled him over and Winzor landed an elbow behind the head. He got up in a groggy state and Winzor went in for the kill. Kurt went for his deadly roundhouse but Drake caught his foot and slammed him hard to the mat. Drake once again rode Winzor down and got the cross armbreaker applied. Kurt sensed it coming, and even through his pain managed to shift his weight over to block the full effects. Kurt escaped, landing four brutal punches to Drake's open face. The bell rang, ending the fourth. Final Round: It was do or die. Neither competitor wanted this to end in a draw. There was too much riding on the line. DND took the iniative. He busted out of the corner with a leg tackle and got Kurt locked in a tight kneebar. Winzor landed a snap kick to the patched eye, and Drake lost his grip on reality. Winzor got back to his feet and went for another kick to Drake's face. Drake caught his foot and re-applied the cross kneebar. The announcers went crazy as Drake managed to pull out his second victory over Winzor in two years.
