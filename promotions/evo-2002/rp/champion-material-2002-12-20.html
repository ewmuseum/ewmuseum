Champion Material

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20-12-02 The lights across the arena go out so that the stage is highlighted. Suddenly Cowboys from hell begins to play as smoke fills the rampway. The crowd instantly explode into cheers as L2J emerges from the smoke wearing his trenchcoat and his beanie. With his title in one hand and a microphone in the other. He struts down the ramp straying from one side to the other to slap hands with his fans. He rolls into the ring and paces back and forth waiting for his music to die down. He then goes to speak but is interrupted by and L2J chant. He in turn waits patiently for that to finish before speaking. L2J- You know people always talk about….Champion material. L2J begins pacing around as the crowd flares up at the mention of champion. L2J-What makes a person the right stuff to have the right to carry this title around with them. Well everyone. I’m gonna let you in on the secret. Being Champion material comes to three simple things. He stops pacing and sits on one of the turnbuckles. L2J- You have to be willing to work for it, you have to be willing to fight for it, and you have to be willing to die for it. The crowd cheer again. L2J- Those three simple things make the difference between a champ and a chump. So how do you separate the two? Who are the champs and who are the chumps? Well if you do your history you’ll know that champs are people like sovereign and of course- L leaps off the turnbuckle to his feet and slowly lifts the title above his head. The crowd flare up again. L2J- Champs make the grade. Champs work for their spoils. Champs are willing to fight- He motions his hand towards the titantron as it sparks into life. It shows footage from the PPV two months ago. The ever memorable scene of Sovereign and L2J stare eye to eye as the cell was lowered down around them. L2J-Chumps on the other hand. The scene then quickly changes to Shocka celebrating a win as in the background Thug Roc can be seen laying into his opponent with a chair. The footage brings a pop from the crowd. L2J looks around with a smile on his face. L2J-Champs fight. Sovereign was a monster. But you have to hand it to him. He was no ones bunny. He took it to every challenger without prompting. Not like you Shocka. You’re just a bitch to a higher power. The pop from the crowd gets bigger. There’s one thing you need to understand Shocka. To obtain the ultimate prize. You have to be ready to pay the ultimate price. This is what champs looks like after they have given it all. The titantron shows the image of Sovereign and L2J lying in the wreckage of the announce table following the german suplex off the cell. Both men soaked in blood and seemingly paralysed. L2J begins to hold the mic tighter as he beings to get heated. L2J-but CHUMPS just cant take the pain. The scene cuts to Shocka sitting stretched up with ice packs on his and pointing at a bruise the size of a hickey on his neck. The shot is dubbed over with the sounds of violins playing. L2J- Champions will pay any price. The scene cuts to last months PPV where sovereign and L2J went through the wall of the third cage in their title match. L2J-Chumps just wanna get paid There is then a crudely manipulated cut n paste image of Thug Roc handing Shocka a Briefcase bursting with money. The crowd chuckle at the image. L2J- A champion is prepared to die fighting for what he believes in. It shows an image of the aftermath of L2J’s title victory. He is in a hospital bed attached to a respirator with stitches all over his face and chest and cast on his right arm and leg. The laughter begins to die down. L2J-But when it comes down to it… Chumps are just plain faggots. The titantron lights up with Shockas head cut n pasted onto miss muffets body as she pies her face with curds and…whatever. The crowd then explode into laughter as they watch Shocka in a plaid dress shit his pants and run away from the spider. The titantron then blinks to black as it is disconnected. L2J- You see Shocka, You are not ready for this match. You don’t nearly have the mentality to for what is ahead of you. You are not that sick. You haven’t spent four years of torture to get this damn title. You cant possibly comprehend what you will have to go through. But you are naïve. And I know that whatever you say you aren’t going to think better of your decision to fight this Christmas. So if you want this title. If you want it that bad. You can find me in the seventh layer of hell. L2J throws down the mic as Cowboys from hell blasts around the arena. He leaps onto the nearest turnbuckle and throws both arms up above his head causing the title to fly around above him. He repeats the performance for each turnbuckle sending the crowd into a frenzy before flipping out of the ring, walking up the ramp and disappearing from sight.
