Were heading home

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-10-03 Moscow, Russia Late Evening Undisclosed Location In all reality, it has been some time since The Syndicate has appeared on television or cut a promo for the fans and roster. In reality, they have been recuperating from their triple threat tag team match against The New Network and Bodysnatchers. In this match, the extremely lovely and sexy Heartbreak Chick was tossed off the platform down to the concrete below and Big Daddy Dreamer was stabbed in the shoulder blade by a damn ice pick from Jason Realms. This of course, does not sit well with either one of them. So while at an undisclosed location somewhere in Moscow, Russia, The Syndicate team, joined by Miss Extreme: Katarina and several members of the Elite Russian FSS are sitting around a table, talking business. BDD: So General Karkov, you need our assistance in ridding Moscow of your little gang problem. What’s the matter, too many of your loyal subjects swayed to the other side? ‘Alas its true I’m afraid, money plays a lot to these people. Putin is trying, I’ll give him that much, but its becoming unbearable around here.’ MEK: It can be done, but are you willing to pay our prices for these services. ‘What are you asking of us?’ HBC: Simple, we want $100 million for the service, guaranteed exclusive rights to the city for several products we’ve been tinkering with, and that Club Duck place I’ve heard so much about. ‘Ahhh the infamous Club Duck, I think all those things can be arranged.’ BDD: One more thing, we want the training facility in Angola and Siberia that our colleague Draven was in at. ‘That, my friends, I don’t know if I can do.’ MEK: Well if you want our services, and become mayor of this town, I suggest you do it, otherwise we walk, and you have another nasty little scandal on your hands. You think the human rights commission would like to know about your involvement in Afghanistan? Perhaps the Hague might. ‘Alright, its agreed.’ HBC: Always a pleasure General. Now if you will excuse us, we have some matters to attend to Stateside. Who is going to give us a lift to Club Duck? I want to see the place for myself before we go. ‘They will.’ HBC: Excellent After a short thirty minute gaunt through the streets of Moscow, The Syndicate and Katarina pull up to Club Duck, the FSS guards push aside the doormen as they enter surveying the scene of pure unadulterated debauchery. Cage dancers, sex on the floor, liquor flowing everywhere. HBC: ‘My kind of place, now get those people out of my personal suite so we can chat.’ The guards rush in and forcibly remove the patrons from the VIP box, they protest but the sight of submachine guns make them think twice. One muddles, a guess they are under new owners again. As they finally move into their plush private box, they scan the scene again and begin to talk about business in Evo. And ultimately, their grand plans for vengeance. As they begin, a pretty young waitress serves them up the finest Russian Vodka. Katarina dismisses her away so they begin. MEK: As I’ve watched the past few weeks, Evo has been doing some strange and bizarre things, most notably, trying to off the both of you. You were tossed through a table, you were stabbed all the while S. sat back and did nothing. But in reality, this goes all the way back to you two nearly single handedly demolishing the Unlimited. But now, they really aren’t a force anymore, though S. might, and probably is still pissed at you both. Why else would all of a sudden, these new teams come into the mix, why else, all these new female athletes come into Evo? Simple, to steal your thunder. You’ve been at the top of the game for seven years now. Seven years, The Syndicate has existed, kicked ass from one fed to another, but here. Sure C, 2-time Tag champ and High-Impact champ, sure you D, former Continental Champ, but both of you should be ruling this place. As I look at it, you got Seara, someone whom you defeated every time C, gunning for all these titles that you deserve. All of a sudden, SkyNight, Fiona, and others are coming in to uproot the First Lady of Evolution. You going to let that happen? What of your plans to become the first ever Female Universal Champion. You think your going to get that by playing S. games. NO! And you D, you beaten Frost twice I’m aware of and he’s getting Universal shots, where are yours. Where are yours C? Basically people, if you want things done right, your going to have to do it yourself. Remember back in EDW, unrestricted warfare! Anything goes, everyone in Evo seems willing to play that way against the both of you, yet for some god forsaken reason, your not doing it. What, the fans, the posters, the promos getting to you? Haven’t you learned, the both of you can do anything you want, and they’ll still love you for it. You know it, I know it, they know it. However, the first thing you two need to do is take care of that little puissant team belonging to Miss Network and redeem the both of yourselves. Failure is not an option people. We have things to do, prepare our things gentlemen, The Syndicate is heading home!
