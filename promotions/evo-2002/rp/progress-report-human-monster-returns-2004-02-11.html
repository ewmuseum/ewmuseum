Progress Report: Human Monster Returns

11 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-02-11 If you knew eVolution in it’s infancy then you certainly recognize the voice of Sovereign. It’s shaky, unstable and screams of a disturbed mind. I know his voice. I know it all to well. And I know that it’s been a while since you’ve hear mine. I’ve Narrated for Sovereign for his entire career. I tried to give a glimpse into him and that’s proven to actually be really hard to accomplish. We all know that he’s insane. Sure, and I know what you new people might be thinking, how many ‘crazy’ freaks are there in the Wrestling Industry who pay there way through life acting like a maniac for the sake of their bills. I even would admit that most of them, the majority are quite convincing. But they can’t touch Sovereign. Because while people condemn this business calling it fake there isn’t a single person that is more real then William Forth, known to us better as Sovereign. For those who don’t remember it was I suppose 11 months ago that Sovereign and James Kimson locked horns in an epic battle. It was a Stretcher match and it was so personal that it was unable to contain itself in the ring, or even in the building. They battled for almost an hour back and forth. The bleed buckets, they battered and bruised themselves all for the sake of 1 thing... the love of a Woman. Leya Forth, she was born a Kimson. The youngest Daughter of Logan and Sarah Kimson and sister to James. Years before now she meet a young fan William at a wrestling event in Chicago. It’s a long story, but regardless of William’s obvious social issues the two fell in love and eventually married. Sovereign was born not long later as he spring boarded into a career in Wrestling, something that Leya was already involved in because of her brother James. The problem rose when it was apparent that William was obviously completely insane. He was violent, uncontrollable and even was arrested and placed in Mental Hospitals many times. When eVo was born Sovereign came calling and in fact won the Universal Championship as eVo’s first champion. He was out of control, violent, ending careers week after week. But when Leya, now who had seperated from Sovereign came back for her husband and joined forces with the most feared and hated man in Wrestling... James came to put a stop to it. James loved his sister, and he fought for her, to show her the error of her ways. But it only strengthened the bond between husband and wife. And so Sovereign and Kimson did bloody battle. The match was over and Sovereign was hurt, and hurt badly. He needed surgery going into the match on his Rotator Cuff due to his style. But the damage done to his body didn’t compare to the damage to his mind. He was off medication, the meds for his Mental problems conflicted with pain medication for his arm. He was thrown back into the Mental Ward in Chicago where he stayed for another 5 months. He was confined to a small room without a bed and a bucket to urinate and deficate in. He was heavily medicated, treated like an animal at times. He was submitted to electo-shock therapy every day and it was stewing in his heart that all he wanted was revenge. I’ve always tried to show both sides of the coin with Sovereign. In fact most of everything he’s done he cannot be blamed for. But then there are the times when you know that he’s in control. His games, how he manipulates you. He’s not to be trusted he’s not to be crossed. Look at what happened to Asis? Sovereign probably wanted nothing to do with him, but only to hurt Kimson. Asis hurt Leya... so Sovereign hurt Asis. What’s the come of that is unknown. But I’m willing to bet good money that the only thing on Sovereign’s mind is James Kimson... and how bad he wants to finish his career once and for all. The Following is the report handed to eVo’s Personal Representative last week. It’s a summery of events that would sum Sovereign’s last 11 months. The first 5 months are quite detailed as Sovereign was actually instituionalized at the time. However the later 5 months are far less detailed as he was released, but was under the condition of his release forced to meet twice a Month with a Doctor. Entry 1: Week 1 and 2 Subject was admitted to the Inpatients warn at Chicago General at 1200 hrs by Orderlies and armed Police escort. The patient was eratic and highly volitile despite heavy medication after surgery to his right shoulder. The patient was placed in a level 1 secure room and kept under Class 1 watch. The Patient slept with assistance of medication however he was restless, often speaking in his sleep. Entry 2: Week 3 through 5th Patient was kept under high level of Security alert after incident with Male Nurse. The Nurse entered the room to perform a physical exam when the Patient startled him attacking him in the room. Thankfully Security arrived before to much damage was done. The Nurse was taken to Emergency and treated for bruises and damage to his Throat after it appears the Patient attempered to strangle him. The Patient refuses treatment or physio therapy for his Shoulder. Doctors are concerned that he will require more surgery if he does not go through with the Physio. The Patient continues to sleep restlessly talking to himself over and over again. Common Phrases spoken...” Jimmy, Jimmy, Jimmy” the patient also calls for his Mommy fairly often as well as something to do with Humpty Dumpty. Most of what he says is non-sense and is attributed to his growing Dementia. Entry 3: Weeks 6-12 The Patient has progressed in his metal aquitiy. He is kept in a secure room and under constant watch but is allowed now to eat in the dining area only if other Patients are not there. This stems from an incident when we first tried to introduce him into a population of other people. The patient lashed out grabbing a plastic knife and attempted to assault one of the other patients while screaming. Neither patients were unharmed. Mr. Forth was confined to his room again but his behaviour improved remarkably, began to sleep soundly, no longer required a secure jacket when taking medication. Entry 4: Week 13 and 14 The Subject’s behaviour was improving remarkably. He began taking the Doctor’s recommended treatment for his Shoulder and was decided to allow him a visitor, namely his Spouse. The meeting went without incident as they were not allowed to be alone however he was given some breathing room as he called it. After the Souse left his condition rapidly declined. The Patient managed to escape his room and attack several patients and one nurse who was sent to the ER with major trauma to the head and back. The remaining time in this period the Subject was restrained to his room and heavily medicated while the Hospital decided what to do with him. Entry 5: Week 15 The Hospital searched for a proper transfer for the Subject however no other local or State hospitals would take him. Charges were dropped by fellow Patients, the injured Nurse tendered her resignation. Entry 6: Week 16-18 The Subject began to improve again. New medications were having a profound effect on him. However unfortunately his insurance was running out. The Spouse was apparently unwilling to pay more. The Subject was growing very discontent with his surroundings, however with the new Medication he was given his own room now, and taken off of 24hr watch. Entry 7: Week 19 The Subject improves on his own. The new meds mixed with counciling are having a huge effect upon him. A plan is formulate for his release. Entry 8: Week 20 The Subject is slated for release in 4 weeks based upon continued improvement. Subject is offered visitors twice a week. His Spouse visits on Monday, his Employer visits on Thursday. Both are uneventful meetings, Subject’s cheer improves Entry 9: Week 20 and 21 Subject has relapse. Destroys his room and is found in the corner wrapped in blankets rocking back and forth speaking non-sense again. However the subject did not resist being placed back in his old room. After 2 days in Seclusion was reintroduced to the population and no further incidents were recorded. Entry 10: Weeks 22 through 24 The subject was doing quite well and despite his short relapse was granted his release. The rest of the time spent was productive as he seemed to take the medication. He underwent a second surgery on his Left shoulder as his commitment to Physiotherapy. The Subject was released under the care of his Spouse on September 2nd 2003. The Subject now was on a bi-monthly check up. These are brief notes. Meeting 1: The Subject arrived with his Spouse and spoke at length about family problems with his Brother in Law. Blamed him extensively for what had happened to him in the prior 6 months. He however made no mention of seeking revenge and instead looked forward to a functional normal life. Spoke at length about a career in Business, possibly opening his own store, however it was obvious that he was not fully functional at the time. Often times during the meeting he would suddenly change the subject to something totally random. Example 1: Doctor: “So William, how do you feel about the medications?” Forth: “I think that they’re okay. Hey, have you ever seen that show called MASH. It’s pretty good. I like that they all wear Green. Green’s my second favorite color.. Next to red.” Example 2: Doctor: “William, now that you have been released how do you think your marrage will progress. Forth: “I don’t know, I guess good. You know I once had a picture frame just like that one. Is that a baldpoint pen or a fountain pen? I once stabbed someone with a Fountain pen once, that didn’t go over well with the Doctors. You’re a doctor aren’t you?” Meeting 2: The Subject began to test the boundries of his physical advantages by making semi-threatening or alluding to phsyical harm to other individuals. However it is unsure if the Subject was testing the resolve of the Doctors or if he was in fact considering causing harm to someone else. His brother in law came up on several occasions by him and not by prompting from the Doctor Meeting 3 It appeared as though the Subject was considering reentering Sports Entertainment as he was several minutes late after meeting with one Daniel Somers who apparently was in Chicago at the time working for the Subject’s last line of work. ProWrestling was a hot topic in the meeting. Mr. Forth described at length the things he had done to other men on national television and that he reveled in it. He spoke even more this time about his Brother in law, it was revealed to the staff that his Brother in Law was also a Pro Wrestler and their one and only match was the cause of Forth’s last stay in Hospital and 2 surgeries on his Right Shoulder. Meeting 4: Mr. Forth’s behaviour was very eratic at this meeting and it was discussed that he return to the Ward for a brief stay. He was not fond of the idea and was begging to not go back. At one point security was called however the Subject’s Spouse arrived to calm down Mr. Forth. He was released back to her care after the meeting in hopes that he was not having a serious relapse. Meeting 5 and 6 Typical meetings. Not unlike the first 2 meetings where Mr. Forth communicated hopes for his future. Spoke at length about wishing to be a parent and was not impressed when the Staff suggested that he avoid parent hood due to his attitudes and his problems with temper. Meeting 7 Mr. Forth spoke at length about ‘working out’ and getting into ‘ring shape’. It was revealed that Forth had signed a long term contract with his former employer and was about to go back into Pro wrestling. The Doctors advised him against anything that could lead to violent behaviour and Mr. Forth commented that it was not going to be like it was before. Meeting 8, Final meeting Patient’s final meeting was uneventful. Physical exam proved him to be in excellent physical condition. The Doctors found it amazing that he was able to concentrate on one task at a time for so long to be able to work out. They gave him standard tests for intellect and memory of which he all passed with reasonable marks. Forth is given a perscription and told to follow it to the letter which he accepts. This final meeting was 2 weeks ago. Since then Sovereign signed a 3 year deal with eVo signed and offered by Leya Forth. Sovereign spent the last 2 weeks training and brewing in his home watching tapes of James Kimson’s matches from the last year. Most times Sovereign sat quietly and sometimes he would rock back and forth mumbling. But as we all saw at Tainted Love... The Human Monster is back. And according to my people... he will be at legends on the 18th. And that means all Hell is going to break loose.
