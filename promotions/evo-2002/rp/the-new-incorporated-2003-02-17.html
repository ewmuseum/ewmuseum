The New Incorporated

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-17-03 'I can't believe that you would actually challenge him to something like that? Is the Universal championship that damned important to you!' The voice of Seara can be heard screaming. The scene fades in and Seara is pointing at Frost and Frost is sitting in a chair with his feet up and his arms rested on his knees. He is just staring at her, and she continues her verbal assault. 'I love you Scott! And you might just throw your life away over a stupid piece of gold!' Seara yells at Frost. Frost just stares at her. 'If you die in that match, you prove that Shocka is too damned stronge for more than half of the eVolution roster AND I will lose you forever! You are the first person I have remotely felt this way about, and you are going to jeopardize your life and mine in doing this!' Seara continues to yell at Scott. Scott continues to stare at her, and she walks over to him and puts her hand on his arms and she looks at him deeply. 'Please talk to me. You not saying anything is doing nothing but making things worse.' Seara says calmly this time. Frost looks at her and then moves a little, and then stands up. 'It's not about the universal title anymore.' Frost says under his breathe. Seara looks at him. 'Than what is it about?' Seara says raising her tone of voice. Scott looks at her, and shakes his head and then points at her. 'This is about YOU!' Frost screams. 'What?' Seara asks. 'This is about L2J! This is about PRIDE! THIS IS ABOUT EVERYTHING THAT I HAVE BEEN GETTING EVOLUTION TO STAND FOR!' Frost screams at the top of his lungs. Seara stares at Frost now, but Frost calms down. 'Lets face it. Whether you want to admit it or not. You wanted me to change, and you know this isn't my old attitude about things. And I know you like this change. Hell, I LOVE THIS CHANGE! And having you ripping down my throat about wanting a title isn't going to make things better! Yes. The title is a bonus in kicking Shocka's ass in that Hell's Fury match. And just think, after that....I will be one more step closer to eliminating the Unlimited threat and you will be one step closer because you are the eVolved champion and you are with the next damned universal champion. Katherine, I know what I am doing!' Frost says, before pausing. Frost grabs her hand and looks at her. 'Let me do this. I have let you do everything you've wanted when it came to Xander. I did what I had to do with Xander when the time came. Do the same thing for me.....' Frost finishes. Frost walks over to the door of the room, and grabs a jacket and leaves. Seara sits down on the chair and breathes heavily and then looks at the door....
