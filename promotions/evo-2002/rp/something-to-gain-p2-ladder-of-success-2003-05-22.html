something to gain p2 - ladder of success

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-22 Climbing the ladder of success has never been meant in such a literal term for four superstars. Malcolm needs this title to keep his career down the right path…Dane and Profusion need this title to burst out of the rookie light so they can establish themselves as legitimate talent in eVo…and Hedges needs this to keep his career advancing and claim a piece of gold here in Evolution. It’s a ladder with a title above it…it’s promotion hanging above it…Four men will lay there career and bodies down on line this Sunday to fight for the gold and there can only be one winner and that’s the man with the most desire to win and the goods to bring it home… The scene opens back up outside the old warehouse where Hedges is standing at the door. He grabs hold of one of the boards in front of the door and gives it a tug. One side rips from the wall. He yanks the other side out and throws it to the side. He pulls the other two off and tosses them aside. Hedges takes a step back and kicks the door with a mighty blow and it flies open stirring up a cloud of dust which can be seen fluttering out threw the open door. Hedges takes a step inside. Inside old chains run above on the rafters. A few old table and counters with odds an ends sit untouched for years covered in dust. Rats stir form here and there as Hedges walks in. the building is an eerie orange glow from the setting sun… “You know…”Hedges begins to speak “This Sunday is a big match for the four contenders. Profusion and Dane are handed shots right out of the gate. My assumption is they want to impress Tyler or S., whoever will control the company after From the Ashes. They received the shot the real question is what can they do with the shot. Will they let it swirl down the shitter or will they grab the opportunity. I fully expect a great effort to win to be put forth by these men. Will it be enough…In my opinion hell no. They are stepping in the ring against the all-knowing Supreme Being.” Hedges pacing around the abandoned building picks up about a three and a half foot piece of rebar and smacks a pole. It hits with a thud… “Kind of made you nervous didn’t it guy. Like the building would fall…” The camera guy shakes yes… “Though the building is weak and worn a bit more than a blow will be needed to make it crumble which is why I’m not selling Mr. Xavier short. I won’t underestimate him so he can’t pull more of the sneaky shit and slither away from my grasp again…” Pausing to scratch his chin Hedges looks around him… “You know enough about Malcolm. I have two other opponents…first this Profusion fellow…We seem to have a common enemy in Xero. That’s where our similarities stop. I didn’t need you to come down Wednesday on legends but it was a welcome bashing on Xero I must say. He’s cost me more than one match. Now it seems you feel you aren’t getting the respect you think you deserve here in eVo. I figure you are going to be coming at this match with a certain determination. Like you have something to prove. You’re an attention fiend. You want to be noticed. In your old fed you where looked at as a proud champion and he come here with your hopes and dreams of glory and you get looked over like a pile of dog shit. You’ve got a prime opportunity to jump out of the new talent coming in here and make a name for yourself. Grab some gold and be thrust back into your comfort position where you feed off attention and limelight because you probably have horrible low self worth. That’s just my opinion and I could be wrong…HA I doubt that…You should have stuck to hockey you pansy.” With that Hedges eyes a rat sitting on a table. Staring at him with beady white eyes. Twitching his whiskers in a coked out speedy rat sort of way. Hedges raises the bar ever so slowly and smashes down on the rat hard. A quick squeak is heard, as the rat remains motionless on the table. Blood runs out of the rat’s head onto the dusty table. He laughs a sickly laugh and goes about walking around the warehouse. “You know this Dane fellow reminds me a lot of a rat. He seems quick…strung out. He’s been watching and awaiting his opportunity for his chance to strike, his chance to survive. He lets some woman come into his house and leech off him with a kid. What a sucker. No wonder he pops pills. He’s been lurking though in the shadows. Waiting to emerge from poverty to earn his keep for the gold digger. What better way than to by winning the evolved title?” Hedges starts to slowly walk towards the corner of the warehouse and with another whack a rat flops of the ground and hits against…motionless… “HAHAHA suckers can’t get away from me…” Hedges continues his search around the warehouse for rats as the scene fades away to black…
