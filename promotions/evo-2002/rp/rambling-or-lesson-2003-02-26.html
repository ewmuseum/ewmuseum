Rambling? Or Lesson?

26 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-26-03 Everyone has a dark side, and everyone has a side full of hope and glory. You are the one who has the power to choose absolute darkness, or purity in light. Which one will you choose? Will you choose the side of darkness that has the ability to plenish you hunger for revenge, a hunger that every person strives for more than once in their life. Will you have the ability to take to the light and conquer your deepest darkest thoughts. Do you have the ability to survive struggling the pre-verbal battle between good and evil? You life is your choice, your faith is your opinion. But as of today, your choices come with friends. Darkness, feeds you to the Unlimited. Goodness feeds you to the path in which Darkness strives to destroy. Darkness seems to be the stronger force in the sports entertainment, so let me ask you. Do you perfer the dark over the light? Are you a confused soul, that needs a flash light to find our your way through our darkness. But you still rely on the light to make it through? Do you think that your meaningless efforts to thwart our cause are effecting us in any shape, form, or way? We are the Unlimited, and you are all nothing more than a mere stepping stone in the battle between good and evil. I am secure in my opinion, in my faith of darkness. I enjoy the company of my leader, Daniel Somers. The company of my cousin Xander, or The Demonic; Asis. But something in me, tells me that my faith is much stronger than theirs. As if, it is calling me and begging me to move on. But to where? All I know is that time will tell all, now I have to worry about bigger fish to fry. Nik VanCore, Yoshii Tanaka. They have attempted to twart my faith, but in their eyes they see it as the noble thing to do. Deep down, they are acting as the pre-verbal flashlight for eVolution by trying to defeat Unlimited. But the team is much stronger and wiser. We defeated Malcolm King, finally. He may of picked up win over me at Tainted Love. But still Darkness reigned and he is gone, for good. All of this ramble, is my chosen words to do many things. Have I confused you? Have I taught you a lesson? Will you listen to my opinion and accept my faith for darkness? Are you part of Unlimited at Heart? It's your time to decide, and remember to bring a flash light......Darkness covers all.
