lies deception finally the truth part 2

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-14-03 [Destruction is right around the corner, only days away for Shocka. New-TV is fed up with lies and childish games. To bad Shocka doesn’t seem to know when things go from professional to stupid. Shout insults at Hedges? Tell lies? Big mistake and once again I will prove why I am the all-knowing Supreme Being to you] The scene opens up back in the room this time Thug Roc is there with Hedges. They are having a few beers and discussing this Wednesday. The camera comes back in and Hedges looks up and laughs. Waving a tape into the air… “I found it…HAHA the infamous Cross-Shocka locker room incident of RDWF…” Hedges exclaims…”Maybe this means nothing but anyone who knows the past knows that it caused Shocka to snap and after this tape was aired peace between us was shaky and crumbled forever when later on I turned on him and robbed him of RDWF glory” “Perhaps one of the funniest segments I’ve ever heard. Mind games it’s pure genius. This one gets not only under his skin but grabs his balls and squeezes the little raisins…HAHAHA” Popping the tape into the TV the scene opens up backstage of and old RDWF show. Hedges is walking with a former diva known as Meghan. They walk down the hall and they hear noises coming from a locker room door with no name on it. Hedges stop and listens as he hears two voices…One sounds like it could be Shocka’s and the other one sounds like it may be Cyriss Cross’s but Hedges can’t tell…) Voice 1(Shocka maybe?)- Are you sure we should be doing this hear maybe we should get a room ro something…. Voice 2(Cross maybe?)- No No No it’ll be fine trust me. You said you wanted to be my partner and now we are finally getting a chance to be real partners… Voice 1- Well if you say so…Have you ever done this before? Voice 2- Of course I have lots of times…Just be quiet and pretend it’s a sucker or something. Voice 1- Ok if you’re sure that everyone who teams up does this but I’ve never done it before… Voice 2- Look where all your old partners are now…Not hear with you…If we do this we could be together forever… Voice 1- I want that…Ok lets go… Hedges hears noises that make him want to vomit he stares at Meghan with the same blank stare They both shake their heads in disbelief but they continue down the hall and walk back into their locker room awaiting this evening match up against Shocka Popping the tape out Hedges and RoC are laughing uncontrollably. Their faces are bright red as RoC slaps Hedges a high five. They settle down and Hedges begins talking again… “You know the old antics and the fact that your shoulder was never injured. You would have backed out and you know it. Every time a match contract was proposed to you with my name you shuttered to think of facing me again. The ratings to see your ass finally get stomped where unbelievable. You hate me…resent me because you where the top dog in every fed until I came along. Countless times it has happened and you can’t stand it…Stand the fact that I am that much better than you. It’s like when the homerun record was broken. It was said the feat couldn’t happen again…Then 5 times in 3 years it was broken. Once you prove something can be done it makes it easier for people to believe it can. When I beat you repeatedly, everyone else did and you should cowboy up to it” “You know I wonder why Shocka can’t admit when he’s beat…”RoC Asks “Never has there been a man able to beat me more than twice. Only one and deceitful tactics where used but I can admit a man named Yogi beat me. He was he better man I can admit that. I have the balls if you can beat me consistently then you know what more power to you. I’ve never had trouble with Shocka in my life. I’ve dominated every match we’ve been in from start to finish and this time shall be no different…” “You remember that Omega comment he made?” RoC asks “That load of shit…I recruited Omega for the team. I thought he had a sadistic side to him that I nurtured with Dark Justice and he grew uncontrollably. He is huge in this business now. If we where to asks him who helped jumpstart his career my name would be there not Shocka’s and that’s a plain fact that Shocka feels the need to lie about. No matter what it is Shocka isn’t better than me at no matter if he admits it or not. Some people just don’t know when to quit but after Wednesday Shocka will wish he did a long time ago…” Roc and Hedges laugh and go backing to watching more tapes of Shocka stressed and confused at how he can beat the greatest enemy of his career.
