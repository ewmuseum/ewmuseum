Deathly Fallings

22 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-22 It's called 'snow' by a lot of people, yet it more closely resembles ash, the blacks, whites and greys all mixing to form what looks like the deathly fallings of a once erupted mega-force of the natural world. A deadly cloud to fall and carpet the world and asphyxiate us all. But that's going off topic somewhat. The main point is, it's not snow, nor does it resemble it. It's just static on the screen of a television set. The static that everybody who's watching that channel can see. Some believe it is just the station and turn over, happily watching one of the meaningless, staged talk shows that flood the airwaves, merrily viewing what they believe to be the breakdown of someone's life, their emotions reflecting how the studio audience is being encouraged to react. Maybe they watch a reality T.V show, where those sitting in a house can watch people sitting in a house, a reflection of what we as people find entertaining. Others turn the television off entirely, taking that extra time to kiss a loved one, or hold a child close to them and tell them those three simple words that all children endear to hear spoken; 'I love you.' Those that do leave the television to work its form of magic though are greeted by something unexpected. The snow starts to melt and a picture begins to form on the screen. The picture does not become completely clear for a while though, it takes a second for it all to come into focus. And even when it is completely in focus, the picture itself is still not clear. The image seems to have been filmed through a home video camera, although that is not important, what is important is the image being seen through it. Thick leaves are in front of the lens and from the movement of the camera you can tell that whoever is filming is standing in a bush. The camera moves forward a bit, pushing the leaves out the way, giving a better view of what the intended target is. It’s a sunny afternoon, sunlight slides in between the leaves of the bush. It’s not a live feed. It’s 9pm in the real world. A house can be seen, or more specifically, one side of the house. It’s only a two story house and whilst not being a mansion, it is rather luxurious. The side of the house that can be seen seems to be the main living room and the kitchen. The living room is seen through a massive set of bay windows whilst the kitchen has more moderately sized windows, so you could see a person from the waist up. Inside the living room you can see a young boy, sitting cross-legged on the floor, transfixed by some program on the television. In the kitchen stands a beautiful woman, chopping vegetables on the worktop, a chore you don’t see many women do now days, let alone one that clearly has enough wealth to employ someone to do it for them. What a quaint scene. The voice is somewhat of a surprise, you almost couldn’t believe this kind of a moment to be ruined by sound. The voice itself is hard, unforgiving, not natural. I would’ve killed to have a childhood like that. Well, I tried. The voice has been edited after the filming has been done. It’s been done with such skill that you couldn’t tell if the voice was male or female, yet all that hear it find something chillingly familiar about it. The camera now zooms in on the young boy sitting on the floor of his living room, innocently watching whatever childhood program it is that will just be a memory one day. With a head of unkept dark hair as seems to be the fashion with young children now days, he stares into the television, his big dark eyes almost glazing over as you watch. He’s the spitting image of his dear old Daddy, he really is. Congratulations. I wonder if he’d look the spitting image of his Father if he were beaten to a pulp and gasping for his last breath? The camera moves away from the innocent child in the living room and pans across to look at the woman standing in the kitchen, presumably preparing dinner. Congratulations on her as well. You’ve done well. She’s beautiful isn’t she? Beautiful in the way that supermodels aren’t, beautiful in the natural way. I can imagine seeing her only just fresh from sleep in the morning, and still having that radiant glow. Her smile lighting up the room in the way that the early morning sun breaking through the window can’t. Her long dark hair flowing around her enticing figure as she gracefully slides out of bed. Again, I wonder how beautiful she’d look after her corpse had spent two weeks at the bottom of a lake? I wonder how her hair would flow then? Whether her smile would be as radiant. Whether her skin would still look as beautiful when it was half eaten away? In the house you see the beautiful woman put down what she is doing and walk into another room behind the kitchen. When she comes back into the kitchen she is carrying a young child in her arms, probably no more than 18 months old. Ahhh of course, I almost forgot about the young one. She’ll be the spitting image of her Mother. You can see the beauty in her already. The smile is already starting to look like her Mother’s. That’s if she gets the chance to grow up to her Mother’s age. Just as the voice finishes speaking, you can see the woman go through to the living room, holding the young one in her arms, going to check on the boy watching television. The camera zooms in one the three of them, smiles all round, like a snap shot of the American dream. Are you proud of all you’ve achieved? Do you like living the way you live? More importantly, do you enjoy your family living? If you want it to continue that way, you know what to do. If you value life, especially that of others, I suggest you do it before it’s too late. As the voice finishes speaking, the picture fades out, to leave nothing but ash, blowing around the screen.
