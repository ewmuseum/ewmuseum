Double Vision

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 17th, 2003 What could be coming next they asked? Each and every week it’s always The Unlimited now that seem to be in complete and total control of the lives and the very well being of every single man, woman, and in Shocka’s case thing that steps into that ring. Now it all begins to change. Except for the thing about Shocka, he’s still out of luck. But on Wednesday night The Unlimited claim to have a plan. They think that they are just going to march into the Cow Palace in San Francisco and force Strictly Prohibited to unmask himself and reveal to the world the form of Tyler Davidson. Maybe they are right and that will be what happens. Then again who is to say they will be able to force anything out of anyone? Strictly Prohibited and I will not go down without a fight and we will certainly not be forced out of our plan by The Unlimited. They will find soon enough that the goal with which they hold the greatest desire for is so much closer than they could have ever possibly imagined. And it yet it still remains far out of their reach. What isn’t out of our reach however are the Tag Team Titles which will be ours come Sunday night perhaps at the expense of The Unlimited themselves. However it goes down I have a feeling that The Unlimited just might be seeing double by the end of the week.
