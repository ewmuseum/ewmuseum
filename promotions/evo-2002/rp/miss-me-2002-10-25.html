Miss me?

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Malcolm King sits in a room surrounded by darkness. On his right shoulder is the OSW World Heavyweight Championship a title he won during an epic fued with Big Grumpy. On his left shoulder the EDW Universal Title a belt he held three times before the federation decided to close doors....before the federation felt the need to Evolve. 'The origins of Evolution....the origins of the Unlimited, are both deep rooted to the legacy of one man....two titles...and three wrestling organizations. First and foremost did you like seeing me back on Evolution TV? I must admit...it was a rush to back in the arena, to hear the fans...to shake the hand of the man that forced me to retire.' the camera pans and focuses onto the OSW World Heavyweight Championship..... 'I take nothing away from the accomplishment of Xander. You single handedly stole the career, the legacy of one of the great performers this company has ever witnessed. And Xander far be it from me to take away from that feat by finding aloophole to get me back into Evo as an active wrestler. You see during my forced sabatical I had a lot of time to think......and I came to the conclusion that I had underestimated Xander, his desire to be the best. A mistake mind you that won't happen again. You see I was David to his Goliath; one man versus an army; and the army won. Or did it? Xander you should have finished the job......but you couldn't. You should have used The Unlimited a little better during my time away...but now it's too late. Xander I almost have pity for you, because every other emotion is clouded by my hate for you.' the camera pans to the EDW Universal title...... 'Asis my friend you chose the wrong night to join the Unlimited. Not many men join what is in essence on the endangered species list, unfortunately like many of your career decisions you don't think these matters through enough...a shame really. You see Asis; Xavier Citizen was all about collecting a check....paying off some medical bills, and dying. So what do you do? You fuck it up...excuse my language, but quite honestly I would have rather faced a very accepting Echo Blackwhisper, than a pissed off Xavier Citizen. And we have Jinx, you were introduced to my protegee Luther Wisdom. You too committed career suicide months ago..when you turned on me during that tag match and joined the Unlimited. Did you honestly believe that action would go unpunished?' the camera pans out to show both belts and Malcolm King..... 'The OMEN Brotherhood (Citizen/Wisdom) are the next wave from the Nation (Urban Legend Wrestling Nation). Our goal initially is to correct the mistake which is The Unlimited...after that restore law to lawlessness. And before you ask....no I am not returning to the ring, but I plan to provide the Brotherhood with something I never had the benefit of; I will be their eyes and ears, and trust me you don't want a mind like mine steering that ship.' as the camera pans out.....Malcolm says something very cyptic.... 'The Unlimited may recruit members..yet missed on the top free agent out there, that is what had prevented from The Unlimited making Evolution a 'Sovereign State' ' in the distance a slight chuckle can be heard....but isn't William in control of Sovereign now, what can Malcolm King be up to or is this another example of misdirection?
