A lesson.

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.20.02 Road to Glory {The road to glory is a long road indeed traveled by many and won by few. Those who win are those who succeed in making it to the rise of the Legends where a true winner will be determined. Many familiar faces have been entered into this tournament former Continental champion Shocka is one of them. But as of late he seems that he is not in the mood to take himself seriously enough to fight in this tournament and even care what he does in it. But maybe it’s just a cheap ploy by him to try and make everyone think that he is not ready to spread the word that he isn't. Allowing him to get the upper hand in the match when his opponents under estimate him. Seems like to much work just to get a little edge but perhaps that’s what it takes. Shocka has been down this road before in the EdW sure it didn't pan out in the end as he was eliminated early on but this time around you could say that he is one of the favorites to win this match. If you asked him he would surely tell you that wouldn't he. And he does have an alliance behind him but that does not mean that he will use it to his advantage to try and win the match. But he could if he needed and that’s all that he needs to glorify his hopes in this tournament. He is a new man…he has been redeemed in his debts. He has been renewed and now he enters the tournament with a less than challenging challenger. Kai Kua a man that thinks he is something big…but he will simply be destroyed by Shocka. When Shocka has confidence he is great but when he has cockiness he is even better. His first match will be a breeze but his second is undetermined of who he will face. In his mind he is preparing for Big Daddy Dreamer but that doesn’t mean that is who he is facing.} The tag tournament {Shocka has also opened and is about to close a new chapter in his career he is going to be fighting for the tag team titles with his partner Jason Steele. This match could prove to be a warm up for the road to glory or it could put a stop to Shocka’s momentum all together. It could serve as a stepping stone in things to come or simply drop him from contention in the Road to Glory tournament.} ~~Shocka sits in his hotel room flipping through channels it’s about noon and he is lost in the television. He could be training right now but is simply not in the mood. His cell phone that is at his side rings and he looks down at it for a moment before he decides to answer it.~~ ‘Hello?’ Shocka questions. ‘Hey this is Daniel S.…’ ‘What?’ Shocka again questions. ‘Yeah its Daniel.’ ‘DANNY BOY!’ Shocka screams. ‘Please don’t call me that…’ Daniel speaks. ‘Why not it’s the only pleasure that I get every day…’ ‘Listen, I’m not in the mood for games and you know that we have a less than favorable past together but I need your help…’ ‘What do you want now? Wasn’t being the longest reigning Continental champion enough for you?’ Shocka says with a laugh. ‘Are you going to cooperate or not?’ Daniel says. ‘I guess…I got nothing better to do.’ Shocka says again smiling. ‘Well listen Scott Frost wants you to keep New-TV away from the ring…he wants no interference if you two make it to the finals in the Road to Glory….’ Daniel says speaking intensely. ‘Why in the hell would I want them at the ring in the first place?’ Shocka screams into the phone. ‘I dunno..’ ‘You tell Scott Frost that I want the damn Notorious to stay the fuck out of this match to.’ ‘Yeah…I’ll be sure to tell him that.’ ‘You better because if you don’t its going to be your ass.’ Shocka yells. ‘Yeah…Frost said the same thing…’ Somers says. ‘And your security guards won’t stop me like they probably did Frost to…the little bitch.’ Shocka screams. ‘Actually Frost beat them up…’ ‘No he didn’t…’ ‘Yes He did…’ ‘Bullshit.’ Shocka says. ‘He took out Justin Famous and Deacon…’ ‘And you think that scares me?’ Shocka asks. ‘No…I was just informing you…’ ‘Yeah well you don’t forget to tell him what I said…since Imma kick his ass in the finals if he makes it that far of course.’ FADE
