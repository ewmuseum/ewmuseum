Dream or Reality

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 9th We find ourselves in Tampa Bay Florida, the sky is dark and the winds howl down small passageways between buildings, but Seara is quite at home here, she has found a dreary back room which she has nestled into. As we enter the room we notice her laying on the bed, her trench coat wrapped tight around her, a few candles light on a small cabinet by her bed. The cover is pulled up to her waist, but it seems to serve no purpose, as the female wrestler can be seen with a sweat on her face. She doesn’t lay still for long, and sitting bolt upright she takes off her trench coat, drops it next to her shoes on the floor and lays back down, never once opening her eyes. It has been hard to track the female as of late, and she has begun to disappear for nearly a week at a time before suddenly calling and asking for a camera to be present at whatever location she chooses. It has become apparent now that she is no longer using the large black pick up truck, but as of yet, no one knows what she is using as transport. She pulls the covers up to her shoulders and begins to toss and turn in the bed, obviously having a bad dream of some sort. For years it has been just us Seara, we have been together, we have been happy, but now, now what, we are separate, we are no longer one, you have changed, your different somehow, I feel as though I don’t really know you anymore and I don’t understand what is wrong. Seara answer me, please, your fading away, please talk to me, you always said you would be here for me, but your not, Seara, what is happening to me. Remember when we were inseparable, like one and the same thing, Seara, why did you change, why did you leave me, wasn’t I good enough for you, did I do something wrong, please Seara, why are you like this, I could have given you everything. But you couldn’t stay with me, why Seara, why?. Things are blurry and Seara can barely make out the figure of her ex boyfriend talking to what appears to be another female, but from where Seara is standing it looks like he is talking to herself. But surely it seems to be impossible that she could be both there and here at the same time, for Seara things have become confusing as of late, and this is another thing to add to her list. I never chose to leave you, it happened, I needed to leave the fed to become more, but I never wanted to leave you, I loved you, I was always going to be with you, but I lost you. We are still one, you are here now, and you will always be here. I am staying with you this time, we will be one again. Seara’s words are directed at whoever decides to listen, but she is not sure who she is really talking to anymore. The battle over supremacy in her mind has become a war, with herself and the thoughts she has been having. Ask Seara and she would tell you that the thoughts are not hers, they have a different sound, they say things she would try to never think, but regardless, they are there, telling her what to do, 24 hours a day. But not even Seara knows if she has been listening to them, doing what they say, saying what they tell her and when they tell her too. Maybe the strange feelings she was beginning to have were their fault, maybe they were pushing her to act in this strange unpredictable way. No you don’t control me, we were never one and the same. Seara’s words penetrate hard into her mind, and she looks ready to say something important, but she doesn’t do anything except stare hard at the image of herself. Taking a step forward she squints her eyes to adjust her vision, and makes out the face of the image, it is her, she gasps quickly and steps further back. She is not a person who has a problem with fear, and would normally beckon something towards her to try and absorb the deadline feelings it carries, but this is too much for the female, and she is clearly disturbed by the sight of herself. She continues walking backwards at a high speed before suddenly stopping lighting a cigarette and cocking her head to the side slightly, she stands with head tilted to the left for a moment, then tilts it to the right and continues to stare at her figure. You really are nothing for me to be scared of, you are just me, and if I hurt myself, you will be hurt as well, this is my advantage over you, I know what you think, what you feel, and why you feel the way you do, I am not stupid, not now, I was a moment ago, I was overcome with shock, but now, I am ok, I have regained myself, I know now why I am here. I came to confront myself, to ask you why I want to feel this way about him, why I have the urge to end his life so that I can keep him forever close. Death I know is something I seem to cherish, I know it is odd, I know you may not even know why, but I do love the sweet smell of death, it lingers in the air, holds like a note being sustained, and it is sweet, it has a sensation that makes your throat tingle. I hate him, I know how much I hate him, but I don’t understand why I can’t do it, I have the opportunity to kill him, I’ve had it so often, but I can’t do it, why not, what is wrong with me. Pausing she looks the face of herself as though she had never seen herself before, she carries on walking until she is nose to nose with herself, and waits to hear the voice talking to her. It is silent, and the lips can’t be seen moving, but Seara nods in agreement and occasionally repeats words in a shocked expression. So you are saying that in some sick twisted way, I am in love with him, oh god, I don’t believe you, it cant be true, but it would explain why I have not got the guts to do it anymore. Two weeks ago and I could have done it, but not now. The image begins to disappear and Seara starts to hum as she walks back in the direction she first came. She sits down eventually and gently rocks back and forth in time to the tune she has chosen to hum. The tone of the song is moody and deep, and full or raw emotion, yet it is calm and slow. Pushing down on the bed Seara lifts herself up, she is breathing heavily and she is covered in a cold sweat. It is an unusual experience for Seara, and she seems slightly worried at first about what is going on in her mind. She knows she has been having conversations with herself before, but she never expected to be able to fall into a sleep and stand face to face with herself, talking about her wants to kill someone or her feelings that she is trying to suppress. Turning the cameraman leaves the room, leaving Seara alone sweating and in a dazed state, thinking hard about what happened to her only moments before.
