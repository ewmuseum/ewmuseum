Last Man Standing and Tanaka!

22 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-22 Amsterdam, Holland Walking along the North Sea The Syndicate, the premier team in the industry, the team that has literally done it all in this business, now finds themselves titleless and it doesn’t sit well with them. Big Daddy Dreamer, former Universal, Continental and Tag Team Champion. Very few in Evo can say that. The HeartBreak Chick, the first lady of Evolution, former High-Impact Champion and three-time Tag Champion are suddenly, titleless. Together they look Northward toward the North Sea, a violent raging storm is approaching and behind them, the city of Amsterdam. Roughly a million plus people live their and their only protection is a 19 mile long, twenty foot high dike keeping back the storm, the fury of the sea. Just like The Syndicate heart. Raging, uncontrollable, violent….. Last week, The Syndicate was robbed of the titles and it angered them. Though, The Frost’s put up a valiant fight, we do not blame them for the loss, nor Morephus, or even Tok’n. No, the blame lays squarely on one man. A mid-carder at best who grew power hungry. Yoshii Tanaka. The green misting freak is himself solely responsible for what happened. What’s the matter Yoshii, bitter you couldn’t defeat us when you had a team so know you decide to pull this stunt. It doesn’t matter Yoshii, for you will pay for what you did, I will see to it personally. Big Daddy Dreamer against Yoshii Tanaka, and lets have it in a traditional style Japanese match….. Say…. A glass coffin match? Winner puts there opponent through a glass coffin. Yeah, that will do nicely. So how about it Yoshii, you have the samurai spirit or are you a chicken shit pussy! Now this week, The Syndicate will take on the Frost’s once again in a Last Person Standing match, in which the only way to win is too beat your opponent senseless and knock them unconscious. A match that can end careers due to the brutality of it all. And for once, I would say this match actually favors Frost and Seara. Why do I say this, not because of their talent, not because of their ability, but merely because it is an attractive opportunity to some greedy team to try and collect the million dollar bounty on our heads. Face it, a million bucks is nothing to us but to the rest of Evo, that’s some serious change. So we must be ready for anything, fortunately, we are. We have a proven track record so don’t ever bet against us. Now last week, the world was shocked when Seara made out with HBC in Evolution’s first ever lesbian kiss, and yeah, I’ll admit it C, it gave me a royal chubby. I got alittle wet too. Hehehehe. But this week….. Yeah, this week will be better, you know why D? Because we get to beat the living hell out of the golden couple. Exactly. Plan still on? Of course, we eliminate Scott Frost, knock him out of commission and then have our way with his wife. A couple of hard right hands here and a deep tongue kiss their. I don’t even think this will be a wrestling match with Seara, more like a rough sex contest. I know, have you seen how unhappy she has been lately in her promo’s? I guess Frost isn’t making her happy, perhaps you should D. Perhaps, you hold her down, I’ll pin her and if I should just happen to manage to ‘slide into her’ then she’ll know why I am called Big Daddy D! Have mercy! ………………
