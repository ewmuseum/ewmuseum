I'm here waiting for you

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 23rd It’s only one day left until Rise of the Legends. The talk, the buzz has been about two things. The Road to Glory, and the Main Event. I, as narrator now for a while have tried to remain impartial. To remain un-bias. To be honest, I can’t wait to be a fan, I can’t wait to see what happens in Toronto this Sunday. I want to see what everyone does. I want to see L2J finally over come Sovereign, for him to leave the Universal champion. With that being said, I’ve been told through my own life that I’m a pessimist. I think that I am, because no matter how much I’d love to see L2J hold that title belt above his head standing on top of the Tier’s of Evolution Cage, I honestly don’t think it can happen. L2J might be fearless. L2J might be a hero, a role model, the man that could bring notorious to the fore front. To lead a war against New-TV and the Unlimited, not to mention the Brotherhood. But no matter how much I personally, and no doubt thousands and thousands of fans across the world right at this moment. No matter how much I’d love to have that feeling, that feeling of joy, and of success I don’t know if it is going to happen. I narrate for Sovereign’s promo videos. I do it because it’s my job here in Evolution. Camera crews refuse to film him, so he mails me the video tapes. I add a few comments, you know post production. I find Sovereign an amazing wrestler. No one can discount the fact that he’s been the most dominant Universal Champion in the history of the championship. He’s held the title in one form of the other for over 14 weeks, longer than anyone in history, and while L2J has co-held this title for the past 4 weeks you can’t help but get the feeling in your stomach that this Sunday L2J is going to be tossed into something he might be mentally ready for, but physically? We’ve seen him in matches like this with Sovereign. Sovereign dominated them all. Now, L2J hasn’t ever gotten a clean win over Sovereign. Sovereign, no clean wins over L2J (Unless you want to count Digital Destruction, the match that Sovereign won, however L2J wasn’t pinned by Sovereign). And the promise so far from all sides is that this match won’t have any screw overs. They both know what is going to happen inside that structure. 2 men, battling in a war. 2 men inside the double cage that hate each other with everything that they are. Two men, fighting for the ultimate prize in all of wrestling, and when it’s said and done, and the dust settles and one man is left standing there are going to be 21,000 plus fans in Toronto in that arena all crossing their fingers that it’s going to be L2J. I will be one of them. Crossing my fingers because I don’t know if there’s a hope in hell that he can do it. Sovereign is a monster. Sovereign is a beast, he’s a machine. His concept of pain is unique, unmatched. He has no respect for his body, yours or anyone else’s. Sovereign doesn’t know pity. He is a sadist and a masochist. Just as likely to do as much damage to himself as he is his enemy in an attempt to create his vision of his Crusade, the Crusade of Violence that lead him to the Universal Title. But like anyone in the past that’s held the Universal Title, go ahead and ask any of them and they’ll say it’s a drug. You can’t explain it, and even for someone as unorthodox in his thinking as Sovereign is, it effects him the same way. It’s the power. It’s the importance. Sovereign wants his Universal Title back, all for himself. L2J is a fighter. L2J is an underdog, he’s a man that has taken so much and kept coming back for more. The type of man that has gained the admiration of everyone in the industry. Wrestling publications rate L2J as one of the top stars today. He’s a high risk, high octane wrestler that is willing to go the distance for what he believes in. He believes in the Universal Title. He wants to hold it for his own. To take it back to his family, his home and to show it to his loved ones. To share it with the fans, to dethrone the man that has caused him so much pain and torment. I’ll probably be in the ACC wearing a L2J T-shirt hoping for the best. Hope being the key word. Sovereign takes on L2J for the universal title on November the 24th. I don’t like the sound of it. It makes me uneasy. To know what Sovereign might do to L2J sort of makes me light headed, weak to my stomach. Sovereign is to cold, to calculating. He’s to strong, to big. He’s to tough, he’s to violent. Not to discount L2J’s will. Not to discount that L2J can’t go toe to toe with Sovereign on many levels. But what do you think Sovereign has in his mind for Sunday? How much of this has already been apart of his plan? We saw it last month, L2J survived by the skin of his teeth and this time around he gets to look Sovereign right in the eye and wonder what the hell is going to happen. They both know what they’re up against. No one has wrestled Sovereign more times then L2J, but add the unpredictability of the Tier’s of Evolution match and we’re all left with questions. Most importantly, who is going to be the Universal Champion after Sunday night’s Pay Per View Event? If only I could see into the future and prepare myself. The Narrator trails off, the scene flows from the dark, blank screen to what must be the Air Canada Center. A wrestling ring, set up as eVo is getting the seating set perfectly, it’s all going to be torn down again tomorrow for the NHL hockey game between the Flyers and the Maple Leafs tomorrow night. But as of right now, the ring is up. The Rise of the Legends ring apron is beautiful, quite articulate and well crafted. Standing dead center of the ring, Sovereign. The shot, classic Black and White, the camera set up far off, zoomed in, you can’t really get that great a look, but you know for sure it’s Sovereign. His body posture, his frame, the hair in the face heavy breathing. The arena seems empty, seats folded up against each other placed in rows of a trolley. It’s only 1 day now L2J. One day until your time comes inside the Tier’s of Evolution. The Universal Title, now that sits on your lap in the hotel room your staying at down the street looks up at you crying. You look down at it knowing that this is it. Two days of getting to jump up onto the security railing and feeling that “special feeling” that you got the other day. Two days to bask in the glory of what it’s like to hold a meaningless title when it’s in your hands. Meaningless because it’s not yours. You have no right to it. You have no claim to it L2J. Lady luck was on your side and you are the personification of pure luck. I never understood the political side of this industry, why some people are favored over others. But you L2J, although are deserving of being in the lime light are sure as hell not more deserving of me in regards to the Universal Title. Yes, you have fans. Yes, you have the skills. Yes, you have every wrestling fan out there cheering you on tomorrow, with the exception of the very few who are fans of myself. I don’t know why they would be, but that’s not the point. You see L2J, you standing there, in the throngs of the masses, soaking in the glory doesn’t change a damn thing. Your sense of reality is still distorted. Thinking that kicks, and punches into dead air or onto heavy bags are going to do anything for you on Sunday is something to laugh at. Air doesn’t fight back. A punching bag won’t grab you around the throat. A speed bag won’t Pile-drive you off the ring apron. You know all that’s true L2J. If not, maybe you’re the one that’s crazy and I’m the sane one. But I am the crazy one L2J. Crazy enough to be licking my lips in anticipation of what I am going to get to do this coming Sunday in this ring. In this arena, with this structure suspended above the both of us. Without any rules, without any boundaries other then what our bodies can take. To expand the threshold of our own ability to block out the pain. Because there will be a lot of pain Sunday L2J. Not the sort of pain you’ve experienced before. Not the pain of the impact of your knuckles on the padded resistance of a punching bag. I’m talking about the sort of pain that would make the most disciplined man cringe and scream. Pain that would give Stephen King nightmares. The Pain that would make doctors scratch their heads wondering what they could do for you to ease your suffering. And when you hear that clang of the door of the cell being closed behind you L2J, and you look across from the ring and you see me, that’s when you are going to snap out of your little fantasy world and realize that you made the biggest mistake of your career. Not just your career... but of your life. You don’t know what the visual of seeing you holding that title does to my insides L2J. That was pain right there. To see a man like you holding the thing that I made famous. I made that Universal Title important, not you. Not S., not Davidson or anyone else. They through it into a pack of dogs and I was the toughest, I was the meanest and I wanted it more then the rest of them. You know that L2J, you were in that pack of dogs and I wrestled it from your teeth and slaped you with my paw and left you to sulk, a looser in the battle. Now, coming in just a day from now L2J you’ll loose the war. A war for the Universe. A war between two true warriors, it’s not a war about trash talking. There has been no witty interviews between us on Legends. There has not been any humor, no pure television. Just two men, that can’t stand the sight of each other, and after Sunday comes and goes there will be an end to it all. One of us will emerge a winner. A clear cut winner and leave Toronto Canada with the Universal Championship. Sovereign takes a step back, he then turns and begins to pace around. After a moment he stops suddenly, throws back the hair out of his eyes. His body with his side now facing the camera in a profile sort of shot. He chuckles to himself, facing what must be the entrance to the arena. You are going to walk out from that curtain L2J, looking down to this ring with me inside of it. Staring back at you, watching every move you make. You know that Body language is %80 of Human communication L2J? I can see in your posture, your eyes, your breathing how you’ll feel. The way you closed your eyes that day in the crowd was a look of “I better get this out while I still can” to enjoy it while it lasts. It’s like feeling the sun on your face on a beach, in the Bahama’s on your one week out of the year on vacation. Get out there, enjoy it, savor it. Because you know that it isn’t going to last for very long. You know deep down inside, and I can see in your eyes, on your lips, the shade of your skin. I see it all in you L2J, and when your crappy music hits this Sunday Night, with this arena filled with all shapes and sizes and genders of L2J shirt wearing zombies I’ll be here, waiting for you. Hoping that your ready for what we all know is going to happen. The blood bath, the climax of our little “feud”. The end to all the violence between you and I. Simply because after tonight, you won’t be able to take it anymore L2J. You won’t be able to stand the punishment any longer. You’ve put your body to the test, pushed it to it’s limits L2J, and tonight I will push it past that, further then you would even want to think about. I hope your ready, I hope your mind is centered, because I want this to be long. I want it to be long and painful and I want it to be the most violent thing I’ve ever done to another human being in my life. And L2J, you know my past, you know the sort of things I’ve done to very, very unfortunate people. You are about to get your chance L2J. To live up to the expectations, hell to get past the expectations of everyone who follows this company. You are the underdog in the minds of many people L2J. I know you think you aren’t an underdog. I know that you are capable. I know L2J that you are not a push over by any stretch of the imagination. But L2J, I know that you aren’t going to beat me on Sunday. L2J, I know that you won’t be the man to beat Sovereign, to cause my Title reign to end. I know L2J that you will fight your best, to the bitter bloody end. But come hell of high water I won’t stay down long enough by anyone’s hand for you to beat me. For you to be the Universal Champion by my defeat. Sovereign turns back facing the camera. Then you start to hear an odd sound, gears shifting. It won’t be long L2J, when you hear this sound again. To hear the sounds of the Tier’s of Evolution lowering down upon you. Trapped inside an unforgiving structure. Trapped inside with an unforgiving person. I will taste your blood L2J. I will break your body, break your spirit and break your heart all in the span of one match. One epic encounter. One last stand. One more night in the ring with the Human Monster. One last time to stare into my eyes L2J. Do that, and see if you can tell me your not afraid of me tomorrow night. Look deep into my eyes and see my soul, hell bent on destroying ever last thing you stand for. Look into my eyes L2J and see if the answer to everyone’s question lies within. Are you a champion? Can you defeat me? How will you survive? The answers are no to all three. You are not a champion, you cannot beat me and you won’t survive. This game ends L2J. It ends in this ring, behind this structure. The Tiers now in view, it hovers above Sovereign as it continues to lower, perhaps now 4 feet from the top rope of the ring. It will be in here L2J that I will end this game. I will end it, not you. I will bring to conclusion the debates. I will show the world that there was a mistake made when Cameron Griffin made L2J the Co-Universal Champion. So sleep tight Knight in shining armor L2J. I want you rested, I don’t want excuses when I face you. I want you ready to go. I want the best of L2J, I want it all. The Tiers now in place, planted to the ground. Sovereign walks forward to the ropes, gripping them with his hands, a tight grip like a vice. It comes to an end in here L2J. I’ll see you tomorrow night. I’ll be right here waiting for you. Sovereign releases the ropes, as the camera fades to black.
