Diary of a Madman

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 20 Diary of a Madman: Part IV September 19th, 2002 It was a surprise to a lot of people. But I think more of a disappointment to most that Sovereign managed to survive last night’s brutal Main Event and leave the arena still the Universal Champion. And while the two sides play off in this internal war here after last night I came to the realization that Sovereign isn’t nearly as terrible as I thought he was in the first place. At heart, he’s a warrior. Like everyone else in this world everyone is good at something. Your parents suggest to you that you work hard at it and try and perfect your craft, and unfortunately you saw perfection in the craft last night when he defeated all those 3 other men despite interference against him. Regardless of how much we hate and loath what Sovereign does week in and week out last night I think that if ever he deserved recognition it was after last night. He took 3 other people to school in a match, designed to be “anti-champion” and he pinned Matthew Boone cleanly and within the rules. All the details in-between, the baseball bat... legal. Throwing Kage through the electrical board... legal. That’s what happens when you underestimate a champion. I’m surprised that Daniel, under the pressure to do something about Sovereign placed him in the match that Sovereign made his living off of. Sovereign even defeated Daniel in a Falls count anywhere match, the 2nd fall of their historic 2 out of 3 falls match. Sovereign prevailed then and this time he did it again, against the highest odds that eVo could construct. He might be insane, and he sure is evil but he is one hell of a champion and last night he proved it. He proved it to a point that he showed me the light. For the first time in my 32 years on this planet, sharing each waking moment with Sovereign I finally understand him. I respect him, and I wish him the best of luck. Although I don’t want anyone to get hurt I’ve come to the realization that perhaps there’s nothing that I can do about it except for just try my best to influence what Sovereign does. I’ve managed to take control last night after the contest, Sovereign was tired and hurt and that’s when I usually can take control at the snap of a finger. I came back to the hotel after a quick clean up backstage and a shower and since everyone was avoiding me I didn’t have to worry about long conversations. So here I sit now, a lot like I did a month ago, the Universal Title is sitting here on my desk beside my laptop and the name Sovereign is written cleanly on it. But this time... this time I don’t feel guilty or hurt. This time I feel a lot better about it all. This time I think that even though I can’t stop him all together, maybe I can make an impact. Help to undo what’s been done. Not my saying I’m sorry or warning people about Sovereign. There’s no point in that, simply because ego is the reason why anything happens in his industry and since my ego is rather limited I am going to try and do what I can whenever I get to the surface. If I arrive at Legends IX as Sovereign then so be it. If I’m William, then I’m going to be the best damn William I can be. And I still have the responsibility of being the Universal Champion. Heck folks Sovereign even has a Title defense that week, against Alias. And I’ll be perfectly honest that I’m not exactly sure why he gets a match after losing last night, but I’m still learning the business. But either way, I’ll be even more honest... I’m not a wrestler. Not mentally at least. I know what it feels like to be in that ring because I feel everything that Sovereign feels. He just deals with it in a different way and I don’t think that I would be able to handle it so well. But regardless of that I have come to the understanding, personally that if Sovereign is champion... then so am I. I have to think like a champion and if I am forced to step into the ring with a man that is as dangerous as Alias is... then I have to fight him, and fight him like Sovereign would. I might not like it but I’m not going to be the one to screw Sovereign out of his title. Out of our title. I am not sitting here saying that I’ve changed, that I’ve come to know Sovereign as a friend. My relationship with him will never be clear. Simply because I understand him now doesn’t mean I want to help him, no that’s not it. It means that I’ve broken new ground and I have high hopes that It’s going to let me slowly start to be able to take control more often, to have more influence. Sure, sounds sort of strange but I’m preparing myself for what could be a massive breakthrough, something that no one who’s reading this right now would be able to understand really. It’s not like this here is a news letter, I’m not writing to a slew of doctors here folks. I know your fans and other wrestlers who are concerned as to what Sovereign’s mind is thinking. Of what at least his other half is mumbling on about. The problem at hand right now is that while I mentally prepare for what could be huge for me and for eVo, Sovereign waits in the wings. He’s here right now, reading as I type confused as to what the hell I’m talking about and looking forward to legends when he gets a little bit of payback against Alias for his attack on him last night. Sovereign feels that any friend of Jamal Kage is an enemy of his before that man or woman even opens their mouths. Sovereign’s quick to judge that way, it’s a personal weakness that I’ve found in him. For me Alias is another man after his own personal goals. Nothing wrong with that is there, I mean it’s what we do every day, it’s what I’m talking about for myself right here and now. Granted that I know very little about this man, all I know is that he likes Pulp Fiction (a good movie by the way if you’ve not seen it I suggest that you go out and rent it) and I guess that’s a good thing. I don’t get many opportunities to watch movies so I doubt we have anything in common other then we seem to both enjoy Pulp Fiction and that he gets a shot at the Universal Title, the belt sitting in front of me. And while I haven’t slept yet since Digital Destruction I still can understand why Alias you no doubt are hopeful. You get the shot of a lifetime, the Universal Title. I welcome you to try and pry it from Sovereign’s fingers. 3 men couldn’t do it last night, even with your help. And so how do you think you’ll be able to stop him all by your lonesome. A little common sense never hurt anyone Alias, and while I do wish you the best of luck this coming Wednesday, my thoughts are of you getting some havoc sent down on you by Sovereign. He didn’t get to finish the job on you at Digital Destruction because of logistics but you and Sovereign are one on one in a Hardcore match Alias, and you may very well be hardcore. I do recall that you were a “Fighter” if I’m not mistaken. But either way my friend, all the cell work you may or may have not done. All the chairs and ladders you’ve sustained doesn’t prepare you for Sovereign. Nothing can, nothing ever will be able to mentally prepare someone for him. You hope, you work and before you go out to that ring you pray that he doesn’t end your career like he as to so many that have crossed him. I would just want to beat you... Sovereign will want to annihilate you. And I know that now, even more then ever before there will be a long list of men trying to make a name off of me and Sovereign. Men like Shodan, Jameson, Frost. I’m sure that L2J feels he is far from finished with us. Matthew Boone may very well become Rising Star Champion this week, but will he still always want his chance at the Universe? Would Sovereign’s brother in Asis want his chance? I’ll say it’s one heck of a complicated situations friends. Sovereign however is no stranger to this sort of thing and he and I will strive to continue as Champion. Both under our own agendas our own choices to make. And Sovereign has to realize now that it’s not a competition between him and me. We must co-exist. We have to be one person if the Universal Title is going to stay around this waist. I’m willing to hold up my end of the bargain Sovereign. I’m willing to go out there and fight for this title. And I know for sure that you are willing to do just the same. You taught me Sovereign that you have to fight sometimes to prove your worth. You taught me that you have to stand up for yourself. And to be honest, no I don’t agree with how you’ve done a lot of things in your time. I don’t agree with you ending all those careers, and hurting all those innocent people. But this is wrestling. This is a fighting man’s sport and I’m going to do my best to go out there and Fight. Fight as best I can, and in doing that I’m going to prove something to you Sovereign. That a man doesn’t need to shed needless blood to prove that he’s not just some throw away of Society. I’m just as sick as you are, on a literal level and not some sort of Layman’s term of “Sick”. We’re the same man, different personalities, different egos. You are out for blood and I’m out for vindication and to try and do what you did. Try and prove that I’m more then what people have told me I am all my life. You managed for the last year and a half be in complete control. All the time it was Sovereign and now William, me, I’m out to make my name heard. It’s kind of funny now that I’m thinking about it and typing all of this. I guess I’m not so different from everyone else. I’m trying to make a name off you Sovereign. Yeah, that is Ironic isn’t it.
