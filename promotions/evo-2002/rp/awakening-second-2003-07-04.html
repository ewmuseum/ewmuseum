Awakening - Second

4 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-04 The smell of victory – I can still sense it, locked behind whatever stench of failure allures me now, after the long fought battles and sweat. I stand here now, feeling useless and desiring someone’s blood on my hands. To feel the smooth, sensual warmth of drying blood caressing my skin. But now there is nothing for me to feel. My bloodlust lives now only inside, waiting for the external to be brought to life. I rest, feverishly. I wait. And that’s all I can do. So what else is new? So many days have passed since the awakening. The sky has gone from blue to pink to gray, passing by in never-ending cycle like the seasons of the Earth. Rain has fallen from dreary clouds, and lightning has sprung across evening skies. The sun has set and risen miraculously time and time again, and yet, after all the blissful days of life, I still feel empty. A sense of being unfulfilled is what it appears to be. My body rejects the life I have so gracefully given it. My soul, rather then receiving a lie, finds itself waiting for the time when I may step out of this costume of good and embrace a darkness that has yet to be succeeded. In the faded distance of unreality, the truth lies. And I can feel that destiny waiting for me to come and unbury it. The now is a shredded group of emotions, each as distinct as the next. Each of them make up apart of me. Each of them roams through my mind from time to time, giving me my new cluster of thoughts that come in through the unknown recesses of my mind. But none of them makes a bigger impact then that of hatred. I watch the world pass me by, realizing that, as special and godlike as I feel, I am only a single entity in this vast wasteland of reality. The names have come and gone, all of them enemies, with very few allies. I have hated all of them. I have hated all of them with every inch of my heart. I wanted to feel their lives waste away in my hands. I wanted to reach in and pull out what little soul they had. I wanted to kill their essence and shit on their corpses. All of them. Each and everyone. But now, after all this, one name comes to mind. One over any other. Thug roC was the last to meet my wrath. And he will be next.
