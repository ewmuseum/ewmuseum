Faithful Consequences - Satisfying The Demon

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/2/02 As the traffic slowly moved along on the highway, Jason’s thoughts rested on the victory, and the sudden silence of the one he had come to know as Jay Jay. As his eyes looked ahead, studying the landscape of the world, he tried very hard to pull his mind out of his fear. The thought that Jay was sitting in there, watching through his eyes, trapped inside something that no man could describe. He could be watching Jason’s movement, feeling his heart beat intensify as his fear overcame him, waiting for that one moment of weakness where Jason could do nothing about it. The car’s heater was enough to give Jason an insignificant feeling of comfort. The breeze outside blew harshly, the trees receiving its cruel blast through out the day. The sun was out, but it might has well have been asleep on the job. It provided almost no warmth at all. If it weren’t for its light, the sun would be completely useless in the winter days. His car slowly edged along the pavement, pushing forward in the sea of automobiles that was the highway. The red tail lights were starting to irritate his eyes. Like eyes, they blinked only momentarily before opening wide once again. They seemed to mock him, laughing at him in their silent way. Red. That’s my favorite color Jason. Reminds me of..., well, I’m sure you know what it reminds me of, now don’t you? Jason’s world was thrown back into apprehension as the words of Jay echoed through the cave that was his mind. “I think I can take a guess, Jay.” Jason waited. Waited for his response. Waiting for the words of damnation to ricochet once more from his voice. Waited for him to reply. I can tell you this Jason – proud of ya. Jason was almost about to be confused, before realizing just what he meant through that. Jason looked down, at the title that sat in the passenger seat. A symbol of my salvation, he thought to himself. That belt had bought him some time. Now all he had to do was satisfy the demon inside of him. Watching his every movement. Justifying his every move. Interpreting Jason’s every thought. What did he want now? Don’t you know what I want Jason? I want to be the best in the world. As long as you can take care of that for me, I’ll leave your body to you. But if you fail me… well, you know where this goes. “Yeah. I think I know you piece of shit. And don’t worry. I don’t need you for my glory.” I’ll remember you said that. But for now, I’ll just get some shut eye. And I think you want to his the gas there pal. A honk came from behind him, an angered old man waiting for Jason to get moving. The traffic had begun to speed up, the red eyes of hilarity no longer shining into his face. Jason drove on, the title in his car glistening beside him. “Don’t worry Jay. You think this title is something? Just wait until I win all the Glory. Just wait.”
