Easy picking...

20 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-20 Some believe you can’t teach and old dog new tricks others say you can. What it comes down to is can the old dog evolve to keep up with changing times. A new trick in and old era is good a new trick in a new era is nothing but old news. The question is can that dog keep up with new tricks coming in and develop ones of his own. Well in this yard there is a dog who’s growing and adapting to new times and he’s coming out with a bigger and badder bite than any dog in the yard… The scene opens up down a rural gravel road with the sun beating down. It’s a hot humid day with heat waves coming off the ground. The huge pastors of grass with spermatic trees sit still. There’s no breeze about of any movement for miles except a darken figure lays under the shade of a large oak tree near the road. He’s resting and as the scene moves closer he sits up and takes a swig of he beer… “ahh took you guys a good while to find me out here” Hedges mutters He stands up and stretches out his huge arms as they gleam with sweat in the sun. His black beater is soaked down the front as he begins to walk down the road… “ You know this Wednesday is a huge day for Hedges as a new growing man. I’ve adapting the old strategy that works but I’m also updating the game to deal with this new aged extreme paced match. My submission moves still have a place in today’s ring but my power and air game need updating and Wednesday he world will see the new me grace ring in my second shot at the evolved title.” Hedges says in sort of a boastful tone He takes another cool long swig of his beer and continues on is way… “Now as far as today goes in the moment and for eVo the change is a new dominant man on the roster, a new champion, and shit-load more destruction. Not many have seen my random destructiveness in action. My utter disregard for the life’s of god’s creatures and others property but I’m not one to dwell on what’s in the future because then the present will come and slap me in the face because I’m not focused” Hedges exclaims… Kicking a can he finds along the road he keeps walking further and further down the orad into nothingness…. “This Wednesday night on Legends I have a shot at the Evolved title against MLX and Seara. Now the last time I think I met Seara in the ring was in a brutal six man tag between New-TV and Tru-TV. New-TV dominated you fools and let me tell you one thing a woman does not belong in the ring this is a mans sport. A womans place lay in the kitchen, laundry room, or bedroom. There’s no ring in those three places and I don’t know what kind of man let her out of them but I just as well send her back as quickly as possible so she can get back to doing dishes. Most will assume I’ll be taking her lightly but all I have to say is that if she steps in the ring with me she’ll get beat just like any other man would” Hedges says confidently Cracking his neck and wiping the beads of sweat from his forehead he swigs down the last it of beer in his bottle and continues walking… “You know MLX we’ve got a bit of battle history between us leaving me trailing behind you in terms of official wins and such. I wouldn’t be so happy as to say though that you beat me fair and square. I was screwed by the changed stipulation clause every time I had you beat and the fast count Fiona pulled at the end is the only reason you are still walking with that title draped over your shoulder. As far as you past history goes with your father and who and what you think you are…I could give a shit less. Your personal life has no concern with me. You bore me and bore the entire locker room. I simply want that gold around your waist. Then when you don’t have to defend it every week and you can go figure out your past and how you came about and just who you are.” Hedges exclaims With that Hedges stumbles across a dying rabbit lying in the road. It’s got a large cut on it’s legs and lays there helpless. Hedges picks the rabbit up by the hind leg and looks it over and drops it. It hits the ground with a thud right on top of it’s head. It’s hind leg twitches and small amounts of blood trickle from the new cut on top of the rabbit’s forehead… “Look at this creature! It’s pathetic, wounded, helpless, and it really can’t defend itself against predators. Look above us…Vultures…scavengers of all things just waiting to drop down and pick apart this pathetic creature. Natures laziest overhead. Don’t even bother killing animals because pathetic things like this are dying all the time. Much the same MLX and Seara are mine for the picking Wednesday. There’s not to much they can do except lay on the mat helpless as I pick them apart piece by peace. Do you know what the beauty of a triple threat match is? I can pin that woman and walk away with the gold. Got to love the non-elimination stipulation…HAHAHAHA” Hedges says with a sinister laugh Hedges then cracks his empty beer bottle on the ground leaving a sharp top exposed on it. He looks up at the vultures above and down at the rabbit. He stabs the rabbit in the throat and a pool of blood begins to soak into the bone dry land as Hedges stands back up and yells to the vultures… “Come and get it…Just like I’m coming for you MLX” with that the scene fades to black…
