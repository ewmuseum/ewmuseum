Just call me X...

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-22 Poetry by X 'A man who stands is a man who has the potential to fall. A man who brings everything to the war is one that takes the risk to lose it all. I am a man who goes against all odds. A man who is different in many ways. A man who lives for what he loves is one that will die a happy man. My love is not of one of affection for another but of affection for my purpose. What purpose does a man like me have?! It's all for the better this war I wage.It's for equality to every man like me. So as the war begins take not the name of a army but nor the name of the man who wages...just take note of one thing, the man..he is the mark of X!' -X It's night time in Queens, New York as qbx,who will be called X from now on, is standing beside his window. He is looking outside of his room down on all the people below. He is smilen as he watches all the steet thugs to old ladies pass by his window. He looks as he sees lighting in the far distance as if a storm is comming.He turns away from the window as he is wearing a white wife beater with some black jeans and brown timberland boots.He looks over as we can see a poster hanging above his bed. It seems to be a poster of Malcom X. He looks over at it as now he stands at the end of the bed looking at the poster as the camera gets closer to him. 'The man who I studied'..X begins to say..'The man that planted the seed in my head..'He moves over to the side of the bed as he places his hand on the Malcom poster.'I do it because of you. I am here for you and my people. Know when everything is said and done with I will dedicate everything in my career not to myself..but to you and our people..my people'.He said as his hand stayed on the poster.He takes a step back from the poster and looks directly in the camera. 'Do you know what it's like?Have you ever been looked down upon because of what you are?Do you believe that everything is equal in this world. That everyone gets treated fairly. You people would begin to think so wouldn't you?'..He looks down as in angered that the people of eVo will see differently then him..'Question what is unknown to your daily life. I cant blame you for that. Although I can blame the corperate world for everything I had to endure growing up. I can blame them for showing me a world it took so long to get too.'He looks over by a table on the side of his bed as on it is a picture of him and his mom outside Queens Bridge apartments.He then looks over at the televison that is very small and is on a stand in the corner of the room..'I saw the world that you people I had. I saw everything that the people around my way only dreamed of. But what did that mean to you, you people didnt give a damn. I look on what you have now and being able to finaly afford it I dont want to dare be a part of it. My contract allows me a luxary I will not lie about that. But until I can make it so that everyone, my people that is , can enojy the oppertunity that you all have then I choose to wait and stay with them..'He says as he still is looking at the televison. He then begins to walk up and stand directly in front of the camera.He turns and as he does so the eVo camera is right in his face.. 'Thats it man, bring that camera right in front of X.Show all of eVo that X is here to make this place right.The movement is here...this is war.'He still stays right in front of the camera not pulgeing one bit. His face begins to give a look of anger as his voice gets deeper.'What is it X?X can hear that question all around eVo as X speaks. What problem do you have with corperate america?Why do you hate them so much?Typical questions from typical people. You are all no more then the same faces to me. Your minds tell you what your eyes see. Your mouth speaks of what they want you to say.They have some what brain washed you as much as they can. Like here in eVo they give the oppertunites to the wrestlers who have the look. The ones that fit in with there corperate world. If you are one that wishes to fall in line and be a man who basicaly has the image of everyone else then that is your choice. Just like it is mine to come out here and speak of what I feel. To tell my people the truth of the world so that they can not feel ashamed of being who they are. So that instead of the belief that you wish for them to think that they too can do what they want. That there goals are no longer just dreams. Dreams are no more than wishful thoughts. Actions are impulses of achievments..'He began to say as he now takes a step back from the camera.He then goes back to the window where he was standing at just a few moments ago... By now the lightning that he spotted has now moven to directly above him. Rain begins to hit the window as he takes a look down on the streets below. The once crowded streets are now empty because of the storm.X takes his hand and places it against the window feeling the rain hit the window as the wind is moving fast.'Isn't it funny how once the streets that where crowded with everyone has now been deserted..I mean lets face the facts. Everyone that has taken shelter is now in there rooms. Well whats the big deal of being in their room?, well what do you do on a rainy day? I know, you sit at your fancy little houses with your white picket fences watching televison. You see that is the point I was getting to, televison. All of those people who grew up in the same enviroment being looked down on people because of what the look like. But you see we are looked down for more reasons but all of these reasons stem back to the one that began it all. People look down on people because of like of money. My people have lack of money because of the image the corperate world gave them. People look down on one another because of where they live. Well my people are forced to live where their do because of corperate america and the image they gave them. All of that is comming to a end..The image is starting to develop..And corperate america..is soon changing.'He said while he looked out on the storm..'Most of this world that we call eVo is a life for every man on the roster. Like I said when I stepped through the door, I am a fighter not a wrestler. It makes me laugh frankly when I begin to think about most of the eVo roster. They all live a life that is a lie. They want the people to believe they are ether some badass who has risen from hell itself, or to picture them gods gift to the wrestling world. But go ahead and ask yourself, is X lieing?Well I think it's obvious that I am showing you my life as we speak. I am fighting for the people who are growing up through the same thing I have been through for 20 damn years..But if I am not lieing then why do I call myself X?Why not my birth given name.It's really simple. My birthname was given to me as a identity. X is me though,its my identity. X lives from here on out for that one caus..for the movement.X is a mark much bigger then myself..X is me..' He stops talking and looks out as the storm is still going strong.He takes a step back and faces the camera so the window is now behind him.'It's now here in the eVo'he begins to say..'The movement that has been tried over and over again is finaly taken place. The one that will never be stoped..the one that will be nothing but history in the making.So as before now you people where use to the quite sound before the storm..What will you do now when the storm is here..how will corperate america survive..its simple..it want..X has spoken.' He then moves turning his back away from the camera. He looks out on the storm as the camera fades to black with the last image we see is X looking outside his window.
