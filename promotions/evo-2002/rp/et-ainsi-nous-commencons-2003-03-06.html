Et Ainsi Nous Commençons

6 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 6th Translation: And So We Begin Fiona Preston sat thinking to herself. She was going to do something, but wasn't quite sure what it was. That ever happen to you? Sure it has. She knew she was going to do something outside, but couldn't quite grasp what. She had no real recollection either. It was a bit chilly out, so it couldn't have been anything too drastic. Maybe she was- Ring She snatched up the phone pressing it agsinst her shoulder so she could play with a quarter, and responded. 'Hello?' “Hey Fiona.” “And this would be...” “It’s Jody. Come on, we’ve been friends for four years.” “Oh yea. Hi. I guess I just can’t get the sound of people’s voices stuck in my head. So what’s up?” “What do you mean? I’m waiting here.” “Okay...Waiting for what?” “You were supposed to pick me up for the movie.” Fiona stopped spinning her quarter. It rolled off to the side of the table, and landed with a metallic sound one would recognize. “That’s what I forgot!” “Gee, thanks.” “Sorry, I was just thinking a lot last night.” “What about? Your little wrestling gig?” “It’s not little. He signed me. In a way, I’ve been here for a couple months though. I’ve been working in their territories for a little while now. Got me a little bank, and I guess I could owe some of this to it.” “So, things are gonna be good then?” “Maybe.” “How so?” “Well, he’s not here.” “How does that factor in?” “Well, the one reason I did this was because of him. And he’s not here now, so it’s all going to be in vain. I’ll still get to wrestle, win all the titles, pocket a huge amount of change...But I’m gonna wind up feeling empty once it’s over with.” “So just find another guy.” “Yea see, you don’t understand the relationship we have. All you know is his name and how we met.” “Hit the nail on the head.” “I’d like to on yours. But I don’t know what I’m gonna do. Maybe I will stick around for the long haul. In the mean time I suppose I could try and find him. Anyway you mighr be involved in that?” “That all depends. Is he single?” “Your not getting involved in this if it’s for your own orgasmic purposes.” “Alright alright, I’ll help you out. Just come over here and pick me up.” “Okay, be there in a few.” Fiona placed the phone back on the receiver, and looked to the clock. For some reason, this didn’t seem like the thing she had forgotten.
