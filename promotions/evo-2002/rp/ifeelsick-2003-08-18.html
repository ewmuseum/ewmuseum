Date: 2003-08-18 The sermon was being held. So far? Flawless. A million fuck-up sheep were busy singing Amazing Grace while I sat silently in a back pew. A cross toward over my head, sending a shadow across this small, old-fashioned, Methodist Church. God was smiling down upon the church and Jesus was there too... or so the preacher told the sheep. God was dead and his son was having an orgy with the wonderful angels of Kingdom Cum. But, I didn't want to rain on their parade. Why I was there probably wouldn't make sense to you. It's even hard for me to make sense of. But, the voice that I kept hearing the last few nights? It had grown to become much more common. I came here in hopes of finding sanctuary. A place to hide. A place to be, oddly enough, alone. But, alas, it seems the church only made the voice worse. I still blame insomnia. Christ spent around thirty-six hours on the cross to save the world, The voice would say as the preacher told us of how Moses parted the Sea. To receive fame. Christ was no savior... he was just a man wanting fame. They worship him because he seemed like a hero. We know what he truly was though. A man wanting fame. Or a man wanting an easy way out of life. Perhaps he didn't even believe in God. Perhaps Christianity is the scam of the millennium. Perhaps Christ was a con man. There is no 'me' or 'I' anymore. There's 'we'. There is no other people anymore. There's 'they' or 'them'. It feels as I'm separated from the rest of the world. We're separated from them. Insanity? No. It's just hallucinations caused by insomnia. Right? WRONG. As the sky collapses down around us, I wonder if another Christ will step up? In hopes of dying on a cross to receive fame. So that they, these sheep, will worship him. Will make stories of how he walked on water. We should read the Bible some time. It's a wonderful fairy tale. Wouldn't you like a best-selling book written about you, Sean? Would you like to be the next Jesus Christ? Some day, maybe that'll be a gameshow. Amazing Grace is coming to an end. Somewhere in the world, Damon O'Halloran might be polishing his Cell title. Making it spiffy and shiny. Treating it as his baby. His child. Somewhere, he might be snuggling up against it as if it were his lover. Using it to provide a false sense of power. Acting as if he's it's savior. It's Jesus Christ... And right here? In this church? The voice is telling me to steal Damon's lover. To make Damon's lover cheat on him. To make that false sense of power be my own. The voice wants me to adopt Damon's baby. The voice wants me to achieve fame. The voice wants me to become the next Jesus Christ, if it's possible. It's as if the voice is a mother wnating the best for her son. Wanting her son to be better than the other kids. And one of the problems is? This voice is oozing of charisma. Of influence. And when it speaks? I can't help but listen. I can't help but obey. And as the preacher gives the closing prayer... I find myself yearning for fame and fortune and that false sense of power. As people begin to stand to leave... I feel sick to my stomach. As the doors open to the night sky... I feel like throwing up. As the neon glow of the crucifix on top of the steeple shines down... the voice tells me exactly what to do. The voice tells me to 'win'. And I find that I must obey.
