The Countdown Begins To A Return

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-10-02 (It is only hours before Legends will take place, almost every wrestler has arrived to the General Motors Plaza and are wandering around the building. Strangly even 'The DiscoKid' Troy Pierce has already arrived and is sitting indian style in the middle of his room on the floor. Pierce sits in only his shorts and slowly moves his arms in various yoga type movements. The longer hair that Pierce has returned with is once again pulled back into a pony tail. Pierce has a pair of headphones on and is quietly singing along with 'Never Stops' by Lagwagon. Pierce is completely oblvious to his surroundings as he sits on the floor. Pierce's traditional wrestling gear is laid out on a table, his wife beater, his jean shorts, a pair of two-tone wrestling boots, his knee brace, and a new addition an elbow brace, along side a regular knee pad and elbow pad, however laying next to that set of gear is a pair of long blue and silver wrestling rights with 'The DiscoKid' written down the side in black letters. It appears as though Pierce has had some new gear made for himself but hasn't decided on what gear he is going to wear. Still in his own world Pierce doesn't notice someone walk into his locker room. The person that enters is Monica Angel and she stands looking at Pierce who still has no idea that he is no longer alone. Pierce is no longer sitting indian style and is slowly stretching his legs and still hasn't noticed Monica Angel standing in the room watching him. Eventually Pierce notices something and pulls the headphones from his ears and opens his eyes and locks them on Monica who stands there sort of shocked for a moment. Pierce then smiles a wry smile at her and asks...) Pierce:'Did you bring the limes?' Angel:'What?' Pierce:'I'm just kidding, it's been a long time since I saw your pretty face Ms. Angel. Is there something that I can 'do' for you?' Angel:'Haha, Troy. Comedian as always. I see not everything has changed since you last set foot into an Evolution ring. Well besides the fact that you came back a hell of a lot bigger than you where when you left.' Pierce:'Yeah, let me ask you something, do my new muscles 'do it' for you? I've been wondering if girls really do dig guys with big muscles. You should see how far I can hit a baseball now, Ball go far, and chicks dig the long ball.' (Monica Angel shakes her head at Pierce...) Angel:'Funny Troy, actually they sent me down here to get an interview with you. I guess they were afraid to send the ring announcer down here to interview you after your 'Emerson' joke last week. You keep it up your gonna end up with a sexual harrasment suit against yourself.' (Pierce has kind of zoned out for the last minute but then jolts back to reality...) Pierce:'What, I'm sorry did you say something about sexual relations?' Angel:'No Troy.' Pierce:'Oh damn, sorry bout that guess I kind of drifted off there, but I heard something about an interview. Go right ahead and shoot any question you've got.' Angel:'OK, lets see. Well first of all you make your return to the ring tonight, are you worried about the chances of re-injury at the hands of Xavier Citizen?' Pierce:'Nope, been cleared for the last few weeks, the bones that were broken in my arm have completely healed.' Angel:'What about Xavier Citizen, he has shown many of the same characteristics that the man you know as Malcolm King used to show. King was never one to enter the ring against an opponent without some sort of reason or some sort of plan. What are your thoughts about that?' Pierce:'Well I'm not exactly sure what I am getting myself into, much like when I entered the ring against King. King and I had an odd relationship. He respected me and I respected him, and when shit hit the fan I could count on him bailing me out. As for Citizen I don't know where he stands, much like I didn't know where I stood when I first entered the ring against King in EDW. But I knew I was in for one hell of a battle every single time I did. The ULWN rules always kept me on my toes, and should ULWN rules once against start showing up, then all the better, cause we all know that ULWN rules go both ways, there can always be the TDKWN rules that I used in a match against King.' Angel:'Citizen is King's protege much like Matthew Boone was at one point your protege.' Pierce:'Yadda, yadda, yadda. I don't see Boone around now that I have made my return, so if your trying to make a connection about not trusting a protege, it's not gonna work.' Angel:'Good enough. But what about a contingency plan for yourself. Xavier Citizen does not stand alone here in Evolution and it appears since your return you once again do stand alone.' Pierce:'Maybe, there are still some people in Evolution that I have a favorable past with, guys like Jason Jameson, L2J and Shodan. All three have stood beside me at one point or another, mainly while we were all in EDW.' Angel:'Recently in Evolution there have been some stable wars taking place. You yourself where in an EDW faction in Evolution a few months ago, and even more so while you wrestled in Insanity Wrestling you stood alongside three other men in a group called the Fatal Four. Which was a group that dominated that federation. Have any of the stables come forward and spoke to you since your return? And more so, would you be willing to join one of the stables? It appears since your return your services could be a great help to one of the stables.' Pierce:'Stable wise, I don't know. I won't say that none of the stables have contacted me, but I also won't say which ones might have either. Maybe I am some sort of wild-card or a free agent that people are going to chase after, but as everyone knows while I have a past with stables I don't screw around. I am back for one reason and one reason only and that is to step back into the main event and win the Universal Championship again. I have been wrestling since I was 19, and I have tasted many championships ranging from the American Championship way back in MEW, to holding every single championship in Insanity Wrestling and holding the Universal Championship in EDW. I like the way those gold belts feel wrapped around my waist.' Angel:'You have almost always been a fan favorite and a guy that goes out of his way to interact with your fans. What do you think the response will be when your music hits later tonight and you make your way down to the ring?' Pierce:'Lord only knows. I have just as many fans as I have detractors. And with the recent absence I may come back to a hero's welcome, or I may come back and no one will remember me. Should the second choice happen then I can do one thing and one thing only and that is bust my ass in the ring and win the fans back over. Without the fans I am nothing more than some average joe working as a bartender or something. The fans have always liked me, and even if I have to work twice as hard to win them back that is what I will do. I haven't forgotten where I came from, I haven't forgotten the people who are members of my fan club. The ones that go out and buy a 'DiscoKid' t-shirt, or buy a 'This Disco Doesn't Suck' foam finger, or bring a 'DiscoKid' sign to the arena.' Angel:'Always a people's champion. As I walked in I noticed that you have two sets of wrestling gear. Are you planning on changing your gear to go along with your new look?' Pierce:'I was thinking about it, but then I thought why should I change what has always worked for me. The jean shorts and wife beater have been what I have wrestled in since I started. I have to thank the fan that made the wrestling tights for me, and I am going to send them back to her with an autograph on them. But I am going to come down to the ring tonight just like I always have, full of energy and ready for a battle. And when the dust dies down and I am standing over a fallen Xavier Citizen, and the crowd erupts I am going to prove that I am back, and that I am back for good.' Angel:'Thanks for your time Troy, good luck tonight.' (Monica Angel goes to leave the room and Pierce starts to stretch again. As she opens the door she turns right and starts to walk down the hallway. However the door to Pierce's locker room doesn't shut completely and as Pierce continues to stretch a figure emerges from the shadows. Pierce doesn't notice this figure but when the person comes from out of the shadows and reveals himsef it is Xavier Citizen who stands motionless and silently and watches Pierce go through his pre-match routines as the scene fades out.)
