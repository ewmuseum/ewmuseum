Foreboding Thoughts III...

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-18 “This is Mary’s place,” Damon said as he and his brother Tom stood on the front lawn. “Why are we at my sister’s house?” “Our sister,” Tom corrected him. “We’re here to check in on somebody.” “There’s nobody there I want to see,” Damon said as he expression darkened. Tom opened the front door. “Get in here Damon. You know deep down you want to.” The brothers walked into the house and looked around. Pictures of Mary and a young girl lined the wall. “So this is little Sarah?” Tom said as he pointed at the young girl. “My niece.” “Yeah, yeah,” Damon grumbled. “Can we go now?” Their attention turned to the stairs as Mary walked down. She walked past the men and entered the living room, where young Sarah sat in front of the television. “Sarah honey, back up from the TV.” She said. “You’ll ruin your eyes sitting so close.” Sarah shuffled back, her ponytail wagging like a puppy’s tail. Damon watched the young girl and smiled. “She’s beautiful,” he said. “I never saw her when she was this young. Mary used to put her on the phone when I called.” “That was before she found out that you were her dad and not her ‘Unkie Damon’, right?” Tom asked. Damon didn’t take his eyes off his daughter. “Yeah. Mary took custody after Omagh in 98', I loved her so much but I knew I didn’t have the time or money to take care of her properly.” Tom stood beside his brother. “And now?” Damon frowned. “The courts won’t let me have custody because that bitch of a sister of mine told them I wasn’t fit to be a father. The judge saw tapes of me in the ring and automatically thinks I’m a 24-7 egotistical psychopath.” “Which isn’t far from the truth, is it?” Tom walked back into the hallway. “Look at you Damon. You’re a big guy, but you’re not stupid. How many times have you been arrested for one thing or another here in America? Take that, add in your in-ring antics, and I doubt any court in the World would give you custody. You can‘t blame Mary for wanting what‘s best for Sarah.” “The Hell I can’t,” Damon grumbled. The phone rang; Mary set down her knitting and answered. “Hey Damon!” She covered the mouthpiece. “Sarah, its Unkie Damon. You want to talk to him?” “Yah!” She smiled, her front tooth missing. She snatched the phone from Mary, holding the receiver in her tiny hands. “Unkie Damo? Hi! How is you?” Damon watched the child glow as she spoke to him on the phone. He remembered telling himself after every call that some day he’d take care of her and raise her as his own. He smiled, leaning against the wall as he watched Sarah’s animated conversation. “You’re the bestest wrestler in the whole wide world!” “It was funny. She was always my biggest fan.” Damon took a deep breath as Sarah handed the phone back to Mary and ran around the living room, excited about talking to her Uncle, her idol. Tom placed a hand on his shoulder. “Time to go bro.” “In a minute.” Tom gave Damon’s shoulder a tug. “Time to go. Sorry.” Damon watched the little girl and began to think of all the time he had missed with her. He began to feel quite angry with himself, at Tom, at everybody. He spun around and grabbed Tom by the shirt. “Why did you bring me here? What’s the point of all of this? Or was this all a ‘kick Damon while he’s down’ campaign?” The dark mist began to settle around the Irishman. “I would have thought you’d figured it out by now,” Tom said. “You’re not mad at QBX or Malcolm because they hurt you. You’re mad because they made you feel helpless. Just like Dad tried to do when we were kids. Just like you felt when you talked to Sarah on the phone when you knew you should have been with her, raising her. You felt like QBX and Malcolm King dominated you, and it scared you.” “Nothing scares -” Damon started to say. “Drop the rhetoric.” Tom’s image was beginning to fade slightly. “Dad said you would be a failure. Sarah says you’re the greatest in the World. You’ve got a father to prove wrong, and a daughter to prove right.” The blackness rolled in, concealing Tom’s body completely. His voice began to fade. “Damon, time to take the bull by the horns. Right now, you’re where you wanted to be. You wanted to be in eVolution and to make things happen. It was your dream. It still is. So go make it happen, bro. Make it happen...” Damon opened his mouth to speak, but Tom’s whispered voice cut him off. “It’s okay Damon. I forgive you. A brother’s love is stronger than...”
