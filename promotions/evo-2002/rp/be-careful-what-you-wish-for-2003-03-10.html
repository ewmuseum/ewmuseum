Be careful what you wish for...

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-10-03 Welcome to Portland the sign reads as Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick step off the tarmac of their private Gulfstream VI Learjet. Nice thing being rich, don't have to fly coach with the rest of the roster. Too bad Wisdom was busy, we needed a long sit down with him, ah well. D: Back in Portland, and what a surprise, snow and rain, just like always. C: Oh come on, dont be so mean, I think its pretty. Look at the mountain over their, just scenic. Your just ticked because I beat you at Playstation on the flight over here. D: Next time C, I was a rockbottom away. C: Sure, sure, that's what they all say. Man I tell you what though, I can't believe, Havoc, and Wideload want another go around with us, especially after you singlehandedly defeated them the last time. D: Well you know what they say, if brains were dynamite, they wouldn't have enough to fart! C: SNAPS! Seriously though, I'm tired of these retards coming in with short-term memory. You beat them for The Syndicate name, nice job by the way, and now, just because they're teamming with Dolo Bentswinger, they think they have a chance against us? Please. I don't know what they been smoking, but I want some! D: Tell me about it. I can see it now, I'm Wideload, 600 pounds of fat tubby goo. C: Ewww! D: I'm gonna squash your girlfriend the Heartbreaker and use you as a speed bump. C: That's about the limit of their intelligence. Too bad for you Wideload, I can handle 600 pounds. Remember Bubba McDowell D? D: How can I not, that overhead belly-to-belly suplex you gave him shocked the world. No one tossed that 600 pounder around like you did. C: That's right! Beauty, brains, and brawn, I got it all. I think I'll use my favorite quote from Maverick in Top Gun....'That's right.....I'm Dangerous!' D: Damn straight! But lets not forget I have a grudge too, dropping me onto my face like that, busting me open. Know how many times I’ve been patched up this month. Every time we have a show! And truth be told, I’m getting a little annoyed about it all. Its only the tenth of March, and I’ve must had like 30 stitches so far. Its ruining my pretty face! Which is why, this time, one, two, or hell, all three of those rejects is going to get busted open! Whether its Dolo, Havoc or Fat ass, red is gonna flow! I guar-Damn-tee it! C: Preach on brotha! Preach on! All hellfire and brimstone! Damn your turning me on! Lets goto the baggage carousel and screw like rabbits for all to see! D: Sounds good to me, but before we do, have you thought about the Wisdom situation. Should we agree to invite him in into our little collective? C: You know, I have thought about it, I think we should give him a Syndicate shirt with his name on, letting him know, if he ever needs help, we will all be their for him. I’m looking forward to becoming a three-time tag team champion with him, so I guess I just basically gave him my answer. D: Yeah, and you know, he showed me a lot last week in our match. I know you two will become three-time champions and destroy everyone in your way. C: Hell yeah! Now which way to baggage!!
