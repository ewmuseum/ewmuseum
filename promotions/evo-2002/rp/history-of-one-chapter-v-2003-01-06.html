History of One :; Chapter V

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/6/03 'I need a shower..' No explanation, no nothing.. That's all I said.. The girl behind the desk was young enough to maybe understand, but she kinda just looked at me confused.. Not really confused, but more or a look like 'you gotta be kidding..' 'Its twenty bucks to use one kid.. ' Stuck up bitch.. She knew I didn't have the money just by looking at me.. 'I don't have that money right now..' I looked her right in the eye, not letting down my guard.. I was going to get in one of those showers.. 'Than I don't have a shower for you.' I wanted to punch her in the face.. Its been a week of hell and she's doing nothing but adding onto it.. 'I haven't had a change of clothes.. A shower.. Food.. A place to sleep.. I haven't had anything in a week.. All I'm asking for is a shower.. It's only running water, I'll be in and out.. I won't even use twenty dollars worth of bottled water in the shower.. Please..' I resorted to begging.. I was breaking down.. I was starting to think I wasn't ready to leave home, but all it took was for me to remember how much I hated the atmosphere. 'There's nothing I can do for yo...' She was cut off by a burly sounding man.. Defiantly a truck driver, I didn't even have to turn around to know that.. He must have been in line behind me.. I didn't notice.. 'I have twenty dollars..' The truck driver but the money on the counter, and I turned around to look at the man.. I than turned back to the attendant.. She didn't wanna give me a shower, but there was money now.. She couldn't turn me down.. I turned back to the truck driver. 'Thank you sir.. Thank you so much..' Normally I woulda nodded and left it at that, but I was estactic.. 'You run away from home boy?' I wasn't looking for conversation.. I wanted to shower and leave, that's all. 'Its not running if no one is looking for you..' He looked at me crossed eyed. Probably didn't know what to say to me.. 'A week without anything eh? Lasting longer than most do..' Normally, someone my age would be a tad nervous when a truck driver of massive size, wearing all black, leather and a cowboy hat, would be scared.. I wasn't.. I had no fear.. 'I guess I had a real reason to leave, unlike most do..' I didn't know if I should have been getting mouthy with him. He did just get me a shower, but my tolerance was low.. Understandably why... Him laughing at the comment didn't help anything, I shot back, 'What?' He took a deep breath.. 'You go take your shower, I'll be sitting over there,' he pointed towards the restaurant, 'when your done, you come in there and eat.. We'll talk.. Man to man..' I just nodded. The bitch behind the desk handed me a towel and I started to talk back towards the showers.. 'Wait..' WHAT NOW?! 'Here..' I didn't know what he wanted, but I walked back towards him and he handed me another twenty.. I just looked at him dumbfounded.. 'Go buy yourself a pair of jeans and a t-shirt.. They have them real cheap here. Its nothing great but its dry clothes..' He turned around and walked away.. I was confused.. I just stood there for a moment.. You'd figure big burly men such as this would pay no mind to someone such as myself.. but this guy was giving me money.. Forty bucks.. Maybe he was going to kidnap me?.. Heh. Kidnap a runaway.. Now that's unheard of.. Eh, its money and dry clothes I guess.. I wasn't worried about the man's generosity, hell.. I didn't even know his name.. All I knew is that I had twenty bucks for clothes and a shower..
