Diary of A Madman

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 1st Diary of A Madman December 1st, 2002 Location: Toronto Canada I did not see it coming. I guess my hat is off to you two, Leya and Impact for being able to blind side me this past Thursday. I did not expect Leya that you would have ever come back into my life. I thought I’d finally gotten rid of your constant wishes for me to come back. For me to return to the fool that I once was. The blind man living his life as if I was trapped inside of a box. Without any future, without any hopes other then a “family life”. I am not here to crap on those who’ve picked to live their lives like drones, working from 9 to 5 and then come home to the woman you married and the children you fathered. In fact, I bet that it is quite nice from time to time. Personally, and I feel strongly that my life with that woman was life sucking. Forced to conform to the world around me instead of the world that was inside of me. To be who I really am, not William but Sovereign. This is the true me. This is what I always wanted to be. And now I sit here, in a hotel room with this old computer writing a letter about my feelings. It almost sounds like I’m back in the doctors chair asking me stupid questions about blotches of ink on a paper. But I’m doing this here because I destroyed my camera when I came back here after what happened Thursday. Needless to say I was angry, and I still am. I’ll be pissed off for a long time to come about this entire situation. I haven’t been able to put to words what has been racing around upstairs in my mind because some of it is so confusing that you wouldn’t even want to speculate at the visions I have conjured up involving Impact and his untimely demise that will come at my hands. Thursday, regardless if we wanted to be there or not Impact we created the most unstable group ever in any wrestling company. I wanted only to hurt you again Impact, and I know that you want to still extract revenge upon me for my beating of you so long ago. I am not going to blame you for being angry with me. Because that’s human nature. Then you took my title and you can’t blame me for what I am going to do to you when I get my hands on you the next time. The only problem is pre-meditation. It’s not like I was looking for you that night Impact. I hardly remember any of it, all I remember doing is slaming that door on your head and laughing and feeling good about myself. I did feel good Impact, to hear the silence coming from you. To hear the crowd in the arena watch and gasp. To watch the video over and over and over again. I’m not going to apologize or say I’m sorry because I’m not. I thought I had managed to rid Evolution of nothing more then an ass kisser of Daniel Somers. Not that Danny is a bad guy or anything, I have a lot of respect for that man but I most certainly don’t kiss his ass like you did those months back. You were just a whipping boy, content to follow around and do nothing but try and eliminate those who you were directed to eliminate. Suddenly your back now and the fans love you. Why? Because I was better then you were Impact, at everything that was asked of me. I did it all because it was fun, enjoyable and I took you out just like the lighting of a match, just that quick and come hell or high water Impact I’m going to strike up another match really soon, and you will get burnt once again. You have to realize something Impact, I might have taken 3 months of your career or whatever, but you took my name. You took my only love in that Universal Championship. And while L2J might wear it now, and we all know that his title reign is full of crap because of you Impact. But while L2J carries that belt it will mean nothing to anyone except for him. We all know that Frost is pulling his strings like a puppet and one day I’m going to cut him free and beat him down like the dog he is and get my belt back. But Impact, for now you are my focus. You have taken my belt. You have taken my wife and I am going to take your career. Leya, I am going to say this once just to get it out here. I have to admit that I’ve always thought about you. It isn’t my lack of feelings for you Leya, it’s my own sense of responsibility to myself. You know the army says “be all that you can be” and that’s something I’ve respected about them. I want to be everything I can be Leya, and that sadly from your end of things means you and I cannot be together. I loved you once Leya, you were everything to me. I feel asleep only hoping to wake up and you be laying there beside me. That time in my life was the best, and worse of all my years. To be with the woman I loved, but yet being a slave to an image of Americana. I had to get out, I was suffocating Leya, and it was not your fault. And now you wish to blame me for everything that has gone wrong since then. You should have divorced me Leya, you should have cut yourself loose. But you just couldn’t let go. I guess I can ask now who’s really crazy? You for not leaving the maniac, or me for just going out and doing my own thing. Why on earth you would want to deal with me again especially after my little rouse at Faith is beyond me. Maybe I’ll get an answer soon enough from you, but I will make it clear to you Leya. I am not as self reserved as you might remember or might realize. You caught me off guard Thursday with your new found friend Impact, and you got your licks in. I still hurt from Rise of the Legends and probably will for some time, but trust me Leya, I will put my hands on you Leya, and I will hurt you just because of the things you did to me last week. I will make sure you regret showing up. Impact, you said that there is no room in Evolution for two monsters. You want to claim yourself to be a monster, then that’s your right to do so. You might have been the lucky one the last week, at Rise of the Legends you blind sided me. On Legends you had more help. This time, this time Impact you are going to see who the real monster is. And if you think that Stormbringer’s demise last week was something to behold then just wait until I get my hands on you this week Impact. Because I will be coming for you. No one will get in my way this week, no stupid commissioner or whatever. I am going to come to Calgary and I am going to give you the beating of your miserable life. Then you can go back to terrorizing Hollywood, maybe you can give the guy from Scream a heart attack or something because Impact you don’t scare me. You don’t intimidate me. Yeah, your bigger then I am, you might be stronger then I am Impact but I don’t care. Nothing has ever stopped me from doing what I want to anyone in this industry, and you will be another victim, a casualty of my Crusade. You will be another victim Impact. There is no avoiding it. Remember what I did to you when I wasn’t even looking to hurt you Impact, then ask yourself if you are going to be able to survive this monster. The only true monster of Evolution. You are just like everyone that’s come up to face me, a fraud. Nothing more then a child with delusions of grandeur. This week, just a little more then 4 days from this moment in time that I am writing this entry into my diary you will get your first taste of the Human Monster. No, you and your choke-slams do not count, you get to have the chance to feel me Impact. To know what it’s like to be in the ring against something like me. You might not be afraid of me, and that’s fine by me. I only care that you have taken from me something so important that I will be willing to go to extremely great lengths to recover it. And while I wait for my opportunity to do that Impact, I will take out my frustrations on you. You Impact, and whomever else you decided to bring into this if you choose so. So tread lightly and if you really are a Monster impact, you will have to bring it all with you this Wednesday in Calgary. Because I will be there waiting for you in that ring, locked gaze hoping for the moment I’ve been thinking about since Rise of The Legends, the moment that I rip your God damn throat out and watch you lay there gasping for air. You’ve entered a world of pain Impact, I hope you know what you are up against.
