Interview with Leya Forth

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Filmed October 25, 2002 Max Dickens, eVo reporter appears in front of the televison set. You have heard about his recent interview that will be aired soon as Max smiles to the camera. He’s wearing a nice casual suit, the background is plain with the Faith Poster above his right shoulder. Max parts his lips and speaks. Thank you for tuning in folks. My name is Max Dickens. In my career in Professional Wrestling I’ve had the distinction of working with, and interviewing some of the world’s top talents. I’ve enjoyed my career a great deal. However I’ve never interviewed someone who was not a wrestler, or someone involved direction with the industry. This time things were different. We’ve all be swarmed with the images and replays of the last 4 weeks here in Evolution. The story of William Forth, how he defeated his inner demons and has come to realize his dream. He’s a champion, he’s the best in the business. But with the recent addition to his life of his former family questions have been raised as to William’s Mental conditioning going into Sunday’s Universal Title match against L2J. I tried my best to speak with William, and although during a phone interview that was going fairly well I seemingly made the mistake to ask William about his wife. That ended the interview. William Forth wasn’t mad. He was just not able to comment anymore. That got me to thinking. What does Leya Forth have to say about all of this? We haven’t gotten her side of the story, so maybe I should try to get it. It took some doing, but I got her contact number and phoned her. I was surprised how she agreed so quickly. Fans emailed in their questions and although some of them will not be shown here she was quite willing to answer them all as best she could. I want to thank Leya for her co-operation and for you, the viewer to tune in today and see what her side of the story is. The shot fades out to complete black. Then white text scrolls across and it reads “The Following may be disturbing to some viewers, viewer discretion is advised” After that text fades out the camera fades back in. The scene is that of a blank room. The walls are black, several eVo posters hang on the walls from the past 3 Pay Per View’s with Faith in the middle with a big ‘Sunday October 27' under it. Two chairs sit opposite each other, one holds Max Dickens in the same attire you saw him in just a moment ago. Across from him is the lovely Leya Forth, the wife of the Universal Champion. She’s wearing a long skirt with a blue blouse. Her hair is well done as she probably had a good stint in “make-up” for her television appearance. She seems calm, although you can tell by her body language that she’s not %100 comfortable yet being on camera. Dickens: Leya, let me start out by saying thank you for coming here today. I know that the last month especially has not been easy for you. I don’t really know what it would be like to be married to William for so long but we’re all hoping today that you can shed some light on William’s past. Why he turned into the monster that he’s trying so hard to make us all put behind us all and what it was like to be with him so long ago. Leya: Well Max, thank you for letting me be here and discuss Will with you and all of the fans. I’m sure that you have a lot of questions. I know William very well, or I like to think I do. Although recently with all the things that have been going on surrounding his Universal Title defense, I don’t know if anyone can really say that they know him. Dickens: That’s a great point Leya. I know that you have your own view on this contest and we will get to that a little bit later on in this interview. But I’d like to start with how you guys met. And how did the relationship grow since then? Leya: Well it’s an interesting story Max, I guess most stories with Will are interesting. I mean, people see him on camera and it’s very real. Of course we all know that wrestling isn’t 100 percent reality. But with Will it’s all very much himself. He’s probably taken it upon himself to suppress himself, several aspects of himself for the sake of the story. But the William you see on TV is just almost a clone of the Will that I met and married 6 years ago. When I met him he was just out of a Mental hospital for the “criminally insane” he had attacked these two Catholic Nuns in Chicago. It was not something he is very proud of, and nothing he had control over. I was living in Chicago and I’m from Dallas and I was working as a Fitness Model at the time. Will was at this wrestling show I was working at as a Valet and I probably caught his eye since I was in this tight little outfit. Anyway he managed to gather all of his courage and come and talk to me after the show. Now, for your information Will at this time was so petrified of talking to anyone because he was just released from the Hospital and didn’t know what to say to anyone. I personally found him rather hansom and this was long before he deteriorated into my little scarface as I called him whenever he came home. But he came up to me and asked me for my autograph, and I never had signed one before so I asked his name and he blushed. I swear it was the sweetest thing I’d ever seen. So he told me his full name, I thought was cute and he was just answering my question directly because that was just his way. I decided to take a risk and asked him if he knew anywhere that I could get a good bowl of pasta. He stood there for a minute and didn’t answer so I figured that this guy was a junky or some sort of pervert and walked off. After I cleaned up and showered I was leaving the parking lot and he was there and told me about this place, I think it was Alphonso’s and then gave me really detailed instructions on how to get there. I guess he was just trying to remember the best place in town of all the books he read when he was a boy. He’s quite well read you know. Dickens: Yes, I know. He gave me a list of his top 100 favorite novels. I couldn’t believe it. Leya: He loved to read, as a kid he told me he wasn’t allowed out of his room often, so his Mother gave him a book every other day for him to read. Anyway, I’m sorry for getting off topic there. So, pasta yeah. I figured this guy was interesting and since I hate to eat alone especially in a city I don’t really know like Chicago I went out on a limb and asked him to join me. Then what shocked me the most was he just up and told me that he was released from a Mental hospital 2 days ago and didn’t think anyone would want anything to do with him. Dickens: Wow, honesty something we’ve all come to know from William. What did you say? Leya: Well I thought for a moment and decided, ya know what, he was released after all. I asked him for how long he’d been there and he said 7 years. After he said that I said, you know what maybe some other time. I waved good by and he stood there and waved to me with a big smile on his face. I went to the restaurant and ate the best meal of my life. Afterwards I went back to the motel and all I could think about was him. Seriously I tried a million things but just wouldn’t get my mind off him. I knew his name, I knew he was a mental case but I didn’t really care. To be honest, my step father is bi-polar so I know what mental illness can be like. And like most girls when I was growing up I had depression although mine was really bad and I tried to kill myself when I was 15. But that’s another story. I just had to find out more about this guy somehow. The next night we were across town and sitting front row was Will. He sat there and was a great fan. After the match was over I went up to him and handed him a secret note. He didn’t read it there and after the show was all over he was backstage sitting across from my room. When I came out his face lit up like a Christmas tree. That started it I guess. Just that I found him fascinating and I was the first woman to ever pay attention to him. Dickens: So it wasn’t like some sort of love at first site thing. Leya: No, not that I didn’t think he was Hansom like I said. But after all he was a sick person. But after a few weeks of talking to him and him showing me around I started to find him growing on me. Although he was extremely shy. He was easy to talk to, but never said much if you know what I mean. After about maybe 6 months of us being really good friends he told me why he was in the Hospital. I broke it off right there, I told him that I didn’t think I wanted to be with someone, hanging out with someone who did something like that. And after about a week I realized how stupid that was and called him up. The next night we went out to this place in the city and I kissed him. It was his first ever kiss besides something his Mother would give him. I thought he was going to have a heart attack hehe. Leya laughs to herself as she relives that particular memory. Leya: He asked me what that was for and I said for being you. That started the dating process and it lasted for a while until he finally got the courage up to ask me to marry him. I said yes right away and we got married about 3 months after that. It wasn’t much of a wedding but it was family and friends mostly wrestlers heh. Dickens: So up to this point he was shy, placid? Leya: Yes, he never once raised his voice to me. He got angry, sure but that’s part of him. He can’t always control himself and I always did my best to make sure he was on his proper medication. I never saw him hurt anyone, he sure did a number on the china a few times but I never blamed him for that. It just gave me an excuse to buy new plates. Dickens: But when did it all take a turn for the worse? Leya pauses and takes a breath. She clears her throat and then speaks. Leya: Well, I want to be honest with the fans. I really feel responsible for this entire Sovereign thing that started. It started not to long after we got married and Will expressed an interest to be with me all the time. I of course his new wife was all for it. And with that he started to train in the ring. He was such a fast learner Max, really fast and within a few short months he was wrestling full time. But what that did was start to manifest itself. See Will is Skitsophrenic. He has trouble sometimes distinguishing reality from fantasy and he started to lose that barrier between them. He started to be like a “Mark” trapped in the business not sure of the difference. He came home at night and he would still be “on”. Medications weren’t helping so we started to switch them off and it just got worse and the day that he got called up in Japan was the day that things got blown out of proportion. Things between me and him. Well they stopped one day, the worst day of my life. I never experienced a human being so brutal... it was... just.... Leya trails off. Max leans forwards and places a hand on her knee to comfort her. Dickens: It’s alright Leya, we can take a break if you need it. Leya: No I have to say this now or else I won’t ever get it out in the open. Dickens nods and Leya wipes a tear away. Leya: He came back from a show and when he walked in the door he had a bandage over his face and was obviously very aggitated. But I had news. Such news that I thought would quite him down and make him calm, and happy. See Max... I was pregnant. I was 3 and a half months pregnant and I was so excited, I mean a baby! Maybe that would be what was needed to really take Will to a new level. Maybe to break down all the walls he tried so hard for all those years to defeat. But when I told him he went balistic! He started to throw things around, he started to yell at me saying that he was going to Japan and he didn’t care about baby this or baby that. See Max, I didn’t want him to go. Death match after death match and destroy my Husband. I knew what would happen to him if he went, he’d totally lose himself in his character. I pleaded with him but then he lashed out and he struck me... here. Leya places a hand over her stomach. Tears roll down her face. Leya: He killed our baby. Leya stops and pulls out a tissue and clears out her eyes. After a pause that seems like an eternity she continues. Leya: And then he walked out the door and I never saw him again until 2 weeks ago. It’s all just... I don’t know what to say. Dickens: Me neither. The camera cuts out as obviously a break was needed after Leya just made that massive confession about William. The shot returns almost instantly, Leya is cleared up, much better now as Dickens is there poised for a question. Dickens: Leya, the most common question we got over the last few days is why did you not divorce William. Especially after that incident that you just described to us? Leya: Do you have a wife Max? Dickens: Yes I do as a matter of fact. Leya: Then you know what it’s like to really truly love someone. That’s it right there. I love Will. All those years that he was Sovereign in his mind, in MY heart I always hoped that Will would find a way to break through. I followed his career, I wanted to know how he was if he was alive, dead, in a jail cell or a hospital room. I paid all his bills, all his needs when ever I could. All in the hope, the pure hope that some day Will, my darling husband would come back to me. And about 2 months ago when he reached out to the world I felt vindicated. I felt so good to myself that my faith in Will survived all the years of being broke, out of luck and heart broken. So when Mum got a letter from Will about a month ago, you can only imagine how we felt about that! Pure joy, for the first time since we had been together, pure joy. All the positive things that happened during our life together all came back to me. I forgot about the baby, I didn’t care. All I wanted was my man back in my arms. Dickens: Don’t you worry that your hopes might be to high? What’s to stop William from turning back into Sovereign? I’m not trying to cut you down Leya, I just am worried that this might all just happen again. Leya: It might just. I can’t say for certain. That’s why I can’t let my hopes get up until I actually get to talk to him! How do I know what to expect when he won’t even speak to me. But that’s what I expected. I came on far to strong for him. I shouldn’t have come to Legends that night, and should have let it grow gradually. But I just couldn’t stand it. I needed to see him. I only hope that Sunday at Faith he’ll be willing to see me. Dickens: You’ll be at Faith then? Leya: Yes, I sure will be. I’m going there to cheer on my man! This is his biggest night and I want to be a part of it some how. Even if its just as a fan in the crowd. When he’s ready to have me there as his wife then I’ll be there every night of every week and every match. Sunday, I’ll do whatever he wants. Whatever he doesn’t want, I’ll just be there. Dickens: I take it your cheering for him to win. Leya: If the Universal Title means that much to him, I’ll cheer him to the end of the world. Dickens: There are some personal questions that fans have asked. About your relationship. For example, when was the first time you and William “do it” Leya: Our wedding night. Will was so shy. I think he was more nervous then anything. He was an amazing lover, it was his first time. And to be honest it sure was not mine. So I got to sort of heh, Mold him to my liking. He was the best lover I ever had. Dickens: Obviously you’re a very beautiful woman, we sort of see William as he is now. When did you first realize that you were seriously interested in him... that way? Leya: That night after I called him when he told me why he was locked away for 7 years. I saw him in a new light, I don’t know why. But I always thought Will was hansom. He has strong shoulders, he’s tall and dark skinned. He was so clean cut then and very sharp. I just decided that it was time to take it one step further. Dickens: When did you if ever lose hope that he’d ever return to you? Leya: I think there were a few times that I almost gave up. Last year in the fall when he was hospitalized again I almost threw in the towel. Of course, that day he walked out on me, that was so terrible. It hurt so much for him to do what he did and I was a mess for at least a year. The thing is Max, it isn’t something that you explain. I guess I could just say I Love Will. I really do, and Sunday when he throws himself to the wolves of the world of Wrestling I want to be there for him. As long as he knows that I love him, and I’ll wait for him for the rest of my life I’ll be happy just to have him back, even if it’s only a second on my death bed. Dickens: Leya, I want to thank you again, but we’ve run out of time here tonight. Thanks so much for sharing us your past and your hopeful future with out Universal Champion and your insight into what he’s like, or what he was like in his past. Thanks so much. Leya: Thank you Max, it felt great to get this off my chest
