Filth and Heros

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-7-02 Hero. Hero... what the fuck is that word? I've been out of this business for a few months, but I can already tell that things haven't changed one bit. Everyone wants to be a 'hero'. Christ. It's a waste of your time, if you ask me. I'm not one to come out here, judging the way you choose to live your lives, but man... it's pretty fucking pathetic. Busting your ass to impress everyone. Hopping through their hoops with the most noble of intentions. Yeah right. Can I fill you in on a little secret? Friendly observation, if you will. Bending to the will of whoever you're trying to please doesn't make you any better of a person than me. So don't even start that bullshit. There's no way I'm going to tolerate it again. Funny thing is that I can get just as far as you without pissing around, trying to please everyone I meet. Meet your new anti-hero. Sunday, July 7, 2002; 3:01 am Jinosyde and Maile's Apartment, McGovern Complex; Los Angeles, California Darkness. Not the kind that night brings, or the kind your overly-dramatic types will claim lives inside of their hearts, or anything like that. This is pure. This is the kind of darkness I've always been able to hide in when I just wanted to get away from the world. You know... the kind behind a pair of closed eyelids? I'll never admit it outside of my thoughts, but yeah... this is basically a nightly ritual of mine, already. I lie here, wide awake, with my eyes squeezed shut, and remember everything these people have said to me, over the course of the day. Every cruel word, and every passed judgment. They all come now, and I do my best to cleanse myself, and push them away. I sit in the dark, and I hide from your words. Like I keep telling people. I'm not a hero, I don't want to be one, and I'll never claim to be one. As far as I'm concerned, it's a worthless title. I'm just a man, and that's it. The day I'm accused of being something more is the day I quit wrestling. Sighing in disappointment at this circular train of thought I'm stuck on, I slowly open my eyes. Every single night, I go through the 'hero' thing, then get good and angry with any number of people I've encountered that day. My life's slipped into a rut, and I'm having a hard time climbing back out. Hopefully this whole eVolution thing helps out a bit. Rolling my head to the right, I get a slight burning in my eyes. Why is that fucking clock glowing so brightly right now? 3:04 The red numbers flare out at me, causing me to squint slightly, as I turn away, and struggle to adjust to this minimal lighting. All I can see is red for a few seconds, before focus returns to my eyes. Finally, things become clear again, and I see her. This is how it always goes, too. I beat myself up, get into a depressive bout of self-hatred, then I just roll over and look at Maile. Then everything's alright. Just past her, a window sits open, letting the gentle silver glow of the moon outside shine inwards. A gentle breeze blows the thin, white curtains, sending the fabric flapping, and billowing towards her. It's beautiful. Those deep, dark eyes of hers are closed, as she quietly sleeps next to me. She looks so peaceful. Sliding backwards, I sit against the headboard, and just look at her, bathing in the comfort and happiness she brings me. She's too perfect. Even sleeping, that face screams mischief... she's always getting herself into something. It's kind of cute. The moonlight pales that honey golden color of her skin, slightly, but I know it's still there. Such a warm color. Black bedsheets, done in a light material are tugged halfway up her chest, and tucked under her arms. The gentle slopes of bare curves beneath that flimsy cloth shield distract me for a moment. May as well just let her sleep. Pivoting, I turn in the bed, and land in a seated position, both feet on the floor. Fumbling in the darkness, I search for yesterdays discarded black cargo pants, and sloppily tug them on, before quietly slipping through the doorway, and into the living room. Yeah, we've got a nice setup here. The lights automatically fade up to a dim glow as I step into the room. Another of the toys that amaze us so much. With a satisfied half grin, I weave around various empty boxes, and other remnants of the move, and plop down onto our black leather couch. It's too hot out, even at night. I can feel the material sticking to my bare back, as I throw bare feet up, and onto the coffee table in front of me. Off to my right, a male voice clears its throat. Without even looking over, I shoot a quick response. 'Yes, I know you're here. I kind of noticed when I woke up, and there was a camera pointed at my bed...' 'Sorry...' 'Whatever. Why are you so early? I said tomorrow morning... morning as in ten o'clockish.' The man behind his camera shuffles nervously, and looks downwards, leaving the equipment pointed at me. Hey, I've got all night too. I'm not going to feel sorry for this guy, because he fucked up. Not like any of you would do it for me, so I'm done returning gestures that don't make it to me in the first place. Casually reaching up, I run black painted nails through my hair, neatening the mess a bit. Turning back to the camera, I slightly raise one eyebrow, questioningly. 'I was just... I needed tomorrow off, and you just said morning s...' 'And how exactly did you get up here?' 'The doorman let me in when he saw the camera.' Sighing, I shake my head. As much as we like the toys in this little top floor suite, I'm already beginning to hate this building. Apparently their security's a joke. Just walk in with the right kind of equipment, and they'll fucking escort you to someone's bedroom. You know... come to think of it, I've been hating a lot of things lately. Just the way people have been treating us in the past few months... I think other than Maile, I hate... everyone. It seems impossible, but also accurate, to me. The sooner I get this over with, the sooner I can get this man out of our apartment. I'll humor him, until he leaves, I guess. I can feel the Maile inspired joy slowly bleeding out of me, as I look down, to the floor. The sparkle behind my eyes dies, and the smile I've been struggling to hold in is swallowed back down. More reasons to hate you all. You keep costing me what little happiness I've got left. Looking back up at the camera, my face is stone. Dead. There's nothing left for any of you to read, and it's your own fault. I open my mouth, and the voice that comes out is a strange one. It's cold and empty... but I recognize it as my own. It's not me, but it's mine. I hate the feeling of what I've become, but go on nonetheless. 'Right, I guess I'm supposed to introduce myself or something now, right?' The cameraman smiles appreciatively, and gives me a short nod of approval as he shifts focus from his shame back to his job. Let him think I'm doing this for him. I don't care. 'Uh... I'm never really good at this. A few of you might recognize me, but don't expect me to feel hurt when most of you say that you don't. Aaron Chamberland. Jinosyde to most people. I had a brief stay in NEW about a year ago and... well... that's the last I was in the ring. There's your explanation for any rust you'll be seeing. And yes, I am that Jinosyde. The guy who founded a group known as La Stygia, and was then ditched by the other members, as they all moved on to greener pastures. 'Hey, at least I learned something from it, right? Right. It's just Maile and I now, and frankly, I think that's for the best. You'll be seeing quite a bit more of her too, by the way. Maile Diaz. Only person I've met who's been able to understand me. The only one who wasn't so fast to judge, like you all are. But I'm getting sidetracked here... 'I'll get right to my match. Haz, huh? Froyberg? I'll commend the booking staff, and inform you all right now that their... sick sense of humor is appreciated. Jinosyde vs. Hanz. I suppose somebody might find that funny. Maybe. Probably not. But still really witty. Kind of. Fitting debut, though. You know... I've always been a fan of the guys who try so hard to be 'irregular'...' Christ, I don't even know what to say when the camera gets on me anymore. It's been too long since I've done this. I should have waited before returning. I should have taken a few tune up matches somewhere. I should have practiced. But no. Now I'm stuck here, in front of this camera, with nothing to say. Struggling to think of something witty, but failing, I continue. '...I mean, where else can you find people who take such pride in their ignorance? Fucking guy's dad was probably a sufferer of chronic masturbation. He was probably eating out his wife in his mom's basement one night, watching Jenna Jameson when he decided that 'Haz' was a 'kick ass' name. It sounds so original, right? So 'straight to the point'. I'm impressed. Thoroughly. Unfortunately, our friend Hanz didn't do his homework. I think he should uh... you know... do a bit of reading, and find out exactly what that 'original' name means. It's quite a laugh, really. 'I guess his stupidity, and choice of original symbolism isn't any of my business, though. If he wants to run around playing the little star that could, hey... why not, right? All I know is that I've got to fight the guy, and he's apparently some kind of 'heavy favorite' going in. Oh, of course. He's only fighting Jinosyde, right? Jinosyde the joke? The guy who couldn't even cut it in NEW? I can't say anything so dramatic as I've spent the past year in solitude, training, or anything like that, but I do think that you're all in for a bit of a surprise...' The sound of light footsteps cuts me off. Well, not literally interrupts me, or anything, of course. It's barely audible... I pick it up though. I'm always good at that. The words stop flowing from my mouth immediately. Small, bare feet pad across the hardwood, approaching the door to this living room. Seconds later, Maile steps through into the room, bedsheet clutched to her chest. An immediate blush passes over her, as she notices the camera, and quickly wraps the sheet around herself even tighter. It kind of makes me smile. Usually when we're out together, she does her best to embarrass me in front of people. Not cruelly... it's just a little game we play. She'll say, or do pretty much anything to get a reaction from me. But now, it's all reversed. I'm at ease in front of a camera, and she has no idea how to react. Turning to me, with the look of a deer in headlights, she notices my grin, and quickly relaxes, sticking her tongue out at me. Smooth, and slender legs peek out from the sheets occasionally as she walks over, and takes a seat on the couch next to me. Sliding closer, she tries to bury herself in my side, as my arm instinctively and protectively wraps around her shoulders. Yeah... I guess I can't really blame her for being unnerved by the camera a bit. 'eVolution... meet Maile. I've already filled you in on her, though.' Turning her head up slightly, she gives me a nervous look. 'You were talking about me?' 'Just telling them why you're the only one who matters to me.' There we go. Brownie points. Smiling from ear to ear, Maile rests her head on my shoulder, and looks towards the camera. Voice full of genuine interest, she asks, 'So how does this work? You just come on here, and talk about your opponent?' 'To tell you the truth, I'm not sure anymore. It's been too long. I know nothing about any of these people, and I've got nothing to say to them.' 'But you're going to beat Hanz, right?' 'Yeah...' 'Well what else do you need to say? Don't you just go on TV, and say you'll beat him? And then he says he'll beat you. And then you fight?' '...kind of, I guess.' 'So you're done here?' 'Not really. I think I should probably insult him a bit, too.' 'Why?' 'Because. He's going to be out there doing it to me, so if I beat him to the punch, I get the good material. And because he deserves it. They all do. Everybody's already counted me out of this match. I've heard them talking. It's all, 'Oh, Hanz's got an easy win to make up for last week'. I don't like being looked past, and...' '...wow. So they all think you've got no chance, or something?' 'Funny how I can join a new league, and everything's all the same, huh?' Christ, it makes me feel sick. This is NEW all over again. I was their joke there, and apparently I am here, too. I hate it. This makes me remember why I quit in the first place. When I look down at Maile, though, I get a twofold effect. She has those melted fudge, dark brown eyes. I can see a silhouetted reflection of myself in them, but it just seems strange. Out of place. All of that dark beauty swirling in there, and then me. She makes it not hurt, but I can't help but wonder why she's here with me. She could do so much better. She could have anyone she wanted. Turning away, I look down at the couch, and lose myself to more of my self-abusive thought. I don't deserve someone like her, and it's only a matter of time until she realizes that. When she's gone, what am I supposed to do? I hate being alone... it scares me. And she will figure out that I don't deserve her, too. Fuck, look around me. I'm just another sell out. I left wrestling because I was sick of these people trying to make me change. I was sick of the fact that they thought that because they'd bought my contract, they bought me. I was sick of selling myself to the highest bidder like that. Now look at me. Nothing's changed about the sport, and here I am. Someone waved a few bills in my face, and I whored myself off without a second thought, until now. It makes me feel dirty. I'll just keep selling myself, as long as they've got the money. I don't do this for some love of the sport, or the fans. I don't even do it for the cheap thrills. I do it for the money. Aaron, the reluctant whore. I know it's wrong, I know it'll cost me everything, and I know it makes my the biggest hypocrite alive, but I can't help it. I just keep prostituting myself to these promoters, and there's nothing I can do about it. Maybe a small part of me expected it to be different this time, but realistically, I knew. I knew I was getting right back into exactly what had ruined my life once. All for a few dollars. I just feel filthy, and worthless because of it. This is why I shun the 'hero' thing you all cling to so dearly. I'm not one. I can never be one, no matter how badly I want to. I'm not a good person. I'm not even a strong person. I'm weak, and I just keep doing the wrong thing. I save myself the heartache of shooting for your high goals and failing by convincing myself I don't want them in the first place. Why soar so high that fall will kill you, when you can just stay on the ground? It may be cowardly, and I may be missing out on a lot, but it's safe. Maile pokes at my side a few times, getting me to look back at her. Looking worried again, she asks, 'Something wrong, softness?' 'Nah. I'm fine.' I try to squeeze off a smile, to comfort her, but I can feel it before it's even there. What comes out is empty, forced looking. It'll only make her worry even more. Why do I always bring myself down like this? I'd like to blame it on you. Hate you for it. Say it's because it's all you people have taught me to do. But I can't... I know this is my fault. Turning back to the camera, I do my best to sound polite, but once again fail. Notice a trend in my attempts at things? 'I'm done. You can let yourself out.' Lifting my arm away from Maile, I rise to my feet, and start walking to a the bathroom. I can feel eyes digging through my back as I move, but don't stop. Normally, it's just Maile's little infatuation with my tattoo, but this is a different kind of look, and I can feel it. Just when I think I'm home free, her voice stops me. 'Aaron... where are you going?' Insecure. She's almost as bad as me for that. The words don't say it, but her tone conveys the message. She thinks I'm trying to get away from her, and it kills me inside. She's the one person I actually want to be as close to as possible. Stopping, I turn on my heels, and look back at her. I hate myself for making her feel like that, but part of me is convinced that nothing I can say will make her feel better. Because I'm just not good enough for that. I search for comforting words, but I can't find any. 'I need to take a shower... I'll be back out in a few minutes.' Quickly turning away so I don't have to see that expression any more, I walk into the bathroom, and close the door behind me. Leaning against it, I beat myself up further, for making her feel bad, before making my way to the sink. Leaning over it, I look into the mirror, and see another person I hate. Hate. I've been accused of having too much of that. People think I hate everything, and really, I guess I sort of do, but there's a reason behind that. When I look into the mirror here, I hate the guy who stares back at me most of all. I hate myself. I hate what I've been through, and I hate what it's done to me. Then when I look at all of your smiling faces, I hate you. I hate you for taking everything you've got for granted. I hate you because I'm jealous of what you have. And I hate you for the way you look at me. But this is really where it all starts. Every morning I look into the mirror, and it puts me in a bad mood for the rest of the day. I hate the fact that I have to see that sell out so often. He's always there, and he always will be. I can run from you, but I can't run from him, and it's the worst. Turning away from the mirror, I try to forget that he's there, as I look to the shower stall. And this is something else I do regularly. I go in there, and I turn the water on as hot and hard as it can come. It hurts, and it burns, but that's just the price I've got to pay. Sad thing is, I never learn. I keep trying, and trying. It never washes him away from me, though. I'll never be clean. But I'll try just once more, quickly... just one more time... maybe he'll swirl down the drain... .fade to eternity.
