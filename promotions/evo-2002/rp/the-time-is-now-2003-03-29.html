The Time is NOw.

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~A camera opens outside a casino hotel in Las Vegas. Outside of the hotel eVo camera's sit and a wait the arrival of the hottest eVo superstars Team Network.As the camera's are rolling a black hummer rolls into the casino hotel's drive way. Out of the black hummer walks Team Netowork. Ms.Network wearing a blue jean dress with some black shoes. As High Impact his wearing Timberland boots, blue baggy jeans, and a black Iverson jearsey. He stands as the camera can not fully see Jason Relms yet but High Impact waist no time as he get close to the camera and begins to speak.~~~ High Impac:It's time fellas, the clock is now drawing to it's closure.The timeline is about to run out. Tommorow night in this damn city you people will watch the rise of the new revelotioun and the fall of a old evolution. That's right, Team Network brings it all to the tables. It has not been done to date what will be done tommorrow night. Tommorrow night a team with more talent than any man in this world win's those damn belts. A team that consist of two men who want those gold belt's more then any one in this damn place. Tommorrow night is the BREAKING POINT. All of our talent has not been shown thus far, but it all comes out in 24 hours. In just 24 hours we show the damn world what we are all about. Body Snatcher's, you are nothing but played out. You had your chances plenty of times before we came along, but now your time as ran out. The Syndicate the same goes for you, as all of peons.. ~All of a sudden Network comes from behind the hummer and slaps High Impact on the back of the head. He tell's him some thing and High Impact and Ms.Network walk into the casino hotel. Jason begins to speak at the camera.~ 'The F'N Networ' Jason Relms: I can not believe that boy. He dare tries to use one of THE F'N NETWORK'S platinum words. I tell you I sure as hell hoped that slap did him some good because we got a big damn night comming up. Well a night of celebration. You see, I have said it each and everytime I have stepped foot in front of all you pathetic PEONS. I have walked in front of these eVo camera's night in and night out teling you what was going to happen the next time Team Network stepped in the ring. I told you when we first stepped foot in eVo door's that Playaz Inc would fall, and then they fell harder then a atomic bomb. I told you before last Thursday that no team in the rumble could stop the brute strength of Jason Relms, and surely enough I single handedly was the last man standing. Same goes in 24 hours, these peons are no better then me. They are just like each and every one of those pathetic fan's out their. As they like you people at home are below me. Respect has never been brought to this belt. And yes I am saying this after only being here a couple of weeks but damn it it's the truth. That shit stopes now. NO BODYSNATCHERS, NO SYNDICATE, NO ONE stops me. No one has yet and damn it in 24 hours no one will ever do it. The FN NETWORK, makes history of two peon's in the tag industy and history in eVo has I and High Impact will leave a long lasting impression on these belts, one that no man will ever be able to forget. 'The FN Network' Jason Relms:I can see it now. Me standing in the center of eVo's PPV with that damn championship belt straped across my arm. Man I can tast it. I said since day one that the FIVE STAR RATING came at a price. I warned all as everyone sat thier asses in their seats and watched in disbelieve. As I have proved the wronging of a great phrase Words mean nothing. The people of eVo now know that when Jason Relms speaks they need to listen, because what I say is indeed the truth and will indeed come true. As I will also but forth truth in another great saying. The FUTURE is now, well I am the future of this pathetic site of a wrestling organization, but in just 24 hours Team Network will be crowned world champs,proving the phrase..the future is now! ~~~He looks down as if placing his belt on his arm, gives a slight chuckle and walks into the casino hotel as the camera turns off.~~~
