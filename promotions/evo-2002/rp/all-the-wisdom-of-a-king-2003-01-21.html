All the Wisdom of a King

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/21/03 {The shot opens on a dew covered backyard very late at night. The sun's light reflecting off of the moon, illuminating the night as it does so often. Xander is standing, somewhat leaning on a fenced in area of said backyard.} 'Well if it isn't just great timing' Xander begins. 'My first match on Legends in a while, and I have to face Mr. Wall Street himself. My first TV match since Legends 12, and I get the honor to take down Malcolm's little jealous protegé. How fitting.' {Xander moves along the fence, slowly} 'Luther.. you really think I can't rid us all of Malcolm's presence? Must I remind you that I have a couple times already... but it's not my fault they let him back in. I can tell now that it will take more drastic measures to actually rid my sight of him, once and for all. I was nice before... but next time, that won't matter.' 'And as far as you and me go... boy. You can try to do whatever you want, to whoever you want... but you always have to remember, that I know what you're doing, and I can stop you. I've done it once, what makes you think I won't do it again? and again? Until you crack like an egg.' {Xander walks back up to the house, the cold air making his breathing obvious} 'Tomorrow night Wisdom... tomorrow night you bring on all your rage, and frustration. Then, and only then you'll understand why Unlimited isn't just a stable name. It's a way of thinking... a way of handling business. The ONLY way to get things done around here.' {As Xander gets to the back door, he stops, and turns dead to the camera's 'eye'} 'Angel... HBC... Asis... me.. and the ONE. It's just beginning.... don't think I'm kidding around. It was people like you who made me put the plan into motion, and it is us, Unlimited, who will reap the benefits.. Wisdom, King, Frost, Seara... the first four names listed.... Wisdom Wednesday, Seara Sunday.... the end is nigh. Take heed boys, and girl. I'm playing for keeps now.' {The scene fades out to black}
