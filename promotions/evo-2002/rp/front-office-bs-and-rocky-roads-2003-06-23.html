front Office BS and RoCky roads

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-23 Surprises aren’t always welcome things in wrestling world…in fact if it isn’t a title shot it’s generally not welcome and being as Hedges was double champion the surprises he got weren’t welcome… The scene opens up in Hedges locker room backstage at the house show. It’s trashed, furniture is over turned and broken, and the complementary food table is all over the room and walls. A bag sits next to the door where Hedges paces back and forth… “You know the shit has really hit the fan. Both titles down the drain because of that idiot Luther. I couldn’t trust that guy to protect a dead raccoon. You see Eric Knight has my evolved title and I want it back. I want my rightful shot for the title that is mine that I didn’t even lose. St. Violence you can have the tag division it’s nothing without twisted reality and the syndicate was shut down and brought back because it had nothing. Though bringing a few nothings back to it probably won’t make it any better. You see you have lifted a huge burden from my shoulders by ridding me of being forced to tag with a wash up. As far as my title is concerned though I’m coming for it you can bet on that. There’s nothing worse than someone losing something for someone else. Luther didn’t win his half by pinning me, and you didn’t win it by pinning me. Rightfully the gold should stay around my waste but that idiot Tyler can’t seem to get his shit strait and puts me in bullshit matches where I don’t even have to lose to get my title taken away from me” Hedges kicks a piece of broken chair across the room in disgust “I had the match won and Luther blew it. I haven’t even heard the man mutter a word in a month. He shows up expects to go in and win matches without a plan and disappears. It pisses me off and he cost me my belt. I’m threw with him but you know I’ll probably get shoved in a few more matches with him because the front office can’t get their shit together but whatever I’ll knock his head in a couple more times. Now another matter that has been pissing me off is Thug RoC…” Hedges shakes his head and looks at the ground… “Just who in the hell do you think you are RoC. You are the one that needed help and protection…Who did you come to when shit got tough and you needed help? Me…You needed my help. I’ve done what you needed me to do for months. I’ve cleaned up your messes and won two titles in the time you’ve done nothing. You got your wish no rematch now shut your mouth. Who do you think you are throwing empty threats at me? Do you think I need to carry around dead weight? Hell no. I stomped you back in NRCW I believe and I’ll do it again if you keep up this power trip of yours. Perhaps you need to look around you. Omega isn’t here to protect you…Realize Jason is after you and I’m the only one who can help you. I don’t need shit from you, so show some gratitude before I show your ass the heel of my foot.” He says angrily before picking up his bag “Eric Knight here’s what I’m proposing for New Dawn…Me and you One on One in a cell. No interference no one else to get involved and we’ll settle who the real Evolved champion is. Come on Tyler or are you going to shove me in another match with that worthless wisdom. You shafted Omega are you going to do the same to me because if you do it will be the greatest mistake you’ve ever made in your life” Hedges shoves the camera out of his face and takes off down the hall in disgust
