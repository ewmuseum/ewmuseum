Champion of the Universe?

2 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-02 So he sat there. Sitting on the bench in the locker room that he shared. His body ached, his head filled with thought and emotion. Maybe 10 minutes ago he left the ring. He remembered the sweetest sound in his life. The bell ringing 3 times. Ding Ding Ding Libby Bowman’s classic wrestling voice echoing over the PA system. His theme music blasting. Fans on their feet cheering so loudly that it almost knocked him off his feet. He felt nothing like it before. Not all the other titles. Nothing like it ever before. 12 years at it, finally he felt fullfilled. He watched Asis roll out of the ring, peered over to Xander laying on the canvas on his back. The Ref had jogged over to him and handed him 30lbs that felt like it was 1 ounce in his hands. He felt a joy that he hadn’t felt in his life. He looked at it, golden glory. Xander’s name carved onto it but it was now his. He remembered pulling himself to his fee, forcing himself to stand. It hurt, it hurt like a son of a bitch as he put one foot on the middle rope and then with his last ounces of strength pulled himself up and then looked at the title. The flashbulbs flickered across the arena. His face bloody and he brought the title to his face and carassed against his cheek. His blood came off on the belt as he pulled it away staring at it and then held it up in the air for the fans to see. He’d wanted for so long to share that moment with them. He fought like never before tonight. Two men proved to be the biggest challenge of his career tonight. Bloody and swollen, hurt, aching and in pain he marched from corner to corner raising up the title as the other wrestlers were removed from ringside. His music started up a second time as he stood in the center of the ring and feel to his knees with a smile on his face. Mixed between tears of joy and tears of pain. He rolled out of the ring and slapped a few high 5's as he walked back down the ramp, each step shot through his legs and up to his back. The gash in his forehead stopped bleeding now but it still hurt like no bodies business. He couldn’t recall all the times he walked through that curtain in his career. Fresh from a great match but this time he remembered it fully. Despite all the hostilities and all the relationships he had backstage at eVo most of the locker room was there standing, applauding. He smiled and spoke softly to a few of them, Tyler Davidson approached and gave him a friendly hug, same with Scott Frost, Daniel Somers and several others. He had to be respected. So did Xander and Asis. They recieved similar applause. Win or lose it didn’t matter. They were all glued to the screen, refusing to take their eyes off the match. They cheered, booed and oooo’ed and ahhhhed with the fans at the same time. The Production crew were so flustered and experiencing such a rush that they were overwhelmed. James looked around for only a short period of time. His aches and pains were not serious, it appeared that Xander was taken to the 1st Aid room to be tended to right away, some of the guys were saying he was hurt pretty bad. But Kimson didn’t pay attention. He was to messed up emotionally. So now he sat. An ice pack on his left elbow and one over his shoulder as well. The Universal Title hung loosely over his shoulder bearly staying on. Leaning on his forearms that rested on his lap he let out a sigh. “Finally” he mumbled to himself. James looked for his bag and found inside his cell phone. His family had missed the show, far to late at night for them to stay up back in America. They had gone back home after Revelations because school was starting back in the States. Dad had missed the long weekend, dad had missed the 1st day of school for his kids and now Dad was Champion. Was it worth it? Physically it didn’t feel like it right now. But he’d been through worse beatings. Emotionally he felt like a king. Scott Frost walked passed and said “your da man” to Kimson who looked up and smiled as Frost headed to the shower. James leaned back, resting his head against the locker door. The Title slipped a bit and fell to his side. He looked at it and smiled. This was for him, it was for the fans. Champion of the Universe? He liked the sound of that.
