An Answer to a Challange

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 11th An answer to a challenge. The view from a balcony over looking a city is always a sight to be seen. Little Rock is a nice town, people are realitively friendly and when they notice, or recognize you they are often very polite. James Kimson spent most of the day down town in Little Rock, just taking in the sites. I’d had been about 3 years since he was there last, he recalled a diner down town just a bit out of the way of the hectic downtown we all know in most major centers. He spent some time there, drinking some coffee and having a slice of apple pie, the house specialty. People recognized him, asked for a photo, or an autograph to which James was always happy to give. He spoke with fans and such and then, as he entered the arena for Legends rumors were swirling. “Hey, James! How are you man? I heard Christoph is gonna call you out tonight!” Christoph!? Why that jackass? I guess he’s a sore looser or something. Probably drunk with power since he was re-hired by Somers a week or so ago. The last few weeks of course have been pretty strange, sitting in hospital beds. Trying to find out as much as he could about his sister, and what happened to Sovereign. But he knew it was time to move on. The People wanted to see James Kimson at Legends and he wasn’t going to be doing it via TV this time. He was going to be there in person. Of course, just like his friends backstage told him, Christoph called him out. Not just James Kimson, but Scott Frost all at once. The Universal Champion, “the Man” in eVo. And regardless of how much respect James Kimson has for Scott Frost, he doesn’t have an ounce of it for Erin Christoph, the New Threat or whatever the hell he calls himself. Coming up with such amazingly witty lines as “The Hype will need a new Hip” James laughed outloud backstage when the little Curtain Jerker said that. He was pretty close to just walking out there and bitch slapping that Russian piece of trash back to Estonia or whatever country he came from. No one cared, Eric Christoph’s spotlight is about as dim as a candle burnt down to the wick, and if it was James Kimson that apparently came and took it from him, James would just as soon give it back if it meant saving him a month waiting to whip his ass at the next Pay Per View. Of course, James wasn’t ever one to think that far ahead in the future. That being said, looking out the balcony window of his hotel room in down town Little Rock, James started to think about what happened earlier that evening in the Main Event. With The mass attack on Team eVolution. He felt the only answer was to go in there, grab himself an equalizer and help out those poor people who got their heads in a noose known as The Unlimited and whatever the hell that other thing was, something starting with an N. “I guess I wasn’t ever much for remembering names” said Kimson to himself as he chuckled. So now the challenge set forth was that Frost and James were to take on Erin Christoph? Yeah that sounds funky to James. Obviously Erin is at a disadvantage, and although James is a sportsman and loves to wrestle he wouldn’t think twice about taking out Christoph with Frost and then finding out just who the man really is in eVo. Title or no title, James never felt the need to wear 30lbs of gold on his shoulder to tell the world that he was the best there is. But for the respect and for the glory of it he’s always sought after the Titles. The eVo Universal Title might just be that one piece of the puzzle known as his 12 year career that would let him rest in piece knowing he’s done it all. That there wasn’t anything left to accomplish and that he could finally go home to his wife and children and not need to take off for months at a time. Then again, maybe he would find that it’s the competition, the sportsmanship of real wrestling that drives him. After all, I doubt anyone in eVo would consider his “match” with Sovereign at Unbreakable to be wrestling. That was a war, a blood bath, a brawl. Against Scott Frost, now there was something that was an attractive prospect. “Then you get that damn Christoph jack ass again. I wonder what he’s got up his sleeve” muttered Kimson. James stepped away from the window and entered the hotel room. He walked past the bed and into the kitchenette and opened the fridge and pulled out a bottle of Scotch and poored himself a small drink. Something relaxing, pleasant to the pallet. Kimson then sat himself down in one of the chairs in the rooms, reached for the remote control for the television and sat the device on his lap. He took a sip from the glass and let the flavor circle around his mouth and then swallowed, looked at the glass in approval. “Sounds to good to be true.” he said. Afterall, obviously Christoph isn’t the sort of man to go into this by himself. He’d have to have backup, and as far as backup goes, James Kimson doesn’t have any. He hasn’t ever asked for it, and he sure as hell cannot assume, that even after twice making a save for Scott Frost, that he wouldn’t waist a second to take out James Kimson. James couldn’t blame him, he’s the champion, and if anyone knows what it’s like to be a Champion it’s James Kimson. The need to have eyes grown out the back of your head. The feeling that you just can’t trust anyone. He doesn’t know Scott Frost, he knows him to say hello, and he knows him well enough to hate to see him get his ass kicked when he doesn’t deserve it. But Christoph has something up his sleeve. Something dangerous, something that James doesn’t like one bit. He shouldn’t have ever trusted Daniel Somers, but at least he feels a bit better knowing that Tyler Davidson was back. Now the power struggle has begun and James can’t help but feel that after tonight, when he went into that ring and did a number on those cats that he just found himself smack dab right in the middle of it all. “Great” he muttered to himself. “Here we go again I guess” Factions, owners at opposite sides of the field, titles, challenges and little ole’ James Kimson stuck right in the middle. I guess that’s how he always liked it. Going where he wanted, and not having any ties to anyone but his own believes, his own morals. James took another sip from his glass of fine scotch and swallowed it, then relaxed his position in the chair before turning on the television. He flipped till he got to CNN so he could catch up on the world before going to sleep, another week was a head of him, and this week coming no doubt would see a lot of challanges ahead.
