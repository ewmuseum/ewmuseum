The Everlasting Terms

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th November {This is the kind of setting that lets the viewer know that the scenery means nothing in this particular promotional video. We fade sharply up; no fancy technological fades on this one. We’re staring at the eyes of one Called Ben Juventud, the newly converted Wiccan. His face fills the screen, his long blood-red hair falling slightly in front of his eyes. Behind him lies nothing but a black background, emptiness and completeness all in one. His face, usually with a smile cracked, is stone. His right eye has a bit of a twitch to it, the permanent bloodshot eye draws your attention. He takes a deep sigh and you find out exactly what I meant by saying that scenery meant nothing. This one is all about words.} 'Bigot.' {And with that one word he begins his verbal assault. The camera pulls back to see that he is standing in front of nothing but a black backdrop, no colour revealed. He is wearing a red cloak; the hood is pulled back revealing his hair. His beard is sharply cut and the scar on his face stands out as usual.} 'A bigot is a person that is strongly partial to their own group, race, religion, etc. is completely intolerable towards those who differ in their beliefs. My opponent has decided to label me and avoid using my actual name so I feel quite sure that it would be in my best interest to do the same thing and label him. I dub thee a bigot, Dreamer. When I first observed your promo I thought that we had much in common, things that could keep our religious differences from coming in the way of whatever could have been between us. We could have been two intellectuals on a plane that is not visited by many others in this wrestling world of cursing and nudity. Unfortunately, with several key words you did nothing but prove yourself an intolerant, insolent, ignorant being. I’m not too sure I like you much anymore.' {Ooh, gee. That hurt his feelings I’m sure.} 'I call you intolerant because as soon as you notice that I am not of the same religious convictions as yourself, you immediately jump on the boat to try to convert me. You mockingly quote my name, you talk about how I am a wanna be that’s so not right, laberling me! Dreamer I will punish you for that, I was gonna play it nice, nice and carm my first match but now, now you have bugged me, I will show you why I Benny is much better than you just because I’m not like you, so I am instantaneously wrong.' 'I call you insolent because you insult my intelligence, my beliefs, and my knowledge of my religion. I know plenty about many religions, bigot, because I did research on plenty of other religions before deciding on one that was correct for me. I know about your bible and how man is supposed to be made in the image of your God. I understand what you mean in that you are a reflection. I understand what you mean in that I am a reflection, in your opinion. You speak down to me like I am mentally inferior to you, when we’re on quite near the same playing field.' 'I call you ignorant because, unlike me, you know nothing about my beliefs. You see that I am a Mexican and immediately label me a wanna be, when there is no correlation between the two whatsoever. I am Pagan, bigot. I am a member of the religious group that focuses on Nature as divinity. Perhaps if you knew more about religions other than your own you’d know the difference between them.' {He finally cracks a little smile, we all knew it wouldn’t last too long.} 'Unenlightened.' 'The term that the bigot has decided to use for describing those that he deems as different than himself. I’ve thought about the particular definition of the word that he means to imply upon those of us unlike himself and I can’t seem to place my finger on it. Unenlightened… strangely enough describes perfectly in one sense what I’ve previously labeled my opponent to be. One of the several definitions of the terms means simply ignorant. I’ve already explained why I feel the bigot is ignorant.' 'Since I don’t feel that I am ignorant, nor am I lacking general knowledge or education, as another definition of the word implies… there is only one way I suppose he can mean to imply for me and those of us who do not believe exactly as he does. Definition number three: lacking instruction. Assuming he means that I am not instructed in the ways of his religion, then he is correct. In assuming that his religion is the only one that matters and the only one that is correct, he is more wrong than he can comprehend. Dreamer, what makes me wrong and you right? That is the only question for you that I leave.' {He pauses for a bit and closes his eyes. He keeps them close and he begins another tirade started by uttering one word.} 'Fear.' {He opens his eyes and he can’t help but shake his head a little bit.} 'Now, it is my belief that you purposely make yourself confusing in order to turn your opponent’s mind into a sponge that just can’t absorb any more information, but I honestly don’t think you purposely make yourself out to be a blatant hypocrite. Allow me to define the word ‘fear’ for you, Dreamer, and maybe you’ll understand exactly what I mean.' 'One, a feeling of disquiet or a state of worrying. You say that you have no fear, yet you ‘worry about society’s future.’' 'Two, a reason for dread or apprehension. You say that you have no fear, yet you have ‘even experienced apprehension for brief periods of time.’' 'Three, extreme reverence or awe, as in a higher power. You say that you have no fear, yet you ‘tremble before God.’' 'Either you and I speak a different language, bigot, or you don’t have such a firm grasp on the words you speak. If you worry about society’s future, even in a sense that you want to be the one to change it in the image that you see it should belong, then you have fear. If you have apprehension for even the slightest moment, even the slightest apprehension, then you have fear. If you tremble before your God, as all good religious folks should do, then you have fear. Fear, no matter how small or seemingly insignificant in your daily life, does exist in you, Dreamer, so don’t pass the word up like it is nothing. You admit through your words that you possess fear, in several senses of the word, yet you still hold claim to the fact that you are fearless. Give up the charade of complete irreverence for your human tendencies, Dreamer, and admit that you have fear. Every human being is blessed or cursed with fear, it is one of our main drives.' {He pauses for a moment, I feel a quote coming on.} 'As the brilliant author, John Milton, wrote… ‘Where no hope is left, is left no fear.’ The reverse also stands true, bigot. If you are a religious man, then your beliefs hold firm that you must also be a hopeful man. Without fear, there is no hope. Without hope, there is no fear. Unless you wish to throw the entire basis of your convictions out the window and change your beliefs, you must admit that you have fear. You must admit to being human like the rest of us. Hold on to your mild delusions of being fearless and you make yourself even more than you already are Dreamer. Even in your own fundamentalist circles you’d make yourself a heretic, a non-believer, some may even say a… {Gasp} '…Pagan.' {Benny can’t help but crack a smile after that one. The smile fades as he utters another solitary word.} 'Change.' 'To cause to be different. To give a completely different appearance to. To transform. The bigot seeks to make society different than it already is. He is out to change society into what he thinks society should be. Like so many false humble men in the past, he utters the one statement that they always try to use in order to make themselves feel as though they look to others less important than they are. ‘I am one man.’ Yes, Dreamer, you are… so why do you think you can, should, will, or even have the right to decide that society is wrong in all its doings? I too think that there are faults in society and in the way people think. I, just like you, chose to use the medium of professional wrestling in order to have my words heard. I, unlike you, am not forcing my ideas upon the ears of the other people though. I only speak of my beliefs, not that they are better than anyone else’s. By trying to convert the world, Dreamer, you only spit in their faces and say that they are wrong and that you are right. It is the ultimate form of conceited behaviour and the pinnacle of self-righteousness. ' {Breath.} 'What the f*ck makes you so special that you declare the rest of us wrong and that you are the only one in the world that decides what society should be like?! {Didn’t see that coming. Eyes closed. Deep breath. Start over.} 'I apologize. It’s not like me to curse and to shout. It just gets so frustrating when placed up against someone that, through his every word and action, feels that he is the one that is charge of saving the world. Through his blind lust for control over others, he merely shuns any alternative lifestyle other than that of his own and declares it immoral, Satanic, and wrong.' 'I’ve watched your video a few times, Dreamer, and I can’t help but laugh at a few places. I laugh when you mention that ‘the wanna be remarks.’ I understand my strengths perfectly well, and I understand yours pretty thoroughly too. I’m not sure what you mean by that, but I’m assuming you are trying to say that my deities are not the correct ones. Another place in your words that forces me to stifle laughter is when you mentioned that I have ‘no chance.’' {Pause. Smirk.} 'Dreamer, I am just as popular as you are right now. The fans of Evolution Wrestling have no idea who I am. You have absolutely no concept of who stands before you now. I am, in no sense of the word, popular. Dreamer, I suggest you learn from the definitions I’ve given you today and try to realize what you say is sheer insanity. You make no sense, through intention or through ignorance, and it makes you look like nothing but a fool.' {His eyes open fully and he stares at you completely. He sighs.} 'And even so, as we met in peace… hopefully so shall we part in peace. Blessed be.' {Sudden fade out, just as sharp as the fade in.}
