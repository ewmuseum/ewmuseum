K t.v. Episode 1

6 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
K-T.V. Episode one Revenge is a dish best served cold and full of blood, but it is even better when you can get it as soon as possible. With Savage Society’s Attack on The American Guardians they aren’t to happy as you could of most likely guessed . The Russian is so pissed he leaves the British dude all alone in a dark room as he walked out last Thursday night of the Evolution arena and hasn’t been seen for close to 5 days now. What has the angry Russian been up to you all ask? The scene opens up on the insides of a night club we can see VVM standing there dancing very slowly hardly even showing acknowledgment to what is going on around him. A bartender walks up to him and like most typical bartenders he puts his nose where it don’t belong. “What’s wrong sir?” Askes the bartender VVM looks up at the ceiling in the same trance he was in before the AG’S match vurse Team Network. “Hello, Do you not hear me or something?” Asks the bartender “I hear you, I just don’t feel like giving you the pleasure of talking to me , the greatest thing to come out of Russia like in forever.” Says VVM with a growl to his voice. (Why dose every damn bartender in this country want to try and make my problems go away. They have no idea what is like to have a monster living inside of them. They have no idea what is like to have a temper as bad as me. Savage Society are deadmen walking. ) You’re still here? Asks VVM. “Yeah, I could let a nice guy like you leave with out a dance to make you feel better.” Says the bartender with a wink and it scares VVM half to death. He looks at the bartender for the first time breaking his trance he was in and as he looks down at him he don’t know if to run , laugh or Kill the idiot. He is wearing tight spandex pants and a spandex vest it wouldn’t be so bad if it wasn’t all pink and his hair wasn’t a lime green . A VVM stare for a few seconds and the guy takes it the wrong way as he starts to flex. VVM looks around at every one else there and it is apparent through his Mask of rage he has walked into a Gay Nightclub. VVM decides he will stay and have some fun playing with the Bartenders feelings. (This guy is so f’n stupid I mean to think a Russian Such as my self would even think of being gay. Damn he should really try and get to the pansy asses of France if he wants some gay loving. I mean any country that is scared of war must be full of Gay fagots. Oh well its going to be so much fun to see if these guys think I be smoking the saugue like these fruit cakes do.) “Sure Lets dance” Says VVM trying to keep a straight face the bartender is so happy he said yes that he don’t notice that his face was filled with laughter. So VVM goes out on the dance floor with the gay bartender and starts to dance they start fast and then a slow song comes on. That’s fine VVM decides but if he touches my ass or my family jewels this man is a deadman. They start to dance slow and in now more then 30 seconds The gay bartenders hands touches VVM’S ass , Family jewels. VVM decides to do a spin move and as the guy is coming back from the spin VVM nails him with a massive clothes line that makes the Clothesline to hell look like a tap. The guy goes flying as VVM laughs and starts to walk out. “ Fuck you guys , Fuck France , Fuck Savage Society.” Says VVM as he walks out the door of a enraged club full of gay men that start to charge him. VVM is heard saying This is all Bombers fault as the camera fades out.
