Khadafi-1 Mixtapes. vol.1

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-29 eVo Incident Report: Filed by: Dan N. Date: May 29, 2003 The following audio tape arrived at the eVo front offices today at 12:45 pm via Fed Ex. It was marked in permanent marker: “X”. Besides the inscription of the alpha on a single strip of masking tape across the top of the audio cassette there are no other distinguishing marks. The following is a transcript of what was on the audio tape sent by one, Ali Khadafi, member of the eVo roster contained within the sub-shell of the newly resurrected Cell Division. *Transcript* (There is only the faint sound of traffic for several seconds before a voice, assumed to be Khadafi’s cuts in.) There’s one thing I need to point out to you, X. We are no longer a people. The black man has seamlessly joined the thick flow of human fecal matter that coats most of the northern part of the continent which you currently reside on. Do you fucking understand, or do you need someone to squeegee that glass window your parents had installed in your navel when they realized how far your nappy head was pushed up your ass? (slight pause here as Ali coughs harshly.) You can fucking fight corporate America all you want, you dick. In case you didn’t realize, the black man is part of corporate America. And those of us that aren’t out there slumming along in our little Audi TT’s are too busy trying to survive to pay attention to anything other than the day to day struggle that consumes us. We’re too busy out there cleaning the toilets of fucking corporate America to be wondering if we’re being oppressed. The black man is too busy scarfing down that McDonald’s double-cheeseburger that corporate America is shilling to notice whether he’s being oppressed or not. That black man is just damn thankful that there is a fast food joint on the corner of [detail withheld] so he can get something into his gut before he’s got to get to his second job to wonder if the “man” is holding him back. (pause) Want to know how I know that? Because I am that black man, working two jobs to put food on the plate of his kids. It makes me sick to see you come strutting out there cutting your ludicrously sophmophoric promo’s and attempting to be an educated black man. It makes me want to wrap my hands around your throat and shake some sense into that third person speaking lump you dare to call a dome. You presumptuous cock sucker. (end transcript)
