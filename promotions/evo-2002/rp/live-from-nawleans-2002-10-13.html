Live from Nawleans

13 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-13-02 New Orleans, Louisiana Mid-day It’s been a rough night for Big Daddy Dreamer. His face, still swollen from the vicious chairshot is still puffy around the eye. The doctor believes he may have a fractured orbital socket in his right eye from when HBC did her new Arabian Facebuster maneuver. Dam little minx! And as much as BDD tries to hide the redness, the swollen area with his sunglasses, the frames themselves when they come into contact with the area feels like a million hot sharp pokers stabbing his eyeball. Such is the price we pay for greatness. Though, ticked off, he doesn’t get HBC this week, no doubt due to some creative finagling, he instead will face another opponent whom BDD has his sights set on him, dead to right. Tommy Michaels. The little bottom feeding guttertrash that tried to make his mark on the world by nailing me with a chair several weeks ago. You would have thought he learned when BDD punked him out three weeks ago, but no matter. It is a good tune up match for Faith, when finally, HBC and BDD go one-on-one. As BDD steps off the first class section from his flight from Tampa, BDD has picked up a new member, albeit grudgingly. Magdalena, the mysterious woman who has seem to interject herself into BDD’s life is also along. She is striking though, long beautiful blondish hair with a killer body, nearly as tall as BDD as well with heels on. Must be about 6’ or so, but it was the fact she did try to warn BDD of HBC’s plans and was the first one to see how he was doing after he was attacked by HBC. Stayed the night in the hospital as well. Why not bring her along to New Orleans, it’s the least he could do. So together they disembark from the plane and head through the terminal over to their rental car. Its been awhile since BDD was here in New Orleans. A sparking city on the river, though below sea-level, the food is incredible here. After some discussion, it turns out Magdalena has never been in this city before. Might as well be the tour guide, take his mind off a few things. BDD drives around Jackson Square and heads over to the legendary French Quarter. They take a few pictures of the place before heading off to the Gumbo Shop (The best place for gumbo in New Orleans bar none!). The waitress sits them down and takes their order. BDD: This place is really good. Tomorrow will go to Emeril’s place. I have a table their. M: Cool. Can we see the Voodoo princess tomb? BDD: Of course, kind of spooky in a way, almost a supernatural vibe. They say that cemetery is haunted at night. All of a sudden a mysterious voice echo’s out …..of course it is. Why didn’t you tell me you were town D…… BDD looks up and takes a second to recognize the individual standing their in front of him. He smiles when he finally does. BDD: Cut your hair Trent? Trent: Something like that. Wanted something different, maybe light a spark in me. BDD: Magdalena, let me introduce to you one of New Orleans local hero’s, Trent Renzor. You may remember him from Nine Inch Nails. M: A pleasure. Yes, I’m familiar with them. I loved the song Fuck you like an animal. Trent suddenly blushes alittle and kisses her hand. Trent whistles seeing the shiner on BDD’s face. Trent: Broken? BDD: Yup. HBC. Trent: No way! I thought you two were close. BDD: Were, she went off and joined the Unlimited. Something about me holding her back or some bullshit like that. Trent: King’s group? BDD: No, they tossed him out. Guess you haven’t been following lately. Trent: Alas I haven’t I’m afraid. Been busy with my record label, working with Marilyn on a new soundtrack, Anne asked me for her new movie. She’d love to see you as well. BDD: Anytime. Well HBC split, were facing at Faith but this week I’m facing Tommy Michaels. Another royal pain in the ass who I can’t wait to bust his head in. Trent: Of course. I think some of my ex-band members would relate. Hey, I’m working on a new song that you might be interested in. Called Army of One, a heavy metal, techno feel. Could go well with you, especially now. BDD: I’ll give it a once over and let you know. Marilyn was thrilled when I accepted his. Trent: I bet, nice royalty commission, not that I don’t get to see a piece of it myself. Well, I’ll be off and let Anne know your in town, I think she’s having a party this week and would love for you both to come. BDD: K, see you their. Trent Reznor pays his bill and heads out of the shop leaving Magdalena alittle awestruck. M: Is it always like that with you? BDD: I have a lot of friends. M: Whose Anne? An ex-girlfriend? BDD: *laugh* No, no….Anne Rice, the author. Interview with a Vampire, Queen of the Dammed, she writes those Lestat novels. The female Stephen King. M: Ooooh. That one. Ok, I’m an idiot. BDD: Its ok, ah the food is here. Enjoy! M: Oh my god, this is so delicious! BDD: Told ya! Fourty minutes later, they continue walking around the French Quarter and stop into a voodoo shop. They rummage through the items as Magdalena holds up a voodoo doll dressed a lot like Tommy Michaels. M: What do you think. Should I hex him? BDD: *Laugh* sure why not. Why don’t you get it and when things look kinda bad, snap the leg right their at the knee. M: *Laugh* Yeah! Clutching his leg wondering…what the hell? I think I will. You can beat Tommy right? BDD: Of course. That is a silly question, I just like tormenting the poor bastard. Truth be told, I’ve beaten him everytime we’ve ever fought. Some reason though, he’s chair happy lately and looks like he’s trying to make his mark on Evolution by taking me out. Could HBC and Michaels be in collusion together, it’s a possibility. Likely, I don’t know. I do know Michael’s ego is as wide as the Pacific Ocean and he hasn’t amounted to anything. Never really held a title of significance and overlooked by everyone. Dangerous? Perhaps. I’m not taking him lightly, but even with my hurt face, he’s going to need more than just his A-game to beat me. M: Well if he grabs a chair, I’ll stop him. BDD: Whoa, whoa, whoa, back up. Who said you were going to ring side? No offense to you, but I don’t know if you can handle it. It’s a very dangerous place out their. These people don’t care if you get hurt or not. M: But- BDD: But nothing! You can come, you can tag along, but I don’t want you at ring side, ok? Michaels is my problem, and after Legends XII, that little butt stain will be rubbed out once and for all. HBC will be in the building and will try to go after me. If I was her, I would. I don’t need anymore distractions ok, nothing personal, just how it is around here. You seem like a great person and I don’t want to see you hurt unnecessarily. M: *sigh* Ok, I understand. I’ll be in the back with this voodoo doll, watching on the monitor putting a spell on him. BDD: That would be cool. Hey look at this……… (Fade out)
