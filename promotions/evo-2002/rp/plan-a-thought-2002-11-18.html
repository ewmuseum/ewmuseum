Plan a thought...

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th November 2002 The scene opens in a quiet location, there is a light coming from around a corner. It seems to fade in and out, and glows in different colours, there is a faint noise, and for a moment it seems like whoever is inside is watching a TV drama. The camera heads for the doorway and as it enters the room; the shot shows a TV and two people with their backs to it. From a quick glance, the figure on the left proves themselves to be Seara, and to her right, her personal trainers son. Although only 3 years older than Seara, he has for the last 4 years taken the place of his father as her trainer when he has been busy or out of the state. He has somehow got a way with Seara that makes her pay attention to him and makes her seem placid and docile when she is face to face with him, maybe it is because he is gentle with her, and knows what she likes to do, and doesn’t like to do. This however is one thing she doesn’t like to do, and it is obvious as she is beginning to fidget and look stressed out. She is sitting alongside Rhys watching videos of other wrestlers, learning their weak points; the main images on the screen are of old wrestlers that were more vicious and blood thirsty than any other eVolution wrestler, including Asis and Sovereign, turning to Rhys she looks at him disappointedly. Seara- Can we not do this another time? It’s just I have things on my mind. Rhys – Get over them, Katherine, we have to do this sometime, me Da has it planned for you, if you watch it with me then it’ll be over and done with, and you and me Da can get in the ring and train. Like his father Rhys has a strong Irish accent, although Rhys has a soothing and calm voice, which seems to make Seara more relaxed. Unlike his father though, the young male does not call the female by her wrestling name of Seara, but rather by her real name, Katherine Skyie Jackson. He is a tall thin male with blonde hair and blue eyes, and people have always managed to jump to the conclusion that he is involved with Seara. And right now she is thinking about the consequences it could have. Seara – What if he thinks this is different. People always think it’s different with you and me. I don’t want him to think it’s wrong and different, I like him, and your great, you do so much for me as a trainer and I don’t want him to think I destroyed his life and then changed my mind, I really don’t want him to think the wrong thing. Rhys – Katherine Skyie Jackson, listen to yourself, don’t go back to the paranoid place. He knows its nothing. He knows I train you and that is as far as it goes. And you never ruined his life, she did that, she lost out on him, and now its your turn, be happy and concentrate on your job, you are here to learn techniques and ways you can use when you are in the ring against monsters like Asis. He is not your only worry, but you can’t focus on just one person, except yourself. When you are in the ring or in your attire, you are Seara, but outside of that, you are Katherine, I know to you, you are the same thing both inside and outside the ring. The queen of Notorious, Scott Frost’s queen, but you are different. You never used to be different, but Notorious have changed you. You have emotions you never showed, you care about people, you love a man for more than you know. You are no longer just a cold-hearted killer, you are a woman, you realize you want and need more from life. But blood will always be what you need more than anything. I have always known that about you, my father knows that about you, and your father is scared because of it. That ruthless killer in you was the same thing in him that began to end his career; he was too blind to see it though until it was too late. Seara is quick to her feet; her fists clenched tight, she swings at thin air and grits her teeth. Seara – He knew what he was doing, he was never blinded by rage, he was focused. I am not my father; I thought I could live up to his standards, to his reputation, but I can’t, I will never be like my father, and I am proud of that. I destroyed him, his career faltered because of me, and now I am living up to the devil he had deep within him, he was never in control of his demon, but I am, I will succeed where he could not, I will destroy the biggest the toughest, I will become the best, I will outgrow his standards. I will head out there and I will prove them wrong, everyone that doubted me, everyone that doubts me still after all I have done. I have proved myself more than once, but I will not stop, I will not allow myself to ever think I can do no better, I will find something, someone, another thing to cause pain and I will always be looking for a way to control the demons. They will be released one day, I am not sure when though, maybe now, maybe tomorrow, maybe next week, maybe a month or a year, or maybe not for a long long time, but when it happens, I will be the demon that I have hidden that I have feared, the thing in me that kills me, eats me, tortures my soul. Unlimited, The Omen Brotherhood, New-TV, you will all feel the change in me, you will know what torture really is, but you are not the only people that will know this pain in me. Others will notice it, and maybe even feel the strength it gives me. Seara pauses as she hears the door open, and a figure walks in to see her standing only feet away from Rhys, talking to him a frustrated tone. His hands on her shoulders trying desperately to calm the female down so that she will focus on the now instead of the pain she has known her entire life. The figure is recognised as Scott Frost, and he just stares at the female with a shocked expression as Rhys looks into her eyes in a way that seems to Frost to be more than it is. The scene starts fading to black as Seara smiles at Frost, calms down and walks away from Rhys towards the male wrestler. When she is halfway between them, she stops and turns to finish talking to Rhys as Frost watches on.
