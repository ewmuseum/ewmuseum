Unforgiving

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.22.02 {Many people can say that they forgive and forget and those same people would probably tell you that its human nature. While that may be true for some its not for Shocka, he still has immense dislike for Scott Frost. But frost gained his respect when he didn’t take advantage and win the title after Omega put Shocka down. He respects the fact that he did his best to eliminate Omega from the match and then fight for his life in the rest of the match. The outcome may not have been favorable for Shocka but that does not mean that he will be UN sportsman like just because he didn’t get his way.} ~The chance for revenge is now just over the hill, it’s around the corner…are you ready for it? Shocka, Kimson, Omega and Sovereign all in one ring. Four combustible elements all going at it in a tag match style come Legends. The problems between the four men have been well documented, Shocka and Kimson don’t see eye-to-eye but they are partners…Kimson and Sovereign are bitter enemies, and Omega and Shocka are also enemies.~ ‘What he did to me cannot ever be forgiven.’ The grief stricken voice of Shocka rings out. ‘He will be taken to his limits and beyond at Legends, I am his mentour and he is the fucking student how can he think that he will beat me?! Just because he got the opportunity of a lifetime to pounce on me while I was down doesn’t mean shit!’ Its obvious that Shocka is speaking about Morpheus Omega as he sits with a sick grin on his face. ‘I am a history maker I am the only person in eVolution to be crowned Universal and Continental champion damnit! Shit I won the road to glory tournament with my hands tied behind my back…I ended L2J…a move that I may turn out to regret…’ He says solemnly to himself as he sits back in his chair. ‘Things have changed drastically since Thug roC left…we lost control of the stable that I once loved to be a part of. Once he was gone it all went to hell and the power struggle started…myself and Omega fighting over who was gonna be the leader but in the end everyone knew that I should be the one to hold that power in my damn hands. I mean how could he even compete with me…sure he may be a monster inside of the ring but I taught him that, he may be able to play mind games but I taught him how! I wanted him to have the advantage when he was watching my back and now he is gonna turn that shit on me?!’ Shocka shakes his head in a moment of disbelief. ‘I mean how could he be so fucking ungrateful! All of the success that he has garnered thus far has been because of me…except on some occasions when he formed the resurrection but I was behind that too…in more ways than you know at least…’ ~Shocka stands up from his chair and walks over to a table set up with some alcohol he smiles and picks up a bottle of Skyy Vodka he opens it and pours some in his mouth. He cringes a little as the taste of alcohol runs down his throat into his stomach.~ ‘You know…I am going to show Omega why they call me the #1 Stunna…I am going to show the human monster why I was Universal champion for 2 months…and I will show everyone why he didn’t want a shot at me he was scared! But now he gets his chance at me in the ring me and him…I will show everyone out there in TV land why I am still the best in the business.’ Shocka laughs a minute and drinks some more Vodka before continuing. ‘Now this one is out to James Kimson my partner for the night against Omega and the human monster himself. I’ve got no problem with you Kimson but if you do get in my way I will make sure that you never walk with your own power ever again! I want you to understand that this is not a friendship that we have it’s simply a business relationship and if you fail to deliver I will make sure that I deal with you accordingly!’ Shocka says before sitting back down in his chair as the camera slowly fades away.
