roC Superstar: Hedges= The Used (Ch.4)

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.14.03 ~His smile seemed innocent enough at first, but it soon turned into something more it was an evil smirk that he had plastered across his face as he received a fax. The fax had his opponent for the next week at Legends it read… Shocka vs. Hedges two men that know each other better than ever, Hedges is delusional about the win/loss record but Shocka is ready to prove that he is wrong. He doesn’t care what lengths he will have to go to, to prove that he is the better man.~ ‘How about this…’ Shocka started in a somber tone. ‘Me and you Hedges clear the record right here right now expel everything else off of it me and you are 0-0 and settle this little on going 3 year feud we’ve had…eh?’ Shocka says with a wink. ‘Or are you not man enough to put the past behind you, are you scared that I am going to get the upper hand once and for all and put you to shame?! Is that why you can’t let the past go, is it because you realize I am so many times better than I was back when you got a few wins over me…I let my ego go…I left my big head in the past and you know what it got me here in eVolution?! 1x Continental champion, the only road to glory champion, and the universal fucking title…but what have you done since being here? Issued an empty threat to the eVolved champion but you know what…now you got me snipping at your heels and this time I am not going to let you go with just a little nibble this time I take a hand!’ Shocka says standing intensely looking at the camera. He stands for a moment glaring at his own body in a mirror the white t-shirt that he bears is completely filled with his muscles Shocka smirks a bit and continues speaking. ‘I have evolved Hedges but that sad thing is that you haven’t in the least! You cannot move on…you cling to those wins you have over me and you know that they do mean something because if they didn’t why would you base your whole damn life around them?! Admit it Hedges I am everything that you strived to be in the NRCW I was the only grand slam champion there…hell did you even win the world title there?! Our past is about to become ancient history because I am going to finish this thing once and for all!’ Shocka says outlining his prior point. ‘But Hedges, there is one thing about this match that does concern me…that is the fact that New-TV is going to be all over the fucking arena and most likely out at ringside during our match just like at Unbreakable when it took 4 of you to put me inside of an ambulance…now what is holding them back this time?! Nothing, Hedges and you need to come to the realization that you are not better than me…but that you just caught me on a few bad days…I was in a rough spell as I recall when you beat me about 3 of the times in that many weeks…after I lost the World title in NRCW and suffered a dislocated shoulder…I went against doctors orders to face you because I have respect for you and I can at least admit that…’ Shocka says as he sits back down and stops studying himself in the mirror he now directs his full attention to the camera. ‘See Hedges you were right when you told roC how to get to me…get under my skin right? Well that used to work but now, I don’t play your mind games…I am the one that showed Omega the way…and he is the one that you brought you up to what you were…and you see he is using you much like I did him to get what he wants…they will talk you up to do whatever they want that is a game that they learned from me! You will turn out just like Omega a worthless sack of shit stuck in the tag division and ever since I fought him I have been taken out of the main event he brought me down…isn’t that horrible Hedges? Well its your future if you wanna be a roC superstar!’ Shocka says with a sort of evil smile about him. ~A knock comes at the door and you see it swing open for Elmer to walk in he smiles and looks at Shocka. He is also wearing a white T-shirt and the same black billabong shorts that Shocka has on. He smiles and looks at the camera.~ ‘Imma beat Hedges’ ass!’ Mini Shocka screams deepening his voice. ‘And you could…but that’s my job this week…maybe you can get in the ring and square off with him next week…’ Shocka says with a smile and then he looks back to the camera. ‘You thought what we did last week was bad…well wait until you see what we have planned this week…’ Mini Shocka says. ‘No…’ Shocka says in a stern voice. ‘That was a one week deal…nothing more like that will be seen from us…’ Shocka says. ‘From now on we take a business approach to everything and we show everyone that we mean business we will not bring down team eVolution because of the sons a bitches in New-tv.’ Shocka says looking down at Elmer who frowns. ‘Alright boss.’ Mini Shocka says as he turns with his head drooped and walks out of the room still sad. ‘Ahh the little guy will get over it as soon as he sees what I have in store for Hedges…if he had the chance to watch the tape of what I did to Omega I swerved him better than the whole sky night return thing…I busted out a whole new style that no one has seen from me because I knew he would study all he could…but he kept to his hard ass wanna be stiff style, but hey if it hadn’t been for new-tv I would have had the match. So Hedges you can watch every fucking tape that you want but you will not expect anything that I throw at you…’ Shocka says as he smiles and walks away.
