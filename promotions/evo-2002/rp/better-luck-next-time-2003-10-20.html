Better luck next time

20 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-20 Scene opens in the infamous title room, located in the Thug roC’s household. The camera does it’s usual panning, showing each and every title, trophy, and award that is located in the room. Thug roC sits back in his chair, tilting all the way back, while watching a tape of last week’s Legends. With the straight look on his face, and rubbing his United Title that lies across his chest, he grabs the remote control and turns the TV set off. He leans up on in his chair and places the United Title on his desk. “People expect me to come here to you all and explain my actions. They expect me to give you a reason why I would attack Shaft-Man and betray a so-called friend. But that’s the thing, I never do what’s expected of me. I DON’T HAVE TO TELL YOU SHIT! Instead, I will leave the situation as it is, and leave it alone. Why should I have to go and explain to you pitiful fans why I did what I did? Why do I need to explain my actions to people that could care less of me?” “If I were to hurt my knee in the middle of the ring, would you all care? Hell no, instead you’d expect me to limp around and continue. And for what? Because you all paid your money to see me risk my life? Because you cheap fans couldn’t even come to see me, but rather sit on your ass at home and turn on the TV? You couldn’t even come and watch me perform, but you want me to risk me body….yea…right.” “You fans know nothing about what I do. You all just sit back and think you know, but you really don’t. Your fat lazy asses sit at home with one hand on the remote and the other on your cock. And if you’re not watching us in the ring, then your hand is on the keyboard, trying to criticize eVo, or PRIME, or some other federation that you think needs critiquing.” “No, I won’t do what you expect of me. I won’t please you ungrateful fans and give you more to laugh at, or to correct. I refuse to feed fuel to the fire. So for you that thought I’d explain why I took Shaft-Man out during our match…Better Luck next Time.” “On to a more important topic, but an unimportant person, is that Scott Evans. A guy who all of his life been some sort of failure in one way or another. No wife, no kids, what a failure. And lets not even touch on the fact about how he lost his evolved title to some new guy that was picked up off the street. And for him, to have the balls to call me a coward…I have something for him. But I’m probably the least of his worries. After all, he just pissed off Shane Wethers. I could only imagine how pissed Wethers would be, he just had his title shot at the United Title, only to have Evans screw it up. Feel sorry for Evans, he has a lot on his hands nowadays.”
