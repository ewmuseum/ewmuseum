The underappreciated minority

14 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/14/02 Malcolm King is seen with the entire contingent of Evolution Referees. The look to have a united and determined look on their faces as malcolm King steps forth and speaks on their behalf.... 'Dan...Dan...Dan; I understand you are stressed but for you to act in a blind and reckless manner is so unlike you. Sure you run the company and can make arbitrary decisions.....but the one you made at Legends, may be your most costly one to date.' He walks around as some of the officials nod their head..... 'It wasn't until I became an official that I didn't appreciate what these men go through during the course of doing their job with little to no credit or respect. So for you to overturn an official's judgement due to what admittedly may be your personal perception on why I was out there to officiate a match, what makes me declaring a match a no contest any different than Lou here?' camera pans to a portly referee who just nods his head.... 'Your arguement that I was trying to screw Asis is a weak one S., for you see if I wanted to do that.....Xavier would have won that match via DQ, afterall Xander did strike an official which is automatic disqualification; but more on that later. You see S. the reason why these men aren't respected by the wrestlers....is because you are constantly undermining their authority.' some refs begin to nod their heads..... 'Well I for one can't sit by and allow it to happen this way. The referee's deserve a little better than what you are giving them. Let's face it...it was my first match as an official and if you could treat me with the blatant disregard you have shown me, someone who at one time was a peer of yours in this business....what is stopping you or your wrestlers from doing the same to a guy like Lou...Joel....or AL?' The room enmass nod their head as a slight murmur starts amongst the referees.... 'Well my new Brothers and I; have come up with a course of action. I was hired to help with maintaining order here and apparently the lawlessness plaguing Evolution starts at the top with the old regime. So S. we have elected to go on strike as of today. The referees of Evolution will not tolerate this callous treatment by you any more. Unlike the wrestlers, the referees are unionized as protection against acts of aggression. So what does a picket line represent to Evolution?' the room falls quiet as Malcolm gets a little more serious..... 'During the PPV the unionized arena workers will not cross the picketline, as well as what could be estimated as 1/3 of your fan based who might also be unionized workers, security, lighting and rigging, among other services...you see no one wants to cross a picketline while their union bretheren are being treated unfairly.' As the referees start to chant strike.... Malcolm develops a slight little smile on his face.... 'This could be avoided however as far as Evolution negotiating in good faith with the referees union. First the Frost v. Asis matchup should represent a compromise....at the PPV, including Xavier Citizen and making it a three way dance would represent good faith. Secondly...providing the referees more power to enforce the rules, hence making them apart of the process and not having their decisions quetioned or overturned due to politics....in return we will offer up a review panel for all officials to aid in weeding out those who are not up to par. We can begin negotiations at the PPV S. with me representing the referees union. You make the match and we agree to work the PPV and the following Legends while we continue negotiations.' The referee's seem to have a unified force behind Malcolm King.... 'Lastly we must address Xander, that issue must have a resolution before the PPV. You see S., when I was assigned a referees license knowing the bad blood between me and some of the people on the roster we negotiated a clause where if I don't attack them....they won't attack me. Thug Roc and Stormbringer thought suspension would be an appropiate deterant. If Xander had an issue with my officiating then there are ways and channels for him to address his dispute. But stiking an official...something must be done. We are willing to waive the one year suspension for lets say.....a thirty day one that bans him from anything Evolution related. Dan I suggest you address these issues soon before.....other means are used and it's taken out of your hands.' the chant of strike strike strike is heard as the scene fades to black....
