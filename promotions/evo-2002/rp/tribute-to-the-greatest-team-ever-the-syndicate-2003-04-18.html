Tribute to the greatest team ever: The Syndicate

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-18-03 Toronto, Canada Syndicate International Early Evening Well The Syndicate has finally returned to their true form, causing several hijinks along the way. First was the out of the blue victory for Tribute, upsetting a certain team who shall remain nameless for sake of embarrassment. Then was the sudden winner take all match this week with Tribute with the #1 contender title shot on the line. The Bodysnatchers, scared at the return of their old ways, decided to forfeit their match to The Syndicate instead of facing their wrath. Lastly, was the match they have a unique interest in, The Network against Night/Omega. Two teams which want to be in The Syndicate position. The Network, a team who has drawn the ire of The HeartBreak Chick and Big Daddy Dreamer in recent weeks, steaming from the set-up stabbing of BDD to the tablebomb of HBC. It is because of those actions, The Syndicate returned to true form. And the other team, a virtual mirror of The Syndicate. A dominate male and female intergender team, but unlike them, we have done it all in this business. Broke new ground, doing the impossible. The impossible shall continue once again as they lead the charge to the undisputed Evolution Tag Team Championship titles. One member, the first lady of Evolution and the most dangerous female athlete on the planet has already held those titles twice, but not with her longstanding partner. An asterisk on her reign that she wants righted. The other, the former Universal and Continental Champion whose fury is raging deep in his eyes, waiting to be unleashed. But for now, other matters beckon their calling. Namely the team of Tribute. A team who imulates the past glories of the famed Midnight Express. Easton and Lane. One can’t help wonder where Jimmy C is floating around waiting to swing his loaded tennis racket. But much like their demise at the hands of LOD, The Rockers, even the Killer Bees, they have never faced anything like the team they are about to face this week. They seem to know their history, have respect for the business, but unfortunately, they also hold something the Syndicate wants. The title shot to reclaim, and most of all, unify the fragmented tag title scene. As history is about to collide, extreme unrestricted warfare is about to be released on the world, and blaze Evolution into new, and perhaps dreaded heights. At Syndicate International, the global headquarters of the multi-billion dollar conglomerate, their co-owners, Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick are busy upon the 81st floor of the training center. Three floors in height, open, spacious, and home to the most state of the art training facility in the world, it is here they perfect their craft. Train to unparalleled condition. One cannot wage a war if they aren’t properly prepared for it. So it is here, HBC on the bench press, lifting 245 pounds with ease, going through her 21 repetitions without breaking a sweat. BDD over on the agility drills, flying through twists and turns, like some kind of crazed Matrix machine. Wednesday night, Tribute against The Syndicate for the #1 contender spot and a match for the titles. What other unique tricks do they have up their sleeve, only time will tell…….
