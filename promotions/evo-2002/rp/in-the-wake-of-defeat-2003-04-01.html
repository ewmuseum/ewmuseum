In The Wake Of Defeat

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st April 2003 A dark room filled with silence and a single silhouette, or so it would seem. The flicker from a candle light in the corner of the room illuminates two figures, one sitting up leaning back against the headrest another lying down by their side. The distant flame illuminates Seara’s face as she lifts herself up and looks around the room, her eyes meeting the patch of floor where her title once lay, now only Scott Frost’s belt lays there. The female sighs for a moment then turns her head slightly, not looking to Frost but past him in a strange manner that he has never seen from her before. ‘You all right?’ his voice is soft and relaxed and he stares confused at the outline of the female in front of him. ‘Yeah, just thinking’ the females reply is off putting, but she somehow gulps back any emotion or thought she has that would spoil the moment and leans towards Frost to kiss him. She eventually sits back away from him, her eyes glancing around the room. ‘Your not disappointed with me are you?’ her voice has a nervous ring to it. ‘About what?’ asks Frost in reply to Seara. Turning the female nods in the direction of the Universal Belt and Scott becomes aware of what the female is thinking about. Reaching out he pulls her into him and lays her down so that her head rests against his toned body. Her fair skin stands out against the darker tanned skin of Frost. Resting her head on him, she places her free arm across his body and hugs him for a moment, reassured by his actions that he is not disappointed with her, but even this is not what Seara wants, she just needs to hear it, and Frost knows this. Lifting her head up he places a hand on each check and stares into her eyes, softly rubbing her skin. ‘Katherine, the belt was not you, you are what and who I love, not that belt, the belt was just a way of proving what I already knew, you are the queen of eVolution wrestling’ Frost grins in a devilish way and Seara pushes him sideways knocking his head and back away from the headrest which sends him into the mattress. ‘I’m serious Scott, I was the longest running title holder in eVolution, no one else has been able to hold a title as long as me, and I lost it to that little dick, that’s pretty useless if you ask me.’ ‘Well I’m not asking you. Katherine, believe me, the belt is nothing, did you have that belt when we meet…?’ pausing Frost looks at the female and she shakes her head. ‘ Well then…why would it matter now? Its just a belt with writing on it, so don’t be a fool.’ Once again Frost pauses and this time he begins to shuffle around in the bed, holding onto Seara’s arms he gently moves her, and within a moment, she is laying completely on top of him, looking down into his eyes and smiling. ‘Now, I think we were still celebrating my birthday, and you wouldn’t want the birthday boy to get upset would you’ his remark is quick and he has a sly look about him, but it is something Seara has come to see as a quality and running her fingers through his hair that has fallen backwards, she begins to kiss him and the scene fades out.
