Last Breath

25 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 25h Hope is not lost. Or so it seems after last night’s Rise of the Legends Pay Per View. When L2J reached to the stars and pulled down that Universal Title a world took an a deep breath of air. Finally, someone had managed to take the Title off of Sovereign’s grasp for the first time. 14 weeks of dominance came to an end last night with Impact’s help. Easily the most shocking event from last night when Impact grabbed a hold of Sovereign’s leg when he was going after L2J to stop him from winning. From below the ring came a ghost of Evolution’s past. A Ghost of Sovereign’s past, a man many thought was finished, retired never to return to the ring. The problem with assumptions are that they aren’t ever grounded in solid fact. While Impact’s near 3 month absence was sorely missed his return surprisingly is gladly received. Once one of eVo’s most hated superstars he returned last night to settle a score with Sovereign. He drew first blood, but now the question is, how is Sovereign going to react? Most think that Sovereign is the king of over reacting. He’s snapped and placed the most innocent in hospital, Impact is a prime case. And while Impact wasn’t loved, wasn’t seen as the most fan-friendly person he most certainly was in the wrong place at the wrong time when Sovereign slammed a door on his head all those weeks ago, purely out of psychotic rage. Sovereign’s reign continued, more men fell to his power but last night, perhaps a step was taken to give all those people some retribution. We all know of Sovereign’s love for the Universal Title. He claimed it was all he was. Just a man, with a claim over all who walked this planet. All who worked in this business. Now it’s gone, lost at the hands of someone who wasn’t even supposed to be involved... Impact, the Hollywood Monster returned last night, leaving Sovereign laying in a pool of his own blood after one of the most epic wars in the history of the sport. A dark room, nearly pitch black forms on the screen, the only sounds are of the human breath. Laboured and painful. You can hear the drops of water falling from a pipe and hitting the floor in a pool of it’s predecessors. The sound of the water cuts the room like a knife. Foot steps are hear walking on the floor above whatever room this scene is in. Only the sound of laboured breathing... drops of water. Body movements, the sound of someone shifting his or her position where they sit. A mild groan.... a breath of air... a grunt of discomfort. Drip... Drip... Drip... A gasp of pain, followed with a shift of body weight again. Silence is cut with the dripping of the water. Drip.... Drip.... Drip... The one thing that made him what he was. The Universal Title around the waist of another man must drive a man like Sovereign wild. We’ve seen him “wild” before. Where do you think this will take him next? Anyone’s guess I suppose. Who will he be after? L2J or Impact? Perhaps both men will have to contend with Sovereign’s feelings. No doubt his anger and rage has been redirected, refocused with perhaps a new goal. Now the Universal might not mean anything compared to retribution. Maybe Impact’s actions have brought out the monster inside the monster. The Hollywood monster.... the Human Monster.... what is worse? Drip.... Drip.... Drip.... You can see some movement from a distant corner. Nothing that you can put your finger on. The light, however faint it is caught a glimpse of something, or someone. The person lets out another hard exhale of air. It sounds painful. It sounds like hell. A grunt of discomfort follows it almost like a symphony exchanging phrases. A dark, evil piece of music. Drip... Drip... Drip... Quite. Nothing, no sound. Drip.... Drip.... It’s lungs expand, it sounds like someone just was stabbed with the quick and sudden stop of the breath. It sounds un-natural. It sounds like a wounded animal waiting to die. Or waiting to get back up. Then a violent cough. Drip.... Drip.... Impact The voice, hurt, injured in it’s expression. But you know that it’s Sovereign. You know who’s voice it is. You know it because you’ve heard it so many times. But you’ve never heard it like this. Pain floats on his breath, his voice. You know that Sovereign took some serious punishment at Rise of The Legends. You saw that contest, the bumps, the falls, the weapons. The Final Chokeslam through the ring. You almost felt it because of how violent it was. Sovereign took the punishment that so many feel he deserved. Now, a wounded man, laying in the corner of a dark room. Impact. Drip.... Drip.... Drip.... A painful breath. Followed by the exhale of a broken body. Impact
