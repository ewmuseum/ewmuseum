Episode 1: The Cogs of War are grinding...

18 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th of February, 2003 The day is in full swing with the lunch time traffic at its peak when a yellow cab pulls up by the filthy curb in New York City, outside a tall high rise building. The doors of the cab swing open and two men, a Japanese man and an American, step out of the vehicle. The Japanese man wearing black and gold Adidas sweats, the other in baggy jeans and an eVolution polo shirt. They step up onto the curb, the Japanese man steps ahead as the American glances up and down the sidewalk, soaking in the Big Apple's surrounds. He smiles to himself as he follows his companion into the building. The Japanese man waits for his friend at the counter, as the receptionist just stares at him, waiting for him to say something. She clears her throat, trying to get the man's attention. 'Uh... can I help you sir?' she questions the man waiting at the counter. 'Getting Tommy DiCastelletta now,' he demands of the receptionist. 'Telling him Karma Dragon here. Telling him Cogs of War here.' The lady looks at him with complete disgust with his lack of manners. She shakes her head and glares at him. 'I'm not your errand boy, sir. How would you like me to call on security?' she asked the man who called himself Karma Dragon. He smiled and looked by the door, where two guards were standing in case of emergency. He nodded contently as he sized them up. His American friend passed by straight to the counter. 'Just call ahead and tell Tommy that Arty Manton and Tomomasa Man'Drin are here to see him, OK?' he told the receptionist. 'OK, sir. He's on the seventh level,' she informed him. 'Heh, he's moving up. Moving up and out of the way, the little worm,' he chuckled to himself. He made his way to the elevators where a group of people were waiting on a lift. He sighed as he looked at the crowd, knowing that he'd have to share it with these people. Share it with them and their smells. Arty pushed through the crowd with Tomomasa, waiting at the front of the line. 'Hey buddy, we've been waiting for five minutes, get in line,' some guy spat at him. Arty turned his head to face whoever it was trying to order him around. 'Well, sorry Sergeant. I didn't realise this was your elevator. Here, why don't you take my spot?' Arty offered the guy. 'Hey, no need to get wise. I was just telling you we've been waiting a while,' reiterated the guy. Arty smiled and tilted his head to the side. 'Aw, poor baby's been waiting. Listen, worm, take your pacifier out of your ass and recognise the greatness before you. Don't you know who the Hell I am?' asked Apple. The guy looked to the other people in line. They shook their heads and shrugged with confusion. Arty slapped his forehead. Tomomasa still waited for the elevator to arrive, not realising his buddy was arguing with the folks around them. 'I can't believe you don't know who I am. You really don't know who I am? I am Arty Apple. I appeared on Legends last week. Surely you people watch Legends,' he told the crowd. They began to shake their heads in confusion. Arty slapped his forehead as the crowd murmured. 'Shut your dirt holes, worms. Listen in to what I'm talking about. eVo. eVolution? The wrestling federation. Surely you worms have heard of that?' he queried. They nodded as they'd heard of the promotion. Arty came forward to the crowd. 'Me and my friend,' he turned Tomomasa around to face them, 'were on the show last week. On Legends. We were talking with S.,' he informed the crowd. They stare at them blankly. Arty's face got red and he started to huff. 'My words are wasted on pathetic worms like you. You ought to be ashamed of yourselves. You call yourselves wrestling fans and you don't even appreciate the greatness of Cogs of War? Pffft. 'You people make me sick. It makes me even sicker to come from this tragic city. It's true what they say about New Yorkers. Well, you are ignorant to the rest of the world. You don't even know a true legend when you see one. Not just one, but two. 'Two legends. You're looking at Cogs of War. Does that mean anything to you inept bottom feeders? I doubt it would, considering that if I had a watt for each of your I.Q. points I couldn't light my refrigerator. Take one long look at these asses people, drool a little and then store it in your spank bank, because the next time you see them will be as we climb the ladder, above you, to greatness.' The crowd boos as Arty crosses his arms as if his job were done here. He grinned sadistically as the elevator had arrived with a ding. Tomomasa entered the lift as Arty just stood there, he raised a single hand. 'Hold the lift a second, Tomomasa,' he demanded his brother in arms. Tomomasa pressed the button for the lift to remain on the floor, in wait of his tag team partner. Arty stepped forward to the angered crowd. 'Take a look at Cogs of War, worms. Take a long hard look so next time you watch eVo's Legends you will all cream your panties and dream of being this damn good. Dream of being this damn great. 'Worms, Cogs of War has arrived in eVo and for the pleasure of your gratitude alone, we will make a demonstration this Saturday. We're scheduled to face Big Daddy Dreamer in a handicap match. The only handicap is that he will leave in a wheel chair. Well, that's if they can find enough of him to scoop into the seat. 'The Cogs of War are grinding bones and broken homes. Watch us. Learn from us. Respect us,' he demanded of the crowd before him. Tomomasa just nodded in agreement as the crowd threw rude comments and gestures at Big Apple. Arty turned and strolled into the lift, only occupied by his partner. The doors began to close and he called out just before the doors shut. 'Worms.'
