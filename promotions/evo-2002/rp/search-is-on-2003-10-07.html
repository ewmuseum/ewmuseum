Search is On

7 October 2003

Date: 2003-10-07 He’d done it. It wasn’t really surprising; he didn’t expect anything less. Nothing would have kept him from Xander, that opportunity was one that he was not going to pass. They could’ve put a seven-nation army in that ring with him and one way or another he would’ve torn it apart. And now he had it. His chance at Xander at Faith. And it was whatever match he wanted it to be as well. That brought him a wry smile in the darkness of his life, he could get Xander any way that he wanted. But that could wait. He wouldn’t make any rash decisions about that one. He’d think long and hard about that. He wanted to give Xander a treat. That was if that whore Leya Forth lived up to her end of the bargain and found Xander. Dragged him out from whatever rotten, stinking place he was hiding in. And of course, he’d have to live up to his end of the bargain. He couldn’t touch anyone in eVo unless in a sanctioned match. That could be difficult, but at the same time he had what he wanted anyway. He wasn’t going to throw away this shot at Xander, no way. That was if Leya could find Xander. If not, he’d just go back to what he was doing before, eventually there’d be nobody left on the roster, maybe that would piss her off enough to make her look harder? Or perhaps just take out one of the “higher profile stars” on the roster? That would definitely get her attention. This was all hypothetical of course. She might find Xander tomorrow; she might have found him by Legends. But then again, maybe not. Maybe he’d be forced to take more action. Who could tell? But he knew that the next couple of weeks would be fun whatever happened…
