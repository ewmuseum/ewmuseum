Date: 2003-09-21 The scene opens in the back yard of Scott Frost and Seara. Rain is falling heavily. A figure can be seen hooded and cloaked, standing out in the rain. Its dark and not much can be seen apart from the red glow of a cigarette. A door can be heard opening and Scott frost appears on the sheltered porch of the house. A cold wind blows and he rubs his arms and runs out across the grass to the dark figure. By the time Scott reaches Seara, he is soaked through, his tight white top see through and his trousers seemingly heavy because of the water. “What the hell are you doing out here Katherine?” he asks, putting his arm around the females waist. She stands completely still except for the odd movement made when she is smoking. Scott turns her to face him, and he can see something dramatically different about the female. She looks ready to say something, but stops herself before any words are spoken. Scott smiles lovingly and walks the female back up to the porch and in through door. Guiding her up stairs to their room where he takes off his shirt and dries himself down before grabbing another shirt and putting it on. “You forget about us going out for a meal tonight?” He asks. The female shakes her head, she is still hooded and sits on the bed and watches the male change. “I’ve been the one lately telling everyone that this is a new era, The Era Of Frost, I’ve told people how much of a change it is, told everyone how much I’ve changed, but the truth is Scott, I’m still physically the same woman you meet when you came to eVo, I’m the same person physically that started eVo over a year ago now. You know mentally I’ve changed like a million times, but my appearance has always been what you seen this morning before I left to go out.” At this last sentence, Scott spins around on the balls of his feet and stares shocked at the female. She smiles from under the huge hood. “I needed a change Scott, I needed a change to suit myself, I’m not the same woman as I was, and I’m not that little girl who was scared of you when you first approached me. I’m stronger mentally and physically. HBC kinda helped push me that extra little bit last week. I mean that little girl that was scared of you when you came to talk to her is long gone, and she never would have done what I did last week. I need to physically mature Scott, because my change came a long time ago. From now on, I really will prove that this is a new era. I don’t know what you will say or how you will react, but everything will be different, right down to the little things, right down to my entrance music and my in ring attire. My trainer has helped me with that, his wife likes to design clothing and she has worked with me the past few days, working on my new image. That’s where I’ve been Scott, I haven’t been stepping out on you, and I haven’t been training all the time, I’ve been changing. And today, I made the biggest change of all, I…I…I don’t know if you’ll like me, and I know it will take a lot to get used to, but I like it Scott, and I hope that that is enough for you to be happy and grow to love the new change.” Seara takes to her feet and walks across the room, and hits play on the large stereo system. In Memory by Shinedown begins to play, and turning, Seara removes unbuttons the long jacket, and then takes it off so that the hood gently slides back. Scott gasps and smiles, he is speechless and can do nothing but smile at the female’s new image. “I’ve been thinking about this for a long time, you know changing how I look, and this is the final outcome” remarks Seara as she looks down at herself. Reaching forward, Scott runs his hands through her soft hair. “I love it, and I love you” he replies looking at his wife, gently he puts his arms around her and kisses her softly on the lips. Parting from his wife, Scott continues to change clothes as Seara stands smiling before joining him. Once changed, she stands in front of a mirror with Scott behind her, a long black dress tightly fitting emphasised her curvy figure and brought out her new style clearly, her shoulder length blonde hair styled sat perfectly around her face and the use of a small amount of make-up had dramatically changed the females entire look. As Scott kissed the females shoulder, the scene faded out.
