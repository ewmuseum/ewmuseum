Fragile

18 November 2003

Date: 2003-11-18 “Scott….” The screaming fills the room and the scene quickly fades in to a dark room, not much can be seen except that which is illuminated by the small strip of light entering the room under the door. The female’s scream suddenly becomes clearer, more intense as she jumps from a dead sleep, wakened by the touch of Scott Frost’s hand. “Scott…I was…I was dreaming about it, I could feel you fall, I…I was there again” stammered the female. A light alongside the bed was switched on and the scene became clear and illuminated with a white glow from the bulb. Scott lay in the hospital bed, not sure what time of night it was. He had woke from an uneasy sleep full of discomfort and memories of what had happened to him. He had woken to the sounds of his wife screaming in fear, her arms crossed on the edge of the bed and her head buried down into the covers, he could hear her cry and he knew she was scared and uneasy. Turning his head to the side he had looked at her and slid his arm over the covers and gently took hold of his hand, suddenly and sharply waking her from the sleep that seemed to disturb her deeply. She had lifted her head from the bed, tears running down her face, and Scott had placed his hand on her cheek. She was safe now, but now she knew it was real. “Gave you a fright back there did I? I’m sorry Katherine” he remarked as he wiped the tears from the female’s eyes. “Hey, I thought I had sent you home to get a real nights sleep anyway” he added smiling. At this, Seara’s eyes glinted and she smiled in her own child like manner. “I couldn’t leave you. I couldn’t go home alone, I couldn’t bear to be there alone Scott,” remarked Seara. She looked Scott in the eyes and seemed to freeze in place and time. “I want you home Scott. I don’t want to lose you…I’m…I’m…I’m going to get a drink, you want anything?” “That’s not what you were going to say Katherine…what's wrong, tell me” “No Scott, it’s fine, I’m just a bit tired, it’s been unreal the past few days, I’m still not getting used to you being like this” Seara smiles uneasily and as she turns to leave the room she grips her stomach and a tear rolls down her cheek. The scene fades out as Scott closes his eyes and whispers the words “I will always love you Katherine” as the door closes behind his wife.
