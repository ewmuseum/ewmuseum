Decisions....decisions

2 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-2-03 Somewhere along the 101 Doing 105 mph in a 65 zone Good to be fast. Yeah! Somewhere along the highway 101, a speeding SLK Kompressor is racing at 105 mph without a care in the world. Cops, nah, they won’t pull them over because they are America’s favorite dynamic duo. Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick. All love them, all respect them, well except for Xavier Citizen who just a major a-hole! Yeah, that’s right buddy, I called you an A-HOLE!!! God this is fun! Anyways, back to the story. It appears that the other A-hole in Evolution, S., has decided to try and cause a rift between The Syndicate and a new perhaps budding alliance with Luther Wisdom and Omen Brotherhood. How? By pitting Big Daddy Dreamer against Luther Wisdom and having HBC call the match. Why? Because S. is afraid of this trio forming together and destroying his little plans once and for all. The Unlimited wouldn’t be pleased either to see and face the likes of those three, hell-bent on making things right once and for all. So speeding down the 101, BDD and HBC are conversing over this very topic. HBC: I hope you don’t expect any favors with me calling this match. BDD: C, what kind of question is that. I know your tough but fair. HBC: Good, just wanted you to make sure you knew that. BDD: Look, as I was going to tell the kid, but hey, why not now right. Might as well get some business done. Wednesday night I’m facing Luther Wisdom, former two-time tag champion with my partner, the lovely C here. Truth is, I have no desire to face you Luther. All this is set up by S. to try and cause some friction between the three of us. For he figures, if he can divide us, then his plans can go forward without anyone stopping him. This is why I have no desire to face you. But since I must, I will. Wednesday, you will go against one of the very best in the business. I know you were taught by one of the very best so it should be quite an interesting encounter. The wildcard in all of this is HBC. Now C, is unfairly caught in the middle of all of this so we can make this real easy for all involved. Wednesday night, I have already promised her that I will wrestle an honest, straight up wrestling match. I expect the same from you. This way, HBC’s job is real easy. Just count to three and call the winner. Clean, simple, concise. I won’t cheat, I won’t cut corners or take a shortcut unless you do it first. You violate the clean match-up, all bets are off. Again, I agreed with HBC on this too. If I do it, all bets are off, and anything goes. Tis only fair. Consider this a unique learning opportunity. You get a chance to show myself and HBC what you really can do inside that ring. Here is your opportunity to impress HBC, win or lose. Here is your opportunity to show your character, what drives you, and most of all, the possibility to team with one half of the greatest tag wrestlers on the planet, HBC. Now, personally speaking, I agree with you on some things and have expressed them to C. I do think you two have unfinished business in the tag division just as I have unfinished business with Xavier. But, here is your shot, your opportunity at greatness. Will you succeed or blow it, that is ultimately up to you. HBC will be watching this match and making her decision based on it. No pressure, just how it is. Lets have a unique match. Perhaps in the end, you could join us. But for now, as you say, we have respective, unfinished business. See you Wednesday Luther and bring your A game. You’ll need it.
