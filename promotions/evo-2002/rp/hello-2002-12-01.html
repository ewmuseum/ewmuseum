Hello..?

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.1.2002 .::Here we are sitting down. It's the new era and where we are is unknown to anyone. Who is that sitting down? Who's he talking to? WHY is he talking to that person? Let's take a gander at what their talking about. ??????: 'Heh, yeah I'll be glad to join. I've been looking for work for a long time. Ever since I left Illegal Fighting it's been hectic. But yeah, so whadda I gotta do to be in?' .::The man he's speaking to is soon to be configured as Daniel Somers, CEO of eVolution. Daniel Somers: What'd you say your name was again kid? ??????: DiAngelo Daniel Somers: Alright so, Angel. I've got a job for you kid. It has something to do with a rouge wrestler who's planning something. I'll give you more of the details soon, but it's getting hectic now. DiAngelo: That's cool with me. Daniel Somers: So what can you do kid? I hear good things. DiAngelo: You should know if you've heard things. Daniel Somers: Nice attitude kid. I've heard from.. we'll call it the Grapevine. I heard through the Grapevine that you once hit a guy with a florescent light so hard that his eyes crossed and he stopped moving for a good 4 minutes. DiAngelo: That was Dark Star Daniel Somers: Yeah, whatever. I also heard that you put this guy in some spectacular move. What's it called again? DiAngelo: My finisher? Daniel Somers: Yeah. DiAngelo: Seven Day Theory. Daniel Somers: Yeah that was it. I loved that tape that they showed me. That move is highlight reel material. I see good things for you here in eVolution. DiAngelo: Yeah, I get that alot. Daniel Somers: I bet you do, DiAngelo. DiAngelo: Call me Angel Daniel Somers: Angel, that's got a nice ring to it. How about we call you that hear in eVolution. Angel: Yeah, that does have a good ring to it. I used to be called Seraph. Daniel Somers: Angel sounds much better. Angel: I agree. But anyways, how is this gonna be? Daniel Somers: Hmm, I think you might do me some good. So-- Angel: Lemme get straight to the point. How much am I getting paid? Daniel Somers: Just like a bounty hunter. Angel: Yeah, you know how it is. But the price comes for the talent right. Daniel Somers: Yeah, I guess so. Hmm. I'll give you about - for the next show? Angel: Cool with me. But I want extra for doing in this guy? I've got alot of things to get. Daniel Somers: Hargh. I got ya. I'll give ya - much extra. Angel: Perfect. Daniel Somers: Well I've got to go plan for the JOS take out. Angel: The 'Who'? Daniel Somers: JOS, what I'm paying you extra for. I'll tell you more about it at Legends. Cool? Angel: Straight with me. Daniel Somers: Alright, later. Angel: One. Daniel Somers: ...one what? Angel: What rock did you crawl out from under? One is another way of saying bye. Daniel Somers: Oh.. well. O-one.. Angel: Heh heh.. One. [{ click }] [{ click }]
