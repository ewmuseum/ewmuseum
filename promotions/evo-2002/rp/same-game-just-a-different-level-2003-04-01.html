Same Game just a different level.

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~A camera opens outside of a gym in Arizona as Jason Relms is standing outside trying to prepare for his match Thursday. He is whiping some of the sweat of his face with a towel as he starts sippin some of the water.He looks at the camera with a smirk of a smile and laughs a little as he thinks about the Pay Per View night when the camera gets closer Jason Relms pauses before he begins to speak and contemplates what he is going to say.He then begins to speak.~~~ 'The FN Network' Jason Relms:Well folks what can I say Jason Relms has made it to the top. Like I said from the start The FIVE STAR RATING was going straight to the top and I be damned if I have disapointed myself. I am sure glad to be out of that dump Las Vegas. But man it seems to be getting worse as this week has landed me in the dump state itself Arizona. I mean the Cardinal's have never did a damn thing but bring a decent quaterback like the snake himself down with the shity franchize. This place need to be burnt. I mean to hot with way to many deserts. The world of tag team in the eVo now revolves around Team Network. And it's funny that those wanna be Playerz Inc 's peons still believe they can threaten Jason Relms after I have beaten them once, wait twice, and to come down to the ring during my damn celebration at the PPV and keep their mouth's just a yapping on and on. Well look Duzza and Status this place looks at the Tag Divison and they know damn well that the EVO mutha fuckin champs are not Wanna be playaz inc as Jason Relms is the FN NETWORK and it's time that all these people below begin to start taking some notes on how to get the job done. 'The FN Network' Jason Relms:That Includes you Nick Vancore. I see that you want to make Strictly X get a high standing. That you want them to become better in the tag divison. Well my friend I don't give a flying piece of shit what you did before. You could of one your first match beat the world champ then beat up two midgets outside the ring because they called you short, Hell I dont' know I dont care. But know you are going about it all the wrong way. You are facing one half of the tag champs. More importantly you are facing Jason Relms. My friend you can't beat me. You can't even stand in the same ring as me. My talent level is on one that you will never reach as my status is one I will keep. I am hated by every man who walks through the eVo gates mo matter if they are fan, wrestler or staff I am the hated man of eVo. But I take pride in it Nick. As the savage beating I will place on you will not be for all those kids to cheer for. It will not be for people to scream out at the top of their lungs announcing I am there hero. It's because I want to, no wait, it's simplys because I can. You are in my way Nick. You are nothing but pawn in this game. Their is a bigger picture that has the FN Network in it. And what Nick, hmm, Im looking and looking and wouldn't you know it, Nick isn't here. I wonder, OH that's it you are nothing but a peon. See man you step in that ring you will be nothing but another victim. ~His cell phone rings. It's High Impact as the two begin to talk and eVo taps into the phone and capture's the conversation~ High Impact'Man, where you at?' Jason Relms'I told you I was going to the gym. Got that match at Legends,And ofcourse I got FIVE STARS awaiting me.' High Impact'Oh yea, against that Nick Vancore dude.' Jason Relms'It's ashame man, these eVo cats still haven't gotten the notice in their lockers I assume that Team Network are the FN Tag Champs at eVo.' High Impact'Yea, that Nick needs to see that Strictly X will never be in the same game as Team Network. Jason Relms'Well let me go man and finish up this interview so I can get back to my workout.' High Impact'Ok, but wait, do you think things are diferent man. I mean life seems pretty cool knowing Team Network is the tag champs.' Jason Relms'Hell No,just reminds me that I am the best in the damn business. Proves what I say goes and what Nick or any of my opponents do or say mean..nothing.I need to open a class called the Five Star program. Try to teach people how to become the best in this game. Since to many people haven't learn the art of the trade yet. Well everyone seem's oblivious to it except the tag team champs.' High Impact'Man if I see Playaz Inc say one thing about us after the beatings we have already gave them I might laugh my ass off.' Jason Relms'Their nothing but chumps. Soon them along with Nick will see even a clearer picture of why I am THE FN NETWORK.' ~Relms hands up the cell phone as he finishes up with the camera on tv.~ 'The FN Network' Jason Relms:Well all you peons of EVO. It is my time to leave and get back ready for this no good pathetic TEAM NETWORK WANNA BE. That's right, Nick you knonw damn well that you would love to be the tag champs like me and High Impact. You know damn well you wish that you would be at the top of the game like ME. You want it, I know you do. But look at it again, you can't have it. It all comes back to me. You want the top that's where I am but you get a reality check boy. And that's that.Love to hate me peons, I dont care because Im the top, You understand, this game, it has me at the top, you got me Nick, you have me at Legends but you will never beat me, you don't deserve this ass beating but damn it you are going to get..Hate me if yall want too, but admited Nick I am nothing less than a FIVE STAR RATING. ~~~The camera fades to black as Jason Relms goes back to the gym and works out some more.~
