Times have Changed (Part III)

31 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-31 Prelude: After a chaotic year, things seemed to be just settling down for Shane, but all memories leave a scar that stay with you for eternity. Memories of past problems, feuds and issues eat away at your inner self and take away the little bit of dignity you need in everyday life. Without that dignity it makes everyday life a constant struggle and goals seem unachievable. They say anything in life is possible and maybe it is, though we cannot deny that sometimes we need a set of ears to absorb our issues and fill us with a bit of self-esteem. Today’s epic involves Shane’s third trip to his local therapist where he aims to release some of the verbal aggression building up inside him. It’s always good to talk especially when you have enough frustration inside to drive you insane. Shane has spent weeks upon weeks resolving his issues resulting from his childhood, with a local therapist, yet Shane has still to overcome the biggest challenge of all, his past. In today’s epic we encounter a scar that has polluted Shane’s mind for many years, but what will be the result if he is to overcome it? Will he still be the same Eternal Punk that gained the respect of people around the world or is this problem just too difficult to be resolved? All questions will be ansared, all critics will be silenced and the rest of the world better hold on tight for this is the return of Wethermania. In the middle of the town centre in Leeds, England stands a large derelict building that has been bordered up for many months. The smell of fuel still lingers in the moist, damp air, from when it used to be a working factory. Yet the place has gained some life as a huge wooden, hand made half pipe, stands in the middle of the room. There are gatherings of people standing around listening to the sounds blasting from a boom box, currently playing Goldfinger’s rendition of 99 red balloons and adding that extra something to the atmosphere of the building. The music is so deafening that you can barely hear the banging sound of a skateboard as it reaches the sides, followed by the rolling of it’s wheels. Junkies sit in the corners of the room, too stoned to remember their names or even where they are, keeping company with girls who have taken the same road and are becoming very ill, due to the hellacious death pills. No one on the half pipe seems to bother as they are too immersed in the rush of adrenaline from skating up and down the unsteady ground of the half pipe. Shane stands at the top looking down at his left foot placed on the black skateboard, with the words ‘Eternal Punk’ printed on it in blood red letters. The words seem to dance in front of his eyes as he stares down at the path he is about to take. With one short breath he lets his right foot push away at the ground and begins to skate the half pipe. Shane has skated since the age of three, yet something doesn’t quite feel right. He feels nauseous and his head begins to spin. He tries to shrug off the sickish feeling but he can’t, as he reaches the far side of the ramp and spins his board around in a 360 anti-clockwise fashion, the world seems to slow down. Shane’s head becomes very weary and all of a sudden he falls forward, as if he was taking a face dive down a death drop slide. Everything becomes slow motion to him; all the sounds of the room become unrecognisable and the volume of his heartbeat is echoing in his ear like a pounding drum. Shane closes his eyes and waits to feel the hard cold floor hitting him, but that time doesn’t arrive. He opens his eyes only to realise that he’s stopped falling, but he is now running and in unfamiliar territory. He feels very unsure as to what he is running from, his mind is scrambled and nothing seems to make any sense at all. Shane continues to run and as he approaches a slim figure in the distance, he reduces his pace and begins to walk towards the faint figure. As he gets closer the figure becomes clearer and he sees a middle aged man with blue eyes and dark brown hair, slickly combed back, wearing an expensive looking suit. “Dad?” Shane calls out in puzzled tone. “Shane, my boy” The voice replies. “What..er…where am I? What’s happening?” he asks his father, still trying to make sense of it all. “There’s nothing to worry about, I just want to know how you are?” his father quizzes him. “I’ve been better, I thought you were dead.” “You can’t kill a Wethers. You of all people, should know that.” His father says in a pleased tone. Shane grinned though he couldn’t help feeling confused. “Shane… I thought you were going to follow the business, y’know? Continue what I created.” The father exclaims. “Continue your business?” Shane replies with disbelief, “If it wasn’t for the damn mafia business then maybe I’d still have all my family!” “It’s always about you, isn’t it Shane?” his father responded sharply. “Didn’t I leave you with all the money you desired? Judging by your lifestyle I wouldn’t think you have any reason to complain. Obviously I’m wrong.” His father protests. Shane looks away in frustration. “I’m offering you the chance to take my business and run it your way. You’ll have more money than you could possibly imagine! Come on Shane, you’re the man of the family now, surely you ain’t gonna refuse my last request. The contract of ownership is in the top drawer of the bookcase at the house. Take it and make me even more proud,” his father pleads with him. Shane shakes his head and begins to back away, step by step, until suddenly he trips and finds he’s falling to the ground once again, almost like de ja vous. As he falls forward he looks up to see a huge black shadow staring down at him. Shane lets out a yell .of frustration and anger “What the hell is happ..” but he is silenced by a sickening feeling of his throat tightening, causing him to choke, but strangely he continued to fall. He feels all the air leaving his lungs and the blood rushing to his brain; his eyes start to darken and he feels a clotting sensation in his head as he begins to drop at a faster rate. No words leave his mouth, he feels too weak and frail to even open his mouth. As he begins to loose consciousness he manages to open one eye to see the floor coming towards him. He closes his eyes once more in anticipation of the hard-hitting feeling he is about to encounter. “No!!!!” Shane shouts at the top of his lungs. Shane sits up on the black leather seat in the office of his therapist. Sweat runs down the side of his pale face as he begins to pant and tries to gain his bearings. The therapist is sitting a few yards away and looks over to him with an expression of concern upon his face. “Shane? Are you ok?” he asks worriedly, as he stands up and quickly fills a small plastic cup with some ice-cold water from the glass jug. He passes the cup to Shane who drinks it so quickly imitating a man that has been stranded in a desert for numerous days. “What did you see?” asks therapist, sitting back down on his chair. “I was at the half pipe in Leeds, the same place me and my brother used to visit when we were younger.” Shane replies, still trying to come to terms with the feelings he’s just experienced. “We’d spend hours skating and pitying the junkies who were wasting away in the corner like rotting corpses with no hope of surviving in the real world. Then, all of a sudden I fell off my board and somehow found myself running down a long road, which led me to my father. He told me that I should continue in the family business” Shane stops and stares into thin air as the therapist scribbles notes down in his notebook. “Did this road seem at all familiar to you?” asks the therapist, very much interested and unusually pleased. Shane shrugs his shoulders, “I didn’t recognise it, I didn’t really stop to look, and I felt as if I was being chased or something “he says, letting out a sigh,” I don’t know… I don’t. The one thing I do know is, it was something I’ve never experienced before”. Shane looks across the room at the shrink to see him twiddling his thumbs with a grin upon his face. “What’s so damn funny?” says Shane in temperamental manner. The Therapist looks across at Shane and chuckles to himself, “Don’t you see it Shane? You’ve over come your biggest fear of all… your past.” He replies. A look of confusion and frustration comes upon Shane’s face. “Huh? I’ve almost pissed myself because I’ve fallen arse over tit on a half pipe and then been chased by someone who could quite easily have been Timmy Mallet and now you’re telling me that means I’m better? No, the only thing you’ve done is turned me into a damn teletubbie!” the Therapist continues to laugh which causes Shane to become even more irate. “Shane, you are OK… your subconscious needed to deal with this memory which has been haunting you for many years. That’s why it all seemed so bizarre; you simply had to see what you feared so you could actually get over it. You took the test and you’ve passed” the Shrink informs him. Shane shakes his head and looks at the Shrink, not so convinced. “Are you crazy?” Shane exclaims impatiently, “I’ve got so many split personalities I go to group therapy on my own! How on earth can I possibly be cured?” The shrink walks across the room and places his right hand on Shane’s left shoulder. “Not everything will make sense, but gradually you’ll begin to feel better about yourself” Shane pushes the Shrinks hand away from his shoulder. “So you’re saying that I’m cured?” Shane asks, “Either that or you wanna hammer me and if that’s what you’re thinking you better…”, Shane is interrupted by the Shrink, “I am saying you are better, Shane. You’ll never be cured but you now know how to deal with your problem” the Shrink declares. Shane stretches, raising his arms above his head. “You better be right, I’m hardly gonna become the front man of the worlds greatest rock band and a superior wrestler if I turn into Mary frickin Poppins!” he says in slightly calmer tone. Shane picks up his coat draped over a chair and slips it over his broad shoulders; he proceeds to walk towards the door and turns back to look at the shrink. “Thanks” he says in a friendly voice “Don’t mention it, if there ever is anything I can do to help you, all you need to do is ask” the shrink replies with a smile. Shane acknowledges the answer with a nod as he turns to the door and closes it behind him. As he walks down a narrow corridor with paisley patterned walls and a red and green checked carpet, he approaches the reception where a chauffeur is waiting patiently for him. The chauffeur is an old guy dressed smartly in the usual black attire with a chauffeur’s hat. “Mr Wethers?” he asks politely, “Are you ready to go? You’ve got a busy day tomorrow in the recording studios; the future release of your second album has already created a lot of media interest.” Shane nods his head as he walks towards the door of the building and the chauffeur follows him, they approach a long white limousine and the chauffeur opens the passenger door. Shane steps inside. “Thanks Tarquin, now can you stop slacking off like a half-wit fool and drive the damn car?” demands Shane in a calm tone of voice, “Right away Mr Wethers” replies the chauffeur with a friendly smile upon his face. During the journey back, all Shane could think of was what he had encountered during his therapy session and how the shrink had told him that he was over the problem. How was he over it? He still felt as if he are drowning in the issues of his childhood, nothing seemed to make sense. Maybe he was scarred with this problem and it would not ever leave him, there are so many ‘ifs’ and ‘maybes’ but not enough answers. The only thing Shane knew was that his comeback to wrestling was going to rock the very foundations of the world as we know it, he had the burning desire from his childhood of becoming a legend like the ones he once watched on television, the ones he used to sit and admire. Even in his later stages of his teenage years he was a great fan of the infamous Kid Wonder. Shane thought of his past career in wrestling and how he was a legendary athlete, but times change, Legends become Myths and Myths are forgotten. But now he knew deep down inside it was time for the change to take place, the New Era is never yesterday’s news. The car suddenly stops as it arrives in the large parking area of Shane’s grand mansion, the car door opens and the Chauffer is stood holding it open as Shane steps out. The house is large and made of the finest white stone with large double glazed windows that are tinted so you can’t see in. Shane walks over to the set of double doors at the entrance of the house; he walks inside and sighs heavily. The inside of the house is as elegant as the outside, the large walls full of pictures, sky blue wall paper and the marble staircase at the end of the hall. There are three doors at either side of the house that led into a large lounge area with an expensive multi-speaker surround sound hi-fi. Shane walks to the end of the hall and stood at the bottom of the staircase. “Hello? Where is everybody” Shane ponders, he stands awaiting a lucid response but doesn’t receive an answer. “Damnit, where is that moronic butler when you need him?” he mutters to himself in a frustrated voice, “Should have known better than to hire a muppet to do a man’s job”. Suddenly a voice was heard, “Looking for me sir?” a tall dark shadow appears in middle of the hallway. Shane frowns, “Ah, slacking again are we Charles? I’m not gonna put up with your lack of punctuality for much longer” Shane protests. “Sorry sir, its hard for me.. I mean I’ve never been a butler before, after all, I’m a prince.” He replies as he stepped forward into the light, revealing that he is Prince Charles. “By the way, my mother called, she would like to meet with you some time. She feels you should be knighted for you tremendous efforts and talent in the wrestling ring.” Charles suggests in a excited manner. Shane responds with laughter, “Haha, yeah as if I’d let that feeble old cow swing a sword anywhere near my head.” Shane says with zest. “B-but she’s very careful and you will then be known as Sir Shane Wethers” The noble prince replies, “Hahaha! Now that would be funny, your mother can’t even walk never mind swing a sword. She is knocking on quite a bit, she’s an old bat with nipples between her ankles, as if I’d wanna see something as repulsive as that” Shane says comically. The Prince looks down at the floor with a tear in his eye as Shane turns and starts to walk up the stairs, dropping his black fleece on the floor on his way up. “Hang that for me would ya Charlie” he asks. Shane follows the stairs upwards and reaches the top floor; he walks across the smooth white marble floor and along to a door at the far right hand corner. He opens the white wooden door and enters. The room is large with a polished wooden oak floor, white wallpaper and large lights. It looks notoriously more new-age than the rest of the house, with a long pool table, a huge wide screen television, two black sofas and a bar area that stretch around the left corner of the room. The bar is full of different beers, spirits and liquors of all kinds. Shane walks over to it and takes a small clear glass from underneath the table; he fills it up with a measure of Scotch whisky and adds two small ice cubes. He then walks towards the bookshelves and takes a small silver key from a little cup, he puts the key in the drawer below the shelf and opens it. There laying in front of him is a folded piece of A4 paper. He takes it and walks over to the coffee table, opening the paper up fully in front of him. The paper is full of writing about the business ownership and it states that it will be handed down in the family, therefore making Shane owner. He places it down on the table at sits down, glaring at it whilst fondling the whisky glass. Charles enters the room with a small tray full of nibbles and potato snacks. “Sir? I brought you these,” informs Charles, “thought they may put you on until lunch.” Shane looks up and sighs. “Wow, corn snacks, and there I was thinking our chefs are useless,” Shane replies sarcastically. All of a sudden ‘mission impossible’ blurts out from Shane’s pocket in a polyphonic tone. Shane takes out his Sharp GX10 Cellular phone and opens the flap, placing it against his ear. “Hello?” he pauses, “Jas, how are you?” he grins, “Shopping? Again? Now that doesn’t sound like you.” He says sarcastically. He pauses again before replying. “No problem, I’ll send a car to pick you up…. don’t worry, I’ll send the limo” he says quietly, then pauses, “Yes there will be Champagne in the car and yes it will be cold, just as you like it” he smirks, “See you soon”. Charles places the tray down on the coffee table. “Does Jas require transport?” he asks “Yeah, so you better stop lurking around like cling-on and get a car down to the high street mall” Shane demands, “Right away sir” Charles responds. “Things are gonna start changing around here…” Shane picks up a small sports magazine; he flicks through it and finds a page that says ‘Evolution presents X-Rated…This Sunday’. He then reaches over and picks up the unfolded piece of paper, he exchanges glances between the two. Gradually a Cheshire cat grin appears on his face “…Starting here, the Era is about to begin!”
