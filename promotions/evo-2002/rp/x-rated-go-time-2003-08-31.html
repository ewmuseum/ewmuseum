Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-31 The fans have lined up for hours to see their favorite Evolution superstar compete in action tonight. They have braved the elements, the cold weather, the rain, even the massive heat wave. There they are, all bundled up in their summer clothing paying their hard earned dollars to see the true greats of this sports, the miserable little leaches that they are. As the black jeep cherokee pulls up to the talent entrance way, everyone strains their neck to catch a glimpse of who is inside the vehicle. The jeep slows to a slow crawl and eventually parks outside of the underground parking garage door. The doors open and everyone goes absolutely crazy when Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick exit their vehicle and raise the Evolution Tag Team Championship up for the crowd to see. Together they both scan the crowd, seeing their legions of fans, all holding Syndicate signs and wearing their latest merchandise. BDD and HBC grabs their gear from the trunk, high-five a few fans and proceed inside the arena where its cooler. The staffer walks them down the long corridor into their locker room. BDD quickly closes the door and locks it behind him while tossing his bag into the corner. BDD: Always England. Man I hate this city! Why is it we always get stuck wrestling in England. They are uneducated simpletons whose only idea of a decent job is factorial work. Pathetic. HBC: I know, I like this city as much as you do but hey, we can take it out on Realms and Wisdom tonight. Hey what is this? HBC walks over to the table where some fruit has been arranged for them and underneath is an envelope with The Syndicate’s name on it, She pick its up and opens it, reading it out loud. HBC: Dear Syndicate, this letter is to inform you that from now on, your titles will be defended 24-7, in any rules you see fit…. HBC and BDD just stare at each other with a smirk of amusement and smile grows across HBC's face as she puts the letter into her bag and starts to pull out her gear. BDD: Boy, they really have no idea don’t they. HBC: No….but indeed they will. But first things first though, tonight we have Realms and Wisdom, and like so many other teams who have a problem with our greatness, they seem to have been offended by what we said to them. Well boo fucking hoo if the truth hurts guys. Hey D, should I wear the red top and the black shorts or the white top and red shorts tonight? BDD: Go with the red and black tonight, the evil heartbreaker mode. HBC: Hehehehe! Yeah, I think I will! HBC slowly begins to change pulling off her top and letting her breast bounce around, and whips off her windpants. For a second she looks at her nude body in the mirror and thinks, dam I look good! She puts on her wrestling outfit fo tonight as BDD is finishing taping up his wrist. They check each other over, satisfied, HBC grabs BDD package as they walk out over to the promo area. Several of the wrestlers see them coming and move out of their way as they adjust their titles over their shoulder, finally catching up to the interviewer who tells them they are ready, and go baby go! BDD: Tonight, X-Rated from England and your Evolution Tag Team Champions, The Heartbreaker and myself, BDD take on these upstarts trying to make a fast name for themselves by knocking us off. Well Realms and Wisdom, that simply isn't going to happen whether you like or comprehend that idea or not! But I will say it appears that we've struck alittle nerve within them. Perhaps it was the truth that got to them, that once someone finally had the gall to spit in their eye and tell it like it really is around here and about them. We beat you the last time, and here, tonight with millions watching at home, we shall whip your asses once again simply because we are the greatest thing walking on god’s green earth! HBC: Realms and Wisdom, every fed needs their comic relief and their jobbers, which I think you will do quite nicely. Guys when are you going to get it through your thick head that all your bravado and little rants earlier means about as much to me as wiping my ass with some two-ply paper. In other words, I don't give a shit. And why don't I, because I've been in the big-time taking on the legends of this sport for five years, headlining every major arena in the World. Sure Madison Square Garden is nice, but its nothing compared to wrestling in front of 80,000 screaming fans in Tokoyo, or watching the Arabs literaly shooting each other for tickets to see us. That we've been declared national treasures in twelve different countries. So while your competing in no-talent matches, which is really sad, we've been booked and kicking ass in every type of match known to mankind. And if you think I'm not above of even hurting myself or being alittle vain about my looks, I've been in matches you would have the stomach to entertain the idea of. But no matter, tonight, Realms and Wisdom, you get your shot. When the bell rings, your in our environment and facing the true icons of this sport, and when its all said and done, the smoke clears from the pyrotechnics, the lump you feel in yoyr throat, you know that one lodged just above your sternum will grow bigger and bigger as you realized you've bit off more than you can chew. Wisdom/Realms, its gametime and no amount of friends you think you have will help you. Tonight, you two asscracks are going to have to work and try to earn a win, but god knows everyone knows you can't do it!
