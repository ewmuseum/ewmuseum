Truly Human

18 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 18th We aren’t so un like Jason. The voice of Sovereign cracks the silence in your ears. You can not see anything right now, just a formless blob of black on your television set. But you know his voice. It curls your lips, shakes your spine. Like Jason Jameson’s reaction to even his real name did to him in Boston this week, his voice is like a shake of salt on an open wound. So many people can attibute that wound to Sovereign. The list of individuals who have had their lives ruined by Sovereign is in the double digits in Evolution Wrestling alone, not to mention his past exploits. But at this point in time Sovereign’s future is something that stands before us all. This week on Legends Sovereign will face Malcolm King, Scott Frost and Jason Jameson to see who is the Number One contender to the Universal Title, a title that Sovereign was the 1st to ever hold in Evolution and held it he did with an iron fist for over 3 months. When he lost that title he claimed he lost his identity. And now, sitting on the edge of pure Insanity is Sovereign, looking over the killing fields of Evolution ready to pick up where he left off over a month ago. He wants the Title to return to him, and if that means that this week me must destroy these 3 other men then it shall be done. Of course, it’s been well documented, the recent events between Jason and Sovereign, especially now that these two will most likely collide this coming week for the Number One Contendership. It’s true Jason. That might pain you to know, but we’re aren’t completely different. His voice again, a knife in your soul Pasts that we wish we could change. Relationships forgotten. Friendships ended for stupidity. Yes Jason Jameson, we might sit on opposite sides of the fence when it comes to quote unquote proper conduct in this world that we live in. But I know that you Jason Jameson aren’t anymore then the sum of your parts. Just like I am. We’re a combination of bones, blood, flesh, muscle and then the key, our heart. Some of us have black hearts, some have hearts of gold. And while a lot of people say that Jason Jameson has a heart of gold and Sovereign has a heart of pure ebony I tend to disagree. Of course, who am I to agree with anyone. I don’t have a valid opinion since I suffer from Insanity, but I’ve never once challenged a Doctor’s opinion on my condition. But Jason, you probably should get checked out yourself. I think you have a bit of a rage problem. Are you that bothered by me Jason? Or are you so afraid that you might have been next that you took me head on? I think that’s a valid question that should be answered. And while I know that you don’t “fear” me Jason Jameson because, you unlike most men that walk the backstage truly are not afraid of me. L2J said he was not afraid and that was a lie. Impact said that he was not afraid but he was a fool. Jamal Kage only wanted me to believe. I showed him what I believed. And now, you sit in your hotel room crying over letters from the past thinking, hoping that maybe you can silence the ghosts of Evolution’s past by declaring war on one man. One man, the one man that has always held his hand around the throat of everyone, that being Sovereign, that is me. You think this is your redemption of all your mistakes? You think that this is your destiny? Your fate!? You have no clue Jason Jameson. You have no idea what it is like to be a victim like all the others. You want this for yourself? Or are you just a masocist trying to punish yourself because one day you snapped. Laughter. The cold laughter of a man that would only laugh at the sickest things is heard as the camera pans out almost. The shot was an extreme close up, so close that it was only darkness. But as the shot quickly pans out you see Sovereign sitting in the shadows of an unlabled room. Sitting there, in the corner, no emotions other then the blank, sadistic chuckling of a mad man You don’t even know what snapped really means Jason. Beating up a man for rape isn’t snapping. Hell, most normal men in your position that day that your woman was violated would have done exactly what you did that day. What was wrong? Did you feel so guilty about it that you tried to treat it as some sort of deep emotional problem? Did you need the excuse? The only thing that happened that day was your true self came out of a shell. You felt that animal power that all men in this world have. Every single man on this planet is a killer, regardless of if they admit it or not. It’s our natural instinct. It’s survival, we must survive. The only way that self sacrifice comes into play is through the minds will to let others survive. Loved ones, innocents. Men like us don’t care. I know that you are like me Jason. You chanced that man down and you beat him, like the dog that he was. You liked it? Of course you liked it. Because you were released. So what is the problem? Let him come out Jason. Become yourself. Prove that you are more then the sum of your parts. Show us what Snapping is, do what you must, be not just a man. But be HUMAN for a change. So few people know what it is to be truly Human. I do Jason, because I’m more human than anyone else in this world and no one would want to believe me. They think being a human is to kiss your wife good night, read to your kids, give to the blind man on the street, walk to work, drink decafinated coffee. Don’t smoke, don’t drink, don’t curse, don’t do drugs. Those might all be good things, and I won’t enter a debate on them. But to restrict yourself to some weak society and how it sees itself isn’t human. Feeling remorse about anything is not human. To be truly Human... is to be what your inner self tells you to be, without consequence. Without hesitation. Can you be Human Jason Jameson? Or are you as weak as all the others before you. I think that you are weak. I think you have what it takes to be truly Human. Stop worrying about your woman Stop crying over phone calls. Come to my world Jason Jameson and see what being truly Human is all about. The basic instinct of our kind. And you won’t care about those things anymore. Be what you can be. Hold it above the heads of the fallen. Dominance is king Jason Jameson, and as I can see in your eyes that you want to be truly human you must realize that this week would be your 1st chance. Can you over come 3 others to be the Number One Contender? Sovereign looks up and smiles, you can see it on his face, a smile from ear to ear. But why Sovereign? Why would you, the Human Monster want such a prize? Why not is my answer. Why not show the world. Why not continue what you really enjoy. The Humanity of Sovereign might seem somewhat twisted, that would be an understatement I suppose. But the Title is everything. It is Dominance. And while on Paper this day Shocka is the Dominant one, he still hasn’t had to defend against me. The man that set the all time bench mark for the Universal Title. But after this week Jason Jameson will have receive a valuable lesson in life. Why he isn’t good enough yet. Because he won’t become the Number One contender this week. Not by a long shot. That is my goal. And then when I do face Shocka for the Universal Title. I will de-throne him in Sovereign style. And Frost nor King can do anything about it. Sovereign lowers his head See you all, in Salt Lake Regards, Sovereign. Fade out
