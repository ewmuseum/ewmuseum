Intermission pt.3

28 May 2003

Date: 2003-05-28 The silver blue smoke curls out from his lips casting a thick billowing contrast to the darkness that surrounds him. The smoke moves in slow motion, thick tendrils of the acrid stuff pour out of his nose at the same time. The camera angle freezes for a second and we get a full picture of Ali Khadafi’s face. His bald head gleams from some light source. Dark shadows fill the hollows of his eyes and the sunkeness of his cheeks. The reddened orbs that glitter with a kid of animalistic lust are his eyes; the windows to his soul. The smoke halo’s his face, hazing our view momentarily as the camera snaps back into real time. It continues to spiral up into the darkness. The camera pulls back a few steps and we are allowed a brief glimpse of what he’s wearing. We see a deep beige denim jacket. The collar and inside lining are fur, allowing us the insight that where ever the fuck Khadafi is, it’s cold. Underneath the jacket he’s donning a gray, skin-tight thermal sweater. It displays his body to a T. We can see the muscles of his abdomen ripple upward as he brings the hand-rolled Cowboy Killer to his mouth. Decorating his right wrist, the camera easily picking up the silver glint amongst the fur, is a thick Rolex El Presidente watch. Ali drags deeply from the hand-rolled cigarette. The camera pauses for a moment, entranced by the glowing embers that dance from the tip of his smoke into the blackness of the night. Finally, we are allowed to see that he is wearing a pair of ash gray dress pants, the thick black belt matching perfectly with his Jackson half boots. Khadafi exhales…. Emerging from the shadows he takes Alex S. by surprise. He’s bigger than Alex had imagined Deb’s boyfriend to be, because she had always dated towards the smaller side. Bigger than her, but yet no quite big. Khadafi looked huge in the thick shadows that surrounded the apartment complexes parking lot. His fists are balled up into clubs and Alex can tell from the man’s demeanor that he was pushing the envelope farther than he really should have. S. turned towards Ali and let his most charming smile creep across his clean shaven face. His best smile splattered across the hard knuckles of Ali Khadafi’s face. His lower lip mashed into his teeth, splitting jaggedly and sending a gush of warm blood spilling down his face. Alex could taste the hot coppery liquid fill his mouth seconds before he stumbled and nearly tripped over his own feet. Seconds before a long, looping haymaker closed his eye for the better part of three days. The blow sent the honorable Alex S. to the ground, howling and clutching his eye. He heard the smoky bass of voice of Deborah M.’s boyfriend cut through the chill of the night. ”Fucking prick wouldn’t leave!” ”You didn’t have to do it like that Ali.” ”Fuck that.” Alex struggled to get up and felt blood spill onto his arm. He could feel it leaking from his split lip like a broken faucet, but was torn between clutching his blinded eye, which had swollen up already, and his wounded mouth. S. finally managed to get to his feet… ”What the bloody fuck is your problem man!” Alex said letting blood spit with each syllable. He couldn’t see either Deb or her boyfriend because he was still wobbling on his feet, his back turned to the pair. ”Son of a BITCH!” ”Ali NO!” Alex heard the impact of the punch before he actually felt it. It didn’t matter anyways because the ground was rising up to meet him…
