Diary of a champ..wait CHAMPS..The Name says it all..

20 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-20 Your Fighting Champ The scene opens inside a local studio in come big city. Inside a booth is no other then cell superstar and newly crowned Tag Champ QBX.He stands in front of a mic like always when he is in the studio. Though this time something is different. He has on his usual attire of black bagy jeans with a QBX shirt of some kind. But this time he has a shinny belt drapped over his left shoulder.He still has got that certain trash talk style face as he just begins to do his usual flow into the mic..by saying.. 'What is it.. quick take a picture.. sorry shaft.. I can't take one with ya.. that be like pulling teeth.. that are false.. like saying you love the g man.. but dont like the cross.. as if I was miss cleo.. sent to see your future.. WAIT,dont look good.. but you use to it.. Legends 50 ya got a small taste.. but it dont matter what time nor place.. if X see's ya.. you getting smacked in the face..' A short pause as he smiles..he continues by saying things about Legends 50.. 'Huh?Who?What?Where.. Thug Roc was there?.. damn must not of seen him.. had to be a glare.. I mean that cat is like from brooklyn.. hell he might even be a bralwler.. shit far as I know.. he could be related to Jerry King Lawyer.. but whatever if may be.. some one needs to say it to his face.. You aint good!..you need to find your place.. go back to singles.. where you belong in your thong.. where you call your hood.. to report to your mom that you are home.. I mean for real..you just aint like me dog.. I guess I got to take out legend number two.. King is already gone.. now your time is up soon too.. I mean I said it from day one.. But i will say it again.. my first words ever..where something u need to remember..' Pauses as he quotes his first line ever.. 'Thug Roc aint got shit on me..' The scene fades with him standing in front of the mic holding that tag belts across his arm... The Other Half The scene opens back inside a familiar room. One that eVo has seen once before. In the middle of the room is Tok'n. The man who is Jason Relms. The room after the scene is focused better can be seen very clear now and remembered very well. This is the same room we last heard from of 'The Show'. The show being the nickname Jason Relms was given to by evolution.Behind him stands those awards that he refused to look at.He still has his back turned to it like he did the last time we left him in the room. But this time he has that Tag Title strap over his right side. The thing that he has but first since the day he walked back into his wrestling home,eVo. He stands with that belt on his shoulder very proudly..He begins to think about all the questions that have been raised to him since him and QBX have became the champions. Some were 'What do you plan on doing since you are both from different divison?' Or there was the occasional 'Do you know who your next oponnent is..Hell there was even the one or two that asked 'Well your weed t-shirt being priced higher next week?' But as he stands with the past behind him and the future ahead..he only mummbles.. 'What the fuck do you think?' He then readjust the belt on his shoulder as he begins to think about everyone that doubted him. As he began to think about what the people of eVo thought about Tok'n being a tag champion. What they thought about the others who they only wished were..he then says in a sweet but yet angered voice.. 'You all wanted me left for dead. This place wanted to see me marked as whatever eVo saw fit for me to be marked. Now I do realise that The Industry is not in this fight a lone. I do take notice that there are some fans that love us..But I also notice that there are some that done like us. Sure they want to see Scott Frost be Tag Champ. Sure they want to see the married couple have the gold. Or maybe even want to see The Syndicate retain what they have had for so long. But listen damn it,that aint happenin. QBX and I formed for a reason. We both wanted our shot at fame. We both wanted to grab the spotlight that we rightfully deserved. So now what?Because we are from different divisons they want to take the belts..Hardly the truth,think people. Think real hard about something. When is the last time the Tag Divison has been worth a damn?When was the last time the people ever knew it was even in eVo. Was it with Scott Frost going after it..no. Was it with Syndicate being champs..No..Was it with bodysnatchers..NO..I will tell you when it sky rocketed..Legends 50..The night that The Industry meant exactly what it stands for..' Another pause as he mumbles once again.. 'What the fuck do you think?' He then continues on to speak about things going on with the Tag Divison and about him and QBX getting the tag belts... 'Really though,Thug Roc and Shaft believe they can take the tag strap away from me and X. What are these people on..druge?!?!I mean really they must of been token one up before they spoke infront of the camera. I mean this is pathetic. We have a wrestler who is past his prime trying to talk bad about industry. Then we have a man that will never reach to any amount of good to say he could be in a state of prime. I mean its sad people. They are telling me this is the best the tag divison had to offer the champs. Hell, I dont think they could last five minutes with me and X in a match. Cell or Ring, we do it all. We aint some flash in the pants who thinks he can do it all. Who only talks shit and doesn't back it up. NO, we do it..and talk it..So getting on your damn horse you always talk about and ride it out of my damn sight..' He chuckles as he then begins to finish up the interview..as he looks down at this belt and says.. 'Evolution needs to buckle up. Because the time of the PG-13 family show could of been broadcasted on disney is over. This aint True-TV or Cock-Tv or whatever the hell thug boy was talking about. This is the Industry..We dont play games..we destory them. We dont wrestle people..we just kick their asses. These tag belts are staying on our shoulders from here to a long dman time to come..So the question is raised once more..' 'What the fuck do you think?'He the answers the question that has popped up numerous times.. 'I dont fucking care..' The scene fades to dark as he is still looking down on his right shoulder at the Tag belt not even paying any attention onto any of his old accomplishments. It is clear to say that the industry plans on staying right were they are at..
