Legends XIV

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-3-02 New York City, New York Grand Marriott Penthouse Evening The First Lady of Evolution, The HeartBreak Chick should be a really good mood. I mean she is beautiful, wealthy, powerful, and yet, a certain individual is becoming a major thorn in her side. Big Daddy Dreamer! I mean, the sheer audacity of him dressing up as Frankenstein last week and nailing me upside the head with the bolt cutters that I used to take him out. Frankenstein!?! He should have dressed as the mummy instead. How many yards of dressing did it take to patch you up? Fortunately, nothing serious happened when you hit me D. Just a bump, that I can hide with my reddish-golden locks. But don’t worry D, I will finish you off, all in due time. HBC rises up from the couch, pulling down on her black mini-skirt as she looks over the cityscape of New York with a smirk on her face. The richest woman in New York she is as of right now, but that doesn’t matter much since she is the richest woman in the world. Suddenly a buzz on the door disturbs her as two females walk into the room wearing short skimpy top and a see-through mini-skirt. She turns and smiles. ‘Ah……the infamous Marriott sisters…. Dallas and Frenchie.’ Dallas: ‘Oh my god! Its HBC! The door people didn’t tell us you were staying here today!’ Frenchie: ‘They are so totally fired! How are you love!?!’ ‘Except for a bump, I’m fine. By the way, you two are taking me out tonight. The rest of my party will be arriving later.’ Dallas: ‘That’s so like cool. Well Frenchie, do you think Stella’s will be the place tonight, or what about P.Diddy’s place?’ Frenchie: ‘Yeah, like P.Diddy club is so raging’ HBC walks over and places her index finger on both of their mouths to shut them up for a second. The Marriott sisters do like to blab on forever in a very clueless sense. ‘Girl’s……Stella is fine. I don’t think I’m allowed into P.Diddy’s club especially since what happened the last time I saw him.’ Frenchie: ‘Did he like try to come on to you?’ Dallas: ‘Guys are so creepy that way, always trying to touch, and grab. Remember that one time tha—‘ ‘Enough! No, the last time I saw Puffy was a year ago on TRL, he got in the way and I superkicked him on national television and stomped him a few times. The audience was thrilled and their ratings have never been higher. Even Carson laughed, but I nailed him too for being a smuck!’ Together: ‘NO WAY!!!’ ‘Way! Now will you excuse me for a few minutes, I have some business to attend too. Comeback around ten-ish ok.’ Frenchie: ‘Ok, its going to be so hella fun!’ Dallas: ‘Quite!’ HBC watches them leave and shakes her head needing an aspirin after dealing with those two. I should send them to Dreamer to drive him nuts! Those two could make anyone go mad! Well lets see what is going on the world today. After several minutes of scanning through she finally reads her email from Somers saying she has a match this week against someone calling themselves Jinosyde. The immediate response is who? Damn scrubs trying to take me on. Won’t they ever learn. A heavy sigh as another knock on the door disturbs her thinking to herself this is the last time she’ll be at the Marriott in NY. At least the Hilton people are respectful and have taste. She opens the door to find the Unlimited with Xander, Jinx and Asis walking through checking out the place, impressed by what they see. ‘Come on in people. Its spacious but not to great, but the view is exquisite. As you all know we have matches this week, some better than others. I get stuck with some non-talent retard Jinosyde while the rest of you at least get respectable matches. By the way, who the hell was keeping an eye on Dreamer last week?’ They all look around the room not wanting to answer the question until Jinx finally speaks for all of them. Jinx: ‘C, baby, none of us honestly thought he would be their, especially after the number you and I did to him. I mean who would have thought that shot you gave him from the chairs and the bolt cutters, not to mention your superkick and me tap dancing on his broken eye socket that he’d be in any shape to show up let alone attack you like he did. We are really sorry about that. It won’t happen again…..right guys?’ Asis: “Yup!” Xander: “Of course, we got your back C” HBC: “You had better. If it wasn’t for me, none of you would have the surplus cash to live the life you are now. If D shows up, I want him destroyed. Am I crystal clear on this! No mistakes! No fuck-ups! No excuses! Because if he does…..all the girls I bring…..gone! No more free ponani for any of you from my friends and the lines of credit will cease immediately. Clear!” Unlimited: “YES MA’AM!” HBC: “Good….get the hell out and get ready, were going out with the Marriott girls tonight! And Asis, that does mean more than just a t-shirt and jeans.” Jinx: “Ha ha! Asis, you got to get all purty” Asis throws a punch at Jinx but HBC stops it with an evil look on her face as both stop dead in their tracks and head out the door. HBC watches the door close and shakes her head again, tossing up her arms in frustration. She walks back over to the window and stares across the city seeing where the WTC used to be before speaking. ‘So this week, I get another scrub trying to make a name for himself at my expense for the Rising Star tournament. Why do I always get the scrubs. Well Jinosyde, as many others have learned when dealing with HBC, be careful what you ask for because you may be unable to handle it. Now, you do fully realize what you’ve gotten yourself into don’t you. I hope you’ve been watching the past few legends and Faith, and that you will see first hand, I am not some sweet girl who lays over for anyone. I’ve beaten the best the in the business and I have a mean streak bigger than my ego. No Jinosyde, throughout your career, you can rest assured, you have never faced anyone quite like me. I’ve been the top cruiserweight in this business for several years. I am one of the top wrestlers of all-time and now, you want to face me, the HBC. You must have quite a deathwish don’t you. The fact of it is this, I don’t need the Unlimited to do anything for me, I just like watching them work. I don’t need chairs or bolt cutters, I use them for my own personal enjoyment, and most of all, and this part is true. I enjoy hurting wrestlers. I enjoy, no, get-off, on busting them open, hearing them scream like they were taking a twelve inch dildo up the ass without any lubrication. That my dear Jinosyde is fun. Watching you twist and contort in pain before sealing your fate with a simple kiss. The Kiss of Death. You still really want to face me? Do you? I guess in Nashville we shall find out, but know this, here is your only chance to back out, you step through the curtain in Nashville…..its your funeral. Lets see if you make the right choice.” HBC suddenly has an evil smile growing across her face…….
