Again Trying To Understand...

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/24/02 When Michaels made his return to eVolution, he surprised and confused everyone by helping Troy Pierce garner a win over Big Daddy Dreamer. As soon as Michaels showed up during the match everyone quickly assumed he was there to screw Pierce and King out of the match. But after nailing BDD with a steel chair, the speculations started flying about why he helped Pierce gain the win. Through those weeks of making his presence known in Pierce’s matches, Michaels has not only attacked Impact and BDD once again, he attack the World Champion himself, Sovereign. No one knows why, but if you’ve looked close enough at him you would see that the answers you’re looking for, are there. It’s just the matter of when, or if Michaels is willing to let someone in on his intentions. Michaels has surely made a few new enemies during his return to eVolution, so whether he likes it or not, he’ll probably have a few people gunning for him when he shows his face again. Big Daddy Dreamer has already gotten his hands on him once after Michaels attacked him backstage, so there’s no telling if Michaels will rethink what he’s doing or not. With the history between Pierce and Michaels, one has to wonder what’s going on in Tommy Michaels’ head. Was Michaels just helping Pierce while waiting for the right opportunity to screw Pierce in a big way? Some would say no because if he was waiting for that chance, he had it when Pierce faced Sovereign for the World Championship. And yet Michaels made his presence felt in that match trying to help Pierce win the title. Has Michaels just gone completely out of his mind? Some would say yes, because he is helping the one man he has truly hated for the longest time. And to finally get his hands on him, he instead tries to help him win matches. But then those who know him well enough, and those that have been able to get somewhat close to him know that his hatred for Pierce cannot be easily put aside and that something had to happen to make him act this way. All the while, Michaels has yet to say one word. The always arrogant, cocky, loudmouth asshole we all know and love hasn’t been the same. Now with Pierce out of the picture with and injury at the hands of Matt Boone and Cori Simmons, will Michaels final reveal what his intentions are? Or will he continue to irritate those who have questions they would like answered?
