Broken

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th October 2002 “Never tell me I care for you, never say I don’t care, I just don’t want to know you, you have no significance to me, you are nothing, just someone, if only you could know why I left, why I chose to disown you, but you would never understand, you wouldn’t believe me, you had it differently, both of you, trusted, loved, wanted, accepted, cared for, but I wasn’t, I was disowned long before, before I chose to accept my new life, before I chose to disown you” Seara’s words are heard before she is even seen, but it is clear she is somewhere talking to her brother. The scene fades in to show Seara next to his hospital bed in New Orleans. Jake is attached to a life support machine, a tube has been pushed into his mouth and down his throat to help him breath, and another sits inside his nose, his eyes are closed and beside him a respirator quietly beeps and a monitor shows his heart beating slow, steady. He is lifeless, motionless; the only noise in the intensive care ward is the sound of Seara’s breathing and the slow quite beeping of machinery. His neck has been strapped up and positioned with a plastic support to stop him moving it, it is attached to a backboard to stop him moving his entire spine. The damage that Asis administered to him was worse than the male wrestler had imagined when he had talked down the camera directly at Seara about his actions. The female is sitting leaning over the bed, close to the face of her brother, she whispers “death is the only way, the one life that is true, you will know how I have felt my entire life” into the ear of her brother, then leans back into her chair. Raising her arms she stretches and yawns, since the end of Legends XII she has sat here, next to her unconscious brother, waiting for one of two things, him to wake up and blame everything that happened to him in her, or for the rest of her family to come to his side. “They have never treated me like one of them, never told me they loved me, I have been told that it was a mistake getting me out of jail, that I was the biggest bain in my fathers life, and she may have been right, but to be told that your own step parent, the person replacing your mom wishes you would go away, die in a corner or commit suicide, that has serious effects. And she was right, things for them would be better if I wasn’t around.” Seara leans back over to her brother; her eyes are red suggesting a lack of sleep or that she has been crying. But it is not known which of the two reasons are the answer for her eyes being that colour. “It is not that I don’t care, I mean I don’t actually care what they do, but I’m not that cold and black hearted, after all he is still my brother, and I suppose somewhere deep inside me I love him and his wife and his nephew, and I do for their sake wish Asis had killed him. He will never walk again, and he has a 3 year old kid to take care of, that kid will feel similar to me when I watched my mother die, but he will know that if only his aunt hadn’t pissed off a huge monster, then his daddy may still be able to walk and do normal stuff, but daddy will be kept in a bed now for the rest of his life, and Asis will find it funny to know that he is the monster who has done this to a man who defenceless and unsuspecting, a man who had a great career ahead of him in sports journalism, but now, he has none of that, Asis has destroyed his chances, and although it is comical, it is I find a bit of a waste of a talented young life, next time Asis face someone who has a chance.' Turning to the camera Seara stares intensely at the lens and grins slightly, almost amused by her own thoughts. “I’m sure Asis that your mom would have looked similar to this before she died, but you wouldn’t know, you haven’t seen her in what was it 8 years, oh no hard for you Asis, poor little baby misses his mommy, well we all do, my fucking mom was killed while I was there, at least you never had to witness stuff like this. It will be your turn one day Asis, you will have to see your drunk of a father in this state and you will know true pain, you will know what it is to see someone you thought you didn’t care about hurt, injured, defenceless. I am not particularly bothered anymore by what you did, I have had some time to think about it, and yes, at first I was pissed off that you had to get to me through someone who never deserved it, but now I see no point, I am not angry, not happy, not sad or confused, not even scared by what you did. I am not stupid Asis, I know if that was me you had in that parking lot, you would not have left me in that state, you would have kept dropping me, kicking me, producing life threatening moves on me until I was more motionless than my brother was, you would not stop until I was dead. But I think maybe Asis that you only went through my brother because in some way you want and need me around, I am a toy to you, I am here for you to destroy, for you to take out your anger on, but it has not all been going your way lately has it Asis, I know you have had your fair amount of swings at me, and yes I have been hurting from our little meetings in the ring, but honestly Asis, I enjoy them as much as you, and as much as these people like to see them. There is something satisfying about being able to hit someone, too see their blood, and something so powerful about being able to take down someone you would have called your friend only a month or so ago. We need to admit Asis, that we both seem to need and want each other around, and at Faith Asis, you will have your chance against me, you will have the chance to go one on one with me, a chance to see how much you really need me, how much you think I am yours.' Standing up Seara walks to the open window, and despite no smoking signs, lights a cigarette, she knows it could cause problems for her brother, and she knows it is dangerous, but no one seems eager to venture in the room while she is there. It is her first time in this city, and her reputation here seems to be high, it has preceded her, and she seems slightly flattered, yet almost annoyed by the situation. She can be seen clenching her fists, the cigarette hanging out the corner of her mouth, and in a moment of pure anger, she turns to the nearest wall and punches it with all her might. Her only want now is too get her hands against human flesh, to feel their pulse race as she grabs them and chokes them while looking deep into their fear filled eyes. Revenge is not necessarily in her books, but anger and a wanting to hurt is. The female walks around the room, muttering under her breath. “To kill you would be a sin to me, to love you and want you would be my desire, to have you would be the feeling of pain and hatred, I want and need, long for the chance, the power, the desire, but there is nothing within this hollow body, I have no feelings for you, I am numb t you. I hate you so much that I wish everyday for the perfect opportunity to finish you, just like Vinnie, but I love you, I have no control, this is how I feel, I feel you within me, I know you are already a part of me, you are like a parasite, infecting me, poisoning me, but you have no control over me, you will never end me, only I can end me. I will love you to your grave, my love will kill you, you will not know how much I love you until I kill you, you are numb like me, numb inside, numb feelings, but I know that I will end you with my love. I will kill you to be with you for all eternity, we will coincide, become one, we will become…” With that, Seara leaves the hospital room, leaving a picture of herself and her two brothers on the bed, tucked under Jakes hand where he is holding it tight in his lifeless state. The scene fades to black as Seara turns out the door and walks down the hallway, her cigarette still burning.
