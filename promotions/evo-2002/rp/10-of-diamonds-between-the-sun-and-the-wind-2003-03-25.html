10 of Diamonds - Between The Sun and The Wind

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/25/03 It sounds like a whisper It seems like a dream And there he was, staring down at his belt sitting in his lap, shining up into his face with every passing orange streetlamp. Driving the streets of Seattle, seeing the streets fly by through the rain covered windows, Jason didn’t see the same scum that he saw every other day of his life. Come to think of it, Jason really didn’t see anything out of his window that gave him any real thoughts. While the gold in his lap should have made him the happiest man on the face of the planet, his soul still carried with it a hole that couldn’t be filled with such a mundane piece of metal. Jason was empty, even when he was suppose to have everything. It breaks and it falls It tears at the seams The essence of the moment was an enigma. Something, a voice of some kind, yelled its dissent through out his mind, yet there was still a sense of bliss filling his mind that couldn’t be silenced. But the longer he lived with it and the more he heard the cry of his agony over the elation of the moment, he realized that, piece by piece, it was all falling back to the way it had been before. Suppose that it happens Suppose that it's real Pulling onto the highway surrounded by the endless miles of Nevada desert, Jason put the belt in the back seat, and thought of Boston. The snow covered cement and chilly air, all slowly melting away and giving life to a new season. Jason could remember springs in Boston, and how beautiful a city could truly be when the sun shined down upon it. The slightly stiffed air mixed with a breeze throwing through the streets. Enough for anyone to be happy. Supposing you're right Suppose it won't heal forever The last time he was in Boston, the snow was falling and the cold went straight to the bone. If summer had ever existed, you couldn’t tell. Inside the confines of a car, staring through a vivid screen towards the life that he threw away. She smiled and laughed, while he ached and cried within. He was so close to her, and yet he couldn’t touch her. She was a distant memory, even though she only sat a few yards away. And I will get old and tired And nothing will get to me He could remember the urge to run out, and take back life, but that would have been the hardest thing he would ever have to do. It would take more strength the he had, even though he body built whatever chance he got. It would take a heart that he hadn’t felt beat for ages. It would have taken something deep inside of him that was buried away, the trunk thrown out to sea and the key shot up to the sky. His happiness was the sun at dawn, yet his misery was the cold wind that blew through early morning. No one will want to have me Longing to be set free Now here he was, driving through the abandoned sand land of the west, with a title that he didn’t know whether he wanted or not over his head and raining in his thoughts. What if I had gone in to see her? What if I did and she was in my arms right this instant? I would be the happiest that I could remember being. Every wish that I have made in my dreams would come true, and every nightmare that keeps me awake at night would be diminished to vague instances of nothing. But if I did… I wouldn’t have been there for the match. I would have missed the match, and this championship would still be only a dream, not a reality. I wouldn’t be the universe’s holder. I would only be… I would only be with her. I would only be with her again. It’s not like that’s important to me, now is it Jason? If only the sun would take me If only the wind was on my side Jason wanted to bang his head against the steering wheel, regardless of the accident that would follow and the injuries that would occur. A mix of happiness and agony was, for some reason beyond his knowledge, far worse then one extreme on its own. The universe in his hands, and the world dragging behind him, Jason drove into Las Vegas, a sign with the ten of diamonds, amongst other cards including the infamous Royal Flush of spades, welcoming him to the city of sin. If only the sun would take me If only the wind was on my side Song Credit: “On My Side” by Ra
