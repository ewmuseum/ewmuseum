Blinding Insanity

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens with the blurred trail of white snowflakes falling down quickly and making it very difficult to see anything. The blizzard whistles and blows through the air with ease, the black night hard to make out behind the snowflakes because of a light post illuminating through the snow like it’s an acid trip. The shadows of the tiny snowflakes accumulated plentiful on the white snow below with the bright light of the sign causing them to cast over. The scene pans slowly, a figure trudges through the snowstorm quickly and with huge, strong strides through the deep snow. The man with his head down and covered in snow like the abdominal snowman, he wipes the snow from his face and shakes his head. The face distorted through the thick falling snow. The man getting closer now, he wipes his face off again trying to make vision of his surroundings, the face of Morpheus Omega can be made out with a disturbed look in his eyes and sinking grin on his face. He looks around but cannot make anything out. Morpheus can be heard cursing to himself, 'Why the hell didn’t I kill Roc last week before I got stuck In Canada? Dirty conniving little prick… That man is pleading for his death wish. Like a leech sucking blood from me! Living off my sick behavior and thinking he can control me like his puppet! All I can say is… The fuck with professional wrestling and death to that little maggot Thug Roc!' Morpheus screams out of rage with hope of escape from this cold and bitter reality. Morpheus continues trudging through the snow like he was a soldier dead set on mission and not stopping for anything. Lights slowly shine brighter from behind Morpheus. Two headlights can be seen through the snow. Morpheus turns around in hope of them slowing down and picking him up. Morpheus dives near the side of the road and waves his arms hoping the kind Canadians would slow down and pick him because of the terrible weather conditions. A voice can be heard screaming from the vehicle, the words heard faint and barely at all. 'Hello over there. You need a ride?' The words are no longer heard. Morpheus trudges through the snow faster and faster, he stops dead in his tracks to find the figure of someone that can’t be made out from the heavy falling snow. ??? Morpheus can be heard talking with the sound of awe, 'It can’t be!' There’s pause and the figure through the snow can be heard with a sick chuckle. The scene ends to Morpheus wiping the snow from his eyes and face in disbelief of who the man really was and why he was there. Scene fades. Morpheus finding himself stuck in the cold streets of Canada after making huge impact at Rise of the Legends but against his own free will. Morpheus decimated the Rising Star Champion Shawn Mason, upon the command of Thug Roc, he who seems to have the Infinite of Evil wrapped around his finger quite well. Morpheus now abandoned in another country that he was forced into by another. He has now encountered another who he is very surprised and shocked to see. The prophecy continues but the path it’s taking is a very shady one. To be continued...
