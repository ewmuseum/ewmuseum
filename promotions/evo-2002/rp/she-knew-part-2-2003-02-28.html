She Knew part 2

28 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th Febuary 2003 A shaft of light filtered into the room from upstairs and pulling herself to a sitting position, Seara looked around the converted basement bedroom in her father’s house. The door at the top of the stairs was slightly open and looking up the female seen a figure appearing against the bright light of the mid day sun. Lifting her hand the female rubbed her fingers over the left side of her forehead and grimaced in pain as her hand glided across the cut still fresh from the night before. ‘ Morning sleepy. You feeling any better?’ Bruce’s voice was gentle and it was easy to tell he did not want to risk his sister having a headache. ‘ You collapsed again when we got you back here; I had to out you to bed. I called Scott, but he wasn’t home.’ Nodding Seara glanced at her brother; seemingly grateful he had taken care of her the night before. ‘What happened? I don’t remember anything after getting up to go to the toilet’ the females voice was shaky and confusion sounded on her words. ‘ What do you mean collapsed, like fell over?’ Sitting down at the bottom of the bed, Bruce stared at his sister and the blood soaked pillow, a stain of blood had been left down her cheek where her blood had flowed with so much easy after her impact with the table. ‘You’ve got to take it easy today Katherine, if your still like this tonight, you won’t be able to fly back to Detroit.’ ‘ But I have to go home Bruce, I have to go and make sure Scott is fit enough again.’ ‘ I don’t think so Katherine. He may be injured, but you don’t even remember getting hurt’ remarked Bruce, pausing he sighed and looked around at a picture on the wall of Seara Frost L2J and Jameson. ‘ I’m glad those guys came into your life…what happened last night, why the hell did you collapse?’ ‘ I felt sick and light headed, and I got up…I…I don’t remember anything else. Guess I was so overwhelmed that I haven’t really eaten much; I’ve been training and spending a lot of time with Scott. I’ve been so happy lately, I guess I kind of pushed my body a bit too far, I wasn’t taking care of myself, I was just so…I can’t explain it.’ Swinging her legs out of the bed, Seara placed her feet on the soft-carpeted floor and began to get out of the bed, but before she had a chance to get to her feet and off the bed fully, Bruce pushed her back down onto it. His face told the female that he knew what she was about to do, and turning his head to the side, he looked over at the weights. ‘Think again Katherine, you’ve gotta rest. You don’t want your fiancée to see you looking fragile do you; I know that is something you hate, looking like you need to be taken care of.’ ‘Bruce, come on, I’ll be alright, it was just a silly fall, I split my head open, big deal, I have to train.’ ‘ No Katherine. You don’t have to train, you don’t have a match on Wednesday, and I’m pretty sure you won’t be pulling any little stunts. Scott won’t let you get hurt if he thinks you’re a bit risky.’ ‘Bruce, honestly you have no fucking idea do you’ Seara slammed her hand into the table next to the bed and her fist disappeared through the wood. When she was annoyed or felt slightly off, Seara was violent and Bruce knew it all too well. ‘ Scott isn’t going to stop me doing anything, but he may try and prevent me, and I have things to do this week. Training doesn’t mean I’m going to fight, this week I’ll have a little someone to teach some tricks to.’ Getting to her feet, the female barged past her younger brother and began to pack a bag. ‘ If I head to the airport now, I’ll be able to get an early flight. Bruce can you love me enough to go phone Scott and tell him I’ll be home early but I’m going to get a cab from the airport. If you could do that for me, I would be so happy.’ Doing as his sister asked, Bruce left the room to go and call Scott, he knew Seara, and he knew she always left when it suited her, she had never been one for staying in one place too long, but it seemed to Bruce that with Scott, she would stay for good. As the door to the room was pushed close, Seara’s cell began to ring a familiar tune and with a smile the female picked it off the floor and read the name on the front. Scott… fading to black, the scene disappeared to the sounds of Seara’s voice, the tone of it completely different to the way she had addressed her brother. To Be continued.
