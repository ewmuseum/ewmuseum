She made me do it........

2 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OCTOBER 3rd 2002 'She made me do it......how else was I supposed to react? Here I am fighting and clawing my way for a spot on the roster; while she take her's for granted. Part of me wanted to eliminate her right there...show Tyler that I was as good if not better. The other half of me however wanted to protect her. Was it my fault that she was in the wrong place at the wrong time? Was it my fault she caught my eye both professionally and personally?' APRIL 23rd, 2004 Bianca Dare is taking a midnight ride to clear her head. She has been consumed of thoughts that the man known to her as Xavier Citizen didn't exist before the year 1989. That woman she met awhile ago who claimed to be his mother, maybe it's time for her to pay her a visit. However now it's about getting the words of her departed friend out of her head; she had just read the latest installment of his journal and was disturbed by his thoughts and motivation towards Evolution superstar Seara.... Oct 3rd 2002 continued...... '........I know what those who know me will say. She is not the Heartbreak Chick, she was not responsible for what happened. I know that I realize that. Somehow though deep inside if given the chance she would. They are two sides of the same coin. I have watched her studied her and as much as I may want her, I feel the need to destroy her. Ohhh my god I am so confused now...am I talking about Seara or Heartbreak Chick? Damn I don't know anymore! All I know is that I need to solidify my spot in Evolution and escape Malcolm's shadow. But being that close to her.....the smell, the feel....who dammit who was I thinking about her or HBC? I can't take this anymore I'm a dying man don't I get a dying wish? Maybe Seara will become my proverbial genie in a bottle....the woman who will sacrafice all to make my wishes my dreams come true. I wish....I dream....I will become Evolution Universal Champion; before I die. Maybe if I rub her three times that goal will become a reality? or maybe by doing it the old fashioned way.....and eliminating her before hand will accomplish the same goal?' OCTOBER 3rd REAL TIME The door bell rings at the palacial estate of Tyler Davidson. His maid promptly answers the door. maid:'He is expecting you.' Both the man and the maid walk to Tyler Davidson's study. Once there the maid leaves both men alone. Davidson:'Long time no see....what can I do for you? I must say your call surprised me?' The man is revealed to be Malcolm King..... MK:'I've come to clear the air; and to ask you to stop this game you are playing...' TD:'Ask me to stop this game? What game would that be Malcolm?' Mk:'This is between you and myself; there is no need to bring them into this.' Davidson sits back in his chair slightly amused that he has rattled the once unflappable Urban Legend..... TD:'King the martyr role does not suit you at all. Contrary to what some may believe the world does not revolve around Malcolm King and how I may feel towards him. Our issues were resolved a long time ago; what I have done was strictly a business move.' MK:'I see.....' TD:'As far as this game you are referring too what would that be? The hiring of your former partner Xavier Citizen who just happened to be the bastard child of your former trainer and mentor William 'The Blackwhisper' Louise; or could it be the my outbidding you for Mr. Louise's school to establish it as a developmental territory. Or is it the fact that I signed William Jr. to a developmental contract and will provide him the chance that both you and Citizen could not....a shot at the big time.' Malcolm humbled by Davidson's words simply states.... 'Tyler this is between us......' Davidson leans over and stares Malcolm in the eye.... TD:'These move is strictly for business. As much ratings as having two brothers who don't realize they are brothers face each other would get Evolution. I'm not about to reveal that....I respect what Xavier is doing, by paying his dues and trying to get out of YOUR shadow. And Malcolm if you think about it, I didn't have to hire X and take on his medical bills when he has friends like you more than willing to help....' Malcolm sits in silence almost ashamed. '.....so before you preach about what is and is not righteous, take a look in the mirror. As far as Willie Jr. he's a talent who just happened to inherit his fathers wrestling school and talent also so my buying that is a business move and not a way to screw with Malcolm King. You see Malcolm life moves on...maybe you should also.' to be continued......
