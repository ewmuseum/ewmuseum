13 September 2003

Date: 2003-09-13 Everybody seems to be looking back and reminiscing or looking forward and predicting. Well, not everybody. But that’s a figure of speech though, isn’t it. Isn’t it? Anyway, there I was getting all teary eyed at Scott Evans’ emotional trip down memory lane whereas I couldn’t help but smile at Aaron Justice’s typical pre-match rant where he stated he would “hand me my ass”…or some tripe like that. Of course, it was only natural that he would state that and the rest of the mumbo jumbo that I happened to catch. It was his riot act. It was his pre-match speech. He’s not going to come out and say anything negative, is he? “Oh, I don’t know about the match. I’m hoping for a win” Nah, sitting on the fence isn’t Justice’s style…in fact, it isn’t anybody’s style to say that. But I’m trying to make a point here. You’ve seen him, a big man, built like a brick shit house and with arms and legs the size of trees. Can you imagine a man of that stature sitting on the fence before a match? Pfft, not for all the tea in China. But what struck me as slightly amusing was the stereotypical big-mans-intimidating-speech. I can guarantee that the words “ass” “kick” and “your” – along with an ensemble cast of words – will feature in any Aaron Justice rant. Why? Because he has no intelligence, because he is a primate and because even he knows that grunting wouldn’t make any sense. Oh and I liked the way Sid used the pun. You know the “I will Hunt your ass down”. Very creative. Maybe this big goof has got some intelligence…but lets not get carried away, eh? I’m not saying that Sid was wrong in any way shape or form. He did hand my ass to me, he gave me everything he had. He pulled out all the stops…but the simple fact is, he didn’t get the job done. Mr Justice. You didn’t get the job done. You can call on Evans’ interference as something that might have stopped you completing the task at hand, but cast your mind back. I had your legs pulled back and had so much pressure on your lower back that Evans’ unintentionally saved you. Don’t worry, I’m going to go into some piss poor “you tapped out” chant because 1) you obviously didn’t and b) anybody who had the creativeness to come up with obvious shite like that is nothing but an overpaid chimp. But the thought is there though, don’t you think? Your height advantage. Your strength advantage…and you simply didn’t make the difference count, at the end of the day. Sure, like I’ve said, you treated me like a rag doll at times in that match…but all it did was bruise me, not keep me down. Wait, is this another “I’m still here. I’m still breathing so you haven’t won” speech is it? Jesus, I hate those. It’s like the generic response for any has-been that decides to return to the stage they once owned while they do get their asses handed to them by some young buck. Urgh. I have the tendency to drift away from the point of topic, so anyway… The fact is, camache, you didn’t get the job done. I will look forward to our next encounter to see if you have actually learnt anything. Although, I highly doubt that. And so on to the bitter Mr Evans. I presume that you are bitter following the task I completed at X-Rated (hey Sid, watch that match and learn how to finish somebody off) and judging from your little cameo role at the end of the Hunt-Justice match, otherwise you wouldn’t have shown up, put your filthy mits on me and crashed my cranium with a chair. It seems I rocked your ego, good sir and it seems that you don’t take defeat lightly, eh? For crying out loud man, get over it. It’s happened and it’s in the past. Okay, so there’s a blotch on your match record. Who cares? Hey, if you would have beaten me at X-Rated, I wouldn’t hold it against you. I would have accepted defeat – all be it grudgingly – and admit that the better man won. But hey, you’ve got a title…why should you be concerned over some English camache that has only been in eVo for five minutes? But that’s it, Scott. I was the better man and you can’t admit that, can you? It just eats you up inside that the eVo new boy strolled in and had his way with you. And that’s exactly what I did. Sure, you were a gusty, stubborn little sod, but I kept chipping away and I eventually skinned you. I guess this is point where I say that it’s bloody lucky that your precious title wasn’t up on the table. Heh. So with that in mind, I think I should a title shot. I deserve a title shot. Course, I could demand a shot but how is going to help? Whenever somebody demands something, they never get it. So why should I curse myself? Before you refuse with a snarl with spit drooling, let me have my final thought, so to speak. You’ve got something to prove…to the people, to you colleagues and more importantly, to yourself. “You so can’t let that British asshole get away with it, Scott. He is so like totally mocking you, yuh-uh” a few of your fellow Yanks would say. Will you listen to them? Hey for all I know, you have every intention of stepping between those elevator cables and going at it with The Postman. Maybe you want to prove to yourself, as well as everybody else, that my victory at X-Rated was nothing but a fluke, that you had an off day and that it would never happen again. And so on and so on and ariston. I’ll be waiting, whatever you decide…camache. Let the games begin…
