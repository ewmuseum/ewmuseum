Molding Clay

10 November 2002

Date: 11/10/02 Laughter is heard. It’s in the darkness, but it’s clear and very real. Laughter might be to strong a word, maybe it’s more of a chuckle. Yeah, a chuckle would be an accurate description of what your hearing. Although you can’t see a darn thing, you’re positive you’ve heard this laugh, or chuckle before. It sounds a lot like Sovereign, the co-holder of the eVo Universal Championship. It’s been a few days now, last Wednesday it was when Sovereign Pile-drove his partner L2J off the ring apron to the padded floor below. The silence in the arena was shocking. Sovereign’s done some amazing things to L2J over the last month or so, however a pile-driver, falling 4 and a half feet onto 1 inch hard padding probably was the most disturbing show if his disdain for the Fearless One to date, and worst of all he seems to be laughing about it. Then silence parts the laughter, the chuckling. Several foot falls break the silence and then a click, and a lightbulb sparks to life. Sovereign stands under the light, hair strung about his face, the Universal Title baring no name place rests upon his shoulder. He took it with him last week, as it appears as if L2J might not make it to the Road to Glory pay per view on the 24th. I told you all that I’m not good at sharing with other people. L2J, you were warned that last week I would be looking for my own personal vision of revenge on you. You were given your chance. You tried your best, and once again you were carted off to the hospital at my hands. I have to be honest L2J, you are one tough son of a bitch. Even after I threw you 15 feet through a table, I cut you up so bad that you almost needed a transfusion. Then I drove your head down onto the hard unforgiving floor! Not a word since. I’m sure that the Notorious is playing their cards to their chest as their fallen champion is probably laid up in a bed with a brace around his neck. I can’t blame them, the figure head, the champion L2J, the fearless one is a cripple and it’s because he didn’t give up when he lost the first time to Sovereign. Hell, I would have let him be on his own marry little way, but he struck a nerve with me. So I decided to string him along with my string with the ball of cheese so that little Rat would enter my trap. And he sure did, but in the end my finger got caught under the killing device. Now I took the broom and beat that little freak over the head and I’m waiting to see if there’s any sign of life. Because that’s what you are L2J. You’re a rodent, vermin, a rat. Something that doesn’t deserve life. Something that has no purpose other then to frighten children and give pleasure to the one that squashes it. You squeak in between the cracks and make my life a living hell for the few weeks now that you’ve been given the honor of being called a champion. It just really pains me to look at any sort of listing on any web site and see Co- Champion. It bothers me to get phone calls from people wanting my thoughts on co-championships. I have Max Dickens calling me every other day wanting to know if he can interview me. I might just take him up on it so I can beat the hell out of that probing bastard. That’s not important, what is important that I’ll be very disappointed if a few things don’t happen around here. First of those being L2J not making it to Road to Glory. That’s key, you see because breaking L2J’s neck is all well and good. But that’s only one body part. I want to break all of you L2J. I want to see you at Road to Glory and destroy every last pound, every inch of your body that night. I want to feel your blood running down my forearms. I want to dig my fingers into your open wounds and pull at them until I rip them open so badly that you’ll beg me to release you. I can close my eyes L2J and see that look upon your face, the blood flowing like wine. Your eyes cloudy, your mouth gapping. That’s something I wish I could frame and put up on the wall of my home for the rest of my life. It was beauty, it was art. Now, it’s time for the true masterpiece L2J, your body is the clay and I will mold you. Sovereign whips his head back, the hair flies out of his face, no more does he have a bandage over his left eye from the House of Pain match. No longer is his arm bandaged from when he went through the wall of the cage. He shakes his head somewhat to settle his hair but still that wild look exists in his eyes. They almost sparkle with instability Secondly, after L2J not making it I would be just as disappointed if Scott Frost didn’t live up to his so called reputation this coming week. I’ve had an itch for awhile, even more so since I have been forced to hold this along side L2j to really defend this title. Scott Frost might have been the Knight in shining armor for the Knight in shining armor, it’s your turn to try and slay the dragon. And I’m a big nasty fire breathing Dragon Mr. Frost. It’s the old Ice vs Fire metaphor I suppose. Maybe that’s something you thought of before hand, I certanly didn’t because it’s not important. But I digress. Frost, you showed up in eVo and I remember the first thing that came out of your mouth that I gave a damn was that you were after the Universal Title. You want to take that away from your friend L2J, then okay. You want to take it away from me, then lets see if your are as cold hearted as you say you are. Let’s see if that’s ice in your veins and not just regular old, run of the mill Blood. Because at Legends this coming week, you get your shot at the Universal Championship. You get your shot at Sovereign, the man that might very well have ended the career of your fearless Knight L2J. The man that has a long and detailed history of ending careers, and if your lucky you might be spared from the list. Personally, it’s not an issue for me. I get a unique opportunity. I destroyed L2J last week, now I get my shot at Scott Frost... to end the Notorious before it even gets it’s foot in the door of “Stable” wars in Evolution. Some find it sad, I find it Ironic that someone that isn’t part of the Omen Brotherhood New-TV or The Unlimited would almost single handedly destroy this great fan oriented faction, hell bent on accomplishing it’s goals, whatever they might be. I’m no stranger to Stable wars my friend Scott Frost. But in the end, your friends aren’t going to be any help to you if they can’t walk on their own. This week Frost, it’s your turn to see if you can dethrone me, or if you can even survive me. Because I have every intention of placing you beside L2J in the hospital for an extended visit. Scott Frost, you might have managed to get yourself into this Title match as some sort of sympathy or revenge sort of situation. But regardless of who your friend is, or what I may have done to him, you need to focus on one thing this week. Your own survival. Sovereign takes a step forward, shadow lurkes over him now as you can hear his breath on the microphone now, Sovereign chuckles to himself slightly, quickly and quietly as he presses forward wit his words. Scott Frost, you are the preview for the Road to Glory. Some might consider you a favorite. But if anyone thinks that the House of Pain, or what I did at Digital Destruction was the creme de la creme of what Sovereign is willing to do for the Universal Championship, then there are a lot of people who are going to have their eyes opened wide at Road to Glory. The fans will be disappointed that the mighty, talented and cold hearted Scott Frost won’t be there. They’ll be heart broken that L2J might not be able to show. They’ll sit and watch Sovereign destroy everything, everyone in his path. You see, this is a crusade of Violence. This is my kingdom to reign. I spent 2 months on “vacation” in attempt to set L2J up. Now Sovereign is back, he’s back with avengence, 24 hours a day, and 7 days a week. I don’t close, I don’t take extended lunch breaks. This week at Legends Scott Frost, it will be you on one end of the ring, looking across at the one man that no one has been able to stop or slow down since American Nightmare almost 4 months ago. The man that ended careers like Jamal Kage or like Impact. And your name Scott Frost, might just very well end up written down in the casualty list beside them. Everyone is talking about the New TV, Unlimited, Notorious, Omen Brotherhood war that’s breaking out. Well here’s a news flash. I’ve been waging war on everyone long before your names became famous. Long before Scott Frost even stepped foot in eVo I was taking it by storm, destroying everything, leaving nothing but blood and broken bodies in my wake. Now, with all this attention on you fools and your stables it’s time for Sovereign to prove that he is the personification of Dominance. I don’t need 3 or 4 friends backing me up Scott Frost, and that goes the same for all you fools plotting in your hotel rooms, your locker rooms or where ever you decided to come up with it all. I’ve done things in eVo that make your children cry, your fans change the channel. Scott Frost, you might want the Universal Title, and you might well be cold hearted. But when I’m done, this week you’ll be nothing but broken hearted, with no friends walking to save you. Sovereign reaches up and clicks the light off... darkness. I hope I’m not disappointed. Chuckling is heard. I don’t like it when I’m disappointed. L2J, I hope I see you at The Road to Glory. I hope I can look into your eyes. I Hope that I can show you what you need to be shown. Show you what you deserve to be given. I don’t want to regret what I did last week. Because I thought, from experience that you were more of a man than that. Chuckling is heard from Sovereign once again, followed by a few foot steps, then silence. I hope that you are ready for what is coming next. Because this time, things will be different.
