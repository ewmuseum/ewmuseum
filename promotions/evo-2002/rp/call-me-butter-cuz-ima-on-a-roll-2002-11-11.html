call me butter cuz Ima on a roll

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not that it’s important, but this promo takes place inside a gym. Evolution superstars are working out throughout the place, getting ready for another house show later in the night. We see Soveriegn squatting about 500 pounds with ease…the camera turns a bit to see Scott Frost trying to bench press the same weight. More over we see Xavier Citizen putting down a curl bar as His Bitch wipes his forehead with a towel. We can see her peering out of the corner of her eye at something; the camera moves again, and we see it is Tommy Levi she is checking out. An finally, the camera stops on Big Daddy Dreamer and Thomas Draven along with Natalia…spotting each other on a tough set of incline bench press? NO. Helping each other on that last military press rep? HA. Try sitting on their asses…BDD playing with a yo-yo…Draven eating an ice cream bar. Hey Draven…I got a frickin match this week, singles dude. Yeah…so what? You’re a frickin wrestler for a living, you tend to have matches. Yeah, but I’m one of THE wrestlers, dickslut, why do I need a singles match against some two-bit, bong toking hack quoting some religious bullshit, trying to sound all edumicated? Dude, it’s a warm-up match for The Battle of Toronto shitass, quit yer bitchin.’ Ah yes…Daniel must remember how good the ratings were when we held EVERY frickin title in EDW, AWF, and so many others at the same time…and thus, wants me to be the person to knock the ratings through the roof, blowing off the last marquee match-up with Soveriegn and L2J. I aint interested though…so you wanna hit up Vegas or something this week and tell the bossman to piss off? Ok, first off lazynuts…we’re contractually OBLIGATED to be there. Second of all, the whole frickin fed is after YOU, YET again, so we probably don’t want to miss this card. It might screw up our angles. And right away, Draven’s hand hits his mouth, for he knows he just committed a wrestling sin. ESPECIALLY for the The Syndicate, who left their “smart” days behind them long ago. They are MARKS once again, even more so than when they were back when Hogan body slammed Andre the Giant and they nearly pissed their pants. A mark doesn’t refer to such nonsense as “angles.” Dude, a…uh…what?!? I don’t understand. Um…I’m talking jibberish…nevermind. We might not collect our paychecks is what I meant to say. Too many Briskdrivers last night. Whatever. You got that Heck shaving-device this week, so just kick his ass and be done with it. Man, that aint the problem. Beating that slutass will be about as routine as my morning shit. It’s afterwards…I gotta stomp on Jinx, Jinosyde, Asis, Xander, when I really don’t care to, but will just because they affiliated themselves with The First Lady of Evolution. Deal with that later, guey. Why don’t you just sit here and randomly talk about this week’s opponent? That seems to win matches for a lot of people. Haha, yeah. I’ll do that instead of wasting time lifting weights Funny how they claim to never work out, and yet are fairly ripped and can kick anyone’s ass. Gotta thank Scottie Scott Steiner for hooking the brotha up! What do you know about this slutbuster? Well, I know he uses both his parents last names, but not his first. Huh? His mom’s last name is slut, and his pop’s last name is whore. It’s got a hyphen, that’s how I can tell. Um…Big Daddy…I think you’ve used your head in too many of your finishers or something. It’s Slutwhore, you know, as in the mythical beast the Ben Juventude Guerra? Mythical? Then this slut doesn’t even exist! I really can hit up the blackjack tables this week! Shibby… No fucktart! It’s just a name. The only thing mythical about him is his so-called talent. Haha, I know, I’m just dickin with ya, cause that’s about how much I’m actually sweating this dude. Man, I wish he was here… Why? Would you tell him he’s a slutass, and then kick his frickin ass? Nah, I’d just tell him he’s a slutass, and wait to kick his frickin ass this Wednesday. That way, I’d still be able to get a kick out of him blabbing for a week as if he actually, you know, has a chance at beating me. Well why don’t you look into that camera that just happens to be right over there and tell him anyway? They both get this bright eyed look that says “How convenient!” Pure sarcasm mind you. BDD looks at the camera, and without sipping on bottled water, pulling his hair back, or pulling down sunglasses…he SPEAKS! Alright….Bongboy You watching on that 24-hour Evolution channel? I hope you are, cause I need to let you in on something. You spewed some shit about racism, religion and popularity, and calling me a bigot or some crack induced narcosis. I hope you referring to the SLIM CHANCE at beating the hell out of me, and not implying that you’d actually done it before. If that were the case, I’d say you’d better dislodge your head from your frickin ass, cause the only thing you’ve done around here is bore the piss out of everyone, give everybody a bathroom break when you speak and serve as the posterboy as to why mothers shouldn’t drink while their pregnant. The chance to beat the hell out of me isn’t anything too impressive anyway. I mean, ANYONE could get that chance…my little brother, some slutass fan in the stands, your skanky ol’ lady, that is if you have one. Doesn’t mean they are actually going to do it. Look, Bennie Bongboy, I think you need to understand something pinche mamon. It’s not like I’m some midcard dude around here. I rule this fed along with my compadres here. I mean, the last time I held a singles title, I beat everyone who challenged me for it so many times each, I frickin retired it due to lack of competition. Trust me Juventude…you haven’t studied shit when it comes to me. I could care less about this match, but pissing all over your worthless sack of an ass suits me rather fine and dandy. So go ahead and forward this message to that pissass lawyer of yours… Juventude (aka Slutwhore, and Bongboy) will no longer require your services after this Wednesday. After that day, he won’t need the service of anyone other than some nurse to wipe his ass and feed him through a straw. Fax that shit to him, big shot. And with that said…I believe there is nothing left to say, other than… Cursing and full-female Nudity kicks ass…….WETBACK!!!
