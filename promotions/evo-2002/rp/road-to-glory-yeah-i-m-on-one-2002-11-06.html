Road to Glory....yeah I'm on one

6 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'I'm dying soon....this is no secret.' The words ring out as if a man who has accepted his fate and just awaiting the inevitable.... 'Time is closing drawing near and I'm stuck in a pointless war. A war of my creation and thenagain not of my creation. Malcolm King and myself were members of the original Unlimited a decision I am both proud of and embarrassed by. You see I'm ashamed that an organization I help found, that I had associated with....has turned into a carbon copy of cliche and idiocy. It is being used as a vehicle to 'get over' those who cannot get over on their own. This war between The Unlimited and Notorius is one in name only; because the truth of the matter is they both need each other, because without that symbiotic relationship they both would be nothing.' Xavier walks around the lockerroom attempting to collect his thoughts.... 'You see I was already 'over' in this sport before being associated with The Unlimited, but since I had disassociated myself with the Unlimited concept as I said before it had become a vehicle a tool littered with misguided drivers and handlers. So it did not suprise me when Asis joined the Unlimited, no what suprises me is that Xander hasn't taken advantage of his new status and continues to play these pointless games instead of making a point himself?' Xavier opens his locker and pulls out a t-shirt it has a picture of SEARA on it with the words 'Naughty is My Nature' on back. 'You see Asis, you apparently made a point a few weeks back....to rob me of my life for a second time. You see HBC and Dreamer did it to me the first time but let's face it; Malcolm has always been a self centered incompetent prick...so I expected him to lose my name. But you Asis.....you made the decision to to stop mynew life, my new name, AND DAMMIT MY NEW PUSH IN THIS DAMN BUSINESS all over this little slut right here.' points to Seara's picture...... 'I mean would Evolution had really missed her? I don't think so...but you Asis took it upon yourself to be my judge, jury , and executioner; symbollically killing the man Echo Blackwhisper. You see the shadow of the man I was trying to escape...you forced me back into. And in that shadow I was reborn for I knew that I could actually use Malcolm for MY benefit this time. It was a simple plan really eliminate you, win the Universal title, and die.....that is all I had to do. But Noooooooooo.....YOU ASIS HAD TO FUCK IT ALL UP BY JOINING THE UNLIMITED!!!!' Xavier rips Seara's t-shirt in half.... 'You see it no longer about this whore, ohhhhh no.....it became a matter of principle. I'M DYING AND YOU MADE ME REMEBER I HAD A PRINCIPLE OR TWO LEFT. A rather shocking occurance if I do say so myself...but the thought of someone like you laying claim to a legacy I help build is just down right......disturbing. I mean ask yourself...what is the point of this present incarnation of The Unlimited? Why is the Notorious fighting you? and why can't you stop your inevitable destruction at the hands of the OMEN Brotherhood?' Xavier bangs his head on the locker until a gash opens at the top of his head and blood starts to run very freely...... 'It makes your head spin trying to think of these answers doesn't it? Being apart of a stable that is about nothing fighting another stable that doesn't even know what it's fighting for, while all along The Brotherhood is watching and waiting to reap the spoils of victory. Along that vein lets talk about victory or the reasons why you won't win at next Legends. I'm dying Asis....so what makes you think I give a damn about advancing in a tournment when I may not live to reach the finals? Ahhh but it'll give me great pleasure to know that I was responsible for you not advancing my friend....for you Asis to lose out on your one chance....to be more than what you are. Don't worry Asis, come Legends...you're going to wish I was the jobber named Echo BlackWhisper.' the scene fades out as Xavier sports a crimsom mask.....
