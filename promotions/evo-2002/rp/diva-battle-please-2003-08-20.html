Diva battle? Please....

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-20 Paris, France Early Evening Damn it’s hot. For several weeks now, the City of Lights, Paris, France has been scorching in 100+ degree weather for several weeks now. So what does the First Lady of Evolution and the all-time Playboy seller do in weather like this….tan nude of course. With a near flawless body and multi-time champion to boot, HBC is enjoying a few moments of solace before heading back into the confines of an Evolution ring. Danielle, her French compatriot is busily trying to rub tanning oil over HBC’s ample bosoms so she doesn’t burn and look bad for her fans. HBC is obviously enjoying Danielle’s hands over her chest as she wiggles in her chair. HBC grabs Danielle’s head and forces it between HBC’s legs. Danielle struggles for a second before succumbing to HBC’s wishes. HBC smiles at Danielle, flashes a little look as Danielle goes to work pleasuring her boss. HBC grabs Danielle’s hair and pulls in tight as HBC moans out in delight as Danielle’s magic tongue goes to work. HBC thrust’s her hips and wraps her long legs around Danielle’s face. HBC bucks harder and harder until HBC screams out Danielle’s name and slumps back into the chair, spent but delighted. HBC kisses Danielle on the lips before standing up and looking towards the Eiffel Tower. “So it has begun, the ultimate diva of Evolution squares off against two females whom I am no stranger too, Seara and Xian. Xian, I remember you from the EDW days where you and Malcom King tried everything in your power to defeat me, yet it never happened. Yourself could never defeat me personally. It was everyone else ganging up on me was the only way to defeat me. What makes you think you can defeat me now. You really think you’ve gotten that much better? Hardly. But alas, I do know how crafty you can be. You once faked your own death only to return trying to unleash some retribution. What was it, a drive-by if I recall. Truth is Xian, you never belong in my level. You are beneath me as Danielle was earlier. Hell, you don’t even deserve that much. But here you are in Evolution, the best of best, everyone a champion in a previous federation. Our superfed as you might say, but I can hear you now in the distance. I’ve won this, I’ve won that, I was never defeated for such and such belt. My response……So what. Truth is, just like your counterpart, you haven’t beaten me, nor will you ever. The fates have it in for you my dear Xian. Sure I may admire your perseverance, but even that over time has become foolish. This is a match for the fans to see me jiggle my breasts and shake my ass. An eye-candy match. I know it, it has no real value when it comes to you and I. Because of that, you will wish you hadn’t faked your death back in EDW because when the ‘kiss of death’ comes, it just may be permanent for you.” “Now, the other competitor, I have a tremendous amount of respect for. I remember when you first appeared in Evolution, how nervous you were. How excited, how you nearly puked in the back before your matchup with me. What was billed to be a great contest, lived up to its name. Seara vs. HBC. Of course you’ll remember how that ended, one stiff superkick right in the kisser and that was all she wrote for you. But even I know that was back then and you’ve progressed much in your time here. You’ve became champion a few times over, had some memorable matches with Xander and even got married to Scott Frost. Congrats on the marriage, but I ask you though, did you marry Scott Frost because you loved him, or because deep down you know that was probably the only way you could get your hands on the Universal Championship. For love, or because you’re a whore. That is the real question isn’t it Seara. “ “See, I’ve been in this business for some seven years now. I’ve seen it all. Sure I’d be the first to admit I shake my ass and jiggle my tits, but I’ve never sold out doing it. I’ve done it for me, not to potentially land some title handed to me or should I say, down on my knees and working for the title handed to me. That will be your legacy when it is all said and done I’m afraid. Seara, the diva with potential, but took the easy road out. I’ve bled more than any superstar here. I’ve taken more bumps, more chairshots, took everything tossed at me and more, and here I am, still flawless, still with a supermodel body, still a champion who didn’t sell out. I’m sure Frost will try to save you in your match, he’s predictable to a fault, but it doesn’t matter Seara, at the end of the match, just as you work so hard to try and please Frost to get the Universal Championship, when I knock out Xian, and superkick you Seara, then, you will do what your best at. Whoring yourself out and pleasing me.
