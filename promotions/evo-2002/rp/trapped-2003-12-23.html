TRAPPED!

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-23 ~Grow…expand yourself beyond what you have done and what you can do. Drive yourself to do more, in effort to prove others wrong. The Road to Glory is a path of hardship…blood will be shed on this long road. One man has already weathered this journey once and he is once again ready to climb to the top of eVolution.~ ‘This fucking thing is out of gas…’ Shocka pulled his rental car to the side of the road and as he jumped out with a look of denial on his face. ~He jumped out and stomped around to the back he looked under the vehicle to rule out fuel leakage.~ ‘I’m stuck in the fucking outback?’ Shocka questioned himself. ’What in the fuck am I going to eat and drink….’ ~Shocka thought back to 120 miles ago at the liquor store he purchased a case of Fosters beer and a bag of jerky. He grimaced as he ran and jumped back into the vehicle he turned the key so the radio would still be on and rolled down the windows.~ ‘I wonder if anyone travels down this here road…I mean it looks pretty deserted…’ ~A red sports car zooms past Shocka’s parked car kicking up a load of dust and such to fly onto Shocka. Shocka raised his fist out his window shaking his fist in a warning sign to the driver…his way of saying ’you’re lucky I’m out of gas or I would find a way to hunt you down and slit your throat mother fucker!’ Okay maybe not so graphic… oh wait…this is Shocka…the #1 Stunna so it is graphic…damn it!~ ~He cracked open a beer and slugged it down quickly he threw it out the passenger window he listened intently to the music as the guitar riff quickened. If you can’t fuck or fight to it, it’s not worth listening to…that was Shocka’s theory and he lived to the beat of the music. Man, Johnny Charisma will sure be in for a treat this week when he is physically and mentally dissected by the most dangerous man inside of the squared circle. Shocka is now more ready than ever to prove his skills inside of the ring and now it’s his time to be stepped upon.~ ~5 beers later and now a patrol car rolls along side of the wounded SUV holding Shocka…drool foams from his mouth as beer can be smelled from yards away. The nice calm Australian officer approached overseeing the action.~ ‘Ain’t you one of them dang wrestlers?!’ ‘HUH!?’ Shocka yelped surprised not even noticing the man until now. ‘Yeah you’re here for a world tour!’ The man screamed now getting excited. ‘Well gee…golly gosh… whatcha doin’ way out here?’ the man now asked being more direct. ‘Well I ran out of gas and I just been…’ He looked down at the beer and took a wiff…he thought of the most polite way to put it…. ‘passing the time…’ He let out a little laugh. ‘Well I can give ya a ride to the next town I guess… we can get you some gas and bring it back out here.’ ‘Sounds good to me man…let me just…’ Shocka cranked the key into the on position and went to roll up the windows none of them moved and he shrugged it off.~ ‘guess we’ll have to find me a battery too.’ Shocka said with an embarrassed smile. ‘No problem as long as you got cash…’ ~Shocka smiled and looked into the SUV he grabbed his couple duffle bags CD’s and such and the case of beer and loaded into the cruiser with the officer. They traveled for miles and miles until they reached this one place on the map ya know? When they arrived Shocka had a heck of a time finding a gas canister and a battery but once he did they were on there way.~ RETURN. ~As the car rolled to a stop and the ignition shut off it was apparent immediately that there had been some tampering to Shocka’s vehicle. He jumped out and squatted looking at one wheel missing as soon as he moved it wound fall and ruin the car…~ ‘Got a spare?’ The officer asked. ‘I dunno…should lemme check.’ ~He popped the back and walked around the tire was gone he rolled his eyes and shook his head no to the man.~ ‘Welp…I better be going.’ The man jumped into his car and pulled away leaving Shocka with a gas can, battery and a partridge in a pear tree? Fuck that shit…we ain’t down with no holiday lovin today…I mean what a fuckin situation… ~Shocka reverted to the only thing he could…to the thing that anyone would at a time like this…alcohol. And now that he was a 1/4th of the way into a case why stop short and let any of it wear off and go to waste eh? He popped open 2 and 3 beers at a time and began drinking them rapidly. After about 5 he stopped and looked at his surroundings…how in the fuck was he going to get out of here?~
