Forgotten Years - V

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Happy New Year “The New Year is only a few minutes away, and by God this town has gone crazy. Hopefully 2003 will treat us just as good as 2002 did.” The television blasted in the background, with the voices of millions being carried through the open windows by the wind. A man, sitting alone and almost motionless, stared blankly into the screen of the television in front of him. With a beer in his hand, and a frown on his face, he stared forward. The arms of his body lay still on the sides of the arm chair. His face, slightly red from the earlier tears. His breath was rough and murky. His senses were all but gone at that point. “As I stand here, all the people around me are going wild. The noise is just so unbearable as they all prepare for what promises to be a great year for our nation and for the entire world.” On his right hand, along the side of his pointing finger, bled a paper cut, formed along the edge of a letter he had opened. His mind wandered back to it every now and then, reading it over and over and feeling the same way each and every time. But, as of about twenty minutes ago, the paper found its way to the floor, resting silently on the carpet next to the chair, his hand dangling above it and casting a shadow of symbolic sorrow. Now he bled, but not enough to care. The crimson fluid stained the carpet, but that was nothing to him. Nothing at all. “Only three minutes remain in the year. Three minutes. And then Hello 2003” Had he been dumb enough to just let her go? She had been everything to him. A world all her own, with nothing but light to lead him. And he just gave her up, like an elapsed moment in time, never to be lived again. How long ago was that? How many years have passed since the day that I left her behind? Jesus, it’s been four years since then. Four years that I have been alone. Four years on this lonesome road, waiting for fate to end me. “Two minutes. In two minutes the ball will drop and everyone will celebrate the coming of their new year.” If I could turn back that clock, and see the things that I have done, would I change them? Or would I keep them just the way they are? Would I be the same callous man that I am right now, or would I be compassionate? Could I have been one of the greatest human beings of all time? Or was I cursed to be this wretched man? To be uninhabited and uncaring? Or was I destined to end up a man of heart and empathy “Ladies and gentlemen, only one minute remains. One minute away from kissing one year good bye, and welcoming the new one. One minute.” Is it too late to turn this all around? Am I too far along this darkened path to turn around now? Or is there still time? Is there still time for me to take it all back, and become the man this world wanted me to be? Could I… “10” … still… “9” … have… “8” … the… “7” … chance… “6” … to… “5” … save… “4” … myself… “3” … from… “2” … my… “1” … end? “Happy New Year!” In the streets below, the voices of a thousand people roared for joy. Collectively, they shook the world apart. They sang and laughed and screamed and smiled, and all of them, together as one, were happy. A year had just died, as another was born. And now, with every second counting away inside his mind, it seemed to him that this was his second chance. A new opportunity at life. He could begin again, and change everything around. God had just given his soul salvation. Jason Jameson took the beer bottle, and tossed it against the wall. He ran over to the porch, and swung open the door, ignoring the chilly air. “Happy New Year!” he yelled at the top of his lungs. All the people below looked up at him and applauded and shouted in agreement. A smile ran across his lips, and he could feel the warmth in his heart again. Ironically, the wound on his hand stopped bleeding. He could feel true happiness again, and he felt like the luckiest soul in the world. Jason was ready to change. This was his second chance. His transformation. His final opportunity to turn it all around. And he wasn’t going to let it slip away. “A new year is upon us, and with it a new opening. Through it all, we can clearly say that we have come this far. And yet, we can clearly say that we have so much farther to go. We have been given a new year, and with it a new life. Happy New Year everyone! Back to you in the studio.”
