The first steps on the long road

27 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/27/02 SEPT. 27, 2002 'Part of me; I guess should feel a little insulted. Tyler Davidson has handpicked Marcus Escobar for me to 'debut' against. A fighter allegedly...who is eager to have a funeral. Funny thing how men like to use death as a metaphor when they find themselves in hostile situations or environments. Unfortunately for Mr. Escobar the terms of life or death have no meaning to me because I know my fate.....I know this is my last chance.' 'I like Tyler's sense of humor, the match I have is not televised and it's against a cell fighter. Maybe Davidson was going for the irony that Malcolm King's career was ended in The Cell environment by a wrestler. So why not end my Evolution career before it starts by having a cell fighter defeat me in the wrestling ring. The match is supposed to be my way of earning a spot onto Evolution TV....little do anyone know I plan to make my appearance felt in an official or unofficial capacity.....' AUGUST 10th, 2004 Bianca Dare is showering getting ready for the debut of Urban Legend Wrestling Nation to a national audience. As the soap and water cascade over her body her thoughts turn towards Xavier....today was his birthday. This was a day she dreaded for she knew that time doesn't necessarily heal all wounds. She gets out of the shower and dries herself off...on her bed is Echo's journal. 'I remember he called me when he found out about the match.....our conversation was one that stayed with me for a long while after that......' She sits down and looks at the journal.... 'Maybe his legacy doesn't necessarily have to die....' Half clothed she takes a notepad and pen and starts to write remembering her conversation with Xavier...... SEPT 27th, 2002 Bianca Dare's cell phone rings. She was just visiting Malcolm King who was in the hospital still recovering from injuries suffered at the hands of Xander in their PPV match. She goes outside to take the call, the voice on the other end is a familiar one.... Xavier:'Hello Bianca.' BD:'Hello Xavier or should I say Echo?' X:'Funny.....is he dead yet?' BD:'You know his injuries are not that serious....he's doing fine.' X:'He spends a week in the hospital and everything is fine? figures he's act like a prima donna.' BD:'That's not fair....this is the first time in 10 years he's had a chance to slow down....' X:'Anyway I didn't call to speak about Malcolm....I got my match it's against Marcus Escobar.' BD:'A cakewalk....I bet.' X:'Not so sure, Tyler wouldn't make it that easy for me...but it accomplished a major goal; it gets me in the building.' BD:'X....are you sure about this plan of yours? I mean really sure....' X:'I am sure, this is something I have to do....plus Malcolm and I made the promise before he died, one of us is going to fullfill it.' BD:'And that other thing.....' X:'She's there I know; but first I need to get by Escobar. Not too familiar with him which is why I need your help...get me as much info as you can I'll take care of the rest.' BD:'I'll forward it to you this weekend whatever I find...becareful this is a dangerous game you are playing.' X:'What is Escobar going to do kill me? I'm already a dead man.' AUG 10th, 2004 Bianca is interupted by the phone ringing. She answers it as Tyler Davidson is on the other end..... 'Yes Tyler, you just signed Booker Huffman? Congrats. Yes I'm going to the ULWN show tonight. No I don't need a limo thanks anyway. One day you and Malcolm will have to bury the hatchet. I'll continue to work on him....he has to realize that what happened to Xavier was not your fault.' to be continued.......
