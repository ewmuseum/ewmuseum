Legends XXX (1)

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.20.03 [Desire] ~Shocka has proven his desire for this business over the course of 30 legends before us by proving on his first appearance that he wouldn’t back down from big time players like Punisher Platinum Cassidy. It was the way Shocka, an underdog in eVolution broke onto the scene and it soon led to a rise in his hatred by the fans and his wrestling skill being all the better for it. He won the Continental title with no problem over Erin Christoph then a broken man a nothing…Shocka made him that way he softened him up for Scott Frost before he finished him. Shocka eventually lost the title like any champion thus far to Shodan after about a month reign. He had a taste of gold and he wanted more…he got into the Road To Glory tournament match and went through every single person until he got to Scott Frost the two faced off in one of great matches that the two had. Shocka proved once again though that he was good enough for the Universal title. This win over Frost at Rise of the Legends propelling him into the Universal title division.~ [Accomplishment] ~Shocka went into Nightmare Before Christmas one of the most hated men in eVolution who got absolutely no respect…but he came out with the Universal title and broke both L2J and Thug roC in half showing them that he was the man. Later that week L2J ended his own life with the downward spiral started by Shocka. At one time he was proud of what he had done but it turned into him being hated even more. But he still had the Universal title, but this brought rage to L2J’s close friend, and a man familiar with Shocka, Scott Frost. Frost attacked Shocka on many occasions and eventually coaxed him into battle where Shocka got the best of him in January but failed in February when Omega turned on Shocka. This is where today’s story picks up with Shocka and Omega enthralled in a battle that will only end when the other is put completely out of commission.~ [Rage] ~Recent events have brought Shocka to a boiling point with him Omega bringing back thug roC a man that Shocka should have properly disposed of when he had the chance. Now the two have beaten Shocka down before a match, but they also suffered a bit of revenge from the #1 Stunna himself. Just when they claimed that Shocka had no back up Shocka and his masked partner stormed the locker room of Omega and beat both roC and Morpheus into the nothingness that they are.~ ‘It felt so good…’ Shocka spoke quietly as the scene faded in. ~He sat in front of a steering wheel in the car a place where he got most of his thinking done. Anytime that he needed to clear his thought he would jump in and drive wherever the road took him. Maybe that’s part of the reason he got into the Wrestling business so he could think over every moment in his life and make decisions not out of rage. This recently changed when he got his hands around Omega.~ ‘The man just seems to do something that boils my blood, he turns me into a maniac when I am around him….i can’t seem to hold back any of my emotions. Usually when I am in a match I try to pace myself and wear down my opponent but when he’s in the ring or even near me it sends me into a fury. A tantrum of sorts that can only be calmed by watching his blood spill onto the mat.’ Shocka speaks in a sadistic tone of his own. ‘People think that Omega is mr. Bad ass and all that well I would like to ask the question out of all of you people…how in the hell do you think he got like that? I trained his ass to be the master of mind games when he was on my team but now that he is against me he think that he can out do me. Sure he brought in that lucky son of a bitch Hedges who does indeed hold six victories over me…big deal wins and losses mean nothing is how you fight in the war. I got the best of him on occasions and probably got myself Dqed a couple times but those matches really don’t stick out as anything important to me…you see when he beat me I was either coming up in a federation or on my way back down. I believe the first time was after I lost my World strap to Dimitri Williams and he had to pounce on my injured body.’ Shocka slowly trails off. ‘You know me and him have quite the history something for record books but that’s not the point here…its Omega and I battling for pride. That’s basically all that is at stake after that son of a bitch good for nothing asshole double crossed me. He does not realize that machine that he has set into motion he may be a force to be reckoned with but I will be the one to use the force against him. I have been inside his mind, I know exactly how he thinks when he thinks it…I can almost sense what he has coming. Just because he is straddling the line of sanity doesn’t mean that I can’t read him like a fucking 1st grade book.’ Shocka says with a slight smile. ‘You see folks, that’s exactly where I have the advantage…both he and thug roC came to me and opened up with everything that they had…I helped Omega progress along in his style, I am the one that taught him agility. He did nothing for me he can study all the tapes he wants but he does not know what is coming like I do from him. He can claim that his style has changed but his violent streak always shows through in his matches and it especially will in this match that we have set up for Unbreakable…you people think that I was going out on a whim when I requested that it be an Ambulance match?’ Shocka questions before shaking his head. ‘I always have something up my sleeve whether Omega likes it or not I will be the one who comes out victorious every time we go into that ring.’ Shocka laughs a bit. ~He turns his car off the side of the road and dials up his cellular telephone he begins speaking still on camera like he is not trying to hide a thing.~ ‘Hey, its Jonathan what’s up?’ He speaks and waits for a response. ‘Yeah, that took a toll on me to…but I think that we definitely got the best of them last night did you see the look on Omega’s face when we stormed into his locker room…he was totally un expecting.’ Shocka laughs for a moment and listens. ‘There is no way in hell that he is going to beat me at Unbreakable…but at Legends triple X…yeah I think he will have the advantage with thug roC at his side in that match.’ Shocka shakes his head and again listens. ‘I knew that I shouldn’t have laid my boots into S.…I was on his good side for so long and with one kick it was all flipped around…’ ‘Listen I have to let you go imma get back on the road yeah I am half way to Vegas already I am going to gamble all fucking day when I get there to take my mind off Omega for a while.’ Shocka says as he smiles and flips his phone closed. ~He gets back into his car and begins to drive away as the scene closes.~
