Rise of the Legends 1

30 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-30 Jennifer Frost comes walking up to a door backstage at a house show, mic in hand. She knocks on the door a few times, and it finally swings open, rather hard. It bumps into Frost, knocking her glasses sideways on her face and startling the shit out of her. Of course, the only response she gets from HBC is… Frostynuts? What the hell are you doing so close to the door, moron? I, uh….was just looking to get an interview with you real quick HBC. Shit…way to give me the heads up. Whatever…shoot. It should be noted that the forty of Olde English in Big Daddy’s hand is already half empty… It’s already pretty much assumed that you will become the next tag team champions at ROTL next week-- Whoa, hey…whoa…back up there, slut. Assumed? Assume NOTHING. Assume any one of the tag team playa haters might just feel like kicking multiple asses that night and have us step back. Nothing’s set in stone, just cause this jank St.Violence says so. BUT…assuming we do win…then what? Well he said he’s coming after you and Dreamer either way and that he is your teams biggest nightmare, or something along those lines… That would be HBC’s eyes rolling, and yes, that would be her laughing… I know they’ve been around the block here, so he ain’t too smart to be saying something like that! With what the Syndicate’s career, especially in the eVolution has been like, but dude, if I had an nickle for everytime some group of tough guys claimed THEY were gonna be the ones to take US down, I’d be a billionare……oh wait…..I already am! You follow me Jennybabe? That would Frost’s jaw dropping a bit… And what’s with all the security at his side anyway? If he so God damn tough, why does he go around paying a bunch of rent-a-cops, oh yeah to make him feel important and sodomize themselves in their leisure time? Could it be that they lays claims that really have no merit? I think so. Oh, and by the way, that was nice of you to do a promo while you were eating lunch, retard. I REALLY wanted to watch you stuff your fat face. Now, since St. Violence is just plain RETARDED, I think they need a history lesson, and I’m just the chicka to lay it out. HBC takes a swig of BDD’s forty, and NO, she does not douse his head in it… Alright, St. Violence…you tried to talk some shit about us being pussies for attacking Lemme explain something to you…we would have attack WHOEVER was left in the ring at that time, but since everyone in the eVo aside from our group and most of the tag division is a bunch of sub-standard midcard osers who had ALREADY been eliminated, AHEM you guys, that’s all that was left to beat on. And you want to talk shit about you guys being the eVo Franchise team? News bulliten…We’ve has done more in Evo on certain nights than you have in your career. Can you argue Frost? Frosty shrugs, but really, it can’t be argued… So talk all the shit you want about the attack. The fact is, after dominating this fed and keeping the ratings as high as it could go with this lack of overall talent, we got tired of getting no respect. You think we haven’t experienced attacks? Do a little research, loser. There’s a link on the eVo homepage, under results. Read up. I don’t expect you to know, cause while we were getting attacked nightly by various groups of thugs last year, you weren’t here…you were headlining some backyard fed with your butt buddy. So I’ll cut you some slack. But so you know, while you were jumping off house roofs onto bed mattresses, we were fighting off the mega-group known as the Twisted Reality, which, ironically, all you guys remind me off – all talk, no substance. And BEFORE that, we dealt with the REAL good ol’ boys. You want to go on about US being the good ol’ boys? Get real. BDD and I had to fight throught he original boys over a year ago…dudes like Xander, Asis, Scott Frost, Sera and Thug Roc…guys whose jocks you two couldn’t even HOLD. Ask around, people here that hate us will STILL tell you…they ruled. But once me and D showed up…they started to dissapear. Oh, I know what you are saying…That was THEN, that was THOSE guys, not us! All I’m saying is we have seen it before, sluts like you two, and we dispose of you easily each time. You’re run of the mill,…and we, well…we are living legends, and since we weren’t getting any respect, we finally had to take it. Well…St. Violence said they will not lie down while you take the fed over, and referred to themselves as the Boogie Men that are coming after you. Ha. Frostynuts, will someone PLEASE remind them that there is NO issue over them laying down and us taking over, cause in case he didn’t realize…we ALREADY have. There’s two things you can do, powdernuts, jack and shit, to quote another legend. And as for you two being the SCARY Boogie Man, ooooooh….SCARY. The Boogie Man is a PERFECT reference to you two slutbusters…a mythical character…imaginary, JUST like your so-called talents. Yay…be the Boogie Man. He never scared me before…and he never crawled out form under my bed and did me any harm. Just remember…the attack you’ve seen….you aint seen nothing yet, big shot. HBC holds up the now empty forty, upside down, then tosses it over her shoulder and laughs as she hears that sound….glass breaking…and Jimmy Eat World still sucking up the joint…
