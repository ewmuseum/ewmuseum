Heaven.

31 August 2003

Date: 2003-08-31 Sparks jumped into the air as the logs crackled in the fireplace. The flames danced to a silent rhythm, casting shadows along the polar bear skin rug on the floor in front of it. Sarah O'Halloran slept soundly, her fleece blanket pulled up to her chin. Spending her father’s last vacation day at his cabin had been fun, draining both of them after numerous snowball fights and sliding down a nearby hill. They spent the evening discussing the horse Sarah just ‘had’ to have; an idea that made her father smile. After a huge supper, Sarah's youthful energy had taken her as far as it could. She curled up on her father’s bear skin rug and fell fast asleep, content after one of the best days she’d had with him in a long time. Damon O'Halloran sat in the dark and watched his daughter as she slept. Tomorrow he’d be heading to the airport, making the short trip across the Irish sea to rejoin eVolution's British tour. Tomorrow he’d go back to being the arrogant ass kicker, the greatest thing to ever set foot inside the cell. But tonight, he was happy with being Damon O'Halloran– superdad. The break had been good to O'Halloran. He’d had time to rest and recover, something he hadnt done in quite some time. Between running from promotional appearances to wrestling overseas, O'Halloran had been swamped. This break was the best thing to happen to him in quite some time. Outside the cabin, snow gently fell from the evening sky. Damon could see the flakes fall against the window as he sipped at his tuna milkshake. He didn’t know when he’d be able to spend time with his daughter again, so he wasn’t going to squander the time he had on things like sleep. But he was only human, and sleep was inevitable. When it finally overtook the big Irishman, the last thing he saw was his little girl rub her nose with the back of her hand. She rolled onto her side, his precious treasure silhouetted in the dying fire light. Life could be good sometimes.
