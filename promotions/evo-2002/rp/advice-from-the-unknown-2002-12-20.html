Advice from the unknown

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec. 20, 2002 'The end of the old year, brings more surprises to the new. I am the High Impact champion, I defeated the only person that seemed to be undefeated. The president. Thug roC has proven to be the championship war-monger of sorts. With New-TV being full of former champions, and obviously future champions. I, defeated their leader. But in doing so. I left my stable, I left the people that I loved and cared for. I walked away from dear friends, and I backed off from the only woman I have really loved.....' says the voice of Scott Frost. The scene fades in and Frost is sitting inside of a seemingly unpopular coffee shop. Frost is sitting down looking start into a wall. A cup is resting on the table, and the steam from the heat and the Minnieapolis cool weather has seemed to be effective in the coffee shop as Frost is wearing a heavy looking black leather jacket. The High Impact championship is resting on the other side of the table. 'I sit here, alone now. Awaiting a phone call from my Katherine, from my friends. Nothing. I sit here, alone thinking. Where is this match against Asis going to take me, the new High Impact champion. I think, what tricks does Xander have up his sleeve as his match is against my love. I remember the echoing re-mark of Seara telling me she didn't want any help with Xander, that she wanted to shut him up once and for all. I sit here remembering that I was the one that single handedly took out every member of the Unlimited except one, and now think that there are possible new members. Possible, new threats to the world of wrestling.' Frost stops and looks deeply into the wall. Frost blinks. 'Than I remember that Asis is part of the Unlimited, the possible new unlimited. What do they have planned for me? What do they have planned for everyone.' Frost says before pausing again. 'If they hurt Katherine, I will destroy them.' Frost continues. A waiter in the coffee shop walks over to Frost and interrupts Frost personal insights. 'Will that be everything, sir?' The waiter asks Scott. Frost jerks a little releasing himself from his deep trance. 'I'm sorry to bother you sir, but I was wondering if it was all you need. If so I was going to go ahead and write up your bill.' Again asks the waiter. Frost looks up at him and then nods his head. 'Yeah....' Frost says in a soft a suttle voice. The waiter pulls out a tab of paper, and then writes out the bill for him and then lays it next to the High Impact championship. Suddenly the boy jerks his head. 'You're Scott Frost?!' The waiter eagerly asks. Frost smiles, and nods his head in reply. 'Wow! You must be here, because of the pay per view then?' The waiter again asks. Again Frost smiles, and nods his head. 'Well, good luck to you Mr. Frost. I got tickets for the pay per view in early November, front row! So I get to see you take out Asis.' The waiter says to Frost. Frost looks up at him. 'You want me to beat Asis?' Frost asks, sounding surprised. 'Well of course, Minnieapolis is one of the coldest states this time of year. So naturally we are going to be big fans of 'The Cold Hearted One'.' The waiter smiles. 'Well thanks, but I ain't 'The Cold Hearted One' anymore. People can call me 'Mr. Intensity'.' Frost says with a smile. The waiter snickers a little. 'What's that?' Frost asks the waiter. The waiter stops laughing to himself. 'I'm sorry. You just don't seem so intense at the moment.' The waiter says smiling. 'Yeah, you wouldn't be in an intense mood if you knew that someone might seriously harm your girlfriend.' Frost says. The waiters eyebrow raises and he looks at Frost. 'Well, actually. I would. The idea would piss me off, and I would take it all out on those who were in my way.' The waiter says sounding very confident. Frost looks back up at the waiter again, and smiles. 'You know, your right.' Frost says very defiant sounding. Frost stands up and grabs the High Impact championship and walks away. The waiter turns and raises the bill and yells at him. 'Mr. Frost?! Your bill! Your bill Mr. Frost!' The waiter tries getting his attention. The waiter slumps his head. 'Ahhh, well. It's not like he drank any of the coffee anyway.' The waiter says to himself. The scene fades to black as the waiter grabs the coffee and then takes a drink.
