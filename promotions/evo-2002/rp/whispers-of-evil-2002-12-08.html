Whispers of Evil!

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'The deadliest athlete' Morpheus Omega, back in the wrestling industry! Returning to back, from of where he left off but with whole new beginning. The circumstances have changed within reasoning for Morpheus to enter the squared circle once again. In the past he lived for the desire that burned inside him for the life, rather this sport. Wrestling was the only thing that made Morpheus adapt and ever feel right, developing the sick persona 'Infinite of Evil' and the 'God of Sleep and Dreams'. This terrifying, sick natured, personality became of what Morpheus is, has thought he was, or ever will be for that matter. If you could wake up and be someone, what you would you be with solid answering, without reasonable doubt, what would this personality be? Would you wake up without guilt and blood running cold through your veins? Would you wake up sick as Satan himself and think nothing can stop you? Unbeatable or defeat able, an unstoppable being that’s gifted with the sickest fighting ability and mind capabilities and intellect almost impossible to exist! The Deadliest athlete in the industry... The history of this man is so unknown it is beyond himself even. This is all he has ever known, while never knowing himself in return. Unpredictable, complex thought...Well Calculated machine without a self-destruct mechanism. The path of destruction continues. The scene opens with minimal lighting coming from a hanging lightbulb fixture, the bulb is dim. Morpheus dressed in all of his training gear, complete with his hands taped tight in white tape. Morpheus is wearing black kick boxer shorts with an image of a skull, smoke rising from around and the top of it, the letters 'MO' can be seen below it and also wearing Doc Martin Boots. Morpheus deaks side to side, he has a sick and determined look on his face, desire and passion seen within his cold, gray eyes. With further view about six feet away from Morpheus is a black punching bag that looks like its been beaten time after time again, with the abuse of this overly powered man. Morpheus leads in with heavy right hook smashing the bag, then push kicking the bottom of it with force. The chains rattle loud above. Morpheus backs up into a stance position, he has weight on one leg, balanced with the other holding guard. Morpheus mutters under his breathe and only loud enough to be heard 'Three straight wins with eVolution and they think they have seen anything yet? With discretion or not, I am forced to compete once again. Inner struggle over not wanting to trust Roc and trusting Shocka… He was the first alliance I formed awakening out of my coma. One half tells me, trust New-TV. Another half is like a pounding migraine, telling me to leave them from mere inches of their lives. Deprivation of their careers... Why do they have to fuck with me?' he demands. Morpheus charges and uppercuts the punching bag with force then thrusts upwards with his knee and sends the punching bag soaring. Morpheus screams, keying up into the heavy, powerful shots that were thrown. Morpheus breathes heavily with that sick look in his eyes, he continues speaking 'This is not an issue to be left alone… I demand truth, justice and further more revenge! Questions will be answered or certain people will not be left alone. Almost like they have forgotten that I was black mailed and twisted into this federation in the first place. Shocka has been my closest of friends and the worst of enemies, at different points in time. They say History repeats itself!' Morpheus speaking like he’s almost trying to convince himself of this expression. Morpheus back heel kicks into the bag and spins around thrusting with reverse elbow, both in same combination. Concrete falls below in tiny bits and pieces from the ceiling. The chain that supports the heavy bag rattles loud. Morpheus deaks around the heavy bag on guard, stopping in different stance positions without error executing different deaks, blocks, and offensive techniques. His body movements like water. Water, because it can flow, as well it can crash! Morpheus speaks with his tone of voice louder, adrenalin rushing, and physically excited staying on the balls of his feet and continuing his exercise. 'History repeating itself… Shocka will be close ally but for how long is the unknown variable. New-TV thinks they can have me at their control and defense, they have another thing coming to them! I am my own man and this plot of trickery and mind games will be their own demise. They have no defense for when I eventually snap? eVolution has no defense for when I slip over the edge and beyond! Time is slowly running out for many around me. Sean Mason, you think you are the toughest? or have something to prove against me because I fucked you up?Pressing an issue for me setting an example... Are you kidding me?' Morpheus chuckles to himself. Morpheus slowing down, he steps back and forth from one foot to another, he continues speaking “Mason has not learned every lesson taught from his teacher, from the one contender that he cannot prevail over. The man holds his Rising Star Championship proud in front of the eVo crowd, but its meaningless. The championship means nothing to them or myself. The championship gold around his waist could possibly be the added, bonus promotion that is tagged along with 110% fake bullshit. Generally when something is taken from you, it is normally responsive to become angry, irate and envious towards the person who has what you think is yours or know you once had. Depriving Mason of the championship, pealing away his pride like old cheap paint on a wall, torturing and punishing him with the pain he will indefinably inflict!' Morpheus pauses, with overload of thought inside his mind. Morpheus snaps out of his occurring lost thought. Morpheus continues speaking to himself more quickly now. Like he’s losing far sight of reality and hasn’t considered stepping back… “eVolution has the fate of federations, that have passed before me to this day, because there will be no quick escapes from what has begun. There will never be easy way out of this situation that you will all soon frown upon until the end. When I fuck the whole bloody lot of you. I have never really played well with others, I tend to snap when I don’t have something I want, but unlike anyone else. I have what I want, when I want it, and there is fuck all anyone can say about it! New-TV and Sean Mason have started to drive be back into destined places unimagined because of frightening fear. Nothing has changed, for I am still reality… Where my fantasy is your deepest, and darkest nightmare! Morpheus chuckles to himself with a crazed tone, he sounds like he has grown angry, frustrated and confused. Morpheus without the serious, cold expression changing from his face, he speaks again. “eVolution has seen whole new light, but the darkness is about to swarm and engulf them. Welcome to the end!” Morpheus rushes and spins into an uppercut lifting off his feet and the heavy bag is torn from the ceiling and fall to the ground below from the driving, powerful impact of the Infinite. The chain collapsing down with loud, uniform rattle. Concrete from the ceiling falls on Omega’s head, he gets dust in his eyes causing him to squint. The facial expression still not changing, like he has just let go of reality. Sick determination that is mentally unstable, but borderline genus! The scene fades…
