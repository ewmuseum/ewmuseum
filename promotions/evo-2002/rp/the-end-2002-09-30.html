The end?

30 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9.30.02 ‘How you doin?’ A voice utters as the covers of a hotel bed begin to move. The same voice speaks. ‘Hey, I am talking to you.’ {The covers again begin to rustle as a head now peaks out from underneath the covers. You can see that it’s Shocka and a look of shock runs through his face. On the opposite side of him stands a man in a red suit with white tie. He holds a cigar in his hand and he again speaks.} ‘We haven’t seen eachother in a while have we?’ The man speaks as Shocka rustles sitting up straight still studying him. ‘Not since you put that bullet in my chest…’ He says in a dull whimper. ‘You still haven’t forgotten that?’ He asks in a slightly sarcastic voice. ‘How could I uncle Tone you damn near ended my life…and you expect me not to remember?!’ Shocka speaks with anger in his voice. ‘Listen ever since you became the eVolution Continental champion we have been watching you seeing what you can do. We truly didn’t think that you would make it past Shodan at Digital Destruction…but it looks like you secured that deal.’ Uncle Tony speaks calmly. ‘I beat Shodan fair and square and I will prove to be the best continental champion as I defend my title the most. More than anyone! I will be defending it for the second time come Legends and I will defend against all challengers that prove worthy!’ ‘Like Python?’ ‘Hey, he looks tough don’t he?’ ‘No.’ ‘Well I bet he is…He is going to be my toughest opposition to date…’ ‘I’d like to see who you get to fight you next week then…’ {Shocka hops out of his Honda 2000 and walks into what looks to be a nice restaurant. He wears a gray Armani suit with a blue tie and carries a briefcase. When he enters he is greeted and taken to a table to be seated. Across from him sits his uncle the red dragon or Tony to those who know him well.} ‘Have a seat nephew.’ Tony Says with a smile. {Shocka sits with a forced smile on his face placing his briefcase next to him.} ‘So what’s this about?’ Shocka asks suspiciously. ‘Its about letting you back into this family deal we got goin Jonathan.’ ‘I don’t wanna hear about this crap…I thought I was out when you gave me some lead…and now that I have stumbled upon some fame you want me back in…’ ‘Listen you little prick if you don’t go in on this maybe I will shoot you again and finish the job that I already started how about that?’ ‘You just shut your damn mouth.’ ‘I am going to be in the area Wednesday night and if you don’t drop that title I will make sure that you loose your life.’ ‘What why do you want me to drop my belt?! You gambling again Tony…’ ‘How else am I going to get out of debt…and It’s a win win situation for me either you drop your belt or I collect on your life insurance policy.’
