Explanation (Part 1)

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.29.02 ~~Shocka has just beaten the Omen Brotherhood…and you may ask what he is doing now? You can see him walking to a rental car in the back of the arena he jumps in and takes off in his car. He parks in a bar’s parking lot and jumps out he heads in to check out the local Canadian nightlife. Upon entering you can see a bunch of over weight back of the wood type people just throwing back some liquor. Shocka rolls his eyes and walks to the bar he sits down and before gazing over the pretty much dead crowd. Shocka looks at the bar keep and speaks to him.~~ ‘What in the hell kind of bar is this?’ Shocka speaks not so softly. ‘Its where some of the people who don’t get out much like to come and just lounge around…’ The bar keeper speaks. ‘I don’t even see any damn cleavage…not even a women in sight!’ Shocka says in an angry voice. ‘Oh there is plenty of cleavage!’ The bar keep says pointing around. ‘Look at that, there’s some ass cleavage right there…and there…’ ~~At this Shocka simply gets up and leaves the bar he walks back out to his car to see an eVolution van creeping onto the scene. Upon seeing Shocka the van quickly turns out its headlights and pulls to the side of the road. The careless driver forgets to remove his foot from the brakes and the brake lights even flash a few times…Shocka simply walks over to the van and knocks on the window of the drivers door. The driver just ducks, the back door of the van swings open and Clay Cash jumps out followed by a cameraman. Clay rushes an intro.~~ ‘Folks! We are here with the man that put L2J through a table tonight and also beat the Omen Brotherhood!’ Clay Cash speaks excitedly. ‘Now how are you doing today sir?’ ‘A little sore…’ Shocka says. ‘Well that can be expected as you have had such a rough week…I mean first you have 3 matches in one night at Rise of the Legends where you proved to be a legend. And then your night at legends XVI where you kidnapped Seara with the help of your reluctant tag team partner.’ Clay Cash speaks. ‘So where is the question in that sentence…’ Shocka asks the man with a harsh tone. ‘Well I am not really looking for an answer just more of some comments on your week.’ Cash says in a sort of scared voice. ‘How in the hell do you think I feel about my week…its probably my greatest one in eVolution history…I mean if you look back I laid waste to everyone that stepped in my way…I was screwed out of the tag titles and that’s the only thing that has gone bad…do you realize the success…I mean 3-1 in 1 week. And then I did get to beat down L2J at the end of Legends…I got the chance to have my way with Seara…and no one can even come close to having such a successful week as me.’ Shocka looks down on Cash with a sneer and Cash begins backing away slowly. ‘So we have come here to ask you about your feelings for next week’s match…’ Cash speaks. ‘What match?’ Shocka asks. ‘Haven’t you heard yet?’ Clay speaks only further aggravating Shocka. ‘Obviously not if I asked what match!’ Shocka says in a steamed voice. ‘Well its New-TV vs. The Notorious but it’s an 8 man tag…match so the catch is you don’t have eight members…and Omega is even in question.’ ‘Anything else?’ ‘Yeah…the ring is going to be surrounded by the Unlimited and the omen brotherhood and Sovereign…’ Clay says studying Shocka’s body language. ‘What?’ Shocka questions… ‘Who in the hell booked that match was it Stormbringer?’ ‘No it wasn’t it was Somers and roC…Bobby got canned!’ Clay Cash yells. ‘Why in the hell did they do that…I mean each of the stables has a bias against the other and we all hate each other…and Sovereign who is willing to attack anyone for even looking at him.’ Shocka says in a voice of disbelief. ‘Well that’s the point…you guys will want to stay in the ring.’ Cash says. ‘So is that the concept of a lumberjack match…’ Shocka speaks very sarcastically. ‘Well yes…’ Clay speaks. ‘Shut up…’ ~~Shocka yells at the man and begins walking away again…Cash stops him by screaming.~~ ‘Don’t you have any words for the Notorious on why you took Seara?’ Cash speaks. ‘Well Yes I do…’ Shocka says turning and approaching Clay Cash…’But they can wait until I am damn well and ready to share them…HOW DO YA LIKE ME NOW!’ ~~Shocka screams before turning and walking away.~~
