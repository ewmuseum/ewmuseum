Talking to your son = not always fun

9 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-09 We open with Thug roC with his son Michael, who is now 17 years old. Not exactly the brightest crayon, but still his son. Both men are found in the movie theaters, waiting in line for some popcorn. Both are pretty upset at the huge line, not to mention that being behind a women who looks to weigh at about 300 pounds, isn’t too pleasing. I mean damn, with her in the way, you can’t see the rest of the line. Anyways, it seems that this one on one time with his son is the perfect chance for roC to talk to him. “Listen boy,” Thug roC starts off, getting Michael’s attention. “I never did get to talk to you and…well…the birds and the bees.” “Don’t worry dad, I already heard this one.” Michael responds. “Lisa told me all about it.” “Wait, your little sister told you about the birds and the bees?” Thug roC asks. Kinda confusing seeing as Lisa is only 6 years old. “Yea, makes a lot of sense. Afterall, that’s how bees got their wings right?” Michael looks to his father for confirmation. “What the hell…ok…what did your sister tell you?” “To my understanding, once upon a time, there were this little insects called E’s” Michael starts explaining, you can tell Thug roC is already confused. “They were little bugs that crawled around with a nail in their ass. Then one day, a bird came down and they did the…well…you know.” “I can’t believe this…” Thug roC said while shaking his head. “Wait, I’m not done yet.” Michael continues his story, “So after the E and the Bird had their baby, it came out like both of them. An insect with a nail, and it had wings. So they named it a Bee.” “Are you sure you belong in the 11th grade?” Thug roC asks with disbelief. “That’s what my LD teacher told me.” Michael responds in confidence. Thug roC sighs, “What does LD stand for Michael?” “Low-Down. My teacher was a big D-Lo Brown fan.” Both of them walk up a few steps as the line begins to move forward. “Want to hear how I got here? Lisa told me that one too. Although Clare added a few things to the story.” Clare being the older sister, at 16 years of age. “I can’t wait to hear this one.” Thug roC says, still in disbelief that his son believes these stories. “Well I don’t remember the whole story.” Michael says. “But it had something to do with the stork getting tired of carrying me and my big head, so he just dropped me from the sky, which explains the bump on my head.” “What bump?” “This one.” Michael then points to the top of his head. Thug roC tries not to laugh at his son. “Boy, that’s not a bump, you just have a big head.” You can see Michael’s expression doesn’t change much. I guess he didn’t understand the statement. “Listen Mike, do me a favor and don’t listen to the stories your sister tells you.” “No problem dad, I won’t even believe the one they said about you.” “They have a story about me?” You can see Thug roC getting angry, just imagine the stories they must have about him. “Yea, the one about how the last time you won a match, you had your hands on someone’s ass.” Michael obviously referring to Thug roC’s match against Jay Jameson, where he pulled his tights to win the match. “Remind me when we get home to disconnect the cable from your sisters’ rooms.” The line seems to be moving forwards pretty well. The fat lady in front of the duo, has a pager that starts beeping loudly. You see Michael turn around and yell “WATCH OUT, SHE’S BACKING UP!!!” The whole lobby laughs and the fat lady doesn’t seem to be amused. Thug roC just puts his head down in shame as the scene fades to black.
