Jason Relms in..3..2..1

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~ A camera opens in a eVo interview room where High Impact runs to the eVo camera. It seem's he has been running for a long time. Camera guy ask where Jason is High responds while breathing heavinly that he is walking behind him. High Impact quickly begins to speak as if he is rushing everything.~~~ High Impact:Twisted Reality, you guys..SUCK. Im here damn it, HIGH IMPACT here to tell Twisted Reality that they are nothing but eVo Tag trash. That's right Omega, I said you are nothing but Tag trash. And if you dont hear me how about get a hearing aid..(looks around as to see if Jason is here yet.Then begins to speak some more)Omega you are nothing but a man who has not facen the facts to reality. You believe you are this freakish man who like control's hell or some shit. Well wake up kid, this is the real world. This world that we live in is one that you will be faced with very soon as the justice system will be served to you as your taste or REALITY comes face to face with you. ~Jason Relms walks in right at the end of High Impact sentence. He looks and gives a silent smile as he goes over by High Impact and tells him like always to go an sit over in the corner.Jason Relms then looks at the camera in his smart ass smirk and speaks.~ 'The F'N Network' Jason Relms:Dumbass, do you EVER know when to shut up.Honestly, you need to know your damn positon man, behind me. It's pretty sad that you have to run two blocks because you want to say a minute worth of words in front of those sorry peons called eVo fanatics.Come here man, say someting to the fans of eVo.(High goes to get up and his stoped as Jason speaks again.)Stop Man, sit back down.(High Impact sits.)Man Im just joking.(High goes to get back up but is stoped as Jason Relms begins to speak again.)Red light..Green light..RED LIGHT. Man just shut up and let the best damn one in the business keep talking. Anyway I got something to say to the man that goes by Sky. Excuse me Woman, I mean I know you must get along perfect in this town, all these wierd freaks and shit. Like I said you got way to much testicular fortitude for a chic. I mean hell, I believe you got twice as much fortitude then my partner. But as for me though, you know what they say, the best thing that will ever grace god s green earth in every aspect is no other then your's turly, THE FN Network. 'The F'N Network' Jason Relms:Omega, take notes from High Impact right. Do me and the rest of the peons in eVo a huge favor. Come on man, you got your pen and pencil. Do you see what he is doing right now. That's right he has his mouth shut. So how about you and your manly woman do the same thing. Because when you step in the ring with Jason Relms there are no game to be played there is life. Look at me damn it, you see it. I want it BAD. I am the eVo fuckin champion. You get me, I don't care about how much you and your chic whine and mone all you want but until you beat the REAL champs I don't want to hear about how yall where cheated. How you are at the top are anything like that. You see the world you believe you in is not the one you will be even able to imagine at Legends. See peon the only hell you will be living his my boot stuck so far up your ass you want even be able to walk. OK SLAPNUTZ, you are nothing but trash. Your no price to this federation just another person, just some one else in the crowd just another example of Team Network. ~Relms walks outside as Ms.Network called him on his cell phone. He walked outside to speak to him. High Impact smiles and then runs in front the camera to begin to speak.~ High Impact:Did you hear that Omega, did you hear the words of a world class wrestler. Well now here the words of your freakin maker come Legends. Ill make Sky short one partner. You got Me? The way I see it you are all talk no action. Like that Team Twisted reality in eVo they suck totaly bad, O wait, my bad that's you guys..ha ha. High Impact:You PEONS,hold on, don't tell Jason I said that. Anyway you kids are nothing to me. You all need to be praying and eating your vitamens or whatever the hell you believe that can actualy make you achieve the impossible dream of beating High Impact. Because like they all say..I AM HALF MAN..HALF AMAZING. ~He sits back down as Jason Relms walks back in. Relms looks as High and begins to speak~ 'The F'N Network' Jason Relms:Where you talking again? High Impact:N.. 'The F'N Network' Jason Relms:Shut up. I don't care..Man Im just picken. Seriously if you didn't talk or if you want to go ahead.. High Impct:Well I did want to say somehting about the.. 'The F'N Network' Jason Relms:Alright man, that was good. Go crank the car up and turn on the damn air conditioner it's hot outside. We are meating Ms.Network at the hotel her flight from her mom's just got back...SO GO GET IN THE CAR. ~waits as high impact leaves.~ 'The F'N Network' Jason Relms:Omega, it all comes down to the final night. It all comes down to when you two wanna be Team Network's stand in the ring and look at the best thing ever to hit evolution. You herd it all the time and damnit you will always hear it. This new era my friend is a Reveloution. So keep speaking of the end. Talk about the end all you want because that might be the only correcy thing that has came out of that stupid mouth of yours. Because we know that the end of the way eVo has been going is over. The New Era of wrestling is on demand and damn it eVo can't be left behind, but you Omega, you are worhtless, you are nothing but any other peon given a decent body to wrestle the ones who were average. Well I aint average son, you never meet some one as good as me. and what..why is that..because I am THE FN NETWORK! ~Jason Relms then walks out as the camera goes to black.~
