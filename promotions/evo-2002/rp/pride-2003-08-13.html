pride

13 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-13 TIME: 11:22 p.m. LOCATION: Bronx, NY The camera fades in to a whispering wind with the aching pains of thousands clashing at the ears. The sun set hours ago, and the moonlight glistens in the air. The only things visible is the sides of the streets, and yet those are extremely dim. Gangbangers smoke and stare as the camera pans across the area, homeless men, women, and children scrounge through the trash for every last bit of food there may be, or a treasure that they may find. Little do they realize that even though they have hope, their lives will soon come to an abrupt end, as it is getting colder on the streets, and shelter is a mere want, and should be a need, but their pride gets in the way of their stupid minds. Just like the pride of all humans who wander the earth thinking the want this and want that, but what they need is blocked out, because their pride tells them, you're better than that, try and get something better, but what their pride doesn't tell them is that they won't be able to get that need. It's the same in wrestling...little cock and balls wrestlers thinking they have a shot at glory strut their stuff, but they don't realize they have no shot that the World Title...NO SHOT...but they continue to instigate and rile up opponents, only to be put flat on their backs and counted out. The camera pans left as steam rises from a sewage drain and hidden in the shadows with only half their body showing is an unknown figure...He shuns away his face, not allowing the thousands of viewers to make eye contact with him...He puts his head down and looks away...The camera moves closer, and he becomes even more uneasy...He begins to fidgit a little more into the shadows, and than as the camera approaches ten feet he sticks up his hand signaling to stop. The man puts his hand down and strays the shadow even more, leaving hardly an arm to show... 'The night is a beautiful world to live in, you know? Nothing more exciting than shadows creaping up and down walls, possibly a person or maybe a mouse, no one ever knows until they meet the begining of a shadow, but not being seen is something even more exciting. I sit and watch all of these people who wander at night and think to myself...why do they continue to live this way, when something so much better is around the corner? Something so pure as a bowl of soup in a shelter, or maybe studying to be a doctor or something instead of smoking their life away...What if? What if they made the change? It makes me laugh to think how people can be so fucking stupid...so god damn arrogant...so proud...so proud...Pride is something that every has...But only the weak use it...the weaklings are the ones who think that they'd rather be beaten to a pulp before they go for a lowly prize...everyone needs that damn Grand prize, don't they? Why though...most don't deserve it...at one time I was too proud...but I quickly realize that pride is a burden...It's a stupidity that everyone has...a stupidity that will lead them to the depths of hell...never to return...I have learned through the years that to obtain something so great means you start low...picking off people left and right...learning weaknesses...gaing ground to the top of the pack...and than...finally...finally...moving in for the kill...The slit of a throat and a corpse hitting the ground...The snap of a neck and the lifeless body laying at my feet...the power is so great...the power is so overwhelming...power...the power...it makes me...and it will soon make me again...the power of this place will bring me to life once more...and in the end...when all of you are lying at my feet...looking up at me...wondering...how? How did he do it...you all must than think to yourselves...you were too proud...and didn't see it coming...how stupid...how idiotic...how deadly it was to you...' The man fades into the darkness... 'The bloodshed starts soon...' The sound fades in the distance as the camera pans around as nothing is left on the streets as if it were an old western movie in a ghost town...something had changed here...something had happened...and now...now they were listening...
