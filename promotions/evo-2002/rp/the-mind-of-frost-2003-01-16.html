The Mind of Frost

16 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan. 16, 2003 The world is dark, the moment is faint and all that can be heard is the thoughts of one man. A man on a mission, filled with pent up anger. A personal vendetta on the line, and thoughts of killing a wanna-be killer. Scott Frost's thoughts over earth the world, and his memories echo like a thunder in the sky. His thoughts are now the voices of anyone and everyone that cares. To him, he is the center of a battle between good and evil. This is what he thinks, as the days approach: 'Do you think I will let him die in such wretched stentch? Do you think I will let him be the highlight of another mans career?' Pause. 'Do you think I will let him live?!' Pause. 'DO YOU THINK THAT HE HAS WHAT IT TAKES TO BEAT ME?!' Pause. 'But first, there is a wall to over come.' Pause. 'That wall, consists of three men. Three men who have been manipulated into my battle.' Pause. 'Jameson, my friend. Sovereign, the insane. King, the genius. Three men, who desire the same thing. I am the only one that stands out from them now. I am after a form of revenge, I am after closure. The Universal championship means nothing to me now.' Pause. 'No one. Will stand in my way.' The echoing thoughts continue, and the world listens. Now, it's your turn to listen. Will you support the neutral man, or will you be overcome by darkness?
