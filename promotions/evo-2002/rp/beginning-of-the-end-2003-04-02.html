Beginning of the end...

2 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up inside the black mitsubishi spider with Morpheus Omega sitting in the passenger side with a look of doom cast over his face, his smirk comes and fades back away... Whatever that is on his mind, probably unimagined and not even close to sane. The highway can be seen in large blur with the car accelerating quickly down the highway. The gear shifting can be heard and the scene pans to Sky Night with a disturbed look on her face as the high revving engine screams through the black night. Sky turns to Morpheus abruptly and smiles. 'What are you thinking about?' Sky Night questions him with a shallow look on her face, she checks the road and glances back at him. 'Just an eventful last few weeks... I am still awe struck myself over the biggest screw over in wrestling by faking your death, we accomplished our task of giving Jonathon the beat down of his life and now finally becoming the greatest tag team eVolution has ever seen!' Morpheus says with a wide grin... 'Almost like the pieces of this puzzle are falling together to easily' Morpheus continues... 'trouble will be encountered, but we will surely bring it all upon ourselves. Every step you take, move you make, word spoken and breath taken; it all has probable outcome. Your fate is direct result of actions taken and in the end, reality is the only thing you have left to understand...' Morpheus retorts. 'You need two things to get through in this world' Sky replies 'You need a sharp mentality and the fastest of reaction. You are always thinking ahead of everyone and theirs no chance anyone can stop you. 'Teach your own' in how to advance through this Twisted Reality! ... Is that where its at?' she questions jokingly. 'Sometimes I get this sick feeling inside of me, like no one could possibly be on the same wave length like you and I. Like sometimes, we know each other far better then we know of ourselves. Without a doubt, an even sicker duo then Bonnie and Clyde. Nothing can compare, when our minds our operating together. Fear the possibilities...' Morpheus shakes his head side to side and sounds like he has never been more serious. 'Do you honestly think eVolution will be able to compare to our skills, when we compete together again?' Sky questions in excitement and continues... 'Last time was just a tease of what will be brought to the table. I really think they are all to blindly ignorant to realize the whole truth of reality. Lies and fabrication are just one of many tricks in this fake, ignorant world we live in...' Sky rants along and has the most downright evil look sketched on her face. 'Don't forget unsanctioned or not, we still have in our possessions the rogue tag team championships. We could throw an endless loop into the tag division and take them by storm. Deadliest alliance known through out the industry and we will define all odds, defeat all limits and set new fucking standard. Stand before us and feel our unholy wrath!' Morpheus says with a snarling look on his face. Suddenly spinning the steering wheel to the right, Sky swerves to pass an approaching vehicle. Heading straight for collision, Sky down shifts and barely avoids hitting the car; she clears from its way. 'Unlike before...' Morpheus continues. 'When I said this is my world... Finally I can speak truthfully, this is our world and THEY are living in it. Time for a lot of people to wake up before they find themselves awakening too late...' Morpheus says and has look of carnal ecstasy. Switching gears and up shifting, Sky accelerates and pushes her lead foot to the floor; she drives straight on with an oncoming transport truck and the driver rampantly starts sounding his horn. Sky Night takes her eyes off the road and turns back to Morpheus. 'Live for the moment...' Sky smiles and licks her lips. 'Look silly, no hands...' she proclaims with an insane look on her face. When Morpheus finally realizes she was not going to attempt the car from colliding with the truck, he reaches out and grabs the steering wheel and pulls towards him swerving and then spins it hard back the other direction. The car on two wheels barely rubs against the truck's metal side. Morpheus pulls on the steering wheel again and straightens themselves out on the road. Sky stares through Morpheus with dark passion and desire, she has a seductive look on her face and the scene fades in the intense moment.
