Friendly Visit

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th October 2002 Last Wednesday would have been embarrassing for most other professional wrestlers. Losing to a female wrestler who you have more than twice the body weight of may be embarrassing to some guys. But not to Asis. A loss to anyone is not something that Asis is ever embarrassed about; it’s something Asis has no emotion about at all. You may try and argue that he must have felt some kind of emotion towards the loss, because of the way he destroyed Seara after the match. He’d tell you that it wasn’t emotion, it was just pure instinct that took over. It wasn’t the first time that he’d reacted that way towards Seara either; nearly two weeks ago he seriously injured her at Digital Destruction, after she failed to capture the High Impact Title. Since that night Asis has fought Seara once and lost to her, in a Triple Threat match also involving Eric Badger. She won the match with a roll up pin and after the match Asis gave her one of his patented moves, the Devil’s Dance, a Jackhammer off the top turnbuckle, through a table, leaving her injured by his hands yet again. This week at Legends he is facing Seara again and once again it is not a simple one on one contest. It is tag team match, in which Asis has to tag with Eric Badger, a man who has had choice words for Asis since arriving back in eVo and a man who Asis was fighting against less than a week ago. On the other team is of course Seara, but along with her is Jason Jameson, a man that Asis has had some of his best battles with in eVo and also a man who seems to have an ongoing rivalry with Eric Badger. But Asis is not concerned about that at the moment. In fact he’s not remotely worried about the match. Because he is in a place that is somewhere he is not all that fond of. He does not have many fears and he probably would not admit to any of us to any of his fears, but he is not scared of this place. But he is looking forward to who he is meeting there… (Asis walks through the corridors, all the walls white and gleaming. He has two big guards either side of him, although neither of them is bigger than him. There is barely enough room for the three of them to walk down the corridor shoulder to shoulder, but the Chief Prison guard insisted on them being with him the whole time. Asis looked somewhat of a sore thumb walking through the white corridors, dressed all in black, his trench coat flowing behind him. Asis walks into a room where the wall at the end of the room has a glass window in it, with a microphone built into it, and a seat on the other side. One of the guards asks Asis to sit down, and informs that the person he is visiting will be out shortly. He knows that they are thinking they should lock him up while they’ve got the chance. Put him on the other side of one of these glass windows. And maybe they are right to think that. But it’s not going to happen, not now anyway, not until Asis allows it to. A door on the other side of the glass window opens and a man walks in wearing the orange uniform of the prisoners. He has his head down looking at the floor, and does not look like the kind of murderer, rapist or serial killer you’d expect to see in a place like this. Despite appearances though, there are two guards with him, who sit him down at his chair. The whole time he is looking down at the floor.) Well hello there James. (The voice gives him an emotion that words cannot describe. It is not fear. It is more than that. It is not terror, that doesn’t even come close to portraying the feeling that Jimmy Orcs is going through. At first he thinks he must be dreaming but then he realises that he can’t be because he has not slept since being put in this dreadful place. He doesn’t deserve to be in here anyway. He’s a good man, he’d never done anything wrong before, he just got scared was all, he didn’t deserve it. And he certainly didn’t deserve to be scared like this. Forcing himself beyond his fear he looks up into the glass and staring back at him is the man that is partly responsible for his being in prison. A man who is quite honestly the scariest thing that James Orcs has ever experienced in his entire life. The look on Asis’ face is surreally calm however. He just sits smiling at Jimmy, who has no idea what to do whether to greet Asis or to shit himself. The man sitting in front of him brought him the closest to death he’d ever been and as a result put him in prison. Strange set of circumstances.) “Hello Jimmy.” Asis says again, a bit more directness in his voice again. “Hi.” Is all that Jimmy can offer in reply. “Aren’t you going to ask why I’m here?” Asis asks, his voice one of someone who is talking to an old friend he hasn’t seen in years. “After all, it’s been a while since we last spoke. I do believe the last time was at the old warehouse in Colorado.” “Yeah I guess it was.” “Well then Jimmy, ask me why I’m here. I don’t need to be here visiting you but I am, so I’d appreciate you asking me why I’ve come to waste my time with you.” “Okay then, why are you here?” Jimmy has never really known how to deal with Asis, he doesn’t think that anyone probably has. And even though there are a few good solid inches of glass between him and Asis, and the fact that there are six guards present, Jimmy feels no where near safe. “Well James dear boy, let’s review the facts shall we? Firstly, my Mother leaves me and my Father to go off with you. To start a new life with you. You broke up my family and subjected me to eight years without a Mother and then when I finally track down the man called Jimmy Orcs, the man my Mother named on the day she left me, he tells me she’s dead. You took my Mother away from me for eight fucking years, subjected me to the worst period of my life and then when I finally think everything is going to be better again, I find out she’s dead. But you didn’t tell me you were responsible did you James?” “But I wasn’t responsible! I never wanted to hurt your Mother I loved her!” (At this point several things go through Asis’ head, many different feelings, all of them bringing to his mind the mental image of throwing himself through the plate glass in front of him and finishing the job he started on Orcs six months ago. Several things go through Orc’s mind as well, many different feelings. Most of which involve the thought that he’s just pissed off a guy who will more than likely throw himself through the plate glass in front of him and finish the job he done on him six months ago. Prison may be living hell but he’d rather have that than be tortured again. Both men are surprised when Asis just sits scarily still and stares at Jimmy with those red eyes from underneath his mass of silver hair, the living demon sitting eerily still just staring at Orcs.) “You say that ever again and I will kill you. Repeat, kill you.” (Orcs doesn’t reply, he just sits in his chair trying to avoid the hard unforgiving stare of Asis, who does not say anything for a while, but just sits looking at Orcs.) “Come on man, visiting time is over in five minutes.” One of the guards informs Asis, who doesn’t break his stare away from Orcs. “Know this James. When you got locked up in prison, when you got taken away from me, all humanity died within me. The fact that I knew that I would never be able to get revenge on you, made me into something I always tried to suppress, for everyone else’s well being. Since you have been in prison, I have taken out my aggression on the same woman, trying to kill her each time I attacked her. I haven’t succeeded yet, something has been holding me back, but I hope you know that it will happen. One day shortly I will kill somebody. And I will blame you. I will come into this prison and I will blame you, it will not be on my conscience it will be on yours. So until that day comes, where I take someone else’s life in or out the ring, I hope you lie in bed at night, wondering whether it has happened yet and how soon we will once again share the same space. I’ll see you again soon Jimmy, but how soon, you won’t know. (Asis gets up and turns away from Orcs, being escorted out of the room by the two prison guards who now walk a few paces behind Asis. As Asis steps out of the prison into the night air, he smiles to himself and thinks towards next Wednesday’s Legends…)
