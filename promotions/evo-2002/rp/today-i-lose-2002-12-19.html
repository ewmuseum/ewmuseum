Today I Lose

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: The Birthday of Perfection The candles on a symbolic cake Blown out by loss and ache The frosting tastes of lime The flames lost in fabled time The wish made upon the fire Trapped within a prolonged desire The day I was suppose to live Is the day I will never forgive The smell of beer filled the stagnant air of the hotel room. A few choice pieces furniture lay overturned and dying on the plain, white tiled floor. The bathroom light was shining, as it took on a role of being the only entity living in the confines of the space. The shower was blasting away, steam rising like the mist off a boardwalk early in the morning. Outside, twilight was slowly pulling everything into night. Jason sat on the porch, staring out over the landscape of a city too lost in itself for any one person to know its true nature. The black pupils reflected a setting sun, as the 19th of December slowly died. The clock behind him clicked and clanked every second, counting away the hours before the day would end. On any other day, in any other time, he wouldn’t care. But, as he sat, celebrating his birthday with self pity and loathing, every second seemed to be cursed. Dawn dragged him into a feeling of remorse and sorrow. His body went limp and heartless as he moped around the boundaries of his prison. The walls yelled at him, singing a song of a past he could never see again. The beer helped to silence their screams. The throwing of chairs and tables gave his rage a reason to live. His existence was nothing more then a life of shame. An existence of mere victories spawned from a culture that was slowly killing itself with every fatal blow to its already sullen head. “Happy fucking birthday, Jason.” The words hung in the wind. The sound of a waterfall emitted from the edge of the balcony. A river of water flowed off, and fell violently to the empty streets below. Jason’s eyes turned to see the shower overflowing behind him. The bathroom light glistened in the clear water’s flow. The armchair and the television set still drowned as they lay there, like rocks gripping the bed of a raging rapid. A beer bottle tried to hold onto the bed, but lost its battle and was sent floating downstream with the rest. It fell off the edge, and came crashing down to the streets below. All this time, Jason never let go of his twisted enjoyment of this event. The voices of angered people rang from underneath, filling the streets with swears and other vulgar remarks. The shower still smacked the tub, and fed the river its source of life. Jason wanted to dive in, and swim away, never to see the pitiful life that he led on down this road again. Lose himself in the watercourse that flowed into eternity. Just take the water like a boat took the wind and forget about everything he left behind. A knock on the door sent him crashing back to his meager reality. “What the hell are you doing in there? Open this door right now.” A sorrowful grin shimmers through the darkened room, beer stained teeth shining in the glaze of a bathroom’s fluorescent light. A glob of spit emanates in his mouth, as he spits it to the floor. “Happy birthday.” Now my life has evaded me My wish a lie tossed into the breeze I will die without a victory Failure chocking me like an intolerable disease Today was the end of hope Lost in a river of time I will never again feel the warmth in my heart And I can still taste the lime
