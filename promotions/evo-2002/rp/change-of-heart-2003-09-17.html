Change Of Heart

17 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-17 The scene opens in the house of wrestling couple Seara and Scott Frost. The house is filled with a light seemingly happy air and voices can be heard. Laughing becomes apparent from the kitchen before two figures stroll out and into the sitting room where they throw themselves down on the seats, collapsing in a mass of worn out limbs and sweat. “I can’t believe you thought you could beat me in a race” remarked the female between breaths as she reached for the television control. Pressing the button she watched as the huge television screen came to life in a blur of colour and fast moving images. Scott began laughing as the images became clear and to his amazement he realized they had turned on to a replay of Legends, the screen filled with the faces of HBC and BDD. Seara could be heard grinding her teeth together in hatred, but when Scott turned to look at her, she flashed him a quick smile and looked quickly away. “Katherine, not again, leave your hatred for them in the ring, they are nothing to us, we don’t have to listen to a word they say. So they hold the titles, they forget that we are the Frost’s though, we are new, exciting, and fresh and we’ve got a hell of a lot more to give to the tag division than they have ever given to it” The female turned quickly to look at her husband, she smiled, but it was not a smile of joy, or sarcasm, she looked angry in a way at the tone Scott had just talked to her. Gulping he knew he done wrong and smiled back apologetically, but he knew the female was about to correct him anyway. “I grit my teeth and you have a go at me, Scott, you have no idea about some things. Yes, I do hate HBC, loathe her in many ways, and respect her, she is a strong female and in her I see many similarities to myself, and I respect her honesty. I have been thinking a lot about what she proposed and this week I will give her an answer that may shock some and make others happy, I am not sure if I am doing it for the best reasons, I am not sure if I truly understand what I think about the situation, but I know one thing. HBC has given me a lot to think about these past few days. I loathe that for so long we have had to look at those belts around their wastes, and I promise you Scott, I will do whatever it takes for you to get a belt around your waist, be it once again the Universal title, or the tag belts, you will have one around your waist sooner than you think. And soon enough, both the Universal title and the Tag titles will lie side by side on this table, and they will go to the ring together around our waists. Once we walked to that ring together as the titleholders, the Universal belt around your waist, and around mine, the eVolved belt. I held that title before anyone else in this game, I was the original eVolved champion, and since then I have never held another belt, now however…now is the time Scott. We will once again hold belts high above our heads together; the Universal title on your waist, the tag titles in our hands and eventually, the United title will be mine.” The female stopped and looked lovingly at Scott whose eyes were alight, it had been a long time since he had seen this kind of passion in his wife’s words and eyes, and he found it the greatest turn of events. For a long time now her anger overthrown all her other emotions, both in the ring and in her private life. “I don’t doubt it one little bit, but why the change Katherine, the other week you let HBC get to you, she was controlling your life, you were bringing work home with you, and at the arenas in the locker room, you always seemed miles away, to busy with the dealings of our private life. What happened to make you get it right?” Laughing the female moved closer to Scott and kissed him gently on the lips, her eyes seemed to gleam with new life, as though a shadowed veil had been lifted from her vision and she could now see what it was she looking at. “Scott, some times in order to make things right, you must first completely destroy the way things are. I did that, I let my anger take control of me, I let out something that has helped me in training, I am more now than I was a few mere weeks ago, and this week it will become more apparent to everyone. My anger is present all the time, but I have managed to find a time and a place for it, you will see in the ring, I am no longer the fighter I was Scott, I am more.” Scott smiled and laughed at his wife, she did seem a lot mellower than she had, for a moment, complete silence filled the room as the pair just stared at each other. Putting his arm around the female, Scott pulled her into him and the pair fell down to lay on the couch, for a while they talked quietly between themselves about where they planned their life going in the future, after a while they fell asleep, Seara laying across Scott who had his arms wrapped tightly around her, his fingers gently rubbing her hair and twisting it softly around his fingers.
