La Chasse Pour Un Fantôme - Partie Deux

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Translation: Hunting A Ghost - Part Two Music Companion: 'Nutshell' by Alice In Chains Fiona lay on her hotel room bed, a blank stare on her face. Her eyes had been tracing the near invisible lines on the ceiling for the better part of an hour. Music still beat through her headphones as the cd kept playing, on auto-loop. 'Nutshell' by Alice In Chains had played about five times now, and she kept listening to it over and over again. Jody thought she was in a kink over their recent failure. They had returned to Garden Grove, California, in hopes of finding what Fiona considered an 'impurity of my fractured mind'. She only uses that term when she was being completely philosophical though. And yet, they found exactly what she would learn to accept: nothing. Perhaps it was the fact that it was her first failure that was keeping her down. The recurring guitar solo began again, as Jerry Cantrell painted a canvas with his acoustic. Fiona closed her eyes again, and left them that way. She rolled over, facing the wall, and started thinking to herself. She didn't know where to check next. She had to be in Portland soon, and didn't know where to go from there. She didn't even know what she expected to find here. Her eyes squinted, as the song began again. Layne's sad voice as he recited the lyrics seemed to be affecting her. Maybe she was wrong to try this. Maybe she was wrong to think there was anything she could. It was like she said, he's a ghost. No one's heard from him, and by now no one knows who he is. She could still spot him a mile away though. Even if no one else would ever know who he was, she would always remember his face. It was etched into her memory. The way his eyes would meet hers. How safe she felt with his arms wrapped around her. The relaxing feeling of his heart beat when she'd lay her head on his chest. And thinking of these memories only made her want to find him even more. To cause him all the pain she felt inside. It had gone too long, and she knew it was time to put an end to it. He had to feel the pain. Because it was his fault she felt this way to begin with. It's all his fault, she kept telling herself. Over and over, repeating in her head. Maybe when Jody came back, she'd try and look again. And yet I fight This battle all alone No one to cry to No place to call home
