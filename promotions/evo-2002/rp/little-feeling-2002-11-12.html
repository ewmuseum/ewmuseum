Little Feeling..

12 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-12-02 'Strictly Prohibited is standing in the doorway of a dark room, with the lights fashing on and of to tell you how creepy it looks, the room lights up, then turns dark, this happens every 4 seconds. Strictly Prohibited looks around while the broken down place flashes all around. Strictly looks over at a T.V which just has lives going down it, he pops in a tape of last week.' Strictly Prohibited: I won, I beat Jinx like I said I would, but yet I left broken, my head pounded, and my pride battered! Thug Roc, oooh if you only had a clue who I was, you would think twice of what I can do to you! You see, your friend may of taken me out, but I did the damage that coast Shocka his title to Shodan, but yes Shodan didn't want the title, and he threw it away, sorta like myself..you see I didn't come here for titles, or glory, just for utter, and ruthless revange on somones soul, and whoever messes with me will get theres in the end! Strictly Prohibited:...Kai Kua, don't get any ideas. I didn't help you because I liked you, no, no! I don't like anyone, and I could care less if you won or loss at legends..you see I had to payback Mark Steele with his little attack on me at the ppv, you see he got his...he loss to a newbie like yourself..that was my payback. Kai Kua I will fight you and keep going in this tourney for a title, I'll keep leading you one place, then making your head spin to another...I am a man of mystery, but I also have a broken soul, that one man will find out how broken it is, that I came back from the dead to come and kick your ass! Strictly Prohibited: My broken soul is from this man, he turned is back on me because I left his little pack in the old days, you see..I will unmask myself one day to the man I have come to destroy..this man will be in fear laying on the mat, looking at me with fear, then..I'll point to him..and unmask myself....to show who I really am...a man with no feeling, just a broken soul, and a broken spirit! Titles are nothing to me, but you see..I'm coming for you...the game is tied...its just getting good...you know who you are, and you know that we must finish this game one day, but maybe I'll just wait, lead you on another tour, haha, to think you have no clue of what, or who I am! One day the world will see, just what is waiting for them..believe me..eVo is in for a suprise...something you will never...ever...FORGET! 'Endeavor my curse! Endeavor my past! Endeavor my broken soul!'
