Mind Over Matter

3 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-03 Happiness? No, that didn’t cover it. Ecstasy? No way, that didn’t even come close. Pure unadulterated joy? Nope, it was pointless, no matter how he thought of describing it, to himself or to others, he just couldn’t get the words right. Of course, he didn’t have to describe it to others; he didn’t talk to many people. Plenty of voices, but no faces to fit those voices. Well, not physical faces anyway, just the celestial ones in his mind. All he knew, was he was glad about what had happened, about what he’d felt four days ago. The pain, the beating with the chair, it was all worth it. Just so he could get up after it, look Xander dead in the eyes and know that he was back. That it wasn’t a mirage or hallucination this time. That Xander was back and that he wanted a fight. Of course, the fact that he’d stalked and threatened to kill his family had probably helped that. He knew that Leya Forth couldn’t get it done, in fact, he doubted that she even made an effort in the first place. But they’d made an agreement and she would now have to stick to it. At Faith, Xander was his, in any match that he wanted, whatever he could come up with. His instincts told him to just go for the most brutal option that he could, but his mind, which he’d actually been listening to recently was saying otherwise. Finding the balance between listening to his violent instincts and his maniacal, scheming mind had taken a lot of time and a lot of practice. His mind didn’t always win, but at least it got a slight bit of consideration now. It had won over Xander’s family. The impulse was to find them, bleed their bodies dry and leave the carcasses piled up in a nice place for Xander to find. But the mind had won and decided to do it a different way. Killing them may not have worked. It may have just left Xander stricken with grief and unable to function, or worse it may have driven him to suicide. Not necessarily a bad thing, he wanted Xander dead, but not like that and not for a long time yet. Yes, he was definitely going to enjoy Faith, whatever he came up with, he was going to enjoy it. Even if it was just a normal match, it wouldn’t matter, he very much doubted that the match was going to end up in the ring, with Xander staring up at the lights being counted 1… 2… 3… In fact, he was fighting the instinct to leave Xander staring face down in the cold water of some god forbidden lake, nothing in front of his eyes but the murky depths of a watery grave. Now it was just the case of fighting mind over matter…
