The Love of Man and Wife.

25 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 25, 2002 'Scottie...? Where are we?' says a sweet sounded female voice. 'This is the American Airlines arena, in Miami Florida.' The voice of Scott Frost replies with a pause. 'Didn't I tell you that I would bring you around the world? And this is just the beginning. In Detroit we only dreamed of Miami, in Miami....we dream of eventually going back to Detroit.' Frost gently speaks. The scene fades in, and Scott Frost is holding a woman in his arms and they are passionately starring into the eyes of one another. 'Scottie? Are you sure you want to do this tonight? I mean, I know it's a big occassion....but.....I, I just don't want me and you to change.' Innocently the woman says to Frost. 'Kelsey, I Love You. I married you, because I Love You. When we got married, we talked about the day that I would be called in finally. My big chance, to make a name for the Frost family. They are my friends, I trust them. We will never be different. No matter how violent I may become towards other people. I could never hurt you.' Frost tells Kelsey reassuringly. Kelsey embraces Frost, and he closes his eyes tilting his head forward next to her ear as he smiles. 'I Love You.' whispers the voice of Kelsey. Frost slowly opens his eyes and stares of into space momentarily still smiling. 'I Love You too.' whispers Frost and adding grip to his arms around her. They release from their embrace. 'Now, I want you to kick everyones butts. Okay?' asked Kelsey playfully. 'You damned straight. That Universal championship is in the bag.' replies Frost with a playful smile. Kelsey takes a short step back from Frost and she looks around and she kisses him on the lips gently, then she looks at him and smiles. 'Kelsey, I'll be in your dressing room waiting.' Kelsey tells Frost and turns around flirty like. Frost smiles as his wife begins to walk away and he opens the door to his car. 'Man. I need to tell her that I we are going to eat take out of lunch everyday.' Frost says joking to himself. Frost steps into the car, and sits down. After starting the car up, he looks behind him as he slowly makes his way out of the arena garage area. He has a front license plate that reads 'IM DA 1' and the scene slowly fades away.
