Shocka: A wild man?

6 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-06 ~The time had come… Libby Bowman had again backed Shocka into a corner at a promotional appearance… Shocka was getting make up put on with a snarl on his face he hated these things but Somers used to tell him he’d fire him if he didn’t comply. It was time…the date showed up on the bottom of the screen the location…you see on the tape don’t ask me what it is…hehe. Shocka sat across from Libby with a fake smile plastered on his face.~ ‘So we are here with SHOCKA!’ Libby shouted in an over excited tone. ‘Who in the hell are you yelling at…’ Shocka butted in. ‘It’s not like we have a studio audience you stupid bitch.’ Shocka snapped at her. ‘And he seems very excited to be here…’ Libby said still smiling. ‘Speed it up.’ Shocka said with a serious look. ‘Okay so Shocka its been years since we sat down and talked we have some catching up to do…’ Libby started but she stopped when she Shocka doing the cut throat across his neck at her. She stopped and then asked a question. ‘So…who’s ass are you going to beat after this…since that whole Greatness scam is up.’ Libby said with a laugh. ‘What did you say?’ Shocka asked not waiting for a response. ‘Greatness is a piece of shit…Scott Frost is a piece of shit…’ Shocka said getting excited now. ‘Whoa there fella.’ Libby Spoke. ‘Shut up bitch…and you can call me Shock-daddy from here on out to.’ Shocka smiled as he through up the double devil horn. ‘What the hell is that?’ Libby asked. ‘shock-A-nomics…’ Shocka said acting like he was coughing. ~Libby just sort of stared at him confused and shook her head.~ ‘OH yeah and I’m the #1 Stunna.’ Shocka said with a smile. ‘And that brings us to the point I was trying to bring up 5 minutes ago….Carl Cash claims that he is the #1 Stunna.’ Libby said. ‘The real one at least.’ She finished. ‘And you know what? That guy has been following me around for years trying to feed off of me…ever since I first said I was the #1 Stunna it spread like wild fire…and then the Big Tymers made a song about me being the #1 Stunna and that was a hit and all and then I think Cash saw the video and he thought it was really cool so he ummm… said he was the #1 Stunna too like me…I guess I am kind of his hero…’ Shocka smiled. ‘Riight.’ Libby shook her head. ‘So what are you going to do at Legends?’ Libby rolled her eyes. ‘Beat some ass..’ Shocka smiled and winked at Libby. ‘And I said you can call me Shock-daddy.’ ‘I’m not going to call you that….its retarded…’ Libby shook her head and stormed off the set. ~Vito Voss ran onto the stage and patted Shocka on the back.~ ‘Good dodge..’ Vito smiled thinking Shocka was clever. ‘Huh?’ Shocka replied. FADE.
