Untapped Legacy

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/17/03 Luther Wisdom is seen studying old tapes of The Syndicate in action. LW:'She is magnificent.....' Wisdom says in reference to HeartBreak Chick. 'You should keep your mind on business kid...' The voice from the doorway is that of Xavier Citizen. He's standing there with his arms folded wearing the new Big Daddy Dreamer shirt..... LW:'Since when....are you concerned about business X? Or do I have to remind you on Legends I'll become something you'll never be....I'll claim the legacy for myself, one you have always coveted.' Xavier laughs...... XC:'Really kid? It seems that you have more than a professional interest in a certain member of your new family? I AM the living imbodiment of The Omen Brotherhood and at the PPV you will get a chance to see why...that is if you make it...' LW:'What's that supposed to mean?' XC:'That means what happens if she decides to choose Dreamer over you?' LW:'She's already made her decision, I'm her tag team partner it's a non issue....' XC:'So why is Dreamer still collecting points then? The way I see it kid that Dreamer wants to protect Heartbreak Chick, and quite frankly he may feel you are not man enough for the job....you will be locked in a cage with the BodySnatchers and some other team....who knows I may be a member of that other team, with you playing the role of my HeartBreak Chick or as I lovingly call it my bitch!' Luther gets up, when Xavier is joined by Dolo Bentswinger. Luther prepares for the worse as Dolo smiles..... Dolo:'Not yet.....I'mwaiting for the PPV.' XC:'It's simple kid.....I want, Dreamer in a singles match at the PPV. I need you to make it happen for me.' LW:'I'm sure all you have to do is ask from what I know of Dreamer he doesn't back down from any challenge...' Dolo:'But you see he might, when he realizes someone has put a hit out on HeartBreak Chick....with specific instructions to break her back.' Luther lunges at Dolo, but is intercepted by Xavier..... XC:'Watch that temper...it'll get you hurt one day little brother.' Luther looks over to the smiling Dolo Bentswinger, trying to break away from Xavier's grip...... LW:'I thought S. already gave the order to WIN the titles at Unbreakable?' Dolo:'Ohhh that's covered.....We go where the money is in all reality.' XC:'And right now it's about you preventing considerable harm to Heartbreak Chick, by allowing me to do considerable harm to Big Daddy Dreamer.....' Luther looks at him slightly confused..... LW:'What do you mean?' XC:'You help me set up Dreamer, I'll help you keep HBC...' Luther thinks for a minute before spitting in Xavier's face..... LW:'This isn't about them, it's about Wed. and the ULWN Legacy.....' Dolo moves in to take care of Luther before Xavier waves him off.... XC:'Not now Bentswinger, that's just Luther's way of letting me know he'll think about it. I know he doesn't want to risk HBC being suspended indefinitely if you or Dreamer come to ringside.' Wisdom looks at Citizen a little shocked at that statement..... XC:'That Dan S. always keeping us on our toes with these wacky last minute decisions...LOL.' Both he and Dolo Bentswinger walk off. Luther Wisdom looks around the lockerroom and finds a bat...... LW:'Xavier!!!! This ends at Unbreakable. With The Syndicate, Crimson Sun, Playaz Inc., Team Network, and Strictly X in the tag team race they don't need me, you and I in a lumberjack Career match!' Xavier Citizen turns around...... XC:'Unfortunately kid, it's not you I want....' Citizen and Bentswinger walk off.... LW:'Then I'm going to have to convince you otherwise.' Luther Wisdom looks back at the video monitor as he sees The Syndicate Team of Heartbreak Chick and Big Daddy Dreamer celebrating another victory.
