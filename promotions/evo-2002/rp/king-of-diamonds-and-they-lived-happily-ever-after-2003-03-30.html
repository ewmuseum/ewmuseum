King of Diamonds - And They Lived Happily Ever After

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/30/03 Awaking to see the sun shining through, and Marilee’s head resting against his chest, Jason realized, maybe truly for the first time, that life was back. After all these years, waiting for the sun to come out and vanquish all his darkness, the dawn of another day came into being, and the world was no longer murky and dismal. The world was now warm and inviting. Jason eased Marilee off of his chest, and put her head to rest on the pillow. She smiled through her sleep, and readjusted herself to a comfortable position. His eyes watched her move, admiring every mundane detail of her movement. Hands gracing across the bed sheets. Hair sliding along her shoulders and her breasts. Lips caressing the air like an unnoticed lover. Jason looked down at her, and, after all he had been through, couldn’t believe she was there by his side. Outside, with clouds drifting across the sky and the desert sand glowing in the sunlight, the world, once almost ignored and irrelevant to a man like Jason, was something so much more. A sense of lost and found bliss that had just been waiting in the rain and was finally pulled back inside. Jason’s miserable heart was now optimistic once again. The void that was his soul had been filled. Jason’s life was complete again. And yet, he couldn’t remember the match he had in mere hours. One of the most important matches he was ever going to have in his professional career was inches away, and he didn’t even realize it. He just stood there, leaning against the window, soaking in all the joy that had come overnight to the desperate man searching for a way out of the tunnel. When Marilee woke up, the match was even farther away from thought then it had been before. Jason walked over to her, abandoning his newfound world of light and hope for her, who had given it all back to him. She turned over, yawning and stretching her arms to the head of the bed. When she relaxed once again, she looked up into Jason’s eyes, as her lips formed a smile. Jason sat down next to her. “Good morning.” he said, bewilderment still apart of his train of thought. She sat up, locking her lips against his, and looking back into his stare. “I really missed you.” she said, hugging him once again, resting her head against him. He caressed her hair, his fingers following the path of a strand all the way down to her lower back. His astonishment, even after all this, was still there. Still he wondered how all this could be true. Or if it was only a dream. Jason pinched himself. And he didn’t wake up. “Do you want to go get some breakfast?” she asked, looking back up at him. He nodded, and smiled. She gave him a quick kiss, and rolled out of bed. The two got dressed, throwing on the same clothes they had soaked with tears the night before. Jason went to grab his clean shirt inside his bag, and saw the two cards he had seen the day before – the queen of diamonds and the king of diamonds, side by side, like a couple eternally linked. A smile crossed his face, and he reached over to his gym bag… … the thought of the diamond giving him a thought that never crossed his mind. “Marilee?” “Yeah Jason.” “Do you still love me?” “Of course I do Jason. I always loved…” “Well, what I mean is do you still love me the way that… the way that we loved each other before… I left like that.” She turned around, and put her arms over his shoulders. “Yes, Jason. I still love you that same way.” He brought his hands up, and showed her the velvet covered holder that she had seen only a few years back. The same holder that held their future inside, the first time she saw it opening on the beach with the full moon. The holder with the engagement ring that Jason gave her. Jason opened it, and pulled the ring out, the sun catching the diamond and giving it life once again. He took hold of her hand, and placed the ring on her finger, fitting her perfectly as if she had only taken it off seconds ago. “Can you ever forgive me for what I put you through?” When he looked up, and saw the tearful stream across her cheek and the tear falling through the crevice of her lips in the shape of a joyful grin, he knew the answer before she said open her mouth. “Of course.” Another passionate kiss, shared between the king and his queen, a fountain outside throwing its essence into the wind and back down to Earth the backdrop to this moment. Jason Jameson and Marilee Ambrose, arm in arm, once again, like the ending of a fairy tale.
