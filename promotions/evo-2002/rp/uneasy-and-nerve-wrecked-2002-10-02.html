Uneasy And Nerve Wrecked...

2 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/02/02 - 2 Days Ago - Michaels is laid out on a couch with his arms behind his head and his eyes closed. Meanwhile a nerve wrecked Campbell is sitting in a chair across from Michaels looking like he's wanting to say something, but not sure if he should. After a while he finally builds up the courage. 'I have a bad feeling about this. What if something were to happen and you get beat this week. Would you be able to give him the answers this early?' he asks, but Michaels just lays there not paying any attention to him. 'Or are you even going to give him the answers when...' Campbell stops expecting a response from Michaels but instead gets nothing. No expression, no movement, nothing. 'I'm not sure what's gotten into you ever since that phone call, but you need to snap out of it and quick. S. is now gunning for you, and it almost looks like he's willing to do anything to get the answers he's looking for.' Michaels begins stirring a little as Campbell calms down a bit. 'You do know that S. has Matt Boone commentating your match this week, don't you? So now not only do you have to worry about participating in a hardcore match this week, which I know you resent doing because it's not your style. But now you're gonna have to watch out for Boone as well.' he says as he stands up from his chair and stretches a little. He then walks towards a small table near the back of the room which has a briefcase and a bunch of papers covering it. Campbell pulls out a chair at the table and takes a seat, as he begins filing through the papers spread all over the table. 'I'm also assuming you're going to react to what happened just a couple of weeks ago. And if you go through with all of this, you're gonna have more trouble on your hands than even you can handle. So I don't know if you've thought all of this through or not, or if you're just going on instinct but you should consider rethinking your plans for this week. But like everyone else, I have no clue as to what's going on in your head.' he says as he places some papers inside of the briefcase. Michaels finally moves by sitting up on the couch. He slowly turns his head towards Campbell, making him feel very uneasy as Michaels just stares at him with a blank look on his face. Michaels then stands up from the couch, grabbing a pair of keys and his sunglasses off of the coffee table in front of his. He then puts his sunglasses on and walks out of the room as Campbell finishes placing all of the papers back into the briefcase. 'I hope he knows what he's doing.' exclaimed Campbell as he stands up from the table, grabbing his coat from the back of the chair and putting it on. Grabbing the briefcase off the table, Campbell follows Michaels out of the room.
