Still in the game...

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-9-02 'Strictly Prohibited walks in the shadows, watching those who was once in the game for purposes more then power, just to show they had what it takes. SP looks around at the empty arena, with the empty old ring. The ring has the Soldout logo, hanging still as it is torn, like my soul. SP walks around, then slides into the ring, using the kendo stick to help him back up. The masked man looks around, to this once huge arena with screaming fans all over the place.' Striclty Prohibited: This place was once a place I performed, a place that many fans came to come and watch great stars. These men put there heart and souls, there blood and there sweat into this sport, but no....others with the greed of politics, and money wanted to rule this....this sport we make are lives. 'SP walks inside the ring, with his kendo stick in hand, waiting for something or someone to come out.' Strictly Prohibited: I used to wait for men to come down to this ring, and fight an innovator or the art of violence. An art that has grown over the past years. Why do we put are lifes on the line? Why do we even care? I dunno the anwsers to these questions, all I do know that the time has come for the crow to rise from its resting place, its tomb, its fortress and return...to destory what has caused koas of this sport. 'SP looks towards the banner of Soldout.' Strictly Prohibited: Why? Why was it that two great promotions fall to the greed of the monsters that rule the monarchy world of wrestling? I fell to the ground, of that pouring rainy day, the day that it came..the day I left the game. Why come back? I have come to put right the wrongs that where set forth by the evil men who thought they where god... 'SP leaves the ring, heads up the stairs to the balcony.' Strictly Prohibited: The game has awoken a sleep gaint, and now....I will avenge those lost....endeavor...endeavor...
