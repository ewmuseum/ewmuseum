Alone And Surrounded part 2

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th December 2002 Alone, always alone, but lately never without another human. The scene opens in a Chicago park, female wrestler Seara is sitting on a bench, her words are the only thing that is heard, the still sky has an eerie silence to it, and the gently falling snow adds to the atmosphere, the holiday atmosphere, somehow spookily quiet yet excitingly happy and alive. Her trench coat wrapped tight around her, the female looks as though she has been sat there perfectly still for hours. She sits alone, a small amount of snows blankets her shoulders and legs, and she stares out into the blanket of white layering the normally luscious green park. Alone, always alone, but lately never without another human being. Tradition, the first in a long time, its like the start of something new, and now is the preparation, the lead up, the part where I get ready, learn how to handle the situations. A family, a new concept, something I re-joined, and now I will spend a traditional holiday with them, but there are newer traditions, eVo, a Christmas ppv, a career that has took a new meaning in my life, it has become its own tradition, week after week I work, even when I am not in the ring, but that is natural to me, its not so much of a new tradition anymore, but I do have something I have never had, friends, and love. L2J, Jameson, you guys are to an extent a new tradition for me, I know I have not done the best for the group as of late with my attacks on Shocka and Omega, but unfortunately, I must admit, this is a part of me that will become more prominent…again…Scott Frost you are a whole new tradition to me, but a good one. Seara stops talking and wipes the snow off her trench coat, standing up she walks across to a set of swings, wipes the snow off the rubber seat and places herself down on it, then begins to move it gently back and forth, never getting up enough speed or height for her feet to move off the soft yet crisp ground. She is holding something tight, clasping it in her right hand, from a small gap in her fist; a small gold chain falls through her hand, obviously from a necklace of some sort. I didn’t come here tonight because I want to talk about Christmas, I am still not sure how to react to it, all the celebration, I realise it is a time of joy, and I will do what I can to fit in. No, what I came here for tonight was to make a challenge before we enter Nightmare Before Christmas. At Nightmare Before Christmas, I may well have a bit to say that is if I do not say it before then, but I also want something at Nightmare Before Christmas. I hope your listening S., and I hope you take this into consideration, I know that Unlimited have a tag team match to defend the titles, and I would love nothing more than to interfere and cost them those belts, but I realise this would pose a problem for things, after all, no one, not even me wants to see The Omen Brotherhood with those belts, as much as I would love to see them away from Unlimited, I do not want those in Omen Brotherhood to get them. So S., for the small price of me staying clear of the ring, which I promise only for that fight, I want Xander. I want him in a standard match at Nightmare Before Christmas, I know he will be in the tag match, but I see Xander as being stronger mentally and with a greater will power than Asis, who is in two fights, so I see why there would be no problem with Xander being in two fights. There is only one thing, if you do give me this fight S., I want you to promise me that you will keep the setting clear, just me and Xander, ringside should be clear, there should be no interference, I want Xander, and only Xander. Now S., as the Christmas spirit goes, I think the idea is for you to think about my idea, and get back to me as soon as possible…you have my number, if not Scott has it… Dropping the necklace into the snow, Seara gets to her feet lights a cigarette and sits down on the clod ground under the swing, and begins to play around with lumps of snow. The look on her face is pure innocent child’s play, but she does not seem to be enjoying what she is doing, in fact, to any one watching, they would say that she was trying to drag herself away from the behaviour she is exhibiting. Finally pulling herself to her feet, Seara grabs the gold necklace, rubs it, kisses the cold metal then places it in her pocket before walking off.
