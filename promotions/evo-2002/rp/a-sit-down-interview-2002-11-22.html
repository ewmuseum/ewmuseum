A sit down interview.

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.22.02 ~~The scene picks up inside of a room set up as the eVolution press tent. A black background sits with an evolution logo on it and a chair on both the right and left sides. In the background you can hear Libby Bowman preparing herself and doing a little bit of complaining.~~ 'Why in the hell Can't I get a big interview with someone like Sovereign or even L2J...but no I have to do this little measely interview with Shocka...' She says to herself. 'What a waste...' 'Libby don't you understand that you could be interviewing the future of this business...he could win the road to glory tournament this Sunday and be propelled to glory and then everyone will remember this little sit down you two had...' Her production manager cuts in. 'Just think of it like that!' 'Think of it like that...good idea...' ~~You can see Shocka moving onto the scene he wears a Rise of the Legends T-Shirt with a picture of him taped to the front. Libby tried not to laugh as Shocka makes his way to his chair sitting down. He looks over at Libby who is still preparing. She checks her hair and make up in a mirror and then moves to sit next to Shocka.~~ 'Hello ladies and gentlemen I am here with rising star and the man that could just pull of the win at Rise of the legends. He referes to himself as the #1 Stunna himself he is Shocka...' Libby Speaks in a faked excited voice. ~~The camera pans and zooms in on Shocka for a moment and then back to Libby.~~ 'Well first off I would like to welcome you to the evolution press tent. And wish you good luck at Rise of the Legends in all of the matches you could very well be competing in.' She says studying Shocka. 'Well you know what Libby I am glad that you had me here but I don't need the luck that you wish upon me. All I need is what I have, the power and skill...the charisma to take down anyone who steps in my way. And i will be fighting in 4 matches come Sunday. You could ask if thats going to tucker me out a bit seeing I have one more than everyone else in the tournament but i look at the tag match as a warm up...i look at it as a way to get the momentum goi...' Shocka is cut off. 'But what if you don't win...then couldn't it serve as a bruising defeat?' Libby asks. 'Why did you just cut me off? Do you think that you're better than me...what have you done in this company?' 'Interviewed some very prostegious stars and I'd like to tell you that you are one of the most entertaining of them all..' 'Yaay for me huh? I'm glad that my words entertain you...i'm glad that when they are meant to scare you...to send a message to everyone that is going to be in this tournament. YOu see i am going to use this as the chance that i have been waiting for to show everyone in this federation that i am not a push over. I beat Erin Chirstoph and everyone thought that it was a fluke. And when I beat Shodan time and time again everyone thought that i just got lucky...or it was because New-TV. Now this road to glory of mine I vow to take on my own with no help from anyone. I will rely on myself and my own mental and physical preperation to get me through this...and not the help of Thug roC or the Steele brothers...you look at me and see someone that doesn't deserve this tournament don't you?' 'Well No I don't...I think you are a very deserving man of the honor being in this tournament...' Libby says in a shocked voice. 'Well do you think that my Opponent Kai whatever the hell it is deserves it more than me?' 'No I don't...' 'Do you think that Big Daddy Dreamer deserves it more than me even though he has already held the Universal title and just let it be like nothing in EdW. See if I ever got to the Universal title I would do everything and anything in my power to make sure that it stayed with me...to make sure that it didn't go to some undeserving litle prick.' Shocka says as he looks straight at Libby. 'Well those are some strong words...' Libby tried to look away from the intensity in Shocka's face. 'Do you have any words for the other favorite in this tournament that being Scott Frost?' 'Well of course I do...Scotty boy thinks that he is something big...he had his shot at Sovereign and he let is slip away like nothing...if I had the chance I would stop at no length to beat the man and I would be the new Universal champion...he can use the excuse that other people got involved but i don't care because thats just a bunch of shit. If he wanted the title that bad he would have overcome everyone that came to the ring and made his dream into a reality. You see Frost is nothing like me, he doesn't deserve to be in this tournament...hell he doesn't even deserve to be in the same arena as me.' 'What do you mean?' Libby questions. 'What do you think I mean...he's simply not as good as me and thats a fact that everyone is going to have drilled into there minds...I will make them all realize that when they were doubting me they should have been cheering me on...when they were booing that should have been cheering. They will all want to jump on the Shocka bandwagon after I beat Frost or whoever is in the finals...but I will simply look past them, like they did me. All the superstars in the back that doubted me will be asking for a title shot but I will simply deny them...turn them away like they did me!' 'Calm down...' 'You don't tell me to CALM DOWN!' ~~Shocka jumps out of his chair and he grabs Libby by the shirt collar he plants a huge kiss on her and then looks at the camera he cocks his head and speaks slowly and quietly.~~ 'The transformation has begun.'
