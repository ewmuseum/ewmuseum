So It Has Been Written

9 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-09 I talked about all the time I wanted on television and how when I came to eVo, I was going to show the whole world what I was about. Well as the weeks go by I am doing just that. This week I wrestled yet another house show. My opponent was none other than Chad Winters, yet another victim to Final Justice. It puts me at 3-0 and already I am the number one contender for what I want. The eVolved title. The next test is the man who beat the champion, he is Matt Hunt. The match is scheduled for tomorrow night. Will I be able to beat Hunt? Of course, I will. Do you honestly think he could stop 'The Perfect Design'? I don't think so. I am the number one contender and after I destroy Hunt, it should prove to all the suits up in the office that it's time to give Justice his shot at gold. I am doing exactly what I told the whole world I was going to do. I told the whole world that I was going to take eVo by storm and destroy my opponents one by one. It wont stop their though. Oh no, it wont. I got big plans and suprises for everyone in eVo. The Postman is what they call him. Matt 'The Postman' Hunt. Well Matt, after I Hunt your ass down at Legends, I'll be sure to mail what's left to your mommy. Your nothing but a two bit piece of English trash. Let me remind you what I did to the last guy from England. Just take a look at The British Bomber. I don't think he will be back in eVo anytime soon. Hunt, just buckle in, cause at the end of tomorrow night, you'll be down in the recieving end of Final Justice. Oh, and my time is coming Evans, so just keep the title belt nice and warm for me. After Hunt, your the next to get sentenced.
