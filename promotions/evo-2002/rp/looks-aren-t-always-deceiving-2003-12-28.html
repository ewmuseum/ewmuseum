Looks Aren't Always Deceiving

28 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-28 Worth the wait? Evolution has long attempted to oppress my talent; infact I often wonder if the powers that be see it as their way of punishing the rich white boy for their less than favourable childhoods, or simply because of their blinding envy which has gripped them ever since they saw Ethan M Coles step out from shadows. Don't think I can't hear the whispers. Send him to the development camps, keep him out of the spotlight. Why? Not because he is found wanting but simply because Evolution would rather take a predictable collection of brute and no brains, rather than taking the guy who Evolution fears because of the opinion his intellect has enabled him to boast. While the likes of Johnny Charisma might be prepared to be a pin prick on Evolutions map of the future, Ethan M Coles is born and bred a winner with no aspiration other than utter domination. Its a sign of the times, when despite ploughing vast resources into research I have been unable to turn up any valuable information on my opponent this Wednesday. Does Chris Jepordy even exist? According to the roster page on the Evolution website, his life is a tale of mystery. I'm an ambitious man. What has Evolution come to when they try and push Ethan M Coles from the scene at any cost, even if it means making up names on the spot to take a place on the supposed elite roster. While I am sitting backstage preparing for my match, I fully expect Evolution agents to be scouring the crowds for some juvenile seeking a fast buck and a few seconds in the spotlight. Legends isn't about granting Ethan the opportunity he deserves, its more about silencing the crowds who are calling my name. I hear them, every time I show up at an Evolution arena; ETHAN, ETHAN, ETHAN. Their cries of desperation as they scream out for some to take Evolution on their shoulders and take it out of the mire and into the future. Though while some are facing Shocka, I am facing some rookie who is greener than grass itself. Money doesn't buy you anything in this business, it appears to just be the source of jealousy from your peers. Some might think I am picking on Charisma. Poor lad but when your nothing more than a manufactured tool, its hard to see past the slave like actions and see some residual of talent floating in the background. On Legends let me lend you a tenner, no better yet make it twenty; with that twenty I want him to go to the nearest shopping mall and purchase himself a mirror. Sooner of later the penny has to drop, there seems to be a huge margin between what you think and what you are in this world. Charisma is not the only one but he does provide the prime example of that which I despise; the ass kisser intent on hitching a ride to the top by clinging onto the coat tails of the person or persons signing the cheques. Truth is something is going to have to give sooner or later. I could buy myself the essential facelift to join the queue of clones but why? This isn't a trick question, look under the bonnet of this bentley and theres more than talk.
