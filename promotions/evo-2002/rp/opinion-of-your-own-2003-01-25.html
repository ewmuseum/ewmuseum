Opinion of your Own

25 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th January 2003 The scene barely opens, but rather seems to appear in front of the viewer. This is a promo like no other that Seara has done in her time at eVo. The female sits at the opposite side of a table from the camera, her hands in the table tapping on the wooden surface. She stares at the camera and begins to laugh. Xander, I managed to catch your little promo on the TV, see I just couldn’t help myself, I heard that Unlimited’s illustrious leader was going to do a promo on eVolution access, and I thought to myself Seara, you must watch it, you must get the opportunity to watch Xander try and make you back out of the match, make you feel worthless and pointless. Seara can hardly hold back the fits of laughter, and she stops talking to catch her breath. In all seriousness now Xander, we really should look at the truth in your little speech, I mean you do have a chance to put me through a table, but then again, it is a table match, and the idea is for one of us to be put through the table, so in all odds, right now it is a 50 50 match. Despite what you say about Frost and me, I know one thing Xander, and that is that it is none of your damned business what goes on between us. Oh and yes Xander, how about you thinking I would come and beg you for help, if I ever need your help, I would never ever beg you, I would struggle to even ask you for your help. Gritting her teeth Seara turns away from the camera. Neither of us understands why I am willing to return to you week after week, annoying you to the point of no return, because all I can think of when I see you, is how much I would love to hurt you, and I will get my opportunity, I have nothing that I can ever give you Xander, except this title. Seara looks down to the title lain out across the table in front of her, her trophy, her medal, the thing she had worked hardest for since she had joined eVolution. It was of little meaning to the female, not because she had no want for titles, but simply because she didn’t know how to feel about the honor it was to be the first to carry around a new title. If you want this title off me Xander, if you really feel you are the next person who deserves to get their hands on it, then don’t hold back, come and take it from me on Sunday night in that ring, take it from me, and show me that you are better than me. I mean surely that is what you’d love to do isn’t it? Don’t fear to be better than me Xander, don’t fear putting me through a table, and whatever you do, do not think that I fear you. You are a man to be respected Xander, I’m not sure why you should be respected, but I have heard many people say that they have great respect for you, so I am sure it is how I should feel for you, but I never was good at judging the right people, even you should know that Xander. Cutting the promo shorter than it was expected to last, Seara gets to her feet, picks up the title and with a cigarette in her hand, walks away and out the room, slamming the door shut as she makes her exit. Who knows what will happen at the PPV, who knows if Seara will go ahead with her plans, and who knows what they are? All this is waiting to become more than just words falling out of the wrestlers lips, all this is waiting to explode on Sunday night.
