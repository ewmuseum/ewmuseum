Mutiny... part three.

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens inside an unknown location but having to guess it would be the hotel bar that Morpheus was trying to find his way to earlier in the night. Morpheus can be seen turning his empty glass in his hand, listening to the ice hit off the sides of the glass, listening to the relaxing noise. Morpheus wearing his traditional, black 'dare 2 dream' t-shirt and his lower body cannot be seen under the booth. Morpheus staring off into another daze, without any worry or care in the world. Morpheus looks up suddenly and can see the smiling face of Robert Knight staring down at him with two glasses in his hand full of liquid substance. Robert Knight sits down directly across from Morpheus and slides on the glasses over to him. Robert Knight speaks with a dominant, controlling voice 'Double rum and coke like you asked for Morpheus. This is one face I’m definitely glad to see and let me say Omega. That was one very impressive performance on Legends the other night. I missed my thanksgiving dinner just to see you and Shocka compete live together once again!' Knight smiles. Morpheus looks up and before speaking, he swallows back half of his drink and sighs out of relief. Morpheus speaks 'Thanks for the drink... I’m still kind of weary of your presence. The fact you decided to miss thanksgiving dinner with the family only to come see me compete. Is there anything wrong Robert?' Morpheus with sound of concern. Robert Knight doesn’t waist any time speaking 'Nothings wrong Morpheus just thought I would catch up on the past with one of my most promising superstars from No Rules Championship Wrestling. Nothing has changed really Morpheus. I’m still drinking myself into my own misery since the fall of NrCw and your still destroying lives and careers like its always seemed you were destined to fulfill. Cheers Morpheus!' the sound of resent can almost be detected within Knights voice. Robert Knight lifts his glass to be met by Omega's glass hitting off the side of each others, 'TIIIIINNNNNNNGGGGGGGGGG!' Knight sets his glass down and Morpheus chugs the rest of his drink back not wasting a drop. Morpheus slams his glass down on the table and lets out another sigh of relief after quenching his thirst. Morpheus wipes the alcohol from the edges of his mouth and replies to Robert Knight 'NrCw has never fallen because it remains within all of our hearts that any of us have ever experienced. The federation that took me in when I awoke from a coma and had no clue on my identity as slowly, piece by piece information came to me about my true persona. You were the man who shaped and molded a lot of our careers to where they are today. NrCw will never die Knight!' Morpheus looking very content and Knight lowers his head and looks back up with lost desire and passion in his eyes. Knight speaks 'Last night at Legends watching you and Shocka compete together for the first time since NrCw brought a single tear to my eye. Without a doubt, two of my finest contenders ever to grace the NrCw ring. The hatred between you two drove both of you insane once Morpheus and I don’t doubt it could happen again' Robert Knight pauses... He continues to speak 'First off we have you Morpheus who won multiple awards after your short stay in NrCw before the downfall. You were the most improved wrestler, had the most lethal finisher and was apart of the Destroyers, the deadliest stable! Also the longest reigning No Rules champion, the most dangerous division we had to offer. On the other hand we have Shocka, 2x World champion, 2 Time IC champion, former stable and tag team champions, the man who defeated Ryan Justice in a three tier, cage match. Wrestler of the year, the most lethal finisher the previous year to when you had won it and finally the man who won heel of the year' Robert Knight pauses taking a drink from his glass... Robert continues to speak once again 'Shocka had a lot longer stay within the NrCw compared to yourself but most definitely you both accomplished so much more then the average wrestler. Only if NrCw didn’t run bankrupt you were on the road to becoming one of my next World Champions. Its a shame that NrCw is now gone, we have passed the final chapter and I’m afraid it is never to come back!' Knight puts his head down and out of view. Morpheus stares at Robert Knight with concern, he speaks 'Like I said Robert, NrCw lives on within our hearts and even though Its not something physically we are now apart of, It lives on with us always. Your concern deeply touches me Robert as well the fact of you missing thanksgiving dinner to watch Shocka and myself compete. You have nothing to be worried about with Shocka and my past involving him. I like Shocka, have risen above and beyond. Claimed title after title defeating opponent after opponent. Overall with Shocka I have no fear, but it would be the end of our careers if we were ever to step against each other again. You know what I mean Knight?' Knight looks up and nods with a smile starting to show on his face. Robert goes to speak and the toned noise of Sanitarium can be heard faintly. Morpheus fumbles through his pockets frantically and pulls out a small silver cell phone. The noise becomes louder now. Morpheus answers the phone 'What the fuck do you want?' There’s pause for a couple of minutes and muffled voices can be heard from the other end of the phone. Morpheus nods and speaks 'Yeah, and you best have damned good reasoning!' Morpheus slams down the phone on the table and he looks to be pissed off about something now, he has a smile that has now dropped into a sadistic grin. Morpheus speaks to Robert Knight with an angered tone of voice 'Excuse me, I have something to take care of before we can continue socializing!' The scene fades... To be continued...
