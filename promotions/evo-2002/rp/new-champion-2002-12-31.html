New Champion

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.31.02 ~Two words describe what we saw at Nightmare Before Christmas…Utter chaos. Shocka won the Universal title with the same move he beat L2J with making it three straight victories in a row for him. And then after the match he could have ended the careers of two men…L2J and Thug roC all in one fowl swoop. He showed everyone in the world that he was true to his word that he was not going to be denied what was rightfully his. He is now a true champion and maybe now the respect that he has wanted will come to be.~ ~Sitting in a chair with a towel draped over his head is a man. The only source of light comes from a candle burning about 5 feet away from him. The only recognizable thing to identify this man would be the Universal title sitting in his lap. He looks down at it with a sick glare before snapping his head upright and giving an evil snare to all that watch.~ ‘They said it couldn’t be done…they said it wouldn’t happen.’ He begins. ‘Everyone thought that I was the under dog that I was the one that would suffer beating. But I proved two things in that ring tonight…I proved that I could beat someone without the help of Thug roC and that I deserve everything that I have.’ He says slowly. ‘No one listened…they all doubted my ability…they all said…you’re good but you ain’t that good!’ He says in a quicker tone. ~He jumps to his feet and throws the title to the floor ripping the towel from his face.~ ‘Well to all those that have doubted me…FUCK YOU! To those that have held to what they believe I praise you but I do not need your approval. Omega and Lee we three kings be stealin the gold…At Legends our plans will come forth and everyone in the world around us will see a whole new team. And Frost? Well you will realize that my win over you at Rise of the legends was no fluke…but it was something that will happen again. You wanted a shot at my title you got it…you see me and you are the only two true men in this federation that deserve to be in the Universal title division. But I will soon prove that I am simply untouchable!’ He screams before walking out of his locker room and slamming the door shot behind him.
