Jimmy Ventin - School Days

9 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-09 The school hallways echoed with the presence of children. I myself, a child of twelve, was a face among many. Jocks and geeks. Popular and unpopular. We all found a spot together on God's latest Roman tragedy. The tragedy of little Jimmy Ventin. People I hated. People I loved. All passing by me in this jumbled up handful of teenage melodrama. All thinking the exact same thing. All wanting Jimmy Ventin to suffer for something he didn't even do. Something he couldn't help. I hated Jimmy Ventin. I envied him for all the attention he was getting. Envy is such a beautiful thing. Envy causes such a beautiful disaster. All of us, marching to the cafeteria to feed our fragile little bodies, we all plotted our actions against Jimmy Ventin. All of us with a unique plan all our own. We all heard stories about Jimmy. About how he received that disease of his. Fucking his father. Fucking his brother. Fucking his dog. Notice none of the rumors involved women. The gay disease was what they called it back then. The big gay angel of death. Never ever did they expect maybe he got it from a girlfriend or at least his mother or sister or whatever. Hell, it could have been something in his genes. They didn't no. They simply made stupid guesses. 'They' meaning our parents, of course. Ignorance was in mass quantity back then. From the day Jimmy was diagnosed and onward, he was labeled a 'faggot' and we all wanted him to die for being anti-Our Religion. We all wanted to put him into the everlasting sleep. We all wanted him to decay faster than us. We all wanted to make him suffer. We wouldn't admit it, but in reality? We were just jealous. We followed our parents like lambs to the slaughter. They wanted Jimmy, as well as his parents, dead. Or at least out of town. We could have interpreted it as 'dead' though. So, we did as they wanted. We were pawns in this fucked up chess game.. They used us as play things. We followed in protests against Jimmy Ventin. We were the ones throwing the bricks through the windows with notes strapped on telling Jimmy to get the fuck away from this town. We did what our parents wanted us to do. In a way, it's brainwashing. It can be called psychological parenting, but it's just a form of legal brainwashing. A true mind fuck. Fast forward. I now sat in a classroom. It was snowing outside and the sky gave off a white, almost foggy, look as snow glistened it's way down to the ground. I always asked for a window seat because I loved watching the snow fall. It gave a serene feeling. A calm feeling that of which I didn't feel very often back then. Pencils continued to carve their graphite traces into the paper. I bet four out of five were writing plans to kill Jimmy. I raised my hand casually above the heads of the other students. 'Can I go to the bathroom?' Nod. Take pass. Exit. The previous echoes of life were gone. It was this type of awkward silence that could drive one insane. It was as quiet as a mausoleum in the middle of the night. The halls were like a wasteland of pieces of wadded up paper and broken pencils. And the closer I got to the bathroom? The louder the 'oof's and 'ahh's got.
