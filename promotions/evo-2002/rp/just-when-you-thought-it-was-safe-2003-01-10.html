Just When you thought it was safe.....

10 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LUTHER WISDOM MULTINATIONAL CLOSES AT 7.10 PER SHARE.... Hartford, CT an old gym on the outskirts of town. It has been devoid of activity for sometime now but Evo Superstar Luther Wisdom knows this place well as it was here under Malcolm king's training that he learned the art of professional wrestling...... 'So this little saga comes full circle. The great Malcolm King has once again graced Evolution with his presence. Allegedly you readopted the guise as The ONE; the defacto top of the Evolution food chain.' Luther pauses for a minute before continuing..... 'However with my stock in Luther Wisdom Multinational falling every week. I must correct this downward slide before it could become detrimental to my future. Ironically this all started as I announced my quest along with Xavier Citizen to capture the Evolution tag team titles, a goal I was personally inches away from capturing that title if it wasn't for a piece of divine intervention.....' Luther enters the ring as he looks up and holds his hands as in prayer..... 'The hostile takeover of The Unlimited will begin at what is considered the bottom of it's food chain. ANGEL you found yourself in a situation that you couldn't have possibly anticipated the negative effects concerning your career. Your association with The Unlimited much like my association with The brotherhood is actually quite similar; both of us were thrust into a war a situation not of our making...not of our choosing. But that is where the similarities end. I at least have an understanding about what I find myself in, which is more than I can say for you....' A voice from the darkness rings out.... 'and what have you found yourself in?' Malcolm King emrges from the darkness to confront his former pupil..... LW:'Well if it isn't Malcolm King or do you prefer being called The ONE?' MK:'What is in a name....' LW:'...so that's the line you fed Xavier when you lost his in EDW?' MK:'He knew the risks and now he's dead. But you you wish to take down The Unlimited...what makes you think you are ready?' LW:'Unlike Xavier, I'm not bogged down with Unlimited history, I feel no obligation to see either it or yourself continue in this business.' MK:'So what makes you think you can defeat Angel....or that I won't stop you myself?' LW:'Because I know more than you think I do about this whole situation and the real reason for this stable war...' Malcolm climbs into the ring to stare right in the eyes of his former student..... LW:'If you could have stopped me, and Troy Pierce you would have done so. If The Unlimited with Xander and Asis could have stopped me, they would have done so. More importantly if Angel even thinks he can stop me....he is more than welcome to try. Simply put I am the next generation of old school, The Unlimited and Malcolm King is a concept that is long out dated, that has served it's purpose, and is found wanting. It's just up to me now to finish the job I started.' Malcolm King turns to walk away...... MK:'I'll save you for Angel, pray you don't get by him. I'll teach you respect in one way or another. Remember I am The Unlimited, I am The OMEN Brotherhood, and more importantly I made you....' Luther says with a sly smile..... LW:'You may have made me, but like the name on the marquee says this is Evolution Wrestling it begs that we do that, I have can the same be said for you?' Malcolm continues to walk out of the gym.... MK:'Remember to turn out the lights when you leave, from this point on you are on your own.' Malcolm shuts the door behind him..... LW:'Am I?' (to be continued....)
