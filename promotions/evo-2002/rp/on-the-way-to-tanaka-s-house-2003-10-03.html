On the way to Tanaka's house

3 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-03 Stereo: This ain't a song, for the broken hearted... Jesus Christ, D, where are we headed? Out. Now get in... Camera zooms in, and freezes on the face of HBC, a half empty forty of Olde English firmly grasped in her right hand, while she left rubs a tired eye. Unfreeze the shot, zoom over to Dreamer, and freeze on his face Stereo: I ain't gonna be just a face in the crowd, you're gonna hear my voice when I shout it out loud... Unfreeze the camera, HBC hops in, Bon Jovi dominating the sound Prick, it's frickin two thirty in the damn morning Good. Then we'll be in Santa Cruz by three fifteen Santa Cruz? What the shit for? No reason HBC ain't the kind of girl that likes being hassled during Drink Time. Unfortunately for her, Drink Time is all the time for her. Flying down Highway 280 South, looking for the 17 connector, Dreamer drinks from the bottle of Lipton Brisk that is kept strapped in the seat with him. His eyes dance all over the road Jesus Christ man, what the hell is with you? I got Banshee boy at Destruction... it's time to leave the Syndicate at home... this one's personal Cut to HBC, who looks more thrown than an abused hooker You've lost your mind, retard. Tanaka is the guy you want, not Banshee... Stereo: Like Frankie said, I did it my way... No, RETARD, Banshee is another target. But Tanaka decided to throw his name into the mix, too... Though somewhat uninterested, she still asks the question That a fact? What the hell happened? Dreamer takes another swig of his Brisk, while HBC finishes off the OE. Rolling the window down, he tosses the empty glass, listening for the shatter upon impact That's a thousand dollars right there, moron Guess how much I care? Enough that I'm going to skip it and tell you about Tanaka. It happened Sunday night, back at the hotel... Camera fades to white, before a black and white shot brings us to said hotel. Dreamer stands at a vending machine, while Tanaka approaches So what'd he say? I'm getting there, just hold on The crackpot camera sees Dreamer turn after grabbing a bag of Doritos; he bumps right into Tanaka, and the two exchange looks Why don't you watch where the hell you're going? Yeah, maybe I should; I wouldn't want to get into any trouble with one of the famous Syndicate... And what the hell is THAT supposed to mean? Oh, nothing at all. Just a little convenient how everything just seemed to go your guys' way tonight. I'd like to see you pull that off without help from the boss Is that a fact? Do you even know who the hell I am? Yeah, and I know who you think you are, too. You're Big Daddy Dreamer, but you're far from the best anymore. You and HBC are great as a team, but by yourself, you're solid mid to upper midcard, but you don't have any business in the main event Dreamer gets right into Tanaka's face, who doesn't take a step in any direction but forward So then what happened, man? Just hold on, and I'll tell you, Jesus Is that why I won tonight, Tanaka? Because I suck so bad? I thought we already covered this. You won tonight because you're one of behind the scene boys Then maybe you should go complain to Davidison. Because if that's what happened, I didn't know anything about it. If you recall, you put a bounty on me, retard Well whatever. You're getting the backlash cause you're here, so deal with it. Or is this a case of you being able to dish it out, but not being able to take it? Not at all. You're the one that approached me, slut. Don't go making excuses cause you couldn't do anything tonight but lose Cut back to Dreamer and HBC, Dreamer again drinking from the Brisk, HBC with her jaw nearly hitting the floor Yeah, it pretty much continued like that for a little bit longer, then he split That son of a bitch. Behind the scene's boys? Give me a break. You should've given him a pen and a piece of paper so he could number his excuses I wanted to, but I figured I'd save it, so that I could number all the ways I'ma kick the everloving piss out of him Sunday. Which is why we're going to Santa Cruz. Biff's there Show both from outside the car looking in, they look at each other, and nod Cause it's... my... LIFE The car speeds onto the Highway 17 offramp, and scene closes with a final shot of Highway 280, night life style
