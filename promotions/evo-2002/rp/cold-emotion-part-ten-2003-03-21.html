Cold Emotion... part ten.

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens amongst the crazy atmosphere of a casino with multiple unknown tourists and gamblers walking through the place with greedy looks on their faces and some of broken men. One man walks past with his face sunk low and a cold look bares in his eyes, he shakes his head as he walks past everyone and muttering can be heard under his breathe... 'I cant believe I spent it all, but at one point I almost doubled my life savings... What will I tell Patricia and the children?' he questions himself under his heavy feeling of guilt. The man continues to walk by and disappears through the crowd of people in the casino. The attention directions to the right where a lady screams loudly and at the top of her lungs. 'YES!!! I hit the jackpot! Weeeeeeeeeeeeeeee' she screams out of joy and excitement while coins fall rapidly from the slot machine. 'What ignorant fools...' someone states with a disgusted tone of voice, that can be recognized as the voice of Hedges The scene pans to the right, Morpheus and Hedges can be seen walking through towering tall over everyone. They both have sick looks etched on their faces as people dodge, deek and duck to stay away from the downright evil looking men. Morpheus turns slightly and acknowledges Hedges with a half raised eye brow. 'I cant believe some of the moronic people you find at these places, they think its luck of the draw when these games are designed to rob people of their money. What idiots!' Morpheus states boldly. 'Do you really think this is the best place for you to be openly roaming around right now with the high suspicion of you killing Sky Night?' Hedges questions with an odd look on his face. 'Should an innocent man have to hide?' Morpheus fires back another question... 'I will not back down from any confrontation little lone a punk scandal of Jonathon. After last legends, Shocka has signed his death wish with his own blood. Who in Satan's name does the man think he is?' he says with an angry tone of voice and spits at the ground with a displeased look on his face. 'Well then, what would you like to do now that we are in Las Vegas again?' Hedges asks and changes the subject with all curiosity of where they were going. 'That's a good question, the last time we were here was one hell of a riot... Remember our endless path of destruction that swept through here like a hurricane? Damn that Wayne Newton!' Omega curses and shakes his fist like there is some kind of resentment. 'What is it with you and Newton? You really don't like that guy and I can see why people don't like him, but you hate him with strong belief. You still never answered what you want to do tonight though...' Hedges says with a bothered look on his face. 'To be honest, I am getting really thirsty and wouldn't mind having a few drinks. You want to head over to the bar?' Morpheus questions. 'Very good call...' Hedges retorts with a widening grin on his face. Looking around, Hedges cannot find Morpheus who disappeared into the crowd by taking few steps back. Searching the crowd of people he cannot be found even standing a foot taller then most men. 'Morpheus???' Hedges screams with a confused look on his face. 'I don't have the time or patience to be dealing with this shit... Where the hell did he disappear to now?' Hedges questions himself out of rage and the scene quickly fades with him turning in a complete circle and hopelessly searching for the Infinite of evil.
