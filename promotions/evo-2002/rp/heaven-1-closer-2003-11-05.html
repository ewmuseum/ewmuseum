Heaven 1: Closer

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-05 Terlayne clasped his hands together and strolled past the monsters that lurked in every street- corner on this, the most eerie of nights. “5th Avenue,” his voice was coarse, slightly broken. He began to breathe out the night’s formless steam and reached into his pocket once again. He tried to find the compromise, attempted to find the half-way point but failed. He felt no love.... All he felt was the metal wrapped in the palm of his sweaty right hand. “What bothers me about you? What the fuck do you think bothers me about you? Maybe it’s the fact that you’re nothing but a parasite, sinking his claws into me, a leach sucking on me until all my life has been sucked out. Though it has been sucked out at your own expense, for every little bit of life I have left Terlayne will be used against you. When I sleep, you will sleep your final sleep. When you lay there at night, in your lonely, cold state and you see the shadows, when you walk the lonely street at night playing company to the petty thieves, howling as the lonely wolves howl high about the mountain tops and you a dark shadow under the tungsten street light, it will be me. I will be the end of you; I will be the death of you. You’ve been living off my success for too long now Terlayne, time for you to fuck off. Time for you to find your own cunt. Time for you to.... Forget you ever existed, for I, Terlayne, I am going to be the air you breathe at night, I am going to be the flesh you beat at, Terlayne I am going to be..... you. The bells chimed somewhere out in the not-so-distant distance between him and the nearby church. The church was small, slightly distorted and looked as if Jesus threw this one down upon the Earth with a caving at the bottom right hand corned with the initials “J.C”. Children could stop in awe of the signature and mothers would blush nearby speaking to her co-hair salon rat about the negative influences of Television and Preachers on their pure, cleansed souls. The curb-side lane seemed like a prop in a movie, it wouldn’t have surprised him if a cameraman jumped around the corner excitedly and announced to him that he was on “You’ve been framed!” The nerve of some people. The gun in his pocket weighed his pants down slightly revealing a narrow shade of light-brown. “7th,” he muffled and stopped before an unfurnished building with Gothic arches. He stared solidly through the winter’s night where he saw shadows moving around in the light. The window revealed a distorted silhouette of a tall man. Then he smiled.... Closer.... Closer to Damon.
