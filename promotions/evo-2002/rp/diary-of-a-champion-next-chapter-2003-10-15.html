Diary of a Champion: Next Chapter?

15 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-15 Well, I’m still here. Despite all the talk that Shocka waited his breath on I’m standing, even walking at a brisk pace. If it wasn’t for that damn 20lbs of Gold and Leather that’s weighing me down. Yeah, Digital Destruction has come and gone. And with two men making claims after weeks of bickering and bull shit I was the man that made good on his word. Shocka said he was going to be the one to take the title off of my shoulder. That he was going to be remembered in the annals of history as the man that ended the Hype. Well, that was a tall order, and despite even getting the rules changed at the last minute I beat his ass down. I won that match fair and square and now Shocka is home licking his wounds shocked and surprised that all the crap that was flying all over the place with a direct traceable path to his oversized mouth was all for nothing but making him look like an Ass. Now, I’m going to admit that Shocka is a talented and dangerous man. In fact I really enjoyed our match from a purely competitive level. You don’t need to go 45 minutes in the ring with a man to really test each other. Shocka’s style isn’t very subtle and he is very smash mouth and rough. I knew that going into the match, we both probably knew it wasn’t going to be some sort of 30 minute match trading holds back and forth. It was going to be a fight, 2 men with similar styles and size pounding the living crap out of each other until finally one of us wouldn’t be able to stand any longer. On that night, it was Shocka that couldn’t take it. His plan with the table backfired, his plan to turn it into Falls Count Anywhere backfired and now he’s a looser and I’m still a winner. Yet somehow I have a feeling that this isn’t the end of it all. I’d be willing to bet that Leya wasn’t to happy with what happened. Hell I’d really actually bet a million bucks lean that she’ll try and find a way to screw me over using Shocka as a pawn again. One thing about Shocka is that he has two qualities that Leya likes. First of all, he’s determined to succeed and secondly that he’s easily manipulated to doing what other people want. Shocka seems to be pushed around by a lot of people. In his past in eVo I remember him being Frost’s little running boy, he’s got his buddy there Val something or whatever and he’s ordering him around. Now Leya, telling Shocka what she wants and what he’ll get if he accomplishes it. “So what’s next?” The eternal question. Leya’s a smart woman, she’s always been a quick thinker as far back as I could remember. She had the best grades in school of the 5 of the Kimson clan. She had all the attention, she dated the Quarter back of the team. I just beat him up when he went to far. That’s why in the end this hurts so much. I tried to do what was best for Leya in my eyes and perhaps, well yeah I can admit it now as a man that I shouldn’t have gotten involved with her personal life, well not in the way that I did. I told her what I thought about Forth, that her Husband was a ticking time bomb and he couldn’t be trusted. Maybe I should have taken a lesson from my Wife who told me that when she married me that she worried every night that I was away. I was so confused. Why worry? I love my wife, always did and always will. But she said to me one night. “James, you don’t get it. You’re my man. I look at you and I see a hansom, charismatic man that any woman on the face of the planet would want to get a shot at just based on your looks. Then if they find out your rich? That you’re a celebrity? Don’t you think that’d give me cause for worry?” I never thought about that before. Hell, yeah I will admit it I had my pick of the Ladies when I was growing up. From 16 years old and on I could get any woman I wanted pretty much. I had a body, a voice and I was smooth. But cheat on my wife? She worried. But she loved me enough to trust me not to screw it all up. Maybe I should have trusted Leya, because she trusted William. Most forget him by now. Sovereign was his name in eVo, and probably the most dominant force to ever step between a set of wrestling ropes. I’ve never faced anyone so dangerous, so unpredictable and so willing to actually kill you in that ring if he had the chance to. I fought him in the MGM Grande for 40 minutes almost in a Stretcher match and then, as a result it seems like his injuries have kept him from returning since. But that doesn’t comfort me at all. Once Sovereign was gone and Leya got to see what he was, nothing more then a monster, a maniac that maybe she’d come to her senses and finally leave her psycho husband. But it strengthened their bonds and now Leya Forth returns to my life and tries to rip it to pieces. “My Plan backfired” Yeah it did, right in my face and the moment I saw her come out from the back that night with the clip board and a contract saying what her position now with the company was it was like my heart skipped like 5 beats. Yeah, taken aback, not sure what to think. Obviously she wasn’t there to help me. Sovereign was recovering, we knew that at eVo1 when he made a rather candid appearance. So is she just a welcome wagon? Will Sovereign return at any point? Will he return this week? Next Month? Show up at my apartment and try and slit my throat? How am I supposed to know for sure. Although , i think I do understand Sovereign more then a lot of people. Most who’ve wrestled him try to block it out, or at least never wrestle again. He’s had a long list of eVo stars that he’s destroyed, never to return to a wrestling ring. But I survived and I put him out. But if he does return then he’ll want a match, not just a random beat down, that would be far to easy. Sovereign may be insane, but he’s a man none the less and likes to do his business like a man. But what about Leya? Sovereign I would at least fight one on one. But I’m not going to go to blows with my own Sister, my own flesh and blood. And am I going to have to sit through opponent after opponent that she hand picks? Who’s next? What’s her plan? I know that Shocka certainly isn’t finished from his point of view. He probably wants a second crack at the plate and I’m not one to turn down challenges from worthy challengers like Shocka is. Regardless of if I beat him last week anyone can beat anyone else on any given day. That’s a proven fact. Shocka came at me for a professional reason, I beat him and now he probably feels personally humiliated, defeated and will want retribution. That being said, we stare down the barrel of a cannon known as Faith, a PPV that was over 1 year ago, the biggest show of the year for Evolution. What is up Leya’s sleeve? If only I could read minds. That’d make life a hell of a lot easier. I guess it’s just my lack of trust. If I had just trust Leya all those years ago I might be able to actually enjoy being Champion. But now I sit here knowing that in the morning I’ll get a call for some sort of appearance because the crazy bitch is trying to run me ragged. I’ve got sore ribs, 4 hours of sleep a night and matches every week. Leya’s got a plan, it’s long, thought out and probably everything’s gone according to it so far. Of all the things I’ve done in my career this was the crowning accomplishment, to be Universal Champion. To defend it at the biggest Show there is. But somehow, someway it doesn’t seem right. Something wasn’t adding up, and Leya had all the numbers up in her head. She was pulling all the strings. She was playing the game. She had control, So now what?
