Rambling? Or A Lesson? prt. 2

28 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-03 Have you ever wondered what people really thought was true bliss? Have you ever wondered what people really thought was absolute power? I look at the body and structure of the Unlimited, and I see a legend. A strong, powerful man. A man who made a difference in eVolution Wrestling. I see me. When I see true bliss, I look into the television of old recorded shows of when I was the holder of the Continental championship. I was the first ever crowned champion, I started the title races. I started the activity of evil persauna. I started it all. Tyler Davidson, he built this empire of entertainment. And through me, Daniel Somers ruined his life and now look at him. Oh wait. YOU CAN'T! Davidson is nowhere to be found, where is his fiancée? I have down all the dirty work in this company, I have started all the ruckus, the comotions, the attitude. It all started with one man, me! Unlimited.....look at them. ME! Championship title race....Look at the titles. ME! HATRED! LIFE! ME! Now look at Scott Frost, our Universal champion. What is he doing? He is getting married to our eVolved champion, Seara. He wouldn't of got as popular as he did, if he didn't sneak in the match and cost me my contract. I would be that Universal champion, and I WOULD BE WITH SEARA! ME! Everything that has happened in eVolution, is because of me. Whether I was gone, whether I was here. I started it all, but doing one simple thing. I STARTED THE MALCOLM KING SCREW JOB! Remember what I told you. Is this rambling? Is this a lesson? You make the the decision.......
