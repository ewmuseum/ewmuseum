Date: 2003-11-03 Back inside of The Syndicate locker room, Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick are just chillin like a couple of villians watching Legends on the closed screen monitors. While they appear jovial, nothing could be further from the truth. Deep inside their hearts beats the fire of rage of knowing that once again, management is trying to screw The Syndicate, and why….. because they are too damn good for this business! Not that it’s any surprise to them. For nearly 7 ½ years in the game, everyone has been trying to overthrow them from the top of the mountain. Teams couldn’t do it, management has yet to do it, because deep down, even the bosses know, The Syndicate pack the seats and are nothing but money!! However, this time, it’s a bit more personal. Management has fully out and out, LIED to The Syndicate! Tyler Davidson himself has personally LIED to The Syndicate! Obviously, this does not sit well with either one of them. It was a few months ago when Davidson himself gave his personal assurances that The Syndicate would be treated fairly because he was tired of seeing them get screwed as well, yet they are still awaiting their assurances. We cleaned up the division, we made it prestigious unlike ever before. Millions watch every week to see what they do, what HBC is going to do and what kind of chaos BDD will cause. And what do we get….the fricken shaft!!! No wonder The Syndicate are ticked. Mind you were not mad at the tag teams, they are just trying to do whatever they can to get noticed, were ticked off at that backstabbing asshole Tyler Davidson. How dare he put our careers on the line like that. This time its personal Davidson, real personal. “Wow……Davidson reveals his true colors. We do the dirty work and he tries to push us out of the business. Not like that’s going to happen anytime soon. So you decide to put our careers on the line against The Industry and some new tag team that your proclaiming the future of the business. Not likely. There has been only one true constant in the tag team world…… us. We’ve beaten the best, the brightest, the most talented, the teams who said were unbeatable, the legends, all of that, and you know where they are…… a distant memory, at best an asterisk in the pages of history. Here we are and here we shall remain, the best ever team, past, present, and future. And if don’t like that… kiss my ass!’ “Right you are! Seven and half years ago, the world watched a unique experiment unfold. The top male and female wrestler joining forces together to form a dynamic tag team. The critics called this move disastrous, now they hail it as innovative, trend setting, a move, unlike ever to be replicated in the successful fashion it has achieved. I would agree with that statement. We have had more success individually and collectively than any superstar could ever hope to achieve. However, we are still hungry. We still get up everyday, bust our tail to be the best and be the champion. I’m sorry you simpletons are jealous of us, our success, our wealth and most of all, our power. Perhaps that is what Davidson’s problem really is. He’s jealous of the power that we hold. Respected, feared, powerful, that is us. Well its real simple Davidson, you can’t be us, you could never be us, and perhaps the most dangerous thing of all, is you pissed us off.” “You know in our history, we’ve never taken any prisoners, we’re extremely ruthless and will go to no end, to make someone suffer. That person will now be you. We’ve put people in hospitals, we’ve broken families, we’ve mutilated opponents, we’ve even killed people, innocents, enemies, it never mattered to us. Oh Davidson, you could have had such an easy easy life, and you threw it away interfering in our business. The Industry and whatever team coming into Evo, man……I’d really hate to be you guys. We see you as an extension of Davidson and for that, you will be destroyed in a most violent fashion. Sleep well Davidson, the blood will be on your hands……until we come for you ourselves…..
