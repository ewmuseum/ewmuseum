Happy Families

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Asis made a big impact at Digital Destruction, having one of the biggest influences in a wrestling event in eVo that he has done so far. He retained his Rising Star Title against Jason Jameson but that was not something that most people recognised about him during the night. It was the fact that after Seara had gone through a gruelling match narrowly missing out on the High Impact Title, Asis came down and brutally attacked her, leaving her in hospital with multiple injuries. And despite Asis focusing on his mother’s murderer’s trial the week before, nothing was said of it at Digital Destruction. But since last Wednesday, nobody has seen or heard from Asis, until now… (All there is black and darkness. Nothing can be seen or heard. Until faintly a musical tune can be heard, a very tranquil sound, the kind of music you would hear from a child’s musical jewellery box. Suddenly, there in front of you, is what looks like a snow dome. But inside is a pair of porcelain figures, entwined in an ever lasting dancing circle, to a musical theme to melt even the most hardened of hearts. Just as suddenly as the dome appeared, Asis’ face can be seen behind it, distorted by the glass. He sits staring at the two figures dancing together, and listens to the music intently with his eyes closed. Suddenly he reaches out and grabs the dome, throwing it across the room and into the wall, shattering it. The two figures stop dancing and just lie on the floor. Asis walks out of the room he is in and into what appears to be a bedroom of some sort. It is decorated as if it is a couple’s bedroom, but the female of the couple has obviously had more say about the decoration of the room then the male. But the room has been trashed, ripped apart as if someone was looking for something. Asis goes and sits on the bed, amongst the covers that are strewn everywhere and starts to rock backwards and forwards, humming the same tune that had been playing out of the music dome.) I liked that song, Mummy used to play it to me when I was little to send me to sleep that did. Nothing sends me to sleep now, nothing. The only tune I hear is what I allow myself to think at night. But that does not send me to sleep. (Asis continues to rock himself on the bed, still humming the tune from the music globe over and over.) Mummy’s house was always so pretty. Always clean, always tidy, even if I made a mess though she wouldn’t shout, she’d just clean it up and give me a smile. Daddy would shout, Daddy would shout and hit, but only when Mummy went away. Mummy went away and she didn’t come back… (Again, Asis starts to rock himself, quicker and quicker and then he springs to his feet and starts to trash the room even more, throwing closets and dressers all over the place, punching walls and really going nuts shouting and screaming at the top of his lungs. He eventually settles down, walking back into the living room which has also been torn apart and sits on the floor.) I haven’t done much since Digital Destruction if you were wondering. Since I put Seara in the hospital, I guess many people have been asking many questions. None of which I want to answer now. They’ll get their answers, when I’m ready to talk about it. And yeah I retained my Rising Star Title big deal, I would have rather lost it to be honest. But it doesn’t matter, I get another chance to fight for it against the young one Matt Boone, one of the three men that my “brother” destroyed at Digital Destruction. It should be fun Matt. The prospect of seeing whether I can do worse damage to you than Sovereign has. I’m looking forward to it. But until then I can but sit in my Mother’s old house, the house she was living in with her “boyfriend” before he killed her and took her away from me. A man who is now locked away in prison for life, a torture that is not severe or permanent enough for him. Yes he got life in prison, but I was going to make him suffer. The torment I was going to prescribe him would’ve been permanent, even after death. He would be haunted forever but now that is beyond my control, there is absolutely nothing I can do to him now. All the feeling I have for him will never be expressed towards him. All the pain, hurt, suffering, hatred and rage I feel because of that one man will never truly be shown to him. Well not directly anyway. But if there is a television set in the prison he is at, he can tune into Evolution and see me express my feelings for him there…
