The last unsettled score outside of the biz

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-30 New York City United Nation’s Building Afternoon The fabled power structure of the international governing organization. The United Nations. For sixty plus years this place has been home to international discourse in a strong effort to root out and cease all conflicts from around the world. Needless to say, it has failed miserably. In fact there has been more wars since the creation of the United Nations than all the times previously in history combined. Warfare….ain’t it a bitch! Today is no different. Outside of the UN, thousands of protesters are bitching and moaning about some conflict in some backwater country, that no one gives two craps about or could pronounce it, let alone find it on the map. Ahhh, they want to be called Burma again instead of Myanmar. Who cares says the two passengers begin escorted in a stretch limo under heavy guard. They make their way through the solid gates and the checkpoints. A few nod and smile, others stand in disbelief, can’t believing of all places, they are in the UN. But they are. You thought they were just wrestlers? You should slap yourself stupid! Seven years in the game with vast outside influence, you will never comprehend the true power they hold in this world. Anyways, they head up too level 39 of the Ambassadorial wing and meet several of their counterparts in a high-level meeting. ‘Welcome back, so what do we owe the pleasure?’ BDD: Cut the crap Ambassador, you’ve been poking into our interest in Algeria again. We told you, that area your interested in, is off limits. This is your last warning. ‘But….we have a need, a mission….’ HBC: It ends now. Your little cronies pop into that area again and you’ll get them back in a bodybag, is that clear mister Ambassador. Or do I need to remind you exactly what we are capable of? ‘No……no…..you don’t. We will back off.’ BDD: No, you will cease all operations in the area immediately. Not backoff, complete and utter withdraw or we will step in personally and you will be finished politically and personally. ‘This is blackmail you know?’ HBC: So what. You think anyone gives a damn about you around here, your expendable. Besides, we already have your replacement picked out, he starts tommorrow. ‘What makes you think I’m……..(BANG!!!) HBC: What an idiot. Never learned to take a hint. Too bad the pressure got the better of him and he committed suicide. BDD: Better dump the gun C, wouldn’t want to be around when his secretary discovers him later. HBC: Indeed….The only thing left now is Evolution…Lets hit the bricks.
