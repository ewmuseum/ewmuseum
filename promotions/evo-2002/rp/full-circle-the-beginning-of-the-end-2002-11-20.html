Full Circle (The beginning of the end)

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/20/02 Malcolm King walks into Dan S. expecting to discuss the issues surounding the Referees Union. He walks in and sees Dan sitting at his desk with a smug look on his face.... MK:'Dan....you got that I just screwed Malcolm King look prior to Malcolm making the best laid plans go up in smoke Look.' DS:'It's nice to see you too Malcolm, have a seat...' Malcolm King sits down. MK:'Well the referee first would like to....' Dan throws a piece of paper to Malcolm which he looks over; as he is reading it Malcolm's face turns white as a ghost as he angrily responds.... MK:'What is THIS?!?!?!?' DS:'It's called a release Malcolm, as of today your services are no longer required in Evolution Wrestling.' MK:'This must be a joke...you can't...' DS:'But I can as per our deal to get you into Evolution. Remember the one that gave you one month to eliminate Xander, without interference from Evo. Terms of the deal were after a months time you would segue into a non wrestling capacity. With the promise to never to disrupt Evolution business...a clause you violated with your recent actions as Referee.' MK:'c'mon Dan you know xander had it.....' DS:'Regardless you are too dangerous to evo business to keep around.' Mk:'I'll resume my in-ring career then...' DS:'Malcolm you don't get it do you? So blinded by hate with Xander you forced me to buy out your Dynasty Wrestling contract....I paid main event dollars for a bitter old man, I want a return on my investment and lets face it Malcolm you are damaged goods. But on the off chance you wish to prove me wrong while you are released from Evo, your Dynasty Wrestling contract which I own by the way, will now resume.' MK:'sending me back to the competition....' DS:'No Jon Page told me that presently they don't have a creative direction for Malcolm king....you should be happy Malcolm you are now truely retired.' MK:'You sonofa....' DS:'Malcolm you got that Dan S. just screwed me and I don't know how to take it look.' MK:'You're going to pay...' DS:'I already have....too much in all actuality. Have a nice life Malcolm King...Evolution Wrestling thanks you for everything you have done.' Malcolm gets up and leave. In the hallway he bumps into Lou the referee... Lou:'So Malcolm how did the talks go?' Malcolm looks at Lou..... MK:'I think the referees should strike Lou, and I believe you should head the negotiations from now on. I feel my being there would cloud the talks based on S. and my past. Don't worry Lou.....I'll be there to coach you through the negotiations.' ELSEWHERE Xavier Citizen crawls out of the bathroom and into bed. His mouth is bloody as pools of blood litter the bathroom floor as well as the toilet. 'Gotta pull myself together, the kid still needs me. Got him in a middle of a war so I can't just up and die now.' he looks around for his phone book he opens it and looks under the entry assholes; there he sees phone numbers for Xander and Scott Frost.... 'Which one....which one...' to be continued...... OOC:Well this is something that has been in motion for awhile now. This roleplay does signify the last 'appearance' of Malcolm King in Evolution as well as all of efedding. The goal was/is to introduce Luther Wisdom as the charcter I will use exclusively after the new year. With his introduction Malcolm has served his purpose. Now phase Two; character development, begins. Once Xavier 'dies' it is my hope to have Luther Wisdom as an established and developed character combining the personalities to an extent of both King/Citizen.
