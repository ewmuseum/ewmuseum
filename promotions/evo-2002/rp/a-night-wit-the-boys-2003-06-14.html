A Night wit the boys

14 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-14 With the weather around 88 degrees and the sun shining beautifully, we open off with the newest member to the eVo family. A blue Ford Contour is seen arriving at a local household. Not to fancy of a car as you can imagine. Just a normal standard model, nothing too expensive. The big man, well over 350 pounds, Machine steps out of the driver side of the car. His attire has obviously been purchased from the Big & Tall store, way too big for your normal person. His triple XL shirt and “who knows what” size pants could fit possibly 2 or 3 average sized males in them. He wears navy blue jeans and a button up shirt with a fire design. He knocks on the door to the townhouse and it’s soon opened by the resident. Machine enters the house and automatically you can tell that you’re at a part. The red, white, and blue streamers make you think it’s already July. Instead you can figure out what kind of party this is with the banner stretching across the kitchen doorway reading; “Congratulations Graduates!” Machine is soon welcomed by two twin girls, both wearing a graduation cap but no robe. Machine embraces both of the girls with hugs and soon with envelopes. “Thanks Uncle George!” One of the girls yells as she takes the envelope. “No problem Shante, it’s just good to see you two graduate.” Machine responds. “What are you doing after college?” “We’re both going into the military.” Shante responds. “Oh…I’m sorry.” Machine says and simply walks away, leaving the twin girls standing alone. Machine enters the kitchen which was only a few feet from the entrance door. He walks in to see a few guys standing around an island in the kitchen. Brett, is a very muscular man although he is very short. And you know what’s the first thing to go when you work out? Your neck. Brett almost has no neck at all, his shoulders are so huge that his neck is almost no visible. Cedric is the guy that you can’t seem to figure out really. Maybe it’s just blind love, but whenever his wife yells, he comes. But when she’s away, nothing but smart comments come out of his mouth…wait a second…that’s every man. Adam is the last of the group, a guy that can never find a clean wifebeater. His smart comments and lack of intelligence can always bring light to the group. We find Machine grabbing a hotdog from the table which was laid out like a buffet line. He enters the group of guys as they are in the middle of a conversation. “Don’t blame me because you’re a Cowboy fan!” Cedric states. “You can count their wins last season on one hand!” “And who do you cheer for Ced?” Brett responds in near anger. “I’m a Tampa fan.” His sentence is then followed almost immediately by a bunch of groans and moans from the group. Ced takes a moment to take a sip of his drink before continuing. “Hey, I’ve been telling people about the Bucs way before they won the super bowl.” “Yea like a week before…” Adam says under his breath with a smart ring to his voice. Brett excuses himself as he goes to the bathroom. “Hold up…”Machine stops the conversation before looking directly at Adam. “Aren’t you from Ohio?” Adam immediately puts his head down. “Aren’t you a Bengal fan?” “Hey! I’m a silent Bengal fan! How did I get in this one anyways?” Adam asks, trying to get away from the argument. “Yea those Bengals are so dangerous with that one Pro Bowl wide receiver they have…” Machine says, putting the room in confusion. “Wait a minute, THEY DON’T HAVE ONE!” The room stops and laughs while Adam puts his head back down.. “Sorry A+, I had to do it.” Machine says, referring to Adam. “So where’s the 151, I’m ready to make me a drink…” The room all look around for the bottle of 151 but can’t find it. The continue to search before Cedric suddenly bolts off and runs down the steps with the bottle of 151 visible in his back pocket. Adam is soon to chase him followed by Machine while Brett is still in the bathroom. The cameraman follows Machine down the steps until the big man suddenly stops. You can hear his heavy breathing as he reaches down and holds himself up with his knees. Gasping for air, Machine waves the cameraman to go around him and he does. We go down the steps to find Adam trying to push a door to a room open. Cedric is obviously on the other side with the 151 bottle. Adam continues to push, but can’t seem to open it. Brett walks downstairs and gives Adam a confused look. “Give me a hand, Ced took the damn bottle of liquor and has locked himself in.” Adam explains to Brett. “Come on Ced, open the damn door!” Brett yells. “You don’t want to take the liquor and get me angry!” “Who are you, the Hulk?” Adam asks. “Well there’s no lock on this door, so he’s probably just leaning against it. On three we push.” “OK” “1…2…” “Wait a second…” Adam says “3!” Brett suddenly pushes Adam forwards and into the door. The door flies open with ease, breaking the door. The door comes off one of its hinges and Adam goes flying in. He trips over what seems to be a empty bottle and starts stumbling further into the room. He goes head-first into the closest where you hear a huge crash. Adam sits up only to have a box of matchbox cars fall onto him, each car hitting him one at a time. “Damn…my bad Adam.” Brett says before cracking up laughing. Machine’s fatass finally comes down the steps as he has been watching the whole thing. “You do know he left the room through the other door before you even pushed him out right?” Machine asks. Brett looks to the right to see Ced sitting and laughing at the whole situation. “I think that’s why Adam wanted to stop the count…duh!” “F*ck you! If your fatass didn’t stop halfway down the steps, you could’ve helped!” Brett yells in retaliation. “I mean you use to be skinny…what happened? “I didn’t like it.” Machine replies with a serious face, but it causes the rest of the room to laugh except for Adam who is trying to get up from the pile of toy cars. “People talk about losing 40 or 50 pounds…shit…I don’t want to wake up and look like Brittany Spears one day. I’m fat for a reason, and that reason is so people can’t Hulk-Smash me through doors like that.” The scene ends with whole crew laughing at the situation, Adam still in the closet and the broken door that Brett put him through.
