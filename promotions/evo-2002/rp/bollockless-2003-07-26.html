Bollockless

26 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-26 (Recorded shortly after this weeks Legends) AAAAAAAAAAWWWWWH His tanned face lay limp on the extreme left of his creamy white pillow, courteously given to him by the kind, indigenous people of eVolution. The room was very dimly lit and at this stage, the curtains gave the sun very little room to shine its weary rays through. His alarm clocked screamed once again as he raised his rugged fist and hit the snooze button. He reckoned he’d have a good thirteen minutes or so before the sun would force him to get up. SHREEEEEEEK A miscalculation perhaps? His legs hit the carpeted floor like two bowling balls being dropped from the pale ceiling. Looking over at his empty bottles of Jack Daniels and Tequila he saw his small note pad which someone had scribbled all over. The content of the note pad seemed very familiar however. QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS QBX LICKS BALLS Oh yeah, and he’s a big homo too. “Who violated my fucking note pad?” His memory of the night had obviously been very hazy. He’d remembered driving up to the eVolution arena, entering the eVolution arena, kicking the shit out of two Turkish fucks, leaving the eVolution arena, but what about the in betweeney bits? One of the Turkish fucks must have hit him back; he didn’t remember having a gash on his nose or even having a relatively large bandage on his nose. “Good, you’re awake,” she sighed with relief. “Daniel Somers has been calling all night; he wants your ass on a platter.” Terlayne shook his weary head, his hair lying like a dead animal on his pillow he perplexed his arms and sat up on his bed. She had obviously been very concerned for his well being. He reached over to his small cupboard and squeezed two alka selzer’s from their packaging into a glass of water. “I won right?” The answer lay clearly in her bemused face and shaking head. She rested her right hand upon his weary shoulder. “No, I did win. We fought in my locker room… I kicked him in the bollocks and killed the fucker.” He said confidently. “No… That was a manikin.” She replied hesitantly. “Oh that would explain him not fighting back and….the hole in his bollocks.”
