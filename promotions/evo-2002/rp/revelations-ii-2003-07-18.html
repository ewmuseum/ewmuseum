Revelations II...

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-18 Damon O'Halloran stood in the alley behind the hotel, watching a rat gnaw away at a bag of garbage which sat in from of a dumpster. Last Sunday had been a busy one for the hotel, what with the eVolution in town. Pay Per View events guaranteed big business for whatever city hosted them. Big business generated great excess, which ended up as waste behind the hotel. O'Halloran was back here looking to make some more waste. Damon walked to the side of the dumpster. He picked up the cinder block which was keeping the dumpster from rolling against the hotel. He smiled as he took aim, and as the rat pulled its head out of the bag, the cinder block crashed down on its body with a wet crunch. “My ears are still ringing from last night,” said Damon as he shook his head. “Two big matches in one night; the Chamber of Survival sure turned out to be hell, and I'm still walking tall.” The rat writhed in agony, trying to pull its crushed lower body down the alley. Damon walked beside it. “At a New Dawn, I did Malcolm King a favour. I took it easy on him. I had carte blanche to do whatever I wanted to him, and I decided that I’d let him keep breathing. At least for another day. If I had only one match to worry about last night, King wouldn’t be worrying about fighting ever again. But since I had to pace myself, I decided that I’d just beat him.” The vermin’s shrill squeaks became quieter, its movement slowed. O'Halloran stepped in front of it. “Last night was sweet. Choirs sang our praise, fans chanted with glee... and the 'Act of God' took out his frustrations on three victims, giving the Cell Title a warm welcome to the game. Welcome back, monkey nuts. All three men got a beating, but that’s what happens when you break Rule Number One: Don’t Mess With Damon.” He looked down at the suffering animal, and with a slight smirk, stomped the rat’s skull under the heel of his boot. “Dark days ahead for the eVolution, and there’s just two things they can do about it... Nothing and Like It.”
