Chronicles of the Mask: Part Two

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-30 Atop of a roof during one of the darkest nights in Toronto, Canada. The cool breeze of the wind, brings a very calm atmosphere to surround you in a state of near extacy. The starry night sky helps with the atmospheric euphoria. The faint sounds of cars honking, and the chattering of pedestrians below lets you know that the rooftop is in the middle of the city. Surrounding buildings are lit up in the distance and you can see the flight of airplanes in the distance as well. Then the sounds of foot steps is heard, and then Greatness walks into the scene. Instead of walking towards the camera though, he walks away and walks towards the edge of the building. 'It's euphoric almost, wouldn't you say?' Greatness asks the camera in a faint voice. The camera man, walks up closer to Greatness to get a better veiw of what he is doing and saying. 'Sometimes I come to places like this and look down on the people who make this euphoric atmosphere a dieing cause. These people have no respect for people like me. I am a person of success, and these people don't care. These people want to see people like James Kimson, and Scott Frost thanking everyone for supporting them throughout their success. But they are just blind. Too blind to see the real support comes from within, these people don't like me because my truth is like any truth, it hurts. They don't want to admit it. They want to go on about their lives, and accepting people these false prophets of stardom as their heros.' Greatness calmly says. A huge honk is heard below and it catches the attention of Greatness. 'Look at that. Below, there is a person walking infront of a car to get to his destination. And someone about hit him, but as I watch him finish his journey across the street I can feel the same feeling I have inside, burning inside of him. The feeling of success, whatever life throws in the way. You are the one that makes things possible, you are the person that makes your life a success. But the person in the car, is upset at the passing pedestrian because he is too blind to see the real objective that was at hand....the passing pedestrian was just trying to be successful.' Greatness claims. Greatness looks towards the camera, and the light shines brightly into his face and although his eyes are shrouded by red contacts they some how seem familiar. 'Do you understand yet?' Greatness asks. 'Do you people understand what I am saying, at all?' he asks again. Greatness turns around and points to himself. 'People like: Myself, Morpheus Omega, Thug roC, Sky Night, Xander, we are the good guys. We are the people who try our hardest to be successful in our lives. To make something of ourselves. We don't care what people think about us, we do what we have to get noticed. To allow people to see that we are the greatest of all time, because we are successful. Look at Omega and Sky Night, they are the tag team champions. Thug roC is a former High Impact champion. Xander, is our new Unviersal champion. Do you see them thanking the fans? No. You see them reeling in their life, and in the success that they achieved. Someday I wish I will obtain success like that. Someday, I will obtain it. I am the Marquee of Greatness, and Greatness has been born into eVolution. I will take the word Greatness, and truely evolve the pure meaning.' Greatness calmly expresses. Greatness walks away leaving the scene. 'But until then, I will be the one tattooing my words in the heads of everyone.' he says walking away. 'Greatness always prevails.'
