I'm a Slave!

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-14 Barry Hales had a dilemma. As the owner of Gold's Gym, he had certain obligations that he had to fulfill. One of them was to make sure that the gym's patrons were happy: give them clean towels, make sure there wasn't anything growing in the shower stall walls. Occasionally, when someone was interrupting several people's workouts, Barry would approach the disruptive person and either give them a warning or kick them out. But what do you do if the guy being disruptive is as big as a house? What's worse, what do you do when you know the guy? Standing behind the counter, Barry looked across the gym. Damon O'Halloran had come in about an hour ago. Usually, Damon would stop and shoot the breeze with Barry before working out. Today he came in and went straight to the weights, still wearing his street clothes. As he passed by, Barry could smell the scent of stale whiskey. That immediately told Barry that something was very wrong with O'Halloran. But now, things were starting to get out of hand. Damon was leaving weights all over the place, stealing towels from people, and openly cussing as he worked. It sounded like if Damon couldn't quite lift the weight, he'd 'swear' it up. Barry stepped out from behind the counter as Damon O'Halloran walked over to the water fountain. O'Halloran would take a mouthful of water, gargle with it, and spit all over the floor. 'Hey Damon,' Barry said. 'What's going on?' O'Halloran turned and walked back to the bench. He sat down and picked up the huge dumbbell off the floor. Barry stood stunned, before following him to the bench. 'Hey Damon, don't blow me off in my own gym.' Damon lay down on the bench, the pair of dumbbells resting on his chest. There was at least a couple days' worth of stubble on his face as well. Puffy bags hung under O'Halloran's eyes. 'You look like crap, Champ,' Barry said. The dumbbells crashed to the floor as the 'Act of God' got to his feet. 'What did you call me?' O'Halloran asked. 'I, uh, I just called you Champ,' Barry said nervously. 'I mean, you're eVolution's Cell Champion, right?' Damon stepped close. 'Of course I'm the Champ! What the Hell kind of stupid question is that? Who does the Cameron Griffin have that can come close to beating me?' 'Terlayne Sterling did it once already, Sean Sterling also looks the part.' chirped a voice from behind. With an incredulous look on his face, O'Halloran turned to the speaker. A young man wearing a Gold's Gym T-shirt was mopping up the water Damon had spit on the floor. 'Excuse me, cunt? Did you just say something,' Damon read the nametag on the young man's shirt,' Lenny?' 'All I said is that Sean Sterling is running roughshot through the Cell Division,' said Lenny as he mopped the floor. 'So technically, he's done more than come close to you. He's knocking on your doorstep.' Damon snatched the mop from Lenny's hands and snapped it over Lenny's head. The young man fell to the floor, clutching his head in pain. Damon dropped the mop segments on the floor. 'Not one of Cameron Griffin's 'proteges' will ever beat me, especially not Sean Sterling,' O'Halloran snarled at Lenny. 'You got that? Nobody beat's the Act of God!' 'Dammit Damon!' Barry snapped as he knelt down to check on his employee. 'Get the Hell out of here or I'll call the cops!' His massive shadow falling over both men, O'Halloran's stare burned a hole into each of them. Damon was obviously out of control, Barry thought. Moreso than usual. The Gold's Gym employees remained on the floor, waiting to see what O'Halloran would do next. Surprisingly, he turned suddenly and walked out the door. 'What was THAT all about?' Lenny asked as he held his head. 'I don't know,' Barry said. 'And I don't want to know.'
