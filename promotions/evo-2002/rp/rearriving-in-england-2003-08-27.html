Rearriving in England

27 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-27 It’s been months since Brianna has had the chance to get in the ring. Ever since the closing of PCW she had had trouble finding a new home. So instead of keeping with the strenuous schedule of working out and practicing moves, Brianna mainly stayed at home in her lab, concocting gadgets as usual. Being knocked off track ultimately gave her the inevitable ring rust which could show it’s face against Yoshii Tanaka at the upcoming Legends….but only time can tell. The scene opens up on an airplane heading for London, England. After last weeks Legends show in England, Brianna had flown back to her home to share the good news personally with Milo (her roommate and long time friend), and is now on her way back for her big night and debut in Evolution. “Would you like something to drink ma’am?” the stewardess asked politely as she flashed her cheesy, forced smile. Brianna looked at the cart she was pushing, not really finding anything to her liking. “Could I just have some water please?” The stewardess pulled a nice cold bottle of water from the depths of the ice and handed it to Brianna who had lucked off and gotten her own little space by the window, with no one sitting next to her. She grabbed the bottle of water and instantly popped it open as her mouth was growing dry with anticipation. Just a few more hours and I show Evolution what I’m made of…..don’t really know much about Yoshii except that he’s fast and has a killer arsenal of moves that I’m going to have to watch out for. I’m pretty sure I can take him, but what happens if I’m wrong? What happens if I screw up inevitably making my debut a farce. Will I be given another chance? Davidson said I had a couple of weeks to prove my ability and value to the company, so does that secretly mean this first match is do or die? This thought causes her mouth to dry up yet again and she pulls the water bottle back to her lips and takes a few more big gulps as her mind continues to race. I can’t think like that though….as easy as it is to think the worse I have to be an optimist about this. I know that I have the skills and overall character to be near the best in Evolution. I have the drive, determination, and the experience to boot! But in saying that I have to admit eVo is a whole new ball field with a different variation of wrestlers then PCW. So I guess that leaves me in a bind. I’d like to think I’m good enough to take on new challenges and quickly make a change, but that might not happen come tonight. Pushing all that aside I will just be happy to be able to step in the ring again. God knows it’s been months since I’ve had that wonderful sensation and thrill that wrestling gives me….Instead of worrying I’m just going to let fate and destiny take it’s course. Loosely translated I’m gonna have to kick some major ass tonight! Thinking that last bit forces a smile on Brianna’s face as she stares ahead at the grayish chair in front of her. She finishes up the water bottle and bites her lip in thought as the plane lands. The scene fades to black.
