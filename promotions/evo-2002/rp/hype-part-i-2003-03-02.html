Hype Part I

2 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 2nd, 2003 The fading in sound of footsteps falling on a long hallway is heard as the camera slowly fades into view. A man walks towards the camera from a great distance away, it’s hard to tell whom it is yet. But the hallway itself is well decorated, many paintings, and fine furniture dress the walls. The floor is hardwood, the walls painted a warm shade of red and blue, lamps on the side give the room it’s light and when you turn your head to notice the nearest painting, you discover it’s a photograph. James Kimson, in a wrestling ring looking out to the sea of fans. It’s a classic photo, one that you probably have seen before on promo posters, or on Video Cassette’s. You turn your attention back to the man walking, and you are now sure that it’s Kimson himself. His voice parts the conversation between the floor and his footsteps. It had been a long time since I was in a wrestling ring. It was probably one of the best matches that I can recall, to be in the ring with the likes of Sovereign and Jameson there was no way to have a poor match. But all and all nothing was resolved between me and Sovereign. Jameson has the chance not to let it all go and leave the two of us alone. Alone to finish our business. Because at Tainted Love, it only really began. No words were spoken, only the sound of our fists connecting with each other’s bodies. Regardless of the details of the match itself it’s clear that Sovereign and James Kimson are just getting warmed up. This is a 2 way street but there better not be any traffic because these two big trucks are set to go one on one again. And it’s going to rip Evolution apart. Kimson stops and turns to look at the photograph and smiles, remembering his younger years. Although only 28 now his career has spanned 12 years. He’s seen the highest of highs and the very lowest of lows. Kimson, wearing regular street clothing inside what obviously is his own home runs a hand through his long hair, a bandage graces his forehead from the match he had with Sovereign and Jameson, battlescars of an epic war. A war that has just begun. It’s going to keep going at Legends. Ah yes, Legends. I’ve been called a Legend Sovereign, as I know that a lot of people in the backstage of Evolution fear you and look upon you with a lot of awe and crap like that. They should Sovereign. You are dangerous and command attention and a form of respect only attainable by Fear. The fear of a man that is uncontrollable. A man that is unpredictable. You are a monster Sovereign, and you proved that to me finally at Tainted Love. Attacking me backstage, fighting me till the bitter end, and although I would have loved to have pinned you and not Jameson in the end there had to be a finish to the match. I took what was available to me and in beating Jameson I cancelled him from our equation. It’s down to the both of us now. You and me. Although this week at legends we both must take up a Tag Team Partner. It doesn’t seem totally fair that I must team with Shocka, who although is a former Universal Champion just went through probably the most brutal match in Evolution history. You get a 7 foot freak. Hell, not that I’m complaining. I’m just trying to figure out if I’ll get to the arena in time to streatch properly. Let’s face it Sovereign, Omega I couldn’t give a damn about. But you Sovereign, if I were you I’d get it over with and tag yourself into me so we can do it the right way. Screw Shocka, and screw Omega. You and I both want the same thing. To rip each other to shreads. That’s what you want Sovereign, because I saw it in your eyes. Yeah, I’m trying to ruin your life, sure only because you destroyed the lives of so many and one happens to be my own flesh and blood Sovereign. So payback starts at Legends in a tag team match when I’ll make your ass history. I don’t care how it goes down Sovereign, if I have to beat down Omega first to get to you I’ll do it. I got my taste of what Sovereign has to offer at Tainted Love. But only a taste, just enough to make me want to beat the living shit out of you even more then I did before. Kimson continues to walk forward, hands in his pocket now as he approaches closer to the camera that’s been watching him. Shocka, I will be straight and honest with you. I don’t like you. I’m sure that’s not a shock to you Shocka, and I’m quite sure you don’t give a crap about me either. But I know what it’s like to be betrayed by a friend and have them cost you something very dear to them. So I sympathize with you to a certain extent. Co-existing is maybe something we should consider. I honestly don’t care what you choose to do this Wednesday. We can worth to attain the common goal that lays in front of us. Fight together as a team, or we can just go our own ways. I don’t take kindly to loosing in any stretch of the imagination. I’m not that great of a tag team wrestler either Shocka, I’m quite selfish in fact. I’ve always been looking out for me and my best interests and you Shocka are just another in a long list of Former champions that I’ve come face to face with. Granted, you were Universal Champion, and a good champion at that and I’ll give the devil his due. But you, as a person don’t really jell to well with yours truly. You are an asshole and that’s just the fact of the matter. Yeah, I’m an arrogant pretentious self endulgent at times. Who cares really, this time it’s not about me just yet. This time it’s the revenge, the payback for a life stolen, for a child unborn. Sovereign is my target Shocka and you can either do one of two things, stay out of my way, or pay the price. That’s really all there is to it. And those are not threats, because I don’t threaten people Shocka. I say what I’m going to do, and then I make good on it. So you can give me leway and stay the hell out of my way when it’s time, or else I will mow you down to get to Sovereign. I am willing to bet that your shockingness is not at 100 percent after your hell’s fury match on Sunday. So you should consider the less physical route. Because I tend to not have remorse when whipping aside those who stop me from reaching my real goals. But I do realize Mr. Shocking that this is a 2 way street. Yeah, you probably can’t wait to get your paws on Mr. Morpheus whatever the hell he is Omega and you know what... you should get your hands on him. Damn straight you should. Hell if someone cost me the Universal Title I’d be fit to be tied up and thrown in a jail cell. And yeah, I won’t stop you from kicking the shit out of his ass on Wednesday if it’s the right time. He’s a dangerous man, or so he likes to tell us all over and over, and I do believe him. I’ve seen the video tapes and I’ve watched the matches to see what he is capable of doing inside that ring, or even and most often on the outside. He’s a champion, he’s a strong, mean bitch. Really that’s all he is. Needing his woman to comfort his insecurities and so Shocka I wish you the best of luck when you go toe to toe with the Omega, but the end message is to stay the hell out of my way. Kimson turns and spots a photograph, obviously probably placed there for a point as it’s a picture of James and Leya, probably a good 5 years ago. Kimson looks upon it with fondness and speaks. This was about, oh 4 years ago? Yeah, I think so. Back after you left her Sovereign. Kimson runs a hand down the face of his sister, her smile is wide and she honestly looks at peace, happy. She looks brighter then we see her now. Her hair, blonde as opposed to the brown we see her in. Her eyes have a twinkle in them. Kimson, smiling ear to ear with his sister, wearing normal every day clothing. The scene is someone’s backyard, they both sit up on a picnic table, some trees are the backdrop, green grass, lawn furnature the who nine yards. That was maybe a year or so after you walked out on her. Of all the pictures I have of my life, there are two that I cherish the most. One with me and my father, the other is right here. My sister, happy again. Happy because she didn’t fear coming home. She wasn’t afraid of her husband coming home and lashing out on her. She wasn’t afraid of people anymore, she was happy to start a new life. What’s so wrong with wanting that for your own flesh and blood. Of course, Sovereign I know that you think this is all backwards and that you might not understand it all. I know that you are a sick man, and regardless of how sick you may, or may not be, someone needs to be held responsible. For the death of your own son for Christ’s sakes. For now taking her back into your clutches. Do you know the damage you caused her? Her body, her mind... her very soul? You stole her from herself almost. Now Leya, the one that the fans see, that you see isn’t the real woman. This... this photo and that woman you see on TV aren’t the same people. They might look the same. They have the same voice, but Sovereign, you’ve twisted her back into a fantasy world. One where finally she has power. Where she has some degree of control. You use her, trying to regain what you’ve lost. And so what happens when you become Universal Champion again Sovereign? Are you going to toss her aside again? Or will hold onto her so that you can have another death grip on the title. And what in God’s name would what solve, huh? I know what happened the last time you were Champion. There is a long list of names, names of good men. Good women, good people who’ve never been seen on Camera again. People that don’t know how they’re going to continue to make a living all because of what? A power trip? That’s what it’s all about isn’t it, a power trip. You and Leya, one great big God damn power trip trying to make yourselves feel good about yourself. Only because you know you aren’t anything but crap, trash. Yes, Leya I called you trash. And I’m staring at you, at what you once were hoping that I can bring you back. Hoping that you can see what you’ve done to yourself by going back to him. Kimson turns back to the camera, shaking his head. He takes a few steps forward and then looks back at the photo before looking up to the camera. It’ll be my intention Leya to prove to you what Sovereign is. That he isn’t the right man for anyone. That he’s a monster, he’s evil and you should just stay the hell away from him. And if I have to fight him tooth and nail, eye for an eye for all the rest of my life to do it, then I will. And if I have to take every single step it will take to prove to you that he’s not human, that he’s more animal then man, then I sure as hell will. I love you Leya, you are my sister. I want you to be happy, but not this fake heaven you have thrown all your hopes into. This is a lie, you and Sovereign. You are using each other, and I doubt you even know what it is that you want in the end. And it is going to begin this week at Legends. I hope that you’ll understand. Understand that this is for you my sister. The Hype, has spoken. Kimson then walks past the camera, shaking his head still, bothered by his Sister’s actions.
