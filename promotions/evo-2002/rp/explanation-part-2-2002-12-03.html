Date: 12.03.02 ~~The scene opens on Shocka standing in front of a mirror he wears a pair of Khaki pants and a black shirt. He wears a long trench coat over his clothes and his hair is spiked straight up. He sports a pair of silver framed sunglasses with blue lenses to match his hair. A knock at the door comes and he struts to the door and swings it open to find Libby Bowman. She carries a microphone and a camera man follows her, the enter the room and a huge smile comes over Shocka’s face.~~ ‘So you bust into my hotel room…I know what you want baby…’ Shocka says in a sly voice. ‘Wha…what the hell did you just say?’ She questions in an angry voice. ‘What, its okay baby…I understand you wants to start slow…’ Shocka again speaks. ‘What the hell is wrong with you?’ She asks. ‘Oh come on! I went to get my pills from the doctor…’ Shocka says. ‘What pills?’ Libby asks. ‘The pills that will destroy Jonathan…’ Shocka starts. ‘The pills that will allow me to break free of the wanna be bad ass and just be myself!’ 'Why would you want to do that...I mean you are on one hell of a role...' She speaks. 'I did it for you! I mean you are the reason that i want to be myself...that i want to change back and show everyone the true me!' Shocka speaks excitedly. 'And i'm married...' Libby speaks showing her left ring finger is occupied. 'But...you didn't wear that at our last interview...' Shocka says in an aggravated voice. 'I usually don't wear it to work...' Libby says. 'But...I love you Libby!' Shocka yells. 'Its okay...many men do...' Libby says with a sort of evil smile. 'Ahh well...you got a sister?' Shocka asks with a grin on his face. 'No!' Libby screams. 'I thought you liked me...' 'Nah...i just wanted to get in your pants...' 'So did you lie about getting your pills too?' Libby questions. 'No...I really am ON them and I feel much better...more like myself...' 'Like the #1 Stunna?' Libby asks in an interogating voice. 'Yes...' Shocka says like he just realized it. 'You know what this means?' Libby shakes her head no. 'It means that I am back and better than ever, the true me is out and L2J and the rest of the bunch of panzies in the Notorious is going to feel my wrath...' He says. 'they will realize that when i beat Frost i wasn't a fluke...that when me and Omega kidnapped Seara it was just the beginning!' Shocka stands and looks into the camera. 'And most of all they will see that the stable wars may have just begun but they are also going to come to an abrupt end...when i destroy them...Because i'm back'
