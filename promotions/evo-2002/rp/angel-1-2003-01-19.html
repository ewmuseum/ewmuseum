Angel - 1

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.19.03 As horrific as it seems I don't want to make it seem like I don't care about you guys so I won't describe the setting of where I am right now. Come to think of it, I don't care about you guys.. so here you go. Lying here on my backside, my arms placed behind my head staring at the ceiling, I can feel the cotton under my body, rubbing against my back and legs. The dimness of the room, the only thing keeping me from the all time darkness is the sudden lightning bolts that jolt down in the night sky. I can hear the rain outside, hitting my window heavily as if it were trying to emerge it's way inside of my house. I turned my head toward the window as the sudden silence of the house startled me. The thunder had stopped, and the lighting and rain had stopped. Darkness was all around me, this wasn't the artificial darkness that people claim to have inside their heart, this darkness was pure. My eyelids smacked down on the top of my cheeks as I then opened my mouth and let out a yawn. The rain had started once more and the thunder had rattled it's way back into my ear. It was like a symphony. The lightning was the Cello, the rain was the Harpsichord, and the thunder was the lovely String Bass. I slowly opened my eyes and noticed that going to sleep wasn't an option anymore. I slowly ruffled my hands from the back of my head to my sides and shoved myself into a sitting position. I slowly started to move forward, sliding to the edge of the bed. It felt as if there were no ground under me at all, atleast until my feet hit the floor. I closed my eyes once more and placed my elbows on my knees, and slowly covered my face with my hands. My palms were sweaty, either that or my face was sweaty. It was luke warm in the room but I was hot. I slid my right hand from my face up my forehead and behind my head. I was weak. I had a sudden ache in my chin. I twisted my head toward the left and lifted up my left hand and placed it on my chin, there was a raspy soar right there. Pain was stroking it's devilish fingers across my chin, trying to pleasure me in it's own tormented way. I turned my body toward the left and slowly exited my room, went across the hall and moved into the bathroom. Instinct through my hand into the lightswitch on the wall beside the doorway. The lights blindsided me, and I nearly lost balance. I turned toward the large vanity mirror on the left wall, and squinted my eyes some. I continued to look into the reflection, seeing a man with an intense look on his face. It's hard to look intense with your eyes squinted but I gained sight as my eyes gained trust in the light and my pupils got smaller. I looked into the mirror and tilted my head backward some, enough to allow me to see why my chin was hurting. There was a scratch on it. Healing had already undergone it, but it was still ripe. The scab that was forming was high red and the color spread around my chin like a rash. I looked into the mirror and slowly began to realize why I was feeling this pain.. it was a sign. Forgiveness is one virtue that I lack, but this is a chance for me to show forgiveness. The scab on my chin is still ripe, as is my trust to Heart Break Chick, Xander, and Asis. The more it rots, the less trust I have for one or all of them. It's obvious.. I still trust you people, don't betray me. Shit, it hurts like hell though. The pain isn't dull.. it's sharp like the blade of razor. I can feel it as soon as I move my chin, but I'll just have to deal with it through the time. It'll heal whenever. My ears were then pierced by the screeching of a bell. It shouted out toward me as an alarm to get my attention, ring-ring-ring. I turned my head over toward the bedroom and slowly took my steps to my destination. The ringing continued vastly, I turned my head over toward the phone and extended my hand and grasped the large hunk of plastic. I removed it from it's space and placed it up to my ear. I could hear talking in the background. A female's voice uttering out toward me, with her lucious vocals, but there was also a male in the background. I looked down at the clock, which was beside my phone, '11:21 PM' flashed in an infered color infront of my face, over and over and over again. 'Hello..?' slid from the pit of my stomach all the way up through my throat like vomit. I spit out the words in a tiresome voice. 'Whassup man?' the fimilar voice through itself right through my head and something tweaked in my head, but not exactly what I had wanted. 'Who is this?' I questioned out toward the anonymous speaker, I stood there somewhat eager for my question to be answered. 'Well it isn't Boomer the Friendly Clown..' the speaker said in a sarcastic tone. I tilted my head some and chuckled at the statement, but I was cut off by, 'It's Xander..'. My eyes widened some and I then eyes widened in rememberance. I thought to myself, 'Oh...' and I nodded my head. '..Oh..Sup?' I replied toward him, I could hear the female voice in the background clearly now, the voice sounded fimilar also, but I never really bothered to think of who it was. 'What happened at Legends was a mistake man.. don't forget that, Alright? Chick is all angry right now cause she knows you're pissed..' 'Not anymore, she has quite the kick though, left a gash under my chin. Still ripe..' 'Yeah, yeah. I just wanted to know if he had an understanding on what was going on. I didn't want anyone fighting inside of the clique now. We're a posse.. remember?' Xander had hissed at me. I laughed some and knew what I had to do. 'Posse... that sounds like something Velma from Scooby Doo would say..' I joked toward him. An uncontrolled chuckle started from the both of us, as he then confirmed his leave, 'Well, I just wanted to make sure there were no hard feelings..' 'Yeah, it's all good..' I replied. 'Later..' 'Yeah...later.' I slowly through my hand down and placed it on the hook. I tilted my head backward and sighed some, rubbing both of my hands through my hair and turning over toward my bed once more. What did I just tell Xander? The opposite of what I was feeling. I was pissed off that I lost a match that I had won. I was pissed off that the reason I lost that match was through HBC. I was pissed off that I wasn't in the Continental Race anymore. I want gold, fame, publicity, to be a posterboy. I won't get that by getting cheapshotted and screwed from every corner that I turn. That's what I wanted to say, but I didn't. I was choked up, I don't think that telling everything will solve my problems. I looked down at the white cango shorts that I was wearing. I turned over toward the television and then slowly fell down onto my bed. I reached on the right side off the bed and grabbed the remote control from the table. I pointed it toward the tv and slid my finger over the circular button. I watched as light blind sided me once more. I squinted my eyes and placed the remote at my side. I lifted my hands up and smashed them together twice, I watched as the bathroom like went off. I then turned back over toward the televison and leaned backward on my propped up pillow. I moved my thumb across the remote control hitting all kinds of bumps and turns and things. I looked at the TV and noticed an infomercial for 'Ass Cream'. I chuckled some and pressed the button repeadiatly knowing exactly what was happening. I thought of them toward myself, Infomercial, Conan O'Brien, Simpsons, Legends, Girls Gone...Legends?. I pressed the button down from the one I was previously pressing and looked ahead of me. I saw me, so I then zoomed in on the action... HBC casually strolled down the ramp and as both men got back to their feet again she slid into the ring under the bottom rope. She quickly got up to her feet again and as Dreamer and Angel both stood up, HBC went for her Sweet BadAss Superkick on Dreamer but he just narrowly got out of the way and she nailed Angel right in the chin. HBC looked on at what she did and then notice Dreamer standing there staring right at her before she quickly backed away and out of the ring. Dreamer turned around and immediately went for the cover on Angel. One……..two………..three! 'Your winner by pinfall…….Big Daddy Dreamer!' pierced through my ear heavily, I could barely endure watching it, but I stifiled myself and continued to watch. 'Dreamer just picked up the victory and earned himself a match against Asis next week for the Continental Title but he got a bit of help from HBC to do it!', Justin Fritz shouted. 'She didn’t mean to do that! It was a complete accident and she was trying to hit Dreamer!' Jack Tyler said in defense of HBC 'Yeah but she hit Angel instead and gave Dreamer the win again.', Jack Tyler confirmed. He spoke of the truth, and I could barely endure it, I felt the pit of my stomach slowly turn from the left to right. I could tell that vomit was going to shoot straight from my mouth. I get like that when I'm very VERY pissed at something. 'That’s not going to sit well with a few people I think.' Jack Tyler obliged. 'Probably not. Right now we need to take a quick break.' Justin Fritz agreed, as the screen slowly went into a commercial break, I had managed to surpress my barfing. I was angry beyond point of measurement. I was to the boiling point. Ready to explode, and waiting for a target. I was ready to do my damage, and my target was going to be either Yoshii Tanaka, Strictly Prohibited, or even Lee eXodus even though he's my partner. Angel has spoken, and he's ready to do his damage. Come and see if you can tame these broken wings. fade.to.the.martini.shot.
