Date: January 25th The scene opens and Seara seems to be just another ordinary person as she sits in the quiet coffee shop. The last time the female appeared in front of the camera, she seemed to be reverting to the female that planned and schemed and talked to herself in third person, and often as another person. The alter ego that is Seara has control of her, and the ordinary female Katherine is sitting inside, waiting for her time to come. She is the one female that reigns supreme in her own private life, and she knows that the control in her is strong enough now to allow herself to survive while Seara takes control of her body. Seara however is unsure if Frost has understood the change in her, but he should know first hand that Seara used to be this way until he changed her into something calmer. He unlocked a great passion and love within her, and the female unknowing how to control it, allowed the feeling to consume her and destroy all she had worked to be. Now this week she will finally be herself again. This weeks PPV is set to be a great one, and Seara is ready to make sure that a selected handful don’t forget it very quickly. Plans are ready, things are looking good, trained her well we did. Seara is quick to begin talking as the scene finishes fading into the close up shot of her. She looks down at the coffee in front of her and almost wonders why it is in front of her, was someone else sharing the table with her, or was it here when the female sat down. Truthfully, she could barely remember the last few minutes, she had thought them away; she had concentrated on the voice within her, and had arrived at this table in this place as if by accident. The pay per view, not far away. Xander, a man who would do anything to get this title from me, and knowing Xander, he will do it, he is a man who likes to plan ahead, plan a great attack on me during our match, to have one of his little fuckers come out and attack me, help him cheat so that the little bastard will walk away with that title around his waist. But even if he does manage to secure the title, he should make sure he watches all around him, because things are changing and you Xander are not the only one that can plan. How knows what I will do, or when I will do it, or even if I’ll do it to you, maybe I’ll teach your goons to fuck off and leave you to fight for yourself for once, that would leave you so vulnerable wouldn’t it Xander? That would leave you like a little child, defenceless and easy to target, think about it Xander, you are nothing. I know I seem to repeat this to you a lot, but you are so dammed arrogant that you fail to realise everyone within eVo is 100 times more powerful and more skilled than you in the ring, and out of the ring, fine you may be good at planning, but lets look at the facts, planning will only get you there, having the skill determination and guts to do it on your own will get you the title, and will prove your worth. Xander, you are shit to me, and you and I both know we hate each other with a great passion, but you have never met Seara properly, you only think you know me, you only think you know, but you know nothing of me. The female stops talking cocks her head to the side and stares into the camera lens with a cold hard stare. Frost disturbs her heartless look as the male kisses the females forehead and sits down. Glad you turned up. The male talks in a quiet calm voice, hoping in many ways that he can keep the female placid enough to talk to him. That was the reason for her arrival here, Frost had called her at the gym and asked her to come met him to talk about something that had sounded important at the time, and now she remembered it clearly like it was only just happening to her. Shaking her head the female snaps back into reality and turns her attention to a piece of paper tightly secured in Frosts hand. I was going to sort out your kit bag for you, and I found this in there. Frost hands the paper across the table to Seara, and she looks down shocked at the sight of what has been placed in front of her. Scott, how…how could you go through my private shit, I thought you understood that the stuff I kept in my bag was private and I didn’t want even you looking at it. FUCK Scott, I can’t believe you’d do that to me. Hold on Katherine, calm the fuck down, it was lying on top of your stuff and your bag was open on the bed, I noticed it and thought we should talk. The female is raging and the camera focuses down on the white knuckles, her hands gripping tight into a fist, ready to lash out, but this is the man she loves, and not even she is sure how she can react to him. Taking a deep breath, she looks once again at the sheet of paper, writing covers it, and on a closer look, it seems to be names of eVo members, the roster has a meaning to the female as a slight smirk covers her face. Ok, so you going to tell me, or do I have to bribe it out of you? Frosts comment is a lighthearted one, and he means nothing by it, the female knows this and she smiles at Frost, but only for a moment, her smile is quickly removed and she looks hard and long at Frost. It’s nothing, honestly Scott, it’s just a stupid boredom thing, I got bored, I began to write peoples names, that’s it. Katherine, I’ve been finding them all over your car, what the hell is up with you, you’ve become more violent than you were before I met you, and you’re different. No Scott, this is me, the real me, don’t be afraid, I have control now, and I love you more with every passing day, just trust me when I say this is something I have to do alone. There is an awkward silence, as Frost seems to try and digest what the female has just told him, but he does so quickly. Toward the female, Frost understands and is caring, he knows the pain in her and the strength she has to do what she does and face life with the outlook she has. He is protective, although he knows that he is not the boss of her, and cannot fight her battles, that are something the female has chosen to control herself. Hey look, lets get out of here, come on, it’s close on midnight, this place is pretty much deserted, and I think you should get to bed for once. You haven’t been sleeping well these days, I can tell, you keep getting out of bed, and you scream in pain while you sleep. Seara looks ready to begin to shout at frost, but instead she turns to the camera. Xander, the pay per view is close, and I will show you the new Seara, you will meet what I have become, up close and personal. The change is upon us, and I am now the true one that will bring around a new era in eVolution, I will change the way you think of me Xander, and I will start this Sunday. scene fades as Frost takes the females hand and leads her out the café.
