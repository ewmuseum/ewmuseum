Full Circle

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 20 Halloed halls. It may not be Madison Squared Gardens, or it might not be the Tokyo Dome, but Toronto Ontario has been on the leading edge of professional wrestling now since Wrestle Mania 6. Most view it as the largest market in the world for the industry, a hub of North America. The 5th largest city in the Continent, home to over 6 million people. The City is impressive, it’s clean, easy to navigate. The people are like that of any other city, although with a familiar, friendly Canadian point of view. As you walk past the CN tower on your way to the new Air Canada Center you can only imagine the things that this building has seen. A year ago one man remembers this town, and what happened to him in the “hallowed Halls” of the ACC. His hour long blood bath with Daniel Somers ended in a draw, he fell just short of his goal of stealing the only thing that Somers held dear to him, the OSW World Heavyweight Championship. Shortly there after under the order of Cameron Griffin Sovereign was taken back to Chicago, placed in a mental institution where he stayed for 11 months. 11 months, to plot his revenge against the man who he thought was responsible, Daniel Somers. Over the course of a year, a lot changed. Somers retired from active work, took up the owner’s chair. He held power in the now defunct EdW, NEW and now CEO of eVo wrestling. Sovereign returned, he joined eVo in a violent act at American Nightmare, and instead of turning out to be the enemy of Somers he sided with him, vowing to prove that he’s not a has been, vowing to become the Monster he knew he could be. At X-rated Sovereign defeated the field of 8 superstars to become the 1st Universal Champion in eVo history when he defeated Jamal Kage in a Ladder match that night. Sovereign was champion, finally and after a year imprisoned the monster was finally released upon the world. Mighty men feel to his feet, one by one the eVo roster began to realize that Sovereign was on a rampage, and that nothing would stop him. With the William persona slowly developing Sovereign was a victim of Somers’ attempt to slow him down, placing him in a 4 corners No DQ match at Digital Destruction. Sovereign defended his title against L2J, Matt Boone and Jamal Kage. Kage’s career came to an end that night, as Sovereign through him off the stage through the electrical circuit board before pinning Matthew Boone, overcoming the odds, a dominant champion. L2J however, wouldn’t be silenced. With Faith around the corner Sovereign gave us the impression that he was gone, that it was William, his passive and past life was in control. L2J and William formed a bond. William’s family came back for him, there was a lot of emotion going into Faith, when L2J and William were set to lock up in a battle for the Universal Championship. But it was all a cover up. William never was in control, it was Sovereign all along, suckering L2J into his trap, and with his family at ringside Sovereign lowered the House of Pain and he and L2J battled for nearly 30 minutes in the most brutal Title match in the history of eVo. Sovereign’s attempt to destroy L2J’s life was a German Suplex off the roof of the cage, through the announcer’s table. The match, a draw, the title declared to be held by both men by Cameron Griffin. It’s come Full Circle. The ominous voice of Sovereign catches your ear. The Narrator’s voice is gone now, the scene no longer black, but now a vision of a street corner. A circular area of what must be a city as there are people running about, cars driving, horns buzzing. A bus stops and several people get on it. You can see it saying Toronto Metro Transit. The black and white camera shot makes some of the images difficult to get your eyes around, but you get the general picture. It’s Toronto, mid day and the little clock on the lower left hand side says that it’s November the 20th, 4 days until Rise of the Legends and Sovereign vs L2J inside the Tier’s of Evolution. Full Circle, Cameron has screwed me and it’s come full circle. Somers has put me in the same town that he managed to survive me in, and it’s come full circle. L2J, full circle is an odd expression, but it makes perfect sense. You’ve fought, clawed and scratched your entire existence just like I have and everything seems to be pointing towards this one place, our definitive moment. This one match will define our careers. Your chance to break away, step into the spot light L2J as Universal Champion. My night is here, to silence my past failures, to emerge the victor and leave this city with the one thing I wanted to leave with the last time, the championship I deserve. I have held that title since August L2J, it’s mine, rightfully mine and Cammy had no right to force me to share it with you or anyone else. You have that belt right now, in your possession and it burns at my stomach every time I picture you holding it, looking at yourself in the mirror with it on. Hearing people like Scott Frost refer to you as the real Universal Champion when they know, everyone knows no matter how much they don’t want to believe it that YOU L2J are a fraud. You don’t deserve to hold that title belt. You don’t deserve to call yourself a Champion. Not while I’m still breathing air L2J will you ever be the Universal Champion, not in my eyes and not in the minds of every man woman and child on this planet because they know that you aren’t as good as I am. No, L2J I’m not much for the old run of the mill I’m better then you are speeches. Because I’m not the run of the mill sort of guy. But lets be perfectly honest L2J. You’ve tried, you’ve failed and that’s all there is to it. I don’t know how you ever got a match with me at Faith in the 1st place, but I made sure that once Somers gave you the chance that you would get there. Because I wanted to finish you off once and for all. I wanted to end the L2J that we know of today. And I over shot the mark. I went to far and I ended up getting screwed by a man that has a history of ruining my life in Cameron Griffin. Now it’s my turn to do the damage on people’s lives L2J. You’re the victim on Sunday. I’m going to do two things, prove I’m better then you, and second cripple you, finish you off. I will embarrass you inside the cages. I’m going to rip your flesh from your body, bleed you like a pig. Whip you like a horse and kick you like the dog that you are. Then I’ll climb to the very top of the structure and take my title belt home with me. To silence the demons, to quite the urge in my body. Everything will have come full circle. The camera then spins, you then see the Air Canada Center, the shot pans up, looking through the front entrance of the building all the way up to it’s roof from the ground. It’s a beautiful modern structure, you hear Sovereign’s breath near the microphone of the camera. You know, they brought the cages in today L2J. I watched them load then into the building. They came in the great big cases, quite large if you ask me. They brought them in the back entrance through the loading dock. I saw the workers stop and have a smoke with their coffee, one of them said “I don’t know about you, but that L2J kid is going to get killed.” I don’t know how to take that. Maybe everyone in the world is expecting me to destroy you. I don’t know about pressure or anything L2J, but I know this won’t be easy. I never said you were a push over L2J. I know your tough, your head strong. You don’t know what fear is apparently, although I think that’s a load of horse crap. But if the common man knows you don’t have a hope then you have to see L2J that you’ve made the mistake in making this match in the Tier’s of Evolution. A double cage, with the Title strung up from the roof. Inside those cages I will break your spirit, break your bones and break your heart one after the other. I don’t care about your friends L2J, I don’t care about how badly you want to be a true champion. It isn’t going to happen. It’s not going to be your night in Toronto Ontario Canada on November the 24 in the year 2002. Because you and I have a date that night. Inside the most dangerous structure in wrestling. Since the Tier’s of Evolution. When everything is coming full circle for Sovereign, everything won’t be so good for you L2J. When you feel the blood flow, when you hear the ringing in your ears. When you look and see me with you locked in your last breath. You’ll want to fight, and struggle but your body will have had to much. You won’t be able to respond to the pain. Your body shut down, and you will lay there and watch as I climb to my title. You will lay there, in a pool of our blood and watch as the Official calls for the bell. You’ll hear it clang in your head like a hammer to your skull. You will hear 20 thousand fans booing not me. But booing you because you FAILED to live up to your promise. You failed to beat me in the match YOU asked for. YOU failed to be that Knight in Shining armor. YOU failed to rid Evolution of the Human Monster, the man who’s ripped this place apart from top to bottom. YOU failed to end my Dominance. YOU FAILED to be the one man, the ONLY man that might have had a chance to end my Crusade of Violence. And L2J you will have failed every single man, woman and child that ever turned their televisions to OSE on Wednesday nights to watch you. To watch you, their hero. You, the man that they want to see the winner. You, the man that has battled, broken, bloody and battered for all these months, to finally get his chance. That one final one on one, fair ground encounter with the man that has plagued you and your followers for 4 months. They will have watched you live, before their eyes fail them. Fail their hopes, THEIR dreams. You’re not alone in this L2J, you have the world watching. And the world will watch you fall short, one... more... time. Take that into consideration L2J when you music hits on the 24th. When the arena fills with the cheers of a hopeful crowd and you walk to that ring to face me. They don’t just cheer your name. They are cheering for the chance to maybe see a new champion. The chance to witness a victory for the ages. Magazines, TV specials, Internet sites you name it have all gone crazy over this match L2J. They are dubbing it the biggest match of the year, L2J takes on Sovereign inside the Tier’s of Evolution for the Universal Title. And it will live up to the hype L2J. I’m going to make sure of that. Just as damn sure as I’m going to leave you laying in a pile of blood and guts, and as damn sure as I’m going to leave the Universal Champion. The camera spins around quickly, and fights to get reorientated, now on Sovereign, who seems to be the camera man himself. Now the focus is on him, not the greatest of shots but you see his face, the black and white shot, people walking to and fro, Sovereign shakes his head so the hair is out of his face as he continues to speak. It is going to come full circle L2J. You had your chance. I’ve played along nicely for long enough. Inside those two cages, surrounded by over a tone of steal, over 20 feet in height you and I will fight for the Universal Title that we “share” and when it is said and done L2J, you will lay there a broken and beaten man. Suvival of the Fittest is the slogan for evolution, and we’re going to find out who’s the best in the entire company on the 24th of November. And L2J, it’s going to be me! The camera spins back to the street, cars drive by. I’ll see you on Toronto L2J. I’ll be here waiting for you. The camera then slowly fades to black, under the chuckling of Sovereign’s obsessive voice.
