The Mystorium[( Volume III ) {PPV RP 3}]

8 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.08.03 ~His breath cut through the cold air like a warm knife through butter. He had just awoken from a restless night of sleep and came to his front door to see it left hanging wide open. His interest torn away from trying to wake up and to finding the new threat, an intruder. He walked curiously around the corner from his living room and into his kitchen with a wooden baseball bat in his hand. When he rounded the corner a figure brought him to raise the bat toward them. He stopped mid swing when he realized it was just Darryl. His pajama’s were tattered and torn long johns his hair was a mess and he stood smiling still.~ ‘Jesus Jonathan!’ Darryl screams. ‘Put the damn bat down its just me…’ ‘Why are you here?’ Shocka questions him with no patience. ‘I was gonna surprise you…’ He says quietly. ‘I guess I did!’ A laugh comes that does not seem to amuse Jonathan much. ‘I’m not in the mood today…I was up most of the night thinking about that damn kid and my bitch of a wife…’ Shocka says looking into the still messy living room. ‘Yeah she tore it up pretty good it looks like…but you know what…maybe you can start over now.’ Darryl speaks in a calm tone. ‘But I don’t’ want to…that’s just the thing I have everything going for me in the wrestling business I am the fucking Universal champion and no one can bring me down from that not even this bitch.’ Shocka says loudly. ‘Calm down man…’ Darryl speaks. ‘I know you better than anyone…and I know what your anger can do to you…I don’t want you to take another turn for the worse like you did 2 years ago…do you remember those days?’ Darryl questions. ‘….’ Silence comes from the end of Shocka as he stares blankly at Darryl. ‘You were so wrapped up in your emotions that you refused to drop the title in the NRCW and Robert Knight sent down 5 guys to beat a message into you…you were out for 3 months man…they really did a number on like they were supposed to but…when you came back you were better than ever. You went into the EDW and showed everyone what you were made of…you proved that you were better than everyone thought…don’t’ Darryl is interrupted! ‘I know!’ Shocka screams as he moves back into the living room and plops down on the couch. ‘I just don’t want to have to start over again…once in my life is enough. I am tired of the bitch ruling my life and I’m going to get this settled on my days off…but I want to meet that kid first…’ Shocka cuts off as he thinks. ‘Okay…get dressed and we’ll go.’ Darryl speaks. ‘Alright.’ Shocka says. ~He now heads up stairs to be alone with his thoughts even if it is only for a few minutes. In these few minutes he is allowed to think of the 4 men that will compete at a shot for his title…can you think of the last time when the competition for the Universal title was so fierce?! NEVER! Why is it that Shocka has to get the rough start at being the Universal champion with a title shot, sure he is willing to be a defending champion but Sovereign has no right to be in that match and nor does Jameson or King…Frost as much as Shocka hates to admit is the most deserving.~ ‘Come on!’ Darryl yells in a fit of impatience toward Shocka. ~The thoughts are now interrupted and its time for him to get back to living his life how we wants to live it for the next 3 days…its his time…its his freedom…~
