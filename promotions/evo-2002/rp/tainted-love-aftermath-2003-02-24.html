Tainted Love Aftermath

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th Febuary 2003 The scene opens in the house of Seara and Frost. Seara sits calm and quiet on the sofa, unsure how to act. She has put Frost back into his bed and convinced him to at least get rest while she does her interview for eVolution Access. The female has a shocked expression on her face and she looks down at the small black box in her hand, clearing his throat the cameraman distracts the wrestler and closing the box she holds it tight in her hand before sliding it into the pocket of her pants, she smiles slightly as she releases the box from her grip and looks back to the camera. As quick as the scene opened, Seara’s smile is gone and a look of anger comes across her face as her eyes stare hard and cold at the camera lens. Licking her lips she grins but there is a sadistic look on her face and her eyes seem to glow, from the lamp in the corner reflecting on her eyes, she looks as though she has completely black eyes except for the white around her iris. What a sweet taste blood is, especially that metallic irony taste of someone else's blood. I am sure Xander that I have said before that I would taste your blood one day, and now I know exactly what you taste like. I tasted the pain that had pulsed through your blood as I sunk my teeth into your finger, I felt the soft feeling of your blood running across my skin, I seen it on my hand as I wiped it from my mouth. But something seems to be so wrong, oh yes, I know, your loss. Oh how you wanted that win, that title, to prove you were the better between us, how you thought you had it all under control, but I’m sorry Xander, poor little baby, it seems as though you were more wrong than even I could have expected. I know how much it hurts when you are so wrong, but for you it must be hard. Our long feud has come to an end, a battle of great proportions, Frost thought I was mental, thought I was signing my death card when I decided on the stipulation for our match, but it seems the trust I have in my old wrestling technique is what I needed to finish it once and for all, and to finish it on top. During the week I talked to Scott, told him what I wanted to do with our match, and he called me crazy, he spent a long time arguing with me, trying to convince me that I would be out of my league, but he could not argue with me. Scott knew then and he definitely knows now that I am a lot more dangerous than I once was, and although I never knew it before, Scott has brought it out in me, I feel a lot stronger now, and it isn’t just because I train more. Seara turns her head gets to her feet and walks to a table, on it lay both the championship belts. The lamp lights up the gold of the metal and the camera focuses closely on the way they sit alongside each other, the straps overlapping. I have the champion alongside me, and together I know Scott and I can become something so amazingly powerful and dangerous within eVo, we will be something no one ever expected from us. The female gently rubs her fingers across the belts and looks at them for a moment before turning back to the camera. How does it feel Xander, I once again beat you and proved who is the better between us, and now I have the Universal Champion alongside me, here to witness the destruction I will continue to give in eVolution wrestling. My champion has his long awaited title and I have managed to retain the title that took so much hard work to get. Seara’s attitude is calmer and her look less sadistic, it seems the female has learnt how to control her anger with a great amount of strength and grace in the way she changes her attitude from one moment to the next. Once again the female begins to walk away from the camera this time heading toward a door. Following her the camera watches as the female opens it and looks in, a smile coming over her face and her eyes glinting. The scene fades away as Seara stands silent and still watching Frost sleep.
