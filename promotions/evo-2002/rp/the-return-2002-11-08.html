The Return.......

8 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-8-02 A maniacal laugh echo’s throughout the room in which only a red light can be seen over by the corner. The windows are open, the shutter smacking against the window plane as the winds kick up to a furious howl making the torrential downpour of the warm wet rains blow across vertically. It is a horrible, miserable day for most, except one person delights in weather such as this. When the lightning crashes in the background, his blue devilish eyes reflects against the window showing his face. Though, it’s a little different now. Gone are the bandages that was wrapped around his face like he was the damn mummy from the 1920’s cheap horror movie with Boris Karloff. He traces the scar on the side of his face where his broken orbital socket at the hands of The HeartBreak Chick. But smiles again devilishly knowing what is coming up for her oh so very soon. The lightning crackles through the sky again as he props up his feet looking out towards the Golden Gate Bridge and Alcatraz. He looks over to the right and see the TransAmerica building, the pyramid shape building that signifies the San Francisco skyline. Behind Big Daddy Dreamer is his head of personal security, Natalia. The Yugoslavian Special Forces Operative whom he brought back following a shoot-out in Sarajevo. Magdalena, the mysterious individual who accompanies him from time to time is nowhere to be found at this moment. Not that BDD really mind’s. Truth be told, he’s kind of happy she isn’t here now, especially after she cost him his match with HBC, and distracted him long enough for HBC and Jinx to blindside him, giving him this scar. BDD motions his hands as Natalia brings over the latest Evolution news and blues as it is commonly called. BDD pops in the DVD and catches the week in review. He delights when he watches him plant The First Lady of Evolution with the Dreamanator. Her lifeless body laying on the mat. He watches Magdalena and Jinosyde converse with her ignoring him. That was amusing. Jinosyde trying to use her to threaten me. Rookies these days. No respect for anyone. He cocks a slide nod when he see’s HBC and Magdalena conversing and leaving together. He points to the screen for Natalia telling her to watch as he rewinds the clip. Natalia calls immediately setting up a surveillance operation on the both of them. Never can be to careful protecting the most powerful, richest man in the world she thinks to herself. BDD continues watching and watches the new rant from his opponent this week. Talks a lot, but not impressed. Just another pretender, Goldberg, no, not quite, Juventud Guerra wanna-be. Lightning crashes again as BDD rises up and heads outside to the balcony to enjoy the rain crashing down on his head. He looks down on the city below, out toward the Pacific and begins……. ‘You’ve got to be f*ckin’ kidding me… The words came out of BDD’s mouth in disbelief. He wasn’t pissed or anything…maybe a bit annoyed…but confused probably sums it up best. The scratching of his head, instead of the typical scratching of his nuts, backs that up… Dude…*muffle*…are you…*muffle*… surprised? The words were muffled because Natalia was in between bites of that Animal Style four-decker, WITH fried onions, like only one can get at the gourmet joint known as In N Out Burger. Luckily they are in Frisco if you couldn’t tell, then you’re an idiot, so they could hit it up. BDD looked across the horizon, staring at that monitor for a second…then shook it off… You’re right Nat…why the hell should I be surprised? I'm going to walk down to the ring. He's going to walk down to the ring. I'm going to kick his ass and if he's lucky he can limp his pointless self back up the ramp. Juventude has been taking too many drags-OFF the frickin glass pipe! Seriously…he really is. Tell me Nat…when did I just like, magically wake up a pussy? When did I somehow lose all that talent I had when I was wearing multiple titles in every fed I’ve been in, or when I was voted into the Hall of Fame? Well, since you asked- Shut up cockslap…I think this entire fed knows, with the possible exception of that Juventude wanna-be bong toker, that it NEVER happened. And seeing as he, after having done nothing of note in his wrestling career, sees fit to take me lightly, I’m baffled. Natalia, there is a village out there somewhere, and it’s missing it’s IDIOT. I think the crap-for-brains wandered into the Evolution and a match with yours truly. I think he joined up with a couple of little-yellow-school-bus veterans to form a team that thinks they can hang with us. I swear, kicking the shit out of the same group of morons got old LAST year…I don’t see why the jackass who runs this place sees fit to put me through the same shit all over again. I was gonna just go out there, and do what it takes to get the win this Wednesday, but now I think I’ma have to bump it up a notch. Apparently these newfound weak-sucks that are infiltrating the Evo—and this refers to more than just the Heck Shavers—think they are actually in MY league. So I hate to get all pumped and motivated on you Nat, but I’m going to actually take some pride in this match. No, no, I’m not going talk about how bad I want it, or how there is NOTHING he can do to stop me and fake a steroid rage in front of the camera as a pathetic attempt as scaring my opponent. But I AM gonna stretch out for the match- Natalia looks a bit skeptic about that… Maybe even not get baracho the night before… Now Natalia’s so skeptic she’s laughing… And totally slapify the shit outta his janky ass. Nat can buy that… Well that REALLY frickin motivates me…to grab the rental car and head to the liquor store. Wanna brown bag some 40’s? Shit home slice, you know how we do it! And like that, BDD’s hat was sideways, and all was good again. Ain’t it a pisser!!
