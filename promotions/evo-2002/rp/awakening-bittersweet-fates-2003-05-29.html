Awakening - Bittersweet Fates

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-29 There’s a lot of plaster and chips of wall paint on the floor. I can feel them every time I take a step over the carpeting, and I think I’ve got a few cuts down there now. But I don’t care. The pain is too silent for me to give it any attention. I prefer the blinding rage instead. The remains of these materials are all over the floor because of the small holes decorating the walls of my hotel room. The blue walls to be precise. Each of them were the piece of the wall that was standing in front of me during my sudden urge to break something, so they each fell victim to my knuckles. And that’s not all. There was a lamp on the table that looked pretty powerless and feeble, just like the lucky son of a bitch that got me down for the three count a few days ago. The shattered fragments of the frame are sleeping in the tub of the bathroom, some of them still quivering after I heaved the lamp into the wall, not bothering to unplug the damn thing or turning it off. The light bulb, through reasons I’m not going into right now, somehow stayed intact. I figure that’s some foreshadowing for you. It tells me that Thug’s head will remain in once I completely destroy his weak body. There was a moment of calmness in all this insanity. I had just recently punched a good size hole in the wall, about the size of a heart ironically, and then pushed over the small makeshift table so I could sit in the chair. I placed my hands along my legs and stared forward, imagining all the terrible things I could do to make myself feel better. They all involved blood and screams of pain. But after awhile, I realized that my hotel room was just that. Nothing but a God damn room. Sure, I could still feel my back up against the mat, and I could still hear the echo of the ref’s hand slapping the mat thrice, and I could still remember the words of Nas over the boos of the audience in attendance, but all that mattered was vengeance now. And through my moment of clarity, where I could already envision the demise of the man that had caused me all this rage, I realized that I was going to enjoy some of these ideas that ran through my head. Some of them weren’t going to happen in the end, and some were most likely going to disappear anyway with sleep, but I figured that some would come to be. Some of these ideas were just too good to give up. Too good and just too much fun. I ended up punching some more holes in the wall, seeing Thug roC’s face behind them each and every time. The blood in the veins of my wrists bled slightly, stinging sharply with the mixture of sweat and plaster. But I didn’t give two shits about any of that. The awakening he provided me was all too exciting. And now I knew that Thug’s ending would be bittersweet.
