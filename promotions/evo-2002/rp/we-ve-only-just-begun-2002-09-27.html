We've Only Just Begun

27 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/27/02 [The scene opens up in a room, almost looking like a war room. Xander sits at the table, and you can see photos of Daniel Somers, Seara, who has a red X over it, HBC which has a yellow highlight around it, L2J who has a small plastic chair placed over it, Troy Pierce who's picture is untouched, and Big Daddy Dreamer's picture, which has a broken heart drawn on it. Xander looks up from his mess of paraphanalia to see the camera] 'Amazing how much can change in such a short time' Xander begins 'Just over a week ago, many people probably thought they'd see the last of good ol' Xander, by the hands of none other then Malcolm King. I told you all that wouldn't happen.. that King would tap out. And he did just that. Unfortunately we'll all be reminded of him enough, with the former Xavier Citizen wandering the halls.. but I have nothing against him.. yet.' 'But, moving on to important matters' Xander says as you can almost see him glow with pride 'We are on the true verge of something great. For a group that Malcolm King started to destroy EDW, I, along with my new cohorts, and an old friend, will take over eVolution, and have everyone in that locker room paying their respect to all of us. People disregard us, they blow us off as some lame group. Could some lame group break up one of the most popular tag teams in wrestling history? Well, just ask Big Daddy Dreamer how it feels to lose someone so close, you could swear they are a part of you. With the additions of the HeartBreak Chick, and former indy wrestler Scott Frost... we will soon be invincible. No one will be able to stop us. Not Sovereign, not Davidson, not S..... ...and especially not Troy Pierce.. or L 2 J' 'Before I go off on other subjects.. let me address Legends X. It seems that Big Daddy Dreamer decided to get together a few glory hole gladiators and try to do something about our recent acquisition. But, unfortunately for him, he failed in getting anyone special. Where as on my side I'll have Jinx, the only man to ever hold the eVolution Cell Championship proudly, he has Shodan. Then, I have Scott Frost. The most promising independent wrestler of 2002, and who does he have? Sean Mason. Then, lastly, you could say Dreamer is in it for HBC, so, that leaves me, and my opposition is Jason Jameson. Soo.... what's the big deal? I've beaten Dreamer, and been beaten by Dreamer... hell, we even tagged up in an impromptu match once to take down the failed, former Unlimited.... but, now that someone is running it that knows what they're doing.... even though no one really knows who is running the Unlimited.... we will crush all of our competitors. eVolution will be Unlimited.... because with us.. no matter where we go... no matter who we fight. We'll always come out on top, because we...are...unstoppable.' [The scene fades out as the screen fades into the Unlimited logo]
