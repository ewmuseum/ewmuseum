Over the Atlantic

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-11-03 Somewhere over the Atlantic Ocean Aboard Syndicate-1 Evening After a lengthy stay in Moscow and St. Petersburg, Evolution’s favorite and most high-profile tag team and singles wrestlers, Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick are heading back to the states to unleash a war upon the tag team division unlike anything ever seen in the history of the business. These two, involved in some of the most dangerous matches ever, have been formulating certain plans and strategies that is going to bring Evo to its knees. But while the team they really want, ducks them this week, another team on hit-list has been scheduled to face them. So much the better to decimate them now and get it over with, instead of letting them sweat it out for a week or two. All shall be destroyed in due time, but this week… the lucky losers are The Bodysnatchers. Wideload and Havoc, a thorn in the side of The Syndicate for far to long, until this week. It nearly brings a smile to BDD and HBC’s face. Katarina is grinning from ear to ear with what is instored. For now, somewhere above the Atlantic Ocean, cruising at 30,000 feet is the pride and joy of the Syndicate International airfleet. Fully loaded with all the amenities of home, HBC and BDD relax on the long 12 hour flight, discussing the match for the week. Both think back to their previous encounters and how each time, they tried to take out each of them through trick or device. Not this time though, gone is the nice sweet Syndicate team. Sometimes, one needs to go with what brought them to the dance, the ruthless instinct, the devious actions that made them famous or infamous depending on ones point of view. For a period of time, no one wanted to face them, for their careers, and their lives were at stake. Now those days returned. As the PPV states, rising from the ashes, so has The Syndicate been reborn. United again, The HeartBreaker, The Dreamer, Miss Extreme. Oh what a wild-dangerous ride its going to be. The first victims of the new look, old school attitude as previously mentioned is The Bodysnatchers. A team that put HBC in a body bag, a team that assisted The Network in trying to take us out once and for all. Too bad they didn’t get the job done. Instead, they have just royally pissed off the most dangerous female in wrestling and a man who is feared as much as admired. The red and black attack is back, too bad for all of you. Unlimited, Network, Playaz’s Club, S., bring all your factions. Bring all the teams, bring all the federations to stop us. This time, their will be casualties. Starting with Havoc and Wideload. The Heartbreak Chick, The First Lady of Evolution. She is still as seductive, sexy, flirtatious and slutty as ever before. But to those who never figured it out, inside her, beats a heart of pure viciousness. Is her flirtation all an act? A grand plan to lower everyone’s guard before plunging the knife in your back. Who is going to find out the real her, only her partner truly knows HBC. Sure, she has an affinity for revealing sexual outfits that show her ample chest. Sure she likes to wear the shortest microskirts as possible to complement her long legs. The smile, that sweet seductive smile that makes both genders melt in their tracks. Admired, desired, by men and women alike. Yet, in complete control. Like a black widow. Toying with her prey until she is no longer amused. She strikes with her patented ‘Kiss of Death’ Superkick, knocking aside all before her. No one has ever gotten up from it. Not Xander, not S., Frost, Seara, nVc, the list is endless. And its going to continue to grow. Then there is Big Daddy Dreamer. A true veteran whose been in many wars, held countless titles, including the most prestigious of them all, the Universal Championship defeating the most deranged, psychotic man in wrestling, Xavier Citizen. Not afraid to step into any hostile environment, the cage, the hell in the cell cage is what he calls home. 92-4 inside that beast. Gored even through the cell, and still he remains, stronger, devious, dangerous as ever before. Some say he’s immune to pain. He’s been stabbed in the ring, nailed with chairs, run down with cars, hit with leadpipes, crescent wrenches, you name it, its damn near happened to him and still he remains. Wednesday Night, The Syndicate return back into hunt. BodySnatchers are the first, but most certainly won’t be the last. Wednesday night, the legendary antics, the infamous deeds, the unparalleled vicious streak returns. New look, old attitude. The legend continues………..
