New Beginning

19 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-02-19 The room is dark. Nothing can be seen, not even shadows dancing in the darkened corners of the room. A voice rings out shattering the darkness as if it were a floodlight in the murky swamp nights of the everglades. It’s been almost a year, may be 2. Time has been lost, much with my courage and self esteem. All pride I once carried was gone. Everything I am, and ever was…gone. Now is the time to get past it all. To bring myself back into the picture. To force the world to remember my family name. A match is struck, which eerily appears to hover across the room by itself, and towards an old oil lamp on a table. As the match touches the wick a fiery light shatters the darkness, and the outline of a man standing behind the lamp can be seen. He moves to where the lamp is sitting on the table and into the light. He looks a bit rough as if he had not shaved in weeks, nor showered in nearly as long. He scratches his head which the hair dangles from almost caked together from the grit. He grabs a cigarette and strikes a match, he then drops the cigarette to the floor with the match in close pursuit. As he leans in the reflection of Ricky Thunder can be seen in the mans face. Not the Ricky the world remembers, yet one that the world has never known. Ricky: The time is now. I must reform everything I was, everything I am, and everything I want to be. I promised my brother I would carry on his legacy, that the family name would not die with him. Ricky begins to argue with himself, with the demons in his head. “I did my part” Ricky: I must fight to get back into the shape I once was in. I’m not out of shape. I am in my prime! I need to get ready to impress the world. To make them all remember the good that was once associated with the Thunder family name. Shane was a great champion in CCW. I was a great champion in CCW. Now look what I have become! I am merely 22 years old and I am a shell of what I once was! NONSENSE! You are in the best shape of your life. They don’t care about you, why care about them! I MUST! I promised Shane! He is dead Ricky! No. He is only gone in Spirit, as you will be soon. I will get past these demons I have been fighting for the past 2 years. There will be no reason to believe that you will be able to control me any longer. You will no longer force me to pity myself and what I was, and what I could be again! No, you are done Ricky. You are washed up. Forget about it, you may as well pick that cigarette up and give it a light Ricky looks down at the smoke stick on the floor. He slowly starts to reach for it as he closes his eyes. That’s right Ricky, you ain’t shit. NO! I am done! I am through all this. Today is a new beginning for the Thunder name! Ricky stomps his bare foot down on the cigarette on the floor. He gets to his feet and stumbles across the dark room, and feels along the wall for a light switch. He finds the switch and brings life to the room. In the room it appears it has been a slum for the homeless for the past few weeks. There are nothing but beer cans lying all over the place, and cigarette butts lying on everything from the coffee tables to the floor. Ricky heads into the small hallway and over to the kitchen, where he pulls a bag from under the sink. He pops the bag through the air a few times to open it up prior to heading back into the living room. He begins to angrily throw the cans and ashtrays into the bag. He moves like a madman through the room, clearing out all signs of alcohol or smoking. Once done, Ricky grabs all the papers off the coffee table and starts stuffing them into the bag. Suddenly he stops what he is doing, as something catches his eye. Upon closer look, he examines what is a collage of newspaper clippings. “Former CCW Owner, Jimmy “Sylkk” Hargrave dies in DUI incident.” “CCW Champion Shane Thunder passes away from cancer” “Former CCW Wrestler, Chang, in search of liver donor.” Ricky: All alcohol, and tobacco related. Here I was killing myself and not even caring….to hell with this! Ricky stuffs the clippings in the bag and ties up the trash bag. He heads through the kitchen and tosses the bag out the back door. Once re-entering the house, he moves through the lower level to the bathroom. He digs through his medicine cabinet looking for his razor. As he locates it he slaps a thick lather of cream on his face. After about 10 minutes of shaving Ricky’s 2 week growth, is taken to a smooth face. He smiles a bit as he rubs his skin, that was hidden beneath the bushel of facial hair. Ricky’s smile disappears as he touches his filthy hair and he reaches for the scissors. He begins to chop at his hair until it is virtually all gone. He cuts it off, leaving only inches. Ricky then strips down and hops in the shower, where he scrubs the hair that he recently found to be disgusting. The water that rolled from the hair was almost blackened with the dirt. Ricky continues his shower until he is content with his hygiene. He the exits the shower and pulls the hair clippers form the medicine cabinet, and begins chopping at the hair again. Ricky cust off the hair until he gets the appropriate flat top look again. Content Ricky smiles and tosses the stuff on the sink. He moves to the bedroom and throws on some clean clothes and heads outside. Once outside he squints just a bit at the brightness of the light then smiles to himself. Ricky: Today is a totally new day for the Thunder name.
