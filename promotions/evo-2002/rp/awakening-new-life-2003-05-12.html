Awakening - New Life

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-12 Time has dragged on. Few days have passed since my win, and yet I feel nothing. I work out a little and I make sure I’m still in shape, but nothing has really changed. I have been alive for a month or so now, and in that time I have defeated unworthy opponents, had one evening of great sex, and even killed a man for thinking he could blackmail me and get away with it. But still I feel empty. As if God gave me the chance of a lifetime and I just sat back and watch it go by without grasping hold. I have this huge opportunity, yet I have done almost nothing. Sounds kind of weird, I’d assume. But then again, realize that I have just stepped into this life a few weeks ago with a full knowledge of the way it works. You could only wish that you had seen what I have when you came into this world. I realized this as I sat down in my locker room, staring at a television screen as End of Days came to an end. (No pun intended, and a bad one at that). I was alone, wondering where the hell to go from here, and I couldn’t decide. A block in the road seemed to come up from nowhere, and now I was contemplating the future, as unknown and mysterious as it was. I stepped outside hours later, the midnight hour’s chill roaming the streets, waiting for the signal of whatever god they say exists to make itself known. Nothing. The drifting wind. The moonlight basking on the concrete. The sounds of a tired city echoing in the air. But no God. Fuck ‘em. I guess I’ll just have to make the next move. I moved on that evening, driving on through the streets as the sun crawled towards the dawn. Thoughts enveloped my mind, and plans came in. Different ones at first, but then ones that seemed to blend into one complex system of ideas. I had no idea where I was going from here, and no direction. All I wanted to do was prove that they were my puppets, but now I am stuck with no course of action. Simple little task, impossible to gain. You know how hard that is? To realize that you’re existence, as grand as you were led to believe that it would be, ended up being nothing. I was slowly becoming worthless, and I didn’t know if I could pull myself out of this. I guess it was when that fool came along that I opened my eyes. He smacked me in the face and woke me from my dying mood, and gave me life. Guess I’ll have to make him pay for it too.
