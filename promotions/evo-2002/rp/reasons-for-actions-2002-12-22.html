Reasons for Actions

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd December 2002 I think it somehow disappoints him, makes him feel unwanted, unneeded, like I don’t want him there by my side, but things are not like that, not in the least. In fact, they are quite the opposite, but if he knew the truth, he may be scared away. I’m not sure how he would react, if he would take it well or not. I want him to, I would love him to, and it would be so amazing for someone to finally understand how I feel inside.M The scene opens to the inside of a car, Seara is sitting in the front, driving and talking, seemingly to herself, but possibly she is directing it at the camera, either way, she is talking and the camera watching her. Driving down the freeway, she begins to indicate as she sees her slip road; the sign reads Detroit, Michigan. The female wrestler smiles slightly as she reads the name, the city where Scott Frost lives, the city he is currently in, and she is heading to Detroit tonight to see him. The clock reads 1:30 am, and Seara gulps down a warm cup of coffee she had got at the last stop. Her cell phone begins to ring and answering it, the female smiles and seems to warm up as she hears the voice of Frost. Hi you… No (laughing) I’m not at my dads, I’m driving at the moment, on my way somewhere very special… Scott, you’re an idiot at times…why would I have any reason to go there…I’m just driving into Detroit now. Yes, I’m on my way to see you… I know its nearly Christmas day Scott, but I wanted to be with you, my family will always be my family…. Actually dad’s idea for me to drive down here, get you and bring you back for a home cooked family meal for Christmas. Yeah, ok, well look Scott, I’ve gotta go, I’ll be at yours soon. Yeah, bye hun. I love you. Seara’s face seems to brighten up even more when she tells Frost how she feels about him, but she somehow seems dissatisfied with what she has said to him. Reaching forward she slides a cd into the car stereos, and we hear the familiar sounds of Pantera’s Cemetery Gates, a song too frequently played in Seara’s car. The volume is momentarily deafening, but suddenly becomes quieter as Seara turns the control and glances to the passenger seat of the black mustang. Doesn’t sound like I mean it, but I do, I love Scott more than I can express, I would give it all up for him, if he asked, but he understands that this career is more than a career to me, it’s a way of life, a way to control and sort out things in my life that I would rather not have. I’m not sure he understands why I asked out Xander though, and said I didn’t want him out there. Firstly I don’t want anyone but Xander and myself out there, simply because I believe we are both better than that, I am not saying I won’t need the help of Scott or L or Jameson, but I would rather do this myself, if I beat Xander and do it without help, I will know I can do it, if I lose to him, without him having help, I will know he is better than me. No doubt Xander will have Asis and HBC and all his new Unlimited members out there with him, coming to his aid, coming to save their leaders arse, but I think Xander may take these words to mind. Xander, I’m sure you don’t want people to think that you can’t beat a puny little girl like me, come on, I weigh 123lb, and am small and fragile compared to you, you could easily snap me like a twig, and sure you could beat me, just like I have every chance of beating you, but do you really want the rest of Unlimited to come out and cheat so you can win, do you really want the embarrassment of knowing you couldn’t beat a little girl like me on your own. Something like that could ruin you Xander, so why let it happen. I know I want to win or lose this match on my own. If I beat you with the help of my love Scott, how would I ever know what I was capable of? If I am beaten because your team came out and cheated so you could win, how will you ever know if you are better than me? Surely this is your answer match Xander, you are scared by the power I have, Scott is stronger than you, and what better way to get to him by hurting me. He won’t come to the ring if I tell him not to, he respects my decision, but if your cronies come out, be sure that Scott will be there to help, and I am sure L2J and Jameson will come out, I am after all still their team mate, and Scott is still their friend. Do the wise thing Xander, leave your little bitches backstage and come out to our fight alone, and leave the ring alone at the end of the match, prove you are a competent opponent for me, that you can handle me on your own. Stopping the car, Seara climbs out and stretches her legs, she looks at the street sign above her and lights a cigarette. From inside the car, the cd can be heard shuffling to the different track, and Medicine Man starts, mouthing the words, Seara climbs back in the car and shuts the door, quickly placing her hands on the car heating, she sits looking around before turning the key in the ignition again, and pulling away, turning right into Frosts road. Eventually getting to his house, she pulls up and looks in, seemingly unsure about entering. There is a light on, and a curtain pulls back in Frosts front room, the silhouette of the large built male wrestler can be seen against the window, a black shadow that waves at the familiar car parked in his drive. The TV can be seen on in the front room and Frost seems to be watching a live TV feed from somewhere, Seara cannot make out what it is that he is watching though. Turning to the camera she grits her teeth then relaxes a bit before taking a deep breath of her cigarette and blowing it into the camera, as the smoke clear from around the screen, her lips part and she begins to talk once more. Xander, I suppose I shall meet you at the PPV, I know I can handle the wait, but can you? Have a nice Christmas Xander, because you’ll miss new years.<.i> Stepping out the car, Seara slams the door shut and walks up towards Frosts front door which opens before she reaches it, allowing her to walk straight in. the door gently drifts closed, and the scene is left blank and filled with the darkness of the night sky.
