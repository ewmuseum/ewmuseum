Justification.

16 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-16 The fan gushed on bed side table but to little avail. He gazed into the television screen to see that all familiar face. The face his he loathed and despised. The grin he’d seen when he looked in the mirror and saw the devil staring back at him. The very same face he saw as he regained his conscious Sunday Night. The blood had dried into scabs but so had his hatred and disdain for Sean Sterling. He gripped the sheets and hid under, acting as the insignificant Terlayne Sterling, not the cold blooded fighter. Under the bed sheets he was rotting matter. In the wrestling ring he was the object of attention for thousands of onlookers. He’d underestimated Sean Sterling. It was like going twelve rounds with Evander Holyfield and he came out feeling unjustified and low on self esteem. He’d lost and he’d have to face up to it and if it happened again.... It won’t happen again. He’d never come to terms with defeat, it was something he could never handle; the words rang over and over in his mind. The bell rung and rung and wouldn’t stop. Fuck the eVolution. I AM the eVolution. The breeze swept in through the bottom of the door. Terayne gripped the knife from his small, cheap, plywood cupboard. The blade rested against his left wrist, then clattered against the cold, marble floor. Then again, he had too much to live for.
