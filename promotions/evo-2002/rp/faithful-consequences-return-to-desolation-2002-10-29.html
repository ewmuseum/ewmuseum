Faithful Consequences - Return to Desolation

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/29/02 The delightful aftertaste of satisfied bloodlust was still in his mouth. The sense of victory still hung in his mind as he sat down in the airport. Revenge had been exacted, and Badger was no longer a threat. He was sent to eliminate perfection. He had failed. Jason stared out the window, mesmerized in the sky’s light blue appearance. It wasn’t so much the sky that impressed him, but it was just something he could stare at long enough to clear his mind, and give him a change to reorganize his emotions and get everything straight. The month had passed by, from losing a title match to gaining a win over a hated foe. Now that vengeance had been paid, there seemed to be a lack of motivation for him. There seemed to be nothing to strive for. Nothing that he really needed to accomplish, or avenge, or take, or need. In his daze, his eyes closed, and the blueness of the sky was overcome by blackness. His mind fell into the abysses of sleep, and his body relaxed itself. None sound overcame his peaceful doze. Nothing was capable of awaking him as he fell away. His eyes opened, and before him lay a barren desert. The wind pushed through the flatland, its brown surface reflecting the bright sun overhead. The sand was lift into the sky, creating a majestic wave in the air above his head. Jason could feel the warmth of the sun as it shined down against his skin. The light blue sky was now a pinkish blue instead. This world was something far beyond anything he has seen before. Jason scanned the desolate setting, the sound of the wind being his only companion. His hands shadowed the sun away, as his eyes adjusted to the brilliance of its daylight. And as they came to pass upon the area straight ahead of Jason, the figure immerged through the sand. One would expect he’d be surprised, but he wasn’t. It was all the same to him. Just another landscape. Just another dream. Just another chance to shake hands with… “Hello Jay Jay. Long time no see man.” Jason’s voice echoed a sense of both consideration and hatred. These two had shared one interesting past. So many memories. Two souls, but with only one body. And only one mind. “Hey Jason. It’s good to see you too. How long has it been?” “Not long enough.” “Too long in my opinion. We should really meet more often then this. How ‘bout next time we meet, we do it at your place instead?” “I don’t think so Jay. In fact, I don’t think we should really meet that often, if ever.” “Oh, don’t say that Jason. You know as well as I do that we have no choice. Me and you – we’re stuck together in this pile of shit, and sadly there can be no escaping that. I have learned to live with it. You should too.” “And why should I accept you. Why should I just allow…?” “You know what? I’m not in the mood for this conversation again. In fact, you’re flight will be leaving any minute. I just wanted you to know that I am really getting fed up with waiting for you to get what I so rightly disserve. Remember Jason, you wouldn’t have got this far if I wasn’t there for you. I lead you to the Platinum Championship in Era. I lead you back to Evolution. I have lead you this far. And remember this Jason…” The sand below Jason gave way, and fell into a hole that ran into the depths of nothing. Jason reacted quickly, grabbing onto the sides of the hole just before his body was pulled down with gravity. Jay kneeled down, and stared into Jason’s fear filled eyes. “… I can end you.” The sand and the world itself all collapsed, and Jason fell into oblivion. Jay Jay stared at him all the way down. As Jason’s eyes opened, the call for his plane was made. He was on his way to Tennessee. There, he hoped to give Jay just what he wanted.
