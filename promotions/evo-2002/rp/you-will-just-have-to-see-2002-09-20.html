You Will Just Have To See

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/20/02 Where am I? What have I done to disserve…this? What sin have I committed? Have I…? I have lived with misery on my back, beckoning my soul into its own dark path. My feet have bled and oozed with puss from the blisters on my feet, as I have walked this black, hot, paved road. My eyes have shed a million tears, all dried up in the air. I suppose they joined the clouds, only to fall back down to the Earth from which they came. And all that time, my eyes stayed fixed on the horizon, hoping that I would one day feel the setting sun’s warmth hit my face, and realize that I was going to be all right. In the years that have passed, the pain has grown larger and thicker, like a cancer eating away at me. The agony that punishes me for every failing I ever made in life. Stabs me in the back for every mistake that I left unsettled. Strangles me for every breath that I forgot to take. All that pain? It is slowly killing me. I fight my inner urge to spit into your faces. I push back all the impulses that try to force me to rip your heart out. I hold it all in, and I never let it out, only because I am so afraid of what my pain filled heart could do. I fear the day that the rage that builds up inside becomes too much for me to hold in any more. I just don’t know what I will do when that day comes. I have left behind so much, and brought forth so little. I have cursed the world with my every breath, and no one hears a word I say. I am perfection in this world of shit, and all people can acknowledge is the smell of their streets. I have tried so God damn hard to make you understand what scum you are, but all you do is laugh and point and go on with your pointless fucking lives. Maybe I should just sit in the darkness of my own psyche, and watch you all slip into the abyss of nothingness. Maybe my mouth should silence for eternity, so that my eyes can see everything that you are. Maybe I should just stand there and smile, as you all beg for forgiveness as you burn in the depths of hell. But wait. What darkness is this that I have been thrown into? What hell am I apart of right now? What sin could I have possibly committed to disserve this? Only He knows why I’m here. And only I can find my way out. How will I get out of here? You will just have to wait and see, now won’t you?
