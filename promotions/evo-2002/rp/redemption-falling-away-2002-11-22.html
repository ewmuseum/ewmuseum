Redemption Falling Away

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/22/02 Pride was something that came only in small packages, if it ever came. Sometimes, when Jason felt that warmth build up in his heart, he wondered if it was really his feelings, or if it were something else inside of him. Like the hands of the parasite of a man squeezing his spirit, his sweat mixing with the blood that pumped through his ventricles, stinging his veins as it pierced through him. Most of the time, Jason thought it was the latter. His eyes glanced up at the ceiling. He was afraid to fall asleep. The weakness behind slumber was his fear. He had done it once, and Jason was sure that he would try to do it again. Sleep was the farthest thing away from his mind, even though his body lay under the covers and the pillow snuggled against the back of his head. Pride was a sin. But so what. So was everything else that I felt, Jason thought to himself. Is it too much to be happy in ones accomplishments? To be proud of yourself? All I ever wanted to do was hold one positive feeling. To be happy about one thing that I have done. Is that too much to ask for God? Is that too much to ask? All I ever wanted was to be content with something. Anything. My life has turned to shit. My dreams have all been shattered. They lie naked and abandoned, wishing that someone better then me would come around and pick up the pieces. Maybe then they would be given some good use, besides being carried around in my pocket with my few measly dollars and my forgotten engagement ring. How many times do I have to say I’m sorry? How many times do I have to curse your name? How many times must I fall to my knees, looking to the sky for forgiveness? How many tears must fall before I can feel a sense of redemption? How long must I live to be forgiven? Jason seemed to be waiting for a response as he lay there. From God? From Jay? From any voice in ear’s reach? Anyone? Is anyone there? Can someone answer my prayer? Jason closed his eyes, and drifted off into an unmerciful sleep. Jay didn’t come into play that night, but something else occurred instead. A nightmare, that only lasted all night, long into the dawn of the sun. The night terror of night terrors. It was Jason, falling, and not being able to scream. When he awoke, one sentence escaped his lips. “Have I risen this high just to fall now?”
