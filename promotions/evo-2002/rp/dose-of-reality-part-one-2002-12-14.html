Dose of Reality!!! part one.

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They say money is the root of all evil, but I strongly disagree because money is fake and the same with this particular evil spoken about. Money leads to self-empowerment and worst off greed! It’s the one thing people overly strive to posses. Money does make the world go around, but what will bring our end? Most foresee war in our shady future but what kind of war will be fought? This war could be derived from money and natural resources but even comparing someone stabbed in the street for a pocket full of cash or another country declaring threats because of free trade issues over gasoline and oil. Money is Fake! Money means nothing… Greed has taken control of one man so badly he has signed his life away for trying to have something that he will never be capable of having. This man thought he could take it upon himself to invade and steal the show. One who strives for the cash, money, bling persona, he thought he could make himself famous one night with the 'Deadliest Athlete in the Game!' This man is Carl Ca$h, the former fedwars legend and legacy all of his own. Little does he know he’s fighting the fearless legend killer and legacy breaker! This should definitely be one interesting epic... The scene opens within a darkened bar that is closed obviously, the only lighting comes from above the bar and as well a black light sign that reads “Eternity Nightclub”. The nightclub Morpheus had been running before the black mailing occurred that put him back in this deadly sport without any decision. Morpheus can be seen with his backside in view, sitting on a bar stool with his head down. Silence fills the air... From side view Morpheus still has blank expression on his face, all though he is contemplating the ultimate scheme to have his presence shown in eVolution, he tilts the pretty close full glass of whiskey to his mouth and swallows it back. Clearly showing now that he is slightly intoxicated and he slams the glass down on the bar. Morpheus curses to himself while slightly slurring his words “Taking cheap shots and trying to ride his flight back to the top. One of the top cruiserweight wrestlers in the business, he comes nowhere close to my skills and ability and mostly my mind set!” he shakes his head back and forth almost like he is disappointed with the situation. The sound of a door creaking open can be heard from down the dark bar. The slim figure of a woman can be made out as she walks through the darkness and under the black light. The most appealing, sexiest figure can be seen. The lady has black shoulder length hair and the prettiest of china doll type faces. Also baring a piercing under her lip, three small studs in her right eyebrow and a hooked stretcher in both earlobes. This stunning smile comes across her as she stares down to Morpheus, she approaches and stands in front of him on the opposite side of the bar. Moment of hesitation… The unknown attractive woman speaks “I have heard nothing but good rumors about you since entering the ring again. Why do you look so down and glum?” The woman has sound of concern in her beautiful voice. Unflinching and the look not fading from Omega’s face, he looks up slowly and meets eye contact with the woman. Now with his bitter look fading into a trusting one. “Nothing is wrong but contending again does not seem right and like I shouldn’t be doing it! Nothing but mind games, lies and betrayal in that business” Morpheus pauses before continuing “Sky I feel I might be land sliding back over the edge!” he retorts. The woman now known as Sky reaches out to touch his face showing her concern, he jerks back quickly and avoids contact with her hand. The look on her face changing from concerned to very irate. Sky snaps her hand back quickly. The situation becoming slightly hostile, Sky immediately speaks with an angered tone of voice, like she has lack in patience. “If anyone Morpheus? Don’t push me away from your life. I thought you had changed and were better than that? You make me feel like I should have to second guess our strong relationship between us!” Sky looks like the smallest teardrop hits her eye, sulking at the thought of what she spoke was true. She stares into the cold, gray eyes of Morpheus not hesitating from speaking her thoughts that she has on her mind. Morpheus looks up and stares deeply into her eyes. “I have always been fond of you Sky Night… I have trusted you with more money then I know what to do with, my nightclub and most importantly my inner emotions. I don’t open myself to anyone, this time I think you should take fair warning that I think I have lost it again, not fully completely but in the process!” Morpheus shrugs showing look of concern and only caring for the better of Sky. Sky raises her hand and points into the face of Morpheus, she has an emotional vibe coming from her, showing on her face as well. She speaks expressing herself freely “You wouldn’t do anything to ever hurt me! I swear that sometimes I know you better then you know yourself. I will stand by you through anything.” She pauses reading his eyes… Sky continues speaking “Morpheus you finally found something that kept you sane and you feel you have to become the unthinkable, evil man with no guilt or sorrow again? I will not give up on you this easily; just remember my background and where I came from… I found the path of darkness once but now I am your guiding light! Your grasp on reality! I am your last hope or chance of ever finding your real self!” Morpheus looks up with his facial expression changing to a very reassured one, he stares into her eyes not breaking contact the slightest. Morpheus looks shy now knowing how she actually feels for him. Morpheus is left speechless and in shock… A small grin comes over Sky like she feels at ease, knowing she has broken through to him. Sky speaks this time with the sound of excitement in her voice. “Morpheus! Ever since meeting you I have had full trust and faith in you. I am quite twisted myself but that doesn’t mean I have to slide over the edge and lost touch with myself!” she laughs. Morpheus smiles before Sky continues “Back in the business that you once dominated but maybe this time it is meant to be. This could be your break you have always deserved. Black mailed back in the business or not this could be fate! Instead of running wrestling companies to their ruins because of their overly paid roster you left on the current injured lists. You have never gotten your chance to prove yourself, being if it’s the sickest, toughest and most talented technical grappler in the business or the Infinite of Evil himself!” “Cruelest, meanest, and deadliest are words I would prefer!” Morpheus conceitedly cuts in before continuing on. “I will not slip into the dark hole of insanity because of your care and devotion… You may not realize what I am speaking about now but I have seen light! Nothing changes with my lustful addiction to this sport and the way I will participate within it, but personality wise my true self will be known! The twisted Infinite of evil and the personality that’s grown and developed since I have known myself!” The situation without that hostile feeling now, he carries on speaking “Like the last breathe of the Big MO himself and fighting to hold on to that breath. Never letting it go! You cannot expect me to drop this sick persona I have developed myself but between us, I promise you will see the real me. I don’t mix business with pleasure yet you are both at the same time, the only exception. Taking care of business right now will be taking out the infamous Carl Ca$h!” Clearly like he has found the desired path he now chooses to walk. This path is unknown at the present time. Sky reaches out and runs her hand across the jaw line and chin of Morpheus before abruptly turning and breaking eye contact. Morpheus with a grin on his face almost bites his lip as Sky night pulls a bottle of Wisers premium whiskey from under the bar, She pours his glass full and one half full for herself. Morpheus and Sky reach out and barely make contact with each other’s glasses, both of them now smiling. Morpheus downs the full drink and sighs out of relief while Sky takes a normal drink from the glass. Both of them with solid eye contact, like they knew each other’s thoughts better then each knew of their own. The scene fades. For the first time in this saga, Morpheus looks like he has found happiness, like he has found solid reasoning for not sliding over the edge into his own cruel, insanity! Like Sky Night has saved the lives and careers of many around the ‘God of Sleep and Dreams!’ The man is still highly unpredictable and not trusted in the least. Just because he shows his inner emotions to one, doesn’t mean he will not take everything out on someone else. This twisted reality is nightmare to another individual... 'The Rise of one is the fall of another!' To be continued!
