Beginning of the End... III

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'If you are going to play with fire, you are sure to get burned...' Morpheus says with that usual evil grin on his face, he holds his hand over a flame and it moves back and forth; thus making the flame dance. The scene opens with him and Sky Night sitting across from each other with a candle burning in the middle of the table, it provides the only light source. The flames glaring bright off both of their faces which creates a disturbed, evil sense to the whole scenario. 'Then why burn yourself to begin with?' Sky questions with a witty look on her face, she smiles and bites her bottom lip. 'If you can believe and overcome the pain, you can learn to manipulate the intense feeling and the flame itself and become its master. The power of believing... Heh!' Morpheus says confidently. 'You can overcome anything if you choose to and without a doubt believe in yourself one hundred percent' Sky replies to Morpheus. 'Somewhere in the back of Team Network's heads, they know they will fall victim to a prophecy that will prove greater then they can only ever hope to dream of...' Morpheus changes the subject slightly. 'I was watching eVolution promotions when you were out earlier and they actually stated that we were challenging them for their titles... Can you believe the nerves of them stupid fucks?' Sky questions with the obvious, bitter tone in her voice. 'You have to be kidding me...' Morpheus states with with a raised eye brow, he continues 'They won those titles under our noses in the first place and they actually have the audacity to say we are challenging them for something we rightfully won? Bahhhh!' Morpheus nods his head with a sick look on his face and pulls his hand away from the flame. 'Come Legends they will have the rudest of awakenings and feel OUR dose of reality full strength. Who do those jokers think they are?' Sky questions and takes a drink from the glass sitting in front of her on the table, she sets the glass back down following. 'Ignorance is bliss, they have no clue and don't know any better... They are weak minded chumps that are on a huge power trip after gaining possession of those titles at Unbreakable! Network have their heads stuck so far up in the clouds right now, they will be left confused as hell as to what hit them!' Morpheus grins with an amazed look. 'Teach your own or be forced to learn, their options have run into a dead end and Twisted Reality will be the end of the line for them. They boarded the wrong train and the track has just about run out. They can flee and escape now or be destined to suffer our collision!' Sky says with that self-indulged look on her face. 'Tomorrow Night on Legends is only the beginning of the end for them...' Morpheus says and downs the full drink sitting in front of him and he lets out huge sigh of relief. With a strange look on her face, Sky leans over the table and smiles while staring into the eyes of Morpheus; she casually blows the flame out and the scene fades to a small trail of smoke rising betwixt the air itself... The insane laughter of the two is heard loudly.
