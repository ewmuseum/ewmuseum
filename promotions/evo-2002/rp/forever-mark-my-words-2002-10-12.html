Forever Mark My Words....

12 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/12/02 The scene starts inside the Steele family house. Jason Steele has just gotten up from eating dinner with his mother and father who live with him in his house right outside of Toronto. The former baseball player places his plate in the sink as his Maid starts the dishes. Steele tells his parents good night as he walks out the door. Steele exits his home and goes to his car. He drives away into a dark ally where he meets a man in a dark suit as they begin to talk. Man: So are we going to do this? Jason Steele: I am still not so sure about this. Man: Well just give it some more thought... I will give you till Wednesday to come up with an answer. Steele: Ok, fine, I will get back to you then. Now if you don't mind I got some things to do. Man: Alright, talk to you later. Steele: Bye. The man in the dark suit walks away and Steele backs out of the ally and drives away. Steele starts driving away from the scene. He pulls up to a local coffee shop and parks his car. He exits the vehicle and walks into the coffee shop. He sits down and orders cup of coffee. Moments later a beautiful woman walks in and begins looking around and waves at Jason. She comes over and sits at his table and the two begin to start talking. Jason Steele: So how is it going Lisa? Lisa: Pretty good actually. You? Jason Steele: Good now that I finally have my career intact. Lisa: Did you finally get signed to the Blue Jays or something? Jason Steele: Not even close. I became a professional wrestler. Lisa: What? Jason Steele: I know it’s a weird career change but last year after starting at that federation and becoming a star fast guy approached me and offered to help me train for the big leagues. Lisa: Well I haven't seen you on TV or anything. Jason Steele: I was just signed this week. Lisa: Oh, any idea when your first match is. Jason Steele: As a matter a fact I am on my way to find out. If I am on the card, you want a couple of tickets? Lisa: I don't see why not. Jason Steele: I think the show is in New Orleans. Lisa: Oh, but how would I get there? Jason Steele: I will find a way if you really want to go. Lisa: I will talk to my boss and see if I can get Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday off. Jason Steele: Why would you need to have all those days off? Lisa: Thought, maybe we could spend some time together and just hang out. Jason Steele: Sounds like fun, but hey I got to run and see if I am on the card before the office closes. Lisa: Ok, then I will hear from you some time tonight? Jason Steele: Uh....Yeah. Lisa: Bye. Jason Steele: Later. Jason leaves the coffee shop in a hurry to get back to his house so he can call the offices. We catch Steele later that night Sitting in a dark room watching videos Jason stands up and hits stop on the VCR. He walks over and turns on a light. As he returns to his seat he turns on a video camera. You see him sitting back down as he begins to speak. Teamed with a man I know nothing about. Facing a group that is by far better known than me. Should I think we stand a chance? Have we the dignity to form an unbeatable team. I seen him fight once. Did I pay any attention? Unfortunately no I did not. I never knew there would be the slighted bit of a chance that we would be in the same ring together during our times here in eVolution. Though it must have been a little impressive I mean the kid won his first match. Let’s take a look back, I approached the kid the night we were given the card. I said hi and wanted to get to know the guy. One of the things he said to me was: 'Don't mess this up... I can't afford to lose my second match.' What the hell kind of shit is that? Don't mess this up I can't afford to lose my second match. You’re a rookie you have nothing to lose. Just as I. Here we are in our first month with the company and you already want to place the pressure on me. Well kid. I got something to say too. Don't embarrass me! Its not I don't think your capable of winning it’s the fact you want to place the pressure on me, but now I turn the tables and place the pressure on you. Talking about my partner is getting no where near the point. This week the federation is doing nothing but testing the talents of two rookies... Who will move on to gain tag team gold? How will the match be stipulated? Will Jinosyde and Steele make a good tag team connection or will it be shot to hell. We won't find out for four more days. We may only have one chance to see where this tag team will go. What are the odds that these two rookies will make an impact and take the tag titles with in their first couple of months? Shocka and Quasar two former EdW wrestlers that made names for themselves. Shocka, the current eVo Continental Champion has a strong chance of holding two titles. Quasar could take a step into the right direction with the help of Shocka but we will soon find out who will defeat who and who will move one. Mark my words.... win or lose there will be no stopping the 'Canadian Hero'. Steele is the name and putting you to shame is my game. Take a whirl; let’s see who comes out on top. Our future depends on it. If I were the two of you I would pack my bags and have a box of tissues ready because you’re going home to momma with nothing. That’s right, NOTHING! Things change, one day you will be on top and the next your working at a Wendy's making a lousy six bucks an hour. The real world is fucked up like that. The future will only tell where you will go; it will not tell you how hard it will be to get there. The power and the strength you have is up to you. Wednesday night we will see how well your future holds but until then it’s nothing but a dream. The future holds dark secrets to the identity of many. Who shall become the champion of the tag teams is one secret of identity the future holds. Who knows could it be Shocka and Quasar or will the two Americans make fools of the two Canadians. The future could hold the secret of my identity; maybe it holds the fact that I will soon become a champion. The title I may hold is unknown but there is one fact. The fact that this Wednesday night I will hold nothing back. I get a chance to shine and stand in the spotlight going into a tournament match looking for some gold to wrap around my waist. I will do whatever it takes to win and I will do what ever it takes to humiliate the Continental Champion. Will he pin me or will this rookie show the world his bright future by pinning the champion. We will soon find out. That night many things will unfold. Many of you are wondering my standing with Mr. Kelley, are you not? The truth is no one knows. Not I, not he, not anyone. One truth that will be revealed is the fact of if he will be in my corner of if I will be here alone awaiting the chance to take gold. It will all unravel soon, wait and see by tuning in on Wednesday night because; the truth nor the future can be told by anyone but myself. Steele Stands back up and hits the off button on the camera and exits the room.
