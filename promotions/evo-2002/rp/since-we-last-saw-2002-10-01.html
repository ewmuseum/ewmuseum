Since We Last Saw..

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-The Past- Pierce has been battling Big Daddy Dreamer in a grueling match at Digital Destruction. From the crowd comes Cori Simmons who Pierce fights off. On the outside of the ring stands the man called Matt Boone, 'The Natural'. As Pierce turns around to focus back on Big Daddy Dreamer, Boone slides into the ring from the backside of Pierce and locks Pierce into The Natural Causes and Pierce hits the ring with a sickening thud. Simmons slides a chair into the ring to Matt Boone and Boone once again picks up the much smaller DiscoKid and slams Pierce down with the F-5. Pierce's seemingly lifeless body hit the chair with and even more sickening thud this time. Even after all of this Simmons slides back into the ring and picks Pierce up and hit a DDT on the chair. Pierce's head hits the chair and a cut opens on Pierce's forehead. Pierce lays motionless in the center of the ring. Boone and Simmons slide out of the ring and Big Daddy Dreamer slowly makes his way over to the deathly still Pierce. Dreamer locks on the Dreamer Crossface just as the referee slides back into the ring. As the ref starts to check if Pierce wants to submit the former EDW Universal Champion lies motionless. After Pierce's arm hits the mat three times the ref calls for the bell. Dreamer releases the hold and Boone and Simmons once again enter the ring and continue their attack on Pierce. From the back comes Tommy Michaels who enters the ring and starts fighting with Boone and Simmons, but he too is overwhelmed, finally Big Daddy Dreamer steps in and helps Michaels. Simmons and Boone retreat, as Big Daddy Dreamer and Tommy Michaels stand in the ring, the two start trash-talking and Dreamer nails a DDT leaving Michaels laying in the middle of the ring next to the man who he came out to help... -Aftermath- Pierce and Michaels lay in the middle of the ring. Michaels starts to stir, but 'The DiscoKid' still lays motionless. As Michaels starts to get to his feet the eVo Medical Staff start to come down to the ring to check on the fallen Pierce. Michaels stands over Pierce looking at him, but still no motion from Pierce. The staff starts to work on Pierce who is a bloody mess by this time. They place a neck collar on Pierce and slowly slide him onto a backboard. The Medical Staff continue to check on Pierce who is still breathing albeit shallowly. They load Pierce onto a stretcher and slowly wheel him into the back... -Aftermath (part 2)- From the rear of the arena a Cook County Critical Care Ambulance tears away from the arena. Many of the wrestlers are standing in the background with looks of concern on some of their faces. Way in the back stand Simmons and Boone both with grins on their faces... -Aftermath (part 3)- The ambulance arrives at the hospital, and a team of doctors are standing by the doors of the Emergency Room waiting for it. A stretcher is unloaded and laying on it is 'The DiscoKid' Troy Pierce. Pierce still lays motionless, but the EMT's have placed a makeshit splint on his left forearm. Gauze is wrapped around Pierce's head trying to stop the bleeding. Pierce is wheeled into the ER and taken into a trauma room. Doctor #1:'What happened to this guy? Car accident?' EMT #1:'Nope, he was beat by two guys.' Doctor #1:'Damn...' -Aftermath (The Next Day)- Pierce lays motionless in a hospital bed, on a respirator. A nurse comes in and checks on Pierce. Pierce has an external fixator on his left arm (ooc - just for those wondering an external fixator is used for badly broken bones), and still is wearing a neckbrace. The nurse checks Pierce's vital and pulls the bandages away from his forehead checking on the cut. She re-wraps Pierce head carefully, and writes a couple of things in Pierce's chart. As she leaves Pierce momentarily opens his eyes but shuts them just as quick. The nurse shuts the lights off in Pierce's room and shuts the door... -Aftermath (Three Days Later)- Pierce still lays in the same hospital bed, sitting next to his bed is his girlfriend. She rubs Pierce's hand. A Doctor comes in and checks on Pierce. He opens his eyes and shines a light checking to see how much his eyes dilate. He then checks on Pierce forearm and starts to talk his Pierce's girlfriend... Doctor #2:'You know he was very lucky. Another 10 or 15 minutes and he would be in much worse shape than he is now. -To Be Continued-
