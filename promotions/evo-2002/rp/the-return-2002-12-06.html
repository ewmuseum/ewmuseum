The return...

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.6.02 ‘I can’t believe this!’ A voice yells in excitement. ‘I get the chance to dismantle the former Universal champion, Impact and the current Universal champion L2J all in one week! IN one night!’ ~~You see Shocka standing in a suit in front of what looks to be a very nice restaurant perhaps he has a business meeting to attend with the rest of New-TV. Or maybe he has found a nice lady that would like to accompany him…only time will tell. As he walks in the lighting is dimmed and some romantic music is playing he is ushered to a table where a few other men in nice suits sit. He looks over at him with conviction in his face and a bit of fear in his eyes. He slowly opens his mouth to speak but the words don’t come.~~\ ‘What wrong?’ One of the men utters in a deeply frightening voice. ‘Didn’t expect to see us alive?’ ‘I saw you get shot and go down…’ Shocka speaks shaking his head remembering the scene. ‘I could have sworn that you three were all on the same boat to hell…’ ‘Well we are here…here to see you!’ Another man speaks with anger radiating off of his body. ‘You know what you did to your Uncle was wrong…’ The man shakes his head and speaks with a bit of disbelief in his voice. ‘We didn’t think you had it in you kid…none of thought you could come through or would with what you said you were gonna do…’ ‘I am not the one that put him down…’ Shocka says with a loud tone. ‘Yeah but you put the hit on him…’ the man says taking a drink of water. ‘So what do you guys want from me now?’ Shocka questions the men. ‘Well you know you are the #1 Stunna and we are kind of broke…we need a loan before this loan shark comes after us…’ One of the man speaks in a low broken voice. ‘What?’ Shocka asks confused. ‘You are actually asking me for money?’ Shocka shakes his head and speaks. ‘Listen guys I don’t know how much I can help you out this time…I mean wait until after the 22nd I might be able to get a raise if I get the Universal title but I am kind of tight on money right now… ‘ ‘Yeah, we saw what you pulled up in…a brand new Porsche 911…’ One of the man utters in a slightly angry voice. ‘Well ya know what…I didn’t think that I would have to bail your lazy asses out of a bind again this week.’ Shocka says with a bit of spite in his voice. ‘You know what…’ ‘WHAT?’ Shocka questions in an angry voice. ‘If you win this match on Legends…’ The man trails off. ‘if I win it what happens?’ Shocka asks. ‘You’ll find out soon enough…but I’d advise you to calm it down a bit…mr. #1 stunna.’ The man speaks with a sick smirk. ‘Do you think that you are the only numba 1 stunna around here?’ ‘What?’ Shocka asks… ‘you can’t be talking about…’ ‘Oh we are!’ The men all say in unison. ‘But…when are they coming?’ Shocka asks. ‘Depends on when you want the beat down of a lifetime.’ ‘But this week?’ Shocka argues. ‘Would you rather have them debut when you are about to win the Universal title…’ The men state. ‘How about he doesn’t come at all?’ ‘He even has a friend…’
