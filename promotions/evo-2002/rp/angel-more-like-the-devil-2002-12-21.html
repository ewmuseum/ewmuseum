Angel....more like the devil!

21 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-21-02 Lee is in his posing in the mirrow, he notices the camera,then stops but still poses a little to get a good shot for a poster for himself later.' Lee eXodus: Ah! I see you have come to the Thrilla's locker room! Let me introduce myself if you haven't heard of me before,I'm Lee eXodus. The Rock Superstar, Brittney Spears b/f. The man with a billion nicknames, The Hottest Man Alive. The Sex Machine. The Surreal Thrilla, Natural Born Killa! Lee eXodus:Yep, thats me hippin to the hoppin, cus there's no stopping me! At the ppv, I get to fight an angel, which is more like the devil! You know, if you think about it I can show myself to the world that I am the real deal, the five star king! I am the bestest crusier weight around! BESTEST! Thats a word damnit, I tell you at school they told me that isn't a world, but I tell you it is! IT IS! Angel is gonna be nothing but fairy dust when I get done with her, or he, maybe angel is both? Lee eXodus: No really, who is this person? I never heard of he/she, so I can't tell you if I'm better then them. Like lets compare myself with Bobby Stormfigger. I am the man compared to that guy! I mean come on! I'm hotter then him, and I look better in a suit, plus people just like me. Now compare myself with Angel...I am the saint of eVo, but Angel is the devil I tell you what. Lee eXodus: Now, after the ppv who am I gonna shock next? I mean can I get a shot at the High Impact title now? I mean my home dawg Thugster was gonna set that all up, but him and Shocka haft to talk now, all his free time is for SHOCKA! Shocka, Shocka, Shocka! Well, what about me! What about eXodus?! Sure I'm illegal in everystate, but damnit I'm good! Lee eXodus: Shocka, Shocka, Shocka! If its not Shocka, then its Omega..Omega, Omega, Omega! I mean he gets girl like HBC and Seara talking to him...and me I get Justin Fritz talking to me...whats going on here?! I'm getting left out I tell you what. Lee eXodus: Well, when is it my time? When do I get to be the center of attention? I have more nicknames then things Al Gore said he invented, but does that mean anything to anyone? No, I mean Jason Steele gets more attention then me even, I bet Thug's Cat gets more attention then I do..all I ask is for my title shot, really thats all. Lee eXodus: Then you got some scary guy in a hockey mask telling me that I should fear him! DUDE! Halloween was two months ago, god get a life! Then you go scarying people and then saying my name?! Dude, I don't even know you! Why the hell are you after me?! I tell you what, I'm gonna go get me a restraining order...yea that dude aint touchin the 'BESTEST' Lee eXodus...yea how you like them apples? Lee eXodus: To sum this up, Angel sucks, and I'm gonna beat him 1, 4 10 right in that ring! 1, 4, 10! Camera Man: Dude, its 1,2,3...idoit. Lee eXodus: Oh...uh...1,2,3! Yea..I was a little dizzy you know didn't remember..(Blushes) Well, Angel how you like them apples? Well, I'm gonna beat you bad dude! 14...I mean 1,2,3 on that mat! BOOOM SHOCKA LOCKA! (Camera FAdes)
