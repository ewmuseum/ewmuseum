Road to Bronxville 1

18 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-18 “Meh, just about as amusing as Damon O’Halloran’s pale, Irish dick.” Terlayne Sterling had obviously not been amused by the town in which he was standing. He took a quick look around, pacing back and forth on the old, bumpy road. He kicked an empty coke can causing a relatively small building to collapse at his feet. A cameraman stood close by preparing to record Terlayne’s every move. “What the fuck was he thinking, sending me to this shithole. This town is smaller than that Suzanne’s tits, which isn’t very large,” Terlayne continued to mock Damon O’Halloran. The cameraman shook his head disapproving of Terlayne’s actions. “Eh, looks like we got a Code Red 54 now come along.” The cameraman who had been sent by HB45 to record Terlayne’s video-diary of his visit home to Bronxville now looked very confused. “You fat, ugly twat, get a move on,” Terlayne exclaimed as the chubby cameraman’s eyes now develop tears. Terlayne grabs the camera off him. “Hey fatty bum bum, I’ll take it from here. Now run along, I hear there’s a good comic book convention a couple of miles from here.” 1-2 minutes later a frustrated Terlayne smokes a cigarette vigorously as he gazes blankly at the blank screen of the camera. He then has a massive break through and spots what he has done wrong and pops the lid off the top of the camera. The camera then begins to shake and heavy breathing is heard. A group of rebellious teenagers lean against the local supermarket tossing pennies out onto the footpath. The red light on the camera is turned on and Terlayne is seen attempting to strike a deal with the teenagers. He then hands one of the teenagers a twenty dollar bill and they enter the supermarket whilst Terlayne remains outside, sneaking a peak in the window. Terlayne is then seen on his phone, dialling 911. He hangs up the phone and a brown brick is seen thrown through the window of the supermarket, covering the area in shattered glass. As the sirens are heard Terlayne runs from the scene and records from a nearby bush as the teens are cuffed and driven away. Terlayne’s face re-appears surrounded by bushes as he points the camera in his direction as he pants heavily. “Summarising my first number of minutes, huff, I have, huff, learned, huff, that, huff.” Terlayne catches his breath and continues. “That maybe I don’t need Evolution, huff. Those kids have showed me, huff, that maybe a new promotion is in order, huff, Terevolution. The show must go on, huff, I must explore this shit hole.”
