Never Ending Fear.....

12 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/12/02 In a dark room sits a man watching tapes of this last two matches in which he has lost and he discovers mistakes he had made. Both these matches being in different tournements he makes a statement that may begin his reign as a co holder of the tag team titles shortly. He begins speaking as a camera is hidden in the darkness taping his words and movements. 'I don't understand how I could lose these important matches. I can't let my partner down, niether can I let the team down. It is time I step up to the plate and show why I was brought into this federation. It is time I make a stand and wipe the slate clean of these messy losses and propose a new attitude with my new look,' Jason says before he stands up walking away from the television. 'The future of my career rests on this never ending fear that I must learn to face. The fear of losing everything I have trained for and everything I put on the line as I was brought to this federation by a man I ditched to become a member of New-TV to claim tag team gold with my fellow member Shocka. I put my past behind to make a new start, yet I haven't given it my all and I have been punished for it with these damaging loses to people I know I can defeat. A fear of a career ending injury needs to fade. The time has come that I step into the light and make a name for my self in the pro wrestling circuit. Will I succeed or will I fail? The time has come that I anwser this question to my family and friends. The owner of eVo is doubting my ability to acknowledge my potential and take it to the next step. I could lose my life and everything it has brought me. But, is it really worth it just to make a name for myself or is it time I step up and become a man of my word and become a champion as I have stated I will become? Time will not fail me as I will not let it. The time has come that this little boy grows up and becomes a man,' says Jason as he turns on a light to reviel his face to the camera clearly. 'I sit here, thinking about how my fame in Canada has now become shame in the United States. I wonder how I can redeem my fame. Must I show the world that I can trust and make the unexpected happen? Or should I wait until my time is here and I get my first title around this wonderful waist? New TV has made me welcome here in eVolution but they can't do anything for me that I can't do for myself. Now its time that I make my way through the tag tournement with Shocka as we face Jameson and Seara in the Semi Finals. The Unlimited will not know what is coming after we eliminate these two pathetic losers that don't deserve to be in the ring with New TV. The time has come and will never pass by that New TV shows this federation who really rules above all,' Jason states as he walks up to the camera and turns it off. Jennifer Carter is seen entering Jason Steele's hotel room with a Camera crew following. She sits down on the bed awaiting Jason who has gone to take a shower. Minutes later Jason exits the bathroom with only a towl on suprised to see Carter sitting there on his bed. Jason quickly speaks. 'May I ask what the hell you are doing here? How did you get in here?' Jason asked. 'I came here to ask you a few questions about your tag match this week. For your other question, I got the key down stairs,' Jennifer says as she stands up. 'Well, don't just stand there, start asking some questions,' Jason says as he fixes his towel. 'Well, I would like to start off by asking you how you feel about being apart of New-TV and how you think you and Shocka are going to do in your match this week against Jameson and Seara?' Jennifer asks. 'Being apart of a group like New-TV is great. I get to work with the federations President, what could be better? And as far as how I think Shocka and I will do against Jameson and Seara. There is no question, this team will go as far ahead as we possibly can. Shocka and I became a team by mistake and now we are a team that can't be stopped. Yes we will have Thug roC and my brother in our corner but that doesn't mean that we won't win this thing on our own. If these two help us win it was not planned. I mean look at Shocka and I, do we really need the help of Mark and Thug, I don't think so. We are capable of just about anything as a team. The two of us have never worked together before, but let me tell you the training for this match has been brutal and we will not go down with out a fight. I will let you know one thing, and that is the fact that this match will be brutal and it will be AWESOME AS HELL,' Jason answers. 'If you guys win how do you think you will match against the Unlimited?' Jennifer asks. Jason begins to laugh as he replys 'How will we match against The Unlimited? The team is a joke, if they think for one moment they will actually be able to elminate us they have another thing coming. With Thug in our corner and on our side, anything is possible and anything will happen.' 'Let's go back to last week and how you losing to Tommy Micheals may have changed view on this business. Has this loss encouraged you to train harder?' Jennifer asks with a questionable look on her face. 'My losing to Tommy Micheals in the Road To Glory Tournement has only made it a point for myself to work harder and not try and take this business lightly. This is a competitive business and I need to realize this, I can't slack off and I can't be friends with everyone. The losing is over and I will prove that this week when I take one step ahead and help Shocka eliminate the team of scum bags and go on and become tag champs in weeks to come. What may the future hold for Jason Steele and New TV? We will find out soon enough as New-TV makes its debut as a team in the ring,' Jason says after Jennifer's question. 'Well, I will end this line of questioning with this. Do you think you and your brother could become tag team champions any time soon?' Asks Jennifer. 'Well it is going to be tough due to the fact that I can't really wrestle myself in a match, but if the chance comes along there is no doubt in my mind that my brother and I will become tag team champions. We have worked together before but never in a major promotion so the two of us have alot to prove in the tag team division and as a fair warning to the other teams.... Watch out because Canadians are Rising,' Jason says as he turns away and walks to the bathroom. Jennifer calls the camera man over and they begin to walk out the door as Mark Steele comes barging in through the door hitting Jennifer. She grabs her mic and hurries out of the room as Mark Steele looks pissed. And the scene fades to black.
