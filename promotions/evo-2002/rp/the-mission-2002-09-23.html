The Mission

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept 23 Dateline: Toronto International Airport Sept 23, 2002 BDD arrives over to The Syndicate International Free Trade Zone where his personal Gulfstream VI-K is awaiting for him. The fuel trucks have already topped off the tanks, with the itinerary planned out. From Toronto to Barcelona for a refuel, and straight into the heart of Bosnia-Herzegovina. Good thing BDD and Syndicate International has strong business ties with the government, fast tracking the visa required to enter. Otherwise, this trip would never happen, legally of course. BDD enters his plane, hops into his usual plush leather chair and downs a stiff drink of Vodka before settling off for some ZZZ’s since this will be a long flight with a short turn-around time. He never even hears the wheels retract underneath the plane. Several hours later, the Gulfstream VI-K lands in Spain for refueling. Its raining, but nothing to heavily that would deter this flight, however it is raining very heavily where he is going, to the fiery, bombed out pits of hell itself. BDD looks out the window, seeing the green grass that surrounds the tarmac, for he knows, none of this exist anymore where he is going. After thirty minutes, he grabs a quick bite to eat as they taxiway out to the flight deck one more time, leaving civilized world behind them. Where he is going, no order truly exist, just chaos, an anarchist playground. Death, destruction, not a whole much else. Several hours later, they finally see their destination and it is raining heavily. Lightning storms illuminate the sky as they see the bombed out reminisce of the city below. At one time, this was the most beautiful city in all of Europe, now, it looks like Berlin in 1942. Hollowed out, on fire, smoke still rising to the sky, nothing like when he was first here some 10 years ago. The Olympics were in town, and this was declared a UNESCO site. Funny how a decade of hate can change things. Now, no sane person comes here anymore. Those that do, leave in a box. The pilot announces they received the all-clear signal and begin their final approach toward taxiway 4A. Fine pilots they are, BDD personally hired them from the RAF. Each has flown hundreds of hours in combat missions, all ex-special ops. Good thing too, because somehow BDD thinks he might need just that. Within a few minutes, the plane rolls toward a protective military type hanger. Members of the Bosnian Army are standing guard saluting as they enter. The Gulfstream opens its doors as a jeep pulls up with five members in it. All of them looking very serious, very armed. One in the back is clearly a female, with her blond hair flowing underneath her black baseball cap. It is then, BDD notices she too is also packing heat by her right hip. She steps out, salutes the two pilots and looks at BDD. ‘You’ve picked an interesting time to come to our country. The Serbians are pressing again making the city unsafe and our jobs most difficult. I’m Natalia, assigned to the Canadian Government Security Forces. All the while you are here, you will have an escort of five people, no exceptions. The locals don’t care who they shoot at nowadays. The UN Mission has already pulled out, leaving us to pick up the pieces persay. I lost two already this morning due to sniper fire, I’ll be dammed if I lose another, especially a visiting dignitary like yourself .I don’t know the purpose of why you’re here, they didn’t tell me, but I hope you will, especially since I’m one of your escorts. I don’t like taking a bullet for reasons I don’t know of.’ ‘I’ll tell you in time Natalia, but for right now, I’d like to get cleaned up alittle bit, then we can go and I’ll soon be out of your hair.’ ‘Fair enough…SARGENT!! Escort this man over to the VIP area!’ “YES MA’AM!” The solider escorts BDD over to the VIP area and stands outside of the small facility. BDD hops in the shower and freshens up, suddenly foot steps are heard as Natalia walks over by BDD, looking at him, sizing him up from head to toe. She smiles, evidently she liked what she saw as she tosses him a towel and sits down on the bench. ‘Your pretty well built, serve time in the Army?’ ‘No, athlete in college, but now I have a multitude of things that keep me busy. Running a international conglomerate and competiting in the ring.’ ‘Ring, you box? Martial Arts?’ ‘I do those, no, a wrestling ring.’ ‘Wrestling? Great, I’m babysitting a wrestler. I thought you were some ambassador or something.’ ‘I occasionally have diplomatic privileges with my government, but I can’t talk about those. I hope you understand.’ ‘I do. Three rules while you’re here. 1. Always listen to me, I’ll keep you alive. 2. Always keep your head down and your wits about. 3. Always wear this.’ Natalia tosses him a bag and inside is a bulletproof vest. The heavy kind. ‘I think you can handle something like that. Failure of those rules and you go home in a box, am I clear.’ ‘Yes ma’am.’ ‘Good, get ready you don’t want to be in the city after sunset. All hell breaks loose then.’ ‘Copy that, yeah, you might want to give me one of those as well…….(pointing over to the .45 laying by her)’ ‘Think you can handle it?’ ‘You’d be surprised as too what I can do……’ Natalia flashes him a smile once again. She really is quite pretty, and something also familiar about her as well, just he can’t place it. Natalia leaves as BDD finishes putting on his gear and when he steps out, the jeep is all ready with the passenger side open. Natalia driving and someone manning the .50 cal machine gun turret in the back. BDD hops in the jeep, as Natalia hands him the .45. BDD loads it deftly as they watched, mildly impressed. ‘Were too?’ ‘Here……..’ ‘Your kidding right?’ ‘No…..its important, for me and for my country.’ ‘Shit! Igor, got your body armor on, we got to go through Sniper Alley. I hope it worth it D!’ The Jeep pulls out of the hanger, accelerating as they go as another explosion is heard in the background. ‘By the way, welcome to Sarajevo!’
