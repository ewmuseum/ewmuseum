A World of Nothing Act 6 - Finale

22 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-22 You wake up in your bed the next day wondering if you're alive or dead. Wondering what exactly last night was. Wondering if it was a dream or reality. Wondering if Samantha, Sam, Vera existed or not. Wondering, but never coming to a true conclusion. Wondering, but never really knowing the truth. Me? I took it all in as a dream. That was the easiest thing to accept. After I pushed the trigger, I don't remember a thing. It was as if there was a giant hole in my life suddenly. There was no sign of the gun. There was no sign of blood on the carpet or brains splattered on the wall. There was no indication of police breaking through my front door. There was no yellow tape wrapped around my residence. There as no white tape outlining where my dead body laid. There were no forensic investigators running amok. Just me. Alone in my-- Not alone. There was warmth beside me. There was another body. A smooth, perfect body. For a moment, I felt nervous again. But only for a moment. That moment gave me the time to pull the cover away to see Samantha, Sam, Vera laying beside me with a content look on her face. Suddenly, I felt more confused than I had before. Suddenly, I didn't understand what was going on. Suddenly, her eyes opened and she gave a bright smile. A smile I hadn't seen on her face at the strip club. And out of her mouth escaped two words and two words alone. 'Good morning.' --Finish Series.
