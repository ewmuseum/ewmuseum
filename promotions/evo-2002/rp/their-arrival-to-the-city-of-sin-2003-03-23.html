Their arrival to the city of sin.

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~A camera opens at the airport in Las Vegas. Evolution camera's are waiting As on time out walks the duo of Team Network. Jason Relms walks out infront of the camera basicaly pushing High Impact to the side. Jason Relms smiles and quickly begins to speak.~ Jason Relms:'I knew you boyz would be dieing to have a few words with Team Network. I mean after all everything that I predicted as came true.I took those Playaz inc. boyz and sat them down like I said I would. I went in their and singl handedly was the reason that arena was packed from head to toe. I mean it is looking like that once again eVo is being shown to everyone but because of Jason Relms.' High Impact:'Jason and I knew that this place couldn't handle us. We knew that Team Network would lead this new era of wrestling as the roster of eVo would go down in flames. And now they stick us in a tag rumble. Evo has the nerve to put us in the ring with what it considers the best tag talent in this place. Jason, how stupid can these people be?' Jason Relms:'Obviously they are not college graduates. I mean what pathetic peons would ever, EVER ask that their TAG ROSTER BE WIPED out. Because if you think that Jason Relms and High Impact will not go into that arena which stands in the poorest excuse of a city than any others in our nation, and not cripple each man. And not set yet more examples of what Team Network is capleable of. It is the city of sin, and well wrath is Jason Relms weakness it's just too damn sad their has to be so many other poor peons that will be left behind wounded in this unforgiven revolution.' High Impact:'You said it Jason, So far we have started off with a W and damn it, it must stay like that for good. Team Network, is not a bunch of loosers lile eVo fans. We made something of outselves, it's called athletes with no prisoners. Socity's punishment is starting, example number one was Playaz Inc..Now two will be..Tag Team Rumble..Because I am ...HALF MAN..HALF AMAZING!' Jason Relms:'Damn, can eVo ever have a limo waiting for us. After all we are single handly saving this so called 'Franchise' of wrestling. I mean it's time respect is paid to the wrestlers who we know will be LEGENDS in this game when it's all said and done. Who know that when the lights are turned off that everyone will be still remembering of their hero's failing in another triumphet battle for the bad guyz.I mean was last legends not a good enough showing. Do you people insist that the fall of your happiness comes sooner than even I have hoped for. My power should not amaze you as by now it needs to be aspected. My mastermind plan is not one of a genious just one of a god. I live to see this shit. I love wrestling and I love destorying. What am I destorying? Well let's see, I am going through the tag divison taking out each and everyone of the pathetic peons that are sadly even in the same divison as I. I am destroying the 'old' style of wrestlling. I am proving that bute strength is all you need. I am proving that the attitude no prisoner's will soon be placed in the guidline of wrestling. I am here destroying eVo. How you ask again? Well the truth be told I bring more glamour and spotlight media to this federation than any one man or group could ever do. But what I do will never be repeated. As while I bring in the fans you will slowly see that the path I leave behind is one of a dieing eVo roster.' Jason Relms:'I ask you how good is Jason relms?I ask each member of eVo do you believe you could go toe to toe with THE FN NETWORK himself. Wednesday night the tag divison see's how Team Network's army is. How this war will be one that leaves a lasting impression on any tag match or rumble any man will see. I told you before I ever stepped foot in a eVo ring and I must remind you now, that when Team Network stepped foot in eVo the bar was being raised. And come wednesday night it's being raised just that much more. Life couldn't be anymore worse for you fellas. As you must of realized by now I am sure that you be in the ring with the beast itself...and the beast is being released...' Jason Relms:' Well I am sure we will speak some time tommarrow as I have a had a long flight and I want to go to the hotel room to rest up. But I want to leave all those pathetic peons with something to think about. Right now, I ahve told you what I was going to do, and yet I have not failed or lied about one thing. So what will I say about Wednesday, hmm, It will be a night to remember, I swear...BECAUSE yeas folks..these words were brought by the greatest to walk the earth..THE FN NETWORK! ~The two jump in the black hummer and leave.~
