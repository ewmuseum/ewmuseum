Comparison To A Society

13 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-01-13 To most people, waking up was the beginning of another day. They’d get up, get dressed, have breakfast, kiss a loved one goodbye and drive away from their house and family on the way to a job they hate but will never leave. Life couldn’t be more the opposite for him. He sat up on the floor of whatever abandoned building it was he was using as a house, in whichever city it was he was “visiting”. Wearing the same dirt and shit covered black clothes that he’d been wearing for what seemed like his life, he opened his eyes to the déjà vu surroundings. This was his life. He enjoyed it. There was no light shining in through the muck covered windows, but that was just the way his life was. Light didn’t seem to follow him anywhere that he went. It was night though. To him, waking up wasn’t the beginning to another day. In this case it was night. But his life certainly wasn’t the American dream like most believed theirs was. His chance of that was ripped away from him at childhood, but he didn’t care anymore. The way he saw it, he had all that he wanted and just recently he’d gotten something that had made things just at little bit better. Six months ago he wouldn’t have cared about winning the Road To Glory Tournament, he wouldn’t have cared that he was now the number one contender to the most prized asset in his profession. He did now though. He had that number one contender spot, he had the chance to face the Universal Champion. And the chance to beat him. Not for the glory, the pride, the chance to call himself the best in the business, none of that. To take it away from them. To take it away from all those that wanted it, that were in the business for nothing but the Universal Title. He didn’t want it that badly, that wasn’t the reason why he got into wrestling. But to get the chance to take it away from them, that was what drove him towards it now. He wanted everybody to know that he didn’t give a fuck that he was “champion” but that he’d defend the title to his death anyway, just so they couldn’t have it. And he knew it would piss the fans off as well. Another good thing about the situation. Maybe he was thinking too soon though. He didn’t have the title yet, he still had to “earn” that. Well he’d “earned” his shot at the title, he’d certainly “earn” the right to have won the match when the time came. He didn’t demolish three people in the Tournament for nothing. And the champion. The mighty James Kimson, the man all the fans loved to cheer for, loved to stand up and sing praises for, the man they embraced as one of their own. He was the man that would start Asis on his journey, the man that would give Asis the opportunity to take the belt away from everyone. There was no real personal hatred there. No actual reason why Asis hated Kimson as a man. The fans cheered for him and showed him respect, despite the fact that he was a constructed champion just like the rest sure enough, but that wasn’t his fault. He did what he was told just like all the others that wanted to make it big, that wanted to have the fans adore them. It wasn’t his fault that the fans were told to cheer for him. He hated Kimson none the less. He was looking forward to being able to show how much he hated him, how he hated the fakeness of it all, how he got paid to be a different person that who he was, whilst Asis was himself and was called a “freak” for it. His personal malice was going to burn upon Kimson’s flesh. And there was only a few days left until then.
