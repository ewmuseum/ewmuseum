The Downward Spiral 6: Search for Justification.

29 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-29 The Downward Spiral 6: Search for Justification. I had no fears about the oncoming colliding of emotional trains. Two adjectives alone plagued my very existence; the alley was dark and empty. Never had I known that my emotional carousel would end up in such bizarre surroundings. I had, however, been prepared for all those distant quarrels that life was set to throw at me. My rasped voice muttered senseless words of hatred and despair. For my sins had been committed, the time had come to leave them behind me. Beyond those ghost’s and monsters of my sins that threw words at me, other monster’s existed in that alleyway. Beyond the filth in this alleyway were monster’s of my conscience that had been playing my mind for quite some time now. Or had I been letting my sub-conscious conscious get the better of me once more? I had let all shreds of humanity out of my grasp but still held onto that little shred of dignity. I wanted justification, desired all my actions to be justified. Blast that infernal racquet, plaguing my mind a constant reminder of something I never wanted to be. The ink coloured sky filament with a host of tiny, golden dots bringing some light to the loneliness of my life. The small escape at the end of my this alley was closed by my disdain for those who still had their humanity. The stars cascaded, as I listened to the voice that called on to me constantly, that voice being my conscience. Perhaps it’d all been a dream? I searched for reality, the hard, coarse wood sticking like a dagger into my stomach provided all the reality I needed. I fell limp onto the hard gravel, sending a line of brown dirt along the side of my naked body. I felt the moon laughing at my calls for help; I felt it whispering words of disdain for my very being. I listened to my conscience for a moment. Nothing. My killing had left a sour taste in my mouth. Oh, the extent of that sin had been far greater than ever imaginable. Still my monsters would not go away; still they called onto my accusations of a serious nature. They questioned my very being, they taunted me. They accused me of being what I hated. I sought to listen to them. Like a knife through my soul each one of their accusations hit me. My hopes to seek two new adjectives in my life had been overcome by their insults. I continued to search through the alleyway, ravaging through rubbish cans for whatever I could find. What had I been hoping to find? I was living far from reality. My hunger stricken life had taken another blow down the downward spiral. Oh, how I felt their insults and craved some light. Their accusations continued, each sending its own stab wound through my chest. Each one, wounding me with wounds which I had felt before. Why won’t they stop? Why won’t they go away? Then it struck me… What they had said was untrue. For I was just happy.
