Tortured in past, Kidnapped in present

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: december 1st 2002 This past week on Legends, things took a different direction from their usual flow of things. At the start of the night, male wrestler Scott Frost found himself waiting for a long time in the car park of the Skydome in Toronto Canada, feeling the extreme cold temperatures as he waited for female wrestler and fellow team-mate Seara, after letting him think she would never show up, she made her appearance, sneaking up on him and surprising him with her quiet unexpected words. Later that night, Seara was left alone in the Notorious dressing room, where she was attacked with a steel chair across the head. At first crowd members thought the attacker was someone she had faced before, someone she knew well, someone who had once been there for her, Asis, a giant man who was known for his unexpected behaviour, and in the past unexpected violence toward the female. Later on, the three other Notorious members found the female, and while tending to her, were tempted away by a masked figure, after their exit from the room, another figure walked in, swept up Seara in his arms and ran out the room with her. It wasn’t long until Shocka entered the ring to admit to the kidnapping of Seara, with a plan in mind he told of what he had done, and why he had done it. Unfortunately for Shocka, Notorious members Scott Frost, L2J and Jason Jameson found Morpheus Omega where he was holding Seara in his arms for the camera to display the view onto the eVotron, and against the will and want of Shocka, took their team mate back. You get to thinking why they feel the need to be so fucking stupid. You don’t mess with the Notorious, especially if your names are Shocka and Morpheus Omega. I never really had a problem with you guys before now, I mean Morpheus Omega, you I did not like at first anyway, but now you have annoyed me even more. I mean did Shocka honestly think that with your help in kidnapping me, and holding me, he would honestly get what he wanted, unless what he wanted was for you Morpheus to get your ass kicked, and to meet us in the ring at Legends XVII, unless that was what he wanted all along, then he failed himself, and you Morpheus, you failed him. You couldn’t even keep me hostage, hold me, and stop my teammates getting me, taking me back to where I belong. I am but one person Omega, 200lb lighter than you, and unconscious, but still, you never managed to keep a hold of me for long did you. I hate to think what you would be like against me in the ring if you cannot even control what happens to me when I am unconscious. For the first time since the camera came into focus on the female wrestler, it pans back away from her face, she is sitting on a bench in the middle of a street, but it unsure where she actually is. Sitting perfectly still she looks hard at the camera, as though she is in a daze. It hit me, hard, right around the back of the head, part of it caught me on the neck, that’s the part that really hurts, the part that gets your brain going in overdrive, you try to imagine when you felt that before, not in the ring. Not many can say they can, not many know the feeling I had, trapped in that car, trapped and watching her die. It suddenly becomes clear Seara is thinking about her mother’s death, but that she has also linked it to the pain she felt at Legends XVI as the chair shot to the back of her head knocked her both to the floor and unconscious. Funny that, funny how you think of things that don’t normally occupy your mind when you are in that sort of situation. Pausing the female lights up a cigarette and takes her cell phone out of her pocket, noticing that she has a missed call, she places it back in her pocket and looks down at her feet. I’ll deal with that later. The words are a mere mutter, and seem to be a reminder and personal note to check the call and sort it out, rather than a comment to the camera. Looking up the female notices a woman walk past with three children, two small boys, and a girl, she smiles slightly and whispers the words happy birthday mommy, happy birthday for all the times I have missed. It is not the anniversary of her mother’s birthday, but Seara is clarifying something for herself, making herself that bit more human inside, acknowledging that she still loves her mother deeply, more than any other human being that has ever entered her life, more than any human that is in her life. I miss you is muttered again and again under her breath and the female watches as the young mother across the street tends to one of her children, crying after they fell over. Watching hard she barely notices as a car passes in front of her view, it parks a few yards down the street and a large male figure climbs out and walks towards the woman and her children, placing his arms around her he kisses her and looks lovingly into her eyes. He is great mom, treats me real good, like you always wanted, just like you would want me to be with, wish you could meet him, wish you could be here to see him, to know him. It is not completely clear if Seara is talking about Scott Frost or another of the many males she knows. But the cameraman decides to go with his first guess of Scott Frost. Turning her head to the sky she sighs and smiles to herself, as if sharing some joke that no one else can hear. I remember it, she told me that we would be there in time to go see daddy before his match, we wouldn’t have that long with him though, then we would go out front to our seats, and I would get to see my daddy take on the biggest man I had ever seen in my life. The Eliminator, he was just over 7” tall and well over 400lb, I had no clear guess at his weight, but I knew it was the most important fight in daddies career. I had leaned over to put a tape in the tape player; mommy had made a tape of her favourite Metallica songs, mommy and daddy were huge Metallica fans, daddy still is. Next thing I knew, mommy gasped and I looked up from the front seat to see a huge car pile up, my little brother was in the back and he screamed cause of the sight of all the blood on the road and the guy in the central lane with a broken neck, his face was the wrong way around, it was facing his back, and Bruce screamed, I remember Jake covered his mouth, but he was trying hard not to scream as well. The shock was all that I could feel, I just sat there staring, but it all happened so quick that I never even noticed myself starting to scream or cry. I just did it, next second the car rose into the air, mommy was screaming so loud and I could only think that we were all dead. That was fear, death is fear, it is the horror I carry in me everyday of my life. Closing my eyes to see mommies face. She looked so scared and she needed me, the car was the right way up, but we had flipped about 5 times and hit off the hood, the car was crushed and I couldn’t move. I could just reach mommies hand to hold it. Jake was in the back he had come out of his seat belt, not sure how he was still alive, but he managed to get to Bruce, he was holding him in his arms. My family was all that mattered to me then. But mommy was the only one I could see; I could hear Jake and Bruce, it was horrible. Mommy cried, she tried to move something but I started screaming, I remember looking down and there was this huge bit of metal in my stomach, there was blood everywhere, it didn’t seem real, seemed like it was in the wrong place. Mommy told me it would be all right, I knew that meant something was wrong and it wouldn’t be all right. Mommy always told me it would be all right when something was wrong, and this time it seemed worse. I looked in her eyes and she looked so scared, I thought I would die, but mommy had been talking about herself. I held her hand so tight that she told me I would break all the bones in it, I couldn’t laugh, normally I would have, but not then, I knew mommy was hurt, I was so scared, I couldn’t lose my mommy. My only friend, my love, my family, mommy was all I wanted in life, mommy would have been better if I had helped her, but I hadn’t moved to help her. I know I was trapped but I could have done something. I didn’t matter what happened to me, I shouldn’t have been so selfish, mommy needed me and I just held her hand and said I loved her and watched her die. As the tears roll down the female’s cheek, she clicks back into reality. Looking at the camera she feels the need to move away or explain her actions, but she does neither. Instead she lights another cigarette to replace the one that just burnt away between her fingers. Placing the other cigarette between her lips she inhales deeply then looks once more at the camera. New-TV, you brought these thoughts to the fore front of my mind with your attack with the steel chair, it brought back the feeling I had as my head hit the back of the seat in my mothers car, and now when you face me in the ring, you will face the side of Seara that was first seen around here in eVo, the monster I poses inside me. I will release what you have unlocked. This week Legends XVII, you will wish you had never attempted that kidnap on me, you will wish you never disturbed the dormant demon I carry within. The scene fades out as Seara inhales deeply on her cigarette and stares into a void of her own thoughts.
