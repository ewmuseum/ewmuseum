A Time Full of Emotion

3 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-03 'Incomplete....' The voice of Scott Frost whispers. The scene opens up and Scott Frost is sitting on the edge of the very bed that he shares with his wife Katherine, also known as Seara in the wrestling world. Scott sits there pondering everything. He falls backwards laying down on the bed with his feet easily planted on the floor. 'So how after everything that has happened. I still feel, incomplete.' Frost again says to himself. Scott lays there staring at the ceiling fan that rotates over the room. His glare seems to pierce the nothingness that fills the atmosphere. He sits there thinking about his life, about his career, about his wife. 'I've done it all. I've accomplished goals in less than a year than anyone else has in my career. I am a multiple time champion. I am one of the most respected wrestlers by the fans. My wife is loving and cares for me in only a way that I actually understand.' Scott thinks as he continues to glare into the vast void that fills his room. Scott turns his head, and looks. The camera pans over and you can see Seara in the other room crouching down still in her blank state of mind, and then the camera pans back to Scott. 'Even together in the ring, and in the backstage. It seems we miss something.' Scott thinks as he now stares at his wife in the distance. 'What has over come her? Her hatred for The Heartbreak Chick has taken it's toll. She has brought it into her personal life. Something a wrestler is asked to never do....' Scott continues to think. Scott blinks slowly, and then stares back up at the ceiling fan and he opens his mouth as if he was to begin speaking. 'I hate the Syndicate. They think that they are the best team in the leauge. I hate them for thinking they are better than me. Better than us. But still, they do hold the Tag team championships. Something that me and Seara set our sights on when deciding to finally get married.' Scott slowly says between sentences. Scott deeply looks at the fan as it rotates and his eyes seem to be fixated on the blades spinning faster and faster. 'Soveriegn state.' Scott says. 'How can that help me?' Scott asks himself. 'How can I use that stipulation to my advanage? How?' Scott continues to question himself. Turning his head and focusing his attention on his wife again. Scott Stares at her and thinks. What is going through his mind only he will ever know. Scott has had his fair share of heartaches and hardships. He can relate to his wife in a lot of ways. They have always seemed perfect for one another. Scott grins a little and stares at his wife. 'Soveriegn state........twenty four, seven.........Soveriegn state..........' Scott says slowly. As his voice trails off his body slowly slips into a deep sleep. The sounds of foot steps is heard, and the camera slowly pans to the ceiling so you can see Scott laying on the bed as if the camera was a bird. Seara walks into the room and curls up next to him and they falls asleep deeply with Seara draping her arm over the chest of Scott. The scene fades.
