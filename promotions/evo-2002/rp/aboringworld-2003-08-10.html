aboringworld.

10 August 2003

Date: 2003-08-10 I won. Holy hell. I won. It's 5 A.M. and the sky is beautiful. Up and away from this room and this bed and the oppressive inevitability of sleep. I've been up all night with a bloody migraine. Ever had a migraine? No? Pray you never do. Looking out at the sky? I'm wondering if God's up there tonight. There's really no sign of heaven out there, but the only reason I'm wondering about heaven and God and angels is because it must have been divine intervention that I won. Right? A miracle and all that nice bullshit... yeah right. It's 5:02 A.M. and the man next door is taking his Poodle out for it's daily walk. How anyone can wake up at 5 A.M. every morning to walk an animal that eats it's own shit around the block is beyond me. I mean, I have a reason to be up. Insomnia. Ever had insomnia? No? Pray you never do. Every morning, I watch the man walk take the dog out for it's walk. The man wears a different color for every day of the week. Red on Monday. Blue on Tuesday. Yellow on Wednesday. You get the point. Never black though. And for some reason? Even though I've never talked to the man, I always get this huge urge to stab a bloody spork into his head. They say television causes violent thoughts. When you don't have a television, what causes violence then? Some people call it morbid, but I often wonder what it'd feel like to kill someone. To hold a life in the palm of your hands and then... crush it. Simply crush it and send it away. It's funny. If a thief broke into my house and held a gun to my head right now, and he happened to ask me what I would have liked to have done before I died... I would have to answer that the one thing I'd want to do would be to kill someone. Then bang. A bullet hole through my head. Flakes of my brain and skull shooting out the back. My neighbor upstairs is just waking up. It's around 5:30 A.M. now and she's probably planning to go out for her weekly coffee enema. My neighbor downstairs is still asleep, but his snoring could wake the dead. My neighbor to the right, as said, has gone out to walk his dog. And my neighbor to the left...? I've never met my neighbor to the left, but she or he or it is awake too. Just like me. My baggage sits by the door. It's 5:45 A.M. and I catch a flight to Newcastle, England at 7:00A.M. And thus, I stand up. I grab my baggage. I flip off the light switch. Goodbye to my wonderful apartment... and then I leave. It's a very, very boring world.
