Date: 2003-06-17 -WHAP!- Right cross and he danced back out fists held high, up near his ebony cheekbones -WHAP!- -WHAP!- -WHAP!- A short combo: two straight jabs followed by a high roundhouse kick. Deb had seen all of these moves a million times. She saw how he danced through the sticky St. Paul night eyes trained on nothing but the bedraggled punching bag that hung in the center of their apartment’s studio room. His Quicksilver board shorts red in the daylight, black in the early morning light filtering through the windows make soft swishing noises as his feet move silently across the hardwood floor. Khadafi steps back and let’s fly with another lightning quick combo, his hands flying. She listens as his hardened knuckles stitch the sides of the punching bag at a speed so fast that she can’t even see them strike the bag. The only way she knows that Khadafi had hit the bag were the sounds of the punches and the indentations that his fists made. Deb watched this display for another few minutes, her clear blue eyes never leaving the map of bruises that Ali Khadafi called a face. Her heart had sunk when she saw what had happened to him on the last Legends. How the wrestler he had beaten the week previous had marched down to the cell and attacked him. How he had tried to choke Ali with that chain, which she could see the necklace of discolored marks that ringed his neck as he stepped in to batter the punching bag with a bomber of a left hand. She watched and silently worried.
