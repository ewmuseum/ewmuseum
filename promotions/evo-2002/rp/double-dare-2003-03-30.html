Double Dare

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th March 2003 At Legends XXX Asis made a bold challenge to anyone that dared, to try and take his Continental Title from him at Unbreakable. Xander did not like this and decided to tell Asis what he thought about it later on during the night backstage. Also, Daniel Somers over ruled Asis by choosing an opponent for him – none other than Erin Christoph. The obvious plan is that the Continental Title will therefore stay in Unlimited no matter what happens. But Asis is no too happy about it… They don’t trust me! Either of them! Haven’t I proven myself to them? Don’t they think I can handle myself? Asis can be seen in his private locker room, mere minutes before Unbreakable begins, throwing things around, punching his locker, generally letting off some steam. They want to keep our Continental Title in Unlimited. I didn’t see any of them do a damn thing to get this belt. And I haven’t seen them do anything to try and keep it since. I destroyed Dreamer in the cell, I retired Pierce, I crippled him, why the fuck can’t they trust me?! I can take out anyone on this roster, including anyone within Unlimited and I swear right now if any one tries to interfere in this match I will do to them exactly what I did to Pierce. I’ve had enough of not getting respect off people around here; I’ve had enough of being thought of as just the enforcer of Unlimited. And tonight I will prove it to the fans, to the wrestlers and especially to Xander and Somers. I am an individual within this stable and despite what Xander likes to think of himself I’m the driving force behind it. If I weren’t around Unlimited would have fallen into disarray long ago. Without me around to protect them all there wouldn’t be an Unlimited. Maybe they should think about that next time before they turn on me. And Erin, oh poor Erin. He’s been put into a match tonight that he really didn’t need. Regardless of the fact that he’s got a match before he faces me, I will take him apart. Regardless of the fact that he is a fellow Unlimited member I will destroy him. If he thinks that he can take this title off me he’s got another thing coming. If he thinks I’m going to go at half pace for the good of Unlimited he’s sorely mistaken. Because tonight I’m going to make sure he doesn’t get this title and if it comes to it I’ll make sure he doesn’t cross my path again. His mind may be focused on the two Yoshii’s but he needs to think about me. Because tonight I guarantee him he will Taste The Pain…
