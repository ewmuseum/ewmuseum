Strategy Session

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-17-93 Santa Barbara, CA Four Seasons Biltmore Strategy Session No one is in a great mood following Legends from last week. HBC is not her jovial self. BDD is in a surly mood, despite the fact of reclaiming once and for all, The Syndicate name. No, instead the dynamic duo face a possibility they have never contemplated in their entire career before. Somers, in his hell-bent quest to destroy The Syndicate thinks he has come up with a grand solution of defeating the greatest team in wrestling history and the biggest threat to him personally. As it stands now, The Syndicate is disjointed, but not by there own design, more due to Somers personal ego-trip. As it currently states, The HeartBreak Chick has decided to team up with Luther Wisdom until they either capture the Tag Team gold or are defeated by the champions, but alittle twist has been thrown in involving Big Daddy Dreamer, Malcom King and Daniel Somers. Somers will choose one of the two to be his partner, and should they lose and be pinned, that individual career is over from Evolution. Do they think Somers is going to put forth a big effort and win……. not a chance in hell. He would like nothing more than one or both of them gone for good. It doesn’t get any rosier for HBC or Wisdom either, for should they be pinned and lose, they will be forced to join The Unlimited against their will and their respective careers will be held in Somers hands. Basically, it sucks all around. So here in this emergency strategy session, BDD, HBC and their team of lawyers are going line-by-line over the contract finding any loopholes written within in. Summer, long-time Syndicate attorney who has helped them on a few occasions point out some vagueness in the wording that could be used to their advantage, as well as two potential scenarios that probably, key word, probably, be upheld in a court of law should they pursue it. HBC and BDD look at each other, contemplate both options and nod their head in agreement. Plan Alpha and Plan Zeta. They both smile at each other with a quick high-five and thank Summer for her tireless work before heading off to the courtyard, beach area to cut this weeks Legends promo….. HBC and BDD are walking along Butterfly beach, a place where all magnitude of stars escape from their daily life and worries as Lexi follows them with a camera around. She points over to BDD who once again has another handicap tag team match against this time a team calling themselves…. The Cogs of War. Which only amounts to a slight snort from the former Continental Champion. He stops for a moment before turning to the camera and addressing the current affairs of the week. Last week, I was faced with a two-fold potential quandary in that I, and I alone, went against something calling themselves, The Syndicate. Two jokers, trying to make a boat full of money at our expense. Trying to confuse the public at large at who the real team, THE REAL SYNDICATE, truly was. I’ll admit, it wasn’t the first time its happened. Its happened before, and it happened again last week. However, the only thing in common is the fact, the true team, the true Syndicate emerged victorious once again, solidifying our name, our copywrited and trademarked name once again belongs to Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick where it belongs. Once again we defended our history, our legacy, our name. Though its only a name, it’s a name of excellence within this business and we’ll be damned in hell if someone uses it besides us! Now as a result of my victorious win, I alone move into the next round of the tag team tournament facing another team of Somers hired guns. The Cogs of War. Excuse me while I have to chuckle for a second because I’ve heard of some bad team names in my career but this has to be the worst one ever. EVER! The Cogs of War? That alone tells me, you two aren’t capable of rising to the occasion for your only a bit-part in a small paradigm of things that supercede you. Bit actors on a world stage. Bit players within this tournament, and for that, you two ass-stains not only will fall to me personally, but fail in your little tasks for Somers as well. Sure, I know he hired you to try to injure me, so that he could perhaps handpick me to be his partner, do all the dirty work, get worked over and lose my place forever within Evolution. But it simply isn’t going to happen. You might say, I’m too good for Somers little plan. You don’t get to where I am by being stupid and always planning several outs in case certain exigencies arise. This Legends, you two Cogs, though why I don’t know you didn’t pick Dogs, it would have made more sense and perhaps give me the hibby-gibbities, will face a true icon within this industry. An Icon in a bad mood, an Icon, that is going to walk all over you and toss you to the fans in the cheap seats where they will proceed to rip you apart worse than I, for being so short-sighted, and a Somers lacky. Before I turn it over to HBC though, there is one more person I want to address and that is Asis. The current continental champion. I’m not going to piss and moan about our last encounter, because truth be told, you beat me, you pinned me, the outside interference had nothing to do with it, you were just simply on that day. However, Tainted Love is a different story. I named that PPV myself last year and this time, you and I go head to head for a third time. Asis-Dreamer III for the Continental Championship, but this time, we are bringing back something that was banned from Evolution for being too violent, a career shortener. The Cell. Similar to the UFC Octagon, it is designed to hurt, injure, in a more confining environment that the HIC which everyone knows I am a master in. The dimensions are smaller, but the pain level grows increasingly more. At Tainted Love, I’m going to prove to the world why the cell has been retired by using you as more than as an example, more than just my bitch, but simply as a tool of destruction of you personally, and the Unlimited as a whole. Within the process, I shall reclaim my Continental Championship for the second time, and you, you Asis, you will wonder what the hell you did to unleash the demonic beasts within me that assaulted you unlike no other, worse than your redneck parents, worse than your nightmares could ever imagine. You step into my world now Asis, and the nightmare, my Tainted Love shall begin. And this week Luther Wisdom and I face the unknown, however as professional as we are, as talented as we are, neither one of us is afraid of the unknown. An old team, who myself and my former partner are often compared too had an affinity for saying, “We snack on danger, dine on death.” Those immortal words shall forever ring true for despite only teaming together twice, we’ve captured the belts, and been screwed out of them. In the short time together, we’ve began to gel as a team, and though we aren’t as fluid as our respective ex-teams, we are damn good. It would be right of me to screw Luther Wisdom out of his shot for personal satisfaction. Loyalty, is something I value. King understands it. Dreamer understands it. And right now, HBC-Luther Wisdom will walk into the fragile nature against potentially our friends and Somers, and do what we have to do, for everyone in involved, everyone except that little peepsqueak Somers. Oh Daniel, it gave me such delight when I gave you the Kiss of Death and knocked you on your ass! You have had that coming for a long time now. I’ve broken every heart in this industry except for one, and that is about to happen if you get in my face again Somers. That kick last time will be nothing compared to the next one. For now the One has revealed himself after stalking me for nearly two years, two years, and quite frankly, since I am a fair woman, I think, no, I know, I will be stalking you and making your life completely and absolutely miserable for the next two years, kicking your ass around every turn, while you’ll be wishing you never, ever, pissed off The HeartBreak Chick! And Somers, know this….. I am very good with a butcher knife and a blender, you care to find out first hand why? Cross me, screw me, cheat me in any way, and you will find out why my reputation stemming from the RGWF is true, as the hardcore princess, the first lady of destruction, and the Queen of Evolution will ring true once again!
