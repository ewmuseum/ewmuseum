Chronicles: First Thoughts of North-Anomica

25 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-01-25 Joshua Northstar, a man of mixed decent being: Caucasian and Asian, raised in the suburbanized city of Kalamazoo Michigan has made his mark in the wrestling franchise eVolution as being the first ever white and asian mix wrestler. Also, he made a mark on the franchise by being the man that did the dirty work that Ethan M. Coles obviously couldn't do. Coles hired Ace of Spades to finally take out Johnny Charisma, a man who has done nothing but ended the careers of wrestlers of eVolution most notably Jason Jameson and Scott Frost. But once Ace of Spades started to lose the heart of action Northstar came from nowhere and helped the cause out by taking out Charisma by himself and a chair. Ethan M. Coles, being the good man he is. Decided to reward Northstar with the money promised to Ace of Spades for the troubles of helping the cause of taking care of Charisma only to accept the money and attack Coles afterwards. This chronicle is what went through the mind of Joshua Northstar after his first week in eVolution. 'I can't believe that, that rich mongaloid bastard actually thought I would just accept the money and shake his hand before I left.' 'I mean, I make a small amount of money even working for eVolution. I make about ten to fifteen thousand more dollars than a burger flipper at McDonalds.' 'I just wanted to.....hai chorp chun suk noi, or Make you more like me just a little bit.' 'I mean, that dumb has money and he isn't down in the dirt trying to survive. So after he gave me all that money, and I still felt the need to continue to survive. I just couldn't stand it anymore. I wanted him to know what I now feel. To have money and need to survive.' 'But I bet he didn't feel like that, he didn't try and surpass his rich lifestyle to think for a second that maybe he isn't any better than me. But no, I bet he thought that I was being nothing more than a neanderthalic brute only to bite the hand that has paid to put food on my plate and shelter over my head for helping him do something he couldn't seem to do.' 'I sure hope that Ethan doesn't think that he will be able to phase me? I mean, think about it. If I am able to destroy a man for money, just think about what I will do to a man to just survive.'
