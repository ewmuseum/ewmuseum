Mercy, Severity

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th February After Legends XXII, Asis vowed revenge on four certain individuals because of their actions toward him on that night. Asis got some of that revenge on Sean Mason and Big Daddy Dreamer at New Year’s eVolution, when he interrupted their match and left both men lying in the ring. He extended his revenge on Sean Mason this past week on Legends XXIII, beating him comfortably to earn himself a shot at Big Daddy Dreamer’s Continental Title, where he looks to extend his revenge further… Asis is for the first time in his life in one of the most famous cities in the world. Despite all of his travelling throughout the US he’s never been to Los Angeles. And as he walks the streets near to the LA Sports arena, he sees nothing special, nothing enthralling or captivating about it at all. It’s just another city to him, just another collective of bodies and buildings. Despite the reputation California has for having nice weather, it is a surprisingly dreary day, but Asis is not complaining. If it were a nice day he probably wouldn’t be out walking the streets. But the weather has not stopped the city being packed, the streets a proverbial cattle market, each way you move, someone is trying to block the way. Asis is not used to this, as it is not very often that he is in a place that is really crowded and when he is, he’s used to people moving out of his way. But as is the norm in LA, everyone walks with their head down, only wanting to get where they’re going, not giving a damn about anyone else. People occasionally bump into Asis’ chest, and despite nearly falling over, they don’t look up but merely mutter under their breath as they walk on, oblivious to who they just brushed past. One child, a boy no older than ten, notices Asis and stops walking. Despite the tug his mother gives on his hand the boy does not move, but stands completely still staring at Asis as he walks towards him. Asis only notices the child briefly, not really caring, just wanting to get out of this sea of bodies. The child’s mother continues to tug at him and eventually starts shouting at him telling him to move along. She follows her child’s gaze and notices “The Demonic” just about to walk past them, and the look on her face is both amazement and horror. She has seen this man on TV when her son watches and although she knows wrestling is fake that’s a hard thing to believe with a monster like him walking towards you. Asis ignores them both and walks past, but as he does he feels a jerk on his trenchcoat, stopping him. It’s not a powerful tug, but enough to make him take notice. He turns around and looks down, to see the small ten year old boy with his hand holding onto his coat. “I hope you beat up BDD real good Mr Asis sir.” The mother then drags her child away and rushes him down the street, without looking back. Asis stands there for a moment, and then moves on, walking straight through the crowds, barging people out of his way as he goes. The event that had happened earlier on in the day had got Asis thinking. He did not care about fan adulation, he’d never had any and had never wanted any and even when a child had told him he wanted him to win a match, it had not changed his opinion. It had just made him think about Big Daddy Dreamer. He now sits in a hotel room provided by eVolution, the room as always when Asis stays in a hotel has been trashed and ripped apart. Mainly to make himself feel a bit more at home and at the same time releasing some of his vented anger. Staying in five star hotels does not appeal to Asis, he feels as if he does not deserve them. But now he sits and ponders what his life has in store for him on Wednesday night… Why would a child wish success on me? I’m the one that destroys the other wrestlers, I’m the one that just tries to hurt my opponent for no other reason than that. I’m not the one that the fans like, I’m the “heel” the one people should hate. Maybe it is just a demonstration of the innocence of childhood; the way children express true human emotion better than most adults do. They know that I am not a “freak” as most people call me, just because of my desires to hurt and maim people. They know that these things are human nature, but most people repress them and think that they shouldn’t let them out because it’s wrong. I don’t. This week BDD will finally have to face me one on one, a normal singles match, no triple threat, no people at ringside and no interference. And then we’ll see who is the better out of the two of us. Because I’ve already proven that I’m better than Mason. I showed him that I do not make empty promises. I got my revenge on him, I took away his chance to become an idol to the people, the chance for him to hold more glory as a “champion.” It’s the same motivation that I have for defeating you as well BDD. To take that title away from you, that leather strap covered in gold that symbolises that you’re “better” than I am. And I still owe you for the original match don’t I? I will win at Legends, I will annihilate you and I will take that precious title from around your waist. And again this week I will probably be wrestling more than one match. I may very well represent Unlimited when we regain the tag team titles. Not for the first time I will be wrestling more than once in a night and probably not for the last time either. And whilst most people may think this is running me down, if anything it’s making me stronger. Wrestling more than once in a night is making me physically stronger and it’s giving me more desire as well. The thought that nearly every week I’m getting the opportunity to take out twice as many opponents and hurt twice as many people makes me more enthusiastic than ever. Which is something you should bear in mind when you get in the ring with me soon BDD…
