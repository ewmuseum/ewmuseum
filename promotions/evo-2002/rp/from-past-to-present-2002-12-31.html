From Past to Present

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31st December 2002 Foul mood, they thought I was in a foul mood, they don’t know me angry, they have not seen me pissed off, neither has Exodus, he has not been around eVo long enough to remember Digital Destruction when Asis put me in hospital, and they thought that because I threw my nurse across the room that I was in a bad mood, that I was unstable, I’ll let Exodus see unstable. I am better than I have shown as of late, I have beat people like Asis on my own, I gave in, never fought hard enough against Xander and his little goons, but Exodus will feel it, feel what I never did to Xander. The scene fades into Seara sitting in Scott Frost’s front room, watching copies of eVolution access, three particular shows, when she had been in hospital after Asis had nearly broken her neck in a vicious after match attack at Digital Destruction. The show had been dubbed Last Thought and had shown the semi unconscious female slipping in and out of a deep sleep, thinking about the last things she had seen and felt, her anger during those shows had progressed and she had become uncontrollable, she had been drugged to keep her calm and safe, but it had not worked, not for one unlucky nurse who felt the beginning of a foul mood that Seara would suffer until meeting male wrestler Scott Frost on his entrance to eVolution wrestling. The anger in her had once again been pushed back, it now lay dormant in her, lay unused, unwanted, she wanted more than anything to keep Frost in her life, wanted him to be safe around her, but she had made her anger more dormant than she should have, she had seemingly killed that side of her, she had got rid of it, not even in ring anger was present in her. The only anger she would come to show since her first meeting with Frost would be the anger that appeared the week after Shocka and Omega kidnapped her from Notorious dressing room. At Legends the week later, she had attacked the pair from behind in the hallway with a large piece of 2x4. The pent up anger within the female was building up in her, pushing to escape, and she had began to snap back in that apartment in Chicago, trying to calm her down, Scott Frost coaxed the female to leave with him. And with a moment of calm approval she joined him and headed back to his hometown of Detroit. It had been the only thing he could think of, to get her out of that place that held those memories for her, but it had unfortunately been to late, she had unlocked her anger and thoughts now came flooding back to her, the pain she had always known, the pain that had kept her sane. She had began to focus on new things, began to concentrate on her life with Frost, learning to enjoy a life without pain, a life where she did not need the pain and anger to survive, but without the pain and anger, she had become just another wrestler on the eVo roster, another star, underrated and not as noticeable. The huge impact she had once made on the other superstars of eVo, on the fans and the fed it self had all but disappeared. Fans remembered when Seara had been deadly, a force not to take lightly, but now, these days she had become less prominent, she had to an extent disappeared and faded into the backstage area. Her fights were normally loses, and showed no real initiative and passion for the game. It was time to change it. Seara needed to reopen her old ways, needed to bring the old Seara back, the winner, the fighter that had struck awe into the crowds as they watched her battle huge cruiserweights and even bigger heavyweights, the anger filled young fighter was what was needed now, no longer could she stay happy at every moment, her personal private life with Scott Frost, that could be calm and happy, he didn’t have to be involved with Seara, he needed to continue to see the side of her that was Katherine again. Frost had brought Katherine out of the female, covered up Seara with her, but now Seara needed to show herself instead of just using the name Seara, she needed to once again become Seara, but she needed more control this time, she needed Katherine and Seara to be there all the time. The compassion love and humanity of Katherine had to be present within the new person, but so did the anger pain wanting for death and blood covered fists of Seara, it would be one person with the qualities of two people, two very different personalities would exist within the one body, and she would control them this, time, unlike before she would have control. Can Exodus really expect to face me now with the changes I am facing? Exodus you will join one of me in the ring, but not the me that has lost everything for a long time, you will face the me that once defeated Asis, the me that ended my stepbrother Vinnie. You will face the real Seara. I am sorry Scott, sorry that I became something else in the ring because I let myself get obsessed with trying to please you, I am sure that I will become the fighter I was when I first met you. No, I am not sure, I am positive, I will be that fighter, and I want you to watch when I get into that ring for the High Impact title against Lee Exodus. I want you to think back to before you came here, what you had seen on the TV, and what you knew of me, and the way I was when I first meet you, in mid feud with what is eVo’s fiercest competitor, Asis, I want you to remember the way I would stop at nothing, the anger that filled me, the pain that consumed me. Scott, I want you to witness me become that in the ring once again; I want you to know that I am strong, and I want you there by my side at Legends when I make my announcement to everyone. Things will change, I will become the person I once was in the ring, and outside the ring, and you will not have to be seen walking around and dating a failure. They change is for the better, and this change will be for the best. Scott, I will become more than I ever have been, and I will be there when you need me. At Legends XX I am going to call you into the ring with me for an announcement, and I hope you will oblige me by joining me to hear what I have to say to you. I love you Scott, and this will make things better. The camera pans back to the TV; another show is on, another wrestling show, looking back at the careers of each and every wrestler in eVo. Seara looks to the image of herself in the ring, pinning the giant Asis, the shot quickly cuts to the show access where we can see a motel room on the screen, Seara’s famous fire incident when she left a motel room and watched it burn to the ground because of a terrible mood she had been in, then another cut to the ring, but not eVo’s ring, a ring in a barn, her grandfathers old training ring on a ranch in Fort Worth, her stepbrother Vinnie tied up in the middle of the ring, hanging from rope, blood pouring from his mouth and nose, broken bones throughout his body. A dark shadowed figure that had helped the female beat her stepbrother and left him for dead in the barn. The dark figure had linked Seara’s segment of the show to the next segment, a span over the career of “The Demonic”, Asis. Turning away from the TV, she lights a cigarette and looks directly at the camera and gives a sadistic sinister smile. She obviously has something planned for Legends XX, but no one, not even Frost knows what the female will do to get her hands on that High Impact title, or what she wants to see Scott Frost in the ring for, could it be the end of something that the female is so closely associated with, or could it be the biggest change and improvement of her career. Could the guesses be right, could she leave Notorious and join boyfriend Scott Frost, or could she dumb him? Could she bring new members to Notorious, strong keen fighters, good in the ring? No one knows, no one except the female, and she is giving no clues, all that we know is that this involves the man she loves, Scott Frost. Maybe she will ask him to return to Notorious, maybe she will promise him wins and titles with them, a future worth more than her life itself. Whatever will be said in that ring is Seara’s little secret, and she is keeping it tightly locked away. A car can be heard pulling up, and Seara quickly turns the TV off and heads to the front door to meet Frost as he returns from the gym. Taking one last deep breath of smoke, the female extinguishes the cigarette on the palm, of her hand, signifying the end of another day of change within her. The camera slowly fades out with a shot of the pair kissing in the doorway of Frosts home.
