Date: 2003-09-09 It wasn’t over. It was far from fucking over. He didn’t like clichés, but the old saying was true; the battle was done, but the war was yet to come. The match at X-Rated, the “title” match was done with. James Kimson had won the match, he had been crowned champion, for what it was worth. No thanks to Xander. It was his weakness that had lost the match. Asis wasn’t arrogant enough to deny that it had been a hellacious match, that both Xander and Kimson had taken and given a lot of punishment. But Asis hadn’t lost the match. It wasn’t his shoulders that had been pinned to the mat. Not that he cared. He was above all that, he didn’t care about winning or losing. To him it was all about pain. And as far as he was concerned Xander hadn’t felt enough of that. He almost wondered if Xander had let himself get pinned by Kimson to escape what he knew was coming. But that was all filed and forgotten about now. There was nothing he could do to rectify the past, the way the match should have happened. Now all he had left was the future. The plan was already in motion, he knew what he was going to do, Xander had a lot to look forward to. Kimson was out of the picture, that side dish was no longer in the way, Asis could finally focus all his attention on Xander. His full vengeance had been a long time coming. He’d had to wait a long time, but that wait was nearly over, all that patience would soon be worth it. It is soon. Xander’s felt nothing yet.
