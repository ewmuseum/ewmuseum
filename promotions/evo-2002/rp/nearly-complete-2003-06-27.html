Date: 2003-06-27 Sahara Desert Syndicate Training Facility 2:00 pm ‘Welcome to Wasteland’. What a cruel sense of humor someone must have had putting this sign here in the middle of nowhere. Yet, nothing could be further from the truth. Miles of open space, in every direction, nothing but sand dunes. The only ironic comfort is that it couldn’t possibly get any hotter. 112 degrees in the shade, that is if their was any to be found, no instead, we get a proverbial wasteland of nothingness, and with the temperature cracking 130 degrees, wasteland seems appropriate. No signs of life, not even the nomadic tribes pass through here. Perhaps it is because they know they will be shot on sight. Here, in Wasteland, is one of the preeminent ultimate survival schools on the planet. Everyone who passes through here and survives, is known, their names placed on the walls of glory. Currently the list stands at 78. While on the other wall, the list of casualties, those unfortunate, number in the thousands. They will not be coming home. All traces of them, forever removed from the annuals of history of the place. And a history it does have. From ancient times to its purchase by Syndicate International, all men and women have flocked here to prove themselves in the harshest of environments. It was first used by the Abyssians, then Carpathians, Alexander himself passed through here, Caesar was routed, the French, English all have used it, Rommell himself, the desert fox called this place hell, a wasteland. It stuck. Today, two more names shall be placed on the list of glory, brave men and women, who proved themselves in the ultimate test of endurance and survival. The Heartbreak Chick and Big Daddy Dreamer. Etched onto it by their head of security, a three time survivor of the wasteland challenge. Finally, the end is near, the rebirth of a new beginning, a new era of greatness. The Syndicate is nearing the completion of their final training phase for there illustrious return to battle. In Evolution……. You evolve, or be destroyed……. Evolution is about evolving greatness to unparalleled heights. Raising the bar, higher than ever thought possible. But Evolving is also a personal process. And believe me, its very personal. Never in the history of The Syndicate, has anything been so more personal than its return, and its unfinished business left behind. EVO1. The biggest anniversary show in the history of wrestling. A show, higher than PPV status. A show that will be a homecoming of epic proportions. The day, The Syndicate returns to action. While the spectrum is mixed in the locker room for and against this title shot, one thing isn’t. The fear they feel when the greatest team returns to action. The fear of wondering if they are on this infamous hit-list. The scene is bleak, as two figures rise up from a sand dune, with the full sun shining down into the camera hiding their faces. The voice echos loudly…………………. ‘Evolution-1. The one-year anniversary show of the largest most successful company in wrestling. And the return of The Syndicate. What more can people want. All across the world, the hype of the show, the return is being covered by every press agency, every media outlet, every arm-chair wrestling site on the net. This show will be the evolution of this industry, and we shall be there. It has been a month so far, and people are getting complacent. The big dog supposedly gone, but alas no more. No more of what wrestling used to be. It’s the 21st century and this industry has devolved back to what it was in the 1920’s. No talent, a bunch of big ugly bullies trying to be a big shot. No more. And most of all, no more nice Syndicate.’ ‘For seven years, we’ve been the crowd favorite. A unique hybrid experiment gone mad that no ever predicted to ever do as well as we did. An intergender team with absolutely no fear. And as the experiment grew into greatness, everyone else followed our grand design. Nowadays, women often compete with men and enjoy high success, intergender teams sprang up across the world, trying to emulate us. Twisted Reality can owe their existence to us! We made it possible. We changed and broke the rules of normalcy. Innovation has always been one of our fortays. But now, after seven years of complying with all the bullshit, and putting up with all the crap that has gone on, being told, sit back, relax, we’re looking out for you. Its all part of the angle, the script, the fans wanted this to happen. No longer….. This time, its personal. This time, we make our own rules. If none of you can handle it, that’s your problem. Not ours.’ ‘There has been a lot of talk from the tag-team division saying how unfair this is. Why is The Syndicate getting the shot at Evo-1 and we aren’t. The answer is simple people. None of you has ever done what we accomplished. We’ve been the torch bearers for this fed and every other fed we’ve been in. We make, and create history. We take on everyone, regardless of their ability or worthyness. We make the fed and all of you money to take home every show. And most of all, we are the most respected, feared, and simply the greatest team to ever walk gods green earth. We accomplished more in our seven years than the history of wrestling combined. Quite simply, were the best. What the hell have any of you done. Other than be like everyone else, bitching and moaning. ‘The biggest bitchers seem to be King’s little group. You should listen to your boss. He knows first hand of our brutality, you’ve only felt alittle bit. Dolo and company seems to have grown a set during our absence, but the funny thing, can you back it up. History has proven an emphatical NO! You’ve had your asses handed to you every time by us, while we operated under certain restrictions that were on us behind the scenes. Not any more. Not only have we been cleared to return, we’ve been cleared to do things our way. Unrestrictive warfare. Do you know what that is? During WWII, the germans u-boats used unrestrictive warfare and sunk everything in their path. No one was foolish enough to sail in the Atlantic or Mediterranean. Men, women, children, all killed with reckless unremorseful abandon. That is what we’ve been given. ‘So its real simple, all those teams, all those so-called superstars who continue to bitch and moan, mouth off, whine about this and that, will seal there fate by their own stupidity. This is the only warning. From here on out, those rants shall be inflicted upon you with such brutality not seen since Hiromisha. St.Violence is the current champions, but on July 3rd, 2003, the world will never be the same, as The Syndicate ride the horses of apocalypse back into Evolution! The camera fades out never showing BDD and HBC’s face…….
