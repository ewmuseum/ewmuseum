Date: 10-4-02 [ p r e l u d e ] Wow, they've set me up in a match in eVolution, possibly one of the greatest federations in the business of physical entertainment, and they put me up against a guy who sounds like he jacks off all the time? C'mon, where's the fun, the publicity? I really do love the way I get put up against someone of relative equivalence when it comes to an opponent but what kind of name is Hanz? Sounds like he's been in jail for half of his life. Anyway, to get the started off right, I've decided to go along with this match just to get into eVolution. Like I really have something to prove? Anyway, i'm preparing to fight with the most hardship that I have, but not just for me. But for all the Canadian's out there who aren't getting the credit that they deserve. For all the Canadian's who are cheated out of everything in this prejudice society just because we aren't American. For all the Canadian's who are actually doing something with their life and not stooping to the low ass feeding level of the American society. You suck America. You suck more than a prostitute at a party. Americans all over the world can hear me when I say this, Bite me. I can't even get a decent job at a food court because i'm Canadian. Shit, why the fuck does it always have to be us? We're people too, they make cartoons about us saying 'Aye' after every sentence which I find as a racist remark, and they stereotype that everyone from Canada says about as, 'Aboot..' which is something that I also find racist. They say that the only thing that came good out of Canada is Maple Syrup. Alot of good things have come good out of Canada, like Me, Dreamer, HBC, and everyone else in eVo who's from Canada. Also, I'd love for someone to tell me something that America created. xBox, wait, they got the supplies from Japan, nevermind. Waffles, wait that's from Germany. Pizza, from Italy. I think i've made my point. America is nothing but a theiving country! America has made nothing of their own but enemies all over this great world, well excluding America. Anyway, I'd just like to prove my point by saying, 'It's true America. Everyone does hate you ~!' [ i n t r o d u c t i o n] [1:04 PM October 4th, 2001. Friday ~ McDonald's] I sat there for what it seemed to be an eternity. Motionless as I stared into the obvious oblivion that lay before me at that time. I continued to wait, wait, wait. My time was slowly wasteing away into a mere decline, removing itself from my lifetime slowly by the second. I gave off a small sigh as I continued to stare forward at the inhabitance that were infront of me. I placed my elbows down on a surface and held by head down continuing to wait. I then pushed myself up as my patience slowly began to fade. It faded faster than I had anticipated. I through my hand down on the hard surface, that seemed to be made out of a semi-thin plastic. I was beginning to wail but I stopped myself and just waited more. I through my head toward the right and looked at the time only to notice that it was going by faster than usual. I continued to stand there, I couldn't take it anymore... 'DAMNIT! ALL I ORDERED FOR WAS A FUCKING LARGE SPRITE, A DOUBLE CHEESEBURGER AND A LARGE FRY! WHY DOES IT TAKE SO FUCKING LONG JUST TO MAKE THAT...SIMPLE..OF..AN ORDER ~!!?' I wailed out furociously over the counter that I had my hands placed on. I was at a local McDonald's, standing infront of the counter waiting for my order. The woman in the front was taking longer than usual. I saw her come from behind the stove and walk over infront of the counter, 'I'm sorry, but you'll have to sit there and wait..' '...' I pushed myself away from the counter and sat down in a chair that was adjacent toward the entrance door. I tilted my head backward and sighed as I turned around and looked at the woman at the counter. She had just handed the man behind me his order. My jaw dropped right infront of me, I lost my cool. I stood up and walked over toward her stamping in a hardcore rage. I stood infront of the counter and expressed my emotions in the open and out loud. 'What the fuck was that?! You make me wait and give him his fucking order as soon as he asks for it!' 'I'm sorry, do you want Canadian Bacon on your burger?' That blew it, I was in a blitzful rage at this point. 'What's that supposed to fucking mean?! DID I ORDER CANADIAN BACON?! NO! DID I FUCKING ORDER CANADI...' the woman had cut me off, I knew she was making fun of my origin, 'How 'aboot' some Maple Syrup?' I was about ready to snatch her over the counter and stomp the shit out of her. This was a flawless example of how Canadian's are treated in America. 'How 'aboot' I kick your ass?' She just looked at me and started laughing, 'Ooooooo, a canadian is going to kick my ass. I supposed I turn around and think 'aboot' what i'm going to do. How about I call the park ranger and tell him to help me with this situation, aye?' I turned around and stormed out of the McDonalds full of anger, rage, hostility. Every negative thing that you could name was somewhere in my being at that point. I turned toward my 2002 Ford Taurus and sat inside of it, slamming the door and bursting out of the parking lot. I continued forward looking ahead of me with a rage that could be detected from anywhere around me. I turned the wheel, my car came to a screeching sharp as I noticed that I was gaining the attention of the...americans. I continued forward without stopping as I was passing red lights without giving notice toward any of the people honking their horns. I was just fit on getting home. I pushed down on the accelerator harshly and continued forward, I saw the light turn red, I continued. That's where it stopped. Everything went white. [ c a n a d i a n ~ h o s p i t a l i t y] [3:23 PM October 4, 2001 ~ Hospital] I woke up in a room surrounded by people, fimilar people. I couldn't remember very clearly how I had gotten there but I had starting lookinga round. A man with Latex gloves pushed me back down onto my back and said, 'This is a canadian here. Of course he was speeding..' My eyes started widening but I had lost all of my power so I couldn't even speak what I wanted to, I had a large bandage on my head and I could barely think without hurting myself. 'Canadians are always speeding. Why don't they just go back to Canada and drive like that. They probably don't know that we go by Miles per Hour here. Miiiles per Hooour.' he said to the nurse. They started chuckling and then walked out of the room still talking. I turned my head toward the door ready to stomp a hole in him. Another example on how Canadian's are treated in America. I through my attention toward the bandages on my arms and legs I saw a large Gauh on my leg and noticed that I had been broken. I tried sitting up but it took almost all of the power out of me just to look at my bandages. I continued to look down at the Gauh on my leg, and gained enough strength to let me sit up. I sat there and started breathing heavily, it was very tiring to sit up straight in this predicament. I grabbed the Gauh and winced in pain, as I looked down at it once more. I was sure it was broken. The doctor walked back in and looked down at me sitting up. He then placed his hand on my chest and slowly pushed me back down. I looked up at him wondering just WHY the fuck he did it. I was actually pretty comfortable like that, NOT! I just wanted to tell him that so he could feel stupid. He looked down at me and shook his head and started righting stuff down on a clipboard that he had in his hand. I started looking around and turned my head toward the remote control beside me. I grabbed it and pressed the power button and watched as the TV came on. The doctor turned down at me and said... 'I'll tell you now. Only American shows are on. They don't show Canadian shows down here..' I turned up to him and blurted out in a sarcastic concerned, 'How about.. you leave me alone?' I gave off a fake smile and then looked over toward the television once more. I saw the doctor turned toward me and shake his head out of the corner of my eye, but I just continued to watch television. 'Today's topic on Nikki Fake, Canada, what's the point?' I looked at the T.V. and blurted out, 'What the fuck?! Is this 'Prejuday' or something? [ For ~ The ~ Slow ~ Ones ] 1. Prejuday, words are nouns that were snapped together into one compound. Prejudice, Day, Predjuday. Now, back to the roleplay. 'Jesus Christ, what the fuck is up with this day? Seems like everyone is against Canada. Typical America.' I continued to look at the TV and noticed this obese-[typical america], redneck-[typical america], looking man who was wearing a red and green, plad, long sleeved flannel and a denim jumper suit with a straw hat on. He had a cat-tail in his mouth as was talking to Nikki Fake. His yellow-brown teeth could be seen as they glowed with ambiance as people were gagging while he was talking. 'WELL! I DON'T SEE WAH AMAYRECANS GOTSTA DEEL WIT CANAYDIANS! THEY AIN'T NUTTIN BUT A BUNCHA MAPLE SURP EATIN JEWS!' He blurted out, I saw a female in the audience start puking because of the way his mouth reeked. I chuckled some and merely said, 'Typical America.' He continued to ramble on about pointless things such as, 'boomsticks, police, waffles, cajuns, and chicken fucking.' I saw 4 people puke in the crowd while I was watching. 'AND MOST'UH ALL! CANADA SUCKS!' The crowd seised there coughing and suddenly everyone pulled out midevil weapons, like Maces, Crossbows, etc..
