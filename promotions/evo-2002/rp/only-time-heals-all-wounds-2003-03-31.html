Only Time Heals All Wounds

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday March 31 It was a story written in blood and hate. The lights slowly dim in and looking up all there is standing there is a nurse. She’s looking around the area around and taking notes. Then a voice speaks, it’s more of a groan. “Please Mr. Kimson. We need you to lay still and just get some rest. Your body has taken a lot of damage last night and it might be a while till you recover fully.” said the Nurse, trying to keep an authoritative yet pleasant voice. My voice groaned I closed my eyes again and time passed. Hard to know how long but it almost seemed like a cut scene in a movie, days later it seemed but it hadn’t been. Perhaps the night passed by, mid afternoon maybe. It’s hard to guess based on the fact that there’s no clock, not a shred of daylight in this closed up Hospital room. Time passed until I finally decided waiting, laying there doing nothing but taking up space sat up slowly. Pain shots through my body. My legs, arms, neck, head, back. He swings his arms over the side of the bed and looks around. Still wearing my wrestling tights, the knee brace is still on and he shakes my head in wonderment. My arm is hooked up to an IV, my suitcase from the arena must have been brought over after I was taken to the hospital as it rests up against the wall. Looking at the IV I grin, I‘ve been there before....sigh. Wait a second. Kimson looks around and then rubs his eyes as if trying to get the sleep out of them. Kimson notices he’s obviously been sedated, or drugged in some matter, pain killers he’d guess and that they must be wearing off at this point. He scratches his hair, but it’s matted in blood and he turns looking into a near by door window, his reflection starring back at him is that of a man who’s seen a war. His face is stained with his own blood although most of it’s been washed away still there’s a hint of the red on his face. Kimson was lossing a lot of blood the night before, to much for almost anyone to survive let alone wrestle a Hardcore match in. Critics have said that their match last night was the bloodiest match in North America since the days of ECW’s height. It would rival any Mic Foley/Terry Funk match. The match was perhaps the most physical match he’s ever been in. The most personal match he’s ever been in. But right now, he can’t remember it. Damn what the hell happened. Kimson thinks hard, recalling only moments. His memory blanks out when he remembers going head 1st into the Slot machine, the glass shattering around his head, the sharp kiss of the glass breaking the skin, deep into his head slashing him open, shredding him like cheese on a grater. Almost like a flash of white light he then recalls taking Sovereign down with History in the Making off the top of the stretcher to the concrete floor. But then nothing. Then recalls Sovereign’s face, a mask of blood, his arm oozing blood grasping Kimson’s throat with the Last Breath. Kimson’s sense of reality slowly disappearing. Then, the sound of the bell. His mind clears, a man in black and white stripes raises his hands. The cheers of the fans echoing but then silence and darkness. Now he’s woken up here. I guess that explains why I’m in this place... where ever it is. Memory of a war over family honor. A war that became more personal then ever thought. Both men wanted to destroy each other. Both men wanted to end each other’s career. Now all that waited James was to learn if Sovereign succeeded. If all he had been through was in vain and that Sovereign was still walking around with James’ sister on his arm. Almost like a trophy in defiance of Kimson’s family name. Or had James came out on top. Sure, there’s no debate that James won the match. That’s in the history books. But who is the real winner? Time passes, and for James Kimson each second ticked forward 3 seconds and then went back 2. He sat there in his hospital bed looking blankly at the wall hoping for word. Hoping a doctor would come to see him. He’d have pressed the nurse call button about half a dozen times by now and waited and waited. His patience was wearing thin at this point and he always prided himself on patience. Waiting for the right moment. Well in this situation, when he needed to know what was going on, patience was becoming a thing for stronger men. James looked himself over up and down. He’d done so many times, accessing his injuries as he had before. Well, aside from the bumps and bruises I look okay. But he sure did not feel okay. Charts were attacked to the end of the bed and he was quite tempted to reach over and grab them and take a look. James was no doctor, but he’d been in enough hospitals and visited enough doctors over his lifetime to know a few things about the Human body. Being a professional wrestler for 12 years would teach you a few things about Medical Jargon and words like MRI or things of that nature. James finally decided he didn’t care about what Doctor’s or Nurses would say to him about looking at it and reached for it, trying his best to avoid dislodging the IV in his arm, but then, inches away the door opened up and in walked a tall man, perhaps 6'3 slim with his hair going thin. The Doctor looks somewhat surprised to see Kimson reaching, then suddenly Kimson stops and slowly sits back down. “I was thinking no one was going to come after I pushed this damn buzzer a million times” said Kimson. “I’m sorry Mr..... uh, Kimson” said the Doctor looking at his chart. “But we’ve been quite busy after the weekend in this particular ward.” said the Doctor. “Well where are am I then.” blurted Kimson. “Your in the Las Vegas General, this is the Progressive Care unit, we transfered you out of Intensive care last night” replied the Doctor. “My name is Doctor Warren, and I’ll be your physician for the next few days while you remain in our care.” he added then looked at the chart. James looked the man up and down trying to read him. He was probably a nice man in the middle of a busy day. James realized himself that he wasn’t exactly in a pleasant mood and was probably overly short with the Doctor. But he settled back in his seated position and took a deep breath. “Mr. Kimson, I just want to say that you Professional wrestlers are daredevils.” said the Doctor. “We had to give you a massive blood transfusion as well as treat you for shock.” he added. “Obviously it’s not the show I though it was. But other then the trauma to your system from that you’ve suffered a concussion as well as some internal injuries that will probably take some time to heal. Things like bruised kidneys and as well two cracked ribs. Lucky for you Mr. Kimson your neck seems to not have sustained any damage in this scenario, and you are damn lucky.” he added. “How long” said Kimson quickly. “Well Mr. Kimson I am not a physio therapist or a Sport’s Medical expert. I know that men in your line of work are famous for not listening to Doctor’s when they tell you not to work. But there is a Sport’s expert in this hospital but he doesn’t come in on Mondays or Tuesdays and thus I’ll refer you to him and set up an appointment for you on Wednesday morning” said the Doctor. “That’s no good Doc, I have to be Phoenix on Wednesday, not locked up in here” said Kimson quickly. “I’m sorry Mr. Kimson but you shouldn’t be doing any traveling and in God’s name you shouldn’t be wrestling anyone for at least 6 weeks” said the Doctor directly. 6 weeks. That’s a long time. “You’re damn lucky that you are even alive Mr. Kimson” said the Doctor. “I don’t know how you survived, let alone the man you were fighting” Sovereign. “Doctor.... what’s his situation” said Kimson. “I’m sorry, that’s confidential” said the Doctor. Confidential my ass. That son of a bitch almost killed me, I at least deserve to know if he’s ever going to be back to his “normal” self again or not. That is only fair. “Do you have any family here in las Vegas Mr. Kimson?” said the Doctor Family. “Wait a second” said Kimson “That man I fought is my Brother in Law” said Kimson. “That makes me family and I have a legal right to know his situation” said Kimson. Who would have thought being his Brother would have paid off. “Brother in Law? How can a man do something like that to his family?” said Doctor Warren. “You’d be surprised Doc.” said Kimson. You would be to. Stupid bastard doesn’t know half the story, but if it means I’ll find out what I need to know then fine. I’ll beat around the bush as long as I can “His wife is my sister.” said Kimson. “We’ve been having some disagreements recently, so are you going to fill me in or what?” said Kimson. “I guess I have to.” said Warren. “Well since you know Mr. Forth you know that he has certain mental health issues and because of them he’s been transfered to a mental health facility in Los Angels where his wife, I guess your sister is living at this time. He is quite mentally unstable right now and there’s a fear that he’s a threat to the nursing and medial staff. Either way he’s not in any particular condition to fight. His right shoulder is a complete mess. He took a laceration on his bicep that cut down into the muscle tissue and sliced an artery and caused massive bloodloss. Somehow he stopped the bleeding somewhat as it says here in his chart he had... wrapped his arm in duct tape and continued to brawl for another 20 minutes. God Damn....” said the doctor trailing off. I remember that. I put that sick bitch through that window and slit him open. “Upon that damage to his rotator cuff on his right shoulder has limited his movement to only %35 or normal and that will require surgery in the near future. There is also a fear of nerve damage on his arm. Added upon that he lost an inordinate amount of blood, and tissue trauma to his arm. He suffered a mild concussion, broken jaw and lost 4 teeth.” said the Doctor. “They’ve been having trouble administering medication for fear of it mixing with what he normally takes and plus the amount of blood loss to his system is causing a lot of concern with the medical personal in the unit he’s currently in.” said the doctor. Jesus, how did he survive is right thought James to himself. At least it doesn’t look like his chances are good. Not exactly something James would have wished upon any man. But Sovereign deserved it. But in the end did James win? He’s with Leya right now, in LA. James felt the bite of bittersweet victory running through his veins as he looked up at the Doctor and nodded. “Well, Mr. Kimson I’m going to give you this piece of advice. If you know of a way to get a check up with a Doctor that will release you then that’s okay. But I won’t release you in the next 48hours myself. Otherwise you’re stuck here in this room and you should make the best of it. I’m going to get a nurse to come in and clean you up and get you into some clothing of some sort. Then I suggest you maybe contact your wife and let her know how you are because she’s been calling your company’s head quarters on a regular basis.” said The Doctor as he looked quickly at his watch. Victoria would be pissed thought James to himself. She was against this whole thing to start and now look at what shape he’s in right now. James leaned back in his bed and looked up at the celling and let out a disheartened sigh before nodding and thanking the Doctor with a quick and quite “thank you.” He may have gotten rid of Sovereign from eVo. But was he finished with him in his personal life? Only time will tell. Only Time can heal all wounds
