The start of The Industry is upon us..

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-30 The scene opens in a small room were Jason Relms is watching his match at the PPV. He keeps watching as he seems to be looking at every detail. It has now entered te point were Relms slams Wisdom through the announce table.Jason presses the pause button as he just glares at the screen..he whispers.. 'The Whole Fucking Show..' He then stands up and gets in front of the cameras. He looks at the cameras with his cocky smirk on his face..He then begins to talk about the night of the PPV.. 'I showed the world. I Know I did it. I can almost taste those titles comming to me. I can see me standing back in the lime light. I know eVo has its fan favorites, but none of the fans, or the wrestlers can deny that Jason Relms is the best damn wrestler to hit the screen. Lets Face it, I made that PPV..Should of been renamed to The Jason Relms Show..' He then takes a pause as people cant believe it. He is takeing the credit of the whole PPV. He's ego is now higher then ever before. All he has in his mind is capturing the eVo Tag Belts once again. That evil looking smile is still on his face. But nothing ever takes him off the topic of Jason Relms..As he says.. 'Everyone might not love me but you damn well know they respect me. Wisdom, I know it must of felt nice when I sent you through the table. I admit it, it felt damn good..But it wasnt because I slammed you..it was because I could feel it then. At that momenet, I had a flash back. One with me holding up the eVo titles into the air..That moment, its about to repeat itself.' He takes another pause as he gets a little bit more serious..He then continues.. 'This place needs to have a respected Tag Divison. We all know that Relms bring respect every were I go. Its time that the whole world faces the music. That everyone looks at Jason Relms and says he is simply..the whole show..' He then looks to the side as a door opens..It's his tag team Partner..'Porn$tar' Perry Quinn.Quinn comes as the two give each other a handshake.Perry then says.. 'Jason, That PPV was off the hook!You did it ALL man..' Relms smiles as he says.. 'What did you exspect..I am the show man..think about it.' Quinn nods his head as Relms then continues.. 'Quinn, are you ready for it?' Perry seems confused as he had to ask.. 'What you talking about?' Relms then continues with a cocky smirk.. 'Our way to the tag titles man. Im leading us to the belts after Revelations. Everyone has there eyes on me..I mean EVERYONE..' Quinn smiles as he says.. 'You damn right..A PORN$TAR is always ready for his performance.' Relms laughs as he then finishes the interview by saying.. 'Evoltuion, you all need to look at the big picture. I dont give a damn if you believe you deserve the tag titles are not. Because there is one team that the truely belong two. As The Industry is building..' As the cameras fade to black he says.. 'The Show is rising...'
