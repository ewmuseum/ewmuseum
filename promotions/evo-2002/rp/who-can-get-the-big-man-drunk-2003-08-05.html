Who can get the big man drunk?

5 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-05 When we last left off, Machine was holding his own in the drinking game. His large liver seemed to be a big advantage, until Thug roC made him a drink. Thug seemed to by as the sky at the time, and didn’t realize that he mixed 151 with Malibu Rum. Machine, the big man that he is, guzzled the mix down anyhow, thinking that it’s impossible to get him drunk. That obviously isn’t true anymore. We re-enter the room with Machine stumbling around while the rest laugh. Thug roC seems to have gotten a lot better, as he takes pictures of the big man, drunk as can be. “I’m not drunk gosh darnit!” Machine says while being questioned by Hedges. “I’m just a little tired, that’s all!” “Is that why you had a staring contest with the teddy bear?” Thug roC asked, laughing at the same time. “He lost! I don’t care what anybody says!” At that moment, Machine starts walking backwards into the couch, before falling over the arm of the couch and lays down. Thug roC is quick to hurry with a camera. Machine tries to cover his face so that he won’t be caught on film. “No pictures!” Machine yells. He then finds the remote control and puts it on his forehead to hide himself. Thug roC just laughs as he takes the picture. The camera pans to a view of the room, Rob B. is seen, still laid out on the living room floor, sleeping. He looks like a big star, laying on his back with his legs and arms spread out. Machine picks himself off the couch as Thug roC has an evil grin on his face. “Hey Hedges, I bet you ten bucks that Machine doesn’t have the balls to kick a guy while he’s down.” Thug roC states. Hedges gets the hint and plays along, “You know what, you’re right. Machine wouldn’t dare.” “What are you guys talking about?” Machine asks, holding himself up by posting against the wall. “Machine, why don’t you go over and kick Rob in his foot? Just to see if you’d do it.” Thug roC snickers as he asked. “RoC, I don’t get it.” Hedges says while Machine walks over to Rob who’s still sleeping. “Why would you want Machine to kick Rob in his foot?” “Simple, Machine is clumsy as it is. Not to mention he’s drunk. He has no aim whatsoever. So by telling him to kick Rob in his foot…” Suddenly Machine cocks his leg back and kicks Rob B. right in the groin. Rob B. suddenly sits up and groans loudly in pain. He is quick to roll on his side and curl into a fetal position. “…he kicks him in the balls instead.” Thug roC laughs loudly along with the rest of the crew that’s attending the party. The scene quickly fades with the whole entire party laughing at Rob B. while he looks to want to cry. It’s not everyday you get kicked in the sack by a 300+ pounder. The scene opens back up, but no longer at the party. Thug roC instead is standing in a ring. The arena is empty, not a soul in sight. You can’t even see a ring crew or any workers. Just Thug roC and the cameraman. Thug roC sits on the top of a turnbuckle looking around into the empty seats. “This arena use to be jam packed. All the screaming roC-TV fans were all standing on their feet while Mason was going down in the roCing Horse. But instead of the fans getting what they wanted, they got robbed. For I didn’t win the Continental championship like I was suppose to. Instead, Mason took away the glory…or did he? Did Mason really win that night? Usually when you win, you go home happy. But I don’t think neither Mason brothers went home happy that night. For I, Thug roC exposed those two frauds for what they truly are. And I’m damn proud of it. And on Legends, I’ll be sure to continue and make sure that neither man, ever forgets how they betrayed their fans.”
