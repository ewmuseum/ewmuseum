Ulimited Possibilities

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/01/2002 ::Weights clang together as the camera pans to see Jinx working out at the Unlimited Complex. He stops his workout and wipes his face off with a towel at his side. He slings the towel across his shoulder. His Cell Championship Belt is firmly attached to his waist. He sits back as he sees the camera crew and awaits them to come and talk to a quarter of the greatest group ever assembled, the Unlimited.:: Jinx: Well if it ain't the American public. It looks like things have gotten very interesting around here. We have got a new television network, that is ran by an old buddy of mine, who is known for shock television. He twists shows and makes them sexier, more violent, and just simpley more hardcore. Which he definately chose the right place to come for that, because eVo has it all. And of course everyone knows the real reason that OSE chose Evolution, it is the only place in the wrestling world that you will find the Unlimited. Which also by the way has doubled in size. We now have the man with heart made of ice, the hottest rookie in wrestling today, Scott Frost and let's definately not forget that hot little number that just so happens to be the High Impact Champion, HBC. Surprise after suprise we have brought into your home every night. You never know what to expect from us next. And that is just how we like it. It also looks like we have a little match going on this week on legends. It looks to me like Dreamer is just a little upset over losing the best thing that ever come his way and not to mention by being humiliated in doing so by the kick heard around the world, so he has gathered a few boring losers to go down with him, when they take us on come Legends. All of this and the only one that has even had the beans to come out and say one word about us this week is Jason Jameson, and after watching his little promo/interview, it was obvious that he forgot his Prozac and Xanax that morning...dopehead. So any of you that wants to try to become famous for destroying the Unlimited, make sure you have your death warrent signed and your living wills all set up for your families, because you will die trying to live out that dream. I would also like to let one that goes by the name Asis, not to get too comfortable with that nice little Title that you are holding, because you may make it through Queasy the not-so-great, but I am going to be the predator that is waiting in the wings to take everything that I can from the weaker one, and all that you have that I could possibly want is another piece of gold. So watch yourself. Jinx is coming for you. As for Dreamer and friends, Asis, and the rest of Evolution, Welcome to your Destiny! ::Jinx gets up heads to the locker room. Once he arrives, he is greeted by a very lovely Heartbreak Chic coming out of the showers with nothing but a towel. The camera man and Jinx drop their mouths open.:: HBC: Hi Jinx. Jinx: Well, how YOU doin? No let me answer that...you are FINE! ::HBC giggles and leaves the camera view, tossing the towel atop Jinx's head. Jinx rips the towel off his head taking in the quick view of HBC naked walking to her personal dressing room. The camera crew keeps their attention to Jinx's reaction. He slings her towel across his neck and smiles.:: Jinx: Man, is it nice to have a lady in the house. ::He shuts his locker after getting a few things and head to the showers.::
