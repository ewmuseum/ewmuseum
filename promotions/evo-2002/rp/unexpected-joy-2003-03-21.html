Unexpected Joy

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-21-03 'Yeah.....I understand. Yeah, I he won the match. Yeah. I get my re-match at the Pay Per View.' The voice of Frost is heard. The scene fades in and Frost is talking on his cell phone, while driving his car. Frost looks to be very concentrated. 'Yeah, I will. Okay. I will see you when I get home. I love you too Katherine, see ya.' Frost says into the cell phone. Frost hangs up the cell phone and tosses it next to him and it lands in the passanger seat. Frost looks to be in deep thought, and then the car slows down and he gets out of it before grabbing his cell phone and he shuts the car door. The camera pans over and Frost is seen walking into a convience store. The scene fades away, and when it fades back in Frost is walking into his home and is met by his fiancée Seara. 'Hey Scott, did you get my drink?' Seara asks. 'Yep.' Frost replies. Seara looks at Frost who actually seems happy, and Frost kisses her and holds her deeply. 'God I've missed doing that.' Frost says to Seara. Seara seeming very surprised smiles. 'Yeah, i've missed you doing that too. I thought I was about to start losing you.' Seara tells Frost. Frost smiles. 'Nothing will come between us.' Frost assures. Seara smiles and then raises her eyebrow. 'Why are you so happy?' she asks. 'Because, I get my re-match at the pay per view.' Frost tells her. Seara looks at Frost. 'REALLY?!' Seara screams with excitement. 'Yeah, I mean. Jason, he pulled out a good fight and this time I will expect it all. He is a good man. But I gave him his chance, and he is giving me my chance to re-claim the championship' Frost tells her. 'Just think about it babe, I have all the right to be happy. I've got you, and I've got this chance to re-claim myself and make eVolution history...ONCE AGAIN!?' Frost announces. Seara cocks her eyebrow. 'What? How?' she asks him. 'Because, if i get the title back I will be the first ever person in eVolution to be a multiple time Universal champion! THAT'S HISTORY! HISTORY!' Frost smiles. Seara looks away.... 'History.....History........' Frost whispers and looks around his house. The scene fades away.
