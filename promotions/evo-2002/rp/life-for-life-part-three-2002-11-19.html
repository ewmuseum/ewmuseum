Life for Life... part three

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life for Life… Part Three! The scene opens in a low class looking living room with a dimly lighten lamp in the corner, the surroundings shady in appearance with an ugly orange and red colored couch resting partially against the wall with a cheap eighties wooden boxed ancient television and all complete with a wooden crate with a plank on top for a coffee table. Empty food containers from various food establishments 'Greg’s Greasy Chicken' 'Slop King Burgers' and the most classic 'Beefy Cheese Nurfin Tifes' are randomly littered across the table. Morpheus can be seen sitting on the couch with his head down almost like he’s contemplating what could get him out of this situation. The scene pans slowly left, the straggly looking mean lady with her dirty blonde hair over her eyes standing wearing her cheap plush navy house coat. She looks like she would tear into Omega all at the perfect chance, gripping a revolver in her hand like she’s one with the gun, like the gun is really with her. The lady steps up closer to Omega. The lady speaks slurring her words like the low class trash of an individual she is 'You know when this all blows over and the heats off this bullet has a reservation for the back of your throat. This is one bullet you bite like it or not, hah nothing but an oversized bitch' The lady laughs all ignorant like and spits in the general direction of Morpheus. Morpheus snaps his head up, his eyes open wide, he’s starting to rage. Starting to feel some the sick freedom he once hold over the Industry and all of humanity. Morpheus his cold gray eyes burning into the lady, he’s staring beyond wild belief at this lady like he’s considering lashing out at her and beat her face in like squashed melon. Morpheus has a mean look to him but the man is about the mind, intellectual genus like you wouldn’t believe, he mentally drives opponents insane before taking them down and hitting them where it hurts physically. Instead Morpheus smiles half sick, but half willingly... Morpheus raises his hand and reaches outwards with his hand pointing at the savage looking lady. Morpheus speaks loud with a deep but reassuring kind of voice. 'When this is all said and done I promise you and your partner in crime will become victims of a prophecy so far up and beyond anything you have ever known or cease to realize. This night alone with them thugs out there, with the police and the trailer park version of Bonnie and Clyde is enough to drive me back insane. I’m right on the edge and your fuckin pushing lady!!!' Morpheus stands up aggressively and quick. The woman aims the gun right at Omega, he’s quick to react and bats the gun from her hand, he pulls one out of his own coat just as fast and points it at her. The gun shines clean and the tables have turned. The magnum with extended barrel from one of the thugs earlier in the night. The woman is appalled at the site of the gun, her face drops into the sudden look of anger and depression. The rabid looking, over sized chiwawa backs up slowly. Morpheus with a grin on his face waves the gun semi pointed at her, he speaks with a little more confidence behind his voice now. 'Sit down on the couch before this becomes the bullet you bite bitch.” Morpheus pauses and laughs. “You cant win with me. You are nothing but narrow-minded, ignorant, moronic trash, you and your man. You will never compare to what I am... Or what I used to be but after tonight the way my knees are shaking, the air is being breathed makes me wonder if I will slip into another crazed psycho episode or not? There is nothing stopping me, its not like it hasn’t happened before. You and your man and everyone who stands in my path tonight are fucked. I’m my own man and I don’t need anything from anyone. Now sit the fuck on the couch!' The woman quickly tries to deek Morpheus and alert her old man. Morpheus looks at her and the gun back and forth, he thumps her right across the head with the butt end of it, her knees buckle out and she starts to fall over. Morpheus catches her and throws her on the couch. The woman dazed and seeing stars and bright light. Morpheus grabs her gun and empties the bullets and he drops them in the empty 'Greg’s Greasy Chicken' bucket. The bucket stained with sick grease. Morpheus almost looks disgusted and wipes his fingers off on the couch along with the prints off the gun. Morpheus carefully holds the gun with his coat sleeve, wiping every piece of the gun he can with care on his shirt and throws it down by the woman. Morpheus quickly on the move sneaks by where he sees the man looking out the bedroom window where he first found entrance. Morpheus stands at the coat rack and finds a red plaid jacket and an eighties trucker, redneck fashioned kind of hat. Morpheus roots through his pockets and empties his belongings, he salvages and discards of his coat in the corner and quickly puts on the coat and hat, he stuffs his belongings into the pockets of the new coat. Morpheus holds the gun and shrugs. He wipes the prints off the magnum and holds it with the sleeve of the red plaid jacket and throws it down by the black leather coat. Morpheus stares into the room at Roger all sneaky and like he’s the lost soldier fighting for his sick twisted right for another hope and chance at evil and insane reign in all powers. He might be washed up but the look in his eyes shows his negative hatred and disturbed passion might have returned. Morpheus laughs to himself Roger can be heard speaking hyperactive and full of energy. 'Holy shit, they are climbing the balcony. The man looks out and Morpheus dodges out the way. The man looks back down and Morpheus quietly unlocks the door. He silently opens the door to the apartment and ducks out in disguise. Morpheus flees the scene of the crime ducking away from the heat as fast he can. Morpheus walks quickly down the corridor of the run down, slum of an apartment building. Police run up the coming stairs. Three of them don’t waste any time approaching Morpheus… One of officers eyes Omega really well, he speaks. 'That’s not him men… Move the culprit could be getting away. I said MOVE!' The officer screams out loud. Morpheus oddly eyes the police like he’s lost in confusion as they bought into his act, he runs down the stairs quickly with a soft chuckle that can barely be heard from him tromping down the stairs. Morpheus reaches the bottom and forcefully pushes the door and opens to find surrounding cruisers with blue and red lights flashing almost blinding bright. Morpheus rubs his eyes from the shocking flash. The officer’s guns can all be heard mechanically, the triggers locking in place. One approaches and speaks loud 'hey you, stop where you are. That’s far enough. Put your hands up and you better hope that you weren’t involved in that mess. Because now I have to work over time hours all into the night over this shit' The officer smirks all cocky. Morpheus with that odd and confused look, he throws in the look of being half scared and right frightened look. The officer radios through his hand held two-way communicator. The rugged looking officer speaks with the same tone of cocky, policemen stereotyped voice 'Hey Arnolds, Is the man you two ran into earlier?' There’s a pause. Moments later the officer can be heard through the two-way radio that eyed Omega down before, his voice distinctly remembered. 'That’s not the man but we need all units up these stairs. I found one man and one woman in this apartment and found the evidence included. The gun was found along with the coat and the works. This woman knocked out on the couch. This is real pretty boys. Get moving up here and clean this up. I have them both cuffed and packaged ready to go for you. Case closed. Over and Out!' The transmission stops. Morpheus stands more calmly and at ease. Morpheus walks backwards and away from the scene slowly speeding up. The fake act of that frightened look on his face slowly disappears and fades into a casual grin. Morpheus walks down the street with his head down way the way he appeared before the chaos all begun except the different attire. Morpheus walking down the street and an expensive looking Cadillac black limousine pulls up slowly beside Omega and rolls to a stop beside him. The tinted black window barely rolls down but enough for only Omega to see in. His face drops some when he sees the man. The man from within the limo speaks and his words cannot be made out, they sound muffled. Morpheus looks like he’s almost bitter and very unhappy about this arrival of someone. Words are heard from within the vehicle loud now 'Get in this limo before I report you to them myself Morpheus… You know who I am and I definitely know about your act. Your choice Omega, you can come with me and listen to what I have to say or rot in jail for the rest of your worthless life”. The man laughs to himself Morpheus snarls half in rage and also in concern. Morpheus stands contemplating to himself if he could maybe run and escape well he could but its like he realizes that its do what this unknown, mysterious character wanted or what he threatens and proclaims could very well come true. Morpheus reasoning with him self in his complex mind, he comes back to his senses and walks around to the other side of the limo. He opens and enters the door quickly and slamming it back shut. The vehicle squeals off quickly and abandons the scene. The scene ends to flashing lights and Roger flying out of the door and falls flat on his face with hard impact. Roger cuffed and the situation out of his control. The scene ends. Morpheus seemed to have gotten himself out of a tight squeeze but has he found himself into another one? The mystery of who was in that limousine and what he wants from him is unknown. What is known, Morpheus could be out with further vengeance but still questioned if it is past his time and washed up. The man has taken the scene by frightening surprise before and its never doubted he could do it again...
