Tainted Love aftermath

26 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-26-03 Los Angeles, California USC Medical Center In the immortal words of Faroque, “Damn!” That is all that can be said following the aftermath of Tainted Love. Strictly Prohibited finally revealed himself as the former cruiserweight sensation, Nik VanCore (nVc). Someone whom HBC has had a lot of history with in EDW. Xavier Citizen flipped his dammed lid once and for all, S. still strutting around like he owns the place. (Oh wait he does doesn’t he?.....Damn!). And The Syndicate are sitting in their private room at USC Medical Center following the carnage afterwards. For those wondering about this dynamic, historical, legendary team, all shall be revealed…. Its room 225 on the third floor. HBC and BDD are sitting in their respective beds, recuperating from their respective matches at Tainted Love. BDD suffered a category-1 concussion after being speared through the cell by Asis. Damnedest thing I’ve ever seen. A few days rest, and he’ll be back to normal. HBC fared about as well after Xavier Citizen tried to gut her like a fish. She required 35 stitches to close the gash on her forehead caused by Citizen. As for Luther Wisdom tossing in the towel like that? Hard to say? His question for HBC? Only she has an answer. The Syndicate is watching the replays of Tainted Love on their portable DVD player, watching several scenes and finally getting to where Wisdom tossed in the towel to save his partner from a further bloodletting. The graphic is disturbing and probably will be edited by those TSN-1 rejects, but in this case, probably isn’t a bad thing after all. HBC stares at the screen, watching Citizen over and over, cutting her with the razor blade as she reaches up and feels the dissolvable stitches in her forehead as rage comes over her face. For once, HBC actually looses it. She fires the portable DVD player into the wall, watching it smash into a million pieces and tosses the vase next to her down the hall nearly missing some poor orderly. BDD looks over at HBC and knows the frustration she is going through. For the second time in her career, someone damn near took her out of the game once and for all. The first time was a tag team who nearly broke her neck, she responded by breaking theirs and making sure they never walked again. Funny thing about the Heartbreaker, as beautiful as she is, as talented in the ring as she is, people always forget she has a mean streak just a vicious and ruthless as Citizen himself. It doesn’t come out very much, but when it does….(damn!) Suddenly, a nurse walks in with a huge bouquet of roses. Must be about 3 dozen in their by the size of it and is places down by HBC. She looks at them and wonders if there from BDD. Couldn’t be, we came in the same ambulance together. She reaches over to grab the card and find out who it is from. “Dearest Heartbreaker, Please accept these as a token to say how sorry I am that I couldn’t help you further in our match with Xavier/NVC. Though I know our partnership could be over as you are free again, I hope you would continue to tag with me, so that we could recapture the tag titles, and finish once and for all, this unfinished business within this division. The Unlimited, S., and my old mentors need to pay for their actions with your Kiss of Death. In regards to the end, I thought, I felt I did what was right. If you hold any animosity towards me in regards to it, I understand. Citizen is a psycho and I had nothing but the best of intensions. Please contact me as soon as you can. Always, Luther Wisdom” HBC places down the card and looks over to BDD. Her friend, partner, mentor of six years and looks down. BDD reading the expression on her face knows what was on the card all to well. BDD: Let me guess, from Wisdom right? HBC: Yes. Basically he was sorry for tossing in the towel and was trying to protect me from Citizen. BDD: Xavier is a bloody psycho. I could just kill him myself and be done with it. Using you to send me a message. ARGH!!! HBC: I know D, I know. Wisdom wants me to continue tagging up with him, finishing this business within the tag team ranks. Sure we were two-time champions under auspicious circumstances, but now, he wants to really team, and really make an impact on Evo and needs my help. I don’t know really what to do. I can understand his side, but I also want to team up with you and level this place to the ground. I don’t know if Wisdom has it or not to do that. It be like starting from square one all over again. With you, we know each others moves so well we don’t even have to think about it. Wisdom and I have kind of gelled, but not where its fluid enough. Plus, I don’t even know him that well really. BDD: It’s a tough call C, believe me, I know. I remembered teaming with Callous prior to you, and the first two months were rough, after that, well you know the story. HBC: D……what do I do? BDD: C. Only you know for certain. I would love to team with you again and rock this place to the core, I also have some unfinished business with Xavier regarding all of this. You two did do very well considering the situation. I’ve been in his position, and in yours. Revenge is a very powerful unifying factor. Basically, C. No matter what you choose to do, you will be very successful in whatever decision you make. Twice you’ve been tag team champions, most ever so far. I enjoy watching you perform. Do I think you have unfinished business their. Yes. I think the both of you were screwed by S. Do I think something should be done about it? Yes. No matter if it is with me or with Wisdom. You will be successful. HBC: You want to go after Citizen don’t you? BDD: Is it that obvious? HBC: Well when that little vein on your head starts popping out like it is now, I can tell. BDD: Citizen will get his. Once and for all, this history between us, this feud will come to an end even if it kills the both of us. You don’t have to make your decision now C, but he does deserve an answer. Look into your heart and decide. If Wisdom crosses you, I will deal with him myself worse than I shall with Xavier. HBC: I guess I’ll know soon. I don’t want to talk about it anymore. You feeling ok D? BDD: I have a splitting headache, and I’m ticked off! HBC: So just like always huh! *Laughs* BDD: *Laughs* Yeah….. (Fade)
