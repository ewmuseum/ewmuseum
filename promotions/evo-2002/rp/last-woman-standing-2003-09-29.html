Last Woman Standing

29 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-29 A scene opens up with an eagle flying right through the sky. The camera looks down and all is seen is dirt and cactus'. The HeartBreack Chick (HBC) is seen running right up to the camera, but is breathing at a very speedy pace. Sweat is all over her head. HBC pulls out a bottle of water and takes a sip. She uses the back of his hand and wipes off all the sweat on her forehead. HBC finally catches her breath and looks into the camera... 'As you watch this promo, you will realize that I am sweating. I am sweating from the five miles I have just ran. I am running the five miles so that I can come to Digital Destruction on top of my game in the most enduranced of all matches, The Last Woman Standing match against Seara. Voice- 'Of course it would be her. Why don't you tell her why?' 'Tell her why? Do I need to? I mean, it's only common sense.' HBC fans herself for a minute and takes a deep breath. 'On Legends, on PPV’s, I fight Seara. One of the top superstars in all of Evolution, a woman who in many ways parallels myself. Lately I have been watching the Evo real closely, and all I see is people bitching. All I see is people bitching about how much power or how much say or whatever we have in the Evolution. Well, if Yoshii, Frost or any other retards haven't cleared that up yet... I'll do it one more fucking time. Big Daddy Dreamer and myself have nothing to do with what goes on backstage. We do not control what happens in storylines or whatever. The only control we have is in the ring. At Legends, everyday, hell every week, we even showed your bitch ass' that we're the best in the ring. Though some idiots, which includes Luther Wisdom, Tanaka and yes even Scott Frost himself, still try to play off what happened. We beat all of your ass', and NONE of you beat us. Some things never change, and if they do... it doesn't happen in a two week period. So if you think that when you got screwed because of us... it'll be our pleasure to demonstrate it once more. Call it luck, or simply put it as we beat your ass'... either way... we beat you.' Voice- 'That you did, C. Though it doesn't stop there.' “No it doesn’t, but for now is a different story, at Digital Destruction the top two females ever in this business go head to head in a Last Woman Standing match inside a steel cage. The Diva against The Playmate, champion against Champion. Never in my seven and half career have a met someone who is damn near similar to me in every way, well except for one thing, I hate to lose and will do everything I can to prevent that from happening. Seara is a top competitor, she is hungry to finally do something she has yet to accomplish in her career…….and that is beat the HeartBreak Chick. 4-0 in singles, 3-0 in tag matches. You’ve come close, but that only counts for so much. In this cage, in this match, I have more experience in my pinkie finger than most of Evolution in these types of situations as Seara will find out. At Digital Destruction, the end of Seara will take place and the new more violent HBC will emerge. More sweat runs down HBCs face as the heat seems to grow. 'My goal in my return to the Evo was to become the first ever female Universal Champion. At this point in time... I am a ruler, but not the one I want to be. My time will come. See, my major problem with all of you is that you are trying to dethrone me of my rightful crown. That is the major problem that we all have against you idiots. Old people have the saying that goes like... if at first you don't succeed, try again. Well, in your case... if at first you don't succeed against me... you never will. Keep that in mind.' The heat begins to intensify as HBC starts to fan herself in a quick motion. 'Seara... I feel I must ask.. why so quiet? You made a challenge.. I accepted, but since then.. you've been silent. What, did you think I wasn't going to accept? If you did.. you should've known better. I do not let down my opponent in anyway. And come Digital Destruction... I'll show you just what I mean. Oh, and after I beat your ass to a bloody pulp, please don't come back with another excuse just take it like you know how.' Voice- 'Alright, C. You still have two more miles to run. Let's go.'
