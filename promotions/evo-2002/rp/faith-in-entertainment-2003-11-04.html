Faith in Entertainment

4 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-04 There are only a few that truly have that ability to entertain the masses, to send them home satisfied, to make their money well spent and go home worry free. These kind of entertainers are hard to come by but do exist. As a matter of fact there are only two individuals with that talent and star power. They can be described with one name. Astonishing Entertainment The tandem of Las Vegas magician Travis Kyle and his friend and assistant Angel Stone. The newest tag team to burst onto the eVolution scene. Aside from being the kings of magic in Las Vegas, the dynamic duo are no strangers to putting asses in seats and performing spectacular shows that boggle the minds of all who watch. The eVolution management knows this for a fact. How else would Astonishing Entertainment be placed in a tag team title match on their first night in their first match in the company? The answer is simple. “AE” is the only tag team that can truly live up to it’s hype and deliver like no other tag team in the industry can. True, you’ve got teams like The Syndicate, The Heartbreakers, The Body Snatchers and St. Violence, but the fans want a team they can believe in. A team that can satisfy their entertaining needs and give them a reason to follow this fledgling tag team division. It all starts at Faith in five days. In five days the fate of the tag team division will take a drastic turn for the better. The division will finally be represented by a duo that can hold the belts with pride, honor, glory and prestige. Faith will mark the genesis of Astonishing Entertainment. And astonishing it will be…
