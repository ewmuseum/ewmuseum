Destruction of Four

24 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th January 2003 Asis has had many different transitional periods in his life, times when something will happen to make him feel differently, act differently. There have been many incidents in his life, which have forced him to become more and more aggressive each time they’ve happened. And while they seem to have happened quite frequently within the last year of his life, Legends XXII was the final straw. Asis’ life has become more and more violent since his introduction into wrestling in EdW and he has gone through increasingly “demonic” changes within eVolution. He has made many enemies in this company and destroyed many enemies in this company. Because of Legends XXII his mind is now completely focused on the destruction of four certain individuals. Throughout his career Asis has promised people that if he did not destroy them originally, he would get them sooner or later. And he always did, these men would be no different to the others. And they’d find that out as soon as they next stepped in an eVolution arena… (Asis has been getting in front of eVolution cameras much more recently which is different to what everyone is used to. He is normally a very reclusive, withdrawn person and very rarely does he actually give a promo for the benefit of the fans or to add heat to his match. But just recently he has become a lot more verbal and has been requesting camera crews to be present for him to reveal his thoughts and feelings for whatever reason. The day after Legends XXII, Asis contacted Daniel Somers and demanded a camera crew be sent to Phoenix, Arizona to find him so he could express something.) A sense of time is something that I have never really had. Days and years both often have the same meaning to me. After I was left only with my Dad as a parent, my childhood was a blur of time. Days seemed like years, years seemed like centuries, by the time I left my Dad and England at the age of 18 to move back to my home land, America, it felt like I was in my later part of my life. Years past of my living on the streets, mugging people for money, sheltering in abandoned warehouses, stealing food, all things I’m not ashamed of doing. I did what I had to survive. But once I discovered wrestling as a career, things changed. Suddenly every minute of every day became precious to me, life was no longer something that dragged on, but was something that should be cherished. But not because wrestling had made me see life in a different light, it merely enhanced my already bleak perspective of life. I’ve never really liked people. They tend to just irritate and annoy me. And when I found wrestling, I found a place where I could express my “feelings” for people and not have to suffer the consequences that I may have to on the streets. I could brutalise, maim and destroy people in anyway I saw fit and it was all allowed. And it still is… (The location Asis is in cannot be distinguished from the camera shot, as has been the way as of late. You can see Asis clearly as the light from the camera is doing it’s job, but other than that light there does not seem to be any. The feeling is that Asis is inside somewhere, as there is no other noise apart from his breath which is fairly controlled, but very, very heavy.) I have been screwed over and fucked about more than enough since I started my wrestling career and even more since I joined eVolution. My take on this has always been that I don’t really care, because to me wrestling is not about the winning or the losing, the fame or the titles, but merely the pain you can inflict on your opponent. That is the thing that I hold most dear. But the events that I went through at Legends XXII changed my opinion slightly. You see within my Continental Title match against Big Daddy Dreamer I was screwed over by four people. Four. Four people have the nerve to attack me and screw me out of an opportunity to prove myself to Unlimited. Sean Mason with his chair attack, he does not even understand what he has done. My attack on him was an act that I had no control over, an act of pure emotion. His retaliation was one that stung me and as soon as the chair connected with my head and smashed it against the ring post, my mind became hell bent on returning the compliment. My mind became focused on BDD as well, when he pinned me winning a belt that belongs to Unlimited and gaining himself a win that he will regret. And then came the masked duo of Strictly Prohibited and Yoshii Tanaka, two men who have constantly attacked me from behind and in the shadows. Two men who wear masks for fear of me finding who they are and slaughtering their families. As soon as they stepped in that ring, they earned my malice as well. When I stood up in that ring and saw BDD, SP and Tanaka in front of me, I was ready. I would have taken on all three of them at once. And I do not blame Xander and Angel for pulling me out of the ring; the frame of mind I was in I would have ended lives. (The camera is very closely focused in on Asis’ face and he closes his eyes briefly as if he is blinking, but his eyes are shut for longer than a blink. Longer than a blink, but not long enough for them to be consciously shut. He then smiles and opens his eyes, the red deathly stare burning a whole in the camera.) Some people say I bring these attacks on myself as “I’m the one that starts it”. But I sure as hell finish it as well. Mason, BDD, Strictly Prohibited, Tanaka and indeed all of eVolution should know I am not one for mind games. I do not do that kind of thing, I’m merely a “tool of anarchy”, I see and then I destroy. And I’m not one for the back and forth way that feuds can be. I attacked him, he attacks me back, so I go and attack him again. That’s not the way I work. I fight, I destroy, I maim, I kill. Admittedly when the events of Legends XXII first happened I was pissed off and I am still now. In fact I’m amazed I’ve stayed calm for this whole interview. At first I was going to ask Danny for any kind of match where I could get my hands on all four men at once, I didn’t care what kind of match it would be, it could’ve been a handicap four on one TLC, explosive barbwire instead of ring ropes, anything goes match for all I cared. And to be honest if Dan wants to change the matches at New Year’s eVolution to that match, I will wrestle in it. But when I thought about things more logically, I realised that Yoshii and SP are already mine. I get to face them in the tag title match that Unlimited are defending in and the match that I will be wrestling in. The fact that there are two mystery teams in that match does not even come into account, whoever they are I do not give a fuck, I will destroy them as well if they stand in my way. And also on the same night, instead of BDD giving me a rematch, the man who screwed me out of my chance to bring more gold to Unlimited will be facing him instead. And I will still be around later on that night. So it will be a prosperous night for me, but the others should watch their backs. Because I have a habit at getting back at people who offend me and this time I plan on it being sooner rather than later…
