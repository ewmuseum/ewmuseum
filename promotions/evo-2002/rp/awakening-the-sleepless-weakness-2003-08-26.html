Awakening - The Sleepless Weakness

26 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-26 I lie awake in the moonlight of the passing sorrowful midnights, my eyes open wide but seeing absolutely nothing. Nothing beyond the confines of my own draining mind, that is. The nights have gone by slowly ever since my birth upon the dunes of a ravaged mind. I stepped outside, through an open door that had been closed all those years before, carrying a smile on my face. Sunlight etched warmth over my eyes, and I passed into existence like winter passes into spring. That was ages ago. Many moonless nights have passed since then. Restlessness overshadows dreariness most evenings now, and my brain wanders upon the nightly stars that dance in radiance of their own life. Questions have come, and answers remain hidden behind patches of shadows I don’t care to venture through. Too boring, and I don’t have time for such meaningless shit. So I remain wide awake most nights, dreaming of dreams that will never come true and scheming of schemes that will more then likely never take place. Thoughts roam freely, but there is nothing for them to grasp hold of. There is nothing worthy enough of their evil thoughts to conspire upon. There is silence. A silence that is by no means golden. I can feel the blood pumping through my veins, but silently waiting to leak from my pores and cuts in the heat of battle. My bones beg to ache, waiting for the moment when they will be pushed to the max after a long, tedious fight. I have yet to take part in what I feel is a much disserved slaughter. My body wrenches for the time when it can truly end life before it can start. I want to rip the world apart, and drink the blood of the tainted. I want to bathe in the tears of those who have sinned against me. I want to breathe the final breaths of a million fuckers, dying at my feet. I wish I may, I wish you might, grant my wish tonight. Death to the lifeless world, with my hands around its neck. These are the thoughts that run through my head as I lay awake, eyes bleeding tears of boredom with the moon shining down on my face through a dirty window. I can feel the beer swarming in my stomach, and the vomit mixing. I need something to stop the sickness. Something to end the boredom. Someone to hurt. Someone to kill. Someone to quench this thirst.
