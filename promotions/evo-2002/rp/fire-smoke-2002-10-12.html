Fire.Smoke

12 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Silent rivers flowed through the already defined black crevices. Locks that stood with their openings providing the river bend. Dusk already settled, as the darkness before it's brethren of Dawn set in. Red glow fading back and forth, it's black and gray offerings fall to the ground beneath. Patience rests like a veil, of wet softness dampening it's surroundings. The familiar clacking sound resides itself again, as another comes tumbling down the rabbit hole. 'You been waiting long?' Silence. 'Whatever. About the file you wanted.' 'You don't have it.' 'All I could find is what you already know, or could come across by yourself. Why not just get in touch with that friend of hers.' 'They aren't friends, and I don't care to do business with him. Thanks for nothing.' 'You know I tried...' Utterances continued, but they were now inaudible. Lifelessness pacing itself down the widened path as few white lights blazed. Orange lighting allowed itself to flow over top, forming it's delicate outline, as it faded away to let the next take over. Meaningless gestures ensued providing the opening to his safehaven. Destination achieved, a relieved sigh took over. Thud as clumps fell off, landing in a pile by the entranceway. Hand rubbed across his face, as he began to converse. 'Things are going fine.' The darkness remained silent, as no answer floated into the air. 'Everything so far is going accordingly. It shouldn't be long now. October 27th is Faith, and by then everything will have fallen into place.' Lifelessness dragged itself away, as did the reflection in the glass.
