Criminal Acts: Part 2, the aftermath

8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 8th 2003 SONAVABITCH! -=[The loud profanity opens us up to the scene of the crime. Thug roC sits in the back of the ambulance, very pissed off. He has just woken up from the attack with a huge headache. Adam Carr stands by with police, answering their questions and Vega is doing the same. Thug roC hops down from the ambulance without assistance and walks around, trying to wear off the pain. Thug roC is rushed by a few reporters who throw a bunch of questions at the same time. Thug roC holds his back in pain as his face tells them how he feels. A few back up before he answers as the others try to hear what Thug roC has to say]=- “I can’t believe this sh!t, how the hell is somebody going to attack me, Thug roC from behind?” Thug roC is very pissed off. “Who the hell has the guts to attack the best damn cruiserweight in the business while he is outside the ring? Obviously somebody wants to play dirty, and I’ll play dirty all right. Whoever did this; better believe that I will get them for this!” -=[Cops hurry to grab Thug roC before he is able to say what’s on his mind. They bring him inside a truck where a lot of video equipment is seen. Thug roC is still furious while the cops are trying to show him something]=- “Now we understand the fury that flows through your veins right now Mr. Rocker” The cop says, trying to calm Thug roC down. “but we just want you to answer some questions while the event is fresh in your mind. Now we have this surveillance camera that was shot from inside the store, and you can see the attack through the display glass.” “And?” Thug roC says, very irritated at the moment. “We just want you to see if you can see anything out of the ordinary that may lead us to a suspect.” The cop starts putting a tape into the VCR. “Fine.” -=[The cop shows the tape where Carr is the first to get hit from behind. The man hits him with a chained wrapped around his fist. Thug roC glances down at Carr before he is struck right in the face with the same chain. After the two is dropped, the two attackers stomped on Thug roC and Adam Carr. They then take their wallet and jewelry before running off. The shorter one drops his chain before rounding the corner, he picks it up and jets down the alley]=- Thug roC looks puzzled as he says, “Wait a minute…rewind that…” -=[The cop is quick to rewind the tape. The tape rewinds to the point where the attackers have stolen the items and start running off. The shorter one drops his chain…]=- “Pause it…” Thug roC almost sounds demanding. -=[The cop pauses as Adam Carr is helped into the back of the truck.]=- Adam Carr enters the truck as the cops pause the tape. “What’s going on?” Adam asks. Thug roC points at the tape, “You see that? -=[The two stare at the ring that one of the robbers were wearing. It looks like a tiger wrapped around the attacker’s finger. And in the mouth of the tiger was a green emerald, and the eyes…. red rubies…]=- ~~~Flashback~~~ We enter the Thug roC’s Washington D.C. home, where he and Adam Carr are watching television. Thug roC sits in his lazyboy and Carr is eating double stuff oreos on the couch. The duo watches the Fox 5 news at noon where they are having some sort of breaking news story. “ Today the release of Greg Vulgar is on everyone’s mind,” The news reporters starts. “Nine months ago, Vulgar was accused of mob criminal action but was later found innocent by a jury of his peers. Many speculate that the trial was very unfair due to the amount of evidence that was thrown out the courtroom. The streets are…” “Hey roC, you scared of this Greg Vulgar guy?” Adam Carr asks as he watches the TV “Are you serious?” Thug roC replies, almost laughing as he thinks about the question. “The guy has a group of low-lifes help him out, stealing ladies’ purses and stupid crap like that, why should I be scared?” “Damn, he had to steal A LOT of purses for something like that” -=[Adam was making reference to the cane that Vulgar was sporting. A very nice one at that. It was made of what looked to be solid gold, or at least gold plated. The top was in the shape of a tiger’s head. The tiger looked up into the sky with his eyes being rubies and a green emerald in his mouth. Very nice]=- ~~~End Flashback~~~ -=[Both Thug roC and Adam Carr wear blank looks on their faces as they stare into the surveillance footage. The scene soon fades to black]=- -=[We fade back in a motel room where Greg Vulgar along with Hit-Kid are counting the cash they’ve accumulated over the last 12 hours. The two count until they can’t count anymore, covering the beds with cash, jewelry and other valuables. The TV plays in the background but neither mena re paying attention. The news plays and catches their ears when they start talking about the recent robberies.]=- “Hey punk, turn that up. I love hearing about myself” Greg Vulgar says with a smile on his face. -=[Hit-Kid goes and turns the TV set up a little bit and you can hear the newsperson talking about the two]=- “At this time there has been a reported 13 thefts of purses, wallets, jewlery and much more over the course of less then 8 hours.” The newsperson continues. “Maybe the most notable of these 13 would be eVolution’s star, Thug roC. Thug roC, a professional wrestler has just signed another contract with eVolution. Coming out of retirement, Thug roC’s confidence was higher then ever, but with this act, his confidence must’ve dropped at least a little. Thug roC….” Vulgar cuts the TV off with agreesion “Hey man, I was watching that!” hit-Kid proclaims “Shut the hell up!” Vulgar screams as he paces back and forth “What crawled into your panties?” “We robbed a f’n celebrity!” Greg Vulgar is obviously pissed “Yea, an athlete at that. We must be great!” Hit-Kid obviously doesn’t realize that the big deal is. He continues to count money until Greg Vulgar smacks everything out of his hands “You idoit! You steal from a celebrity, that means you steal from his fans. You steal from his fans, that means you steal from their families! All of this goes to the media, and now more and more people will be out there after us.” “Calm down, by the time they figure it’s us, we’ll be on a plane to Moscow therefore they won’t be able to find us for the robbery, the stolen case evidence, nothing at all.” Hit-Kid shows him the Moscow plane ticket and smiles again, not worrying about a thing. “You better hope it goes that smoothly, cause if it doesn’t, and I go down, I’m taking you with me!” Hit-Kid gets worried“Wait, I didn’t….” “ Shut your mouth and keep counting!” -=[The scene fades with Greg Vulgar storming out the room and Hit-Kid counting more money]=-
