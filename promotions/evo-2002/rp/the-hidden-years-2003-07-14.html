The Hidden years

14 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-14 Scott had awoken October 31st, 1999 in a back alley with no real perception of reality. The only thing that he had a clear perception of is what he lost. He had been happy whilst his wife and child were above the ground but now that they were gone the only thing that drove him was the hope that he'd meet them when he left this life. But the problem was that he couldn't kill himself, it was just something that was unthinkable and so he did the thing he thought would kill him, he lived in the streets. He could have gotten a job easily but he just didn't have the desire. It was on this day that Scott made his most definitive decision after being helped by someone. He had been walking to McDonalds with enough money to buy some fries when a limo approached him. When it stopped next to him and he saw the man inside he wondered what this was about. 'Hello, I was hoping you could direct me too The United Center, my driver got us lost.' the man inside had said to Scott in a normal tone. Scott being niced the directions and just before the man drove off he said, 'Thank you for your help, it seems that I something that may help you. Here's my card, if your ever in Detroit stop by my gym and I'll give you a free session.' The man drove off and Scott put the card in his pocket with the thought that he'd never go to detroit and yet the idea of that free session intrigued him. It took him a few months to finally earn the money and the courage to go to that gym. When arrived there he was suprised. It was when he got there that his brother's last words came to him and then he realized that he must train. He entered the gym wearing the same clothes he had worn when he directed the man to the united center. The man recognized him immediately and then it began, then Scott's entering into a brave new world was signified. But it took more than that for him to realize how big this really would be for him, it even took more than just one look at the trainer to realize that he was Scott's childhood hero 'The Machine' Bill Dwyer. When realized all this everything began anew for him he would never remember the ordeal he had gone through whilst concious.
