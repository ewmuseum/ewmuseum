This Party Sucks

6 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-06 The scene opens up inside of a crowded room. People (mostly guys) are either standing around and talking, or dancing in the middle of the room to the music being distributed by the DJ. It’s pretty dim in the room, but with a few well-placed tall lamps, and a disco ball on the ceiling, you can see your way around. Sitting on one of the only chairs around is Brianna Washington who looks like she is having no fun at all. It’s surprising because she’s wearing a nice red dress that stops a few inches above the knee….and normally at least one guy would be trying to hit on her, even if he looked like he had just walked out of Bill Gates closet. “Brianna…why are you sitting there girl? Come out and shake a tail feather.” Brianna looks up at her flamboyantly dressed friend, Milo, who has a smile the size of Texas plastered on his face. She gives him an evil glare and his smile fades. “Now what did you tell me when I agreed to come?” She asks through gritted teeth. “I told you you’d have tons of fun.” “Remember when I asked you what kind of party this would be? And you told me it was open? Now I wore one of my best dresses only to come to find the only ones here are gay guys. I’m sorry but being perceived as a finely dressed drag queen by everyone is not my idea of fun.” “If you are so unhappy why are you staying?” Milo snaps back. “Because you have my car keys.” “Oh.” Milo blushes a bit and reaches into his pocket. “Well here, take em.” He tosses them to Brianna and she catches them in mid air. Brianna gets up out of the chair and starts to walk away. Milo shrugs his shoulders and sighs until he turns around and reapplies his smile, getting back to the dance floor. Brianna pushes and snakes around people until she finally finds the exit. She whips the door open and throws herself outside where she notices a quick difference in volume. “That was just torture.” She exclaims to herself as she looks around in the dark for her car. She spots the tree she remembers parking by and rushes over, jumping into the car quickly, ready to get out of this place. “I came here to relax and maybe meet a guy….and I get nothing out of it. Instead of unwinding for my big upcoming match I got all stressed out. I was honestly ready to knock that one guy out who said I could never pass off as a real girl and that my drag outfit was pathetic.” Her face grows red with anger as she thinks about it and she revs up the engine to her car. “I can’t let this party get me down though. I got to keep up my psyche. I’ve been on a pretty good roll as of late in Evolution and I don’t want to ruin it. Two huge victories and I’m not ready to stop the streak. In fact my aim is to keep it going as long as possible. But I know the further up the card I move the harder it gets so the Gadget is ready to get as tough as needed!” She turns on the radio as she starts to drive off and loud guitar strumming and drums are heard as the scene fades out.
