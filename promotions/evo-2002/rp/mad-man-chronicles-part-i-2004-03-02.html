Mad Man Chronicles Part I

2 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-02 “11 Months. Do you have any idea how long 11 months is? No, it’s not 44 weeks. It’s not 330 some days. To a man like me, that’s a Lifetime.” Black, dark. The voice, you can’t mistake it. If you knew eVolution in it’s infancy then you certainly recognize the voice of Sovereign. It’s shaky, unstable and screams of a disturbed mind. I know his voice. I know it all to well. And I know that it’s been a while since you’ve hear mine. I’ve Narrated for Sovereign for his entire career. I tried to give a glimpse into him and that’s proven to actually be really hard to accomplish. We all know that he’s insane. Sure, and I know what you new people might be thinking, how many ‘crazy’ freaks are there in the Wrestling Industry who pay there way through life acting like a maniac for the sake of their bills. I even would admit that most of them, the majority are quite convincing. But they can’t touch Sovereign. Because while people condemn this business calling it fake there isn’t a single person that is more real then William Forth, known to us better as Sovereign. For those who don’t remember it was I suppose 11 months ago that Sovereign and James Kimson locked horns in an epic battle. It was a Stretcher match and it was so personal that it was unable to contain itself in the ring, or even in the building. They battled for almost an hour back and forth. The bleed buckets, they battered and bruised themselves all for the sake of 1 thing... the love of a Woman. Leya Forth, she was born a Kimson. The youngest Daughter of Logan and Sarah Kimson and sister to James. Years before now she meet a young fan William at a wrestling event in Chicago. It’s a long story, but regardless of William’s obvious social issues the two fell in love and eventually married. Sovereign was born not long later as he spring boarded into a career in Wrestling, something that Leya was already involved in because of her brother James. The problem rose when it was apparent that William was obviously completely insane. He was violent, uncontrollable and even was arrested and placed in Mental Hospitals many times. When eVo was born Sovereign came calling and in fact won the Universal Championship as eVo’s first champion. He was out of control, violent, ending careers week after week. But when Leya, now who had separated from Sovereign came back for her husband and joined forces with the most feared and hated man in Wrestling... James came to put a stop to it. James loved his sister, and he fought for her, to show her the error of her ways. But it only strengthened the bond between husband and wife. And so Sovereign and Kimson did bloody battle. The match was over and Sovereign was hurt, and hurt badly. He needed surgery going into the match on his Rotator Cuff due to his style. But the damage done to his body didn’t compare to the damage to his mind. He was off medication, the meds for his Mental problems conflicted with pain medication for his arm. He was thrown back into the Mental Ward in Chicago where he stayed for another 5 months. He was confined to a small room without a bed and a bucket to urinate and deficate in. He was heavily medicated, treated like an animal at times. He was submitted to electo-shock therapy every day and it was stewing in his heart that all he wanted was revenge. I’ve always tried to show both sides of the coin with Sovereign. In fact most of everything he’s done he cannot be blamed for. But then there are the times when you know that he’s in control. His games, how he manipulates you. He’s not to be trusted he’s not to be crossed. Look at what happened to Asis, did Montego deserve to have his Career ended in a fit of rage? Sovereign probably wanted nothing to do with him, but only to hurt Kimson. Asis hurt Leya... so Sovereign hurt Asis. Montego disappointed Leya, so Sovereign beat him towithin an inch of his life. What’s the come of that is unknown. But I’m willing to bet good money that the only thing on Sovereign’s mind is James Kimson... and how bad he wants to finish his career once and for all. “It’s a lifetime, and for what you took from me James Kimson... my own Brother... I will make you regret everything you’ve done from the day you put me in the hospital until the day that you Die.” said Sovereign’s voice, almost calm it was. “11 months. I don’t know if you realize what it’s been like Jimmy. They took me from Vegas in that Ambulance and they destroyed my will. They locked me in a room and they looked at me from a little tiny window. They looked at me and wrote down everything that I did. Wether I sat there, or if I paced around or if I tried to rip the padding out of the wall. You don’t realize what it was like Jimmy. You can’t, no matter how I describe it. And the humanitarian side of all of this is that I wouldn’t wish it on anyone.” “Your punishment Jimmy is going to be deep. Deeper then you could imagine. And in just a few days at Legends you are going to be there. It’s confirmed. I’ve been told, I’ve called and called to double, tripple check. Because there are a few things you need to hear Jimmy. You need to hear them from my own mouth, and to hear them face to face. Not over the television, or across the Internet. Not on the phone just face to face. Because Unbreakable is still weeks and weeks away. But I don’t think either of us will be able to wait that long. I know your seething in your big house in upstate New York with all your land and you little family running around happy that Daddy is home. Maybe you can take your mind off it for a few days. But I know that deep in the back of your mind at least the only thing you see is a picture of me laying over you costing you your Championship. The first drops of snow falling from the mountain that you climbed that is going to cause an Avalanche.” “Everything that you once were is going to change Jimmy.” “Everything you wanted is going to be taken from you” “Everything you’ve dreamed of, ripped from your soul.” “After we are finished, James Kimson will be nothing more then a collection of photos in the wrestling books of the future. Tribute shows to you will pass through time. Because people might in time forget you. But they’ll never forget the beating I give you at Unbreakable. The sounds of my fists smashing into your body will echo through modern times like a clear ringing bell.” “I’ll see you in Columbia Jimmy”
