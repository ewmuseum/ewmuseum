The mystorium(Volume IV)

20 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.20.03 ~The darkness in the hallway of the hospital is enough to drive a person insane, the smell of a hospital is something that most if not all wrestlers are used to. But this one has something different to it…death is in the air. Nurses and doctors run past the waiting room screaming with orders in a fit of emotions. They find the room that they are screaming toward.~ ‘Come on Jon…’ The voice of Darryl cuts through the chaos. ‘Yeah, yeah!’ ‘Holy shit!’ Darryl screams. ~As they round the corner of the hallway Darryl breaks into a sprint toward the ICU. You see nurses doctors and family rushing in and out. Darryl puts his hands on his head and he wraps his arms around a distraught mother. She cries openly into his arms before looking over his shoulder she now sees Shocka.~ ‘Oh my god Darryl you came through!’ The mother yells. ‘Of course I did…’ He says. ‘Margaret this is Jonathan…or as your son knows him Shocka…’ ‘Hi…’ Shocka says in a sort of shy voice. ‘I’m very sorry to hear about your son.’ ‘He’ll be so happy to see you!’ The mother yells. ‘I’m glad to hear that….’ Shocka says. ‘Listen if there is anything I could do to help you just let me know and I’d be happy to try.’ Jonathan says with a smile. ‘Just you seeing him will be all I could ever ask for!’ She screams with happiness as the last of the doctors empty out of the room. ~One of the doctors grab her and pull her aside to explain something as Darryl and Shocka go into the room to see the boy lying in the bed. His eyes are slightly open but they shoot open when seeing Shocka. He smiles and tries to speak but he can’t. A nurse speaks to him.~ ‘Just write it down…you don’t need to speak yet…’ The comforting voice says. ‘Its okay buddy…’ Darryl says. ‘Hello Shocka’ The Nurse reads from a paper handed to her by the boy. ‘Oh hey guy…you sure are a tough little one aren’t ya?’ Shocka says. ~The boy nods and writes something down and hands it to the nurse again.~ ‘He asks if he can see the Universal title…’ ‘Shit…I don’t have it with me!’ He says.
