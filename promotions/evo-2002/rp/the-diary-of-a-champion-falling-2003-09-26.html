The Diary of a Champion: Falling

26 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-26 The last thing I remember was falling. However me and Shocka got to where we were last night is beyond me. To be completely honest that was the 1st time I was ever really completely knocked senseless in a match. Yeah, people have come from behind, blasted me with whatever and sent me to “la la land” more then a few times. Hell I got used to what it felt like to wake up missing the last 7 or 8 hours of your life, in a hospital bed in one of those damn gowns that don’t cover your ass. I hate those frigging things. But last night, I remember falling. To be honest I thought that was it. I knew that when he pushed me off the ledge with that move of his that it was over. That I was not going to walk ever again, or worse. Shocka tried to end my career, tried to cripple me over what? Scoring with Leya? Hoping O’Halloran would win the match? Lets be honest, I know that Leya is beautiful, hell I’m her big brother and I used to have to beat the boys away from her with a stick when we were teenagers, and when we were working together all those years ago. And I’d have to admit that to the ladies Shocka isn’t hard to look at. He’s strong, young and charismatic. But lets be honest about this, Leya is out of Shocka’s league and if he even laid one finger on her I bet that Sovereign would have a few words with Shocka, and I think that is one person that anyone in this world would want to avoid. So what’s the motive? What’s driving Shocka to try and cripple little old me? Did he realize that if he did cripple me, and since the match was ruled a no contest because of his interference in the 1st and last fall that he wouldn’t get his match at Digital Destruction? Maybe, who cares. Shocka obviously is full of himself and is quite proud to admit that he has been the Universal Champion and that he wants to be called Champion once more. Nothing wrong with that ladies and gentlemen, who wouldn’t want to be the Champion? The money, the adulation all that goes with it. And not to say that Shocka wouldn’t make a great champion, he sure kicked my ass last night, and kicked it damn good. But lets be honest kids, he’s not James Kimson. I could pick out of a hat a dozen people better suited to face me at Digital Destruction. What happened to Asis? He’s left on a road to destroy everything in his path, but why not give him a shot at me? Maybe he doesn’t want it, he after all is pretty fixated on Xander, who by the way by right deserves a rematch. So there’s two guys. Jason Jameson was runner up at the Caged Rumble with yours truly, he’s got nothing since. No help from the committee. I guess that they’re just hoping that they can hit a home run on the 1st at bat. But let me say this. Shocka, he might be skilled, charismatic, strong and have a mean streak. But he’s not faced off against a really, really, really pissed off James Kimson before. And the only man in eVo that has was Sovereign, and he’s still nursing the wounds from when I whipped his ass all over Las Vegas at Unbreakable. And as the wood crashed around me and I blacked out, all I knew was that if I was going to be able to make it to Digital Destruction, then I would make sure that that Son of a Bitch would get what’s coming to him. That if he thinks that he’s leaving Digital Destruction as a champion then he’s in a dream world. Living a life of fantasy. Because there’s no way in hell that’s going to happen. Because I can walk. I can breath, I can sing, and I can dance the night away. And I’ll be dancing all over Shocka’s face at Digital Destruction. If not before then, because what he did to me at Legends 51 warrants a little bit or retribution. I’ll be at Legends 52, despite what he wants or what he had hoped. He won’t be given any awards, no trophy or prize for what he did. What he will be getting is my foot up his ass. Because he pissed me off. He’s pissing me off right now even with me thinking about him. He doesn’t know how much that hurt, I don’t know if he’s ever taken a fall like that. I don’t know what he’s gone through in his career. Maybe that was nothing to him, maybe he’s one of those crazy stupid wrestlers like Sovereign who does that every week. Me, I’m to smart on a general level to get caught in those situations. I leave that shit up to mid carders trying to be noticed. I am a wrestler, I wrestle. I am the Champion, and I defend that title to the best of my ability, despite being thrown off the top of a 12 or 15 foot cage through a table. That hurt like a bitch, one of, if not the most painful experiences of my entire life. But like I said, I’m walking. I’m breathing fresh air. That means one thing, that Shocka’s time is up. That he failed in his attempt to rattle me. His attempt to get the upper hand. No, I’m not at %100. Who would be after that. Who knows if I’ll be recovered by the time the bell rings at Digital Destruction. I’m not going to use that as an excuse for anything. I don’t have time for excuses. All I know is that I am wrestling tomorrow afternoon at a non-televised event as part of some promotion for the remainder of the tour. I figured as much, that when I came to in the hospital that Leya would have already arranged for my release to make sure I’d be in action. I don’t even know who against. It’s not important, I’ll tape and lace up like I do every time. This is just another time I have to do it hurting everywhere. But like the old saying goes, Pain is temporary and Pride is forever. I’m in a world of Pain. My elbow still isn’t fully healed. My back hurts, my ribs my neck. But that’s nothing compared to what I want to do to Shocka at Digital Destruction. He tried to cripple me. He TRIED to. At Digital Destruction, I’m going to do whatever it takes to retain my title, so I can cripple his hopes and dreams. So I can cripple his ego. So I can cripple Leya’s plans. And if in the process of all that I can Cripple Shocka.... then I’ll do it. So I hope Shock Daddy Shocka-licious The Shocking one, or whatever else the hell he wants to call himself has a back up plan for after Digital Destruction. Because whatever he’s got in mind, the end result isn’t going to be with the Universal Title over his shoulder. I hope that doesn’t come as a shock. Excuse the pun, he hurt me, yeah that’s for damn sure. But he’s nothing but a joke. The Hype has Spoken
