The Silence, How It Sounds To Me

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/15/02 Sitting on the plane, staring out the window, waiting for something to happen, if anything was going to happen, Jason was lost. Listening to the sound of silence, he seemed to be anticipating something. Something to awake him from this fantasy trapped in reality. Something to dig up the dirt and release him from the casket six feet deep. Something. Anything. The Notorious were now a team, and, for the first time in God knows how long, Jason didn’t feel alone. It gave him a sense of belonging. He was no longer by himself, suffering with no one to turn to. He was now strong, with others to carry him if he ever needed them, and vice versa. But, even with the company, he still felt an awareness of the silence that has suddenly began only a few days ago. The feeling of being alone was somehow still there, and nothing was going to get rid of that any time soon he thought. The world was still against him. He could feel it. Their eyes still stared with hatred. Their voices still rang the words of a thousand negative remarks, each directed towards hurting him in some fashion or another. They all watched, waiting for their chance to take his life from him. So many eyes, gazing into his soul. So many times had he felt it, yet done nothing. He was waiting now. Waiting for the first strike in this foreseen battle. Who would it be from? Would it be from Tommy Michaels, or Jason Steele, or Shocka? From Shodan, or Sovereign, or S.? Or would it be someone he least expected? Like L2J? Or Frost? Or Seara? Or would it be the one person he had been waiting for – Jay Jay? Who would attack first? Who would be the one to take the first action and make this war official? Who would be the one that would risk everything to take away Jason’s everything? Is it you, over there? Sitting in the corner, your eyes haunting and damaging, like the eyes of a predator. Or you? The one with the sinister smile. A smile so misleading. So devastating. So promising. Are you the one? How ‘bout you? Reading your life away in the pages of a newspaper. You all try to act innocent. I can see your guilty eyes. Trying not to stare. Trying not to reveal yourselves. Trying so hard not to expose the feelings of hatred you hold so dear. You are all against me. The world is against me. So is God. Dear God, why do you beckon me? Have I hurt you? Have I sinned? What have I done to disserve this? What weapon have you chosen? What method of administering my untimely demise are you planning? Where are you hiding it? The gun in your breast pocket? The blade in your sleeve? The necklace you wear around your neck? Or is it something completely different? Maybe you were expecting me to fall into your trap. Maybe you thought I was going to be misled by your promises of love and support. Maybe you thought that I didn’t see the rage building inside your heart. Maybe you didn’t think I was prepared to die. I am prepared to die, but it will not be by your hands. Come and try. I’ll see through you, and I will survive. You are nothing to me. Nothing to anyone, and you cannot hurt me. Come on. Come and try and we’ll see who will end up bleeding to death on the soil of our finale. Come on. Come on. Come… The silence was getting to Jason. And Jay knew it.
