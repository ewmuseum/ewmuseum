New Dawn.

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-22 Shocka…<--who’s name is that?! ~Was Greatness right? Could he possibly be correct in all of his words? Of course not…sure Shocka was the man that every heel aspired to be, but things were different now. Shocka was still great…hell he is the #1 Contender to the Universal title and do you think he has seen a shot? Hell no, and you know why? Its because of Greatness…had Greatness not did what he did Shocka would be competing for the Universal title. But now Shocka had to deal with Greatness…he had to try and have a good match night in and night out with a man that was almost at a level playing field…almost. Shocka is in the best condition of his life and now going into a New Dawn he will show everyone, that he is still the man.~ ‘You know what Greatness.’ Shocka started off as he stood in front of a camera. ‘Everything you say and do is growing old…the fans don’t just boo you because they love to hate you…they boo you because you are boring and repetitive.’ Shocka smiled as he paced back and forth. ‘You need to realize that you will not get a rise out of me by bringing up things in the past…sure I lost the title to Scott Frost. He was the better man on that night but if I had the chance again to get in the ring with him one on one I would.’ Shocka sort of snarled as he shook his head again and continued speaking. ‘Greatness, you are nothing that your name implies…you’re not great what have you done to prove that you are the greatest? All I have seen you do so far is kiss some ass…and get a lucky win over me…that’s all!’ Shocka yelled now as he stepped back trying to regain his composure. ~It seemed more and more now that he started to lose his temper over this subject of Greatness…something that could lead to his downfall in the match if he couldn’t control it.~ ‘There is something about you Greatness…I can’t exactly put my finger on it but you are hiding something. You get under my skin like no one else ever could have…now if you wanna talk about what I used to be you bring up the Road to Glory tournament…sure…I lost my title to Scott Frost. But do you remember who I beat to gain the name ‘road to glory champion’…yeah that’s right the same man that took my Universal title away. But that is far besides the point…you need to focus on me…you better make damn sure that you have everything in line when you step into the ring with me because I am going to destroy you.’ Shocka said with confidence overflowing from his voice. ‘Now shut the camera off…so we can go get some beers.’ Shocka said with a slight laugh.
