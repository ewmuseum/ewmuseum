Cue The Retaliation...

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Denver, Colorado. The Pepsi Arena. All that I could see before me as I stared around was the huge grin on the faces of Thug Roc and Jay Jay Stylez. Like the equator their smile seemed to stretch for miles and as I lay lame and vunerable I could do little as I recalled their euphoria this past Wednesday night. Then nothing. All was black. Until I was brought back to life by the sickening sound of the baseball bat on my skull for a second time in as many weeks. I saw little. I remember even less. The only small recollection I have is feeling the sensation as blood dripped from my brow and a surge of pain raced across my face. I could not see the man behind the assault, but sight was not necessary, in my mind I could see very clearly once more the smile of a man who had achieved the impossible. Suddenly I felt a touch once more to my forehead, I flinched, then again I couldnt help it. I began to sweat vigourously, as I awaited the iminent immpact I awoke from my sleep and the flasbacks ceased. As I forced my eyes open with a great degree of effort, the strain of the bright light I was met by proved a test too much, thus my eyes remained closed. I lay limp, tired and battered from the exertions of the previous weeks. It seemed up until two weeks ago I had avoided the fate of a baseball bat clubbing against my head for the entire length of my career. How things change in an instance. I felt a great hatred against Jay Jay for what he had done, especially as it was the second time in only two weeks. Then again as I lay with my head resting on the pillows I recalled my old man saying that old cliche; first time its their mistake, second time its your own. Suddenly I then heard a voice whispering in my ear. 'Sean are you ok babe?' the voice asked. I stirred again trying to force my eyes to open. Flinching I could just see the concerned face of a young brunette before me. As the seconds ticked over in my mind I realised who this friendly figure was and taking her hand I returned the warmth back to Danielle. It had been fourteen months since we had first met on a warm night in Edmington, but not once since then had she seen me subjected to this amount of punishment. Running her hand softly over my cuts it seemed out of the two of us she was disturbed more at the thought of the damage which I had suffered. Sure I had cuts deep enough to sow seeds in and bruises which made my skin look more blue than its usual tan shade, but somehow I had found comfort in that which awaited me this Sunday. A night which gave me the opportunity to re-write so many wrongs... Its for that reason as she spoke I retained an optimistic perspective; this Sunday was yet another chance to claim the gold I felt owed. No amount of physical punishment was going to hold me back, I now firmly had the scent of the Rising Star title in my reach and that was the only means of retaliation I needed. 'I dont know, what are we going to do with you Sean?' Danielle said to herself trying her best to race a smile in the face of adversity. Instead of replying with speech, I simply gripped her hand that little bit tighter. This woman meant the world to me and yet at the same time so did the business I had toiled in for so long. I couldn't stand to see her suffer like she was, but at the same time this Sunday I had the opportunity of fufilling my own expectations. Finally I could claim gold in Evo. Now came the moral issue though, was I right to be putting my gain infront of Danielles happiness? As I looked back up at Dannielle and my eyes opened further I could see her pain, but at the same time I could feel the pain within me. A pain that was bourne by the simple fact that I had failed thus far in Evo. 'You know Sean you could just retire?' she said raising a sly grin, 'I mean what about becoming a DR you've spent most of your time in hospitals of recent?' 'You really think I've any ounce of sense in my head after Legends' I replied in a joking manner with little hesitation.. 'No I guess not' Danielle replied once more. This time though it was a response filled with discontent. Still gripping her hand I realised the worry she was going through. If only I could find success. Something which showed all the trials and tribulations were warranted. 'I can do this baby, I know its hard, but this Sunday I will do this'. The response was certainly not pre-meditated but somehow I felt the words were well chosen. However Danielle disagreed, leaning closer she spoke in my ear for a second time. 'No Sean' she said, 'we can do this..' Danielle raised a smile once more. Although this time the smile was more one which implied hope. Suddenly my spirits were given a dramatic boost. The doubts were erased and the backing was in place from those I loved to aspire toward my dream. Then again was it just my dream now?! As I looked into Danielles eye the answer was seemingly no. Behind the worry, behind the fear of what might happen, lay the same ambition I had for myself. Sometimes you don't need to understand someone elses dream, you just have to realise what it means to them. Danielle realised the importance. Wrestling was my life and so long as she wanted to be apart of it, she would need to go through all the blood, sweat and tears. It was something she had known since day one and despite it getting harder to endure, she felt no need to question its merits thankfully. As she looked down at me in a mixture of both pity and pride, I rubbed her hand gently as it sat in mine. Gently she kissed me on my forehead, before then getting to her feet once more. 'Now Sean get some rest please' she said in a tone which made the request sound more like a plea than anything else. I nodded, before then letting my eyes close once more. Drifting off into a deep sleep I was left to reflect. This was my third PPV in Evo and yet already it was set to become my most important.. Why?? Because this time I was not just fighting for me; this time you couldn't help but see the bigger picture..............
