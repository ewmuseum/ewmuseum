Notorious by Nature

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct. 15, 2002 'Prisoner number, 569144 has escaped. If found, proceed with caution. Repeat, please proceed with caution. Escapee is considered, not armed but very dangerous.' A female voice is heard through what sound to be a police radio. The scene opens up in a wooded area, rain pouring down and lighting flashing and illuminating the sky as the sound of thunder rolls following the flashes of lightning. A man squatting down on the edge of a pond. Lightning clashes and fills the sky with lights as you see a man slamming what seems to be chains against something. The camera pans over to the front of the man, who is banging the lock of the chains against a very sharp looking rock. The lock quickly pops off and the man drops the chains and leaps into the water. Long, noticiable beams of light penatrate through the wooded area and everytime the beam would come near to the man he would duck under the water. The sound of dogs barking echos, but is quickly muffled out by the boom of the thunder that rolls across the sky. 'Frost?! Where are you Frost?! We know you are near! We found your chains! Just come out and nobody will be hurt. We will escourt you back to the prison and everything will be fine. Think of your wife, think of Kelsey. Frost!? WHERE ARE YOU?!' A man with a flash light and a leashed dog is seen yelling into the atmosphere that is already shrouded with booming noise. Suddenly Frost in his bearded and close shaven hairstyle stands up out of the water, and a look of determination is on his face as he walks solomnly to the prision guard. The dogs begin to howl as if they found their track. Lightning clashes in the air again causing a huge boom that seems to catch everyone except for Frost off guard who is still walking. Almost in a quite, slow motion style all attention is focused on the onward Scott Frost. His wet beard dripping back into the pond, and his damp clothes sticking to his body. Frost approaches the man and his dog to quickly reveil a big rock inwhich he smashes into the top of the dogs skull and quickly spins around sending a sharp elbow strike to the throat of the prision guard. The man and the dog fall down instantly and Frost stands above the man and looks at him breathing heavy. 'I am thinking of my wife. Why do you think your down there struggling for air now! Promise me you will make sure everyone forgets I ever was here. Or so help me I will kill you.' Frost demands breathing hard. The man looks up at Frost who bends down and grabs the gun out of the mans gun holster and points the barrell of the gun towards the mans face. The man instantly gets a look of fear in his eyes. 'OKAY! OKAY! YOU NEVER EXISITED!!! NOW GO!!!' The prision guard yells. Frost unclips the gun and throws the clip into the middle of the pond and drops the gun. Lightning flashes and fills the sky with light once again as the mixture from the light and the rain hitting the eyes of the prision guard almost blind him all he sees next is nothing and the sound of someone running away into the woods....... The scene fades away and then fades back into the sight of Scott Frost looking into a pond surrounded by woods. He is standing where he was set free by the guard. He looks down and he sees a rusty piece of medal embedded into the soil at the edge of the pond. He retrieves it, and reveals a gun clip. Frost smiles and throws the clip into the pond once again and the scene fades away. 'That was the last time I went to that pond. The wooded, damp area that gave birth to my new life. After that day, I swore my inner demons would never take control of my again. I wouldn't kill another man, No more people will go to the hospital. But that was until I met him. Erin Christoph. He told me, 'You gotta be ruthless to be recognized.' Those words are my motto for living now, my wife backs me and those words up to date. I will do anything to get to the top of the ladder of success. I've already taken out Erin Christoph and shut the mouths of Tyler Davidson and Unlimited up. Now, with my only friend L2J. My wife Kelsey. And the on going plublicity that Seara and myself are getting now. My life has only begun to change, which demon will surface first. Who is next in line to get swormed by the cold front that is notoriously called.....Scott Frost.' the voice of Frost speaks as it fades away.
