Just Who Is At Gate Fifty Two?

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-27 ::Flashback:: June 26th 2003 @ 19:00 hrs. 'Sean?' cried one of the camera men down the corridoor, 'I thought you were scheduled to be at the kids wrestling camp tonight?' At this point Sean turned toward the direction from which the shout eminated. Sean didn't reply he just nodded glancing down at his watch, before turning away once again and making off into the distance. Dressed in a white vest and a pair of combats it appeared Sean had come from the gym as he attempted to prepare for the match which awaited him this coming Sunday. 'Sean are you in a rush?' the man asked hoping to catch Sean before he was out of ear shot. Once again Sean attempted to ignore the cry, but not to be defeated the camera man raced after him with the camera upon his shoulder recording just incase. 'Sean?' the man cried again. Once turned on the spot and looked toward the man chasing him. 'Sorry im in a bit of a rush' Sean said in a muffled tone, muffled not just by him coughing but also the way he had his hand pressed close to his face. 'Oh sorry' the camera man apoligised, 'some other time maybe?' Sean nodded. At that point the camera mans cell phone began to ring. As he reached into his pocket he heard the sound of running infront of him and when he looked back up Sean was no longer there to be seen. Confused the camera man stood, but then again Sean had been scheduled to arrive at the camp at 5pm so it was no suprise he was in a rush. 'Hey' the camera man answered the phone, 'you never guess who I've just seen' he paused allowing the caller to take a few random guesses in his ear. 'Nope' he grinned, 'Sean Mason' 'What?' the camera man replied slightly suprised, 'are you sure?' 'No I have not been drinking' the man replied scratching his forehead as he tried to clear his thoughts. 'I best phone security' the man said, 'your definately sure Sean is there?' 'Ok.. Ok..' the man replied irritated by the insistance of the caller. Cancelling the call, he reached into his phone book and searched out the number of the security on hand to police MSG over the coming days. 'Hello security?' the man asked as he put the phone to his ear. 'I'd like to report a possible intruder around gate 52' the man said in defiant tone, 'the man appears to be attempting to impersonate Sean Mason. I thought it was him myself, but the fact that one of my collegues is currently on a promotion with him at a wrestling camp would suggest other wise.' 'Yes I am sure' replied an aggitated camera man who fought off the suggestion he was seeing things, 'infact I would worry less about my twenty twenty vision and more about yours for letting him in here.' 'Thanks' the man said cancelling the call before making off in the direction he had thought he had seen Sean head.
