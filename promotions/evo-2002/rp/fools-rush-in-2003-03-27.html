Fools Rush In

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/03 [The shot opens in a hardly lit room. Xander walks into focus on the screen.] 'So' He begins. 'The times now drawn close. Try and try as you might, you have no power over me. Definitely not enough power to throw any portion of my life into disarray Mr. VanCore.' 'I'm not sure which drug it was exactly that led to to even involve yourself into, not only, Unlimited business, but, my personal dealings. One month ago you cost me a title. May not be the biggest prize in the game, but it would have proved my point over Seara. But no. You had to stick your nose into the shark cave. Now, you pay the price.' 'Week after week I've outsmarted you. One upped you. All leading to Legends Triple X. You tried to humiliate me by forcing a loss over, not one, but two female wrestlers. Backfired, didn't it Nicky? This is my proof that you can never match wits, nor ability with me. Who do you think is running this show? A member of Unlimited. Who do you think okays most of the attacks, vandalism, and various items of mayhem? A member of Unlimited. But who is really behind it all? Asis? Please, you see what happens when he has a thought of his own. Christoph? Not nearly, he is a lieutenant in this army of darkness. S.? He has the power to get things done, and me... I am the one who masterminds it all. Without me, there is no Unlimited.' 'And you go after the most integral cog in this machine of destruction? Fool.' [Xander walks away from the camera as the scene fades]
