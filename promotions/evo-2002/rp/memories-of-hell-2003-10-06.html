Memories of Hell

6 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-06 He raised his arm higher than he had last time. It was going to hurt like it never hurt before. Donovan saw it coming before he even got home. He had gone out with some friends after class. He had called home but there was no answer. However, chances for him to go out and actually enjoy himself didnt come very often. So he decided to go along anyways. Three hours had passed since he called. His mother had been home, but why didnt she answer the phone? His father would be home from work soon. Donovan laid his thing on the recliner in the corner of the room. The room was quite spacious. To look at Donovan, no one would ever guess the family he came from. Most thought of him as merely another scrub from the other side of town. However, the realit was a bit different from average opinion. Truth betold, his father had earned his family a very healthy life. The problem was that Donovan benifitted from it very little...almost none at all. The house was comprised of eleven rooms. The family room, the den, the dining room, a very large kitchen, two bathrooms and four bedrooms. The remaining room was a study of sorts for his father. His father referred to it as a study anyways. It was more of a in-home bar, really. His father spent hours each night there. Donovan continued on from the family room to the den and started up the stairs when he saw her. Midway up the stair rest his mother. She was laying almost lifeless. He knew what had happened. She had overdosed again. His father a drunk...his mother a drugged whore. He picked her up and arried her to the bathroom and placed her in the shower. Donovan turned on the cold water and called his sister. She was on her way. Just as Donovan was leaving the room, he heard the front door creak open and slam shut. 'Shit!' He thought to himself. Donovan quickly went to his room and shut the door. 'Where you at you worthless piece of shame?' He knew his father was referring to him. After a moment of silence, he could hear his father tredding up the stairs with thunderous steps. A loud pound on the door... 'Open you god damned door. Where's your whore of a mother?' Donovan hated it when he referred to her like that. He really wasnt sure what he hated more though, the fact that his father referred to her that way...or that he was right... 'She's in the bathroom. She blackedout again. Nat's on her way her to take care of her.' Nat is what Donovan had always called her. Her real name was Natasha, but everyone called her Nat. 'She what? What the f*ck is wrong with you? You know better than to leave her ass alone! Come here! Open the door, or I'll do it myself!' Donovan sat lifeless in the corner. Afraid of another beating, he sat silently, hoping that he would go away. Suddenly the door crashed open... His arm came down with a fierce force. Donovan's head snapped back and smacked off the wall behind him. His father raised his arm again...again, his head snapped back... Outblaze woke with in a cold sweat. He reached and rubbed the back of his head. He would have sworn that he felt blood. He sat for a moment thinking back on the nightmare of his childhood...He reaches up and wipes the sweat from his forehead before lighting a cigarette. The flame of the match lights the room momentarily. He shakes out the match and the room quickly fades back to nothingnes.
