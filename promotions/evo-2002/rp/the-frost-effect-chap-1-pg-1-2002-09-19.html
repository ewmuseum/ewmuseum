The Frost Effect: Chap. 1, pg. 1

19 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 19, 2002 The faded scene of life, becomes more contrast as a cold wind blows and on by passers walk by and hike their collars over thier neck to protect them from the apparent cold wind coming in. Women tuck their neck down as they lift their shoulders to cover their neck, and fold their arms over to try and hide the effect that the wind is having on the women. The camera spans as it watches the reaction of the others but suddenly it spans over and noticed that someone has left their jacket on a bench, but nobody is near to claim it. The camera amazingly catches someone come out from the darkness of an alley-way. It's Scott Frost, the very man that screwed Erin Christoph out of a job and viciously attacked eVolution owner, Tyler Davidson. The camera man walks over to Frost. 'umm...Mr. Frost?' questions the camera man getting Scott Frosts attention. 'Would you mind giving some kind of, 'I'm here' promotion?' again questioned the camera man. Frost looks at the camera man and then he looks down at the jacket on the bench, and he sits down as he picks it up and looks at it. The jacket is a very dark brown leather designer jacket that looks to be made for a male. 'Such a waste of time.' Frost looks over at the camera man, and then he throws the jacket into a trash can as you hear the howling sounds of another hard blow of wind bellow throughout the city and in the background people are hurdling together instinctively trying to make some form of heat, but Frost just looks on sitting on the metal bench with his t-shirt and jeans on. 'Would you like to talk about what happened lastnight at Digital Destruction? I know that the world is definately wondering.' stated the camera man. Frost looks over to the camera man, and shakes his head slightly grinning. 'Lastnight was just the beginning. Lastnight. I did exactly what Erin Christoph used to speak about. He used to say that to get recognized, you have to be ruthless. Lastnight, I was ruthless. And he was right. Because after I hit the Frost Effect on Davidson. I made an impact in this federation. I was recognized. But the fans, as the new bad ass of eVolution. I was recognized by Daniel Somers, and Tyler Davidson as the man that isn't affraid to take the V.I.P's of this federation to get recognized as what I am. Lastnight. The world saw the end of a warlord, the world recognized the beginning of a new era. The only place to go now, is to the top. Where do you think someone in my shoes would attempt to go? The Continental division? The High Impact division? The Rising Star division? No! The only other place that someone with my stature is allowed to go. Universal.' remarked Frost before pausing momentarily. A black car slowly parks behind Scott Frost as he continues to sit on the bench. A small crowd of wonderers have slowly developed as they stagger in the cold. Frost looks around, and notices that someone is wearing an offical eVolution wrestling jacket and this causes Frost to point at him and continue. 'eVolution is probably wondering. How does someone who just debuted plan on becoming a Universal division contender?' says Frost as he replies to his own statement. 'Simple. I attacked Davidson for a reason, and if you are too blind to see my reasons then you yourself need to watch the encore presentation of the pay per view event that I myself has made it exactly what it means. Digital cable, never once saw the destruction that lastnight had. And I was involved in it. I destroyed Erin Christoph, because I wanted to prove that his division. The Continental division was too small for me. The biggest division for the biggest rookie in the business. I made the FedWars top ten before I even decided to sign with eVolution. These people; Matt Boone, L2J, Alias, Jamal Kage, they all need to realize that this my time now! Each and everyone of you failed to defeat the Universal Monster, The Universal Champion, Sovereign. I won't fail. I can't fail. The frost came into full effect lastnight. And I have become the immune to the disease that is frailty. Sovereigns frail body will, just like Christophs and Davidsons fall to the frost effect. The Universal championship will shine in all of it's glory on the icy shoulders of hate, the ice cold hearted villian known as Scott Frost will become the new monster. A more stable monster. A monster, who has every step already planned out. Now all I have to do, is get Sovereign to realize that no matter how much he 'believes that he is champion.' the frost is coming. Winter has taken over. Hell just froze over. Sovereign. When we meet. It will surely be a cold day in hell, because the Universal monster gets frost bit for the first time.' retorted Scott Frost. Frost stands up and looks around, and then he opens the passanger side door of the car that parked behind him. The plates of the car ironically read, '1' as it speeds off.
