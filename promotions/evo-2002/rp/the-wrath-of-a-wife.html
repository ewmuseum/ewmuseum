The Wrath of a Wife

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We start the day in a Washington D.C. car lot where Thug roC and his good friend, Machine, are looking around for a new car to buy for Thug roC, better known to his friends as Terrel. Machine, a very large man, looks carefully at the cars while Thug roC is just strolling through the lot. “What are we doing here again?” Thug roC asks, obviously annoyed with this search. “You buying a car? You don’t even got money!” “Ummm…” Machine hesitates. “I’m not here to buy a car Terrel.” He says lowly. “Then what are we here for?” Thug roC asks. That’s when you see a man in a suit approach both men. He has a nice black tie on, very professional. “You here about the job?” The man asks, looking directly at Machine. “Job?” Thug roC says surprised. “Yea, that’s me.” Machine replies. The two then walk away, leaving Thug roC a few steps behind. “What job Big Man?” Thug roC asks aloud, trying to catch up. We fast forward later in the day while cars constantly speed by that same car lot. You can hear a few cars honk at the dealership’s dog mascot who stands at the corner and waves to the traffic. Thug roC is seen leaving the dealership, but he’s walking rather then getting in his vehicle. He walks to the sidewalk where he approaches that dog mascot. “This is truly sad.” He says to the mascot. “Shut up damnit! I’m trying to attract customers.” Surprisingly, the mascot is actually Machine in a dog outfit. Thug roC can’t help but to laugh while he watches his friend, obviously boiling hot in that suit, wave to strangers as they drive by. “I’m going to go inside and get an air freshener for the car.” Thug roC says, taking a step back towards the building. “Air freshener? For what?” “I have a friend the size of a baby Shamu, outside in a dog outfit making him sweat more then a prostitute in church. Not to mention that when you sweat, you smell like rotten cheese and spam. If you’re riding with me, I need at least an air freshener.” Thug roC then walks off, going back into the building as Machine continues to work. Thug roC enters the main building and is looking around for some sort of air freshener. He starts walking around until he’s approached by a salesman. “Hey,” Thug roC greets him, “do you sell air fresheners for the car?” He asks. “Why of course.” The salesman answers. “But while you’re here, why not look at a car?” He then starts making his way towards a car, and Thug roC just sighs as he follows him. “This is our brand new all new car, The Legacy. It is an SUV, but can be transformed into a pickup truck with just a few minor changes. It has built-in…” The salesman is then cut off by Thug roC. “Wait a second, an SUV that transforms into a pickup? Didn’t you guys steal that from the Chevy Avalanche?” Thug roC asks. “We didn’t steal it, we…uh…re-imagined it.” The salesman responds. “What’s the difference between that and stealing?” “Uhh….ummm….” Suddenly you see the Salesman jump through a window and escape the building.” Thug roC just stands there puzzled. “And all this time I’ve been using the door like a sucker.” He says sarcastically. He continues walking until a second salesman approaches him. “Hello sir, I can tell you scared our rookie salesman away.” He says. “Walk with me, talk with me.” The scene fades away with roC walking away with the salesman. We come back, later in the day with Thug roC making it home. He walks through the door to see his two kids in the living room. Michael, 13 years old, and Clare, 10 years old, are both watching television. Thug roC walks in and seems confused. “Why are you two watching Barney?” Thug roC can’t help but to ask. “We can’t find the remote.” Michael replies. “We’re just stuck on this channel.” “What about getting up and turning the channel?” Thug roC says. “Look, watch.” Thug roC walks up to the TV and changes the channel manually. Both kids sit in awe. “Ohhhhh….Wow” Michael takes a glance outside. “Who’s car is that Dad?” “Oh…ummm…mind your business son.” Thug roC replies. “You bought a new car without asking mom?!” Clare exclaims. “We know who will be on the couch tonight.” “Clare…” Thug roC is then cut off. “You know mom wouldn’t do that to Dad Clare.” Michael says. “That’s right.” Thug roC seems glad to have support. “She’ll make him sleep outside again like when he bought all that stock into that sport nobody heard of.” Michael says. “HEY! Soccer is on the rise damnit!” Thug roC responds. “Besides, once your mom sees the car and understands why we need it, she’ll be more than happy.” Thug roC looks at his kids who both shake their head in unison. “She won’t be happy?” The kids continue to shake their heads. “Ah, what do you guys know? Your mom hardly trusts either of you.” “KIDS!” Shannon, Thug roC’s wife yells down the steps. “Can one of you come up here and help me count my money? I’d wait for your dad to come home, but I don’t trust him!” The kids just laugh as Thug roC is shocked at such a comment. “You kids, go figure out how the VCR works without the remote or something. And just wait, when your mother and I come down, she’ll be head over heels over that car.” Thug roC comments as he starts making his way up the stairs. He enters the room and Shannon has her back turned to him. She obviously thinks it’s one of the kids because she has yet to learned that her husband is home. She’s cleaning up what looks to be old socks as Thug roC stands behind her. “I can’t believe this. Your dad is a grown ass man and…’ Shannon then turns around and sees her husband. “And…he’s the best thing to ever happen to me. Hey hunnie.” Thug roC just laughs, knowing he’s about to step on thin ice. “Hey babes, I missed you today.” Shannon then suddenly slaps Thug roC on the back of his head. “What was that for?!” “I’m not sure yet, but when you start conversations like that, I usually do that later on anyways. So I’m just getting it out my way.” She replies quickly. “Now what did you buy that will make you set up a tent outside again?” “Wait a second, this time I bought something for you though!” Thug roC states. He stops and looks at the shocked looked that his wife holds. “That’s right, I bought you a…” “Thank god.” Shannon interrupts. “Cause usually you buy something for yourself and just claim that it’s for me. What did you get me?” “Ummm…uhh..a…uhh…treadmill.” Thug roC then drops his head as he realizes that was a horrible lie. “A WHAT?! YOU TRYING TO SAY I’M FAT?!” Shannon exclaims. “Wait…I…uh…” “Well I’ll tell you something Mr. Rocker, you better take that thing back and buy me something that I actually want…or…or else.” “Or else what?” Thug roC asks curiously. That’s when he receives a harsh look from his wife. “Ok, ok, I’ll take it back. Thug roC walks down the steps where Machine has arrived and is standing beside the television, changing the channels for the kids who still sit on their asses, watching the television. “There, just watch music videos.” Machine says, still dressed in that large dog suit. He walks over into the kitchen and takes a seat at the table. He picks up the paper and waits for Thug roC to say something. After a moment of silence, Machine broaches the topic. “So, what she say about the car?” “I didn’t tell her.” Thug roC replies as he sits down. “So what did you say?” “I told her I got her a treadmill.” Machine, still in the dog suit, rolls up the newspaper and starts hitting Thug roC in the head with it. “No…no…no.” He says with each hit. “What were you thinking?” “I wasn’t…” “That’s your problem!” Machine interrupts. “Good luck fixing this one Terrel. My advice is you get a bigger tent than the one you already got.” Suddenly, you hear a loud yell from upstairs. The kids all look up towards the ceiling as well as Thug roC and Machine. Everyone hurries to their feet. “She must’ve seen the car out the window.” Thug roC announces. “EVERYONE CODE RED!” With that announcement, all four spread out like roaches with the lights cut on. We see Clare hurry into the closet and closes the door. You then see her open the door and her arm reach around to a large piece of wood, probably a 2x4. She pulls on it and closes the door, letting the board fall across the door. Michael is scurrying around the room, nowhere to hide. He then makes a break for the door but doesn’t realize the storm door was closed. He runs right into the glass and immediately drops backwards. Machine is already nowhere in sight, leaving Thug roC the last one out. He turns his head to every direction, looking for a spot to hide and decides on one. He goes to the coffee table and pulls the cloth off the covered it. He shoves the coffee table away and puts the cloth on him before getting on all fours, making a perfect coffee table. Shannon storms down the steps and turns her head towards the living room where she finds nobody. She looks at the front door and sees Michael laid out on the ground. “Oh yea, I learned to lock that door.” She says in his direction. She stands behind the couch and continues her search, she then notices the 2x4 that blocks the closet door. “Where then that come from?” She says to herself. She goes and slides the 2x4 out of the way and realizes that someone must be inside. She quickly opens the door and Clare comes flying out, obviously leaning against the door. Clare lands on the ground and looks up at her mom. As a last attempt, she just closes her eyes and plays dead. Shannon continues her search for probably her two top suspects. She can’t seem to pinpoint either of them though. She then flops down on the couch to take a moment to think. “OH GAWD! Who’s elephant just sat on me?!” The couch says. Shannon doesn’t enjoy the comment as she gets up and pulls the couch cushions up to find Machine hiding under them. “Why is there a large Snoopy hiding in my couch?” Shannon asks. “Oh, hello Shannon.” Machine says, but only gets a dirty look from Shannon. “Did I say elephant? I meant petite young beautiful…” “Save it, where’s that excuse for a husband of mine?” She asks, but only get shoulder shrugs from Machine. Shannon then notices the disfigured coffee table that sits in front of her. She can’t help but to smile though. Michael, Clare and Machine all stand up and wait for a barrage of yelling and arguing, but don’t get any of that. Instead, Shannon just smiles. “Ok kids, lets play a game of speed. Which ever one of you can quickly jump up on the coffee table will get a cookie. “A cookie!!” Machine exclaims. “NOOOO!” The coffee table yells. “Anything for a cookie!” The Three Hundred pound Machine jumps up on the coffee table that immediately collapses. “MY GAWD! KILL ME NOW!” The coffee table exclaims. As Machine gets off the coffee table, Shannon pulls the cloth off to reveal Thug roC on the ground holding his back in pain. “I…I can’t feel my legs.” He says. “Get up Terrel.” Shannon says as she watches her husband slowly get up. “Now I’ve taken a lot of crap from you over the years.” “And I too have…” Thug roC starts until he looks at his kids who constantly shake their heads in disapproval. “…never mind.” “But that car has got to go.” Shannon finishes. “Wait a second, we can’t do that. I mean, the kids love the car don’t you kids?” Thug roC replies. The kids then both reply, “Yea we love the…” Shannon then gives her kids the evil eye. “No dad, we hate the car. Can’t stand it.” “I see how this works.” Thug roC says. “So lets take a vote between the four of us, but everyone just write their votes on a piece of paper. That way, when the kids vote for me, you won’t know who voted for who.” “Fine” Shannon answers. “But let me remind my kids, I know what your handwriting looks like :evil:” Machine hands out pieces of paper and everyone writes. “Ok, just write down who’s WRONG, me or your mom.” Thug roC tells his kids. Everyone balls their pieces of paper and turns them into Machine. He unfolds the first one and reads it aloud. “And it says, Shannon.” “HAHA!” Thug roC celebrates. “The kids think you’re wrong!” “Only one vote Terrel, sit your ass down.” Shannon replies. Machine then unfolds the second one, “And this one says, Terrel.” That’s when Shannon smirks as the votes are even. “And the third one says…Daddy.” Thug roC looks at Michael in disbelief. “That wasn’t me,” Michael replies. “You know I call you Dad.” Machine unfolds the third one, “The final one, says Dad.” Thug roC then gives Michael a second look of disbelief. Shannon stands in victory. “Looks like that car has got to go.” She states. “You almost won Terrel.” Machine says. “But just like in the bed, you came up short.” Machine then laughs at his witty remark. “Shut up Lassie.” Thug roC replies. “Fine, the car will go back, but I won’t do it with a smile.” “Fine by me.” Shannon says as she watches Thug roC and Machine leave the room. “Mom,” Clare says. “When are you going to tell Daddy about the $300 shirt you bought?” “Mind your business you!” Shannon says as the scene fades away.
