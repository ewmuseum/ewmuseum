Attention Seeking

5 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-05 Legends has just gone off the air. A bloody Matt Hunt stands alone in an empty hallway, staring aimlessly with a menacing expression. Some echoic footsteps are heard as Hunt continues to stare into space and the medic appears next to him “Mr Hunt, you need to come back to the table. I need to patch you up” he said as his voice reverberated up and down the hallway. Hunt didn’t react as he was breathing heavily through his nose, his muscular chest expanding and contracting. Silence followed as the middle aged medic looked uncomfortable and hesitant to speak “Mr Hunt…” he stuttered At that moment, Hunt slowly reared his head round and fixed his eyes upon the EMT, his disgruntled facial expression remained “I really have to get you patched up, before any permanent damage is done” spoke the medic in his soft tone Hunt blinked and moved his eyes around in a thoughtful manner, before nodding slightly with his injured head. It’s as if he returned back to earth. Hunt turned around as the medic did and walked slowly back down the hallway, limping from the ankle which was worn down in the match he had only moments earlier. The medic walked the same pace as Hunt did, watching his every move. As they both distanced themselves from the camera shot, the scene faded to black. But it was only for a few seconds, as footage of a sombre Matt Hunt returned. He was now fully clothed wearing a thick winter jacket and a pair of jogging pants, with a baseball cap covering his shaved head. The medic’s work was visible as a few butterfly stitches are patched above Hunt’s left eye. Still feeling the effects from the Kimson encounter, Hunt limps through the parking lot as the cold air strikes him. He ignores the bitterness as he approaches a silver car, perhaps a rental car as drivers door has a local telephone number emblazoned across it in black lettering. Hunt’s kitbag dangles off his right shoulder as he digs into his pocket, bringing out a key on which he aims at the car and presses the key with ease. He stumbles to the boot of the car, where he opens it and swings around, dumping his medium sized kitbag into the large open space. He reaches for his head with a groan as he pulls the boot down, slamming it to ensure that it is properly locked tight. Hunt then stumbles around to the driver’s door, slowly opens the door and gingerly sits in behind the wheel, immediately adjusting the angle the backdrop of the chair to get comfortable. He inserts the key into the small slot known as the inigntion and then drops his head back, sighing with a groan as the his warm breath meets the cold air. He then brings his head forward and slightly lowers it, shaking his head and closing his eyes. He starts to talk, but doesn’t acknowledge the presence of the camera, which is only a few inches away “Nothing to be ashamed of, nothing whatsoever” he said in a soft tone “Bloody good showing if I do say so myself, brings back the good old days…” he continued as a hint of a smile appeared on his face “At least I didn’t lose!” he said with a chuckle, which only lasted for a few seconds “Bastards. Why couldn’t they just stay away? I was hoping, just hoping that this one wouldn’t be pissed on by some interference. Fucking bastards” he snarled with a venomous tone “Couldn’t leave well enough alone, could they? No, they just had to get in there and ruin another great opportunity for me. Hang on, what the hell is going on here? Why am I letting them piss about and walk off, laughing about their night’s work? Why am I sat here like a prized idiot, pissing and moaning about it?” A somewhat confused expression appeared on Hunt’s face as he finished that last sentence. “This Damon fuckwit, he stuck his nose into my business, screws me out of two title matches into two weeks…and he has the gall to tell me that I don’t know what I’m getting myself into? Does this camache want some attention? Because he’s going the right bloody way about getting it.” Hunt pauses as a more thoughtful look appears “In fact, I can probably see where the guy is coming from…” he starts “Lets see, he was part of that cell department. Now it’s no well-kept secret that 90% of eVolution’s fans didn’t really give a shite for the cell department. I can’t see why they should, seeing as it was just a glorified UFC rip-off and all that…Anyway, for all I know he could have busted his ass week after week and received nothing in return, no respect from his peers and no respect from the people…whether he wanted them to like him or not. So anyway, this cell department shite eventually meets it’s maker, so to speak, and much like the former cell head person guy, it’s killed off and met it’s death. Boo fucking hoo” he pauses momentarily before continuing “So this Irish pixie needs to find a new home, so he pisses and moans and acts as if everybody gives a shit by bad mouthing eVo and going on about OSF or whatever. Again, nobody shows the slightest bit of interest because…well, why should they? So this guy thinks up a brainwave, decides to get in the arse of one of eVo’s biggest baddies and decides to target a man – ala moi – who is slowly moving up the ladder and probably getting the respect from his peers and the people that he wanted. All just for attention…” he guffaws and shakes his head before sharply turning to the camera with menacing expression “Well who’s attention did you want, O’Halloran? I’d say the people still don’t give shit about you, but you’ve got my attention, camache…and that’s not necessarily a good thing. Course, you’re not going to take any notice of me running my mouth…but I may as well say what I’ve got to say” he barks as he adjusts his seating position to a more comfortable one “Lad, I piss on your “final warning” with delight and ease because Postman here will piss on you from a great height…literally and rhetorically. Now next week, I get an immediate chance to get another shot at James Kimson and the Universal Title. Thug roC, who was also chair happy earlier tonight, will be the man who I’ll be facing and co-incidentally, for the second time in three weeks. Now it don’t take a brain surgeon to realise that you two have got something cooked up…well that’s fine, because I have a contingency plan of my own.” There’s a slight pause as he re-adjusts his position so that he’s back fully upright in the chair and behind the wheel. “Remember the last thing I said to you, O’Halloran? After you’re little “get outta my face” rant? ‘We’ll see…we’ll just see’” he smirks as he reaches to pull the door towards him “Let the games begin….” He concludes as he slams the door shut and starts the ignition, slowly pulling away moments later.
