Justice will be dealt.

16 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-16 It was an agravating night. I stepped into the ring and was facing none other than The Postman. I dominated like always, but he did manage to make some good offense. Right when the match reached it's pinnacle, something happened. Scott Evans stuck his nose in. He ruined my match. I wasn't going to just let it go. He attacked Hunt and made the match a no contest. He ruined my perfect record and he was going to get the perfect beating for doing it too. As he stood their and attacked Hunt, I walked over and dealt with the situation. I attacked Evans. Then it went down hill from their. Hunt thought he could handle Evans, but we all knew he couldn't after the beating I gave him. We argued back and forth and it gave Evans the advantage. Out of no where I was blind sided and gave Evans his last 5 minutes of glory. Now Evans get's his chance once again as he faces Thug Roc for Untied Championship. Oh, and what does eVo leave for me? The night off. I don't want the night off, I want my revenge. It's Legends 50, a big show for eVo. I should definitally be on the card. Just cause I am not booked at Legends 50, it's not keeping me from being their. I don't care if they tell me I am fired if I show up, hell if I have to I will buy a ticket and be a fan for the night. Either way I am going to be their and my eye will be on Evans. See it's not over between me, Hunt, or Evans, this is just the begining. I want a rematch in some shape with Hunt, to prove who the better man is. We all know it is me, but I still want my rematch. I heard him bickering about how my legs are like trees and this and that, but Hunt, I wasn't going to tap and you knew it. You heard me say in just a few short seconds I am going to work myself free and slam you to the mat. You knew you where going to get beat. You where scared. Hell, you probablly told Evans to come out and save you, so you didn't have to get beat by 'The Perfect Design'. Either way Hunt, your still behind me. I am still the number one contender for that eVolved title. Don't worry when I get the belt, I'll be sure to give you a shot. You'll just have to earn it first. Let me move on to Evans. The coward that had to blind side me. I got a proposition for you Evans. I want a match with you. I don't care if it is for the title or not, but I want a match. I know your booked at Legends 50 against Roc, but I want a shot after that. What do you say about Legends 51 Justice VS Evans? I'll come ask you at Legends 51, just let me know if you want your ass handed to you. Oh and come Legends 50, I got a ton of suprises for the world of eVo. Legends 50 you will see what revenge is. You will see the chosen ones, the select few, and the greatest players in the game. It's a night you wont want to miss.
