3 of Diamonds - Scars and Tears

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/4/02 Steam rose in the bathroom, collecting in droplets on the mirror. The water trickled down the side of his face, his body, and collect in a small puddle at his feet. Nice hotel, but bad plumbing, Jason thought. The soapy water was rising steadily, but nothing to get in a fuss over. Even if it was, Jason figured he wouldn’t care anyway. Few months back, something similar happened and his reaction was quite the same as this one. Come to think of it, when the hell had he cared about anything in the past few years. As he turned off the shower and as the water sank away, he climbed out, pushing open the glass doors. He stared down at his clothes, which had been the same since Sunday and hadn’t been washed since. The sweat and blood would be the appropriate words to use in these thoughts, but the only word Jason could think of was wasted. Wasted life. Wasted tries. All he did was waste his time chasing a career that would never have anything good to offer him except more scars and more tears. Jason took the complementary white hotel room towel and dried himself off. As he brought it up to his hair and began to use friction to eliminate the water, he stared into the mirror where a single drop of water cleared a skinny path of reflection. He saw his turquoise colored eye staring forward, wrapped around a black iris and surrounded by an empty white. Lost in this unfamiliar enigma, Jason’s hand crossed over the glass, wiping away all the barriers between him and his mystery. Jason, staring at his own naked body, didn’t recognize what he saw. All he saw was the shell of a man that had long since passed away, crying away the rest of his days in a casket. Now all he could see, staring back at him from a miserable, dreary looking glass was a ghost. A ghost with an aged gash across his chest, from a past too familiar to forget. The scar would always be there with him, reminder of true love and pure hatred all at once. “So this is what you’ve become?” The reflection seemed to come alive with Jason’s words, and the rage suddenly had a new charge. Jason’s veins pumped blood through his body, and his fists rolled together in his rage. Without hesitation or realization, his fists smashed against the mirror, glass and blood falling together into the white, porcelain sink like the ruins of a mountain tumbling to the earth. Jason stared down at the shards, looking at his own image broken up and blanketed with blood. Only then could he truly see his reflection staring back at him, and not a specter wandering the world, searching for the path with his name carved into the signs. The sight of seeing acerbic, sharp glass oozing with the thick, crimson that rippled through his heart gave Jason the thought that had run through his mind many a time. He could simply pick up the mirror, and introduce it to his wrist with a slice. And then the impending darkness could end all the sorrow. Jason didn’t know how long he stood there, staring down at the piece of what he had just destroyed and wondering what it would feel like to end it all. Could have been mere seconds, or it could have been an eternity. It was only when a tear mixed in with some of the blood on one particular shard that his wandering ended, and his mind was awake once more. The thought was dismissed. It was no longer an option. Jason wrapped the towel around his waist, and went to get some bandages for his knuckles. And as he walked out of the bathroom, he unknowingly saw the three of diamonds, staring at him lifelessly from the bureau.
