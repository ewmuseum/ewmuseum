So am I the second person to ask this question?

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/31/02 Luther Wisdom sits in a darkened room alone with only his thoughts and a newspaper. He has clipped out two items and placed it on his mirror far in the distance. One clipping is the Obituary of Xavier Citizen December 31st 2002 (late edition of the newspaper carried this) the second clipping is todays NYSE closing for Luther Wisdom MultiNational.... 'How do they expect me to think straight during a time like this?' he says in a somber voice..... 'Life is so short so precious...we are here today and literally gone tomorrow. Why do we do the things we do? I can't seem to come up with the words....' he starts to choke up a tear running down his face..... 'My partner, my friend Xavier Citizen died this morning. he was my mentor together we were to capture the Evolution and go to the top of this business. So how do they expect me to plan a funeral.....when I have these emotions running wild in me? How can I plan a funeral.......WHEN MY FREAKIN STOCK IS DOWN TO 9.34 a share?!?!?!?!' he throws the rest of the paper in a blind rage..... 'You see this is about priorities. We all knew Xavier was going to die...so why did he have to pick this week to do so? Why not when Enron collapsed? It was because he like everyone else is jealous of The Next Generation of Old School. Sure he gave it the old honorable try but in reality his sick cancer having ass should have been in a hospital and not trying to wrestle to recapture some legacy.' he calms himself down...... 'But I can't blame my stock falling on a dead man. No; besides being a public relations nightmare I can't make him see the error of his ways. No I would have won the tag title and subsequently doubled WMN stock if it wasn't for the assistance of Troy Pierce. I guess I should thank you for aiding me.....so I thank you. Now I want to offer you some free advice....' his look turns dead serious..... 'I've been involved in the legacy of two other men; I will not make that mistake a third time. If you choose to pursue this relationship...you do so at your own risk. Pray my friend you don't become bad for business.' he pauses.... 'Speaking of business.....it seems the person Xander was speaking to has finally revealed himself. All I have to say on the subject......ONE, I know you better than you know yourself. Don't even try to think this is your way back to Evolution old man. Times have changed and the student will become the teacher. You may be an Urban Legend.....but I am The Next Generation.' Luther puts his head down as a figure appears in his room hidden in the shadows..... LW:'Was wondering when you'd show up?' Wisdom says withoutout looking up..... LW:'know this if you are running around in Grey and crimson jumpsuits again not even YOU can stop me!' (to be continued......)
