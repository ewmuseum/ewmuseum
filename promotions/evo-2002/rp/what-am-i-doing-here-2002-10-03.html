What am I doing here?

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OCTOBER 3rd, 2002 REAL TIME The tension in Tyler Davidson's office is so thick you can cut it with a knife. Both Malcolm King and Tyler Davidson are here in a session to clear the proverbial air.... TD:'Why the smug look King?' Malcolm King has since regained his composure.... MK:'You do have the contract of William Louise and Xavier Citizen; I on the otherhand have what is called a managerial license.' TD:'Big deal.....that is impressive how again?' MK:'Simple I have a proposal for you, I act as both William and Xavier's manager as a tag team.....we'll use The BlackWhisper name. What you get out of the deal is someone that can control X....' TD:'And what do you get out of it?' MK:'The satisfaction of helping the Evolution tag scene...giving back to the federation so to speak?' TD:'This wouldn't have anything with The Unlimited being in the tag tournament would it? Plus Echo is trying to escape your shadow...remember?' MK:'Tyler you know sooner or later he will do something to Evolution, he has an agenda....you and I both know it.' TD:'Well the prospect of having a signature tag team is tempting.....tell you what Malcolm, he earns a spot on the roster you get to manage both him and William as a tag team.' Malcolm King gets up to leave. As he is leaving Tyler Davidson thinks about what just happened..... TD:'Thank you Malcolm.....Echo won't like this but you may have just presented a solution to a potential problem. The question I have now Mr' King is what is your agenda? And why are you still willing to use your ex-partner as a tool?' JOURNAL ENTRY FOR ECHO OCTOBER 4th 2002 'So my next test is yet another former cell fighter The Outkast Eric Knight. What is Tyler trying to do kill me? Ohhh I forgot I'm already practically dead. What's strange to me is what I heard from Monica Angel. She told me Malcolm King was seen in Evolution offices the other day. What is that asshole up to? I shouldn't refer to him as such but he has a tendency to interfere. This can't be good especially with the rumors flying around that Davidson just purchased the ULWN...a thing that can't make Malcolm happy; is all the more reason for me to worry. Malcolm likes to make deals....and somehow I have the feeling I may be apart of one. But what can I do....as part of my deal with Tyler I gave him creative control. I hate this feeling....' 'Lucky for me Knight is the next lucky victim. You see Wed. Match is for a chance to actually earn some entrance music for myself....yea!!!! Every good wrestler needs a good entrance and the music is the first part of the equasion. Look at me excited at the possibility of getting music...whats next pyro? But Eric also represents the chance I have to prove not only to Davidson but myself that I actually belong here....and that I am a viable contender for the Universal Championship, but where does Malcolm fit in this equasion?' 'Finally there is the lovely Seara I hope I made a first impression she's bound to remember. Sure Tyler doesn't want me on TV past my alotted time...but then again, who's going to stop me?' to be continued.......
