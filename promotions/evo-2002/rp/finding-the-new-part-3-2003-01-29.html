Finding The New part 3

29 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-29-03 'Scott. You opened up. Finally, I can feel exactly what someone else feels.' The voice of Seara is heard. The scene fades in and Scott Frost and Seara are sitting down in a booth at a resteraunt. Scott looks over at Seara, and places his hand on hers. 'Katherine. I feel nothing, like you do. You want pain for pleasure. I want you for pleasure. But you did, you did open up this new side of me. A side I have been battling to hide for ages.' Frost says to Seara. Seara looks at Frost. 'You said you killed a man. What happened?' Seara inquires. Frost looks at Seara and his head falls down as if he is ashamed. 'Scott, what happened?' Seara asks again, but only with concern. Frost looks up at Seara and nods his head. 'I was eight-teen....' Frost begins. The scene fades away and you can once again see the young Frost standing with his friends. They are standing on a street corner in a circle and laughter is the only thing that can be heard from the group of friends. 'I was hanging out with the normal crew, and we were surviving broke ass Detroit with our lives. But none of us were man enough to do the things others did for cash. So we gambled....' Frost says narrating his story. A small group of guys comes walking over to the other group and one of the men looks at Frost. 'Ay yo, Frost. What's up wit the Bulls this week?' The young Frost looks at the man, and smiles. 'Bulls? Well, lets just say that if you bet on them. I will be taking your money, again.' Frost says sounding very cocky. The group of guys laugh, even the two guys that came with the first one. The guy talking to Frost smiles and looks around placing his hand slightly over his nose and he sniffs. 'So you got jokes eh?' The guy asks. 'Yeah, I mean. You're a Bulls fan. But lets face it, the Lakers are killing everyone. I hate the Lakers just as much as the next guy but they are running full spread over the sheets.' Frost replies. The guy pushes Frost. 'You dissin' the Bulls?' The guy asks again. Frost nods. 'Don't diss the Bulls, man.' The guy demands. The guy pushes Frost and Frost looks at him trying to with hold his rage infront of his friends. Frost doesn't say anything and he just stares the guy down. The guy throws a right hand and Frost is quick to the counter and moves to the side and slams the guys face into the brick wall with a very sickening thud. The group, including Frost all look at each other and then one of Frosts friends pulls out a cell phone and dials on it. 'Uh, yeah. Nine, One, One?' Frosts friend asks. Frost stares at everyone, and then at the lifeless body. 'Yeah, my buddy just got attacked and he fought back and now the attacker ain't moving. We need some help.' Frosts friend announces. Frost looks at his friend. 'Yes ma'am, he is still here. Alright, we will keep him here. Thank you ma'am.' Frosts friend announces again. Frost looks around. 'Dude, why ain't he moving?' One of the other guys ask. Frost looks around and the scene fades back to Seara who has a very intrigued look on her face. 'So what happened?' Seara ask eagerly. Frost looks at Seara and shakes his head. 'Well, the impact caused an immediate hemerage to his brain and he died instantly. But since he attacked me first, and I was defending myself. I got immunity. I was let go free, but that still creeps behind me every day. I don't want to lose my temper like that again.' Frost says to Seara. Seara looks at Frost and this time she grabs his hand and looks at him with comfort. 'Well, the way you explained it. You seemed kinda scared. I don't think anger had a part of it at all.' Seara says. Frost looks at Seara. 'I wasn't scared. Part of me knew what I was going to do. I felt a loose brick on my neck when he pushed me the first time. I had the attack all planned out in my head, but I waited for him to strike because I wanted to be looked upon as innocent. But I wanted to kill that kid, for no reason. Just everything flooded my brain, I hated my life in Detroit and I wanted some kind of retribution for what that place has put me through. But now I am rich, I got my own house in the 'good' part of town and I got you. I don't think that my anger can take over too much.' Frost informs Seara. Seara looks at him. 'But what about Shocka?....' Seara asks. Frost stares at Seara with a look never seen before on him. His head slowly shakes back and forth and then he looks down at the table. His head jerks back up and looks at Seara. 'Come on, lets go...' Frost says. Frost grabs her hand and they leave, and the scene fades to black.
