problematic part 1

2 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-02 Wrestling is a game of skill and determination. Being a master comes with hard work and intelligence but it also relies heavily on chance. You can bet if you get one shot and you grab hold of it when you slip up you get a second chance to prove you really are the true master. Third shots are rare and hard to earn. It’s a fact tough if you’re high up in the front office you can get them with the snap of the fingers. For instance Hedges is not a man of high standing in the front office so he received his second shot but if it isn’t grabbed on Legends he may never get another one. It’s his time to shine without Luther Wisdom butting in and stealing his limelight. One man is capable from rising from the bottom and dominating this federation and he’s already taking a stand. The scene opens up in a bar with Hedges standing in the corner alone throwing darts and drinking beer. He’s slowly draining a pitcher into his glass before eVo shows up and he shakes his head and has a seat. He props his feet up on the table and continues throwing darts while sitting down. The cameraman comes over to him and counts down from 3 and starts to film. “Sometimes you need a break from all the bullshit going on around you, things that make life pretty unmanageable. I’ve got a million problems and only one answer to all of them. The problem is they say fighting isn’t the answer. Psh…What do the peace loving hippies know anyway. They just can’t fight. Simple primitive way to solve conflicts….from New- TV to front office politics to Luther Wisdom and back to my title. Ryan Justice in the cell… man there’s a million men who I wouldn’t love to just (holds is fist in the air) rock them between the eyes a few times. It solve my problems and make my life a shitload easier” Hedges slams back his glass of beer and begins pouring another. He motions for the sound guy to go grab the darts off the board and bring them to him… “Now a huge problem of mine has become New – TV…I mean they’re” Hedges pauses and grabs the darts off the table and begins tossing them at the board and begins speaking again.. “Now as far as New-TV goes they are extra baggage I’m lugging around with me. With partners you are supposed to have to worry less about the shit going on around you. With them I’m continuously trying to watch may back and stay loyal and watch theirs. Then when I start having to watch my back from my partner’s something’s wrong. Terribly wrong…” Hedges motions for the man to go and get him more darts…. “You know I can blame this mostly on Thug Roc but in no way is Omega exempt from his share of the blame. First off when Omega go suspended we never objected and took into our account the advancement of our own careers. What kind of partners where we? We weren’t partners… Omega held us together and brought us together. I was purely focused on simply winning my evolved title back, which I was screwed out of but that’s another story. Then Thug realizing he had lost all of his glory and was fading fast because of the arrogant prick he was tried to show he had some power and boss me around…HAHAHAHA boss me around that little twerp should learn his place. Back when I was an emerging superstar I dominated him. Hell that’s when he was Shocka’s bodyguard. Hell he hired me for protection he’s a puss. I refuse to let a wash up attempt to push me around because his fifteen minutes of fame is fading. As far as I’m concerned Thug can take his ego and tie it to the end of my boot because they both need to be shoved up his ass. Especially since he cost me my spot in the cell at A New Dawn and I haven’t forgotten about that nor do I plan to let it slide” Pausing to drink some of his beer Hedges continues speaking… “Now Omega that idiot is more bronze than brains. He can’t wait for a good opportunity to strike he just goes for the first shot. Well he shot my ass in the foot I’m not going to stand for it. His stupid woman has gotten to his head. He’s turning soft on me. Sky Night that c*** couldn’t take care of herself and has Omega head over heals for her. She taged along for the fame and the money and then we imploded because of her. It’s Jamison for Christ sake. It’s not like he’s good or anything. She couldn’t even handle herself and she’s supposed to be a superstar why does she need us to bail her out. Jamison is lucky though and you know I hope he gets lucky and instead of breaking thugs nose again breaks his neck. As far as the match between Thug and Omega I hope both the idiots destroy each other so I don’t have to deal with either one of them later on down the line and that would cut the chains I’m pulling behind me off” Hedges gets up out of his seat and goes over and orders another pitcher of beer as the scene fades and part one ends…
