Bar Brawl

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-06 'I fucked up Josh “Shockwave” Horroway tonight' Trevor yelled as he slammed back a beer. Trevor already had like ten beers since legends ended, and couldn't remember how many shots he had before the beers. 'You fucking stomped his ass around that cell bro' Donaven pounded back his beer as he laughed with Trevor. Donaven was like Trevor Brother, they basicly grew up together. Been friends since they were in diapers. Donaven was a Wrestler/Fighter like Trevor, but right know he was sidelined from a injury that he recieved in a fight in Vegas. Trevor pounded his last bit of beer as he slammed it down hard on the counter, as it smashed under his hand. It quickly drew the bartenders attention as he walked over to Trevor. 'You may be some big shot in eVo, but your not here, so settle down or leave.' The bartender remarked at Trevor. 'What, shut the hell up and get me another beer, before I knock ya out' Trevor flicked the broken peices of glass at the bartender. The Bartender waved for two bouncers to come over as Trevor slowly stood up, and Donaven followed. 'You know what, fuck yo......'Trevor quickly punched the bartender right in the face as the bartender dropped like a sack of patotes to the floor. The bouncer grabbed Trevor and slammed him hard into a Jut Box as, he gave Trevor a quick left punch. Trevor slowly staggered back as he quickly came back as if the punch didn't even touch him. Trevor released a bunch of kicks and punchs at the bar, as his last kicked nailed the bartender just under the temple knocking him right out. Donaven couldn't fight due to his condition, so he grabbed a beer bottle and broke it over the other bartenders head, as Trevor ran and tackled him picking him up and slamming him into a bunch of stools as Trevor started punching him in the face. 'Come on bitch get up' Trevor screamed in anger as Donaven got Trevor off the bouncer. 'Lets get out of here' Donaven pulled Trevor towards the door. 'Whatcha looking at' Trevor taunted the witness as Donaven pulled Trevor out the door. 'Fuck....'Donaven stopped in his tracks as Trevor still taunted on as three cops walked towards them......
