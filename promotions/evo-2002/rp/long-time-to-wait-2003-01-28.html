Long Time....To Wait

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-28-02 'The scene starts off with Strictly Prohibited watching the skys as he sits outside in the colds watching the jets fly by in the cold. His mask covers his whole face and his hair is tucked under the mask as you can't tell anything else after that. Strictly walks into the airport with people staring at him with kids hiding behind there mothers, and fathers stepping infront of the mothers with fear that this man could be the next false hope for this world, and a fading hero nothing more.' Strictly Prohibited: I walk around day after day, hour after hour, and minute after minute thinking of what just happened at the ppv. Where has my head been at? I was attacked at the ppv (chuckles) I didn't think anyone had the balls to mess with what you can't rip a dent it....(silence.) Listen, do you hear that? It's the sound of fear! These people have it, and so do the unlimited for I Strictly X! I am gonna put an X threw everyone of your heads you stupid cowards! You filth! You stupid bunch of mindless machines! Unlimited you have messed with me, and now I mess with you the mind games have already begun! I braught in a little help with this matter, because I can't walk this path alone for this scene. I needed a little back up so I phoned a good friend who has had my back since $W, and was screwed! He came to help me out, and now you have no idea who he is either...hahaha What is your god telling you now Unlimited? Is it telling you now that you have no idea what I am doing to your minds, you think you know this so well, but then you know nothing at all! My prophacy has been laid, and when they call my name...the mask will come off...and your mouths will drop to the floor. My name is nothing more then the past, and that's where it will stay till the time is right, and when I feel that it is time for me to show you just what I can do. Right now Unlimited you think that I am some garbage eVo picked up off the street, but no...hehe I have a past... My past is full of greatness, and my greatness isn't over it just has begun! Unlimited you have no idea who I am...and your minds tell you that you do know that I am no match for you..but what your mind isn't telling you is that you shouldn't judge a book by it's cover you should read a little into the book 'chuckles'. I am focused on what my task is, and what I am here to do..I am here to destroy the evil that lives in eVo, that evil will fall...all that evil will fall. Whoever attacked me last week has some death wishes, or is just plain crazy...maybe your more braver then you seem. Whoever you maybe hooded stranger...remember in the back of your mind whatever goes around...comes around! My vision has come much clearer in this long legacy I will uphold onto you all here in eVo. I have graced the steps of this company three times...EdW, nEw, and eVo...I've been here before, and I'm not just some lacky your hearts believe I am. I'm no man of mystery, but now that's all I'm gonna share your little hearts will just haft to live with this, and that is I am coming for you Unlimited...and when it's all said and over...when you wake up in that hospital bed with that X on your forhead you will know that you where just branded by Strictly X! Remember Unlimited...Your Already Dead!
