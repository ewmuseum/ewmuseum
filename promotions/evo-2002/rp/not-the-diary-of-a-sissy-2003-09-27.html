Not the diary of a sissy...

27 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-27 ~Shocka’s mind had become that of a mad man…it was pushing out words and sending ideas all over. The actions he took against James Kimson worked how he liked them too. James took notice of Shocka, he was knocked senseless…be to be truthful. That’s nothing compared to what he has in store for Kimson at Digital Destruction.~ ‘Kimson, I left you lying…but you are still breathing…you think there is a reason for that? When I could have snapped your neck from up there with a piledriver or something even more sick…you should feel blessed…you have received a chance from the man who paved the way for people like you to hold that belt. Sure you’ve been around the biz and you’re a veteran…but I am one of the people that made sure eVolution stayed stable during the first 6 months…I put asses in the seats then and they stuck until now. Do you realize this?’ Shocka questioned the camera. ‘Nope you’re to ignorant for that…you will take notice of me Kimson…and my name will be forever imprinted in your head…I will be the man who ended the hype.’ Shocka showed a sick smile. ~What drove this man you ask? Plain and simple it was the lack of respect he received, Shocka won the Continental title, he beat Scott Frost to become the Road to Glory champion for the first time in eVolution…something no one else has done. And to cap that off when he did get his shot at the Universal title from that he won the belt. What can be better than that?~ ‘I did what I set out to do at Legends…I drove my point home…I’m in this to destroy you James…I’m In this to end the hype and get the Universal title for myself…sure I used the help of your beautiful sister…and when I take your belt I might earn myself some indecent liberties…all night long…who knows James only time will tell.’ ~He now stood you could see he was starting to get a little more excited.~ ‘I won’t fuck up like you James…I’m not going to lose my temper…I will not lose sight of what’s at stake in the match and I will do what I have to do to beat you. That #1 Stunna from the cage will be forever remembered as the prelude to me beating you at Digital Destruction and going on a second Universal title reign. You see…I saved you from Damon…I knew what he could of done in there and I figured if I make it look vicious and put you down for a few minutes then he’d back off and be happy with the destruction. Had I left you and him go in that cage you would have never made it to the PPV…and I would have never got that shot at you. Sure I would have to go up against an opponent with no wrestling ability…but I bet he hits hard…doesn’t he James?’ Shocka’s lips curled to a grinch like grin and he let out a little laugh. ‘Hey Kimson next time you see Damon thank him for me will you?’ Shocka asked as the scene now faded.
