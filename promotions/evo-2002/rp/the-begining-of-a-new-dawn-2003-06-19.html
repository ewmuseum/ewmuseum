The begining of a new dawn....

19 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-19 Toronto, Canada Syndicate International Headquarters Section 21-A *Top Secret* It has been a number of weeks since The Syndicate has spoken, or graced the airwaves of Evolution television. All contact from them has come from encrypted code. Many are wondering, many are waiting, many want to know what Big Daddy Dreamer and The Heartbreak Chick have been up too since their near fatal match with Twisted Reality. All remember the horror of that match in which three participants were put on the shelf. BDD, HBC, and SkyNight. All capable, all dangerous opponents, but, one person made a fatal mistake, he didn’t destroy The Syndicate. He only injured them, and that will be his downfall….. Morpheus Omega……you should have finished us off….when you had the chance. You will come to learn Morpheus, failure is not an option. Failure will not be tolerated. Failure…….will get you killed. The Syndicate is enternal. We are forever. Like a tidal wave of power. We may bend, we may morph into something else, but we are forever constant, and when we hit you with the full power of The Syndicate Collective, you will drop to your knees and pray to whatever god you worship, that you finished us off. But……… you didn’t. Your biggest mistake of your feeble career. You didn’t get the job done. You have the come the closest of any competitor within our seven years of existence. For that, I guess you should be commended, but we don’t reward half-assed jobs. We do not tolerate them. We simply……. End them! So Morpheus Omega, the so called ‘God of Sleep and Dreams’, the person who thinks he runs Evolution, know this. We never forget nor forgive. We watched how your pathetic ego allows you to get the better of yourself. Have you even checked on SkyNight? Have you even checked on her current condition? How she is doing, feeling, or have you been so deluded in your own manical grandious that you’ve simply forgotten about her? We haven’t, and we took oh so something precious from her. Science is an amazing thing, especially when you work for our company. Research and development knows no boundaries to us. Your failure to look after your partner will be revealed in due time. And I assure you Morpheus, it will forever haunt your dreams. However, that is in the near future, and we are in the now, the present. You are currently suspended aren’t you Morpheus. Piety, but not surprising that you let your ego get the better of you. Oh Morpheus, you deluded fool. You really haven’t a clue do you. Tis funny, someone else was once like you, but then he learned, he learned how the system of complexity really is. That everything is intertwined, symbiotic of each other. However, your no where near as bright as the former Malcom King was. So here you are Morpheus, hell bent to destruction and no one giving a damn about it. Alone, isolated, paranoid, delusional. Haunted by what you see could have been, but never will be because of your own pathetic little failures in life. The only friend you had, you turned your back on. Pathetic. But then again, knowing you, you feel as if you don’t need anyone. All those years, you kept people away, delighting in the torment of your peers, now laugh at you for you really don’t have a clue as to what is going on around you. Stuck like cement in the sands of time. Evolution means evolving. Evolving into something greater than thought possible. For years we have evolved in every federation we’ve ever been in, starting back in the RGWF. Evolving from Somer’s tyrannical reigns of the AWF, EDW, and into here. Evolution is a fact of life, you evolve or your niche gets destroyed by someone better than you. However, as Darwin states, sometimes, things mutate and skips several generations of evolvement. A super species reappears from the past and conquers what initially destroyed them. This super-species transcends and returns to its rightful home, on top of the food chain. July 3rd, that super-species returns home to Evolution to return on top of the mountain, before hunting down that pathetic little prey made, no, forced them to evolve. The Syndicate will be coming back to Evolution, and this time…… no one is safe…….not the faces, not the heels, not even the fans. This time……… it’s personal…….
