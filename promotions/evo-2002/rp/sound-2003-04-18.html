Date: 4/18/03 Cars and screaming and cell phones and dogs. Birds chirping and babies crying. Thunder in the sky and wind through the streets. Men swearing and women flirting and boys fighting and girls giggling. This is the shit I heard every second of every day. It’s like some concert that is completely unrehearsed borage of sounds that God just threw together into one mishmash of noises. Nothing clear was said and no one came up to you and explained what all these sounds meant. They were just there, giving the world a voice that I sure as hell didn’t want to hear. But what freakin choice did I have now? The world – welcome to it mother fucker Another day, another city. Milwaukee was the name, and of course I was the game. This place looked just like all the others, with buildings rising into the sky and streets packed with cars and semis. Sometimes I wondered just what it would be like to walk around in Chicago or Sacramento or Miami or Dallas. Now I know that it won’t matter where the hell it is that I go – they’ll be the same. Except Dallas. Those rednecks seem to aggravate me a lot more then any native of Chicago or Sacramento ever could. But I was in Milwaukee, and I was surrounded by all the little people that Jason loathed and loved at the same time, if that was at all possible. Just like the rest, their faces showed no emotion and their expressions were meaningless, but I knew that all of them felt the same about each other. They all hated each other. None of them realized how equal they all were. Instead, they just figured they were all different for the stupidest reasons imaginable. They were all scum. I wished I could flush them all away like the shit that they were. As the sun set in the distance, and as I walked through what appeared to be a rundown part of the city, I heard a voice echo through an alley behind me. “Hey honey. Looking for a good time?” I turned around, and leaning against a wall, smoking a cigarette and smiling in my direction, was a young woman. She looked to be in her mid twenties, with all the shapes and curves to go with her. Nice breasts. Great ass from what stuck out in my view. Arousal? Most definitely. “Sure. Why not?” I walked over to her, staring over her red dress and her legs, she tossed the cigarette to the ground, and walked over to me, her high heels clicking to her stride. Red lipstick and brown eyes. She was very attractive. And I knew that she would totally obey my every command. For the right amount of money that is. Sounds good to me.
