Fire.Ashes

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aftermath in times of unseen futures. As the lifelessness drags itself across pathways, it's destination becomes clear. Tall and shiny, as if a true knight adorned by his armor. Sliding waves of shimmering relfectives as what still remains untouched glides in. Desolation ensues, as the ringing and the meaningless blabber come, and fade away again. Sullen eyes that glare through the panes of see-through fascination. See-through souls crystal clear with dark intentions, roaming free and about without penalty. Inequality soaking through like the most porous of sponges. Cheerful chiming motioning further commotion. Lifelessness moving slowly like a shadow through light encompassed passages. Solitude again ringing like a deafening silence throughout an empty cell. Impatience setting in like a stillness emobdied by anger turning into it's rage. Calmness, as levels turned down resume again their quant positions. Entrance, center of attention looming inside resting into another relaxed contortion. 'Hi Jeremy'. Silence. 'Listen. Despite not being scheduled to compete in these house shows, it's in your signed contract that your required to show up. If accidents happen and we have someone who can fill in for the match, then we do it. It's a little hard to do that when the next person scheduled doesn't show at all. Your also not staying in the hotel where the other guys do. That's fine, but your havn't been seen with any of them at all. Not on flights, riding together. Jameson, one of your friends, hasn't talked to you yet. I mean, what the hell happened to you. It's almost like you fell off the earth.' Silence, as the solitude loosens. 'I don't know if this is the way things should be going. I realize the angles you've been in may not have turned out the way you wanted, and we are stacking up your loss record, but you have to show us something to make us change our minds. Or at least get you a new storyline. We could try and work out an angle with one of the new signees. Like-' 'Just put me in a match.' 'Well, if we do that we should evaluate-' 'Just put me in a match.' Lifelessness discouraged further ramblings as they slowly wined down to a halt. Value slowly trying to make it's comeback. As valiancy choked on it's own words, fearful eyes slowly closing for the moment.
