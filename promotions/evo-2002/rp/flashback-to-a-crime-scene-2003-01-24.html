~{Flashback to a Crime Scene}~

24 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/24/03 typed -{1/22/03}~set {Two days earlier.... just after Legends went off the air... the 'after-party' camera crew found it's way to Xander walking down the hallways to the Unlimited locker room. As we near the door, you can hear sounds of glass breaking, various things hitting the walls, the floor. Just general sounds of chaos. Xander hurriedly makes his way to the door, and as he throws it open, he hits the floor, ducking a television monitor that goes out the door, and practically explodes upon impact with a road case.} 'What the hell is going on in here?' Xander asks as he makes his way to his feet. {The cameras make their way around back of Xander seeing the sight inside. It's a disaster area. Broken mirrors, table pieces all over the place. Clothes ripped, and tosses about. As the camera pans to see the other side of the room, you see Asis punching a locker room, which has a hole, just about the size of Asis' fist in it. As both the camera, and Xander catch the sight almost simultaneously, Xander runs over, and pins Asis' shoulders to the locker} 'What the hell are you doing?' Xander all but shouts at the steamed Unlimited enforcer. 'Mason, Dreamer, Prohibited, Tanaka, I want their fucking blood!' Asis grits through his teeth at the second in command. 'Tonight it's their lives!' 'Calm down man, we got them in four days at the PPV.' Xander rationalizes. 'I don't fucking care!' The brawn announces back. 'They breathed their last breathes tonight.... they are gonna die for what they did!' {Xander, taken aback a little as his apprentices' words, tries to be the cooler head.} 'The PPV man.. we'll get them. We got Tanaka and Prohibited in the tag title match. And Dreamer and Mason are gonna work each other over... so they'll be easy pickings for us.' Xander tries to calm down the ever ready Asis. 'Mystery opponents or not, we got them Sunday. We need you to be thinking straight... Sunday is a huge day for Unlimited.' 'Fuck huge day for Unlimited' Asis shoots back. 'This is war.. no one touches me, let alone gets away with that shit! They will all fall, one by one.. by my hand. And anyone else who gets in my way will feel the same fate.' {Asis pushes Xander away, almost knocking him down, and exits breaking the door off the hinge as he leaves. For the next 30-40 seconds you can hear a variety of colorful language echoing down the hall, until it fades out} 'Now they've done it.. damnit.' Xander says to himself. 'Stupid sons-of-bitches.' {The scene fades out of the destruction that was once a locker room}
