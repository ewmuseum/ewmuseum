Family Issues

23 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd October 2002 Only a day after Asis visited the house of Seara’s father, the female wrestler has shown up. She looks tired and somewhat dazed. Since Legends last week when Asis broke the neck of her brother, she has been at his bedside, thinking hard about the nicest thing to do to save her nephew the agony of a father who would never be able to treat him like a real son. Moving her brother to a local Chicago hospital, Seara took her 3-year-old nephew to see his father and had to explain that daddy would always be like this, daddy had been put through a nasty attack which he didn’t deserve and now because of a man who had grown up near him, he would never be able to teach his son how to ride a bike or throw a ball or play sports. After seeing the look on her nephews face, Seara began to think hard about her true emotions, and where she seemed not to care about her own brother dying, she felt sorry for the small blonde haired blue eyed child who always gazed deep into her eyes. After returning the small child to her father’s house, she took a long slow walk to the hospital on the opposite side of town. Soaked by the pouring rain she slipped into the hospital undetected, past the reception desk and down the hallway to the elevators, taking it top level 3 she slipped out the large steel doors and across the hallway into the private room of her brother. Her own flesh and blood, a boy who would have given his life to save her, a man who would still even now give his life to make hers just the slightest bit better. He had lain in the bed so silent and motionless, his eyes open, but nothing seemingly registering. Asis had done more than he had thought, he had damaged Jake in the head, and although he was looking straight into Seara’s eyes, he didn’t even know she was there, in his eyes he may well have still been asleep on life support, struggling to breath and stay alive long enough to see his son. Leaning over him Seara had whispered “he knows you love him, and he knows that I love you, he just doesn’t know that I only love you because you are my brother and it is an unwriten ruke that I should love you” with that Seara covered the eyes of her brother and kissed him on the forehead, then releasing his eyes she pinched his nose tight and covered his mouth with the other hand and using her foot managed to pull the main switch out the wall. Pressing down tight she released her brother the nicest way she knew, to turn off his life and force him to suffocate to death. After she had snuck out and found herself here, outside her father’s house in the Chicago suburbs. Strange that I could help kill my brother, my own flesh and blood, someone I should want to protect, yet I could do it, I have no regrets, it was partly the best thing to do, but here I am outside a house where I spent a minimal amount of my life, a house where I know the residents, my father, a strong respectable man that I would go to the ends of the earth for, my stepmother who I would love to take in the ring and show her the hell she put me through, and right now my younger brother and my nephew. I know that stuff but I cannot go in there, there is something so wrong about it, they are complete strangers, I do not know them well enough to enter their house. 'We are one, inseparable, together forever; we will always have one another to turn to. One another to hurt and love, we are one, we are the ultimate pair'. Words my father used to say to me when mom was still here. Yes Asis look, I do have feelings, but I could care less what you do to my family, these people are mere shadows of people I once knew. They are not the people I once loved. Gail will always be there, waiting for me to take her life in revenge for the life she gave me. She broke my father down, turned him against wrestling, and I could never allow that. Daddy is the only one I want, daddy is the only one I love. You say Asis that you hope you taught me a lesson about family; all you did was show me what I don’t need. I stayed with Jake because it was the right thing to do, and hell where else was I to go, answer me that Asis. I know you must think it got to me to see you attack Jake, but now that I have had time away from the rushing crowds I have had time to realise that I do not care. In some way I found a sense of pleasure when I watched you attack Jake, you done something I could not, you done what I always thought about, you pushed his suffering to a whole new level, showed him what I have felt my entire life, small rejected unwanted trash, and I know Asis that you will agree with that last remark so don’t bother, hold your breath. Stepping closer to the house, Seara looks in each window as she passes it by, she finally stops and stares in at the window, at first seemingly looking at her own reflection, but as the camera moves it becomes clear she has found her father in his fitness room. Unable to attend the fitness school where his daughter was trained as a fighter, he sits in his wheelchair lifting weights most days. Today he is doing something new, reaching up he grabs hold of a bar with both hands and pulls his entire body weight up, he continues with the same easy movement he continues to do pull-ups on the bar. Standing perfectly still, Seara watches and counts under her breath as her father lifts himself up and lowers himself over and over again. Turning to the camera Seara looks hard at it. Faith Asis is where I will prove I am more than you think I am. I may not be like him, but be sure Asis I am willing to take you to the max. I will fight to the end, this will be the last time you face me in this feud, I am fed up with you Asis, you are no longer an exciting challenge, rather more of an old accomplishment. I will only say one thing about that man, stay away from him Asis, if you touch him, I will bring up something in life you may want to keep hidden, he is worth more than you, he is worth more than the pain I could cause you Asis. You mean nothing important to me, and just so you know, I can back up my words with actions. Turning back to the window Seara looks in at her father who is still pulling his body up level with the bar, then lowering himself again slowly. Although in a wheelchair, this is a man who does not let his disability get in the way of an active and healthy lifestyle. Hard to believe how cruel family can be, I remember the last thing I heard from him as a teenager before I left his life for good, ‘you are nothing, you will always be nothing, you will amount to shit, you are a waste of the oxygen you breath’ cruel and unusual considering I was always daddies girl up until that bitch Gail took him from me, stole my daddy. I loved him then, and I love him now, and I will love him till I kill him, make him suffer for all I have been put through at his expense. He let that bitch control him, he tortured me, made me lose the little faith I had in humanity after mom died, and now I may have a chance to gain back a little of that trust in life, but I can’t do that until I face Asis and end our petty fighting once and for all. Most people think I do this because I love him, and maybe I do, maybe I don’t, I would not know if I did love him, I do not know love, I have never felt real love, I know the love of pain, but I am not sure I even know that anymore. I have heard people call me a tortured soul, and I would have to agree, I am emotionally distressed, I have not known emotions for so long I am not sure if I can feel anything except pure rage and hatred, so maybe I do love, but I will never know it. Seara’s movement suddenly becomes very erratic as she begins to back away from the house towards the road behind her. As she gets far enough away from the camera, it becomes apparent she is holding something, but unlike normal it is not a cigarette, this time, Seara is carrying a long solid wooden rod that she spins around in her hand at a great speed, occasionally passing it to her left hand and back again to her right hand. Faith Asis, you and me will meet face-to-face, one on one, and we will see then how much you think I need family. You will see who needs someone around to care for them. As soon as you decide that you are willing to allow me to disappear from your life, I will, you Asis made the first move, but at Faith I will make sure it is no longer going to happen. Do what you may to my family; until Faith is over I will have only one thing on my mind, but don’t think that means you will find it easy getting into my fathers house, I will always be near by, waiting Asis, waiting, I can sit forever and wait, but be warned if you do come back here and try to get to my father before Faith, I will give you a preview of the show. I have finished with you Asis, we are not friends like you have said, and I doubt we ever will be, but I will always be there, behind you, waiting to pounce, waiting for a reason to hurt you like you did to me back at Digital Destruction, or maybe Asis, I wont need a reason, maybe one day soon I will hurt you, take you out, make you feel how I did on that day. I still remember the feeling of my head being crushed, and at Faith I will repay the favour, you will feel the same amount of pain as I did, if not more. Faith Asis, is not far away, be prepared for a different kind of fight. With that Seara places herself in a seated position on her fathers lawn and begins to rock back and forth humming a tune to herself.
