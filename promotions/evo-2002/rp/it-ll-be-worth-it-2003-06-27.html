It'll be Worth it

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-27 'Alright kids that's enough leave your Father alone!' The voice followed by a collective groan of disaproval and the laughter of several adult voices. The camera fades in to a hospital room, sitting on a bed is eVo and wrestling superstar James Kimson, wearing a hospital gown with 3 small girls all mobbing him with hugs and laughter. A smile is on his face, perhaps the 1st smile in the last 13 hours or so. Also in view, a lovely woman holding a child, perhaps just a year or so old with a smile, not like Kimson's though. It's dressed with more concern then seeming Kimson's joyful smile. This here is his family. James and Victory Kimson with their 4 children, united in a hospital room probably like they have before. Kimson's arm, his left arm is wrapped quite heavily right now as the voice that you first heard was that of a man in a doctor's lab coat walks in, smiling while holding a clip board. 'Hello Walter' said Victory as she walked over and kissed the Doctor in a friendly jesture. 'Vicky, how are you! You look as beautiful as always.' said the Doctor. Obviously they've met before. The Doctor, Dr. Walter Vogel is the family doctor of the Kimsons. He's dealt with them all since a young age, with James himself since James's not so successful debut in wrestling 11 years ago. Kimson trusts him implicidly and Dr. Vogel is a family friend, if not just a specialist in sports medicine. 'Okay kids! You heard Uncle Walter, you and your Mother go and wait outside while I talk shop with him okay' said Kimson 'Aww Dad, but we wanna stay with you!' said the youngest Kimson offspring Julie. 'Yeah Dad' was a collective reply from the others. 'Now children, you know how it is when Daddy gets hurt. He tries to act all tough' said Victoria. 'C'mon lets go and get something to drink okay?' she said, trying to con her children. 'I want Icecream!' said Sarah, the oldest. 'Come on Sarah sweetheart, you want Icecream lets go see if we can get it. We have to leave your Father to his business here okay' said the Mother. The kids gave a collective 'Bye bye' to their loving Father, young Logan in his Mommy's arms reached out with his fingers moving and wiggling to his Dad but was taken with his sisters in search of a treat to distract the youngens. After the enterage left the room Dr. Vogel started to read the chart and looked up at Kimson 'You're nuts Jim' said the Doc. 'What? C'mon Walt, you know me better then I know myself sometimes. I wasn't going to say no.' said Kimson. 'Yeah, that's why you are nuts. I ought to throw you up on the 4th floor with the rest of the mentally disturbed. Jim, you just had surgery on your elbow and went in against a man in a match known to be dangerous! Now you are asking me to find a way to get you ready for this what....Caged Rumble? Jesus Christ Jim, now even YOU should know that is a huge mistake!' said the Doctor, sounding a bit perturbed with Kimson's request. 'Listen to me Walt, you know that with or without your help I'm going to be in that damn match. You know what it means to me to not miss time because of injury, and there are millions of people all over the world that are expecting to see James Kimson in that match fighting off 19 other men. That's my job Walt, you do yours!' said Kimson. Walter looks through the chart and sits down and places the chart on the nightstand and looks at Kimson 'Jim, we're friends. I remember how we met, you wheeled into the ER with a brace around your God Damn neck and I knew then and there you were a wreckless Son of a Bitch. But Jimmy, look at what you're risking injury for? Who cares if you win the match if in a years time you can't lift you're 18 month old Son over your head? For what, a shot at a Title? Against the man who just reinjured your surgically repaired elbow? What's the cost of success to you Jimmy? The World knows your name! The Wrestling industry sits and waits for your every move! What's a title belt good for other then padding an ego? Whats...' 'Listen Walt' said Kimson cutting off his friend. 'I know what you are trying to do, I understand and I know why you're trying to talk me out of it. But like I've told you a dozen times, you can't know what it's like until you do it. You know what a Drug addict is like, how many have you treated, how many have you saved? I'm an addict Jim, and I want 1 last fix and if it happens to be the end of the road of my career then so be it!' said Kimson. 'I've worked 11 years. 11 YEARS Walt. I've bleed and cried and clawed my way from NOTHING. With nothing handed to me my entire life to reach a level that only few can even think of. Yeah, an industry watches me, millions of people world wide would love to get a picture taken with me. Those things drive a man Walt? You ask me why I do it? Ask yourself why did you become a Doctor?' said Kimson. 'That's an unfair question' said Walter 'Hell no it isn't, answer the fucking question!' said Kimson. 'Well.... I guess because I wanted to help people' said Walter. 'Help people. Don't you think that's a cliche answer?' said Kimson 'Don't get me wrong Walt, I know you want to help people. Hell, if you didn't want to help me you wouldn't have become my families' doctor. You wouldn't have delivered me 4 amazing and beautiful children. You wouldn't be welcome in my home any day of the week and hour of the day. We're friends Walt, best of friends. So I'm going to ask you to help me reach something that even I didn't think I could do. If I win that match, if I get that one last Shot at the Universal Gold.... then maybe I can be the Greatest of all Time. Maybe I can break down that wall in my mind. Maybe i can be the best wrestler that ever lived. Rival those names that I grew up idolizing. But I can't without your help. I need to be in that match Walt, that match means everything to me. I'll have years to recover from what happens. I know I'll get hurt, you know I'll get hurt. How many times have I come into this hospital and had you fix my broken body? To many to count. So just do it one more time God Damn it!' said Kimson Walter bows his head in thought, the inner struggle. Help his friend achieve a dream at the cost of his future? Even without the elbow injury he took at Legends Walter advised Kimson to not return to wrestling so soon after the surgery he had 3 weeks ago. Most men take anywhere from 6-8 weeks to recover from such injury. 'Jim though had a talent for recover' thought the man to himself. 'Oh why am I bothering, he'll do it on his own anyway, stubborn bastard' he thought to himself. 'You're going to wrestle regardless of if I help you aren't you' said Walt. Kimson pauses. 'Yes, yes I will.' said Kimson. 'You Bastard Jim' said Walt. 'Lets take a look at it then' Walt takes the arm and begins to unwrap it. After a minute it's exposed, discolored and swollen. Walt grimices with the thought of the injury then starts to mumble to himself. 'Well X-ray says there's a hairline fracture' said Walt. 'But I'm more worried about the swelling due to the blow and the scar tissue getting seriously inflamed. That would lead to the possibility of more bone spurs and you'd have to get surgery done again, and this time there'd be no 3 week return time. You'd be out of action for months.' said Walter. Kimson looks at the swollen forarm. 'We could put a cast on it' said Walter. 'A Cast huh.' said Kimson. 'It would protect the fracture, but I doubt it will help with the pain. For that we'd have to give you a shot' said Walt. 'Freeze it huh' said Kimson. 'it would dull the pain, but it still would hurt like a son of a bitch. At least you wouldn't start crying the second someone touched it' said Walt. Walter touches the arm, Kimson recoils in pain and lets out a quick groan. 'Jesus' said Kimson. 'I don't know about the cast Jim, you'll have to get that cleared with your people. Last time I worked on a professional wrestler the owner of the company refused to let the cast be used. Apparently it's a forgien object or something like that' said Walt. 'I wouldn't want the cast, that'd be to much of a target, someone would try to just rip it off right away.' said Kimson. 'But what's the healing time on this fracture.' said Kimson. 'If you're lucky and don't take to much damage, a couple weeks, it's not a serious break' said Walt. 'Problem there is Jim that you're going to be wrestling, that'll slow down the heeling. We'll have to wrap it up heavily regardless.' he added. 'Yeah, I can deal with that.' said Kimson. Walt looks at Kimson and grins, obviously not all that happy with the situation. Kimson looks at the arm in awe of how bad it looks. 'It looks worse then it feels' said Kimson. 'Jim' said Walt 'What.' said Kimson. 'I'm going to ask you once more. Not as your doctor. But as your friend. Just let it go. You'll have other chances to do this. You're only 28, in your prime. You'll get other chances at this title. It's to much to risk! What if you draw number 1? You'd be screwed, 19 other men all of them wanting you most of all out of the match because your such a favorite.' said Walt. Kimson leans forward, with his free hand he brushes some hair out of his face and looks at Walter 'Walt, it's worth it if I can pull it off. It's worth it even if I go in there and try my best. It'll be worth it, trust me.' said Kimson. 'Okay, fine' said Walt. 'Lets get to work' Walter gets up and reaches for a pen and starts to jot down some items on a piece of paper as the scene fades to play, Kimson smiling, Walter not pleased.
