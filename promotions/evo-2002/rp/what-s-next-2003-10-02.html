What's Next?

2 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-02 '....One year' The voice of Scott Frost is heard. The scene opens up, and Scott Frost is sitting on balcony of his hotel room. You can see in the background his wife Seara sleeping in their bed. Scott stands propped up against the guard rail of the balcony. 'It has been one whole year, and I have accomplished more things than anyone else in eVolution.' Scott says to himself. Scott looks down at the passing cars, and a slight breeze sooths the air. 'In only three months, of my debut. I became the number one babyface wrestler here. I have become the first man to hold the Universal championship twice. The only man to take out every member of Unlimited. But yet. I still feel incomplete.' Scott continues talking to himself. Scott turns his head slightly and then turns it back to his previous position with a grin on his face. 'I've got a beautiful, and caring wife. I've got the world on my shoulders. But still it seems like there could be more weight there.' 'I am one of the many reasons that fans come to the arena's to watch eVolution.' 'Merchandise with my name on it, sells.' 'But still.....I feel, incomplete.' Scott says to himself. Scott lets out a gentle sigh. 'One year.....I've done so much. But i'm ready for more. Digital Destruction....I settle a score that should of been settled long a go.' 'In only a few days. I will be the fastest rising wrestling superstar. But what is next? After Jameson....After doing all of this. After everything...the only thing left is to continue....' Scott pauses. 'Continue, down this road of glory...' Scott says looking deeply into the sky. The scene fades away with Scott looking into the horizon filled with lights from building, streets, and cars.
