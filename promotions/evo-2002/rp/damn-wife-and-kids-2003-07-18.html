Damn wife and kids...

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-18 We find ourselves at the home of Thug roC and his family. The Washington D.C. home is above average. Nothing you’ll find on MTV Cribs, but not a section 8 home either. Thug roC sits in his living room, watching a classic Sean Mason match. He watches Mason contend for the Continental Title against Asis in a hellacious battle when his youngest daughter, Lisa, approaches him. “Dad,” the 8 year old approaches, “When are we leaving for the fair?” Thug roC, with his eyes glued onto the television responds, “In a minute sweetheart, Daddy is doing some homework.” Thug roC continues to watch as Mason is seen being thrown over the top rope and to the outside. Thug roC isn’t just watching though, he is more or less, studying the match. Lisa goes over to her mom, Shannon, and is obviously upset. Shannon then approaches Thug roC as she doesn’t like what she is seeing. “Terrel!” Shannon yells, “When are you going to turn that tv off and take these kids out? They’ve been wanting to go to that stupid fair for 3 days now!” “I always take them places.” Thug roC replies in defense. “Remember when I took them to go see one of the Jurassic Park movies?” “Terrel, that was the original Jurassic Park, which was over ten years ago!” “Well remember when I took them to see that dumb ring movie that they wanted to see?” Shannon crosses her arms, “You mean The Ring? You wanted to see that, not them! And because of you, Lisa wouldn’t answer the telephone for 2 months!” Thug roC chuckles a bit. “Lisa kept saying ‘they’re waiting for me to answer’ every time the phone rang! So Terrel, either you get your black ass up and take those kids out, or….” Thug roC finally takes his eyes off the TV to look up at Shannon. “Or what?” “Or I will tell Lisa what really happened to her hamster.” Shannon then smirks as Thug roC’s face gets serious all of a sudden. “You wouldn’t….” “Just wait until she finds out that Lucky wasn’t so lucky once Daddy decided to play hot potato with him while he was drunk.” Shannon smiles once again. “That’s blackmail!” Thug roC replies. “And you told me that the blood in the garbage disposal was really ketchup…” “Fine, I’ll take the rotten kids. But I won’t like it” Thug roC says, before putting his attention back on the television screen.” Shannon leaves the living room very happily as Thug roC watches Sean Mason competing on tape. “There’s something different about that guy…and I’ll be the one to expose it. So that everyone in the world will realize what kind of a fraud Mason truly is….”
