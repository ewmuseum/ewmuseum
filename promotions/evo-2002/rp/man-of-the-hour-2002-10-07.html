Man of the hour.

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/07/02 The crowd sits patiently, talking amongst themselves as the logo, '~•~' flashes on the titantron. Everyone sits in confusement as they hear, 'Hanz Froyberg; man of the hour.' coming from the tron and an old video of him and his friends wrestling in his backyard years ago. The camera had a date on the lower right side, 'October 1st, 1994.' 'Hanz has always been a fun loving person. Even when he was younger he had lots of friends.' His father said as they showed a shot of Hanz doing his very first Hanzenzimmer. Afterwards pinning his opponent to get the win. 'When I was younger my father was obsessed with me wrestling. At the competition he and I watched all the other competitors in action, studying their flaws, strengths, ability, and their technique. That's where I get my competitiveness, no doubt. And I thank my father for helping me through all these years, teaching me everything I know today.' Said Hanz. The camera then showed Hanz winning his very first national wrestling match in Frankfurt, Germany. 'At age fourteen, I was diagnosed with Diabetes, which reeked havoc on my emotions. For a long time I didn't want to do anything but wrestling. I didn't want to do anything with friends, family, or any religious services. To this day I am still struggling with my religion, wondering if god is real. From that moment on I just worked harder. At school and wrestling. I am still insulin dependant for the time being, but my intake has gone considerably down.' Hanz said as the logo, '~•~' flashed constantly and it showed a clip of Hanz doing the Hanzlich. One in normal mode, then again in slow-motion. Then suddenly, Disturbed hits over the PA system, the audience not sure what they're witnessing. Then four strikes of pyro shot up and Hanz Froyberg was seen in the ring. Everyone sat in their seats, confused, wondering how the hell he got there. The music shut off, and Hanz looked at the audience, grinned sidisticly, then rose the microphone to his mouth. 'You know, I could go on for hours, blabbering about how eVolution is lucky they have me. But that's not my style. No, I'm just here to let you people know that I'm here at this e-federation to entertain you. That is what this business is all about. Pleasing and entertaining the fans. But my question to you people is what is entertaining to you? I mean, you only cheer for those who praise you.. whether or not they put on a good show. As Hanz was about to continue, he notices that there were a few boos in the croud. He grinned again. 'You see? Those who boo me, why do you boo? Those who cheer me, why do you cheer? I'm here not only to wrestle my ass off, not only to put on a kick ass show, but to also find out what you people consider 'entertainment' to be.
