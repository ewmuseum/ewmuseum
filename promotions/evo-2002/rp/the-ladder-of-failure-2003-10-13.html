The ladder of failure

13 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-13 'Why....must I resort to actions normally taken by a desperate man?' Luther Wisdom is seen alone walking in circles. He holds in his hands the plastic and foam replicas of all the Evolution titles in his hands; the ones sold to kids at the concession stands at Evolution events. 'Apparently it seems the only way to hold a title here in Evolution is to place one on my credit card and recieve the HeartBrak Chick promotional calender as a thank you for spending my money on Evolution ShopZone.' He places the titles on a table taking one and holding it up for the camera to see.... 'This is the replica of the EvolutionTag Team Championship; a title I've held on three occassions and destined to do so again. I need to however clear up something concerning my relationship with Malcolm king. He has returned simply as an advisor to myself and Quinn, nothing more. He attacked QBX as a way to say......hello remember me? Under his management I shall be a title holder again. Let's face it this belt is the only one I have a realistic chance of ever holding in Evolution.' he picks up the replica of the Cell Championship..... 'Presently to hold the tag title you must be proficent in cell fighting as well. So if locking me into a cage and having me beat the hell out of another man for your entertainment ; then I'm your man. My goal is to get a fair chance no matter the division and if that means entering the cell and beating everyone to a bloody pulp.....so be it. Not because I feel like I have to, only because I could care less about following what management wants to promote here anymore.' he moves on to the Evolved Title replica....... 'You couldn't pay me enough to wear this belt again! No offense to the division but in all honesty it was an insult to me to wear the belt; an albatross with the promise of greater things. But like all promises it was made to be broken. I get the title and concensus was it had went to shit.....an opinion I would have agreed with if it wasn't made 5 minutes after me being crowed champion. Ohhh but wait Hedges took the title from me and all of a sudden the potential was again realized for the belt....and held during his numerous no shows. Apparently it's on it's way to prestige again......or being made the tool to make sure the holder keeps with the agenda. What agenda you may ask? How would I know I never played well with others.' He then picks up the replicas of the United, Continental, and Universal titles........ 'Now we move onto the belts I can never hold here. I mean in all seriousness how can you hold a belt you are shut out of competing for? Collusion would be the term used in other sports and politics for this practice...and in wrestling what is it called? The United title...a bastardization of the ULWN belt given to Thug Roc. You see Roc I attacked you not because I hate you....hell I could care less about you. The ULWN belt was built up over the years held by some of the most promising talent in this sport....only to be reduced to a prop and handed over to someone management happens to have a special feelings for. That was a slap in my face and all that wrestled for the ULWN. If Evolution was gonna combine the titles at least have the respect to have the ULWN champion in the match and not have him drop the damn thing a week after winning it at the ULWN house. Does Evolution want to continue invalidating eveything so their egos can get over? Give back to the f'n business that has given so much to you!' Wisdom composes himself before continuing......... 'Do we even know who the Continental champion is? Or better question does anyone even care? To be the second highest title in Evolution it is constantly overshadowed by the wars waged for the United, Tag, and Cell as well as the Evolved titles. It has become a non factor but then again it's also a tool used by management to promote who they want. Well I'm happy to say I don't need that belt as it seems that taking charge of the situation is something I've grown accustomed to doing.......' holds the Universal title replica before strapping it around my waist...... 'Tyler Davidson you and Evolution management need to answer a few questions for me. Why haven't I main evented a Legends since the seventh one? Why is it I have seen men whose careers in evolution I help jumpstart....surpass me and fail on the ladder of contendership to the Universal title? Why must I constantly watch as new guy after new guy gets push after undeserved push and then hear the whinning of we don't have contenders for title X? Well Evolution, Davidson I am sick of it! I've been here since day one and have done all asked of me and for what? To be shut out....that's my reward. Well Tyler come Legends I have booked time on FU-TV, yea I hate Tok'n but it seems like the only forum to address this topic with any unbiaseness, I want you to tell me to my face why I've been treated like a bastard son to this federation. Don't offer me a chance to climb the ladder either....I've seen it in action and it only leads to failure. No Tyler I want you to tell me man to man why........before I take matters into my own hands and circumvent the system.' (fade to black)
