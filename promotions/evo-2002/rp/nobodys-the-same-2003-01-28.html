Nobodys the Same...

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::Scene Opens:: Sitting backstage prior to Legends, Sean Mason looks a man full of thought. Having faced Seara for a third time and this time lost, these past few weeks had been a time of reflection for the youngster. When Sean debuted in FAW all of 24 months ago, many doubted his credentials. As a newcomer to the industry its understandable that the old guard should be both apprehensive and weary. Of those people who enter as freshers into a promotion, the chances of success are less than slim. For every person that succeeds in this business, there are another ten who now find themselves on the dole and out of work. Wrestling in reality is a dream for so many, but the challenge of turning a dream into something much is a test too much. At the age of 26 and with those 24 testing months to recall and draw upon, few could doubt he has made the grade in wrestling, he has survived the many cuts and changes in rosters. In Evolution alone, Sean was now a past champion; a man worthy of a degree of respect. So what next?! What should be the next aspiration of 'The Prodigal One'. Simple. Survival and consolidation had both been achieved, now was the time for Sean to attempt to push on and strive onward. Now was the time for the Sean Mason legacy to enter its growth phase and what better way than by capturing yet more highly sought after gold. After all titles are the springboards wrestlers use and why shouldn't they?! No matter what language, no matter what setting; everyone knows the value of gold.. Everyone knows the bearer holds the power and nobody disputes that fact. So to answer the previous question of what's next, well its simple, more gold; but first Sean must win the trophy that is Asis. 'Nobodys the same' Sean began. 'Not a single soul in this world of millions of people is an exact replica of somebody else however similar they may seem. Sure comparisons are drawn, but the truth is that comparisons are a very vague tool of decribing someone to another. In this day and age, and with this belief in my mind its not hard to believe that I find much to criticise in such general descriptions.' Reaching into his back pocket Sean pulls out what looks to be a small booklet. As the camera moves in closer, it appears that the booklet is infact the program for the forthcoming Legends. Opening the book to the first page we find a card schedule. There for all to see is Seans name, pitting him against none other than Asis. Rolling the program up, he rests his chin upon the tip of the program and ponders further. Suddenly a huge grin emerges on the face of Sean. Although Asis had caused him much heartache of late, the sheer thought of last weeks Legends and the message he sent to Asis that night brings a degree of comfort regardless. Chosing to reflect on that memory Sean continues, 'Legends just gone was quite a night. Although Revenge is often seen as a failing of men, for me it provided the perfect motivation to answer my critics and determine my path for the coming weeks. Retribution was sought last week and it was achieved. Now I am looking forward to a match which attempts to settle some issues, a match which offers me the opportunity to fufill yet more dreams and all because of this dreaded revenge people speak so badly of' Sean said pausing. 'So whats next Asis?! Up until now the battle has been fought somewhat an im-moral standing, are your words of late really based on your true feelings; or just a provado you've chosen to showcase?! Up until now, its been almost startling the way you have chosen to continually tar me with the same brush as that of your previous rivals. What makes you feel that just?! I was thinking about this of late, thinking about just how you were able to come to such conclusions when suddenly my mind presented another explanation. Could it be that you are un-nerved?! Would you rather believe that I am just another clone of one of your past conquests, rather than seeing me for what I actually am?! Asis, incase you hav'nt realised my name is Sean Mason. I'm not Strictly Prohibited and im not Takura!' 'Having said all that I did then hear your oh so brave and valiant words at the end of your commanding speech. Interesting,' Sean said raising an eyebrow. Dropping the programme to the floor Sean smiles, before looking up at the camera once again. 'At New Years Evolution, you were correct in saying that your not scheduled to face BDD. Now ask yourself, why is that? While you ponder that, let me put this to you. Have you heard the phrase 'threes a charm'? Takura, SP, BDD; theres your three. Notice however, I'm not in those three though, the reason for that being is your wrong, we aren't the same. I will give all those people their dues, but by no means are they Sean Mason and while I can't safeguard their safety and victory against you! I can safeguard my own! During Legends, I took the time to do a little bit of scouting and why shouldn't I?! With a notepad and pen I then proceeded to do the necessary scouting. You were afraid of me then and you were right to be. Asis why change a trade of thought? On Thursday the start and end to the story are going to be exactly the same, only this time your tackling the complex face on and when paranoia sets in, theres only going to be one winner. Step forward Sean Mason' Sean says with a huge grin on his face. Turning away from the camera its apparent Sean has little anxiety going into this match. Recognising and controlling the threat is one thing though, on Thursday it will be time for Sean to provide the necessary execution. ::Scene Closes::
