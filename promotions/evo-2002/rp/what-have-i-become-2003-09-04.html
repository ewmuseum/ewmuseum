What have I become..

4 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-04 The scene opens in a small room. Inside is Jason Relms.He is in the middle of all kinds of trophies and awards that he has obtained through out his whole career. He goes down the list from being the first ever AWF Universal Champion, to being half eVo tag champ. He just looks at them with no emotion on his face. He seems to be not even moved about all these awards. This is the first time in a long time we have seen him by himself and away from his Tag Partner Perry Quinn. Relms takes a second after looking away from his old memories as he says.. 'All this..All of these accomplishments.But yet I feel nothing.' He goes to look once again at his trohpies. But he quickly stops himself as he shakes his head as if he doesn't want to let himself dwell on the past.He then says.. 'People say the past make the man. But my question is, IF a man has no past, then what is he?You can not stand in front and deny he is nothing, as he stands in front of you. But sure you say he has had to have past. But like everything in life, things can be erased. Sure after it's gone there could be scars because of it's removal, but the fact remains, it's gone.' He then looks down at the ground thinking about his greatest matches. Thinking about the people like High Impact, who have been with him through his wrestling career, and who have left him. He begins to say.. 'This sport is more than just wrestling. This sport is more than just entertainment. It is a form of life. It is not what you do,it is what you live. No way around it, this is your dream, when you make it,you see real quick it becomes more than just a part of you, it basically becomes you. Why though, why do we let things like this happen?Everything can be raised in a question. An sure, everyone will have a answer, but what you do is no more than your own action. Unless you let yourself be controled by some one else, by a manageman of sorts, like I have let myself do for so long. But that to is my choice. As I could of walked away, I could of taken different paths. But the time to were I look up to someone is over. The time that I carry some one on my back, that too is over. Its simply a new start..I am simply eraseing it all..Do you see?No,all you see are the scars left behind..' The scene fades to black as Relms back is faced towards all of his accomplishments ever in his wrestling career as it then cutts to a commercial. A Poem Can you kill.. if it never lived.. can you destory.. what has never prospered.. can you take.. what has never been.. can you dream.. if you never sleep.. can you win.. if you never loose.. can you be something.. and then be forgotten.. why.. what... how...-Jason Relms
