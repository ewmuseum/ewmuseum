7 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.7.03 'I ain't scared to go to jail!' In the middle of some inspirational speech to boost his 'thug self-esteem'. 'I ain't scared of gettin' beat by da popos! Shit, they can lock me up in jail!' This almost to his self, 'I ain't afraid of takin' a shower with big men and watching the water drip down the crack of they ass, then you just want ride it all ni-' Realizing everyone is looking at him disgusted, he tried to come back to redeem himself, 'Ya'll kno' wut I mean.' 'No' Together, everyone shook their head while still looking at him disgusted. 'Wutever, letz get up outa diz joint!' Like 'follow-the-leader' everyone got up the same time he did. I was going to say something but after unmeaning fully ease dropping on what he said, I wasn't even going to bother. | I couldn't get my game straight. It was almost like I was pinion in guilt, but to get my mind off of this, my dogg Duzza and I chilled in the house playing cards. | 'Yo, how da fuck you gon' take dat hand like dat? I put a fuckin' spade down, u had a damn diamond. Tell me how da fuck you won dat hand? Cuz I'd like to hear diz shit.' I awaited Duzza's answer at which will justify his volition of action. 'See, what had happened was, my vision kind of fucked up.' Duzza looked over at a nigga next to the ice box. 'Yo playa, pass the Courvoisier' Trying to get more drunk than he already is. 'Stop drinkin' and maybe yo ass will see str8. Damn.' I drunk down a 40 between smoking Mari J. 'I'm high as fuck... DAMN!' 'What?' 'How you get so far away Duzz?' I was freaking out as I took another hit of my Mari J. 'Yo Duzza. I didn't kno' yo ass had a twin.' 'Wha?' Duzza busted out laughing. 'Yo ass needs to stop smoking that shit.' *continues to laugh* 'And you sayin' I need to stop drinking, man fuck you.' Duzza placed the whole deck on the table. 'I win.' 'Yo Duzza, why yo ass put a bird on da table?' With the Mari J fucking up my head, I began to trip out seeing things that aren't quite there. 'Why am I da only nigga 'n diz room dat got a problem with this bird Duzza jus' put on da damn table?' I Tried to quell the illusion at which was unfathomable to me; as to why no one informed me Duzza became a magician and have no problem with the bird on the table. | The laughing seemed to play a 'sing-a-long' tone in my head as I was so high I begun to laugh too not knowing what I was exactly laughing at. | 'I'm serious.' | [I must have been the clown that day because everything I said made the crowd of people laugh harder and harder. Through everyone who seemed to be far, blurry, and have twins that day, one person caught my eye; Darnel. Fidgeting with his hands uneasily, he sat there just watching with a faceless expression. Almost like something straight out of 'The Twilight zone' as I turned away to take another hit only to find Darnel missing after soon returning my bloodshot eyes in that direction again. It really didn't faze me to much because I didn't think anything of it. I just fixed my red Phat Albert shirt and pulled another blunt from my black baggy Phat Albert jeans and got my mind back on the party.] | 'When yo ass gon' put dat damn bird away?!' | [By this time I wasn't even playing, but the laughter grew once more and I continued to stay in a state of perplexity. Smoke reigned in clouds which hovered through the house with the kitchen being the producer of the smoke. The kitchen was very small if I remember straight, or there were just too many black folks in it, I forget. You know how black folks like to throw a party in a small ass place... Anyways, while everyone kept laughing, I got a phone call from Buuday...] | ***The phone conversation*** 7:59pm 'AJ!' 'What?' 'Oh lord... You won't believe wut happen'd to uz.' By the deep ghetto/Ebonics ascent, immediately I knew it was Buuday. 'Diz iz wut happen'd, Sheeca and I wuure in the jewelry sto' lookin' 'round fo' sum uar(ear)-rings or sumn' like dat when ol' bitch at da front desk startin' actin' a damn foo!' 'She started actin' a damn foo?' Tripping off how the front clerk tried to get crunk with the ghettoest girls to step foot out the projects, I asked quite amused. 'A motha fuckin' damn foo!' 'Like how?' 'Ok diz iz how it went down. I wuz lookin' at sum uar-rings dat wuure real expensive riight, and den all of a sudden, da bitch quickly approaches and says they cost too much! I wuz like, 'BITCH, YOU DON'T KNO' ME! BETTA GET UP OFF ME FO' I JUMP ON YO BITCH ASS!' Den she gon' tell me to calm down fo' she getz da police. Lil' Thug bursts out laughing 'Yo, where wuz Sheeca durin' all diz shit?' 'Wait, I'ma get there.' Buuday reminisced, 'After I cussed dat bitch out, people began to gather 'round and she started playin' it like it wuz crazy black lady attackin' innocent white lady and I wuzn't haven't it. So I continued cursin' her ass out till da police came and told me to leave the sto'. Datz when Sheeca came 'n. It wuz so funny as she came walkin' 'n to see wut wuz goin' on. The look on her face when she saw me wuz like... well actually, come to think 'bout it. She wuzn't surprised.' 'Yea, ok, then wut went down?' 'Well Sheeca stated she wuz a lawyer and wanted to kno' wut wuz happenin'. We explained da whole story to her, and then Sheeca did her lawyer skills on the officer to get me free when all of a sudden da white lady turned into Ms. Psycho bitch! Wait a sec. AJ-' | I listened on the other line as the real action threw down. Throughout the commotion and yelling I made out Sheeca beating down on the white lady. Buuday, who was still holding the phone in her hand, screaming, 'WHOOP HER MOTHA FUCKIN' ASS SHEECA!' and 'MAKE SURE DA BITCH WALKS AWAY WIT NO TEETH!' shortly followed by 'DAMN! DON'T KILL DA BITCH! Said like a true black person, cause you know how we like to scream shit in a act of provocation when a fight is on the break of the horizon. Black people are like guns, we kill without sorrow... Metaphorically speaking that is... Anyways, Buuday picked back up the phone.] | 'THUG! THUG! THUG! OH MY FUCKIN' GOODNESS! OH MY FUCKIN' GOODNESS!' Buuday still being a cheer leader for Sheeca who continued to whoop the ladies ass. 'THUG!' 'UH?' 'SHEECA JUMPED ON DA HO AND IZ WHOOPIN' HER MOTHA FUCKIN' ASS AS I SPEAK!' 'Damn... I knew Sheeca wuz ghetto as hell. Jordan X told me, 'Ghetto name, ghetto chick.' But I didn't believe his ass. Damn... No one pulled Sheeca off of her yet?' 'Hell no! They to fuckin' scuured!' Buuday laughed 'Yo Thug, I gotta go!' 'Wait! I wanna hear diz shit!' 'Not today! I jus' heard a cop sayin' they 'bout to bring 'n da S.W.A.T. team! Shit, I'ma roll up outa diz bitch fo' I catch a cap 'n my ass! Two Fingaz at a playa, bye!' Buuday quickly hung up the phone before yelling, 'SHEECA! BRING YO STUPID ASS ON! THEY BOUT TO CAP DIZ PLACE UP! You kno' how dem pigs don't give swash shit bout up black folks!' | Laughing, I hung up the phone. Darnel was doing crack, and... It hit me, today was the day. I knew what I had to do; lights, camara, action. | -Today {March, 07, 2003} | The wooden ceiling fan circled slowly over my drugged body as I could see music in the air. I must have been pretty fucked up cause I started to hallucinate too. It's amazing what ecstasy does to a body. You get fucked up, start seeing music, and seem as if you were in a world of your own having a great ass time. I brung the weed to my lips once more, puffing on it before watching smoke ooze out from my mouth in black intoxicating clouds. The sun beat in from the window making lines of neon-light across my brown intimidating body with 'Piru' etched out on my chest. Buuday and Sheeca were lord knows where, I could give a fuck at the time. Beep...Beep...Beep... | 'Fuck-you-PCW' I whispered to myself in an auspicious tone for a new start in eVo. My alarm clock soon went off. It was all said and done. A grin grew across my face as the eVo contract laid beside me fully signed with exclamation marks. I smirked adding a slight laugh at the end, 'Fuck-you-PCW...'
