1 November 2002

Date: 1/11/02 With his mind clear and his anger all but gone, he had felt like he was on top of the world. Everything that had made him depressed was a thing of the past. Jason knew now that if he was going to spend the rest of his life in happiness, he was going to have to leave all of that behind him, back in the graves where they belonged. But now, he wasn’t sure that he was the kind of man who could just forget about everything that had happened to him. His memory was playing games with him, tossing his soul around like a disregarded house in a hurricane. His question was would this little hurl through the sky give him a safe landing, or would he just be broken apart into pieces of rubble, disastrous reminders of a home that was no more. But none the less, Jason walked into a fancy restaurant, his face expressing happiness that had evaded him for almost four years. The waiter led him to a nice table in the corner, and handed him a menu for his meal. Jason thanked the man, and asked him for a few seconds to decide. The waiter nodded, and quickly trotted away, helping out a couple on the table next to his. Jason looked down at the pages of the menu. Flipping through them, his appetite grew in anticipation. Pictures of steaks and shrimps and soups and other meals filled the pages, along with their respectful orders. His eyes scanned each name, trying to decide which one to choose, and enjoying himself doing so. Life was good again. He could do something so ordinary, and still feel the muscles on his face working to keep his smile alive. A short laugh escaped his lungs, and his heart rose out of its darkened corner and into his glowing soul. Sound’s like everything is going great for you, Jason. Jason heard the voice of a man ring in his ear, and felt his smile disappear. His eyes closed, and his heart slipped back down into darkness. Jason’s mind wandered the halls of hatred again, and he could see a figure leaning against a wall, his eyes shining with a mixture of blue and red. “Hello Jay Jay.” Hello my good friend. How long as it been? “Not long enough, Jay. My life would have been a lot better if I never had to hear your voice…” Oh you know that you couldn’t live your life without hearing from me again. I am you, Jason. You know that. You’re the reason that I exist. Don’t forget that. “How could I? Every time I hear you, you seem to remember to remind me of just that.” That’s because you always make it look like it’s my fault. It’s not. But whatever. The past is the past, and I’m not about to go digging it up for you to get a good whiff of it. I just wanted to say something. Give you a piece of my mind. Let you know how I think things stand. “As far as I can tell, everything is all right. Nothing is wrong with me right now.” Jesus, Jason. Don’t you even remember Nightmare? You were killing him Jason. Killing him. He didn’t stand a chance against you. You had a monstrous lead over that son of a bitch. And then bam. All of a sudden he gets all those falls, and you find yourself at a disadvantage. I mean, I’m sitting there, watching you take move after move, pin after pin. I wanted to jump out of my seat and smack some sense into you. But I just stayed silent and watched. Almost cried when I saw you not pin him after that move. God damn, Jason, it wasn’t pretty. “Well it’s behind me now, Jay. I don’t care about that anymore. I was just a title. Nothing more. Shodan got his little insignificant moment of victory, and I had the pleasure of knowing that I came that close to taking away everything that he had.” But that’s just it, Jason. You didn’t. You almost did, but you didn’t. Don’t you remember one of the last things I said to you? I would be watching you, and making sure that you didn’t fail me. I can take this little iron man match as a failure. “Then go ahead. What can I do about it? I can’t go back and win, Jay. I gave it my all, and it just wasn’t enough to get the job done. What the hell do you want from me?” I want you to be the man that you were shaping to be when I came around. You remember that man? The one that didn’t have a problem with taking a man’s life and dangling it off the edge of existence? “Yeah I remember him Jay. And I am so ashamed to say that he was apart of me.” Why do you say that Jason? Wasn’t he good to you? Wasn’t I good to you? “You know, Jay, I was just starting to be happy again. My life was just beginning to make sense again. I don’t need any of this. I have left him behind, and I don’t think I will ever see him again.” That’s just the thing, Jason. It seems that you started something with this asshole. Sovereign, I think it was. “Did you see what he was doing to Impact? No man disserves to be beaten like that. No man. I don’t care what the hell he…” No man, Jason? What about the man who raped her, Jason? Was he just an exception to the rule? “That’s different. She didn’t do anything to him, and he attacked her and violated her. I would never allow anyone to hurt her like that. Never.” So you think that becoming this pussy whipped version of yourself that you will be able to get her back into your arms? Is that what this is about, Jason? The same girl that you left behind? The one who you didn’t think was good enough to choose over me? “Marilee is worth more to me then you ever will be Jay. You are nothing to me. Not anymore. Maybe before you meant something to me, but now you are just a part of everything that I have thrown away. She is more then I ever disserved, and I’m not going to let you take her from me.” Don’t worry, Jason. I don’t want to take her from you. In fact, the thought hadn’t even crossed my mind. All I want from you is to show this world that you are better then them. Now, I will allow you to carry this little change for as long as you think it will help you, but I don’t want it to make you weak. On the contrary, I want it to make you stronger then before. “Then what do you want me to do?” Make sure that they learn that you are their superior. “How do you want me to prove that?” Glad you asked. I want you to show Sovereign just what you are. I want you to demonstrate just what Jason Jameson does when he is angered. I just want you not to fail me with Sovereign the way you failed me with Shodan. And with Scott Frost. And with everyone else you have decided would beat you. “And what if I lose, Jay? What then?” I don’t know Jason. I just don’t know anymore. But I’ll tell you this. My patience is running thin. I don’t know how much longer I can hold back. “Would you like me to take your order now, sir?” The voice opened Jason’s eyes, and he stared forward into the man’s eyes. The man looked a little confused, but not scared. The smile on his face was proof of that. “Umm. Can you give me a few more minutes?” Jason responded. The waiter gave Jason a little nod, and then walked away. The menu wasn’t important right now. Jason closed his eyes, and stared forward into the gloomy images of his mind. He listened to the air, waiting for a voice to intrude, but there was no sound. Just an empty space, with the echo of nothingness running through. Jason opened his eyes, and realized that the smile was no longer there.
