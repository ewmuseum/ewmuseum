Date: 2003-09-16 ~He lifted as hard as he could this was his time, he had to be ready for anything. He curled the weight again and this time you could see veins bulging…he went down again and lifted. It was his time off and he was doing this shit? Shocka knew what he had to do to get into the proper shape.~ ‘Come on shocks.’ Vito could be heard saying over the music Shocka was focusing on. ‘Shut up Vito.’ Shocka demanded. ‘Calm down buddy.’ Vito backed off. ‘You don’t get it do you?’ Shocka asked him and he dropped the weight. ~Shocka had, had enough indeed and he was ready to show Voss what he was about. This man was a fucking leech and he could not be dropped…sure he could come in handy somehow but not right now. Shocka doesn’t need his help to train, look at him for christ’s sake I think he can handle that task on his own.~ ‘Meet me at the trail at 2.’ Shocka’s voice was dry and bitter. ~Voss nodded and walked out of the gym he knew that Shocka needed this time to himself. Or perhaps he finally got the hint that he was not wanted around? Doubtful…he is pretty thick headed…he still thinks he has Shocka under his finger. How wrong could he be? Shocka is finally back on track…it was proven with Jason Jameson last week…you saw it didn’t you? #1 Stunna and it was over…just like always. But all of you people were surprised weren’t you? You didn’t know Shocka had that in him anymore…well you are about to find out that he has that…and then some.~ ~Shocka actually liked being over sea’s now, because now when he went on a jog it was to new to him. The landscape was beautiful the towns…he had the chance to enduldge this week…for once he gets to relax. But he will do nothing of the sort…whoever comes out of Legends 50 the Universal champion has one hell of a challenge at Digital Destruction.~ ‘So you wanna know who I want to face?’ Shocka looked at Voss trying to see what he was hinting at. ‘Yeah…who would you rather take on for the Universal title..’ ‘You know what…It really doesn’t matter who it is…but I have feeling I’m going to be the next multi time Universal champion in eVolution.’Shocka said with a sly smile ~FADE~
