Blinding Insanity... part two.

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Continued from Blinding Insanity, The scene opens back up amidst the wicked snowstorm. Morpheus standing covered in layer upon layer of snow, still wiping his face off to realize if what was happening was real or if this was just a hallucination from the freezing, cold wind that’s blowing right through him and the frost bite that’s slowly starting to spread across his body. Morpheus trudges up through the snow only to find none other than Thug Roc himself standing beside his limousine. Morpheus doesn’t look pleased at all. Thug Roc yells to Morpheus loudly 'Hey Omega… Looks like you have found yourself in quite the tough spot. I think you should get in this limousine because my work with you is far from done and over' The words carry through the wind away into nothing. Morpheus stands contemplating if he should chance the AWOL through the snow, trying to escape into the impossible to find warmth and freedom. Morpheus slowly turns from Thug Roc and stops half way because immediately Thug Roc starts yelling loudly. 'Morpheus I swear if you turn around and take the wrong destined path here, now and today and you decide to turn your back on your new family, myself and New-TV then I swear your life is over. I promise that you will be never more in this life that you thought you had all perfect control over. You need to wake the fuck up and realize that you cant move out of check without anywhere to move, or no block or sacrifice to save yourself. This is the last time I tell you Morpheus, turn around and get in the limousine now!' The words echo through the wind. The wind blowing rapidly, both men difficult to see from the piling snow. Morpheus turns around slowly back towards Thug Roc, he has a strange grin across his face. Morpheus trudges through the snow towards Thug Roc in the deep snow. Morpheus stands towering over Thug Roc and speaks as if him and Roc had finally connected in mutual agreement, with a tone of voice that would be spoken between friends and family 'Thug Roc, you must really care about or need my services to be hunting me through this snow storm after you were thrown in jail over those alleged charges. I will get in the limousine on the one condition that you tell me exactly what you want from me and be straight the fuck up with me and I will do the exact same with you!' Morpheus nodding and wiping the snow from his face and eyes once again. Thug Roc smiles and replies 'Then I think we have ourselves a deal Morpheus. Its nice when people can look at things from equal stand points' Both men with strange grins on their faces and both men with their expressions mystery and not read. Morpheus blocks all sight of Thug Roc and ducks down and enters the limousine. Thug Rocs expression on his face goes from the strange grin down to a dropping sick grin like what he had spoke about to Omega was all lies and a huge charade only to trick Morpheus into what ever he felt he needed to, like he’s playing Omega for the biggest chump ever imagined. Thug Rocs look fades from his face as he enters the limousine and slams the door shut behind him The scene fades to inside where Thug Roc is wiping his face and hair off with a towel and Morpheus is peeling the soaked, frozen solid leather coat from his back and throws it on the floor of Thug Roc's Limousine without care or respect. Morpheus is wearing a black 'Dare 2 Dream' t-shirt underneath with black jeans as well. Morpheus runs his hands through his wet hair trying to get all the snow from his head and his wet t-shirt sticks to his massive, cut upper body. Morpheus cracks his neck and breathes deeply into the warm air. Thug Roc sits on the other side of the limousine staring at Morpheus with a blank expression on his face, almost like he’s lost daydreaming about something. Slowly, an evil grin on his face starts to etch and Morpheus looks up and he snaps out of it. Thug Roc speaks first 'Sunday Night you slaughtered the Rising Star Champion Shawn Mason. I told him he would feel the wrath and that’s what he felt!' Morpheus replies to Thug Roc, 'What do you want from me in Evolution? You have to have some kind of plot if you supposedly followed me and found evidence to bring me down hard. Black mailed me into your dirty work at Rise of the Legends, then pursued to hunt me down in a blizzard but for what?' Morpheus looks like he’s somewhat lost at mind and shakes his head side to side Thug Roc continues on speaking to Morpheus 'Rise of the legends which man did you see compete in four matches? Which man in Evo have you had past with before and once became deadliest stable in one single federation awarded to you. You see Morpheus. I know your past with Shocka and about the both of you very well. Like it or not Morpheus, you will become part of New-TV with myself and Shocka having all control over you' Thug Roc breaks out into sick laughter Morpheus quickly cuts him off with a strange but calm eased kind of voice, 'Why not work with me instead of thinking you can control me and then ending up face down in a puddle of your own blood in the near future to come? If you think about it, it sounds more logical! Just remember, I will never forget this past week and the bullshit you have caused me but at the same time you have brought me back into the game. The mind games and physical war that I once dominated in my unholy reign. Roc your on the fine line of either being of the most wicked ally and having your face caved in like stomping on a watermelon. A very fine line!' Morpheus stares with that mean look of his into the eyes of Thug Roc. Thug Roc hesitates from speaking like he’s thinking of his next words carefully all like both men are playing acts. Both men not be trusted or believed because these men are not the typical peachy keen kind of contenders. Both of these men are lost in deep confused games of the mind that will continue on until one man over rules through insanity and downfalls upon the other. Thug Roc finally looks up and simply smiles. Thug Roc speaks like he’s changing the subject of discussion quickly 'Anyways you have been signed in your debut match with Ryan Flesco. This will be the night where Morpheus returns to himself and gains all recognition he’s always been given. Where the fans will hate every move you make, when you destroy and decimate Flesco like you owned and controlled Mason last week at Rise of the Legends. Thursday night on legends, New-TV will walk through with no troubles or worries in the world. We will steal the SHOW!' Thug Roc laughs all disturbed like. Morpheus replies to Roc 'Thursday Night you have me debuting against Ryan Flesco? Turn of events putting me back into the industry that I brought federation after federation to the ground. You have me facing someone when you very well know I could black out at any god forsaken time and end they’re lives. You really don’t know what you have got yourself into Roc!' Thug Roc smiles while nodding, he replies 'I know exactly what I have gotten myself into and New-TV like I said will steal the SHOW! Trust me and for some reason you think you can bluff your match and think you can got off that easy you will be only making things worst for yourself. If you pull a screw over on New-TV ever, I swear you will be locked up quicker then you can unlace your wrestling boots. You understand me Morpheus? The balls in my court now...' Morpheus doesn’t reply but only smiles almost like he’s staring right through Thug Roc and could care less of what he’s speaking about. Two men that are buying into each others games and only thinking and believing what each of them want to. Living in the ignorance of not realizing about the others thoughts. Both men with building steam and tension, with Legends only not even a day away who knows if Thug Roc and Morpheus will even make it to legends. The scene ends with both men staring each other down with sick looks on their faces... Morpheus has triumphed over Shawn Mason taking him out quickly and without chance of retaliation. Now moving on to debuting against Ryan Flesco just another poor individual that will feel the wrath of Omega even if the Infinite doesn’t feel its necessary. This situation with Morpheus, Thug Roc and New-TV just keeps getting uglier. This eventually will explode into mass confusion. Was black mailing Morpheus the biggest mistake of Roc's career or the smartest, intelligent plot of any before his time? We will have to wait and see...
