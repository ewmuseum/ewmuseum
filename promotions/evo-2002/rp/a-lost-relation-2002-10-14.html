A Lost Relation.

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/14/02 Jason Steele sits in his living room watching TV as he watches MTV the doorbell rings. He gets up and answers the door. He looks and its Jennifer Carter wanting another interview. Jason Steele: It’s late, and you want an interview? Jennifer Carter: All I want is a few statements about you. Jason Steele: I already told you everything you need to know, now leave my house. Jennifer Carter: I was thinking we could go grab a cup of coffee or maybe even a beer. Jason Steele: I could use a beer but I am not driving. Jennifer Carter: That’s ok; I will make the camera man do the driving. Jason Steele: Sounds like a plan to me. You can ask me a few question on the way there if you want. Jennifer Carter: Ok. They get in the car and drive to a local bar on the way there Carter asks Steele a few questions. Jennifer Carter: Now Jason, we have heard you have a brother that has spent some time living with your real father. Is this statement true? Jason Steele: Yes, I do have a brother that lives with my father... but I haven't seen either one of the two for almost five years. Jennifer Carter: Well at eVolution we thought you might want to know that we had a young man enter our offices by the name of Marcus Steele, and he claimed to be your younger brother from Calgary. Jason Steele: Really? Jennifer Carter: Yes, this is true. Your younger brother has found you as he is looking for a place to call home in the world of wrestling as well. He told Davidson that he wanted you to be his tag partner sometime in the near future. Jason Steele: Awesome, is there anyways I could see him? Jennifer Carter: As a matter a fact there is, look behind you at the table there is a man with short spiked hair. You should recognize him in a slight way. Jason Steele: Yeah that’s him from what I can remember. Jennifer Carter: Well go over and talk to him. Jason Steele: Ok. Jason Steele gets up and walks over to Marcus and begins to talk with him. Marcus Steele: Long time to no see bro. Jason Steele: Yeah, tell me about it. Marcus Steele: Looks like you got your self a home in the wrestling world. Jason Steele: Yeah, first match this week. Marcus Steele: Yeah I know I am going to be there. Jason Steele: You get tickets? Marcus Steele: I guess you could say that. Jason Steele: Were you signed or something? Marcus Steele: Yeah actually I was. Jason Steele: We going to start our old tag team again? Marcus Steele: I don't see why not. Jason Steele: I guess we could do that after I win the tag titles. Marcus Steele: You really think your going to win those belts? Jason Steele: Of course, I mean yeah they got me facing these to veterans with another rookie but I still think I can take those belts. Shocka and Quasar have nothing on me. I mean look, I have what it takes. I proved that back in Canada doing the Indy promotions. Do you know how many shows I sold out and how many matches I won? Marcus Steele: You helped sell out five shows, and if I am not mistaken those were the five shows you guys did for free. And as far as the amount of matches you won, you did win quite a few but you got to remember, most of those you won due to using chairs and did I forget your woman bashing people with lead pipes. Jason Steele: Shah.... They might hear you. Marcus Steele: Ok, look you got talent but your just going to get your ass handed to you if you keep thinking like you do. Jason Steele: Listen up little bro, I now understand why Mom let Dad take your ass away and that is because you’re just like him. You two are both two assholes that think you know what it takes to make it in any form of a career. Guess what brother. Here in eVolution I hope we meet sometime so I can show you want a real ass kicking is all about. Jason stands up and walks away back to the bar with Jennifer Carter. Jennifer Carter: What’s the matter? You don't seem too happy. Jason Steele: I am not happy. Yeah I am glad I got to see my brother but I now know why our mom got rid of him. He is just like our father. But enough about that. This Wednesday is the chance of a lifetime. Many had to try out to get on this roster but the owner of this place saw potential. Now its time I show him why he gave me the spot on this talented roster. Shocka has nothing to prove and Quasar has all his dignity to lose. Right now, Aaron needs to know that if he fucks up it will be his ass that gets the beating of a lifetime. Forget everyone else, I have worked my ass off for a chance like this and if anyone, I mean anyone fucks it up it better well be me and me alone. If one person costs me this match they will pay the price that I feel is equal to my pain. He may be my tag partner for this tourney, but being on my side does nothing when you stab me in the back. Shocka and Quasar look out because there will be some sparks flying when Steele meets skin. We are in it to win it and your here to be beaten so eat your Wheaties boys because you two Americans are going down for the count. Jennifer Carter: Anything else? Before he could finish hearing the question, Steele stands up lays some cash on the bar top and leaves the bar. Steele begins to walk home. As he starts walking Jennifer runs out and yells for him but Steele just ignores her as he walks away. While walking we hear Steele talking to himself. Jason Steele: Why did I even talk to him? Why lord why? Am I supposed to finish raising this boy so he can become a true man or am I stuck sitting here with a thumb up my ass waiting for him to change? As the saying goes; only time will tell. As I walk I can't keep my mind on the straight pace knowing that in two days I must stand in the ring with a man I know nothing about, a man I must learn to trust. I have never been so unhappy. I must make this man gain my trust, but how, and where will we find the time before Wednesday. The ideas are left un told but sooner or later the time will come when the man I stand in the ring with will already have my trust previous to the match starting. Will I find the will in my heart to trust this man or will I be forced to let the feelings of an un trust worthy partner burn inside as the team that has never been known gets torn to shreds. I want gold around my waist more than anything in this world but can I do it with a man I know nothing about? Will the stipulation work to my advantage to where I work with a man that I know has logical experience in this business or will I be left alone with a stranger that I can not, could not, and possibly never will trust? Could this battle turn into an everlasting friendship or will it become a bitter hatred? Nobody knows and nobody cares. The one thing I care about is how I am going to make it home and get some rest. Just then a truck pulls up, its Jennifer Carter and her camera man. They tell Steele to get in the car as it begins to rain. They drive him home and he enters the house. Jennifer and the cameraman drive off as the scene fades to black
