History of One :: Chapter IV

6 January 2003

Date: 1/6/03 We talked for hours. I even missed supper to talk to her. It wasn't about anything important either, half the time it was just nothing. Shooting the shit as they say now.. We only hung up because her parents needed the phone.. My parents probably didn't even know I was on it. They never made any calls.. We never got any calls either.. Once I put the phone back on the hook, I just fell back into the bed and closed my eyes. I tried picturing Heather in my head but it wasn't working.. She was too beautiful for my mind, too perfect for me to even imagine. It was only her why I kept going to school. As soon as I was sixteen I was ready to quit and leave.. I didn't wanna be around these parts anymore. I wanted nothing to do with Asbury.. I hated it everything about it.. I never understood how someone such as Heather Lexington could come out of a place like Asbury was. Sure, I guess I was fooled. It was nice on the outside, but deep down everything sucked.. For me at least.. Myself and Heather never got anywhere.. I did lose my virginity to her though, only to find out a few days later that she had a boyfriend that she never spoke of.. I wanted to kill him.. I wanted to collapse his face around his skull. I don't think anyone has ever felt quite like I did at the moment. I wanted to bury her right next to time when I found out.. The day I found out, was the day I was gone.. That was the day I said screw it and did leave.. I didn't even pack.. What I had on my back was it.. I waited for everyone to sleep, and out the door I went.. I didn't know where I was going, I didn't care either.. I never could explain my rage for living in the 'perfect' household. Just something about it irked me and I wanted nothing to do with it. Rarely I regret leaving.. Rarely. I had no idea where I was heading. I just started walking, I wasn't paying much attention to the direction, all I knew it was out of Asbury and no one was around to stop me. I don't see why anyone tried either. Not even my parents.. Maybe they saw it coming? Maybe they just didn't care. I'd put my money on not caring. I slept on the side of the road that night.. And the night after.. I didn't bother trying to hitch a ride.. I knew nobody would bother.. It's not like I was some huge psycho killer either. I wasn't big for my age, I was average I guess.. No one had a reason to fear me from just my looks.. I wasn't exactly sure where I was heading, but I knew it was towards Pennsylvania. How'd I know? Gas station maps. I got looks from everyone.. The attendant, people filling their tanks.. I didn't blame them.. A dirty, wet teenager just strolls up to the gas station and uses the bathroom and walks out.. It had to be odd, but people these days have seen everything. I'm sure they forgot all about it by now. It was almost a week before I had eaten again.. I really did leave unprepared. I felt so dirty, the same clothes, sleeping on the ground, only shower I had came from when it rained.. It was when I stopped at a Truck stop.. It was in Bartonsville Pennsylvania.. Took me a week to walk frm Asbury to there, which is only a four hour drive is my calculations are correct.. I wasn't getting all that far fast.. Truckers.. Now they truly have seen everything.. I walked in there soaking wet and not even one of them passed a second look.. All I saw was showers on the outside sign.. I didn't have money, but I had to try.. I was tired, hungry, dirty and poor.. I needed some sort of salvation..
