Date: 4/4/03 A darkened wasteland stretched beyond the edge of infinity. The sky was a blue mist, trapped in a sinuous shape, floating about the world. An illumination gave the barren land a way to see, but that radiance was unrevealed. There was nothing to see, nor anything from which to wonder upon. Nothing but a never-ending landscape of deserted sand, a piercing wind screaming in the air. A lone bed rested in the middle of this land. Made of some kind of stone and sheets flapping in the heavy breeze, the black bed covers rose out of the plot, a man’s body to be precise. Unmoving, even for his own breath, the man lay perfectly still, the pits over his eyes haunting and undisturbed. In his mind, he recited an elegy of his choosing. If the ground had been solid, made of granite or marble, his footsteps would echo through the land, bouncing off the walls that lay beyond the mist. But with the sand dancing in the air and the desert wind singing breathlessly, there was no chance of this. He walked, ever closer, the mind of the man in his bed still speaking. Never flitting Slowly but surely, each step as common as the next, the man walked towards the bed sitting in the middle of nowhere, like a gravestone atop a hill. Each step imprinted in the sand by his weight, and each step buried by the wind mere seconds later. His hands were made of diamond, as the fists never calmed. His hair was strands of grass, dancing in patterns like that of a prairie. His eyes glow in the rays of whatever light gave this land life, and they drew nearer with every incoming second. The man in the bed? Still is sitting His body never moved. Within, a million memories mixed together in the telling of his life, falling away like the sand of an hourglass. Each moment of his existence came flooding back, before being thrown away, never again to reemerge. Now he was the remains of a being long since drowned in his throbbing sorrow, waiting for his eyes to close and his light to burn one last time. But for now, words flowed through. Still is sitting And when the man from the periphery of the world stepped over to the other man in his bed, casting down a shade over him like a condescending mountain over an insignificant anthill, he stared down through the sheet, seeing the image of a lonely man dying below. A sinister smirk formed across his lips, as his mind thought of one terrible image. One that, deep inside, the man in the bed already knew. And feared. A dreadful sight if there ever was one, but the man didn’t seem to care. It was what he was expecting to see. “So, here we are, Jason. After all that self loathing shit, you started to clean yourself off, and you were well on your way to succeeding and gaining everything you ever wanted. I was ready to let you live, Jason. Leave you alone and let you wander off on your own.” All this time, Jason never flinched. Never moved. Never grinned or frowned or smiled or cried. He just stared up, dying in his own way. The wind became something more, strength multiplying and tossing the rain against the face of Jay. His eyes never flinched, and his evil grin never relaxed. And Jason’s blank stare remained, eyes sharply staring into nothingness. That is dreaming “I’ll tell you, Jason, I really didn’t want things to work out this way. The world is just too small a place for the both of us. That’s all. You got in my way, and pushed me off the road. Now you have to face the consequences of your actions. Just like you knew you always would.” And still, his mind never let go of its paralyzed state. With a world bleeding all around him, Jason was alone, his hair streaking blood down to the black sheets and down the side of the bed. Jay’s own body dripped of it, but he too would remain unscathed, no chill running through his spine and no repentance for what he was about to do. Remember everything that you were and everything that you ever lived, because in a few moments, the day will die and you will be forever trapped within. Remember the warmth of the sun. And the taste of the salt in the ocean. And the chill running through your veins in the snow. And all the world that you will never see again. “Remember the darkness and the light. The nightmares and the dreams. The silence and the echoes. Remember what it was like to smile, and what it was like to frown. And remember what it was like to cry with pain, and cry with joy. “Remember your mother. And your father. Even then, with the thoughts being ripped apart in his mind and erased from existence, he didn’t move an inch. Even now, with the world falling apart around him and the sand drowning in puddles of a bloody downpour, he didn’t cringe. Even when Jay leaned forward, choking away Jason’s soul with his stare, Jason didn’t make any movement. “It’s my turn. “Good bye Jason.” From out that shadow And then, and only then, did Jason close his eyes. Jason felt a tear fall down the side of his temple. And he remembered it, vaguely but surely, if only for a moment. The final tear would be Jason’s final memory. That lies floating on the floor “Shall be lifted.” Nevermore And then, all in the impulsive moment of a second, the world was silent. The lighting dazzling the sky stopped shooting through. The thunder echoing along the empty desert stopped crashing. The crimson rain stopped falling. The harsh wind stopped blowing. The mist floating around the realm disappeared. The world had gone completely dark. For mere seconds, the darkness was silent, no noise or sign of life existing within its grasp. But suddenly, a joyful whistling began, one of a song that had never been heard and would most likely remain a secret in the mind of the wind. Footsteps along a hard marble floor followed, as they traced an invisible path towards and invisible goal lead by an invisible man. With the turning of a knob an instant later, a stream of white light penetrated the darkness. The shadow of a man leaned all the way back, spanning the light carved out along the ground. Jay looked back into oblivion, his eyes glowing and his grin favoring the light. Birth had been a grand experience, but he decided life was going to be a grander one. Jay turned away, and closed the door. To be opened nevermore.
