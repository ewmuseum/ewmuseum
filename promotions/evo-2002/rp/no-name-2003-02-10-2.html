Date: 10th February, 2003 The scene had slowly came up from that blank that you would normally view, to the vision of a devastating figure. The large body. The deadly.. Oz. 'Xander? The guy I got paired with? I could rip his heart out and put it on a plate. He's nothing more than a drone, a talentless drone, corrupted by the position that he has. I guess it's like when a kid puts on a red cape and pretends to be super man. That reminds me of Xander.' Oz had nodded his head. He had flexed his wrist some, and from behind him came the genius. The lovely, the talented, by god the slickiest, dirtiest motherfucker alive. Angel. 'Xander? I can't do anything but laugh at him. He's a weakling, no power at all, he has nothing more to do but brainwash himself into thinking he has talent. He doesn't. The talent in the Unlimited is far from Xander. Then why is he the leader? Because the rest of them are idiots. They're idiots for not seeing that they've been under the rule, the watchful eye of a weakling. It's hard to believe that people can be that stupid..', Angel conjured up. He had stood beside the watchful eye of Oz, and looked into the camera. 'The Unlimited is full of drones, full of weakling drones that can't do anything but follow orders. They even have Dan S. I thought he'd be smart enough to not take that offer up. Why join them? You're under the watch of Xander? Are you under the watch of Xander, danny boy? Hey Angel... can I ask you a question?' Oz turned his head over to Angel, and then smirked some. 'Why sure you can..' Angel said fakely, in an artificial type of acting way, sarcastic in somewhat but not toward Oz. A hollow smirk threw itself onto Angel's face. 'Does the Unlimited have a leader?' '...you know? I never thought of that. There is the boss of Xander, danny boy, and then there's Xander. Will this question, ever be answered? I hope so..' they both continued on with there joking voices and meaningless ramble. 'Well if so, I want to join the Unlimited when I grow up too! I wanna be just like Xander!' Oz joked, he laughed some and turned back to the camera with the chuckling tone in his voice. The large grin on his face gave away he was joking.. '..tick..tock..tick..tock. Times a wastin' Xandy. Be careful, you might lose again. Well, you're good at that. We'll just see if you can do something for me. To prove that you're a fuckin' man.' Angel smirked after the statement... '...this is to you and your homosexual Unlimited 'clique'. If you and your gay cousin, Erin, and get through the match without help from any support at all, you have our word that we'll leave eVolution.' 'Should we do that?' Oz questioned his brothers wager. 'Picture Xander without getting anyhelp...' Angel encouraged his brother... 'Good point, but what if he does get help?' 'Heh..heh, this is where the fun begins. Xander, if you DO get help from anyone, you have to be my personal butler for a month. If you leave your duty as a butler, or decide to hit me or do physical harm to me in ANY way.. you lose your contendership for the Universal title. You've got some of today and one full day to think about it.. be a man Xander.. wait.. nevermind. Just say yes...' The brothers laughed, laughed, and laughed until we had to... .fade.to.the.martini.shot.
