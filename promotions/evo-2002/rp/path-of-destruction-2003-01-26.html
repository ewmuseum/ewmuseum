Path of Destruction

26 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/26/03 PARENTAL ADVISORY EXPLICIT CONTENT {The scene opens up, no more then two hours after New Year's eVolution has ended it's programming night, in the hallway leading to the Unlimited locker room. As the camera gets closer, it picks up similar noises heard after the last show this past Wednesday night. The door is cracked open just slightly, so the cameraman decides to make the crack wider by opening it} {As the scene inside comes into view, it's a sight of total chaos. Lockers toppled over, glass everywhere. A television monitor busted with a steel chair lodged inside of it. And in the middle of it all, we see the shape of a battered, bruised, bloodied, exhausted Xander} 'I'm tired of this SHIT! Tonight was OUR night.' The defeated second pains out the words through his teeth. 'First that bitch cheats to keep her fucking title by having everyone she's 'loved' come out to help her loser ass. Then that bitch HBC betrays us and costs us our Unlimited championships. And someone fucks with the lights, and helps Wisdom beat Angel.' 'Even with S. on our side, it's a fucking conspiracy.' 'But.. this is all going to change. Now that everyone knows who the ONE is... and what power Unlimited now holds.... it'll be their asses being handed to them.' 'Frost' 'Seara' 'Mason' 'Wisdom, King and Pierce. It's all going to come down now. You boys fucked with the wrong man for the last damned time.' 'No more mister nice guy.... you all need to watch your backs. Asis, S., Christoph, Angel and myself are out for blood. Especially me. I will not be dealt with in such ways ever again.... trust me... a dark cloud of pain and suffering is about to cover eVolution..... just you fucking watch.' {Xander picks up a large chunk of wood from what used to be a bench, and throws it at the camera, forcing it to snow, as it hits.}
