The Naughty circus? (Part 1)

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.15.02 ~~Steam overflows from the hood of a silver car the hood is raised and a man stands far back from it watching the steam spew for a while. As we move in a little closer we can see that it is Shocka standing in what looks to be a brand new suit with an eVolution tie on. He stands holding his phone to his ear but he can hear nothing but a voice telling him there is no service.~~ ‘Damn Rocky Mountains!’ He screams at the phone. ‘Why did I have to get stuck in the middle of nowhere because I insisted to drive…hell I know this state like the back of my hand…’ He slams his hood shut and walks around to his trunk he opens it and studies the contents inside. ‘And not a car in sight damnit…and its all because I took this road on purpose because I knew that not many people would go this far out of the way just to get to a little restaurant that I love to eat at…’ He now slams his trunk shut and walks around the car for a moment. ‘Now if I can’t get service here maybe I should climb that hill and try and get some towers to picks this piece of shit phone up.’ ~~Shocka takes off his suit jacket and throws it into his car he locks it behind him and begins a trek into the wilderness. As he comes atop a smaller hill in front of the big one he sees a semi followed by an old Chevy van. Shocka runs as fast as he can toward the road and makes it just in time for one of them to pull over. A rather large man in a red flannel jumps out of the semi and a pair of dirty pants looks at Shocka. Shocka studies him for a minute before the big man speaks.~~ ‘What the problem?’ The man speaks ‘Well my radiator hose exploded…’ Shocka speaks ‘Well why do ya think that was?’ The man asks. ‘Does it matter right now?’ Shocka says trying to act as calm as possible. ~~Out of the other side of the semi pops a China man probably about the age of Kai Kua the big man in the flannel turns to him and points and speaks.~~ ‘This is my….friend Sai…he’s new from China you should see the flexibility he has….’ ‘I don’t really wanna…’ ‘Show him Sai!’ ‘Sai show you how me flex.’ ‘No! I’m good…’ ‘Come on let him show ya pal…’ The man speaks. ‘He thinks that all young boys fit in by showing off for ya, ya know? He’ll do anything you say if ya know what I mean…’ ‘I should slap you….’ ~~Shocka suddenly thinks of someone that he knows…it’s a member of the eVolution roster Kia Kua a China man trying to fit in…he shakes his head disgustedly and then speaks to himself.~~ ‘Is that how Kai got his job…I mean I knew S. was desperate but could he be that desperate!?’ ‘What’d ya say pal?’ ‘Ahh…nothing…’ ‘No come on share it! We’re a friendly group.’ ~~Shocka looks at the man puzzled as he looks at the China man with a disgusting smile on his face and the China man snickers back. Shocka then looks back toward the mini vans to see a whole group of rag tag freaks…he looks back at the man and then at the freaks.~~ ‘How many of those are your kids?’ Shocka asks half joking. ‘Only the little one…’ The man smiles. ‘Say ya need a ride back to Denver? It’ll only cost ya a little…’ The man motions likes he’s giving a blow job and does a damn good impression I must say. ~~The China man drops to his knees and begins unzipping the man’s pants but he slaps him on the head and zips them back up pointing at Shocka. The China man runs to Shocka but the fat man grabs him and slaps his hand. The China man runs and jumps into the semi hiding as the fat man looks over Shocka and the scene slowly fades.~~
