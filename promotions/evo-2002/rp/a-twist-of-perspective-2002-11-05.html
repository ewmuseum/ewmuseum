A Twist of Perspective

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/11/02 Two wins on the bounce I thought to myself. It was a something I thought best not to dwell on for too long. After such a baron spell though, two wins seemed so much. Laying on my bed with my arms behind my head and my eyes staring at the ceiling I tried to re-focus. To put my feet firmly back on the ground. Evolution stopped for no man and if I needed proof, it was that I had yet another test in only two days; perhaps my sternest to date. Perhaps the point was being laboured too much, but wherever I go at present everyone was talking of my credentials. Going into every match I suddenly seem to have a degree of expectation surrounding me. Having experienced this weight for so long elsewhere prior to Evo though, it seemed weird that I was so weary of it, but the truth is I was. I paused. Scanning round the room I turned my head and began staring out the window and into the nights sky. The stars were out; the moon was showing itself in all its glory and yet I just seemed to be staring through it all and into my own little world. Was the hype really deserved I thought? Had I earnt the lavish praise? Or was I just dwelling on something which was slightly exaggerated? I'd always been a crowd favourite in Evo. Week in week out, I had come to the ring to a chorus of cheers and never once to be greeted by anything else than a warm and receptive audience. Suddenly though, the critics too were following suit. Having beraded me and abused me as a nearly man for so long, suddenly there focus was not on whether I was good enough for Evo, but instead whether Evo was good enough to cope with me! I stirred. Coming back down from my slightly dreamy state I was brought down to earth by the sound the rooms phone ringing on the bedside table. Rolling onto my side I contemplated ignoring the irritating tone. Did I really feel in the mood to talk to an Evo reporter? Three days of solid mat work and I was on in need of some well earned rest; tonight was set aside just for that reason. My limbs were weary and with my match but 48 hours away I couldnt afford to over exert myself; but then again having not tasted defeat for so long, perhaps I should answer the phone and revel in the past few days events. Then it hit me! Like a tonne of bricks I realised I was being pathetic. Turning away from the phone I continued to let it ring as I stared back at up the ceiling. If I ever wanted to be anything more than the nearly man, than a low carder in Evo, I had to begin to think like one. Taking my hands from behind my head I rubbed my face in frustration at my own thoughts. No disrespect to Stylez, but would Scott Frost really get excited over a win over a newcomer?! It was this thought that put it all in perspective. Sure I'd beaten Boone by DQ and that was a big turning point, but as yet I hadn't cemented my name in the spotlight! Why in the hell was I therefore thinking about anything other than Jameson this Wednesday. This was truely the biggest match of my life and despite my training being well managed, my mental thoughts were certainly off on another planet! With that I sat upright. Suddenly my topic of thought became all that much more focused. With my back against the headboard I looked forward. When I say looked forward though, I mean well past the television that sat before me and more toward the potential opportunity that had been given to me in Nashville. At that point I realised I was sitting in a room of quiet once more. The phone had stopped ringing, but it meant deep within me I could hear the frustration bubbling from inside. Jay Jay Stylez, Gerico and Eric Knight meant something; but it was time to take a dramatic step forward into the place I really wanted to be now. Thoughts of appreciation for my past few weeks successes had now firmly dissapeared from my head. My legs still felt weary, but now the adrenaline had gotten hold of me I was at the mercy of my ambition. Jumping to my feet I reached forward and grabbed my leather jacket which sat hung on the chair opposite my bed. After donning the jacket I then checked my pockets. My phone, my wallet and my cell were all present and correct; I was set but where to go?! I could hear the cogs working tirelessly in my mind, when suddenly it all slipped into place. A trip to the video shop was in order. Bursting out of the door I charged down the stairs leaving the previous scene in my wake. I wouldnt be gone long, as the nights work had certainly only just begun. Scene closes.
