Dr Coles is in Session

29 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-29 Something tells me Johnny Charisma is hell bent on getting into the record books; fair play to him is what I say. It takes an inenviable amount of talent to take pride of place in an episode of history and a book which proves the solution for so many uncles come Christmas time when seeking gifts for their nephew in your chosen subject. One things for sure though, that record is in your sights. How many times have you mentioned Scott Frosts name now?! I think you beat both the record for repetition of one single name a long time ago, maybe its time to step back and try something a little different. I'm jealous of Johnny Charisma? Johnny Charisma is without doubt the largest collection of testerone charged cells I think I will ever have the pleasure of coming across. Well excuse me for being a true student of the game but when I try my hand at something I tend not to just jump in head first, the internet being the ingenious invention it is presents me with a 'tool' for doing my research. You should be happy, I mean if I relied on just watching Legends I doubt I would even give you the time of day. Truth is according to the website your meant to be someone to fear; your hyped almost as a monster but then again I guess Evolution's pen pushers want people to believe just that, here is a pawn they hold, they don't like suprises and by heck you don't suprise them with anything you do. Your not arrogant, your just naive; naive to think even for the slightest second your anything more than some glorified jock chosen not for his in ring capabilities but his lack of mental ones. Your right on one point Charisma that I will have to admit, I did download your verses from the internet. Why? Simply, because I couldn't stand the thought of having to look at your ignorant carkus any more than I had to; Evolutions biggest mistake gives me nightmares on a nightly basis. Theres nothing to analyse any more, than what I have said in weeks gone by. Evolution see's its business as a collection of various equations which are selected to give a desired answer. I f**k Evolution up, thats why they put me in the shadows; I present myself not as a tool but as the answer its just I'm feared because I make everything else obselete. Including the need for Johnny Charisma. Of Scott Frost? I fail to see the importance. The guy has had more injury layoffs, than you have had steroid shots. I've long watched Evolution and have heard him say almost exactly the same thing as you. Sure, point to his accomplishments but don't ignore the fact he is not here now. If your campaign for prominance is built upon a number of past accomplishments then so help you god, I will stalk you to your grave, if not for Evolutions fans, then for the fact my entertainment system at my homes were not designed just so they could fit you on one screen. Revenge this is not, but a crusade instead. There is more than one way to skin a cat and if costs me every penny of my heritage then so be it, as this is a deserving cause; I intend on breaking a mould. Fifty pounds says you could'nt prize yourself away from your ego boosting reflection, if you did then perhaps you might see the reality before you. I might appear to be an internet geek to you but what else should I expect you to say, I'm different, you just don't understand me. I'm not Scott Frost. I'm not Jameson. While they prided themselves on their performance in the ring, I pride myself equally on my performance out of the ring. Your success stems from the fact your equation summed to a greater amount than those previously mentioned, I intend to prove though that when asked certain questions not everything is as it seemed since their is not always one answer to a question. This isn't about Charisma, hes a small part of a much bigger problem. The diagnosis is stagnation, the cure comes in the form of me. So let Legends be my arrival but come close it will only be the first chapter of a much bigger story; money can't buy you everything, for everything else I have talent.
