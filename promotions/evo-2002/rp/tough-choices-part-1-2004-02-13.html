Tough Choices part 1

13 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-02-13 (OOC:Forgot title when posted before; this link will allow access from Timelines board...man I hate work; LOL.) 1001 Holds is sitting in the lockerroom, he seems a little distraught. Dolo Bentswinger still having after effects from the double anklelock from the PPV limps in. Dolo:'Whats up?' 1001:'I can't believe I lost to Shaft-Man twice!' Dolo:'Look forget about that...we got the belts and that's all that matters. Speaking of which did King let you know who the mystery guy was?' 1001:'No....he seemed as surprised as I was.' just then a knock on the lockerroom door; before either Dolo or 1001 Holds can answer Madison RavensEcho walks in.... MRE:'I need to speak with Dolo......alone!' 1001:'Why....' Dolo:'It's okay....' Dolo Bentswinger interupts him as 1001 Holds looks at him somewhat suspiciously. 1001 Holds leaves the lockerrom area, as Madison moves in closer to Dolo and hands him a file.... MRE:'You realize what's in this file don't you? Give me one reason why I should let you go out there and compete in your current condition? You represent a risk not only to this organization but to the wrestlers themselves....' Dolo looks down at her in a very intimidating fashion. She seems unfazed as she continues speaking to him..... MRE:'......you know what I want. This whole Era of Destruction thing has gone on too far. It's bad for Evolution business and is potentially detrimental for your career. Especially if we are unable to insure you.' Dolo:'I won't betray King.' MRE:'He's blinded you to his cause he's living in the 80's; the days of Junkyard Dog, S.D.Jones, Thunderbolt Patterson are gone...dead. That style no longer sells, his dream of old school wrestling has gone the way of the dinosaur and is counter productive to Evolution's business.' Dolo:'lol....aren't you just a legal desk jockey? A mouth piece for Daniel Somers? Maybe I didn't make myself clear the first time I WON'T BETRAY KING!!' Madison is taken aback a little by that comment but maintains her composure....... MRE:'Really Dolo? Your history paints a different story.....wasn't it you who after a two time tag team title run with Malcolm in the old OWA split from King to try singles success? However your singles run wasn't nearly as successful as his now wasn't it? As he rapidly rose through the ranks...you on the otherhand was suspended for 'performance enhancement abuse' when Malcolm wasn't there to protect you.' Dolo grip on the file he has in hand begins to tighten...... MRE:' So what did you do with your second chance after your suspension? You come back the night Malcolm King claimed the first of two OWA World title reigns; and you and your new partner The Vertabreaker took Malcolm...new title in hand, and broke his back. So his title reign lasted what......three minutes before the OWA board was forced to strip him of that belt. You were paid a lot of money by Big Grumpy to do that weren't you?' Dolo enraged shoots back....... Dolo:'No amount of money in the world could......' MRE:'Who said anything about money Dolo?' Madison eyes shoots toward that somewhat crumpled file in the hands of 1/3 of Evolution's tag team Champions....Dolo Bentswinger. to be continued........
