Angel - 2

Date: 1.19.03 Sunday, January 19, 2003; 11:41 pm To be utterly and horribly generic, it's a dark and stormy night. I won't go into the boring details of how the moon shines through the pouring rain, sparkling in the beading drops of wetness on our windshield. The windshield that's the only thing separating she and I from eternity stretching on before our eyes. I won't explain how the pale glow it casts wraps itself around the honey of her skin, counteracting that warmth with a pale blue tint. Or how beautiful it is. Because that's 'boring', right? Maybe to you, and maybe to her, but it isn't to me. To me... it's my everything right now. I suppose I just get like that when I'm driving, though. Our conversation hit a slight speedbump about an hour ago, when she fell asleep against the side window. So now? Now it's just my mind and I standing against an army of boredom. Frankly, we just got our ass kicked. I gave in to it, and I can feel any deeper reaching centers of my brain shutting down. So much for any hopes of meaningful introspection tonight. That euphoric nothing holds me, now. No thoughts, no concerns, no worries, and no fears. Just the road and I. We're almost there, though. We're almost where we belong. Lei... she's amazing. On a particularly straight stretch of highway, I turn, one hand on the wheel, and glance over at her. I can't explain it... this just seems almost surreal to me. It's the only thing I love about shutting yourself down in the wake of a crushing wave of boredom.... something else takes over. When you let your mind fade, something else picks up the slack. 'Let's Dance' by Edwin and The Pressure faintly wafts out of the Pioneer CD deck I slapped into this car months ago, volume low enough not to disturb her, but high enough to take me away. My mind doesn't need to do anything. This song, the memories it carries, and the emotions they bring do all of the work right now. As I look at her, they fasten invisible paintbrushes on my eyelids. As perfect as the sight of the most beautiful girl you've ever met, sleeping in the moonlight is... this makes it even better. Colors fall onto her that I can't describe. They're love. Everything about this moment moves the brushes, and paint her in love. With a smile on my face that not many people will ever get to see, I shift up to fifth which really isn't necessary in this car, unless you mean business, and depress the gas pedal. Speed. Looking at her, there's no doubt in my mind that this is the right choice. She deserves this, and I don't want to make her wait any longer than she has to for it. I made her a promise, and for once, I won't have to break it. For once, I won't be a disappointment to her. For once... I'll deserve her. She smiles, and it melts me. Why would somebody like her even look twice at a guy like me...? It makes me feel somehow selfish, that we're together. Like I'm holding her back. I know she could do better than me in a heartbeat. I know it, and I hope that she does too. I... I can't explain it, though. I know that someday... 'Lei?' 'Yeah?' She shoots me another of those smiles, and as much as it shakes me, it only motivates me further. 'I... I know what people who look at us say. And I promise you that it won't always be like that. Lei, I promise you that... some day soon, I'll be something. That I'll be the kind of guy who des...' Cutting me off, she steps forward, and wraps her arms around my shoulders. I don't care what she says. I do need to do this. For once, I'd rather just be good enough. I don't want to be something that somebody has to be ashamed of, anymore. 'You're n...' 'I mean it. I'm going to make something of myself...' Letting out a happy sigh, she rests her head on my shoulder, and smiles. 'I don't doubt it for a second...' At the time, I meant it more than anything else I'd ever said, but... I was beginning to have a hard time believing it. What could a guy like me become, really? Other than even more useless. Less deserving. Well it turns out that there is something. Wrestling. That career choice is infinitely more logical than any other I could make, based solely on how... illogical it is, if nothing else. There probably never will be anything 'normal' about me. I've accepted that. The offer to work in this area came completely out of left field, and took me by surprise, but at the same time, I knew it would be something strange that eventually got me. It was even odd how it happened. You've got to understand that I work at an autobody shop, so any kind of opportunity like this is... beyond comprehension for me. Well, worked, past tense, I guess. It's kind of like a fairy tale, but instead of an ever after, I get a lot of fights and money. Actually, I take that back. It was nothing spectacular enough. I was at the gym, working on my... ah, whatever. It's nothing spectacular. Somebody'll tell you eventually, anyway. Christ, why am I even thinking about this, anyway? Tonight is my night. Tonight's the night that makes the last eighteen shitty years of my life worth it. That thought makes me smile, and at the same time, calms my wandering mind. There are no worries about not living up to what a girl like her deserves, and no dread of the thought of waking up from this dream left. All that I've got now is the sound of rain pelting the windshield, and fighting a losing battle to roll down it. Water sprays sideways off of the car, as I accelerate further, and pass a semi. Splashes shoot up along either side as I weave through the lanes. We're almost there... The night goes on, the stars still shine, and the music keeps playing. Blah blah. Fast forward twenty further minutes of boredom, and I find myself turning off of the highway, and onto a narrow, private lane. Trees line either side of it, thrown illumination of my low beams silhouetting them in the pale starlight. They're... tropical looking. Sort of like palm trees. What the fuck am I, a botanist? Beautiful, though. I think I may be able to get used to this kind of life. This private, silken paved road stretches on for what feels like fifteen minutes, before finally reaching the destination of our trip. A trip booked under the pretense of a car show. Lei thinks we're just going to represent a friend of mine's shop... little does she know. Pulling to a stop at the doorstep, I park the car in front of a house. Our house. It's small, but the most beautiful piece of real estate I've ever seen. What would we need with a larger one? 'Lei...' Reaching across the interior of the car, I gently run the backs of my fingers down her cheek. Silence. She's still asleep... normally a pin falling in the next room will wake this girl up. Lightly, I poke at the side of her jaw. Still nothing. I shove with a bit more force this item, and finally, she mumbles something, curling up against the window further. Shaking my head, I lightly pinch her nostrils shut. Still no reaction for a moment, but eventually, her mouth opens, and she lets out a quiet cough, swinging out of my grasp. Reaching up quickly, she rubs at her eyes, as I let out a chuckle. 'What the fuck, Angel?!' 'We're here...' My laugh dies to smile, which parts my lips ever so slightly. Excitement. With a quick flit of my eyes, I direct her attention to the window she's resting against. Turning slowly, and skeptically, she peers out of it. Silence. 'So what's this? Whose house...? Don't tell me we're staying with another one of your weird friends...' 'Like it?' One thin eyebrow arches, and her lips curl in a confused half smile. She's completely lost, and call me a sadist, but I love it. It just makes the surprise that much sweeter. Looking through the windshield, she sneaks a peek at the yard, then turns back to the house at her side. 'Yeah... hard to see through the rain, but it looks nice. So yet again, I ask... whose house?' 'Ours.' Cue overly dramatic thunder crash... now. Right on time. The pure and utter fluke of that one makes my grin widen even more. Before another question can slide through her lips, I have the driver's door opened, and I'm stepping out, into the rain. Sliding my jacket off, I toss it into the car for her, and shut the door behind me. We didn't anticipate the storm, so she's not exactly dressed for the weather in there. Rain pelts down on me, matting a thin, 30Tran button down tee to my chest, but I don't care. Nothing can slow me down now. Not now. Adrenaline courses through my body, heart beating in my chest, as I prepare to give her what she really deserves. A man. Roils of visible steam rising with the warmth of my body, into the cool night rain, I casually stroll around the hood of the car, and pull her door open for her. In an umpteen amount of sizes too big, my thin black coat hangs loosely off of her shoulders, as my arm finds itself wrapping around them. Cuddling against me in the rain, she uses my size for shelter, as we scurry into porch by the front door of the house. Digging through my pocket, I pull out keys, and reach for the doorknob, but she stops me, one small hand closing over my wrist. 'What's going on here, Angel...? This is getting kind of weird...' With my free hand, I lift hers off of mine, and hold it as I always have. Comfort. She relaxes enough for me to get the door open. [[[edit- cue Matrix-style camera rotation. On one hand, the look on her face is priceless, and I never want to forget it... but on the other, my curiosity is overwhelming me. I want to see if it really looks that great, but I don't want to turn away from her. I've got a friend out here, and gave him a few instructions. I asked him to set a few things up for our arrival... judging by her face, I'd think that he followed them to a tee, but I'm almost afraid to look. For the first time in... a long, long time, Lei is rendered completely speechless. 'I... it....... are... are you joking? Please tell me that you're not joking...' Sliding in behind her, I wrap both arms around her waist, resting my chin in that soft hair of hers. Alright, so maybe I'm a bit impressed, too. It's a view of the living room that we've got from here. The lights are dimmed, with small tablet candles scattered about the room, and lit. A fireplace centered in the far wall accents them with a roaring blaze, all throwing their dancing glows across the room. Quite possible the largest flat panel television that I've ever seen hangs on the wall one over from that, across from the most beautiful black leather sofa that I've ever seen. Plus side of hanging with a car crowd? Some wiseguy took the initiative required to mount a dim undercarriage neon glow tubing set beneath the couch, done in a pale blue. Some random movie is playing, sound turned off, with a guy hitting on a girl in a club. An impressive stereo, hidden somewhere softly plays Dot Allison's 'Colour Me' for us. A touch I requested, knowing full well what this song means to us. Simply put, it's... perfect. 'I'm not joking. It's ours. For real.' Silently, she slowly walks into the house. No words. She just stands there, looking at everything, as I walk in behind her, and close the door. Again, I smile. Justice. Now she knows what I felt like the night that we got together. Happy beyond description, but... too happy. It gets to a point where you're scared to touch anything, because it might just fizzle away like some wonderful dream. Her eyes close, and I know that she's praying to not wake up from this. 'Paid off completely. No debt, no bills, no worries.' 'But...' 'Ask me how. I dare you...' Grinning I egg her on. I know that she's curious like I am. She's got to know every facet of every detail of everything in her life to be happy. She won't rest until she knows the whole story, which I take a sick sense of pride in. I'm proud of how effectively I've been able to hide it all from her until now. Turning to face me, I see tears in her eyes, but I know that none will fall. Stepping a few steps closer, she cuts the gap between us. Inches from me. 'How could you ever, and I mean e... no. Fuck it. I don't care. I love it.' Pausing, she turns away from me for a moment, and lets it all soak in, all over again. She turns back, with a wavering smile. 'I love you, you crazy fuck.' Two small hands find their place on my chest. Leaning forward, she rises to tiptoes, pressing the length of her body against mine. With a light shove, she sends me backwards, falling against the door, and leaning against me. Hands sliding down my chest, and to my waist, she kisses me. I suppose this is where I say something cliché about birds singing, bells ringing, and all of that? Too bad. There's none of that. There's just her and I, and that's all I'll ever need. No amount of stupid descriptors could do it justice. As she draws back from the kiss, fingers at the top of my pants, and biting my bottom lip, all that I hear is that music... as the tides, they draw and close and the seasons turn again will the moon still wax and wane? will you always colour the day? you're in heaven this is heaven now you're in heaven now i'm sliding, it's intriguing so young, yet too old to learn this is heaven now... I never want this night to end. .fade to the martini shot.
