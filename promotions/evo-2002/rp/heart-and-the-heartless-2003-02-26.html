Heart and the heartless

26 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/26/03 'Damn what am I a school boy?? I sent her flowers....I'm trying to explain myself for actions at the time seemed right. I couldn't let Xavier do that to her; he wasn't only sending a message to Dreamer...but to me also. He felt it would be him who took me to my first championship here in Evo. He felt it would be him who I would be dependant on as my career progressed in Evo. It seems his Malcolm Envy had gotten the best of him where he ventures to the realm of irrational thought. For xavier that is nothing new...but this time, he seems to have a purpose, a plan; which makes him dangerous. Dreamer will go up against him...try and stop him, and no offense to Dreamer that is what Xavier wants. He wants him to strike from a point of rage, of anger. The old axiom of if you want someone to go away just ignore him.....Xavier feeds off the attention because he feels like he's been denied it all his career. The only problem with that is X won't go away.' 'Which brings us back to Heartbreak Chick......do I have the right to strike back at X for what he did? everything inside of me tells me Yes. She's my...was...maybe will be again....partner. She was attacked on MY watch. It was my past that opened her up to his attack. Then again so was Dreamer's past with X. They have a hatred for each other....X's problem with me is one based on a perceived betrayal, X....well he just hates Dreamer. Plus Dreamer's past with HBC makes it more appropiate in everyones eyes that he avenge his partner. Problem is I've been where X's have been....and can go down that dark road I can given the proper motivation. She may have provided me with it. What's all this talk anyway.....when it comes down to it the person who will avenge HBC will be none other than HBC herself. Just the thought of that makes me almost feel sorry for X.' 'However S. will milk this psuedo soap opera for what it's worth. I'm booked against Dreamer in a match with HBC as referee. A match made partly because of my actions at the PPV and the quote unquote lack of killer instinct I displayed. Nothing could be farther from the truth. I was protecting my partner...maintaining a relationship with a wrestler I have grown to respect from an attacker I know all too well. The possibility did cross my mind that by losing the match I would lose her as a partner...but it wasn't about me and my career at that moment it was about protecting my partner. She will make her decision soon enough, one I will respect either way. Right now the decision I'm concerned about is the match I have with Dreamer.....no I'm not questioning her integrity. The decision I'm questioning is mine? When I look at Dreamer do I see a man, a wrestler who has earned my respect....or do I see the man I envy because of his relationship with Heatbreak Chick?' ELSEWHERE...... Xavier Citizen is seen at a hospital, looking inside of the room where Big Daddy Dreamer and Heartbreak chick are getting stitched up....an orderly stops him. orderly:'Do you belong here?' XC:'I think so.....afterall an artist often likes to view his work and sees what areas he needs to improve in.' orderly:'I'm calling security...' As the orderly tries to leave. Xavier grabs him and forces him to look inside of the room through the little window in the door. XC:'You see that....the little lady is delivering a message to the big gentleman in there. I'm sure at this moment he wants to send one of his own....but I doubt he will find the words to do so. I want you to help him find the words.....' He takes the orderly and throws him through the door sending the whole thing collapsing around him startling the occupants. Citizen looks at The Syndicate...winks...and blows a kiss as he walks away. Once in the elevator he stops it between floors. XC:'How many people must be broken before Big Daddy Dreamer apologizes for his past transgressions. All I'm looking for an I'm sorry...that's it.' Xavier hears pounding as security tries to get access to the elevator.... XC:'How many more Dreamer......is your pride and ambition that great that you have become blind to the needs of others? I for one hope so....because I'm having fun.' Xavier starts the elevator again as he goes down one floor. As the car comes to a stop and the doors open he sees security there with weapons drawn. He cracks a smile before saying..... XC:'boo.....' (to be continued)
