*From the Ashes Incard* - Learning the Ropes

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-26 (OOC NOTE: This was in fact an incard that I sent in for From the Ashes that for some reason did not appear. I blame this solely on myself, and have no qualms towards the staff about the matter. I have been asked to post them, so here you are. Thanks.) ------------------------------- We catch a shot of Dane sitting someone backstage watching a small television monitor. He sits with one hand on his chin gazing at the television. '..Folks you are watching From the Ashes LIVE on PPV. And Man what a stacked card we have lined up for tonight with every title within eVolution getting defended here tonight! They people are filing the Centre Bell here in beautiful Montreal, Canada by the masses..' Dane remains entranced in the television set. An aging man walks to a nearby table. Dane pays him no heed. The man has a black shirt and jeans on and long white hair, neatly tied back. On the table sits a coffee maker and he pours himself some. The man turns to Dane sitting in the chair and smiles. Dane ignores him. 'Pay Per View time! I love these. The action. The drama. The suspense of not knowing who may win. Great isn't it?' Dane, of course, would love to convey his true emotion about From the Ashes. How it's his first Pay Per View.. his first match no less. He would love to tell him how much winning would shoot him into the limelight. He would love to tell him that his mind is a cloud of confusion with the constant reminder he may be a father anytime soon. But instead, he acts like he isn't there. 'Yeah.. Say.. you one of the boys? I haven't seen you around here.' The feeling was mutual, only Dane definitely didn't want to see this guy. He figures maybe if he talks he'll go away or something. 'If by 'boys' you mean I'm on the card tonight the answer is yes.' The man sips his coffee as Dane continues to watch the preparations for the PPV begin to unfold. '..Wow I can't believe From the Ashes is only a few minutes away from kicking off here LIVE! So many great matches.. So many bitter rivalries need to be settled! We need to know who will be controlling eVolution..' The man sets his coffee down and pulls up a chair beside Dane. He does not scoot over to make room. The man looks at Dane and extends a hand. 'Name's Brice. Brice Berkeley. I help out with a few technical things here and there backstage.' Dane breaks his gaze to pause and look down at the man's hand. He slowly give a quick half-hearted shake and turns back to his television. 'Good to meet you.. Ahh.. I didn't catch name?' 'That's because I didn't throw it.' Maybe some grand sarcasm will run this old fart back into the woodwork. 'A wise-ass, eh? Yeah I was too back in my day.. cocky as hell..' Dane blatantly rolls his eyes. 'I was a pro wrestler too, rough and tough as they come. 'Badass' Brice Berkeley. Yep. I went toe-to-toe with the best of them.. Frank 'The Tank' McCutcheon.. Phillip Morrison.. Haystacks Calhoun..' 'Wonderful. Now we share a common bond.' Dane's sarcasm was slowly seeping into this man. He sits back a bit. 'Yeah well.. I just thought you may want a few pointers on how things operate around here. I mean you are a rookie..' Was this guy trying to take jabs at Dane?! Surely he jest. 'And you are an old, washed-up, technical school drop-out who loves to ramble his maudlin life review to anyone within a five foot radius. But thanks for stating the obvious, Gramps.' Dane coldly looks this man in the eyes this time. He scowls at him before turning away. The man's notrils flare. 'Fine. I was just trying to help.' 'Help me with what? What can you possibly want to help me for? I don't need it.' They stare at each other in earnest. Was this man really trying in vain to someone reach out to Dane? 'I just wanted to show you around..show you.. the ropes.. Look I know you're name is Dane. I saw your face on the lineup. I also know that you want to keep to yourself. And that's fine. But if you will please let me just show you something, I think you will definitely have a greater chance of winning that match tonight.' Dane stands up. The man follows suit. After a tense staredown, Dane slowly nods. 'You have a last name.. Dane?' 'Well, I did..' 'Why don't you tell me about it?' Dane gives a faint smirk as they begin to walk out of the shot. '..And here comes the pyro! We are gearing up for one EXPLOSIVE night at 'From the Ashes'!!'
