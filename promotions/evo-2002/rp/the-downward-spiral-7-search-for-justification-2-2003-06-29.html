The Downward Spiral 7: Search for Justification 2.

Date: 2003-06-29 The Downward Spiral 7: Search for Justification 2. My God’s a shabby little bitch trying to make a scene. “Are you OK?” An all too familiar high pitched voice called to me as I lay naked in that dark alleyway. Though my mind had been awake as had my body by the looks of things my eyes still remained closed. I heard her light footsteps hitting the ground, creating their own song on the hard, alley floor. Calling on to my senses, opening my eyes to a whole new carousel of life. I woke in that alleyway not as a murderer, not as a rapist but as one of God’s creatures, filled with innocence, naked the way we had all come into this world. My first signs of life came when I saw her tight, leather pants clinging to her near perfect legs all the way up to her slim hips. Perfection opened up, overshadowing all my feelings of hatred and disdain. I had been carrying a cross upon my shoulders similar to that of my predecessor. My cross, however, had not been forced upon me by any one person but it was however, the way my life had been going. Falling slowly down that downward spiral. I had neared the bottom and searched for answers in rubbish bins for quite some time now but my answer had been clear to me from the beginning. Everything was clear now. My destiny was clear, my life was clear. My destruction was no more. Darkness and hatred were overshadowed by feelings of love and care. Finally, the purpose of my life had become apparent, no longer would I listen to those voices accusing me of being what I’d hated the most. “Wh… Where am I?” My ever rasped voice replied to her angelic, high pitched one. The answer beamed in her eyes as my heart sunk. At that very moment I felt my body drop down even further. There she stood, asking me whether or not I was OK. Her eyes were always the give away. It was Tate. I fell further down the spiral. Far beyond my downward spiral. Tate’s coming brought an end to the downward spiral. Now I had come further down the spiral. “Tate… would you please fuck off?” “No.”
