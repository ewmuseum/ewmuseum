Hysteria

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th February, 2003 Hah, this is the funniest i've experienced in a long time. I can't believe that we actually had to audacity to pull that off on a group of that calibur, I guess coming back into the ranks of wrestling was all that bad after all. I can't believe that I actually had to forfeit a win though. Atleast they know I mean business though. I've come here to demolish what opposes me and what opposes me now, is a faggot with a girl name and a guy with a losing streak the size of a porn star's penis. Now, I've finally gotten my ray of sunshine, my spotlight, my glory. I may have to share it with my brother, but it's better than not having any at all. Sharing spotlight with my brother is the best thing I can do right now, I'm back with my brother, we're about to start a legacy that eVolution has NEVER seen before. The Unlimited reign of terror, well what they had, will never amount to what me and my brother are about to do. We'll destroy all who oppose, all who claim to align, all who think differently of the legacy. All who think that they can stop us. We are what we claim to be, unstoppable, untouchable, not 'unlimited', but the Kysis. Oz Wow, I hated to do that but it was the thing that I had on my mind the entire time I was in the Unlimited. Xander was a loser leader, a dead beet and a guy who could never get the job done. Either Asis or I had to always come out and asist him because he was too fagalicious to get the job done himself. I can't do anything but spit on the face of Xander and the way that he actually thought I was his friend. I can't do anything but spit, and laugh. Xander thought I was a friend, I don't even see how Asis puts up with him. Asis could do a whole lot better than to hang under the tainted wing of Xander, Asis has bigger and bigger things that he could do. Most of the Unlimited do. Most of his 'lackies' as he refers to them, can beat Xander in a fight and actually come back and laugh about it. Hah, me.. friends with a homosexual of that calibur. Hah..hah...hah. Me..hah, friends with Xander. Angel They both were seated in the room, looking at the television that stayed positioned infront of them, Angel on the leather Lay-Z-Boy whilst Oz layed down on the long black sofa. He had his arms crossed across his chest, with his feet crossed also looking into the television, Angel just ran his hand across his mouth to remove some of the Heineken away from his upperlip. They were watching... they were watching.. Legends. Oz had no kind of serious look in his face, but more of a laughing type of face, but Angel. His face was slender and serious. Full of hostility, Oz was probably wondering what he was thinking, but Oz refrained from showing it and continued to watch the television.. A second referee finally came down the ramp and slid into the ring as Xander turned Seara quickly around and had scooped her straight up onto his shoulder’s in a standing fireman’s carry. He held her up there for a moment before taking a few steps to the side and flipping her over right down into a death valley driver before going for the cover. One Two The pin was broken up by Oz! 'Oz just made the save for Seara!' Justin Fritz exclaimed, the shocking expression on his face showed more than concern, but somewhat delight. 'What the hell is he doing?!' 'I don’t know but it looks like Xander has just been set up!' 'Oz has a death wish.' Jack Tyler said in a low tone of voice. Oz's chuckle rose from his throat and jumped out of his mouth. He stopped chuckling some and continued to look into the television. Oz drove a double axehandle shot right down across the back of Xander’s head as he still covered Seara. He got back to his feet and reached down to pick Xander up before he sent Xander right into the ropes and nearly ran right through him with a massive spear that left Xander lying on the mat holding his ribs. Oz grabbed Xander again and began lifting him to his feet but before he could do anything else out of the crowd, his brother Angel came sliding into the ring. 'Here comes the retired Angel to talk some sense into his maniac brother!' Jack Tyler exlaimed, relief shot from his voice but he still had a somewhat concerned type of facial expression. 'I don’t know if that is even possible.' Justin Fritz said, chuckling somewhat after. He was loving it, his entire facial expression was giving it off. 'Someone better do it or he’s a dead man.' Jack Tyler said in somewhat to the defense of Xander and his Unlimited henchmen. Angel, wearing a pair of blue jeans and his leather jacket, ran right up to his brother and shoved him back a few steps and away from Xander. Angel took a moment to lean over Xander and check on his condition before he got back to his feet and stared across at his brother. Angel slowly began taking off his leather jacket and then yanked his shirt off over his head and threw it at his brother. Underneath the shirt Angel had just taken off he had an identical black shirt with the word “Kysis” in green grunge letters. Oz took the shirt his brother had thrown to him and put it on so they were both wearing matching shirts as the two men lifted their arms into the air as the fans were unsure of just how to act. 'Now what is he doing?!' Jack Tyler questioned loudly. He tilted his head somewhat and continued to stare desperately for an answer at the gloating Kysis. 'I think Angel was in on it! He swerved Xander and The Unlimited!' Justin Fritz confirmed, and boy was he dead right. 'He’s supposed to be retired damn it!' Jack Tyler confirmed, but to any smart person, it was obviously false. 'He might still be but he’s here with his brother obviously.' Justin Fritz confirmed. The two men stood over both Xander and Seara as suddenly swarming down the ramp came both Erin Christoph and Asis. Angel and Oz saw them as the came out the backstage area and after a quick kick to Xander by Angel the two men scattered out of the ring and disappeared into the crowd as Christoph and Asis slid into the ring. 'I don’t know what we just saw happen but Asis and Christoph have just run off Oz and the traitor Angel.' Justin Fritz confirmed. Angel's grin slowly start to emerge getting wider and wider by the moment. 'This isn’t going to go over well with The Unlimited. There will be hell to pay.' 'I don’t doubt that for a second. I think that due to what just happened this match has also been thrown out now.' said Justin Fritz. 'That’s not fair. Xander should win by disqualification then. He was attacked during the match.' Jack Tyler continued to whine. The expression on Angel's face slowly eroded from a sinister smirk, to a laughing like grin. A small chuckle emerged from his lips as he continued to shove his attention directly into the Legends footage. 'It was his own partner that attacked him!' Justin Fritz exclaimed in defense of the Kysis 'Well……..still.' Jack Tyler murmured. The scene then cut to a commercial break and the silence stayed. The grins on both of the men were obviously about to explode as Oz turned his head up and looked over toward Angel who was sitting there with a very large grin on his face. Oz couldn't take it anymore. Oz broke down at the last sight of the broken down Xander. Angel then caved with him and began his manical laughter. Both of them, errupting like volcano's with furious laughter at the hurt member of the Unlimited. It was evil toward the average human being, but it was funny to these two devilish demon children. They continued to laugh until more footage came on... Xander is seen walking into the office of Daniel Somers with a huge grin on his face. Xander opens the door only to find the dark room empty of Somers's presence. Xander flips the light switch and as soon as the lights come on the door is slammed into Xander’s body knocking him backwards and Frost comes out from behind the door with a huge monkey wrench in hand. He punches Xander in the face, and grabs him by the hair. 'Attack her again Xander, and the same damned thing is going to happen to you.' Frost angrily whispered into Xander’s ear. Frost jerked Xander around and reared back with the monkey wrench and pounded him in the back of the head with the monkey wrench and droped it at his feet. 'Don't fuck with us!' Frost yelled as he walked out of the room, and Erin Christoph just so happened walks up when Frost left. Erin found the slowly staggering body of Xander laying on the floor of Somers's office and Erin checked on him as the scene faded away. Angel errupted with laughter, he was gasping for air at this point, he was off of the Lay-Z-Boy and on his face cringing with his arms wrapped across his abodmen. Oz was slowly stood up from behind the couch, tears in his eyes as he got one more glance of Xander and pointed and began to laugh once more. He fell backward behind the couch and continued, the laughter removed his low pitch voice and got high. He continued to laugh as he slowly crawled from behind the couch and looked at the television at the down Xander, he continued to gasp as he slowly got enough wind to say a couple of words, most of them interrupted with laughs. 'Did...did..did you-' He was interrupted by his laughter. Angel was practically crying, keeled over on the Lay-Z-Boy with his face clinched in his hands. He rolled over onto the middle of the floor, the soft black velvet carpet running against his back, still laughing. 'Did..you see how he hit him, man?!' Angel exclamied, the sentence was immediatly followed by constant laughter, and the jingling of keys sounded in an opposing from. The sound of a door opening jittered forward and the approaching wife of Angel, Lei Ming, waltzed into the room looking down at the two men. She chuckled some and then began to talk. 'What're ya'll doin'?' Lei looked at the slowly standing Oz, and the still lying Angel. Oz had enough wind to get out an answer for the question.. 'Nothin.. we laughin' at dis dude on tv. We work wit 'em, he like da sorriest bastard up there I swear, ask Angel, he was friends wit 'em..' Oz said, slowly gaining composure and walking over to the front of the sofa and looked ahead at the telelvision once more. He fell backward and plopped down into a laying positon watching. 'He-he wasn't my friend. I acted like it to get some publicity, don't believe Shaun...' 'Yeah, we'll I brought ya'll some food, it's on the table in the kitchen, ya'll make me seem like i'm ya'lls momma or somethin' Oh yeah, she did call looking for both uh ya'll.' Lei confirmed. 'Aiight, let's go eat bruh...' Angel looked over to the laughing Oz, as he watched Xander confirm that he wanted some of the Kysis. Angel had stood up and left the room along with Lei. Oz slowly stood up and headed over toward the kitchen, but whilst walking Oz shouted, 'Angel! Xander said he wanted some of the Kysis!'. Finale is what it's come down to, and now we must... .fade.to.the.martini.shot.
