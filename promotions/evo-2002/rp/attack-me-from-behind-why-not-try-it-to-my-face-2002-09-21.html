Attack me from behind....why not try it to my face

21 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/21 -{Scene opens at a radio station in Orlando, Florida. The ending of Eminem's 'Without Me' remixes into the beginning of Pink's 'Get This Party Started'. After about 3 minutes of the music play, couple of commercials play which includes an ad for Citifinancial Loan Company, Hyundai commercial, and an ad for Evolution. After the ads end, DJ Benny Jones comes on the air.}- Benny Jones: Welcome back everyone to the station that puts the grooves in your moves, KKTY-104.8. I am the man with the plan, Benny Jones, rocking your world from 5 pm until 10 pm every weeknight. You just heard the latest mix from Eminem and Pink. Well it is now time for the segment where I bring out our guest celebrity. Tonight our celebrity comes from the world of wrestling. This man is considered by many to be the top cruiserweight wrestler in the business today. Ladies and Gentlemen, give it up for my guest tonight.....Evolution's very own.....Gerico and Anna Marie. -{Benny Jones applauds as Gerico and Anna Marie enter the studio. Gerico is sporting black parachute workout pants, black Evolution t-shirt, and his red mask while Anna Marie is sporting blue jean shorts and a red tank top. Both grab a chair and sit down next to Benny Jones. The show theme song fades out as Benny returns to the mic.}- Benny Jones: Welcome to the show guys. I am glad you guys could stop by knowing the schedule you guys have. I mean Evolution just had the PPV, Digital Destruction, pass and now the hit TV show Legends is just right around the corner and I know you guys have been out doing some promotional work for the Evolution brand. Gerico: Thanks for inviting us to the show, Benny. It isn't no problem for us to stop by and share the microphone with radio's #1 DJ. Benny Jones: Well first off let's start talking about your background. Tell us a little bit about yourselves, how you came across that mask, your wrestling style, how you guys met...stuff like that. Gerico: I started my career out as most people would know on the small independent scene right after I got out of high school. I then proceeded down to Mexico and joined my first ever organization there which was nothing huge. There I learned the ways of the lucha libre style wrestling and along with the technical style I already learned in wrestling school, I became the man you see in front of you. I also met Anna Marie in that organization as well. One thing lead to another and we are still together as we speak. Benny Jones: What about your mask???? I mean I never seen a mask like it. Kinda looks like a bird???? Gerico: The mask kinda has a centimental value to it. The eagles on the side represent how I move around that ring. Many wrestlers have said that I fly around the ring as free as an eagle that my feet seem to never touch the ground. The origin of the mask was something I picked up in Mexico. Many of the lucha libre wrestlers in Mexico cherish the mask as it hides the identity of the man, making them mysterious. Benny Jones: Now lets talk about the Evolution brand of wrestling if we will. What seems to be your focus in the organization....your main goal(s)???? Gerico: When I got the call from Evolution saying that they would like to interview and possibly sign me to a deal, I was estatic. I mean this was the break I had been looking for to enter the big time of wrestling. My main focus is simple, succeed in that ring and climb my way to the top. My main goal...to be the best, most entertaining wrestler on the roster. I want to be the wrestler when the fans walk out of that arena, is the one they are talking about My goal is to win a title and defend it like a champion should while honoring it with prestige and dignity. Benny Jones: Now I saw here where you debuted this past week at Digital Destruction in a dark match if you will against Python. Care to share your thoughts on how you believe your performance went???? Gerico: I have to admit, I was nervous as hell. When I laced up my boots and started preparing myself for my first match, I was afraid that I would be a flop. Then Anna Marie came up to me and gave me the most encouraging words that a love one could give....'Go out, do your best, entertain the crowd like I know I can, and the rest will fall into place'. The adrenline rush was nothing like I imagined when I was launched out onto the stage area and the roar of the crowd going throughout the arena. As far as the match went, Python gave me a good match and I thought I did fairly well. Yeah I made a few mistakes so I would have to grade myself a B- in that ring but I was satisfied. Now watching Eric Knight come out and interfere in the match was another thing. Benny Jones: Let's talk about Eric Knight if we can...'The Outcast' as most call him. The man is a formidable opponent coming up for you and definately no stranger in that Evolution ring. Tell me, what is your strategy and thoughts going into this match???? Gerico: I can describe Eric Knight in one word.....coward. I mean anyone who has to come out and attack me from behind....that is no real man, that is a little boy who is scared and fears what he sees in that ring. When someone sees something they fear, they tend to try and eliminate it and that is what he attempted to do at Digital Destruction. I mean he walks out, climbs up to the ring apron as I was setting up for my finisher and shoves me off to the outside, nearly capitating my head on the guardrail costing me the win. I don't know what Eric Knight was thinking but I will tell you this much, he picked on the wrong man to use as his stepping stone. My strategy is simple....I plan on bringing it right to Eric Knight and if I don't win that match, I will at least get a touch of revenge cause Eric Knight will know that he was in one hell of a fight after that bell sounds to end the match. Yeah I may be looked at as being one of the smaller guys in the organization and I may be considered the underdog due to the fact I have wrestled one match in the Evolution. Eric Knight has yet to impress me. I mean the guy owns a 2-2 record, likes to attack guys from behind....nothing impressive with that storyline. It is simple, Eric Knight is going into Digital Destruction looking to use me as a stepping stone in his return to the ring but in reality what will happen is that I will use Eric Knight to make an example....don't underestimate someone you know nothing about cause I just might surprise you. Yeah I look for this to be a high flying match but I have yet to see anyone who can hang with me in that ring when it comes to high fly moves but come Wednesday...we will find out who will fly and who will crash. Eric Knight....bring your A game Wednesday cause I plan to. Benny Jones: Well fans, that is all the time we have here today. I want to thank my special guests for being on the show, it was definately a pleasure. Again, give it up for Evolution's own Gerico and Anna Marie. You can catch them this Wednesday night on Legends. The show looks packed from top to bottom with top notch matches so don't miss it. We have to take a commerical break but when we return....we have some System of a Down for you. Don't change that channel.... -{The show's theme plays as the on air sign turns off. Benny Jones turns and shakes the hands of Gerico and Anna Marie thanking them again for stopping by. Gerico and Anna Marie exit out the door to the booth as the scene fades out to black.}-
