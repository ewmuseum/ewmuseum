History of One :: Chapter VI

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/6/03 I was never so refreshed.. The shower was the best thing going for me at the time, soon enough though.. It would be food.. I was so hungry.. I don't know how I survived.. Rain water seemed to be the only thing keeping me the slightest hydrated. Hardcore eh? Not really, it was all worth it getting away from Asbury.. I wanted nothing to do with that place anymore and I was getting just what I wanted.. A life with problems, hard times, and even harder way of living.. I always felt that the time I spent getting away was my time of enlightenment. I realized that though it was hard and could have killed me.. I liked working through it. I liked the adventure.. I liked being on my own, taking care of myself.. Even though I wasn't doing that great of a job, I still liked the idea. I left my wet old clothes in the shower room. I wasn't worried about it, I'm sure whoever cleaned it would throw them away.. So I could careless.. My new clothes.. Oh, they were so comfortable. Maybe a bit odd looking, but comfortable.. The jeans were just plain black cheap jeans, and the shirt.. It had a skull with a snake crawling through it. It kinda fit though, considering its how I normally dressed, but without the design.. My hair was all over the place, I didn't see a point in doing anything with it.. It was clean, I didn't care how it looked... When I walked over to the restaurant, the truck driver was sitting at a booth near the buffet. He only had a soda infront of him, I supposed he was waiting on me.. I walked over to the table slowly and sat down.. I didn't know what to say or anything so I just sat there starring at him.. He just stayed leaned back and looked right back at me.. 'Feel better?' He asked, I nodded. 'I'm Teddy..' I just looked at him for a moment and blinked.. 'Lance.' He extended his hand towards me, I just looked at it.. 'Nice to meet you..' He gave me money and was about to give me food, Iguess I could be the least bit generous. So I shook his hand.. 'How about we get some food and talk?' He asked, through his glasses his eyes seemed lost.. I just nodded... Truck stop buffet, normally you think of nasty food only a truck driver would eat, but god damn.. I was so hungry.. I piled anything I could on my plate, I didn't care what it was. People were giving me odd looks all around the place. I paid no mind to any of them.. I was hungry. I needed the food and wanted the food.. A week without food really puts it to ya. I was weak.. I kinda regretted not eating the vegitables the last night I was at home.. 'So, where you running from boy?' I didn't take to being called boy.. 'My name is Lance.. And I told you, I'm not running from anything or anyone. No one is chasing me.. Remember?' I didn't mean to sound so snappy, kinda just happend I guess. 'Alright than.. Lance.. Where'd you leave to end up here?' Nosey prick wasn't he? He was paying for my food, so the least I could was answer his questions... 'Asbury New Jersey..' I was too busy eating to give him a full blown answer... 'Nice place, why would you leave there?' He just wouldn't stop asking the questions.. He barely touched his food. He seemed to worried about interigating me.. 'Didn't like my life style.. Wanted a change..' Keeping my answers as short as possible I thought would give him the hint I didn't want to talk.. 'So you run off here? You could be dead right now..' Now he was going to lecture me, just what I needed. 'And because of kind folk such as yourself.. I'm not..' 'What would you have done if I wasn't here a half hour ago when you were begging for a shower?' Trying to out smart me.. It wasn't going to work.. 'Walked in the back and took a shower anyway..' I was being honest.. 'And gotten yourself arrested?' This guy thinks I didn't have my shit all in one basket.. I lasted a week on my own, longer than most do on their own.. Christ.. 'Yes, shelter, food, and showers in prison..' He wasn't going to get the better of me.. I knew what I was doing and the consequences to anything I was planning on doing. 'I guess you have a point, but they would have taken you home..' He just didn't stop..I wanted nothing more than to just eat.. I put my fork down on the table and leaned back starring him in the face.. 'They can't take me anywhere if they don't know who I am.. Now excuse me, I would like to it.. It has been a week..' I picked up my plate and walked to an empty table on the other side of the buffet.. I saw the man laugh and shake his head.. Its not like he could take my food away from me now.. He's still going to pay for it.. People like him don't go back on things they do. People like him are the ones that goto heaven.. I'm not.
