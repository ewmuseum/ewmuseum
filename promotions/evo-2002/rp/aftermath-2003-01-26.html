Aftermath

26 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.26.03 ~The American Air west Arena has never witnessed anything as violent as they just have. When eVolution is in town there is no telling what will go down. Three main things, a debut, a revelation and a very violent ending. The debut James Kimson, the revelation? Daniel Somers is the one?! But the violent ending the one that left everyone reeling…Shocka proved that he would stop at no length to keep his title. He took Frost and put him through a flaming table and left him in a heap of burning flesh. He didn’t come out of the match without injuries though…~ ~EMT’s are gathered around and people are rushing in and out of the scene grabbing medical supplies and such. When one of the people in the front of the group move you can see a stretcher with Shocka on it. It is propped up and they are all flocking to tend to his arm. It looks badly blistered and a little on the toasted side the screams of a grown man can be heard~ ‘Jesus! Give me some morphine or something…’ He screams. ‘My arm still feels like it is burning!’ ‘Just calm down and control your breathing Jon…’ A EMT shouts. ‘Easy for you to say you don’t have first degree burns on your god damned arm!’ Shocka shouts as he grabs the collar of a EMT. ‘I said I want morphine!’ ~Into the scene you see Morpheus Omega come in he grabs EMT’s and begins tossing them out of the way he walks up and looks down at the arm of Shocka and shakes his head in disbelief.~ ‘Anything worse than burns?’ Omega asks. ‘They said they think I have a slight tear in my bicep…’ Shocka says in a low voice. ‘Burns are no fun…’ Omega says. ‘Yeah and these fuck nuts won’t give me any pain medication for it…’ ~Omega shouts a wicked glance towards one of the EMT’s and he begins taking off his chain belt but the man starts searching through his medic bag. He pulls out a syringe and a vile he injects some medication into Shocka. You can hear a voice screaming.~ ‘Let me thru! That’s my best friend!’ the voice screams. ~Once the man gets through you see its Lee exodus he has the Universal title in his hands and he throws it at Shocka it slams onto his burned arm and he lets out a scream of pain.~ ‘What the hell are you doing LEE?!’ Shocka screams at him. ‘I just thought you wanted your belt…’ Lee says in admirable voice. ‘Did you see what I did to your idol?!’ Shocka screams. ‘Yeah.’ Lee Answers. ‘I’ll make your pain twice as bad if you don’t get out of here!’ Shocka yells. ~This encourages Lee to turn around and sort of jog away from the scene. The medics now start to put some burn ointment onto his arm and begin wrapping it. He jumps off of the stretcher and they hand him a sling he puts it over his neck and slides his arm into it. Omega hands him his title and he holds it in his left hand as he walks out of the scene slowly as the feed cuts.~
