Do I know you? Even better than you think

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/11/02 'To understand the man Asis....is to understand the responses of a pavlovian dog. Xander says strike...so he does. He uses the Unlimited as an outlet of rage. To know him is to understand him....funny I never really seemed to care about Asis.' Xavier Citizen is standing in the parking lot of the arena...his thoughts turn to the best place to make Asis feel pain once the match starts to get out of hand which he is confident it will..... 'Even a simpleton as yourself must appreciate the drive and determination it takes to make another man scream out in pain. But apparently you and I are thinking along the same lines. You see this match isn't about who wins and who loses; it's about survival something I've become accustomed to do recently. The whore Seara served as your gateway to pain, simply put....she is the reason you have me in your life now. The nature of your relationship with her is a moot point to me because she has served her purpose, and given me you...Asis as the target to release MY anger.' He starts to pace around the parking lot..... 'You don't seem to fully understand Asis....somehow you don't seem to fully get it. Your one dimensional view of what will occur on Legends is a little troubling. You feel it's a simple matter when in fact your task is just that more difficult.....you see much like you I feel the need to make someone my bitch, if not Seara then why not you? As for Luther hell beat on him all you want he is only my partner out of necessity not by choice.' Xavier stradles a motorcycle as he looks up to the ceiling....he moans.... 'Really Asis, isn't the thought of you beating me into a bloddy pulp the least bit erotic in nature? Does it get you off knowing that no matter how much blooding me up is pleasing to you.....that I'm pleased just a bit more also? You see the more you beat me the better I like it for in all reality it's the only way for a dying man to feel alive. Unfortunately....the longer I live, the greater the desire to kill you becomes. You showed weakness, compassion even by letting the whore live.....maybe someone should show you how to get that job done.' He gets off the bike and heads over to a concrete wall. He bangs his head against it 5 times until he busts his forehead open...... 'Maybe another time, another place Asis we may have been allies...however that time that place will never come to past, so to you Asis I will do the next best thing......and put you out of your misery forever.' (fade to black)
