From the desk of The Syndicate

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From the Co-Chairpersons Syndicate International [detail withheld] Toronto, Ontario, Canada From the desks of the greatest Tag Team in wrestling history: The Syndicate Dear: Mister Somers Last week, a message was given to us, a very clear message from the front office of Evolution, The Unlimited, and The One, or should we say Mister S.…..you. In this message, you presented us with somewhat of an ultimatum. An ultimatum directed specifically at The HeartBreak Chick and Big Daddy Dreamer. The ultimatum in that if The Syndicate, the team you fear the most, decided to team together, that in somewhat, though knowing you, ALL PROBABILITY, would lose their potential tag title shots if we participated earlier in the night. Now having worked with you Somers for a year, stemming back from the AWF, EDW days to now, we don’t trust you the slightest. The stock market is more trustworthy than you are. Hell even O.J. Simpson is more trustworthy than you are. So we are at a quandary, an impass, until finally, we have reached a mutual solution on behalf of Big Daddy Dreamer and myself. Because it would please us to no end to see you pissed off beyond all disbelief. Angered that once again the mighty Syndicate team foiled another ‘One’ plan. And most of all making Daniel Somers look like a fool to millions of people watching worldwide. So this is the final solution…. At Legends this week: The Heartbreak Chick, former High-Impact and Tag Team Champion, will team up with none other than….. LUTHER WISDOM! Because it is a GUAREENTEED TITLE SHOT against your Unlimited bitches. Failure to comply will result in serious legal and financial aspects for your company and you personally. Because in the fine print, we have added and your lawyers have signed it for you, if you personally Mister Somers or any member of The Unlimited interfere in anyway, shape or form in this title match, then at Tainted Love it will be. The Syndicate vs. Daniel Somers in a (Glass Coffin Handicap Match) Now in regards to Big Daddy Dreamer and his partner. It has been repeatedly pointed out that Thomas Draven IS NOT an active member of Evolution and hereby disqualified from teamming with BDD. However, anyone else is free to join him other than HBC, so just to keep you guessing BDD will announce it at his leisure. On a further note: We noticed you brought in another team trying to claim the rights to The Syndicate name. You must have a very short attention span for the last time you pulled something like this and another Syndicate team, we broke their damn necks and ended their careers. You’ve angered us for the last time Somers. Tainted Love begins a whole new season of Syndication! Cheers always, The HeartBreak Chick and Big Daddy Dreamer The Syndicate!!!! XOXOXOXOXO
