Evolution 1.0

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-30 The Remembrance Former sight of the WTC Evening The news has been speculating all day why the Canadian Ambassador to Algeria committed suicide, not that these two care. They have other plans on their mind. Another black-box mission in the bag and now they can finally concentrate on the thing they love the most. Kicking ass and taking names in Evolution. They watched with great interest ‘The New Dawn’ PPV and was shocked and dumbfounded at the strange happenings during the tag team invitational match. Four teams destroyed before the match, one team chickened out, and the surprising end in which those darn lights went out again causing confusion. Some people even said that the Syndicate was there! New music, new attitude, unrestricted warfare. The Syndicate is back. God have mercy on us all….for they won’t. HBC: Last night, ‘New Dawn’ was just that, a new dawn in the age of wrestling. During these past few weeks, we sat back and watched Evo with great interest, to see if anyone can step forward and lead this company. Much to our surprise, no one did. Xander finally won the Universal championship, a lot of new faces have been coming and going in and out of here like a damn revolving door. As we sat back, we were disgusted by what we saw. This place is in shambles. Too much wrong deserved ego. Frankly, most of you haven’t done shit here. There are the notable exceptions, those we’ve fought and have respect for. Seara is back, good to see her return. Omega still crazy and with whom we have an unsettled score. But for now, one team in particular…. St. Violence…. BDD: St. Violence holds something that collectively and many too most people’s surprise, something we haven’t accomplished as a team. The tag team championships. HBC captured those titles on two different occasions, she is looking for the three-peat, whereas I am looking to hold Evo gold for the first time. During these past few weeks, there has been a lot of bitching about our guaranteed title shot. I think Tyler said it best, if you have a problem, do something about it, otherwise….fuck off! No one has stepped forward and done shit. Why, the answer is painfully obvious. They can’t. They can’t back up their bitching and moaning, so like an armchair quarterback who thinks he can lead his team to greatness from his plush chair, they can’t do a damn thing to stop us. You guys had your shot last night? What happened? Cat got your tongue! HBC: Maybe it was the pipes, the chairs, the cunning convincing, or truth be told the biggest weapon of all….fear, The fear and most of all the repercussions of their actions if they actually entertained the thought for a split second. And they thought wrestlers were dumb. Evo-1. The one-year anniversary show marks our official in-ring return against St.Violence. I could not think of a better name than St. Violence. For they will be the first team to feel our new improved, and unremorseful violence against Eric Knight and Deacon. You two tasted the gold, kept it warm, but now its time for the gold to come home to rightful place, around our waist. Besides, the gold looks better around my sexy waist than those two asscracks. BDD: Agreed. Knight and Deacon, are just the first of a long list of people who will feel our new taste of wrestling. Gone is the crap of yesteryear. Now is our time. Now is our time to blow the roof off the place unlike anything seen in this industry. The tag titles will be first, but all gold is set on our crosshairs. When you smell that certain odor of fear and the little hairs stand on the back of your neck and don’t know why, its real simple…….The Syndicate is coming for you, so kiss your ass goodbye……
