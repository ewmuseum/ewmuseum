Thoughtlessness

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.28.02 Rage: The anger grows beyond anything that has yet to bee seen. The Continental title has been taken from him and he is left with nothing now that Shodan again cheated to win. But now Shocka is left with nothing but his rage. He has yet to cool down and is left in permarage. Now he faces Shane Hillard in a Road to Glory match, this is a road that Shocka has traveled once before. Now the #1 Stunna himself takes on the self proclaimed hardkore icon Shane hillard. What makes him hardcore? Did he ascend to the top rope grab his opponent lock him in for the #1 Stunna and drop from the top turnbuckle to the floor through a table?! Oh wait that was Shocka at Faith. Does Shane have a proven record in eVolution in hardcore matches?! Does Shane know what he has in store for him with a man as powerful as Shocka not holding back anything? The Road to Glory: The road to glory could prove to be a bumpy one for Shocka it could prove to be one that he will have to travel on his own. He lost his Continental title because Shodan cheated to win and now he has been excluded from a match to decide a true champion. Shocka was the greatest champion in all of eVolution history and now he is being shoved to the bottom rung and being forced to fight his way to the top. He has been on the road to glory before only to be beaten and taken back to the bottom in EdW but now Shocka is the best that he has ever been. He has proven himself time and time again and what has he gained? Some respect from a very small amount of people so this will let him open the gate for everyone to know and respect him. At the end of the Road to Glory you are allowed a shot at any title that you want…you simply get to pick the title you want and go after it. The choice will be simple for Shocka at which title he will set his focus onto. But the task at hand is what he first must address. He has to beat Shane Hillard…then he has to move on and beat another and another and perhaps another opponent. And while all of this is going on he has to try and keep his concentration off of Shodan and the continental title that was stolen from him. Shane Hillard: The road that Shane Hillard faces in front of him a rough one indeed. His first obstacle is to take out a man at the level of competition of Shocka. A man that is a former Continental champion and a fierce competitor. And on top of all of that he is infuriated about how he lost his title. And since he can’t get his damn hands on Shodan he will put them around the throat of Shane until he gasps for air. Until he is left unconscious and blue in the ring. And there is nothing that Shane can do as he doesn’t stand a Chance at beating Shocka. {The scene is set Shocka has lost his title to a lesser man he was beaten by a cruiser weight for the title he held for almost 2 months. He was cheated out of it plain and simple and now he is on the road to glory. His focus of course is not on his match but on his title.} ‘God damnit!’ A scream is heard as we cut into a hotel room. Shocka sits looking at himself in a mirror studying his own face. ‘How could I let him beat me?!’ He says with doubt in his voice. ‘I underestimated him that’s all…’ He says in a regretful voice. ‘No damnit he cheated its not my fault he didn’t want to win fairly!’ Shocka screams into the mirror staring deeper into his own eyes. ‘I should have taken the pills….I should have brought them with me and swallowed them it’s as simple as that. What was I thinking when I stopped taking them…’ Shocka speaks at himself angrily. ‘The anger is festering inside of me and there is nothing I can do to get it out…’ A sudden calmness comes over him as he continues to speak. ‘Yes there is…I can take my rage out on Mr. Hillard.’ His phone rings and he grabs it from his side he lifts it to his ear and turns it on and ensues in a conversation with whomever is on the other line. ‘Hello? Yeah I know what I have to do…yes it’s the Road to Glory…really you got some footage on him….alright yeah I will be right over…’ He slaps his phone shut and slides it back into his pocket as he grabs his keys and jacket. He slips on the jacket and spins his keys on his finger before slipping out the door in what seems to be a more tranquil mood.
