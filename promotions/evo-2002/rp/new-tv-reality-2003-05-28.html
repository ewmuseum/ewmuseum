New-TV Reality!

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-28 New-TV Reality The scene opens and someone can be seen prowling around, hiding in some bushes. The unknown person is incognito and tries to remain secretive. A rustling can be heard from the bush and the person seems some what anxious. The person shoots from behind the fern and rises to his feet, he has a cracked out look on his face. The man with an unorganized blonde afro puff and has a tall, skinny build. The man walks back and forth and mumbles to himself. 'You can do this! This is your day and no one can take that from you... Now lets do what no smart man has ever done before' The man says to himself. The strange character walks up to a door and breathes heavily, 'You do this!' he says under his breath. The man raises his hand and holds it near the door and motions to knock, but each time his hand does not hit the door. The man starts to hyper ventilate looks to be very nervous. The man closes his eyes and has a bizarre look on his face, he knocks on the door lightly. The man jumps back and stares all around with the sketchiest of looks on his face. 'Looks like no one is here!' he proclaims and turns around to leave. 'State your business... I shoot trespassers on sight!' someone announces. The man turns around and Morpheus Omega can be seen with an assault rifle in his hands and a crazed smirk on his face. The man has a red dot shining on his forehead from a laser scope. The man with a dumbfounded look on his face, he goes to speak and nothing comes out. The man looks like he has seen death itself, he holds his hands showing there is no opposition of threat. 'My name is Ronnie Size! I am the newest on eVolution staff for reporting and tonight will be the first edition of New-TV Reality!' Ronnie explains. 'Oh Really?' Morpheus questions and lowers the rifle from being aimed at the man's forehead. 'If I would of known that, I would of shot you the first chance I had... I will give you ten seconds to run and after that I will fire and release the dogs to finish what's left of you!' 'This is really something else, I came up with the beautiful name like New-TV Reality and you still want to have me shot and killed? Im not even eVolution reporting, I just thought this could be a huge break. I will leave, but I will take my sweet ass time. Shooting me, would be doing me a favor!' Ronnie retorts and has a very depressed look on his face, he frowns. 'Wait...' Morpheus says and puts the gun down inside of the house, he walks out and continues talking 'You don't even work for eVolution and you had the nerve to walk to my door and say what you just did... You have attitude and an edge, I like that!' 'Really?' Ronnie questions with an excited look on his face. 'No...' Morpheus replies with a sarcastic grin. 'Oh...' Ronnie frowns once again and turns around. 'Where are you going?' Morpheus questions and continues speaking 'I thought this was the first edition of the most deranged show on the airways, New-TV Reality!' 'Thank you soooo much Omega, you know I have been a huge fan and have followed your work since back in the NrCW through Resurrection and Fedwars to FDW and until now! You are helping me out a lot and maybe I can now become the reporter I have always wanted to be...' 'Where did you find the eVolution camera man?' Morpheus asks. 'They have a camera crew around your house and following you like 24-7 from what I hear, they never know when they will catch you with some kind of public scandal. Even CNN, the tabloids and one of Jay Leno's crews are parked out front of your house!' Ronnie explains to Morpheus 'Maybe I was going to shoot the wrong person then!' Morpheus laughs to himself. 'Can we get started with the interview Mr. God?' Ronnie questions and snickers. 'Never call me Mr. God or even God again, failure to follow instruction could cost you your life...' Morpheus reasons with him and continues... 'Lets get started little man!' 'First off Morpheus, I would like to congratulate you for drugging the Syndicate and keeping their mouths shoot for an entire night... With HBC that would be the first time, if you know what I mean...' Ronnie slaps his knee and laughs at his cheesy joke. 'Lets be serious here, I don't have time for messing around... Damn amateur!' Morpheus complains and shakes his head, he know bares an impatient look on his face. 'You have now held the eVolution tag titles three months and during that reign became the official Unified tag champions. Some would say your the sickest team to ever compete in eVolution, would you like to comment on this?' Ronnie questions and smiles. 'I would have to say your stating the obvious... If you are going to make it with this job, you will have to start trying a lot harder then that kid!' Morpheus explains to him. 'At from the Ashes, you walked with an undefeated night for New-TV and all members took home victories and I must comment, very impressive work. How would you comment on such allegations as you screwing MLX out of his eVolved championship in the ladder match?' Ronnie asks. 'I would have to say Malcolm Luther Xavier got what was coming to him, I am sorry to say. New-TV always steals the show and it was only a matter of time before Hedges took what was rightfully his. MLX was not screwed out of the title, but out smarted by the best in the business' Morpheus answers and continues 'MLX is a legend in his time, but does not compare to any of us!' 'I would definitely have to agree with you' Ronnie replies with another cracked out look on his face, he stares off into space like he was looking right through Morpheus. 'Right, but get on with your interview Ronnie!' Morpheus instructs him. 'Ohhhhhhhhh my bad, I kind of drifted off... Where are we?' Ronnie questions and pretends to shuffle through some papers. 'Here we are! How would you comment on the Syndicates performance and attempts at winning your championships?' he asks with a serious look on his face. 'They are really two of a kind, I mean all BDD has is dreams and I think heartbreak chick is only heart broken over the fact she doesn't come home with this every night. That team defines the word jealousy... This Legends will be their final straw!' Morpheus answers. 'They have been foolish, thinking they can out battle or trick the deadliest athlete in the game and the queen of darkness... With all of the chances they have been given at winning the titles, they probably feel that victory is only a stepping stone away, but miles at the same time!' Ronnie was boldly and snickers to himself, he gets carried away with his pathetic, evil wanna-be laugh. 'We almost done?' Morpheus questions and looks at his watch. 'Look at the time..' Ronnie says and continues speaking 'On Legends you compete against MLX and the Syndicate in a triple fall match with Sky Night and Hedges in your corner, how do you feel about all of New-TV's gold championship gold on the line in such a high risk match?' Ronnie asks. 'I feel the same as I would defending my titles any night, sorry for the team who will feel the endless destruction of New-TV and the unholy wrath of the Infinite of evil... I could care less if Malcolm was balling his eyes out over the fact, he lost his title to something greater then he has ever known or if the Syndicate want to make alleged accusations of us drugging their water... I mean its pathetic the excuses the three make for themselves these days. The three of them are good for each other and will need to get used to each other because they could very well end up sharing a hospital room after Wednesday night! I feel very confident and secure walking in this match because once again we ALWAYS steal the show!' Morpheus replies with a crazed look. 'Thank you for being so cooperative tonight Morpheus for our first edition of New-TV Reality... I look forward to watching the match myself!' Ronnie barks out. 'Good catch name, New-TV Reality... I think you have the job!' Morpheus replies. 'What do you mean, what job?' Ronnie asks with another sketched look. 'New-TVs personal reporter with this show you call New-TV Reality, you have showed you have the parts and took the initiative to want the job. You can start on Legends if you can make it until then!' Morpheus laughs with a downright evil tone and grins. 'What do you mean if I make it until then?' Ronnie asks with an estranged voice. 'Release the dogs!' Morpheus says and continues speaking... 'Good night Ronnie Size, I hope to see you another day. You know how to find yourself out right?' he asks. You have to be kidding me...' Ronnie says and laughs, he all of a sudden doesn't find it to funny. Dogs can be heard barking loudly, their vicious noise screams through the silence of the night. Ronnie screams like a school girl and turns running... A pack of eight pit bulls can be seen tearing dirt and running full force at him. Morpheus enters his home and closes the door. Ronnie runs and disappears behind a grouping of trees... 'No.. No.. Nooooooooooooooo!' screaming can be heard from the camera and the scene becomes distorted. The view jolts when the camera hits the ground and dogs with teeth raised tear into the overweight, obese camera man. Blood sprays on the lens and the scene fades to horrified screaming from the painful attacks of the guard dogs.
