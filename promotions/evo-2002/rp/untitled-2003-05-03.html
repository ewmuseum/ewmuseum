Untitled

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens on beautiful, white sparking sand on a Hawaiian beach... A guy wearing a Hawaiian shirt and straw hat comes stumbling down the beach with two coconut drinks in his hands, the man seems to be incoherent and very intoxicated. The man trips over his own feet, he falls face first into the sand and remains motionless a moment. 'That man looked over and realized I was topless and fell flat on his face, the buzzard probably has a mouthful of sand too...' The voice of Sky Night can be recognized and heard. The scene pans. Sky Night can be seen reading a novel, wearing bikini bottoms and topless, she lies on a beach towel sun tanning. Sky has a pair of sunglasses covering her eyes. Morpheus lies on his stomach and has his face buried into his t-shirt. Sky chuckles to herself watching the man. 'Amusing, it really is...' Sky continues and shakes her head side to side, still in disbelief of what happened. Sky waiting for the man to get back to his feet, she giggles. Moments pass, the man shows sign of life and can be seen struggling about, trying to push himself out of the sand. The man rolls and sits up quickly. The man spits and rubs his face. The man with an annoyed, bothered look about him. 'Stupid rock buried in the sand... I stubbed my toe all because of that stupid stone...' The man says angered, he growls loudly. 'I oughta...' The man stops speaking mid sentence, he turns his head and stares at Sky with an awestruck look. The man notices that Sky Night cannot help or contain herself from laughing at him. The man's face becomes all flushed and he fixes his hat. The man gets back to his feet covered in sand, he still has his drinks in his hand, unharmed and never spilt a drop. The man shakes his head and looks very embarrassed. 'Careful..' Sky hollers. 'You might hurt yourself stumbling around like that. Maybe next time you will fall on something not so forgiving like sand...' she comments with an intrigued grin. The mans's face becomes dark red, he looks very upset and storms down the beach. The man doesn't look back for anything and Sky snickers to herself. Sky stands and wipes more sun tanning lotion on her arms, smiling. Sky rubs and works the lotion into her skin. 'Legends is going to be absolutely amazing when I finally prove to HBC that she is nothing but second grade trash...' Sky says and pauses a moment. 'Sure Heartbreak you are cute and all and have an attractive figure, but you only have the looks and not the sexy attitude. What's one without the other?' 'Besides...' Sky continues speaking. 'I have some personal issues with that twinkie pop tart... She feels its justified to be stabbing my man across the chest with a knife, during our match? That nimrod is lucky that we only doused her man with gasoline and lit him on fire. Them two made the wrong move, so we had to retaliate and seek revenge. We will do whatever is necessary to prove we are the best team in all of eVolution, retain our championship gold and make every other team's employment, living hell...' 'What are you on about now?' Morpheus questions and sits up, he yawns and stretches like he had just awakened from a twelve hour sleep. 'You're twisted! 'Who asked you anything...' Sky replies defensively. 'I will beat her hands down in that competition, then beat her face until she looks like a whole new person... I wouldn't wanna mess with her beautiful looks or anything' Sky nods sarcastically and snickers. 'I have my first week off after not having one for how long, I am enjoying my vacation and must you talk about work? I really don't wanna hear it, Im serious too...' Morpheus demands. 'And who are you to tell me, what I can and cant talk about? Do you think you are my father, and can tell me what to do?' Shy questions and then screams. 'HUH?' 'Have we had a few to many drinks?' Morpheus questions and speaks like he was talking down to her. 'I don't have the patience to be dealing with this right now, can you save this for at least Wednesday, the night of the show? I want to relax and actually enjoy my vacation!' 'Why would you question me drinking, like it was some kind of issue?' Sky questions bitterly. You know how many times I would have to listen to you ramble on about your wrestling career??? HBC claims to be the hottest diva in all of eVolution, she will never live up to those standards in my pretty green eyes. You can do whatever the fuck you want during your vacation, I am on personal business myself and could care less if you even attended the show. I could handle this with my hands tied behind my back, wearing a blind fold over my eyes and swaggering drunk... HBC lives my reality now, she has gotten under the wrong skin!' she nods with a sick, twisted look on her face... Sky snarls. 'We will be seeing lots of skin from the both of you on Legends... Not attend the show, I would miss eVolution's first lingerie competition with you and HBC. Not a chance, I will definitely be there. I can see you two rolling around the ring with barely any clothes on, fighting to the death. This will be the sexiest athleticism I have ever seen. Make eVolution history!? Morpheus retorts. 'I am starting to grow very suspicious about you two, HBC and yourself. I have heard rumor about you two celebrating in the locker room with a bottle of champagne, when you had the last laughs after destroying Dreamer and Mason. I don't think your story matches up...' Sky accuses him. 'How many times do I have to tell you, nothing happened with me and HBC... I am starting to grow slightly annoyed with your wrongful accusations... I don't know what shenanigans you think I have been pulling, but I want your delusional, paranoid thoughts kept to yourself... Understand me?' Morpheus questions with an irate voice, he lies back down on his towel and buries his face into his t-shirt again. The scene fades with Sky Night standing with an astonished look on her face.
