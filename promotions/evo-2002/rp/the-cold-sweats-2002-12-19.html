The Cold Sweats

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/02 {The screen is covered in black. Nothing to be seen, then the audio fades in first. You make out the voice as Xander on the phone. As you listen in on the conversation, the video feed slowly fades in to show the interior of, what looks to be, a loading dock. Most likely the Xcel Energy Arena. As Xander paces on his cell phone, you pay more attention then not} 'Yeah, it's all set man. Still some skepticism, but they're coming along.' Xander starts, reassuring the other half of the conversation. 'They'll understand why they're being left in the dark. This CAN NOT get out before hand. It's like a loved one ruining the surprise by guessing their Christmas present before the opening. This will be a Christmas present no one will ever forget.' {Just them Xander notices the camera} 'Man. Gotta go. They're stalking me again' Xander says. 'Yeah.... Nightmare Before Christmas.... No problem.... Heh.. yeah, that's the plan.... OK, see ya then. Later.' {Xander hangs up the phone, then focuses on the camera crew} 'Don't you have any respect for people's privacy?' Xander rhetorically asks. 'A little me time on the phone would be nice around here. But hell, since you're here, I got a few people to talk to.' 'First off.' Xander states as he starts pacing again, thinking about his words.. choosing them carefully. 'Let's start with the OMEN Brotherhood. Tag team title match. Many people have wondered who will represent Unlimited in the match. Well, each of us have a singles match that night, but, I decided to let Asis tag with me to defend them. C has a hell in a cell match to think about, and it's her chance to prove her worth. This is a rebuilding period. Out with the weak links, and continue with the strong. Only the strong will survive. Me... Asis... Luther Wisdom.. Xavier Citizen. As we all know.. I'm the one who retired Malcolm King... a mentor, trainer, and who knows what else to these two men. Asis is a destruction machine, and integral part of the tag team title reign. With me directing traffic, and holding up my half.. this match will do nothing but eliminate the OMEN Brotherhood from eVolution wrestling, once and for all. Taking this stable war to a whole new level.' {Xander takes a few breathes and he continues to pace, until stopping in his tracks and staring at the camera} 'Then' He continues. 'We have Seara. You finally get your chance little girl. You get to take a shot at the big bad wolf. You never fully understood why I 'harassed' you all those many weeks. You never understood what my problem was with you. Should I let you in on a little secret?' Xander asks.'You want to know what my beef was, and is with you? Let me shed some light on the subject.' 'There is no reason. No, personal reason, anyway. It is simply Darwinism at it's best. Eliminate the weak, and the strong will get stronger. In a pack of animals... any sickly, or elderly... basically, any of the pack that is weak, the rest of the pack will eliminate. Well, unfortunately around here, most people don't seem to care, or, you've slept your way around to get enough protection. That's fine. That's another natural affect of trying to carve one's own survival out. You're not one of the less educated ones, I'll give you that... but, you still are weak. You MUST be eliminated. And I will do just that.' 'Don't worry too much though, I'm sure that Benedict Arnold will be there to nurse your wounds, like the pussy whipped bitch he is. Well, until Asis tears him a new orafice or two. Then you both can share a hospital room. eVolution has no room for people like you. You are the vermin that scatters disease over a country. You are the bug that kills millions with your own weaknesses. You are the poison... and we... Unlimited.. are the cure. eVolution will soon be cleansed of all it needs. It will be pure again. By the hands of Unlimited, we will unleash a horror of unspeakable terror upon eVolution wrestling... and no one, and nothing can stop it. The wheels have been set in motion... things will change. Evolution as a company will change... and it will never... ever be the same again. The Nightmare has started.... and unholy alliance of mind, body, and soul has been set in motion. The Winter, Fall, Summer, and Spring's fury all put into one. We are UNLIMITED.' {Xander walks away leaving the camera man standing, until it fades out}
