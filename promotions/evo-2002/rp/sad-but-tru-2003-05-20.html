Sad but 'tru'

20 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-20 ~The way most people grow up is considered normal…sure there is a select few that take there problems and pay prices not even Somers could afford for a physiatrist. People just need to find a way to express their feelings and the way that wrestlers do it is through violence. The way that people cope with problems aggravates Shocka…and why wouldn’t it…when people bitch, moan and cry about things that they can’t change what is it getting done? Why not put the frustration into something productive like say body building…something that Shocka does whenever he gets the chance…its one of his many ways of self expression. Now you may ask yourself why can Shocka be so angry at a man like Ronan Pierce?~ ‘How could he attack me from behind?’ Shocka said to Scott Frost who sat across the room from him staring into a wall. ‘How could he betray us like that?!’ Frost screamed still not looking away from the wall. ‘Dude can we have a little less pissed off time and a little more stunna therapy?’ Shocka said in a sarcastic tone. ‘Do you not understand the magnitude of what has happened to us?’ Frost said finally directing his attention toward Shocka. ‘Oh what is the big deal with the Tru One…’ Shocka said being cut off. ‘What’s the big deal?! He knows me and you better than anyone in the world…shit he brought you into EdW in a big way he taught you things that you needed to know and you told him things that he could use against you later!’ Frost said standing up and walking back and forth throughout the room. ‘You need to sit down…and focus think about your belt.’ Shocka said. ‘How in the hell can I when I know that Krowe could be running around with Xander…running around with the Unlimited, with the NTICW cronies…’ Frost said. ‘Wait…I get what you’re saying…he and Ronan could be talking about me right now…’ Shocka said with a look of rage on his face. ‘Now you see why I am so incredibly pissed off!’ Frost yelled as he flipped the glass coffee table in the room. ‘How long do you think he has been talking to those Unlimited fuckers?’ Shocka asked showing the same concern as Frost. ‘Its hard to tell right now…I mean he can work anyone and everything to his advantage I wouldn’t be surprised if he was against us since the beginning…’ Frost said spelling it out for Shocka. ‘Have you talked to him since he left with Xander last week?’ Shocka asked. ‘Nope…haven’t even seen him.’ Frost said with a bit of disappointment in his voice. ‘I’d expect better but who knows what they were talking about…’ ‘Listen I’ll try and find him on Wednesday and try and get some answers from him and see what is going on…’ Shocka said to Frost trying to reassure him. ‘Yeah, that sounds…good.’ Frost said grimly as he walked out of the room. ‘Jesus…’ Shocka muttered under his breath. ‘I haven’t seen him that pissed since the two times that I beat his ass…’ Shocka said to himself. ~Frost’s head peeked back into the door with a sort of half smile.~ ‘You thought I was gone eh?’ Frost questioned Shocka with a slight smile. ‘Uhh…I was just joshin ya…’ Shocka said as he turned facing the wall again ‘I knew he was there…’ Shocka said trying to convince himself ‘Still here Stunna boy…’ Frost said as he still peered in. ‘God damnit go away so I can talk trash on you!’ Shocka said with a laugh. ‘He sorry I couldn’t help you out with the Ronan problem I just got a lot on my mind man…’ Scott said as he shut the door. ‘Yeah me too…’ Shocka said to himself. ‘Like partying!’ ~Shocka ripped his tear away pants from his legs and stood in his boxers dancing in front of a corner mirror. He soon took off his shirt and flexed yelling at himself in the mirror.~ ‘Who does Ronan think he is anyways…he can’t handle this!’ Shocka said palming his right bicep. ‘And he doesn’t stand a chance against this…’ Shocka yelled slapping his left bicep and smiling in the mirror.
