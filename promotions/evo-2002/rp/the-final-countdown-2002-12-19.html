The final countdown

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ottawa, Canada Royal Canadian Parliament Building ‘Tis was The Nightmare before Christmas, and all through the house, not a creature was stirring, not even a mouse, and as the children hung their stockings with care, I’ll be hanging Big Daddy Dreamer fifteen feet up in the air……’ HBC relishes out to a group of twenty children under the auspicious of the Canadian Government. Many of the Parliamentary members start looking at each other in frantic as HBC changed the classic tale of Christmas to suit her own needs. But hey, screw them, they invited me here to light their tree of there’s. As HBC finishes the tale, she signs a bunch of autographs for the kids, poses for the camera which will splatter her picture all across the countryside as the first non-Prime Minister to ever light the Royal Tree of Canada. She smiles and flashes a wink over to Prime Minister Cheric who escorts her over to the switch. The countdown begins, HBC’s licks her lips as it approaches zero and flips the switch on the hundred foot Canadian Pine illuminating the square several blocks in every direction. If these simpletons only really knew what I thought about all this. Sure, it’s a great honor being the first non Prime Minister bestowed this task, but I have better things to do. Still the publicity is always good. Especially when its about me she says with a devilish delight. She notices off in the distance a podium has been set up meaning that there is more to this lighting ceremony than meets the eye. Interesting…..I wonder…. Wouldn’t that be a hoot she thinks to herself. Upon completing that thought, two members of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, decked out in full ceremonial gear escort HBC over a red carpeted area surrounding the tree. I’ll be damned…… they are. Delightful! Absolutely fucking delightful. Guess I better be on restrained behavior. The RCMP walk HBC over to the area where Cheric is awaiting her and ask her to kneel down as he pulls out a sword and taps her on each shoulder, proclaiming her to the people and to the world, HBC is now a full fledged Ladied member of the Canadian Royal Order. A special distinction being that she is the only female member of the C.R.O. Lady HBC. Damn! I thought I reached a high-point two years ago being Knighted by the Queen, but now a full Ladyship. Will wonders never cease! Cheric shakes HBC’s hand as a little thought runs through her head. Hey I could run for Parliament now, whether here or in the United Kingdom. Wouldn’t that be a gas! She poses a few times for the camera knowing this historic day will forever be immortalized as she comes up to the podium to address the crowd and Parliament in attendance. ‘Prime Minister Cheric, friends and citizens of Canada. I gladly accept this honor knowing all the ramifications of being bestowed a full Ladyship. History will forever remember this day as I, one of your own people, will have the dubious distinction of being the first ever appointed Lady of the Canadian Royal Order. Knowing its long the storied history of the C.R.O. I promise to bring it into the forefront again as the protector of Canada from all enemies both from outside and within.’ A round of applause rings out from the crowd. ‘Saying that, there is one potential enemy that must firmly be rooted out and destroyed once and for all. A citizen who has renounced their ties to our great country of Canada. A former icon of the people now turned terrorist for hire. That individual is none other than Big Daddy Dreamer! Our people watched in horror as he tried to single-handedly destroy the city of Toronto, and yours truly. A man with total disregard for our way of life. And if it wasn’t for a truly courageous citizen running him down with their own vehicle, who knows how much more damage that madman would have caused. Big Daddy Dreamer is a menace to society and needs to be stopped at once. Which is why as my first task as a member of the C.R.O. I will stop this madman once and for all.’ Another applaud rings out from the crowd, but HBC catches someone standing in the back looking at her, motioning for her to speak with him. A slight smirk comes across her face as she nods. ‘I will stop this madman as a gift to the fine citizens of Canada. Consider this my Christmas present to you all. At Evolution’s Nightmare Before Christmas, I have challenged Big Daddy Dreamer in a match so dangerous, that I am putting my very life on the line. I challenged Big Daddy Dreamer in the match he made famous, a match that his record inside the beast is unparalleled and will never be matched by mortal men. A match in which he has only lost in on 3 occasions. That match is…….The Hell in the Cell!’ A collective gasp echo’s out. Indeed even they know what his record is inside that monster. ‘Big Daddy Dreamer, in his lifetime is an impressive 102-3 inside the Hell in the Cell. Though I am no stranger to being inside that structure as well, even I’ll admit, I’ve never been in that type of match one-on-one. It has always been in a tag team, fatal war games type of match which I’ve been 12-0. But that is misleading because the man I’m going to face was my partner in those matches. Am I concerned…..yes. Am I afraid…..no. I have a lot of material on my opponent in addition to my own first hand observations. At a Nightmare Before Christmas, the world will watch first-hand the sheer destructive force of the Hell in the Cell, unlike anything witnessed in the history of the wrestling world before. Big Daddy Dreamer will fall to the Kiss of Death, as his spirit will be broken by the cage! No longer will BDD be able to terrorize us, our people, our way of life as he is permanently put away, once and for all! This I promise to you all. Thank you…..and Merry Christmas.’ ‘The audience erupts in approval as HBC shakes several members of Parliament’s hand before heading over to the mysterious individual, The Syndicate head of security, Thomas Draven. They exchange pleasantries as Draven pulls out a manila envelope and hands it to HBC. Puzzled, HBC opens it up and notices it’s a letter which she starts reading. ‘Dear HBC, It has come to our attention that the war between you and Big Daddy Dreamer in all probability won’t come to an end anytime soon. We beg you to reconsider and put an end to these hostilities between you both immediately. Failure to do can and will result in some undesired, catastrophic actions. None of us would like to see that happen to you or Syndicate International. In short, get your ass in gear and work it out between the both of you. Otherwise, we’ll do it for you and our results are guaranteed to work. Sincerely, The Collective’ A look of rage comes across HBC’s face as she crumples up the letter and grabs Draven by the collar. Draven slaps her hands away as HBC backs off. She can’t be mad at him, he’s just a messenger. ‘Draven…..who gave you this letter?’ ‘I don’t know ma’am. It was on my desk leading me to believe we may have a mole among us. Security is looking into it for we believe as your expression on your face concurs, we don’t believe this to be a joke or a hoax.’ ‘Dreamer! It has to be him. Re-double your efforts to find the source of this and make them pay dearly for it. I authorize it, Priority 1 Alpha. Find Dreamer and this so-called Collective! Destroy the Collective, I’ll handle BDD myself.’ ‘As you wish my Lady’ ‘Now if you’ll excuse me, I have a meeting with the Defense-General. He’s very interested in some our recent breakthroughs…….
