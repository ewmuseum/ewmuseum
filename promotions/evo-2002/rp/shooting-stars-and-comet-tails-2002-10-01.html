Shooting Stars and Comet Tails...

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-1 Matt Boone sits, clutching the Rising Star Championship in his heavily muscled arms. The Title still reads Asis, as Boone wouldn't allow anyone in the backstage area to correct the problem. Boone cradles the title in his arms and slowly rises stretching out. Boone stares right into the camera, his eyes darker than they were two weeks ago, the last time he made a public statement. Boone puts the title on his shoulder and starts as if to speak, but just yawns. Cori Simmons then arrives into the camera's view, and stares into the camera... Cori simply smiles, and steps aside as Boone does speak this time. Matt: So, this is what it is to be a champion... this is what it takes. And this is how it feels to have all of those uncaring hypocrites boo you when you walk out into an arena. This is how it feels to FINALLY come to my senses. Some of you wonder what were you thinking Matt, what did you do this for? Me, I wonder why didn't I do this earlier? All of you who boo me now, ALL of you are my enemies... my actions have been a harbinger of progress, of success... something I hadn't seen before... and yet you despise me for it? What have I done to you, why have you forsaken me? I was just a good kid who wanted to win a match or two, you are the ones who created the villain out of it. How am I supposed to extract the revenge against Tyler Davidson that I so desire without extracting it via wins? The average person that loses everytime isn't going to be taken seriously. I think that you people would prefer to see me winless, you people would be much happier if I never held a victory. Yeah, that's it isn't it? None of you people want to see my hand raised, you just want to see your pulp heroes like Tyler Pierce get their wins... sure you liked me for a bit, when I was teaming with him, when I was the muscle behind his operation. Well no longer will I be hired muscle for a man that doesn't give two cares about my own personal mission. No longer shall I allow my mind to be warped by someone that would prefer me to mourn the loss of my grandfather and the greatest screwjob that has been seen in Evolution against me by keeping quiet and 'fighting the good fight'. And no longer am I going to talk to you pathetic fans who have held me down too long. Simmons- Tyler, can you hear it... that's the sounds of your reign coming to an end. What you've done to myself and to the individual sitting next to me is beyond repair... there's no way to bridge the gap now. What happened to Troy is just a warning shot... he was too good for you... ratings are going up on OSE... that's not good... something must be done. What's our plan? The systematic elimination of every person that is making this company a success. Troy was first, who is next is anyone's guess. Don't think we can pull it off? Call up Pierce and find out how he's feeling... he might not answer the phone though. Or ask Justin Credible what happened last time I told him that the end was near for his career. This is a very deadly game... and it won't be us getting hurt... With that, Simmons waves at Boone and the two walk off, the Rising Star Title dragging on concrete as he walks away
