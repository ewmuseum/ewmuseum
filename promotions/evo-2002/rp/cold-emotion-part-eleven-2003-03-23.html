Cold Emotion... part eleven

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens with Morpheus Omega holding a look-a-like Elvis Prestly by the collar of his shirt and high into the air. Morpheus has a mean and aggressive look on his face as he continues to shake the man violently up and down. The man screams desperately for help. 'Security, somebody or anybody come and help quick!' the man screams. 'Shut the hell up, you know where he is. Don't you?... I bet you know where to find him you fake ass mother fucker!' Morpheus retorts. The man having problems breathing now, 'Whooooo???' he barely gets the words out of his mouth and loud choking noises can be heard as the man gasps for breath and his face turns bluish red. Having mercy on the man, Morpheus drops him to his feet and turns slamming him into the opposite wall. The man cringes in pain. 'Don't play stupid with me, you know who I am looking for. The pitiful Wayne Newton. You fake elvis fucks and Newton are in conspiracy, you all work together and think you are so damn good' Morpheus shakes his head with multiple eye twitches and waits for a response. 'Of course we all work together, this is...' The man gets cut off. 'Where do you stand now? You are guilty by association before the fact and now you admit that you all work with each other. Something fishy is going on here and I don't like it' Morpheus proclaims. 'If you would let me finish my...' Morpheus cuts him off again. 'Your not going to be finishing anything because you have met the end of the line with me and you are not going anywhere until I find that dead fuck, Newton!' Morpheus screams wildly. 'Sentence...' The man squeezes the word in time to say anything and adjusts his collar and coughs trying to regain his regular breathing pattern. 'I would tell you what I meant by that...' The man continues speaking and sounds a little under the weather. 'We all entertain in viva Las Vegas and of course I know where to find him. I know of him, I don't know him personally and maybe if you asked me nicely and never violated my personal space, I might of thought about telling you. You are in some big trouble now chump!' the man says and flinches. Reaching back far to throw a heavy bomb, Morpheus thrusts into the shot aiming for the man's head. At the very last second his wrist is caught and redirected towards the side of him. Morpheus stumbles and locks eyes with Hedges who stands with an impatient look on his face. Elvis continues to tremble with both hands blocking his face. 'You have to stop disappearing on me like that... I never know what kind of trouble I will find you in and I should of figured you were harassing someone. What's this guys buzz?' Hedges questions. 'The imposter little fuck, he knows where Newton is and he's with holding valuable information to me. I want to know now!' Omega demands. Without thinking twice Morpheus lunges at the man again, but Hedges has his wrist tightly in his hand and pulls him away from the innocent, elvis dressed character. Morpheus rages getting into the face of Hedges and does not back down for a second. 'What the hell is wrong with you?' Morpheus questions Hedges. 'With the heat you have on yourself right now, you need to stop fucking around and stop causing these scenes. You act like you are destined to spend time in prison and this definitely would give you a worst image being dragged from a casino and arrested before Legends XXX... Think about it Morpheus!' Hedges screams trying to reason with Morpheus. 'Fine, but he best tell us where to find Newton now!' Morpheus screams into the face of Hedges and with his free hand grabs the shoulder of the Elvis look-a-like who was trying to escape. 'Security!' The man screams... 'Someone help!' 'What will it take for you to tell this giant fuck where to find Newton, name your game and then we can reason!' Hedges states. 'What's this look like, the capital pay off city of the world?' The man questions with a cocky tone of voice. 'Actually, yeah it is!' Hedges replies with an odd look on his face. 'Oh yeah... I forgot from all the blood rushing to my head! You pay me two hundred dollars and I wont have security have you thrown out, arrested and I will tell you where to find Wayne Newton' he daringly throws out the offer and both Morpheus and Hedges deeply contemplate. 'Tell us or we will bash your skull in!' Morpheus screams. 'Not so fast Morpheus, let me handle this one' Hedges redirects his attention back to the brave man to actually made such a sketchy offer. 'How about this!' Hedges continues... 'We give you one hundred dollars and draw us a map for our own convenience. You will not press anything higher then a C note from me and you are lucky you are getting anything in the first place!' 'What's going on down there!' someone screams. The scene pans and they are now identified as two security guards with mag flashlights in their hands and held shoulder high. The guards have concerned looks on their faces as they eye down the suspicious looking duo. 'Two hundred or nothing!' the man mutters. Security approach quickly and Morpheus releases hold of the look-a-like and has a plastic, fake look on his face. Both security look back and forth at all of the men and question the elvis imposter. 'There any problems here?' the one security guard questions. 'Actually we have big problems...' The man states with a serious tone of voice but hesitates from finishing the sentence. Behind the midst of the confusion with everyone standing close and bundled up, you see Hedges slap a wad of bills into the man's hand and then scowls at him with a mean look in his eyes. 'These men are unable to find the greatest attraction of all of Vegas, they need to find out where Wayne Newton is playing tonight!' The man says with a more cheerful voice then from before. 'Greatest?' The one security guard says. 'Attraction?' The other security guard questions. 'I can gladly tell you where to find the next playing show and actually I could even give you some tickets. These things are basically given away around here these days. Last I heard, he started smoking crack and the show went even further down the shitter' he shakes his head. 'Newton definitely puts the term 'Rock' into rock star...' The other security guard says and continues 'The pop can rock star maybe!' The security guard reaches into his pocket and hands Morpheus two tickets with bold lettering reading 'Wayne Newton'. The eyes of Morpheus light up with flames burning in them strongly. An evil grin arches on his face and he turns to Hedges. 'This is what were doing tonight... We are going to pay our old buddy Newton a little visit. You down or not?' Morpheus questions. 'Why the hell not' Hedges replies and sounds like he is resentful towards going and like he doesn't approve of the idea one bit. 'Catch you around!' The Elvis imposter walks off the scene flipping through a wad of green bills with a big smile on his face, he nods and winks to Omega. 'Enjoy yourselves at the show tonight...' The one security officer says and continues speaking 'Or at least try to because I hear this is quite the dive!' The two security guards disappear off the scene and Morpheus has the same big grin on his face. Hedges looks like he is fuming and like he is very upset about something. 'Free tickets to the show, how can you beat that?' Omega questions. 'I know something better, two hundred dollar ones because that is what those tickets really just cost me! Free???' Hedges questions. 'Relax and when we hit an ATM machine, I will pay you back the cash. You don't even know how bad I want to wrap my hands around Wayne's woman like throat again. Last time I put his bitch ass right through a table. Looks like his nightmare has come back to haunt him and its ironic because Shocka has found himself in the exact same predicament...' Morpheus says holding the tickets like they were his last and only hope at having fun while in Las Vegas. The scene fades with both men walking down the corridor.
