A World of Nothing Act 3 - Life, Death, and Something Else.

22 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-22 Samantha Lynn. Or Sam. Or Vera, her stage name. Samantha, Sam, Vera. She was what ever single parent in America hoped their son wouldn't come home with. Samantha, Sam, Vera. She was an exotic dancer. Or an erotic dancer. Or a stripper, put easily. But, she insisted she wasn't any of the three. What she did, it was art. So what if it involved nudity? So what if there were a few sexual things? You hear Christians always complaining about pornography. You hear forty year old women yelling about nudity in movies is unacceptable. What they all fail to realize is that, if the world ended up like God intended, there would be sex on the streets. Or so she explained to me. For whatever reason, I'm diverted from going to the bathroom. I'm diverted from going back to my table. I'm entranced by this woman, who at this point is sitting on my lap. Looking down at me, she's saying something I'm supposed to consider important. In reality, I don't think it's important. Just the ramblings of a stripper... yet I'm intrigued to stay. 'I know what it's like to be dead,' She says, grinding her self against me. 'And I know what it's like to be alive. But, I'm neither.' It was this sort of thing that kept me there, in that chair. At this point, I was supposed to have a boner.... but it was as if her words were cutting off the circulation of my blood to my penis. There was no hard-on. Just me and a girl. Her rubbing herself against me, half naked, talking about stuff that is far too over my head. 'I believe that none of us are really alive, you know?' And grind. And grind. And grind. And stop. Repeat. 'I believe that we all think we're alive, but in reality we're just dying. There is no living.' 'We're all dying?' I don't know why I asked. 'We're all dying. One second at a time.' This chick. Samantha, Sam, Vera. This chick, she's making me feel as if I've never even been born. At this point? I'm not sure if I have been. Confusion is taking over at this point. All that's sensible begin evacuation of the head in three, two, one... Blast off. 'Twenty more bucks.': She mutters, not going on with her philosophy of life and death. And me? I eagerly shove a twenty dollar bill down into her bra.
