Legends XXX(2)

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.21.03 ~Inside of the Ghost Bar in the Palms hotel is Shocka he has a huge grin on his face and he stirs a drink with one hand and has his arm around a women. He looks into her eyes with a huge smile on his face and takes a drink of his screwdriver. The stool he leans against crinkles his Hawaiian shirt. His khaki pants look well pressed and his hair his gelled forward with just his bangs spiked upwards. He walks up to the bar and asks for a shot of rum he takes it straight and slams it down on the bar making a face.~ ‘That’s some strong shit…’ He says as he walks away with a drunken smile on his face. He sees and eVolution camera crew coming into the bar and smiles wildly almost running toward it they are here to shoot some promotional footage for a new eVo video package not to talk to Shocka. ‘Can I cut a promo?’ Shocka asks with obvious slurred speech. ‘We really aren’t here looking to talk to any wrestlers…’ One member of the crew speaks. ‘Wha?’ Shocka questions. ‘We are supposed to get some footage of the Vegas strip and Vegas itself…it’s a special assignment for legends XXX.’ The same man speaks. ‘No no no!’ Shocka yells. ‘You will have the privilege to talk to me!’ ‘Maybe for a couple minutes after we get our shots…’ The man speaks a little more aggravated. ‘Well is that piece of shit on?’ Shocka questions. ‘Of course we need to get a shot of the people.’ ‘Well fuck yeah then!’ Shocka screams. ~The camera moves past Shocka and it now cuts to its view. The camera moves outside to the balcony from the ghost bar and starts to look over some angles to hit.~ ‘Wait a fucking minute!’ You can hear the unruly voice of Shocka speak. ‘I’m the former Universal champion you can’t treat me this way!’ He yells. ‘Former is the main word you damn chump!’ The camera man screams as Shocka laughs and grabs the camera running from the crew. ~His shots are mostly of beautiful women scouring the bar and when he lifts it over his head you can see the angry crew chasing behind him.~ ‘Masked partner! Masked partner! I need you!’ Shocka screams in his drunken rampage as he drops the camera and just stands there studying what he has done. ‘I didn’t do it!’ He screams at the crew. ‘You are such a fucking idiot!’ the soundman screams. ‘I will report this to Mr. Somers.’ The camera man himself screams. ‘Its not even broken!’ Shocka says like an innocent little boy and picks it up with feed still coming from it but the lense is smashed. ‘I can see you!’ He screams to the man as they roll their eyes and just storm out of the room. ~About 15 minutes later security of Daniel Somers storm in and confiscate the camera from him leaving him alone in the bar as the scene fades.~
