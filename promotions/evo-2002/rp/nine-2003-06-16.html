Nine

16 June 2003

Date: 2003-06-16 Jacob's life had built up to this point for last 9 years it was on January 2nd 1985 that he could no longer take it. Every moment he had only thoughts of his son's future to comfort him and now it was beginning to look bleak for his little family so Jake bailed on his children and killed himself at their 9th birthday party. It had began as a beautiful day, Scott and Adam were allowed to stay home so they could prepare themselves for the spetacle that was to be their birthday party. All their friends had been invited and they couldn't wait to begin the festivities, at 4:00 the guests started arriving and by 4:30 the games had begun. The party dragged on 2 more hours before the gunshot rang through the air, Jacob had been watching the party for the past hour from his room whilst the other children's parents ran the party. Everybody got down when they heard the gunshot for fear that it might be a gang attack but then they looked into the window and saw the blood splatter, they saw Jake's faced pressed against the glass gun still pointed at his head. Scott and Adam fainted at the sight of that and later awoke in a boy's home were they would be cared for until some immediate relatives were found. Scott had forced that day out of his memory his entire life since then but it always comes back to him in his nightmares. He had awoken the day after the party in an unfamiliar environment and screamed, Adam had been waking up slowly until Scott screamed and then he was fully awake and ready to help his brother with whatever ailed him but then he had the same fears as his brother when he saw where they were. Scott and Adam had been put in a boys home were they usually 'straightened' the children out, it had been the only one that was willing to take 2 trauma cases such as them. The home was named, 'Jack Forrester's home for boys' Jack the owner was one of those people that believe what you were never caught of doing was okay so whilst the feds weren't looking he 'straightened' the children out by treating them like they were inmates, if someone did something back they got stuck in 'The Box' by themselves for 24 hours that is unless they screamed loudly enough for someone to call the cops. He also worked the children like there was no tommorow and somehow the children didn't go insane. Usually they would forget about their experiences and make sure not to do anything to be put back in there. Scott and Adam were the kids that everyone picked on for their first few weeks there, but in their 4th week one child nicknamed, 'The Giant fatty' took it to far and Scott felt his first real surge of adrenaline. TGF was what you would call overweight, he stood at 5'8' and weighed around 220 lbs., TGF had decided to make an example of Adam by beating him up. Adam took one punch to the head and he went down, Scott saw this and tackled TGF despite his little chances of knocking him over. TGF had the wind knocked out of him and then he recieved a broken nose and one scar on his cheek because of Scott's attack. Over the next few years Scott and Adam would contiously be a problem to this establishment, all of Jack's 'authority figures' would recieve beatings from Scott and Adam everytime they tried to harm either one of them. Jack began to realize that they would be the one's who would bring him down so he decided on a plan to end their 'reign of terror' over his little establishment.
