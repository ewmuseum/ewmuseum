Shadows Playing

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th December 2002 The festive season has begun. The most celebrated holiday in the year, throughout the entire world is almost upon us all. The celebration of the birth of Jesus Christ, the world’s once saviour. Although this meaning for Christmas has pretty much been forgotten. Of course there are still people who go to church on Christmas day, who see the birth of the son of God more important than what Santa Claus brings them, but this is a rare thing. Stockings over the fire place, the big meals with assortments of food, being with your family and friends, the presents, this is what Christmas now means to people. But there are some that don’t celebrate at all. Some to whom the 25th of December is just another day of the year. Of course these people are few in numbers as well, to most people Christmas has some sort of meaning. No matter what your religion is, no matter what your beliefs are, Christmas means something. But Asis has forgotten what the day is. He knows it’s supposed to be something special, but he has forgotten it because of the painful memories attached to it. The whole month of December is one he’d rather not existed, just because of that one day. And he’s hoping that Xander will let him express his feelings for Christmas on the entire eVo roster… You wake up from a restless night’s sleep, restless from the excitement of tomorrow being Christmas Day. Although tomorrow is now today. You lay in bed for a few more minutes, letting yourself get more excited until you can’t stand it any longer. You jump out of bed, pulling on your dressing gown and sliding on your slippers. Your room has been visited during the night and has been decorated with tinsel and a stocking has been left full of presents, free of charge as always. You fling open your bedroom curtains and are greeted by the sun reflecting off a world covered in glistening white gold. It snows almost everyday in Chicago during the winter months and normally it wouldn’t make a difference to you. But on a day like today, snow seems magical. Everything does. Ever so slowly, being as quiet as you can, you open your bedroom door, your stocking in your hand and creep down the hallway towards your parent’s room, avoiding the floor boards you know to be creaky. Even more carefully then you opened your own door, you open the door to your parent’s room, and creep in, trying to wake them up and surprise them. But as you walk in, as always, they’re sitting up in bed already waiting for you. You open the presents in your stocking and afterwards you go downstairs with Mum and Dad to find the larger presents that they’ve hidden in various places around the family room for you. The special game of hide and seek that is another magical touch your parents give you on Christmas day. After opening your presents you embrace your parents and they return the gesture, in one of those moments in your childhood that you want to last forever. Leaning towards your ear your Mother whispers “I love you Zak.” Squeezing her tighter you reply “I love you Mum.” Asis wakes up from the dream of one of the few happy times in his life. It seems such a long time ago now, part of someone else’s life. Everything stands out, still and back-lit, so different to the ever moving sludge of the present day. Nothing is real until it’s gone: before then it’s just shadows playing. Today is a joke accidental and flawed. Asis sits up on the floor, looking across at Xander lying on one of the two hotel beds, the other one meant for Asis. The dream is a memory, but to Asis it’s just his dormant mind playing tricks on him, that’s not how he remembers Christmas, the memories are much more painful than that. He looks up at the clock on the wall, seeing that the time is in the middle of the early hours of the morning, but regardless of that he cannot just stay in the hotel. It’s too nice for him, he doesn’t deserve it, besides he doesn’t feel comfortable. Already fully clothed as that’s how he’s used to sleeping, he gets up, grabs his trench coat and walks out the door and out the hotel onto the Minnesota streets. He walks around for a long time, thinking of nothing in particular and before he knows it, the sun is starting to rise. He walks to the Xcel energy centre and stands there looking up at the building, the last place eVo will be before it moves to The Target Centre for the Christmas pay per view. The last pay per view of the year. The last evolution event of the year, a year in which Asis has forged himself a name for people to fear within eVo. But the year is not over yet. Not by a long way…
