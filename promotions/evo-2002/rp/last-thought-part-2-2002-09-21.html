Last thought part 2

21 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: september 21st 2002 We find Seara in her hospital bed where she was last seen by the camera, she seems to be asleep, but her continuous muttering has been described by the nurses as the same kind of speeches she gives to the camera. It is unclear if she is in or out of consciousness, if she is dreaming or purposefully doing it. The cameraman sits down and focuses the lens onto the female’s face, this is possibly the most peaceful we have ever seen Seara, and the cameraman relaxes around her for the first time since he first meet her not long after she had joined eVo. Although the camera is filming, this is not a live TV broadcast like normal, this time, the camera is only allowed to record because some things have to legally be edited out, and with Seara not even knowing what she is doing or where she is half the time, it could prove difficult to make a live TV broadcast suitable. Realising this, the cameraman places his camera on a tripod and sits back with a cup of coffee, trying not to wake Seara, if she is even asleep. He is soon hypnotized by the peace and serenity of the wrestler; nothing seems to be bothering her. Nothing at all. Well, nothing until now, she begins to turn around violently, and lashes her fists out into the air; her moans turning into screams like the one’s that could be heard from her at Digital Destruction. She suddenly begins to talk to herself, her eyes firmly closed and no idea that the camera is filming. Her erratic movements stop and after a seemingly scary and stressful few minutes for her, she lays still and lets herself think about everything. 'I can’t believe I was so bad, I should have finished that match, and I should have climbed that ladder, not HBC. It’s good she won it, it will show Thug roC, he will learn soon enough. But I should have won, I should have not given up that easily, I never should have given up, I don’t care how much pain I was in, I shouldn’t have given up”. Her words make a certain amount of sense, but it is hard to tell if she is dreaming now or not. Her eyes are going through REM, and she is showing signs of sleeping, in between words she takes deep breaths, and occasionally gulps down hard, but she is talking as though she were awake and walking around. It is an odd situation, but the cameraman has to trust his instincts to leave the camera running and sit back to watch her actions. “I could almost feel him, feel Xander when Asis came out, he was scared, he was really scared, the look on his face. I remember seeing Xander coming out, it was so clear when he ran out, I knew he wanted to kill me, but I knew I could take him, fight him then and there, I had enough energy for him, just him though, anyone stronger more of a challenge and I would have been in serious trouble. I was in trouble and I never knew it, I should have sensed him coming, I should have known he wasn’t there to help me. But I did know he wasn’t there to help me. I remember turning around to that huge clothesline, that knocked me down, nearly broke my neck, I could barely move, it knocked me hard, I had no idea what it was at first, I just caught it, then I seen Asis above me, his huge body, all that power and strength, the power to kill someone whenever he wants, and he nearly killed me. I’m not sure if he would have stopped if Dan S. hadn’t come out, he could have killed me at any moment, but he never, he hurt me, but he left me alive. Unknown why, Seara begins to describe Asis entrance and the clothesline he hit her with that knocked her to the floor holding her head, she has a noticeable credit for detail as she describes it perfectly to the exact detail. She takes nearly 10 minutes to describe the entrance made by “The Demonic”, but when she has finished she takes her time to breath deeply, gripping at her chest, seemingly still threatened by the feeling that first hit her when Asis arm impacted with her head, quietly she describes falling to the mats after that hit as. “The slowest moment of my life, everything went so slow, and the pain of the hit seemed to go on forever, I never thought I would hit the mats, but when I did, it all sped up again, things went normal, I could sense the pain, and I could taste blood in my mouth”. Turning onto her back she almost faces the camera, but her eyes are tightly shut, and she is still going through REM, the entire situation is quite scary for the camera man as he has no idea what Seara will do, of if the injured wrestler is even able to get out of her bed and fight. “ I could hear them talki9ng when they carried me away, they kept talking about all the blood I was losing, I was losing blood, that’s why Asis knocked me out, he finished me, he has put me out for 2 weeks, I heard the doctors talking, they said I would have to stay here for just over a week, then I could go home, they want me to go live with my family, but I have no family, my father and brothers are dead to me, and now, the last family I had is gone thanks to Asis, he has destroyed the one thing I wanted out of life. And he will never know how much that hurts. My only family, a link to my past, a link to my ex, a way forward, a new way of seeing life and feeling about things, a reason to have emotions, it’s all gone. I never knew until the doctors told me the other day, I never knew about the life I could have”. Her words have made the female wrestler curl up into a foetal position, holding her lower stomach, even though her eyes are closed, she is no longer going through REM, and a tear rolls down her cheek. Calling her name, the cameraman waits for her to reply to him, to show she was awake, but she was still unconscious, she had been slipping in and out of a deep sleep all day, a sleep where she was no longer in control of her body. “I don’t know if I could have handled it, a small innocent thing that needs attention every second, I am not mother material, but I wanted that child, it was the only part of my life that felt right or good, I was actually happy, I was ready to do something for once, ready top sort out some stuff. I had only known for a week, and Asis came along and spoiled my chances, he went to my stomach, I keep thinking maybe he knew and in some strange way he was helping me, but I know Asis, he is a monster, a giant, a killing machine, he would never have tried to help me, not with that kind of thing, and if he had wanted to, he would have made it clear. But Asis isn’t like that, he wanted to hurt me for one reason or another, in fact, he needs no excuse to hurt anyone, he has no control, he will hurt, and for the reasons I like to, the pain of others is addictive. It is a powerful feeling to be able to injure another person, if it’s a wrestler, it is a great feeling to see their name on the injured list, and know you did that to them, you put them there. And Asis you can do that with me, you can look at my name on that list over the next two weeks, and you can know you inflicted the pain that I am injured from. You will know my suffering is your pleasure”. Once again Seara roll’s over in her semi asleep state, she yawns, then places her head on her pillow and seems to fall back into a relaxed and drug induced state. The cameraman begins to pack up his tripod, but leaves the camera balanced on a table to continue to film the female wrestler, knowing that he has already got enough of her uncontrolled talking on tape.
