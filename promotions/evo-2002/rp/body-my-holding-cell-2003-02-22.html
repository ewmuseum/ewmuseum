Body My Holding Cell

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd February 2003 In just a few days, eVolution will host another pay per view. Asis will be wrestling at it against Big Daddy Dreamer, a man with whom he has developed both a professional and personal feud over the past few weeks. It will be the seventh eVolution pay per view Asis has wrestled at and some say seven is a lucky number. But Asis does not believe in luck, he believes that only men determine what they do, luck has no part of it. And this Sunday at Tainted Love he is going to prove this fact to BDD. The fact that they are wrestling in the once retired cell does not scare Asis, far from it. It excites him. He has not had the opportunity since joining eVo to wrestle within the confines of any kind of cage. In fact never in his wrestling career has he wrestled in a cage and although the public and other wrestlers may think this is a disadvantage, he does not see it this way. BDD is an experienced wrestler, a veteran in all respects. And although he may have wrestled in cages before, chances are he’s never wrestled in one against a man like Asis… Not long now, not long till another monumental moment in my wrestling career. My first ever in cage experience, something I have been looking forward to for a long time. Now ever since the match was booked, I have overheard people in the backstage area saying how you’ve got the upper hand BDD. You being the veteran, having the experience, and me being the relative rookie when it comes to cage matches. But they seem to be forgetting something… Asis sits in a darkened room, candles being the only light source. There are objects in the room, none of them can be seen properly but as per usual they have been destroyed and don’t resemble what they once were. Everyone seems to forget that just because I have no experience within cage matches doesn’t make me any less dangerous. If anything BDD it should make you more wary of me. When I am in there with you, you will have no where to run. You will not have anyone to come in and help you. It will be me and you locked in there together until the match is over and one of us can’t continue. And I plan on making that person you and I also plan on making sure that you can’t continue. Being in a cage is second nature to me, I’ve always felt trapped that way, unable to be free, to do exactly what I want. And what happens to caged animals? They fight back, they retaliate, they become even more dangerous. The cell is just going to be an extension of my rage, I am going to use it to my advantage and I am going to hurt you BDD. You talk about your demonic beasts; do you even realise who you’re talking to? I am The Demonic there’s a reason why people call me that and once again you’ve proven that you talk without thinking. Because hearing you say that you’re going to unleash yourself just plain pisses me off. There are people in this industry that have the untapped potential to do that, to truly become “human”. People like Jason Jameson, hell even Shocka, but for some reason people don’t do that. They hold themselves back, they never embrace what they could be. Maybe there is a reason for that, I mean I am called a “freak”, people say I am “insane”, there are many different labels given to me, but I prefer to think of it as being human. The whole time Asis has been talking he has had his head down staring into the flame of one of the candles that he has lit in front of him. Now for the first time he looks up and straight into the camera, his red eyes blazing. You BDD, you do not have anywhere near what it takes to release yourself, to be free. And because of this you will suffer at Tainted Love. In a days time, you will be all mine, locked in a cage with me, no where to run, no where to hide, just pain for you to feel and for me to inflict. I’m not saying all this because I want to retain my Continental Title; I have no love for anything which gives me “status” above anyone else. I don’t care whether I have this or not, I did not request title shots. And I am not going to make anymore effort to keep it than I do to win a match. You see BDD, something you should maybe think about, is that you’re going into this match wanting to win it, to prove yourself to the public, to S., maybe even you’re trying to prove something to yourself. But whilst this is what you’re thinking about, all I want to do in this match, is cause you pain. That’s all I ever want to do, I don’t step through those ropes wanting to get another win on my record, I want another pint of blood on my hands. I want to maim, I want to destroy people’s lives. And within this cell is the best place I can think of to destroy yours…
