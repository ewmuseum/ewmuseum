Date: 3.7.03 -Yesterday Afternoon- | The wooden ceiling fan circled slowly over my drugged body as I could see music in the air. I must have been pretty fucked up cause I started to hallucinate too. It's amazing what ecstasy does to a body. You get fucked up, start seeing music, and seem as if you were in a world of your own having a great ass time. I brung the weed to my lips once more, puffing on it before watching smoke ooze out from my mouth in black intoxicating clouds. The sun beat in from the window making lines of neon-light across my brown intimidating body with 'Piru' etched out on my chest. Buuday and Sheeca were lord knows where, I could give a fuck at the time. Ring...Ring...Ring... | 'Hello?' Like I wasn't quite fully in my body I pulled my shit together to reach over and answer the red phone which sat on my oak wood dresser. 'Who da fuck iz diz?' All voices that day came new to me. I was completely out of it. 'Wut? Man, you need to stop smoking that sticky-icky-icky shit partna'' Still in an extreme state of perplexity he continued to talk on about some things that didn't really stick in my mind. 'Blah blah blah, Blah blah blah, Blah blah blah...' It all mumbled like a school teacher on a Charlie Brown cartoon, I didn't understand it, but like the kids on the cartoon, I just nodded my head and acted like I knew. | High as hell, my mind started to ponder away. Thinking up shit that makes no sense to a sober person but funny ass fuck to a higher than a motha fucka person like myself at the time. | 'HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA' For no apparent reason to the person on the other line, I began to laugh at funny illusions playing out in my head. Of course, he just had to be saying something sad at the time: 'Wut da fuck?! Why da hell you laughing?! I jus' said my momma died!' Trying to go off on a nigga, he kept going on and on about how I was wrong for this and that but like I said, I was too high to care. 'Wait, who da fuck are you again?' After that all I heard was 'FUCK YOU' and the sound of the dial tone. Soon following, I let the phone slide out my hands hitting the red carpet floor below with a Thumb! | Feeling like the room was spinning in circles, I wiped the sweat which was seeping from my forehead at a extraordinary fast pace away and took another puff of my weed before setting it back on the ash tray which sat on the dresser besides me. Apart of me felt like picking up the dirty drawls, shirt, pants, and all other accessories worn on the naked body that laid around the room but then again I knew my black ass wasn't going to get up and do shit about it. A breeze brung the noise of Atlanta streets into my room while at the same time pushing forward the wooden blinds making a clacking sound as the blinds would soon come back and hit the wall because they were to big to hang inside the window area. As I sat there I could here the front door swing open, allowing Buuday and Sheeca to laugh historically at things ghetto woman would laugh at. | 'Yo AJ? You up in diz motha fucka?' Ghetto as can be, her voice shouted through the house grabbing my drawn attention. 'Wut da fuck ya'll want?' Or I thought I had said that, it could have been just in my head because Buuday busted in the room shouting my name once more before spotting me stretched out across the bed through the weed clouded room. 'Why da fuck you didn't answer me motha fucka?! You heard me callin' yo bitch ass name!' On and on she went about this and that, that and this. All it took was, 'shut up bitch' and she got quiet... Well maybe that came forth because she was searching for her gun... It didn't matter. 'Yo, did I get dat phone call?' Said to whoever heard me at the time, guess it was Sheeca: 'What phone call?' Her voluptuous lips colored with red lipstick spread apart making each word sound as sweet as pure chocolate. Coated with caramel skin tone setting off her incredible beauty with an ass and well sized breast to match. My lawyer/friend... Damn I wish I could tap that ass but she wasn't on that level with me, we're just friends, how she'd put it best. 'Wait, you mean that phone call from eVo?' I looked at the unsigned PCW contract and felt it was an aberration for me to go through with it. Fine, small words written elegantly, intelligently across a white paper with a yellow copy behind made up the PCW contract which lied on my silky texture red mattress. 'Uh...' Re-gathering my train of thought, 'Yea, wut they say?' As if I didn't know already. 'Oh, they just want you to turn on PCW and join eVo. That's all' Blindly connive, she spoke of it with good sense of rectitude like it was perfectly ok. 'So, are you going to do it?' | Each word spoken so simply by her made it more difficult for me. It was like she was trying to encumber me with guilt for even thinking of turning on the place that made me without even knowing she was doing it. I puffed my weed again and again trying to stay high so I wouldn't give a damn. | 'MOTHA FUCKA!' BOOM! About twelve bullets leveled my bed but I was so high my reactions were slow to get up and see Buuday standing their with a revolver shotgun. 'CALL ME A BITCH AGAIN!' BOOM! | By that time I knew, 'this bitch was trying to kill my ass.' So I did like any smart brotha would do; grab my shit and dive out the open window before hauling ass down the back alley to my black Mercedes Bens with 20 inch rims parked outside. | ***7:47pm that afternoon*** | Like swallowed Ammonia, the guilt ate away at me inside. But I couldn't let that concern me now. I had Piru business to take care of. The dimmed now turned orange/red light from the setting sun projected barely bright blindly light off my platinum silver 20 inch dubs as I cruised down the street sipping on Gin and Juice. Broke down apartment buildings pictured on the outside with ghetto kids running ramped, red brick building up the structure, thugs thugging, and old people still trying to get their thug on all flew like a scene in motion through my heavily dark tented windows. I turned on Piru street and pulled up to my boy’s house, Darnel. Huge ass quantities of BBQ brought a hungry exodus of black folks leaving their home to the 'Annual Black BBQ Reunion' or what I like to call, 'get your grub on time.' Darnel's white wife-beater rippled in the low winds while he took off the white apron he was currently wearing. Dressed to thug in his black Fubu jeans and And 1 shoes, he greeted me with a pound(dap) and a blunt as I rose from my drop top. | 'Wut up nigga?' Said in the process of giving one another a pound(dap). 'I thought yo black ass wuzn't gon' sho'' Darnel continued as we walked to the backyard. 'Shit, I wuzn't gon' show up. But I got to much shit to get off my mind.' As if I didn't even speak, Darnel's attention went directly to a white guy with clean cut blonde hair, tight jeans, and a button up blue shirt that didn't help his image much. 'Yo cracka, you at da wrong motha fuckin' place. You needz to bizounce.' Making him leave as if he were a dog not wanted on the premises. I would have joined in to knock(diss) on his ass but PCW and eVo were primacy in my mind. | The scent of meat floated in thin grey clouds while we continued to walk to the backyard. Music blasting as loud as possible, crazy ass black folks dancing on each other butt ass naked, sane black folks grubbing, and... oh shit... Wayne with his sorry ass set at a bench table with his crew. Nothing but gold teeth in his mouth. Said so his talk would never be cheap, but he's the cheapest motha fucka I ever ran across. His hair corn-rolled in a criss-cross pattern, dressed down in Fubu skeletons(cloths). Wayne and I go way back in the day when he dime-d my ass out for robbing that corner store. Of course I whooped his bitch ass and he had the nerve to tell everyone while I was in jail, that he whooped my ass. With Wayne and I in the same area, there was an inevitable altercation to go down. |
