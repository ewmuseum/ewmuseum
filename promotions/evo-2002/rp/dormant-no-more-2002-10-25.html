Dormant No More

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th October 2002 You chose to obey the rules follow the choices, turn around and open your eyes to a fully closed position. Now you are ready to enter the domain the one world where it all chooses to focus its eyes tightly on everything that doesn’t exist in the reality of life that you see purely with your eyes, but honourably and successfully on the world you see with your mind. Not just any part of your mind, but the dormant part, close your eyes long enough and you will feel it awaken. She had felt it once before, it had opened new doors to things she had never known possible in her own life, and now it was once again opening, but it was happening with her eyes open. Standing there she could feel her own thoughts change from something she had considered normal to something she almost feared. A want to kill the one person she could possibly love. The scene opens at a local Chicago back alley gym. We have seen Seara here before when she viciously beat into her training partner and then meet with Asis in the changing rooms and quickly left. This time she is in mid war with Asis and is only a few days away from what she considers her biggest eVo fight to date. Throwing a punch towards a wall Seara screams out in frustration as the crimson liquid flows down her knuckles and drips onto the floor. The noise of it hitting the wood can almost be heard when the room is completely silent. Yet again Seara punches toward the wall and smiles as her own blood splashes back into her face. The killer in me is a killer in you my love. We are here now together I am ready for this. We will be together, I will love you and kill you, we are dead together. Blood once more splatters up Seara’s face, hitting her lips she licks it away and looks around the gym as though expecting someone. The room is dark and only one small strip light hanging from the ceiling illuminates it well enough to make out Seara clearly. Training, always ready, always prepared, always training, pushing myself to the limit, preparing for him, he will kill me at any chance he gets, but I am ready. Always ready, always prepared, always training, and always thinking about my love. Ready to kill you, show you the killer that I am, the killer in me, free, I will release it all. It is out; she has unlocked it in me. My own killer. Asis can harm me, could kill me, but she is the worst killer, diluting my mind with her ideas, pushing me, making me do things, but I have control now, I am becoming whole. She will fully become part of me; we will be full at Faith. We will rule the ring; we will kill ourselves to kill him. Seara begins to move in a predatory animal like behaviour as though she is hunting something or someone…hunting a smell. She sniffs and inhales deeply through her nose. Then turning back to the wall she continues to punch. While she had been turned away the camera had got a glance at the wall. The brickwork was broken away, obviously from years of punishment such as Seara was doing, and it was covered in the crimson of her blood. Just above the blooded section of wall, the words “always here but never seen” had been scrawled onto the green paint in black marker pen in Seara’s handwriting, below it where the blood had began to run down the wall were three names, at the top of the list was Asis, his name now barely visible due to coverage of blood, and below that, the names of her father and his wife Gail. Her fathers name written in great detail and with great care and love had been placed in the middle between the two other names. Asis, above, a man who seemed intent on destroying Seara’s family to prove a point of some sorts to her, and Gail below, a woman who Seara believed had already destroyed her father’s life once. Both people who had been in her life and were destroying her family. Gails name had been written with a great hatred and each letter seemed jagged and sharp, almost pain filled. Through it a line had been drawn a line with the blood from the fists of Seara. Always ready, always good, always ready. Seara’s words are erratic and uncontrolled and she seems to be talking on the spur of the moment, not paying attention to the fact that the camera is only feet away from her, being missed by left punches and covered with spots of blood as her knuckles explode against the wall. I have been waiting a long time for this. Turning Seara looks at the camera and then down at her knuckles and with one finger wipes the blood from the other hand, lifts it to her face and licks the metallic tasting fluid from her skin. On Sunday I will step in the ring with one intent, not to win, although I would not complain of I did, but rather to tear Asis, show him our childhood all over again but from a different point of view. This time I will show him how much it still hurts now as an adult. I may only be young, but I have been through enough to torture Asis with the pain of the memory. My memory remains, it is alive and strong, it has escaped the dark shadow of my mind, woken something up inside me, showing me the true nature of the pain of the truth. Seara suddenly lets out a huge scream of anger and pain at the thought of her own tortured childhood, hated and removed from everything she entered and became accustomed to. A nightmare is the only way most normal people could describe what Seara had been through, but Seara would say nightmare was a too friendly word, it was hell, a life much like that of eternal damnation, torture like that described to children when they are taught about the seven worlds of hell and the dark ruler of them all. ………Zack Samson, a small silver haired child, with strange sadistic red eyes sits in the shadows of an alley between houses. He stares hard at the new girl on the block as she slowly walks up the alley; she is bruised and looks to have been in a fight, but there are other reasons for her bruising, a fight would have better for her than anything she had suffered in that training ring. She backs up into a corner next to Zack and slides down trying to hide. She is tall for her age, but he still seems to hide her body away from sight. From around the corner a group of people appear and advance towards them. “Move Samson, let me have her and you won’t get hurt, no actually sorry I lied, you will get hurt”. Getting to his feet Samson towers the children his age, but matches the height of much older high school students, the smaller Katherine Jackson stands up alongside him and steps toward her stepbrother Vinnie, pulling her fist back she lunges out in sheer desperation to save herself and try to buy the pair a few seconds to escape over the fence, but it does not work, and Zack looks determined not to back down. A fist quickly connects with the face of Katherine and she staggers backwards into a fence, hitting her head and falling to the floor, she is quick to jump up. “Yeah, he has a bit of training with her dad, he’s a wrestler, her and her brothers go all kinds of fighting” remarked Vinnie, his words hate filled and jealousy ridden, back to her feet Katherine steps in front of Samson, “It has nothing to do with him, just let him go”, “Let the freak go, you have to be joking, look boys the little bitch thinks Samson is innocent, shall we let him go” the words of the oldest member or the group are projected in a loud clear voice and the group cheer and holler behind him. Once again Katherine throws a punch, this time at the leader, he is quick to punch back, hitting Katherine in the jaw, she falls straight to the ground with the force of the punch and her head connects hard with the concrete making a loud thud. A large fight is quick to follow with kicking and punching being the main form of attack. Samson is now involved……… Turning to the wall Seara begins to punch it once more. Tortured by my peers, ridiculed for knowing him and never trying to attack him. Bullied for my father. Still tortured by my peers, but the pain has grown stronger, it is tearing me apart and recombining me, rearranging my inner self, the importance of caring is no longer great in me, but I will control myself, and at Faith this Sunday, I will face one of my peers, little Zack Samson, not so little anymore though are you Zack. I will torture you for all the torture I have been through. I will tear you apart because I know I can fight you, I know I have what it takes. Someone told me once that all I needed to do to inflict pain and win was to feel the pain I wished to inflict. As you can see Asis, I have no problem with inflicting pain on anyone, and this Sunday we will battle; this will be the biggest and most violent and dangerous we will ever have together. You are mine; all mine, never anyone else’s, and always mine. Always ready, always prepared, always hurt, always dead. Always my inner self…
