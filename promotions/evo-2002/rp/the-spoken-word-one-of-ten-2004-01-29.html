Date: 2004-01-29 The Spoken Word: One of Ten A Night in Holleewoodee -------------------------------------------------------------------- Glassjaw - 'Motel of the White Locust' 'Welcome to Hollywood, girl. Wake the fuck up to Hollywood, whore!' -------------------------------------------------------------------- 'Welcome to eVo.' Those were the words spoken to Santiago Rodriguez by his new boss. The man they Black Heart. The man at the top of the California independent scene. Any man who has just gotten a contract from a highly prestiged promotion such as eVolution has to be concerned with training. Has to be in the gym, training. So is that where Santiago is now? Nope. The scene opens up in a crowded Hollywood nightclub. The pulsing beats of Sean Paul's 'Gimme Da Light' emanate through the speakers, and the bright, exotic lights flicker on and off, giving the vibe of an 80's disco. Green. Blue. Red. Yellow. Green. Blue. Red. Yellow. What an environment. Sharply dressed males stand around, with a drink in one hand, behind a beautiful woman who is insistent on rubbing her backside on the male's crotch. Often times, turning her head to face her man and sharing a fresh, dirty kiss. Santiago Rodriguez walks in, smiling, wearing a white long sleeved shirt with the sleeves rolled up halfway. The top three buttons are left untouched, unveiling his chest, and a bright, white choker rests along his neck. Acid-washed, 80's style jeans are ended with brown boots. The Armani sunglasses rest just above his forehead, and immediatly, due to his 5'10', 205 pound ripped body, the attention is brought upon this athlete. The club 'scene' don't seem to be familiar with professional wrestling, considering no one comes up for autographs. Instead, Santiago Rodriguez, along with his entourage of friends, slickly walk towards the bar, with Santiago leading the way and suddenly becoming the center of attention. Already, a beautiful blonde wearing a tight black dress smiles at Santiago, with a glass in her hand. She obviously has a few drinks in her already, because on her journey to the bar to greet Santiago, she stumbles into the bystanders in her way. Santiago removes his sunglasses and takes a seat on the barstool. His friends, three men by the names of Jeremy, Shane, and Rich, are all sharply dressed as well. ' What can I get for you? ' The voice is that of the bartender, a frat boy who's bleached, streaked hair is drenched in all flavors of different liquors. His t-shirt reads Acid, apparently the name of club. ' Give me a hard Hurricane. Thanks. ' The bartender smiles and walks over to his liquors, working hard to keep his customers proud. The blonde lady walks up to Santiago and the rest of the guys, with a smile on her face. ' Hello, boys. I'm Miriam. ' Jeremy, Shane, and Rich all stare with 'ooh's' on their faces. Santiago seems to be the smooth-talker. ' Hello, Miriam. I'm Santiago, and these are my friends Jeremy, Shane, and Richard. ' ' Are you guys in a band or something? ' Her question is one of lust, and Santiago laughs. She is obviously only approaching those who look as if they have money. ' Actually, I am a pimp, and these guys are my bitches. ' This draws a silence from Miriam, who isn't that drunk to realize what kind of answer that was. The three guys just stand and look at each other with open mouths, unable to speak after this description given by Santiago Rodriguez. ' Are you serious, Santiago? ' ' No, Miriam. I am a professional wrestler, and these three guys are athletes who I am trying to help out and get themselves a wrestling opportunity as well. ' Miriam is impressed, and slides herself onto the barstool next to Santiago, seductively, grazing his legs with hers. The bartender returns with Santiago's drink. ' Oh, is that for me? ' But Miriam doesn't wait for an answer. Her hand reaches over and grabs the glass, pulling it towards her, and raising it up to her mouth. The first drink is always the most impactful with a hard drink like this. Surely, there is no way Miriam can hold this drink down. And she doesn't. After just three drinks of the Hurricane, Miriam gags, and puts her hand in front of her mouth. Running towards the bathroom, Miriam can't hold it, and drops to her knees, throwing up all over the floor. Everyone begins to laugh hysterically, but Miriam doesn't seem the slightest bit embarassed. Noticing that she may return, Santiago and the guys gather their things and walk towards the dance floor. Now the interesting beat of 'Gangsta Nation' by the Westside Connection begins to pulse through the speakers, gaining a large fan reaction from this Cali crowd. Santiago has no trouble finding a girl to grind against. A very nice looking brunette immediatly ditches the current guy she has just met and has now begun feeling on Santiago's chest and arms. Turning around, she grinds her backside on his crotch. Jeremy, Rich, and Shane have a little bit more trouble, and are sent to the losers wall with the rest of the dance-less people. Just staring at Santiago, they strategize how they are going to get girls the way Santiago does. ' Maybe we should just use the wrestler act? Just tell girls we are professional wrestlers like Santiago. ' ' Shane, I think the girls will find out that we are just lying. Not only will we not get laid, but we will also be looked at as liars. And I don't do that. ' ' Shut up Jeremy! Quit being a saint. Come on Shane, let's go get some chicks! ' The two guys walk towards two women who have just entered Acid. Smiling, they approach the girls, who seem to be looking right by them. ' Hey girls. I'm Jeremy. And this is Shane. We've been noticing you all night. ' ' Umm, that's cute, but we just got here, honey. ' ' What Jeremy is trying to say, is that we are professional wrestlers for the AXIS Wrestling Alliance. ' The girls look at each other and begin to snicker. The blonde one begins to speak. ' We watch AWA, and we have not seen or heard from you... ever. Plus, don't you need some muscles for that? ' The other lady laughs and takes the blonde by the arm, leaving Jeremy and Shane pissed at each other. Shane punches Jeremy in the arm as they walk back towards the snickering Rich. ' God, Jeremy. Did you have to say that? ' ' What? I thought that was our plan! ' They stand next to Rich again, who's just smiling at them. ' I told you guys that wouldn't work. Just look at a real man at work. ' Rich leaves the two, trying to prove a point. He tries to act cool when a lady walks by, but they just walk right past him. 'Loser', one says. But Rich is determined. His spirit isn't broken easily. Until... Rich walks towards this fairly decent looking lady, definatly not at the level of those the others have tried hitting on. And DEFINATLY not at the standard which Santiago grabs girls on a regular basis. ' If I can't get this girl, I'm leaving. ' Speaking to himself, he tries to pump up his own ego. Approaching this lady, he misfires on his first approach by leading himself to walk right past her. He then follows her up. ' Hey, you've got a really, great body. ' Smiling arrogantly, he walks up to the woman. Putting his arm around her, she quickly removes it and delivers a slap straight across his face. ' Get away from me, you freak. ' Santiago sees this and gives his girl a long kiss before walking over to the three boys. He's laughing hysterically. ' Why are you guys trying to be like me? It'll never happen. It's barely 12. C'mon, let's go. ' So the three exit the door to Acid, and their night on the town has just begun.
