Deadly Sins 'Pride'

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-29 Pride is excessive belief in one's own abilities, that interferes with the individual's recognition of the grace of God. It has been called the sin from which all others arise. Pride is also known as Vanity. When you become lost in the world of society like today, one can easily take his skills and accomplishments and use them to feel better about themselves. They feel this sense of great power and become over confident in themselves and develop a back bone, pride. Some could say pride is all a man has for himself, others would say pride is a direct reflection of being stubborn. Pride can be taken out of complex and be taken very far. Men, Women and children have died over such a thing. The worst thing you could do to someone is insult their pride! The Scene opens on a man that is self driven by pride... The most intimidating, snarling look can be seen on Morpheus Omega face... Morpheus sitting on a black leather couch, he shines his eVolution championship to perfection. Moments pass and for a second, Morpheus almost seems content about something... Morpheus stares face down into the championship and his reflection can be seen glaring from it. Morpheus looks back up with a huge smile, he nods his head. 'Many speculate the chance of Twisted Reality losing their championships on Sunday!' Morpheus says and continues speaking. 'I think some people have started to become ignorant!' Staring back down into the reflection of the title, Morpheus sighs and seems very impatient about something... Moments pass and Morpheus continues speaking once again. 'The syndicate make me feel restless and insulted with their multiple chances they have received to prove themselves over us, but they fail every time. It seems the only thing I have left, is punishment and pride. I love every second of it, watching others suffer at the hands of something superior. Pride is my... FrIeNd!!! You wouldn't want to interfere with my business, would you?' Admitting he has sinned, though he does not fear the outcome... 'Nothing can come close to stopping me, many have stood before me and they always leave with the same fate. I have no mercy and nor do I care about any of the hundreds who have suffered. The sickest alliance in the business New-TV, we lay our pride on the lines every second of the day... When you step into the ring and face off against one, another... You are wagering for your pride, for self esteem and the bragging rights of being the best... Would you gamble with your pride against the deadliest athlete in the game?' encouraging and questioning others pride, he stays hell bent on his own. Just an example of Morpheus many sins...
