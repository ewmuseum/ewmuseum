Welcome to the Desert

8 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-08 It is a hot day in the Macedonian desert outside of Skojpe, the capital of this country in which the world nearly fell to its knees some 2,500 years ago. For you see, this is the place in which modern combat tactics, used today by armies around the world, and those whose craft is combat. Lessons of past, return once again into the forefront of the modern world, but this time, it isn't a war of global conquest. Just a war for the conquest of the Evolution. The Syndicate has ruled Evo 24-7, but now, another challenge has poised before them. Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick know all too well that it takes more than just physical muscle to break your opponents, but also a grand strategy of mental, emotional, and psychological warfare to lead ones army to complete victory. Within the classical study of martial arts and combat tactics, lessons of the old masters are as fundamental a part of training as physical development. The Taoist masterpiece, The Art of War, Sunzi Bingfa / Sun-tzu ping-fa, has often been calle d the most profound and influential book on martial strategy ever written The Syndicate often refer to it as the quintessential study of the inner-mechanics of organizations in conflict. Its enduring wisdom has influenced the thrust of martial and militar y science in the East and West for over two millennia, and now in 2001, its passages will be no different in leading them to victory. The Syndicate campaigns within the wrestling world, lead by Big Daddy Dreamer and HBC, have often been described by histor ians and tacticians as the pre-eminent display of military leadership in the modern wrestling ring. With countless victories on a multitude of magnitudes BDD and HBC utilize efficient logistical support, strategic intelligence, innovative strategy and exe cution of battle plans to lead them to success. This Thursday at Legends, the strategic plan to lead the Syndicate in retaining the Tag Team Championships shall be revealed as they emerge victorious, putting an end to those wannabe Syndicate clones, Scott Frost and Seara. For to understand and even have a chance against The Syndicate one must have a basic understanding or concept of the ideology that binds them together in the squared circle of conflict. The idea was born that of five years ago, long before the Hardy's unique concept th at we see current today. The idea of a superior mixed gender tag team was unheard of at the time, but little did anyone know besides them, it was a grand experiment of design that revolutionized this sport, and garnering them the accolades and championsh i ps held world wide. The sins of the pasts, coming together to destroy the world and everything in their sight. And now, the sins of misdeeds of ages past, come into bearing against as Frost and Seara will be that like cannon fodder of the greatest war of history of mankind.
