Life Breeds.......

19 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 19h, 2002 Life is a funny thing is it not? There are so many things that people do not understand about life. So many things that I do not understand about life. All of life’s secrets are open for the world to find and yet there isn’t a soul among us that has been able to do it. We all however, do know the very basic fundamentality of life. Life breeds…… What exactly does it breed is the question for which there are many answers which could no doubt be contributed. Violence, death, and destruction are but three words for the same answer. Life delivers it all to us and then more often than not takes it right back away. All three are a fundamental part of everyone’s daily life whether they know it or not. Somewhere at sometime there is someone who is part of your family that is suffering from one of the three. Even if it’s a long forgotten aunt or uncle, they are still there and they are still suffering. There is nothing that you or anyone else can do to change that. It is one of the basic and simplest facts of our short and pitiful lives upon this globe of mud that we call a planet. I have learned from a very early age what it is like to suffer needlessly. Everyday of my childhood had been one struggle after another, trying to survive on the streets with no one at my side other than several other homeless bums. In retrospect it was this struggle that had turned me into the man I am now. It was this very struggle that had made me out to be who I am, the outcast of the more common and acceptable society. I am the one man that all the mothers and fathers fear deep down that their child may one day become due to the confines of this cruel, cruel world we find ourselves a part of. I’m sure my mom must be so proud of me now for I am the man which her husband, my bastard of a father could not be. I fear nothing that can be placed in front of me and that is least of all a man who must wear a mask to hide his true features. A man such as Gerico is the true disgrace to societies today. He is too scared to remove his mask for fear of being an outcast like me. The cowardice which he possesses is staggering and that is why he was the first. That is why I made him the first man to feel my pain upon my return. He will not be the last though. No not by a long shot. At Legends I will make him feel it even more. I will make him drink of his own blood as he lies in a pool of it and I will make him see the truth. I will make him realize just what being an outcast really is and how it’s not a good thing to hide your true self for you will be seen through. Sovereign…… He did do quite a number on me now did he not? It was that very beating which brought me the conclusions and goals which I now have in mind. It was Sovereign who was responsible for it all and thanks to him I now have even more pain to share the world. By the end of time each and every one of you will share in my pain with me. You will all know what life is like when you’re an outcast.
