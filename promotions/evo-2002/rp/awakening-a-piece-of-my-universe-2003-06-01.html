Awakening - A Piece of My Universe

1 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-01 I can feel the blood pumping through my veins like the water through a fire hose. My heart’s emotions crowd my thought, and my rage builds inside with every passing second. I think if I just sat down long enough and listened to my pulse roam through my arteries until my body bleeds through its pores, I might be able to get the same satisfaction as the one I await in my brisk coming future. But that’s just the thing I can feel killing me as I stand here – awaiting the inevitable. I can still feel all the pain running through my nerves, every bruise turning purple and every cut slowly flowing blood through. The fight was days ago, but deep down I am still in that ring, beating my way through the minutes and striving for the victory. I can still feel the voice of the crowd crawling up my spine and reaching into my psyche, ordering me to finish off this bastard once and for all. The heat from the audience and the action inside that squared circle still builds inside my muscles, and I can still feel their intense pull on my bones. I can feel all this, and yet I can feel the world pissing away everything else that matters to me. Life has been a wonder so far. Ever since that chilly evening in Boston many years ago, when Jason’s personality psychologically spilt in two and gave birth to the demon you see before you today. My eyes, blurry and blinded by the brightness of life, opened to see through the windows of his. The first awakening had appeared. I can still feel myself born into life, dragged out of oblivion and into the limelight that was existence. He could feel me come to life too. That was the first sensation I felt as I came into being. The realization that I was apart of another life, one much weaker then I was, and realizing that I would be apart of him for many years to come. There’s no way to describe the feeling. It’s more then I can explain in the words of humanity. More then any expressions or sentences could ever imagine. Beyond the experiences of this inscrutable life you call your own. I have been apart of something far ahead of anything you could ever envision through your simple brains. And now, I can feel my past running through me. It’s still apart of me, and sadly, no matter what I do, it will always be there. A piece of my universe, twitching and itching like a rash. One rash that could only be healed through the blood of another soul.
