Sunrise

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 7th 2002 The scene opens in a completely different location for Seara. Although we have witnessed her late at night and very early in the morning before, we have never really seen her interested in scenery, but although she requested a camera be here, the female wrestler herself is not here. Footsteps are heard by the cameraman, but no voice is heard for a long time, not until the feminine voice begins. Seara – Cameramen can be very influential. The words were clear, bold and the voice obviously belonged to Seara, but her figure could not be seen, it soon becomes apparent she is standing behind the camera. She walks past it and sits on and old piece of cloth, looking out over the ocean and an orange and pink sky, it is early enough for Seara to confuse her own breath with that of cigarette smoke, and she sits calmly watching as the sun slowly rises over the horizon. Seara- There’s something quite beautiful and mysterious about the sun rising, something that could also be quite deadly to some. I personally think the sun rising is beautiful, but in the wrong context it is eerie and generally not a pleasant thing. Much like Asis. The other day, you seen me reading a letter from the people that were once my family, and you heard the words of a cameraman, describing how he thought I felt about Asis. How he thought I felt, I mean how could people believe him, quite easily I noticed. You believe that I thought I took Asis out, he is a giant, a huge man compared to me, I was lucky to get him off his feet, I was lucky to get him covered long enough to get the 1…2…3… I am shocked, if not more than he was when I pinned him, but still, I managed to win…somehow. I do not pride myself on the win, it is not the greatest thing I have done since joining eVolution, and I have no need to brag, I am not like all those wrestlers that think just because they took out someone of Asis stature, or somehow managed to pin them for the three count, that they can go on about it for the next week or longer. There is no need to continue to celebrate; there is always a new enemy, someone else you can plan to crush, someone new to add to your list of hated wrestlers. So after this is over don’t expect me to bring up my win over Asis every time I get in front of a camera, in fact, don’t expect me to mention it again after this interview is over. Eric Badger was not much in our fight, I have never entered the ring with him before so I cannot comment very well on him, but what I do know is that he was easily taken out of our match, and left me and Asis to do most of the battling. But I am sure Eric Badger is a worthy opponent, and I’m sure like most people he will have a reason for going down, after all, even I have had excuses for going down, but not now, not this time. Seara looks at the camera cold and hard for a moment as it walks around her to look at the warm somehow pleasant smile on her face. When she finally realises that the camera is not going to move away from in front of her, she takes to her feet and moves closer to the water that is gently lapping up at the beach. Turning she looks at the camera with a bewildered look, something has obviously been on her mind she got here, and it is apparent in her voice and the look she is now giving the cameraman. Seara – this week I will look upon Eric Badger and I will watch him fight alongside Asis, and I will decide if he is a worthy opponent. I will also fight alongside James Jameson, a man who I have never even paid a great deal of attention to, a man who I will have to work hard with to keep the challenge. We will fight in the ring, Asis, James Jameson, Eric Badger and myself, we will tag team, and I will try as hard as possible to walk out of that match both a winner, and on my feet, this week, I will try to stay away from Asis after the match, even if it means an early escape from the ring. I don’t care if people think I am some kind of wimp, you just try being attacked by Asis. He put me through a table and crushed my head between two steel chairs with his entire body weight. I don’t think that you would want to hang around too long with him, I am not scared, but right now Asis needs to calm down a little, he’s more fucking crazy than ever before. For the first time since she has arrived, Seara ventures into the pockets of her trench coat, first producing a lighter then a pack of cigarettes, she takes one and after a few minutes consideration lights it, then removes the defaced coin she had last time we seen her. While spinning the coins she mumbles the words of a child’s nursery rhyme, focusing hard on the metal between her fingers. Unpredictable and unexplainable Seara kisses the coin before putting it back in her pocket and holding her ribs tight. Seara – Pain is the blessing of life, pure life pure feeling, without pain and anger there is nothing, but pain and anger can become everything, they are all a person needs, all I need, all I want, all I ask for. Seara has slipped back into her unconscious babbling mood and she stands perfectly still muttering words about her need for pain and hating, she seems unaware of her own voice for a while, until finally shaking her head and looking up at the sky, it is a light orange and yellow and she smiles at it. Seara – As a small child I stood here once, just after my mom died, I stood here with my family, looking at the sunrise, it was mom’s favourite place, she gave birth to all of us here, under this very sky, on this beach, that house was ours while mom was pregnant, dad used to rent it out from a work mate, and when mom would get close to giving birth she would move the family here, I don’t know how she managed it, but we were all born here at exactly this time of day, under the rising sun, she would be children of the sun, but I wasn’t, I got lost under a blanket of black. Once again the female wrestler snaps herself out of her lifeless worded state by shaking her head, she turns to face the camera, and looks hard at the cold dark lens. There is a hint of deep thought and concentration in her eyes, and she occasionally smiles, not the warm smile she had when mumbling children’s nursery rhymes, but a dark uncontrolled smile, one of pure anger and frustration. Seara – At Legends this week, we will meet Asis, and we will fight, and you will know the anger you have distilled in me, you will know how much pain you put me through. As for James Jameson and Eric Badger, you too will know how much anger I have; Jameson will witness me dealing it out from our corner, while Badger will feel it, and if he has a problem with it, and wishes to try to be like his partner Asis, he can try to take me out after the match, but this week will be different, no one will take me out after my match. Asis made me stronger. What doesn’t kill you makes you stronger. I am stronger thanks to Asis, I do not mean to sound like I am praising him or inviting him to take out once again, but I am thanking him for what he did to me, he showed me something I had never seen before, and he made me remember why I don’t trust people. Time is getting dark, and death is growing ever stronger. As the scene fades out, Seara repeats the words “Time is getting dark and death is growing ever stronger” over and over again, almost hypnotized by the way the words sound in her own ears.
