I'm Hippin To The Hoppin!

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-1-03 'Lee is in his house playing video from NBC, where his good buddy Thug Roc is making a match that it is Lee eXodus the Surreal Thrilla, Natural Born Killa! Brittney Spears B/f, Mr. Cashflow, Mr. Everynight...oh well the nicknames just keep on coming, so that would be hours of nicknames. Lee jumps up and down, and dances seeing this news, then notices he is facing Seara?!' Lee eXodus: AHHHH! She kicked my ass last time! (Jumps up and down throwing a tantrum!) Lee eXodus: Well, I am more skilled now that I beat Angel,I'm sure I can squeek out of this match with a win, and become the new eVo High Impact champion, since I am Mr. Highlight Reel (RVD taunt.) Lee eXodus: Run, but you can't hide little Seara, not even your Backstreet boy looking b/f can save you from me beating you up. If, you didn't notice that at the ppv, I held a Scott Frost sign, because he is my hero, and Seara's b/f. You see all along I knew that, so I wanted Scott Frost to see how cool I am, and see no reason to interfer with the match itself. I'm so smart, and I just told everyone on T-V..geez I'm a genius. Lee eXodus: You see, even though Scott Frost insulted me at the ppv, with his comment about Thug Roc being my shower (used fingers) buddy....that wasn't true, this month I haft to shower with the five star fags, but that's not the point! Lee eXodus: I'm not a fag! I hate you all! Now, Scott Frost is cool with me you see, he just said that cus he didn't want to seem like he was favoring me (shrugs). I think maybe he is in denial knowing that he had to drop his title, and I'm gonna beat his girl friend up,so he wanted to seem tough, but I saw him in the halls and he smacked me behind the head, so I guess that means I'm cool with him. Lee eXodus: Oh, I'm also cool with Sean Manson. What? It's Mason? No, no its Manson he's the singer of that one bad with the scary dude who sings songs? Yea, well me and Manson went out on the town one night, and the next day I woke up in a dumpster, but that's not the point! The point is I have lots of friends! Lee eXodus: At Legends, I'm gonna beat Seara, then I'm gonna beat up on the next Legends the lead singer of that one band Sean Manson! Cameraman: Dude, his name is Sean Mason, the eVo Rising Star chamion...god you are an idoit. Lee eXodus: What?! No! It's Sean Manson! Who the hell is this Sean Mason?! It's Manson! Damnit its Manson! (Jumps up and down throwing another trantum as the scene fades.)
