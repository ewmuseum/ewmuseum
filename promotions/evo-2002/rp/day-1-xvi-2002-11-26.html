Day 1: XVI

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 26, 2002 'Scott. It's okay. This thing happens.' A female voice is heard. The scene fades in, and Scott Frost is sitting on a bench in what seems to be a park. The sun rises, and captures Scott Frost's body almost broken. His quivering body shakes with rage, and the only person that seems to stop him is his secret to the world love; Seara. Seara sits to the left him, with her right arm wrapped around him squeezing his right shoulder and her left hand gripping onto his left bicept. Seara looks concerned for the man, that has made her realize she is human and still has feelings. She rests her head on his left shoulder. 'Scott. Please. It will be okay.' Seara again tries to comfort Frost. Frost continues to look down at the ground, with his hands rested on both sides of his head. He slowly begins to rock back and forth lightly. 'What is wrong? This is not the Scott Frost I grew to want and need! So you lost one match, you won two other ones! You beat Asis, You beat Jameson. It must of been hard to have to go up against a friend, in the ring. But how do you think I feel losing the Rising Title tournament to Sean Mason?' Seara again tries to comfort Frost who doesn't seem to want to listen. Frost looks up and stares infront of him. His eyes piercing the sky, as he is deep in thought. 'I want to know what you are thinking, I want to understand why you feel like this. Right now; you, L, and Jameson are all I have. But me and you, we have something. Something I haven't been able to give for awhile, something that you gave to me after losing your wife. I hate to see you like this.' Seara says to Frost. Frost closes his eyes. 'Katherine. It's not that. I don't care about that damned tournament. I will get that Universal championship, one way or another. But it is you I am upset about. I am, upset that I disappointed you. I should have been out there in your match to help you! You would have done the same for me.' Frost says to Seara, sounding very shameful. Seara smiles and looks at Frost. She takes her left and places her first three fingers on the right side of Frost's face giving him no choice but to look at her. She smiles again, and Frost stares at her as if he is uncertain about something. 'Scott. That is part of the business. You win some, you lose some. I lost. I can't do anything about it, except try harder and kick some ass.' Seara says smiling. Frost smirks at Seara last remark. 'You know. I can't remember the last time I actually had someone care about me.' Frost gently says to Seara. Seara's eyes widen, and she smirks. 'That makes two of us.' Seara jokingly replies. 'You gotta a match this thursday. You do know that, right?' Seara asks Frost. 'Against who?' Frost retorts with his own question. 'Sovereign and Impact.' Seara announces to Frost. 'BY MYSELF?!' Frost over reacts, not knowing he is in a tag team match up. 'No. L2J is going to be your partner.' Seare assures him. Frost looks around and then looks at Seara. 'This is my chance.' Frost says sounding very excited. 'Chance for what?' Seara replies sounding very confused. Frost smiles again and quickly kisses Seara. 'You'll see. Come on!' Frost jumps up, holding the hand of Seara and the scene fades away. (To Be Continued...)
