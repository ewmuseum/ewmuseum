I'll show you! You'll see...Trapped pt 2

30 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-30 ~As he lay stranded watching the sunrise all he could do was smirk the alcohol was still in full effect and he still had beer to go. But you know what this was fucking amazing mental preparation you see this sets him up to be clear minded. To focus on what he wants to do to Johnny Charisma... for now he was unaware of thoughts or intentions of Sir Johnny.~ 'I can't fucking wait until someone else drives bye...' Shocka spoke with a slurred tone. 'Or before i work up enough fucking energy to walk out of this damn place.' He rose half way and looked in the distance to see dirt rising...there was a car! ~His face gleamed and he began to gather his things once again he was ready to take off. But the car turned the opposite way and drove away from him he began screaming and flipped the middle finger at the car. He sat back down and opened another beer he slugged it down quickly and tossed it at the pile.~ ‘I’m walking out…’ He stood now ready he had his shit and was ready to go. ~3 Hours later and about 12 miles down the road Shocka now approaches what appears to be a small town. He walks into the nearest gas station and grabs a phone book for shits and giggles? I think not…to call a fucking taxi you moron.~ ‘Yeah come pick me up please!’ Shocka spoke into the phone with desperation in his voice. ‘Ten minutes…sweet.’ Shocka hung up the phone and now walked to the back of the store to buy a bottle of water he brought it to the front paid for it and headed outside to wait. ~When the taxi arrived Shocka jumped into the back, he’d never been so happy to see a taxi driver in his entire life.~ ‘To Melbourne please.’ Shocka directed and he was on his way. ~Sleep came faster than he ever thought it would in the back of a cab on a bumpy road. When he awoke he was already nearing one of the larger Australian cities, Melbourne. He looked at the running fare it read 222.24$ his eyes lit up and he realized he would never be able to pay that. He quickly thought of an exit plan he scooted close to the door grabbed his bags opened the door tucked and rolled hitting the pavement and rolling trying to avoid road rash. When he came to a stop he rose to see the taxi cab stopping he smiled did a nip up grabbed his bags and was in a dead sprint before the cabbi could open his door Shocka was a good 50 yards. The man turned began to give way in his car but it was no use as Shocka cut off road down through a gully and then a across a river soaking his pants.~ ‘The drunken sprint to get away from a cab fare…nothing like it.’ He smiled as he now looked into the City he was a mere half mile away from downtown now it seemed. ~He pulled out a sheet of paper with his hand writing smeered across it giving the directions to the wrestlers hotel. 20 minutes later his walk was over and he was checking into his hotel room provided by eVolution. A shower and a nap later, the buzz had begun to wear off.~ ~Many question needed to be answered and they will be right now…how did Johnny Charisma pull of a victory over Scott Frost and Jenna Jameson…Jason Jameson that is…it was a flash in the pan type of a thing ya know. Johnny is fully aware of what Shocka is capable of in the ring so he is aware that he is dealing with a full fledged veteran.~ ‘Now it has begun for you Johnny the end is nearing you realize that now that you have drawn the battle lines we are fiends. That you will now have to deal with a man that is untouchable to most in this world…what makes you think that you are up to my level? Just because you beat a couple of other former piece of shit paper weight champions like Scott Frost and the man that beat Scott, Jason…so big fucking deal. Now you are going to be stepping into the ring with a man that has seen and done it all in eVolution and is ready to be going around for a second time and you are just some young jobber that I have to dispose of. So you can puff up your chest with all of the air that you need but you will still come to the same conclusion at the end of the night…you simply can’t handle me I’m going to drop you from the top rope on your back and head leaving you motionless just like I have Scott Frost, L2J, Kimson had been laid out by me, roC, Omega ask anyone on the roster what I have put them through.’ His grin was now one of pure evil…his intentions were direct. ‘I’m in danger…young man you have no idea that you are only treading water…eVolution is a small pond with a few big fishes and lots of little ones buddy…people like you and Scott Frost are just fish food for people like me, Xander, and Asis…the sooner you realize that….the less pain you are dealt.’ FADE.
