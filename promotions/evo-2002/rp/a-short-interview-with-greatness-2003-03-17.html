~~~The camera opens outside of the airport in Seattle, Washington as Jason Relms as just got off the air plane. He is walking to his car as one of the people from the airline places all his lugage in his black hummer. He looks around and before he can even get into his car one of the eVo camera's are their waiting to get a couple of words from the newly signed superstar. As they come up to him Ms.Network comes out of the airline and hopes in the hummer. She ask Jason please not to take a long time that she is ready to get to the hotel and go to sleep.So the eVo interviewer begins to speak.~~~ Interviewer:'Jason Relms, glad to see you can finaly make it to Seattle, So are...' 'The F'N Network'Jason Relms:'Shut Up,Don't you ever make it sound like I want to be in a place like Seattle, Washington. This place is filled with stupid asses and trailer park trash. I mean when you have a great superstar like Gary Payton, The DAMN GLOVE man who has been the reason for the little glory they had, and what does this town do? Trade his ass, Well Gary I am happy for you because you are better then a pathetic half ass basketball team that the Sonics are. Seattle, what is that damn thing it's famous for,The Neddle or something, I mean it sure as hell aint the seahawks.I know the reason it's famous, it's the town with the most peons in it. And soon the number is about to raise two because Playaz Inc. should be arriving soon enough.' Interviewer:'So I take it you saw their interview.' 'The F'N Network'Jason Relms:'Yea I saw it, then I turned it. Then when I saw it again, what was it I did, O yea I through the damn remote at the tv because it disgusted me to think that evolution would let such trash air on televison. Dudda, Dummy, Duzza, WHATEVER your damn name is you and your partner status 'Average' did nothing but prove the point I was making , your nothing but peons. When the faces are clued to the tv it want be to see the wanna be's, it want be to see the world champ or the president of eVo, ALL eyes will be on THE F'N NETWORK JASON RELMS!' Interviewer:'Mr.Relms, I see that Ms.Network is here, where is your partner,High Impact?' 'The F'N Network'Jason Relms:'Do I look like his damn baby sitter. You exspect him to be by my side every time I step into the Fuckin camera. I don't know where he is right now. But if I wanted to talk to him I would call him. I mean it aint like he needs to be right here by myside. How about Ask a decent question.' Interviewer:'OK,Well' 'The F'N Network'Jason Relms:'Damn it, shut up PEON!Remind me to tell Sommers to fire your ass. Let me show you how to conduct a damn interview with Jason Relms. First off refer to me as F'N Greatness. And ask me how many rating's I will bring in. What is it like to be so built?What time do I plan to be tag champs. Who will bleed more in the match Dummy or Status 'Average'?' Interviewer:'Ok,F'N Greatness,Who will blee..' 'The F'N Network'Jason Relms:'Damn Man, You suck. Give me the mic and get lost, I hope your mamma has got you a job back at the house selling lemonade because you sure aint good enough for Jason Relms.' ~The Interviewer walks away as Jason Relms has the mic and finishes the interview himself.~ 'The F'N Network'Jason Relms:'Come Wednesday, I show the world how great Team Network is. Like I said the time is comming for Playaz Inc. And also for the night that evolution see's things will never be the same. Wait let me tell you why,you know it's comming. Press record at home, and start flashing the pictures as I tell you why..because I AM THE F'N NETWORK!' ~~~Jason Relms gets in his hummer and rides off as the camera fades to black.~~~
