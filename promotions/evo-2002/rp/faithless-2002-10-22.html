Faithless

22 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd October So, Faith is now even closer, just a few days before Seara and Asis finally get to go one on one and see just who will come out on top. Or even just who will come out alive. Because although there is no stipulation added to the match yet, fans and employees alike are talking amongst themselves of a stipulation being added to the match that would suit both wrestlers temperaments. Even if no stipulation is added to the match, with the amount of personal issues surrounding these two wrestlers, it will be a battle no matter what. More fuel was added to the already raging inferno by both wrestlers this week. Seara attacked Asis after his match with a steel chair leaving him with a possibly broken nose. Later on in the night Asis got his own form of twisted revenge, when he attacked Seara’s brother in the parking lot, leaving him in hospital unconscious with a broken neck, never to walk again. Since then Seara has had certain words to say to Asis, things that hopefully she can back up with actions, otherwise she might die to regret them… (To us the house is familiar, but we can’t figure out why. From the outside it looks nice enough, quite a quaint little house, ideal for a quiet life in the suburbs of Chicago. A nice little one floor bungalow, in the perfect location for a retired life. The night is clear, not a cloud in the sky and the moon and the stars shine brightly. But although it is a clear and calm night, it is the middle of October, and the air is bitterly cold. Although looking picturesque from the outside, inside is far from normal. The living room is trashed, the furniture tipped over and thrown about the place, the wooden table snapped and splintered. The television set’s screen has been smashed a huge crackling, fizzing hole where the pictures used to come up on the screen. The kitchen looks fine, nothing out of place, and nothing has been taken away, weird how it’s not in a state like the living room was. Moving towards the bedroom it’s obvious that it’s going to be in a mess because firstly the door is off its hinges. As you move through the doorway you scan the room quickly taking in the entire room has been destroyed. Sitting on the bed amongst the mess is a man you know, but have never met. A man you hoped you’d never have to meet but knew eventually you probably would. Asis sits on the bed looking straight at the man who just stares straight back at him, seemingly showing no fear. Asis respects this, as he sees that the man respects himself. Especially seeing as he’s in a wheelchair and would have no chance of standing up for himself. Maybe if he weren’t in a wheelchair he would actually stand a chance. Back in his day he would’ve been some kind of opponent, if he weren’t in a wheelchair he would stand taller than Asis and he wasn’t small either. Not as big as Asis, but he’d still be able to take most people on and give them a good fight. Sitting in his wheelchair the man is thinking that four years ago he would be able to put up some kind of fight against this man. He knows what Asis is capable of as he has watched him fight his daughter several times on television and if he was twenty years younger he’d probably put up some kind of resistance against him. But as it is, he’s thinking about whether he’s going to be straight out killed, or just left on a life support machine like his son Jake. He notices a cameraman in the room with them and thinks that it’s obvious Asis has pre-orchestrated this. But then there’s the question of whether he’s just doing it to get Seara pissed off, or whether he’s actually going to attack him for fun?) I suggest you sit still and stay quiet old man. That is if you value your life. (Seara’s dad stays completely still, but gives Asis a hard glare.) So, just a few days away from getting my hands on you Seara. Just a few days before I end this stupid feuding once and for all. Just a few days before I will make your suffering end. Since Digital Destruction you just haven’t been able to leave me. Since I put you in hospital for the first time you’ve held a grudge against me. I did what I did because I was fed up with it all, all the speculation about us, about our relationship together. I did it to make people realise that you’re not someone that I’m gonna let get in my way. You’re not my lover, my friend, you’re not anyone that I’m gonna let effect my life. I think I’ve proven that over the past couple of weeks and I think you’ve also come to feel the same way about me. I hope also that I taught you a lesson about family and love last Wednesday when I put that cretin of a brother of yours in hospital. I wanted to show you that you should cherish what family you have left. Even if you think that you hate them, family is precious and you should be grateful that you’ve got it. I hope I taught you that by reducing the amount of family you have. And now I’m here with your dear old Dad, considering whether I need to teach you another lesson about family that will also hopefully teach you how to not disrespect people like me. But you’re not going to see whether or not I’m gonna do it. I’m gonna let you think about whether I have or not…
