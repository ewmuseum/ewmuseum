Criminal Acts: Part 3, Learning more

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 9th, 2003 -=[We find the gang (Thug roC, Adam Carr, Vega, and Machine) all at a local restaurant called Alia’s. The crew walks into a fancy place where formal wear is required. Adam Carr approaches the host with money in his hand]=- “I would like to make reservations…” Adam Carr says, trying to be as polite as possible. “OK sir” The host responds as he flips through his schedule. “I can fit you in on December 14th sometime in the afternoon…” “Oh no…” Adam Carr says. “I forgot to tell you the date, I’m sorry. I want to make it for next Saturday” “HAHAHAHAHA!” The host laughs loudly, finding this VERY funny. “What’s so funny?” Thug roC asks. “ HAHAHAHAH! Everybody knows that you need to book reservations more then a week in advance. Maybe you should try IHOP or somewhere else sir.” -=[Adam Carr turns back to the group very upset. All four don’t know what to do as they are trying to find ways to help their friend. They’re obviously on a budget now that the thousand dollar necklace has been stolen.]=- “Well we can always try Pablo’s off of Route 1, they have good food” Thug roC mentions. “Isn’t that the place where that girl got food posioning?” Vega asks, trying to recall the event. “Oh yea…” Machine then states, “What about Red Lobster off of Dale Blvd?” “Tasha won’t eat there because of that man who complained about the food and got shot.” Adam replies. “Well that’s a good reason.” -=[The group all hold their heads down. Machine notices a blonde haired man sitting at a nearby booth wearing a Dallas Cowboys sweatshirt. ]=- “That’s what I’m talking about. A Dallas fan here in the D.C. area.” Machine says in a very happy tone. “So?” Adam Carr doesn’t see anything special about it. “You know how hard it is to find a Cowboy fan?” Machinea asks. “Let alone one here in the DC area. I’m going to go meet him.” -=[Machine leaves the group towards the blonde hair guy. That’s when you can almost recognize him from before.]=- “Hey man, you from Texas or something? It’s pretty rare to find hardcore Cowboy fans now that they’ve started losing. I mean just because the Boys had two or three bad seasons, or 7; everyone hates them.” Machine stands there with a goofy smily on his face. “Bug off punk. This was on sale cause nobody else would buy it.” The guy says, not even looking up at him. “Oh…well the Cowboys are a good team, don’t underestimate them.” Machine’s tone isn’t as high anymore. “They can easily make a comeback this season.” “I’m tired of your presence, can you leave now?” “Damn man, just trying to be friendly” “Have you ever went to sleep and never woke up?” The guy asks. “No” “Try it sometime.” “Well….” Machine then stops talking suddenly. He looks at the man’s ring which is a tiger ring with the trademark ruby eyes and an emerald in his mouth. Machine turns frightened and backs away. He is almost terrified as he approaches Thug roC “Awww…did the big bad man hurt your feelings?” Thug roC says, mocking Machine. “Look at his ring…” Machine whispers “HOLY SH!T” Vega screams. “Shut your mouth Vega, he must be with Vulgar’s gang” Adam Carr stating the obvious. -=[The guy’s cell phone rings and the group goes quiet to hear what he has to say. They all turn their backs to him, making it look like they aren’t listening. It is obvious to the watching fans that this guy is indeed Hit-Kid, Greg Vulgar’s right-hand man]=- “ Yea man….it’s no problem I already bought the tickets….Yea we leave tommorrow at noon….No not BWI, we’re leaving out of Dulles…Aight bye” Hit-Kid then hangs up his phone. He looks up and sees the group of four quietly leave the resturaunt. “Aight, we need to call the cops and let them know about tommorrow” Thug roC says as the four enter the SUV “Tommorrow we need to be at the Dulles Airport by noon. Obviously that blonde hair is gonna leave the state, and if we catch him, we might get to Vulgar.”
