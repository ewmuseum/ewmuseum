Rise To Glory Or Fall To Shame

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/23/02 A clock ticks away, its pendulum echoing in the wind. The seconds slowly tick away, with the minute hand waiting to meet its target. The future erases the past, now only a memory lost in doubt. The time nears, and soon, only one will rise to the occasion. I have told you that I don’t care about you. You could live forever or die the most painful death imaginable, and all I will do is look away. I will simply spit into the wind, and walk away. I can never care about a world that slowly shits itself into a hole in the ground. I could never feel any sense of sorrow for people who have dies if they never had a life to live in the first place. I can not care about existence if I know that I can never last forever. The only thing that matters is happiness – it will be the only thing we can experience that will have any significance to our lives. We can believe that love is the best, but it isn’t. It’s only pain after pain, agony felt for no compensation. Hatred is only envy. No emotion can ever last, so why not take the one that has the most positive effect on you. Maybe that it why seven men and one woman have decided to take part in a battle of athleticism and strength to see which one will come out on top. One will rise to glory and take this whole thing. That man, or woman, will have been declared one of the best. Only then can one of us be happy. Only then can one of us carry something with them that actually matters. Only then can we be content with the lives that we are burdened to live. I won’t make up some random bull shit. I’m not going to go on a tangent by saying that ‘I will kick all their asses and become the Road to Glory champion’. I will only promise you that I will put on a show, and fight until the very end. If it isn’t enough, so be it. Someone else will win, and all I’ll do is turn away. I will not care. I will survive. I will rise. But the real question is… When will I come back down?
