Words are only threats..

18 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-18 The scene fades into a room where the young black man is standing. The room is very dull looking. Not to much furniture as the only thing that seems to be in it is a table a chair and lights in it. You can tell easy that its just a small room of a apartment but the yon black man being X just stands in the corner. He is wearing some dark blue baggy jeans with a white t-shirt. The white t-shirt has his name 'The Black Label' written across the front. Also he has on some bornw timberland boots. He stands there looking at the table. He then points down to the table.. On the table is a pencil and some sheets of paper. He then takes a seat behind the table as he writes... (Everything you see is written by X) 'This is no threat..I am taking a day of silence..A day to morn Damon as it is not his fault..' He then looks up at the eVo cameras. He then looks back down and continues to write.. 'A pawn in this game..One that has only the hope of the true capture..One that moves few steps..not in giant leaps..The difference between us is more than just color Damon..The difference in this game..As in a game of chest..Your no more then what I said you were..except a white pawn that is..Figured I add that since you want to make black and white hats..anyways..you have no chance..You have no oppurtunity to put me in check..because in this game my friend..there is more then just one dominate player..There is more than just one powerful figure on my side of the table..lets just say he is a black KING..' As soon as he wrties that he looks over to see no other than a poster of his manager on the wall..He then continues to write.. 'Damon..What is it you see??Why do you honestly believe that you are better then The Black Label??Are you so celf centered with yourself an your irish background that it hasnt lead you to the truth behind the wall you place in front of your eyes..' 'Why is it Damon?..All these questions I have is for a purpose..Its to help me morn better. Haveing the background of your ignorance and the choices you have made to favor this corperate america will do nothin but help me undestand why it is people like you dont remain just ordinary people when I am through..Why people like you just remain..a..Xample..' He then places the pencil down as he sits in that chair still in complete silence...He has a smirk on his face as he stands infront of the eVo cameras..He then points to his chest were it shows the word 'Black Label'.As the scene fades to black..
