Its fun to be me....The HBC

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-28-02 Continental Title ~ Four Corners Match Shodan vs. Jason Jameson vs. The HeartBreak Chick vs. Tommy Michaels Dateline: Toronto, Ontario, Canada Location: HBC’s Estate, Lake Ontario Time: Late-evening Beware of the woman scorned. Is a taleful tale that always seems to come true and repeat itself like a vicious cycle. Never ending, never changing, not even in Evolution. Faith though is something some people believe in, believe in so desperately eventhough their ending or demise is right before their very eyes. Still though, they shrug it off believing in Faith that it will somehow pull themselves through the unavoidable. Those people, are morons. Big Daddy Dreamer is one of those people. He thought he had all the answers, he thought he knew me and the depths that I would go, but the fact of the matter is my dear Dreamer, you, like your Faith, were gravely wrong. You really had no idea how far I would go to defeat you, to solidify my place in history as not-just the greatest cruiserweight of all-time, no, my place is higher than that. Higher than just the greatest female athlete of all-time, because quite frankly, I’ve already achieved those two feats. No. My place since from the time I was a little girl is to be the greatest ever, of all-time, in anything and everything. And I’ve achieved it all, except as the top wrestler of all-time. You Dreamer, were just a catalyst to help fuel my quest. At Faith, history was made. Teacher versus student, but unlike those Hollywood scripts which we all know are fake as Anna Nicole sincerity, the student prevailed. Actually that isn’t the appropriate word……Conquered. That’s more like it. The student conquered her mentor, doing something that hasn’t been done in six years. Doing something that has never been done by any wrestler in history. Sending Big Daddy Dreamer away on a gurney, because he was unable to move under his own power. No one in your illustrious history D, has ever done that have they. Of course not. People have injured you, people have beaten you up, but no one, has ever sent you away on a gurney and even possibly, just possibly, have ended your career. The legendary Big Daddy who ruled nearly every federation with an iron fist was taken down once and for all by ‘The Heartbreaker.’ I must admit though, I do get a certain delight in destroying you. Breaking you. Hell D, its better than sex! Now I know what you have been saying when you hold someone’s career in the palm of your hands, the power it gives you. Now I understand because as I said D, I hold your career in the palm of my hands. To do with it what I desire. Hurting you further, letting you rest, or just making you paranoid. The secret I hold for example gives me the power to control two people. Two people who will do my bidding because they know the consequences if they don’t. Magdalena is one of those people. Isn’t it a bit ironic she of all people is the one who distracted the referee at the key opportune time? It wasn’t me. It wasn’t the Unlimited. But her. What is her agenda? Her motive? You must admit she did come out of nowhere D? I thought you were bright D, I guess not. Perhaps D, just perhaps……she could be working for me? What a delightful ironic twist THAT would be. She has some explaining to do doesn’t she D? But will she? Will she? I don’t think so. See, like I am doing, is making you fall further into madness from which there is no escape from. By the way D, how is your head? You still seeing stars? Can you see at all? Are you blind yet? I must admit I haven’t gotten the word from my spies yet so please enlighten me won’t you D. I know for a fact your orbital socket was broken thanks to yours truly. I can’t imagine Jinx pounding on your eye felt all that good. Nor can I imagine all those chairshots you took at Faith could have helped matters much. How many stitches did it take to close that gash I put into you. Please tell me, we have a little poll going in The Unlimited. I said it took about 30-40 stitches. Am I close? Oh Dreamer, Dreamer, Dreamer, Dreamer. Oh how much I delight in tormenting you. How it felt when I took those bolt cutters, swung like Barry Bonds, and smashed them into the side of your face. Hearing the cracking sound, the sickening thud of twenty pounds of pig iron smashing into your injured area. I got off on it D, I really did. I had a major orgasm in the middle of the ring watching you fall unconscious. And since you know I go both ways D, I delighted in taking Magdalena, and wrapping her pretty face around those steel steps before giving her ‘The Kiss of Death’ in superkicking her jaw clean off her shoulders. You know something, come to think of it, ‘The Sweet BadAss Superkick’ is so cliché now. I mean everyone is doing it. From this day forth, it shall be renamed ‘The Kiss of Death’. That is more appropriate don’t you think? I mean I am the HeartBreaker after all. So I tell you what, why don’t you two take alittle time off, regroup, and try to somehow, just somehow, defeat me. In the mean time though, I’ll still be around. I’ll still be manipulating the both of you from behind the curtain, still covertly and overtly tormenting the both of you. Maybe I’ll break that other eye socket of yours D. Wouldn’t that be fun? But for now, I have something else to do. It appears that I’m scheduled to be in a four corners re-match for the Continental Championship. A belt I should have rightfully won the first time. Shodan, Jason Jameson and Tommy Michaels are all vying along with me for the Continental Championship title. Shodan, the most recent inaugry of the gold having just won it a few days ago at Faith in a grueling, brutal match-up with Thug Roc’s Shocka. Not that there is any love lost their, I’m always pleased to see Thug Roc crying in his boots about another plan that didn’t go his way. However, this isn’t Faith and I’m not Shocka. I’m the HeartBreaker, the new, improved, take no prisoners, sassy, sexy, oh so slutty, ass-kicker that just knocked off a legend in our industry and put him on the shelf indefinitely, HeartBreak Chick, HBC! There is no way in hell you are a hundred percent heading into this match-up. I know your going to be determined to keep your belt, and that is real noble of you. A fighting spirit stemming back from the Samurai of old. Yes, I know your Japanese training skills quite well. How much you admire their culture, so I’ll tell you what I’m going to do. I’ll contact some of my old friends back in the F-1 promotion in Tokyo and see if they have a spot for a former champion like yourself because at Legends XIII, my favorite number, that gold you hold so dear, you can ‘Kiss’ it goodbye. Besides we all know the gold looks so much better around my sexy waist than any of yours. Let’s be real. Who would the people rather see. Some hulking brut with twenty pounds of gold hidden by a flabby stomach, or me, the pin-up pictorial queen of wrestling and the gold, conveniently placed, tantalizing my assets. I mean really. The choice is real dam clear. However, why is beyond me, both Michaels and Jameson are also in this contest. As I look at it, Tommy, you owe me. You owe me, what for you might add, well its simple, you owe me because you couldn’t defeat Big Daddy Dreamer without my assistance. He basically handed you your ass in that match until I decided to come down and even things up. If it wasn’t for me, you would have never have won that match. I distracted him long enough for you to nail him with the chair. I gave you that match so you could elevate your career a notch and now Michaels, its payback time. I’m here to collect. And the price is the Continental Championship around my waist. Because Tommy, you really don’t want to get on my bad side. Have you taken a look around as of late. The bodies I’ve busted and tossed aside like yesterday’s trash. No, you do this for me, and I will see to it you are compensated handsomely by The Unlimited. Cross me, and you can share the hospital room with Dreamer. Lastly, we have Jason Jameson. You know something, I’ve always wondered this. Are you somehow related to porn queen Jenna Jameson? She’s pretty hot. She could eat me out. If you two are related. Give me her number and I’ll go easy on you, its that simple. If your not related to her, then you really have nothing I’m interested in, so hence, you must be wasting my time. And The Heartbreaker doesn’t like her time wasted. It is the one commodity in this world that their ISN’T an abundant supply of. So if you are wasting my time Jason, then bad things will have to happen to you. Real bad things. Seeing that Halloween, my second favorite holiday, is rapidly approaching, I just my have too disembowel you for nothing more than my own enjoyment. Because I like those scary kinds of things. With the mood I’m feeling, give me some sharp objects and let me goto town. I’ll show you what the Aztecs used to do on that very day. Carve out your beating heart and hold it up to the gods before crushing it underneath my stiletto bootheels. Now that is an image! Jameson, wrong place, wrong time, stay out of my way, and nothing will happen to you, get involved……..hey, we got plenty of coffins out back with your name on it just waiting for you. Now before I go, I know one thing most of you simpletons are dying to know. What will the HeartBreaker be wearing for the annual Halloween Costume contest? Well I guess your just going to have to tune into Legends XIII and find out. Until then, pleasant dreams……….
