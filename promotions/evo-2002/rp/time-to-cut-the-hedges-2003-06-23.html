Time to cut the Hedges......

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-23 Luther Wisdom is seen with the BodySnatchers in Malcolm King's lockerroom. He's not distraught in losing the titles, in fact he's actually happy that he has been relieved of that albatross which was Hedges....... 'I replay the events of Saturday night in my head.....and I smile. It was like a liberation, a new awakening for me as now I have a new forum The Genesis Pit to make my message heard.' The BodySnatchers mill around in the background....... 'Hedges the same office politics you speak of were the same forces that had benefitted you greatly. You claim to not have heard from me in a month.....which ironically is 30 days longer than we had heard from you. Yet you have the audacity to claim you want the Evolved title back? Do you know something we don't know Hedges? Can we assume that the same politics that apparently helped me out.....now aid you? I mean I have every right for a rematch you do...even moreso in my opinion. Afterall you left me in the match....in the middle of that ring. I'm sure someone overly concerned with that title would have been a champion and fought through the linked situation instead of taking his ball and going home. That is of course unless you knew you were slated for a rematch....then again wrestling ettiquette dictates that I....not you face Eric Knight for the Evolved title first; but lets see the political machine at work.' Dolo Bentswinger looks out of the window..... Dolo:'She's here......' Both he and 1001 Holds leaves to meet this so far unidentified woman.....as Wisdom continues. 'Only those who are weak minded and equally weak in ability blame everything on what is a percieved notion of office politics. It's easier to blame your short comings on Tyler Davidson Hedges than to take a look in the mirror and be honest with yourself......you are not that good. Overrated actually comes to mind. You got your spot...through past associations to begin with and then have the nerve to accuse or better yet condemn those who may or may not have done the same.....look around for a minute Hedges you are nothing without NEW-TV; and yet your little taste of success has forced you to bite the hand that feeds you. In my opinion Hedges you are the epitome of pathetic......so it stands to reason your constant crying about the backstage will get you your shot as undeserved as it may be.' Wisdom cracks a smile....... 'Which means I will have to take matters in my own hands and bring to reality that is the fine print on everyones programs which states.....'card is subject to change'. There is no way Hedges your unreliable ass will get another run at the Evolved title. Is it any coincidence that since we shared the title that the marquee has always listed my matches as the actual title defense and not yours?......ohh I forgot Office politics. Too bad your theory is not a sound one...I am the last man Tyler Davidson wants as a champion in this federation, my history of stability has a lot of questions surrounding it. So unlike the BITCH half of the former Evolved and Tag Team champions; or should I say the Evolved and the division with too much competition for Hedges.....I'm going to break it down real simple so that even Hedges can understand.' Wisdom goes over to the counter top and pours out two glassess of wine...... 'Eric Knight you don't have to worry about Hedges because it is I who will take not only your Evolved title but also The Tag Team titles held by ST.Violence. That is the very least Evolution owes me for carrying around the dead weight Hedges. But I like to collect intrest.....and it will be me finally staking MY claim to a bigger belt; regardless of what Malcolm King may think.....or want.' Luther is disturbed by the shutting of a door. He turns around with the two glasses in his hand to see a very shapely figure whose features are hidden by the shadows.... woman:'Long time no see.....' Wisdom:'You are a sight for sore eyes....' woman:'flatterer you don't mean it...LOL; Malcolm will be surprised won't he?' Wisdom:'King still thinks you are in Japan....he wouldn't approve of what I'm about to do.' Woman:'Ohhh really and her I thought we could have a threesome.....' Wisdom:'LOL...King's married he wouldn't....' Woman:'I meant business wise like in the old days.' Wisdom:'Ohhhh.......don't worry I'm actually doing a little freelance work.' woman:'mmmmmm....yummy.'
