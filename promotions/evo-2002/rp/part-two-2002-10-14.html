Part Two

14 October 2002

Date: October 14, 2002 We continue from where we left off. A completely ravaged Jinosyde, or Aaron Chamberland sits himself down on the blood absorbing sheets of the bed, beside his 'girlfriend' Maili, the blood came from her. He continues to sit there with his head down and tears rolling down his cheeks. His thoughts slowly become nothing but a mere clot of tragedy, disaster, and heartbreak. Why Maili? Why not me? Who could've done this? What could've done this? What kind of sinister mind could kill a woman of complete innocence. What kind of sinister mind could attack an innocent person to get back at another person, someone who had nothing to do with anything is attacked for no reason? I will figure out who did this, I will figure out who did this and when I do, they've live to regret they crossed Aaron Chamberland.. forget about the live part. I slowly forced myself upward from the bed and slowly took astep away from it. The plain white t-shirt that I was wearing had a large streak of red going down the back. I wish I was there when it happened, it made it seem like the red streak on my back.. was yellow. I turned toward the broken mirror and slowly took a few steps over toward it. The glass was somewhat attracting me to it. Whatever it was that got me over there, and whatever it was, I think it changed my life forever. I slowly took my small step toward the mirror and came toward a halt. I looked at it and turned around behind me, I saw the word, 'MURDER' written grungily on the mirror. I turned around and looked over Maili's body and saw the word 'REDRUM'. It caught me off guard, I stumbled backward abit bumping into the creamy white, dirty looking dresser. Off of it fell onto the ground, a book. I turned toward it and slowly kneeled down, scooping it up and slowly unsnapping the lock. I stared at the suade surface, a burgandy suade that was almost inviting to the spirit and the mind. It was like it shouted out, 'Open me up..' I couldn't resist the temptation. I slowly opened it to a random page. Unknown Book: Page 28 'I don't think I know how long I can take it. I won't tell my angel, Aaron, about my problem. I think he'd take it too much as a surprise. He'd probably take it too seriously too, everyone says that we should get together, that he's like my guardian angel. I think so too, but it's hard to quit what i'm doing and how it's going to be. I'll never tell, not for my life. Even if it means that i'll get killed for it. I will never tell. I looked at the pages and the entry and slowly looked toward the ceiling. I was thinking toward myself, 'What is she doing?'. It wouldn't leave my mind. It wasn't going to leave my mind until I figured out, that's probably the reason why she didn't tell me. I looked at the pages, and slowly licked the tip of my index finger, I slowly slid the pages toward the left looking at all of the entries. One caught my attention. It caught my attention because of the color that it was written in. Not in black, but in red. Was this a special entry? Did she just lose a black ink pen? I couldn't take the chance of not reading it, I had to know what she was doing and WHY she was killed. Diary of Maili: Page 41 I'm getting more clients as they come. Most of them are black, but that's what happens when you do what I do in Los Angeles. A grungy city that is over-estimated to be cool. This town sucks, and all the guys here are poor. I'm taking my business else where, maybe i'll follow Aaron. Yeah, I'm going to follow Aaron because this gives me a chance to get more money in a fast amount of time, he travels everywhere. My services are needed elsewhere. Whatever the matter, I can't let Aaron find out. He'll lose all of his respect for me. I hope he doesn't know already, he's seen me around a couple of times and asked me what I was doing. I would normally just tell him that I was walking back to my house, even if I'm facing the wrong direction, or I'm like ten! blocks away. I hate lying to Aaron, but I have to. I slowly looked at the book and slowly closed the book and locked it back to where it was. I slowly slid it into my pocket and looked down at Maili's body. I looked over toward the letters on the wall and said toward myself, 'What the hell does that mean?' I turned over toward the door and slowly walked over toward the door and opened it. 'Someone'll find her body, nothing more I can do here..' I looked down at her body once more, the gigantic hole in her chest. I couldn't just leave her, I reached into the opposite pocket of the book and slid out my 33-90 Nokia, and dialed 911. The call was answered.. '911 Emergency Line, what's your request?' the woman who answered said to me as calm as possible. I thought it would sound different, I'd never had to dial 911 before so I was startled. 'Um, yes! I need an ambulance and the police here quickly! Someone murdered my girl... my friend! Someone murdered my friend! There's a gigantic hole in her chest, looks like someone worked a knife into her or something, I don't know. I can see her insides and her flesh is rotting, the smell is unbearable! Hurry!' 'We'll be sending them right away. What is the address?' 'Can't you trace it? It's not private, I'm in a hotel, just get here as fast as you can.' I hung the phone up and left the room quickly. If I would've stayed any longer. I would've died of suffocation. The stench was unbearable. I can't take this shit. I must know who it is! I slowly turned toward the steps and walked down them slowly. I looked as I saw everyone looking around. Commotion everywhere as I saw the police man looking toward me. 'Excuse me, son. Are you the guy who called us?' 'Yeah, my friend up there was murdered. Go take a look investigate and all of that shit. I can't stay up there any longer.' 'Hey! You're Jinosyde from eVolution! I want an autograph! Please...' 'Look, will you just do your fucking job you moron?!' I about had a coniption. I was angry to a point that can't be examined. I couldn't cry anymore. I could only tear, but I couldn't cry for her. Just... just like my dad. 'Yeah, yeah. Anyway, tell me what you saw happened. Eddie. Go up there and check out the body. Laura, asist him. Where's the medic at?' Sirens from the distant background could be heard clearly. I saw the people rush through the door with stretchers and all of the other. They looked around as the police officer. Pointed up the steps at 'Eddie'. They followed him up the steps, the odor was seeping through the open door, of the room and was coming down the steps. I believe I was the first to smell it coming down the steps. Either that or I became kin with it. I knew I reeked of that stench, and I reeked of another stench. A stench of failure. I felt as if I failed Maili in protecting her. I don't deserve to be called her guardian angel. I don't even deserve to know her. 'Now, tell me all that you saw?' 'Well, when I walked up there, the room was in complete ravage. I looked around to see if anyone was in there, I was cautious but then I saw some writing on the wall. I caught my attention for a good second but then I smelled this foul stench that just hit me right in the face and churned my stomach completely into a knot. I turned over toward the body and there was Maili... //t-h-i-n-k\\Should I tell him about the Diary? No! and then that's when I called you guys.' 'And where were you before the murder commenced?' 'I was at the club, I don't know when the murder happened or what it happened with, all I know is that she's dead and I'm going to find out who killed her.' 'You must leave the work to.. HEY! Where're you going!' 'I don't have time to listen to your bullshit, I'm gonna find a killer... no worse. A monster.' I pushed open the strong wooden doors infront of me and slowly stepped out into the night. Looking for something that is feared by all. A reaper. A reaper of the innoncent. .fade.to.eternity.
