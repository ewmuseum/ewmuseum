Before the United title match

26 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-02-26 Hours before his upcoming United Championship title shot, Big Daddy Dreamer is sitting down with Evolution magazine writer Jenna Haze talking about his life, Syndicate, HBC and his future. Haze: Thanks for joining me today Dreamer, I really appreciate it. BDD: Not a problem, shall we begin? Haze: Tonight you face off against Damon O’Halloran, what are your thoughts on the match tonight? BDD: Simple really, I plan to win obviously. Nothing against Damon but the United Championship is the #2 singles title here in Evolution and truth be told, he is nowhere near the second best wrestler in the company. We have a lot of guys, myself included who are far better than Damon and we should be the ones holding the belt. Damon is a flash, the current feel good player, but like many others, fade out as fast as he came in, which is why, it is my duty to the industry and all those who have paid their dues to win this belt, and defend it accordingly. Haze: So basically your saying, he was handed the belt and isn’t worthy of holding it. BDD: Yes exactly. What has he done really. Nothing. Now don’t get me wrong, he looks to be a great worker and has some ability, but deep down he doesn’t have that ‘X’ factor, that IT factor that packs the seats every night like myself or HBC for example. So as I said, tonight, Damon has to go down in the crossface because I won’t have it any other way. Haze: I see. BDD: I’m not sure you do Jenna. See the United Championship is the only belt I have not held during my career in Evolution. I’ve won the Universal, I’ve won the Platinum, I’ve won the Tag titles twice, this one is the Grandslam for me. BDD, United and Grandslam champion, nice ring to it isn’t it. I’ll be damned if Damon screws this up for me. If he don’t lay down like the bitch he is, he will regret ever getting in this business and ever facing me. I’m in the business of hurting people. I enjoy hurting people. Damon is just another name holding something I want. Haze: Is the Syndicate over as a tag team? BDD: Never say never, right now we are focusing on other opportunities but we will always team together and be a threat to the tag titles. The world knows that. Next. Haze: What do you think about HBC’s sudden rise to fame? BDD: First off, it isn’t sudden. She has been paying her dues for over seven years. She earned everything she ever got despite management, the old-school male mentality or the thinking that women shouldn’t compete with men. HBC defies all that. HBC is hands down one of the greatest superstars of all time, better than 90% of males on the roster and its about damn time the world took note of that. I tell you this, and I better see this in your magazine. HBC will become the first ever female Universal Champion, bet on it. She deserves it and she will become it. Now if you excuse me, I got to get ready for my title match. Have a good day Miss Haze.
