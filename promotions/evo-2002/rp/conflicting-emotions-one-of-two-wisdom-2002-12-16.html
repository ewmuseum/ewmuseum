Conflicting Emotions...one of two (Wisdom)

16 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/16/02 Luther Wisdom is sitting in front of a bank of videoscreens. Each monitor has a different video image of Sovereign from his OSW days right up until last weeks Legend. He seems to bo on the phone with someone..... LW:'Of course I know what I'm doing....I have Sovereign right where I want him. Please.....he seems to think that I am without my own resources, much like a Pavlovian dog if you provide the proper stimulai he will react. At Legends he will react just the way we want him too. No he won't injure me at Legends....quite frankly he doesn't have it in him to do so; don't worry the tag team title shot is safe.' he toggles a button on his console freezing an image from last weeks Legends. He takes a look at the still image.... 'In fact I may have found something that I can use to my advantage. Can't go into it now afterall both you and I know what a headcase he is.Actually using him as a pawn will be easier than expected based on his psychological profile or should I say lack there of. LOL....no I don't mean getting intouch with his inner William. Legends will be what I consider first contact, however it won't be the last. Career suicide? Who me ? Never! I find it interesting that I can actually put a muzzle on this particular wolf.' he stares at the still image. It's obvious that the person on the other end has been in contact with Sovereign in the past..... 'He does implore a bit of revisionist history doesn't he? No bother...it's not about the past it's about the present and future. Much like this stable thing one battle doesn't make a war. Of course I'm going to war with Sovereign......as I said before what do I have to lose? Hell it's not in my nature to sit around and be used as cannon fodder. Before the night is done I will make an Impact on Sovereign he won't soon forget.' he hangs up the phone..... 'You see dear Sovereign....fate has cast me in the role that you and I are unaccustomed of playing.....that is underdog. It is the one you feel that is not a threat to you, is the one you must worry about.Legends serves as only an appetizer for what is to come. You actually think I care about a partner who doesn't give a damn about me? A title shot when, and excuse my bluntness....when a program with you will get me quote unquote over. It's about seizing a career opprotunity and Sovereign, you are it. Win or lose my friend you are right I do have a lot to gain...which in all honesty is fine by me. You see Sovereign I want you to beat me within an inch of my life....only to have the realization that the following week I will be back; that you the monster just can't get the job done. I also want you armed with the knowlege that no matter where you are.....I won't be that far away. Do I need to ask X...or Malcolm about you? Nahhh I want to experience it for myself. Remember Sovereign you only get one chance to make a first impression...I hope yours isn't; lacking to say the least.' the phone lights up but he doesn't answer it.... 'What do you want X....' he continues on his thought on Sovereign.... 'I want to piss you off, but more importantly I want you to show me why HE thinks so highly of you. Is it respect....is it fear? You are quick to underestimate...quick to offer excuses. As you so astutely put it falls do indeed count anywhere, which last time I checked is a two way street. Don't worry Sovereign do what you can....say what you must to protect your mystique. He has a plan which involves you, as do I. As we have stated before win or lose I have an awful lot to gain I know that I understand that. My motivation is really quite simple.....take the measure or the man, tame the beast. I'm a gnat Sovereign...one that will make you take notice for a long time to come.' Luther sits back with a wide smile on his face, as if he knows something about Legends....that he's not letting on. (chapter 2: Xavier Citizen....later on)
