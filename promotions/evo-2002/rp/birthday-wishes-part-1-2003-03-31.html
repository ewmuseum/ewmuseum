Birthday Wishes part 1

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-31-03 'Scott, Scott....You are the first ever two time Universal champion in eVolution history!' The voice of 'The Tru One' Joshua Krowe announces. 'What's wrong with you now?' Krowe adds. The scene fades in and Frost is sitting on a couch and Seara is laying down with her head resting in Frost's lap. Frost looks up at 'The Tru One'. 'Nothings wrong, can't you think past wrestling for one second and realize that I sitting here with my Fiancée as she sleeps?' Frost questions Krowe in a whispering voice. 'The Tru One' Joshua Krowe looks at the couple with a weird look of awe. 'I'm sorry, I don't know what I was thinking.' Krowe replied. 'Listen, I'm gunna get out of here. Congrats on the title win, and....' Krowe pauses and looks at his watch. 'It's one o' clock, so happy birthday man.' Krowe tells Frost. Frost smiles and nods his head and begins running his fingers through the hair of Seara. Frost bends down and whispers something in the ear of Seara as Krowe leaves. Seara's head pops up and she is grinning devilishly. 'He is really gone?' Seara asks. Frost nods his head in reply and smiles. 'Well.....' Seara pauses. Seara kisses Frost, and straddles his lap. 'This will be the first time in forever since me and you had time to be alone. Mr. Birthday Boy.....' Seara says smiling. Frost shakes his head. 'That's, Mr. Two time Universal champion....' Frost says slyly. Seara climbs off Frost. 'Sometimes I think that title is more important to you than anything else, even yourself.' Seara boasts. Frost looks at Seara, and slides the title away from her. Frost slides next to her and kisses her on her next. 'Katherin, you know I only care about you baby.' Frost says in a teasing voice.... Suddenly someone can be heard walking in the background. 'Hey, Scott....Do you know where the mustard is?' Lee eXodus says as he walks in the room. Lee notices that Frost and Seara are aggrivated at this point and just stares at them. 'No, he doesn't Lee. Now get the hell out of here before I kick your ass!' Seara demands. Lee smiles, and looks surprised. 'OH NO! WE TOLD SHOCKA THAT WE WOULD COME VISIT HIM IN THE HOSPITAL!! I GOTTA CALL BRITNEY!' Lee announces. Lee begins to run into the other room and trips and lands flat on his face. By this time, Frost and Seara are out of the 'mood' and they stand up and Frost grabs a jacket and slides it on Seara. The scene fades to black.... (To Be Continued....)
