Live from SynCity!!

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-21-03 Las Vegas, Nevada The Palm Midnight “Heh heh heh” is heard from the Penthouse suite of the Palm ultra-luxury resort on the Vegas strip. The occupants, the world’s favorite tag team, The Syndicate. They quickly flew into town after a surprising and controversial Legends in which nearly everything went according to plan, well almost everything. But none the less, a good night to be had, so its party time. After boarding the Syndicate-1 Learjet and whisking off to SinCity or should I say, SynCity cause Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick are here to light it up bring the Syn to SinCity! The HeartBreaker, dismayed at losing her match, but putting up a valiant fight against pretty much the entire BodySnatcher team holds her head up high as she hikes up her micro-mini skirt and thigh-high fishnet stockings. ‘Damn body bag match! You know they were trying to send you a message and using me in the middle of it all. Now I have a mark out on me saying break my back, I know its Xavier Citizen. That maniac psycho! He should have been put to sleep a long time ago. Psychoward isn’t good enough for him, I say drag him out to the street, hind and quarter him and then cap his ass! Finally run him through a chipper-shredder for good measure!’ ‘I sympathize with you, I do. He’s been trying to get at me since I supposedly took the Universal Championship away from him back in EDW. A long time to hold a grudge huh!’ ‘No kidding! What a Freakwad! But alas D, look what you have in your possession, the thing that is most precious to him in all this world, the ULWN Championship! His supposed legacy, now in the palms of your hands. So what are you going to do with the belt D?’ ‘You know, I haven’t quite decided. I’m toying with a few ideas, all of which he won’t like, but the main one, which I’m leaning towards…. is retiring this trinket once and for all by locking it away in my Swiss vault, and retiring it as the only undefeated ULWN Champion in its psychotic, tumultuous history. Or I could just melt it down into a gold plated plunger so that way when my servants unstuff the toilet, Xavier will always know what I thought of him…. A piece of crap!’ ‘Oh De-lish! I like it! Psycho boy couldn’t handle it! His legacy, his entire existence, down the shitter once and for all! You know D, your just as sick and twisted as I am. So what do you think, the see-through mesh top or the sluty baby –T?’ ‘The T, it goes better with the skirt.’ ‘That’s what I was thinking. So now that you scored 5 points and are in the running again for the tag titles, and me and Luther have 5, what do you think, The Syndicate versus the world at Unbreakable.’ ‘Perhaps, I know you and Luther want the gold and that’s cool with me. Consider me the guardian angel of the new tag regime.’ ‘Look out world, the big bad booty baddy is back and kicking ass!’ “LAUGH!!” ‘So this week, its you and I against pretty much the entire tag division. Crimson Sun, Playa’s Inc, Strictly X, Body Snatchers and Team Network.’ ‘Yup. And all gunning for us as well because they know we are the premier team, the biggest threat, and basically the best thing in tag team wrestling since P&J. But you know what else, I’ve noticed a lot of teams starting to copy or emulate our success. I mean isn’t it strange that all of a sudden the concept of a mixed team is catching on in Evo and elsewhere? Whereas we’ve been doing this for six, hell almost seven years.’ ‘Yeah I know. I’ve also noticed a lot of females coming into Evo, Fiona, Nightsky, and that new chick with Team Network. Its cool and all, just something different. I can see it now, S. thinking of a girls of Evo video and playboy issue. Been their baby! #1 seller well, at least for another week until Tori’s comes out and then will see. I wouldn’t mind doing her, what do you say D. Alittle 3-D action with you, me and tori? ‘Don’t have to ask me twice! I am soooo their!’ ‘HA! I thought you’d be! So getting back to this week, I see Strictly X, Playa’s and Crimson Sun squabbling among themselves while Bodysnatchers and perhaps Team Network focusing on us, concur?’ ‘I hate to admit it but yes, also I’m sure Xavier will lurking around somewhere and I wouldn’t count on Luther for this one.’ ‘I know, he is doing well you know.’ ‘I know, its not him I’m concerned about, its Xavier. I know he wants me at Unbreakable in one of his weird gimmick matches. I have no problem facing him and bitchslapping him around the ring one more time. But even when I beat him, he’s like a bad itch, he keep’s coming up again, again, and again. Which is why, I think I’m going to retire the belt and melt it down!’ ‘Oh SnaPs! Well I’m ready D, what do you think of my outfit?’ ‘Slutty-riot girl, I like it!’ ‘Coolio, off to Rain we go!
