Phone Call - Transcript

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/04/02 Phone Call - Transcript Marcus Campbell & Tommy Michaels Tuesday, November 4th - 9:30 am 'Hello.' 'Michaels, what are you still doing at home? You were supposed to be in Nashville last night.' 'Calm down Campbell. I was a little busy yesterday and couldn't make the trip.' 'Busy doing what?' 'That's none of your business. Look, I'll make the trip tomorrow okay?' 'No, that's not good enough. You need to be here by tonight.' 'Ah gee dad, can't I stay just a little bit longer?' 'Damn it Michaels, stop joking around. You know this attitude change of yours isn't working very well.' 'I'd say it's working very nicely. Maybe it's your turn to make an attitude adjustment.' 'Whatever. If you want to miss your match on Legends, then be my guest. But if you get fired don't come complaining to me about it.' 'Campbell, you worry too much. I'll be there for Legends so don't get your panties in a bunch.' 'Just make sur.....' 'Would you hurry up Campbell, I've got things to do and people to meet.' 'Fine, just make sure you'r.....' 'Give me about one minute to finish up this call and we'll be out of here.' 'Who are you talking to?' 'Just a couple of women I met yesterday. We were about to leave until you called.' 'So that's why you were busy last night. You were too busy picking up women to make the trip huh?' 'Well yeah. What'd you think I was doing?' 'I was hoping you were on your way here, but obviously you've got other things on your mind.' 'Campbell, as much as I would love to sit here and listen to you flap your gums. I've got places to go, so I'll talk to you Wednesday when I get in.' 'Don't you hang up that phon.....' (click) 'You piece of (click)'
