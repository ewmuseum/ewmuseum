Fairytale Hell

26 January 2004

Date: 2004-01-26 So he’d have to wait. He thought that kind of thing was behind him now, he’d had enough of the mind games and having to be patient when he was dealing with Xander. It wasn’t one of his more admirable qualities, he found it hard to do, but it looked like it was going to have to be done. Of course, he was slowly proving himself right. Robert Brooks coming out and organising this ridiculous non violence contract signing between the two of them was precisely the kind of thing he’d been trying to point out to the fans. The fact that these two men were being used as marketing tools, just a way to make money off the fans. There were some of them he knew understood this, they knew what the business was all about, but even so they watched, purely to appreciate the lengths that the athletes went to to best one another. But at the same time he knew that the majority of people that handed over their dollar bills each week didn’t understand, they didn’t know that they were being brainwashed each time they stepped through the gate, bought their food, held up their sign saying who they loved or who they despised and cheered along with everyone else week in and week out. Those people were the only reason he was still around. They hated him, he in turn hated them back, but for entirely different reasons. They hated him because in their eyes he was an “evil” man, a malevolent being who cared about nobody but himself and only existed to gain pleasure through hurting others. Well, suppose they did have a point. But it was more than that now, that was how it was at the start, but it had evolved into so much more. He still enjoyed hurting people, there was no doubt about that, he still felt that people should pay for all the short comings in his life, they should all pay for having it so easy when his life was so hard. The fans though, they were the ones that drove him to what he felt he had to do now. The though kept repeating over and over in his brain, he had to take the Universal Title, he had to take it away from them all. He wanted to walk down the ramp and have torrents of abuse wave over him as he dragged the belt along the floor, not giving a shit about it or the fans, just pleased that they couldn’t cheer for a “champion.” He wanted to disrespect the belt, to disrespect the fans, to make them all hate him that much more for having destroyed their little fantasy world where the good guy always wins. Well the good guy wasn’t going to win in this fairy tale, no way in hell. He’d play the game, he’d go along with their rules, not because he didn’t want to get fired, any other circumstances and he wouldn’t give a fuck. He could find somewhere else to rupture lives. But the thought of taking that belt from them consumed him, he had to do it, and to do that he had to obey the rules for a change. He could wait. He’d done it before, he’d do it again. It didn’t matter how long he had to wait, it was only drawing out the inevitable. I will meet you Kimson at Tainted Love. There will be a war. And after all the bloodshed, and there will be bloodshed, I will emerge as the new “champion” of eVolution. There is no stopping it. But let’s get past this week first shall we…
