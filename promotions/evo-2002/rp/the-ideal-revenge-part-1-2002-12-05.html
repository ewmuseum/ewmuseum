The Ideal revenge part 1.

5 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec. 5, 2002 'Does anything ever go right anymore?' Frost says to himself. The scene fades in, and surprisingly he is alone. No Seara, nobody linked to Seara. Just him. He is sitting on a bench, looking around. Soul searching it seems. 'What can I do? They are getting the upper hand.' Frost again says something himself. Frost sits there, and sits there and sits there. Silence is the only thing heard, it's too cold outside for the cherping of crickets and it's to late for the passing of cars and people. Frost sits there thinking, bundled up in a light jacket staring on towards the ground. Then suddenly Frosts head jerks up. 'We are of champions....' Frost whispers to himself. Frost jumps up, and he grabs his soft blue coloured cell phone out of his pocket and he looks at his watch and smiles. He opens his phone and begins to dial a number and he continues to smile as it is pressed up against his ear. waiting... waiting... waiting... 'HEY!' Frost cheerfully replies. 'Yeah, I know it is five thirty in the morning. Katherine, can I come see you? Where are you?' Frost asks. 'Yeah, I know where that is. Okay I will be there shortly, I got a phone call to make. Love you, bye' Frost quickly hangs up. Frost stops what he is doing and he mouthes the words, 'Love you' and smiles as if he liked saying that to her. Frost opens his phone back up and starts punching some numbers. Then he waits again. waiting.... waiting.... waiting.... waiting... The camera fades out, and then fade into a dark office. The only light in the room is that from the night sky and the blinking red light on the answering machine. Something clicks. 'This is the office of Daniel Somers. Leave a message and I will get back to you as soon as possible. Thanks.' Obviously the answering machine reacts to an incoming phone call. 'Somers?' the voice of Scott Frost is heard through the machine. 'Somers. I got a proposition for you. Before you release the legends card, I want something from you. I am going to prove that I am Notorious, and if you refuse. I will just go to Davidson, so just call me on my cell phone [detail withheld]. Later.' Frost hangs up and then the scene fades away to black.
