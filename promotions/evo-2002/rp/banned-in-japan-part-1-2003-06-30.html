Banned In Japan(Part 1)

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-30 :::Headline::: Former UFC/PRIDE fighter, Drake Nathanial Dukes took wrestling a bit too far tonight. During his match with fellow wrestling, Seiji Motoki, he used an illegial strike to the throat, causing Motoki's air passage to swell up very quickly. The fighter did not stop there however, as he quickly laid the young wrestler out with two consecutive roundhouse kicks to the face. Motoki was rushed to the hospital and it was announced at the end of the show that he had passed away. All of our hearts and prayers go out to this brave individual. :::Self Defense?::: 'It was an accident', I screamed at the chairman of the company. 'Accident? You call that an accident?! What you did was very very bad! You have no idea just how serious this is. We could get shut down for this, Drake.' I tilted my head to it's side, staring intently on the chairman. 'You better stop bullshitting, and tell me what really happened Drake, or I'll have you arrested.' I wasn't the type that enjoyed an ultimatum. I slung my glass against the wall, sending shards of glass flying as it shattered. 'I already told you what happened! We were caught up in the moment, and the son of a bitch threw an illegial elbow to my nose! I lost my temper, and I nailed him in the throat. I couldn't control it', my voice was shaking as I replied. The chairman shook his head in spite of me. 'Don't you Americans have a phrase or something? What is it, oh, 'two wrongs don't make a right.' Am I correct? I believe I am. So why in the hell would you risk another fighter's life because of an elbow to the nose? That's rediculous. You better come up with a better story than that.' The chairman was almost threatening me now. I felt my temples pulsate violently in my skull. 'I... I wanted to win sir. I wanted to impress the crowds. I wanted them to see just the athlete I truly am. I never intended to kill that man.' Just as I finished speaking, I heard a familiar voice from down the hallway. The sound was growing closer as the seconds passded. My long time friend and manager, Dean Cross had stepped into the heap of an office. He looked around in disgust then focused his eyes on me. 'What the hell are they doing with you Drake?' Cross raised his eyes to the chairman of the company, speaking 'You know damn well that he can't be charged with anything, here Kasaki. All of your fighters signed contracts.' Cross smirked as he continued, 'Besides, it's not our fault that your Jap's can't take a little punishment. I mean, shit, Drake barely even touched the poor kid.' 'YOU! YOU GET OUT MY OFFICE!!' Two of Kasaki's men placed their grubby fingers on Cross. I was just about to make the second biggest mistake of my life.... :::Caught In The Crossfire::: They started to lead Dean out the door, but they wouldn't even get that far. I violently lifted the table in front of me off it's legs and tossed it aside, sweep kicking Kasaki as it fell. Kasaki fell to the floor, holding his hip. I took the pain of his hip off his mind as I broke his nose with a palm strike. With the owner down, I turned my attention to the two goons holding my mentor. A roundhouse for the first, just to get his attention. He came at me with his cheap billy club and caught me on the side of the head. I focused on the pain for a moment, attempting to channel it away and managed to avoid a second swing. Grabbing the man's head, I drove my knee high into the bridge of his nose, hearing a loud pop as a result. The man crumpled to the floor, leaving only his partner to contend with me. He too came at me with a billy club. He seemed a bit more expierenced than his partner. He took numerous swings at me, connecting with only a few before a landed a backhand punch to his ribs. He stumbled backward and I rushed forward with a pump kick that knocked the grease monkey through the door. He remained on his feet, pinned against the wall in the corridor. I had my way with him,. throwing several right and left hands. He put his hands up to shield the blows. Just what I wanted. I grabbed the man by the shoulders and drove my knee directly into his left kidney. I saw the familiar pain in his eyes as he fell to the floor, and passed out. Cross protruded through the half broken door, dusting his hands off as if he'd contributed to the duel. I smacked him on the shoulder playfully then got serious. 'We've got to get the hell out of here, Dean. These guys don't fuck around. Kasaki is a made man. He'll have headhunters all over Tokyo looking for us.' Cross nodded in agreement with me. 'Well then Drake-a-boy, let's get our sorry asses out of this dump. Let's stick to US. They have better sushi anyway, haha.' Somehow, sushi just wasn't a key thing on my agenda. Getting out of this country was going to be damn near impossible. But then again.... I am a man that gambles with the impossible odds..
