Before They

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So far do they let you go. There's a wall, and you try jumping over it. But, foolishly you under-estimate the wall's height. You jump forward, hit the wall, and slide back down onto the floor. You get up, dust yourself, and walk away. Or, you try again. And again. And everytime you jump you get a little higher, until finally you sail over top of it. As you run to towards the light, it only seems to keep getting farther and farther away. Until you stop, and the light dies out. Or, you keep going. You go until your blood boils, and your eyes are closed because they are covered in sweat. And then finally, your at the light, and it's all you hoped it would be. The mountain peak seems to keep getting higher and higher, and after every lift as you pull yourself up, it just get's more and more hopeless. Until you reach up for the last time, and you start to climb back down. Or, you drag yourself up the side. Scratching your knuckles on the rock as you slowly climb faster and faster, as the adrenaline takes over your body and in an awe of strength you go without stopping. Hand after hand you keep going higher. And then, just when you can't take it, you reach the top, and gaze across the land which you came to see. Did they lower the wall? Did they move the light closer? Did they make the peak level with you? And as you came across the side, you realized that beneath the wall...there was nothing, and you fell. And you fell. And as you grasped for the light, the flame went out. Slowly, the darkness surrounded you again. Again. And as you stared across the land, it all came tumbling down. As you fell, you slowly remembered. You remembered. So far do they let you go, before they take it all away.
