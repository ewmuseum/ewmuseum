Yesterdays Fad Is Todays Chip Paper..

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With his eyes closed and a bandaged head, Sean hardly looks in the condition of his life at present. Standing above him is a Dr hovering with a clipboard and chart. As the Dr takes readings from the various sets of equipment Sean begins to stir. Then as the Dr leans across it seems Sean has awoken be it still very much groggy following the nights events during Legends this past week.. 'F**king hell' Sean utters as he tries to force his eyes opening. With a great degree of effot Sean finally prizes his eyelids apart only to let out a huge groan as he looks down at the mess he is in. At first his vision is blurry, but as the time passes it becomes impossible for Sean not to notice the distress his body is in. Similar to a man who has been very much in the front line, Seans arms are cut and much of his body wrapped like a mummy. 'Dr?!' Sean groans turning to face the man who stands alongside his bed. Worried for Seans well being the Dr turns immediately and looks down at him with a concerned look. 'Are you ok?' the Dr replied. 'Would you like some more painkillers? Its not far from the time when your second dose is to be taken now..' Sean is just about to say no, when he turns further only to feel a rush of pain going up his side, 'maybe its for the best' he gasps trying to put the pain to the back of his mind, 'but before you do dissapear can you just give me a run-down of whats wrong with me??' Putting his paperwork to one side the Dr smiles, before perching himself on the end of Seans bed; 'of course I can' he replies. 'Your suffering from a concussion and also a number of lacerations to a number of different areas to your body' the Dr continues only to watch as Sean turns away in distress. 'On a brighter note, we did extract all the glass from your cuts and we expect a speedy recovery at this rate.' Sean turns back toward the Dr not too quickly though so to avoid hurting himself further. Looking at the Dr with a glazed over expression he takes some comfort in the Drs diagnosis, but is quick to continue the open questioning. 'So when can I escape this place?' he asks. 'So desperate to leave? I thought you would have enjoyed a few days of nurses waiting on your every command?!' the Dr replied with a large grin on his face. Sean though is still very much on a different planet and thus his response is non-existant. 'Well anyway' the Dr says re-grouping his thoughts, 'in seven days perhaps?' 'Seven days!?!' Sean screams. 'Wrong answer doc, try again!' 'How do you mean?' the Dr replies. 'Well' Sean says pausing, 'what days it today?' 'Saturday' 'Well in that case I'll be outta here in two!' Sean snaps back, 'that gives me some travelling time'. 'Travelling?' the Dr responds with a look of horrer. 'Sean what happened to you last Wednesday was not something minor! You took a beating and you can't expect your body just to come back from it straight away! You need time to heel' the Dr continues waving his hands frantically.. 'What about the mental scars?' Sean snaps back. 'You think I can sit here and let the nightmares plague me?! Right now I can barely remember my name or what I had for dinner last night, but there are somethings that a man can not forget whatever happens to him! You think I enjoy closing my eyes and seeing that sick b*****d kicking the living s**t out of me?! Sure I dont remember exact details, but with time I am sure they will flood back to me and when they do, don't you think there is a need for a response?' 'But...' the Dr says in a panic only to be interupted again. 'But nothing' Sean interupts by saying, 'Dr I suggest you go dust off your magic wand! If you think I am going to miss Legends this week your way off the mark! Morpheous sent me a message and im not going to give him the satisfaction of knowing im suffering as a result. So I took a beating!?! These things happen, but I've never been one to let sleeping dogs lie! Morpheous thinks hes some kind of tough guy, a re-occuring nightmare for the whole of EVO. Sean Mason however is about to wake EVO up to reality and make sure Mr Omega is nothing more than a distant memory, last weeks bad dream...' 'But..' the Dr tries again in vain.. 'If you would'nt mind' Sean says weakly waving the Dr away, 'I gotta get my rest, I got two days' Sean groans before slumping back down on his pillow.. The footage then ends as Sean turns on his side and closes his eyes once more.
