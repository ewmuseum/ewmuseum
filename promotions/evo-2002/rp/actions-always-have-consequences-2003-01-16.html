Actions always have consequences

16 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/16/03 The room is one that is very familiar to him. This is the place where he ,Dan S.,and Brandon Kelly were held captive by The ONE. Malcolm had always intimated that during the course of the old angle that an actor had assumed his role in a double cross by Xander. He finds it ironic that this abandoned warehouse might hold a clue to who the ONE is. However that is not the only thing that is troubling him. His ex-partners body is somewhere and as always he is the prime suspect in it's disappearance. His one time student cheap shotted him while he was searching for answers, a confrontation that was unexpected to say the least. However even more shocking that Dan S. granted him a shot at The Universal title in what is obviously a trap....... MK:'Dan you never seem to get it, do you? You of all people know that I am a patient man...one that is not easily detered from my set agenda. I had given your champion a one month reprieve something you should have honored Danny boy. However now I find myself placed in a position where I can take control of Evo....much like I did EDW, and use it as my own personal playground. Somehow I doubt Dan would allow that to happen so soon so quickly....but as I stated before I am a patient man.' Malcolm walks over to the shackles that were once used to bind Dan S. to the wall for the weeks he spent imprisoned here..... MK:'Do you miss your home Daniel? If you keep toying with me I could arrange for you to revisit your cell for old times sake. Speaking about old times I see you have included my good friend Sovereign in the match. I have met him in the ring only once, and I must say that he is a force if not a freak of nature. But tales of my destruction at his hands were greatly exaggerated and when all is said and done you may have been able to scramble the brains of my protegee, but I am a different story entirely. Dan knows all too well that unlike the other two men in this match....I have a personal reason to beat you. Remember in OSW; yourself, Big Grumpy, and a man we all affectionately know as BOB tried to destroy me and my legacy. You three made a mockery of me and my career as there was nothing I could do about it. BOB although entertaining was a slap in the face to me, the damage done to me took a few years to correct; and now I'm given the chance to do something I should have done those many years ago. It is your inclusion in this match....which is having me entertain even being apart of it. Even if I don't win it....preventing you from doing so is all the satisfaction I need.' Malcolm walks out of the cell and down a long hallway..... 'Scott Frost and Jason Jameson; I neither know you that well nor do I care to. Obviously you two are the top of the Evolution food chain which would explain your involvement in this match, I'm sure becoming Universal Champion would be the icing on your career cakes, a well deserved honor and motivation I'm sure both of you can use. Do I need motivation to win this match....not really. You see the old adage applies if you are not up for this match then nothing...absolutely nothing will get you fired up. So why am I not jumping for joy? You see the four of us are fighting for a title that I have held three times before in it's previous form. What could motivate me to go after a belt when this who entire fed was created because of me? How many championships must I win before I become bored? OSW was a victim of my boredom when I seized 50% ownership of it. EDW was a victim of my boredom when I deemed it not worthy of exsistance. Why should I be jumping through the hoops that Dan S. put forth when in all reality I am the one who is controlling the hoops? I do so next week for the simple reason....it will entertain me. I gave the champion 30 days to realize his mistake and at that point I will impose upon him the futility of him continuing as champion. For now Frost, Jameson, Sovereign you are here to entertain me, you are here so that I can show Dan S. no matter what he has planned for me, I'm always one step ahead. You three gentlemen deserve to be in the spots that you are....just pray that my need for entertainment don't turn into a need; to win.' Malcolm begins to walk off as he is heard muttering.... 'Wisdom, I will deal with you later.' (OOC:Work day is over so I had to cut things short)
