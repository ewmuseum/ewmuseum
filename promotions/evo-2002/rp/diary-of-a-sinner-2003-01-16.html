Diary of a Sinner

16 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.16.02 Entry No. 1 ----------------- Name: Angel ----------------- Subject: This... ----------------- Date: 16th January, 2003 Well, this is kinda gay, but soothing, i've been told my my shrink that the dreams that I've been having may be leading to insanity, so i've been told to keep a journal for things that happen to me. Well, here we go, here's the start to my journal... or as I'd like to call it. The Diary of a Sinner. I had a dream, a dream that I'd met Satan. The dream was ages ago and it's kinda strange that I woke up with a pentagram looking scar on my wrist the next morning. All I can say is, crazy, insanity, delerious, or carnage. I've been sitting here looking at it wondering out it got there and how come it's right there. These little power things that I'm supposed to have aren't really real? Or are they? This should be on that show Beyond Belief. It'll sure get me some money to show tell them my story but I can't feel sad or good about it. All I can do is hope that it doesn't mean what it meant in my dream, because I was put in the Asylum for about killing this guy with a golf club. Better that than the pen, eh? I can't believe my last resort to complete sancutary is to write to myself in a little book with an Angel on the front of it. This is ridiculous, but as I've confirmed once more, it's pretty cool.. I guess. I've seen guys do this in Asylum but it's very unnatural. They said I had to turn this into the people at eVolution as a reminder that I might be losing it and going over the edge. The shrink think it's because of me losing so many times during my stay at eVolution. I think the same thing, I've been thinking about this hard and the more I think about how I lost the match, the more I want to rip out HBC's hair, locke... by locke... by locke. Gah, I started forcing the pen down, anger seems to overrun me sometimes and I can't control my actions. I love inflicting pain.. it's just so... stimulating to me. I get a nut off... of making others squeal like pigs about to catch a hatchet to the side of the neck. I love... pain. I love to destroy things. That's what seperated me from the other kids at my old time school, when I was I was younger. I was considered a bully because I beat up everyone... police... DAs... teachers... kids... my parents... everyone. I've been arrested alot, I can't seem to keep that off of my mind, the things i've seen in my life would kill a normal person, but I guess i'm not normal. To sum it all up... i'm no one's Angel. I've been thinking of shifting from my good time Angel name... to Seraph, Seraph got me respect, Seraph got justice... Seraph got me fear. Angel is always walked on to the extent of where he can't take it anymore. Sometimes I feel torn apart, by both sides... Angel.. Seraph.. Angel.. Seraph.. Angel.. SERAPH. I lost it again. I was put in a solitary place when I was Asylum. The nut house always gave me my sanctuary but, I was surrounded by other nut jobs. Some clown freak was put in here because he was cursed by some priest and he killed a lot of people. I want to rip his fuckin' face off right now and chew on it infront of him as he grovels from the pain. There's a girl in here, her name's Raven. She's been called a witch many atimes in her life, as she sits in one of the cells murmuring to herself about some girl named Kelly, and how she's going to kill to the bastards who murdered her best friend. There's this guy in here who's just thrown out from all sides. He was a boxer, a low time boxer that you probably never heard about. He lost a fight, but the fucked part was, the doctor who was supposed to fix him up, bet the fight on him. He lost 500 dollars from what I heard, so the doctor got his revenge... and removed the eyes and teeth from his face, and sowed his eyes and mouth shut. Remember the clown freak I was talking about? The priest who 'cursed' him, is in here too. That's the devilish part. I hear that he says that a demon was inside of him so he slaughtered alot of people, didn't bother me that much though. He didn't kill me... so I don't give a shit. There was this crazy chick in there with a bloody wedding dress on, claiming that she will find her Mr. Right. She often beat herself in the face with her lunch tray so they gave her a styrofoam one to keep her from killing herself. There was this cannibal there too, everytime he looks at his food, he claims to see ears and eyes and magots, shit like that. As I've said many atime before... not my problem. As I was sitting down minding my own business, scratching letters into my chest with a plastic fork they gave me, a man missing an eye came in. I was ready to rip his fucking head off and shove it up his ass for disturbing me.. or just for the sheer fun. But he came closer and told me he could give me what I wanted. Anything that my heart desired would be granted and he would fufill my wish. I declined his offer and sent him leaving, I didn't trust him much, so I stayed there for 4 more months until I was pulled out for good behavior. I finally saw the light once more, the dark days in the asylum will never leave me.. and I think by the way that I'm feeling... I might do something to end up there once more...
