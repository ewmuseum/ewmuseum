It's Over

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th April 2003 “Promos” as they are called in the wrestling business are tools designed to give wrestlers airtime, to let the audience know who they are. They are also used by wrestlers to gain popularity from the audience, or to generate “heat” and make them hate them. But most of the time, promos are used, to warn your opponent. They are used to tell your opponent what you’re going to do to them during your up coming match. To tell them how pissed off you are about how lucky they’ve been recently to avoid being put out of action before the match can even take place. To tell them how everything they say to you in the promo before yours does not matter to you one little bit. The audience soon recognise this is the voice of Asis, not a narrator as the picture to Asis’ own promo finally comes into focus. He’s sitting in a place you can’t determine, but it seems as though he may have gone back to his fondness of living in run down factories and the like, after his brief stint of staying in hotel rooms provided by eVo for Unlimited members. Or maybe eVolution got tired of him destroying the rooms and stopped him for staying in them. Either way, he’s somewhere that no one else is, and probably would not want to be. Mason, you’re the kind of person in this business that I hate. The kind of person that I am exactly the opposite to, the kind of person that I have no intention of becoming. This business is all about the glamour to you, it’s about the fan appreciation, the kids with your shirt on, the ones holding up banners and cheering you on. You’re in this business to be the best, to walk out from behind that curtain one day with the Universal Championship belt around your waist. But your highway on the road to success seems to have hit a pretty fucking big roadblock, namely me. You think that my Continental title is just another rung on the ladder to the Universal Championship don’t you? If you get this belt off me then you’ll get noticed and get a chance at the “big one”. But you’ve got to get this belt off me first. Which means you’ve got to face me in the kind of match that is the environment I crave, the kind of environment where I can hurt you using anything I want. You questioned my ethics? You asked whether I know about ethics? If you came from a childhood like mine you wouldn’t give a shit about anybody else. You can’t remind somebody about ethics if they don’t have any to begin with. I’ve been taught throughout life that there is only one person in this world you can rely on and anybody that tries to get in your way you shouldn’t give a damn about. As far as I’m concerned anybody that tries to stop me doing anything is already dead. I don’t hurt people because I’m told to, you really think Xander and S. have control over me? I enjoy hurting people, I just hurt the people that they tell me to, they channel my anger for me. You can’t stop Unlimited Sean, one person like you doesn’t have any chance whatsoever. Nobody can stop Unlimited; Unlimited stops when it wishes to do so. But more to the point Sean, you can’t stop me. What task are you trying to see through Sean? The task of taking me out and stopping me from hurting others? Or to not let me injure you? You can’t stop yourself from being put in an “early career coffin” the only way you could’ve done that was to not accept the match. You’re only hope now, is that I stop myself from ending your career. The mind games are over now Sean. All the notes backstage, all the attacks, everything. In a few short hours you’ll be stepping inside that ring with me and only me and an assortment of toys at my disposal. The waiting is over Sean, the mind games are over, unfortunately for you Sean, it’s over…
