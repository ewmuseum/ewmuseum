Sinister plan part 1.

27 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 27, 2002 Unlimited. A force to be wreckoned with. A force not of hatred, but of mass destruction. The Neutral face of the hybrid government of eVolution. Tyler Davidson, and Daniel Somers slowly and reeled in the continued controversy that is Unlimited. Unlimited tackled a big crowd, but only a few are on the spot. New comers join, Scott Frost and Heartbreak Chick. They have already proved their worth, Frost taking out the thorn in the side of wrestling itself; Erin Christoph. Heartbreak Chick showing that she is the force to wreckon within the Syndicate coporation, she turned the jaw of Big Daddy Dreamer into meer puzzle pieces. The continued destructive path remains to grow, will there be a fifth? Or will the next oponents in the way of Unlimited just feel the destructive powers of a group that has yet to be stopped? Unlimited are in control, your destinys in their hands. Who is the next, to become a resource of....Unlimited? 'Kelsey! Come here please!' yells the voice of Scott Frost. Foot steps can be heard, as the scene fades in and Scott Frost and is sitting on a couch reading a piece of paper, and Kelsey can be seen quickly coming into the room. 'yeah?' questions Kelsey. Frost puts the paper down. 'You remember what I told you, about having to be ruthless to get recognized?' Frost replies to Kelsey. 'Yeah?' once again questions Kelsey. 'Read this, then...' Frost hands Kelsey the piece of paper he was reading. Kelsey skims over it, as Frost watches her. Her eyes widen as she reaches the bottom of the paper.... '.....Frost, your job is to destroy that thorn in my side. L2J....' Kelsey reads from the paper. 'Scottie! Please don't!' Kelsey pleads. Frost looks away in shame. 'Ruthless to be recognized....' stammers the voice of Scott Frost. Frost looks away from his wife, for the first time he is affraid to look in her direction. She stares down at him with a scared look on her face. 'Not L....' Kelsey looks away. 'How could you do that to the very person that got you into this business? The only friend besides me you have ever had?' Kelsey complains. Frost looks down. 'Unlimited needs it.....' Frost stammers once again. 'I supported this Unlimited change, but....' Kelsey begins but gets interrupted. 'BUT NOTHING! I CHOSE UNLIMITED, UNLIMITED CHOSE ME! I HAVE A JOB TO DO! BUT FIRST.....Legends X.' Frost eagerly informs his wife. Kelsey stares Frost. 'So...your putting the L2J attack off?' questions Kelsey. 'No.' Frost exclaims. 'I am challanging L2J to a match up. Legends XI. I want to be the reason why L2J can't make it to Faith to face Sovereign and win that Universal championship. He should be wrestling for the High Impact. L2J is a cruiser weight! So, I am challanging the Universal cruiser weight...to a Kendo Stick match up.' Frost calmly tells Kelsey. 'Kendo Stick? That's your specialty match from the indy feds.' Kelsey exclaimed. 'What's your point?' Frost asks. 'You will kill him.' Kelsey again exclaims to Frost. 'That's not all. L2J seems to worse, and get even more battered and bruised when people are out watching him...waiting to attack. So the match will be a Kendo Stick/Lumber Jack match.' Frost smiles. 'Lumber jack? Who are you getting to be the Lumber jacks? And why Legends XI?' Questions Kelsey. 'Legends XI, because by next week. My body count will be....Three. Erin Christoph, Tyler Davidson, Big Daddy Dreamer. In two weeks. Jason Jameson, Sean Mason, Shodan, will be added to that body count. That's enough people to put me over in the Universal championship division. L2J is number one contender. I want to prove to Somers, and Davidson.....and OSE that I am Universal material.......' Frost tells Kelsey. Kelsey looks at Frost with a sinister female smile and she kisses Frosts cheek. 'Your absolutely right.' Kelsey tells Frost. Frost looks at Kelsey. 'The Lumber jacks are going to be, Xander, Jinx, HBC, Sovereign, and anyone else that Somers puts in there. Legends XI, I will do as Unlimited bids and that is be the idea of DESTRUCTION!' Frost happily tells his wife. Kelsey looks around. 'What about your match at Legends X?' asks Kelsey. 'That will come in all due time.' Frost tells Kelsey. (To be continued...)
