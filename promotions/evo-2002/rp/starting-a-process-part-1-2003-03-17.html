Starting A Process part 1

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th March 2003 The scene opens in a park in Detroit. Seara sits on a bench looking out to the surrounding sea of green, the occasional grey path flowing through the coloured ocean blanketing the ground. The female looks completely peaceful, and this seems to be the first time she has relaxed in front of the cameras in a while. Things in her life have been happening at a perfect rate for her and life from her perspective will be hard to make better. At home waiting for her to return is her fiancée, eVolutions Universal Champion, Scott Frost, a man the female would go to the ends of the world to please. Her private life has disappeared from her airtime with the lead up to Legends that happened on Thursday night, and since then, she has kept herself away from the cameras. Her private life has been private enough, her life with Scott Frost is something the female wants to cherish right now, she knows with a PPV coming up and the new stable, that airtime will be more apparent for her, and her private life will become more public, but she will be able to cope with it, she knows the cameras are not always there, and her life outside eVo will have time of its own to grow. Panning around the camera stops on a small dog laying on the floor, whistling on it, Seara holds out her hand, it is the dog Jason Jameson gave to her as a late Christmas present. It is the first time it has been seen on the cameras, but it seems apparent that the female is fond of it, and enjoys watching it run around the park. Sitting forward she looks directly into the camera, acknowledging it for the first time since it faded into the image of her on the bench. Sighing her smile disappears and her face becomes devoid of emotion. Not the right time to piss me off Omega, and you roC, you have pissed me off one too many times. I have no fight this week at Legends, but I will be there, looking for both of you. Reaching into her pocket, Seara removes a cigarette from a packet and putting it to her lips she lights it and inhales deeply. You two really don’t understand what the bells for do you? Now, I’m willing this once to let you guys try again, and try it properly. Now just so you know, the bell signifies the start of a match, which is when the teams or competitors are meant to begin the battle of strength. You must feel proud that you had to make a move before that bell to win the match. Jointly, you weigh a combined 530lbs. Omega, you alone weigh more than myself and Shocka, yet still you and roC felt you had to take Shocka out before the match even started so that you could win by having only me to face, and yes, when a handicap match appears, and the double team weigh 407lbs more than the opponent, I do believe the double team have the advantage. Now when one of you weighs more than both of us, I’m sure that once again you have the advantage, and if you were really as good as Thug roC would like to think, you would have let us both in the ring, waited for the bell and annihilated us, but from where I’m standing it looks like you made it easier because you were worried. The female pauses and as she looks back towards the camera, she appears to be smiling sadistically and laughing. I wonder Omega if you remember kidnapping me with Shocka. I’m sure you do; after all, you did feel a large piece of 2x4 connecting with your skull, but you see Omega that is nothing to the way I will make you feel, you will not know when or where or why, but I will make u feel pain and if I can, I will humiliate you for all to see. Right now, you can feel safe though, but then again it isn’t long till Legends, watch your back Omega, I may be behind you when you are least prepared. Her expression changes and Seara’s smile changes to a calm controlled look. Reaching down she puts the lead on round the neck of her dog and slowly gets to her feet. Shocka, you are safe, you are a good guy, and I see you going a long way. You are after the same as me, and I will make sure we get our fight against those two cheating little morons soon enough. Now Shocka, what do you say to joining me for some tag team action in the very near future? Turning Seara walks away and only when she is a significant distance away does she stop and turn back. I hope for you Shocka that you make the right decision. I know you will be valuable to me… The scene fades away with the female walking off towards the setting sun in the distance.
