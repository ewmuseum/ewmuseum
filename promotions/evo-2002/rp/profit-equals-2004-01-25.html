Profit Equals....

25 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-01-25 What was he thinking? I mean where did that boy really think he was doing when he bit the very hand which fed him only seconds earlier?! Joshua Northstar, where do I start with a man who would do not better than take that money I handed him and invest it into a burial site for his career. You don't try and run before you can crawl, most of all you don't mess with a man of my stature, since I'll just break your legs before they even have time to spawn into the foundations they were designed to be. Legends was meant to be a night where both the power of the pen and the power of the pound communicated a clear message to my foe Johnny Charisma. Today hes probably waking up in some hospital nursing a few war wounds but I ask you, what happened to the recital I promised?! Fans across the world are going to be a distraught, Northstar I don't make a habit of breaking promises but when I am being carried away in an ambulance its kind of hard to recite the English language in the manner if deserves. Days I had spent, no weeks in fact preparing and your meandathol actions ruined everything I had in mind. What have you gained? I mean what have you gained other than an enemy who is intent on embaressing you to the same extent I felt embaressment when I saw through my hazy vision blue flashing lights. I'm flattered you thought attacking me would mark you on the map here in Evolution but do you really think a man like me is going to cower in fear?! No length is to far, I can still taste the blood I spilled on Thursday and I fully intend on sampling yours at the first possible second. We had a business arrangement, did Charisma catch you with a flailing elbow and knock the sense out of your skull; I just hope people took a long hard look at you on Legends, blink this coming week and you will miss your last few seconds in this wonderful place. I'm not a man of much patience and as soon as Legends was through I went on the front foot. I can only believe you have no morals, probably some street player jealous of my position so you should feel right at home in the street fight I have scheduled for this coming Thursday. This world is all about supply and demand, you demanded my attention and I'm about to give you a surplus of supply which will leave you counting the cost; now how profitable does that decision you make seem? In dummies terms for you, if you think you got the better of me, then I advise you not to waste energy thinking again.
