eXodus A roCsuperstar too?

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-10-03 'Are you ready?' a voice asks. The scene fades in and you can see Joshua Krowe, Scott Frost, Seara, and Lee eXodus talking to a man. 'Lee, if you're ready for your time in the booth have at it.' The man says to eXodus. Krowe, Frost, and Seara all look at Lee trying not to laugh and Lee cracks his neck and back pumping himself up. 'Are you sure you wanna do this? roC will just answer back, with something about you rather than Shocka.' Krowe asks eXodus. eXodus looks at Krowe and then nods. 'I don't care. This is my time to have fun with New-Tv.' eXodus replies. 'Okay man, have fun.' Frost says patting eXodus on his back. eXodus smiles, and proceeds into the sound booth. Krowe shakes his head, and Seara busts out laughing at eXodus as the sound booth door closes. 'There goes one brave ma...boy......What is he exactly?' Krowe asks. 'He is a Frostcicle.' Frost says with a proud smile. The scene cuts in on eXodus in the sound room and a faint sound of music is heard. eXodus has headphones on and one ear piece is positioned onto the back of his head so he can hear what he sounds like, and for the first time ever eXodus looks like a professional. yo, yo, yo... My name Lee eXodus and I'm the one you fear I'm a rock superstar, dating Britney Spears unaware of why i'm rappin' it's because this Thug roC ish ain't happenin' You wanna bring up your world well welcome to mine open up the doors and your destined to find A two time universal champion, his names Scott Frost you don't wanna mess with him because in Team eVo, he is the boss First ever eVolved, Queen of the fed Seara is cold as ice until she is in bed....... Former Universal, Continental dream A Road To Glory Champion, he rose quicker than steam Shocka is the hardest, one of us all. Because he put up with Thug roC in charge and holdin' the ball eXodus suddenly looks at the window of the sound booth door and you can see Scott Frost laughing, and Seara looking really pissed off. Joshua Krowe has his eyebrow raised, and seems like he is waiting for something. eXodus holds his thumb up indicating he is done. He steps out the room and the sound booth engineer comes out too. The engineer looks at eXodus. 'That wasn't part of the script, you were supposed to do your version 'My Name Is' by Eminem.' The booth engineer says looking upset. 'You just wasted my time, and your time and we will have to do that again.' The booth engineer adds. eXodus looks at the man, and smiles. 'Put that on a CD, Thug roC has got to hear that.' eXodus tells the man. The man looks very upset, but retreats back into the sound booth room. Frost pats eXodus on the back and wraps his arm around Seara. Krowe walks next to them and the scene fades with the group slowly walking away like a four person MOD Squad.
