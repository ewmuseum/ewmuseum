The Entrance

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 23rd, 2002 ***The scene opens up in the Triple A, American Airlines Arena, in lovely Miami, Florida, where the Evolution ring crew has been working hard for the past 6 hours, getting ready for the ninth edition of eVo's Legends. Untopable feats of athletics and wrestling ability have all been shown on the previous shows, but nothing shall even come close to what is going to happen this week. Eric Badger, failed model, has been entered in a tag team match with a partner known as Shocka. The two of them taking on Jason Jameson, and Shodan, two former buddies from EDW. This match will have epic proportions, and Eric wants to spark up a flame and pick up a victory here in Evolution, as he never had the chance to pull one out before he left. His main focus in this match, is none other than Jason Jameson, who suffered his first Evolution loss to the hands of Asis at Digital Destruction, due to the interference of one, Eric Badger. These two men have the potential to be huge superstars in Evolution, and all over the world, as long as they stick to the basics of wrestling, and provide kick ass shows for the audience. Jameson was supremely pissed off at the fact that Badger made him lose his chance to capture his first of what he hopes is many Evolution titles. Asis was given a huge save from Badger, who will probably want to have a beef with him later on down the line. Everyone is waiting with anticipation for Legends 9, to see what will happen between the two young Stallions, Jason Jameson, and Eric Badger. The camera is turned on backstage at the American Airlines Arena, where we see loads of superstars already inside the building, as if mentally preparing for their respective matches. All of the guys are chumming around, being all friendly, and thats when Eric Badger walks into the scene. A dead silence follows as he stares at all of the wrestlers, and they stare back. Eric takes a backward step, as he more than anyone is a little intimidated at the sight of all these Evolution Superstars.*** EB - 'Holy shit guys, who died? You're all looking at me like I'm some sort of bad guy, what did I do wrong? Oh, it's either the Jason Jameson thing, or the bad note that I left on here a few short months ago. Well hear me out, I didn't choose to leave on a bad note with the entire locker room, I followed a dream of mine, a dream that I thought I could persue, and I found out that I couldn't cut it as a model, but one thing is for sure, that I can cut it as a wrestler. All of you here staring at me know that, I've been up and down the roads with most of you, and I can take the shit as much as I can give it out. So please, let me back into Evolution, because I have a job to do like each and everyone of you, I'm just trying to make a living, and I'm trying to give the fans some entertainment along the way. Daniel Somers paid me to comeback, and attack Jason Jameson. I did that job, and now Jameson is pissed, giving me something else to do around here while I re-built my skills, work off the ring dust, and begin my quest to become the best damn person here in Evolution. The one man that I don't see in this crowd right here, staring at me like I'm some sort of cheerleader with my bra stuffing coming out, is Sovereign or some shit like that. Where is he? Is he in hiding? Does he know that once I dispose of Jason Jameson that I will be gunning for him more sooner than later? Well I am, from here on out, Jameson is my marked man, yet when Jason is paralyzed and laying in a hospital bed somewhere, I'll be coming for you Sovereign. Eventually, you and I will clash, and it will be more than a clash of titans, it will be a clash of warriors that are willing to put everything they've got on the line for a title belt. It's all about winning that prize, and I was here when you won, and you are yet to lose that belt of yours. I'm up to that challenge of bringing the beast down to size. But Don't get me wrong, Jameson, you are the next piece of agenda on my mind, and come Wednesday, the tag team match, I will have no remorse for your bodily functions, and the most possible harm that I can produce will be inflicted upon your weak self. My challenge is still issued Mr. Jameson, any type of match you want, anytime you want it, anywhere you want it. To quote Journey, 'Anyway you want it, thats the way you need it, anyway you want it.' Bring it on, I'll be patiently waiting until Wednesday for your answer, and I hope you grow some balls and agree to this. As for the rest of you guys, I couldn't give two shits of a lamb's ass about any of you, so piss off and stop staring at me, just because you all know that I can take you, get out of my way before I have to get physical.' ***The five wrestlers that are staring at him pick Eric up above their heads and carry him to the parking lot as the scene fades to black.*** FTB
