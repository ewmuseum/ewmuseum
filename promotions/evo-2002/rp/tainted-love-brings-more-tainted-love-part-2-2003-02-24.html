Tainted Love Brings MORE Tainted Love part 2

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you awake? Frost’s voice could be heard by the female and turning she set her eyes on the male who stood in tight jeans, half naked. Course I’m awake, you know I don’t sleep during the day, and I had the interview earlier. The female’s reply was soft and gentle and her smile was warm as she stared deeply at the tall male standing above her. She was sitting on the floor in front of a light fire, looking at it she glances back at Scott. Sorry, I thought it would be okay to light it, it was really cold in here and I thought it would just be really warm to sit in front of a fire, I can’t remember the last time I got to do it. Sitting back Seara leant over and picked up a glass of red wine, she had found herself able to relax now for the first time in many years, and although she felt very comfortable and amazed at the ease she had to let her guard down, she somehow knew it was something she would have to restrict to the house and hotels, rather than allowing herself to relax too much backstage at arenas. No, it’s nice. I’ve never light it before. Smiling the male sits on the floor next to the woman he has come to love. She smiles in a shy way and looks down at the floor, almost nervous and anxious about being around Scott. (“The butterflies, that’s what most people call it. When you are really in love and they approach you or your in the same room as them, you can feel their presence, it gives you goose bumps and a chill down your spine, the hairs on the back of your neck stand on edge and you feel tingly and indestructible”.) The voice of Seara’s brother Bruce filled her mind and memory and the female drifted away for a moment into a memory she had gained only months before when she reunited herself with her family. (“You think I’ll ever feel that Bruce?” her voice was emotionless and lacked the knowledge and skills of human contact and interaction. “Of course you will. Katherine, you are a beautiful girl and your intelligent when you calm down”. Seara smiled, it was a forced smile, and you could tell the female was not used to the kindness of others.) Shaking her head Seara looked at Scott and slipped back into reality, and picked up the glass by her side then took a sip of wine, he had not broken eye contact with her since he had sat down, and she felt slightly uncomfortable, but it seemed to be a good feeling and she knew by the warm feeling on her face that her cheeks were red. What were you thinking a minute ago, you looked a million miles away? Frost’s question was soft and filled with warmth that caught Seara in mid thought. Just something Bruce told me once. Katherine, you…you do know I was serious before don’t you? Asked Frost. Getting to one knee he held out the black box that he had found on the couch where it had slipped out Seara’s pocket. I was being serious Katherine, I love you more than I have ever loved anything, and I want you to be there permanently with me, what do you think? Seara gasped and leaning forward put her arms around Frost. Of course I will. The reply was simple and clear and hugging the female tighter Frost kissed her cheek, then leaning back he slipped the ring onto her finger.
