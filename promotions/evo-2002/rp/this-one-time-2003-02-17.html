This one time...

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.17.03 ~The day for reckoning has come; the days now draw ever so close until Tainted Love. Hell’s fury will come down upon thee in a wild match between Universal champion Shocka, and the challenger for his belt Scott Frost. These to men have a storied past but Shocka has obviously had the better out of the exchanges of these two. Remember back to Rise of the Legends when Shocka fought in 3 matches in one night to become the First Road to Glory champion and them moved onto take out L2J eventually leading to his death. Shocka has defended his title multiple times in between against Frost, Sovereign and Jason Jameson himself. He overcame all of the odds and showed everyone what he was truly made of but now…well now Scott Frost wants to finish off their feud with one last blow out. Shocka doesn’t even know what a Hell’s Fury match is but he has accepted it just to spite Frost. Shocka is not afraid to defend his title against the likes of Frost a man that he has beaten time and time again and why should he be…I mean he has had the greatest mentor in the business to face Cruiser weights in thug roC and he has been taken to all new heights with Morpheus Omega at his back. The sky is the limit for Shocka and at Tainted Love he will just be getting started.~ ‘You know the things that I do for this company…’ Shocka stands in front of a mirror shaking his head side to side, he draws water from a sink the cold faucet is turned on and he splashes some on his face. ‘I mean I go out drinking last night and then I get up at fucking 6 this morning to go do a promotional photo shoot for Tainted Love…I bet Frost is sleeping in right with Seara at his side.’ {Shocka turns and brings a towel to his face wiping off the dripping water he walks out of the hotel bathroom and into his actual room. He sits down on the bed and flips on the television just to catch the end of a promotional commercial for this week’s legends.} ‘With stars such as Troy Pierce, James Kimson, Scott Frost, then me?! WHAT THE FUCK IS THAT!’ Shocka screams loudly. He stands up and grabs a coat and heads out the door. {As he passes by a bell boy he simply gives him a shove and sends him tumbling into a cart of bags that topple with his weight. Shocka lets out a little snicker as he makes his way through the hotel lobby people gazing at him with half smiles but not all are smiling some glare at him for what he has done. With a wider shot from the front a shirt is now revealed to say ‘I Killed L2J’ with a large smiley face on it under the text. He reaches into his pockets and pulls out his keys he turns off his alarm and unlocks his doors and jumps into his car. Right as he is about to start it an eVo camera crew rushes him like lions to a feeding. Shocka rolls his eyes and then simply looks at the camera and begins speaking.} ‘You know what? I am going to show everyone in this federation exactly why I am the Universal fucking champion…until all of you people realize that I deserve the respect that I demand I will keep destroying little bitches like L2J and lead them to there deaths. I started with one member of the Notorious in L2J and now I am going to move onto Scott Frost, I showed Jameson what I was made of when I destroyed him in the ring before. Scott Frost must have a pretty thick skull though because I have tried to beat some sense into time and time again but he simply doesn’t learn, Seara must get better in the sack every time he looses so he wants to keep coming back for more just like at taco bell. You people have seen the fear in his eyes when he stares at me from across the ring and he keeps claiming that all he wants is redemption from his friends? I think he just want to try and prove that he can handle me, but you see no one in this federation can contain me…my closest competition is also my best friend! Frost you can do everything you can do through me off my game PLAN but it simply won’t work…I have to much skill to much game to get twisted and torn by a bitch like you. I won’t play your games, I am comin straight at ya like a freight train and there are no stops because this ain’t no Amtrak! You will go straight down like the fucking Colum-BIA! Something ain’t right in your head, I’ve tried to fix it many times with punches and kicks but now I’ll have to pull out the big guns and show you why people call you the snow blower…I don’t want you to pretend like my dick is a sno cone just because you’re the frost man don’t mean I need to be frozen again…’ Shocka looks at the camera one last time before slamming his car into reverse and taking off.
