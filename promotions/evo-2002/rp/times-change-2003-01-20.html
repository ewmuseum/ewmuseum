Times Change

20 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th January 2003 It’s getting close to Legends XXI and Asis has still not been seen by anyone since last Wednesday, not even Xander. After his actions on Legends XX he disappeared and has not re-emerged. But he knows that this Wednesday is important, not for him, but for Unlimited. He couldn’t care less whether he won the Continental title, not on a personal level anyway. But to bring more gold to Unlimited, that is his goal. And it makes it even sweeter that he can deprive Big Daddy Dreamer of it in the process. A man that despite what BDD thinks, he has never faced before. But a man that he looks forward to facing. And in a day he will get to face him and destroy him to keep him from the one thing he craves the most. Success… We’re very different people you and I BDD. We crave very different things in life, when before hand I thought we were in one way or another fairly similar. You see I do not want anything out of my wrestling career except to maim and hurt people and at one point I thought you might just be as malicious as myself. But I now realise that like so many others in this industry you are all about one thing. Greed, the thing that runs so many men’s lives. But you see greed blinds men, it blinds them to the truth. The truth that in the end we are all puppets being played with, being told what hoops to jump through, what goals to aim for. You are one of those puppets BDD, just like the rest of them, but I have seen through the deceit, I have seen through the lies and empty promises. That’s why I try to achieve nothing more in this industry than to hurt and torture people, innocent people like you… So you have a problem with Unlimited? Some kind of vendetta? Get in line. Everyone has a problem with Unlimited, but that’s the way it is liked, that’s the way it should be. If everyone didn’t care about us we wouldn’t be Unlimited. And you having a problem with Unlimited is fuelling you going into this match right? Well here’s news for you, you’re not facing Unlimited, you’re facing me. You need to be focused on this match, otherwise I’m going to take you apart just like I did Strictly Prohibited and just like I have done so many other people in eVo. You’re not special BDD, you’re not different to anyone else. What makes you think you can beat me? You’re success in other feds, the fact that you were once a long time ago the Universal Champion? Times change my friend and in your case they are definitely not changing for the better…
