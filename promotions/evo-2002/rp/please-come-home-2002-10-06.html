Please Come Home

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 6th 2002 Things have become noticeably strange and different lately when Seara is involved, and the female wrestler has been showing signs of her attitude changing dramatically. Sitting on the floor of a local child’s play park she pushes a swing as it swings towards her, in one hand she has a cigarette, and tightly clasped in the other is a defaced coin, she looks at it, and as the swing comes back towards her she pushes it away and gets to her feet. She only moves a few feet away, but moves under a small sheltered hut to stay dry in the heavy rain, the heavy gusts of wind are not much help though as they push the rain under the shelter with her. It is colder tonight than Seara has felt it in a long time, but she has become slightly resilient to the cold. She huddles deeper into the corner of the wood and steel structure and continues to stare hard at the coin in her hand. She has not yet realised the camera’s presence, either that or she is choosing to ignore it. She reaches into her inside pocket and drops the coin in, then pulls out a small envelope, it is still sealed and she opens it, carefully inspecting the writing on the front, she recognises it, and grinds her teeth in pure frustration, but continues to open it anyway. There is a strong sent of males deodorant from the inside of the envelope and lifting it to her nose Seara takes a deep breath, then mutters something about the familiar scent of her father. Turning the letter towards a light she reads it quietly to herself, occasionally laughing aloud, she finishes reading and looks up at the camera. Seara – they want me at the funeral, they said it wasn’t my fault, but who are they joking, they know I wanted him dead. She looks hard at the cameraman, her eyes flickering, she suddenly realises most people will have no idea what she is talking about, carefully she unfolds the letter once more and reads it, this time she looks at the camera and reads the words loud and clear, she has no real interest in what her brothers and father have said to her, or how much they want her back in their lives, she just continues to read the words as though it were part of a book, laughing at the most intimate deep emotional sentences, it is all a joke to her. Finishing the letter she screws it up into a ball, knowing that her family will be watching her every move on the TV, and throws it towards a bin. Seara- Vinnie should have never have asked to meet me, he knows that I wasn't ready to face him in a calm manner. I never killed him, but he did die while fighting against me, but it was his idea. Vinnie was always stubborn, he never knew when to give in, never wanted to admit I was dangerous, but he learnt his lesson. And now he will have all eternity to think about what he did, and why he pissed me off. He ended the career of the guy who I once called father, and I revenged it, no one deserves to have their careers ended in that way, not by family. To have to spent all your life in a wheelchair, knowing you could have been the best wrestler ever. That is a fate worse than death, to have to regret not doing the things you wanted to, regretting not dying for the rest of your life. To suffer in that way because of family is worse than death, Vinnie got off lightly, and now he has all eternity to regret what he did to the man who was once my family. Seara has become immune to her family; she has disowned them and is now proving to the world that when you are lost to someone, you can never find them again. For Seara, her family had been thrown into a black void, and she had no reason to let them out, she had found it easier and more interesting to have no family, she was emotionless, and carefree. With Legends XI coming close, she had no reason to want her family in her life, but she had every reason to want time alone to think about her next match, against the mighty Asis, a man who had twice destroyed her, a man who she had taken out once, and had beat once. But Seara is resisting talking about Legends right now, and standing up, she walks away from the camera and out the park gates.
