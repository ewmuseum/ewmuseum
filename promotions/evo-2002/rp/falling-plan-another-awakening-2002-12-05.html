Falling, Plan, Another Awakening.

5 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My hand cradled the drink on the table, as the ice clinked against the glass. It moved again as I lifted it, and then set it back down again. I watched the people here, from my spot in the corner. A little table with nothing but an ash tray and a view of the bar. Some smoke rose into the air from the cigarette resting on the ash tray. Things felt good. For a while, it seemed as though everything was falling into place. The roll he was on kept bringing him a little higher up, catching glimpses of the Paradise that rose above him. The title in his bag at home didn't mean much. Honestly, only one mattered, and he knew it. But it wasn't about that. The belt he had was one that Jason wanted, but couldn't have. He had kept proving him wrong, letting him get little hopes of glory before it came crashing down. And here was where it seemed as though there was some divine plan. This guy across from me almost looks like Jason. Pity. I threw some paper onto the table, and put out my smokestack. I was actually excited a little, anticipating the time when we entered our battle ground again. Pretty soon, Jason would be in for another awakening. This one might not be that easy to wake up from.
