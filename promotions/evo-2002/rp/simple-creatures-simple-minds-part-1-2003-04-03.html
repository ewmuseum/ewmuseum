simple creatures simple minds part 1

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3 [War is peace a wise man once wrote. Oh how true that is exactly. I know for many of here we get peace at being at war and fighting battles but in all wars there is a winner and a loser and being as Hedges always wins that must make the rest of you losers. Fate has signed your check and Hedges is here to cash in on it because when you meet you destiny I won’t be over tea and crumpets, oh no, it will be over blood and sweat.] The scene opens up with Hedges sitting in a row of pews inside a church. It’s relatively small inside and light dimly by hundreds of candles all around. Behind the alter is a large stained glass cross shedding it’s colored light onto a statue of Jesus praying. The church is empty except for the occasional nun walking to and fro going around into the back of the church. Hedges yells at a passing nun to walk over to him… “Peaceful place isn’t?” he says sounding awfully cheery. “Yes it’s relaxing, as it should be, this is a house of god” the nun replied back “You know it’s almost to peaceful. It seems to shelter all the hate, crime, and violence of the real world. I could see why you’d never want to leave.” exclaimed Hedges “Yes that is why I am a nun. I vow to give my life over to god and do exactly as the bible tells me” the nun says back Hedges begins to chuckle a little bit and pats the seat next to him. “Have a seat sister we will talk about the wants and needs of God shall we” He says “Why I’d love to. It is what I’m here for to help God’s children” the nun replies “Now about the bible. You say its God’s word and all. It’s the map of you life. Your destiny to follow it, am I not correct?” Hedges asks “You are” says the nun “Now take this for example how do you know the bible speaks the truth. You have faith right? You just have to trust as it says. To me that sounds like a hunk a B S. If I said god spoke to me and he said go and get a hundred slaves does that justify the action?” Hedges asks “Why certainly not!” the nun says raising her voice slightly “The bible does not forbid it though. It does not claim that slavery and war are bad so how can it be the center for all morals and values. Sister it shelters you from the real world. Sure there is crime and violence but there is also a lot of love and good things out. Pleasures to indulge yourself in. God wouldn’t have let us create the wonderful things we have unless he wanted us to enjoy them. Simply because a few cardinals and the pope say something’s wrong doesn’t make it. Who are they to tell me what’s right and wrong. God hasn’t spoken to them. They have there position by vote not by God’s word.” said Hedges “You make sense, but you have to have faith” the nun replied “There you go with that again…take this for example.” Says Hedges Hedges pulls out a piece of paper and writes down “go out and make sweet love with one of my children. Sex is a beautiful thing and I wouldn’t let you do it if it was wrong” “Message from God” Hedges proclaims “But you just wrot..” Hedges cuts her off “Yeah where you there when the people wrote the bible though? It’s the same thing. It’s a load of crap you know? You put you trust in a lie and live in a place that keeps you from living life to the fullest. They tease with wine when you could have whiskey. It simple pleasures of life you miss.” Hedges argues “But I have spent so long and it’s so certain. I can’t just turn and walk away” The nun says Hedges lights up a cigarette and laughs a bit as she says he can’t smoke in here “You know God created the tobacco plant man didn’t make it. So I will smoke in his house. Now You can walk away. You can reclaim your life. Stop wasting time. Hell god forgives sins anyways. You’re missing out of your sexual prime for fuck’s sake. Let’s go I’ll show you a night on the town…” hedges stands up with a smile on his face and the scene cuts out
