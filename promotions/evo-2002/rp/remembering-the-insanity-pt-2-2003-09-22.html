Remembering the Insanity pt. 2

22 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-22 Scott remembered everyday maybe he dwelled on the topic too much but he couldn't forget that memory no matter how vague it was. He had always secretly wanted to go back there and see for himself what his house had become. He needed to know so the nightmares would stop, he needed to see if his fears were true that the house was gone, that it had been destroyed. That house was a monument to the former life he had, to his story, and the stories of those who had lived within. As he sat within a taxi looking around seeing the familiar scenery that brought his state of mind back to the young man he had once been. He was no longer the same man he had been happiness no longer filled his pale blue eyes, fear was no longer an expression he remembered. Maybe it was because of the way things had gone for, maybe it was because of Black Hills but he realized that there's nothing to fear, he has nothing to lose and nothing to gain from winning or losing, they didn't matter, nor did staying alive in his opinion, each day passed by marks another day towards his demise and he'd never forget that. At any moment he could find himself back in the reality that he had years ago, he could find himself within a familiar and yet vacant neighborhood. His appearance differed little from what it had been those years ago, the scar was still there, the body was the same maybe slightly more toned but he looked the same as he had been those years ago, but the outside didn't matter. He no longer felt like a lucky man the hand he had been dealt had turned sour a very long time ago and the game was already over. As the Taxi pulled up to the address that had been burned into his brain he saw it, the same house it had been all those years ago yet there was something different. There was a different smell in the air, no longer that smell of life but a strong smell of death and the conditions of the inside of the building on added to that. The hallway was the same as he remembered it when he left it all those years except there were no more bodies, the bloodstains were there, so were the empty shells. He walked from the hallway into the living room his head held in shame, the living room was a different story, the windows showed evidence of numerous break-ins, the bare walls and the floor the only witnesses to those crimes. As he walked through all the rooms he found himself within an empty house with rot the only thing to see within. Then as he stepped into his child's old room he saw something the caught his eye. A childish toy lay on the floor the only possesion it seemed that hadn't been stolen. Scott picked it up and realized that this had been a present, his son had recieved it three months prior to his death, it had been a present from his Adam. Maybe it was because he needed a link to the past before he forgot it completely maybe it was because it was sacred in the sense that it resembled the happiness he once had. As he walked out of the house leaving the doorway open to the world hanging from the hinges. Scott entered the cab and something seemed to strike him. Maybe it was grief over lives the could never be saved, for those who were gone. Then he found himself remembering a phrase that he couldn't put a name on but it was sure that it had been important at the time. 'We all search for immortality, but few of us learn that it's just right in front of us.'
