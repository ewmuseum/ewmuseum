On This Night Victory Is Everything

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/11/02 ::'Moments make men.. ::Men however can make those moments happen' Backstage moments before Rise of Legends deep inside from within the Air Canada Centre we find Sean Mason standing with a mic in his hand and a camera crew standing before him. It had been a long week of preparation for Sean deep inside of the gym, but now was the moment where he had to stand up and be counted. Tonight Sean had to prove his title credentials, in a tournament in which four individuals strived to be recognised in the form of gold for their efforts in Evo. Dressed in joggers and a white vest top he exposes his athletic physique, but more importantly he shows off what is becoming a totally new side to Sean. Having struggled for so long in his early days within Evo, everything was now so much more about business. The funny lines, the charismatic Sean Mason was taking back seat to the focused and ruthless Sean Mason. The objective is still the same though it would seem, Sean is still here for the fans; but the means of executing his plans are now less glamourous. Gone is the highflying focus, now Sean has returned to his roots. In FAW drop kicks and high risk had proved acceptable, but Evo was different. So many have perished in Evo. I could go on for hours listing names of superstars who have proved nothing more than one night wonders, Evo's roster is determined by natural selection that is certain. Only the strongest in both mind and matter have survived the test of time and even for those taking the next step had proved a task too big for some. Tonight was Seans fourth PPV within Evo and up until now gold had been snatched from his clutches on every occasion. Tonight though that just couldn't happen. If ever a facial expression told a story it was now. Seans face was that of a man focused on a mission and determined to leave victorious at all costs. Tonight was the night during which Sean had to finally take that all important step, for the fans, but also for himself.... 'You ready guys?' Sean asked the crew who stood before him. They responded with a nod. Taking a moment to collect himself he then gave the signal. With one wave of the hand the cameras began to roll and Sean began his usual pre-match verbal input. 'Its been a long grueling journey since I arrived in Evo' Sean began by saying. 'Since the start of September I have been busting my ass so that I could live out my ambitions, but up until now I'd left each of the PPV's I'd been apart of a dissapointed man. Each loss I have picked up on the way though I would like to believe has made me stronger. I've never been someone who has dwelt on the past and despite my many setbacks I've tried to always draw an optimistic perspective from each of my outings. Physically I feel I've always been ready to move onward, but up until now I guess if we wanted to be critical perhaps I was naive mentally. Personally, im a firm beliver that the better man will always come out on top. I'd gone through my career believing that and up until now I had no reason to doubt it. Evo though during these past four months has brought me back to reality with a thump. Sometimes no matter how much you believe destiny can be determined by yourself, sometimes you have to remember that deep inside of the locker room potentially there is someone ready to pounce just to get noticed or to get even. First time you get taken out is their mistake, though if it happens again its your own. Im baring that in mind as I go into nights tournament. Tonight is not just about me and three others, because at any second a spotaneous thought backstage may change the path of destiny. I need to be alert. I need to be aware of that, but what I certainly don't need to be though is afraid!' Sean paused. As the camera took the opportunity to move slightly closer the scars from the past fortnight still could be made out, however the marks were faint. As Sean looked back up he could see one of the crew look at the marks and quickly he looked to dismiss them. 'They haven't gone I know' Sean said pointing towards the faint outline where the stitching had taken place, 'but then again I had'nt expected them too so I am not dissapointed'. With that the crew member turned away trying not to engage in a line of thought it was very clear Sean did not want to proceed down. However it seemed that the touch paper had now been lit and Sean had no choice but to address the matter further. 'Ive said it before and I will say it again' Sean began, 'the past has so little effect on the present. Take Thug Roc for example. Once upon a time im sure he was a man of integrity and a great master of the ring, but as times change so do the performance levels of people. Im a better wrestler than when I got to Evo and that is because I stood by my claim that I am good enough to be competing here. I've been naive before, but I am determined that will not happen again. I guarentee not one of the persons involved in tonights show goes in without problems from their past. Take Seara. Perhaps you could question whether she is more scared than me. I mean emotionally it has certainly been a rollercoaster for of late, but I know going into tonights match we could see a performance which claims gold. Then there is the other match. Two relative newcomers to Evo. They will have doubts, of that I am sure. For them this is their first major test in Evo, who wouldnt have some major nerves going into a competition of this kind. Can they put that behind them? If so there is no reason why they can not leave victorious. I guess what im trying to say is, however much we might think the past can play a factor, nights like tonight are determined alot by who can pull the performance out at that particular moment. Its every man and woman for themselves and if ever there was a night when I personally needed to be on my game it was tonight. For the first PPV ever, nothing seemed personal and tonight I just needed to be a ruthless business man. If I wanted gold, I had to take it and to do so I had to win two falls by any means necessary... 'Do you remember my debut?' Sean asked. The crew from behind the camera answered with a simple nod. 'Then you will remember that Seara and I first crossed paths all those moons ago? Is it just me who finds that slightly intriguing? That two people could be thrown together three months on in a match which means so much to both. For myself and Seara I can really see the first match as an opportunity just as great as the title shot that results from it. Tonight will show just how far both of us have come since arriving in Evo. Im looking forward to it and I really hope Seara is on form and mentally ready for this as much as I am. Forget about Frost, forget about Notorious. Emotions can so often get in the way and tonight I want you to know that despite the stakes, despite the pressure I have placed on myself; I plan on bottling up all those emotions and focusing them in one direction. Toward victory. When that bell rings Seara your merely a statistic. For these fans and for myself im not gunna look at you in any human way. Simply because from a personal standpoint when it rings again I intend on ensuring it is me and not you who stands with their hand raised....' With that Sean drops the mic to the floor and turns off into the distance. Normally pleasantries are exchanged. Though tonight, it would seem there is preparation to be carried out. Switching the cameras off the crew turn to one another contemplating the highly charged words which Sean spoke. Could tonight be his night? Or will he continue to be Evos 'Mr Nearly..'
