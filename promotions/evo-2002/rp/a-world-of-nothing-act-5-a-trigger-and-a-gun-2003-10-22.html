A World of Nothing Act 5 - A Trigger and a Gun

22 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-22 Sitting in a wooden chair in a dark room with a gun against your head, your heart skips a beat. A sudden feeling of nervousness fills your body. The nervousness, it didn't exist previously... but now? Now you want to put the gun down. No... One part of you wants to put the gun down. One part wants it to stay. One part really doesn't care. This is nothing new. It's not like I haven't done this several times before. Deep down, I've been suicidal since I was born. But, then again, we're all suicidal deep down. Me? I'm just more obvious. When I pull this trigger, it might start a chain reaction. Next door, someone will hear a gunshot. They'll call the police. The police will barge in and see me. They'll see me and run to me. They'll run to me and feel half sick as I'm recorded as America's newest dead man. Twitch. For one reason or another, I'm feeling sick at this point. The trigger, at this point, is wet with sweat. A drop falls from the tip of my nose, down to my lap, as the words of Sam, Samantha, Vera run through my head. 'We're all dying, you know. None of us are living.' I'm about to die. How's that, babe? 'People like to believe their living though because they're scared of death. Are you scared of death, Sean?' No. 'You're better than half the people in this world then.' Thank you. It's funny how one minute you can be considering retiring from your job and the next minute you can be considering suicide. It's funny how one minute you can be happy and the next you can be so depressed that you need to result to the power of a bullet. It's funny, but I felt suddenly confused. I pushed the trigger.
