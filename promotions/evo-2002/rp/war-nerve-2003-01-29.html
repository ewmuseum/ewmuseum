War Nerve

29 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th January 2003 Another pay per view has passed, more revelations have occurred within eVolution and despite how it might have appeared to most, Asis had a fun night out. New Year’s eVolution was supposed to be a night for Unlimited and whilst Asis was not betraying Unlimited, he really did not care about the tag team titles that night. In fact, in the match itself, he was not concentrating completely and there was a good reason for it. He was thinking about the plans he had for the match following his. A match involving two men that he despised with all his being. Two men that he left lying unconscious in the ring later on… Uncharacteristically Asis is in Unlimited confines, the so-called “War Room” of Unlimited. As per usual it is a darkened room, with only one light on. This light is above the circular table in the middle of the room, the table that is in between Asis and the camera. I warned them, I fucking warned them, I told them it was going to happen, they didn’t listen though did they. No they didn’t and look where it got them. Asis is pacing up and down the room, he knows the camera is there but as he sometimes does he is paying no attention to it at all. He is talking to himself, smiling to himself and even laughing to himself. The regression that Asis gets from being a fairly intelligent man into this sort of state happens in a heartbeat. The aggression, the violence, the rage it is always there, but the state of mind changes, and it definitely has done that. He now turns and looks at the camera and for the first time talks as if he knows it’s there. They had their warning. They were told that I was not the kind of guy that took lightly to being upset. And now they’ve paid the price. BDD and Mason both have paid the price. BDD may have remained champion, but that has worked to my advantage. Because now that I have sent them both a message, I get my chance to take them on, one on one, no interruptions. Mason, you’re first. I say first because you will not stand in my way of facing BDD for the Continental title. You speak of your attack on me during Legends XXII as if it was some sort of victory to you. Well Sean, it wasn’t. It really served you no purpose except to fuel my anger which I think I showed a glimpse of to you at New Year’s eVolution. That’s right Sean, a glimpse of the hatred that I hold for you, the rest you will feel this week. That attack at New Year’s eVolution was just a taster of what I want to do to you. Leaving you lying in the ring to most people is nothing different to what I’ve done to anyone else. But to me it held special meaning Sean. As you may have noticed after Legends XXII I was not in the best of moods. I wanted revenge on several people and I got some of that on you at New Year’s eVolution. But for one reason or another Sean, it felt different beating the shit out of you compared to the countless others I’ve done it to. It felt even more satisfying; not because of what you did to me the Wednesday before, but purely because it was you. And before you jump to any conclusions such as jealousy, everyone knows my standpoint on people like you within eVolution. You are the guys that are in it for the normal reasons. The fame, the money, the glory, the titles, the thrill of hearing the fans chant your name and scream for you and most of all the business is your life. I have stated time and time again that none of this appeals to me, Sean the only reason I do what I do, is to hurt people. To cause them pain. That is what appeals to me most about wrestling. And hurting you gave me the biggest thrill I’ve had in a long time. And I plan on feeling that again on Wednesday. Asis is still pacing up and down and eventually stops, slamming his hands down on the table in front of him and hunching over staring into the camera. His hair falls about his face as always and his red eyes seem to glow with the reflection of the light. You were right about one thing though Sean, nobody’s the same. And while I may not have ever faced you, I have faced countless people like you. People who for all intents and purposes are exactly the same kind of person. The kind of person that cares about the fame, glory, fans, all of that bullshit, the kind of person that I can destroy at my own will. Because Mason you have never, never faced anything like me. You have no clue what you have let yourself in for, absolutely no clue. You’ll find out soon enough though and I gurantee that you not be walking out of that arena Wednesday night having added to the Mason legacy. Because despite what you think, survival has not been achieved yet…
