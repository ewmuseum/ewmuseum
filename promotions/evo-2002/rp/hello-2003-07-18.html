Hello

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-18 Since I can remember... I've always had problems believing in things. While the other kids children in school ran around believing in UFOs, Ghosts, and Vampires, I... simply didn't. I questioned things, and still do. I questioned the existence in God and the Devil. Ghosts and Goblins just weren't real. How could they be? It was an impossibility. Angels? Aliens? Zombies? Santa Claus? As time passes and the world changes, these often mystic beings mature. What they used to be turns into something new. Scientists estimate the fall of Christianity and the belief in Jehovah will happen before the year 2100. By 2100, we'll have proof that there's no such thing as Ghosts. Those monsters living under your bed? By 2100, you can rest at ease. Aliens and abductees and mutilated cows will all be a thing of the past. By 2100, Science will have ruined the world. By the time we're dead and gone, the fact that we ever worshipped gods or believed in ghosts will join the woman who impaled herself on a car gearshift and the man who had a hamster shoved up his bum in the Urban Legends section. The fact that we even believe in Gods and higher powers is Neanderthalic. The fact that we're so uncomfortable with ourselves that we put our faith in greater beings sounds like something an ancient civilization would do. Deep down, under the skin, we must be nothing more than Neanderthals. Ignorant, nescient, Neanderthals. By 2100, we'll have a cure for cancer. But, have you ever asked yourself if perhaps another 'you' is out there somewhere? A unofficial clone? Have you ever wondered if you have a twin out there that you didn't know about? Is it really Scientifically impossible? Despite my disbelief in most 'wonders of the world'... I've always believed that there's another 'me' in this world. Another. The huge question here is... what if you find this 'other you'? Is that possible? A lot of people ask me if I know Terlayne Sterling... You know, by 2100 we'll all have clones. Fake reflections of ourselves except, the clones will be better than us in every way. Superior. And what then? The clones will live our pointless lives without any desire? No reason to live? It's like giving birth to a stone... As we evolve we fall. As we fall we begin to crawl. As we crawl we sink deeper and deeper into oblivion. And as we near oblivion we find that the year 2100 and the fall of mankind isn't so far away. ...hello, Terlayne.
