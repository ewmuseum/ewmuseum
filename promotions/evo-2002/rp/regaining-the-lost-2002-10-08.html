Regaining the Lost

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/8/02 Quasar was mere seconds from obtaining the number one contendership for the High Impact title, but it was snatched away from him by a new face to eVo, Andrew Martin. Instead of being closer, Quasar was set back. And now instead of going one on one for the title, it's a three way. Normally Quasar wouldn't be stressing, but there is a huge problem. He doesn't know who the third participant is! How are you supposed to prepare for such a big match when you don't even know who you are facing? It's damn near impossible to get your full mind plan together. All Quasar has to go by is his will to win. His will is high, but is it possible to battle two people and come out victorious? The scene opens up to a dark alley. It's poorly lit, with only the guidance of a street light. It's a normal alley scene with the big garbage can to the side and trash spread all over the ground. Off in the distance we here cursing and yelling. The camera turns to an angle and from around the corner comes two figures. The figure behind the first dives and tackles him to the ground. The camera gets closer as the guy on top is still yelling and screaming his lungs out. We suddenly see that the man is Quasar, but the guy underneath him looks highly unfamiliar. Quasar: You thought you could get away punk?! Man: Get off me. Quasar: Give me my damn wallet! Man: There! The man tosses a thick leather wallet from his hand in desperate need to escape. Man: Now get off of me! Quasar: You wish you could get off that easy. The man at the bottom begins to struggle and manages to shift his weight. He pushes Quasar off and then springs to his feet. Quasar steps towards him but the guy swings sending Quasar to the ground. The man scoops up the wallet and then takes off down the dark alley running like the wind. Quasar slowly gets up and holds his head down. He turns his head slowly and we see blood trickling from the gash in his lip. His jaw tightens as he kicks a garbage can across the air. Quasar: Society is so messed up!!! I come out for one second and instantly I am mugged. What's rightfully mine is taken and does anyone care to help? NO!! He starts walking towards the light escaping the dark alley with much anger flaring in his eyes. Quasar: Two hundred dollars is easily replaceable, but the whole thought is still there. No one as deserving as me should be kept from what is rightfully theirs! That ass back there knew that I was better and so he robs me! Where is the justice? I worked hard for that and he just snatched it up like it was nothing. He reaches a red sportcar and pulls a set of keys from his pocket. He steps over towards the driver's side, unlocks the door, and sits down in the car still contemplating about this terrible event. Quasar: The whole world obviously has this fetish with me. They just can't leave me alone. I can understand it's because of my greatness, but there must be a line drawn somewhere. And if takes reinforcements to do it then so be it. From now on when I walk these codemned streets I will have bodyguards. If someone even breaths in my direction thier legs will be snapped in a matter of seconds. A short pause. Quasar: All of this of course applies to eVo as well. The only difference is I will be returned what's rightfully mine, whether it is given up or I have to fight full heartedly for it. I honestly don't care, just as long as when the night is through, I finally have my High Impact title back in my hands. He inserts the key and revs up the engine. He steps on the gas and takes off as the scene fades to black.
