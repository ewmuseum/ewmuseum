Diary of a Champion: Strapped Down in a Gurney

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-24 So I just lay there. My entire body screaming. I don’t know about most of you all, but if you’ve ever had a Rib injury it’s probably the most tiring and painful injuries you can have. I remember when I was reading Mic Foley’s book not long after he mailed me a copy that he said after all the shit he went through and put his body through that the most painful injury he ever had was an ab strain. Doesn’t sound like much. But when you think about it, it’s all center of mass. Your toro is the largest part of your body and your ribs protect your internal organs. So you mess with your ribs, or pull a muscle down in your abs it’s going to affect you. And this is effecting me. It hurts to move it hurts to breath It hurts to think. So I just lay there, strapped down in a Gurney in the hallway of an emergency room in Japan. People running around like it was a cartoon, poking and touching me asking me questions in Japanese. Now, I’ve been to Japan a number of times in my career. After-all Japan’s wrestling scene is big time, they love wrestling. The fans are more polite, they look up to us with great respect and so I always felt great about performing in front of them, although cutting promo’s and stuff for the sake of television always felt weird. They generally pipe in the chants and applause because Asian fans are far to polite to scream and jump up and down, they clap, nicely. After the show they don’t swarm you in the parking lot then quietly hope that you come to them. Normally I would have gone right out after the match and signed until my pen ran dry. But normally I don’t get my ribs caved in during a match by some Son of a Bitch with a superiority complex. Each blow with that chair felt like a gunshot going off into my body. My ribs, already banged up real good from when he put me through that table just before Digital Destruction, then that match with him at the Pay Per View itself didn’t help my cause any. Now, after like a dozen blows with a chair my ribs are clear broken. I think I heard a doctor say 4 broken ribs... but my Japanese is pretty rusty, he could have been saying he needed 4 nurses to carry my body around the hallway. So I just lay there, strapped down in a Gurney wondering how bad it was. I was still in wrestling trunks, my knee brace was taken off and it was resting at my feet while the nurses and interns ran around looking for someone who spoke English. I was wondering what the weather was like back home, thinking happy thoughts to get me through another night in a hospital. It had seemed to be the most effective way to get through it all simply because, after being a wrestler for so long you realize that the ER is like a secondary home. I have run into a lot of co-workers and promoters just by chance because I had to get something checked out. A CAT scan here, a broken wrist there. At this point I was counting my blessings glad it wasn’t my neck, or that I had no puncture in my Lungs, or that I didn’t take any serious blows to the head or lost any blood. Just ribs. Didn’t sound so bad, until I tried to sit up and the pain shot through my body like a knife through butter. I didn’t want to try that again as I reached for my gym bag that was sitting beside me. I could hear something, a ringing. So I reached over to the bag and pulled out my Cell phone, it was ringing “off the hook” I pressed the talk button. “Yeah.” I said, bluntly, it even hurt to talk. It was Rob B. on the other line, he was part of the eVo committee. At least someone called to see how I was. Justin Fritz rode with me to the ER in the ambulance, so many guys had been sent over to Hospitals all over the city, in fact I was the only one sent to this hospital. Maybe someone was looking out for me, or maybe to single me out because I’m quote unquote “more important”. Rob assured me that quote unquote “that would never happen again” and that “Leya wasn’t going to be allowed to get away with this” sort of story. It felt like he was trying to be honest. To be honest on my own part, I knew that he was just trying to fix an already drastic situation. Shocka lost at Digital Destruction and it was eating at him on the inside. So he decided to try and gain even more of an upper hand for Faith by busting me up. So what was my next move? Well... it was to get the hell out of this damn Gurney! So I just lay there, strapped down in a Gurney, seething. I wanted to get back at Shocka for what he did to me. It had nothing on the surface to do with Faith. In the end I’ve always been a firm believer in the old phrase that What goes around comes around. Dad always used phrases like that, he told it to me when I was 6 years old when I was a bit of a bully with other kids and what happened? I got my ass kicked by a bigger, stronger and meaner kid. Shocka, he’s a bully. That much is a fact, and he’s got a mental problem with losing. He can’t take it, and so he sat there, brooding and bitching that I beat him fair and square at Digital Destruction, he is all over him. So now here is my Number One Contender, with a chair in hand playing the xylophone with my rib cage. Hey, I mean no one likes to lose. That’s just part of the Human condition. But we have to take things as they come. Shocka is getting a second chance here, all because he was at the right place at the right time, kissing the right ass at the right time. Leya Forth, our beloved committee member and my long last Sister would have taken anyone to be Shocka. The First person with any sort of physical skills and with her help to get the title off my shoulder and make my life a living hell. Shocka, he might be strong and tough, and he might have taken some killer batting practice when he was in school, but can he beat me? He hasn’t yet. Can he beat me with Leya Forth wearing the pinstripes? I think that he’s got a pretty good chance. Especially when it feels like someone’s jabbing me with a fireplace poker every time I take in a breath. So I just lay there, strapped down in a Gurney, sleeping. I was tired, my body wanted to rest, it wanted to simply shut down, but it was hard to sleep, the world buzzed around me it seemed. Rob B. assured me that everything was going to be taken care of, and that the doctors would make sure that Leya couldn’t book me in any matches over the next 6 days but that I would have to be at Legends next week simply based on my “drawing power”. That people would feel ripped off if the Champion Poster boy of eVolution was missing televised shows. I wasn’t in any position to argue with Mr. B., I was going to be there even if he told me to take the month off. But right at that point I was pretty much ready to lay there, strapped down in a Gurney and let the world pass me by while I lay there helpless. The clock at the end of the hallway was reading 3:14 in the morning, and I hadn’t even spoken to a Doctor who had any sort of English skills at all. I knew my ribs were broken, I didn’t need a Doctor to tell me that. I just wanted to be reassured somehow. I wanted out of this God Damn streatcher and into a bed or something. Maybe something to dull the pain? I didn’t think that was a seriously insane request, after all, the bruising was looking rather nasty, it was gonna hurt to shower in the morning, let alone eat, walk. Yeah it was effecting me. Yeah it hurt alright. But it wasn’t going to hurt half as bad as what I was going to do to Shocka when I got my hands on him So I just lay there, strapped down in a Gurney, plotting, scheming. Most fans look to the good guy to do what’s right. They come from the point of view of the “baby face” was to be honorable. Yeah, maybe that’s the way it should be. But when I get blindsided by some no good piece of shit, he’ll get what’s coming to him. He’ll be at Legends next week, he’d probably want to keep me weak for his big reward match at Faith. Well, it simply wasn’t going to work that way. It wasn’t going to be so simple for Shocka. Because I was laying there, in a Gurney, smiling as a laugh escaped my lips.
