Forgotten past...

17 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-17 The scene opens with all of the current New-TV members sitting around a table at a nightclub, they all have content looks on their faces; they all went undefeated and won thier matches on legends. Morpheus Omega has a despised, but satisfied look on his face; he slams back a shot and sighs out of relief. Sky Night standing to the side of Morpheus with a sexy grin, she blows a kiss to him. Thug roC and Hedges with drinks in their hands, they listen to the dark, dirty techno blaring loudly. Hedges turns to roC. 'Last legends was just the beginning of my new reign!' Hedges says smiling. 'This week I have my shot for the Evolved championship!' he comments. 'I think when push comes to shove, after last week beating Xero and Exodus by yourself. You should have good leverage over your competition' Thug mentions. 'Keep your eyes on MLX, but play your cards right with him... Watch the ambition Seara will put into the match towards MLX, then strike them both when the timings right!' 'Why do you think you know, how my match will go down?' Hedges questions with an odd look on his face... he continues. 'I can handle the two of them, like I beat the two of those skirts the other night... Hah, thanks for the concern though' 'You wanna know, how I know that this will all go down?' roC questions and continues 'Because I have seen both of them compete enough times to predict their every move... We should save the talk for the locker room and check out some of these beautiful women in here!' he says with an excited voice. 'No doubt, I have seen nice ones around...' Hedges says to Thug, he turns to Morpheus and Sky; he speaks 'Do you two want to come down to the bar and grab more drinks with us? Or wait here?...' he questions. 'We will wait here, but bring us back more drinks while your up! If your think you are going to be champ then you best be able to drink like one too' Morpheus says with a crazed smile, he slams back another shot and smiles. 'Don't get lost bar side too long with all these women you have been talking about!' 'We will at least bring you back drinks before we disappear into the party scene' Thug says smiling. 'Tonight we celebrate a flawless new-tv night on Legends. Syndicate and Jameson should learn from their mistakes made when they faced the best cruiser weight champion in the world and best tag team to ever embrace eVolution!' Thug says with a wide grin on his face. 'WE ALWAYS STEAL THE SHOW!' 'Soon the timing will be right...' Sky says out of nowhere. 'For a cloud of darkness to sweep over eVolution and and a battle will be fought. One army so sick and twisted, you could only picture in your worst and most terrifying nightmares. The chosen one will seek and destroy, but also follow. When the timing in imminent' Sky snickers to herself, like she thought she was alone... 'What did you just say?' Morpheus turns to her with an odd look on his face... 'Uhhhhh... huh, what?' Sky Night says with a completely different tone of voice, she seems kind of baffled and confused like she doesn't know what happened. 'No, what did you just say Sky.... That was really fucked up and delusional like you were with us, but not at the same time. You are one strange girl!' Morpheus retorts. Thug roC and Hedges turn to walk away, like they were weirded out by Sky and her unusual rant. Thug yells back to them 'Holla back at you soon!' They continue talking and disappear down some stairs and into a crowd of people. Morpheus still staring oddly at Sky. 'I don't know what you are talking about... Honestly and you should be the only one to believe me, when I tell you that. Can you tell me what I said?' Sky questions. 'You were talking about some dark cloud, and a battle to be fought. Something about a chosen one to follow and seek and destroy when the timing is imminent. This is no normal ranting out of the blue, where did that come from Sky, that was fucked!' Morpheus says sounding concerned. 'I have no clue what your talking about, the only thing relatively close would be these bizarre dreams that I used to have when I was a child, it was something like that... but its the last thing I want to talk about, it bothers me just mentioning it!' Sky says sounding deeply upset. 'I think we should talk about this!' Morpheus demands and sounds even more concerned now, he continues 'Sky you cant hold this back and not face a fear, you would only make it worst... This is childish, what's wrong Sky?' Morpheus asks. 'I SAID, I DON'T WANT TO TALK ABOUT IT!' Sky screams and sounds very angry, she turns her back to Morpheus and breathes shallow... Sky says in a more calmed voice. 'Its in the past, I want to forget about it... Never mention it again!' Sky instructs Morpheus with a serious tone of voice. Sky quickly walks away trying to hold back her fear and emotion. The scene fades - to be continued.
