Date: 4/3/03 [Life is full of pleasures and pains and every human is capable of giving them sometimes someone gives a bit more of the other or one and not the other. Hedges takes the second] The scene opens back up with Hedges giving the priest the two finger salute as he and the nun walk arm and arm out of the church. The priest puzzled for a moment watches them go out the door. He runs after the sister. “Where are your going?” the priest ask bewilderedly “She’s coming to get her life back you phoney crock. If you where about god you wouldn’t be driving a BMW and you get some seats for those stupid benches or wait does the bible prohibit comfort in life. Now scram she’s got a life to live.” Hedges shouts at him The nun gives the cold shoulder as Hedges helps her into his black hummer and shuts the door. He flips the priest the bird as he stands in shock staring at them. Hedges gets in and drives off.~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The scene opens back up as the nun has changed into a black dress hat goes a bit above her knees it shows a bit of cleavage and not to much back. She’s a bit pale from the lack of sun from the convent. A pretty girl with short blond hair and a set of kahonas that would make any man double take. “What do you do for a living Mr. Hedges?” The nun asks “I’m a wrestler. Used to be in the army but some shit went down and I had to leave. Now I’ve been fighting people for large amounts of cash for about 7 years now. It’s a good living and what I love to do. I’ve made quite a few enemies but none that even put me remotely in danger.” Hedges exclaims “That’s good…I guess…You ever hurt anyone?” The nun asks nervously Hedges laughing hysterically responds “ Just about everyone I step in the ring with gets hurt but that’s what we are paid to do and it’s a dog eat dog world and I just so happen to be a dog catcher so not to many fuck with me” “That’s good, I think…” the lady replies “Of course it is…stop being so sheltered…You know though indulging in so called sin isn’t a bad thing you know it’s actually the best part of life. Morals are for sissies. The only code to live by never hit a woman unless stricken first other than the it’s fair game. I have no codes of ethics to hold by I take actions as I see fit as they come. Sometimes different people get different actions out of me because I give them the power to control their fate but always they fall like the rest.” He says The scene ends up in a hotel hallway with the nun stumbling drunkenly with hedges to a room. He opens it up and she goes in he stands in the hall chuckling to himself “Oh misguided creatures to bad they all are lower than me. It’s so easy to manipulate and con anyone into anything. Even the purest of life forms fall victim to me. Funny, amusing disgusting, soon to be degrading depending on how you want to look at this it all plays out to be the same. I am the all knowing supreme being and I can fuck with any like I did this pathetic woman. Male or female you will learn not to play games with Hedges or you will be fucked in the end and generally I don’t mean literally because a homo is something I’m not…” “Hurry up” the nun yells laughing “Virgins I love em” With that he walks into the room and she wraps her arms aorund him and starts kissing him has e kicks the door shut and the scene fades to black
