La Chasse Pour Un Fantôme - Partie Six

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 21st The food was perfect. It wasn't anything spectacular, mostly due to the fact that the cooking equipment wasn't the most modern and advanced. It also didn't have any special zest or appeal to it. But it was excellent. It made you feel like you were in a cafe, which they were. That wholesome feeling you get because you know that these people are strictly here for you, and you only. It makes you feel special, or important. It makes you feel what they're not trying to express. But by now, she was beginning to become unsettled again. All throughout their wondrous dining ordeal they really engaged in small talk. Raising questions and giving answers an essay long where a simple utterane would've sufficed. For a while, they both felt like normal people. For a while. 'Sure you don't want a smoke?' 'Positive. What makes you think I'd start? Just because you offered?' 'You used to take what I offered you.' 'That's something I've been trying to forget.' 'Bad experience for you?' 'More of a ridiculous situation that's nothing but a flash out of history. Usually you don't remember things that have no importance.' 'Alright then. Why am I sitting here now?' 'First I want to know something. What are the drugs for?' 'Oh, so you know about them huh?' 'How else do you think I found you?' 'I've been experiencing a little...pain lately.' 'That's your escape huh?' 'You could say that. Why do y-' 'Is he using it?' 'Not that it's any of your business, but no, he's not. You should know better. Why do you care anyway?' 'Because I want to talk to him.' The waitress, and her other accomplices looked across the cafe, as his loud, almost mocking laugh rang out. He continued to chuckle as he attempted to light his cigarette. Unfortunately, his exhaling gasps were causing the lighter to keep going out. 'This is why I knew this would be something close to impossible.' 'I'm sorry. Look, seriously, I am sorry for laughing. But, you can't really be serious about this can you?' 'Why can't I be?' Although her expression had remained the same sullen, down-played emotion, his changed. The humorous tone and laugh in his last sentence was changed drastically into a cold stare that she almost considered inhuman. 'Because maybe he's tired of being fucked over and over again by you. And I don't mean in the sexual way. As it is, your not really worth wasting a load on. In my opinion at least.' 'You know what? Fuck you. You'e always just been an angry, repressed asshole, so fuck you. Are you gonna tell me where he is or not?' 'Why the fuck should I? Almost everytime he has something good going for him, you come along. And it's never to congratulate him, or show him a good time. It's always to try and bring him right the fuck back down.' 'Well maybe I feel a little cheated.' 'Maybe you are. That's life. Get over it, or fuck on out of it. Before you start making a list of demands or wants, maybe you should think for a minute. Try and come up with a good enough reason for someone to give you the shit your asking for.' He yanked something white out of his coat pocket, and started moving his hand rapidly over a napkin. 'You'd think by now you'd realize that all you cause is pain. But you just keep on coming. He's either going to wind up dead, or giving your eulogy.' That last part struck her. Him making a reference to her dying over this. But it didn't phase him at all, as the napkin flung across the table. She didn't have time to say anything, as he yanked his coat and walked away. She watched him leave, feeling sorry for herself. Something silver in his pocket cast a quick glare, before it was hidden back into the recesses of the black fabric. She figured it was just a flask, given his history of alcohol abuse. But it had more of the shape of the butt end of a small firearm than it did a liquid container. That didn't enter her mind, as she slowly opened the piece of cloth. Scribbled, but legible, was an address. Finally, she was able to breath a sigh of relief. The hunt was over. The ghost, had been found.
