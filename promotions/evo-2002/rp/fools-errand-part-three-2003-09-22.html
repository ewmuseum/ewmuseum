Fools Errand Part Three

22 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-22 “I’m just saying.... It’d just be a whole lot easier if I stayed down for the ten count is all...” A distressed Terlayne said with a B&H hanging from his mouth. They’d been talking about their match that night in Damon’s locker room. Damon was clearly aggravated, having the top three buttons on his shirt open and he clenched a handful of hair. “No, for the last fucking time it’d be too obvious.... Now what I want to happen is to make Sean Sterling look like the biggest fool possible, retain my title and make you “happy” in the process,” Damon grinned. By happier he obviously meant richer. “It’s not that I disagree... It’s just such a stupid idea...” Terlayne replied, stroking his forehead self-consciously. Damon’d told him that by throwing the match he’d be doing both of them a favour, he just failed to see the bright side. Damon began sweating like a paedophile on a school bus as he began to tell Terlayne his plan... “So you not only want me to throw the match, you want me to say that you’re the greatest and all?” Terlayne obviously disapproved of Damon’s plan and it seemed like an agreement would never be reached between the two. Eventually, Damon began to tell him of his new plan. Light Bulb. “So you want me to stay down for the ten count then?”
