Hidden Action part 1

18 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-18 The scene fades into a dark room, light by a solitary candle in the far candle, it throws shadows across the small floor space of the motel room, illuminating two figures sitting in silence at a table, seemingly waiting for someone or something to appear. A knock at the door disturbs the silence and getting to their feet, one of the figures walks across the room and opens the door. The figure is familiar and there is something about the long black trench coat that marks a change about to take place. As the third figure joins the others, there is an awkward silence. 'What in hells name is he doing here?' The males voice rings out into the previously silent room and in one swift the newest figure steps forward and raises a hand, ready to hit the male. 'Don’t start children, I invited you two here for a reason, now sit down and don’t question me again.' A female voice so familiar comes from under the black hood and trench coat. Removing the hood, the female reveals herself. Seara. Sitting down the males look at her, their faces black, no light managing to pierce under their hoods. 'Where’s Frost?' Both the males ask simultaneously, their voices are so familiar but it is hard to place them to a face or name, something about them is far too mysterious and they seem to not want to be seen at all, although the female has willingly let her face be seen by the camera that has followed her here tonight for the first time in a long time. 'Scott. Scott’s busy with his own things.' Seara’s reply was sharp and somehow filled with anger, although she did not mean to sound as though she and Scott Frost were no longer getting on well, the two males knew somehow that the female was just angry, she had been for a while, she had kept to herself around eVo and was rarely seen around anymore, she had become somewhat of a mystery. New wrestlers to EVolution were slightly unsure of the female and who she really was. Most questioned her and her relationship with Frost, saying it was more a publicity stunt rather than anything else and that the pair were no longer trying to make fans believe they were together, but those who needed to know about the relationship knew the truth and that was enough for Seara right now. 'I know this is hard for you, I mean you hardly see him, you are off training all the time and Frost is always up to something.' The female turned back towards the table and slammed her hands down on it, making the males jump. 'This has nothing to do with Scott, our relationships fine. I know we don’t spend much time together, but if I don’t bother trying to fix my mistakes, then eVo will never remember why I was the eVolved champion. Scott will not be involved in my business here, ever, I don’t want him getting hurt and if he gets involved that’s all that will end up happening, now lets stop this talk of Scott and get to the point of why I asked you both here.' Pausing Seara lights a cigarette and paces around the edge of the room while she smokes it. The room is desolate now except for the table in the centre and the pile of old furniture in one corner. It has all been destroyed and looking at it, the males figure the female has been here for a while on her own. They are right to an extent, this motel room in downtown Detroit is Seara’s own little place, she moved herself in when it became free and instructed the manager and owner of the place to never let anyone else stay in that room, and to never go in there. The name she gives at reception is Katherine Jackson, although the men at reception do not believe she is really the eVolution wrestler. 'Fine, so why did you call us here?' Ask the males after a long wait, their question seemingly infuriates Seara who hates to be rushed into anything that she is responsible for, but in an air of calm thought she is simple in her reply. 'Business. I have a little task I want you two to help me with. I chose you two because I think you could be very valuable to me.' Seara turns to look at the figure that had first been there with her. 'You’re a friend; someone Scott seems to trust and I too have trust in you, which is why I asked you here first. And you, I believe you could do some damage with a bit more direction.' Seara looks at both the males and steps back away from the table. 'Now eVo’s wrestlers have been letting themselves slip a bit, I noticed it when I lost the title, I forgot why I had so much hate and passion here in eVo, but now I remember something, I remember how I felt when I first came to eVo, when I was the newest rookie, I looked to heels like Asis for support and safety, and he taught me a quick valuable lesson, heels are not to be trusted. Now some of them have got a little too big for their boots, and some are more arrogant and self-absorbed than they always were, and I believe it is about time the heels of eVolution were taught a little something. There’s also the little trouble we seem to be having with a rookie around eVo, it seems he is a little too cocky and cocky is something I don’t tolerate. Now there is only one thing I want to hear from either of you before I leave. Are you in or are you chicken shits? I want to know that I can trust you and you are with me on this before I give out any more details or information.' Standing deadly still Seara waits until she hears both males say they want to know more and that they are with her before she abruptly leaves the room. She is gone only a moment before she returns. 'Be ready, I’ll call you when I’m ready for you, and don’t get too comfortable, I want you gone before I get back.' Once again the female leaves, slamming the door shut as she exits and the scene slowly fades to black as the two males stare at each other, barely able to make out each other’s faces in the dark room.
