A new Brotherhood....wait a minute it's just me

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/6/03 'My little girl asked me daddy why is Scott Frost belt bigger than yours?' Malcolm Luther Xavier stands on the balcolny of his hotel room looking out to see the predawn sunrise. 'I had to tell her in all honesty; that the reason Scott Frost's belt is bigger than mine is quite simple......Daniel Somers refuses to make me a BIGGER ONE!!!!' he starts to pace back and forth as if reliving the conversation...... 'However instead of taking my concerns over various issues like a man, Daniel Somers has opted to keep me off of TV. He is using his power to keep the greatest evolved Champion of all time off of TV...there by justifying his refusal of giving me a bigger belt.' his hands grip the side of the balcolny.... 'What in the hell must I do?!?!?! Go for the Universal Title myself?!?!?! That is a belt I have held three times already...albeit through proxy, so why must I feel obligated to make Scott Frost feel pain just for your amusement Somers? admittedly a fun way to spend the afternoon but an excercise not worthy of the greatest evolved champion of all time.' looks down to the street below and starts to wonder if the evolved Championship can bounce....... 'You see Somers. YOU were the man I wanted to face. Mind you not as validation for my place here in Evolution because as the title itself says I am the personification of Evolution Wrestling.....it's about making you realize I am everything this federation is about.' starts to dangle the title over the balcolny....... 'Yet you said No....without a giving it serious thought; and you left me unbooked for Legends as if to not deal with me, as if you had more important things. WHAT IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN MAKING THE CHAMPION WITH THE FED NAME IN THE TITLE HAPPY!?!?!?!?! It's time you get your priorities straight Somers!' pulls the belt back and throws it over his shoulder..... 'Have no fear Somers, I make it a habit to fix your constant mistakes. I will correct the obvious oversight and book myself on Legends.....on which I will defend MY title. You should pay closer attention to areas you need to and not NTICW or Evolution's Tag Team division. One way or another Somers you will take notice and you will give me what I want....' he turns to walk away..... '.......a bigger belt.'
