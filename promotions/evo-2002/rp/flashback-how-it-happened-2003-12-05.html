Flashback: How it happened...

5 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-05 There is a sound of knocking on a door. '...Come in.' the voice of Scott Frost can be heard. The scene opens up, and two police officers one male and one female walk into the hospital room where Scott is surprisingly standing up next to his bed fixing the back of his hospital gown. 'Hello sir, are you Scott Michael Frost?' The male officers asks. Scott looks at him and slowly nods his head as he continues to mess around with the tie of his hospital gown. 'Sir, we have some bad news pretaining your wife, Katherine.' the male officer says to Scott. Scott stops what he is doing and looks at the officer, and then looks at the female officer and watches as she tries to hide her face by looking down. 'What? What happened?' Scott asks anxiously. The officer shakes his head slowly. 'Sir, maybe you should sit down?' The male officer suggests. Scott looks at the officer. 'Why?! TELL ME WHAT HAPPENED TO KATHERINE!' Scott demands. The female officer steps up, and puts her hand on the male officers shoulder and shakes her head. The male officer looks at Scott and looks like he is trying to find words. 'Sir, we found her car in a pile up caused by a transfer trunk.' The officer says. Scott looks in shock. 'But we didn't find Katherine, and we looked for hours for her body but it was dark and close to a lake. We feel that she was flung from the vehicle into the cold water and drifted down stream....' The officer says before Scott interrupts. Scott, Now in tears says. 'No...No...this can't be. I just saw her not ten minutes ago! NO!!' Scott screams in horror. The female officer walks over to Scott and places her hand on Scotts should in the same slow manner as she did the male officer. 'We're so sorry for your lose. But we have a team looking to see if she will appear down stream.' The female officer says to Scott. Scott jerks back and looks at them. 'Why?' Scott asks. The two officers didn't know what to say and just stood there uneased. 'Why didn't I just let her stay with me? It's all my fault.' Scott whimpers to himself. The officer shakes his head. 'No sir, the driver of the transfer truck said that her car swerved at him and that was the last he remembers from the impact.' the officer says trying to reassure Scott it wasn't his fault. 'BUT SHE WANTED TO SLEEP HERE!! BESIDE ME!! AND I WOULDN'T LET HER!! WE WERE GOING TO START A FAMILY!!!' Scott screams in a teared frenzy. Scott looks at the officers in tears. 'Please leave....' Scott asks trying to be polite. 'Sir..' the female officer responds. 'I SAID GO!!' Scott screams, and sniffs trying to hold in the frustration. The officers looked at each other, and turned around and appologized to him again before leaving and shutting the door behind them. Scott looks at the them leave and then he grabs the chair that is next to the bed and he throws it across the room and continues to cry as he slides to the floor against the wall. The scene fades away....
