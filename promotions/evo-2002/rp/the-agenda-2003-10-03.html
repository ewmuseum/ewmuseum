The agenda

3 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-03 'Evolution Wrestling have you missed me?' The question rings out as the camera spirals down to a desk which seats not only Malcolm King but Luther Wisdom....Malcolm starts talking as Wisdom sits back with a huge smile on his face.... 'It seems the bastard son of this dysfunctional family known as Evolution Wrestling has returned, yet deep down I know there are those of you who hate the fact that I have found yet another way to circumvent the system.' King pauses as a highlight plays on the bank of monitors behind him of QBX 'winning' the retirement match. 'My motivation is simple.....unfinished business. You see before Evolution management decided that the best way to market itself was the continued supression of talent, somehow Question Beyond X....slipped through the cracks and found himself a champion here in evolution Wrestling. It's actually been a sickening display on who Evo has decided they must push down the fans throat while there are others equally if not more deserving being used as fodder to soothe delicate egos. However QBX, outof the overrated champions here in evolution you are the most overrated there is. I know some of you will question on what authority can I say this. Easy....namely your opinion or input in this matter is irrelevant, and as a founding father of this federation in which you draw your check I can say whatever the hell I want.' Luther starts to chuckle behind him...... 'But back to my former protegee QBX, do you think it a coincidence that when the Cell division needed a legitimate name...they asked me? My sole purpose was to put a familiar face on the division, and simply to give you QBX the rub. You have been and will forever be nothing but a shadow to me. So Evolution got hurt by their own rule when you won the tag team title. The tag titles is open to any one affiliated with Evolution or not. You X.....are a fighter not a wrestler and Evolution bylaws never addressed that eventuality. So the flood gates is open and any joe off the street has license to take your belt away. Don't worry no average everyday joe is gunning for you QBX ....no little one the man who will become your most recent nightmare knows you very well; afterall I made the mistake of training you. I am a multiple time Universal and Universal tag champion and will do more in the next seventy-two hours than you will during the expanse of your career. QBX you have failed the movement......and that failure has come at a very high price.' Luther Wisdom leans forward half smirking....... 'Is there any legitimate reason why I haven't main evented a Legends since number 7? For that matter been taken off the houseshow rotation? Funny how we are given the power to choose who can and can't be over; and who is deemed unsuccessful and yet don't give the opprotunity to others to at least try and fail. There is a whole hypocricy that is political by nature and the result is the stagnate product you see at the top.' King looks at him with curiosity....... 'Don't worry what more can Evolution do to retard my career? You Malcolm can say what you want based on your status here but for someone like me who when I won the Evolved title it was quote unquote a title that's gone to shit. Mind you the brainiac let me hold it a full five minutes before giving that insightful assessment, I realized it's not what you do in this company that matters....it's who you don't embarass. So I'm gonna use whats left of my obvious failed career to settle some personal issues and upstage the lords of evolution in the process. Big Daddy Dreamer......there is an unresolved issue between us that sooner, rather than later....will come to ahead. Tanaka placed a million dollar bounty on your head, a cash sum I intend to collect and use towards the better marketing of me, since evolution has failed or refuse to do so properly. And really Dreamer your career is a small price to pay for my greatness.' he leans over into the camera....... 'Don't let your ego fool you Dreamer.......I'm more dangerous than you can possibly comprehend, for I have absolutely nothing to lose' (fade to black)
