Luther Wisdom Multinational

30 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/30/03 Luther Wisdom stands there...alone in the lockerrom with 1/2 of the universal Tag Team titles draped across his shoulders. His head is down as for the first time in his career he starts to act and talk as if he is truly humbled... 'I had aspired from day one of my journey into evolution Wrestling to become a champion. I was brought in to aid Xavier Citizen....and my focus was to gain, to capture the Evo Tag Team titles. With Xavier gone many thought that goal would be left unfulfilled. Afterall I was without a partner in a war no sane man would want any part of....' Malcolm King emerges into the scene and stands behind Luther Wisdom..... 'But what people forgot...is that I was trained by a man who uses insanity as a weapon, what people forgot was that I was a partner of a man inwhich sanity never visited...what people forgot is that when you are out of your mind all you needed to restore sanity was The Luther Wisdom Experience!' King steps to the side to reveal an OMEN Brotherhood t-shirt..... 'So it was the bastard of choas that captured 1/2 of The tag team titles. But as the old saying goes behind every man there is a good woman.....well HeartBreak Chick....I don't want you behind me, but beside me.' Malcolm looks at Luther..... 'Don't get me wrong normally I would mean that as a sexual advance....but these titles are about business, Luther Wisdom Multinational is about business and quite frankly it makes good business sense to hold onto the gold, and to diversify into new markets. Do I trust you Chick.....not really, do I respect you....yes. What went on between you Malcolm and Dreamer is between you Malcolm and Dreamer. For me this....' points to the title..... '....is strictly business. What you and I have HBC is a partnership....something we initially may not have wanted, yet something that isn't that bad once you think about it. You have a handicapped match and I know you have Dreamer, but realize this if you want it or not I too will be there to protect my investment.....which means keeping you as healthy as you can be so WE can defend these titles.' Malcolm King puts his hand on Wisdom's shoulder as Luther steps back..... MK:'It took me a few days to step back from the situation and analyze what happened on Sunday. My protegee neeeded someone in his corner...and I'm sure he was as surprised as everyone when I stood side by side with him. Imagine my shock when he won the tag team championship and yet I was denied that honor. I don't particularly like Big Daddy Dreamer or for that much care for him in and out of this business....but I find myself agreeing with him, when you Dan S. revealed yourself as The ONE......it became much bigger than any petty fued I may have with him.' Malcolm takes a step forward closer into the camera...... 'You think you have put two combustable elements together and that Dreamer and I will do the job you and your Unlimited haven't been able to do. LOL...don't make me laugh S.; you see through the marriage of Wisdom and Chick you have ignited something that not even you can stop. You've brought upon yourself and The Unlimited the combined focus and hatred of two of the most accomplished and talented stars in this business....myself and Big Daddy Dreamer.' King begins to snicker...... 'Yeah it does pain me to say that Dreamer is talented, but it's the truth. The point where trust and liking him have long since departed.....the realm of mutual respect remains. I respect Dreamer and his ability and if he wants to have a war with The Unlimited.....then he has an ally. The worse thing you could have done S. is make an enemy of me. Unlike Luther my focus is not winning championships for personal gain, it's all for one up manship. Initially to prove myself Xander's superior....but now that you have revealed yourself his puppet master, he he he.....it's about taking this fed and no prisoners. I've done it in OSW, EDW, and many others before hand. It's time for Evo to take the next leap in her evolution....let's hope she is strong enough to survive.'
