Faith Thoughts

15 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-15 Kramer sits in his dark brown recliner; eyes fixated on the wide television screen displaying the most recent Evolution show, the big Faith PPV! He’s watching his match against the recently released Simon Krask. Kramer has just hit a spear and is now spinning Krask round and round with the Ferris Wheel of Destruction. “That move nearly knocked me out. Haven’t done than in over a year….still feeling the damn effects.” Kramer said as he watched Krask give in, and the after effects of the move causing him to stumble a bit. “Then this is where my induction to eVo got good….” He chuckles to himself as The Select come down to ruin his victory, and then the chair shot from Troy Chryst. As he watches the chair connect, he flinches in his seat, remembering the feeling of the cold steel chair smashing against him. “My first match and already I have an enemy.” He pulls the remote up and clicks the stop button and the screen goes blue as he places the remote back down and crosses his arms over his chest. “I may not be the sanest or smartest at times, but I don’t take kindly to cheap shots, especially after a victory as big as winning my debut match on a pay per view. Now I know some people like Troy get their jollies out of cheap attacks and that’s ok, but they have to realize that payback is always around the corner. I got pushed around a lot in the past and I’ve taken it regretfully. The times have changed, the fed is different, and I won’t settle for it anymore. Over this two week hiatus I have some thinking to do. Mainly what I’m going to say to Troy on the first Legends after the break. Whatever it is I promise something will be said whether I have to confront The Select as a whole or not. Once I figure that out I’m not giving the situation anymore thought. It’s not worth it to me to be worrying about this all throughout the break so I’m just going to relax and rest up.” He starts flipping through the channels and the scene slowly fades out.
