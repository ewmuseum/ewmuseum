HBC gots to have Faith

21 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-21-02 Toronto, Canada Late Evening Club Wasteland The air is starting to get crisper, fall is on the horizon and will quickly move into winter, yet something is still dark and colder within the Toronto air. The HeartBreak Chick, shocked at the recent events from New Orleans in which her ex-partner, Big Daddy Dreamer, actually had the audacity to put his hands on her. Her! The Heartbreaker! A big right hand, a boot to the gut and a powerbomb. How dare he! After dam near fracturing his jaw, how dare he. But still stranger things are afoot within Evolution. Must be because Halloween is coming up. We have some newcomer, claiming to know me. Only one man knows me and I’m wrestling him at Faith! But as HBC sits down in the plush VIP room within Club Wasteland, a thought drifts to another, but shakes her head. Couldn’t be, he no longer wrestles anymore. Gave it up and went to college. Even then, he didn’t truly know me either. It must be all a mind game, too bad for Strictly Prohibited it isn’t the fellow in question for he never wrestled in EDW or $W. Kayman perhaps?? We had wars, intriguing. It just might be….. But suddenly a smirk comes across her face when thinking of another. The so-called confidante of Big Daddy Dreamer, Magdalena. She takes a long drink of her Strawberry Margarita thinking of New Orleans and Legends XII. She was all squared away to nail BDD one more time with her Sweet BadAss SuperKick and then that little tart had to interfere, grabbing my leg. I wonder how shocked she was in the back when I finally figured it out. How remains a question but I have my hunches, but funny that even she is keeping it a secret from BDD. That man will never be able to trust another woman before this is all done. How easily he is to control, to manipulate. I really thought he would be harder considering his background, but then again, usually those are the easiest if you find the right button. Like a grand puppetmaster, he has been dancing to my tune. Losing to Michaels like that, not concentrating on the match. Counted out of all things. That has got to be a first for him I think. Oh D, you simpleton, you really don’t have a clue as to what is going on don’t you. You liked to think yourself the master manipulator, always strategizing, always planning several steps ahead, but your biggest flaw was you were always too forward thinking and not noticing the little things right in front of your face. Had you, all this may have been prevented, but alas, despite your Stanford credentials, you are just another piece of my master puzzle, carefully orchestrated some two years in the making. Not that you ever suspected mind you. That’s the great thing about being me, I’m rich, powerful, beautiful, dangerous and most of all, patient. Quite the deadly combination if you think about it D. Your off your game thanks to me. The cold calculating person you once were doesn’t exist anymore. Your impatient, reckless, and now prone to stupid mistakes as evident by your actions the last two weeks. I mean come on D, how in the world are you going too even come close to beating me when frankly, your not all their, mentally or physically. You have a broken eye socket, thanks to me and some fancy chair work. Mentally, all you can think about is revenge, but even then, even then, last week I still noticed it within your blackened and purple eye, the moment of hesitation and doubt. The ever so slight pause before your reaction. You hesitated in hitting me with that right hand of yours. Which is really funny because you know and I know, that is your weaker hand of the two. You threw the right instead of the left. While it may not mean anything to these morons watching at home, but it speaks volumes to us doesn’t it Dreamer. I know you like a book. All your strengths and weaknesses, and though you have few weaknesses, they do exist as you have shown the world in recent weeks. Your aura of invincibility is being shattered every day. No longer are you one of the most feared men in our industry. Now, your just average. So tell me D, how does it feel to have your entire world fall apart around you and if you think that little cheap groupie is going to save you, you better keep dreaming. I know her as well, and believe me D, the secret she holds it’s a doozy, and though I shouldn’t be telling you this, her little secret D, will break you worse than my departure from you. Worse than that kick I gave you, worse than stabbing you in the back and joining the Unlimited. But you don’t have to take my word for it, why don’t you ask her yourself, if your man enough. You’ll probably view this attempt as another plan to get under your skin, but D, I don’t have to do that, I already have. This, well……… perhaps I may have a feeling for you still. But make no mistake Dreamer, at Faith, you will go down, you will be beaten, and you will be heartbroken, not once…….but twice! Think about that one D, think about that one…..Nine days, and your mine…..
