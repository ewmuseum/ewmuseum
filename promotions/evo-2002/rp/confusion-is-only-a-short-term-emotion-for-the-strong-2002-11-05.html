Confusion Is Only A Short Term Emotion For The Strong...

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/11/02 Having dissapeared out of sight earlier, we find Sean Mason standing in the parking lot of the motel which we had spotted him in earlier. Still sporting the same clothing as before he stands sifting through his trunk when suddenly he looks up with a look of satisfaction for all too see. In his right hand he clutches an object which he holds wrapped in a black sheet and thus covered from sight. Tucking the object under his arm he then closes the trunk, before then locking his car and dissapearing back into the motel and back into the much warmer surrounds of his booked room for the next few nights. Once inside his room he throws himself into the armchair closest to the door, before then placing the object firmly on his lap before him and then beggining to un-ravel and reveal the mystery that sits hidden from sight. As the sheet falls to the floor, I would certainly not blame you for being curious of the next sight. Sitting delicately on the black sheet is a title belt of much architecture and glory. Its near perfect condition suggests this is not something that has been taken lightly and that it is something that has been cared for over time and as Sean moves his arm slightly toward him the letters F...A..W can be seen. Seans eyes by this time are now totally fixed on the belt. Rubbing his hand gently across the gold he looks down with both pride and a certain degree of happiness, before he looks up again. Now the identity of the object becomes obvious. As the light hits the crest which sits as a central focus on the front, the viewer can now clearly see the words 'FAW International Champion' engraved in all their glory. Suddenly Sean can be heard muttering, the words are not clear but it would seem the topic of thought is purely by his facial expression. Having enjoyed success for so long in FAW we had always known failure was a bitter pill for Sean to have been swallowing of late. Sitting before him was a set of memories littered with glorious victories. Back then Sean was the man. The person everyone aspired to be, the question Sean was now asking himself was, what had changed?!? Why should Sean not be the trendsetter?! What was stopping him from bettering those memories with new feits from Evo?! The answer.... NOTHING... Placing his title belt to one side Sean leaned forward. His eyes by this point were now filled with emotion and his head filled with thoughts of excitement. Legends was to be his night. The barrier had now been broken and Sean had to realise that not even Jason Jameson could prove worthy of preventing the past becoming the future. Is Jasons mind on the match?! Who knows! And quite frankly Sean couldn't give a damn. Whether Sean was faced by Jason, Jay Jay or some bitter female ghost from Jamesons past; there was nothing that Sean deemed worthy enough of restraining him as he set on the path towards the top. With the adrenaline now rushing through his veins his mind now began to drift into forward thinking thoughts of Legends. This wasn't anything personal! Sean could see no reason why he should break Jason Jameson in half, but then again there didnt need to be a personal motive. By having the stakes so high, no amount of respect was going to cause Seans mind to waver from the obvious goal. It was time the killer instinct of old showed its ugly face once more. This was all about business and in business the reality is that you need to be ruthless. Still looking at the title beside him he recalled nights of carnage during which he had shown no remorse; Legends was to be no different. Everything was now so clear. It seems weird to think a title could focus a man as much as it was doing, but then again few can imagine just what this piece of metal means to a man until you have experienced it. Harnassing both the ambition and desire which he felt deep from within Sean looked upward and realised it truely was now or never. Wednesday night required Sean to raise his game to levels not yet seen during his time in Evo and if Sean could lock on the sharpshooter for the third time in a row then his dreams would indeed be realised; and once more Jason would close his eyes, only this time he would awake not to see the face of Jay Jay, but instead to taste that bitter pill that is defeat... Scene fades to black...
