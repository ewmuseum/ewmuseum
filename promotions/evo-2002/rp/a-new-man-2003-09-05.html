A new man?

5 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-05 ~His head pounded…he celebrated his long awaited victory over Cash…but he celebrated a little bit to hard. Shocka’s face was pale as he moved to the bathroom he unloaded a few carbs the fun WAY! One more time, but now the cache was empty…he took a sip of water…add to what needs to be flushed. Alcohol poisoning is never fun but with a hell of a hang over it’s a killer.~ ‘This is how I spend my fucking nights…’ Shocka shook his head. ‘This and beating people up to make money…what a fucking life…’ Shocka grinned before his throat tensed up and he made another deposit. ~His head began to pound and he slouched to the side something had definitely changed in him. He now had regained his sense after all, well the best way a wrestler knows how. A night of drinking turned into a morning and now mid afternoon when he wakes again the effects are brutal. Voss thought it would be funny to see who could drink more...Shocka drank him under the table but he paid the price. Beyond the drinking though Voss has no idea what Shocka knows...~ ~Fast Forward to Wednesday night Shocka hears about his match from Vito Voss his good friend. ANd now he sits in his hotel room staring straight out the window speaking on a cell phone to his girlfriend Jennifer.~ ‘I tell you what…I’m onto that stupid son of a bitch.’ Shocka spoke into a cell phone now listening. ‘So are you going to come over tonight?’ Shocka listenend his face grew to a smile. ‘You’re coming?’ He nodded. ‘Do what you can? Alright see you tonight Jenn.’ Shocka hung up the phone and the camera pulled back. ‘You know what?’ Shocka’s mood seemed upbeat…something was obviously wrong. ‘I haven’t felt this good in years…I don’t know what happened but I’m in a good mood today…I guess I got a lot of sleep or something…’ Shocka’s voice faded as he saw Voss walking toward him. ‘Shocks…’ Voss started slowly trying to feel the vibe from Shocka today. ‘Why the fuck won’t you leave?’ Shocka asked trying to play the nice guy. ‘Well I just heard something on the scanner…’ Voss was stopped. ‘No you didn’t…you can’t fool me anymore you son of a bitch.’ Shocka smiled moving close to Voss. Voss didn’t back paddle any though he held his ground to Shocka’s surprise. ‘You think you can punk me out?’ Voss asked with a cocky grin on his face. ‘Get out.’ Shocka said with a sinister smile. ‘Or what? You gonna make me or somethin’ Voss asked since he’s Italian and all. ‘I’m Sicilian numb nuts.’ Voss said. ‘Go cook me some fucking pasta…you can take it to America for me.’ Shocka just starred down Voss. ‘You know what…fuck you…’ Voss turned and walked about half way to his room…he stopped and turned it seemed he had something left to say. ‘But they are coming for you…’ Voss smiled and walked out. ~Had his words got to Shocka? Or will he fight through it since he’s big and tough?~ ~Shocka just shook his head at Vito he wasn’t buying a word of it…he turned back to the camera and spoke.~ ‘You know what I got for that son of a bitch?’ Shocka asked not waiting for a response. ‘You’ll have to wait till Legends for that… Oh, and since the show conveniently popped up I have a few things to say to Jay Jay Jameson…I’m on a roll as of late ya know…I ended Hedges, Carl Cash and now it seems that you are placed in my path. I’m surprised that my point hasn’t been taken yet…but this week I think I’ve got a chance to hit a home run right into the lap of the committee. Because this time when I go into the ring and end a career, at least it’s a fellow main eventer.’ Shocka’s smiled showed he was far from joking. ‘And you know what Jason, it’s not really anything personal…well except the fact that I’ve been hearing about you since EdW…watch him he’ll he a main eventer someday…that’s what they’d say. But at the time they were saying that I was the one in the main event…you took the focus off of me! You better be at the best you can be when you go into the ring with me this week…because if you are a tick off I will see it, and I will capitalize. I will prove that my veteran status here means something…’ Shocka finished the sentence as a knock came at the door. ‘This is the police open the door.’ A voice called from the other side.
