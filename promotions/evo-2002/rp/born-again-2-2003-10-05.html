Born Again 2

5 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-05 Death is something I've never really figured out. Evidently, when we die we go to a better place. Presumably, that place is what the world calls 'Heaven'. We put in a great deal of effort to avoid death. We go to extraordinary extents to delay it and often consider it's intrusion to be a tragic event. So, when we die, if we go to a better place, why do we try to delay death so much? When people die and they're sent to live in a wonderful place, why do we mourn? If there is a Heaven and their is a God and their is a place in the sky that's better than this rock we're living on, why are we scared of death? I'm not scared of death. I've learned that the end is all that's ever true. Why people mourn over death is something I've never really figured out... --- I remember it all as if it were yesterday. I sat inside my car on the way home from Judith's house. It was a day or so after X-Rated... I had broken up with Judith and I knew that right then she was laying in her bed, wrapped in sheets, crying her eyes out. That morning, rehearsing all the things I'd tell her, it seemed easy. When it actually came to standing in front of her and telling her it was over, I found that it was hard not to speak in vowels. I sighed and closed my eyes. Close your eyes for a split second and anything can happen. I opened my eyes and continued to watch the highway. Thoughts of suicide were running through my head. Not once did I think of my job in eVo. At this point, I didn't care. Right then, I simply needed an escape from reality and death seemed like a nice way out. Death was something I never really understood. I sighed and closed my eyes. Close your eyes for a split second and your whole life can take a U-Turn. I opened my eyes once again and looked down my cigarette to see the end half-burn. The ashes fell down all at once to land on my pant forearm. There was that slight bit of pain... but I didn't mind. Pain was another way of escaping reality for me. Some call that 'masochism'. I call it a diversion. I sighed and closed my eyes. Close your eyes for a split second and you could find yourself on a downward spiral. A loud sound echoed on the hood of my car. I made a mistake this time. I opened my eyes and there in the middle of the road laid a body. There's a difference between seeing a dead body in a coffin and seeing a dead body laying in the center of a road. You might hear yourself asking where did the person come from? Right then, it was around 1:00AM and there were no cars for miles. No houses. No nothing. I found myself asking where did the person come from. Close your eyes for a split second and you could open your eyes to find that you killed someone. In school, they teach you a lot of important things evidently. Reading, writing, and arithmetic. Yet, they never teach us how to deal with death. They never teach us what to do if you just accidentally hit someone with your car. They never teach you whether you should simply call the police and report it or simply drive around the body and go on with your life. They never teach you if you should get out of the car or not. They never teach you how to react or how to feel. How did I react? Well, I smiled. There was this odd feeling of power. I suddenly realized why serial killers killed. I suddenly realized why murderers murdered. There was a sense of power in it all. It was as if taking life gave life to you. I kept telling myself that this was a good deed. The person would have died in a Nursing Home of some sort of painful disease had I not put the person out of his or her misery. I kept telling myself I did nothing wrong. I took a deep breath... and drove past the body. Leaving it to be found later. With his or her death, I felt born again. ...it was going to be a long drive home.
