Awakening

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Twenty-One years ago a beast was born. Apon it’s birth, it’s parents and the beast itself did not know it’s potential and what it would eventually grow up to be. Throughout it’s life it never really realised what it was and for that reason it took a while to discover what it’s potential was. But even when it knew, it refused to try and become all that it could, fearing of what might happen to itself and those around it. The human side had slightly more control but for years the malicious side and the slightly more human side battled against one another, until one day something happened to sway the balance of power. An event that left the human side crippled and allowed the beast to become completely dominant. The death of a loved one is something that is hard for anyone to cope with, even for someone with a dead heart. The already very unstable persona of Asis was once again tested as to how much it could handle and for a while it prevailed. But it could not last forever and after seeing his Mother’s killers imprisoned Asis finally lost all control. The thought of the people that killed his Mother, the one person he truly ever loved, being locked away in prison away from him, was too much to bear. The fact that he would never be able to hurt them, make them pay, was something that took away all sense of humanity from him. It turned him into something even more destructive than previously seen; it awakened a side of him that had been suppressed and dormant for twenty-one years. And now that it is awake, it wishes to make itself heard… (The night sky is covered in cloud, only patches of sky can be seen, the stars glinting through. Asis looks up at the sky, lying on his back on a hilltop. Quite a tranquil thing for a man like him to be doing, considering the fact that two weeks previously he brutally attacked his “friend” leaving her in hospital with multiple injuries, including neck trauma from crushing her head between two steal chairs with the weight of his own body. But at the moment “The Demonic” Asis seems quite peaceful. He sits up and stares down the hill, where a prison can be seen emerging from the fog that surrounds the area. There could be any number of reasons for Asis to be near a prison, but one major one that happened two weeks ago is the reason for his appearance.) Down there, in that prison, are two people who murdered the one person in my life who I have ever loved. The one person I have ever had feelings for that weren’t hatred and malice. The only person who ever treated me like a human being, the one person who always smiled at me and made me feel normal, despite my appearance and no matter how I was acting. That person was taken away from me, by those two people who are now locked away from me, locked away from the pain and hurt I want to inflict upon them. A man that my Mother left me and my father for, was the source of her death. I suppose that’s ironic. But irony can sometimes be a good thing, although Orcs will not find that out just yet… (Asis stands up looking down at fortress below. A fortress that is meant to keep people from escaping, but he wonders whether it is as good at keeping people out of it. It’s strange he thinks, that he is so captivated by a place that most people would say that he belongs. Locked away in the proverbial cage, like the beast he is. Always makes him smile, not on the surface but on the inside, when he hears people talking about him that way, makes him feel in his way normal. At least that’s how a part of him felt, the part of him that has now taken complete control. It is not a multiple personality thing, he is one and the same person, it’s just now he is all that he can be.) Something happened to me. Something that changed my life forever. The thing I was and the thing I now am, are two very different entities. I may not have changed straight away but I thought I was doing everyone a favour, holding it in, not allowing myself to cut lose, become what I knew I could. And for a short while I managed it, but as Seara knows and now everyone else does, I had to let it out. There was no particular reason for me taking it all out on Seara, she was just the person that I chose. She tried to become too close to me, she suffered the consequences. She should have known that I am not stable, that I am not the kind of person who can hold down relationships, even just friendships. Anything close to me gets destroyed, by choice or not, Seara knew that and she ignored it. She has no one to blame but herself. As for my match last week, it was a relief. To finally be free of the belt, it does feel better. To not carry the belt around and be seen as a “champion”. It was something I despised, being seen as one of the more famous wrestlers in eVo. Boone can walk around and call himself a champion for all I care, at least he wants to be a champion. (Asis the whole time has been staring down at the prison, even when he was talking about matters in eVo, he has been staring intently down at the prison. Suddenly he snaps out of it and turns away, facing away from the prison.) The Masked Attacker. (Laugh). I can’t believe that Danny boy has had the nerve to say that I requested the match. I did no such thing, I couldn’t have cared less that this Masked Attacker cost me a match and my title, as far as I’m concerned I was done a favour. And Eric Badger, I have no quarrel with him, I’ve never even met him, he was in eVo for a few weeks and then he disappeared and has only just returned. But just because I do not care about these two individuals, does not mean they are going to escape lightly. It applies to anyone within eVo, just because I may not have a problem with you, does not mean you will not suffer. Because trust me everybody in eVo will suffer at my hands at one point or another. So for those of you I’m not facing this week, sit back and watch and pray that I’m in a better mood when I face you. I promise that one if not both my opponents will have to be carried out of the arena, they will not walk. But strangely enough, I would like to know who the Masked Attacker is…
