The Unmasked Truth

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-22-03 'nVc is sitting on the train waiting for it to come to his hotel for the ppv tomorrow, he hasn't said a word since his unmasking, it wasn't really a party or anything when he took the mask off. The unmasking truth came out when Nik VanCore was and still is Strictly Prohibited. Everyone hates that name Nik VanCore, and everyone doesn't understand him at all but what can nVc do about that? nVc leans on the pole while his hands hang onto the stop puller. nVc seems down about all this, but he look up at the camera with this look of anger.' Nik VanCore: Well Unlimited, you got what you wanted. You now know who I am, I am Nik VanCore! I maybe the most hated person in the back, and in this universe but I exspect that. Why do you think I dawned a mask in the first place? Was I suprised HBC, BDD and Tanaka came out? Yes I was, because HBC and I had one hell of a fued back in the days of EdW for that Suicidal championship that I beat her for in Hell's fury match, and some other high impacted matches. BDD and I never had gotten along either, so was I suprised with the help last week? Yes, yes I was. Nik VanCore: Unlimited you may have unmasked me, but this means now that I am unmasked, that my true skills may come out as well, when you step in the ring with me this time, I will be a little different in pace. I will destroy this group, and now Unlimited you have gotten more then you wanted, you may of wanted Tyler Davidson, but you have gotten something a tad more harder to deal with....you have gotten The break-age Superstar, the Sensation Of Innovation! You have gotten Nik VanCore, and that's all you need to remember! 'nVc looks back out the window, then looks back at the camera.' Nik VanCore: I am back, and there is nothing else I can do about it. My enimies know I am back, and now that they know. (pauses) I could very well be a deadman walking and I very well be dead tomorrow, or the day after. My pride is gone from this sport, and I had to act like someone else so nobody would know who I was, but now that you know who I am going to beat the unlimited with Tanaka and win those tag team titles at this ppv! I am gonna do something nobody ever thought I could do and win a world title, one day I will get my shot at Shocka, or if Scott Frost wins tomorrow...maybe I'll get a shot at him, but you know I could really go for an Unlimited ass kicking after what they did to me at legends... Nik VanCore: I'll let you come up Unlimited and take your best shot, but if that's all you had for me at Legends then your sadley going to learn to respect me! Unlimited vs nVc is what it is huh? you have awoken a sleepy giant and I will make sure you pay for what you did, now Unlimited if you want to beat on people then by all means go ahead, but I'm just telling you here and now that when you do that....I will be there with a kendo stick in hand whacking your brains out and watching your blood cover the walls, ring, cars, anything! Watch your backs Unlimited, just because I lost the mask doesn't mean I lost my stalking way... For one last time Endeavor my Curse, Endeavor my pain, Endeavor my LIFE!
