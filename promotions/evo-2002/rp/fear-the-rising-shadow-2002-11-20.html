Fear The Rising Shadow!

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/20 After entering his hotel room, Mark Steele begins talking to himself to a camera about how he feels about his brother and his upcoming match against Ben Juventud and Jay Stylez Junior. 'The wrestling business is about to see something they never expected. Meet someone they thought would never make an appearence that would be thier shadow. These wrestling superstars will learn to fear thier shadows and I step foot out of them all. The Rising Shadow has fallen once, but a shadow never dies. Its always there walking beside you. Your eyes will never forget the first time you saw your shadow. As a child you would try and run away from it but it was always beside you or behind you. You may think a shadow is a reflection of light off of your body but the truth remains the shadow is a reflection of what you shall become and what you have accomplished. Something you left behind in a career of some sort. Lets take my brother for example. He left Canada at the top of the rankings. When he came here he was nothing, a simple good for nothing nobody. We now see him along the side of Shocka going after the tag team titles. Thinking of himself as usual and not reminding himself that his little brother is in New-TV as well and is his official tag team partner. Could he wait for me to come to the federation before going after tag team gold... NO, He Couldn't! The time has come that everyone understands the pressure I am getting from my friends back home. They all think that since I lost my first match in eVo that my brother is better than me in everything. I got news coming to them. My brother has lost two singles matches and has only won one damn tag match. And that qualifies him as better than me' What kind of peopld are in this world? What kind of standards do they have set? I have always been better than that pathetic freak. My brother doesn't care about me. All he cares about is himself, when do I get a shot at some gold. When do I get to join a tournement. When lord when is my pathetic older brother going to forget about himself and help his little brother? This man is so selfish not even himself could solve his problems,' says Mark Steele as he hears a knock on the door. 'Who is it?' Mark asks. 'Its Jason, and what the hell are you doing in there? It sounds like your talking to yourself,' Jason Steele asks. 'Nothing, just cutting a promo. Think you could give me about ten or fifteen more minutes here bro? Go talk to Shocka or something. I will meet you down at the bar later on' Mark says before he opens the door. 'Sure, I can give you about fifteen minutes,' Jason replies as he walks away. 'Thanks bro.' Mark says as he watches his brother walk away from his room 'Now, back to business. Lets get to Rise of The Legends. This coming Sunday I am facing two men in a triple threat match. I am looking forward to this because it will be my pay per view debut. The Shadow will finally rise from its fallen state, the superstars around here are no longer safe, the time has begun that we all realize that the world is not safe when your shadow is following you in the dark when you pass the bright street lights. The future is here and you will not be alone. When your sleeping you will know someone is there. Ben and Jay, your time has come and your future will no longer be a highligh of your past. You will learn to fear the ones you love and the ones you hate. You may think your safe but while your near me nothing and nobody in your life is safe. The Rising Shadow has not fallen yet, your life will become the hell you wish you would never visit. With my technics you will feel my wrath and understand my pain the heart break I have taken and the punishment I have recieved. The student of all will be the teacher of many. I have the power and I have the strength that will haunt your greatest dreams.Rise of The Legends will be known for the Rising Shadow. I will make my neverending mark and your lives will never be the same again,' Mark speaks before turning off the camera and leaving the room to go get Jason.
