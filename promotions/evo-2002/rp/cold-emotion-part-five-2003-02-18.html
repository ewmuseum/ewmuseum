Cold Emotion... part five.

18 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in the confided space of darkness. The shadowed over figure of Morpheus Omega can be seen sitting with his back against the wall with his elbows on his knees; one hand holding his head up while tilted sideways and the other he repeatedly opens and closes, with it cracking in and out of place. The cold look showing on the face of Morpheus with no emotion and not flinching the slightest. The only movement from the sadistic, Infinite of evil is his shallow breathing which can barely be noticed and his nervous clenching of his hand. The twisted thoughts and emotion of this crazed monster are unknown. Though with the deceitful lies, tricks and mind games from his long time partner Shocka and his tag team partner and significant other Sky Night are probably slowly eating and tearing away at him. Like a freight train of bad thoughts colliding in his mind; not knowing what to make sense of anymore and what is fake or reality. The man like a ticking time bomb, ready to self detonate into the mass destruction of those innocent or guilty around him. The silence builds heavy suspense. Out of nowhere and unexpected, Morpheus snaps his face forwards and straight on view with a snarling look of rage. The breathing erratically becomes heavier with the change of mood and attitude. Morpheus cracks his neck side to side and raises his arms into a fit of rage and screams loudly at the top of his lungs into a sick war cry. Lowering his arms, Morpheus grinds his teeth trying to keep the sanity he never once had. The left eye starts to twitch uncontrollably. 'They thought they could pull a fast one over the deadliest in the game, like I have never been there or done that before? They think that they can out smart their teacher, protector and guardian who has master minded the sickest of operations? Shocka, the one who cannot even tie his own shoe without assistance or an instruction manual!' Morpheus shakes his head to the side and instead of the following chuckle, he breathes deeply and sighs in his own misery. 'The question being, what do they have to prove sneaking around behind my back?' he questions to himself. 'Where do they think they can take themselves without the only warrior who has ever gotten their ungrateful asses anywhere in this business?' he questions again. 'What the hell are they trying to accomplish?' he screams out loud and starts breaking out into cold sweats. The eye twitch continues to get worst, his body starts to randomly twitch and casually shake like he is on the fine line of mentally breaking down and sliding back over the edge. Raising the one side of his lip into a sick snarl. Morpheus presents his clenched teeth like he was a wolf showing his fangs before fighting another from his pack, over who got to eat from the slaughtered and viciously killed deer. All signs of human nature and sane thoughts have disappeared and clouded over due to his immense rage; like all thoughts and actions are now pure animal instinct. Questionable, deranged thoughts race across his mind leaving him angry, bitter and confused. 'With Shocka, I stated before that history always repeats itself but if one thing I know about that man is he always has some immature, stupid motive towards everything he does. The man only thinks of himself, but I would like to know how Sky has become mixed up in this! Shocka and Sky may think they are good and like they have all the cards up their sleeves but they are missing every joker, king and ace in that deck. Why retaliate with war? When I can defeat the two of them in their own games. Dare 2 fuck with me! Hah ah!' Breathing heavily with an insane, evil look on his face. 'When it comes down to the end, you hurt the ones you love. Sky had maybe touched an inner side to me never released to anyone and maybe added to make the situation worst trying to hurt me. I don't feel pain, guilt, remorse or even sorry for my actions. I grow colder with every action taken towards me from friends and anyone close in my life. Sky may feel she can toy with emotions like a I am damned puppet on a string. Sorry to say, what comes around does go around! The playing field will change and when I hurt the one I love. It will be the kind of pain she will never forget or ever recover from. Shocka and Sky have started the premeditation to their own funerals and only keep digging their death beds further into the ground. I will continue to hold back my anger and play their games, but I can only contain myself for so long!' he shakes his head lowly in shame, followed with another big sigh. Morpheus looks to be reasoning with himself, like he still does not want to believe what he saw before his very own eyes. Morpheus closes his eyes and lets his face fall and rest into his hands. The scene fades.
