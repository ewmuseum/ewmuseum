One question..

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-23 The scene fades into a empty room. There is nothing inside a room except the man who we know as 'X' The Black Label. He is in the middle of the room at a table were he is writting notes about things that are going through his head. Something pops in his head. The thought 'What does it take'..He then begins to think about the cell tag match..Where him and his mentor will team up to go against Ali and the lucky irish man,Damon...He then mumbles in a soft voice... 'What does it take..' He looks down on the sheet of paper as he quotes something he has wrote earlier today... 'A man who climbs to the top...Is a true champion..' He then looks at the cameras in a puzzled way... 'Why did I write this?' As he looks no long at the sheet of paper but directly into the camera. He does not smile or anything as his face is motionless. All he is doing is thinking about what he just said..As he finaly replies to his own question.. 'Its the journey I am forced to take. Its the ladder that the corperate world feels need for a man of my birdon on them..to climb. Why though?,why should it be The Black Label who is forced to climb instead of average corperate america who is given it...Any why can not anyone in eVo see my views except King..Please, Tell me why you people live in a reality that is all controled by one..by one who does judge the color of your skin..by one who can make it difficult for those like X..but one who will be defeated..but one who will be forced to take heed in my threats..To the one..who will be taken over..by the one who is souly against it..by the one who will re write what you pepole have seen in our history books..By the one who will climb the ladder...By the one who will be crowned a true champion..by the mark of X...We should all take heed in the threat of...THE BLACK LABEL..' He picks up the sheet of paper as he reads off yet another quote he has written down.. 'The Black is slowly covering the cell...' He begins to chuckle as he thinks about it for a second as he then replies... 'Just the like the night slowly takes over the bright sun. Just like the night moves over and conqueres the skys..Just like the night, as it slowly passes leaving only one trail over you..leaving only one thing in your sight..the sight of darkness..The sight of the shadow..The sight of what most can not understand. As the movemant has been in place for over some time now, people have asked all around this nation..Why is it now so strong?What did King do to make it so powerful? That my foes, that answer is right here..He called the mark X..He asked The Black Label to do waht the movemant has been stoped from doing..To spread the word while I take over this Cell Divison..And now, my mentor stands as a Team mate for me..My mentor stands as a question when it comes to the cell..Will he get in? Will he be succesful...But I remind this coperate world we live in that the question concerning King should not be the main thought. As there is a bigger question you all should refraim your attention too. There is a question that no one has yet to be answered..There is a question that is beging to ponder the minds of all of eVo..The same questiong that haunts the night fall..As it moves slowly but surely over the land..not stoping...The question that you ask is 'How'..how can something take over something so powerful as the sun..and leave nothing but darkness...Yes,..that same question is the one that should be in the front...The same one you should be trying to answer...But what I just told you my foes,that is some what of a hint to the question I speak of. The answer...the answer will come in time..As my reasoning will be explain...but for right now..Ill just leave with the question...' He looks dead at the cameras as he says the question he was talking about... 'What is..the question beyond X?'.... He smiles in a sadistic way as the scene fades to black with the image of his smile being the last thing we see as the cameras then cutt to a commercial for the PPV.
