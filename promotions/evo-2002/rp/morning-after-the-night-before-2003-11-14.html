Morning After The Night Before

14 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-14 Monday November 10th 2003 Tokyo, Japan 8:33am The scene fades in to a room and immediately, you recognise it as a hotel room. To the left, is a large window that displays a fantastic view of downtown Tokyo as the early morning sun starts to rise from beyond a collection of skyscrapers and other buildings. The blinds have been pulled back rather half-assed, as they hang mid-way across the window. In front of us, three framed Japanese modernised pictures hang in a diagonal pattern from left to right. They display traditional pieces of Japanese art – from a young girl in a Geisha to some Japanese calligraphy and so on. The wall itself is a creamy colour, with a white boarding which leads all around the room as well as on the skirting boards as well. Below the three framed pictures, and straight ahead of us, there is a large double bed with a small bedside table situated on either side. On the tables is a lamp with a triangle shaped lampshade and on the left hand table is a telephone with a small book beside it, which could possibly be a list of phone numbers or a menu for room service. There’s also a silver pot of either tea or coffee, as well as a small white china cup and saucer and a small bowl each for the sachets of milk and sugar. On the bed itself is Matt Hunt. It appears as if he’s been awake for a short while, as he’s laying covers and reading a newspaper. He sits upright, leaning back on to the headrest as the paper rests on his thighs, which are slightly elevated. Hunt is wearing a plain white t-shirt, although it’s hard to see what else he is wearing as the bedspread covers him from the waist down. A white bandage surrounds Hunt’s head as he relaxes and he turns over the last page of the newspaper. Quickly, he scans the back page before folding it up in half and tossing it aimlessly across the bed before glancing up at camera with a motionless expression while slowly folding his arms. He simply stares ahead as the shot pans in before opening his mouth to speak in a soft tone… “The morning after the night before…” he begins as a more relaxed expression appears “Despite coming out of Faith with a title, I have to say that I feel bloody awful. Think of your worst hangover and multiply it by ten. You think I’m going over the top? Well how about I smash your cranium with a chair over and over and over again and tell me that I am going over the top. Usually when I have a hangover, either a nice fry-up or the old “hair of the dog” theory would help the thumping go away…if only for a short time. But if you think I’m letting somebody bash me again then you’ve got another bloody thing coming” He pauses as he groans, reaching for his head and slightly adjusting the bandage. “But you’ve got to take the pain, it’s what this business is all about. You wanna perform inside that ring then you’ve gotta take anything and everything that comes at you. Dealing with injuries such as this just goes with the territory. I can’t say that Justice is feeling much better though as I must have given him a fair few shots to his back…I bet the man can hardly move. But the fact remains; he’d tell you exactly what I’m telling you. You take the pain. Course, he’ll be out for a while…the back is a danger zone for wrestlers and must be nurtured back to full strength. But at the end of the day, I really couldn’t care less about the bloke.” He glances out to the window before continuing “I knew what I was getting into. I knew that there was a fair chance that I would be staggering up the aisleway. You need to take risks in order to achieve your ambitions and goals, some are calculated risks and some are just acted upon impulse. I guess last night was a mixture of both…calculated in terms of sending my opponent in there, but also impulsive as my emotions were running high” Hunt reaches over to his bedside table and grabs the small teacup, taking a slight sip of the contents before replacing it back on the matching saucer. He reaches for his head again before continuing to speak “So upon reflection of last night, I guess it was a case of ‘mission completed’. I set out a task to systematically take part The Select man by man before the big finale and taking the eVolved title in the process. Rimian and Tok’n were struck off the list, as was Justice after last night. QBX managed to slip past…but there’s always time, there always is. Some might say that I should get over it, I’ve seen off the majority of The Select, including Aaron Justice…No. I’ll officially complete the task and finish QBX off, so to speak. But as I said, all in good time…I have got the eVolved title and defending that title is all that matters from this point on.” Hunt adjusts his position so his back is right up against the headrest before talking again “Now, I’m not the type of person to come out and make this speech about how I’m going to defend this title against any challenger, that I will prove myself to be a worthy champion as I take my opponents apart etc, etc, etc. Nah, far too cliché for me. Instead, I’m going to about my business until a challenger does eventually cross my path. I guess you could call that an open challenge…but each to their own I guess. Do you want me to holler “this goes out to all the boys in the back…I know you’re all listening!” Heh, I don’t think so…this isn’t Stamford Connecticut and this isn’t the 1990’s.” The camera pans back as he brushes the covers off his lower body and slowly steps out of bed, one leg after the after. He walks gingerly towards the camera, across the velvet carpet, and eventually halts at a small chair, which has a medium sized black holdall placed on it. He carefully unzips horizontally and digs into the bag, eventually pulling out the bright and shiny eVolved championship title belt. The Postman holds the belt up and a self-satisfied smirk appears on his face before bringing it back down and backing up slowly against the bed. He sits back on the edge of the bed as he places the title belt across his lap before reaching for his head as he starts to talk. “This thing right here signifies that I am at the top of this particular mountain, which is the eVolved division. The US and Universal titles are a long way down the road for me, but at this moment I am the “king of the castle” so to speak. Heh, don’t worry…I won’t let this title get the better of my ego because I’m not that kind of person. If anything, I don’t need any title to tell me that I am good enough to be a titleholder…but seeing as I am, then I guess that kind highlights it in bold then…doesn’t it?” He adjusts his position on the bed as he concludes… “I’m aware that this title automatically places a bullseye on my back, or head or wherever, and I expected that. Any champion expects to be in the snipers firing line. I guess to those who feel that they can cross my path and give me a run for my money…well, let the games begin.” Hunt stares up at the camera while he clutches the eVolved title belt and within moments, the screen slowly fades to black.
