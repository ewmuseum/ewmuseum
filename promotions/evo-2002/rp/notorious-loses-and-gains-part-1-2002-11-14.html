Notorious loses and gains; part 1

14 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 14, 2002 'Scottie....I.....I' The stammering voice of Kelsey Frost can be heard. The scene fades in, and Scott Frost is looking down at the ground and he is looks to be very depressed. And then Kelsey walks over to him, and tries to hug him and he pushes her away and quickly looks at his wife. 'I'm SORRY!' Kelsey screams in Frosts' face. Frost just stares at his wife. 'I CAN'T DO IT!!' Kelsey continues. 'I CAN'T LOVE SOMEONE WHO THINKS HE HAS FEELINGS FOR ANOTHER PERSON! FOR HIS FRIEND!!!' Kelsey screams. Frost looks at her. 'You have nothing to say?!' Kelsey demands. Frost looks at her. 'FINE! I already got the divorce papers. I don't want any of your money, I don't want you to remember me. I just what this relationship over!' Kelsey finishes. Frost does nothing but look at her, and she falls to her knees crying. 'Why can't you just say you don't like her, like that?' that stammering and upset voice of Kelsey muffles throughout the atmosphere. Frost reaches out and grabs Kelsey's hand. 'Don't touch me! I can't do this, I can't. I can't do this...' Kelsey continues to cry. Frost finally opens his mouth. 'Kelsey. Nothing is going on between me and her, I worry about her. Asis wants us both dead, and you know that! I worry! But I ain't going to stop you from divorcing me. Because you just said it yourself. You can't do this. And I told you, the first time you said you couldn't do this that me and you were over because I didn't want you to be put through this because of possible friends, and possible enemies. I'm signing those divorce papers. But I am leaving you money in your bank account. You are not going back to that hell hole of an apartment in Detroit.' Frost tells Kelsey. Kelsey shakes her head almost like he understands Frost. 'I know. I Love You, Scottie.' Kelsey looks at Frost and wipes her eyes. Frost looks at her and nods and looks around. 'Where are these papers?' Frost says. Frost looks around and wraps his arms around Kelsey as she hands her the divorce papers. Frost looks at her one last time, and kisses her forehead and signs the papers. 'I'll sleep on the couch tonight. I guess this is goodbye.' Frost has a very half hearted smirk on his face as if he knew he gave away his dog. Kelsey kisses Frosts forehead and then walks away crying again. Frost breaths out heavily, and then suddenly his cell phone rings. 'Hello?' Frost asks. Frost smiles. 'Katherine....are you doing okay?' (To Be Continued...)
