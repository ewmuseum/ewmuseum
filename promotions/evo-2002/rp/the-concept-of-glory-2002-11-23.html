The Concept of Glory

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd November 2002 Wednesday was a day that Asis will never ever forget, not because of what happened on Legends, but because of what happened previous to it. His birthday, the time when you’re supposed to celebrate was ruined by a culmination of things. Namely his parents. One dead, the other as good as, in his eyes at least. The visit to his Mother’s grave for the first time since her passing was a traumatising event for Asis, almost as traumatising as her death itself. And meeting a Father whom he had not had contact with for four years. The meeting with his Father was also something that left Asis scarred as his Father had some things to say about Asis’ Mother. But despite what Asis’ Father said about his Mother, Asis did not raise a hand to his Father at all. Instead he went outside and took it out on his surroundings. He then went to Legends that night and beat Xavier Citizen, advancing in the Road To Glory Tournament. The tournament itself was not important to Asis, at one point beating the living hell out of Xavier Citizen was, but by the end of the night Asis just wanted to destroy someone. Now he will potentially be wrestling in five matches at this Sunday’s Rise of the Legends pay per view. But despite the fact that Asis could win the Tag Team Titles this week and earn a shot at the title of his choice, the thoughts of what he went through with his parents last week must still be in his mind… Glory. (Laughter) The sheer concept of it is something that I don’t care about in the slightest. And to think that I have been entered in yet another tournament that I didn’t ask to be, for something that I do not want. The last tournament was for the Rising Star Title, which I won not because I was the best wrestler, not because I wanted it the most, but because of the fact that I was the most aggressive. You see aggression is something that I’ve had to learn to deal with my entire life, but now I just don’t bother. I don’t try and control myself anymore; it’s not worth it to me. Xander is trying to control my rage for me, give me focus for it, but it’s not working as well as he might have hoped. Sure enough him giving me targets to take out my anger out on does help, but it does not suppress the feelings of anger I have all the time. Anger that I feel toward everyone, including myself. And people do not do me any favours by sprouting some of the crap that they do around this place. People like Jason Steele, one of the members of New-Tv a group who like to think they are main players in this stable war of eVolution. I’ll admit that I did not expect them to beat Seara and Jason Jameson, I thought that The Notorious was better than that, but obviously not. But despite the fact that you beat them, you will fall on Sunday, because Unlimited is unlike anything you have ever experienced. I’m not the one to sit here and say to you “you’re wrong, we’re better than you” but I know for a fact that you will not win the Tag Titles this week. Xander has got me and the rest of Unlimited focused on our tasks, of eliminating anyone who gets in our way. Jinx and I will win the Tag Titles because I want to make you and Shocka pay. I want you to pay for your ignorance of thinking that New-Tv actually has what it takes to stop Unlimited. And I am especially looking forward to facing you Jason, because you do not seem to know what you are coming up against here. This is a stable war, this is not the USA vs Canada, you’re not fighting for the pride of your country. You are no longer even fighting for the Tag Team Titles, I have already taken them away from you. All you need be concerned with now is survival. You need to be one hundred percent focused on getting through this match without loosing too many pints of blood, or getting too many bones broken. Because I don’t care about the Tag Titles, they are just something that Xander wants Unlimited to have and he has got me focusing enough hatred on you and Shocka to want to hurt you so much that you have to be carried out of the arena. Jason you say that you are going to show everyone what pain is? Does that include me? I really hope you think it does, because you have no clue what pain is. I have lived with pain my entire life, and my only reason for entering into this business was to cause people pain. So I hope that you try and show me what pain is, because then you will know the true meaning of the word, when I make you Taste The Pain… (The look on Asis’ face is midway between a smile and a scowl, something that is not normal to be able to do. He starts to laugh, his head tipped back, his hair falling behind his head exposing his face, his mouth wide open as he sickly laughs.) Shocka I have wanted to fight you for a long time. Ever since you joined eVolution in fact. You have always proved to be one of the arrogant types that I despise, someone who takes their fortitude for granted. And now I look forward to Rise of the Legends with a new found sense of excitement. Because I know I could potentially be facing you twice. If you survive our tag team match and don’t get carried out of the arena straight away, and if we both make it to the final of the Road To Glory Tournament then I will get a second chance to finish off what I will start in the tag team match. To be honest, I couldn’t give a fuck about this tournament, but the first match I have in the tournament is something I care about. The chance to finally get in the ring with a man who may be a bit pissed off with me after what I’ve been doing for the past couple of weeks. I finally am going to go one on one in the ring against the leader of a rival stable, Notorious. I finally get to wrestle Scott Frost. But if this were just a normal one on one match, I would be maybe thinking about the possibility of being defeated. Hell, I admit I’m not the best wrestler in the business and my temper can get the best of me and cost me matches. But this isn’t a normal one on one match that Frosty has got to face me in. He gets me in a match that is being fought under no disqualification rules. You see, we’re on my territory now Scott. The kind of place where I can do what I want, how I want, and not have to suffer any consequences for it. And that is just how I want it. You’ve been a bit delusional recently haven’t you Scott? Thinking that Notorious is the dominant force in eVolution. Thinking that you and your band of warriors can be the saviours of eVolution and eradicate Unlimited. But you are right about one thing; you have eliminated the vast majority of Unlimited. But you haven’t faced me yet. And I promise you, I will prove a bigger test than you could imagine. Xander doesn’t need to get me pumped up for our match. I’m focused on it of my own accord. It will be fun Scott, oh yes it will. You’ve faced my “brother” and failed, now let’s see how you do against me…
