You fools can't be serious

20 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-20 Thug roC, probably in the best mood ever, sits in his office. His smile is obvious and his feet are kicked up on his desk…like normal. But there’s something different about him as he rubs his United Title, placed over his shoulder. He is leaned all the way back in his luxury chair, looking out the window and into the sky before he starts his statement. “You fools can't be serious right now. I'm the all time heavy weight champion in this game. I'm leading the league in at least six statistical categories right now: Best flow, most consistent, realest stories, most charisma, I set the most trends, and my interviews are hotter.” “Indeed, look at me now. I’ve defeated everyone that has been put in front of me. Shocka, Jameson, Scott Frost, all you fools that didn’t think I could make it this far. I put down the biggest and the baddest. Just look at my track record thus far. In less then one year I’ve proven that nobody can stop my streak. Where’s those Mason brothers at now? Answer that question. Did Jameson, or did he not, lose his mind after I beat his ass at A New Dawn? And once I left New-TV, what happened to its other members? Hedges was too busy getting his ass handed to him 24/7, and Omega, HA! Sky Night probably got him chained up somewhere for not asking how high he should jump. I am the key to everything that goes on around me, the ratings has never been higher. You all doubted roC-TV, said it was just a gimmick. Said it was all something to just get Thug roC over, but obviously that’s not true. Did you not hear the fans during that ladder match? They were loving me. Without a doubt in my damn mind, they LOVED it.” Thug roC laughs a bit, then he continues to gloat. “Where you all at? Is there nobody out there that can defeat me? Scott Evans thought he was worthy to come up and challenge me for my title…did you hear me? MY TITLE! That means it’s mine, and nobody else’s. Evans says he was robbed, thinks that he could’ve won a title, bah. Does he not know that Thug roC never comes in the match without a plan? People think that without my arrangement with Leya Forth, that I would’ve won, lets be serious. Who has defeated me thus far, riddle me that. A few lucky pinfalls, that’s all so far. A couple guys think that my ‘plans’ are a form of cheating, but that’s not what they are. When a football coach goes out and exploits a weakness, he’s not accused of cheating. Instead, he’s praised for his intelligence. That’s why I’m not only the best all around athlete, but one of the smartest in the game.” “November 26th, to some it’s the eve of a holiday, but to others it will be a moment when they realize. See, cause it’s scheduled that eVo comes back to town, and that means roC is back in action. So when you arrive to the arena, when you read the chalkboard and realize you are about to face me. You will come to that realization that you can’t compete with me. So what can you say to those guys who feel bad once they realize they face me? Hehe, what else can you tell them? Just, better luck next time.” Scene fades
