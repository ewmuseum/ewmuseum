something to gain p3 - taste like victory

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-22 One man can only take so much pain and punishment or can they? Pain is simply a state of mind. Why not turn every emotion and feeling into one of enjoyment? If a man has true control of his mind he can accomplish anything. A man can tolerate as much as he wants to tolerate. Whether he lies down and chooses not to accept any more pain or punishment is entirely up to them. Hedges won’t lie down for anyone or anything. He’s got as much to gain as anyone… The scene opens back up inside the warehouse where Hedges sits on an old fifty-five gallon drum. He leans against a pillar and a bloody pole lies next to him with a dead rat lying next to his feet. He quietly sits there and then hopes up. His grow wide and he begins to laugh… “You know everyone in this match can prosper greatly from a victory. The losers may simply get looked over like yesterdays news. Everyone has something to gain and something to lose. No one can guarantee when there next title shot will come along. As relative new comers here in eVo Profusion and Dane may not see another piece of gold in a fight for a month or two. No one can tell in this business. Perhaps if a desired performance isn’t reached the owners may not grant more shots to those who don’t perform to bring in money. I’m about to simply lay my body down on the line to take this title…” Hedges stabs a rat with the pole and lifts it’s lifeless body up on the pole and twirls it around staring at it… “You know wounds heal with time. If I don’t die Sunday then I will be emerging From the Ashes with the gold. I go back to the bottom rung more than likely if I lose. That’s something that isn’t going to happen. I refuse to let another opportunity slip threw my fingers” Hedges walks over along the wall and picks up a shard of broken glass… “You see the four stitches on my forehead from Wednesday. I enjoyed them. They remind me of each time Malcolm has slipped away with some dastardly trick. Because I will be putting five in Malcolm’s head for everyone that’s in there right now. Pain is pleasure in this business and if you don’t like stay out of the ring…” With that hedges raises the glass to his head and pulls it across the top and blood begins to trickle down… “HAHA…that’ll be a few more so I guess Malcolm will be up to about forty by the time I’m done with him. He’ll look like a train wreck after Sunday. He will walk out empty handed finally because he can’t call the shots and he knows I am in control. He won’t be on top of his game. I’m focused and determined as I’ve ever been and it’s time the focus went onto winning some gold and advancing my career” Hedges licks the blood from his lips… “Mmmmm…Taste like victory…Sunday you three all will walk into the flames but only I will emerge from the ashes…” Hedges says with extreme confidence as the scene fades to black….
