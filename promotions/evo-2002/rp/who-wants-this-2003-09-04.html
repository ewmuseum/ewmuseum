Who Wants This?

4 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-04 Seems like things only get better. A return to eVo, that gave me a win over Scott Frost. Beating Jameson like there’s no tomorrow inside of a huge cell. Almost ending Ryan Mason’s career before it officially started. And now, I am the United Champion. The scene opens in the trophy room of Thug roC’s home. Much like we saw at the last Legends, title belts, bowling trophies, science fair ribbons, and much more, are all showcased around the room. The desk that sits in the middle of the room has Thug roC behind it, sitting in his chair. You see the ULWN title on the right corner of the desk, the Continental Title on the left corner, and the new United Title over his shoulder. “Multiple cruiserweight championships sit in this room. A few tag titles here and there, and at one time, my most glorious title, the $oldout National Title. But now, the United Title sits at the top of my priority list. I will go down in history as the first ever, eVolution Wrestling United Champion. My name will be in the record books, it’ll be in the wrestling tapes, not to mention, in everyone’s mind for now on. And how did I do it? By having fun. That’s the key of having a good career, by having fun while you do it. And believe me, I have a lot of fun ending careers. Look at Jameson for your first example. Although his career might not be over, he has yet to be the same ever since I beat him to a bloody pulp in that cell.” “Now look at Ryan Mason, sitting at home with a back brace on. Don’t look for him in the ring anytime soon, as I almost ended his career before it actually started. He wanted to be aggressive, and I bet he regrets that. I was more aggressive then he will ever be in his short life. And now he sits at home, wishing he never set foot into that ring with me.” “So what’s next? I will defend my title against any contender who wants a shot. Although, I already know that I will be a long reigning champion. Nobody will be able to see me or my skills in the ring. Whoever wants it, then just step up. But word from the wise, roC-TV has yet to be cancelled or taken off the air, and it won’t happen anytime soon.
