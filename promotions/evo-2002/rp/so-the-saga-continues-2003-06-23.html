So the saga continues......

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-23 The BodySnatchers former holders of the Evolution tag team championship; while their reign was brief the taste of gold left them wanting more......Downstairs at the warehouse complex The King Army calls home The Tone is seen looking at a set of monitors on the video wall...... Tone:'Look at this...these ULWN upstarts; are actually thinking about rebelling. Give these indy ingrates a taste of TV and suddenly they think they are worthy of being on Legends....' 1001:'Does King know?' Tone:'No.....part of being his director of security is handling small matters such as this.' Dolo Bentswinger:'Becoming a staple of The Genesis Pit are we?' Tone:'LOL....guranteed TV time my friend now they are television these houseshow events and using the ULWN as fodder, I can get myself even more over. Plus we can't all be high paid mercenary tag teams as yourself.....lol.' Both BodySnatchers laugh as Dolo and 1001 segue into promo mode...... 1001:'This week we face America's Guardians in a russian chain match. The stipulation is that you and I are chained to one of those inbreeds; and it basically becomes a tug a war with the chain coming into play and the better man winning.' Dolo :'Sounds barbaric....sounds brutal...and a little bloody; I like it. The only thing missing is the bodybag....no wait we can bring those too. Let me break it down in a language you both can understand....' Dolo punches a hole through the wall.....surprising The Tone. Tone:'You are paying for that not me....' Dolo:'Molotov, British Bomber in the land of the free and the home of the brave only one thing here rules and that is the mighty dollar. Capitalism is essential to our way of life. So when you prevent the aquisition of various funds....in this case gold it goes against our very nature.' 1001:'Last week on Legends was not a fluke, a well crafted an orchestrated plan that came to fruition. Having eyes and ears everywhere is a plus in this business. Also taking care of business and not allowing this thing to go to a third fall. Not based on any fear of American Guardians but Evolution management. A Japanese Exploding ring match?!?!? A russian Chain match??!?!?! It seems that the powers that be want the BodySnatchers to be at their brutal best when it's time for us to reclaim the tag team titles.' Dolo:'Which means that at EVO1 it'll be us.....and not the Syndicate who will claim the belts from our current champions St.Violence. Hedges dismissed you as retreads but then again it's easy to dismiss the ramblings of a baby such as he. We know better....we've fought the wars with you and was not the paper champions with all due respect to Luther; that he and Hedges were. Thankfully that experiment ended quickly. However Heartbreak Chick and Big Daddy Dreamer.....your title shot is a reward based on a delusional dream our owner has to make EVO1 a special show. However it will be special...for it'll be the beginning of the Reign which will dwarf that of Twisted Reality.' 1001:'The time of transitional champions is done.....you have Luther Wisdom who is quite frankly meant for bigger and better things; you have Hedges who wants to return to the comfort of getting by life in the easiest way possible. St.Violence whose luck runs out at EVO1...and The Syndicate, the self proclaimed greatest tag team of all time. Well we have a differing opinion...on the tag associated with Greatness. Malcolm says this is a venture he does not endorse...fine we understand and respect that. But Syndicate know this....if by some stretch of imagination you get the strength to actually show up at EVO1.....you; regardless of anyone elses agenda you become our number one priority.' the phone rings.....Tone answers it and after a brief conversation interupts the BodySnatchers..... Tone:'That was QBX....King wants us to meet him at the resturant.' Dolo:'So who is going to tell Luther?' All three men look up at the ceiling.......then Tone and Dolo look at 1001 Holds. 1001:'Damn'
