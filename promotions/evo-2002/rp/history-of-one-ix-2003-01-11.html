History of One :: IX

11 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/11/03 YMCA. I hesitated going in at first, never being in one, I didn't know if you had to flash a Christian card or something... I took my time, slowly going through the doors that led into the main area. There wasn't too many people in here, just two girls working the counter.. Girls? I had to stop and grab a second look, and I was indeed correct.. Two girls. I didn't know what to make of it, I figured it was just labor laws.. The room was glass and you could see the basketball court, weight room, pool, all that junk.. I didn't think I had to do anything, so I just tried walking through the door.. 'Ahem..' One of the girls was trying to get my attention, I stopped in my tracks and turned around.. 'Yeah?' I didn't bother walking towards the desk, I didn't feel like answering any questions. I just wanted to go find something to keep me occupied. 'Are you a member?' She asked, eyeing me up, she knew the answer.. 'Nope..' Why I even bothered answering her was beyond me. 'Your going to have to sign in than please..' I just nodded, went to the desk, and signed the paper.. 'Thank you.' She said with a faint smile.. 'No, thank you..' I turned and walked into the large workout area, all kinds of machines were in here. Stationary bikes, rowing machines, place looked more like a gym than a Y.. Than again, I've never actually been inside a YMCA before so I wouldn't really know what one looked liked. It defiantly wasn't what I expected it to be, but it was something... At the time, I wasn't exactly the biggest fish in the pond, but I had determination and the time to further myself. I could make myself bigger and stronger, for myself.. Who else did I have but myself? Everything I did from here on out was for me.. The YMCA was my start to becoming the person I've always wanted to be.. Not the tall lanky kid no one noticed. I wanted to be the big, tough, kid that no one fucked with.. It wasn't really a goal or anything, but the feeling would be nice. Most of the people in the YMCA were a bit older than I, well, considering it was a school day, they all were older than I. None of them really payed me any attention, most of them just kinda shrugged me off as some kid playing hookie who needed something to do. Whatever, I didn't notice them much either. I wasn't worried about the meat heads who were benching five hundred pounds or whatever. I didn't care about them or what they were doing. I wasn't impressed. I doubt impressing me was what they were trying to do though, maybe some fine women or something of the sorts. That's why most guys do it right? Or they are gay. One of the two. I stared light, just the ten pound hand weights.. I came up with my own routine. Thirty each hand, than switch to something else.. I did the thirty, looked around, stationary bike. I nodded to myself and went and rode that for fifteen minutes.. Than what? Before I finished I looked around a bit more, treadmill.. Ten minutes. That was it, that was my plan.. Keep it simple right? That's what I was doing, I was sixteen.. I didn't want to over work myself, but I didn't wanna be too easy on myself either. I didn't know what I was going to do in life, but I did know I was going to get in shape.. Simple?.. Not really.
