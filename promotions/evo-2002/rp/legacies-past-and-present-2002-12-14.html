Legacies past and present

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/14/02 Luther Wisdom has just finished his morning workout. As per his ritual he stops by the local krispy Kreme to get his motivation for tomorrows workout. Today the line is absurdly long in the drive tru. 'Well I guess now is a good a time as any to record my promo...' An Evo Camera crew he promised to treat with coffee and donoughts get ready as if they shoot the Promo now that saves them time later. Wisdom turns down the Common CD in his car as he prepares to deliver his promo.... 'Let's see I'm in the middle of this Stable War, my partner and myself have earned Tag team title shots at the PPV, and I have the pleasure of facing Sovereign on Legends...so I'm in a great mood.' The crew look around as if confused.... 'It's like this, Sovereign is no longer Universal Champion, the mystique about him is somewhat broken. Even still I'm not expected to win...hell he's expected to kill me. So you see no pressure.Don't get me wrong come Legends I'm all about handling my business, and quite simply getting killed by Sovereign is bad for my business.' they advance one car length..... 'You see if I get killed, possibly injured by Sovereign at Legends; that jeapordizes my Tag Team title shot at the PPV. A point I didn't expect to be given the agenda that was thrust upon me. Think about it Malcolm King and Xavier Citizen haven't recieved a title shot at anything during their time here. My partner is dying...each day could be his last, so a shot at the tag team titles is something that can't be taken for granted. So a loss, homicide, or whatever you want to call it at the hands of Sovereign is not something I can allow to happen.' He pulls up to the window and orders Three dozen glazed as well as coffee for everyone.... 'You see this sport is about using fear and respect to your advantage. Do I fear Sovereign....yes. You have to have a healthy fear of who you are facing in the ring...the possibility that you may lose, get injured is there for me. Understanding that and taking steps to ensure that doesn't happen is my responsibility, my job. Do I respect Sovereign.....again yes, he was evolution's first Universal Champion, he carved a path of destruction unlike any other, that alone has to be respected. So the question you want me to answer...and I guess everyone wants me to answer Will I win at Legends...the answer is simply yes.' He looks at the crew faces as they have alook as if he just signed his death warrent... 'I know what you are thinking....answer me this question who is Universal Champion? Simple logic he can be beaten...so why not by me?' ELSEWHERE Deep in the bowels of The Hartford Civic Center, you see Xavier Citizen in the fetal position. He has a blank look on his face as drool comes out of his mouth. He slowly smiles as he begins to speak..... 'Darkness will soon take me....I know this to be true.But I have unfinished business, that with Troy Pierce. As much as I don't like to be the babysitter of that bastard Luther Wisdom...HE'S MY BASTARD!!!!! You being a member of the Nation automatically makes you one of us......even though I wasn't consulted. Your being in Evolution makes you brotherhood business...even though no one asked me about it.So far legacies that should have been mine have been handed to lesser men. Xander has control of The Unlimited an organization I was a member of, some would say the forgotten member. And you Pierce you were handed the leadership role..a function by rite that should have been mine.' Xavier rolls to his back...... 'At Legends I face you in a last man standing match. For me this match is about vindication....for you Troy Pierce it's about validation. Your win last week was nothing more than a fluke. Why did Malcolm have faith in you....and not in me? Is your being here a signal of things to come? I can't accept that Troy Pierce; disco is dead and so are you.' (fade to black.....to be continued)
