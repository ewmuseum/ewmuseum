roC Superstar: New Friend (Ch.2)

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.05.03 ~He knew as he lie back in his car seat that he had to keep his cool when he was at the arena on Wednesday. He expected that roC would do everything that he could to try and throw him off but Shocka was going to do everything in his power to make sure that he kept himself under control something that seemed to grow in difficulty with the passing of every week. He had begun to take his pills at a furious rate to cool him down; they had a relaxing effect that led him into a sort of meditation. That is how we find him now, in his car the place that provides the most relaxing environment for him. His radio is tuned into a local station that is running things about the war. He shakes his head and speaks.~ ‘It seems that this is all that is on now a days…people are obsessed with this war, they can’t go on living there every day lives with out having to see civilians die. Its just wrong!’ Shocka shouts to himself. ‘I have been living my life as I normally do though…I have been putting up with thug roC’s shit I thought that I got rid of him last December where he almost drove me insane but now he is back and more infuriating than ever! And this time I can’t do a damn thing about it because if I lay just one finger on him I get shipped to prison if it’s not in a match…I dunno how or why he got that restraining order…but I guess it just proves that he is terrified of me…’ Shocka speaks to himself taking a deep breath he again speaks calmly. ‘I am able to wait though…I can calm myself just enough to hold me until our next match…maybe that coward will want some of me next week…’ He shakes his head and pulls his chair up. ~People have begun to realize that Shocka is not a bad guy he is just misunderstood. The car starts up and he begins to move away from the scene he pulls into a parking lot and stops. He jumps out and walks into a local diner he walks in and studies the crowd he seems to be looking for someone in particular. A midget is walking towards them but he simply looks over him and continues searching.~ ‘Hey!’ A voice calls. ‘God?!’ Shocka asks looking around not seeing anyone talking to him. ‘Down here idiot!’ The same voice screams ~Shocka attention is directed towards the ground where he sees a midget standing about 3 feet tall coming about to Shocka’s belly button.~ ‘Oh hey little buddy!’ Shocka screams. ‘You know where I could find Elmer?’ Shocka asks. ‘RIGHT HERE!’ The midget screams. ‘Really? You are the one that called me and said you could offer some help with thug roC?!’ Shocka questions a little skeptical. ‘Yes…now do you want to know what I have in mind or not?!’ The midget asks. ‘Alright!’ Shocka screams. ‘I got a table over here!’ The midget says as he turns around. ‘You want me to get you a boaster seat?’ Shocka asks half serious. ‘Fuck off.’ The midget says as he climbs into a booth. ‘Just wondering I mean you are a pint size little guy aren’t ya?!’ Shocka asks laughing. ‘That’s it I am out of here!’ The midget says jumping down from the table and beginning to walk away. ‘Wait man I was just joking!’ Shocka screams. ‘Well if you are interested put 50 bucks on the table right now and I’ll give you one of my ideas…’ The midget says. ‘What?!’ Shocka asks. ‘I’m gone!’ The wee man says as he walks away. ‘No stop!’ Shocka says putting 50 dollars on the table. ‘That’s better!’ The midget says as he begins walking back towards the table. ~He jumps back into the booth and stands up at the table and begins with his idea.~ ‘Well first I thought that I could…’ ~The midget continues speaking as the scene fades.~
