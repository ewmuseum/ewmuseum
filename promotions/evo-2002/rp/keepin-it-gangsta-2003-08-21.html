Keepin' It Gangsta

21 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-21 Machine…the gentle giant. Funny how that term is used. A giant, a guy who’s obviously much larger then the rest. And to be gentle? That doesn’t really fit into the cell division all that much. But that’s what they call Machine, a guy who’s obviously larger then the rest, yet he isn’t some big bully. Machine instead is known for his laughter, jokes, and sometimes rhymes. Today it looks like we’re going to hear more rhymes as Machine shows up in the booth along with Thug roC, both men ready to drop some lyrics while producer, Rob B. sits on the outside of the booth, controlling the beat. “Ok Machine, let me hear what you got.” Rob B. says from his seat. “And no more eating in the damn booth! You left pizza crusts on the floor last week.” “What? I’m surprised you left any food behind you greedy bastard.” Thug roC replies. “I don’t eat pizza crusts. They have too much grease on them, I have to watch my weight.” Machine says in his defense. He rubs his stomach while doing so. “Watch your weight? Aren’t you about eleven pounds shy of a big rig?” Thug roC begins laughing at his own joke as Rob B. laughs just to be polite. “Come on big man, get on the mic unless you want me to show you how it’s done.” “Step back and learn from a pro.” Machine shoves Thug roC out of the way to step up to the microphone. He searches through his papers before finally finding his verse. “She said she was a stripper but she wanted to rhyme and if I gave her a deal she make her legs stretch back to her spine I told her hit the treadmill, 'til she run out of time And in the Eve and No Doubt, she was ‘blowin my mind’ I'm from the A-town, put 'em through a GA course Cause 'it's all in the game' like EA Sports From scams and money grams, there's unions that wanna wire me 'You think you know but really don't' - put it in your 'Diary' I'm quick to knock 'em out, they comin in three spurts I do like Spielberg and see that yo' ‘DreamWorks’ My weed is all lavender, my drink is blue My gat'll, ‘Poke-a-Man,’ like a Pikachu (Ma)Chine eats a whole bunch, yet takes small bites And she don't wanna get on top, cause they scared of heights” “That’s good big boy.” Rob B. says as he turns off the beat. “Thuggy, lets go.” “Ok rookie, move and I’ll show you how it’s done.” Thug roC says, cockingly walking past the much larger Machine. “Thug's livin' la vida loca Only guy in the hood you can come see for either weed or coca Nark's wanna see me and my team in a chair They heard about the kid with the high beams in his ear D.E.A. been lookin' for proof since 9-3 When I came through in the Benz with the roof behind me Tell them jake's through on bullet proof's and find me You need extinguishers to go in the booth behind me Who the fuck wanna beef My Fendi knits be 3X so you can't see whats tucked underneath And I might not even drop Just take my advance and make a small town in Cleveland pop 'Vivrant Thing' on my hip, that will make you 'Breathe and Stop' Rock ya chain in ya shirt, Your roll with the sleeve on top You really know where my heat stay at I leave people MIA and I ain't talkin where the Heat play at” Rob cuts the music once more, and starts switching the beat up. Machine steps up to the mic along with Thug roC, both preparing to rap. Thug roC (Machine) We your favorite gangstas, favorite gangstas (do better both) Before these slugs sink hitters, weighs like anchors We don't own clean guns (all our skets is dirty I toss bullets, New York killas just like Testaverde I don't keep the scope on the ratchet And for the dope I'm gonna catch it) Make sure the joint ain't point blank so they can't open your casket My whole crew (got glocks on 'em) In a hurry to shoot, (like they put shot clocks on 'em) All that gossip we blast, better see if they possibly has A V12 ambulance that will get you to a hospital fast (We done came out the cages in shackles I ain't call a pager to track you I'm loadin' up gauges to whack you We been riding together, And us back and forth), it's like puttin' Saddam Hussein and Bin Laden together Soon as you get a crumb, they wanna bury ya That's why I travel with a semi, like Eddie in 'Coming to America' (Silencers, it sound like it's hummin' when I'm airin' ya Won't know you hit, 'til your body start numbin' in that area) The kids don't want to see the toast of mom and daddy Plus we rather be roastin' Charming fatties In a toasted armor Caddy (And we come through, with chains glisten and thangs spittin' Hollow shots'll leave your brain missin' Ghetto R O C (and big Machine) We gettin heaps of complaints for Keepin' it Gangsta Rob B. seems very happy with the performance as both men seemed to have thought it was pretty good. Thug roC looks up at Machine and smiles. “Just to think, with me on the track, you’ll become a star.” Machine just grins. “And with me on the track, nobody will toss the CD out the window like they do all your promo tapes.” The two continue to bicker as the scene fades to black.
