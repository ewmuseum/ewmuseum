Lost

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.24.03 ~Lost, is it possible for one to be lost within themselves…be torn between two lovers perhaps but not lost to as where they are going in life is it? For one man that is involved in eVolution wrestling he is indeed lost, spinning in his own mind not knowing who to strike…His former stable mate tore into him last night with great fury and left him in the middle of the ring to be pounced upon by the challenger for his title. But the challenger for his title didn’t take the opportunity that was laid out right in front of them from Morpheus Omega, no instead Scott Frost tried to fend off Omega and get him away from the ring. Thus comes the conflict, how can Shocka be in tune with a man that he has a great passion for hating just because he saved his ass in the ring…I mean it is the same man that escaped Tainted Love with his Universal title…but on the other hand at least Scott did the honorable thing and beat Shocka on his own. He let him recover from the beating of Omega and start making his way up the ladder then boom he slammed him through a C4 exploding table!~ ‘FUCK!’ A helpless yelp erupts from a large man that bears a hoody over his face. ‘I can’t believe that son of a bitch turned on me!’ The man again yells stepping closer into the light of a street lamp. Snow slowly falls from the sky and begins to pile up on the streets. The face of the man is now revealed to be Shocka and he looks visibly upset and confused. ‘How am I going to redeem myself…I’ve mad mistakes in my life but none as big as this…I let the Universal title slip right through my grasps and I have 2nd degree burns on my back because of it.’ He shakes his head silently and droops it looking for answers within himself. ~How can someone as prominent as Shocka be withered away into a broken man by a loss from Scott Frost, I mean if he wanted a rematch he could just demand it. But something is not right within him, he seems…different.~
