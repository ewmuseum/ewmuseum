What I Am

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/11/02 The rain falls fast, and slowly the ground drowns. The soil turns to grime below our feet, and slowly eats us up in its devouring hunger. Tears don’t appease its craving. Cries don’t make its slow down. The rain keeps falling. The grime keeps growing. The end draws closer and closer. And all we can do is sit in our lawn chairs and watch the fireworks. I ask myself. Did I ever succeed in making you die just a little but quicker? That’s all I wanted to know. Were you going to fall from grace just a little bit earlier because of the actions of my soul? Was I going to feel you last breath on my neck? Was the stench of your decaying flesh going to be my favorite fragrance? That’s all I wanted to know. My only mission in life is to share the pain of my tortured soul with the world. Nothing more and nothing less. I could take that pain and turn it into something positive, and give the world one less tear, and make the universe that much better, and give newspapers a few articles about the what a humanitarian I am, and get a medal for my ways, and win some prizes, and forever have my name be synonymous with the names of the greatest saints our world has ever known, and maybe, just maybe be the person that this world could actually love. But that would be the easy way out. Besides, I don’t like the world that much. What has it given me besides pain and grief? Shit. That’s what the world has given me. A large, smoking pile of shit, towering into the sky like. I could cry about it, like you all would. Or I could see the beauty of it and mold it into a work of art, like you all would like me to. Instead, I decided to grab a shovel and hurl it back at you, yelling “Merry Fucking Christmas” as I laugh into your faces. Maybe I am bitter. Maybe I am resentful and hateful. Maybe I wish you would all die. But that is only because of what you made me. And now, I must win back what is mines. There is this certain title, wrapped around the certain waist of a certain individual. He thinks he can control me, but he has another thing coming to him. I will get by his little obstacles. I will overshadow his reign. I will take back what was mine to begin with. You can sit and drink the rain, or lick the grime, but I will spit with the rain, and shit on the grime. The fireworks shoot off, and I’m there to fight back. The end draws near. And it will lie in my hands.
