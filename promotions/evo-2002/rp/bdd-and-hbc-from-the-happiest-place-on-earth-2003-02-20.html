BDD and HBC from the happiest place on earth!

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-20-03 Anaheim, CA Disneyland Somewhere on Space Mountain…..WOOO! Legends has come and gone, and The Syndicate has dodged one bullet in the form of S., but still that arrogant a-hole is causing tremendous problems for Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick. BDD, taken out early in the Cogs match I guess should be thankful for Xavier Citizen, but truth be told, rather kick his ass left and right for his interference and then screwing over Malcom King. Though King and BDD never did get along, treachery remains. But for now, that is going to be put aside as BDD and HBC enjoy a day off before preparing for perhaps the biggest PPV of their entire career, Tainted Love. Nice thing about being a superstar is that you don’t have to wait in any lines at Disneyland. BDD and HBC walk up to the entrance way to Space Mountain in which the line guy recognizes them and escorts them through the handicap walkway to place them in the front of the line. A few people bitch and complain, but once they see who it is, they step back alittle bit to let The Syndicate have their fun in the fun. HBC and BDD pull down the lap bar as the funky space music begins to play behind them in their chairs. The flashing neon strobes hit and whoosh! Off they go! Left, right, right, left, left, those who been their know the drill, a flash of the camera on the downward spiral and whoosh, done is the ride! BDD and HBC look at each other, give a quick high-five and head off past ‘Honey I shrunk the kids’ to take a look at the photo. HBC gives a goofy look while BDD poses. HBC: Not bad D. BDD: Damn, do that look in the ring and I think they’ll fall over laughing. HBC: Oh shut up smart ass! *Punches on shoulder* BDD: Worried about Sunday? HBC: Probably no more than you. Concerned, you better believe it. As good as Wisdom is, he’s too temperamental. He doesn’t think with his head, just his heart. Noble, but not in a time like this. I need him clear of thought, perhaps, ticked a little bit, but focused. Is that too much to ask for? BDD: I think the kid will do just fine. I mean he’s got you after all. Two-time Evo Tag Team Champion with him. So that much is working fine. But, yeah, I know what your saying. Seeing what happened in Frisco could, could screw him up a little bit. Just talk to him before the match, perhaps go out the night before and discuss things. Both of you are going to have a hard enough time at Tainted Love with what seven, eight teams, all gunning for you. If the kid doesn’t have his head screwed on right, its going to be a long, long night. HBC: And that is my concern. Sure he’s gifted in some areas. King taught him well, I just hope he keeps his cool and does his job. I hope King instilled in him that much. Otherwise, yeah, I agree with you. Tag title go bye-bye. S. really does despite us doesn’t he? BDD: Well, when we constantly foil his plans, and whip his cronies left and right, I’m sure he is a little resentful of us. HBC: Awww…… Boo fricken hoo. Cry me a river S. BDD: That’s the spirit! Truth be told, he’s jealous were not working for him, then his life would be much simpler, instead of the constant mess it is now, thanks to us. HBC: HURAH! I’m hungry, wanna get a Buzz Lightyear Pizza. BDD: Snaps! Hell ya! $9.00 slices of pizza coming right up! HBC: LOL! After eating their slices of pizza, taking a few pictures with some fans, they proceed off to French Quarter for the bomb ass ride of all-time! The Pirates of the Caribbean! Again, because they are who they are, they don’t wait in line like simpletons. Isn’t it peachy to be famous! ‘Yo ho ho, a pirates life for me!’ starts singing HBC as they proceed down the dark and scary tunnel! WEEEEE!!! First drop! HBC: Kinda looks like our bank vault doesn’t it D? All the gold, jewelry, and booty! BDD: Yeah, but I want the red head! HBC: HA! No way man! She’s mine! In chorus: WE WANT THE RED HEAD!!! As hysterical laughter rings from the boat. They relax a bit watching the battle scene unfold until they get to the red head at auction. WE WANT THE RED HEAD!!! WE WANT THE RED HEAD!!! BDD: S. couldn’t even score with the animatronic! HBC: SNAPS! Ain’t that the truth! The only date he can get is a plastic blow up doll! And even then, they deflate on him! BDD: LOL! HBC: So you ready for Asis? BDD: Mister PMS boy? Hell yeah! I want my Continental Championship back and to put him through hell within the cell, even better. They banned that baby for a reason, and I’m going to show him why. Unlimited, sure they’ll try to interfere, but in the end, when I whip his butt from metal cage to metal cage, and recapture what is mine. S. will be even more ticked off, all the better to foil another plan of his, and when you successfully defend your title, you and I stand their together, waving our gold in his face as he erupts blowing that little aneurism he has. It will be hella sweet! S. crawling into the back going damn, damn, damn! It’s what I live for! Hey buddy, one more time through! ‘Sure thing sir!’ HBC: Damn tooten! As difficult as Tainted Love will be, even S. knows to be weary of a pissed off Heartbreaker and the Big Bad Booty Daddy! Tainted Love, The Tag titles remain around my hot and sexy waist and the C-strap comes back to bootylicious! And S.…..isn’t that a bitch! WE WANT THE READ HEAD!!!
