Scattered Thoughts

27 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-27 ~Scattered thoughts, scattered memories, the life of a professional wrestler well documented injuries through time. Get over it; Shocka has spent his fair amount of time in the ER’s he has been banged up with the best of ‘em. Kimson just can’t admit that Shocka got the better of him, a week before digital destruction softening him up with a table, then at digital destruction he was taken to his limits. And to top it all off Shocka heard about a new work out routine similar to a baseball player swinging a bat…he just wanted to try it out is all. Kimson you were in the wrong place at the wrong time, simple as that Shocka was looking to set an example and you just happened to cross by in his path.~ ~He had arrived early three days to be precise, he was preparing for something but only he knew what that was. He left Voss even this time; he’ll be here soon enough though looking for him…just like always. Kimson needs to check out of the hospital if he wants a fight, he doesn’t have to go looking for Shocka. Shocka has no need to run he doesn’t fear Kimson in fact he invites him. If he can get himself up to a jog that is…~ ~Shocka dropped off his luggage and was out to find something to eat his friend Jake had come over from Oregon to see him. Shocka hasn’t talked to anyone from the States in person outside of eVolution staff in weeks…it was almost unbearable…as no one here shares the same mental capacity as Shocka. They just can’t to the kind of conversation he can bring.~ ‘You know what James? You’re probably resting gently now in bed taking shallow breaths so you can breathe pain free. But you know what…I got over it…I accept the fact that you are probably injured. But you know what I hope you are 100 percent by Faith…I want to show the entire world that I am still the same destructive force I have been. James you know full well the damage I can inflict…and now it’s going to cost you your Universal strap…the belt will be mine again…I cannot trip up this time around…’ ~Shocka stopped and looked around him for a second he was the first one of the wrestlers to arrive. The crew would probably be there again tomorrow to finish the set up…he had a full night to waste away.~ ‘Before I go James, I’d like to issue you this warning…you think what I have done to you in these last few weeks is intense…you just wait to see what I have in store for you…at faith you will see the real Shocka…you will see what I am truly capable of…up to this point all of my moves have been coming to a head…you have only seen a sampling of what’s coming…and now your sister is involved also…the odds are against you…and they will continue to stack this week.’ ~With that Shocka smiled and walked from the camera’s view he had thing to do tonight…rather than train…medical treatment for a concussion wouldn’t allow that now would it?~
