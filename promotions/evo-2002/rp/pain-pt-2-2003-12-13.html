Pain Pt. 2

13 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-13 He screamed for it, begged for it, even prayed for it, but he recieved no pain. There was no feeling to him whatever, no hot, not cold, just nothingness. His life had been take over by numbness, everything went by too slow or too fast to take in and concentrate. He'd won once again but it wasn't the victory he wanted, he needed to defeat the numbness all over his body and feel again, live again. He needed to light his celestial fire again, get things going again to continue up the mountain. He'd experienced many things in his life but nothing like this, before he'd been able to feel sad or scratch himself and feel pain but none came when he tried to force the feelings upon himself. No matter what there was no feeling there, and he began to wonder what's the point in living without the ability to feel? He'd awaken every morning in a rush to feel again, to truly live again, he did what he could to give himself feeling and every morning he found himself thinking that suicide was the only way out, but he cleared the thought from his head, he couldn't do that, no matter what it was not an option. He'd never fulfilled his childhood dreams of becoming the best, of being better than any other man in his family. That was the only thing that had kept him going, that drove him to live. Maybe it was because he was trying to drive away the want to commit suicide that had caused him too be less careful this morning, it didn't matter though, the cuts and bruises would heal and he didn't feel them anyways so who cares? Maybe not feeling wouldn't hinder him in the ring at all maybe he was looking at this the wrong way, or maybe it was just part of his daily irrational thinking, he had no idea which and maybe it could make him better but not feeling was a weakness as he'd learned many years ago. Maybe it was that he'd grown up to fast maybe that was why things in his life had been so hectic. Why everything he'd loved had been taken away in a heartbeat, he'd lost everything that meant anything within the process of one night and the whole question to him was why hadn't he desired to end his life then? Why had he chosen to live a broken man? The answers no matter how hard he tried to find them wouldn't come maybe he was in too great a rage to let any of those desires come forward until his most desperate was fulfilled, but maybes were all that came to him and he needed something that actually meant something, something that was definitive and noting like that could come. It was all lost in the numbness that surrounded his brain, he was fighting it, but it would soon overcome all his defenses and take over everything within he feared. Slowly he found himself truly overcome, slowly it swept through his mind and he began to go in limbo, awaiting passage to another realm. He had lost this final fight, how would he continue? All these thoughts swept through the nothingness he secceded into.
