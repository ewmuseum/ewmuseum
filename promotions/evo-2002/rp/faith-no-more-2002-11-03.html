Faith no more

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-11-02 The scene opens to see L2J Lying up against a head board in a hospital bed. Various medics and nurses have gathered round his bed seemingly preparing to take the 23 stitches out of his forehead. It is clear from the look in L2Js eyes that he has been sedated. His eyes remain open but his consciousness is in question. He just lies motionless as the stitches are cut away from his face thinking about the last thing he can remember of that faithful night. 1 The count began. And he could do nothing but stare up at the lights that fell away from him so gracefully. Paralysed but for who long. He had nine seconds left to finally obtain his dream, and all he had to do to achieve it was stand up. Not even that. Simply show a sign of life. But in the light there was comfort. Other than the comfort he was surrounded by broken bones, splintered wood, ripped steal and puddles of blood. The light managed to keep its appeal because it showed no pain. And there he stared. 2 William. The animal. The man behind the scheme. The man who suckered me, who led me around for a month to bring me to this one final moment. Just like everyone I have made the mistake of trusting. Sovereign, like Asis before him, Daniel Somers before that, and Khris Kouger before him. One by one have turned on me when it mattered the most. And have struck me down as I was about to shine. But Sovereign was different. Because the false trust he put in me did not go to plan. Because L2J still lives and breathes and slowly but surely is stealing from Sovereign what matters to him the most. 3 His family. The real victims. Those who watched their son, their brother, their husband once again disappear from them. In front of their very eyes. They were used. He used them, he manipulated them, he created this false hope, just to get to me. And for this they have my deepest sympathy. For what their loved became for some stupid game. To lure me to this one place. He sacrificed the love of his family in an attempt to bring about my destruction. Was it worth it? 4 He tried to make me scared, for a month he tried to build up the shock, and at its climax he was going to trap me. But the flaw in his plan was he expected me to be scared. He looked at me with that smile on his face. A smile that I had the pleasure of watching disappear as I stared back as the cage fell around me. As all exits closed in the lair of this monster I looked him straight in the eye. And I was the one that wore the smile. And that’s when he knew all the planning and sacrifices he made over the past month were for nothing. Because I was ready. To fight. Because what he had planned out for a month I have been planning for my entire life. And it began. The battle between a man who would die for his dream, against a man who dreamed of killing. 5 The repercussions. His plan came to end. But the result wasn’t as he had wanted it. As not only the slayer but also the demon had been defeated, the two fierce rivals had nothing left. And they both lay dormant beside eachother amidst the carnage that they had created. This was no fairy tale ending. Sovereign had made sure that I couldn’t be a knight in shining armour. He knew it had all gone wrong. Everything he had thought out so carefully had failed him. So he destroyed us both in the hope of going back to the drawing board. But a new weapon besides trust will have to be found now. 6 The lights aren’t so warm now. As the blood collected into pools either side of my nose I began to lose sight of the light. It brought me back to reality and I realised there was only 4 seconds left. 7 There he was. We shared the same pile of destruction. I looked at his crimson face amidst all the debris and forced myself to not contemplate who I must have looked. I watched him as he bled. My eyes burned as the blood began to trickle out of their pools and down my temples. And I hoped he wouldn’t get up. I hoped that he would never give up. I wished I had an ounce of strength left to take hold of the piece of splintered wood that had come to rest on the fingertips of my left hand and strike him one last time in his vulnerable state. Then I realised. I was as bad as him. He had made me that way. 8 The lights were gone. Everything was red. But I no longer needed comfort, because I had no feeling left. I didn’t even know if I was conscious. I had to be. Because I could still hear the monotonous voice of the referee. It was the only thing left that was keeping me connected to reality. It was the only thing I could think about. The next two seconds seemed to last a lifetime. For that lifetime I was paralysed, my dream had escaped me. 9 A friend. A friend is an enemy who can ask for favours. But I felt like I needed one. But I don’t remember why. My only friend has brought with him 4 more rivals. But I still feel grateful for having Scott Frost on my side. It may be the only thing I have left as an advantage over sovereign. Or is Frost just next on the list. Has he got in line to be the next adversary. A friend is an enemy who is waiting for the right time. 10 I remember what came first. The anger then the pain. All my life. My destiny brought me to this point. All that was left to do was rise up off the floor and my prize would be mine but then I remember why I couldn’t do it, I had to lie there. I couldn’t even blink, I had no choice but to stare at my own crippled corpse. As I was lifted out of the carnage and led to the hospital. And it was over. There was no fairy tale ending. Just scars that would never heal. L2J begins to come around and finds himself staring up at a sterile light in his hospital ward. It takes a moment for him to get his bearings then he drops his feet to the floor and notices the bandages round his ribs, arm and leg are gone, he then feels his forehead to find the stitches have been removed. He remains unphased as he gets his jacket off the chair by his bed and limps out into the hallway. He is cut off by a nurse who begins protesting at his activity but L2J couldn’t pay less attention to her as he stumbles through some double doors. He knows exactly where he is going. When another scheme is going to launched against him he wants to make sure that he is there. Ready and waiting.
