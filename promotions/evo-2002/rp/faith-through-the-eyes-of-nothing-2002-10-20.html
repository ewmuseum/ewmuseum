Faith Through the Eyes of Nothing

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have watched the sins pass One after the other Like dominos falling in place The time has come for someone to take control To bring an end to this world’s futile quest for forgiveness You will never be forgiven for your sins You will die, rotting in the ground with the parasites of filth to devour your carcass Your falsely penitent souls will be thrown into the depths of hell Torture will be your everything Forgiveness will be your void The fires will burn you Scorch your skin Transform your bones into pitiful ashes Sear your soul until there is nothing left My sins are there But I have paid for them Repaid my God with misery Compensated with a desolate life And rewarded with only more agony to top off my own pile of shit I look to the sky Feeling his eye Burning a hole Through my soul But of all things I know that He’s there But I don’t know if He cares The world prays to their Lord And begs at His feet Not for wealth or preservation of life, like so many lie about But for forgiveness For an unworthy salvation It is that thing that they call their Faith That tells them that He will listen That He will understand And that He will care But my faith tells me another story It tells me that He just stares Gazing and laughing Into all of us Faith Just another word for lie The lies that we tell ourselves Every second of every day I am my own judge I have judged you And you are condemned to perpetuity in nothing Sins can never be forgiven But they can be compensated I will take my price Your fucking life And then maybe Just maybe You will realize that your Faith Is nothing
