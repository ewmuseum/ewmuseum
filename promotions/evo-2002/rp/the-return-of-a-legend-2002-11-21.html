The return of a Legend

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Paris, France BDD’s Penthouse Apartment Evening time. Amused……that’s all Big Daddy Dreamer can really say as he looks toward The Eiffel Tower that dominates the 12th district. The shiny beacon that was at first looked down upon by its citizenry only to later become one of its crowing achievements. Sort of like my opponent, The HeartBreak Chick. You remember that wharehouse don’t you C. The day you came in, scared out of your mind and with my help, mentoring and guidance you became like this tower over in the distance. A shining example of the future of this industry. But like this tower, it is prone to rust, getting old, and creaky. It needs to be constantly repaired, repainted, so that it shines again. Obviously your not old in an age sense C, but your latest rant about how I supposedly turned my back on Canada, that’s real old. I taught you better than that. Losers always bitched about how their friends turned on them, or that they were screwed out of something. Frankly C, I would go so far as to say that you’ve been hanging around too much with our supposed ‘National Hero’ Bret ‘Hitman’ Hart with all the bitching and whining you’ve done lately. It really doesn’t become you. You know something though, perhaps it is better this way. We’ve always been arrogant, pompous and hell, down right self-righteous. Our position in life gives us that, but you C, you’ve gone off the deep end. Before, you wouldn’t piss and moan about the screwjob, we would just plot our revenge and carry it out. But now, is whine, whine, whine, waaaa, waaa, waaaa, oh Unlimited come and save me! Grow up C, stop being a whining crying little bitch! You’ve bitched about how I supposedly held you back, yet you join a company of non-talents who quite frankly are only using you to further their own careers and get close to you because they couldn’t in any other way. Jinx seems to be living the high-life, Xander and Asis as well, but I wonder who is footing the bill for it. Oh I know, you are! You bought yourself some cheap friends to make yourself feel better about you. Because, all of a sudden, the self-proclaimed First Lady of Evolution, because a royal pussy and couldn’t do anything on her own. Gone is the HeartBreaker who single handedly defeated the best in the business, by herself, shocking the critics, silencing the doubters. Now, they are screaming you need help because truth be told, you do. You, in two months, did more damage to your reputation than you ever thought possible. Instead of going after the big one on your own and proving a point, you instead gave everyone reason to doubt your creditability and that C, is going to take a long time to ever get back! You remember how they doubted us, that a mix-gender tag team could ever SURVIVE, let alone, BECOME the greatest tag team in the history of this industry. You’ve pissed everything away and for what, to prove something to me. Hell, C, I knew you could do it along time ago, you never believed it in yourself. Drake even knew it passing the torch to you to run with it, but now, you’ve snuffed it out. Week in and week out, you come out here trying to pass the buck about why The Syndicate is no more, you’ve superkicked me what? A good half dozen times at least and still I stand. You went even so far as to try to end my career with chairshots and boltcutters into my eye socket and still here I stand before you telling you, straight up…..’The HeartBreaker….sucks!’ And I don’t mean dick either! People would run in fear from you, cringe when they saw their name next to yours, no longer. Its true, you have a mean streak as long as mine and I’m sure people were shocked at the depths you went to try and destroy me, yet, as I said, here I am before you, looking at you with both eyes staring back. As I said to you before the first feud you ever had in this business, make damn sure you finished the job, because one day, one day, that individual could come back and take you out instead. Which leads us to the Battle of Toronto. You started something you thought, you tried to finish, but you didn’t. You tried to take out your mentor and failed. Sure, you notched a win over me thanks to some help which will always be an asterisk next to that match because of it. But even their, you didn’t finish the job. So here I am, and Toronto is calling. I am calling, I called you out in the match that made you famous. I could have picked a ladder match, I could of have picked a hardcore match, but no, I decided to have you in the match you created. The match you made famous, just one to piss you off, and two, further tarnish your international reputation. Los Angeles put you on the map, Times Square solidified your place in history, Toronto though, however, is where things get derailed for you both personally and professionally. Personally in the fact, we are among our own people, our friends, those who had looked up to us for years and embraced us with open arms. They will see what you truly are. A fraud. The press is going to hound you forever over this. You will lose creditability within governmental circles and that ambassador position you were seeking to your ancestral home when you retire…….poof, history. Your not to blind to have seen the signs already have you. The stock of SI has already dropped 30% thanks to you. Hell, I’ve been getting even richer because of it short-selling it. Personal things aside, professionally though it’s a different story. Should……should you somehow defeat me cleanly, the world is your oyster, if you need help, the world will continue to turn on you. Should I defeat you, nothing really much becomes of it. I gain alittle bit of revenge, but this match, the Battle of Toronto, you have WAY more to lose than I do. Because of that, it’s a win-win situation for me, and a damn tough if not impossible match for you. Funny thing though, you talked about how I would destroy the city just to defeat you, yet you come across as the saviour of all of Canada. Sure……I will destroy the city to defeat you, I don’t deny it. Anything goes in this match, including as you say grabbing some old lady and using her as a weapon. So be it. The difference between you and I has always been real simple. I am who I said I was. You always put on a façade. Even still now. You were surprised at the match. Even alittle bit afraid. Because deep down, you know, like all my feuds previously, I don’t start them, but I dam sure end them, including this one between you and I. Big Daddy Dreamer against The HeartBreak Chick for The Battle of Toronto. No one is safe, no building is secured, nothing, and I mean nothing will ever be the same in our city afterwards, not even you. Not even you. A Dreamanator off the CN Tower will kill the both of us, but, it’s a small price to pay to end it all between us. The city is our battlefield, nothing is safe, nothing is sacred anymore. I didn’t make it this way, but I’ll damn sure end it all, once and for all C. Big Daddy Dreamer is coming home and you and the rest of Canada had better damn well be ready for it………………
