Cold Emotion... part twelve

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens somewhere in the shady streets of Las Vegas where it looks like they have crawled into some kind of ghetto, suburb area. The flashing lights are replaced with the odd garbage can with fire burning from the top of it and the packed streets of tourists are now randomly, wandering homeless people. The scene pans to Morpheus Omega and Hedges walking down the streets with mean scowls on their faces like they are very bothered by their surroundings. Hedges turns to Morpheus Omega. 'What kind of hole are you dragging me into?' Hedges asks rudely. 'Stop your whining you son of a bitch!' Morpheus replies. 'Pardon me?' Hedges questions in return. Both men stare at each other like they are ready to full out throw bombs and battle in the street. Both men breathe deeply and continue walking and do not say anything. Finally Hedges turns again. 'I dare you to repeat what you just said to me. Come on, I dare yah!' Hedges tests the waters by challenging Morpheus with a dare. 'Still sounds like whining to me...' Morpheus proclaims and starts walking more quickly then Hedges with a smirk on his face. With sudden burst of energy and rage, Hedges runs full tilt and checks Morpheus until the wall with a lot of strength. Morpheus remains still a moment and a gasp of air can be heard. Hedges still behind him and holding him against the wall, he screams into Morpheus ear. 'The only one whining will be Shocka on thursday night, Sucka!' Hedges with a disturbing laugh and he turns to start walking again. Without thinking twice, Morpheus turns and picks Hedges up by the sides of his head and off the ground. Morpheus turns and throws him against the wall and stares him down with a mean look in his eyes. 'Fuck you!' Morpheus replies angered, but shortly after a chuckle of laughter can be heard from him; like you know he was joking. The former resurrection members parade down the street and walk past a group of three homeless people, who are gathered around their ghetto fire place with lost looks on their faces. The one dashes quickly behind Morpheus and the man cannot be seen except one arm around his throat and a piece of sharp glass being held to the side of his head. 'No one moves or I will stab and kill this faggot ass, bitch!' The homeless man can be heard yelling from behind Morpheus. 'Excuse me piss stain?' Hedges questions with a very surprised look on his face, he continues 'You have to be joking me right?' Another homeless man approaches and gets in the face of Hedges and has a bad ass look on his face besides the caked layer of dirt and his scruffy beard. The man looks far up into the eyes of Hedges. 'You haven't heard? Bum Fight Krew is sweeping the nation and we will not put up with anyone's shit for no one. Give us all your valuables and we may decide to spare your lives!' The man screams with a maggoty tone of voice and serious look on his face. 'Huh' Hedges screams while doing a double take and like he is lost in world of confusion, he continues 'What did you just say?' he asks. 'I saiiiiiii' the word trails off because the man is picked up spine busted right through the flaming garbage can. The can crumples upon impact and fire spreads from underneath the man's body. Before the other man has any time to react, Morpheus flips him over his head from behind and spins him around. Morpheus twists the mans wrist and throws the sharp glass object behind him. Morpheus screams out of rage and hits a wicked DDT that makes the bum's head smash off the hard, concrete sidewalk with impact. The other bum runs yelling loudly. 'They took out the leader! Everyone scatter, I think those men are the spawn of the devil!' he screams like it was for dear life. Getting back to his feet, Morpheus is pulled back up by the extended arm of Hedges. They both look down at the pathetic men who lie hopeless on the ground with their hopes and dreams crushed of being like Rufus the stunt bum from BFK the movie. Both men continue walking down the road like this never actually happened with big smiles on their faces. 'So... You ready to take one hell of a round out of Newton?' Hedges questions Morpheus with a funny look on his face. 'Ready? More then you would know it. I hate that bitch almost as badly, as I hate Shocka! Temporary releaving of frustration I guess' he shakes his head like he knows Newton and Shocka have found themselves in very bad ways. 'Its funny because you stated how Shocka and Newton have something in common and also because when you finish Stunna's career at the ppv, he will end up like those jokers we laid out back there' Hedges says with a big grin on his face and laughs psychotically. 'I think you forgot one thing though, the homeless pieces of shit back there could walk... When I am done with jonathon he will be luckily if he is only paralyzed from the waist down! It will be all about the downhill, shopping cart riding for him... Weeeeeeeee!' Morpheus screams with this crazed look on his face, he veers side to side walking quickly with loud laughter heard from both men. The scene fades.
