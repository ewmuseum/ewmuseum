The Unforgiven II

15 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th January 2003 Hospitals are a place that not many people like. Some people don’t like the smell, the long corridors, the thought of the fact that there are people in there dying, but to Asis it’s just people. He’s not the greatest at getting along nicely, and hospitals are crammed full of people. All the time. And when people try to tell you what to do, even though you could normally quite easily reach out and break their necks, it becomes even more annoying. The fact that just because you’re injured someone else has complete control over your actions, it’s not a nice feeling. Asis would have been out of that hospital straight away, but he’s not stupid, he knew how dangerous neck injuries can be, he knew that if he didn’t let these people do their job, there was a possibility he could die. Of course he didn’t know that he could have got up and walked out of the hospital, it wouldn’t have been life threatening, just would have caused him a serious amount of pain. But hey, that’s something he’d spent his life in. And now that he was out, he planned on letting all of eVo know about it, especially certain individuals. One of them being Strictly Prohibited, the man that seems to have some kind of vendetta with Unlimited. But he will soon learn that picking a fight with Unlimited is bad enough, but picking a fight with Asis is worse at the best of times, let alone when he doesn’t want to play… (The location does not matter. It could be a warehouse, it could be a hotel room, it could be a back alley, it could be an abandoned theatre hall, it could be a building that has been scheduled for destruction, these are all places that Asis has spoken to the eVo cameras from. But right now, this time, he won’t let them show where they are. Maybe he does not feel it’s important for everyone to know, he might think that he wants to say is too important for anyone to care about where he is. Or maybe he has another reason for being so secretive. But unless he tells us another day, we’ll never know…) A leave of absence it’s called. The time when you’re away from your place of work I call it hell. Evolution is not only my place of work; it’s my life. I don’t mean that in the “I’d do anything for this company and anything for my profession” kind of way, I mean it literally is my life. I have nothing else, eVo is all I have. No family, no friends, just a place where I can do what I want and not have to suffer consequences that I would out on the streets. Hurting people was all I was ever good at, all I could ever do, and that is the sole reason I joined this business. People like Strictly Prohibited don’t seem to realise that. You talk about your prophecy and your valley of lost souls and you’re very metaphorical. So what I’m going to say is something that you should be able to understand. I’m going to kick the shit out of you. It’s just that simple. I’ve had enough of mind games and going the long way around getting to each other, once we get in that ring it’s just me and you, and about 20 minutes of pain. There will be no trickery with the lights, no spray paint and no goddamn kendo stick. It will just be you stuck in the ring with a man that you do not comprehend. You’ve gotten yourself into a very bad situation here, because you don’t know what you’re up against. You think that you can challenge Unlimited because it makes you feel big about yourself? It will be the worst mistake of your wretched life. And thinking that I’m nothing to be up against? The second biggest. (A sharp intake of breath can be heard, and of what you can see of Asis’ face behind his straggly hair there seems to be a flinch of pain, as his neck spasms slightly.) You may be right though, I’m not in the best condition of my life. My neck still hurts from Nightmare Before Christmas and the doctors have told me I shouldn’t be wrestling, but I’ve had worse. Besides I hardly think it’s going to matter when I go up against you. Even if you do get the upper hand and focus on my neck, it’s not going to benefit you. It will just fuel my rage, fuel my anger, and you will suffer for it. You say I should sleep with my nightlight on? Why? Should I fear you? Should I worry that you’re going to hurt me? SP I fear no man, especially not someone that gives up over 60lbs in hate and suffering to me. And I’m going to make that count at Legends. See I’ve been in one of these bullshit tournaments before. A tournament to see who is the best and who deserves the label of being a “champion.” To wear a belt round your waist to prove that you’re better than other people. I didn’t ask to be put in that tournament and I didn’t ask to be put in this one either. But SP, I won the last one. I became the first Rising Star Champion in eVolution, not because I wanted to the most, or because I worked the hardest for it, but purely because nobody could stop me. I kicked everyone’s ass, just because I was the most dominant force in the tournament. And not for personal glory, nor for the title of being a “champion”, but for Unlimited, I’m going to win this tournament as well. The more titles Unlimited holds, the more political power we hold within eVo. But it’s more than just the glory of Unlimited SP. It’s the pleasure of being able to take you apart, to rip the flesh from your body, make you taste your own blood, make you scream out in pain and know that you made a mistake. And only when those screams die out from unconsciousness, only then will I stop your suffering. I will prolong it for as long as I can, because this is no longer just for Unlimited, this is personal SP. I will make you suffer, I will make you pay, for I am The Demonic and you’ll know never again to cross my path with your ignorance…
