I Found Your #, It's 1-800-Xander-Sucks!

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-03 'nVc is leaning against a car window, with his shades covering his eyes. nVc is all dressed in normal cloths and walking into the Unbreakable arena. nVc shakes hands and high fives people who want to see him. He walks to his dressing room and sets down his bags on the coffee table.' Nik VanCore: Yea, been awhile since I asked for some T.V time isn't it? Xander, time after time you seem to just get in my way, when you know when push comes to shove Im gonna beat you at Unbreakable! I'm sick of you and your little group coasting me matches when I have them won. Xander, I maybe not the same Nik VanCore that beat man after man in SHOW. I might not might be the same guy who won time after time in $W, but one thing will not change....I will beat you at Unbreakable! Nik VanCore: You know, months ago I had a mask on, and you know I beat you with a kendo stick almost every week Xander. Did you forget that? Oh yes, I beat you with a kendo stick, spray painted your ugly mug also. Hell I even saw HBC naked in the shower? How many guys can say they saw that? Wait...I can! Nik VanCore: Now, I team with Team eVoluion! I'm gonna make a difference here in eVo. I'm gonna clean it of scum like you Xander! Wait, calling you scum is a disgrace to scum...I'll call you an asshole instead....wait that would insult assholes to..oh well I'll let you have your own little catagory? How's that soung? Nik VanCore: I know what will happen at Unbreakable Xander..I'm gonna pin your shoulders to the mat 1,2,3! End of story..now get out of my face! 'nVc pushes the cameraman out of his dressing room.' END
