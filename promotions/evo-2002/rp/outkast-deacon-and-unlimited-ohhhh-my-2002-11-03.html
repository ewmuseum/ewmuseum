Outkast, Deacon, and Unlimited ohhhh my......

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'I HATE YOU!!!!!!' The voice of Xavier Citizen rings out in an otherwise quiet room. Wisdom is in the corner meditating as a slight smirk comes across his face..... LW:'Stop looking at that poster of Heartbreak Chick.....' Xavier Citizen puts down the poster and heads over to his tag team partner.... XC:'You don't understand....it's because I have such love for her that I can hate her as much as I do. In order to hate you must have a great deal of love; to hate without love is....well stupid.' Luther stops his meditating.... LW:'Focus on our match this week....the n on HBC later.' XC:'You mean our punishment for our actions of last week. Those same actions which go unspoken of because of the simple fact...we broke script. What can I not...get myself over too? Am I not NOTORIOUS enough....am I not INFAMOUS enough, that I can't do what needs to be done to finish the job? Must I escalate the amount of violence to make a point around here.....it seems that may have to be the case. You have two factions warring over a legacy that is not even theirs.....You see if any man, any individual was to put the wounded dog which is the Unlimited to sleep; it should be ME!!!! IT's MY RIGHT. But then again.....who would get over if I did the job? So we get resigned to dark match status....a status I ironically held a few weeks ago and still....STILL found a way onto Evolution television. Last week I was lying face first in a pool of my own blood and The 'Unlimited' had the nerve to actually be proud of that moment. Asis and Jinx actually thought they did something. Which shows me one thing....Xander is the only one of you who can actually hold an intelligent thought in your head; for he realizes I'm just toying with you for the moment. Xander is NOT as dumb as he looks; and even he must realize That We could have ended the Unlimited if we wanted to. Twice already we got the proverbial drop on you...once at Faith the other at Legends. Asis, Jinx ask yourself......why put HBC on display knowing you were still able to retaliate? It's because like a Pavlovian dog you responded.....better than I had hoped, the first two times merely served as an introduction....the third time my playthings, will it be the charm?' Luther Wisdom arises as Xavier goes back to looking at the HBC poster..... LW:'So it seems we stepped on a few toes last week. I question....why is it we get the blame for The Unlimited and Notorious lack of inititive. You see as much as The Unlimited is a 'force' the Notorious has proven just as ineffective; in fact they are enablers to The Unlimited...giving them an inferior product to war against. Sure a simple mind your own business would be applicable in this situation if it wasn't for one thing.....The Unlimited is our business, after all who do you think originated the concept? So as much as we would like to end this experiment gone wrong......we will allow you to take your best shot against the Unlimited. Word of advice Frost, don't fumble the ball......or you may not like what happens when we pick it up.' Luther walks to the kitchen and grabs a Pepsi..... 'So we face an Outkast and a Deacon in a dark match. Funny because Outkast was at one time a talent before he squandered it away by rendering himself unimportant in the growing creature which is Evolution wrestling and Deacon....well at least he will show up. I would go on about how we will beat the two to a pulp but lets face it, the outcome based on talent alone is a foregone conclusion. Ahhhhh but our night ladies and gentlemen won't end there, you see we are officially unofficially in this Unlimited War...but which side do we assist? You see X here really feels the need to end the Unlimited himself and the upstart Notorious is a group that is just in the way. Where me on the otherhand....I'd rather see the two factions destroy each other before we finish either one of them off...it's a catch 22. You see I have a reason to hate The Unlimited....yet I don't have one to hate Frost and his gang....yet.' XC:'We hate Frost because Seara is the one who started this ill will between me an Asis.....' Luther Wisdom shakes his head..... LW:'Somehow I don't like the thought process you are following......' XC:'IF the Notorious want to take my light....why not give them a glimps of darkness?' Xavier Citizen walks off; Luther shakes his head before dialing his cell phone..... LW:'Malcolm.....we have a problem....'
