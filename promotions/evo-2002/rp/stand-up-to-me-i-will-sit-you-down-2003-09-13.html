Stand up to me..I will sit you down!

13 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-13 A Pre-Recorded tape that has finally arrived to eVo headquaters. This was recorded some time shortly after Legends 49. The scene opens backstage as walking out of a locker room door is Tok'n,the man formally known as Jason Relms. Tok'n begins to make his way towards the parking lot area as eVo crew followed him yelling all kinds of questions like,'What did you fake your death?Do you hate eVo?What is you status with Perry Quinn?', just to name a few.The questions seem not to fase him one bit. He just has his bags on his shoulders showing the cameras no attention. As he began to make his way to the parking lot he says.. 'Are you ignorant?' The eVo press is taken back a little bit. But they still followed him. Getting impatient Tok'n says.. 'Ignorance is the name of this companys camera crew. You assholes are so stupid that I think I need to draw a map for you guys to get a good picture to go away. But you know what..I do want to say something..' As he keeps walking he smiles a bit and says.. 'You know, I really got eVo good tonight. They talked about evolving. They wanted some one to make a true evolution. Well I have done just that. I have went from a man who listens to what the corperate world said to do. Who made a show for each and every person every time I walked to the ring. The man who once held eVo Tag Gold. And now what was I doing.Nothing, just trying to carry people like Perry Quinng,High Impact and Luther Wisdom on my back. You people make me sick. I look at them, and I actually feel some puke rolling up my throat. But then the only image that makes me stop thinking of that, is pictureing those tag belts around my shoulder. Around the man who built this tag divison. I have been a camera man from day one. I have been a crowd pleaser. I was the first aWf universal champ, but yet I was never shown any respect when I walked in eVo headquaters. You see Jason Relms was nothing but a man who ran his mouth when the cameras turned on and when they shut off he went silent..Now..' He chuckles a bit as he took his bags and re arranged them on his shoulder for a better comfort feelning as he continues to say.. 'Tok'n...He is one that will be noticed. He will be the man that will reach Tag stardom very soon.So that you people make no mistake, I will not shut my mouth when those lights turn off. Damn it, it is time people listen to me. But not just the fans. It's time for the worl including people who worck for evolution to listen, and take some notes while they are at it.This time of playing around is over. You pussy of evolution better realise that you too can not run from me. Everyone has a target..but there will only be one mark..that is the tag belts..' He reaches his black hummer as he goes to put his bags in the hummer but some of the eVo crew begin to get in his way a bit. They then jump away from his door as he made the remark.. 'Get the fuck out the way..' He then gets in his hummer and drives off leaving the camera crew stunned by the new attitude of the one who calls himself Tok'n. Tok'n address... ~~~These people are not ready. They believe they have seen great champions come through..but yet they have seen nothing to what I will bring. Sure I was already a tag champ. But that was when I carried the team. I plan on getting some one that will be able to put his part in too. And as a warning for the rest of you who watch the tag divison..for the rest of you who wrestle in the tag divison, get ready. As you do not know what is about to approach you. You are not ready to endure what Tok'n will bring you. Sure it will be entertainment. Sure it will be worth more than your price of admission. But its aftermath will be one that can never be forgotten... Now when it come to Legends 50 a milestone will be reached. As you people want to see the best wrestling in the world..that night you will see just that..and more. Shaft, you dont want to enter that ring. You do not want to step in the same arena as myself, much less the same damn ring. But i guess it will happen.I guess you are stupid enough to just that. Ignorance will be the answer of this though. Just like those camera men.You are both oblivious to the fact. That though I am the best entertainment fans could want. The pain, the tears that come with what I will do to the roster will be unbearable..or should I say...let the rise of the new foundation begin..as something is getting a face lift..and The Industry is looking like it's definition...Get in it's way, and you will fucking wish you didn't.. fuck the legends of what eVo was..welcome what eVo is going to be.. Tok'n has spoken~~~
