Stained with failure, but Rendezvous with Revenge

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 17, 2002 Damnation, you always follow me without letting me go. First the loss of my friend, then the loss of my match. You've followed me through my entire time of exsistence, when will you ever let me go into my sanctuary? The day I die? I doubt you'll let me go then. I loss my father, then my mother, then my younger brother. What next? Me? No, you'll take all that you can from me before you put me out of exsistence. I hate you. I hate you Damnation. I will always hate you, but I have to live with you. Why won't you leave me alone, you have me cradled into a corner, like a newborn child in the hospital, like an afraid animal, with a Franch Spa 45 pointed right infront of it's head. Damnit, leave me be. Let me find my rest, my sanctuary, let me atleast get a breath of wind before you clamp me down with your vice grip. I hate you damnation. I hate you. I will for eternity. It'll never stop, it'll never stop, not until I drop, and my time loses the clock. I'll hate you damnation until the day I die. When I forget how to cry, and I can't decypher the sky. When the sky finally dies, and God learns how to cry. My soul will stay stained. Failure conjures the stain. I can't take the pain. It's driving me insane. What's this at the door? Opprotunities? Jason Steele my opponent. My tag team partner for a moment. The man who took my chance at gold. Why? Why was it him, and not Jinosyde the bold? I can't stand you damnation, you rot my soul. From my lovely youth, to the day I grow old. Will you ever leave me? Ever leave my soul? Your vicegrip just holds me.. forever, for ever. Please leave me be, let me find my sanctuary in heaven. Don't drag me down to death, with the rest of my dreams. I don't have the time to fall in between. Damnation, I hate you, so much and it grows with galore. Damnation, i'll fight you, forever, forever more. I've sat here for what feels like an eternity, but according to the small, white numbers at the far corner of the glowing monitor, it has only been about fifteen minutes. It must be about that time of year again; the thoughts become cloudy and the mind is blocked from any direct path of decision, much like the sun is kept from spreading its rays of light by the arrival of dense, cumulus clouds preceding a storm. 'Storm..' The word rolled off of my lips, allowing the outside world to taste my inner thoughts for a few faint seconds before they dissipate and return the room to its previous state of restful silence. However, much to the world's incongnizance, the thought correlated to that single-worded enunciation is much, much more. It's massive form rests within the caverns of my mind, growing more each passing second. At last, as it forms into a definite, indentifiable entity, I lean forward and allow it to escape. 'Hrmm.' My fingers moved into immediate action, darting from one key to the next across the compact keyboard, connected to the nearby $5000 dollar work of technilogical art. My mind questioned this: Is that it? Was that even a word? Could this possibly be all that this supposed deep thought has spawned? I responded: No, it isn't. My mouth arched upward in somewhat of a half smile before returning to its stable position, a flat line across my face, which some tend to refer to as a grimace, but in all honesty, it is just me being me. Words erupt onto the screen as my eyes darted from side to side, quickly scanning over the available information on one Jason Steele. The Canadian, huh? Glancing at his mugshot, it would seem quite evident that this title fits him well. He has that look about him, one which practically screams naivety. He's not been tapped by the harsh reality of the world around him. However, I wouldn't dream of ripping his eyes open; it is simply not my place. Unfortunately for him, this refrainment won't keep me from bring about harm in any other way. '253, huh? Damn, someone took advantage on the whole steroid wave.' I cocked my head to the right side as an amusing thought sparks. 'Heh, either that or you just got too involved in the entire ordeal, therefore explaining the absence of mass on your part. Yeahhh, that would also explain the man boobs.' My finger taps against the monitor, pointing out the grinning Steele's perky chest. Oh well, in any case, he shan't be too much of a problem, physically anyway. I've not really looked into his past just yet, but I am almost 100% assured there will be nothing of much splendor in it. The click of a mouse button later, my presumption is proved to be true. He comes from a bunch called the Steeles. Not much to say there, I see. They're one of those families that was raised with a certain predisposition in their family tree. It was almost definitely brought about by an elder in the family, who was either highly successful, or not successful at all, which in turn would cause him to either push his offspring to follow in his footsteps or makeup for his failure. Basically, they are drones, programmed at a very early age to fulfill a task. In this case, it would be to become a wrestler. 'How...sad.' I couldn't help but laugh a little at this concept. I mean, I myself am what you might call a wrestler, but this is purely by choice. I find it entertaining, to some extent, the crowd yelling for a figure they don't even know, performers preparing for their ten minutes of glory, and owners beaming with delight at earning a pretty penny. It's like watching a couple of kids play tag. The overall act is pointless, and to see them actually gain some sort of happiness from it all is amusing. What musters up a good laugh over this is the fact that Jason here needs this activity. It has been ground into his brain, and he cannot really ever escape it, even if he wanted to. He's a prisoner, destined (or doomed?) to play this eternal game of tag. The best part is he doesn't even know it. I shook my head and commenced reading more into Jason's biography. Ah, this only gets better and better by the minute. Not only is he a worthless drone, but he's not even good at completing his task. He's done nothing but win a few second-rate titles, one being the tag titles, which in my opinion proves nothing about a wrestler. I could go out and find any random person, train a bit, then win these chunks of waste. It's all based on chance, and we all know chance can never be claimed as one's own acheivement, or at least we should anyway. 'This is gonna be easier than I thought.' This guy is such a joke! I wonder if he actually places himself on some sort of pedestal, simply because he is a Steele. Heh, that would be funny if he did. That plastic frown of his mug much proves that he is at the very least proud to be what he is. If I were to shine reality into those unseeing eyes of his, I wonder if they'd pop. 'No, Aaron, it's not your place.' Oh well, it's still fun to think about.. [interruption] All of a sudden, my attention was torn away from the monitor as a loud knock echoed throughout the apartment. I raised an eyebrow and turned toward the door, which almost seems as if it is calling for me to advance toward it. 'Who the..' My mind then shoots back to an earlier phone call I made. 'Right, the pizza..' I pushed myself up from the chair, my triceps flexed beneath my black tight fitting, polyester shirt. As I stood straight up, I became light headed for a few seconds. I rubbed my eyes, trying to push away the feeling of dizziness. 'Arr...gotta get away from this computer for a bit anyway.' After saying this, I turned toward the door, my heavy footsteps echoing throughout the otherwise vacant apartment. Stepping up to the door, I pressed my eye against the tiny peephole, catching sight of an acne covered teenager, holding a red case in the air. I grunted, then reached for the door handle. [lapse] 'De-lish.' With a sharp smack, my lips closed in around the hot, oozing cheese. I let out a sigh of satisfaction, savoring every morsel of melted mozarella before finally swallowing it with a loud gulp. It is always best to order a pizza with extra cheese. The excess grease is a small price to pay for the absolute divinity each bite brings the mind, body, and soul. 'Heh.' I laughed to myself as I realized what a dork I could be. Come on now, worshiping pizza? What will be next, running around some sort of ring with tights on, throwing grown, sweaty men all over the place? 'Ugh.' Grimacing, I tried to block the previous thought out. I don't want to think about my profession in that light. It's disgusting, seriously. I often find myself wondering if some of those other guys actually know what a shower is. I mean, I can see smelling a little raunchy after the match, but some of those fools come to the ring stinking like ball sweat before the match. I'd hate to be the two cent whore who goes down on one of them. 'Ech.' I shuddered, trying to block out the thought, but it is to no avail. Somehow, the pizza didn't look quite as tempting anymore. Oh well, I need to cut down on my fat intake anyway. Placing the pizza back into the box from whence it came, I closed the box and lifted it off of the smooth, black countertop. I then turned and proceeded in the direction of the silver oven on the otherside of the kitchen. With one final step, I found myself standing within inches of the oven. I started to reach for the handle with my free hand, the pizza box resting atop the other, but paused as I caught a glimpse of my reflection in the shiny surface of the oven. Dark circles are starting to form underneath my tired eyes. I blinked, then feel a rush of exhaustion pass over me. How long has it been since I slept last? Shit, I can't remember. Whatever, I have work to do before I can rest. In one swift motion, I jerked open the oven door, slide the pizza inside, and fliped the dial to warm. That should keep it pleasant until my appetite comes back. With a loud slam, I fliped the door shut, and turn away from the contraption. Time to get back on task. Rubbing my eyes, I began the slow journey back to the computer desk, which sits against the northeastern wall in the living room. I glanced over at it, and couldn't help but frown. In all honesty, I could care less about what this shmuck Steele has accomplished throughout his life, let alone the fact that he even exists. Reading about him is like reading a children's story. No matter how different the set of main characters, or setting is, the plot is always the same. A simple man, with a simple life, and more than likely a simple death, he'll never really amount to anything more than what has already been determined for him, and that is... 'Ball sweat.' A smile creeped across my face as I stared down at Jason's picture, and thoughts his personal hygeine (or lack thereof) began to bounce around inside of my head. I'm sure he is just like those other smelly fools I have had to put up with in the past. Oh well, I will find that out first hand at the conclusion of this week, though I am all but excited to do so. 'Whatever.' I took hold of the black office chair just in front of me, pulling it out from underneath the desk, then proceeding to seat myself atop it. I slid myself in and immediately begin scrolling farther down the page devoted solely to my stinky opponent. Hey wait, I didn't see.. 'Oh fuck..' Seraph Ahriman Lucifer? Seraph?? The name pounded in and out of my head, causing waves of intense confusion to pulsate throughout my entire body, starting at the very center of my brain and working their way out to the very edge of my fingertips. The palms of my hands pressed tightly against the desk, causing it to creak underneath my weight as I hunched over the keyboard. A cold sweat began forming across my forehead, dripping down the side of my face, and then onto the keyboard like tiny rain drops. 'Storm..' For the second time this evening, that word exited my mouth. However, this time it has much more meaning behind it. In the dark caverns of my mind, the 'storm' has finally hit. There comes a time when one must break out of his shell of security, turning his inner thoughts into a new form of energy. As the sun's vibrant energy is cut off right before any tempest, it is soon thereafter replaced with a new, more violent type of energy, coming in the form of rain, snow, hail, sleet, or whatever else it is mother nature deems appropriate. My time to transform is now. It has been quite awhile since I have faced these memories from the past head on. After that rather unfortunate neck injury I sustained, it took much physical therapy to recover, but even more personal mental therapy to block out what had happened. And what had happened? 'Ha.' I remember now...that bitch. I have no pity for any of them now. What was once my mission of mercy has now ceased, exploding into an inferno of hate and rage. I no longer want to enlighten, but instead destroy. They'll pay for being such insolent fools. She robbed me of my purity, now I will rob them of their souls. 'Ahh..' Smiling and taking in a deep breath, my blood pressure began to drop. The heavy beating of my heart slowed down to a gentle thud. Sedation flowed through my veins now as I gradually lifted my head up from its position, hunched over the keyboard. Now, where was I? Seraph. Yes, that was it. How could I have forgotten about all of this? Jeeze, I didn't realize what a perfect job I did in pushing out the past. Ah well, can I honestly expect any less out of myself, seriously now? Whatever, I do recall where this name came into my life before. It was not too long ago, when NEW was sinking under. I had to face this man in the Destination Tournament. To make a long story short, he beat me. Being one of the few who can actually claim to hold a victory over me, I hold a decent amount of respect of him; that doesn't mean I don't wish a painful death upon him. His success is my failure, and for that, I can never praise him. 'Ugh.' Shaking my head in disgust, I forced myself to stop thinking about Seraph. I need to stay on target going into my match with his brother, and cannot afford to fuck this up. For Christ’s sake, this match is for a shot at the world title. Just thinking about allowing that shot to fall into my opponent’s hands is enough to cause me great nausea. There is no way in hell I am letting Jason slip past me. As far as I am concerned, he is nothing but a wannabe and does not even really deserve to be in this match. No matter, I’ll show him his proper place, limp, unconcious on the mat. Maybe then he’ll grow a brain and move on to something he is actually good at, though I wouldn’t expect such an intelligent move to come from a worthless robot such as him. 'Whatever.' Leaning forward, I continue with my examination of the man in question. His bio is really fucking boring. After reading for about ten more minutes, I could feel exhaustion sneaking up on me. I could barely keep my eyes open, and can therefore no longer concentrate on anything I am reading. It is all a blur now. 'I don’t care about this anymore. I know I’ll win…this guy sucks.' I arose to my feet, switching off the monitor and dousing the room in darkness. I suppose I should get some rest, but in doing so, what would I truly be accomplishing? Sleep is empty, just like everything else. I need to train more. Argh, but this sleepiness is really starting to get to me. Maybe there is a Dunkin’ Donuts open somewhere. It can’t hurt to find out. Rubbing my eyes one final time, I turned toward the doorway. Taking a few long strides, I advanced in that direction, trying my best not to step on any unknown objects in the dark, though I don’t know why this is even a concern of mine. I kept my place outrageously clean. Just last week, I... 'What the fuck?' Why am I even thinking about this? It has no relevance to anything. I need to stop this mind wandering bullshit. There is no way in hell I am going to fall into a foolish rut like most of the eVolution roster. They’re all idiots who don’t give a shit about anything that is important. The only ones with a true grip on reality are William Forth and Scott Frost. I will be one who succeeds. It starts this Friday, and will continue on until I get exactly what I want. Feeling the cold metal of the door handle, I grasped it and turned it to the right. Stepping through the doorway, I soon found myself outside of my apartment. The door slammed behind me as I turned to lock it. After this, I began walking down the long hallway, headed toward the row of elevators at the end of the hall. Increasing my pace a bit, I began biting my lip. I have quite a long journey ahead of me when you think about it. .fade.to.eternity.
