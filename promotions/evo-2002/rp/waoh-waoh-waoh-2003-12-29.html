Waoh, Waoh, Waoh!

29 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-29 'I've listened to the whinning and crying of Ethan M Coles. Talking about me being a tool for this business.' 'Isn't it sad when someone you don't even know is jealous of your success?' 'Isn't it pointless for them to continue to bring my name up numerous amounts of times, just because the higher up's chose me over him.' 'I mean, look at him. Then look at me.' 'This is a computer nerd, setting out to exact some strange revenge on the internet. Almost a stalker to the company. Constantly putting a farce infront of a name, and staring at the eVolution Roster from the eVolution webpage.' 'Then you have me. Pure and utter Charisma. The saint of wrestling. The epitomy of everything is wrestling. Johnny Charisma. The women love me, and the men love to hate me.' 'Listen to my words, Ethan, because I know you will probably download this off of the eVolution website and add it to your cyber library of things to remember about things you can never have or be. I am the greatest thing that has ever happened to this company! I put out Scott Frost! I put out Jason Jameson. I came, and then I leaped to the top wrungs of the latter of success and in that leap, I flew over you. And you are just made.' 'Call me concieded. Call me, arrogant. I don't give a fuck what anybody thinks about me. But, I think you have a problem with me. Because you are nothing compared to me.' 'Analyze that, you space-age computer geek.' -Charisma
