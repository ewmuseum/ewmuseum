Telling the truth

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-22 We open the scene with a dark room where only one light shines. The light shines onto a chair which has it’s back turned to the camera. Slowly, Thug roC turns around in it, wearing a New-TV shirt and gold chain around his neck. “They just don’t realize it. So I’ll paint the picture. The crowd is on their feet, the cameras all flash rapidly. At every impact, the fans all oohhh and ahhh throughout the match. Finally, when the music hits at the end of the match, the crowd goes wild. They cheer and cheer constantly because their favorite star just took home the Big W. That’s exactly what happens when New-TV is on the air. The four of us, truly are what you all need to get use to seeing. We are the New Era of eVolution, and whenever we’re on the screen, the ratings go straight to the top. That’s why we are New-TV. Don’t believe me? Then just look at the facts. First, we have the newest member of New-TV, Hedges, the fastest up and comer currently in eVo. He’s been here for such a short amount of time, yet he has already made himself a strong contender for the evolved Title. Whereas you have guys running around her for months that haven’t seen gold since they shook hands with Mr. T. Then we have Twisted Reality, Morpheus Omega and Sky Night. Without a doubt, both competitors have the tag team division on lockdown. Even tag team veterans in Big Daddy Dreamer and HBC have problems taking them out. We examples of that these past two weeks. Sky Night, obviously the deadliest women competing here in eVo have shown exactly what she can do each and every week. Omega is out there each and every week, destroying whatever and whoever gets in his way. Just ask Shocka if you can’t remember. Then you have me, Thug roC, the best damn cruiserweight in the business. I’ve done my time in the front office, and that’s when I created this great team of athletes. And now, as a wrestler, I keep the team together with my leadership. Just look at the past 3 months since my return to the ring. I’ve already defeated Scott Frost, threw Shocka through the door of a cage, and pinned Jason Jameson. That’s the past 3 Universal Champions, all dominated by me, r-o-C. Does that not paint the picture any more clearly for you? Does that not just show exactly what I’m about? But no, it’s not good enough for you bastards. You all still doubt me, including Jameson, a guy who has no room to talk. The way I see it, if you’re laying on your back after being pinned by someone, you shouldn’t want another chance to end up your back. And that’s exactly what Jameson has earned himself. Both of us will go at it at From the Ashes, and I’ll tell you exactly what’s going to happen. I will walk in there as the greatest cruiserweight in the business, I will prove it in the ring, and when the smoke is all clear, Jameson will be staring at the lights while I walk out the victor. You can all doubt me if you want to, but you’ve all done that before. And there’s not a time that my promises haven’t came through. I told you all that I was going to whoop Shocka down to size, and I did. I, one of the smallest in this company went toe to toe in a cage with that big Neanderthal. I told you all that Jameson would pay, and he did. He paid with his loss column when I pinned his shoulders on Legends a couple weeks ago. And now I’m telling you this, Jameson won’t win at From the Ashes. Instead, he will have nothing but respect for me after I beat it into him. And don't act like it won't happen, I'm simply telling the truth' Thug roC turns back around in his chair as the scene fades to black.
