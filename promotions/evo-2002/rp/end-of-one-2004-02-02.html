End of one.

2 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-02-02 ~Untouchable…Shocka thought of himself as of one of the top competitors in eVolution wrestling…no this is not a year in the past…when Shocka was indeed the man who was on top. Fending off Scott Frost, after winning the Universal title. You know what…those times weren’t so fucking long ago. And you know what else? They could be about to happen again…something has been awakened. A beast of sorts, a man coming with a purpose now…instead of drifting match to match not carrying about winning or losing. Xander you sparked something that cannot be reversed. You saw a glimpse of it at Legends but there is much more to come my friend. You realize that you are now on a list similar to the one the PPC crossed when Shocka was first unleashed in eVolution. Xander, you are about to find out what Shocka is truly still capable of…he is not a weakling under the control of some being. He is a free man, roaming the plains for one reason, to watch you bleed to death.~ ~This was his daily routine he thought to himself as he stood in line at the grocery store, I bet he could take everyone in this store. The boy bagging groceries, the old lady checkers, the teenager sweeping…damn skippy. And hell look at the customers…is this what a normal person thought about at the store? Shocka looked around glancing at other people…what were they thinking? Probably about there family…or about the great/horrible sex they had last night. They probably didn’t even notice the greatness that was just feet away.~ ~He stepped forward…holy shit…was this a step in the right direction…what in the fuck music were they playing…couldn’t they get some decent tunes in this fucking place. This shit annoyed him almost as much as Xander…as soon as that word crossed his mind his eyes shifted glaring straight ahead now, his grin faded.~ “Shallow skin, I can paint with pain I mark the trails on my arms with your disdain Everyday it's the same - I LOVE, YOU HATE But I guess I don't care any more... Fix my problems with the blade While my eyes turn from blue to gray God, the worst thing happened to me today But I guess I don't care anymore…” ~”Everything Ends” Slipknot, he related everything in his life to music…he now came back to reality.~ ‘Sir…sir!’ The clerk was ready for him but he was in a far away place lost in thought. ~He stepped forward dropping his items and looked over the lady…how long had he stood there? OH well it didn’t matter now…I wonder if when the fans thought of him they thought of him doing this…on this day.~ .fade to darkness.
