7 days

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 18 Tier’s of Evolution. Two cages, the Universal Title suspended above the top cage. No rules, no boundaries. Two men, men who have nothing but hatred between them. The only thing they have in common is their desire for the recognition that comes with the Universal Title. Controversy surrounds them. For months now, back and forth they have attacked each other. Tried to prove dominance. Chairs, barbed wire, fire and cages have been used. The victims have been pushed aside, sides have been taken. But regardless of all the other people that could become involved in this massive Main Event at the Rise of the Legends, it really comes down to two men. Two men with nothing but hatred for each other. Sovereign, and L2J, the co-universal champions of Evolution Wrestling. In the end the question at hand today is what to make of this match? The Tier’s of Evolution match, that debuted months ago in a brutal contest between King, Xander and Christoff left many mouths open in shock, horror and entertainment. We’ve seen Sovereign and L2J in a variety of contests here in Evolution. But this time, inside a double cage structure many are thinking.... does L2J have a chance? Does Sovereign have a chance? How even is it? Who’s the underdog? Most would instantly point at L2J and ask him why on earth would he want Sovereign inside an environment especially after what happened inside the House of Pain. Most then would respond and say, well L2J didn’t lose that match. Then the counter argument is, well Sovereign didn’t either, it was Sovereign that cost himself that match so to speak. Of course in sport sides will battle back and forth. And while the majority of people that watch our product will cheer L2J to their deaths over his adversary Sovereign several things have to be taken into consideration. First, Sovereign is undefeated in singles competition in eVo. He’s the longest reigning Universal Champion in history, including Edw, he’s been the only champion in Evo, up until Cameron Griffin’s highly debated ruling that put the title half on L2J’s shoulders. L2J, himself has never held a major wrestling championship. Some say he’s a rookie, the man that has everything to prove and nothing to lose. That could very well be correct. However most venture to speculate that L2J’s will is stronger then Sovereign’s. His desire to prove everyone wrong might be enough to defeat the Human Monster. But has Sovereign’s reign run it’s course? Is Sovereign just to much for the 208lbs cruiser weight. Tale of the Tape states physically Sovereign is at a huge advantage. Nearly 80lbs and 3 inches on L2J. Most say Sovereign is the strongest and toughest man in eVo. Without a doubt his willingness to put his body on the line in a match has been proven unprecedented (see the house of pain German Suplex off the top of the cage). Sovereign’s ability to take and dish out pain is his advantage. He’s ended careers of budding and current superstars on the snap of the finger. So what is going to happen at next Sunday’s Pay Per View Event? Legends this past week saw L2J get the last laugh before our epic match next week. L2J’s shocking return after his apparent career ending piledriver was no doubt a shock to Sovereign. L2J’s toughness has never been in question, and when he left the arena with the Universal Championship it has left critics and pundents scratching their heads. That being said, Sovereign is never at a lack for words. As the narrator’s voice trails off we’re left now with a scene. While before the voice over rolled over a zooming image of Sovereign and L2J, part of a promo poster for Road to Legends we now have in front of us a wrestling ring. The room is rather dark, poorly lit and sitting in the corner of the far side of the ring is the co-universal champion Sovereign. You would guess this must be the cellar of his home. Most high profile wrestlers have rings in their homes, and although Sovereign might be “crazy” there is no discounting he’s made his money in this business. His need for material success isn’t the same as most wrestlers, but he’s added this ring to his home as what he would refer to as furniture. Sovereign sits quietly, the black and white image on your screen is classic of eVo Sovereign as still camera crews refuse to visit the Human monster for an interview. Sovereign’s voice cracks the silence... Some would venture to say that I am probably pretty upset over what happened last week at Legends. With L2J returning and attacking me, a giant brawl breaking out, and when the smoke literally cleared L2J left the ring with the Universal Championship belt. I suppose that if I really think about it, yeah that upset me. After everything I’ve went through for that title to still have to share it with that little snake makes me angry. But I was, to be perfectly honest folks very happy that L2J came to the ring that night. To know that he’ll be at Road to Legends, to know that he’s willing to put every last ounce of himself on the line inside the Tier’s of Evolution puts a big wide smile on my face. Two cages L2J, to levels of pure pain and destruction. It’s all the fun we had last much doubled, and I do intend to do what I failed to do last month. I’m not talking about just winning the match. I’m talking about ending your career once and for all. I’m talking about taking you places that you didn’t know existed. I’m talking about hurting parts of your body you didn’t know you had. L2J, you’ve been the thorn in my side for far to long. You’ve sucked onto my life blood and taken my very identity with the Universal Title. You’ve soiled it’s good name. November 24th will end it. That title belt, and all it represents will be one again placed around my waist. Your blood will flow down my fingers, my forearms, to my elbows and drip off of them like sap off a maple tree. You want the Tier’s of Evolution so that you can either go out in a blaze of glory, or the one slight chance you might be able to get to the top first. But, L2J you miss the point. The end of the match isn’t really the focus. It’s what I am going to do to you from bell to bell. By the time one of us makes it that far the other will be dead or near death because you will have to beat me so badly L2J to even have a hope in HELL of getting your fingers on that belt on the 24th of November the year 2002. On that day L2J you and I will go one on one for one more epic slaughter. Everything will literally hang in the balance as the title will be suspended from the roof of the Air Canada Center. The Last time I was in Toronto was my battle with Daniel Somers. Are you prepared to go through that same punishment? Are you willing to take this match anywhere, where anything could happen? I know your little friends are probably going to be watching your back. Making sure the match is fair. And don’t worry L2J, I don’t need help to beat you. I don’t need anything but my own two hands. But all the little toys make it more fun don’t you think? That night in Toronto, a year ago things happened in that famous match that changed two careers. So this match, has a lot more meaning to me then you might have originally thought. Before, you know it was just the “Sovereign wants his title back” and while that remains very true. This time, in the same town, the same arena that I was screwed by Cameron Griffin the first time I refuse to let history repeat itself. I will not leave the arena without my title. I refuse to loose. I refuse to be screwed over again just for the sake of messing with a man that “doesn’t know what is going on all the time”. For the sake of quieting the “monster” that lays within this body. To silence the mad man screaming out of my head. I’m a lot more together then people thought I was back then. I might not have seen it, so much was going on at that time, and it was easy to pull the wool over poor old Sovereign’s eyes. But now... now everything is different. Now I am the champion, or at least I damn well should be the champion. But I’m going to avoid another one of those long rants about what Cammy did after Faith. Because that’s the past. Right now, and in the next week is what matters. What matters is that in a week from today there won’t be any talk about Chambers, or anything like that. They’ll be talking about what the hell L2J is going to do to survive Sovereign. And I’m going to be asking the same thing L2J. What the hell can you do? I know that your ready and willing to try and do everything you can. But it’s not going to be enough. Not this time L2J. Sovereign pulls the hair out of his eyes, now you can fully see his face. He sits up slightly, although just noticeable enough. You’ve held onto this belt with me for a month now L2J. You’ve had your turn to call yourself champion. And even if it was because of a mistake on my part, trying to hard to destroy you, I have tried my best to get along. Well... I did try and break your neck, and although it didn’t work exactly as well as it was intended I got over that real quickly when you screamed out the words Tier’s of Evolution. This truly is Evolution L2J. It’s calling you, and it’s survival of the fittest. The weak will be swept aside. And you’ll be batted off to the side by the back of my hand L2J as I walk through you on Sunday. You will fight, you will do your damn best. And I’m sure, positive that you’re going to hurt me. I don’t expect anything less from you L2J. But You will bleed. You will be thrown around. You will be put through tables, hit with chairs, burnt with flame. I am going to do everything that I can possibly think of to you in the course of that match. I will draw it out, to make your suffering prolonged. I will make you feel like your life is coming to an end before finally pulling the belt from it’s brace hanging from the rafters of the Air Canada Center. With your broken, bloody body laying under me, gasping for air. Clawing yourself to your feet by my wrestling attire then only to realize that your dream of being the Universal Champion was crushed. It would be over, dashed. You’d be a former champion, and not even having the chance, the HONOR to defend it. You are going to get the worst of this title. No defending it, and you will have the dishonor of losing it in a blood bath against the man you’ve vowed to defeat several times now. Your Universal reign is over L2J. It shouldn’t have ever started. But as quickly as Cameron Griffin said that you and I would be Co-Champions, that’s just how quickly it’s going take for it to sink in for you after that final bell rings at Rise of the Legends. When the Ref points at the time keeper, when he raises my arm in victory and not yours. And you’ll look up at me, through the blood, the hair, the lights and see me staring down at you, holding the Universal Title, back in my hands, where it should be! It shouldn’t have ever left. But now, Evolution comes calling you L2J. Sovereign then reaches up, places his hands on the top rope and quickly pulls himself to his feet. His 6'3, 280lbs frame is distinct with his thick muscles, his almost talon like hands. His face, marked with the scars of his past. His eyes, wild, his mouth twitching. The past month no doubt has been testing Sovereign’s resolve. We all know what the Universal Title means to Sovereign, we all know what it means to L2J. Who wants it more L2J? You or me, that’s a loaded question. I know how badly I want it L2J, and I can’t peek into your soul and find out what it all means to you. But I’ll venture to guess that it’s pretty damn important. The vindication of an entire career, building, swirling to this one fateful night. Yeah, that can be said to the both of us. In the biggest match of our careers. In the most brutal, vial match that Evolution has to offer. Kiss your wife an extra time before leaving for the air port L2J. Hug your child just a little tighter when you put the little one to bed the night before. Say an extra prayer at Church. Eat one extra power bar, have one extra work out. Do everything that you can to prepare yourself in every possible way. Teach yourself to ignore the pain L2J. Teach yourself to block it all out. Because it’s the only way that your going to survive. But with all that, your only going to learn that Sovereign is an Unstoppable force. The Unmoveable Object. You can’t stop me L2J. No match, no gang, no weapon is going to stop Sovereign in Toronto Ontario Canada on November 24th. Only an army, armed to the teeth would be able to stop me from getting my claws back on that Title belt. And you aren’t an army L2J. Your just a man. Nothing more, and nothing less. Sovereign leans his weight up on the top ropes as he wraps his hands around the cord. He leans down, looking deep into the camera. It’s early Monday morning L2J. The clock just struck midnight only a few minutes ago. That gives you less then 7 days. 7 days to think about everything you’ve done in your entire career. Then you can ask yourself if it was all worth it. And then the answer is going to be there, when you lay on your back. Laying there, bloody, broken and beaten. You know the answer now L2J. Then that is when the proof will be staring you in the face. And dealing with the truth is easier said then done. Sovereign lets go of the top rope, it shakes with the sudden release. I’ll see you in Toronto L2J. Sovereign walks to the side as the camera fades to black.
