Swimming with the sharks

23 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-23 The Great Barrier Reef Australia Faith has come and went and eventhough it didn’t go The Syndicate’s way, they are more determined than ever before to raise bloody hell on the tag team division and all of Evolution for that matter. Funny thing what a break can do. Besides going on vacation, spending time with friends and family, recuperating from minor nagging injuries, it gives time to plot, plan, and formulate the most deviant of desires. While swimming with the sharks in the Great Barrier Reef, somehow, everything seems all peaceful in the world. The HeartBreak Chick, wearing a most revealing diving suit that would make any Victoria Secret model blush is slowly making her way to the surface, exhaling all the way so that the tiny bubbles don’t disturb the aquatic fish around here. Big Daddy Dreamer, busily holding an underwater camera, films her all the way up and catching the hammerhead off in the distance. Together, they surface and board the dive boat, tossing off their gear, and storing it in the back. HBC takes off her diving suit, showing off her sexy nude body and laying on the sun platform working on her tan. BDD tosses her over a bottled water and sits next to her discussing a few things. “God, its finally nice to get away for awhile and actually have a real vacation.” “I know, its such a rarity these days with our travel schedule, business, commercials, interviews, film and television schedule, its just endless. But this, right here and now….is pure heaven.” “Too bad it ends in four days….” “Awwww D…..don’t be such a party pooper. Enjoy the moment, because when we go back to work, its going to be hell on every Evolution superstar, not just the tag team division. You and I both know, we single handedly carried that division. We brought the ratings to it, and like you, its time to branch out from it. Of course we have some unfinished business their, namely with St. Violence, using our own explosive cage idea against us, un-creative simpletons. But you and I WILL make them suffer and put them out of this business once and for all. We will capture the tag titles again! I will become a 4-time Tag Team Champion and you will hold it twice! Its unavoidable, just as I am the sexiest female diva in Evolution.” “True dat! So what are your plans in the singles ranks C.” “To hold all of them of course. Thug Roc and I have some long standing issues, also I want to pursue the Evolved title and Universal title. The first ever female to hold the grandest prize in the history of the business. I’ve made quite a mark but this, this will be the cornerstone to my greatness, to cement my place among the best of the best, once and for all! What about you D, what are your plans?? “Well Asis and I have unfinished business when he tried to end my career by spearing me through a cell. Also I think the cell division really needs a wake up call. I mean I’ve held the Univeral, Continental and Tag titles but the cell title, no one has more experience inside a cage, hell in a cell than me. I’ve got more time in those things than 10 wrestlers put together. Yeah, I think its time for an awakening…..” “Ahhhh yes…… A rude awakening….. As great as we are together…… they have no idea of our individual destructive power…… In Australia, the hostile takeover begins….. Now where is that tanning lotion…..
