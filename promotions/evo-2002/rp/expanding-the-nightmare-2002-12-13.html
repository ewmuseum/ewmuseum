Expanding the Nightmare

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/13/02 {A few short hours after Legends went off the air, backstage at the General Motors Place in 'Couver, BC. We find a door that reads 'UNLIMITED'. The door is opened enough to see inside. Inside you can see Xander packing up his bags. You also see two sets of luggage already packed, and ready to go. Only assuming these must belong to Asis, and the HeartBreak Chick. As the door creaks open, Asis comes out from the shower room area} 'X man, you can't be serious about this.' Asis almost pleads. 'Dead serious. This has been taken too far.' Xander says as he stops his packing. 'It's been too long now. As must as I try.. as much as we do, we're still laughing stocks of eVo. I beat Malcolm King. I retired him. Not L2J, not Sovereign, not Frost, not even Thug- roC. I did, and what have I ever received for it?' Xander retorts. 'We have the tag titles' Asis answers the non-question. 'Tag titles? -We- did not win the tag titles. -I- did not win the tag titles. I will defend them... and there is not one team that can pry them out of our cold dead grasp. But I am solely focused on what is RIGHTFULLY mine. And this is how I can get to it.' 'But, I mean man, can we trust this plan? Is it fool proof?' Asis inquires. 'Don't worry. I don't leave anything to chance. If it weren't for me, you wouldn't even be here right now, right? I didn't take a chance on initiating you... you were a calculated plan. And look how well it's worked out.' Xander gloats. 'True... but...' Asis starts before being cut off. 'Don't worry so much. That's not why you're here. I take care of Unlimited business, in the planning sense, and you take care of Unlimited business in the physical sense. It's a perfect formula. I'm the brains, you're the braun, and C's the beauty.' X reassures Asis. 'It'll all work out. The nightmare has only begun. Wisdom, L2J, Seara.... all the others.... they have no idea what they've opened up. All they will know, is that I.. we... Unlimited will not stop until we reach our goals.' Xander praises. 'What exactly are our goals anyway?' Asis questions the brains. 'Why, my young apprentice. That doesn't even matter. My goal, is whatever I feel, and it'll always change to suit what we need to do. As is your goal, HBC's, and anyone else ever involved with us before, and from now on. It's how we are.. we are Unlimited.' {The scene fades to black as that familiar smirk crosses Xander's face}
