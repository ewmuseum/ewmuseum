Light and Shadows

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 10, 2002 Light and Shadow The black and white camera shot clicks in after those 3 words are spoke. The voice, and the camera tell it all. Sovereign is standing now in front of your vision, standing with his head bend down, shoulders hunched his breathing heavy. Sovereign, the monster we’ve all grown to know and hate. Love to hate in fact hasn’t been having his way as we’ve grown accustom to lately. Ever since Impact cost him the Universal Title to L2J at Rise of the Legends repeatedly we’ve seen Sovereign on the end of beatings that we’ve never seen him take. Being choke-slamed through a ring, beaten with chairs etc etc. Once he was the man doing the punishing, but Impact now may have his Number. Sovereign wouldn’t agree with you though. I somehow doubt Sovereign will agree with anyone even himself. The mind this man has is rather distorted and we saw that he’s just as dangerous as ever before with attacks on the likes of Jinx, Stylez Junior and of course the elimination of Bobby Stormbringer. With Legends now tomorrow Sovereign has a new challenge. This 4 corner’s match is rather high profile, the Number 1 contender to the Universal Title, the Champion himself and of course Impact and Sovereign. All 4 men have something against each other one way or the other. Most likely the ego’s involved between the likes of Impact or Shocka, the championship on L2J and the basic unpredictability of Sovereign will make for an interesting contest. However with the heated rivalry between these individuals the question will arise if this will actually be a match, or just an all out brawl from bell to bell. That seems to be status quo these days for Sovereign whenever Impact is involved, just a fight without regard for rules. This match however has implications. Anyone that would get a pinfal in this match would most certainly be boosted in the Universal Title picture. Sovereign of course, champion for nearly 4 months hasn’t been given his rematch and we here doubt that Sovereign even cares about that at the time being. Or care about it while Impact still draws breath. Shocka, the Number 1 contender by right due to his victory in the Road to Glory tournament and Impact, a former top dog returning to glory against the monster that tried to end his career. With plans to make this match as straight ahead as possible from the Leya/Impact camp we’re sure that Sovereign won’t feel very comfortable going into the big match at the upcoming Pay Per View. Not to discredit Sovereign’s ability to wrestle, we’ve seen him showcase great ability when he portrayed himself as William. We know his strength and toughness are top notch but Impact is not whimp. Impact has size over Sovereign (something Sov hasn’t had to deal with lately) we know that Impact as someone in his corner. The one person that knows Sovereign better then anyone. The only person to suffer more at Sovereign’s hand then anyone ever has, his wife Leya Forth. So who can blame Sovereign for having a one track mind going into legends? How will it effect him in the match? Does Sovereign even give a damn about this match, or is it just a vehicle to get his hands on Impact now that neither can touch each other due to Comissioner Griffin’s ruling last week in Toronto. That being said by yours truly your friendly narrator, I’m sure that we would not be staring at Sovereign unless he had something to say. Light and Shadows. Shocka, you are a man full of bold statements to say anything about me. I could say that you don’t have the right to disrespect me and the only claim you have to the Universal Title is that you won a tournament. Granted Shocka that’s how I won the Universal Title. I beat 7 men in 1 night, the last match in a Ladder match and went on to hold that title twice as long as you and your little nickel and dime Continental Title. But boasting isn’t something I tend to delve into. Because it’s simply to classic. It’s to form. It’s what everyone does in this business. They grab a camera and talk trash about whomever they must do battle with next much like you did the other night at your little club. Personally Shocka I find you boring. Trying to hard to capture a glory that you would only get simply because that title was stolen from me by Impact. You’re right, you probably are stepping into the light, out from my shadow and that’s fine. I personally don’t care about you or what you do in Evolution because you are not a concern of mine. I don’t care about you. I don’t care about the Universal Title at this point in time, and you can go ahead and take it from L2J, or let him keep it for all I care because all I do care about is Impact. My revenge on him for taking that belt from me is more important than anything else in this world. You might be the number 1 contender Shocka, but when Impact is finished once and for all, and you are the Universal Champion you will be wishing that you never mentioned my name. Because I will come back for my title. My belt that I made famous and gave it credibility not like in that other federation EDW when the belt changed every other week. You have to see Shocka that you might be young blood, but young blood still can be spilt just as easily and if you know anything about Sovereign, I like to shed blood. But yes, I don’t care about you Shocka. I don’t care about the match on Legends. It’s just a platform for me to get my hands around Impact’s throat and choke him with my bare hands. You and L2J can do your little dance and have your little fight and that’s okay. I don’t care if you try to fight me, or if you try and fight Impact. Everyone that gets in my way of getting to Impact will be a victim of my Crusade. I will take you out Shocka quicker then you can say Number 1 Stunna or whatever the hell you call yourself. You’re insignificant. You’re a pawn, a peon. You mean nothing to me, your like grass in a field, just another of millions that are out there. Someone with a little talent and charisma with some a gang of people backing you up, making you more then you are. But in the end Shocka I know that you are probably going to be the next Champion. L2J shouldn’t be the champion he should be laying in a hospital bed if it wasn’t for Impact. So you see Shocka, Impact is my focus. Impact is my agenda and I’m not trying to hide it. He knows that I am coming for him. Security tried to get in our way last time, and if anyone like you Shocka or L2J or anyone tries to stop us this time, well the same thing will happen. Sovereign will take them out, Impact will probably because our mutual hatred for each other transcends any title matches or anything else in eVo. Yes Shocka, you might be headlining Nightmare before Christmas. But everyone will want to be tuning into Impact trying to get his revenge on Sovereign. And I want them all to tune in. Because I want their dreams to be shattered like what Impact did to me at Rise of the Legends. I want them to see his bloody face mashed on the canvas. I want them all to see the “Hollywood Monster” make his final film, the beaten corpse of a fool that came back when he should have stayed at home reading scripts and growing flowers or whatever the man does in his spare time. You want to step into the light from the shadow’s Shocka then that is perfectly alright by me. But remember, when you come out of your cover, you become a target. A target that will most likely suffer an undesirable fate. You are a child Shocka, playing with fantasy, and I thought I was the crazy one. Sovereign raises his head, he thrashes his neck and his hair goes out of his face, flipping over his brow. Sovereign steps out of the shadow and faces the camera in clear light. His eyes are wide open, in that crazyed state we see when Sovereign is really in a hostile mood (more often then not) and he speaks more. You have a big mouth Shocka, and although you might be the Number 1 contender, you aren’t the champion yet. And whatever the future might hold for you and your career I’m going to tell you now. If you try anything on Wednesday Shocka, I will drastically shorten your career. You might laugh, and say “oooo Sovereign threatened me oh, I am so scared” or whatever wise crack you decide to make. Because that is one thing you seem to excel at Shocka, wise cracks. But rest assured Shocka, no matter how many friends you have they can’t contend with me. You might be the next rising star in Evolution. You might be the next Universal Champion for all we know. But you are NOTHING compared to me. You aren’t as dangerous, you are not as ruthless. You don’t have the mind-set to contend with someone like me. For you the Universal Title is nothing more than a trophy. It’s a cash cow. It’s something to flaunt in front of your friends, peers and women. The Universal Title to me was the only thing that ever made me feel good about myself. You tell me who wants it more, you or me? And since you can’t possibly understand what goes on in my mind I’ll take that as a default and I’ll win that little debate. Shocka, you and I will share a stare tomorrow night. You and I will trade blows but I hope you aren’t disappointed if I don’t really care about you, your agenda, your New-TV, your big “main event” at the Pay Per View. I’m a one track mind sort of person Shocka, and if you were on my mind you’d be about track 10. Sovereign now is right up on the camera. His breath is slightly fogging the picture as although Sovereign has invested in a decent cam-corder it’s probably not high end, otherwise he’d most likely have a color shot. As for you Impact. As... as for you Leya my wife this week will not be one to forget. You will realize why coming back here was a mistake. And in Calgary Alberta Canada you will realize that this Monster isn’t going to be giving up any more titles to the likes of the both of you. I am evolution’s Monster. I am evolution’s Nightmare, every night not just before Christmas, and when the two of you see me tomorrow you will go through torture at my hands. I might not be able to touch you Impact. But I can hurt you. I can cause you grief. I’m not just some run of the mill beat you up and leaving you laying in a pool of your own blood Monster. I’m much more. I’m much worse than that Impact. Leya knows what I can do to a person without laying a hand on them. Impact, you are in for a world of pain on Legends. Then, finally when I can get my hands on you at the Nightmare Before Christmas on December 22nd I will rip you limb from limb. I will make you scream in pure terror and pain. Sovereign takes a step back and moves to walk off, but stops. See you in Calgary Impact. Sovereign then smiles and laughs to himself and walks off as the camera fades out to the Legends Logo.
