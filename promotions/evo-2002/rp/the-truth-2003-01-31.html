The Truth

31 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31st January 2003 New Year’s eVolution was a huge event with plenty happening both inside and outside the ring to keep even the most bored of people entertained, but Legends this week had a different effect on the evolved Champion Seara. Sitting backstage most of the night, Seara made no attempt at making her presence felt, she merely stayed hidden and out of sight, but she could never be found when being looked for, the female had taken her station at many different places throughout the time she was in the arena, watching the other wrestlers of eVo talking amongst themselves, watching the drama unfold, and after the departure of Frost, the female had left not much later to go and check on her love. Her night had been intrigued by the words of Erin Christoph, unaware to the male wrestler, the female was close by when he exited the boiler room, and she stayed hidden out of sight to listen as he talked about Frost and his attacks on Unlimited. Seara was now thinking at great pain, no thanks to Xander who attacked the female in the parking lot, hitting around the head with a Monkey Wrench. A small lump on her head now, the female was suffering a headache but nothing much more than pain when she would reach up and inquisitively touch the cut made by the blow of the heavy implement. Frost reached out and grabbed the female’s hand. Leave that alone. God Katherine, what is it with you and blood. I know your life, but you seem to love blood so much. Seara looks at Frost who is sitting alongside her in the back of a cab; she can tell that their conversation in the restaurant is still affecting him in someway. I wish you knew how it felt. His murmured voice was quiet, and he didn’t mean for Seara to hear him say it, but she had now, and staring at him, she wondered what she could say to somehow make him realise they shared something haunting in their past. Seara’s past had been created not long after her entrance into eVolution wrestling, but nothing had ever come of the terrible thought she carried with her from day to day. You don’t think I know what you feel like because of the murder. You’re not alone Scott. I know how you feel, and you cannot deny that for me. I find no link between our actions and the way they were carried out, you murdered a man in defence, or so it would seem to anyone who noticed it happening or was to hear the story the way you told me back there in that restaurant the other day. I do know what murder and death are like. I know you know what death is like. Frost’s words snapped at Seara, and shaking he put his head down into his hands. Uneasy by the way she had made Scott feel, Seara gently placed her hand on his leg, unsure how to confront his anger, she was somehow scared by the temper she knew Scott carried within him, but he did not know that she felt this way. Lifting his head he looked at the female whose body was turned slightly so she could see his entire body without having to sit uncomfortably. I didn’t mean to bite your head off. The attitude in Frost’s voice was completely different now, and Seara tried hard to smile, but she had now got to tell frost about her stepbrother Vinnie. Frost had not been around eVo when Seara and her stepbrother had come to blows, those had been the days of Erin Christoph, and back when Seara and Asis were apparently friends, but much had changed, and Seara had for a long time believed her actions were between herself and Asis who had known of her plans to change her misfortune. Asis had the females thoughts locked away in his mind, a place no other human could enter, and Seara had kept it locked in like a childhood memory, barely visible to her thoughts, but it had not happened that long ago, and every day, Seara found herself thinking about what she had done. There was no regret in her thoughts, and she seemed proud of the memory, but her father had loved Vinnie and he had been her family for so long. What’s wrong? Asked Frost, he could that from that expression that life for the female was still torture, the ring was bringing it back to her, and home life was sometimes unsettled for the female who often felt like a guest and nothing more. Spending most of your time moving around had that affect and Seara had grown up used to it, but now she was staying in hotels and Frost’s house, and sometimes it made her feel like she had nothing of her own. It was not often that that female felt like this, but she was still at heart a free soul who wanted nothing more than a car and the road. Life was changing now for the better and it was hard for Seara to settle in quickly, this was her first home, her first shot at a real life, and adjusting to the love and trust Frost gave was strange at first, she had never felt this before. She had pretty much settled in now, but occasionally when she thought back to the times of her childhood when her mom had been alive, she wondered what would have happened if that crash hadn’t killed her. Would she be this angry, would her actions have happened, and would she be in eVo with the love of her life, the only person she had ever learnt to trust fully about her life and feelings. Turning back to Frost Seara gives an awkward smile. Yeah I suppose I’m alright, you just have to know about my life, you don’t really know me, I was different before you met me, I never had a home, I never had a friend or anyone I loved. My family were a burden; the biggest pain in my life was Vinnie. My stepbrother. Seara pauses, she has seen the shocked look on Scott’s face, and she waits for the question she knows he will ask. But…but I thought you only had Bruce and Jake, why didn’t you tell me you had a stepbrother. The question has been asked, and Scott seems to not want the answer, but Seara had been right, he needed to know her past to know why she was becoming the way she was. Vinnie was my stepbrother, and as you well know, Asis killed Jake, he left him…that way. Seara refuses to discus her brothers injuries, they hold pain for the female, and she avoids this line for too long. Bruce is the last. Not long after I started here I met up with Asis again, I had known him in my childhood, he was my friend for a short while, and at one time he helped me torture my stepbrother, later, I killed him, murder, intended murder, it was planned, I ended his life for what he did to my father. My father was the greatest wrestler alive, he was an amazing man, he was my idol, my hero, I loved him with all my heart, and that little fucker ran him over with his own pick up truck. I got him out to the ring at my grandfathers ranch in Fort Worth and in the middle of a huge storm, I allowed him to challenge me to a fight. My dad had trained me, and his instructor was my instructor, still is. Anyway I knew that ring like the back of my hand, and I had been a death match fighter in my amateur fed, so I knew I could take Vinnie to the extremes of pain. I did exactly that and when I found the weakest part of the ring, I got ready. I pinned him, I was chocking him, I placed all my weight on his neck, squeezed the life out of him, and when that ring began to snap, I fell into the hole with him, I twisted and broke his head, I left that little bastard looking at his own fucking arse. He was dead because I planned my every move, I knew where not to put pressure on that ring, I knew exactly how to make it crack, I knew where to lay to kill him, and I knew how to make it look like a accident, something neither one of us could control, but I had controlled it. Fuck, I was the one that called an ambulance, and the cameraman and interviewer, they just stayed there shocked, and they didn’t believe I could do it. No one did, no one ever believed I killed him, it was an accident as far as the world was concerned, and now you me and Asis are the only people that know what I did that night, it was barely even 6 months ago. I murdered someone who ended my father’s career, and I ended his wrestling career, I gave him that career, I finished it. Scott looks shocked as the female’s words fade to silence in the cab. But he somehow understands a lot more of Seara now; just that little bit of information has changed his view that little bit more. Scary stuff, but hell, I guess I understand what was going through your head. And hey, you know I’m here for you, you don’t need to keep that shit in, if that’s what your like when your angry, then come talk to me, let me know what’s going on with you. I still love you, and I’ll be here, no matter what. Thanks Scott. I had to let you know though, I feel the need to kill again sometimes and I don’t want you to be on the wrong end of my moods. I know it’s not pleasant to hear that kind of shit about me, or to think about anyone killing, especially with me changing. I don’t want you to get hurt, I don’t want to turn on you in a moment of anger, and I don’t know how to confine my anger to the ring. Scott grabs the female and pulls her close to him in his arms, he begins to comfort her and the scene fades to black as the pair sit in each others arms, a tear rolling down Seara’s face.
