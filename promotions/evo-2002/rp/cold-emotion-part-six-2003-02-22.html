Cold Emotion... part six.

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens inside of a bathroom with bright, blinding light that shines from a light fixture above the mirror. The mirror swung open and different various pill bottles can be seen inside on the shelves. A couple of the bottles are empty and thrown in the sink, someone in lost hope of finding more medication. The scene pans and half lying on the floor and slumped against the wall is Sky Night with her eyes and all gazed over and she looks to be high on the meds. With a slight smile but blank look on her face. “Someone in evolution has made one of the biggest mistakes of their life. They violated me by burning my back with an iron brand. You think that this deep scold will stop me from competing at Tainted Love? I swear the downfall of 1001 holds and his chump friend. Nothing will hold Twisted Reality down from winning the evolution tag championships. It is all part of the master plan; in the end and only then will you actually realize. It sickens me how sometimes others can be so ignorant to the truth and misconceive facts they very well realize. Sometimes the ones who you think are your friends, are really the ones you hate and on another extreme your enemies are the ones that you like... Definitely something to think about” Sky proclaims with slurred words due to the effects from the pain killers that she has herself jacked up on. Pulling herself up slowly, Sky has a bothered look on her face and finally reaches her feet and holding her one hand over the area burned by the brand. Finally Sky replies with weak chuckle because of the stressful recovery. Sky removes the ice pack from behind her back, she throws it into the sink. Opening her eyes wide, Sky stares at herself in the mirror and she looks like she has seen far better days. “The time is coming where you lay everything on the line and there is nothing left to be lost. The recent and previous evolution tag champions the Body Snatchers; they do not have the slightest chance in hell of walking out of Sunday night alive. I swear on my word that they will suffer tomorrow night with all greater cause. It would have been one thing to take your defeat from a woman 1001, but not to where you violate someone and scold them with a burning piece of metal. That piece of shit!” she swears with an angry tone of voice. Rubbing under her eyes, Sky observes the dark bags under her eyes probably from being strung out the last few days since Legends. Sky’s fits of rage and wildly thrown emotion, showing key sign that she is not messing around and to be taken seriously. Breaking down with odd chuckle and crazy like slur, she reacts estranged which is probably one of the side effects from the multiple plan killers. “You know… The truth is a very far fetched thing and can be covered up and mislead very easily. What is the answer to of what lies in the future is very irrelevant. The games have only started to begin” she laughs. Sky twirls strands of her hair with her one hand and aimlessly stares at herself in the mirror, she lets out a big sigh and the scene fades.
