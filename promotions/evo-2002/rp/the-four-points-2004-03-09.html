The Four Points

9 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-09 So much to do and so little time. It was a timeless phrase really, shame he couldn’t remember the guy that had come up with it. But then again, could anyone? Was there even one particular person that was quoted as inventing it? He was probably thinking too much into things again, it didn’t matter, much like a lot of the stuff he thought about, but it was relevant. The Joker. Jack Nicholson as The Joker, in the first Batman film, that’s where that quote had come from. At least, that’s where he remembered it from. Maybe that was where it came from originally? No it couldn’t have been, that film wasn’t that old. Or was it? A bit off track maybe. But, so much to do, so little time. Malcolm King. He could have spared himself, he didn’t need to get involved with his affairs. There was nothing personal between them, he just wanted to prove to everyone how false he was, how he let himself be used by the very company he said he was against. How he tried to contradict all that Asis said, when in reality he was doing nothing but enforcing every word of it. Shame the fans were too fucking stupid to realise. James Kimson. His return was inevitable. After he had taken his title at Tainted Love, he knew he’d be back for his rematch that he “deserved”. Deserved because it’s one of the rules that the company makes to entertain the crowd once more, take the same match, change it slightly and the crowd will forget about the one before, they’ll think this new one is even better and more exciting, when in reality it was always damn near the same match. He didn’t care that Kimson wanted a rematch. He’d taken the belt from him to prove a point and now that he’d done that, he sure as hell wasn’t going to hand it back to him easily. And their rematch was going to be an exception to the rule, it wasn’t going to be the same match they had at Tainted Love. Well, almost… Sovereign. The name alone made the men and women of eVolution feel anxious, afraid even. Not many had come against him and survived. It was well documented that he was one of the most dominating men in the history of eVolution, one of the most crazy and sadistic human beings to grace God’s green earth. He plain scared the shit out of everyone. Not Asis. They knew all about one another, they were very similar people, by no means the same, but no where near different. More than once they had described one another as brothers, more than once they had shown that the other was possibly the only other man in the world that they respected. Or so he’d thought. At Tainted Love Sovereign’s timing was bad. Asis couldn’t blame him for wanting vengeance on Kimson, put in his place he probably would have done exactly the same thing, irrespective of who the other guy in the ring was. But there was just one tiny little problem with that night. Sovereign had done to Asis what not many got the chance to do and regardless of his feelings towards his “brother”, he now wanted vengeance. After all, he couldn’t let William have one up on him could he? All he wanted was the opportunity to get him back and then they’d be quits, no need for anything more. Only he knew it wouldn’t go that way. They knew one another, they knew that now that one had defiled the other there’d be no stopping it till one was satisfied. Besides, Asis had what Sovereign longed for, what everyone in eVolution longed for. He was obsessed with it, more than any other had ever been and whilst their goals had once been similar, now they were very different. They were now opposing forces, but it couldn’t be any other way. With the label of “champion” he would always be opposing everyone. Just the way he liked it. So of course, in the minds of the “powers that be”, there could only be one rational way to settle this dispute and to gain money out of it. Put all three men in the ring together, let them beat each other within in an inch of their lives, which they would surely do and for the added incentive, put the Universal Title on the line. Sounded fair. Two problems though, one of which was related to the aforementioned Malcolm King and the belt that signified his status. The belt that had been stolen and melted down into it’s original form, all over the argument of which was the “true” Universal Title belt. It didn’t matter to him which was the true belt, he knew that. But King also knew that it did matter to him that he had no belt, because without that symbol, the fans didn’t know for sure. You see the fans could not just live with the fact that Asis had beaten their champion, their hero, they were too materialistic for that. They needed physical proof and for that, he’d have to take King’s belt. And he wasn’t going to steal it when he wasn’t looking like King had done to him, no. He was going to beat him in the ring for it. For all intents and purposes, Asis was going to once again fight to take the Universal Championship, even though by rights he already owned it. He would call King’s bluff, he’d do everything that King was asking of him, because he knew he could. And who was it that was responsible for all of this? Xander. A name, a face from the past. A man who tried to take credit for all that he was today, a man that tries to claim he created Asis. He didn’t realise that all he did was suppress Asis, he held him back from being what he is now. As soon as Asis had gotten Xander out of the way, he went on to become the dominating force that he is now and he would be more than willing to put him out of the picture now. Asis harboured never-ending hatred for Xander and there was no doubt that the feeling went the other way. Soon enough the pair would meet once more. And Asis had a feeling that time would be soon…
