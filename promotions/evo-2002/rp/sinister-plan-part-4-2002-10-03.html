Sinister plan part 4

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct. 3, 2002 'Plan two must come into effect now.....' secretly the voice of Scott Frost speaks. 'Are you sure your going to do this, he is going to be really pissed after he finds out what you have planned......' the voice of Kelsey secretly replies back to Frost. 'Listen, he won't do anything if he knows whats right for him' says the voice of Frost as the scene fades into view of a dark brown oak wood table.... Suddenly the dark brown oak wood table is impacted by a tan colored kendo stick. The impact is so hard, that bamboo dust flies into the air as the center of the cane breaks open releasing tension everywhere on the cane and unraveling the bands that bound the wood together. 'I will crush him. He might think that we are friends. But I will crush him. Legends XI, the beginning of the end begins.' very calmly Frost says as he smiles. 'What if he gets to you first? What if he gets the best of you at Legends?' Kelsey asks Frost. 'Don't worry babe. This might be the first time you've heard about it. But this has been planned since, since I won the XPW World championship in Detroit against Maxium Dred that tuesday night.' retorts Frosts. 'Isn't that the day that you met him for the first time?' asks Kelsey. 'Yep.' solemnly replies Frost. 'He told me..' Frost pauses, 'He told me that I was the best....he is pretty much the reason I am here.' Frost continues. (To be continued...)
