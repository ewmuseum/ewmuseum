Unlikely Team

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10th March 2003 The scene opens to the ever-familiar sight of the house of Scott Frost and Seara. The sitting room is dark, the curtains drawn and the light being provided by one solitary candle on the table in front of the female. The female sits in her usual attire of black jeans and a tight black top; her belt lays on the table top in her view and also in camera shot, looking down at it she sighs and continues to smoke her cigarette, only looking away at the sound of movement somewhere else in the house. Turning back to the camera she stares hard and long at it, thinking closely about her words before deciding to talk. Past is the past. Past is the present, but now the present is going to be effected and the past must be put aside. Shocka. A man who for me the past is tainted with hate, a man who once tried quite unsuccessfully to kidnap me with the help of Morpheus Omega. This is a man I am to be teamed up with. Shocka, you may be a large man, u may seem dangerous to some, you may seem frightening and someone I should watch my back around, but I will not worry about you. Looking up the females eyes become illuminated by the candle light which flickers red and yellow on the white of her eyes, and she rubs her fingers over the front of the eVolved title. Something seems to catch her eye a moment later, and getting to her feet she walks out of sight and then returns holding the Universal title, placing it next to her own title she seems to smile in a sadistic way, the corners of her lips tilting up and a small laugh can be heard from her. Shocka, I know you will not try anything with me for the simple reason that you know I will be a valuable asset to you in that ring, just as you will be a valuable asset to me in that ring. I would love to see Thug roC ended on Wednesday night, but unless we put away our differences and work together, it will not happen. We are both out there to get rid of two people that seem to be a great annoyance to us. Morpheus Omega has been nothing but an annoyance to me since he turned up here in eVolution, and as for roC, what could I say about roC. He is a bain in my existence, roC has done nothing but screw me over at every chance he has had, and this is it, I will stop him once and for all, and I’m sure Shocka that you would like to rid him from your sight as well. I enjoyed their little reunion last week on Legends, it seemed that they were a great pair, fuck ups. Annoying wastes of space. There really is nothing nice I can think of to say to them or about them. So instead of wasting my breath I will simply tell them to be ready for the ride of their lives on Wednesday night, because I will take you to the edge and back again. Once again the female gets to her feet and abruptly leaves the room, cutting what was meant to be a half an hour TV promo into a ten-minute one ended conversation. The camera is left focused in on the two belts alongside each other as the scene fades to black.
