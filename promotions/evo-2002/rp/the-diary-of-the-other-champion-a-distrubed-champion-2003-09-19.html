The Diary of the Other Champion: A Distrubed Champion

19 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-19 I can’t stand it anymore… It’s driving me nuts… Everywhere I turn… Every time I get up…. Every time I lay down… I hear that damn voice… It haunts me, even during my sleep. It was one good deed… And now I must pay for it… I pay for it with my ears I pay for it with my mind I swear I’m going crazy. Cause if I hear him say Fo’ Shizzle one more time…I’m goin to scream! Thug roC scurries around his hotel room, locking the door three times, pulling down the window shades, and then disconnecting the phone from the wall. Thug roC sits on his hotel bed, looking around, like a nervous wreck. He then takes a deep breath, breathing in through his nose and then out through his mouth. He gets up and grabs his wrestling bag, pulling out his wrestling attire and United Title Belt. “Scott Evans, truly a great athlete. But unfortunately, he was beaten that night. Maybe, the better man didn’t beat him, cause we’re may never know who was the better man that night. But he definitely was beaten. That small package that he applied turned out to be the worst move he could’ve done. Looks like Evans has some business with other guys before he can come back at me for the United Title.” Thug roC unpacks his bag completely, that’s when there’s a knocking at the door. Thug roC just smiles and ignores it. “Believe me, that Shaft guy, he’s not a bad guy or anything. But if another demo tape is found in my car stereo…well…lets say eVo might have one less member. I saw he needed my help that night. I wasn’t going to sit back and watch an old friend of mine getting beat up on, no matter how annoying that friend could be. QBX nor Tok’n has any right to try and jump him like they tried to do. Both men needed to realize that they won’t get away with attacking Shafty for no apparent reason.” Thug roC has a towel in his hand and a change of clothes. He gets up and makes his way towards the bathroom. “Tok’n and Q will realize…roC-TV is back on the air. So better luck next time.” Thug roC opens the bathroom door and goes to enter it. He suddenly walks backwards to see Shaft-Man walk out with a bathrobe on. Thug roC looks in shock as Shaft-Man turns on the TV and sits on the bed. “Tell me something Thugster, why do you get such a nice room, and I only get a couch in the lobby?” Shaft-Man asks. “Better question; why do you have on my bathrobe?!” An angered Thug roC asks. “This is yours?” Shaft-Man is confused. He stands up and looks in the mirror, and when he turns around to see the back, you can see the back of the robe reads ‘roC-TV’ and it has Thug roC’s face printed across the majority of the robe. “I would’ve never known.” Shaft-Man says surprisingly. “But us being partners…” “Whoa…wait right there. We are not partners. That was a one shot deal. I gave Tok’n one good shot to the face, so that ends the deal.” Thug roC says while walking towards the door. Thug then opens the door and points. “This is wear you make your exit. Peace out Shaft-Man.” “Don’t worry roC, I’ll see you in the morning.” Shaft-Man makes his way out the door, but stops mid-way through the doorway. “Oh yea, and by the way Thuggy; your toilet is stopped up. Sorry.” Thug roC’s face turns angry as Shaft-Man leaves his room. Thug roC slams the door and on the outside of the door, you can see the door hanger reads ‘roC-TV’ but the roC is scratched out. Someone has written on it, therefore making the door hanger read ‘Shafty-roC-TV’ as the scene fades.
