Digital Destruction 2002 Re-visited/King's last stand in the cell

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-22 'I relive that match over and over in my head.......I question myself daily on what did I do wrong how did things get out of control?' {Justin Fritz: The most personal match in eVolution history is moments away from going down! Jack Tyler: And let’s not forget the end of Malcolm King’s career! Justin Fritz: That does seem to be the case according to King himself.} 'The commentary I heard afterwards as I was recovering in the hospital...the only time I've ever viewed that match from digital destruction 2002, I don't have to in all reality because the events are always playing in my head......' {'Antichrist Superstar' by Marilyn Manson are heard. Then as the voices in the song collectively hit the 'HEY!' the lights go on, then go off for the next two beats until the guitar kicks in and Xander comes out from the back to a large pyro blast on the staging. As the crowd jeers their fallen hero Xander makes his way to the ring shrugging it off. He wipes his feet on the apron, then enters through the ropes and backs off into his corner.} 'I always hated that song.......' {As King walks down the aisle and into the CELL King gets nailed with a charging axehandle body block from Xander. King drops Xander with a Gorilla Press Slam on the mat. (Ding, ding, ding); as the door to the CELL is slammed shut. Xander connects with a low blow; sending King to the mat. King recovers and scales the cage he goes to the top and executes a flying headbutt attack on Xander; who easily dodges. Xander executes a flying knee drop right to the gut of King. However King has enough presence to counter and reverse the move into a modified Texas Cloverleaf. Xander powers up and kicks out sending King into the side of the cage. Xander closes into King as gouges Xander's eyes out. King then sets up Xander and performs a Russian Leg sweep into the side of the cage taking both men out.King again recovers as Xander is slow to roll into the middle of the ring, his head still ringing. King scales the cage until he reaches the top opening of it. He stands on top looking out to the crowd who are cheering loudly. he winks and does a shooting star press off the top of The Cell onto the prone form of Xander!!!} 'He's the champion now......and yet I was in control, the culmination of my 10 year career as I the teacher goes onto eliminate my student, that's how it was that is how it should have been.....' {King gets up. He double underhook face slams Xander hard to the mat. Xander gets up and irish whips King into the side of the Cell following it up with a flying forearm right to the face. The shot busts up King as he slumps against the side of the Cell. Xander firmly in control executes an over the shoulder stomach breaker on King. Xander then executes a STF on King with the hope of making him scream 'I QUIT'. King bites the fingers of Xander forcing him to release the hold. As both men stand up Xander chops King. That move is followed by a short-arm clothesline which sends king crashing to the mat.Xander straddles King and hits him with a heart punch; forcing King to gasp for air. Xander puts King in an arm grapevine submission. King powers out of the move and nails him with a belly-to-back suplex. As King moves in towards Xander; Xander gives him a well placed kick in the gut followed by a bear hug. Continuing the damage done by the earlier heart punch.} 'Was it the environment? I was an accomplished wrestler a master of submission and he was just....just Xander. this was my time to leave a lasting mark not only on Evolution but wrestling on a whole.' {King low blows Xander. King applies an arm wrench to Xander. Xander goes for the reversal and whips King into the cage door. The force although mostly absorbed by King's back causes the door to buckle. Xander follows it up with a spear....the impact causes the door to give way as both men spill out to the arena floor.Once on the floor, Xander executes a reverse DDT on King. Xander firmly in control short arm clotheslines King, sending him crashing to the floor. Xander seeing King rolling around decides to scale the outside of the cell. He turns around and is perched like a bird of prey as he is roughly 10 feet above the arena floor. As King stands disoriented; Xander pushes off of the cage and executes a diving headbutt which catches king squarely in the head above sending him down yet again. Xander also sacrificed his body but is quicker to recover than King. Xander whips King into the cage and props his hand on one of the links. Xander reaches in his tights and pulls out handcuffs as he cuffs one of Kings hands to the cell. King still trying to recover has his back to Xander. A 'fan' throws Xander a belt, which the referee fails to see as he is trying to get the cuffs off of King. Xander takes the belt and lashes into the back of King. After about 19 lashes Xander wraps the belt around King's throat and places a knee squarely in the King's newly whipped back hoping the pain would make The Urban Legend scream I quit.} 'I was in control but somehow....somehow we stumbled out into his environment. At this point I became desperate, I had do something I started to panic.....' {King mule kicks Xander in the family jewels...forcing Xander to release the belt and back off if only for a moment. King places his foot against the cage and pushes off with all his remaining strength. The mixture of force and sweat is enough to rip Malcolm's arm from the cuffs at the expense of a few layers of skin. However Malcolm staggers back into the arms of Xander who suplexes him on his head and exposed upper back. Xander drags the body of King to the wrestling ring. In the middle of the ring he taunts the crowd before executing a cradle piledriver on king. Xander now is firmly in control as he drags the limp body of King into the ring ropes and ties him up in it. Xander goes out of the ring and grabs a chair from ringside. With King tied up in the ropes Xander delivers 5 chair shots to the still scarred back of Malcolm King. King slumps into the ring like a rag doll. Xander takes the body of and props it on the top rope. He looks outside to the announce table and motions as if he is about to put King through the table. Xander sets him up in a DVD and flies off the top rope; but king shifts his weight in mid air to turn the move into a reverse DDT as both men come crashing through the table.....with Xander being first.Both men roll around the debris in pain as King takes a shard from the broken table and jabs it repeatedly into Xander's ankle, causing him to scream in pain. King then takes the TV cords and wraps it around Xander's neck hoping to choke the life out of him. Xander in a move of desperation rakes King's eyes forcing king to release the cables. King battered and bruised slowly makes his way back to The Cell. Xander slowly staggers behind him. Once in the cell King starts to fiddle with the padding at the bottom of the Cell. Xander makes his way back into the cell as he sees king's back turned to him. As Xander goes in for the attack.....King spins around and nails Xander with a pair of brass knucks that he had hidden in the padding earlier. Jack Tyler: Where the hell did he get those from?! Justin Fritz: King had them tucked away somewhere but he just knocked Xander the fuck out! Jack Tyler: Not fair, not fair! He shouldn’t be allowed to smuggle weapons into the Cell!} 'I was the better prepared man, I thought of every single contingency.....except one, Xander's desire not to stay down.' {Xander goes down like a ton of bricks. King follows up with an ankle lock submission on Xander's ankle that Malcolm impaled earlier. Xander starts tapping out violently but refuses to say the words I quit. Xander summons all of his strength to power up from the ankle lock and nail Malcolm with an enziguri. Malcolm disoriented goes down. Xander uses this moment to follow up with a sharpshooter. Malcolm begins to tap...but refuses to say I quit. The more Malcolm taps....the more pressure Xander puts on until.....Malcolm is no longer tapping. The refs check Malcolm and it seems Malcolm King has passed out. The ref taps Xander’s shoulder as Xander releases the hold and slinks out of the Cell; as Xander is announced the official winner of the match. The EMT's rush to the Cell to tend to both men as the crowd in unison gives a standing ovation. Libby Bowman: Winner of the match due to Malcolm King being unable to continue...... Xander} 'On that day my career ended...right in the middle of the cell. That was the greatest match I have ever had in my 10 year career (OOc:That's a shoot thanks Xander) It forced me to retire from wrestling and catapulted Xander to the Universe. Sure I returned as a special attraction, referee, and now manager....but the Cell, that damn cell is what ended it for me. Now almost a year later Tyler Davidson has ordered me to the exact same place where Malcolm King the wrestler died, ironically I'm in there with another protege....but will history repeat itself? Can I bring myself to actually enter the Cell? Can I......?????' to be continued....
