Opposites and Equals

16 November 2003

Date: 2003-11-16 At all costs. He remembered those words, those words that had gone through his mind a few hours before. His silent vow to nobody but himself, that he would do whatever it takes to keep Xander down. He didn’t think that would involve himself staying down as well though. As he’d stood on the edge of that ramp way, looking down at the drop below, he knew that it must be done. So he did it. And when he was lying at the bottom, amongst all the wreckage, he knew he must get up. But he couldn’t. More through his own fault then through Xander’s attempt at punishment, his neck was in a bad way and he couldn’t get up. That was a week ago. He didn’t mind that much, he’d put Xander through hell and despite the pain he was in, he was happy. But it wasn’t enough, it never would be, he wouldn’t be happy with all that he did to Xander for a long time yet. It was a long way from over yet though. That one match didn’t constitute all that he had planned for him. The only chance Xander had was to run away and hide again. Highly unlikely now though, he knew what would happen if he did. Joke about touching your family? It was threat and not an empty one at that. Man and boy? Try man and monster. You made me Xander? You have no idea what you’re talking about. A long history of abuse and neglect created me. You may have helped give me some direction, some focus for my rage, but now that focus is upon you. For a man of such age and wisdom, you know nothing of what you have stalking you. Xander, for a man that spent so much time in the presence of that which hunts you, you really have no idea. I’d love to teach you though…
