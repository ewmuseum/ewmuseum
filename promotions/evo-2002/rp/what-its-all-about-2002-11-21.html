What its all about

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-11-02 It has spanned three years. It has been battled across two federations, It has taken the top spot of two Pay per views. It has given the fans one of the most memorable, and yet horrific moments in Evolution history. And now after all this time. After the years of intense competition between two of the finest athletes this business has to offer. After years of pain and suffering. It has to end. Three years of a lethal rivalry are now being forced to come to a conclusion. And one man will finally be announced the better. The stronger man after three years of conflict. It will be a war. After all this time of each man trying to prove their dominance, Neither of them is going to be willing to fall at the last hurdle. For both men. It will be death before defeat. The scene opens in L2J’s rec room. But it could quite easily double as a tribute hall to the fearless superstar. On the far wall next to all of his training equipment hangs L2J’s Illuminati dreamweaver snowboard with several boarding trophies. On the opposite side of the vast expanse of empty floor boards his bass guitar is propped up against the wall next to his amp and just below his platinum award. Next to that is his wrestling attire. The four different outfits he most commonly wears. One kit though he may not be wearing to the ring ever again due to the back of the shirt being torn and ripped from the attacks in the last PPV. It is a reminder. He knows what his goal is but he must remember what the consequences can be. Sitting in the middle of the silent room is L2J. He sits with his legs crossed and his eyes shut in his Lau Gar kung fu suit. His left hand is heavily taped up and bandages can be seen extending out over his left shoulder. There also seem to be some markings around his neck, possibly where the doctors had to treat the superstar after the assault he received two weeks ago. Slowly L2J rises to his feet, raises his left fist up level with his neck, and his right set firmly at hip level. He leads with is right foot, rises up onto his toes and stands prone. But his eyes stay shut. Sunlight from a high window leaks through all the dust into the room as L2J suddenly explodes into life. Starting with a series of swift right mid kicks to a roundhouse. Then a series of right left jabs to a high block then counter throw. He then raises up his arms and turns to face the other way, changing his stance. Heading back the way he came with a bicycle kick to a shin splitter quickly followed up with a sweep the three left back hands to a hard right. He then turns round again, crossing his legs and descends to the floor. With is eyes still closed. The silence had barely been broken as L2J danced around the room. His eyes stay closed but his face twists up with concentration. He is only three days away. From a turning point in his life. He could finally achieve what he feels his purpose on this earth is. To finally be the top of the game at his profession. To be hailed as a hero and to earn the applause of the fans and the respect of the locker room. But then L2J opens his eyes and looks over at his lacerated wrestling attire. Life isn’t always a bed of roses. And he could be destroyed once again. He could have to go right back to the drawing board and wait for another chance. Maybe in a year. A decade. Or maybe this is it. This could easily be his last chance. Maybe his life only last another three days. Suddenly there is a knock at the door. L2J takes a deep breath, rises to his feet then walks to his front room, grabbing a towel on the way. As we walks into the hallway of his house he only now realises that its quite a sunny day, he wipes his brow with a towel then opens the door to find Scott Frost on his doorstep who goes to say hi, but then looks his friend up and down in his full Kung fu gear. FROST: He Mr. Norris can I have your autograph? L2J raises a brow then half smiles before giving his friend the finger and stepping to one side welcoming Frost in. L2J- You wanna beer? FROST-Sure L2J pulls off his shirt and throws it randomly to one side as he walks over to a pile of clean laundry on his couch. Frost looks up at L2J as he walks across the room and sees several horrific scars on the fearless superstars back. He looks as though he got on the bad side of a Bengal tiger but Frost knows exactly where those scars came from and he knows the human monster that did it. L2J has his clean black shirt on by the time he gets to the fridge. Grabs two beers and heads back over to Frost. The two take several gulps. L2J then gets confused as to why his friend has come to his house and remained totally silent. L2J- Scott? FROST- What? L2J-What the fuck do you want man? FROST- Look man I have to ask. It’s not that I don’t appreciate the save or anything last Wednesday. But why the fuck are you not in hospital? You took a pretty damn nasty bump a few weeks ago. The bookies in the newspapers were taking bets. They had three to one odds that you’d die. L2J- It wasn’t that bad man. Sovereign’s fat ass broke my fall. L2J laughs as he takes another swig of beer. But Frost didn’t really seem to get the joke. FROST- Look man I don’t care fat his ass is, that’s potentially a fatal injury. L2J- Oh sorry mum. Look man what are you getting at? FROST- Look man I really hate to say this. But over the past 3 months, you have been hospitalised seven times. Seven damn times. Most other wrestlers on this planet would have hung up there boots and retired after four visits in their entire career. You have been through a hell of a lot man. And if I am gonna be brutally honest. I don’t think you’re in a fit condition to wrestle at this PPV. I don’t think you can win. And I think you know that too. And capable as I may be. I dot want to have to run the Notorious myself. L2J and Frost have both been in this business a long time. And have more experience than most in that ring. And Frost is the one that is talking sense. And as much as L2J hates to admit it. L2J- You’re right. L2J walks across the room rubbing his hand across the marks on his neck. L2J- I have been put through a lot. More than most people could possibly tolerate. And Because of the hell I have been through these past three months my chances aren’t looking too great. But that’s exactly why I have to step into that ring this Sunday. I have been through too much for this to just give it up now. I cant do that. I wont. All my life I have dreamed about being at the top. And now I’m finally there. But I don’t want to share. Why should I only have half of the spotlight. I want it all. And this may be my last opportunity. And granted that Given the circumstances the odds may not be in my favour. But your chances increase if your willing to give up everything. And for this I would. Frost watches L2J walk across the room to a sports bag sitting on a table. L2J unzips the top and carefully pulls out the Universal title. Frost nearly spews his beer all over L2J’s couch. FROST- Where the hell you get that?! L2J swings the title up onto his right shoulder and pats it a couple of times. L2J- Heh heh, I liberated this after I saved your ass last Wednesday… Suits me don’t you think. See man this is what the notorious is missing. And this Sunday I’m making no mistakes. I’m hardly fully fit. And I’m pretty sure I’m pretty fucking sure I’ll be enjoying nurse hospitality for a long while after. But I am not leaving rise of the legends without this. I need to show the world what its all about. FROST- What? L2J- Come on. Its only 30 minutes to Toronto from here. You can drive. Frost is still somewhat shocked at seeing his friend with the title on his shoulder as L2J grabs a trenchcoat and slips on his boots. Frost heads out to his car as L2J locks up the house. Frost still seems shocked to see the title hanging from his friends hand. L2J notices Scott is staring. L2J- Get used to it man. Its time for the Notorious. The two share a smile as they enter the car and speed away to Toronto. The journey passes quickly as the two discuss a course of action for the PPV and L2J gets used to holding the gold. They soon find themselves outside the Air Canada Center where a large crowd of Evolution fans have already gathered. L2J looks out of the window and a smile lights up across his face. He turns to Scott as he opens the car door. L2J- This….My friend…Is what it’s all about. L2J quickly steps out of the car and is instantly recognised by the crowd who respond with fits of screams as L2J places the title back on his shoulder. He plays up to the crowd as he makes his way to the entrance and waits for the moment he has been looking forward to his whole life. This one brief moment that he has gone through hell for. He prepares himself, takes a deep breath then suddenly turns and sprints towards the crowd control barrier. He leaps onto then raises the title above his head and closes his eyes. The crowd burst. The screams are unbelieveable as the crowd surge forward to get their hands on the champion. This is the moment. A moment that L2J never wants to end. A moment that he would die for…..A moment he doesn’t want to share.
