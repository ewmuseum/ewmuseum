Imagine....

1 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-02-01 Imagine this.... One day your conducting your own business, when suddenly a third party takes it upon himself to become involved in something he has no part in. Then imagine rewarding that man regardless, despite the fact you never once asked for his help. Imagine how it then must feel to watch him bite the very hand that fed him, opting to take more than his share and try and make a name for himself at your expense. Imagine the anger, imagine the need for revenge that would be flowing through your veins. Then imagine the week following. A cold winters night, the two men clash and the man who wanted the world gets the payback he so deserves. Imagine the satisfaction. Am I sleeping well? Should these fans be fearing for the well being of their champion elect? What I did was as clinical and ruthless as it was sinister; more importantly though it was successful. How am I sleeping you ask? Bloody brilliant I would reply. Have I stepped out of my depth or into my element this coming Sunday night? On paper some might doubt my credentials, unlike the ogre like figure of Northstar I am not customed to bar room brawls, nor am I into the chair shot and lead pipe beating lingo. However any man of fear and desperation can weild a chair, its hardly something fitting of a mensa candidate. I'm neither a man living in fear or desperation, I am simply a man keen to feel that satisfaction and sense of well being long after the close of the Pay Per View. You wanted to brawl with Ethan M Coles? I grant your wish, I'll stoop to your level if thats what you so wish. Though ask yourself, should I beat you within your element, then what is left for you?! Legends was about accepting the challenge you couldn't spell let alone say when you attacked me the fortnight before. No love will be shown in a weeks time, no love will be lost, put the champagne on ice. The champagne for me and my fans, the ice for you and your battered dreams. Let me raise a toast; to dream, no matter the cost it brings with it.
