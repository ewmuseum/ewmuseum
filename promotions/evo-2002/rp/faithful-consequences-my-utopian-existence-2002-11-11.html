Faithful Consequences - My Utopian Existence

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/11/02 “Hey Jay. Jay? You there asshole?” What…? What the hell do you want Jason? I was in the middle of something. It’s called sleep. Something I’m sure you enjoy just as much as… “What happened the other night, Jay? Why was the window shattered? Why was my title belt hanging off the edge of the God damn porch? What are you doing to me? Tell me right now you mother…” Calm down Jason. You’ll give yourself a headache. Do you know what happens in here when you get a headache? It ain’t pretty. It looks like thunder coming over the horizon with a tornado right behind it. Sad thing, really. “I’m not joking with you. I want to know just what the hell you are doing to me when I don’t know it. I won’t to know just who the hell you think you…” Have you ever dreamt about waking up and seeing the world in front of you? “What the hell does that have to do with…?” It is something, I’ll tell you. You can feel eternity gazing back into your eyes. The air lifts you up into the sky like a newspaper caught in an updraft. The world runs through your veins, rushing through you heart and into the rest of your body, like a shot of heroin. “Shut the fuck up Jay.” Your eyes are shut, with your sense of touch taking control over your mind and your soul. It is all you ever wanted to feel and more. It’s the sensation that only dreams can bring you. Only your greatest fantasy could give you this consciousness. “Jay, shut up! Right now!” All I wanted to do was stay there, locked in time, never to be awakened from this daydream. The only awareness I would ever need would be the images flashed before me, like a theater’s projector shining onto the darkened canvas of my eyelids. The sounds of the empty air around me? The odor of oblivion’s breath? The taste of the tears of my overjoyed eyes? They would have no effect on my paradise. My utopian existence. “God damn it Jay! Why the fuck are you trying to kill me?!” But then, just when everything I ever wanted was gripped in my arms, I saw something that took it all away from me. It ripped a hole through my heart, and bled my life away. It was the hatred filled parallel of myself. It laughed into my face, and taunted me with its stare. Suddenly, all this rage built up inside of me, and all I wanted to do was get it out. “Yeah, Jay. Get it out. Get it all out. But if you would just listen to me, then maybe you wouldn’t…” My eyes opened, and then there was nothing. The world had burned away, and nothing was left except the ash stains on my hands. They would be washed away from all the sweat I felt with my anger, and all the tears I shed with my doubt for life. “You? Cry? Who would have thought? See Jay. You are just as pathetic as you claim that I am.” I have waited in this prison. Waited for my life to break free, so that I can feel the air go into my lungs and so that I can feel the warmth of the sunlight hit my skin. I have faith that someday I will Jason. Someday, when age has caught up with you, or when you are too weak to go on, or when time has ticked its last second in your grace, I will feel the world below my feet. “Never Jay. You fucking never will.” Those dreams I have Jason? Of existing in my perfect life? Of thinking that someday you will give me everything I ever needed. Those are my consequences, Jason. My consequences for having faith that you will give me the world that I always wanted. But I’ll tell you Jason. My faith is running short. I’m starting to believe that your time is running out, and that I will be forced to take control. “We’ll see about that Jay. I’m sure I can control you better then you think.” Oh yeah? Then what happened the other night? Why was the window shattered? Why was your title belt hanging off the edge of the porch? You see Jason. You can’t control me. And slowly, ever so slowly, I am taking what dreams I have left. My faith is slowly slipping away. My consequences will soon be yours to pay. Your life is slowly dying in my hands. I think I want to leave you to your thoughts Jason. “No! Wait asshole! What did happen that night? What happened mother fucker!” If you had been listening to me, Jason, you would have heard the answer. “Bull shit Jay. What happened? Tell me God damn it. Tell me!” I have to go now. I have so many things to do, and an eternity left to do them. But remember, Jason. “Fuck you Jay. I don’t need to remember anything.” I’m watching Jason. Watching you. And if I have to, I will end you. “Fuck… you… Jay.” Farewell Jason. Until next time. “No wait Jay. Wait asshole. WAIT!” Only a desolate wind. Jason knew he was there. Somewhere. Watching. And waiting. Waiting for his dreams to end, and his reality to begin.
