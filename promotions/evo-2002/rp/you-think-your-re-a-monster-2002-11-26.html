You think your're a monster?

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.26.02 Enter a monster? Leaves a monster. Monster reigns… Monster is bad… Monster cruel… Comes back a monster… Monster mad… Monster Cruel… Monster reign? “I’ll admit it… that night, three months ago, you messed me up pretty bad. I’m not going to deny that. I was at the hospital for over a month… OVER A GOD DAMN MONTH.” The sound of absolute silence is… mute… non-existent… but o so deadly powerful. How powerful could it be when it conceals the monster within Michael Scopes, Impact? The Hollywood Monster? The silence is deafening… not physically… but mentally. How could such silence come from such a gargantuan man? How could such silence come after three dreadful months of silence? “See, on that fateful night, my career may have been halted… but it signaled the demise of you. Indeed, you put me out… but… you didn’t take me out! My neck? It wasn’t broken. The vertebrate didn’t rupture. In fact… my neck was not the problem during my painful climb back here…” A light tapping sound, probably from a foot, starts to resonate in the silence. The silence was now gone, but the dark and black void still remained. In this darkness… vision was out of the question… this was the type of darkness that concealed the horrendous and vicious animals that lurked in the deep waters… this was the type of darkness one would contract a phobia to… and this darkness was not going to go away. “I could have been here a month ago… but did I? No! See, that last month… that last month was planning! Everything you do… everything you will see… all calculated from the insanity this mind went through seeking to avenge my destruction with your defamation… this time… I WILL BE THE INFAMOUS… and you? AND YOU THE HELPLESS VICTIM.” Finally after a uncomfortable wait, a light crept into the dark void… Impact could be vaguely seen… the light sliced through his body like a knife through butter… his face was drooped downward to the floor as he sat on the chair, tapping his foot in a hypnotic rhythm. “There is NO room for TWO monsters.” End foot tapping. 'End Sovereign.'
