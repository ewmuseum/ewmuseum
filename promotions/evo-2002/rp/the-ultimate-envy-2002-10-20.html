The Ultimate Envy

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 20th, 2002 I hate the way you walk I hate the way you talk. I hate the way you move. I hate the way you soothe. I hate your smile. I hate your style. I hate your voice. I hate your choice. I hate your life. I hate your wife. I hate you all together. I hate you through all weather. I hate you forever ever more. I hate you because you're a dirty whore. I hate you because I don't understand you. I hate you because the tips of you hair is blue. I'll hate you forever because I don't understand. I don't understand you not from far or near. I hate you because you are what I fear. I sent you a message to insult your style. I thought the words would sink in for a while. I hope that you care so that it rots your soul. I hope you care so I know that you'll grow cold. You used me in places where I didn't belong. I cried for days because I knew you were wrong. When I finally told you I had a fit. You responded back calmly, with I'm sorry. I admit. Your words will die, and give me the win. I know you won't die, not until the end. 'I don't understand your roleplays...'- Epimp101 Ebony, solid ebony. Nothing more than darkness that engulfs one inside of it. Not the artificial darkness that is claimed to be in the heart of tormented ones. This darkness is pure, a darkness that can hide people, that can keep you away from the world. A sudden stray light appears, and there sitting down on a black stool with all black on besides his face is Jinosyde. Long blonde bangs and all. He tilts his head and smirks. The light shining down on him and nothing more. 'Here I am, sitting in my undisclosed location. In my own personal hell, infront of a camera represented by eVolution and Old School Wrestling. I'm sorry about your cameraman, he... loaned this to me for a short while.' Mumbles, mumbles as if the person were being gagged by an object start to sound after he finishes his statement. Jinosyde turns his head toward the left and looks into a deeply ebony corner. He just lets off a solomn laugh and turns back over toward the camera. He tilts his head toward the right a small bit, looking deeply into the camera as if he were confused. The long, light bang smacks him across the face as he does so. 'What's that you say? You're mumbling, let me listen to you closer. You're saying... you're saying you want to be shut off. Can you do that? Can you shut a human off? Okay, I'll do that for you.' With a hardcore sinister smile he slowly stands up and grabs an object that was beside him. A long, thin silver thing with a groove at the end. He then slowly turns and heads over toward the area. The blackness englufs him, a sharp swing and a thud can be heard as a wail engulfed in a gag can be heard. Jinosyde can be seen sitting back down on the stool. 'Where was I? Oh yes. eVolution. My own personal playground, my sancutary. My hell. I love you eVolution. It's just vermin inside of you that enrages me to destroy you. One of the vermin. Jason Steele. Impudent? Yes. Of course he's impudent. He's a Steele. Along with his candy assed brother. They make me laugh, they're both fools. I can't wait to see his brother in action, makes me cringe with excitement. I can't wait to snap the little twigs body in half. I've been training, keeping myself in top shape to work out this shoulder. That mistake won't happen again. You will be impaled. Now, back to your brother. Your older brother. Such a loving family. It makes me want to vomit all over this floor under me. Jason... Jason, Jason, Jason. You really are a comedy relief. You make my day brighter when I wake up. because I know it's only a few days before I can snap that pencil neck of yours. Or better. Rip through your stomach.' He slowly closes his eyes and then lifts them up, once more. His eyes showing demonic expression. The contacts in his eyes make his eye nothing more than stray dot. He would scare little children and grown adults. One grown adult in particular. He titled his head toward the side as he turned back over toward the corner, the mumbling began once more. 'Didn't I tell you to shut up?!~ !!?' The mumbling stopped almost simultaneous when his voice arose. Jinosyde then turned back toward the camera. He clapped his hands together and held the tight against each other. he leaned forward and placed his elbows onto his knees looking into the camera. 'I know where you live. Where you go, your home number, your brothers address. Your father's address. Torment is what I feed on. Yours especially. I know you're hurting inside, you'll learn to live with the pain. I did, I want you to experience my pain. The pain of not having a father. The pain of him getting killed. Getting impaled through the heart. You'll experience that pain. Who'd you think that is in the corner? Some cameraman? This is my camera. And someone you know very well in the corner.' A sinister laugh was given off by Jinosyde. He turned his head toward the wall he walked to, slowly lifted his hand and grabbed something. He then pulled down it down and all light came on. Why look where they are. In Steele's apartment. Who is that in the corner? With the black mask over his face? Jinosyde slowly pushed himself off of the chair and walked over toward the corner in an arrogant, but sinister stride. He grabbed the mask. He proceeded to snatch it off, but stopped. 'I'll let you figure out who it is.' He walked over toward the camera, and started to fidget with it.
