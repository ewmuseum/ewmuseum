Have I truely evolved..?????

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/31/03 Xavier Citizen is standing in his kitchen completely nude, save for the Evolved title which covers up certain unmentionable items. He's fixing himself some pancakes and sausage, but thoughts are definitely on the night before..... 'Finally I emerge out of the shadow of Malcolm King. I have accomplished something not even he could do and that is win a championship belt here in Evolution. But now my focus.....has changed, I must right the injustices of those who have wronged me. And much like I orchestrated the departure of his mentor from ever wrestling for Evo ever again.....I must do the same to Luther Wisdom, who ironically will be my tag team partner this up coming Legends.' some grease pops out of the skillet striking the title belt as well as a portion of his lower abdomen. The pain stings for a minute but soon fades as the naked Evo Superstar continues...... 'Last night Seara what we shared between us was mere foreplay. Goal one was accomplished......taking from you the title because quite frankly I can. Step two will be a little more simple, preventing you from using this setback as a springboard for greater things. Finally my dear sweet Seara; ridding Evolution of you once and for all....as I originally intended. So next time you are making whatever you call it with Scott Frost...it'll be my image you see, and my name you'll be screaming....however I doubt it'll be because of a fit of extasy, but I can't do everything for Frost now can I...' he removes his pancakes and sausage from the stove...... 'Plus with all the other people I've managed to piss off Wisdom, Big Daddy Dreamer, Asis to name a few....the revenge list is long indeed. However I am a fighting champion and being a part of a tag match isn't my idea of defending....so I'll take the titles name to heart, and put it on the line.....damn what the Evo booking committee says. If Big Daddy Dreamer or Heart Break Chick get the pinfall...they win the title. Sure that screws Luther Wisdom but what do I care?' he sits on the hard wooden chair, naked except for the belt....he looks at it and begins talking to it..... '.....you mean an awful lot to me; you know that don't you? Don't worry after breakfast we'll pay Seara a visit and see how she's doing after last night.' he looks up with a sly smile..... '....what the hell the belt can come too.' he takes off the belt; his private parts now obscured by the table... 'As for you...Evolved title seems too impersonal; I'm gonna name you Scotty after the man I have plans to take everything away from....' he throws the belt clear across the room....... 'NOW SHUT UP SCOTTY; and let me get something to eat.' He begins to eat his breakfast as the eVolved Championship or the newly christened 'Scotty' sits in a corner of his kitchen, waiting to be defended.
