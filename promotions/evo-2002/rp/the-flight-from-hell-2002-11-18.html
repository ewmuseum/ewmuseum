The flight from hell...

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.18.02 ~~A crowd of people flood the floor of the Toronto airport, different kinds of people in Canada for different reasons. Perhaps some are dodging the Army or maybe even dealing drugs. But another group of men are there for a different reason…and that reason is to destroy all that step in there way…this is the story of ‘Throw Shocka from the plane.’~~ ‘I swear I will come down if you just give me a damn drink!’ A voice screams. ‘Okay sir just sit down in your seat.’ A lady says. Whispering comes and now you see thug roC standing in the aisle way of a plane trying to contain Shocka. Jason Steele is taking off his belt and sitting on the other side of Shocka. Thug roC does the same, as Shocka doesn’t notice as he is looking in the direction of the flight attendant. Suddenly both Thug roC and Jason Steele make there move and wrap the belts around the arm rests and Shocka’s arms pulling them tight and leaving him contained to his seat. Both men high five and laugh as Shocka looks down at his arms and starts to kick the seat in front of him. A head pops up and it’s SAI! The Chinese hooker himself. ‘Sai save you!’ Sai jumps into the aisle in a Karate pose but Thug roC catches him off guard with a right hook. Jason Steele studies Shocka for a moment then speaks. ‘Looks like someone made a little friend on that “ride” you told us about!’ Jason then begins to laugh.. ‘Shut up and get these damn belts off of me!’ Shocka screams with impatience. ‘Not until we get to Toronto…’ roC speaks sitting down next to Shocka patting his head. ‘Hope you used the bathroom before we left…’ roC says laughing as Shocka grows a little red in the face. ‘Oh my god what died?!’ You hear Mark Steele scream from behind them. ‘I think it was something in pants’ Shocka says with a laugh. ‘Man what has gotten into you?’ RoC questions. ‘I mean last week you were Mr. Intensity and this week you are taking yourself way to lightly.’ ‘Yeah man come on, you have the biggest series of matches in your career and you aren’t even focused on them!’ Steele shouts at him. ‘I figured that if I take myself to seriously the chances that I make a mistake will be greater…but if I take myself lightly if I make a mistake I won’t even care…’ Shocka speaks with a lighthearted tone. ‘Well if you make a mistake you won’t be in the Road to Glory tournament anymore damnit.’ Thug roC yells getting impatient with him. ‘But if I take myself to seriously I could also snap…’ Shocka says getting a little more serious. ‘I haven’t been taking my medication…’ He says with his head down. ‘WHAT?!’ roC screams. ‘How in the hell could you not be taking your medication…this is going to be the biggest PPV in eVolution history…’ roC screams. ‘Just leave it be man its for the better….’ Shocka speaks. A hard slap comes from thug roC at these words from Shocka and his head shoots toward thug roC with an evil glare. ‘Don’t you ever fucking touch me like that again!’ Shocka screams. ‘See…your rage is gonna overcome you and he’s going to take over…’ roC says a little nervous. Shocka begins flexing his forearms and you can see the belts expanding slightly. ‘What in the hell are you talking about…’ Steele looks a bit confused… ‘He comes out?’ ‘It’s a long story…’ roC speaks a little uncomfortable as the scene fades
