'The Reoccurring Struggle'

3 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-03 Struggle - To make strenuous or violent efforts against opposition. Every minute of a day, a human is wandering the earth struggling.. the struggle surpasses and they move on with their ordinary life. But to one man, the struggle has followed like a hunter to its prey. A reoccurring nightmare in a twisted, tortured mind.. a straight jacket can contain a man for years and years. Not able to free themselves... The dream reoccurs of a struggle... The struggle takes place in a white padding cell. The dream reoccurs, the struggle of one ravenous monster. Set aside from the world, the struggle to escape. Escape from the confinement... The animal cannot free himself from the death lock which is a straight jacket. “Argh... Ahhhh!” The noises echo, slowly fading into a distant memory. The urge to break free, the urge to escape forever. The white padding cell can hold anybody. A mere human can suffer mental illness in this confinement. But to wonder how can a normal person be kept inside this structure to break bodies and minds. Some of us have gone through the torture all our lives... can we really adapt? One man has been through this and stills keeps his mentality...or does he? The world knows this animal as Hymm... Him who doth possess the primitive instinct. Him who doth hunt. Him who doth kill. Him who doth survive. He, is him...Hymm. The struggle for survival reoccurs.. The reoccurring dream plays repeatedly. The agony from the imagery of the dream takes its toll... Not one can take this punishment.. this gross injustice. A gross injustice indeed. Placed in a cell, covered with white... contained inside a straight jacket, but for how long? How long can one keep a animal tamed? Captured? Domesticated? Not once do we hear about the terrifying truth on what goes on through these walls. Stories have been told from generation to generation... a reoccurring struggle to escape... The struggle for escaping.. The speed increases of the dream... thoughts become louder, louder! Images of pain and tortured souls race through ones mind. The struggle continues, it reoccurs for the long run. Distant voices are heard as they slowly go away.... but surely come back. Your soul crumbles in straight fear. Sweat pours down your body, the images race and race throughout your mind. The throbbing gets to you as you feel the temperature rises... rises until a unbelievable degree is reached. The white pads become stained with red blood... The illusions have reached its toll... The unbearable thoughts continue. A man rises, a tortured soul rises alongside. A straight jacket is torn to shreds as a demonically a animal breaks free from his own power. A demonic structure of a human breaths heavily. Hymm stares possessively into the camera. The evil glare... strikes fears in the viewers. “My struggle is over... but it is only the beggining” The words alone leave you breathless. A man that never speaks comes across with a strong point. The struggle has diminished, your struggle has just begun.
