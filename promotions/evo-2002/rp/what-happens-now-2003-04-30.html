What happens now?

30 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/03 Fiona will win We need a more credible Evolved Champion MLX who? 'I heard the whispers....and I ignored them. I heard the naysayers...and didn't give a damn...I heard it all and I laughed. Laughed all the way to the proverbial bank. Laughed at those who wish to protect their own spot.....by challenging mine. Laughed all the while knowing each and every one of you could have just puckered up and kissed my ass. Laughed.....as I entered the ring knowing I was going to end the streak of the hottest rising star in evolution. All good things must come to and end my dear, and fortunately for me....I have never been that wholesome. So I laughed and I laughed and I laughed.....but then my laughter stopped.' MLX is shown hanging upside down from one of the rafters high above the arena. The Evolved Championship is clutched to his chest as tightly as possible..... 'My laughter was stopped because on the night of my greatest triumph....when I showed the whole world exactly what I was made of, MALCOLM KING upstaged me yet again. This seems to be some recurring theme between King and myself. What little success I get, he feels the need to remind everyone he's still alive. However this week on Legends he promises to do something he hasn't done with me since we met......that is have a civilized conversation with me. Do I trust him....no. Am I afraid of him....no. Will I turn the other cheek....no. You see much like all those naysayers since I won the Evolved title.....KING is just as ignorant; only with him moreso because he should have an idea on what he's talking about.' he pulls himself up before the blood rushes to his head...... 'Problem with all those naysayers....is that when the line is drawn in the sand, no one ever seems bold enough to cross it. They seem to want to judge...and yet don't want to do anything about it. The Evolved title is open to any challenge which come my way, because unlike some people here I don't use my spot on the card as some sort of protective blanket....even if that means lowering yourself, or moving your self up the card to shut me up. I welcome it.....because whatever you dish out, will be repaid in kind. Trust me I'm not that hard to find.....'
