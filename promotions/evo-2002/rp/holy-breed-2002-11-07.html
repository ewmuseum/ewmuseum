Holy Breed

7 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/11/02 (The scene opens up with an Evolution camera crew walking down a wet, dimly-lit street somewhere in what is believed to be South Texas, maybe San Antonio. It must be, for we can see the Tower of Americas in the distance. It is cold out, the bums in the street are trying to cover up from the below freezing weather. Small flakes of snow are coming from overhead, as more of the homeless scatter into their various holes, and boxes. Then a shadow is spotted in the distance, a cloud of steam or smoke coming from around him. As it approaches, the camera crew begins to kind of back away, but they stop, waiting for to see if this person will show theirself. Soon you can see this person's long hair waving in the wind. Then, we can see this person is none other than Ben Juventud. He is clad in a long, black leather trenchcoat, black leather gloves, black Raybans, a black Machinehead 'Burn my eyes' shirt, baggy black jeans, and of course...his black combat boots. He grins slightly and leans against a wall of a building next to him.) Juventud: Glad you could join me on an evening such as this... (Juventud reaches into the front pocket of his coat, and pulls out a pack of Camel Reds. He pops a cigarette out, puts it in his mouth, and lights it with his Zippo...kindly adorned with the Confederate Flag. He takes a drag off of his cigarette and looks down at the ground sort of laughing to himself.) Juventud: How goddamned pathetic. How oh so idiotic can he be? Dreamer...yeah, I'm talking about him, a loser, a in-bred ass. For one, how the fuck do you think that you Can even justify the level of competition of me? Can you even come to terms on how brutal this will be for your behalf? Can you even turn the ego dial down a little bit, just to see that simply...YOUR FUCKED? It's pure comedy gold, Ben Juventud Vs Big Daddy Dreamer Come on...how can you not laugh at that? OH WAIT! I forgot, you not the man who is going to be giving out the ass kicking. (He takes another drag, and lets the smoke free of his lungs. Still smiling.) Juventud: Now...let's get serious here. Dreamer, I will break you down individually First The Mind, all the mind games in this match will have you dazed and dazzled then I will move in for the kill. That hurt eye of yours?! Shame look at me 100% ready. But as for Mr. Dreamer...who in the hell are you? What crack did you crawl out of? Personally I could care less, but I sure would like to hear as to why in the hell I got signed to face some pathetic little old Timer... Just to clue you in as if you forgot who you were when you began hiding, your the old timer. You know? Seriously, I really don’t know how the hell you were allowed to weasel your damn way into this match. Because...I know it sure as hell wasn't to even the odds. Maybe in that feeble little mind of yours you decided that it was time for you to get a little bit of air time...or maybe it was to see if you could actually hack it. I'll tell you this my friend, you can't and won't hack it against the new breed of Evolution, The NEW man to turn the heads of the watches. And I believe I speak for the People in OSE here, I think it would be best for you to just slip away, and hide in a little corner somewhere, because...you don’t look in good favour for this match, you might get hurt and we don’t want a broken neck on OSE television now do we?. So do yourself a favor...run, and never look back. (Juventud takes one last drag off of his cigarette, and he flicks it into the street. He looks up to the camera, and pulls his head back.) Juventud: Now...Dreamer, for your own good, I suggest that you keep your fucking mouth shut this week, we don’t want to see a big hype to see you fail, to crack under pressure, To be the great big let down that you are. I'm sick and fucking tired of People droning on and on like a broken goddamned record. I'm sick of having to hear that annoying ass voice of people who have stud in my way before. So I suggest dreamer, that you take what life you have left and clear off, away from me, away from evolution and a shure as hell site away from Sov. Get a fucking brain and realize something. The reason your not the champ here is because...YOU FUCKING SUCK! Your promo's, your skills...all are lacking...like I said, you fucking suck...plain and damned simple. So do me a favor...please, stop touting about how good you are, because frankly, I'm far from impressed. Though, I know for sure that you will be, once you finally get to face Me, Ben Juvie in that ring this Wednesday...something I look forward to. You know why? Because I finally get to shut you the hell up...I finally get to shove my foot down your ego fed throat. Juventud: So, I guess...in short Dreamer. Fuck you... (He looks to the street and watches a car pass by slowly. Then as it turns a corner, gunshots ring off in the night sky, and an engine's roar...and tires squeal. Juventud looks back at the camera crew, who are trembling. He smiles and stares into the camera.) Juventud: Now let me tell you why I signed for evolution, simple matter of the facts was I need a place to shine, I looked at the roster and I see some stars…Xander, Mason and Sovereign and then I saw some week challengers some HAS BEEN’s and the one that stuck out the most was…you guessed right Big Daddy Dreamer, Dreamer your best days are past you, EDW you was good, not great but good, look at me, and well I am on my way to being the elite player in Evo, all I need is a few wins under my belt and well I will soon have them, starting with you, then again...it might start to annoy me, beating everybody, might get a bit boring but then I could face Sov, the master the man I envy, look at him dreamer that’s the kind of man you want to be, that’s the kind of man who diserves to be the champion, not a man like you. so I advise you to shut the fuck up before you even start. You can bet one thing for sure, it's this. Once you step into the ring myself...you are going to beg for goddamned mercy at my young fresh and hellbent hands. Your stepping right towards the gates of hell, so when you step through...watch your step...it's a long fall. And none of that crappy luck will ever end up saving your sorry ass. (Juventud looks to the ground and stuffs his hands in his pockets. He breathes out steam into the night sky. He turns towards the street, but looks slightly at the camera.) Juventud: So far, so good Dreamer. I've been quite successful at running your little Mind gauntlet, I hope that your aware that it's only a matter of time before we face each other one on one...but the thing that will kill anyone who stands in my way and tries to take this match away from me, I am up for this. I will have earned it. And I will revel in every second that I get in the ring with you. Dreamer, I'll be waiting...patiently... (Juventud is now looking at the ground.) Juventud: Come Wednesday Dreamer you will truly know why I call myself your Holy Breed... (He pauses for a second and looks to the camera, turns, and does a crucifixion pose...his head lowered.) Juventud:...why I am your fucking Savoir... (The camera fades out as Juventud drops his arms, and walks off into the dank night.)
