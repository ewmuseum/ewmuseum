The Power of the Dollar :Part II:

18 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-18 :Flashback: 'Mr Coles' the doorman said somewhat suprised to Ethan Coles marching in his direction, 'I didn't know you had a booking for today?' 'I don't' Ethan replied, 'usual table will do' 'But....' the doorman said slightly off guard. 'It is ready isn't it?' Ethan demanded to know. 'Of course sir bar a few minor adjustments' the doorman said as you watched him squirm before Ethans very eyes. 'May I suggest a drink at the bar sir before lunch?' 'I am an athlete, do I look like I can go on drinking binges at this time of day?' Ethan retorted. 'No sir' was the pathetic response. 'You know I could make a call and have your ass fired this second you know that don't you?' Ethan paused, 'I am going to go for a drink and in a few minutes I am set to be joined by a Mr Dukes. Show him to the bar, else you may find yourself sailing from this dive on your P-45. Comprende manual?' 'Yes sir. But sir...' the doorman replied before being interupted again. 'WHAT?!' Ethan snapped. 'No matter sir' the doorman head replied as he head dropped. 'My name isn't Manual' he muttered once Ethan was out of earshot. The restaurant in question was a small Italian in South London. Manual as Ethan continually refered to him as, was infact Guissepe and it was his restaurant. Despite having bought this restaurant three years ago, Ethan continued to abuse his staff by refusing to take the time to learn their names. Manual was the waitor from faulty towers, so in his mind Manual was what every waitor was called. The truth was Guiseppe had once owned this restaurant, but did not have the financhial clout to see out his dream of running the place by himself. It was not by choice he turned to Ethan, more it was essential for him to stay above water. Ethan provided the cash injection, but in return he got to treat Guiseppe and his staff like shit. In the end although it had been described to Ethan as his private area, the staff had segregrated Ethan away from the other customers for fear of him offending their custom. In an enclosed bay window area a table sat lavishly decorated with the finest silver, it was Ethans den and no expense was spared just so Ethan had little to complain about. Guiseppes had become a favourite for the B list celebraties and once or twice had been the setting for a few film stars over on filming. Ethan himself thought of himself as the star of the show and should anyone famous adorn the establishment he was keen to point that out to those in question. Ethan referred to it as doing the rounds. Normally Ethan at least warned the restaurant of his impending arrival, but having left his sparring session red with rage only a few hours earlier he had not wasted his time with that minor detail. Time is money and though Ethan had plenty of money, Ethan had neither to spare for anyone. 'Martini' Ethan said to the barman as he took a seat by the counter. 'Straight?' the barman replied without looking up from the dish washer. 'I beg your pardon' Ethan screamed in horrer, 'how dare you question my sexuality'. Everyone in the bar turned to see the look of horrer on Ethans face and the look of shock on the barmans as he realised just who had made the request. 'I was talking about the drink sir, I am so sorry' the barman said begging for forgiveness. 'Just get me the damn drink' Ethan demanded 'and may I suggest you keep your mouth closed unless spoken too!' 'Ice sir?' the waitor asked. 'YES' Ethan screamed causing a scene again. 'Is there a problem?' Guiseppe said trying to calm the situation. 'I want his ass fired' Ethan said pointing to the barman as he placed the drink on the counter. 'I'll tend to that sir' Guiseppe replied, 'in the mean time your table is ready' 'About time too' Ethan said jumping to his feet, 'next time I don't expect to have to wait. Look what happens when I do' Ethan said scowling at the waitor again. 'Yes sir' Guiseppe answered before ushering Ethan from the bar. Guiseppe led Ethan to his table, before leaving him to cool off from the earlier argument. As for the waitor in question he kept his job, had Ethan got his way on firings there would be no staff left; truth was some of the staff had been fired as many of three or four times, Ethan just forget who he had fired and Guiseppe was hardly going to remind him. Hell once, Ethan had demanded the chef be fired forgeting that was Guiseppe himself. Then again as Ethan would say...... 'Who the hell is Guiseppe?'
