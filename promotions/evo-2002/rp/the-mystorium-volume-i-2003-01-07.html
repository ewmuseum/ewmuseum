The mystorium(Volume I)

7 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.07.03 ~Last time we saw our hero he was in a hometown bar that he has ventured to before, he was left in a drunken stupor overcome by past memories…and now? Well we find him driving down main street at an average pace he has his one hand placed on the steering wheel while the other is directed to fiddle with his lips. He wears a backwards St. Louis Rams cap and a gold chain goes around his neck. A Marshall Faulk jersey is placed roughly on and crinkled in many places. He also has on a pair of khaki pants. The Universal title is not with him…today is about something better…some bigger his first day of official vacation. He studies the road as he drives in an endless pattern of boredom. His cell phone goes off and he reaches toward the stereo and the hands free speaker phone comes on through his stereo.~ ‘Yeah.’ He says in a calm voice. ‘Hey man. Its Darryl how you doin?!’ The voice erupts over the speakers. ‘Woo! Good man…I haven’t talked to you in ages…what’s up?’ Shocka utters with a bit more excitement to him. ‘Aren’t you gonna be in town the next few days man?’ The long time friend asks with some intuitive nature. ‘Yeah, I’m gonna be here until the 21st’ Shocka says with some gleam to him. ‘Well maybe we can get together and catch up or something man.’ Darryl says. ‘Sounds good man, you got my number hit me up anytime…maybe we can go out clubbin tonight and try to find some women.’ Shocka says. ‘What?!’ The man says in a sort of confused tone. ‘I thought you were married dude…’ ‘Oh yeah,. That whole thing is over man…’ Shocka says in a funny tone. ‘What you got divorced?!’ Darryl Yells. ‘Not quite yet…’ Shocka says with a laugh. ‘So are you two just apart or like its not finalized yet?’ Darryl asks. ‘Well its just not finalized…I really don’t wanna sign the papers because that bitch is gonna try and take me for everything I got…’ Shocka says. ‘Yeah and I bet she would get it in this town…people are kind of against you since what happened this week on Legends…’ ‘You think that would affect it?’ Shocka says in moderately serious voice. ‘Um…I’m guessing so you know how judge’s are in this damn town…’ Darryl says. ‘But anyways…whata think?’ Shocka says. ‘Yeah maybe we can do something tonight…’ The man says sort of trailing off. ‘But hey man…I know this kid’s family he just got in a pretty bad accident a gun went off and he was shot in his neck they think he might die…’ ‘And…’ Shocka says. ‘Well he is a really big fan of yours and he would like nothing more than to meet you…I mean its like his last wish or something man..’ Darryl says. ‘Well I will see what I can do…I mean that’s horrible do they know for sure if it was an accident?!’ Shocka says.
