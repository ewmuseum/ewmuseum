Date: 2004-02-29 The Ethan Coles Commemorative Coronation Ceremony :Foreword: Welcome friends, to one what will undoubtably be seen as one of the biggest nights in the history of the Sports Entertainment industry. I, Ethan Coles, am fortunate enough to have a championship reign now in the offing not as a result of the power of the pound but instead thanks to the gruelling exploits I exposed my body to these past few weeks; grueling exploits for you the fans. Tonight is not an attempt by your favourite Englishman to revel in his own glory but an attempt to raise the profile of a division once dominated by mindless thuggery. I am no thug, I am Ethan M Coles, son of Sir John Coles and though I may no longer be taking the family path I have no intention of dropping the basic ethics and strengths that gave my family prominance this past century. Its for that reason I chose here as the setting; the Coles family estate was the obvious choice since I am a Coles, while wrestling may not be a past family stomping ground, a Coles success is a Coles success. We celebrate success, we never grow tired of it and we certainly won't accept any substitute for it so long as the Coles sit upon this planet. So wipe your feet, dust off your best suit (it might just be enough) and let the Coles treat you to a small taste of the kind of success that is possible regardless of your demographic state. I am Ethan Coles.... I am your champion....... :Schedule: Opening drinks in the lobby. Concert upon the lawn. Coronation Parade. Retire to the dining area to enjoy a three course meal, with entertainment provided. Speeches. Your champions closing speech. Releasing of the dogs. (Joke! Well at least it is so long as you don't take too long too leave)
