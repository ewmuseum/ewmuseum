Fools Errand 1

6 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-06 “Ah fuck.” His beer glass spilled over onto a document with his and Tyler Davidson’s signatures must have been his eVo contract. “For fuck sake, I hate it when that happens,” he shrugged, sweeping the document onto his purple shag carpet. “Oh well.” He’d not been TOO psyched about the pay per view, the fact that he was going to kick the shit into Damon O’Halloran and Sean Sterling was nearly inevitable. As long as he walked out as the cell champion, looked really cool, made his opponents feel really shitty about themselves little else was important. He knew all too well that Damon O’Halloran was scum; he was the kind of person that would lick your arse for nothing, only that you’d owe him something in return. He was flying high and within a few hours he’d be laughing in their faces. “Fucking hell,” he sighed as he picked up the ringing phone. “Yeah?” ... “Me?” ... He hung up the phone with an astonished look on his face. It was Damon... And he was being nice...
