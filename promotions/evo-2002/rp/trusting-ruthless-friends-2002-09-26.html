Trusting Ruthless Friends

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 26, 2002 Two seperate worlds, opposites, yet alike in so many many ways. Both these worlds are engaged in an eternal war with one another. The war of Good verses Evil. Which is which, you decide. Only one thing will out last both armies, only one thing will survive in the end. And that special magical little item is that inwhich we call, undieing love. 'Scottie, I don't trust her.' Kelsey says to Frost. 'Who?' Frost asks. 'HBC, I don't think you need to be involved with her.' exclaims Kelsey. The scene slowly fades in revealing Scott Frost and his wife Kelsey standing inside of a gym area. Nobody is in the the gym room except for the two. 'Kelsey, you have nothing to worry about. Nobody is going to take me away from you. Only you can do that.' Frost gently speaks to Kelsey. 'Xander, Jinx, and HBC. They are all my friends. I was ready for this, I was made for this. We are going to dominate the wrestling world.' Frost smiles. 'And guess what?' adds Frost. 'What?' asks Kelsey. 'You are going to be there with me. Technically you are my manager, and my wife. So you are allowed anywhere you want. You get to play your part too.' Frost tells Kelsey, trying to calm her down. 'You will be by my side, every step of the way.' Frost assures Kelsey. Kelsey looks down at the ground, and then she places her hands on his chest coming up closer to him. 'You promise?' asks Kelsey. 'I promise.' says Frost. Kelsey smiles, and gently kisses Frosts' lips. 'I Love You.' says Frost. Kelsey smiles and kisses Frost, and then looks at him. 'I Love You too.' Kelsey replies. Frost looks around, and then steps away. 'I gotta go to the bathroom, go run the tredmil and I will come back and join you when I am done.' says Frost. Kelsey nods her head and they go their seperate ways, as Kelsey gets on her tredmil the beeping sound of the Mexican hat dance is heard, and Kelsey stops and quickly strides to her gym bag to pull out a cell phone. 'Hello.' says Kelsey. 'Hey.' Kelsey replies 'Listen, call me on the speaker phone I am on the tredmil right now. Scottie is in the bathroom.' says Kelsey. Kelsey hangs up the cell phone, and almost immediately a telephone can be heard ringing. Kelsey once again quickly strides across the room and presses a button and runs back to the tredmil. 'Hello.' Kelsey says, once again. 'Hows it going?!' says a voice. 'Pretty good. How bout the ever popular L2J?' replies Kelsey. 'It's good. Got this Universal Championship thing. I should be wearing that title, not Sovereign. But all those chair shots, they are messing with my head' L2J says with laughingly. Kelsey just looks around not knowing what to say. 'Well, I just wanted to call and see if Scottie was around. Last time I saw him he was wrestling the independent circuit in Detroit, Michigan. And we haven't had time to hang out. But with this whole Unlimited thing, I don't know if I should. I didn't know Frost was a member of the Unlimited. You do know that they are the 'bad' people in eVolution, right?' questions L2J. 'Yeah, but he is happy. He wasn't a member until Digital Destruction. Xander and Jinx just asked him for help, and said if he did he would be apart of the greatest stable ever created. I mean, I couldn't help but push the fact that things like that just don't happen to an independent wrestler all the time. So I'm supporting him. I'm sorry that you hadn't offered anything. I bet you that you and him would be a very dominate tag team. Maybe you will have the privelage to wrestle one another in the future?' Kelsey exclaims. There is silence, but Kelsey continues to run on her tredmil. 'That's kinda what I wanted to talk to Frost about. I don't like him going Unlimited. It's bad for his persauna, even if he is known around the indie circuit as the 'Cold Hearted One' I don't want to see him ruined in the majors, ya know?' says L2J. 'Yeah...' Kelsey is interrupted by a beep on the phone. 'Hold on, L. Someone is beeping in, gotta answer that.' Kelsey tells L2J as she runs to the phone and drinks some water. She presses a button. 'Yes?' Kelsey asks. 'Is Scott there?' says a voice. 'No, may I ask who is calling?' Kelsey questions. 'Yes, tell him he have a plan.......it involves, L...2...J.' the voice says and hangs up. Ironically enough, just as Kelsey clicks back over to the other line with L2J waiting, Scott frost walks in. 'Hello, L?' Kelsey asks. 'Yeah, I'm still here.' L2J replies. 'L! What's going on?' Scott bellows towards the phone. 'Nothing much, just wanting to talk to you about something.' L2J replies. 'Uum....hold on L. Let Scott call you back. I just remembered something I was meaning to talk to him about. I will see you later, nice talking to ya.' Kelsey says. 'Alright, see ya.' L2J replies. Kelsey hangs up, and looks over towards Scott. 'Scottie. Someone called from Unlimited, I think it sounded like Xander.' says Kelsey. 'What'd they want?' asks Frost. 'They want....' Kelsey pauses. 'What?' Frost asks again. There is silence. 'Kelsey?' Frost questions. Kelsey looks at Scott. 'Scottie, I think Xander wants you to take out L2J.' Kelsey replies. 'What makes you say that?' Frost again questions. 'Because the person that called, said that they have a plan and it involves L2J. Please don't do anything to hurt L. He is our friend.' Kelsey exclaims. Frost looks around, then he gets a sinister look on his face. 'Kelsey.' Frost says. 'You gotta be ruthless, to get recognized.....remember that.' Frost says as the scene fades away.
