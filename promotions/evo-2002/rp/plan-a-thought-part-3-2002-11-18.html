Plan a thought...part 3

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th November 2002 The scene opens back to the room where we last seen Seara and Frost. It is light and unlike before we can now see a large training set up. It looks to be a personal gym in which Notorious’ members have been submitted as members, free to use it when they feel the need. At the moment, the camera has not spotted the figure of Frost anywhere in the room, although it is certain that he is somewhere close, watching carefully as female wrestler Seara trains harder than she has ever pushed herself before. Rhys unlike Frost is clearly seen as he stands above Seara encouraging her and trying to push her further. He knows that she is on a natural high, different from the one she gets out of injuring people and making the blood flow from as many cuts on their body as she can humanly do with her fists and the few weapons she uses both in and out of the ring. Rhys – Concentrate Katherine, if you don’t concentrate your going to hurt yourself. Seara – I am, I’m working as hard as I can. Rhys – Your not, your thinking about other things, now pay attention and do this right or you’ll get hurt or I’ll take a page from me Da’s book. Seara pauses in mid thought and lowers herself to the floor, she holds herself close to the tiles as she looks up at Rhys and laughs slightly, she knows he is right, she is not working to her full potential, but the thought of what happened with Frost is the most prominent thing on her mind at the present. Rhys – Come on Katherine, work. You may have trained longer than ever before, and you may be doing more work but your not exhausting yourself, your not sweating, or even breathing hard, you look like you just started this second. Seara – Surely that’s a good thing, means my stamina has gotten better since last time you trained with me, which was well before Faith. Proves I’ve been working on my breathing and concentrating more on what I’m doing. As she continues to talk she refuses to look up at Rhys who lowers himself to his knees in front of Seara. However he fails to look at the female, and instead stares away to the other side of the room where a shaded figure stands with weights in his hands, pushing himself, making himself concentrate and thing about everything that is happening to him. Seara – Our secret, but for how long? Asks Seara, she looks nowhere in particular, but instead gets to her feet and walks over to the treadmill where she begins a half hour training run. She starts with a gentle walk, but within moment’s she bursts out and turns the speed up until she is sprinting to keep herself on the machine. Knowing us, not long when it involves the others. I mean come on; so far we have had no secrets from each other. And if we couldn’t stop it happening when I came here before, do you think we will be able to hide it long enough in front of the guys? Spinning around, the camera focuses on the figure who was a moment ago lifting weights above his head. He is now standing looking at Seara, watching her run as though she were being chased and her life depended on it. The camera zooms in and focuses a bit more to reveal Scott Frost; he is in a pair of baggy basketball shorts and trainers, and does nothing but look at the female. To his left and slightly behind him is Rhys who says nothing. Seara – I don’t mean with those two, I think they should know, I mean if it is going to change the way we act and behave around each other at fights, then the guys need to know what is going on. What I mean is how long do you think we can keep it between just Notorious, I mean if something happens to you in your fights, you know I’m going to be the first out there, and I’m hoping you’d do the same for me, people will eventually get suspicious of it. They can only say that it is because we are both Notorious for a while before they begin to question it, and you know Fritz, he will jump to the conclusion before L and Jameson even know about us. Frost – One, you know I will be there to back you up, your not just my team mate now, and two, it’ll probably be out within the next few weeks. Look at when you joined, people thought you were with Asis, and the first time we talked, you were scared of me, but people thought I was cheating on Kelsey with you. We knew all the stuff was lies, but they didn’t, however it seems one little thing is happening now. If people want to accuse us before we tell anyone then let them cause we will be spending time together as Notorious as well as spending time together as Scott and Katherine. Seara stops dead as her hand falls onto the power button on the running machine. She at some point in her life hated to be called Katherine, even now it sounded foreign to her, Rhys had always used her real name, but she had got used to it with him, with Frost however she expected him just to call her Seara. She turns to Frost and looks at him, then glances past at Rhys, he nods and throws a towel towards her, after six hours of training, he has decided that maybe she should work alone for a while, take a break, then come back, without Frost, who has become her distraction. Walking toward Rhys, the female begins to think about what he truly feels about the situation, and what he thinks will happen because of this change, the unlocking of something deep within Seara, something that she hadn’t felt before, sure she had had a boyfriend, but she had never felt like this with him, or maybe she had, maybe she chose to block it out and focus on removing herself from amateur wrestling and into mainstream wrestling. She had moved up from amateur wrestling, but her emotions, these were new, it was like starting something all over again, she was back in the amateur leagues and she didn’t know what to do about it. Now she would need Rhys for more than just a trainer, he would become her mentor, a guide, he would help her discover her true feelings, he would protect her from her demons, and not let them destroy her or ruin Frost’s career and life. Seara was unstable at times, she knew this, and now with Frost it scared her, what would she do if she turned on him, not only would she lose love, but she would lose her team mate, she feared herself, feared the lack of control she had over her ever changing moods. Seara – The tiger hidden in the bush, waiting for the right time to pounce, to attack, to kill. Seara’s words are quietly whispered into the ear of Rhys as she passes him by and heads off to get changed. As she gets just past him, he turns to face her and with a calm Irish accented voice remarks. Rhys – The animal that loves, preys, kills to survive, the control over passion is always there inside the beast. Wait for the beast to come to you, do not go for the beast, she will kill you. Seara is your beast Katherine and you have her caged, now learn to belong to the now. The strength to belong to the now. Rhys had always told her to belong to the now, and as Frost listened on; he seemed somehow alone and isolated as though he had come across a conversation on a bus and had listened to it. He understood nothing about it, but it was not his greatest worry. What worried Frost had been Rhys line Wait for the beast to come to you, do not go for the beast. Although a metaphor for Seara’s own darker side, the side filled with rage, the side she had become all those years ago. Frost could not help but think that somehow it was also referring to Asis who had attacked Frost more than once now. His worry, was Seara, going after Asis could prove fatal for her, she had got off lucky before with him, but with Xander behind him, he could do serious damage to her. Seara re-enters the room, changed and freshened up, ready to leave and get something to eat. Inviting Frost and Rhys she heads outside to light a cigarette, but is only joined by Frost, and as the pair leave to go for dinner, the scene fades to black.
