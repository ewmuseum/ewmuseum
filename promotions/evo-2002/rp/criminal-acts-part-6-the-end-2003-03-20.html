Criminal Acts, Part 6; The End?

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March, 20, 2003 -=[Greg Vulgar, as nervous as ever, walks from his car to yet another 7-11. He keeps looking over his shoulder, knowing that he’s a wanted man. He nervousness was best displayed earlier when he thought the lady was about to call the cops on him. He walks up the 7-11, with the mindset on buying some Black and Milds until he realizes a poster on the window. He walks by it as if it’s nothing. Then he stops and looks back at it. The poster read: FOUND; one box full of stolen jewelry along with a purse with the unusual tag of the letter “c” on it. If this is yours, or you want it back without the cops being around. Be at the old RFK stadium, 11pm Saturday night.]=- “SONUVABITCH!” Greg Vulgar yells very loudly, scaring birds away and all -=[We look at the sunset as it is about 5:30pm Sunday. Thanks to the magic of television, we can easily fast forward the day; watching the sun suddenly drop and the moonrise. We are at the parking lot of the old RFK stadium in Maryland, the former home of the Washington Redskins. Greg Vulgar’s rental car is circling the parking lot multiple times, thinking to meet Thug roC in the lot, but he doesn’t see them. He has the watch on his wrist, and the necklace on the dashboard. He stops and turns to his right where a gate entrance is obvious. It’s so obvious that it has neon signs pointing to it saying, “Enter here Dumbas.”]=- “Dumbas?” Vulgar asks himself while reading the sign. -=[Dumbass is spelled wrong…probably one of Machine’s signs. Vulgar gets out his car, acting as tough as possible, but he knows this might be a set up. He goes through the player’s entrance gate of the stadium and walks down the long tunnel. On the other side of the tunnel is the field. All the lights are on and it’s still leftover from an old soccer game, so the nets are still up. Around where the 20-yard line is, Thug roC sits in a chair, playing a gameboy advance. Greg Vulgar has the watch and necklace in hand now, as he walks towards Thug roC.]=- “Aight wrestle boy,” Vulgar begins. “I got your stuff, where’s mine?” -=[Thug roC keeps his eyes on the game as he points down to the bag containing all of Vulgar’s stuff. He saves his game before standing up and looking Vulgar in his face]=- “Aight lets make this exchange so we can end all of this. I want to get out of here so I won’t have you following me everywhere I go.” “Aww,” Thug roC mocks. “does the big bad boy want to go home now? How sweet.” “Do you think you scare me roC? You think I’m terrified of you or something? Well you’re wrong, you see I can take you out whenever I damnwell feel like it. So just give me my stuff so you can take this damn necklace and watch” -=[Vulgar throws the watch and necklace at the feet of Thug roC. Thug roC looks down at them with a pissed look on his face. Thug roC reaches down and picks the bag up from the ground and throws it at Vulgar’s face connecting with it. Vulgar drops to the ground instantly and you can see bricks fall out the bag as it hits the ground.]=- -=[Thug roC jumps on Vulgar and starts hitting mounted punches. Vulgar is able to shove the small cruiserweight off of him as he gets to his feet. With a bloody face, Vulgar gets up and ducks a spinning kick from roC and hits him with a boxer-like right hook. Thug roC drops momentarily. Vulgar takes the chair roC was sitting in, and folds it up. He raises the steel chair high above his head and swings at roC, but roC with a drop toe hold, causes Vulgar to drop on it himself. Both Vulgar and Thug roC get up around the same time and charge at each other. Vulgar pulls out a cow taser and electorcutes Thug roC. Thug roC drops to the ground in pain and Vulgar is quick to escape]=- -=[Vulgar runs as fast as he can, knowing this was all a set up. His face still drips with blood and face red from the punches. He reaches the entrance gate, only to find it shut and a silver SUV parked on the other side of it. There is only a small space between the top of the gate and the ceiling. Greg starts climbing until he’s suddenly pulled down by the near 7 feet tall Vega. Greg looks at the big man with fear and goes for his taser but Vega punches him, causing him to drop it. Vega takes Vulgar by his head and attempts to ram him into the gate, but Greg blocks it with his foot and slams Vega’s instead. Vulgar goes for the taser, but Vega grabs him by his hair and pulls him back. Vega lifts him up in a bearhug and rams him into the gate. Vulgar slowly slides down to the floor, until kicking Vega right in the family jewls. Vulgar crawls and grabs his taser. Vulgar quickly tasers Vega until he drops to the ground. Vulgar soon realizes that the taser is out of power and throws it on the ground]=- -=[Vulgar scales the gate in a spiderman-like fashion. He lands ontop of the SUV that was blocking the gate and onto the ground. There is 50 yards seperating himself and his car now. He doubles over, trying to catch his breath when the SUV car door swings open, hitting Vulgar in the back. Vulgar hits the ground and looks at who did this, and the other 7 footer, Machine, steps out. Vulgar tries to plead with Machine before low blowing him as wel. Vulgar then holds his shin in pain. It’s obvious that Machine had a cup on. He lifts Vulgar up and throws him into the SUV as hard as possible. Machine measures him and attempts the big boot, but misses and goes through the car window. Vulgar hits a few shots on Machine’s face before Machine shoves him to the ground. He takes his foot out the window and goes after Vulgar. Vulgar rounds the SUV and opens the trunk and crawls in. He tries to close it behind him, but Machine is there to swing the trunk open. He is then surprised as Vulgar hits him across his face with a wrench. Machine drops to the ground. He gets up, staggering around and Vulgar takes him by his head and rams him into the neon sign that was outside tha gate]=- -=[Vulgar quickly runs towards his car. He is cut off by a small Toyota in which Adam Carr steps out of.]=- “SH*T!” Vulgar yells -=[Just when you thought there was nobody else to fight, the two start going at it. Vulgar takes a lot of punishment, seeing that he’s already went at it multiple times. Vulgar is getting beat badly until his trademark low blow is used. Greg Vulgar quickly runs into his car, but Adam Carr is soon following. Vulgar can’t get his car started and Vulgar pulls him out the car. With keys in his hand, Vulgar stabs Adam Carr in the stomach with them. Carr stumbles back, looking down at his own blood before Vulgar punches him with the keys in hand. Carr drops to the ground and Vulgar pulls a lead crowbar out of his car. He looks down at Carr who is out of it]=- -=[Vulgar raises the crowbar high above his head until a woman grabs him by his ear. Vulgar drops the crowbar in fear]=- “Now what have I told you Chancy?” The Woman yells. “I told you repeatidly not to steal! DIDN’T I TEACH YOU BETTER THEN THIS? HOW COULD YOU DISGRACE OUR FAMILY NAME LIKE THIS? WHEN WE GET HOME…..” -=[The woman takes Vulgar by his ear and they walk away, but you can still hear her yell at him]=- -=[Minutes later, after the woman called the cops on Vulgar, we see the ambulance and EMTs at RFK nursing to Thug roC and company. Shannon and Tasha are there to see their husbands.]=- “What are you two doing here? I thought we told you to stay home?” Thug roC asks confusingly Shannon stands there with her arms crossed. “Well we had to check and make sure everything was alright” “Besides, we knew you guys would need our help” Tasha says with a smart tone “For your information, we had everything under control.” Thug roC says, trying to convince himself as well -=[The two ladies give Thug roC a look of disbelief]=- “We did….” “Who was that woman anyways?” Vega asks. “And where did she come from?” “ See,” Shannon starts. “Clare, aka, Clare Vulgar, is on the deaconess board at the church. We just figured to clue her in on a few things, just in case you guys got in trouble.” “Well, I hate to say it, but nice going. You girls did a good job.” Adam Carr admits “Hold up, you told his mommy on him?” Thug roC asks. “Yep” “Well… whatever works. “ “Behind every great woman, there’s a great man” Tasha declares “And behind that woman, is me” Adam Carr says with a huge grin -=[Everyone stops and looks at Adam, but he doesn’t care at all]=-
