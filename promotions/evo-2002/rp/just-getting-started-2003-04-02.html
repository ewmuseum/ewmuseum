Date: 4-2-03 [It’s funny how things work out in the long run. Shocka has run from me for years but he can’t ever seem to get away from me. Never ever beaten me one on one and he’s tried many many times. Death has jsut walked in eVo's front door and few know it. Soon though the world will be introduced to the master of destruction] The scene opens up inside a dimly light bar. There’s a man standing behind the counter serving up drinks to a muscular man dressed in all black. He’s got a light an and stands up carrying a jack and coke. He walks over to an empty pool table and racks them up and lights a cigarette. He notices a cameraman walk threw the door ad he stands up waving them over “Took you slow bastards long enough to get here” Hedges says in a stern voice “We got held up in traffic!” the camera man exclaimed. Hedges slams down part of his jack and coke and laughs a bi at the man “You lying sacks of shit. We’re in the middle of downtown St. Louis it’s about midnight. I’ve lived here my whole life traffic isn’t bad. You where probably getting you’re jerk on to Shocka tapes.” Hedges yelled at him. “Hurry up I’ve got things to do” The camera man hoist it up on his should and counts down with his fingers 3…2…1 and Hedges sits on the pool table smoking. He stares blankly into the camera this his face turns to a serious expression. He begins to speak. “You know NTICW folded after I made a glorious return to the ring shortly after I came back. The most sadistic fucked mind in the game got the shaft but luckily my old buddy Omega was having a few problems here with my old punching bag Shocka. It’s puzzling even to me on how that man is still alive after all the beatings I’ve given him and I’ve watched him take. Clearly the mans ego gives him some kind of protection. I would have surely thought that he’d go running once I came back.” Hedges flicks the cigarette onto a table with a few guys drinking they look up at Hedges and he laughs and gives them the finger and goes back to talking. “Now not to many people know me here nor do they know what I’m capable of. I’ll give them a chance to soon find out why I am called the all knowing supreme being. Shocka’s been shown and soon that bitch Seara will find out. You know it’s funny she seems to think I’m some blow off help New-TV brought in to give them a hand hell know I came here to show the world that I am still a god. I’m normally not above hitting a woman if the circumstances are right like if they step out of the kitchen and she seems to have stumbled her way into my domain. Wrong move sister and you said yourself your soft and you weakness if my favorite things” Hedges takes a shot and breaks the balls and glances over at the men whoa re staring at them drinking with pissed looks on their faces “Now this Lee guy seems to me nothing more than a smaller version of Shocka. He’’s got a huge ego and he hopes around the ring like a fucking bunny. Well all know what happens to a rabbit when it runs into a truck.” Hedges glares back at the three men at the table “What the hell are they staring at” Hedges mumbles He lines up the cue and jumps it off the table and into there pitchers of beer. It spills all over them and they stand up with bright red faces. And fist clentched. “Hey how many drunks doe it take to beat three drunks” Hedges ask they come over “Shut the fu…” The mans cut off and Hedges yells “One” and punches him in the jaw knocking him onto the floor. He swings the stick and nails the other in the side of the head sending him toppling down and kicks the other man in the stomach and gives him a power bomb onto the man sprawled out on the floor. “Worthless fools. You just leanred a lesson that many other people will learn. I do what the hell I want, when I want and there’s not a single sole in the world who can stop me. My path of destruction ahs only just started here in eVo and everyone will soon lean why I am the all knowing supreme being” The scene fades as Hedges slams down his drink and throws the cup at the men on the floor. He tosses out a 50 onto the bar and walks out into the night as the scene fades
