A learning expirence (Part 2)

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.24.02 The scene kicks into gear with Shocka standing against a tree where a cross is placed in the middle of a pile of rocks. He slowly moves to a kneeling position and looks at the cross his long black trench coat hangs at each side as he exhales and you can see his breath. He pulls his arms up and brings his hands to his face and begins caressing his goatee as he seems to be in a brooding type mood. Flashback: The young Johnny stands in front of us with a rock in his hand. One of the other boys that had attacked him before stands about 5 feet away from him tied to a tree. Many kids stand far back afraid that if they move He will indeed kill the boy. Johnny turns around and takes a quick glance at the other boys he jumps forward and they all let out a gasp. A smile comes over his face. 'You all realize that I have the power...I possess something so great that not even the doctors that evaluated me can understand.' He smiles a sadistic smile as he turns back to the boy. This time he thrusts the rock into the boy stomach and he lets out a howl of pain. Jonathan drops the rock and lays a punch into the boys face. He proceeds to grasp the boys chin on both sides and yells at him. 'If you scream again I will damn sure end your life.' The boy speaks harsh words for a 6 year old. 'Hey Johnny we're sorry let him go!' A scream comes from behind. Jonathan reaches down and grabs the rock he hurls it toward the crowd without even looking before jetting his attention back to the matter at hand...back to the boy tied to the tree. 'Do you know what death is Sammuel?' Jonathan asks as he walks around him studying his face. 'I understand at the tender age of 6 and some people don't understand until they are gone...' He smiles wickedly and continues speaking. 'I have been proclaimed a genius and I will live up to the status.' Jonathan find another rock and lifts it this time slamming it onto the boys arm perhaps shattering it before he stops and looks him over you can see the pain in his eyes but he doesn't let out a yelp. 'You have already learned.' Jonathan this time drives the rock into the forehead of the boy shattering his skull and leaving him a limp body. The crowd storms him but with the turn of a head most of them stop the ones who don't are pulled back by others. 'This is the beginning of the end for all of you...You beat me once shame on you you beat me twice...shame on me!' The wicked smile on his face vanishes and he slowly looks around before one of the boys runs to the scene with a parent one of them chase down little Jonathan and tackle him to the ground. They hold him down and begin yelling at him. He simply spits in there face. 'I didn't know better!' Shocka cuts in with his face buried in hs hands. 'I didn't understand what death was...the rehabilitation should have worked...why didn't it?!' As he pulls his hands from his face a look of rage crosses over him and he begins screaming. 'Its all Societies fault! They should have punished me so I didn't do it again...' His words grow to nothing more but a whisper as he looks to the ground in shame as he slowly moves from the scene. The camera slowly fades on the old cross and pile of rocks.
