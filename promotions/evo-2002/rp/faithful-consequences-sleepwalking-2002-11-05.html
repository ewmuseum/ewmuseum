Faithful Consequences - Sleepwalking

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/5/02 I dream. You scheme. The evening was in full effect. The night had blanketed the world with its routine darkness. In respects to day, the night was hushed and silent. Only the nocturnal would be seen lurking in the streets of the forgotten cities. The hotel room was slightly lit from both the moonlight and a nearby streetlamp, with the room being a mixture of both the natural and artificial. The walls stood still, basking in the quiet that only came at this time of day. The armchair, the television, the lamps, the porch – all sat silently, seeming to be in a state of anticipation for something to happen, and drowning in their state of isolation and pause. Jason slept quietly, dreaming of a past that he could never have again. He smiled, thinking that it was really his arms that were holding her, and really his lips kissing her body, and really her warmth and the feel of her heart beating that was keeping him alive. He didn’t realize that it was only a dream. A fantasy held only inside. A barrage of images that are transmitted from the brain as it rests in the body’s slumber. Nothing more. But he didn’t mind. He didn’t care one bit. He was with her again. And, until he awoke and realized that it was nothing but a lie, he would be happy. Truly happy. With nothing in existence to take it from him. But, unnoticed by Jason who was lost in mind, a pair of eyes studied his hotel room. Staring at the empty walls. Looking down at his hands. Staring out the window. He was admiring the world, and gazing into the eyes of a reality he had seldom seen with his own vision. Only through the window of his prison could he see this life. Now he could smell it. Taste it. Hear it. Feel it in his hands. Jay looked around, the Jason’s soul momentarily through into the abyss of his brain. Jason was truly sleeping, and had no idea of what Jay had done. Jay had sat in the corner of his cell, waiting for Jason’s eyes to shut and his guard to fall asleep at the switch. When he did, he ‘took the key’ so to speak, unlocked his bars, and stepped out of the walls. He was free, if only for the moment. Jay didn’t want to get caught just yet. He needed Jason for a little while longer. Jay walked around, a smile glowing on his face. The sensation of feeling the air against his skin. The warmth of the room brushing over him. The breath that went through his nose and out his mouth. It’s more then I disserve, he thought to himself. He looked over the room, and caught a glimpse of golden reflection on the armchair in the corner of the room. The smile on his face suddenly grew, as he realized just what it was. It shined into his face and embraced him, like the passionate kiss of a lover you never met before in your life. He walked towards it, drawn to its beauty. He looked down at it. He picked it up. He stared. For what seemed to be hours, all Jay did was stare into the polished face of the Continental. And then, just as the sun began to wink over the skyline, his smile turned into an angered frown. Jay grabbed hold of the strap, and then smashed into through the glass of the porch. His breath hurriedly caught up with his anger, as he stared down at the piece of gold that he had just nearly through down to the streets below. Jay walked over to the bed, and threw himself down. With his breath still in a rage, he closed his eyes. And Jason suddenly woke up, and looked around. The sweat covered his forehead, and he realized that she wasn’t lying in his arms. His face showed sadness and frustration, and he could only wish that he was still lost in his dream. Then, to make himself forget of the dream, he felt a chill run down his spine, as a breeze blew through the room. He turned around, and saw shattered glass blanketed across the porch outside his room. There, shining in the sunlight, with one side hanging off the edge, was his title belt. Jason got to his feet, walked through the glass with ease, and picked up the belt. Jason looked around, and wondered just what the hell happened. Little did he know that the answer was lying inside of him, looking down at the floor, wondering a million thoughts all at once. Now I exist. And you resist.
