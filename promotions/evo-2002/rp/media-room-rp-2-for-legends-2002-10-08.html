Media Room (Rp#2 for Legends)

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/08/02 The scene opens in a small room where we see 4 rows of seat's with reporters holding up recorders and notepads. The door swings open and the prez of nEw wrestling enters the the nEw media room. The reporters stop talking amongst themself's and begin to pay close attention to Daniel Somers. He walks up to the podium and begins to address the reporters and fans in the Background Somers: 'Good Morning.' Everyone replies to Somers with a simple good morning. Somers speaks again Somers: 'Today we are here to interview on of the *Rising Superstar's in eVo Wrestling, and he goes by the name of Jay Stylez Junior!' The fans in the background begin to cheer and chant Jay Stylez Juniors name. Somers begins to speak for 1 last time. Somers: 'So just stay seated and Jay Stylez Junior will be here in a minute.' Somers walks to a desk with his name plate and sit's down. The door swings open for the 2nd time and Jay Stylez Junior comes into the room, Fans cheer in the background and begin to chant for him. Jay Stylez Junior walks up to the podium and then all the questions begin to fly at him Reporter1: 'Jay Stylez Junior, How do you feel about being in eVo, and debuting this Wensday?' Jay Stylez Junior: '1st off I would like To start off by saying good morning to everyone. I am very happy and honored to be apart of eVo Wrestling. And glad that I will be debuting this Wensday. Yah just better watch this legends cause I guarntee it will be one hell of a match.' Jay Stylez Junior smiles and begins to answer another question. Jay Stylez Junior: 'I am very happy that I will be kicking off my carrer agains, “Pompous Perfection” Andrew Martin, Strictly Prohibited, Sgt. Jack O’Hara.' Reporter2: 'What do you think about your other 3 wrestlers that you will be fighting?' Jay Stylez Junior: 'Well I really dont now anything about them I just now this will be a great match-up'. Reporter3: 'Jay Stylez Junior, you seen the card can I get your thoughts on It?' Jay Stylez Junior: 'Legends XI will be great,.' Reporter4: 'We are going to play a little gam Jay Stylez Junior, When I say something you tell me what you think about it.What comes to mind when I mention GAUNTLET?' Jay Stylez Junior: 'When I think about Gauntlet I think about sweat, Blood, tears and hard work!' Reporter5: 'I supose you seen your match?' Jay Stylez Junior: 'Of course I seen my match.' Reporter5: 'A 4-way math, that I will be coming out victouious Fans begin to cheer for Jay Stylez Junior The reporters keep threwing questions at him, while the fans are cheering in the background. Somers gets up and walk to the podium and stands next to Jay Stylez Junior and begins to speak Somers: 'Well It is getting a little late, Jay Stylez Junior has to go to a autograph signing so we will be leaving!' Jay Stylez Junior and Somers walk off the podium and out the door, while fans are cheering and the reporters are talking amongst themselfs again. The camera fades to black and goes to a eVo commercial of Legends XI
