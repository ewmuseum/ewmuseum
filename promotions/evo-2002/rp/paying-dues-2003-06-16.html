Paying Dues...

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Damon rolled the towel tight, biting down on it to brace for the near-overwhelming pain that was coming. A cold sweat started to break, beads of perspiration trickling down his forehead and neck. He gripped the edge of the sink, knuckles whitening and veins running like blue cables up his forearm. This wasn’t going to be pleasant, but it had to be done. All this just to take a piss. As the stream of urine splashed into the toilet, O'Halloran exhaled hard through his nose. He wouldn’t cry out, refused to. Still, some things were out of his control. Thankfully, the towel muffled his yell. He held firm, washed with relief when it was over. O'Halloran spit the towel out. He bent forward to flush and paused momentarily. Blood in the bowl. Not a good sign. Damon splashed cold water on his face and looked at himself in the mirror. He was flushed, bloated from the mass quantities of drugs in his system. He was pushing three hundred pounds now, and it was starting to take its toll, yet he never questioned his drug use, it wasnt enhancing his performance, he saw it as a way of surviving in the hrash world that he lived in. His joints ached every morning, not used to the stress placed on them in such a short time. But all in all, he didnt need drugs to enhance his performance. When O'Halloran hit the gym, people stood in awe at the monstrous quantity of weight he’d lift. Sure, he’d get the occasional nosebleed from the strain, but it was minor compared to the gains. Damon O'Halloran had become part of the world's elite in the Cell Division. Wasn’t that what it was all about? Being recognized as being the Best? Damon was now pushing himself to the breaking point. He had to. Not only did he have the Legends match to win, but he had to deal with 'The Black Label' X. Maybe things had gotten a little out of control with X. Maybe Damon had gone too far with what happened to Ali Khadafi. But Damon wasn’t concerned with what the boys in the back thought of him. He wasn’t a locker room politician. He didn’t play games. O'Halloran was an angry man, and had a lot of frustration to work out. Sometimes people got hurt in their dealings with Damon. If 'The Black Label' wanted to push the issue, Damon was more than happy to push back. There was a thin line between fantasy and reality. In fantasy, 'The Black Label' X was the World’s Greatest Athlete who’d be facing Damon O'Halloran on Wednesday. In reality, 'The Black Label' X had to get in the ring with a completely different man. In fantasies, the guy with the white hat always got the girl and saved the day. They were heroes, saving innocent people from the horrible villains. In reality, good people got hurt by big bad men. Rarely were there heroes around to save them. More often than not, any heroes that were around just got hurt as well. Reality was often painful, physically as well as emotionally. Damon patted his face with the towel. If this were simply fantasy, the evil 'Black Label' X would smite the valiant Damon O'Halloran at Legends. The fans would cheer him as he raised his arms over his head. But 'The Black Label'? A snowball in Hell had more of a chance than he did. X was days away from a near three hundred-pound reality check, O'Halloran thought to himself as he walked out of the bathroom. Painful didn’t begin to describe what was coming.
