War Status

19 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-19 Battles come and go, they’re frequent, sure they’re furious, but they’re normal. Wars are few and far between. And they’re fucking intense. Makes them more fun though. What Asis was in, was the middle of a war. His entire life had been a series of battles, one continuous war, but in the world of wrestling, he’d never really had that much to compete against. He’d had a couple of furious battles with people, the likes of Jason Jameson and Troy Pierce, but never had he been involved in a wrestling war. The situation with Kimson and Xander had been escalated to that status last week when Tyler Davidson made what he might have thought to be one of his best decisions since being owner of eVolution. To have the three biggest “stars” of eVolution in one match with hardcore stipulations for the Universal Title, that was guaranteed ratings right there. It was being billed as one of the biggest matches in eVolution history. But there was one thing that Davidson hadn’t taken into account. What would happen when two of those “stars” were out of action for the next few months? Asis already had personal issues with Xander, that was in no way a secret. In fact, Asis had chosen to make those issues a very public thing. He could’ve dealt with it behind closed doors, a scream in the shadows and it would all be over. No one would have to know. But that wasn’t what Asis wanted. The revenge on Xander had to be out in the open, everybody had to see the kind of pain Xander was going to be put through, they had to see just what Asis’ wrath was. They have to know it. They have to see it. They have to feel it. It wasn’t planned as such. Asis knew somewhere deep down when the retaliation would happen. Somehow he knew when he would finally cost Xander all that he held dear. It wasn’t a conscious thought, it was one of those in the part of Asis’ brain that thought rationally, the part that Asis didn’t really know he had, but the part that would take over from time to time. The side to him that should be really feared. X-Rated was something that Asis looked forward to. He’d seen Kimson’s interview; he’d heard what he had to say. He’d listened to Kimson get it wrong as he said that Asis wouldn’t have gone through with the match against him on Legends if he hadn’t wanted the title. There were two reasons for that: revenge on Xander and the chance to take out one of the self proclaimed “greats” of the ring. The Universal Title made no odds to Asis in this pay per view match. All he wanted was to get his hands on two men that he hated in the kind of match he was born for. He could almost taste the blood already. It was the taste of vengeance.
