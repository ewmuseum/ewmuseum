Evil defined.

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.09.02 ~Thrown into a battle that he has fought before now rises a champion that will prevail as the best ever. A man that is not only a fighting champion but a damn good one at that. He took the man that the fans love, the mouthpiece for everything good in eVolution and he destroyed him with 4 chair shots to the head. Left him lying in a pool of his own blood.you may ask what's next for this younger superstar and soon to be legend?! The only thing that Shocka is focused on this week again is the destruction of Scott Frost but this time he has a worthy team mate in Sovereign. It's a dream team of sorts.Shocka the best Universal champion teaming up with Sovereign the first.~ ~We find Shocka amidst of a bunch of scatter papers looking over each one very carefully trying to find the one he is looking for. Not the celebration of a successful title defense that we expected. He looks over them and begins speaking to himself.~ 'Transcripts of each of my matches.move for move in eVolution thus far.' He speaks with a sinister tone. 'But the one against Frost was definitely the most memorable.my first title defense and it was a huge success.I mean if Frost wants any more of me one on one than he will damn sure regret getting into the same ring with me. He now knows what I am capable of and that I will stop at no lengths to keep this title.even without Omega he was going down for the count but this way it left more of an impression on him. 4 bone jarring chair shots, leaving him bloodied lost in his own world. I saw the look in your eyes Frost.you were at your weakest point at that moment.and if you think that is the end of it you are dead wrong.' He again speaks evilly. ~Shocka darts his head up at the camera and begins smiling at it with a sick look in his eyes.~ 'The #1 Stunna is going to rise to all new heights this coming week.he gets to take out two men that the fans look up to that they absolutely adore.and myself and my teammate two men that they simply cannot stand are on a wrecking train the next stop is Jameson and Frost. Frost you will suffer the same fate of L2J.that worthless little scumbag is finally out of my hair.and now Scotty boy you are the next to suffer 3 losses to me in under a month! If you would like to keep fighting me I will keep destroying you! Time after time, week after week.hell we can go at it at house shows if you would really like to get involved.you do not realize the chapter that you have opened up within me. I am out to make your life a living hell, and so far I have done a pretty damn good job.you will suffer even greater if you do anything.just the smallest little comment to drive me even further in my rage.and Jameson? I've had a problem with you since my first day of eVolution.' Shocka speaks shaking his head a little. 'I dunno what it is about you but every time I see you, I simply grow angry. And now with the human monster having my back in this match you will not even get a blow in. You see Jameson you have enraged the beast and that simply cannot hold well for you, and if you are too dumb to understand what you have done you will find out at Legends 21 when me and Sovereign destroy you and your former fellow teammate. There is nothing you two can do to prepare.Frost has stitches in his head and you soon will have matching ones.' Shocka says looking back down at the papers. ~He grabs one and lifts it up looking at it, he turns it around and he shows it to the camera the top reads. 'Nightmare Before Christmas' under that his match and under that it reads 'The aftermath' in italics. He smiles as he begins to read it.~ 'Shocka gets back to his feet and stands upright on the top rope as he pulls Thug roC up with him and quickly sets him up for the #1 Stunna again. Shocka lifts Thug roC into the air and leaps off the top rope as he brings Thug roC down and on top of L2J driving both men right through the table at ringside with all his weight and power behind it. Jack Tyler: HELP! Someone help them..well just Thug roC, the hell with L2J. Let him suffer. Justin Fritz: I say let roC suffer and help L2J. Jack Tyler: Screw that.' Shocka finishes reading with a smile. 'Now when will you people realize.that this was just the beginning of a reign of dominance and destruction.the sooner all of you do the less punishment I give you!' he says with a scream as the scene slowly fades.
