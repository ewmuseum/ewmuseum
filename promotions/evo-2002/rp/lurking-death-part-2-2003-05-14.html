Lurking Death Part 2

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-14 Off in the sunny state of California, Tyler Davidson knew not a thing about what the future would hold for him. The fact that his whole entire life was about to come crumbling down around him was not even the slightest thought in his mind. He had just finished his first ever appearance on the Tonight Show with Jay Leno and now he was doing nothing more than preparing himself for his chance at glory. The $oldout Wrestling Global Title was going to be on the line as he would battle Mr. Showtime on Thursday night. This would be his first real shot at the Global Title since the inception of $oldout Wrestling but things never seem to fall as planned for most people, Tyler least of all. Elsewhere, Erin Christoph sat back and he watched and waited for the moment of his destiny. The former Russian gangster had turned his life profession into the wrestling business but still now his old life had often come back calling for him. This job he now found himself in was much easier than anything else he could ever have imagined. It started with nothing more than a simple stakeout of a Manhattan townhouse occupied usually by Daniel Somers and his wife Jessica. Neither of those two were his targets though. He was sent by one Malcolm King to keep a close eye on the woman closest to Tyler Davidson, his fiancée Heather McDonald. King was apparently concerned that Somers would somehow get a message out to his friend who would in turn go to the woman. Christoph’s objective was to make sure that didn’t happen by any means necessary. Christoph got up from his chair in his own temporary apartment in Manhattan and with a cold glass of beer in his hand he walked to his window. A telescope sat just inside the window and Christoph put an eye up to it and looked across the street at another tall building. Standing out on a balcony standing together in the cold wind blowing through the city was Heather McDonald, and alongside her the wife of Daniel Somers, Jess. The two of them stood there talking and looking down on the streets far below them not knowing that they were being watched and the danger which lurked ever so near.
