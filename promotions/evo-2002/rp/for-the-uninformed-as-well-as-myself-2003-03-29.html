For the uninformed as well as myself

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/29/03 'There have been questions about my viability as a contender much less a champion. I know there are whispers about my match, my status not recieving the proper build up. The majority of which reside in my head. But really Seara....you must have known that it was a matter of time before we renew the special relationship that we once had.' Citizen is revealed deep within the bowels of the arena; apparently in the boiler room. 'The voices in my head say there is no way I can win the title at the PPV...there is no way, I can beat Seara for the Evolved Championship. There are even those voices that suggest that it should be Luther Wisdom in the match and not myself. A shame that I don't even listen to the voices that reside deep in my head, so I could give a damn about anyone elses voice. Wait a minute....there is one other voice I want to hear...that of Seara screaming in pain, wishing it was extasy...as she taps out from my wanton molestation of her character...of her career.' Citizen is seen bandaging on arm with tape....... 'The thing that has killed you Seara is complacency....you are comfortable being a champion, you are comfortable being booked in fueds which exploit your lack of potential; and quite simply you are comfortable being Scott frost's bitch. Mind you not a bad thing, as there are many a night I too wish I could be his bitch....BUT THEN i WAKE UP AND REALIZE HE IS NO LONGER UNIVERSAL CHAMPION, so what will be in it for me outside of a jar of vasoline.....and regret. But seara you know this is about more than Scott Frost, it's about what you owe me..' He looks up into the sky with a tear in his eye..... 'Many months ago I said you were not worthy of the spot you have in Evolution, I told you that I was taking your spot....which promptly got my ass handed to me by Asis, and The Unlimited. A situation that had me side tracked for the better part of my time here. With me out of the picture you were able to acheive a certain amount of success that you could only dream of....but did you at one time, at one point, sit back, think, and decided that may be......just maybe.....you should have thanked me?' Xavier turns to he camera the tears on his face now dried up...... 'You should have thanked me for not opting to end your career until this very moment and time, you should have thanked me for allowing you to fly under the radar long enough to make a career for yourself....YOU SHOULD HAVE THANKED ME DAMMIT....for not going ahead with my boob job, giving you yet another diva to contend with. But most of all you should have thanked me for relieving you of the burden of having to defend that title in the relatively obscure way in which you have. Not only will I be looking for you Sunday Night to tap out.....I will be looking for you to say THANK YOU Xavier, and for that all I have to say is......' blows her a kiss.... 'You're welcome.'
