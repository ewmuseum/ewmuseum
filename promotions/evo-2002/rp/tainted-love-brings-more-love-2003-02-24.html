Tainted Love brings MORE love

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-24-03 'Are you okay? Do you need anything?' The voice of Seara is heard. The scene fades in and Frost is laying in bed, and he has a smile on his face. Seara stands next to him, and you can see on the ground is the eVolved and Universal championship laying side by side on the floor. Frost looks down at the titles and then smiles, and looks up at his girlfriend. 'I'm fine. Just fine. How are you feeling?' Frost asks. Frost turns over and puts pressure on his burn from the C4 explosion and he grunts in pain, and tries to look up at his girlfriend but can't even open his eyes due to the pain. 'I'm good, but you need to rest. Put some more of that medicine on your burn, and try and stay off of it. Get some rest.' Seara tells Frost. Frost smiles. 'Only if you come down and celebrater, erm, lay with me.' Frost slyly says. Seara looks at him and slightly grins. 'Maybe later. Right now, you need to rest.' Seara demands. 'No wait, wait.' Frost says as Seara walks with her back turned and almost through the doorway of the bedroom. Seara turns around after hearing some moving around on the bed and Frost is on the floor with a little black box in his hand. Seara looks shocked, and Frost looks uncomfortable and his free hand is putting pressure on his burn. 'I know it's a shock, and given the PPV name Tainted Love. My love for you is beyond tainted, and I know that this sudden. But we have been through A LOT together, and I want to continue that. What do you say?' Frost asks. Seara looks on with shock, and Frost continues to wait for an answer but Seara looks speechless. (To be continued....)
