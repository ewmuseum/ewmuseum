The Quest

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept 22 Toronto, Canada BDD’s Estate Early Evening It has been nearly a month since that fateful day, the phone call that he will never forget. The day, Katarina has moved on into the netherworld. And since that time, nothing really has been the same. BDD distanced himself from his responsibilities at Syndicate International, he distanced himself from his tag partner of six years, The HeartBreak Chick, he distanced himself from pretty much everything that matters. Except his gym workouts. Their he find solace, pushing himself to the point of exhaustion and continuing beyond it. Perhaps in an effort to injure himself, perhaps just to elevate himself into a new consciousness, perhaps their really is no reason….except guilt. A guilty mind, can trap your soul into unspeakable hell with no way out, just torment, terror, and sadness. Though BDD has continued to perform at a high level. Nothing really has been clean with him. His match with Troy Pierce, outside interference. Though perhaps it was a good thing because his mind wasn’t on the match, even though it should have been. Except for one fleeting moment, the sucker shots to Tommy Michaels. For some unknown reason, they felt good. A moment of clarity, a moment of, of what used to be. Big Daddy Dreamer, wrestling veteran, legendary superstar, one of the top guys in Evolution, hall of famer in three federations, suddenly finds himself, why am I even doing this? I’m a multi-billionaire dozens of times over. I’m one of the richest and most powerful people in all the world, yet none of that seems to matter anymore. Now I know what my good friend Steve Austin is currently going through. Not painkiller addiction, something more deep down and disturbing. A purpose. What is my purpose now? What am I suppose to do now? HBC seems to have dealt with it swimmingly. Perhaps it is because she was distracted from it all. Too busy concentrating on the matters at hand. Quasar, Mason, Seara, the High-Impact Championship. All of those are nice distractions. For once, I am envious of her. Though she has been in this position before, ages ago, before she was a superstar. Hell even before she became a wrestler. But dam, how well she is doing. She blew the roof off the place with her ladder match at Digital Destruction. Definitely a match of the year consideration. I should call her, I need to get over this funk, and I think, finally, its time to put things in the past, and move on……. BDD reaches down and speed dials HBC on her private direct line. After a few ring, her sweet melodic voice answers…. ‘Hello’ ‘Hello C, its me.’ ‘Well long time to hear D, how have you been?’ ‘I’ve been better.’ ‘I see, hey you know we have a match this week against The Unlimited. Tag team action.’ ‘Yeah, I saw that. Xander and Jinx I believe. Not the orginal Unlimited Team that barely took Tag Team of the Year honors over us, but formidable none the less.’ ‘Indeed. So you going to show or what. I only ask that because you’ve been a hard man to find lately. I need to know if your going to be their, as my partner like old times, or if I need to find a last minute replacement.’ ‘I’ll be their C, you know, no one does tag team action like we do.’ ‘That is true D, D……can I be honest with you?’ ‘Of course you can…….’ ‘Are we done? I mean, is The Syndicate over? Is BDD and HBC over? Personally and professionally? Because I’d really hate for that to happen. You’re my closest friend D, hell, you taught me dam near everything I know about this business, and even more about life, business, and how to be a global empire. No one knows this, but when I won the High-Impact Championship, I dedicated it to you. I’ve never said that before because I was hoping to tell you face to face, but you disappeared at Destruction and I didn’t get a chance too. I know you’ve been taking it rough as of late, having doubts, second thoughts, and I know you miss Katarina, even though she plotted against us. Hell D, she tried to have you killed if you remember. But, losing a loved one is always difficult and people react differently to it. I immersed myself in my work, I got over it, I dealt with it…..you need to as well D. Because if you don’t, this will transcend what you do in the ring, but affect your everyday existence. You’ve already shown the classic symptoms. Withdrawn, isolated, not taking pleasure in the things you used too, depression. You blew off Nichole’s advances. You still haven’t screwed your playmate wrestling partner. Why do you think that dildo got out? Its all I can do in the meantime, so I don’t know whatever it is you need to do to get over it and move on, but please do, please D, please do it for all of us, for yourself, for me, even for Katarina, ok. Give her, her one last request. You know what it is, you have the power to make it happen, to do it, do it. Do it to save yourself and all we have worked tirelessly for. The Syndicate is not going to go away, we’ve been doing things ok around here, but please D, do what it is to save yourself and most of all comeback to us. We’ll be waiting for you with open arms. All of us, all the staffers, Nichole, Alexis, Lexi and most of all myself. Please, we need you.’ ‘Your right C, your right. I’ll be at the arena Wednesday night but I’ll be arriving that day, probably a few hours before hand, I’ll see you their. I have alittle business to attend too.’ ‘Ok, I’ll be waiting for you…’ ‘And C, congratulations on all you have accomplished here. Their will never be a finer High-Impact Champion than yourself.’ ‘Aint that the truth, and thank you. Thank you for the flowers too, my favorite kind no less. I knew you cared. Go D, go what you need to do. Everything will be taken care of when you arrive. *Click*’ BDD sighs heavily before pushing another set of buttons. ‘Airport, this is BDD, fuel up my bird, were going to Sarajevo.’
