Return of the Golden Boy: Part I

17 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-17 'Name?' the security guard said as he leant down looking into the front of the Audi TT sitting stationary before the barrier. 'Ethan M Coles' replied the man sitting behind the wheel of the flash sportscar. Having been waiting for a number of seconds, it was clear the mans impatience was growing as he sat tapping the steering wheel waiting to be allowed entry. Dressed in a black shirt and silver tie he was dressed to impress and despite hiding underneath a pair of oakleys you could tell he was not a man who believed in waiting. The security guard took a second scanning up and down his list of names. 'I'm sorry could you give me that name again' the security guard said looking up once more having not had any success. 'Geez' Ethan sighed pressing his hands against the steering wheel in frustration, 'I didn't know that breeze I felt was coming from the gap within your skull' he muttered. 'What?' the security guard answered abruptly, having only caught the word skull he was unsure what had been said but he got the impression it had been anything but complimentary. 'I said nothing' Ethan said breaking eye contact. 'Name is Ethan. M. Coles.' 'And your sure your meant to be here?' the guard replied as he glanced down at his sheet, this time he kept his ears wide open. 'Are you trying to say I am going crazy?' Ethan shouted as the frustration grew. 'No I am trying to say your names not on the list' the security guard said puffing out his chest as he sensed a confrontation. 'So your telling me I can't park my car in the car park?' Ethan said shaking his head in disgust. 'That is the case' the guard replied pulling his clipboard to his chest. 'Well in that case' Ethan said pausing as he opened the car door, 'be a good boy and play valet while I am gone. Infact scrap that, I'll keep the keys; can't be too careful these days' said correcting himself. Once to his feet Ethan took his briefcase and slammed the door shut before applying the car alarm. 'What the hell do you think your doing?' the guard said stepping in Ethans path. 'Going to see Leya' Ethan replied sidestepping the guards advance. 'Wheres your pass?' the guard said glancing down at his clipboard once more. 'Just..... There' Ethan said as he suddenly out of no-where pulled a pair of handcuff suprinsing the guard as he chained him to the metal bollard a foot away from where the pair stood. 'Damn looks like your too tied up to check' Ethan chuckled to himself as he stepped backward and admired his handy work. 'SECURITY' the guard screamed at the top of his voice. Ethan quickly checked over his shoulder, 'you don't mind if I let myself in since your busy do you?' At that point Ethan quickly darted into the checkpoint and raised the barrier, before raising back past the guard avoiding his feeble attempt at a one handed restraint and back into his car. Engine on, first gear. 'Leya. Hold on honey here I come' Ethan said as his grin grew further. Adjusting his oakleys he reached down and pressed play on the CD player. Within seconds 'without me' began to play from the speakers to which Ethan's response was to sing along. Heaven help us.
