Time...

16 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-16 Tick. Tick. Time… Tick. Tick. Time never sleeps. Time never rests. Time continues on its malignant mark in pursuit of our lives. Wearing away - unseen by our ignorance, unheeded by humanity's refusal to accept the inevitable. Always believing that we can change the unchangeable. Always so sure of immortality, immune to fate - always so wrong. Tick. Tick. Time… Tick. Tick. Time shortens our existence. An impetuous, insignificant, MEANINGLESS existence. Like a cockroach, compared to the lowest form of life, like the man they call Sterling. Tick. Tick. Time is running out for him, for you, for us. His time will be cut short. His tyrannical attitude, his overbearing ego - only condemns the minimal pardons he has to spare. He can't escape from his time, from his damnation. I have tried. I have taken it upon myself to show him the error of his ways. I have taken it upon myself to lead him to the Elysian fields of hope and prosperity. But yet he refuses to follow at his own will! Yet he remains in this decayed estate of pain - a pain that I shall make him grow close to. He refuses to follow me, and like the cockroach he is; he scatters only where the light shines; to his safe haven of comfort. Tick. Tick. Time…. Tick. Tick. Time is running out. There is no longer a sanctuary, there is no longer a safe haven. There is no place to hide - he cannot escape time. He cannot alter his ill-fated destiny. He cannot change then unchangeable. Tick. Tick. Time… Tick. Tick. A time forgotten amidst the false senses of security. A time forgotten, a time that underlies this feeling of immortality. A time misplaced within his ego-precious mind. A time only too remembered within my foreclosure of torment. A time of pain, a time of sorrow, a time of hurt, a time of suffering. A time that I shall bring. A time of darkness, a time of woe. A time where the forsaken shall rise and revolt. A time forgotten by Sterling, a time unknown, a time signifying change - a time that runs out. Tick. Tick. Tock.
