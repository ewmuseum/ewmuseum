Date: 2003-06-21 'Trevor Norman you have been Found Guilty, by the Offical court of Ontairo.' It were as if Trevor had just lost his life, as he sunk down into his chair. Trevor kept his eyes focused on the ground, as he could here all those cheering for him being found Guilty. This can not be...... I am celebrity now days..... not some punk teen...... 'Trevor Norman please rise.' Trevor didn't move a inch, as for some reason a smile appeared on his face. 'Trevor Norman Please rise, now' 'I'm good sitting actually' Trevor replied as he lift his head up and looked right into the eyes of the judge. 'Do you think, this is a laughing matter Mr Norman.' 'Nah' Trevor said as he finally rose up on his feet, and shot a cold glance towards the Judge. 'Due to mainy views, the jury did find you guilty, but the also believed you to my a weapon to the public. You will be on probation for a 2 Month period, and if you use your fist in a public area outside of eVo you will spend acouple years in prison.' The Judge explain to Trevor Trevor turned around and walked towards Donaven, as one of the jury members made a remark towards Trevor. 'You should be locked away you nut.' Trevor stopped right in his tracks as he looked at the jury member, and just shock his head as he exit the courtroom with donaven.
