One month ago......part 1

6 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-01-06 MALCOLM KING: A hospital at an undisclosed location. The hallway at this particular facility is barren except for a few nurses aides and security. Sitting hidden in the shadows is a man who has familiar features but is still obscured via the shadows. A doctor walks out from one of the far rooms and approches the man and stops just enough to hide the shadowed figure even more. Dr:'He was in a pretty bad accident....you know.....' man:'I know.....I arranged it.' Dr.'....and his family?' man:'Wife and daughter are taken care of, as far as they know he's doing 'business'; the wife understands she remembers being kayfabed herself.' Dr.'I'm uncomfortable with this......he has a significant portion of his memory loss. Short term...long term; I can't say for sure.' man:'He'll have some memories...especially the ones I feed him. I don't have time for you to run any more tests or aide in his recovery. For years he used people as pawns; now it's karmatic that he now becomes one.' Dr.:'I...can't allow it in good faith......' man:'I don't pay you to have faith...can I see him?' The doctor reluctantly concedes and says yes. The man gets up and makes the long walk down the hall way.He has a duffel bag thrown over one shoulder as he opens the door to reveal the patient. It's none other than MALCOLM KING. The former Evolution superstar is sedated and bandaged. He is sleeping peacefully unaware of his surroundings. man:'So here lies the great Malcolm King. The master manipulator and puppet master of the wrestling business. For years you have been equal parts friend as well as nemesis. How does it feel? How does it feel .......to be on the recieving end of someone elses agenda? To not be in control of your own destiny? To know that no matter what you do....someone else will benefit? Malcolm my most hated friend....I have need of the mayhem that only you can bring. But right now you are only a blank canvas, needing the right artist to paint the masterpiece which will serve as the resurrection of your wrestling career. To tell the truth; I am quite happy that it was me that took away from you the one time of happiness you found in this business, that being your retirement. Wake up King......your reeducation is about to begin and end with this........' He reaches into the duffel bag. On his shirt you see cuff links with the letters DS. Once in the bag he pulls out what appears to be a championship wrestling belt; and throws it on the prone body of Malcolm King. It is the old EDW Universal Title as the scene fades to black....... DOLO BENTSWINGER 'Years of steriod abuse has finally caught up to me. For every injection I knew the risk.....but what the hell it's my body. This was the only way I could make money....this business was all I knew it was something I was good at. I didn't have time to learn the technical aspects of this business because a brother needed to get paid now. I knew the risks and I continues to do it. I needed the edge and was intoxicated with the money, the fame, the business. But this wasn't supposed to happen. I stopped dammit!!!! Malcolm told me I was slowly killin myself by doin that shit; so I stopped. I wasn't supposed to happen to me. The needles were supposed to be clean....I can't have HIV. The doctor made a mistake.....I'm a professional wrestler dammit; that HIV thing is not supposed to happen to me. The doctor is wrong...and I need money. The man called me and offered me a return to Evolution Wrestling. The doctor wants to run more tests to make sure I'm not a potential risk.....a carrier as he puts it. I need money so I don't have time for more tests. When I was on the juice........I never had HIV. So now that I'm off I'm supposed to? fuck that......the doctor is wrong, and I need money....' Dolo Bentswinger is seen in a bathroom talking to his reflection in a mirror. From behind him a woman's hands start to caress his chest as her head pops from behind his shoulders....... woman:'Was wondering when you are coming back to bed?' the very naked woman starts to ask him. Dolo opens the medicine chest and behind a vial of anabolic steroids; pulls out a wad of cash. 'Here...' he states to the woman. She looks a little shocked as she states....'I'm not here for your money.' He looks at her and says matter of factly......'Funny....that's the only reason I brought you here; you can leave now.' As he puts the wad back and hands her a $20 for cab fare. She looks at him a little speechless before walking off in disgust. He turns back to the medicine cabinent and looks at the vial..... 'I need the money.......I need an edge.' 1001 HOLDS TOKYO, JAPAN 1001 Holds sits alone in a jail cell. After the retirement of King and the disbanning of The BodySnatchers he took a few dates to work for EJapan wrestling. However upon his arrival he was arrested and thrown into jail, crimes still unknown...but made to sit nonetheless. A guard comes and announces he has a visitor. Shakled and chained 1001 Holds unshaven and all is lead to a small room; where a very attractive woman wearing a business suit with a breif case greets him. 1001:'Who are you?' woman:'I am Rio Madison.....your attorney.' 1001:'Funny I don't remember hiring a lawyer......I'm innocent by the way.' R.M:'You didn't hire me....and that's what they all say; however the Japanese government begs to differ on your declaration of innocence.' 1001:'So why are we talking?' R.M.:'The man who hired me has use of your services. Seems as a professional wrestler on your level is rare indeed. You've been granted a limited work release in his custody and the ability to travel to the United States.' 1001 Holds is skeptical but he's been in this prison for awhile now so any chance at freedom sounds good to him. 1001:'And how do I have to repay your employer?......mind you I haven't been in jail THAT long.' R.M.:'Please mister....this is strictly business; all you have to do is babysit someone. I'll give you more details on the plane ride home. Now get your things together as I finalize the paperwork.' 1001 Holds just sits there a little confused as he is happy to be out; but apprehensive of this babysitting assignment. to be continued..................
