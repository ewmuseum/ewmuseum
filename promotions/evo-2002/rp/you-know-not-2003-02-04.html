You Know Not

4 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th Febuary 2003 After the last promo cut by female wrestler Seara, she is found alone. The location is dark and it is hard to identify exactly where in the room the female is. Once again Xander, you will get to meet me in the ring, and I’m sure you are excited as hell at the prospect, much like I am now. I mean I can’t quite hold in the excitement. I may just have to resort to being happy about-facing you. The sarcasm in Seara’s voice fills the room and lighting a candle sitting just in front of her, the female illuminates her face with a low dark shadow falling over her eyes. It is a disturbing sight to see, but the cameraman is well away from the female, and she seems rather docile and calm at this moment in time. The female sits at a table, her elbows leaning on the pine surface and her head resting in the hands, her fingers tightly clasped together. Her title belt is nowhere to be seen, and the cameraman begins to assume that this promo will have very little to do with the aforementioned Xander. But this is Seara’s air time, and as from her previous promo which outlined the death of her stepbrother Vinnie, it is clear to see just how much can happen when Seara is involved and in control of her actions and thoughts. Wednesday night. Legends. The things that could happen, the stuff that will happen. Xander, you by now know a little about me in the ring, and I’m assuming that you will be ready for Wednesday night, after all, you are now missing Angel, who was a huge member of Unlimited, a man who I am sure could have done both me and Mason some serious damage given the chance. But no, Angel is no longer with eVo, after his untimely retirement, you are stuck pairing with his brother, Oz. I have never before now come across Oz, and I wonder what is so special about him that Angel trusted him to be able to replace him in the ring with someone with you capability. I mean after all Xander, you are no doubt a great and powerful laugh. Seara remains straight faced, but it is completely clear that she is not serious about her words of compliment towards Xander. Oz I know nothing about you, and you are the latest rookie for eVo, a rookie who will get to fight me in your first match within this federation. You should prepare for nothing, it is unpredictable what I will do, and I have no idea what Mason will be like, after all last time I faced him, I beat him for the evolved title. I am the first to carry it, and I will certainly not let it go without a fight, and you Oz will not waltz in here and try to replace any of the great superstars who have passed through this federation, you most definitely will not replace Angel by a long shot. Despite this information though, I will enjoy getting in the ring alongside Mason to face off with you and Xander. Xander, our feud dates way back to when I first started here in eVo, you automatically took a dislike to me, and I the same for you. It is not hidden knowledge, and I figure it is only worsened by the fact that I chose a man who betrayed you and your precious Unlimited, rather than taking an opportunity to get you behind my back as my friend. It must really suck to know that you don’t really spark that much interest in many people, fans or wrestlers. You are not all that you once were and although I’m sure this must be disappointing for you, you are certainly making your return to fighting well known, your record after all is something to be seen by all, an amazing winning streak there. Whatever has happened to Seara since she last cut a promo has inspired her to throw the insults to Xander more than ever, and she knows that she could be killed if Xander decided to give the orders, but Seara seems not to care about any of it, she knows only now that she must make herself more dominant and more powerful in eVo and she must prove to a man who is in lead of great power that she is not to be taken lightly. Seara is ready to face Xander, she expects surprises with the naming of Oz as Angels replacement for the fight, but nothing could surprise her more now than the sound of her cell phone ringing. What do you want? Called me to try and scare me before Wednesday night has you. Think again. Get lost and get a fucking life you… Taking the cell from her ear the female hangs up before she has even finished her own sentence. She slams her cell down onto the table and listens to the crack as the plastic casing snaps around the inner phone. Gritting her teeth and fists the female looks away from the camera and swings her arm across the table sending her cell and the candle flying over the room. The cell phone smashes and breaks in to two separate pieces as it impacts the wall of the room, and the candle merely hits the floor, snapping into three or four pieces, the flame gradually fades out as the little piece of wick burns away inside the small wax lump. Worthless junk, its all-worthless, why should I spend my time worrying who is calling me or what they want. The ring is what is in my mind now. The pain, the glory, the need to see and feel the blood of another spill on the matt or trickling down my fist. Some people think this makes me a psychopath, but you know not how I feel or why I feel, you know not what I think, maybe you should try to put yourself in my position just once before you try to accuse me of something. Maybe your awakening will seal my triumph here once and for all. But what am I expecting, you Xander will never awake to see my time or my triumph, you would rather lie dead than admit I have what it takes around here. It does seem that I have achieved what most thought I would fail at, and it seems I’m achieving much more these days. But still people like you Xander have closed your mind to me, decided I am not good enough to be here, but I have a few things to do around here, and a few people to deal with, and I am not going to back down anytime soon, if I can help it, and I certainly will, I will thrive, I will resist people like you, and eventually you will have to face the fact that I am worth it, I am good enough to be here, and I am a worthwhile contender. Xander, this is mainly between you and me, but do not think that will stop me from trying to destroy Oz if he gets in my way of you. Xander, you will be mine, I will have you for myself and one day, things will be very different around here, I cannot say exactly when they will change, but they will change for the better and you can bet I will be in the heart of the change. Taking to her feet the female smashes her way past chairs, knocking them to the floor, and then pulling the door open she slams it hard against the wall, making sure that the handle slams through the plasterboard wall. Her exit is loud and destructive and the female disappears quickly out the room, seemingly on some kind of mission.
