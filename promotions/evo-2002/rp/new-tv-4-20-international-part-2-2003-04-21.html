New-tv 4:20 International! Part 2

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4:20 We reenter the smoke filled room which is starting to clear up. We see Hedges open a small window to make the room more visible. Thug roC has turned on the radio and plays the local rap station. “Omega won’t like that.” Sky says while trying to finish the blount. She is interrupted by Thug roC who snatches it out her hand. “What are you doing? There are 3 other people in this room!” Thug roC says before taking a long puff. “Did I hear a cop siren?!” Sky says with a shocked look on her face. Thug roC turns his head towards the window and Sky snatches the blount back from Thug roC. “Sucker…” Sky Night says while walking away with the green. Thug roC stands there upset with himself. “Damn this song sucks.” Hedges says referring to the radio. “Wouldn’t you say so Morph?” Hedges gets no response. Morpheus Omega is still zoned out and has no idea what’s going on at the moment. “Stop dissin the music, the beat is nice.” Thug roC responds. “I don’t see you makin platinum hits. “Oh yea, and your flows are just great.” Hedges says sarcastically. “I don’t see you doin better.” “Watch and learn junior, better yet, listen and learn.” Thug roC says before sitting down at the table. Sky Night, with blount in hand sits to listen as well as Hedges. Omega is still out if it, not knowing what’s going on around him. Thug roC starts listening to the beat from the radio before starting. Ok, one time here we go Sittin here on that faithful day 4-2-0 2 double-0 3 we all know how to do it Thanks to the request, I’m gonna drop some new shit Puff, puff pass, we'd rather grass then glass No alcohol, that's a blast from the past If you ask for some drinks, the only answer you'll get is 'nope!' 'They also say, with booze you lose and with dope there's hope! Today we only deal with that greeny green Puffin 12 inchers like Thug roC's 'Mini Me' We don't deal with 4 inchers, they get no chance The only 4 incher here today is in Omega's pants Not like he can hear this, a few puffs and he's gone 4 Puffs that were shorter then leprechauns Sky is all serious, always ready to blast off She was also ready when she got Omega to 'blast off' Hedges feelin the lost to Shocka, something that I never did So puffin is the only way so he won't blow his lid That's ok Hedges, we won't blame the lost on you We can't do that on TV that's all New New-TV doin it old school, that's what we're good at We're not like Team eVo who just sits around to chat We celebrate holidays, gettin green like money I don't mean the holiday that has a big white bunny It's April 20th, the day the smokers come out Knockin at the door like 'Can Mary Jane come out?' But Mary always plays, she's always on the scene This has been a flow from New-TV and my good friend green. The scene fades once again with the room smoking up to a point where nothing is visible.
