NBC (Part 1)

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.14.02 ~One step closer…the standards have been raised and now its almost time…as the days grow closer one man becomes more and more ready. The PPV is now only 8 days away and Shocka holds two pins over L2J beating him both times with the #1 Stunna. And what can he do about this? What can L2J do to prove himself? Nothing all he can do is come to the realization that he is simply not good enough to beat Shocka. Shocka is a man that has risen to the top of eVolution holding wins over L2J, Christoph, Frost, and a variety of others. And now it’s his turn to get a shot at L2J one on one in the ring on the grandest stage of all eVolution. The Pay Per Views have progressively gotten better over the last few months and now this could prove to be one of the biggest. Not only does it have a great main event but also a variety of important supporting matches like Sovereign vs. Impact and the pinnacle of the stable wars. The omen brotherhood facing off against the Unlimited for the tag titles. ~ ‘Its finally going to be mine!’ You hear the familiar voice of Thug roC shout. ‘What’s gonna be yours?’ Shocka questions. ‘Only the most coveted title in eVolution the Universal title…’ roC says with excitement. ‘What?!’ Shocka looks at him with an angry look. ‘I think that that title is going to be mine…I mean you are the one that lost to Scott Frost and I am getting the shot not you!’ ‘Oh come on, you’ve got to credit me on some of your success.’ RoC says in a sort of pouting voice. ‘Yeah you did help me…realize I need to shave my goatee off…’ Shocka says laughing. ‘When are you going to start taking this match seriously?!’ roC screams with anger at Shocka. ‘Maybe when it’s a little closer roC…’ Shocka says. ‘Well there is no better time than the present to start preparing…’ thug roC shoots back. ‘Well ya know what? I already hold two pins over L2J…imagine what he is going through…he is doubting himself…he is wondering if he is up to par to be in the ring with me and the realization that he will come to is only going to bring him down that much more. He can’t hang with me in the ring and the sooner he realizes that the sooner I get my belt!’ Shocka screams ‘Finally some enthusiasm from you about this match…’ roC says with a sort of smart-ass tone. ‘How could you say that I have been waiting for this chance to be able to get into the Universal title since I first join eVolution…hell since I was in the EdW!’ Shocka yells at roC. ‘Well you should make it a little more obvious.’ RoC says. ‘Well ya know what? That would give him the advantage if he knew I was a little over excited about the match…I don’t want to give him the chance to pounce on anything…I will go into this match with no weaknesses…I don’t care if there is no stipulation either…’ ‘If there was a stipulation you would have the upper hand though.’ RoC says thinking to himself. ‘I already do! I have pinned him twice…what more of an advantage could I have?’ Shocka says. ‘Well ya know…you do seem to shine in hardcore matches and since he is a lot smaller than you, you could through him pretty far through some stuff to wear him down.’ ‘I don’t need that advantage I will work him over with my technical skills.’ Shocka says sort of offended. ‘Plus I could break him in half with my new signature move…the Shocka rocka. Yes a modified Boston crab…’ ‘What?!’ roC says. ‘You are supposed to consult with me before coming up with moves.’ ‘What is your problem…the Shocka rocka is a fine name if I do say so myself…and the move well you want me to show you?’ ‘Bah…I dare ya to try and get me in it.’ ~After about 5 minutes of fighting we picks up with Shocka holding thug roC in a headlock giving pulling out his hair..~ ‘They say I’m stiff in the ring now…wait until I get into the ring with Seara…’ Shocka laughs to himself. ‘That didn’t come out right…’ ‘Yeah it didn’t… and you are stiff in the ring even when we are training!’ roC says. ‘I’ll show you stiff!’ Shocka rolls his eyes and laughs at his own joke. ‘How about you show L2J stiff?’ roC asks. ‘How about I show HBC stiff huh huh…’ Shocka raises his eyebrows a little and laughs as the scene fades.
