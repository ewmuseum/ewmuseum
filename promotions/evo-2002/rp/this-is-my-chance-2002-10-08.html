This Is My Chance....

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/10/02 Fade into a small studio deep inside of the eVo HQ. Sitting opposite one another are the familiar faces of Sean Mason and Gabriel Gordon. Just as the faces are familiar, so is the set-up. The walls lay bare, but the room is packed to the brim with a variety of camera equipment as far as the eye can see. One thing very noticeable is though that is slightly un-familiar is the broad grin on Seans face. Gone is the slightly downbeat expression, instead it is replaced by a huge smile which he showcases for all to see. Despite his loss this past week on Legends, it seems Sean is not overly dissapoint, but then again who can blame him... He has just been granted a shot at the Rising Star title he never thought likely.... 'So Sean' Gabriel said pausing momentarily as he ushered the cameras slightly closer, 'when you ready we will begin if thats ok?' 'Ready as I'll ever be' Sean said in a bubbly manner. With that the cameras begin to roll. Gabriel places his notes to one side and prepares to get down to business. Turning to the camera he pauses momentarily and smiles in a welcoming manner. 'So Sean' Gabriel begins without any hesitation, 'tommorow night what are your thoughts!? I mean excited? Nervous? Its certainly a big moment in your eVo career thus far. Did you ever think that this would come about so soon? I mean with no dis-respect meant, your start has hardly been what you had hoped...' 'Then again, I hadn't really banked on being paired up with a spotlight hungry tyrant' Sean said brushing off Gabriels remark. 'Thug Roc?' Gabriel asked.. 'Now now Gabriel' Sean replied, 'how you guess?! I mean wheres the fun now that you guessed so soon?!' Gabriel responds with a bout of laughter, before attempting to regain his composure once more. 'Does it feel weird taking a step away from the Impact division? I mean should you not leave victorious this week, could it be anything more than an un-timely distration?! Does that worry you is what I am getting at....' 'Worry me?!' Sean said raising an eyebrow. 'Title shots don't worry me. In truth I'd take a title shot everyday if I could. Its title shots we aim to capture and make no mistake I am only to happy to accept this one. Theres not many who can boast a reign as Rising Star champion on their CV, I am not about to join the minority!! If anything its a welcome break' Sean paused. 'If variety is the spice of life, then this opportunity at something else should be good for me. It also gives me a chance to show that I can compete out of my element. Legends is an opportunity to see how far I have come in eVo and also an opportunity to erase the memory of the past week. In the tag match I didnt hold my own in a match which had me competing against non-cruiserweights. The elite wrestlers. Those wrestlers who earn un-conditional fame, are those who can go that little bit further. Im a cruiserweight by definition, however im a wrestler by trade. Going into Legends I need to remember that I can't expect to go to the top by playing safe with the lighter guys. Legends is a chance to stake my claim at the grander prizes and nudge those pen pushers with a timely reminder of what I am capable of. Matt Boone a month away was deemed fit to challenge for the top honours here, a victory over him would go a long way in re-building a reputation which has been hurt by a record in eVo that is not worth contemplating. In truth I have so much to gain going into this match. Should I win, there isn't much that I can't savour. I'll be a champion, I'll be a winner and most of all I'll be making a bold statement to everyone within eVo. A win over Boone would give anyone confidence and with Faith just around the corner, its not hard to understand that a victory at Legends would really give me some degree of rhythm'. 'And of Boone?' Gabriel asked. 'Are you worried of Cori's presense for one?' 'Worried no, weary however I am' Sean replied as the smile dissapeared to be replaced by a more serious expression and tone of voice. 'Gone is the boy who could do no wrong. Gone is the boy who was as green as grass. Matt Boone is a different Matt Boone to the one of a month ago. I mean the guys mean, lean and jilted by his failure to succeed at the highest level. Truth is, hes a perfection reflection of his mentor. In some ways I wonder if the title draped on his shoulder means anything at all? Is it merely an aimless trophy gathered on his travels or does it actually mean something to the cold and callous Boone?' 'How do you mean?' Gabriel asked leaning closer. 'From where I sit, Boones a man who wants to prove something to those watching. He wants to prove he can be everything Sovereign is. A monster. A man to be feared' Sean said leaning back in his chair attempting to relax. 'Only problem being, im not about to roll over and make this simply an exhibition. I have my own agenda going into Legends as I said before and if Matt thinks im about to see this as a damage limitation exercise he is so wrong. Faith is in the future, im dealing right now with the present time. I want to win things in eVo after all, what better time to start than by starting this forthcoming Legends in Tampa. Troy Pierce is no Sean Mason. Different person, different time and different motives all together mean no comparisons can be drawn. All I have to ask Boone is, when change is the name of the game, are you sure you can still use the same answers?!' Gabriel smiles. Content with his footage he calls time on the recording. Rising to his feet he thanks Sean for his time and as the cameras fade to black Sean is seen moving off into the distance. The opportunity is there for all to see and you'd be naive to think Sean was doing anything other than attempting to gather it with both hands held high....
