They All Fall Down

24 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/24/03 {The scene opens in the self-proclaimed 'Unlimited Warroom'. Xander is walking around a table which has the usual Unlimited protocol on it.} 'So, it comes down to this yet again' Xander begins talking as he paces around the room. 'Two days from now...New Year's eVolution. Two matches... two titles... four Unlimited matches.' 'First, let's talk about the multitude of wannabes who are going to try, and try their best, to take the Unlimited Tag Team Titles away from us. The only ones who even dare show their faces, are the duo of Yoshii Tanaka and Strictly Prohibited. Yoshii... you're mistake was teaming up with a man who has such a blatantly obvious death wish.' {He continues walking, keeping his composure} 'Prohibited... you have stuck your nose in Unlimited business one too many times. Why? I have no clue. I'm guessing you thought you could make a name for yourself by attacking, or one upping Unlimited.. but all it did was piss us off. I've tried to hold Asis back from destroying you, because I enjoyed your tricks. But you overstayed your welcome, and now we have to stop it before you really piss Asis off. I have held him back for as long as I can... and now he has free reign to do what he shall to you.' 'But, let's not forget the biggest pest to Unlimited. Seara. I don't know why you keep trying girl.... because you know that there is no winning against me. Whether I decide I want to pin you, 1, 2, 3.. or just hurt you. No one can help you. Jameson is busy trying to steal Frost's thunder... Frost is busy with Shocka, Jameson, and the fact that he's pussy whipped has only hindered him. I don't see him trying to help his lap dog out any when she's being brutilized.' 'Sure, you'll probably say you told him not to, to prove something to yourself, or someone who couldn't care less.... but that is your own misgiving. This time, though... the stakes have been raised, two fold. On the one hand, we have a tables match. I haven't had one of those in a while... but ask anyone around, and they'll tell you that I have absolutely no aversion to putting anyone through a table. Man, woman, child, owner. Whatever.. it doesn't bother me one bit.' 'Then, to make it all that much sweeter.. we add the eVolved title onto the pile. So, not only can I hurt you... beat you in another match... put you through a table... but I can take the one thing you worked so hard for. It will all be mine Seara... you will think different... but what you think is of no consequence here. You do not decide what happens.. you can only try to fight it, and still lose.' 'What, oh whatever will you do after Sunday passes us by? You'll have still never beaten your big nemesis. You'll be pulling splinters out of your ass for days. You won't have the eVolved title anymore. How, oh how will Frost ever still respect you? You will end up being nothing more then his toy. He will use you, and when you've served your purpose, he will toss you aside, and move on to greener pastures. Don't believe me? Go ahead, don't then.. just don't come back crawling saying you wish you had asked for my help, instead of encouraged my wrath.' 'Let's start the new year off with a bang... and no, not the kind you'd prefer... the bang of you hitting a table, and my pyro going off in victory.' {The scene fades out to black}
