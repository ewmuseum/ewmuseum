Cold Emotion... part seven.

26 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Actions speak louder then words!...' The voice of Morpheus Omega can be heard with a following snicker from the 'Infinite of evil' himself. The scene opening with Morpheus Omega towering tall with full shot glasses in both of his hands. Staring up from sitting on a barstool is Sky Night with an odd smile on her face, but stunningly beautiful as always. 'More then you would know it!' Sky replies with a devilish smirk. The two of them staring into their eyes, making every desperate attempt to read each other. Like an awkward moment for the both of them. 'Lets make a Twisted Reality toast, for all the hard work we have put ourselves through the last couple of weeks' Morpheus changes the subject while handing one of the shot glasses to Sky. 'To the most mentally, deranged tag team in the history of this business... 'Twisted Reality!' To the bitter revenge with the Body Snatchers and owning that bitch 1001 holds. For making the best move of our careers and hanging that chump Shocka out to dry for his Universal championship. Lastly for walking out of tainted love with the tag championships around out waists. CHEEEEERS!' Morpheus says with a snarling look, he laughs sadistically. The two of them hit their shot glasses together, they hesitate before taking their shots. Morpheus puts his down the hatch and doesn't think twice about it. Sky Night casually takes her shot and finishes it. Resting the empty shot glass on the bar. Morpheus follows lead and puts his closely beside it. The silence continues between them. 'What would you label our type of love?' Shy questions in a hypothetical way, like she was getting at something else by asking. 'Twisted as fuck!' Morpheus replies to her question. 'I would definitely say, It is the coldest of emotions and we do not have the average kissing and loving relationship. I mean, we are two of the sickest individuals on the face of this planet' Morpheus states with full confidence. The two of them remain quiet and staring in one another's eyes. The situation becoming more in-depth between the two of them. 'I mean, It all finally comes down to this... Think about this one, Do you really hate to love or do you love to hate?' Morpheus nods with a disturbed look and leaves the question up in the air. 'Everything has compromise in the middle' she answers. 'Specially something like that. The reason I asked, is sometimes I don't know where to begin with you. I think I can explain my emotions and inner feelings I have towards you, but other times I feel that I am lost and in the dark. Maybe this feeling is merely cold feet from caring so much for you. I have never cared for anyone, not even my family' Sky Night has a saddened face and looks to be crying out for help. Morpheus extends a hand and brushes the hair from Sky's face; she seems tense and afraid by jumping slightly. The terrified look remains in her eyes shortly, it fades back away... 'SKY! Why do you tense up and look as though you fear me at times? If you cared or had any trust in me, you would feel sheltered and protected in my presence. Its like you do not accept me in your life and you have been mixed in the head since we met' Morpheus with the twisted cheerful look sinking down into an angered frown. 'How can I trust someone who turned on their best friend?' Sky asks with an intrigued look on her face. Finally getting somewhere with the conversation, the point of conflict is clearly questioned. 'Shocka is no friend, he never was to me. Im still awe struck over the fact that he believed, I was his friend coming to eVolution. I broke when he made his little speech of how he wanted to be the best eVo Universal champion ever and how no one could take his precious championship. I gave Shocka his double dose of reality at Tainted love. I did him the favor of educating him on his utter ignorance. Face it, without me he was going nowhere fast in the first place' Morpheus states boldly and displeased to discuss bottom-feeding trash like Shocka 'You wanna know what I think of the situation? I personally don't like the little shit myself, but your past jealousy of him having the Universal championship and yourself with no major accomplishments here. You acted on child like resentment and jealousy! It showed through like a queer still in the closet. You can say anything to try and make me think otherwise and I will still not believe you!' Sky states with a sly smile on her face, she continues 'I KNOW!' 'I don't need the games or bullshit from you. Where the hell do you get off making wrongful accusations like the one you just made? Since day one of the fake reuniting of myself and Shocka's friendship, I have been plotting on how he would taste my revenge. That man has always basked in the image that I portrayed for him. Taking full credit for work, but Shocka was always so quick to point the bloody finger. The burning rage inside of me, will be released first with the number one 'Stunna' I am his living nightmare of reality!' Morpheus retorts with a serious tone of voice and breathes deeply, his eye twitches slightly. Turning his back on his significant other, tag team partner and only person who ever chipped the ice at his inner emotions; Morpheus vents by putting his fist through the wall with ease. Turning and shaking his head side to side, Morpheus looks very unimpressed of this situation. 'You are opening up a can of worms you will never be able to shut, if you ever talk down like you are superior to me again. You have to understand when I say 'This is my world and your all living in it' that I mean this is MY world and you are all living in it. You can chose to triumph with at my side or stand down and back the fuck off!' hesitating a moment... 'You have no choice in the matter. You have no stand point to make choice in anything that has to deal with me!' Morpheus turns upon finishing his words. 'Waittt!' Sky screams with an upset voice. 'I never meant to anger you. I was only trying to become closer with you and you blew everything I said out of proportion. Without a doubt in my mind, I love you but I cannot explain it for the life of me. You have to understand, I have torn emotions for you and it hurts not being able to explain our love. The way you feel about our love hurts, how I feel and the whole idea in the very first place. Life is a fucked up thing and I just think the two of us should guide each through it. Control was never what I wanted, but sometimes I feel you should stop being a cold-hearted prick when it comes between the two of us' Sky states with a single tear falling from her eye and rolling down her cheek. Before the tear falls from the jaw line of Sky, Morpheus catches it inside the palm of his hand. The two of them remain quiet and lost amongst each others eyes. Both of them touched deeply and very confused of each other and also themselves. Both of them approach each other. Morpheus surrounds and holds Sky, he lets her feel eased and at rest inside of his arms. Sky Night sniffles and sounds deeply upset. 'One day, when this all said and done. The two of us will look back and think foolishly of ourselves right now. Like we are lost and troubled. As my disturbed friend Roger would put it. 'Just two lost souls swimming in a fish bowl... year after year...' I guess you need to find yourself before anyone else' Morpheus states with the fakest of emotional looks on his face. The look immediately shifts into an evil look with his sickest of grins. The scene fades in the awkward moment.
