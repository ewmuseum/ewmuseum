Words of Determination, From eVo's 'Mr Nearly'

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/09/02 Hidden under the peak of his cap, a battered and slightly dissapointed Sean Mason sits looking down at the ground of the eVo studio. Still recovering from the PPV, Sean can be seen grimacing slightly as he adjusts his positioning for the cameras. The PPV was a night where four men gave so much, but only one walked away a victor. Like any war, the price of any presense is huge, and to only victor may the spoils be given. Dressed in a pair of jeans and a white t-shirt with no logo on whatsoever, Sean gives the impression of a man who is trying his best to bounce back from yet ANOTHER defeat. For those who have watched of recent, it is very clear Sean is turning into the nearly man of eVo. Close to achieving so much and yet always left empty handed. Its a bitter pill to swallow for the former FAW multi time champion, but nevertheless while there is fight in the dog, the dog will fight on. 'You want some water or something before we start Sean?' the crew man asked. 'Nah, its k, just wanna get into this' Sean responded. 'I've had alot to dwell upon these past few days and its better I get this task outta the way and get back to the gym. Last thing I need to be doing is reminising over what nearly was...' 'Sean...' another crew man cried from the distance.... 'Over here' Sean replied with a sigh. 'Where?!' 'Over here' Sean said waving his arms frantically in a sarcastic manner, 'did your mum forget to pack your glasses today or summink?!' Suddenly the previosuly un-seen crew man can be seen in the distant. With a slightly flustered look he bends down toward Sean and whispers something which can't be heard in his ear. Sean takes in the words, before looking away in disgust, 'what do you mean the photographers off ill?! Did he not see the PPV, I mean if hes got the flu and hes missing today then why the hell is my battered and bloody ass in the building!?!?!...' 'Im sorry Sean' 'So am I' Sean replied with a tone of disgust. 'You wanna cut some sort of promo now then' Sean said turning back to the first seen crew man. 'I mean its not like I have anything better to do' Reaching for his hand held camera the crew man nods enthusiastically. In the moments that pass the camera is set up in preparation, while Sean takes the opportunity to get a moment to chose his words carefully. 'Ready...' the camera man smiled, now poised with his camera. 'Good' Sean replied. 'Can I just point out, for many an improvised promo may be a burden not worth considering, but since DD its been hard to think about other than the match everyone wants to know about. So close. So close I went to becoming a champion in eVo, but once again by the end of the show the harsh reality of life was highlighted once more. Theres a thin line between success and failure. Right now the nickname 'prodigal' seems more like a ball and chain round my neck than a word of inspiration. It was a match that really shouldnt be one to be dispondant about. To hear the roars of the fans, to hear the cries of excitement from the masses; its one of those feelings we as wrestlers live for. However nothing can erase the image of HBC standing with gold draped over her shoulder. As I lay on my back in agony I knew once again I was leaving a looser. Despite the bloodshed and the previous near misses, I was leaving a broken man, with a dissapointment ravaged soul' Sean continued with a sigh. 'However if there was one good thing to come out of the night, it was that the self professed king of stranobi left without his gold and instead was left to contemplate the feeling of being nothing more than a pretender to the throne. I feel kinda sorry for him in a way. I mean having held the gold for so long it musta come as a shock to his system when he lost. Now when he looks in the mirror, all he has for comfort is the paper crown which sits perched on his head. Credit should be given for his commitment and fight at the PPV, but when you've screwed with so many for so long its hard to forget the past memories of corruption. I remember actually fearing for Quasers health and safety during that match. At the moment Thug Roc was kicked backstage to rot in his own self pity, I couldnt help but wonder how Quaser was going to cope without the continued support of his new found father figure. Like Laurel and Hardy the two have walked together and yet the two were seperated at the PPV. Looking ahead I wonder if lightning will strike twice. It seems almost a horrifying thought that these two could be split once more; then again do I really want the two to not show together. Lets not forget something, I HATE both with a passion....' Sean paused for a moment before leaning forward as his eyes became filled with a firey passion. 'No matter how many days go by, I shall always be forced to relive the past months and the events that conspired during them. Never one to make any excuses, I will try not to blame others for my failings, however I dont think I would be too far of the mark if I implied that my mistakes have not come without a certain degree of illegal aid. Plus lets not forget the simple truth, Quaser cost me the impact title yet again at the PPV. With that all in mind, the motivation is only to obvious going into my match with the king himself at Legends this week. It seems only fitting that the rot be stopped against a man who started the whole slide into motion. Then like I say, Thug Roc can not be forgotton. Sure he may not wrestle here. Sure he may be but a man proping himself up using the power of a pen. Nevertheless though, he is a figure that has disrupted my progress as of late. Of late its been like a game of chess. Thing is, up until now for every move you have made I havent made the right response. Is the game over though!?! Hell no... In truth its only just begun and it will only be when one man leaves the other for dead that check mate should even be considered. LIke I say, seen as you have been calling the shots of late, let me make a lil'request to Mr Roc. Roc I want your petty ass at ringside during my match at Legends'. 'You do?' the camera man splurtled slightly shocked by the invitation.... 'I do..' Sean paused. Suddenly the rage in his eyes has gone and now it seems Sean is in a more calculating mindset. 'I want Thug Roc there as a witness. I want him to watch in horror as I take the fight to Quaser. Then Thug when it all becomes too much, I want to watch as you make your ever predicatable and pre-meditated save. Whether it be with the power of the pen, mic or perhaps heaven forbid with your dusty fists, I shall expect the attack and when you make that move, expect to see a totally different side to Sean Mason.' 'You really think thats such a good idea?' the camera man said butting in. 'No' Sean replied. 'But I'd rather oppose the oppression, than be ruled by it. Sure at DD the ref did the right thing, but in some ways I couldnt help but be dissapointed that Thug Roc was banished from ringside. I mean sure that match was left to come to a fair conclusion, but what about next week, or the week after? By banning Thug from one night all you are doing is delaying the obvious and giving him the satisfation that he has been noticed without any true punishment. I mean do you really think he cared about Quaser loosing the gold?! Hell no. The challenge is for someone to step out from the crowd and truely prove that they can threaten the dominance he weilds. Thats something I have to aim for...' Suddenly a man can be heard crying out in a hysterically confused tone of voice. 'Sean... Sean....' 'Yes... Yes...' Sean replied with a degree of sarcasim in his voice as he looked up to see the second crew man accompanied by a photographer opposite him... 'Arr... So for what should I owe this pleasure. I mean a fit and healthy photographer, what more could I want'. Looking away with a weary look in his eyes, he looks down at his watch before turning back to the camera man he had been talking to for the past hour, 'well kid looks like the show must go on, despite its little stoppage. Go easy..' With that Sean gets to his feet. The camera pans to black as Sean then moves towards the white screen which acts as the backdrop for todays shoot. Its another contractual obligation that proves so mind numbingly boring for the professional wrestler, but at the same time its something that gives them yet more time to think. Having said that Seans words suggest hes thought long and hard. Now its time for actions. Roll on Legends, Sean aint fussed about waiting.....
