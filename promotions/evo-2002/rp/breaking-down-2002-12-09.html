Breaking Down

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-9-02 (Last week after a three month hiatus 'The DiscoKid' Troy Pierce returned to Evolution with am open challenge, a man with a familar name took him up on his challenge. This week Pierce returns to the ring against Xavier Citizen. Now only a few days before Pierce enters the ring again we find Pierce in downtown Vancouver wandering around. Pierce walks in and out of a few stores and eventually makes his way to a park and sits down on a park bench...) Pierce:'Wow, it seems like I was just in Vancouver like a year ago standing inside of the General Motors Plaza with the Insanity Wrestling Heavyweight Championship. But now after three long months on the sidelines I make my return to Evolution. Ever since I showed up last Wensday it's been the same questions, 'Where have you been? What have you been doing?' Well as for where I've been, I'll tell you it wasn't in the United States. When I checked out of the hospital one week after the injuries I suffered at the hands of my former protege Matt Boone, I left the country and traveled to New Zealand. There I worked with a former Strongman competitor and now you have the man that you see before you. No longer am I 'The DiscoKid' that stood in the ring when Evolution opened for business. I am bigger, stronger, faster and have a whole lot of things to prove now. So Xavier Citizen, a name that Malcolm King used to use. The Malcolm King, a former foe and a former ally all wrapped into one. I never knew where I stood with King, and I liked that, but at least he brought limes. So I went out and checked into a few things Xavier, and what I learned was not a damn thing. You are a question mark, the only thing I learned was your style in the ring. I learned that this match is not going to be a cakewalk. I haven't set foot in a ring since I was injured, so I'm pretty sure that the ring rust is going to be 5 layers thick. But know this when my music hits and the lights dim, my pyro rocks the arena and the crowd erupts all of the ring rust will turn into adrenaline. But I also learned that you probally know a whole lot about me by now, much like Malcolm King, always making sure you have a plan. So I have no idea what your plan is, Malcolm King once laid a challenge down for me and pulled me into the main event in EDW. As for you Xavier now either much like King did you are out there scouting me, seeing what kind of man I am, seeing that I can do in that ring. But you have no ULWN Championship for me to take, you are just going to be the man that is going to go down in the history book as the first man I defeat on the road to the Evolution Universal Championship. That belt was wrapped around my waist in EDW, and I liked the way that is felt. It has been a long time that I have had a gold belt wrapped around my waist, and this Wensday my quest begins anew. Don't slack off Xavier, I don't want a cake walk, I want a challenge, and don't forget the Sought After can come from anywhere, at anytime, and when it does almost no one kicks out. When the back of your head bounces off of the mat and you are laying there looking up at the lights and the referee counts to three and then you see me stand up with hands raised in the air, you know this disco doesn't suck.' (Pierce stands up from the bench he was sitting on and continues on his walk as the scene fades to black.)
