Prepare Well And All Will Succed

2 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd April 2003 A large bang opens the scene and Seara bursts into shot, her hands taped up and blood dripping down her fist. “Come On Katherine, pay attention, Legends is tomorrow night” The female swings towards the punch bag one more time and as it swings back toward her, she grabs it and yanks at it with her full strength, ripping it from its secure fixture on the ceiling. Her breathing is heavy and laboured and she is red in the face, anger fills the thin-framed female and she seems to growl as she breathes. “That’s it, wild animal instinct” coming into view her male instructor shows his face, it has been a long time since he worked with Seara, and she is showing signs of change. “I’m too fucking soft” her remark is accompanied by a towel being shoved into her hands, and she wipes the sweat from her brow. “I got that title and I couldn’t even keep a hold of it, I have lost all my fight, I’m weak, I’ve become pathetic, it’s horrible. But this week will be different, Legends is mine, the tag match will be something people will remember for a long time. Shocka and eXodus best pull their weight. I know those guys are strong, good fighters, motivated enough for this match, but still, I am not used to the help, I can cope in a tag of two people, but this will be immensely different for me, I just hope those guys realise that I am out to kill this week. Omega and roC, we go back a way, and we all know we want to kill each other, and now is your opportunity, but the thing is, you will be unsuccessful, I will obliterate you, make no mistake about that.” Stepping out of shot the female places herself on a stool and lifts up her kit bag, going through it, she finds her original costume, only a slight difference has happened for it since she meet Frost, but this costume, it symbolises Seara’s attitude, she knows what she can do to people, its not as though she is completely defenceless, she is a fighter, a survivor and as of late she’s been thinking about the crimson flow of another wrestlers blood running down her hands. The blood dripping from her hand momentarily catches her attention and she looks toward her hand resting on her knee. “Blood is an amazing thing, it helps keep us alive, its such a valuable part of our bodies, and it is fascinating. But another’s blood is what I want. Thug roC, your blood is what I’m after, you have fucked me over too many times, and now I want my revenge on you. I am offering you the chance to go one on one with me this Thursday night, when our two teams step in the ring, I challenge you to be the first in the ring to face me, you will get your chance to do what you want with me, and I will be able to tear you limb from limb. I will smell your blood and flesh; I will taste your pain. I hate you roC, you are the bain in my life, and the sooner I rid eVolution wrestling of you, the better it will be for everyone around here, well except your little butt kissing friends Omega and Hedges. Omega you could be great, solo though, without that little screw up, do yourself a favour and leave him behind when I end him, as for Hedges, I am really not sure what you were thinking, joining New-TV, I would think anyone would have more brains than that. But hell obviously not, not you anyway. You could have joined us Hedges, even Unlimited would have been better than New-TV, but its your funeral, I’ll just be the one helping get you ready for that day. You chose the wrong people to team with Hedges, and unfortunately for you, you will pay for their mistakes. You could redeem yourself however and join us, it is a good idea, and you know it would be worth your time, either that go solo, because I promise you, being with New-TV will be a living hell for you, and I will make it so.” Once again, the female gets to her feet and stretching out, she begins to warm up again. It is not long before the female is strapping ankle and wrist weights to her and is climbing onto a treadmill. With the females coach by her side screaming and shouting into her ear, she seems to fade away into extreme concentration, and the scene fades out.
