Ping Pong and Memories

9 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-09 The scene opens up inside of a very cluttered basement. It’s about the size of an apartment with a thin beige rug on the floor and white painted walls, which gives it some vibrancy. In it is everything from bags of clothes, to broken appliances, all the way to an entertainment area complete with a couch and big screen TV with game systems attached all over, and a ping pong table placed up against a wall. “Time to meet your wrath Sir Ping Pong.” A very lively Kramer Wild Wing says as he rushes into the basement from the steps with a paddle in one hand and the tiny ping pong ball in the other. Kramer steps up to the ping pong table and gives an evil glare at the wall as though it were a taunting opponent. “You want some of this whitey?” He waits a second and furrows his brows as though the wall had said something insulting. Without further hesitation he smacks the ball across the net and it instantly comes back, but he isn’t ready for it at all as it goes sailing right past his swing. “That was lucky….it won’t happen again.” He rushes over to pick up the ball from the floor and goes back to his previous position, sending the ball sailing over the net. It comes back and he manages to hit it, literally slamming it against the wall, which serves as a mistake as it sails back in full force, connecting with his face. “AHH!” Kramer says holding his left eye and backing away from the table. He removes his hand and his eye is squinting, still feeling the stinging effects of the ping pong ball. “I’m beginning to think this is a bad idea.” Kramer says to himself as he sets the paddle on the table. “Don’t want to risk killing myself so I’ll just stop right here.” He looks around the basement for a moment and notices a small area behind a stack of boxes in the corner, where a shelf is. He walks over to it and scoots the boxes out of the way to give him some room. A plaque on the shelf reads “Wrestling Stuff”. “I forgot this was down here.” Kramer says reaching up to pull a photo album down amongst other things. He tucks it under his arm as he walks over to the black sofa and sits down, ready to take a look at his past for the first time in a long while. He pulls the cover back and “JEW Days” is written as the header. His eyes take in all the pictures of his glorious days in JEW (which weren’t many). He flips through some more pages in the Just Electrifying Wrestling section and sees magazine and newspapers doing pieces on him. “This was where I got my start….this started the Wild Wing era.” Passing over a few pages he flipped over to the section reading PCW. Now that was a place with a lot of good and bad memories, Kramer thought to himself. One picture that stood out above the rest almost brought a tear to his eye. He and his father were side by side, and Kramer had a shining belt around his waist, the Extreme Championship. “He was always so proud of me.” Kramer said softly, almost in a whisper. The more he looked at the picture the more he missed his father. Not wanting to deal with the pain he slammed the book shut and tossed it to the right side of the couch. The reason the picture hurt so much was because his father was no longer around. He had passed away about nine months ago due to heart problems. After that happened Kramer removed himself from the wrestling scene. His father was his inspiration for wrestling and without him he couldn’t do it anymore. Or so he thought. As Kramer went all over trying to find a job that would suit him, he began to realize that wrestling was his life. That’s why he signed up with Evolution two weeks ago. They were the only ones who would give this ring rust wrestler a chance. “Man I almost forgot! Have to go meet Ty at that restaurant!” He jolts up from the couch and rubs at his eyes and sniffs a bit to clear the tears, as he races up the steps. The camera focuses in on the picture of Kramer and his father. The scene fades….
