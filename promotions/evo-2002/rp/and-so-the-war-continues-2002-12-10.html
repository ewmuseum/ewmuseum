And So The War Continues....

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Only a few hours before Legends and as the cameras pan backstage we find none other than Sean Mason roaming the corridoors. Seans alone at this point, probably reflecting on his match tonight in which he finds himself competing for the first time with Big Daddy Dreamer. Although Sean had expected to meet Morpheous Omega sooner rather than later, the presense of BDD is somewhat of a suprise. Sean never asked for BDD to be involved tonight so it will certainly be of interest to the fans just how the two men interact. Of late Seans past few weeks have been relatively successful. Having captured the Rising Star Title at the PPV, since he has seemingly moved on to the front foot and begun to make people take note of his presense much more frequently. Seans never been a man shy of words, but recently his words have been backed up with bold actions. With every passing week Seans confidence has seemingly been growing and that can only be a positive for both the EVO fans and himself. Dressed in a white vest top and jeans he looks a relaxed figure and at this point it would seem he is just sampling the atmosphere. As he looks down through the glasses he sees a box laying on the ground before him. The box is semi open and its contents are slightly visible through a small opening on the top of the box. Reaching down he opens the box slightly more, to reveal a number of programmes that are from the forthcoming Legends. Taking a programme out he stands upright and begins to flick through the pages as he leans up against the wall behind him. Just then from round the opposing corner the ever predator like camera crews emerge from the shadows. Not saying a word they spot Sean and are quick to lock their lens on the EVO star..... 'Can a guy get no peace in this place?' Sean asked pearing over the pages of the programme. 'I mean sometimes, just sometimes, it would be so nice if you could enquire whether you were allowed to film me and not just do it without any word of warning!' Slightly startled by Seans knowledge that they were filming the crew remain speechless. Suddenly in a slightly squeeky voice one of the crew members plucks up the courage to speak. 'Im sorry Sean' he said, 'is it ok?' he then asked tentatively. 'What happens if I say no?' Sean snapped back in a sarcastic manner. The crew simply shrug its shoulders, to which Sean responds with a sigh. Rolling the programme up in his hand he looks down at the crew with a thoughtful expression. 'I guess its been a while' he then continued, before then placing his shades on top of his head and composing himself. 'So lets talk. Infact lets be more precise, what I mean is lets talk about tonight, as you can imagine its at the forefront of my thinking right now!... 'Everyone has seemingly noticed of late, but im told Rise of Legends was perhaps a major turning point in my fortunes. Having struggled for so long people have looked back at my success against Seara and pointed to that as the moment I finally step out of the shadows and firmly into the bigger picture. Thing is, up until now not one single person has questioned my method of doing so!' Sean said raising an eyebrow. 'In the weeks leading up to Legends I had alot of time to think about things; most notably Evo. For so long I had been on the receiving end of some pretty brutal punishment, I kinda began to realise that something wasn't quite working for me. Its at this point I began to question my approach; was I being too forward or simply not positive enough!?! After alot of soul searching though I came to a conclusion; I missed the ruthless streak any good champion requires to make it in this business. In EVO, with the exception of perhaps L2J, it would seem that nice guys simply don't win prizes. Sure they win friends, but then again I hadn't come to EVO simply to meet new people. Wrestling is my career and as such my goal had to be to move upward in the food chain and I wasn't going to get the job done as things stood! Its not that I feared taking the risks, its not that I objected to the clinical approach; but for some reason I was not playing to my strengths, instead I was just trying to appease the fans with a charismatic performance. Don't get me wrong the fans still mean as much as they ever did, its just I now realise that they would rather I was beating the bad guy, than getting beat as a result of my somewhat naive showman nature... As for tonights show, well its an opportunity to set a few records straight; so lets talk about Morpheous Omega. Morpheous' Sean said in a determined tone, 'tonight the gauntlet continues it would seem. Is there ever an end to the conveyer belt of brainwashed talent Thug Roc sends in my direction?! Sooner or later there must be an end to the number of weak minded minnions that Thug seems to be able to drag up off the street! With that in mind Morpheous are you really so sure I should be fearing another slave of New-TV? For so long now me and Thug have just never gotten along, Perhaps its a personality clash? Perhaps its just because im all that he was?! Or perhaps its just because for the first time it would seem that someone is not scared of fighting his regime which has descended upon EVO like an dark cloud. Never one to take kindly to criticism, ever since then Thugs sole mission has seemingly been to take me out of business. Look at me though' Sean said with a smug grin, 'do I look like a man whose lost the battle?! Not to mention the fact that in my locker room lies recognition of my efforts in the form of the Rising Star Title.. Morpheous my point is what makes you different? Why should I live in fear of a man who seemingly lives in fear of the very same man I have tormented for so long?! 'Tonight though, its not just about me and Morpheous, tonight also pits me in a match with two other individuals; one as my partner the other as my second opponent' Sean said taking a moment for breath. 'Talking of my partner, I'm certainly not neglecting to realise his presense has a significant effect; its just a shame he couldn't show me the respect I know I deserve. Its strange how some people are; while some are willing to extend the olive branch, others seem inclined those men who are charged with helping them. Out of my element? Or simply stepping into the spotlight and knocking your ego for six?! After your words im struggling to seek anything more than a victory from this match and the thought of befriending you, is almost as enticing as laying on a bed of nails! However tonight we have to get along and if you really want to prove your worth I suggest you worry less about my performance and more about how your going to hold up your end of the deal! Its simple, you watch my back and I watch yours; the result hopefully being C and Morpheous get the a reality check thats been a long time coming!' With that Sean paused again. Opening the programme once more that sits in his eyes wonder up and down the programme. The focus on his face is obvious but the subject of his thoughts at present is unknown. Suddenly though Seans eyes light up, 'I knew it was in here somewhere!' he said as the smile on his face continued to grow. 'My partner may well be a dreamer, but goes to show im not! Its true! Thug Roc is dusting off the cobwebs and competing in the ring again! EVO's very own wrestling artifact is stepping out of the office and swapping a pen for some spandex pants if this schedule is to be believed!!' Sean begins to laugh to himself and seemingly even the crew can not constrain their laughter. 'Scott Frost though' Sean says pouting his lips in a sarcastic manner again, 'not really liking your chances their old man. Only this time your not being allowed to send the minnions in to fight your wars! All your scars are mental from me, seems like Frost is going to get the first bite of the cherry in terms of making the superficial also.. Trust me though im second in line! Infact if im right, my match is before yours, perhaps its time the tables turned! Infact....' With that the crew look puzzled sitting behind the camera. Just as they look to quiz Sean on the meaning of that statement Sean reaches into his pocket and pulls out his cellphone. Raising his hand in the air he calls for silence as he quickly dials a number and puts the phone to his ear..... 'Hey Greg.... Its Sean.... Can you put off the transport from Legends for another hour?.... Why? Because I can't be missing Thugs return to the ring, being the fan I am!' Sean chuckles... 'Alright cool...Ok thanx man see you then....' Placing the phone back in his pocket Seans grin is obvious. Not waiting to answer any more questions though Sean dissapears off into the distance. Scene fades to black...
