A Game of Risk!

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/21/2002 After knocking on Mark's door Jason goes back to his room awaiting his brother, while waiting he sits on his bed thinking and talking about how he feels about his match coming up at Rise of The Legends as he realizes what is really at stake...... His pride! 'Laying it all on the line for gold that needs help from others, I have wasted my time thinking about how I could have been a champion by now. How I could be the Continental or Rising Star champion. I have failed in my time and its time I settle the score by taking out this team they call The Unlimited. They can think that nothing can stop them but the truth will be shown this Sunday when New-TV wins the tag team gold taking the team to new heights and giving them even more power. This game of winning and losing is over. I have set my goals and I have set my rules. No longer will I let my little brother think I am selfish but I will show him that I an truly better than he ever will become. The titles flying around this federation will soon in fact lead to one day me being a champion, a worthy champion to any federation and it being in eVo only makes it sweeter. Proving once again that Canadians made this business and we all deserve to get what is coming to us in our future and that is becoming nothing less than NUMBER ONE! You Americans think we should all feel sorry for you because you lost somebody or you know somebody that knows somebody that last someone in that damn September eleventh. You were attacked, what else is new? You jumped into thier business you nosy snobs. Now, I challenge any American to a battle. If you think you got what it takes, if you think you have the guts and the chance of glory to step into the ring with me. We will play a little game of risk, I will wager anyone, just about anything that I can destroy thier career. I have had enough of being this little quiet Canadian wanting to just gain what he can on his own, I want some gold around this fucking waist. All you see here is The Canadian Hero written on my shorts. Its time that is covered with a title that says something and means something. Yes, you may be looking at a man that will soon hold one half of the now vacant Tag Team titles, but thats not enough, I came to the states to represent those from Canada and how am I supposed to do that with out a meaning to my stay, for crying out loud the states are horrable. They have teenagers drinking, fucking, sucking, and having kids. This place makes me feel sick to my stomach. I am not sure how much longer I can handle any of this. I have held it all in long enough, my future is now and the risk taking is on. The Unlimited will never know what hit them after this Sunday as I and Shocka take them on in our tag team title match, the time has come for thier tag title journey to end. The fear of this change of mind will strike you in the eye as I hit you with with the Steele Rod and the Stainless Steele with a force and brute stregth that I have never shown the world before. The Unlimited will be playing the Game of risk as they step in the ring with I and the rest of New-TV. The future of the tag division will no longer vary from team to team. The fate of it all lies in the hands of New-TV. I will do anything in my power to keep the titles out of the hands of The Unlimited. I deserve this chance at glory. It was me that kept myself in the tournement. I had help from no one. It is time that I, The Canadian Hero takes my name to its full extent and represent my country like no other. I, Jason Steele will show you what pain is. You will realize that nothing comes as easy as you think and that the past I keep hidden will soon be revealed. Are you ready for it? I think not.' Jason ends his words as he hears a knock on the door. 'You ready?' Mark asks from behind the door. 'Yeah, one sec, let me put on my shoes,' Jason says as the scene fades to black.
