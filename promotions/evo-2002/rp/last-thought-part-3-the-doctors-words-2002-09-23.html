Last Thought part 3 - The doctors words

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23 sept 2002 Certain things happen every once in a while to make a person think about their life and the things they want from it. At some point in his life, Seara’s father had given up roaming around, had settled down and had decided it was time to start a family with his current girlfriend, his choice gave him firstly a son, then Seara, then another son. All three were focused on the life of their father. Seara being more interested in the business than her brothers. She like her father was hard to control, and hated to be settled, it took a long time to get Seara to calm down a bit, but with the issue of being settled, she found her anger built up more and she became more dangerous and aggressive in the ring. Her tag partners at the time tried hard to control the females in ring temper, but she was determined to end anyone’s career to get herself noticed by a bigger fed, a professional one. Her private life soon began to falter, and her boyfriend, and tag partner Dave began to find she was becoming unsettled and her attitude was beginning to deteriorate. He tried as hard as he found possible to keep Seara interested in their private life and relationship, and tried to keep her focused on the wrestling only when she was in the ring. But the female wrestler had a lot of anger building up inside, and she soon began to show it. She yet again became a wanderer, but kept her relationship going, she finally got accepted into a professional fed. Evolution owner Daniel Somers accepted the Chicago wrestler, and she decided to continue with the way of life she had grown up knowing, wandering, unsettled and always on the move. After an intense discussion with her boyfriend, the female began to make appearances on eVo access, where she made a big impact. But none of this had ever really changed her. She was always the way she chose to be, except now she had something more. Credit for her hard work. Since she had been brought into the hospital on Wednesday night after Digital Destruction, she had been forced to think long and hard about many things, and she had heard what doctors had been saying. She knew that with doctors stopping her fighting for the next two weeks, she would have to find something to do, something to keep her time busy. Doctors had suggested she go back to Chicago to be with her family, but Seara refused saying she had no idea where her family was, and that she had no need for them. Only half of her words were lies, she hated to talk about her family, and preferred to believe that all of them were dead. She seemed to find some easiness in the thought of her family being dead. She could care less if they got into trouble or if they missed her and were worried about the neck injury Asis had caused, it was all part of who she was now. “I know what I’m doing” she started, her words quiet and hardly recognisable. Seara - I knew what I was doing, I knew what I was doing when I left the guys to join eVo, but I never thought it was possible, I never knew about it, I never even would have guessed, I thought I was bleeding internally, but I never knew I would be bleeding for that reason, I mean it wasn't recognisable, and nothing pointed to it. She is making no sense and the cameraman just looks straight at her, wondering what she is talking about, but Seara has often been hard to understand, she has however normally come forward and described or explained what she was talking about. This time it seemed that she would not be doing that however. Panning around the camera focuses on a nurse who sits down on a seat in front of the female. She looks at the camera for a moment then turns her attention back to Seara. Nurse – So how are you feeling today dear? Seara hated the friendliness around here, it was too often, everyone was constantly happy, smiling and talking in high bouncy voices, it was too annoying for the female wrestler, she couldn’t handle being around these people much more, but in her state, she was finding it hard to go any where, even mundane tasks were painful and hard for her to perform at this point in time. Seara – Like shit, I think that sums up how I feel right now. I mean come on, you get your neck nearly fucking broken by someone you thought was your friend, you begin to bleed really fucking bad, you get carried in here on a stretcher, and you find out you were apparently pregnant until you were viciously attack. You go and do all that and tell me you wouldn’t feel like shit. It’s not easy to feel real great when shit like that happens to you, trust me. I’m fucking living through it. Nurse – surely there must be something positive about all this? Seara – yeah, yeah, there is, wait let me think, oh yeah, I know now, I’m fucked, I am dead inside. Positive enough for you, or maybe I could tell you that I am even getting bored of my own thoughts, things that kept me company all my life, and all I can think about now is Asis, and the way he took me down with that huge clothesline that nearly broke my neck. Yeah, I mean how could I not find any of this shit positive. Fuck woman, you have never been here have you, you may be a fucking nurse, but have you ever been in a fucking hospital bed injured, knowing one more hit to the neck could have broke it, or that one more vicious move like having your skull crushed between two steel chairs just one more time would have killed you, those as your only thoughts, it kind of gets you thinking, makes you maybe appreciate for a second that the monster stopped to look at you in that way. I seen his eyes, I could feel his anger staring into me. I know how he works, well, how part of him works, I don’t however know why he did what he did to me, and I believe I will never know why he did it, unless he tells me, but so far he has not appeared since Digital Destruction. Seara stares hard at the camera now, trying to avoid the nurse’s upset look, she can tell she has hurt the woman’s feelings, but she has lost her patients already, she is fed up with people being nice to her all the time just because she is an injured wrestler. She has no time now to be with the nurse, and she turns onto her side to face away from the young fair-haired lady. Nurse – I know you don’t want to talk to me, and I know you are pissed off with me being so friendly everyday, but it’s my job, I have to be nice. And it wouldn’t do you much harm to try to be nice to me. Damn it Seara, I am only trying to make the time go faster for you, but if you would rather tune into the TV everyday to catch the latest wrestling news, then feel free. I would rather you drove yourself insane so they could lock you up; you’re a dangerous freak. You shouldn’t be allowed to be out on the streets, you should be locked in a secured room and left to die. You….you….you freak of nature. Spinning around, Seara grabs the back of her own neck, and the nurse’s neck. And although she is risking serious damage to herself, she jumps out of her bed and lifts the 5”7 woman off her feet high into the air. Tipping her head back as far as she can, she looks up at her. Seara – Freak of nature. Think I should be locked up. Left to die. Oh you are one for jokes aren’t you? I could seriously hurt myself right now, but I’m willing to take that risk for you. Freak of nature. Ill give you freak of nature. Seara’s sentences are fragmented and make no sense, but it is obvious that her foul temper has just got worse, and lowering the nurse back onto her feet she looks deep into her eyes. Her hair has fallen into her face and it is hard to see her features, but as she throws the nurse across the room her hair is thrown back and her anger is revealed by the dreadful look on her face. She holds the back of her neck and rubs it, obviously in great pain from her violent little stunt. Slowly getting to her feet the nurse carefully helps Seara back into the bed and injects her with a mild slow working sedative to calm her and stop the pain before eventually knocking her out. Nurse – What’s the point Seara, as it is, you won’t be able to fight until Legends XI at the earliest, and if you keep going like that, you will be out of the ring for longer. And I’m pretty sure you will want to be there sooner rather than later, I mean you do have that rivalry with Xander that I’m sure you will want to take care of, and Asis must be on your list of people to go after, and then there is the ever handy L2J who came to your aid, I mean what will you do to L2J. Seara – Xander is still on my bad side, and I will deal with him when and wherever I feel is appropriate, as for Asis, he shall be fine doing what he wants to do. I am not saying I understand why he did what he did, but I know Asis and I know he has a motif behind nearly all that he does, and I have no reason to go after him, after all, he did help me find out I was pregnant, and with his attack on my stomach, helped me lose it. I have nothing to say about why Asis did what he did, except that it was his choice, and he is the only one that decides what he will do. L2J has done nothing to upset me or please me in any major way, so he will remain where he always was with me, safe, he shall however be safer than he was before. He managed to get “The Demonic” off of me, and I suppose in many ways I should be grateful for that, but at this point, I can’t be grateful for anything. Seara’s eyes begin to close, and she seems to become limp and lifeless, she has passed out from the drugs. Turning to the cameraman, the nurse smiles. Cameraman – Aren’t you scared, she just threw you across the room, and you just continued to work as if everything was normal. Nurse – I would be lying if I said I wasn’t scared of her, I have never had to deal with anyone quite like her, she is scary and unique and tortured, and I really think by talking to her and not acting scared, I can gain her trust, and help her, or at least get her put away. The cameraman pans back and watches the nurse as she checks certain things on Seara. As the nurse leaves the room and the door shuts behind her, Seara’s eyes open, and she laughs momentarily before closing her eyes again and drifting off to sleep. The camera fades to black, another visit to see Seara finished, and more information gained about her situation and her injuries. Which we so far know as a broken rib, a whiplash style injury to her neck, which the extent of is still not fully known, and a terrible temper that has her constantly injected on a daily basis to keep her as calm as possible, she is obviously not receiving a strong enough dosage, as it only seems to be helping her slightly with the pain she is in, apart from that, she seems very active and in control of her own anger. The camera focuses in on a large bruise around her neck and on her shoulders, and fades to black with a close up of the blue and purple discolouration of her skin.
