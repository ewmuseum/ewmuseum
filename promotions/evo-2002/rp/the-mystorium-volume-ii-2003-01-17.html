The mystorium(Volume II)

17 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.17.03 ‘They think so but they can’t be totally sure I mean you know how much crime is here…so who knows?’ Darryl says. ‘That’s messed up they didn’t find who did it or what?’ Shocka says very interested. ‘Nope…but they do know that his uncle owned the kind of gun that shot him but they can’t seem to find him or his gun he disappeared like an hour after it happened…he had a falling out with them and said something about how he was gonna hurt the kid or something.’ ‘That’s messed up!’ Shocka says in a sort of saddened voice. ‘Yeah…so just think about visiting him man..’ Darryl says… ‘I will…I’ll probably do it.’ Shocka says. ‘Hey man I hate to cut this short but I gotta take off…’ The man says waiting for a reply. ‘Okay man talk to you later.’ Shocka says. ‘Late.’ Darryl replies. ~Shocka hangs up the phone and shakes his head in a sort of dumbfounded look on his face. He begins speaking to himself.~ ‘What the hell kind of uncle is that…it sounds like my damn family!’ Shocka speaks to himself. ‘ I hope they find that sorry piece of shit he doesn’t deserve to be roaming our streets.’ Shocka says. ~Shocka pulls his car into his driveway and he jumps out of his car to see his garage door spray painted, it says. ‘FUCK YOU Johnny!’ in large bold blood red letters. He shakes his head in amazement as he tries to sort it out if its disgruntled fans or his insane wife.~ ‘What in the fuck?!’ He shouts aloud as he walks up to the door. He shakes his head and continues speaking. ‘You better hope that you son of a bitches are out of here…’ ~He walks back to his car and takes his keys out so he can get into his house. He walks past his garage and hears something inside fall. His head darts toward the garage window and he runs over to it and punches in his security code and the garage starts to rise.~ ‘You better have an army back there…I’m not in the mood to fuck around!’ He yells. ~When he opens the garage he sees a cat standing there purring at him…he rolls his eyes and shuts the garage as he now walks into his house via the front door.~ ~When he gets inside the living room is torn to shreads the couch cushions are strewn about everywhere. He laughs to himself for a second until he looks over to his 62 inch plasma flat screen TV screen with a red high heal through it! He throws his arms in the air and punches the wall leaving a good sized hole. He screams with anger about his wife before grabbing the couch and flipping it over. His cell phone rings and interrupts his rage as he calmly comes to a stop and grabs his phone.~ 'Hello?' Shocka questions. 'Hey man its me again...' the voice of darryl can be heard. 'I thought about what you said and i do want to do something tonight..' He says. 'I dunno man my house got torn up pretty bad by that over bearing bitch...' Shocka screams. 'Hey, maybe i can head over there then and see you're new car...and maybe help pick up a little?' Darryl says with sympathy in his voice. 'Hey if you do, you should pick up a case of beer or something...' Shocka says as he begins picking up his house. ~As the conversation comes to an end Shocka is left wondering about 2 things. The kid and what he is going to do about his wife.~
