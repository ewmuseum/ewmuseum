Jinx's Junk

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-05-02 ::The day is bright and sunny. The camera shifts down to the unpleasant site of a large Junkyard. Cars, scrap metal, and junk piles up at heights of thirty or forty feet in the air. Jinx is spotted moving through the aisles of destroyed appliances and machinery. He is pushing a large grocery cart that is already halfway filled up. He looks up to see the camera crew.:: Jinx: Ya know, I have to expect that it doesnt matter where I am or what I am doing, that you idiots are right behind me. There is absolutely no privacy anymore. I swear that if I didnt know any better, I would think that you were making some kind of television reality show called, 'How to Annoy a Legend.' Anyways, since you are here, I could explain what I am doing in the Junkyard. ::Jinx pulls out a small water pipe from a small pile at the side of the cart. He inspects in for a minute and swings it around quickly. He smiles and drops it in with the rest of his evil little basket of tricks.:: Jinx: You see there is an old saying.....'One man's junk, is another man's treasure.' Well I like to think that I put some of this junk to some well-deserved use.....up against the head of my opponents!!! In particular this coming Wednesday against none other than Tommy Michaels. And trust me Tommy Boy, I could care less what was said on the other end of that phone conversation, but if you plan on telling anyone what was said, I would advise you to let them know before this Wednesday. They have set this match as a hardcore match and in turn made this Tommy Boy's last match. Legends will show everyone why I am known as the 'Core' of Hardcore. As everyone knows, I am the first, only, and last Cell Champion. Until there was no competition left in the division, our stupid president wouldn't even let one of his precious wrestler's step into the ring with me. Do you know why? Because out of his own mouth, he stated that I was too dangerous. That he could not risk his wrestler's health by putting them in the ring with me. But now the time has come that he has no choice. So as for Tommy Michaels and the rest of Evolution, Welcome to your Destiny!!!
