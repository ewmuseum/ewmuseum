roC superstar! (Ch. 1)

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.31.03 ~March 30th 2003: A bitter man he is indeed, I see he uses crest indeed! The meaningless thoughts ran through Shocka’s mind as he lay perfectly still inside of the back of an ambulance. Hedges had vacated it so he could fill up with gas and pay…Shocka played up that he was still knocked out. As soon as he heard the pump click back where it belonged he was going to make his break. Hedges checked in one last time, through the back window to see Shocka lying on the cold metal floor of the ambulance gashes still bleeding. Swelling had taken over most of his face by this point and the welts quickly turned to bruises. He slowly lifted his head and as quietly as he could he pulled his body toward the door. He felt for a second and took a breath before flinging the door open and running like a mad man. The Las Vegas night had turned crisp and the air beat down on Shocka’s bare chest like needles. His wrestling tights were slightly ripped and his boots didn’t allow much spring in each step but he rounded the corner before Hedges emerged slurpee in hand to see his hostage had fled~ ~Shocka stopped a minute to check his surroundings still in Vegas just nowhere close to where he needed to be. Hedges was running a full tank of gas, Shocka knew this he jumped into the nearest car stopped at a light and the driver immediately took off around the other cars and threw a red light. The ambulance whipped around the corner and slowed to a stop, the light was still red. The driver was not wise enough to just flip on the lights and watch other motorists flee. An obvious flaw instilled on him by thug roC himself.~ ~March 31st 2003: You see the obvious wounds of Shocka covered with blood soaked bandages, as he lies half awake from a long night. A couple bruises across his forehead resemble the link of a chain as he smiles and takes a sip of some water at his bedside. Balloons at his right with get wells and flowers attached he sat up with a smirk and spoke his first words.~ ‘You know normally this isn’t my favorite place to be while I am steaming mad but this morphine drip right over here seems to do the trick. I can’t believe that roC and Omega could be so moronic as to leave me in the care of Hedges…I mean I think that Omega possess more motor skills than him and he can barely spell his own name.’ Shocka shakes his head as his nurse walks into the room. She’s not a bad brunette not the most beautiful but not as bad as thug roC’s momma. ‘You have a visitor Mr. McCade’ The lady nurse speaks with a kind voice. ‘Send them in…’ Shocka speaks annoyed. ~Lee eXodus runs into the room with a Britney Spears life size cardboard cut out he whirls around the bed and when he turns to set his bag down he whacks Shocka in the face with her feet.~ ‘DUDE!’ Shocka screams and Lee turns innocently. ‘WHAT MAN?!’ Lee screams. ‘Did you take your riddlen today?!’ Shocka demands. ‘I lost em!’ Lee shouts. ‘Yeah right you probably sold them to some kids at Unbreakable!’ Shocka laughs. ‘And why don’t you watch out with where you swing that dumb bitch broad of yours!’ ~Lee Drops the cutout to the ground so she doesn’t have to hear what is about to be said.~ ‘Don’t you dare talk to her like that just because you’re mad at me!’ Lee screams as he points to his chest. ‘Alright man just calm down…I mean don’t make a cripple whoops your ass!’ Shocka says back. ‘Bring it big man…bet you can’t even get up!’ Lee screams in a taunting voice. ‘Look at me I’m big and strong and got beat by 4 people but I can’t get up! Go cry me a river!’ Lee screams. ‘You fucked up Lee!’ Shocka says sitting straight up. ‘I got so much morphine in me right now I can’t feel my tongue!’ Shocka yells. ~Shocka jumps up out of the bed and gets Lee in a head lock his IV is removed from his arm and his pulse drops as it the connection of it goes out. The nurse rushes in to see Shocka and Lee ‘horse playing’ and the nurse breaks it up.~ ‘This is a hospital you two should no better than to mess around like this you are grown ups!’ She yells as the two stop fighting. ‘You fine girl!’ Lee yells and he wraps his arm behind her and they walk out of the room as the scene fades on Shocka sitting back down. ‘I was once a roC superstar!’ The screen reads.
