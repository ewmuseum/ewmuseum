'Til Death Do You Part

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/03 Will you take this woman to be you’re lawfully wedded wife Ronan was now a threat to a way of life, and, while being an influential piece of the puzzle, he still felt useless. Time had certainly passed by now, many years leaking away one after the other like droplets from a pipe. The water was beginning to run out, and before long there would be none to continue on. Better use that water for some good while it’s still there. To have and to hold Sitting in the fast food joint, looking around at what his life has become, Ronan takes a bite out of his sandwich and reminisces. Smiles and tears of the woman he had decided to spend the rest of his life with, now only a name thrown away. Chewing away, his face wore a dismal mask, his eyes enclosed by dry tears. He wouldn’t let himself cry. Ever. Every now and then, though, his sleep didn’t really care. From this day forward Faces passed by, some of them looking over at him and then shying away. He didn’t mind. He was use to being a nobody by now. Ronan didn’t expect anyone to recognize him anytime soon, since he had only been in one show so far. He wasn’t a face that stuck out in that crowd anyway. With guys like Scott Frost and James Kimson, a old, tired man like that of Ronan Ferrell wasn’t anything special. Maybe it could have been, once, but that was another story. For better or worse Taking a sip of his soda, he looked out the window, seeing a young couple walking, hand in hand, in the park across the street. He could remember times like that. Being with her. Being young. Being happy, without a care in the world or a scar to live with. Those were the days when being alive meant something. But now, it was all about trying to get back what he never had to begin with – a career. For richer or poorer Ronan took another sip, and then got up from his seat, leaving the rest of his uneaten food to decay on the table top. Outside, parked in the lot was his new car, paid by the salary from his federation after his debut on Evolution television. His whole life, and basically that was all he had to show for it. A new, white Mercedes, something he never really wanted to begin with but bought out of self pity. In sickness and in health Opening the door, the stench of new leather hit him, as he sat down in the driver’s seat. He looked behind him, to the backseat of the car, where his old suitcase and his new gym bag lay waiting for his return. Clothes and other belongings – that was what he carried with him on the road, and all he had anymore. He figured he could sell the house and live on the road for the rest of his life, but that wasn’t what he was planning on doing. Ronan opened the suitcase, and dug around. Tossing his shirts and socks out of the way, he looked for one thing that he had made sure to bring along before he left to go on the road. In a golden frame, the same one he had gotten on that day of the picture, surrounded his glass encased memory. To love and to cherish There Alexandra was, smiling and looking as beautiful as God had made her, standing in Ronan’s arms. The day came flashing back to him, remembering how good it felt to realize that she was going to be with him forever. The day that he never had to worry about being alone again, because his life was now set in stone, never to be empty again. The day that he knew that he had made the right choice. And then there he was. Alone and tired. Waiting for his opportunity to come. But it wasn’t getting any better, and now he was stuck with this image that he would never get old. That he was the ageless Ronan Pierce, out to prove that he is the fiercest wrestler alive. That he would never die. ‘Til death do you part Ronan was alone. And no amount of success would save him from that.
