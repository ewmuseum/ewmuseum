Eternity's Beauty Ripped Away

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/8/02 I had a rather unique dream last night. I don’t think I’ve had a dream like that before, or will I ever have one like it again. My eyes opened up, a blue sky smiling at me from the heavens above. The clouds slowly crawled over head, each seeming to wave with their movement across the sky. As I stared up, I wondered where I was, or how I had got there. I sat up, and stared around, seeing a field with only a few trees marking the green grass. A slight, comfortable breeze swept over the grassland, creating a majestic pattern of waves in the green that blanketed the ground. I could feel myself smiling, my heart holding all the happiness in the world, as I watched it happen. And now all I can do is envy my dreamed self, wishing that I could have truly been him at that very moment. I stood up, and looked around even more, taking in all that I saw, realizing its beauty lying before me. A river flowed to my left, its graceful movements only adding to the splendor of the scene. The sun shined down from above, warming the air so that it was just right. The wind didn’t die down, but only kept up its steady pace, which I didn’t mind in the slightest. I could stand here, mislaid in a sphere of everything, waiting for the end of time to arrive, and I would never have given it up. Not for anything in this life or the next. Nothing could have matched the feeling I held inside. Hate and love – they were nothing compared to these emotions running through me. A million questions ran through me as I stood there for the briefest of moments. How long did I stand in that one spot? How long did I take in all that I could see in the one position? How long could I have been lost in my own surroundings? Did I ever have to leave? Could my eyes stay closed forever so that I could enjoy this beauty until the day I died? Was I seeing heaven on the back of my eyelids? Was I, for the first time in my life, truly alive in my slumber? Was this a gift from God? Could I take this with me, and hold it in my hands forever, like the embrace of my one true love? Was I real? Was this real? Was anything in this world beyond me? Could I reach out and shake God’s hand if I wished? Could I grip the flames of Hell if I stepped to far away? Was this existence what I was meant to find? Was this beauty shadowed by the blanket of filth? Was I...? A figure began to walk towards me, off in the distance. My mind went blank, as all my attention was directed towards this mystical being living in this numinous dwelling locked inside my mind. It drew closer. And closer. Time went by, and I didn’t mind. Minutes or hours or seconds or days, it didn’t matter to me. The sun was on my back. The echo of water flowing filled my soul. The wind carried my weightless body into the sky. I was untouchable in my world. And nothing would change that. But as it drew closer, I saw her. This world I found was beautiful. This state of mind I found myself in was nothing short of magnificence. I could have been and heaven and not have known the difference. I never wanted to leave this beauty. But, in all the beauty, she was everything. The world seemed to bleed away as she stood there before me. Her hazel eyes glowing brighter then the sun above my head. Her hair’s dancing in the wind, which outdid the makeshift waves of wind in the grass. Her smile, melting away the rest of the world. I had found Eden in paradise, and she was standing in front of me, staring into me with all her beauty. And everything had changed. “Hey Jason.” The words echoed through the unbounded field, as if the wind carried her voice and multiplied it a million times over. I remained silent, enthralled by all her beauty as if she were the gates to all of eternity. Her smile had wrapped me up in a casing of pure love, and would never loosen its grip, and I didn’t care. I always talk about perfection. This was the perfect place for me to live the rest of my life. “Jason. I love you. I still love you. And I will never let that go. And I want you to remember…” My eyes ripped opened, and the magnificence that was my perfect dream was torn out of my heart and soul, and cast away into nothingness as if none of it ever mattered. The empty and darkened hotel room remained, its presence seeming to laugh into my face, impassive to anything that I just experienced. The headlights of street cars below filled the room, showing me that I had returned to the world that never made sense. My everything turned into nothing with just the awakening of my mind. My eyes had betrayed me. Instead of a beautiful blue sky, they brought me into this blackness. The grass that had brought me all the emotions in the world were nothing more then a cheap duplicate picture of renowned painting hanging on my wall, not carrying the waves that they had in my vision. I wanted to pull out my hair, knowing I would never feel that exquisite breeze blowing through it again. I wanted to cry, tears of sorrow and pain, but I couldn’t. They were already adrift in a river I would never see again. And what of her? Was she my ephemeral breath of life? Was I bound to die alone, with only the memory of the most magnificent beauty to shadow my end? Was she the only happiness I could ever hold in all my pain? When I awoke from that dream, I knew one thing. I was destined to die alone, and I would never see her beauty before me again.
