Another Demonstration

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd April 2003 Unbreakable, much like Legends XXX did not go the way Asis had planned it. His open challenge on Legends XXX was over ruled by Daniel Somers who wanted the match at Unbreakable to be Asis vs Erin Christoph so the Continental Title stayed in Unlimited. Asis was none too happy about that. Then at Unbreakable Erin Christoph was fired by the returning Tyler Davidson, which was a shock to everyone. But the thing that pissed Asis off the most was the return of Cameron Griffin and of Sean Mason, two people that he put out of action under Unlimited eye. And they tried to make a mark on Asis at Unbreakable, but as so many people do, all they did was get his attention… Why? Why the fuck do people not listen to me? Why do people not pay attention? You would’ve thought that people would know what I am capable of. Or maybe they just need another demonstration… Asis is pacing around a trashed hotel room (as is so often the way) and is most likely referring to Unbreakable. He walks past a table and lashes out with his left arm, knocking a television set flying across the room. It smashes into the wall and Asis winces in pain holding his left shoulder and arm. This week has not been very good. Maybe that’s a bit of an understatement but more or less I haven’t been a cheery kind of guy lately. And it all stems back to my “family”, the people I’m supposed to trust and respect. The people I should receive the same recognition from. First Xander gets threatened by the fact that I can think for myself. Just because I make a decision about how I want to defend my title he gets worried. Like I told him before, the Continental Title belongs to me, I won it, and I decide how to defend it. I may be Unlimited but my title is not. He obviously doesn’t believe in me enough to let me handle my own business. If I weren’t around to protect him and Somers, Unlimited would have faded out long ago. Which brings me onto Danny who is another subject entirely. I never really got along with him very well, ever since I joined EdW. Then he becomes part of eVolution and then he becomes leader of Unlimited. He was another one who always seemed sceptical of me, never thought I had potential or whatever. Generally just over looked me, just used me to take people out. Then as soon as I have a thought of my own, he over rules me deciding it’s safer to have a match where the one title within Unlimited cannot be lost. Again, he doesn’t believe in me. Well anyway that fucked up didn’t it Danny boy? Seeing as you let two people creep back into eVo that you made sure I had a hand in getting rid of. Cameron Griffin and Sean Mason, two men I had no personal grudge with, it was just business. But now, especially you Mason, have gained my personal attention, something that most people tend to live to regret doing… Asis stops pacing and leans against a wall, away from any windows so he is cloaked in darkness. He rests his head back against the wall, closing his eyes, his hair falling out of his face. Griffin you really were nothing but business to me, you didn’t bother me one way of the other. You were a problem for Unlimited and as my job entailed I took you out to try and solve that problem. But now you’re back you’re trying to get some sort of revenge. Trying to punish me for what I did maybe. Spearing you through the door of your own limo is obviously something you didn’t enjoy or agree with. Well I enjoyed it very much and if you don’t tread carefully around me you may find something similar happening again soon. Asis now leans his head forward, the blue moonlight from outside shines through a window and partially illuminates his face. He opens his eyes to reveal his deathly red eyed stare, a very intense and serious look now on his face. And finally I come to the man who has caused me the most grief since at Unbreakable. I am of course talking about Sean Mason. Griffin brings you back with him at Unbreakable and of all people you decide to piss off me. Fair enough I had a hand in putting you out of action for the past couple of months, but now is not a time when you want to come back to eVo and get on my bad side. I noticed you looking at my Continental title – my Continental title. I’ve never been one to aspire for greatness, to wish for success, to want to be a champion. Winning this belt was no different at first. It was just another title I’d won because I didn’t want my opponent to have it. The previous owner of this title was the kind of guy who lives for success, who lives for titles. Which is exactly why I took it from him. And when I crippled and retired Pierce, that was in defence of this title. So I’ve become quite attached to it. Kicking people’s asses is something I’ve been doing all my life, and now this belt gives me more drive to do it than I’ve ever had. I used to go on just raw emotion but now I’ve got a possession to keep hold of as well. So Sean if you want this belt you’re going to have to try damn hard to take it from me. You’re not on my list of favourite people right now as it is, which you’re going to find out at Legends. And whilst you may have the two time Universal Champion on your side, Scott will soon learn that being the top champion does not make him untouchable. And just a little reminder, I’d like to let Xander know that if he wants this match to go our way, then he should do a professional job and stay the hell out of my way. See you all soon boys…
