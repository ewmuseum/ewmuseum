The emergency lights are on as the woman who grabs her cell phone screaming in pain..She then dials a number and waits for the person to pick up...The person does as she screams in the phone.. 'Get your ass to the hostpital now..' She then throws the phone in the back as her husband the one driving tries to calm her down... 'Honey its ok...We are two minutes away..deep breath..come one..deep breath..' She then looks at him and yells.. 'Yea this is all your fault to..You better be glad I always wanted a boy...' He then steps on the gas even more as they go flying past cars..The man then turns off the road and onto a small off ramp..He takes a left at that light as he then turns into the Lansing Hostpital..He begins to freak out as he pulls up to the emergency door and slams on brakes..He quickly jumps out the car and yells.. 'Help, My wife is haveing a baby..Help..' Medics are outside helping his wife into a wheel chair in no time..They quickly wheel her back into the her room as the doctors said she has just started her contractions an it will be a long time still before the baby was born..The man stayed at the desk as he still had Forums to fill out. He quickly starting to fill them out as the woman asked for his insurance card..He pulled out his wallet and gave it to the woman behind the front desk as we can see the man's name is Gavin Frost..The woman goes through his files and hands him back his card as everything is ready..Before walking to the back Gavin goes outside to use his cell phone..Its about 3:00 am in the morning so he gets ready to wake up some one..A older womans voice answers.. 'Hello' The woman says as you can tell she was woken up by the phone..Gavin quickly begins to give her instructions... 'Mom, Quickly get down to the hostpital..She's haveing the baby..' The older woman then quickly replies.. 'Are you serious..Well I got to wake up young Scott and get him ready..We will be there as quickly as possible..bye..' Gavin hangs up the phone and quickly runs back into the hostpital..He rushes to the back as he first has to ask the woman in front the directions to his wifes room. He quickly goes through the back doors and into the elevators were he goes to the Third Floor..He quicky gets out as it opens and begins to walk down the hall..He has to pass the room of newly born babies as there are none in it tonight..But he mumbles under his breath.. 'Tonight must be the special night..for my boy..' Gavin finaly reaches the room as he sees his wife in the bed with a smile on her face..The medicane they gave her has just kicked in as she has stoped feeling pain..The doctor came in the door and whispered for Gavin to walk over.Gavin walks over to him as he is then told by the doctor.. 'The medicine want last to much longer as she doesnt have to much more time to go..She is half the way to full dialated..So be on your toes..' Gavin shakes his head yes as he then goes back and a waits at the end of the bed holding his wife's hand.. About a hour later Gavin's hand is being squeezed tight as the medicine has warned out..She hasnt dialated to much more but the pain is back..Gaving quickly gets the doctors as he waits a little outside the room..The vison of his wife in pain is something that bothers him a little.but he does realise the outcome of it..He stands outside with his hand on his head as he then walks though the doors and out to the waiting room..In the waiting room is his mom with a three year old kid named Scott Frost..Scott is running around the waiting room pretending to hide from Gavin..Gavin smiles as Scott has went and hide behind one of the candy machines..Gavin walks over to the machine and shakes it alittle as Scott then laughs and jumps out at Gavin..Gavin then pulls the kid up into the air and throws him..he then catches him and places him back onto his feet..Gavin then says.. 'Hey Scott..you ready to have you a cousin..' Scott then smiles and grabs around Gavin's neck like a sleeper hold as he says.. 'Yea Uncle G...You ready..' He then smiles as him and Scott play around for a bit..Scott is three years old and is close to his grandma and uncle..Gavin then gives Scott some money to get a snack and drink..Scott runs off over to the machines as Gavin goes to his mom..His mom smiles and wraps her arms around Gavin..She then says.. 'My baby is now haveing his own..I just cant wait..' Gavin stands infront of his mom as he is very emotional right now..He stands as he holds his moms hand.. 'Aaron Jackson Frost...I love you....' A tear comes down the side of Gavins face as his baby boy has just entered this world.... The Book Of Cold:Chapter Two A kids dreams Age 7 I had a dream. One Monday night when I was 7 years old I had a dream that I would never forget. That night everything was the usual o every Monday night. My cousin Frost came by to watch wrestling with my dad and I just like he always does. We watched it like always,said our goodbyes then off to bed. Like all nights I went to bed with the same thing running through my head. I would picture myself being like one of the wrestlers. I would imagine myself being the world champion as I stood in front of all the people holding the belt up high..These thoughts were usual the ones that put me to sleep as it did that night. But the dream wasnt your normal dream. In the dream I was older and wrestling one of my dads friends. He was a very well built man with dark black hair. It was like in my head I created my own wrestling world. Like as I looked around me it was my own ring. It was my arena..everything was dedicated to me. As I remember like it was yesterday standing in the ring about to fight Bill..My dad's friend..The look on his face was unforgettable. It was like he was trying to kill me with his eyes. Just piercing down into my heart..Wanting me to just fall out.. The dream gotten worse real quick..He charged at me and low blowed me..The whole time after he stood above me kicking me in my side..Then reaching down trying to choke the breath out of me..That's when I woke up..I was straight up in my bed trying to breath for my life..At seven this dream was something else..I was use to the super hero ones to were I would save the day..Or the occasional one with me just standing in a wrestling ring with a belt around my shoulder..then just wakeing up..I stayed awake the whole rest of the time. When I was in the bed the only thing that was going through my head was... 'What did it mean?' I quickly thought the worse. Maybe it was because I wasnt suppose to follow my dream, to be a professional wrestler. Right when that thought entered my young head I quickly began to worry. Everything since I could remember anything had something to do with wrestling. It had been my dreams..it had been my heart for so long.. When my parents got up I quickly went to the kitchen and had breakfest with them. At the table I told my dad everything..He told me just to shake it off that it was nothing but a bad dream.I looked over at my mom as she was doing her usual thing by trying to assure me that everything would be fine by saying.. 'You will still be a wrestler..' I then finished and went back in my room as I played video games for another hour or so. I then begin to listen to some music as the night before still hadent left my mind one minute..All of a sudden my phone rang..I didnt answer it at the time as I was just in my own world thinkign about things..My dad came in the room and told me to pick up the phone..it was Scott..I answered as Scott had called to see what was up as he had to tell me he couldnt come over today because his new bike had gotten a flat tire. I told him everything was cool as I quickly had to tell him about the dream I had..When I got through telling him about everything I had dream it was silent for a while on the phone as he was doing the same thing I had been doing..looking into the whole situation trying to find out what it ment..He then said.. 'I got it..' Scott then began to explain that maybe it was a test. To see if I could handle the real side of wrestling. Scott and I got a long all the time because of this.If it wasnt for him I would of never had chosen wrestling as a career as that one word he said stayed in my mind..'test'.. 'Thats it!' I told him as I was excited an almost droped the phone. He had figured it out. It was to see how I would react. To show my the other side of wrestling. To see how I would react if I wasnt on top. To show me the other side of the situation an see if I had what it took to keep going after my dream. I told him thanks as I then hung up the phone and continued about my day..As it was the normal day again..Doing nothing but playing basketball in the back yard..but it was that night that showed me more into the dream.. That night when it was time for me to go to bed I got in with the same thought on my head. Would I habe another dream to test me??Thats all I could think about. But since I woke up so early from the night before it didnt take me long to fall asleep. It was a continuation from the dream I had the night before.There I was in the ring getting choked by Bill. He had the look on his face as if he was not letting go. Then from no where I reveresed it. I got just through him off and began to take over the match myself. I then did a couple of moves and went for the pin...1,2,3.. As I then woke up..I looked over at the alarm clock as it read 8:00am. This was the usual time for me..I smiled as then looked up at the wall in my room..That very moment..I knew..I could feel it..as I mumbled to myself... 'Its not just a dream..its a reality..' The letter has arrived to the front office of the cell divison..A secratary for the cell opens it up and shows it to the productions crew as they look at it before the camera fades black..it says.. Forgotten Cell, The place has been named. The people of eVo are not waiting for a evolution. No, these pathetic maggots are wanting a Revoltuion. They have been doing what the cell has been doing, dieing. Wishing for a change. Wanting to be held like the true babys these fans are. I will hold them though. I will be the one who knocks that pathetic excuse of a cell champ off..When I am done, the people will know that I will be there for them. Because I have been where they have. This world has shit on me..this place will not though. I will take it over because of my anger and hatred towards everything. This place has never seen me, and will never seen another like me. Do not think this is the happys days my cousin Scott has brought the eVo fans..no, I bring the hate..I bring the true life..Do not fear me though..Do not try to forget me..no..embrace me..embrace the new religion! -signed The New Religion...Aaron Frost
