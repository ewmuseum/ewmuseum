You gotta have FAITH!

21 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct. 21,02 The scene opens up in Jay Stylez Junior's locker room, We see Jay Stylez Junior looking at the highlights of his match between Sgt. Jack O'Hara. Jay Stylez Junior picks up the volume to the television and begins to pay close attention to it. <::Television::> Stylez is quick to get back to his feet and this time grabs hold of the steel chair before making sure O’Hara is staying down longer and coming down across him with a senton splash. With the chair still in hand Stylez gets up and heads to the corner again. He climbs the ropes a second time before coming off the top and down across O’Hara with a chair assisted frog splash and going for the cover. One………two………..three! <::Television::> Jay Stylez Junior sits up and lowers the television so that he can speak on his match at the Pay-Per View and other stuff. Jay Stylez Junior: 'Wow what a match I had wensday verses Jack O'Hara. I have to say that I must of underestimated him. But once he Powerbomed me at Legends XII and almost cost me my wrestling carrer that woke me up and I relised I cant just come into eVolution and talk shit, I have to prove myslef. That means I have to bust my ass and beat some of the top wrestlers of eVolution just so I can get noticed around here and that I what I plan on doing no matter what I have to do.' Jay Stylez Junior begins to lay down but then hears a knock on the door. He screams out 'Come In' the door opens and in comes a beautiful Africian Americian Women. Jay Stylez Junior lays up with a look of amuzment on his face. She looks at Jay Stylez Junior and begins to smile at him, Jay Stylez Junior returns the smile. She has a paper in her hand with the eVolution logo on it. She begins to speak to Jay Stylez Junior. Joy: 'Hello, Jay Stylez Junior.' She says smileing. Jay Stylez Junior: 'Uhm Hello. Can I help you with anything?' He says with a smile. Joy: 'Well I am just here to let you now that you have a match at the 'Faith' Pay-Per View.' She say's while handing him the paper. Jay Stylez Junior: 'Uhm thank you.' Joy: 'You are welcome. Good luck to you in your match.' Jay Stylez Junior: 'Okay.' Jay Stylez Junior looks at the paper and there in bold letters is his match. <::Faith Card::> Non-Title ~ Four Corners Match Jay Stylez Junior vs. 'The Prodigal' Sean Mason vs. Gerico vs. 'The Outkast' Eric Knight Originally a six pack match but with the releases of Quasar and Andrew Martin this one has become a four corners match. Sean Mason feels robbed of not only the Rising Star Title but the High Impact Title as well as he tries and makes himself noticed in this match. Standing in his way is Jay Stylez Junior who pulled off a huge upset win over Sgt. Jack O’Hara. Gerico and Eric Knight have past issues themselves and are also along for the ride in this one as they try and pave their own path to glory. <::Faith Card::> Jay Stylez Junior begins to smile and speaks 1 last time. Jay Stylez Junior: 'What do we have here. Non-Title~Four Corners Match + Stylez Junior vs. 'The Prodigal' Sean Mason vs. Gerico vs. 'The Outkast' Eric Knight. This is my time to shyne and show everyone in eVolution that Jay Stylez Junior isnt a joke it all starts here.' Jay Stylez Junior threw the paper on the table and walks out the locker room. The camera zooms into the paper and begins to fade black and then goes to a commercial break. -Scene ends-
