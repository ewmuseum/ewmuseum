Loving The Gold

7 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-07 [The scene opens up inside a fancy hotel studio room where Aaron Justice is shown sitting in front of a monitor with a camera crew around him. He has the eVolved title draped over his shoulder.] It's a night that will go down in the history of eVolution. It's a night no body will ever forget. It's a night titled Digital Destruction. The eVolved title was put on the line as 'The Perfect Design' Aaron Justice was set to face none other than Matt Hunt and the current eVolved champion Scott Evans for the eVolved title. The battle went on all night and it was a back and forth style match. I remember Hunt coming at me hard and I remember Evans coming at me hard, but neither where going to stop me on my quest for gold. Not Evans and surely not Hunt. I was destined to become the next champion and everyone knew it. Them two must of thought other wise though. [The cameras move towards the monitor where different scenes from the eVolved title match are shown. The cameras then move back on to 'The Perfect Design'.] We went at it all night long and it eventually turned into a numbers game. My boys in The Select came down to make sure everything was going to happen fair and to insure that we would all see the ending we wanted to see. Hunt and Evans provoked the members of The Select all night long. Many people keep asking me why did I let The Select get involved. Well I didn't. The Select where their to cheer me on. The Select was their to show their support. When Evans provoked them, he was attacked. When Hunt provoked, he was attacked. My followers did not start this battle. They simply defended their selfs. Hunt and Evans put the attacks on them as an excuse for when they lost the match. It was simply strategy on their part. Nothing more, nothing less. Lets move on from that though, because Digital Destruction is over and 'The Perfect Design' brought more gold into the family of The Select. See, The Select is all about gold. Their isn't a group that could hold more than us. One thing that really burns me up though is I beat Scott Evans for the eVolved title and then all the sudden he jumps up in rank because of it. I guess I made him famous or something. Evans, I'll worry about you though later boy. Right now I still got The Postman on my mind. Hunt your the man on my mind. Your the guy I want next. We are not done. Yea, I got what I was searching for, but I could of had it a while ago, but you cost me my first shot at it. I got a proposition for you though. Win your all your matches up to the next pay per view and you'll get a shot at me and my eVolved title. What do you think about that? [Aaron stands up off his chair and grabs a hold of the eVolved title. He puts the title across his arms and shows it to the cameras. Aaron then tosses the title back up on his shoulder.] Hunt, I am giving you the shot. You have to make it happen though. I am sure you can win your matches, but you'll never beat me. For once you will be dealt your Final Justice. [Scene fades to black.]
