Go big or go home...or something

20 August 2003

Date: 2003-08-20 Dissociative Identity Disorder, often called multiple personality disorder, mental illness in which a person has two or more distinct identities or personality states, which recurrently take control of the person’s consciousness and behavior. The person often gives the alternate identities their own personal names, and these identities may have characteristics that differ sharply from the person’s primary identity. In addition, a person with this disorder experiences some degree of amnesia, in that one personality usually will not recall what occurred when another personality controlled the person. People often act and feel differently in various settings. For example, teenagers may act differently at a party than they do at school. However, people in good mental health maintain continuous awareness of themselves no matter what the situation. Individuals with dissociative identity disorder do not. They experience sudden shifts in consciousness, identity, and memory. They may find themselves in a strange apartment and not remember how they got there, or discover new clothing in their closet without knowing how it was purchased. Their identity is fragmented into pieces with different emotions, memories, and styles of interacting with people. They may shift from being passive and accepting of advice from others to being hostile and uncooperative. They are often at war with themselves, with certain personalities being quite critical of other personalities. At times one personality may go so far as inflicting physical harm on one of the other personalities. In one case, a woman with dissociative identity disorder carved the words “I hate Joan” on her forearm while in a different personality state. In 1994 the American Psychiatric Association (APA) changed the name of the disorder from multiple personality disorder to dissociative identity disorder. Psychiatrists wanted to emphasize the fact that the disorder does not really consist of many personalities living in one body, but rather of a failure to integrate various aspects of identity into a unified personality. In a sense, people with this disorder suffer not from having more than one personality but rather from having less than one personality. Shocka had a problem…there was no doubt about that but how deep did it run. It seems that Shocka is more intense than he ever has been. But is that a symptom of the disease? Vito Voss also plays in…he and Shocka have a somewhat, unique past. People have constantly questioned the relationship as Shocka proved to be critical of Voss to people before Voss even came to eVo. Shocka had something in his mind that was tearing him apart…stripping him down with every single step. But it drove him to do that much better…you see, what’s going on inside of his mind is like a competition. The man we know as Shocka is in a struggle for control of a body with Jonathan McCade…the true owner of the body. Or is it the other way around? Things like this seem to get twisted very easily. Prescribed medicine is what controls Shocka, but over in England its harder to come by. Voss was supposed to pick up a bottle of Shocka’s medicine and bring it along…he failed to do so. This furthers the amount of evidence against Voss and it proves that there indeed may be a struggle of consciousness…for lack of better terms. What would Voss have to gain with destroying Shocka you ask? Well he could cash in a life insurance policy that he is a beneficiary on since both of Shocka’s parents have been long dead. Or maybe it’s something deeper than that, perhaps an old rivalry the two have gone to far. Maybe Voss is just a sick fuck who loves to play games with people’s heads, something he did very well. ‘Dude…’ Voss’s voice erupted over Shocka’s ears awakening him. Voss sounded very convincing. ‘They are after you.’ Voss stated. ‘What, who?’ Shocka asked sitting up rubbing his still adjusting eyes. ‘F.B.I man.’ Voss had a grim look on his face. ‘I don’t get it..’ Shocka’s head was still spinning from last night…a long night of drinking Shocka arrived to his hotel room at 6 AM with a woman…since then he has been awaken by phone call after phone call…and now Voss. ‘I was just starting to get back to sleep man…just go.’ Shocka waved him off. ‘You can’t sleep…’ Voss shook his head. ‘Hold on I got you something.’ Voss walked from the room and Shocka stood to go into the bathroom. He slowly staggered in and grabbed a bottle of Tylenol he took his fair share with a glass of water and leaned against the sink Shocka turned now facing the mirror he noticed that his face was very pale and his eyes had huge bags under them. He looked blankly into the mirror. ‘Legends is going to be hell…’ Shocka blurted out and then continued speaking. ‘I finally get Hedges in the ring ready to end him…and I haven’t slept in 24 hours…’ As Shocka continues speaking he reaches down wards and slowly turns the faucet on. Water begins to flow from it and he reaches now with both hands and pulls the water to his face splashing it on. In the mirror you can see three simple letters 'XES' written in a color familiar to blood. Shocka looks up seeing the lettering and rips his head backwards as if he is expecting something different. On the wall behind him is the word 'Sex' and in the mirror it is projected backwards. He turns his head slowly back to the mirror and speaks. For all of you Roman Numeral buffs its 666 ‘I don’t know what the hell is going on…one minute I’m somewhere and then next thing I know I’m in a bar with Voss. Or training or even just sitting there almost in a trance…’ Shocka spoke to himself…that was the only way he could truly think, aloud. He whimpers under his breath as his voice grows with intensity he lifts his head quickly staring into the mirror. 'I'm not afraid of fate...' Shocka’s eyes fell to the back of his head after muttering the words…he was now gone. Who replaced him is not known, the eyes return to normal but this time a snarl is present on Shocka’s face. The words echo off of the white washed walls as Shocka now moves back from the mirror and looks all around him. The once nicely polished white steel on the stall doors begins to mold into nothing but rust. The walls that were once covered with white paint now have splatters of blood all over them. Shocka slowly backs to a urinal he watches the change as his focus on the water that is now overflowing from the sink. Before the water hits the ground it’s transformed to blood. His head begins to jerk side-to-side studying everything around him. He stares into the mirror and sees the image that he always wanted, he sees the man that he always wanted to be. The perfect image of himself as projected by the American Society. There is a flash and you can see Shocka standing back by the urinal and everything is back to normal. He stands shaking in the corner as a man comes into the men's room. He sees Shocka and begins to leave but Shocka speaks angrily as the scene again flashes. The doors of the stalls are again rusted blood is splattered all over the walls and mirrors flames are to the right and Shocka runs at the man. He stops in front of him and studies him. The man looks over him before stepping back as Shocka stands in front of him like he is being judged. The man reaches into his jacket and draws a knife from its sheath pulling it up quickly. He begins going toward Shocka and raising the knife Shocka is stopped up against a wall and the man pushes the knife towards Shocka's eye ball you can see the reflection of the knife in the blue eyes of Shocka. 'Shocka, you are not real...I am the one that contains the body, you are just a figment of my imagination.' The man, or whatever we now know as Jonathan smiles sinisterly and begins in with a sadistic laugh the man speaks still laughing. 'You don't think that I am real? You don't think that I will take over control of the body? Do you not think that I can or are you just denying it to yourself? It will happen again, fate comes faster and faster for you.' Again the flash and we now see Shocka cowering on the floor he is in the fetal position under a sink. Voss barges in and lifts him up knowing he just had an episode. This had become a regular occurance…Vito knew it wasn’t a good sign but being the good friend he is…he simply laughed it off. Vito dropped a bullet proof vest on the floor next to Shocka as the scene faded.
