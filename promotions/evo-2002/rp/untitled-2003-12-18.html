Untitled

18 December 2003

Date: 2003-12-18 He found himself in a dark hallway with a bright light before him, it seemed like only a few feet to walk but as he walked it seemed to move further and further away. He began to run to try to catch it, he ran hard and fast, his leg muscles began to ache which was a surprise that he hadn't realized yet. He began to sweat from exhaustion and finally he fell, he heard a voice that he hadn't heard in so long, out of the light it came strong and hard, it was Deanna's voice, 'Scott it's not your time yet.' Then the dark hallway fell away and he found himself laying on the floor in his apartment. The numbness that had overtaken him was gone he could feel again, pain swept over him as it never had before and he found himself overjoyous. It was as if he'd came back from the dead to live once again and fight for a forgotten cause, to avenge those who had wronged him in life, but he'd never really been dead, he just lost all feeling like his father had. He found himself overenergized for this late in the night, but he was fully himself again that numbness that had begun to take over his life was dead and all feelings he'd longed for had come back. But there was something that lingered in the back of his mind, a memory trying to resurface, but it didn't matter he needed sleep more than ever, but then he realized what had happened? Why had he fell into nothingness what was it that had caused him to fall away like that and it only took a few moments to realize what. There was the sound of voices down the hall in his bedroom. He got up fists clenched and ran to the door, there standing in his bedroom where the two men that had knocked him out both looked familiar for some reason, but it didn't matter he had to get them out of here because this was his house, his home and nobody would invade it unpunished. He ran at both of them and unleashed his fury in two violent swings. He heard the clatter of teeth as they both fell to ground. He stood before them and began to realize that there was something bigger at work here, there was always someone higher up behind everything and now was the time to find the perpertrator here and nothing would stop him until this was over. Then he realized this was a familiar scenario but there were no guns involved. He picked both of the men up and dragged him out of his apartment, whoever had sent them would pay, but not now, he would do it later. This scheme didn't matter right now he had to be ready for anything, anything that could come and slowly he found the numbness returning maybe it was his destiny not to feel or maybe it was his mindset that did this. He didn't know and didn't care, there was something he'd forgotten that he had to try to remember something that had happened long ago, but he couldn't concentrate, his mind was clouded and he couldn't focus. Death had been something that never scared him but now he felt that unknown fear creeping up on him he felt himself once again hidden. Once again insane and he wondered if anyone was sane until he fell asleep.
