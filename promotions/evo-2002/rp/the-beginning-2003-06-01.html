The Beginning

1 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-01 It had been Jake Evans's first day in LA when he abducted the woman who would become the mother of his children. Jake had mental problems which he did take pills for, but there had been no good medication out there to suppress his schizophrenia and when he switched into his second persona he had the tendency to do cruel things to women. He was never caught doing those things, but this one time something would happen different. Jake had been dressed in a suit and tie for his meeting with his new boss to make sure that he made a good first impression, but as fate would have it he switched over to his second persona. He saw a woman walking down the street dressed in a blue dress seemingly not stopping her name was Helen Peterson. He pulled up besides her in a 1974 Pontiac Firebird and said smugly, 'What's a pretty lady like you walking here?' Helen had thought that he would just give her a ride, but when he just kept on driving straight she knew that something was going to happen to her. 3 hours later they were in a deserted area in the desert and Jake stopped the car and said in the same tone and same voice, 'Do you want to get out of this alive?' His thoughts were cut short when he switched back into the normal Jake Evans; a new expression came over his face, which was of disbelief. Helen was by no means going to allow anything happen to her but she had the same fear of being raped and molested, but when she saw a kinder expression upon her abductors face she realized that he had some sort of problem mentally. She feared that he would become the same angry person he had been just moments before so she did what she thought he wanted without any thoughts of remorse. 12 hours later Jake would awake in the back seat of the car with his feet duck taped together and his hands duck taped behind his back. From what he could see he was in front of the police station and from what he could immediately realize he had been tricked into this position. But when no officer came out with handcuffs and just Helen returned he thought something was up. Helen drove to Goldfield, Nevada where she got a hotel room, once she had done that she took the duck tape of off Jake and brought him inside, questions had been whirling through his head for hours to ask her and when he realized how pretty she was they all turned into mush in his mouth. She led Jake to the room that she had gotten and then Jake could finally form words again. 'Why didn't you bring me into the police station?' Jake had asked in a croaky voice. 'I have been looking for a way to get out of my parent's business and house for years and this was the opportunity.' 'Why are you taking me here?' 'Well I am not going to allow the person that probably is going to a father to my children to run off without helping me take care of them.' 'Wait what'd you just say?' 'We had no protection in the car, so most likely I'm pregnant.' Helen said in a flat voice. Jake couldn't believe it, hours ago he was going to sign a contract with a wrestling federation that held its shows within the confines of Los Angeles. Now he was going to probably be a father and his life wouldn't be going the way he had planned. With that simple sentence their lives had begun together and when their children were born they would find themselves within Jake's parent's house in Chicago, Illinois. Little did either of the parents know their fates, Jake would commit suicide on Scott and Adam's ninth birthday, and Helen would die in a car accident 1 month after their children's birth. From the start it seemed that this family was doomed to destruction before it even began
