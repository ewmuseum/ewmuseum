Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-12-03 'The scene stars off with Lee eating some jello filled puddin? How the hell do you eat jello filled puddin?! That would like make me throw up thousands of times! You sick bastard! Jello filled puddin is the devil!' Lee eXodus: Momma, this little birdy is talking to me telling me how cool I am, it is da shiz niz! Momma: Don't you swear in my house Leeward! Lee eXdous: Momma, don't call me Leeward! I am Lee eXodus! The Surreal Thilla! 'stomps up and down' I am Lee! LEE! NOT LEEWARD! Momma: I only call you Leeward, because god told me to name you Leeward. Lee eXodus: BAH! BAH I SAY TO THAT! 'shakes his fist at anger towards the sky'. Momma: Eat your jello filled puddin sweatheart! Before it gets cold. Lee eXodus: How can puddin get cold if it is already cold? 'Lee's mother smacks Lee with a wooden spoon, as Lee grabs his cheek because that seemed to hurt the poor lad. Jello filled puddin?! How can you even eat jello fulled puddin?!' Momma: you making fun of my puddin sir?! 'What? You can hear me?! AHHH! GET AWAY FROM ME LADY! AHHHH LEE HELP! AHHH! NOT THE BROOM! NOT THE BROOOM! AHHHHHHH!' Lee eXdous: Momma always said lifes like a cup of jello filled puddin, you never know what you gonna get. Momma: I never said that Leeward. Lee eXodus: Oh I know, but I saw Forrest Gump a few nights ago, it was da shiz niz sucka! Momma: Don't swear in my house Leeward! 'smacks Lee in the face with the broom'. Lee eXodus: OUCH! I swear my homies gonna beat you women! Momma: If your homies are coming over go clean your room, cus I don't want your homies to see your dirty room, with those dirty undy stains. Lee eXodus: MOMMA! DON'T TELL THEM ABOUT MY DIRTY UNDY STAINS! Momma: Learn to wipe better Leeward. Lee eXodus: ERRRRRRRR! YOU DAMN BIZZZZATCH! 'Lee's mother starts to beat Leeward with her cooking supplies like, a few pots over the head, then a spoon...oh wow she has that new Boxing guy grill....wow I never seen a green over a guys head before....whoa look at that she has a whole set of china to...ouch that haste to hurt...Lee I'd get out of here if I was you....oh shit! LADY LEAVE ME ALONE! AHHHHH THE BROOM! AHHHH! NOT AGAIN! Lee eXodus: I'm gonna beat Seara up just like I did 'half conscious' Whoa look at the pretty lap dancers in the sky...I wish my homie slices could see this. Whoa why is 'Louie Louie' by Kingsmen play? I hate that song...turn it off! AHHH! STOP SAYING LOUIE LOUIE! AHHHH! THE HORROR! Anyways...wooooo 'still barely conscious' I gonna beat Seara for her belt, so I can be just as cool as Scott Frost...whoa is it Scott Frost or Seara that is my hero? I forgot....maybe it was ASIS? Or...no I think it was Earl the popcorn guy or something. Oh well, I'm gonna beat you Seara with my hip hoppin disco skillz! This would be a awesome spot for a theme song. HEY! I'll make my own! 'Plays air guitar while laying in a pool of blood' Lee exodus: I'm Lee to the iso....Lee to the hiso...eXo...to the ciso....dus to the bizo! I can rap like a monkey with wings, and sleep like a genie! No eVo don't got nuttin on me, cus I am da shiz niz! HAHAHAH That sounds like swedish cheese or something don't it? Hey Al Gore I'm talking to you over there, hey what you doing in my house? Hey yall, Al Gore is in my house! HOOOOTY WOOOOOO! Shiz Niz playa! 'At this point Lee rambles on and on about this lamp saying it is Al Gore, but he soon passes out and we thought it was safe to leave, but we found out that Lee's mother is very, very scary...so we ran....we ran for our lives! We out of here! AHHH SHE'S COMING! AHHHHHHH! DAN S. SAVE US!' (end)
