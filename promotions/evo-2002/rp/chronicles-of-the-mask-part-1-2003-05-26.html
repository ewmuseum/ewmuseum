Chronicles of the mask: Part 1

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-26 Inside of a dark room, plagued by the disolent silence of everything. A swinging ceiling light with a dim glow, tries it's hardest to illuminate the room. All of a sudden the sqweaking sound a door creeks open, and the dim sound of foot steps is heard following the creeking of the door. The door is heard quickly slamming hard, and the sound of foot steps becomes more apparent and clear. Suddenly a lightly viewed shadow is seen walking up towards light, and then a man walks over to it and puts his hand on the light stopping it from swinging. Quickly you realize that the man holding the light in place is none other than the wrestler that eVolution has been pushing to debut since the pay per view in April, End of Days. The man wearing his steel blue coloured mask, and devil horns similiar to that of the dare-devil mask smiles and you can catch a good glimps of his eyes which are shrouded with red contacts. His magnificant build looks to be familiar some how, but as always with numerous occassions, it could just be the light from the ceiling light. The man, we all come to realize as being known as Greatness opens his mouth as he begins to speak. 'Greatness, is only tolerated by those who imagine themselves dawning the tag. When I first became a wrestler in the independent circuit, and I first stepped into that ring. I knew from then on, why I was in that ring. Why the pivital moment of gazing into the fans eyes would set me apart from everyone else. I was born into greatness, I was taught with a rough childhood and adolescent years. I was quickly developed into a man of many words, and a physic that could impact a body like a train.' the man sharply says. 'I seen people like: James Kimson, Shocka, Jason Jameson, and even the late and not too great former two time universal champion Scott Frost. I've seen them all quickly get their time to rise to the podieum and announce their reign as some kind of wrestling phenom. But let me set the record straight, people like: Morpheus Omega, Thug roC, Daniel Somers, Asis, and the new Universal champion Xander. They are the people who are the phenoms, they are the people that look past the wants and needs of the people and they are the people that get the job done!' the man again says with a sharp tone. The man known as Greatness shakes his head slowly as if he was in a state of disbelief. 'But yet, you people wanna look at them as the bad people. You wanna look at them as the people that hate this sports industry. You people shun people like me away from existance, simply because you wanna see the people with charsima worship the ground that you fans walk on. I had to find a place of solitude simply to talk to you REAL wrestling fans. I had to find the most disolent place on the face of the earth, and turn it into a form of sanctuary for myself. A safe haven from the world that is plagued with people looking for a struggle between good and evil. But let me ask you people something, what is the actual defination to that saying, 'good and evil'? Who gave you the right to choose which is which. How do you know that the people you trusted, won't turn on you? I mean, look at Unlimited. They wanted wrestling equality, and then when the stable equaled up to measures they took eVolution over with a fierce force. Now, Tyler Davidson. eVolution's new owner, he has set the stone and made people like myself and Xander into yet another minority for the sports entertainment era. You people make me sick, you people are the ones who are evil, and myself. Well, I am Greatness. I am the best. I am the man of the hour. Unlike Scott Frost, I truely am that cold chill that runs down your spine. Because when you measure up to the mark of Greatness. You measure up to a phenom!' the man says walking away. The scene slowly fades away as the ceiling light begins to slowly rock back and forth.
