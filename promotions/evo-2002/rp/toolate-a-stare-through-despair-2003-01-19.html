TooLate - A Stare Through Despair

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/19/03 Her name was Michelle. Jason knew her. He had known her for awhile now, but hadn’t seen her for years. Michelle was one of Marilee’s best friends, and they had been with Jason many times before. Jason had always known the slight jealousy that Michelle had for Marilee all those years, but never took advantage of it before. Never even thought about it. He loved her too much to ever do that. Jason had loved Marilee, and she knew that, but that didn’t stop her with William. “Oh my God!” she yelled, throwing her arms around Jason. Reluctant at first, Jason went to push her away, but decided against it at the last minute. “How long has it been, Jason?” “Too long, Michelle.” He answered, the despair still on his face, resting on her shoulder. “How are you, Jason?” “I’m…” Really fucking terrible, thanks for asking. “… good. I’m good. How ‘bout you?” “Great. I’m fine.” Her squeeze loosened, as she fell back down from her toes and looked up at Jason. I forgot how attractive you were. “You look great, Jason.” Jason could only nod and smile. “What brings you back here?” “To tell you the truth, Michelle, I don’t really know. I came back here…” “You came back for Mari, didn’t you?” A slight expression of disappointment came over her, as her eyes begged for an answer. Jason looked into them, feeling a sense of pity build in his heart. He could tell her the truth, if he himself knew it, or he could lie to her, and make himself feel slightly better. “I don’t know.” Jason could slap himself, but he just didn’t have the strength to care. “Well then why did you come back?” “I guess I thought that I owned it to myself to come back. Be it doesn’t matter. I just found out that Marilee has someone else.” “Really?” she said, the enjoyment of the opportunity returning to her. “I’m sorry, Jason.” Jason kicked some snow, and threw his hands into his coat pockets. He stood there, forgetting everything around him and only thinking of her. How could I have lost her? What did I do to disserve all of this shit? Am I too late? “Is there anything I can do?” Jason looked up at hear, still wearing the face of sorrow, and giving Michelle a little scare. She frowned, and seemed to begin to turn so she could walk away, but Jason responded. “Sure.” She turned around, and smiled. And as he stared into her eyes, Jason’s despair was all but gone. Jason took her arm between his, and the two walked away, the cold that much warmer now. And all that time, Jason’s mind ran around one question. What the hell are you doing?
