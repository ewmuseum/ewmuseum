Redemption and Revenge

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb 16th 3 men 1 Ring History has told us that Triple Threat Matches are unpredictable, and chaotic. Now, lets look deeper into our situation. This match isn’t your average Triple Threat, it’s also No Disqualifications. That means anything is legal. A Chair is just as good as a headlock. Choking someone with a TV cable is just as fair play as a clothesline. Then, with that taken into account, look at the 3 participants. Jason Jameson, the man out to prove his worth and burry his sorted past. Nothing seems to have ever gone right for Jameson, and while last month he did what was thought to be the impossible in defeating Sovereign via pinfal it wasn’t enough for him. It was the method of how it went down that drove Jameson to want a second crack at Sovereign. Not only at Sovereign now, but at James Kimson. Hanging in the ballance is his bitter past, always looking at it over his shoulder, now a chance to leave it behind him with a victory at Tainted Love James Kimson, the returning ICON of Professional wrestling. 8 times he has been called World Champion. He’s stepped into the squared circle with the greatest wrestlers of our time, and he’s beat them all. Now he returns, with nothing to prove other then that he says he’s still one of the best, and that he has a personal, a very personal score to settle with Sovereign. The relationship between Sovereign and Kimson’s holder Sister Leya is what drives Kimson. When he returned to television at New Year’s Evolution it was Kimson that cost Sovereign the match against Jameson. Kimson’s interjected himself upon Sovereign and now Jameson a few times, the rivalry blossomed, it bloomed and now at Tainted Love it will explode. Then there’s Sovereign. One of the most hated men in Evolution. One of the most feared men in Evolution. 3 and a half months he dominated as Universal Champion, and it was Jason Jameson that robbed him of his chance to regain it. It was Kimson that robbed him a second time to regain that title. Sovereign’s hatred for both men runs very deep. With Jameson wanting to make a name for himself off of Sovereign, and Sovereign feeling that he hasn’t gotten revenge upon him yet. Mix that with Kimson’s demands about Sovereign’s wife you can only imagine the deep and dense feelings that Sovereign holds for both me. He wants to hurt them, and although I am sure that he wouldn’t have wanted to fight both of these two huge superstars at once, the no DQ is probably the icing on the cake. Sovereign loves no DQ. He thrives on violence. Now, don’t get me wrong. I know that Jameson and Kimson are both experts in the art of violent wrestling. Hell if you know anything about Kimson its that he’s been in every sort of match that there has been in (maybe an exaggeration but you get my point) and we’ve seen over and over Jameson’s will to do what it takes. But lets be honest folks. We’ve seen what Sovereign is capable of doing when the rule book is thrown out the window. It’s never a pretty sight. Look at what he did to Jamal Kage, L2J, Impact just to scratch the surface of what he’s done. Now, throw him into the ring with his back to the wall. Some people draw an annalogy that Sovereign is an animal in the ring. What happens to an animal when he’s caged? I think you understand the point that I’m trying to make. So that if anyone thinks for one second that Sovereign is at a disadvantage at Tainted Love, you should recheck your thinking. Because he hasn’t ever been at a disadvantage. And if the past is any proof, he’s always got something up his sleeve. But do Kimson or Jameson have the foresight to see it before it goes down? I think another thing you should look at is, how can Kimson and Jameson co-exist? They won’t. This is truly every man for himself. Every man for himself, finally. And so you sit there and you judge me? Sovereign’s voice fades in over the voice over we commonly can associate with Sovereign’s interviews, promo’s or rantings. After those words said, Sovereign’s voice drifts into a chuckle, a cold meaningless laugh that chills your soul. The hairs on the back of your neck stick up, you get an odd taste in the back of your mouth. You judge me over what? Because I love your sister. The only person that ever gave that to her. And you Judge me because of why? Things in the past? Ghosts in the night that have been forgiven by her long ago. But you come from the shadows and you sneak up behind me with the intention of ruining everything that I’ve worked my entire life to get, all because of something that never even happened to you. It happened to someone else. And if you had been any sort of a brother then you should have been there for her. Been at her wedding, been the one to come crying to. But now James Kimson, you were off making millions, raising titles above your head playing for the throngs of wrestling fans at the height of the industry laughing at those who thought that they had it good because you just seemed to have it better. So now you come here and judge me, how pathetic is that. Has the romance of your career finally died out and you are just looking for perhaps that one last program to sweep you back to the love of the population? I wouldn’t know, and yes I do know how popular you were. I remember you on A&E’s biography. I remember you doing commercials for Nike, and I can sure as hell remember then ad campain that you did for General Motors. Yeah Kimson, I watched television when I was allowed to and I saw you on it more often then not. That urked me, knowing that my Brother in Law had it good all the while Sovereign lay locked up in a nut house because of what? Being a Human being, and not lying to himself about who and what he is? You are the lie Kimson and you sure as hell aren’t fooling me, and you aren’t even fooling Jameson. I’ve got 10 times more respect for Jameson then you Kimson. And I’d rather bite his face off then speak to him. But still, judgement is your name James Kimson. Judgement over things that might not have been under my control. People like to make light of my situation. They pass me off as crazy, or sick. They don’t know what that means. And while I never wanted sympathy over the things that I’ve done in the past, I certainly regret some things. What happened to Leya happened. And there is nothing that I can do to change what happened. There’s no use to apologize because that is what I was at the time. Why say that I’m sorry for things that I can’t be honest about? I was a monster. I was sick and to be honest I am still a sick freak. I don’t even really regret the loss of the child, because in the end he would have probably ended up just like myself and there’s no room in this world for two or me. The pain I caused my wife is what I wish didn’t happen. So you probably don’t believe that and that’s up to you Kimson. I don’t care what you think, or what your purpose is here in eVolution. But you stepped onto a landmine called Sovereign and I will blow your freaking head off at Tainted Love. I’ve taken out men that are bigger, stronger, faster, better wrestlers then you. Maybe they weren’t the total package as you have proven to be in the past. No, none of them were Eight times a world champion. I once never respected that of anyone, but after holding the Universal Championship for so long I know how difficult it is to win back titles. So perhaps Kimson I can respect that you are tenacious and that you are resiliant. That being said I can’t wait to tear you limb from limb inside that ring. I can’t wait to feel your blood running down my forearms. You are the next victim of my crusade, and while I am not neglecting Jameson, you Kimson are what I want. You are who I am after. You and I have a deep hatred. Me and Jameson, is strictly a professional issue. He beat me, because of you. And for that I will need to beat him to regain my status as the next in line for the winner of Frost and Shocka on the same night we lock horns. Now the scene comes into view. Sitting in the corner of a dark room as ussual is Sovereign. Sitting there, hands on his lap as his legs are straight out on the floor, his hair in his face, his posture poor and his voice crackling at times. Sovereign lifts up his head for a moment then lowers it to speak At Digital Destruction I fought in a 4 way no DQ match and I won, destroying 3 other men, sending all of them to the hospital afterwards. This match, this time isn’t about the Universal Title. It’s about redemption and revenge. Redemption from Jason Jameson’s fluke victory and Revenge upon James Kimson for his involvement. For his costing me the Universal Title. For him even being in Evolution. He doesn’t know how Leya as stayed up at night’s crying because her Brother can’t accept her lover. I sat there looking at her with a look of pity. Yes, pity because she now knows what it’s like to have lived my life. The people who are supposed to support you turn their backs on you and even work and do whatever it takes to remove you from any sort of emotional release. I was locked, beaten and drugged. She, physically is fine. Emotionally she’s a mess. All the things Kimson said about their relationship, their love for each other is true. They were the closest of friends until now he returns to rape her of the thing she loves the most. Oddly enough me. And so I finally get a new love. Robbed of the Universal Title, my only love before hand and now someone is trying to take from me a love that can love me back. That isn’t like a whore being passed around the backstage like the title is. And although that title will be mine once again down the road, right now all my focus is on Jameson and Kimson. To get what I want from them, not what I deserve. I deserve to be Champion. I want to destroy those two bastards inside that ring. I want their blood. I want their careers served to me on a platter with all the fixings. I want that classic image of a pig tied up with an apple in his mouth brought before me so I can carve and mold it as I desire. I can just see Jason Jameson with an apple in his mouth, eyes shut, legs and arms tied behind his back. I can smell and taste his blood. I can see James Kimson on a table, his blood flowing down his face. I can see the pain, feel their anquish. This month’s Pay Per View will not let me down this time. Last time, Kimson robbed me. Jameson Robbed me. They will regret it. And I will get what I want. Sovereign chuckles to himself as the camera fades to black
