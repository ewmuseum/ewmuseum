Book of Cold:Chapter Three

27 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-27 The Book of Cold:Chapter 3: When the Dream flatlined and was reborn Age:16 I remember when I was sixteen. I stood in my bedroom one afternoon. As I was getting ready for a match. I was in one of the many backyard wrestling programs in America. My friends and I all had the same dream. We all wanted to be wrestlers. We all wanted to reach the big stage. We called out establishment 'Backyard Wrestling Inc.'..BWI. When we first started BWI it was mainly just for our pleasure. But after about two months we started to actually start having a fan base. When that started most of our fans all got big heads. Just with those few people they already lost the concept of wanting to be a wrestler. As if they had already made it. The day in my room...I stood in my blue jeans and plain whit t-shirt, knowing that tonight was my time to leave it all..My time to have the last impression to the higher authority. So I told my friends it was my final fight. All of them were saddened but they really didnt care. All they ran through their heads was the fact that more attention would go to them. But I just couldnt think straight.. The whole day I had this feeling. Like what if me leaving was a sign that I shouldn't be in wrestling. That maybe I should of fallen in the trap of local success so that I would want to stay. Then a thought would cross my mind that maybe me doing this ment that my time was comming. Maybe it was a sign to show me that I really wanted to be a wrestler. A life dream fading away is one thing no man should ever have to go through. When your dreams are taken..your heart is gone..you are no longer who you are..Its the worst feeling in the world..That day I had decided that my dream..had died.. With two hours left to the show I still stood in my room. I didnt talk to any of my family or nothing. It was as if I locked myself in a small prison so that no one could get to me. No one could try to tell me that this wasnt the end. Because I had made it final, this was the last time Aaron Frost would ever be in a wrestling ring. I finaly took a seat on my bed. As I reached over to my wrestling boots and placed them by feet. I couldnt even look up. I just glared at the floor. I looked at the black boots with the white lettering of 'Frost' written on them. A tear came down my check..I mumbled to myself.. 'Why..why is it over?' The guessing has came back to play. It's as if I couldn't bare the thought of leaving a ring. But I knew BWI wasnt my place. And being so young it is hard to pick up a wrestlilng job at a major company. But why though, why not just stay with BWI..I must of let the big heads of my friends turn me off of the sport that I have always loved..that I thought I would never leave..until that day.. Finally..It was time to go over two blocks where we had built the ring. I saw the whole road was packed with cars. I couldnt believe it. The people had spread the word that it was my last time and it was so warming to see the welcome I got...but I still told myself.. 'Tonight..I leave the only home I ever had..tonight..' As I balled my fist tight and said what I had said a million times that day.. 'It's over' I walked over by the ring as I watched all my friends matches. Then I looked as it was down to the last match..Frost vs Trey. Trey was a friend of mine since middle school. I looked over as they had bought a ladder from the ACE hardware store and made it a no DQ match. When I entered the ring the people chanted and went crazey..but I just kept saying.. 'It's over'.. The match got started as me and Trey went at it. We hit one another with the ladder about four times. We through each other out the ring about 7 times. Finally I did something I thought I would never do. I grabbed Trey and hit my version of a falling powerbomb. Back then that was my perfected move. The people chanted as they were waiting for me to make the cover..I shook my head as I looed over at the ladder..I then set the ladder up in the middle of the ring..Well I climbed to the top and when I went to jump Trey hit the ladder and sent me flying into the air..I landed hard on thering. As I then blacked out.... Trey's View when he blacked out: Frost laid in the ring motionless. The medics got there as soon as possible..They felt his pulse..nothing...They saw that he flatlined. They got the charges and pumped them into him. As they wouldnt give up..then the wierdest shit happened..Every one had thought he was dead..then he woke up..As the lights we had on the tree's were in his eyes as his blue eyes looked glazed with the bright lights on them.. End of his view.. I then woke up..looking at all the people around me. And the crazey stunt I hard tried to attempt that went horribly wrong. I then looked over as it was like I was giving a second chance..Like I was born again..in that ring..as I mumbles.. 'This is..home'..
