Preparing to return to greatness

2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Toronto, Canada Syndicate International Floor 67. Deep inside the cavernous Syndicate Training facility, we find Big Daddy Dreamer busily working out as his manager Veronica Caine (already paying dividends) is overseeing his progress. BDD bench-pressing 450 pounds with relative ease notices the lovely and talented HeartBreak Chick entering from the side door and conversing with Miss Caine. “Looks good doesn’t he?” “Indeed he does, I must say he is by far the best pure athlete I have ever seen in my life. No offense Miss HeartBreaker.” “None taken, I agree with that sentiment, Big Daddy can be as dangerous, as ruthless as strong as any competitor in the industry. He has all the tools, but then again, I’m biased aren’t I, being with him for seven years now. I know what he can do. I’ve witnessed it and felt it first hand, its just getting him back to the old days, back when he enforced the industry with an iron hand. Back when people feared facing him because it meant one of two things……” “What was that?” “A battered and broken body or a damn broken neck. I swear, I’ve lost count of the amount of necks he’s broken over the years with that crossface hold of his.” “I know he’s retired some of the games all time greats.” “That is true, I know the intensity that beats inside of him. Deep down I know he wants another shot at Asis and that it’s eating at him, watching him hold the Universal Championship. While I know he is sincere about me winning the title, I know he wants revenge on the man who he believes caused him to fall to mid-card status. The man who put him through a fucking cell wall. Just as I know he has unfinished business with Malcom King and Xander. Seems like an EDW reunion of sorts for these guys since they were all the major players.” “Well, he does get his shot at Xander soon. Its already been booked, main-event at Legends.” “I must say you work quickly Veronica, nice work against Davidson by the way.” “Thanks, from you I hold that in high regard, intrusting me with him.” “Well you had the right credentials and drive, he needed a spark and I think you gave that to him. I haven’t seen him this determined in quite some time. Does he know about Xander yet?” “One way to find out……… yo D……. you might want to take it up a notch, your next opponent is no push-over…..” “Yeah……. Who might that be?” “Xander” “Yessssssss! Its about time I finish him off once and for all. I’ve been dying to snap that man’s neck. I nearly had him twice in EDW but fortunately, his little stack of dimes managed to survive.” “Yeah…… I know your undefeated against Xander, and been on a major roll lately except for O’Halleron..” BDD tosses the weights to the floor at the mention of Damon’s name. Veronica steps in front of D twirling his hair with her finger “He got your goat didn’t he D…. take your anger and frustration out on Xander. Unleash the demons within you, and believe me, you’d be more unstoppable than Soveriegn ever was. Crush Xander like the bug he is and I will get you Damon on a silver platter.” “Good, I don’t give a shit about the title, HBC can take it from him, we all know that, what I want is a bit more personal, I want to injure, maim and decimate O’Hallaron. I want to make sure he never is able to walk to his favorite pub, down his favorite pint of Guiness or fuck his favorite sheep! HBC you are right about him, he is a major fag! And fags, like gay marriages must be stopped once and for all!” “Exactly D….. concentrate on Xander, destroy him like you destroyed everything he was, is, and could be. Take him out, then, we will proceed to the next step. HBC gets Damon’s title, you can break his little neck, agreed?” “Agreed!” “Agreed!” “Then it is settled, now go sprint me 3 miles….. go…..go…GO!!!”
