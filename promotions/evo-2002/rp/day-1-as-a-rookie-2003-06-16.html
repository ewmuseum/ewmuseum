Day 1 as a Rookie

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-16 Machine is seen walking down the busy streets of New York, the site of this week’s Legends. Thursday night in Buffalo, the massive man is looking to make an example out of Josh Horroway in his debut match. Josh might not’ve made that great an impression on the fans last week, but don’t expect Machine to take him lightly. The fans might not remember the big man, but he will do his best to make sure they won’t forget him. Machine turns a corner and enters a building, a wrestling building. The home of eVo’s training center for wrestlers. Even with Machine in the cell division, he still tries to make it into the wrestling business, where he truly wants to be. With his duffel bag over his shoulder, he walks into the main gym where a ring is set up and weights are to the side. Machine goes and signs in before making his way to the locker room where an official stands outside of it. “How’s it going Rob.” Machine greets eVo referee Rob B. “They didn’t tell you rook?” Rob asks. “Rook?” “Yea,” Rob laughs before continuing. “The eVo rookie locker room is that way.” Rob points to a door that nobody really uses Machine looks at the door and looks back at Rob to see if he’s serious and indeed he is. He then takes his bag and makes his way across the main gym to the door Rob pointed to. Machine opens the door and sees nothing but metal. A furnace, the ventilation system, thermostat, pipes, and all. “What the hell?” Machines says to himself. “If Mankind pops out and gives me a mandible claw, I’m gonna scream like a bitch. Machine finishes getting dressed and comes out the boiler room in large baggy jeans and a white wifebeater. Much like Kingpin in the daredevil movie. Machine is soon called to the ring by Rob who is the trainer for the week. Machine climbs up onto the ring apron and enters the ring, awaiting instructions. “How’s your new locker room?” Rob asks while everyone smiles and laughs. “Not funny…”Machine responds. “What’s wrong, being in the bigtime not quite what you think?” Rob laughs a little. “Come on, take a few bumps to start off the day. Take them square in the middle of the ring.” Machine walks to the center of the ring before taking a deep breathe. He throws his massive body back for a bump….and goes straight through the ring canvas. The whole room bursts into laughter as the big man climbs out the whole. “This is going to be a long day…” He mutters to himself as the scene fades to black.
