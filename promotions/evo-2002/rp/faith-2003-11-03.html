Faith

3 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-03 'I have never been big on religion.' 'But still, I have faith.' 'I've never been big on hope, and dreams.' 'But still, I have faith.' 'Throughout my entire life. I have been given everything as I see fit.' 'Who needs faith?' 'I was given my scholarship to the University of Indiana, to play Soccer. I was given my training sessions for wrestling. Then, I was given a chance to make a name for myself in the worlds greatest wrestling franchise. And now, I am given the chance of a life time.' 'I live off of destiny, and I look past faith.' 'My destiny is to end two dynasty's.' 'Dynasty's of two former Universal champions. A fan favorite, and a psychopath.' 'Those, are the ones that will need faith. They will need to pray, and believe that whatever they worship or look up to; will guide them in the path of redemption and defeat me.' 'They will need faith.' 'They will need the faith to carry them above them limits inwhich they can already go. I've taken them both out, numerous amounts of times. I've seen them both bleed. I've seen them scared, I've seen them unconcious. And I know their weaknesses.' 'But still, when I think about it. I've already been given faith.' 'I have faith.' 'Faith that what I know, will be the end of....' 'Scott Frost, and Jay Jay Jameson. Forever.' 'Do you have faith?' -Charisma
