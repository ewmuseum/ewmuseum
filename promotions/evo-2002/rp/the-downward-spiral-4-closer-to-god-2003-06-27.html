Date: 2003-06-27 The Downward Spiral 4: Closer To God Deep inside, deep within my hollow, selfish soul I knew that I wasn’t saving her from herself. I wasn’t helping anyone. I was doing this for myself. I need release. I craved sex. I craved Tate. I needed Tate. Tate was my release. I would give it to Tate like no other. We would engage in intimate sex. Today, however, today I was a pervert. Today I was depriving her of that sexual right she possessed to turn me down. To say no. “Just shut the fuck up.” I paced in a clockwise direction, rounding the room, stalking my prey. Tears of terror began to slowly release from her eyes, down her nose, off her chin before hitting the wooden floor with one tiny drop. My final sin, maybe not. My last confession, maybe not. But certainly my first. It needed to be done. My past had always been my responsibility. Tate was my past. Tate was my own. Tate was my responsibility and I had always taken care of my responsibilities. But now she hung, back facing me as I slowly took my hand out of her side. She hung there like the kittens had. She would hang there to rot, similar to the story of the kittens. She was now nothing but cow dung. The cruelty of my sin, the extent of my sin, I’m a sinner, I saw this for the sin it was against eternal life. I saw her soul like a white soul snatched away. Just the way I saw those kittens, through my eyes she was scum of the earth. Or maybe I should rewind slightly to make things a little clearer… She lay there, her head bruised, a dark purple colour existed now before that awful blue-black colour would appear, destroying the beauty of her face. Like money left unguarded, just laying there for the taking. I slowly slipped her tight, black pants down to her ankles. She hadn’t been wearing underwear as I expected, thus making it easier for me to commence. In. Out. Grind. Thrust. Moan. Moan. Moan. This was the release I needed. Grr. Grr. Grr. This was living life to the full. I finished up with one final gasp. The rest of my actions would speak for themselves. My actions would be questionable as my salty tears streamed down my face. My emotions unquestionable. I stuck the dagger slowly into my side, moving it slightly in both directions before removing it quickly. Her pale face lay motionless on her shoulder. Six times and only six did I dig that dagger in her side. The daggers wound made larger by my hand which I inserted slowly, moving it about in her insides. Later, I would use both to make it larger and that brings you up to this moment in time. I slowly dialled those three numbers on her house phone, my glove slipping slightly off my hand. 9 Pause. Uncertainty. 1 Is this really where my life has gone? 1 I’m a sinner. A bad person.. Ring. “911 accident and emergency.” “Yes, I’d like to report a break in.”
