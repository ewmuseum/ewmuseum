Faith 2

8 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-08 Tokoyo, Japan Budokan Hall It has been a whirlwind twenty-four hours since the arrival of The Syndicate on Japanese soil. From the moment they landed they have been beseiged by fans, made an apperance on NHK-1 station, stopped off at MTV Asia headquarters, did two radio shows, and another interview by the FWM promotion, and finally their last stop is now, outside of the Emperor's Palace. Just across the way is the Tower of Japan, a mock-up of the Effiel Tower in Paris. As they approach the Palace, the Emperor's royal guards are standing at full alert, and though heavily armed, causally cordial to their honored guest for the day. They have gotten advanced word that they were coming. The Emperor is a big fan of their's. The massive double doors open when one of the Emperor's personal attache comes out to greet them. Yoshii: Welcome Dreamer-san, welcome HBC-sana, you definately honor us with your presence today. The Emperor is delighted that you both excepted his invitation for tea. HBC: It is we who are honored Yoshii-san. Yoshii: Your Japanese is excellent HBC-sana. HBC: Thank you, as you know I competed over here for nearly two years and learned the language along the way. Yoshii: You must have had a good tutor, you speak the Imperial form of it. HBC: It was Anoki who taught me. Yoshii: Antonio? HBC: Yes...I believe he is a member of your Diet now isn't he? Yoshii: Yes, a true citizen of the people. Follow me please. HBC and BDD are led through the courtyard, past the Royal Bonzi Gardens and off to the West-end of the Palace. The Palace is certainly breath-taking to see the very least. A huge Shinto shrine peers from the background as they continue walking over to another garden, The Emperor's personal and very private garden where a table has been set up in advance with several chairs. BDD goes to sit down but HBC taps him on the shoulder, shaking her head no, not until the Emperor comes. Sheepishly, BDD nods, its been a long day. Another moment passes before Hiroito comes out dressed in a business suit and sits down. When he does HBC and BDD does as well. Hiroito: Welcome to my personal garden. BDD: It is magnificent, your magisty. Hiroito: Please call me Hiro, we are friends so we can dispell the plesentries. HBC: As you wish Hiro. Thank you for inviting us. It is indeed an esteemed honor to meet you. Its not often we get to have tea with the Emperor. Hiro: *laugh* no it is you two who honor me today. I have been a big fan of you, the both of you for some years now. I used to watch you perform in the F-1 promotion HBC, and you Dreamer when you took on Masato Tanaka. That was a classic match. Tanaka is a personal friend of mine by the way and raved about you to me after the match. He said you were the most honorable Gaijin on the tour. And you HBC, you have won the hearts of our people with your grace and beauty in the ring. The both of you honor us again by bringing the Evolution here to Japan and we are looking forward to an exciting show. HBC: Well we try. Myself and Dreamer face the Industry and this new team of Astonishing Entertainment. All of us have a bit of history together, especially with our careers on the line. BDD: Yes, we’ve faced the Industry before, however they truly lack the ability and honor to run the tag division and AE, they are out for a quick buck against our name. Selfless dogs. I’m sure you have many like that in the Diet don’t you. Hiro: Hai! We do, too many I'm afraid. Always looking out for their own interest instead of the common good. Shameful. HBC: That describes our opponents perfectly. Shameful individuals, thinking about themselves. Granted this business set it up like that, but they have no respect for honor, dignity, humility. Hiro: That's probably why they don't advance far isn't it. BDD: Perhaps. Their personal life, isn't our concern, all we do is go to the ring, do what we do best with grace and style, entertain the fans who paid alot to see us, and give them a great show. When we arrived, everyone is cheering for us so I guess that means we've been doing something right all these years. Its amazing really since our promotion is eight thousand miles across the Pacific and they know what is happening every week. Hiro: As do I, I can assure you that. I'm sure you both will get your hands on Davidson and you will regain your respective championships back. If not, I'll make an Imperial decree! (Laughter by all) HBC: That would be cool. By order of the Emperor of Japan you will such and such. Who could argue with that? Hiro: Certainly not S. or Davidson BDD: You do watch everyweek don't you. Hiro: A guilty pleasure...yes, I enjoy it. It appears to be quite a power struggle in the high office lately with Foryth coming back. Any inside thoughts you can tell me??? HBC: I'm afraid you'll just have to tune in just like everyone else does Hiro. Hiro: As I said, you two were always honorable. It was a pleasure meeting you both, but before you go, two things, one, feel free to wander around as my guest, the gardens are especially nice this time of year. Security knows your here so don't worry, and two, I have a gift for you both. (Hiro waves his hands over to his attache who comes over with two large long items wrapped with silk, depicting the Emperor's Royal Crest. The attache bows as he hand them to the Emperor and walks off.) Hiro: As I have said before, the both of you have brought great honor to our people, entertaining them, enlightening them, and with your business, employing them. You personally have honored me by attending today and for that I want to bestow upon you, a special gift. I know you know your Asian history Dreamer and collect many fine works of ours, and you HBC are the same. You have been a friend to us and for that I thank you. I would like to present to you both, these items for the each of you. They have been guarding my Royal heritage for nearly a thousand years, and with that, I present, the Royal Katana's of The Emperor's Guard. (BDD and HBC look at each other in amazement) Hiro: Honor me by excepting them. BDD: They will go in a place of esteem honor as friendship between us, your people, your family, and yourself. Hiro: Thank you, today is indeed a great day....their is one more thing you can do for me....... HBC: What's that? Hiro: Defeat those honorless dogs The Industry and AE...and if not to much trouble, plant Davidson in a Syndicator for me. BDD: Hiro....consider it done....consider it done.....
