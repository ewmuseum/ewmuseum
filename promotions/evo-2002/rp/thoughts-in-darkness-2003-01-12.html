Thoughts in Darkness

12 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Darkness. Blackness. A lack of light. Whatever you want to call it, it’s there. But it’s not because you can’t see anything. Asis was used to this kind of thing his whole life, but it was a physical kind of darkness. The kind of darkness that resulted from being kept in an unlit room most of his childhood years. This kind of darkness, the darkness of the mind not being active, is not something he has ever experienced. With the kind of mind Asis has there is always plenty of activity in it, it’s never dark and silent. But then out of the darkness, out of the deafening silence, Asis can hear a voice. His own voice, or at least he thinks it is. He can’t quite recognise what it’s saying, but it’s something he knows from a time when it was louder, brighter, a lot more active. Then the face, the piercing red eyes, the face of a man that he once knew, a demon that could destroy anyone, that could not be suppressed. Until Nightmare Before Christmas, a night when he got sloppy and got punished for it. Punished in a way he didn’t think was possible. Got punished in a way that he had done to countless other people in the past, and in the way he would have done to Scott Frost if he’d been given the chance. He can see a bit of himself in Frost, a bit of the ruthfullness, the lust to see your opponent’s body broken and battered in front of you. But lying in the hospital bed, the neckbrace firmly secured round his neck, the straps double tightened to keep him still, all Asis can do is think. Something that he does more than many would believe, but in the frame of mind he’s been in for the past week, they have not been pleasant thoughts. Many people have crossed Asis’ mind; the man who was responsible for him being strapped to a bed to prevent him hurting himself was the front man. But other people were on the list as well, one of them being a man that was beginning to frustrate him. Strictly Prohibited, he just didn’t seem to understand. Unlimited beat him down, and he came back and pissed them off again, claiming he was more dominant and so on. And now Asis has been chosen to rip him apart on his return, which he is quietly confident of doing. Shutting him up permanently. And also, Shocka, the man who bragged about L2J’s death. L2J was the only person in Asis’ life who had ever come close to a friend, and now he was gone. They may not have been as close to one another in the past six months as they used to be, but that does not mean that Asis did not still respect him as a friend. And then Shocka being the loud mouth whore that he is comes along and brags about his death, maybe trying to play up to the public, cement himself as a heel in the business, but for whatever reason he’d said what he had, he would now regret it. Shocka might taste the pain this week, or next week, it will happen, he just won’t know when it’s going to happen, until his face is being smashed against concrete, and he sees his own blood blinding his eyes. It matters not who Asis has to go through, Shocka will pay, one way or another, sooner or later, he will pay. And then in all this is a man who attacked him during his tag match at Nightmare Before Christmas, a man who has left the week before Asis returns. A man they call “Disco”. Asis would have his revenge on him as well, one way or another they will all pay, and Asis will have his redemption.
