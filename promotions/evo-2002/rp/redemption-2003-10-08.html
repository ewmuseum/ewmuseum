Redemption

8 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-08 I can still hear it. Pitter-pattering rhythemically against the car window. The rain, dropping like a thousand tiny bullets against the glass, put me in a trance as I drove through the rain soaked streets that night. My vision was cut off by a curtain of falling water which seemed to follow me as I progressed foward. I turned to my right and saw Neil, my elder brother by a year. He never looked so peaceful as he did that night. He gently tossed back his curly, golden brown hair, which much resembled mine. He turned to me with the same look I remember when he confessed to me his worries over my run ins with the law several years ago. That oh so familiar look of concern over my well being was one that I became accustomed to before I decided to battle the demons that haunted me, starting around my fifteeth birthday. 'Patrick?' The sound of his voice pierced the hypnotic trance I had fallen under, like a needle to a balloon. I turned to him, trying to ignore the look I had come to dread the previous years. 'Yeah Neil?' I responded, the sound of my own voice alarming me. 'I've been worried about you lately. You haven't been sleeping well, and even now, you seem to be preoccupied. What is wrong?' 'Nothing,' I answered, flatly. I am such a terrible liar. I should have at least hesitated, or let him know that I was giving an inkling of thought to the question. 'Are you thinking of Kayla again?' And then it hit me. The dull pain that prepetually lulled in my heart was upgraded to a sharp, stabbing pain of forgotten emotions. You see, it was two months to the day since... 'I think you should seek professional help,' Neil broke through. 'No,' I answered shortly, wanting to sidestep the entire conversation. As I did this, I spotted a flashing yellow light in the distance. The light disappeared momentarily, and was replaced with a red light slightly above it. I jammed on the breaks of the car. After that moment, the rest was a complete blur. I can only remember the car spinning out of control. The last thing I remember was the sound of a blazing horn, and my brother's last desperate screams as the car slammed into the side of another moving vehicle. Darkness... Complete and utter emptiness... The preoccupation over my dearly departed former fiancee Kayla... Left me blind to the things that were still there... Perished... Slowly my senses began to obey me once more. All I could hear at first was a low murmering. As I began to slowly open my eyes, I realized I was lying in a hospital bed. I felt like I was being reborn, but I was soon to learn it was the contrary. 'Wha?' I remember myself muttering that first syllable as I slowly sailed through the gap between the catatonic and the living, breathing world. My eyes began to refocus themselves. At first, the only sight I saw was a white blur, leading me to believe I had died and was heading for the afterlife. In many ways, I did die. I knew I would never be the same again. Colors, shapes, sound, feel, smell, all slowly returned to me, as if slowly leaking into my pores and through my system. I felt myself swallow. 'Patrick?' I looked up to see a doctor hovering over me. All at once I wanted to know when, where, how, and most of all...why? 'You're going to be just fine. Just lie there and take it easy.' And all at once, the pain hit me. I felt a pulse of pain throughout my head, hitting me like a million chisels. I instinctively attempted to grab my head, but found my arms very limited in movement. 'Easy there. You broke your arms and your left leg in that accident.' 'Neil...' I struggled to gasp, still weak and in some ways wanting to crawl back into the comfort of my state of total numbness. The doctor only shook his head and softly said, 'I'm sorry...He's gone.' I can't say that I cried right then. I'd be lying if I said I did. Right then and there, the sobering thoughts returned to my head. I was no longer in a blanket of clouds. This was very much real. My brother, Neil, who raised me from the time our parents abandoned us, was gone. The man who bailed me out of prison when I robbed Mercer's Grocery Store, and pleaded with the judge not to send me to Juvenile Hall, was gone. But Neil was so much more than that. He was a humanitarian, a friend, and an overall success. He took his situation and overcame adversity. Now, he's gone...and I killed him. The pain was all too real now. First Kayla, now Neil. I always took advantage of Neil, and now I could never do that again. I still remember that expression. The look of concern that washed over his face for me. Maybe I was the one who should have been concerned. Maybe not so much for myself, but for everything else around me. Kayla's death didn't teach me a lesson. When she was yanked off this Earth, another pawn sacked, it wasn't alarming enough. It was only when they laid Neil to rest, his body once again becoming one with mother earth, that the sobering message bled through. The cries of all his close friends, his girlfriend, and those who just adored him from afar, struck me like a million different needles, each sharper than the last. And yet, I could not bleed...I could not cry, because crying would prove how weak I was. I am not weak... I will never again be that man. I will never again be the man who lived. Patrick Powers is no longer alive. Inside of me, the heart that once pumped fresh blood to all my organs, the blood that flowed through the chambers of my heart and through my veins is no longer flowing. It is now moving at a steadily slow rate, eating my organs alive, waiting to die. I am now finishing Neil's dream. He almost made it, but I struck him down in his prime. I was enraged, numb, and powerless when Kayla was slain in our neighborhood, but why feel that way now? I did the same to my brother. My selfishness killed Neil that rainy night. It wasn't Neil that died that day at all. It was me...
