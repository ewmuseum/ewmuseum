A New Dawn(Prologue)

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-26 'You sure have been quiet lately' said Donaven as he pulled a chair up to Trevor. 'Been thinking alot lately' replied Trevor as he leaned back in the chair waved a waitress over. 'About what?' asked Donaven. 'yeah can you get me and my friend here a Molson Canadain.' said Trevor as he flipped the waitress a five.' Its hard to explain dude' 'Aight man, but if you need to talk man I am here for ya.' said Donaven as he slammed back his drink and then walked off. Only three days away from one of the biggest matchs in his life, in his career. Their was so much on the line, so much at risk in this match, So many questions that I could not answer..... Will I fight Marcus Escobar...... Will I make it to the Final Cage...... Will I walk out the Cell Champion.... In The End..... 3 days away, this match could be the match that will jolt my career ahead, and finally get me noticed. Why am I worring so much? This isn't me, I am the fearless Trevor Norman, I walk into shit with my eyes shut all the time, not knowing what could happen. I need to snap out of this, all those fools are a bunch of bitchs, and all it will take is one punch for them. Yeah, I have nothing to worry about, these follos aren't nothing to me
