Criminal Acts: Part one, 'In the Beginning'

7 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 7th We enter the Thug roC’s Washington D.C. home, where he and Adam Carr are watching television. Thug roC sits in his lazyboy and Carr is eating double stuff oreos on the couch. The duo watches the Fox 5 news at noon where they are having some sort of breaking news story. “ Today the release of Greg Vulgar is on everyone’s mind,” The news reporters starts. “Nine months ago, Vulgar was accused of mob criminal action but was later found innocent by a jury of his peers. Many speculate that the trial was very unfair due to the amount of evidence that was thrown out the courtroom. The streets are…” “Hey roC, you scared of this Greg Vulgar guy?” Adam Carr asks as he watches the TV “Are you serious?” Thug roC replies, almost laughing as he thinks about the question. “The guy has a group of low-lifes help him out, stealing ladies’ purses and stupid crap like that, why should I be scared?” “Damn, he had to steal A LOT of purses for something like that” -=[Adam was making reference to the cane that Vulgar was sporting. A very nice one at that. It was made of what looked to be solid gold, or at least gold plated. The top was in the shape of a tiger’s head. The tiger looked up into the sky with his eyes being rubies and a green emerald in his mouth. Very nice]=- “Yea, maybe you can find something like that to buy Vicki for your anniversary,” Thug roC says. “Wait, I have to buy something?” Adam is almost amazed at what Thug roC had to say. “Of course, it’s your 5 year anniversary” “I know that, but I thought a nice dinner and a night on the town would do the trick. Why would I have to buy jewlrey?” “ You just don’t understand women like I do.” Thug roC says. He sits up a little to lecture Adam, “They bank on these even numbers like 5, 10, or 20. When you have a 5 year, or 10 year anniversary, you must pull out all the stops and get the perfect gift. If not, they’ll think that you just forgot and hurried to go rent “Men In Black” to watch for the night. “Hey that only happened once!” Adam Carr screams, “Besides, I have two weeks, what’s not to like? You got any ideas on what I should get?” “It’s a tough call, maybe you should make it.” “You’re no help,” Adam Carr proclaims. “maybe I’ll ask Machine” “ Machine? Are you serious?” Thug roC tries not to laugh. “He’s the same guy that thought a scholarship was a boat full of students. You don’t want to ask him.” “What about Vega? He’s responsible.” “Yea if you call getting drunk, hitting a parked car, and running to the nearby police station to hide responsible. Just leave it to me, I’ll steer you the right way.” “Are you sure?” Adam Carr asks “Trust me on this one.” Says Thug roC. “I know what to do.” -=[We go to a house in Arlington, Virginia where a black SUV is pulling up. Out of it comes Greg Vulgar. He is very recognizable not to mention the cane that he uses. He obviously doesn’t need it, being only 41, but he carries it around him to make people know how important he is. He walks up to a house that has two cars in the garage and the grass freshly cut. The flowers are just now starting to die from the fall weather and the wind causes the chimes to signal loudly. Greg Vulgar doesn’t look happy as he goes and bangs on the door as loud as possible. His pale white hand is seen when he knocked on the door, and an obvious scar is on the back of it. The door swings open and a black lady is there to receive him. Both look confused at who the other one is.]=- “Who the hell are you and what are you doing in my house?” Greg Vulgar yells. “Your house?” The woman asks, looking even more confused then Vulgar. “Excuse me, but I bought this house 2 months ago from a lady that was very happy to give it to me for cheap.” “What? How could Sarah do such a thing?” “Ha, you must be her deadbeat husband that was sent to jail.” The woman chuckles a little bit before continueing. “She mentioned how you might show back up, expecting to see her. Well she left town a while ago and all your stuff was in an auction about a month ago. Good luck getting it all back too. So while you sit in your nice fancy SUV, get comfy, cause you might have to live out of it for a while.” -=[The black woman slams the door in Greg’s face and Greg turns in anger. He marches down the steps and back into his SUV where one of his buddies sat waiting. The guy in the passenger seat was Fred. A young man in his 20s with blonde hair and blue eyes. Some called him Hit-kid, or Hitler Kid, him being the description of Hitler’s perfect race. He has a look like “what happened?” and Greg hesitates before answering]=- “That b*tch Sarah sold the house when she heard about me being locked up.” Greg Vulgar crosses his arms with a very upset look on his face. “That goody too-shoes gets on my nerves.” “I guess our plans on going to Europe are gone” Hit-Kid says. “Says who?” Vulgar is almost shocked at what he says “Well Sarah had all your stuff, and I don’t got a job. How are you going to pay for a trip to Europe?” Vulgar pauses before replying, “We’ll do this like we use to. We’ll get the money by any means necessary.” -=[We go to another SUV, but it’s silver. The Avalanche goes down the road with Thug roC, Adam Carr and Vega all inside. The three sit as the rap music plays very loudly. They go through downtown D.C. with a little haste until Adam turns the music down to talk]=- “So Vega, I heard you were in Desert Storm” Adam starts the conversation “Yea it’s true.” Vega replies, trying to sound modest “Yea,” Thug roC interupts. “when Vega was in Desert Storm, both sides were shooting at him.” “ Haha, very funny roC.” Vega says, not looking to pleased. “ If you’re so smart, why were you eating with sticks the other day? Explain that genius” “Those were chopsticks you dumbass…” Thug roC says before putting his head down in shame. “Oh…” -=[The three hop out the car at a nearby jewlery store. The two look up at the store sign that reads “Washington Jewls”]=- “Whoo hoo! Hot dogs!” Vega yells, “I’ll catch up with you guys a little later!” -=[Vega runs to a nearby cart that sits at the corner of the road. Thug roC and Adam Carr just walk into the store which is full of glass cased jewlry. The two walk around as Thug roC commentates]=- “I’ve been coming here for a few years now.” Thug roC says. “The work is very good and the owner is a friend of mine. So I’m sure he can hook us up with a little something.” “What about that one? That necklace is real nice” Adam Carr says while pointing. “Wow, those are some nice diamonds. Must be worth a few grand by far.” Thug roC then points down. “But this one over here is mine” “The watch of bracelet?” “ The watch, I’ma get that today”. -=[The owner comes out from the back and asks the two if they need help. The two soon purchase both the watch and necklace before Vega even returns. They start walking away, both wearing their new bling bling on the way out the door. As they step out, the both look to their right to see Vega stuffing a hot dog in his mouth. They both start walking towards Vega when Adam suddenly drops to the ground hard. Thug roC looks at him, then looks behinds him to see two masked men in jeans and t-shirts. They hurry to beat down on Thug roC before he realizes what’s happening. With Vega’s back turned to the event, he isn’t moving. The two men snatch any and all forms of money from the two, including the new watch and necklace. Finally, Vega turns and starts running at the two. But they are quick to get up and run off into an alley where two motorcycles were waiting for them. Vega stands above both Thug roC and Adam Carr who are laid out]=-
