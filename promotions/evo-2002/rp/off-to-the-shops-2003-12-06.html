Off to the Shops

6 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-06 Dressed in a thick bomber jacket, Ethan felt sure he had prepared for every eventuality that might occur during his marathon five minute work into the Precint. Why the driver had not got him closer bemused him! I mean sure there were no entry signs, but it need only be ignored once there was no way in hell Ethan was going to come back to the slum which was the West Quay in Southampton, England. Like a trooper Ethan battled against the elements, be it only a slight breeze, before moving inside and going in search for the reasoning behind this impromptu trip. Ethan was indeed still smarting after his ejection from Evolution to the indys, Ethan felt like nobody was taking him seriously within the wrestling circuit because of his private fortune. However what Devlin Jackson had not solved, a mask surely would. One thing that was great was the restraint being showed by the fellow shoppers on site. Ethan knew they knew who he was, or at least he thought he knew that; therefore it amazed him that hoards of crowds were not clambering for his autographs, maybe this place wasn't so bad as he had thought?! Manners is a major plus. Nobody dared tell him the truth, nobody dared even mention that Ethan M Coles was blending in for a reason; the reason being, nobody knew who Ethan was to segregate him from the general riff raff on hand. Ethan had only been exposed to the spotlight of Evolutions main programing for a brief five seconds fame. Despite Ethan believing he was a mainstay being punished by the envious, during his last outing in Evolution he had on countless occasions been thought to be just one of the crew. Devlin had looked the part and thus served the purpose making him look like a wrestler and not a rich boy on a school visit. Back to the mission at hand though, re-inventing himself and ensuring he received the credit his self proclaimed talent deserved. Ethans thinking was, how could they call him a rich boy if they did not even know the rich boy existed?! Question was which one?! As Ethan stared through the shop window he could see a vast collection, neither the witch nor skeleton one appealed. It needed to be suave, like its beholder. Sophisticated, like the mans mind who hid behind this tool of disguise. And unique; Evolution was already like a 24/7 Halloween parade. Ethan was not just another soul churned from the conveyer belt, Ethan was a star in the making; all that was needed was the stage for a star to shine.
