Debut (RP#1 for Legends)

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/05/02 The scene opens up in the hallways of eVo. We see a young man about 6'1' or 2 standing in the hallway infront of a eVo bulleton board. The young man is wearing a mask with a white t-shirt and blue jeans with some black sneakers. The young man is reading the eVo bulleton board which reads. Legends XI Preview The young man begins to read the card too see if he debuts at legends. Live on OSE! From The Ice Palace ~ Tampa, Florida 7:59PM EST Wednesday Night October 9th, 2002 eVolution brings its trip through the state of Florida to an end this week as it presents Legends XI. The Tag Team Title tournament has continued and will do so again this show as two more unlikely teams find themselves paired up for a chance at some gold. We’ll see the debut of some new faces as well as all the stars selected to enter eVolution last week will make their in ring debuts this week in a big battle. In the main event L2J and Scott Frost will settle some unknown scores as they go one on one with lumberjacks lining the ring and doing who knows what else. The Universal Champion, William will also be in the house and only he knows what you could possibly expect from him. Non Title ~ Dark Match Hanz Froyberg vs. Aaron “Jinosyde” Chamberland The two wild cards left in the final five decisions face off in the dark match to determine just who gets that final spot. The German star Froyberg will battle the youthful Chamberland as both men look to prove that they are worthy of that spot. Non Title ~ Submission Match Echo BlackWhisper vs. “The Outkast” Eric Knight Echo must continue his quest this week to try and earn his spot in eVolution like he had begun just last week. Making his first official television debut Echo will battle against another former cell fighter in Eric Knight who has been in a slump that was hard to break out of in the past few weeks. Losses to Gerico, and the team of William and L2J have been all he’s accomplished since making his return at Digital Destruction. Now for the sake of his career Echo will do whatever is possible to pick up another win at the expense of Eric Knight. Non Title ~ Standard Match “Pompous Perfection” Andrew Martin vs. Strictly Prohibited vs. Sgt. Jack O’Hara vs. Jay Stylez Junior The former High Impact Champion, Quasar the Great gets a shot to regain his title. If he wins this match against the hot newcomer, Gerico then next week he’ll get his rematch against HeartBreak Chick. If not then the title shot will go to Sean Mason. Gerico on the other hand will have the chance to make a huge impact in eVolution if he’s able to knock off the former champion in this huge match. High Impact Title ~ Triple Threat Match w/ Big Daddy Dreamer as referee Quasar the Great vs. ???? vs. HeartBreak Chick© The High Impact Title is on the line again thanks to the orders of Thug roC this past week. The newest member of The Unlimited must defend her title in a triple threat match against former champion Quasar and an opponent to be named later. The odds certainly aren’t stacked in the favor of the HeartBreaker and when you add Big Daddy Dreamer into the mix as the referee things get even worse for her and her High Impact Title reign. Tag Team Title Tournament ~ Tag Team Match Jason Jameson and Seara vs. Asis and “Too Xtreme” Eric Badger Two very unlikely teams find themselves paired up this week as part of the Tag Team Title Tournament. Asis and Badger had just one week earlier been busy beating the hell out of each other in a match which also included Seara. Now the injured Seara will team with a man she has no connections with and certainly no allegiances with in Jason Jameson. The only thing on the mind of Jameson now is getting his hands on Eric Badger who has eluded him the past few weeks since Digital Destruction and maybe even getting some retribution against Asis as well. Rising Star Title ~ Standard Match “The Prodigal” Sean Mason vs. “The Natural” Matt Boone After getting a week off last week Boone has been scheduled this week to defend his Rising Star Title against the number one contender to the High Impact Title Sean Mason. Mason gets this chance to capture his first taste of eVolution gold as he battles against Boone in preparation for his title match against the High Impact Champion at Faith. Boone looks to pick up where he left off and continue his reign of terror and pain that was started with Troy Pierce at Digital Destruction and always lurking in the shadows you can be certain that Cori Simmons won’t be far behind. Non Title ~ Hardcore Match Tommy Michaels vs. Jinx Once again Tommy Michaels finds himself placed in a hardcore match. Last week Molten wasn’t able to get the job done and beat Michaels but this week Michaels will have to step into the hardcore environment again and go against The Unlimited’s very own Jinx. A much tougher challenge as Jinx has already proven himself quite capable of doing what is needed in order to win in the hardcore environment and Michaels will be trying to keep his secrets just that. Non Title ~ Standard Match ???? vs. Shocka© Shocka gets another break this week courtesy of Thug roC. Last week he easily defeated Python in a title defense and now this week Thug roC has given him the week off from defending his title. Shocka is still in action though as he finds himself paired against a mystery opponent that no one knows except for Thug roC. Non Title ~ Lumberjack Match L2J vs. Scott Frost Apparently there are some issues still existing in the past of L2J and Scott Frost and both men have asked that this match take place and the ring be surrounded by various eVolution superstars. Already confirmed lumberjacks are the remaining Unlimited members, William, Matt Boone, Big Daddy Dreamer, and Shodan. One way or another, this match is certain to cause a lot of trouble for both men as the ring will be surrounded by not only allies but their enemies as well. The young man is done finished reading the card for the next Legends. He turns around to the camera which is behind him and he begins to laughs. The cameraman notices that is is the newcomer Jay Stylez Junior. He gets serious and begins to speak about the card. Jay Stylez Jr.: 'Non Title ~ Standard Match... “Pompous Perfection” Andrew Martin vs. Strictly Prohibited vs. Sgt. Jack O’Hara vs. Jay Stylez Jr. Finally I get a match to debut and show that other superstars in the back that I am a Impact playa...I cant stand here and run my mouth about these guys cause I dont now nothing about them. I just now that I will give it 150% of what I have to climb the ladder in eVo. Jay Stylez Junior walks off of the site of the camera...The camera zooms into the bulleton board on the matches of Legends....The camera begins to fade to black and go to a commercial break. -Scene ends- OOC: Didnt have much time, I was very busy with studing for a test. I had to think of something. enjoy
