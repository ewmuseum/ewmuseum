The Aftermath

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept 23 Dateline: Sarajevo Big Daddy Dreamer has arrived in the bombed out, war-torn city of Sarajevo under escort of Natalia and four members of the Bosnian Army. At this point, none of them know the true reason why BDD is in the country and his personal mission he must fulfill before leaving this country. The jeep is speeding along at 50 clicks through the city with the ever scanning soliders looking for danger from every turn. Everyone on the street is given a long hard look as they proceed by. This is definitely not the place BDD once knew as the jewel of the Adriatic Sea. In fact he can’t help to feel a bit sorry for these people. All those years of war, destroyed everything they used to hold so dear. Perhaps when he gets back to Canada, he’ll set up a humanitarian effort to rebuild this once proud city. Natalia spins around in a hard left avoiding a truck still smoldering in flames, probably attacked at point blank range from a RPG-7. Looks like no survivors as blood still stains the curb as the street children pick through the rubble looking for anything of value or to eat. Yes, this is not the place he once spent a month backpacking through those years ago. All of sudden, Natalia slams on the brakes nearly smacking BDD head against the console. ‘We might have trouble up ahead. You may want to unholster that thing just in case it gets alittle hairy ok?’ ‘Yes ma’am. Don’t worry, I know what too do. I’ve been in situations and places like this before.’ ‘Oh really, we may have to chat about that sometime. Igor, ready the machine gun.’ ‘Attention up ahead, clear the street immediately, this is a combative area. We are the Bosnian Army. Clear at once!’ With those words several people do run inside for fear of their life. Others though, the bravado ones, taunt the soliders and begin to throw rocks at them and then Molotov Cocktails. Natalia looks at them and shakes her head, almost sadly. ‘Warning shots only people, no need for further excess loss of life around here. Igor, if you please.’ The .50caliber machine gun roars to life in short concentrated bursts at the street 100 feet in front of them, kicking up dirt and fragments of rocks in an effort to get them to vacate the area. Fortunately they do turn tail and run away as Natalia kicks the jeep into high gear, speeding down the street narrowly running over several of them in the process. BDD looks over at her as she looks back. ‘Always remember, them or us, because some of the more die-hard people around here, they don’t care about anything except revenge. Those were pretty much just demonstrators, we have four factions though in the city, warlords if you prefer, and the place you need to go, is in the heart of one of them. Not very nice place anymore. I’m looking to get the hell out of here myself very soon!’ ‘How long have you been here?’ ‘Two weeks, and I’m ready to transfer my ass to someplace safer. Beirut perhaps? Even Iraq is nicer than this! Shit! Hold on!!’ At that moment another group of people flood the streets, this time armed. They begin taking aim with their AK-47 rifles. Bursts of bullets ring overhead as Igor begins immediately opening fire on the crowd as the two soliders sitting behind them begin firing their rifles as well. Four people fall dead in the streets as Natalia whips around the bend distancing themselves from the mob. One solider is hurt, wounded in the arm but able to function. Ten minutes pass as Natalia speeds through the narrow city streets at 70 kilometers per hour. Finally they arrive at BDD’s destination. The Church of the Angelic Cross. Even here, the war has taken its toll. Blackened by fire and soot from the nearby buildings, and looted, this shining beacon of what was good with the world, forevermore tarnished. The three soliders man the jeep setting up a security perimeter as BDD and Natalia enter the Church. Inside has been ramshackled. Statues smashed, wooded structures destroyed or set ablaze. Natalia stops and looks around all the while holding her sidearm out ready for surprises as BDD walks down the hallway with his .45 out as well. BDD rounds the corner before coming to a thick solid oak door that has been untouched by the war. The marble walls on the other hand show a discouraging story as bullet fragments can been seen knicking the finish off of them. BDD opens up his backpack and pulls out an ancient key and sticks it in the lock while Natalia looks on with surprise. He twists the key around as a large *clunk* is heard echoing through the hallway. BDD grabs the handle with both hands and all his might swings open the solid oak door, disappearing through the opening. Natalia checks around one last time before following him down into the abyss……. BDD pulls out a high-powered magna light and checks the hallway for any sign of intruders. Seeing that there is none he continues down the steps, flashlight in one hand, his sidearm in the other. Natalia follows by his side, perplexed by this mysterious figure in front of him. Wrestler? Businessman? Emissary? What or who exactly is this person?? He was calm under fire, that is for sure. Soliders mentality perhaps. Something about him or his past that betrays his fasode. Natalia keeps a close eye on this one. Kinda cute too. Dam this business she is in. How she wants out. No more Army life for her. Something better, something perhaps, no, its too soon for that. She can’t make that call just quite yet. With those pondering thoughts she follows BDD three steps behind him. He certainly seems to know where he is going though. Odd. BDD pauses at the juncture and decides to goto the right and is rewarded with a statue that he has seen before in his travels. Another door. He pulls hard on this one but it won’t budge. He asks Natalia for her help and together they both pull with all her might as it barely opens just enough to allow them both to pass through. And their it is. A single stone coffin. Ornately decorated from hundreds of years ago and as he reads the inscription from old Crylic, he reaches down and pulls out a golden necklace. He carefully folds it up and gently wraps it around the transposed figure, inscribed on the coffin. Natalia looking on all the while. ‘What is that?’ ‘That Natalia belonged to this person right her, the ancestor of a friend of mine, who could not make this journey herself. She spent ten years trying to find this artifact, and had no luck with it, only through the reasources I have were we able to uncover it and return it, righting a blemish on her family history. She never knew I finally found it for her. She would have been so thrilled to know and been here herself to do this, but alas, she couldn’t make the trip.’ ‘Its probably a good thing, as you’ve seen, its not a nice place anymore. But when you see her, she’ll be happy I’m sure.’ ‘I won’t know, she no longer exist in this world. She was killed.’ ‘I’m sorry to hear that, did they get the person?’ ‘Your looking at the person who did it………’ ‘What!?!’ Suddenly the cold steel of the sidearm is pressed to the back of his head as he can hear Natalia’s erratic breathing. ‘It was all an accident. She, her name was Katarina, she was my manager and a former wrestler. Also my friend, business parter, and occasional lover. She turned against us, my corporation and sided with the enemy. She fought me in the ring and things got out of hand. She retired from the ring due to a neck injury, and wanted to desperately go back. We, I, said no for fear of her reinjuring herself. She didn’t listen. My partner arranged a plan to spike piledrive her, just injuring her only, so that she could see the error of her ways, because she the type of person who would have competed until she was killed in the ring. There was no stopping her. The spike didn’t do enough and I applied my finishing hold which can break the neck easily. I sinched her in, she passed out and was sent to the hospital. Three days later she died due to brain swelling. The doctors did all they could for her, but it wasn’t enough. Wereas I though I was saving her life, I ultimately ended it and I have been paying for it ever since. It was her last request in this world to right this wrong on her family history. To return something that was stolen ages ago, her quest, is finally finished. Its all I can do to honor her, to fulfill her person, private legacy. Perhaps now she will be able to rest in peace knowing the last final act in her chapter has been completed. That is why I am here. That is why I put you and your troops in jeopardy. To fulfill a promise to her. It won’t bring her back obviously, but perhaps looking above, she is smiling down. I am so sorry Katarina. I love you. All those years, all those travels, all the jokes, everything. I will forever hold you near and dear in my heart and I constantly think about you. Perhaps one day, we will be together again and I can say this looking at you…..you meant the world to me. You captured my heart, my soul, and from this moment, when I win the World Championship, it will be for you, and I’ll comeback here, right on this very spot, and present it to you. I miss you.’ Natalia who had the gun pressed to the back of his head, moves it away and has a tear streaming down her face. ‘That was very eloquent. I’m sure she would be happy knowing you fulfilled your promise and think of her so dearly. I wish we all had someone like that. We need to go, its getting dark and the factions come out at night. ‘ Together they both make their way out of the catacombs and the minute they step outside, Natalia turns her head hearing the faint whistle sound from a distance. She looks toward the jeep and tell them to run but its too late. A mortar round hits the jeep dead center, killing all three members of her detail as BDD tosses her to the ground, covering her up as fragments rain over and on top of him. She crawls out, screaming at the carnage. Off in a distance, she see’s where the shell came from. She takes aim and fires two shots, dropping one of the members while the other four press forward. BDD tosses her back in the Church as he follows behind closing the door. ‘Dammit!!!! We’re fucked!!! That was our ride home, no one is going to come out here now. I hope you like being interrorgated BDD! They know were here and won’t stop until were dead. Shit!’ ‘Natalia, got a radio?’ ‘Yeah, but no one is going to come out here, not from the base anyways.’ BDD snatches the radio out of her hands and tells her to cover the door. Natalia tries to hold the door shut and dives for cover when it starts splintering from bullets peppering the door. ‘Bossman 1 to Eagle Group, need immediate evac from The Church of Angelic Cross. Over.’ ‘Copy Bossman, ETA 10 minutes. Over.’ ‘Better make it 5, were pinned down by unknown number of assailents, don’t know how long we can hold. Activating locator device. Bossman out!’ ‘Their coming through, I need help!’ ‘Help is on the way Natalia.’ ‘Not from the base!’ ‘No, I always come prepared for a cold reception. I thought your government actually wouldn’t help us so I brought my own support team. They’re coming, they’re coming to rescue us. Dam! Reload! ‘Here! SHIT!!!! I’m hit!!! I’m hit!!!! Fuck me I’m hit!!!’ BDD drags Natalia behind some rubble out of the way while he hides down in a dark corner with his .45 ready. He checks his watch and hope they are early. Three goons enter through the door. He takes aim at one, pulls the trigger and drops him like a wet sack. They turn and start firing in all directions. From about 30 meters out, he gets ready to fire again and watches the both of them drop in quick succession as three people burst through the door wearing black swat outfits.’ ‘BOSSMAN! BOSSMAN YOU IN HERE!!’ BDD walks out from the shadows, lowering his gun to the ground relieved to see them. He tells them to assist Natalia as they quickly treat her gunshot wound in the arm and notice she too had a vest on which saved her life as two shot are imbedded in the Kevlar. ‘She comes with us, the plane ready?’ ‘Yes sir, but we need to hurry, the locals are surrounding the area.’ ‘My mission is done here. Katarina can finally rest now, and I can finally move on from this. Get us out of here!’ Back on the plane, Natalia is strapped to the seat in front of BDD with an IV drip in her other arm and the wounded arm all bandaged up. She starts to come around and once again BDD notices how pretty she really is, even with all the dirt and soot on her face. Guessing around 5-9, 5-10, good build, and finally seeing how voluptuous as she fills out her fatigues nicely. ‘Where am I?’ ‘On my plane. ‘ ‘I can’t be here, I need to get back to my base…..’ ‘Your base was overran, no one got out except us and my crew. Everyone is gone. They think you didn’t make it. Your free in a way. ‘Where are we going…..(saying groggly) ‘The US, just rest, everything will be fine…….everything will be just fine’ (Fade out)
