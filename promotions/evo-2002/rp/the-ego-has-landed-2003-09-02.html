The ego has landed...

2 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-02 Last night felt great, it really did. I wouldn’t go so far as to say that it was orgasmic, but it was pretty bloody close! But let’s back up a little… Nineteen months ago, I decided to take a break from this business. I had had enough. I wanted time to myself and time to my family…two things which you miss dearly when on the road. Thankfully my career hasn’t been riddled with injury after injury but nonetheless, after nine years of constant competing, you body does show a little wear and tare and the pain that comes with the territory. You take the chance to take a break. Being an illustrious athlete that I am, I received a lot of calls from certain promoters who pleaded that I should join their company upon my disbandment of my former employers. Some thought that if I was saying that I was taking a break, it really meant that I was retiring and that I would not step into a wrestling arena ever again. They were concerned that Matt Hunt wouldn’t grace them with their presence and give life to their television shows if I said I was “taking a break”. And who could blame them for thinking that? I’ve thought about retiring for the last few years before the nineteen-month hiatus. I guess everybody does at some point in his or her career. At thirty-four years of age, I’m by no means “over the hill” but then again I’m not exactly a spring chicken. As the months passed while working in my father-in-law’s wrestling academy, the urge struck me a few times. Seeing those kids start from scratch reminded me of my early days in Manchester and how life was tough at the time. Before, I was full-up with the wrestling business…now I’m starving. Course, those kids are not a scratch on me and they probably never will be. I’m the most celebrated athlete some companies have ever seen, along with my impressive accomplishments and charismatic entertainment value. But it made me realise that I wasn’t finished with the wrestling business, not by a sodding country mile. I’ve always been a “keep-fit” fanatic, but now I had to work hard. Heh, at some point I didn’t even know what my goal was, as foolish as that sounds. All I was fixated on was to be in perfect physical condition, so that when the days does come that there is a call…I’d be ready. Well, I’m always ready and my body always has been perfect, but you still have to put the extra hours in, right? Of course, my main priority was to resume my journey in the wrestling world and continue this fantastic Matt Hunt legacy, where another chapter has to be written. But the calls didn’t come. I contacted a few names in the business, so they can spread the word…but I’d get more help from a pregnant farmyard animal with arthritis. I was furious. A year ago these idiots were begging me to reconsider my decision to take a break…now they want nothing to do with me? I actually starting thinking to myself that maybe the wrestling world has grown since I was last in competition. Maybe I’m not the big name I used to be. Maybe the world doesn’t revolve around me and that I wouldn’t be missed. Pfft, yeah right. They can keep their bingo hall wrestling organisations and their poor excuse of a roster. I couldn’t give a hoot, to be perfectly honest. Despite the setback, I continued to work. I worked myself so hard that I almost fell seriously ill. But I soon recovered, as you do. After some serious thinking and discussion with my trainers, I decided that even though I wanted back into the business; I wanted to go somewhere where nobody knows my name…and that they weren’t bloody glad I came, if that makes any sense. I wanted to start on the bottom rung of the ladder and blow everybody else away as I climbed to the top. I’ve done it numerous times before and I’m bloody sure that I’ll do it again. I’m determined like that, you see. I’m very proud of my previous accomplishments and I’m not afraid to mention them every now and again. But I wanted to go to the middle of nowhere and start all over again, to prove a point. Cut out a lot of mumbo jumbo and legal talk and I’m here in eVolution. But imagine my surprise and disgust that a certain name from the past has been plying his trade here for a good while. I felt like packing up my bags and heading back out the door. But I realised that this camache owes me one. I’m not the kind of man to reveal my weaknesses, but this man is my weakness and has been since I haven’t been able to defeat him inside the squared circle all those years ago. The Hype, pfft…some things ever change. But I had to be careful. I don’t want to get obsessed with gaining revenge on this individual, I’ve had that and it took over my mindset. It was all I was able to think about and it was eventually the beginning of the end. So I’ve decided to keep my distance. Let him soak in the adulation of the people, again. Let him celebrate his title victory. Let him have his time at the top. I’m not going anywhere, I’m just getting started and it’ll all be a matter of time before the big balloon that is “hype” is burst into little pieces. So back to last night, where I defeated the “prestigious” eVolved Champion, Scott Evans, in fine fashion. To give the camache some credit, he’s a gutsy, balls little mooch…maybe he’s the eVolved Champion for a reason. Meh, damned if I care. I loved every minute, even when he was dishing out his version of “punishment” (for the record I made it look worse than it did, you know – to give the guy some self confidence). It had been a long time since I stepped inside the ring for some competitive action and I’m glad I made the decision to return. Sure, the people recognised me. I put British Wrestling on the map, for crying out loud. Course, that goes against my “go where I’m unknown” theory, but that the hell. This was my debut match in eVo. The booing was like music in my ears and it felt great. I was giving the slap to that grade-A camache Evans and it was fantastic. I’ve had the starter…now for the main course. Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girls. The Postman has arrived. Let the games begin…
