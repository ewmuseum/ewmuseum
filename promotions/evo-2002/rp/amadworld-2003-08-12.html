Date: 2003-08-12 Ever had that dream where you're falling from a building? Or off the top of a mountain? Or from a cloud? You fall. And it feels oh-so-real. Falling through air. Further and further and then right before you hit the ground... ding. You wake up and find yourself in a cold sweat. Underneath your covers, which are wet with sweat. In the warm and safe confines of your house. That delusion of safety all around you. And thus, you're able to shake the dream off. You're able to lay back down and go back to sleep. Ever heard that urban legend about how if you die in your sleep, you die in real life? Well... lately I've had these reoccuring dreams. Dreams where I', falling down and splattering against the ground, but not dying. Dreams where I'm being shot in the head, but just bleeding. Dreams where I'm being ripped limb from limb... but still breathing. It's like I'm immortal. I don't die. I'm invincible. I'm indestructable. I'm perfect. Perfection is like masturbation without the mess. It's one of the reasons I don't like to sleep. One of the reasons I simply don't sleep. I don't want to be perfect. I don't want to be immortal. I want to know I'm human. I hope I die soon. I call it insomnia, but it's forced. With insomnia, you can't sleep. I can sleep... but I don't want to. So then what's it called? Fighting a heavy eyelid gets lonely. Often, I have conversations with myself. And often? I talk back. I always tell myself that it's sleep playing tricks on me. I'm not insane. I'm not talking back to myself... but some times I wonder. 'Fuck you.' You seem hostile, Sean. 'Hostile?' Quite. Perhaps you should simply lay down...? Get some-- 'Sleep.' It's almost like mind-control. I'm told what to do... and I obey. It's like I don't own myself. It's almost like I'm a slave to my own mind. And what if that's the case? What is that called? Schizophrenia or something of the sort? I had the same dream tonight. And this time? I died. And I find it almos sad but... I found that the dreams in which I do truly die are the best I've ever had. It's a very, very mad world.
