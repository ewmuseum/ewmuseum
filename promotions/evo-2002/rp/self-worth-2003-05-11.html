Self Worth

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, May 11, 2003 Shrouded by darkness our Hero steps out from the void wrapped in his own self-pity. The familiar face from the other night, but somehow different. He’s cleaned up his look, no longer greasy or dirty from his choice to live in the anguish of homelessness. He wears a new clean white button down dress shirt and leaves the cuffs unbuttoned. His shirt is untucked and only buttoned to center of his chest. His long blonde hair hangs freely covering most of his face as he begins to venture out of his own personal Hell. The temptation has finally reached its peak when he steps out from the ally and walks toward the city. All around him are people stricken with poverty, the poor, the sick, the homeless, and many other raffs of society. His eyes are cold as he walks among them, headed for the inner portions of Los Angeles, and wastes no time. Up ahead a law enforcement officer is questioning a homeless man. With the two in heated debate Siege passes by both only to catch a glimpse of the police scanner. “914-25 Code: 187, 301 North Weilsure Dr.” Siege stops panning all throughout his mind, 187, the police code for homicide. By chance could it be a small coincidence in reference to his opponent? People die daily over trivial matters of the heart; love, fame, money, power, all which man desires in his black heart. What story is told behind this day’s code? A love that hurt so much it had to kill? A malicious attack for personal wealth, or maybe someone felt that soul had to be sent Home early. He walks on, but is mentally haunted by images of his past. Matches and foes long forgotten suddenly rush into his mind as he remembers his awful malice. Violent outrages and uproars, crying women watching in horror and horrible screams of men in pain. He staggers as the images begin to come to him faster, each one more vivid than the last, and he runs. Turning into the nearest ally he grabs his face and leans against the building. He moans then grunts as his visions haunt him. He now sees his own demise, the fall of his former self. It made him what he is today, all carefully orchestrated by one. Siege sees his face and his pain turns into his rage. He sees his greatest foe, his very own Dr. Frankenstein, a man known as Equtis Genisis. Siege removes his hand from his face and grits down on his teeth. His fist smashes into the side of the building and he breathes harder and harder. Turning away from the street and heading into the new ally he kicks a small trash can over. He grabs a 2x4 lying on the ground and hacks away at the dented can. The board snaps and Siege is left holding the base; a mere fragment of the whole, just like himself. His breaths are deep and short, and his anger settles. This man’s life has been destroyed. Everything he’s ever known or loved was taken from him. Out of the darkest regions of his soul a powerful hate has been spawned and from that hate a dangerous rage has been born. Man’s controlling nature and black heart gave life to this man. A man like this feels as he has no worth to society, so he sits on the outskirts and watches from afar wondering when he can retake his place among them. Because of this Siege has vowed in his heart that he will set his wrongs right and seek vengeance against those who try to stop him. All the while searching for that one man…Equtis Genisis.
