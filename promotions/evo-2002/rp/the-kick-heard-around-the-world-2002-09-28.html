The kick heard around the world!

28 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept 28 Key Biscayne, Florida Day-time Legends IX, shocked the world. The people will forever be talking about what happened during that fateful tag-team match. Two teams, two legendary teams that has stood head and shoulders above everyone else in the history of Wrestling. The Unlimited and The Syndicate. Each team, internationally renowned, respected and most of all, feared. While the Unlimited combination has changed through the years, their place hasn’t. Much like the Four Horsemen of yesteryear, the individual parts come and go, but the legacy remained in place. The Syndicate, two people, no one thought the combination of a male and female wrestler would ever amount to such greatness, yet, they have shattered every record book known, and held every prestigious tag title in the world. The experiment from hell and like a virus, destroyed everything it touched. So it is no wonder, everyone, the fans, fellow wrestlers, the dirt sheet writers, even ESPN were all interested in the Unlimited/Syndicate tag team match. Two legendary teams, one ring, but…..one surprise, that no one ever saw coming. The mother of all swerves, the double cross of double crosses, the screw job worse than Montreal. Legends IX will go into the history books as the day The Syndicate died. The day, The HeartBreaker herself broke free and unleashed the superkick heard around the world as it fully connected on Big Daddy Dreamer’s chin, knocking him silly, and silencing him, once and for all. The stunned look, the shock, even he never suspected it which made it all the more sweeter. The newest Unlimited member, The HeartBreak Chick, the Evolution High-Impact Champion, The Princess of Hardcore, and a dozen other names, how is she celebrating this monumental occasion? By laying out on the beach in a very skimpy and revealing two-piece swimsuit working on her golden perfection tan, smelling the cool salty sea breeze air and loving every dam minute of it. Will the fans turn on her? Not very likely, she brings what they want, what they pay for every week, action personified, attitude, and most of all, a smoking body that no one in this world has and that is why they scrimp and save every week, just to get close to the HeartBreaker. A little closer to heaven, and beat off every day to her Playboy pictorial spread. She doesn’t mind, she’s quite flattered by it all really, but then again…..perhaps, she really doesn’t give a damn anymore. Why else would she do what she did last week. Perhaps we should ask her herself. HBC is laying out on a large oversized beach towel, enjoying the sun rays before that little storm hits as Evolution’s camera crew catches up with her. Annoyed by the break in her little downtime, she knows they must have a million questions to ask her, and what the hell, why not. It be fun to finally, finally unveil the master plan, nearly a year in the making. She invites them to sit down but not to block the rays as tanning is a serious business. They set up the camera as HBC flicks her tongue, licking her lips in a sultry manner, moistening them up for her latest dialogue. “Well, well, well, if it isn’t Evolution’s crack reporting team trying to get the scoop of what happened last week. So tell me, how does it feel to be kept in the dark, not knowing that this was going to happen? Don’t worry know, no one really knew, no one really knew when the ultimate kick was going to happen, except for me. And the first question out of the gate is why, why would I ever turn on BDD? My partner. My mentor. My trainer. My friend. My lover. Why would I ever do that? Truth is, why not. It’s a ruthless business we are in. There are no such things as TRUE friends in this business. But it always wasn’t like this. Just, realistically, within the past year things started to change. I woke up perhaps, realized what was going on and now for the first time, the world is truly going to get to know the man called BDD, and why, why I didn’t do this sooner. The fans see BDD an entirely different way I see him. They only see the television side of him, not the behind the scenes of him. Lets start at the beginning shall we.” “Six and half years ago I was at a trying point in my life and through the encouragement of a friend, they sent me to BDD in Toronto. Sure, I’ve heard of him prior to that. He’s a dam local hero in Toronto. Can do no wrong kinda thing and sure at first, when I met him, I was intimidated by him. He was a superstar in his own right. A World Champion giving back to the community. Dr. Feelgood. I tried out, and knocked his star female student on her ass, you all knew her as Katarina. Yes, the very Katarina whose life ended inside in Evo ring. At that moment, he agreed to train me personally and I accepted. Hey, he was a World Champion and quite the hottie too I thought. For six months, he trained me, pushed me to my limit and beyond it. He was a demanding teacher yes, but, I’ll give the devil his due, a dam good one. He finally felt I was ready for the big time and got me into the RGWF based upon his word. I didn’t disappoint. Won numerous singles titles, but I never got the hang of tag matches because I had shitty partners. I mean the worse! Finally, we concocted a novel idea, a kick-ass intergender tag team that could compete with the best in the business.” “The looks we got, everyone dismissed the idea right away, until…..until, we won the tag team titles and held them for three months straight. Then they finally caught on. BDD was hailed as a genius. However, it takes two, two people to make a team. The next couple of years, everything was wonderful, we won every title in every federation we were in. We became close, very close. We became intimate. They call him Big Daddy D for a reason you know. He was my first and the only guy I’ve been with. Still the professional life is a hard one, even for us. We weren’t what you would call a traditional relationship. We brought other women into the mix because it was fun for the both of us. We both got what we wanted. Basically, on a personal side. He is a perfect man for me. I have no problems with his personal qualities. I didn’t care when he brought home some blonde for the both of us to fuck her brains out. We got off on that kind of thing. I’d bring home women too for us to do. It was fun. The Commissioner Nichole Kelly was very enjoyable for us. No, he is an endearing human being, someone who I really could spend my life with. However, the problem is the wrestling business side of things.” “I don’t mean Syndicate International, he has done wonders their as well. I’m talking about fulfilling a promise to me, made a long time ago. He has been a World Champion over a dozen times in his life. I celebrated every time he won. But you know what though, he has never, ever, offered me a World Title shot of anykind. He has never offered me a title shot of anykind. World Championship, IC, Hardcore, you name it, he never, ever stepped in the ring with me. In the earlier years, I didn’t mind. I was still learning the game and he was a proven veteran. Yet, the past two years, nothing. Nothing at all. That hurt. That hurt me deeply because he wasn’t showing me the proper respect that I deserved. Don’t you think D, your star pupil, your partner can be, perhaps, even better, than you. The fact of the matter is, that is precisely the problem. I am better than him now and he doesn’t either, or want to admit that.” “I look back to the FWA. BDD, a tag team champion, with me I might add. That is all, that is the only title he won their. I won the Intercontinental, the #2 belt in the fed, and the Hardcore title. I made more of a mark than he did, and he for some reason, couldn’t accept or admit that. Then, EDW. Once again, we win the tag titles. No shocker. He wins the Platinum Championship in a grueling tournament and defeated Xander in the finals. Do I ever get a shot at his title? No! Instead I’m practically forced to accept these dam suicidal career-ending matches to remain on the PPV. While BDD is living the high-life, I’m wrestling in Glass Coffin matches, hardcore matches, through the streets of New York City just so I can get my trinket championships. Sure, three-time Suicidal and two-time Canadian champion is nice, but dammit, I deserve better than that! I deserved a shot at your gold D! Not risking my career like that. You owed me that much! You owed me that much! But no! Still nothing. So, I decided at that moment, to put you to the test. To see if deep down I was right about you.” “I made a secret phonecall to a certain member of The Unlimited back while we were in EDW. Funny, we were the ones, you and I to hold them in check, and now, I’m forced to use them to prove a point. I made a phonecall, two weeks before your Universal title shot against Malcom King, who as you know, was a member of the Unlimited at that time. I’m the one who arranged the outside interference so that you were able to defeat Malcom King and win the Universal Championship. And I sat by waiting once again, for you to live up to your promise of granting me a world title shot, and again…….NOTHING!!! NOTHING FROM YOU!!!! My heart, my spirit, broke that day. I’ve made a living breaking people’s hearts and on that day, you broke my heart. I thought we were passionate about what we do. You always had an open match against me and willfully never took it. I wanted to wrestle you. I wanted to prove to you, to everyone, that I’m not just in your shadow, but equal if not better than you and you never gave me that chance. No one has ever broken the heartbreaker before, but you did. You’re the only one in this world ever to cause me such pain that I cried sometimes at night. Did you know that? Did you know that D? Of course you didn’t! Why would you.” “On that fateful day, when you finally lost the EDW Universal Championship, I knew I had to take matters into my own hands. It showed me, without question, you would never give me what I truly wanted. It was then I launched this elaborate plan to get even with you once and for all. But I will admit, I had doubts. I thought perhaps, maybe, just maybe, you would come to understand what I was going though, but you didn’t. Then, it was time to finally make you hurt. It was time to break your heart! And realistically, it was simple. You fell for my plan just like every other superstar that crossed my path. All I had to do was find that little chink in your armor. It was difficult, but you finally revealed it with Katarina. Your little side love interest. You were so easily exploitable, it was just a matter of pushing the right buttons. And little Jamal. How it fit so perfectly into my plans of brainwashing you. It was just so perfect. The perfectly trained killing machine you were, grew of all things, a conscious. Gone was the killer instinct, that was your weakness. You felt sorry for Katarina, dying by all purposes, by your hands. It was your crossface that did the final damage. Hence, your guilt ridden conscious was my playtoy.” “For one week though, I thought you nearly had me going, when Sovereign gored me, but your compassion once again came through. I knew then, you were mine and playing you like the ultimate puppetmaster that I am. You did everything according to my design. And you still will. The Syndicate is dead and buried by my hands. How everyone has tried to accomplish that feat only to find it destroyed from within. You were played for a monumental sucker. How does it feel D? How does it feel to be a chump! And now Dreamer, you get to face The Unlimited of epic proportions. You get to face your worse nightmare, The HeartBreak Chick staring across from you. No one knows your mat game better than I do. No one knows you better than I do. Your tough D, you were a great champion, key word being WERE. But no longer will you hold me back, no longer will you keep my ability under your tight leash. Now D, now its my time to shine and make no mistake Dreamer, when we face, I’ll have nothing but contempt for you. Quite frankly D, it was fun knocking you out with my Superkick, it will even be funner when you suffer further at my hands.” “This time D, you’ll be the one down on all fours, begging me to stop, begging me to end the suffering your going through. And all I’ll do, is spit right in your face and tear you a new one. The only thing I’m going to miss from all this, is the great sex from you. But hey, there are plenty of people awaiting in the wings for me. Take a long hard look D. Because this is the last time, you’ll ever see me, ever see my long legs wrapped around you, ever feel me, that warm moist inside between my legs. The last time you’ll ever feel my succulent lips on your throbbing member. The last time you’ll ever feel my sweet ass. The last time D. The last time. Now, all you’ll feel is my pain, my fist wrapping your skull and my boot stuffed in places you never thought possible. This isn’t my fault D, its yours. All I wanted was one title shot, just one, showing to the world, I was better than you and could be a World Champion. You took that away from me, and so now, I will take the business from you.” “Oh yeah, one more thing I must do. In regards to your position within Syndicate International…….YOUR FIRED!!!!’ HBC tells the cameracrew to take a hike as she gets back to her serious tanning session.
