Looking Back..

26 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-26 Scott didn't know what he would become in his life as a child he had no sense of direction he always thought his brother would go on to be the successful one, but that didn't happen. That destiny was placed upon Scott's shoulders when Adam died before his brother's, his best friend's eyes. Scott always thought that the final sentence to be uttered by Adam was as insignificant as where they were but now as he looks back he realizes that it is more than insignificant and that it isn't a coincidence that his wife and child died the next morning after his brother's death. There's was something that burned brighter and deeper within Scott that drove him to strive to do what his brother never had the chance to and his father couldn't. He strove to become the best he could before time would take its toll, he needed to compete in the present he could no longer use his anger about the past to drive him that was what he had done and it made him look unproffessional compared to those that set the standard at which he competes. All of this only goes to prove Scott's motto which had always been life goes on as far back as he could remember. He always stood on both his feet not falling no matter how hard the burden had become, he proved he could stick with it and now as goes into his first title defense the only thought is what his brother told before he died, 'You've got to win.'
