Full Circle 1:1

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-13 Monica Angel is having a sitdown interview with the current Evolved Champion Malcolm Luther Xavier. He seems a little agitated as he looks back and forth between Monica and the title belt..... MA:'First off MLX welcome.....' MLX:'Thanks for having me.' MA:'I would be remiss if I didn't ask you if you had any regret on what happened to fiona Preston and your role in what turned out to be her last match?' MLX:'Regret?.....What in the hell for? Am I responsible because she decided to refuse medical attention? It is my job to hurt people...that's what I do for a living. She was breathing when she left the match....in my opinion if she didn't love herself enough to get the help she needed it's not my problem.' Monica a little uneasy about his answer decides to move on...... MA:'Okay let's move onto someone that is YOUR problem......Malcolm King, is it true what he revealed at legends?' MLX pauses for a minute before looking at the title and then back at Monica..... MLX:'Yes' MA:'Your thoughts?' MLX:'About what my career being a lie? I knew the deal when I signed on.' MA:'Care to explain?' MLX:'You mean tell the truth about The Urban Legend? Sure seeing as how King wants it out there. This whole saga started over 15 years ago. We were students at the Connecticut Wrestling Academy run by current Evolution referee William Louise....' MA:'Your father...' Monica interuptted with..... MLX:'Yes; my father......King, or should I say Demetrius. Come to think about it I never really did catch his last name as he's always insisted we call him 'D'. Anyway King, myself, his cousin Shadrick/Black Havoc/Priest RavensEcho, the men you have come to know as Dolo Bentswinger,1001 Holds, Wideload, Damien Legend/The Tone were all apart of my father's inaugural class. King had an idea that would benefit himself, me and his cousin...' MA:'What was this idea?' MLX:'Well we were all struggling to make it on the independant scene, King had limited success as Demetrius Echo or Echo, myself I had to try to live up to my fathers legacy as he was an excellent wrestler in the deep south territories, and Shadrick never really had a passion for the business but liked beating up people. That's when D had the idea of creating the character of MALCOLM KING; he proposed it as a franchise wrestler similar to McDonalds...an Urban Legend in every sense of the word, a man who literally can be three places at once....' MA:'Seemed to have worked well....' MLX:'Yeah he was successful....you see D loves the business and when it was time to give up being Malcolm, he couldn't. The original plan was to rotate the Malcolm King character amongst ourselves. However D kept on getting himself in situations which warrented he play the role. As consulation I was brought in as his partner Xavier Citizen and Shadrick developed the Black Havoc character and later Luther Wisdom stand in for promo purposes....but all the inring work of Malcolm King, Xavier Citizen, and Luther Wisdom was infact D.' MA:'So what was the problem......' MLX:'He cost me my career....I was always the next in line to be Malcolm King, hell the only reason there was an Xavier Citizen was due to D's incompetance! Everything we planned was almost flushed down the drain when he lost the name Malcolm King...' MA:'Lost it?' MLX:'Yes....lost it to SOVEREIGN and his lacky BOB in OSW.' MA:'Ahhhh I remember that....' MLX:'So do I because that was the beginning of the end of my career.....' to be continued......
