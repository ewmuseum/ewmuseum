No repenting. No forgiveness. No penance. Only punishment

12 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-01-03 It was a strange night, Wednesday Night. No one probably saw it coming. No one figured it was going to happen but it did. And after it happened the world wasn’t the same as it was before. I’m sure you all think that I’m talking about L2J. And although I hold a deep sorrow for what happened to L2J on Wednesday night, because he was my friend I will be quick to say that no, it isn’t L that I’m referring to. It is Sovereign. Sovereign returned on Wednesday Night. Oh, I’m sure you all are saying... but where did he go? And although that is a question with a lot of answers let me get to the point about a few things. Recently, and I’m sure that if you all look back to about a month and a bit ago something happened to Sovereign. He changed, he lost his focus all at the hands of Impact when he lost the Universal Title to the late L2J inside the Tier’s of Evolution. Sovereign’s mind became clouded, so many feelings forming in his brain. So many thoughts racing through his mind that he took his eyes off of what he is. He is a monster. He’s been told that, and for a long time he never believed it until he won the Universal Title on August the 14th in the year 2002. That was 4 months ago, and when Impact decided to take it upon himself to cost Sovereign that title, Sovereign’s world was turned upside down. So many things had changed. His wife had returned, pretending to be Impact’s ally, but all along her plan was to aid Sovereign. Sovereign, not sure about anything, unsure if he could trust his woman. Not sure if he was able to fulfil that moniker, the Human Monster. At Nightmare Before Christmas he proved that he is the Human Monster. And with or without Leya’s aid in the match it is clear that Sovereign was not far from victory over Impact. That was the 1st PPV that he wrestled in eVo that he did not Main Event. He defeated Impact, and this week on Legends XX Sovereign officially made what he liked to call in his own world a return to remember. Jason Steele, Ryan Flesco were the 1st to victims in Sovereign’s return to his Crusade of Violence against Evolution. Both men’s careers here in Evolution are over at least, nothing to say for the injuries that both sustained because of Sovereign, who came down to the ring and destroyed both men in front of the entire arena. Then it was Impact. Once again, Sovereign had the Hollywood Monster outsmarted, tricking him into The Cell a structure banned and not unfamiliar to Sovereign where he dismantled Impact slowly and painfully, until one loan soul cried out that it was enough. One man who’s seen the damage that Sovereign can create and didn’t seem to want it to go on any further. Jason Jameson. As Narrator Jason I suppose I have questions that you need to ask yourself. Why? Sovereign hadn’t done anything to you? Of course perhaps it is only a matter of time before he cuts down each and every man and woman that works under this flag we call eVo. Why risk it? You had your chance when you had back up in The Notorious, now a name from eVo’s past that no longer lives. So now you pick a fight with the most dangerous man in sports entertainment? What is it you are after? I personally Jason don’t doubt your ability one bit. I think you are outstanding, I think you are a superstar. And I truly hope you are the answer to the prayers of so many to finally find that knight in shining armour to slay the evil dragon known as Sovereign. Pardon me if I sound hopeful. Because when there is no hope is when normal men look for that one glimmer of hope in the exceptional men that we look up to. We look for Heros. Someone needs to stop him, this time some HAS to stop him, or else it will go so far that no one will ever be able to come back. The scene fades in, it’s a dark room, nothing really that stands out at you except for the shadowed figure of Sovereign, sitting in the middle of the floor surrounded by nothing that can clearly be made out. His hair hangs about his face, his shoulders rise and fall with the rhythm of his breathing as you can hear and see his cold breath floating into the air. It seems now that you must be in some cold room somewhere. Perhaps a meat room or something. Of course you cannot tell and it doesn’t really matter a lot to you once his voice, that deranged voice cracks the silence like a dart through the air. It was a night to remember. A night that no one will ever forget. From start to the end of Legends Twenty the men and women of Evolution put on a show to remember. I know I won’t forget it. The feeling of revival. I felt born again that night. When my two greatest enemies were vanquished by the time the cameras stopped rolling I was filled with a sense of jubilation. Impact, the man that came back from the grave so to speak returned at Rise of the Legends to rob me of my identity as Universal Champion. At Nightmare Before Christmas I ruined him, rejoined by my loving wife, my life partner I sent Impact the message that his return was folly. It was a foolish mistake, from when I took him all over the arena, till when I broke that 2 by 4 with Barbed wire over his empty and thick skull I thought him the lesson I thought he’d not soon forget. But apparently I hit him so hard that he forgot the pain I put him through twice now. He wanted more. He wanted redemption. He wanted to be a hero. But only fools are hero’s these days. And once again his ambition got the better of him. Agreeing to fight me within the confines of the Cell and there, in those minutes following the opening bell I ended that man’s career. He is finished. He is nothing. He will never come back. It is over. The one man that could take my title from me, Impact. The one man that people said could end my Crusade. The one man that would end the reign of terror is no more then a pathetic moral human being. He isn’t a hero. He is a failure, the ultimate failure. Now who will you send, Jason Jameson? Is he the new Hero sent to slay the demon Sovereign? Is he the one man now? After Kage, L2J and Impact tried this Jameson is going to be the one to succeed? Who is he to get involved in my business, he came down to that Cell and he tried to save Impact, a man that deserves nothing more then to be tossed in the ditch when you drive down the highway at top speed. He is trash, garbage, human waist. And now this young punk Jason Jameson comes from no where and attacks me with his force, with his power, his speed and a steel chair. I was shocked that he would try. I was stunned at the weakness of his blows. He is nothing. Jason Jameson, you have stuck your nose into the business of a man that you are best to leave well enough alone. Yet the problem now is that you’ve already gone to far. You have started it, it has begun and all of your pain and suffering that you’ve endured all your life will be nothing compared to what I will do to you in the coming weeks. You’ll wish that you never decided to be that hero. You will wish that you never watched on the monitor as I choked Impact to unconsciousness. Wish you never saw him backstage getting himself prepared for his redemption that ended in his demise. And now you sit at home, or in a gym, or at a desk pouring over old letters and wondering what your next move will be. How to counter Sovereign before he acts upon you. You know that you are over your head. How in the hell are you going to survive you might ask yourself. The answer is simple, it’s that you won’t Jameson. You might as well have put a gun against your head and pulled the trigger then to face off against me like you did on Legends. To interrupt my fun, that is the cardinal sin my young friend. From that sin there is no repenting, no forgiveness, no penance, only punishment. As Jameson your first taste of what you have gotten yourself into will be this Wednesday at Legends 19. You team with Scott Frost and face myself and the current Universal Champion Shocka, and while Shocka might currently be considered the “Top Man” we all know who the best in this business is and that is me. I am the most dangerous man in wrestling today and you will find out 1st hand this coming week. Sovereign whips back his head, hair flies out of his face and he turns his head now facing the camera. The lighting only adds to atmosphere as you can’t make out a lot of his features but his eyes, focused upon the camera, the black and white shot as always with Sovereign, his cold breath floats up into the air as he takes in several deep breaths in his pause. Shocka, you and I must be partners this coming week. And although I do not work well with others this time I’m going to try and make an exception in your case. It isn’t because you are the Universal champion as much as it is that I’d rather face you when you were 100 percent. I have a bit of a history of not playing nice with my partners and several times they often leave the match in worse shape then the people I’m supposed to be fighting. I do know how to wrestle tag matches Shocka, I’m a former tag team champion in my past and I do know how to co-operate. I just opt not to most of the time. That being said I know you have personal issues with Scott Frost and that is all well and good. I hope that you can settle those this coming week as I will be trying to convince Mr. Jameson that getting into my business is not something anyone should do without an invitation. You see Shocka, you are the Universal Champion, something that I once was and that I shall once again be. And if I become Universal Champion at your expense then fine, I personally don’t care. All that matters to me Shocka is that someone has MY title belt over their shoulder and that burns a hole in my heart to know that I was robbed of that belt and never given a rematch. Now wether you are the sort of man that would offer me that match or not is beside the question. But I will beat down each and every single challenger to that title until you have no choice but to show the world if you really are the “Number 1 Stunna” or if you are just a paper champion like L2J was. As for Scott Frost, the man that once was the forefront of the now dead Notorious. The man that for some reason has been given the opportunity over the man that was raped of his title and never given the chance to have a say in a rematch. Yes Scott Frost I’m bitter about it and I am pissed off and I envy that you were given the opportunity over me. But Cameron Griffin’s idea of proper and fair treatment seems to fly out the window whenever it deals with Sovereign these days and it always has. And regardless of your issues with Shocka or New-TV I will be watching you very closely Scott Frost. Because I want what you have, I want that spot back. And if I have to beat you to within a thumbs length of your death this Wednesday coming then I sure as hell will. If on Wednesday you fight for the honor and memory of L2J then I will to. My memory of him is the man that got everything for not earning a damn thing. I hate him, all he stood for and I’ll use that hatred as the fuel to burn you and Jameson alive in that ring Sovereign turns his head away from the camera. Cold frosty breath floats from his lips. See you all in St. Louis The camera fades to black slowly, then fades to the Legends Logo
