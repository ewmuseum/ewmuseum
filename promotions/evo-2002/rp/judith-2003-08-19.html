Date: 2003-08-19 It must be Tuesday because Bob is wearing his cornflower blue apron. THe smells of greasy, awful food and the sounds of the city surround me. I sit by a window. Watchng people pass. Observing them. There goes a mother with her baby. Skinny and made to look like Calvin Klein or Tommy Hilfiger tells her to look. I'm disgusted, but I wouldn't mind fucking her right about now. The baby looks like it's on it's way to a Diaper commercial. I feel even moe sick. A man in a Tool shirt passes. His wrist wrapped with the usual 'punk' black leather spiked bracelets. His hair had long, blue bangs while the sides, top, and back were shaved. Just the bangs. A certified freak, or so society tells us. He would hate you. 'He' referring to the 'freak' that just passed. To be absolutley honest? I'm observing these people in hopes one will catch my interest. Maybe a friend would be nice. Maybe. I mean, I have Bob... but honestly? I don't truly consider Bob a friend. More of... a butler. Maybe the uncle that lives on my couch and you can't ask to leave. And I suppose the feeling is mutual. We've no need for friends. Right. I look down at the table. A dollar lays beneath my left hand while I hold a pen in the other. The pens red as I draw a little moustache across George Washington's face. I always wrote things on money. It's a federal offense, but money is the only paper people won't throw away. If you want the world to know something, you write it on money. SLAVE. Write it in big, bold, letters. Make sure people can notice it. Or make it more subtle if you want. SLUT written right across George Washington's head. You can't buy happiness. When you make money, ask yourself if you enjoyed making that little piece of paper. Are you fulfilled now? Has your life been given more meaning ever since you gained that one dollar? MORE THAN 370 FETUSES DIE EACH YEAR IN ACCIDENTS ON AMERICAN HIGHWAYS along the border. A girl walks into the diner. ONE NATION UNDER DOG. She's a good looking girl. IN DOG WE TRUST. She sits down at the booth behind me. THIS IS COUNTERFEIT. And I'm compelled... IF YOU WANT A GOOD TIME CALL [detail withheld]. ASK FOR PRESIDENT BUSH. I look over my shoulder at her... WE ARE SLAVES. 'Hi.' I say. Nervous. The voice is protesting. WE ARE DRONES TO THE GOVERNMENT. 'Hi.' She replies. Not smiling. A cigarette hangs limply from between her lips. We don't need her. '...I'm Sean. Who are you?' A quick cock of her eyebrow. '....Judith.'
