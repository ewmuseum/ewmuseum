Like Cutting Down A Tree - 1

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-12 I can hear a car pulling up. It's got that same Pontius sound I heard leave. It didn't distract me from the book though. He mutters some profanities, as he unlocks the door, saying I'm becoming paranoid. Hi, I say. His back doesn't answer, as he slaughters the black animal on the meat hook next to the door. 'What are you reading?', he asks. Same thing as before, I say. I got a call from someone earlier. He didn't tell me too much. I'm gonna need a ride from you tomorrow or something. 'Why?' I need to go somewhere. Guy sounded so weird, out of place or something. You'd think somebody died. Gave me an address I needed to go to. And I'm not getting my license re-newed until next week. Do you know this guy or something, he asks, plomping on the couch across from me. No, I say. So why go? 'Something about the way he sounded I guess.' I know where this is. I look up from my book, and see him glancing over the paper that I wrote the address on. 'And that means that someone really did die.' 'Why do you say that?' 'This is the morgue. He sounded edgy because someone died. If they're calling you in, it's probably because you're going to be identifying a body.' Well fuck me.
