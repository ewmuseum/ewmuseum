Date: 2003-05-25 Smoke drifted from his lit Marlboro in pale rafters. The crumpled pack sat on the worn desk next to him, to his left, but he hadn’t been able to see out of his left eye since some time last night. Alex winced at the thought of the prior evening’s events and how an awkward situation had turned into a bad one. Alex was dressed up- pretty fly for a white guy –in his mind. The multicolored shirt swirled in an intricate pattern that formed the outline to a smoky dragon, his tight leather pants matched perfectly with his pointed Louis Vittan boots. Alex was smokin’. He was hot to trot. All you ladies out there watch out, because Alex S. was on the prowl. Alex was in town, Hamline, Minnesota to be exact for only a few nights and being the fly fella that he was he had plans to hook up with some of that bomb cootchie all of his black friends were raving about. He flicked his cell phone out the breast pocket of his shirt and scanned the list of names that were in his phone book. He raised one cultured eyebrow at one particular name, mouth bowing in a delicious smile. Deborah M.. He had dated her for about three months last summer just as Deb was graduating high school and Alex himself was entering his third year of college. They had met through a mutual friend and while they didn’t exactly hit it off famously things were soon in the works for serious petting and tonguing. Alex knew his body looked good, and the young girl couldn’t really resist the attraction of an older man showing her so much positive attention, or his washboard abs and his tight chest. Alex S. knew he was the man and that it would only be a matter of time before he fucked the young and impressionable Deborah M. retarded. But that never really came about. She rejected most of his advances, and while she wasn’t a virgin Ms. M. only grudgingly gave him a hand job in the front seat of his Saturn towards the end of the relationship. Deb had broken it off and they had both promised to remain friends and keep in touch. ‘Well, sweetie’, thought Alex as he hit the “send” button on his chic cell, ‘We’re going to be keepin’ in touch tonight.’ The cell buzzed in his ear three times before a cheery feminine voice picked up. ‘Hello?’ ‘Hey, sugar, you’ll never guess who this is.’ Alex smirked, playing it smooth. A little neon sign went off in the dim recesses of his mind “Game on”. ‘Well…’ ‘It’s Alex, sugar!’ He didn’t allow her a chance to reply, ‘What are you doing this evening?’ ‘Oh hey Alex.’ Not the excited reaction he had expected, but it was something he could work with. ‘What’s up?’ ‘Nadda mucho. Just wanted to know what you had on tap for this evening. I’m in town doing a camp and had the night off. Thought you might want to kick around with me for a bit. Where are you anyway?’ Deb told him and he was pleased to note that she was less than ten minutes away from him via car, which the firm that was hosting the camp had so graciously rented for him and some of the other counselor’s. ‘I’ll be over there in ten and we can figure out what to do after we catch up.’ He buzzed into his cell, grinning madly into the humid night hands already digging the car keys out of his pocket.’ ‘Sure, you can meet my bo-“ *click* Alex’s phone cut out and he frowned down at the luminent face of his cell. He saw that the battery had died and he shrugged the thought away just as soon as he tossed the phone into the leather passengers seat moments before he gunned the engine to life. Alex wondered who Deb wanted him to meet as he turned out onto Como Ave., but pushed the thought away as he began to ponder whether or not he would be able to loosen Deb up enough to get a little dome from the wench.
