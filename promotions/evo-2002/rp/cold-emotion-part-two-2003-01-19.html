Cold Emotion... part two.

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in the hostile environment that was left off in. Morpheus pants heavily, holding back his uncontrollable immense rage. Morpheus holding his head up with one hand and clenching his fist with the other, as his right eye twitches nervously. Questionable, deranged thoughts cross the mind of Morpheus Omega and he breathes deeply and barks out 'Why!' breathing deeply again 'Do!' breathing more deeply 'People!' breathing very deeply and his eye twitches again 'Have to!' he hesitates. Morpheus breathes deeply and cracks himself in the jaw as hard as he possibly can, he shakes his head and screams 'FUCK WITH ME!!!' The words echo through the room and upon them finishing someone appears at the doorway, standing at the entrance to the room, the cameras pan revealing Sky Night in the doorway with a very concerned look on her beautiful face. Sky Night remains quiet and locks sight on Morpheus. Silence fills the air, with the only sound heard is the loud panting of Morpheus. Suspension building between the two of them. Sky Night walks further into the room and strangely stares into the eyes of Morpheus without worry or fears. Sky Night takes it upon herself to break the silence and speaks 'Morpheus... Tell me what is wrong!' Sky demands with sound of sincerity. Oddly, Morpheus remains staring off into nowhere almost like he is staring right through Sky and ignoring her presence. Morpheus using selective attention as he continues to breath heavily. The look on Sky's face becomes very concerned and waves her hand in front of his face and screams 'Snap the hell out of this, NOW!' The arm of Sky Night extends back and swings forward with her hand flat out to slap the face of Morpheus. At the last second her wrist is caught in the tight grasps of Morpheus, hesitating... Morpheus releases the hand of Sky Night who looked slightly afraid for a second. Sky Night returns to standing at comfort and ease. 'Just leave me to myself... This is no ones business but my own!' Morpheus demands with eyes locked on Sky Night! Shaking slightly with the deep breathes, the eye of Morpheus twitches again and he screams 'Leave me alone!!!' The words echo loudly through the room. Turning her head to the side slightly like she was going to turn and leave, but hesitated. Sky Night has an interesting concerned kind of grin arched across her face. 'You know for the deadliest athlete in the game, you look like one sorry pathetic loser right now!' Sky proclaims and shakes her head disapprovingly. With a loud sigh, Sky Night goes to turn and exit the room and something catches glimpse to her eye, she walks behind Morpheus and finds the crumpled up paper ball and examines it in her hand and looks back and forth, at the paper ball and Morpheus. Walking with a smooth, sexy stride she returns and approaches Morpheus yet again, this time with an unusual smile. Standing impatiently and hesitating from speaking until he acknowledges her again. Impatiently and out of nowhere Sky Night speaks 'What is this?' holding the paper ball in her hand and in front of Morpheus face. Morpheus quickly snatches the paper but barely misses the grab. Sky Night holds the paper higher above her head and demands once again 'What the hell is this?' Awkward silence fills the air again which is fitting for the awkward moment. Sky once again very impatiently speaks once more 'If you are not going to tell me, then I will open it and find out myself! 'Its Nothing!' Morpheus mumbles with his head down. Sky Night starts unfolding each crease slowly to get reaction out of Morpheus. No reaction until just about time of pulling the creases straight to read. Morpheus shoots up on his feet, towering over Sky Night and snatches the piece of paper from her hand. Morpheus snarls in the face of Sky Night! 'This is my business and we can discuss this later. I just need time to think and Im not in the mood for your company right now!' Breathing deeply and he continues 'You just have to take my word on this Sky!' Morpheus nods touching back with reality for a minute. An angry scowl comes across the face of Sky as she turns and curses while exiting the room 'My fucking god!' she slams the side of the wall and grabs the handle of the door. Morpheus screams loudly 'Sky!' The door slams shut loud and the mumbling of 'Go fuck yourself' are heard from down the hallway. Morpheus looks real impressed and continues breathing deeply with his eyes wide open with that sick look. The scene fades to Morpheus examining the document thoroughly.. The scene fades to the mentally unstable Morpheus Omega. Scene Closes...
