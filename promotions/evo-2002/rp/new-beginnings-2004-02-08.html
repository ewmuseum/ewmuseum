Date: 2004-02-08 NOTE: This is the first time that I posted a REALLY LATE RP! But hey, it's up! Enjoy! Things are about to change here in the eVo... Changes of massive proportions... A psycho entering for one purpose----to become the best. Why should I belong here, you ask? Well, since the whole 4WW mess happened, people wondered if I could ever step foot into another wrestling ring again. Until now. Now, the eVo is my playground, my stomping grounds. Get ready for a new breed of eVolution, ladies and gentlemen, there’s no way you can stop it. Resistance is futile! The time is here... The time is now! January 26, 2004 Springfield, Illinois The Kreed Residence “What do you mean I’m fired, Fey?!” I said, almost yelling into the phone. My mind race as I listened to Fey’s explanation about my upsetting Patti A., Eliza M. and Tyler B., members of the elite federation, 4 Winds Wrestling. My expression hardens with anger. “Apparently, my opinions aren’t allowed in your tight-knit fed...All I made was some comments about some fucking angle and those three come running to Mommy...Spare me with your apologies. You may say that I’m terminated, but I say I quit your fucking company! So, fuck you, fuck your husband and fuck your so-called ‘good name’ of the 4WW!” I slammed the phone down and got up, pacing a bit, trying to calm myself down. The audacity of her! I get fired over some stupid comments I made over some stupid angle. I was right----my opinions aren’t allowed in that fed. They never have been in the past and they aren’t now. Fuck them, I thought to myself. You don’t need them. They need you more than you need them. You helped build that company to what it is today. Out of the center bedroom on the far side of the house, exits Uriko Yakama, my new wife. She is carrying a blanket, rubbing her eyes and lets out a yawn. She notices the angered expression on my face. She’s not your traditional Japanese woman who can barely get to the height of five feet four inches. No, far from that. She’s a five-foot-ten, weighing one hundred and sixty pounds, with shoulder-length black hair, green eyes and a slightly tanned complexion. Her build is mostly is athletic, with a well-defined muscular physique, sculpted to perfection. My wife... Ever since last Monday, we’ve been married. We tied the knot at Greg Mussilano’s place, a large, three-floor house on the “richer” side of Springfield. Uriko stares at the reflection in the mirror for a long time. Something’s amiss. She recognizes the person inside the reflection... It’s her...isn’t it? She’s not sure. Seeing her dolled up in makeup and dressed in a pretty light pink wedding dress is something she hasn’t seen before. Being dolled up in makeup is one thing, but wearing a wedding dress is another. Next to her, sitting on the edge of the toilet, is Sophia Mussilano, Greg Mussilano’s wife. She’s a pretty brunette, about the same height as Uriko. She’s trying desperately to break in the three-inch pink high-heel shoes. Even though Sophia wears a size ten, the attempt to break in a pair of size-nine shoes is a bitch to break in. She removes the shoe off her foot and looks at Uriko. “I’m sorry, Uriko,” she said, staring up at her. “These things aren’t budging. It would be better if my feet were the same size as yours.” She pauses for a moment, looking at Uriko. “Uriko? Are you okay?” Uriko made no reply, but continues to stare into her reflection. What if she doesn’t make a good wife for Simon? What if Simon loses all his love for her because she screwed something up? What will she do then? Will she move back to Japan and find someone who will truly love her? Can she find someone there who would truly love her? Her reverie is instantly broken after Sophia’s next words. “Earth to Uriko. Come in, girl!” She breaks her gaze off the reflection and looks down at Sophia, somewhat in a trance. “Huh?” “Are you okay, honey?” she asked, with a edge of concern in her voice. “If you have stared into the mirror for much longer, you’d burn a hole through it.” Uriko manages a weak chuckle and smiles at Sophia. “I’m a little nervous, I guess,” she said, her voice a bit shaky. Her eyes moisten with tears. She tries to fight them back, but they are threatening to overwhelm her. Sophia notices this right away. “I know how you feel, honey,” she said, getting up and looking at her dead in her eyes. “When I got married to Greg, I felt exactly the way you did. I wasn’t sure if I would be a good enough wife to Greg. Now, seven years later, we’re still married. All my worries went away and I’m still in love with my husband.” “But I’m not sure if I can do this. I might be making a mistake.” “Trust me, Uriko, if you really love Simon and he really loves you, things will work out. Sure, you’ll have your fights here and there. A lot of married couples do! Hell, Greg and I had a little argument about how the wedding should go last week!” She finishes with a giggle. She notices that Uriko is feeling better. She gives Uriko a hug, holding her for a little while. “You’ll do fine, Uriko. I know you will.” Uriko squeezes her tight. All her fears and worries are instantly gone. She looks at Sophia and gives her a kiss on the cheek. Sophia places her hand on the cheek, trying not to blush. “What was that for?” “For helping me,” Uriko replied, a smile curls the ends of her mouth. “I’m ready to go down to meet my husband-to-be.” “Good.” She looks at Uriko’s wedding shoes, picks them up and shows them to her. “You wanna wear these?” “Nah,” she said, curling up her nose mockingly. “Those things hurt my feet. I’ll go barefoot!” “Whatever floats your boat, kid,” Sophia replied with a smile. Uriko looks at her with a strange look in her eye. “What did you say?” Sophia laughs. “Never mind,” she replies. “C’mon, let’s not keep your man waiting.” Uriko nods in agreement and they both exit the bathroom and going down the long corridor. They make it towards the head of the steps, surveying the crowd----of about one hundred people----below. Uriko’s gaze catches a man wearing a black tuxedo, with long, thick Jamaican dreadlocks. She slowly walks down the steps, anticipating wedding bliss... “Is there something wrong, sweetie?” Uriko said, after another yawn, blinking the sleepers out of her eyes. She knows when I’m upset, she’ll approach the matter gently. I turn to face her, my rage building up by the second. “That bitch fired my ass over something that could have been resolved in a professional manner,” I said, keeping my temper in check. The last thing I need is to blow up at her over something that’s not her fault. She looked at me for a moment. Fired? What would he be fired for? “What happened?” she asked. “Was it something you said?” “Yes,” I replied. There’s no point into lying about it. “I made some comments about an angle Tyler B., Patti A. and Eliza M. are involved in. I guess they heard my comments and reported it to the ‘Head Bitch in Charge.’” “You do you think you’ll come back in if you explained your actions?” I shook my head. “I highly doubt that,” I said, sighing. “Fey says that I’m not a team player. Bullshit! I’ve done my share of angles and feuds. What has she done? Practically nothing. All she did was sit on her ass and run half of the fucking helm, and attempting to pull off a feud with Patti, which went down the tubes anyway.” After that was said, the phone rang. I checked the Caller ID, which read: UNKNOWN NAME UNKNOWN NUMBER on it. I let out a sigh and picked up the phone to answer it: “Hello?” I listened for a little bit. “Hello, Mr. Somers. What can I do for you? Well, as of now, I’ve been fired from the 4WW...You want to hire me? That’s great! When...No, I can’t make the trip down there tonight, I have a concert show at nine, but I’ll drive down there tomorrow...Starting on the Tainted Love Pay-Per-View is fine. Who is my opponent? Kira? Is that a female? Okay. That’ll work then. Okay, you too. Bye.” Uriko’s looks at me, her eyes register curiosity. “What was that all about?” I grinned. “That was Daniel Somers, owner of the eVo, and he has hired me to his federation.” Uriko smiles. “That’s great, honey!” She runs up to me and gives me a hug. “When do you start?” “On the eighth of February, which is their Tainted Love Pay-Per-View. Would you like to be my valet?” She shook her head instantly. “Hell no! I didn’t enjoy my time there in the 4WW, so I’m gonna stay here. Besides, I’m your wife now, and I can stay behind, watch the house and root for you in the comfort of our home.” She pauses for a moment. “Who’s your opponent?” “Some woman named Kira,” I replied, half grinning at her. She looks at me, knowing exactly what I’m thinking. “You’re gonna play with her, are ya? Like how you and I wrestle in the bedroom.” “Something like that,” I replied, “but I’m going to have to hurt her as well. Sure, she’s a woman, but that doesn’t mean I’ll won’t be aggressive. She is my opponent, woman or no.” “This match might be a turn-on for me, then,” Uriko said, feeling the juices flowing in her body, feeling the anticipation of...well, I think you can all figure that part out on your now. She looks over at the clock mounted on the wall. It’s quarter after seven. We better get going. We have to set up and rehearse by seven-thirty. “Indeed,” I said. “Let’s head out, then.” Uriko ran back into the bedroom, getting properly dressed. Going out wearing nothing but a evening gown won’t cut it for the cold, Springfield weather. I went over to the closet, grabbing my and Uriko’s coat out of the closet. Facing a woman for my first match...this should be interesting. It shouldn’t be any different than wrestling with my wife... I guess we shall see... We’ve Come to Terms.
