Date: 2003-10-03 …C can I call you C I don't care. Just because I don't talk about, worry or care about you doesn't mean that I'm taking you lighty. No no no… The camera view fades back a bit…we see HBC on the left of a monitor, and BDD on the right…each holding plastic cups full of beer…I’d tell you what they are wearing, but that is no more relevant than what they ate for breakfast, which is TOTALLY irrelevant… …Man in fact you are one of the true stars not only of the Fed but in all of Wrestling history. At least that shitsniff has something right, D… …You went from being the kid in the helmet thatrode the short yellow bus to school everyday to a champion of the eVo. I mean damn…. They both pop their heads up for a second and share a peculiar look… …So keep using naughty words like 'Fn' and 'Heck'…. Now they are scratching their heads… …I'm sorry I made you get all pumped on C, but I'm sure she liked it… Now they look at each other, and they try to hold it…they really try, but…. They bust out laughing… That dude is SERIOUSLY frickin stupid dude. Oh man…Jesus F’N Dick C… Oh, bro, you don’t have to tell me. I SAW it. I swear, if you won wrestling matches by being unoriginal, immature, and a complete LIAR…he’d be a living legend! Good call…was I the only one that heard him rip ME off in that promo? Nah dude, the entire eVo channel saw it. Somers ought to be taking legal action against that slutass Tok’n, cause I thought Seara was the only one allowed to steal from you to further her character. Watch it dude…those sort of words are for marks only. But really, I could have sworn that just the other day, I referred to her tag-partners as little-yellow-school-bus-riders, and lo-and-behold, he’s painting some picture about you supposedly being THAT kind of dude, with drool and all, the NEXT frickin day! And really, who looks the lamest when Seara makes a statement like that? Her. Why? Cause me and you have bitched slapped her and her partners not once, but TWICE. God damn, I think if I got punked by a retarded kid who drools on himself, even ONCE, I’d lay the wrestling career to rest and find something else to do. That’s just pathetic. Yeah, and normally I’d point out how he tried to talk shit about our slang, which is HELLA old, seeing as everyone talks shit about our venacular, usually about a week before they start using it, but there was something even BETTER that he pulled. You know what I am talkin’ about Punk Daddy? GAY JOKES. Tok’n…you didn’t seriously go there, did you? Jesus… You, Senior Dickslap, made a crack about me supposedly getting all pumped on D, but him probably liking it. Duh! I’m a playmate retard! Every guy and girl wants a piece me. Even Seara had a small taste and liked it deep down. You don’t have a chance in hell Tok’n of getting with me. Well, no need to complete the sentence. Cause I was bullshitting anyway. The level of ignorance and hypocrisy on display with this loser is impressive, for sure. I tell you what, Seara, as a one time special offer, not available in stores, I’ll promise to not only slap the frickin piss OUT of you this weekend, but I’ll slap a little sense BACK into you. Sound like a good deal? It should, cause it’s the best you’re getting since you don’t want to hookup with me. I swear man…Somers needs to start doing IQ tests on some of the people he hires, it’s getting a bit scary… Word. How about this? Metallica SUCKS!!!
