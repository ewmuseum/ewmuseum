Unbreakable!

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.29.03 [Unbreakable] ~What do the words mean to you? Shocka used to think that he was Unbreakable that he was simply perfect and could not be brought down by any one being. Now this may still stand true but he is not as sure of it in his own mind and this little bit of self-doubt is what drives him to be better. In a person such as Omega still under the control of the egomaniac in thug roC you could only expect them to act in that matter. And how sad is it that a cruiserweight a nothing could have control over such a big man…makes you wonder how tough Omega really is doesn’t it? He may think that he is the master of mind games but the ones calculating them, isn’t even in the match at Unbreakable. Shocka and Omega one on one in a very dangerous type of match up there will be no pin falls, no submissions hell no count outs, no rules…anything goes and the only reason to win is by placing your opponent inside of the ambulance and shutting the doors with your opponent inside.~ [Destruction] ~What kind of mass carnage could come out of a match like this you may be thinking? Well with the personal stakes at an all time high between Omega and Shocka the destruction that will ensue has been building over the last month or so. The animosity only continues to rise each day as it draws closer, at Unbreakable two powerhouses will collide in what will prove to be an epic battle to end it all.~ ~The scene opens inside of a well lit hotel room Shocka sits with thoughts running through his mind as sick covers his face and seems to transform him into something different.~ ‘You know…’ He speaks. ‘Omega is going to be shown what I am made of tomorrow he may think that he can just man handle me and treat me like dirt like I am roC or something but he can do nothing of the sort!’ Shocka screams to the camera. ‘I am going to be the real man in this match and demand that my team stays in the back during our match because I want this match to be one on one so we can prove who indeed is the better man no matter how hard it will be for Omega I ask him to do the same.’ Shocka says looking down at the ground. ‘you know what…I am not really in the mood to talk I just need to concentrate so why don’t you bastards just get the hell out…’ Shocka speaks in a very serious tone. ~The camera visibly moves off of a tripod and out of the room as the scene fades.~
