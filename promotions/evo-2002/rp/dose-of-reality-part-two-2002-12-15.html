Dose of Reality!!! part two.

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The path of destruction is still uncertain and definite bodily threat. The man suffering derived from sickness, from having no inner conscience. The thinking pattern of this well calculated machine almost not human, this man has the reputation of Hitler in the wrestling industry, the careers he has ended and the life’s he has threatened and ruined. The man journeys with unjust cause. Striking when he feels necessary, he has no control! Things have slightly changed from this reputation carried within Morpheus Omega, former XWO World Champion, FDW Unanimous Champion and lastly the first drafted FDW Unanimous Hall of Famer. Now Morpheus has someone that has slightly broken mental ice through to him. With Sky Night in the picture it’s either for the better or worst, there is no in-between. Trust has started to develop but his thought and reasoning still very uncertain. Time unfolds more quickly now, faster then others have the chance to react. What will become of his fate? ... The scene opens in the darkness of an undisclosed location. The only light emits from a massive television screen. The picture looks to be rewinding very quickly, the picture going backwards and hardly made out all blurred like. The picture stops on eVolution's ring with BDD and Sean Mason in the ring awaiting Morpheus Omega. Then out of nowhere Carl Cash crawls out from under a rock, he makes his mark by joining for commentary at the announce table. Cursing can be heard 'Dead little fuck!' The scene pans around to the side. Morpheus Omega can be seen sitting with a deadly grin across his face on a barely scene black leather couch. The glare off the television can be seen reflecting partially off of his face but majority is shadowed over in darkness. Morpheus curses, 'That was the moment Carl Cash signed his death wish in blood, he just didn’t know it yet. The ignorance of that pathetic chump! Trying to steal my time, my glory and trying to advance through eVolution at my cost. I promise his time will fall short with his new career decision. The question is not about being past Carl Cash's time, but matter of his career ending like it would of then and will now!” Morpheus shaking his head back and forth, he is disappointed the courage Cash thought he had. Thinking he can advance over himself and his wrestling career. The thought running through his mind, if he could take out the 'Deadliest Athlete in the Game' he would have the restored fame he once held. The fame he held before falling short to another contender. On the television screen the match continues on. The match under way and progressing, impatiently Morpheus fast-forwards the tape now, stopping where he’s lifting BDD high into the air with a military press and hitting a reverse, falling DDT. Sadistic laughter can be heard throughout the large, darkened room with an echo. Without fear in his eyes, Morpheus stares on like he’s amazed by what’s happening. Nodding with that insane look in his eyes, he continues speaking, sounding sicker each word spoken. 'The next mistake Cash makes is coming up next. When Cash decided to lay is greasy paws all over me, interfering in my match. The man should have given up after my lesson of respect. Showing dominance because that squared circle is my kingdom that I walk through triumphantly. That ring is my domain, no one ELSES!' he demands loudly. Becoming more excited now, Morpheus stares with his eyes glued to the television. At this point, the rattling of the chain can be heard in the background. Morpheus screams out 'This is where he felt my wrath! The lesson taught of what happens when you poke the bear! Coincidental, the legend Carl Cash will have that title striped of him like a criminals rights at Legends XIX!' he laughs uncontrollably. Morpheus watches himself snapped and beyond control with an iron chain in hand after taking out BDD and Sean Mason with it. Carl Cash is in the ring behind Morpheus, hunting his prey and trying to purchase what he thought was his ticket back to the top. Carl swings the chair and he misses, he takes the large boot of Morpheus smashing into the chair and across his face with brutal impact. Carl's legs fold and give way as he falls into instant slumber on the mat. The proclaimed legend lying unconscious on the mat with his eyes rolled back in his head. Morpheus continues speaking 'You are nothing now, like you always have been nothing. Sure you have held many cruiserweight titles through the duration of your career, but you cant even claim to be the best! You have lived in the shadow of Thug Roc as far back as I can remember. You were second best then and I will prove you to be the worst now!' The laughing continues following his word. Morpheus continues speaking with a sarcastic but serious tone “You are a mere ant waiting to be stepped on and crushed. That’s the only comparison that I can come up with for you Cash! Mr. Platinum himself has the audacity to step to my level and challenge me? You have pranced into the wrong contenders yard Cash. I have proved to Sean Mason what happens when you impose threat on the ‘Infinite of Evil’ and I will gladly show you as well! Welcome to my reality Cash!” pausing… The television can be seen fast-forwarding again quickly. Morpheus stops around the time of Seara swinging and missing the face of Xander. Morpheus continues fast-forwarding through the tape and can be heard cursing again, “Not the point I’m wanting to prove but definitely not forgotten about!” he laughs and continues, “Seara can wait for hers!” carrying on with more sick laughter. The play icon can be seen on the television… Morpheus pulls the chain with Mason padlocked by his ankle on the opposite end, he takes Mason by this throat and throws him over his shoulder advancing towards the Black Monty Carlo. Morpheus speaks more quickly now and excited in his own sick way. “This right here should be my prime example to Cash. I decimated the Rising Star Champion, Sean Mason after I gave my fair warning. I warned that persistent little fuck, but he wouldn’t listen. Look what happens Carl, LOOK WHAT HAPPENS!” he screams. By this time now, Carl lies on the hood of the car with the chain still dangling from his ankle. Morpheus from a standing position on the ground jumps and lands double footed on the hood of the car. Metal heard giving way from the tremendous weight. Morpheus stares down at Mason, he chuckles on the television, which matches the one he has well watching himself in action. Morpheus screams, “OPEN YOUR EYES CASH, BECAUSE I SEE NOTHING BUT BAD TIMES AHEAD FOR YOU BITCH!” breathing heavily, his eye also twitches. The television screen shows Morpheus hesitating, holding Mason in the grappled position. Morpheus can be heard speaking on camera “Dare 2 Dream Cash!!!” Morpheus lifts him high into the air and executes the perfect reverse diving brainbuster. Mason shatters the car window result of this move, Code Omega! Mason smashing through to the inside of the car, the glass shatters ear deafening loud. Cuts, bruises, scrapes, cover his body while he lies unconscious. Morpheus on his knees, he has the exact same sick look on his face that he has while staring at the television watching. Morpheus with his grin ear to ear, like nothing had amused him more then watching Mason suffer the pain inflicted by himself previous to this, he continues speaking “I asked Mason if he would realize his mistakes when it was all said and done and I hear he swears his revenge over me even in the hospital. Another mistake…” pausing. Morpheus continues speaking again “The question being… Will Carl Cash make the same mistakes that Sean Mason made? Cash is just like Mason, another cruiserweight that thinks he is the best in business. You will need a lot more then speed to take me down Cash! You have one skill when I have charisma, the mental ability and the physical power. Cash, Money… Bling everything you have, because I guarantee its not enough!” The scene fades black. The letters “MO” burn into the right corner of the screen before if fades. The anger building inside the ‘tough as nails’, Morpheus Omega! The outcome of this personal vendetta between Morpheus and Cash will soon unravel into answers. When they step foot to each other, face to chest on Legends XIX! Does Carl Cash have what it takes to put this giant to rest?
