Flashback

15 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-15 It was August 19th 1997, Brianna Washington’s pre wrestling days. Her boyfriend of three years, Thomas Dorn had proposed. Brianna wasn’t ready to make that commitment not sure if she felt Thomas was really the one. After rejecting his proposal Thomas let words fly and leg loose his emotions, throwing things all over the place. As a result Brianna took off not wanting to face it. The darkness had filled everything in eyesight. Night had set and the cold air blew through the trees giving them that eerie ruffle sound. A figure came into view, nestled tightly in a black trench coat. The person shuffled quickly to a car door located in a deserted parking lot. “Bri don’t do this.” The figure yelled tapping on the glass. In the car sat Brianna Washington who had just been through a rough spot. The man on the outside was her now ex-boyfriend, Thomas. “Go away Thomas.” “Can’t we work this out or something? I’m sorry I overreacted ok. I just love you so much, you gotta understand that.” He pleaded as he put his arm on the car and peered in through the fogging window. “Just go….please!” Brianna yelled slamming her arms on the steering wheel with frustration. Feeling that there is nothing more to do Thomas walked away biting his bottom lip in anger as Brianna watched him disappear into the darkness. We cut back to present time. We see Brianna Washington sitting on a chair staring down at photographs of Thomas and herself….the happy years. Those pictures are what sparked the memory of the day all of that happiness ended. “Still looking through that album? Give it up.” Milo said walking over to pry it from her hands. Instead of putting up a fight Brianna let go and stared off towards the wall. “I don’t even know why you still have pictures of that jerk.” “You can’t get rid of the past Milo.” Brianna replied lightly, still staring off into space. “You need to stop thinking about him. You haven’t even heard from him for over four months so just let it be.” “He called the other day…..wanted to congratulate me on my success in Evolution.” “He called or he didn’t….it doesn’t matter. Don’t let that cloud your mind….you do have a match tonight remember?” Milo says sternly, realizing he has to give Brianna some motivation in the right direction. “Yes I do and I suppose your right.” “Now get going!” Milo says, pulling her up to her feet and playfully pushing her out of the room. The scene slowly fades….
