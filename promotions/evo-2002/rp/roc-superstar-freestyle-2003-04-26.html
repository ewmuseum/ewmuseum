roC superstar- Freestyle?

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.26.03 ~Shocka has had quite the week indeed, he spent the majority of it in the state penn. On Wednesday afternoon just hours before Legends went on the air Shocka convinced a judge to let him out of police custody. No one knew about it not even Scott Frost it was a total surprise to everyone but the man that was probably most surprised was thug roC. Shocka saw the look on his face when he stood face to face with him before Shocka took him down. RoC was the luckiest man alive on Legends though as the cage rose and officials flooded the ring. If Shocka only had a little more time with him one on one in the ring he would have destroyed him and made sure that he would never fuck with Shocka again. The jail time did nothing to Shocka except further enrage him. Now he is going to show roC just what he has made of. RoC can do all the research and tape watching that he wants but the fact of the matter Shocka has the ability to switch up styles just like he did against Omega. Shocka knew that Omega would watch as many tapes as he could so he went into a sort of high risk mode…and only Shocka knows what he has up his sleeve for this cage match.~ ‘I can’t wait until tomorrow night…’ Shocka uttered as he walked out of Xero’s hotel room and down the hall. ‘I am going to show everyone what I can do inside of a cage and that I will take thug roC down like the sorry son of a bitch that he is…I am going to make sure that he will not be able to feed himself after this match…’ Shocka smiles a little as he continues walking. ~As he rounds a corner he pulls out his security card and opens the door to his room. He walks in to see Elmer sitting on the bed watching TV with a big smile on his face you can hear grunting and Shocka rolls his eyes and speaks.~ ‘Ordering porn again eh?’ Shocka says disturbing Elmer from his trance. ‘Uhh…it’s the real world!’ He screams. ‘Yeah….i guess this is how you get pumped before a big PPV then eh?’ Shocka asks with a half smile. ‘Listen man…I’ll be good just give me like 10 minutes before you come back in here…’ Elmer says. ‘Well why don’t you put that chain thing on?’ Shocka asks sarcastically as he smiles and walks out. ‘Fuck off dude!’ Elmer yells as Shocka slams the door and walks away and down the hall again. ~He now goes into the hotel provided gym and looks around to see no one he smiles and gets on the treadmill. He takes off his shirt and begins running in just a wife beater and a pair of tear away pants. He smiles as he looks up watching the TV it’s a commercial for Malibu’s most Wanted Shocka laughs as he speaks to himself.~ ‘I think that roC should be the star in that movie not Jamie Kennedy! I mean I bet I could throw down a better flow than roC….’ Shocka says with a smile as he thinks for a minute and then starts out. ‘I’m a killa Surreal thrilla Not Brittany’s boy Not even a decoy! When people see Me on the streets They be like hey boy! I saw you destroy roC You roC’d his world Busted his face wide open With the blows straight outta…’ Shocka trails off realizing how sad that was as he looks in the mirror and shakes his head before trying again. ‘People talk about thug roC! Like he’s straight from the block. But the only block he roC’s Is the one with lot’s of cock.’ ~Shocka smiles and he stops on the treadmill and shakes his head.~ ‘That shit is harder than it looks…’ He says with a smile as the scene fades.
