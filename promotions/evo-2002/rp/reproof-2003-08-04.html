Date: 2003-08-04 It had happened. After the endless nights spent in nagging anticipation, it had happened. After the never ending thought of what it would be like, it was sweeter than he could have thought. The feeling of getting his hands on Xander after such a long time, the feeling of spilling his blood, it was beyond anything he had ever felt before. Although something had happened that he didn’t want to, but that he should have taken into consideration. Most people would have seen that a man having his chance at glory being taken away from him would make him spoil someone else’s vengeance. Asis hadn’t seen it though. A man blinded by rage doesn’t see much but red. Kimson had put a dent on that which should have been unspoilt. In the mood Asis had been in for the past two months you would have expected him to dent Kimson’s fucking head in retaliation. Maybe he felt like it, but Asis had learnt something over the past two months, something he hadn’t had in a long, long time, not since his Father used to beat him. Restraint. This wasn’t because he had respect for Kimson like he did his Father, it was simply that Asis had no business with Kimson. All he wanted was to cripple Xander, to make him pay for all that he had done. Not just for what he did two months ago with the wrench, but for all he had done since knowing Asis. But it was one warning and one warning only. He may have restraint, but he hadn’t mastered it yet. Kimson got in Asis’ way again he could guarantee Asis would make business with him. But maybe Kimson had done Asis a favour that night. If he hadn’t interfered Asis may have gone too far. He may have done something that he’d regretted. Not kill Xander, no he wouldn’t have any remorse or regret about that at all. But to spoil what he had planned, that would have been a shame. The attack on Xander at ReVelations was just a taste of what he had coming. There was a hell of a lot more planned. Told you I’d been practising restraint. He was looking forward to this Thursday. His first official week being back in eVolution, not having to stalk around, everybody would know he was back. He would be able to once again get face to face with those who had oppressed him. And if Davidson wanted Asis to jump through hoops to “earn” his chance to get at Xander officially, then he would do just that. His rage knows no boundaries and Bomber would just have to deal with that. Although he won’t. It was the second coming, the return of the demon. Hell’s wrath would soon be unleashed once more and everyone would know the name of the beast that bore its name…
