Misconcieved Intentions

15 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.15.03 ~A proud champion…how would you define this term…a man that has successfully defended his title. A man that has shown the world that he is the best by beating and driving a man to his own death. By destroying the self proclaimed greatest universal champion…and now he gets to set out and watch 4 undeserving men battle it out for the #1 Contender position to his title. He is ready for anything he has already defended his title against Scott Frost but lost due to DQ on his own part. He showed that he is ruthless, and he doesn’t care what he has to do to keep his title.~ ‘You know what? I am going to show everyone in this federation what being the #1 Stunna means next week!’ You hear Shocka screaming from the drivers seat of his BMW Roadster. ~You can see him glance over at his passenger Morpheus Omega, who has sort of a sick smile on his face.~ ‘But first…I have to show you a good time I mean we are in my home town!’ Shocka shouts in excitement as he takes a sharp left turn into a parking lot. ~As the men enter the bar a bunch of people look at him in disgust and some even begin booing him angrily.~ ‘You’re a sick bastard!’ A man yells. ‘And you’re a fat fuck!’ Shocka retorts at the man. ‘Shut up you stupid son of a bitch!’ The bar tender yells at the first man yelling. ‘Fuck you…I’m outta this joint…if you let in trash like Shocka and Omega then I don’t wanna be here!’ The man screams and a crowd begins brewing behind him as they push there way past Shocka and Omega. ~Omega turns in anger and begins following the men but Shocka grabs his arm and pulls him back whispering to him.~ ‘Not tonight Omega, we are here to have fun not beat people up…besides we did enough of that tonight anyways.’ Shocka says with a giggle in his voice. ‘I’m in the mood to beat some ass though…’ Omega speaks with an angry tone. ‘All in time man…all in time...’ Shocka says as he sits down at a bar stool. ~Omega sits down and orders a beer as Shocka orders a screwdriver. Within about 20 minutes both men are visibly ‘calmed down’ as there cheeks are red and they are laughing uncontrollably.~ ‘Now this is what I call training!’ Shocka screams. ~Four glasses sit in front of him all drained of the vodka and Orange juice. Omega smiles as he looks at the eight empty beer mugs placed in front of him.~ ‘No daddy no!’ A young voice yells. ‘Shut up son…you are a piece of shit and you don’t even respect me!’ An angry father yells down at his young son. ‘Dad…I love you…don’t hit me again!’ ‘Don’t you listen! Just shut your damn mouth and you will be fine!’ The man screams. ‘Imma tell mom!’ The young voice blurts out before another blow comes… ‘Yeah Jonathan you like that!’ ‘FUCK YOU!’ The boy yells as he escapes the grasps of his dad. ~Shocka sits straight up from his slanted position on the bar as he seems to wake from a dream…no it wasn’t a dream a flashback.~ ‘That son of a bitch…’ Shocka says to himself. ‘That’s the next person we’ll have revenge on Omega!” Shocka screams with anger in his voice. ‘Who?!’ Omega asks very interested. ‘My father…that sorry son of a bitch he used to beat me every night just because he could that drunk fucking asshole! But if it wasn’t for him I wouldn’t be in this business…’ Shocka says slowly fading off. ‘Whoa man that’s some deep shit…’ Omega says in a sort of sympathetic voice. ‘That is the rage that drives me Morph…he’s the reason that I train so hard…he’s the reason that I am what I am…but the worst thing is…I’m starting to turn into him…I’ve drank every night for the past month just to make myself feel better…’ Shocka again says this time though he jumps off the bar stool and walks to one of the pool tables. ~He grabs a pool stick and slams it over his own forehead as Morpheus jumps up and runs over to him he grabs it before he can get to the sharp edge.~ ‘What do I have?!’ Shocka screams to Omega. ‘What do I have to live for?!’ He screams in a drunken rage. ‘Everything man! You are the Universal champion of eVolution that is saying something right there! You are one of the best superstars in eVolution and if you keep on your path you will be the best!’ Morpheus says trying to calm down Shocka. ‘Why…’
