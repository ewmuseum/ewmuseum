Remember the Time

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/26/02 AUGUST 10, 2004 'Today was his birthday. It's a shame really he was taken away from us far too soon.' A woman stands in front of a headstone. The Words 'Here Lies Xavier Sin....aka Xavier Citizen b. Aug. 10, 1971- d....' The date of death is partly obscured by a shadow. She spins around to see the figure of Malcolm King standing beside her. Woman:'I knew you would come, can't believe he's gone.' Malcolm kneels down to the grave still obscuring the date of death.... 'You would think after all this time I could accept that he's gone...and yet...' He looks up to her, her eyes beaming back down... 'I know Malcolm I know.....' Malcolm stands up and throws his arms around her as they walk away from the gravesite. An hour later after an early lunch and a session of remembering their fallen friend. Malcolm drops the woman off at home.... MK:'Bianca if you ever need me for anything....' The woman long time manager of both Malcolm King and Xavier Citizen (SIN/aka Damien Legend) is named Bianca Dare. Even after the team known as The Shadow Sector had split up she had kept tabs on all the wrestlers in her stable. BD:'I know who to call...say hello to the family for me okay.' They kiss as Malcolm reminds her ULWN goes national tonight and he hopes to see her there. He speeds off. As she walks up to her condo. She starts to think about Evolution Wrestling and how they had become a viable alternative to the WWE; and now Malcolm and his ULWN is threatening to do the same. She sighs.... 'I wish Xavier was here to see this...' As she goes to unlock the door, he foot hits a package. It has her name on it with a note. Once inside of her condo she opens the note it reads: Thought You might want to have this. Tyler Evolution Wrestling Bianca opens the package to reveal a book. The cover has inscribed on it ECHO She quickly opens to read the first page...the handwritting is that of Xavier. 'Sept 26, 2002 Welcome to the first day of my last days. I was able to convince Tyler Davidson to allow me into Evolution Wrestling. I think he did it more out of spite than anything else. However I am here.....and I am wrestling until the time comes where I can't awaken any more. He even gave me a gimmick name...Echo BlackWhisper, partly as an inside rib towards Malcolm King. Funny how I can't escape my erstwile partner even when I want to. But now it seems I must pay my dues here in Evolution.... a ten year veteran now has to fight for his position here. Paying for the sins committed by another man....' Bianca leans back and remembers the first time she met Xavier Citizen; it was Feb 10, 1989 at William Lewis' wrestling school. It was her, Xavier, and a man known then as Demetrius Echo (an identity she found out later was also a stage name) who would become Malcolm King. She reflects on the man Xavier Citizen.....who would be called Echo BlackWhisper....and eventually be buried as Xavier Sin. A tears come to her eyes as she continues reading about Echo BlackWhisper and his final days in a wrestling organization known as Evolution Wrestling..... to be continued.....
