Pointless Ramblings

8 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-08 In recent weeks, I’ve kept myself to myself. I haven’t socially interacted with anybody. I haven’t given any interviews. I’ve kept my distance. All I’ve done is let my brawling do the talking. Either I’ve handed out a beating or I’ve been handed my own arse on a plate. Fact remains, I haven’t uttered a single word…kind of. Am I brooding? Erm, nope Am I sulking, for whatever reason? Heh, not bloody likely Has something upset me into silence? Mmm, I guess you could say that I wouldn’t exactly say upset but more ‘pissed off’ than anything else. Yeah, that sounds about right… Instead of complaining about the problem down a microphone to an arena full of fans, into an interviewers dictaphone or into a keyboard for a column like this; I’ve just gotten straight down to business. Maybe that’s how I should conduct business from now on, keep quiet and throw the fists. For the last few weeks, I have mostly been preparing myself for a date with destiny. Am I going to build my next match up like it’s for the good of mankind? You’re bloody right I am, it’s what this business is all about…whether it be the lower card opening match or the main event for the “biggest prize in the industry”, every match is built as if it’s the biggest thing ever, that nobody daren’t miss it. Anyway, de-railing a tad there, back on to the topic… Aaron Justice has been in my thoughts, long before the match at Faith was even sanctioned by God almighty himself, the big boss of this company. I’ve been content to keep my distance and do what I love to do…run my mouth. Now there’s an Americanised phrase if I ever heard one. So like always, I’ve been trading one liners and trash talk with Justice…until it hit me, I needed that extra edge. Sure, my standard of trash talking mentally defeats my upcoming opponent even before they stroll through the curtain…but I just wanted that extra something to let Justice know that I’m not an ordinary trash talker. What’s makes me so sodding different than any other “trash talker wants to act tough to prove a point”? Well, nothing. But this is my thought and I can say what the bloody hell I like. So I decided to take a step back after I devised this Operation Slap mission, which I would have gotten away with…if it weren’t for those meddling Select fools. I gave my rant, declaring war on The Select, and that was all that needed to be said. Now, it was time to get straight down to business… Rimian…check. Tok’n…check…QBX…erm, well you know how that turned out. Hey, I’ll hold my hands up and admit that QBX got to me before I could get to him. Wise move, because he knew that if I did get the first blood then…well, don’t need to explain, do I? Fact is, he needed a little help from his friends and thus, I was spilling blood like when a female has the painters in. Not exactly getting down to business, I know. But hey, I ain’t Superman for crying out loud. At least I admit that I got my arse kicked, which is more than can be said for some egomaniacs out there. Justice locked me in a cage, nothing more to be said there. All you lot need to know is that I escaped…yes I escaped without any harm. So to tonight and the eve of my confrontation with Aaron Justice, which will take place in the confines of a steel cage. Woooo, takes you back to the 80’s doesn’t it? “So-and-so versus so-and-so within the confines of a steel cage!!!!!” Ah, good ol Mean Gene. Anyway, I have kept my silence…so to speak…and I have collected all my thoughts. All I have to do now is step through that cage door, through the ropes and embark on a battle that neither Sid nor myself will ever forget. Meh, no longer I’ve kept my “silence” if this shite is anything to go by… Let the games begin…
