Requiem to Innocence - Ballad to Sin

8 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-08 We pass through our mother's body, bathed in world of white light as soon as he come out from her body. We're held in the hands of our father or a complete stranger who happens to be doing the procedure. We're born and, thus, lose all innocence we formerly had. The second we feel gloves touch our small bodies, innocence dissolves into nothing. And right then, the umbilical cord is cut. Flash to twenty years later and we find ourselves thrown out into a world of sadness. All innocence we ever had is forgotten and we are now exactly what God intended us to be: Sinners. White people? Sinners. Black people? Sinners. Muslims? Sinners. Jews? Sinners. Christians? Sinners. By being born, we've sinned. That's God's big joke. That's God's sick sense of humor at play. We're born without any chances of getting into heaven. People tell me what I do, this whole fighting thing, they tell me it's a sin. That violence isn't right. They tell me what I do is wrong. Ever since religion went mainstream, you always have someone shoving bullshit down your throat. Some fucked up rule that the originators of religion thought up. Take sex for example. Sex is a creation of God, pure and real and beautiful. Yet, you've got religion saying that it's a sin. Adam and Eve were innocent. Sex use to be innocent. Don't you wish Eve hadn't ate the forbidden fruit? To think. Innocence was killed by one woman. Eve was the original sinner. What I do, this whole fighting thing, I consider it to be beautiful in it's own way. It holds importance to me, thus that makes it special... and yet, it's a sin evidentaly. How funny. Faith. When Damon's blood is on my fist? I'll be sinning. Faith. When Diablo's been decimated by me? I'll be sinning. Faith. When Terlayne's been reduced to a pulp? I'll be sinning. If what I do is sinning, then oh well. I really doubt I could live without what I do anymore. The thrill. The excitement. I'll be sinning... and I wouldn't have it any other way.
