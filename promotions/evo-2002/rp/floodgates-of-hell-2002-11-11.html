Floodgates Of Hell

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th November 2002 Since legends last week, female wrestler Seara has not been seen. Although not in hiding, the female has kept quiet and has kept well away from cameras. Now involved in three title chances, she has become focused and with Notorious beside her, she is more confident in her wrestling style and ability. Growing evermore powerful, the female seems to have little fear of the male orientated world around her and the males that she fights against in the ongoing struggle to prove herself to everyone that ever doubted her. Last week she had a chance meeting with Asis as the pair clashed in the hallway between their dressing rooms, it was a seemingly calm experience as the pair stared through each other, but it seems Seara has not given up on beating Asis. Like the other Notorious members, she has the strong thoughts in her head to destroy Unlimited, in most, Asis who has affected her the most in her life, her love for him which seen her nearly dying, her hate for him which brought the same pain into her life. Now Seara must face Asis in a whole new light. As Notorious get ever closer to Unlimited and ending the people that have hurt them the most. Personal demons for everyone, even if you sit there thinking you are perfect, better than me, better than anyone that has ever suffered, you have demons, pretty bad ones, demons that will drag you to the ground, secret demons that will kill you. You Lee Exodus have found your first demon, a defeat to me, the rookie female of eVolution. Not as experienced in the same ways as HBC, but then again, who would want to be? I was like you once Exodus, a new one, I thought I was better than I was, thought I could take on everyone, thought I could not be beaten, thought I was indestructible, I wasn’t too quick to learn, actually I think for everyone here, they can admit that at some point when they are looking towards their next match, they think of themselves as superior. Even you Exodus, you were cocky, too smart, you should watch yourself around here, there may be more here than you can chew off, so watch yourself. You went in to our match with a cocky attitude, thinking you were the better of the two, but it is my time now. Seara will become more than thought possible. I will be the least of you your worries. I have thinks better to do than show you the pain a rookie must face in order to become part of this lifestyle. Trust me, pain is the easy part. Seara looks at the camera from under her hair; she is leaning across a table and a plate of food in what appears to be a proper house, clean and undistorted. We are unsure whose house it is, but it certainly is a different setting for the female to be seen in, warm enough for her to be out of her trench coat, she sits and digs a fork into the food in front of her. Sitting back she drops the fork onto the plate and looks around at the large dinning room. The interior design tells you that the owner is financially sound and has no money worries, and the size of the room echoes the idea. The cameraman looks around for evidence of whom it belongs to, but he has no clues to work with, and has to guess that it is the house of either L2J or Scott Frost. But neither male is present, so the cameraman has to announce that he is guessing about the house ownership. The camera continues to look at the female as she sits still, not moving a single muscle in her body, except to breath, which she is doing slowly and subtly. From this angle it looks as though the female has stopped breathing all together, but a small strand of hair blows in front of her mouth as she gently exhales. Nice aint it, and I know the one answer you want from me about this place, whose is it, Frost’s L2J’s, maybe Jameson’s, or maybe someone else’s, well I’m not at liberties to tell you. I never invited you here, you found out the address of where I was staying, and you swung by to check out where the female member of Notorious has been for the past week. Well now you know where I am, so don’t bother to think about asking. It is not my fault your source hasn’t told you whom the house belongs to, that is something you will have to find out for yourself. Seara once again begins to look around the large room before reaching over to her plate and pushing it toward the table centre in a child like manner that would tell a parent the child was being moody and didn’t want to eat, or that they were full and had no intension of doing anything other than playing with the meal that had been prepared and placed in front of them. Seara certainly had a few child like habits and qualities, but she also exhibited qualities not normally associated with females. Lighting a cigarette the female slouches down into a chair and looks at the camera with a cold hard stare as though looking through the large bulk of plastic and metal and directly at the cameraman. Legends XV, Jason Jameson and myself against New-TV, the last match before the tag titles match. The winner of this match faces the Unlimited’s seemingly unbeatable forces. But we have something to offer, and although we may not have the opportunity for other of our team mates to take our place in the ring, Jameson and myself make a strong and focused tag team, and both of us are going out there for more than just gold. Yes it will be a great feeling to get my hands on gold, to have that belt around my waist where it belongs, and to bring more gold to Notorious. Once I have that gold, it will not be mine and mine alone, it will belong to us, the team, it will represent our strength and ability to work as a group, as a set, as a family unit. I am not a separate person all the time, in the ring, as part of Notorious, I am part of a group, we need everyone to focus, and we need to be able to prove ourselves, I doubt highly that anyone will be able to stop us once when we are full. We will be the next big stable within eVolution. As Exodus thought before our match last week, he thought he would be the next big thing, but I stood in his way simply because he posed a problem for me and the chance to get my hands on a different belt, and I will continue to stand in peoples way if they try to get in the way of myself or any other Notorious member. Together or separately, we will become the best. Standing up Seara walks to a window and looks out at the pouring rain. The droplets running down the glass add to the already dark atmosphere in the room. For the first time, the cameraman comes across a smashed chair laying in the corner of the room alongside a large cabinet that has until now hidden it from view, he focuses in on it, and Seara quickly explains that when locked in a room and treated like a caged animal, she reacts by taking out her anger on the things around her. Turning on a light she illuminates the room, which has always been light by candlelight, and shows the cameraman small piles of destruction around the room. Locked in to eat, protein filled foods. To gain energy, to make myself stronger, to put on some weight, to give myself more of a chance against other eVo members. Outweighed. Out powered. Seara was once weak, but they do not see that her weight is her hidden secret. Light as she is, she is quicker than others, nimble, fast off the mark, and stronger than her opponents, she is a hidden bomb slowly ticking, waiting to explode. The female pauses for a moment and stubs her cigarette out in an ashtray that has apparently been used quite frequently since the female arrived here last Thursday. Seara destroyed Lee Exodus in the ring last week at Legends, and she is sure that it will be a while before he attempts to get back in the ring with any female wrestler. She will face New- TV members this week, and she will destroy them. The ticking time bomb Seara will explode when she takes her anger out on the only other stable powerful enough to face Unlimited’s tag team. But Seara knows one thing, she is more powerful, and with Jameson by her side, she knows they can win and can take Unlimited to the floodgates of hell. The cameraman seems confused as Seara continually refers to herself in the third person perspective. Turning she walks toward the door of the room and knocks on it, after a short wait, she is let out. But by who is unseen. And it is still unsure just how the cameraman got in the building and into this locked room without a key and without seeing the face of the figure that was letting him in the room.
