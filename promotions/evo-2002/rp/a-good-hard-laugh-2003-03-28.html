A Good Hard Laugh

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-28-03 'I don't understand why you are so intense, Scott.' The voice of 'The Tru One' Joshua Krowe can be heard. The scene fades in, and Scott Frost and Seara are sitting next to each other and Josh is standing up talking to them. Frost looks to be very concentrated, and Seara is holding him trying her best to calm Frost down. 'Listen, you trust me. I am a verteran in this sport, I know all about the trials and tribulations of wrestling and I know you feel betrayed. But Jameson is still your friend, don't think of him as an enemy just because he beat you. Think of him as a true friend because a week later, he is giving you a chance for redemption. Trust me, that's what real friends do.' Krowe tries comforting Frost. Frost stares into nothingness, and un-announced Shocka comes walking into the room and he has a ham sandwich in hand and he looks to be enjoying the sandwich. He stops, and looks at Frost. 'Umm....I thought you said I could make a sandwich???' Shocka says. Frost looks at Shocka, and then at Seara. 'What is it Scott?' Seara asks. Shocka nudges Krowe. 'What did I do?' Shocka asks Krowe. 'Nothing, he is just concentrating too hard on his re-match with Jameson at Unbreakable.' Krowe replies. Shocka nods his head in understanding, and then he takes a bite out of the sandwich. 'Scott, maybe everyone needs to leave and we need to spend some quality time talking about all this. Please don't be upset.' Seara tells Frost. Frost looks at her. 'Where's Lee?' Frost asks. 'What?!' Shocka, Seara, and Krowe all say at the same time. Frost looks at everyone. 'Lee said he was going to play a joke on VanCore, and I haven't seen him since.' Frost says. VanCore comes striding in cleaning off his hands, and Frost jumps up. 'What'd you do with Lee?' Frost yells. VanCore looks at him with shock and shakes his head. 'I don't know what you're talking about man.' VanCore replies. 'BULLSHIT!' Frost yells again. VanCore looks at everyone and everyone looks at Frost. Suddenly Lee comes skipping in the room holding a piece of cloth. 'HEY SCOTTIE!!! I MADE YOUR NEW SHIRT!' eXodus says very happily. Frost looks at everyone, and he sighs with relief. Frost smiles, and turns and gives Seara a kiss. 'What's it look like man?' Frost asks eXodus. eXodus opens it up, and the shirt says 'I Love My Frostcicles' in white and light blue letters. Everyone begins laughing extremely hard except for eXodus. 'What? What?! What'd I do?!' eXodus questions. Everyone laughs and the scene fades away.
