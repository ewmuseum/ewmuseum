Time For a New Truck?

2 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-02 The scene opens up inside of Kramer’s garage which just like his basement, is full of junk. There is so much junk in fact it’s a wonder he can even fit his truck inside. There are some things Kramer has, organizational skills are not really one of them. Several seconds go by as we get a chance to soak in all of the untidiness. Finally Kramer comes strolling in through the door off in the back. “Aggg!” He says as his foot meets a brick and he nearly does a face dive onto the concrete. He leans over and tosses the brick to the side, realizing that chances are he’ll do it again. Without wasting time he rushes over to his truck and jumps inside placing the keys in the ignition. Before he puts the car in drive he reaches up to his visor and plucks off the garage door opener. The opener instantly starts up and slowly lifts upwards. After a few minutes of utter slowness it’s finished rising and Kramer races out. *Later That Day* Kramer is sitting in the small lobby of a car repair shop. As he was cruising down the street his truck broke down just as it had done a week ago. Not having much to do today anyway, Kramer hasn’t really been bothered by the fact that he has had to wait over an hour just to find out what’s wrong. To pass the time he has been flipping through wrestling magazines laid out on the small table in front of him. “Oh a whole section deservedly devoted to Evolution.” He scans the page for mention of his name, but doesn’t see it. He kind of expected that so he doesn’t let it bother him. But then when he sees a small section reading “The Select=A Forced to Be Reckoned With?” he slams it down. “The Select is a forced to be reckoned with because it seems as though they can’t get anything done on their own. You go in a singles match with one of them and instantly it becomes a handicapped match.” He takes a breath and tries to calm himself down, but it doesn’t help much as his hatred is too much for the group of men. “Then you have men like Troy Chryst….men who almost never realize that sometimes when you mess with a good guy, you’ll lose. Maybe I’ve done what he’s wanted in letting him get to me. It’s hard not to. You get attacked from behind a few times and you really develop a hate for a guy. But I don’t think he really understands that he’s angered me in a lot of way others have never done.” Just as he continues on his spiel a greased up mechanic walks in and walks up to Kramer. “So what’s wrong with the car?” “Sir there’s a lot wrong with the car. You have the messed up carburetor, the brakes are close to just dying out all together, and a whole list of other things.” The man thrusts the clipboard in his hand towards Kramer who waves it off realizing that his car is a hunk of junk. “How much is it going to cost to fix everything?” “I personally think it would be good to just get another car altogether. You’re a wrestler I’m sure you dough all over your house.” “Well I guess I could look into buying another car. I have had the truck forever….I suppose it’s time for change. And you’re right. I’m back into wrestling and I don’t have to worry about saving every little penny to make ends meet…I am making ends meet!” “Uh…that’s great.” With that the man walks away. “The Wild One is definitely back!” Having only been back in wrestling for a month or so Kramer has just now realized that he is in fact back. With that comes change. He doesn’t have to scrap for cash so he can pick up his life. Winning his first couple of matches is almost change enough because he’s actually winning! Now he looks to keep up the streak when he faces The Select in the upcoming Legends. And that victory will bring in a whole new chapter into his life… The Scene Fades
