It draws nearer...

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.21.02 Scott Frost Scott Frost thinks that he is something big in the tournament even in this federation. But what has he done? Sure he took out Erin Christoph and left his bitch ass on the injured list or dead somewhere. But Shocka did that same thing when he absolutely destroyed Erin and took his title then proceeded to hold the Continental title for a very impressive reign. But hell that’s not the point is it…cuz we could ask ourselves what exactly Scott Frost has done thus far in eVolution. Nothing that is the main question here isn’t and this competition between the two will not end with the tournament…but it will end Frost’s reign. It will put the Notorious one down on New-TV. The stable wars will only get more deadly and more intense especially when New TV beats The Unlimited. Fear Shocka has felt many feelings in his body and he is man enough to admit that fear is one of them. But this feeling of fear is not for Kai Kau and perhaps not even for his possible second round. But he has never been in the ring with Scott Frost…he’s never seen how he works. Sure this could prove a disadvantage but he will nonetheless destroy him. Take him to his limit per-say and make him wish that he never got into the road to glory tournament. A lesson learned. After the rise of legends culminates and there is a road to glory champion crowned a lesson will be learned by all who step in there way. It will be that they are better than you…And not to fuck with them…now Scott Frost will learn this lesson if he makes it to the finals to face Shocka one on one. Sure there are other things that could play into this final like other people winning but what are the chances of that? ~~The scene picks up inside the bar in the hotel room. Shocka sits on a bar stool with a drink in his hand. Some beautiful women walk by him and he checks them out as they walk by but they don’t seem interested. He is wearing an all black suit and an ivory tie. He takes another swig of his drink and walks up to a woman he begins speaking.~~ ‘So what’s up?’ He speaks slyly. ‘Nothing how about you?’ The woman looks up and shakes her head at him. ‘What, do ya like what you see?’ Shocka speaks in a cocky voice. ‘More like what I see over there!’ She points to thug roC and brushes past Shocka like his nothing. ~~He turns and laughs to himself walking back to the bar he sits down on the bar stool and orders up a rum on the rocks. He slams it back and then spins around looking for another woman to pray on.~~ ~~Ahead lies a dark alley and behind a brick wall the only light provided is by a street light that is flickering on and off. It fades for a minute and when it comes back on Shocka is standing against the brick wall. After another flicker you see another man standing in a lab coat about 10 feet away. He has pitch black hair and a scraggley beard. He speaks.~~ ‘You think you can hide me?’ ‘What?’ ‘Don’t you dare pretend you don’t know what the hell I am talking about…’ The man screams. ‘Are you crazy?’ Shocka questions. ‘No but you are…’ He states. ‘Who are you?’ Shocka speaks. ‘You know damn well…you tried to hide me with all that damn medicine but you knew that I would shine through again and bring your ass back to all the glory.’ The man speaks intensely. ‘Jonathan…don’t you ever forget that name…’ ‘Wait…’ Shocka speaks. ‘Wait what don’t you understand that Shocka is the in ring persona but Jonathan is the more violent one…the man that you truly are…’ ‘Riight…’ SCENE FADE.
