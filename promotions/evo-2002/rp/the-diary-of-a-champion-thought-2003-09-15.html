The Diary of a Champion: Thought

15 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-15 Well talk about your surprises! I mean, I had no clue what the hell was going on when I saw my sister come down that ramp. I heard Sovereign’s music and I felt my heart sink into my shoes. Not because I was afraid of him, but of what he was going to do. My family was at ringside, I was broken and hurt from X-rated and knowing Sovereign like I do he is the most vicious and unrelenting man I’ve ever traded blows with. But for what Sovereign has physically, Leya had in the form of a knife stabbing through my heart. She’s my sister, I love her and I hate her at the same time. That being said she has a knack for entrances. I remember watching her shock the world on eVo television numerous times. Debuting when Sovereign had the world captivated with his plan to destroy L2J a year ago. Then turning on Sovereign, joining Impact only to be a double cross and siding with her husband again. It really hurts. More then any chair shot, harder then any bump to see my Sister still with that maniac even though he’s nowhere to be seen. Actually, I was sort of hoping that I wouldn’t have to see her. Not for a long while to come. I felt she needed some space. After all she’s always been so stubborn, having to have her own way. I can’t say that’s not a family trait. But with Leya it’s 10 times worse then with any other of our family. And now she comes back to eVo, not as she was before but in a position of power! She’s part of this Damn committee. And while I’m sure that at least someone in that committee will have some sense I have to say the quicker that Tyler Davidson comes back to day to day running and the sooner that Leya is gone I’ll rest a little bit easier. Lets be honest here. I haven’t slept in days. I don’t ever remember feeling this uptight, this upset over something. The accomplishment of being Universal Champion was so important to me and it still is believe me. But now everything is spiralling down and I am not in control anymore. Being forced to wrestle my friend Scott Frost next week is just the start, or so I think. Don’t get me wrong, I’ve been looking forward to this match for some time. Every now and then me, Scott and Seara would go down to the bar of the hotel and have a few beers and talk about matches that we wanted. Me and Scott were both very honest about the fact that one day we wanted to work a match against each other. I have nothing but the upmost respect for Scott and what he’s done for the company. He’s the longest reigning champion we’ve had and the only multi time champ in our history. That being said, this was the sort of match that we both didn’t think would come so soon. My 1st title defense, not 2 weeks after X-rated where my bones and muscles ache like you wouldn’t believe and now I have to go up against a relatively healthy Scott Frost. But hey, not to make excuses. It’s part of Leya’s plan. I have a feeling she won’t rest until the title is off my shoulders so that when she’s ready she’ll find a way to end my career after she’s taken everything that I hold dear. I don’t know what’s going to happen for sure, but I know that has something to do with it. Look at what she’s done already. Scott Frost, and now Shocka all in 1 night. She’s trying to test my resolve, see how I’ll deal with the pressure. A completely useful tactic and I’d have to admit that it is going to be hard. Hard as hell to be exact and I don’t know how I’ll deal with it. I mean, Shocka for instance is a former Champion. In fact he was a great champion if I recall. That being said he beat Jason Jameson last week and that’s not an easy task. He’s back from his slump, he’s on a roll and he’s always been a very dangerous man. Especially when the Title is involved. And so I sit here, typing all this out for my own reflection. My Sister turning her back on me again, but now in a position where she’s holding all the cards. I’m just a pawn, a Championship pawn. I know that her first goal has to be getting inside my head. Why else sign Matt Hunt? Probably my greatest rival in my entire career. I mean I know he’s not going to get a match with me anytime soon but his presence is enough. Hell, why interrupt the middle of my celebration other then to rattle my chain. No doubt I’ll have to defend this title week in and week out. Not that, that bothers me. I want to defend this title. I want to prove why I am and should be Universal Champion. Not because I managed to win one of the greatest matches in eVo history, but because that I am the best in eVo in that ring. I mean there isn’t any other way to do that then to go out there night after night and fight, fight to defend your title. But this is different. She’s not going to make it sound so valiant, so easy. Take this week. Legends 50, this is big time. Headlined by this Frost vs Kimson match. It’s going to be off the hook. But I gotta believe there’s going to be some hitch, some stipulation added at the end to favor Scott. Not because she gives a damn about Frost, but because all she cares about is destroying me. And because she can’t do it herself she’ll have to get others to do it for her, wether they want to or not. And Leya is good at getting people to do what she wants. She married Sovereign didn’t she She became a Committee member in Evolution Didn’t she So many I’m counting the days until she finally finds the fly in my ointment. The trick that she’ll need to take this title away from me. But rest assured that it’s not going to be easy. It’s not going to be easy for either of us. But this war is going to be fought on a battlefield that is going to leave everyone in eVo in it’s wake. Some way or the other either she’s going to leave, or I’m going to. There’s not enough room in Professional Wrestling for more then one Kimson clan member. And her last name isn’t Kimson anymore
