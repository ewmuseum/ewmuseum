Single Handed part 1

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th Febuary 2003 Legends this week was a shock from the moment the first person arrived at the arena. When Seara stepped in the ring, she at first met a strong team, but was quick to learn things are not always what they seem. After his attack on Xander in the ring and the appearance of brother Angel, Seara disappeared backstage to begin a few last minute changes to her plan. Now after eVo she sits in a hotel room, planning exactly what next week will bring to her. Legends should have been a time of shock change and pain, and it was for many. Frosts attack on Xander was the most shocking thing to the female wrestler, never has anyone really stood up for her, Asis once did, but the female barely remembers the days of herself and the Demonic being friends. Now an enemy, she tries to dismiss the thought of his ever once helping her, she knows it was a once in a lifetime thing and she not bother to dwindle on the fact that he showed some compassion once. Not annoyed, never hate him, never annoyed at him, even though he destroyed one thing. Xander was mine. Should have attacked him at legends, should have been me behind that door. Happy he did it, love him for it, but Frost is risking so much doing that. Xander will not let him away with it, and this feud needs to stay between Xander and me. I love you Frost, and I want your protection, need it as well, but don’t want you to risk getting fucked up by Xander's goons for me, its not worth it, you know I will just continue to fight him and feud him until it is over. Can’t stop the feeling, can’t stop the need, need him in my life, need to feud him, need to fight him, need to hurt him, denied it. Oz denied me the opportunity to really get my hands on Xander that fight should have been Xander and I, but Oz and Angel stepped in and destroyed the plans, but tonight things have been changed. Different plans, newer better idea’s, things so much funnier now. Seara goes quiet and reaches forward to adjust the camera on the tripod, it has been a long time since the female has been left with a camera, but she has requested it for a week so that she can take care of business as she has so rightly called it. She turns on the bottom of the bed to look at Frost who is lying still in the bed, she can tell by his breathing that he is not asleep, but instead quietly listening to what the female has to say to the camera. Even he wants to know what she is planning for the weeks leading up to the next PPV. Next weeks legends is bound to be something very different and Lee eXodus can expect a little present for me. I already know what stipulation I want for our match, and all I am going to say to you is get fit eXodus, train hard and prepare for pain like you have never felt, prepare to realise I am not who you think I am. Things will be a surprise to you eXodus, and I’m sure one or more people will like the stipulation I have planned. Frost will probably not agree with me, but I am sure there are plenty of other wrestlers and fans who would love to see this go ahead. While on the subject, there are a few little things to be dealt with at Legends, and if all goes to plan, a few people will wish they never fucked around with me. The scene has barely been light, but is quickly cut off as Frost climbs out of the bed walks to the camera and switches it off.
