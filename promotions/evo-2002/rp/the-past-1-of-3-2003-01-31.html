The Past (1 of 3)

31 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.31.03 Funny, me and my brother switched places. It's different this time though. Angel was the lighter side of my brothers true ability, for that... he sucks. It's okay though, for where he fucked up, Oz will take over, not will speed, but with brute force. No one can survive the Tenth Degree, not even my little brother, so what makes anyone in eVolution think that THEY can handle it either? Silence is abroad me, nothing more than the bloddy ruins that lie behind me... my past. I was sitting there, only 4 years old. How can I remember this? This scrips and scraps of memory of my amnesiatic past can often be protrayed, other times that I try to remember, my brain starts to ache. I have a truly difficult type of life, why was this utter torment destined for me? Why not someone more deserving? It had to be spoken onto me... oh well. No use in crying... I was four. The scraps of me lying there, nothing more than a small infant, playing with my younger brother Angeles. We ignored the constant yelling, well atleast I pretended to. I couldn't help but hear everyword that mom and dad yelled at each other... 'WHO WAS SHE?!' flaired through my ear, I tossed my head down and felt the tear roll down my cheek, it was a hot tear. I never really ever cried when I was younger, only a few times sparingly. That was one of those times. I turned my head down toward Angeles who had a large smile on his face and proceeded to tackle me to the floor. I could do nothing but hold up my artificial happiness to keep my brother from suspecting what had happened. The words... those horrible words that split me up from my little brother Angeles, I couldn't stand them. I hear those words every day, the same words echo through my head sliding my memory closer and closer into inevitable rememberance. 'WHY YOU ALWAYS BLAMIN' ME FOR SOMETHIN?! I TOLD YOU THAT WAS A REPORTER FROM THE RING!' My father yelled out toward my mother. I kept up the artificial happiness, Angel's was pure, I envied his carefree soul. I envied my little brother. He was the eye of truth, nothing could upset him. I wasn't a whiner, I just knew what was going on, as of my brother Angel, he was too young to understand... for that I envied him. Swift words, shooting through my ear like bullets... bullets of devouring hate!. 'OH WELL WHAT THE HELL WERE YOU DOING IN BED WITH HER?!' My mother questioned my father with questions that would stumble the average man in his tracks at that very second. My father was brawn on the outside, but the average man at heart, maybe that's wy Angel and I got split up? 'I TOLD YOU! I WAS SITTING DOWN AND SHE SAID HER BACK HURT SO SHE LIED DOWN! WHY DON'T YOU BELIEVE ME?!' the screams continued to grow louder, Angel could suddenly feel the impact of the words coming from our father and slowly turned his head around. I had to keep him from noticing the truth, so I playfully stumbled over him and straddled him into a submission. He began to laugh and forcefully shoved me off, rolling ontop of me and grappling me in an amatuer wrestling hold, sure he didn't know what he was doing, he was a natural. I struggled for about a minor second, well, that struggling was artificial also. I couldn't stand beind so fake, it was twisting the pit of my stomach from the left to the right and then back toward the left, I could've puke through all of the fakeness, but I kept calm and continued to grasp hold of Angel's attention. The yelling continued to burst out toward the room we were in. 'BECAUSE YOU PULLED THIS SAME SHIT BEFORE! NOW I'M TAKING MY SONS AND LEAVING, GOING TO SACRAMENTO TO START A BETTA LIFE! A LIFE WITHOUT YO DUMBASS, JEVAR!' My eyes widened, as I turned over toward Angel, the carefree smile on his face, he continued to slap me in the face playfully but then we were interrupted. The frantic footsteps came toward us, closer and closer, the petit footsteps of a woman continued to gain on us until finally our mother busted into the room staring at both of us, from behind her came my father. The yelling continued, Angel looked at the both of them confused and just began to observe, he took it better than I'd thought he would. The words, shot through my ears faster and faster. I could barely catch all of them but I caught the most important words of all... the words that seperated me from my brother... 'YOU AIN'T TAKING BOTH OF EM! TAKE ANGEL! I'LL TAKE SHAUN AND YOU GET THE HELL OUT MY HOUSE!' I watched as she edged closer to me, and placed a nice kiss on my cheek. I continued to look at my father as he walked over to Angel and rubbed his head slowly, then walked over toward me. My mother grasped Angel into her arms and began to walk off. My father right behind her only turning into his room after she left. The last I heard and saw from my brother was the most painful thing I'd ever felt... saying goodbye to my brother. '...Bye Shaun..', Angel waved toward me with a small tone in his voice. He continued to look, watery eyed, and I looked toward him and just sat there, before I could run after him, before I could even say anything... they were gone. Out of my life forever. I could never take the pain of losing anyone of my loved ones ever again so... I isolated myself from feelings and other things that would get into my way. There was a new Shaun, a Shaun that would always make an impact... but nothing will ever scar me as badly as the final goodbye from my little brother... Angeles Sanchez Dellacruz. through broken windows, and dirty glass, my past in beyond there.. but the dirt is too thick and my eyes are too weak. My past, locked away from me.. isolated from my being, only to be remembered my the loved ones who reminded me of some of things that happened when they were around. The sudden impact, my heart was ripped from both sides and the name Shaun was broken.. the future had made a path for the new being.. the new warrior, the one who would thoroughly destroy all...Oz.
