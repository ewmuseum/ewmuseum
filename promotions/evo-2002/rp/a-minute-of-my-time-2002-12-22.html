A Minute of My Time

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-22-02 (Evolution once again will hold a pay-per-view event, it has been a few weeks since 'The DiscoKid' Troy Pierce has returned to the wrestling ring and a familiar but new foe has stepped up and challenged Pierce. Xavier Citizen, the protege of Malcolm King has taken offence to Pierce's history with King feeling that he stole the attention that he deserved from King. The ULWN Championship has switched hands again this week with Citizen defeating Pierce to return the championship to The Omen Brotherhood. Recently a camera crew caught up with Pierce for a few minutes to tape a brief interview at his home in Syracuse, New York. Pierce sits in a leather chair playing Madden 2003 while the camera crew sets up. Pierce is blowing out the Miami Dolphins 49-7 with the Buffalo Bills...) Pierce:'We ready to roll?' Camera Guy:'Almost, couple more minutes.' Pierce:'Ok.' (The camera crew finishes up their work while Pierce continues to play Madden...) Camera Guy:'K, we're ready to roll Troy.' (Pierce continues to play Madden but starts to talk...) Pierce:'Hey by the way guys did S. send over some limes for me?' Camera Guy:'Not that I know of.' Pierce:'Guess I'm not gonna send him a Christmas Card this year, guess instead of coal he's gonna be getting the #50 he's been deserving. I didn't forget Dan.' (The Camera Guy looks on in wonder, and then shakes his head at how laid back Pierce is.) Pierce:'But back to the main reason we're here today. So Citizen you managed to with the ULWN Championship back. It's cool, I've got no problems with that. But I do have a problem with you thinking that I stole King's attention away. I did nothing of the sort. I never wanted to be 'The Urban Legend' I respect the man, he and I batteled our way across the country fighting over that ULWN Championship. Our blood was spilled all over that belt, mine, his, and now yours. You say that I am a member of the Nation, I may be, but I am a reluctant member. I was never part of The Unlimited, and I am certainly not a member of The Omen Brotherhood. I am 'The DiscoKid' one of the best out there. But Citizen you now have the championship back, congratulations. The belt carries a worth to me, but it is not something that I require to be whole. Welcome to my brotherhood, the men that have wrapped the ULWN Championship around their waist and have defeated the man who held the belt. Your blood is not mixed with Kings and mine. How does it feel? How does it make you feel to have defeated a man who you don't believe to belong? Does it make you happy? If it does good, cause Citizen I learned one very important lesson from King, it is never over, the battle never ends, espicially when it comes to the ULWN Championship. Go back take a look at some of the stuff that went on when King and I were beating each other senseless over that belt in EDW. I ruined a lot of good wife beaters during that time, blood just doesnt wash out. Citizen you and I are closer than you could imagine, both have been shunned, while you believe you where shunned by your mentor, I was shunned by my protege. But it appears as though I do not have a match this week, however I do have a plane ticket to go to the show, and as a member of Evolution I have the right to be in the arena in the back, but I also have a ticket to sit ringside. So maybe you'll run into me in the back, maybe you'll run into me while you are wrestling in the ring and I'm sitting there watching. Remember Xavier anything can happen and more than likely will, the ULWN Championship is always changing and always evolving, I learned how to play the game while facing King, the master. You also learned from King, and you learned well the match that I defeated you in with the ever changing rules was a good idea, but Citizen can you keep fighting like you have been. You don't look so good these days, you don't look good in the ring, frankly Citizen you look like shit, take care of yourself 'brother' cause you never know what is going to be around the corner, you brought this fight back to me, I did not come to you with this fight. But I have never backed away from a fight, and I definetly backed away from a fight with King because I knew what I was getting into, so Citizen you and I are much like the yin and the yang where one ends the other begins. See you sometime soon 'brother.' (The feed cuts out quickly after Pierce finishes his last word...)
