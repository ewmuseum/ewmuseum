All he knows

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22 September He has been through so much. This business is what he lives for and it looks like its killing him. He went through hell, he went up against all odds, he pulled out all the stops. He did everything to come out on top and give the fans what they want. But all he can remember is the feeling of those nails piercing his skin, his face. All he can remember is desperately getting back to his feet and giving every last ounce of fight he had in him. All he can remember is being thrown off the stage and slowly watching what he lives for fall away from him. L2J’s eyes suddenly open and he finds himself staring in his rear view mirror. Looking deep into his own eyes. All he has now are cuts, stitches and broken bones. He looks across the street from were he his parked at a simple terraced house. He is home. He has come back to England without celebration. There were no fans waiting for him, because he has come home empty handed. All he has is dark clouds and dismal weather. And L2J is now suffering with a heavy dose of reality. He reaches over to a sportsbag and opens it up. It is filled with cash that L2J has earned over the past month which includes a large bundle of cash from PPV main event revenue. He binds all the notes together then swings the car door open. His hair and trenchcoat immediately feel the effect of the wind and blow wildly as he walks into the front yard. He steps up the front door and raises a hand to knock, but he pauses. He can hear the sound of kids arguing about some kind of game coming from inside the house. He lowers his hand, slides the money through the letter box and turns sharply and heads back for his car slowing momentarily to clutch his torn and bruised stomach that stings underneath the bandages. He goes to leave but his car door is not the only one he can hear opening. He looks up to see a beautiful 20 year old girl standing in the doorway. L2J had been busted. And the girl runs over and doesn’t hesitate to slap her boyfriend across the face. L2J feels it a lot more with all the cuts on his face. Claire- You said you would be back for the summer. (L2J thinks hard but really cant say anything to keep the peace). L2J- Something came up. (She goes to slap him again but thinks better of it when she realises one of his wounds has re opened and is bleeding down his face). Claire- We saw your fight. Do you know what you’re doing to you family? I was round here coz your whole family, Even Jamie had stayed up to watch your big match. We all had to sit there and watch you in that fight. And your mother left the room leaving me to try and convince Connor that you were ok. (L2J’s eyes well up at the mention of his 5 year old step brother). L2J- Damn, He must have just started school. Claire- Yeah he’s doing good. He got a lot of attention because of you. Everyone wants to be friends with L2J’s brother. You know how much he looks up to you. He’s been dying to show you his new hairstyle. It probably looks better on him than it does on you. (He quietly laughs to himself. But the smile doesn’t last long as he sees Jamie walk out into the front yard, he Sees L2J and rushes over to him. Without anyone saying a word Jamie puts a hand against the cuts of L2J’s face. Then starts using sign language directed at L2J. Whatever is being said is totally indecipherable to everyone except the two relatives. The look on young Jamie’s face quickly turns from sympathy to smiles as L2J gets down on one knee and the two start playing a game that looks like a combination of rock, paper, scissors and a thumb war. Jamies laugh has everything but sound as he runs back to the house seemingly expecting L to follow him. L2J rises back onto his feet and watches Jamie run back through the front door). L2J-How is he? Claire- He’s doing just fine. The money you send made sure of that. He’s turned out to be a damn good footballer. All it took was your mum to go down to the school and kick up a minor stink about not letting a deaf kid play. Turns out hes a natural. L2J- Sounds like you lot are doing pretty good. Claire- We get by. They all miss you. But they understand that its to provide for us. (There is a long silence. Which L2J breaks very reluctantly). L2J- Look I have to go. Take care of my family for me ok? Claire-You’re just gonna leave? Can you not at least stay for your birthday. It would mean so much to Connor. He hasn’t seen you for almost a year. L2J- I think youll agree that he should see me in the state I’m in now. But ill be back for my birthday. (L2J kisses his girlfriend on the cheek and while supporting his stomach, steps back into the car. He is just pulling away as Connor and Jamie rush out into the front yard. But Claire cuts them off as L disappears from sight. The superstar looks crushed. He knows he doesn’t want his family to see him like this. But he knows that money has to be made. And he knows the only way to do it. He knows he isn’t finished with Sovereign).
