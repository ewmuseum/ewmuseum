There's nothing to fear pt. 1

27 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-27 Emotion had left him long ago. Lust was a thing of the past. Scott could be what you may consider an emotionless super soldier, everything within his being that had been human died long ago; pain and memory were the only things that he knew within his being. It wasn't that he had decided to remove himself from emotion; it was that the things that had kept him in this reality had deteriorated. His skin tone had already begun to fade as his life slowly began to make its way to a final end. He knew there’d be a higher punishment and retribution for what he had done in his life when all this ended. There was always something higher than what had been done, he had learned that long ago. Fear and loathing were the only emotions he regretted in his life. The only thing he knew was that he had to keep going no matter what, there was something he was fighting for, recognition. And there was nothing to would let him stop until this quest was over with. Scott Evans would be a name to remember by the time he was done. This week didn’t matter, the cage match against Shane Wethers had no real value all it could be was another win or loss, all that mattered right now was getting recognition. And defeating Thug Roc for the United Title was the only that was going to happen. Shane was just a pawn in this game in Scott’s mind and you needed to reach for the King. The fact that pawns could become Knights, Queens, or Bishops wasn’t part of Scott’s irrational thinking. Rational thought was a practice that Scott’s mind was no longer capable off. As Scott rest upon his bed the only things that came to mind were memories and desires that had long ago left him. Everything flowed to him as the memory of that night that seemed part of another life when he found out the bad side of people. He had only been six but this memory had been something that had haunted his nightmares for many years. His father had locked him in his room for bad grades at school but that wouldn’t stand by him, he needed to get out of the house. He opened the window and left 2 hours later dark had fallen and he needed to figure out where he was. Then a car pulled up beside him a drunk man and women sat in the car as they pulled up the warnings about not talking to strangers were overtaken by his need to get home. “Where you headed, son?” the man asked with a strange grin upon his face. Scott looked at him and with utter horror he saw himself being pulled inside the car by the man, rape wasn't on either member of the couples mind there was something else there something evil. He knew what would come next and he couldn’t stop it even if he tried. But luck was on his side, his screams met the ears of his father who was slowly driving down the road. As he heard the screams he sped up to reach the sound of his son. Adam sat in the car and he was the first to see the situation at hand. What Jake saw horrified him he knew that there were many reasons behind what . He pulled the revolver from the glove compartment, the next he knew it was pointed at the mans head as he was an inch away from pulling down his pants. Before he knew it bullets flew from the end of the gun and the man and woman lay dead, his boy was safe and in 2 days both his children would become orphans. That memory that Scott had never thought about came to him for some reason and for some reason he knew that it was more than just a random shuffling of thoughts.
