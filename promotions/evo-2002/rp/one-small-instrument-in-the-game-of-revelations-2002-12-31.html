One Small Instrument, in the Game of Revelations

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/31/02 {The scene opens up on Xander in a darkened room. Too dark to tell many features, other then the moving form of the Unlimited mastermind.. or is he?} 'My my my' Xander begins in his most demeaning tone. 'How high and mighty we think we are, don't 'we' Seara? You think in such small context, it's almost too amusing.' 'You really think Unlimited attacked you in our match because I couldn't beat you?' Xander laughs to himself. 'You, my dear, are very, very short sighted.. and that is why you are simply a follower in life.. and not a leader.' 'You are, what we like to call, Exhibit A.' He points out. 'You were the first strike point in our Revelation. I shouldn't do this, but since this much has come to pass.. I feel it's not news to the ones who can actually think straight.' 'First, we took you down.. and hurt you.' Xander stops his walking and steadies himself. 'Why? Why you? Frost. You aligned yourself with the man who will get you crippled.. and scarred... soon.' 'Second' He continues. 'Take out Luther Wisdom, and Xavier Citizen. Unfortunately, time seems to have taken Citizen out of the picture for us... and I'm not sure who Troy Pierce thinks he is... but he's stepped into the picture at the wrong time.' 'Third.' Xander winds down. 'We introduce our new member.... Angel. No one saw it coming.. and not alot of people seem to care.... yet. Why? He's relatively new.... and he hasen't wreaked the havoc that only Unlimited can allow.' 'And last, and definitely not least....' Xander smirks. 'Everyone's Christmas present. The return of The One who will help make all of your sinners understand.... and repent.' 'You see, sweetie' Xander says mockingly. 'It's not all about me beating you, or you beating me. Our match was one small instrument in the Revelations of Unlimited. What does this mean for you... and for me? Oh we'll meet again. You can trust me. As long as you still draw insulin breath, I will plan for it to stop. On the drawing board, as we speak, we still have Frost, after he cheated against Asis. Pierce, for his interference. You, because you think you are more then you are... and that little head hole of yours will be your demise. And lastly, L2J... because of EDW business.' {The scene fades out on Xander's face}
