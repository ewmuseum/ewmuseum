Enter The Era Of Frost

2 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-02 The scene opens in a dark room, the light is flicked on and from out of camera shot, Scott Frost appears, it is unclear where he is, but it is clear he has made it home enough for himself and Seara. “What the hell have you been up to Katherine, it stinks of burnt plastic in here” remarks Scott, the scene quickly flicks to the female who sits on a table, a melted bunch of pictures lie in front of her and anger seems to fill her entire body. Looking at the female’s hand, Scott notices the cigarette, something he has not seen her do in a while, he other hand is gripped tight, white knuckled she sits in complete silence and looks straight through her husband as though he does not exist in this space or time. “Katherine, Kat, baby, hello” Scott's attempts to get her attention are useless and walking over he gently places his hands on her shoulders as thought trying to stir her from a deep sleep in which she is trapped in a nightmare. After a moment, Scott touches her hand, and the female jumps, snapping out of her trance like phase. The female looks somewhat confused as she looks at Scott, not knowing that he had been there before that split second in time. Shaking her head she pushes her long hair back and takes a deep breath then lifts the cigarette to her lips. “Where were you last night Katherine? I went to sleep cuddled up against you, so happy that I could feel you close to me and I knew you were safe, but I woke up half way through the night and you were gone, I got up to find you, but you were nowhere, what happened to you?” asks the male, concern is in his voice, but he is also weary from a tireless night and a hard day of training. “I had work to do,” snaps the female in a short sharp reply. Scott steps back, removing his hand from the females touch, he looks shocked and does not quiet know what to say. “I left a little message for HBC in her kit bag, predictable little woman, at the gym, leaves her stuff there in a locker, doesn’t lock it, doesn’t think she needs to, thinks she’s better than us all. HA, BDD and her have a surprise coming, want us to step down from the tag division, don’t think we belong there. Sorry guys, oh did I say guys, sorry HBC, forgot you were both girls, or was that guys, I’m not sure? Come on… you two walk around like you own eVolution, but next to you H, I’m the longest running female here, I’ve got just as many rights as you, you and your pathetic little BDD, or are you just jealous and scared of a new power coming to the tag division that may teach you something, you have never been good for long without each other, and now you are together once more, and I want to take you down, Scott and me against you and BDD, what you say Chick, you think you can handle the strength the pressure, the speed, the youth still alive in us. Come on HBC, I’m waiting for an answer, next legends, in the ring I will call you out, and I want an answer, if you chose wrong and say no, I will take you out myself, hurt you so much, if you say yes, I will honour the answer and wait patiently and excitedly for our match. Not excited because I get to wrestler in the ring with the great HBC - and yes, among female wrestlers, I do admit you are one of the few great – no not for that reason, excited because I will get to devour you like the hungry animal I am. I want your blood on that matt Chick, I have for a long time, I just didn’t worry about it, I had bigger fish to fry, but now HBC, my plate is clear and you are on my menu for this weeks special. “ Seara falls silent and suddenly looks at Scott who now sits across the room leaning his head in his hands and staring intently at his wife who slipped back into another trance. He stands up and walks back to the female, gently kisses her and whispers something to her, and then quick as he appeared in the room, he disappears into another room off to the right hand side. “The tag division has just entered into the Era of Frost, double the power, and as the Bodysnatchers said, we do have a list of great resumes, and now, although the tag division don’t want us, we will over through this division, we are here to last. Bodysnatchers, keep your eyes open, even when you sleep at night, you never know when you’ll next see me, that goes for the rest of you as well. We will take the tag division to a whole new level, the Era Of Frost.” Seara finishes and without a word climbs off the table leaving the melted pictures lying there and walks off to follow Scott. The scene fades to black, as the door slams shut…
