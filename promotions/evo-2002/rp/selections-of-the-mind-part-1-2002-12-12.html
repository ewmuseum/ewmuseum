Selections of the Mind: Part 1

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.12.02 Selections of the Mind: Part 1 “CAREFUL WITH HIS NECK! IT MIGHT BE BROKEN!” screamed the paramedic. That day may have been the worst day of my life. My career both as an actor and wrestler was about to be shortened considerably, or not have one at all. But a scream like that almost straight into my ear didn’t help. But then again, I was too weak to say shut up. What was I to do? My mind knew that I was conscience, but my body was not. It was too limp and frail at that time for it to anything. Even if my mind said shut up, my body did not. It just did not react. “Fucking bastard!” I screamed at the top of my lungs… within my mind. The emotional pains I had at that did were immeasurable. My career… my blooming career… about to be cut off by a reckless human being, never the less a wrestler! A simple man in the sport I had loved since I was a child. All I wanted at that time was for Sovereign to fill the immeasurable pain and suffering I was going through that time. I wanted him scarred just the same as me, if not more. “FUCKING BASTARD!” I screamed once more at the top of my lungs. I breathed and felt the autumn air in my mouth and neck as I tried to release my anger in a crass two-word phrase, but I, nor the paramedics could hear anything. Slipped. I woke up the next day in the hospital still in my wrestling gear. Draped over my body however was a blanket to give me warmth. I didn’t care for warmth… I just wanted to smack Sovereign. I wanted to get up but I was kept in my bed with various levers, pulleys and gizmos that I, one do not care about, and two, forgot about. The only thing certain about what my body was entrapped in was a neck brace. I could feel its hoarse cardboard like material crushing onto my delicate skin. My vision at that time was blurred, but slowly, my surroundings started to became more aware to me. When my vision had returned I saw a nurse with her back faced to me. “O you’re up Mr. Scopes.” Said the nurse, as she turned around towards me in a nice casual smile. “You’ve had quite the ride Mr. Scopes!” “The hell happened yesterday?” I questioned brashly. I was still angry and I wasn’t going to let it aside for a pretty nurse at that time. At that time, I felt a large stinging at the top of my head. I felt like dying right there and then. The pain was immense and had no words to describe it. It was that terrible. “Well, you were unconscious… until now that is…” “Do I need surgery?” I interrupted once more in my rude and brash fashion. The pain started to grow as I saw her maintaining her smile. My vision started to blur once more as my eyes closed to try and relieve the pain sensation. “Well…' she said softly, 'we’ll find out that soon.” I slowly started to drift in a physically somber sleep… but inside my head; my mental state, a disturbed, vengeful plot started to develop. “When am I going to back at him…” I thought, “How am I going to get back at him?!”
