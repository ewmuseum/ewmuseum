Seasons Greetings...even if it is belated

29 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-29 The scene opens up with a multi-coloured glow from a Christmas Tree. Focus is automatically altered as the shot pans back and numerous amount of coloured festive-like balls and tinsel is scattered around the tree in a presentable manner. The view continues to pan back to reveal Matt Hunt sat in a large leather chair, noticeably wearing a dark red smoking jacket, a pair of slippers and what seems to be a yellow paper hat, the content of a Christmas cracker. A pipe is also wedged in the corner as he puffs away, glancing through a page of a broadsheet newspaper. He places his right foot on his left kneecap, groaning pleasurably as he relaxes. Next to him is a small table which has a lamp, a small glass bowl of assorted nuts and a brandy glass, presumably half-full with brandy…obviously. He chuckles to himself while ‘Winter Wonderland’ plays blissfully in the background… ”Sleigh bells ring, are you listening, In the lane, snow is glistening A beautiful sight, We're happy tonight. Walking in a winter wonderland” This festive looking Postman glances up and notices the camera. He folds the paper he was reading and drops it gently on the floor beside him and also takes the pipe out of the corner of his mouth and places it on the table next to him. “Greetings and a belated happy holidays to each and every single camache!” as beams as he reaches back for his brandy glass and takes a sip “Oooh yes sir, hits the spot!” he gaps as he holds the glass and continuing to speak “Tis the season to be jolly, fa la la la la la la la la!” he chirps, while taking another sip of the brandy “Ooooh, get it there!” he shouts in glee as he sucks his teeth and licks his lips “YES!” “No, I’m not drunk…you could say that I’m merry and why shouldn’t I be. I’ve taken this week break in good fashion and decided to celebrate the holidays and as Noddy Holder once belted…IT’S CHRIS-MAAAAAAAAAAAAAS!!!!!!!” he bellows while giggling to himself “Ever noticed the silent ‘t’ in Christmas? We don’t say Christ-mas do we? No, we say Chris-mas. Yes, Christ…Christ…Christ…he died on the cross for all our sins you know…” he blerts while continuing to giggle “Oh dear, those guys at The Fast Show…god I miss them!” he sighs while glancing to the ceiling “So anyway, I know that Christmas…Chris-mas…was a good few days ago, but hey…I’m still in the holiday spirit and that is the point of this little vignette…this skit…this segment…this thingymajig” “Chris-mas is a time for goodwill towards all men…” Footage flashes in for a few seconds of Hunt smoking Aaron Justice with a chair across the back, at Faith in October “Chris-mas is a time for forgiveness…” Another clip of footage, this time of Hunt snatching a mic off interviewer Jennifer Carter and shoving her out of camera shot on a recent Legends show “Chris-mas is a time for giving…” Clip of Hunt landing a trademark flurry of right hands to Thug roC, who is slumped in the corner “Chris-mas is a time for receiving…” A still picture of a bloody Matt Hunt from Faith, arms raised high with his then-new evolved Title “Chris-mas is a time for…er…more receiving” Another still shot, this time from Hunt’s victory over Thug roC a few weeks ago. In one hand he has his evolved title and in the other the United title – both raised high in the air. “Chris-mas is a time for family…” A picture of The Huxtables, from the 80’s hit sitcom ‘The Cosby Show’ appears. When the feed returns to Hunt, he stares at the screen with a bewildered expression and shifts his eyes from side-to-side in a bemused manner “Er…Chris-mas is a time for token gestures…” A 10-second slideshow appears, with Hunt flashing his V two-fingered salute in different situations and positions “And of course, Chris-mas is all about the kids…” Another still picture…this time showing those lovable orphans from the film musical ‘Annie’ in mid-dance routine. Once the feed returns to Hunt he has a confused frown on his face and can be seen mouthing ‘what the fuck?’ He shrugs and continues “So really, Chris-mas is a great time of year. Er…” he pauses as he takes another sip of his brandy and the needle on the record player with ‘Winter Wonderland’ comes to an abrupt end. “There wasn’t much point to this, was there? I just wanted to wish everybody a Merry Chris-mas…” “Christmas has gone, Matt…” interrupted a voice off camera “I DON’T CARE!!!” he hollers back before composing himself “…and a Happy New Year…has New Year’s gone yet, Michael? HUH? HAS IT?” he belts again, awaiting a response “No…” comes the solemn reply “I should bloody hope so…” Hunt mutters back Silence follows as Hunt shrugs and picks up the pipe, puffing away “Ooh boy, New Years Day in Melbourne should be fun…” he mumbles as the camera zooms back in to the Christmas tree and eventually fades out to black.
