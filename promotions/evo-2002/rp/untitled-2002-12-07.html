Untitled

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not what a lot of people expected recently. I’m talking about the issues that have risen up in eVo concerning 3 individuals. Two sides, three people. Now, as I always try to be un bias I can’t help but finally take a breath of air in relief that Sovereign perhaps is getting a taste of his own medicine. We all know the history of what happened between Impact and Sovereign. We all know the history, to a certain extent of what the relationship between Leya and Sovereign is. But now it is all coming together. With Last weeks legends behind us we learned of a very important match. Sovereign and Impact will go one on one officially at The Nightmare before Christmas pay per view event. The match, a standard match. Sounds a little bit odd considering the hatred between all the participants involved. Leya Forth, wife of Sovereign’s former personality William apparently put in a request to the new commissioner Cameron Griffin last week to keep this match standard. To push Sovereign even further out of his element against Impact, who’s proven to be able to stand up to Sovereign in each and every instance they’ve come into contact with each other since Impact’s return. Sovereign, so used to dominating. Sovereign, being so used to impose himself physically on every man he’s be inside the ring with since his return at American Nightmare might finally have met his match.... a woman and her monster. All things considered we all know how much Sovereign is probably looking forward to the official match, especially now that neither Impact, nor Sovereign are allowed to touch each other unless involved in a Sanctioned Evolution contest. We all know how well Sovereign works within the rules and this week Sovereign will step into the ring against Impact, but also with Shocka and L2J the Universal Champion, the championship Sovereign had a death grip on for almost 4 months but this match is Non-title. I somehow don’t take much comfort in there being an actual match. Ever since Rise of the Legends every match Sovereign has been in has ended in a brawl, a no-contest match simply because Sovereign has gone wild. Now imagine what he will have to contend within himself if he can’t vent his frustrations out on the man that is causing him so much trouble in recent weeks in Impact, the Hollywood monster. Let us not forget also Leya’s role in all this. Leya Forth went out and sought Impact, the one man she felt could rid the company of the Monster that lived under the proverbial bed. Impact, the bigger, the stronger of the two. Impact, now with Leya at his side, the one person that knows more about Sovereign than anyone. The one person that has suffered more than anyone at his hands, from his wrath. Most take the physical punishment Sovereign gives out, but the emotional side lives with her every day of her life. But it’s not over, Sovereign this week refused to give her what she wanted, finally after all these years a divorce. Sovereign wants her to suffer even more. He is a Human Monster. Human only because he breaths our air, and eats our food. Everything else about him is in-human. He’s a fowl beast, and a lot of people are going to look forward to see what Impact will try and do to Sovereign at The Nightmare before Christmas, and hope that Sovereign’s Crusade of Violence comes to an end before the new year. Fade out the Darkness Fade in the darker. Sovereign’s mood recently has been taxed with the revelation of Impact’s return intermixed with his Wife’s return. Both with similar agendas, to ruin what’s left of Sovereign’s life. Sovereign remains in Canada, now in Calgary a few days early for Legends, but he’s there none the less. The black and white camera shot has now fully formed on the screen, with Sovereign sitting in the corner of a rather plain room, nothing particular around that strikes your attention, just Sovereign, head to his chin, hair strung about his face, hands on his laps that are crossed together sitting “Indian style” on the plain carpeted floor of what you will assume to be his hotel room. The clock on the camera ticks away saying 22:09 and only the sound of this breathing is heard. Still his breathing is somewhat not it’s normal self (if Sovereign could ever have anything about him “normal”) and although you are probably not a doctor you’d guess he is still effected by his broken ribs he sustained as a result of the Tier’s of Evolution match at Rise of the Legends. However, that being said Sovereign’s breathing is interupted by his voice, unstable, emotional and angry. How surprised am I to find Cameron Griffin once again at the root of a lot of my problems. I don’t know if he’s trying to do his job or if he is just trying to eliminate me from the company. Personally I have no idea what his problem with me is. When I first knew him he and I got along well enough. I helped him, I gave him all my talents and skills to use as he wanted. I was a human wrecking ball and all he had to do was point in the direction of what he wanted destroyed and it was done. I never did anything to Cammy, and now he’s made it a point to make my life more and more difficult. Now, first of all let us get the record straight for all you people out there that thought I went and took Stormbringer out for someone else, like Dan Somers or whatever. That’s false, it’s just not correct. I did what I did to Bobby for many reasons. First of all he’s an idiot, secondly of all he makes no sense and thirdly because he changed the rules on me at Rise of the Legends. And once you do something to Sovereign, Sovereign gets you back. Stormbringer got what he deserved Cammy, and I want you to know that soon enough you are going to get what you deserve as well from me. That being said my friends it’s obvious that now things have gotten out of hand so to speak. Impact can’t touch me and I can’t touch him unless it’s in a match. Personally I never thought it got out of hand, and not at once point in the last month have we not had any contact at all unless it was a match. Well, I guess Cammy just wanted to pull the strings a bit, and now with Leya trying to push this match to be a standard contest with rules and everything it’s sort of taking all the fun out of this. Regardless of all these new events Impact and Sovereign will face each other at Nightmare before Christmas. TO be honest I’ve never looked forward to Christmas. Even as a small child, my Father, at a young age ruined it all for me by telling me the truth about Santa. But now things have been looking up for the last few days. Impact, you’re my victim, my opponent. You might have the advantage in a run of the mill wrestling match. But if you think I have never wrestled in one then you should go back and check out the X-rated tournament when everyone except for Kage I wrestled straight. I won, I was champion for a long time Impact and now you think you have me cornered with your friend Leya, who just so happens to be my wife. You sure have woven a nice little web Impact, trying to get yourself into the best position for yourself. I’m sure that it doesn’t look good for poor little Sovereign, about to get the pay back he deserves from all the people he’s hurt over the years. Yeah, you won’t see me loosing any sleep over you Impact. I’ll be staying up all night looking forward to what I am going to do to you inside that ring on December 22nd of the year 2002. You came back Impact, into my life and took from me the Universal Title. The Title that I made important, the title I brought credibility to and now you think that you will be able to walk all over me in some sort of wrestling match? Trust me Impact when I tell you this because it’s going to be a very important life lesson for you. There is no such thing as a run of the mill, straight wrestling match with Sovereign. It doesn’t happen, it’s not a part of any reality. I am going to give you a lot more pain then were looking to receive. This is no cake walk, this is no party for you Impact. Injured or not I will destroy you at the Nightmare before Christmas and you will wish you never came back to Evolution. Sovereign shuffles his weight somewhat, his legs no longer crossed he pulls one leg up bent and rests his head against it, then starts to rock back and forth. A chuckle escapes his lips as he speaks on. Impact, I was watching yesterday the tapes of what I did to you on that day. I remember them taking you off in the Ambulance to the hospital. I remember Danny upset with me for what I did to you. Most thought I ended your career. I figured I came damn close but ending it, I wasn’t sure. I personally didn’t think about it for a while, I didn’t care. But I always in the back of my mind took comfort that a fool like you was hurt somewhere sitting at home with his family consoling him or something. Always hoping to be that hero that comes back and gets revenge. Maybe you’ll finally be that knight in shining armor that Evolution needs to purge the world of this monster. Some would say that it’s only going to take a monster to slay a monster and although that could very well be right, I’ve never much listened to what anyone has to say. You Impact, in your mind might be a monster, someone who is dangerous and should be taken very seriously. And while I take you seriously, and there is no doubt in my mind or body that you indeed are dangerous all that isn’t going to mean a whole heck of a lot when you are laying at my feet Impact. You came back, no one wanted you to except for my beautiful wife, and her feelings on the subject are shouted across the land. Hatred, hatred for me. She’s not the only one Impact. You two might make a good team. You two might actually hate me more than anyone on this planet. You two might even be able to take me out if you get what you want. But you two don’t realize that this isn’t what it seems. Leya and Impact taking on Sovereign. Sounds a but staked. Who’s the hero here, the big strong overpowering giant of a man with his assistant, and partner in crime. Or the poor, sick and injured once was champion? Sort of ironic if you look at it from that point of view Impact. And while I’ll be the only person in my corner so to speak I will give you everything I have to over in that match. You can have my blood and my sweat. You can take my bones and my soul but in the end Impact one way or the other you will not leave that arena without serious harm. Beat me if you can Impact, but there’s no way you will walk out of that ring. There’s no way you will walk into the next Legends under your own power, or without the aid of some sort of device that will need to hold up your worthless carcass. This week at Legends I get into the ring with you again Impact. We aren’t on a team, we aren’t fighting for anything important. And while I do think that taking some frustration out on L2J would be a good time I’m telling you this now. You are going to face ME on Legends. To hell with those other two that are in the match. I want you to look across the ring and see me. And then I want you to know what it will be like to look into my eyes and know what you will be up against. Because if you even make it out of Legends alive Impact, if you get out by the skin of your teeth then you can start to look forward to the TRUE beating you are going to be in for on the 22nd of December, the year 2002. Sovereign then begins to crawl towards the camera. He is slow in his process probably still because he feels the pain of his broken rib jamming into his body. He then stops, brings his hand up and wipes the hair from his face. His eyes are wild, wide and piercing into the camera as he parts his lips to speak. As for you Leya, as I said at Legends, your life will be made even more unbearable as weeks go by. You think you’ve one uped me by bringing Impact back to destroy me. But I can’t be destroyed. I’m bigger then the two of you, the three of us. You should have left me long ago. I wouldn’t have cared, I wouldn’t have made a fuss about it and let you free to live your life. To be honest I did love you Leya, I deeply did. You were the one person that had any ounce of hope in me. Now you’ve ruined that dream, that vision of a loving wife. And because you’ve cross that line of fantasy into the depths of reality you are going to be punished just as badly as Impact. I’ll never forgive you for bringing him here and costing me my title. I’ll never forgive you for anything that you do from now until I am finished with him. You might have been the master mind, you could have been the one that started all of this Leya. But I am going to finish it. I’ll put Impact back where he belongs and I’ll make sure that for the rest of your days you will regret every knowing me, or any past personality I may have held a name to. You should have stayed away, you should have gone back to Chicago and lived your life. But you didn’t and you are going to pay a heavy price Leya Forth, my darling wife. I hope that the both of you know what you’ve done. Because this week at Legends I will prove my point. Impact says there’s only room for 1 monster in Evolution. Well he’s right, there is. And if he things for a split second that it’s him that will remain behind, then he will need a wake up call. He will get it this week, and the bell will still be ringing in his ears after I finish him at Nightmare before Christmas. Sovereign sits back, chuckling to himself. I have been naughty, and I haven’t been nice. But Christmas is still coming for Sovereign, and a few days early at that. Sovereign stands up Happy Holidays Impact. Sovereign walks off, the camera cuts to snow.
