Indy Promotion Results

27 June 2003

Date: 2003-06-27 'Mason is firmly in control here' were the words from the lips of XCW play by play announcer Dean Hammond as he watched Sean Mason compete in an indy promotion match only a few days before his big PPV match this coming Sunday. In the ring Mason had controlled things from the off countering a huge burst of explosive right hands from his young rookie opponent, before then rolling through into a hammerlock hold. Wrenching down Mason pushed the youngster down before then scoring with a facebuster. ::Flasback:: 'Huge facebuster by Mason which gives him a foundation to build upon' Dean cried. 'You know its not often XCW fans are treated to an Evolution superstar but they appear to be enjoying every moment' his partner Dwayne Chambers replied. 'Its a chance for the fans to see a man who is at the very top of the mountain as the Continental Champion' Dean pointed out as he looked down at the second most important Evolution title which sat before him Mason takes Risk his opponent and sets up for a snap suplex, before then rolling through into another one which he bridged into a pinfall..........1.............2........Kickout by the youngster. 'Kickout there by Risk, but you have to think its no or never for the young rookie against this experience pro' Dwayne said turning toward Dean. Pulling Risk to his feet Mason sent him flying toward the ropes, before then following up with a hiptoss which sends him opponent tumbling across the ring. Risk quickly tries to get straight back to his feet, but is sent back to the canvas on four or five occasions under the weight of numerous knife edge chops to the exposed chest of the youngster. Just then Mason signalled to the crowd that the end was very much nigh, leaning backward he looked for the midas touch only for Risk to duck at the last minute causing Mason to get all tied up in the ring ropes. 'Mason just missed the Midas Touch' Dwayne remarked, 'its not often you see that' 'If ever' Dean replied. Making use of a number of stiff kicks to the ribs of Mason, Risk attempts to look for the head scissors using the ropes for leverage but Mason counters tossing Risk to the canvas with a bang. Risk is quick to rise to his feet though fighting through the pain knowing tonight could lead to him gaining his big break in wrestling. Ducking underneath a right hand from Mason, Risk comes off the ropes but only ends up running straight into the grasp of Mason who picks him up like a feather and scores with a high impact spinebuster before then rolling through into a sharpshooter. 'What the hell is this?' Dwayne asked confused. 'The end judging by the expression on Risks face' Dean replied, before then watching as the youngster began tapping the canvas begging for mercy. The bell sounds at this point and the referee calls for a break of the hold. 'We haven't seen that before' Dean announced for a second time. 'Nevertheless it has resulted in a successful performance by Mason' Dwayne replied. 'Its reminiscent of something I have seen before though' Dean said watching as Sean enjoyed the victory as the referee held his hand high in the aftermarth of this warm up match to New Dawn.
