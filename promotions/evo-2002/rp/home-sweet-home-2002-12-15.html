Home Sweet Home

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 15 Falls Count Anywhere In the world of wrestling there are a lot of matches that exist used to truly send out a brutal message. There are many matches that are out there to express hatred for your fellow man. But in the Falls Count Anywhere match you deal with the must violent and unpredictable contest in Professional Wrestling. No Boundaries. No rules. As long as the Ref can keep up with the two combatants the match will continue until finally one is pinned or gives up. Through his 6 years of actual competition Sovereign has been in 28 Falls Count Anywhere contests. His last was the 2nd fall of the best of 3 against Daniel Somers in August of 2001 for the OSW World Championship, and Sovereign took that fall. He dominated it, over a superstar of the caliber of Somers. No one since then has dared to fight Sovereign in this sort of match. Out of those 28 times he fought Falls Count Anywhere he never lost, each time being declared the victor and now in 2 weeks Impact must face Sovereign inside the rules of perhaps the most unruly match in all of wrestling. All thanks to Sovereign’s deal with Leya. Of course, the question remains that we know what Leya wants. But what does Sovereign really want? Not the most comforting thought that would go through your mind. And while Legends went off the air with Shocka’s hand raised in victory with his 2nd straight pin of L2J, Sovereign’s hand was slamming into the face of Impact, a horrible end to a horrible night for the “Hollywood Monster” and Leya While Impact gets to sit and bite his nails Xavier Citizen must bare down and fight Sovereign this coming week in this match. Sovereign’s relationship with Daniel Somers must not have suffered recently as Sovereign put in the request personally. Somers made the match, and as Evolution returns to the United States after a 3 week tour of Canada the main event at Legends will be Citizen vs Sovereign, the Fall counting anywhere. Unsure are we about Citizen’s thoughts about this match. And although Mr. Citizen is a very worthy opponent and I’m sure that he can hold his own against most if not any man on this planet, you still have to wonder about his chances? I personally have a feeling that the outcome of this match is far more then just who ever gets the “W” on their record. But it’s a message to Impact, a message to let him know that perhaps he should have stayed away. Perhaps his pride has gotten the better of the big man. Sovereign might have not ever faced a man of Impact’s size and strength. But Impact has never faced a man as methodical, as ruthless as Sovereign? So does it even out? Footsteps fall on a wooden floor. The sound of wind up against a glass window and the breathing of another person are heard. The only things spark your ears until the breaking of silence. The voice, Sovereign. The Tone, calm. Sovereign enters into the light from a dark setting, his face is lax, no emotion. The hair is pulled out of his face, hooked behind his ears as the sound of the wind once again racks the windows. Welcome to my home. Yes, the place where I make my home. For months you know I’ve been on the road. Secluded in hotel rooms. In Hopsitals, in arenas on busses, or Airplanes. This is the first chance I’ve got to get back here in a long, long time. I bought this house with Leya about 5 years ago. It’s a nice, quite part of Chicago. Not many people around, mostly young families with small children. Of course this is actually where Leya lives now. She’s stayed here for all these years. I see her personal belongings all over the place here. It’s dark, and the power is out so it’s sad that I can’t show you all her nice things she’s bought with all my money over the years. I actually finally figured out see. I figured out why she never divorced me. It’s because I’m a wealthy man friends. I have made my money, and in honesty I don’t care to much for it. I have no use for gold rings, fine cars or drinking Campaign out of the bottle of my personal collection. I don’t vacation in the Bahamas and I don’t go Skying in the alps. Most people with my sort of income do those sorts of things. However they don’t really give me much cause for joy or celebration. I mean, this place is a palace now. I see her big screen TV. She’s got a water bed upstairs. She has walk in closets, there are extra keys to a Porsche here on the table in front of me. She has been living it up while I’ve been bleeding for years on the floors of high school gyms all over this planet. So I guess that answers it. She wants to divorce me now... maybe because she’s found her own new cash cow in Impact. Sovereign walks around, the light from the camera is powerful enough to cast some visual aid on the room now as it seems the power is slowly coming back on. There’s one hell of a storm going on outside right now, the wind is banking off the windows as if it’s trying to break into the house and steal the carpets. But Sovereign picks up a black book. I never thought that she wouldn’t have gone after other men. You’ve all see Leya, she’s quite a hot momma as they say. Impact and Leya... I guess that makes sense. He’s a big strong dominating male with a lot of money. That seems to be what she wants these days. Money and revenge. I never thought of her like that. Simply because when we were together money meant next to nothing. We just wanted to be a family. Well obviously those things changed. I guess time really does change people, no matter how much inside of you you’d wish they’d always come back. That’s what she said kept her going. Right to my face at Faith. Even though she thought it was her past coming back to make everything better I know that she still loves William. And if I had a heart that was not a vicious animal like I seem to be I’d probably miss her to. Actually to be honest there were nights when I was sleeping with the rats and bugs where I’d close my eyes and remember her. Remember how she treated me. I’d remember her touch, her voice and I thought to myself that some day. That maybe some day I’d be able to come back into this house and live a normal life. Those days have passed. Sovereign throws the book across the room. Funny how I’m being pegged the villain when this woman stole my money, my house and my title right from under me. It’s not like I can really control my temper folks, and you all cheer her and Impact on like mindless zombies hoping and praying that he’ll destroy me at this up coming Pay Per View event. But it won’t happen. It’s not going to happen because Impact is going to wish he never EVER came back here when I’m finished with him in our Falls Count Anywhere match. The last time I fought one, I destroyed 3 other men to retain my Title. I ended Jamal Kage’s career that night at Digital Destruction and Impact, this time I won’t let up until your broken body is lifeless and the fans in the arena are crying in their seats. You and Leya took from me the only thing I held dear to me in the Universal Title. And while that little title belt rests on L2J’s shoulder right now I know that some day I will get it back. But Impact, it’s not something I’m thinking about right now. I’m thinking about all the things I want to do to you at Nightmare before Christmas. I know that you’re a big bad man Impact. I know that you will hurt me. But I know that there’s no way that you’ll survive me. You sit back and remember what it felt like with the nurses and doctors surrounding you in that Hospital. You remember the pain that shot through your body when you even tried to move. Well Impact, you won’t even be thinking about wanting to try and move after I finish punishing you for the sake of what you did to me. I will beat you, bludgeon you, cut you, break you and do everything to you that my Body will allow. You see Impact, people are documenting that you are bigger, stronger then me. Well you didn’t seem so big when I chopped you down to size the last time we came face to face. You didn’t seem so dangerous to me when I stood in your face and watched you loose control all those times in Vancouver. You aren’t so hot Impact. You are not some Monster that you’ve been hyped up to be. Although for sure I’ll admit that you got the best of me twice now. But when a man’s back is turned I don’t know how valid it is. I’ve hit many people when they’re backs were to me. Sure, I might not consider it to be some sort of major accomplishment but it sure felt good. And every single time I finally faced them in the ring I ripped them limb from limb. This time, Impact you have crossed me. You got your little taste of revenge already Impact and while you still no doubt want more you better realize fast that in a week at Nightmare Before Christmas your revenge will be bitter sweet. Bitter by defeat and sweet by the taste of your own blood! Sovereign flips over the sofa and then grabs the coffee table and flips it over as well. He then pulls a book case over and then pulls the big Screen TV down onto it’s face, the sound of shattered glass is heard as Sovereign screams out something that makes no sense. Impact, you and I will beat the hell out of each other. Impact, you will suffer a fate worse then anything that I have done to anyone before. That in and of itself should put you into the proper mind frame. You should call your mother and tell her you love her, pay all your loose bills and give your belongings to charity because where you are going Impact, you won’t need them. You are heading for a world of pain and suffering when that bell rings. I will be there looking across from you Impact, my eyes will be locked in yours and that is when you will attack. I know you Impact, you want to hurt me. You want to cause me a lot of pain, and suffering as well. I mean, hell I don’t blame you for wanting that, I almost broke you God Damn neck for no reason. But the problem is Impact that you came back. You came back and bit off more then you can chew. As for you Luther Wisdom, you indeed are being led to the wolves. A hungry wolf. An angry wolf and an animal that has been waiting for a good feeding in some time in me. Impact has got my hunger growing and now it’s time for the pre-show warm up. Yes, all sounds rather cocky and arrogant. Not like me really. But you’ll excuse me if I disregard all you worthless babble about having nothing to loose. You sure as hell have a lot to loose Wisdom. You and your partner’s title match. Your entire career to be honest. I don’t care about tag teams, and I don’t care about brotherhoods. You go and ask Malcolm King what it’s like to step into the ring with me and he’ll tell you about the time I suplex him off the top of a steal cage, I split him open ear to ear and left him laying while my former friends pummeled him to a pulp. Go watch the video and ask yourself if you are the sort of a man that really has anything to lose. You more then likely have a lot more to gain out of this match Wisdom than anyone. The underdog beats the monster. It would be a heck of a story my friend. But it isn’t going to happen. A nd while the dirrect outcome of this match isn’t want is important to me, it’s more like everything in-between the start and the finish. All that fun stuff where I beat you down, torture your body and make you wish that you hadn’t been involved. Made you wish you never said a word about the match. No I’m not the Universal Champion. Yes I can be beaten Wisdom. But for you to beat me it’ll take a hell of a lot more than anything you can put together. I lost the Universal Title because I was held down by a 350lbs man after I fell through 2 cages, off the side of another and was rasing a 200lbs man up a ladder. That’s what it took to beat me for the Universal Title Wisdom. I’ve not had my shoulder’s pined yet in Evolution. That match at Rise of the Legends was my 1st singles loss. So can I be... be beaten? Yes, I can and I have been beaten. But you Wisdom are about to really discover what being beaten is all about. You want simple Logic Wisdom... as simple as it comes. This week at legends you will be the example of what a Falls Count Anywhere match is like with Sovereign. I’m going to make you bleed, I’m going to make you hurt all over just so that Son of a Bitch Impact can see what he’s got to look forward to at Nightmare Before Christmas. Respect me all you want Wisdom, I’ve earned it. Fear me all you want Wisdom.. I have earned it. Bleed for me all you can Wisdom... you’ll earn every last stitch that will be used to close the wounds. Sovereign turns his back to the camera and starts to exit the room. See you in St. Paul Luther Sovereign chuckles to himself wildly as the scene fades to black
