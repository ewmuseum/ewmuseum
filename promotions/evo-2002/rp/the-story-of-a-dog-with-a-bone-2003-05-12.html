The Story of A Dog With A Bone

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-12 ::Foreword:: Sometimes people are so foolish. When Sean Mason says something, it isnt something that should fall on deaf ears. I told Asis, I told him I was not in Evolution to lose. I told Asis I was not in Evolution to watch him as he revelled in the failure of others. From the moment I returned from injury I told Asis, that nothing was going to distract me from my mission. In truth I've returned to Evolution clutching the metaphorical bone. The bone in this case is Asis and no matter how hard he tries to shake me from him, there isn't anything that could stop me from getting straight back up and taking Asis to a point he thought was set aside for his opponents. Nevertheless Asis ignored me. Asis tried to sweep me under the carpet and bury my hopes well before time was called on my challenge. Fear may govern the lives of those Asis has faced in the past; but he would be a fool if he truely believed deep down that I am living in fear of the man I promised to destroy before the fans of Evolution only weeks before. Often as a child I would stay over at my friends houses occasionally. During those times I can remember my friends complaining of their fears and most commonly of darkness. I don't know if Asis was like that as a child, but what I do know is that when the lights went out during Legends this past week his face would have been ravaged by fear. Fearing not because of the darkness itself, but what he knew it might hide. As I wrapped my arms around his body I could feel the fear pumping through his veins; I could feel the sweat flowing across his body and I could feel the very moment he realised that the dogs of hell had risen from the grave he had tried to dig only a week earlier. Fear was only the start though, I didn't show during Legends to make a man fear me, I came to send a message that even a fool could understand. Etching that crutch onto the surface of his neck and dragging the air from his lungs in the process, was my attempt at showing no matter how he tried I would continue to jump back and match my bark with a fierce bite. ::Scene Opens:: Its warm afternoon as the cameras fade into a scene of beauty and tranquility. Standing in the sunshine looking across a beautiful landscape is Sean Mason. In the background sits a picnic scene, but for a second it seems Sean has left both the food and his friends in the background as he looks to address more pressing matters. Raising his sunglasses, he places them upon his head and turns toward the camera man who has been called in to record the brief words of 'The Prodigal Son'. Raising a rhye smile he checks upon his friends, before taking a step toward the camera and step away from tranquility and back toward the war zone. 'This isn't about sending yet more messages to Asis' Sean began, 'its about building upon a point.' Pausing Sean put his fist in the palm of his other hand and collected his thoughts. 'When Legends went live it was obvious by the smug look upon the face of Asis he believed he was the victor. The very smile, the very stride of Asis suggested victory had been his during the last pay per view; but since when has a war been won prior to the opposition being neutralised?' 'Coming out of the PPV I will admit I felt rough. Infact the events of the PPV really took a toll upon my body, testing me to limits I thought existed far beyond those I had previously seen. However no amount of foundation is going to hide the fact that Asis too felt the pain, Asis too suffered for his gains and no amount of editing was going to hide the fact that my aspirations had died upon that night. All the PPV did was strengthen my resolve and reminded me just how bad I want to turn the tables of Evolutions new myth' Sean said staring right down the lens of the camera. 'I've said all along I respect what Asis has achieved, but the simple fact is I object to his means. Following the PPV I learnt the monster sure as hell had weaknesses, that inspired me upon waking to my senses; however upon reflection perhaps I was too leniant, I wasn't ruthless enough. I had the heart, I had the desire; I simply didn't have the ruthless streak needed to condem Asis to the history books of the Continental Title. To beat the monster, I learnt the hard way that you have to beat him at his own game'. 'With all that in mind though, this week I know its not about Asis, however don't be under any illusion I've forgotten about him either. Seara is merely a means of gaining the right to do battle once again with my biggest foe. Seara and I have a history, but trust me you could mickey mouse for all I care and you would still be on the receiving end of a serious beating Evolution had not seen for some years. In some ways I'm not going to talk too much about Seara the person, I'm simply going to talk about Seara the moving target. When that bell rings and the target steps before me I can't afford to hold back and I can't afford to leave anything less than a victor. I expect Asis to come seeking revenge, I just have to be ready for that. In the first place I have to seek the win, I've the scent of Asis to drive me on, its just a case of ensuring that referee has no choice but to count three and take me one step closer to Asis' Sean said as the emotion began to take over. 'One step closer to ending the myth' Sean said leaning toward the camera in a menacing tone 'until such point I am a man with a unquenched thirst for the blood of the one man I dispise without sense of mercy'.
