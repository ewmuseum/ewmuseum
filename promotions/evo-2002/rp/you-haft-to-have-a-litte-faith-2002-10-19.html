You Haft To Have A Litte Faith...

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-19-02 The scene starts off with Prohibited in an a run down apartment where he doesn't know how he could of lost to HBC and that other guy who doesn't even matter to SP right now. The half torn mask of Strictly Prohibited dangles with left eye patch of the mask ripped where you can see an brown eye of the man under the mask! Strictly Prohibited gets up and throws some boxes, and some chairs around in the broken down place, where the heat is never on, and the coldness is forever. Strictly Prohibited walks over to a busted mirror to look at himself, but then looks away really fast.' Prohibited: Jinx, you miss guided fool, you dare to stick your nose into someones business that isn't yours to stick your nose into! HBC you may of gotten the better of me for once in your life, I'll give you that, you did outsmart me for once, maybe because I'm not the innovator I once was, maybe because my denial just is in my way, I have no idea but you see HBC we are not finished! I am in this ppv, which is bad news for you HBC, I get to go one on one with Jinx in a hardcore match, heh....you fools you dunno what you are doing, you just think you know me, but really you have no idea at all, what I can do to you! Jinx your deathbed awaits you as I will fight you at Faith! Faith is what you will need Jinx to get past this match with me fighting you! Faith is what HBC will need when I get my hands on her! I felt my blood on my hands, on my face as you two beat me to the ground, with not one ounce forgiveness. 'Prohibited moves away from the mirror grabbing the kendo stick that he cares for so much, he turns right around and smashes the mirror with the kendo stick! He smashes it with every crushing blow, he turns around and breaks something else with the kendo stick!' Strictly Prohibited: Well Jinx, seems that you and I have some unfinished business that we need to take care of so I can take care of that bitch known as HBC! Jinx you have no idea who your stepping in the ring with, 12 months I sat at home, I sat and watch the destruction of this business that is pro wrestling, after $W turned there backs on me! Everyday I waited for my moment to return....but when will this moment come? When will this moment be? You see, I will show who I really am when I feel is right, when I take out a man, or a women that needs to see a ghost from its past, that they thought was gone forever! Oh have I waited for a moment to come back! You have no idea how long I have waited to put this kendo stick threw its skull, you have no idea how much I want to destory HBC! 'Prohibited takes a deep breath, and grips the kendo stick some more.' Strictly Prohibited: You see HBC, Jinx, you two have no idea what danger you are in with me watching your every move, watching your every weakness, you dunno where I came from, you dunno who I am! All you do know is that I am here, that I can come at you at anytime! You live with that for rest of your pathetic lifes! This is my one shot, my once chance to take care of you! Not that I won't have more chances but I came here for bigger means, bigger people. Not taking you two out of your spotlight, but I came here for a man who doesn't even know I'm here yet, one day he will know who just is after him, right now he doesn't even know who is after him, he knows is that he is happy at the moment. All he knows that nothing can touch him! All he knows is that he does have an fallen angel watching him. Strictly Prohibited: HBC, you are still the same women I beat every week back in EdW, but yet you have no idea who I am, you have no idea of my past, what battles i've been in before, what danger you people are in! I am the 'Messiah Of Wrestling'. Don't think you are safe from your maker, because I am here to fix the wrong you people have made, the people who need to know when to call it quits, you see 12 months I sat at home watching the carnage while I sat at home, not able to get work because of people not willing to put up with my way of living, not willing to watch such an innovator as I! The score is still tied, the score is still even! This score we have...is just getting good, so sit back....and watch Jinx meet his maker, while I beat him to his last breath, to where he can't even continue! All I want is my chance back at you HBC, jinx, and the person I have yet to name! Endeavor my curse! Endeavor my soul, Endeavor my past!
