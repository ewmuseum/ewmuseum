The Coronation: Part III

7 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-07 As the guests arrived, Ethan was promptly reminded by the function manager of the need for his presense to be felt on the 'floor'. The floor not in the literal sense but the intention was to convey the need for Ethan to be seen at his own function and not for him to be hidden from sight. Ethan being Ethan had no problem with entertaining a crowd, no sense of nerves had been detected and indeed Ethan continued to imply that any nerves should not be felt by himself but by his guests who perhaps were less familiar to such grand surrounds as the Coles estate. The first sight the guests see upon arrival are a number of stewards who promptly see both them and their cars ushered into position. The lobby is decorated from the very floor to the ceiling, attention to detail is pivotal and as you suvery the scene almost in a trance you are continually reminded by the auderve carrying attendants that this is only the beggining. Forget the sausage rolls and the pork scratchings, on each platter sit various samples of global cuisine and once again no sign of penny pinching. 'Name please sir' the man said on the door tentatively greeting one of the later arrivals. 'Bob' was the reply accompanied by a belch. 'Surname sir?' asked the man tasked with greeting what looked to be a drunken stray. Bob looked back puzzled. 'Surname sir' the man said growing impatient. At that point enough of a stir was generated to grab Ethans attention. Ethan was standing on the balcony surveying the scene but he had missed the arrival of Bob, which was suprising given the fact he did stand out from the crowd somewhat in his tattered shreds of cloth. Quickly scampering down the stairs, Ethan made his presense felt. 'Bob' Ethan said ushering Bob away from the crowd. 'I'm suprised you could make it' Ethan said aloud, very much suprised since he had given Bob both the wrong day and wrong venue in the hope of shaking him from his sights. 'Champagne sir?' the waitor said to someone in the background. Bob didn't need to be asked twice, infact he didn't need to be asked at all it would appear. Turning round he moved in for the kill taking a glass from the silver platter and then turning back to his second priority the convesation with Ethan. Bob raised his glass looking for a toast, Ethans response was a woeful sigh. 'Benjamin' Ethan called over to one of his aides. 'Take Bob outside and show him the grounds' 'The grounds?' Benjamin said reluctantly. 'Yes the grounds' Ethan remarked with a stiff jab to the ribs. 'Right away sir' Benjamin replied with the wind somewhat taken from his lungs. 'And don't rush over any aspect' Ethan reminded Benjamin with a telling glance. 'Well of course not sir' Benjamin replied sizing up his mission. Keep Bob away from the party seemed to be the unspoken task. Mission impossible?! Only time would tell.
