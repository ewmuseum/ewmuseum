A dream continues..

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-29 'In the bible it says what goes around comes around hommie shot me three weeks later he got shot down.Now its clear I am here for a real reason,he got hit like I got hit but he aint fuckin breathin.' -Curtis aka 50 cent The Dream The scene opens in Queens, New York outside of some old apartments. X is standing outside of his home with two of his friends.The three men are just chillin. All three are young black men around the age of 18.X is wearing a black poofy zip up jacket with a white shirt underneth it with some blue jeans. The man to the left of him,Cash, is wearing close to the same cloths as X. The man to the right of X,Rob,his wearing a white hooded jacket with the hood over his head. Cash looks around as he isnt saying much witch worries X. X looks over at rob and says'Whats up with him?' Rob looks at X and shakes his head no. He then reaches in his pocket as he pulls out a gun. He looks at X 'Man there has been some foul brothas running around talking about us. They are the same brathas that took it too Cash's older brother last week.'Rob says. X shakes his head no as he cant believe it. He looks over to Cash and tells him'What did we say Cash?Did we not say we where not going to bring down our community? All three of us said that we would not result to the killing of more black americans. To much of this shit goes around. We all need to learn to end conflicts with words not guns.' He looks at Rob as Rob places his head down as if he is sad for even bringen the gun out. But Rob never puts the gun up. X looks at him getting impatient as Rob looks away from the two men and says'I cant do it X. These brathas said they where ganna take care of Cash next. And if you had respect for us you would understand. If you really think we are like a family you would have one of these for when they come.' X then stands right in front of Robs face as he cant believe he just said that. He looks over at Cash as he too reaches into his pocket and pulls out a gun.'Are yall getting crazy on me? Damn it we agreed that this is not the fuckin answer.' The two men ignore X as he then just sits down on the steps with his head in his hands. A yellow explorer then begins to come down the street. Rob and Cash jumps up with there guns hanging on there side.X slowly stands up waiting on the car to come. Rob looks at them and says'Thats them X.'X looks at both of them and says'Listen no going off with guns damn it.' The explorer reaches the apartments. Six men jump out the car as they all go and stand in front of the three men. X looks at the guy who is leading the crew. The same man that is standing right in front of X. X looks at him and begins to speaks'Listen my people, we are here not to kill one another. We are the same people fighting against the white mans world. Why do we see the need to kill one another? Why do we see the need to go against our people? We can solve these issues by discussions and debates instead of knives and guns. If you pull those triggers it will leave blood over these streets. The same color of blood that runs through your veins. From the people with the same color as you. As X goes to place a hand on the mans shoulder one the mans friends begins to shoot as he hits X in the side. Cash then shot the leader dead in the chest as Rob shot the man who shot X. As soon as they shot fire the other men shot back at Rob and Cash as both of them where hit. Rob was hit in the leg and shoulder as Cash was hit in the heart. Cash lied motionless as Rob was on the ground in pain. X stay motionless holding his side. There was only four of the other crew still remaining as they all jumped in the yellow explorer and ride off. End Of Dream He wakes up with his hand on his side as he looks down and you can see the mark from the gun shot . He sits up in his bed as he speaks. 'X said things would not result to violence.But Ali you have got in X's way,and for that reason you are not going to walk out that cell. Realize though the movemant is in place as your are not a victim but a stepping stone. Live it up with your friend Alex all you can..because the black label is here..as it is the mark of X..dont forget that.' The camera fades black as it is early in the mourning for X but he crawls back into the bed for some more sleep.
