Mind Games

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-22 Within the confines of a small room, with a huge desk and a twenty-four inch plasma television leaning against the wall and resting on the desk. There is a black VCR next to the right arm of Greatness, who is tenatively staring at the television watching the match were Shocka lost the Universal championship to Scott Frost. As Frost was sent running forward he managed to get a foot up on the table and using his speed and momentum ran up the table and into a whisper in the wind variation that connected on Shocka. ”What a move by Frost!” Justin yelled. “He displayed some incredible agility just then.” 'What is wrong with you Shocka? You used to be soo great. You are a former Universal champion, shocka. You are the first ever Road To Glory champion, Shocka. Where are your balls at Shocka? WHERE?!' Greatness screams. The video on the television suddenly freezes up and Greatness spins around in his chair, Greatness through his eye and mouth expression underneith the mask is apartent that he is upset. 'Shocka, where is the man that had plan after plan rolled up under his sleeve? What happened to the man, that was one of the greatest heels of eVolution history?! WHAT HAPPENED TO YOU SHOCK?!' Greatness screams away. 'WHERE DID YOU HIDE TO?!' Greatness conintues. Greatness throws his hands up into the air and rolls his head a bit. 'Oh yeah! I forgot! You hid behind the man that beat you, and took your life away from you! You have became the eptiomy of success, and then your soul....your passion....your heart, it died. You hid behind it all, and you protected them when they needed you most. You should of been a real man. You should of fought back, you should of made yourself....your pressence...YOU SHOULD OF MADE YOURSELF KNOWN!!! You were the greatest, then you allowed Scott Frost to one up you because psychically you were to weak. Mentally you should of learned, instead of fell. Shocka....it's going to be me and you, heel against face. But I ain't going to be like you, I ain't going to fall behind the end. I will continue to kick and punch until I better you. Win, lose, or draw at A New Dawn. YOU will go up against Greatness. Tell me something Shocka, do you see your name on the Marquee? Will you feel the Marquee Effect? It's up to you Shocka...will you stay weak, or will you punish me.....Shocka.....it's up to you now...' Greatness pronounces.
