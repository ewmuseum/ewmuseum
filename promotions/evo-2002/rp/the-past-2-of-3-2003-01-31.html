Date: 1.31.02 I've been seperated from my brother, the only thing that really contained any importance toward me, and now he's gone. My entire world, is gone... I can't take this, why did it have to happen to me? I know it was eleven years ago but it's hard to forget about a thing that you lost back then, there's not a day when I don't think about my little brother Angel. We're supposed to switch parents every week but they decided not to do that. I want to spend a little time with my brother and I can't because of their dumbasses. Well, it's been alright around here... My dad's got me training to be a Professional Boxer when I grow older, but I told him I was more into Ultimate Fighting, he insisted boxing was a better profession though, so I'll go ahead and do that. I've gotten alot bigger, my body size and muscular spand as grew alot larger, a whole lot larger. I'm only fifteen years old but I look about eighteen, atleast that's what people tell me. I'm just beginning High School, I was retained in the 5th Grade for bad behavior so I'm right here with everybody else. It's about that time to start training with my father. He's calling my name... god, just leave me the fuck alone damnit I can tell that he don't want to listen so I better go ahead before he gets bitchy... I turned my body over toward the left, facing my father in the large squared circle. I looked at him with his palm bags strapped on tightly trying to wave me over toward him. 'Come on..boy!' He waved faster, so I began to jog over toward him. I can't believe that his man has got me fighting for no reason, I like the training believe me, but I'd rather be over somewhere with my little brother, wrestling or something. My father was holding the pads tightly up against his hands pushing them forward, he was getting agitated, so I rolled into the ring and stood infront of him. He smirked some and held them in place and the training was on... '...LEFT!' he ordered out toward me, exclamitory in a way and instinct shoved it's way into my body and sent my left arm from it's original resting place right into the red, sand filled bag. 'Right! Left! Right! Right! Left! Left! Right! Right! Right! Right! Left!' I through my hands at the precise moments that he shouted out his commands, my right arm launched itself forward, my left hand smashed into the bag, my right hand smashed into the bag once more, followed by another swift right hand that exploded forward into the bag, then a sudden left threw itself outward and smashed into the bag, followed by one more quick left, and a sudden right at appeared after that, then my fist exploded with rights, right, right, then I finished it off with a dynamite left. I watched my dad's arm fly backward and sand began to leak from the bag. I kept my stance up and looked forward, and waiting for another command, but he stopped and dropped the pads at his sides, folded his arms across his chest and smiled some at me. He untucked his left hand and rubbed it against my head and began to praise me...'You gettin' betta boy!' I couldn't help but smile, but then I noticed a picture of Angel that caught my attention. I shook my head some and backed away from him. My thoughts were completely on my little brother, he was probably thinking about something else though. I stepped from the ring and headed over toward the wall, walking slowly, the knuckle tape stitched onto my hand began to get slippery and sweaty but I ignored it. I looked forward and stared directly into the picture. He was smiling, it was a recent picture of him, and he looks just like me. Only his hair is shorter than mine. I smiled some and turned away from it to go back to my father when I had noticed that he was talking to a man, I stayed in my position and watched him thoroughly, I noticed that he was acting pecuiliar so I lowered my head and took about 3 quarter steps closer and silenced myself to get a good ear on exactly what he was saying... 'Just gimmie a couple more months, i'ma have the money for the rent no time. I promise...' 'You said that the last couple of months ago, and now that it's that couple of months, I want my rent money...' 'Just gimme a couple of more months!' '...allright, but you have 4 more months, if you don't have the money by then, you're out of here and on the street.. alright?' 'God bless you...' 'Yeah, yeah... you just make sure to have my fuckin money...' We're behind on the rent? Why didn't dad tell me? I would've pitched in and helped him pay the money... I know what i've got to do... .lapse. 'How old're you kid?' The man's breath had reeked of Paul Masson and shit. He stepped closer and awaited my answer, and I maintained the lie... 'Eighteen...' 'That's good enough, well kiddo, you can't go by your own name here so you've go to make up a name over here...' '...i'll be... Angel...' 'That's kind'uva fruity name but oh well, whatever floats your boat. Your first fight will be against a new comer, the better of the two of you will have an iron clad contract to us the Underground Fighting Zone.' 'Alright, who is it?' 'A guy called The Bear..' '...whatever you say...' I hate what I have to dominate myself by fighting people to probably death just to help my father out. But I can do it.. if it means to help out my family, I can do it. I can do this, I'm going to own this fighting zone and whoever has the balls to stop me, come at me... but these guys are 20 to 40 years old. It's crazy. I'm only 15 years old, but I have what it takes... just watch.. i'll help you dad... just watch.
