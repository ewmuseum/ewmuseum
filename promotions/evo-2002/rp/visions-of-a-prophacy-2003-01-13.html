Visions..Of A Prophacy!

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-13-03 'Learking in the shadows of the halls of the evo libary, is a man you all fear everynight you lay your head to rest, and every moment you feel you are being watched!' Strictly Prohibited: Hmmm, looky here..the big bad beast has signed the dotted line to the valley of the lost souls I have ended. Asis are you even in the condition to fight for your life like you will at legends? I question your motives Asis, but this is your desire to face me then face me you will, but fare warning..I don't plan on losing, and I don't plan on going easy on you. I look forward in this tourney for me beating you then advancing onward in the Title tournyto become a champion. You and your little friends have some soul searching to do Asis, and you will do some soul searching after I knock you around a few times with my kendo stick! Tick, tock Asis your time grows near as the clock will chime and my mask will show..you hell, and I'll show you things that you yourself thought you knew, but never dreamed of seeing! Asis, your time grows near and your little friends will not save you from what I have planned for you, and your friends. Asis sleep with the nightlight on, because you surely will need it..because I never sleep, and I never stop watching what I am gona kill..and you Asis are next on the long list of my prophacy!
