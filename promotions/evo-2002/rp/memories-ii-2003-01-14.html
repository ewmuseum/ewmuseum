Memories II

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 14th, 2003 The blood that was spilt remains stained, dried upon the pavement of the site where it had all changed. He was the one responsible for it all. It was his actions that took it to this level. He is the one who forced me to take these steps. He couldn’t just stay dead could he? No he had to come back. They ALWAYS come back! No matter what it is that people like me do it’s always the evil in our society which seems to resurface. He took it all away. But most importantly he took her. He will not get away with that in the least but I learned my lesson the first time. I learned that I must bide my time this time around. I cannot allow myself to rush into this battle that I cannot win alone. I must outsmart those who think they are the chess players. In reality all of them are nothing more than pawns in this game which I now play. From day one the pieces have been in motion. The wild card has already been in play thanks to me and he simply awaits the time much like I do. From the start no one even questioned the decision or who he even was. Soon those who are in the way and opposing us will regret that choice. The coincidence of it all never even escaped me. My wild card lies in wait much like the enemy. They have both watched from the shadows as the pieces moved, each one seeking the quickest and easiest path to take the queen.
