#1 Stunna

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.8.02 Four corners match {Four corners in a ring…four corners which will be occupied by four men that are in a heated rivalry…Four men that have deep rooted hate within themselves for each other. Shocka hates L2J…L2J hates Shocka….L2J hates Sovereign…Sovereign hates L2J…Sovereign hates Impact…everyone hates Impact. Now with these four men in the ring you could easily say that this will be the biggest match to ever be on Legends…all four men are men that could walk out with the eVolution Universal championship on any given day. L2J holds the title, Sovereign is a former champion, Shocka is the #1 Contender and Impact is an old star rising again. In this match violence and hatred will overtake everyone inside of the ring…it will be like wild animals fight for there survival.} ~~The slowly churning of a camera can be heard as it clicks on…you can read a little icon in the corner of the screen that reads E-TV. You see Shocka sitting down on his bed he grabs his phone and picks it up dialing a number. His delayed speech can only be assumed to be because of the person on the other end of the phone.~~ ‘Yeah I realize…that…you know what? No I don’t have time to listen to your bullshit I got enough on my hands!’ Shocka rolls his eyes and you can see him becoming more visibly angry. ‘You know what! I can’t handle your shit…I have my own stuff and I am dealing with some other stuff…No I am not going to tell you what it is….I’m NOT CHEATING ON YOU! What the hell is your problem.’ You can see Shocka’s face turn bright red with intensity as he continues yelling. ‘Don’t you talk to me like that…don’t you hang up that phone!’ Shocka screams before he slams the phone down himself. ~~He stands and begins pacing around the room thinking to himself. He runs his hands through his goatee and then over his spiked hair. He is in deep thought before he stops and begins speaking.~~ ‘I need to make them realize that I don’t need to support them!’ Shocka speaks to himself. ‘And I need to get focused on my match at legends damnit!’ ~Shocka’s cell phone can be heard ringing over his voice. He picks it up and begins speaking in a hurried tone.~ ‘Yeah roC I know…I don’t know roC…I don’t know…I dunno why damnit…why don’t you talk to them…yeah look them up….I dunno…I don’t know!’ Shocka sits down on his bed and continues speaking. ‘You want me to meet you where? Hold on let me write this down.’ ~After writing something down Shocka directs his attention toward the mirror he stares at himself in the mirror long and hard.~ ‘I am the man that pinned L2J…I am the one that held his shoulders to the mat for 3 seconds and was declared the winner…in those three seconds I could have became the Universal champion…I could have made L2J cry in that ring by taking his title.’ ~Shocka stands up looking over his self in the mirror he grabs his bag and picks it up he places it on the bed. He takes out a shirt suitable for a night club and begins speaking.~~ ‘The first phase to focusing on this match is to get my mind off of it…’ Shocka smiles to himself and stands up. ~The scene fades and you see Thug roC standing talking on his cell phone he is directing his attention to an eVolution camera crew. He is making some plans setting them up for Shocka to cut an impromptu interview. He discusses them going over the plan carefully trying to make sure that every detail is followed that nothing can go wrong.~ ~A Porsche now slowly pulls up in front of a club called the ‘Hot Box’ Shocka jumps out with a shiny shirt on. It is covered with a black grid and silver shine radiating off of it. He walks to the front door of the club and speaks to the bouncer.~ ‘Its Jonathan McCade…’ He speaks with a subtle tone. ‘GO right ahead they are expecting you…’ The bouncer speaks to him allowing him to go in. ~As Shocka walks in a camera begins recording and thug roC runs up to him and whispers something into his ear. Shocka looks a little bit stunned as the light is shining in his face. The club is empty except for Thug roC, Shocka and the camera and sound man.~ ‘We are here with the #1 Contender for the Universal title Shocka…and he is going to be sharing his feelings with all of us here tonight on New-TV!’ ~Thug roC screams with a s mile looking into the camera he now steps back and allows Shocka room to speak. Shocka looks slightly confused before he thinks of something to say.~ ‘You know what! Sovereign you are making a mistake…you are only focusing in on one man in this match that involves four! You are making your feelings known that you are only worried about one person and that is Impact. Well I am going to make sure to break you of those feelings; I am going to make you take notice of me! I am going to make you and everyone else realize that I am the best thing in this federation! I am the #1 Contender for the title that you used to hold Sovereign I knocked you down on the ladder and I didn’t even have to face you to do so!’ Shocka screams before moving over to a bar stool and sitting down. ‘You know what….You are going to be made an example of…me and you have never locked up in a ring…we have never shared a stare…and that time is going to be a special time in your life, the time when you realize that the human monster will fall victim to the #1 Stunna…you better realize that I am what the ratings are spiking for…and that you are just some boring played out mental patient with the mentality of a 4 year old and the comprehension of a new born! You don’t want to take notice of me? You just wait until I have you grasped in position for the #1 Stunna my friend then you will be begging for mercy…you will be wanting to join New-TV but the fact of the matter is we don’t want any washed up pieces of shit on our team…we are all about the New blood. Like me! You see I am shooting to the top of eVolution I was the best and longest reigning eVolution Continental champion and now there is nothing that can stand in my way of beating L2J and Nightmare Before Christmas which I will be headlining not you Sovereign!’ Shocka shakes his head a bit before continuing. ‘How does it feel to be left in the shadows? Huh? Before it would be Sovereign that, sovereign this…but now? Now the focus has shifted towards me…I have gained the upper hand and I am going to be the greatest Universal champion ever! I lived in your shadow and now its time for you to live in mine…you will realize what its like to be left behind. When I first joined eVolution no one thought that I could beat PPC…that I was just brought in for comic relief…well they couldn’t be more wrong…look at me now…I am only inches away from the Universal title and just days away from laying a beating into L2J, Sovereign and Impact that they will never forget!’ ~Thug roC walks up to Shocka and pats him on the back and then focuses his attention to the camera.~ ‘More exclusive interviews to come to from New-TV and don’t forget to kick back and enjoy a nice refreshing Mountain Dew.’ Thug roC slaps on a fake smile as the scene fades.
