final warning to eVo...evolved title contenders watch out p.1

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7/03 The scene opens up with a man walking in the darkness of a storm coming towards the camera as a narrator begins speaking about him… Narrator speaking “Underestimating is probably to most common mistake in the world, people expecting something less and getting something more. Homeowners get estimates for a job and get charged several thousand more. The typical pompous American walking the street thinks the Iraqi is a primitive foreigner incapable of fighting against the most advanced armor. Well they are proving they will stand up against a superpower no matter what the odds. Typical of eVo they have heard but they have not scene the man in action. They expect some pathetic newcomer. They expect a simple Iraq. A pathetic third world country is what they consider him. Hardly, he’s a superpower in this sport, a man who’s spent 7 years atop the sport of kings. Over 50 different championships have come across his shoulders in his career. Hedges himself has evolved from an underground destruction to a worldwide nuclear holocaust and to eVo’s surprise it’s hasn’t been a dud bomb shot but a nuclear missile and is on his way to hitting. This man Hedges is a force to be reckoned with and he has beaten some of your top world title contenders one of the picks to win NTICW’s title. Starting at the bottom rung is an easy task for this man. He has been overlooked many times and every time he throws all the doubt out of his way to the top. The warning he is sent is clear…He can do what he wants when he wants, gets what he wants and there’s nothing a single sole in eVo can do about it” With that the narrator stops speaking as the man seen is Hedges with rain dripping off him and a sinister look on his face as the scene cuts to black him laughing in a deep demonic tone as he green eyes begin to glow in the camera light and it fades and reopens inside a large mall. There is a large crowd lined up in front of a table which is positioned in front of a large eVo banner the scene then pans into a back room where Hedges and Thug Roc are relaxing. They can here the fans chanting outside waiting for eVo stars to appear for autograph signing… “Man these sorry ass fools probably expected some idiot like Shocka or Lee to come sign shit for them” Hedges laughs Roc takes a drink of his beer and replies “Oh you know it. They’re going to be pissed off these mystery star autograph signings give me a kick every time I walk out there. Ah look at the time we’re ten minutes late…” “Eh, what’s it matter I could really care less how long these fools wait. I wonder why they aren’t at work though it’s like 3 in the afternoon” exclaims Hedges “You know burger flippers and trash men work odd times” Roc chuckles out Hedges and Roc continue to enjoy their drinks and shoot the shit about the worthless eVo stars… “…You want a real tip Roc on how to really get under Shocka’s skin.” “Oh you know it Hedges” Roc sits up paying a bit more attention “You’ve got to knock his ego down to size. Humiliate him. You do this best by outsmarting him. He hates his typical big guy no brains act that he’s associated with. Just make a mockery of his intelligence. Hell he’s got a midget with him tear, him to pieces for it.” “Yeah makes sense” Roc proclaims “Oh it’s reality. I’ve spent years making him look like a fool. Playing off his words, making them backfire. That mans got nothing on me. Once you get the mental edge the physical falls right into place 6-0 singles over him never ever has he pinned me and he never will. He’s just an overrated chump. Plain and simple.” Thug laughs and throws his empty bottle against the wall as glass shatt6ers all over the floor. “You know Hedges New-TV is practically the strongest force in eVo today. I can’t see a group with enough talent to overcome one of us, especially in team evolution.” RoC boast. “Oh your damn right, Frost, Shocka, Lee, are a bunch of overrated beach boys. They have looks but the talent must have passed them up. I see nothing in my path of becoming the evolution champion soon. Pick up a few under card belts on my way to the top before I cash out on the current champions ass.” Hedges claims confidently. “So who do you plan on going after next?” RoC asks Hedges chugs down the rest of his beer and begins speaking “I’m thinking that fool Xavier. He claims he needs a bigger belt but I don’t think it matters because he won’t have one when I get a hold of him. His time is limited. Give me a week or two to pick up a couple wins and earn my shot at him and he’s as good as dead. I haven’t even heard from any of the top five in the few weeks I’ve been here and I smashed Lee, who's the number one contender for the belt, already last week so earning a shot will be easy as beating Shocka for the 1…2…3” They laugh as the scene fades out
