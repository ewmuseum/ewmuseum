Cold Emotion... part thirteen. END PART!

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens inside some bar in Las Vegas... Sky Night struts with a devilish, sexy smile on her face and has looks that you would kill for. Approaching a table the scene pans to find Morpheus Omega and Hedges sitting with sick, grins on their faces as they eye down the beautiful woman. Morpheus turns and catches Hedges glaring at her and snarls staring him down. Immediately Hedges changes his attention back to his drink and slams it back not wasting a drop. 'Ahhhhhhh' Hedges lets out a big sigh after quenching his thirst and nods to Morpheus and Sky Night. 'What a night!' he proclaims. 'New-Tv took Shocka to school and back tonight and I defeated him in his own trademark match. That has to kill the ego!' Morpheus says and has following laughter about the topic of discussion. 'Stepping from the shadows of darkness, he knew absolutely nothing when I shocked the world and returned from my faked death... I am truly the Queen of Darkness and took everyone for bloody surprise. Shocka actually thought I would leave the deadliest athlete in the game for a chump like himself? What an idiot!' Sky Night retorts. 'New-TV orchestrated the devised of all plans to find revenge against the number one Stunna and it all fell into place almost too smoothly' Hedges says with a grin on his face, ear to ear. 'Everyone thought New-TV and Twisted Reality died when Shocka and Sky supposedly double crossed me weeks ago. They never stopped to realize we are the dirtiest bastards in this game and they never seen through all of our lies and fabrication. Hell, even S. agreed to go along with our plan to hit better ratings. Gullible pricks, every last fucking one of them!' Morpheus says nodding with a sick look on his face. 'Truth be told...' Sky Night says and hesitates a moment before continuing 'We played everyone like the chump that buzzard Shocka really is. We are the deadliest alliance that sports entertainment has ever laid their eyes upon. Together we will rule and triumph!' 'Speaking of together, where the hell does Thug keep disappearing to? I thought he said he would meet us out for drinks tonight?' Morpheus says with a peculiar look on his face. 'He said something at the last second about needing to visit his family because he had not seen them in awhile...' Sky answers. 'And how would you know?' Morpheus asks. 'Well after your match when you distracted by your self-centered conceitedness and you scaring young children, I was giving him head in the locker room and he mentioned something about it...' Sky says with a soft chuckle following like she was trying to get rise out of Omega. 'What did you SAY???' Morpheus screams while jumping to his feet and the snapped look can be seen in his eyes, he looks like he is about to flip the table and senses the sarcastic tone in her voice. 'I really don't have the patience for your games you little whore!' Morpheus says with a bitter look on his face, he takes a seat back at the table and shakes his head side to side while staring at Sky. 'Excuse me? Did you just call me a whore?' she asks with a funny tone of voice and blows him a kiss... 'Your the only whore here!' 'Enough!' Hedges interrupts their conversation... 'Shocka is the only whore and the whore of all whores and lets leave the conversation at that... We will title him 'King Whore!'' Hedges laughs. 'That little puke doesn't deserve the title of a king, more like the joker because all he seems to make me is laugh my ass off. Like the look on his face when before I kicked it last night!' Sky grins. 'Too bad we never found that Newton fool the other night... I really wanted to see you dummy him when we got lost in ghetto hell...' Hedges says with a following shrug. The lights go dim inside of the bar, someone walks on the stage wearing a cheap tuxedo and has a improperly placed toupee sitting on his head. The man clears his throat and taps the microphone which makes a high pitched noise, all three of the present New-Tv members cover their ears immediately. The man with a dumb look on his face looks around. 'Tonight we have a special surprise for all of you' The man speaks with an excited tone of voice. 'Tonight we introduce to you the famous and gods gift to entertainment... Wayne Newton!' he screams. Drums roll in the background and the man expects reaction from the small crowd who are inside the bar. The three shake their heads back and forth and Hedges looks back to Morpheus again. 'Speak of the devil!' Hedges winks and nods with a sick look to Morpheus like his Viva Las Vegas wishes have finally come true. 'Like icing on the cake, first we embarrass the hell out of Shocka with my tainted victory at Unbreakable and now I get to beat Newton like the red headed step-child he really is...' Morpheus says with a crazed tone of voice and looks back and forth at Hedges and Sky Night. 'Shocka and him must be cousins, I can see the relation...' Sky Night says with a sexy smile and bites her bottom lip. Staring down Morpheus with a seductive look on her face. 'Too bad Shocka had the smarts to escape from our trap earlier, we could of fed him and Newton to the pigs together!' Hedges says with a snarling look like he meant every sick word of it. 'NOW INTRODUCING WAYNE NEWTON!' The man on the stage screams into the microphone with the queerest of looks on his face. The bar goes dark and not one cheer is heard from inside the bar from the deadly trio who seem not even close to impressed. A figure can be seen walking up the stage and remaining silent a minute. A light slowly fades back on and Wayne Newton can be seen on stage with a big smile on his face. Stepping foot to foot he approaches the microphone. 'Hello Las Vegas!' Wayne screams. 'How you all doing tonight?' he questions with a displeased tone of voice like he was not satisfied with the turn out for his surprise entrance. 'You best have that eight ball of crack!' Newton mutters behind himself to the man who introduced him. 'Lets start out tonight by having a member from the packed audience come up on stage and sing-a-song with me this evening. How about that lovely lady down the third row?' Newton asks. 'Thanks for the invite, but I think this guy sitting beside me is an even bigger fan of yours Wayne. Invite him up!' Sky Night says with a deceiving tone of voice and winks to him following. 'Come on up big man!' Wayne says with a very weary tone in his voice when he realizes the actual size of Morpheus; like he is cluing in who Morpheus is, but cant put a handle on it. Without thinking twice, Morpheus stands and triumphs towards the small stage that Newton stands on. Morpheus strides up the stairs only taking one step and stands towering above Newton with a strange look on his face. Wayne smiles and shakes the hand of Morpheus. 'How you doing today Mr...? what did you say your name was?' Wayne questions staring far up into the eyes of Omega. 'I didn't, actually!' Morpheus hesitates and continues... 'The name is the Infinite of evil, Satan's Assassin, The God of Sleep and Dreams... Heh eh!' Morpheus answers with an evil ring to his voice. 'You don't remember me?' Morpheus questions. A blank look falls over Wayne's face like the pieces of the puzzle have slowly come together. After moments of silence, Wayne turns his head out of fear and looks back again. Wayne trembles slightly. 'Mo.. Mo.. Mo.. Mo.. MORPHEUS???' Wayne stutters and questions. 'What kind of duet you have planned for us tonight Newton?' Morpheus questions with a disturbed look on his face, standing impatiently. 'I.. I.. I was thinking... Um Um... Kindly Forgotten or maybe even for.. forgive me! How's either of those su.. sound?' Wayne questions. 'I was thinking maybe Sanitarium by Metallica?' Morpheus questions and nods to Hedges and Sky Night who whistle from below. 'I.. I don't.. Knu.. Know that One!' Newton replies. 'Then we will have to settle for Code Omega through the table? Hah ah ah!' Morpheus says with the sickest of tones in his voice. 'Do.. Don't know that Wu.. one either?' he scratches his chin with a confused look on his face and looks around blankly again. Not even seconds later, Morpheus turns Wayne around and grapples with a turn at the same time. Morpheus can be heard laughing and dives off the stage and puts Newton directly through the closest table with his trademark move the Code Omega! The table breaks in half with the wood splitting in jagged, broken design with large splinters sticking up everywhere. Morpheus stands back up and spits in the face of Wayne Newton who looks to be unconscience. 'Definitely some kind of relation to Shocka... A dumb, stupid, gullible son of a bitch who got what was coming to him... Heh eh, Lets get out of here before I get thrown back in jail...' Morpheus says with a grin. 'Good idea...' Sky Night replies and continues 'Even through you were never in jail. You tricky fool' Sky stands up and bends over to grab her coat and lifts one leg off the ground showing the perfect ass shot and she stands back up and turns on one heel. 'You guys ready to leave?' she questions. 'Whatever you say boss!' Morpheus says with aroused look on his face and turns to notice Hedges already half way to the door. 'HEYYYYYY!' The man who introduced Newton screams loudly. 'Thanks a lot because without performing I Don't have to pay him his crack!' 'Think of it as payment for all of our drinks tonight, talk to you later Chez!' Morpheus replies picking up his black leather coat and walking from the bar with his arm around Sky Night. The scene fades.
