Expressing My Feelings

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-23 Hedges…seems like the guy won’t learn. He doesn’t realize who exactly I am. I’ve defeated all the top contenders around this place, and he dares to question my authority. Yes, it’s true, New-TV might just be on the verge of defeat. With Omega suspended, and Sky on the injured list, it’s up to just me and Hedges to hold the fort down, and it looks like he can’t even do that. There’s a chain of command in New-TV, and he needs to realize where he is on that chain. I can promise him this, he’s not on top of the chain. Where’s Omega when you need him? He’s always around when you don’t need him, but the second a bitch gets out of line like Hedges, he’s nowhere to be found. New-TV will continue to go on strong, but soon, it will go on without Hedges. Cause the next time he steps out of line, he’ll find himself on the outside of New-TV. I created New-TV, and I won’t let it die because of a bitchass like Hedges. That’s for certain. I can’t help but to laugh at all the obstacles that are always placed in front of me. Then I laugh harder as I overcome each and ever obstacle. When I arrived to eVo, they said nobody knew me here. They said that I wasn’t going to be able to make a name for myself, well they were obviously wrong. Then I returned, and Scott Frost decided to step in front of me, to spoil my return. Yea, me and Omega took care of him easily. And now we got Jason Jameson. So he lost the handicap match, therefore I don’t have to grant him a rematch. Well that was until I had to take on two bums last week, so the rematch score is even. But the match can’t happen unless I sign the contract for the match as well, and I won’t do that. It’s not because I’m scared of Jay or anything. Just look at our last two times we’ve been in the ring, I’ve pinned him twice! I took him out in that tag match and at From the Ashes. So why would I be afraid of him? If anything, he should be scared of me. I’m the one who embarrasses him each time we step into the ring. I’m the one who keeps pulling out the wins and giving him the losses. Do you get angry Jameson? Does it upset you that you’re a former Universal Champion, yet you can’t defeat me? Do I get under your skin Jay? I know if I were you, I’d be pretty pissed off. I mean, you at one time where at the top of the eVo world. You were the King, the ruler of the company. But now you’re not even a Prince. Here I am, coming out of retirement for one last time, and putting your ass on your back multiple times. Why should I have to prove myself to you again? Jameson, in case you haven’t realized, you get NO REMATCH. I’m Thug roC, so Better Luck Next Time!
