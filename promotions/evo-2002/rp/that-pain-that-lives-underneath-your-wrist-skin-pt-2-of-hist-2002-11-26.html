That pain that lives underneath your wrist/skin.... pt. 2 of ??? (HISTORY REVEALED)

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov, 26, 2002. Physical Pain... Oh how I love it... it is my other medicin, it makes me feel better and is cures me right up, one slice of the wrist, a cold beer and feeling nothing but better. Back to that god damn pain, as I sit here, all alone, I feel like I'm in my own planet, I feel like I'm in my own habitat and no one around me cares about me, as a child I was never loved, I was hated as a matter of fact. My father never loved me, he beat me untill I couldn't stand for god's sake. Nothing was more hurtful than when my father litterally beat me. I felt pathetic, a little kid, I can remember the first time ever.... I was nearly 10, and I was in a football game, I was the reciver and I got a pass, no one expected me to catch it, at this time my father had love and devotion for me, but here he knew I could do it and he wanted more than anything for me to catch this ball. I was wide open, it slipped into my hands and back out, there was only a few seconds left in the game, championship game, I was one of the worst players in the league, I was wide open, and with this catch the Trotters would have won the league championship since the last time 30 years prior. I had the ball in my hands and glanced over to my father, he sat, cheering for me, but I dropped it. I never felt worse in my life, this is when that pain kicked in, the thing that literally ruined my life. My father got up and left right at that moment, I thought he went to the car, I didn't think he would leave but, for some damn reason he did. I walked home for 3 hours because this was a far way out, oh I felt terrible, carrying all my equipment I decided to go to an overpass, and just jump off. I tried but some guy pulled me down and brought me home, I guess it was my guardian angel and he knew I was in trouble... After that damn moment, I have never felt the same, this pain lives in between two of my veins and between me and my father, however he still lives, I moved out at only 14. I lived on the streets untill I could afford my own place, I bought an apartment at 16 after dropping out of school and working at a nearby farm, picking fruits by hand. This family was very happy and I ended up staying there a lot of the time. I got enough money there and went out looking for a real job, I left them and thanked them dearly, I do not know where they are today but they're somewhere, I know it. I went out and discoverd wrestling, first I bought a $5 ticket to an indy-promotion, which was quite a bit of my budget, almost an hours work for just a ticket, and I went in, I got in a fight with another local fan when the promoter noticed me, he saw my anger and my hurt, and he brought me to the back, that night I debuted as 'Flesco', my last name. I remember this night, the best $17.30 dollars I ever made, I went out and bought myself a pizza, after working here a long time I decided to leave and find better work, but I didn't, back on the streets for me. After looking for another job, I left my hometown of Georgia and found myself down in Miami, I have no idea why or how but I was there. I saw a promotion, MEW (Miami Elite Wrestling) and I decided to check it out. Larrson Crew worked there, he liked me and he signed me, I liked it so I stayed, but I wasn't making good money. I lived in the shows upstairs so I wouldn't have to walk to work, while I wasn't doing this I earned a job as a Miami Pressing Co. This earned me enough money, I wasn't living like a king, but fuck what do you expect a kid with only a grade 9 education to do? I didn't care, I don't want to go back to school, I was too bad, my teachers were litterally scared of me... So I'm here today taking over all your minds and making you all scream. I'm here describing the pain that flows through my body, fuck how do you all even know I got this, could it be a gimmick? Some ask, but no, this is real, real as real can get, I'm here because I need to, I need to be here to live, I need to live here to make some damn money, I ain't here to be like this 'Morpheus Omega' and be some rich asshole and just here cause I need gold, fuck that you don't know; My Pain... My Suffer... My Hate... My Rejection... My Drugs... My Sorrow... My Life...
