'Shocka'ing the Nation

29 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-29 'New Years. January first, two thousand and four. The dawning of the rise and fall of years.' 'Will this prove anything less or worse for Mr. Charisma. Mr. Charismatic. Mr. Thursday Night?' 'No.' 'I start the year off with a bang. I start the year off, defeating yet another former Universal champion. Shocka.' 'The so called number one 'Stunna'.' 'The fact of the matter is, nobody cares about him anymore.' 'He was washed up, when he was used as a pawn in Scott Frost's game to get close to Xander under the alias of Greatness.' '....Oh yes.' 'I know all about you, Shocka, you dildo weilding son of a bitch. I know about your past, as being the second ever Continental Champion. I know your past of winning the first ever Road To Glory Tournament. I know everything about you. Because before I made it to eVolution. I studied it. How do you think I was able to get the one up over Scott Frost, AND Jason Jameson in the same match? How do you think I was able to defeat everyone in the ring.' 'Yes, everyone!' 'That little fluke, where you helped out those two crazy son of a bitches, The Heartbreakers. That doesn't count. Why?' 'Because I had them feeding from the palm of my hand. Anxiously awaiting what I like to call, 'Pure Charisma', and you stole that from me.' 'You are on my shit list my dear foe.' 'You will be the next victim. And I will have you laying in the same exact hospital bed that I left Scott Frost in.' 'You call yourself, 'The Most Dangerous Man In The Squared Circle'?' 'Huh?' 'I think not. Danger is what Soveriegn brought to eVolution. Danger is what I bring to eVolution. Danger is what you will be in.' 'As a matter of fact.' 'They need to rename Legends to Danger just for January first, because that is what you will be in. Total, and utter danger.' 'Shocka, you might be gunning for me. But I've already fired my first shot at you, and from the looks of it. I knicked your shoulder. So consider this a warning shot, and you better believe that Legends will be a direct hit....' -Charisma
