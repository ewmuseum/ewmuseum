What Would Xander Do?

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/29/03 [The shot comes in on the face of a clock. Emphasising the second hand you can faintly hear the clicking it makes, as it makes it's minutely trip around the face. The clicking gets louder, until you notice it is not the clock, but a human voice making that sound] 'Tick, tock, tick, tock' Xander copies the sound. 'You hear that Nicky? That's your time running out. One more day. One more day and I'll put an end to this little game you've tried to play, and failed. You seem to think you've gotten under my skin, or thrown me off my game. Boy, you don't even know what to do first to get that started. You are more of a fly buzzing around my head. It's annoying, but after a couple of missed swings, you crush it in the palm of your hand, ending the annoyance.' 'That is what Unbreakable will be for me. Me crushing the annoyance that is Vanny Vanny the 10 cent tranny.' 'You seem to forget something though Nicky, sure, you got a shot in here and there a few months back. But, do you remember how all of that ended? Yup, we caught you, and punished you for your insolence. Now, it's time to pay up. Time to cash in your chips. Time to hurt.' 'eVolution is a dying entity. Only thing holding it together is the strength of Unlimited power. You will get a first hand taste of this, in about 28 hours. I am prepared to do what ever I have to to teach you the lesson, are you prepared for the pain, agony, and utter humiliation? I hope so, cause it doesn't matter one bit to me.' 'When getting ready, trying to figure out how to go about this match... just remember one thing, and it'll help. Just ask yourself.. What Would Xander Do? Then, you'll see the light.' [The scene fades to static snow]
