Welcome to my personal playground

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-25-03 Quebec, Canada “Upon my command…….unleash hell…..” What a fitting tribute to Rising from the ashes. Tonight, two teams, the two greatest teams in the history of wrestling will go through hell for the richest tag team prize in our industry. The loser, will be no more. Tonight, either Twisted Reality or The Syndicate will come to an end once and for all. Big Daddy Dreamer, The HeartBreak Chick, Morpheus Omega, SkyNight. Four superstars, one destiny. One fate. Who shall rise from the ashes…… The Syndicate team seem pleased at the voice over for the PPV Commercial. Here we are in Canada. The Syndicate homeland in which they have never lost in Canada, nor do they plan on starting tonight. A Hell in a Cell exploding ring match to settle the issue, but will it. Will it truly and finally end the rivalry between both teams? Truth be told…..probably not. After tonight, it will probably intensify to new unparalleled heights. Tonight, The Syndicate have revenge on their mind. Hatred in their hearts and homicide in their souls. And why not…… Twisted Reality has gone to great lengths getting under The Syndicate’s skin. Setting BDD on fire. Hitting HBC with the title belt. Suckering HBC in the lingerie match. However, in the cage, in the cage. Pain will increase. A match BDD is 82-4 as a singles competitor. A match HBC is 8-0 as a singles competitor, a match as a team we are undefeated at 29-0. Welcome to our personal hell Reality. A reality, of twisted proportions. Because you see, tonight, its about to change, the mood is about to change stealing a line from tazz. Tonight Reality comes to end. A crowning of a new dawn under The Syndicate. Three times for HBC, the first for BDD. But the real hell won’t be inside the cage or the exploding C-4, no…tonight the real hell is for one person……SkyNight. As deadly, and beautiful as she is, she is no Heartbreaker, but, she will have her heart broken, her spirit crushed, and her mind altered once more. The sins of her past will come to light. Oh yes Sky, we have found out all about you and your previous life that you’ve tried to keep secret. Now, we hold the key to your past and future in which the only way out for you Sky is to submit to us. Submit to our every desire about you. And don’t worry Sky….. We know you like it rough…….
