Still Dreaming

23 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another year is nearly over; another year of torment has almost past. But this has been a good year of torment for Asis, because he has not had to suffer alone. This year Asis has been part of two wrestling federations and has discovered a love for the sport. But not for any other reason than that it gives him a place where he can hurt people and get away with it and he intends to carry on with the sport. And at Nightmare Before Christmas he gets to face several people that he would love to give pain to. The Unlimited defend their tag team titles against The Omen Brotherhood and in a one on one match Asis gets to face Scott Frost, a man that he has a bit of history with. A man that he does not hold any warm and fuzzy feelings for. A man that he’d love to hurt… (Asis wakes up on the floor again, sitting up with sweat running off his body. Nightmares were not an uncommon thing to him and he’d learnt to deal with them over the several years he’d been constantly having them, but dreams, they were something he just couldn’t handle very well. The kind of dreams that were memories that you only remember while you’re asleep. The kind of dreams that remind you of a previous life, a life so far away that you cannot touch it, a dream of a perfect life you once had. A dream of loving parents, of a perfect Christmas day. Something you will never experience again… Asis looks around him to realise that he is once again in a hotel room, a hotel room that is also occupied by Xander and HBC, his fellow stable mates. He lies back down on the floor for a while, trying to remember the details of the dream he has been having nearly every night since the beginning of the month now, but as always the only thing he can remember is a feeling, a feeling that he remembers but does not understand. Childhood happiness is the feeling Asis can remember, but it was seemingly such a long time ago and he has been through so much since those days, that he can no longer contemplate what feeling happy is like. As he has done nearly every night since being in this hotel now, Asis gets up and gets dressed, and leaves the hotel to go for a walk around Minneapolis at night, it’s the only way he feels comfortable. And it also gives him time to think, not about his dream, but about what he could do to his opponents at the pay per view…) Dreams and nightmares, often thought of as good and bad in that order. But for me it is the other way round. Nightmares are quite comforting, dreams are disturbing. I don’t understand the dream I am having at the moment, I only remember it properly when I just wake up, when I am in that limbo between being awake and still dreaming. But both The Omen Brotherhood and Scott Frost should know that Nightmare Before Christmas will be very real. The pain that they all feel will be very vivid, very earthly. The Omen Brotherhood seems to like to live in the past. They refer to the old Unlimited a lot, the “glory” days of the Unlimited. They are yet to realise that despite Unlimited’s glorious past, the best is yet to come. Unlimited is growing, the in ring Unlimited is not all that is us, the name is indeed what we are. And at the pay per view they will realise this. Luther Wisdom seems fairly confident that the Brotherhood is going to take our tag team titles away from us. That it is going to be like a stroll in the forest on a spring day. Unlimited is the bear just coming out of hibernation that will rip you to shreds. (For the first time in a while, Asis takes out a packet of cigarettes and lights one. He inhales deeply and then takes the cigarette out of his mouth and looks at. He has been walking for quite a while now and has ended up on a bridge above a river. He looks at the cigarette and then throws it into the churning water below, and a nearly full packet and a lighter soon follow it as well.) Then we have “Mr Intensity” Scott Frost. The man who is so intense that he has to have a waiter, an ordinary person tell him how he should be feeling angry. It really shows how intense you are Scott. If you have to have some ordinary person tell you that you should be pissed off and should want to take out people who threaten you and your loved one, then you are going to get obliterated come the pay per view. I’ve never liked you Scott, I don’t have a problem with most people that I destroy, rarely is it a personal thing, it’s just me taking out my anger on whoever is unfortunate enough to be around at the time. And whilst me taking you out will work to Unlimited’s advantage, I am going to get a lot of pleasure out of your pain. Hearing you scream while I torture you in that ring is going to give me goosebumps. I don’t know why I dislike you so much, but there’s something about you that just triggers something within me, makes me want to watch your blood spray out of your nose onto the canvas, makes me want to wrench your leg out of it’s socket. The last time we fought you beat me, and while I care nothing for wins and loses, that one has left a scar. A scar that will not heal until I have made you suffer. I don’t care if I lose to you at Nightmare Before Christmas, because quite frankly if I hurt you it doesn’t matter. The reason I didn’t like losing to you before was because I hate you, but now that I’ve got you one on one again, just maiming you will be enough. (The camera looks past Asis while they’re walking to notice that they’ve walked full circle and come back to the hotel. The one camera crew that had camped outside of the hotel that Unlimited was known to be staying in and had latched on to Asis as he walked outside. Asis is just about to walk back into the hotel when he turns back towards the camera.) Oh I almost forgot, I’ve got a special little Christmas message for Strictly Prohibited. You’re right I don’t fear anything. And I most certainly do not fear you. Running around attacking people in the dark does not warrant fear on my part; it doesn’t make me respect you either. It just makes me want to show you what it’s really liked to be stalked. So at Nightmare Before Christmas Strictly Prohibited, fear me. And make sure you’re looking over you’re shoulder the whole time, but don’t worry about the lights, they’ll stay on, so you can see my face as I show you the meaning of pain. Indeed this message goes out to all of eVolution, beware the one who stalks you, beware the one that hunts. For that man will take you to hell and back, because the year isn’t over yet. Merry Christmas all…
