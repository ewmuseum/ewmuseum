History Of One

4 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/04/03 Hello, my name is London Rockwell.. None of you know who I am, and I'm sure none of you care.. Why would you? I'm sure all of you have seen people come and go plenty of times and I'm just going to be another one.. I'm sure you've heard that too, what haven't you people heard? Seriously, all you wrestlers know so much about what your doing you close your mind off to anything else. People like me normally don't come to wrestling, well.. Real people like me.. People that don't use a life like I have as an excuse. At least seventy percent of the world of wrestlers, uses something like a fucked up child hood as an excuse.. As a reason to come into wrestling and release they're anger.. That's not really why I came.. I never really though of myself as a wrestler.. More or less a fighter. I have fought my entire life. Everything I have, I have fought for.. Everything I have had and do have, I have fought to earn.. Wrestling? Yeah, it's a step down but hey.. Any of the big fighting promotions don't seem like they want anything to with someone like myself. Why would they? Who am I? I'm London Rockwell, a faded icon.. How can I be an icon though.. No one knows me.. In my own mind I am an icon.. I am someone of importance.. Just because none of you know me doesn't mean I can't be something. People come into this business claiming to be sensations, legends, anything you can think of.. Can I not be a icon? A faded one at that.. One that means nothing.. That's what I am basically.. An unknown icon.. Soon enough though, that will all change.. I'll make my mark.. Not like any of you believe it as of now though... History of One.. I'm sure no one cares about it, but really.. I don't care about you, and I'm sure that the feeling is mutual.. I'm not worried about that though.. I was different than most kids I guess.. I didn't grow up to abusive parents, or on the streets.. A nice middle class home is where I resided. My family had enough money to have what we needed and a bit of what we wanted.. We never complained about anything, I had two brothers. Both younger, they were good kids.. I dunno how they are now, haven't talked to them in years.. Anyway.. As I was saying, the house wasn't bad, the tension was slim to none.. Sometimes I ask myself why I would have left, I never really figured that out.. I didn't feel right there, it was like I was lost or wasn't meant to be. Sometimes I wish I never had, but that's only when things are at their worse.. When things can't get any worse than they already have.. Other than that, anything I have done I don't regret.. People don't make mistakes, you either do or you don't. Enough side tracking.. Home was a nice place, as far as I can remember everything was good.. No problems.. Every now and than a small argument but that was put aside quickly and never brought back to mind. Yeah, a normal family right?.. That's what I thought, and for some reason I didn't like it. It didn't seem like me. It didn't seem like what I wanted.. To me, this perfect little household was all too odd, and I didn't want part of it. I wanted away from it.. I dealt with it for awhile.. Christmas was always my favorite time of the year. Parents saved money for a few months before hand and than bought us the majority of what we wanted.. It was nice, I liked it.. That was a plus to the whole family life.. I wasn't sure what I really wanted until I was Six teen.. The age where you supposedly rebel against your parents.. My brothers never did that.. I suppose they liked the life. Why wouldn't they? I never preached about why I didn't. I never gave my insight.. I figured no one cared.. Chances are I was right.. The only time I really talked about it was at school.. There was this one girl there I was kinda fond of.. Heather.. She was beautiful.. She was really the only girl ever had feelings for..When you think about it, its quite odd.. No one else really thought anything of her.. I guess they all though she was kinda odd and never paid much attention to her.. She had all of my attention.. Every last bit of it.. Her black locks, dark majestic eyes.. It just drew me in.. I first met her in seventh grade.. From than, until I stopped going to school.. We had every class together.. I didn't mind.. At first I think she was scared of me.. being one of those mid eighties rock n' roll type kids.. I guess she had a reason.. Even though she did seem quite.. What's the word I'm looking for... Outlandish?.. Herself. 'Hey..' That's all I could get out.. She just looked at me oddly, and shy'ed away.. 'I'm Lance..' She nodded.. 'Heather.' I knew her name, she didn't have to tell me.. I was kind of at a loss for words.. I never really talked to any other girl.. I had a weird taste in girls, and was kinda shy... 'So, how are you?' I was such a conversationist.. Really.. 'I'm fine..' She still looked a bit scared and at a loss for words just as much as I was... 'That's good..' I felt like a fool.. 'How about you?' Took me off guard, but I guess it was a start to some sort of conversation.. 'I'm alright I guess, just getting through another day at this hell hole.' I hated school. With a passion.. I didn't need it, they taught me nothing I would use in everyday life.. Sure, some kind of education on the history, science, whatever.. I had no future in any of those fields, so why need them? 'Yeah, I know.. I'm starting to get tired of it myself..' Whoa.. She seemed so smart.. Someone that would like schooling, learning, whatever.. I was surprised and that drew me in even further to her. I wanted to know more about her, I wanted to know anything I could.... 'Hm.. Wanna skip this class? I don't think they'll notice..' I had to ask.. I was growing balls, in such a short time... 'And do what?..' She didn't seem to pleased with the idea.. 'I dunno, just talk or something..' I was being honest, its not like I expected to go smoke pot or anything like the stoners did when they skipped.. That's probably what she thought though.. 'Sure, sounds like a plan..' I liked the answer.. And from there.. We just left, didn't even bother going near the class room.. We went back behind the stairs until it was all clear, and than we just walked around the halls talking.. We talked like we knew each other forever.. I don't know how she felt, but I was in love.. Love at first sight.. I never believed it until than.. I never even thought of it until than.. But I found her, and I liked every aspect of her.. Heather.. I wish I could remember her last name...
