Dust to Dust.

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-22 I cannot claim to be something I'm not. You won't find me turning objects gold. Nor will you ever catch me pulling a rabbit out of my ass like some of these other guys here at eVo claim to do. I'm just an ordinary John Doe. Boring. Simple. The guy inhabiting the apartment below you with a girlfriend burdening me with fatherhood. Is this why I think I'm 'deserving' of some tin-pot title? No. I'm not 'fronting'. I have no gimmick. What you see is what you get. I don't think I'm the shit. I don't think I'm 'Number 1'. I certainly don't wear sunglasses at night. So what am I? I am a guy who's been walking the streets for the past night, figuring out what the hell I'm going to do. I have a major PPV debut match coming up. I'm also going to be a father. On top of this, I am coping with a serious pain killer addiction. Well.. it's not an addiction if you admit it, right? At least that's what they say at the seminars.. I pass a television store window and catch a glipse of the news. I stop and catch a few of the latest headlines. They all involve some kind of suicide bombing or lofty political talks. Is this the 'real' world? Of course. I live in that ordinary 1950's world where everything comes in two tones: black and white. And since I live the 'normal' life, I suppose I can assume I don't have to worry about my troubles at home, paying bills, and spending money on illegal vices such as drugs and cock fights? Wrong. And just as soon as I begin to turn pessimistic about everything, I overhear an interesting tidbit on a commercial. I almost do a double take as I look into that store window, several 24' TV's looking back. It's eVolution. A promotion package hyping the 'From the Ashes' Pay Per View. Various clips of high-paced action and intense brawls complete with a Voice Over telling me to order it. It felt great. I wasn't on the promotion, nor did my name flash in lights in the main event. But that didn't matter. I could see myself smile in the glass reflection as the televisions turned black. Smiling about my own eVolution. I walked away from the store front realizing that even with all these low spots in my life plauging me, I always have a few ups to accompany it. I have a beautiful girlfriend. I have a job.. hell I have a match debut live on PPV in front of thousands of people, for a big championship no less. This was truly the sign I needed to persist. I circle the town in slow walking strides. I must have walked for hours thinking. Just thinking. I begin to make my way home, with a new lease on life. But of course, whenever you set yourself up, you are very likely to fall flat on your face. And I did. I was mere yards from my apartment complex when my cell phone goes off in my pocket. I think nothing of it and answer. On the other end is my soul mate Katy. I'm just about to tell her the great news of my divine intervention.. of how I think I may be able to change. 'Dane?' I am nervous at her tone. I know something's wrong. It was probably 10 -o'- clock. 'Yeah.. what's up baby, where are you?' I remember my eyes darting wildly around in that night sky before I had reached the door of our apartment. 'I'm at home.. and..' Her voice quivers in harmony with my jangling keys. I fumble through it and place it in the keyhole. Before I can turn it- 'I think my water just broke.' I am floored.
