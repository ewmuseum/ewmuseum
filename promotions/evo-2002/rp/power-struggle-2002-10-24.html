Power Struggle

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24 October, 2002 “Power is not given, power is taken from the weak. I have learnt a lot from my time in the Army I have the scene the world, I saw Iraq take the power away from Kuwait, it had to be taken back, which it has been. On September 11, 2001 America lost some of his power over the world, it came to a complete stand still. The point I’m trying to make is come Sunday Night at Faith, I’ll be taking the power from you Michaels, the power to keep what is yours . I don’t need to be smart, I have you running right into my plans, I know your every move, I know you inside out, seems to me that your running scared… I hope that’s the case. You can run, only so far, I’ll be waiting, told you before Michaels I get the job done, S., has given me a job a mission if you wish to gather information that your holding from him. On Sunday he get what he wants. To be honest with you, I don’t care what your holding, all I care about is fore filling and completing a job.” Jack, still suffering from the thumb to the eye, his right eye is very bloodshot and the swelling has come up, he places the ice pack on it so he can be ready in time for Sunday. But even in this state Jack, would go to the ring, to think a thumb to the eye would stop him, taking a bullet in his arm didn’t stop him in the Gulf, his pride to his mission made this man what he was, the force he is about to become. To think that Tommy Michaels has a change on Sunday is a long shot, the monster has been let out, he aint going to be stopped that easy. “MICHAELS, YOU’LL PAY FOR THE EYE! Smart move trying to stop the hunter from seeing his pray, we have 6 senses, but take away a mans sight and he is nothing, I nice try hasn’t worked, it has only made me more focused on making you hurt. Let me tell you now you, god forsaken maggot your going to hurt, and you’ll wished you’d never been born, your nothing and you’ll never be nothing!!” Jack laughs to himself, as he throws the Camera his has been sent by eVo to cut interviews with into the corner of the room. No one wants to be close to this man monster, that goes by the name of Jack O’Hara.
