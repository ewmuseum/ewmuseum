That pain that lives underneath your wrist/skin.... pt. 1 of ???

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov, 26, 2002. (((Do you ever get that damn fealing? Yeah.... I bet you do, its that god damn pain, it lives in you... It won't go away, it just keeps with you and puts you down every god damn time to try to put it all behind you... its that damn pain that lives inside your skin and the only thing that makes it better is pain, yeah? You know what I'm talking about? Nah, you don't. This pain usually exists in you're wrist, and when it comes back the only thing that makes you feal better is a razor... This fealing is empty-ness, it comes around at the worst time, when you're busy as hell and you just want to drop everything and just be like 'fuck it man'. Well, this pain isn't imaginable, it just sticks with you untill you've had too much and you burst it's bubble, however it's not that easy, you can just get so angry, you gotta get out the razor and make yourself bleed. None of you know this, it's emotional pain, a strong weakness, maybe nothing can overcome it so you have to turn it into physical pain to make everything stop. Everything goes so fast, nothing slows down, ever. It keeps spinning through you're head and when you have had too much it just stops revolving, and ends up coming back later. Nothing can solve this problem but suicide, my vurtue, and my future. Nothing feals better than when this itch doesn't stop than pulling out good old Razor Blade and making your own damn wrist bleed. One day, hopefully all of you will witness this, but I doubt it, one day when shit's going through youre head its like a bullet to the back, you don't expect it and it burns inside, you just want to leave it all and die. Sometimes when this happens you'll scream you're damn lungs out and nobody is there to help you, this fealing is undescribable, it's like a damn headache that won't ever seem to stop right? Then after a bit, it just.... desinigrates, and goes away? Well this pain doesn't go away it's that god damn headache for days on end, and when you need it to stop, it tells you to go fuck yourself and keeps beating like a bass drum. Screaming, doesn't help, screaming gets anger out, and this isn't anger, this is something that only happens when you feal like the only person on the god forsaken planet, youre all alone and you've got to make it, but you can't, you can't find the words to say or the things to do, you can't do shit, all you go is lock yourself indside and pray to god for it to stop, but it doesn't it beats again, it keeps you locked in a consealed place and you can't do anything, because it doesn't exist, it's just an itch you can't scratch, it bothers you untill you've had enough and after some shit happens to you it pops and you feal free for you're remaining days.... Tick.... Tick.... Tick.... Goes the clock but when you're times up, boom it comes back and feals worse than ever, this is an undescribable fealing, you just want to get away and do nothing. You feel like you're no one, nobody talks to you you're just looked upon and wondered at. When you're going to burst and you sit down to think, it hurts more, don't even think about this because nothing in life exists that is more painful than this, it makes you want to throw everything away and just want to die. I want to die, every day this goes through my head, nobody cares or wants to hear about it, damn this is terrible, I'm a ticking time bomb, so I have an escape, wrestling. I build this up and release it infront of millions, people look upon myself as one sick indiviual, they see this man snap in the ring and I'm a whole 'nother person everyone looks scared and want not, but nah, I'm just pissed, and whoever steps in my path, look out... Wrestling is my excape, it flows adrenaline through my body, probably the only escape for this pain. As it flows through nothing can stop me, absalutly nothing......))) (((The scene cuts in, it's a broken down apartment, absalutly nothing valueable must be in this house. We camera walks through numerous ammounts of beer bottles and wantnot. The camera walks through a messy ass living room and can hear deep breathing coming from the bathroom.))) (((The camera walks through the mess to the bathroom, to see The True Blood himself going through this 'pain'. Ryan is extremely pissed off, he has cuts in his wrist and is shirtless. He has a pair of dark baggy jeans on, the camera walks behind him and notices he's laying on the ground, breathing deeply.))) Ryan: WHAT THE FUCK!!!!! FUCK EVERYTHING, I HATE YOU ALLL!!!!!!!!!!! (((As he says this he stands up and slams his head into the huge mirror, of course it breaks on his impact of his skull, blood drips out from his head and poors all over his torso and his bathroom floor as the scene fades out....)))
