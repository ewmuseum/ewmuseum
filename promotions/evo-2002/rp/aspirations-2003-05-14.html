Aspirations

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-14 Ignorance is something those that have confidence seem to be a victim of. Sean Mason is an example of this. He talks about how foolish I was for not believing that he is full of hope, full of the will to succeed, and maybe I was. Maybe I underestimated his heart, his strength of will. Perhaps it was the case that I was a bit foolhardy to think that I could crush his soul by crushing his body. I’m willing to give it a second go. Fear Sean? You have no idea what that word means. See, to me fear is having to hide away behind locked doors because of the way people treat you merely because of your appearance. Fear is living in abandoned buildings, having to fight and steal just to feed your aching stomach. Fear, is your Mother leaving you with an alcoholic drunk of a Father at fourteen years old, to find out seven years later that she was killed by the very man she left your family for. When the lights went out in the ring this week Sean there was not an expression of fear on my face, that I have never felt within wrestling. No, when the lights went out and I heard the music, I felt satisfaction. Satisfaction at the fact that I knew it was you, I knew you’d come back for vengeance. I have often said about people, that you have to push them a certain distance before they reach their peak of potential. The point at which they truly become human, when they are willing to sacrifice all else to do nothing but hurt and maim the person that they despise. I think you’re getting there Sean, that attack with the crutch was a step in the right direction. But I’ll see what you’re capable of yet. I’m glad you are willing to admit that I took you to a physical place you didn’t think existed; a kind of pain that was unimaginable. But I admit, I felt the pain too during our match at End of Days. But why wouldn’t I? After all, despite what some people say, I am still human. I bleed like we all do. But just so you know, you are nowhere near to ending anything. If at all, you’ve just started it. However, this isn’t all about you Sean. This week I’ve got much bigger prey to hunt. A man that it’s no secret is possibly the guy I hate most in all of eVo. And it’s not because he’s a bigger champion than I am, it’s not because he has a fiancé, money, fame, admiration from the fans, none of that. I just hate him. It started off as he was just a target for me to take out for Unlimited’s purposes, but after that I began to despise him more. This week I get to prove something; to Somers, to Xander, to the fans, to Frost and most of all to myself. That I am the next dominant force in eVo. I’m the guy that’s rising, I’m the one that you should look over your shoulder for. It used to be about just the pain, now I’ve got a taste for gold and I want more. This Continental Title gives me a driving force to hurt people, it gets people interested in fighting me and gives me easy targets. Imagine what would happen if one day I captured the Universal Championship? I would have my pick of the herd. I would have limitless opportunities to hurt anyone I wanted, as they would all come to me. This week could be the first step in that direction. I’m coming for you Scott and even I don’t know what I’m going to do…
