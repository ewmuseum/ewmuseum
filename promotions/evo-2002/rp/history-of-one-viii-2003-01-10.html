History of One :: VIII

10 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/10/03 Philidelphia. That's where I ended up.. Why there? I dunno, just seemed to stop there and decided it was the place I was going to stay. I figured since its a pretty popular place, there had to be somewhere I could stay and live in for however long I would need. I wasn't worried what people would think, but what did bother me was what I was going to do. I was sixteen and had nothing but the clothes on my back. Philidelphia didn't seem much of a place I would be intrested in, but it would do. It had its parts I could relate to, but at the same time there was just something about it that would be worth living there for. I dunno what it was, maybe all the people? Maybe the ghetto? I dunno.. I'm not saying I liked the ghetto, but I am saying it had a real feel to it. It felt like a place that wasn't made up and picture perfect. Was it what I was looking for? To an extent, as soon as I found a place to call home, everything would be so much better.. Everything would be perfect.. I would live my life the way I wanted to, and the way I felt it was meant to be lived.. Finding residence, for someone my age at the time, wasn't exactly the easiest thing in the world. It was actually near impossible. Homeless shelters, foster home, or a orphanage.. Those were my choices, other than that I had no other escape, and the last thing I was going to do was live out on the street.. Shelter's had food, beds, and a warm place to stay.. That was my best bet, maybe not the most luxiourus but that was really the last thing I was worried about. So, naturally, I walked around until I found the nearest home.. The place wasn't all that filled really, it was a bit damp out, I figured the place would be filled with homeless trying to get another bowl of soup.. To my surprise though, only about fifteen people were milling around the joint... The people working the soup kitchen seemed to take a liking to me.. Or maybe it was that they felt bad because I was still a child to them. In my own eyes, for getting this far.. I was much more than just a child. The Shelter was right next to the YMCA, I was by far a Christian, but they had things I could do.. You didn't have to be a member, so I thought it would be an easy way to spend my days.. I decided to go there the following morning.. After a long awaited night in the cot, it wasn't much but it felt like heaven compared to what I was sleeping in. A cot, with a blanket was like the Ritz compared to mud and dirt. Even the make shift showers were nice. Yet they were not much compared to the truck stop shower, but it was STILL a shower.. Something I haven't had much of lately. I fell asleep as soon as I hit the pillow of the cot.. I ignored the old homeless guy next to me, and just fell asleep.. Babies don't sleep this well..
