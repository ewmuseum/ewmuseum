Feeling about his lost on Legends XI

21 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/21/02 The scene opens up in a hotel room, we see Jay Stylez Junior sitting down on a cold metal seat wrapping up his waist with bandages while he looks out the window. Jay Stylez Junior is wearing blue jeans with black boots no shirt and the usual mask, he has no smile on his face...This is the 1st time we see him serious. Jay Stylez Junio is still hurting from the powerbomb at the hands of Sgt. Jack O’Hara, that almost ended his wrestling carrer. Jay Stylez Junior strugglez to get up from the metal chair. He finally makes it to his feet. Jay Stylez Junior leans against the half craked window and begins to speak to the camera about his lost at Legends XI and his further plans in eVo Wrestling. Jay Stylez Junior: 'Legends XI...What a night that was. A night where not only I made my debut but 3 other new comers too. I have to say congrats to Sgt. Jack O’Hara with his victory. But now I have him 1 on 1.' Jay Stylez Junior threws himself on the bad barely making it and begins to speak in a painfull tone of voice. Jay Stylez Junior: 'As...you can tell Im..not 100% to face Sgt. Jack O’Hara again. BUT I am going to block all the pain that I have and go to Legends XII and give it all I have. I do not care if I have to put my body on the line just to pick up the victory as long as I win Sgt. Jack O’Hara fair and square in the middle of that very ring that he powerbombed me in.' Jay Stylez Junior tries to seat up and cant, he grabs on to the sheet's and finally pulls himself up to a sitting position on the bed. You can still tell he is in pain cause of the look on his face. He begins to talk one last time. Jay Stylez Junior: 'You see Sgt. Jack O’Hara you used your size and strentgh to defeat me. But some this Legends I will use three things to beat you and that will be speed, Smartz and more speed. So catch me if you can or die trying, cause I now I will.' Jay Stylez Junior lays back down on his bed and turns on the VCR which has the tape of Legend XI in it. He press play on the VCR remote control and begins to study the tape and examine the mistakes that he made. The camera fades to black and goes to a commercial break. -Scene ends
