Yucky Yakuza

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 10th, 2003 Again the two sat and discussed. Plans must be made and must be formed to deal with the treachery that was certain to come. However, people that were unexpected to say the least had chosen to resurface and show themselves at a most inopportune time and cause a little bit of difficulty it seems. It was something that the duo would have to contend with this week on Legends whether they had liked it or not. Now they were in a hotel room not far from the Compaq Center where Legends would take place on Wednesday night and they sat and planned what their next move would be. “The Yakuza made quite a mistake if they think that they can come for me so easily,” Yoshii began. “And why is that?” Strictly responded as you could hear a muffled laugh from under his mask. “It’s not their fault you had bad business dealings with them.” “It is a safe thing to say that I am hardly the same Yoshii Tanaka that they no doubt remember,” Yoshii answered cryptically as he glared across the room at his partner standing near a table. “They don’t know that though Yosh,” Strictly answered. “As far as they are concerned you’re the same as always and they are coming for you.” “A lot of things have changed and I will not be cowed into subservience by them and the Crimson Sun,” Yoshii replied angrily. “I’ve never even heard of the Crimson Sun before last week.” “They have certainly heard of you,” Strictly responded again. “It doesn’t matter though. We’ll take care of them this week and after that it’s The Unlimited’s turn.” “Only if you watch your back,” Yoshii warned. “They are after you now.” “That’s funny actually,” Strictly said. “You got the Yakuza after you because they think you’re someone you’re not and The Unlimited are after me for the same reason.” “Yeah apparently the idiocy is spreading worldwide now,” Yoshii agreed. “There’s nothing like it.” “Maybe on Wednesday night we will reveal the truth?” Strictly asked looking over at Yoshii and tugging on the bottom of his mask. “No not yet,” Yoshii quickly answered. “You can soon though. As a matter of fact maybe even at the next Legends?” “You mean the one in San Francisco?” Strictly asked. :”Yeah that’s the one,” Yoshii answered. “Should be a good night to come out for you and will even give S. and Christoph something to think about.” “Good idea,” Strictly agreed. “Let’s do it.” “It will be done and so soon will they,” Yoshii said with a grin.
