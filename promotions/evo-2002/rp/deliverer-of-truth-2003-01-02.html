Deliverer of Truth

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan. 2, 2003 'I have what he wants? He wants to try and take my love from me?' The voice of Frost can be heard speaking in a very solitude state of mind. The scene fades in and Frost is staring into the only face he has grown to get used to since his departure from the Notorious. Granted he has been with his girlfriend, the one he calls his Queen. But fatefully, he looks into the rectangular shard of reflective glass and he stares. He seems to be waiting for an answer from the reflected version of himself. 'He can try. But it will never happen. Katherine is mine, and she loves me too.' Frost says to himself. 'Hmph.' Frost smiles as he looks back into the mirror. Frost looks behind as a sound of movement in the distance is heard. 'He doesn't need to worry about Katherine. He needs to worry about the Universal championship. He thinks that my life is wrapped up in my relationship with Katherine, but he is wrong. Has doesn't know the half of it. Katherine and myself we both share the same understanding that are careers are our life, our hopes and our dreams. We both come second to each other, and eVolution comes first. The people come first, and our success comes first. We faithfully support one another, and after it's all said and done. We spend the rest of our given time to one another. Devotion isn't something that Shocka knows or understands. Something that he doesn't comprehend. Thug roC, obviously was an annoying little prick that bossed people around and ran his life like he was the top of everything. But roC was the best thing that happened to Shocka. Shocka doesn't even realize it. He doesn't realize that he lost that bit of devotion he had, when he lost the attachment he called a manager in Thug roC.' Frost pauses for a moment. Frost looks behind him once again as another sound of movement is heard in the distance. Frost looks at the door behind him, and a very muffled sounding voice can be heard. Frost looks back into the mirror, and he breathes heavily followed by a smile. 'roC was his devotion. Shocka, devoted his time to make the president happy. Shocka devoted his time to become the ultimate wrestler. Now that he is the Universal champion, his devotion will deminish; slowly rotting away from his body and mind. His heart for the game is gone already, his mentally challagned life is quite annoying to the fans ears and eyes. Shocka is the man, that doesn't even have a mission in life. Deciding that you are going to make everyone's life a living hell because you have no life of your own, isn't a mission. Shocka has no mission. The fans hate him, the staff loaths him. The network, wants him gone. Nobody likes Shocka anymore. He doesn't even have the dignity to divorce his illegitimate wife.' Frost again pauses. With a heavy breath, Frost looks down. 'But he has achieved something, that I have only came close to. The Universal championship, is rightfully his. He defeated my friend, L2J. He is the true champion. But I get my chance to end that. I get the chance of a life time, for the second time in my life. The fans want it, L2J wants it, Katherine wants it, I want it, and hell now even the man who I grew to hate wants it; Thug roC. Come Legends, mistakes won't be made. Whether you want to believe that or not. Mistakes are something that can't be made now. Mistakes won't happen. Shocka, you've pinned me for the one, two, three at Rise Of The Legends. But you must admit, there was only a hairline of difference between the winner and you almost lost that. I almost had you. I am the greatest opponent you have ever faced in the ring, and at Legends. You don't want my loath, my hate for someone who strives to take from me the only thing that I can garuntee won't leave me. I laugh at your thoughts, thinking that Katherine is yours. She admitted that she loves me, and you're just plainly upset because you don't have anyone that loves you either. Well, you bring your anger. I will bring my talent. And we will decide, who is the better man. Who came with the right cards in hand. Because Shocka, your going up against a new man. A man with a new love, a man with a career that is only blossoming to be the best. You are messing with eVolution's Mr. Intensity. And like a cold chill going down your back, I will....STUN! The 'number one Stunna' Frost concludes as more movement is heard. Frost looks back and the door to the room he is in, slowly opens and Seara's head pops out and she is wearing a very short shirt and some low cut cloth shorts, Frost smiles. 'Are you coming to bed yet? I can't sleep without you, you know.' Seara says playfully. Frost smiles and leaves the room, the scene fades to black as the door is carefully closed.
