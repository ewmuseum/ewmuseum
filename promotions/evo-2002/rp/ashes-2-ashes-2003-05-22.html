Ashes 2 ashes

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-22 ~The time had come many times before the feeling you get right before a big match, that feeling like your stomach is turning over. A feeling that if you are a wrestler you have to come to peace with, come to love it. IF you cannot love that feeling then there is no way you can compete in any sort of big match situation. Ronan Pierce may be the elder in this match and he may be more experienced but Shocka has the big match experience needed to put on a show for fans of eVolution wrestling. Ronan has brought hell’s fury down upon himself when he began to toy with the mind of Shocka. And now Shocka is more ready than ever to compete in this match and show Ronan and everyone else that he is still a main event player. Just because he faded from the picture doesn’t mean he doesn’t still dream about once again holding the title.~ Every day that passed left him more and more angry with himself for things he didn’t do…and better yet the things he didn’t do right. Shocka was not a forgiving man inside of the ring anyone you asked that had been in the ring with him would tell you that. ‘I don’t understand…’ Shocka said into a phone as he frowned. ‘And there is nothing that they can do for her?’ Shocka asked the person on the other line. ‘Yeah call me as soon as you get an update. Alright buh bye…’ Shocka said as he hung up the phone. His chest was draped with a button up flame shirt unbuttoned his white wife beater was stained with a couple spots of blood around the side. Perhaps from matches prior or an event occurring in the home who knows. ‘Who was on the phone?’ A voice calls from the bathroom. ‘Oh it was just one of my old high school friends…’ Shocka said. ‘What did they say baby?’ The same voice called this time as she walked out of the bathroom with a robe and a towel on her head. ‘I dunno he was saying something about how is mother is going to die I wasn’t really listening.’ Shocka said with a smile. ‘Ahh you’re a brat!’ The lady said slapping him on the chest and pushed him to his bed. ‘It’s like he wanted me to buy him something probably or money I dunno its always something from people.’ Shocka said. ‘Well we can get your mind off of that real quick.’ The lady said with a smile. ‘Nope I already told you that I’m not going to do that until after the PPV and that’s probably only if I win…or we could have some angry sex if I loose but don’t count on that eh baby?’ Shocka asked. Lauren had come to Shocka somewhere around March 1st he had tried to keep the relationship a secret from the boys in eVo and the media but he knew it would soon come up. Shocka had recently ‘popped the question’ so to say and she accepted the two seemed happy as ever. ‘You can’t do that to me it’s just not fair!’ She screamed playfully. ‘You just wait its like I’m being starved and then when I come back I’m show you the best time of your life.’ Shocka said with a smirk. ‘You promise me that you are going to be careful and not hurt yourself right?’ She asked. Shocka’s flight left in an hour and a half he only got to spend 1 night at home this time as he had promotional work to take care of in Montreal this week. He was getting ready to leave as the security lines at the airport would be unbearable as it is but if he was late he’d probably miss the plane.~ ‘Listen I love you so much and after the PPV I am going to fly home as fast as I can to be with you no matter if I’m hurt or not!’ Shocka yelled. ‘Don’t get hurt!’ She demanded as Shocka laughed a little. ‘Okay baby…remember give Ronan a couple moves for me because no one attacks my boy.’ Lauren said with a smile. ‘I love you…see you in 4 days..’ Shocka said as the scene faded.
