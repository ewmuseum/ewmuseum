Contemplated.

19 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-19 Cameron Griffin had made it quite clear what his intentions for me were. When the microphone hit his lips I lost the last shreds of dignity I hung on to so desperately. I’d been through the motions Wednesday and the night was something I couldn’t have gotten out of my mind soon enough. The buttoned down silk shirt clung to my body as the rain fell gently. I paced steady past the slums and street dwellers, overpowered with the feeling of loneliness. Loneliness. I’d left her behind, she’d be in court and I’d be on another marketing venture in Japan with the evolution crew. Even after the last night, I didn’t feel cleansed or dignified. I was alone, lonely. “Hey, watch it why’nt ya?” The temperature fluctuated slightly and I felt the sudden need for release. Why did I care so much for the tiny amount of pay that barely matched up to the salary of a store clerk? Why did I feel a certain passion for fighting? I walked up the four marble steps which lead to the large wooden door of the court room. She stood outside a small door, her complexion undimmed. The water flowed from my hair which fell slightly below my eyes. I’m sorry sir, can I help you? She gathered her briefcase and documents and stepped through the door. Excuse me.... sir? The receptionist had been mumbling senseless nothings for some time now. “Sorry... I’m fine.” The assertive receptionist then walked by a door with the word private engraved on the top, left hand corner. Forget her. Forget Carmen. Forget me.
