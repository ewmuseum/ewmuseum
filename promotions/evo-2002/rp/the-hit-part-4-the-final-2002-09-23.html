The hit (Part 4) The Final.

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9.23.02 {The two men from before now stand in a fixed position Tony has reached for his gun and is pulling it forward Jonny is pulling his out but not quick enough a shot is fired and Jonny falls to the ground. A thud is heard and we are now looking down on the man that was shot…blood is coming from under his leather jacket and spewing down the side. He lies motionless as the scene now fades to Shocka writing a something on a computer. He speaks the last few words aloud.} ‘That’s the last time that I saw my uncle Tony alive, my group took care of him and the rest of my family against my will…’ He says trailing off with pain in his voice. ‘If they could only know what I have been through, if they knew what I have overcome to get to this position in my life…’ He stops again and then continues speaking. ‘If they realized then maybe I could get the respect that I need from them.’ {He saves whatever he is typing and stands up shirtless you can see a scar above his left nipple something that we have never noticed or taken enough time to recognize. He looks down at it and rubs the scar briefly before speaking.} ‘I mean my own uncle shot me because I couldn’t come through with the money for a hit…he wanted to kill me but he didn’t succeed.’ He shakes his head and walks into the living area of his motel room. He sits on the couch and turns on the TV searching for something to watch. {Its almost time for Shocka’s hometown team to play the Buccaneers in a Monday Night Football game for the ages. Usually he would be physced to watch something like this but something is different about him today. Something has seemingly changed.} ‘I had the chance of a lifetime to destroy Shodan on my own and I could have done it…I may have beat him on my own but know I made a deal with the devil.’ He speaks with an angry tone. ‘You know that was the best decision of my damn life. Now I get to hold this title for that much longer I get to hang onto it for that much longer. You see Shodan will never get another chance at my title he had his chance and now I must move onto bigger and better things but you see him and Jason Jameson get to feel the beating of there lives. They get to take on Too Xtreme Eric Badger and me. Too Xtreme is the man that beat me in EdW to end my winning streak. And now me and him get to take down the two men that also fought in the EdW.’
