eVo's final warning

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7/03 [Sometimes people get a little more than they bargained for. New-TV went looking for the best and they brought him back and to eVo. Now a new day has been brought forth upon eVo and he brought with him a reign of darkness…] The scene opens back up with Hedges and RoC laughing and drinking and then a quick knock is heard as the owner of the mall walks in. He looks nervous and stressed out. Beads of sweat pour from his forehead and he looks like a complete mess. RoC holds a beer into the air for him and he shakes his head no. The owner nervously begins to speak to them… “Guys you’ve been here almost 2 hours and you where supposed to start like 48 minutes ago” glancing at his watch “52 minutes ago now. The crowds getting restless out there and I was just wondering if I could get you guys to go out there. I’m afraid they are going to start rioting.” The owner manages to stumble out… Hedges and Roc glances at each other and Hedges cracks a half smile and then his face turns serious. It fills with blood and turns red as he begins to speak in an angered tone… “Just who in the hell do you think you are coming into our dressing room and hurrying us along? Tell them to wait. They’re here to see us…We aren’t here to see them. I don’t want them to sign anything of mine. Think about this next time you barge in here…I’ll snap your neck if you demand something from me again…” “I…I… was just asking..” The owner blurts out “Don’t second guess me. What a blatant show of disrespect. You have balls coming in here now get out before I rip them off…” Hedges makes a fake lunge at him and he runs out of the room. RoC and Hedges laugh and finish their beers and head out of the locker room and out into the area behind the banner. They walk out behind the chairs as the crowd sees who it is and begins to boo loudly. Hedges and RoC throw up their middle fingers and sit down. The first kid comes up wearing a Shocka shirt… “I can’t believe it’s you low lives…” The kid says in a smart-ass tone of voice “Who are you calling a low life you redneck trailer trash. You’re wearing a Shocka shirt of all things. You want some beefed up fruit go to a gay bar now get out of here before I knock the last four teeth in your mouth out” Hedges yells at him. “Ingrate rednecks…” RoC mummers to hedges The next fan comes up to them in a Thug RoC T-shirt and a bandana wrapped around his forehead. He gives RoC a high five “You know New-TV is the best shit to happen to eVo. Hedges you know I’ve followed you from before the Resurrection and Omega. Damn right I thought I was going to have to meet Xavier of some loser like Seara. I hope you guys tear team Evolution and I can’t wait to see what sick shit you pull next Hedges” “Always good to meet someone with half a brain” RoC exclaims The kid takes his picture and goes along. A fat man comes up in a Xavier shirt and Hedges waves him along. The man gives a funny look and RoC sands up and syas “get lost tubby go get some Ben and Jerry’s” “Fuck off you over paid freak. I just wanted an autograph for my kid” The man yells at them “Yeah and I want your ass to get out of the way of the sun it’s getting cold…” The man lunges over the table at Hedges who slides his chair back and RoC quickly mashes his face on the table. The man slides off onto the ground with a flurry of insults from New-TV. Security hauls the man away and they sit back down and sign a few more pictures in silence. Just then a group of people wearing team eVo and Shocka shirts comes up. Hedges and RoC look up and point to a sign they have made on the back of a folded picture. It says “NO AUTOGRAPHS FROM PEOPLE IN TEAM EVO SHIRTS” and wave them along. “Hey assholes we’ve been waiting hours to get something signed. You can’t make rules because you don’t like who we cheer for…” one of the men exclaims “Hey Hedges” Roc asks “Yeah RoC?” Hedges answers “I think we fucking can because we’re New-TV and I think we did because your ignorant enough to support team EVO” Roc yells at them “Your nothing anyways” The man says as he turns away RoC and Hedges stand up “Say that to our face punk” Hedges yells at the man The man turns around and says it again. Hedges laughs and spits in his face. The men and his friends swing at them, RoC and Hedges block and swing back. RoC pulls one up on the table and power bombs him threw it. Pictures fly everywhere as Hedges whips one threw a store window and it shatters as the man flies into a mannequin and a rack of clothes. Hedges sends another down with a big boot as RoC pounces on another and hits him with a furry of left and rights. They jump off and look at the four men that lay before them. “We’re out of here. If security can’t provide adequate protect for me to sign pictures I’m gone. It’s in the contract,” RoC yells Hedges picks up one of the guys and throws him over his shoulder as RoC and him walk out of the mall and into the parking lot. Hedges throws him into the back of his Hummer as RoC waves a good bye and Hedges hops in. Hatebreed’s I will be heard blast from the speakers as he burns out and the scene fades.
