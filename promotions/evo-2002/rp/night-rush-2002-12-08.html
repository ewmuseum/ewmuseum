Night Rush

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th December 2002 The scene opens to Seara walking down a street, a cigarette in her hand and her silver and black cell phone in her other hand, she is looking around the streets as she seemingly wanders with no real reason for being out. It is the first she has been around since Legends XVII, and she has a smile on her face. On her back is a bag seemingly full, but of what it is not certain. Dialling a number she holds the cell to her ear and waits for the ring to start and the answer to happen. Hey… Yeah…. Look, I was just calling to see if I could meet you, I really need your help with something… Ok, so you know me well. Rhys, listen to me, just keep him there, I’ll be there as soon as I can… I know I walked out on you, but you think I was going to break my back for a stupid exercise…. It wasn’t his fault. Stopping in her tracks Seara smiles and goes redder than normal, it is apparent that the person on the other end of the phone is Rhys and that he is telling the female something she finds embarrassing. Hey, you say that again and I swear I’ll… Exactly, do nothing but admit I love him. Yeah, ok Rhys, I’ll be there in about an hour, I’m on foot so it may take longer, but don’t go anywhere, and don’t let him leave. What was that? Oh right, I just need advice about something…. Thanks, but not here, not now…I’ll talk to you soon Rhys. See ya. The scene fades and opens back up in the gym where Seara is sitting talking to Rhys. The pair are in the bar area and Seara faces Rhys, looking at him with a shocked look on her face. She has been sitting there for a while, and looks as though she has got a lot on her mind. Seara – I have no idea what to say Rhys, I mean I talk to you to make sure I am not feeling things that don’t exist, but I do feel this way, yet I still have the urges. What do I do, I know Scott is unhappy, as a team and as singles we have not had success for a while now, and I know he thinks it is his fault, but he doesn’t seem to trust me and believe that it isn’t his fault, we are just on a bad run, and he still blames himself. And I want to fix it, I want to destroy people, I want to prove Notorious is more than they seem. Especially to New-TV, they think little of us… Thug roC. Pausing Seara looks to the camera and forgets for a moment that she is with her fitness instructor. And begins to talk either the camera or those watching her every move and action. The emotion being recorded for all to see and share in. Seara- You I seem to hate, you Thug roC, you seem to be close to many of the people I hate, and maybe now, maybe I need to control my hate. I have had a past in which I have harmed people, I have found it easy to hurt and end lives, but since Scott Frost came into my life and brought Notorious with him to me, I have changed. I became calm; I became what I never thought I wanted to be. I found in Scott Frost something I had not felt about anyone before, a passion, a love that I let into me, I began to trust another human being for the first time in a long time, he held a security for me, a true feeling of belonging, and then he gave me the chance to join him and L2J, Notorious, I accepted and within a short time began to feel like I had friends, for the first time ever. It didn’t take long till Jason Jameson joined us, and I admitted to having human feelings, I became normal, I became a real person, but still I have deep hating for people like you Thug roC, you and New-TV, more so at the moment than Xander and Asis. We both know they cost Notorious the match at Legends XVII, yes L2J and Jameson could have won that match, but that was a match that needed Notorious, the team, we would have been victorious, but two people stood in the way of Scott and myself, and now I have the knowledge that they will wish they never got in our way. When, where, how and small details like that do not matter, but I do know it will happen. I have fought Asis before, I have put in hospital by him, and although I respect the strength of “The Demonic”, the sheer determination in him that makes him a fierce and powerful opponent, I do not feel a fear or a worry about facing him. As I have already said, I do not know when it will happen or where, but I do know it will happen, I am sure that I will show Unlimited a thing about Notorious that they never knew. But back to you Thug roC, you dragged your old friend into our match as the fourth person, and why, because you are scared, scared we may muck up your beautiful face. Seara’s sarcastic remark has not gone unnoticed, and Rhys begins to laugh at her last remark. Rhys – You’re going to get yourself killed saying things like that. There is an air of joking in the Irish accent, and Seara smiles back at Rhys, but seems to take no notice of him, and instead turns back to the camera, but before she can really sign her death warrant, her cell begins to ring with the sound of Metallica’s 'Enter Sandman'. Removing it from her pocket, a smile emerges on her face as she reads the name Scott Frost, and pressing the centre button, she lifts the 3310 to her ear and answers with a cheerful “Hey you”. She listens to him for a moment, and then looks down at the table, before asking Rhys for a pen, and she begins to writer on her napkin, which had once been placed under her beer. Yeah, that’s great, I need to see you anyway, I have to show you something, and I have something for you... I know I didn’t have to, but I think you’ll like what I got for you; it is something that means a lot to me… No Scott, it’s nothing like that. Ok, I’ll talk to you in half an hour; you did say room 227 didn’t you? Ok, half an hour. Bye hun. Love you. Seara places the cell back in her pocket and gets to her feet to leave, she nods to Rhys who from what he heard seems to understand what Seara is up to. Getting t his feet he hugs the female, rubs her hair and watches her walk away. As she leaves the gym another ring tone can be heard, this time it is “Just So You Know” by American Head Charge, and reaching into her pocket, the female wrestler produces her cell phone and a pack of cigarettes. Lighting a cigarette she raises the cell and presses the button once again. Hey Bruce, what's up…
