The Unlimited Life

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-20-2002 ::We look in on a bar in South Florida. Half-druken people are all around whispering about the man that is sitting at the bar. The bartender gets his attention and notifies him that he seems to have everyones attention and accuses him of causing them to drink less. The man by the name of Jinx stands up and takes the bartender by his shirt and pulls him close to his face.:: Jinx: Do you really think that I dont know that? I have the undivided attention of any room that I grace with my presence. If you have a problem with me sitting at your bar paying you money for your alcohol, then that is just too bad. However when you have the beans to accuse me of ruining your business, then that is where me and you have a problem. ::Jinx slings him over the bar and brings him down across a table behind him, crashing the table to the ground and scattering the people to the other sides of the room. He jumps back to behind the bar. He yells, 'Drink up everybody! Open bar!!!' The people storm the bar ordering up everything from Sex on the Beach to Fuzzy Navels. Jinx shows off that he still has his skills of bartendering from his old Player's Club days. After everyone has their drinks, Jinx throws a towel across his shoulder and notices the EVO cameraman that is filming it all.:: Jinx: Well I might have known that anywhere that I go and anything that I do, that you would have to be close behind. What can I say though, it is just a price that you have to pay for being a legend. As everyone knows, the Unlimited has had some troubled waters lately. First we eject Christoph for him being the control freak that he thinks that he is. Then we open the door for Scott Frost, which if we are giving the devil his dues, looks to be the hottest rookie in professional wrestling today. Shortly after we become a triad of feared individuals when we also add the fourth member, the finest doll in wrestling today, HBC. We started out by dominating eVo, we became even more feared as the days quickly passed, and then we hit a snag. Frost being the spineless, gutless excuse for a wrestler, turned his back on us and our cause. Now because of this, Xander became very suspicious of me and C Wednesday night and grilled us. That is fine with me because I definately understand where he is coming from. Our announcers have announced that there is dissention amongest the ranks of Unlimited, which has no truth in it. We are now much stronger than we have ever been. The Unlimited does not wilt after pruning takes place. We only get bigger. By the way did everyone catch what took place on Legends in C's match? That is what the Unlimited is all about. We stick together and watch each other's back. Frost didnt have what it takes to be Unlimited. Speaking of that match, there was a man in that match that I have had problems with as of late. The first thing thing that he did in eVo was make a mistake. That mistake was that he came in and attacked me first. He thought that he was going to come in and make a name for himself by attacking the 'Core of Hardcore.' He then decides to start playing with my girl, C's head. SO, me and her decide we are going to unmask him after the match at Legends, but we just didnt quite get the job done. However, they have graciously decided that they would place me in a hardcore match against that man, by the name of Strictly Prohibited. By the way, did I mention that it is a HARDCORE match. Sounds like it might just be right up my alley, being the first, reigning, and last Cell Champion. Since he thought that he was going to make a name for himself by attacking me, then he should have studied me out and known that a hardcore match was definately not the type of match that he wanted to be standing face-to-face with me. So, after I tear him to pieces Wednesday night, me and C will finish what we started last week and unmask him for all the world to see the ugly mug underneath. Speaking of C, Dreamer, I will be at ringside with her at Legends, and you just better hope to God that you don't happen to be thrown out of the ring, because I might have to just take out some frustration on you. However, most importantly, Scott Frost, know that me and C are always in the wings and after Xander gets through with you me and her have some unfinished business to take care of as well. As you should know better than anyone, the Unlimited are like a pack of wolves. When you have a fight with one of us, the whole pack will be on you before too long. That is a lesson that you are soon to learn. SInce you couldn't run with the pack, then you are nothing more than prey. So for anyone that is not Unlimited, let me be the first to say: WELCOME TO YOUR DESTINY!!!!!!!! ::A man walks up to the bar and asks for a draft. Jinx just looks at the man like he has just commited a serious crime. The man asks if he is just going to stare at him all night or if he is going to pour him a beer. Jinx starts to pour the beer and instead of handing it to him he pours it on his head. He jumps the bar, throws the towel around his neck and brings his face down across his knee. He then does the Jinxed Jam on the man, leaving him lying on floor. He then simply walks out of the bar into the cool Florida night.
