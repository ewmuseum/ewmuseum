Live from the City of Lights

2 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-02 Paris, France HBC’s European Flat Early Evening The sounds of laughter are coming from The First Lady of Evolution, The HeartBreak Chick as she sits outside on her patio overlooking the Eiffel Tower. It has been an enjoyable week to say the least. HBC and BDD on one single night, dismantled the entire ULWN tag team division, defended their own titles and captured another set of gold, making them the undisputed tag team champions in any land. The Syndicate promised some surprises, it promised that they were going to play by their own rules. I hope those that watched Revelations, finally understand. Seven years in the game, and for once, they are finally free. Free to do whatever moves them, free to call there own shots. Free to dictate whatever their hearts desires. HHH had a saying once, ‘screw the rules, play the game.’ How eloquent, for that is what The Syndicate, in their long and storied career are finally able to do. SCREW THE RULES!! Revelations was just a beginning, a message as to how far The Syndicate could go. Wargames, a match they have had a lot of success in, but why should HBC mess up her pretty little face? Why should BDD have to associate with bottom-feeding pond scum. Its beneath them. The world watched in shock as they pulled a ‘Matrix’, causing the wargames cell to imploded upon itself, trapping four teams in the process. Genius, yes, smart, absolutely, calculated, you better believe it. The best in the business showed once again, don’t ever underestimate the most dangerous and successful team in history of the business. So what of with the Bodysnatchers? Truth is, who cares. They were a means to an end and they knew that. They knew they couldn’t defeat The Syndicate. They haven’t been able to defeat them in the past, present or future. They realized their own limitations, and that really did set them free. Mercenaries, thugs, whatever you want to call them, they were smart enough to know that they were going to have their asses handed to them royally. But, what about something else. Perhaps they could gain something from a no-win situation, and that is what HBC presented to them. Cold hard cash, and a guarantee that The Syndicate would look after them in Revelations. They could have trapped The Bodysnatchers with the rest of the trash, but they are loyal Evolutionist’s, and deserved better. Enjoy your money gentlemen as HBC and BDD think to themselves toasting each other, clanging the fine crystal filled with red French wine from the Soveriegnia region. ‘It’s a beautiful night, the moon is out, the Eiffel Tower looking grand as ever. All in all, a nice night in the City of Paris. Its too bad we have to get back to eVo business though’ echos out from the full pouty lips of The HeartBreaker. Looking grand as always in her tight short black dress, and showing off her ample assets. Paris has always been a love-hate relationship for her, but she has grown since her youth to appreciate the city. ‘Indeed it is. Nights like these makes me glad we’re multi-billionaires. And now, undisputed champions, once again how it rightfully should be,’ quips BDD. ‘Yes….I will say the ULWN titles are just an added bonus, something to taunt the little people with, but your right, undisputed once again with my long time partner. Your first reign, my third, it doesn’t get any better than this.’ ‘Well, actually it does. It seems eVo, and who can blame them, decided to put us in the main-event at Legends against someone you know well, and someone whom I’ve been looking to extract some revenge on for quite awhile.” ‘Oh really……do tell D, do tell…’ ‘Luther Wisdom and Jason Realms.’ ‘Really now…….how interesting. I thought Luther quit after that whole King fiasco. And Realms, yes, you have been dying to get your hands of him for months now. Especially after that’ says HBC as she gently rubs BDD shoulder blade, ‘that day were he stabbed you with an icepick.’ BDD balls his hand into a fist and slams it down on the railing but then smiles. ‘You know C, the old me probably would have rushed into something that would have disadvantageous. Now, I will destroy him in my own way, just as we have all the other teams around here. I remember that day when the Industry worked with Twisted Reality and coincidentally the Bodysnatchers. That little bitch hit the bricks before I dreamanator her, but alas, Jason Realms returns and my revenge shall begin.’ HBC just smiles as her partner as looks over to the Eiffel Tower again. ‘It appears that your boy Realms is calling himself the whole fucking show. I swear RVD did a disservice to that. Every a-hole is calling themselves the whole fucking show. I saw it even in bowling of all places the other day. Pathetic. Truly is.’ ‘Well what do you expect from a no-talent simpleton. What has Realms done, other than annoy me, which is a dangerous thing to do. He comes, he goes, does anyone really care about him. No. That is the thing that drives him nuts. No one cares about Jason Realms. Just a name on the roster, another whipping boy for our amusement. Of course, that doesn’t mean I won’t break his scrawny little neck when given the opportunity. No one stabs me and gets away with it.’ ‘Exactly. Not only is Realms going to have a problem with the undisputed champions, but even his own partner can’t stand him. I can see it now, friction between the both of them. As much as Luther wants to desperately break out of King’s shadow, Realms, hell, he’s just going to derail it for him. Luther is a cool guy, won the belt twice with him, but with Realms, he’ll never achieve what he desperately seeks….respect. I don’t really want to bad mouth Luther, but hey, all fair in love and war buddy. I’ll give you the kiss of death just like any other opponent. You know first hand how bad that does hurt don’t you Wisdom? Hey D, here’s a question for ya, I actually was bored and caught Realms little promo and wanted to get your opinion on something.’ ‘K, shoot.’ ‘Realms is a flamer isn’t he? I mean have you seen how he acts with his so-called buddy? I’m telling you, their something more than meets the eye between those two.’ ‘You mean like playing butt-darts.” ‘Exactly! My questions though is….whose the bitch, and whose the bull?’ ‘Realms is definitely the bitch!’ HBC and BDD both start cracking up! ‘Yeah, I think your right on that one D, well shall we head over to the Champ De’Elysses?’ ‘Lead the way C, lead the way.’ HBC starts heading toward the door shaking her ass as BDD just stares and watches doing his famous APA quote, ‘DANG!’
