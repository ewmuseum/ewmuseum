Faith: Aftermath

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct. 28, 2002 'Scottie?.....' the wandering voice of Kelsey Frost is heard. The scene opens up and Scott Frost is laying in a bed at the hospital, and he seems to be just waking up as the camera catches him yawning and forcing his eyes open to see and adjust to his surroundings. 'Scottie, how are you doing? Katherine called me and told me that you and her got pretty beat up at Faith. I am sorry but I was with my mom, and we were out to eat. I hope you can forgive me.' the sad voice of Kelsey is heard. Frost slowly turns his head and looks at her and smiles as he reaches to hold her hand. 'Seara? How is she? Kelsey, she is my friend. Seara can't be hurt!' Frost says gripping the hand of his wife bringing her hold a little. Kelsey cocks her eye up, and looks at Frost. Frost looks around and looks sad, but then he looks back into the eyes of his wife and he smiles. 'Thank you for coming babe, you are exactly what I needed to get this pain to go away. Have you heard from L, about his match or whatever?' states Frost. 'Yes. It ended up brutal, with a draw. Nobody is for certain who should be the champion.' Says Kelsey. Frost looks at the far wall. 'Hope he is alright.' Frost says into nothingness. Kelsey looks at Frost. 'Scottie. I have something to tell you.' Kelsey says. Frost looks at her now with a worried face. 'What is it?' Frost asks eagerly. Kelsey looks at him and blinks. 'Your lined up to face Jinx at the next legends. If you win this you continue on in the Road To Glory tournament at November.' Kelsey says. 'Jinx? hhm. Funny.' Frost says. Frost looks at his loving wife. 'It's funny how in almost two months, I have single handedly be given the chance to take out three Unlimited members. I caused the demise of Erin Christoph, the fall of Xander, and now Jinx. I know for damned sure that Jinx is nothing compared to Xander. Everyone say that match with me and Xander, we fucked each other up. Now, less than a week away I get to take out his best friend Jinx. The beauty of everything is amazing. Road To Glory.....?' Frost finishes. Frost smiles. 'Only fitting that almost two months ago I debuted in eVolution. eVolution is my first big time federation, and I am in the top five for the Universal Championship. This road that I have chosen, with my friends. It is definately the road to glory, and I ain't going to let some washed up Hardcore legend take that away from me!' Frost says getting up. Frost walks over to his bathroom door, in his hospital room as Kelsey leaves the room. Then suddenly Seara appears at the door and Frost and Seara look at each other with out saying and words and Seara looks sad and Frost looks overwhelmed then the two of them go their own ways and the scene fades.
