Notortious loses and gains; part 2

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 15, 2002 'Scottie.....' The whispering voice of Kelsey Frost is heard. The scene fades in, and Frost is sprawled out on his big leather couch loosely wrapped up in a blanket and his pillow slowly slipping to the floor. Kelsey leans over the couch and slightly nudges her soon to be divorced husband, and Frost jumps to his feet in excitement and from being bothered while in a deep sleep. Frost slowly look at Kelsey and then looks back down at the couch. Frost yawns, and then looks at Kelsey. 'Scottie. I've already got my stuff packed. I'm leaving now, there is a taxi waiting for me. I'm so sorry that it came down to this, but I want you to be happy. And I was thinking. Maybe trying something with another female wrestler instead of a female wrestling fan would be better. You have more in common that way, and will be travelling to the same shows together. If you and Katherine do get together, I won't be mad. I will always love you.' Kelsey finishes. Kelsey looks at the drowzy Scott Frost, and he looks at her like he is trying to understand her but didn't. Then Kelsey bends over, picks up her bags and walks out of the house and a door shutting to be final. Kelsey Frost promised never to be in the life of Scott Frost again after this, and when they make promises they never lie no matter how hard. Frost looks around awaiting for something, but nothing happens. For about five minutes he stares towards the ceiling but still, nothing happens. Then he sits up and he runs his hands through his hair and yawns. The phone begins to ring. 'Hello?' The yawning voice of Frost responds. Frost pauses, rubbing his eyes. 'Hold on, I'm gunna put you on speaker phone. I just woke up.' Frost says waiting a response. Frost presses a button on his phone, and then places the phone back in the phone jack. 'Alright. What do you want, Krowe?' Frost speaks. 'That is no way to talk to one half owner of eVolution Wrestlings Network. Especially, 'The Tru One' Joshua Krowe.' Krowe says. 'Okay. What the hell do you want?' Frost demands. 'I want to know what you got planned for Asis at the PPV. I wanna know if your plan equals more ratings that what I have planned?' Krowe retorts. There is a pause. 'Frost?' Krowe asks. 'What?' Frost retorts back to Krowe. 'Haven't you thought of anything?' Krowe asks. 'No.' Frost says. 'Why?' Krowe demands. There is another pause. 'Frost?' Krowe questions. 'WHAT?!' Frost screams. 'geez! What's wrong with you? Your wife divorce you or something. Don't you get an attitude with me!' Krowe demands to Frost. 'As a matter of fact she did divorce me! And you know what! If you want ratings for the PPV trust me, you will get your ratings. But only after I destroy Asis! What I do to that son of a bitch is only going to be half of what gets your ratings. There are other people on my list! There are other fuckers that want more, than what they can dish out. Malcolm King, to be exact. Jinx, to be exact! Xander, TO BE EXACT! HBC! To be exact. I have single handedly takin Unlimited apart. From Erin Christoph to Jinx. Asis is next. Malcolm King, isn't going to stop me. Xavier Citizen won't, Luther Wisdom. UNLIMITED SURE AS HELL WON'T! Only person that can stop me now, is Katherine.' Frost says. 'Katherine? Who is that?' Krowe asks. 'It's apparently the only person that cares about me anymore!' Frost screams and then hangs up the phone. Frost looks down at the phone and then rips it out of the wall and throws it across the room. The sound of glass shattering in the background is good indication that something broke. Then scene fades to black. (To Be Continued....)
