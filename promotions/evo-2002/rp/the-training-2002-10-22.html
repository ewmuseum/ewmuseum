The training

22 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-22-02 Toronto, Canada HBC’s Estate Mid-day “Its is funny how…..even now” as Shirley Manson sweet melodic voice echo’s out from the state of the art speaker system inside HBC’s spacious 10,000 square foot gym inside of her immaculate estate. HBC is busily preparing for the upcoming Evolution PPV, Faith where she will for the first time, go head to head against her former partner in what as being built, the battle of all time. Everyone’s dream match finally taking place, with the dirtsheet writers claiming this to be bigger than the Triple H-Shawn Michaels match. Indeed, it is perhaps without question, HBC’s biggest match of her career. As she looks back to the legends she defeated over the years, this match up, will make or perhaps, break her career. Always on the edge of greatness, just waiting to jump into the top-tier of singles competition within the industry. Not being just content being the top female wrestler in the world or the top cruiserweight in the world. She wants to the best of the very best, and to do that, her former partner, Big Daddy Dreamer must go down for the count, cleanly, decisively. There can be no mistake. BDD has shown himself growing more and more vicious within the recent weeks, the right hand, the powerbomb, he is very close to regaining the edge and I can not let that happen. Fortunately for me, he doesn’t know that little secret I hold over him, and when the time is right, I will use that information to crush him like a bug. No more will I be in his dam shadow. No more will he get the glory that I deserved! No more will the writers say I’m riding on his coattails. Coattails my ass! I helped carry him the past year with his nagging injuries he’s been battling. Oh no, at Faith, it will be my crowning moment in the sun, my place cemented in history and its just too dam bad it comes at his expense! A very determined and focused HBC thinks to herself as she rapidly goes through her stretching routine, working out all the muscles in her legs, arms and back. She is going to need to be at the optimum flexible level for she knows if given half a chance, BDD will stretch her out, trying to make her submit, something she has never done in her life. Knocked out sure, unconscious, yup, but never ever submitted and I’ll be dammed in BDD is the first to do it to me! HBC closes her eyes and throws a couple of shadow punches, imagining various striking points on the human body. Pressure points they are called. When hit right, it can immobilize anyone for several minutes. BDD mass is going to present a problem, right strike fast, quick, hard and often to soften him up. There, there it is, right behind the right ear, his weakness….. A good solid shot to that spot right their, given his current condition and broken eye socket will knock him unconscious. What was it my old Krav Marga instructor said, it takes 200 PSI to shatter that area. No problem. A kick, will probably dam near take his life away from him….. a punch, will feel like Mike Tyson hit him senseless. That is the spot, that and the broken orbital eye socket. Can you just imagine the pain if I nailed him with my superkick or any other move I desire on that broken socket. You may just go blind D if your not careful. Which I know you won’t be. Blinded by rage, you fall so perfectly into my trap. Ah D……at Faith, your shining career could come to an end. As you were so fond of saying in the past….. “I hold your career within the palm of my hand, and as I look at it, I smile, because I could let you live to fight another day, or I could just end it now, that my friend is real power. Despite all your ability and gifts as each finger closes, another part of you belongs to me until eventually, you are no more….. ashes to ashes, dust to dust. Good god, what a rush!” HBC blows on her hand as the camera fades out into blackness and a silhouette of a fallen Dreamer laying on the mat ever so faintly in the background.
