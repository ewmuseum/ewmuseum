Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.23.03 ‘Where in the hell is he?’ Shocka questions wildly. ‘I dunno sir some spectators said that both he and Mr. Omega were in here just an hour ago…’ The man says as he looks around the large crowd in a casino. ‘Call security it shouldn’t be hard to find those two big fuckers.’ Shocka says with a blatant display of impatience. ~He wears a flame button up shirt with a white shirt under it he has a pair of khaki’s on again and his hair today his spiked straight up. People glance at him and smile and wave as he walks through the crowd annoyed.~ ‘Where could that big son of a bitch disappear to?’ Shocka questions of himself and looks around. ‘Sir…we have word that they were spotted by the crap tables just 20 minutes ago…’ The casino employee speaks. ‘Fuck it…I am not going to hunt them down I’ll just wait until legends triple x to get my fucking hands on them and strangle the life out of both of them.’ Shocka says as he turns around and he slams into a person with a painted face. ‘Watch it sucka!’ The voice screams. ‘Hey man I didn’t think I’d see you here this soon!’ Shocka screams. ‘Yeah Jon, I mean we started this shit with Omega last week and I plan on being here this week to help you finish this shit.’ ‘Good…well I dunno where in the hell Hedges and Omega are I figured that we could just get a hold of them at Legends man…’ Shocka says trailing off. ‘Where in the hell could the go?’ The man asks. ‘Beats the shit out of me…I say we just head to the MGM Grand and do some gambling!’ Shocka screams with a bit of excitement. ‘Sounds good to me man…but hold on…’ ~The man with the painted face spins and runs and grabs someone and they rush toward Shocka and out the door as the scene fades.~
