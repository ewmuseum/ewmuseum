The Black Label speaks out.

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-29 The scene opens outside of Queens, New York as sitting inside his apartment once again is X. He has a black t-shirt on with a white letter X written on it. His pants are just some nike basketball shorts with some nike sandals on. He has some music playing in his apartment as the eVo camera crew ask X to please turn it down.He turns his head as wondering what in the world for. He goes and turns his radio off as he is handed the 'Mixtapes' that his opponent Ali made. He puts them in and listens as he begins to laugh. When the tapes are through he takes them out and goes over to a small trash can. He looks at the eVo cameras and smiles as he drops the tapes into the trash. 'Thats where they belong' he says. He walks back over to his chair as he takes a seat. He looks at the camera with a cocky smile 'Ali you are nothing but a pathetic peon. Think about it all Ali. You really are quick to shout at shit before you think and listen. Lets recap everything on the Mixtapes. Lets have a review. First Volume 1.' He looks over at the picture of him and his mom on by his bed. He shakes his head as he is recaping the first mixtape. It seems as though he is replaying the tape through his head. He looks down on the round with a smile. He looks back to the camera and says... 'Aight, first things first. Ali, you sent those tapes saying this and that before X believes you said them with out thinken. Example, You said that X has his head stuck up his ass, and that the black people where a part of corperate america. Well Ali, X never said that the black man wasnt a part of corperate america. X said that corperate america is unfair to the black man. So maybe it is you who does not understand. For example, you are the one working two jobs and eating at Mcdownalds. But listen to me, the man who owns that Mcdownalds is a part of the white corperate america. It is him who plays our people like a string puppet. You dont see that though. You are blinded like you said by your having to work two jobs to place food on the table. So you go ahead and try your hardest to wrap those hard working hands of yours around my throat, honestly Ali. X wants you to try..try your ass off because when its all said and done..you will be the one like the typical black man you described yourself to be..still working..as your will stands to be nothing compared to mine.' Pauses as he smiles in a type of cocky manner that we havent seen X use before. 'Your a smart man Ali. But a man who says things he just cant accomplish.What X means is you claim all types of things as your tape proves, you shouted all types of insults at X. But as you are the one who asked all the questions let me ask you one. Do you think it makes you a better man to call X all those things. Do you actually believe that those words will affect me. Or do you say it because it apeals to your persona. Ali do you say those things to try and hold up your 'gangsta' state of mind.' 'The young black youth of the world believe to be a thug are what have you that they must use words like that. They bellieve like yourself to tell some one that he is a 'cock sucker' is taking his man hood from him and will result in insults from him. X doesnt need to keep the street persona like you feel. Ali, one day when you grow up from being one of the youth of america, you will see it all. One day it will hit you like a brick wall, you will see its not the hood that makes you a man. Its not the words that leave your mouth that makes you a man. The one thing that seperates a man from the streets from your average joe is his knowledge.' Short pause as he puts his head down. He then looks up as he says... 'Thats Right,the knowledge of everything he has ever learned. To take all that and place it into his brain.'He gives a chuckle as he looks at the cameras and says'Well that is Volume 1.Dont think I am forgettin about Volume 2.He then takes a cigar and lights it as he begins to slowly smoke it. While he is doing so he thinks about volume 2.He looks over and grabs something to place his cigar in. He then puts it down as he looks back at the camera.. 'Volume 2, the one thing you claimed to have for X. The one that said who the fuck is X?'He looks as in disbelief.He smiles'Who the fuck is X?'..Short pause still in shock..'X believes you lost your damn mind with volume 2. But you know what X is ganna do for you?X is going to let you know who the fuck X is..as you would put it.' X looks over at the X poster that is above his bed. He smiles again'Well Ali, X would assume that you want to know kind of a biography so to speak. Birthname, Malcom Braggs. X had no dad but X's mom was around till around I was 18. Thats not important though. As X has lived in Queens, New York since X was able to breath. This shit is home for me..But X is not a wrestler. As if your little ears would have been listening and that big mouth of yours shut you would of heard X say X is not a wrestler, that X is a fighter. No though, you have to be to busy trying to be slim shady or something. ' Takes a pause..'Make all the metaphores are what have you at X. Tell X that your foot is a gun next. X could give a rats ass. You are no more then a man wanting to get by life. You have no purpose put to be my xample. To be the one chosen by corperate america for X to make his mark on. See it and see it good Ali, what you have endured before in a cell is nothing. What you have watched on tv is nothing but bull shit. Listen to X now, get your pen and paper out and take some notes here.' X grabs a pen and sheet of paper as if showing Ali. He smiles and places it back on the table. He continues..'It will be a fighter against a mcdownald eatin middle class black man. Thats right X said it. And time when a grown man is talkin bitch shut your mouth...Thats X's little imatation of Ali. The kid gangsta that corperate america has produced.' 'So you are damn right that X is still here to wage the war against corperate america. Your damn right X ganna do it by doing what he does best, fighting. Ali, X pittys you when it comes down to step in that ring. Every man that will see your worthless body left in the ring will have pitty on you. But none will show sorrow. None will show the need to care, they all have seen the actions that you seem to have the need to take. Its only your mistakes ..Its only your vision that is blurred so heavinly. It is simply something you dont understand. Like the others..Thats what it boils down to. You are just not capbable of being something greater the next man. You do not have the compacity to raise the bar to the next level. All you can achieve for yourself is being the mark of the 'average' black man.' 'X, the mark of what you will soon understand but what you will always question. X being the man that will show you what fighting is exactly about. X is the man that is showing corperate america that the black man can be more then just a helper as you were to put it. Can do more then just particapate in, that the black man can lead it. Thats what X is here for..the equal oppertunity..Learn it Ali..X is sure he will hear from you soon..until then though..remember you are not going just against a man with a dream..but the mark of X..the black label...' The camera begins to fade as X picks the cigar back up. He then goes and stands directly infront of the cameras where he blows the smoke into the lens as it then cutts to a commercial brought to you by eVo.
