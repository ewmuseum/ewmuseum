Beyond Messed up... [part 2 of 2]

30 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 30. 02. {{{The scene cuts in, Queen's st. outside of Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Sirens are heard from a mile away as a soon to be eVo superstar is rushed to the hospital via alcohol abuse. Ryan Flesco is the man in the back, right now possibly being pumped in the stomach, will Ryan make it to his debut match? You're damn right, there's no stopping this man from his dreams, sick or not, broken bones or not, nothing is stopping this man from tearing apart his opponent, Morpheus Omega.}}} {{{The ambulence reaches the Toronto Metropolitan Hospital and enters with a burst of speed. They rush this man out of the back and into the hospital, inside there's people waiting, broken bones, bleeding, but the hospital seems to be over their heads with patients, and being that time of the season, many doctors are away. Ryan is throwing up everywhere, he's coughing hard, and seems to be hard of breath. The two ambulence drivers rush him into a bed and a doctor soon comes in.}}} 'What's wrong with him?' Doctor asks. 'He's had a TON to drink, much over his legal levle' one driver responds. 'Oh my god, you're damn right, this man needs to be pumped, auximhilator pump quick.' {{{The driver rushes to get the pump, he brings it back in a hurry and the doctor places the mouth piece on Ryan's mouth, he tells Ryan to breathe, Ryan does and inhailes some type of chemical to pump out his stomach. Ryan throws up all over the ground, this is too much and almost pukes a lung out, it is everywhere in the hospital ground, many people actually throw up themselves.}}} {{{About an hour later, Ryan walks out of the hospital, he's not knowing where to go and when but he's going somewhere. Ryan is very far from his home and isn't in the mood to walk, he walks for a bit to maybe find something to lay under.}}} 'Holly shit that hurt, what the hell just happened?' Ryan says, thinking someone is actually around him. {{{Ryan rubs his eyes, he notices no body is there, and he reaches in his coat pocket, he's got a few more bottles, he cannot believe the doctor did not check his pockets for more bews. Ryan however, does not pull out any more, he's had enough for one night.}}} {{{The scent of this place is of bews, cigarette smoke, and some cheap deli perfume. It could make you puke, however Ryan is used to it, usually smelling his his house's odor. Ryan actually pulls out his own pack of cigarettes, he lights one and begins to walk some more. He reaches a Mcdonald's and walks in, it's very late at night yet still open. Right now they are serving breakfast, it's nearly 5' a.m.}}} {{{Ryan walks up to the counter... No one is in line, there's only three people working.}}} 'May I take your order?' The cashier says. 'Yeah, get me 3 hash browns.' Ryan says. 'Ok, $1.50 please.' The cashier says. {{{Ryan pulls out two $1 bills and hands them to her, collects his change and his food and walks back outside, he sits down, smoking the cigarette again, he sits down outside the store and just waits for a bit, some decent shelter, could be very worse. Ryan sits back and notices how shitty his life really is.}}} What the fuck is wrong with me, god damnit, the lord totally fucked me, I was a good kid, and he made my life a living hell. Why can't I get a decent pay check? Why can't I get a good home? Why can't I get a girl that isn't a hooker? Seriously, I'm sick of life, it's pulled me down too many roads I don't want to go down and I'm just sick of where life leads those who do not pray to the lord. The lord lost all belief in me, I don't care anymore, I'm used to this shit, and once Evolution gets me some decent wage then I'll be fine. Once again I will tell you, I have nothing to lose, absalutly nothing, I'm sleeping outside of a shitty ass McDonald's for godsake. Morpheus Omega has shit to lose, and when it comes time that will matter. I'm throwing whatever I got, my whole body and this guy won't take whatever because he's got shit to lose, watch out kid.... just.... watch out.
