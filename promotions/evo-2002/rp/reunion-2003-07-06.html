Reunion

6 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-06 It might go down as the most intense battles for the right to be a Number One contender in the history of Professional Wrestling. But after two (and some might say 4 matches, depending on how you look at it) James Kimson overcame the odds, in what would be seen as a story to inspire people for years to come and become the Number 1 contender to the eVo Universal Title. James Kimson seldom was considered anything but a mega star. 11 years of blood, sweat and sacrificed earned him the monicer of Legend. But for the 1st time in perhaps years he was not the favorite in a match. Why? Because of a broken left arm. But James fought, not only did he fight but he fought such an uphill battle that at several points it seemed impossible to overcome. He entered the Caged Rumble at A New Dawn, the 3rd man in the match, his arm taped heavily facing down two of the worlds top workers. He fought for two hours, tooth and nail, every ounce of strength he had in him to tie Jason Jameson with 8 falls a piece. Then, only 4 days later he had to enter a match to determine a winner, 2 of 3 falls. Each fall a more challenging and more dangerous stipulation. Everything hanging in the balance. Kimson’s career flashing before his eyes when he slept. Dreaming of what still could be accomplished, what he had yet to do. In eVo he shocked the world, putting both Erin Christoph and Sovereign on the shelf indefinetly. Battling Scott Frost for the title, defeating Jason Jameson himself. But now, now things are different. Victory was at hand for James Kimson at the year anniversery of eVolution. He won the hearts of the wrestling world once more. Now he had his chance, with nothing standing between him and Revelations. Nothing standing between him and the currently Champion, Xander. Redemption and revenge as it was Xander who broke Kimson’s arm in an attempt to keep him from winning the Caged Rumble in the first place. But it backfired. And now Xander has no choice but to realize his fate is set. He must face James Kimson for the Universal Title at Revelation. It’s written in stone. Written with blood. The van pulls into the drive way and comes to a stop. It’s a warm summer day just outside of Buffalo New York as the sun beats down on Sunday. The home is large, comfortable looking on the outside as the doors one by one start to open in out walks a family of renown. To be exact it’s the Kimson Clan. Victoria Kimson, wife to eVo superstar James Kimson comes out of the passenger side holding young Logan in her arms as the 3 girls pile out of the back. James himself exits the driver side as he unfassens the keys from the ignition as the children are all wearing their nice “Church” clothing. The Kids, obviously happy to be out of the uncomfortable seats in their families church instantly start to skip and jump as children do in celebration. Kimson smiles as the kids rush towards the door around the hedge as Victoria passes him by smiling. Kimson himself is dressed formal as he bends over to grab the Sunday paper from the paved drive way and looks at the front page and skims the headlines. A shout is called out by Sarah, the oldest of Kimson’s children. But it’s heard as she rushes back around the corner, excited about something. “Pappa, Uncle Mikey is here!” says the young girl with an over excited tone. “Mike?” said James, surprised by the news. “Yeah, and he brought presents!” she screamed in the joy only known to a small child. Kimson drops the paper and scoops up his child and together they head towards the house. The arrive, door is open as obviously the rabble has moved into the house. After passing through the door, into the front lobby they arrive in the Living room. Sitting on the sofa with Julie and baby Logan sitting on the lap of family friend Mike Barnes. Kimson’s history with Barnes is long, as it was Barnes who is considered to be a legend in his own right, the 46 year old retired grappler, has been credited for giving Kimson his break in the industry. The Two men have had impressive matches, high rated feuds and a deep personal friendship. James hadn’t seen Mike in about 2 years now, not by his own accord but Mike’s desire to distance himself from the wrestling world so that he could finally declare the end to his career. Mike sets the kids down and stands up. James lets down Sarah and approaches Mike and they share a hug as friends do when they are re-united after such a long time. “Mike, man it’s been to long” said James, a smile on his face. “Well, it has been. I’m sorry that I haven’t kept in touch Jim, but I was in New York last week and I thought that I should come by and see my favorite people!” said Mike. “Yeah and he bot us toys!!” screamed Julie “Goo goo” babbled Logan, clapping his hands in an uncoordinated manner. The adults share a laugh over Logan’s attempt to fit in. “It’s good to see you Mike. I’ll go get some refreshments then?” said Victoria. “Yeah that’d be great Honey, whatever we have should be good enough for this old timer” said Kimson with a croked smile. Kimson turns to the kids and motions with his head for them to exit the room “C’mon kids, go off and play and we’ll give you plenty of time to harrass Uncle Mike later on... that is if Mike will stay for Super” said James. “I couldn’t think of leaving without abusing your wife’s cooking.” said Mike. “Uncie Mikey” said Martha, the second oldest “Will yo wessel wif us?” asked the cute child. “Absolutely” said Mike. “But I don’t want you to pick your father as your partner because I’d probably cripple him” said Mike jokingly. “Ew so siwwy” said Martha as she walked a way happy. The kids gone. James sits down across from Mike who watches the kids leave with a smile “Makes me wish I had kids ya know” said Mike. “Well if you stuck with a woman for more then 6 months Mike” said James, “Hey now, don’t pick on an old friend for poor taste in women.” retorts Mike in a playful manner. “Actually I met a nice lady in Europe last month, she’s 21.” said Mike “Jesus Mike, she’s half your age” said James. “Yeah I know, that’s why its fun” said Mike with a wink. “Listen James, I wanted to come here today because I just wanted to say sorry for taking so long to get back to you about those emails. I’m sorry to well. Well for missing your fathers funeral. I wanted to be there but I had a commitment that I couldn’t get out of” said Mike. “Don’t mention it Mike. We’re all busy people. Sometimes we can’t make enough time for ourselves let alone the people we care about.” said James. “But why did you come here today Mike. I can tell it’s not some random drop off, bring gifts for the kids sort of thing.” said Kimson. Just then Victoria reappears with a couple of beers and puts them down on the table and smiles and exits again knowing she probably should let the men have their conversation in privacy. “Well I couldn’t ever slip anything past you Jimbo.” said Barnes. “Well to be honest I came back because I’ve been watching Legends. I’ve been sort of tasting those cometitive juices again.” said Mike. “Aw for Christ’s sake, your not thinking of workin’ again Mike are you” said James. “Oh Jesus christ no. I’ve been watching for the sake of watching good TV. You know what the industry is like now adays Jim. 90% of it is bullshit. It’s crappy workrate, half ass promos and weak writting. But not in eVolution. You guys have got it. You’ve got what the old days had. You’re work with Sovereign was some of the best shit I’ve seen on TV in years. All the guys that work in your end of the woods can actually wrestle. To many kids go out there now expecting no more then 5 years in the business. They throw themselves off the top of cages, burn themselves with flaming barbed wire, all sorts of crazy crap. I mean, working hurt, I know that Jim. Hell I worked hurt for the majority of my career. But because I could work I managed to last for 25 years. I worked the Independants my career as a superstar and could have a match with anyone.” said Mike. “What the hell are you getting at Mike.” said Jim. “I’m just worried that your going to go into this match with Xander and put to much on the line in some crazy match. Jim, you work better then anyone in that company. You’ve already shortened your career with your neck and I know for a fact, and personal experience that when you get into a program that you feel strongly about. The writing, you like the guy your working with in the ring. You want to give it everything you got. Jim, you’ve got a lot to give but you could risk it all.” said Mike. “Damn Mike, I know what’s at stake. It’s business.” said Jim. “If they guys say it’s a gimmick or whatever match then I have to go with it.” said Jim. “I’ve never been afraid to do what’s asked of me. That’s how I got to where I am. I’m a worker Mike, just like you taught me.” “Yeah I know Jim. I know your into this match. Xander busted you up, he dodged your bullet. Now you got him locked down just don’t go and make it some crazy match just for the sake of raising the draw rate” said Mike. “You don’t need the money.” said Mike. “Yeah I don’t need the money. I don’t need some crazy match either. But whatever makes this match work is what’s going to happen. I can’t wait to get my hands on that son of a bitch for what he did to my arm. How he tried to screw me out of the title match. I’m expecting him to do even more, even worse. But you know Mike, you know me Mike. You know I’ve got a plan to put him off his game” said Jim. “Figures. What is it this time?” said Mike. Kimson takes a swig of his beer and smiles. “I’ve hired a trainer” said James. “What?” offered Mike in return. Kimson chuckles to himself. Then takes another sip and puts the beer down. “Yeah, I found someone that knows how to beat Xander.” said Kimson. “I can beat him myself mind you Mike. That’s not a concern. But this, this is the icing on the cake. You don’t have to worry about this match Mike. I appreciate the fact that you want to give me advice. And you spent all your life helping me out. Paying for my motel rooms on the road, picking up the tab in the bars. You introduced me and Victoria. You are my mentor Jim and no one knows me better then you.” said Kimson. “Jimbo, I just think that you might be putting to much of yourself into this match.” said Mike. “But I don’t want to convince you to not go through with it. Because to be honest I’d love to see you with that Title belt. I’d love to come visit you when you win it. Because just like you, I’ve won more world titles then you can shake a stick at, but never something so big as the eVo Title.” said Mike. “I know what this means to you Jim. I want to help you if I can. But I want to help you as a friend. I don’t want to get in your way, I don’t want to impose on you.” said Mike. “I appreciate that. But Mike, besides this Trainer plan of mine, this has to be me by myself. Xander is crafty, he’s got powerful friends but I’m not afraid of what he brings to the table. I’ve never been afraid of anyone or doing anything. But enough of business Mike.” said James Kimson stands up and so does Mike. “Lets go shoot a game of pool and then take those damn kids to school.” joked Mike. Kimson laughed out loud and the two old friends exited the room together and spent the day as old friends
