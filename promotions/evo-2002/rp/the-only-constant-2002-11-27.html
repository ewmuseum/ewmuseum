The only constant

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/02 Luther Wisdom and Xavier Citizen are reviewing tapes of the tag match from Rise of The Legends. X's condition it seems is deteriorating daily but he is still stong enough to hide it from his partner. Xavier coughing excuses himself while Luther delivers a promo.... 'It is a shame, what happened to Thug Roc at the PPV. Trouble with women is something no superstar should let interfere with business. But enough about Thug Roc...enough about NEW-TV. What the Brotherhood did Sunday was make a statement....what is that statement you might ask?' Looks over at 1/2 of the Evolution Tag Team titles they 'stole' this past Sunday... 'The statement is quite simple, The Brotherhood should have been apart of the tag tournament from the start! And no matter what goes on....WE are the individuals who will define, and control the tag team picture here in Evolution. As far as a stable war....that is a concept which is antiquated at best. The Unlimited are a shadow of what they....or excuse me WE once was, The Notorious are for all intents and purposes distracted with one another, and NEW-TV a nonfactor before....and now more of a nonfactor with Shocka being the only reliable flag bearer.' In the back room Xavier Citizen wipes blood from his mouth. His cancer is catching up with him. He has one of Malcolm King files out...it's the bio of a wrestler named Nation RavensEcho; a student of ULWN. XC:'The day of my death is coming soon...before that happens I will establish Luther and the OMEN brotherhood as one of the cornerstones here in Evolution. But we are in a middle of this conflict which I may not leave to see resolved, Wisdom needs someone he can rely on and quite frankly that egotistical bastard Malcolm King took himself out of the picture by losing his pissing contest with Dan Somers. Luckily I for once have a plan......' raises the folder..... 'I'm a call away from a New OMEN Brotherhood....but first things first, for one I'm still alive. So that means Shocka and NEW-TV has the unfortunate luck to face myself and Wisdom while I am still living and breathing. This matchup has been billed as one of NEW-TV seeking revenge for them not gaining the Tag team titles. LOL.....how can you be mad at not winning something you never deserved anyway? Simply put Shocka you were in the wrong place at the wrong time. NEW-TV occupied our spot at the PPV, and it was just my way of letting you know....You are out of your league....NEW-TV is out of their league.What we have with The Unlimited is a highly personable matter....HOW DARE YOU BRING BUSINESS INTO IT!!!!!' cough cough 'Those Tag team titles are The Brotherhood's, and we will eliminate anyone....I mean anyone, who get in our way. I sure hope that NEW-TV does something stupid and try.....it's been awhile since I had a good workout.' to be continued.......
