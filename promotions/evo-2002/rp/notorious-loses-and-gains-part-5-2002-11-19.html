Notorious loses and gains; part 5

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 19, 2002 'I don't care what you want. You and Asis are going to go into that ring under no disqualification rules.' The voice of Daniel Somers can be heard screaming. The scene fades in and Scott Frost is standing infront of the desk of Daniel Somers. Somers is sitting in his chair, and he is looks very uptight at the moment and Frost looks to be very pissed. 'And quite frankly, I hope that Asis beats those ridiculious breads loose sunday.' Somers continues yelling at Frost. Frost looks at Somers and smiles and shakes his head slightly. 'Somers. What do you have against me?' Frost asks. 'All I ever did was take Erin Christoph off of your roster, WHICH YOU SHOULD APPRECIATE!' Frost retorts. Frost smiles and then points at Somers, who looks at him wondering what he is pointing at. 'OH! Your made because I attacked your friends Tyler too. Well, maybe you should get over it. I had to do something to make Unlimited think I was with them. You should appreciate everything I have done for eVolution Wrestling.' Frost again retorts at Somers. Somers gets a stone hard stare that is now glaring at Frost. 'Oh...wait! I know! Your mad, because my friend L2J is sharing the Universal championship with your little teddy bear; Sovereign.' Frost finishes smiling. Somers points at him. 'Why are you even here?' Somers asks Frost. Frost smiles. 'I'm glad you asked me that. You see Danny. I can call you Danny, can't I Danny?' Frost asks Somers with a very sarcastic tone of voice. Somers stares at Frost and rolls his eyes. 'Good. Well. Danny. You see. We have a problem. At Rise Of The Legends, there is a chance that yours truly has to face Shocka. Now, don't get me wrong. I ain't affraid of Shocka, but he is allies with probably the most corrupt man in eVolution Wrestling since the entrance of Erin Christoph. I don't trust New-TV. So come the chance that yours truly and Shocka so happen come toe to toe in that squared circle that you so graciously call your meal ticket. I want you to take everything in your power to not allow New-TV, Unlimited, OR Omen Brotherhood to come down to that ring. If you can do this, Danny. I can promise you now, the TRUE Universal champion L2J....or the Continental champion Jason Jameson, or The Queen of Notorious, Seara will not come down to the ring either.' Frost stops and looks at Somers awaiting a response. Somers looks at Frost. 'Now you know I can't promise you that. But just to make things more suspensful for, yours truly. I will say that, I will try.....' Somers tries to reply, before getting cut off by Frost. Frost slams his hands on the desk and looks at Somers who quickly shuts up. 'NO!' Frost demands. 'YOU, WILL! You see, If anyone else besides Jameson or myself wins this tournament. eVolution will quickly fall to ruins, until Notorious feels that justice has been fulfilled. You see, whether you...Thug roC, or Davidson wants to admit it. Notorious owns this place! We are the dominate force right now. WE are exactly what everyone needs to get free of the torment that Unlimited has put everyone through. And you are sitting here and telling me you will try?!' Frost screams. 'EH EH! Ain't going to happen. Notorious has done EVERYTHING for this federation, and the CEO the big boss man. The new Vince McMahon of the wrestling world is telling me he will try? NO!' Frost continues to scream. Frost throws his right arm behind him and points towards the door of the office. 'HOW MANY PEOPLE!? How many more people, am I going to have to go through to make you happy? I have lost two matches since I have been here. TWO! I have single handedly taken out the vast majority of Unlimited! I have formed the safe haven of eVolution, which is Notorious! I have trampled through most of my opponents, to her you say you will TRY?!' Frost continues to scream and yell. Obviously hearing all the screaming, eVolution Wrestling Chief of Security; Justin Famous comes storming into the office followed by his right hand man Deacon. Frost continues to to scream disregarding the arrival of Famous and Deacon. 'Fine! Asis, Jameson, Shocka, Big Daddy Dreamer, Sovereign, Malcolm King, Xander, Heartbreak Chick.' Frost names a few members of the eVolution roster. 'You can bring anyone of them out in that tournament, you can do whatever you want to see fit that they win the tournament because it's going to cause good ratings and a better friendship with you and Old School Entertainment. But I tell you right now. I will kick your ass, I will kick everyone elses ass, I will kick 'The Tru One' Joshua Krowes ass, I will kick Camerons ass. I will even......' Frost scream and then stops. Frost quickly turns around and jumps at Justin Famous and Deacon and wraps his arms around their throat as he falls and laying them out with reverse bulldogs onto the hard floor of Somers room. Somers stands up to look at what just happened and Frost stands up and cleans himself off. 'I will even kick their ass too!' Frost scornfully remarks towards Somers. Somers looks at his security members in shock and Frost points in Somers direction. 'You will do this for me, because whether or not you like it. I am winning that damned tournament.' Frost finishes to Somers. Frost walks out of the door closing it behind him. Somers looks at the door as it closes and Famous and Deacon slowly fight to get back to their feet. The scene fades to black. (To Be Continued....)
