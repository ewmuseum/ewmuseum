Eyes Staring, Dreams Wearing

6 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/6/02 Dusk had already pulled all the light down over the distant curve of the horizon. A few stars winked in the darkened night sky. The snow of the sky fell in dreary paths that brought them down to their grave. In the center of an isolated field enclosed by an empty forest sat an unmarked green car, its license place only jargon and useless. The headlights rammed through the falling snow, catching a glimpse of its graceful fall down to Earth. A radio sang a lifeless song. A simple fear to wash you away Within lay a man who didn’t really want to be where he was. He didn’t want to exist in the fashion he had every day since his birth, and only wanted to be the best that he could be. His nostrils inhaled a large quantity of oxygen, held it in self pity, and then released it back into the confines of his automobile. His arms, useless to him, rested across his chest, crossed for both warmth and emotion. His legs, tired yet undeveloped, were seated across the otherwise empty passenger seat, their muscles calm. His eyes stared forward through the windshield, paralyzed to the blizzard. Unemotional towards the world around him. Uncaring and heartless towards any sign of humanity in the slightest form. All he wanted to do was live. That was it. Nothing more. Nothing less. An open mind canceled it today The world was slow and silent. The people were just shadows. The past was just books collecting dust in the attic of a library. Feelings were really the only thing he had, and all of them were pessimistic and didn’t really help him resolve anything. Sometimes they were only another burden to carry on his back as he walked the path of self loathing. Sometimes he wanted to hold his breath and see how long it would take him to die. Maybe he could drown himself in sorrow, and never look back. A silent song that’s in your words In the night sky, a rift of white light burned through the world, lighting everything all at once. Beyond it shined the images of a two eyes. The edges of the rift rippled. The images were at first blurry, but became crystal clear in only seconds. The moving picture revealed a gym. A few weights lifted by the arms of a mysterious body. A very attractive woman walking by, shooting a glance and a smile straight into the image. He shook his head, disappointed, but still somewhat understanding. If there was one person he understood, it was the one locked behind those eyes. Jason’s eyes, he reminded himself as he stared into the rift. It was the only way Jay could see outside this snowy, desolate world of his. This was his abysmal hell – trapped inside an anti paradise of tears and saliva. Nothing mattered here. Only the world out there was important. And things were getting to the point where it seemed that he was never going to see it. Now, it looked like he was trapped in his prison forever. A different taste that’s in your mind The blizzard suddenly ended. The empty field surrounded by snowy trees melted away. The car exploded into debris that almost immediately disintegrated in mid air. The only thing that remained was the dark sky, now only a dark entity, and the rift. Jay stared up at it, lost in thought and waiting for something to happen. Suddenly, he decided he was tired, and turned away. He walked over to a door, which seemed to suddenly appear out of thin air, shining brightly with an unknown source. Jay opened the door, and slammed it shut behind him, its crash echoing through whatever oblivion he called home. [Song: “Buddha For Mary” by 30 Seconds to Mars]
