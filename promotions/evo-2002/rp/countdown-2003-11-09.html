Countdown

9 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-09 Not long now. Just a few hours left. The last time he felt anything anywhere near this was before he faced Kimson and Xander a couple of months back. But that was nothing like this. Then he’d had the chance to get his hands on Xander, but there was another variable in the equation, the variable that did indeed stop him from having as much fun as he wanted to. But there was no variable this time. There was only him, Xander and anything else he wanted to bring into the mix. That ten count was going to be the most relieving ten seconds of Xander’s life when Asis was through with him. But it wasn’t going to be easy for Xander. It wasn’t as if he was planning on taking him out as quickly as he could and letting him get away with a ten count. He was going to make sure that he’d punished him in every way that he could imagine before he allowed him the luxury of that ten count. Not long now. Just a few hours left. Sitting watching the world go by he wondered what Xander was doing now. Whether he was preparing for the match, physically, mentally, whether he was kissing his beautiful wife and kids goodbye, saying goodbye to his loving family, knowing that the next time he saw them would most likely be from a hospital bed. That was the plan anyway and he was sure Xander knew it. He wasn’t stupid and he didn’t underestimate people. He knew exactly what he was capable of, he knew that it wasn’t going to be easy. He very well knew that his life was probably in danger. So why Xander was going along with this god only knew. Perhaps he thought it was the noble thing to do. To make himself look good in front of the millions and millions of eVolution fans. Or maybe he really did love his family enough that he’d put his life on the line to safeguard theirs. He didn’t have an answer, he couldn’t think of one, but then he probably wasn’t thinking hard enough. It had come to the point where he didn’t care about the why Xander was doing it. All he knew, was soon he’d have Xander in that ring for the first time in a long time. Not long now. Just a few hours left. There had been a few setbacks along the way, but all in all things had worked out well. He was, in a way at least, pleased with himself. He’d done what he needed to, nothing more and nothing less. He knew it was going to be worth it. That ten count was going to be like a countdown to ecstasy by the time he was through with Xander. Ecstasy as a relief from the pain. And he’d make sure Xander would stay down for that ten count. At all costs.
