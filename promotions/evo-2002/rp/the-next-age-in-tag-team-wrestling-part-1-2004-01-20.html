The next Age in Tag Team Wrestling part 1

20 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-01-20 'How appropiate that the words ding dong the witch is dead....has been running through my head in preparing for this coming Legends.' Malcolm King is seen sitting in the middle of the ring in what is for now an empty arena. His thoughts and mind are clearly on Heartbreak Chick and Big Daddy Dreamer, collectively known as The Syndicate. 'There has been concern that once the destruction of The Syndicate is complete, that the tag team division will somehow cease to exist. Well I am here to say that a new age in tag team wrestling will dawn......but enough of the angles the gimmick and whatever promotional bullshit the staff here wants to spit out about the dire state of eveything not associated with their own agenda. Why pontificate on these matters when in all honesty the history between us is there....no hype needed or necessary. Sure The Syndicate's eventual demise will be a set back......but the name on the marquee is Evolution isn't it?' he stands to reveal the EDW Championship behind his back...it rests in his hands as the light relflects off of it..... 'It started with this......we all know it. No matter how many revisionist wrestlers/bookers we have here those who have been here since the beginning....knows the genesis. Dreamer you and I fought over this.....you and I fought over HBC...you and I fought and fought and fought until we ran out of things to fight over; and then you made it personal.' he throws the former world championship over his shoulder...... 'The question has been asked why won't I just give it up and die? What hold does this business....this federation seem to have on me? Like the phoenix I keep on rising from the ashes not because I want to...but because it's my nature. Time and time I put over The Syndicate and with all due respect....and I do respect HBC and Dreamer, they could not kill me; they could not keep me down because we are two sides of the same tainted coin. The destruction of The Unlimited.....yes at the hands of The Syndicate; my creation....or was it? You see The Unlimited you destroyed was so far removed from what it was intended to be what it was originally that the cliche wrestling stable you destroyed was Unlimited in name only; or as I have joked Evolution's own version of NWO-Bteam. But before that there was the Universal Championship......the title you caused me to lose, only to regain again as if it was nothing more than a minor set back; let's face it no matter what anyone tries talent always rises to the top. There is a reason no one wants to recognize my contributions to this company why would anyone willingly be overshadowed?' he leaves the belt on a ring corner as he exits the ring........ 'I am one of the reasons why this federation is even in existance so to say the tag team division is going to die with no Syndicate is at best ignorant and at worse stupid.But this really isn't about Evolution, it's apparent stars, or management....it's about the three of us. About Malcolm King, Heartbreak Chick, and Big Daddy Dreamer. Three federations, multiple titles, and argueably the three most over people without a belt in any company......no I did not forget you are tag team champions, just after Legends that status will change. And you mention Luther Wisdom becareful what you wish for he wasn't the only one I.......' his cell phone rings; he answers it and cracks a smile........to be continued. (OOC:Will finish up after my meeting didn't realize what the time was as I have a lot more to go over.)
