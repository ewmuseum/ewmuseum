Sinister Plan Part 2

29 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 29, 2002 'Are you really going to do this?' Questions the suttle voice of Kelsey Frost, wife of Scott Frost. The scene fades in, and Scott and Kelsey are standing in a room filled with books. A huge table lays in the center of the room, and Kelsey props up against the table and then sits non-cholantley on the table as she looks over to Frost who is standing there with his arms crossed as if thinking about something. 'Are you really going to screw him out of something so important?' Again questions the wife of Scott Frost. Frost still staring towards his wife. 'Yeah...It has to be done. Someone has to start the destruction.' Finally replies Frost as he takes a few steps closer to his wife. 'But I gotta figure out what to do about the eight man tag match. Xander, wants me to concentrate on Sean Mason. But it's hard to concentrate when you know that L2J is lurking....and trying to get me out of the greatest faction ever created in the history of professional wrestling.' Frost adds to his previous statement. 'Kendo Stick.....Lumber jack. I respect L2J. But you seem to be out to kill somebody at Legends XI' Kelsey replies. Frost looks around and then picks up a Kendo Stick. 'I am going to show him what it's going to look like. I'm bringing this to the ring at Legends X. The Eight man tag match. Sean Mason will be the first to feel the Cold Front of the kendo stick.......then....on to L2J at Legends XI.' Frost announces as he observes his weapon of choice. Kelsey looks over to Frost. 'Don't kill them. It's OSE's fault for putting this match together. They wanted more violence. I think they are just trying to punish those for men.' says Kelsey. Frost looks at Kelsey with that sinister smile that he has slowly became known for. 'Exactly. OSE wants violence....and to them, violence equals ratings...' says Frost as he slams the Kendo stick down on the table just inches away from his wife Kelsey. 'OSE wants violence. Well, eVolution did a good job hiring me. Because Unlimited has just got their violent factor topped up a notch. Wednesday night, when we go head to head...toe to toe....kendo stick to body.....It's going to be Unlimited..it's going to be the Cold Hearted One....Xander......Jinx.....The Heartbreaker....standing up in victory. Proclaiming the victory that is already inevitably ours.' retorts Frost he is interrupted by the ringing of the telephone. Kelsey walks over and presses a button on the telephone. 'Hello?' Frost asks. 'Hey man! It's L.' Whats going on? Frost and Kelsey look at each other. (To be continued....)
