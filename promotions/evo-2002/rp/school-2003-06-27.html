School

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-27 Scott had spent the last few months in his Uncle's home preparing himself for his first day of public school in nearly 4 years, he didn't know what to expect when he stepped into the building and neither did Adam, but they both found out who the school bully was right off the bat, he decided to try to jam Scott into a garbage barrel. Bad idea on his part, he grabbed Scott by the ribs leaving his arms free to give punch the bully in the face. It only took two hits for the bully to realize that he was beat in the strength department, he already knew that he couldn't compete in the brains department when Scott raised his arms. When the bully ran away all Scott did was chuckle and wipe the dust from his shirt before walking into the office to find out what his classes were Adam followed after him wondering whether or not Scott would be caught for that little fight. They got their schedules and went to their home rooms, the day began just like every other day announcements with everything that the school thought would be important to say on the first day of school. Scott and Adam had only been in the building once before very briefly with their Uncle during the summer vacation so their Uncle could discuss the situation at hand with his nephews. He wanted to make sure that they had been enrolled for the next year so he didn't have to home school because he had to attend to business matters during the day. Scott and Adam didn't know what to expect when they walked into the classrooms, and in both cases something was wrong. Adam was accepted immediately while Scott was put in a homeroom with the school bully. Now as you could most likely guess the bully doesn't like to be beaten so he won in another way, he made sure that is was known that anyone who hung out with Scott would be bullied until they were out of high school. Scott didn't care what happened he knew that everybody would be afraid of him once someone decided to do something about him during morning recess, then the respect would come his way. Adam was luckier, he was put in a homeroom filled with kids from the 'in-crowd' and somehow he fit in with them. School dragged on through the day for both Scott and Adam, the only thing of the day that meant anything to them was gym and they were lucky for one reason, you didn't have to shower after class. Today they played dodge ball in gym and Scott was the first to be 'it'. It only took on hit to the side to get another kid in the class going, but the thing that the kid forgot was that Scott had the ball and when the kid got in Scott's face the only thing that he'd need to gain was a second wind. Scott pushed the ball into the kid’s stomach on the side the gym teacher was watching and punched him in the stomach on the side the gym teacher couldn't see. The kid fell to the floor dumbfounded, he thought that if any punches were to be thrown they'd be coming from him but he was wrong, he hadn't anticipated how confrontational Scott was. Scott knew that tomorrow would be the day for the respect to come his way no matter what. During morning recess Scott just sat off the side watching everyone chat while the kid he knocked the wind out of watched him awaiting for when the bell rings to go over and try to beat up Scott. The layout of the front of the school was simple, there was an 'island' with a circle of concrete around it and the concrete led to the stairs. When the bell rang Scott walked straight to the school but on the little circle of grass in the middle of the front yard of the school he was sucker punched by the kid he knocked the wind out of. The Kid only chuckled and said, 'C'mon get up boy, I got's to introduce myself to ya.' Scott chuckled at the same time and pulled the other child's feet out from underneath him, then he raised his fists ready for the fight. A circle of kids stood around them screaming, 'FIGHT! FIGHT!' through the whole thing. The teachers stood off to the side remembering the incident last year when they tried to break up a fight, they remembered the blood splatter that had resulted from that so they thought it best to just look the other way while the fight happened. One child from the circle screamed, 'C'MON JEREMY KICK HIS ASS!' Scott chuckled at the remark and made sure that anyone thinking that he would lose would get a rude awakening. He thought about his life and his anger came surging forth like a wave and within moments Jeremy was unconscious and Scott's fist were covered in Jeremy's blood. The crowd stood dumbfounded as the nurse pushed through them and picked up the 'victim'. Scott just chuckled and pushed through afterwards knowing that would he end up home for that. Adam was one of the only children who decided not to witness the fight, he knew the outcome before it even began because his brother wasn't ever going to lose a fight, all he had to do was remember the things that made him angry and BAM! The other kid would be down for the count. Adam began talking to one of his friends in homeroom and he heard about the Phenomena of pro wrestling for the first time. Little did he know what the troubles the dream that formed in his head would bring him.
