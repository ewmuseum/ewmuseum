Date: 2003-10-22 Previous: ++[Sarah]900483476232211=+Damon -DamonX672020475Sarah=+Sue&swnwjjLII<1 dOn+t 44832920 fu(|<> My shoes lay in a pool of a dilute-orange coloured liquid. The blade which rested in my pocket pierced my skin gently and some thick blood ran down to my ankles. My hair covered the majority of my face which was buried in my hands as I sat on the wooden bench three blocks away from the O’Halloran household. My self-conscious was urging me to go back. To go back, take the blade out of my pocket and cut her throat. Why? To get at Damon. To sicken him. To finish him emotionally. To scar him. To make him feel how I feel. My head looked around frantically, expecting that at any moment Damon would once again appear and look for ‘Fran’ as his daughter screams coming to terms with the day’s events. Inflict emotional wounds so deep that she’ll be lost in her loss of innocence. I continued to edge closer to the house, now only a block away from the screams and yelling. I opened a small, wooden gate leading into the back garden and sat picking the daisies from the desolate garden. “Sarah... I love you, you know I love you.” Damon’s words were rushed and slightly mumbled. Right now you could be listening in on her father crying as he watches a tape of a hooker fucking you as his distraught daughter watches. Watching as you slice her and the hooker and put them in the same black bag and dump them on his driveway. Imagine the headlines.... .... and they’d never know. He’d be too distraught to touch you ever again. You’d be invincible. You would have done the things and you could undo them too. I put one hand on the switch blade and the other hand on the door-knob. My body’s weight pushed to one side and my hand twisted the door-knob. I watched them sitting down, him telling her to forget about what happened, that he wouldn’t let anything happen to her.... ever. It didn’t matter. The damage had been done.
