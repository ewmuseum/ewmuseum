The Return?!

3 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-03 When you had finally thought your horribly painful nightmares were over, like everything was safe and okay. You have only convinced yourself, if you thought I would stay away. I have returned to reclaim my twisted glory, leave mark like never before and take what is rightfully mine. I am one of the sickest and greatest warriors to ever step foot into the eVolution ring! The deadliest athlete in the game coming where, to his home. Where I can rule with an Iron Fist, destroy all who stand before me and prove just what your really fucking with. 'I have made a huge decision!' I announced to Alex Caine. I lay down on the weight bench and cracked my knuckles. I looked up at Alex who stacked the weights at 320lbs. I felt a grin widen on my face. Alex stepped behind me! Alex asks with a delayed response,'Oh yeah, what kind of decision?' he looked at me oddly. I felt this sick, anxious dark feeling fall over my body and I said, 'eVolution! I am making my return soon!' 'That's really...' Alex sounded hesitant, 'Something else I guess! Congratulations on the good news!' 'Bad news in the eyes of eVolution!' I replied and felt the demons rage inside of me. 'I haven't been myself since I went separate ways and I feel I have left unspoken and goals unaccomplished!' Alex looked down and smirked, 'I know how you feel, like something inside of you just wasn't there. Almost like you are missing something you cannot find worth the life of you!' I spoke out in a rant, 'I have a list of names who will not go unpunished and I mean serious business!' I clenched my hands down tight on the weight bar, I nodded to Alex and he lift the bar off the supports. Alex said, 'You had a devastating tag championship run, One of the best those titles have ever seen!' I lowered the weights and replied, 'I have a new path and journey to follow now... All will become clear soon!' I took a deep breath and lifted the heavy load, exhaling while pushing up. Alex retorted, 'I almost feel sorry after the destructive chaos Twisted Reality brought to eVolution before!' 'I may have a long path, but I will meet my destination, fate will play out like it was meant to!' I said and lowered the weights, taking a deep breath, then pushing the weights up and exhaling! Alex replied, 'You sound like you have put a lot of thought into this!' 'You better believe it, but honestly it was to bound to happen in my career once again. Mark my words, I will obtain the Universal Championship before my time is over! It will be one of the darkest days for wrestling to ever witness!' I said spitefully and my voice was filled with anger. I lowered and then lifted the weight bar. 'The insanity has only begun, I twisted and manipulated, controlling the minds of everyone in eVolution. The infinite of evil has come back to play again!' I said with a dirty look my face, and lowered and then lifted the weight bar. 'The pain and misery forgotten will be quickly remembered!' I thought to myself, what am I without eVolution and what are they without me! I felt awkward and out of place when I left. I have come back with a sick sense of pride and goals and challenges I never had before. I have come back to walk the walk, talk the talk and own, stamp and brand anyone who stands in my way! Alex asked, 'Who is your first target?' 'Time will only tell!' I replied with a demented look on my face.
