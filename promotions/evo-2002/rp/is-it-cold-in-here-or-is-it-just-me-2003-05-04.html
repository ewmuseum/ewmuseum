Is it Cold in Here, or is it Just Me?

4 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/4/03 The scene opens on a driveway. The driveway leads up to a very well kept house surrounded by a couple trees, and overlooking what appears to be the Los Angeles, California skyline. All of a sudden, a sporty neon orange VW Beetle pulls up in the driveway. Out steps one of eVolutions newest returning superstars, Chad Winters. Decked out in his cut-off jean shorts, and white tank top, the spiky blonde caucasian looks into the camera 'Hello everybodies at home' Chad begins. 'The Chad is here at the posh estate of non other then Mister Xander.' Chad giggles like a school girl 'Let's see what he is up to tonight!' Chad states as he turns and saunters up to the front door. Chad rings the bell. After a minute, no one has answered, so he rings again. This time, after a few moments, Xander opens the door. Xander is obviously in for the night, as he has on his relaxing clothes. 'Oh shit, what the hell are you doing here?' Xander begrudgingly asks The Chad. 'To see you silly!' The Chad gleams back. 'Why?' Xander asks. 'And how the hell did you find my place?' 'Because, you a special guy around eVolution.. and, he told me not to tell you how I got your address. It's a secret!' 'OK, what? What is it? You know it's like 10:30 here, right?' Xander quizes the Ice Cold man himself. 'Of course horse, I'm not stupid! I'm on Hawaiian time anyway, so it's earlier.' Chad says as you can almost feel yourself getting stupider. 'Get on with it already. Questions, answers, whatever, hurry.' Xander rushes. 'Ok, ok. No need to get blue Lou.' The Chad goes on, annoyingly. 'So, my question.' After a few moments of silence, Xander breaks the calm. 'Yes?1 Go on already? God damned hippies.' Xander mutters to himself. 'Ok, ok. Would you like to purchase some candy, so that eVolution will send me on a trip to training camp, so I can be the new announcer for Legends?!' Chad almost screams out in pride. 'You're not serious?' Xander says. 'I am, totally!' Chad retorts. 'You come here. To my house. Late at night. And want me to buy candy, so you can be a future announcer for Legends? Are you freaking serious? Wait, is this a joke by S.? Christoph? To try and throw some levity into things, or something?' Xander wonders out loud. 'Nope, this is the true Maguillicuddy!' Chad looks as if to orgasm at the thought. Xander slams the door in Chad's face, knocking him down onto the porch step. 'Well! That's hospitality for you!' Chad huffs off to his Beetle, fires it up, and drives away.
