Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens viewing a black mercedes with dark tinted windows parked on the side of the street. The car with midnight black paint with chrome, twenty-inch rims and detail. The car lowered down with ground effects and has an overall stealth effect. A man wearing baggy blue jeans, wife beater and a tuque pulled down over his eyes comes strolling down the sidewalk and closely observes the car with full concentration and attention 'Thats a bad ass ride, I wonder what Lucy would think if I came to pick her up behind the wheel of that!' The man says with an excited tone of voice, he slowly approaches the car with an awestruck look. The man standing beside the car now, he bends down and peers into the window; though he can only see his own reflection back in return. The man waves his hand in front of his eyes and shakes his head side to side. The man casually and completly turns with a content smile on his face and strokes his straggly, redneck chin hair. The man walks around the car to the drivers side and pulls out a black box from his back pocket, he opens up the box and it looks to be some kind of lock picking kit. 'Hah ah, It dont matter what kind of wheels someone has cause this cat could steal it. Nothing can stop me, cause Im the grand selector, the infamous Hector... Hah ah ah' he says and starts laughing uncontrollably. Pulling one of the custom pieces from his took kit, Hector approaches the lock and hesitates moment; he speaks 'Dont worry, I wont hurt you baby...' he says and the pick can be heard on the side of the locks metal. 'Daddy has found himself a Mercedes!' he adds. Hector lowers his head to carefully pick the lock and out of nowhere the door flies open. Hector stumbles backwards and falls on his head, he tries to get back up and falls over with his eyes rolled back. The scene pans with an irate Morpheus Omega stepping from the driver's side door. 'You fucked with the wrong car essay, you see these 'wheels' as you put it? This piece of machinery belongs to me and you tried to fuck with something that is mine. I should gut you like a fish!' Morpheus screams with an angry and disgusted look on his face, his left eye begins twitching. In burst of anger, Morpheus runs over and kicks the man in the mouth; he lifts his leg high and stomps with heavy impact on the side of his head. Blood sprays from the brutal blow and the man looks like he could easily be dead. Morpheus paces back and forth with a deeply bothered look. 'Better yet, I will smash a brick off his face until it resembles a bloody, gooey mush! Beat his face in beyond recognition and teach him the never forgotten lesson of death. You stepped into my world' Morpheus rants and directs his attention back towards Hector. Morpheus walks back to the car and reaches behind the driver's seat and sure enough pulls the brick out, he has a sick grin on his face walking back towards the individual. About to punish the thief on the most cruel, unfair and unjust court system brought about. Where anything goes... Morpheus stands towering above the unconscience man with brick high above his head. 'Time for your reconstutive, facial surgery from your's truly... 'The Infinite of evil! You played the game against Satan's assasin and lost. Accept my punishment and feel my unholy wrath you maggot' Morpheus screams loudly on a power trip and a sick chuckle is heard. Leaning down towards the man's head, Morpheus swings the brick far upwards and comes down with sheer strength. Someone runs into the side of Morpheus and pushes him. The brick crumbles on the ashphalt, inches from Hector's head. Morpheus gathers himself and stares wildly. 'Morpheus!' Sky Night screams with the file folders in her arms and face to face with him. 'We have to go now, security knows someone was inside and I suggest we leave... Forget this punk and drive before the cops come!' she breathes deeply and out of breath. 'Whats going on here? Is that you Lucy... Have you come to save me? I knew we were meant for each other...' Hector says in a daze with no recollection of being knocked out or what is going on now. 'You shut it before I let him smash a brick off your face, you hear me?' Sky questions and directs her attention back towards a snarling Morpheus. 'Get in the car now!' Sky Night demands while running to the passenger side. Morpheus does a double take of the car and Hector like he was trying to make a decision. Morpheus furiously storms back towards the car and takes one last look at Hector, he smiles... 'You will get yours another day!' he says boldly while getting into the car. They both close their doors and the scene fades following.
