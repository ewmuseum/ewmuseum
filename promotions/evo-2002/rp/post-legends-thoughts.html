2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Toronto, Canada, home of the First Lady of Evolution. HBC is sitting on her plush couch watching over and over Legends from last week when she had the United Championship firmly in her grasp, only to be thwarted by a mystery man and that two-bit chicken shit Daniel Somers. HBC rolls tape again…. Damon slowly gets up, and HBC lunges forward with her superkick, but the move is ducked. She turns around and is given a short arm clothesline that flips her around before she lands hard on the canvas. The crowd is silenced as Damon finds this a perfect time to taunt. The crowd then goes wild as they see Big Daddy Dreamer running down the ramp. HBC is being checked on by the referee as BDD gets up on the ring apron. Damon notices BDD and turns around and BDD grabs him by the head and gives him a stun gun across the top rope. HBC is up as Damon staggers around, right into that Kiss of Death! Damon falls to the canvas and HBC makes the pin. But the referee is trying to get BDD away from the ring. Out of the crowd, a man comes out and gets on the top turnbuckle. He leaps off and nails a frog splash across the back of HBC and hurries to exit the ring. “Who was that?!” HBC rolls onto her back in pain and Damon makes the pin. One…. Two… Three!!! Damon takes the win and the crowd can’t believe it. BDD enters the ring just as Damon exits. Damon is greeted by the man who made the interference with a hug. The two celebrate their win as HBC is being aided by BDD. On the eVo-tron, you can see Daniel Somers clapping with a huge smile on his face. “That must be Somers’ insurance policy he mentioned earlier!” “Well obviously he’s getting the results he wanted because Damon is still the champion.” “What a crooked way to win a match.” Indeed…… what a crock of shit is more like it. O’Hallaron knowing full well he couldn’t defeat me had to resort to something I figured that he would. So tell me O’Hallaron, how much cock of Somers did you have to suck to finagle this kind of deal. I always knew you were a peter-puffer. Those damn Irish always were a bunch of cock sucking complainers. No wonder the English viewed them as second-rate citizens and gave them all the Guiness they could handle. So, St. Patricks day is around the corner and I’m sure Damon will piss himself drunk on green beer looking to screw little leprechaun’s. Enjoy your little gay holiday because The HeartBreaker isn’t going anywhere soon. Deep down in your gut, you knew I had your ass beat. You knew I’m better than you. For all your experience inside the cell……. Oh wait….. that’s where you tried to get a date isn’t it, face down and ass up….. none the less Damon, HBC isn’t going anywhere. I will have that title, whether next week or at Unbreakable….. I will become the United Champion and you will feel the Kiss of Death again. But this time, when you look up to the sky, seeing those lights, I will be standing over you holding the title, and then punt those teabag sacks of nuts into the Scottish Highlands. Your days……. Are numbered……
