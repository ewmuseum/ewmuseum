Escape

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th November 2002 A night that had made a difference for many, a night that crowned so many champions, a night that would stand out for four individuals. Titles in their hands, L2J had succeeded in gaining the Universal Title, no longer having to share it with Sovereign, and Jason Jameson still carried his Continental Championship. Seara however had lost her title shot to opponent Sean Mason, but had congratulated him, shook his hand, and raised his arm high in the air above both their heads. It had been a mystery to everyone why she had done this, but surely something in her mind had told her it was the best idea. For whatever reason she had done it, it had left her just as confused as the audience surrounding her. After the ppv, she had left the dressing room without telling her teammates why she was in a rush, or where she was going, instead, she rushed on, grabbed her bag and rushed out to get into a waiting cab. Frost had stood shocked at the female’s speedy yet silent exit from the building, but he had known that he could track her down with great ease. He had found her the day after, and had talked to her about Rise Of The Legends, but she had disappeared again, and left Frost wondering where she had gone once again. (The room was well light, and people could be heard moving around upstairs. From the look of the room, this was Seara’s childhood bedroom, and a partial training room. At one end a normal teenagers room, the walls covered in posters, but instead of the sexy male models, actors and musicians, her walls had been covered in posters of wrestling heroes and idols, the two biggest posters dominated one entire wall, one of her father standing on the top turnbuckle of the ring, holding up his first ever title, the other poster of her father in mid air performing a swanton bomb from the top of a 20 foot cage to the outside of the ring, both meaningful posters to the female. As Katherine she sat on her bed below the pictures, looking around at her room, a small personal training area at the opposite side of the room, a place where she had forced her pains and worries away with body numbing exercising. She had not been at home often as a child or teenager, but she had been there enough to secure that this basement conversion room was her own room, her sanctuary.) Seara - A lot of people questioned the actions I took at the end of my match with Mason on Sunday night at Rise Of The Legends. Yes I shook his hand; yes I raised his arm high in the air. But I ask you if he did not once deserve that? Did he not deserve to have his hand raised high; did he not deserve that belt to place around his waist? Both Mason and myself have had the same pattern of fortune here in eVolution. When we began, both of us had a poor record, but that changed, and as of late, we have been on form, I do not think that I deserve that belt any more than Mason, but we were the contenders for it, and unluckily for me, I was not on as good form as I have been. Things have been hectic for me in the last week. This place has been awkward to get used to. Settling in to my family, acknowledging them, trying to make up for all the wrong I have done to them, all the pain I caused them. I know that I was the nightmare child, creating trouble everywhere, and hell, look at me; I’m not much better these days. Never having friends or a need for them, just a need to take out my anger on anyone or anything within my grasp. The pain was so real I cannot begin to comprehend life without it. Even now the pain is stronger than I care to feel. Pain has to be weighed out though, and one thing weighs out my pain. (Seara pauses and listens to the sounds from above her. It is clear there is someone else in her father’s house, another male besides her father and younger brother.) Seara - Only two left, my step brother Vinnie, dead, I do not care about that, it is a relief to me, my older brother dead, dying because of the injuries caused by Asis, having his life prolonged on a life support machine, a broken neck, a cripple, paralysed from the neck down, and the nicest thing to do was turn off the machines, they were forcing him to be alive, he was dead inside. You could feel it when you were with him, that eerie dead feeling you get in cemeteries, that was in his hospital room. And Gail, my fathers girlfriend or wife whatever she was to him, no longer a part of his life, she had said something about my mother, said something about me, about my brothers, and her son, and my dad had dropped her, he hadn’t loved her for a long time, but he could never end it, he needed security. Now he has me. Now we are together again, my family, never the same as when mom was alive, but together. (Seara laughs for a moment then focuses back on her previous mood and state of mind). Love, to be in love, to love, to be loved, to kill to love, to love the kill, to hunt the loved. Not knowing what it is, they mystery of the word, the enigma of the emotion, but this enigma and mystery are unfolding right in my grasp, and no matter how tight I squeeze my hand, they still unravel. (The door opens and Seara turns her head to the side to see two male figures. Her younger brother and another figure behind him, in the shade, his face blanketed by a dark shadow from the door). Bruce – Katherine, there’s a guy here to see you, Scott something or other. (Her brother’s words throw the female into a fast fix mode as she suddenly and quickly straightens herself up and makes herself look more presentable. Then with an air of relaxation in her voice she asks him to send Scott down. Laying back she looks to be in a very calm mood. Stopping in his tracks Scott Frost looks around the room staring hard at the posters on her wall above her head.) Frost – Your dad I expect? I talked to him on the phone, he told me you were here and to come over. I got worried when you disappeared again. Seara – Yeah, I’m so sorry about that, just had this horrible feeling that something wasn’t right and I had to come back to make sure. I would have called you but I left so late that I thought you would be asleep. (Seara pauses and looks up at the posters). My father, he was the reason I got into this career, I look up to him for everything. Here, come here, I want you too see something. (Frost does as Seara asks and walks over to her bed, he takes a seat close to her, facing toward her. Looking into his eyes she puts her arms around him and gives him a hug to reassure him that things are different now and that she will no longer run without saying anything about it first. Once she has let go of him, she leans back and lifts up her t-shirt to reveal the scar that runs across her stomach. It is the first time she has purposely revealed the scar to anyone that is unaware of her history and the crash that killed her mother. Slowly Frost reaches toward it and runs his fingers along the huge 10-inch long and inch wide scar, his fingers follow the scar right across her body and he stares into her eyes in a questioning way.) Seara – It was caused in a car crash when I was 3, my mom was killed, and that is what happened to me, I got something in me, it hit me and cut into me, I don’t remember what it was, but it stopped me getting out of the car, they had to cut me free. I…I…I just sat there, I just sat there Scott, held her hand and looked at her. I could have done something, but I sat there, it was my fault she died. (Frost places his fingers over Seara’s lips and looks at her, unsure of how she should have helped or felt. After all she had been too young to know anything, too young to understand. But from the look on her face, she had been at the perfect age to blame herself for her own mothers death, the perfect age to carry the guilt with her forever, no matter what anyone said to tell her otherwise.) Frost – Don’t beat yourself up over this. You know it wasn’t your fault. Look at me Katherine, there was nothing you could have done, you were three, and it was not your fault, you mom was the unlucky one, but she left you with something so important. A passion, she left you with an emotion for her, the feeling of love, and because you felt it for her, you can feel it again. Seara – But it wasn’t fair that my mom died in that crash when my brothers and me walked away, I should have gone with her, it shouldn’t have been just her, she was my life Scott, I never loved anyone after I lost her. Until now. (Seara looks at Frost with a look that could be described as pain hating fear guilt and love all balled up inside on human, waiting to explode out in a ball of passion and pain. Frost returns the look to Seara with a loving and caring stare, his eyes seemingly smiling at hers.) Frost – Hey, if you had died, where would I be, I wouldn’t have you, your too important now, you need to focus on the now. Come on Katherine, look at me. You know you mean a lot to me, and I’m glad I got the chance to meet you. If I hadn’t met you, I would not be this happy. Now look at me. Come on Katherine. Give me that smile. (Looking up, Seara gives Frost the smile he is waiting for, kisses him and thanks him for his words. Then she turns her attention to the door at the top of the stairs, it is open and the figure of her father can be seen looking down the stairs. His wheelchair casting a shadow on the top four steps.) Father – Your friend staying for dinner, it’s ready and there is a place if he wants it. There’s also the sofa bed if he wants to stay the night, after all it’s late now. Seara looks at Frost smile and gets to her feet, then takes hold of his hand and leads him out of her room. The scene fades to black as Frost jokingly picks Seara up and carries her out of her bedroom in his arms.
