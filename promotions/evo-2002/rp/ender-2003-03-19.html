Ender

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th March 2003 Another rivalry has started, another that Asis is looking to end. And he sent that message to Troy Pierce at Legends XXVIII when he attacked him and left him lying on the stage in agony before a scheduled match that didn’t happen. Thanks to Asis’ boss and leader Daniel Somers, Asis has the perfect way to now end this feud between himself and Pierce and maybe even end Pierce’s career. A match that has only ever been seen in eVolution once before. A match that gives Asis the kind of atmosphere that he was born for. A match where escape is literally damn near impossible. At Legends XXIX Asis will get his hands on Troy Pierce, in tiers of eVolution… Once again he is sitting in a white room. White walls, white floor and white ceiling. No windows and seemingly no door either. The all black of his clothes creates an eerie contrast with the colour of the room, making the figure instantly recognisable as Asis himself. It’s clear to see, that under his mass of black and silver hair, Asis is actually grinning. But a grin from Asis is not like anyone else’s, and it does not necessarily mean he’s happy either. You see this smile Pierce? This has not disappeared from my face since last Wednesday night. It’s unusual for me to be smiling and for that I thank you. But unfortunately I don’t think you share the same happiness as me do you Troy? Now I hope you don’t personally bear me too much ill will. Unlimited’s little attack on you was not my idea. Far from it, in fact I did all I could to try and persuade Dan otherwise. Because I wanted you at one hundred percent, I wanted you to be untouched for our match at Legends XXIX. I wanted you to come at me with everything you had, so I could destroy and humiliate you and prove that you’re no match for me. You weren’t any kind of competition before and you most certainly are not now. Asis turns away from the camera and his smile disappears. He appears deep in thought and when he turns back to face the camera his smile has gone, but the deathly stare from his blood red eyes is there. The circumstances have changed somewhat now Troy. When it was just a one on one match you wouldn’t have beaten me. And now the situation has been altered a little bit Troy. It’s tiers of eVolution, something that doesn’t get used very often, probably because it always causes severe injuries. But think about it Troy, this is the kind of atmosphere I thrive on. Anything where there is the potential for me to hurt my opponent more than in a normal match gets my blood pumping faster, gets the adrenaline flowing, it gets me excited. What I did to you on Wednesday night was just a taster of what is going to happen to you this week. That little beating was just a warm up. This Wednesday night you’re going to be locked inside two steel cages with me. And the only way for you to win, will be to escape, through winning you will survive. But you will not beat me, if you do somehow miraculously win the match you will have survived, not won. But how long you survive will be another matter. The way I treated Dreamer in our cell match will be nothing compared to what I do to you Troy. Dreamer and I, that was merely business, that was about getting him out of my way and Unlimited’s way and making sure he would never bother us again. You are pleasure Troy, you’re a personal matter. Whilst I’m sure Danny will be happy if I get rid of you, I’m doing it for myself. I’m going to destroy you just to please myself. This has nothing to do with my Continental Title, I want to ruin your life, I want to be able to be the person that can say I crippled you and ended your pathetic career. Because I am not just going into this match to try and hurt you, I’m going into it to try and physically cripple you for the rest of your life. Hell, if I thought I could get away with it I’d even try and kill you. You see I warned you before not to piss me off, not to try and gain my attention. And I hope you remember that you started all this Troy. You started this all over some spilt beer. Well when you’re standing across from me in that ring on Wednesday night and you’re wondering what you’ve let yourself in for, just remember if you hadn’t have started this, you wouldn’t be in the situation that you are now. This week will be your ender troy, I hope you’re counting down the hours, because that’s all you have left…
