Time Is Always Changing

15 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-15 Kramer’s problems with Troy Chryst were growing with every day. It’s as if they had nothing to think about besides the two of them, waiting until the next time one could get the advantage and attack. What all started out as an initiation for Chryst has inflated to a ball of fury. When they aren’t costing each other matches they are busy talking trash….what every fan wants to see. The tension is so tight between the two in fact that when it comes time for them to finally meet toe to toe in the ring in the handcuff match, things are definitely going to explode. The scene opens up outside of a small comic book store which is jammed packed with people trying to get the latest issue of Cricket –O- Doom where the magical Mr. Cricket takes on Dr. Grasshopper in hopes of stopping him from unleashing a deadly virus to the world. From around the corner comes a well groomed Kramer Wild Wing who has his short hair styled with gel, wearing a black shirt and somewhat baggy black pants giving him an overall solemn look. He walks past the crowd unnoticing of the frenzy and takes a few more steps until he stops. “What’s going on here?” He asks himself somewhat interested. He walks to the nearest person which happens to be a thin greasy little man who’s hand is wrapped around a flyer. Kramer taps him lightly on the shoulder which causes him to jump having been startled. “What?” The man asks irritably as he tries to push into the crowd. “No need to be so snappy. I was just wondering what was going on here?” Kramer replies wishing he would have picked someone else to ask. “It’s only one of the biggest days ever. Number 152 of Cricket-O-Doom is out! Everyone wants it and there are only a few copies…now if you’ll excuse me.” Spotting an opening the man darts through and nearly gets his head taken off with a swing of an elbow. Kramer watches as the frenzy carries on and then looks around at the people walking past, laughing themselves, wondering why people would fight over something so juvenile as a comic book. “God I remember these days…..” He whispers to himself remembering the idea of never growing up and finding refuge and fun in seemingly childish things like comic books and games. It was those times that things didn’t really get to him. You could throw a rock…no a whole barrel of rocks at him and he would shake it off and keep his smile. But now those days seem long gone as a happy day is a rarity anymore. And things do get to him, even people like Troy Chryst who he doesn’t feel deserves it, yet he gives in to the temptation and continues after. “It’s all in the past I suppose…” Saying that he takes one last look at the frenzy and walks away down the street, hands shoved deep in his pockets, a face full of untapped mysteries and feelings.
