Revelations...

18 July 2003

Date: 2003-07-18 My eyes are closed. God, it hurts to open them. It hurts to breathe. Everything hurts. “Daddy?” Slowly, my lids begin to rise. The light is like a red-hot steel spike punching through my head. I’m lying in the middle of a wrestling ring, my daughter Sarah kneeling beside me. Her little fingers are drawing shapes on my chest. I try lifting my head, but I feel like I’m stuck to the mat, held there by a giant invisible hand. I try to speak, but my throat feels crusty and nothing comes out. Must be from the blood running from my nose, into my mouth. Sarah smiles and stands up, looking quite pleased with herself. The tips of her fingers are red from the paint. I notice that her knees and shins are red too. That’s when it hits me: it’s blood, not paint. My blood. I look to the side. The crowd is screaming, making all sorts of noise. I just can’t seem to hear them. The referee is holding the second rope up for the paramedics. They look worried as they crawl into the ring. I’d laugh, if I could breathe. Don’t they know who I am? I’m Damon O'Halloran, an 'Act of God'. Nothing can stop me. A little blood’s no big deal. I guess they’re moving me, because everything starts passing me. Finally able to move on my own, I sit up to tell the paramedics to put me the Hell down. I’ve got places to go, asses to kick. Only there are no paramedics around. I’m floating. That’s not a good sign. I look down, and see why everybody looked so panicked. A three hundred and fifty-pound man, lying in a pool of blood, his hands twitching occasionally. The whites of his eyes are red, and blood seems to be pumping from his nose and ears. Of course, the man is me. I see what Sarah was writing on my chest: “I luv u” The old woman’s prediction was right. I just died in the ring.
