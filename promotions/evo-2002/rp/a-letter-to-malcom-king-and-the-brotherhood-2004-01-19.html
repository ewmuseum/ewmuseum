A letter to Malcom King and The Brotherhood

19 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-01-19 To: Malcom King and The Brotherhood of X….. As Freddy Kruger once put it, ‘why won’t you just die!’ For again, here they are coming back for another Syndicate beating. You would have thought they learned their lesson by now, but brains and intelligence was never your strong suit was it guys. I mean you got the Dean Malenko rip off in 1001 Holds, when in all reality all he knows is one, getting pinned for the 1…2…3. And Dolo Bentswinger, who I hear is quite the stud on the gay dance scene. Bending over and swinging it in for the 1…2…3. God its so easy picking on those two. You really thought Malcom King would know better than getting those two rejects. It kind of makes me personally question if King is as smart as I gave him credit for. Perhaps its all those beatings he took at the hands of us, all the stress we put on him, destroying his empire and his precious Unlimited faction. Perhaps he still rages about be costed the Universal Championship or that he cannot defeat The Syndicate or any member thereof it. Perhaps, just perhaps, its just age….and the sudden realization that he is past his prime and all the notable success is behind him. That he will press on like Terry Funk, Hulk Hogan, and Ric Flair, knowing his role in this industry is a diminished one. What real choices does he have, feuding with the Syndicate is certainly a box office draw, especially stemming from the years of feuding across three different federations and all across the globe. Otherwise, its managing, something to relive the spark of yesteryear. As we think upon the King/Syndicate era, there certainly have been many monumental moments. HBC at one time was viciously stalked in which King was behind, Dreamer wins the Universal Championship at his expense, Unlimited destroyed from within thanks to HBC. The Brotherhood destroyed several times, his protégé Luther Wisdom turns his back on King thanks to The Syndicate’s influence. Every goal and aspiration, gone in a puff of smoke thanks to HBC and BDD. His family life has suffered tremendously. He has alienated his wife and daughter, who are trying for the past year to move on without him and make ends meet. His precious wife, once his biggest cheerleader, now relegated to working double shifts at work. And what should be his most proudest moment, his daughter…..hating him with a vengence because daddy is never around. That she must endure the constant torment of her peers in school because daddy airs his dirty laundry on television for the world to see. The world views her daddy as a murder for what happened back in EDW and Xian in particular, a freak because of the tormented twisted mind he spouts every week, and other times, a loser. Hard things for a precious child to endure. No wonder she is struggling in school. No wonder your wife is very lonely, bitter, and tired. But that doesn’t matter does it King, you stopped caring for them a long time ago. Frankly, they are just a footnote to you aren’t they? Instead you surround yourself with talentless acquaintances looking to stab you in the back because they think it will further their own career. Malcom King, you should have gotten down on your hands and knees thanking me for ending your career when I did. I gave you a second chance at life. I gave you a second chance with your family, your wife, your daughter, and yet, you threw it all away again, and for what? Nothing really. That is truly the sad part. Through all of this, will have accomplished nothing and given up everything that was and is important to you. When I ended your career in the career versus career match, I did look out for your best interest, I allowed you that chance to go out with your head held high, proud of the things you’ve accomplished, and you just threw it all away coming back here again. You’ve tainted all you have done and frankly given us little choice to truly, and finally take you out of the game not by ending your career, because hell, you’ll be back again shortly, no…. apparently, we are going to have to make this a permanent thing, a permanent physical disability that will prevent you from ever setting foot inside a ring again and confining you to an home-based intensive care unit. I know you personally have a bad neck King. I remember when I damn near broke it in two. I know your knees are shot and that they need replacing, your lower back screams everytime you get up out of bed everyday and yes, I even know about that bad right hip you injured in a car crash that was hushed up by the press and management. Truth is, you’re a walking cripple. But I’m telling you now King, this is your only chance to fade away and keep your dignity and pride intact. Because, were done being nice to you. This, this will preserve the legacy of the King/Syndicate days, should you press on, should you personally want to interfere in our business, then your past, your glories, your memories will I promise you, become nothing, and we will not only take you out of Evolution, but permanently send you into a wheelchair the rest of your natural life. You will be that old guy in the nursing home who everyone think’s is crazy, demented with mental disorders and wearing depends because you are unable to take care of yourself. What a sad ending to Malcom King. Someone who helped shaped this industry. It would be truly tragic, and completely avoidable, but that King is up to you. You know where the Syndicate stand. Face off and your finished. Fade away, and you preserved your pride that one day your daughter will understand and be an awe of what you’ve accomplished. The choice is yours. Dolo and 1001 holds, this week, you face us again because of our good graces and due to the fact you acted like a bitch and jumped us from behind. Its pretty pathetic that is the only way you can actually get the advantage on us, but hey, whatever. It just makes us want to punish you even more. Truth is, you were never that good of a team, I remember when we bought and sold you like some cheap whore on the street. However, we are getting rather tired of you. We feel you are keeping some good potential teams from getting a spot in Evolution so we must get rid of the dead weight around here. This is Evolution after all. You haven’t Evolved at all. Same predictable team you always were. So yes, we will have our little contest. You’ll get in a few good shots, but ultimately at the end, you will fall like so many times previously into Syndication. Down on the ladder you will go, and thank god, because as I said, were tired of facing scrubs like you, who don’t even deserve to be in the ring with greatness that we are. Sincerely, The Syndicate
