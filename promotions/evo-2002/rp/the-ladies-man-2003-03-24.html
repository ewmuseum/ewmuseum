The Ladies Man

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/03 [The scene opens up in a locker room backstage at a house show eVo is putting on tonight. Stuffing the last of his personal items in his gym bag, Xander starts talking, before he looks up to the camera.] 'So, now my female problems grow by two.' Xander starts, almost seemingly talking to himself. 'First I have to deal with Frost's cumsponge Seara, and now I still have to deal with her. I swear to God I wish I could get her off of me already! Honey, I know Frost ain't much in the, let's say, ramrod department, but seriously, this is bordering on sad now. You know you ain't gonna get none of this, so you really need to move along.' 'As far as Fiona though, I'm sorry it had to come to this baby. The match wasn't my idea, and neither was the attack last week. I've been having some troubles getting through Asis' thick skull lately, so I whole-heartily apologize for that.' 'I sincerely meant that I was gonna keep an eye on you, cause you are promising. Maybe Asis is feeling threatened or something, I dunno, the boys got issues. Anyway, let's say this match be more of a one on one, between you and Seara. I'll stay out of it, I mean, there's nothing for me to gain in this match. I've beaten Seara down so many times now, I've lost count. This is your chance to prove you got game. I know I do, Sear thinks she does, but all she wants is to go one-on-one with a real man, like me.' 'Think of it seriously, and I'm really not trying to threaten you, but just ask that Xander-groupie herself what exactly happens when you get on my bad side. Remember, I have an army at my disposal, and, the man in charge around here, whether Davidson would ever let anyone believe it or not. I am not a man to be messed with... ask anyone.' [The scene fades to black]
