The Thing That Should Not Be

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/11/2002 Asis has not said much as of late, but that certainly does not mean his actions have been quiet. Despite his recent oral silence Asis has proven that actions do indeed speak louder than words. The Faith pay per view was an event that in most people’s eyes cemented Asis’ stature as one of the main stars of eVolution. He beat Seara in a table match to finally end their personal feud but even after winning, still attacked her and caused her another trip to the hospital. However the damage done to Seara could have been much worse, but Asis seemed to stop himself from delivering a fatal blow with a sledgehammer. Different people have suggested different reasons for this, but Asis has not yet told anyone his personal reason. Also at Faith Asis done something that surprised everyone, something most thought he would never do. He joined a stable, but Asis didn’t join just any group of people. He became part of one of the most dominant forces in wrestling history, Asis joined The Unlimited. And he did so at the expense of Scott Frost, whom he took apart after his match against The Unlimited’s leader Xander, at the request of Xander. But whilst joining The Unlimited has gained Asis some “allies”, it has given him even more enemies as two more stables have emerged within eVo, both of which seem to want to eliminate The Unlimited and take over from them. And while The Omen Brotherhood and The Notorious scored minor victories over The Unlimited at this week’s Legends, it was ultimately The Unlimited who came out of the night on top. They left The Omen Brotherhood lying in pools of their own blood and once again Asis targeted the leader of The Notorious, Scott Frost, leaving him with more injuries after throwing him through a window… (As the scene comes into focus Xander, HeartBreak Chick and Jinx can all be seen spread out in a very large and luxurious hotel room. It’s unusual to see them all together outside of eVolution time and you may not expect them to all be in such luxury, but there they are. There may be a reason for them all being together, as Xander is sitting in an armchair, his eyes vacant as he looks deep in though. Jinx is sitting on a chair with something in his hands that could be a knife but it cannot be seen clearly, but he is playing with it, turning it over and over in his hands. HBC is lying provocatively on a couch, reading the latest copy of Playboy, wearing just a low cut vest and a pair of hot pants. But even though most of The Unlimited is here, the camera is here to see only one man and apparently he is not in the room. But out the corner of the camera shot, a man can be seen sitting in the corner of the room. A corner of the room that seems a lot darker compared to everywhere else in the room. The camera moves over to see Asis sitting in a corner of the luxurious room that is to his liking, that is everything has been trashed and it seems a lot darker for some reason. Whatever rage made Asis destroy everything has gone now and he sits by himself quite calmly. The camera does not infuriate him either, in fact he barely acknowledges it, he makes no movement, but he knows it is there…) I often used to think how appropriate the phrase, “It’s the nature of man-kind to fear that which they do not understand” was. But now I think that even though that’s true, it is equally as true that man-kind will always question what they cannot comprehend. Because nobody can understand or comprehend what I am and I have always been used to being feared, but now people are questioning things about me. So many questions that people might expect me to answer, but it is up to me whether I do or not. And maybe I don’t feel like answering them. But then maybe I do. You see I sit here and look at the three other people in this room and I know I have no link with these people. We have nothing in common, none of us do. From this people are already probably thinking up more questions, mainly along the lines of why I have chosen to join their group. The truth of the matter is not even I know fully. I don’t know why I decided to join The Unlimited, I don’t know why I feel drawn towards Xander, but I don’t care either. I haven’t joined Unlimited to have people to take care of me, to watch my back and make me feel secure. I can take care of myself. And like I said, I don’t have anything in common with these people and do not feel as if I have to protect them for any reason. But I do feel drawn to Xander and I do what he says, not because I have to, but because I want to. I’m not Xander’s lapdog; I suppose in one way or another, being part of Unlimited gives me direction in my life, gives me focus to my rage. Xander points, I destroy, it gives me specific targets to turn my attention to, and I like the people that Xander chooses. (For the first time Asis looks up at the camera and he has some sort of smile on his face, although what kind of smile it is could not be understood. Only he knows what kind of smile it is, what thoughts are provoking that kind of grin. And Asis’ mind is not somewhere you want to be.) Seems like Unlimited brings me fresh prey anyway, I don’t think that people like us very much. I mean, The Omen Brotherhood has already tried to take me out once: didn’t work. Scott Frost, The Notorious’ leader has also tired to stop me from interfering in his plans and look where that got him. Thrown through a window. The Omen Brotherhood tried to hurt a fellow Unlimited member and they were left lying in their own blood. They may think that we’re old hat and that they are new and superior, but so far they have proven nothing. Just that despite what they think they know about Unlimited and me is completely wrong. They will keep coming at us and I will keep defending us and they will loose the struggle. As for Seara and why I didn’t kill her when I easily could have? At first I didn’t understand why I didn’t do it. Why I couldn’t do it. And then I realised that she was right all along. I do need her around; I have to keep her alive. That’s why I defended her from Xavier Citizen in the first place. But she was not completely right. Although I need to keep her around it’s not for any reason she may think of. But I’ll let her worry about that one. See many people have faced the thing that should not be and those that haven’t soon will. Because there is a war coming. And I think all those not with Unlimited need to ask themselves something. Are you sure you’re on the right side?
