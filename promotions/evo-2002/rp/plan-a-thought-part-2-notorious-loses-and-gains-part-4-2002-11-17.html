Plan a thought...part 2: Notorious loses and gains; part 4

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 17, 2002 'You know your right.' Seara smiles and says to Rhys. The scene opens up where the last Timeline of Seara left off, with Frost starring at Seara and Rhys. Frost with a look of concern towards Seara as she continues to talk, runs his hand through his cornrolled hair and continues to listen in on Seara and Rhys. 'Your right. I have opened up to something.' Seara says and smiles. Rhys looks onwards towards Seara and smiles. 'Katherine? Are you okay?' Frost asks Seara uncertain about what she is talking about. Rhys smiles. 'He calls you by your actual name too. You've caught yourself a good one Katherine, don't do anything stupid. Or training session is going to be a bitch.' Rhys says smiling and glancing over towards Frost who is sharing a silent moment with Seara as they stare at each other. 'Katherine.....' Rhys says. 'Yoohoo! You there?' Rhys again tries to get Seara's attention. Rhys waves his hand inbetween Frost and Seara, and Seara snaps out of her daze. '..uuh....Yeah. Your right Rhys. Your right.' Seara smiles at Frost. 'Umm...Rhys. You think me and Katherine can get some time alone?' Frost says to Rhys. Rhys smiles and nods before turning around and walking away. 'Don't do anything I wouldn't do!' mocks Rhys as he leaves. Seara immediately turns red. 'What is he talking about?' Seara says trying to change the subject quickly. Frost smiles. 'Wha...' Seara tries getting out but is interrupted by Frost. Frost presses his index finger on the lips of Seara 'Shhh....' Frost assures Seara. 'I want you to know something.' Frost says to Seara. Seara looks at him now confused. 'I want you to know, You aren't the reason Kelsey left me. I promise you. It is me. She is going to be fine, and so am I. But the reason I am to blame is because.....' Frost tries to finish but it quickly interrupted by Rhys. Rhys comes running in, and grabs his jacket. 'Brr....it's cold out there. Sorry' Rhys says quickly fleeing the scene. Frost looks at the door close and then walks over to lock it, as Seara is still looking at him confused. Frost walks back to her, and grabs her hand and she smiles again. 'Katherine. When I first met you, I wanted to protect you. You are a friend like no other. I say that because.....when....well. When Kelsey left me, I realised that I luh....' Frost tries saying and is quickly interrupted by Seara. Seara reaches around his neck and pulls him into her as she kisses him and then they both start kissing very passionately. After about five minutes, they stop kissing and Seara realises what just happened and she looks shocked but happy. Frost looks to be in a whole new world as he smiles. 'I'm sorry. I....I had to. Frost....I don't know what I feel for you, but I know that it confuses me. I want you, I need you, but yet I don't know if I should do this.' Seara says. 'How is this going to effect us, The Notorious? I don't know anymore, but I know I liked that.' Seara continues. Frost looks at her and grabs her hands and brings her in close to him and he puts her hands on her chest. 'Seara. L, he knows. Jameson. He will. But this. This can be our secret. Until we know how to cope with it.' Frost assures Seara. Seara looks at him and smiles. 'Scott. I've never felt this before. I don't want to be your rebound from your ex-wife.' Seara announces. Frost looks at her and slightly shakes his head. 'No. I was okay with her wanting that divorce, because I knew who I wanted. I know it sounds wrong, but I wanted you way before she asked for that divorce. I just didn't want to scare you away.' Frost tells Seara. Seara looks at him with concern. 'Asis is going to hate this.....' Seara says obviously joking about it now. Frost smiles and kisses her forehead. 'Screw Asis! He is getting his ass handed to him Sunday anyway. 'But what about Jameson?' Seara questions. 'You do know that there is a chance that he is going to face you sunday too. Please don't ruin this. We, The Notorious. We need Jameson.' Seara continues. Frost looks at her. 'Don't worry about it. If it comes down to me and Jameson. We will both give it our all. The best man will win that match, and either way it's a win for Notorious. Don't you worry about that. The two people that I am worried about are Asis and Shocka.' Frost says to Seara. 'Shocka? Why him?' asks Seara. 'Because he has Thug Roc behind him. I mean, Roc is our president now. Roc will definately try and screw everyone that stands in Shockas way so someone in New-Tv will actually win something as prestigious as the Road To Glory Tournament.' Frost answers Seara's question. Seara looks at Frost, and Frost looks at Seara. 'I won't let New-TV mess up any chance Notorious has to win that tournament. Especially if you make it to the finals.' Seara assures Frost. Frost smiles and the scene fades to black as they share one last kiss. (To Be Continued.....)
