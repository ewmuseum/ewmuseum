The Situation May Have Changed, But The Goal Remains The Same....

23 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/10/02 Sat down waiting patiently is the figure of James Collins, one of Evo's newest recruits to their existing reporting team. Fidgeting as he sits he looks quite a nervous character. His youth is obvious, the curtains and freckles are real give away but nevertheless it seems Evo saw fit to send him to talk to Sean Mason. Sean arrival is a dramtic one as you might expect. As the door swings wide open Sean can be seen emerging dressed in a white t-shirt and blue jeans. Dangling from his neck hangs a silver chain holding a silver cross, and after the recent words of Thug Roc lets just hope hes in aa much of a forgiving mood as the cross would suggest. 'Sean' says getting to his feet to greet the Evo star, 'good journey?' 'Journey?' Sean said raising an eyebrow, 'is that what they taught you to ask at interview school? I mean com'on kid lets cut to the chase if you wanna talk to someone about journeys I suggest you go pick yourself a bus driver from the street!' 'Im sorry' James said trying his best to apoligise for what Sean made out to be a serious mistake. Taking a deep breath of air Sean looks back at James with a pity filled expression, 'first day on the job?' 'It that obvious?' James replied. 'Well...' Sean said before grining broadly, 'how about we start over? I mean I havn't got time to mess about simply because I hope you can understand I got alot on my mind! I mean its not everyday your stripped of any opportunity you've worked ya ass off for over the past few months is it?' 'Impact title shot you mean?' 'Well done kid, least you've done your research' Sean replied. 'Its a bitter pill to swallow thats for sure, I mean the thought of Legends still puts a shiver down my spine. Sure I've won titles and lots of them, but just because of these last few months the impact title began to become more of an obsession than a tiny scalp on the road to greatness. When I came to Evo it was all about the big titles for me. After my success in FAW who can blame me for aspiring to carry all the gold and thinking about just that. In some ways the Impact title was more of a stepping stone for me at the start. The board asked me to step into the breach and I was only to happy to oblige. I mean it was a challenge. For me to step out of my submission element and into a much more high paced kind of division was an intriguing challenge. It was a chance to prove my worth to Evo right from the start' Sean said taking breath. 'Though its amazing, when things start getting personal suddenly the ante becomes all that much greater also. Legends was about sending a message and when I held that title in the sky it felt like I was reaching that finishing line. Put it this way I saw the end of a very long and bumpy journey. The culmination of it all in one historic moment which was set for Faith. With that in mind, just imagine how I felt when I got the call from my agent saying the title was out of the picture. If you can't imagine exactly that, then try picturing how it might feel to have your whole floor taken from under you. But lets not dwell. I mean dwelling upon the moment got nobody no-where fast. Now I have just gotta turn my attention to the new problem. Faith pits me against three men who have alot to prove and have been given the stage to show themselves in their full glory. For Jay Stylez its his PPV debut and like the others I expect him to come out all guns blazing. I just can't see it going down any other way!' 'So your prepared? James enquired. 'I mean it won't be a case of you under-estimating the challenge that faces you?' 'And where would that get me?' Sean snapped back with a raised eyebrow. 'No-where..' 'Exactly' Sean said interupting again. 'Right now im picturing Thug Roc sitting at home with a huge smug look on his face. You think I enjoy picturing that? Picturing his smirk as he lingers on the thought of getting one over on me. The answer to that is a plain and simple no. Just imagine how that smirk would grow if I were to lose a match I would be expected to win. I need to respond in a manner of a champion. Its all about getting back on the bike as soon as possible and getting on with business. No crap, just plain and simple I intend on walking in with my head held high and my foot up my opponents ass within seconds of the bell sounding. What doesn't kill us makes us stronger and if these last few months have taught me nothing but one thing, its that theres alot of room for improvement in terms of my current performance! Its time I got back to what got me to the dance in the first place!' 'One thing thats been very noticeable is your stepping away from your submission roots of old since joining Evo' James paused, 'should we expect you to venture back in that direction?' 'Just that' Sean replied leaning toward James with a very serious expression for the cameras to see. 'Every week I go out into the arena to be met by a raptious applause, its about time I began paying back each and other echoed cheer from my fans. How do I do that you ask? Simple! By giving them what they want. Its time I began focusing my attention towards those who have earnt it'. 'Anyone in particular?' James asked listening with his pen at the ready. Looking back at James, Sean then ponders his next words before speaking. 'Lets just say its a long list and time will be the only thing I need to use to voice my grievances...' With that the discussion continues off camera as the scene fades to black leaving the viewer to wait for Faith.
