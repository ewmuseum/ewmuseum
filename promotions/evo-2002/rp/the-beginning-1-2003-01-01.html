The Beginning - 1

1 January 2003

Date: Post-PPV 'I'd sell My soul My self-esteem a dollar at a time One chance One kiss One taste of you my magdalena' Magdalena.A Perfect Circle As Shodan roams down the halls, only janitors can be seen. The time is 1:30 AM, and most have cleared out. The road crews with sound and stage are still present, but most have left. Reitired to their hotel rooms, or already on flights home. He was not one of those people. In fact, he hadn't been seen before, or after his match. He was just now becoming apparent. It was clear he was headed to his locker room. Hanging from his right hand was the title he had fought so hard to keep. As he walked by people, he'd give a weak nod, and crack a pleasant smile. But underneath that, there was just a black-tooth frown. The politeness and empathy were just a facade until he got away from them again. Finally going past several doors, whose name plates had been removed by now, he found his. As he placed his hand on the knob, he actually stared at the logo. For the first time, he actually stared deep into it. He ran his hand across it, taking in the texture of it. He had thought it up when he was first starting out, pulling side-jobs in indy feds till he got a break. A large, bold S made up the base. On top of that, was an outstretched, dark blue one. Small electric bolts shaded red decorated the sides. It'd been so long since he actually looked at the image. His own creation, his own design. He let go a sigh, followed with a smirk. He turned the handle and stepped inside. As he turned around, he realized he was now looking at himself. The full-length mirror hung against the wall reflected himself. His title caused a shining glare across his chest. He tilted his hand a little, and it moved up, till it covered his face. The body in the glass appeared broken, and bruised. As he lowered his belt, his eyes did too show this feeling. Eyes that almost seemed to sink back into his head. Empathy, with a hint of emptiness. He gave another weak smile, as he turned around. And for the first time, he saw her. Sitting down, eyes carefully following and noting his every motion. However, his motion had stopped now. And all he could so was stare at her with wide eyes, and an open mouth. 'Hello, my dear Jeremy.'
