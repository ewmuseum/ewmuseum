A Welcome Return

20 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-20 What would you say makes a 'Legend?' Would it be money earned by your job? Would being a legend mean that you have the fame and fortune that is part of the 'American Dream'. The ability to go out and buy anything your heart desired? Owning several homes all across the world, summers in New York, Winters in California, vacations in England, Carribean, Japan? Success means legend? Some people, yeah they'd agree with that statement. I however will be the first to slam his fist onto the table and say no. I'm a legend. Not because I have millions of dollars, fast cars, big houses. Not because my children have trust funds. No, not because I fly 1st class everywhere I go. You see, if I was taught anything by my father when I was growing up, following him around from highschool gym to bingo halls back to the school gyms is that to be a real legend you have to make the sacrifices that so few are willing to make. Yeah, look at all the people in the world who've made money off whatever the hell it is they do. Look at professional sports, look at sports entertainment. Hell, Marc Mero, although he's a good guy, I got no problem with him, he's made some good coin in his day, but is he a legend? No, he won't be in Legends of Wrestling 3. But what about people like Terry Funk, he's a legend, is he rich? No, but he's well off I'd say. I know that I have more money then Terry only because I wasn't as crazy as that son of a bitch. Still, I have more respect for a terry funk then I do for a Marc Mero any day of the week. Why? Because he sacrificed so much for a legacy, for his legendary status in this business. You say, 'Hey, you ever seen Terry Funk?' and any wrestling fan worth anything will say hell yeah. Am I a legend? Yeah, I am. No, I don't mean to gloat, but I'm not here to tell you this for the sake of saying I'm better then you. I'm here because you need to know why. You need to know why next week Xander why you're little dreams of facing Tyler Davidson at A New Dawn will be shattered and you'll be the one going into a cage with 20 other men fighting for the chance to face me. The camera cuts out from it's blured image before you. You couldn't make a damn thing out until now as it pans out. Standing leaning up against a wall is James Kimson. The man who made a return at Legends that, well Legends are made of. Successful Elbow Surgery behind him he'll be going up against Xander for the Universal Title in the coming week with Tyler Davidson as the special official. That's all well and good, but if Xander hadn't barged in on Kimson's home coming perhaps we wouldn't be in this situation. Maybe Kimson wouldn't be talking about Xander. Maybe he'd be talking about that Caged Rumble, something he seemed so excited about at the start of his return on Legends, not something that seems so important if all he needs to do is beat one man next week, not 20 to become the Universal Champion. All be it that Xander is one hell of an opponent, crafty, resoursfull and skilled. Kimson moves away from the wall and steps forward, he parts his lips to speak. Now before we get to far ahead of ourselves Xander, this isn't me sitting here saying you aren't worth anything. Yeah, I'm going to say that you are a Legend yourself. I've said it before, and I just said it again. You should be flattered because I don't exactly have a history of saying nice things about people who generally have a good way of pissing me off. See, Xander my problem with you is that you have no idea what you are up against at any given time. You focus only on what goes right for you, when something goes wrong you have a selective memory. I can strongly recall your involvement in this power struggle situation being a flop and I had a strong hand in it. Of course, 2 weeks away is a long time in wrestling but I was not exactly surprised to see you on Legends busting in on my little home coming. Of course I sure did enjoy kicking your ass at the same time. But Xander, you probably forget our 1st one on one encounter a few months back where thank God for you're unlimited friends you managed to escape with a DQ, or a no contest or something I can't remember. All I remember is me beating the shit out of you all match long. You see, Xander you are great. You might rank in the top percentile of all time great wrestlers. You surely are an ICON in the Hardcore style. But if you think that has anything to do with our match this coming week you need to wake the hell up and see something that's very clear. What's clear is that you and your reign as Champion are through. Wrestling Tyler is fine, you've done it before you've beaten him and I'm sure that he wouldn't apreciate me saying this but he's not an active wrestler here in eVo. What, he's had like 3 singles matches all year? Maybe a few more I dunno. But Davidson, for all he's accomplished in his career isn't James Kimson, and you Xander aren't in my league either. Yeah, I respect you and that is a weapon that I have. I know that you Xander don't have any regard for anyone but yourself and that seems to be what gets you through the days when you look at the mirror and look at the scars along your body, wearing them like badges of some secret society. Kimson takes a few more steps to the side and turns looking away from the camera before taking a breath and speaking while turning back to face it. You think I haven't bleed Xander? Yeah I've bleed. All over this planet, from North to South, every major wrestling Market I've been there bleeding all over the mats. Japan, yeah, Africa, yes. I've worked my ass off in the UK to be an ICON there. Count the 8 world titles, count all the other shit I've done and ask me if I got handed a damn thing. My first match I broke my neck but I came back and wrestled for 11 years, blood, sweet and tears. While trying to raise a family and be a husband to my wife. I've done enough in 1 year in this business to be a legend by any standard but I've done it for 11. So you think you and your little reign as champion is an obstical for me? You think you intimidate me with your gang of thugs called Unlimited? You think your mind games with Davidson means a God damn thing to me? Only so often does a man get to perform in front of his friends and family. I got that chance at Legends and you came down and had the nerve to cut me off. Why? Because you have a belt saying Universal Champion on it? Just because you are the champion doesn't give you everything Xander. And winning it from Scott Frost might have been a huge accomplishment but you know what Xander, Frost isn't here anymore and if you're looking for the biggest dog in the yard to pick a fight with, well you found one. And I love to fight, I have big teeth and when I bite Xander I sure as hell don't let go. And I know already Xander you're plotting, working on some sort of scheme to get out of that match alive and with your title. Yeah, and you guessed it I've seen them all. Some of them are fool proof, but this time it won't be so simple. You have Tyler wearing the stripes and i'm sure he won't put up with your BS this time. I know you are a coward Xander, I know that deep down you are afraid of me. You should be. Because I'm a LEGEND Xander. They call me the Greatest of all Time. They call me The Hype, maybe you never needed nicknames Xander. Maybe you've got millions of them. I'd like to hear them some day. Maybe when we're old and retired you can tell me all your stories when all the hatchets have been burried and peace and quite is all I want. But right now, in this moment Xander I have 1 more sacrifice I want to make. That's doing whatever I can to beat you this coming week on Legends. I am not saying it'll be easy. I know you are dangerous. I know that you've got every trick in the book under your thumb. But Xander, you have to recognize the fact that you and your title run are in grave danger this week and that I'll do what it takes from my end to become Champion. Kimson stops and walks a few paces towards the camera. He puts 1 hand in his pocket, the other one hanging freely at his side as he continues. One thing you learn, and Xander I am quite sure you know this lesson is that sometimes nothing goes as planned. I'm quite aware that I could walk out of the arena next week without the title. I know what it's like, and it's quite disheartening to get a title shot and screw it up. There are always other times, other changes. Other roads one could take to the top. I've traveled most of them. But like I was saying in Buffalo before I was cut off by you Xander was that I'm more then willing to take the high road. The rough, bumpy gravel road. No comfort, only pain. That road is the Caged Rumble match. 20 of the best wrestlers in the world today, housed inside a cell surrounding the ring. The only way to win is to be the last man standing. The rules are simple, to eliminate a wrestler he must be ejected from the cell itself, not just the ring inside. The possibilities are brutal. Throw them threw the door, maybe threw the wall itself! It's not comforting to know the only way out. So that means that like 19 other men inside that structure I'll be damn carefull about where I walk. Managers, owners have always told me 'James, you're ring sense is remarkable' maybe that's true. I've always made sure I knew where I was inside the ring. But what about a match when you're never safe. When danger lurks in every face. 19 other men, hell bent on becoming the Number One contender to the most prized title in our company. 19 other men looking for the chance to break out and prove their greatness. 19 men, who want to be 'the man!' Man, I don't like those odds. A slew of people who all want exactly what I want, all for similar reasons. All thrown into 1 barbaric structure and told to destroy each other for the chance. I'm not painting a pretty picture here ladies and Gentlemen. I know how it sounds. But to be perfectly honest it doesn't change a damn thing. If I fail to beat Xander for whatever reason, I'll still walk into that match. I'll walk in with my head held high and I'll go in with only 1 intention. To survive. To make it through to the last man. To eliminate that man and win the match. I'm not after the old 'i'm going to eliminate 19 other men to win.' Lord knows what number I'll draw. And if I happen to draw number one or number twenthy-nine it won't matter. The only thing that matters is that I win the match. The only thing that matters is that I become Universal Champion, weither it be next week, or in 6 weeks time. The bottom line is that you will see Universal Gold over my shoulder, that's not a garuntee, it's not a promise. It's a fact! Kimson turns to exit the scene The Hype.... has Spoken! Kimson exits, the camera fades to black.
