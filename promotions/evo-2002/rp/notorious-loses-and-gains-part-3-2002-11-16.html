Notorious loses and gains; part 3

16 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 16, 2002 'There will never be another like me.' Frost thinks to himself. The scene fades in and Frost is looking into a mirror. He rubs his left hand over his face and his index finger and thumb end at the chin of his beard. He looks back into the mirror and slowly shakes his head. 'So much has happened to me in just a matter of days. Now, in just a few more days. I have a chance to win the biggest tournament in the history of eVolution Wrestling. One day I am this champion of an amatuer federation, and next I come out of a show in the Pepsi Center found in Denver, Colorado and I faced the Universal champion. THE UNIVERSAL CHAMPION! And now, I am apart of my third major pay per view. Rise of the Legends. Wow.' Frost says to himself. Frost looks around. 'But then there is the fact that me and Kelsey are now divorced. She thinks it's Katherines fault. But she is my friend, to me. Friends come first. Yeah, I feel something for her. But question is. Does she feel for me? Katherine. I'm winning this tournament for you, you are my queen of wrestling.' Frost again says talking to himself. 'Now we have L2J. He is one half the Universal champion, and dear friend to me. He is the reason I am in this business. We are like brothers. But there is something standing in our way. That Universal championship. I am the number one contender, and now if I win this tournament I'm gunning for the title that he shares with a mad man. It is nothing personal to L2J. Nothing at all. I mean, look at me. I came from an amatuer league, and now the Universal championship is only moments from my grasp. I will do whatever it takes to touch that title and protect it. I've already been ruthless, and I've already been recognized. Now the only thing left to do is freeze hell over and become the quickest rising universal champion. Malcolm King, Xander, Sovereign, Asis, not even L2J will stand in my way.' Frost finishes as he looks back into the mirror in a daze. Frost closes his eyes and looks down at the counter top of the mirror and reaches forward and turns a knob and the sudden sound of pouring water is apparent that he has turned a sink on. Frost reaches into the basin of the sink, and lifts some water and splashes it on his face. The wet beads of water rest in beard of Frost and he raises his head looking into the mirror once again and a few drops fall from his beard and lands in the water obtaining sink basin adding that dramatic dripping sound that some consider a form of torture. Frost breaths in heavily and then he reaches behind him and pulls his white t-shirt off revealing his slightly tanned skin and another white wife beater. 'So much pressure. So much anger. So much trust. My career. Everything. Everything is on the line. Everything. First it comes down to me and Asis. That is the son of a bitch that needs to be shut up. But lets face it, he is much bigger man than myself. By me challanging him, putting more anger into him. I have challanged my career at the same time. But that doesn't worry me. What Asis has done to Katherine in the past. It is not proper. He has pissed me off, and now he wants to take aggression out on me? Asis. You've fucked with the wrong person. I am six foot tall, two hundred and twenty six pounds of pure aggression. You have to take factors into this match. What will be going through my head, we already know that you are a mad man. But think about it. I just lost my wife. I felt that glimpse of greatness, last week when Notorious took out Unlimited and Sovereign. Asis. I loved that feeling. That feeling to be on top. Do you really think that I will allow you to be my maker and destroy that chance? No.' Frost concludes. (To be continued...)
