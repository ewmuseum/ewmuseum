Trying To Understand...

19 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/19/02 Most people don't understand why people do the things they, or act the way they do. And it's mostly because they don't know the full story, or history of that person to fully understand them. In very few cases, some people do know the full story but still can't understand them. With this case, the latter part doesn't qualify as most people have no clue as to what happened in the past with one Tommy Michaels. Something happened around August 17th which lead to the phone call that Michaels got just before the eight man tag match at Legends IV was about to take place. The shot changes to the backstage area where you can see Tommy Michaels yelling into the cell phone in his hand as Marcus Campbell, his agent, looks on. 'I don't care what they said,' Michaels shouted. 'I want to know the truth now. Why the hell is Nichole Kelley in eVolution!' He pauses for a moment listening to the answer of whoever is on the other side of the phone line. As Michaels listens to his answer the sound of another phone ringing can be heard and you see Marcus Campbell scramble for the phone in his pocket. 'Hello? What? Wait, wait he's right here. Hold on a minute.' Campbell puts the phone down to the side and starts trying to get the attention of Michaels who is yelling into his phone still. After a minute Campbell grabs Michaels cell phone and yanks it out of his hand as Michaels turns to look at him. 'What the hell are you doing?' Michaels asked. 'You have another call,' Campbell answered. 'It's an emergency.' 'What?' Michaels said puzzled. 'Where?' Campbell points to the second cell phone lying on the chair and Michaels picks it up. Before he can even say hello the voice of someone on the other end breaks through and Michaels goes wide eyed in shock before hanging the phone up and running down the hallway leaving Campbell behind as the scene fades to black. After receiving the phone call, Michaels rushed out of the Nassau Coliseum as fast as he could. For the next three weeks nobody had heard a word from or about Michaels. Nobody knew where he was or what he was doing. Then at Legends VI during a six man tag match, Michaels made his surprise return to eVolution. Although, he made his return in a way no one thought was possible...
