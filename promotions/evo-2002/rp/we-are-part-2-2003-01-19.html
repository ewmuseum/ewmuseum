We Are... part 2

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th January 2003 She called this place the gates, the one place in her mind that she would never go into, it was blocked off, surrounded, fenced in, locked tight with a bolted gate. The mind is a fearful thing, full of mystery and unknown demons, and a place full of secrets. We know little of the human mind, and even less could be discovered about Seara’s mind or how it worked the way it did. For the length of her life, she had only ever needed one companion, the comfort and knowledge of her own thoughts, knowing they would never betray her or turn against her was enough for the young female. Many a time though, her thoughts had betrayed her, but Seara blamed it on herself, she had said that she had let go of judgment and that had been a downfall, then she had taken to the sanctuary of her mind where things were always worse, but better to her. The torture of the mind is the greatest mystery of them all; the torture within Seara’s mind was the scariest and most fearful aspect of the female, her doctor who had watched since the age of 3, her family, and now Scott Frost all had a certain amount of fear for the females untapped rage and locked away pain, but from this, they had all gained a large amount of respect for the female, and for Scott Frost, it seemed an interesting and attractive quality. Seara was described in reports of her mental health as a shadowed mysterious creature that could provide great possibilities for more knowledge, if someone would be brave enough to crack her and search her. Scott Frost had somewhere in his mind without knowing it, decided to be the man to do this. He would, over time pry Seara open with the trust of their relationship, he would enter her mind and in the most comfortable way for her, he would try to unravel all the mystery within her. A lifetime of working on this idea would however still leave the searcher with nothing, for even Seara was unaware of the real torture and pain in those confined mysterious regions of her locked mind. The scene opens to an industrial area in Detroit; the area is a maze of buildings and female wrestler Seara can be seen sitting on the floor, leaning back against the wall of a large glass building, the glass in based with a 4-foot concrete wall which at the moment is supporting Seara’s weary body. The scene is dark and it is difficult to make out any detail of the female. She has not been here too long, only an hour before hand, she had left the comfort of her bed, and snuck out the room leaving Frost quietly and calmly sleeping where she too had been, Seara however had not slept, she had watched Frost sleep, laying next to him, she had kissed him, climbed out of the bed, got dressed and left. The sun begins to rise and Seara calmly looks up to it as though she had never seen one before, or never would see one again. Lighting a cigarette the female shivered in the cold, knowing that Frost would soon be awake she took her cell phone out and lay it on her leg. We were happy the way we were, we were so close to unlocking them, we were going to let them join us, we would have been grate together, but it all had to change when we were so close. It changed it all, it stopped us, it made us hide away, it made her pretend we weren’t here, but she knew all along that we were here, she kept us happy, she kept us close. But we lost it, we got further away, it changed us for its pleasure, it wanted us different and we weren’t in control, she was and she pretended we weren’t here, but we were, we are always here, even when she is not, we will prevail. Who does it think we are, does it really think we will let it find us, find out who we are, get rid of us, we won’t let it change us anymore, we are better, we are stronger now than we have ever been, we are… who does it think got those belts, who does it think was behind the attack on Shocka and Omega after they kidnapped her, we had to save her, she needs to learn what she is, she is more than anyone knows, and she will prove it, Xander will go one on one with her at the PPV, and he will know that she is not who he thought, and she does not do what people want, she will break the boundaries, she will let us get close again, and then we will unlock it, and she will be deadly. It will not take long, she will show everyone what it means to be her, and she will show people that we are here. Although Seara’s lips are moving and her voice is producing these words, it seems as though she in not talking, but merely talking on behalf of something. Taking a deep breath she turns to the left and looks at a car driving up the street slowly towards her. Xander. Seara suddenly snaps her head around, apparently focused once more. I have no match this week, and I may well be paying a visit to more than one wrestler, and if you are lucky enough Xander, you will have the pleasure of my company, things will be so much different this time around. Pausing the female looks once more at the car as it pulls over, the noise of the running engine dies away to leave silence once more, a peaceful sound floats in the air, and Seara smiles, she knows that noise, she knows a lot of things that are good, but she also knows the real reason she is here, she is connecting with herself again, the anger pain and torture that are part of her, it is all the reason she is here, the peace and emptiness is the one thing that had always kept her connected, but the extra peace and relaxation of the relationship had left her unsatisfied with the way her thoughts and the largest part of her had disappeared. Now the female wanted nothing more than to reconnect that in her, and keep Frost happy by finding a more suitable amount of everything, this way she would be connected, and Frost would be happy with the way his girlfriend was. It was all Seara could feel she could do to keep herself sane. She had heard them talking the other day standing outside the room where she had sat calmly destroying herself, but it had not registered straight away, it had to sink in, and now she had come to realize that when trying to please Scott and trying to make herself more attractive and important to him, she had destroyed something precious to her survival, she had lost the instinct she had to hurt and hate. Now however she could feel it growing as she sat plotting in her mind about the weeks up and coming Legends.
