One Month Ago.....part 2

7 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-01-07 MALCOLM KING Alone in a hospital room sits the one time Evolution Wrestling star. Alone with nothing more than darkness and an IV, he sits amongst a muddled maze of thoughts and memories. He barely remembers how he landed here. But he can't help but feel the secret to his future lies deeply in a past he cannot remember. He's been told and educated on his past after OSW, but while the information he has recieved sounds familiar he is unsure if he can trust it. As if on cue......a woman, the same woman who has been helping him piece together his past emerges into his hospital room. MK:'Hello Rio....' RM:'How are you feeling today Malcolm?' MK:'My head hurts....so better than yesterday.LOL' RM:'So ready for another history lesson?' MK:'No.....still trying to get down what we have went over already.' RM:'So you have querstions then?' MK:'Yes....I remember BOB in OSW winning the rights to my name, and the CEO of the company getting married. I even have recollections on people like Dan S., Tyler Davidson, Cori Simmons, The Disco Kid, Josh Sutton, The Tru One,Xander, etc. But this whole Unlimited thing is a little cloudy.' RM:'Really how so? Maybe I can clarify?' MK:'I can't get around the fact that Big Daddy Dreamer and Heartbreak Chick were the head of the Unlimited. That they lead a hostile takeover of EDW and renamed it Evolution. That they used this group to systematically eliminate every EDW star until there were only a few left to fight the good fight. I'm sorry but on my vague memories of them it sounds a little too gimmicky for The Syndicate.It's not their modeus operandi. Plus why in hell would anyone name a federation Evolution? No Dreamer and HBC would be a little bit more narcassitc about that, I can see Syndicate Nation or something....and the guys in hoods are too KKK-ish for them. I can't get past that fact that my gut....' RM:'That your gut wants you to believe that you too didn't fall victim to their scheme. Afteralll Malcolm you prided yourself on being better than that. My employer still believes you are, since technically you are the last Universal Champion.' MK:'So he wants me to wage a war against Evolution Wrestling....to bring down the house that The Syndciate built? Something just doesn't seem right about it....' RM:'Would you like to see physical evidence?' MK:'In this day and age that can be altered....it'll prove nothing.' RM:'Then again it could provide you with all the confirmation you need. Afterall before your accident, weren't you and Dreamer fueding over your title? What did you always use to say desperate men do......' MK:'Doesn't feel right.....something about this just doesn't add up.' RM:'Then maybe it's time I told you what they did to your wife.....your daughter.....' DOLO BENTSWINGER/1001 HOLDS 1001 Holds has been stateside for a week and is enjoying his freedom to the fullest.From behind him a shadow is cast. He turns around to see his longtime tag team partner Dolo Bentswinger. 1001:'Must you always sneak up on someone?' DB:'Heard you ran into a little trouble in Japan...' 1001:'Thanks for the assist by the way...' he says sarcastically DB:'I had business to take care of.' 1001:'Speaking of which I assume she contacted you also concerning The Syndicate?' DB:'Piece of cake job...it's that other thing..... 1001:'....concerning Malcolm, I know. So what are you being blackmailed with?' DB:'does it matter.....since when did the BodySnatchers need a reason other than money?' 1001:'The BodySnatchers my friend ceased to exist when you left me rotting in that jail cell in Japan. Or was I always the fall guy for your dealings concerning the Yakuza?' DB:'Have no idea what you are talking about...' 1001:'You never do......but to the matter at hand Big Daddy Dreamer and Heart Break Chick; it's time we collect on an old reciept and it's my understanding that we along with Malcolm only answer to one person in The Evolution heirarchy.' DB:'Unlimited power I like that....and that thing with Malcolm?' 1001:'I've been told to do whatever it takes, to keep him from the truth.' DB:'You know it may mean......' 1001:'Whatever it takes.' to be continued................
