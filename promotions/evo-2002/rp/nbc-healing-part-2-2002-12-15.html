NBC:Healing Part 2

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.15.02 ~As we last left Shocka, he was in rare form…he was in the mood to kid and joke. He was not in a fighting mood, as he has seemed as of late. Rather he was more laxed in his thoughts and his being. But you can feel a change mounting upon him, you can feel something is going to snap inside. With everyday that passes and he is forced to put on a mask he will grow that much more weary. It seems that every day Shocka become a bigger target for everyone. Even his own teammates. With recent attacks from Seara and being in a match with Sovereign he is starting to feel the effects of being on the road. When we last saw him he was on morphine prescribed from a doctor but the time has lapsed on the injections. And now this is where we pick up.~ ‘Now quite what you expected?’ A voice questions in a forced way. ~Shocka sits in a chair with one of his legs propped up and ice on it. On the other knee is a hot pad that is flared up a great amount. With no shirt on you can see some pretty bad bruising across the chest and shoulders of Shocka. He supports his neck with one hand in a sort of slumped position.~ ‘Ya know…I’m feeling pretty rough…Sovereign slammed me to the mat with a release German suplex allowing me to land with all my weight on my neck.’ Shocka speaks shaking his head and pointing to his neck. ‘The doctors said that I should have about a month of bed rest to heal this neck up…wanna know what I told him. Fuck you…’ Shocka says with a half smile on his face. ‘Now all of you people and all of you eVo superstars at home are probably wondering…how can you go into a match as big as this with a possible neck injury? Well ya know what this title is something that I have wanted for all of my life. Sure I could have herniated discs in my neck but I am willing to risk a greater injury to get to the universal title. The doctor prescribed me some vicadin but I didn’t cash it in…in fact.’ ~Shocka pulls it out of his pocket and tears it in half throwing it on the ground.~ ‘I am not going to need that to beat L2J…all I will need is to bring myself…in fact I am taking it upon myself to ban thug roC from ringside.’ Shocka says. ‘What?’ roC says as he walks in with a plate of food looking at Shocka half offended. ‘You are doing what?’ roC asks ‘You aren’t going to be at ringside…you can watch from a skybox.’ Shocka says with an attitude. ‘What in the hell has gotten into you?’ roC asks with concern. ‘I dunno…before a big match I have to focus…and I don’t need the damn distractions you can bring…’ Shocka says evilly. ‘Well then I will only come when you need to train I suppose.’ RoC states. ‘Yeah maybe…’ Shocka says. ‘Maybe my ass…you are going to need me to be able to train…because L2J is a cruiser weight…I am a cruiser weight and I bet you that I am faster than his ass. I could out run him and out move him…a match with me is like a match with… a cheetah!’ ~roC says getting excited.~ ‘roC…calm down man…just let me get in tune with my thoughts…get in tune with myself and then I will call you…’
