To Live Is To Suffer

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th October Another week for Asis that many people have speculated about, a couple of things that people have thought strange. The thing that most people are talking about and trying to justify. To be fair it’s not as if Asis needs an excuse to attack someone, but the words he was screaming as he attacked Echo BlackWhisper suggested there being a reason behind it. But unless he chooses to tell someone about it, he’s not going to answer anybody’s questions. And the loss that Asis suffered at the hands of Seara and Jason Jameson is something that got people talking even more. Throughout the match, Asis and Seara were never in the ring at the same time. Maybe this was just the way things happened, maybe Seara didn’t want to face Asis, there is a multitude of possibilities of why this happened. But either way, people were surprised that at the end of the match or part way through it, Asis didn’t attack Seara again like he has been doing for the past couple of weeks. After the match he did attack someone however, it was his tag partner for that match Eric Badger. Asis seemed to be upset at the fact that Badger lost the match for them, but people have also been saying that maybe he attacked Badger because he couldn’t get to Seara, either way, Asis has got to face Badger at this week’s Legends… (As of late Asis has been travelling around a lot, he has been spotted by people in several places you wouldn’t expect him to be seen at, the most recent being at the prison where his Mother’s murderer is incarcerated. He has not done an interview or cut a promotion in front of eVolution cameras for quite a while now. This has been for a multitude of reasons, including the fact that Daniel Somers has been quite wary of letting eVolution employees near him as he has been going through a bit of a violent transition as of late. Also Asis has been moving too quickly and frequently for him to be found for any cameras to catch up with him and if they did he may well have refused to let them shoot. Or just beaten the hell out of the lot of them. Who knows. Right now Asis is in New Orleans somewhere, awaiting this week’s Legends. Getting to a city where an event is being held early is not something Asis is used to and he appears to be having trouble killing time.) Mine. Always mine, always, never get taken away ever. She’s mine, she won’t be taken away, not by him, not by anyone. I’ll kill anybody who stands in my way, kill her. (The words have been spoken by Asis before, at least words similar to those being spoken. Although last time they were not really spoken they were more of a scream, as Asis was destroying Echo BlackWhisper in his locker room. Asis is sitting on the floor inside of an old factory type building, the kind he always manages to find no matter where he goes. The floor is dirty and there is broken furniture everywhere, old wooden chairs and tables obliterated, shattered into shards of wood. It’s obvious that Asis has found a way to keep himself busy, venting a bit of anger in here would maybe save a few people employed by eVo. Asis suddenly becomes aware that there are cameras present and looks directly up and stares at one of them. Even though it’s dark inside the building he’s decided to call home for this week, as they always are, you can still tell that Asis is staring directly into the camera. The cameraman seems to be a little bit nervous as the camera begins to shake in his hands.) Seems like a very long time since I did this. Since I last spoke in front of a set of cameras, baring my soul to all those who want to listen. But then I can choose how much I want people to know, and right now, I don’t feel like talking. (Asis gets up and turns to walk away, when the cameraman asks what he thinks about the public’s perception of his relationship with Seara.) I know what the public think of me and Seara. But nobody it seems can make up their mind. Some people think I hate Seara, some think I am in love with her. And while both statements are equally as true as one another, neither could be farther from the truth. People are asking why I attacked Echo BlackWhisper and why I said the things I did while I was in the middle of sending him on a free trip to the emergency room. Truth is, I did what I did because I enjoy it, I like to hurt people, I like to see their blood on my fists, I like to see it spurting out of every orifice, I like to make others bleed and hurt. There may have been a reason for me doing a number on BlackWhisper, there may not have been. All that matters is, while I did it, it felt good. It’s always felt good to hurt people and hurting him was no different. I know I haven’t been right lately and a lot of what is happening to me I don’t really understand, all I know is I like it. I feel free. Free in a way I’ve never felt before, I feel as if I can do what I want and not have to worry about anything, and hell I probably can. My feelings for anyone and everyone are not clear right now, they never have been, the only true feelings I’ve ever felt were towards my Mother, all other feelings are a cloud of emotions that I cannot break through. Now I don’t even know what I’m saying, this is all so strange, but exciting. (Asis has been getting more animated the whole time he has been speaking, but it is not in an aggressive way as we are so used to seeing him act, rather in a strange way, it is almost like happiness. That is until the cameraman asks about Eric Badger.) Eric Badger a man who claims he is “too extreme.” He didn’t seem “too extreme” last week when he got beaten by Seara. He didn’t seem “too extreme” last week when I dropped him with the Asisination after the match. And he sure as sin won’t seem “too extreme” this week when he is lying in the middle of that ring bleeding. You see something in me really doesn’t like him, more so than the average person and that will be demonstrated on Wednesday. All my life I lived by the phrase “To Live Is To Suffer” and this week Eric Badger will find out the true meaning of that statement…
