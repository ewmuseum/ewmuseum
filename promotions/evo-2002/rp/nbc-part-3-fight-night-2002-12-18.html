NBC Part 3:Fight Night

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.18.02 ~The scene opens inside of a perhaps a gymnasium or industrial building in the fair grounds. A scene very vividly remembered by wrestlers who came up and actually paid their dues. Ones that have performed here in front of very small crowds. Not thousands but only scores of people turning out and the pay off also very little if any. But the center scene is not that of a wrestling ring, but a boxing ring. The crowds flood in ready for some good ole ‘Wednesday night fights’ seems an interesting setting. Tables line around the ring side with people paying 150 dollars to rent them. The announcer screams out.~ ‘And now making his way to the ring…The Northside Stunner!’ ~The crowd erupts with boo’s as the man makes his way to the ring he goes through the ropes and his trainers put on the sparring helmet and jocks. The announcer again cuts in.~ ‘And now coming to the ring, Alan ‘Hardcore’ Castleman!’ ~Cheers roar in as a man in a blue robe runs to the ring he jumps onto the apron and goes through the ropes. The crowd continues chanting for him as the announcer continues speaking.~ ‘He’s on quite a hot streak folks 5-0 and so is his opponent the Stunner he is 4-0…its a battle of wits!’ ~At ringside we see a man with an eVo hoodie on with the hood over his head. As we move in we can tell its Shocka. He also sports a pair of sunglasses trying to disguise himself. He has rented out an entire table for himself so he’s not disturbed. The fight begins and Shocka watches intently speaking to himself.~ ‘Hook him, upper cut his bitch ass…’ Shocka says. ‘Man if I was in that ring I would destroy the Stunner…’ He says again to himself. ~The announcer glances back and a look of glee comes over his face, he runs over to Shocka who rolls his eyes. The announcer sports a eVo Hat and begins speaking to Shocka.~ ‘You want to do an exhibition round with someone?’ The man asks. ‘What?’ Shocka says looking at him. ‘Well I could maybe get someone to box you…’ The man says. ‘And you think I wanna box?’ Shocka looks at the man with a very disturbed look. ‘Listen man…I know you won the golden gloves a couple times…I have followed your career pretty closely.’ ‘Bah…’ Shocka cuts in. ‘Come on for the fans!’ The man screams. ‘Fans…what makes you think that I care about all of these back wood pieces of shit?’ Shocka says to the announcer who holds the mic dangerously close to his mouth. ‘Sorry folks…’ the announcer says. ‘Oh shut up…’ Shocka rips the mic away. ‘You know eVo is in the state and we are gonna rock the house on Sunday…but I feel like a fight tonight…is there anyone out there who thinks that they can handle me?’ ‘Who the hell is that?!’ A voice screams from the crowd followed by a few with the same question. ‘Do you idiots have cable TV?!’ Shocka screams. ‘I’m the man that’s going to be the next Universal champion…you know wrestling!’ ‘That’s fake!’ A large man screams from the audience coming closer. ‘Oh is it?’ Shocka says looking at him. ‘You know who’s gonna win in the beginning…then you just make up some bullshit in the middle…’ The man says as he keeps moving toward Shocka. ‘Do we have a fucking off season?’ Shocka asks. ‘No…’ ‘You think we get to be home every week?’ Shocka again asks. ‘No.’ ‘You don’t think that we get hurt in that ring…’ Shocka yells with the man now in his face. ‘You probably do if you fuck up…’ the man says now into the mic. ‘You are nothing but a fuck up…otherwise you would be in that ring boxing!’ Shocka says. ‘How about I kick your ass right now?’ ‘Do it!’ Shocka screams. ‘Touch me I fucking dare you!’ He laughs to himself. ‘What’s funny?’ The man says. ~The fight has now obviously stopped and everyone in the room is paying attention to the confrontation.~ ‘The look on your face right’ Shocka slaps the man… ‘NOW!’ ~~The man backs up with a smile on his face…he grabs the mic and jumps into the ring.~ ‘You want some of me?’ The man asks. ‘Let’s get into the ring and settle this..’ Shocka says sliding in. ~The Northside stunner and Hardcore have now both ripped off there gloves and are handing them over. Shocka puts on the gloves and the man starts to take off his helmet but Shocka waves it off. The man has armed himself with gloves and a helmet…~ ‘What you scared I’m going to fuck you up to bad?’ Shocka says with intensity in his face. ‘No…’ The man says taking off his helmet. ‘Lets do this…1 round 3 minutes…you’re gonna die!’ Shocka screams and the bell rings. ~The man comes out of the corner swinging and catches Shocka a couple times in the face sending him into the ropes. Shocka comes out of the ropes and uses the force to throw a punch. The man’s nose immediately starts bleeding. Shocka now begins to work his body so he covers it up and again blasts him in the face. Shocka begins laughing as the man falls to the ground and the ref counts to 10 and its over.~ ‘I told you!’ Shocka screams at the man… ‘Now you wanna see how fake wrestling is? Someone gimme a chair!’ ~The ring announcer reluctantly hands a chair into he ring and Shocka lifts the man above his head and simply drops him face first on it. The man grunts in pain as cops storm the ring and pull Shocka off of the man and remove him from the building as the scene fades.~
