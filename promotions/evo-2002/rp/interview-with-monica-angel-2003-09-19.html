Interview with Monica Angel

19 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-19 '...and we'd like to thank you for coming here for this interview, Scott.' the voice of Monica Angel is heard speaking The scene fades open and Scott Frost is sitting in a black chair, across a table with Monica Angel who has a clip board in her hand and her legs crossed. 'Well lets get down to business, since we all know that you are all about the people and the business.' Monica says to Frost with a smirk grin. Frost looks at a Monica for a second before grinning. 'Well, the people want to know. How is your marriage to Katherine Jackson also known as Seara of eVolution?' Monica asks. 'Well, actually it's like any normal marriage. Of course there are things me and Katherine don't see eye to eye on. But it's our love that drives our marriage. It's everything that makes us, us. Her attitude to our family, and our business is exactly what I was looking for in a woman. But honestly, that has nothing to do with what I was under the impression this interview was about.' Scott replies. Monica smiles. 'I know I just wanted to break the ice.' Monica responds. 'But, you are right. We are wondering what your plans in eVolution are.' Monica announces. Frost nods his head. 'Well my plans are to get back into the business full force. After my stunt to get closer to Xander, and understand how someone of his stature reacts to life as Greatness. I took a form of hiatus. Then I talked to Tyler Davidson and decided that it would be best for me and Seara to enter the tag division and replenish our business relationship along with our personal relationship. But now it's back to business. Me and Seara are perfect once again in the ring, well amped.' Frost responds. 'What about The Syndicate, and Jay Jay Jameson?' Monica replies with another question. Frost shakes his head. 'This is something that I don't know about. I mean, The Syndicate was who I am aiming for. I want those Tag championships and be able to share something with my wife. But when Jameson decided to interfer and cost us the championships. It tipped me over board, so I cost him his match up against Matt Hunt. But then he came back and cost me my chance at the Universal championship! That was the last straw. I am happy that the Committee has decided to place me and Jameson at Digital Destruction.' Frost responds. Monica nods here head. 'After Digital Destruction, it will be your first offical year in eVolution. You have signed a ten year contract deal after abruptly quitting eVolution after losing to Xander and him obtaining the Universal championship. Do you have any plans for this upcoming Digital Destruction?' Monica asks. Frost nods. 'Of course. Last year, I proved to Unlimited that I was a force to reckon with. Now, it's my chance to prove to Jameson that too. I have kicked his ass before, and I will definately do it again. Jameson has changed, me and him used to be tight. Used to be friends, and something has changed in him. But whatever it is, I will certainly beat that high horse out from underneith him.' Frost replies. Monica switches her legs begin crossed and then puts her finger on the clip board. 'We got an email from a Fredrick Manchester of Manchester, Indiana. He asks, 'How do you feel about James Kimson being the Universal champion, and if he continues his reign of champion after Digital Destruction. Will you gun after him?'.' Monica reads the paper. Frost smiles. 'Kimson as the champion. It's good. I mean, he is my friend. One of very few now. I hope him the best in the future with the title, but the next time I get the chance I will not hesitate to take him up on that chance. Nothing against him, but lets face it. I made history with that title, and my eyes are definately set on it just like the rest of the roster. But I am also gunning for the tag team championships and Seara seems to be helping that cause out while I deal with some outside interferance.' Frost answers. Frost adjusts himself in his chair. 'Anything else?' Frost asks. Monica smiles. 'Yes. If you win the Universal championship again, who would you like for your first title defense be against?' Scott looks up in the air as if thinking. 'Honestly, I would like to face Xander again. I understand that Xander has taken a leave of absense due to personal business. But lets face it, or last meeting wasn't that great. He won over me because of the ropes breaking and he got lucky and his arm landed ontop of me. Me and him have unfinished business to take care of. He is a great competitor, but honestly he gets on my damned nerves with his whole 'I equal ratings' bullshit.' Frost answers, sounding very truthful. Monica smiles. 'Well thank you for the time, and I wish you the best of luck a Digital Destruction.' Monica finishes it off. Scott stands up and shakes hands with Monica Angel telling her thanks and the scene fades away.
