The Joker - Epilogue

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/30/03 And through it all… The card game in the Arco Arena The flights to Boston The fist of blood to the mirror The drunken phone call in the phone booth to Francis The sense of hope in the hotel The tears in the snowy night outside the restaurant The giving up of all happiness The pinfall that changed everything The gold around his waist for the first time The drive into sin city The bum outside of the hotel The return of a lost love The long night of forgotten passion The kiss that sealed the engagement Through all of it and much, much more… There he was Smiling in the darkness Leaning against the brick wall that stood Lonely and spanning miles and miles There he was A deck of cards in his hands Watching the world through eyes Tearing and watching all There he was Planning and scheming within his confines The sounds of shuffling cards Echoing through the corridors There he was Smirking to himself Watching lips of the passed Embrace the newborn devotion There he was His shuffling never faltering And each card face up Smiling and laughing There he was His deck of a million jokers No kings or queens or aces or anything Except for those jokers And there he was Watching His name and plan In the making The time is nearing He says to himself Looking through the windows of life He had never felt so alive Tick tock Goes the clock And the joker Shall lead them
