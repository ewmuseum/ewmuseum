The brink of insanity...

1 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-01 Scott's high school and college years were nearly the same. The difference between them was that in college he didn't get into as many fights. Through high school and college he was one of the star atheletes, in high school he played football, hockey, and baseball; once in college he decided he'd rather play football and so he took a football scholarship. He studied crimnology in college and dropped out once he found out he was a father. His high school sweetheart had been pregnant when he separated from her, she called him 3 months before their child was to be born to allow him to see the birth. It was the beginning of 1995 when his child was born, Scott's wife and child would only live two more years before their lives would be ended right before Scott's eyes. The events that drove Scott to the brink of insanity all happened in 2 days. On January 17, 1997 it all began, Scott had been undercover for the past few months, and things had been going smoothly until the mob boss, Vinnie Tarantillo decided it was time to tie up some loose ends. Adam Evans, Scott's brother had borrowed some money from Vinnie to begin his training to become a proffesional wrestler, but Vinnie wanted the money back faster than Adam could give it and he sent a few guns to take him out. Scott had been meeting Adam in a coffee house at the time when the guns tracked down Adam. Adam ran into the building bullets following him and then the guns saw Scott and his cover was blown. The only thing that Scott could do was to blow away the guns but he couldn't pull out his rifle fast enough and Adam's was shot in the chest 3 times, his heart hadn't been hit but he was loosing blood fast and he would die. Scott took out the guns before speaking to his brother for the last time. 'Scott, am I going to be okay?' Adam asked with a blank expression in his eyes. Scott was holding back tears whilst holding his dieing brother's hands, he replied with something that he thought would give his brother hope, 'Yea, you'll be fine it'll all be fine.' Adam's eyes still stared forward blankly and the expression his face was blank also it seemed that he was already dead, but he couldn't die without telling Scott what he wanted him to do, 'Scott, you have to live my dream of becoming a wrestler, you have to do it for me. Get the basturd that did this to.' Adam said faintly before his loss of blood killed him. Scott face hardened and he rested his rifle upon his shoulder before leaving the building, anger flowed through his veins when he entered Vinnie's warehouse killing everything that moved. Little did Scott know that Vinnie wasn't in his office, he was out enjoying a night on the town with a couple of girls. Scott went went home that night his shirt blood-stained, his pants torn, and his eyes still watery. His face had never looked as grim to his wife Deanna, but once she learned what happened she too was stricken hard by Adam's death. Scott woke up that morning with his wife making eggs in the kitchen and his two year old son clapping his hands on the couch. Scott's memory of the previous night was currently gone, he stretched out and walked into the kitchen, he kissed his wife and went and sat in the living room with his son. After eating breakfast and playing with his son he had to get ready for work, he went into the bathroom as the mafia hitmen busted into his house. He walked out of the bathroom razor in hand, the hitmen had his wife and child in hand with guns to their heads. Scott screamed as they pulled the triggers, they chuckled at his expense as they dropped the corpses of the Scott's family. Scott got the same grim look upon his face as he opened his closet and grabbed whatever weapon he could stuffing into his pockets or wherever he could hide it. He said to himself, 'They want a war they got a war!' He left his home for the final time and shot out the back tires of the van containing the men that just killed his wife and child. The tires screeched as the van tumbled over spilling out the men inside, they tried to grab their weapons but they had bullet holes in their head before they knew it. Scott would get his revenge no matter the cost, no man would slaughter his family without getting the proper payback. Pity and death was for the weak, the strong would be the ones who would survive. Scott wouldn't be taking any names as he stepped into the warehouse guns blazing. Tonight would be a night to remember.
