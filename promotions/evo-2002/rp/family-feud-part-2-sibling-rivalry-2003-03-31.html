Family Feud Part 2: Sibling Rivalry

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/31/03 ~Hate is such a strong word...it really is. But do I hate Shocka? YES! Shocka almost ruined New-TV, something that I created and nurished until his sick mind wanted me out of the picture. He'll pay...he'll pay. -=[Later in the day, school has let out and Michael and Clare are coming in the home. At the same time, we see Thug roC come down the steps as they have just entered the home arguing like usual.]=- “Michael I’m tired of you!” Clare yells. “Just leave me alone!” “It wasn’t my fault anyways,” Michael responds. “It’s not like I lied about it. Thug roC steps in, “What’s going on?” “Your son Michael ruined the best opportunity for my future success!” Clare says. “That’s not true, cause the only success you’ll have in the future is flipping burgers at McDonald’s. And I didn’t ruin that chance at all.” Michael says with a smile on his face. “Michael…” Thug roC says with a stern voice. Now Clare, what did he do now?” “Well, we were at school…” Clare begins as a flashback sequence begins with Clare describing the events. “And I was talking to the starting Linebacker for our team…Dave McNair” Clare pauses as she thinks about this boy Dave. You can see a thought bubble above her head as she thinks about him. We are then taken to earlier in the day where Clare is talking to Dave at school right after the dismissal bell had rang. “So…” Dave starts off, showing his shyness which is unusual for a jock like himself. “Who are you going to the prom with this year? Dave asks, not even looking into Clare’s face. He continues to look at his hands while he twiddles his thumbs. “Nobody yet, I haven’t been asked yet.” Clare says with the biggest grin on her face. She obviously knows the next question to pop out the boy’s mouth, and the anticipation is killing her. “Well I was thinking…” “Yes?!” Clare kinda springs up to her tippy toes and back down quickly, waiting for Dave to ask her out. Suddenly we see Michael show up out of nowhere and interupts. “Hey Clare, don’t forget tonight dad set you up with an appoinment with that foot doctor.” Michael says, killing the atmosphere. “Foot doctor?!” Dave questions. “Oh,” Clare tries to think of a responds. “I…uh…tore a…bone in it or something when I was little. It’s just a checkup.” Clare then motions with her head for Michael to leave but he’s confused. “That’s not why you go to the doctor.” Michael says. “You still have that foot fungus that never went away.” “Foot fungus?” Dave asks with an outraged look on his face. “Yea, Clare just got it like a week ago. They think it’s from not washing it enough.” “MICHAEL!” Clare yells, trying to make him stop. “Oh yea, mom wanted to know why you go through three pairs of socks a day. I told her you stuff your bra, but she didn’t believe me” Michael says. Automatically, Dave looks at Clare’s large chest and laughs a little bit. “Well Clare,” Dave stops to laugh a little bit. “I’ll see you later some time, ok?” “Wait, what about prom?” “Um…it’s against my religion.” Dave says while jogging away. We then see Clare’s face turn evil as her head turns and stares down at Michael. We then leave the flashback feature and find Thug roC cracking up laughing on the floor rolling around. Clare stands there with her arms crossed and tapping her foot while Michael is also laughing at the incident. “That wasn’t funny!” Clare declares. “He was going to ask me to prom!” “No he wasn’t…” Thug roC responds, trying to stop laughing. “According to him, foot mold and fake breast would be going against his church.. He must belong to the church of foot doctor and protection of cotton rights.” Clare storms out the room leaving Thug roC and Michale cracking up laughing.
