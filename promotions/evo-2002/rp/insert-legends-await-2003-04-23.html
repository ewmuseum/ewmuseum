Insert ~ Legends Await

23 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 23 Lights Camera Action. Fading in, the shot of a wrestling ring in a dark room, most likely a basement as you can see resting up against the ropes, with work out clothing on is James Kimson. His hair tied back, his muscles pumped from running the ropes for the past while working on his cardio as he turns his head to the camera and smiles. Kimson hops over the top rope while holding onto the bottom and flips himself out of the ring and takes a few steps to the right and sits down at a bench, dumb-bells at his sides and he then picks up a bottle of water and takes a healthy swig. Oh, Xander. Man, I was wondering when you’d open that big mouth of yours and let the bullshit fly like a cow with the runs. Lets get a few things straight before tonight goes down. First of all, Yeah I’m sticking my nose in your business. Why, because eVo is my business. Everything that happens in here effects me, and I am not the type of man to sit and watch the world pass me by. I’m a man of action. Unlike yourself who claims to be a man of action and then goes and starts fights with people who haven’t stepped into an eVo ring for months, or at the very least gets a half dozen guys to help him wipe his mouth after he gets S. to feed him in the mornings. You Xander, are nothing but a worthless coward, fixated on trying to get the big bucks. And because you aren’t better then anyone else in eVo you try to play the politics so that you can position yourself high up on the “food chain” like you mentioned. But as far as the food chain goes, you think I’m on the low side of it all? Just wait until you step one on one against the real Icon in eVo, me James Kimson. And I’ll each your sorry ass for lunch and wish I could go back for seconds. You see Xander, winning matches when you’ve got a 2 on 1 advantage isn’t a victory. It’s basically “I’ll beat you now to get the win, but just wait till I figure out how to fight on my own and we’ll go at it again”. But you see Xander, the thing is that I know that your little dogs are going to be drooling at the mouth to get their hands on me. Regardless of what you tell yourself in your little war room while you plot and scheme to take power the fact of the matter is Xander that I am everything that you wish you were. I could and can do anything I want in eVo and I sure has hell don’t need to kiss anyone’s ass to do it. I’ve been the sort of man that does his own thing whenever he wants and that’s why I have the respect that you so desperately want. And all you’d ever get is false respect from your peers. I mean hell, look at your situation Xander. Your best pal Erin Christoph pinned you last week the 1st second he could. Asis looks at you with pitty and loathing because he knows that he’s better then you. The only reason why S. is helping you out is because he knows that he needs backup, not because he thinks you’re the only guy that can help him. But because you are the easiest man to confuse on the face of the planet. So why don’t you just wake up and smell the coffee before I knock your ass out in the ring at Legends! So you can sit in your little special room Xander and plot and scheme and come up with winning streaks and call yourself the biggest dog in town. Wait a second that’s true, because you aren’t anything less then a little Bitch. Kimson takes another swig from the bottle and swallows it down before putting the cap back on it and licking his lips Xander, you and I might have a lot in common. Long careers, many accolades and things like that. But Xander, I didn’t need to kiss ass, I didn’t need thugs following me around. That’s why I know that I’m better then you. And I didn’t pick you to go after. It’s the way it had to be. You want to say that Sovereign laid a beating on me, or whatever you said, you said what....molested. Wow what a catchy term Xander. But hey, were’s Sovereign now? Sitting at home in a padded room? Nursing his injuries while I’m back here wrestling. And back here wrestling the likes of the so called “Hardcore Icon”. You know that’s bullshit because Sovereign is 10 times more Hardcore then you have ever been. And what did I do? Be the 1st man to EVER cleanly beat him. I beat him in the bloodiest match in Evolution History, and I beat him by myself. Who did you beat that night Xander? Nic Vanwho? Yeah Xander, you’re really the “Top Dog” in eVo. Yeah man, I bow in respect for how amazing your accomplishments have been. Accomplishment 1, got signed for some reason, Accomplishment 2, got beat up by Seara over and over Accomplishment 3 got a cool office you’ve dubbed the “War Room”, probably located at the Y in New York, and Accomplishment 4 beat Nic Vancore. Yeah, good show pal. Yeah I’m real afraid that you are going to “take care of your business” at Legends. And since you are the top dog a dogs business is either humping people’s legs or taking a piss on a fire hydrant. So take your pick Xander, I don’t care. But as for me going after you, puh-lease. Don’t flatter yourself. Kimson picks up the two weights but rests them on his lap and smiles, shaking his head. It’s not you I’m after. I’m not after anyone Xander. I’m here to do two things, that’s first to entertain fans and secondly to help people from getting screwed over. I’ve seen it to many times Xander. Seeing people get screwed over in the politics of wrestling. And that’s what you are doing. Getting yourself boasted to a new level and then it’ll be only a matter of time that you buy your way to a title shot, get your thugs to come down, screw Frost over and walk away with the gold. Then its all about keeping the belt out of everyone’s grasp. I didn’t come here for the Universal Title, but if I have to I’ll be very happy to take it off your hands. I want to be Universal Champion Xander, I’ve love to be. I know what it means to be a champion. I know how it makes me feel, I know what it means to the fans to have a Champion they can put their hopes and dreams on, a man like Scott Frost. And I’ll be damned if you or Erin Christoph or whoever takes that away from him without a fair fight. He deserves to have matches that are fair. That are clean. Matches that he won’t have to fight against all odds. I would love to wrestle Scott Frost for the Universal Title down the road. But Xander, you are a speed bump and I’m going 100mph. So you could slow me down. I know that you are dangerous Xander. Anyone with half a dozen curtain jerkers drooling over you is a dangerous man. Pointing and saying Kill to all your little mini bitch dogs then after wards scratching behind their ears. I’m not afraid to get my ass kicked Xander. I’m ready for anything that will happen at Legends and every single week afterwards. But are you Xander? Because if you were would you have all those people following you around? Would you be working along side the CEO of the company? I don’t think so Xander. Because no matter how many names you give yourself. And no matter how hard you pat yourself on the back the fact remains that the only thing you are is a damn coward! Kimson positions himself back to begin the weight’s portion of his training and before he starts, he pauses and sits back up. He places the weights down on the ground and looks back to the camera And Xander, after I finish with you at Legends, finish beating you despite all the odds against me. I think that Christoph has some payback coming. So actually, I’ll be very disappointed if he doesn’t get involved at Legends. Because I want to see his ugly face, I want to see him down at ring side. Hell, maybe I’ll get a BBQ and some lawn chairs so he can sit out at ring side and enjoy the match. Enjoy watching his fearless leader get the crap kicked out of him by The Hype. So that after he’s done watching he is more then welcome to step into the ring and we can get this challenge thing done. And so I can get my payback on Mr. Happy-go-bashy Chair boy Erin Christoph. I guess that sort of means that The Unlimited are going to have to deal with The Hype at Legends. Because I am going to get my payback on you guys for what happened last week. And then we’ll just see what is in store for The End of Days. Kimson leans back and starts to pump the old Iron as the camera fades to black
