Thats Just The Way It Is.

16 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 16, 02 The scene opens up in a hospital room. The room is all white including the bed sheets, we see Jay Stylez Junior laying down on the hospital bed in the white hospital clothes without a shirt. We see that Jay Stylez Junios is taped up from the last match that he had against “The Prodigal” Sean Mason last wensday legends. A knock at his hospital door wakes him up. He tries to lean up in the bed but cant. He tells the person to come in with a look of pain on his face. The door opens and in comes a female interviewer from eVolution, she is dressed up in a all black dress with a mic in her hand and behind her a camera man. She begins to talk to Jay Stylez Junior while he lays back down in the hospital bed. Female: ' Hello Jay Stylez Junior, How are you feeling today?' Jay Stylez Junior: ' How do I feel? What kind of fucking question is that, look at me.' He says while pointing at his ribs. Female: ' Ok, Im sorry. I just want to congratulate you on your victory against “The Prodigal” Sean Mason .' Jay Stylez Junior: ' Yeah, Thanks alot.' Female: ' What do you think about the upcoming Pay Per View?' Jay Stylez Junior: ' Your talking about 'Rise Of The Legends' arent you?' Female: ' Yes.' Jay Stylez Junior: ' Well I didnt get a chance to look at the matches that will be on the PPV cause I was stuck in here all freaking night.' Female: ' Well Junior, You are lucky.' Jay Stylez Junior: ' And why is that?' Female: ' Cause I brought you a copy of the events that will be in the PPV.' Jay Stylez Junior: ' Great.' JSJ says with a sarcastic tone of voice. The female hands him the copy of the matches that will be on the PPV. Jay Stylez Junior places it on the desk that is next to his bed and he begins to speak to the female once again. Jay Stylez Junior: ' Thanks alot for everything. You can leave now.' Female: ' Uhm...Ok hope you get better and good luck to you in your PPV match.' The female leaves his room. Jay Stylez Junior lays up in pain to look at the card. He grabs the card and looks at it. Non-Title ~ Standard Match Ben Juventud vs. 'The Rising Shadow' Mark Steele vs. Jay Stylez Junior Jay Stylez Junior gets off of the bad holding his ribs in pain, he grabs his leather jacket off of the coat rack and leaves the room. The copy of the card falls to the ground. The camera zooms into Jay Stylez Juniors match and fades to black and goes to a PPV commercial. -Scene ends
