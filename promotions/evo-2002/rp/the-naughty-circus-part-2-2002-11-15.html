The Naughty Circus (Part 2)

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.15.02 ~~The scene picks back up with the semi moving down the road at a high rate of speed with the mini van behind it. Shocka is nowhere to be seen on either side of the road…has he pulled a Kai and given in just to fit in?! The fat man driving does seem to have a mighty pleased look on his face as he keeps on truckin.~~ ‘Oh my god that’s big!’ You hear Shocka utter. ‘I dunno if I can make it man!’ Shocka screams. ~~A figure now comes into view walking down the road with a cell phone held to his head he walks with his suit and a duffle bag.~~ ‘Yeah I never noticed how big Denver looked until just now…how far Am I away? I dunno about a mile now…’ Shocka looks back and sees his car about a football’s field length away. He smiles and speaks. ‘Think you can come pick me up?’ ~~Thug roC speeds up in a brand new Red Mitsubishi Spyder he skids to a stop to see Shocka sitting on top of a hill looking at his phone. Thug roC jumps out and looks down the hill to see Shocka’s car he looks at him with a look of shock and amazement.~~ ‘You called me out here for this?! Your car isn’t even 300 feet away!’ ‘I have to save my energy for all my matches at Rise of the legends.’ Shocka speaks. ‘Well you got nine days to rest from that what you think that you are going to be alright?’ Thug roC says sarcastically before getting back into his Spyder waiting for Shocka to get in. When he does they continue the conversation. ‘There is a possibility that I will be in four matches in one night at Rise of the Legends…can you understand how hard that is going to be?!’ Shocka questions. ‘Yeah and I realize that I am a cruiser weight and I know how to train for fast pace matches and even matches that last a long time…I got cardiovascular stamina if ya know what I mean…’ ‘So all I have to do is find some fine Canadian ladies then?’ Shocka says smiling. ‘No…you are going to go to an aerobics class 3 times a day once we get to Canada.’ RoC speaks. ‘I’m not going!’ Shocka crosses his arms and begins to pout. ‘Why not? That’s at least 1 lady from each class….’ Thug roC says with a smile. ‘Hell I might even tag along…’ ‘Not a bad idea man not bad at all!’ Shocka smiles… ‘I bet Jason wouldn’t mind joining me either plus he needs to be fit to destroy the Unlimited with me.’ ‘Yeah we are meeting him at DIA.’ ‘Ya know I love Denver International Airport…’ Shocka says smiling… ‘Why’s that?’ ‘I dunno just thought that the name was cool to say…DIA…say it with me roC….’ ~~roC glances over at Shocka and then thinks to himself remembering where Shocka was before his car broke down.~~ ‘Yo man…does your mouth hurt?’ roC asks. ‘Huh uh…at the dentist they gave me some laughing gas and I felt like I was floating…I guess it didn’t where off before I left because I forgot to shift my car and some shit blew up…’ ‘What gear were you in…’ ‘Gear!?’ ~~Thug roC laughs to himself as the camera fades to Shocka being carried by Jason Steele onto an airplane. A flight attendant stops him and speaks.~~ ‘He’s not going to be a problem on the plane is he?’ The attendant asks politely. ‘Nah…he’ll sleep like a baby…’ ~~Roc walks behind the two with Mark Steele who is stuck with carrying the bags to check them in.~~ ‘I think that he’ll be crying like a baby after that laughing gas where’s off’ Thug roC speaks to himself. ~~Once aboard the plane you hear a loud scream that sounds like a little girl and as the plane takes off you see Shocka pressing his face against the window screaming.~~ ‘Told ya.’ You can hear thug roC speaking softly.
