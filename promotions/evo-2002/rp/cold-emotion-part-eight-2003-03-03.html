Cold Emotion... part eight.

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens with the disturbed, struck face of Morpheus Omega; he deeply contemplates like something sick is boiling inside of him and just waiting to be unleashed. The traits of losing what little sanity he has left are clearly showing... The uncontrolled ranting to himself, the uncontrollable eye twitch and mostly his unpredictable temper. The legacy of 'The God of Sleep and Dreams' unfolds before our very eyes... With Morpheus Omega double crossing Shocka out of the blue, his paranoid thoughts of not trusting his significant other and knowing the fact of Shocka and Sky Night were conspiring about something previously on Legends. The bold facts sticking out in his mind, like they are burning away at him of what the actual truth maybe. Morpheus grinds his teeth and has an angered frown that is more then fearful. Thinking to himself with all that has went down in the previous weeks with his former partner, his girlfriend and the fall of New-Tv. The unanswered question left up in the air would be, when will Morpheus slide over the edge and never return? Time is running out and Morpheus Omega becomes more mental daily, what will be the final straw that pushes him into a world unimagined or not dreamt of by all? 'Gammmmmmeeeeeessssss... WHY THE FUCKING GAMES' Morpheus screams and questions himself, he pulls at his hair with a crazed look. The eyes of this troubled soul and down-right evil man are bloodshot and bulging wide with an insane look. Breathing deeply, the eye twitching becomes the worst seen yet. Morpheus snarls with a very disturbing war cry that follows afterwards. 'They all fall down. They all fall down. They fall down.' he repeats himself and becoming louder each time. 'THEY all fall down. THEY ALL fall down. THEY ALL FALL down. THEY ALL FALL DOWN... Because that's what they do. Fall down, flop dick, fucks in the ass and always with their Fucking Games!' Morpheus states violently by screaming with a frustrated tone of voice. The breathing finally becoming more shallow, he calms down and eases himself back into a half sane state of mind by how he appears. The face sinks down to a saddened look and the eye twitch becomes minimal. The last of few clear thoughts inside the mind of the Infinite of evil. The final moment of clarity visional through the eternal darkness. 'What are the stakes?' he questions himself loudly. 'Finally laying our prides on the line and teaching the allegedly and proclaimed professor his final lesson. Shocka has bitten the hand that feeds him one to many times and its finally time for the bigger dog to show his teeth and take that hand clear off. Shocka may flaunt around and tell everyone he is the best, but deep down he has feared me since NrCw. The behind scene stories of federations when brawls go bad are usually covered up the best possible and there is one secret Shocka will never tell. Shocka deep down inside fears everything about me, but he will never tell you why and I wouldn't want to insult his pride enough to tell you. I will just embrace the world by ending his pathetic career. Jonathon McCade, if you are a religious man then I would pray because one way or another you will truly be sorry for the nightmare you have created. Disciplinary action for every move you make, step you take and everyone you fake...' Morpheus lets out a depressed kind of laugh. 'Shocka has always defined the word fake... The man has lived the hated image who has never been accepted by anyone, an image that was created out of desperate cry of help to be liked or ever be somebody. This image was driven by the biggest, self-centered ego in the wrestling industry. Shocka scraped and scratched and was the dirtiest of bastards to get himself to the top of anywhere. Lying, cheating and stealing himself victories and tickets to the top. The ride of living this game of lies, betrayal and deceit; It ends now and here. When all is said and done, Shocka will be nothing more than an average chump. I started the war and retaliated at Tainted Love, Now its his turn to make move and strike back. Does the number one Stunna really have what it takes to conquer the deadliest athlete in the game?' Morpheus questions and laughs sarcastically like the answer is plain and simple. 'Shocka should think of the situation like this; he used me in many ways and I have used him in return... Why live in the past, when you can fight and survive for your own future. Jonnie boy, he has never seen things in the largest of perspective but only in his selfish dream world that he surrounds of himself with ignorance. This is my world, he is living the reality that I control. Not him. THAT I CONTROL!' he screams into another loud rant. The look on his face immediately changes into an angered frown. The left eye of Morpheus wildly twitches, he screams out of rage and the scene fades to the shocking image of Morpheus Omega. Start praying Jonathon, start to pray.
