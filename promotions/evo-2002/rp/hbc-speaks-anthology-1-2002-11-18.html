HBC speaks.....anthology 1

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-18-02 Toronto, Canada Syndicate International Tower Evening The First Lady of Evolution, The HeartBreak Chick is sitting in her immaculate, spacious office watching the first snowflakes fall over her fair city that she has called home since birth. Though she did check out another rival promotions PPV, and was delighted to see some of her friends back in the mix like the Dudleys reunited. The Genetic Freakazoid returning and HBK winning the World Championship, a small faint smirk grows across her face at The Rise of Legends PPV on Sunday that is going to blow the Survivor Series away. She scans across the city from Lake Ontario to the South, Missaguia to the West, the York district to the North and of course Victoria district to the East. She pans to Skydome, the CN Tower, Victoria Park, University of Toronto, her alma mater and all the striking skyscrapers that try to dominate the skyline only to be dwarfed by the CN Tower and her own building. The city is her oyster, the crown-jewel of Canada. Too bad HBC may have to level the city to the ground just to defeat her ex-partner in crime, Big Daddy Dreamer! Doesn’t he have any respect for our fine city! Challenging me to the infamous ‘Battle of Toronto’ match. You think he would have chosen something he might actually defeat me in, but no. Perhaps all those chairshots, all those superkicks, all those shots to the head from the bolt cutters finally scrambled his brain beyond repair. I mean its not like he doesn’t know what he’s getting himself into. He watched me in the Battle for Los Angeles against Isabella Drake and Sierra Striker which ultimately ended both their careers. He was their last year for ‘The Battle of Times Square’ when Alex Kayman called it quits after facing me. Though I still delight in watching me punch out Carson Daly and P.Diddy. The battle of East versus West ended when I unified the both of them under my velvet grasp. But now, now…I’m fighting for my own city, my own home, my multi-billion dollar tower and enterprise. I’m fighting for every Torontonian that ever lived here against one of its own. Sacrilegious!! Not since The United States invaded Quebec City has there been such a defiant act such as this! BDD in his vengeful quest to see me not succeed is ready to tear apart every fabric of Canadian society! This is just as bad as when our so-called ‘Canadian Hero’ Wayne Gretsky decided to turn tail, take the money and run to Los Angeles! What the hell is the matter with these people! Alas, no matter. I shall give Big Daddy quite a homecoming when he arrives……before destroying him once and for all! Hell D, what in the world makes you think you can defeat me in this match. You know full well the depths I have gone in these kinds of matches. I’ve ran over innocent pedestrians, mowed down the elderly and homeless, demolished buildings right on their foundations, its unrestricted warfare. Draven damn near destroyed the place last time a battle was fought within our city streets, but now, now, you want to take me on, in our childhood hometown! Are you thinking that I won’t perhaps destroy a famous city landmark to crush you because my heart might suddenly start to care? Get real D! Reality check! If I don’t destroy you, there will be nothing left of our way of life up here! Yet, you forget that don’t you. Canada loves me! I’ve represented it at the World Championships, Junior Olympics, and dozens of other international events in my youth. Yet….you never did. You ran to America to play football, turning down the CFL. What, Canadian athletics isn’t good enough for you? You could have represented us in Davis Cup, but no, you didn’t think it important did you. Instead you showed us your true colors a long time ago, to hell with Canada, long live Big Daddy Dreamer! You bastardized son-of-a-bitch!!! So yes, at Rise of the Legends, this match is personal between me and you. You didn’t get the message when I kept Superkicking the teeth out of your mouth. You didn’t get the message when I had that little lap dog Magdalena try to talk some sense into you. No, your so stuck on yourself that your obvious to the outside world! Well then fine D. At Rise of the Legends, you and I will face for the second time, and like the first, I will give you ‘The Kiss of Death’ and bring all the greatness, all the glory Toronto has down crashing on your pompous dead carcass! Farewell Dreamer, it has been nice knowing you all these years because after Legends, you’ll be nothing more than just a distant memory……
