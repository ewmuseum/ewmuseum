Mutiny

30 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens with a large group of people randomly yelling and screaming, scattered ones carry signs holding them proud above their heads. The scene opens outside of an expensive looking classy hotel, all the people are crowded around the entrance, their reason unknown. Closer up we notice the signs read 'Burn in Hell Infinite', one with the anti symbol around the words 'Antichrist', and finally one that’s a white cross that reads 'Morpheus Omega, lies here today. The group of people yelling, One distinct voice can be heard over all of them. The voice sounds of hitch pitched screaming, one of a lady 'That man will burn before I rest, so help me god! That man has had my child in and out of shocking seizures driven from fear from one year ago today until now! The death to that man and my child will finally rest peacefully again!' She demands to the crowd. The lady sounding dead serious in her voice and the group of people start yelling louder and become scattered angry mob cheers. This situation definitely out of control because these people don’t look like they came to have a Sunday picnic and socialize to the others. More like, want to tear down the hotel and skewer Omega's head on a stick and parade around with it until hell freezes over and until forever after. Questionably and unanswered are these raging, Morpheus haters sicker then the Infinite himself? The doors swing open with two large men wearing black casual suits and black and white security t-shirts underneath, they have cold faces, also expressionless. One man turns and speaks into his ear piece/microphone about the situation, the other one yells loudly to address the crowd, they all become quiet and let him speak 'All of you clear out before we contact the police and have you removed. We will use force ourselves if necessary” The man pulls out pepper spray and aims it towards the crowd. The other security guard leans over because the crowd screaming like raged lunatics hailing their signs like it’s the last and dying cause that could save the planet. The man speaks 'I don’t think that’s a very good idea Jenkins. We have pepper spray and they are crazed rioters that will rush us fast. Lets just give them what they want and call the police.' The man nods assuring. The first security guard yells out with a calming voice like he has the situation under control. 'We can meet in the middle, tell us what you want and we will give it to you. Then no one can be hurt.' The man fakes a smile, he’s very afraid of the intense heat boiling from this angry mob. The man signals for the crowd to back away. One man that’s dressed in ordinary fashioned clothing, with the most ordinary hair cut approaches the front of the crowd. The man, medium sized with black hair and clean cut mustache. The man yells loudly 'WE want an exclusive interview with Morpheus Omega!' The man pauses, than screams, 'NOW!' The security guards huddle and stand talking amongst each other quietly. The one signals them to wait, holding his index finger straight in the air. They walk back inside the hotel. The doors closing inside the hotel front lobby, the mob sounds muffled through the closed doorway. The security guards frown in fear at each other. Lost thinking what that group of men and woman could accomplish with enough driven anger. Something catches the glimpse of one their eyes and its Morpheus walking off of an elevator. The man standing tall, towering over with a sick smile but seeming very content. The man walks towards the sign that reads bar. The one security guard reacts instantly and yells loud and full of energy. 'Mr. Omega, the press our outside wanting an exclusive interview! They are in rave about your performance at Legends last night!' The man smiles glowingly. Morpheus stops and turns his head barely acknowledging the man, but stopped dead in his tracks. Morpheus slowly turns and stares at both of the men not flinching from his stance or the look on his face that clearly shows he could care less about press and who these people are or what they have to say. Morpheus strides quickly and towers over both men wearing all black. Complete with the leather, 'dare 2 dream' t-shirt, black jeans and finally the doc martin boots. Morpheus has a glaring shine come from his chain-linked belt. Morpheus looks down on both men and speaks 'Are you kidding me? Hah ah… Press dislike me because I lash out and attack them. People resent contenders like me that have no flaw in perfection, the sick mental thoughts that run through my mind, the sick natural instinct and black outs that have lead to the fall of others into their miserable end...' Both security guards with that feared look they never lost from their faces and by this time now, Morpheus is huffing and puffing, getting excited over the lies these rent-a-cops speak. Morpheus rushes and stops dead center and merely inches from them, they run opposite ways screaming like woman. Almost the tone of Seara while she was held captive at Legends sixteen against her own free will, almost. Morpheus speaks to himself, plotting the ultimate scheme to rid these pathetic angry mob, 'I know in Resurrection we took things far but to the point where large groups of people join and want to have me beat down like a red headed step child? That’s taking it a little bit too far!' he starts pacing back and forth and all of a sudden. The look of sudden genus thought hits him. Morpheus cracks open the fire extinguisher case near by and removes the red handled axe and red extinguisher. Morpheus with both in his clutches, the axe rested against his shoulder and the extinguisher rested into his arm aiming the hosed spout. Morpheus kicks open the door to find the raging mob. Loud screaming can be heard. The scene fills with white cloud, thickly from the fire extinguisher and nothing can be seen. Screaming and battling can be heard loudly and the scene slowly starts to become visible. Fists and boots can be heard hitting off of face or faces and other body parts, still the cause and aftermath cannot be seen. The scene becomes partially visible and half the crowd lies beaten on the ground with each other stomp, kicking, punching and doing whatever offensive attack they thought they were using on Morpheus. While the whole time he hadn’t moved. Loud sirens are heard on the scene and the crowd becomes very frantic. Morpheus casually throws the axe back inside where it looks to be of no threat and holds the extinguisher proudly trying to pose as a good Samaritan. Police rush on the scene screaming 'Freeeeeeze' at different times and scattered, periodically 'Put your hands on your head!' The group starts to die off with a lot of them running and others being arrested. Another siren is heard and the patty wagon screeches to a stop and eight officers in swat gear disperse in different directions from the rear doors. Morpheus stands looking proud like he’s a hero from tricking the angry mob that wanted to kill him into beating each other until the police came. Borderline genus and insane, what makes the clock tick in this delusional, sick fuck? To be continued…
