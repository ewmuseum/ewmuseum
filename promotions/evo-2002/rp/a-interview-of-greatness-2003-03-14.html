A Interview of Greatness.

14 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~A camera opens inside a studio of the Tonight Show with Jay Leno. Evolution camera's are walking around backstage looking for one of their members of Evolution. They arrive at a door that has a star on it and reads 'Jason Relms'.They knock on the door as Jason comes and answers it. He is wearing a black shirt with white lettering that reads 'Five Star Rating' on the front. He is wearing some nike warm up pants with black shoes. He looks at the camera as he has a bottled water in his hand. He motions them to come in as he takes a sip of water and places it by his dressing room mirror. He hears one of the evolution crew say to the other 'I wonder why he is here?' Jason seeminly mad at the question gets one of those smart ass smiles on his face as he looks directly at the camera and begins to speak.~~~ 'FN Network' Jason Relms:'Well I figured you boys would be showing up to get a piece of the action. I told you people when I showed up I was going to do things different. I said that when Jason Relms showed up a five star rating is a gaurante. I guess you can say that the other 'Networks' around have gotten wind as my agent called me this morning and said you have a interview with Jay Leno tonight. I knew right then that I could swear on my life you guys would be here wanting me to comment on what I will say about the federation, about my match and every other damn thing.Well I don't plan anything, I just go out their answer what I wish and tell what I want.Im a true superstar, hell I am the only FN superstar you got. So all of you who think you are all hot shit can get your panties in a wad all you want, when it comes to measureing up with Jason Relms all you peons come up short, exspecialy thouse wanna be's,playaz inc.' ~Jason Relms walks by the evolution camera's as a man wearing a Tonight Show staff t-shirt comes and gets him out of the dresser room.He walks as he hears Jay Leno giving him a welcome. The camera's switch to the tonight show cameras as it shows Jason Relms walking out infront of the big televison. He walks and shakes Jay's hands as the crowd doesn't give him to warm of a welcome. Leno seem's puzzled but Jason just smiles. Jason sits in the chair as Jay sit behind his desk. Jay begins to speak.~ Jay Leno:'Man, these people here didn't give you too warm of a welcome.' 'FN Network' Jason Relms:'I know man, I don't understand it. Just because half the people in this town that have brest also have penises isn't my fault. Damn, I didn't tell the NBA to stick LA with the clippers. The problem is you boyz, girls, well IT'S don't know how to treat some one of such greatness with any respect. Sure you guys got the Lakers, but we all know Kobe Bryant is no Iverson. Everyone that gets some one who is seven feet tall and is allowed to travel every time he gets the damn thing will win a couple of championships.The bulls did it because of greatness, the Lakers do it because of BULL shit!' ~The crowd begins to boo as Jay quickly tries to calm them down.~ Jay Leno''Hey man, I live in California too.Are you trying to say I am a it. Im also a lakers fan so please, take it easy man. But tell me, how long have you been in the wrestling business.' 'FN Network' Jason Relms:'Jay, I have been beating up peons every since I can remember. I don't want to talk all about my old accomplishment's I don't have respect for people who switch federations and brag about achievments they did at another place. Kinda like that Yao ming guy, I mean he can score all the points he fells when he was playing in Japan, but I was watching espn the other day and I saw the man miss a dunk. I mean that's like a diver missing the pool. The kid can touch the damn goal he is so tall and to miss the dunk is just no excuse. But I got in wrestling in federations at the age of 18. I have practiced since I was 12 years old. About two blocks from where I lived was a private gym that my uncle Rod owned. He was a boxing fanatic so he had one of their rings installed in the middle. I would always go again the members and even against my uncle every day. I was never into anything else like I am wrestling. I live to love it. While people live to hate me. I mean it's just like I pictured it, me on top, everyone else nothing but pathetic peons below watching me, wish they could be just like me.' Jay Leno:'So, you have very high views of watch you will do in evolution. I see your first match is this comming wednesday on Legends. How do you feel about facing the group called Playaz Inc?' 'FN Network' Jason Relms:'Jay, excuse me while I laugh. Sure those couple of peons have one a couple of matches. But they where not facing the best in the business. I had a fan yellin at me comming into your studio talkin about how they where once tag champs are some thing and if that is true Jay I realy need to hurry up and get those tag belts to put across my shoulder because it is to shameful that those two have been introduced to Gold in any federation but exspecialy the one I have signed too. These two poor excuses of wrestlers need to realize that Team Network is nor here for games. We are not school, we do not have recess, we absolutely do not play. The only thing I have left to do Jay is show them. That is all that is left. When I take one of those peons left them up slam them on that cold hard matt look around as they look up at me only seeing my figure as the bright lights blurr their fison. As I hold one of them down for the one, two, three and get another victory for the bad boyz of evolution, Team Network. It's funny Jay Im going to stand over their bodies like to worthelss corps make that sorry excuse for a ref raise my arm and before I get out the ring spit right in their faces for even thinking they were worthy enough to step inside the ring with THE FN NETWORK!' Jay Leno:'Why do you call your self The FN Network?' 'FN Network' Jason Relms:'I bring it all when I am in that ring. You see me I tell it all. I got the body of a god and the talent of no other man in this business or on this planet. I dont believe anything when it comes to Jason Relms, I only make people believe because I know. I know come next Wednesday I will whip Playaz Inc.'s asses no questions asked and yes High Impact will be in my corner the whole time. FN Network is given to me because FN is the strongest word anyone can use and damn it I am the strongest best man any one will ever meet. Network, is what everything is ran on. It refers to your corporation, wrestling and any and every other person who has a tv. I mean lets face it everyone one's a televison. And people do different things for people to watch their own Network. Well I am what evolution has done. I am what your company has done as well because they know that all these people here and every one at home wanted to hear what Jason Relms has to say wether it be about wrestling or about anything else I have to speak about.' Jay Leno:'Well we got to take a quick commercial break want you stay back for one more segmant , be right back we are here with one half of the new signed tag team to evolution, Mr.Jason Relms!' ~The camera cuts to a camercial, when the camera comes back Jay begins to ask more questions.~ Jay Leno:'So I know your partner has said he was a high flyer, what type of wrestler would you describe yourself?' 'FN Network' Jason Relms:'It's easy Jay, a powerful one. You see this body?(He looks at his arms as he flexes)This body will sooner or later slam every man that is considered nothing in evolution or is considered to be their own legend. My style like I have said before is undescribable. When I get in the ring just pray that the team they put Team Network against will be able to walk out own his own when I get done with him. It's that simple, no prisoners, jay, NO damn prisoners. Life considers hell to be a hot and unberable place. I tell you what, you interview playaz inc. after they face Team Network they will be more then happy to say hell would of been a better place. Truly Jay, I am not kidden. Statuz Quo, on of the Playaz Inc member kissed my woman. He had the nerve to take his filthy trailer park trash lips and but them on my woman. I will not have for it Jay. First he and his partner, dummp, ditzy, hmm,O, Duzzy will see that they are not just going to be a example. Hell a statement want even do it justice. Pray Jay, take your two hands and put them by that big ass chin of yours and begin to pray for those two peons. Do IT! ~Jay then quickly does what Relms says as his body size his very scary as he is well built.~ 'FN Network' Jason Relms:'That's it Jay. While you are at it you might want to ask about moving out of a crapy state like California. But anyways make sure you say them damn things extra good because they will need it. Every single bit of it.' Jay Leno:'Man I guess I could see why you want to take it the extra mile.Well It's been fun but we are about out of time.' 'FN Network' Jason Relms:'Jay,This thing isn't over till I say it's over. Come next Wednesday Team Network show's the world who is the best, who is worthy of the tag belts, who is TOO DAMN GOOD!Fuck this!' ~Jason then gets up and walks out. As the camera fades to black. The evolution camera cacthes up with him outside in the parking lot of the studio.They ask him for a closing statement about his visit to the tonight show.~ 'FN Network' Jason Relms:'Well first I am glad to get out of California. I mean Okland can't win a world series.Lakers have everything rigged for them. And the damn clippers can't win a game.And on all that the Raiders can't get any body under 40 to play so they get waxed in the super bowl. Yup, can't wait to leave this place. The FORSAKEN child is being born. The FN NETWORK is going to take his name and make it show that yes I am too damn good, yes I am better then those two peons of playaz inc, and yes Team Network will be Tag Champs sooner than later here in evolutin.Life is wrestling people. That is what I live, this is what I do, this is what I am best at. Just in a few short days I prove to the world who the FN NETWORK is..Playaz Inc..Im patiently waiting..and waiting..' ~~~The camera begings to fade to dark with a smart ass smile..As the tv is now pitch black.~~~
