The Massacre at JFHOB

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-23 The day had started easy enough for Scott on August 15, 1989. It seemed as if nothing bad would happen on that day but he was wrong, Jack Forrester had attempted over the past 3 months to get Scott and his twin brother to join his 'enforcers' to help enforce the rules at his boy's home but all attempts had failed. Jack wouldn't beg Scott and Adam to that for him today, Jack had forced to the brink of insanity knowing that if he were to hit the boys he would be shut down and his establishment where he made all his money would be gone, his pleasure at seeing the look on the faces of the boys after they walked out of 'The Box' would be gone and he'd be in jail. He had to do something drastic in a room that the janitor would not have access to. Adam had a bad feeling when he awoke but he told no one and he wasn't suprised when one of Jack's enforcers came running at him and Scott during lunch holding a butcher's knife. It was then that the only friend that Scott and Adam had been able to make in their time at the JFHOB would begin something to avert the attention from them. Steve Williams was what you would call a problem child, all the boy's at this 'home' stayed away from him because he brought problems with him all kinds of imaginable problems and this act was the only thing in his life that had happened that was good for someone up to this point. Steve jumped on the boy who was running with the knife and the boy tripped and the knife went into his eye and through the back of his head, it was then that the massacre started. Jacob Stevenson had been sitting in the kitchen when one of the so-called 'Enforcers' was murdered, he saw that kid Steven Williams take him down he chuckled to knowing that Steve had finally done something good, Jack's downfall would be today and Jake knew that. When Jacob accidently cut off his finger all he did was laugh and the laughing wouldn't stop until later that night when the cops would run in and stop Steven's rampage of destruction. Jacob was slightly retarded and it could easily be said he was looked down upon for that, but despite that Jacob survived when the masses ran his way and he wasn't trampled like many others. He stayed out of the way and he lived to go back to his parents. Jack's little home was more of a detention hall than anything else, and the pure fact that Scott and Adam were put there was because they were problem children that needed their problems sorted out but their stay here would make things worse. TGF had been teaching a group of boy's from the home about the bible when the other children ran in screaming he told them to stop and stay calm but his voice wasn't loud enough and the just ran to the front door which had already been locked. TGF saw Steven when he ran in and screamed it had been almost as if Steven had became a living, breathing version of Jason Voorheses. Before the rampaging herd of children had ran in TGF had been bored but now as Steve made his way towards TGF, he wanted to be bored again. Steve just chuckled when he saw the expression of fear and TGF's life ended while he was screaming at the top of his lungs for Jack. During all this commotion Scott and Adam had stayed calm and just sat in kitchen awaiting the cops, they knew that Steve would have done something like this even if they hadn't shown up here all those years ago, they could escape from the building but the thought currently didn't cross their heads they were still staring at the floor which within 15 minutes had been covered with blood and the remnants of the boy's suffering. Then the thought occured to them that this had happened for some bigger reason, but that disappeared when they remembered that there were explosives in the basement. They slowly disappeared down the stairs to get them to make this night even more explosive, they thought, 'May as well get rid of the incriminating evidence.' Jack had been whistling and swinging his keys around his right ring finger when the janitor approached him, Jack knew that something must be up because the janitor never even looked at Jack even when Jack had been handing money to him to not tell the feds about all the things he had been doing. But the expression on the janitor's face had been awestruck. At first he stumbled over words trying to tell Jack what was going on, then he finally got it out and screamed, 'PEOPLE IS DIEING! THERE'S A MURDER ON THE PREMISES! I'm going home early.' Jack was awestruck at first when Janitor just ran off, then the words hit him with the full effect and he realized now that he'd be put away for life, he did the only thing he could think of and ran to his car but someone had gotten there before him, the only thing left of his car was a charred frame. Someone had gotten into the explosives and Jack knew he was done for. When he saw Steve Williams standing in the doorway with that menacing look upon his face he knew he was done for. He knew who the one doing the murders was when he saw the hook in Steve's hand smeared with blood and with little pieces of ripped flesh upon it. He gulped when Steve plunged the hook into his chest, he tried to scream but nothing came out and the last thing he saw was Steve's arm over his head and Scott and Adam running out of the building followed by all the children who had survived. By the time the cops had arrived the story was already sorted out, all the children except for Adam and Scott had literally no idea what just happened, when Scott and Adam told them what they were to tell the cops that became to the story of what had happened. Jack had went crazy and went on a killed spree, after around 20 kids had been killed he blew up the building with the explosives that had been in the basement. All the children told the cops the same story but they couldn't help but think that something else had happened especially by the looks of one kid who's clothes were blood-stained. But Scott and Adam easily explained what happened to him, he had been the last child who Jack attacked but he escaped with no scratches by locking Jack in the building and stealing his keys, the cops believed the story to an extent but they couldn't help but keep their suspicions. That night all the children that had parents were brought back to them, Scott, Adam, and Steve were the only three who were left in the dark that night. They were put into a better boy's home and within 2 months Scott and Adam found out they had an Uncle on their mother's side that was going to adopt them. They were fortunate because their Uncle had just won the lottery, but Steve never left the boy's home until he was 18. Scott and Adam began their 'new life' with their Uncle shortly after moving into his house.
