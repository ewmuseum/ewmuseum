Seeing clearly

19 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-19 He had awoken minutes earlier but it was his preference every morning to pretend this was 1996 and his life was happy again but he knew when he opened in his eyes he'd still be in the same hotel room he'd spent that last few weeks of his life in he wouldn't in his house with his son jumping up and down on his stomach to get him up. He would no longer hear the calls of, 'Do you want scrambled eggs this morning?' from his wife. He'd never be the same man as he was back then and he knew it, he didn't need friends after what happened, it seemed everyone and everything he ever cared about had been snatched away right before his eyes and all he could to forget it was run, run as fast as he could. But he could never escape the grief and now the only thing he did was try to leave an everlasting mark on this world before he got to meet his family once more. Maybe he went to wrestle because of his brother's last request, maybe it was to leave his name on this planet before the time came for him to be brought back to the reality he'd had once before. He would find the inner peace that he had sought since that fateful day in 1997. But there was no way that he'd ever forget why he didn't just get it over with when he saw his brother, wife, and child die. He had something he had to do before he was gone and there was nothing stopping him, sure he could have gone the same road his brother had took that ultimately led to his death but he needed to do it the right and at the same time do what his brother had told him. If it took all the time in world he had it, but nothing would stop him from becoming the greatest as his brother had envisioned himself. He still remembered every moment of pain as he stepped into the ring but that was what drove him to succeed because he couldn't stop. He needed to remember the last thing his brother told him and he needed to live out his brother's dream. The days of the past were far behind Scott and yet he looked to them to see his future, he needed to remember the pain to see clearly no matter how broken up he became.
