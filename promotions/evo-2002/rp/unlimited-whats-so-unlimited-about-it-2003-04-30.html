Unlimited...whats so unlimited about it?!

30 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.30.03 ~It was 2:15 in the afternoon his phone hadn’t stopped ringing all day. The relaxation that he wanted so much was, was lost on the stress of the day. End of Days had come and gone, he did what he said and beat roC in the cage. No matter the circumstances a win is a win is a win. Shocka glanced at himself in through the rear view mirror peering at his black eye for a brief minute.~ ‘I’ve won the two most recent battles but…I think New-TV still wants to wage war…’ He said to himself in a barely audible tone. ~He swung the wheel viciously to the lift and pulled into a hotel room he carried in his bags and gave them to a bell boy who delivered them to his room that he was assigned in the lobby. He casually walked into the bar set off to the right of the lobby and he ordered 4 screw drivers, he slammed 30 bucks on the table and walked out with the drinks in his hands. He took the elevator to his room when he arrived he tipped the bell boy and sat down on the bed.~ ‘You know something it seems that I have acquired a little bit of class being around some high rollers around here…I mean this town loves me I have respect every where I turn…New-TV could never make it up in this joint because they are to busy smoking joints of there own…those sorry fools haven’t even dreamt that they got rooms like these.’ Shocka said as he set up a fax machine. ~The machine started to beep and started to print a fax. Shocka smiled and spoke again.~ ‘See I am so high class that as soon as I turn this damn thing on people are trying to get a hold of me!’ He laughs and takes the fax off the fax machine and looks down at it. ‘Sure as soon as I finish up this little war I had with New-TV representing Team eVolution I am sent into battle again…but this time its competition from the unlimited a man that I know oh so well…the first crowned champion in eVolution…but I was the first man to beat the first crowned champion in eVo and that makes me better than you like I always have been Christoph…ever since I exposed your little secret that you really aren’t Russian and that’s why you hit me with a fucking dildo…that you got out of your own bag as we established many months ago…’ Shocka said after reviewing the fax he was sent. ‘Joshua Crowe is a good man for sending me that though…it’s the unreleased card…hehe how could someone not high class get a hold of that?! I mean come on!’ Shocka shouts again in a happy voice. ~A knock at the door draws his attention toward it he smile and points at himself and walks towards the door.~ ‘Listen man…I think its over.’ Elmer said entering the room. ‘What is little buddy?’ Shocka asked as he slammed the door and went and plopped down on his couch. ‘This relationship I mean since all of our segments weren’t aired because my pranks were lame you know it you don’t have to make me feel better by keeping me employed.’ The little man spoke. ‘How about this….’ Shocka started out seemingly not thinking about what he was saying. ‘You can just be me and Frost’s managers…’ He said as he realized what he had just done. ‘REALLY?!’ Elmer screamed excitedly. ‘Yeah…sure…’ Shocka said rolling his eyes. ‘YES!’ Elmer screamed. ‘Well you gonna have to go I got some things I have to attend to..’ Shocka spoke shooing the little man out and slamming the door behind him. The scene then fades.
