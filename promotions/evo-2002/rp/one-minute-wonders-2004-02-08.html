One Minute Wonders

8 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-02-08 Question. What do the following people have in common? Gina G. Chesney Hawks. Danny Cadamatri And Carl Cash. Have a long hard think about it, the answer is there if you think long and hard about it enough. Gina G burst onto the UK scene as a singer ear marked to excel within the Eurovision song contest for her country and perhaps with a major pop career in the offering. 'Ohh Ah Just a Little Bit' was the song but it proved to be a little bit too much for most people. 'I am the one and only', a song that has gone down in history. Now name me another Chesney Hawks song?! Now lets consider the English football divisions. Danny Cadamatri, a man set aside as someone who could be the future of English football. Now the only thing worth mentioning about the former Evertonian is his previous pineapple haircut. One season he was a star in the making, next he was a comet entrenched in the lowest crater. Then Carl Cash. A contextual example Carl Cash is, in the sense this is a man who was a hit with Evolution for all of two seconds. Why refer to this? Why bring it up now? Simple, I would like to set aside a special place in this kliq for a new entrant. Joshua Northstar THIS IS YOUR LIFE.... Any little boy can weild a few fists and share a one night stand with the word bravery, however men don't spend the next few weeks licking their wounds when times get rough. Whats wrong Northstar? You've gone a bit quiet haven't you! Money talks but I doubt even a small fortune could drag you from the rock your hiding under at present. These fans don't deserve this, they pay their weekly earnings to see men exchange the powerful dollar for blood, sweat and tears; they don't expect to see a living example of a one minute man. Hardcore matches are often the most dangerous environments a man can experience, don't worry I've done my homework to that extent. I said when this match was first announced, that Joshua Northstar could go down as having the shortest career in history; I thought the least you could have done is filled it out with a bit of verbal garbage. Nobody remembers a no hoper in the ring and everybody soon forgets a man whose had a cat bite his tongue. Don't worry you have'nt spoiled the occasion for me, you've met my expectations and those thousands of pounds spent on my soon to be Hardcore Title will be a good investment. Infact its for the best really, that title was made for a man with big shoulders; not a twig like frame who wilts under the slightest burden. Perhaps you'll suprise me, surpass yourself not that on current form that will take much; just realise though that while you might believe your leading me into a false sense of security, right now I am in reverse just to hang at your level and this body has a full five gears at his disposal. As a closing thought let me say this, fans need a champion who can stand out from a crowd, not a champion who wants to hide within the crowd. Forget wheres Wally, the new game is where is Joshua?!
