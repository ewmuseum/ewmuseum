One On One

23 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-23 The scene opens up inside of a decent sized room which has no light, but what is streaming in through the window. We see a bed with dark red sheets and white pillows, a big beautiful mahogany dresser, and a few other pieces of furniture indicating that this is someone’s bedroom. We hear the turning of the doorknob and the door swings open and a hand reaches out to flip on the light switch. In walks Brianna Washington with her curly hair held up in a fluffy white pony tale, wearing a pink shirt and dark blue jeans. “Yes!” She exclaims merrily as she makes her way into the room, heading directly for her dresser drawer. She pulls it open and reaches in, pulling out a thick black notebook with diary written on the front in white lettering. Brianna places the diary on top of the dresser as she reaches over and picks up the phone dialing a number and waiting for someone to pick up. “Hey Milo! Guess what…yeah I got the job!...thank you…well technically it’s not permanent....yeah I have to earn my spot. But I think I can do it…I should be starting the next few weeks…You have to go? Ok, but when you get home we’re going out to celebrate ok?…Yeah you can bring Greg if you want. Ok see you later.” With that she places the receiver back in place and plucks the diary from the dresser, carrying it over to the desk where she takes a seat. “Haven’t had anything good put in here for a long while, so this is a pleasant change.” She says to herself as she pulls a thick pen from the desk, flipping through the pages of her diary, stopping on a blank page. She wraps her left hand around the pen and pulls it down to the paper and begins to write. August 23, 2003 Well after months of being out of a job, I think I’ve found a new home. Evolution is a great place and I think it will take me to a new level in my career. And if it happens that things don’t work out there, which I am hoping won’t be the case; I am willing to go almost anywhere just as long as I can wrestle. No matter how much pain and broken bones it will cause, wrestling will always be a passion of mine that I will always pursue. Now I just have to see where time takes me. She stops writing and puts the pen down staring at the wall to gather more thoughts, but nothing comes. So she closes the diary and keeps it on her desk to write in later in the day. Just as she is about to sit and relax on her bed, Milo comes strolling into the room. “We’re here.” He says to Brianna as he leans against the door in his tight black pants and red silk shirt. “Ok. Well just let me get changed and then we can be on our way.” Brianna says sitting up on the bed thinking about possible outfit options. “Take all the time you want Bri. I’m sure I can keep Greg company for a while.” He flashes a big smile and winks as he exits the room. “Milo has to be as gay as they come.” Brianna says to herself, giggling a bit as she opens a drawer at her dresser. The scene slowly fades as she rummages through the shirts with Evolution still emblazened in her mind.
