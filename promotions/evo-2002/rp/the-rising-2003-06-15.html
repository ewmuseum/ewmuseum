The Rising

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-15 ~It had once again overtaken him…it was rising inside from day one he knew that it would progress but not this far. It was going to go to far…from time to time it consumed him but he learned how to keep it down. Something in him knew, it was right in front of him but couldn’t see it. Shadows cast over his deepest thoughts as now all he could think about was what his next move against Greatness could be.~ ‘They said I should keep it under control as long as I kept my temper in check…’ He said into a telephone. ‘Yeah I know…I can’t let him or Xander get to me this week…Sean Mason and I are going to have to show everyone what we can do.’ Shocka smiled as he hung up the phone and got up. ~He grabbed his leather jacket and a helmet as he went outside, in front of him was a brand spanking new Orange County Custom chopper his face was on the gas tank and it read Shocka on the oil pan. “eVo” was written on the carburetor Shocka quickly sped away as the scene faded back to his hotel room.~ ‘You know this thing with Greatness is not going to come to an end this damn week…it never can because of the count out…no one has the advantage…some how I need to get the upper hand…I need to wait for him to make the first mistake.’ Shocka smiled and shook his head for a second before continuing speaking into his tape recorder. ‘They said that having a recollection of my thoughts is the best thing for me to do…once I finally defeat Greatness and prove to everyone that I am the best in this business one journey will come to an end and another will begin.’ A smile again crossed his face. ‘I can’t fucking believe I am talking about this shit in my personal thoughts…I need to get out of this pattern…I’ve got to beat it this time.’ His words sort of trailed off in a hurt manner. ~He stood up and whirled around smiling evilly as he again began to think aloud.~ ‘To gain the edge…to get the upper hand I have to look under the mask…I have to know who I am dealing with…’ Shocka shook his head and this time with the flick of a wrist the tape recorder had shattered into hundreds of pieces. ~The rising had begun, it was here to take him and everyone around him for a ride…to show them the way.~
