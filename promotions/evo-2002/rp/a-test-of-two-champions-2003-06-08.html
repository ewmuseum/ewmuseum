A Test of Two Champions

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-08 With his Continental Title draped upon his shoulder, Sean Mason stood before the cameras once again ready to expose himself to the media and all the hassle which comes with it on a weekly basis. Although his face remained masked, it was clear as Sean cast his eye down at the strap upon his athletic frame that before the camera stood a proud proud man. Upon being given the cue, Sean take a moment to compose himself. With so much experience to call upon it had got to the point in his career where Sean was no longer in the position where he needed to be rushed. Sean having excelled as much as he had in this industry, was now in the position to call the shots and as he stepped forward toward the lens he was going to make damned sure he dominated Evolution with the charisma and charm which had made him a firm fan favourite in this company. 'Ladies... Gentlemen... And Xander' were the first words Sean let out of his mouth. Still adjusting to the wearing of his mask his words were somewhat muffled, but they were projected loud enough for them not to be mistaken. 'Next Legends see's myself competing at a time when things could probably not be better for myself. As a wrestler I pride myself on my accomplishments and upon my right shoulder sits the fruit of my labours' Sean said tapping the title he had captured only two weeks prior. 'In wrestling opportunities do not come along all the time, but with only a fortnight of my reign in the past it would appear I am already enjoyed the perks attached to this lofty position. Having disposed of the frankenstein, it would now appear I am set to go one on one with the man some would say made and breaked him in recent years. Xander' Sean paused adjusting his title, 'much like Asis I find it hard to describe you in one word. Instead I would classify you as being a sick and twisted breed similar to that of the monster you helped create. Though I have to say, while you have seemed more to stick to being the brains of the operation it was intriguing if nothing else to watch you step up to the plate and get your own hands dirty for once. A wrench it would appear is your weapon of choice. The ring your setting. Suprisingly though your victim in this past week was none other than your very creation!' 'I'm not sad to see the back of a man who has destroyed many careers in this past year, I am just dissapointed it could not have happened sooner. All I will say on the matter of Asis and his impromptu meeting with a wrench, is it could not have happened to a nicer person' Sean said showing a broad grin despite the covering of his zebra like colored mask. 'Having said that though, I find it hard to believe Asis was the victim of a random strike. The more likely explanation is that he was the victim of a cold and calculated action of a man feeling the effects of being at the top of the mountain and the vertigo associated. I have served my time on the shelf and if you have any intention of condeming me to that kind of sentence I would rid your mind of such foolish thoughts right now. Asis may not have been an isolated incident, but I refuse to entertain the thought that he was the warm up act for 'The Prodigal Son'' Sean said stepping further toward the camera and raising the tone of his voice somewhat. 'You and I will step into the ring' Sean stated pointing toward the camera, 'but lets make something abundantly clear from the off the wrench is not part of the equation much like my Continental Title is as well. By all means use your trusty tool to settle family disputes, but the minute you bring that wrench in my direction things get out of hand and ugly fast. You could well leave Legends as the man with the reputation of having one of the shortest title reigns in Evolution history, but that will be the only reason you will grace the history books as a result of our match. Your not my maker, I made myself and going into Legends I will continue to do so. When I do call time on my career , it will be upon my terms and not of those of some mindless metallic object you weilded in the fashion of a mindless two year old only a week ago. Destiny shapes our fate and I will not allow you to shape my destiny, I may though just give you a timely reminder of what it is to lose something you cherish most. Friendships appear to matter little to you, I wonder though if the same can be said for that piece of gold you hold so dear!?!?'
