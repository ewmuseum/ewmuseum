The Stunning...

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-18 Damon O'Halloran sat quietly at his dining room table, sipping a cup of coffee as he read the morning paper. “Hell in a handbasket,” he muttered to himself. A svelte brunette walked into the kitchen wearing an oversized bathrobe. Her shoulder-length hair was messed, her makeup smeared from sweat and sleep. “Morning Damon,” she said sleepily. “Do I smell coffee brewing?” O'Halloran looked up from his newspaper. “In the kitchen…. Uh, what was your name again?” “Janet,” she replied. “How can you not remember my name after last night?” Damon looked at the clock. “I’ve known you for all of twelve hours, and maybe an hour of that was actually spent talking. Besides, so many women come through here, it’s hard to keep track. I should have a guestbook by my front door.” “How special,” she muttered as she poured herself a steaming cup of coffee. Janet looked around as she stepped into the dining room. “You’ve got an awfully small place,” she commented. “I thought all you professional wrestlers were rich and lived in mansions.” “First of all, not all wrestlers are rich. Just us successful ones.” Damon finished off his coffee. “Second, it doesn’t pay for me to have a big house when I’m never in it. I’m home maybe thirty days out of the entire year. I’m not about to let the government screw me over in property tax for a house I’m never in.” “Oooooo, big AND practical,” Janet smiled. “I thought you wrestler types were all stupid.” “No, what’s stupid is sitting in MY home wearing MY clothes, and making smartass comments to ME.” Damon leaned back, tilting his head and looking at Janet from the side. “You’d better not be naked under there. I don’t want any chick stains on my good housecoat.” “What am I supposed to wear? You tore my clothes off of me last night. I can’t even find my panties. “ The big Irishman turned his attention back to his newspaper. “Check the driveway. They might still be hanging on the car’s antenna.” Janet scrambled to her feet and rushed out of the room. “Good God Damon! Why didn’t you bring them inside!” “Why would I? It’s not my underwear. Besides, you can pick them up on your way out.” Janet poked her head back into the dining room. “Out? Where am I going?” Damon folded the newspaper. “Home, or where ever it is you go. I’ve got things to do today.” “But… but I thought we were spending today together.” Janet brushed a lock of hair out of her face as she stood beside him. “Now why would we do that? You think I’m looking for some kind of relationship where we hang all over each other, go shopping and all that crap?” O'Halloran appeared quite amused at the thought. “Well yeah,” Janet said, the hurt apparent in her eyes. “You said I was beautiful. You said I was special.” “Oh you are special. Nobody’s ridden the Damon Express like that for a long time,” said O'Halloran. “But I spent Thursday Night manhandling Ali Khadafi. After that match, I needed release. So I got you drunk and brought you back here.” Janet was stunned. Stunned and hurt. “You bastard!” “And then some,” said Damon with a smirk. “I… I feel so dirty,” Janet said. She seemed to tighten up as she hugged herself. “I feel so used. How could you do something like that? I have feelings, you know!” “How could I do something like that?” O'Halloran stood up and walked into the kitchen. “Because I’m Damon F'N O'Halloran. You have feelings? What does that matter to me? I do what I want, to who I want. Like that son of a bitch Ali Khadafi, for example. For the past couple weeks, he’s been my bitch, and he never even knew it. I used him, and assuming by some miracle he gets past this Wednesday and goes on, I’ll abuse him.” Damon came back into the dining room, a fresh cup of coffee in his hand. His housecoat was lying on the floor. “Hey Jackie? Jackie? Does this mean you’re ready for Round Two?” “It’s Janet, you asshole!” she yelled from down the hallway. “Go to Hell!” The front door slammed shut. Damon walked to the window and watched Janet, he blouse torn along the side, holding the front of her jeans closed as she stormed down the driveway. “Women,” O'Halloran said as he sipped his coffee.
