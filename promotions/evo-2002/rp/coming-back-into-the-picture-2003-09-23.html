Coming Back Into The Picture

23 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-23 As I sit here in the keystone state of Pennsylvania, I think about all the mistakes I have made in life and I think about everytime something goes wrong, I head back to Pittsburgh to regain myself. Was it my way of thinking or is it that I simply am home sick?Either way it doesn't matter to me, because it is a way to clear my head. I am still frustrated when I watch the video tape of my match with Matt Hunt and here the promos cut by Matt Hunt. It really makes me sick in my stomach. Sid he calls me. The guy is so stupid he can't even remember my name. Now he wants to play referee. I bet he can't even count to three. His brain is probablly so rattled after the beating I gave him. Either way I know the odds are stacked against me. He wants that eVolved title as much as I do. He knows he has better odds of beating Evans then me, so of course he will fast count for Evans. Then Scott Evans, the eVolved champion. The guy that stuck his nose in the wrong place. Evans, I rattled your brains at Legends, and you had to blind side me with a chair, to get me out of the ring. Just imagine what I am going to do to you this week at Legends. I am not going for the quick win. I am going to make you suffer, then when it is all said and done, I am taking your title from you. Oh and I hope you enjoy not having titles. Remember I am the one that prevented you from getting the united championship belt and come this Legends I am going to take your eVolved championship belt. Evans, this was a war that was going between me and Hunt to claim who really was the best. He beat you, so he figured he deserved a shot at your gold. I am the number one contender so I get the shot. We had a match and that would of made it clear to Hunt that I was the one getting the title shot, but you stuck your nose into my buisness. Now I am going to make it my buisness to take you out of the picture. Oh and Hunt, I know you'll be in the ring with us. Make a turn the wrong way and I'll destroy you. It's my night to claim the gold. If you want a shot after I win, I'll give it to you. You'll just have to prove you deserve it. Right now, you don't deserve it. All I am saying is come Legends, you all will see why I am 'The Perfect Design'. You will all find out why champions are broken and egos are shattered. At Legends both Hunt and Evans will be dealt Final Justice.
