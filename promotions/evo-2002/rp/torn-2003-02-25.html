Torn

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.25.03 It sometimes feels like a burden I want to succeed. Is this a good quality? I wonder what’s next? Nothing. More and more it’s an animal Waiting to be seen Faced with someone’s failures A sickening sight indeed, indeed, indeed In the beginning it seems that no one Thinks beyond the point of having fun. As ‘Wonder what’s Next’ by Chevelle plays on a car stereo the scene slowly moves outwards exposing the driver of the car to be Shocka. He is leaned up significantly because of the 2nd degree burns that cause his back to burn. He slowly nods to the music, as it seems to reenact everything that is going through his life right now. ‘I wonder what’s next…’ he slowly says with the beat of the song. ‘Nothing.’ As he breaks his focus on the road he shakes his head for a moment seeming to gain some sort of thought back. ‘Omega will have his revenge served to him on a gold fucking platter…he thinks that he can fuck with my head like, fuck with my life I am going to end him. I am the one that showed him everything that he knows…when he first came back to the RDWF he had no idea who he was he was left in a comma burdened by amnesia…I saw the perfect opportunity to use this oversized idiot as my personal henchman…it almost backfired in the NRCW but I got him back and he was under my control but now he’s fought his way out of it surprisingly I thought that he would get it within the first month but he just stuck with the thoughts that he was my best friend and protected me at all costs. So he finally after 2 years gets to knowing what the hell has happened and then he takes it out on me…it wasn’t all my idea…’ Shocka again slowly fades and looks down at the speedometer he slowly pushes the gas pedal to the floor and the car takes off, he shifts up to fifth gear and the car continues to go. He laughs wildly at the speedometer easily goes beyond 100 MPH, it doesn’t seem to phase him as it reaches about 110 a tire pops and the car jerks wildly to the side…he keeps it under control until the pull of the tire rips the car into a ditch at about 50. He slams against the steering wheel because he just had his lap belt fastened. He slowly takes off the seatbelt and gets out of the totaled car; he shakes his head and walks away slowly. ‘What in the hell happened to me?’ He asks himself in a sort of confused manner. He slowly spins to see the car in the ditch and he runs to grab his cell phone and his keys. He rips open the trunk to see his duffle bag he grabs it and slings it over his shoulder as blood runs into his eyes he stops dead in his tracks. He slowly moves away from the car realizing that he is hurt and he sits down on the side of the road with his phone. He calls 911 and within minutes help is dispatched his way. He sits to reflect upon what has happened in the last 72 hours and seems to come to peace with it. ‘Hmph…’ He stands shaking the dirt off of his pants as the cops pull up. One jumps out and hands him a rag to wipe the blood off his face. ‘How’d this happen?’ The officer asks with a pen and paper. ‘It’s a long story…’ Shocka says. ‘I got time…’ The officer says seeing that Shocka is visibly disturbed. ‘It all started about 2 years ago…’ he says as the scene slowly fades. Getting in touch with himself is the only way to make sure that he has made the right decisions throughout his life. He will now look into every detail of his life to make sure that this is what he wants in life…he no longer just wants to wrestle he wants to fight…he wants to fight 1 man and that man is Morpheus Omega.
