Becoming Different

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13th January 2003 The scene opens to the sounds of Faith No More, the calming soothing words rediscover the beauty of the song Easy, and somewhere in her state of bliss, Seara is hiding away from everything, the High Impact title laying on the floor in front of her. The shock of the death of fellow wrestler and old team mate L2J has snuck into Seara’s head, and is quietly bubbling away inside her as she thinks about her up and coming match with Sean Mason for the unification of the High Impact and Rising Star Title and the female is quietly captivating. Things are somehow so much different for the female than she would ever have wanted them to become, but changes have to be accepted, and life has to be continued, no matter how long it takes a person to realise life goes on and things change, they will inevitably come to realise this one fact, and when they have chose to realise it, they will eventually accept its life changing forces. Life had changed a few weeks ago for the female wrestler because of a conscious decision she had made of her own accord, and now, things had changed because life simply has a way of throwing new things at a person to make them cope with the feelings of humanity growing within them. Seara knew this, and she didn’t want to deal with it just yet, she had other things to do, she had accepted a long time that death was a simple and apparently important part of everyday life, and it had to be dealt with in whatever way possible. Seara’s career had always been her way to cope with the death, after her mothers death, she had focussed on her fathers career, and when she had become old enough to get in the ring and fight for a living, she had used her own career to delete the pain of death. Delete, a word normally found on the computer keypad or on the messaging section of a cell phone, but it was a word Seara had found herself using to describe the way she overcame things she didn’t want to feel or think about. She would simply delete what she wanted removed, and it would no longer be a problem for the female. It sounded somehow malicious that she could describe things in this way, or that she could even do this, but it was simply her uncontrolled emotionless way of dealing with her pain and anger. Her anger however would never be deleted, much like many computer programs, it needed an uninstall program, and Seara had removed it from her hard drive, making it impossible to ever remove the anger she carried deep inside. Seara looked up for the first time and remembered the cameras that had arrived at the house nearly an hour earlier. They had not set up or began to film for a long time, but when they eventually did, Seara was inaccessible, she had seemingly become lost within…within what was not certain, but it was according to Frost usual as of late to lose her for hours on end to her own thoughts. I would sit here for hours talking about L2J and his untimely and unfortunate death, but I will not pursue that topic just yet, or even tonight for that matter. Seara turns back to looking down, seemingly at the belt, but at the same time, her eyes seem to penetrate the leather and metal of the High Impact Title. Closing her eyes, she sits peacefully for a moment before reaching to her side and grabbing a packet of cigarettes. Last week I went one on one with Lee eXodus for the High Impact title, the winner of that match would then go on to face Sean Mason for the unification of the two titles. I went into my match against eXodus believing I would win, things have changed, my style is changing, and if people did not realise it last week when I won that title, they will certainly realise it this week when I take that title from Mason and join it with my own title. I know how egotistical it may sound, but I have deserved gold for a long time, and I am not about to jump with joy about the fact that I now hold it. Instead, I will fight harder, and prove that I am deserving of keeping this title. Seara seems to have little to say on the subject, and picking up the title from the floor, she gets to her feet and walks away from the camera before taking her place on the sofa where she calmly sits and once again slips into a state of bliss where she mumbles under her breath. I will show all of them, they will respect me, and I will rid Mason of that title, it will be mine. I will be the first holder of the unified title. The scene fades out as Seara rocks back and forth and repeats her words over and over.
