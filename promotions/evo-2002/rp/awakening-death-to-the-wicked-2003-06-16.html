Awakening - Death To The Wicked

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-16 Well it seems that Thug is still begging me to end his life. He doesn’t come right out and say this of course. Instead he tells me through his tedious and pointless actions. It’s not bad enough that I had the odds stacked against me in a handicap match, but the fucker has to be the referee. It’s not bad enough that he’s the dirty ref, but he has to attack me during the match. And God damn, if that’s not bad enough, he has to cost me my rematch. But that’s all right. Revenge is a dish best served cold. Few days have gone by. Isn’t that the way it always is? Things take place in the space of a day that piss you off being recognition, or that upset you to the point of tears, or that make you the happiest person on the face of the planet. Part of you, buried somewhere in that muscle you call a heart, rests a little innocent piece of your soul that thinks that moment is going to last forever. Jesus, will this pain ever go away?! Why did this have to happen? I hope this feeling lasts forever. The moment, as uplifting or aggravating as the seconds could be, always falls apart and drifts away. They always descend into the depths of our memories for us to carry with us everyday until the one we leave this world for good. It is for this reason, and I think this reason only, that I want to murder Thug roC. The thought of slitting his throat with a switchblade has crossed my mind a few times, as well as blowing his head off with a shotgun, brains splattering across the wall. Then again, I wouldn’t mind drowning him in a puddle of his own blood, or crushing his body inside one of them junk yard contraptions used for cars, or just simply tossing his ass off the roof of a skyscraper and letting gravity do the job for me. You see what this prick has done to me? You see what state of mind I have been forced to endure over the passed few weeks because of the things he has put me through? I thought at first that he would be a nuisance easily taken care of. Pushed aside of the line so the next person could step forward and take the challenge. But this bastard just won’t learn. It’s going to take something quite drastic. Death to the wicked son of a bitch. That’s how I see it, and that’s how the rest of the world should see it. Pompous prick.
