Life for Life... part two

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life for Life… Part Two! The two gunshots can be heard with the loud bang, the noise enough to make your ears ring and swell shut. Morpheus and the thuged out gangsta turn abruptly and see a police cruiser down the street. Two cops are behind the car with high powered rifles aimed and locked down, targeted on both men. Immediately following the gunshots screaming can be heard coming from the general direction of the police officers 'Freeeeeze... Drop your guns and nobody will be hurt. This is the police, I repeat. Drop the guns and no one will be hurt' The voice can be heard screaming. Morpheus shakes his head lowly, Morpheus holding the Oozy in his hands tight lets the gun slip down his finger turning around on it self and lets the gun drop to the ground. Morpheus slowly raises his hands partial way and the crack slinging, street thug points his gun directly at the police cruiser and starts unloading rounds. Morpheus doesn’t waste time and ducks and dives into the nearest alley opening. Morpheus looks back from the alley in the general direction only to witness the police sharp shoot the face and chest of the thug multiple times. Round after round piercing his body and he cries out in pain as he drops to the ground pouring blood from multiple holes in his body... Morpheus quickly throws his body back out of view of the police because he knew they would be coming for him soon. Morpheus rises to his feet quickly to run and stops dead in his tracks. Morpheus turns and runs back and grabs the thug stocky in appearance who’s the only one alive he knows of. Morpheus takes his gun and reaches in his coat and it disappears somewhere inside. Morpheus picks the man up over his shoulders and starts running down the alleyway as fast he can with a man over his shoulder. Morpheus stops around the end of the alley and dumps the guy off near a pile of boxes and kicks the massive stack over the man hiding his where abouts... Morpheus can hear the sirens around the surrounding area and can see flashing lights down the alleyway where he came from. Morpheus looks around frantically and finds an old, rustic looking fire escape and jumps and barely grasps it, falling back down. Morpheus jumps again grabbing the fire escape and clutches it tight. Morpheus pulls himself up with ease. Screeching can be heard from the weak, old metal and Morpheus doesn’t waste any time. Morpheus stops in front of a window and pries at the window to pull it up, he struggles and then back fist smashes through the window. The glass breaks and falls down below, the noise echoes through the alleyway. Morpheus dives into the darkened room, he sits in the darkness breathing heavy... Morpheus speaks to himself, his breathing still deep... 'What the fuck just happened back there. Stupid worthless street punks that thought they could have their way with me. Then one gets blasted in the throat. I drop another who knocks himself out. Then that bitch has to get in my face only for the men in blue to show up and blast him. Something is definitely wrong with tonight and myself, I think I’m slowly going insane for the thousandth time' The lights turn on blinding at first inside the room. A large white man stands in the doorway with redneck appearance facial hair with his old lady standing behind him with a pistol and looks not afraid of using it. The man standing in socks and briefs clutching a sawed off five gauge shot gun and his significant other wearing a housecoat with her hair all messed up. The man screams with a loud, deep tone of voice 'You have ten seconds to explain yourself or I swear you will be pumped full pound after pound of lead. Speak now!' The man snarls. Morpheus raising his hands showing he’s not trying to impose threat. Morpheus with that blank expression still on his face speaks like he could care less about the redneck couple’s veil threats. Morpheus speaks with calming, eased words. 'Interesting story really. First I almost got mugged by crack slinging, gangsta thugs. One of them shot their friend. I knocked out the other, then the cops showed up, they shot the other one. I disposed of the living one in the back alley. Climbed your fire escape and broke inside to find safe hiding... Now who the fuck are you?' Morpheus turns his head with a dirty look on his face. Both men stare each other down a long while with nothing being said. The straggly woman behind the man yells with ignorant slurred words 'Shoot the little piece of shit Roger' The man not taking his gun locked off Morpheus, both men breathing heavy. Roger speaks to Morpheus with a displeased tone of voice. 'So right now you have heat all around my place. My doors are probably going to get kicked in and have me sent to jail for ten years. You have found yourself in a bad way. You go march yourself back into the death trap waiting for yourself outside before I kill you myself' The man snarls again pumping the barrel of the gun loading it. Mechanical noises can be heard from within the gun The man staring down Morpheus with a sick look on his face, like he thinks he knows the first thing about the disturbed sense of evil. Morpheus instead of revolting in his sick tendencies or trading words of hatred he replies with a calm voice and laughs. 'You’re bluffing. You have to be bluffing because if you are going to go away for ten years as is and then you shoot me. You bring more heat on yourself because they have the immediate area surrounded and then you go away for life, even if its self defense or not. How you going to explain shooting a man without a gun to the judge when you are wanted on charges yourself?' Morpheus laughs to himself in his own self-pity, he replies again 'damn amateurs' The man snaps out of rage and runs at Morpheus and tears him up slowly by his coat. Morpheus rushes himself the rest of the way to his feet after Roger had struggled, Morpheus stands towering over Roger and speaks calmly once again. 'If you were smart, I would turn off that light about now or were all going away for a long time. If I made it up here, so can they.' Morpheus chuckles like he couldnt care the less either way. The man rushes to the light switch and flicks it off. Someone from within the room can be heard making a “Shhhhhhhhhh” noise and Morpheus speaks very softly 'They are down there right now. If they seen or heard us I really don’t know but they are definitely down there' Morpheus says quietly to Roger and his better or worst half, what ever the hell she is... Glass can be heard stepping on and voices can be heard from outside. One of the officers can be heard distinct and clearly from below and outside. 'This broken glass was not here this morning. This is where I fucked up that Listerine drunk for my morning exercise. See there’s his blood and empty mouth wash bottle there' The voices can be heard but muffled. Footsteps can he heard leaving the scene. Lights are turned on outside of the room and Morpheus and Roger can be vaguely made out. Roger hits Morpheus in the back with the butt of the shotgun and he screams 'Get the fuck moving before I stop caring and blast a whole in your back the size of a basketball. You understand?' Roger hits him in the back again but this time not as hard... The man snarls once again and Morpheus starts walking out into the other room where the man is directing him. The door shuts silently behind them leaving the scene in darkness. The scene ends still with mystery eluding itself once again. Where will this story lead the former deadliest athlete in the wrestling industry? Finding him self in more and more trouble the further he takes himself on his delirious journeys. Is Morpheus the same man he’s always been, the sick delusional fuck or is Morpheus giving up on hope, giving up his claimed and destined named titles. Is Morpheus Omega finally taking the big leap out of the only Reality he has ever known? In due time these unanswered questions will be drawn into conclusion... To be continued...
