Initial Shock

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th November 2002 After Legends XV on Wednesday night, Seara left the arena with a seemingly different attitude to the one she has had of late and throughout her time in eVolution. Although the female and her tag team partner Jason Jameson lost their semi final battle against New-TV, the female seemed somewhat satisfied and happy with her new found power. She knows she still has to wait for a shot at the tag team gold, but at the end of the night she helped in a spectacular display as all four Notorious members, including the injured L2J, took out the next possible tag team champions, and the rest of their stable. The Unlimited were left by Frost, Seara, L2J and Jameson in a mess in the ring with the human wrecking ball Sovereign lain out beside them, his Universal Gold leaving the ring in the hands of Notorious member L2J. This wasn’t the first or last thing the female was involved in all night. Out of the ring she was seen meeting with Scott Frost in their dressing room as the male wrestler questioned her about her disappearance during the previous week. The male wrestler showed his care and concern for the female and put across a point that she was to be careful and stay close to him. Seara however foolish she seems has no intensions of spending all her time reporting Frost about her whereabouts, but she respects that while the stable wars are raging high and dangerous, that she should be careful and should keep herself safer than she would normally be used to. During the coarse of Legends XV, Seara featured in the speech of “The Tru One” Joshua Krowe who entered the ring to discuss eVolutions ratings. He was quick at suggesting angles that specific wrestlers could use to bring up the ratings of the company. For Seara, he suggested that she admit her true love for Frost, and kick his wife’s ass. The female had laughed and shrugged it off in front of her teammate. (Now back at the hotel room of The Notorious, the female wrestler could not help stare at the 6’0 226lb male and think hard about everything that had happened to draw her into the stable and his friendship. She had always resisted friendship from new people, and had never felt trust or closeness like she did in the presence of Frost. It wasn’t to say that she had no trust for the rest of her team, but with Frost she sensed a strong feeling for him, something that drew her to him at the start, and something that was drawing her deeper in. she quickly began a friendship with L2J, and had seemingly eased off on the paranoia about everyone, accepting the figures around her, and the figures that surrounded them.) (The scene fades in as Frost ends his sentence. The camera pans around the room, and it is obvious from the mess on the table and the angry look in Frosts eyes that he has just been shouting and complaining about a fight or a mass destruction session. Seara looks up to him with an apologetic face; it suddenly becomes clear that Seara is our culprit. On the table lie the remains of all the 5 dinner chairs that had surrounded the table, all of them smashed into tiny pieces. They had only been smashed and broken that morning while Frost L2J and Jameson had gone down to the restaurant for a cooked breakfast to keep their energy up. Now Seara is sitting with a cigarette in one hand and blood covered knuckles on the other. Turning she watches as L2J and Jason Jameson walk out the room before she turns her attention to Scott Frost, she watches him for a few minutes but he is now busy talking on his cell phone, he is sat across the opposite side of the room, and the female cannot hear what he is saying, although she can tell something is strange around him.) Seara - Things have been happening that no one can explain. No one, never anyone, never an explanation, never a truthful answer, never the truth to start with… (Seara looks directly at Frost as she is speaking, smiling slightly as she begins to laugh at herself.) Seara - Legends XV, it was wrong and so right. The Omen Brotherhood should not have advanced but as always, Thug roC fucked me over. Every time he has been down to the ring when I have fought, he has prevented me winning. Now he and Omen Brotherhood must watch their backs carefully, I am not one for taking a screw job lightly, and I am fed up with Thug roC, he has found an enemy in me, someone who isn’t afraid to fight him back. I offer Thug roC the chance to get in the ring with me, one on one, no interferences allowed. No stables, just you and me, in a hardcore match, I know you have the height and weight advantage over me, but I am not an easy opponent, and I do not give up easily. You are only one of my few worries, but feel free to accept my offer. I will be waiting for you, and you won’t have to worry about finding me to accept my offer, I’ll be easily accessible. Destruction…destruction it’s all about the destruction… (Her final sentence has caught the ear of Scott Frost and he turns to look at her, putting his cell phone on the table, Frost walks over to Seara and sits down beside her on the sofa. He looks at the camera and then at Seara and urges her to continue with her thoughts; she stares at him while thinking about all the things that have been said between the two since they met.) Seara – Unlimited took one shot from us, it was our first blow. L2J proved that not even Sovereign could keep a Notorious member down. And when Unlimited battle back, we will be ready. (Seara pauses and lights another cigarette, then turning her attention back to the camera she focuses herself, coughs to clear her throat and takes a drink from a bottle of milk.) Seara - Joshua Krowe, you made a nice little intro for yourself. You pointed out nicely that you don’t normally come down to the ring. And we were honoured to have you grace the ring with your presence. (There is an air of sarcasm in the voice of Seara, but also of seriousness. For Seara, Joshua Krowe is not an important figure, she has no real need to know him, and she doesn’t know how to react to him, serious or in a joyful and carefree way. But Joshua Krowe has got her thinking about her real reasons for joining Notorious and the reasons why she became such good friends with someone in such a short time zone. Things that have happened since she had first meet Scott Frost had been different from the way they had always been.) Frost – You all right? Your thinking too much about what he said. Come on Seara. (Frost’s words seem to avoid the hearing of the female, and she sits staring at the floor below her feet.) Frost – Seara, Seara, listen to me, hey, hello, anyone home. Katherine, hello, pay attention. Katherine. (Seara jumps back into life and looks at Frost, no one outside her family has ever called her Katherine, she has been known as Seara, a girl who finds it easy to lose her bearings on reality and will react in uncontrollable and unpredictable ways to thinks that she does not like or find worthy of her attention.) Frost – Look, forget what he said, he is just trying to distract you; you need to be paying attention at all times. It may get a bit dangerous with Asis around after what we did, and I need you concentrating now, we have the PPV to plan for, and just coz your not getting tag gold doesn’t mean the Queen of Notorious can let herself be distracted, you still have other gold to go for. (Seara nods in agreement with Frosts statement, but seems to still be ignoring him; she is in shock after he used her real name, but not just because he used it, because of the way he used it.) Seara – Yeah the PPV, you’re right, I gotta focus, not let him… (Seara’s words suddenly begin to fade away as though she has forgotten what she was saying only a second before. She sits now just staring at Frost, their eyes locked onto each other.) It is hard to tell what is being thought, but as the scene begins to fade, the last thing we see is the pair moving closer and the sound if The Notorious hotel room door opening. The camera is completely black before anything is seen, and there is no noise to give hints toward what is happening.
