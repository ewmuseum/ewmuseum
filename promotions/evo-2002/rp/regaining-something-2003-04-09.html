Regaining something......

9 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9/03 'It wasn't supposed to be this way...' MLX is seen sitting with the Evolved title in one hand his head in the other.... 'He seeks to destroy everything, everything...just because he couldn't keep himself out of the spot...' The image starts to become staticy and distorted. The volume drops out as the picture is slowly replaced by an image of Malcolm King. King is sitting in his chair in front of the infamous bank of monitors used during early days of The Unlimited, behind him are the members of Tribute. Damien Legend and Priest RavensEcho. King:'This was the man I had intended to lead my army. Billy is clinging onto what little career he has left, sad really that his attempt to validate himself has turned into a career of co dependence and obsession.' Damien Legend:'You promised us Twisted Reality; when do we get them?' King:'What I promised you was the tag team champions.....Twisted Reality is not a team to take lightly or to be disrespected. You are in all honesty not ready for them....plus you want to eliminate the competition....The Body Snatchers, who have a major issue with Twisted Reality.' King turns his attention back to the monitors.... King:'And right now your problem is linked to my problem. MLX has hired the team of 1001 Holds and Dolo Bentswinger, former partners of mine.....and men responsible for breaking my back way back in the OWA. So it would seem the forces and numbers are against me. Trust me Billy.....I have a ton of past alliances from OWA, OSW, EDW here in Evolution that will assist me in eradicating you. My influence reaches very....very deep here.' Priest RavensEcho:'You've taken the first step, by cutting into his television time so he doesn't have time to spread his propaganda.' King:'It's time for truth...it's time for Evolution to know The secret of Malcolm King, Luther Wisdom, Xavier Citizen. The one man army...the Urban Legend. Luther Wisdom the young man of mystery seemingly a rookie; isn't it funny what a mask would hide? Or xavier Citizen...somewhat crazy a little off in his thinking and yes facepaint would emphasize this. Finally malcolm King sans mask, sans facepaint, who wouldn't know that three distinct personalities wouldn't share the same genesis? MLX...Billy you were an aspiring wrestler who couldn't find your niche...until I proposed the idea. Until I took you under my wing and told you not to worry....because I made Malcolm King a franchise. Much like the many faces of Superman.....once one man left the role, his shoes could be filled by another without missing a beat. You and Shadrick were next in line to step into the Malcolm King franchise.....you were the next one to start your career by carrying on that has already been established....' Damien Legend:'But you got impatient had to make your own mark, using King as the foundation....' Priest RavensEcho:' On Legends both you and the BodySnatchers will see the error of your ways...and you will realize why the man has always said he had an Urban Legend Wrestling NATION...' the scene fades and returns to MLX, who apparently has been unaware his signal never got out to the massess....he stands and walks away with title in hand.
