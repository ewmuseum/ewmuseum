The Contract Has Been Signed

28 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-28 It was a house show, but it was my debut. My music hit and I stepped out from the back more ready then ever. I thought to myself I am going in their to make an impact and to show everyone in eVo what I am about. When I stepped out I looked into the ring and saw some small foreign man. The disgust began their. I knew this wasn't going to be much. I made it to the ring and we imediatlly went at it. The little guy had some balls. I guess it made up for his size. He tried all kinds of high risk stuff, but in the end, it just wasn't enough. Since the match, everyone knew I was part of eVo and that it wasn't just another tryout match. They knew I was in their to destroy the competition. My phone has been ringing off the wall. The reporters just haven't gotten enough. All week it has been one of those leave me a lone type of things. That is when I decided to leave Charlotte for a little while. I packed my bags and headed to the airport once again. The plane landed and I was back in my old home of Pennsylvania. The airport was pretty crowded, which was kind of shocking. It's a weired airport though in Pittsburgh. They got a damn shopping mall right in the center of the airport. You don't see that too often. I walked into the store LIDS to pickup a new baseball cap and I saw a guy that use to work for me at the tattoo shop in the store. It shocked me, because I haven't seen anyone from the shop since like 1999 or so. We sat and talked for a little bit and I told him all about the wrestling thing. When I told him I was starting from the bottom again, it kind of shocked him. He kept saying hell I remember a year ago you where the main event on television. I told him buisness is buisness. After a while went on, I told him I had to get going. Said I had to catch a limo over to my parents house. He offered to take me their, so I took him up on his offer. That I should have never done. I walked outside and he opened the trunk of an old 69 Challenger. It's the same car he had when I owned the tattoo shop. The dust flew when he opened the trunk. You think the man would keep it clean, since he has been hanging on to it for so long. I joked with him and told him I was getting him a new car. He must of thought I was serious, because he pulled into a used car lot and said thats the car you can buy me. It was a 95 Mustang GT. I laughed, but he didn't think it was funny. I told him to open the trunk. I got my suit case out of the trunk and walked over to the owner of the car lot. I handed him a stack of hundreds and told him I wanted that car. The man looked shocked when I handed him $5000 in cash. He started the paper work and I stood outside talking to my buddy. The guy came out and said everything was ready, I just needed to sign some papers. I signed the papers and he handed me the keys. Right then I walked outside, opened the doors to the Mustang and started it up. I threw my suit case in the trunk and then hollered over to my buddy. I told him hey thanks for the ride man, I was going to drive from here. He was pissed. He knew I wasn't giving him the car. Hell, why should I? I made a joke and he took me serious. I drove to my parents house and pulled into the driveway. My mom came running out when she saw who it was. I looked at her and smiled. She said let me guess, your here to relax. I said yes mam. Now I am relaxing.
