Twisted Reality

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.06.03 ~Reality, reality is something that you can have an altered perception on a sort of Twisted Reality. A spin put on things so sick, so violent that no one could comprehend what was about to go down. This twisted Reality will take down eVolution by eliminating the three stars that are below Twisted Reality! Jameson, Sovereign, and Frost. Shocka, Omega and Night have never had a better opportunity to make a name for themselves than they have this coming Legends. Shocka and Omega have a storied history of being a tag team and it seems that Sky Night and Omega work well together. Hell you could go as far as to say that they are a well oiled machine ready to take out anyone that gets in there way.~ ~We are now swooped into the trashed hotel room of Shocka he is standing above all of it with his Universal title in his hand. He has a glass of orange juice mixed with Vodka in it in his other and he is smiling like no other he speaks to himself.~ ‘Its February 5th 2003 and I am here with the Universal title and am one of the best wrestlers in eVolution…’ Shocka says in a slurred tone. ‘This night is gonna be great I got all the booze I want and everything that I could ever need!’ Shocka yells as Omega walks in. ‘Hey man!’ Omega screams with a little joy in his face. Its one of the first times we have seen this. ‘You got anymore of that Russian imported vodka?!’ He screams as he stumbles into the room. ‘Nah man I finished it with this shit…’ As he finishes he sentence he drops the glass and it slams down with orange juice spilling everywhere. He just stands there like a guiltily little kid looking down at the mess with a sort of innocent look on his face. ~Sky Night now barges into the room and pushes Omega onto the couch in Shocka’s room she looks at the orange juice soaked floor and then at Shocka and rolls her eyes.~ ‘Come on guys! We have to get focused sure its fun to celebrate and all but you two are fucking wasted.’ ‘Shut up…I’m the damn Universal champion I can do whatever I want!’ Shocka screams in a slurred tone. ~He makes a face at Sky and pushes over a chair and smiles she rolls her eyes and walks over and picks it up. Shocka laughs in his altered state of mind and pushes it over again Sky picks it back up.~ ‘Stop it!’ She yells at him. ‘Shaddap!’ Shocka yells with a hint of rage in his voice. ‘Yeah Sky come on get down with the sickness…’ Morpheus cuts in getting off of the couch. ‘I mean we are going to show everyone what we are made of next week.’ He says. ‘Oh shit…’ Sky speaks. ‘What?’ Omega asks as he looks up at Shocka who is dancing on a glass table. ‘Get down!’ Sky yells. ‘Fuck you, you know who I am?!’ Shocka yells and drops the Universal title and it slams onto the table and sky runs and picks it up and shakes her head. ‘You two are worse then fucking 3 year olds!’ ~Shocka jumps off the table and walks over to the couch and picks Omega up he throws him onto the couch in a belly to belly suplex.~ ‘See!’ Shocka yells. ‘HAHAHA!’ Morpheus laughs as he stands up and Slaps Shocka. ‘What bitch!?’ Shocka pushes Omega and they laugh. ~Omega reels back to hit Shocka but Shocka tackles him onto the couch and Sky runs and pulls them off of each other.~ ‘GUYS!’ Sky yells. ‘Just because you bastards are drunk doesn’t mean you should fight!’ ‘But its fun!’ Shocka yells. ‘Yeah!’ Omega screams. ~The scene fades on Shocka and Omega in a twisted state of reality as Sky tries to keep both of them in control.~
