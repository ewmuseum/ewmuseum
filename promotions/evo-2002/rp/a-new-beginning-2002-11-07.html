A new beginning?

7 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.7.02 'This is it...' Thug roC speaks to Shocka. 'Its your oppurtunity to prove everyone wrong to show that you have the skills to make it to the universal title and beyond.' He says smiling 'The road to glory...' Shocka speaks with question in his voice 'But I don't want to forget about the Continental title. And now I have to continue in the tag title tournament against Jameson and Seara...' 'Get over the continental title.' T roC says in a very harsh tone before continuing. 'Its your time to shine, your better than that damn title and when are you going to realize that...the sooner you do the sooner you get to the Universal title.' 'What about the tag titles?' Shocka questions. 'I mean I am going into the 3rd and almost last round for them and the second round in the road to glory tournament.' 'Don't worry about New-Tv' Thug roC says 'thats my job.' The scene begins to dimminish as another picks up. Shocka holds about 75 pounds of weight and is curling it to his chest. You can see that its not that difficult he is just toning his arms. 'Come on enough with the muscle man image, we've got to work on your speed.' Thug roC says smiling. 'You and me in the ring...' A laugh comes. 'The best cruiser weight in the business, me vs well you...' 'The best continental champion that this federation has ever seen. And the future unviersal and tag team champions. Imma crush you roC!' 'You've gotta catch me first!' Thug roC jumps into the ring, Shocka follows. Shocka bounces off of the ropes but Thug roC evades it by hitting the mat. Shocka jumps over him and hits the opposite side ropes. He goes to slide feet first into thug roC but Thug roC jumps up and over him. Shocka slides all the way outside the ring. Thug roC backs up to get some speed and runs at Shocka diving over the ropes onto Shocka. Shocka catches him and lifts him over his head he throws him into the ring. Thug roC lands on his back hard and rolls through to his feet. Shocka gets onto the apron and jumps to over the top rope. Thug roC now drop kicks him throwing him into the ropes and bringing him toward roC. But roC drops in time to trip up Shocka allowing Shocka to smash face first into the ring. Thug roC jumps up and climbs to the top rope. As Shocka gets to his feet thug roc dives off but Shocka ducks and thug roC smashes hard into the ground. Shocka now grabs roC by the head and sinches in a headlock. he lifts thug roC and releases the hold now throwing him over his head causing thug roC to land face down. Shocka rolls him over and covers him 1...kickout. thug roC quickly jumps to his feet and speaks. 'You're speed is improving.' Thug roC jumps from the ring 'But you're still not fast enough...' 'How fast do i have to be to beat Sovereign?' 'HOw quickly you forget...L2J is the co-champion and most of people in the tournament are almost cruiserweights. And hell L2J is...' 'Bah.' Shocka gruns before leaving out of the ring. 'Guess I'll have to show off my technical skill to ground the cruiser weights.' 'Or...you could try and get faster.' 'Listen roC...you like cruiserweights. But you knew when you signed me that I am basically a powerhouse...' Shocka sighs before speaking again. 'You just need to find another light weight client to take the reigns of the high impact title...' FADE
