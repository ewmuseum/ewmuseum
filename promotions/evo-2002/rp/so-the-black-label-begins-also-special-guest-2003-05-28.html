So the black label begins..(Also special guest)

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-28 Wisdom The label of something is what represents it. When something is labeled it usualy stays forever. A label is something of meaning. A black label is one of the X. The X,the unknown man who reaches the top. The man who goes against all odds to further his and his peoples caus. The man that is a more than a question, the man who is the black label..X X The scene fades in as we are standing outside of eVo headquaters. It seems that the camera crew is waiting on one of the Cell Divison fighters. As about a minute has gone by the camera crew has spotted a black eclipse pulling in to the parking lot. The camera crew gets to rolling as the man then steps out of the car. It seems to be Questions Beyond X,we who call X, he begins to walk towards the eVo building. He is wearing some baggy blue jeans with a t-shirt. The white t-shirt is one of his personal items as it has a bold 'X' on the front with a quote that reads 'This aint wrestling...It's fighting'.He walks past the eVo cameras and into the building. The cameras are right behind him. He goes to one of the secretarys who then give him directions where to go. He nods his head and continues to walk down a hall. As he finally reaches the door he was told to go to. He opens to door an sees that there are two chairs and a man standing in front of one of them. The man is not of eVo but a man that X wanted to conduct the interview. The mans damn is Earl Hughs. He is a older black man that is probably in his early twenty's.. Earl goes over and shakes X's hand. The two men then go and sit down in the chairs awaiting for them on the set. Earl then begins the interview. 'Well..'Earl begins his questions as he is very nervous. He took a long pause after his first word and started back over and finished his sentence.'Well X, I want to first thank you for letting a man like myself be here to conduct this interview.' We learn with a back ground check that Earl Hughs has been dieing to be a journalist. He went to college two years at Flordia State . He had a full ride there because of his academic grades in high school. But After his second year during the summer he made a big mistake in his life, he got his girlfriend Tracy pregnant. Him and Tracy though are now married but where just dating at the time. Well this caused a demand of money for the two young couple so Earl has to go out and find a job to make some money. Well he found out that just a high school education doesnt go far. Now though Earl his taking night classes in a small college in Flordia so he can finaly complete his major in journalism.All of the background check with this information was found in a letter that Earl written to X after he saw his interview on last weeks Legends. The camera looks at X as he smiles and says'No problem' to Earl.Earl then gets better composure of himself as he asks X his first question.'X, how do you feel about being in Cell Divison.' X looks at him and smiles a bit. 'Well Earl, X has been asked this question before but X does not mind answering it again. Cell Divison is perfect for X. As X is a fighter not a wrestler. Thought it may not hold true for all the others who plan on getting in that cell. Before they do though they need to ask themselves if they can take it?If they can take a beating? Personaly X thinks that none of these Cell Divison fighters are really fighters. I look at them is low class wrestlers. But before you all can jump the gun and ask X where does he get off saying this. Its all because X knows all. You will come to realize the truth of this matter as the months and months come to pass. But realize that what X does in that cell is one that will be remembered but want be forgotten.'X pauses as his voice gets a little bit lmore agressive while he continutes to speak. 'The Cell Divison needs to take note that X is more than a man fighting for himself, but for his people. That the cell will not be able to contain X no matter what man they place X against, X will rise above all..and finally make everything right.'Earl looks at x as he ask another question.'X,Why do you refer yourself to X. I mean you dont use I or me or anything like that?' X smiles with a cocky attitude says'Because X is more than just a normal man. X is more than a man. Commen everyday people reffer to themselves as he or she or me or whatever else but X is not a common person. X is here to make a difference , to start a movemant. Your mind like most others in eVo will not be able to comprhend that fact that X leads a better caus for the world. As X is here to stop the terrible reign of corperate america. What better place to do it all then inside the Cell Divison? There is no better place as the way X sees it is there are no rules. There is only one winner when a man enters that ring. Its a break from what corperate america has offered you so far in eVo. True it has been established before. But let X ask you a question Earl, why did it fail?' Earl looks confused as he replies'Well X, I just dont know'.X smiles and says'Well Earl lets looks at the one big fact. It makes no difference if talent wasnt there because if they wanted to people would fill the card up no matter where they had to find em. X will let the only truth of this manner be exsposed to all of eVo. It is because of corperate america that it shut down. So before you people disagree or tell X that this was before the time of X so the knowledge of why would not be available to X.Then go ahead say it, cuz X is not listening. X sees the truth unlike the world eVo forces you all to live. The one where you are forced to be the role model. Lets rephrase that,the role model for uper class america. Damn, the facts are all there. X has seen em long enough, hell X grew up watching corperate ameriacas champions allways different people but the same layout,if you know what X means.' Earl is shocked at what X says.Earl looks at him and ask another question'So you believe that corperate america favors to have a white role model?' Instead of the camera focusing on both men it zooms in closer on X.X looks directly into the camera as he speaks.'You tell me Earl?Damn it you tell me how many job offers you have gotten?Why do you think X asked you for this interview. X is starting the movemant, the change. How many world champions have been black?Tell X?How many?MAke it better for X. How many have you known to be the leader of a federation. To be the market tool, to be the one credited for its glory for being its leader. Now it is not a lack of black wrestlers but a lack of will by my people. Not every man like X has the will to keep fighting. Not every man like X has the oppurtunity that I have. Dont you see it? Corperate america tries to run this place to it better suits there culture..not ours.'X pauses as he feels very strongly about this topic. He takes a deep breath and keeps talking.'The black label..thats what X represents.The Black label..the label is the one that will be the leader..as X was afraid to use the word role model. Afraid that my people would miss the direction that X is starting all because X was being compared as a role model. Lets be frank with it, no man here fights for a real caus. No man here fights for more then themselves. They all are here because they want to be looked upon as role models. You see a role model is a icon so to speak. One man that stands out far from the crowd. The one that the crowd listens to, the one that corperate america loves. Where X,the black label..no difference the his people. X stands with them in the center..in their hearts..while the role model stands on top of everyone..X stands with them..with his people..' Earl nods his head as he looks at the card for next legends. He begins to speak'X, it says here Cell Divison his getting its start in eVo on the next legends..It also says you will be fighitng in the first match..against the man named Ali Khadafi.' X never turns to looks at Earl as he is still focused souly on the camera. He does not show no emotion as he speaks.'Ali a man of my people.'He pauses as he shakes his head no. He then starts back.'X would of known that corperate america would do a thing like this. Take the black label and place him against one of his people. Well Ali, X is sorry but for the movemant to continue you have to understand..what X will do to you..well X can only appoligies a head of time.' Earl shocked once more but he hurries with yet another question.'Why are you apologiezen?Its a part of your job? X shakes his head no as he speaks.'Young earl,you like the rest who X is sure are confused. Look at it like this, X signed with the Cell Divison because X wants to make it all right. The Black label is here because of the simple fact it has finaly reach the new era. Ali, you will have to understand the the black label is X, your skills your views are no more than any other of our people who have tried and failed. X has no doubt you are proud of your heritage but X has doubt in your will. Assured to the fact that X believes you would sell out. Or the fact you would give up against the corperate america and just remain yourself to being at the bottom of the food chain.'He pauses'the fact is there though..you are one of X people..for tha is why the black label..is sorry.' Earl nods his head yeas and continues witht he questioning.'Well I..'He is interrupted as a man walks in the room. We can tell this because of the slamming of the door. The cameras never turn around as they were instructed to keep the cameras on the set.X then cocks a smile as he stands and speaks.'Earl, your time is done so please leave.'Earl stands up and shakes his hand as he walks out the room. X stands in front of the camera with it only being him on the set. He looks at the camera as the other man behind the cameras has yet to be seen. 'A man with his mentor. Everyone has always had the one to give them knowledge. The one to look up too for advice. Well X to has a manager. A mentor to go with for questions that X needs answered. That man is here.'The man then steps out from behind the cameras as we see it is Malcolm.He stands right beside X as he says only three words.'The Movemant begins..' The camera then stays on the two as X finishes up the interview by saying'Ali you have no choice of winning or losing. You though will soon find out what it is like to fight against not another man..but X. X represents the black label, something X is assured you only dream about. Something that you could only have wished of achieving. But like X said earlier, your will is just not strong enough. Everything will come to equal out in the future..but why now?Its simple..the movemant is why..the X is why. The time of role models is over..The time of the black label..it has just begun.' The camera fades to black with Malcolm and X standing on the eVo set.
