Nice golden globes

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-01-29 Los Angeles, California The Golden Globes Awards Albeit a brief break from the daily ring, the promotional tours never end for the worlds greatest tag team, and no it isn’t those losers from the WWE, its Big Daddy Dreamer and The HeartBreak Chick who despite greeting friends here, aren’t in the greatest of moods. They worked for six long months to regain the tag titles, only to be screwed out of them again…..but alas, hope, light at the end of the tunnel, the devil themselves have been released from The Syndicate and baby……its gonna be brutal!! So here we are, BDD all dressed in Armani and HBC vamping it up backstage for the press and photographers, seizing the opportunity to address several things before a worldwide audience for Evolution… BDD: So we aren’t the champions, but dammit, we shall be again very very shortly after we and I personally finish some longstanding business. The first is Daniel Somers returns, that evil backstabbing bastard. Everywhere we go people ask us what do you think of Somers, what will he do against you, and so on and so on. Somers is a bastard who cannot under any circumstances ever be trusted. He will shake your hand holding a knife in the other waiting to stab you. Fortunately for us and for him, we all know it. However, Somers is also a businessman who loves ratings, because more ratings equal more money for him. So he will do whatever is in a financial best business sense. Just like us. Truth is, we are cut from the same mold. We do what is in our own best interest and nothing more. His and my best interest is to see that megalomaniac Tyler Davidson put in his place. After all we can’t have two pompous committee members running things around here. So…..its like this, Somers knows we are money in the bank, he knows and would delight in watching me destroy Davidson and that is what will happen. But afterwards, after Davidson looks like a wreck on the 101, then its only a matter of time before he tries……tries…..to stab us in someway beneficial. But this week, we get an early Tainted Love present. Now we all know Tainted Love is my favorite PPV of the year. It’s the time of year when I perform the best and headline it. This year will be no exception. Tainted Love….. Davidson and Dreamer, one on one, retribution for his actions. Retribution for trying to eliminate The Syndicate once and for all, retribution for making a mockery of the division we built from the ground up. Oh yes, this week he gets a little preview of the hell he will be put under as I stretch his dead carcass, feeling his bones snap, the veins pop in his head, his vertebrae crackle, watching in such delight as he taps, taps, taps, taps, screaming to be let go!!! Oh yeah, its gonna be sweet!! Perhaps he should ask Monica Angel how it feels! But I know you heard enough of my rantings and want to see Miss HeartBreaker, so without further adeu, the greatest cruiserweight champion in the business, HBC. HBC: Oh D, you are too kind. Absolutely true, but kind none the less. Yes, this week is going to be historic because finally, over a year in the making, Thug Roc can no longer finally duck me. We have a bit of history going back, he made a name for himself costing me my High-Impact Championship, interfering in the match. He interfered time and again, helping Quasar the Great trying to defeat me, and unfortunately, our schedules never crossed paths. I must say…….. how convenient. How ironic that he goes around boasting that he is the best damn cruiserweight in the industry, yet has never faced me. Afraid Thuggy? You should be. Yet, it is mildly amusing to watch him fawn over me like a dog in heat. Afraid to face me? Of course you are. Any idiot can see what you are doing. Your sitting their thinking, damn…… if I can get HBC to be my woman then perhaps, she’ll never want to face me and forget all the things I’ve done in the past to her. Then my legacy might be preserved and she won’t kick my ass all over the place. But how wrong you are. You see Thuggy, you were right on something…… I do want you. That right, I can admit it. I want Thug Roc, I want him bad, I want him in the worse way. I want him so bad that I lay awake at night thinking about what I’m going to do to you. Now, not to over inflate that ego of yours, its purely physical! I want to lock up with you and beat your stupid ass all over the ring. I want to kick your head off and punt your nuts through the uprights for a 50 yard field goal. See Thuggy, its been a long time coming, the one person whom deep down you know is just as good if not better than you, and has her undivided attention focused solely on you. Be careful what you asked for Thuggy, you wanted me and my attention…… you got it, now, god help you from avoiding it…… Legends, your mine, and you will find out that I am a very very giving girl. Just not the kind you’d like……
