History of One :: X

12 January 2003

Date: 1/12/03 A year and two months. Thats how long I stayed on that program I had for myself. It worked out in the end, I grew to about six foot and I was pretty thick. Mostly muscle and I was pretty strong. I was happy with the results in the end. No one really bothered me, I even got to know the girls that worked the desk, and some of the people inside. Most weren't very friendly, someone my age doing what they are doing at the age they were at, and getting further than them. They had no reason to be happy with me. They didn't have to jump to whatever conclusion they were at either. I guess thats just how things go these days. I wasn't worried about it.. After the time I had been working out, I wanted to put myself to use. I wanted to figure out what I could do with my life.. I had no idea, everything was just so blah to me. I knew I had strength to work in a warehouse or something like that, but I also could have gotten into underground fighting or unlicensed boxing. Either one of those I could have handled, atleast in my opinion I could have. Overall, I knew I had a handle on my life, and I wanted things to start getting into line. Right now was the point in time I had to make up my mind on what I wanted to do, and how I was going to do it.. I wanted to make money, but I wanted to be able to do everything I had worked for, for a year and two months... I still worked out as I tried to figure out what I was doing. I still put my body to the test each and every day.. Than it occured to me.. Wrestling. I seen it on TV, I knew a bit about it.. I find a cheap school, and a part time job, I could make my way through it. How hard could it be? I was in for a harsh reality when I found Deacon Triage.. Yeah, you may know him as the MMA fighter over in Japan, maybe you don't.. He taught me how to fight.. And he was cheap, and only about ten minutes from the shelter. 'Who are you?' He glared down on me, the guy was massive.. 'Lance..' I didn't know what else to say, I wasn't scared, but he was going to be an instructor. I had to respect him. 'Yeah? How can I help you Lance?' He knew what the answer was going to be, but I guess he figured he should ask anyway. 'I want to learn how to fight.' I cuaght a glare from him the second I said that.. I dunno, maybe it was the way I said it, but he didn't like it, and it was obvious. 'Fight? You understand that fightring is an art form more or less, its not about kicking someone's ass, its about training your body, and becoming a stronger person. A person of many levels and talents... Can a young man like yourself handle that?.. Lance..' I just looked at him for a few moments, I didn't know what to say. He smirked a bit and went to walk away.. 'Yes, I can handle it.. How much?' He turned around and looked me right in the eye, he inched closer to me. 'Where you from?' What? I was going to get the whole twenty questions now? 'Asbury, New Jersey..' He nodded, 'What about family? You here with them?' I didn't know how to answer that, so I was just honest with him, 'I don't have a family. I live in the shelter down the road..' He looked puzzled. 'A shelter? How old are you?' 'Seventeen.' I shrugged. 'And you live in a shelter, want to learn martial arts.. Hmph.. How do you expect to pay for this?' He looked at me out of the corner as his eye, as he went to turn around again.. 'I'll find a way..' He shook his head, obviously disappointed.. 'Come back tommorow, and we'll talk..'
