Date: 5-11-03 After what appears to be an awaking for Hedges of sorts we find him claiming to be going back to his roots of evil and deranged actions. Perhaps he meant the take no shit attitude of pre resurrection days of the post inhumane sick twisted killer of post resurrections days…perhaps a healthy median in between; only time will tell because Hedges is simply a stick of dynamite with a light fuse… The scene opens up later that night with Hedges now walking alone on the streets on downtown St. Louis. He appears to have sobered up a little but still can’t seem to walk in a straight line. He stops and looks at the paper machine; the headlines read STORMS RIP THREW MIDWEST. He stares at the machine… “Pshh…who cares if I rained a bunch around here. Some pretty obvious reporting right there. What’s a little rain going to do to somebody? Melt them?” he yells to himself. He pulls out a cigarette and lights it up and fumbles for some quarters in him pocket. He pulls out a wad of cash and a bit of change. He only has a few pennies and a quarter. He shoves it back in his pocket and turns to walk away but flips around and shoves his foot threw the front of the paper machine. He pulls one out and opens it up smoking and glancing at the headlines… “It’s all crap!” he yells He flips out his zippo and lights the paper on fire. He shoves it in the machine as the stack catches on fire. The machine goes up in smoke and flames come out the front as Hedges walks away as if the fire never happened… “Wednesday is going to be a much better day. The headlines will read HEDGES VICTORIOUS AGAINST ALL ODDS. Handicapped or not I’ve beaten Lee and Xero’s time has come. No one cost me matches and gets off without suffering the consequences. Who does he think he is messing around in my business. That’s a crime that requires capitol punishment. It’s just the time to serve out some justice Hedges style. If they think because I’ve been in a slump I can’t step back on top on the mountain then surely they jest. Underestimating me will be their last mistake.’ He says to himself He makes a turn into an ally and steps into a puddle and shakes his foot off and keeps a walking. A piece of paper sticks to his foot but he doesn’t notice and keeps walking. It flaps with each step and a cat runs out from behind a dumpster and pounces of the back of his foot and rolls around swatting the paper. Hedges stops and looks down at the cat. Without hesitation he pulls his foot back and kicks the cat against the wall. It falls to the ground. It’s light orange colored cat, extremely thin from lack of food and probably a life spent surviving in the alleys. Starts to move and tries to limp away with a look of pain in it’s eyes but Hedges walks up to the cat and kicks it back across the alley into the dumpster… “Much like Xero, that sneak attacking pussy won’t be able to run in from behind and cost me the match. They’ll have to do it on their own, face to face. You my furry little bastard will just have to suffer the same fate for screwing with me” He spits on the cat and kicks it back across the alley and into the wall as it smacks the ground and lays there motionless as Hedges turns and walks back down the alley whistling the turn from “crazy train” as the scene fades to black
