Awaiting the Big Night

31 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-31 The sun has risen outside in London, England and people are out and about taking care of their daily business. Some have something really special on their mind however that makes them near giddy with anticipation when they think about it. In just a few hours they will witness what is bound to be one hell of an Evolution event, X-Rated. One person seen scurrying around, trying her best to get herself ready for the night is the newer eVo signee, Brianna Washington. She crosses a small street and makes her way to a park loaded with jolly kids and parents. She finds an empty bench and takes a seat with a newspaper in one hand and a cup of hot coffee in the other. She places the coffee down momentarily as she unravels the newspaper, trying to read….but her eyes won’t focus. After several more tries she gives up and places it down beside her, picking up her coffee. “No matter what I do I can’t focus on anything today.” She says to herself as she takes a gulp of the steaming coffee. “The thought of tonight has got me all twisted inside. This very well could be the factor that gets me a permanent job or not. So I have no choice but to win. I already know that’s going to be too much trouble. Come on now…the guy I’m facing is a monster compared to myself. He’s seven foot three hundred something pounds. If we wanted to be factual here, he’s three times myself! This only furthers my belief that this is the big night for me. Why else would they set me up with a monster? What better way to prove my worth than to beat Goliath?” She pauses to take a sip of her coffee. Instead of continuing up right away she decides that a stroll around the park will help her exert some of the energy built up. She gets up and steps onto the paved trail and starts her walking, weaving through people along the way. “The only one who is truly going to care about my first eVo PPV match is myself. The fans in attendance aren’t there to see me prove myself. They are there to see the eVo veterans such as Xander, Asis, and Shocka. I’m more like an snack before the big meal, just a mere appetizer. So the only back up I have for this big night in my history is myself. That’s why I just have to take a moment to clear my mind and focus on the night at hand. It’s going to take a hell of a lot of planning and thinking to come out of tonight in one piece.” She cuts around more people and makes a quick turn into another crowd of people. We lose focus on her and the scene fades to black….
