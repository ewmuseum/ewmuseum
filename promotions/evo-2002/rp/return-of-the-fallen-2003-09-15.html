Return of The Fallen

15 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-15 Meddlesome thoughts roll throughout my head as I sit here and watch the world pass by me this day. I feel empty to it, as if I living outside the common box that people associate with their day-to-day lives. It’s truly interesting to sit and watch your reflection form in those around you. Though, my reflection is somewhat different. It appears darker than most, shadowed by my past, eclipsed by hatred, and blacked with vengeance. To have so much time pass since my initial hatred, I can not bare to release this power that builds with my rage. “The Fallen Hero,” they sometimes called me as I never prevailed in my life, or never really had lasting accomplishment rather. Now, I sit just to be discarded as another worthless pawn on some other Hero’s plight. And so my dark revelation begins again…. A gray hue shines down upon an altar within an old torched stone church. Moss grows wildly against the widowed walls of the forgotten church. A shadow moves within the darkness, the Forgotten Hero, who moves stealthy to avoid true detection. On the altar rests a maul; the hilt covered in ancient Gallic all the way to a steel head, where in the center, etched deep into the stone a Celtic Cross lies. Eclipsed in the still darkness a figured encloses on the altar slowly circling the altar. He extends his hand on the hilt and slowly carcasses it before stopping it the furthest reach on the handle. An all too familiar voice echoes throughout the silence night as if it were cutting deeply into souls of men. “There was a time when my physical strength had nothing to do with my only means of survival, but then I was changed. It would seem as if I were left for dead in mental and physical anguish while others reaped about my former life, and then I was feared.” Tracing the maul he continues, “My vengeance was swift and my hatred multiplied. The more revenge I sought, the darker and blacker I had become. And here I stand, yet again. Thought to be your final resting place, Vexsul, but it would seem that the darkness in my heart shall not permit me peace.” “It is a strange fate that I must suffer so much hatred and pain for such trivial things. Pride, honor, and dignity…a few things I no longer care for. This time let them see the true Fallen Hero,” he says gripping the maul by the hilt and slinging it over his back. Our Hero rushes to his dying breathe, one Sweet Release, one final jest.
