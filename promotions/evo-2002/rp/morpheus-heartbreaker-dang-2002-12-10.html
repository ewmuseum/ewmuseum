Morpheus HeartBreaker (DANG!)

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-10-02 Paris, France BDD’s Penthouse Apartment Evening Rise of the Legends was a showcase of premier talent, and premier violence. Up and down the card, everyone gave their hardest to entertain the fans, pushing up buyrates to new levels for a wrestling federation. Especially the Battle of Toronto. I’m sure everyone thought Miss HeartBreaker had this match in the bag, knowing it was her specialty match, but in the immortal words of Gomer Pyle......’Surprise! Surprise! Surprise!’ One Dreamanator on the cold hard concrete down in Union Station and it was all over. But then suddenly, without warning, I’m struck down by a car. Deja Vu man! Of course HBC was quick to pass the buck saying it wasn’t her, that she had nothing to do with it, shifting blame over to Quasar of all people. For arguments sake, lets say you were right and it was him, he would have ran you down while you were unconscious on the street. So no, it wasn’t him, but none the less, the matter is being looked into by my crack security staff. I expect an answer shortly and if it turns out it was you, or that you had a hand in it, sever repercussions will follow. Now this week, you appear to be teaming with The Omen Brotherhood. Another psychotic cult under the influence of Xavier Citizen, Malcom King. Who is it you have, Morpheus Omega? Wasn’t he the guy from the Matrix? And after watching his little rant, he doesn’t know Kung Fu either. Seriously C, where the hell do you dig up these retards! Now I’ll admit, Sean Mason is clearly out of his element and I don’t expect him to do much other than to take a beating, wearing them down and hot tagging me to clean house. I know what you can do C so I’m not going to waste my air-time dealing with you. Morpheus on the other hand, the so-called ‘most dangerous man in wrestling’ doesn’t impress me much. Frankly your just another jacked up steroid tatooed freak whose mental capacity has been clearly warped by your boss. And speaking of your boss, how has he been since I whupped him royally back in EDW? Ah well, no matter, your probably here to do what he couldn’t, but sad matter is, you won’t be able to do it either. See Morpheus I actually respected your boss. He was an interesting cat, but you, your boring. I’d rather watch another Scott Steiner boring rant than hear you open your yapper trying to be all smart, saying your going to destroy us and rule this fed. *Cough* *Cough* Yeah right. I swear if I had a dollar every time I heard that I could by Syndicate International ten times over! Get some new material would ya! Wow! I’m so impressed you can hit a bag. Whuppy do! A bag doesn’t hit back, but I do Morpheus. Not only do I hit back, I hit back extremely hard and have taken out the best, baddest, and brightest in the business. Frankly Morpheus, other than an unusual name, your just another wannabe pretender trying to make a name for himself at my expense. Well buddy boy, its not going to happen. Sure you have C with you, but she trust The Brotherhood about as much as I do. So yeah Omega, this Thursday is going to be real interesting. Me and Mason against Unlimited/Brotherhood. Dang! One more time for my dear friend Ron Simmons who just retired from the ring recently .......DANG!.........
