Lurking Death Part 8

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-05-15 What are dreams? What do these things really mean to us? Why do we even have them? Everything that our body does seems to have some purpose to our own well being. Whether we know about that purpose or not is not the true question. After the previous nights sleep I had found myself for the most part of the day wondering about that very same question though. What purpose did dreams serve in the benefit of our body and most of all in our brain? As far as my own knowledge could see they truly did not serve any purpose to have. At times the thought of them could be enough to make a small child not want to sleep at night. There was never any telling of what kind of creatures or what sort of evil might have lurked in the sleep filled nights of the people. Children of course were usually the only ones who would ever realize something of this kind however. Adults might have them but were often to stubborn to admit fear to themselves and as a result would simply brush their dreams off as eating the wrong food before bed or a drunken hangover perhaps. It’s the children though, that perhaps in reality do know better than the parents who to them are often times the smartest people alive. It’s usually the parent who washes the fears of the children away with the false hopes of safety and serenity in their time of sleep. There were no people of the sort to do those things for me on that day so long ago. There were no wishes of pleasant or sweet dreams being sent my way on that night. The whole thing my dreams had brought me on that night was tidings of darkness and a morning after of much doubt and bewilderment. Not much had changed about the condition of S. “disappearance” as Jess was worried sick. Then on the other hand I had Heather constantly badgering me about being on the road on a regular basis now. The past week since FTW’s cowardly attack I’ve been trying to figure out who they were while also keeping an eye out for any sign of S. Nothing had turned up on either front and we headed to New York City to visit his wife Jess. She was obviously not in the best of condition not having any clue where her husband was at now. The stay didn’t last long for me though as I had to leave the next morning on a flight out to California. Heather was not at all supportive of me leaving but I had gone anyway while making it clear to her that she was to remain in New York with Jess. Now though I was finally in my hotel at Sacramento lying in bed for a restless night of no sleep. Sadly though, that morning sleep did not come very easily for me anymore. Perhaps it was because of all the arguing with Heather, or the disappearance of my best friend but something had certainly stressed me out to the point my dreams were full of visions of the damned. I could never be sure as they all went by so fast that I could hardly makes heads and tails of it until the end. What I saw at the end though didn’t appear at first to be anything even remotely related to the previous visions. The end seemed at first glance to be something so much more peaceful I guess you would say. It seemed a vision of warmth and friendship but then it all turned dark and blurry. Things happened and what I saw in front of my eyes chilled me to the bone that night to the point that the good nights sleep turned into a very sleepless night. I was frightened to even close my eyes again for the fear of simply saying the same visions in front of me. The visions of death and pain that I had seen were not something I wanted to think of. No the visions that came to me that night I had thought were better left a distant and locked away memory. That’s what I had told myself over and over again as I leaned over the bathroom sink with water dripping down my face and staring at myself in the mirror. My blood shot eyes peering into my very own soul as I looked forward into the deep recesses of my mind. At least that was the way things had seemed to me though as I stood there trying to shake the images from my mind. What I was really doing of course was trying to convince myself that what I saw and the thoughts in my head were nothing more than a series of hallucinations brought on by the over indulgence the previous night even though I hadn’t drank that much. The sounds of my mother’s voice as she try to sooth me back to bed after a bad nightmare went through my mind as though she was there in the room with me. She wasn’t of course as she had died several years earlier. There was no one there. No one but me and my reflection in the mirror and neither of the two would be of much comfort this night. Neither of the two would be able to sooth the nightmares away with the magic words of calm and comfort that had seemed to work so well all those years ago. I ran some more water from the faucet over my face again and wiped my face off in the towels. I was tempted to remain seated in the living room all morning and watch nothing more than morning talk shows but the idea of it was enough to put me back to sleep again. Eventually I had no choice but to give into the demands of my body and return to the bed. It had taken most of the morning hours to pass before I was really able to fall back into a deep and comfortable sleep again. When it did happen though I wasn’t greeted by the visions I had seen before again. Something about these that I saw now were a bit different yet in some many ways similar as well which left little doubt in my mind that they were in someway linked together. Before waking up I again saw the image that I saw last week, the larger individual standing over the fallen body of someone else. From the right hand there dripped something from his hand which appeared to possibly be blood. I finally got the look at the face of the man lying at his feet and to my own shock it was S. that laid there. Before I could find out anything about what was going on the phone began to ring. I rolled over and looked at the clock sitting on the table and saw that it read 3:32AM. I fumbled around in search of the phone before I finally got my hand on it and answered the call. ”Hello?” he muttered into the speaker barely able to open his eyes yet. He attempts to push himself up out of the bed but with little strength behind it as a voice comes back over the phone. ”T-ty?” a female voice said. At first sound it could have been mistaken for his girlfriend Heather though the sounds of sobs and tears from her makes it quite difficult to tell exactly who it was. “Yeah, this is him. Is that you Jess?” he asked suddenly coming to his senses with a bit of alarm. “Y-y-yes it is,” she slowly answered him. She was obviously quite distressed but what it was about he had no idea. Fearing the worse though he answered back to her. ”What’s wrong? Did you here something about Dan?” he asked with his alarm increasing further. At first he heard nothing but silence and the sound of Jess crying before she would answer again. “N-no I haven’t,” she eventually answered. “It’s about Heather, Tyler.” “What about her?” he asked unsure of what the problem could be. ”I’m so sorry,” was the only response she would give at first. “Jess, what happened?” he asked again feeling an even greater sense of urgency. “I’m sorry Tyler but….” Jess began before breaking off again in tears before being able to finish the sentence. “She’s dead.” The phone fell from Tyler’s hand and came crashing down onto the ground below him. Tyler in a state of complete shock sat at the edge of the bed and slowly slid off onto the floor his head in his hands as the tears slowly become to come forth and the only sounds left is the voice of Jess on the phone as she screamed for Tyler to answer her and the sounds of his own crying as the scene faded to black.
