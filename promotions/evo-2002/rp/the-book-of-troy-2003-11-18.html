The Book of Troy..

18 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-11-18 Book Of Troy:Chapter One:Where it all began... The scene opens outside a local gym where standing outside is Rogue wrestler Troy Chryst. It has yet to be determined where we are exactly at. It seems to far up north as you can see smoke comming out of the mouth of Troy. Troy walks over back and forth in front of the old gym. Looking at the very top of the gym he stands and reads +:'In memory of Jhon Wilson..' He looks down at the ground below showing no emotion on his face. Just glaring at the ground he stands on. The what seems very familiar ground. He doesnt even look back at the Rogue cameras..All he does is stare down at the ground..He begins to talk still showing no attention to the cameras.. +:'You selfish bastard. Why damn it?Why did you leave for me to deffend all for myself. This day feels all to familar. So cold, so a lone. Something that I have delt with every since the time you left Jhon. Why did you leave? Why did you just give up on your own life? You where the only person I trusted. The only man I ever looked up to..And how do I get repaid Jhon? You leave me all by myself. In this world that does no more for me then what you are doing now. You where the only one Jhon. I never really had a father you know. That's why I never brought them to see my matches. They like my high school and most of my family looked at me as if I was the outcast. But I can't blame them. I refused to cooperate with such low lifes. There was no need for me to be social with that who are unworthy of it. But Jhon, I made you worthy damn it. You but TRUST in me..You showed me what talents I had in a ring..but damn it..WHY..why did you give up..' Troy then falls to his knee's still looking down at the ground. It seems he refuses to look up are the gym door that is right in front of him. As he goes to look up he makes himself remain glareing at the ground. As if he is some what frighten to enter this gym. The same gym that belonged to the only man who ever believed in him. But the same gym that belongs to the man who gave up on his life and left him.. +:'I always told you Jhon. Every night I told you smoking would kill you. And being the stubborn asshole you are of course you didn't listen. You ignored me. Then what damn it. What did you do?You got the news you had cancer. But you where to good to have treaments. It was too much to tell anyone. It was too much to tell me. Why Jhon, why didnt you come to me. That's all I ever wanted. I trusted you..and yet everyday you make me wish I never did. As if I never came to this old gym..' Troy then gets up as he stares down the door to the gym. He charges at the door as he kicks the center of it. Obviously the gym is very old as the hinges on the door squeed loudly as a big rig. As he walks inside he looks on the wall as on it are very old pictures. Dust seems to be every where in the old raggy gym but it has a good amount of light in it. Not because of light bulbs, but because of the sun pearing in through the windows that stands at the top of the gym. Troy walks past all the pictures as he looks over into the larger part of the gym that is in the middle and inside is a old wrestling ring. The ring is in terrible condition. The ropes are dropping down as the ring matt itself has just been there collecting dust. Troy never show's any emotions the whole time.. While he stares at the ring he kinds of goes out into his own world as he begins to remember a old dream about a momment he had in this very gym.. The memmories.. +:'I think back to the time I first ever stepped foot in this ring. I was only 15 and I wanted the world. I didn't stop for anything as I took everything to the edge. I pushed the envelope and made a name for myself just in practices in the gym. And the first time they called for me I knew it was time. As I watched Chris the man I was going to face, stand in the ring I just knew it was going to be my night. Even then I was hated by everyone. I was told by Jhon that I have a way to make people despise me. I know it's because deep down they wish they where me.' +:'I have no problems in showing or telling people what I feel. I am the only person that I have ever known to simply look some one in the eye and tell them what I feel and how I feel about it with out giving a damn what it meant to them. It's funny, these corporations just produce more and more spwans of the same thing and yet our pathetic peons always agree to by into them..' +:'But that was not the case here. Just being a bunch of young kids at the time wanting to be something we always loved to do. That was it. We just wanting to continue our pasion for what we did and what I did best. I stepped in that ring taking charge the only way I knew how. I took it to the extreme..as that night I was accepted as one of this gym's greatest.. +:'But yet did I care? No has I had no reason to be. No reason to try and show effort to something that has just let me showcase my talents. All honestly Jhon, now that I think about it. It was your pleasure to see me wrestle. I brought people in this gym to pay for you to keep these damn lights running. Honestly Jhon, what the hell did you do for me?Ill tell you what!The same thing everyone else has done for me in this sad pathetic place..' +:'Nothing..' Scene fades black...
