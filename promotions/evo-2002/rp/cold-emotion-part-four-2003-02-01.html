Cold Emotion... part four.

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens inside the basement of the 'Eternity' Nightclub where Morpheus Omega studies multiple fighting techniques and also where he releases massive amounts of rage and anger. The personal gym of the Infinite of evil resembles a dark, modern dungeon. The punching bag can be heard struck repeatedly and someone can be heard keying up into a heavy shot thrown. The chains on the bag rattle and cursing can be heard from somebody. The scene directs to the punching bag, where Sky Night looks physically exhausted and like she is letting out rage of her own. Sky wipes the sweat of her brow, she takes a short break and breathes deeply. 'The violence that I will bring to eVolution fighting aside one of the greatest athletes to walk the earth, nothing can bring end to this clique. The only explanation is this is our twisted reality and together, myself and Morpheus will dominate. Trickery is one of the greatest tools of the devil, with a twist of lies and deceit. You will not only live side our twisted reality yet it will become of you!' Sky Night proclaims with her words ringing of evil. Sky Night shakes her head back and forth, she returns back to the bag and leaps high and hits a spinning roundhouse that sends the bag flying. Sky Night keys up, screaming in rage and lands with a sick snarl on her face. Sky breathes heavily. The creaking of a heavy metal door is heard and a darkened, large figure can be seen. The image portrayed looks to be of one, you would only think to imagine in the worst of nightmares. The figure becomes more clear as Morpheus steps from the darkness, still half his face shadowed over and the sick grin clearly seen. Stalking from the shadows of darkness, Morpheus quietly observes Sky Night through the darkness. Sky turns and catches the attention of Morpheus and slightly jumps and looks frightened. Sky reacts by jumping high and heal stomping the ground in rage, screaming with another key-up 'Why the hell do have to sneak up on me like that?' Sky questions by screaming loudly... The only response is a low, dark chuckle from Morpheus as he slowly makes his way over with a shallow look on his face. Morpheus stops and stares at Sky Night, like he is reading her every thought. 'If you cannot trust your partner, who can you trust? Sometimes my presence is made without any knowledge. Covering your back when you think I am elsewhere. Sky Im everywhere, all the time' Morpheus states, followed with a smirk. Without hesitation, Sky Night returns the smirk and speaks with sound of resentment 'I almost bet... I am smart enough to cover my trails and back step without anyone having anything on me. You cannot be everywhere, all the time. Who is to say at times, I am present when you don't have the slightest clue. The watcher in fact, is really being watched! I am not your regular girl next door Morpheus!' she laughs. Sky breathes heavily with her head down. The darkened grin looks to be permanently attached to her face. Morpheus with his head back, nodding slightly and laughs trying to contain from the funny comment of Sky. 'Definitely not the girl next door, but still at mind, body and soul you compare nowhere close to the sickest being to walk this earth. My existence cannot even be proved, little lone comparable. The ability to destroy someone at mind, then tear them apart with physical abuse does ten times the damage compared to just the physical abuse on its own. You only win battles with the mind!' Morpheus says quite confidently. 'You cannot con a con, regardless' Sky replies and continues 'Sometimes when you think you have all the control in the world, you have none!' Sky states like she meant that with hidden meaning. 'Other times though, You fall into the hole you have dug for yourself' Morpheus says with the sickest of looks on his face. Pausing a moment, before continuing 'Never dig that hole Sky!' Morpheus says with one of the most disturbed looks of emotion on his face before disappearing back amongst the shadows, like he is no longer present. With the twist of dark, cold emotion from the both of them. Their thoughts and feelings unknown. The mystery eluding between the two characters like they are lost within each others mind games. With another scream of rage, Sky Night jumps high with a snap kick and lands back on the balls of her heels. Breathing deeply with that sick, snarl still present on her face. The scene fades to darkness...
