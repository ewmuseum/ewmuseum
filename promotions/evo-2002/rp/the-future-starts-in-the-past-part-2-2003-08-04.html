The Future Starts In The Past part 2

4 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-04 Legends draws near and things within the lives of Katherine Jackson and Scott Frost have been busy. In the ring, Katherine, better known as female diva of eVo Seara has had a changing career, with many ups and downs and a constant struggle to prove herself. She has always been short of friends and found it hard to communicate with other people, but eVolution wrestling has changed her from the young woman she once was when she began here. And one person, male superstar Scott Frost has worked hard to change the female’s perceptions of people. The female inside and outside the ring is now an accomplished woman with a life she is proud to lead, her anger although still strong and her temper no longer as short are still present, but she has found a control within herself that she never thought she could have. Before the death of one of the females very few friends L2J, Seara found something to change her life forever, for better or worse. Scott Frost. When he first seen the female, he knew they would become a team although scared of the large male, Seara seemed content to hate him and avoid his words and touch. Still married to his first wife, Scott approached Seara asking her to join a stable with him, she refused and soon Frost enlisted the help of fellow team mate L2J to recruit the up and coming young star to the team. When asked by L, Seara was quick to accept the offer; L2J was already of course someone the young wrestler trusted. In her feud months before with Asis, L2J had come to the females aid without being asked to save her from an attack by the male. Once she became a member of Notorious, the female began to settle into the swing of things around eVolution and soon began her first victories, which pushed her into the ranks; she was a quick feisty young fighter with a tremendous anger and will. Over her time in Notorious before L’s death, Seara seen Scott Frost change and although she dare not admit that she had a heart or cared, she began to fall quickly for the male who was going through a divorce. Scott finally broke from his wife and lost all contact with her, and Seara became the focus of all his attention as he quickly became infatuated with her. It was soon obvious that something was beginning to spark between the pair and it was not long before they became more than just teammates, a partnership of great strength and trust formed. Seara soon enough became the first eVolved champion of eVolution beating a man who she had loathed since her entranced to eVo, Xander, Scott Frost was also to become the Universal champion. Months down the line and eVo has celebrated its first birthday, many great and wonderful things have happened within the fed since it opened just over a year ago and this week, eVolution wrestling is about to have another record log for its books. The first couple to get married in the ring of eVolution. This week in England, the new setting for the fed, fans will witness the actual wedding ceremony of Scott Frost and Katherine Jackson. “Mr and Mrs Frost” remarked Seara, “I can’t believe it, I always thought I would live alone and die alone forever miserable, and now, I am getting married in only a few days, I cannot believe it, I am so shocked.” “You really are different” Seara’s younger brother stands looking at her in her dress and smiles before putting his arms tightly around here. “I am so proud of you” he adds, he looks at his older sister for a moment and wipes a tear away from her face as it rolls down her cheek “Why are you crying?” “I’m not sure, maybe because I’m so scared yet I want nothing else, I can’t live without him, and I know I never will have to, but still, I am scared of what this will all mean” “Its means my big sister is truly in love and all that she has ever feared has been proven wrong, I really am proud of you. Hey, you realise that I get a brother now” realizing his words are wrong, he looks at his sister and tries to take her mind off her late brother Bruce, who died after an attack from male wrestler and monster Asis. “Asis will pay one day soon, but not now, this is my time, and I will forget about the pain that big idiot caused me…. for the day at least. I wish Bruce could have been here, but in some ways, he is” remarks Seara holding up a necklace once held by her brother on his wedding day, lifting it she slips it over her head and rubs it gently with her thumb. “Asis and his actions will not ruin this, not for me, not for Scott, I will not let him deny me this” turning Seara looks into the mirror and her eyes are caught by the image of Scott Frost in a silver frame sitting on the cabinet next to her. Picking it up she kisses the picture and stares loving at it as she slips away into a day dream.
