Times have Changed (Part II)

28 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-28 Prelude: Last night Shane paid a visit to the local therapist to talk about his childhood, the trauma of growing up in a family involved in organised crime. Shane also revealed a secret to the shrink about a relative of his who is currently working for global wrestling. This rumour left the world spinning with questions as to who this mystery ancestor is. Wrestling websites and newspapers have been circulating the topic and running polls to see whom the fans think it is. The only clue that was given was that the mystery person was once a GW International Champion. Besides the quizzing going on between fans and wrestling geniuses, Shane Wethers is preparing for another appearence in the immense surroundings of Evo. Only time will tell if Shane Wethers can bring this new found glory to an all time high. (Ever get that feeling that you’re lost in an uncontrollable nightmare and no matter how you try to crawl from it, you’ll always be lost in a wave of dysfunction? All the time you continue to ask yourself ‘why me?’. Well that’s my life, after growing up in a family full of crime, love and hate. The two things don’t mix, it’s like oil and water, all it does is create a mess that will spread even further with time. Witnessing the faked death of my own brother when I was young didn’t exactly give me the stability that at ten year old needs. I weren’t exactly prepared to be an only child and sometimes I wished I’d wake up out of this terrible nightmare that was eating away at me. As I grew older I became more entrapped in the unusual way of adolescence, but I were slightly more melancholic than your usual twisted teenager. When I were 12 we used to go down to the wreck where we would play fight, but this fighting wasn’t always so playful… if I had to count the amount of times we all went home with broken bones, bust faces or bruised bones, then I would have more figures than your phone number. Who would have thought that as I grew older a movie like fight club would have so closely resembled my youth life, so similar it’s almost humorous. My Father never wanted me involved in games like this, but he could hardly punish me for a little bit of youthful aggression when my whole family were involved in countless crimes that even Hannibal Lector would be proud of. All I’ve ever tried to do since the day I were born was escape from this life of organised crime, every man has his dark side but when you are involved with a family like mine, the dark side becomes a little too repetitive. But now the running has made me tired and there comes a time when you have to accept your upbringing and make do with things. I developed a love for punk music at an early age and began playing with a band by the time I was 15, but my parents didn’t agree. The Rat pack were more my fathers favourites, he had the honour of meeting Dean Martin when he was only Seventeen, he slept with the Rat Pack Vinyl at the side of his bed every night without fail, even my Mother began to get worried. But anyway, where were I? Oh yes… My Father, he loved his job and the feeling of power, he always intimidated the locals but was always well respected. Anyone who paid him respect could be guaranteed protection under my Fathers wing, but if anybody even dared to mess with him you could count on your brains being decorated over the morning newspapers. We would spend a lot of time together, which is probably the reason I have ended up with more issues than Damien Demento. A time that particularly sticks in my mind is the Christmas season of 1985, me and my dad were spending some time together on his usual house rolling routines… ) ::A young Shane Wethers sits staring starry-eyed out of the window of an all black ford sedan as it travels down a snowy road with the headlights on full beam. Shane’s spiky clumps of hair stick up on end like a world famous sculpture artist had carefully molded them. Paul Wethers sits in the drivers seat, his hair gelled back in the typical mafia way and he holds a fat Cuban cigar between his lips. He glares down at the roads as he drives carefully. Even for an English man he looks the part, wearing a mahogany coloured suit with black pin stripes. Shane is wearing black jeans, a t-shirt and a red sweater. He is taller than any average ten year old, but can hardly see out of the window.:: Shane: Poppa? Where are we going? ::Paul continues to drive for a few seconds before looking down at Shane:: Paul: We’re off to sort a little bit of business, father and son style. For once, you can come and see Papa at work. Soon, you could be running a business like me, earning lots of money and being the main man. Would you like that? ::Shane nods his young head as his father continues to drive down the long open road. Before long the journey is cut short by blue flashing lights in the mirror. Paul looks curiously in his main mirror and tuts, cussin’ to himself under his breath like a temperamental youth.:: Paul: Just when you think you may get a little bit of peace, it always changes when the damn pigs show up. ::Shane looks up at his father:: Shane: Pops? Why do you say pigs? There are no pigs here. ::Paul smirks as he begins to slow down before pulling in at the side of the narrow road. He turns to face Shane with a frustrated expression upon his face:: Paul: Pigs are another name for the scum of the earth. Law Enforcement, its their jobs to make everyone’s life a misery by being a set of moronic kill joys. ::Paul turns off the engine and brings the palm of his hand sharply upon the steering wheel in anger. He watches the Police Officer park up behind him and step out of the vehicle. The Officer walks over to their vehicle and Paul winds down the window. The estranged officer is wearing the usual uniform and has short dark hair and a clean shaven face.:: Paul: How can I help you officer? Cop: Would you please step out of the vehicle sir? ::Paul nods his head as the cop walks around the back of the car. Paul looks over at Shane; giving him the parental ‘everything will be alright’.:: Paul: Hey, reach in that glove compartment there and take out that blue cloth. ::Shane does as he says, he opens the compartment door and see a large cloth wrapped around some heavy object unknown to Shane. He reaches in and takes hold of it.:: Paul: Be careful damnit! ::Shane jumps, a little startled, he hands his father the Cloth. Paul unwraps it and keeps it away from Shane; he steps out of the car and walks over to the police car, cleverly putting the item inside of his coat. Shane sits silently in the car for a few moments, tapping his fingers on the door interior. All of a sudden there’s a loud gun shot and Shane panics. He turns back, looking out of the rear view window, he sees his father stepping out of the car and walking towards their car. He opens the door of the drivers seat and steps in, closing the door behind him. He hands Shane the wrapped clothed item and smiles at Shane.:: Paul: Put that back for me would ya kiddo? ::Shane places it back in the glove compartment with out hesitation as Paul restarts the engine. All of a sudden Shane snaps out of the flashback and is sat in a black leather chair in the confinements of his local shrinks office. The shrink sits across from him, laying back in a chair and scribbling notes on a pad of paper. He is wearing a blue suit and black Armani shoes.:: Shrink: Mr Wethers, it seems to me like this inner emotional turmoil is the reason for your aggressive behaviour, your dark side so to speak. The past has scarred you mentally and I cannot change that, all I can do is show you how to deal with it. ::Shane sits up and sighs heavily:: Shane: But I don’t wanna know how to deal with it, I’ve got my own way of dealing with the problem and that’s by tearing my opponents limb from limb. There’s no better feeling from deep down inside of my gut, than to hear the sombre cries of my opponent when I kick their bitch ass around like an old soccer ball. I have my gentle side, my happy-go, live life to the max, music loving life style. I’m an Eternal Punk to the bone, I’m the front man of a world famous punk band and I’m so damn big that being humble isn’t even an option. I’m the guy who put the brits in ‘celebrity’. That’s all my happy go side, but every town has it dark corner and I have a dark side to me. If I’m pushed to the limit I will make that person pay and they will suffer. ::The shrink nods his head and continues to jot some notes down on his pad:: Shrink: Shane, have you though of other ways to release your anger? ::Shane frowns and shrugs:: Shane: I’m a professional wrestler, what do you expect me to do? Take up ballet? ::The shrinks grins:: Shrink: No, I mean meditation. Your living a fast paced life. Fast music, fast cars and basically, getting beaten over the head with a chair for a living isn’t gonna hide you from your problems. ::Shane ponders for a moment before replying with a smile that a Cheshire cat would be proud of:: Shane: I know, but it’s hard to be pissed of when you’re unconscious. ::The shrink stares at Shane observantly and twiddles his brass plated pen through his fingers:: Shrink: Last week you told me about your relation from…er…GW. Now I must admit that the news, even to me was a bit of a shock. After all, you too seem so alike and so very, very different. Does you’re ex…um...Lexi know of this? ::Shane stands up and walks straight over to the water dispenser. He pulls out a white plastic cup from the holder and fills it up with water. He drinks the water down so fast, any one would think he’d been stranded in the Sahara Desert for the last five days. Shane throws the empty cup in the metal trash bin and sighs. He runs his hand through the infamous blonde spiked clumps of hair and shakes his head like a man lost in a world of confusion:: Shane: Lexi knows about it, she even knows him, that’s why I keep her away from him. Just check his passed records, you know the sort of things he has done and it’s not pretty at all. Some of things he’s done are the reason I’m hear talking to you today. My family is full of bloodthirsty cons with enough aggression in them, to wipe out the Middle East. Is it any wonder my mind is full of bad memories? Is it any surprise that I take great joy in beating the hell out of the taxman every time he knocks at my door? ::The shrink nods his head sympathetically.:: Shrink: I am very sorry, I know how it must feel… ::Shane frowns and interrupts the Shrink:: Shane: You understand? You think you understand? Have you ever had a family that’s only obsession was to make someone spill every drop of blood from every bodily organ? Hell no.. so don’t give me any bullsh#t about what you think you know. I haven’t come here for your sympathy, I’ve come here cos’…. ::Shane falls silent, lost for words. He sits down on the seat and rests his head in his hands:: Shrink: …cos you need to talk, get it off your chest? ::Shane looks across at the Shrink:: Shane: yeah, I guess you could say that. ::The shrink smiles:: Shrink: see? I know more than you think. Shane, I’m here to listen to all you have to say, if you need to get rid of these mental depressions by talking it over then I will be your ears, but I don’t think that this is all as half as bad as it seems. ::Shane frowns and looks at the Shrink:: Shane: What the hell are you talking about? Shrink: Well you take your aggression out on your opponents don’t you? ::Shane nods his head:: Shrink: and you are obviously successful. So? You’ve already found the best way to deal with it. You’ve come here to talk to me because you’ve found a remaining member of your family in the wrestling business, and this is something that doesn’t fit right with you, maybe its because its something you haven’t had to deal with before and your unsure about doing the right thing. I know you wanna destroy this guy, but something deep inside of you is eating away at your conscience, asking you if this is the right way to go about it. ::Shane stands up in fury:: Shane: Don’t you even go into that, you no nothing more about me than what I have told you!!! So don’t start analysing me like I’m a lab rat cos that ain’t happening. Don’t contact me doc unless you’ve got something or useful to tell me. ::Shane walks out of the office, slamming the door behind him::
