The Power of the Dollar :Part I:

16 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-16 'Can you please hurry up, do I look like a man with time on my hands?' Ethan screamed down the landing at his butler who was scurrying from room to room prior to their departure. 'No sir' the young smartly dressed man responded with a degree of concern in his voice. 'What are we waiting for anyway?' Ethan demanded to know. 'I'm just trying to pack the remainder of your attire sir' the butler replied. 'Why was that not done sooner?' Ethan questioned somewhat frustrated. 'You only asked me to do it fifteen minutes ago sir' the butler replied somewhat flustered by the whole ordeal. 'Oh' Ethan paused, 'well just get a move on' he said before glancing down at his rolex. It was a good few hours before he was due to meet Devlin, but Ethan was always very keen to be the first man on site simply so he could familiarise himself with the surroundings and though he wouldn't admit it, to some extent he would search out escape routes. Devlin was a very intimidating man; size, strength and just his sheer manner of going about things. This was not a meeting of like minded individuals, it was a clear display of the power of the american dollar. ~Flashback~ Sitting backstage after yet another dark card, Ethan looked a man less than content with things standing as they were. Here he was, a man of millions and yet he was finding himself competing against scruffy wrestling amateurs who did not seem to know what a toothbrush and comb were. Ethan had been told he had to earn his crust despite his millions, but surely this was taking the piss, or at least thats how it felt to Ethan M Coles. Reaching into his inner suit pocket he pulled out his rather flash cellphone. 'David Dukes' Ethan said into the phone before raising it to his ear. 'David we need to talk' he said into the phone, 'people just aren't taking me seriously, infact I think there is a conspiracy out to get me'. 'Yes I do think its jealousy what else could it be?' he replied obviously aggitated, 'I mean nobody can say I deserve to be placed with this lot. I'm beggining to wonder if its time to hurry up my progression, can we meet for lunch?' 'Great, about an hour' Ethan replied glancing down at his gold rolex again, 'oh and bring a few wrestling magazines, we are going shopping'
