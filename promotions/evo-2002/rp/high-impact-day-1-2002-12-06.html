High Impact: Day 1

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec. 6, 2002 'A Vendetta, an often prolonged series of retaliatory, vengeful, or hostile acts or exchange of such acts.' The voice of Frost is heard. The scene opens up and Scott Frost is sitting on a couch looking into a tan coloured book with black lettering that vaguely makes out to be, 'Dictionary'. Frost nods his head, than he closes his book and sets it on a table. 'I got my match. The stable of champions, is soon to lose another champion. I am gunning for Thug roC, the heart and soul of New-Tv. Leader versus leader, President versus employee. This match is made, legit and straight out. Scott Frost will take on the President for the High Impact championship, and there is absolutely nothing he can do about it? Why? Because Tyler Davidson, said so.' Frost flares out, while talking to himself. There is a knocking sound that echo's throughout the room. The sound catches the attention of Frost and his head jolts to the left and he stares towards his door and he stands up slowly. Frost walks towards the door, and news commentator; Josh Smith is standing at the door with a camera man and Frost breaths heavily. 'What?' Frost asks. 'I was wondering, since we are staying in the same hotel. If maybe I can get an exclusive on Scott Frost, the leader of Notorious?' Smith asks obviously sucking up. Frost looks at the camera guy, and then back at Smith. Frost shrugs and waves his right arm back as if welcoming the two men into his hotel room. Smith and the camera man both look into the room which seems to be very dim lit and Smith looks at Frost and than reaches for the light switch. 'May I?' Smith asks Frost. Frost shrugs and turns around, as Smith turns the lights on and the room quickly illuminates and kinda catches Frost of guard and Frost momentarily covers his eyes until he has adjusted to the change in lighting. Frost sits on his couch, and Smith sits on a chair which is diagonal to Frosts left. 'So, what does the magnificent Josh Smith wanna talk about.' Frost says using sarcasim. 'Well, actually. I want to know why you challanged the President of the company to a match for the High Impact championship. As far as I was concerned, you are a heavy weight and the title is a light weight title.' Smith says to Frost. Frost smiles. 'Obviously, for one, you haven't paid attention to who is the Universal champion. My long time friend, L2J. What is he? He is a cruiserweight, the Universal championship is a heavy weight title indeed. Second, I am two hundred and twenty-five pounds. Which is the maximum weight for the cruiser weight division.' Frost retorts to Smith. Smith looks at Frost and squints his eyes. 'Right here it says you are two hundred and twenty-six pounds on the roster page.' Smith asks. 'So people aren't allowed to lose one pound in the wrestling business, these days?' Frost again retorts. Smith looks at Frost. 'You've really put some thought into this challange, haven't you?' Smith asks Frost. 'You damned straight. Ever since I lost that Road to Glory tournament, Notorious has been set on a losing streaking. L2J is the only one with something to brag about nowadays. I am doing this for....' Frost stops. Frost looks around, confused. 'What is it?' Smith says with concern. Frost looks up at Smith. 'I'm doing this for myself.' Frost says with demenour. 'This is for me.' Frost says again. Frost nods. 'For me....' Frost looks up at Smith with amazement. The scene fades away as Frost and Smith look at each other. Frost with a look of amazement in his eyes, and Smith with a very confused look on his face.
