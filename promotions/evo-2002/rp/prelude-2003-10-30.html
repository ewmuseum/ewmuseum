Prelude...

30 October 2003

Date: 2003-10-30 Hatred, contempt, and pain. Those are what are you in your future. Do yourself a favor and jump off a fucking bridge. It will be a better way to die than what I've got in store for you. You think you know what pain is. You've got no idea. No....fucking....idea. Then, he woke up. It was another day. Another cold, pointless day. He woke up to look around in his bedroom, to find some sort of purpose. He found none. All he ever found was the cold. Ever since he lost his title. He didn't feel the same anymore. Something.. something was wrong, very very wrong. He lost his life. His idenity. Everything he ever held dear to him was slowly taken away, one by one, until he fell into a deep insanity. An insanity that clawed at the surface, screaming to be lead out. He buried it. Deeper and deeper, he buried it under a facade, and a stupid vendetta, and a goal. He didn't want to. He didn't have to. But yet, that's exactly what he did. Damon sat up, rubbing the sleep from his eyes. He stood up, stretching, and walked over to the window. The ground was covered with a new-fallen snow, shining under the sun like a brilliant coat of whiteish-silver that had been covered onto the land by an expert artist. Children played, laughing and chasing each other through the winter wonderland, and O'Halloran couldn't help but smile. As he looked down, part of him saw himself, running through the snow with his younger sister Mary chasing him.... Wait, his younger sister? The sister that had deprived him of his daughter?! The sister he now hated. Damon turned his head away from the beautiful scene, and shook his head, as if he was trying to shake the cobwebs out of it. He looked around his bedroom, and found what his heart cried out for. He picked up a pictureframe, and turned it around. There he was. A young boy, sitting in the center, with his leg wrapped around a football. But was someone else supposed to be in that beautiful picture? Someone, that he had blocked out all memory of. Did he know this person today?! Who or what was missing from this picture. He had a sickening feeling in the pit of his stomach. ... Terlayne?
