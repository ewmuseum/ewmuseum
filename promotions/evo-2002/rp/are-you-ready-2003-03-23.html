Life as we know it will always be looked too as second best, because Mr.Greatness is standing right here..And folks that's how you become TOO DAMN GOOD! Because it's their luck to which one I toss over the top first.' Interviewer:'So I see you seem to be confident with the rumble match.So any last words before we wrap things up?' You all know the channel, and you all know you will be tunned in, to see the best, to see..THE F'N NETWORK!' ~~~Jason Relms stands up and walks out as the camera fades to black.~~~
