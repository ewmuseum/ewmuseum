Confused... part two.

2 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-02 I felt an intense, painful, pulsing feeling inside of my head and I held my head wishing the pain would go away. I slowly opened my eyes and the light was crucially blinding, I squinted from how bright the upper lights were. I let out a loud groan and felt bothered with pain from this hardcore headache I have and the tense, stabbing pain in my knee. Suddenly it hit me, I remembered I would never be able to walk again. I immediately became down and depressed further then the pain had already brought me. I felt a cold tear form in my eye and I wished the world would end. I faced the bare misery and opened my eyes completely... The room looked typical for a hospital room, plain walls and a chrome framed bed with freshly-washed white sheets. I looked around for a calendar, I didn't know if I had been out for days or even weeks. I was alone... On the table beside me there were a bouquet of flowers and a few cards. I picked up the first and it read, 'Morpheus get well soon, we miss you in the eVolution locker rooms... Thug roC!' I became confused reading this card, even after our troubles and hardships he still expresses care for me. The next one was a deeply emotional card from Sky about the love she has for me, and how I will be well soon and we can live a happy life somewhere warm in the tropical islands... I suddenly became confused once again... I thought long and hard again, remembering my tragic wake up during surgery and knew how hurt she felt for me. I had never seen her look that upset before, not even when she thought Jason Jameson was going to stab her in the throat. I realized Sky must have more love for me, then she even has for herself; like I helped carry her through life. It all became clear, Sky was a completely different woman then I had ever imagined! I felt a smile crack, but I bet the look on my face was still a miserable one... All the love in the world couldn't make this sick pain inside of me go away, I don't mean physical but mentally. I put down the card and sighed. At this point, I didn't know what to do with myself... I was lost and confused, down and out, depressed and physically handicapped. My life has now ended and everything is over, I might as well die. I forced myself to sit up in bed and noticed the front of the unread card said 'Happy Birthday and it really got my attention because the front of it had a creepy looking clown on it. I picked up the card and looked inside, it said 'Morpheus, you ever fuck with me again... I will bust your other knee!' I was out raged, it was signed Jay Jay Jameson. I became frustrated and very angry. I had then fully decided that I would kill Jameson, that bitch will die! I tore the card into pieces and layed back down and closed my eyes, I lost recollection.
