An Interview with The Hype

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 5th, 2003 A few days after “The End of Days” was shown live across the world on pay Per View one of the most recognizable faces in professional wrestling sat down with eVo’s journalist Max Dickens to speak on the events of the evening. Obviously the most talked about subject was the Main Event that saw our interviewed superstar James Kimson fight it out in a No DQ Universal Title match against Scott Frost and antagonist Erin Christoph. The Match saw Frost retain his title, but he did so off the finishing move of Kimson. Opportunity knocked for Scott Frost and he retained. Max Dickens sat down with The Hype in what was another classic one on one interview between these two men. Kimson spoke candidly about Frost, Christoph, Daniel Somers and a whole host of other eVo wrestlers and personal. He spoke in detail about the match and what his thoughts are on his future in eVolution. The scene opened up with a very stripped down set. A eVolution banner is acting as a backdrop between Kimson and Dickens who sit across from each other in folding “dirrector” style chairs. There’s a small wooden coffee table between them that is host to a jug of water and two glasses, both filled. Kimson is wearing his typical garb, the leather jacket, the jeans. He sits as comfortably as he can, probably stiff from his combat at The End of Days. A bandage graces his forehead from where he was “busted open” during the match. Dickens sits across, a black blazer, black slacks and a white t-shirt. Dickens wearing his glasses, his hair neatly combed back smiles as the director probably just gave him the signal for action. Dickens: James, thanks once again for sitting down with me. I know we’ve been through a lot of these interviews since we meet 4 years ago. Kimson: it’s always been fun Max. We’ve had some interesting talks like you said. Hopfully this one will be up there with the best of them. Dickens: I’m sure it will be. So, I guess the best thing to start with James is what are your thoughts, mentally and physically about what went down last night. How were you prepared for the match, what were your thoughts on the outcome. Kimson scratches his goatee for a second as he sits up a big in his chair and clears his throat. Kimson: Well I have to admit I think in all honesty I should be Universal Champion right now Max. There were numerous times in that match when I had it one. The instance where I had Christoph beat through the table, probably the 1st real chance in the night for the title to change hands. But you know that’s an example of these Triple Threat matches Max, they’re unpredictable. And like you asked, how did I prepare for this match, in all honesty there’s no way to prepare for any match. You have to trust your own body, and you need to know your opponents. Last night, I wasn’t familiar with Frost, not as much as I was with Christoph as I had already wrestled him twice now. Frost on the other hand to me was the wild card on so many levels. See, I know what it’s like to have to defend a title when the odds are stacked against you like they were with Scott at End of Days. You have to go in with the mentality that you have to do anything you can to retain that belt. Because if you’re not willing to do that Max, the match is over before it even started and you are looking at yourself in the mirror the former champion. To be a champion you have to have a winning attitude, and Scott Frost displayed it at The End of Days winning the match. Dickens: Do you hold any harsh feelings towards him about it? It’s pretty obvious James that the two of you have not been seeing eye to eye as of late. Top that off with it being your finishing maneuver, History in the Making that was the deciding factor in the match. Kimson: Hard feelings? No, I wouldn’t say so. Like I said Max, you have to do what you have to do to retain titles. That’s all that Scott did. The chair shot he layed on me was an accident, so he says. And I bye that, sure. I have no reason not to believe the man when he says something. I hit him last week on legends to let him know two things. One, that we’re even. Two that I was serious about the Universal Title. Lets be honest Max, that title is the 1 last piece to the puzzle that is my career. To get that belt means a lot to me. Even if it’s at a man whom I respect like Scot’s expense. As for finishing the match off History in the Making? That was just me not watching where Frost was. I thought he was out of it, I was wrong and I’m man enough to admit that. Frost knew that Christoph was out, because once you get hit with History the match is over one way or the other. So here I’m sitting with a sore neck and no title. But I’m not going to cry over spilt milk. I know that Scott is a man and he’ll give me a shot once he finishes with Xander. I’m patient enough to wait. I never expected to be given a Title match so soon after arriving, and I’m thankful for that. I don’t know what game is being played between the owners. But James Kimson isn’t going to be a pawn in their game. I’m here right now to entertain fans. The fans who pay the money, the fans who put money in my pocket. They deserve to see what they want, and if they want James Kimson kickin’ Unlimited ass, well then they’re going to get it. If they want James Kimson to beat the holy shit out of Scott Frost, then I’m not going to deny them. Of course, there’s a cut off for my own morals. But Max, you know me. I’ve always been there for the fans because they’ve been there for me. I guess men like Daniel Somers, no matter how egotistical and power hungry he really is, deep down he knows what the fans want. He doesn’t want to deny them, because he knows that the second he losses the fans, he losses the company. Maybe that’s why I got a title match. Or maybe it’s all because Christoph asked for it. Wow doesn’t he look like an idiot. “Oh, I want a title match.. I’m important.... I’m the superstar.... I’m the best” or whatever the hell that jackass was bitching about. Yeah, he looks great now, getting pinned in the match he set up. I guess that goes to show Max that some people were meant to be winners and most others are lossers. Dickens: It’s true that he asked for the match. But I have a feeling that Christoph and you haven’t solved your issues just yet. Kimson: Well yeah that’s true. I enjoyed whipping his ass on Sunday. But at the end of the day he lost, and when the record books are written I lost as well. Of course Max, I wasn’t pinned in the match. I haven’t had my shoulders held for the 3 count in quite some time. That’s because I am, like I said a second ago a winner. I’m a champion and not to sound egotistical Max, but I don’t need the title belt to know that I’m a champion. It is a nice trophy, you get the respect, there’s money that comes with it. But I’ve done more in this industry then the likes of Erin Christoph would dream of, and probably ruin his pants in the process of the dream. I’ve had the opportunity, and while Christoph’s crowning achievements consist of being the 1st ever champion in eVo history and being able to bake really good apple/cinimon cookies I’ve done more in 2 months in eVo then he’s done in his entire career here. See a pattern Max? So Christoph comes in, murders someone one of the most cowardly acts a man can do. Then he wins his little title belt goes around like he owns the place and gets fired eventually after he screws up and gets caught. I came in, cost Sovereign his 1st match via pinfall. My 1st match I pin Jason Jameson in a triple threat match with him and Sovereign. Next month I pinned Erin Christoph himself in a romping good time of a match where I hardly broke a sweat. Then I beat Sovereign in the bloodiest match in recent wrestling memory. Then I go and get a Universal Title match. So Erin Christoph somehow through all that thinks he’s better then yours truly. Of course, that’s all subjective Max, anyone can draw their own conclusion to anything. I somehow think that pinfall victory of mine over that sack of puke sort of proves my point. Victories a side, yeah Max I don’t feel like I’ve settled the score. Some fans might take the have full approach and say “yeah well James you did cost him the Universal Title.” That’s one way to look at it for sure and I know that I’ve heard some fans say that to me the other day when I was signing some autographs in Buffalo. But I don’t look at it that way. See, Christoph and me. Well that’s no longer professional. We wrestled, except for one happy chair swinging occasion I left him laying on his ass looking up at the lights. I’ve beaten him, and I beat him cleaning. That’s as far as it goes for me, I could care less anymore. But on a personal level, the man bothers me. He broke out the chairs, the chains. He’s called in the troops, had rules to matches altered to his liking. That is the sort of stuff that pisses me off Max. And because I never really felt satisfied with the beating he got at End of Days, I’ll be more then happy to give him another one if he is up for it. Dickens: James one thing a lot of fans, and critics alike are wondering is your opinion on the Universal Title picture. The top contenders and obviously after End of Days we have a new Number 1 contender to the title. And that is a man you faced the Legends before End of Days. Kimson: Well the Universal Title is just like every other federation’s major belt. It’s hunted down by everyone. It’s not like a mid-carders title. It’s not a stepping stone. That’s the height of your stay if you can hold that title. Look at the men who have held the Universal title in eVolution and ask them what’s happened after they lost it. The only exception is Scott Frost who managed to rise tot he occasion and regain it weeks later. But look at Sovereign, easily in my opinion and many people’s the most dominating force in eVolution history. He ruined careers, he held the title longer then anyone else. He defended it in matches that most people wouldn’t have even thought of wrestling in. Then L2J, a weak champion who had personal demons, the only thing keeping him alive was the title. I don’t want to speak Ill of the dead but most will agree he was a poor champion but a good person. Then Shocka, not a bad champion by any means but he’s since fallen aside sadly, held down by the ankles from New-Tv, his chance to really break to super stardom is at his finger tips. But he’s been to the mountain, he needs to buy new climbing gear or something. Then look at Jameson, a man trying to find himself and when he finally received the respect the man deserved it was lost only weeks later. I have a lot of respect for Jason and we have our own personal issues that we resolved at Tainted Love. I managed to come out on top over him and Sovereign. As far as today goes, yeah Xander is the number one contender. Yes, We fought a week before End of Days and if it wasn’t for Christoph I had him pinned. No one has ever kicked out of History in the Making, and regardless of how tough Xander is, he’s not tough enough to survive. It was a good match. I knew that Xander would be a challenge but I knew I could beat him. The problem at this point in time Max is that he has the power. He’s the top contender and he’s also got the CEO of the company eating out of the palm of his hand. Or is it visa versa? Who really knows, but the fact still remains that the Universal Title match this month is Scott Frost taking on Xander in what will prove to be a match of the year candidate I’m sure. Either way after that match is over I think by that time they’re going to recognize, the winner that is two things. First, that if you want to be called a true champion you have to fight me one on one. Face to face without anyone else in the ring but the Ref. Win or lose it proves your willing to put it all on the line for that one chance at greatness. Scott Frost, I’ve got lots of respect for him. But he knows that his victory at End of Days wasn’t pure. Yeah, he pinned Christoph, he won the match and he’s still the man around here. But he didn’t beat me. He beat Christoph off of my move. Xander, he chickened out in his chance to really show the world that he is capable of being a major star. But what happened? He was on the verge of defeat, his friends came down to save his ass from losing to me. So after this months’ extravaganza, I hope that eVolution gives what the fans want. That’s a title match, 1 on 1 with the champion between me and either Xander of Frost. I don’t care which one it is. But I’ve fought my entire life to finally get a chance at a title this big. I’ve won all the others. I’ve beaten every man put before me one after the other. So as for the Universal Title picture Max, it’s a very interesting picture. It’s a masterpiece of wrestling genius. Trapped in a power struggle between two men who want what’s best for themselves. Dickens: Well it’s obvious James that you’ve taken the side of Tyler Davidson in this struggle. Kimson: Obvious? Well maybe to some people. But lets straighen the record. I’m on my side. I don’t know Davidson enough to trust the man fully. I’m trying to make it an even playing field as much as one man can. I get into that locker room and I see 20 or 30 guys with one thing in common, no one trusts each other. Hell I doubt even if the factions that’ve formed can trust each other. Team eVolution, who knows. I mean in the end Frost is the champion and each of those individuals wants that title. Look at Unlimited, you can’t tell me Max that Asis trusts Xander. New TV? I wouldn’t trust any of those jackasses as far as I could throw them. And so I’m standing there, between all of this wondering what the hell the problem is. And I’ll tell you what it is. It’ Daniel Somers trying to turn this into his own little playground. Now what’s happened with Davidson coming back? Well it’s a chess game, using us as playing pieces and our careers as the prize. Well, I got my piece all picked out Max. I’m the King, because come hell or high water in this game Max I’ll be the last one to fall. Dickens: James, thanks for doing this today. I did receive several requests from fans and one of the most common ones was name association. So I’m going to say a name and you’re going to say the 1st thing that comes to your mind. Kimson: Yeah, hit it. Dickens: Daniel Somers Kimson: Smart man, to smart for his own good Dickens: Asis Kimson: Talent, pure talent Dickens: Malcolm King Kimson: Lots of respect, done a lot for the business. Dickens: Morpheus Omega Kimson: He’s got a lot going for him. The day he steps in my way is a day he’ll regret. Dickens: Shocka Kimson: His career is just getting started. I’m going to watch him for years to come. Unless he screwes up and gets hurt. Dickens: Scott Frost. Kimson: A strong champion. A role model. Dickens: What about, say.... Big Daddy Dreamer? Kimson: Overrated. Dickens: Ronan Pierce? Kimson: Who? Dickens: Ronan Pierce, he wrestled Seara at End of Days. Kimson: Doesn’t ring a bell. Dickens: Okay, how about Tyler Davidson Kimson: A man who’ s trying to do the right thing. Dickens: Well James, I guess that’s it for today. I hope that you continue your success in eVolution and I’m sure we’ll be speaking again soon enough. Kimson: Well Max, I appreciate that forum to speak in. You do a great job and I just want to see the fans get what they want out of me. I’m glad to be here and I’m looking forward to the rest of Spring and going into the summer.
