Date: 2003-08-18 Location: London England Time: 3:30pm It has been a long few months for eVo superstar James Kimson. After a ruthless battle with Sovereign over a 2 month period to a long feud with former eVo superstar Erin Christoph, now no long in eVo because of Kimson’s victories Kimson has moved on to fight tooth and nail in the Universal Title division trying to get his hands on the 1 thing that’s eluded him. He’s had to jump through hoops, deal with serious injury, tremendous odds and even at times self doubt. James Kimson was never one to doubt himself, he fought through it all. He won the 20 Man Caged Rumble match along side Jason Jameson, then defeated him just 3 days later in a 3 stage of Hell match to win the right to face the Champion (Xander) at Revelations. It was something he worked so hard for, and victory was snatched from his fingers by revenge and rage. The emotions of Asis. That being said, it’s clear that Kimson’s feelings on the situation would be complex. He proved that 2 weeks ago at Legends when he confronted Asis about his actions at Revelations. The two were then put into a match just a day ago to determine a number 1 contender, the man that would get his hands on Xander at X-Rated. 3 men involved, 3 different stories with 1 title held in the grasp. The motives criss crossed all over the place, and last Thursday on legends Kimson vs Asis proved to be a match that few will forget. It had brawling, it had wrestling and lots of emotion. And in the end it was a draw, both men pinning each other at the same time so that Tyler Davidson had to rule that there would be a triple threat match at X-rated for the Universal Championship. Xander, recovering from injury facing both his most hated rivals in James Kimson and Asis. A match that could go down in history as the biggest Universal Championship match of all time. Today however it was time to get James Kimson’s thoughts on the subject. The nearly 12 year ring general had captured the World title 8 times in his career. Countless main events, countless big time matches. Out of the 3 men in the match Kimson probably had been in more big match situations then any of the 3. Knowing Xander’s storied past and Asis’ youthful rage he had an advantage in this match. He knew what it was like to be there. But this time it was different. In fact according to James in this interview he explains what it’s like to be in these situations. He gives a very candid view of recent events. His feelings towards Xander and Asis and why he’s fought so hard and long for the Universal Title, a goal that to some seems to be glory hounding or perhaps “just another belt”. James Kimson sat down with eVo columnist and reporter Max Dickens in Kimson’s London apartment. It was a warm day in London. Life in the Kimson residence in London was rather rare over the last few years. However with eVo forced out of the United States at the moment the Kimson Family has taken back to their London Loft over the last few months. Lucky for the family it was Summer and the children wouldn’t be in school, however that is going to change. A lot of changes are supposed to happen. 1st day back at school was always a celebration in the Kimson household, new faces, new experiences. But these days the father of the family, and international wrestling superstar has had a lot on his mind. Chasing dreams and goals is a taxing lifestyle but everyone has to try. James Kimson’s storied history would leave a lot of people to think he’s got nothing left to prove. As Max Dickens takes a seat and bows on his coffee James Kimson shifts his weight in his seat to get comfortable. Max and James have sat down a lot over the last 5 years and discussed what was going on in the world of professional wrestling. James looks around at the room as he waits for Max who has broken out a pad of paper with his questions on it as he always has had in the past. Dickens: Well James, thanks again for taking the time out of your day to speak to me and give the fans what they want. Kimson: Max, it’s always a pleasure to sit here and talk shop with you. Especially now more then ever. So I want you to feel comfortable to ask me any questions you want to ask me and I’ll do my best to answer them. Dickens: Well first of all, last week was rather controversial. First off, the match itself was made in strange circumstances. Obviously you and Asis were set to just simply settle your differences but Tyler Davidson felt that was a big money match right now and that you two needed to do something official. Your thoughts? Kimson: Well that entire thing stemmed from me needing to confront Asis. I know that he was expecting it, and despite the cocky attitude he’s got he knew all along that I was coming after him 2 weeks ago. I’ve never hid from anyone and I always confront the people who get involved in my business. Asis returned in a big way. He picked a hell of a way to make a statement and he made it at my expense. Not to many people do anything at my expense Max, you know that’s a fact. But Asis, despite all his talent and all his self confidence when it comes to the ring knew that if I came down there at Legends 2 weeks ago he’d have a fight on his hand. I’m sure he was prepared and in fact was looking forward to it. He was looking for a chance to fight someone he knew deep down wasn’t some curtain jerker like he fought all those months he was Champion or like all the pipsqueeks that he’s mowed through during his career here. He’s right when he mentioned this week that yeah, yeah he’s never fought anyone like me before at this level. We squared off once like 4 months ago and I got the victory. But that match means nothing at all. Asis is different, he’s stronger on the inside, a renewed sense of purpose. That’s what makes the man a threat to anyone, see he’s got motive now. He feels he has a right to take what he wants from Xander if that’s what will make him feel even with the score. Asis has all the physical tools, no one’s doubted that. Why do you think Xander recruited him in the 1st place, he did it because Asis was and is a threat to him and everyone else. Dickens: Since you obviously have respect for Asis’ abilities, it seems clear that he doesn’t care about you and what you’ve accomplished in your past. It’s also pretty clear that on a physical level you and he drew even this week, your thoughts? Kimson: Well I could give a damn if Asis doesn’t respect me. That’s fine, and it’ll be his downfall in the end. The ticket to victory is to know your enemy and to respect what he can do. We fought to a draw this week, it was a tough match and I knew that Asis was hungry, fresh off a serious injury. I was tired, warn out and to be honest Max I could use some time off but I can’t do that. I don’t make excuses and I give credit where it’s due. Asis fought me tooth and nail and we both came out with a shot at Xander. That match last week at Legends has set the stage for probably the biggest match in this companies history. Maybe the biggest Title match of my career. I don’t remember a match that I’ve looked forward to more with the possible exception of my match at Unbreakable with Sovereign. But that match was all personal, this time there’s more to it. There’s a personal attribute with the fact that Asis stuck his nose in my business, that Xander still hasnt’ been repaid for breaking my arm. But the professional end of it is just as big. Dickens: Asis and Xander both have made comments about your quest for the Gold. Xander said you don’t have what it takes to beat him, and Asis seems to find it somewhat pathetic, that you think your worth nothing but a strap of leather and gold. Kimson: Well Xander is a confident man. We’ve all watched him grow and hunt that title for years now and finally he’s got his paws on it. Xander is dangerous, intelligent and he’s willing to do what it takes to keep that title. He knows now that his back is to the wall, 2 men coming for his title who both equally want it. That’s a lot to swallow for Xander who has to figure out how he’ll keep his gold. And in the end Xander is so good that he might keep it. Triple Threat matches are impossible to call straight down the middle in terms of who will win and lose. There’s not many factors. Especially with the stipulations in this one. As far as Asis’ comments are, those are the comments of someone who doesn’t know what he’s talking about. Asis hasn’t been to the top. He doesn’t know what it’s like to hold something so important. To know that feeling of accomplishment. So he’s shot it down. If he didn’t care about the title then he wouldn’t have gone through that match with me last night. But I know Asis wants the title, you can see it in his beedy red eyes. The title however is secondary to Asis and he just basically wants to get his mits on Xander, and that part of the puzzle, as I’ve said before I respect. Asis vs Xander is a very personal situation that needs to be resolved. But we’ve all been thrown together. Asis’ comments were the comments of someone who has no idea what I’m about. I’m not out to get trophy’s and titles to impress people. It’s not me that calls himself an 8 time champion, that’s marketing that Davidson’s been pushing. I could give a damn about all the people I’ve beaten. The only man I care about beating now is Xander and get that title because that is something I’ve fought for, and I can’t give up on it when I’ve gotten so God Damn close. You know what it’s like Max when you get so close to something that you can taste it and then be shut out from it. It’s not important why you went for it. But that you were denied it. The Universal Title would be the ultimate accomplishment for my career. I’ve sacrificed so much for that title and now I’m going to give it one last try, against the toughest odds yet. I don’t give up Max, you know that. But Xander and Asis are about to find out that it takes a hell of a lot to keep me from getting what I deserve and what I want. The Universal title is a personal and professional goal. Professional because it can cement my legacy, let me take that one giant step to immortality in wrestling. So that the day when I decide to walk away from this business and grow old watching my kids play in my backyard, skin their knees, go to school so on and so forth can be done with me leaving everything out in that ring, taking no extra bags with me. I love wrestling Max, but like everyone you can’t do the same thing your entire life. I have the luxury of a unique relationship with millions of people from Asia to Canada and everything in between.. I won’t give that up likely. I’ll give all that up once we’ve both decided that it’s over. And it’s not over by a long shot, and most certainly not until I claim that title for myself. Dickens:What are your thoughts on X-rated. Now that it’s clear that it’s a Triple Threat match you now have to face Xander and Asis at the same time, no holds bared and the title hanging in the balance. Kimson: Well straight up Max this is a huge money match. Tyler Davidson is a brilliant man. He saw an opportunity for a big match with either one of us with Xander at X-rated and it worked out the best for the company with the triple threat. Davidson’s probably going to poster the crap out of every town and city from here back to London billing this match. You think of the names and the history between the 3 of us and you have instant big time money. While that’s 1 part of it I know that Davidson’s past with Asis and Xander is pretty detailed. Me and Tyler are friends outside of work, there’s no secret there, and while that obviously didn’t play much of a role in all of this we’re looking through the barrel of a shot gun ready to go off in this match. Eliminate any rules short of the pinfall or submission has to take place inside the ring. Then you take a Hardcore machine like Xander, willing to do anything to keep that belt. You take Asis the powerhouse monster hungry for respect and revenge and then take myself hell bent on the gold as well and you’ve got one hell of a Main Event for X-rated. This is going to be one of, if not the most challenging matches of my career, I’m not afraid to mention that at all. I know that Xander probably wasn’t to pleased about it, and I doubt Asis cares a whole lot. The match itself will be pretty intense, I could tell you that right now, simply based on the issues at hand and what hangs in the balance. But we’re still 2 weeks away from the show, just imagine what will happen between now and then. Obviously we’ve already seen a lot go down. Xander hasn’t shown his face in a while since Asis beat the crap out of him, last week’s match with me and Asis that people are labeling a classic. So this week it’ll be interesting to see what happens. Dickens: Well James, I want to thank you again for your time. You’ve always been very forward in these interviews, I’m sure that this won’t be our last. Kimson: Most certainly not Max. Thank you for giving me this forum to speak in. Cut camera
