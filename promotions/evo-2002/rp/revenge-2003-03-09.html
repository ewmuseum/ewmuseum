reVenge

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{As the taste entered his mouth he knew that he could not get enough of it. Morpheus Omega was in Shocka’s grasps he was in his arms and Shocka laid into him with everything that he had. And he just wants more…sure he was almost killed in the match being hung over with a chain but do you think he minds? He knows that revenge will come at a price, but Omega will be the one to pay. Shocka and Omega’s hate continues to grow by the second as the plot thickens each week. The two men will be in the same ring again this week and Shocka has eVolved champion Seara in his corner and Omega has that cheating lying son of a bitch Thug roC in his corner.} ‘I can’t believe that son of a bitch has the nerve to come back to eVolution…’ Shocka says as he stares intently into a camera. It’s a rare interview with the man known as the #1 Stunna. ‘You think that I am going to take being pushed around by some little fucking cruiser weight? Fuck that he doesn’t have anything on me…sure he claims to be the best cruiser weight but its not like that division ever took off in every federation around the world like being the heavy weight champion or being the Universal champion. I put thug roC on the map here in eVolution because I let him into my game…he knows how I work…Omega knows how I work…But I taught Omega…and I studied roC…’ Shocka speaks as he shakes his head. ‘You know…I was done with roC once I broke his little bitch ass in half and ran with my belt because I didn’t need or want his help. I directed him to stay in the back but did he? Same with Omega…now I think this is all starting to come together…roC got into Omega’s brain and talked him into coming down to the ring and Tainted Love just to screw me out of my belt because he was jealous! It makes so much sense now…’ Shocka sits back with a look of intensity on his face. ‘People don’t realize what I went through with that belt…I mean I ended a man’s life by taking his title…’ He pauses and looks down and shakes his head. ‘It wasn’t my fault….’ He starts to trail off. ‘But I was able to put that shit behind me and talk myself down a few times with the help of a few close friends, who are still my friends by the way…’ ‘You know what though…if my feud with Omega ends anything like it did with L2J I will be the happiest man on the planet…Omega has no idea what he has started…it’s gonna be like fucking world war III when my plans come full circle…now that I’ve made it all the way around. I cannot believe that Omega is riding on his high horse like he is punking me out in this feud or something, like he had the advantage…I mean what has he done in eVo…what did he do anywhere? Nothing! Omega is not going to come out of this match at 100 Percent I am dead set on ending him.’ {Shocka finishes.} FADE.
