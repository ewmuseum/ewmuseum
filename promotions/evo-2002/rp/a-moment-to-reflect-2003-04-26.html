A Moment to Reflect

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC)) This was intended to form part of the previous episode of Legends. Apoligies, but its only since today that I have been able to have any net access this week. Backstage the cameras pan in between matches to find that despite not being booked during tonights show, Sean Mason is very much in attendance. Dressed in a pair of dark kharki colored jeans and a plain white vest, Sean looks a man on a mission as he waits patiently with EVO reporter Craig Moyles. The crew are set and ready; however the tension could quite easily be cut with a knife. 'Its been a long time Sean' Craig began just coming into camera shot. 'Since your return little has been heard from your camp, instead it would seem your relying very much on actions this time around'. 'Actions indeed' Sean said looking up toward Craig prior to turning back toward the camera. 'For the past few months I've been confined to simply talking a fight, its a welcome change to be able to step out of the treatment room and into the face of my opponent. During my silence I have trained, trained and trained some more; however as the buildup toward the PPV intensified, I allowed myself the timely luxury of reminding my opponent just what all this preparation was in aid of and the very purpose of a return some would deem 'too early'. If Asis was here right now I'd have but one question' Sean smirked, 'I wonder if it was as good for him as it was me?!' 'We all saw the present you sent to Asis this past week' Craig began 'and there can be little doubting that was an attempt at getting into the head of your opponent, the question I have for you is do you think you were successful in doing so?' 'As a child I am sure you were introduced to the concept of ethics. Ethics being the one thing parents try and install in the mind of their children from an early age. That present was as much about reminding Asis of ethics, as it was about getting getting into his head. Unlimited seem to believe they are the divine force within EVO, that they are for some reason allowed to take careers and destroy careers at any moment of their choosing. Let me ask you Craig where are the ethics of that?!' As the words rolled off Seans lips you could begin to see a degree of anger in his eyes and what was more apparent was that this was as much about revenge as anything else. 'I'm not going to deny that going into this forthcoming PPV that Asis does not pose a threat, because he does, he poses a grave threat to not only my title challenge, but also to my career. Do you think I enjoyed watching Asis and the rest of Unlimited parade sickening grins on the back of each and every one of their little conquests?!? Only a few weeks ago I was on the shelf for that very reason, from the side lines I watched Asis come out week after week talking of the demise of Troy Pierce, the destruction of Big Daddy Dreamer and the willingness of Asis and CO to continue to harp on about what was to come. Well lets get something very clear, I'm not about to confine myself to another stay on the shelf. I am not about to allow someone like Asis to place me in an early career coffin. Its time someone not only took upon themselves to stand up to Unlimited, but it is time someone stepped right before them and threatened their very existance. Any man who stumbles and has neither the pride nor courage to get back to his feet, could never truely have ever believed in his cause. However take a look in these eyes' Sean said taking a step toward Craig, 'do these look like the eyes of a man without a cause?! Infact scrap that question and let me ask you this Craig, do these look like the eyes of a man without the conviction to see this task through?!?' 'Well...' Craig stuttered 'Well nothing' Sean interrupted by saying, 'this man you see before you is the one with the conviction to this see this task through and this is the man who is to turn the chief predator of EVO into the chief course on the diet of EVO's Prodigal Son. Forget thinking about a starter, I plan on taking the big fish and serving him up on a silver platter such that for once it will be the turn of Unlimited to be buying the wreaths and placing them upon another past wrestling star; only this time the tears of dismay will be replaced with tears of joy as EVO revels in the moment Unlimited began to crumble around the seams'.
