A mistaken past...a wrestler?never herd of such a thing

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-06 The scene opens as X who calls himself the black label is standing inside his room in Queens,New York. Infront of the man is a tons of papers. He is searching through files as he makes a comment.. 'Where the fuck they get the idea of me being a wrestler..' He keeps on searching through the papers as he finds his application he filled out for the cell divison..He begins to smile. He takes a break as he smiles.. 'This is what the problem was..' He takes the paper and shows it to one of the cameras as it is the exact application he sent to the eVo when he got accepted..He points out that he applied to eVo wrestling at first..That he new nothing about the cell divison at first..he looks at the camera and explains.. 'You see people..Right here shows that I actually applied at eVo..but not for the cell divison, but for the wrestling..So you see this is where the mistake of my past comes to play...' X then sits down as he begins to explain in detail.. 'I tried to play one over on corperate america..I tried to show them that Garcia Malcom Braggs,my full birth name, was like all other wrestlers in eVo. I wanted to match the typcial model in eVo. I wanted to gain acceptence with out them thinking any different..I wanted just for that time period..to blend in..' He reaches into the pile of papers and throws out a certificate..It was a tournament for teenagers..a kick boxing tourny. WinnOn the sheet it says'er of the Queen Kickboxing classic...Garcia Malcom Braggs'.. 'You see I was trying to hide is all along. Then the night before I step into the ring, I read a article that says 'qbX is a fighter trying to step in the cell'. No no, X is a fighter who wanted to be adopted in a wrestling world. Before I knew about the cell divison I was wanting to get into eVo. I was wanting to get rid of corperate america..You people wouldn't believe how many times I got the shoulder because of my fighting style. All people would say to me is wrestling isnt about fighting..its about entertainment..So just a few nights ago when the article was published.... A short pause.. 'It was just another attempt by corperate america to bring down the X. It was just another attemp to try and place me back to square one. I will give it to Ali, he came in the cell an he handled his business. You won this battle Ali..but you are far from winning the war..keep that in the back of your mind..' He looks as he takes the application that was nothing more than a lie and tears it up..He walks over and places it in the carbage can.. 'Its over..Whos fault does X try to blame it on..No one but myself..I should of never tried and lied to my people to gain acceptance.But dont worry it want happen again..The truth is out there..everyone now knows that yes,the mark of X..the black label is a fighter..but its sorry for Josh whore, or whatever his name is...because corperate america has angered X..and its time..to show the world what real fighting is about..' He pauses as he stands and looks dead into the camera.. 'Josh, its over my friend..Your time is over. You believe what you went through at legends is something. You believe that you got your ass handed to you?Well knockouts are for pussies..because this legend will be more than a knockout..it will be a massacre..as the mark of X will live to reign the cell divison..take my word for it..the words of the black label..' The scene fades to black as X looks at the cameras and the papers he put in the trash. He then takes a match and lights it..he throws the match in the garbage as it catches on fire..smoke begins to rise as X does nothing but look down at the fire and smile..scene is now black..
