An After Revelations Party

29 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-29 We open with a huge party at the Thug roC house. Many eVo stars are present, but many are ones that nobody really likes. We see the likes of former New-TV members, Hedges and Morpheus there, but the former stable are keeping their distance. Cell division members, Machine and DND are also present at the time of this party. Drinks of all kind are going around along with card games of all sorts. There are a few select ladies present, but this seems to be mostly a celebration with the boys, rather then a blowout party. Today we'll be watching two of eVo's stars at this party, Thug roC and the big man, Machine. Outside on the porch, we find roC with a few buddies of his. Morpheus is outside as well, but like before, they are keeping their distance from each other. The scene kinda resembles the 4:20 day where everyone is sitting in a circle while Omega is rolling the paper up. Thug roC seems to already be done as he is barely standing with Sean Mason's mask in his hands. He puts the mask on and starts parading around. 'Look everyone....I'm Sean Mason...' Thug roC says, slurring his words at the same time. 'I think I can fool everyone with this corny mask, but obviously I didn't fool Thug roC...he's the greeaaaa.....' Thug roC suddenly falls to the ground with a thud. 'Yea, looks like ol' roCy has had enough.' Omega states. 'But me on the other hand, I've just begun.' Morpheus continues to roll the paper while the camera zooms in on Thug roC's half-dead body. We go inside the house where Machine is playing poker with the likes of Hedges, Trevor Norman, and some others that aren't recognizable by the eVo fans. Seems like everytime you lose a hand, a shot of 151 is the punishment, or the reward depending how you see it. Machine looks wide awake as the others around him are barely able to keep their eyes open. 'How the hell do you do it Machine?' eVo official, Rob B. asks. 'You haven't won a hand yet, but you remain up and wide awake....' 'I have a large liver.' Machine says jokingly. 'That's not the only thing large on you.' Hedges laughs. 'Remember that time they kicked us out of Old Country Buffet?' 'Yea, that was hilarious.' Machine responds. 'Now they have a sign out front that says 'All you can eat, or 75lbs, which every comes first.' The group laughs as Machine gets up to get him another drink. 'Damn, Machine got up and I thought it was an eclipse.' Rob B. says while everyone laughs. 'Very funny....Hey, there's no more margaritas!' Machine looks around the kitchen counter, trying to find more of the lovely drink. 'Thug roC knows where the mix is, tell him to get it.' Hedges continues to play. He wins the next hand only to watch Trevor Norman and Rob B. take another shot of 151. Rob gets up and staggers a little bit. 'That's it, I'm done.' Rob staggers into the living room, aiming for the couch, but falling short and just lays on the carpet. Laying on his back and with his legs and arms spread apart like a star. Machine goes out the kitchen door to the porch only to see Thug roC being picked up by Scott Evans. Machine just laughs at Thug roC who still has Mason's mask on. 'Hey roC, you tryin to make another margarita mix before I go?' 'Damn big boy, you ain't drunk yet?' Morpheus asks with a very surprised look on his face. 'Last time I checked, you were losing every hand of poker.' 'Big Boys never get drunk.' Machine responds as Thug roC stumbles into the kitchen. 'Now if you excuse me, I have a margarita to drink.' Machine enters the kitchen and goes back to the poker game without Rob B., who is still laid out. Everyone is blind to Thug roC who is behind them making the margarita mix. Thug roC puts some 151 and some pina colada in the blender before mixing it. Obviously, there wasn't enough magarita mix as the drink still looked brown. 'Hmmmm, I guess I better put in some more mix.' The obviously high Thug roC reaches for some more Pina Colada mix, but there isn't anymore. Instead he notices another white bottle and thinks that that is more mix. 'This should even it out.' Well, not exactly. It wasn't more mix. Thug roC finishes the mix and throws the 'mix' bottle away. Thug roC pours the mix into a large 8 oz cup. Thug roC continues to stagger as he hands Machine the cup. 'Go ahead big man, go ahead and chug that whole cup.' Hedges encourages. 'I'm telling you, I don't get drunk.' Machine says before smelling the cup. 'Damn it's strong. What you put in here roC?' 'A little bit of 151 and a whole bunch of mix. You should be fine.' Machine takes the cup and starts chugging while everyone encourages him to continue. Machine finishes and wipes his mouth off as he slams the cup down. The scene starts fading as the camera zooms into the trash can, at the bottle of mix that Thug roC put in the margarita. The bottle didn't say Pina Colada though. The white bottle was actually Malibu Rum.... Scene fades
