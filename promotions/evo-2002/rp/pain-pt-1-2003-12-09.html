Pain pt. 1

9 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-12-09 Pain.. Pain is something we all experience in some form or another, pain is something we all recieve no matter how sheltered we are. To feel is to live, to truly live a life you must be able to experience pain and pleasure, but what if neither sensation comes anymore? There's a simple answer to that, your dead already. Scott's father had told him that before he died, he'd told him many things, but that was what had stuck, because he'd seen his father do many things, but the most amazing thing was that he could walk away from anything as if it never happened, barfights, car accidents, you name and you'd see him walking away with a vacant expression upon his face and blood coming from somewhere on his body. There was no sign of pain or anguish within his entire complexion and Scott thought that what he said was to say he was already dead and it was easy to believe that, but what Scott didn't know was that he'd suffer the same fate. Maybe not in the way it happened for his father, but slowly Scott had lost all emotional pain many years ago with the acceptance of his wife and son's deaths. What he never realized was how much he longed for the pain and pleasure to come back, how much he needed them to show how alive he really was. But no matter how much he begged and prayed they were dead feelings to him, maybe not to most people but his pain was dead. Which meant he was dead in a way. But how could someone dead be so alive? How could Scott still have the energy he had to compete? His father had lost everything when his pain left, he had become in a way dead, laying in bed for hours after waking up and barely even showing any signs of energy except when he needed to. Those questions would remain unanswerd as Scott stood in his apartment big as life trying to accept his fate. There were the maybe's everywhere, maybe this, maybe that, but the maybe's didn't matter to him right now, he needed something definitive, he needed to find why it was he'd been losing so much as of late. It never pained him to lose, it confused him, it disoriented him for a while, but he'd get back on track, the eVolved Title had been something to start, but he had to build upon that success. The question was what was he missing in the ring, why after losing that title had he been on such a losing streak. Many things came to him as possible answers but there was only one thing he could really be missing, and that is the fire he'd once had, there was no bright flame burning within him anymore and he knew it. He had sensed its weakening before he realized what it meant, he had no more inspiration anymore, he was no longer the man on the mission he'd once been. Now he was Scott Evans, plain old Scott Evans.
