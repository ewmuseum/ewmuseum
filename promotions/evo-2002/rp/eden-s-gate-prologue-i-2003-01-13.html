Eden's Gate :: Prologue :: I

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
London shifted around his pile of shit he called an apartment. Kicking clothes, newspapers, beer and soda cans all over the floor. The place was trashed. He didn't look pissed. His face was pretty indifferent. Not having much emotion. He tilted his head to the side and just nodded slightly. His apartment, was robbed. It couldn't have been much of what they got, considering there wasn't much to be had. London walked through his living room and into the kitchen. The refridgerator was face down and the food spilled all over the floor. 'How could no one hear this ruckus?' He was thinking outloud. Not even bothering in kitchen he turned and headed towards his bedroom. The door was shut and didn't seem to have been opened.. Slowly he opened it, only to see that everything in his bedroom was intact... 'What the fuck...' Whoever the intruders were, they wanted something and knew exactly what it was. They must have found it, but what were they looking for. London walked over to his bed and sat down. He looked up at the ceiling for a moment and than fell back on the bed. 'Not what I need. Not at all. What the hell did they want? I have nothing...' London was thinking out loud again, when alone. You tend to do that more, you feel like someone is listening to you. He sat back up and rest his head in the palms of his hands for a moment, trying to think of what it could have been that whoever was after. 'I don't have anything of importence. Not anything that someone would want..' Flashes went across London's mind picturing everything that was left laying out, while he went out for awhile. Just some papers, magazines, and his wrestling license. Nothing that anyone would want. 'Who the fuck would steal a wrestling license? That couldn't be it..' He had his doubts though.. He had to go investigate. London went back into the living room, he left it laying on the table by the couch. Shifting through all the other papers, coming up with nothing.. Now getting a bit frantic, he starts tossing papers behind him removing the cludder from that area. 'Ahh, good thing..' He found the license, he knew that wasn't what they were after. 'I suppose I should report this.' London shrugged and walked over to the phone.. He picked it up.. No dial tone. 'You gotta be kidding me.' It was pretty late, almost midnight. The landlord wasn't in until noon tommorow, and he didn't want to bother others to see if there phone was out also.. As he sat there pondering, he noticed very few cars in the lot when he pulled in.. He shrugged it off as a coincidence.. Annoyed about the phone, London walked back into the living room and sat down. His TV was smashed, so there goes watching some TV.. Sitting in the quite always annoyed London, he needed some kinda distraction or noise. He just needed something playing in the background. The silence was bothering him.. He started to think he was hearing things. His mind racing, hearing footsteps that seemed to be coming from the hall. London's eyes shot towards the bottom of the main door. The light in the hall flickered a few times and than died.. London tilted his head back and took a deep breath. Not one to panic often he remained calm and titled his head to the side. The footsteps grew louder, he wasn't sure if it was in his head or if it was really out there. 'Someone fixing the light..' The footsteps got to right outside his door and stopped. Having nothing else to do, London went to the door. He listened... Nothing. He opened the door at a normal pace.. Three shots. All three point blank in his chest.. 'Why?' Was all he thought as the first two hit his chest.. He didn't even see the attacker.. He layed there.. In a pool of his own blood.. That was two years ago.
