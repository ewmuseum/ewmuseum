Somebody, Someone

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th December 2002 Many people were surprised by Asis’ actions at Legends XVII, when he attacked his fellow Unlimited member Jinx. In their match against The Omen Brotherhood, Asis gave Luther Wisdom the Asisination, before delivering it to Jinx as well, before dragging Jinx on top and leaving him to get the pin. Then after the match Asis continued to direct his anger and torment towards Jinx, putting him in the Fade to Black until he was unconscious. It has got people wondering, if he can do that to a “fellow” team member, what is he capable of doing to other people? (Asis can be seen sitting in what appears to be the boiler room of the General Motors Place, the arena where this week’s Legends is supposed to be held. From the amount of steam and condensation though, it cannot be certain where Asis, he could be in a health spar’s sauna, but it’s not very likely.) Last week I destroyed someone who was my allie. But he was my allie because Xander told me he was. I had no feelings towards Jinx one way or the other, I felt no pity for him when I left him unconscious on the mat, yet at the same time I felt no anger towards while I was making him unconscious. Xander explained it to everyone at Legends, Unlimited is for the strong. For whatever reason Xander decided that Jinx no longer had what it takes to be part of the Unlimited anymore, he didn’t have what it takes to survive. Because of this I was told to make an example of him, show everyone what Unlimited does to its own, to give our enemies a taste of what we’re capable of. (Asis stands up and moves around in the steam, you can’t be sure what he’s doing, it’s so misty that if he hadn’t have spoken you wouldn’t know it was him. But then he looks at the camera and one bit of light reflects off his silver hair that is drenched and stuck to his head through condensation. And then you can see the red eyes, the deathly penetrating stare, the eyes that look as if they glow…) I haven’t got a match this week, no opponent has been placed in the ring for me to obliterate. But it doesn’t matter, I’d rather have somebody, someone, but I’ve never had anyone so it just doesn’t fucking matter! Interestingly enough, Luther Wisdom faces Seara in a singles match, with the winner’s stable earning a shot at Unlimited’s gold. Two people in this match that I hold no fond feelings for, but whatever happens I get to take on people that I don’t like, so it comes out well for me. Frosty also has a match this week, fighting for his own personal gold, against the leader of New-Tv. Two leaders battling it out here, maybe they feel it’s for more than just the chance to call themselves “champion”, maybe they think their personal feud will somehow effect the stable war of eVo. I might have to show them that whatever outcome it doesn’t matter… See in some people’s eyes not having a match is a good thing for everyone in eVo. But it’s probably worse. It means that at Legends I’m going to have a lot more free time on my hands. And I’m going to have to find a way to fill that free time and I guarantee it won’t be playing chess. See you all tonight…
