Questions of Faith

16 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/16/02-10/26/02 October 16th, 2002 Moments after his unique victory on Legends XII Jason Steele is celebrating by himself as Brandon Kelley steps foot into his locker room and sends his congrats. Brandon walks towards Steele and the two begin to talk. Brandon Kelley: So what is your answer? Jason Steele: My answer to what? Brandon Kelley: Am I joining your side or are you going to let your brother come into this federation and make a fool out of you by pissing you off, and are you going to let Shocka and Thug Roc try and drag you into their little group until this tag tourney is over and then beat you down until they can strip your half of the title and give it to Quasar? Jason Steele: Look, I am sure you could help me out a lot but I can fed for myself. If you really want to be at my side and follow me to the ring then fine, but one thing has to be set straight. Brandon Kelley: Ok. Jason Steele: I can handle Shocka, Quasar, and Thug Roc, they are nothing to me. The only relationship me and the group will ever have is a relationship in the ring. If they want to win the tag titles they will stay on my good side. For now they are not my allies the only one that is would be Shocka and that is because we are now on the road to the championship. Brandon Kelley: Now that I think about it, Shocka and the road to the tag titles is the least of your worried mind. Jason Steele: Worried mind? Brandon Kelley: Yes, you are worried, correct? Jason Steele: Worry? What is there to worry about? I have proven myself. If it wasn't for my self and my victory I would not be in the predicament. I have nothing to worry about. I face a man I could never trust at the ppv they call Faith. In all reality I have no faith and I have no worries. This man thinks he is nothing more than purely great, when the truth is he is a nobody in this place. Brandon Kelley: What are you saying? Jason Steele: Jinosyde thinks his is nothing less than great. I have a feeling this will be quicker than that tag match. This man has nothing but his past to talk about. I have more than him, he feels I am a jobber, but when the bitter truth is revealed he will be the one lying in the center of the ring busted open with his on blood falling to his lips. This man talks about how he knows me and how I smell like ball sweat. He knows nothing and he will always know nothing. The future of this Canadian Hero is bright but that damn Canadian has nothing going for him. His failure almost cost me my first match. He failed but I succeeded. Jinosyde you think you got what it takes and you think you know who I am then we will find out October 27th at Faith. The loss on Wednesday was your flaw; you didn't want it to happen. Yet it did. It comes to show you that you’re not all you think you are. I got in the ring with a man I didn't trust and walked out with a partner I know I can't trust. How do you think I feel about you? Knowing that you failed me and betrayed any trust of mine you could have had. Now it’s my turn to take something away from you. Its time you lose that damn pride of yours. To me, you will be nothing and you all ways will be. You think your past haunts you know, just wait till you see your future. You take a glimpse at me and you think you know everything, saying that popping steroids is my game, though hard work and dedication is the true drug of my choice Thinking wrestling was my first choice, but watch the tapes and you will know that wrestling was not my first choice and it surely wasn't my only choice. To be honest my father was indeed a former wrestler but he never made it to the professional level after a neck injury that almost placed him in a wheel chair for life. You think you know me Jino but to be serious you have no idea about my past and you know nothing of my life and to be honest with you for a moment, you never will understand me. Brandon Kelley: Well that’s good to know that you’re not worried about your match with Jinosyde but, Jason we need to discuss further our deal of me being your manager. Jason Steele: Kelley, you can take that deal and that idea it’s self and shove it up your ass. Because all your going to do is hold me back and drag me down when you fall. I am not up for that. So why don’t you take your sorry ass back to your million dollar custom made Italian leather couch and begin to realize that nobody wants you here. Not I, not S., not Davidson, hell not even your very own ex-wife who by the way says to tell you to stop calling her. So, leave and never speak to me again. My career has just began and frankly yours ended with the fall of EdW. Brandon Kelley exit the room and walks down the hall to the exit of the building and to the parking lot. Jason Steele then stands up and shuts the door and sits back down. Jason Steele: I sit here build up with envy of a man I barley know. I am envious to the fact that he actually thinks that He is superior to me. I have been through more tough times than he ever will. If he would look in the mirror he would see a loser. Men that can’t stand to lose but will never pull off another win. Next Sunday morning you may want to check your pulse because your heart will be racing faster and faster every moment with fear running though your veins. You will feel threatened. Wednesday night I saw you laying on the mat with your shoulders pinned to the mat as I was attempting to get our team the win. Your shoulders were down and I was covering a man across the squared circle. I understood then why I could never trust you. That would be because I envy your need to win. You feel as if you can win every match and that you’re better than us all. Look at you your two hundred and forty-two pounds. I have at the least thirty pounds over you and you’re looking at me as if I am a scrawny bastard, when you’re the one that is smaller. The thought of you ever beating me scares me, because if a man of your fortitude can beat me then I don’t know why I am in this business. Then again there is no way that a man like you could ever defeat me. Stating that I am a second or third generation wrestler is a lie. I have been in wresting for sometime now. I was doing wrestling in middle school on the team and then going home and performing in a backyard federation that my brother and I started. For some time now I didn’t know why I liked wrestling so much and I now understand why I always had it in question as if I should be apart of this business. Its people like you that make this business go down the drain. People who can’t stand to lose. Though it maybe your job but win or lose you get a hefty pay check at the end of the period. Win or lose next week I know that I will pull in some cash. Though with winning I could earn a possibly two thousand more than losing but still why do I need the extra cash? So I can spend it on worthless things, such as hookers, stereos, and house that I will visit maybe once or twice a year. Why would I go and do that? So before you go and predict the fact that you’re better than me. Prove to me that you are worthy of this great business and become a man of this sport and not a man for the cash. Staring in the eyes of any opponent is nothing less than a pure joy. I love smelling the fear over the joy of confidence. There is nothing more breathe taking than that scent. You may feel confident but the truth is that Sunday night you will be crawling on your knees to a stretcher yelling and screaming for revenge. The trust issues I once had no longer linger because I know now what your truly all about and that is winning, winning, winning. I understand that once I beat your ass Sunday night you will be whining and crying on how in some way shape of form you got screwed. You will take a brush with a great one and you will know that winning will never mean a thing. Its time you learned your lessen about messing with the wrong people and pointing out shit that has nothing to do with me. Take a trip to my past and you will feel like a moron because you know nothing as you always will. The facts you try and point out are flaws of your own imagination. The camera fades to black as Jason Steele stands up and begins to change out of his wrestling attire, Steele leaves the arena an hour later and is finally caught back up with him a week later as he arrives to the Louisiana Superdome where he meets up with his younger brother Mark Steele. The cameras begin to role as the two men begin to speak upon the upcoming pay per view Mark Steele: The family will unite and form an unstoppable team that no one can destroy. My brother and I will finally unite as one again and we will make sure that our first victim will fall and then crawl to the point he must pray and question his faith. Jason Steele: The two brothers will become one and our experience will rise as we work together to work our way to the top of the rankings. We may not be able to go after the tag titles for a while but we sure in the hell can knock a few heads around and take ourselves high in the rankings. As I take my time and think about my match with Jino and wonder I will show the crowd that this man is nothing to them and that he is just a cocky arrogant bastard that doesn’t even deserve to be a part of this wonderful company. The man I will be facing at this pay per view is an unworthy Canadian. I won and he lost, many people say I screwed him; the truth is he almost screwed me. I was the man of the hour, I held my shit up and all he did was get his ass beat and pinned. I held the team up. Though he didn’t get to move on in the tourney, I did. I undoubtedly believe I am better than him in ways he will never know. I worked my ass off these past few years trying to make it in this business and now I have some punk that has been working his life and building it around this business wanting to take my spotlight and shine it on him. Mark Steele: The questions of Faith will soon arise and you will not understand how to answer. Is it what we all need in our lives or is it like everything and just a pile of crap that the people want us to believe? Will faith help us in times of crisis? Will it help us in time of loss? Will the bullshit these people feed us about faith and how everyone needs to find us help lead us to a victory of any sort? There are millions of answers because everyone believes in something different. It’s up to your self to find out what you believe and how far you will go. Will you praise the lord everyday just to see a miracle happen? Or will you rise above it all and find out what this religion shit is all about. Faith is nothing more than a lie; it’s just a cover up to make people happy. You love god? You love Jesus? So do you still believe in Santa Clause or should I just come out and say you’re a fucking moron, for loving these people. You never met them, you don’t know how they were, and you don’t know if they even existed. Learn to accept life for how it is given to you. They say drugs are bad but read the bible you will understand those guys were tripping on some kind of shit. Will this world ever understand that if God did exist that he wasn’t the angel he has been pushed out to be. Follow your own footsteps and learn from your mistakes in time you will realize that religion is a way to face your fears. Grow some balls, grow some guts and learn to deal with life yourself or you will never be able to face death and what the truth about it all is. Live your life to the fullest for you will never know when or if it will end, so take the risk and learn from the mistakes. Jinosyde thinks he knows all but do you know about the words I am speaking. Do you know that this shit is the truth? Do you understand we are not alone in this galaxy or this solar system? Learn that god if he did exist didn’t stop at making humans on earth. Take a look around, you don’t know everything and you never will. Stop making judgments and understand that your failure to succeed is nothing to our liking. Jason wanted you to succeed as much as he wanted to. But he didn’t care if you actually did it with him. As long as he didn’t turn out to look so bad he was fine. He has a reputation to keep in tact so if you want revenge, then take it out on yourself because in his eyes he did nothing wrong but everything right. You need to seek revenge find what it’s about and go some where else for any revenge done can return you haunt you later in life. Think about it. Think about the questions I stated earlier and how you would answer them, then tell us all how you feel about faith and revenge. Are you man enough to face your fear of losing to a man that could be better than you. Or are you just afraid of losing it all? The scene fades to black.
