A Letter FROM Home

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Written October 3d, Arrived October 6th Dear Will. I’ve never felt anything quite as strong as what I felt when I read your letter to me the other day. Actually to be honest William, when I got the letter I left it on the counter top for about a day and a half, I didn’t look at it, I didn’t open it. I just simply wasn’t ready to read whatever was inside those pages. I’m sure that you understand that sweet heart but it’s just that everything has been so hard for so long. I’m sure that you don’t know anything of what’s happened over these past years but that’s not your fault, and no one in the family blames you for anything that you’ve said or done to us. We know more than anyone what can happen to you when your on your bad days. And while we did not support you in your career I always said a prayer for you at night when I went to sleep. Even more so after our last meeting. So you can imagine my hesitation to read what you wrote. Then I broke down, I called your sister Marge to come over so that someone could be here when I read it just incase it was to much. I clasp her hand and read it out loud and we both cried. We’re both so happy to hear from you. We do love you William, you are apart of our family, regardless of what the world has turned you into. We’ve seen you over came it. I watched you on television last week, I see you on there and I know it isn’t some story that they’re putting you through. I understand your business is about a lot of things that aren’t as they seem William, but I saw you there and I looked at you and saw that boy I raised. I saw that child that I used to hold in my arms and kiss tonight. I didn’t see the beast that almost destroyed my marriage or nearly crippled your brother. I didn’t see the man that put to Nuns in the hospital, or the man that Killed another man in one of those God awful wrestling rings. I saw my son. I saw the man that I always wanted to see, standing up for himself, fighting the good fight. I cried when I saw you. You’ve grown into a man, not masked by hate and revenge. Your not that monster that took my son away from me 6 years ago. There hasn’t been a day gone by William that I haven’t thought about you. No day has gone by that I haven’t questioned myself if I had done something wrong when you were in my womb. I hated myself and blamed myself for so long William, and I know that your reading this and probably asking me not to say such things. But since we have never sat down and spoken about my guilt I needed to write it hear. I know that it’s not my fault, your father and I were very careful with you and as your father put so cruelly it’s just the way God made you. Although I refused to believe that, and you in your letter to me last week proved it! Your not just a victim of circumstance William, you’re a human being. You’re a man, you’re a success and you’ve conquered your demons! You don’t know how Proud I am of you Honey. I’m crying even as I’m writing this. Marge is beside me with a huge smile across her face because I haven’t felt like this in a very long time. Of course Honey, it’s not so good news around here. Your Father first of all, well it really pains me to say this but he passed away about a year ago from Lung Cancer. I know that you didn’t know about it, and we tried to get a hold of you to make sure you knew. But at the time you were in the Mental Hospital and the Doctors said that you were not allowed visitors or messages from anyone because of how bad you were at the time. I don’t know how right that was, I figured that they would have told you or something about such a loss to your family. I guess that’s how it goes sometimes, I know that they didn’t exactly treat you all that well there but sometimes Doctors have to do things that might not seem right to a lot of people. A lot of people made some very rude comments about you, and I’m sorry that so many of our family and our friends don’t respect you as a person William, but they were all looking at you from that damn “Sovereign” persona that ruled your life. Your father was a very special man William, and although you never got to know him because he was not exactly the most “open” of people he deep down loved you. He was your Father William, and I know that the last time you saw each other you basically tried to kill him he did Love you. Actually after that happened and we left the building he got in the car, cleaned the blood from his nose and didn’t say anything negative about you for a very long time. And it wasn’t because he was stewing in his juices. It was because no matter how bad a Son can be, his Father will always love him. He’s looking down at you now William and smiling, I can tell. I miss him so much, Marge moved back to the city about 5 months ago with her family and they keep me company. As you can imagine 35 years of marriage you can really take him for granted. Sometimes I wake up in the middle of the night and cry because he’s not there behind me snoring away. I wish you could have been here for the Funeral William, perhaps the next time your in Chicago I might take you to his Grave site and show you his Tombstone, he built it himself actually, you know he was quite handy. As for your Brothers, Roger is working in Houston Texas on an Oil platform and making outstanding money. He had run into some serious problems with money about 5 years ago with credit cards and gambling. His construction company went under and he moved to Texas, took the proper courses and now is a roughneck on one of the platforms. He works for 3 weeks at a time then 3 weeks off. He generally sticks around Houston though on his days off. His money problems are all gone, and he’s about to Marry this wonderful girl from Montreal Canada, her name is Monica, she’s a really pretty girl and she’s got her Ph. D in English Literature and will be starting to teach in Denton at the University of North Texas! She’s a sweetheart of a girl. On a note you might find interesting Roger called last week and he said that on the boat they watch you on Television. He’s told everyone that you’re his Brother but they don’t believe him, it’s funny because obviously you have quite a fan base William, a lot of people find you rather interesting and I know that you have always wanted to have one Standing ovation in your life, for anything really and I know that you’re going to earn that ovation William because you are a wonderful person! Regardless of what the past says. Anyway, that was a rant, Roger wants you to write him to prove that you are who he says you are. It would make his day. I’ve enclosed his address at the bottom of his letter. Roy turned 18 two days ago and is currently in school in Sweden! He got accepted to an exchange program over the summer and got sent off to Sweden to study his grade 12! He’s doing really well. I really miss him though, you can imagine because Will, you haven’t been around in 15 years neither has Roger either so Roy has sort of been the spoilt one in the family. He’s so smart William, and such a kind boy. He never has a bad word to say about anyone, he never over reacts to life’s little challenges. He has a girl friend in Sweden to, I was a little bit surprised about that but apparently she goes to a private Catholic School and I’ve even gotten to speak to her on the phone, she’s an angel William. Oh how I wish I could tell you all these things in just one letter but there’s so much to say. We have so much time to make up for but I know William that it’s next to impossible. I know you are busy, weekly shows, appearances, traveling, all those things. But maybe sometime soon you could make a trip home. There is a guest room here William, and I’m ready to see you again. I mean, it will be sort of strange, I mean how can we truly know that “Sovereign” is gone. I mean, I believe you. I know, I trust your word. You’ve always been honest with everyone and if you say truly and with complete Faith that Sovereign is gone then you are welcome into my home, and back into our lives with open arms! We’ve missed you William and we hope that this situation isn’t a temporary one! I hope that you write back to us as soon as possible. I’ve got my TV set and ready to go for Wednesday night because I want to see my Boy on television again. We’ve also got a few things here at the house that are yours that we’d like for you to have back. There are some pictures and some other odds and ends that we think you should have. Plus there was something from your father that he wanted you to have. You see when he was told about his cancer the wrote a letter to all of his children, and told me to give it to them when they were ready for it. Roy, Marge and Roger all have theirs, now maybe it’s your turn William. I’ve not read it, because it’s written for your eye’s only. I hope that if you come to visit you’ll read it and learn something about how much your Father loved you, regardless of how he never showed it. And on that note Son I’m going to end this letter. I don’t know what else to say on paper, I just would think that face to face would be so much more proper. I know you feel the same way because you even told me that you hated talking on the phone or writing letters. You felt it was far to impersonal and that if you were going to have a meaningful dialog with someone you must have it face to face. I agree with you William, when you are right, you are dead on. I hope you get this letter in good cheer and health. May God bless you William, I love you so very, very much. Love, Mom. PS: I hope you aren’t afraid of Dog’s anymore Honey. I got a Boxer just after Dad died because I felt it would help me out emotionally. He’s a great dog but I know you had a fear of Dogs when you were a boy. I figure a big strong man like yourself has probably gotten over that.
