Training Day

2 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-2-03 'GET UP FROST!! GET UP!!' The voice of 'The Tru One' Joshua Krowe can be heard screaming at the top of his lungs. The scene opens up and Krowe is standing over Frost, in a wrestling ring in the middle of a gym.. Frost staggers to get up, and Krowe smacks Frost in the back of his head. People gather around the two men training and someone are unimpressed with Krowe's behaviour. 'I SAID GET UP!!' Krowe screams. Frost looks up at Krowe and reaches for him. Krowe releases the angry look on his face and helps Frost up. Krowe whipes off his forehead, and Frost staggers backwards off into the corner of the ring and he holds himself up by the top ropes on each side of him. 'You okay?' Krowe asks Frost. Frost is breathing hard and the blood from his mouth and ears just says it all. Frost shakes his head and tries to stand up. 'We've been training........for.......eight straight hours. I'm.......I'm suprised.......that you haven't broke my nose.' Frost manages to get out. Krowe nods his head reassuringly. 'I'm sorry, I just know first hand the damage Xander can start doing after he starts a winning streak.' Krowe says. 'So you need to take the hard bumps.' Krowe adds. Frost looks up at Krowe and a bead of sweat falls from his brow. Frost takes his taped up forearms and whipes away the blood from his face and he stands up, and just stares at Krowe. Krowe looks at him and smiles. 'Bring it on....' Krowe says to Frost. Frost stares at Krowe and waits for Krowe to make the first move and when Krowe barely takes a step forward, Frost spears Krowe down to the mat but keeps ahold of his legs and then quickly locks in the Elevated Clover-leaf he calls 'Domestic Blend' and Krowe is screaming and yelling and begging for Frost to quit, when Frost finally does he looks down at Krowe. 'I took out every single member of Unlimited, single handedly before you came along in Team eVolution. I appreciate the help, but I end Xanders streak. Oh, and Asis? He has to worry about Sean Mason.....' Frost pauses and smiles. 'At Legends, I will prove that even though I am the 2002 Face of the Year but I am still the cold chill in everyone's life....' Frost finishes. Scene fades.
