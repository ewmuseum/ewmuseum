Mind if we crash the party?

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Xavier Citizen and Luther Wisdom, the tag team known as the OMEN Brotherhood by their manager and mentor Malcolm King. The question posed to them by many in the Evolution lockerroom is simplpy why? Why are they here and what do they hope to accomplish? Right now however they are seen at the Evolution Concession stand buying Unlimited merchandise...... LW:'Look a Tyler davidson bobble head....' XC:'Pick one up after awhile it may become a collectors item; or I can pop the head off like I used to with my sisters barbie dolls...' LW:'Are you implying that Tyler is a barbie doll or that you used to play with them?' XC:'....I hate you!' Xavier soon notices the Evolution camera crew and decides now is as good a time as any to deliver a promo.... XC:'The Unlimited.....the greatest stable of all time. LOL; kinda of a loaded statement don't you think. You see it's easy to be great when you are constantly confronted with mediocrity. A stable filled with talent that can't possibly get over on their own but together find the courage to become feared....loathed....disliked. How many times must you hear the rambling of idiocy before you realize that The Unlimited is very much limited in name as well as fact. Ahhhh but this issue I have with Asis is personal; you see it's all over a whore on his rise to the proverbial top.' Xavier pays for an 'I came to see the Unlimited' t-shirt..... XC:'You see Asis when you attacked me it served as a wake up call. I was content collecting a check and doing as I was told by Tyler Davidson. Hell I had realized that champions are a dime a dozen; but jobbers....they are the ones that stay employeed. Hell why be a Hogan, a Flair, an Austin.....when being a Brooklyn Brawler, a SD Jones, a Tommy Dreamer ensures that you will collect a check regardless of your performance. And Asis, I was at that point in my career....until you attacked me. But I'm not made at you...in fact I applaud your initive; no Asis what pissed me off is that YOU DIDN'T HAVE THE COURTESY TO SAY THANK YOU.....for not coming after you and allowing you to join the Unlimited when you did. Your motivation was also flawed...as that skirt in my opinion was not worth it...I could have been her problem...and not yours. But Asis what's done is done....I cannot change the past as you set the train in motion. As much as I didn't want to go back to that sick sorted asshole Malcolm King....you forced me; as much as I didn't want to become part of a tag team....you forced me. AS MUCH AS i HAVE TRIED NOT TO LIKE CHRISTINA AGULERIA.....I have to admit her new single Dirrty holds a special place in my heart; but HELL I BLAME YOU FOR THAT TOO!!!!!!! Enjoy your time with the Unlimited Asis....because you have just gained me as your number one fan. Is it however really an obsession if the person knows you are coming for them?' Luther Wisdom throws on an Evolution cap...... 'Sing praises my brother; for the Next Generation of Old School has arrived. What better way.....I said what better way; than to go against theself proclaimed greatest stable alive The Unlimited....the stable that had a legacy of contribution to the closure of EDW; wait a minute that wasn't done with this group of guys. A stable which spawned the birth of not one...but two promotions; wait that wasn't with you guy either. So let us run down the accomplishments of THIS particular Unlimited.....' buys a poster of HBC in a bikini; unrolls it and smiles.... LW:'Ahhhh yes that is a major contribution. But lets face facts....Xander, Jinx, HBC, and Asis have done nothing to add to the legacy of the Unlimited. quite the contrary they are living off of what the name used to mean. Xander has yet to use this platform for the superstardom which is by right his....yes I do recognize talent. No what this Unlimited have become is a vehicle for disgruntled and underacheiving talent to use for a rub. One man in particular I have issue with.....Jinx. He's riding the wave....hopping the coattails and hoping for the proverbial pot of gold at the end of the rainbow. A failed Cell fighter in a failed Cell division with hopes that failure for him will not happen a third time......' looks at Citizen.... 'Unfortunately for brother Jinx, things come in threes and this situation is no different. But now that we are here the question is what does Evolution do with us.....what does The Unlimited do with us? Well if the current Unlimited track record is any indication.....like the San Francisco Giants they can't close the deal. Malcolm King...was supposed to be out of this sport; what happened? It's called follow through gentlemen something Xander and his men can't seem to accomplish. What doesn't kill us makes us stronger.....I doubt the Unlimited has what it takes to kill us.' XC:'Tic toc....tic toc....The Unlimited forgot to set their clocks back an hour so their 15 minutes of fame has expired a long time ago. What to do what to do? First we make an appearance on Legends and accept whatever retribution will come our way; you see boys and girls you have one shot and one shot only to make a lasting impression.....try to get it right this time around!' Luther leaves his change on the table.... LW:'Keep it, The Unlimited may need something to go into their retirement funds.'
