Homework...

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in darkness and a loud clicking noise be heard in perfect sequenced, time intervals. A shadowed over, darkened figure can be seen creeping past a window where a dim light was shining out; the figure disappears once again. A few moments pass and nothing is seen or heard in the surrounding area. A high pitched, shattering noise can be heard screaming through the silence extremely loud. The scene pans to the right side, where the same figure can be seen reaching through a broken window of what looks to be an office door. A sharp sounding cry can be heard from the person, they step back and hold their left arm up. Turning slightly, the face of Sky Night can be made out vaguely through the darkness. Sky turns back around and reaches through the window once again, she turns her arm and the mechanical noise of the door being unlocked can be heard. Sky opens the door and quickly ducks inside; hoping that she didn't draw to much attention by smashing the window moments ago. The door shuts back behind her and a streak of blood can be seen running down the jagged, sharp glass. Inside of the room, Sky Night can be seen with a flashlight in her hand and prowling around with a stern, blank look on her face. Sky Night advances over to a filing cabinet and starts flipping through file folders and stops at one and stares. Sky removes it from the group of them and tucks it under her arm, she continues searching and finds another folder which she removes also. Sky with a mischievous smile on her face, she opens a folder and searches through it. 'Medical records, social security numbers, addresses, hell even how much those overrated chumps are being paid' Sky says and chuckles, she continues 'This will be exactly what I need!' The cabinet door is slammed shut, but slowly opened again and Sky has a widening grin on her face. Sky flips through them again and this time the bold lettering can be seen on the folder that reads 'Heart Break Chick'... Sky holds the folder to her face and kisses it. 'This one, I am very sure will help me in the future. HBC is definitely the first lady of eVolution, but she will also be the first who is forced to retire if she doesn't stop trash talking Twisted Reality!' Sky retorts and tucks the file under her arm and closes the drawer. Sky turns off the flashlight and not wasting any time, she strides quickly and pushes the door back open and slides to a stop outside of it and looks side to side. Someone can be heard down the corridor. 'McHenry, I just heard something down the east wing. Lets go stat!' a hushed voice and light footsteps can be heard. Another voice is heard replying 'right, lets go!' Sky with a calm-collected look on her face, she dashes down the hallway and turns into a pitch black hallway. Sky running now and cursing can be heard 'shit!'. The flash light turned back on, it shows a dead end hallway with an oversized eVolution symbol painted on the wall. The flash light is turned back off and the cracking of glass can he heard from being stepped on. 'You check inside and I will overlook the area and make sure they are not trying to flee the scene of the crime.' The one voice can he heard saying. Once again the other voice replies 'alright!' A foot kicking off the wall and a key-up from Sky Night can be heard. The footsteps become closer like the man was within feet now. The flashlight shines partially the direction of her and back away to the other side. A sigh of relief hard and the light shines brightly inside the dead end of the hallway. The man with an angered look on his face searches the area, but Sky Night cannot be located. 'Damn punks, how those teenaged freak children break into here I have no clue... This time, we are going to catch and beat them like their parents should have well they had the chance' The man says complaining to himself and turns back around the other way. Moments pass and the flashlight of Sky can be seen and pointed towards the roof, she can be seen semi-clinging to the roof and in the corner. Sky breathes heavily in the intense moment and carefully jumps back down to her feet, she wipes sweat from her brow. Listening carefully, Sky can over hear the voice of one of the men from before. 'They must of snuck past us and fled down the west corridor and most likely the south hand stairwell. You take the elevator now and meet me coming up the stairs, while I run down. We will play some cat and mouse on their asses and beat them with our clubs and taze them!' The voice says rudely. The footsteps can be heard leaving the scene and becoming more quiet again until they eventually cannot be heard at all. Sky turns off the flash light and disappears back into the midst of the dark shadow, her voice can be heard... 'amateur rent-a cops!' Sky laughs and disappears once again and the scene fades.
