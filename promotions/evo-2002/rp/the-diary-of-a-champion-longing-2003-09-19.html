The Diary of a Champion: Longing

19 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-09-19 He tossed his keys on the table as he flicked the light switch on. Being in Europe was starting to really work on him now. It’d had been a long, long time since he even got a chance to go home to Buffalo to be with his family. But now it wasn’t getting any easier. James never minded being on House shows, in fact he really enjoyed them. He got to play a little different role. The Fans just wanted to see the Champion. He’d been working a program with Thug roC recently on the shows and was trying to help roC get a little more over as a Face by acting a little more heelish. But it seemed like no matter what happened he always had the fans cheering for him. Not that he minded, that in the end was the reaction he wanted. But home is what he wanted. And now with Leya in charge of the committee she was booking him left right and center to keep him completely off his game. He had to fly to Paris tomorrow for a quick photo shoot, then he was coming back to London that night to be at a football game he was supposed to appear at. Then the next day an autograph signing, then photo opts for Faith and then it just kept going. He was working on 5 hrs of sleep and his training routine was chopped in half. But with all that being said “What the hell is all this crap about Undisputed Championships” he thought to himself. First off the Cell Division wasn’t something that he cared about. Hell, most times he didn’t even watch the fights backstage because he was far to busy focusing on what he was having to do that night. He wasn’t a fighter, he didn’t know what the hell to expect, especially against this O’Halloran guy. Sure, he was bad ass and a tough customer and regardless of what the rules were he knew that he was in a lot of trouble. So what if there was a Wrestling match afterwards. You can’t wrestle if you are out cold. He knew that he was a brawler in the ring. A lot of people classified him as a well rounded wrestler. Hard not to be when you’ve been at it for so long. And he felt that maybe he could pull out a few tricks but this was the Cell. This was something completely foreign to him and he didn’t have a clue how to prepare himself for it. Maybe if he just takes as much as he can and not risk anything and try to even it up in the wrestling match. Yeah, maybe that was his only hope. Because he wasn’t about to lose his Universal Title to someone who hadn’t even fought a wrestling match before. So what if he’d be an Undisputed Champion, who cares. He didn’t want that, he didn’t want to fight in the Cell and or the Ring Three of Four times a week. That would be to much for any man. But he just couldn’t give Leya that satisfaction of him losing or even worse just giving up. Submitting to her. He couldn’t do that, it would be wrong. And worse it wouldn’t be who he really is. He walked through the kitchen and opened the fridge and pulled out a jug of water, ice cold and then grabbed a clean glass out of the dish rack. He gave it a quick look over to make sure it was clean and then poured himself a tall glass of water and took a healthy gulp. His body was really sore, more sore then normal. He wasn’t yet back to %100 from X-rated and his match tonight with Scott Frost was particularly physical. “It was a great match” he thought to himself, although marked with interference and cheap shots. Shocka meant business, that was one thing that he knew he could count on. And despite Shocka’s arrogance and self righteousness he was a competitor and a dangerous threat to Kimson’s championship. But right now Shocka was the least of his worries. He had a 5'7 110lbs woman who was his boss and sister trying to destroy him one week at a time. He had a chain linked fence with a monster inside that was looking forward to proving the Cell was just as exciting as a Wrestling ring and with a beef with Cameron Gri... “There ya go Jimmy” he thought to himself. “I think it’s time I give Cameron a call.” He looked at his rolex watch, it was around 2:30 and to late, far to late to call Cameron tonight. If anyone knew Damon O’Halloran it was Griffin, and maybe the two could strike some sort of deal. At least some advice would be enough to settle him mind, put himself at ease a little bit more. He didn’t fear Damon, not at all. He just was afraid of what the motivations were on all parties. Did Leya want to finish him off in one fell swoop? Or was this part of a bigger plan? Either way he had to be prepared for everything. He walked over to the living room taking a sip from the water that he had in his hands. This was his London flat that he and his wife bought 2 years ago when he was wrestling full time in the United Kingdom. He loved London so much, thought that it was a great city, and although yes he missed home he kept this place here because he liked a chance to go somewhere else, somewhere he called home. He walked last the Television, last time that thing was turned on must have been a year ago maybe. He sat down on the sofa and looked to the answering machine. The number 1 flashed, he knew who it was. Victoria always called after a show, always wanted a call back because it made her feel better. He pressed the button closest to him, the cold computer voice spoke. “You have....1... new message. To listen to the message pre...” He cut it off, knowing what to press, the computer processed the command. Victoria’s voice came from the speakers. “Hi Honey, just calling like usual. I know that you’ve been busy with everything that is going on but I was hoping that you would be back home here in Buffalo for the 22nd. I hope that you remember what day that is.” “Damn, that’s Martha’s Birthday!” he muttered under his breath. “I know that things are sort of out of control but you know kids, it’s her 8th birthday and I don’t know if she’d understand why her Daddy isn’t there at her big day. You only turn 8 once. But anyway, I just wanted to call, I watched the show tonight, you did a great job and I love you very much. Okay, gimmie a call back in the morning maybe?” The click of the phone hanging up sounded like a huge thunderclap. He had been separated from his family before. That’s the life of a Pro-wrestler. But missing birthdays wasn’t something he had done yet. Birthdays were a huge thing in his house. Parties, gifts, clows, whatever the kids wanted Daddy saw to it that they got it. But in the end he realized that all they wanted was Daddy to be home. When you are on the road 6 days a week, sometimes all 7 your kids start to really miss you. And when you aren’t even 10 yet, well they don’t always understand. With the family now at 4 kids, Sarah who was 10, Marta who was turning 8 on Monday, Leya was 4 and little Logan who wasn’t even a year old yet it was getting harder and harder to be there each time. He reached for the phone, he wanted to call home. He spotted his watch and counted back 4 hours... 10:30, Vicky might still be awake, but if he woke up any of the youngens then it would be hell to pay for Vicky. Wait for tomorrow, call at around 8 and talk to everyone! Yeah that would be the trick, let them all know that he misses them. But wait, 8 tomorrow night he had to be in Manchester wrestling at a live event. And his cell phone didn’t exactly work that well overseas. He took his hand off the phone and slumped down. His back sort of ached as he took a sip from the water. He knew he should just go and get to bed. But it almost wasn’t worth it. His mind was running a mile a minute and he didn’t know what was what anymore. Leya Shocka O’Halloran Being stuck over here 4000 miles away from his daughter on her 8th Birthday. This was starting to look like it wasn’t worth it. Maybe the old saying is true, “be careful what you wish for, because you may just get it”. That being said, he was the Universal Champion. He wasn’t going to give that up. Because regardless of what he wanted, what his Wife wanted that there were millions and millions of people out there who deserved to get what they pay for. He was paying his price. In the end, he hoped that what comes around, goes around. He looked out the window, London was quite at 245 on a Thursday night. Really it is a city that hardly sleeps. There’s always something interesting going on. He thought about a walk, but his knee begged him to remain seated. He took a final big gulp of water before shutting his eyes for a moment, letting out a deep breath of air before calmly and quietly... and even peacefully falling asleep.
