Frost Effect: Chap. 1: Ruthless; page 2

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 20 'Scottie! Get over here!' yelled a deep voice. The scene fades in, and you can see three men standing at the edge of a bridge. Then another male appears who looks like a young version of Scott Frost. His bowl style hair cut, parted in the center falls infront of his face slightly as he looks over the edge of the bridge with what seems to be three friends. 'What do you think it is?' calmly asked another voice. 'I don't know.' another voice whispered. 'It looks like a body.' calmly announced the young Frost. The camera pans over to the side of the bridge, to reveal a man sprawled out in a sacrifical position. Suddenly one of the other four says something.... 'Lets go down there. It might still be alive.' demanded one of the three with the young Scott Frost. A sudden silence fills the air, and the only thing that can be heard is the suttle bellow of the cold town air. 'Yeah. He's right.' suddenly Frost speaks up. The four young men look at one another and then back over the bridge almost in sync with one another. Then they start walking to the edge the bridge. Pure silence, just the sound of wind blowing through the air can be heard. They get to a decent drop off point and each young man drops down and they continue their decend to the bottom of the bridge with each other huddled together. When they reach the body, the young Scott Frost takes charge of the group and he bends down next to the body. 'He ain't breathing.' states Frost as he checks for a pulse. 'He is freezing cold.' says Frost. 'Man, what are we supposed to do now?' everyone begins to wonder. One of the men start to walk away to the other side of the bridge away from the body. 'Kenny! Where are you going!?' yelled Frost. 'I think I am going to be sick!' replied Kenny. 'Michael, Donnie. Go with him make sure he is okay. I will be fine. I am going to go look for a cop up on the bridge.' exclaimed Frost. The young Frost begins his journey back to the top of the bridge after the other two men acknowledge Frosts request. The young Frost is whispering to himself, as his eyes are open with shock. 'What are we going to do?' whispered Frost. 'They are going to blame this on us.' the now trembling voice of the young Frost slightly echo's throughout the area. Suddenly the converstation that the young Frost is having with himself is interrupted. 'Scottie! You better see this!' yelled one of the other young men. The young Scott Frost stops and turns around as the camera pans over with him. Two people can be seen standing up, and Frost begins to run down the incline over to his small group of friends. When he reaches his friends, Kenny is on the ground passed out. He is breathing deep breaths in his unconcious state. 'Scottie, look at the cement incline of the bridge.' Michael exclaims to Frost. Frost turns around, and again the camera pans over with him and you can see on the cement incline in black spray paint huge letters..... 'The world is under unequal order. You have to be ruthless, to be recognized.' whispers the young Frost as he reads the lettering on the incline. Frost looks back down at the body, and then he nods his head with a shocked look on his face.
