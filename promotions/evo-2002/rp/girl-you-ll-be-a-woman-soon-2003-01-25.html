Girl... You'll be a Woman... Soon

25 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/25/03 {The scene opens once more in the Unlimited warroom. This time you can see the television suspended from the ceiling, always tuned into eVolution's home channel, just in case anything of interest would come on. Xander is seated this time, no doubt going over his game plan for Sunday. Being in two matches, not even just two matches, two title matches. Plans are going ahead full-steam} 'Well, well, well, if the little girl doesn't finally open her trap.' Xander states sarcastically. 'You know Seara... the funniest thing about you... is that somehow you're under the impression that I need help. See, this is where you're wrong. I've accomplished many, many more feats then you ever will, on my own. The thing about Unlimited... and why we attack the way we do is simple.' {Xander looks to the camera} 'Domination.' {He leans back in his chair} 'To show your dominance, you are forced to play your trump card. See, me, Asis, Christoph, Angel, HBC, and now with The ONE back.... we are eVolution. Do I need to attack you? Yes. Do I need to have others attack you? Yes. Why? Because you are ignorant. You think that somehow you are going to change eVolution wrestling? Little girl.. listen up. Do you know who the ONE is? Do you know who I am? Do you know what Unlimited is about? I didn't think so. So before you go spouting your mouth off about shit that you will never be able to do, let alone comprehend how to start... you need to think.. .think of the ramifications... This Sunday is a huge day. I have a feeling by the end of the broadcast... nothing will ever be the same for non-Unlimited ever again.' {Xander slides the chair up close to the table in which he is seated before} 'The safest bet for Sunday.. is on all things Unlimited. Me, Asis, Angel, and The ONE all have matches... matches against people who have burrowed under the collective skin of Unlimited.. and it's time to weed them out, and destroy them. If not physically, mentally... if not mentally.. anyway we can. We are Unlimited. We have destroyed the Notorious... New-TV... The OMEN Brotherhood is all but dead now... which leaves just us. The way it started, the way it will end.' 'One group to lead it all... One man to lead that group... many victims in our wake. Sunday is step two in the beginning.. of the end.' {The scene fades out to static.}
