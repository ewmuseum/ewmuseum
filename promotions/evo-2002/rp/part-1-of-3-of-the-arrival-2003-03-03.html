Part 1 of 3, of the arrival.

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 3rd, 2003 'Part 1 of 3, of the Arrival.' How does it feel? What's it like? Your heart pounding quickly, your hands shaking, your eyes becoming irritating and blurry. You become confused, living for the moment, the match, the glory. Your hand is raised high in victory, but why all the signs of being nervous? Why, do you know why your hand was raised in victory, why do you feel so proud....so honored? The crowd chants your name, they jump up and down, scream and they all rumble the arenas' floor, though you haven't a clue why. Sometimes knowing what not is going on could mean the difference in winning and losing. What do you think? How did it feel gaining the advantage, being the man of the hour, the show, and the night? Was it all worth it? Ask yourself again. Of course you won the match, what does this mean? ..:::: Suddenly a man comes into view, his face reaches the light, his massive figure fulfills the spotlight. Again, he embraces the moment. ::::.. They say one mans' loss is another mans' gain, is this true? Besides a win, what was there to gain, respect? Do you think you gained respect, attention, an award for beating me? How does it feel with having an edge, a win, a supreme desire to out-compete your adversary? Did it feel good? You loved every moment of it, I know you did. However, not all matches are decided to have a winner and a loser. Some are based on advantages to get you to where you want to be, yet, how does that help a man who has already reached the top of the world? You believe having a championship makes you a champion? If you don't, you better start! ..:::: Chuck, the very man who had taken an ass kicking last week, issues out a warning. ::::.. Very soon, one man of the roster will experience first hand, exactly how it feels to beat me. If you believe you have seen the end of me with one defeat, think again. You will see how it feels to be scared, to be afraid, horrified. You will soon catch yourself in nightmares running from an insane man, but it won't be me, oh no. Where I seek for revenge, there's no where to run. The man you will see yourself running from, will be....YOU! No, don't be confused. It didn't have to end up like this. Ask yourself who created the monster in me, me? No, it wasn't me. Before the man who kicked my ass did so, I was silent, was I not? Therefore, you know who you are, you created me. It was you. That's right run, keep a pace jog if you must, but what will you do when you become tired? When you're exhausted, you will then and only then realized exactly what you created. When you see me in the ring, if you see me again, it won't be me, rest assured. That's right, look back, what do you see, me? Am I behind you? No, I am not... KEEP LOOKING, REMIND YOURSELF OF THE HELL YOU CAUSED FOR YOURSELF, BUT NO MATTER HOW MUCH YOU LOOK BACK, I WON'T BE THERE! No, I won't be there, but that doesn't mean I have forgotten about you. As a matter of fact, I'm already ahead of you. Waiting like a wolf at night sketching out to capture his prey, I am scaling you little man, I am waiting, I am stalking, creating a pathway to allow myself to destroy you. Awaiting I am, to create a more dynamic mess than what you have done for yourself. Sometimes we humans think alike, sometimes we think too much. Have you ever thought about consequences for your wrong actions? Better yet, have you ever thought about death? Now is the time you ask yourself: 'How will I be remembered?'
