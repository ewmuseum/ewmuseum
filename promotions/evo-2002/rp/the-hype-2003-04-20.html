The Hype

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 20 “Yeah, but Agnus” said a voice. A pretty familiar voice. It’s James Kimson’s voice to be exact and as the scene begins to fade in you see Kimson pacing back and for, a cell phone held to his ear. The room is a studio room, there are eVo logos, posters and the likes. Looks like Kimson has arrived at eVo HQ for some filming after Legends on Wednesday. However, it seem like right now Kimson’s not concerned with that, and is a rather important convorsation. “Agnus, listen please. All I want is Frost’s cell phone number. I need to talk to him and.... Yes I understand you can’t just go around giving out phone numbers..... yeah..... well it’s important that I speak to him before I leave tonight. Because ..... yes because he blasted me on Legends.” Kimson’s voice seems somewhat frustrated with the lady on the other line. It was only Wednesday when Kimson was victim of 4 brutal chair shots one after the other in the main event at Legends. A match that shouldn’t have ever started but that’s the way the game is played. 3 of them were from that no good piece of crap Christoph, who now seems to be next in line for the Universal Title, probably at End of Days. Regardless of where the leaves Kimson he knows 1 thing. That what Christoph and Xander did was intentional. But the 4th and final chair shot to Kimson, who was just finally recovering from a concussion at the hands of Sovereign at Unbreakable is afraid that one last shot might have set him back a week. But that’s the way the game was played. Kimson just wanted to know if Frost knew who he was hitting or not, it’s something he needed to know. Because if it was intentional, well then all the help Kimson’s tried to give Frost was a waist of his time. And Kimson doesn’t like having people jerk him around, and waist his time. “Listen Agnus, if you can’t do it.... I understand. I’ll find some other way to get in touch with him. But if he calls in, you give him my cell number okay? Tell him to call me. Thanks” Kimson presses the talk button on his phone and flips the cover over before pocketing it in his leather jacket’s breast pocket. Kimson looks around the area, there are make-up people walking around, some TV crew people setting down cable. One of the Stage managers walks around barking orders at people as Kimson watches wondering if Frost is going to call him. Who knows what’s next, Christoph issued a challenge to Kimson and Frost. Regardless of what’s going on between Frost and Christoph, Kimson knows that he wants to get a bit of revenge on Christoph for his tactics this last week. Sure, yeah Kimson’s gotten his ass kicked before, he’s used to it. It’s no big feat. But every time he’s gotten his revenge, and it’s always sweet. Erin Christoph crossed the line between annoyance, and deserveing to get the shit beat out of him by The Hype last night at Legends with those chair shots. And of course, Xander will get his this coming week. “James” a voice said. Kimson must have been day dreaming about what’s been going on recently as the person calling for him somewhat startled him. Kimson stood up as he had taken a seat on a bench near by. It was the Stage Hand, a young, mid 20's man with a head set and a clip board. “Yeah” said Kimson. “We’re all set here for you to tape” said the Stage Hand. “So you can stand or sit or whatever” he added. “Standing or sitting, this guy had no idea what it was like to step into the ring and here he was telling me what to do?” Kimson said to himself. The Industry has changed. Like hell I’m going to read from any teleprompters. “I’m going to lay it down here and get out of this damn Coffin of a Studio. Damn my head hurts” said Kimson to himself again. Kimson got up and walked with the Stage hand who pointed the general dirrection of where he wanted Kimson to stand. Kimson walked over and rotated his shoulders and took a deep breath. The Stage hand quickly began a count down from 5, then pointed to Kimson to signal it was time to start. Kimson looked into the camera “You know, I’ve been around this business since I was 16 years old. I’ve been through some of the most brutal matches, on the poor side of some of the most brutal ass kickings and I’ve dished them out as well. But as far as last week went, with what happened between The Unlimited and myself. Well lets just say that it was another in a long history of seeing James Kimson get laid out at the end of a show. You never get used to it. It always hurts your pride. So regardless of what happens from this point on, the how’s and then when’s I think that Erin Christoph, you are going to get what’s coming to you. Not from Frost, but from me. You see, you brought in the chair. You decided to take it as far as it went, and what that means is that I’m going to kick the shit out of you Christoph, you and all your little buddies, in your little power struggle. You crossed the line with me, and for that I’m going to repay it to you in kind and you’ll regret ever stepping into the ring with me. You managed to win at Legends, and that might very well make you the Number 1 contender. But regardless of if you ever win that title, or if you end up falling short every time, you have to realize Christoph that it’s only a matter of time. Maybe days, maybe weeks or months. But I’m going to get my hands on you. And when I do Christoph it’ll be History in the making for you.” Kimson shakes his head somewhat and smiles before speaking again “As for you Xander. You got screwed this week by your own man, and if that’s any indication of what I think of your wrestling skill, to even get pinned by someone as pathetic as Eric Christoph, you still managed to loss the damn match after you got your whipping boy Asis to put Frost out for like what 5 minutes of the match? Who the hell told me that you were a legend, because if I knew who it was I’d slap the color out of their face for being the biggest bullshit artist in history. You’re nothing Xander, I couldn’t even look back to the last time you even won a friggin’ match on Legends. And so you just hide behind your little faction, kiss Dan S.’s ass all night long so that you can remain in some sort of spotlight. Yeah, Xander, the Hardcore Icon or whatever you call yourself. You’re going to have to step into the ring with a real Icon this week on Legends Xander. Someone who’s not afraid of the Unlimited. Someone who doesn’t give a damn about who you have in your pocket or what you have up your sleeve. Because just like you have probably done, I’ve been through it all. I’ve had a lot of shit happen to me over the years Xander, and I’m more then ready for whatever you have ready for me this coming week at Legends. Because I’m only expecting that you bring your ass to the ring so I can beat the crap out of you and send a message to Erin Christoph that I always get payback on whoever’s wronged me. And Xander, your little bitch Christoph crossed the line. And you know how it goes in the real world. A childs parents are responsible for his actions. Christoph is nothing but a little brat kid trying to gain the attention of everyone and Xander, you’re his Mother babying him and trying to encourage him. Well he just bit the hand that feeds him. But that squabble is better saved for another day Xander because I’m going to walk all over you at Legends and leave you laying in the center of the ring like I’ve done to so many before.” Kimson turns around runs his hands through his hair and shakes his head back and forth, then as he turns back speaks more “As for you Scott Frost. I don’t want an apology, because you no doubt aren’t the sort of guy that will run around saying he’s sorry for what he’s done. You’re a man of action, you’re your own person and I don’t care if you’re sorry or happy about what you did at Legends. All I want to know is if you did it on purpose or not. Not much to ask, and I damn sure think you owe it to me after the times I’ve risked my ass running into that ring to save you. Weither it was a sprained neck, or a concussion I had no fear. But the second it seems you had the chance you smashed a chair over my skull. So what’s it going to be Frost? Friend of Foe, and I’ll take whatever you want to give me. We don’t have to like each other. Hell you don’t have to respect me or anything I’ve done. You never needed to thank me. You never asked for my help, but I’m only a human being and I have a heart. You say you are the cold hearted one. Lets see if you live up to that. Because Frost, at Legends I’ll be in the building. And I will be looking for you. Because I am going to get an answer by the time Legends goes off the air, or else our relationship will take a turn for the worse. Because if there’s one thing I can’t stand is a man who won’t step up and take responsibility for his actions.” Kimson looks deep into the camera, his smile is gone as he clearly concludes the interview “So Frost, Xander and Christoph all have a date with me at Legends. Frost to explain himself, Christoph to get what’s coming to him and Xander to get his ass kicked all over the damn ring by yours truly. The Hype has spoken” Kimson turns and walks off as the scene fades to black.
