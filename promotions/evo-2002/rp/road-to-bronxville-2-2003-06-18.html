Road to Bronxville 2

18 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-06-18 The camera zooms in and out of a Dunkin Donut’s store to where a man is seen indulging in what appears to be a chocolate glazed donut. He zooms in once more as the cream begins to dribble down from his mouth, continuing along its path until a tongue containing the remains of the Donuts reaches out and licks the cream. “Today, my loyal viewers I have come to explore the diet’s of the people of Bronxville.” He continues to zoom in on the man who is indulging like a rabid savage in the donut. On his third and final donut, the man bites off the top and begins to lick the raspberry filling from inside the donut. “Ladies and gentlemen, I have just come to realize that there is a severe lack of, shall we say poonani, or pussy round these parts.” The man becomes more and more intimate with his donut and begins to stick his nose in it and rub it down along. His head rotates from side to side as he continues to lick the raspberry filling. “The species of Bronksville have most certainly found an alternative to make up for their, shall we say, non existent pulling abilities. I shall now put this man through both a physical and mental test in a most gruelling challenge.” The camera focuses on the man who has just finished indulging his donut. Terlayne sneaks up into a bush which is located directly outside the window where the fat man is eating. “Hey fatty bum bum, hey fatty bum bum.” The man is obviously offended by Terlayne’s remarks and rises to his feet. “We have action ladies and gentlemen.” Terlayne is spotted from inside by the donut eater who is not hesitant in making his way outside. Some of the raspberry filling is still dripping from his mouth. The man then holds his gut and runs in Terlayne’s direction. The camera then begins to shake as Terlayne pants and jumps in the back of a pick-up-truck. The driver slowly makes his way out of the Dunkin Donuts car park as Terlayne teases the obese man with the donut. The obese man is sweating heavily as Terlayne begins to eat the donut, slowly, teasing the man further. The pick up truck stops and the man soon turns around. “Giving you my two cents once again, ladies and gentlemen, I have learned that the people here are a primitive, sexually frustrated people.” He hops out of the pick up truck with the camera and continues. “I must continue. I must not let this area go un-explored.”
