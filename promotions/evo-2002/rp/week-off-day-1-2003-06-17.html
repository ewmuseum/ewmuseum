Week Off. Day 1

17 June 2003

Date: 2003-06-17 Khadafi stepped into his next punch knowing that he’d follow it up with and uppercut. Knowing that he would follow the uppercut up with a knee strike. Knowing that after the knee strike would come the head butt. Knowing that after the head butt would come a flurry of hooks and then a huge roundhouse kick. Ali felt only mild displeasure when his sparring partner hit the blue canvass in a bundle of pain. He had gone down halfway through the chain of moves that Khadafi had thrown at him. The head butt had caught him unawares and sent him reeling. His hands had slipped down for a second and Ali had snaked a shot in there. He always sneaks shots in. Crafty little punches that would swell an eye shut, split a lip, or fatten a cheek. Or those kicks. They had a way of wrapping up a training session quickly. Especially when they struck you in the face. You world would blur for a moment or two and by that time you could already hear the wind whistling as Khadafi’s next shot came humming in. It was always bound to be a big bomber of a shot too. Ali didn’t believe in fucking around when it came to his strikes. They were always designed to flatten your ass. 24-7. Even now, as he danced around the ring sending zingers out to another sparring partner, the other recuperating in the far corner of the old ring, he was lashing out with deadly shin kicks. Khadafi’s partner grunted in pain as one particularly pretty kick floated over his defenses and smashed into his ribs, rippling the red Dropkick Murphies shirt he wore upon impact. Here comes another thing about Ali right now: he’s just barging right on in with that combo. Pushing his partner up against the ropes like he was some fresh college virgin instead of a 6’2” 255 pound bouncer from Las Vegas, Khadafi rains punches on every open spot on his opponent’s body. They hit the poor mans ribs, shoulders, stomach, and eventually they’ll reach his face and he’ll have to take a seat like his buddy. See… Right there Khadafi just jacked him in the jaw when as he tried to pull away. He’s going to be in a rough spot now… Ali’s getting a little tired. I can tell because his last kick wasn’t as crisp as they were half an hour ago. Still did the job though. Khadafi waits in his corner while his partner struggles to get his headgear off. It doesn’t work, so the man just slips down to the floor of the gym to get a drink to try and clear his head. Ali is watching him with bright eyed intent. He can’t wait for the sparring to start again. .can’t. .fucking. .wait.
