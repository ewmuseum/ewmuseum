Uncharted Wisdom

18 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/18/03 Luther Wisdom sits in his chair looking out in the distance. He uses this time to reflect on all things happening in his life....more importantly the constant struggle of life and death. 'I came here to Evolution to do one or two things. Establish my legacy...and win a few titles. Funny when you think of it those are rather simple goals, and goals I trust are not unique to myself. Then this past week happened....I was confronted with my own mortality deep inside of a boiler room.' He emerges from the chair and walks out to the patio....he takes in the crisp night air before continuing..... 'Xander I was thrust into this war of stables. Let's cut to the chase, this isn't about any other stable in Evolution...it's about The Unlimited plain and simple. It's about the OMEN Brotherhood who for lack of a better analogy is the bastard little brother of The Unlimited. However on Legends little brother has a chance to beat the living hell out of big brother....something I look forward to doing.' he rubs the back of his head...... 'Xander, just because Malcolm has pretty up mucked up your head is no excuse to impose your insecurities onto others. The way I see it I was close to doing you a favor....eliminating the man you just can't seem rid yourself of in Malcolm King. but your ego took over, and much like your apparent lack of leadership skills with The Unlimited it seems you couldn't stomach the thought of someone else accomplishing what you have repeatedly failed to do....rid the wrestling world of Malcolm king. Have no fear Xander you will stand face to face with the man that holds the future in his hands.....the man who will complete the job you have failed to do so many times. First I eliminate you from the equasion, then your prostitute Angel. Then I will finally rid the wrestling world of The Unlimited and last but not least I will make it so Mlacolm King doesn't bother anyone ever again.' Wisdom looks up to the stars..... 'You see Malcolm, unlike Xavier I refuse to live in your shadow. I have been a consistant part of Evolution Wrestling for months now...yet it's you that waltzes in here and is automatically given a shot to win the Universal title. Am I not the only one that thinks that is wrong? You claim you want to do what's best for business...here's a suggestion, stay out of this business. It's MY time now Malcolm, and know this......next time Xander won't be around to save you. Unlike him.....I finish what I start.' fade to black
