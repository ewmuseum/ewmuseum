We Are... part 1

18 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 18th 2003 How long has she been like this, I mean shut away and distant? The voice clearly not Frost’s filled the room and the shot faded into a silhouette of Frost and another male figure, as the shot clears a doctor can be seen. She’s been kinda going like it, she was getting like it before the death of L, and since then, she has been really distant, not eating, her whole life has changed and she has become pretty violent. Frost’s voice replies to the question, and moving, the camera gets a shot of the inside of the room, furniture is shattered around the room, and the female is sitting in the middle completely silent rocking back and forth and occasionally mouthing the words of the song playing in the background. Seara seems angry to a large extent and seems to be reverting back to something more violent and unorthodox within her. Frost has been watching her dramatic in ring behaviour change lately, and last night after returning home, he had heard her screaming in pain and anger as she locked herself in the room and destroyed it. After nearly 5 hours solid of the noise, he had called her father, but now her brother was here with a doctor, a family doctor who had seen Seara like this many times before. What have you been doing to help her? The doctor’s question was simple enough, but Frost thought to himself for a moment, not fully sure of what the doctor meant by this. When she finally unlocked the door, I went in and just sat there hugging her and talking, you know letting her know I’m here for her and that I love her and that she doesn’t need to be alone through all this. Unfortunately Mr Frost, that is all I can tell you to do. Katherine doesn’t deal with things well, and the violence is her way with coping with it all. She has to find her own personal answers and anger makes her think. Just keep her active, don’t act like she is any different, keep her lifestyle normal, keep her in the ring fighting, and out of the ring, carry on your life, she will eventually snap out of it. Pausing the doctor looks at the female’s younger brother Bruce and sighs. He knows that this may be the last time Seara will be able to push her temper back into the background, but he also knows that the bagged up emotion and pent up anger is killing the female, and he wants Frost to understand that no matter how scary the prospect may seem, the female needs the anger to control her and that she must let it out otherwise she will eventually explode in a fit of rage that could be her own death or the death of another person. I hate to say it Mr Frost but Katherine must let her anger out, encourage her to bring her anger out in the ring, don’t let her or make her pent her anger up and keep it locked inside. You love her and want her healthy and normal, then you convince her that the anger is good for her, that she should not fight against it, but use it to her advantage, if you do that, she will realise you love her even if she has an unpredictable temper, and hopefully she will settle down quickly at home. I know she loves you Scott, and I know you can help her, but would you mind if I stayed here with her for a while, just keep an eye on her, train with her the way I used to, maybe I can talk to her. Frost looks at Bruce then at the doctor, but does not reply, after a few minutes he asks Bruce to go wait in the front room so they can talk and he shows the doctor out of the house and thanks him for the advice and the inconvenience of his journey and time. Willing the doctor offers to give Frost a personal number that he can call if he comes across any troubles with Seara, taking it, Frost closes the door and goes to talk to Bruce. The camera does not follow him, but instead goes back to Seara to see if she will talk to the camera. Sitting on the floor in the middle of the room, Seara smokes a cigarette and rocks gently back and forth. We can control it, we can handle it, we are preparing. The female’s muttering is unexpected and setting up the camera on a tripod, the cameraman leaves the room, hoping that if the female is left there, she will reveal something about her current state. We need to prepare for next week; we need to be at our best to keep it. It’s what we wanted all along and now we have it, and we don’t want to lose it. It seems from her rocking and muttering that the female still hasn’t noticed the presence that people have had, or the current presence of the camera filming her. Stubbing the cigarette out in an ashtray at her feet, Seara grabs a piece of wood, probably shattered table or chair, but whatever it was from is now unrecognisable and is part of the shattered mass of wood spread around the entire dining room. Screaming the female turns still sitting and throws the lump towards a shelving unit which contains wine glasses and expensive champagne glasses, there is even a bottle of champagne sitting on the bottom shelf. The wood collides with a thud and smash of glass, wooden shelves fall from the wall to the floor as the huge weight of the wood falls through them, snapping most of the shelves, and the glass shatters to the floor, small shards fly outwards and a few embed themselves in the arm of the female, but she barely even notices that it has happened. Champagne covers the floor and splashes up the wall and somehow makes Seara open her eyes to what she has just done. But it is quickly forgotten and she turns back to staring at the same piece of floor as before, blood running down her arm and dripping onto her leg. We won’t let him take it from us, we are stronger now than we ever were, and we will not let him cheat, we will prove stronger, more powerful, he will not be able to handle us, we are more than he will be able to comprehend, we are the strength that others pray they had, we are the new, the ultimate, the unique, we are…
