Wired to the Moon I..

16 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-08-16 Rachel crushed her cigarette in the ashtray beside the bed, blowing a cloud of smoke into the air. She slid out of bed, not wanting to wake the huge man sleeping next to her. Typical man, she thought to herself. As soon as his needs are taken care of, he’s fast asleep. Sifting through the pile of clothes beside the bed, Rachel found her underwear and slipped them on. Satisfied or not, there was a genuine thrill about having sex with a celebrity. He might not be a movie star, but high profile professional wrestler was still a celebrity. Every time she heard that eVolution was coming to town, she got goosebumps. As she continued to get dressed, she kept an eye on the restless Damon O'Halloran. She didn’t want to wake him, knowing that he was usually quite unpleasant when someone woke him up. If she was lucky, she’d be able to slip out of his room and down to her car without him even knowing she was gone. He’s certainly moving, Rachel observed. I know he was pretty wound up about his boss pulling his strings from under him. Wonder if that’s what got him so worked up? As if on cue, O'Halloran bolted upright. Rachel shrieked, startled. Damon was sweating again, this time not of passion. “Are you okay?” Rachel asked him. Damon wiped his eyes, swabbing the sweat from his forehead. “You still here?” “I was just leaving,” Rachel stated as she checked to make sure she hadn’t forgotten anything. She walked to the mirror and visually gave herself a once-over. Damon stood up, still naked, and walked to the bathroom. “Good. Get out.” The steady stream Rachel heard seconds after O'Halloran disappeared into the bathroom told her that the conversation was over. “I know you won’t, but call me next time you’re back this way,” Rachel said as she let herself out of his hotel room. Damon flushed the toilet and filled the sink with cold water. Splashing the water onto his face, he stared into his reflection. Sex, drugs, alcohol… and still he had the nightmares. Nightmare, to be accurate. The same dream, for the most part, with the same ending. “What does a guy have to do to forget?” Damon asked out loud. What indeed.
