Ring Ring

22 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-10-22 We open up in a semi-big room, with white walls and floor. The only things in the room are a small table with a bottle of beer in the center, and a person standing on the opposite corner. It's Brianna Washington who is wearing an all black outfit, which causes her to stick out even more in the room. She has something clutched in her hand and she looks at the bottle through squinted eyes. She takes a step back and a few moments later she tosses something near the bottle which causes it to explode and shatter into a thousand pieces. 'Yes!' She yells as she goes to inspect the damage. She looks around through the broken glass and finds a small black ball. She picks it up and instantly drops it as the steam rises and the heat singes her hand. 'Huh!!' she huffs loudly. 'It's still not working the way I want. It's exploding on contact, but it's still keeping the heat afterwards.' As she begins to contemplate ways to correct the problem a knocking is heard. She rushes over to the door, which would be absolutely unnoticeable without the golden handle. She pulls it open and standing there is her flamboyant friend, Milo in a bright red silk shirt and tight black jeans. “There’s a phone call for you.” As he says this he looks over her shoulder and sees the shattered bottle all over the table. “You know I really don’t understand why you work stuff like that. Scientist already have things to blow stuff up.” “That maybe so, but it serves for good practice. Besides inventors do everything….from toys to explosives.” “Sometimes you really scare me.” Milo says, never truly understanding Brianna’s fascination with inventing. Realizing that the person on the phone has been left hanging, Brianna rushes past Milo and into the hall where she scoops up the phone on the stand. “Hello?” “Brianna?” The voice replies. As soon as she hears it her face grows tense. The person on the other end is Thomas. “What do you want?” “Nice to talk to you too.” He says sarcastically which causes Brianna to roll her eyes and sigh. “Just wanted to tell you that I ran into an old friend of ours the other day.” “Who?” Brianna asks quickly, very curious to know who he’s talking about. “Remember Bernie Sirillo?” “That guy who used to own that big electronic company?” “Yeah that’s him. Well I was on my way to a meeting and we just bumped into each other. He asked how I was and how you were. We got to talking and he told me that he would love for the three of us to have dinner some time. Told him Saturday would be good, so leave your plans open.” “You couldn’t have consulted me about it first? I did have plans on Saturday.” “Listen hun, it’s probably nothing important, so just be at the Modea Riveria at six o’oclock.” Before Brianna could get anything else in he hung up. “What an ass!” “What did he want?” Milo said as he walked by with a Maxim magazine rolled up in his right hand. “Nothing important….just being his old self.” Brianna slammed the receiver down and walked off, very upset. Now she’s going to have to head off to Legends to participate in a tag match against HBC, a woman who has been a thorn in her side for the past couple of weeks. With Thomas’s phone call Brianna is now agitated, and will definitely take no crap. The Syndicate is going to have their work cut out for them…
