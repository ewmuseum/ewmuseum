problematic part 2

2 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2003-07-02 Flash in the pan superstars like Scott Evans quickly suck in the world. They’re blinded by the underdogs victory. It’s seemingly the American way. Anyone can do anything but in reality that doesn’t fly under Hedges watch. Scott Evans didn’t win that belt from him he took it from Erik Knight who didn’t win that from Hedges either. Wisdom blew Hedges chances of capturing his belt back as a whole. Hedges wants his belt back fair and square. He can’t get a one on one match ever but that’s no surprise and once again on the year anniversary of legends Hedges will reclaim what is rightfully his, the evolved title. The scene opens back up in the bar this time with hedges shooting pool. There are only a few people left as the clock on the back reads 2:30. A few people sit at the bar as Hedges paces around the table taking shots here and there. He looks back up at the camera and begins to speak. “Now that cell match has opened my eyes to an old star who once again wants to cross my path. Ryan Justice knows where the key to fame is and that’s beating me. He tried in the cell but was unsuccessful as everyone saw. I won’t let that go over my head and Justice and I shall meet again in the ring soon because he has made the mistake of becoming a problem I’ve decided to fix in the early stages. Justice I will serve you up on a silver platter mark my words your next after Legends.” Hedges cracks a newly racked set of balls on the table… “Now for my title goes the whole reason I’m here and competing on Legends.. Sure I’ve been complaining a lot about losing my title but rightfully so. I never lost it to begin with. If Scott was to beat me tonight I’d go back to the drawing board and work towards earning another shot but I rightfully get my shot at the belt finally after several weeks. I earned my rematch. Drake was just shoved in for shits and giggles. I hope he gots SARS fighting over in those Asian countries in those worthless matches. You know this Drake guy I haven’t actually seen fight much but I hear he’s pretty tough. Fighting those three foot Asians who managed to come out of it all with one of his eyes can really make a man out of you. I hope he can see clearly out of his other eye though that I will be walking out of this match champion. Pride fighting is alright but his place is in the cell division and not in a ring where wrestling is taken as a skill and we don’t cat fight our way around a cage. You see Drake …out of one eye…AHAHAHAHAHA…damn I crack myself up… I’m a man on a mission with a goal of ending his problems so I can truly focus on what matters to me gold and glory.” Hedges sinks the eight ball and goes and sits down and pours him self a beer. He drinks it about half way down and belches. “Now Mr. Evans let’s think Back to A New Dawn and how you wouldn’t even have that title if I hadn’t had helped you. You are worthless and it was to set you up so I could knock you down. Erik Knight was un deserving so I took what is really mine and gave it to someone who is slightly more deserving. I want you to know I control that belt and it is mine. It’s become and obsession and a passion to retrieve it. You have it now and I’m coming to take it. Erik Knight is a sorry excuse for a man. I beat him and his partner and Wisdom blew it. Big surprise there though. He claims he is a champion when he didn’t even beat one. It’s BS and Scott I don’t care about you…your wife…child…your brother or his stupid dreams. I think your whole family is a bunch of sissies and I’ll eat your dead child’s carcass for breakfast. HAHAHA fool you aren’t truly focused this match anyways only your dreams and ambitions. So I know you aren’t even 100% focused on this match because I know there is something besides getting your ass kicked that you really want to do with your life. My suggestion is to throw the belt down and walk away but if you choose to stand and fight you will end up with the rest of your family and that’s six feet under. Now as far as the table match goes bring out the wood because both of your Asses are going to get a whole lot closer than you already are to it” Hedges slams down the rest of his beer “Realize I am he all knowing supreme being and I control your asses and walking out the champion is a fact and a promise” With that Hedges gets up and walks out of the bar as the scene fades to black
