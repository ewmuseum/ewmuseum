A New Booked E-Wrestling Fed: the Prime Time Wrestling Federation

4 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ladies and Gentlemen... A new Booked E-Wrestling Fed is in town... And that is the Prime Time Wrestling FEDERATION!!! Hello, Hello!!! The Prime Time Wrestling Federation has arrived!!! Below this is a little information packet about the PTWF. ------------------------------------------------------------ Prime Time Wrestling Federation Information Packet ------------------------------------------------------------ I. --The PTWF in General-- The PTWF stands for the Prime Time Wrestling Federation, and is one of the many imaginary fantasy wrestling leagues on the internet. The PTWF exists to provide wrestling fans with a creative and enjoyable gaming format. Ever dream of putting on tights and walking down that long aisle into the squared circle like the big feds? Well the PTWF is a alternative, as you can create your own wrestling character and guide him to the World Title!!! The PTWF was created by [name withheld]. Mr. [name withheld] never ran a fed before, but has roleplayed in fed's such as, ERWI, ICE, PTWL, EXTREME, HOW, WAeW, IeWF, and NOW. Mr. [name withheld] has also wrote matches for various leagues. So the PTWF has some experience in the front office. II. --PTWF Rules-- The PTWF will not allow any "Out of the World" gimmicks. Meaning, I will not tolerate wrestlers with gimmicks that are too far-fetched. For example I don't want any bionic plumbers or clowns in my league. NO comic book gimmicks, just keep it plain and simple. As a general rule of thumb, applicants whose wrestlers are similar characters the league already established in the PTWF, are not admitted, even if they are top roleplayers. I mean, would you want to have five undertakers running around? ALL wrestlers must be original and not taken off any 'real' wrestler. (i.e. no heartbreaker, stone colds, or nature boys) You can only submit ONE singles wrestler or ONE tag team , but not both. Players tend to enter four or five wrestlers in one league, and only pays attention to one of them. The other three or four wrestlers he owns just sit there, eat up space, and do nothing. Also this allows you to focus on one primary character to guide. In order to ensure each player recieves sizable activity and participation in the PTWF, the roster must be kept relatively small. The PTWF allows 25 singles wrestlers and 6 tag teams. This allows each wrestler a match once a week and the attention the player deserves in a league. Upon being admitted to PTWF, the President will ask for a sample roleplay. Here is an idea of what is expected of you in your sample roleplay. III. --PTWF Particpation-- Participation in the PTWF is essential. It is the key to success. Roleplaying (see Roleplaying Regulations below) takes up much of that participation. You are required to submit a minimum of two flashes per week. One of those flashes is your major flash. Meaning it is your all-around big time flash that developes your character. The other flash is a shorter, but by any means equal quality to that of your major one. Read Roleplay Regulations below for full understanding of your roleplaying standards. Other ways of participation is sending in strats, sneak attacks, and getting involved in your current angles and storylines. I will get into it in more detail as the league progresses. IV. --PTWF Programs-- The PTWF currently promotes a live weekly card: PTWF Saturday Night Mayhem. In addition, there are two features that come out during the week: -PTWF Wednesday WrestleCenter -PTWF Monday Trashtalk Pay-Per-View's occur every four months. V. --PTWF Roleplay Regulations-- The PTWF emphasis on, good writing=good push. A long roleplay doesn't neccessarily mean that is a good roleplay. A well written quailty 6K roleplay can beat out a long, dull, boring and dragging 12K roleplay any day of the week. Though, the longer the roleplay the better. Remember the three most important things when creating a roleplay. Content, Length and Detail. Your flashes will be sent out in the weekly features, Monday TrashTalk and PTWF WrestleCenter. Your big major flash, no shorter than 3K, will be distributed in the report show, PWF WrestleCenter. This is where your character developes more of his personality and a chance to be heard. Monday TrashTalk is the smaller but equally good roleplay. It is based soley on the happenings in your match on Saturday Night Heat. Failure to produce roleplays of any sort, be they interveiws, strats, or whatever, in a short period of time will result a cautionary warning from the President, and if the inactivity continues, your wrestler may be suspeneded or fired by the PTWF altogether. --The Ground Rules-- Nobody is stopping you from having fun, and that is the main reason you come to the PTWF, is to have fun. But like all good things, there must be rules that each player must abide by, in consideration of the The PTWF Council and your fellow players. Please follow these regulations: DON'T use another player's wrestler, unless there has been an OK by the other party and by the President. Furthermore, don't ask for your wrestler to hosptialize another wrestler unless there has been consent from the other party and the President. DON'T attack announcers or any of the PTWF staff, unless there has been express permission by the President. You can attack as many no name jobbers as you want. DON'T use excessive profanity. Often when you go into realization of your character, obscene language is all part of the game when it is hot and heavy that your wrestler is going through. However, dirty and very vulgar words will be edited out in your flashes. Words such as "ass" will be allowed, though stronger words than it will not. You have to keep in mind that such language is not acceptable in television programmings. It is very possible to express your anger and extreme emotions without using a string of four letter words. VI. How does the PTWF operate? The PTWF is a ''booked'' league. Meaning the match results are predetermined by the PWF council. Results are not made up of, with that single match in mind. Generally, it accounts for the bigger picture, meaning the angles, storylines, and so forth. And your basis of roleplaying helps a tremendous way in helping the match results. However, for instance, two good roleplayers have a match against each other, both have been flashing all week, and now it comes to conclusion that the President will take it upon himself to determine a results with a coherent storyline in mind. VII. Titles The Prime Time Wrestling Federation currently sanctions three singles titles: PWF World Heavyweight Championship (Universal Weight) PWF Interconinental Championship (Universal Weight) PWF Skyweight Championship (Weight limit-245lbs) The PTWF also sanctions one tag team title: PWF World Tag Team Championship (Universal Weight Limit) The addition of another Tag Team Championship is currently being considered by the PTWF council. Titles can only change hands by pinfall or submission, unless specified before the match. VIII. Rankings PTWF uses a point system for its rankings. Once getting admitted to PTWF, the wrestler starts off with zero points and works his way up by getting victories. A wrestler is not deducted any points if he loses. Point System are as follows: +20 points for match win by pinfall or submission +10 points for match win by disqualification or countout +5 points for match draw IX.
