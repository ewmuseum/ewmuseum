PTWF Timelock, episode one

3 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
********** ************* * * ****************** * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * ********** * * * * * * * ** * ********** * * * * * * * * * **** **** * * **** **** * * **** **** * * * * * * * -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- PRIME TIME WRESTLING FEDERATION -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- "PTWF Timelock" with Doug Jones -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [M/A/R/R/S's "Pump up the Volume" loudly plays as the camera shot opens to a rookie PTWF Color Commentator Doug Jones standing in front of a wall of monitors each with the PTWF logo on them.] DJ: Hello, fans and welcome to the first ever edition of "Timelock" which is your weekly look at all the news, views, reviews, previews, highlights, and sidelights, cheer and jeers, and a look ahead to the future of the Prime Time Wrestling Federation!!! DJ: I'm your host for "Timelock" Doug Jones. Enough small talk, let's get to the latest news in the PTWF!!! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- HEADLINES -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- -=- Steve Spartan, a wrestling legend, although he may not be too much liked by the fans, has been bothered by the PTWF President [name withheld], Vice President [name withheld], and Front Office to sign a contract with the Prime Time Wrestling Federation. -=- Joey Rappoport a rookie wrestler, is looking to be a wrestling legend, and a superstar in the Prime Time Wrestling Federation to capture some gold. Here's a little clip from his workout. [Cut to shots of Joey Rappoport firing rights and left's on a sparring partner. Then Rappoport throws his partner into the ropes and excutes a clothesline. Then Rappoport picks up his partner off the mat and excutes a Running Powerslam on his partner.] Looks very impressive doesn't it? -=- "Killer Kid" Brad Slack one of the most popular legends in wrestling ever, has donated $1,000,000 to the Ronald McDonald Charity Fund. Here's some comment's from Slack when he donated the money. [Cut to "Killer Kid" Brad Slack attired in a Brad Slack PTWF T-Shirt, and Black Shorts standing up behind a stand with a microphone on it. The camera then turns and looks around the room and spots kid's cheering with their Brad Slack Shirts, and Shorts on. The camera turns back to Slack. Slack smiles to the camera and then speaks.] "Killer Kid" Brad Slack: I will do anything for the need to keep these kid's heart pumping, and I will do anything so they can drink clean water, and I will do anything so they can eat good fresh food, and I will do anything to make sure these kids, get a good education like everyone else does out their. [Then a bunch of cheering starts] "Killer Kid" Brad Slack: Thank you, Thank you! [Cut to Jones standing in front of the wall of monitors.] DJ: You know, [name withheld] has always bothered Steve Spartan to join even before he officaly announced he was starting this organzation. I wonder if he ever will sign him! [Suddenly the President [name withheld] walks into the studio and then next to Doug Jones in front of the wall of monitors.] DJ: Hel... Hel... Hello Mr. President, si... sir. BK: Hello Doug. I just overheard that comment that you just said if I ever will sign Steve Spartan. DJ: Did... Did... Did you like... like it sier... sir? BK: What do you think you half-wit?! OF COURSE I DIDN'T!!! Besides anyway, I'm going to show everyone on this show later on, in fact now, that Steve Spartan will sign a contract with the PTWF!!! Bring in Steve!!! [Suddenly, Steve Spartan walks into the room with a plane Green T-Shirt on, and plane Green Shorts with a piece of paper on his hand.] SS: Hello. BK: Hey Steve!!! Here's the pen!!! [Steve takes the pen from [name withheld]'s hand and then signs the paper. Then Steve gives the paper and the pen to Mr. [name withheld]] BK: Ladies and Gentlemen, Spartan is PTWF!!! SS: I am!!! Now lookout PTWF Skyweights and Heavyweights!!! Cause I, Steve Spartan the bad-ass of the PTWF, and wrestling will kick every wrestler's ass!!! DJ: I never thought he could actually sign Spartan! BK: Amazing isn't it. SS: Now I must go. See you in the ring PTWF wrestlers!!! [Spartan walks out of the studio] BK: Well, I must be going. Bye. And don't you say anything like that again cause if you do you'll be fired Mr. Jones!!! DJ: Yes Mr. President. [When the President turns his back on Doug Jones, Jones mouths Bite Me. Then the President leaves the studio.] DJ: Geez Golly Whickers he makes me sick. Now on with the show!!! [Suddenly noise is inside the studio. Suddenly, Rex Thompson walks in front of the wall of monitors attired in a Blue Blazer and Blue Shorts. Then Thompson blurts out words.] RT: Rex Thompson is in the house!!! DJ: Good gawd!!! DJ: What are you wearing? RT: Just a Blue Blazer and Blue Pants!!! DJ: Those are shorts you overgrown fat pathetic loser!!! RT: DON'T CALL ME THAT DOUG!!! Besides, they are Pants and just like the President said earlier, you "Half-Wit!" DJ: Then you look you blind person!!! RT: Fine, I WILL!!! [Rex then bends down and looks and then has a mean reaction on his face. Then Thompson yells.] RT: WHAT IN THE GOD DAMN HELL AM I WEARING!!! DJ: Told ya!!! DJ: Who's the half-wit now?! RT: Alright!!! I'm fed up with you!!! Let's go one-on-one right now!!! DJ: Fine!!! [Doug Jones fires a right that nails Rex Thompson on the nose and then kicks him in the mid-section and then Jones picks up and throws Thompson into the wall of monitors. Then the wall of monitors crack and break and sparks of electricity start flying all over the place.] DJ: Oh SHIT!!! Anyways, let's get to the damn PTWF roster which will be rankings soon!!! [More sparks fly out] -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- PTWF Singles Roster -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- "Killer Kid" Brad Slack 0-0-0/0 pts. "Bad Boy" Scott Bradley 0-0-0/0 pts. The Master of Disaster 0-0-0/0 pts. Stero 0-0-0/0 pts. Chapel 0-0-0/0 pts. Joey Rappoport 0-0-0/0 pts. Dark Stalker 0-0-0/0 pts. Bane Wilson 0-0-0/0 pts. Steve Spartan 0-0-0/0 pts. Bone Thugs 0-0-0/0 pts. The Frightener 0-0-0/0 pts. Max Kruel 0-0-0/0 pts. Travis Berkenstock 0-0-0/0 pts. Jarred Tokarek 0-0-0/0 pts. Nick Kelly 0-0-0/0 pts. "Ace" Johnny Davis 0-0-0/0 pts. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- PTWF Tag Team Roster -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The Original Jersey Boys 0-0-0/0 pts. The Unearthly Powers 0-0-0/0 pts. Dark Stalker-Bone Thugs 0-0-0/0 pts. Sk8 Brothers 0-0-0/0 pts. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [Cut back to Jones] DJ: Well... [The electric sparks are still flying out from the monitors] DJ: Everyone's been waiting for the first card well here it is on the segment, The month of May in the PTWF!!! It will be this week in the PTWF once everything get's rolling! [A spark hit's Jones] DJ: OUCH!!! God damn it!!! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- This May-June in the PTWF -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Here is your first look at the action which will take place in May at the Prime Time Wrestling Federation Palace!!! Monday Night Massacre-May 26, 1997 1st Round Skyweight Title Tourney Bane Wilson vs. Steve Spartan 1st Round Interconinental Title Tourney "Bad Boy" Scott Bradley vs. Joey Rappoport 1st Round World Tag Title Tourney The Original Jersey Boys vs. The Unearhtly Powers 1st Round World Title Tourney The Master of Disaster vs. Chapel 1st Round Skyweight Title Tourney Nick Kelly vs. "Ace" Johnny Davis 1st Round Interconinental Title Tourney Dark Stalker vs. The Frightner 1st Round World Tag Title Tourney Dark Stalker-Bone Thugs vs. The Sk8 Brothers Main Event: 1st Round World Title Tourney "Killer Kid" Brad Slack vs. Stero The Final Decision! "Win or Regret it!" Live and only on PPV!-June 28, 1997 Winner is the first ever PTWF Skyweight Champion! Bane Wilson or Steve Spartan vs. Nick Kelly or "Ace" Johnny Davis Winner is the first ever PTWF Interconinental Champion! "Bad Boy" Scott Bradley or Joey Rappoport vs. Dark Stalker or The Frightner Winner is the first ever PTWF World Tag Team Champions! The Original Jersey Boys or The Unearthly Powers vs. Dark Stalker-Bone Thugs or The Sk8 Brothers Main Event: WINNER IS THE FIRST EVER PTWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION! The Master of Disaster or Chapel vs. "Killer Kid" Brad Slack or Stero -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- DJ: Well that does for the first edition of Timelock! From Rex Thompson, the President [name withheld], and from all of us at PTWF News, Goodnight Everybody!
