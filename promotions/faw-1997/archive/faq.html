Joining 1. Intro. And background of the FAW FAW stands for Fucking’ Awesome Wrestling, and it is one of zillions of Fantasy Wrestling Leagues that exist on the internet. It exists to provide wrestling fans with a creative outlet: don't like the way wrestlers' angles are handled in the big feds? Then create your own and guide him to the World title! The FAW opened up its doors on March 31, 1997. I, President W. have plenty of experience in E-Wrestling, and know how to write up good and exciting cards, but for the most part it’s up to you to make things happen. 2. Cards and Shows 2.1 Saturday Slugfest- Saturday Slugfest is the (well you guessed it) card that comes out every Saturday. This card will have 2-4 dark matches and 5-7 commentary matches. I would rather not have any specialty matches on these cards, and rather save them for PPV’s. 2.2 Tuesday Night Hell- Tuesday Night Hell is the card that comes out every Tuesday. This card will also have 2-4 dark matches and 6-8 commentary matches. Same thing go’s with the special matches, but there are exceptions. 3. Information/rules 3.0 Commentators/staff Billy Richards- Face commentator. A very happy-go-lucky kind of guy, who loves his job, and professional wrestling. Earl Casem- Neutral commentator, one of the best in pro wrestling today. A nice man from the south. Damon- Heel commentator. A former wrestler who left pro wrestling due to a knee injury. He would someday like to come back to wrestling and "Kick those no-good ‘I love everyone’ wrestlers asses!" 3.1 Belts World Belt North American Belt World Tag Belts There may be some more to come later. 3.2 Title Defenses The owner of the World belt has the right to the following: 1. Not grant a title shot to a wrestler ranked below #5 2. Grant a non-title match to a wrestler ranked below #5 But he must also abide by these rules: 1. Give a title shot to a wrestler ranked #1-4 twice a month. 2. Defend the title at every pay per view (that can count for one title defense for the month) The owner of the North American Belt has the right to the following: 1. Not grant a title shot to anyone ranked below #10 2. Grant a non-title match to anyone. But he must also abide by these rules: 1. Give a title shot three times a month to a wrestler ranked 1-10 2. Give a title shot at every pay per view. The World Tag Titles holders must give a title shot twice a month to any tag team. 3.3 Rankings Rankings are a tough thing to figure out how to do. So I am going to go with a very basic, very simple rankings system based on points. 2 points for a win, 1 for a draw, and 0 for a loss. 3.4 How matches work/types of matches The way matches are decided are based on a number of things. Angles, roleplaying, strategies, and the ideas of the bookers all come into play. The key is roleplaying. If you roleplay well, you will have a much better chance of winning the match against someone who rarely roleplays. NEVER complain about losing a match, because fantasy wrestling is not about that. It is about having fun, and that’s what you should try to do. The best part about not running off of a simulator is that there is no limit on what type of match you can have. Sure you have your cage matches, barbed wire matches etc. but how about a street fight? Or a weapons surrounding the ring match? It’s all possible in the FAW, though those matches should really be saved for ppv’s. 3.5 Pay Per Views Ah yes, one of the best parts of wrestling. Our PPV’s are guarenteed to be amazing. There will be at least 10 matches in our pay per views, including all three title matches. There will be one a month, though I cannot just go off and tell you right now what pay per view will be what month. 3.6 Flashes There is a flash link up on the page. Do not abuse it! You are required to write a MINIMUM of two flashes a week. I really don’t see how hard that can be. There are many things to remember while writing a flash: 1. Never use another wrestler in your flash w/o his permission. 2. While cursing is allowed I never want to see a flash covered in curse words. Be reasonable with them. 3. QUALITY! NOT QUANTITY! Stay in character! Check spelling! 3.7 Sneak attacks/interferences Sneak attacks are something that you as a wrestler need to be careful of. Just because you write to me to sneak attack a wrestler doesn’t necessarily mean you will succeed. Before sending me a sneak attack or interference think to yourself ‘Is this something my character would do?’ ‘Is there a point to this?’ Do not sneak attack a wrestler or interfere in a match unless it is part of an angle, or a fued, or something. Don’t just do it because you feel like ruining a match for someone else. 3.8 Strategies Strategies are also very important. A strategy is when you send me (or the writer of the card) your wrestler’s plan for the match. A sample strategy is this: Billy Bob wants to start off attacking Jimbo with punches, and back him into a corner and hammer away on him. The he’ll do a bunch of power moves, pinning at every oppurtunity. If worse comes to worse, he’ll just get Jimbo out of the ring and nail him with a chair. 3.9 Stables There is a max of six wrestlers per stable! Don’t even think about going nWo, because I do not want any rip-offs of angles, or wrestlers. 3.10 Angles An angle is a long term idea that you want to play out. Angles are what makes wrestling interesting so come up with some good ones and work with them. 3.11 FAW Managment The FAW Managment as of now includes me, President W.
