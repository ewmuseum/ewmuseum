Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

XWA's own 4 May 1998 broadcast billed itself as “the last edition” of its long-running weekly show, Monday Nightmare, returning after unspecified “problems” with a stacked card.

The broadcast was hosted by its own on-air announcer and site owner, “The Icon” Chris Faenza, alongside broadcaster Stevie J; the card opened with returning wrestler Ben Ramrock defeating former US Champion Steve McNalley by anklelock, and continued with the returning trio the Bad Boys defeating “Prince” Osirus Hassan and Chris Smith when Puff Daddy nailed a top-rope splash.

A Light-Heavyweight Championship match followed: champion Todd “the Man” Beauchamp, who had himself requested the title be put on the line, submitted Osirus Hassan — making a second appearance the same night — with the STF. The broadcast then turned to its main event build, a match between top contenders Rip and SEVEN for a World Heavyweight Title shot, its loser promised a mystery opponent for the US Title, interrupted by an unannounced wrestler called Fury attacking an already-battered Mase.

The surviving text breaks off there, mid-sentence, before Fury's attack or the main event itself plays out; no further part of this broadcast, and no later XWA document, survives.
