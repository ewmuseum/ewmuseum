Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

UEW describes its own origin, in an October 2005 FAQ, as the work of owner character David Bizzel (“The Bizz”), who took money from “his fabulously successful spatula clearance warehouse” and bought a single ring to run indy shows in the basement of an abandoned church in Owen Sound, Ontario. The same document's own account of how long ago that was does not agree with itself — one passage implies a founding some fifteen years earlier, another implies ten years of prior syndication under a different show name — so no date is adopted here.

By October 2005 UEW billed itself as based in Toronto, returning to Owen Sound once a year for its Last Man Standing pay-per-view. Its two regular broadcasts were REAL UNDERGROUND!, a Friday show hosted by commentators Royce “The Voice” Beaucroix and Bart Barry, and a Thursday news program fronted by “The Challenger” Roger York.

Four of its twelve monthly supercards were “Signature Events,” each crowning its own namesake honor — Master of Submissions at January's Battle Resolute, God of Hardcore at April's All Out Attack, Hell and Back at July's Jacked Up in July, and a thirty-six-wrestler Last Man Standing elimination match every October — feeding into the promotion's top honor, the Lord of the Underground, alongside Heavyweight, Cruiserweight, Television, Two Man Tag Team, Stable Tag and Hardcore titles, and a Hall-of-Fame-style Eternal Championship bestowed on inductees rather than defended; named recipients include Desolate, Dawkin the Undying, Roger York, Mabel Rogers, The Stickman, “Showcase” Sam Samson and Edith O'Ryan.

Its officials were named in the same document: head referee Rebbecca North, referees Jaccob Jolley and Richard Clayton, timekeeper Ross Jameson, head road agent Lauren Sickle, and head of security Desolate — “The Silent One,” staged as a fallen angel who had already wrestled and held titles in an earlier era of the promotion.

UEW spent the fall of 2005 recruiting, its October, November and December posts seeking additional wrestlers across every weight class and offering several vacant titles, the Lord of the Underground among them, to newcomers. The last surviving post is a one-line call on Christmas Day 2005, signed off “Currently Eddie!”; nothing further survives.
