Of this group, we need 10-15 to be willing to wrestle hardcore. Also of note: your character may start a champion or an established talent if you sign up now. Sign up now. -What We Do- The UEW, or Underground Elite Wrestling, is a fantasy e-wrestling federation. The main purpose of an e-wrestling federation (e-fed for short) is to allow its members the chance to control a wrestling character of their own creation, interact with other members and characters, control their career, etc. UEW is not an actual wrestling organization, it's for entertainment purposes only. [name withheld] and [name withheld] run UEW. They are a pair of enigmas. Actually, they'd just rather introduce themselves in person. This is their first go at fed heading, but both are quite experienced at e-wrestling. Most of the existing legends of the UEW are based on e-fed characters we've played, or wrestlers we've built in video games. The Story of UEW The UEW was started by owner Dave "The Bizz" Bizzel when he took money from his fabulously successful spatula clearance warehouse and bought a single ring to host indy shows in the basement of an abandoned church in Owen Sound, Ontario, Canada. Slowly, he gained regulars, and things kind of snowballed. Now, 15 years later, he has a sizable fed, with various talents from around the globe. The Underground Elite is now based in Toronto, but it returns to the Sound once a year for the Last Man Standing PPV, and at least three times for REAL UNDERGROUND!, the weekly UEW show aired on Fridays on the Fight Network in Canada and ESPN 2 in the States-- a stable home after 10 years of syndication as "UEW Weekly". ESPN and Fight have requested that the titles be in IMMEDIATE contention, and everyone wants to be the first to take on a belt on international television! The Bizz is a life long wrestling fan. On the whole, he's Neutral; he tries to give the fans what they want, but sometimes you gotta be the bad guy. He's a tough man in his late 30s, and doesn't care much for BS. He keeps the heels from becoming psychopathic in the extreme, and expects the faces to earn what they get-- all in the interest of a good show. THE SHOWS Currently UEW offers two shows, REAL UNDERGROUND! and Notes From the Underground. REAL UNDERGROUND! The UEW's recently debuted weekly show Friday on the Fight Network, hosted by commentators Royce "The Voice" Beaucroix and Bart Barry. It runs an average of two hours, features 4-6 matches, interviews, and promos. It airs mainly from the Underground Arena in Toronto, Ontario, Canada with occasional tours. Notes From the Underground Thursday nights on Fight, the UEW's news & views show Notes from the Underground hits! Promos, updates, words from The Bizz and the occasional classic match. (Watch for Desolate Vs. Dawkin, Hell and Back, from 1995). Hosted by Roger "The Challenger" York. SUPER CARDS UEW has a super card style pay-per-view the last Sunday of every month. Of these, four are considered Signature Events, with a special title linked to them. The other eight are just good, strong super cards. SUPER CARD PAY-PER-VIEWS Happen in February, March, May, June, August, September, November, and December. Featuring an extra hour on the normal two-hour broadcast (not including the inevitable live event overrun) and cards ranging between 6-9 matches. THE SIGNATURE EVENTS At each signature event, a title is handed out, signified by a ring won in one of four signature events. The title is by the year, and therefore not in contention beyond the event. Total of 7-10 matches. JANUARY-- BATTLE RESOLUTE Featuring the Master of Submissions match. APRIL-- ALL OUT ATTACK Featuring the all out brawl for God of Hardcore. JACKED UP IN JULY Featuring one of the bar none most grueling match types in wrestling, HELL AND BACK.
