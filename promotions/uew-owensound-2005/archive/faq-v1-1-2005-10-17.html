INTRODUCTION --------------- As with most FAQ lists, this should pretty much cover any questions you might have about Ungerground Elite Wrestling. Now, with added subliminal messages. ------------------- 2. WHAT IS UEW? ------------------- -A- THE BASICS The UEW, or Ungerground Elite Wrestling, is a fantasy e-wrestling federation. The main purpose of an e-wrestling federation (e-fed for short) is to allow its members the chance to control a wrestling character of their own creation, interact with other members and characters, control their career, etc. UEW is not an actual wrestling organization, it's for entertainment purposes only. -B- HISTORY The UEW was started by owner Dave "The Bizz" Bizzel when he took money from his fabulously successful spatula clearance warehouse and bought a single ring to host indy shows in the basement of an abandoned church in Owen Sound, Ontario, Canada. Slowly, he gained regulars, and things kind of snowballed. Now, 15 years later, he has a sizable fed, with various talents from around the globe. The Underground Elite is now based in Toronto, but it returns to the Sound once a year for the Last Man Standing PPV, and at least three times for REAL UNDERGROUND!, the weekly UEW show aired on Fridays on the Fight Network in Canada and ESPN 2 in the states-- a stable home after 10 years of syndication as "UEW Weekly". -C- WHO RUNS UEW... ...FOR REAL? [name withheld] and [name withheld] run UEW. They are a pair of enigmas. Actually, they'd just rather introduce themselves in person. This is their first go at fed heading, but both are quite experienced at e-wrestling. Most of the existing legends of the UEW are based on e-fed characters we've played, or wrestlers we've built in video games. ....IN GAME? In the fantasy world of UEW, it is run by Commissioner David Bizzel, AKA The Bizz. The Bizz is a life long wrestling fan. On the whole, he's Neutral; he tries to give the fans what they want, but sometimes you gotta be the bad guy. He's a tough man, in his late 30s, and doesn't care much for BS. He keeps the heels from becoming psychopathic in the extreme, and expects the faces to earn what they get. ------------ 3. THE SHOWS ------------ Currently UEW offers two shows, REAL UNDERGROUND! and The Pipeline. -A- REAL UNDERGROUND! The UEW's recently debuted weekly show Friday on the Fight Network, hosted by commentators Royce "The Voice" Beaucroix and Bart Barry. It features 4-6 matches, interviews, and promos. It's a traveling show. -B- The Pipeline Thursday nights on Fight, the UEW's news & views show Pipeline hits! Promos, updates, words from The Bizz and the occasional classic match. (Watch for Desolate Vs. Dawkin, Hell and Back, from 1995). Hosted by Roger "The Challenger" York. ------------- 4. SUPERCARDS ------------- UEW has a super card style pay-per-view the last Sunday of every month. Of these, four are considered Signature Events, with a special title linked to them. The other eight are just good, strong supercards. -A- SUPERCARD PAY-PER-VIEWS Happen in Febuary, March, May, June, August, September, November, and December. Featuring an extra hour on the normal two-hour broadcast (not including the inevitable live event overrun) and cards ranging between 6-9 matches. -B- THE SIGNATURE EVENTS At each signature event, a title is handed out, signified by a ring and won in one of four signature events. The title is by the year, and therefore not in contention beyond the event. Total of 7-10 matches. JANUARY-- BATTLE RESOLUTE Featuring the Master of Submissions match. APRIL-- ALL OUT ATTACK Featuring the all out brawl for God of Harcore. JACKED UP IN JULY Featuring one of the bar none most grueling match types in wrestling, HELL AND BACK. OCTOBER-- ALL FALL DOWN Featuring the marathon, 36 wrestler elimination match LAST MAN STANDING. ------------------------ 5. BEHIND THE MICROPHONE ------------------------ -A- Royce "The Voice" Beaucroix Lead commentator on RU!, Royce was Metatron, manager to UEW Head of Security Desolate in The Silent One's ring days. He's a wellspring of knowledge. He tends to favor the technically strong and highflyers. He's a southern gentleman to a t, though years on the road have softened his accent. -B- Bartholowmew Barrington (Bart Barry, The Last Nameless One) RU! color guy. Bart is an honest, blunt young man who respects the hell out of his co-host. He's been into the UEW since its inception, profiling it in any magazine he can and running UnderGrounded, the formost UEW fansite. He likes the power houses and the brawlers. And he does not gush (excessively). -C- Roger "The Challenger" York Mr. York is a Day One UEW Eternal Champion. He's been here since that you buck Bizzel impressed him at an indy show, and he won't hear of going any where else. Roger leads the UEW Interviewer Crew. Occasionally, the words "Are You Up... to the CHALLENGE?" will echo out, and Roger will unleash the C-Train and the Rise to the Challenge on some cocky young buck. Even Dawkin leaves /them/ alone. -D- Mabel Willis Short, cute, and razor witted. You don't do an interview with the Little Powerkeg. You survive it. The raven hair and eyes shine with intelligence. Mabel constantly gets the better of anyone dumb enough to bluster in her face. ----------- 6. REFEREES ----------- An important note on UEW Referees: Take your average "jobber". Someone who has the skills to wrestle, but not the "mic/face"-- sommeone who can get in there and move bodies. Train him in submissions and takedowns. And teach him to take as little crap as possible. That's our AVERAGE ref. -A- Rebbecca North You only underestimate our head referee once. She has taken down men three times her size for pushing their luck. She has the least patience for, as she calls them, shananagins. She's still got her Irish Brogue... but she's perfectly understandable. -B- Jaccob Jolley Rebbecca's second in command, and does his job just well enough to keep it. He is willing to sit back and just let the wrestlers settle their differences. He seldom rules a count out or DQ, and gives a TWENTY count, the only UEW ref to do so. -C- Richard Clayton Rich is relatively inexperienced as a referee, but very keen. He's slowly but surely gaining respect in the ring, but doesn't always "catch" the call. -D- Timekeeper/ Ring Announcer Ross Jameson He calls out names. He rings the bell. And he tries not to get hurt. What else do you want from the guy? ------------ 7. ROAD CREW ------------ -A- Head Road Agent Lauren Sickle Try playing den mother to our roster for a week, and you'll learn why Lauren is prematurely grey. She also makes sure the venues are covered. Her assistants Robert, Roberta, and Mirranda assist. -B- Head of Security Bebai Daath, Desolate The Silent One. Old Red Eyes. The Fallen's Grace. Desolate's "true" background is unknown. He claims to be a Fallen Angel, who rejected both Lucifer's and Michael's calls to battle. Instead, he walks the Earth, protecting those forgotten in Heaven and Hell's war. He is a Haballah-Malakim-- his charge was, supposedly, punishing the damned and freeing those worthy of redemption. Perfect background to be UEW head of security, even sans his twenty years in the ring and twelve runs at various titles. The Truth? Well, he may be this junkie from West Vancouver who nearly ODed. He might be a teacher in training from Toronto that overdid the pychodelics. An ex-con escaping his violent past. Or he /may/ just be what he says... ------------ 7. ROLEPLAYS ------------ -A- CONTENT Roleplays are a major component of creating and guiding a character. The better the quality of your roleplay, the better the chances of getting a push(so long as the roleplays are part of an angle). Use your judgement with regards to content. One thing to note-- the Fight network does not bleep language (it's a Canadain specialty television thing), but does look askance when someone swears every second word. As for racism, sexism, hell, any -ism-- [name withheld] had a gay cousin. He was the king of the gay joke. He was also the first to call someone on true bigotry. Since all publically shared RPs are submitted to the fed-heads to add to the Pipeline, we can easily tell you when you've crossed the line. So yes, play a chauvenist pig-- just don't expect us to show much patience if you aren't willing to have him be a target too, or you take it too far. Also, realize fantasy is fantasy. OOC comments of bigotry of any sort that offend are grounds for ejection, even if it was "just a joke". -B- APPROVAL The fedheads have the right not to use an RP if they feel the content is questionable. It's rare, but it happens. When it does, odds are we'll send the roleplay back to allow the opportunity of a re-write. -C- STORYLINES, ANGLES, ECETRA! Got a storyline idea? Excellent. Direct it to the brick wall. That's [name withheld]'s high school nick name, he played rugby! Just pair up with a wrestler or two, and get going! Bear in mind that the fedheads have input, so everything fits together. The heads will also work to help handlers who need a storyline to find one. Remember-- storylines are how your wrestler gets on air! No storyline, you're just jobbin'! -D- Activity If you have a storyline, you need to submit an RP for inclusion in the Pipeline ever week that story is run. If you don't, the storyline may end prematurely, and your ring performance will be in the toliet. Understandably, real-life issues could get in the way of your participation. But TELL US. If you feel you need time off and your characters must remain inactive for a period of time, just tell US so we don't book you for a bit! --------------------- 7. MISCELLANEOUS RULES --------------------- -A- ALL DECISIONS ARE FINAL, NO REFUNDS We use TNM to "roll out" the matches. You'll notice our results don't look like TNM output files-- that's because the heads go through and write them up proper. TNM ensures no favorites are played; push is used as a roleplaying reward, which makes better roleplaying worthwhile. We occasionally reject TNM's outcomes-- [name withheld] and [name withheld] are long time table top GMs, so we won't hesitate to fudge for a story-- but once the show is out, that's it. The story is told. On to the next episode. -B- THAT TIME HONOURED TRADITION We'd perfer you "job" your title if your character leaves the UEW while holding one, rather than vacate it. It just flows better, story wise. -C- IT'S ONLY FUN UNTIL SOMEONE LOSES AN EYE If a sneak attack or dirty trick seems the best way for you to foward the plot, make sure you tell the handlers involved and the fed heads. Believe us, shock and fear for characters should be an in character thing. -D- ANGLES MAKE THE WORLD GO ROUND ANGLES decide if you get a match, have a shot at a title, or get that grudge settled. The story decides your matches, and it is the strongest force in the UEW. -E- NO TOUCHING THE NPC'S The non-player characters, or more specifically, the announcers, officials and other people who the fedhead handles may not be injured or physically harmed in an angle without permission of the fedhead, and if you seek such permission, you better have a very good reason for why you are doing such an angle. As a matter of fact, all usage of NPC's require permission to use them, except for the interviewers, but keep in mind that the interviewers must be made available for everyone, so no attacking them. And if you do get permission to use an NPC other than an interviewer in a flash, please remember to stay true to their characters. If you don't, the fedhead reserves the right to edit such flashes. -F- THIS AIN'T XPW, YOU KNOW. IT ALSO AIN'T NO TEA PARTY. Hardcore matches in the UEW requires a waiver be signed, and that it only happen when sanctioned. Yeah, you can ambush a guy with a cane during a feud, but don't expect to bring a staple gun to every match without the ref getting in there and DQing you. Not even Jaccob will let that slide. Within Hardcore, remember your wrestler's style outside of it. Sabu is amazing because he has the skill to back up his stunning risks, Mick Foley learned to make his girth an advantage, and Violent Jay of ICP has some of the most beautiful followthrough we've ever seen. Hardcore in UEW NEVER means violence over skilll, and our character creation system reflects this-- Hardcore is not a style. Our match writing reflects this. -G- PLEASE DO NOT FEUD WITH THE MAN IN CHARGE The Bizz is created to be almost a force of nature, beyond the wrestler's reach-- if things get effed up, he is our deus ex machina, or plot device to get the train back on track. As such, he's not a "feudable" character. He is GOD in the UEW. -H- IF YOU DON'T ASK THE QUESTION, YOU WON'T GET THE ANSWER Questions about anything, if you ever have them, should always be directed to [name withheld] and [name withheld] ------------- 8. THE TITLES ------------- - A - BELTS Belts are the most contested titles in the UEW. They are NEVER assigned. Stealing one is grounds for automatic dismissal from the federation. A belt shifts ownership when the current #1 contender defeats the standing holder of the belt. Some belts preclude others; belts that are noted as TANDEM can be held at the same time as any other belt, including Lord of the Underground. LORD OF THE UNDERGROUND-- The highest belted honor in UEW, the Lord of The Underground's contenders may only be chosen from amoungst those who hold the other belts. The Lord must defend at every Pay-Per-View; his title is safe during regular broadcasts. Upon gaining the Lord title, a Champion's current title is vacated unless it can be held in Tandem. UEW HEAVY WEIGHT CHAMPION-- Open to wrestlers of 226+ lbs. Defended against a #1 contender selected by the commisioner from amoung the top 10 ranked heavy weights in the federation , usually through a tourney or battle royal. The title is defended at any event the Commissioner cards a defense, though a title match outside of a pay per view is unusual. UEW CRUISER WEIGHT CHAMPION-- Open to wrestlers of 225 lbs or less. Defended against a #1 contender selected by the commisioner from amoung the top 10 ranked cruiser weights in the federation , usually through a tourney or battle royal. The title is defended at any event the Commissioner cards a defense, though a title match outside of a pay per view is unusual. UEW TELEVISION TITLE-- Open to the first wrestler every week to register their challenge with the Commissioner. The TV title is considered very demanding, as it is defended at EVERY TELEVISED UEW event. If the television title is lost during a PPV where the standing Champ is up for Lord, he STILL gets his match. If he is defeated BEFORE the PPV, the honor passes to the incoming TV Champ. UEW TWO MAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS-- Open to teams of two wrestlers of any combined weight. May be held in Tandem. Defended against a team of #1 contenders selected by the commisioner from amoung the top 10 ranked registered teams in the federation , usually through a tourney or battle royal. The title is defended at any event the Commissioner cards a defense, though a title match outside of a pay per view is unusual. UEW STABLE TAG CHAMPIONS-- Open to stables or groups of 3 or more wrestlers. May be held in Tandem. Each stable fields a team of three or more men, which may change composition between stable matches. Defended against a team of #1 contenders selected by the commisioner from amoung the top 10 ranked registered stables in the federation , usually through a tourney or battle royal. The title is defended at any event the Commissioner cards a defense, though a title match outside of a pay per view is unusual. Stable Tags almost always have the Elimination stipulation, to balance the handicaping of a large vs. small stable-team. This is a NEW title. UEW HARDCORE CHAMPIONSHIP-- Open to any wrestler willing to sign the Hardcore Waiver. Taken over by the first such wrestler to pin the current champ at ANY TIME during an event that the commissioner calls for a defense. Once the title has been so won, the "defense" is considered complete for that event. The Commissioner can and will call for such a defense at ANY time. The Hardcore champ is also the only champ allowed to call for such a defense by his own violition-- though it is open to all Hardcore Listed Wrestlers or none; he may NOT choose an opponent. EVENT HONOR RINGS Instituted this year to celebrate the UEW's start on Fight and ESPN 2. These titles are signaled by a ring which is kept forever by the winning wrestler, engraved with the honor's name and year won. The rings are tied to a particular event. They may only be worn in ring during a hardcore match. Stealing one is grounds for automatic dismissal from the federation. They make one eligible for entry into ONE LOTU #1 Contender tourney or battle royale during the next 12 months after their recipt. Before, the honor had no marker. Rings have been retroatively awarded to past Big Event winners. MASTER OF SUBMISSIONS Decided at the January Battle Resolute Submission Only match. The two to four participants are choosen by the Comissioner based on skill with submissions and current ranking. GOD OF HARDCORE Decided during a hardcore, falls count anywhere, last amn standing elimination brawl at April's All Out Attack. Any one registered as Hardcore who does not participate loses hardcore status for a year. Injuries are not an excuse. HELL AND BACK Pioneered by head of security and UEW Eternal Champion Desolate during his ring days. Decided at Jacked Up July. Two Wrestlers. A specially desgined three tier Hell in a Cell ring. First one to get to the bottom level, pin their opponent, and get back out wins. All weapons ar removed from the cell. Other than that, anything goes. The participants are those elected Most Tense Rivals at that year's fan polls. LAST MAN STANDING Decided at ALL FALL DOWN in late October, the 36 spots in this grueling extended match in an enclosed octagon are filled three a month over the course of the year. The match is a highbred of Desolate's signature "Total Elimination Fatal Three Way" and head of talent Roger "The Challenger" York's famous "Ultimate Challenge" marathon matches. By a lottery-decided order not known until the day of the event, the first six competitors are released into the cage. Submission, pinfall, or DQ (the match is NOT anything goes) means elimination and replacement by a new competitor-- as well as removal by The UEW Eternal Champion elected by the fans to referee if you're reluctant to go. Your current foes will help-- the next elimination cannot happen until the current one is exiled from the cage. The winner gets an IMMEDIATE LOTU title shot at the Remembrance PPV in November, as well as the "Ring Bearers Entry". The UEW signature event is always called by the Commish/ Owner Dave Bizzel and Roger York, if he hasn't been elected to referee. OTHER HONORS ETERNAL CHAMPION-- A belt awarded to all those inducted into the UEW Hall of Fame. Past recipients include Desolate, Dawkin the Undying, Roger York, Mabel Rogers, The Stickman, "Showcase" Sam Samson, and Edith O'Ryan. Wrestler, Heavy Weight, Cruiser Weight, Tag Team, Stable, Hardcore, Referee of the Year awards-- Voted by the fans, yearly. ------------------ 9.
