A May 1999 Usenet e-fed billed as a globe-touring, application-based promotion out of Albany, New York, recruiting a roster and staff around a four-title schema ahead of a first World Heavyweight tournament. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Warriors of Wrestling introduced itself on 19 May 1999 with a full handbook, opening its overview section with a claim to legitimacy few e-feds bothered to stake: “one of the few federations, who have a World Heavyweight Title, that lives up to its status as a World Championship.” Every pay-per-view, it said, would be “hosted in different country,” and it billed itself as “booked, flash packet roleplay federation.”

Its weekly schedule ran two shows out of Albany, New York — Sunday Night, Live and Hardcore, carrying seven to ten matches plus interviews and a weekly World Television Championship defense, and the Wednesday Night Power Hour, written mostly in results summary with full commentary reserved for its main event. Four championships were on offer — the World Heavyweight, United States, World Television and World Tag Team titles — with the first World Heavyweight Champion to be crowned in a four-man tournament whose participants would be decided by random draw on the promotion's first Wednesday broadcast. Its own handbook billed a president, Peter Tiger, and a three-person broadcast team, play-by-play man Rick James, color commentator Hairy Johnson and a third booth voice, Jamie McGivern.

No tournament result, card, or title change survives; how far the World Heavyweight tournament got, or whether it crowned a champion at all, isn't in the surviving record.
