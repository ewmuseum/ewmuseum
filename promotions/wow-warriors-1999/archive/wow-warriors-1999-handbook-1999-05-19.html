Section 1 - Federation Overview Section 2 - Telivision Scheduale Section 3 - Titles and Awards Section 4 - Personalities Section 5 - World Title Situation Section 6 - Application Section 1 - Overview The Warriors of Wrestling Federation, is one of the few federations, who have a
World Heavyweight Title, that lives up to its status as a World Championship.
The WOW travels the globe, as every pay per view that we hold is hosted in different country, with the main event featuring a World Heavyweight Title bout. The man who is the World Heavyweight Champion has the right to stake a claim as one of the most outstanding athelete currently competing anywhere in the world. The WOW is booked, flash packet roleplay federation that wants to bring fun back to the game that we all love.
Section 2 - Telivision Scheduale Sunday Night, Live and Hardcore This is the main show of the week for the Warriors of Wrestling. It emminates live from the WOW arena in Albany New York each week. There will be sevn to ten matches, plus in-rings and interviews, and the World Telivision
Championship will be defended every week. Wednesday Night Power Hour This is a secondary show for the WOW, with all matches be written in summary form except for the main event, which will be written in full commentary. This show will tarvel around all over the country, holding different themes based on the upcoming pay per view.
Section 3 - Titles and Awards World Heavyweight Championship - The most prestigous title in the sport. This belt will be defended on special occasions meaning at all Pay Per Views, and at the bigger Sunday Night, Live and Hardcore cards.
United States Championship - The man who wears this belt is the king of North
America. he is the man who has tarveled all around the continent winning match aftre match proveing his worth. This man is also the number one conteder to the World Heayweight Championship
World Telivision Championship - This is the working mans title. This belt is defended every single week on Sunday Night, Live and Hardcore.
World Tag Team Championship - Only the team with the most unity, and the most continuty will be able to hold onto these belts. Like the World Heavyweight
Championship, this belt holds much prestigue, and will be defended as such.
Section 4 - Personalities Peter Tiger - The Warriors of Wrestlings main man, the president. This is the man with all of the power in this buisness and a loud mouth to back it up.
Rick James - The main play by play man on both of the WOW's shows. He tends to slightly allign with what is right and just
Hairy Johnson - The color man on Sundays show. He has a snyde remark for everyone, and anyone, so look out, you could be the brunt of his viscous verbal assault.
Jamie McGivern - She is the third commentator for Sundays show, and joins Rick on Wednesdays show as well. She is one hot number, and what the man can't get, she usually somehow pursuades out of the men.
Section 5 - World Title Situation The WOW's first ever World Heavyweight Champion will be crowned in a four man tournament on Sunday Night, Live and Hardcore, the participants will be desided by random draw on The first Wednesday Night Power Hour.
