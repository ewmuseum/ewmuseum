A GeoCities-hosted fed, one of three unrelated feds sharing the acronym "IWA" swept into this project's own census at once, staging real WWF-era wrestlers under President NateDogg across a 1999 run of two vacant-title tournaments, weekly TV, and dueling World and Hardcore Title chains. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

IWA (Icon Wrestling Alliance) ran under a president known on the site only as NateDogg, its own about page also noting that its operator had separately joined a differently-named fed, "Icon Championship Wrestling" — a confirmed coincidence of near-identical names, not a shared identity. One of three unrelated feds sharing the acronym "IWA" swept into this project's own census at once.

Triple H is the earliest-attested IWA World Champion, defending against Sting at "Sweet Surrender" in a match whose finish does not survive. By the fed's own next served PPV, "Barbed Wire," the title had passed to Turney, an original character, who lost it to Canyon after two masked men — revealed as Chaos and Shawn Michaels — ran in to swing the match.

Raditz's arrival Written by AI, 2026

At Saturday Night Massacre (14 August 1999), a masked 6'8" figure drops from the ceiling behind Unforgiven during his billed European Title challenge against Chaos, knocking Unforgiven out cold with a Krionic Kick before the bell even rings — then wrestles Chaos himself, revealing his name only once the belt is already around his waist: "My name is Raditz, I'm here to destroy everyone in the IWA! Starting with you Chaos!" Later the same broadcast, Raditz reappears during Raven's own Intercontinental Title defense against Ringmaster, pulling Ringmaster out of a near-fall cover and finishing him with the Krionic Kick to keep Raven champion — Raven names him his new "right hand man" on the spot, only for Chaos to spring over the guardrail and bulldog them both before the two men finish celebrating. At Barbed Wire (August 1999), Raditz — now accompanied by a second Dragon Ball Z-billed ally, Nappa — wins the Hardcore Title from O.G. Chunk, Nappa knocking out a would-be rescuer, Big E, along the way. Raditz is the corpus's own last-attested Hardcore Champion, his own debut night's two title-picture interventions paying off inside a single PPV cycle.

The president turns into the target Written by AI, 2026

President NateDogg spends the PPV/TV era inserting himself physically into the fed's own biggest matches — fast-counting Corporate-SDH to the Stables Titles at Sweet Surrender, then working the same show's own World Title main event as special guest referee, taking a beating from Triple H before counting Sting's own pin. By the house-show era that October and November, his own top stars turn the weapons on him instead. Teaming with Mad Stoner against Sting and X-Pac on 29 October, he is chaired from behind by Ringmaster, mid-match. On 6 November, he breaks a bat over Mad Stoner's own back to help HBK win — and fast-counts the pin himself — only to be left in the ring afterward as Stoner cashes him anyway, a comic beat that still ends with Natedogg on the losing side of his own interference. On the fed's own last held show, a dark-match opponent, Boa, has the better of him for most of their bout before abandoning it unresolved — and Ringmaster attacks Natedogg afterward with a chair and two turnbuckle powerbombs, the surviving corpus's own final image of him left bleeding on a twisted chair, not moving.
