IWA’s Survivor Series (4/18/1999) — Part 2 of 2

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New Jack vs. Stone Cold Steve AustinDavid Penzer: This next match is scheduled for onefall in a lumberjack match. First introducing the lumberjacks. The lumberjacks representing Dungeon of Doom are Mankind, Paul Wight, Shawn Michaels and Sycho Sid. The lumberjacks representing Degeneration-X are Triple H, Steel, Freebird Michael Hayes, and Chyna. Now coming to the ring, representing the Dungeon of Doom, standing at 6'2" tall and weighing 252 pounds, the master of the Stunner from Victoria, Texas, Stone Cold Steve Austin. And his opponent, representing Degeneration-X, standing at 6'1" tall and weighing 255 pounds, the master of the Balcony Dive from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, New Jack.Before New Jack gets in the ring, he has a stare down with Shawn Michaels. New Jack says something to Michaels and Michaels punches New Jack across the face. All of the men on the outside start to brawl with the opposite stable. Stone Cold Steve Austin jumps over the top rope down onto New Jack. Stone Cold grabs New Jack and throws his face into the steel guardrail. Stone Cold rolls New Jack into the ring. Stone Cold whips New Jack into the ropes. New Jack throws a drop kick at Stone Cold. Stone Cold falls back out of the ring onto the DX side of lumberjacks. The four DX members stomp on Stone Cold and then roll him back in the ring. New Jack grabs Stone Cold and suplexes him down on the mat. New Jack bounces off the ropes and drops a senton splash on Stone Cold. New Jack hooks his leg for a cover. 1...2...Stone Cold kicks out of the cover. New Jack pulls Stone Cold up. New Jack throws Stone Cold over the top rope onto the DOD side. DOD rolls Stone Cold back in. New Jack grabs Stone Cold and whips him into the ropes. New Jack attempts a hip toss. Stone Cold reverses it and throws New Jack onto the DX side. Suddenly, the Degeneration-X members start to beat up on New Jack. DX rolls New Jack in the ring. New Jack is out. Stone Cold covers up New Jack. 1..2...3Winner by pinfall: Stone Cold Steve Austin Larry Zbyszko: What's going on here? I thought New Jack was DX.Michael Cole: Well it looks like DX is trimming the fat here.Larry Zbyszko: New Jack was a member for a few days. Let's just keep moving right along here with out next matchup.Mr. Fro Chuck Brown vs. DoggFather for the VACANT IWA Intercontinental Title David Penzer: This next match is scheduled as a ladder match for the Intercontinental Title. First coming to the ring, he stands in at 5'8" tall, 6'2" with afro, he weighs 210 pounds, the master of the Disco Ball, from Milwaukee, Wisconsin, accompanied to the ring by Lucy the sex machine, Mr. Fro Chuck Brown. And his opponent standing in at 6'8" tall and weighing 247 pounds, representing the Dungeon of Doom, the master of The Dogg Pound, from Compton, California, DoggFather.DoggFather carries the ladder down to the ring. Before DoggFather gets to the ring, Mr. Fro jumps over the top rope and delivers a plancha. The two men brawl on the outside. DoggFather throws Mr. Fro into the steel guardrail. DoggFather runs at Mr. Fro but Mr. Fro back body drops DoggFather in the stands. Mr. Fro grabs a chair from the stands and hits DoggFather with it. Mr. Fro lays the chair down on the ground and drops DoggFather down on it with a scoopslam. Mr. Fro whips DoggFather back to the steel guard rail. Mr. Fro throws DoggFather back over the guardrail to ringside. Mr. Fro rolls DoggFather back in the ring. Mr. Fro walks over to the ladder and throws it over the top rope, inside the ring. Mr. Fro rolls himself in the ring. DoggFather stomps down on Mr. Fro. DoggFather pulls Fro up and whips him into the ropes. Fro bounces back and DoggFather flips Fro with a back body drop on top of the ladder. That could've broken Fro's back. DoggFather pulls Fro up and scoop slams him down. DoggFather bounces off the ropes and attempts an elbow drop on Mr. Fro. Fro rolls out of the way and DoggFather hits his elbow hard on the ground. Mr. Fro grabs DoggFather up and whips him into the ropes. Mr. Fro hits DoggFather with a punch to the abdomes. Fro steps behind DoggFather and hits him with a German Suplex. Mr. Fro picks up the ladder and drops it across DoggFather's chest. Mr. Fro pulls DoggFather up and slams him down with a shoulder breaker. Mr. Fro pulls DoggFather up and pushes him into the ropes. DoggFather comes back backwards. Mr. Fro drops DoggFather down with a Reverse DDT. Mr. Fro signals to the crowd for the end. Fro climbs the top rope and drops down on DoggFather with the DISCO BALL!!! Fro sets the ladder and starts to climb it. Then, two men appear from the back. They knock Mr. Fro off the ladder. Mr. Fro falls all the way onto the announcer's booth from about 15 feet up in the air. Badd Ass Billy Gunn and Roaddogg Jesse James revive DoggFather and set the ladder up for him. The men go to the outside and grab Fro. They make Fro watch DoggFather climb the ladder and take the belt off of the wire. DoggFather wins!Winner and NEW IWA Intercontinental ChampionDoggFather Larry Zbyszko: I can't believe he made Mr. Fro watch that. That was creul.Michael Cole: Well you know what's coming up next. It's the grand finale and here we go.Degeneration-X vs. Dungeon of Doom for the VACANT Stables TitlesMichael Buffer: Ladies and gentlemen, it's time for the IWA Survivor Series main event. This match continues the brawl between the Dungeon of Doom against Degeneration-X. Are you ready? For the thousands in attendance and the millions watching on pay-per-view around the world, ladies and gentlemen, let's get ready to rumble. This match is a Hell In A Cell match scheduled for onefall. First coming to the ring, it is a group of four men consisting of Freebird Michael Hayes, Steel, Triple H, and Rob Van Dam, they weigh at a total of 1118 pounds, Degeneration-X. And their opponents consisting of Mankind, Stone Cold Steve Austin, Shawn Michaels, and Sycho Sid, weighing at a weight of 1077 pounds, Dungeon of Doom.The cage drops to the floor and the referee signals the beginning of the match. All of the men pair up with eachother. Freebird and Mankind fight. Rob Van Dam and Stone Cold Steve Austin fight. Steel, Triple H, Shawn Michaels, and Sycho Sid stand there looking at eachother. Then, Triple H, Shawn Michaels, Sycho Sid, and Steel all get big grins at eachother. Mankind and Stone Cold Steve Austin stop fighting to look at what is going on. Then from behind them, Freebird and Rob van Dam hit the men with forearm shots. All six men start to pound on Mankind and Stone Cold Steve Austin. Triple H goes under the ring and pulls out four pair of handcuffs. He takes eachone of Stone Cold's arms and handcuffs him to the cell. Then he takes one of the other pairs of handcuffs and handcuffs Mankind's hands together. Steel pulls a chair out from under the ring and whacks Mankind across the head with it. Then Steel hits Stone Cold, who is trying to break free. Freebird pulls out a table from under the ring and lays it out. They set Mankind on top of it. Sycho Sid climbs to the top turnbuckle and jumps down on Mankind. Rob Van Dam lays on top of a knocked out Mankind for a cover. 1...2...3Winners and NEW IWA Stables Champions: Degeneration-X After the match is over, they take Mankind and throw him face first into the cage. They all take chairs and start taking turns beating the leaders of the DOD. Then they call for the cage to be opened. Degeneration-X walks away with the Stables Titles. Larry Zbyszko: All along it was Shawn Michaels and Sycho Sid. Everybody said it was them. Then they fooled everybody and made people think it wasn't them. Michael Cole: It's amazing how smart these people are. For everybody at home don't forget to tune in for next month's special event, Thunder.
