IWA House Show (11/2/1999)

House Show 11-2-99 House Show11-2-99 Show Time and Mystery Partner vs. Rohn and Ryan Jericho First Ryan and Rohn came down and waited in the ring. Then Nothing's Left by the ICP came on and out came "Show Time." The 4 blocks shined up on the IWA-Tron, with one more block filled in with the face of Scott Hall. Then Hall came from the back and took on the Jerichos with "Show Time." The Jerichos took the early lead, but the fresh Scott Hall came into the ring and cleared it. "Show Time" went on the outside with Ryan Jericho, while Rohn stayed in the ring with Scott. Scott delivered an Outsider's Edge and took victory. Winners by pinfall"Show Time" Marcus Rosario and Scott HallRingmaster vs. Big Poppa Pimp Ringmaster came down to the ring and fought with Big Poppa Pimp. This match was fairly short, and ended when Mad Stoner came down to the ring and smashed Big Poppa Pimp with a chair. After the match, Mad Stoner tried to get Ringmaster to hang with him. Ringmaster just walked off. Winner by pinfallRingmaster Francis Taylor vs. New Jack Francis Taylor had a great match showing against New Jack. Quickly did he defeat New Jack with the Handy Grip to New Jack's balls. Winner by submissionFrancis Taylor The Cat vs. Rob Van Dam The Cat and Rob Van Dam both had great showings in this match. Rob Van Dam messed up though when he missed the Five Star. The Cat took advantage and walked home with the Feliner. Winner by pinfallThe Cat Decipher vs. Shawn Michaels Shawn Michaels did the very best he could in this match, but couldn't do enough. BioHazard came down to the ring and beat Michaels with a chair, attempting to loosen him up before their World Title match at Massacre Thursday. Winner by interferenceShawn Michaels Mad Stoner vs. X-Pac Mad Stoner came down to the ring while the fans booed him like never before. Then X-Pac came down to the ring and beat on Mad Stoner to no avail. Mad Stoner came back with a few chair shots, and was about to take victory until NateDogg came down to the ring and knocked him out with a chair. X-Pac delivered an X-Factor towards victory. Winner by pinfallX-Pac Next 3 Events Thursday, November 4, 1999-Dallas, Texas-Reunion Arena, Thursday Night Massacre Saturday, November 6, 1999-Oklahoma City, Oklahoma- IWA House Show Monday, November 8, 1999-Wichita, Kansas-IWA House Show
