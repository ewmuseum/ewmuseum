IWA Pre-PPV Show (11/20/1999)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IWA Pre-PPV ShowThe show had two dark matches. The first one was requested by the fans. The ticket takers took a poll from everyone on the way in. Hammer Time vs Limp Dick. The two actually put on good show. From highflying action to hardcore action when the fight went into the crowd. It ended in the ring. Limp Dick went for his spash from the top called "Flapping in the wind", but Hammer Time moved out of the way. Hammer Time finished things off with "Can't touch this". Hammer Time got the 1, 2, 3. The next match was Fat Bertha vs Hard Head. Fat Bertha pretty much had her way with him. Hard Head did get his finisher off, he bounce off the ropes and did a flying headbutt in Fat Bertha's gut. Hard Head bounced off and Fat Bertha just stood their laughing, it had no effect. Bertha picked him up and powerbomb his as to the mat. She bounced off the ropes, jumped in the air, and sat on his chest. Fat Bertha took the 1, 2, 3.Before the matches that brought the crowd their in the first place come out. Mad Stoner walk out onto the platform, and goes off on how he'll be the one in charge here after Sunday, but more important, Stoner came out to introduce his new manager...mini-stoner. A midget, dressed just like Stoner. Stoner said the only reason he hanged with mini was so that mini-stoner could lite the 5 foot glass bong, while he hit it, but Stoner took a liking to him and now works here as his manager. Stoner went backstage and the show started.Wicked Lester vs Buh Buh Ray DudleyBuh Buh made this a slug fest from the start as the two men exchanged punch and kick combo's all over the ring. Buh Buh saw an opening, and hit Lester with a piledriver. Buh Buh hit afew more moves before Lester reverse a powerbomb into a DDT. Wicked lived up to his name name with a death valley driver off the second turnbuckle, then Wicked went to the tope, waited on Dudley to stand, then flipped over Ray, hoked his head and sat on his ass. Buh Buh head snaps forward, then falls to the mat from the neckbreaker. Wicked with two more moves before Buh Buh reversed a irish whip and hit Wicked with the 3-D. Buh Buh hooked the leg 1, 2, thre..Wicked kicks out! Buh Buh can't believe it. Buh Buh backs the ref into the corer, pointing his finger into his chest and spiting in his face as he yells. Lester from behind, rolls Buh Buh up, the ref with a really fast count. 1, 2, 3! Wicked Lester wins!Justin SanevsRohn JerichoJericho didn't welcome Justin with opens arms, but with punches and kicks. Jericho seemed to be taking Sane to school from the start of this match. Hitting tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, a running powerslam, and reverse suplex, but Sane made a comeback. Justin Sane hit Rohn with a brainbuster, then threw on a surfboard stretch. Sane broke the hold, picked Rohn up and sends him down with a reverse DDT. Somehow Rohn made things start to go his way. Rohn hit Sane with a missle drop kick off the top ropes. Jericho picks Justin up and sets up for his sidewalk slam, but somehow Justin rolls back down to the mat and ties Rohn up into a small package. 1, 2, 3! Justin Sane wins!The Rock vs New JackNew Jack once again proved why he was hardcore, by taking the fight to the crowd and beating the shit out of the Rock with chairshots and whatever else he could get his hands on. New Jack lead the Rock back to ringside and threw him over the guardrail. New Jack made his way back into the ring. The Rock didn't have much offense, but he had it when he needed it. New jack picked the Rock up and out of nowhere the Rock hit the Rock Bottom! Rock bounced off the ropes and hit his elbow drop, then made the cover. 1, 2, 3! The Rock is your winner!The HitmanvsReignw/ CrimsonThese two put on one of the biggest technical showcases the IWA has ever seen. Big time moves, big time reverses. It went back and forth the whole match, and Crimson even got into the action. Reign pick Hitman up for a slam, but instead pushes Hitmans legs back down to the mat,then drove his head to the mat with a reverse DDT. Reign picked Hitman up, Hitman broke the hold and dazed Reign with a huge right. Hitman bounced off the ropes and sent Reign down with a inverted bulldog. At one point in the match, there was 9 back to back pin fall attemps by both men. Reign pick Hitman up for a tombstone, but Hitman started to shake his legs, Reign lost his balance and started to tip backwards, Hitman lands on his feet, but spins Reign around and drives his back onto Hitmans knee for the backbreaker. Hitman stood up, and Crimson, who is on the apro, springboards off the top rope and bulldogs Hitman into the mat. Crimson rolls out of the ring on the other side. This went in Reigns favor after that. Reign pulled Hitman to the top with him and sets up for what looks to be a piledriver...NO, flapjack off the top ropes! Reign picks Hitman up and throws him into the corner. Reign runs and hops onto the second turnbuckle and starts punching Bretts face, the crowd yells out six when Hitman runs out the corner with Reign and drops him into the inverted atomic drop. Hitman grabs Reigns legs and set The Sharpshooter up half way. Hitman tries to roll Reign over but Reign fights it off. Hitman tries again but Reign fights it off again, Hitman tries again, then suddenly rolls him the other way, Reign wasn't ready for it, and rolls over. Hitman has it locked in...Reign taps out. Hitman is your winner!Mad Stoner vs HBKWell after the two made to the ring, it was over quick. HBK tied up with Stoner and Ringmaster slide into the ring from behind, and hit Stoner in the back with a bat. Stoner hits the mat. Ringmaster hits Stoner two more times while he is down. HBK pulls Stoner up and holds him from behind, Ringmaster smashes the bat all over Stoner body, and after each hit Ringmaster can be heard saying "Is this what's going to happen to me!", then to end it, HBK moves out of the way as Ringmaster brings the bat down onto Stoner head, the bat snaps into two. Stoner hits the ropes and stumbles forward, and just because he can, HBK hits Stoner in the side of the face with Sweet Chin Music, Stoner hits the other ropes and stmbles into Ringmasters powerbomb! The bloody mess as Stoner lays in the ring, as the two leave.Natedogg vs BoaFrom the start Boa had his way with Natedogg. Boa beat Natedogg down, pulled him up and did it again. Boa whips Nate to the ropes and sends him down with a spinebuster. Boa slaps on a boston crab. Nate somehow makes his way to the ropes. Nate holds onto the ropes. Boa drops one leg spins around and picks both legs again and then yanks Nate off the ropes and Nate crashes onto his chest. Boa picks Natedogg up and whips him into the corner, Boa walks over and picks Nate up and places him on the top turnbuckle, Boa climbs up...cradle DDT of the turnbuckles. Boa drags Nate to his feet, but Nate brings up the low blow, Nate piledrives Boa into the mat. Nate pulls Boa up, runs with him and throws him over the top rope and to the floor. As Boa was airborn, Rigmaster came out of the crowd and slide into the ring behind Nate with a chair in his hand. Ringmaster runs and hits Nate so hard in the back of his head, his moms teeth rattle. Nate goes down. Ringmaster drags Nate to the center. Then starts drivig the top edge of the chair into Nate's back, ribs, and behind the knee. Boa gets up, looks at what is going on, then turns and leaves. Ringmaster unfolds the chair and sets it in the center of the ring. Ringmaster drags Nate to the corner, and the two make their way to the top...Powerbomb off the top turnbuckle! The back of Natedoggs head is driving into the seat of the chair! The chair falls over showing the huge dent. Ringmaster picks it up and unfolds in again. Ringmaster picks Nte up, and another powerbomb, Nates head hits the top edge of the chair, and the chair legs buckle and fold in. Nate lays on the twisted piece of steel, not moving only bleeding.Ring stands above Nate and yells "You think I'm just going to stand by while you screw me!". Ring spits into the bloody face of Nate then leaves.Remember the rsults of house shows don't affect ranks or win/lose results. This house show was brought to you by...
