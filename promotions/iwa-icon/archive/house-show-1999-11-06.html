IWA House Show (11/6/1999)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IWA HouseshowFrom Oklahoma City, Oklahoma11-6-99The IWA house show drew in 13,000 for the show. The first warm up match was Bad Bob vs Limp Dick. The match went back and forth from the start. But Limp Dick hit his splash from the top rope, which he calls "flapping in the wind". LD got the 1, 2, 3. On the way backstage Taylor comes running out and can be heard asking if Limp needed help with his problem. Limp pushes him away, but Taylor pesters him all the way backstage.New Jack vs Farren KnightNew Jack brought his garbage can full of goodies down to the ring with him. But it didn't stop Knight from kicking his ass. NJ got a few weapon shots in, but that was about it. Knight fought threw the pain. He wore down NJ with a chin lock and a figure four to the head. Knight ended the match with dropping NJ on his head with the HANGOVER! Farren Knight gets the 1, 2, 3!Mad Stoner vs HBKThe world champ took it to Stoner, Stoner had his moments, but overall HBK controling the match. Towards the end HBK hit a flying elbow off the top. HBK covered but Stoner kicked out. HBK tried to whip Stoner to the corner, but Stoner reversed and hit the Steamroller! HBK stumbled out and Stoner set him up for YOUR CASHED! Nate slides into the ring and hits Stoner in the gut with a bat. Stoner drops HBK. Nate brings the bat down, across Stoners head, it busts in half! Stoner stumbles and turns right into SWEET CHIN MUSIC! HBK covers and Nate counts the 1, 2, 3. Nate hands HBK the title and raises his hand in the air. Nate then takes the belt and tells HBK to pick up Stoner. HBK does and holds him up, Nate runs and swings the belt, Stoner moves and it hits HBK, Stoner spins Nate around...YOUR CASHED! Stoner talks some trash, then leaves.Scott Hall and "Iron" Damein Briggs vs The JerichosA great match and alot of team work. Hall and Briggs had the advantage. Briggs sends Ryan down with a somoan drop, then Hall jumps off the second turnbuckle and hits a knee drop to the face of Ryan. Later in the match Hall gets thrown out of the ring. The Jerichos pick Briggs up...double brainbuster. They both climb a corner and jump off hitting a double diving headbutt. Hall saves the pinfall attempt. Rohn is the one thrown out of the ring. Hall picks Ryan up with a waistlock. Briggs springboards off the top ropes and sends Ryan down with a inverted bulldog. Briggs makes the cover, but Ryan kicks out. Hall picks Ryan up and sets up for the OUTSIDERS EDGE, Rohn is on the apron and is able pull Ryan down. Briggs runs at Rohn and swings, Rohn blocks and sends Briggs out of the ring and to the floor with a suplex. Rohn stands up on the apron and blocks a punch from Hall, grabs his head and jumps to the floor snapping Halls head off the top rope, Hall stumbles into a powerbomb by Ryan. Ryan holds the legs and spins Hall over for a Elevated Boston crab, the WALLS OF JERICHO! Rohn goes crashing down on Hall's head with a leg drop from the top, then slingshots over the top rope and hits Briggs standing on the floor with a cross body splash. The ref checks Hall's arm three times...Hall is out. The Jerichos are your winners."Showtime" Marcus Rosario vs Francis TaylorAnother great matchup. Taylor getting ready for his match with Drunkaholic brought a pink chair down to the ring with him, but Showtime took it away and beat the hell out of Taylor with it. Showtime drops the chair on Taylors chest and climbs to the top. Taylor stands up with the chair in hand and Showtime jumps off and hits the Movie Cut, driving the chair into Taylors face. Showtime makes the cover but Taylor kicks out. Taylor somehow turned things around, by hitting Showtime with a running powerbomb. Taylor climbs to the top and waits, Showtime stands, Taylor jumps off and hits Showtime with a missle drop kick. Taylor pulls Showtime up and gets HANDYGRIP on! Buh Buh Ray Dudley slides into the ring, and hits Taylor breaking the hold. Dudley pounds on Taylor untill Showtime gets back up, they whip Taylor to the ropes, he bounces off and they go for a double clothesline, but Taylor ducks, bounces off and sends them down with a double clothesline. Taylor whips Showtime to one corner, the Dudley to another. Taylor runs and climbs to the second turnbuckle, then hits 5 tea bags on Showtime. Taylor rus and hits the second turnbuckle on Dudley, but Dudley brings Taylor down with a sit down powerbomb. Dudley whips him to the ropes, Showtime picks him up in a waistlock and falls back, Dudley hits the 3-D on the way down! Showtime covers Taylor for 1, 2, 3.Cyrus Fulton vs DrunkaholicBoth of these guys fought like champions. This match was in the ring, then n the stands, back in the ring, then back into the stands where Drunk just pounded on Fulton. Chair shots to Fultons back and head, by Drunkaholic. Drunk even had enough time to drink down a couple of beers given to him by fans. Drunk drags Fulton back to the ring and throws him over the guardrail. Drunk gets over the guardrail then instructs a fan to give him his chair. Fulton stands up and Drunk brings the chair down on his head, Fulton falls to the floor with the hair still on his head. Drunk makes his way to the top and goes for the Joykiller! Fulton gets his leg up and hits Drunk in the jaw, Drunk misses his mark and lands on the ground. From there Fulton brought the fight back in the ring were he nailed a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Fulton whips Drunk into the corner and follows it up with a jumping knee lift. Fulton climbs to the top and bring Drunk with him, he sets for Bastard-Plex, along the way dow Drunk breaks the hold and lands on his feet. Fulton stands up, Drunk spins him around Ghetto Stomp! Drunk pulls Fulton up and jumps up on the top turnbuckle...EXPLODING KEGGAR! Drunk makes the cover, as Y2Z flies out of the back. But they don't get there in time. Drunkaholic gets the 1, 2, 3! Y2Z is in the ring and they start stomping on Drunkaholic. Biohazard and Decipher run out and join in the fight. The sow ends with a huge brawl.Remember Houseshow results don't effect any stats or rankings.
