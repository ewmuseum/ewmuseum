IWA’s Saturday Night Massacre (8/14/1999)

The scene fades in as the pyro goes of for the start of Massacre. "Piggy Pie" by ICP plays over the PA system. Johnny Terrible and Brian the Brain walk out from the back and head down to ringside. They take at seat at their booth, and put the headsets on.JT: Hello everyone, and welcome to Saturday Night Massacre. We have a action packed card for you tonight. HBK will defend his World Title against Turney.Brain: Who cares about those two rednecks, when right before that, my boys Steel and Stoner will be taking out the thrash in the IWA and will bring the Tag Titles back to Corperate SDH were they belong.JT: Sting will try moving closer to that World Title shot by wrestling Public Enemy #1, Unforgiven will face Chaos for the European Title, there will be a four corner free for all, for the Hardcore Title, and Raven will defend the IC gold against Ringmaster. It looks like Nikki Dial is in the ring, so let's not wait any longer.Unforgiven vs ChaosEuropean Title Match"Unforgiven" by Metallica plays over the PA system.Nikki: Coming down to the ring at this time is the challenger. He stands at 6 foot 3 and weighs in at 275 pounds...Unforgiven!Unforgiven walks out from the back as the fans cheer. If walks down to the ring with his chair that has "Unforgiven" written on it. He slides into the ring and raises the chair into the air, as the fans cheer."Long road outta hell" plays over the PA system.Nikki: Coming down to the ring at this time is the current Crusierweight Champ and European Champion. He stands at 5 foot 11 and weighs in at 167 pounds...Chaos!Everyone in the place boo's as Choas walks out. He walks half way down to the ring, stops and looks above Unforgiven. A huge 6 foot 8 monster drops from the ceiling behind Unforgiven. UF turns around, and tries to hit the man with his chair. The beast grabs it away from UF and shoves UF. The beast throws the chair at UF and UF catches it, the myster man then follows through and nails the chair into UF's face with a Krionic Kick! UF is out cold. Chaos stands outside the ring watching the whole thing. The mystery man is handed a mic.Raditz: My name is Raditz, I'm here to destroy everyone in the IWA! Starting with you Chaos!Chaos doesn't look to happy and slides into the ring. The two stand toe to toe. Raditz towers over Chaos, but Chaos holds his ground. The two stare eachother down, then the bell rings.Chaos raises his knee for a low blow, Raditz bends over. Chaos drops his belt on the mat, hooks both arms...double arm DDT into the belt. Chaos stomps the back of Raditz's head a few times. Chaos picks Raditz up and whips him to the ropes, Chaos sends Raditz down with a huracanrana. Chaos climbs to the top turnbuckle. Raditz gets back up and catches Chaos. Raditz sends Chaos off the top turnbuckle with a chokeslam. Chaos hits the back of his head on the belt. Raditz sends Chaos to the ropes and almost rips Chaos's head off with a Krionic Kick! Raditz covers Chaos. 1...2...3! Raditz is handed the belt and his arm is raised. Raditz looks at the belt in disgust and heads for the back.JT: What the hell just happened here?Brain: Didn't you see it? Raditz just came down from the ceiling and kicked Chaos's and Unforgiven's ass!JT: Well it looks like Raditz is your new European Champion. I wonder how in the hell Raditz got into the IWA in the first place, I never heard of him.Brain: Who cares, lets get going on the next match.Lex Luger vs Hardcore TomvsOG Chuck vs CanyonHardcore Title matchin a four corners free for allHardcore Tom, OG Chuck, and Lex Luger are already in the ring. Then "Snap your fingers, Snap your neck" plays over the PA system. Canyon comes walking out from the back. Luger rolls out of the ring and races toward Canyon.OG knocks HT down with a clothesline to the back of the head. Luger and Canyon meet half way down the ramp, and starts exchanging punches. A ref runs out from the back to cover the action on the ramp. Canyon DDT's Luger on the ramp. Canyon picks Luger up and runs with him. Canyon smashes Luger's head into the entrance frame. The scene goes back to the ring where OG is stomping Hardcore Tom in the corner. OG pcks HT up...gut wrench suplex. OG rolls out of the ring and sets a table up outside the ring. OG rolls back into the ring. The scene cuts to the top of the platform. Canyon grabs Luger and throws him of the platform and down to the floor, 10 feet below. Canyon backs up, runs and jumps off the platform hitting Luger with the Rock Slide! Canyon makes the cover. 1...2...3! Luger is out! In the ring OG drags HT up the turnbuckle. OG sets up...and powerbombs HT threw the table on the outside. OG makes a cover. 1...2...3! Hardcore Tom is out!JT: Well it looks like it's down to the champ and OG. Let's see who will walk away the winner.OG rolls into the ring the same time Canyon slides in. The two tie up...Canyon sends OG down with a piledriver. Canyon picks OG up, but OG hits Canyon with a low blow. Canyon bends over. OG jumps in the air and sends Canyon down with a jumping leg drop. OG picks Canyon up and whips him to the ropes. OG goes for a clothesline, but Canyon spears OG down to the mat. Canyon thats the top turnbuckle cover off in one of the corners. Canyon picks OG up and rams him face first into the exposed cable. OG is busted open! Canyon hooks onto OG...pump handle suplex which sends OG over the top rope and out to the floor. OG reaches under the ring and pulls out a singapore cane. Canyon reaches threw the ropes and tries to grab OG, but OG brings the singapore cane up and hits Canyon right between the eyes. Canyon is busted open. OG rolls back into the ring and hits Canyon across the back 4 times with the cane. OG picks Canyon up, reverse suplex. OG flips Canyon over and climbs to the top ropes. OG jumps off, driving the cane into Canyon's throat. OG drives his knee into Canyon's back, and put the cane under his chin and pulls back. OG has Canyon in the middle of the ring! Canyon eyes are rolled up into his head. The ref checks the arm, it falls, it falls a second time. The ref pcks it up one more time, Canyn, for a split second holds his arm up, but then it falls! The ref calls for the bell. OG Chuck is your new Hardcore Champion!JT: Man these two guys put on a great show, but OG Chuck was able to pick up he win, and is IWA's new Hardcore Champ. During the match I was informed some thing went down backstage. We have it on tape, let's take a look at it.The scene starts out with Austin walking backstage with a beer in his hand. Then out of a dressing room a man runs out and hits Austin in the back of the head with a lead pipe. The mystery man, then starts beating Austin with it while he is on the ground. Teen comes flying into the scene and shoves the man of Austin. The mystery man runs off. Teen helps Austin. Jericho walks by the scene looking down at Austin shaking his head. Jericho walks right into Lord Soth. Jericho tells him to watch were he is going, and walks off. Lord Soth stares Jericho down the whole time. The scene cuts back to JT and Brain at the booth.Brain: I don't know who that was, but I like him already.JT: I bet you do Brain, well the man goes by the name Jeff Halesz. What he has against Austin, I don't know. Well that little stunt may have helped your boys in team corperate out tonight Brain.Brain: You don't say.JT: Well lets get ready for our next match.Ringmaster vs RavenIC Title MatchRavens RulesDX's theme music plays over the PA system.Nikki: Coming down to the ring is the challenger. He stands 6 foot 11 and weighs in at 316 pounds...Ringmaster!Ringmaster makes his way to the ring.Raven comes running from behind and hits Rigmaster in the back of head with the IC Title. Raven picks RM up and drops him on the guardrail. Raven runs with RM and whips RM into the steel steps. Raven rolls Ringmaster into the ring, then slides in. Raven picks RM up...EVENFLOW! Raven goes for the cover. Sid runs out from the back and slides into the ring and brakes the count. Sid grabs Raven and chokeslam! Sid picks Raven up...POWERBOMB! Sid yells at Raven.Sid: I'M COMING AFTER YOU AND THAT IC TITLE!Sid rolls out of the ring. RM crawls over to Raven and covers him. 1...2..RM is pulled out of the ring by Raditz!JT: Where in the hell did Raditz come from!Raditz picks RM up...Krionic Kick! Raditz gorilla presses RM, then throws him threw the ropes back into the ring. Raven covers 1...2...3! Raven holds onto the IC Title. Raditz throw RM out of the ring. Raditz an Raven have a stare down, then the two men hug. Raven is handed his belt and a mic.Raven: Everyone! This is my new right hand man, Raditz! And together we will rule the IWA!From out of the crowd Chaos jumps over the guardrail and onto the apron behind Raven and Raditz. Chaos springboard off the top rope and bulldogs both men. Chaos picks them both up and smashes their heads together. Both Raven and Raditz roll out of the ring, and head for the back. Chaos is standing on the top turnbuckle, telling Raven and Raditz to bring it on.Brain: So Raven is the idiot that brought Raditz to the IWA?!JT: It looks that way. Well that does it for the first half of Massacre, we'll be back right after this commercial.
