IWA’s Barbed Wire (8/1999) — Part 1 of 2

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Barbed Wire: The IWA PPV Larry Zbyszko: Welcome to Barbed Wire: The IWA Pay-Per-View! We're here at the Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Georgia, for a huge IWA pay-per-view event. With me always is Michael Cole. Michael Cole: Tonight, we're gonna see the each and every title, except for the Online Title, on the line tonight. In our main event, it's Canyon vs. Turney for the World Title. Canyon has definitely showed a major rise towards his stardom in the IWA, while Turney, one of the best champions in history, is near the height of his game. Larry Zbyszko: Well, actually, there has been a downfall in the last few weeks in Turney's stock as a wrestler in the IWA. Yes, he is the champion, but he could probably do alot better than his recent showings. Michael Cole: Well, without further adue, we're gonna kick this show on the road as we have a fight for the Cruiserweight Title. Jeff Halesz vs. Hardcore Tom vs. ChaosCruiserweight Title David Penzer: This following matchup is scheduled for onefall for the IWA Cruiserweight Title. First headed to the ring are the challengers. First headed to the ring, standing at 6'0" and weighing 225 pounds, the master of The Verdict, from Calgary, Alberta, Canada, The Public Enemy Jeff Halesz. P.E. 2000 busts over the IWA SoundScreen but nobody appears from the back. Then NateDogg shows up and begin to speak. President NateDogg: Well, there's just a little something about this Cruiserweight Title match, see, neither Jeff Halesz or Hardcore Tom is in the back. They didn't show up. My guess is they saw each other in the airport and kicked the shit out of each other. But if they see this on pay-per-view, ya'll give me a ring from the hospital. Chaos is the winner of this match by no-show. Winner by no-showIWA Cruiserweight ChampionChaos Larry Zbyszko: What a quick matchup! Man, Chaos was so good I didn't even see those other guys. Michael Cole: He must have scared them way away. They'll be alright though. Larry Zbyszko: I don't really know what to say about that match, but Chaos still has the belt. Michael Cole: I think we should move to our next match. Freebird vs. Death AngelEuropean Title David Penzer: This following matchup is scheduled for onefall. First headed to the ring 5'9" and weighing 199 pounds, the master of the Death Kiss, one of the soul survivers of the Flock, from St. Louis, Death Angel. Out from the back appears NateDogg again with microphone in hand. Once again, he speaks. President NateDogg: Well, I'm gonna make this one short and sweet. Death Angel didn't show up either. So Freebird wins by no-show. Winner by no-showStill European ChampionFreebird Michael Hayes Larry Zbyszko: This is about the greatest pay-per-view ever. But we've got 5 more high quality matches to go. Michael Cole: Well, we need to go to Penzer one more time. Let's pray that he doesn't get interrupted again. Raditz vs. O.G. ChunkHardcore Title David Penzer: This following match is hardcore rules for the IWA Hardcore Title. First headed to the ring. Standing at 6'2" and weighing 320 pounds, the master of the Gangsta-Bomb, from Chambersburgh, Pennsylvania, O.G. Chunk. O.G. Chunk's theme shoots out over the IWA SoundScreen as Chunk appears from the back. He throws his hands high in the air as the crowd fills with boos. He walks toward the ring with the title strapped around his waist. David Penzer: And his opponent, standing at 6'8" and weighing 324 pounds, from the Saiyans, the master of the Unknown Power, from Planet Vejita, Raditz. The Saiyans Theme hits the IWA SoundScreen as the arena fills black. The lights begin to fill a little as a man is seen walking in a robe towards the ring. The lights then fill the arena as Raditz is seen behind O.G. Chunk in the ring. The man in the entry area pulls off the robe to see Nappa. Chunk turns around and runs into a big kick to the chest by Raditz. Nappa runs to the ring and throws a chair into Raditz. Raditz catches it and bashes it across the knee of O.G. Chunk. Chunk lets out a blood curdling scream and Raditz lifts him to his feet. Raditz whips Chunk towards the ropes, but Chunk falls to the ground. Nappa steps into the ring as Raditz begins to beat down on Chunk. Both men then begin to pummel on Chunk. Big E appears from the back and runs in the ring. Nappa hits Big E with a Chryonic Kick the second he steps up and knocks him out of the ring. Nappa pulls up Chunk and launches him into the corner. Raditz steps back and then hits O.G. Chunk with THE UNKNOWN POWER!!! Chunk falls straight to the ground as Raditz makes his cover. 1...2...3. Winner by pinfallNew IWA Hardcore ChampionRaditz Larry Zbyszko: Now I'm sure glad to see some action in here tonight. By far, that was probably the greatest match on the card. Actually, it has been the greatest match. Michael Cole: Yeah, and both Nappa and Big E came out to help their partners. I would expect to see those two teams in a little bit of a fight in the future. Larry Zbyszko: I'm really into this pay-per-view, so we need to get into another match. Let's go to our Tag Team Titles match. Turney and Stone Cold vs. Freebird and Triple H David Penzer: This following matchup is scheduled for one fall for the Vacant IWA Tag Team Titles. First headed to the ring, former champions, at a combined weight of 547 pounds, Turney and Stone Cold Steve Austin. Turney and Austin show from the back as the crowd boos at them. They look to the ring as the referee holds the belts high and they assure each other that they will belong to them once again. David Penzer: And their opponents, representing DX, at a total weight of 576 pounds, Freebird and Triple H. Are You Ready shoots over the IWA SoundScreen as Triple H and Freebird appear from the back accompanied by Chyna and Sexy Sabrina. They pay no attention to the fans as they crotch chop at Turney and Austin. Turney and Austin run out of the ring and go right for Triple H and Freebird. Turney and Austin run over Triple H and Freebird with clotheslines. Turney grabs Freebird and whips him towards the ring. Freebird runs into the side of it. Turney then grabs Freebird and rolls him in the ring. Turney begins to pound on Freebird as the referee tries to break up Austin and Triple H. Chyna sneaks her way into the ring and hits Turney with a Crotch Chop. She sneaks out as Freebird makes a cover on Turney. Turney gets covered for over a three, but by the time the referee gets back, it's too late. Freebird whips Turney into the ropes, and then attacks with the IRON CLAW!!! Freebird pulls Turney to the ground, but Austin comes from behind and hits him on his head with a chair. Freebird falls to the ground. Triple H runs into the ring and kicks Austin in the gut. Triple H underhooks him and then drops him with the PEDIGREE!!! Triple H goes for the cover, but the referee doesn't count because he is not he legal man. Triple H drags Freebird over to their corner and then steps out of the ring. He then tags himself in and enters the ring. Chyna and Sexy Sabrina wake Hayes and pull him out of the ring. Triple H grabs Austin and throws him outside the ring. Triple H then grabs Turney and whips him into the ropes. Triple H goes for a clothesline, but Turney ducks. He spins Triple H around and drops him with a spinebuster. Both men lay out on the ground as the referee makes his 10 count. Turney gets up and goes for a cover. 1...2...Triple H kicked out at the last second. Turney pounds his fist in disgust. Turney looks back but Austin is still out. Turney gets up and puts Triple H in a choke. The referee makes him break it so Turney then applies a sleeper. Triple H fights it, but to no avail. Then Sexy Sabrina steps in the ring and hits Turney with a high heel. Triple H covers Turney. 1...2...Austin makes his way into the ring and kicks Triple H in the back. Austin pulls Triple H to his feet then whips him into the ropes. Austin lays a kick to Triple H's gut and then drops him with a Stunner. Both men lay limp in the ring as the referee makes another 10 count. Turney rolls his arm on Triple H for a cover. 1...2...Triple H is dragged out of the ring by Chyna. He shakes off for a second and makes his way back in. Turney goes right for Triple H. Triple H throws a shoulder into Turney's stomach. He then hops over the top rope and drops Turney with a neckbreaker. Triple H tags in Freebird. Triple H runs over to Austin and hits him with a forearm. Austin tries to get into the ring after Triple H, but the referee blocks him. Triple H goes to grabs Turney and then drops him with the Pedigree. Triple H steps out as Chyna comes to the other side of the ring. She drags on Austin's leg as Freebird covers Turney. 1...2...3. Winners by pinfallNEW IWA Tag ChampionsFreebird and Triple H Larry Zbyszko: Well, our vacant titles aren't vacant anymore. Triple H and Freebird did what they said they would, and that was win. Michael Cole: We don't have much time for talking so we have to get to our next match.
