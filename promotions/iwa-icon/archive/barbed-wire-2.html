IWA’s Barbed Wire (8/1999) — Part 2 of 2

Barbed Wire: The IWA PPV Sycho Sid vs. Chaos vs. O.G. Chunk Intercontinenal Title David Penzer: First I have a short announcement. O.G. Chunk will not be able to perform in this matchup due to injury. Now, first headed to the ring, stabdubg at 6'9" and weighing 313 pounds, representing Degeneration-X, the master of the Powerbomb, from West Memphis, Arkansas, Sycho Sid. Sid appears from the back as the DX Theme shoots over the IWA SoundScreen. He screams at the fans to where he can be heard all over the arena. He walks to the arena and grabs a little fan with a DX poster. He then shares crotch chops with the kid. David Penzer: And his opponent, standing at 5'11" tall and weighing 167 pounds, the master of the Reaver from Grand Haven, Michigan, Chaos. Chaos comes from the back as the arena fills with a blood red color. He makes attempts to scare fans as he makes his way to the ring. Chaos steps into the ring and goes for a hit on Sid. Sid just looks at him and clasps around his throat. Sid lifts Chaos up and drops him with a Chokeslam. Sid then pulls Chaos up and drops him with a POWERBOMB!!! Sid steps on Chaos for the cover. 1...2...3. Winner by pinfallNEW IWA Intercontinental ChampionSycho Sid Larry Zbyszko: I hope Chaos can live. Sycho Sid just killed him. Michael Cole: Well think Larry, there's almost a 150 pound difference there. Chaos is a little guy, and Sid is a big guy. There's not much a guy can do. Larry Zbyszko: Well at least he tried. Here's our next match for the IWA Stables Titles. D-Generation-X vs. nWo Turney David Penzer: This following matchup is scheduled for onefall for the IWA Stables Titles. First headed to the ring are the challengers, Triple H, Shawn Michaels, and Freebird Michael Hayes, D-Generation-X. The DX Theme hits the IWA SoundScreen as DX appears from the back. They are escorted by nobody this time. David Penzer: And their opponents, the IWA Stables Champions, Turney, Stone Cold, and Teen, nWo Turney. nWo Turney appears from the back as Turney's Theme, Thunderkiss '65, hits the IWA SoundScreen. Teen starts it off with Freebird. Both men tie up as Freebird reverses it into a hammerlock. Freebird pushes Teen into the DX corner immediately and begins to pummel on Teen. Turney and Stone Cold come in for the save as the referee gets Teen away from DX. Teen tags in Stone Cold as all of DX's members head over to their corner. All men begin to brawl together as Freebird takes Teen to the outside. Freebird grabs a chair and pounds down on Teen. Austin and Michaels also take their to the outside. Austin lays Michaels on a table and flies through it, sending Michaels down along the way. In the ring, Triple H and Turney begin to brawl. Chyna makes her way toward the ring with a chair. Triple H hits Turney with a high knee making Turney dazed. Triple H holds Turney in a half nelson as Chyna pulls the chair back. At the last second, Turney ducks and Chyna knocks Triple H out. Turney grabs Chyna and chokeslams her then rolls her out of the ring. Then Turney grabs Triple H and elevates him with the TURNEY BOMB!!! Turney goes for a cover. 1...2...3. Winners by pinfallStill IWA Stables ChampsnWo Turney Larry Zbyszko: Everybody, we have a pleasant surprise for you tonight, the return of Michael Buffer. Canyon vs. TurneyWorld Title Michael Buffer: Ladies and gentlemen, it's time for the Barbed Wire: The IWA pay-per-view main event. Are you ready? For the thousands in attendance and millions watching around the world, ladies and gentlemen, let's get ready to rumble!!! First headed to the ring, standing at 6'4" and weighing 246, the master of the Avalanche, from an unknown place, Canyon. ANd his opponent, standing at 6'10" tall, weighing 295 pounds, the master of the Turney Bomb, from somewhere in Texas, Turney. Both men are in the ring as the referee begins to check them. He signals the start of the match, and almost immediately, somebody sneaks out from the back. Turney has Canyon in the corner, and somebody hits Turney with a bone. He falls to the mat as the referee begins to count. 1...2...Turney kicks out. Turney gets up and begins to pound on Canyon as another masked man comes out, this time a little bigger than before. He comes in and hits Turney with a superkick. Turney falls flat to the mat and Canyon makes his cover. 1...2...3. Canyon wins the title. The man comes back in the ring and pounds on Turney again and again but then Turney begins to regain an advantage. Canyon and the other masked man head their way to the back. The other masked man reveals himself as Chaos. Then Turney grabs the bigger masked man and Turney Bombs him. He pulls his mask off to reveal HBK. He grabs a mic and begins to speak. Turney: Well, I've proven I can beat the best, because I am the best, but I was screwed. I'm outta here. Winner by pinfallNEW IWA World ChampionCanyon
