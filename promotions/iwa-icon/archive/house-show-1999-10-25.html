IWA House Show (10/25/1999)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IWA House Show 10-25-99 10-25-99Mobile, Alabama ShowTime vs. HavokNon-Title These two decided that they would fight when Havok came out for an interview for the fans. Then ShowTime came from the back with a referee along side him. Havok hit Rosario with the TOTAL DESTRUCTION, but "ShowTime" made it to the ropes. "ShowTime" came back and dropped Havok with the Show Stoppa for the victory. After the match, Roasrio tried to burn Havok, but referees filled the ring and held the man back.Winner: "ShowTime" Marcus Rosario Rohn Jericho vs. Hemp Man Rohn Jericho took out Hemp Man very quickly. After the match, Bald Man came down to the ring and taunted Jericho. It was a mistake, cause Jericho delivered the SIDE SALTO to Bald Man. Winner: Rohn Jericho New Jack vs. Big Poppa PimpHardcore Titie New Jack took on Big Poppa Pimp in a very good Hardcore Title matchup. New Jack had Big Poppa Pimp bleeding and then delivered the Balcony Dive for victory. Rob Van Dam then appeared from the back and brawled with New Jack, exciting the fans of their pay-per-view match on Halloween. Winner: New Jack Orge Lambart vs. Drunkaholic These two men fought in a non-title matchup, although Orge did bring his belt to the ring ready to defend. Instead, Orge used his belt to gain victory after an ORGE ENDER to Drunkaholic. Winner: Orge Lambart BioHazard vs. Sting These two men had their energy worked out in a non-title matchup. Sting took this match to new heights though. Sting got the fans really into this one with 3 Stinger Splashes. Then he applied the SCORPION DEATH LOCK!!! Winner: Sting X-Pac vs. Mad Stoner Mad Stoner took on X-Pac in this match where he made his return. X-Pac had a good showing, but Mad Stoner showed no ring rust. After a Your Cashed, Stoner took victory.
