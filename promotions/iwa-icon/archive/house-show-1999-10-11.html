IWA House Show 10-11-99 IWA House Show10-11-99 All House Show results will be in short form, just because they don't count, and it's easier, so get over it. New Jack vs. Kasino The House Show opened up with Kasino fighting New Jack in a Hardcore brawl. New Jack hit Kasino with his Balcony Dive, but Sycho Sid came out and Powerbombed New Jack. Kasino took home victory. Winner by pinfallKasino The Cat vs. Sabu The two wrestlers fought against eachother with great strength. The Cat proved why he was the greatest though after dropping the Feliner on Sabu, taking victory. Winner by pinfallThe Cat Rob Van Dam vs. Big Pete RobVan Dam defended his Hardcore Title against new jobber, Big Pete. Rob Van Dam killed Big Pete, finishing the match with a Van Daminator. Winner by pinfallRob Van Dam Jigga vs. Hammer Time Jigga came down to the ring and showed that he's not about fun and games. He totaled Hammer Time and proved that he deserves a Hardcore Title shot. He finished Hammer Time with a Jigga Juggla. Winner by pinfallJigga Rohn Jericho vs. Bald Man Rohn Jericho has a descent matchup, but the Bald Man got a few good shots in. Rohn Jericho hit the Side Salto and was about to take victory when Cyrus Fulton came to the ring and hooked in the Bastard-Plex. Bald Man took victory. Winner by pinfallBald Man Canyon vs. Drunkaholic In this pre-mainevent match, Drunkaholic came down to the ring and promised Canyon a six-pack of beer if he could beat him. Well, Drunkaholic ended up smashing Drunkaholic with the beer and dropped Canyon with the Exploding Keggar for victory. Winner by pinfallDrunkaholic Freebird Michael Hayes vs. The Brewer Both men had their ups and downs in this match. Near the end, The Brewer connected onto Freebird with the Recycler. He was about to cover when Raven appeared from the crowd and Evenflowed both men. No Contest This IWA House Show took place in Huntsville, Alabama. The next 3 IWA Events will take place as following: Tuesday, October 12, 1999-Chattanooga, Tennessee, Tuesday Nitro Thursday, October 14, 1999-Knoxville, Tennessee, IWA House Show Saturday, Octber 16, 1999-Nashville, Tennessee, Saturday Night Massacre
