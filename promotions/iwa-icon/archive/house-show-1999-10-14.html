IWAHouse Show10-14-99The October 14th House Show takes place in Knoxville,TN.Bald Man vs Limp Dick vs Rohn JerichoA first Jericho wasn't on the card but he made his way down to ringside and forced this into a three way match. Jericho defeated both men by pinning one on top of the other one. Winner:Rohn JerichoX vs HammertimeX, for alittle guy came out and blew Hammertime away. X hit a shooting star press and a diving head butt off the top. He even piledrived HammerTime off the top ropes. Winner: XRVD vs Hemp ManRVD showed everyone at the house show why he was the whole F'n show by kicking Hemp Man's ass. Hemp Man start to get a few moves off, but RVD was too much for him. RVD hit Hemp Man with the five star frog splash and got the 1..2..3! Winner:RVDBrewer vs SevendSevend actual gave Brewer a run for his money. The two man battle in and outside the ring. Sevend looked like he had things won with three huge chair shots to Brewers head, but Brewer kickout. Sevend went for more chair shots, but Brewer dodged and picked Sevend up for the Recycler and nailed it! Before the three count could fall, Orge came out and broke up the count with a shot to Brewers head with the World Title. Orge and Brewer fought all the way to the back. The match was thrown out. Winner: DrawBerlin vs Cyrus FultonThe best wrestling match of the night. Both men pulled out kickass moves, one after the other. Berlin hit moves like a brainbuster off the top ropes, reverse DDT, and a bulldog off the ropes. Cyrus hit a big time move off the top with his bastardplex, a running powerbomb, and DDT. Berlin hit the Black Deat, but pinned to close to the ropes, and Fulton got his foot up on it, breaking the three count. One reversal later Cyrus had the Nightmare on. Berlin didn't tap out, but passout. Winner: Cyrus FultonMain EventDrunkaholic vs HBKThis one was over quick. HBK went for the Sweet hin Music off that bat and missed. Drunk dazed HBK with left and rights. Backed him into the corner and hit the Exploding Keggar! Drunk got the 1..2..3! Winner: Drunkaholic.
