IWA’s Sweet Surrender (6/27/1999) — Part 2 of 3

Sweet Surrender Part 2 New Jack vs. Shawn MichaelsGangsta's ParadiseDavid Penzer: This following matchup is scheduled for onefall in a Gangsta's Paradise matchup. First headed to the ring standing at 6'1" tall and weighing 255 pounds, the master of the Balcony Dive, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, New Jack. And his opponent, standing at 6'1" tall and weighing 227 pounds, representing Degeneration-X, the master of Sweet Chin Music, from San Antonio, Texas, the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels. New Jack appears from the back as he gets cheers from the crowd because of his major improvement lately. Shawn Michaels appears from the back, alone. He throws crotch chops at he crowd as they boo even louder. He steps into the ring and begins to flex as the pyros go off behind him. New Jack ties up with Shawn Michaels. New Jack puts Michaels into a headlock. Michaels pushes himself out of it and goes into the ropes. New Jack knocks him over with a drop toe hold. New Jack gets up and drops an elbow to Michaels' back. New Jack sits on Michaels back and begins to pound his face into the ring. The referee begins to count to five, but New Jack stops almost instantaneously. New Jack applies a chin lock to Michaels. Michaels begins to get up to his feet. Michaels runs backwards and rams New Jack into the turnbuckle. Michaels stands New Jack back up in the corner and throws chops at him. Michaels then stands back and applies a foot choke. The referee begins to count to five but Michaels pushes him away. The referee pushes Michaels back and Michaels gets up in his face. New Jack grabs a chair from the weapons and hits Michaels in his back with it. Michaels falls to the mat and doesn't move. New Jack rolls him over and sits on his chest for a cover. 1...2...Michaels pokes him in the eye. New Jack rolls off Michaels and holds his eye. New Jack starts to get to his feet. New Jack turns around and Michaels delivers him a little SWEET CHIN MUSIC!!! Michaels goes for the cover. 1...2...3. Winner by pinfallShawn Michaels Larry Zbyszko: Michaels again won a match due to his little tricks. Somebody is going to get him back. Michael Cole: You wanna talk about payback? This next match has payback written all over it. Oh yeah, it's for the IWA Stables Titles. Larry Zbyszko: We've got front row tickets to hell in a cell, or just pure hell for anybody!Michael Cole: Let's go down to the ring with David Penzer.House of Pain vs. Sex, Drugs, and Heavy Metalvs. Degeneration-XStables Titles David Penzer: This following matchup is scheduled for onefall in a cell for the IWA Stables Titles. First headed to the ring, a trio representing the House of Pain, weighing a combined weight of 767 pounds, Raven, Mad Milldawg, and DoggFather. And their opponents, a trio representing Corporate-SDH, weighing a combined weight of 827 pounds, Mr. Fro Chuck Brown, Mad Stoner, and Steel. And their opponents, the IWA Stables Champions, weighing a total of 847 pounds, Triple H, Orge Lambart, and Ringmaster. The House of Pain appears from the back. Raven still looks disgusted with his facial expressions, and is obviously still dissapointed. Then Sex, Drugs, and Heavy Metal appear from the back. They get a huge pop as the go to the ring through the crowd. And then Degeneration-X appears from the back. Like always, they get booed and throw crotch chops. Triple H starts it off with Steel. Steel and Triple H tie up, but Steel throws Triple H back into the corner. Triple H runs outside the ring and starts to try to find an opening to get out. He begins to climb the cage. Steel leaves the ring an grabs Triple H off the cage and drops him with DEATH FROM ABOVE!!! Ringmaster flies off the top turnbuckle and hits Steel with a suicide back body drop. Ringmaster rolls Steel back in the ring and gets Triple H back in the ring. Triple H and Steel are both slow to get up. Triple H leans and tags in Ringmaster while Steel tags in Mad Stoner. Stoner ties up with Ringmaster. Stoner backs Ringmaster back into the Corporate-SDH corner. Fro and Steel begins to pounds Ringmaster to the ground. Stoner goes to the middle of the ring and runs toward Ringmaster. Stoner hits him with a baseball slide to the nuts! Stoner pulls Ringmaster up and whips him into the House of Pain corner. Milldawg climbs to the top turnbuckle and knocks Ringmaster over with a huracanrana. Stoner grabs Milldawg and sends him outside the ring. Stoner tags in DoggFather. DoggFather ties up with Ringmaster. Ringmaster backs off and tags back in Triple H. Triple H gets into the ring as DoggFather gets the first taste of his #1 contendership. DoggFather ties up with Triple H, but Triple H slings DoggFather outside the ring with a hiptoss. Raven runs into the ring but Triple H back body drops him onto DoggFather. All three House of Pain members are out on the outside. Out from under the ring comes President NateDogg with a chair and a microphone. He begins to speak.President NateDogg: I think I found a trap door. Oh, cool, just the place I wanted to be.NateDogg steps into the ring and hits Triple H with the chair. Stoner and Fro come from behind and take out Orge Lambart. Ringmaster steps into the ring and takes the mic from NateDogg.Ringmaster: This wasn't part of the deal.President NateDogg: No dice! President NateDogg takes the chair and whacks it over Ringmaster's head. He covers Ringmaster and makes his own count. 1...2...3.President NateDogg: Your winners and new IWA Stables Champions, Corporate-SDH. Winners by pinfall and NEW IWA Stables ChampionsCorporate-SDH Larry Zbyszko: NateDogg just helped out his fellow SDH members win the IWA Stables Titles. What a match!Michael Cole: What do you think Ringmaster said when he said "this wasn't part of the deal?" Larry Zbyszko: I don't know. Whatever it was it obviously didn't mean much to NateDogg. But now Corporate-SDH has taken the crowd as the best stable in the Icon Wrestling Alliance. Michael Cole: That's just too much. It was a great matchup We must go back down to the ring with David Penzer though. Raven vs. X-PacEuropean Title David Penzer: This following matchup is scheduled for onefall. First headed to the ring as the challenger. He stands at 6'1" tall and weighs 245 pounds, he represents the House of Pain, the master of the Evenflow, Raven. And his opponent, standing at 6'0" and weighing 212 pounds, the master of the X-Factor, from Minneapolis, Minnesota, X-Pac. Raven appears from the back. He holds a microphone in his hand and begins to speak. Raven: X-Pac, I don't know what got into me on Tuesday when I attacked you, but I'm sorry. I think it was the fact that I had been screwed over by my partner again. Let's make this match a great one, and remember, it's Raven's rules.X-Pac appears from the back as he holds the European Title in the air high. X-Pac throws crotch chops to the crowd and gets them cheering for him. X-Pac vs Raven for the European Title in a Ravens Rules Match-UpBoth men are in the ring now, and Raven walks up to X-Pac and puts his hand out. X-Pac looks at him for a few seconds then shakes his hand. The ref asks for the bell, then the two tie up. Raven puts X-Pac into a headlock and squeezes hard. X-Pac then throws Raven into the ropes, X-Pac leapfrogs over Raven, Raven comes back off the ropes and X-Pac connects with a spinning heel kick. X-Pac pulls Raven up by the hair, and brings him to the corner and starts bashing his head into the turbuckle. Raven sticks his foot out an block it, then grabs X-Pac by the hair and rams his head into the turnbuckle. X-Pac stumbles back and Raven hits him with a drop kick and sends X-Pac threw the ropes and he lands on the floor. Raven rolls out of the ring and picks X-Pac up. Raven takes X-Pac and whips him into the guardrailing hard. Raven walks over and shoves the time keeper out of his chair and throws it into the ring. Raven picks X-Pac up and rolls him into the ring. Raven climbs back into the ring to find X-Pac standing there with chair in hand. X-Pac rushes Raven and cracks him over the head with the chair, then again , and one more time. Raven stumbls and lands on his ass in the corner. X-Pac starts pumping his arms, and runs and nails him with Bronco Buster! X-Pac pulls Raven up, but Raven gives him a low blow. Raven springs up and connects with a knee to the face. Raven grabs the chair and sets it up in the middle of the ring, turns and grabs X-Pac and gives him a frontface suplex onto the chair! X-Pac rolls around on the ground holding his gut. Raven rolls out of the ring and goes under the ring and pulls out a table. He throws it into the ring and rolls back in. Raven sets it up near the corner, when he turns around X-Pac is up and nails him with a dropkick. Raven rolls over the table and lands near the Turnbuckle. X-Pac walks around the table and grabs Raven. X-Pac hops up to the top ropes and drags Raven up with him. X-Pac gives his signal.Cole:It looks like X-Pac is going to try and send Raven threw the table with the X-Factor!X-Pac is gettng read when Raven breaks the hold and hooks onto X-Pacs Head. EVENFLOW from the top ropes and threw the table! Raven hooks the leg, and the ref counts. 1...2...3! Raven is your new European Champ!Cole:What a match! Those two turned it up here tonight.Larry:Yes they did, lets get ready for our next match.
