IWA’s Sweet Surrender (6/27/1999) — Part 1 of 3

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sweet Surrender 6-27-99 Larry Zbyszko: Welcome to IWA's Sweet Surrender. We are in Beantown, yes, Boston, Massachusetts. Tonight, we probably have the greatest card we've ever had. To discuss with me is my partner, Michael Cole.Michael Cole: Thank you, Larry! We are here in the Fleetcenter, and we're ready to tear it up in here. We've got a sold-out crowd here, and we've got the matches to back it up. Every single title, except the Online Title, will be defended tonight. The Cruiserweight Title will be given an owner in a four corners match. The Stables Titles will be defended by Degeneration-X against Corporate-SDH and the House of Pain. Larry Zbyszko: In that match, I would have to select Corporate-SDH to take the Stables Titles home. They are all star power and could take anybody out. And of course, DX is standing in their way. Michael Cole: And you can't forget the fact that the House of Pain is nothing but falling apart as of late. And in our main event, we have the match for the gold. Yes, the IWA World Title. In that match, the champion, Triple H, will defend against the challenger for the second straight month, Sting. Since the beginning, these men haven't been able to get along. In their previous two meetings, they have split them down the middle. You can call this one a tie breaker if you may. It's going to become a great match. And if Sting wins, he will enter the IWA Hall of Fame. Larry Zbyszko: Apparently President NateDogg also has some things to say tonight. Everytime he says something, it's always important, so look for some blockbuster announcements to come our way. Michael Cole: Well ladies and gentlemen, it's showtime.Freebird Michael Hayes vs. TazHardcore Title David Penzer: This following matchup is scheduled for one fall for the IWA Hardcore Title. First headed to the ring as the challenger, standing at 5'11" tall and weighing 287 pounds, representing the House of Pain, the master of the Hyatakashimae Tazmission, from The Bronx, Taz. And his opponent, the IWA Hardcore Champion, standing at 6'6" tall and weighing 330 pounds, the master of the Iron Claw, from Badd Street, USA, Freebird Michael Hayes. Taz comes down from the back and stands on the entry ramp as he throws his hands high in the air, confident of becoming the IWA Hardcore Champion. Then Freebird appears out of the back as Taz stands in the ring. He gets a face pop, surprisingly. He steps up to David Penzer and takes the microphone. Freebird Michael Hayes: Taz, there's no doubt in my mind that I'm a defending champion. But there's no way I'm gonna fight tonight, unless I get a stipulation. Now I like the House of Pain, but there's one thing that I don't like. And that one thing is you. So Taz, how about a stipulation such as, loser leaves the fed. The fans in the crowd go wild as Taz takes the microphone. Taz: You know what, Freebird, since I know I can beat your ass with both arms ties behind my back, I accept!Taz throws the microphone at Freebird's face and then kicks him in the gut. Taz grabs Freebird by his arm and whips him into the ropes. Taz runs into Freebird with a shoulder block, but Freebird stands tall. Taz leans back and bounces off the ropes again. He drives another shoulder block into the Hardcore Champion, but Freebird stands tall. Taz leans back once again, but this time, Freebird sends him flying with a big backbody drop. Freebird climbs to the middle turnbuckle, and then jumps off with a flying legdrop. He drops onto Taz's neck and follows it with a cover. 1...2...Taz kicks out. Freebird pulls Taz up and whips him into the ropes. Taz runs past Freebird's clothesline attempt, and into the ropes. He bounces back, but Freebird drops him down with a tiltawhirl backbreaker. Freebird follows that move up with an elbow drop. Freebird bounces off the ropes and flies onto Taz with a big splash. Freebird walks outside the ring and grabs the microphone and begins to speak.Freebird Michael Hayes: Correct me if I'm wrong, but isn't this hardcore rules. Taz, where are ya at? I'm kicking your ass.Freebird puts the microphone in his pocket and points out to the back. An IWA crew member rolls a dumpster down to the ring. Freebird climbs into it and pulls out a ladder. Taz goes to throw the ladder at Taz as he gets up, but Taz kicks the ladder into Freebird's face. Taz runs to the rope and then jumps onto it. He drops onto the ladder that is on Freebird with a springboard moonsault. Taz is unable to follow up the moonsault because he injured his chest. Taz is slow to get back up. Taz pulls Freebird up and whips him into the ropes. Freebird sends a flying drop kick into Taz's ribs. Freebird jumps onto Taz's ribs and then drops onto him with an elbow drop. Freebird walks onto the ring apron and looks down into the trash dumpster. He reaches in and pulls out a microwave. He sends it flying at Taz, hitting him into the ribs. Freebird pulls Taz up and sends him outside the ring. Taz hits the trash dumpster head first. Freebird steps out onto the ring apron and throws him into the dumpster. Freebird climbs to the top turnbuckle and flies into the dumpster, landing again onto Taz's ribs. Freebird lifts Taz, but Taz hits him with a trash can. Freebird falls out of the trash can and onto the pads on the outside. Taz climbs to the edge of the trash can and jumps off with a flying plancha. Taz pulls a chair out from under the ring and whacks it onto Freebird's back. Taz pulls Freebird up and sends him into the ring. Taz goes in, but limps his way into the ring. Taz pulls Freebird up and whips him into the ropes. Taz his Freebird with a knee to the stomach, and grabs him. Taz goes for a TAZMISSION, but Freebird blocks it. Freebird turns it into a brainbuster. Freebird drags Taz to the center of the ring and hooks in the IRON CLAW!!! Taz fights it off, but eventually passes out. Winner by passoutFreebird Michael Hayes After the match, Triple H runs down from the back with a chair. He slams it over the head of the IWA Hardcore Champion. Triple H stomps down on the body of Freebird before more referees go down to the ring to get him to go to the back. Larry Zbyszko: Well, it looks as if the Icon Wrestling Alliance has trimmed the roster just a bit more, now deleting Taz. Michael Cole: Don't you think that was just too much of a guy to get rid of though? I mean come on. For the time that he was here, he made alot of noise. Larry Zbyszko: Maybe, but he hasn't showed in a couple weeks for IWA Nitro cards. That obviously isn't gonna get on the President's good side. Michael Cole: That was a great matchup. Not as hardcore as expected though. They didn't use the stuff that Freebird used to defeat Mankind last month. Michael Cole: And don't forget about Triple H. That battle seems to be heating up quite a bit. Overall, I think it was a great Hardcore Title matchup. Larry Zbyszko: We've gotta head back down to the ring with David Penzer for our next matchup. Orge Lambart and Shawn MichaelsVs. Sex, Drugs, and Rock and RollTag Team Titles David Penzer: This following matchup is scheduled for onefall for the IWA Tag Team Titles. First headed to the ring as the challengers, at a combined weight of 512 pounds, representing Degeneration-X, accompanied to the ring by Sycho Sid and Mankind, Shawn Michaels and Orge Lambart. And their opponents, at a combined weight of 540 pounds, representing Sex, Drugs, and Heavy Metal, accompanied to the ring by Lucy, Sex, Drugs, and Rock and Roll, Mr. Fro Chuck Brown and Mad Stoner. Shawn Michaels, Orge Lambart, Sycho Sid, and Mankind appear from the back, together, and throw crotch chops at the crowd as they boo loudly. They play it off as if the crowd wasn't booing. They are probably used to it. Then Mr. Fro Chuck Brown and Mad Stoner appear from the back. The fans go crazy as they hold their IWA Tag Team Titles high in the air. Mr. Fro begins the match with Michaels. The two men tie up, but are evenly matched. The back off for a second but tie up again. Fro puts Michaels into a headlock. Michaels pushes Fro away and into the ropes. Fro flies back and knocks over Michaels with a cross body block. Fro gets up and starts to lay the boots down onto Michaels. Michaels rolls out of the ring and regroups himself as he chats with Sycho Sid and Mankind. He rolls back into the ring and points up to his hand for a game of mercy. Fro doesn't go for it but instead grabs Michaels and whips him into the ropes. Fro leapfrogs over Michaels and kicks him in the gut after he bounces back off the ropes. Fro turns around and drops Michaels with a face smash. Fro lays his legs on Michaels shoulders and hooks his legs for a cover. 1...2...Michaels rolls his shoulder off the mat. Fro pulls Michaels up and whips him into the corner. Stoner hooks Michaels' arms as Fro runs into him with a big splash. Michaels falls to the mat and starts to drag himself toward Orge. Fro hooks in an ankle lock submission but Michaels lays his arm over the middle rope. Fro releases the lock. He lifts Michaels up and whips him back into the corner. Fro kicks Michaels into a sitting position while leaning on the turnbuckle. Fro steps back to the middle of the ring and drops onto Michaels with a bronco buster. Fro drags Michaels out to the center of the ring and climbs to the top turnbuckle. Fro drops onto Michaels with a DISCO BALL!!! Fro goes for the cover. 1...2...Orge Lambart steps into the ring and hits Fro with a short drop kick. Fro gets up and tags in Stoner. Stoner pulls Michaels up, but Michaels cowers away and tags in Orge. Orge ties up with Stoner. Stoner whips Orge into the ropes and hits him in the gut with a knee lift. Stoner follows that up with a legdrop down onto the throat of Orge. Stoner hooks onto Orge's leg for a cover. 1...2...Lambart kicks out. Stoner pulls Orge up and whips him into the turnbuckle. Stoner hits Orge with the Steamroller. Stoner pulls Orge out of the corner and whips him back into the ropes. Stoner catches Orge and puts on Too Baked. Shawn Michaels climbs to the top turnbuckle and drops onto Stoner with a missle drop kick. Lucy goes over to the DX side and hits Sycho Sid in the head with a chair. Mankind chases her away before she gets a chance to hit him. Stoner falls to the mat as well as Orge. Mr. Fro climbs to the top turnbuckle and connects off with the DISCO BALL onto Orge Lambart. Fro rolls Stoner onto Orge for a cover. Fro goes to leave the ring as Mankind comes from the other side and hooks in with a Mandible Claw. 1...2...a man from the audience pulls Orge to the ropes. Stoner leans over the top rope and starts to hit the man, but the man hits Stoner with a foreign object. Orge grabs Stoner from behind and rolls up Stoner for a cover. 1...2...3. Winners by pinfall andNEW IWA Tag Team ChampionsShawn Michaels and Orge Lambart Larry Zbyszko: Oh my god! They just screwed Sex, Drugs, and Rock and Roll out of the IWA Tag Team Titles. That wasn't right. Michael Cole: SDR&R is sure going to want to get payback. They've got the chance to do it as the night progresses also.Larry Zbyszko: Speaking of the night progressing, let's get to our following matchup.Ringmaster vs. Stone Cold Steve AustinWinner to recieve Online Title shotDavid Penzer: This following matchup is scheduled for onefall. First headed to the ring, standing at 6'11" tall and weighing 316 pounds, representing Degeneration-X, the master of the Lion Tamer, from Woodward, Oklahoma, Ringmaster. And his opponent, standing at 6'2" tall and weighing 252 pounds, the master of the Stunner, from Dallas, Texas, Stone Cold Steve Austin. Ringmaster appears from the back as the crowd begins to boo. He throws crotch chops that set off the pyros. Then Stone Cold appears from the back as the crowd goes crazy. Stone Cold throws a double bird at Ringmaster and then he runs and slides into the ring. Stone Cold and Ringmaster trade hits until Stone Cold takes the lead. Stone Cold grabs Ringmaster and whips him into the ropes. Stone Cold kicks Ringmaster in the gut and goes for the STUNNER!!! Ringmaster pushes him into the ropes. Stone Cold bounces back and Ringmaster hits him with a big boot. Ringmaster drops onto Stone Cold with a leg drop and hooks his leg for a cover. 1...Stone Cold kicks out of the cover. Ringmaster gets up and whips Stone Cold into the ropes. Stone Cold runs into Ringmaster and throws a series of punches while sitting on his chest. Stone Cold bounces off the ropes and drops a knee onto Ringmaster's face. Ringmaster rolls out of the ring as he realizes he has a gash on his head. Ringmaster walks away and says "Forget About It!" Stone Cold leaves the ring and drags Ringmaster by his hair back towards the ring. Stone Cold whips Ringmaster into the steel guardrail. Stone Cold grabs Ringmaster and sends him back into the ring. Stone Cold grabs a chair and sends it into the ring. Stone Cold rolls himself into the ring as Ringmaster begins to stomp on him. Ringmaster pulls Stone Cold up and whips him into the ropes. Stone Cold ducks under a clothesline attempt by Ringmaster. Stone Cold goes for the STUNNER but Ringmaster makes an amazing reverse into a German Suplex. Ringmaster drags Stone Cold to the center of the ring and hooks in the LION TAMER!!! Stone Cold drags himself and reaches the ropes. Shawn Michaels appears from the back. Ringmaster sends Stone Cold outside the ring. Ringmaster begins to argue with the referee as Michaels delivers Sweet Chin Music to Stone Cold. The Big Man Turney appears from the back with a chair. He chases Michaels around and to the back. Turney heads back down to the ring and keeps Ringmaster away from Stone Cold for a minute. Stone Cold gets up and slides himself back into the ring, only to be stomped by Ringmaster almost immediately. Ringmaster drags Stone Cold to the center of the ring again and applies another LION TAMER!!! Stone Cold reaches out and grabs a hold of Turney's hands. Turney drags him to the ropes. Ringmaster is forced to release the hold. Ringmaster gets up and begins to reach out toward Turney on the outside. Turney threatens to hit him with the chair, so Ringmaster turns around. Stone Cold kicks Ringmaster in the gut and drops him with the STUNNER!!! Stone Cold goes for the cover. 1...2...3.Winner by pinfallStone Cold Steve Austin Larry Zbyszko: It looks as if Stone Cold beat DX at their own game. So this Thursday, Stone Cold will face Mr. Fro Chuck Brown for the Online Title, and Ringmaster will face Raven and fellow DX member Shawn Michaels on Massacre!Michael Cole: It looks as if Raven is in for another one of those handicap matches instead of a 3-way battle. At least he has plenty of experience. Larry Zbyszko: Well next we have an interview with a man everybody knows, the President and owner of Icon Wrestling Allaince, President NateDogg.Tonight, Tonight by the Smashing Pumpkins fills the P. A. system as NateDogg appears from the back with a microphone. President NateDogg: You guys know how everytime I come out here to fire somebody? Well I'm breaking tradition. I've only got good things to say. Now these good things have to do with our main event for the World Title. Well, the cage that is being used in our Gangsta's Paradise next will also be used in your match Triple H. And Helmsley, I've got one other surprise for you. Now think back to the eigth of this month. The whack heard round the world and then the cover. Well Hunter, you're looking at the same guy who's gonna be making that same count with you flat on your back tonight! I'm the special referee. Have fun. Also, good luck to my boys in SDH tonight. Steel, get that IC Title. Fro, win the Cruiserweight Title. And all three of you, win the Stables Titles. Good night. Down for your Gangsta's Paradise match next.
