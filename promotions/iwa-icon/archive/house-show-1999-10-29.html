IWA House Show (10/29/1999)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IWA House Show10-29-99Beaumont, TXThe first of two warmed up matches, was Hemp Man vs Hammer Time. It wasn't a long match, and Hammer Time got off his YOU CAN"T TOUCH THIS finisher. Hammer Time got the 1, 2, 3. The second warm up match was Limp Dick vs Fat Bertha. The crowd had good laugh as Big Bertha slapped Limp Dick around the ring, and made fun of his limp dick. Bertha finished with bouncing off the ropes, jumping into the air and landing on Limp Dick's head. Fat Bertha got the 1, 2, 3.Rohn Jericho vs RVDRVD had his moments, but over all Jericho had his way with RVD. Jericho finshed things up with a SIDE SALTO off the top rope. Rohn Jericho is your winner.New Jack vs BiohazardNon TitleThe two men put on a good show. The match went to the stands were New Jack beat the hell out of Biohazard, with chair shots to his head, back, and legs. New Jack brought Biohazard back to the ring, where he went for the cover, but Bioazard kicked out. From there Biohazard reversed things by hitting a a frontface suplex, then sends New Jack down with a high angle back body drop. Biohazard throws on the boston crab. New Jack fights his way slowly to the ropes to break the move. Biohazard whips New Jack into the ropes, and hits him with TOXIC WASTE when he comes back. New Jack is in the center of the ring, and isn't going no where. New Jack taps out. Biohazard is your winner.HBK vs CanyonBoth men put up a good fight, but over all HBK was in charge. Canyon hit a brainbuster, then a Russian leg sweep. But Canyon couldn't keep HBK down. Canyon was laid out by SWEET CHIN MUSIC, and cover Canyon. Orge made it to the ring. And broke up the count. Orge starts laying into HBK and backs him into the corner. HBK blocks a punch, and fights his way out. Canyon is back up and spins Orge around...AVALANCHE! Canyon gets back up, and HBK sends him back down with SWEET CHIN MUSIC! HBK covers and gets the 1, 2, 3! Drunkaholic vs "Showtime" Marcus RosarioThese guys had the fans in awe in this one. Hardcore, highflying action in this one. It went back and forth at the start. Showtime, then put together a powerbomb, hangs onto the legs and falls back, and slingshots Drunk into the top turnbuckle head first. Showtime makes it to the top...shooting star press! Showtime drags Drunk up and Showtime climbs to the top, then drags Drunk up, Drunk rakes Showtimes eyes, Drunk spins Showtime around, then sends him threw a table on the outside, with the ghetto stomp from the top rope. A piece of table is drapping over Showtimes head, Drunk jumps off the top...Joy Killer! Drunk rolls Showtime back into thr ring . Drunk grabs a chair and throws it into the ring. Drunk rolls back into the ring. Drunk picks Showtime up, Showtime bounces off the ropes and sends Drunk down with a clothesline. Showtime makes his way to the top. Drunk lands next to the chair and picks it up, Drunk stands up and turns around. Showtime jumps off the top and sends the chair into Drunks face with the Movie Cut! Showtime picks the chair up and unfolds it, then sits it on the mat. Showtime picks Drunk up, then sams the back of Drunk's head into the seat of the chair with 5 Star Production! Showtime keeps Drunk down for the pin. Drunk kicks out at 2 1/2. Showtime picks Drunk up and whips him into the corner. Showtime picks Drunk up and sets him on the top turnbuckle. Drunk pokes Showtime in the eye, sets him up...EXPLODING KEGGAR! Drunkaholic gets the 1, 2, 3!Main EventSting and X-PacvsMad Stoner and NatedoggThe match was better than people thought. Nate and X-Pac start things out, and Nate held his own forcing X-Pac to tag in Sting. Nate got a few moves off, but the tide turned Stings way. Sting and X-Pac had two tags, while beating on Nate. Nate somehow escaped and tagged in Stoner. Stoner is in a nailed Sting with a few moves, but X-Pac came into the ring a hit Stoner in the knee from behind. Putting them back in charge, Stoner recieved a worse beating than Nate. Both X-Pac and Sting are in the ring, and they pull Stoner to his feet. Stoner bashes both of their heads together. He hooks both X-Pac's and Sting's heads. He brings Sting down with a DDT and X-Pac with a neckbreaker. Stoner crawls to his corner where Nate has his hand out. Ringmaster climbs over the guardrail with a chair in hand, and hits Nate in the back. Nate falls off the apron and Ringmaster starts beating Nate with it. Stoner starts pulling himself up in the corner when X-Pac flies in and drives his face into the bottom turnbuckle with the Broncobuster! X-Pac picks Stoner up and shoves him. Stoner stumbles right into the SCORPION DEATH DROP! Sting gets the 1, 2, 3!The houseshow ends.
