IWA’s Survivor Series (4/18/1999) — Part 1 of 2

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Larry Zbyszko: Welcome to Survivor Series! Tonight, on April 18th, in San Antonio, Texas at the Alamodome, we're live! With me is my partner in crime, Michael Cole. Michael Cole: Thanks Larry. Well, with the rapdown tonight, we've got 4 titles on the line. You've got Mankind and Mad Stoner for the Hardcore. You've got Mr. Fro and DoggFather for the Intercontinental. For the Tag Team Titles, Mr. Fro and Mad Stoner team up against Triple H and Steel of DX. And in your main event, it's the Dungeon of Doom vs. Degeneration-X for the Stables Titles.Larry Zbyszko: That match has to be contraversial. Rumors have been flying about who is going to stab the DOD in the back. For all we know, it could be DoggFather or Paul Wight or even Sting.Michael Cole: Well there's only one way to find out. And that's by getting started, so here we go. Mr. Fro Chuck Brown and Mad Stoner vs. Triple H and Steelfor the VACANT Tag Team TitlesDavid Penzer: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is scheduled for onefall for the IWA Tag Team Titles. First coming to the ring, weighing in at a total of 540 pounds, accompanied to the ring by Lucy, the Sex Machine, Sex, Drugs, and Rock and Roll. And their opponents now headed down to the ring, weighing in at a total of 535 pounds, representing Degeneration-X, Steel and Triple H.Triple H starts it off with Mad Stoner. Triple H points up to his hand to start off with a game of mercy. Mad Stoner falls for it and Triple H delivers a poke to the eye. Then Triple H cracks Mad Stoner in the face with a knee lift. Triple H pulls Mad Stoner up to his feet and then whips him into the ropes. Triple H runs over Mad Stoner with a clothesline. Triple H walks over to his corner and enters the fresh man, Steel. Steel grabs Mad Stoner up and drops him back down with an inverted atomic drop. Steel then grabs Mad Stoner from behind and attempts a reverse DDT. Steel goes to pick Stoner up and Mad Stoner flips himself over. Steel turns around and receives a kick to the gut by Mad Stoner. Stoner grabs Steel and powerbombs him down to the mat. Mad Stoner tags in Mr. Fro. Mr. Fro climbs to the top turnbuckle after the tag and jumps off, delivering a splash onto Steel. Mr. Fro hooks Steel's leg for a cover. 1...2...Steel kicks out. Mr. Fro pulls Steel up and whips him into the ropes. Mr. Fro jumps over Steel when he bounces back. Steel bounces off the ropes once again and this time delivers a clothesline to Mr. Fro. Steel grabs onto Mr. Fro and tags in his partner, Triple H. Triple H wraps Mr. Fro's arms around the ropes at the turnbuckle. Triple H throws a few knife edge chops at Mr. Fro. Mr. Fro breaks free from the corner, by making Steel let go after a forearm shot. Mr. Fro grabs Triple H and throws him into the corner. Mr. Fro sits him on the top rope and climbs up there with him. Mr. Fro delivers a huracanrana to Triple H. Fro goes for a cover. 1...2...Steel stomps on Mr. Fro for the save. Steel pulls Mr. Fro up. Steel and Triple H whip Mr. Fro into the ropes. They deliver a double clothesline. Triple H steps out of the ring without officially tagging in his partner. Steel whips Mr. Fro into the ropes. Mr. Fro ducks under Steel's clothesline attempt and kicks Steel in the gut. Mr. Fro scoop slams Steel and crawls to the corner to tag in Mad Stoner. Mad Stoner runs directly at Steel and takes him down with a cross body block. Mad Stoner hooks onto Steel's leg for a cover. 1...2...Triple H kicks on Mad Stoner saving his partner. Steel pulls Stoner up and kicks him in the gut. Triple H runs and delivers a lariot to Mr. Fro who was standing at his corner. Steel drops Mad Stoner with the Devastator. Steel signals toward the crowd for the end. Then, all of a sudden, the lights in the arena go black. Several people in the audience turn on lighters so you can still see a little bit of the ring. What you can barely see is a man with the figure of Sting beating up on Steel and Triple H. Sting Scorpion Death Drops both men and puts Triple H in the Scorpion Death Lock. Then the man dissapears as the lights turn back on. The referee sees Mad Stoner laying on top of Triple H. The referee makes the count. 1...2...3 Winners and NEW IWA Tag Team Champions: Mr. Fro Chuck Brown and Mad StonerLarry Zbyszko: Triple H and Steel were cheated out of those belts. Michael Cole: They had them. Steel was about to finish them and Sting has come down. Larry Zbyszko: Well, I think that pretty much takes Sting out of probability of being that certain someone who chooses to stab DOD in the back.Michael Cole: Let's send the fans to the ring with David Penzer. Freebird Michael Hayes vs. Owen HartDavid Penzer: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is scheduled for onefall. First coming to the ring, representing Degeneration-X, weighing in at 330 pounds and standing in at 6'6" tall, the master of the Iron Claw from Badd Street, USA, accompanied to the ring by Sexy Sabrina, Freebird Michael Hayes. And his opponent, weighing in at 225 pounds and standing at 5'11" tall, the master of the Sharpshooter from Calgary, Alberta, Canada, accompanied to the ring by Debra McMichael, Owen Hart.Freebird Michael Hayes and Owen Hart tie up. Freebird hip tosses Hart over Then Freebird applies a chin lock. Freebird pulls back on Owen Hart's arms. Owen uses his leg strength to pull himself up. Owen kicks back and delivers a low blow to Freebird's groin. Freebird falls down to the mat. Owen Hart goes down to Freebird's feet and attempts a SHARPSHOOTER!!! Freebird Michael Hayes kicks Owen Hart away from his feet. Freebird gets up and whips Owen Hart into the ropes. Hart bounces back and receives a clothesline. Freebird lays on top of Hart and hooks the leg for a cover 1...2...Owen Hart kicks out. Freebird gets up and lays a leg drop across Hart's neck. Freebird pulls Hart up and delivers an armlock to Owen Hart. Owen Hart throws an elbow to Freebird's face. Then Hart throws another kick to Freebird's groin. Sexy Sabrina steps on the mat trying to point out to the referee about the low blow. Debra McMichael comes over and pulls Sexy Sabrina off the ring apron. The two women get up in eachother's face. Sexy Sabrina gets the shirt ripped off of her. She's not even wearing a bra! The referee stares at her along with Owen Hart. Freebird Michael Hayes rolls out the ring and grabs a chair out from under it. He rolls back in the ring and hits Owen Hart across the back. Sabrina points in the ring at Freebird. The referee turns around seeing Freebird Michael Hayes having the IRON CLAW!!! on Owen Hart. Eventually Owen Hart taps out.Winner by submission: Freebird Michael HayesLarry Zbyszko: Show me a replay of that Sexy Sabrina. I would most definitely like to see that again.Michael Cole: No doubt. If this is gonna happen all the time, I'd do it for free. Larry Zbyszko: Well, hopefully we'll see more. But we've got to go back to the ring for this next matchup.Mankind vs. Mad Stoner for the VACANT Hardcore Title David Penzer: This match is scheduled for one fall for the Hardcore Title. First coming to the ring standing in at 6'7" tall and weighing 285 pounds, representing the Dungeon of Doom from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, the master of the Mandible Claw, Mankind. And his opponent, standing in at 6'8" tall and weighing in at 330 pounds, from Milwaukee, Wisconsin, the master of Your Cashed, Mad Stoner. While coming down to the ring, Mankind rolls a dumpster full of weapons to use. Mankind sets the dumpster across the announce booth ringside. Mankind pulls out a table and puts in inside the ring. Mankind rolls himself in the ring, only to be immediately stomped by Mad Stoner. The referee calls for the bell to signal the beginning of the match. Mad Stoner pulls Mankind up and whips him into the ropes. Mankind bounces back and Mad Stoner delivers a spinebuster. Mad Stoner hangs onto Mankinds arm and puts him in an armbar submission. Mankind reaches the ropes and the referee tells Mad Stoner to let go. Mad Stoner pulls Manking up and whips him into the ropes again. Mad Stoner telegraphs it. Mankind takes advantage of it and delivers a double arm DDT to Mad Stoner. Mankind waves his arms to the crowd for fan support. Mankind bounces off each rope and then drops the Socko Elbow on Mad Stoner. Mankind lays the table up in the corner of the ring. Mankind pulls Mad Stoner up and throws him into the table laid out in the corner. Mankind goes outside of the ring and jumps into his dumpster full of weapons. Mad Stoner goes after him. Mad Stoner makes it into the dumpster. Mankind picks up a VCR and breaks it across Mad Stoner's head. Stoner falls out of the dumpster. Mankind looks through the dumpster for another weapon and he pulls out a hockey stick. Mankind jumps out of the dumpster and whacks Mad Stoner with the hockey stick with all the momentum of falling. Stoner grabs his back in pain. Mankind grabs Stoner up and throws his shoulder into the steel steps. Mankind pulls Stoner up and attempts a suplex. Mad Stoner blocks the attempt. Mankind attempts it again but Mad Stoner makes another block. Mad Stoner suplexes Mankind on the outside. Mad Stoner looks under the ring and pulls out a steel chair. Mad Stoner whacks Mankind across the back with it. Stoner looks under the ring again and finds a basketball rack. He picks it up and throws it down on Mankind. Mad Stoner pulls Mankind up and smashes his face into the announcing table. Mad Stoner rolls him into the ring and then follows Mankind. Mad Stoner pulls Mankind up and whips him into the ropes. Mankind goes under and Mad Stoner clothesline attempts. Then Mankind kicks Stoner in the gut. Mankind bounces off the ropes and delivers a spinning neckbreaker to Stoner. Mankind hooks Stoner's leg for the cover. 1...2...Mad Stoner kicks out. Mankind pulls Stoner up and whips him into the ropes. Mankind clotheslines Mad Stoner. Mankind pulls Mad Stoner up and attempts to throw him into the corner. Mad Stoner reverses it and sends Mankind into the turnbuckles. Mad Stoner runs back and delivers the Steamroller to Mankind. Mad Stoner signals to the crowd for the finish. Mad Stoner throws Mankind into the ropes and telegraphs YOUR CASHED!!! Mankind hops over Mad Stoner and drops him with a Rocker Dropper. Mankind pulls out Mr. Socko! Mankind puts Mr. Socko on his hand and applies it to Mad Stoner's mouth. Mad Stoner's sholders fall to the mat. The referee makes a count. 1...2...3 Winner and NEW IWA Hardcore Champion: Mankind Larry Zbyszko: What a hell of a match! Mankind showed Mad Stoner who is really hardcore. Michael Cole: Well, these titles deserve who deserves them the most, and Mankind proves to be the most hardcore man in the IWA. Larry Zbyszko: Now to you fans, I'm going to send you to the ring with David Penzer for our next matchup.
