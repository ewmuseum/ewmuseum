GEWF

15 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello. My name is Austin, the president of the GEWF. I am only one of the presidents. The other president is my brother, Nathan. Okay. Enough of that crap. The GEWF is a new wrestling federation. The league is not simulated. It is ran by roleplays meaning the better roleplayer you are, the better the chance of winning. If you want to sign up, go to http://members.tripod.com/~WIWFederation/gewf2.com ... Maybe you don't want to be a wrestler in the GEWF and just want to be a manager or something. If you want to be a manager, just fill out the manager part of application. Okay...Say you want to write cards and updates or be a ring announcer or something. You will have to email for that. My email address it [detail withheld] Here is what the GEWF has. The GEWF has... an IRC server for live interviews. 3 single belts, 2 tag team, 1 royal rumble belt, and 1 survivor series. 3 cards a week-Sunday, Tuesday, and Friday. on of the wildest wrestling federation in the history of e-wrestling. We have ladies taking off their clothes, wrestlers hitting their opponents with cars and a lot more. Slammy Awards e.g. Best Roleplaying, Diamond Debuts, ect.. any match you make up. Say you want a car hitting contest match. You and your opponent will get in cars and ram into eachother trying to hurt eachother. The GEWF allows you to draw your wrestler and manager and scan them so I can send them out in email. If you don't have a scanner or can't get use of one, you may ask me to draw him since I am a pretty good artist. In the application that I mentioned a while ago, it asks you for you wrestlers looks. If you ask me to draw your wrestler and you don't have a detailed description, then I won't be able to draw you. Say that you put "big muscles" in the `wrestler's description' then I can't draw you. If you have any questions, email me at [detail withheld]
