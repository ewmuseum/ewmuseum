HOW: Two Years and Still Going

14 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all: After two years of simming out matches, High Octane Wrestling has gone booked. We are on temporary hiatus until we open up our doors again on
April 1st. The reason? Regrouping our priorities. Will we be the best? Probably not. Will we get recognition? Probably not. But we have always PROMISED on-time cards...and have! So check us out at
http://www.angelfire.com/mo/highoctane The rules are simple: 1.RPing determines your win/loss record based on Human Judgement System. 2.Two singles per handler, or one single and a tag team; which may be split if the angle developes so. So no lose.
3.Stay within eticit, ie, no racial slurs, use of other handler's characters without permission, etc.
4.Have Fun! 5.Two cards a week: Thursday Thrash and a Monday touring house show breakdown type thing. And of course, a supercard once a month.
6.Don't get discouraged. If you have a complaint, take it up with the
Committee. Flash sheets are sent out on a BCC format so there is no spamming or arguing among the entire fed. And be patient. Once we open again on the first, we promise not to let ANY of those of you in HOW down!
7.It's that simple!
