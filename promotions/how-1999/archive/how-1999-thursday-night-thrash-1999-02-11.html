HOW Thursday Night Thrash

11 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi,I just thought that I would introduce you all to a sample night in High
Octane Wrestling.I might make this a regular thing.I hope to show you the diversity in the fed,even though it is simulated.I hope any free agents reading this will like the look of this and take a look at joining.The ruminations are my own work.
High Octane Wrestling Thursday Night Thrash Results Non-Title Match Black Widow(Badass Champion) def. Razor via falling splash Mala Mala/Shakedown def. Jean-Guy Lemieux and Les Russell by DQ Cremator def. Atlas by DQ Non Title Match Bronco Buster Baker/Das Traumer def. Sgt. Nat Turner/Taryn
St. Archer(World Tag Champions) by DQ (Baker/Traumer get title shot next
Thursday Night)
Fire def. Josh Ballance via the Fireball Cruiserweight title match Scorpion(Cruiserweight Champion) def. H-Bomb by submission Brothers in Arms(Demonspawn/The Zealot) def. Team Bushido(Akira Shingen/Jason
Grey) via bulldog (The Annihilator challenged Demonspawn to an Ultimate
Survival Death Match next Thursday,which was accepted) World Title Match Fatuma(World Champion) def. Baron Blob by DQ Main Event:Grand Prix Tournament Match:Bodybag Match(4 Corners-No DQ-No Countout-Elimination) Chris Berus def. Bobby G.,Tennesee Twister,and Walker Quinn (Chris Berus moves into semi-final against Holdup to take place next Thursday) Ruminations Wow.Five DQ's in one night.The referees were the strictest they have ever been in the history of this fed.But still there was a great double main event.Baron Blob was never in it when his manager,Duke Donald interfered.Chris Berus and Walker Quinn fought it out for 25 minutes before
Chris finally stuffed Walker into the bag.He looks to be the favourite to get the Grand Prix title shot at Pure Octane against the Annihilator.Next card is on Saturday with a warm-up for the semi-final matchup,when Holdup and Berus get it on,plus another tournament first round match,with a former GP champ,Duke O'Brien.See you there.
