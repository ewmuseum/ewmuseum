Press Conference with President Richter

10 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Scene opens to the Ball Room of All American Towers in Baltimore Maryland...There are Hundreds of Reporters on hand from all of the major Television and Radio Stations for this Major announcement from the Owner/C.E.O of All American Productions and the AIWA, Darren Richter...The Reporters are finally asked to take their seats as Conrad Alexander steps up to the podium....] Conrad Alexander: Ladies and Gentlemen, thank you for joining us this morning Live here within All AMerican Towers...As many have speculated, the Owner and Chief Executive Officer of ALl American Productions will be making a major announcement that will not only change the AIWA, but the entire wrestling world forever... Ladies and Gentlemen, The Owner of American Internet Wrestling Alliance...Darren "D-Rock" Richter!! [The reporters begin to jump to their feet as Richter climbs the steps of the stage and makes his way to the podium and once again the reporters are asked to take their seats and to give their undivided attention.] President Ricther: Ladies and Gentlemen, thank you for joining us this morning for this momentous occassion. When I started this company...All American Productions, I had no idea that it would have the great success that it has...We started off as a very small promotion, and struggled for a long time, and actually on two occassions, I closed the doors on this idea, with no intentions on ever returning... As many people know, things change in this business all the time...YOu come across people that are very unappreciative of how you do things or what it is exactly you are doing...and, then there are those people that you become life long friends with...those that have shown time and again that they are not only loyal employees, but loyal friends as well... Now, as the AIWA has re-opened it's doors for the third time, things have become greater than I could have ever possibly immagined...and that, is due in large part to the people that had faith in my ideas and more importantly, All American Productions. Today it is my great pleasure to announce both the end, and a New Beginning... As of Four P.M., Yesterday Afternoon, All American Productions made two major business deals all in one day... Firstly, ALl American Productions did purchase the World Wrestling ALliance, and the contracts of all of the WWA's Wrestling Talent... This opens the door for great opportunities within All American Productions, and will bring some great young talent to an already great Roster of Wrestling Superstars...And will open up the market in other area's and opportunites in the World of Wrestling. And Last, but certainly not least...All American Productions has made a business decision that I think will only benefit our employee's, and most importantly, our fans throughout the world... All American Productions has come to an agreement for a business merger with a group of Investors based in New York City, and has purchased the rights to Television Broadcasting Giant, The "World Sports Network". With this Business merger comes Many great things that will benefit All American Productions and the AIWA...Firstly, both of our major shows, "Thursday T.N.T" and "Sunday Night High Impact" Will now be aired on the "World Stage", meaning that our television program will be broadcast not only within the United States, but a total of Fifteen Countries that span the globe, including Japan...which will open up the door for many of the great superstars of the organization to go on tour overseas. The One Significant change that will be made, is that the name "AIWA" or American Interent Wrestling Alliance will be no more as the Wrestling Promotion will now be called, The "WIW", Or "World Internet Wrestling". Everything about the Company will remain the same, except for the fact that we will now have more abundant business opportunites throughout the World, which of course will only bring more revenue, and allow All American Productions to bring in some of the top talent in the Professional Wrestling Industry, plus raise the monetary value of each and every Wrestler who is under Contract with the Company. So, as everyone can see, this is only a plus for All American Productions, and we are very excited about this great opportunity for both our employee's and our fans. This Thursday Night Will mark a new beginning for All American Productions, as World Internet Wrestling will make it's world Wide Debut with Thursday TNT being broadcast to fifteen countries around the world. Ladies and Gentlemen, I Thank you for joining us this morning, and I can guarantee that this will be the beginning of something special in the Wrestling World. Thank you for your time... [President Richter leaves the Podium, and shakes hands with several of the members of the WIW Executive Committee, and then leaves the room as the scene fades out]
