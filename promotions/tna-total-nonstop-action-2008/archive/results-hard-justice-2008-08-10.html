Christian Cage ducks it though and both men STILL continue to run up the ropes as they both have got to be getting tired. But this is what professional wrestling is all about. Here we go, both men are back in the middle of the ring, running at each other, they both go for clotheslines AND double clothesline!
Both men are down on the ring mat just laying there motionless as the fans watch on, wondering what more could go on in this championship last man standing match. Either way, both men's shoulders are down so the referee has no choice but to start the last man standing count!
