TNA iMPACT! 07-31-08 TNA iMPACT! July 31st, 2008 Universal Studio's Orlando, Florida Introduction As the fireworks stop and the fans settle in, TNA iMPACT! comes to you live from the sold out Universal Studio's arena in Orlando, Florida. The card has been set and things are about to get under way but before any other action goes down we head on over to the broadcast table with both Don West and Mike Tenay.
Mike Tenay: "Hello everyone and welcome to iMPACT! I'm Mike Tenay and beside me is Don West."
Don West: "Thank you, Mike. There are some HUGE match ups tonight!" Mike Tenay: "There sure is, Don! I can't wait to see what the night has in store!" Don West: "Major match ups that will definitely help ignite the flame for our pay-per-view!"
The action turns towards the ring side where Dave Penzer holds a microphone in hand and is ready to announce the opening bout.
TNA Singles Match Stone Cold Steve Austin vs Triple H Dave Penzer: "The following contest is scheduled for one fall." He pauses. Dave Penzer: "Introducing first, from Victoria, Texas, weighing in at 252 pounds, Stone Cold Steve Austin!"
"Glass Shatters" By Disturbed hit's as Stone Cold Steve Austin makes his way out. It's not him though, it's ... SHARK BOY DRESSED UP LIKE HIM!
Dave Penzer: "And his opponent, from Greenwich, Conneticut, weighing in at 265 pounds, Triple H!"
"Time To Play The Game" By Motorhead plays as Triple H makes his way out. Unfortunately, that's not him either, it's ... CURRY MAN DRESSED UP LIKE HIM!
Mike Tenay: "What the hell is going on, Don?" Don West: "Seems to me, Dixie Carter had a weird idea tonight for a match." Mike Tenay: "Mocking other superstars who aren't even on the roster." Don West: "It's priceless ain't it? The bell sounds. Both Shark Boy and Curry Man who are dressed up like WWE wrestlers Stone Cold Steve Austin and Triple H tie up. Curry Man has the advantage as he takes Shark Boy down to the ring mat with a headlock take down. Shark Boy gets up though and pushes him into the ropes.
Curry Man comes back and ducks a back elbow shot. Shark Boy then runs at him and hit's him with a running high knee to the face like Triple H does. He quickly gets on top of his opponent to make a pin attempt as the referee gets down on the ring mat and makes the count.
1.. 2.. Only a small two count though as the fans are loving this action! The match continues though as Shark Boy now lifts up Curry Man and looks to hit the Pedigree. A maneuver that Hunter Hearst Helmsley himself uses. Curry Man however reverses it though and goes for the Stone Cold Stunner, something Steve Austin uses. That doesn't happen either though!
Don West: "This is messed..." Mike Tenay: "I'd say, Don, I'd say!" All of a sudden the music of TNA World Heavyweight Champion, Christian Cage comes on as him and Tomko make their way out from the tunnel and down into the ring. They both attack Curry Man and Shark Boy as the referee calls for the bell. Double disqualification as they toss them to the outside floor and listen to the fans boo them!
Dave Penzer: "This match has been ruled as a result of a double disqualification and is a ... DRAW!"
Result: No Contest Draw via Double Disqualification Making A Statement Christian Cage and Tomko stand in the middle of the six sided ring after what they had done to the two men wrestling previously before with microphones in their hands. It seems they have something on their mind!
Christian Cage: "So I get here tonight and as soon as I do, I find out who I'm teaming with here tonight."
The fans begin to chant Sting. Christian Cage: "Yeah, that's right, it's him." He pauses. Christian Cage: "I have to team with the same man I am facing at Hard Justice for MY TNA World Heavyweight Championship."
Tomko has his arms folded against his chest and shakes his head as Cage continues... Christian Cage: "I just want to say one thing..." He looks out to the fans. Christian Cage: "How the HELL am I going to be able to trust him? I mean... this is the man I have to face for my title, and Jim Cornette decides to team me up with him to take on the Pittsburgh boys?"
The fans begin to chant Cage Sucks. Christian Cage: "Shut up!!!" Tomko tries to silence the crowd, he then takes the microphone and begins to talk. Tomko: "Respect this man as he is YOUR champion. And respect me, as I just got back from Japan from beating the great KENTA!"
Fans boo even louder as Cage gets back on the mic. Christian Cage: "Jimbo, just know one thing... You can screw with my head all you want, BUT YOU'RE NOT GOING TO TAKE AWAY THIS WORLD TITLE!"
He smirks... Christian Cage: "Cause THAT'S .. HOW I ROLL!" Christian drops the microphone as him and Tomko make their way to the back. TNA Singles Match Angelina Love & Velvet Skye vs Kelly Kelly & Stephanie McMahon Dave Penzer: "The following contest is scheduled for one fall." He pauses. Dave Penzer: "Introducing first, the team of Angelina Love and Velvet Skye. I give to you... The Beautiful People!"
Both Angelina and Velvet make their way out to the ring wearing sexy ring attire as the fans cheer for them only because their HOT!
Dave Penzer: "And their opponents, the team of Kelly Kelly and Stephanie McMahon!" Kelly Kelly comes walking out first. She is followed by Stephanie McMahon. Together they walk to the ring!
Don West: "TNA Knockout action, Mike!" Mike Tenay: "These four women will be apart of a battle royal at the pay-per-view to determine a #1 contender for the TNA Women's Knockout Championship!"
The bell sounds. Angelina Love starts the match by attacking Kelly Kelly right away during the pre match talk with Stephanie. Love with a right hand and Kelly returns with one of her own. They exchange right hands until Kelly gets in a few elbows. Kelly with an elbow to Love and kick to the face from the corner.
Kelly takes down Love and then chops her. Love with a clothesline in the corner then tags in Velvet Sky. Sky slams Kelly down on the ring a few times. Kelly tries to fight back with a few shots, but Sky grounds her again with a big clothesline. Kelly blocks a kick holding Sky's leg. Sky flips forward to get out of it. Rake of the eyes by Sky on Kelly followed by a big kick to the head.
Cover by Love... 1..............,2...........Got the Shoulder Up!!!!!!!!!! Sky covers Kelly a couple more times but still cant get the pin. Sky misses a clothesline in the corner and Kelly manages to get to her corner were she goes to tag in Stephanie but she drops down off the apron and is walking up the ramp.
Don West: "What is Skye doing, Mike?" Mike Tenay: "Dont tell me she is going to make Kelly Kelly fend for herself for the rest of the match."
Back to the action, Sky goes for the tag to Velvet and she gets it. Yes they call this the Beautiful Destroyer. Well they hit it and heres the pin.
1.............,2..................,3................ Dave Penzer: "Here are your winners, Angelina Love & Velvet Skye, The Beautiful People!" Result: Angelina Love & Velvet Sky def. Kelly Kelly & Stephanie McMaohn via Pinfall World X Cup Returning Featuring: -Team Cage -Team Douglas -Team Sting -Team Styles - & More! TNA Eight Man Elimination Tag Team Match Aj Styles, Jay Lethal, Rob Van Dam, Samoa Joe vs Andrew Martin, Bobby Lashley, Brother Ray, Matt Morgan
Dave Penzer: "The following contest is scheduled for one fall!" He pauses. Dave Penzer: "Introducing first, from Tampa, Florida, weighing in at 285 pounds, Annndddrreew Maarttiinn!"
Andrew Martin makes his way out from the tunnel and receives boo's from the fans. He is quite angry at that.
Dave Penzer: "And his opponent, from Orange County, California, weighing in at 280 pounds, The Samoan Submission Machine... Saaaammmmoooaaa Joooeee!"
That annoying music plays and the fans are unsure of what to think. Either way, Samoa Joe makes his way to the ring.
Mike Tenay: "Two new stars on TNA iMPACT!" Don West: "That's true, Mike. But who will get the job done?" The bell sounds. The match starts out with Jay Lethal from team 1 and Andrew Martin from team 2. Both men tie up. Andrew Martin takes the advantage as he pushes Jay Lethal back into a corner. The referee tells him to back off so he does. Jay then comes running at him but is back elbowed in the face and down to the ring mat. Martin tags in Matt Morgan now as he comes in and goes for a huge elbow drop. Lethal however moves out of the way and then tags in Aj Styles.
Aj comes in and grabs Matt. Morgan shoves him into the ropes though and Styles comes back and is then hit with a side walk slam. Morgan tags in Bobby Lashley who quickly gets on top of Aj Styles and goes for a quick pin. He can't even get a count though because Styles leg is on the ropes already. Bobby then picks him up and lifts him up over his shoulders. Styles slides off and then tags in Rob Van Dam.
RVD comes flying off the top ropes with a kick to the chest of Bobby Lashley that sends him down. RVD now runs to the ropes, comes back and hit's the rolling thunder! Rob now tags in Samoa Joe who steps inside the ring and grabs a hold of Bobby. He whips him into his corner and Lashley is tagged out by Brother Ray. Ray comes in and boots the mid section of Joe.
Mike Tenay: "This match is good." Brother Ray then tries to whip him into the corner but Samoa Joe reverses it and then runs in with a shining wizard kick to his face. More of a washboard attack as the fans scream 'OUUUU' aloud as they all know that had to hurt. Ray falls to the mat as Martin reaches over and tags himself back into the match. He runs at Joe with a big boot but Joe ducks it. He then tags in Rob Van Dam who comes in and hit's Andrew Martin with a superkick.
Don West: "Team Styles wins!" RVD tags in Jay Lethal. He comes jumping off the top ropes with an elbow drop! He makes the cover!
1... 2... 3!!! Dave Penzer: "Here are your winners, the team of Aj Styles, Jay Lethal, Rob Van Dam and Samoa Joe!"
Result: Aj Styles, Jay Lethal, Rob Van Dam, Samoa Joe def. Andrew Martin, Bobby Lashley, Brother Ray, & Matt Morgan via Elimination
Hard Justice Featuring: Live! on PAY-PER-VIEW Sunday, August 10th, 2008 Sovereign Banks Arena Trenton, New Jersey Get your tickets now! TNA Singles Match Jim Cornette vs John Cena Dave Penzer: "The following contest is scheduled for one fall!" He pauses. Dave Penzer: "Introducing first, from Tampa, Florida, Jim Cornette!" TNA Management Director Jim Cornette makes his way out from the back to the ring. The fans cheer as he will be wrestling tonight!
Dave Penzer: "And his opponent, from West Newbury, Massachussetts, weighing in at 240 pounds, Joooohhhnnnn Ceeennnnaaaa!"
The music of John Cena's plays and the fans actually boo him! He doesn't care though as he gives them all dirty looks on his way to the ring!
Mike Tenay: "Can you believe it? Jimbo wrestling?" Don West: "It's true, Mike. I don't know what to think haha." The bell sounds. The match up starts off with Jim Cornette in the corner. John Cena is in the other corner, trying to get the fans going, but it's not working! They seem to be booing him as Jim Cornette comes walking out from the corner. John Cena points at Cornette and laughs. Jimbo doesn't look to happy about this as he stomps on John Cena's foot. John doesn't even flinch as he starts laughing at him.
John Cena now grabs a hold of Jim Cornette and throws him into the ropes. Jim stops and grabs the ropes. He tells Cena to come on! John follows him as Jimbo escapes from the ring. He starts to walking around the ring as John follows him. Cornette then begins running as does Cena who catches him. He tosses him back inside of the ring. Jim gets up on his own two feet as Cena rolls in under the bottom rope.
Don West: "This is weird." Cornette begins stomping down on Cena's back as the fans cheer for him. Jim then gets on top of him and starts decking him with right hands. The referee tells him to stop so Jimbo does. He gets off of John Cena who seems pissed as he stands up and decks Cornette with a right hand. Jim is in the corner now as Cena runs at him. Jimbo moves but Cena stops himself before going mid section into the turnbuckle.
The match continues as Jim tries to crawl away. John Cena turns around and walks behind him, stalking his prey. John then lifts him up and whips him into the ropes again. Cornette comes back and Cena runs at him with a clothesline that knocks him down! John now pumps it up with his shoes and looks to end it as he signals for the end. He grabs Cornette and lifts him up on his shoulders. Jim however has a suit on and slips off Cena's sweaty shoulders.
He grabs him from behind with a school boy pin! 1...2...3!!! It's all over! Jim Cornette beat John Cena! Mike Tenay: "Oh ... my!" Dave Penzer: "Here is your winner, Jim Cornette!" Result: Jim Cornette def. John Cena via Pinfall TNA Today Backstage after defeating John Cena is Jim Cornette the TNA Management Director. Jeremy Borash: "Mr. Cornette, you just defeated John Cena in your first wrestling match, what do you -"
Before he could say anymore, Jimbo cut him off. Jim Cornette: "That was great wasn't it?" He huffs and puffs. Jeremy Borash: "Well I..." Jim continues... Jim Cornette: "And just for your information, that wasn't my first match, Cena!" He pauses. Jim Cornette: "But you want to run your mouth, well, I shut you up, didn't I?" Jim shouts 'WOOOOOOOO' aloud as he seems quite happy. Jeremy Borash: "Well then... how bout that." He gives the camera a weird look. Jeremy Borash: "Back to ring side!" TNA No Disqualification Tag Team Grudge Match Christian Cage & Sting vs Kurt Angle & Shane Douglas Dave Penzer: "The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is a no disqualification tag team grudge match!"
He pauses. Dave Penzer: "Introducing first, the team of Christian Cage and Sting!" Christian Cage comes out last as Sting makes his way out from the other tunnel and Cage makes his way out from the tunnel he's not coming through.
Dave Penzer: "And their opponents. The team of Kurt Angle and Shane Douglas!" Together Kurt Angle and Shane Douglas walk down from the back and begin to talk strategy. Both men will face off at Hard Justice.
Mike Tenay: "This match is going to be weird." Don West: "Yeah, no kidding. All four men face their opponents at Hard Justice but have to co-exist with them tonight!"
The bell sounds. Cage and Sting stand on the same side of things as both men watch each other closely as they will be going one on one August 10th for Hard Justice to determine the TNA World Heavyweight Champion. Kurt and Shane, two buddies from Pittsburgh, will go one on one at Hard Justice as well. But tonight, a tag team no disqualification match will go down. All four men looking to proove something. And now it looks like Cage and Angle will start this match off for the fans.
They tie up. Angle goes low at Cage's legs as Christian tries to push him off. Angle hangs on tight though and just as Cage is taken down to the ring mat, he tags in his partner Sting. Sting steps in and boots the back side of Kurt Angle as Kurt let's go of Cage. Christian rolls out of the ring now as Sting picks up Angle and body slams him down and across the ring mat. Sting runs to the ropes, Angle quickly gets up and back elbows him right in the mouth!
Mike Tenay: "What a quick come back!" The match continues as Kurt Angle walks over and tags in Shane Douglas. He steps inside and grabs a hold of Sting. He picks him up and then whips him into the corner. Slowly but surely he walks over and hits him in the chest area with a few open hand chops. Sting however has taken enough and fires right back. The fans are loving it, cheering and getting right into it. That is until Douglas knees him in the mid section to slow him down.
Shane now tags back in Kurt who comes in and hit's a European uppercut on Sting. He falls down as Angle stands over top of him and points to Cage, Christian doesn't even seem to care though, as he claps, praising for them to beat on his opponent for this Sunday. Kurt now grabs his body and lifts him up. He wraps his arms around his waist and points to Cage as he then slams Sting down to the mat with a belly to belly suplex.
He hooks his leg for the cover. 1... 2... Only a two count! Sting got his shoulder up as the referee signals there was just a two count. Don West: "It sure does seem like a 3 on 1 match, Mike." Kurt now has Sting down in a arm bar submission maneuver as the crowd chants for Sting who starts to come alive thanks to the roaring fans. The momentum builds and builds as Sting gets up to his feet. Back elbows to the mid section of Kurt and he let's go of the hold. Sting now runs to the ropes, comes back and fires Angle down with a clothesline. Shane Douglas gets in now, he comes at Sting who tosses him up and over the top ropes!
The fans are going wild as Sting comes over to his corner and looks to tag in Christian Cage. However, Cage isn't there!
Don West: "Where's Cage, Mike?" Mike Tenay: "He's running away!" Christian is walking up the ramp way towards the tunnel as Sting leans on the ropes and points to him, telling him at Hard Justice he's done! Sting turns around, both Kurt Angle and Shane Douglas attack him from behind. They now whip him up the ropes. Sting comes back, DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! Shane is up, dropkick on him and he's outside of the ring now! Kurt comes running at Sting again, Sting drop toe holds him down, he puts his leg over his, SCORPION DEATHLOCK!
Angle's got nowhere to go, TAP! TAP! TAP! Dave Penzer: "Here are your winners, the team of, Christian Cage and Sting!" Result: Christian Cage & Sting def. Kurt Angle & Shane Douglas via Submission The TNA logo appears on the television screen as the camera fades. Tune in next week to iMPACT!
