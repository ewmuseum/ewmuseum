Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This wing portrays Total Nonstop Action, the real wrestling company. Total Non Stop Action Wrestling is a fan federation's fiction about that institution, not the museum's claim about the company or the people it names. The federation's own front page calls it an online roleplaying eFed and says it is 'no way shape or form the real TNA'; its news has the company open its doors in Orlando on 1 January 2008; and its rules page of June 2008 admits only real wrestlers, no created ones. Nothing it stages is the real company's history and none of its belts is the real one.

It builds a continuity of its own. Its news opens on Tuesday, 1 January 2008, 'TNA Opens; Superstars Sign', with Jim Cornette put in charge as director of the company, and runs through three weeks of signings of real wrestlers, Hulk Hogan, The Rock, Kurt Angle, Christian Cage, Shane Douglas and Mark Jindrak among them. Its news of 17 January says every TNA championship was on the line on its opening iMPACT! and its news of 24 January that they had been decided; on 14 January it announces a first pay-per-view, Against All Odds, which it stages at the Bi-Lo Center in Greenville on 10 February. Its news stops on 11 February. A front page captured in March and again in June 2008 carries its owner's notice, TNA HAS CLOSED, saying he had tried to rerun it one more time and was moving to help a friend run a WWF Old School fed. By 20 June the news is back with a story that the company had gone bankrupt and was 'ALIVE AND WELL' again under Dixie Carter and Jim Cornette.

The second run holds a weekly iMPACT! at the Universal Studios arena in Orlando and two pay-per-views, Victory Road on 13 July at the Reliant Center in Houston and Hard Justice on 10 August at the Sovereign Bank Arena in Trenton. Its recovered results pages are iMPACT! of 31 July and Hard Justice, where Vince Russo, announcing that TNA has bought out a rival federation, XCW, says he is '50% in charge' beside Jim Cornette, Jeff Hardy wins the vacant X Division title, the Beautiful People win the vacant Women's Knockout title and Christian Cage keeps the World title against Sting with Tomko at his side. A World X Cup starts that month, and No Surrender, on 14 September 2008, is announced.

Most of it is not held: the results of every other show, its news after 21 August 2008, and its biography pages beyond five. A listing of its directory shows picture uploads, among them portraits of later champions and pictures for Halloween Havoc and Turning Point, stamped as late as 24 December 2008, so it ran past the last page recovered. Another TNA fan federation of the same hub, whose news dates TNA's first show to June 2008 after a purchase of World Championship Wrestling, is a different federation with a different story. The two share the company they portray, a hosting hub and one wrestler: the same handler's Mark Jindrak, who signs here in January 2008 and joins the other in June 2008. A wrestler carried between them does not make them one federation, and the name they share joins nothing, so they are kept apart.

This federation’s fiction portrays the TNA.
