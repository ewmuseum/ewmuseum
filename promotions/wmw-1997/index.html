Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Wrestlings Most Wanted introduced itself on 18 August 1997 promising three cards a week plus pay-per-views, a Tripod-hosted homepage, and a title structure of three singles championships, two tag-team championships, and a stable championship. Five days later its own promoter admitted the one thing missing was wrestlers: “the only thing we don't have right now is enough wrestlers...if you have any interest check out the sight and your mind will be made up.”

Through September it kept recruiting on several fronts at once: a vice president to handle light commentator roleplay and help keep the fed running smoothly, a dedicated writer to voice a commentator, and, always, more wrestlers “for good roleplaying.” The same week, an independent fan roundup of the newsgroup's own e-feds listed it by name and URL alongside others, corroborating its existence from outside its own recruitment copy. By 29 September it reported steady growth, still “room for a few more wrestlers,” open to “good roleplayers only.”

Its last surviving documents, both dated 13 October 1997, ran side by side: an ordinary recruitment repeat describing itself as simmed by TNM with mandatory roleplay, and a reflective staff column on the toll of the hobby's own immaturity, aimed at handlers who insulted unpaid staff in public rather than settling disputes “on an e-mail to e-mail, one to one basis.” The column closed by congratulating “all fedheads for either running a fed or trying to run a fed in this immature internet society” and offering to link any fellow fed head's own page.

No document survives after 13 October 1997. No card, roster, or result of its own is in the record.
