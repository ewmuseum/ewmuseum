Fact or Fiction????

13 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi there, I'm sure I'm not the only one who has experienced immaturity in a wrestling federation before. The WMW had alot of problems with teenagers or even older people that feel the need to insult the hard work the staff puts into a fed. All the feds out in internet land are truely great because of the effort we put into it! We don't get paid to do this, we do this because we love the sport and becasue it makes us feel good to run something we can accomplish. Some role players that we accept into a fed ruin it for others by putting the fed down, the staff down and feel the need to take it a level higher than what it should be taken to. I feel the need to write this out to all of you readers, because of the level some people have taken by bringing someones eles accomplishements down. If anyone feels to insult and make people feel bad, you should do it on a e-mail to e-mail, one to one basis! It shouldn't have to involve everyone who really doesn't care. I also want to say, if your a fedhead send me your address and I will link it to my page, I congradulate all fedheads for either running a fed or trying to run a fed in this immature internet society! For those of you who feel the need to slander another fed, they won't be looking at us or anyones eles fed as a crappy thing, they will be looking at you saying how immature and pathetic you are for putting people down in your spear time. Go read a book or something!! I'm sure god gave you a mind to think and not dicrimintate with. Thanks for listening!!!
