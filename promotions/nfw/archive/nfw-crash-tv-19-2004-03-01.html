NFW Crash TV 19 1 Mar 2004 North Charleston Colesium Fair Warning (FADEIN: NFW Headquarters – Boston, Massachusetts. Thursday, 11:30 PM EST. The penthouse floor was quiet, except for the pen strokes being made by NFW President, QUENTIN SULLIVAN. The lone figurehead of New Frontier looked tired, his sleeves rolled up and his glasses gently resting on the middle of his nose. Three rogue conferences, false legal information being fed to the media by Chad Merritt of the CSWA, an audience that had tripled from the start of Season I… At this point, all the chips had been placed on the table. The Ultratitle was at stake, for if it fell into the wrong hands – NFW would certainly lose its rights. But as long as that could be prevented… …From the distance, footsteps could be heard. SULLIVAN looked up with a quizzical expression, certainly not expecting any visitors…) "MISSSSSSSSSSTER SULLIVAN." (SULLIVAN’s eyes slitted for a moment, before returning to his paperwork…) SULLIVAN: "Hello Michael, what gives me the honor?" (In walked, NFW East Spokesman MICHAEL MANSON. Wearing a long black cloak, black pants and shin length black boots. MANSON tossed a videocassette onto the table…) MANSON: "I believe you’ll be happy that OUR portion of the show is complete and satisfactory to you’re liking." SULLIVAN: (not looking up, scribbling) "Whatever." (MANSON paced around the office, inspecting the artwork with a deadpanned expression.) MANSON: "There’s rumours going around that you sold Craig and this place down the river." SULLIVAN: (still ‘working’) "What else is new?" MANSON: (holding up a letter) "I suppose I could bring up what I received from our former boss and owner. It’s quite informative." (SULLIVAN finally put down his pen and leaned back in his chair with an audible sigh. MANSON started nodding, while looking at the artwork…) MANSON: "Impressive smokescreen, I must say. I’ll give him that much…" SULLIVAN: "Is that so?" MANSON: "Nixon being kidnapped, he suspects most people close to him as the perpetrator that paid of Prosser. The UWA gaining underground funding and possibly restarting…" SULLIVAN: "Yeah…real juicy gossip, huh?" (MANSON turned around and eyed SULLIVAN for a moment before leaning his back against the wall…) MANSON: "He’s named you as one of his suspects, that’s quite interesting. How you conveniently knew about Prosser, the UWA Board of Directors still in place…and failed to mention ANY of this to him." (SULLIVAN cocked his head and slightly smiled at the insinuation of MANSON…) SULLIVAN: "Its certainly a twisted web, Mike. I suppose since I had happened to receive something similar that you know that starting your conference went against everything he had wanted." (MANSON smiled briefly, his yellow teeth showing – then his deadpan returned…) MANSON: "A fair warning if I choose to cross him in the future, even though I prefer a crucifixion. But it seems, he’s gonna cover that one already. Besides, I’d believe I see eye to eye with his mind over you…especially with his…shall I say viewpoints?" (SULLIVAN leaned over, his expression becoming slightly frustrated, but moreso angered…) SULLIVAN: "What’s this about Mike? You got your conference, you got your freedom. You’ve taken YOUR side, what good does this do me?" MANSON: "I figured you could use a warning…" SULLIVAN: (while squinting angrily) "FROM WHAT?" MANSON: "If you’d read between the lines, you’d see the motive and the danger for what Miles represents." SULLIVAN: "What’s that?" (MANSON shakes his head in disappointment, not enthused with SULLIVAN’s lack of mindreading.) MANSON: "I’m almost glad I got out when I did, although Miles would make a formidable adversary…" (SULLIVAN snickers slightly, shaking his head at MANSON – now himself thinking lesser of the other man in the room…) SULLIVAN: "Is that right?" MANSON: "You’re blind. Let me envision for you, okay? When Craig comes back, it won’t be to save this place…it will be to find revenge – plain and simple." (SULLIVAN leaned back in his chair, his expression now becoming stoned and defiant) SULLIVAN: "We’ll see Mike. Unfortunately, we’re getting psychotic ramblings from a man that thinks," (quotes the air) "everything is coming to an END. Believe what you want, I’ll keep my faith that Craig believes that to drive himself to figure out a way to get this league out of the mess its roster has happily hurled itself towards. Such as YOURSELF." (MANSON walked towards the door and stopped just short of exiting) MANSON: "Maybe…maybe not. Either way, he’s talking about doom and destruction. Just don’t forget that when he returns…" (MANSON exited, his footsteps fading out as SULLIVAN stared blankly ahead – the quiet of the Penthouse Floor of NFW Headquarters was deafening…BLACK.)
