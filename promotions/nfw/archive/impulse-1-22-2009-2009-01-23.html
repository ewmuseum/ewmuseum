Impulse (1/22/2009)

23 January 2009

suddenimpact — 23 January 2009 MEMBER INFORMATION Name: RK Email Address: Katz knows how to reach me, otherwise stick with PMs. AIM/Yahoo Messenger: none Preferred Method of Handling: Any Best Way to Contact you: PM WRESTLER INFORMATION: Name: Impulse Nicknames: The Marathon Man Height: 5'11" Weight: 188 Handedness: Ambi Looks: Masked wrestler. Besides that, thin and wiry (Iggy Pop with more actual muscle). No scars. Tattoo of a question mark on his left bicep.
Hailing From: New York, NY Disposition: Grounded and good natured. Equal parts realistic and optimistic. Believes in fairness and justice above everything else.
Gimmick: Masked, well rounded wrestler. Ring Attire: Plain colored mask, matching long tights of any color. Wears black boots and elbow- and kneepads that match his mask and tights, only a shade darker. Wears a wrestling T-shirt to the ring - matchwriter's choice. If it's a contemporary shirt he will throw it into the crowd, if it's a vintage shirt (and he has shirts that go back to the 80s) then it's his personal property and will hand to a ring attendant.
Theme Music: The Offspring - "Half Truism" Ring Entrance: Walks out when the guitars kick in, tries to slap as many hands as he can on his way to the ring. Does a lap around the ring, climbs the stairs and slingshots himself to the top turnbuckle where he raises both hands. After that, he hops down to the mat and either waits patiently for his opponent or waits patiently for the referee to ring the bell, depending on who came out first.
Tactics/Style: Mat wrestling, submission wrestling, hooking, high flying, imaginative improvisation. His moveset is limitless. But he is a classic babyface and will always fight fair. If an opponent brings a weapon into the ring his goal will be to disarm him and toss the weapon away, regardless of where the referee is. Does not curse during interviews.
He does not consider hooking to be cheating, so if he is desperate he may use a hook to turn the tide, but will be careful to never permantly damage his opponent.
FINISHER A: Sudden Impact Description - Superkick to the jaw. Setup - Does not matter. Can pull it out of nowhere or can down his opponent and wait a la Shawn Michaels
FINISHER B: The Message Description - Submission hold. Setup is anything that gets Impulse behind his opponent. He hooks them over the shoulder by the neck as if he was going for a neckbreaker/Rude Awakening, but quickly pulls his legs up, kicks his opponent in the back of the knees and when they drop to their knees on the mat, he holds on by their jaw and pulls. Added bonus, if he can rest his own legs on his opponent's for additional leverage
Strengths and weaknesses: 1. Optimistic. Believes the best of people up to the point of being proven wrong, but can easily recognize warning signs.
2. Resilient. He's thin and wiry and can take a lot of abuse, and can therefore wrestle at full capacity for longer than most.
3. Size. When he's out, he's out. Has to get creative against larger opponents and will not always have time.
4. Stamina. Wrestled in the TV Title Battle Royal at Wrestlestock II for 118 minutes straight (was third man in the ring and one of the last eliminated).
5. Strict babyface. Will never cheat, even against five to one odds. 6. Ethical. Will always side with a party who is getting unjustly screwed, regardless of affiliation. Does not believe anyone "deserves it."
MATCHWRITING GUIDE: On paper, he is the perfect wrestler, who can fly, mat wrestle, take opponents down, and do it all with style. What he can and will do in the ring is limited only by his size compared to his opponent's, and the imagination of the matchwriter.
CAVEAT: Will never cheat. Will never use a weapon or use illegal moves, not even in a NO DQ match. No exceptions.
CHARACTER BIOGRAPHY: He was born in Manhatten in 1985 and wanted to wrestle from the time he was 10 years old. There was a place to learn close by and started working out and training while still a teenager. He proved a natural and soaked up everything he could from anyone who was around. His size and agility made him a natural high flyer although he always preferred mat wrestling. His trainer also taught him how to hook, a la Lou Thesz and Ad Santell. Several months after finishing college, he was hired by Craig Miles for New Frontier Wrestling. The mask was his idea, and as far as he knows, only Craig Miles knows who he is under it.
Look, he's young, he's talented, and all he cares about is wrestling a good match. For the moment that's all he's saying.
