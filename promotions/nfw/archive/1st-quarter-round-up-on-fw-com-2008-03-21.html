Off to Valinor — 21 March 2008 Thanks to everyone who contributed. Any and all feedback would be welcomed. http://www.fwrestling.com/index.shtml Off to Valinor — 23 March 2008 did anyone read the article? LOL Legion — 23 March 2008 Very good article that helps people who want to get into the fed jump on or helps anyone that lost track... shall be intrigued to see where the 3 letters thing goes ;-), in a way Legion's been mentioning something about a war... :P
suddenimpact — 23 March 2008 I thought it was very well done. The 'characters' versus the 'business' side seemed like it was clear cut and blurred at the same time, if you see what I'm saying.
Brock Alyas, just as an example - he's doing a really good job of selling the fact that the "off-camera" Brock is just a barely reined in version of his 'character' and how this could affect (for good or bad?) the business dealings NFW is trying to do.
But on the other side of it, it's just a declaration that most of us already know, that NFW is already the best at everything it wants to do, whether anyone puts us up on a pedestal or not.
"NFW is a really happening place!" "Yeah, I know." Word. Macc24 — 24 March 2008 I thought the article did an excellent job of wrapping up what's gone on during the last couple weeks here. It will hopefully spark some interest for those who have continued to read and indulge those who haven't to get caught up.
I particularily enjoyed that Katz pushed the off-camera antics of Alyas - that's exactly the controversy I hoped to create through some of my roleplays, defenitely where I want to be in terms of handling right now. I hope to open him up as a roster member who up coming opponents will lose sleep over.
Thanks a ton Katz. In terms of the next article - possibly a closer, in depth look at some of the newer characters as well as a close look at a lot of the guys who are making noise in the NFW right now.
You know, reward those who have been working hard - in whatever way they'd like their character to be recognized.
I think it's great we have the backing of FW to create some noise for NFW. -Macc
