Shawn Matthews vs. Joey Melton vs. Hornet "Make it happen, boys.." Shawn Douglas is walking to an NFW charity event when he is stopped by the company's seasoned interviewer, 'Just' Mark.
"Shawn Douglas, just recently, challenges by the superstars of the NFW have been flying all over the media. Standings want to be improved and grudges settled. Most recently, "Showtime" Shawn Matthews challenged BOTH legendary CSWA competitors in the form of Hornet and Joey Melton. Being the recent newcomer in the promotion making quite a stir yourself, have you any challenges for the NFW superstars out there?"
SD smiles at the camera. "Well you know, I'm not one to go out and publicly look for trouble but I was hoping that I could get to test my skills versus the same Shawn Matthews who had the b*lls to go out and throw himself into the fire by challenging two legends in the game today. I know my name isn't Hornet and it damn sure isn't Joey Melton and I also realize that you've been having poor competition lately but I think... No... I KNOW if we went at it, it would be one hell of a match.
So why not postpone your challenge versus the two CSWA greats? You can consider me as... a 'warm up match' if you will. And for myself, it'll just be one more learning step and one rung higher in the quest for the Ultratitle."
'Just' Mark nods and thanks Douglas, letting him go on to sign autographs and partake in the NFW charity raffle.
"Well there you have it, ladies and gentlemen. Shawn Douglas, the rookie 'Phenom' quite literally, who just recently beat both NFW alumni Shawn Hart and the NFW North Division B leader Kazuo Shizaki in his first two weeks with the promotion has challenged 'Showtime' Shawn Matthews to a one on one encounter!
