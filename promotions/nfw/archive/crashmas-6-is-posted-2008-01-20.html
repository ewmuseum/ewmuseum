Crashmas 6 is POSTED!

20 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Off to Valinor — 20 January 2008 http://nfw.fwrestling.com/ Link to Vote on Show: http://www.fwrestling.com/a/events/EEAAFpFupF.shtml Hope everyone enjoys it and thanks to everyone that helped. :cool: Legion — 20 January 2008 Great show! debut of the Codemaster in NFW's good to see particularly since he's on the side that could be seen as the beginning of his 'revenge' over a Summer slaughter incident...
On a more character related note the addition of the Randalls segment with Legion was awesome to see, helps I've read CSWA again over past few days as well as the modifications to the rook segment and the surprising addition of him in a pivotal role in the other NC match (adds another name to the grand scheme of things when you read the match) ;-)
Off to Valinor — 20 January 2008 One last segment was added right before the War Games match entitled "Karmic Slate" Thanks for the feedback through IM so far, I'd like to see some posted more in these notes...but this is NFW and therefore I understand the odds of that. :cool:
Also, thanks to Legion for continuing to provide feedback. I'm glad you liked some of the surprises I had for your character. hehe
Legion — 21 January 2008 Renner bringing Codemaster gives me a chance to do a line that Vulcan says to snake in MGS1 in the future to Rook though... next few months are going to be very interesting as more people get entangled in the web that Legion weaves ;-) (If only Logic was still around... him and Rook as a team would be fun!)
Nova — 21 January 2008 My favorite part about this show (besides the developing Legion/Rook angle that I love - the image of Rook standing in the ring robotically continuing to shake Legion's hand is oddly hilarious to me) is how the presence of EL PRESIDENTE Eddie Mayfield was felt throughout in how decisive the show was. The new "no-nonsense" approach isn't just rhetoric; he's literally cleaning up match outcomes like Calvin Carlton's mother with a Flowbee direct across your domepiece (+ 10 pts for brand recognition).
This was easily the most results-oriented show we've had since "version 3" kicked off last summer. Rook and Teresa (I'm assuming if you're reading this far down a feedback thread, spoilers probably aren't going to ruin your day) get wins that finally iron out the long overdue National Title championship scene, though Stevens getting to pick his shot is solid, solid booking just because Jamar is consistently great with promos and the "he'll announce it in a post-show press conference" bit really jives with the character. We get closer to finding some EMT Title-holders, and last but certainly not least, we see Hiroshi deposed at the close of the show, which is the real mark that 2008 is upon us and we're turning the page and building a new arc in the Frontizzier.
On to SUPERCRASH. I'm diggin' all this. Legion — 22 January 2008 Nova- best image to me between Rook/Legion was Legion shouting at Rook whilst he just stands there... he wouldn't be shouting in the usual way though more a tim Olyphant's character in the Girl Next Door way! plus the interference for the National title match with razorwire (particularly the crown... nice touch!) shows he's going to make a LOT of enemies quite quickly :-)
