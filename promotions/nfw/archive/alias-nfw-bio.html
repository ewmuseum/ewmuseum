Alias Biography Eye Color (bright) Blue Nicknames The Original Pulp Hero, The Tin Angel, The Spirit, Bigby (short for Big Bad Wolf, which his daughter's mother said he was acting like) Hometown San Francisco, California (born in Bartlett, Tennessee) Birthday The Second day of April in Nineteen-Seventy Five Height 6'4 Weight 243 Alias "The Tin Angel" Chris Phoenix (ACW; '93-'97) Biography Out of the night that covers me, Black as the Pit from pole to pole, I thank whatever gods may be For my unconquerable soul. In the fell clutch of circumstance I have not winced nor cried aloud. Under the bludgeoning of chance My head is bloody, but unbowed. Beyond this place of wrath and tears Looms but the horror of the shade, And yet the menace of the years Finds, and shall find me, unafraid. It matters not how straight the gate, How charged with punishments the scroll, I am the master of my fate: I am the captain of my soul. 'Invictus' by William Ernest Henley Christopher Declan Sheffield was born in Bartlett, Tennessee, the son of a former British pro wrestler Brian 'Flyboy' Sheffield and Rebecca Sheffield a 'star' of B-horror movies at the time. Although Rebecca deeply loved her only son, the alcoholic Brian was obsessively jealous of their relationship. Due to this, Chris's earliest childhood memories are filled with pain from his fathers abuse. 'You read the bible? There's a passage I memorized. Ezekiel 25:17. 'The path of the righteous man is beset on all sides by the inequities of the selfish and the tyranny of evil men. Blessed is he who, in the name of charity and good will, shepherds the weak through the valley of the darkness. For he is truly his brother's keeper and the finder of lost children. And I will strike down upon thee with great vengeance and furious anger those who attempt to poison and destroy mankind. And you will know I am the Lord when I lay my vengeance upon they.' Brian Sheffield finally murdered his wife when she attempted to leave with Chris, before finally turning the gun on himself. Chris was then raised by Rebecca's sister Michelle and later attended Santa Angelo High School in San Francisco. He bottled up his grief and anger over his childhood, diverting the drives spawned by his sufferings into his own physical perfection and, ironically enough, the world of professional wrestling. Here was the place where he thought he truly belonged. 'I've been sayin' that kinda shit for years. And if you've ever heard it, it meant your ass. I never really questioned what it meant. I just thought it was just a cold-blooded thing to say to some unlucky bastard before I slammed his face into my knee and made his nose spray crimson. But one day something happened that made me think twice. It brought up some painful memories, one's I wanted to forget.' Sadly enough Chris decided not to finish high school, instead he traveled to the city of Los Angeles. Where, after making the rounds in the independent circuit, he quickly became was a rising star in All-Star Championship Wrestling. In his early years of wrestling for ACW, the 92-93 season, Chris met one 'Nitro Beaver' Kyle Morris, who was also training Joey Malone and Daniel Phillips at around the same time. Morris would lay the ground work that Chris would eventually return to build his technical ability on. It was only 1993 and he was only 18, but he was also an impressive high-flying who went by the name of 'The Tin Angel' Chris Phoenix, even participating in international house shows in Japan and Europe. Thanks to some connections with Morris, Chris would even wrestle in several dark matches for the storied Championship Wrestling League... already making ground to bypass the dreams of his father. Chris Phoenix also found a 'mentor', suited perfectly with just as acidic an attitude as his, and that man was one 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs. They stepped on alot of feet while wrestling in ACW's upper-middle card. In the end... this solid friendship wouldn't last. Those shockwaves where felt then, and the aftermath is still being battled across now. Due to the shockwaves that where felt through the breaking apart of the SVJ/Chris Phoenix alliance, amoung other things, Chris quickly got in over his head with gambling problems and was beaten within an inch of his life by a gang of thugs at a show in New York. He had an angel on his shoulder that day though, by the name of Rufus Tanner, known to his friends as Eightball. Eightball worked for Joey Leone a crime boss who wasn't a big fan of the gang that attacked Chris. Now Chris owed the Leone family. Alongside Eightball, Chris became a 'professional', a working man for a family of crime. In the lime light, Phoenix all but turned to ashes, dissapearing from the ACW and the circuit's of CWL and CWA without a trace. Chris Sheffild however, started to make a name for himself in the underground fighting and dirty boxing circles as a vicious, powerful man born of determination and might. Thanks to both the vicious attack and a chance at a new life he was a changed man far different from the wrestler he was. For better or worse. Now... the latin definition of 'alias' is 'another time'. A place in time, much different from where Chris Sheffield, that tin angel, once found himself, is where he would find himself. Another time... much different from where he once was. Any vestige of those 'Tin Angel' Chris Phoenix days no longer excisted, only this Alias. Though honestly... it did end up being for the better. Only, as he would find out, only in the long run. 'And now I'm thinkin', it could mean you're the evil man. And I'm the righteous man. And the .45 I have here inmy breast holster, he's the shepherd protecting my righteous ass, in the valley of darkness. Or it could be you're the righteous man and I'm the shepherd and it's the world that's evil and selfish. I'd like that. But that isn't the truth. The truth is you're the weak. And I'm the tyranny of evil men. But I'm tryin'. I'm tryin' real hard to be the shepherd.' Now you see... you can't stay underground forever. As much as you want to stray from your past there's always something that pulls you back, as opposing forces look to give you that push. That force came in the form of one man for Alias. Edgardo Jr. Owner of the WWRPG. Ed knew there was something about Alias when, through business connections, he saw him fight against several other nameless men in the Warehouse. Alias didn't just have potential, in Ed's eyes, but marketability as well and was promptly signed the next day. The months leading up to the first show Alias kept a very low profile, away from the media and even from his fellow wrestlers. The first show changed all that because Alias wasn't booked in the during the show, but in a dark match and adding insult to injury... he was, well... injured. Two weeks on the shelf after his not so glorious return changed Alias for the better however, his fighting spirit was back, this shit wasn't about money anymore. So even before his wounds healed he made a challenge to the feds only Grand Slam champion, Ravnos. Sparking a feud that would evolve both the WWRPG, Ravnos and Alias. There first match, after the challenge, ended brutally with Alias being drove through a table courtesy of a Ravnos plancha. From there it was a back and forth war of blood and broken wood. At one time Alias even attacked Ravnos while he was inside his limousine, after crawling in through the sun roof, Alias drove Ravnos's head through a side window. The WWRPG's commissioner 'Crash' Geirtz had seen enough. soon enough the match was signed. Thirty Minute Iron Man Table match. It was the sleeper match of WWRPG's return PPV Spring Fever, and shaped both men for what was to come. After 20 minutes Ravnos had built a size-able lead, 12-5, and was slowly walking away with the win. Suddenly Alias made a surge, trying to gain the victory. Ultimately it came down to just one table with the clock clicking down, only 20 seconds left, Alias had to go all for nothing, he stacked the tables outside the ring. One table making a small pyramid with two more and he set-up Ravnos for the move that started it all... a plancha from inside the ring. Short story is Ravnos rolled, Alias lost. One thing that Alias had quickly gained was from that match however was respect. Soon after, Alias was one of WWRPG's merchandise figureheads, the Anarchy 'A' shirt was selling as quickly as it was being produced. Still, Alias felt that he had failed himself. He couldn't destroy the monster like everyone wanted him to, but along with the likes of Ruben Ross, Death Knight, and Trey Savant... Alias was quickly becoming one of the promotions top faces. He was beginning to embrace the lime light just as much as he had pushed it away just one year before. This was why he had to finish what he started with Ravnos, close the first chapter of his new life. After a few turmoil filled weeks post-PPV in which Alias and Ravnos brought there feud to a new level, an ending to there feud was decided... Alias vs Ravnos, Best-of-Seven series. What would follow was not only physical and scientific wrestling to a tee, but also what a feud like that had needed to end it. Once again, Ravnos drove out with a commanding lead in the series, three matches to one, winning the first two matches with a well placed foot on the rope, something that could only be chalked up to veteran experience. No one saw the series going longer then five matches, maybe six if Alias was lucky. Thing is Alias didn't care, the only person he could really depend on was himself, a fact that was evident. Ravnos and Alias had gotten to a point of knowing each others moves and styles so well that they had almost wrestled the first four matches to a stale mate. Not only out-smarting the veteran but also out-wrestling him, Alias won Match Five using an interesting Rope-A-Dope method tiring the larger man out before finally going for the kill in front of an ecstatic crowd. Match Six was simply put, The Rage in the Cage, part of an entire show of cage matches. Alias walked into Rage in a Cage with a distinct advantage, no longer seen as the underdog, he had won, in an amazing turn of events, a huge house show tournament in San Diego which included the Top 16 wrestlers in the WWRPG, including the home town hero Ruben Ross and Slater Foxe the Hardcore Champion, among others. Alias walked out of Rage in a Cage, with the series dramatically tied up 3-3... meaning that no matter what this series had officially cemented Alias in the main event. When he walked away with his second tournament win in as many weeks the critics had turned the tables and where now on his side. The world always seems to turn upside down when you least expect it though, as Ravnos came away with a shocking win in a five star Match 7, just one week before WWRPG's second PPV, the Iron Man Quantlet. The big name main event, the Iron Man Quantlet, was for the WWRPG's biggest prize. The World Heavyweight Championship. 15 of the fed's most embittered men battling for it. Alias wouldn't walk away with the title that night, that right went to Trey 'The Bomber' Savant. However he finally got the one thing that had been eating away at him, the big win over Ravnos, when he eliminated his most hated enemy from the huge Quantlet match. The following week, the next chapter in his career began when he and fellow number one contender Ruben Ross battled it out in a 5-star match for the 'I Quit' Title. Several times both men where on the verge of just giving up... until finally Alias submitted to the Fade To Black, a Sharpshooter. Once again coming out of a big match without a win, Alias cemented the fact that he would one day soon challenge for the 'I Quit' championship and finally win the big prize. He almost didn't get a chance however, when on the next show Alias was confronted by Edgardo about joining Ed's very own 'Union', a stable that every major heel in the company was linked too. Much to the delight of his fans not only did Alias tear up his contract but he forever solidified himself as WWRPG's pulp hero when he layed out the fuming CEO and owner of WWRPG with a devastating piledriver. Alias high wouldn't last long though, as he was viciously attacked by Edgardo's top man and the WWRPG's most hated man, the now ex-Hardcore Champion Slater Foxe later the same night. Slater drove chair shot after chair shot into Alias's already bad knee, before locking on a vicious single legged Boston Crab. Just like that, the man who had walked away winning with every tournament held by the WWRPG up till that point, the man who was nipping at the heels of the new World and I Quit Champion Ruben Ross, was now out of action for anywhere from four to six weeks. Pissed the fuck off... didn't describe Alias's state of mind, he was once again a man filled with murderous rage, not for Ravnos, or some group of small-time New York thugs. No... all he wanted to do was two things, take down Slater Foxe... hopefully ending the bastard's the career... and finally get a hold of the one championship that he had had his eyes on since square one... nah, not the World, he liked fame but not as much as glory. He wanted the 'I Quit' Title, finally getting recognition as the best damn fighter in wrestling. So the bell tolled, just four weeks later, the night that Slater Foxe got his shot at Ruben Ross's WWRPG World Championship. The match didn't end with a pinfall or submission... nope, it ended with the celebrated return of Alias, with a vicious attack on Slater Foxe and a save for Ruben from even more of Ed's troops. Here's the bitter sweet end to Alias's wrestling history up till then, though. WWRPG had run into financial troubles, and the next show, after Alias's comeback, was never aired... the world never got to see Alias finally winning his 'I Quit' title. Nope. Hundreds of other things where never allowed to happen and Alias and Slater Foxe never ended up meeting at WWRPG's signature PPV Wrestlebowl III for Alias's 'I Quit' strap. Yeah life's a bitch... wrestling's a bitch... Alias became fed up with politics and money problems that continued to follow him, like a plague, to every fed he was in after WWRPG. That was until his return to an old... almost forgotten home. All-Star Championship Wrestling... Max Douglas. Scott Perry. Done and gone. Television Champion. Check. Legend! Saved the company. Beat the bad guys... all while the dickhead former owner sprays gray on his dash and subsequently rotted in hell. Which all adds up to? Original Pulp Hero... is the knight in shining armor. Twisted, ain't it? Impulse... Brian James. Osyrus. Great Dragon. Tempest. Steve Christ. Superstar... Vince Jacobs. Television Champion? 84 days straight. Undefeated, on his own terms. ACW Wrestler of the Spring... 100 day undefeated streak, in singles matches, from March till June 2003. 21st Century Wrestling? Another story for another day. Superstar... Vince Jacobs. A ghost that wouldn't stay dead. When your a man like Alias.... your past tracks you down so it can kick you in the ass. Pounded and Fused II. What was supposed to put the rivalry on ice... only strengthened it. Still. Alias = #1 contender to a vacant championship. Which all led to the true drama... between two friends. Jason Kain vs. Alias... the now infamous Best-of-Seven series. Faith. Hope. Love. Strengh. Will. Defiance. 'Ratings.' I'll make sure to put everything together in much more detail in the near future... but let's just leave you with four more words. Alias. ACW. World. Champion. natch ... Reality though, it doesn't have perfect endings... or perfect beginnings. All-Star Championship Wrestling came to an end in December of 2003, well for Alias atleast. Even with the biggest gain of momentum in the federation's history, ACW no longer had enough steam to maintain a TV deal. Alias blamed Brian Carter. Carter didn't listen. Alias then found himself in the same predicament as his father had years before... either stay with the federation that he had a hand in making, that was now sinking, or take the bait of a bigger federation and keep the momentum of his career going. After weeks of fighting against the latter... Alias finally agreed to leave ACW. His mentor in the business for the last decade, Charles Dunn, even ended up signing his release papers. Rumor has it now, that ACW has taken to a traveling road show style of federation... touring all over the United States and into Europe to keep afloat. Hell, at least it went out with a “bang”... right? For some reason though, Alias felt like less of a man then his father was in requards to ones loyalty. That fact ate him up inside. Don't ask Alias about leaving this home of his, the federation that made him who he was today, because he won't talk about, it leaves a bad taste in his mouth... he'll give a quaint 'Fuck you'... dream of what could have been, what is now, and then change the subject. Ask him about the beginning, however, and he'll have one word for you. Action! No, wait. Reaction... Alias career in Action! had started off with a bang. His debut, on the 85th edition of Pressure Point, sent shockwaves across the wrestling world when he defeated Sharc, Action's recently crowned Player, well... sort of. A few days later, and Alias was the central guest on the 24th, and seemingly last, edition of Whatever. The week after that he fought fist to nail with the ORIGINAL Action! Champion, Jeff Garvin, in what could only be a forerunner to what would happen between either man in the future. This all led to Action's Christmas show, the PPV and New Years celebration in Las Vegas... and the day that changed Action!, making it a shell of what it once was, on the eve of Christmas eve. Details are still shady... but it all stems from Reed Young's evident and steady loss of power within A!W. Reed wanted to once again push such ...wrestlers... like Christopher Fox and LLB back into the spotlight, scuttling aside the resurgence the company had enjoyed with Jeff Garvin, KSZ and Max Danger, most notably, in the upper card. Reed had never been competent as an actual leading figure head for Action!... but he all but proved it when, in this dispute with the likes of EM Pleh and Joe Black, among others... the idiot king put them in the position to give him free reign on Action!... or walk. They walked. Most everyone followed. Sharc. Max Danger. Wrestling 101. ... and your Pulp Hero. Reed got nice and drunk, drowning the stupidity, and tore up each and every contract. Yeah it was official. HOHOHO... Merry Christmas. 2003 had come to an end in a way that Alias could have never predicted. Not by a long shot. Alias's contract negotiations with WWC subsequently fell through, due to his participation in the mass exodus in Action! It seems that when you get hit hard... and hit hard during the holidays, well, thoughts turn to home sweet home. The ringing in his ear and that shining gold, one way or another it had given him something to do. What is the real definition of a martyr anyway? Alias, as he says, was headed south for whatever awaited him there. If not for family, faith... or redemption... Then hope. Something old school, as you knew it would be... that would be new to the world and a finally a fresh start for the Original Pulp Hero. All things to happen since then... well, that's written history... ... and what's etched in stone and plated gold, are eight words. Alias, the first Champion of the Squared Circle. One of many firsts, actually. He was Tyr. The bravest of the gods. The only one that didn't fear the Wolf. The reason... there was a Tuesday (Night of Wrestling, that is). He even got to meet his daughter, Issabella Samuel, Izzy, for the first time. An event in itself that held another story for another time. Speaking of firsts... Alias also became the first man to lose to lose the Championship. The first man to be screwed out of the Championship. Still, Clash would be a match for the ages... one hundred and five days into the reign of the Original Pulp Hero, two months after one hell of a three-way match against Brandon Youngblood and Coral Avalon at Unprovoked. It would be... Sonny Silver, the devil himself, who had gone through each of Alias's friends... Randalls, Rook, Frampton... even Monet... before he got to Alias. And he got to Alias, oh yes, just when Alias got to him. ... and many things, including $5000 in the palm of Mark Sheilds hand, later... Things had come full circle for Chris Sheffield, the Pulp Hero who became an overnight pop culture celebrity, became a Pulp Hero once more. ... and though it was a vicious cycle, he was still himself, amoungst the wars on three fronts that raged amoungst professional tragedy and personal triumph... It was still Alias - Original Pulp Hero. So what if he was a little worse for wear? It's not like 2004 would last forver. Ecspecially for the spirit... 2004? How about 2005. Let me TELL YOU about 2005. Tons of shit happened. Including Alias's retirement from place that made him, ACW. In part because Ahab finally killed the Great White Wale, as it where. And also in part, because Alias lost all sight in his left eye. Though I'll tell you all about 2005, and how it ended, later. Later? Later. It's aftermath is playing out right in front of your eyes. This is not so much a set-up move as it is Alias's secondary finisher. Homage to both GGM and The King. Modified Buffalo Sleeper Hold or Mexican Surfboard/Crossface Chickenwing hybrid. The victim is kneeling on the mat, Alias is standing behind them. Alias places both their feet on the back of the victim's bent knees and sits down, leaving his knees bent so that they dig into the back of the victim. Alias then grabs one of their arms (either arm can be targeted, usually the person's strong hand) and pulls it up so that the arm is raised above the person's shoulder and bent behind them. Then Alias traps the opponent's wrist in his elbow. Alias locks his other hand around the victim's chin, reaching under the victim's free arm to do so. Alias finally locks both his hands together over the opponents chin and pulls back, applying pressure to the victim's neck and locked arm while stretching them across their knees, and giving the 'free' arm limited motion.
