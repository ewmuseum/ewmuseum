More thoughts later... brusch — 4 September 2013 Re: HEADSHOT 01 @ RELOADED 16 Available! More in-depth thoughts than smiley faces: - Thrilled and a bit honored to get the Triple Crown Championship off of Rook. I have a lot (a lot a lot) of respect for Pants and think he's a fantastic handler, and to have the character who ended his 5ish year reign is just a huge rub. Love the concept of the smart-belt, love the potential opportunities for future feuds, love that this storyline (which has basically been running since the very beginning of the Grand Prix and started up right when Spooky Doom [lol] was ousted) got to see its way through. Happy feelings and thoughts.
- On the one hand, it makes sense that since it's been essentially 5 months since Jack Bryant's near-murder occurred that he could theoretically be "ready and rarin'" for Impulse at the next show...on the other hand, I think there are a lot of really interesting and next-level stories that can be told if a different route was taken than this seg. Nothing against JB personally because I'm definitely a fan, I just wonder what could have happened if there were lingering injury/concussion/PTSD-type concerns brought up in this segment. Then again, it could be a situation where the wrestler thinks he's fine when he's really not, and is getting set up for potentially horrific consequences by ignoring lingering symptoms...in which case, I am COMPLETELY re-engaged with this story.
- Glad to see Legion win here; I like the sneaky shadowy group he's forming and his purely chaotic intentions he's working with, it's important to have a unique heel dynamic in a group when there's others like the HFC and the Guild around. Each group has their own specific goals and their own specific self-interests, and even though they're all some flavor of sinister it's nice that they're different parts of the spectrum. Also, Lamont's commentary in this match is just full of lawl for me.
- More Calvin Carlton, please. Everywhere. All over my show. - Seems that a show intro was re-intro'd; if the goal is for each Headshot to have their "intro-grafix" then ok, but I don't know if it's necessary. Quick little squash for Seven, but some INTRIGUE~ post-match with the Impostor Stryfe. Definitely interested to see who this guy is and what his next moves are against Austin and Co.
- YES MORE CALVIN CARLTON ON MY SHOW! - Well THAT was unexpected. With other teams on the roster (not to mention the massive Atken/Alexander feud earlier in the year), it's a huge shock that the end of the Superfly Express's reign comes at the hands of essentially a screw job by Rook, Blaine and Carlton. Incredibly one-sided and nefarious, and it's one of those stories that makes the Hellfire Club just so easy to hate and boo, which may have been necessary after their losses earlier in the show. Will be fascinated to see who (and how and when) will perpetrate the comeuppance for this travesty of justice. A fun world title match follows, though it's interesting to me that Impulse is essentially guaranteeing Harmen a rematch regardless of circumstances - the undying idealism and/or hyperextended sense-of-fairness is one of those character traits that Impulse possesses that seems to have one of two eventual outcomes: either there's going to be a Cena-type "half-boo, half-cheer" scenario because there are many people out there who would view "yeah I beat you, but I want to beat you when you're better so there's no question that I'm the better man" as just the worst, or it will bite him in the ass when he extends the olive branch one too many times and a wrestler will get fired up to the point of "unstoppable force" when the situation presents itself. It's still a really cool character trait though, and one you don't really see much.
Rook Black — 14 September 2013 Re: HEADSHOT 01 @ RELOADED 16 Available! I am thrilled and honored that you came to join us. The National Title and the Triple Crown Championship was a really troublesome legacy. I can remember a conversation with Katz that took place about two years ago I think where he said something to the effect of, "Wow, they're just going to keep blaming you for the booking of this title, aren't they?"
One of the main reasons that I really hoped that this moment would come with LDO was the fact that he was not indifferent to the belt. With the standard response to Rook's success being an attitude that the belt was middling or worthless and that Rook's retaining of it likewise not praiseworthy (fuck you guys, seriously, fuck you, fuck you, fuck you, fuck you), the fact that LDO took a determined interest in it was the single best thing that could've happened to me. And with that in mind it was a rather easy thing to decide to customize the defense of the belt to LDO, which gave birth to the Final Boss persona. The arc of Rook being this anti-hero figure, but then taking a full turn to the bad guy realm felt like a good reveal, something that could conceivably happen in the arc of a videogame. With LDO literally being the heroic player one, at first idolizing/respecting the Rook character, but then discovering the truth and surpassing him.
But don't think that I threw it away. Like I had told Warren ages ago when I felt Deacon had come to take the Triple Crown Championship, that I expected to lose the belt but I sure as hell wasn't going to go down without trying my best to keep it. And in this regard, I consider myself super lucky to have had Rook look as strong as he did in the last defense where loss felt quite inevitable.
As for the smartbelt, what could be more cool to LDO than a belt you can play videogames on? do some autographs, then hand a few lucky kids wireless controllers and suddenly you can play a round or two of super smash bros or mario kart at appearances? Not to mention it being hackable, modern, and customizable to the limit of the imagination? Give it a voice and it could even be a character, why the hell not?
In finality, it feels like a genuine privilege to hand the strap over to a creative and enthusiastic guy who I'm sure will go on to do some great things with the Triple Cro- oh, cool, I can start calling it the Triforce now too. And now, like most champions, you have the stressful moment of discovery where you realize that you are very dependant on your challenger in order to give your reign legitimacy. I can only hope that someone steps up and plays it as straight and genuine as you did for me.
