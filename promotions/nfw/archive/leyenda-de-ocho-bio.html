Leyenda de Ocho Biography Eye Color Green Nicknames The Cartridge Cruiser Hometown Chicago, IL Birthday 2/12/90 Height 5'8 Weight 188 Handedness Right Looks Has dozens and dozens of different video game-themed luchador masks and tights. Has a dirty blonde tuft of hair on his chin and a couple video-game related tattoos on his chest and left arm. Fit but not super bulky. Has been seen sans-mask outside of the ring, with a wildly disheveled mop of dirty blonde hair. Biography Leyenda de Ocho (real name undisclosed) was born and raised in Rogers Park, a diverse, beautiful, yet crime-riddled neighborhood in far north Chicago. A small and lonely nerd, Ocho dove headfirst into the safe havens that video games and televised professional wrestling afforded him, so much so that they became his entire world. He refuses to touch any game that came out on a system released after the 1990s; in his opinion, these games strive so hard to produce photo-realistic visual effects that they had became too similar to the "real" world, a world where he was still struggling to find his place. As he grew up and entered adulthood, he became less awkward, though no less obsessed with the alternate universes his mind had latched onto in wrestling and old-school video games. He is now living out his personal dream, unifying his two great obsessions into a single bizarre journey of personal escapism that may one day swallow his mind whole. Wrestling Style High flyer. Mix of Rey Mysterio, 90’s Chris Jericho, and Sonic the Hedgehog. Finisher Move Actualizar Finisher Description Corkscrew Second Rope Quebrada Finisher Setup Move Shining Wizard Other Moves EARLY MOVES: 1: Dropkick (likes leading off with this) 2: Spinning Heel Kick 3: Knee and shin strikes 4: Headscissors takedown 5: Flying Cross Body MID-MATCH MOVES: 6: Frontflip Senton 7: Frontflip Double Axehandle 8: Exploder Suplex 9: When Irish-Whipped, climbs up turnbuckles and throws a dropkick 10: Sunset Flip 11: Hurricanrana 12: Plancha LATE-MATCH MOVES: 13: Moonsault variations 14: Eight strikes to opponent in corner 15: Knee strikes to opponent in double underhook position (followed by double underhook suplex if cruiserweight) 16: Dragon Sleeper (vs. cruiserweights); Single-Leg Boston Crab (vs. larger opponents) 17: "Media-Jefe" (opponent in Tree of Woe, Ocho hangs outside ring with legs wrapped around opponent's throat) 18: Tornado DDT Ring Entrances As the entrance theme slowly builds throughout the arena, Ocho steps out from behind the curtain, pauses and raises both arms up holding out four fingers on each hand. As the drum beat kicks in, he sprints down the entrance ramp and hops into the ring underneath the bottom rope, keeping his momentum until he slides all the way out the other end of the ring (ala Paul London). He hops on top of the announce table and poses for the crowd, eventually going for a high five with the announcers (it's up to the announcers whether or not they high five back). He then hops off the table and into the ring, bouncing up and down, facing down his opponent if he's already entered or the entrance ramp if he hasn't. Gimmick Nerdy luchador who is obsessed with old video games. May or may not have an undiagnosed form of Dissociative Identity Disorder. Strengths Flies with the best of them. High level of personal resolve. Master of just about any video game released before 1999. Weaknessees Has an obsessive, one-track-mind side to him that can get him into trouble. Poor brawler. Zero alcohol tolerance.
