Nathan Storm Biography Nicknames 'Unbelievable' Hometown Denver, Colorado Biography 'Unbelievable' Nathan Storm hails from Denver, Colorado. A gymnast in the ring, he works off the ropes, the turnbuckles and anything else he can stand on, and finishes with the Springboard Bulldog. At the AAWC's Oktoberfest in October 1996 he ambushed Arrogance in the aisle and put Peyton Wright through the Spanish broadcast table with a springboard somersault splash, then joined the brawl that ended the night. At the UWA Random Rumble in February 1997 he suplexed a table over the top rope onto three men, dumped a bucket of smelt over Winston Steele and survived to the final four. In the King of the Hill ladder match for the vacant UWA World Championship he leapt off the ladder to stop anyone else reaching the belt, took Craig Miles' Russian Sickle from behind, and watched Javid Dones take the title. Finisher Move Springboard Bulldog
