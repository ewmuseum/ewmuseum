MrWest — 24 December 2007 MEMBER INFORMATION: Name: [name withheld] Email Address: [detail withheld] AIM: [detail withheld] Preferred Method of Handling: whatever Best Way to Contact you: PM WRESTLER INFORMATION: Name: Professor Tremendous (given name: Charles "Chazz" Tremendous) Nicknames: Prof T aka The Good Professor aka The Extreme Technician Height: 5' 9" (5'11" with his lifts in) Weight: 222 lbs. Handedness: Left Looks: Long, luxurient, flowing, jet black hair. A finely toned yet not overly muscular build.
Hailing From: West Newton, MA Disposition: Heel Gimmick: Esteemed Professor of Wrestlology - thinks he is the smartest man in wrestling; probably isn't.
Ring Attire: A variety of differently colors and pattern tight and ring apparel to suit his mood {often partial to blacks and purples though). If there is a chill in the air, might wear a Flair style sequined robe (or not)
Theme Music: "She Blinded me With Science" by Thomas Dolby melding into "Rockafeller Skank" by Fatboy Slim
Ring Entrance: After a suitably sufficient pyro display as meeting his standards as a world class champion of champions, The Good Professor will honor the unwashed masses with his presence.
Tactics/Style: New Japan Junior Heavywieght. Lots of Suplexes with a healthy dose of top rope. (I guess mostly Jericho with a touch of Benoit)
Strengths: 1- Quite agile and a far better (and well rounded) technical wrestler than his egotistical and occassionally bufoonish image should allow.
2 - No moral qualms about doing lilerally whatever it takes to win a match. 3 - Incredibly resilliant and unimaginably lucky. The Professor will often absorb massive ammounts of punishment until his dumb luck kicks in and he manages to "steal" a win.
Weaknessess: 1 - Nowhere near as smart as he thinks he is. Professor Tremendous will tend to create elaborate and risky schemes to win matches (even when none are necessary) that will then blow up in his face.
2 - An egotist who truly believes he is a born leader and great teacher - Professor Tremendous will often gather lower card "talents" to assist him assuming that he can make them an elite fighting force. (He cannot).
3 - As he is an transpaerntly amoral liar and a cheat, Professor T has a tough time keeping friends.
MATCHWRITING GUIDE: Finisher: The Honor Roll (A Rolling German Suplex into a Rolling Stright Jacket Suplex into a Bridging Dragon Suplex)
Submission Finisher/Favored Rest Hold - The Final Exam (an Inverted STF) Signature Moves: The MidTerm (a Tope Rope Somersault Guillotine Leg Drop), The Pop Quiz (a series of spinning heel kicks timed to hit just as the foe rises from the previous one), The Spunk Face (A reverse Stickface/Bronco Buster)
Other favored moves: Top Rope Wheel Kick, Enziguri, Snap Suplex, German Suplex, Straight Jacket Suplex, Butterfly Suplex, Dragon Suplex, Monkey Magic Suplex, Sitdown Front Brainbuster, Wrap Around DDT, Tornado DDT, Bulldog, Violent Green Hold, Arabian Piledriver, Top Rope Rocker Dropper, Body Slam, Slingshot Powerslam, Poke to the Eyes, Stiff Slap to the face, Kick to the groin.
CHARACTER BIOGRAPHY: Titles Held: CWSA UNIFIED World* Heaveyweight Title (current), Monkey Championship Wrestling Extreme Heavywiegth Champion (or whatever it was called), 2 x MBE World Heavyweight Champion, 3 x MBE North American Champion, 2 x MBE World Tag Team Champion (once w/ Star Rider, once with Tyrone the Tidy Giant and Allworld), MBE Extreme Champion, MBE Unified Champion, MBE Total Elimination "Sole Survivor, PBPro Global Tag Team Champion (w/ Mr Amazing), HLLUno Six Man Lucha Libre Champion (as El Tremendo #1), JoshiPro Womens World Champion (as the masked Madam Tremendaux), and that's not even counting all of the fake titles he has claimed to have held.
Affiliation: Founder and Leader of TeamTremendous. Formerly The A! Team (w/ MrOutstanding) Profile: The Prof is basically a self-absorbed, amoral prick. He considers himself the be all and end all of wrestling both skill-wise and intellectually; a trait which commonly ends up landing him in situations way over his head. But the fact that he is able to justify even the lowest of actions, as long as it benefits him, often helps him escape these exact same situations. Don't let the almost comedic levels of arrogance fool you, for underneath that exterior lies a highly skilled technical wrestler whose world travels have left him well versed in the American, Puroresu, Lucha Libre, Japanese Garbage Fed, and Martial Arts styles. Plus he is just damn lucky a lot of the time.
Professor Tremendous carries around a facimile of the CWSA UNIFIED World* Heaveyweight Title which he claimed to have wonfrom Troy Windham in Rio De Janero during one of that companies down times and has defended in various federations ever since.
Until recently Prof T was the cult-like leader of a band of sycophants called Team Tremendous who finally wised up to his mistreatment of them and turned on/abandoned him during TEAMS most recent CoC Tournament.
After the loss of his Team, Prof went on a bit of a drunken bender and it was during this time that he unwittingly signed a binding personal services contract with Mike Nichols and NFW.
Professor T abhors NFW and really doesn't want to be here. jediPREZ — 25 December 2007 The director of Charlie Wilson's War works for NFW? That explains SOOOOOOOOOOO much.
