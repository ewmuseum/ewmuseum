Justin — 26 June 2009 MEMBER INFORMATION: Name: Justin Email Address: [detail withheld] AIM/Yahoo Messenger: [detail withheld] WRESTLER INFORMATION: Wrestler Name: Rich Mahogany Height: 6’1” Weight: 210lbs Hailing From: Austin, TX Handedness: Rich doesn’t use his hands, that’s what The Ladies are for! Disposition: Generally, heel, but can go either way. Gimmick: The Ladies Man, The Love Machine, etc. In the ring he’s the biggest chicken**** heel you’ve ever seen.
Ring Attire: Depending on what color scheme he’s going with at the time, he’ll always come to the ring with a vest and a bow-tie of the same color. In the ring he wears regular, if a bit tight tights, kneepads with stars on them, and boots.
Looks: He’s got long, light brown, feathered hair, beautiful green eyes, immaculately white teeth, beautiful abs, tremendous thighs… etc.
Ring Entrance: Rich loves The Ladies. He plays to them, dances for them, wiggles his pelvis at them, blows kisses to them, takes kisses from them, hands out roses, room keys, lude and lascivious winks, etc. To anyone with a penis, he is a complete douch-nozzle.
Theme Music: “Love Man” by Otis Redding Tactics/Style: Rich is quick, and he’s technically proficient, but he’s going to win most of his matches the old fashioned way: cheating. Any dirty tactic you’ve ever seen, from hair-pulling, finger-stomping, all the way to back-rakes and eye-gouges. If it’s cheap, and it works, expect Rich to use it. Often.
5 Specialty Moves: Rich only has 3 “signature” moves. Unless you count every dirty move in the book as his other two.
1. The Crotch Rocket: Cross-Legged Cradle Piledriver 2. The Sex Panther: Stump Puller, with extra grinding on the back of the victim's head just for good (gross) measure.
3. Springboard *****slap: Used mostly as a counter when whipped into the ropes, Rich will springboard from the middle rope, twist, and come back with a ridiculous five across the jaw.
Finisher & Set-Up Moves: The Sex-Plex: Bridging Fisherman’s Suplex, held for a pin. Set up generally by a kick to the junk, or anything else that’d double the opponent over.
Strengths: 1. Deceptively quick. 2. Slippery, from oiling up. 3. Has a third eye for referee position, allowing him to cheat. Weaknesses: 1. Low tolerance for pain. 2. Easily distracted by The Ladies. 3. Likes to pose and dance a bit too much. In 100 words or less, please give a biographical profile of your character: Rich Mahogany is a slimy, grimy, lowlife gigolo. The Ladies love him, and he loves The Ladies. Is there really anything else to know?
