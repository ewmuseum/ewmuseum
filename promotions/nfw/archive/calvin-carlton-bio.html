Calvin Carlton Biography Biography Calvin Carlton was born as the only son to Bel Air, California's wealthiest black woman, thus making him a son of privledge. Calvin, a mama's boy through and through, always had whatever he wanted or whatever Mama wanted for him. Calvin's dream since he was a child was to become a star wrestler. But lacking any physical ability whatsoever, Calvin had to join the industry in another manner. While in college, he started interning for the UWA. When the NFW debuted, he pestered and annoyed enough people to be given an on-air role as a C-level announcer, where he slowly aggravated enough people. One of the persons whom wronged him was former NFW star Alex Wylde, who buried Calvin in a dumpster during an interview. Wylde at the time was feuding with WildStar. Carlton, with a wealthy background and a knowledge of finance, convinced WildStar to let him become his "portfolio manager" and created Five Star Enterprises in the process, also serving as the financial overseer for the Oblivion Express, Japanese cohorts of 'Star. Five Star Enterprises would not last long, however. The Oblivion Express were outshined by other tag teams and fell out of favor with Carlton. WildStar, a consumate athlete already angered by Calvin's managerial style, would end up finding out that Calvin's mama had financially taken over Five Star Enterprises for her baby son. WildStar and Carlton would soon split up as well, leading to WildStar becoming an annoucner in NFW. Carlton would soon move on to bigger and better things. Calvin was irked by one tag team in particular, jealous of their success and popularity. This team was Ryan and Lincoln, The Showstoppers -- then the sport's hottest young babyfaces. Calvin had verbally accosted them at a NFW Show (on the "Hollywood's On Fire" segment). When confronted by Calvin, they were jumped from behind by "Fabulous" Frankie Fargo and "The New American Stud" Brandon Mueller, a forgotten tag team from the sport's southern regionals in the 1980s known as The Original Showstoppers. The OSS and The Showstoppers would soon enter into one of the all-time greatest tag team rivalries, spilling blood in rings all over the country. Also feuding with The OSS was HellMachine, managed by "Doc" Martens, the daughter of a wealthy shipping magnate. Carlton would viciously attack Martens at every moment -- memorably calling her a "slampig" in one instance. Calvin was also known for his "Ten Seconds" segments where, in ten seconds, he would speak about his views of the sport of wrestling, politics, or personal hygiene tips. But many hypothezised about Calvin's emotional state -- claiming, perhaps accurately, that he hated women because he felt emotionally castrated by his powerful mama, whom he obsessively worshiped. Calvin's mama helped finance the successful relaunch of the NFW and, as a result, he was named commissioner of the NFW's Southern Conference. The first person to become clase to Carlton in the relaunch was Joey Melton, the first CSWA World Champion who was Calvin's "boyhood hero." Clavin managed Melton while as the commissioner -- a controversial conflict of interest, to say the least -- but they, too, would part ways as Calvin became jealous of Melton's romantic daliances with Lindsay Troy. Carlton and his mama signed Eddy Love for the NFW. Calvin tried to partner Love and Melton together but they soon clashed and Carlton sided with Love and billed him as the NFW South's greatest attraction. Upon the end of Season 1, the NFW decided to break up the conferences in another way. Calvin's power has been stripped. Eddy Love has retired.
