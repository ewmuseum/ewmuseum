JN dba EASTPREZ PRESSURE CHIEF CAT HERDER COFFEE BOSS BigPimpin — 8 April 2014 I enjoyed this show, although it could have been better (and I lay some of that blame at my feet as well).
Would have liked to see more on the weigh-in, but Calvin is always entertaining...I can just imagine Steve Harvey dressed all in pink, and I lol.
I'm too stupid to understand the abbreviation JTTS. I get the context that it's code for jobber, but don't know what the letters stand for.
OSS/Shadowrunners did its job, moving on. Peter Windham and his Gold Star Slot thing was great, segment of the night. PBR with a well-deserved win, though I don't get the Slacknife from Windham. I guess he's just supposed to be an asshole?
I have in-character reactions to my match that will be posted, but grats to the Robbers for retaining.
Strife vs. Stryfe was the match of the night, highlighted by the wild result. Stryfe got a HUGE win, and gets to be #30, while Strife gets to be #1.
I didn't read the Pirate show yet, that feedback will come later (after rp I suspect). Random Rumble looms...
