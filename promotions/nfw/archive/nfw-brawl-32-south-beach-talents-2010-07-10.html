(FTB) High Flyer vs. Tchu DROID REPORT: High Flyer vs. Tchu (Elite Championship Interfed 1st Round Match) SUBMITTED BY: Jack Cunth Hey all, not sure if everyone knew this was going down, but High Flyer had his first match in NFW in awhile...and it just happened to be part of an interfed championship tournament to crown the vacated Elite Championship that Steve "Awesome" Knox first won way back when. Well, first things first...Flyer looked a little different. A little clean-cut and instead of walking out with a shovel, he was carrying around a Ghostbusters sign with a mask crossed out. Second things second, Flyer's made WAYYYYYYY to many enemies. Tchu and him are no stranger to each other, but Tchu may be a stranger to some NFW folk...he's from PRIME. http://prime.e-wrestling.org, if you want to check them out. Anyway, things went back and forth for awhile and started to get a little out of hand as Flyer got a little out of hand. Fortunately for Tchu, unfortunately for Flyer...not only did American Panda show up, but so did FUTUREQuis, the latter catching Flyer with a necksnap over the top rope which predicated a vicious Dragon Suplex with a bridge from Tchu to secure the victory....after Referee Scott Speranza stopped American Panda from entering the ring. Needless to say, the Elite Championship Committee can't be happy about the outside interference and in a post-match interview, neither was Tchu who chastised the NFW offices for allowing such clutter to be involved with a prestigious and global title. Erik Black vs. Brock Alyas DROID REPORT: Erik Black vs. Brock Alyas (PURE Title Contendership) SUBMITTED BY: Jack Cunth Nobody knows what was more surprising: -Brock Alyas AND Erik Black passing their PURE Title regimented Olympic Drug Tests... ... -Or Brock Alyas AND Erik Black managing to wrestle upwards of 22 minutes in a highly (no pun intended!) entertaining match. Definitely both competitors pulled out all the stops during the match as Alyas battled a significant muscle pull suffered in the Jack Bryant match in Cleveland, while Erik Black adjusted to his first REAL opponent inside the NFW squared circle. Things took a sour turn for Alyas early on when he attempted a Press Slam on the lightweight Black, but wasn't able to hold him up as his back gave out and nearly got pinned out of the situation. Black moved on the offensive, hitting quick strikes and a series of aerial highlight reel moves before suffering a coughing fit. Black trying to stabilize his lungs tried to keep Alyas in a chinlock, which got him jawjacked viciously and Alyas followed that up with a Stun Gun. Alyas kept going for the power moves, hitting a spinebuster and powerslam for near falls, but Black was able to counteract Alyas' finishing Exploder Suplex as Brock repulled his injured muscle allowing Black to hit a flipping neckbreaker. Black nailed an enziguiri and hurricanrana for a near count, then nearly iced the match with a standing ovation Frog Splash. Unfortunately, the impact sent his own lungs into disarray as he climbed the turnbuckles one more time attempting the 420 (450) Splash...only to hit solid mat canvas as Alyas rolled out of the way. Brock draped his arm over Black, getting the 3-count and a very important victory in the PURE Championship scene. Castor Strife (c) vs. Rich Mahogany RICE: Welcome back, welcome back. As if the action in Miami wasn't hot enough, the EN-EFF-DUBYU TEE-VEE TITLE is on the line when the self-proclaimed Ladies' Man, RICH MAHOGANY takes on NFW's creepiest, and only, director, the God of Snuff, CASTOR STRIFE. So I'll send you off to Kerry O'Connor and Lamont Hollywood midway through the encounter that took place earlier tonight! (BLAST OF STATIC!)
