SouthernBoy — 30 August 2002 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-30-02 AT 01:27 PM (EDT)]MEMBER INFO ---------------------- MEMBER Name: [name withheld] Email Address: [detail withheld] WRESTLER INFO ------------------- Wrestler Name: Shane Southern Height: 6'4" Weight: 245 Hailing From: New Orleans, LA Handedness: Left Gimmick: Southern boy raised on Bourbon Street in New Orleans. He’s not a red-neck per say and doesn’t play into the stereotypes, but he does speak with an fairly heavy southern accent. He's a face with a slight ego and sharpe edge to him.
Ring Attire: Long white tights, red letters PARTY'S down one leg, OVER on the left leg (super-kick leg). He wears a Steve Austin style knee brace on his right knee.
Looks: Muscular build. Long brown hair with blond streaks usually pulled back in a ponytail behind his head.
Ring Entrance: The harmonica from his theme music starts as Southern comes out to pyro. He stands there pointing at the fans for a while, then jogs down to the ring slapping hands with fans. He then goes to each turnbuckle raising his hands.
Theme Music: "AIN'T GOIN' DOWN" - Garth Brooks Tactics/Style: Technical. Risk taker at time with flying moves. 15 Moves: Lariat FaceBuster Scissors kick Side slam Standing drop kick Irish Whip followed by running back elbow Reverse DDT Knife-edge chop Cradle Pile-driver Rocker Dropper Lou Thez Press 2nd rope leg drop Tilt-a-whirl Slam Cajun (Russian) Leg Sweep Submission/Weardown holds: Abnominal Stretch Indian Death Lock Setup: Franken-Southern Bourbon-Street-Plunge (Gutwrench Powerbomb) Finisher: Party's Over Super-kick Strengths: 1) Can take a lot of punishment and go a long time in the ring 2) Very strong & agile Weaknesses: 1) Sometime moves are too dangerous and gets in trouble trying to do too much 2) He's a face - Pays too much attention to others and fans 3) Bad knee - injured in motorcycle accident. Opponents like to go after that.
