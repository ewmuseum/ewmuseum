NFW news: Vancouver tabbed for Midnight Madness Finale!

5 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Vancouver tabbed for Midnight Madness Finale! Posted by Natrone Rice on 5 Jun 2006 Tom Adler and Eddie Mayfield will...team up? Unsolved Mysteries might as well take a front row seat in Vancouver, British Columbia because if that team isn't strange enough, Eli Flair and Rook Black are getting in the ring together...and only time will tell where they're going. Oh yeah, Joey Melton squares off against KVC, the man that cost Lindsay Troy (Melton's fiance) the Hulk-A-Thon. ...if you don't count the other unsolved mysteries of that affair... Its all on the tip of the iceberg as the Western Conference will hold their regular season finale of Midnight Madness up in Canada. Names have been signed, and a partnership never conceivable will be born one night only. Tom Adler made the challenge, all other parties have accepted and now Adler/Mayfield will face Craig Miles and a mystery partner of his choice. One has to wonder, who's playing who there... That's not the only hot properties ready to rumble, as Joey Melton will literally have to fight for his woman for once, taking on the gigantic 'Kodiak' Vic Creed in a match that may throw one of these men into Lindsay Troy's playoffs bracket. Speaking of the brackets, two byes are up for grabs. The first one will be Alias vs. Scott Riktor, two friends slowly looking like enemies will certainly be looking for a victory to escape whatever first round pratfalls are lurking. Pratfalls are the least of Eli Flair and Rook Black's worries, as they'll be lucky not to get destroyed in a time warp considering their first ever match in the now defunct tSC. Not to mention that the Group D bye will be in the hands of the victor. Rounding out the card, Manpower and Nova will try and settle the question of whether human beings possess negative IQ, while Guy Boudreaux and Gaige Caldwell will undoubtedly be misunderstood by every man, woman and child in attendance.
