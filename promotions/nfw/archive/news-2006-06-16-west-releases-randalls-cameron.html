NFW news: West Releases Randalls, Cameron

16 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
West Releases Randalls, Cameron Posted by Natrone Rice on 16 Jun 2006 'The Wolf' Mike Randalls and 'The Last Angry Man' Marcus Cameron led a list of Western Conference releases leading into the regular season finale. Randalls and Cameron have been the subject of heavy debate since their grotesque rematch at the recent Wrestlestock PPV. Its a match that has left Marcus Cameron in the hospital to this day, and paralyzed a former doctor from the state of North Carolina. Meanwhile, Randalls himself has not made any contact with the media amongst 'smark' speculation over whether the situation is a work. Many dirtsheets were incredulous over the high-risk manuevers in the match, as well as Randalls' recovery time during the match. Calls for steroid and drug testing for Randalls continue, but 'The Wolf' is no longer employed by any wrestling federation anywhere in the world...some insiders are now wondering if this latest incident might've been the last straw for Randalls. NFW is still trying to work out the situation with Norman Quiry, Malik Roland and Andy Johnson, whom were fired by Craig Miles even though Miles relinquished Western Conference control earlier in the season to Quentin Sullivan.
