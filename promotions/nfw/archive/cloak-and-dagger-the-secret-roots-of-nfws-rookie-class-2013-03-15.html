LQJT86C — 15 March 2013 Cloak and Dagger: The Secret Roots of NFW’s Rookie Class Cloak and Dagger: The Secret Roots of NFW’s Rookie Class Just a little side project I'd been working on that got sideTRACKED. It started out as some story-building stuff for my own guys, but I expanded it into a feature article (in real-life tattoo/extreme culture/arts magazine BIZARRE) on some of the recent NFW rookies. Please read and leave lots of awesome feedback. If you're upset I didn't include you, well, I did put out a casting call like two months ago.
