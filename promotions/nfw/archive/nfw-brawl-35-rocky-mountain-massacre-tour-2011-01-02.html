PHIL ATKEN vs. JACK BRYANT The beginning and middle of the match featured about as much excitement as you would expect between Jack Bryant and Phil Atken. Bryant was extremely aggressive, grounding Atken and pummeling him with a simple attack featuring suplexes, wrestling takedowns, and powerful strikes. Atken spent much of the match running around the ring, using Greg Herpin as a blocker, and sneaking in nutshots. It was truly a wrestling clinic. With his pure wrestling skill failing him (pause for laughter), Phil Atken would turn to his newly anointed best friend, Bill, and an old friend, Dick Dirkwood. Dick jumped onto the apron, immediately gaining Bryant's attention, and, shortly after that, found himself flat on his back on the unforgiving concrete outside. As Herpin checked on Dirkwood, who should be up for an Academy Award given his "performance", Atken brought Bill into the ring and crept up behind Bryant. Atken, Bill's handle in hand, unleashed a mighty swing. And missed horribly. JB caught a glimpse of the bucket cutting through the air and ducked, quickly spun, and drove a heavy boot into Atken's midsection. It was now Bryant who held the bucket, which he slammed onto and over Phil's head! The scene was chaotic. Dirkwood, pretending to be hurt on the outside, Herpin sliding into the ring and looking on in horror, Atken stumbling and flailing around the ring, and Jack Bryant measuring his prey up like a hungry lion. Flyer catapulted off the turnbuckles, bounced off the top of the ropes and caught Rook with a Full Force (Hart Attack) Clothesline! Flyer went for the cover, but found himself dragged away by the returned Lord Coyner Pollard who then turned him over and delivered a Scot Stomp! (to the nads naturally) Flyer writhed in pain, while Pollard hovered over him and dropped a series of elbow drops. Legion got up slowly, shaking out the cobwebs and noticed the downed Rook and Curiosity. Legion stumbled and rushed over to them, he started gouging and raking at Rook’s eyes…he started headbutting Curiosity repeatedly until he was either dizzy or concussed, the world wasn’t sure, but as Legion stood up he seemed drunken in a happy way…for him. Too bad that Legion’s euphoric moment was noticed and then interrupted by the Lord who grabbed him frm behind and drove him into the mat with a Dragon Suplex. The impact of the move sending Legion sprawling out of the ring, possibly unconscious. Flyer struggled up with hatred in his eyes and tackled Pollard from behind, before he could take advantage of the bloodied and blinded duo Rook and Curiosity. Flyer rammed Pollard’s head into the mat a few times before getting up quickly and springboarding off the ropes with a reverse elbow smash into the downed Pollard’s back of the head! Flyer stayed on the offensive, reeling Pollard into a front facelock and completing a crowd-popping Springboard Tornado DDT! Flyer could have gone for the pin, but decided not to…instead scaling the turnbuckles, but Rook Black stumbled up and into the ropes…inadvertently spread-eagling Flyer across the top turnbuckles. Flyer tumbled over and hit the mat, once again grasping at his crotchshotted area during this match. Black picked up Curiosity, dropping him with a Uranage and went for the pinfall…the eyepatched referee only getting to a two! The ref stumbled up as did Lord Coyner Pollard…Rook looked to be trying to hook Curiosity in a Camel’s Clutch, but Pollard caught him from behind with a British Bulldog! Rook faceplanted in the mat, allowed Pollard to scale the second turnbuckles and hit a legdrop across the back of his neck. Pollard then positioning Rook for the "Americans Are Behind" (Regal Stretch) submission finisher, but instead…Curiosity hit the Schrodinger’s Smack (Shining Wizard) into the back of Pollard’s head! Rook got up seeing Curiosity go for the cover and desperately broke up the pinfall before it started. As both men got up, Rook hit his patented Gauntlet Throwdown (Chokehold STO) out of nowhere! Somehow, this invigorated High Flyer who caught Rook with an Enziguri to the face! Flyer willed Curiosity to crawl over and cover Rook Black, the crowd erupting in shock at the turn of events…the referee getting in position for the 3 count, but he got blindsided by a Legion shove. Unfortunately, Flyer was ensconced with Curiosity finally covering Rook that he also never saw Legion…wielding a metal plate knucks as he pulverized Flyer in the back of the head. And thusly, Legion…the last man standing arranged his final presentation for the evening. Whispering something ominous in Flyer’s ears, he then proceeded to drape Rook Black, Doc Curiosity and Lord Coyner Pollard over Flyer…a picturesque snow angel three-count later and the man of Chaos had succeeded. Three men had pinned Flyer, a champion and two challengers. The announcer stating such and to the increasing crowd's shock stating that the referee has decided to leave the decision in the hands of the NFW Executive Branch. The National Guardsmen stormed the ring, pulling out Rook Black before anything further could happen to him… Jeeves and Eegor pulled out their respective employers. The ref saved Flyer and Legion stood there grinning menacingly wishing all…a devilishly good night and new year.
