EastPrez — 24 September 2013 Quick update: I'm hoping to have 17 in the can by the beginning of next week, maybe sooner. As of now I'm suspending the HEADSHOT! format and RELOADED will be Classic Coke. During the wait, I would like all active members to give me a '5-Card Plan' which will basically get your guys to RANDOM RUMBLE.
This well help me see where you're forecasting your characters for this quarter, and a great jumping-on place for us to all begin working together. After I collect this data, I will be reaching out to all of you to talk about your plans and we'll make it all work out.
I am also thinking hard about reconstituting BRAWLS with handler-writing priveledges (!) and any of your plans can shorten out for BRAWLS. Again, not sure about adding them yet, but we'll see once we find our rhythm. My plan is getting cards out quickly --- adding BRAWLS is to 'thicken the story soup' on the way to RANDOM RUMBLE, not drag out the year.
So consider the phrase '5-cards' to mean "what are your guys doing leading up to RR". So your 5-Cards will be: RELOADED 18 BRAWL 66 RELOADED 19 BRAWL 67 BRAWL 68 RELOADED XX BRAWL 69 RANDOM RUMBLE So, please send all that stuff to my email box, not here - unless you're open to sharing your ideas with the 'boys' and we can go from there.
