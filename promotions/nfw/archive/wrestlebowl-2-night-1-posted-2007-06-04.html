Legion — 5 June 2007 Well I thought I give comments on one particular match (not that I haven't read the rest of the card, just need to read a couple of things again to give a more in depth analysis)
Since the inclusion of Miles into the missed FWO I've always been intrigued with his character particularly with the added suspense of who kidnapped Nixon during the Deacon angle which was finally revealed at Crashmas 4 to be Eddie, of course the
added players in Avery, Mystic etc led me to believe at the time that it was part of a power struggle mixed with Mayfield's sense of redemption over what he believes to be thievery by Miles of his legacy.
Throughout this season we've seen a new side of Miles (or as some people may say the ONLY side) as his obsession to get the man who made his life a living hell and forced him to fight a man of faith has led him to alienate himself from nearly everyone... only Eli, Ivy standing by him.... however what only the Wolf knew started to show it's true colours...
Wrestlebowl II in Dallas... nearly 2 hours of hell (and god knows how much time to write the thing.. much kudos!) and after a valiant effort the biggest change in the landscape of NFW had begun.
All I've got to say is... Read the match and be strapped in because it looks like it's going to be a even crazier ride (though one big question... Can this unit be more manic than the 7th Seal or have we entered the deepest circle of hell...) :-)

Nova — 25 June 2007 Well, I got through Night One in time for Night Two, which was my goal. I have to say, thank God for Miles/Mayfield on this card. It really gave the show the epic feel that I thought it needed. Oftentimes with "two-night" events I think way too much emphasis is placed on the second night as the finale and the first night as the warm-up, and while I think to a degree that's still true in this case, the sheer volume and heinousness (is that a word?) of Miles/Mayfield, along with the swerve at the end (which I agree with Tom was masterful) really helped Night One stand on its own. I also thought the warm-up to the match was a great summation of some of the characters' highlights and storyline turns leading up to this, and really helped me as a non-informed reader to understand just how deep this feud runs...or ran.
Seeing the free-for-all tag mayhem just made me happy, mostly because of the appearance of the Rogues but also just because it appears that the tag division will be quite hot come NFW 3.0's kickstart, and ain't a damn thing wrong with that. Renner has been a huge part of PRIME's tag resurgence, and if he has the same effect here with the writers assembling to help out, and if the Professionals are really actually going to be a tag team, then I think the tag division will really be competing with the singles for attention, and I haven't seen that happen...in ever.
Eli/Ryan is another great addition to what will surely be a monumental show in the fed's history. I don't get to be Flair's last match like I thought, but everyone benefits as a result.:D
I wanna wrestle dangerous monkeys. That is all.
