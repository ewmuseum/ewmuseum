NFW news: Impact of 'REMEMBER THE ALAMO'

2 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Impact of 'REMEMBER THE ALAMO' Posted by Devin Millwood on 2 May 2011 The Windham Clan survived the in-fighting and disarray surrounding All the President’s Men at SUPERCRASH 3, but the real question now is at WHAT COST? On the heels of what should have been their greatest victory to date, President Eddie Mayfield imploded the celebration unveiling another ace up his sleeve as TSUNAMI and MIKE RANDALLS ravaged the whole clan in a brutal sneak attack that left JJ Deville and Troy Windham in the hospital overnight. Not only were those two part of Mayfield’s plan, but as PURE Champion Bobby Jack Windham tried to escape – none other than DEACON showed up, walking through a blast of pyrotechnic fire to put down the self-professed evangelical man of purity. In the main event, ALL THE PRESIDENT’s MEN saw 50% of New Frontier go relatively up in smoke to the Windham Clan due to the actions and consequences around DORCHESTER STRATTON’s time in the ring. Stratton, the notorious rulebreaker that ended the career of Tsunami’s best friend, Wildstar – refused to tag anyone in NFW and when saved from the clutches of Dan Ryan by Eddie Mayfield, he turned on the President ‘causing him to be pinned by Ryan…and then got himself DISQUALIFIED moments later. This put the NFWites at a disadvantage and while Impulse courageously eliminated Dan Ryan and Castor Strife exorcised a few demons in pinning Troy Windham – JJ DEVILLE came out of nowhere to secure the final pinfall on Strife with Strife’s own finisher. Other debuts in the night revolved primarily around the TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP ladder match, where JACK BRYANT was able to defeat the likes of High Flyer, Legion and the newly onboarded STALKER with his mysterious MR. MYERS subordinate…as well as a return from former CO-TV Champion and GLOBAL superstar, SHAWN HART! Bryant and Hart battled on the hook for the belt, Bryant hitting a low-blow on the Jiggy Master to disengage him and win the belt for himself. Bryant was then served up with a Double-Arm DDT by Stalker onto a ladder. Meanwhile, HIGH FLYER or THIRTEEN as we came to realize…wrestled for nearly 90 minutes in FOUR matches trying to overcome the apparent blackmail methods of LEGION to secure both the aforementioned Television Championship as well as the NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP. Flyer as THIRTEEN was able to defeat Rook Black with the help of Legion…under the circumstances that Flyer would repay Legion later in the night. When Flyer failed to do so, Legion unraveled the plot of THIRTEEN first costing Flyer the National Championship against Doctor Curiosity…then forcing him to unveil himself as he SCREWJOBBED Curiosity out of the National Championship in the IMPROMPTU TRIPLE THREAT rematch with ROOK BLACK – leaving BLACK as a TWO-TIME NATIONAL CHAMPION…and walking out of the ALAMODOME with the belt he fought so hard to protect. And what about BANDIT’s awful night? First, in a COUNTOUT victory over the HOLLYWOOD WRECKING CREW in the EMT Title match – he was DITCHED by his partner PETER WINDHAM, leaving him open to a vicious assault by Calvin Carlton, Malik Anderson and Blaine Hollywood. Then, later in the night – MIKE RANDALLS possibly tore his arm apart and Jack Bryant later added insult to injury. This man can’t buy a break! So, where are we now? NOBODY KNOWS. The Windham Clan have received paperwork for their 50% share in the company as EDDIE MAYFIELD and the rest of NFW obviously prepare for an unsettling future. But as MAYFIELD alluded to – Randalls, Tsunami, Deacon, Shawn Hart and Stalker were only the BEGINNING of a rash of open-ended contracts he sent out GLOBALLY. Rumors are swirling around possible returns, possible people Eddie’s bringing in…as well as possible men that the WINDHAM CLAN will want to bring in. Only time will tell how this plays out, but in the eyes of many NFW FANS – there’s rarely been a better time to be watching it all play out.
