[end quote] Echo (Echo-co-co) BigPimpin — 9 January 2014 I'll have more detailed comments in this space later, but I will say that, speaking as a wrestling fan, that Ocho/Legion ending, combined with the Brawlsgiving Fan Feast & Related Events, made me hot to see the Reloaded XX undercard.
Legion — 9 January 2014 A great show leading to Reloaded - the Black Market getting a fair amount of enemies, good to see the Shadowrunners carrying out their warning from a fair while ago and gives a twist that Legion can use in his recent theories with Doug Mayfield being part of it all.
Fun interview with Bryant and good to see the Sons Of Liberty team making an appearance! Biron — 10 January 2014 GET READY FOR RINGSIDE - Good call on Daft Punk. The animation bit is solid. I’m really enjoying the WENDY DREWES / TED CALDWELLER partnership - seems very genuine, easy. I can see/hear them going back and forth with each other. As somebody else I was talking to said … “these people are real” and it’s so true - all our characters are 3-D. Anyhow, big props on quickly and successfully developing these co-anchors.
FAN FEAST - GREAT SEGMENT. So much small, fun stuff happening here - sweet Random Rumble tees, Langston w/ the FIREBALLS, Mayfield and Fiona interaction. Oh, and how about MAYFIELD/LAMONT. That was hilarious. Tip of the cap to whoever dreamt that bit up. Of course, PETER WINDHAM crashes the party and that whole sequence was tremendous. I liked LDO laughing at MAYFIELD and then getting payback from the SECRET SERVICE! Really glad to see LANE CASH bust in and attack KUROYAMA after he chased PETER into the parking lot. Lane’s becoming such a world-class douche. Segment ends w/ Peter flipping everybody the bird from the protection of Lane’s limo! Gold.
QUICK MATCH RECAP feat. HOLLYWOOD and ANDERSON - EPIC DEBUT, BRO. I’ve heard some about JAMES JOHNNY JONES and, after reading this, I’m a fan. I want to see more from him. Good to see BLAINE pick up more steam - as I’ve said before, I like Blaine and want to see him featured more. Same goes for MALIK … he needed a win and BARTOLO ROJAS came across as legit to me. Good matches for recaps.
SONS OF LIBERTY v. THE PILGRIM EXPRESS - Now, this wouldn’t be a good Thanksgiving show without JEBEDIAH and OBADIAH - they worked great for a one-time team. We haven’t seen Eddie in action much so this was good. Mayfield/Mercado is a fun team. Really good line from OBI/JEB about Mayfield throwing fire from his hands (and being a devil worshipper). This was actually a solid match and I LOVED the fact that LONE WOLF comes down and wipes the Pilgrims out. NOW that’s a Thanksgiving show. Good to get the SOL a win.
EastPrez — 10 January 2014 [quoting] LQJT86C said: > You guys are putting these out faster than I can read them! I've gotta do some catching up, haha. NFW's on fire.
[end quote] Biron — 11 January 2014 PETER WINDHAM’S COMMISSIONER CHALLENGE AGAINST XAVIER LANGSTON I think I mentioned in the B68 thread that I was looking forward to seeing more between Langston and Commissioner Peter Windham - I still shake my head when I think about him as Commish (he’s such a creep … and it’s so awesome). This one actually picked up at the FAN FEAST, where Langston potatoes (I think) dumped all over him. Funny move to have him get the jump on Peter by beating him out to the ring. Really solid match w/ Langston looking strong before a Windham comeback. There’s a lot of potential for fun here. I want to see where it goes from here.
120 SECONDS w/ CALVIN CARLTON Dug this segment … love that Wendy keeps muting Calvin and what a kick Caldweller gets from it. Some good rips, but I think the closer on Shaniqua was best. I legit LOL’d when Calvin started to call Wendy Drewes a “slampig”. I can’t wait to see what kind of mischief the Harmen/Calvin connection gets into.
QUICK MATCH RECAP w/ JACK BRYANT and STEEL VIPER Try to avoid talking about my own characters too much, but I like the mindset and aggressiveness that JB has shown in the past few weeks. He’s almost in the same mindset as when he was chasing DEVILLE around. Love the HORSECOLLAR DRIVER and the follow-up reaction.
