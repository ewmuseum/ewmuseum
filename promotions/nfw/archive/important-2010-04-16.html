User Poets — 16 April 2010 Katz would probably do this but I don't care, I am. For the new folks, and the old heads who forgot - if you're not booked for one of the three shows that DOES NOT MEAN you're not able to be on it.
Just post an RP in the thread and you'll get booked. Seriously. jediPREZ — 16 April 2010 Thanks for the reminder, Mr. Pete. We're slightly changing things up this go-round, where the best RP/seg from someone not booked is automatically booked to a win/squash. All other RP's/segs will be used to set up a match on that exact show or a subsequent one on the mini-tours.
Also, if you're booked on Cincinnati...feel free to RP on Philly or Detroit and vice-versa for all 3 shows. There's no limitations in that regard, except for the amount of RP's/segs you'll be able to do.
Just so everyone understands the perspective around it: If you remember old NWA telecasts, wrestlers would promote upcoming house shows whether they were on the shows or not. You'd see alot of cool collaboration/developments through that. So our goal is to try and mimic that feel/attitude.
