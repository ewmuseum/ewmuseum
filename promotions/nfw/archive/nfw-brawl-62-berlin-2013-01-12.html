Nothing happens, and the music cuts out to more booing) H’WOOD: (shaking his head) "Pathetic! You know Castor, this isn’t McDonald’s where you can just no-call/no-show because the night manager took your Thursday shift away! Sorry everyone, I guess that’s it for BRAWL in Berlin. At least you got to see me for the first time..." (CUTTO: The entrance, where ALEX AUSTIN, LUCKY SEVEN, and STEEL VIPER walk slowly to the ring) H’WOOD: "No! Go back! Nobody wants to hear from the help!" (Austin climbs into the ring, Seven jumps over, and Viper steps over as Lamont backs away from them) H’WOOD: "I SAID GO BACK! You know what, on second thought GET IN HERE! I want to know what the hell you have to say for him." (Austin calls for a mic, and one is tossed to him from ringside) H’WOOD: "Go ahead ham sandwich, say what you gotta say!" AUSTIN: (pauses, steps out and takes stock of the arena) "My name is Alex Austin, and I am a student of Castor Strife." H’WOOD: "Good for you! Now why don’t-" (Steel Viper SNATCHES the mic away!) AUSTIN: "You said your peace, now we say ours. As I’m sure you’ve noticed, Castor has not made it to Berlin. He is with us in spirit, but not in the flesh, and for that I apologize." (Boos) AUSTIN: "But the fact is, you people needed a reminder of what this place was like before Castor Strife hitched it to his coat tails." "Let me take you back to a MAGICAL time...when the World Title was sitting on Eddie Mayfield’s shelf between his PS3 and a picture of Anne Mystic. When the supposed ‘Champion’ was padding his record with gauntlet victories involving James von Doom and out of shape Ultima junkies. When all the talent had dried up, and Cameron Cruise was chucking ladders at Impulse in the main event. When the so-called best wrestler in the world was moaning into the microphone wiping his ass with toilet paper on national television." "It was all a really nice corporate attempt at building a ‘rebel’, but in reality, there has been only one man with the guts and vision to stray from the path for the betterment of New Frontier Wrestling, and his name is CASTOR V. STRIFE." (Cheers and boos) AUSTIN: "So it makes me sick, literally makes me ILL (grabs at stomach) when I see these fan polls naming Jack Bryant your favorite wrestler. Really? REALLY? Hey, no offense to Jack Bryant, he’s a nice guy, but besides being Fiona Love’s personal clam lapper, Jacky boy hasn’t done a hell of a lot to move the pendulum around here. By the way Jack, Viper says ‘YOU’RE WELCOME!’" (Viper nods his head up and down) AUSTIN: "Eddie likes to peddle this fantasy, that it was him who cleaned this place up and made it a showcase for the best talent in the world, but that’s no more true than saying people find parking violations and overdue paychecks entertaining. At least JJ was TRYING to screw this place up. What’s everybody else’s excuse? Castor took this league’s banner and FLEW IT WORLDWIDE! He took the World Title, and RESTORED IT to respectability! The only reason we’re here right now, in Germany, is because Castor created that demand out of thin F[BLEEP]KING air!" (Many in the crowd are still cheering at the mention of Castor’s name, but Austin is wearing on their patience) AUSTIN: "When JJ was screwing with payroll, it was CASTOR who stepped in and got you all paid! It was CASTOR who got Jack Bryant that deal with Gatorade! CASTOR landed Superfly on the cover of High Times! CASTOR got us that Supercrash front page story with the LA Times while Eddie sat on his ass acting like he was too good to promote his own brand!" (Lamont walks over to Viper’s mic and quickly snags it away, immediately backing up. Viper steps over to him, but Austin holds him back) H’WOOD: "Hey, correct me if I’m wrong, but weren’t you the guy who turned your back on Castor in the first place?" (BIG BOOS!) AUSTIN: (purses lips; nods) "It’s true...I stabbed him in the back. The man who plucked me from obscurity, when I was sleeping in my car at the edge of the water in Coon Rapids, Minnesota, with a trunk full of submission grappling titles and canned food. He made brought me to the big stage, AND I BETRAYED HIM! And I’ll have to live with that regret for the rest of my life. But while lesser men would have relegated me to the dustbin, Castor took me back made me into the disciple I am today. That’s the WISDOM of the man...the VISION that only he has." "Yeah, I once helped forge his signature on a contract that eventually handed his company over to Eric Dane." "But if I could do it differently, I would have found a way right now to forge his signature on the CSWA contract that’s sitting on his desk, so he could get the payday and RESPECT he deserves!" (Boos!) AUSTIN: "And as much as he wanted to be here tonight, we all stepped in, me, Seven, Viper, Mother Lana, and prevented him from boarding that plane. After all he’s given to you, we will not watch him give any more." "We’ll see to it that he leverages the CSWA deal to land in EPW, where he, Sean Stevens, and Anarky CAN PULL THE HOUSE DOWN EVERY WEEK! (boos) Or arrives in DEFIANCE to help them reach that next level of greatness. Impulse, we appreciate the rematch offer, and you’re a hell of a wrestler, but as far as the Guild is concerned...you can go surf the World Title up your girlfriend’s ass." (Boos!) AUSTIN: "But in his infinite love for NFW, which I will never truly understand, Castor would want to see the Guild remain here and finish his last act in the Frontier: to destroy JJ Deville." (Some cheers mixed in with boos) AUSTIN: "That is my penance. I will stay here with my good friends and fellow Disciples of Strife, and do the will of the One True Visionary. I will be the one who toils and suffers, not him. His suffering is over." "Castor needs not show himself any longer. HE IS BIGGER THAN THE GAME! From the moment of his return four years ago, he has consistently and accurately predicted all events. He predicted the rise and fall of the Windham Clan! He told us of his title run years before it happened." (CUTTO: LUCKY SEVEN pulling at her own hair, staring up at the rafters) AUSTIN: "He walked into foreign organizations and won their biggest prize on threat of retirement if he failed. He foretold the events of Ultratitle, and won that too. Told Eric Dane EXACTLY what would happen to him after Supercrash, and it went accordingly." (CUTTO: Viper staring out at the crowd through a gasmask; BACK to Austin) AUSTIN: "He told us YEARS AGO of the metamorphosis he was going to impose on Impulse. Castor knew going into Perth, that the Cutting Room Floor could very well be the end of his reign. (smiles) How fitting that Impulse would be denied the title in a clean match, but later realize his dream through blood, glass, and splinter, in the most unclean fashion." "To defeat the monster of his nightmares, Impulse had to become what he feared. Congratulations, Impulse. You graduated with honors. He wanted me to tell you that." (Austin walks in a semi-circle around the ring) AUSTIN: "Do you people even know what I’m saying? Do you understand my words? English, German, it’s all the same. You’ll never really know, because you’re not one of us. You never got to know the man. The wisdom he shared with us pales in comparison to what he gave you. And just when you figured out what you had...it was GONE. A diamond in the mouth of a skull." "Look, I know there’s no future for me here, especially after tonight. And that’s fine. I’m not in this for myself. I am nothing, no one. I am the manifestation of my master’s will. I am his mouth, I am his fist." H’WOOD: "You’re sick, kid. You really are! Why don’t you go back there and figure out HOW TO GET OVER, because this? This ain’t it! It ain’t cuttin’ it!" (Austin walks up to Lamont’s face) AUSTIN: (whispers) "I’m not in it for ME, Lamont. We’re not in it for WE. It’s all for HIM. Got it?" H’WOOD: "Yeah, I got it." AUSTIN: "I don’t think you do. I think you’re nothing but the father of Blaine, a marked man as far as the disciples are concerned." H’WOOD: "Hey, stay away from me! It’s in my contract – I’m not to be touched!" AUSTIN: "Do you want to know what he said to me on his hospital bed? With glass STUCK in his skin like shrapnel from a bomb. With splinters lodged in his face, Lamont. He said, ‘Alex, my brother...’, and he pulled me close to him. And I said, ‘Castor, what would you have me do? Your beloved NFW, what would you have me do in your absence?’ (Lamont is slowly backing away) AUSTIN: "And you know what he said to me?" (Whispers) "KILL" (pulls a necklace from his shirt) "THEM" (on it hangs a rectangular shard of glass) "ALL!"
