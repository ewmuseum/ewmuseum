WRESTLERS' INFORMATION: TEAM NAME: CUATRO EQUIS Individual Names: Dos Equis and Futurequis Individual Nicknames: The Drunken Luchador and That Really Pissed Off Guy From The Future Who Barely Resembles Dos Equis
Heights: 6-0 and 6-2 Weights: 95.5 kilos (210 pounds) and 245 pounds Handedness: Right (Both) Looks: Similarities: Both wear a green, gold and black mask, with two red X's on the forehead of the mask. Matching body suits/boots.
Differences: Dos had longer brown hair poking out the bottom of his mask; Futurequis has shorter blood red hair poking out. Dos has brown eyes; Futurequis has green eyes. Dos has a beer belly, where Futurequis is solid. Dos Equis wears a gold cape to the ring, but takes it off before wrestling. Futurequis sports a UPC code tattoo on his arm and heavily tapes his wrists and fists.
Hailing From: Luchador and Austin, Texas circa 2019. Disposition: Futurequis veers toward heeldom, while Dos Equis seeks facedom. Gimmick: Abusive relationship of sorts, where the fun-loving Dos is the victim of his future, angrier self. Futurequis claims to be Dos Equis from the year 2019, and is trying to toughen up the fun-loving, alcohol abusing NFW rookie.
Theme Music: "All Nightmare Long" by Metallica Ring Entrance: Depends on what's going on storyline wise. Maybe they're arguing. Maybe Dos is slapping hands with the fans while Futurequis ignores them all. The match writer can surprise me. The only key is that they will never be friendly with each other.
Individual and Team Tactics/Style: Dos tends to do crowd popping spots and luchador-based moves, whereas Futurequis is more about brawling and injuring his opponents body parts. As for offense, think Hardy Boyz/Edge & Christian, except with Dos doing the high spots and Futurequis being the launching pad or set-up man.
Individual and Team Strengths: Dos Equis: Uses his body without regard for his own health, beer dulls the pain, innovative wrestling moves. Futurequis: Ass kicker, psychology, sadistic tendencies. Team: Cool moves/spots; they're in synch with each other and always know what the other is thinking; ability to absorb a lot of punishment.
Individual and Team Weaknessess: Dos Equis: Tendency to injure self, liver angry, potential for ugly botched spots. Futurequis: Distracting to Dos Equis, selfish, easily angered. They don't get along; risk of injury due to their movesets/style; not a lot of strength to combat bigger opponents.
MATCHWRITING GUIDE: Dos Equis: Springboard leg drop; Asai hip toss; Shooting Star DDT; Beersteiner (huricanrana); Alabama Slammer (XX holds his opponent as if for a slam, but first spins around airplane spin style, then eventually hits a very dizzying powerslam); 0.21 - Tiger feint kick to the head of an opponent draped on the second rope. It's essentially a 619, but with a name that is based on his Blood Alcohol Content rather than an area code; Screwdriver - Piledriver out of suplex position, (a.k.a., Steiner Screwdriver); Carbomb - A swanton bomb; Ghetto Hell - enzugeri; Guinness Punch - A springboard corkscrew punch; Jagerbeer Bomb - Spinning sitout powerbomb; Adios Motherfucker 2 - A gut buster from the top rope; Angel's Wing - lifting double underhook facebuster
Finisher & Set-Up Moves: Bulimia Nervosa Bomb (Diving Double Stomp to Belly)/Hangover (Double Underhook Brainbuster)
Futurequis: Clockstopper (Deathlock/chinlock combo); Future Shock (Styles Clash); Xtreme Xecution (Ace Crusher); swinging neckbreaker; Russian leg sweep; drop toe hold onto ropes or a weapon; inverted death valley driver; DDT/implant DDT; brawling around the ring/in audience; Austin crab; backbreaker.
Finisher: Chaos Theory (inverted brainbuster) Team: Doomsday Beersteiner (Futurequis puts opponent on shoulder, Dos comes off the top with a rana; double drunkkick ("drunk" dropkicks); liver attack (Futurequis holds an opponent over his knee for Equis to jump down with a double stomp or knee to the back); Dos uses Futurequis' back while he's on hands and knees as a launching pad to hit various moves on opponents in the corner or to the floor. Futurequis drop toe holds opponent onto rope for Dos to hit the 0.21. Whatever other cool/fun combos the matchwriter comes up with.
Finisher: 4X - Tandem Xtreme Xecution (Dos Equis lifts the opponent up and then falls backward as if for a flapjack as Futurequis hits a simultaneous Xtreme Xecution, aka Dudley Death Drop).
