AFTERSHOCK Spring Training Road Report, 5 May 2014

5 May 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally recorded on May 5, 2014. . . (FADEIN: Coming back from a previous commercial the spacey intro to ‘FIRE AROUND’ by RICH ROBINSON accompanies various GETTY IMAGES wire photos of NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING in action - CANCER and JACK HARMEN sitting alone in a locker room, BLAINE HOLLYWOOD and LAMONT HOLLYWOOD exiting a limousin, PRESIDENT EDDIE MAYFIELD meeting with local news reporters, KERRY KUROYAMA signing autographs, posters and t-shirts for excited NFW FANS granted backstage access before a show, PETER WINDHAM making an impassioned speech to DIRTY DEEDS at the guerilla position, but LANE CASH is more focused down the valleys of both BEAUTIFUL BLONDES cleavage. As the song kicks into gear, we actually see a few highlights of ALEX AUSTIN delivering a half-nelson suplex to a wrestler, the ring and show at a converted ice rink! THE BLACK MARKET and the CHROMATIC DRAGONS are brawling to a packed-to-the-gills arena going wild. . . ‘CAUSE HERE COMES TEDDY ALEXANDER!) WENDY DREWES: (V/O) “Welcome back to ESEN RINGSIDE and our special hour-long Monday Night edition, where we’re catching back up on WRESTLING WORLD WIDE!” (CUTTO: ESEN STUDIOS -- The music softens to a faint background wash as the cameras are focused on a front-facing shot of the ESEN RINGSIDE studio desk where WENDY DREWES and TED CALDWELLER sit across from each other. Behind them is the mammoth flatscreen plasma television inlayed into a cherry-oak wall backdrop etched with diamond cut-outs. The plasma wall flashes the NFW CRIMSON STAR logo spinning in a repeating animation, while various photos of today’s and yesterday’s iconic ESEN-based superstars and events are hung around the wooden backdrop including ROOK BLACK holding up the inaugural TRIPLE CROWN TRIFORCE championship, FRANKIE FARGO and BRANDON MUELLER crushing beer cans together on the flatbed of their circa 2002 MISSISIPPI MOUNTAIN QUEEN monster truck! KAZUO SHIZAKI and DAN RYAN trying to gain an advantage on each other with the NFW NORTH banner hanging above the ring, XAVIER LANGSTON bicycle kicking PETER WINDHAM at RANDOM RUMBLE 2014, CASTOR STRIFE bloodied and arms up in victory as fireworks explode around the FUTURESHOCK 2 BANNER, and JACK HARMEN kneeling in front of CALVIN CARLTON thrusting up the NFW WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP towards the sky like it’s Simba. Animated red and white LED lights blip around the back wall, while a top strip is reserved for a ESEN NEWS ticker report on various wrestling and MMA federations. DREWES has her blonde hair downed, but straightened from her normally wavy locks, she’s also wearing a red shirt underneath a black blazer with no glasses on for this show. CALDWELLER is sporting a white shirt with bubble-gum pink, vertical stripes as well as a forest green sports coat with no tie. He’s got a little stubble going, while his short, curly black hair is gelled down as best possible) DREWES: “Certainly we wouldn’t be paying the bills, drumming up interest in the advertisers as well as satiating the sales and marketing staff here at ESEN if we didn’t at least start checking back in on the NEW FRONTIER. Traversing through AMERICA’S HEARTLAND as the FRONT OFFICE starts shaping that course starting in WICHITA, KANSAS. The road to FUTURESHOCK TWO is a challenge within itself, but as contract negotiations and injuries swirl around this locker room - we’re really seeing some of these guys wanting to KEEP a spot on the roster being put through the paces.” CALDWELLER: “Over the last several years, it’s an approach that NFW has adapted within it’s business model, they’ve spread out the big shows and because a lot of wrestlers are trying to compete across multiple platforms while also making contract demands -- well, you really want to find out who’s going to wrestle for your banner in front of 800 fans, let alone 80,000 at FUTURESHOCK 2.” DREWES: “You can really track this back to when the ULTRATITLE was kickstarted by Chad Merritt under the ruse a global tournament.” CALDWELLER: “Yup, that definitely saw wrestlers avoiding non-compete contract clauses, which has definitely been something NEW FRONTIER continues to battle with today’s talent on. It’s also what’s had them labeled as misguided elitists by industry insiders, critics and even fellow federation competitors.” DREWES: “I found it interesting that instead of trying to develop a global partnership with peers, they’ve moved more money to the developmental UWA.” CALDWELLER: “Certainly a lot of developmental talent can be seen on these 800 person shows. . .like Norfolk and Fremont, Nebraska.” DREWES: “That’s right, just last night NEW FRONTIER sold out Omaha, Nebraska’s CenturyLink Arena with 18,000 fans, but the two previous weekends they were holding shows in a converted ice rink and a convention center.” CALDWELLER: “There’s a payoff to getting on this tour and with as many injuries or departures as NEW FRONTIER will see from larger event fall-outs like the Random Rumble, this is the best way to move up the secondary title ranks as well as position yourself onto the main roster for RELOADED and BRAWL shows leading up to the big daddy of FUTURESHOCK.” (SFX: The piercing shrill of a 2400 BPS DIAL-UP MODEM CONNECTION!) DREWES: “And speaking of BRAWL, that’s the sound we love to hear at ESEN RINGSIDE as it means we are CONNECTED to the Captain on the Bridge of the BRAWLOPTIMUS PRIME ENTERPRISE - NATRONE RICE!” (UPPER-LEFT PICTURE INSET: A profile shot of NATRONE RICE smiling. . .) NATRONE RICE: (V/O) “HELLO WENDY! HELLO TED!” CALDWELLER: “I assume you learned a lot corn this month, Natrone!” RICE: (V/O) “CORN AND OSBORNE - that should be this state’s motto, but I’ve loved every minute of it! It’s been a real treat to get on the road, get out in America’s Heartland, the BLACK AND RED CORNHUSKER state has really treated us well!” DREWES: “So before we get to the big Omaha show last night, Natrone . . . we kinda opened this up going back to the types of changes we’ve been seeing out of New Frontier the last year. We’re in 2014 and if you go back to 2004, this was what New Frontier got away from once they entered the global market -- staying out on local tour stops, not needing the television cameras, but still testing this roster and who’s committed to the craft as well as sold that NFW is where they want to hone it.” RICE: (V/O) “No doubt, that’s what’s been great about the continuing increase in NFW BRAWL’s popularity, as we started to get away from the ULTRATITLE season format we’ve had to find our way the last several years in where to put the BIG MONEY matches and how to develop the BIG MONEY talent. Sure, everyone knows about the UWA, but not everyone gets those opportunities -- touring America like this, or even Europe like last year gets us to check in on local talent while saving those costs for the three-hour RELOADED supercards or mega-events like RANDOM RUMBLE which pretty much go from day to night! Now, the BRAWLS have gotten on television much more consistently since 2010, I still remember when any house show was a BRAWL!” CALDWELLER: “So, the television cameras aren’t going out to the Fremont Ice Arena, or the DeVent Center at the Divots Conference Center. . .so, I don’t get to see the tapes which makes me appreciate the live shows plenty, but Natrone who are some you consider surprises to see out on the road and putting the time in? No doubt, alot of these young guys expect to get on television from day one here.” RICE: (V/O) “No doubt, there’s alot of EXPECTATION from anyone that signs here, just as much as there is from the fans on ANY night we put on a show. . . and the FRONT OFFICE from Fiona to Eddie, they want to see how BAD you want to shine here. You’ve never lived until you see Eddie Mayfield throwing out the first pitch at the Fremont Fast-Pitch Softball League playoffs during the day, then him scouting a team like ILL FORTUNE at night, but jetting off a day ahead to Omaha to meet up with the Mayor and do a promotional appearance with him, Mike Rozier and Turner Gil. Back to ILL FORTUNE to answer the original question, Ted. . .” DREWES: “Are you sure you don’t want to talk about Eddie’s Heisman pose in that Instagram photo?” RICE: (V/O) “HA! Well, Ted got in his question first!” CALDWELLER: “Austin’s a hothead, this is somewhat surprising to hear.” RICE: (V/O) “Well, ALEX AUSTIN and LUCKY SEVEN wanted to leave their mark at the RANDOM RUMBLE and didn’t accomplish that as much as they liked, so members of the GUILD. . . swallowed their pride and got out on that road. They competed against local competition in Fremont, they met up with the SICILIAN SWAT TEAM in Norfolk -- dominated both matches for a victory, but more importantly they got on the mic and made it known to both THE GRAVEROBBERS and the THE CHROMATIC DRAGONS that they shouldn’t get comfortable with their current rivalry. And Austin certainly trolled these towns, but made it a point to remark that ILL FORTUNE weren’t going to sit idly by at that series getting hyped, they’ll SHOWCASE and SHOW UP everywhere until they get heard.” DREWES: “Speaking of the Guild, any word or action from the winner of the 2014 Random Rumble, CASTOR STRIFE?” RICE: (V/O) “Well, I guess we can go with some words. CASTOR didn’t make an appearance, but did leave videotaped promos to play at each stop, pretty much gloating about being the best on the planet and that this new alliance between Jack Harmen and Cancer cannot stop his march to Futureshock.” CALDWELLER: “I’ve really found it surprising that we haven’t seen Castor, Harmen or Cancer address anything through an extended interview or promo through NFW Films thus far.” RICE: (V/O) “Well, if anyone just saw how Jack Bryant’s contract negotiations ended, I suppose they should be a little more diligent by the time we get to the starting point of the ROAD TO FUTURESHOCK.” DREWES: “So, there were about 800 people at the Fremont Ice Arena, we jacked it up to 2500 at the Devent Center at Norfolk’s Divots resort and conference center -- So Natrone, for those small-town fans, were there any other highlights you thought were interesting or newsworthy? RICE: (V/O) “Would Mike Randalls’ return to the squared circle after a year-long absence count?” DREWES: “HELL YEAH!” RICE: (V/O) “Well, it wasn’t a marquee matchup or a challenge against his sworn enemies, but Randalls being Randalls. . . decided a tune-up match against local competitors isn’t a tune-up match unless it’s a HANDICAP match. Randalls went 1-on-2 and it’s clear that he’s had to adapt his style after the injuries sustained in 2013. Nevertheless, he’s certainly acting in a ruthless capacity even by his standards. Randalls got the win via submission, but after the match things got REAL INTERESTING as DORCHESTER STRATTON came out on stage looking like he’d just played 36 on the luxury golf course and proceeded to promise Randalls that he’d send him packing from the sport for good. Before Randalls could get any words back at Dorchester, BLAINE HOLLYWOOD came out from the other curtain claiming he was the one that injured Randalls and that rather than focusing on a uneducated primate, he should be worrying about Blaine coming after this TELEVISION TITLE. Of course as they started mouthing off further at each other, they realized Randalls was halfway towards them and retreated immediately. DREWES: “That’s going to get real interesting, considering that there are men like Legion calling out Dorchester as well.” RICE: (V/O) “And that’s a great segueway into the big show at Omaha that brought the tour of the Cornhusker state to a dramatic close.” DREWES: “How so?” RICE: (V/O) “Well, even Legion was in for a surprise later in the night, but it was quite the scene to START the show. Blaine Hollywood and his father, manager, mentor and advisor, LAMONT HOLLYWOOD arrived in style, but had their limousine parked in such a way that it was delaying Dorchester Stratton and Veronica Abrams Rumsfeld’s arrival.” CALDWELLER: “I can start putting two and two together here. . .” RICE: (V/O) “Well, hold on Ted ‘cause the equation may be going CALCULUS style. Dorchester exited his limo and started yelling at the HOLLYWOOD ROYALTY driver, which prompted Blaine to return against his father’s wishes and to open the show - OMAHA was treated to an impromptu parking lot brawl between the long-time stable-mates, team partners and hedge fund investors! Armanis were torn! Gelled hair was tousled! (DREWES laughs) But the BRAWL wasn’t broken up by anyone except both LEGION and MIKE RANDALLS arriving to join in the fight! Both Dorchester and Blaine got the hell out of dodge with Stratton LEAVING the arena instead of announcing his next Television Title defense as planned!” DREWES: “Wow, that might not make the Front Office too happy. . .” RICE: (V/O) “Nope, but the night didn’t get much better for Legion’s prospects either. During the middle of the sold-out show in Omaha, the BLACK MARKET surrounded the ring as LEGION destroyed a local competitor finishing him off with the LEGIONAIRES DISEASE splash. So that was going well, his new "Legio mihi nomen est, quia multi sumus." t-shirt was looking nice. . . and after the match, much to the crowd’s disgust - the GRAVEROBBERS were sicced on this poor guy by Legion UNTIL the CHROMATIC DRAGONS ran out and made the save. While the Dragons battled the Robbers, TEDDY ALEXANDER roared out only a few minutes fresh off a victory over Ryan Oracle and KICKED and unsuspecting LEGION’S HEAD OFF with the Angerbash Bicycle Kick! That forced the Black Market to leave the ringside area in retreat, while the Chromatic Dragons watched Teddy Alexander nervously. Conspiculously absent was GREG HERPIN, who had tried to align himself to Teddy and the Black Market before the Random Rumble.” CALDWELLER: “Sounds pretty ugly for Legion there. . .” RICE: (V/O) “Not as ugly as JJ DeVille trying to spearhead a Kickstarter Campaign for the Future Farmers of Nebraska and getting booed OUTTA THE BUILDING! I don’t think anyone is trusting this new turn so to speak, and everyone’s waiting for him to be medically cleared to wrestle to see if JJ really is trying to take a different approach in the New Frontier.” CALDWELLER: “With him aligned to a vengeful and psychotic Mike Randalls, I wouldn’t believe him either!” RICE: (V/O) “But honestly, the worst night might have gone to the newly formed Dirty Deeds stable. ‘ROLLS’ ROYCE RAMSEY suffered a pinfall loss against ‘DA MAN’ XAVIER LANGSTON after ‘DA COMMISH’ PETER WINDHAM botched a chairshot interference attempt to Ramsey's disdain. LANE CASH ate a FALCONS PUNCH from MALIK ANDERSON after repeatedly trying to load his boot to the BEAUTIFUL BLONDES’ delight. . .which pissed off BOTH Peter and Royce, so Peter used his Commissioner powers to book an impromptu TRIOS style match for DIRTY DEEDS against any challengers. . . And then the SHADOWRUNNERS showed up and scored a massive upset victory after LANE CASH’s smoke break cost his team and caused PETER WINDHAM to get rolled up by TOD DESTINY!” CALDWELLER: “Sounds like the fracturing of these guys is going to come soon and fast. . .” RICE: (V/O) “Well, that’s interesting because close to the end of the night, KERRY KUROYAMA defeated COJONES MERCADO after a Kuroyama Driver. Post-match Devin Millwood interviewed Kerry, who wished Leyenda De Ocho a speedy recovery. And things got more interesting when XAVIER LANGSTON walked out and accused K2 of positioning himself above ‘DA MAN’ and he’s EYEING opportunities like the TRIPLE CROWN CHAMPIONSHP as much as K2. So just when you think we’re gonna see a LIONS CUP redux, PETER WINDHAM’s back out with DIRTY DEEDS flanking his side and announcing that NEITHER of these guys are getting anything without his COMMISIONER STAMP OF APPROVAL, so he’ll get something ready for them BOTH in WICHITA, KANSAS - the next NFW BRAWL!” DREWES: “Well, I’m sure you’re excited for that announcment!” RICE: (V/O) “It is nice to see something set for the agenda!” CALDWELLER: “Interesting to see Devin Millwood out there with Kuroyama, he sent me a draft of an article he’s writing called “SHOULD WE SAVE KERRY KUROYAMA?”” DREWES: “. . .that sounds ominous.” CALDWELLER: “I’m just going to congratulate Devin on keeping his profile undercover for that interview because I don’t think Kerry is going to agree with ALL facets of this article.” DREWES: “And how about the main event?” RICE: (V/O) “Well, ILL FORTUNE had come out after yet another CHROMATIC DRAGONS victory and highlight why they deserve as much of a shot and opportunity in this federation as ANY TAG TEAM. So, PRESIDENT MAYFIELD being PRESIDENT MAYFIELD came on the FRONTIERtron, which was in storage for two shows mind you. . . and announced they get the BIGGEST opportunity of the night, they can face CANCER and JACK HARMEN, the WALKING DEAD in honor of their ABSENT mentor and their inability to overcome CANCER by themselves should have improved after their recent tune-ups.” CALDWELLER: “Oh man, that’s cold.” DREWES: “It’s getting real cold for ANYONE that’s associated themselves with Castor. Think about it this night - Peter, Legion and now Alex and Lucky Seven!” CALDWELLER: “Cancer said he’d be the source of disease after this. . . this makes it sound like he was infected way before he arrived.” DREWES: “Unless you take Castor’s tongue-in-cheek comments about Buffalo more seriously than others.” RICE: (V/O) “Well, surprisingly ILL FORTUNE did get the win at the fifteen minute mark. . .” DREWES: “WHAT!?” RICE: (V/O) “. . . but not so surprisingly after CANCER brained Alex with a sickening chairshot. (DREWES: “Ah.”) From there, he gave LUCKY SEVEN the newly christened FADETOBLACK headlock driver and proceeded to repeatedly punch her in the face until the CHROMATIC DRAGONS, KERRY KUROYAMA and YES - XAVIER LANGSTON ran out for the save. And I’m guessing that this has to be a theme in Devin’s article on Kerry Kuroyama, Ted. . . the limited number of heroes in NFW against the once-again reforming stables of psychotics and gloryhounds.” CALDWELLER: “That’s 500 points, Natrone!” RICE: (V/O) “I’m sure. Considering Langston and Kuroyama were having words in the middle of the ring and had to be calmed down by the Dragons. Austin and Seven aren’t injured, but that match was interesting that Jack Harmen spent more time with Calvin Carlton and that World Championship than the match itself. . . which was FINE by CANCER until no pun intended. . . he started feeling winded.” DREWES: “Wow, that’s interesting and weird, so par for the course in NEW FRONTIER. We really don’t have a sense of WHO or WHAT is happening in WICHITA, but much like we cover WRESTLING WORLD WIDE. . . I’m sure the WorldWideWeb will have some updates out on NFW’s website.” RICE: (V/O) “As well as the ESEN MOTHERSHIP’s daily NFW FILMS updates!” DREWES: “Alright, thanks for joining us NATRONE! Sounds like NFW is going to bring the Oz to Kansas pretty soon! (RICE laughs) We’re going to take a quick commercial and catch up on the rest of the wrestling world as well as catch up with EL GRINGO LOCO from the GUERRILLA WRESTLING RADIO on SIRIUS/XM satellite radio!” (FADETOBLACK.)
