-NOTTHEMOUNTIE- Height: 6'1" Weight: 338 lbs. Handedness: Right Look: Big fat guy, bushy brown hair, straggly beard, pot belly, Torgo thighs Hailing From: Montreal, QC Disposition: Heel Gimmick: Got kicked out of the Mountie Academy, now wrestles. Loves poutine. Ring Attire: Gravy-stained short-sleeved red Canadian mounted police shirt, black spandex wrestling trunks, black elbow pads, gold wrist bands, red boots
==TEAM== Theme Music: French National Anthem beginning (think Glass Joe's entrance music from Punch Out) followed by "I'm Afraid of Americans" by David Bowie and Trent Reznor
Ring Entrance: For most crowds they curse and make insulting hand gestures as they walk down to the ring. The only crowds they won't do this for are in Quebec City and Montreal, where they come out to heroes' welcomes and soak up adulation. (they HATE other Canadians and Americans)
Invidual and Team Tactics/Style: MSSR. F IV - Technical marvel. Think of your random indie guy like Super Dragon or Brian Danielson.
NOTTHEMOUNTIE - High-energy big man worker. Does some high-flying stuff that most fat guys wouldn't try. Reminiscent of Big Van Vader, Undertaker and American Balloon.

Easily fooled MATCHWRITING GUIDE: MSSR. F IV Regular Moves: Overhand chop, elbow strikes, roaring strikes, suplexes, various submissions, standing Shiranui... Mssr. F's moveset is big, so you can be liberal
Signature Moves: American Swinelock (Nigel McGuinness' bridging Rings of Saturn - it's #7 on the list), Bastille Day (Over Easy), Cocky Pin (think Chris Jericho), Bordeaux Crab (Walls of Jericho with headscissors), Driver Fantastique I (ultra-quick tilt-a-whirl into a tombstone piledriver... alternate finisher)
Finisher: Driver Fantastique II (Tombstone Piledriver into the top turnbuckle) NOTTHEMOUNTIE Regular Moves: Chop, belly attack, corner belly attack (Hornet splash), butt bump, jumping lariat, powerslam
Signature Moves: Fatsault (moonsault from top rope), Burp of DOOM~! (belch right into the face of an opponent, leaving them dazed from the awful smell), Flying Motorboat (springboard chest attack to a standing opponent's face)
Finisher: Mount This! (inverted Styles Clash, Styles Clash from the tombstone position) TEAM Signature Double Team Moves: St. Lawerence Squish (Mssr. F with the back body drop to a whipped opponent, NTM catches and hits a powerslam), Fundy Bay Bash (NTM with a back body drop to a whipped opponent, Mssr. F catches the head and hits with a stiff knee), Piggyback Mount This! (Mount This! done with Mssr. F on NTM's back)
Finisher: Quebec Avalanche (Named out of protest for the Nordiques being stolen from Quebec and moved to Colorado, Mssr. F puts the victim in a Romero special/Mexican surfboard, but doesn't lay flat on his back, rather holds the victim so that his chest is upright, facing the turnbuckles. NTM comes from the top and hits the prone victim right in the chest with a brutal clothesline)
CHARACTER BIOGRAPHY: Mssr. F was a wrestling prodigy who was born to grapple his whole life. Bred from a young age, he watched every local wrestling promotion and got his hands on as much mainstream stuff as possible, mostly from Europe where he idolized French national hero Monsieur Fantastic. He idolized him so much that he whined at his parents to let him go train over there until his father couldn't take it anymore, took out a second mortgage and sent him to Bordeaux. Now, he's fully trained and ready to make a splash against second-rate Americans, Western Canadians, Japanese and especially hosers from Ontario. He hates Ontario.
NTM liked wrestling too, but he really wanted to be a Mountie. However, he flunked out of the Academy. He swears it was politics. They swear it was because he gained 40 pounds within his first week because the Academy was located right around the corner from a Tim Horton's. You make the call.
These two Quebecois met at an indie show in Oshawa and hit it off immediately due to their shared disdain for anything that wasn't French or French-Canadian. Now they're among the most lethal tag team forces in the indies and they want to test their mettle in the best tag team scene in the majors today: NFW.
