Zack Dragon Biography Eye Color Black Nicknames The Black Dragon Hometown Parts Unknown Birthday Unknown Height 6'1 Weight 234 Alias "Black" Zack Dragon Handedness Right Looks Pale white skin, just-beyond-shoulder-length straight jet-black hair, never seen without a shirt on even in the ring. If it’s ripped, his build is rather plain despite his height and weight.. Biography Little is known about the man who calls himself The Black Dragon. When asked, he will tell you that he is ageless and transcends time. Research suggests that he was a member of several federations prior to this one, with limited in-ring singles success. However, after a brief stint as the President and CEO of a large umbrella federation, he disappeared from the wrestling world. Why he did so...and why he's back...is something of a mystery. Manager Ancalagon Wrestling Style Using defense as a form of offense. When he's on offense, he'll lean on his technical prowess, some weardown holds, and basic striking. Strengths 1) Smart - extremely intelligent, doesn’t make a lot of “dumb” mistakes in the ring. 2) Jumping ability - Dragon can get air. Adds extra oomph to his leaping moves 3) Good technical wrestler - specialize in the strategy of defense as a form of offense, but he can get in and out of a hold exchange with the best of em. Weaknessees 1) Physical ability - aside from jumping, he's not a physically unique or imposing individual. 2) Punishment - employing a “defense as offense” strategy in pro wrestling can expose yourself to a lot of damage. The best case scenario is always ducking and dodging and twisting out of moves, but often, if he’s wrong, he opens himself up to greater damage maneuvers 3) Callous - while he understands emotions and can, in the right scenario, empathize, his general default is really a detached social state. Aside from his partners in the Chromatic Dragons, he doesn’t have a ton of friends. Ring attire Zack Dragon wears black full-length wrestling tights and black wrestling boots, both with green trim. He wears a black T-shirt plain on the front with with open green “dragon” eyes on the back. He wrestles with his hair tied in a ponytail.
