NFW Brawl 37: To the Alamo, 1 April 2011

NFW Brawl 37: To the Alamo 1 Apr 2011 Don Haskins Center, El Paso, Texas (seats 12,222) Welcome to the Show! ANNOUNCER V/O: "The following program is a JOINT presentation by ESEN Television and SIRIUS/XM satellite radio." (MUSICUP: "SLOW NIGHT, SO LONG" – Kings of Leon) (QUICK CUTTOs: IMPULSE pushing open box suite door with a placard reading "RESERVED FOR THE PRESIDENT," DAN RYAN hitting a vicious Axe Bomber on an unrecognizable opponent! ROOK BLACK and HIGH FLYER circling around the ring, FLYER making his devil horn pose and charging in! LEGION ducking under a clothesline from BRET KELLY. LORD COYNER POLLARD being led to the ring by JEEVES.) NATRONE RICE V/O: "We are deep in the heart of Texas! And after the last RELOADED, you better believe that we’re all on high alert with the ever-growing Windham Clan, some of us are even wondering what’s going through the mind of the Executive office as whirlwind rumors about a possible company sale are in the mix as well!" (FADEIN: NATRONE RICE outside the DON HASKINS CENTER in El Paso, Texas. More affectionately called ‘THE DON,’ by the UTEP student body…it’s a raucous scene behind the makeshift outdoor studio as RICE and a few production staff are settled in and ready for the show. From the looks of things and RICE’s casual t-shirt and shorts attire, Spring has arrived in the El Paso air.) RICE: "Welcome to El Paso, Texas fans! We’re outside THE DON, and we’re about 30 minutes removed from another rafter shaking, swerve taking evening of NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING action! Four matches hit the slate as LEGION and DAN RYAN had some warmup matches for their SUPERCRASH plans which were revealed at the end of the show. Meanwhile, Rook Black’s National Championship defense against High Flyer did not go as expected…while Lord Coyner Pollard and Impulse found themselves in the Main Event, DIPLOMATIC IMMUNITY on the line after an Executive order! And if that wasn’t enough, and frankly…if it wasn’t obvious enough, THE WINDHAM CLAN, DYNASTY and Television Champion CASTOR STRIFE were in the building trying to learn about their own respective dates heading into SUPERCRASH 3 which will be broadcast LIVE from one of the biggest arenas in the state of Texas. More on that later and after we get through the recaps of this evening, we’re going to open the phone lines for the radio portion of the show…if you haven’t heard some of the crazy news, well…once you do, I bet you’re gonna want to call in and speak your mind. Anyway, we’re gonna take a quick 30 second station identification and we’ll get right to the first couple of recaps with LEGION facing off against BRET KELLY and DAN RYAN squaring off against LA ESTRELLA 100%." (FADEOUT) Legion vs. Bret Kelly (FADEIN: BRET KELLY walking to ringside amongst little fanfare. CUTTO: LUCI4 leading LEGION down the ramp amongst swirling red lights accentuated with flickering white strobe lights.) RICE: "Alright, one of our first matches was LEGION taking on one longest tenured wrestlers in the New Frontier, Bret Kelly. Of course, that hasn’t guaranteed much success in Kelly’s career." (CUTTO: LEGION attacking KELLY before the bell, ramming him shoulder-first into the ringpost and pulling him out by the seat of his pants…delivering a Back Suplex! LEGION quickly picks up KELLY by his slicked back, greasy hair and hooks in a double arm…DDT!) RICE: "Legion was on the offensive real early and didn’t let up on the journeyman. Quickly attacking him before the bell, basically toying with the man’s health and general well-being if you ask me." (CUTTO: LEGION delivering a Stun Gun across the barricade, as the ref tries to get him away from the gasping KELLY…LUCI4 delivers a vicious kick to the solarplexus!) RICE: "If that wasn’t enough…and I know you Sirius fans can’t see it, but Luci4 got her shots in as well. Luckily, the referee was in no business to let Kelly’s career end on the spot…and for the first time I can recall in my memory, we had a referee telling a competitor that either he end the match or he’ll call it off via knockout. That’s how bad of shape Kelly was in at this point…" (CUTTO: LEGION taking KELLY up to the top rope, nodding at the referee who looks pale…) RICE: "And Legion thusly hit a SUPER BRAINBUSTER or what he loves to call the NINTH GATE TO HELL and got the 3 count on Bret Kelly. Kelly was stretchered out and I’m really starting to wonder if the 39 year-old can keep taking the beatings he does…" (CUTTO: LEGION smiling evily with his arms raised in victory…LUCI4 shouting at fans in the background.) Dan Ryan vs. La Estrella 100% (FADEIN: LA ESTRELLA 100% flipping into the ring to a cheap crowd pop…) RICE: "Up next was the possibly the mismatch of the last two millenniums. The Lord or Eddie Mayfield can only tell us why Dan Ryan was booked against the 170 pounder LA ESTRELLA 100%, but we almost witnessed a record setting match…" (CUTTO: RYAN walking out under strobe lights flickering, while ‘ZERO’ blares. He’s definitely getting a mix of home-state love and Windham Clan hate, pretty much split in two from the sounds of things…) RICE: "So, I think we all know how this one turned out…Ryan wasn’t accompanied by the Windham Clan to the ring, but from other activities on the FRONTIERtron earlier in the night…we knew they were there and getting ready to sign their contracts for SUPERCRASH 3. Troy Windham warning of NFW’s literal death coming on the horizon…" (CUTTO: ESTRELLA criss-crossing around the ring off the ropes, while RYAN cricks his neck once or twice. RYAN steps into the middle, ESTRELLA goes for a Tilt-a-Whirl Hurricanrana, but RYAN tosses him off! ESTRELLA kips up and right into a BIG BOOT to the face! ESTRELLA goes down like he gets shot, slowly pushing himself back up…eating a running Axe Bomber!) RICE: "This one was under two minutes fans. We’re checking the record books, but man alive…" (CUTTO: RYAN driving ESTRELLA to the mat with a HUMILITY BOMB and nonchalant cover for the 3 count.) RICE: "It was vicious…it was quick and while I don’t think the level of competition serves notice to anyone interested in the vacated World Championship, it speaks to the fact that Dan Ryan is NOT messing around. Anyone knowing Ryan’s history in NFW can orate a tale of unfulfillment and passed up opportunities…even his one championship reign has gotten lost in the shuffle. Maybe with guys like Nova, Joe the Plumber, Felix Red and Michael Manson unable to compete or choosing to retire…Ryan feels more at home, more alive and more DEDICATED to a place he’s either brushed aside or fallen short in. Only time will tell…and with the Windham Clan behind him, he’s even more dangerous than ever. We’ll be right back after a quick break!" (FADEOUT) Into the Lion's Den Earlier in the evening, before the start of the show… "President Eddie," said Catherine Windham, through the intercom buzzer, "Randall Knox is here to see you." There was no answer for a minute or so, and Catherine looked at me and shrugged. She gestured toward the door, and I walked in. Into the Lion's Den. Eddie's office was sparsely decorated; makes sense, we're only here for the night. Catherine is a gatekeeper, not a secretary. "What do you want?" asked Eddie. The President had several stacks of papers on the desk, and he looked like he was busy. I'll be quick, I said, and I put the envelope in my hand on top of the stack closest to him. I'm here to return this, I said. That got his attention. The plain white envelope was overstuffed with folding cash. "What are you trying to pull," asked Eddie, "You earned your bounty when you took Psycho Boy out." I took him out, as you say, for my own reasons, I explained. I was not trying to remove Mark Windham from the company, and I did not ask for, nor was I expecting, to collect the bounty. Eddie slid the envelope back toward me. "You earned it, kid. I don't throw you too many bones here so if I was you, I'd take it and say thank you. Truth be told, your bones may need the health insurance deductible if history means anything." Instead of taking it and saying thank you, I sat down in the chair opposite Eddie. He raised an eyebrow: clearly this is not what he expected. I also wanted to apologize to you, I said. I disagreed - openly – with the way you treated Joe. In my opinion, he earned the World Title with a clean victory in the ring, and he deserved the respect accorded to the Champion. He started to answer, but I kept going even as his nose bristled with a sneer. But I do not and will not ever support the lack of respect he showed to this company and the World Championship. You were correct, sir, for reasons I don't agree with - but you were still correct in your decision to take Joe's belt from him, and for that, I apologize. There seemed to be something unfamiliar in Eddie's face. Respect. Just a sliver. "Appreciated, Knox," he said, "Now get out, I'm busy." I stood up, but lingered. I'd like one more thing, I said. "Make it fast." Dan Ryan, I said, at Supercrash. That got his attention. "You sure about that?" Aside from the fine print, that's it, I said. "And what, pray tell, is the fine print?" I sat back down and told him. Believe it or not, he thought it was brilliant. And he said yes to everything I asked for… …as long as I accepted certain conditions of the deal, which surprising to me…made me smile causing me to feel something unexpected. Respect. Well, just a sliver. Rook Black (c) vs. High Flyer (FADEIN: HIGH FLYER walking down the aisle to a mix of boos and cheers…) RICE: "The next match on the slate was for the National Championship, regardless of whether the belt is holed up in some Communist country’s secret warehouse or not. High Flyer was the purported challenger of the night and earlier, ‘JUST’ MARK caught up with ROOK BLACK in the locker rooms to know his plans for the double defense SUPERCRASH situation…as well as his take on the President’s speech from RELOADED concerning himself. Needless to say, Rook once again shows you can never tell what to think with him…actually SUPPORTING the President’s viewpoints and apologizing for the transgressions with the title belt, informing the world that hell or highwater…the belt would be at Supercrash in mint condition. He also noted that while there are threats within Mayfield’s dialogue, actions were not taken…so, he believes he may still have the support he will need in the future. With that Rook announced that he would face THIRTEEN in the first match…and the winner would go onto face DOCTOR CURIOSITY, which also meant that LEGION would be Special Referee for the first match. Consequently, the FRONTIERtron cut to Legion at the announcement and at first he looked PISSED and then smiled as it fade away." (CUTTO: ROOK surrounded by the NATIONAL GUARD, walking down the aisle slowly as some fans hurl insults his way.) RICE: "Now, within all this TEXAN pride of the Windham Clan…people forget that Rook Black is from this state…maybe even the state itself as none of us would consider Rook the consummate Texan wrestler by any stretch of the imagination." (CUTTO: FLYER and BLACK in the ring, pacing around…FLYER makes his devil’s horn taunt and charges! BLACK sidesteps, FLYER hitting the ropes and catching a discus forearm from the champion! FLYER staggers backwards and BLACK uses the momentum to initiate an Irish Whip followed by a Back Body Drop!) RICE: "Regardless of their sanity or the clarity of mind in their promotions for matches, it’s no question that High Flyer and Rook Black are two world-class athletes that have shown stretches of pure dominance over the past decade." (CUTTO: FLYER hitting a dropkick on ROOK! FLYER kips up and hits a Springboard Elbow drop! He only gets a two count before a kickout, FLYER then jumps to his feet and catches ROOK with a running knee lift! CUTTO: BLACK pulverizing the top of FLYER’s skull with a barrage of rapid-fire overhand elbow smashes! FLYER drunkwalks around the ring and ROOK drives him to the mat with a Spinebuster! ROOK stays on the offensive, running off the ropes and dropping a flying elbow drop! ROOK gets a two count, sneering at the ref…) RICE: "The match stayed hot for twenty minutes, going back and forth…a couple of close calls throwing the whole National Championship picture into disarray. Of course, that may be why a SPECIAL appearance was made by a future SPECIAL referee…" (CUTTO: FLYER on the ring apron, preparing to climb to the top rope as ROOK’s staggering around…The camera swerves around showing LEGION running down the entrance aisle! FLYER gets perched and never sees LEGION hopping onto the apron and bumrushing him! LEGION shoves him off, sending FLYER crashing to the padded cement floor…as he laughs wildly, ROOK sees the ref ringing the bell and he decides to rush LEGION! He slingshots him into the ring, stomping and punching away!) RICE: "So, High Flyer wins the match via Disqualification…but let’s face it, he’s the one worse for wear…Legion manages to slither out of the ring after Rook goes for an Irish Whip. Quite the end scene…" (CUTTO: LEGION holding the back of his head, ROOK leaning over the top rope motioning for him to come back and fight it out. LEGION shakes his head and starts laughing maniacally…suddenly turning to the camera and screaming "YOUR NUMBER IS UP, THIRTEEN! AT SUPERCRASH ALL WILL BE REVEALED!") RICE: "And yes, radio listeners…that was the insane LEGION yelling for THIRTEEN, I don’t know if that’s a good or bad sign for the champ at this point. But we’ll see at SUPERCRASH, that’s for sure!" Lord Coyner Pollard vs. Impulse (FADEIN: LORD COYNER POLLARD walking behind his butler, JEEVES. POLLARD’s wearing his normally extravagant Brittania-themed and quail-feathered robe. Meanwhile, JEEVES is wearing a standard tuxedo and holding the DIPLOMATIC IMMUNITY accord papers.) RICE: "So, we came to the main event…two of the top wrestlers in the NEW FRONTIER. Lord Coyner Pollard has laid out an extremely impressive record, albeit with some help from the Executive Branch. Whether it’s been Brittania Rules matches or wrestling under the ridiculous DIPLOMATIC IMMUNITY accord, he’s now a top contender for all titles in NFW." (CUTTO: IMPULSE walking down the entrance aisle, focused straight ahead towards the ring…as he gets closer, a small smile on his face. Almost as if he knows something that LCP doesn’t…) RICE: "As usual, the last match is what brought the real twists and turns to the night’s activities. And I won’t lie, in this particular case…it was shocking at who had what at stake. As LEE-BABY SIMS went over the competitor introductions, he then announced that there was an ADDENDUM to the main event from the PRESIDENT." (CUTTO: LCP and JEEVES clapping, the butler holding up the Diplomatic Immunity accord papers…) RICE: "I know what you’re all thinking. Two words. DIPLOMATIC IMMUNITY…" (CUTTO: POLLARD’S jaw dropping, JEEVES fainting!) RICE: "And you would be WRONG. In a complete and utterly SHOCKING moment, what went down was INCREDIBLY unlike anything we’ve been seeing out of Eddie Mayfield. The President apparently had decided that since the DECLARATION of WAR had been announced between the WINDHAM CLAN and NFW that even our closest allies would have to figure out where their battle lines would need to be drawn. Therefore, and I swear I couldn’t believe it when I heard it…if Lord Coyner Pollard lost the match, he would no longer be able to compete under the rules of DIPLOMATIC IMMUNITY or create ANY stipulations until otherwise decided by the Executive Branch of New Frontier!" (CUTTO: POLLARD chasing LEE-BABY SIMS out of the ring, while the referee tends to JEEVES who’s still unconscious.) RICE: "I mean…something had to be up, right? Eddie Mayfield making rulings that FAVOR Impulse? Or legitimate FAIRNESS and EQUALITY!?" (CUTTO: Staffworkers helping JEEVES out of the ring, POLLARD still trying to grasp over the ropes at the frazzled LEE-BABY…unbeknownst to the British Royal, the referee signals for the bell behind him, literally giving IMPULSE a head-running-start!) RICE: "So, quicker than Lord Coyner Pollard could scream "ARE YOU BLOODY MENTAL!?" everything was flipped upside down, including himself as Impulse connected with a running dropkick sending him up and over the ropes to the outside of the ring! Impulse slid out of the ring as Pollard gained his bearings, pulling the robe over the Britsh Royal’s head and ramming him into ring apron! Pollard fought out of the robe as Impulse kept it up with a few vicious chops and the fans were rallying behind the former TV Champ…" (CUTTO: IMPULSE hitting a slingshot bodypress, almost getting a quick three! QUICK CUTTO: IMPULSE with a backslide for two and a half!) RICE: "Impulse really tried to put this one away early, using Pollard’s shock against him, but give credit to the Englishman…he fought against the emotion, the tide and momentum…around five minutes in, he turned a Hurricanrana attempt into a Sitout Powerbomb that nearly ended the match and certainly shut ‘THE DON’ up. And that ain’t easy to do to 13,000 people at once!" (CUTTO: POLLARD hooking IMPULSE and hitting a trio of rolling Vertical Suplexes! POLLARD finishes the third, while rolling over for a pin attempt nearly winning the match. QUICK CUTTO’s: POLLARD with a series of vicious forearm uppercuts! POLLARD hitting an Inverted Atomic Drop! POLLARD dropping IMPULSE with a Reverse Neckbreaker! CUTTO: JEEVES cheering on his employer, looking soaked from getting doused with water to regain consciousness…) RICE: "Pollard went into tear-apart mode, beating down Impulse mercilessly…like he was some peasant who asked for a lunch break at his summer winery villa. But let’s face it, this youngster is from BROOKLYN. This youngster is called THE MARATHON MAN because of how long he can last and how much damage he can take…" (CUTTO: IMPULSE flipping out of a British (German) Suplex attempt, hooking POLLARD from behind in a Full Nelson and sweeping the leg for a vicious facebuster! QUICK CUTTOs: IMPULSE with a split-legged clothesline! IMPULSE with a series of vicious reverse knife-edge chops! IMPULSE with a quick boot to the gut, followed by a DDT! IMPULSE perched on the top rope and delivering a FRONT-FLIP NECKBREAKER!) RICE: "So, it shouldn’t come as a surprise that the Britons retreated to their desperate measures to achieve victory…the only difference this time that it wouldn’t be considered legal maneuvering in the ring." (CUTTO: JEEVES hopping on the apron and distracting the referee as IMPULSE hooks in a Camel Clutch! JEEVES squirms past the referee entering the ring causing IMPULSE to break his hold since it’s right in his sightline. IMPULSE rushes towards JEEVES who jumps out of the ring. CUTTO: POLLARD tucked up in a fetal ball, looking like he’s digging into ring tights. CLOSEUP: POLLARD standing up and trying to hide something quite sparkling and golden around his fist…) RICE: "Only this man would have CROWN JEWELS KNUCKS. Not even BRASS! We’re talking 24-KARAT with emeralds, rubies and encrusted diamonds!" (CUTTO: POLLARD wildly swinging a right and missing IMPULSE’s head! IMPULSE hooks him and delivers a high-angle BACK SUPLEX, but takes a point blank shot to the skull in the process! The crown jewels fly off Pollard’s hand out of the ring upon impact! CUTTO: AUGUST DE LA ROSSI and PETER WINDHAM scurrying down to ringside!) RICE: "So, with Pollard knocking out Impulse with those crown jewels in the process of taking a nasty suplex, they’re both down. And the runts of the Windham Clan showed up! August De La Rossi hopping on the ring apron and distracting the referee, while Peter Windham aka Problem Child aka Total Scumbag viciously assaulted Jeeves! So, with the referee trying to keep order…TROY WINDHAM runs down. And now the fans are FIRED UP. They’re cheering, booing…it’s just plain loud…" (CUTTO: TROY shaking up a can, while sneaking around the ringside area…whatever it is…he sprays it in the face of LORD COYNER POLLARD!) RICE: "Troy Windham sprays Pollard in the eyes with god-knows-what! The man’s now blinded, while the referee is completely blind to the situation. Impulse isn’t even conscious from the cheapshot he’s already taken. The referee turns around and doesn’t know what the hell to do…he starts the standing ten count. SOMEHOW, both men get up…I have no clue how…" (CUTTO: IMPULSE handing over the ring ropes, looking half-concussed. POLLARD’s using the ropes to figure out where the hell he is…and stumbles into the middle of the ring, and more importantly a desperate SUDDEN IMPACT SUPERKICK.) RICE: "Out of nowhere, a desperation Sudden Impact Superkick from Impulse connects on Pollard’s jaw and we’ve got a following three count. Troy Windham goes into hysterics over the result, laughing madly…while BOBBY JACK WINDHAM, JJ DEVILLE, BANDIT and DAN RYAN start making their way towards the ring." (CUTTO: BANDIT, DEVILLE and BOBBY JACK running down as DAN RYAN slowly strolls. IMPULSE catches BANDIT with a sliding dropkick, while DEVILLE charges in the ring and starts mudstomping POLLARD! BOBBY JACK joins him on POLLARD as BANDIT, TROY and RYAN start pacing around IMPULSE’s area…) RICE: "And it’s starting to look real bad in there…Impulse is surrounded, Pollard’s getting the crap beat out of him…" (CUTTO: WINDHAM laughing with RYAN and pointing to the FRONTIERtron, IMPULSE can’t help himself to see what’s so funny…and sees TROY spraying POLLARD in the face. WINDHAM turns and points at IMPULSE, "First, I’m gonna break your code of honor…then WE’RE GONNA BREAK YOUR NECK!") RICE: "Impulse sees the replay of how he won…and runs off the ropes, flying out of the ring at half of the Windham Clan to crowd roars, but it’s immediately not reaping much award. Essentially, lauching oneself into a Five to One scenario only works with a lot of weed and listening to the Doors in the dark. BUT…the surprises were only starting…" (CUTTO: CASTOR STRIFE running out! CALVIN CARLTON and THE HOLLYWOOD WRECKING CREW following suit! JACK BRYANT! HIGH FLYER! ROOK BLACK!) RICE: "Maybe taking cue from the president, we’ve got ALL champions on deck and in the fight! Jack Bryant and High Flyer joining the fray as well…well, not so much Flyer as he decided to bring children’s bubbles and blow them around the brawl. Carlton got a nice overhand smash on Peter Windham, but before things could get completely out of hand…security started running out, the National Guard started running out…guys dressed like the Secret Service, referees…you name it. As they got the tornado settled, PRESIDENT MAYFIELD came on the FRONTIERtron. And somehow, that helped quell what was becoming an in-ring riot. Troy Windham got his clan huddled together in one corner of the ring, while the NFW guys paced around the ring as the champions stayed inside." To the Alamo (CUTTO: MAYFIELD on the FRONTIERtron, while JACK BRYANT paces the outside area holding a chair…some of the WINDHAMS eyeing him closely. POLLARD is being helped to the back by JEEVES, not staying for the pageantry to say the least.) RICE: "Mayfield starts on a fortuitous note, stating to Impulse that whether he wants the decision reversed or not…it’s not going to happen because he knows from earlier tonight he may have to start winning dirty a little more often. Impulse remains stone-faced before giving a slight nod of agreement and we’ve got the crowd officially buzzing as some of the Windham Clan eyebrows pique. Eddie then addresses the Windham Clan, stating that with the World Title needing to be filled he’s not going to take a chance on them destroying the proceedings…so it’s going to be essentially put up or shut up time. For the pleasure of our viewing and listening audience, we’ve got the recording cued up and ready to go." (FADEIN: PRESIDENT MAYFIELD on the FRONTIERtron lighting up a Camel and sitting behind his office desk, while the crowd is still buzzing loudly.) MAYFIELD: "So, here’s the deal I’m going to offer you, your boys and some of the wrestlers already in the ring. At SUPERCRASH 3, five members of TEAM NFW will wrestle against five members of the WINDHAM CLAN (LOUD POP!) in an ELIMINATION RULES Tag Team Match." (MAYFIELD takes a drag off his cigarette as the crowd cheers…) MAYFIELD: "Starting off...if Cameron Cruise doesn't show up for this match, HE'S FIRED. He's the reason you first showed up here, he should at least suffer with the rest of us. I’ve already talked to Impulse and he knows that if he enters this match, he will be one of four men placed into a World Championship tournament AFTER Supercrash 3…and the catch for the Windham Clan? If Dan Ryan is NOT part of the team, he will NEVER get a shot at what he considers (quotes the air) HIS (unquote) belt. So, Ryan…since I know you’ll be entering knowing this..." (QUICK CLOSEUP: RYAN looking up at the FRONTIERtron stone-faced. CUTTO: Back to the President…) MAYFIELD: "Your FIRST match in that four-man tournament will be AGAINST IMPULSE. (LOUD CHEERS!) As for the other two men that I think have earned places in this tournament, they will have to stand by NFW and be apart of this match…the first is a man already out here, CASTOR STRIFE." (CLOSEUP: STRIFE pacing back and forth, still focused on TROY WINDHAM…his head turns a bit at the mention of his name.) MAYFIELD: "The second is a man NOT out here…and a man that’s going to have to earn his stripes to get a chance at what he thinks he’s already earned. (MAYFIELD takes another drag.) DORCHESTER STRATTON. Calvin, you tell your BOY it’s time to fight like a MAN. He’s made me laugh with what he did to Wildstar (LOUD BOOS!) I appreciated the moves he put on our FORMER Plumber. (MORE BOOS!) But this is a different time now, this is a time where if he wants that belt…it’s going to take more than something I know a little about – SCREWJOBS." (CLOSEUP: CALVIN CARLTON nodding in approval, standing between BLAINE HOLLYWOOD and MALIK ANDERSON. BLAINE’s scoffing, while MALIK sneers. CUTTO: Back to MAYFIELD who’s exhaling a smoke ring…) MAYFIELD: "Now, Troy…if you don’t accept this match…if you choose to walk away from this deal, I’m going to use my EXECUTIVE powers to my full advantage. I’d hate to have to book a 5-on-1 Cage match for yourself (LOUD CHEERS!) …or start making Dan Ryan wrestle with one arm, or ban Bobby Jack from making any religious references on any broadcasts or he’s FIRED. (MAYFIELD takes another drag) So, it’s your choice Troy. Start putting it all on the line or start living in HELL." (BLAST OF STATIC!) (CUTTO: Back to RICE in the outdoor desk studio…) RICE: "So, that was the deal laid out and what we THOUGHT would be the deal. But if you know Troy Windham, then you know not everything goes to plan. Troy was given a microphone to accept or deny the deal, but came up with some conditions of his own…" (BLAST OF STATIC!) (CUTTO: TROY WINDHAM microphone-in-hand, laughing as he adjusts his sunglasses.) TROY: "Eddie, Eddie, Eddie…y’know when I’m hanging out at Spago doing lunch with Bob and Harvey Weinstein, or doing my morning jog with ScarJo and Sean Penn, I’m told the same thing…always take the deal with the BEST INCENTIVES. And ever since I walked into this wretched, backwater promotion (LOUD BOOS!) I’ve had a simple goal in mind – BURY THIS PLACE. So, I see that you want the WINDHAM CLAN on the battlefield…I GET YOU. I notice that you don’t name a FIFTH man on your team…" MAYFIELD: (grinning) "We all need an ace in the hole, right?" TROY: "But you still don’t get ME, Eddie. And I’ve got my own ace in the hole. See, I’ll name all five Windham Clan members that will walk into the match because I know we’ll STAND TOGETHER. Deep down, you know this SH(BLEEP!)HOLE can’t do that. So, here’s OUR incentives. We want FIFTY-PERCENT stake in NFW if we win this little (quotes the air) CHALLENGE MATCH (unquote). (CROWD GETS LOUD!) And to prove how serious I am about this, we’ll go with a few conditions into the match to keep everything (laughs) HONEST. FIRST – we want BOBBY JACK WINDHAM named as the new PURE CHAMPION. (LOUD BOOS!) There is no PURER Windham, therefore is no PURER wrestler or man in NFW…" (CUTTO: BOBBY JACK smiling widely and yelling out to the crowd, "I AM A PURE MAN OF GOD!" The crowd boos back LOUDLY!) TROY: "SECOND – if that dirtbag Castor is allowed into this match and your 4-man tournament, I want to make sure he’s like everyone else…and has SOMETHING TO LOSE.
