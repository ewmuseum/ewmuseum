NFW Crash TV 22 1 Jun 2004 Paul Tsongas Arena Bait (PHILADELPHIA, PA, 8:45 pm, June 1st - In the heart of brotherly love, late in the night, most people have left the office for home. A humid, stagnant air hovers over the city, the camera showing a sniper’s view of a large stone building, a man walking in front dressed in a black trench coat, black pants and boots, a black skullcap pulled low on his head, neatly hiding most of his mid back-length dreadlocks. The man angrily slams through the revolving door of the building. The inside cameras reveal the figure to be 'Prodigy' AVERY PROSSER - African American loyalist and Commissioner of the NFW North., Mid-30's but has the look of a man who has had worries beyond his age for some time. PROSSER is clean shaven, besides for the Dizzy Gillespie soul patch on his chin and maintains his 2 part paranoid/worried demeanor crossed with 9 part raging vengeance. PROSSER quickly enters into an opened elevator and disappears. PROSSER angrily glares at his watch before the elevator opens with a quaint ring. As the North ‘dictator’ exits we see him walking into a floor level that is under construction.) AVERY PROSSER: "You’d think with all the planning, they’d at least make the place look nice. . ." (PROSSER walks by a front desk area that’s just been painted, white cloths hanging where your normal company logo plate would reside. Avery rounds a bend and knocks on a door, quickly opening and closing the entry point. As PROSSER enters, the camera now reveals a large boardroom where several figures are sitting in the shadows. Smoke ominously wafting in the air…PROSSER brushes it away with a cough.) PROSSER: "What the hell? Getting in your cancer sludge before the No Smoking signs arrive?" (a female voice answers…) "Welcome, Avery...I’m sorry the accommodations aren’t ready, but these things take time. Nobody can rebuild a holy temple overnight…" PROSSER: (looking around with a scoff) "Whatever. You told me to meet you here on the 1st. I’m here." "We thank you for your promptness. Your attentiveness to detail certainly was a deciding factor in us choosing you. We’d like to think of this as a progress report, nothing more. We’d like to know how our investment looks in your eyes and if anything is needed." PROSSER: "You mean, the North?" "That’s a start." (PROSSER smiles smugly, his white teeth glaring in contrast to the shadows he stands in.) PROSSER: "What can I say? They’re all folding like lawn furniture. By next season, nobody will be left. They’ll either try and transfer, or just quit. They all hate us." "Good. We don’t want any rogues left over from this season." PROSSER: "What about Manson? He’s starting to overstep his bounds…" "Let him. Like we’ve said from the beginning as long as his goal is to win that Ultratitle he’s just as easy to manipulate as anyone else. We’ve got more things to worry about…" PROSSER: (raising an eyebrow) "Such as?" "Miles." PROSSER: (laughing) "I thought that Jesus freak put him in a chair." "Depends on who you believe. We’ve got sources saying he never made it to the hospital and he’s been in contact with some of your old war buddies…" (PROSSER’s grin fades to a worried look…) PROSSER: "Who?" "The Great White Whale." PROSSER: "You mean—" (a male voice interjects) "The Name that Shall Not be Named." (PROSSER snorts, the thought of that ‘traitor’ stepping into this War sends a flood of hatred down his spine…) PROSSER: "He doesn’t have the guts." (the mysterious female continues…) "Don’t underestimate your actions, Avery. Your words have cut through this business like a knife on a throat. Everyone is watching, everyone is waiting…for retaliation. Miles is more paranoid than you are…if that’s possible…he’s not looking for friends. He’s looking for partners." PROSSER: "So what do we do?" "We wait. We play a game of cat and mouse. And we set a trap." PROSSER: "A trap?" "To look vulnerable…to look weak…" PROSSER: "Won’t that risk everything?" "We’re way past that, Avery. We don’t know what Miles is up to. And we won’t until he comes back." PROSSER: "So what should I do? You want me to bring Miles back? Are you crazy?" "No, Avery.
