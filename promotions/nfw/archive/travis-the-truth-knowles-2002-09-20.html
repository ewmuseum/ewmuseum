Matthews — 20 September 2002 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Sep-20-02 AT 05:17&nbsp;PM (EDT)]MEMBER INFO MEMBER NAME: [name withheld] Email address: [detail withheld] WRESTLER INFO Name: Travis Knowles Height: 6'6" Weight: 283 pounds Hometown: South Bethlehem, PA Age: 22 (turns 23 on October 1st) Handedness: left Background: Knowles didn't have the best childhood. He bounced around foster homes and never really know who his real parents were. At 17, Matthews bumped into him after an AAWC show and took him under his wing. Knowles has been training with Shawn Matthews pretty much for the past 4-5 years. He's wrestled in some backyard leagues and in some mom and pop promotions, but NFW is his biggest federation to date. He's become a great all around wrestler... good at technical moves, and power/brawling moves. He's got a lot of charisma and intensity. He talks really fast, and can be too gimmicky at times... like the Rock.
Ring Attire: Black shiny pants. He wears a t-shirt to the ring, but then takes it off when he wrestles... t-shirt varies according to feud.
Looks: Very muscular physique. He's got dark brown hair that's a little past his shoulders. When he's not wrestling, he ties it back. He's got a very thin beard (like Charlie Salinger from Party of Five). He's got a pretty dark tan, even though he's from Bethlehem.
Theme Music: "One Step Closer" by Linkin Park Ring Entrance: When he gets into the ring, he gets up on the bottom turnbuckle and spreads his arms showing his muscle, sorta like Triple H.
Style: Technical, but knows how to brawl. Fifteen moves: Samoan drop German suplex Belly to belly suplex Double underhook powerbomb Tilt-a-whirl suplex Pumphandle slam Double underhook suplex Running shoulder block Elbow from second rope Big boot Pedulum backbreaker Fallaway slam Knees to the stomach reverse DDT bulldog Set-up move: boot to stomach Finisher: Lifts the guy up in the air for a powerbomb, spins around twice, and drops him on the back of his head... uh, call it "THE TRUTH HURTS" I'll come up with something else later.
Strengths: 1. Very good technical wrestler/brawler. 2. Very young and determined 3. Resilient Weaknesses: 1. His past 2. Too cocky for his own good 3. Very trusting of people despite his past... gets let down a lot.
