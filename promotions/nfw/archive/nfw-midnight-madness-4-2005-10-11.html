Mike Randalls' eyes looked empty, his hair was ragged. Looking at him close up, you'd notice week-old shadow underneath his unkempt beard. Still, Deacon could see the volatile energy inside Randalls that worried him five years ago, the first time he encountered a much more dangerious version of 'The Wolf.' From afar, Deacon had watched the changes in Randalls' life with piqued curiosities. Eye contact was made between both men, but neither knew what to say. Much had changed since five years ago, but the past remained very prominent in each man's mind. Randalls walked slowly to corner where Deacon was standing, down the steps from the Joint's back entrance. Randalls pulled off his faded brown cowboy hat, the parking lot lights illuminating the night's battle scars. "Who you waitin' for?" Randalls broke the silence, digging his hands into his pockets. "A cab t'at is late," Deacon said, not even certain what he should say. He was starting to feel more awkward than that time Craig Miles and Nixon tried to confess their sins to him. Randalls seemed to be getting more and more violent these days, and sometimes not by choice. Marcus Cameron had brought something back into Randalls' life that certainly affected him perversely. Cameron was driving Randalls to insanity. Deacon lost to Marcus Cameron. Mike Randalls tried to end Deacon's career 5 years ago. The math was quickly adding up in Deacon's head. "You want a ride?" Deacon tried to speak, but the genuine surprise just made him laugh awkwardly. Randalls' eyebrow rose somewhat, but Deacon held up his hands in apology. "Sorry, t'at just weird t'hear."
