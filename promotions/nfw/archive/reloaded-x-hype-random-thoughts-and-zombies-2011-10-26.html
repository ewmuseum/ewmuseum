jediPREZ — 26 October 2011 So far right now... The show outline is up to XVII. Due to the in-story date, one character will be returning to NFW premises...but as a zombie.
ANYWAY. Feel free to predict matches or comment on promos/stories for what's shaping up to be a fun, exciting card due in 2014.
Justin — 26 October 2011 Predictions: Impulse vs August de la Rossi Am I missing something or is this just a way to shoehorn Impulse into the show? I'm sure there's something ZOMGHYOOJ planned here, and I'm sure it'll be good, but from the standpoint of someone not "in the know" I'm not looking forward to this at all. Impulse either goes over like Superman, or gets beat to **** by sheer numbers. Maybe this is where Impulse goes heel.
Bandit vs Nova This one has Cluster**** written all over it. Both bounties are on the line, and since nobody outside of Adrian Willard has shown any interest in collecting on either one of them, I can't be sure how it'll go down. One thing is for sure, this is where a lot of people who aren't booked in matches will get thrown into the card and plenty of general mayhem will ensue. No Contest due to mass interference.
Lowell vs Jack Harmen Nobody cares about the National Title, no one accepting Rook Black's open challenge is all the proof of that you could possibly need. More cluster****ery here Black and what's left of the National Guard show up to "oversee" the proceedings. If I had to flip a coin, I'd say Harmen by a ****hair.
Bobby Jack Windham (c) vs Deacon vs Legion vs Stalker I'm not even going to try to make sense out of this one. There's people hanging from a cage, there's whatever "Penticostal Rules" is, and there's a bunch of people vying for a fake title with no heat. Remember when the Pure Title was the new World Title? Anyhow, I'd say either Deacon wins the belt and gives it some purpose, or Stalker manages to **** everything up with his legion of Horror Movie ripoffs. Either way, Lucia does not go home, not this soon.
JJ Deville(c) vs Jack Bryant Finally, a match with some heat! This one has been built up rather well, and it's time to blow off. There is no possible outcome other than "new Champion Jack Bryant," but the story here is how you get there. Personally I would have booked a Strap Match for this one, have JJ forced to be no further than ten feet away from Jack at all times, but I've heard just enough of the Falls Count Anywhere plan to be interested, I just hope this one doesn't hold the show up.
Castor Strife (c) vs Dan Ryan The one thing that takes away from this match is that it is Strife's first title defense, and outside of the "somebody's gotta challenge Castor after Impulse loses tournament" there's been very little build up. Billy and [name withheld] are both top of the line guys, and I'd love to see them get something more than a one-off straight up match. However, since I think there's a lot more mileage to put on the Impulse/Strife machine, and I think Ryan's got a lot of Windham Clan issues to tie up, I'm gonna take Strife to retain in what will turn into the night's sixth cluster**** in a row.
Stalker — 26 October 2011 To be honest my main interest in Reloaded X is what the f*** Katz has planned for us all. Being on the eve of Halloween I predict mayhem, hi jinks and some real weird **** to go down.
Hopefully The Fallen will have some fun. User Poets — 26 October 2011 I think the show is going to be a show of transition. That's not a bad thing. The Windhams are going to be heavily involved, considering their involvement in four matches, three title matches, and two definite main events. Plus the Bounty match, who the heck knows what will happen there.
I think the National Title has gotten a raw deal, partly from Pants not really being 'here' on a regular basis. Rook Black has not been involved in an angle in a long time so it's easy to overlook, but Pants is good enough that he just needs that one little push to be at the forefront again. Lowell vs. Jack might not be the biggest thing on the card but Devin vs. Ford is justas big as Billy vs. [name withheld] in my experience.
The PURE Title is much the same, only it's not a real championship so it's kind of been shoved aside. If Bobby Jack loses it it will be the first time someone has actually done so. My money is on Deacon since I am fully aware of what Warren can do, but Stalker and Legion have both impressed me these past few shows.
Nova vs. Bandit will be a clusterfk. But aside from JJ vs. JB that I think should blow off here, and Castor vs. Ryan which I think deserves to have a non-Godbooked finish, this show is most likely going to be setting up Act 3 of the Windham Invasion.
And I wouldn't really call Impulse vs. August 'shoehorned into the show to give Impulse a match' - in-character, it makes sense as Impulse has been verbally sparring with the Windhams since they showed up and it's just like them to condescend to him and give him their 150 pound non-wrestler as an opponent. Out of character, since the match is currently assigned to "the conglomerate," it doesn't take much brain power to figure that there's going to be some kind of angle happening.
