jediPREZ — 25 May 2010 So, I'm also going to put the word out on the street regarding this. Essentially, as long as your character is native solely to NFW, they will be able to participate in the match.
The reasoning behind this is that I want NFW in-story to be showing a concerted effort on refurbishing and re-cultivating their homegrown talent. There's alot of characters out there in multiple feds, so I wanted NFW to take a stand against that in a unique mindset that the TELEVISION CHAMPION should be representative only of NFW on TELEVISION.
I know our current champion is in more than one fed and that angle will play out as well. If you have any questions, feel free to ask them here. Otherwise, as long as your character is ONLY in NFW, feel free to RP in that thread for a maximum of 3 times.
