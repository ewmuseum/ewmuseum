Point of View Biography Biography Eric and Jason met at Fiennes and Dandy Wrestling School in Louisville, Kentucky. They formed a quick bond and became best friends. After graduating Jason took Eric into his world of backyard wrestling and they've uploaded several well-received youtube videos. They're managed by Jason's girlfriend Bronte Lakes. Lakes grew up idolizing Poison Ivy. Ivy, who's struck up a friendship with Bronte, has gotten the boys a tryout with NFW. Jason grew up loving NFW, but believes its lost its way and wants to bring the edge he believes the league has lost. Eric just wants to get even with Brandon Mueller for ruining his mom's life. They're aerial extremists who hope to be able to duplicate what they've done in Jason's backyard to a live NFW wrestling ring.
