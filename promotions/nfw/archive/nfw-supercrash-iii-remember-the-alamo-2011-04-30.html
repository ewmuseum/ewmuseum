NFW SUPERCRASH III: REMEMBER THE ALAMO 30 Apr 2011 The Alamodome, San Antonio, Texas (seats 77,000) Out of the Fire I: The Professor, the Shaman, and the Unknown Soldier. "This is more important than just one match, and more important than who controls the NFW for the next however long." I agree that this is bigger than just one match, I said, but isn’t who controls the New Frontier fairly important? Laughter. "You’re very young," said the Professor, "and you’re very naive." Rosie and I were at the arena by eleven AM just like Eddie Mayfield wanted. He had already blocked out tonight’s event, and was very clear to the undercard they there wouldn’t be any time extensions on any of their matches, because he wanted the Main Event to have as much time as possible. He didn’t want any of the Windham’s tears to be lost to local broadcasts, I think was his exact phrase. Then I got a phone call from a familiar number in a strange locale, asking if Rosie and I wanted to meet for lunch. Sitting around the table, Rosie and I, plus three familiar faces, I had a sense of deja vu from Wrestlestock II. She winked at me, but so far hadn’t said a word past standard pleasantries. I understood – last time we were around a bonfire and she was talking spiritual matters. This was a little… closer to home. "Maybe I’m not as versed in the history of the wrestling business as you guys," said Rosie, sipping her water, "but isn’t the point to keep the Windhams from taking over?" More laughter. The Professor is enjoying this, I think. "The Windhams won’t take over," said the Professor, "They could win the match, but that’s short term, and on Troy’s part, short – sighted." "Long term planning was never his strong suit," said the Unknown Soldier, "Troy can’t see past the end of his nose." He’s smarter than that, I said, he’s maneuvered the New Frontier into this position. Eddie forced him to take the match, but Troy dictated all the terms. I hadn’t shown it for weeks, but I was nervous. A lot was riding on this. "Troy dictated the terms?" asked the Professor, "No, he didn’t. He didn’t get anything that Dennis wasn’t prepared to sacrifice." Rosie and I had the exact same reaction – we looked around quickly. Careful with that, I said, if Eddie hears you used that name then all bets’ll be off and it’d be Five to One out of spite. "No one here gets out alive," said the Shaman, "I think you’re safe, kiddo." I looked at Rose, and we both laughed as well. I was finally getting some respect from Eddie Mayfield and wanted to keep it that way; we were both overreacting. Regardless, I said, I know this match is probably the most important one I’ve been a part of so far; if we don’t beat the Windhams, they get half the company. Not enough to take over completely, but enough to grind everything to a halt. "So you’d give up?" asked the Unknown Soldier. Of course not, I said, just, if Troy gets the nod, things will get tricky. "Don’t do that," said the Professor. Do what? "Bet against Eddie." Oh, my money’s on Eddie, I said, because that’s something that I have control over. I can effect how the match goes just by being there. "Don’t do that either." Do what? The Professor looked at the Unknown Soldier, and took a long sip of beer. "First rule of a Professional, Knox," said the Professor. I’m confused. "Never bet on one," said the Unknown Soldier— "—and never bet against one," finished the Professor.
