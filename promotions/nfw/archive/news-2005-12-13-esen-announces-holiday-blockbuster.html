ESEN Announces Holiday Blockbuster! Posted by Natrone Rice on 13 Dec 2005 In a press conference (Click Here To View) that certainly shocked alot of people tonight, ESEN and NFW President Quentin Sullivan announced a TRIO of shows scheduled to air during the holidays. Not by total surprise, Eastern Conference wrestlers and fans found out that CRASHMAS will be returning, bigger, better and crazier than ever before. Doc Silver will defend the NFW World Title against Jonathan Marx, a stipulaton Marx chooses per last week's 6-man contest. Marx is hinting at a First Blood match that might put Silver at a serious disadvantage given his history. Also scheduled is YYJ vs. Dan Ryan to face the winner of Silver/Marx, Hiroshi/Manson, Red/Cruise...and one only wonders what's next for Beau and Kooter. However, speaking of World titles...the night before Crashmas...live from Philadelphia, the UWA...that's right THE UWA will televise "THE FIGHT BEFORE CHRISTMAS" with Eddie Mayfield defending the UWA Title against Eli Flair. NFW viewers have to be quite puzzled at this turn of events, but rest assured the originally Western Conference scheduled match will still count in the Western Conference standings. In perhaps a concession, or brilliant business strategy, Quentin Sullivan and Craig Miles have agreed to televise UWA events to lessen the run-in factor of NFW Western Conference shows. In return, Miles and Mystic are no longer in control over NFW West decision policy or scheduling, that goes to Sullivan. UWA is wildly popular amongst cult fans and has many loyal Philly fans lining up in droves to get tickets to the Spectrum. ...but the results of this...remain to be seen. Rounding out the holiday run, will be a New Year's Eve Bash live from Tijuana, Mexico...where you can get rid of the UWA...but you can't get rid of Craig Miles...as the boss is taking them to the land of tequila where nobody should expect anything less than everything. There might even be a Mexican Cockfight...according to Calvin Carlton. Here is the full Week 6 lineup for the holidays: DEC. 24: "THE FIGHT BEFORE CHRISTMAS" Eddie Mayfield (c) vs. Eli Flair (UWA World Title) DEC. 25: CRASHMAS Doc Silver (c) vs. Jonathan Marx (NFW World Title) Yori Yakamo Jr. vs. Dan Ryan Jason Payne vs. Lindsay Troy Felix Red vs. Cameron Cruise Kevin Powers vs. Maggot vs. Beau Michaels (Elim. rules) Michael Manson vs. Kin Hiroshi DEC. 31: NEW YEAR'S IN TIJUANA Brandon Youngblood vs. Mike Randalls Joey Melton vs. Jeff Garvin (Chicken Wire Cage) Frank Heimdal vs. Ghost Manpower vs. Craig Miles N. Quiry vs. Seymour Almasy Troy Windham vs. Marcus Cameron Guy Boudreaux vs. Scott Riktor Rook Black vs. Tom Adler KVC vs. Alias vs. Uriel (Elim. rules)
