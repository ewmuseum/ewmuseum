Guy Boudreaux Biography Eye Color Green Nicknames The Ragin Cajun Hometown New Iberia, Louisiana Birthday July 10th, 1980 Height 6'0 Weight 200 Biography To find the home of Guy Boudreaux you got to go way down to Louisiana. Then you have to go all the way to New Iberia. Then you have to get in a boat and go across the way. Among the cypress trees you’ll see a little shack. That shack is the place Ma Boudreaux and Pa Boudreaux raised little Guy Boudreaux. Guy had himself a normal childhood for a Coonass. He had pets. In fact he had lots of pets. He had possums, swamp rats, and gators for pets. Guy’s Pa worked as a gator wrestler and his Ma worked at the bait shop. Like many coons, Guy watched wrestling. Now at six foot and barely two hundred pounds soaking wet, Guy couldn't be an alligator wrestler like his pa. So he decided to pack up his bags and become one of those big time wrestlers you see on TV. Now little Guy Boudreaux is special. He is very cynical. Not just you’re run of the mill cynical either. He honed it to a skill. Never in a life had you ever seen a more cynical boy then him. When Guy got to the big wrestling show he saw all them boys in colorful outfits. They would climb up real high and jump onto the other people. Boudreaux enjoyed watching them wrestle and wanted to join them. When Boudreaux talked to the owner it was his lucky day! The owner hired him on the spot! Though Boudreaux didn't know why the owner kept laughing when he asked to join but he guessed he was just happy to hire him. Well Boudreaux, though he had no fancy train, started wrestling the next night. He was so proud he invited his ma and pa to watch him wrestle. Turn out Boudreaux got to wrestle the Champion for the title! He was so happy he didn't even mind having to wear a silly costume. He thought it was all part of the show. Now Boudreaux sat at the curtains all night and watched all the other people go out and wrestle. Finally it was Boudreaux's turn! He jumped to his feet and shouted "Euuu Weee!" His music played and he marched out to the ring proudly. He looked over and saw his ma and pa had front row seats! Then Boudreaux's opponent got announced and was he a big boy. Suddenly Guy didn't feel so good. He started to look around for a way out. Needless to say Boudreaux got a mudhole stomped out of him. About ten seconds later Boudreaux found himself on his back and being pinned. About three seconds after that and Boudreaux heard the other guy's name being pronounced. He didn't feel so proud anymore. Then they started laughing. First the champion, then the referee, and then the owner! They were all laughing at poor Boudreaux. His ma and pa watched on with tears in their eyes. After the show Boudreaux backed his bags and went home with his ma and pa. He didn't want to be a wrestler anymore. That night Boudreaux took down all his wrestling posters off the walls. If they were just going to make fun of him, then he didn’t want any part of them. Now that night there was a knock on they door! Boudreaux's ma answered the door and nearly dropped her glass of water! Some man in one of them fancy suits was a knocking on the door. She was confused as to why this man was knocking on her door. It turned out that man was one of them big time wrestler promoters. He saw Boudreaux out there and wanted to train him! Boudreaux was going be officially trained by some fancy city slicker. Needless to say, Boudreaux's ma went and fetched him from his room and told him to stop being so rude and say hello to his guest. When Boudreaux saw that fancy suit and heard about the training, he nearly jumped out of his slippers! So Boudreaux packed up his stuff and went with the man to go training in the big city, Baton Rouge. And that, the say, is how Boudreaux started to wrestle. a. Begging for Mercy Description: Begs for mercy and sneaks in a low blow Frequency: **** Finishing: * Tidbit: When Guy starts to take too much damage, he begs for mercy until the opponent gets cocky. Boudreaux surprises them with a low blow. Variations: none b. Bleeder Description: Taking a move, Guy starts to bleed Frequency: **** Finishing: * Tidbit: It is very common for Guy to hide fake blood on him. He takes a move then breaks open the fake blood making it look like he is bleeding badly. When the opponent drops his guard, Guy attacks. Variations: Self bleed or fake blood c. Cross Body Splash Description: Cross Body Splash Frequency: **** Finishing: ** Tidbit: One of the few offensive moves Guy Boudreaux has learned. This can also be used as a reversal. The Cross Body Splash is Boudreaux’s second favorite top rope move next to the Moonslaut. He can get great high and distance for his Cross Body. Variations: Reversal against charging opponent, reversal against opponent’s Cross Body, from top rope, from guard rail, Springboard off the ropes, bounce off chair to the ropes (called a Double Springboard Cross Body Splash), off the apron, diving over top rope to the outside. d. Crawdad DDT Description: Crucifix Pin into a DDT Frequency: ***** Finishing: ** Tidbit: This move is more common as a reversal and rare as an offensive move. The idea is simple, Boudreaux gets into the Crucifix Pin position but before rolling backwards, he continues spinning around and catches them for a DDT instead. Variations: Reversal to Lariat, reversal to punch, reversal to charging opponent, Boudreaux running at opponent. e. Disappearing Act Description: Disappears from action Frequency: ***** Finishing: * Tidbit: Another example of trickery. Guy will disappear and reappear to surprise the opponent. Variations: Under the ring, into the crowd, behind a curtain, etc. f. Double Shot Description: Blast opponent with an illegal object than falls to mat Frequency: *** Finishing: **** Tidbit: It is not below Boudreaux to use an illegal weapon. If the referee takes a bump Guy will grab a weapon and blast the opponent with it than drop to the mat and pretend he was hit as well leaving the referee unsure what happened. This can at times give Guy a cheap win. Variations: Chair shot, title shot, baseball bat, steel pipe, cookie sheet. g. Gator Trap Description: Scissor Kick Frequency: **** Finishing: ** Tidbit: This is his big trademark move. It doesn’t usually finish off opponents but it does leave them in perfect position for the Bourbon Street Hangover. Variations: Bouncing off the ropes at doubled over opponent. h. Moonsault Description: Backflip Frequency: **** Finishing: **** Tidbit: Guy has learned countless variations of the Moonsault. It is a perfect move for Guy as if he has a second he can find a way to pop out a Moonsault. If he can get high enough it can finish off an opponent. In the course of an average match you will see about three Moonsaults. Variations: Off the top rope. Springboard off the ropes. Off the apron. Off the top of a ladder. Off a steel chair. Standing. Off the guard rail. For higher impact, leaps off a chair to the ropes and Springboard Moonsault off the ropes. This is known as a Double Spring Moonsault. i. Possum Description: Falls to the mat Frequency: ***** Finishing: * Tidbit: Possum is a shining example of the trickery style of wrestling. Guy takes a move and oversells. When the opponent drops his guard, Guy attacks. Variations: none j. Rednecked Description: Indian burn to the neck Frequency: *** Finishing * Tidbit: This is about one of the only submission holds Guy Boudreaux knows. Sadly it’s not even very good. The opponent most likely won’t tap out but they will have a major pain in their neck. Variations: none k. Shining Wizard Description: Various situations. It’s basically a flying knee to face. Frequency: **** Finishing: *** Tidbit: This is one of the few offensive moves Guy uses. It can come from anywhere and is mostly used to turn the tides when in trouble. Variations: Standing, opponent on mat. Springboard off opponent’s knee while they are kneeling. Jumping off a chair, guard rail, apron, etc to a standing opponent. Off top rope or springboarded off ropes if opponent is charging. l. Swamp Gas Description: Fake choking to green mist Frequency: ** Finishing: *** Tidbit: This is something a bit rare for Guy Boudreaux. He will act like he is choking or having trouble breathing and when the opponent is off guard he will spit green mist into their face. Variations: none m. Thief~! Description: Stealing a trademark move Frequency: *** Finishing: *** Tidbit: If Boudreaux watches an opponent wrestle enough, or has enough matches against the same person, he can mimic their finisher or trademark moves and use it against them. Variations: none n. Throw Me Something, Mister! Description: Bead toss with a flash to Groin Kick Frequency: ** Finishing: *** Tidbit: This is one of the odder moves Guy Boudreaux uses. He tosses the opponent Mardi Gras beads then lifts his shirt, exposing his chest. While the referee and opponent are wondering what the hell he’s doing, Guy kicks him in the groin. Variations: Plastic green beads, plastic gold beads, and plastic purple beads.
