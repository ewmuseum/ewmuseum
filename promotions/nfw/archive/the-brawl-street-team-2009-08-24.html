The BRAWL Street Team

24 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
jediPREZ — 24 August 2009 NFW will be moving towards a heavier-based house show results format after CRASH 50. This means we may see 5 house shows before a TV Show. As we move towards this concept, it will require team members to help write the shows.
Therefore, I would like anyone to sign up for the BRAWL Street Team using these guidelines:
1) You view narrative matches as a chance to have fun, but not get tediously locked down into the mechanics of a match. I.E. -- describing a headlock is not important nor entertaining.
2) When tasked with a lower-mid card BRAWL match, you will be able to provide something that is 1 page MAX.
3) When tasked with a upper-card match, you will be able to provide a match that's entertaining within a 1-3 page MAX limit.
4) When volunteering for a special/rare BRAWL show that will require more extensive writing, you will be able to provide FIVE-STAR AWESOME SAUCE in a 3+ page format...and trying not to go crazy and hit a 7-10 page mark unless you're writing a designated 30 minute legendary match.
5) You are tired of the formulaic approaches of angle fed's recapping their matches and have studied past BRAWL's to understand that certain POV's/recaps are used for entertainment and have worked very, very well.
6) It will not take you 1 week to write a match. If it would, you must note this in the relevant Lineup Threads once you sign up for this team. If you have signed up for the TV SHOW team, please stay focused on that area unless we really, really need help here.
7) Signing up for this team means you are volunteering for every forthcoming BRAWL show as long as you are on the roster. See #6 for any cases where you will need to take a break/miss a show.
8) If you hate writing TV/PBP style matches, but want to help NFW's results become more timely...this is somewhere we can really, really use you.
Feel free to ask questions or voice concerns, thank you! CURRENT TEAM MEMBERS: JEDIPrez Ehhhhhhhhhhhh! Pete! J.L.V. for Prime Ministah!
