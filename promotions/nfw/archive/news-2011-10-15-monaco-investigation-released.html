Monaco Investigation Released! Posted by Devin Millwood on 15 Oct 2011 Jack Harmen impersonating a nun in the parking lot was the least of NFW's worries, and not surprisingly, just one of the many charges that affected it's schedule in September... but the man who should be WHOLLY blamed faces his own problems with Monaco and comeuppance at this week's Hampton Brawl - JJ DEVILLE. It started innocuously enough, Deville trying to escape another situation of his own doing -- this time utilizing NFW sanctioned and WINDHAM CLAN representative referee Pee Wee Troutman to make a mid-match addendum in his TV CHAMPIONSHIP defense against Jack Bryant in a steel cage. The metaphor not lost as Deville's leap out of the cage led to a ruckus and full-scale backstage brawl between Jack Bryant and anyone standing between him and Deville. What happened next was incorrigible as Deville contacted local police authorities claiming Jack Bryant and NFW wrestlers were conspiring to viciously attack, maim and assault him. Deville's testimony against the roster that night was full of half-truths, full-truths, wide-scale lies and indignantly ignorant accusations. Some of those truths lied within the Wellness Policy currently followed to a crossed tee in NFW -- which means we can't DIVULGE the offenders, but just state they also faced misdemeanor fines. Good thing, this wasn't Istanbul. Other charges/violations: Jack Harmen - fined for impersonating a member of the clergy Lowell - passport issues Joe the Plumber - passport issues Jack Bryant - vandalism Eric Dane - vandalism, unlawful gambling betting on casino grounds involving animals, illegally parking a speedboat on a city square JJ Deville - false testimony, lying to police, bouncing a check to a local charity, eating babies... Yup... Good thing this wasn't Istanbul. There were more charges for wrestlers no longer on NFW's roster, there are some wrestlers that RESIGNED in lieu of the circumstances created by JJ Deville's actions. At Hampton Coliseum, this Sunday night... NFW returns to live TV without much to say about this, but the actions will certainly show themselves when JJ Deville walks out. One has to wonder what kind of case the Executive Branch may be building up in the shadows as well.
