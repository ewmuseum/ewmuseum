Should the NFW HOF induct more than 1 person per year?

3 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
jediPREZ — 3 June 2011 DISCUSS and VOTE. User Poets — 3 June 2011 I voted yes, but only if there's sufficient cause. FOR EXAMPLE... Nova was inducted this year, perhaps Nova and YYJ could've both been inducted as the Season 2 conference champions.
Biron — 3 June 2011 If there is good reason, yes. Otherwise, there's no need to add too much fat to the HOF. Nova — 4 June 2011 Because I'm already in, I vote no. F*ck all y'all. But seriously, yes. The ten-plus-year history is not only one of NFW's strongest creative draws, it's a remarkable feat in and of itself. There are so many characters and handlers who have played a major role in the fed's development, there's no harm in at least opening the floor for more than one nomination per year.
Ford — 4 June 2011 Sure, if you're inducting a tag team. Otherwise, eh. Maybe if you do, do it twice a year. I like that there isn't necessarily a "class" for induction. Rather done on a case by case basis.
Justin — 4 June 2011 I'd say no more than a few. The WfWA used to induct like 15 people per year, by the time it was over everybody was in the HoF.
Hell, I was in it twice IC and once OOC and once as a fed. **** was watered down, son. jediPREZ — 4 June 2011 I think there should be one selection by the 'HEADS' of NFW...and what we might see looking at the voting...is another vote later this year. Heck -- Felix Red, Eli Flair, Joey Melton, JTP, WILD(5)STAR are all options in my head for next year alone!
