Ultrajira Biography Eye Color Brown Nicknames The Beast from the Far East, The Kaiju They Call Brownjira Hometown Billed from "The Far East" Birthday Unknown (rumored to be in his mid-to-late thirties) Height 6'4" Weight 358 Handedness Right Looks -Caucasian male, body type resembles more of the ideal nose tackle in football. Thick barrel chest and body, some fat, but nothing horrendous to speak of. -In the ring, Ultrajira wears several different costumes and face paint in the way Rey Mysterio wears colors to fit the theme (ie, wearing local sports team colors). -For his main theme, he can be seen wearing pants-length black leggings with a picture of his face on either side in a red and black half-half pattern. He wears a Hannibal-style mask around the mouth, but takes it off when he makes his entrance, sticking his tongue out like a lizard. -The face paint underneath the mask is either some type of black and red pattern or black and yellow. Fed Titles H&G World Heavyweight Championship (x3), H&G International Champion (x2), H&G Tag Team Titles w/Andy Sharp (x2). Fed Awards The quietest killer you know. Biography Marcus Brown got his start wrestling around the Southwest territories of wrestling, a member of the famous Brown family. He started his career in All-Star Championship Wrestling with lackluster results. After inking a deal with Honor and Glory Wrestling based out of Tokyo, Marcus became an overnight sensation simply for destroying opposition with complete ruthlessness and displaying great athleticism for a man his size, all without saying a word. He was dubbed "the Human Horror Film" and "Brownjira" by fans for his terrorizing in-ring style executed flawlessly without uttering a word, the latter of which stuck as he went to great success. Now returning to the States as “Ultrajira,” Marcus Brown heads back to the States as the reluctant tag team partner of fellow costumed crusader Captain Justice. Both men wronged by their wrestling families and wanting to start new identities of their own, the Monsters of the Pacific have come together to battle evil here in NFW. …My money, however, is pretty much on NFW ****ing up their minds. Wrestling Style Athletic big man Trainer "Twilight" Ichiro Li / Elijah Brown (brother) Finisher Move Nuclear Claw / U-Crusher Finisher Description Tongan Death Grip / Top Rope Splash Special Moves See movelist. Other Moves Headbutts, kicks, CLUBBERING FOREARMS, Powerslam, Short-Arm Clothesline, Elbow Drop, Standing Body Splash, Gorilla Press Slam, Throat Thrust, Running Hip Attack (The Butt-Butt). Signature Moves: Gravity Beam (Running Knee to head of grounded opponent in the corner; can also use this anywhere an opponent may be propped against steps, guardrails, etc.) Rotary Swing (Giant Swing, mainly a crowd-popping move but when pissed, can slam an opponent into a guardrail/table/etc.) Sonic Burst (Savate Kick) Red Spiral (Swinging Side Slam from the Fallaway Slam Position) Atomic Ray (Reverse STO/Flatliner) Spine Destabilizer (Over the shoulder Spinebuster) Gimmick Silent, brooding monster that generally follows whatever delusional and outlandish ventures Captain Justice partakes in. Has a backstage reputation for being a legit tough man and won't hesitate to rip out your spine, use it to floss, then hand it back to you. Strengths Tough mothereffer, incredible mobility for a big man, relentlessness. Weaknessees Relentlessness may breed fatigue, tunnel vision, and primitive-minded so he may not understand everything going on around him. Ring attire -For his main theme, he can be seen wearing pants-length black leggings with a picture of his face on either side in a red and black half-half pattern. He wears a Hannibal-style mask around the mouth, but takes it off when he makes his entrance, sticking his tongue out like a lizard. -The face paint underneath the mask is either some type of black and red pattern or black and yellow. Theme Music "Superhero" by Daft Punk.
