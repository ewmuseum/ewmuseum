NFW Brawl 30: A Left Turn at Albuquerque, 15 June 2010

15 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 30: A Left Turn at Albuquerque 15 Jun 2010 University Arena (University of New Mexico), Albuquerque, New Mexico (seats 18,018) A Proper Welcoming of the Show (FADE IN. Sitting in his dressing-room, Lord Coyner Pollard is looking over some manila folders. Nearby, Jeeves is waiting patiently, dressed in his pristine uniform) Pollard: (closing one of the folders) And this is your recommendation, Jeeves? Jeeves: Yes, M’lud. Pollard: You realise that your reputation shall be damaged should We fail. Jeeves: Yes, M’lud. Pollard: Have you anything further to add? Jeeves: No, M’lud. I feel that these two are the best suited to the task and will offer adequate assistance in this contest. Pollard: Well, then. As much as We are loathe to align Ourselves with any unsaved Colonists, We shall go into combat, for one night only, with Shawn Hart… and High Flyer. Jeeves: Excellent, M’lud. Pollard: We just pray that their recent dalliances do not spill over into Our quest. (FADE OUT) Bienvenido al Nuevo Mexico! V/O: "The following program is brought to you by Sirius/XM Satellite Radio and ESEN." MUSICUP: "And the Band Played On" – the Black Crowes The setup is familiar, a budding and comforting notion that we’re sticking with some things. We’re our in the summertime, desert air…a large desk radio jockey setup adjacent to a mammoth production truck bearing the Crimson Revolution Star of New Frontier Wrestling. Yet, tonight’s slightly different. A little more raucous around the edges. College Kids that witnessed the spectacle known as NFW having a hard time comin’ on back down from the trip… RICE: "WELCOME ONE! WELCOME ALL! We are in NUEVO MEXICO, down at the PIT University Arena! That crackling audio comin’ through your satellite radio somewhere on I-15 or maybe you’ve just turned in LIVE! ON E-S-E-N! We’re on the radio, we’re on the tele and we just blasted out three hours of professional wrestling mayhem! FOUR MATCHES go down including a wild, wild finish to the show as once again, a man like Legion puts out a challenge that only he would think is APROPRO in this place!" (CUTTO: LEGION in front of an NFW backdrop…) RICE: "Legion put out the challenge for an Icepack on a Pole match to Lord Coyner Pollard even after EL PRESIDENTE Eddie Mayfield announced the Around the USA in 80 Days Tour! Before we even hit our first stop, Legion’s throwing a monkey wrench into the Grandmaster DS’s plans and the Executive Office WAS NOT PLEASED. Part of me thinks that Mayfield’s protecting some British Interests NOT named Legion and he changes the rules to a SIX-MAN TAG setup, no pins going down until the Icepack is taken OFF the pole." (MUSIC CUT. RICE laughs and wolf-whistles) RICE: "Now, we know that FutureQuis and American Panda were Legion’s two choices, but not until tonight…not until that opening moment with Lord Coyner Pollard was anyone in the PIT knowing would be the main event as he selected two GLOBAL superstars in Shawn Hart and High Flyer as his partners…heh, think Mayfield stacked the odds there? Guess what? It wasn’t enough, but good ‘ol Eddie MIGHT have had more tricks up his sleeve! We’ll get to that later, we also had two matches with one of them apparently affecting the Around the USA in 80 Days National Championship setup!" (FADEOUT) RICE: "When we return with recaps of those two matches, maybe you’ll be able to guess who interfered in the main event!" Cary Turkington vs. Mike McGee BRAWL REPORT Submitted by: Hugh Jacksmen VIA DROIDARMY It’s not my real name. I swear. Then again, would we really believe that Mike McGee and Cary Turkington are using their real names? Well, maybe Cary…he still looks like he signed up for something drunk and just keeps drinking to forget he’s here. On this night, he’d be going up against Mike McGee, who’s simply just going by Mike McGee right now. No Middle Management, No Accounting, No Office Space shirt, tie and khaki combos. McGee came out in simple royal blue wrestling trunks first, Turkington lumbered on down the aisle second, decked out in a wifebeater and blue jeans. Turkington bumrushed McGee as he let the ref check his boots for weapons, Turkington launched out his huge arsenal of right fist! right cross! right hook! Right uppercut! McGee finally blocked a right hand, jabbing out of the predicament and bouncing off the ropes before hitting a jumping forearm to Turkington’s pudding forehead. Turkington was up quick, taking the standard armdrag, armdrag, armdrag into armbar by McGee, which the raucous drunks didn’t really like. McGee caught on quick, springboarding off the ropes into another armdrag, then a dropick and a high elevation elbow drop that missed. Turkington mushed and beat down McGee a little more sending him careening into the turnbuckles, but missing a freight train knee. McGee hits a bridging back suplex, a couple of rollups and cannot seal the deal. Turkington comes back with some fishhooks and eye rakes, then moves onto some slams and a kneedrop. Turkington goes up top, wobbles a little and misses a fistdrop. McGee with a victory roll out of nowhere…and gets the pin! AAAAAAAAAND THE CROWD GOES WILD. Well, maybe not so much. Result: Mike McGee wins via pinfall. The Mighty Impala vs. Ultrajira MUSICUP: 2 x 4 – Blind Melon. RICE: "HEYOHHHHHHHH! WELCOME BACK TO THE BRAWL! Ladies and gents, what a night we had as you just got caught up on a DROIDARMY report from Hugh Jacksmen…if that’s his real name! He seemed a little too even-keeled in his voice for me, didn’t seem TOO EXCITED, knowwhati’msayin? KNOW IT!? Well, before we hit our big two matches we had a some…JUST MESSED UP NEWS. SOME FREAKIN’ FRAKKIN’ BULL—UH-UH, NATRONE! Don’t say it man! Alright, I’m on the down-low right? I know what’s going on in this fed on some peeled away levels and I thought we were going ALL-JAPAN. ULTRAJIRA vs. TSUNAMI. Tsunami’s National Championship Around the Days in 80 Days finale stravaganza up in the air…and NO. Word is ROUGH. Tsunami’s GONE. OUT. NOT MEDICALLY CLEARED." (CUTTO: ULTRAJIRA in the ring, seeing PRESIDENT MAYFIELD on the FRONTIERtron…MAYFIELD’s reading a statement apparently…" RICE: "With news passed along by VERONICA ABRAMS RUMSFELD, the manager of Dorchester Stratton and FORRRRRRMER partner of Tsunami…apparently, the man’s had a few too many concussions in the ring and New Frontier wasn’t willing to foot this bill. But Eddie’s going to SUPER MARIO the situation and one-up the proceedings. Not only will Ultrajira fight for Tsunami’s spot in the National Championship future, but also his NFW CONTRACT." (CUTTO: Green, red and yellow lights swaying…) RICE: "Now, some of you may have read the news on Captain Justice…the former partner of Ultrajira, seemingly fired for some performance enhancements showing up on his narcotics test administered by New Frontier Wrestling. Say what you will about the policies, but jokes are one thing with Erik Black, but at least we’re WEEDing out that stuff huh?" (CUTTO: THE MIGHTY IMPALA wearing a bright yellow and royal blue robe, diamond and gold necklaces, bracelets, etc. hanging off his various appendages and neck. IMPALA is escorted by a row of male tuxedo servants dropping various flower petals on the way to the ring…) RICE: "Now, here’s the interesting part…before the match started, there was an Executive Office amendment to the match effectively stating that this one was gonna be under DIPLOMATIC IMMUNITY RULES." (MUSIC CUT. CUEUP: Canned Crowd boos!) RICE: "HELLO! NEW SOUND FX? GET OUTTA HEAH SAL GIORDANO!" (QUICK CUTTO’s: ULTRAJIRA and IMPALA chopping the hell out of each other, but ULTRAJIRA wins it out and hits a thrust kick to win the battle! ULTRAJIRA with a back body drop! ULTRAJIRA with a quick powerslam and quick two! ULTRAJIRA on top and getting pushed off by a servant, the referee seeing this…and doing nothing! IMPALA choking ULTRAJIRA with no five-count being made…) RICE: "I mean, if you want to talk about the odds being stacked and VERY evident? Ultrajira got away with nothing, while the MIGHTY Impala…catch the emphasis there, the MIGHTY Impala was basically able to do what he wanted. Eventually, that was just going to catch up to one-half of the former Monsters of the Pacific…" (QUICK CUTTO’s: IMPALA hitting a Michinikou Driver! IMPALA locking in the ‘DIPLOMACY IN ACTION" Cross-Arm Chickenwing…ULTRAJIRA tapping!) RICE: "So not only did the Mighty Impala win the match, send Ultrajira packing, but he’s ALSO in this National Championship picture instead of Tsunami…WITH Diplomatic Immunity and if you’re Lord Coyner Pollard and you have a good history with PRESIDENT Eddie Mayfield along the same lines…either you’re goose just got cooked or the odds were improving in your favor. That answer would come in the main event, if you didn’t figure it out by now…but before that, BROCK ALYAS…SOBER? BWUHHHHHHHHHH!?!?!?" (FADEOUT) RICE: "We'll be right back!" Sean Stevens (c) vs. Brock Alyas RICE: Alright fans, we’re back on the BRAWL! We’re going to take it to the DYNAMIC DUO, the STRAWBERRIES and WIMBLEDON CREAM of the NEW FRONTIER – Kerry O’Connor and Lamont Hollywood are still in the Press Area of the PIT UNIVERSITY of ARENA! They’re doing some highlight work tonight, some recapping of the PURE CHAMPIONSHIP as ‘THE KING OF PLANET EARTH’ SEAN STEVENS, the self-proclamied champion took on… BROCK ALYAS. The same Brock Alyas that’s been arrested for trafficking, possession and been M.I.A. since we kicked back off in May! …for the PURE…the PURE Olympic Drug-Tested to Qualify for a shot as a shot to our current champ, JAY-TEE-PEE, who passed his test, but is so certifiably insane he’s still being ridiculed as a crackhead. (wolf whistles!) What a world! BOSTON’S FINEST! WOODMAN! TAKE IT AWAY! (FADEIN: KERRY O’CONNOR standing in front of an NFW backdrop wearing a three-piece suit and holding a microphone. Next to him is LAMONT HOLLYWOOD, dressed considerably sharper. Sorry O’Connor, that’s just the way it is, pal) O’CONNOR: Welcome back to THE PIT in Albuquerque, New Mexico! BRAWL continues here on ESEN with a World Heavyweight Title match…well, sort of. H’WOOD: What do you mean, "sort of?" Brock Alyas versus Sean Stevens for the World Heavyweight Title, Beanfry- call it that way ‘cause that’s how it is! O’CONNOR: Except it’s for the PURE World Heavyweight Title, an accolade placed on Stevens by his new manager, Bloodhunt. They’re calling this thing legit, but Joe The Plumber is STILL the NFW World Champion proper, despite President Mayfield’s wishes to the contrary. H’WOOD: Hey, my weight’s behind PURE, the only World Title that counts around here! I don’t know about you, but I sure as heck don’t want to be represented by some guy running around here, calling himself ‘THE BOUSE’, chugging all-purpose cleaner like it’s Schweppes. O’CONNOR: In any event, Stevens put his self-awarded PURE title on the line tonight against Brock Alyas, making his return to the ring after a long-layoff that may or may not have included rehab. Judging by his appearance, though, we’re thinking he stopped freebasing all of three days ago. So let’s go to the highlights… (CUTTO: BROCK ALYAS leaning up against the turnbuckle looking pale as a ghost, cold sores lining his mouth, scratching his forearm until blood cakes beneath his fingernails. He’s shaking, and QUICKLY he turns to his side! Phew, no one’s there. He digs into his pocket to pull out a snickers bar, and shoves it into his mouth forcefully, the whole thing, chewing rapidly) O’CONNOR: Brock was NOT looking good on this night in particular. H’WOOD: The kid’s in desperate need of a speedball, O’Connor. He might just need a little bit of crack, and then he’ll be good to go. O’CONNOR: I think that’s a bad idea. (CUTTO: ‘XXX’ SEAN STEVENS emerges confidently from the entrance with the PURE World Heavyweight Title strapped around his waist. Soon after, BLOODHUNT appears at his side, as they both walk to the ring) O’CONNOR: Stevens, ever confident, nearly 10 months removed from taking Joe The Plumber to his limit in an unforgettable main event matchup, now comes to the ring wearing the PURE World Heavyweight Title, an honor bestowed on him by the two-time NFW World Heavyweight Champion, Bloodunt. H’WOOD: See? There are your credentials, right there. O’CONNOR: Brock’s normally known for his brutal bullying of opponents, but on this night he looked sluggish and weak compared to his usual self. Stevens started out gaining the upper hand with a series of technical gains, wearing down the physically distressed Brock Alyas more and more. H’WOOD: Brilliant game plan on the part of Stevens. I heard Brock’s been calling his sponsor every night, begging to be kept from going downtown for one more sweet hit off the pipe. Its obvious Brock was in no shape to wrestle, and Stevens comes to PLAY HARD always. (CUTTO: Brock agonizing in a Stevens figure four leglock. He’s pounding the mat with his fists, the crowd giving him the pity cheers. Finally, he works up enough energy to reverse the hold) O’CONNOR: And of all the things we could’ve predicted for this bout, one of them wouldn’t be the crowd willing Brock Alyas to life, but hell, they’re rooting for him to get clean! The people love a good comeback story, don’t they Lamont? H’WOOD: Who, the Roman mob? They should be grateful to live in a time where the only PURE World Champion can be seen wrestling on a weekly basis. (CUTTO: Stevens breaks the reversed hold, but Brock is first to his feet, though stumbling. He’s sweating bullets, both from the match and the crack-cocaine withdrawals. Seriously, this dude wants to COOK. But those times are gone, and now Brock is shaking his fists, reveling in the crowd cheers that only get louder as he challenges Stevens to come at him. Stevens looks, left, looks right, furrows his brow and CHARGES at Brock who spears him!) O’CONNOR: Stevens should’ve stuck to the plan of wearing down Brock, but there was a price to be paid for charging at a man on his second wind. He was taken down immediately and HAMMERED with lefts, rights, more lefts, and finally Brock brought him up for a piledriver- TWO COUNT ONLY! H’WOOD: Apparently he’s switched from crack to SPEED. O’CONNOR: Unfortunately for Brock and the fans who’ve come to like him, the burst of energy was short-lived. After tossing Stevens into the turnbuckle, he charged in and got a kick to the face. From there, Stevens had control and wouldn’t relinquish… (CUTTO: Stevens hops onto the top turnbuckle and drills Brock with a missile dropkick) (CUTTO: Stevens putting Brock’s head into the ground with a floating DDT. He goes for the cover- two count) O’CONNOR: A series of high impact maneuvers had Brock gasping for air, itching his skin, and all kinds of things related both to the match and past drug abuse. H’WOOD: Hey, don’t make excuses for the guy, this is what happens when you take on a clean, all-natural athlete the caliber of Sean Stevens. O’CONNOR: With Bloodhunt pounding on the mat, screaming for Stevens to "put this drug addict out of his misery," Stevens did just that… (CUTTO: Stevens, sweaty and smiling to a booing crowd, takes Brock up for an inverted suplex and cuts him HARD with an X-Terminator) O’CONNOR: One X-Terminator, and that was all she wrote for Brock Alyas. The winner and STILL…what was that again? H’WOOD: PURE World Heavyweight Champion, Beanfry, GET IT RIGHT. O’CONNOR: Yes, right. Stevens retains his belt, and Brock Alyas goes back to the drawing board, but most likely rehab. I have a feeling as he overcomes his physical addiction issues, we might be in for seeing a new and dare I say improved Brock Alyas? H’WOOD: What are you asking me for? You know I think he’s dirt. O’CONNOR: Back after this… Icepack on a Pole Match (FADEIN: Out from the entranceway steps LORD COYNER POLLARD, white ruffled shirt that reeks of aristocratic snobbery, followed out by HIGH FLYER and SHAWN HART. LCP snatches a ‘USA’ fansign and casually rips it up, keeping his stare dead ahead the whole time) RICE: "Back at the Brawl here, and in the main event it was ICE PACK ON A POLE as you see the Lord Coyner Pollard lead out his team of High Flyer and Shawn Hart." (CUTTO: LEGION coming out in cutoff jeans and a grungy t-shirt, leading AMERICAN PANDA and FUTURE EQUIS to the ring) RICE: "They’d be up against Legion’s team including American Panda and Future Equis, and it didn’t take long for Legion to make his presence known. Right away he went after the Good Lord, engaging the traditional Brit wrestler in a slugfest. And while Panda went to chomp Flyer’s JUGG-YOO-LAH…turn around!" (CUTTO: BAM! Shawn Hart hits Panda with a superkick to the back of the head, sending him through the ropes to the outside) RICE: "Rabbit kick to the medulla scrambled the hell out of Panda’s eggs! Meanwhile, Lord Coyner sought to slow Legion’s quick pace at every turn, utilizing hammerlocks, chin locks, armbars, anything he could to wear him down." (CUTTO: Rest hold- the Lord’s neutralized Legion with a sleeper as he shouts to both Flyer and Hart to grab the ice pack!) RICE: "The back and forth would last a good ten minutes before Future Equis FINALLY broke the stalemate by grabbing the icepack while hoisted up on his partner’s shoulders. But just as Panda let him down…" (CUTTO: High Flyer dropkicks the ice pack into Future Equis’ face) RICE: "…Flyer killed his momentum on the spot! With the ice pack FINALLY pulled down, pinfalls would now be counted. Future Equis would almost score one over High Flyer moments later when he irish whipped him into a hurricanrana, but Lord Coyner made the last second save." (CUTTO: Legion and American Panda are on the outside, with High Flyer and Shawn Hart splayed out on a table) RICE: "Team Legion would take control later in the match, but they overcompensated and paid dearly. With Future Equis on the verge of finishing Lord Coyner Pollard in the ring, referee Speranza would object to Legion and Panda’s use of tables on the outside. The Lord was down and damn near out after a cradle piledriver from Equis, but an unwanted visitor would change all that…" (CUTTO: Running in from the crowd is the MIGHTY IMPALA, who grabs Future Equis from behind and locks him into a crossface chicken wing) RICE: "Who’s that: a UN envoy? NO! It’s the Mighty Impala! And his submission hold would give the Lord enough time to recover, and before you could say PRINCE AKEEM, the Impala was OFF THE SCENE!" (CUTTO: Lord Coyner Pollard, finally on his feet, locks in a full nelson on a wobbly Future Equis) RICE: "And you just knew Pollard was going to capitalize! PICTURE PERFECT dragon suplex, and Speranza was there in time for the count. ONE-TWO-THREE and by the time Legion and Panda realized what happened, Lord Coyner Pollard was celebrating like Cornwallis after the Battle of Camden! Clearly the greatest British victory on American soil since then with a MAJOR ASSIST from the African envoy with diplomatic immunity! The Impala strikes again, and this is more than parking tickets he’s gotten away with. Lord’s team will determine the initial seeding placements for the National Championship finale of the AROUND THE USA IN 80 DAYS tour…and woo! I’m out of breath and out of time! The fire’s out in Albuquerque, next stop: Reloaded!"
