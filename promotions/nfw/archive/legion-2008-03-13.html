Legion — 13 March 2008 MEMBER INFORMATION: Name: Matt D Email Address: [detail withheld] OR [detail withheld] AIM/Yahoo Messenger: n/a Preferred Method of Handling: (FW Promo, Freestyle RP, Angle/Storyline, Hybrid): Hybrid of FW promo and angle/storyline
Best Way to Contact you: (PM/Email/IM): PM, Email or MSN if you have it WRESTLER INFORMATION: Name: Legion Nicknames: The Devil's Reject Height: 5'10 Weight: 225 Handedness: Left Looks: Think of a combination of Raven and Jimmy Jacobs, mostly either wearing the For We Are Many shirt or bands like Ministry, Lesbian Bed Death etc
Hailing From: Manchester, England Disposition: Heel Gimmick: A manipulator, likes to see how things plan out before getting involved, in some cases (as seen at Crashmas 2007) he will set the chess pieces as he sees fit...
Ring Attire: Band t-shirt and denim cut off jeans Theme Music: Prelude 12/21 intro into Pet by A Perfect Circle Ring Entrance: With 'Sickness' carrying the briefcase containing various weapons he analyzes his oppoment methodically as he walks down the ramp absolutely focused ignoring the cat-calls etc by the crowd
Tactics/Style: A mixture of the Hardcore and Lucha, think a mixture of Raven, Jimmy Jacobs and Judas Mesias (if he had more charisma and didn't need Jim Mitchell to talk for him)
MATCHWRITING GUIDE: His basic moves are strong style punches and kicks with a mixture of MMA muay thai kicks, Power moves include the hangman's DDT, given his history with 'Stamford' feel free to give him some upper card wrestler moves (way to get cheap heat...), when the wrestler has been stunned a whip into the turnbuckle may begin the Ninth Gate sequence which is a Super Brainbuster from the top rope and then to add insult or pain if needed he'll wait for them to get up, buzzsaw kick/curbstomp combo called the Six Million Dollar Drive to Hell!
CHARACTER BIOGRAPHY: Returning from a self-imposed exile bought on by apathy working for a fed that impersonated WWE wrestlers he began to train for a more 'Indy' style by watching tapes, he's been at many significant events not only in NFW's history such as the reveal of Wasp/El Toro being Miles and Mayfield and their reunion as the Professionals but at the last FWO event which culminated in the 'Neo Riot'. If not there he's watched many tapes of his opponents which gives him a psychological advantage.
He's also looking for recruits to join his stable (which is inspired by the Age Of The Fall crossed with Project Mayhem) who will do anything to unnerve any enemy...
Legion — 7 September 2008 obviously replace Sickness with Luci4 in the entrance... also following the upcoming Crash legion's music will be the Judas Kiss by Metallica
Legion — 26 October 2008 the new music for Legion (and this will be the theme for the foreseeable future for Devil's Rejects as a team) is Declaration by Bleeding Through with finnis, fatalis,spei as the intro on special occasions
Legion — 26 September 2009 Following Crash 50... theme music will be The Overly Dramatic Truth by El-P
