Eddie Mayfield Biography Eye Color Green Nicknames "Hot Property" Hometown Jacksonville, Florida Birthday 3/11/1976 Height 6'1 Weight 250 Alias El Presidente, Bonnie Mayfield's Only Son, The Dirtiest Player in the Pool Hall, Zartan Handedness Right Looks Eddie Mayfield closely resembles Kiefer Sutherland, ten years back from modern-era Kiefer, now with slicked-back hair and a red/brown heel beard. Not chiseled, Mayfield's physique models a 80's NWA heels' look - a brawler's tone, with a bit of a gut, hairy chest. Eddie Mayfield is notoriously known to smoke Camel brand cigarettes. Fed Titles UWA World Championship, NFW President SWA World Champion, AAWC TV Champion CSWA UNIFIED Tagteam Champion (W/ CRAIG MILES) Fed Awards 2008 Mid-Year Heel Other Feds CSWA, IWC, SWA, AAWC Other Fed Titles SWA World Champion, IWC Tag Champion, AAWC TV Champion, CSWA Presidential Champion, Unified Tag Team Champion Wrestling Style Mid-South Old School Finisher Move Screwjob 2: Electric Boogaloo Finisher Description Samurai Driver '84 (Nasty Sitout Tombstone) Other Moves Camel Clutch, Hot Shot, 2nd Rope Kneedrop, "Fahrenheit 451" (Cobra Clutch Sleeper/Russian Leg Sweep combo) Flicked Cigarette to the eyes, "FPS - First Person Shooter" Scorpion Deathlock Entrance Music 'Voodoo Chile' -- Ben Harper Gimmick Current NFW President, semi-inactive wrestler.
