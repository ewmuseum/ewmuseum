RumorMILL w/Devin Millwood 10/12/10 Posted by Devin Millwood on 12 Oct 2010 Devin Millwood brings you the latest rumors. This week: Major announcement forthcoming? Let's get something straight: I hate "major announcements." Why's that, you ask? Because they are rarely major. It's a cheap way to hype your product, with little to no substance. "Major announcement! The US Title will be defended live on PPV!" Are you kidding me? That's not a major announcement, that's just an announcement. With my loathing for the trivial use of the term "major announcement" in mind, I bring you...a major announcement. For real. Sources tell me a deal is in the works, damn near sealed, that would involve NFW's purchase of a major American wrestling company and the creation of a brand new competition using the season format. This ain't from Eddie Mayfield's limo driver, or the guy who drives the breakfast truck Fiona Love gets her coffee from. I'm talking the kind of sources who, when they tell you to place a bet, YOU PLACE THAT BET. Details are still foggy, but the deal is as good as done. Nothing will be signed for certain until everything is set and ready to go, but here's what I'm being told: -NFW will honor most of, if not all of the contracts in place at said company, should the buyout go through. -The new competition will be run in a seasonal format, on a brand new television show separate from Reloaded and Brawl, using a combination of the incoming roster, the current roster, and prospective talent. -This is NOT Season 3, and will not in any way involve Ultratitle. It's likely that the competition will be run under the bought out franchise's banner, owned and produced by NFW with perhaps a third party funding source (this last bit cannot be confirmed for sure). -No word on when the show might air, but some are guessing December 2010 or January/February 2011. It will, of course, air on ESEN. -There will be a direct relationship between this new show and NFW's programming, but I'm being shutout from any further info. Needless to say, this would be a major shakeup, perhaps the biggest since the announcement of Ultratitle Season 1. Now more than ever, keep your ears to the ground and your eyes open.
