AFTERSHOCK Post RR Road Report, 1 April 2014

Originally recorded on April 1, 2014. . . (CUTTO: ESEN STUDIOS -- A front-facing shot of a studio desk where WENDY DREWES and TED CALDWELLER sitting across from each other, behind them is a 60-inch flatscreen plasma television inlayed into a cherry-oak backdrop with cuts forming a diamond pattern around the screen. The screen flashes the NFW CRIMSON STAR logo spinning in a repeating animation. Various photos of today’s and yesterday’s iconic ESEN-based superstars and events are hung around the wooden backdrop including TEMPUESTA DEL FUERTE, VIVIAN RYCONIK arm-in-arm with a smiling SHANE SOUTHER, RAYNE taking PA notes for 'COMMISSIONER' MANSON, HORNET and EVAN AHO at the NFW SOUTH training center, LEYENDA DE OCHO and KERRY KUROYAMA backstage at BRAWLSTATE, IMPULSE holding up the NFW WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP, ARMANDO MONTEZUMA adjusting his turban, MIKE RANDALLS standing face-to-face with MARCUS CAMERON, CANCER standing above a fallen CASTOR STRIFE. In between the diamond-cut patterns within the wood are animated red and white LED lights. DREWES has her blonde hair pulled back in a ponytail, wearing a blue blouse buttoned to the top. She’s also got on thick, beige-rimmed spectacles. CALDWELLER is sporting a white and red checkered shirt, dark brown tweed sports coat with no tie. He’s clean-shaven with his curly black hair cut short and receding slightly.) WENDY DREWES: "Welcome back to tonight's telecast of ESEN Ringside, I'm Wendy Drewes and with me is Ted Caldweller. Now, just over two weeks ago NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING held the 2014 incarnation of the RANDOM RUMBLE that still has fans buzzing not only from what happened that night, but the resonating effects of the federation's future. Nobody would have figured that within the same twenty-four hours, we'd possibly watch Jack Bryant's last match as men like Cancer, Mike Randalls, JJ Deville returned. . . with all that buzzing down, we thought it'd be interesting as we catch up with the NEW FRONTIER on the road. Long-time followers of the New Frontier know that the BRAWLS, RELOADEDs and certainly events like RANDOM RUMBLE get their shine and television time on ESEN Television, let alone our little news program here. But what happens in-between, the various house shows and non-televised or satellite radio broadcast events certainly help NFW keep the bottom line out of the red zone, but also push the needle on the development on the New Frontier from the bottom to top shelf." ED CALDWELLER: "Right, Wendy . . . and it's the time that helps not only the younger superstars gain their foothold and grip with the on-the-road lifestyle required in NFW, but also starts giving us a glimpse into what machinations and showcases we may be able to expect when the Q2 kickoff and fabled ROAD TO FUTURESHOCK begins." DREWES: "The tagline's been delivered as 'THE PAST IS THE FUTURE, THE BEGINNING IS THE END' - and no doubt an allusion to a lot of the bubbling lava in the now rumbling locker room volcano. AND YES, that is a pun to what we're DUBBING (CALDEWELLER: "HEY NOW!") the AFTERSHOCK report for the Random. . . RUMBLE. Three pitstops in the Illinoiscorridor as BRAWLOPTIMUS PRIME starts getting oil-changed and fueled up for BRAWL 72 in Wichita, Kansas. As a special treat, we're going to bring in NATRONE RICE live on the line to go over as much possible from these three shows and maybe what we can expect over the next couple of months before BRAWL 72 hits the air." CALDWELLER: "I didn't believe there was a place called Skokie until I saw his Facebook photo stream." DREWES: “Obviously, you don’t know where the world’s best homentaschen are!” CALDWELLER: “GUHHHHHHHH?” DREWES: “OH YEAH, BUDDY.” (SFX: The piercing shrill of a DIAL-UP MODEM CONNECTION!) DREWES: “Uh-oh, I think we’ve got Natrone coming in!” (UPPER-LEFT THUMBNAIL GFX: A profile shot of NATRONE RICE smiling. . .) RICE: (V/O) “HEY THERE WENDY!” DREWES: “Trouble’s brewin’ in studio, Natrone! Ted’s dissin’ on Skokie, Illinois!” RICE: (V/O) “The World’s LARGEST village!?” DREWES: “YES.” RICE: (V/O) “Whoa.” CALDWELLER: “According to my research on wikipedia, I am afraid to make a joke or Craig Miles will come back to life and kill me.” DREWES: “Ok, we are going right past that off-ramp and to the on-ramp somewhere on an Illinois highway with NATRONE RICE, host of the NFW BRAWL and all-things ROAD MAXIMUS PRIME himself as chief correspondent and play-by-play announcer for the NFW AFTERSHOCK house show barnburner tour that’s now going down south to WICHITA, KANSAS over the next month to an official SIMULCAST BRAWL that we’ll all be apart of.” RICE: (V/O) “That’s right, Wendy, but let me tell you that the barns were BURNING and not because of the Patron Saint of Violence. . . well, maybe a LITTLE ‘cause of that man, but this rosters hot for a fight on Saturday Night as the ROAD TO FUTURESHOCK 2 begins. After what 30 men experienced at RANDOM RUMBLE, the spotlight, the pageantry, the sheer test of your will against psychotic physical brutality. . . who wouldn’t want to be shuffling their way to the top of the deck!?” DREWES: “So, that’s great Natrone. And aside from the opening house show in Skokie, NFW did pass through Joliet and Springfield this week. Hopefully, we’ll see the interns get some good videos up on youtube this weekend. So, let’s start catching up and seeing how the dominoes are starting to fall. . . out of the gate, we heard from the CHROMATIC DRAGONS after the Random Rumble and this tag-team’s starting to really cement themselves as the biggest threat to the GRAVEROBBERS’ hold on the EMT Championships.” RICE: (V/O) “That’s no joke, Wendy. These guys are training and tuning up for what they are expecting is going to be a BRUTAL contest that will also probably, most likely and DEFINITELY not go according to plan. . . ‘CAUSE YOU CAN’T PLAN AGAINST CHAOS, the keeper of Secrets and leader of the BLACK MARKET, Legion. . . oh, we will get to him in a moment, but back to the Dragons. Check THIS. They squash SST in Skokie, Can-Am Connection in Joliet and BIG THINKERS in Springfield, and after every match they make sure to get across ONE THING.” CALDWELLER: “They want the Graverobbers.” RICE: (V/O) “DING! That’s a hundred points, Ted! But Legion. . . hehehehehe, THIS GUY. He hears it all and this week, right after one of those tune-ups. . . he came out to the ring without a match. THEN SITS down in the ring and pontificates about Futureshock, the End of Times BlahBlahBlah Bringing back the dead. I’m pretty sure he may have said that Ozymandias has awakened the Apocalypse Now of our generation, but before that SOLILOQUY developed. . . WELL, HEY NOW! THE CHROMATIC DRAGONS CHARRRRRRRRRRRRGE the ring and that makes the Graverobbers appear and we’ve got a BRAWL not shown on a BRAWL unfortunately, ‘cause it took out a concession and broke about 11 chairs and 7 shovels. By the end of the fight, ambulance chasing lawyers were buying scalped tickets!” DREWES: “Wow, so that’s definitely going to reach a head soon or someone’s getting arrested. Nothing like a traditional tag-team rivalry for those belts.” CALDWELLER: “Well, that seems to be going on with more than a few belts lately.” RICE: (V/O) “Oh yeah, Ted. . . in Joliet, CALVIN CARLTON showed up with the BIG BELT in a blue bonnet basket and cut a promo on the mental state of Jack Harmen. . .” DREWES: “What about his mental state? (RICE laughs) Seriously!” RICE: (V/O) “Unfortunately, he’s in a good place mentally Wendy ‘cause he’s already calling out the banners to be made for Jack Harmen and Castor Strife at Futureshock, and how Castor better look at his 401K plan like Jack Bryant should have. . . ‘cause he’s going to be really regretting the investment firm Eddie Mayfield would choose.” CALDWELLER: “Any sighting of Cancer? I mean, we gotta figure that might be the reason any NFW fan may be tuning into this segment of RINGSIDE!” RICE: (V/O) “Leave it to the local indie wrestling promotion to put up their best and brightest, who got turned into a bright smear on the canvas after CANCER had his way with him. . .but we’ve already got someone trying to cut in line in front of Castor Strife and Eddie Mayfield. DA MAN XAVIER LANGSTON came out and CONFRONTED CANCER, but the psychotic sadist. . . exited out the other side of the ring with a large smile. Interestingly enough, there were too many fireworks with DA MAN and DA COMMISH, but Windham and the Dirty Deeds tag-team got a victory over The Original Showstoppers.” CALDWELLER: “How the mighty have fallen.” RICE: (V/O) “Speaking of which, JJ Deville tried to speak on behalf of a local children’s hospital in Skokie and got booed out of the ring. REALLY WEIRD. But just to take us back to normal in Joliet, his former HFC mate BLAINE HOLLYWOOD stepped inside the squared circle and earned an easy victory in a tune-up match, then was interviewed by his father, mentor and manager, LAMONT HOLLYWOOD. (DREWES groans) Where I’ll be honest. . . quite surprised to hear the venom and threats delivered to Dorchester Stratton and Mike Randalls. And you’ll be hearing a great deal about the fact that HOLLYWOOD ROYALTY has the DIVINE BIRTHRIGHT in NFW.” CALDWELLER: “I saw there’s already a youtube video up on Malik Anderson. . .” RICE: (V/O) “HAHA! You can’t go two seconds without mentioning Malik Anderson if you just talked about Blaine Hollywood, and that’s why I LOVE their newfound rivalry, because of how much they must HATE this. But yes, Teddy. . . Malik flat-out might have broken this local wrestler’s jaw on the show in Springfield. THE FALCON PUNCH would make Superman wet his tights, I am telling you! And Malik made a big-time call-out to not only Blaine Hollywood, but TELEVISION CHAMPION and former stablemate DORCHESTER STRATTON.” CALDWELLER: “Malik’s had his opportunities, you gotta wonder if he should get another one over someone like Kerry Kuroyama.” RICE: (V/O) “After K2’s showing at Random Rumble, I think the world is his oyster in that regard. And while the man didn’t come across as inconsiderate in an interview with Devin Millwood in the ring, he has strongly hinted he wants a shot at ANY title in NFW and for Teddy Alexander to be ready when they cross paths in the ring. He won’t need a taser to make him pay for his crimes.” DREWES: ‘NICE! He may not have won Random Rumble, but quite the elimination list. . . and there’s more than a few analysts that recognized how much his efforts enabled Castor Strife’s victory over Cancer.” RICE: (V/O) “And Wendy, I wouldn’t be remiss without providing some FASHION UPDATES on the NFW runways. (DREWES: “Oh god.”) First of all, the town was all atwitter and heavily on twitter as SHANQIUA CARLTON’S SHE-UNIQUE SUMMER SOLANGE STREETWALK fashion show attracted thousands behind the Joilet chicken wire factory. (DREWES laughs) And in sadder news. . . Jack Bryant merchandise was looking pretty lonely even at a SEVENTY-FIVE PERCENT. . . discount.” (DREWES pouts) DREWES: “I am SO nutted down over this!” CALDWELLER: “Maybe he’ll finally get healthy and get back to us.” DREWES: (back to smiling) “Ok, we’ve got a minute left, Natrone. . . I know you got more!” RICE: (V/O) “Definitely do, Wendy. . . and I think everyone in New Frontier Wrestling agrees with Ted’s words there for the record. But when one loses a spot. . . that can always mean ONE stays open. If you thought things were getting wild with MACBRY at the RANDOM RUMBLE. . . he got a NON-TITLE shot at DORCHESTER STRATTON for his efforts!” DREWES: “WHAT!? OH. MY. GOD.” RICE: (V/O) “Well. . . STARDUST LIGHTNING didn’t strike silver and platinum, but definitely had Dorchester off his game from the start! I don’t think many power brokers are dealing with this in the boiler rooms and Veronica Abrams Rumsfeld never dares enter the East Village when she’s in Manhattan. But Dorchester’s ego supercedes anything else and this fight got hot and heavy. . . and then in a complete mindgames moment that may have slightly backfired. . . MACBRY laid a WET THREE-WAY KISS ON DORCHESTER AND VERONICA!” CALDWELLER: “Between Shaniqua and this. . . we’re definitely turning this red state blue.” RICE: (V/O) “HAHA! Well, don’t forget Obama, Ted. . . anyway! DORCH and VERONICA literally peeled out of town, they left the match, took the countout loss, got in the limo and we haven’t seen tail nor hide! MacBry gets the countout victory, but it’s not a pinfall or guarantee just yet. As for MOSSBERG. . . hey, he got a victory against a Springfield-based wrestler, but I think we can cut to commercial now too.” DREWES: “HAHAHA! Yeah, talk about a cruel youtube! We’ll be right back. . . after this.” (CUTTO: A half-mile away from an arena, late at night near a two-lane highway intersection. The main focus of the shot is MOSSBERG in a black hoodie, jeans and untied Adidas. MOSSBERG adjusts the black dufflebag hung over his left shoulder as a large bus pulls into view and stops. The MUSIC to the Ferrigno-INCREDIBLE HULK television show starts playing as MOSSBERG boards the bus. The picture peels back to reveal the buses destination of ‘PHILADELPHIA’. FADETOBLACK. . .)
