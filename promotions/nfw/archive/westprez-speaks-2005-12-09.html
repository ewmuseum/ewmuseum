Off to Valinor — 9 December 2005 Its a lazy Friday out here in California, but I figured while I had some time I should keep people up to date on a few things...after all, we're just reaching the halfway point of the season. I suppose I should say something constructive about the fed...right? Hello? (hears echoes)
Right, so... Week 5 West Show/Overview Everyone say it loud "Dan, Dan, he's our man! If he can't do it no one can!" After [name withheld] apparently got lost in the world of Warcraft and GTT5 (more on this later), the Rook Black/Scott Riktor match was passed off last night to Dan. Also with the note to him that everything else is completed (even JN got in the mix :eek: ) and his match is the last piece of the puzzle. He complained about not having any pressure, so I hope this helps. Sunday at the latest, or right under your very noses before that when you least expect it cause Dan's the man and he did it like...you get the point.
At the very least, I think we should look at this positively as this will be the first set of East/West Shows that will be posted within the same week, with a new web design as well. To me, while things may be running a tad slow...nobody's holding the other end up either right now.
This weekend we'll officially set foot on the midway point of the regular season and it feels alot quicker than last season. With an added show, added handlers and added responsibilities. With the talent here, if we got paid to do this...sure things would be on-time and in much more detail...but we don't get paid to do this, so I'm happy we're still moving at a steady pace. Everyone give each other a thumb's up, pat on the back, smoke 'em if you got 'em, toast it to roast it...alright moving on.
GTT5 There was an RP tournament on PTC, another board that for the most part runs like RP feds...except they use narrative predominantly, but not all the time. Anyway, seven NFW related figures: [name withheld] (Uriel), Jeff Deville (Deville), [name withheld] (Maggot), Pants (Rook Black), Sean (Seymour Almasy), myself (Craig Miles), [name withheld] (Brandon Youngblood).
Congrats to Andy for winning on a no-show...apparently how he's still in the mix here too...somehow. ;)
Congrats to Sean for winning his match. And congrats to the rest of us for jobbing in the first round. Yup, that's right we ing suck D[BLEEP!]. But you know what they say, if you can't win anywhere else...you can win in NFW! Everyone really pat Pants on the back extra hard.
[b]ANNOUNCEMENTS: Alright enough about current events you don't care about and onto what's coming up. As you know there's a FESTIVE announcement coming up for the East show. I'll give you a hint, if you're interested in being part of a multi-segment piece be sure to watch Saddam Hussein's trial and keep notes for me.
Along with the East Week 6 note, the West Week 6 lineups for Tijuana. WRESTLESTOCK The Western Conference will take off on the road for three shows along the California Coast from Weeks 7-9. The final show will be held in an outdoor state park that's yet to be determined...for those of you familiar with the local climate...you might understand why.
WEEK 8...RUN [name withheld] RUN! As will be the custom for every 4 weeks of the season, handlers will be elgible to sign-up for some interconference activities. Much like All-Star Week last season, this will be the special interconference week...but this time...we're going to make it ALL based on RP and In-Card segment performance. ALSO, there's going to be ALOT of points up for grabs for those willing to throw their names into the hat. There could be up to three different events, or only one...Gregg, myself, JN and [name withheld] all have some ideas brewing in our head, but nothing will be finalized until we're closer to the mark.
From this announcement, I'm sure I'll get alot of thoughts...which is why I've put out the warning ahead of time. When the signing sheets go up, there will be more info...maybe even an announcement on what the interconference events *will* be.
CLOSING STATEMENT Everyone IM/PM/Email Dan. Everyone laugh at and mock Pants. Nobody's going to read the West show, its way too long. Hug yourself, hug a friend, strength and honor. Yes, "Run [name withheld] Run!" is catchy and likely to get used.
