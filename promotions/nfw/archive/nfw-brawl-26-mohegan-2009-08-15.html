NFW Brawl 26: Mohegan, 15 August 2009

15 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 26: Mohegan 15 Aug 2009 Mohegan Sun Arena, Uncasville, Connecticut (seats 10,000) Monsters of Pacific/American Panda vs. CUATRO-X/Teresa Quaranta The road felt long. A myriad of blacks, greys and the not-quite orange fluorescents of another late night racing towards the inevitable. The drugs, the scotch, some sports entertainment radio clown shouting about the same story you slept with open eyes to just the same time yesterday. The road is longer, the perpetual struggle to spout originality in repeating contexts… "Thickfreakness" –TheBlackKeys And NFW rode on… somewhere…the Mohegan Suns Arena as recalled, a harvest breeding Connecticut hazily passing through the late summer. With Brock Lesnar’s colon schematics now fully discussed in Sirius’ serious segued from programming, the tenor screech of Calamity pouring some Patron down the hatch electroshocked the eardrums. Where they were…not important. Where they were going…Crash 50. The white whale, the Holy Grail, the most unlikely words ever to formulate in the brains of such shining wizards as Craig Miles or Vivi La Siegel! A mirage of copulating wrestling geekdom during a 10-year roller coaster of local Cheesesteakian homage to counterculture theoretical psychotropic therapies to a push for classical analysis of a Southern region simplicity. It could only begin on the freakside, the underbelly grease that NFW slides through and back again leaving a stench that twitches the nose of "respected" society and company "Presidents." "Tin Soldier" – The Small Faces Musical introductions need to be foregone. The slow churning butter spread known as Crash 50 formed around Mohegan Suns Arena… Six wrestlers, tackling each other in a three versus three environment would open the show with a bang. …literally. The 16,000-plus were treated to a loud pyrotechnic introduction to the glorious kingdom. Vivid, shake-your-head in "Who spiked my Kool-Aid?" fashion could only ensue as a team of AMERICAN PANDA, the most vicious 270ish monster this side of this planet with such an adorable misnomer. …and MONSTERS OF THE PACIFIC, well when you take the charming Captain Justice into the equation…they’re a misnomer all their own. Ultrajira, well…he’s Pacific. Yeah, so that’s just ONE team. As if the three creations of God needed THREE MORE people in that ring with them to make things go crazy. CUATRO-EQUIS, the team of Dos Equis and FutureQuis were teamed up with the former National Champion and Queen of Existentialistic SubCulture Sects, TERESA QUARANTA. Somewhere, Charles Xavier jumped out of his seat and freaked all these minds were in the same place at the same time. Then, he faceflopped ‘cause this is wrestling and that’s how we roll. This match in particular beheld all the highlights of a highway demolition derby involving Mustangs and Tonkas. Applauded was the determination of Quaranta to keep flying at the charismatic Captain Justice, who seemed shocked that an Urethrean (as he would proclaim!) could hit that hard. His salute of chauvinistic smarm replied to with a square side kick in the jaw, spinning leg lariat and back handspring elbow rebound off a reversed Irish Whip. …which didn’t knock him over as Dos tagged in asking if Teresa took singles…and promptly took a boot to the face, after playing around with his junk too much. Dos scrambled like a Waffle House egg, rebuffed and furiated over his flashback to the Bunny Ranch…only to get reeled into a Atomic Drop, Back Suplex segue. TQ kept the fire hot, scaling the turnbuckles and blasting CJ with a missile dropkick as he cried about the inequalities of Justiceonian Creeds of Women. Taggery followed, Ultrajira stepping in and belly busting the Dos in the torso. The impact sending the Cervezalibre into the turnbuckles, while American Panda made the tag as Teresa Quaranta exited smugly...leaving Dos to fend for himself in the process. CULTURE CLASH! </60's Batman font> CHOP! CHOP! HEADBUTT! HEADBUTT! CHOP! CHOP! HEADBUTT! HEADBUTT! CHOP! CHOP! It doesn’t matter who was doing what does it? They staggered in a circle… CHEADOPBUTT! And they were down. And they were up. DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! And back down. TAG TAG. You know the story, the charismatic Rock N’ Wrestling Cyndi Lauper loving man rolls into an abandoned town, where a psychotic man from the future just wants to hurt somebody. Maybe Captain Justice shouldn’t have struck a pose. FutureQuis probably wouldn’t have dropped him on his head so hard with a Funkitronic Suplex (hands were through the legs and stuff). The assault didn’t let up as FQ barraged CJ with a cycle of kicks, kneestrikes and fisticuffs that had CJ seeing a whirlwind of stars. And so the story goes, a big boot turns the tide. A beersteiner twists the wind. A crazyfunk martial arts kick to the throat makes us feel a little wrong inside. And then you learn that the Future apparently means reality will be a cold, lonesome, painful, headropping suplex of a life. Maybe with some possible backbreaking through tables…just that sad, old tale. And you see the Stars and Stripes represented cosmically with out of this universe biceps, clotheslining and big booting and clotheslining and wound-up right crosses that make you want to live out your life with a handlebar mustache. Maybe that’s what lurks underneath the mask…we can only hope. In the end, nationalistic principles come under fire in revolutionary times. The freaks have crawled from the cellar, laced and wasted on everything we left to grow. How else can you explain a man in a Panda-libre mask deciding to try and rip out the jugular of FutureQuis just because he divided the team by claiming Pandas only come from China. Captain Justice watching and wondering and realizing… A possible communist was in his midst. Allegations accentuated basic character assassinations providing a distraction for FutureQuis to steal a beer from his partner and use it recklessly. Of course using it over Ultrajira’s head was fruitless leading to a reactionary headbutt. Glass shards everywhere, Dos yelling at his partner and meanwhile in the ring…Panda was choking TQ to Captain Justice’s abhorrence. A chokeslam blocked by his own partner, Panda shoved Justice who shoved back… "Can’t Stand It" – WILCO TQ tried to regain her bearings catching Justice from behind with a kick of "questionable" delivery, Justice falling through the ropes hurt. Ultrajira and FutureQuis brawling on the outside of the ring and Dos Equis perhaps…showing his first moments of anger. Springboarding and catching Panda from behind with a flying dropkick. The masked Panda-libre bouncing off the turnbuckles and into a double rollup. The referee making a little fast of a count, probably to get out of the wild, wild affair this was turning into…and rather quickly as an enraged, embarrassed and bullrushing Captain Justice hit the ring. TQ was the first target, pointed and made an example of…taking a hard elbow strike, followed by a spinning forearm shot that knocked her out of the ring. Dos quickly jumped through the ropes, quickly back to his normal self…beer loss aside. That left American Panda standing up…Captain Justice awaiting him. A staredown. A possible prelude to Crash 50 insanity. And security having to stop Ultrajira and FutureQuis from destroying a hot dog stand and possibly arresting them. Legion vs. Brock Alyas vs. James von Doom "Myopic Vision" – Captain Beyond And so the night takes a strange turn…Monsters of the Pacific, American Panda…more or less not coming together in mutual admiration and perhaps, setting up for something sinister down the road. As if the creepy walk of freakdom would be let to simmer down… BROCK ALYAS. Cracked out and spastically blinking. LEGION. With Luci4, bloodstained and dented chair in hand… …both wrestlers in the ring, pacing and smiling evilly. Calamity noting that the goth freaks were pushing to the front, Sean Toombs surmising that the FRONTIERtron displaying a Presidential Seal perhaps would give way to the supposed mystery question mark next to Alyas’ and Legion’s name on many of the handed out billboard posters. President Mayfield recorded a message for both men, choosing to avoid be seen at a Native American Casino that disrespected the morals of the Squared Circle Office. Legion and Alyas, both offenders of the Wellness policy…Legion an offender of basic moral principles, Alyas of basic decency. And yet, now that a man had returned to NFW by contractual rights…there was someone far worse that had shown how much "CHANGE" was still needed to be fought for by the President. …there was laughter from the house PA… Tonight, the President would show how far the NFW needed "CHANGE." Something, that he promised after Crash 50 "THE GREAT AMERICAN SMOKEOUT." And then… JAMES VON DOOM walked out. "Used to Be" – Rory Gallagher Everything that Legion had grown from, everything that Brock Alyas needed as an excuse to smoke crack… They were subjected to VON DOOM. Legion…he thou betrayed after banishing the beast. Alyas…he thou that sling the rock, deal with those on the rock. Von Doom walked slowly…then he ran, his amoeba black-suit sliding belly-down into the ring…stomps be damned. Fingers clawed at eyes, throats, mouths… Wild fists flew, spastic kicks struck and nobody dared to strategize or theorize. The fans didn’t know who to cheer, who to boo, just watching in puzzled confuzzledment. How do these men make a living? How do these men not suffer brain damage after what they do? Legion didn’t exactly let go of the chair in all of this. The referee didn’t exactly care to get in the middle of it. "Presidential directives dictate the soulless, directionless, vermin of the mat be damned to the damage they willingly wrought upon themselves through the simple, structured, sardonical suppositions their singular psychologies fabricate." I don’t understand that either, I don’t think Eddie really wrote it. Von Doom headbutt anything in sight until Legion axecapitated his head with his bloodstained chair. Alyas always the freight train rushed into about three chair shots, which Legion lamented to Luci4 asking for another after breaking it upon Brock. Legion’s once fast-paced style slowly showing it’s age after a long stranglehold against the nether-regions of NFW. But the precise kneestrike and sliding dropkick to both competitors kept them on the mat long enough for Luci4 to bring forth his second pet, a fine steel colt firm in the hands. THWACK! CRACK! ZING! Three chairshots and Legion felt at home, his opponents trying to find their ways away from his steel dispositions. He grabbed Alyas, tossing him through the ropes and catching von Doom with a rushing kneestrike thereafter. Legion hooked a double arm, leaning back for a DDT on his pet chair. The impact rolled von Doom over, Legion going for the pin…the ref apparently gone. Mostly because Brock Alyas grabbed him and tossed him out of the ring. Legion heard Luci4’s shouts, popping up to see Brock Alyas with a seatless chair and running at him. Legion ducked, but Brock hooked the chair around his neck and whiplashed him to the mat. Using the chair, like an overgrown cracked out mutant trying to slam a wood log to China…Brock started piledriving his chair onto Legion. Luci4 rushed into the ring, hitting a spinning leg lariat to the back of Brock’s legs and thusly, taking him down. Von Doom then found himself the unlucky recipient of Luci4’s nails ripping his eyes upwards…thusly leading his body into the same direction. Von Doom stood. Luci4 leapt and when for a Hurricanrana, but von Doom hooked his arm around the ropes…Luci flipped out onto Legion, not helping her soulless soulmate. Von Doom got out of the ring, quickly retrieving a table and sliding it into the fray. Tossing chairs and other ringside equipment into the squared circle. The bell started ringing as the referee knew this wasn’t going to be pretty…and then the timeskeeper’s bell was tossed into the ring by von Doom as well. Alyas stood up slowly catching Legion with a Spinebuster! Von Doom was behind him and clocked him with the timeskeeper’s bell and echoing ring causing the crowd to groan! Von Doom grabbed Alyas and delivered a kneelift to the midsection and then took him over with a gutwrench suplex! The attack on Alyas allowing Luci4 to climb the turnbuckles to the blindside… …Legion stood up slowly, gingerly walking around and dodging the charging von Doom just in time, with a drop toe hold onto the tipped over table! Alyas got up slowly and Luci4 launched…MISSILE DROPSAULT! Brock’s momentum carried him out of the ring…Luci4 got up, Legion shouting instructions… The crowd booed even more… "Have Love will Travel" – The Black Keys KOOTER JOHNSON. CAMERON CRUISE. Partners in War Games. Partners in the atrocities NFW’s existence committed against their self-respect. Cruise struck Alyas outside the ring, catching him with a kneestrike to the gut. Luci4 never saw Johnson for she would’ve warned Legion. Instead, as Legion set up von Doom on the table, blind to him was Kooter clutching Luci4 on the turnbuckles…by the throat. Preventing a peep from her spittling mouth… Kooter Johnson is no Captain Justice. He pushed forward, Luci4 crashing to the cement. Legion turned around and found Kooter’s boot crushing his face. He wouldn’t be able to remember the chokeslam through von Doom and the table. He wouldn’t remember Cruise and Johnson nodding at each other, a new relationship that could prove far more beneficial than their last. He would remember that Dos Equis didn’t come...an aversion to the Chaos… He would remember that FutureQuis didn’t help…one that should embrace it… And Unicorn Mask, later…that would be different. Impulse (c) vs. Problem Child vs. High Flyer "Lemonade" – Blind Melon We take our last turn down the long, dark road towards something nobody ever could figure to exist. Crash 50. A smokeout. What would be left on our tongues? What salt would be poured on the earth? Well, break out your Devil Horns and black armbands… HIGH FLYER. The now controversial masked assassin of the New Frontier walked out, bowing his head…wearing a black armband for his fallen comrade BatPulse, struck his Devil’s Horn pose and quickly ran to the ring. As the crowd booed, little did they know they should save some air… PROBLEM CHILD came out next, spastically shouting at the crowd and hopping around like a bunny on mushroom twinged Mountain Dew. So, why would we put these two men in a ring? What would be the purpose, the de-facto delegated finale until a show to everything we knew about New Frontier? A TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP. IMPULSE. He slowly walked out, knowing full well that one of his greatest rivals stood in the ring haunted by the death of his bat-cousin. Meaning…he knew High Flyer was a Lunatic for a reason. Problem Child on the other hand, smelled like something different. Naturally, Problem Child’s sour odiferous existence thrust upon the gagging masses was a smell unto it’s own. However, he was the ally and subordinate to a man that Impulse would face at Crash 50. His current enemy of the squared circle…Castor Strife. In a would where the television championship would NOT have to be defended on an untelevised night, who else but Impulse would willingly put up the belt along with his pride in a situation like this? And maybe that’s why so many hate him, why Eddie Mayfield surrounded him with such hated scum. The bell rang, PC and Flyer smiled as Impulse readied up. In they charged, Impulse putting the blocks up as Flyer and PC thrashed at him with punches, kicks, overhand forearms, knees. Impulse tried buckling down, but the voluminous bashing overwhelmed the young champion. Double Irish Whip. Double Back Body Drop. Oh yeah, it’s Impulse. The champion landed on his feet, dropsaulting in Flyer and getting up just in time to drop toe hold PC into the turnbuckles. PC flopped around and Flyer was up in a hurry, catching Impulse with a running kneelift to the gut. Doubling over Impulse, backing him into the ropes and unleashing a barrage of chops and right hands. Irish Whip, not quite. Reversed. Dropkick by the champion. Flyer almost up and over and out, but fights back in… only to take a champion Dropkick that DID send him out. PC gained the senses of his mind just in time to take a high-altitude running forearm and then a whipping side elbow strike that had his eyes crossed sideways. Impulse capitalized with a Reverse Springboard Bodypress and nearly had an early victory. Unfortunately, High Flyer was in the ring stomping out any chance of a pinfall victory… …Flyer started stomping anything that moved, Impulse or PC until both tripped him up. Double Spreadeagle Split! NO! Impulse with a stomp to Flyer’s chest, he would never dare do something illegal…and probably the only guy in NFW to never do anything illegal, PERIOD…inside the ring, outside of it…Thailand, Australia… PC then went for a roundhouse kick that Impulse easily anticipated, countering with a reverse legsweep. PC kipped up, bloodcurdlingly screamed and fired off a reverse knife edge that Impulse ducked under…into a Crucifix Cradle and got a two count. Small package by Impulse, another two count. Oklahoma Roll, one count…only ‘cause Flyer clotheslined him. PC got up in a dizzying daze, took an Inverted Atomic Drop by Flyer…slapped Flyer out of being betrayed so badly, which earned him a sidekick to the leg and then the head. Impulse up…kicked in the gut. Flyer hooked him, lifted him up…PILEDRIVER! Impulse bounced, landing on the mat motionless…the crowd watching in shock as Flyer covered. ONE! TWO! NO! Impulse kicked out just in time, PC then caught Flyer with a running bulldog as the Snow Salesman rushed up angrily yelling at the ref. PC dragged Flyer to the middle of the ring… Camel Clutch positioning…sidewinding forearm blasts…Flyer’s face turning into a bashed tomato. PC dropped him back down, bounced off the ropes…Leaping Legdrop to the back of the head! PC rolled over Flyer, doublestomped him…SPRINGBOARD MOONSAULT! ONE! TWO! Impulse with a sliding dropkick breakup! Impulse stayed down, still dazed and confused for so long… "Slow Night, So Long" – Kings of Leon Flyer was up and yelling, punchdrunk walking straight into a kick from PC. Another kick. PC bounced off the ropes with a bursting shoulderblock strike that knocked Flyer to the mat. Impulse slowly rose and PC’s eyes slitted… With a quick pull of the trigger, Impulse nearly had his back snapped with a STO Backbreaker Drop by PC! Pinfall? NO. A mounted reverse chinlock had Impulse’s neck and back wrenched halfway to Tucson, PC leaning back and low… Which just happened to work out for High Flyer. CRACK! A LACKA! CRACK! Flyer blasted a running kick to the upper back of PC, then two to the back of his head…Impulse laid on the mat, in a lot of pain… Flyer ripped up PC and body slammed him ON TOP of Impulse. Then quickly leapt off the middle ropes, twisting his body and hitting a double legdrop on PC, therefore, Impulse suffering the brunt of the impact. Randall Knox groaned, grimaced and gurgled something about Problem Child’s body odor. High Flyer pulled PC up, dragging and then chucking him through the turnbuckles, a resounding thud sounding off the ringpost smacked by PC’s head. Flyer ripped up Impulse and hooked him around the waist, LET IT SNOW! (Northern Lights Suplex) Impulse popped out at two, Flyer grinding his teeth. He grabbed Impulse by the nape, hooking him in a front facelock…snapping back for a DDT! EXCEPT… Impulse held onto the ropes, Flyer hitting the mat in a whiplash and Knox grabbing his free legs… ELEVATED BOSTON CRAB! Identity Theft Irony! The stapled submission of Flyer now being used by Impulse on Flyer himself. BatPulse was turning over in his grave! While protestation would not generate…Problem Child would at least provide the personification of what they’d fight for…a Twisting Flying Headscissors on Impulse not only broke the stolen submission, but allowed Problem Child a chance to reciprocate High Flyer’s latest decision to throw him headfirst into steel. A facial fish hook with enough sour Red Bull enamel cracked spittle from PC’s spazzing shouts of fury to make the ref gag. Problem Child wrenched and clawed at Flyer’s face, the referee trying to pry him off. Impulse grabbing him by the backseat and reeling him for a Back Suplex, NO. PC lands on his feet and goes for a Side Russian Legsweep! Impulse counters with a headbutt, followed by an elbow to the side of the head, PC staggers around and whipped around by a quickstrike powerslam! "The Bucket" – Kings of Leon Impulse popped up…took a deep breath… STANDING SHOOTING STAR PRESS! The crowd erupted, PC didn’t even know he was getting pinned… High Flyer did… Kicking Impulse out of the pin attempt at 1, grabbing PC’s hair and dragging him up…pushing him into the corner, wailing away with wild rights…PC’s gums bleeding out as Flyer didn’t stop punishing him with the hard fists, Impulse up on his feet and grabbing Flyer, pulling him out… NO DICE HOLMES. Flyer cracked an elbow strike and then blitzed the unsuspecting Impulse with a square headshot to the bridge of the nose. Impulse went down and Flyer grabbed PC again, whipping him hard across the ring and slamming into him with a running elbow smash! Impulse got up slowly, realizing Flyer’s preoccupation with PC and slowly walked to the opposite corner. PC’s eyes lolled, Flyer kept firing right fists…as Impulse flipped up and around with a back handspring! ELBOW SMASH! PC now on the horizontal bottom of the pile…Impulse jogged out as Flyer turned around with an annoyed look…Impulse with a back body drop! NO. This time Flyer landed on his feet, yelling and charging towards Impulse who sidestepped out of a RUNNING YAKUZA LOCOMOTIVE KICK! …that squarely struck Problem Child in the jaw. Flyer laughed crazily at his own mistake, perhaps the smug satisfaction of PC’s fading consciousness overtaking the assertation that a dear old friend stand behind him… Thusly, he turned right into… SUDDEN IMPACT SUPERKICK! Sweet Willard Connecticut, we can move forward. A three count is made. A final, dusty trail….a smoke in the distance. We can see Crash 50 faintly…it is inevitable, but first we hear… …CASTOR STRIFE charging down to the ring. "Hands" – The Raconteurs Strife quickly hit the ring, while Impulse was handed the Television Championship…striking from behind with a vicious clothesline! Impulse hit the mat, taking stomp after stomp from a fresh, lurking bloodgutter like Castor. He quickly grabbed the championship, fixing his hair in it’s reflection as Impulse stood up… BELTSHOT! Impulse was down. Castor stood over him with a smarmy, maniacally twinged smile and tossed the belt to the mat. Castor turned Impulse over. As God was our witness, he would make him humble. CAMEL CLUTCH! Impulse shouted as High Flyer stood up wearily…looking at the unconscious Problem Child, then Castor…figuring out whether to smile or lash out in anger. And then the crowd cheers piqued his curiosity… UNICORN MASK! The crowd roared in thunder, the masked madman running to the ring and sliding in…taking a few shots from a weary Flyer as he got in, but it didn’t matter. Unicorn Mask tackled him to the ground, punishing him with a vicious succession of right hands. UM shot up to his feet watching in disgust as Castor Strife wrenched Impulse in the hold…and ATTACKED! The crowd leapt in a frenzy as Unicorn Mask grabbed Castor by both eyes, twisting him and hooking him for a… DEE! DEE! TEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE! Strife was DEAD ON IMPACT! Unicorn Mask grabbed at his shattered horn and started headbanging, the crowd’s adrenalized momentum amped from this very moment brought to a frightening, surprising HALT. THWACK! LEGION. A chairshot to the back of Unicorn Mask’s head, the crowd not even seeing the demonic backstabber come out with the chair in the first place…so caught up in the tide of Unicorn Mask. Now in comparison, they were in whispers. Unicorn Mask stumbled around the ring, a now stalking and in need of ibuproefen PROBLEM CHILD waiting to get mean. He caught the masked freak with another doled out STO Backbreaker Drop. Castor rolling out of the ring, falling to his knee…but having the satisfaction of seeing the wincing, pained look of Randall Knox as he laid on his belly. Meanwhile… THWACK! THWACK! THWACK! Legion gave another three chair shots across the back of Unicorn Mask to the shock of Problem Child and widening grin of High Flyer, who stood to shake Legion’s hand… THWACK! Legion stood over the wreckage in the ring, a twisted grin on his face as he set up the chair in the ring, picking up UNICORN MASK onto his shoulders afterwards… DOS EQUIS! CAPTAIN JUSTICE! LA ESTRELLA 100%! P. KING DUK! FUTUREQUIS! AMERICAN PANDA! …we’re all masked and LATE for the DVD landed on the chair. The crowd’s softened cheers trying to raise the deflating moment of destruction in the ring. All the wrong moves landing on all the wrong people. Castor and PC retreated from the area, where Legion led an ensuing brawl…as PANDA and JUSTICE battled each other just trying to get to Legion, while CUATRO-X didn’t know whether to protect him or hurt him with War Games only so…close. KOOTER JOHNSON rushed out, Legion struck FutureQuis inciting more brawling…security arrived. Impulse walked away gingerly, championship title over his shoulder and refs by his side. High Flyer leapt off the turnbuckles onto one big pile of madness as LUCI4 ran out with her own chair. Legion was getting battered, but bathing in his created chaos. The dust settled a tenspot later, security and staff finally controlling the spent wrestlers…blood, some teeth lost, objectionable and vilified behavior…and they’re supposedly the "good guys." …and so we go.
