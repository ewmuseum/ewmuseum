Crash TV: Week 5 LIVE! Posted by Natrone Rice on 7 Dec 2005 The fifth installment of Crash Television in Season 2 has hit the airwaves and the Eastern Conference heads into the regular season halfway point with alot of peculiar characters affecting not only the standings, but overall leadership. One could blame Felix Red...and for the most part...they'd be right. A night that was supposed to possess a main event six man tag and two tag matches were altered significantly from one notorious Felix Red ally...and a horrifying new one. Michael Manson/Felix Red def. Lindsay Troy/Dan Ryan, due to the fact that Dan Ryan never landed in Tokyo, Japan this week. Instead...his private flight was HIJACKED by The Mad Hatter and taken to New Zealand! Hatter and Red have been allies since the onset of the season, and this left Lindsay Troy in a two on one situation not even she could overcome. Two other wrestlers were eliminated from the night's competition as a man (can we call him that?) claiming to be the birthchild of Beau Michaels, Mercedes and Cameron Cruise named KOOTER CRUISE attacked Jason Payne and Kazuo Shizaki in the locker rooms. YYJ def. Maggot -- well, the attacks changed a tag match to a singles match...and nothing else can be said except for this. Yori Yakamo Junior has been in 4 matches...and won them all. One of the big surprises in the first half of the season. The main event six man tag team match with enough participants and rules to turn into a Constitution ended with a double pin! Jonathan Marx and Kin Hiroshi earned their stipulation rights and we'll get NFW World Championship title shots; Marx next week in fact. Susano-O-Mikoto has announced all participants were awarded ten points for the match... ...although that could be at the behest of Beau Michaels, who in a surprise/impromptu match booked by Mikoto backfired on the Grand Elder as Beau defeated Panda Mask (a rumoured Mikoto enforcer) and now until Week 6, Mikoto is under Beau's power! The show ended with the footage of Dan Ryan's hijacking along with the startling news that Kooter Cruise is a Felix Red henchmen. God only knows what's in store for the East now.
