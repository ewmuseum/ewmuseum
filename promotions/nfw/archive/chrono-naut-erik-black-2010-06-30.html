"Chrono.naut" Erik Black

RStrawsma — 30 June 2010 Here's the deal... through a legal loophole, Erik Black overcomes the drug screening hump that has kept him from an NFW contract for the past couple months, and finally gets his name signed in ink. Now, he needs an actual living, breathing professional wrestler for an opponent -- lest he leave another crowd in stunned and confused silence by grappling with a blow-up doll in his next dark match experience.
The thing is, even though he's gotten around a drug screen, he clearly hasn't given up the stoner lifestyle. He still lives and acts in direct defiance of New Frontier's new wellness policy. I'm up for having a match with anybody, but I'm looking to eventually find a character that might be more ethically inclined to support the wellness policy, and serve as an ethical rival to Black. If anybody is interested, let me know.
Macc24 — 3 July 2010 I'm game. Brock's opinon of the of the wellness policy is that it's very tabu to him, especially the state of affairs of what would go in in shows when he joined in 08.
However, he's only recently come to terms with the fact that he must obide by the rules so there could be an interesting essence to that match, lemme kno if your down!
RStrawsma — 5 July 2010 Cool, man... I'll get a segment or an RP in to make Black's signing official, and we'll kick off this shiznit shebacle.
