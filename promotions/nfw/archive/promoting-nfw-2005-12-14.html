Off to Valinor — 14 December 2005 We're in RP session, sue me...I've got nothing better to do. :cool: As anyone on fwrestling.com could tell you, people come and go. As we all get older, the more likely we're going to have less time to dedicate here. Even I won't be here forever (unlike Chad), but I'm trying to take the steps necessary for everything to carry on without me if that's the case.
With our turnover rate, its always hard to find capable people to replace the people we lose.
I've pretty much exhausted all the resources over at efednews.net, EWN and PTC, I'm also fairly sure anyone who's anyone on those boards have at least heard of NFW at this point. If you've ever checked out the links dropdown, you'd notice the Roughkut and X-Net links. Both sites keep a database of efeds, as well as rate them based on the hits to their site from our site. Not that it helps any, but if you ever feel bored and want to help the fed -- click on 'em. Hell, anytime you click on the firefox links we get points over at spreadfirefox.
To the contrary of the previous paragraph, I'm not a huge shiller. I suppose some people might feel I advertise for the West alot...although join notes shouldn't really be deemed 'ads,' moreso -- fedhead administrative duties. I've hyped the site opening, show dates, etc...
But I'm interested in hearing your thoughts on what we can do to reach out to other communities, or what other communities there are out there to reach out to. It seems like there's not much outside of what I'm starting to consider the big three: FWC, PTC, EWN. All A1E folks sit down, as obviously you're on FWC enough to be deemed one of us...unfortunately. I know Google has the RSPWF groups, but those have less organization than the old Prodigy.
What else is out there? What else can we do to say "Hey check us out for a good time?" Besides the whoring of feedback notes or Beau Michaels, in which case either of those end up in a response and you're in the fed...you're so jobbing.
Anyway, food for thought...or discussion. Please don't quote any love songs Tard. Also if you're a lurker that's never said "Hello," please do so. We won't bite. ...well...maybe Lindsay will if you look like Batista.
Steve — 14 December 2005 I think starting two divisions of 50 was a mistake to begin with. I know it's hard to nail down a core group of 8-10 that will always be there, but if I read that right...you could be looking for new talent with four weeks left in the season?
You need a cut off of when new guys can come in. Probably around when they could realistically expect to walk in and make a play at the playoffs.
Having 'dark matches' for new guys when the playoffs are about to start, or something like that...really doesn't make much sense to me. I think it lessons the focus on who is here and the season itself.
I guess the question is at this point, today, how many new guys are you needing? And for this year?
If you're talking in general terms about seasons 3, maybe cross that bridge when we get there. And as far as promoting NFW to new eyes, sounds like you've covered all the bases.
Mr.Batee — 14 December 2005 Since my federation is a member of PTC I'll ask an obvious question, did you ever consider putting together a federation and asking for a spot? My thought process behind it is this. The more links you have up, the more your community will be exposed. But there is more to that than just placing a link on your site. If you join another community, you should be active in that community because one hand washes the other. I'm posting here because I would love to use your resources and this is my thanks. Plus you guys seem very open to new ideas. I'm not going to shill for my site because that's not what the post is about, but I found that when you build up your affiliates, your site tends to do a bit better.
Finally, I think people base popularity on activity. When something is posted in the PTC forums, there are regular people who will respond almost 100% of the time. From what I can see, they have about 20 or so people like that trolling around the forums 24/7. For me, just listening to what others think brings me back to that site. If I come back, then they have accomplished their goal... to build a community.
More interaction on the forums. More affiliates [posted on the main page] That's my two cents. Off to Valinor — 14 December 2005 Steve -- I think we started with 36 this season...and we have 34 now. I'm not looking to be accepted by PTC by joining PTC...especially for the fact that most of the East Conference have been on these boards for years now. FWC is an extension of the old Prodigy days, as is NFW in many ways.
I think there's alot of activity here in terms of writing, and for the most part I believe everyone really likes the fed (I hope) that's in it. Our feedback threads are usually small...but that's just baggage from the Prodigy days that doesn't go away for some.
...really, I'm just wondering if there's other communities out there I don't know about, or a method without continuous annoying shilling to get people out there interested.
