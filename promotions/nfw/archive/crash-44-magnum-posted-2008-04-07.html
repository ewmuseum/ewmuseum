Overall, this was a really good show. There were spurts of greatness, but it never dropped below 'good.' I wish I had more to say about the whole thing, but everything is currently building to Wrestlestock, and it shows.
But it's building really, really well. The opening segment with Ryan really pushed the idea that his match with Nova is going to make or break his reign. As a big fan of both characters, and a big fan of logical builds, Ryan got over the idea that "I'm the man, but Nova could very well become the man when we face" very well. If this was a 'real' promotion I wouldn't be able to call a winner at this point.
On the other side of it, while Ryan and Nova are the 'mega-stars' that are fighting for the top of the NFW, Brock Alyas and Legion/Theresa are swiftly moving up just as fast, and I think whoever wins at Wrestlestock will have to deal with them all sooner than later. Brock's demeanor seems to be that he finds it... quaint, is the closest word I can think of, that his opponent dared show up, like 'Who is this guy who gets in the ring with me, who thinks he can hang with me?' I can't wait to see someone finally stand toe to toe with Brock for a full match, and I think, when his first loss inevitably comes to pass, how Macc pulls out of that could very well be the last piece of the 'complete' character.
Legion/Luci4/Theresa (am I missing anything?) certainly have my attention, but where do they go from here?
