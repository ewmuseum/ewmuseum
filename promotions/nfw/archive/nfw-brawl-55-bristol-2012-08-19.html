NFW Brawl 55: Bristol, 19 August 2012

19 August 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 55: Bristol 19 Aug 2012 Bristol Motor Speedway, Bristol, Tennessee (seats 88,000) Welcome to the Show! V/O: "The following is a JOINT presentation from ESEN and SIRIUS/XM SATELLITE RADIO…" (MUSICUP: ‘DAY IN THE LIFE’ – THE BEATLES) (FADEIN: An airplane high above the clouds…the gentle introductions of England’s finest gently caressing the airwaves… Rolling plains and countryside span towards the horizon below, the song continuing through its first verse. QUICK CUTTO: SPOOKY DOOM sitting first class on the airplane, looking out the window. QUICK CUTTO: The airplane landing safely…) NATRONE RICE: (V/O) "With the highly lauded BRAWLAPALOOZA coming to a close, New Frontier sets it sights on two fronts – a historic RELOADED in Jones Beach that’s only a few weeks away from shutting the door on one of two legendary careers. But tonight front and center stage will belong to the 2012 GRAND PRIX tournament…" (CUTTO: DOOM walking through the airport and coming up to a guy holding up a sign reading "DOOM, S." and nodding at the guy while saying "Cheers mate…first stop, Buckingham Palace – hehe…" which gets a weird eyebrow raise from the driver…) RICE: (V/O) "A tournament that will earn the victor a title shot of their choice against the TELEVISION…NATIONAL or EVERETTE MEMORIAL TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS…" (CUTTO: DOOM riding in the back of a limousine and reading a guide on the history of BRISTOL, ENGLAND…) RICE: (V/O) "But this time it’s not only the finest of NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING’s cruiserweights allowed entry…but open to ANYONE IN THE WORLD." I’d love to turn you on… (As the iconic song starts hitting its mid-break cacophonic, sonic explosion of a peak we’re treated to a SPLIT-SCREEN of DOOM reading his book, while a JEFF GORDON billboard has been graffitied to have him missing teeth, while on the other side shows a FLASH CUTTO REEL explosion of SUPERFLY EXPRESS walking out to fireworks! BRISTOL MOTOR SPEEDWAY’s 40,000 person infield leaping up in unison! IMPULSE standing underneath the makeshift infield FRONTIERtron, where only 2 feet below there’s a denizen of raucous fans holding their hands up high…) Woke up, got out of bed… (CUTTO: DOOM exiting his limo, furrowing his brow as he spans his grassy surroundings – loud, blaring music overheard in the distance…) DOOM: "Where are we? Stonehenge?" (A NFW VIP fan slaps DOOM on the shoulder, walking past him with a 64 oz. Steel Reserve cup… "YEAH, BRO! HAMMER OF THE GODS TONIGHT!" JASON MURRAY walks up beside SPOOKY DOOM, first looking at his surroundings in disgust… then at SPOOKY DOOM…) MURRAY: "What? You expected some class from this place? Yeah, so did I…" (MUSIC CUT. FADETOBLACK.) (FADEIN: The outer fields of BRISTOL MOTORSPEEDWAY, TENNESSEE, where there’s a lineup of some classic NASCAR vehicles from the days of WALTRIP, YARBOROUGH, PETTY and EARNHARDT SR., but looking most impressive is the BRAWL-OPTIMUS PRIME PRODUCTION TRUCK. Sparkling underneath the raceway stadium lights, there’s thousands of NFW supporters per usual around the area and mugging for the camera! CUTTO: Inside OPTIMUS – we’ve got NATRONE RICE getting set at the disc jockey desk that’s got a stack of papers, a dozen of mini-flatscreens displaying various ESEN action tied to the NEW FRONTIER and a lineup of the newly released BRAWLAPALOOZA bobble head connection complete with SUPERFLY EXPRESS championship editions… RICE makes the nod to the control board…) RICE: "ALRIGHT, ALRIGHT! WELCOME TO THE BRAWL Y’ALL! Your CAPTAIN for the evening will be myself, NATRONE RICE and we are coming to you LIIIIIIIIIIVE from BRISTOL, TENNESSEE home of one of the most famous racetracks in the world! Maybe the itinerary wasn’t so clear for some of our participants highlighted in the opening of the show, but once everyone’s compass was pointed towards the wrestling ring situated in front of 40,000 infield fans for a day of ROCK N’ WRESTLING GRAND PRIX STYLE… let’s just say IT. WAS. ON. Now before we hit up the show results, let’s give a shoutout and thank you to Dexter Wayne’s Mutton Chop Mission, Sara Jay Harris and The South Bank Band and our headliners, Whiskey Johnson’s Jug Funk Band. Quite the four hours of music in the early afternoon for the Bristol faithful and I’ll say I’ve never seen a Jug Blower get that many people clapping in my life!" (CUTTO: BRET KELLY backstage with DEVIN MILLWOOD – talking at the camera passionately.) RICE: "Things started off weird and slow, but hey – who are we to ignore the pageantry of an NFW show as long-time journeyman and winless Bret Kelly came out for an interview with Devin Millwood to give an update on his long-time partner Mike McGee’s condition after the BRAWLAPALOOZA attack by Freddie Sagawa. Millwood was the backstage reporter filling in for ‘JUST’ MARK that came onto that scene in a bad way, getting attacked as well. Rumor has it that he may have found representation in Cameron Lee Waubash, which just SCREAMS of something afoul in a bad way. Anyway, only the lord knows if Sagawa’s back in Pennsylvania terrorizing a local community, but he wasn’t here on this show to the chagrin of Bret Kelly. Kelly not one to pass up the dramatic flair for doom, apparently CHALLENGED Sagawa to show up and enter this NFW GRAND PRIX, stating that he’ll be the first man to put him on his… well, the censors kinda bleeped it out from there. So, yeah… if Freddie Sagawa has access to the premium sports packages of DIRECTV… Brett Kelly may die soon." (CUTTO: ROOK BLACK in the same backstage setup as we just saw, TRIPLE CROWN CHAMPIONSHIP over his shoulder and being interviewed by DEVIN MILLWOOD…) RICE: "The TRIPLE CROWN CHAMPION was also on-hand for an interview as well before the official show began. There was some definite discussion around the RELOADED 5-WAY STAIRWAY TO HELL match that he’d be competing in, but also around the scouting of the GRAND PRIX that would be going on. With Impulse in the Grand Prix and the Stairway to Hell match – there was a definite direct mention and discussion of his former tag-team partner, but you could almost sense that he’s really looking forward to mixing it up with Eric Dane. The Only Star has said a lot of things in his rise to the World Championship picture that denigrated and accused Rook Black’s former National Championship reigns…and current Triple Crown Championships as worthless. So, forget the razorwire… you can figure there’s gonna be blood with that one." Spooky Doom vs. Ikan Jobtooyoo vs. Leyenda De Ocho (CUTTO: SPOOKY DOOM walking underneath the FRONTIERTRON and approaching the long rampway to the center of the packed infield, where tens of thousands of fans loudly boo him! CUTTO: DOOM wide-eyed at the NFW spectacle, but his face quickly turns to a sneer back at the fans.) RICE: "Now, one of the heralded entries into the NFW GRAND PRIX was certainly NOT the man named Spooky Doom – and we don’t mean that from a competitive standpoint. On the contrary, we mean this from the type of reaction fans of ESEN have been giving this guy since his entry and exit from this summer’s Ultratitle tournament. Now, Doom has pretty much riled other fed’s fans, administrators and financial backers for the last several months. And while Lamont Hollywood was confusing his famous uncle as the legendary EARL OF DOOM… well, here in New Frontier – well, we still don’t know who Doom is referring to." (CUTTO: IKAN JOBTOYOO raising his arm as he’s announced in the ring… a steady stream of trash pelting him.) RICE: "Now, we brought one of the worst titled jobbers in the history of mankind that I hope never gets another match here… or another word on our television station…" (CUTTO: LEYENDA DE OCHO spinning around underneath the FRONTIERtron, while green and white fireworks explode! His mask adorns the pixilated image of the Green Abobo, which matches his green and black tights.) RICE: "And we may have brought in someone that knows the key to our President’s dear, dear heart… and well, Rook Black’s current title plate design. More importantly, the 22-year old wrestler LEYENDA DE OCHO pretty arrived in Bristol, Tennessee with one thing in mind – to try and take out Spooky Doom." (FLASH CUTTO’s: DOOM with a back body drop and clothesline combination on JOBTOYOO – before being spun around into a hard right hand by DE OCHO! DOOM walking along to the top rope with an arm wringer on JOBTOYOO – only to have his leg pulled out by DE OCHO causing a spread eagle shot across the top rope! DOOM with a back elbow shot to DE OCHO followed by a discus throat thrust chop! JOBTOYOO walking into a boot to the face by DOOM! DOOM with a STO on JOBTOYOO, but taking a springboard bulldog from behind by DE OCHO!) RICE: "So, the Grand Prix opener of the evening with the non-NFW participants was a single-elimination Triple Threat match with simple fallout of whoever got pinned, got sent home… while the other two participants would move onto the next round." (CUTTO: DOOM hitting a Satellite Headscissors into a Diamond Cutter – the crowd booing loudly! As he scales the top rope, DE OCHO pushes him off and out of the ring! CUTTO: JOBTOYOO eating a Shining Wizard into a Lionsault Combo for the 3-count by DE OCHO! CUTTO: DE OCHO getting his arm raised, but eating a chairshot from behind from DOOM – immediately incurring the wrath of all the fans who boo loudly and start tossing their beer cups towards the ring! CUTTO: Back to RICE in the OPTIMUS PRIME studio…) RICE: "So, it looks like we have a budding rivalry in LEYENDA DE OCHO and SPOOKY DOOM… and possibly a much larger one with the fans of NFW and this SPOOKY DOOM guy. We’ll be right back!" (FADEOUT) Akita Hoshi vs. P. King Duk Windham vs. Impulse (TRI-SCREEN CUTTO: On the LEFT, IMPULSE rolling his neck and keeping his arms loose. In the MIDDLE, P. KING DUK WINDHAM, wearing a = W = Martial Arts-styled headband, is wielding a kendo stick. On the RIGHT, AKITA HOSHI, in full face paint, stalks back and forth in his corner.) (SLOW FADE ...) (CUTTO: LIVE! w/ NATRONE RICE, all headphone’d up, sitting behind the usual bobblehead covered desk - it’s Akita Hoshi, Impulse, Superfly Express, ‘Fabulous’ Frankie Fargo, and Jason Murray this week. RICE, cracking a wide grin, shoots an acknowledging nod to the recently tuned-in cameras.) NATRONE RICE: "You bet your SWEET-BIPPY that’s there’s more, ya’all! After an entertaining and exhausting weekend in Hershey, we’re in NASCAR Country and haven’t slowed a bit! The needle is bouncin’ off that red line! This next matchup has all the ingredients to be a wild one - not to mention, painful. We’re talkin’ a three-way, first man eliminated does not qualify for the GRAND PRIX! As you probably saw, it’ll be the former Television Champ, Impulse set to face the ever-dangerous Akita Hoshi, and the unlucky P. King Duk Windham. Not gonna lie here, folks, but this reeks of our good President handing out a little punishment to Duk for joinin’ the Clan, but that’s just me! Here’s the highlights!" (QUICK CUTTOs: AKITA HOSHI ripping into P. KING DUK WINDHAM’s chest with GUNSHOT Knife-edge Chops! P. KING DUK munches on simultaenous dropkicks from IMPULSE and HOSHI, who both kip back up and begin trading strikes! IMPULSE takes P. KING down with an arm drag into an arm lock; P. KING pushes back to his feet, but is flipped ass over tea kettle with a wrist-clutch Judo throw from the Marathon Man! HOSHI takes P. KING on the Tour de Turnbuckle Smash and, with a dazed P. KING stumbling around, finishes it off with a Springboard Moonsault! IMPULSE hits a trifecta of snap suplexes on an out-of-it P. KING DUK! P. KING stumbles into the RED MIST from HOSHI! IMPULSE comes in for the SUDDEN IMPACT Superkick on P. KING, but holds up as he catches a glimpse of his mist-covered face; HOSHI immediately charges in, from off-screen, and DESTROYS WINDHAM with a Baisaku Knee Kick! IMPULSE breaks up the count!) RICE: (V/O) "Duk might have gotten a fair shake from Impulse’s Code of Honor, but it was the Marathon Man’s boot that he’d be remembering!" (CUTTO: IMPULSE laying P. KING DUK WINDHAM out with the SUDDEN IMPACT Superkick! Senior Official GREG HERPIN slides in for the elementary ONE-TWO-THREE! HOSHI, red tongue hanging out, watches on from a crouched, attack position! CUTTO: Back LIVE! w/ NATRONE RICE.) RICE: "Ouch! P. King got his duck STRAIGHT COOKED, son! When Impulse and Hoshi weren’t after each other, they knocked him around pinball machine-style! Guess that’s what happens when you dishonor your family and join the Windham Clan! P. King is eliminated from the GRAND PRIX. No surprises there as Impulse and Akita Hoshi MOVE ON!" Jack Harmen vs. Nein NATRONE RICE: "In a departure from the usual, we'll be trying a new feature here. Our producers had actually filmed a number of our fans live at BRAWL with their own coverage of the event. Among the best of them, was this guy." CUT TO: A middle aged long haired hippy, clearly a fan of SUPERFLY, doing a small dance in place shaking his blonde ringlets back and forth. He stops with a huge split armed pose and begins to narrate without inhaling a breath. HIPPY: "So the thing is that we have JACK HARMEN, whose name is his NAME, going up against NEIN whose name is NOT his name, and it's like the set up to the philosophical argument, or ZEN KOAN, of the irresteebull force and the immovable object, and yet it wasn't because both objects were actually moving and not so irresisterbull. So, yes. HARMEN does the thing where they dance around in a circle, but NEIN, against the advice of the mysterious dude called EEGOR, whose name is also NOT his name, puts his head down, and uses his fingers to make devil horns like this." (The dirty HIPPY bends over with his fingers extended from his head. He starts stomping like a bull.) HIPPY: "And he's rearing to go, and HARMEN is like, `what? THAT’S MY DEVIL TAUNT' and he starts stomping in place, grunting like Mama Cass getting her banana split licked while NOVA, whose name OUGHT to be his name, is screaming to nobody in particular, slapping at his belly which has the greatest airbrush design I’ll talk about later – what’d he say? OH YEAH – FUNK THAT! Do the choochoo!'" (The dirty HIPPY brushes back his hair, taking a deep breadth…) HIPPY: "And HARMEN is `what?' which isn’t great ‘cause NEIN’s hopping around like he’s crapping peyote and then he's all CHARGE at HARMEN. And HARMEN is all CHARGE too and you know what happens?!? HARMEN does the LOCOMOTIVE kick right on that bentover bullrushing head and I swear by the PLEIADES and ALL THE FREE LOVE I NEVER EVER GOT if he did not turn NEIN upside-down and inside-out so hard at the same time NEIN turned into two different people." (The HIPPY shrugs) HIPPY: "Maybe. Anyway, HARMEN covers and EEGOR throws himself into the ring as fast as pretty fast dude, but he doesn't make it in time, and he stomps HARMEN, and then is like all, `whoa, maybe I acted a bit rashly there'." (The HIPPY holds up a finger) HIPPY: "But now is the good part!" HIPPY: "NOVA gets in the ring, and you know he's been eating the cupcakes. HARMEN slams EEGOR in the middle of the ring. NOVA is on the turnbuckles, and takes off his shirt." (HIPPY's lip trembles.) HIPPY: "He's got SHAGGY's from SCOOBIE DOO's smiling face airbrushed on his tummy." HIPPY: "HARMEN starts jumping around in the ring like it might be on fire, and he's pointing and hollering." HIPPY: "AND NOVA started slapping his belly faster than Keith Moon after snorting a 32-ball of coke and one of those espresso coffees while HARMEN screams with crowd and NOVA does a big belly flop splash on that EEGOR dude. I know that it means something, but fuck if I know what." (CUT TO: NATRONE RICE, who’s head hits his desk dejectedly. FADETOBLACK.) Jason Murray vs. Frankie Fargo vs. Phil Atken (FADEIN: Back in the BRAWLOPTIMUS production truck with NATRONE RICE…) RICE: "Welcome back to the BRAWL Y’ALL! We’re gonna hit up our last highlight of the night – three NFW competitors in PHIL ATKEN, JASON MURRAY and FRANKIE FARGO! Atken trying to regain some chances at gold all around, while Murray and Fargo probably are on the lookout for the SUPERFLY EXPRESS EMT Championships…" (MUSICUP: "STOP KICKING MY HEART AROUND" – THE BLACK CROWES) (CUTTO: As the ringing slide intro to the song hits, we see PHIL ATKEN walking briskly down the entrance ramp, sheepishly grinning with wide eyes at the surrounding spectacle of fans rocking out to his theme song… CUTTO: JASON MURRAY getting a subdued reaction in comparison to his valet BRONTE LAKES, who’s sneering at all the cat calls and wolf whistlers… CUTTO: Spinning spotlights swirling around as the packed infield pumps their fists up in the air! The song kicks into high gear just as FRANKIE FARGO comes out in a frantic high-step run underneath the FRONTIERtron… CUTTO: Rebel Flags being held up in the air…) RICE: "And let’s face it, there were no saints walking down that ramp for this one… Atken, Murray and Fargo pretty much throw the rules out the window for various different reasons…" Just come out and say it… (CUTTO: ATKEN countering a double back body drop by rolling over his opponents, landing on his feet and spinning around with a perfectly aimed drop-low spread-eagle dropkick into both nether-regions of his opponents! MURRAY crumbles, while FARGO’s spit hits the third row as he drops cross-eyed) …just spit those words out of your mouth… RICE: "Atken never meeting a ballsack blitzkrieg that any Dirty Scot wouldn’t be proud of…" (CUTTO: JASON MURRAY launching out of the ring with a Plancha onto ATKEN! ATKEN takes a high-heel stomp from LAKES to boot! FRANKIE FARGO’s staggering on the outside and gets tossed into the crowd by MURRAY! FARGO tries to fight back in, but eats a rolling leg clothesline – MURRAY dumping himself upside-down in the first row off the richochet…) RICE: "Murray bringing that backyard aerial brawl brigade into a raucous environment, all the while spouting off about what NFW’s supposedly lost in character and soul…" (CUTTO: FARGO getting crowdsurfed back into the fray unbeknownst to MURRAY, who turns around into a 32 oz. Busch Lite faceblaster followed by a kick to the gut and battering ram into the ring steps! FARGO holds up the BUSCH LITE cup and points at BRONTE LAKES, while rubbing his crotch area and screaming "YOU GOT THAT CURTAIN MATCHING THE DRAPES, GIRL!?") …Stop kicking my heart around… RICE: "And Frankie Fargo’s just plain…insane." (FLASH CUTTO’s: As the first extended guitar break hits, we got MURRAY launching off with a swan dive bodypress on FARGO! ATKEN screaming "GO GO ZIDANE!" and dropping a spiking headbutt through the spread-eagled and exposed JASON MURRAY! FARGO flying off the top rope with a double axehandle on ATKEN, sending the Scot into a stagger and ripe for a ripping back elbow strike! FARGO going for the pin, but getting pulled up by MURRAY who hooks him into a Crucifix Cradle! ATKEN breaks up the pin by springboarding off the ropes for a diving legdrop! ATKEN flipping out of a suplex by FARGO only to take a reverse hurricanrana from MURRAY! MURRAY gets dragged up by a fish-hook of the mouth by FARGO and right into a whiplashing uppercut forearm!) RICE: "But once things started rolling, these fans were treated to a match that really had them rocking!" I said in the beginning… (CUTTO: ATKEN coming from behind MURRAY on the top turnbuckles, pulling his leg out and causing him to crotch across the top turnbuckles! CUTTO: ATKEN hitting the TOWER OF LONDON on MURRAY to a crowd explosion! CUTTO: ATKEN getting dragged off the pin by FRANKIE FARGO – ATKEN literally trying to scratch and claw his way back to the nearly unconscious MURRAY… only for FARGO to clamp down and hook in a CAMEL CLUTCH! As the fans roar, we can hear FARGO scream out "YER LUCKY I AIN’T SIX INCHES SOUTH – I’D MAKE YA FEEL THE SAME POP I WANNA GIVE THAT BLACK CHERRY OF SHANIQUA!" ATKEN’s too befuddled by that taunt to even answer the ref’s questioning of him tapping out or wanting to quit…) RICE: "And I’m telling you these three were giving out some stone cold shots out there – you’d think this might be for it all with the way they came at each other!" …I can’t stand looking at you… (QUICK CUTTO’s: MURRAY blasting a side kick to the back of FARGO’s head to break up the submission! MURRAY hooking a BOSTON CRAB on ATKEN! FARGO delivering a back suplex to MURRAY to break up the submission! FARGO hooking a TEXAS CLOVERLEAF on ATKEN! MURRAY breaks up the submission with a airborne shining wizard! ATKEN’s rolling out of the ring, holding his back and walking up the ramp – the crowd booing! TEDDY ALEXANDER storming out and chasing him back into the ring!) …just Stop… (CLOSEUP: ATKEN backpedaling right into a suicide plancha bulldog from MURRAY to a crowd explosion! CUTTO: ATKEN taking a nutshot from BRONTE LAKES!) …Kicking my Heart Around… RICE: "Phil Atken thought he had enough, but little did he know he had more…getting chased back into the ring by his new associate Teddy Alexander… and right into the crossfire of Jason Murray!" (CUTTO: FARGO catching MURRAY with a kneestrike as he enters the ring – reeling him in for a STUMP PILEDRIVER to a huge crowd explosion!) RICE: "But Frankie Fargo turned the tides and really ended up as the hometown favorite." (FLASH CUTTO’s: As we skip over the guitar solo and use technical wizardy to hit the song coda – we get a reel of highlights including FARGO hitting a Reverse Neckbreaker and getting a close two count on MURRAY! ATKEN with a cradle suplex on FARGO, but MURRAY breaking it up with a rolling senton! MURRAY going for another submission on ATKEN, but getting rolled into a small package that’s broken up by a FARGO sliding dropkick! FARGO hitting the MISSISIPPI MUDSLIDE LEGDROP on ATKEN! FARGO strutting around the ring… MURRAY hitting a SHOOTING STAR LEGDROP across the back of FARGO’s head as he goes to pin ATKEN! ATKEN mustering a last-ditch effort to flying shoulderblock MURRAY out of the ring – then pin FARGO with a hook of the tights!) RICE: "But Jason Murray’s breakup of a pinfall leads the hometown favorite down the path of defeat, out of the GPX… and a Phil Atken pinfall coming out nowhere to win this with Murray the neutral survivor! That’s all we got for you tonight folks – a little quick, a little dirty…" (MUSIC CUT.) RICE: "But we’re not too far away from RELOADED 13… which I’m sure we’ll be a show for the ages! SEE YA!"
