NFW news: Week 7 Continues Ratings Increase

4 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Week 7 Continues Ratings Increase Posted by Natrone Rice on 4 Mar 2006 The critical acclaim may not have been awarded at the recent ENN Awards show, but ESEN officials were pleased to announce that ratings for both Crash TV and the first Wrestlestock Tour show for Midnight Madness continued the trend of increased ratings on their network. To view Crash TV Week 7: Click Here To view the Midnight Madness Week 7: Click Here Rather than retreat amongst the criticism for Week 6's offensive nature, neither conference stopped pushing the envelope. In Tokyo, Crash TV viewers were saved from the whole match b/t Jonathan Marx and Beau Michaels, but any Season Pass subscriber could describe the pupil-burning sight of Jonathan Marx winning the Pole on Pole match against Beau Micheals. Kevin Powers and Lindsay Troy got in a huge brawl for their match, and YYJ debuted the Yori-abbit to his own demise in his NFW championship match against Doc Silver. On the flip side, viewers of Midnight Madness watched the Western Conference nearly burn down the Pacific Beach Boardwalk thanks to some antics by Troy Windham, as well as witness not one...but TWO bloody brawls along the boardwalk and beach area. The first caused Mike Randalls, NOVA, Brandon Youngblood and Marcus Cameron to spend a night in jail due to some property destruction in the amusement park, while Joey Melton and Craig Miles took the long scenic route for a 30 minute slugfest that included Dixie Horns, the OSS attacking them from Jet Skis and Calvin Carlton nearly running over Melton with a yacht. The show ended with Troy Windham happily burning down Valerian Garden's (Eli Flair's wife's rock n' roll troupe) bus, while members were trying to get some shut-eye. As far as statistical data shows, it looks like NFW is on pace to eclipse its quarterly ratings record on ESEN, as well as its viewership on FWrestling.com. Last year 9% of visitors to fwrestling.com visited NFW exclusively, while right now the figure has leapt to 25%.
