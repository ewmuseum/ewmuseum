Ill Fortune Biography Biography Alex Austin is a multiple-time world submission grappling champion. Lucky Seven (Laurie Strife) is a world class gymnast and former Cirque Du Soleil performer. Both are relative newcomers to professional wrestling who dismiss the sport's traditions and history in order to pave a new way. The two met while under the tutelage of the world famous Castor Strife, who is also Laurie's older brother. Her oldest brother, indie wrestling promoter RA Palazzo, debuted both her and Austin in the now-defunct Next Level Wrestling (NLW) back in 2010. When NFW bought out NLW later that year, they acquired the pair's contracts. While Alex helped Castor Strife form The Guild, Seven was sent down to Philadelphia to train with Teresa Quaranta in NFW's developmental league UWA. The next year, Seven captured the UWA Women's Championship, and embarked on a major tour of Japan where she won a major tournament in SLICE, ESEN's international joshi (women's pro) affiliate. By the time she made her NFW debut at Brawl 62, Alex Austin had temporarily taken over The Guild in Castor's absence and recruited her to be at his side. After an unsuccessful attempt at winning the Triple Crown Championship, Alex Austin parted from The Guild and asked Seven to form a team with him. While still maintaining allegiance to her brother and The Guild, Seven has made Ill Fortune her priority. Some credit Austin with bolstering Castor Strife's submission defense, which helped him defeat Impulse and win the vacant NFW World Heavyweight Title in 2011. His goal now is to make a name for himself by capturing the Everette Memorial Titles with Lucky Seven. Because of their highly specialized skill-sets and relative inexperience, Austin and Seven play to their strengths while in the ring. While steadily improving his overall game, Austin is primarily a submission wrestler. Almost nobody can hang with him on the ground, but he can't brawl and is terrible at using the ring to his advantage. On the flip side, Seven is lightning quick and mostly utilizes high-impact strikes in the ring. She trains kickboxing almost as much as wrestling, and her kicks and elbows are fast and deadly. The ex-balance beam gymnast and circus performer is a master at using the ring or any other structure to her advantage. If she can leap off of it, she will. However, most of the NFW roster can out-muscle her on the mat, so if she can't stay ahead with her speed and strikes, she will tag in Austin to slow the pace with his expert grappling. They have a peculiar hot-cold style offense that is highly unpredictable and difficult to gameplan for. Wrestling Style Submission and High Impact Finisher Name Burning Man Finisher Description Austin hoists an opponent onto his shoulders in an electric chair, and Seven hits him with a flying enzuigiri off the top turnbuckle.
