NFW news: Court Injunction Slows Pirate TV

20 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Court Injunction Slows Pirate TV Posted by Natrone Rice on 20 Apr 2006 We wish it were a shocking move, but Anne Mystic quietly has prevented Craig Miles from airing the broadcast of UWA Pirate TV citing contract issues, as well as footage she's not ready to release publicly in fears of her repuation being tarnished. Rumours have it that Mystic seeks more money for the sold-out show, as well as creative control over the telecast where some internet sites have reported she'll have participated in a match she's not extremely proud of, as well as a shocking conclusion to the main event.
