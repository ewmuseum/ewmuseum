Off to Valinor — 26 March 2008 I was just wondering if anyone had any thoughts, comments or ideas they'd like to toss out for me to use before I start hammering away at it over the spring. I'm anticipating a summer finish, btw.
Right now, I've got the basic framework, color scheme and close to the logo being finished.
I'd really to like hear some ideas on what you'd like to see for: -The Front Page? The Site as a whole? --Roster Page/Individual Roster Pages? ---Event Archives, Show layouts (the same, diff. per type of show?) ----Incorporating the forums? -----Roleplays? ------Anything outside the box that you think would work well for NFW? Keep 'em coming! :cool: suddenimpact — 26 March 2008 -The Front Page? The Site as a whole? The front page doesn't need to have much to it, it's just a 'starting point.' Links to all the other sections, of course - maybe a 'Latest RP' and 'Latest Note from Management' link to FW Central, links to a 'Top story' and 'Latest Event' and 'Next Event' with the rundown and a few notes about each match.
--Roster Page/Individual Roster Pages? Simple and straightforward. Maybe a 'Highlight' every week or month? And is there a way for us to update this ourselves?
If not, it should be a simple matter to take the roster that's on the RP board and just do a weekly/monthly 'update.'
---Event Archives, Show layouts (the same, diff. per type of show?) This is really the only thing that a website for a fed *needs*. I like the way the Ultratitle layout had the shows archived by year - every January, the previous year's shows should just get archived and the 'Events' page can start fresh.
Doesn't matter to me how the shows are laid out. The current format is fine with me - 'shows' being written in PBP with segments written however the handler wants, and 'house shows' written in summary from the POV of the person watching. Anything else is gravy.
----Incorporating the forums? Third base? -----Roleplays? If there is a way to link/stream (I don't know coding, can you tell?) the latest RP threads, I think it would be a good idea. But this could be on the main page as clickable links.
------Anything outside the box that you think would work well for NFW? Fantasy-fantasy booking? Take a current roster member and pair up against a former roster member, run through their strengths and weaknesses, and take a poll on who would win? I don't know, I'm pretty low - rent when it comes to what a website 'needs.'
