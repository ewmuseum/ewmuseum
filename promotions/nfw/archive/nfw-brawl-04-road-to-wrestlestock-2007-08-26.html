NFW Brawl 04: ROAD TO WRESTLESTOCK, 26 August 2007

26 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 04: ROAD TO WRESTLESTOCK 26 Aug 2007 Rogers Arena, Vancouver, British Columbia (seats 20,000) Intro Three weeks later, and I’m happy to say that we finally found a living soul that can even recall through the haze of smoke and stench of alcohol that New Frontier Wrestling left across up north in Vancouver to kickoff the Wrestlestock 2 tour. I’m Richard Reese reporting for NFW Brawl and this report has been derived from a tiny Canadian gentle boy named Devin Woods, the last human of 18,000 that left this Earth for the Mothership. It took a lot of lawyers and access to one of the most top-secret drug testing facilities, but the Little Boy Devin was able to remember the dementia of the opening kickoff. From the logo of Dan Ryan emulating a senile man being whipped by Nurse Snow White all the way to the fact that New Frontier Wrestling workers were the ‘security’ for this show, let’s just say that sobriety, morality and just plain common sense got left way behind during this bombshell. And that wasn’t more prevalent when the first match of the evening was announced as the Met-RX Express, Powermaster and Manpower faced off against the new NFW squad "The New VD," comprised of Johnny Rage and Johnny Styles – two pranksters with a heart of gold and mostly poop for brains. MET-RX Express vs The 'New' VD Of course, I don’t think either Manpower or Powermaster possess a brain, so lucky for the The New VD, they had a mental advantage over their opponents. The match went about five minutes as a bunch of NFW workers went through the crowd handing out some sort of capsules and sugarcubes according to Mister Woods. After watching some of his friend’s head explode, he was informed that the New VD had won the match after Powermaster and Manpower clotheslined into each other into unconsciousness allowing for "Mr. Irresistible" Johnny Styles to hit a standing twisting moonsault across both men for the pin. Rook Black vs. Clifford Hugo It was then time for Rook Black to go up against a Mystery Opponent, many believed this would be Kooter Cruise or Doc Curiosity making his NFW debut, and these people are what we generally term to be retards. Rook Black came down to the ring because otherwise there wouldn't really be a Rook Black match. Not only did he come to the ring but he also pivoted INTO it. The crowd they went all a-buzzybiz about who could possibly be the mystery opponent. Sadly, such is life in the mystery opponent industry it was some guy the crowd didn't particularly give a shit about. I blame NFW, in general, for everything wrong with my life. He made his way into the ring and Rook Black promptly kicked his Clifford Hugo’s in. The lights caught Black's shiny pants, those bitches are as shiny as all hell according to Devin, almost burning his eyes out the back of his brain. Annnnnnnnyway, after kicking in Hugo's pretty little face, which could have possibly caved his head in. As he then proceeded to sit down upon Hugo's neck he noticed Legion watching on from the front row. Legion was reported to have been glaring or perhaps gawking. It was very shady but it didn't put Black off from his game plan. He continued punishing Hugo's neck to the point Hugo was screaming "ouch my neck" but this did not quell Black's neck based rage as he dropped fist upon fist down upon it. I may have lost count but there was something like 17 fists to the back of one Clifford Hugo's neck. The referee at this point stepped in and asked Black to stop dropping fists because it was boring the crowd to death, Rook shrugged and dropped another fist. Legion continued to glare at the match as Hugo tried his best to crawl away from Black and his fistings. However Black dragged him back into the centre of the ring and locked in his Crossbow submission hold of submission. Within mere seconds Hugo was tapping and Rook Black was declared the victor of the contest. His hand was raised as proof of this victory. Everyone then left the ring and the show continued. Billy Lovemuscle vs. Phil Atken After the devastating Rook Black victory, the guitars started janglin’, the crowd started hootin’ and hollerin’ ‘cause Billy Lovemuscle was here. Billy was making an early run in the Television title division, but on this night Billy Lovemuscle went from bad to worse starting against Phil Atken. Atken’s had a little hot streak in New Frontier Wrestling and was pencilled in for the National Championship tournament after what he was able to accomplish nefariously this evening. Accompanied by his best friend and manager Dirk Dickwood, Atken and the Dirkinator used the loose NFW rules in place for their benefit. Lovemuscle was able to use some early momentum, taking Phil along the dusty trails with some clotheslines, a bulldog and a leaping elbow drop. But after getting pushed off the top rope by Dirk, Phil was able to hit a leaping DDT, a round of kneedrops and then turned over Billy into a modified Camel Clutch. Dirk only riled up Billy by slapping him across the face, Lovemuscle stood up and stumbled backwards slamming Atken into the corner. Lovemuscle worked over some fists standing atop the turnbuckle, Atken brought him out and countered with an Inverted Atomic Drop followed by a chopblock. Atken tried to hook in the Figure 4, but Lovemuscle kicked him out of the ring via his arse. At this point, Devin Woods sadly explained that he started trying to hump his best friend’s girlfriend in the ear, got hit over the head with a chair. He came back to his senses and saw Teresa Q running down to the ringside area. Teresa had a match later that night, was also pencilled into the National Championship tournament and most importantly, had a hearty disdain for Billy Lovemuscle. Coincidentally, Dirk tried to hug her, only to get Northern Lights Suplexed through the Canadian Broadcast table. Lovemuscle was setting up for a piledriver on the concrete, but Teresa Q flew in with a spinning leg lariat to the back of his legs. Lovemuscle folded like a chair, which is ironic ‘cause Q then wailed a chairshot across his kneecap. Of course, Phil Atken wasn’t thrilled with this for some reason…maybe ‘cause he’s a misogynist pig ‘cause he backhand slapped Teresa and screamed, "DON’T GO THERE GIRLFRIEND!" Teresa responded by spitting in his face, which Phil sold like he got shot by a Winchester in the face. Q took off up the ramp, not looking behind as Atken rolled Lovemuscle into the ring and hooked in his "Humiliation" Figure Four Leglock. To Lovemuscle’s credit he would not tap, but nonetheless he passed out from the pain allowing Atken to procure victory. Teresa Q vs. The Sailor Man Naturally, Teresa Q. versus the Sailor Man was tabbed right after this, and this one didn’t last long at all. Q. was taking pictures with fans at ringside, but Sailor Man attacked and dragged Teresa into the ring. With stomps and passionate high sea offensive superlatives in regards to wenches, Sailor Man then proceeded to hit a nasty powerslam for a near pinfall. To Teresa’s credit she survived that and a double stomp that Devin told me made him puke ‘cause he couldn’t bear the thought of her ovaries exploding. Teresa dodged a big splash and started rousing a comeback with a vicious roundhouse kick followed by a tornado DDT which almost got the Sailor Man pinned. Teresa went up top and hit a Moonsault Bodypress, but again couldn’t keep the portly man of the Seas down for the count. Not too shockingly, Billy Lovemuscle started hobbling down to the ring on crutches after Teresa’s involvement in the prior match. A Eunuch Provocateur (don’t ask) followed and the crowd sensed a big win in Teresa’s favour, but buzzed as Billy stumbled into the ring swinging the crutch wildly. Teresa kept rolling away from the desperate crutch shots, Billy yelling in frustration. Billy was so distracted that he didn’t see Sailor Man fetch his oar, and you guessed it, shatter it across the back of his kneecap. Billy screamed in horror as Sailor raised his broken oar over his head howling like a Tusken raider according to Devin. Teresa leapt to her feet and hit the Process of Illumination out of nowhere on the Sailor Man, getting the three count and huge victory. Lovemuscle’s condition has been listed as an ACL injury that may take him months to recover from. David Tui vs. Dan Ryan vs. Rocko Daymon The next match to appear before both us and our eyes was David Tui vs. Rocko Daymon vs. Dan Ryan. It was certainly quite the THREAT of a match, ho ho ho. During the match there were a lot of those wrestling moves that people seem to enjoy. All your favourites were there from the suplex to the back suplex. From the top rope suplex to the top rope back suplex and back again. It certainly got the crowd to both rock and roll in their seats. After a good bunch of that back and forth action that the kids on the street rave about, Dan Ryan manage to take advantage of the situation, dominating over both Tui and Daymon while letting out very manly yells. While Ryan was making his manly man screams, he was taken down with an equally manly low blow by Tui. What however wasn't very manly was Caitlyn Daymon who was at ringside. I heard this song once saying that "woman are whore, all whores" and I have pondered how true this statement was. I’m guessing that a few of Rocko’s opponents might’ve felt that way when Caitlyn Daymon slid a chair into the ring, which Rocko picked up. I almost typed Rocky there, wouldn't that have been an embarassing situation. Anyway Caitlyn slid the chair into the ring and grabbed another chair herself. She then also slid herself into the ring. Rocko picked up said slid in the chair. As Dan Ryan was staggering up to his feet clutching his balls he was SMASHED with a double conchairto. Many who witnessed it were reported as saying "ouch". The referee called for the bell, apparently smashing Dan Ryan's head in is illegal in a pro-wrestling context. Rocko and Caitlyn continued to beat down on Ryan, aided be a recently revived David Tui. The beatdown continued for a good few moments before SEAN STEVENS ran down to the ring to make the save. He was indeed what many would call a HOUSE OF FIRE. Or is it HOUSE ON FIRE? I'm not sure, however he cleared the ring of both men and women types and aided Dan Ryan back up upon his feet. Ryan was unsure how to take this action however and both men had an awkward stare down for what seemed like at least three minutes before parting ways and partying hard. Cameron Cruise vs. Maggot Cameron Cruise against Maggot followed, a matchup of two NFW veterans involved with all of the singles titles in the New Frontier. Cruise started off a house afire, blasting away with a flurry of quick jabs to Maggot fresh off downing a fifth of Crown Royal. Cruise followed that up with a series of dropkicks, a few armdrags and a vicious side elbow smash that sent Maggot’s spit flying towards the upper deck. Cruise went for a Hornet Splash, but Maggot actually caught him and charged out of the corner with a modified Running Spinebuster that derailed Cruise’s momentum off the tracks. Cruise writhed on the mat as Maggot started stomping his head towards China and then dropping a vicious knee across the bridge of Cruise’s nose. Maggot worked Cruise over with a shoulderbreaker and an Anderson Family armbar, which had Cruise screaming for holy murder. Cruise got to the ropes, but his arm looked limp as Maggot ripped him up by the hair and took him over with a butterfly suplex. Maggot reeled Cruise into a standing headscissors, but Cruise fought him off with a back body drop. Cruise quickly leapt to the top turnbuckles taking down Maggot with a bodypress for a two count. Cruise grabbed Maggot and sent him packing into the turnbuckles and this time hit the Hornet Splash, just as Maggot pulled the referee into the way. Cruise tossed the ref out of the way, monkey flipped Maggot towards the middle of the ring and caught him with a Superkick as he rose to his feet. Cruise went for the Shipwreck, but Maggot flipped him over and Cruise rolled into the corner taking his own Hornet Splash and flopping facefirst into the canvas. Maggot screamed "Its time to take the trash out!" Of course, the crowd cheered as Kooter Cruise charged into the ring blasting him across the back of the head with a clothesline as Felix Red sauntered down the aisle. Maggot and Kooter traded right hands, Maggot’s eyes bulging at the huge and unghastly sight attacking him as Felix grabbed a seat on the turnbuckles. Cruise rose to his feet and grabbed Kooter, trying to get him out of the ring, but Felix flew off the turnbuckles hitting a flipping Diamond Cutter. Maggot leapt on Felix, but got tossed off by Kooter, who lifted up Felix in a chokehold bawling about him killing his daddy. At this point, the bell rang as the groggy referee signalled for a no contest. Kooter tossed Felix down after a nutshot from Maggot, Felix rolling out and backpedaling up the aisle as Maggot escaped Kooter’s roaring clutches. Meanwhile, Cameron laid on the mat holding his head apparently the worst for wear in the situation. Coral Avalon vs. Hunchblack It was then time for Coral Avalon vs. Hunchblack. Oh god, I think I'm going to be sick just thinking about that THING again. Jesus Christ it was horrific, Hunchblack wasn't that great looking either. Ho, ho, ho. Please kill me now, no really please. My life is based around these terrible jokes, the sweet taste of death is the only way to release myself from the pain I inflict upon me... Much like the pain Hunchblack inflicted upon Coral Avalon in the opening moments of this contest. See how flawless that transition was. I segue PERFECTLY. I am a master seguer. Hunchblack did indeed have the upper hand for a lot of the bout, which is sad because he is a disgusting mutant and no doubt impossible to pin. The match itself actually had the crowd into, something about deformed human beings really perk up a wrestling crowd. I think they saw a lot of themselves in the Hunchbacked Black gentleman. A Hunchblack if you Will. I just find him to be street urchin filth, and the mutated kind is the worst kind. After some of most beautiful restholds that my eye has ever witnessed, Hunchblack attempted to nail Avalon with a running senton. However as he was gathering up his speed and he gathered enough speed to be SMACKED BY ROOK BLACK OUT OF NOWHERE. Black smashed a chair in his horrific hunchbacked face then climbed into the ring to celebrate with Avalon. When the referee raised Hunchblack's hand Rook Black was incredibly confused. Maybe he should have done a run in when the ref wasn't directly looking at him. Avalon, mildly upset at these developments some would say began shoving Rook in a pissed of manner. Coral then left the ring in a pissed-offly manner, leave Rook in the ring with a look of confusion on his face. A look that expressed the word "hurrrrrr". Honey I F*cked Your Mom vs. Felix Red & Kooter Michaels-Cruise The main event finally hit with Honey I F*cked Your Mom facing off against Felix and Kooter Cruise, who appropriately weren’t on good terms after what happened in the Maggot versus Cameron Cruise matchup earlier in the evening. Kooter stomped in a pout down to the ring as Felix walked in front of him, completely ignoring Kooter’s yells at him. Mittens and Yori’s entrance was an eye spectacle that apparently gave Devin some seizures, which caused him to piss himself. The match seemed to go just as well for all the participants involved as Kooter was too much to handle for his partner or opponents. Apparently, seeing his father beaten up by Felix struck a chord in the giant as he mauled through the two cruiserweights in the early part of the match. Press Slams, Power Slams, a double chokeslam out of the ring. However, using a little heel chicanery and one massive double kick to the groin, Mittens and Yori were able to take Kooter down to the mat before delivering a double Vertical Splash. Kooter rolled away and tagged in Felix (still on the turnbuckles) who didn’t seem too interested in wrestling, until Mittens and Yori hiptossed him into the ring followed with a double dropkick. Acting listlessly and barely defending himself, Felix fell prey to a nasty bronco buster from Mittens followed by a face shattering 976 from Yori. Felix kicked out at the two count, spitting blood in Yori’s face, which made him roll around in agony claiming he’d been given the AIDS. That last part might’ve been embellished by Mr. Woods. Kooter not exactly understanding the situation, really thought Felix had AIDS and charged the ring blasting away on HIFYM with reckless abandon until the ref had to try and get him out of the ring. During that distraction, Felix threw some undisclosed powder into both their faces, Mittens fell out of the ring landing on his back unconscious. As Mittens’ paws faced the rafters rigidly, Yori got caught in the Ecstasy of the Agony and the three count. And so ended the show according to Devin Woods, but not the hallucinogens that won’t leave his body.
