American Panda vs. Dan Ryan BRAWL RECAP: Dan Ryan vs. American Panda By: Buckeye on the Barbie Via: iPHONE wizardry The Cincinnati fans were privvy to that of "2001: A Space Odyssey" and emerging from the back came the Endangered Enigma, AMERICAN PANDA. With a roar to the adoring, the Walking Contradiction made way to the ring to awaiting the arrival for his well-travelled opponent. This match is a crucial standing point in the career of American Panda since narrowly missing out on the Bunkhouse Brawl crown, the Panda is looking for some career direction. DAN RYAN's arrival was chorused with boos as one of the most successful wrestlers on the planet discarded the fans nonchalantly as he stared down the ramp toward the Panda's direction. With a cutthroat gesture Ryan began the charge down toward the ring, sliding beneath the bottom rope and as the bell tolled, the pair collided in a tornado of fists. The tornado turned into a game of punch tennis which saw American Panda backing Ryan into the corner, where he relinquished with the knuckles and lit up his chest with a knife-edge chop echoed with a "WOOOO!" from the fans. The second chop rang out with the same cacophony yet the third was a swing and a miss, with Ryan ducking beneath the forearm and behind Panda, shoving him into the corner. Having passed the slap baton over to Ryan, American Panda was struck across the back with a big forearm before being whipped across the ring. With sixty pounds of advantage, Dan Ryan leapt into the air crushed the Endangered Enigma against the turnbuckles with a bone-crunching splash, sending Panda staggering out of the corner. Capturing Panda from behind, Ryan drove him into the canvas with a belly-to-back suplex following up with the first pin cover of the evening, garnering an early two. With his first pin attempt unsuccessful, Ryan rose and continued the onslaught, taking the lead of the situation leaving the fans with little hope for their American Panda. A sidewalk slam which shook the bleachers, followed by a running knee drop to the sternum saw Ryan attempting his second pinfall but the Panda's paw shot toward the heavens alleviating the fans woes and Ryan's attempt for victory. Ryan frustration was becoming noticeable as his shots became stiffer, yet his exasperation led to a window and through said window leapt the American Panda in all his Panda Glory, blocking a vertical suplex attempt and spinning through with the Panda Paw, which sent Ryan stumbling backward to the ropes gasping for breath. Panda charged forth and clotheslined Ryan to the outside where the fans in the front row were given opportunity to pat backs and give Ryan a piece of their mind for messing about with their NFW World Champion. On the outside, Panda gave Ryan a taste of his boot before tossing the ring steps right into his face, both the clang and cheers rang our loudly before Panda turned to the fans. Surprisingly, Ryan showed a great deal of fortitude and charged forth with a sickening big boot of his own, leaving Panda layed out ringside. Ryan teased a count-out, sliding back into the ring and forcing the referee to make the count yet at seven he was climbing over the ropes. Eight he made a cut-throat gesture. Nine he leapt, forcing the referee to quit his count as the knees drove down onto the spine of American Panda. Having taken the action back to the ring, Ryan continued his charge. A spinebuster slam looked as if it were curtain for American Panda yet he was able to pull up a shoulder before the third strike of the canvas. A rope whip saw American Panda duck a clothesline attempt and rebounded with a Lou Thesz press, riding Dan Ryan down into the canvas where he unleashed with some hammer-fists before the referee pulled him off. American Panda turned to the fans shouting "THIS IS YOUR HOPE SPOT, KIDS!" before pulling Dan Ryan to his feet, capturing him in his hands with a double handed choke lift. He began to gnaw at the neck of Dan Ryan, chewing his fuggen jugular, before spitting the red mist-slash-blood in his face. It seemed as if the American Panda had Dan Ryan right where he wanted him. He grabbed Ryan around the throat, trying to land that double-handed chokeslam… …and somehow, someway, Ryan broke the stranglehold and got his own one-handed version on Panda. Of course, Ryan was blinded so when he chokeslam countered Panda, he didn’t realize he did it out of the ring! The adrenaline burst subsided as Ryan fell to his knees on the mat, screaming in pain and holding his eyes. The ref was checking if he was ok, when… HIGH FLYER. Racing down to the ring with a plastic bag, wrapping it around Panda’s head! Panda started freaking out and you could tell this by his arms flailing and that the INSIDE of the plastic bag was getting misted.
