NFW Reloaded HOLDS COURT!, 12 June 2011

12 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Reloaded HOLDS COURT! 12 Jun 2011 North Charleston Coliseum, North Charleston, South Carolina (seats 13,295) JJ DEVILLE (lead plaintiff) vs. NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING (defendant) V/O: "Are we ready to go live?" V/O 2: "Yeah, Kerry, let’s roll…" (FADEIN: A panoramic crowd shot of the Charleston crowd going ballistic! All kinds of signs are being held up – CASTOR FILMED MY WEDDING NIGHT, I-M-P-U-L-S-E, Hey JJ: Sue This.) O’CONNOR: "Hey there folks, I’m Kerry O’Connor and with me is my co-host Lamont Hollywood—" H’WOOD: "Co-host? Don’t you mean star—" (The microphone makes a noise – it starts warbling and screeching, both O’CONNOR and H’WOOD start tapping on their microphones… Suddenly, the crowd starts LOUDLY BOOING!) AUGUST: "No! NO! NO! I’m August De La Rossi. The OFFICIAL VOICE OF NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING! I’m sorry I’m late, fellas, but I was busy in the back finishing up my latest column for Pitchfork. I’m really glad Elephant 6 got around to remastering the Apples In Stereo albums but they could have done a better job, don’t you think?" (There’s a big pause.) H’WOOD: "What in God’s name are you talking about, you little twerp? Look, no one wants you here. You’re lucky that I don’t take off this belt and whip you with it like I did your mom--" AUGUST: "You take that back! My mom was a saint! She understood me! MY FATHER NEVER DID!" O’CONNOR: "Cool it, you two. Please. With me is Lamont Hollywood, the long-time color commentator extraordinaire here in NFW… and now we’re joined by a THIRD person. Appointed by the new co-owners here in NFW to be THEIR mouthpiece… is August De La Rossi." AUGUST: "I prefer to be called a ‘TRUTH TELLER’ Kerry. Because that’s what I’m here to do – to cut through YOUR corporate spin and misinformation to these sheep out there who are too DUMB to realize that they’re being lied to and manipulated by THE MAN AND THE MACHINE. And I’m here too—" H’WOOD: "So, Kerry, I’m REALLY looking forward to tonight’s show." AUGUST: "Hey, you cut me off!" H’WOOD: "I know!" O’CONNOR: "Tonight we have a hell of a card, headlined by two matches that will go a LONG way to determine our next NFW WORLD CHAMPION! Impulse versus Dan Ryan. And Castor Strife, who has become a bit of a CULT HERO in the past few weeks, against the embattled Dorchester Stratton." H’WOOD: "I’ve been a big backer of Dorchester for the past few months. He’s become like a SECOND SON to me. But he’s petulant and bratty and he thinks he can do this without the help of his mentor Calvin Carlton!" AUGUST: "You realize that NONE of this matters, right? Aside from living in an existentialist nightmare of a capitalist society, that Dan Ryan’s in the picture? The WINDHAM CLAN’S DAN RYAN? I doubt he’ll even need FIVE MINUTES tonight against that smug tool of the corporations Impulse – and his bad taste in industrial metal – and then he won’t need even TWO minutes against whoever it is he takes on next!" O’CONNOR: "So, folks, it’s almost time to—" (CUE UP: The opening synth chords of "Blue Monday" by New Order as the Titantron screen spells out W-I-N-D-H-A-M C-L-A-N in Texas Flag colors. The crowd IMMEDIATELY starts to boo. And pelt garbage as out walk the intense Dan Ryan, a surly Bandit, a laughing Peter Windham, a catatonic Christian Cruise, a praying Bobby Jack Windham, JJ DeVille holding his stack of papers and Troy Windham, holding a microphone and wearing his trademark $500 sunglasses. They pause at the top of the ramp and then, in unison, begin to walk to the ring ignoring the catcalls and trash!) AUGUST: "Give it up! Just give it up! WE ARE IN CHARGE!" H’WOOD: "Sit down. Your skinny jeans are ripping." TROY: "Thank you SO much for showing your southern hospitality! We are SO happy to be here in South Carolina, a state known for… well… known for its dire poverty and third-world literacy levels! It truly is a land where all children have been left behind! But that’s neither here nor there! Because we here in The Windham Clan have a SPECIAL announcement! You see, our CO-president Eddie Mayfield and his little helper bee Calvin Carlton thought it FUNNY to take command of our spiritual homeland, THE WINDHAM RANCH, built on the ranchland that made up MY CHILDHOOD HOME! And while I have property all over the world… none here in South Carolina, since I don’t have a need in my real estate portfolio to own a run-down trailer… it still TROUBLES ME to know that the fields that I grew up running throughout and the bedroom where I, at the age of ten, lost my virginity to the captain of the cheerleading squad –" AUGUST: "A true fact!" TROY: "Has been turned into a tacky spa put together by the Carlton Estates. Well, you’re all going to learn that you JUST CAN’T DO THAT!" JJ: "Allow me to introduce myself. My name is JJ DEVILLE. And I, unlike all of you here tonight, have a COLLEGE EDUCATION. I graduated from NORTH CAROLINA STATE – (a lot of boos at that) UNIVERSITY! A NATIONALLY RANKED RESEARCH INSTITUTION where I ranked as THE BIG MAN ON CAMPUS! And I, as part of my search for both knowledge and understanding, took ONE PRE-LAW CLASS. And it is to my understanding that Eddie and Calvin TRANSFORMED THE BELOVED WINDHAM RANCH into a commercial real estate property without going through the Sweetwater Planning Board OR Zoning Board of Appeals! And you can’t just do that!" TROY: "So, Eddie… Calvin. GET OUT HERE NOW. And, in fact, get the ENTIRE ROSTER OUT HERE! EVERYONE IN THE BACK! GET OUT HERE NOW! THIS IS YOUR BOSS SPEAKING!" (CUT TO: The top of the stage as PRESIDENT MAYFIELD makes his way out first, cigarette in mouth, followed by CALVIN CARLTON. MAYFIELD’s wearing a charcoal grey suit and CARLTON’s wearing his signature LA LAKERS royal purple/gold suit.) MAYFIELD: "Everyone…stay cool." (Slowly, everyone comes out – Impulse, Castor, the HWC, Jack Bryant, Legion and Luci4, Stalker, Eric Dane, P. King Duk, Calvin Carlton, Dorchester Stratton, High Flyer, and a few others not visible behind the crowd. Those that are feuding are keeping conspicuous distances from each other, security surrounding everyone as well.) MAYFIELD: "So, what’s this about, Troy? And keep in mind – you’re grossly outnumbered. And there are some OTHERS floating around here… ohhh, I don’t know WHERE they are and I don’t know WHO they could possibly be this time. But you’ve always enjoyed the spotlight and you’ve got a LOT of eyes on you right now." TROY: "That’s fine, Eddie, that you’ve got some secret reserves in the back. I’d line up some backup to if I were you, considering that the people on the stage with you are nothing more than a bunch of curtain jerkers who are one step away from moving my furniture for a living! And that gets us to our NEXT point." JJ: "You see, Eddie and Calvin BROKE THE LAW. And me and our attorneys are certain that EACH AND EVERY SINGLE ONE OF YOU KNEW ABOUT THIS IN ADVANCE! Just as you did when we were unjustly attacked and I had black mist – NOT GREEN, BUT BLACK – sprayed in my face by that cheap shot artist TSUNAMI… IF THAT IS INDEED HIS REAL NAME! And because of that, all of you have been SUBPOENAED TO GO AND TESTIFY IN SWEETWATER ABOUT THIS!" (Ryan lets out a chuckle.) JJ: "You all IGNORED the court orders which puts YOU IN CONTEMPT OF COURT. So, guys, come on out, now!" (Out come about two dozen South Carolina State Troopers.) JJ: "You guys have a choice. You either GET ARRESTED en masse on the spot! Or—" (The camera cuts to a bus in the parking lot that says FUNG WAH EXPRESS BUS with a bunch of Chinese letters. The thing looks like it’s ready to crumble in fire.) JJ: "Or you get on that bus that we kindly rented for you, which will drive you to Sweetwater, where you will testify before a court. And NONE OF IT WILL GO DOWN ON YOUR PERMANENT RECORD!" (Jack Bryan is immediately telling a cop to arrest him. MAYFIELD tells him to stop.) TROY: "It’s YOUR choice. And we’ll give you a few seconds to mull it over. Doo doo do doo doo dooo ddooo doo." (Troy continues to hum the Jeopardy theme song into the microphone. MAYFIELD looks around at his roster, then the crowd as they’re getting REAL LOUD. All of a sudden, a slow smile forms on the PRESIDENT’S face and he reels in CARLTON, while pulling out his cell phone. MAYFIELD hits a button, while talking to CARLTON who’s eyes start widening quickly. CARLTON starts nodding negatively as MAYFIELD clicks off, while briskly walking out from underneath the FRONTIERtron are FIONA LOVE and two other business-suited men. They walk through the roster, while the crowd’s antsy buzzing intensifies.) TROY: "Time’s UP! What’s it going to be?" (MAYFIELD turns around his smile now gone, telling CARLTON to relax…even though CAL’s screaming "THIS IS A BAD IDEA!") MAYFIELD: "Before I answer…I just want to hear clearly and VERBALLY that this lawsuit, subpoena and litigation is being directed by DEVILLE and not the WINDHAM CLAN, that he is the lead plaintiff of a class action suit against NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING and somehow a f(BLEEP!)king prosecutor for SWEETWATER, TEXAS and the Windham Ranch deed owners." TROY: "Uhhh…" H’WOOD: (V/O) "I…I…I didn’t know he had that in him." O’CONNOR: (V/O) "I swear to Mary Mother these Windhams are trying to turn us into LAW & ORDER!" AUGUST: (V/O) "You mean JUSTICE & FAIR WAGES, Jelly Bean Bandits!" (CUTTO: DEVILLE cutting into the picture…) JJ: (snatching the mic that remains in TROY’S hand) "You’re goddamn right this is MY lawsuit! You failed to ignore the commercial zoning regulations, YOU’RE GONNA BE PAYING US TILL YOU’RE SIX FEET DEEP! GET ON THE BUS! ALL OF YOU!" (MAYFIELD nods and looks back at LOVE and the business-suited men. LOVE gives the thumb’s up.) MAYFIELD: "Alright, boys…MY legal advice team are advising me… (slightly smiles) to AGREE WITH YOUR TERMS!" O’CONNOR/H’WOOD: (V/O) "WHAT!?" (CUTTO: The crowd’s jaws dropping for one millisecond before immediately launching into a cacophonous BOOS! CUTTO: TROY WINDHAM looking surprised, while JJ DEVILLE launches his hands up in victory! BOBBY JACK screams out "HALLELUJAH!") MAYFIELD: "I hope you realize what you did JJ, I really hope you realize what you just did…" JJ: "Whatever, Eddie. You and your boys are going to have to get on that Chinatown bus and drive overnight!" (PRESIDENT MAYFIELD turns towards the roster, holding his hands up and his palms facing them. A moment of mature pleading to commence…) MAYFIELD: (over LOUD boos! "PRESIDENT DENNIS IS A DICK!") "We ARE going to Texas and we are going to jail. And I am POSITIVE that some of you MAY have warrants (long, smiling, thoughtful pause.) Especially at that bar where some of us took Katherine to tackle the most buckingist bucking mechanical bronco in Texas. F(BLEEP!)CK – I think I’ve got some loose warrants I never took care of. And I’m also sure that one of you WILL TRY TO SHANK SOMEBODY. DO NOT DO THIS. WE ALL ENTER THE BUS WITH EMPTY POCKETS, Y’HEAR ME?" (MAYFIELD pauses to get that last point across… CUTTO: STALKER shaking his head disappointedly. CUTTO: LEGION and LUCI4 whistling, while depositing some sharp objects into their mouths. CUTTO: Back to MAYFIELD…) MAYFIELD: (over LOUD boos! "YOU SELLOUT!") "WHATEVER you do, DO NOT TALK. I DO THE TALKING. Nobody says NOTHING until the lawyers standing next to FEE right here, TALK TO YOU. And as far as I’m concerned, JJ isn’t going to get past me since he’s going up against New York City’s finest…" JJ: "F(BLEEP!)CK YOU!" MAYFIELD: "You all gotta trust me… (EDDIE turns back and looks out to the crowd) YOU ALL GOTTA TRUST ME TOO! WE WIN…EVERYONE LEGALLY ATTENDING MEMBER OF THIS SHOW WINS." (CUTTO: The crowd still hasn’t let up the booing…some throw trash at Eddie!) MAYFIELD: (turning back to the roster) "Calvin will take care of the trip back—" CALVIN: "I will?" JJ: "GO! GO! Just leave your stuff! There isn’t any room!" You and your boys are going to be in a WORLD OF HURT." (The entire roster is staring at The Windham Clan grimacing. Peter Windham starts singing "Na nan a na nanananana hey hey hey goodbye!") O’CONNOR: "I can’t believe this! The Windham Clan just shut down the show!" H’WOOD: "This is unreal. UNREAL. I’ve never seen anything like this. That punk JJ… he just loves all these little power trips—" AUGUST: "Oh, Kerry, Lamont… JJ’s got something to say to you!" JJ: "I meant EVERYONE! OLD MAN HOLLYWOOD! SH(BLEEP!)TBEANS! GET UP! GET UP AND GET ON THAT BUS! YOU TOO LEE BABY! I DON’T CARE ABOUT YOUR PEDOPHILE CHARGES! EVERYONE GET ON THAT BUS!" AUGUST: "(Laughing uncontrollably.)" H’WOOD: "I… I am going to enjoy it SO, SO, SO incredibly much when these guys die." (They both stand up and reluctantly go to the back. CUTTO: TROY WINDHAM clapping and motioning to the audience with wide gestures…) TROY: "Alright, that’s our show for tonight! If you’re wearing a WINDHAM CLAN T-Shirt or other related merchandise, you are able to receive a FREE REFUND! But as far as everyone else… I’m not sure what we can do for you! After all, times are a little tough!" (The crowd just starts pounding with garbage. The Windham Clan is cackling with laughter as the crowd is booing. Someone tries to hop the ramp but security gets to him first. FADETOBLACK…) …are you still there? …waiting? …it couldn’t be real… (FADEIN: BLAINE HOLLYWOOD stepping onto the bus, his nose bristling as he elbows away a STATE TROOPER. "GET YOUR HANDS OFF ME!" The trooper pushes him forward as BLAINE’s apparently the last to be seated. We can hear CALVIN shouting from outside the bus, "I’LL FIX THIS BLAINE! I SWEAR TO GOD, I’LL FIX YOU!" CUTTO: BLAINE walking past IMPULSE in the front sitting next to KERRY O’CONNOR. LAMONT HOLLYWOOD has been forced to sit with LEE-BABY. BLAINE keeps walking past JACK HARMEN and P. KING DUK…there are two open seats. One next to DORCHESTER STRATTON and one next to MISTER MYERS. BLAINE looks side to side and sits next to MYERS, STRATTON turns his head while rolling his eyes.) BLAINE: (sniffing around) "Jesus man, you smell like the taint of a 60-year old bull’s dick" JACK BRYANT V/O: "You’re lucky!" (CUTTO: BRYANT sitting next to LUCI4.) BRYANT: "This one smells like the cow’s twat he humped." (LUCI4 spits out a paper clip necklace with razor blades.) BRYANT (gagging) "Jesus sister, I don’t need t’see how Legion gets off." (FADETOBLACK as PRESIDENT MAYFIELD boards the bus, CARLTON wailing in the background.) ...but it was. Credits JJ DEVILLE (lead plaintiff) vs. NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING (defendant) - Written by The Cabal
