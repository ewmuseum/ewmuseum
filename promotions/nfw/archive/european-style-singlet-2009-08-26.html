I can picture Wildstar in a singlet, just not the leotard-style. :cool: GreggG — 26 August 2009 For those interested in WildStar's history: 1) He was created by Chad and Steve a long time ago. He was Hornet's first protege. They used him on-and-off back in the early days on *P. He was a guy who'd have one of their lesser titles. Sometimes some random RPer would use him for a while.
2) His most prominent role in that time period was teaming with a character named HEAT in a team called WildFire. They won the CSWA straps (I think).
3) The dude who RP'd HEAT left, so WildStar was a free agent and was used like a NPC guy today. During the same period, Tsunami became the CSWA World Champion. He lost the title in one of their "Tournament of Champion" things and I was flailing with him. I always loved the name WildStar (sounds like he'd be in Guardians of the Galaxy or a lesser-tier Marvel team comic book) and picked him up.
4) 'Nami and Star were first called The Suicide Squad. They became the CSWA's most prominent tag team of that time period. They were in every league on *P and fared well depending on what promotion they were in.
5) After a while, Steve convinced me to change their name to NC-17 as an ode to the amazingly trashy PG-13. I did that, but their tagline was "for adult audiences only" and their gimmick was that they weren't wrestlers for a kid's show and envisioned them like Benoit/Malenko in ECW, only with 'Nami more like Sabu.
6) I came back after a long hiatus and re-dubbed them "Dawn of a New Day." They were like The Eliminators -- WildStar as the ultra-serious mouthpiece. In retrospect, not my best work ever. They also feuded with JN's DRS team. JN, naturally, killed me.
7) In JN's league, I wanted to try something different. There was match for the tag belts on a card. But, before hand, there was a thing for the best aerialist wrestlers in the world. Tsunami entered last second and tore his ACL. So, 'Star went solo in the tag match, held his own, but succumbed at the end.
8) After that, I turned him heel and made him a RVD clone. He was the "Five Star Wrestler" like how RVD was "Mr. Monday Night." He got over big-time like that.
9) When NFW debuted, 'Star was in the promotion. I used him a little like RVD but with a lot more later-period Mr. Perfect. He was a wrestler who also brought in with him The Oblivion Express, who were a Doc/Gordy rip-off tag team and part of Five Star Enterprises. The OE went by the wayside.
10) 'Star also brought Calvin Carlton on board as his manager. 'Star was feuding with Alex Wylde, who picked on Cal a lot. So Cal came on board and turned Five Star Enterprises into Calvin Carlton Enterprises. Cal soon brought in The Original ShowStoppers to feud with The Showstoppers.
11) Over time, I turned 'Star face and he had "differences" with Cal. His face shtick was like face Mr. Perfect (think feud w/ Flair) mixed in with Kevin Costner's character from Bull Durham.
12) Now you've all seen him since the DC/Nami/HWC stuff began. He's been an announcer for the Sirius Radio shows. We threw that in there since we knew where this was all headed a long time ago to set it up.
EastPrez — 26 August 2009 Also, DC Stratton was a guy I made eons ago that bounced around alot of indy Feds. I created him as a untrained daredevil who was a backyard wrestling phenom. In his debut league, he actually won some sort of "tough enough" contest an won a contract, so he was wild and reckless and sloppy, but did amazing aerial stuff. His nickname was the "new king of the backyards" and was modelled after Billy Kidman with Robert smith's haircut and mikey whipwrecks wardrobe. When be hooked up with Tsunami, they became the Suicide Kings, in honor of their pasts, along with their reckless ring style. One of the best tagteam names I ever dreamed up.
