Crash 46 and Knox segmentry.

12 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
renner — 12 August 2008 While I'm trying not to work on my website (going back to PHP scripts you haven't tinkered with in months is tough going, I gotta say), I might as well bring this up.
I'm planning to do an Elite Championship victory speech for Crash 46, possibly to open the show (up to Katz on that one). It'll have huge fanfare, because Craig Miles will likely be exceedingly glad that an NFW guy was able to win the championship. I'm talking an entrance almost worthy of those seen in Colossus V (although I don't think Steve is getting shot out of a cannon).
It'll start with a speech by Steve, but then he starts getting interrupted by many roster members who all want a crack at the championship.
While anybody can join in on this eventually monsterous segment (Chris, remember that segment over in PRIME where Nova threw his coming back party? This is going to be like that,), the following are some key people I'd like to have:
- Nova - Joe The Plumber - Felix Red - Cameron Cruise - Brock Alyas - James von Doom (oh, and leave the Koopa at home, James) - Legion w/ Luci4 Obviously, there can, will, and SHOULD be more than just the above seven. I probably will handle most of this on my own, otherwise I'd have to co-write this whole thing with a LOT of handlers, and I feel I'm pretty familiar with most of you by now.
The flow of the segment is pretty simple. Steve comes out to his huge fanfare, and starts making his speech. Midway into it, the first interruption (either Nova or Alyas) starts, then we get some banter. Second interruption (whoever it wasn't in the first one), more banter. Then JTP, more banter. Then a bunch of other guys, now coming in so quickly that we don't even have time for banter any more. Obviously, a huge brawl needs to erupt from this, but nobody should die. Too terribly, anyway.
...Well. Maybe von Doom. Anyway, if you're in, you're in. Let me know if you're in. Katz, if you don't want me to do this, just let me know. It's going to be a segment of ridiculous length, after all. XD
