Off to Valinor — 19 October 2004 Hello all, Just wanted to update you on what's going on with the playoffs, why the RP cycles are a little (a little!?!?) off.
The Wild Card round basically has about 5 more pages to go, as I've decided to try and condense alot of the UWA/NFW/expansion prologue material into the card.
Why? Well all the matches were obvious decisions due to the real lives of some folks competing. Unfortunate, but what can you do?
Well, seeing as how the remaining round should have 100% attendance - I thought it'd be better to get all the UWA/NFW stuff out of the way before we can focus on our conference semifinalists and the run to Wrestlebowl.
Seeing as how the North RP is already progressing nicely, if anyone wants to request a deadline before the 28th, I'm fine with that. The South, I imagine wants the full time to the 31st when I am looking to post that card.
The Wild Card round, I figured could be posted on the 24th, even though I'll probably have it finished sooner, once I get some edits back.
After the 31st, I'm going to put up a Wrestlebowl note. The fact is that not everyone will be on the card. With the title match requiring alot of time as well as some storyline things, I don't want to have 10 matches that would take me 6 months to write.
So unless there's a plethora of matchwriting offers, and not short-form/recap style - the real deal, PPV style...I'm probably going to limit the card to around 5-7 matches. Two definite matches will be the Ultratitle Final and Eddy Love vs. Deacon.
Anyway, all that can be discussed in a few weeks, obviously. Any questions, PM or email me. SUMMARY of what the hell I just said: WILD CARD: Oct 24 (85% complete, all matches booked) DIVISIONAL PLAYOFFS: Oct 31 WRESTLEBOWL: Start thinking, limited spots on the show Off to Valinor — 27 October 2004 Seeing as how nobody in the South has RP'ed for whatever reason, everything is once again getting pushed back. This includes all deadlines.
In fact, you won't get a definite one until I see RP from the South that's sufficient.
