Good job, guys. GreggG — 1 June 2011 Some thoughts: 1) I really liked the JJ/Impulse stuff a lot. It was pretty much exactly how to book that. When I wrote the SuperCrash match, my intent was to have Impulse and Castor come off super strong with Ryan and Dorchester shockingly chasing them a bit. Impulse and Castor have had their own epic feud for a while and they have to get moved up to the World Title picture -- they have to get moved up somehow. Dorchester's been in the hunt for the title for a few cards now and he's done enough dastardly **** that he's really believable as a champion. And Dan Ryan is one of the handful of guys in the history of fantasy wrestling you can just hand a title to w/o any reason and it will work.
JJ's my favorite of the new Windham Clan guys so far. I've really hit a good stride w/ his character -- I was having a hard time getting it down and the last two RPs I did with him before SuperCrash and now after have really nailed him down as a whiny crybaby who, now that he has an ounce of power, is just being a total douche by making people jump through these needless hoops just for the satisfaction of fulfilling some pathetic need for revenge.
The 50/50 booking -- Impulse looked like he was going to win but JJ found a way to worm out even with the advantage of a referee in his back pocket -- worked pretty great. Impulse needs the momentum headed into the semifinals; JJ needs to get over the top as the Windham Clan member you most want to punch in the face. Troy and Dan Ryan are completely respectable -- you might hate them, but they're legends. August and Peter are lackeys. Bandit's a mercenary. Bobby Jack's pretty contemtible but I don't have him clicking as well as I did in NLW. But JJ? He's talking SO much crap about how good he is and then doing everything he can to duck out of having to prove it. Is he as good as he says? That's one of the big narratives I'm going to do w/ JJ.
2) I really love Jack Bryant and think that, depending upon how fast we go and how things break, that character's going to be at a Joe the Plumber/Mikey Manson/Shane Southern level.
3) The Cruise stuff is difficult. The concept of the character and heel turn is all on me. Like I said before -- the angle was telegraphed but that's not a bad thing. Everyone knew CM Punk was going heel way before he became a heel. But since this is Fantasy Wrestling, it's really hard to book something over several cards since there are so many variables and things can just slow down for whatever reason. So I kind of used the timeframe of like when R-Truth turned -- he came in as a face, John Morrison screwed with his head, at the end of the match he was now heel.
[name withheld] had the idea of Cruise joining The Windham Clan. I said no at first because I didn't see how that could possibly work. But then I read his note about Cruise feeling by himself. That was the best RP [name withheld]'s ever done, IMO, and was a great jumping off point to reinvent the character. He needed a huge jumpstart to get back going into the flow and I think the direction we have works out.
I just really hope Tom RUNS WITH THIS. This could be a very hot character change and direction.
jediPREZ — 1 June 2011 I think something that's interesting in this game in general in comparison to the 'sports entertainment' we're trying to emulate -- is how much deeper in the shadows we're willing to go to put over a face or heel turn.
If we think back to the 80's or look at wrestling today on television -- I'd say 90-95% of turns are transparently foreshadowed in your face that you roll your eyes about it, while in our game we really try to hide it 90-95% of the time over several to dozens of shows.
Cruise's turn on this show may be the first time in a long time in NFW that we just ripped off the band-aid in front of the cameras, so to speak...and it's made for some good discussion!
NWA/WCW had a few good ones that just came out of nowhere -- Nikita on Lex over the US Title, Barry Windham on Lex in the tag title match to join the Horsemen...now that I think about it...Lex Luger was one hell of a patsy!
