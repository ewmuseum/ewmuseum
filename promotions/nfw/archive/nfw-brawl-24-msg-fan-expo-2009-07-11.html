NFW Brawl 24: MSG Fan Expo, 11 July 2009

11 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 24: MSG Fan Expo 11 Jul 2009 Madison Square Garden, New York City, New York (seats 20,000) Welcome to the Show! The summer begins and with its coronation comes the NFW FAN EXPO, a weekend long jaunt in New York City that announced the NFW Grand Prix last year. Most summer EXPO’s give the fans a real treat of an announcement, but his one sold out like hotcakes upon the live broadcast of CRASH 49. Any true NFWite, any mountain high FrontierHead…they knew the main event for CRASH 50 was going to be announced for the weekend’s finale. Everyone from Adler to Zane was on the tips of thousands upon thousands of tongues that arrived in the Big Apple for this moment. Madison Square Garden. The most anticipated match announcement of the year. Only in NFW. Oh yeah, the best part for me? I get paid to type stuff about it all. XM/SIRIUS CHANNEL 240, I give thee life! "Dead Leaves and the Dirty Ground" by the White Stripes blasted as M. Wayne Grossard, Sean Toombs and Calamity took their usual roles for the show. Grossard thanked President Mayfield for the wonderful atmosphere and chance to work in a world class organization, Toombs thanked President Mayfield for not conducting wellness tests for himself and Calamity, while Calamity said he’d gleefully chest bump President Mayfield for the decision to uphold the $2, 22 oz. beverages that are served at the Expo. I guess that’s par for the course. Word had it that even people who hated wrestling were clamoring to get in the event due to the drastic price difference from the New York City. With the fans piled into MSG, the night was set and the action was started off with a couple of debuts. American Panda vs. P. King Duk NFW DEBUT SHOWCASE The next bout was a tale of two very different men and two very different impressions. P. King Duk was first out and, in all his ferocity, carried a kendo stick. Out second, with "2001: A Space Odyssey" in the background, was a man wearing a mask ... panda ears on top. Before you start laughing, do realize this man, American Panda, stood six foot-six and weighed close to two hundred-eighty pounds. P. King Duk was aggressive and on the prowl, but his speed was no match for the Panda's power. Forearm shivers, European uppercuts, and a massive big boot took the wind out of the feisty Jap's sails. After ducking a clothesline that would have surely beheaded him, P. King Duk landed an oddly familiar kick combo, which he finished off with a spinning back kick. Only, not so much, as the giant American Panda (who was unfazed) dropped a massive forearm across Duk's back. Another Irish whip put Duk in a bad spot, the Gorilla Press or we'll call it the Panda Press. AP strutted around the ring, showing off his tremendous strength, and punctuated the statement by dropping Duk on his face. One Jugular Chomp (not literally) later and American Panda collected the elementary 1-2-3. Rich Mahogany vs. Julius Scissors NFW DEBUT SHOWCASE Julius Scissors sported possibly the worst haircut in Garden history. His opponent, Rich Mahogany, hip thrusted and gyrated to the theme "Love Man" by Otis Redding his entire walk to the ring. Mahogany got the jump on Scissors at the bell with a dropkick, which sent Julius through the ropes to the outside. Mahogany stayed on the prowl, springboarding and corkscrewing to the outside, and hit the Springboard Bitchslap on the defenseless barber. After a few vulgar words for a young female fan and a customary crotch thrusting, Mahogany rolled Scissors back into the ring. Mahogany, the Austin native, impressed the Garden crowd with a series of technical holds mixed with unnecessary cheating (finger pokes of doom). However, a sneaky kick to the groin followed by the Sex-Plex (Bridging Fisherman's Suplex) would be the curtain call for Scissors, the hard-luck loser and winner of "Worst Haircut in NFW History". Steve Knox vs. Legion Creepy music. Creepy lightshow. The Devil’s Rejects walking out. A horse jumping into the crowd and robbing someone at knife-point. One of those things didn’t happen. The lightshow wasn’t that creepy. I kid, I kid…oh how I wish I didn’t have to. Luci4 and Legion awaited their grudge match opponent in Steve Knox, who had cemented himself as a top contender in New Frontier Wrestling…only to take a break and compete in an All-Star Canadian promotion. Bret Kelly would be proud of his career choices. Knox looked all business in the ring, but kept a slight smirk on as Legion immediately charged. Chaos Rules in effect for the whole match, meaning No DQ’s…no countouts and knowing Legion…no penalties for using a butcher’s knife in a murderous fashion. The smile soon faded as Knox found himself under a lightning barrage of fists, kicks and spinning backfist attempts. Knox got cornered, which allowed Legion to fly in with a knee. Knox reversed an Irish Whip caught Legion with an Overhead Belly to Belly. The match pretty much played out as the first frantic seconds. Legion pushing a frenetic pace that Knox had to offset with a thesaurus of suplexes and strikes. Both attacked each other’s head/neck area, trying to set up for their finishers. Luci4 started proving to be quite the difference later on as she aided Legion with attacks outside the ring, which was allowed under his rules. Now, I know Legion is crazy…but could Knox be considered crazier for setting himself up like this? Of course not in NFW because the universe needs to balance itself out. Strangely enough in ours, it means that Felix Red is going to become an interested third-party citizen as well. You see Steve Knox and Felix Red have a very MUTUAL and IMPORTANT wrestling match on Crash Television’s 50th episode. Something about a WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP PENTAGRAM MATCH. Do the CAPS help? Just wondering. This match itself came to a conclusion as Felix Red indulged himself in a little Chaos, while Luci4 was on the turnbuckles about to pounce on Steve Knox…who laid draped across Legion…although Legion had hit a DDT. Luci4 prepared to leap, but had her leg pulled out by Felix. Bad landing wouldn’t begin to describe what happened to Luci, but I can’t exactly describe what Calamity described either. Of course, M. Wayne Grossard didn’t know who he should be rooting for right now since he disapproved of everyone in the ring. He then thanked President Mayfield for finding a way to keep him impartial against his own wishes, so he could become a better announcer than Kerry O’Connor. Legion stood up wobbily as Felix Red tried to remain in his eyeline, a beaming smirk on his face. When Legion’s cobwebs cleared, he saw Luci4 and then Felix…realized what must have happened…and then lunged towards Felix, who dodged out of the ring. Legion was giving him a mouthful and Steve Knox slowly rose to his feet, which Legion noticed. Legion whirled around and ran off the ropes missed a clothesline, then Knox took off towards him on the rebound… GOLD RUSH! AXE BOMBAHHHHHHHH! ONE! TWO! …THREE. Felix Red smiled as he backpedaled away as Steve Knox stood up and saw the carnage around him, then Felix Red on the FRONTIERtron…as well as a replay of how the match ended. To say the least, his AWESOME smirk wasn’t in effect in post-match glory...but it could’ve been worse…he could’ve lost. Kelly Masters vs. Brock Alyas Roaring out from the back was Brock Alyas, who appeared to be directed by Mayfield's hand at Crash 49 in the Target Center. The Ninth Mile, somewhere in his ranting and raving, specifically asked for Felix Red to join him in the ring (and not for a tea party). Not surprising that the Detroit native was looking for a brawl and, by God, his wish was finally answered. Except, there was no Felix Red. Instead, "Indestructible" by Disturbed played as NFW newcomer, Kelly "Machine" Masters stepped out from behind the black curtain. "I don't know where Felix Red is and I don't really care. What I do know is that you're standin' inside a wrestlin' ring and you're asking for a match. Just so happens I came to the Garden lookin' for a match myself. So how about it, Alyas? You an' me, right now." Alyas invited (Matrix style) Masters to the ring and the rabid fans, who were enjoyed the Fan Expo, exploded in cheers. As soon as the Wisconsin native slid into the ring, he was pounced on by Alyas.. Thick, powerful forearm shots kept Masters grounded and kicks to the ribs furthered Alyas' cause. Finally, he pulled the Machine up by his blonde locks and sent him back down with a 90 degree turn belly-to-belly. The cover barely got a two and Alyas punished the newcomer with a series of shit stomping boots to the head-neck area. After Irish whipping Masters, Alyas landed a purdy back-elbow that dropped Masters like a ton of bricks. The Ninth Mile yanked Masters back to his feet, nailed a few sharp jabs, and went for the KO right cross. However, Masters didn't stand around to see what would happen. He'd lowered his level and flipped Alyas over with a fireman's carry. A cross armbreaker led to a rope break, which Alyas used to his advantage, regaining the upperhand with a knee to Masters' midsection. Masters would block another attempt for a belly-to-belly and sent Alyas head over heels with one of his own. Stomps to the neck only temporarily sidetracked Alyas, who pushed to his feet, but found himself in a wrenching front headlock. The Detroit native was able to spin under the front headlock and Masters matched his technical display with a reversal of his own. A hammerlock backdrop followed and scored a two count. Masters took Alyas down with an Irish whip and the kitchen sink then immediately slapped on a seated chinlock. Alyas was nowhere near tapping and Masters dropped rabid forearm strikes across the back of his bigger opponent's neck. The tide would change again as Alyas utilized a clinch to land stiff knee strikes to Masters spleen, kidneys, and midsection. His clothesline attempt landed and nearly beheaded Masters. Another pinfall earned a long two count. Bringing Masters back to his feet proved a tough task, as the superb technician rolled Alyas into an inside cradle for the count of three ... actually, just two as Alyas exploded out of the pinfall attempt. Speaking of exploding, Alyas, who beat Masters to his feet, pulled the Machine up and set him up for the EXTRA MILE EXPLODAH! The attempt was thwarted by Masters, who managed to lock in a guillotine choke (with a body scissors) in the center of the ring. Alyas had absolutely no interested in tapping (does he even feel pain?) and decided he'd head toward the ropes. After a good half-minute to a minute of clawing and struggling, the Ninth Mile found himself in a familiar spot. He was blacked out ... the Detroit native never did tap, but courtesy of a body vice guillotine and three drops of his arm, he'd allowed Kelly Masters to pick up an impressive first victory in NFW. Masters, barely hanging around to get his arm raised, headed toward the back and even ignored the 22 OZ. (only two bucks at a concession stand near you!) that the New Yorkas offered. Dos Equis vs. High Flyer Vengeance is fleeting and perhaps Jack Harmen cound ensare it within his grasp. BatPulse still in the hospital, duct-taped and hooked up to enough house trees and roots to make a Mother Oak cry…it was on his mind, which is why Jack Harmen is also completely crazy. Did we mention, the "hospital" is in the East Wing bathroom? His opponent tonight would be Dos Equis, who was accompanied by his partner FutureQuis. It was Dos Equis that had pinned Flyer on Crash 49, it was Dos Equis who had driven BatPulse through a table and shattered him. (Did I just type him? Oh. My. God.) This was a grudge match of epic proportions and it showed as Flyer quickly rushed out of the ring, vaulting through middle ropes . Dos Equis dodged out of the way, FutureQuis watching his partner in disgust as Flyer quickly got up in an adrenalized mania and ran towards Dos. Dos took off in complete fright, knowing Flyer’s eyes reminded him of the time he hadn’t felt alcohol touch his lips for a whole 96 hours. Dos ran around the ring, his partner grabbing a hold of him by the hair and tights, then tossing him into the ring. Flyer quickly followed suit, brushing past FutureQuis as if he wasn’t there. Hard right hands rapid fired from Flyer into the skull of Dos who was trying to get out of his entrance gear. Irish Whip follow and a huge back body drop from Flyer. Flyer wailed and pounded on dos with a series of kicks, strikes, knees, gouges, hooks for the next several minutes. Finally, Dos countered a Powerbomb attempt into a hurricanrana! Flyer was a little loopy, but still in bullrush mode. Missed clothesline by Flyer, Back Suplex counter by Dos! Finally, both were down. Finally, Flyer seemed…subdued. Dos did a belly dance and a jumping splash. Controlling the next few minutes with his 12-pack aerial offense, Dos Equis had Flyer on the ropes…until he charged Flyer while he was there and got flipped out of the ring. Flyer went out and continued on the attack, but FutureQuis rushed over and caught him with a clothesline to the back of the head. The crowd started to boo FutureQuis, while the ref yelled at him. Dos slid out of the ring, grabbed Flyer and rammed him into the ring steps. Dos rolled back in the ring, while the ref and FQ continued arguing. Finally, Flyer was able to get up and made a move towards Future, but the ref stopped him. The ref pointed him back towards the ring, so Flyer begrudgingly climbed onto the apron…still jawing with FutureQuis as the referee rolled in the ring and started counting. Of course, Flyer was now distracted from the person he hated in the first place. Dos hit a dropkick to the unsuspecting Flyer, who teetered…and finally flew off the apron…right into a FutureQuis dropkick, while he was in mid-air. The crowd freaked out, the ref rolled out and started screaming he was going to ban FutureQuis from the ringside area. Flyer let out an angered yell, then…climbed under the ring. This of course, completely confused Dos Equis who started pacing around the ring like a dog trying to find his own tail. FutureQuis shoved the ref towards the apron, screaming at him that Flyer was under there. Instead…the ref rolled in the ring, making an "I have my eyes on you" motion towards FutureQuis. Flyer came out the other side… …well, he was bloodied. He was holding something. He was crying. There’s a good chance we now know where Nova went after getting fired. Flyer rolled back in, then rolled another rotation to avoid a double stomp! Flyer then caught Dos Equis with a low blow and attempted a small package. The ref slid in late only getting a two count, Flyer complained and got rolled up for another near pin. Both scrambled up, BEERSTEINER! …where Flyer stuffed a screwdriver…in the backseat of Dos Equis’ pants…and then allowed him to get pinned. You know how this one ends, right? Dos Equis jumped around like he knocked out Clubber Lang, while FutureQuis saw what happened and charged in the ring screaming…It was too late. A glint in the ref’s eyes had shone even before FQ tackled Dos to the mat. The ref dover in there for a FIGHT FOR THE FUMBLE! Which the ref won somehow…holding up the screwdriver, pointing to Flyer’s bloodied scalp… REVERSED DECISION. FLYER WINS BY DQ. Impulse (c) vs. Problem Child With the Madison Square Garden at new heights of excitement (and intoxication), it seemed to be time for the NFW Brawl MAIN EVENT. Who would it be? Joe the Plumber, Impulse .. maybe Doc Curiosity? The New Yorkers would be subject to one of their own, but it was not JTP or Impulse. No sir, instead the sounds of "Scentless Apprentice" by Nirvana accompanied Bronx native, Problem Child as he appeared from the back. Or at least, it was assumed to be PC. Problem Child was in rare attire - dreadlock wig, Dodgers uniform, baseball bat over his shoulder, and syringes protruding from his cutoff jean shorts. Nobody in NY was particularly fond of Problem Child, but his Manny impersonation drew rave reviews from the New Yorkers, who despised the former Red Sox slugger. However, the honeymoon would be shortlived. "As much as I love New York, I can see why the Dodgers left Brooklyn. You people disgust me! Honestly, why is it that all the welfare and rat fucks reside in New York?!? We had two of the greatest franchises in baseball history and YOU FOOLS drove one away! Now look at you! Bill Clinton was getting BJs in office the last time you won a World Series! AHAHAHA!" Cue "BOOOO!" And, let the beer tossing commence. Problem Child ducked one or two beers before finally be splattered rather cleanly by a third. He brushed the generic Miller product off his replica Dodgers jersey and jumped on the apron. "Real mature! I'll send you my cleaning bill!" he hollered, pointing in the general direction of the beer tosser. Seeking asylum, Problem Child finally slipped into the ring and turned up the heat. "Hmm ... now, where was I? OH RIGHT ... New York sucks! Which, by default, means all of you suck, too! It seems I'm the only decent thing New York has ever produced. It's me or a fat, disgusting plumber with more drug problems than Heath Ledger! Oh, too soon? Or wait, how that about goody-two-shoes Impulse?!? There's something to be proud about ... a real fuckin' Brownie Scout he is. Too bad that last week, he finally had to face some real talent. Did you see where that got him? HE TAPPED OUT! I think even saw him CRYING! WAAAH! WAAAH! They may call me a child, but he's the BIGGEST BABY IN NFW!" The Garden exploded in boos, drowning out Problem Child, who waited patiently for the crowd to die down. Of course, mocking the fans and Impulse with fake crying didn't help him at all. Despite the ridiculous crowd noise, the faintest hints of "Revolution Baby" by Queen V. "LEFT! LEFT! LEFT RIGHT LEFT!" If PC thought the crowd was loud before, his ear drums were probably near being blown out as Impulse, microphone in hand, stepped out. Impulse, the New York native and target of the latter part of PC's tirade, was not the happiest of campers. He raised the microphone to his lips, started to speak, but stopped to allow the noise in the Garden to dissipate. "It's quite convenient that you fail to mention the constant cheating, the three-on-one attacks, and the fact you handcuffed my tag team partner to the steel guardrail. I did tap out, but there was nothing clean or honorable about that victory. I plan on dealing with Castor Strife, sooner rather than later, but how about, tonight, you and I settle up? How about for THE NFW TELEVISION TITLE!" The Garden's roof damn near blew off. Inside the ring, Problem Child hesitated for a moment and then accepted the challenge. New York was going to get a MAIN EVENT and, even better, it was a Television Title match. Impulse sprinted down to the ring, slid in, and immediately started exchanging blows with Problem Child. DING! DING! DING! PC, the better brawler, gained the advantage against the smaller Impulse and finally put him down with a discus lariat. Problem Child jumped on top of the Marathon Man and rained down rights, lefts, and forearm shots. PC ripped Impulse to his feet, whipped him into the turnbuckles, and delivered a crushing clothesline. After working over Impulse's body with "punches in bunches", he whipped Pulse into the opposite turnbuckle. A corner splash was on his mind, but Impulse ducked out of the way at the last minute. PC's chest collided with the turnbuckle and, as he turned around, he was met with a spinning heel kick from the Marathon Man. It was a dominant display of mat wrestling holds and high-flying impacts from that point on as Impulse showed why he had won the Sears Tower match. A snapmare followed by a dropkick to the seated PC allowed the opportunity for the first pinfall of the bout. One! Two! NO-HO-HO! Impulse was immediately back on the attack, persistently working on Problem Child's neck with a wide variety of elementary and innovative holds alike. An Irish whip was reversed by PC, and on his return, Impulse was drilled with a springboard DDT. PC immediately headed toward the turnbuckles, where he waited for Impulse to get back to his feet. It was like for the STDDT (leaping top rope DDT). Only, Impulse had a different plan in mind. SUDDEN IMPACT! Checkmate, game over, see you next week! One! Two! Three! No? Down the aisle and to ringside was none other than Starshooter, who yanked Impulse off of PC. The Marathon Man quickly jumped to his feet and exchanged words with Starshooter. Whatever was said prompted SS to hop onto the apron, but all he received for his willingness to fight was a SUDDEN IMPACT for himself! While the referee checked on Starshooter, who'd taken a nasty spill, Problem Child snuck up behind Impulse and incapacitated him with a nutcracker special (see: low blow). Seizing the opportunity, PC sent Impulse into the ropes and nailed a flying knee. One frog splash later and we had another pinfall. One! Two! Not today! PC cursed the Gods and told New York to "go somewhere else and die". With the pleasantries said, Problem Child brought Impulse to his feet and mounted him on his shoulder. Oh yes, time for A.D.D, the move that would end this match. Except, Impulse slid down PC's back, quickly turned, and hooked PC up for a neckbreaker. That's not ordinary flippin' neckbreaker! THE MESSAGE! Problem Child waved his arms, screamed bloody murder, and tried to pull away from Impulse, but it was all in vain. There would be no escaping the submission, not by natural means and PC gave in, tapping out just as a charging Castor V. Strife rushed to ringside searching for a weapon…. Castor looked around and found something that BRAINED Impulse. The Television Title! "BOOOOOO!" Strife paid little attention to the rowdy Garden crowd and instead ensued to French kiss the TV Title! Sharshooter slipped into the ring and he helped a woozy PC to his feet. Impulse was dead to the world, or so it seemed as Strife and company stood over him. If fans thought Crash 49 was bad, what the group had in store for a defenseless Impulse could be worse. That fear was quickly disposed of as the National Guard arrived! No, seriously. The NATIONAL Guard. Rook Black accompanied by twenty-some odd men, all wearing "National Guard" badges. They hit the ring simultaneously as the crowd roared their approval. Of course, Strife and co. quickly abandoned ship, but the damage was done. Rook eyed the three men, who quickly moved faraway from the ring. Turning his attention back to his partner, Rook Black pulled Impulse to his feet. The young Randall Knox, still woozy, suddenly became wide-eyed. C'mon, there were twenty freakin' men in the ring with Rook Black ... what would YOU be thinking? Surely, Impulse, with a foggy mind, was trying to figure out just WHO his tag partner was (rolling with a small army and all). As he stood with assistance from Black, Impulse stared down the aisle at his nemesis, Castor V. Strife. This was not over, not by a long shot. Presidents and Pentagrams The National Guard remained in the ring as RookPulse left to an ovation…mostly in deference to the hometown wrestling prodigy, Impulse. The lights died down as the FRONTIERtron displayed the NFW PRESIDENTIAL SEAL. "Hail to the Chief," ironically has become a natural sports entertainment vehicle to initiate an overabundant amount of booing from thousands upon thousands of fans. Does this speak to the viewpoint of government in America? Or does this speak to the NFWite population’s narrow-mindedness? PRESIDENT DENNIS EDWARD MAYFIELD walked calmly to the ring, noticeably absent from his side was the Eddie Mayfield Commemorative World Heavyweight Championship that is now stored somewhere and being studied by GREAT men. Mayfield was in suit and tie, walking with a slight limp and flanked by Cojones Mercado and Secret Service. With the amount of security at ringside that could only mean that Mayfield…was not taking any chances, which usually meant he knew Joe the Plumber was in the building, or even something more dangerous. …if that exists. A podium and microphone was set up, while the NFW CRIMSON STAR flag rolled out from the rafters, draping over one side of the ring. Mayfield addressed the microphone with a confident smile as his music died down. Over a loud raucous mix of boos and screaming, Mayfield finally took the floor. "We are nearing my FIRST YEAR in office. A lot of you may hate ol’ Hot Property for what he’s done, but you cannot deny that EDDIE MAYFIELD has SERVED THE PEOPLE of NFW." M. Wayne Grossard stood up standing and clapping. He might have been the only one in Madison Square Garden doing so, but Eddie Mayfield had the spotlight…something he rarely ever loses. "Before I came into office this federation was turning into an illogical haven for wrestlers better served to entertain hippie asylums than WRESTLING fans. Now, I know you NFW fans are freaks and geeks alike. I know some of you NFW fans think that Nova humveeing in a death race against Michael Manson on the mountains of Mars also qualifies for an entertaining Main Event, but you have to GROW UP and realize there’s a REAL WORLD around you." Mayfield laughed as the crowd loudly booed, "And that’s a market share that my college educated staff provided enough hour long Powerpoint Presentations to prove only a MONKEY wouldn’t understand! And let’s face it…half of you that made your mecca to Japan’s CRASH TV’s probably DON’T understand." HEY! THAT’S FELIX RED’S MUSIC! Ok, so nobody really said that, but it was blaring and the last remnant of the NFW EAST ELITE walked out from the entrance curtains to crowd roars. He wasn’t smiling, but he didn’t look angry. The relationship between Mayfield and Red had long been bitter and the first notion that the drugs weren’t going to work anymore. Red had outwrestled most of what Eddie threw at him every step of the way by this point…and now they had come full circle, only Felix’s confidence couldn’t overstate the champion he’d have to dethrone. Felix paced back and forth once in the ring, taking in security for a brief moment, but keeping his eyes on Mayfield. Mayfield seemed a little angered by the interruption, but not very surprised. Then, Felix pushed through a corner and sat himself on a top corner turnbuckle. Eddie grimaced and was about to continue his speech, when… HEY! THAT’S STEVE KNOX’S MUSIC! More roars and Eddie throwing his hands up in the air in disgust. He paced behind the podium as a wide-smiling Steve Knox strolled out and made his way to the ringside…auspiciously picking up a chair as he climbed in. He went to the opposite corner of Felix Red’s position…and took a seat. Knox then motioned for Mayfield to continue, which Eddie returned with a FU arm motion. The President gathered himself, "Unfortunately, you…my NFW citizens underestimate the difficulties in dealing with men that don’t understand RESPECT," Mayfield glared at Felix, "or INTELLIGENCE," Mayfield glared at Knox while the crowd started booing loudly. Mayfield put his hand up as if he was asking the crowd to slow down… "You see, NFW’s President doesn’t have to be liked ‘cause I sure as hell don’t like ANY of you," Mayfield pointed to the whole crowd getting a BOOMING BOO response, but kept going nonplussed. "I don’t like ANY person standing in the ring, right now." That killed the crowd response into a mixed reaction of cheers since the National Guard looked at each other shrugging, while COJONES looked around nervously with shifting eyes. "NFW needed a President that would remain UNBIASED, NFW needed a President that HATED everything that the locker room was trying to become. Starting with that sniveling kissass Calvin Carlton, to the vanilla milkshake Impulse, to the masked drunks, the mask obsessed freaks, to the addicts to the Doomtarded…" LOUD BOOS. Mayfield gritted his teeth. "…all the way to the NFW WORLD CHAMPION." LOUD CHEERS! "JOE! JOE! JOE!" bursted from the crowd. "Because while I can RESPECT that Joe the Plumber has stayed undefeated, as long as I have this position of POWER…I will HATE IT. I will FIGHT IT…and I will find ANY WAY to DEFEAT JOE. LOUD BOOS! "JOE! JOE! JOE!" continued… "The FUTURE of NFW is not in the hands of…of a MAN that represents the WORST in our society…of MY SPORT. The FUTURE…" DOGS BARKING! OH DEE BEE JOE THE PLUMBER stormed out of the entrance curtain to a standing ovation! The WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP draped loosely over his shoulder, JTP took no time getting to ringside in a stained wife beater, grimy pants and ripped apart tennis shoes. JOE climbed into the ring wild-eyed, stomping in a circle first making eye contact with Knox, then red…and finally settled in the middle of the ring, staring directly at President Mayfield glowering at him. Joe made a quick motion towards the President, nearly a dozen Secret Service closing in and COJONES cocking his chair back. JTP quickly caught on, knowing the odds in his deranged mind somehow. The champ paced back and forth, unable to stay in place…his head randomly twitching, his lips moving and meaning he was grumbling something or another. Mayfield pointed at JTP, "If I even hear an UGHN…" "UGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHN!" The crowd responded as JTP simply raised his belt in the air and lowered it. Mayfield remained unfazed, "If I hear even that out of you…if you even take one shot at me, you’re OUT. I’ll fix up the next contract so you’re out of the fed, WIN OR LOSE!" JTP snarled, but he didn’t bite as Mayfield smiled and kept his eye contact on the champion. "Joe…I know you’re thinking that you’re SPECIAL. I know you’re thinking that there’s NOBODY in the world that stands a chance. But I disagree, Joe…see, you’ve been pounding on this roster, but let’s face it…if the TWO in this ring are all that I have left against you…exactly WHAT are you?" The crowd booed loudly as Mayfield smirked, "You’re not the BEST. You’re not CHAMPION of the WORLD. You’re champion of NFW, Joe. You’re champion of the people for right now…but the world, Joe…I knew the WORLD of WRESTLING would step up to this challenge. I knew I could find two WRESTLERS that will expose the trash you inspire in that ring." MORE BOOS! Mayfield soaked them in as JTP returned a mouthful off-mic, while Knox, Red and the whole security started feeling tense. "See, I have a theory, Joe…" Eddie talked deeply with a twist of menace, "I think that you’re low-class FILTH for a reason…that at some point in life, you weren’t BORN into this greasy mold of stink…you were BEATEN into it. So, you’ve got resolve…but there are BIGGER DOGS than you in the BIG FIGHTS. Where MILLIONS are gonna train their eyes on you and your shortcomings." The crowd loudly booed as Mayfield mockingly flipped a note card for his speech, while JTP was doing everything in his power not to charge the President. Screaming at himself, slapping himself in the head… "You and NFW’s best WRESTLER and best FREAK are in the MAIN EVENT for CRASH 50, Joe…a four-hour LIVE, FREE TELEVISION event that will be viewed over MULTIPLE networks. The crowd’s boos started turning to slackjawed "WHAAAAAAAAAAAT!?" "Millions upon MILLIONS of eyes are going to be on you, Joe…because I am building the WRESTLING SHOW OF THE DECADE. CRASH 50: THE GREAT AMERICAN SMOKEOUT, four hours with FOUR title matches. A FREE BILLBOARD POSTER given to every fan with the lineup after the show, Joe…’cause after all, I’m a PRESIDENT of the PEOPLE…so much to the point, where I’m going to rock EVERYONE’S world right now by completing the puzzle pieces to the MAIN EVENT." The crowd was freaking out, loud as hell as JOE’s eyes went bug-eyed and he might have been salivating. Felix adjusted himself to ‘ready’ position, while Steve Knox stood up. Mayfield yelled at Secret Service for bottled water, taking a sip as the crowd started stomping and clapping. "Your 4TH challenger, Joe…quite simply, he’s here to answer a simple question that people usually DREAM about. And it will take place in the REAL GAHHHHHHHHHDEN! BOSTON, MASS! THE TD GAHHHDEN, HAHA!" New York City did not like that. Not at all. BLACKNESS. Time to ride ***** JUST BLAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAZE! "KING BACK" by T.I. …hey… …HEY… HEY! THAT’S…’TRIPLE-X’ SEAN STEVENS’ MUSIC! BOOM. The sonic blast of fans screaming reverberated across MSG as ‘XXX’ SEAN STEVENS stood under the FRONTIERtron. The surrounding mania drove JTP into a frenzy, wild-eyed and jumping on the ropes while STEVENS stood stoically on the ramp taking in the standing ovation with an unnerved expressionless demeanor. Looking all business in jeans, white tennis shoes and a black t-shirt with two ominous blue eyes infused with lightning. "TRIPLE! EX! TRIPLE! EX!" The crowd chanted loudly as Stevens walked down the ramp, but losing his fan luster a little bit as he made sure to avoid contact with the fans. Laser-focused and staring at the ring where JTP stood on the ropes, where he’d have to enter. Stevens smirked as security pulled back Joe, climbing up the ring steps and making a mock tip of the cap towards Felix Red, who didn’t seem thrilled about Stevens’ presence. Knox observed cautiously on the side, looking back and forth…in tune with the… "TRIPLE X!" "J-T-P!" Volleying chants from the crowd, while Mayfield lit up a Camel Cigarette and exhaled deeply. "Of course, Joe…to broadcast FOUR HOURS of FREE TV, to get contracts signed with MASN (boos!) …NESN (LOUD BOOS!) and the YES! Network (LOUD CHEERS!) to hit up FOX SPORTS Mountain, West, Northen, Eastern…to be in negotiations with ESPN2, but turn them down because they have too many demands…and not worry about it...you need a little more than just SEAN STEVENS." The crowd booed as JOE turned back to EDDIE, an interesting inquisitive look on his face. "We wouldn’t have hit a MILLION on PPV, Joe…not with Stevens, not with Shawn Hart…not with Max Danger…two names I know the SMARKS would love to JUICE into a sock upon hearing." LOUD BOOS! The Legacy and All-Star faithful recognized the names of their top dogs. "I’m the PRESIDENT, Joe...its my vision to put you under so many lights, so many eyes…that I’m going to let them WRESTLE EACH OTHER. A ONE-TIME, ALL-STAR LEGACY EXHIBITION match between MAX DANGER and SHAWN HART." "HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!" The boos turned FAST to the chants. Each and every fan looking at each other bug-eyed as two of the EWTORCH’s all-time heavyweights were locked, stocked and barreled to shoot at THE GREAT AMERICAN SMOKEOUT. "And that wasn’t enough, Joe…I wanted to make sure eyes that weren’t on this SPORT, were going to be on YOUR FILTH. I wanted the world to see how DEGRADING, how DISGUSTING you are." The crowd died down, they didn’t even know anymore…sounding like an overdosed opiate beehive one puff away from passing out in ecstasy. Eddie took a deep puff on his Camel Cigarette, smiling widely as Sean Stevens had himself flanked by security. He looked coolly at Mayfield, expressionless and impossible to read. There were questions that would come from his past, there would be questions that would come with his current championships. You got the sense he knew the ULTIMATE match would have to come with a personal price…that’s the way NFW was PROFESSIONALY run. Mayfield exhaled and looked at Steve Knox, "I needed to find a HALLMARK of Mid-South Traditional Wrestling." Mayfield inhaled and turned to Felix Red, "I needed a man that’s popped more pills than Lou Reed." He exhaled and turned to Stevens, "I needed man that…" Mayfield inhaled and smiled, then exhaled. "…could get under your skin…" Mayfield smiled too wide for Stevens’ trust, this was offsettling. "EXPLICITLY." Stevens heard the word booming from Mayfield’s mic…and deep inside, he knew. He knew the one man… "And you, Joe…" Mayfield locked eyes with JTP, who was heaving and frothing at the mouth. Their eyes remained deadlocked for seconds as the beehive had begun getting a little chaotic, loud screams, cameras flashing… "You are the NFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION. You were destined to face GREATNESS on this show, but I now know how to turn them ALL against you." The crowed booed, Mayfield took a long, surly inhale on his Camel exhaling the cloud of his smoke into JTP’s position. "You are a MAN of the people, Joe…that means that you are only a man to them. It means there’s something GREATER than you that I could use to have them turn against you, that I could use to show you how the COMMON MAN is supposed to be treated." Mayfield flicked his cigarette away, after a long inhale. "It couldn’t be a heel like me to turn them against you," Mayfield woefully shrugged. "It couldn’t be a MAN like you," Mayfield grinned widely, exhaling the last of his smoke into Joe’s direction again. "But he heard something in my address at 49, Joe…for whatever reason, he CAME to my office. A beacon of light in your slimy, homeless alley world, an angry and defiant resolve to prove something to YOUR people. To prove something to the same fans that for whatever reasons…chant YOUR name. And I knew, Joe…he COULD bring the WORLD to you, Joe. To see YOUR FILTH." "JOE! JOE! JOE!" Mayfield looked around and smiled, "Listen to them, Joe…it may be the last time…because after tonight, the game will change. They will NOT believe in you, like they believed in him…warranted or not." The beehive was loud, the Joe chants were dying into the literal buzzing storm of crowd anticipation. "You may be their MAN, Joe…you may be their soul, but you can’t be what he was…you are NOT their HERO." BLACKNESS. The haunting opening notes of "Back in the Saddle" by Aerosmith started to rev up the crowd as flickering strobe lights drowned out the thousands of camera flashes also accompanying the moment. The guitar and drum intro churned, kickstarted and blasted it’s engines to… "I’M BAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!" The Steven Tyler shriek was matched with every light bulb in Madison Square Garden turned to blinding white. The crowd nearly toppled over each other trying to get the viewpoint near the FRONTIERtron. A man stood there with a t-shirt only the NFW faithful would recognize via it’s simple three-word statement… "PAUL IS DEAD." He turned around and the crowd went "GUH." Did a doubletake, then would later needed to be told what they did in the next 10 seconds during their INSANE, RAFTER QUAKING ROAR! Grown adults had to check if they pisssed themselves in their screaming blackout. I swear, I saw someone dribble a little. Eddie Mayfield, once known as WASP stood proudly defiant as Joe the Plumber, Felix Red and Sean Stevens watched a single man move quickly to the ring, slapping hands amongst the nest of chaotic ringside fans. HORNET The crowd shook, the Garden was quaking. Hornet walked up the steps to the middle of the ring, blue jeans and black shoes accentuating the t-shirt choice. He ducked in as the crowd started chanting "WELCOME BACK! WELCOME BACK! WELCOME BACK!" There had been sightings over the last couple of years, but not many…a legend that didn’t believe he was done, waiting to find the opportune moment to prove it. Felix Red hopped off the turnbuckles, while Steve Knox moved a shade closer to his chair. Sean Stevens was no longer focused on Joe the Plumber, instead he locked eyes with Hornet IMMEDIATELY. An unspoken, irreparable hatred and discordance built between them. JTP didn’t react well to the chants, his cheeks started puffing as Hornet walked past Stevens…turning his attention the champion. HORNET. JTP. STAREDOWN. Hornet’s nose bristled as he got close to Joe, nothing ever prepares a man for the first whiff and even as his eyes watered…they did not break from the champion’s. JTP got unseasonably close, but Hornet…would not budge. "HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!" The chants started and they broke Joe’s concentration as they turned to something else… "HORRRRRRRRRNET! HORRRRRRRRRRNET!" Now, it was Joe’s nose bristling only…he reacted differently, although it made the crowd just as happy. He rocked Hornet with a right hand! He aimed another, but Hornet blocked it and delivered his own right! STEVENS TACKLES HORNET! KNOX TOSSES JOE THE CHAIR! FELIX DROPKICK INTO THE CHAIR AND JOE’S HEAD! Joe tumbled over and crushed the podium. The crowd went nuts as Stevens and Hornet donnybrooked on the mat! Security trying to pull them apart as Joe bulled up to his feet and picked up Felix and drove him into the corner! Knox stood in the middle of the chaos, but was pulled away after trying to waistlock Joe for a Suplex. The National Guard and Secret Service were too strong in numbers and separated the FIVE SUPERSTARS now destined to fight for the WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP at CRASH 50, "THE GREAT AMERICAN SMOKEOUT." WORLD CHAMPION, JOE THE PLUMBER. FELIX RED. STEVE KNOX. "TRIPLE X" SEAN STEVENS HORNET And what about EDDIE MAYFIELD, your PRESIDENT? The first swing from Joe to start the mini-brawl was met with a deftly timed duck behind the Crimson Star flag. Mayfield watched the rest with a sly smirk on the entrance ramp, where he was far away from the action and flanked by security. The crowd around him chanted, "EN-EFF-DUB! EN-EFF-DUB!" And hopefully…nobody crashed their car, while listening on the radio. That might have been too much of something Steve Knox likes to call… AWESOMENESS.
