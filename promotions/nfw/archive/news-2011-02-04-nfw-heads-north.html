NFW news: NFW Heads North

4 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Heads North Posted by Natrone Rice on 4 Feb 2011 Confirming rumors about a rare trip to Canada, New Frontier Wrestling will trek up North along the Rocky Mountains for two shows in February. One NFW Brawl in Billings, Montana in mid-February will precede a landmark NFW Reloaded from the Rogers Arena in Vancouver, Canada which will close out the month. The Brawl features one championship match as well as an all-star National Championship division tag team match that may provide some insight into what President Mayfield wants to accomplish with that title's future. Meanwhile, in Reloaded...Troy Windham is contractually bound to enter the NFW squared circle in a special tag team challenge match when Impulse and Dan Ryan take on Castor Strife and Dorchester Stratton. All four men have issues with the newly formed Windham Clan that's seemingly invaded the New Frontier, while being led under the supervision of Troy. Things are going to be interesting, that's for sure! Stay tuned, there's certainly going to be plenty of surprises yet!
