NFW news: JTP Indefinitely Suspended

4 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JTP Indefinitely Suspended Posted by Devin Millwood on 4 Mar 2011 Rumors were swirling after the latest NFW RELOADED, but in the biggest possible surprise of all -- President Eddie Mayfield told the millions of NFW fans the one thing we didn't expect... THE TRUTH. In reports that are being confirmed by Sterling Infosystems, a New York based 3rd-party firm that handles all NFW drug testing...Joseph Theodore Plummer did not show up for mandatory testing, which is required to fulfill contractual obligations with NFW. The Executive Branch released the formal letter that they sent to Plummer before the second leg of the Rocky Mountain Massacre Tour that along with mandatory testing, Joe the Plumber would be unable to compete in a wrestling match until fully examined by the medical staff. Plummer had been avoiding this since Crash 50 after Dan Ryan delivered a massive Humility Bomb after a 100 minute title defense against 4 of the world's best wrestlers. Since then, Joe the Plumber has suffered devastating attacks at the hands of Dorchester Stratton and Dynasty, as well as during the recent Windham Clan invasion. St. Luke's Hospital in Denver notified NFW that Plummer had left their premises after arriving via ambulance merely hours before, preventing any further testing and examination from their personnel. Early last week, the only sign of the plumber was seen in a package delivered to President Eddie Mayfield himself, which was Plummer's platinum version of the NFW World Championship with a several plies of toilet paper duct-taped to the front plate of the belt. The words "I QUIT" rumored to be smudged in Joe's 'personal' brown ink. NFW was left with no choice, but to indefinitely suspend Plummer until further notice and removing him from the active roster. As of the time of this story's release, the NFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP is still officially vacated with no action plan in place to fill a new champion.
