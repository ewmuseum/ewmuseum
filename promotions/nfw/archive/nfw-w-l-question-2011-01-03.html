NFW W/L Question

3 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
jediPREZ — 3 January 2011 As I'm compiling the archives and auditing them for accuracy, I have a question to everyone...
Should a triple threat match victory be recognized as a Singles win or Triple Threat win? What about a Pentagram Match? Or the Sears Tower match? They are noted as cluster matches right now...and thus, a cluster victory.
EpyonMarx — 3 January 2011 When I did a similar thing for A1E (except I was using a point-based system), I gave it as 3 points plus a bonus for speciality match (plus a further bonus for a winning a title). So, speciality win?
jediPREZ — 3 January 2011 Well, it's more or less how it's being tracked on the website. EXAMPLE: http://nfw.e-wrestling.org/bio.php?username=pollard&page=moves There may be a triple threat victory or loss that's not showing up as a singles victory for Pollard. Would you rather see a separate line for the Triple Threat W/L's, or just include them in the singles record?
Macc24 — 3 January 2011 Singles record. It should just be the overall record. You won, or you lost, IMO. LQJT86C — 4 January 2011 Singles record. I recorded Castor's loss to DJ Havok and Jonathan Marx in a triple threat from 2003 as a singles loss, even though Havok pinned Marx. The way I see it, if a champion loses that match, he loses the title; it's a loss. So the same should be applied to everybody when fighting in a triple threat. I did notice that if you label a match "Coal Miner's Glove" or anything other than singles, it doesn't register, so I quickly stopped doing that for the sake of accurate record keeping. But it would be cool if those were recognized.
jediPREZ — 10 January 2011 I've gone through all the posted shows and updated to singles records. Will probably go through at a later date again just because I probably skipped one or something.
Midnight Madness 11 and Crash 38 to go in terms of capturing all matches. We're very close to accurate records and it's very cool.
Dan Ryan has practically locked up most singles matches and victories. One of the few dual season Ultratitle wrestlers to participate in a v.1 or v.3 era.
