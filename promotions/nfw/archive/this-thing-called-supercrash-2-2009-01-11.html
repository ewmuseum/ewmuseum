Finally made it through the show. It was a real treat, like all big shows we usually wait a hot minute to put out.
The opener...I'd be furious if it was less than stellar because Katz knows how to open and especially close a show as well as anyone I've seen...but this was something special for sure! Montages always kind of "unify" a card for me by bringing all the players together, and this did that but with NFW's patented (and Mayfield-disapproved) drug-addled madness. The Facey appearance was a little bittersweet, because I'm pretty sure Joe's had to cancel his IFE run here...but I hope this isn't the last we see of Ford (though how many feds can you handle, homey?).
Commiserations to Renner. Losing two belts is never fun, and Knox has been one of the 'Dub's hottest winners recently. But it'll be very interesting to see how the guy cloaked in gold who can't stop talking about his awesomeness reacts to a dismal night like this one. Conversely, the win for Rook Black is HYOOGE because he's slugged it out since the v.3.0 kick-off with limited results but a great storyline, so this bodes very well for angles in the future in general.
The Grand Prix comes to a head, and it's not perhaps the likeliest victor (though his W/L record in tournaments should have alerted me, perhaps?), but watching how Doc C treats this massive victory will be fun. Impulse benefits tremendously from this show as well...far from his underdog beginnings, he's a bonafide contender at any level now, and him calling out the (would-be) World Champ is a sign of that. For a host of reasons, Impulse has become one of NFW's most interesting characters to me.
The Tag Title Match was honestly the only one besides the Main that I really gave a solid read through. I've come to expect greatness in the matches that have defined the Suicide Kings/HWC feud, and there were no disappointments tonight, either in the action which was brutal or in angle developments with the return of WildStar to win it with his old pal Tsunami (to what chagrin of Stratton's, we wonder?). Honestly NFW has just been lucky as sh*t to have JN and [name withheld] handling these teams and single-handedly keeping a division not only visible, but sub-main or damn, main-eventing even, most shows. That's a result of the care that's gone into making this a classic tag-team wrestling angle that relies on the foundations of good wrestling drama without coming across as tired...at all. That's what I got for that.
And then the Main Event! Wowee, Devin, you wrote a piece of work and I loved every minute of it. Congrats on the win, I truly, unequivocally pity the muh'fuggin' fool who steps to JTP. I've eaten up your JTP promos from Day One and continue to hawk your awesomeness in every "Which Character(s) Do You Like" thread I run across. JTP is the perfect FW translation of that - I mean this in a good way - [name withheld] sleaze that only you can manufacture in that adorable, demented dome of yours. *Salute*
