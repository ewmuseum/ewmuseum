What might have been?

12 July 2013
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ford — 12 July 2013 Figured we'd have a discussion about ideas / concepts we wanted to run but never got off the ground for whatever reason.
My biggest disappointment was around super crash 2. If you check the intro, Flyer and Facey are together doing the opening montage. That was to be the start of a partnership between the two characters. Well, a partnership filled with bad blood. But super crash took a while and facey'a handler left.
Anyone else? brusch — 13 July 2013 Ocho vs Spooky Doom: the blowoff match. No, for real. I started a feud with him in IWF (that trickled a bit into NFW) because I wanted to challenge myself, and the angle itself was turning pretty interesting. Then, "Spooky being Spooky" happened, prematurely ending things before we could have that cathartic tearing into each other.
