Off to Valinor — 13 March 2008 http://nfw.fwrestling.com/ Hey...close to the date, I'm freaking out! ROCK*THE*REVOLUTION (htt://wwwp.fwrestling.com/a/events/EkpVZEpVpk.shtml)! Legion — 13 March 2008 Good show! Seeing the first encounter between Rook and Legion was great and the finish made perfect sense considering Crashmas... Legion's revenge will be epic, he needs to send Rook a message... maybe TQ could be his messenger and maybe it's not just Legion that wants TQ to pay... what about Sickness? ;-)
suddenimpact — 13 March 2008 I enjoyed it as well. Felix Red's continued slide down the ladder, as El Presidente said, could go hand in hand with a big breakout as the best way to get a push is to be widely loved or widely hated.
The National Championship still seems to be the hottest title as the multiple challengers all seem to add a dimension to the circular feud instead of distorting it.
Line of the night, in my opinion, was in my match. "Calamity and Toombs are trying to call the match, but its not in their job description." This was definitely a successful experiment, I think. Macc24 — 13 March 2008 I had a blast reading it. This House Show concept forces you to read every little detail and not skim whatsoever. I loved the Nova match as well as Legion and Rook, I thought those were awesome. And Nova acting on the prophecy's desire for his success being held down by Hyperspace Kid?
Deadly. Great show, looking forward to St. Patty's, -Macc Nova — 15 March 2008 And Nova acting on the prophecy's desire for his success being held down by Hyperspace Kid?
Deadly.That won't be easy for the EAGLEstar to explain to his comrade. HE SAW A FLY! Great show. The POV of the re-capper is so fresh, the show reads like a breeze and you're done before you know it (always a good thing). This Brawl really moved plotlines along in a way that makes the Missoula show important as a point of reference, which is awesome considering even the card was drawn up in the dark.
LINE OF THE NIGHT: "If you've ever seen a Midget take off faster than Speedy Gonzalez on a gallon of crack oatmeal smothered in coconut cocaine milk...then you were in Missoula, Montana precisely at this moment."
Off to Valinor — 16 March 2008 Thanks for all the cool reviews. :cool: Thanks to Macc for one of the matches. ROCK*THE*REVOLUTION (htt://wwwp.fwrestling.com/a/events/EkpVZEpVpk.shtml)!
