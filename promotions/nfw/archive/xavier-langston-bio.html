Xavier Langston Biography Eye Color Blue Nicknames DMXL, XL Hometown Newark, New Jersey Height 6'1 Weight 230 Alias Da Man Handedness Ambidexterous Looks Mocha skin, dirty blonde hair in dred locks, dirty blonde James Harden-style beard, blue eyes, wiry and fit but not overly muscular. Wrestling Style Martial Arts based Striking with a fair amount of High Risks and a bit of a Submission game. Strengths 1) Speed and athleticism - Langston is a quick and aglie man 2) Street-smart - Langston understands people and how they work, and it's incredibly hard to hustle him or fool him. 3) Martial Artist - Langston has studied a form of karate and it has given life to his striking game. He uses his strikes with great efficiency, and they are both precise and lethal Weaknessees 1) Strength - he ain't got a ton of brute strength - his strikes and moves are all lethal due to precision execution. Lifting anyone who's a 250-300 is a struggle, anyone over 300 is a no-go. 2) Focus - While Langston is hard to hustle or fool, he is prone to showing off to a crowd. 3) Ground grappling - While Langston is a fast, agile man, if someone is able to ground him and force him into a grappling exchange, Langston will struggle to counteract, preferring instead to escape or get to the ropes. If he's unable to escape, his frustration level will rise and he'll just attempt to beat his way out (with mixed results depending on the grappler's size/strength/brawl/martial arts background). Ring attire Xavier wrestles shirtless, showcasing the black Old English X tattoo on his chest. His arms have black and orange striped bands around his upper biceps, and he tapes his wrists lightly. His lower body is covered with black and orange striped tights, which mesh well with his black and orange striped wrestling boots. When walking to the ring, he has a black sleeveless trench coat with "Da Man" sewn into the back in orange fabric that he sheds after entering the ring, handing to the attendant when possible.
