Los Olvidados Biography Biography The masked luchador clad in yellow REY AMARILLO first appeared on the indie scene in the year 2000, making various appearances across the globe. His infrequent appearances and stunning high-flying talents helped him to develop a mythic reputation within small circles of underground wrestling communities. By the year 2002, he crossed paths with the aspiring wrestler ERIK BLACK while touring through the Midwest, taking on the young and brash daredevil as his protege and giving them the mask and monicker of EL CABRON. Thus LOS OLVIDADOS was formed, and toured extensively over the better part of a year as Black’s skillset improved and the two slowly made their way west. There were eventually plans for the two to take the act to the leagues in Japan, before Rey Amarillo mysteriously disappeared. Black continued on to Japan and went on to become semi-prolific through various federations under several names and guises, while keeping the El Cabrón identity only through his work in the independents as a way of sticking to his roots without fully giving himself away as a major league competitor. Over the next twelve years, he established himself a polarizing figure in professional wrestling… eschewing the traditional competitive motivations toward victory, instead looking at the sport of professional wrestling more as an artform and vicarious outlet to a lifetime of rage and self-deprecation. Many have said that he has always had the potential to go far, but lacked the discipline and the willpower to truly improve himself and become an elite athlete. Black has argued for many years that he has willingly held himself back, seeing success and improvement as compromising to a way of life that he is mostly comfortable with, even if the rest of the world perceives him as a loser and burnout. His most recent work involved mostly unsuccessful campaign to destroy Empire Pro under the alias of REZIN. EPW would eventually close its doors anyway at the conclusion of Wrestleverse V, leaving Black out of work and relying on the old El Cabrón mask and the indies to bring home a steady paycheck. He got into a usual routine of mediocrity and sell-jobs… until one day, to his surprise, he was visited upon his old friend and mentor Rey Amarillo. Convincing his once protege to pick up where they left off twelve years ago, the two have reunited to bring LOS OLVIDADOS plans back to fruition… this time on a larger scale. Wrestling Style Amarillo is a finesse high-flyer and tecnico luchadore. Cabrón is a daredevil high-flyer and martial arts expert. Both men are spot artists, and make their greatest gains in matches through unleashing perfectly timed and opportunistic tandem techniques. At points in matches, they will string together chains of aerodynamic moves in synchronization -- usually finishing a few moves in after they perform the same move in the direction of one target, which ends with Rey Amarillo nailing the move and El Cabrón hilariously landing on his face. Cabrón usually starts off the matches, trades licks before inevitably getting caught up in the showboating, then eventually gets his ass dominated through a series of hard and completely oversold bumps. Amarillo patiently watches until an opportunistic tag-in, in which he flies in and saves the day through an expertly executed aerial onslaught. Cabrón is also capable of well-timed saves, whenever he chooses to channel his inner Bruce Lee and counter a move with a face-melting moonsault. Finisher Name The Carcosa Driver Finisher Description Assisted Corkscrew Neckbreaker. El Cabrón holds opponent in place with a low waistlock while Rey Amarillo takes a run off the ropes before vaulting off his partner’s back, grabbing the head, twisting around in air, and landing on his back with the back of the target’s head coming down on his shoulder. Move Set - Stereo Moonsaults - Stereo Dropkicks - Double Rope-Flip Body Attack - Double Flipping Neckbreaker - Rey Amarillo Signatures (Springboard Forearm Strike, Tornado DDT, La Mistica, Tope Con Giro) - El Cabrón Signatures (Cabrónsault, Cabrócanrana, Spinning Cloven Hoof Kick, Missile Dropkick) - Rey Amarillo Finisher (La Antigua - tilt-a-whirl leg-scissor facebuster into rear-naked choke) - El Cabrón Finisher (G.O.A.T - supposedly stands for Gyrokinetic Overhead Aerial Takedown, but is basically a standing Shiranui) Ring Entrances Arena fills with guitar drone… lights may come down at bigger events. Rey Amarillo comes out first, stands at the head of the aisle, and waves his hand slowly out in front of him spanning the crowd, like he were casting a spell over them. El Cabrón emerges next, spinning out like the Tazmanian Devil and caps it off with by stopping himself by a big double-fist pump to charge up the crowd… only to find himself facing the wrong way. He realizes his error, corrects himself with a 180 degree spin and shakes it off with a goatish to the fans. The partners make eye contact, nod, and stride down to the ring together, Amarillo moving with bold determination while Cabrón bobs to the music.
