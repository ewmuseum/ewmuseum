[CAW Poll] Whom should I create first?

31 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TH — 31 December 2005 Alright, so I got Smackdown vs. Raw 2006 and I created Maggot. My plan is to create the entire NFW roster, or at least as many as will fit on my memory card (30 I believe is the limit). Since Maggot's in the East, I wanted to create a guy from the West now. I also want to create a guy who's been there from the beginning, uninterrupted. So whom should I pick?
The Guy — 6 January 2006 I have a semi-decent Seymour Almasy (PTC/GCW/Clint poser) version on my SvsR06. The character is a pain in the ass to make as i never have enough layers to do all the morphs I want.
Haven't made a Guy Boudreaux yet, though I did do Joe Average. I hate his entrance. Jason Payne — 6 January 2006 I got the finishing touches on Payne coming to fruition. Hopefully be able to have his info up for you soon.
Whats Your Alias — 7 January 2006 I've created an Alias I'm comfortable with. Who, as it always turns out, is like an alternate reality version of the actual eW Alias, kinda not him, but still him, with a kickass pair of pants... and still someone I still always enjoy creating.
Later days, Russ QueenOfTheRing — 7 January 2006 [quoting] Whats Your Alias said: > kinda not him, but still him, with a kickass pair of pants [end quote] The clothes make the man, they say...
