NFW news: Deville suing NFW for SC3 Attack

7 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Deville suing NFW for SC3 Attack Posted by Devin Millwood on 7 May 2011 Even though he spurned the Windham Clan's victory in the Supercrash 3 Main Event, JJ Deville is now suing NFW for what happened afterwards. Details are sketchy, we're not sure if this is a publicity stunt on behalf of Deville alone or the Windham Clan as a whole, but we have received confirmation that lawyers for JJ Deville are putting together a court case in lieu of the post-match attacks administered by Tsunami and Mike Randalls. Tsunami in particular is the target of this case as Deville is claiming that he has suffered physical, emotional and mental injuries caused by Tsunami's black mist being sprayed into his eyes. Word has it that a number of NFW wrestlers incredibly including Dorchester Stratton have been asked this week to come back to San Antonio, Texas for deposition statements. Others that have given testimonials are all Windham Clan members, Cameron Cruise, Bret Kelly, Lamont Hollywood, Dynasty and Kerry O'Connor. More to come...when we learn more about this.
