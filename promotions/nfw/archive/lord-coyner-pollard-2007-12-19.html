Lord Coyner Pollard of Mercia, Norfolk and Wessex, Duke of Suffolk, Earl of Powys...

19 December 2007

EpyonMarx — 19 December 2007 HANDLER INFO: Name: [name withheld] E-mail: [detail withheld] Preferred method of handling: FW Promo Best way to contact: PM WRESTLER INFO Name: Lord Coyner Pollard of Mercia, Norfolk, and Wessex, Duke of Suffolk, Earl of Powys, and of various lands in Northumbria and the land of the Ancient Britons.
Height: 6'0" Weight: 190lbs From: One of his estates in Mercia, Norfolk, Wessex, Suffolk, Powys, Northumbria, or the Devon/Cornwall area.
Physical Description: Long brown hair, going just below his neck, brown eyes, no piercings. He's a slim man, not very muscular to look at. He dresses in a VERY stereotypical British aristocracy manner - frilly shirts, very formal. His actual clothing changes depending on his mood, but doesn't stray far from Medieval through to late Edwardian (20th Century) dress. In the ring, he wears a singlet, normally a crimson red, sometimes a blue with silver piping.
Theme tune: "Americans are Behind" by Bruce Dickinson Entrance: The music starts. Once it kicks in (after the "Hello!"), he walks down to the ring, mouthing some of the words to the crowd. Nothing fancy, nothing flash. He's British, don't you know, and we don't need flashy showy things.
Quote: "Americans are behind" "Jeeves..." History: Don't ask. He'll start talking to you about his family history, and you REALLY don't have the time to listen to several hundred years of family history now, do you?
Character: A very pompous, stereotypical, silly Brit. Everyone beneath his social class is beneath him, and therefore stupid. And if you're a Colonist (aka. American)... you're just behind.
In-ring style: Technician. Best parallels would be Steven Regal, Dave Finley, Pat Roach, Mark 'Rollerball' Rocco, and lots of other British names which few outside Britain probably know any more. He is NOT a brawler, a high-flyer, or a power-house, but he's very good at grappling, especially at applying moves without the need to pause - if he knocks you to the canvas, he will apply a hold almost instantly. One of the quirks from the old British style of wrestling.
Finisher: Americans Are Behind Finisher description: Regal Stretch Moveset: Straight-jacket Abdominal Stretch Hammerlock Forearm smash (aka European Uppercut) STF Indian Deathlock Shortarm clothesline Dragon Sleeper STS Lady of the Lake (aka Johnny Saint Special - This is a move used to trick an unsuspecting opponent. The wrestler sits down, crosses his or her legs, tucks their head into their chest and wraps one arm around their ankle (so they are effectively rolled into a ball). The wrestler then extends their remaining arm between their legs and then waits. The opponent, ostensibly confused, normally takes the offered hand, at which point the wrestler rolls forward and into an arm lock. So sayeth Wikipedia)
Various suplexes Various other wrestling holds Strengths: 1) He's INCREDIBLY technically sound. 2) He's British. 3) He knows how to get under people's skin with taunts Weaknesses: 1) He's a pompous donkey (not literally...) who's not above taunting too much. 2) He has a fiery temper when things don't go his way. 3) He's a bit of a coward. Other important note:He's a typical, old-style heel.
