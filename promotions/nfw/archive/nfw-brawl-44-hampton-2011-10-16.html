NFW Brawl 44: Hampton, 16 October 2011

16 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 44: Hampton 16 Oct 2011 Hampton Coliseum, Hampton, Virginia (seats 13,800) Welcome to the Show! (FADEIN: A panning shot of the outside of HAMPTON COLISEUM as the cheers of fans are audible from within. The large multicolored columns which encase the coliseum give it the impression of a spaceship, an aesthetic more appropriate to Frontier yesteryear. CUTTO: Pyrotechnics exploding inside the arena (SFX: Wheeeeeeeeeeeee! POP!!!!) lighting up the roiling sea of Frontier maniacs all liquored up and ready to watch the cast of NFW v4.0 set it off!) NATRONE RICE (V/O): "GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOONNNNNEEEE…ARE THE DAYS, when BRAWL crawled on its HANDS and KNEES to catch scraps at the table of the flagship Frontier programs! I’m NATRONE RICE, and if I may speak frankly, it was more difficult in HAMPTON, VEE-AY to spot the folks who WEREN’T on the show than spotting those who were! The talent pool gathered under the bright lights of the Hampton Coliseum ran the Frontier gamut, and everyone laid it out on the line to jostle for position as we move into this new era of Craig Miles’ psychotic science project known to the world as New Frontier Wrestling!" (CUTTO: Fans climbing over one another in the standing-only sections to get their signs on-camera… "DEACON – HAMPTON BELIEVES!"…"THE STRIFE MAN COMETH!"…"WINDHAM/TROUTMAN 2012!"…"STALKER = WORST FACEBOOK FRIEND EVER!") (CUTTO: ERIC DANE walking swiftly through the back of the arena, his hands in his pockets and his head lowered. CUTTO: ADRIAN WILLARD in the locker room, rotating his shoulder and tossing a few quick jabs. CUTTO: NOVA by ringside, taking pictures with a group of fans and then downing a beer to cheers from the crowd. CUTTO: DEACON in a private conference room-turned-chapel, CHRIS SHEFFIELD joining him in prayer.) RICE (V/O): "Fans, we had ERIC DANE taking on ADRIAN WILLARD on the undercard…Dane of course making his mark in recent weeks, inside the ring as well as showing himself to be capable on the microphone, and Willard proving the same…that match delivered on its promise! That match was followed by a Frontier DREAM-MATCH between two longtime ‘Dub vets in DEACON and NOVA, and the question with this one wasn’t whether these two would deliver the goods, but whether they’d get through it without any of the enemies they’ve made recently putting in appearances of their own…a question which was answered, to be sure!" (CUTTO: A QUICK-FLASH of the EMT participants in succession, followed by highlights of the DIRTY ROTTEN SCOUNDRELS capturing the EMT Championship from the HOLLYWOOD WRECKING CREW at RELOADED 8.) RICE (V/O): "Of course we all know the Frontier is the spiritual home of tag-team wrestling in the 21st century, and on this night the mettle of the champs was tested as D-R-S defended their straps against THE CHOSEN ONES, aka BOBBY JACK WINDHAM and recent =W= Clan convert CHRISTIAN CRUISE, and the team of STALKER and MR. MYERS! Stay with me, fans, and you might see gold swapping hands in Hampton!" (CUTTO: CASTOR STRIFE alone in the locker room, running a hand over the World Heavyweight Championship which rests on his shoulder. CUTTO: IMPULSE, walking down a long corridor and talking quietly with ROSE CALLASANTOS. CUTTO: JJ DEVILLE scribbling furiously on a blackboard, drawing arrows between names which visibly include "Impulse," "Troy Windham," and "Jack Bryant," among others. DAN RYAN stands in the corner, arms crossed, ignoring Deville.) RICE (V/O): "And in the tradition of saving the best for last, what a main event on the evening as NEWLY-CROWNED WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION CASTOR V. STRIFE and the MARATHON MAN he took to HELL and BACK went up against the brightest stars of the Windham Clan, the dynamic JJ DEVILLE and the #1 Contender to the WHC, DAN RYAN! I know you’re anxious to see what happened, and I can promise you that the actors on this stage of violence were not limited to the folks on the billing, so let’s dive right in and get this FREAK-SHOW rolling!" JJ DEVILLE = GARBAGE [FADE IN: The Parking Garage of the Hampton Coliseum had a roped off area for wrestlers and staff to enter and exit the venue, it was about a million miles and a bunch of twisty-turny corridors away from the dressing areas and offices and other such set-pieces. Backstage workers milled about, low-level talent such as P. KING DUK and MIKE MCGEE came and went, but one man stood out in the crowd. JACK BRYANT, in Wranglers and a "Roll Tide" tee, paced back and forth, back and forth with a sledgehammer resting on his right shoulder and bad intentions clearly etched across his face.] [QUICK CUTTO: NFW’s beat reporter, ‘JUST’ MARK, trotting up to where the Birmingham Stallion stalked back and forth in front of the entrance to the inner-cloister of the backstage area.] ‘JUST’ MARK: "Jack! If I could get a quick wo- " [A death-glare from BRYANT was all it took to shut Just Mark up mid-sentence. Jack snapped his head to the right, the camera followed, and was met by the Only Star. ERIC DANE, not yet dressed to wrestle, made his way over to where Jack had been holding vigil over the parking lot.] ERIC DANE: "Jack." JACK BRYANT: (nods) "Eric." [The two bumped fists while ‘JUST’ MARK watched on, just out of earshot. DANE took a quizzical survey of the situation. [Dane further surveyed the situation.] DANE: "And off the top of my head I’d say he’s considering letting you take JJ’s place. Temporarily, of course." [Bryant glowered at ‘Just’ Mark. Dane, never shy from a broadcasting camera, took easy control of the situation by doing what comes natural.] DANE: "Look, Marky Mark, if you’re gonna stand there in slack-jawed confusion, the very least you could do is put your mic-stand skills to use and stand there and try not to let Jack notice you too much." [Eric grabs the microphone and pulls it close, focusing on the camera.] DANE: "Here’s the thing. "Unlike Adrian Willard, who’s only gonna leave Hampton tonight with a few sore joints, a concussion, and a massively bruised ego, ol’ JJ’s gonna be leaving less one ugly mug. Understand?" [‘Just’ Mark just nods.] DANE: "Now, ask me a question, and try not to make it interesting." [The Frontier’s junior journalist clears his throat.] MARK: *ahem* "Well, ah, any truth to the rumors coming out of Monte Carlo that you and Calvin Carlton are in contract talks?" [The Only Star cuts a nasty glare at ‘Just’ Mark.] MARK: "Are you with The Dynasty?" DANE: "You know what? Wrong question. Now you get to ask Jack one and see whether or not he answers you with his boot in your ass." [Mark gulps.] MARK: "Alright... Jack, with JJ- " [He is cut off abruptly by the Birmingham Stallion stepping directly into his face. As Dane and Bryant have inched toward ‘Just’ Mark and the camera, the door into the venue has been left slightly unattended.] BRYANT: "Ah’m gettin’ tired ‘a you, runt. I got nothin’ ta say ta you, or JJ, that I can’t say with this sledgehammer." [An upside down garbage can makes it’s way sneakily into the background.] DANE: (smirking) "I guess that about says it all, eh Mark?" [Behind them the bottom of the garbage can pops up. JJ DEVILLE’s face can be seen clearly, along with the NFW WORLD TELEVISION TITLE somehow, his eyes wide in terror at the sheer proximity to Jack Bryant and his new toy.] DANE: "Now, if it’s all the same to you, I’m gonna go get some popcorn, watch the JB KILLS JJ show, and then go get ready to embarrass me some Adrian Willard." [The can pops up and with much haste enters the venue. ‘Just’ Mark remembers that he is employed by the New Frontier.] MARK: "Well there you have it folks... [He is interrupted again, this time by both DANE and BRYANT turning away from him and the camera and making their way into the building themselves.] [CUTTO: JJ DEVILLE pokes his head back out the door and winks, giving the viewing audience at home a quick thumbs up before scampering away like a thief in the night.] ADRIAN WILLARD v ERIC DANE (CUT-TO SPLIT-SCREEN: ADRIAN WILLARD, all jacked up like Schwarzenneger, tossing the curtain aside.
