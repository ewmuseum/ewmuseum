EastPrez — 27 October 2013 http://nfw.e-wrestling.org/content.php?p=results&id=179 WOOP! WOOP! (THAT’S THE SOUND OF THE POLICE!) Big Thanks to Biron and all the people who turned in segs, NFW BRASS and a big hello and welcome to Flyin' Brian and the debut of Xavier Langston! Hope you dig it.
Reactions/feedback encouraged and appreciated. RELOADED 18 may be up by the middle of the week. JN BigPimpin — 29 October 2013 It's good to be aboard. As I told EastPrez, I was happy with how Langston came off, and I enjoyed the show as a whole. Loved the overall focus on the world title and emt titles. Dirty Money is a fun team to read. And the format is a good one for quick hits for sure.
Look forward to future collaborations. EastPrez — 29 October 2013 Thanks man - appreciate the note. JN brusch — 31 October 2013 Quick thoughts: Matches were predictable but fun! Excited to see Langston in the fray - I remember LDO having an early foray with Plain Brown Rapper and things going well after that . The Harmen/Akita mist-on-mist clash was a fun spot, Dirty Money is one of the most fun tag teams on the roster (and I want to see more) and the match with Graverobbers was solid. The HFC continues to slide a little bit into the backburner in the main event; further build to the Reloaded world title match. Only thing that was missing from that final scene was Bryant...he was talked about a couple times throughout the show, but with the staredown and quick-attack kick from Harmen, it seemed like there was an opportunity for just a little bit more.
Loved the Stryfe promo, building more INTRIGUE~ into the identity and motivations of this mysterious fellow. Glad to see the continued use of "blah blah blah BLACK" to close things out - because I have this deep-seeded desire for unnecessarily ridiculous puns in all things, and even though this was ridiculous, I find it funny for reasons that may only make sense to me. I'm weird.
Also - are we getting a Stark on the roster? Excited for more! EastPrez — 31 October 2013 Thanks - it was a cool read. Thanks for the thoughts.
