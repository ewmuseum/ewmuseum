Manpower Biography Eye Color Green Nicknames The Tower of Power, the Superpower, the Higher Power (EAT IT, VINCE!) Hometown Born, San Francisco, CA. Resides currently in Memphis, Tennessee Birthday September 10th, 1977 Height 7' Weight 395 Alias The Big Behemoth, The Beast From The Mid-East Biography Ladies and gentlemen, what you are about to see in the realm of NFW is what happens when a billion-dollar body with a five-cent brain happen collide. Born in Los Angeles, he had very humble beginnings. Which is to say the kid had a very short attention span all the way to kindergarten. And even beyond. But what set young Michael Dearborn apart from everybody else was his immense height and weight at an early age. By twelve, the young upstart had grown to nearly six feet and almost two-hundred pounds. High school rolled around and our fair giant took an interest in basketball at first. Unfortunately, he was certainly no Shaq…he couldn’t make free throws. Or do anything else, for that matter. You see, Alexander Dearborn has what we like to call “a stupidity problem.” His ideas of fun revolved mainly around spinning in circles and pretending he was Lord of the Thundercats rather than getting serious and focusing on anything in school. This was all in his freshman year, might I add. So, yes. The giant Dearborn kid became a natural-born troublemaker. He’d use his advantageous size to just rough up people and take their money for baseball cards. His parents tried everything. Military school. Hypnotism. Psychology. Yup. Nothing seemed to settle their wild child down. It’d be years before the boy finally managed to settle down and get a job in at a local shipyard and for several years, that was that. Then along came the likes of his uncle, Ryan Dearborn. Loser. Drunkard. All-around no-good schemer. His brother, Alexander’s father, never approved of anything having to do with this moron. See, he saw something that nobody else did in Manpower. Let’s remember three things. 1) He was big. 2) He could quite possibly fight. 3) HE WAS BIG. So when the gears turned in the head of the much older Ryan Dearborn, he convinced young Alexander to compete in the world of professional wrestling. He saw how those idiots in WWE land pampered to big men. If he found somebody willing to teach Alexander how to wrestle, he’d make millions…possibly. Ryan, himself, was a former wrestling student. Problem was, he sucked ass at it. So he talked young Alexander to head to a local wrestling school headed by Sonny Silver. There, Sonny spent several nights a week for many months teaching this colossus how to wrestle. Eerily, though, Alex was good at it. VERY good. TOO good for a big man, some might even say. After his training was complete, Logan referred both young Alex and Ryan to an up-and-coming federation known only as NFW. The resurfacing of the famous federation had made waves. And if Ryan had anything to say about it, so would Alex. He gave Alex a new wrestling identity and a new way of life. Alex’s parents wished him the best in spite of Ryan taking on the role of his manager. With Ryan Dearborn by his side now, here he is. He doesn’t need to cheat. He doesn’t need your help. He doesn’t need the help of any objects to win. He’s gonna do this solely with Manpower. (Note: Ryan Dearborn got fired the first day. Seemed that Fiona Love took offense to him referring to him as, quote, 'a piece of ass.' Then, he was managed by a bum. Now, Calvin Carlton. Huzzah!) Other Feds None Other Fed Titles None...yet!
