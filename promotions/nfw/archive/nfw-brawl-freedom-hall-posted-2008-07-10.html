I didn't quite understand the setup for some reason before the match, but once I realized how it was going down, I was marking for every twist and turn.
***SPOILER ALERT*** HIGH POINTS: Doc C's 80-minute run. Couldn't have chosen a better character for it, especially what with the going half the time with half a pinfall thing. Sounds like something out of a Curious Rules match, which I'm sure is what Katz was going for. Also, I really liked Andy's RPs.
Facey's White Mist. Ewwwwww. Sars getting the win. I concurred in the result, he brought the brutality this go-round as he has been doing since he showed up, and nobody's really sure how to handle him...except maybe Devin. Those sick f*cks can out-sick-f*ck each other all day. And that just sounded weird.
Almost everyone qualifying. With a lineup this awesome (and it WAS), it'd be sad to see anyone go, so it's no huge surprise the only actual eliminations were the two folks who didn't RP (Sorry, Jay :(). The second round of this beast will be BRUTAL, and as a current titleholder I'm simultaneously enjoying the hell out of this, and sweating nuts for who will emerge Teh Victor.
