NFW Brawl 46: Montreal, 18 December 2011

18 December 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 46: Montreal 18 Dec 2011 Bell Centre, Montreal, Quebec (seats 21,273) Welcome to the Show V/O: "The following is a JOINT presentation from SIRIUS/XM RADIO and ESEN TELEVISION…" (MUSICUP: ‘HANDS’ – THE RACONTEURS) RICE: (V/O) "OHHHHHHHHHHH YEAH! We don’t have much time on this Sunday Night, CHILLIN’ up in CANADIA – where the NEW FRONTIER BUSTED ITS HUMP at the BELL CENTER in MONTREAL!" (QUICK CUTTOs: BLAINE HOLLYWOOD standing under a shower of white sparks cascading from the rafters… LEGION walking out as CASTOR STRIFE backpedals with both the WORLD and PURE Championships over his shoulder, while watching them with an approved nod – apparently impressed with their dignified strut… JACK BRYANT holding up the TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP high up in the air to crowd roars… ERIC DANE giving some fans the Spaceballs Presidential Salute… ROOK BLACK watching closely as JACK HARMEN’s boots are checked by referee GREG HERPIN.) (CUTTO: An external shot of OPTIMUS JR. – the MASSIVE and glistening NFW FILMS production/recording studio. CUTTO: NATRONE RICE sitting in the captain’s seat, adjusting his headphones and microphone…he makes a ‘CUT’ motion to the studio mixers.) (MUSIC CUT.) RICE: "ALRIGHT ALRIGHT – welcome to the BRAWL, Y’ALL! We’re just a little bit removed from a clash across the northern borders at the BELL CENTER in MONTREAL, CANADIA! That’s right, I’m STILL callin’ it CANADIA – but that’s not what’s important. Obviously, going across the border to our northern sister country wasn’t going to be EASY with JJ DEVILLE. Well, lessons learned are hard lessons in the past and this time – we so far haven’t experienced any headaches. Now, we’ve got FOUR MATCHES on the slate – but more importantly, we’re using tonight’s show for some BIG-TIME YEAR-END SPECTACULAR SUPERSHOW RUMOURS now REALITY NEWS, which may mean a little bit of a cutback on our highlights reel… we’ve also got a major update on a possible career-threatening injury for one of the BIGGEST names in New Frontier. Along with that? Well, come on now – what else could you POSSIBLY want!? OH HEY NATRONE, I saw some ROSTER NEWS – how about that? WELL ALRIGHT – let’s confirm the IRONICALLY FACEPALMING first…as Canada did NOT accept LOWELL’s passport, so there’s a real chance he’s going to miss any shows for the next couple weeks. We’re still awaiting confirmation on whether this is a ploy by JJ or Eddie, but for now there may just be a real chance that Canadia doesn’t trust the boy! Other than that, we can also confirm that Adrian Willard’s on the inactive list and YES, THAT NAME YOU SEE IS AKITA HOSHI – if you haven’t checked out the NFW Films piece – maybe you’ll know that the living legend formerly known as TSUNAMI is going to be fighting with a different flavor for the DARK STORM." ROOK BLACK (c) vs. JACK HARMEN RICE: "Alright, so we’re gonna go over the NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP match that took place tonight – ‘cause frankly, this one’s the QUICKEST to go over. Now, with the National Guard gone and some dude named Emilio running around – you may be wondering why he’s not on the roster page, well word has it that neither Eddie Mayfield or JJ Deville have seen any sort of work application form. And lord knows, Rook Black wasn’t going to be a help after our BRAWL in DC show! So, this show starts off with FUGAZI blaring! We got ROOK walking out to more boos than normal, the man’s maintained a stranglehold on that belt by even more nefarious methods than the crazies that have come after it. He apologizes for the match recently on NFW BRAWL and hopes to appease the fans, the administration and his championship status by OPEN CHALLENGING Jack Harmen to a match. WELL WHADDYA KNOW? Harmen’s in the building, he’s ready to go – only problem is that Jack Harmen has to go through more security checks, pat-downs and discomforting friskings than a man named Allah Ackbar Bin Laden at La Guardia airport! First, we’ve got Bell Centre employees checking his locker room bags, then we’ve got =W= Clan appointed security specialists checking through his sh(bleep!) and if that’s not enough – then NFW SECURITY checks him as he walks down the aisle." (RICE shakes his head in disbelief, chuckling a bit…) RICE: "So naturally, Greg Herpin our Senior Official, takes a good two minutes of checking Harmen for weapons, masks and asking if he’s been around anyone else’s locker room or luggage. Things are insane at this point, the crowd’s booing while Harmen’s giving them the Devil Horns and gyrating for a blushing Herpin… and Rook Black finally gets some cheers by rolling in and rocking Harmen with some fisticuffs! So, things got hot and heavy early – only thing we didn’t even get into a middle sandwich portion of what could’ve been a decent match. Harmen took control with a Tornado DDT… and then all of a sudden, the former #13 of the National Guard whose real name is Emilio… tears down the aisle in a sprint, chair at his side… slew of NFW security running after him. They can’t keep up and Emilio slides in the ring and CROWNS THE HELL out of Harmen. Herpin’s ringing for the bell immediately – Rook not knowing what’s going on except he’s lost by disqualification. The seat nearly popped out, but Emilio made sure the cherry didn’t stay in its skin as he then blasted Rook with the chair! The seat landed in the 4th row, Emilio stood over both Harmen and Rook…the boy cackled, I kid you not…and it was a little creepy. Then, he calmed himself…reached into his pocket and pulled out a bright green version of what used to be Impulse’s mask, raising it up in the air in triumph…before lacing it on and running back through the crowd to avoid punishment from security." (RICE laughs) RICE: "Only in NFW would we have a story where Harmen impersonates Impulse, only for this to now happen. And yes fans, we’ve heard some stories in regard to Calico Rose and Randall Knox – they were NOT at the arena this evening…and rather than commentate on what’s going on with them, I’m just wishing both the best right now. ANYWAY, we’re going to take a short commercial break and then a wild 6 man affair involving THE GUILD, THE FALLEN and ERIC DANE stuck in the middle of it all!" (MUSICUP: ‘STUCK IN THE MIDDLE’ – STEALERS WHEEL) (FADEAWAY…) ALEX AUSTIN, LEGION & PETER WINDHAM vs. THE FALLEN & ERIC DANE (MUSICFADE: ‘STUCK IN THE MIDDLE’ (coda) – STEALERS WHEEL) (CUTTO: CASTOR STRIFE walking out underneath the FRONTIERtron – the GUILD for CREATIVE INTEGRITY logo shining brightly. STRIFE finger-polishes the WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP glittering over his shoulder, while the PURE CHAMPIONSHIP is draped over his other shoulder. LANA DREMIRE and ALEX AUSTIN walk ahead of him on the declining rampway, looking all-business. LEGION walks out, STRIFE backpedaling in step with him, nodding approvingly. Out last is MAGNUS dragging a neck-shackled PETER WINDHAM wearing a KORN snuggie.) RICE: "No words can describe the GUILD for CREATIVE INTEGRITY right now, no audio commentary, but our lucky ESEN subscribers can definitely agree that a picture says 1,000 words!" (CUTTO: LEGION with his arms outstretched, CASTOR handing MAGNUS the PURE CHAMPIONSHIP to strap onto LEGION – while LANA pets PETER WINDHAM’s head gently. PETE’s hair is already disheveled, there’s a good possibility LANA’s been playing gothic gimp dollhouse judging by his faded eyeliner and he’s repeating the words, "Troy will save me, Troy will save me…" over and over and over…) RICE: "So, the GUILD was out first…two champions slated to fight together against a little bit of an odd matchup." (CUTTO: LUCI4 bungee-cod strapped to the back of MR. MYERS, FREDERICK KRUEGER III taking off his bowler hat and bowing, while ‘HOCKEY DUDE’ JASON VOORHEES does a DAVID LEE ROTH split-jump while rocking out to some air guitar. STALKER walks out slowly, brushing his hand over LUCI4’s cheeks as ERIC DANE walks by briskly, making a beeline for the ringside area. Double-nay-Triple-taking a look back at the conglomerate of freaks behind him. As he nears ringside, DANE walks to the opposite side of the GUILD gritting his teeth.) RICE: "So, this was a FALLEN-REQUESTED, JJ DEVILLE approved endeavor of a 6-man TAG TEAM match. On one side, you had the newly minted Guild faction with the addition of PURE CHAMPION, Legion…on another side, you had Eric Dane tongue-in-cheek firmly implanted by JJ Deville into a freakzone of a warzone on the side of Stalker’s Fallen. For those of you writing out official scorecards… which would be downright FRIGHTENING, it was actually ALEX AUSTIN, LEGION and the KORN-SNUGGIED Peter Windham taking on Dane, Stalker and Frederick Krueger III… And let’s get down to the obvious notion that Dane tried to jump ship, turn tail and run, but Jason, Shamon, Myers weren’t so keen on letting him get out of Dodge. Leave it to Dane to make a real effort on escaping and breaking through, but finding himself face to face with a FRONTIERtron full of JJ DEVILLE’s grill threatening suspension, no pay, charity hours and a 3-hour physical examination first thing on Monday. So, while Dane was walking back in a fume, we had a match going on. Early going, had Austin gaining the upper advantage on Krueger. Legion tagging in and keeping that going. For some godawful reason, they tagged in Peter Windham…who with or without a gothic-themed snuggie wrestling attire… well he’s not going to set the wrestling world afire. He gets into a world of trouble, but makes the tag to Legion… who takes a cheapshot from Dane on the apron, leading into a freeflow DDT by Stalker. Austin breaks up the pin attempt, but things get bad for Legion as Dane’s now more than happy to participate in the proceedings. Dane helps work the neck and head, Stalker locks in neck vice after neck vice… Krueger goes into knife-edge chop mode and Legion ducks one of those, Back Suplexes Krueger and HOT TAG – ALEX AUSTIN." (CUTTO: AUSTIN running into the ring and hitting a hard right on KRUEGER! STALKER jumps onto AUSTIN’s back, DANE slowly creeping through the ropes. AUSTIN manages to backpedal into a corner turnbuckle slam on STALKER and take him out of there with a running bulldog! As he gets up, DANE sidles up on him and delivers a leg scissors facebuster!) RICE: "Things get frantic again, this time Dane causing damage to Alex Austin…allowing Stalker to drag his henchmen to the corner, tag himself in and deliver not one, but TWO Piledrivers to Alex Austin. At this point, Dane and Castor Strife started having some words, which led to Lana Dremire SPITTING on ‘THE ONLY STAR,’ which wasn’t received too well. Dane hopped off the apron, only to hop right back on as the FALLEN converged on him. Stalker slapped Dane and slingshotted him into the ring and as Dane went to argue with him… Austin hits a Dragon Suplex, getting a two count! Austin makes the tag, Dane tags in Stalker and we’ve got The Bell Center BALLIN’ in volume!" (CUTTO: LEGION with a kick to the gut – goes for a BRAINBUSTER, but STALKER counters with the 180 landing behind him and hits a Side Russian Legsweep! STALKER rolls up, hopping onto the second turnbuckle and MISSES a Senton as LEGION rolls out of the way! Both scramble up, LEGION ducking under a clothesline and hitting an INVERTED STO! LEGION goes for the pin, getting a close two count!) RICE: "This one ends unceremoniously as PETER WINDHAM attempts mutiny by hitting a MOONSAULT onto LEGION during a pin attempt on STALKER! Eric Dane than catches PETER with the STARDRIVER, but gets pulled out of the ring by Castor Strife and MAGNUS…LANA DREMIRE digging her fingernails into his eyes and allowing Magnus to hit a press slam on the concrete! But it’s back in the ring, where Stalker comes out of the mess by crawling onto a spastically shaking Peter Windham and getting the pin. The post-match festivities saw the Guild fighting back the Fallen, while Eric Dane climbed over the ring barricade and escaping what was most likely going to end in bloody, unconsciousness for himself. Can’t say I wasn’t a little disappointed that he missed out on THAT fun! ALRIGHT – when we get back from the break, BIG injury rumors and the BLAINE HOLLYWOOD/187 STEEL CAGE SOIREE!" (FADEAWAY…) BLAINE HOLLYWOOD vs. 187 (MUSICUP: ‘BLACKWATER’ – THE BLACKWATER FEVER) (FADEIN: A steel cage lowering to bombastic firework explosions around the ringside area, the crowd going wild! CUTTO: THE DIRTY ROTTEN SCOUNDRELS walking out with SHANIQUA CARLTON in a winter ’12 ‘SHE-UNIQUE’ fashion show preview. SAAFIR’s in what’s becoming his traditional fine silk suit, tie get-up, but 187 stays close to his traditional attire. Black ringpants, black ‘BLACK STAR’ t-shirt and a Kevlar vest strapped around his torso. SAAFIR’s got both EMT titles draped over his shoulder. CUTTO: BLAINE HOLLYWOOD scoffing as he walks underneath a cascading waterfall of white fireworks near the FRONTIERtron area. CALVIN CARLTON’s to his left, wearing an all-white suit and MALIK ANDERSON to his right, black shades, black polo, khakis and wingtipped shoes. BLAINE’s in his traditional OXONIAN robes and colors for the night’s affair…) RICE: "WELCOME BACK Y’ALL – TO THE ENN-EFF-DUB BRAWL! So, we’re gonna quickly recap the singles STEEL CAGE showdown between half of the EMT CHAMPS, DIRTY ROTTEN SCOUNDRELS – 187 and half of the FORMER two-time champions, the Hollywood Wrecking Crew – BLAINE HOLLYWOOOOOOOOOOD. Over the last few months, there’s been a shake-up in a division that for the most parts been DOMINATED by The Hollywood Wrecking Crew for two years. Their only true rivals completely broken apart and halfway assimilated if we remember the transition from DC STRATTON to DORCHESTER STRATTON – and more on that guy later! The DRS by hook or bycrooked – they’ve had the number of the HWC. Saafir’s taken down Blaine in singles, they’ve RETAINED their belts several times…and there’s little birdies thinking that it’s more about SHANIQUA outsmarting her brother CALVIN, than what’s going on in the ring. Tonight, the rivalry heated up even further as it takes A LOT for Blaine Hollywood to set foot in a steel cage against a mercenary like 187. Well, this one was about the HWC earning another shot at the tag straps. With the Chosen Ones refusing "to wrestle savages" as they liked to put it and the Dark Storm nowhere to be seen, Calvin Carlton negotiated an opportunity. If Blaine beats 187 in the cage, HWC get ANOTHER shot. If not, Shaniqua gets a handcuffed Calvin ALONE in the cage for ten minutes." (CUTTO: SHANIQUA talking smack and laughing at CALVIN, who’s looking obviously and tensely nervous.) RICE: "So, this one started as the last few have…with fists FLYING! 187 took the early advantage off that, tossing Blaine headfirst into the cage a few times and delivering a pretty impressive double-underhook throw suplex. Clothesline, Clothesline, Elbow, Elbow – 187 started following that all up with some kick combos and shoulderblocks in the corner, then went for a big boot…and Blaine dodged out, 187 crotching himself awkwardly on the turnbuckles. Blaine took control after that, hitting a Back suplex and starting to work over 187’s back with a series of side and front backbreakers, a vicious sidewalk slam and nearly getting the pinfall after an Anderson Spinebuster that had Malik smiling brightly. 187 was able to battle back hitting a nasty powerslam and Powerbomb – only got a two count after that. 187 then started going on the windshield wiper routine with Blaine’s face and the cage, Hollywood getting busted open…which I’m sure Dynasty’s freaking out over if there’s any upcoming photoshoots. 187 hits a Piledriver, gets a two…and then Blaine counters a chokeslam with an Inverted STO counter. Blaine hits a Northern Lights only getting at two…Blaine CLIMBS the cage, goes for the big splash and MISSES! Carlton faints, 187 goes for the pin…AND ONLY A TWO COUNT! People are losing their mind at this point as 187 leans Blaine against the Steel Cage charges full bore for a Spear, but Blaine gets out of the way! Both men are on weary legs at this point, Calvin Carlton makes a HUGE play – throwing god knows what kind of powder into 187’s face – obviously, this tips off a huge brawl outside the cage…while Blaine hits the blinded 187 with the "THAT’s ENTERTAINMENT" BACKCRACKER for the win. Brawl outside the ring doesn’t stop until both =W= security and NFW Security break everything up, but the damage was certainly done… we got reports of Blaine at the hospital getting stitches and 187 needing 60 minutes of medical eye rinsing. And these are minor injuries compared to the BIG, BIG news coming out of the dirtsheets..." Injury Report (RICE takes a deep breath…) RICE: "Rumor has it and it’s only rumor that Joe the Plumber may be out INDEFINITELY pending another medical exam. In DC, Dorchester Stratton hit the Package Piledriver on the entrance ramp near the end of the match. Post-Match, not only was JTP unable to move his head, but he apparently passed out. So what we know right now is that he DEFNITELY suffered a Grade 2 concussion and isn’t able to compete in the near-future. The longer-lasting, possible permanent injury has to deal with his neck which suffered another singer or a complete fracture depending on the reports you read. What we know now is that JTP’s still on the roster, but we may be looking at a disappointing flameout to an illustrious 3-year career in NFW." JACK BRYANT (c) vs. DORCHESTER STRATTON RICE: "So, as security separated out HWC and Dynasty – it was DORCHESTER STRATTON who wanted to make his presence felt. Saying that he had once again RID NFW of JOSEPH THEODORE PLUMMER, he demanded a shot at WORLD CHAMPION, CASTOR STRIFE immediately – stating he knew he was in the building and he knew that JJ Deville could make it happen." (CUTTO: DEVILLE walking onto the ramp surrounded by =W= security, pointing at STRATTON…) RICE: "Of course, JJ instead belittled Dorchester asking if that was his real name or if DC was – and then said that Dorchester COULD fight for the World Title, but would first have to wrestle against JACK BRYANT and deliver the Television Championship back to the =W= Clan. After a few minutes, JACK BRYANT came out with the Television Championship and surrounded by security. He said he AGREED to the match stating that he was going to be a fighting champion, regardless of the BULL(BLEEP!) come out of Stratton or Deville’s mouth. Let’s get it on, right? RIGHT? Well, Stratton wasn’t happy and it got worse in the opening few minutes as Bryant got the upper-hand – naturally, Calvin and Bandit came out for moral support…but Dorchester wasn’t having any of it. He rolled out of the ring and against Calvin’s pleas decided not only to deride Jack Bryant on the microphone, but pretty much state he’d NEVER subject himself to wrestling for such an inferior title. HOWEVER, he’d be more than happy to take Jack Bryant out of the fed, so he could move on to BIGGER and BETTER things – namely the World Championship. So, out came Bandit…the HWC…and Jack Bryant must’ve felt like it was the same sh(bleep!) just on a different day. That was until ERIC DANE ran out with a chair, clearing out the area before DYNASTY could do any damage…and as Dorchester was backpedaling to the back, running lip service back towards the ring – AKITA HOSHI aka the wrestler formerly known as TSUNAMI emerged with a kendo stick, blasting Dorchester and the HWC repeatedly until they took off through the crowd. With the uneasy tension between Dane and Bryant in the ring, we were able to get out of the show without any JJ Deville report filings which was nice for once. So, that just about covers it for the show – ESEN subscribers, we’re gonna be taking you to some classic programming in a couple minutes. SIRIUS/XM – don’t change that dial! We’ll be taking calls for the next hour and I’ll have a couple of guests including Canada’s own Steve Radder reminiscing about his early NFW days…and a few medical experts to discuss the injury severity for JOSEPH THEODORE PLUMMER. Anyway, stick around Sirius – and ESEN viewers…have a happy holiday!"
