Remember that Code of Honor thing?

25 February 2005

Off to Valinor — 25 February 2005 Guess what? We're gonna be stupid enough to try something in its vein again. I'm not sure whether or not there will be an actual Code of Honor to follow, that'll have to be thought out by the Wise Men.
However, there will be rules in place to make sure that each and every match are wrestled under conditions that promote honorable wrestling, and punish those that don't employ those methods. (sorry Sean ;))
Details aren't ready to be explained yet...maybe some ideas can be given to us under this note...
...but you've been warned. In fact, expect many ideas to derive from Japanese feds themselves...I wouldn't know much about them, but hopefully some of you do and can send us suggestions.
--Katz Off to Valinor — 25 February 2005 And maybe to clarify more... Its not actually something that'll ever get written. It'll be more like an unspoken conduct of the locker room, and those that disgrace that are subject to some sort of punishment.
TheOriginalSE — 25 February 2005 I have no clue what you are talking about. Can you explain? Manson — 25 February 2005 It's more locker room conduct, like the sort of thing the Undertaker gets angry about all the time. Certain unspoken rules which everyone's supposed to know without being told and which everyone will likely eventually break. It will mostly derive from Japanese wrestling traditions, where things are treated more as a straight-forward sport than in America.
TH — 28 February 2005 If the code of honor is broken, does the offender have to commit ritual suicide? Ya know, since we're gonna be in Japan?
Manson — 28 February 2005 No, but you will have to face up to disciplinary actions taken by Minister of Conduct and Official NFW East Photographer Bi-Sexual Beau Michaels-Cruise.

But then again, I've handled a lot of characters in a lot of feds, so who knows. J_A, Certified schizophrenic, and owner of 1/3 of A1's registered screennames
