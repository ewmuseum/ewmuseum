Reel-to-Reel Strap Match: High Flyer vs. Brock Alyas

7 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LQJT86C — 7 July 2010 High Flyer vs. Brock Alyas. RP Max is whatever Katz deems it to be. I'll be writing this, long form (hint hint: put it on Reloaded!)
STIPULATION: Competitors are tied to opposite ends of a strap. Anything tied to the strap is considered in play and legal; meaning you can hit people with chairs if you grab one, or the ring bell, or you can even whip each other with the strap. Tiny monitors will be hooked up to each turnbuckle. Match can be won either by pinfall, or by a person touching all four turnbuckles before his opponent can touch one. As a turnbuckle is tagged, the corresponding monitor will go from static to a Castor V. Strife Productions logo.
WINNER: Receives a shot at the NFW Television Championship on the next episode of Reloaded.
SPECIAL GUEST REFEREE: Castor Strife's publicist and ring valet, BLANCHE BARNETT.
