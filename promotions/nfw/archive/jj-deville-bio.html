JJ Deville Biography Eye Color Brown Hometown Greensboro, NC Height 6'5 Weight 225 Alias JJ DeVille Looks JJ has long hair tied back in a ponytail. He has an athletic build -- tall and on the slender side, but with definition. Out of the ring, he wears Buddy Holly "nerd" glasses and button down/Polo shirts neatly tucked into dress slacks. Fed Titles NFW Television Championship Biography JJ started off in the CSWA as a total embarrassment, wearing half-shirts of outdated 80s hair metal bands and daisy duke jean shorts. He lost his first match in truly humiliating fashion to "Luscious" Lance Leisure -- his offense had no effect on a fellow rookie. JJ soon started outwardly showing his worship of Eddy Love and Troy Windham -- his two favorite wrestlers who were then a duo known as Playboys, Inc. They hired him as a "personal assistant" which meant he did their dirty work -- cleaning windows, washing and parking cars, fetching dry cleaning, etc. (He once even swept up Eddy's hair after Mike Randalls cut it in an attempt to surgically reattach it.) JJ also showed a knack for filing various frivolous lawsuits. These were largely ignored, however he used a hidden bylaw which stated that all Greensboro Champions must live within the borders of said city. JJ, being a native son, used that loophole to procure that title. But JJ soon showed an actual knack for wrestling and one unique quirk -- he had an uncanny ability to learn the moves of his opponents, seemingly on the fly, which gave him an incredibly diverse moveset. It also allowed him the element of surprise. He eventually ended up in a 1 on 1 match against Troy Windham, his former mentor. (They were briefly tag champions, only they dropped the belts because Troy had to tend after his brother's health. JJ admirally defended the belts in a 2 on 1 match but eventually succumbed.) In one of the classic matches in CSWA history, JJ pulled off one of its all-time biggest upsets and pinned his sometimes abusive hero. JJ had arrived as a burgeoning superstar and threat to win the CSWA World Title when that promotion shut. At the same time, JJ began taking classes at North Carolina State University. His fame as a wrestler allowed him to become quite popular -- he soon dubbed himself as "The Big Man on Campus." JJ majored in kiniseology (the study of human body movement) and psychology while also minoring in pre-law. However, JJ consistently tried to get back into pro wrestling but found the doors shut. He claims it was because he was blacklisted due to his associations with Troy Windham and the CSWA. Others (probably more accurately) claim it was because of his outrageous contract demands -- asking for millions of dollars, private dressing rooms with catering service and private jets. He grew incredibly bitter over these years and is taking his wrath out on the NFW roster, most of which he views as beneath a man of his talent. He has sued the entire roster several times, using the power he has as a Windham Clan co-owner of the company to make people travel against their will to testify in various depositions. His ego has also grown enormously, much to the chagrin of even his stablemates. He walks out underneath and spins, looking himself on the video screen, smirking. He then struts to the ring and threatens to sue various audience members. Gimmick Petty, vindictive, giant ego who uses various forms of manipulation and a sense of political power to get into the head's of his opponent. Strengths 1) ABILITY TO MIMIC HIS OPPONENTS -- There's no one quite like him in the ring in this regard. He has a unique ability to "steal" his opponent's various holds. This throws them off their game -- wrestlers can't train against themselves, after all! 2) UNPREDICTABLE MOVESET -- This unique ability gives JJ the chance to constantly update his moveset. He brings a little bit of every opponent he's ever had to his style. Whereas most wrestlers can use 8-12 moves, JJ can use upwards of 20-25. 3) PSYCHOLOGY -- JJ has an ability to irritate seemingly everyone. He knows this and uses it to his fullest. He makes his opponents lose their cool and become too aggressive, which he uses to his utmost ability. Weaknessees 1) HEAD GAMES -- JJ's incredibly talented. But instead, he likes to use petty head games to his advantage. This keeps him from using the most of his abilities and serves as his own distraction. (I.E. -- Trying to use his opponents moves against him whereas a better move he knows might suffice.) 2) STRENGTH -- He lacks the upper body strength that larger wrestlers have. 3) ATHLETICISM -- JJ's natural abilities as a wrestler don't match his athletic ability. He doesn't have the agility/quickness that the rest of the NFW has. Ring attire JJ comes out with an elaborate robe that spells out "DE VILLE" in an array of jewely. He wears standard tights with his interlocking initials in sparkly white on the rear. Theme Music "Beverly Hills" by Weezer
