Off to Valinor — 17 December 2008 [detail withheld] To be honest, due to the complete fallout of matchwriting responsibility from my volunteers...I'm not even sure what I want to do anymore with the SuperCrash show. Its really hard to bear down and write 4-5 matches that you were not intending to write in the first place. At the same time, it was supposed to be one of the biggest shows of the year...and it'd kill me to shortchange the show.
So where are we? I don't know. I know how I wanted each match to turn out, but being the age I am and the hours I work...its not something that's just going to happen overnight either.
So, yeah...I'm alive. And this isn't a call for people that aren't good at matchwriting to volunteer just to get this done, that's worse than shortchanging it.
I guess this is just something to let you know I'm still out here pondering our next steps. I know in-story, the Republican Party will have sued NFW for use of "Joe the Plumber" to explain the hiatus. :cool:
