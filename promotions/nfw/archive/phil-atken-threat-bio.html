Ring Entrances "Heavyweight Champion of the World" by Reverend and the Makers begins to play as out from the back saunters Phil Atken adorned in a shimmering black and gold robe. Phil power struts to the ring in a manner that leaves many of the fans confused and a little bit upset but Atken has a wide smile all the way down to the ring. He hops up onto the apron and disrobes, handing off his robe to a ring attendant then pivots on into the ring, ready for some wrestling action. Gimmick Pretty much a loser (maybe a lovable one!!!) Currently abandoned by his family Linked to THE ROOP media establishment due to family connections. It would seem that The Roop has a vested interest in the career of young middle aged veteran Philly.
