NFW Brawl 25: Norfolk, VA, 8 August 2009

8 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 25: Norfolk, VA 8 Aug 2009 Norfolk Scope, Norfolk, Virginia (seats 13,800) Monsters of the Pacific vs. Southern Remedy The Norfolk Scope was packed, stacked and sold out for a night of exciting NFW action that would be the debuts of several new superstars and NFW homegrown rookies in the late summer of 2009. The first match seemed to speak to this notion as the old era was faced with the new era. As the fans settled in, the Black Crowes’ "Remedy" brought the house to their feet… Charlie Crowe and Duane Skynyrd looking pudgy, pot-bellied and sporting 12-packs came out slapping hands and looking every day and more of their near 40-year old ages. Of course, when you need a paycheck…you’ll put your bodies on the line against anyone. ENTER: MONSTERS OF THE PACIFIC. Captain Justice & Ultrajira’s first ever appearance in the New Frontier was Fast, Furious and quite the carwreck for Crowe and Skynyrd. Y’see, while Crowe and Skynyrd weighed up to a soft and plump 415 pounds, their opponents were nearly 630 pounds of rippling muscle. Guess how this one went down? Within two minutes of the match, Crowe was unconscious out of the ring and being tended to by EMT’s after suffering a broken nose following a CJ clothesline and Ultrajira Thrust Kick. Within five minutes of the match, the bell was ringing. Captain Justice hitting his Holy Justice! Powerslam on Skynyrd to set up UJ landing a top rope splash, appropriately titled the "U-Crusher," since it’s very likely that Skynyrd’s ribs were crushed into a fine, white powder that Felix Red would be proud of. The three count followed, the fans gave a tepid cheer for the duo that just dismantled their Southern brethren. Something tells me these two could be DANGEROUS and gain an NFW following quickly…just not in the face of such local destruction on this night. ...Speaking of local destruction and someone being dangerous…CASTOR STRIFE would be out only five minutes from the end of this match. Impulse's Neverending Strife Everyone’s back from the concession stand with their calories and sugar snacks. What’s next, they wonder? Something, perhaps, to make them forget their wives, taxes, and responsibilities for but a moment. And in that moment, they happen upon the entranceway… "VENUS IN FURS" by THE VELVET UNDERGROUND Lou Reed sings of shiny boots of leather, colors made of tears, whiplash and girl-children in the dark. The screeches of the violin serenade CASTOR V. STRIFE on his way to the ring, gas mask firmly strapped to face, shimmering black rope blinking in any and all directions. Grasped tightly in his left hand is a golden statue man holding a circular emblem. BLANCHE BARNETT walks alongside him, unfazed by the crowd that spits on her strapless pea top (clearly aiming for her cleavage). "This ain’t no way ta treat tha daughter of th’ Grand Wizard of th’ KKK!" she thinks to herself. Well, you can take the bitch out of the hills, but you can’t take the hills out of the… "This is exactly why I don’t do house shows." Castor says taking off his gas mask. "Is that how you greet the director of this year’s Toronto Film Festival People’s Choice Award winning film? Let’s try that again, shall we?" Oh God, is he really walking back? He is…get ready. And one more time… "VENUS IN FURS" by THE VELVET UNDERGROUND Sorry Lou, once more about the shiny boots and such. Castor’s heading back to the ring, this time to a brutal harmony of boos. With the mic in his hand, he pauses to survey the audience before pursing his lips. "Your greeting needs work, though it’ll do. I wouldn’t expect a gaggle of suburbanite dullards to appreciate an artist of my magnitude, but that’s going to change real soon. You see, I know Impulse is in the building…" The cheers start: IM-PULSE! IM-PULSE! The man who survived an onslaught after getting the raw deal at Crash 49 is here, maybe even to shut Castor up. "That’s right, cheer him on. In fact, get him out here because I’d like a word. You heard me Impulse, I’m requesting your presence! Your girlfriend’s here, I see her front row; mostly likely pregnant with your illegitimate son. Don’t you townies use prophylactics? Or did you forget to take the second pill after 7:30?" The crowd lights up when they see Impulse walking fast down the aisle, TV title in hand. Impulse is a no-frills kind of guy, so there’s no grand entrance, no music playing. Castor motions for him. Once in the ring, he gets right up to Castor, face to face. "Ahh, Impulse, so good of you-" but Impulse grabs the mic. "Be quiet," Impulse says a lot calmer than I expected. Amazingly, Castor’s listening. "Let me get something straight," continued Impulse, "Whatever your problem with me, we’re dealing with it at Crash 50." The people are cheering. Somewhere in the back, Eddie Mayfield hates Impulse about one percent less because of his company shilling. Maybe. "But she’s not on the roster," continues Impulse, pointing to the girl in the crowd. "You don’t talk to her. You don’t yell at her. You don’t involve her in what happens in the ring." He adjusts his grip on the TV Title belt over his shoulder. "Do we understand each other?" Impulse just went to hand the microphone to Castor, but at the last minute, he dropped it on the mat. Wow. This is the closest we’ve seen to actual emotion out of the Marathon Man in almost two years. Castor picks the mic up, brushes it off. "Too close for comfort, is that it? I could leave her alone. In fact, I could even forget about you too, and that’s really why I asked you out here. You see, the people in this audience saw what I did to you at Crash. Beneath the layers of their Wal-Mart sales rack clothing, and deep within their peasant hearts, they know who the real Champion of World Television is. And I…I know who it is too. And so do you." The chants of IM-PULSE are getting louder; Castor pauses, smiling. "These people may be incapable of comprehending an artist like me, but even they know when a sequel’s on its way. So here’s the deal: you do what’s right, you hand that belt over to me. And in return, our match at Crash 50 will instead be allotted to airing scenes from my greatest pieces, including my new film, "Cry, Whore Mother, Cry." For your sake and for NFW’s, don’t make me run it on IFC." Man, don’t these people ever get tired of booing? Impulse grabs the mic again, "The short answer? No." They really like this Impulse guy, and they’re letting Castor hear it. "The long answer? NOOOOOOO." A few chuckles from the fans. "The only way you’re getting this belt is by being the better man and beating me one on one in the middle of the ring." "I thought you might say that," Castor started, "so I came prepared." Castor’s offering Impulse that golden statue now. "This is the Toronto International Film Festival People’s Choice Award. I won this last week for directing an avant garde masterpiece guaranteed to stimulate the highest of European intellect- something you and the rest of these normies would know NOTHING about! But in exchange for your compliance, not only would I GIVE YOU this trophy, the closest you’ll ever get to being on my level, but I’ll also credit you as the Dolly Grip when my film hits select theatres in New York City. Go ahead, take the statue, I already have four." Oh man, that’s pitiful. What say you, Impulse? Impulse grabs it out of Castor’s hand, and in one fluid motion, tosses it into the crowd to a chorus of cheers. Sorry Castor, that’s what you get for bringing your toys to school. "HA! You think I brought the real thing? (Points to his head and smiles) Decoy." This guy’s nuts. Impulse with the mic now… "Sure, Castor. But after Crash 50? After I beat you? Do me a favor and find someone else to obsess over." Impulses tosses Castor the mic; crowd’s still cheering the reigning TV champ. Castor, you’re up… "Well, you see the thing is-" OH SHIT! Castor popped Impulse with the microphone! Him and Blanche are stomping Impulse out like a fire! Ouch, she’s wearing heels, too. Castor grabs the belt and just LEVELS Impulse with it. Man, check out Impulse’s girlfriend Rose, she’s hanging over the guard rail screaming at Castor. Castor slides out of the ring to jaw back at her, and now Blanche is out there too. I can read his lips, he said something like "…and you’ll get to bury him too, if he shows up at Crash!" That’s harsh, man. Uh oh, the ladies are at it now! I bet they’re all like, "Don’tchoo be talkin’ to mah man and shyit!" Wow, I think Blanche literally said something along those lines. I don’t speak ‘hillbilly’ so I can’t be sure. Oh my, Rose just shoved Blanche into the ring apron! OOOOOH! CASTOR TWO-HAND SHOVED ROSE INTO THE THIRD ROW! She’s got popcorn and soda all over her! Impulse is up and he just BLEW-HIS-STACK! Holy crap, he baseball slid Castor into the railing and won’t stop throwing punches! I’ve never seen Impulse lose his cool like this, ever. This is F’ED UP! He’s got some sort of choke hold on Castor and won’t let go! Blanche’s heel kicks to the head aren’t helping matters, apparently. Rose is still down…what the hell? People are surrounding her, trying to help her out. I know Castor’s big and all, but it was just a shove. Wait a sec, I think she’s having breathing problems. Oh no! I know what this is, my brother had it. She’s having an asthma attack! The paramedics are already out; Impulse sees them and releases the hold. In the words of my former roommate: "This shit just got REAL!" Impulse is at her side with his hand on her back, and he looks like he’s directing traffic with the paramedics. There are no boos or cheers, but an extended holding of the breath. They’re putting her on a stretcher now and this DOES NOT look good. Castor’s mouth is bleeding down to his chin, and he’s just sitting there with his back to the ring smiling. All this time trying to get into Impulse’s head, and he’s finally there. Meanwhile, that belt is sitting face-up at center ring. It looks lonely, but it’s going home with someone in Boston. Brock Alyas vs. Bret Kelly With the fans coming down from the Castor/Impulse situation…Bret Kelly came to the ring first with very little fanfare, other than a loud drunk man telling him that he's 'gonna die, Eh!' Yeah, I don't know either. Brock Alyas came in with somewhat more of a reaction. The rumor is that his absence earlier in the year was drug related, which made him a hero to the NFW faithful. In fact, I believe a "HY-PO-DER-MIC!" chant just sprung up. New Frontier Wrestling: On the Brink of Class. Once the bell rang, though, it was all over. Brock looked at Bret and laughed at him, and offered him a free shot. Kelly landed a punch to the side of the head, and Brock offered him another free shot. Maybe it's time for a different approach, eh, Bret? The Canadian Heartbreaker fired another one, but Brock caught him by the wrist and forced him to his knees, where he kicked him in the head. Twice. I'm sorry, three times. Lift, and drop. Lift, and lawn dart into the corner. A few of the more daring folks down by the ring started a wave. Brock spat at them, and, thinking better of it, spat on poor Bret Kelly, too. Mister Canadian was on his hands and knees, trying to crawl... somewhere... but Brock stepped on his ankle in the center of the ring. Boy from Canadia ain't... goin'... nowhere. Except up. And down. With the EXPLODAH. And a second, just for good measure. Bret Kelly wasn't moving, but Brock leaned back against the ropes and gestured to his downed opponent, as if to say 'This is the best they've got for me?' Greg Herpin finally told him that if he didn't go for the pin, he'd get disqualified. For a second, he looked like he was gonna wallop our esteemed senior official which would do the same, but instead, he stood on Kelly's chest for the three. Ouch. Problem Child vs. Teresa Quaranta Whenever Castor Strife opens his mouth on house shows, Problem Child is usually made to suffer immediately. So with this night and PC’s incendiary promos on television leading up to the house shows, you can only imagine what he’d be up against. PC was out first awaiting his opponent and started smiling devilishly as Teresa Quaranta came out of the curtains, but uncharacteristically acting stoic all the way to the ring while PC hopped like a cracked out rabbit in the ring, grabbing his crotch and then making a V-shape with his fingers, popping his tongue through and wriggling it around like a live fish in Snow White’s piehole. (NFW Season 2 reference, don’t fire us Eddie!) PC overly confident or ignorant from either not knowing TQ’s title history or his success out in the World’s Finest New Era of Next Generation Prodigies left him open to a vicious opening start from the stark former National Champion. TQ seemed focused and leaning a little towards some nasty tactics that had the fans slightly booing the early-going as she fishhooked PC in the mouth and slapped him around. PC, not to be underestimated these days, fought back quickly and very angered by the disrespect from TQ after she spit on him and asked if this reminded him of his mother. From there, the fans got on their feet several times as the hard-hitting, fast-paced cruisers seemed to amp up further and further as the match wore on. Nipple Twisters, crotchshots were on display…although, sort of in the reverse of how you’d probably want to see them. Late in the match, PC took control with a vicious Stun Gun followed by a Springboard Elbow Drop right to the chest of TQ. A cocky cover allowed TQ to kickout, PC went for a Standing Moonsault and TQ countered by putting her knees up. TQ hit a bulldog and then awaited PC to rise to his feet, went for a Hurricanrana… …and took a PC Powerbomb instead. The fans booed as PC celebrated wildly in the ring, bringing up TQ for his A.D.D. Spinning Tombstone finish… Only to find himself cinched, locked and pinned via a Victory Roll. PC went ballistic, TQ escaped from the ring immediately as the crowd roared and taunted Problem Child as he angrily stayed in the ring until security "escorted" him out. James von Doom vs. P. King Duk Duk waited in the ring for his opponent, and he was immediately the most popular person in the arena. Why? "In Fiction" by Isis. James von Doom, that's why. A chorus of boos filled the place, along with a chant of "VAR-GA-SUCKS!" Of course, von Doom waved to the peeps and blew kisses and generally behaved like an ass. And of course, they ate it up. ....Ew. So we get to the match itself, and it's pretty much a dismemberment. I don't think Duk got a single piece of offense in. Four minutes of brutality, during which about a third of the audience got up for a smoke or a drink, or a snort or a shot, or a pill, or a hallucinogenic suppository. This *is* the NFW, after all. I stayed put because Cojones Mercado told me he was watching me. Von Doom ended the match mercifully quickly with the Commercial Break, and the fans who remained, and the ones who returned - applauded at the sight. But he wasn't done. Von Doom went out of the ring and grabbed a microphone from Lee-Baby, and stepped back inside. "This is what you offer me?" he asked, "This is the best you've got?" Boos. "I tell you, if I'd known it would've been this easy to dismantle this pathetic company, I would've waited until the odds were less stacked against you - like if I was paralyzed or something." Wow. They really hate him. "Or are these so - called wrestlers just drunk and stoned? Are they too busy picking their asses and eating cheetos to come out here and shut me up?" And he stopped and smiled. "Of course not... they're all afraid of me. Because they know I tell the truth. And all you so - called heroes, and this company itself... will feel my wrath!" He shrugged his shoulders and threw the microphone at Lee - Baby. C'mon, man, what did Lee ever do to you? He's just trying to pay for a sixth annullment. Of course, von Doom stopped in his tracks the moment a military - style drumbeat came over the sound system. The fans waited for half a second, until they heard the familiar "LEFT! LEFT! LEFT RIGHT LEFT!" before their reaction turned a complete 180. James von Doom vs. Impulse (c) Revolution, baby... that's my sound... Like Queen V said, the NFW Television Champion was in the building. Impulse was dressed for a match, and had his title belt wrapped snugly around his waist as he walked to the ring. Lord, I hope Cojones isn't reading right now, but I'm glad to see this kid. He walked past Lee - Baby and shook the poor guy's hand, and took the microphone from him. Yeah, we've got another referee out here. I think what's about to happen is about to happen. "You've been talking for weeks, James," said Impulse, "and even though the actual movers and shakers in New Frontier Wrestling haven't seen it necessary to respond, we're here, and we're listening." "Are drugs and alcohol a problem here?" asked Impulse, to a rousing cheer. I don't think the fans here are cheering what they think they're cheering. "Maybe, maybe not. For my part, whatever people want to do in the privacy of their own homes isn't the business of the police, the government, Shepherd Mayfield, or you, James. Felix Red may have been a different story - Felix may have needed the intervention." "But you, James? Is that what it's come down to? Accusing the heroes and villains and wolves and sheep in this company of being on drugs?" Von Doom looked annoyed at the interruption, and wouldn't you be? He's being told he's an idiot in so many words, and he didn't think to hold onto his microphone to interrupt the kid. "Somehow I feel like I've seen that movie before, sir. But let's get past that." Impulse took off his leather jacket and dropped it to one of the attendants. And his T-shirt. "You're insulting the integrity of New Frontier Wrestling, and that's something I don't let stand." And there go the fans. They've been waiting for this, I think. "So whatever the story is, James - whether you're a million zillion times better at this than anyone else, ever, or you're a weak, pathetic talent that everyone should gang up on from the start, or you're a master manipulator trying to convince the company - or yourself - that you're more than a footnote in the NFW... there's only one question on my mind." He stopped talking to unhook the NFW TV Title belt from his waist, and handed it to the official. "How brave can you be with a real opponent across the ring from you?" With that, Impulse handed the microphone back to Lee - Baby, and asked for the bell. I like that, the kid's got manners. But von Doom is now arguing with the referee over this, and he's leaving the ring to a ton of boos. Impulse caught him by the ropes, and asked him what's up, and I think-- is he actually trying to tell us that he just wrestled a full match and that he's at a disadvantage? P. King Duk is a good guy but that was barely a warm-up! Impulse turned around to walk away - and von Doom with a hammering forearm to the champion's back! Impulse dropped to the mat, and by the time he got up, von Doom had sprung to the top rope and dropped him again with a double axe handle! I don't know if the title is on the line or not, but von Doom just went for a cover and almost got a two! Scoop of Impulse and a slam, and von Doom is off the ropes with a legdrop! Another cover, and another two count! Von Doom stood up and raised his hands in mock victory, and gestured for Impulse to get up. The TV Champion was up on his hands and knees, and von Doom shove-kicked him over, to the incredible booing of the people. I'm not ashamed to say I booed along with them. Another kick--and Impulse caught his foot! He spun one leg up and snared von Doom behind the knee with it, and scissor-kick-something-or-other'd von Doom to the mat, and rolled through with his shin in the back of von Doom's knee and his wrists busily twisting von Doom's foot to a side that it probably shouldn't be bending! The Virus lunged with every free body part and grabbed the ropes. Now the people stood again, and applauded. Impulse was up and gesturing to von Doom to 'come and lock up.' Wow. For some reason he didn't seem quite as enthused this time. Von Doom circled, and when Impulse went to lock up he jumped back. Boos, as you can imagine. Second verse, same as the first, only this time, von Doom turned to the crowd and raised his hands in victory. Bad idea, sir. Impulse latched onto him with a full nelson, kicked his knees out from under him, and forced the self - proclaimed Virus' face into the mat. Quick flip, and a cover, ONE... TWO... Kickout by von Doom. Scoop and a whip into the ropes, and an elbow to the stomach doubled von Doom over, and Impulse with a scissors kick dropped him again... and there he goes. Von Doom has left the building, and Impulse wins by countout. I'd love to see what would happen if he didn't have that option. American Panda vs. La Estrella 100% There are moments in life you never forget. For many people in Norfolk, Virginia…it was their first glimpse of the new NFW sensation American Panda. La Estrella 100% a renowned lower-card veteran of the New Frontier found himself across the ring. Certainly after the Ric Flair meets the Wolfman entrance, nobody knew what to expect from a wrestler coined as "The Walking Contradiction" in his press sheet. Did we mention he’s 6’6, 280? Yeah…it seems that on this night, NFW was sure to show the oncoming of crazed beasts into what had become a ‘cruiser’ heavy fed. Estrella opened up with a flurry of kicks, chops and smashes that just about did nothing as he ran full on freight-train style into a huge big boot from Panda. Panda seemed to keep the repertoire basic from that point forward, landing a bevy of slams, elbows and kneestrikes before breaking out a spinning Palm Thrust to Estrella’s head that left him unconscious on the mat. Panda then grabbed Estrella in a double clutch Neck-Hang, sprayed mist into his eyes and if that weren’t enough…a sitout Chokebomb followed as did the three count. If anyone’s wondering about a wild card entry to win the Bunkhouse Stampede at Crash 50…this could be your man...or should I say thing? CUATRO-X vs. High Flyer & Legion The main event for Norfolk was intended to be a tag team attraction hyping up the War Games match to take place at Crash 50. Cuatro Equis against High Flyer and Legion. Now, most of you would just think that this was a purported job to screw over High Flyer. …most of you probably don’t know Legion then. Legion came out with Luci4 after Flyer with the fans booing lustily, while Cuatro-X got a big roar from the crowd. With the intestinal fortitude and lack of sanity between each of these athletes, one could argue this would last 45 minutes or 4.5 minutes. Unfortunately, for the fans for the Norfolk…it was not on the side they were hoping for. Flyer and Dos were the first to start the match, Dos getting the advantage with a some hiptosses and dropkicks. Legion avoided a tag from Flyer, but managed to sneak in the ring after a Dos back body drop on Flyer…to nutshot Dos from behind. What a teammate this guy is. FutureQuis knowing the script, quickly vaulted into the ring and tackled Legion. Within two minutes of the match, we had a pier-six brawl with the ref losing complete control of the match and then his consciousness as Luci4 plowed him with a chairshot from behind. This opened the floodgates from the locker room appropriately, Kooter Johnson and Cameron Cruise racing down to ringside and helping their captain High Flyer. With the beatdown underway, the only saving grace came flying out. Unicorn Mask rumbled into the ring with a Singapore Cane, swinging wildly and connecting on Kooter and then Cameron. He then smashed Luci, but that snapped Legion into action…taking a chair and bashing Unicorn Mask in the back with it, then repeatedly slamming it into his back and ribs until he was tackled by Cuatro Equis. With everyone brawling outside the ring, Flyer took advantage of the situation and hooked in the "Cold Snow" Elevated Bahsten Crab on Unicorn Mask, the fans booing lividly as security, refs and some staffworkers had to race to the ring and pull him off of Unicorn Mask. Meanwhile, the rest of the War Games competitors brawled through the crowd and to the backstage area where Scope officials later handed Eddie Mayfield a 4-figure bill for their destruction proudly displayed on the FRONTEIRtron to markouts. Broken mirrors, doors, light fixtures and production equipment littered the backstage until the wrestlers were torn apart due to their lack of stamina. …and so we left the Virginia borders and made our way further north…to Connecticut. And for anyone wondering, Luci 4’s interference actually gave a disqualification victory to Cuatro-X.
