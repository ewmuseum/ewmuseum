Bobby Jack Windham Biography Hometown Sweetwater, TX Height 6'6 Weight 255 Looks Bobby Jack has a dirty blonde mop-top and a scruffy beard. This is not done out of any concern from style, but more that he just likes it that way. He's stocky in build and looks farmboy strong. Biography The 21-year-old nephew of Troy and Mark Windham, Bobby Jack's the latest star to come from Sweetwater. Bobby Jack was an All-State athlete in football, wrestling and track at Sweetwater Area High School and was named by the Abeline Reporter-News as its Texan Athlete of the Year two years in a row. Bobby Jack accepted a wrestling scholarship to Texas Tech University, where he lost only twice in his career, both in the NCAA finals his freshman and sophomore year. Bobby Jack had to sit out the Beijing Olympics because of arthroscopic knee surgery. Instead of waiting around, he's decided to be the latest in his lineage to become a pro wrestler. Wrestling Style Bobby Jack wrestles a strong/amateur style of wrestling. He's incredibly strong but also surprisingly agile and quick. He likes to use a variety of simple, traditional moves (and suplexes) but will throw in a strike or two, but usually nothing more than a slap or a punch. If he's able to get an opponent on the ground, he likes to try and use submission holds. He will never climb to the ropes. He's definitely prone to showing off his strength to a disadvantage, is arrogant with his mat skills and can definitely be out-paced by smaller and faster wrestlers. Finisher Move The Sign of the Cross Finisher Description Running Razor's Edge Finisher Setup Move Sweetwater Dreams Setup Description Clothesline From Hell Other Moves 1. West Texan Bulldog 2. Olympic Slam 3. Scorpion Deathlock 4. Deadlift Suplex (suplex from when opponent is on the mat) 5. Belly-to-Belly Suplex 6. West Texan Stampede (Oklahoma Stampede, but no self-respecting Texan will ever give any credit to Oklahoma) 7. Rolling German Suplexes 8. Ankle Lock 9. Jumping Piledriver 10. SlackKnife (Snap Reverse Neckbreaker -- Troy's finisher.) Ring Entrances Bobby Jack walks to the ring slowly after a few seconds into Amazing Grace. On the ramp, he'll make the sign of the cross and then will drop to a knee in prayer. He'll then converse with God for several seconds, before walking slowly to the ring. Gimmick Christian Jock Snob, in the vein of Tim Tebow, but to another level. He LOVES prosletyzing his views. Anyone who he is not aligned with he has dubbed an unpure sinner. He believes it is his duty to make everyone adopt Christ as their savior -- and, not just that, but especially his own strident, extreme views of Christianity. (He's not that far from protesting the death of soldiers because of the growing support of gay marriage.) Strengths AMATEUR ABILITY -- He's one of the most heralded amateur-style wrestlers of the past decade. He's nearly flawless as a mat wrestler. POWER -- One of the strongest pound-for-pound wrestlers a live. A total freak in the gym. ATHLETICISM -- On top of his strength, he's also incredibly fast/quick for a man of his size. He was a nationally ranked high school decathalete, showing what a well-rounded athlete hs is. BRAWLING -- He's not afraid to slug it out in the ring, having spent a lot of his time as a playground bully in Sweetwater. Weaknessees PROSLETYZING -- Bobby Jack uses wrestling to spread the Gospel. And, in fact, he does this WHILE wrestling quite a bit. When he believes he has completely defeated his opponent, he will frequently grab a microphone and pray for the destruction of his foe. Naturally, this gives his opponents time to rest and heal. CRUISERWEIGHTS -- There are not huricanranas or planchas in NCAA wrestling. His lack of experience against high flyers really shows. FRUSTRATION -- He gets more and more flustered the more his opponents kick out. He's used to dominating in college. The pros are another level entirely. He gets frustrated not being able to just purely crush people. CROWD -- Bobby Jack thinks that he's doing God's work, and that he should be idolized as a role model/hero. When the crowd turns on him, it really distracts him. Ring attire In the ring, he wears wrestling headgear and a singlet, usually in Texas Tech colors.
