NFW news: Brandon Youngblood Released

31 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon Youngblood Released Posted by Natrone Rice on 31 Mar 2006 Fool us once, shame on you. Fool us twice, shame on us. If Brandon Youngblood ever gets back in New Frontier Wrestling, we all may have to change this saying for good. Confirming reports that had been going on since the Pacific Beach brawl ending in 4 arrests, Brandon Youngblood's contract was nullified 24 hours after the Hulk-A-Thon. Quentin Sullivan noted several reasons behind the decision, "Brandon...he's got alot of misguided talent right now, and after two attempts to help him maximize the potential -- alot of wrestlers backstage, as well as West Conference staff just didn't feel like he belonged. He's young, emotional and at times abrasive. Not to mention, the business with Calypso in ACW...basically had Beau Michaels calling my office every day to request a match with him..." ... "Just kidding on the last one. Brandon just couldn't fit in here, or find the motivation to do so, we've got people calling every day willing to cut off an arm to get a shot at the Ultratitle...which was the direct opposite of him, so we made the move." Youngblood's standing position has been replaced currently by Malik Roland, although Youngblood only garnered 5 points in his 3 matches here in the fed...not making a dent anyway.
