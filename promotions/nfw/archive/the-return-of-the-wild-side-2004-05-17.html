The return of the Wild Side.

17 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Al! — 17 May 2004 Ok so I need a better name for the segment.. sue me. Here's the skinny, I'm shelving Campbell for a week or two to let him fully recover from his injuries as I'm tired of writing him into asskickings and there are a bunch of storylines I need to either set up or knock down that need him out of the ring for a bit.
That being said, he's still under contract and Sully expects him to be productive so I'm planning on bringing back an interview show along the lines of the Human Highlight Reel or, more appropriately, my favorite.. Piper's Pit. First show, next card.
I would like to get one or two guests lined up for the show and this is a perfect way to tie some feuds together or give them some added oomph. You can expect I'll be doing some serious mouthing off too.
I'd like to request Lindsay Troy to be one of the people on the show as that's a no-brainer. But that still means we could get one or maybe two more before I rant.
If interested please reply and we can work things out tween us. Regards Al Every wrestling show needs a little Springer. QueenOfTheRing — 17 May 2004 I'm game. Al! — 17 May 2004 Lindsay Troy is confirmed. I have a request for the involvement of the Fem Phenom Nakita DuBov
If the handler is interested please reply. I'll be in touch. Al TSiegel — 17 May 2004 I sent a message, but why not, this is just as fast. The Cruise Brothers along with Cameron's wife/manager Mercedes Devon would like to schedule a show.
