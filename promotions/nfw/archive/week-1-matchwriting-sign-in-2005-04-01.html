1 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WestPREZ — 1 April 2005 Please respond to this thread with what match you're writing. GENERAL RULES: Keep in mind that any matches not called will most likely get a one-two paragraph review in Sportscenter form, no matter how much you RP. With NFW expanding and my role changing, don't expect your match to be 7 pages and satisfactory every week unless you write it. When the playoffs arrive, things will change in that regard.
ANYONE can write a match. If you are not writing your OWN match, get two thumbs up from either me/Mike, or the two handlers. Your own match just requires me/Mike.
Responding to this thread does NOT have to mean you are writing a full match. It means you are RESPONSIBLE for the match you've selected. It is up to YOURSELF on length/details/etc.
If you are not comfortable with the person writing your match, contact myself or [name withheld] immediately...and we'll try to rectify things in accordance with your concerns.
This will keep things organized, and if you have questions about deadlines, I suggest you read the Week 1 Lineup Note in this forum.
