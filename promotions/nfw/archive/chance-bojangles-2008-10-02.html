Doesn't make big decisions without it. If he thinks that a higher power should make the decision for him, he'll roll the dice. Literally. Carries two golden dice in his pocket. Snake eyes means "no".
Ring Attire: Black Olympic style wrestling costume. Cowboy boots. One black elbow pad and his wrists taped up.
Looks: Shaggy brown hair. Shaggy full length beard. Scar across his right eye and it's been replaced with a glass eye. The glass eye is a dice displaying one dot. He wears an akubra, which is an Australian style bushman's hat. It's tan in colour. He wears a black t-shirt and a tan suede coat with a fluffy wool collar. Like a coat you might see a hunter wear. He wears jeans which are faded and dirty and half calf length cowboy boots.
Ring Entrance: He walks down smoking a cigarette and stomps it out right at the ring steps, climbs up and removes his hat by resting it on a turnbuckle and slips between the ropes. Waits for his opponent.
