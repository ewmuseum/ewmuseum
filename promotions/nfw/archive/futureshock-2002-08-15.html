NFW FUTURESHOCK, 15 August 2002

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FUTURESHOCK 15 Aug 2002 Tsongas Arena, Lowell, Massachusetts (seats 7,800) Deadly Service (FADEIN: To the baggage claim area of Logan Airport. Early morning. The a.m. sun shines brightly through the huge windows, overlooking landing airplanes onto the tarmac, and the parking areas on the other side of the airport. People slowly make their way down two big escalators and wait near their appointed carousels, looking up at delivery monitors to make sure they're in the right place. Out of the crowd, we see SHANE SOUTHERN, duffel bag slung over his shoulder, leather jacket, jeans and tennis shoes step off of the escalator, then put sunglasses on a few seconds after getting caught full-on from the blinding sun glare. He makes his way over to 4D, and stands, hands in pockets next to an unmoving carousel. Suddenly, claxons blare, as the belt begins to move, and spit out luggage. SHANE looks over at a set of metal suitcases and goes to reach for them, when a gloved hand reaches into the shot and grabs the handle first! The camera pulls back, as SOUTHERN stands face to face with a unimpressive figure – a man in a black suit, black tie, and sunglasses, face unimportant.) MAN: "Shane Southern, right?" SOUTHERN: (Squinting at the man) "Yah… What’s it to ya?" MAN: (Grabbing the luggage and pulling it off of the belt and setting it in front of himself) "I’m Dusty, your driver. Sorry I was so late. Traffic, you know…" SOUTHERN: (Letting his guard lower) "Ah.. yeah.. Yeah… whatever. That’s all of it, let’s get goin’ then." (The man nods and starts wheeling the luggage towards the street. They exit through the big glass doors, as SHANE stops in his tracks, blinking quickly! The camera goes around him, as the driver pops the hatch and puts the bags into – BLOODHUNTS ‘Death Limo!’) SOUTHERN: "What the (BLEEP!) is this s’pposed ta be? This some sorta’ JOKE?" DRIVER: "Not that I know of, sir… I have orders to get you to the arena, (Digs in his pocket) and also orders to drive really slow, for some reason. I don’t get that…" SOUTHERN: "Well, for some reason I think I got an idea ‘bout that. (SOUTHERN reaches into his pocket and pulls out a twenty) Take this and make sure I get there and NOT slowly." (SOUTHERN slaps the bill into the driver’s hand, who smiles and takes it.) DRIVER: "No problem, I’ll get you there in NO time." (The driver slams the hatch shut and briskly walks around and gets in the car. SOUTHERN uneasily climbs into the open-topped car, and settling into the backseat, frowning. He looks over on the seat next to him, and picks up a garment bag. He unzips it, and there’s a suit in there – the same make that Kennedy had on the day he was shot! SHANE curses aloud and throws the suit down quickly, as the car starts off and peels into traffic. FADE TO BLACK.) Deadly Dealings V/O: "We got a deal?" (FADEIN: BLOODHUNT standing in a phone booth outside a gas station, listening to a computer alterated voice…he looks out to a car where MISS AMERICA aka EMILY is sitting in the passenger seat, napping. He then looks to another car, this one unmarked with the driver wearing a baseball cap and sunglasses, and strangely talking on a cell phone…BLOODHUNT shifts his body and is now directly facing the car…) BLOODHUNT: "Yeah, we got a deal." V/O: "No funny business?" BLOODHUNT: "I’m not a funny guy." V/O: "Cute. DON’T mess with me…(laughs) Well, I’m glad you noticed my car. You don’t seem to happy, right now. I’m sure you’re wondering who I am at this point." BLOODHUNT: "Well, I’m sure I’ll find out soon enough. Won’t I?" V/O: "Don’t try anything, ‘Hunt. Bring the merchandise to the ring. I wouldn’t want to see anything you care for get hurt. Like that little lady of yours." BLOODHUNT: "You touch her, you’re dead." V/O: "You just make sure none of your pals show up tonight, ‘Hunt. Like I said, I just want YOUR money. If you don’t bring it, your girl gets it. If you do bring it, she doesn’t. And then, maybe I just might help you with Southern. You walk out there, you wrestle Shane Southern, you don’t know I’m coming. You bring any friends, or anyone you can hire…it’s OVER for your girl. This goes the way I want, or you pay a higher price. See you soon, ‘Hunt." (Suddenly the car in front of BLOODHUNT peels out in reverse!) BLOODHUNT: "You don’t know how much I’m looking forward to it." V/O: "Don’t be stupid, ‘Hunt. Only once you give me what I want…will you even have a chance of getting what you want." (The phone clicks and the dial tone starts. BLOODHUNT hangs up the phone and walks out of the booth towards his car. He climbs in the car and closes the door, waking up his companion.) EMILY (while rubbing her eyes): "Where are we?" BLOODHUNT: "At a gas station." EMILY: "We got gas an hour ago." BLOODHUNT: "I had a phone call." EMILY: "About what?" BLOODHUNT: "Don’t worry about it. (‘HUNT looks over to his girlfriend, who doesn’t look pleased with that answer) It was business, that’s it." EMILY (now a little less angry): ‘Was that Monty? (while rolling her eyes) Ugh! Why won’t he leave you alone…" BLOODHUNT (sensing an out, giving a half-laugh): "Yeah…yeah, that good ‘ol Monty. Always coming up with some crazy plan…" (‘HUNT smiles and MISS AMERICA pauses before leaning back in her chair. MISS AMERICA: "Yeah, well I hope he doesn’t do anything stupid. I swear, I’ll kick him back to Cuba." BLOODHUNT: "Yeah…me too." (FADE TO BLACK) Black Ice V/O: "expect most of this nasty weather to continue through next week. Back to you, Bob." However, they are looking for a Caucasian Male, over 6 feet tall and 200 pounds last seen at a rest stop along I-95 in Greensboro, North Carolina. The FBI believes the man drives a white Honda and have not ruled out the possibility that he is ARMED and DANGEROUS…if you have any information regarding this case, please contact your local authorities immediately." QUENTIN SULLIVAN, his black Armani shirt unbuttoned at the top is hot, bothered and shouting instructions at any NFW employee that crosses his path. MILES: "There’s a genius in that man…" SULLIVAN: "Shut the hell up before I hit you." MILES: "(laughs) No seriously man. Under that White Zombie vibe..." SULLIVAN: "Jesus Christ…" MILES: "I wonder if he sleeps in a coffin…" SULLIVAN: "MILES!" MILES (taken aback): "Geez, Quent - you don’t get anywhere with an attitude like THAT. Anyway, I just had a little mano y mano session with OUR new friend, Michael." SULLIVAN: "Our?" MILES: Yes, OURS. We had a heart to heart, man." SULLIVAN: "I don’t even know if he knows he was here." MILES: "Probably not." (SULLIVAN laughs and shakes his head, but all of a sudden the door blasts open and VIVIAN RYCONIK wearing a black business blouse and holding a clipboard rushes in.) SULLIVAN: "Vivian, what’s going on?" VIVIAN: "Why isn’t my father wrestling tonight?! Here I am going over the backstage checklist, and lo and behold there’s no special requests from my father for his dressing room. So, I’m like, THAT’S WEIRD. So, then I look down to the match schedule and whaddya know - there is NO match for MY FATHER!" MILES: "Now just hold on—" VIVIAN: "NO! You tell me NOW, why YOU took him off this card! MILES (with a sheepish grin): "Umm…Viv…babe, did your father happen to mention that he’s the one that NEVER signed the contract." VIVIAN (fumbling her clipboard): "What? W--what are you talking about?" SULLIVAN: "Wait, is he even in the building? We didn’t even think he had left Lake County." VIVIAN: "Oh my god. What’s going on? Why wouldn’t he? I-I-I don’t know, I just spoke to him on the phone. Hold on. (VIVIAN takes out her cell phone and pushes a few buttons) That’s weird, he called from this area code…but not the Arena." SULLIVAN: "Well, I don’t know what to tell you Viv. We weren’t sure what to do and we haven’t heard from your father, so we thought it would be in our best interests not to go forward." MILES: "Yeah, Montezuma and his crew are already cramming requests down our throats airtime to basically run your father down for backing out…" VIVIAN: "Oh my god. This can’t be happening. I know this is your father, but I don’t think you know what’s on that contract." VIVIAN: "Just get that contract to me and I’ll make sure my father gets here!" He throws it on the table and pulls out a cigarette and lights it.) MILES: "You know, Vivian probably would put up a scrum in there, if she knew about that hair stipulation…" SULLIVAN: "Craig, you’re not going to let her sign that contract are you?" MILES: "Don’t worry, Sully. I gotta good feelin’ about tonight. If this was Rick’s planned final night, I don’t think he’d miss the party." MILES: "Its gotta be the people you meet…(MILES laughs and pats SULLIVAN on the back) Alright, boss – it’s SHOWTIME." SULLIVAN: "Let’s do this." MILES smirks, nods and blows out a cloud of smoke) MILES (while closing the door behind him): "SCOTTY! FIRE THE TORPEDOES!" (FADE TO BLACK) Welcome to the Show!
