TH — 6 July 2005 Before anyone says anything about this being a shill for an outside league, this was Katz's idea :)
Anyway, as most of you know, I'm getting this TEAM thing together, adn as most of you could surmise, I'm going to need a lot of characters for this thing. I wanted to get a good cross section of folks, not only from A1 and FW, but from the EWN and PTC circles too. I know a bunch of you guys out West are from those circles.
If you read the FAQ, I made note that I woudl try to incorporate angle fed style into this but after speaking to Dan from LoC, [name withheld] and Katz, I figured that it would be very hard to do that without letting the guys book their own matches. I've pretty much made the decision that at least for season 1, the league would solely be RP-based.
So, would you guys still be interested in handling a character in TEAM if that was the case? I'd like to know, because I really want some different characters participating in this league, like Angelo Deville, Rook Black, Alias and Seymour Almasy to name a few. But I'd have to know if you'd be okay with it being strictly RP.
All feedback is welcome. I won't bite you, I swear. Really. ;)
