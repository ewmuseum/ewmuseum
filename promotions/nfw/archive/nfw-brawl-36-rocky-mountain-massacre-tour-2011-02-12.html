NFW Brawl 36: ROCKY MOUNTAIN MASSACRE TOUR!, 12 February 2011

12 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 36: ROCKY MOUNTAIN MASSACRE TOUR! 12 Feb 2011 RimRock Auto Arena, Billings, Montana (seats 12,000) Welcome to the Show! ANNOUNCER V/O: "The following presentation is a JOINT production provided worldwide by the EXTREME SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT NETWORK and SIRUIS/XM SATELITTE RADIO." (MUSICUP: 'BADGE' - Cream) (FADEIN: RimRock Auto Arena, Billings, Montana. Fans are walking outside cheering raucously...CUTTO: ROOK BLACK looking up at LEGION on the FRONTIERtron nervously. CUTTO: BOBBY JACK WINDHAM delivering a sermon to the crowd. CUTTO: PROBLEM CHILD getting the tar beaten out of him by CASTOR STRIFE.) NATRONE RICE V/O: "WHATUP, ENN EFF DOUBLE YOU! This is Natrone Rice and we're making another stop on the Rocky Mountain Massacre Tour for a special 60-minute edition of the NFW BRAWL!" (CUTTO: NATRONE RICE backstage at the arena, working a modified desk jockey area for radio purposes. As usual, he's all smiles and looking ready to go...) RICE: "We're cuttin' the fat tonight for the broadcast as we've got some highlights, clips and an encore showing of Bobby Jack Windham taking on Lord Coyner Pollard! But that's not even the main event as Castor Strife faced Cameron Cruise FOR REALS this time with the Television Championship on the line! And we're going to get to that, but we're not missing a beat as we dive into our first recap match of the night that's surrounding the ominous, mysterious and downright befuddling National Championship situation. For all we know, that title belt's still somewhere in North Korea and part of the upcoming Homefront video game storyline...and before Rook Black answers any questions on how he's getting that belt back, he had alot on his plate tonight as he THOUGHT he'd be dealing with a tag team match. Instead, Legion flipped the switch on the whole situation...so, let's check this out!" (FADEOUT) Rook Black/High Flyer vs. Doc Curiosty/Legion It’s with a bright shiny red fire hydrant that Legion clobbers High Flyer, in his guise as "The Snow Knight". High Flyer collapses, and Legion follows up with one more for good measure, slamming the hydrant down between Flyer’s shoulders. Turning to the camera, Legion grins, "Can you count, Rook?" he asks, "Because I’ve just subtracted one tag partner from your team, which gives us a different outlook on things, doesn’t it? Myself, I’d count your chances at less than zero." Legion lets go of a cackle and walks off with a smirk. - Meanwhile at ringside, Rook Black had just made his entrance, and looks upon this carnage displayed on the big screen and says, "Oh snap." Doc Curiosity makes his entrance, and with the usual shenanigans accuses Rook of hiding High Flyer somewhere around the ring just so he could ambush Doctor Curiosity. Ironically, Legion ambushes Rook from his hiding place under the ring as Curiosity confronts Rook, and this "tag match" begins. Cheered from time to time, yes, Doctor Curiosity cannot resist a cheap shot, so for quite some time, he and Legion clobber Rook before the ref sends Legion to the apron and the match can START. DING DING DING! Doc proudly swaggers over to Rook and bounces his head off the turnbuckle for a bit, and does a ten-punch-count-along, only it doesn’t get all the way to ten punches, since when the crowd shouts "Nine!" Doc stops. … Rook begins to show signs of life, so Doc tags in Legion and we wind up with a series of quick tags that keep Rook from mounting much of any offense, and when he almost looks like he’s going to get somewhere, they just cheat a little and things are back to normal. Almost by default, Rook’s occasion bursts off offense are met with cheers, as he’s really trying hard to get anything working. Dizzy and staggering, Rook hits a counter overhead belly to belly suplex that actually sends Legion sailing over the corner, crashing into Doctor Curiosity, to land in a heap on top of Eegor. Momentarily not getting the holy crap beat out of him, Rook Black lifts a feeble arm into the air, to the crowd’s delighted cheers. Then he hits the three jerks with a plancha. It was great. He slid Legion into the ring, locked on CROSSBOW, and before a submission was even a remote possibility, Doc fixed that right up, and it became more "punish Rook for living" business. BUT THEN Well, I don’t want to spoil it, but I’m thinking of a number between 12 and 14, and it comes with a sock full of quarters. Okay, I’ll tell you, it was Thirteen, who hit Doctor Curiosity upside the head with the bright white blackjack, prompting legion to jump down from the apron and slink away and prompting Rook Black to stand there and stagger like someone about to be hit by Doc’s finisher. DING DING DING! DQ! Doc and Legion .. win? Rook shakes out the cobwebs, and sees 13 standing over Curiosity who is in a small puddle of bloody bloody blood. Rook launches himself at 13, but 13 spins around as he dodges to the side, smacking Rook with the Blackjack right in the forehead, causing a tear in the sock and spilling coins all over the ring. Rook goes all "TIMBER" and collapses. Let that be a lesson to you. Well, except for Legion who had escaped the carnage and has a microphone, smiling and letting the mysterious 13 and the world know... "You do not fool me...and soon enough should it suit me, I will lift the wool off all these..." Legion points to the audience and then the ring, "sheep's eyes." Easy Bounty (CUTTO: Back in the locker room, ‘JUST’ MARK is standing by with PROBLEM CHILD, who’s donning spiked/receding hair and a t-shirt that reads ‘CHEAP HEAT COMMUNITY COLLEGE Class of ‘98’) MARK: For those of you listening on Sirius/XM or tuning in on ESEN, I’ve got a… (PC uses thumb and forefinger to pick out a snot, flicks it onto Mark’s jacket) MARK: …ummm, special guest. The latest member of the Windham Clan, Problem-(interruption) PC: Whaddya mean LATEST? I’ve been running with these dudes since FOREVER! Anybody whose anybody knows that when you talk about Problem Child, you’re talking about a very close, PERSONAL FRIEND of Troy Windham’s. So when he showed up in San Antonio to kick the ever-loving PISS out of Cameron Cruise, man…he didn’t even have to CALL ME. I just KNEW, like best friends always know when the other needs them. It’s like dolphin sonar, or when twins tap into each other’s thoughts. The BEST FRIEND BAT SIGNAL went up, ya know? MARK: I think we get it. But how exactly are YOU assisting the Windham Clan? PC: Well Mark, I’m what you’d consider a SELF-STARTER. Troy and Mark and Bobby, they don’t have to TELL ME to do something. I just know what they want automatically and I DO IT! So I took it upon myself to make up a list… (reaches into pocket, pulls out a scroll of paper that rolls down to the waist) …of ALL you MOTHER-F[BLEEP!] who did wrong by me and the Windhams over the years. Don’t worry, I checked it twice, I know who’s been naughty and nice, and if you’re on this list the Windham Clan’s gonna be ON YOUR NUTS like PUBIC LICE! HAHA! YEAH BABY! YOU F[BLEEP]KED WITH THE WRONG DOJO! JTP, Dan Ryan, Impulse, Rook, Castor Strife, I’m calling ALLLLL YOU F[BLEEP] out one at a time, two at a time, IT DOESN’T MATTER! MARK: Aren’t you a little worried calling these individuals out, when you’re standing here BY YOURSELF? PC: Worried? When the BIG BAD BANDIT has my back? Nah, YOU need to worry. I’m runnin’ around here stealin’ iPods, throwin’ people’s clothes in the shower, taking a dump in female lockers…and no one’s sayin’ SH[BLEEP!]. Cause it’s WHATEVER, WHENEVER, WHOEVER, HOWEVER- you feel me? This is how we’re LIVING LIFE in the Windham Clan. Sure I take my fair share of abuse, but in the end I’ll dive in the line of fire just to save their lives. I’m like the GUNGA DIN of the Windham Clan. So if you or anybody else has a problem with that, you can send your mouth STRAIGHT DOWN SOUTH! MARK: Oh COME ON… PC: And ONE MORE THING! Castor Strife, if you’re listening, I got you where you are today, and YOU NEED to acknowledge that. But not before getting down on your knees and BEGGING Troy’s forgiveness for all the threats you made this week. But hey, if you’re feeling strong, COME ON DOWN and test the waters, buddy. Troy’s the BIG KAHUNA but I’m the undertow, so- (SFX: Muffled dialogue) (A hand pops out from off-camera and closes a washcloth around PC’s mouth. The camera turns: it’s CASTOR STRIFE, who now has his former tag partner in a headlock while continuing to smother him with some sort of substance. PC tries wrestling away, but Castor VIOLENTLY rips him side to side until PC begins to fade…and fade…and fade. He’s unconscious in a matter of moments. Castor lifts beneath his arms and begins to drag him) CASTOR: Alex! Grab the camera! (Camera shakes; the protégé ALEX AUSTIN comes into view) AUSTIN: Hi. Can I borrow this? Thanks. (Camera follows Castor as he drags PC down the hall and turns a corner. As Austin makes the bend, we see Castor stopped in front of a locker room with a plaque on the door reading "PRESIDENT MAYFIELD". He takes a hand-written letter out of his pocket and a roll of scotch tape. Castor tapes the letter to PC’s forehead, stands up, and walks away) AUSTIN: Hold up, I’m coming… (Places the camera in PC’s lap facing up, just below the letter which reads…) To our esteemed President, Please send payment to the Television Champion. Cash only. -C (A moment goes by before we hear hurried footsteps and gasping. The camera is picked up by an exhausted camera man, and quickly turned off) (FADEOUT) Bobby Jack Windham vs. Lord Coyner Pollard (FADEIN: Back w/ RICE...) RICE: "So quite the situation with the National Championship still brewing as the ominous THIRTEEN once again makes his presence felt, but it's starting to seem a little different as LEGION may be getting under his skin. And then after that, Castor Strife making his presence felt against former friend and now Windham Clan enemy in Problem Child. Of course, nobody's sure if PC was even ACCEPTED by the Windham Clan so I've got to wonder if our President is gonna pay Strife a dime! Anyway, coming up next, we're gonna give an encore presentation of the match between Lord Coyner Pollard and Bobby Jack Windham which was under Britannia Rules AY-KAY-AY, however the LORD wants to wrestle. And while you'd think this was the topic at hand for Bobby Jack's pre-match sermon, instead the crowd in Montana got an earful of how many sinners and liars were in the audience. Anyway, without further adieu...let's roll the videotape!" (CUTTO: RINGSIDE -- KERRY O'CONNOR and LAMONT HOLLYWOOD on the taped call.) O’CONNOR: Next up we’ve got the Britannia Rules match! H’WOOD: I love what Lord Pollard does in the ring, but really? Britannia Rules? It’s so slow! O’CONNOR: It forces opponents to switch up their game, we saw against Legion that the brawler had to change his tactics and play Pollard’s games. We’re done with the introductions, Bobby Jack getting some last minute advice as Lord Pollard talks with Jeeves. H’WOOD: Seconds away, round one! O’CONNOR: Pollard in quickly with a double leg takedown, spins round and into a headlock on the mat. Remember you’re not allowed to attack a downed opponent, but if it’s in one fluid move you can apply a hold. H’WOOD: And you wonder why British wrestling went down the crapper. O’CONNOR: Pollard demonstrating great technical skill against Windham, keeping him grounded. H’WOOD: Windham’s not going to quit this early. O’CONNOR: No but over the length of the match this kind of thing builds up. Five rounds, three minutes each, two falls, a knockout or a disqualification for the win. H’WOOD: Under normal rules I’d love that kind of thing, but there’re so many damn rules in this one! O’CONNOR: Windham turns himself and gets a foot on the ropes, Pollard back to his feet, and Windham up at the count of 3. H’WOOD: He looks as confused about the count as ever. O’CONNOR: If you’re grounded and not in a hold, you could get counted down as a knockout. Pollard tries for a collar and elbow, right hand by Windham! H’WOOD: And the referee holding him back? O’CONNOR: Closed fists are illegal, and Pollard went to ground. Up at a count of five, Windham comes in and Pollard behind with a forearm to the kidneys, foot on the back of the knee and grabs the headlock. Windham must want this round to come to an end just so someone can explain the rules again. H’WOOD: I want someone to explain the rules! Lord Pollard grinding on the headlock, Windham fires him off the ropes, Pollard holds onto the ropes, Windham charges in and clotheslines Pollard over the ropes!! (Although the fans are loving seeing the snob tumble to the outside, the referee gets straight in Bobby Jack’s face, warning him about his actions. Windham looks bemused, as Jeeves helps his master back into the ring. Pollard stays on one knee to prevent Windham from being allowed to attack, and only when he’s good and ready does the Lord get to his feet) O’CONNOR: Pollard taking his time, one hand on the rope – and there’s the bell for the end of round one! H’WOOD: Why do you sound so excited about that? O’CONNOR: It’s my job to be excited about matches. Bobby Jack’s trying to go after Coyner Pollard, the Lord’s sat in his corner. H’WOOD: Dumb Windham doesn’t even realise he gets a rest between rounds. Whether anything actually happened or not. O’CONNOR: He’s finally heading back to his corner, Pollard getting some words from Jeeves, maybe some strategy? H’WOOD: Why on Earth are you asking me? This is the second match like this I’ve ever seen! How would I know what goes on between rounds when a Lord is in the ring? O’CONNOR: There’s the bell, round two starts. Pollard is in first, Windham I don’t think realised the bell rang, Pollard with a snapmare and holding on to the chin and back of the head, twisting the neck. (Pollard continues to wrench on the neck, as Bobby Jack gets to a knee. Pollard switches to a side headlock, but Windham is ready for it, taking the Lord down with a back suplex! He’s quickly to his feet and fires a stomp at Lord Pollard, but the referee steps in and gives Windham a warning, the Texan slapping the top rope in disgust) O’CONNOR: These rules are going to take a lot of getting used to for most of our superstars. Windham I think’s on a final verbal warning? H’WOOD: It’s a public warning next isn’t it? O’CONNOR: Pollard back to his feet, Windham with a right to the jaw! Another right hand, a third SIMS: Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has awarded the first PUBLIC WARNING of the match to BOBBY JACK WINDHAM! BOBBY JACK WINDHAM has one public warning, for use of closed fists! H’WOOD: The crowd don’t like this, do they? O’CONNOR: Are you surprised, Lamont? The referee did warn Bobby Jack, these are the rules whether we like them or not. H’WOOD: I kind of like them. They’re growing on me. O’CONNOR: Bobby Jack arguing with the referee, schoolboy ONE!! TWO!! Kickout by Bobby Jack! Lord Coyner Pollard almost went one up, instead gets taken down with a clothesline. H’WOOD: And Windham stopping himself there, he really wanted to stomp the Lord. Is that blasphemy? O’CONNOR: No, he’s not the Lord. Just a Lord. H’WOOD: More than you’ll ever be. O’CONNOR: If it meant being a snob, I wouldn’t want to be. Pollard in trouble as Bobby Jack switches his game, big scoop slam and a huge clothesline as Pollard tried to get to his feet! H’WOOD: But look how smart Pollard is, staying down and rolling near the ropes. He’s getting into the head of Windham who just wants to fight! O’CONNOR: After some of the things Pollard said about the Windham family I’m not surprised! Pollard up and away from the ropes, collar and elbow, Pollard goes behind but Windham smartly to the ropes. Pollard releases the hold, gets an elbow to the jaw for his troubles. (Bobby Jack turns and rattles Pollard with an open palm strike, checking with the ref that that’s OK. Getting the nod, he hits another open palm to the jaw which he follows with an Irish whip, and a big back body drop! Instinctively he starts stomping on Lord Pollard, and the referee signals to the time keeper again) SIMS: Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has given Bobby Jack Windham a SECOND PUBLIC WARNING! Bobby Jack Windham has TWO PUBLIC WARNINGS! O’CONNOR: One more and he’s disqualified! H’WOOD: So kind of like rounders? O’CONNOR: You mean baseball? H’WOOD: No, rounders. Jeeves was teaching me about it earlier. O’CONNOR: Bobby Jack as there’s the bell, Windham going back to his corner, he’s looking really frustrated in there! H’WOOD: Round 2 over already? Wow! (Bobby Jack starts arguing with his corner, while Jeeves applies an ice pack to the shoulders of his master. The referee waits in a neutral corner, getting some abuse from the Windham corner, but shrugs his shoulders as if to say "I don’t make the rules, I just enforce them.") SIMS: Seconds away, round 3! O’CONNOR: Bobby Jack sprints across, Pollard side steps. Windham hits the corner, Pollard waits – forearm smash! Irish whip by Pollard, drop toe hold, into a headlock but Windham straight to his feet and a shot to the kidneys! This is better, faster paced action. Off the ropes by Windham, Pollard taken down with a right to the midsection H’WOOD: And the referee telling Windham to open the fists or he’s going to get disqualified. O’CONNOR: Windham being stopped by the ref again, no attacking a downed opponent. How frustrating must this be? H’WOOD: And we’ve not had a fall as yet. O’CONNOR: Windham arguing with the referee, Pollard from behind with a bulldog! H’WOOD: And straight into the cover. O’CONNOR: Quick kickout by Windham, but Pollard back to his feet first. Windham trying to up the tempo right to the jaw, Pollard against the ropes. Windham comes in H’WOOD: But gets pulled back by the ref! He can’t go at the man in the ropes. O’CONNOR: Look at Windham! He’s turning beet red! ROLL UP!! ONE!! TWO!!! THREE!! Pollard had his feet on the ropes but catches the pinfall! SIMS: Ladies and gentlemen, the first fall of the contest occurs in the 3rd round, and goes to LORD COYNER POLLARD!! O’CONNOR: Bobby Jack is FURIOUS with the referee! H’WOOD: Even if the ref had seen that, Lord Pollard has diplomatic immunity! He’s really stacked the deck, made sure he has the advantage, and I LOVE it! O’CONNOR: Pollard back to his corner, Bobby Jack and the Windhams are in the ring arguing about the feet on the ropes. (The British Lord smiles, stretching in the corner as he waits for the start of round 4. He talks with Jeeves as the Windham’s threaten the referee, but the official stands tall) O’CONNOR: The Windham’s pleas falling on dead ears. There’s the round, Bobby Jack charges Pollard. Sidestep by the Lord, kick to the leg, step-in knee to the midsection and a European uppercut. (The Lord grabs Windham by the head, but the Texan with a rake to the face stops the building advantage. The referee gets in Windham’s face again as Pollard stands against the ropes shaking off the effects of the illegal move) O’CONNOR: Windham not happy with the ref, flips him off – low blow!! (SFX: bell ringing) SIMS: Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has DISQUALIFIED Bobby Jack Windham! The winner of the match, by disqualification… LORD COYNER POLLARD! O’CONNOR: Bobby Jack stomping on Lord Pollard! Jeeves is in trying to help his master, the referee trying to pull Bobby Jack away – in come the Windhams! (The ring fills, as the members of the Windham clan stomp away on Lord Coyner Pollard. Jeeves jumps on the back of Bobby Jack, only to get dragged down and floored by a big right from Mark Windham. Troy, meanwhile, grabs Pollard and hits a vicious piledriver with assistance from Bobby Jack, Mark stomping away on Jeeves) O’CONNOR: I don’t think this is over, but the Windham Family standing tall in spite of the loss by Bobby Jack! H’WOOD: I wouldn’t want the Windham’s pissed at me – or Lord Pollard’s family! What’s going to happen with these two families? O’CONNOR: We could be in for a war, but for now, Lord Coyner Pollard has the battle – but doesn’t look like much of a winner to me. (FADEOUT) Castor Strife (c) vs. Cameron Cruise (MUSICUP: ‘CHECK MY BRAIN’ – ALICE IN CHAINS) (FADEIN: Circled numbers count down from ‘4…3…2…1’ on the big screen, and CASTOR STRIFE steps out of a foggy gray-lit/hyper bulb-flasing entrance way with a black "devil horns" gas mask strapped to his face, and the NFW TV Title around his waist. He drops to his knees, slicks back his hair, and turns his head left to right, surveying the audience. SPLIT-SCREEN opens up: CAMERON CRUISE walks out casually, doing a spin to peak behind his back before turning toward the ring again. He sneers, smiles, nods his head in approval that nobody named "Windham" has bumrushed him, and continues on) RICE: "Billings got to see it’s first NFW title defense, as Castor V. Strife put the TV strap on the line against a GAME Cameron Cruise. And when I say this veteran was game, I mean he was DIALED IN from the moment the bell rang. Not taking this title shot for granted after Troy Windham robbed him of the last one, Cruise met Strife tic-for-tac in a battle of technical gamesmanship. And one would have to wonder if Strife's actions earlier in the night with Problem Child would be a precursor to anything." (CUTTO: Castor throws a leg- caught by Cruise. Castor comes up with the enzuigiri- Cruise ducks. Castor comes BACK with the mule kick, but Cruise catches that too, drags Castor back, and applies a toehold/crossface) RICE: "Here you’ve got two experienced guys who can control the tempo. Both are of near equal size with Cruise being slightly bigger. But the difference maker seemed to be Castor’s speed and athleticism. After a while, Cruise just couldn’t keep up." (CUTTO: Cruise with the waist lock- Castor has his legs wide and arms around Cruise’s forearm, looking for a counter. Quickly, Castor reverses and puts a waistlock on Cruise which Cruise reverses back. This time, Castor swivels his arms and spins face-to-face with underhooks, and executes a picture-perfect BELLY-TO-BELLY) RICE: "Leading up to the match, Castor promised he could keep Cruise safe from Windham Clan interference. Hard to believe that was being enforced with a five-foot, hundred pound brunette at ringside. It looks like Castor, whose fixation with Troy Windham has been growing more massive by the day, was using Cruise as shark bait. Would the Windhams bite? Meanwhile, in the ring…" (CUTTO: Cruise drives a shoulder into Castor against the turnbuckles. He goes for the Irish whip, but gets reversed. As he bounces off the opposite corner, Castor rushes in with a back elbow to the jaw that drops Cruise) RICE: "Just as the action started heating up, somebody made their way out of the entrance to ringside. One of the Windham Clan? No…doesn’t look like one of them. Some wiry little guy, dirty from head to toe, worming his way over to Blanche." (CUTTO: The unidentified filthy little man begins talking with Blanche, who opens up her purse and pulls out a FAT WAD OF CASH. The man flashes a sickening grin and happily accepts the cash before returning back up the aisle) RICE: "Damn if that ain’t the 75 grand Eddie Mayfield paid ‘em for Problem Child! Crazy money being paid out to those who carry hits on the Windhams. Blanche hands it over the sickly looking mystery guy and he disappears- investing with Madoff would’ve been smarter." (CUTTO: Castor hits a superkick! Cruise pops back up, stumbling. Castor catches him by the waist and drops him on the ropes for a STUN GUN!) RICE: "CLICHE-KILLER from the champ! Goodnight Cameron Cruise! ONE…TWO…THREE! Castor retains, but HOLD THE PHONE- ‘cause BOBBY JACK WINDHAM and AUGUST DE LA ROSSI are headed to the ring." (CUTTO: Bobby Jack and August don’t run, but walk to the ring with the confidence of two young men with the power behind them. Castor slides out of the ring, grabs his title, and watches as they enter and circle the fallen Cameron Cruise) RICE: "August delivers a swift KICK to the ribs of Cruise while Bobby Jack taunts Castor to climb into the ring. Castor turns and smiles, adjusts the belt on his shoulder. Something wrong? Why isn’t Castor jumping on this? Hasn’t he been challenging them all week- HOLD UP! One by one, the crowd rises up in a wave to check out the entrance. DAMN, that is one BIG DUDE running full speed at the ring! Spiked shoulder pads, war paint, PRUSSIAN WAR HELMET! Don’t turn around, Windhams- YOU DON’T WANNA!" (Segment of ‘smart fans’ start chanting "MAG-NUS! MAG-NUS! MAG-NUS!") (CUTTO: The BEHEMOTH-like hellion raises a large, spiked medieval forearm into the air and CRASHES IT DOWN on August and throwing him out of the ring!) RICE: "Bye bye August! Bobby Jack gets one glimpse of this man and the look on his face tells me they are VERY familiar! It’s the NLW WORLD OPEN WEIGHT CHAMPION- MAGNUS! Cruise wasn’t the shark bait- it was BOBBY JACK ALL ALONG! Magnus with a PUMP KICK to the face of Bobby Jack! He’s got him up for an inverted power bomb- the deadly DESTRUCTO BOMB I heard so much noise about comin’ outta New York. Bobby rides the momentum over Magnus’ back and in a confused state throws a couple rights to his chest. Irish whip? No way. Magnus reverses and chases Bobby down with a running CLOTHESLINE over the ropes! The crowd in Billings has collectively crapped their pants for a GWAR-esque monster in a European war helmet!" (CUTTO: MAGNUS runs up the turnbuckles and throws up his arms triumphantly to a BIG REACTION!) RICE: "On the outside, Cruise and August brawled their way into the crowd! Castor chased a stumbling Bobby Jack up the entrance before stopping and chucking his GAS MASK at him." (Bobby Jack turns around, holding his head) BOBBY JACK: "GOD AS MAH WITNESS, YOU’RE A DEAD MAN! A DEAD MAN!" (Blanche runs over to pull Castor back by the waist) CASTOR: "You’re NOT TROY! I wanted TROY! EU-THA-NA-SIA!"
