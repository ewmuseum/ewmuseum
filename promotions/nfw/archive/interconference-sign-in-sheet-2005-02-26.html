jediPREZ — 26 February 2005 Here's the deal, I've left 4 weeks out of everyone's 12 week schedules as OPEN. These 4 weeks will be the ONLY opportunity for NFW East to visit the NFW West and vice-versa. Its also the only 4 weeks where you can do special challenge matches within the conference, so keep that in mind.
With that said -- if you'd like to sign up for any interconference matches, please respond here. If you want all 4 to be interconference -- please specify. If you only want 2, same deal. Also if you have a specific opponent from the West in mind, be sure to say who. I know that roster hasn't been unveiled totally yet, but the first 9 were on Wrestlebowl.
HOW ARE THEY BOOKED? Ah yes, the age old question. Well, it'll most likely be a combination of any RP's you do, segments for the card, your current push status and if you're writing the match. (yeah, I'm that cheap) I'm not sure if we'll be doing a traditional RP thread -- unless its one of the CSWA guys against you...since we know how to handle those.
Also, they will be booked by the Original Triumverate of me, JN, GG -- unless two handlers come to a decision themselves, which would make our lives alot easier.
