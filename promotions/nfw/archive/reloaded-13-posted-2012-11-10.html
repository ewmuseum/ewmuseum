Biron — 15 November 2012 Well, it took us three years, but we did made it. Not the greatest RELOADED that's ever come out, but Randalls/Windham (plus the fallout) really carried the show along. Going to give some quick thoughts ... I've got a boatload of other shit to write.
- Good to see Eddie put a hurtin' on Peter Windham ... I kind of hope that Billy flips him back to Problem Child.
- Cameron Cruise is FREE. I don't know if [name withheld] is around anymore so I imagine the impact won't be tremendous. I feel like a broken record at times, but it'd be great to see Tom make a run at Rook or me or anybody with a new Cameron Cruise.
- Five-Way was alright ... certainly had the potential to be much more, but it did its job. Not too surprised to see Pete take the W ... made sense. Rook and Dane finally getting to tangle up was good ... though I don't think anything else will come from that off Justin's end!
- TV Match was alright ... I got done what I wanted, but didn't do it how I originally envisioned. Oh well, got a few more chances!
- Oh, that Superfly Express segment was GOLD. Really happy that team came together ... looking forward to what those two do with a beat up and broken Calvin Carlton (who needs to start wheeling and dealing ASAP - that's how he do!)
- Great reveal on Windham being the guy who got JJ blackballed ... very interesting! - Windham/Randalls ... Katz wrote a SICK, SICK match as expected. Literally carried the show. The forming of The Hellfire Club is awesome. That stable is better than both The Dynasty and Windham Clan, IMO. No fluff there. Three of the Frontier's best (and worst) joining together to run the show. Can't wait!
Well done to everybody who wrote! Should be a wild ride as we pick up speed and move forward!
