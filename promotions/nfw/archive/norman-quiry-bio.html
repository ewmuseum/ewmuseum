Mortimer Hguyen III Biography Hometown Blackpool, England Birthday May 18, 1973 Height 6'3 Weight 241 Alias N. Quiry Biography " The patient is a recipient of paranoid schizophrenic with homicidal tendecies. Secondary diagnosis is a borderline personality disorder, or maybe even multiple personalities. He is medicated with a highly proven vaccine, but experiences frequent hallucinations as a result of his medical health. He seems convinced that he shares his mind with another being. Footnote: Interests focus mainly around the supplied brain puzzle residing in his cell. " My body cringed as the doctor's cold words creeped through my ears. My name is Elissa Quinzell, properly referred to by Mr. Mortimer Hguyen as "Quiz.". Given the knowledge, courtesy of various family members, I have assembled this brief summary of Mr. Hguyen's orgin. Unfortunately, Mr. Hguyen suffers from paranoid amnesia, meaning that his memory of his past has been erased. I am here to revive that vanished past. An ambitious man from the very start, Mr. Hguyen had it all. Born and raised inside of the big city, breeded by a family that hosted a wealthy mother and an even wealthier father. All the Hguyen's wanted was a son who would follow in their footsteps as a successful business man. They went about doing this by supplying Mr. Mortimer with various educational programs at a young age. They all proved worthy as the Hguyens watch their son grow into an established man. In high school, he reigned as captain of the ROTC and finished top of his class with a 4.0 GPA, graduating cum laudae. He followed in the footsteps of his retired father and attended law school, quickly ascending among the ranks and taking his father's place in one of the State's finest Law Firms. Records show that he displayed every bit of his father's passion and desire to win. His win-loss ratio boasted his ability to prove even the most guilty man innocent. In a quite frankly manner, he was one of the best. ...This is where I come in. I was hired as his personal assistant, but over the years we became so much more than that. We became best friends, we grew an unbreakable bond, at one point we even established a relationship. Mr. Hguyen has supplied me with my every need and my every want...for that I can never repay him...so I offer my services as his personal life assistant instead. Then a period came that would change his life forever. Brian Tomas, an old friend from High School caught up in the law. Mortimer recieved the phone call from Tomas, pleading for his support in a big Drug bust scandal. Realizing the hazards of representing such a mischevious scandal, Mr. Hguyen hastly declined. Tomas, though, wouldn't take no for an answer, bribing Mr. Hguyen with a large amount of cash...so large that Mr. Hguyen could not decline. He accepted, knowing that if such a sideline project were to be discovered, he would lose both his job and possibly any chance at any future endeavors. Sitting in front of Judge Ruthos proved ghastly, as the Jury returned to the courtroom to delcare Tomas a guilty man. Tomas didn't take too kindly to wasting his money on Hguyen, only to be found guilty. He snatched out a .22 glock, triggering it in the back of Hguyen's neck, leaving him bloody in the middle of the courtroom as security quickly tackled Tomas to the ground, arresting him on the spot. ...The aftermath proved fatal. Nearly two months in the hospital resulted in Mr. Hguyens current state as a recipient of paranoid schizophrenic, swiping a vast percentage of his memory. Mortimer's disability did little to change the fact that he constructed an illegal sideline project, thus he was sent to a Rehab Prison in Detroit, Michigan. The following was found in his personal joural inside of his cell... Day One: Where am I? These painted walls...This cement floor...These shackled bounding my ankles. An ID tag is attached to this jumpsuit that I wear...I snatch it off, reading its contents. Name: Mortimer Hguyen, Age: 29, Sex: Male, Condition: Recipient of paranoid schizophrenic w. suicidal tendecies...what is this? Where am I? Who am I? It says Mortimer Hguyen, but how am I for sure? Across the room...sitting on a rusty white table...a jigsaw puzzle. I think I'll play with it. For days, Mr. Hguyen toyed with that puzzle...it was a difficult puzzle, meant to keep the inmates busy for weeks on hand...but it took but a day for him to complete it, the final picture being that of a man's face...whom you ask? Why, that has yet to be known. Reports have it that he constantly completed and re-completed the puzzle until he was able to complete it in under a minute. Days later, another entry was discovered... Day 10: This Jigsaw puzzle...it fascinates me. In the last 10 days, I have compeleted it over 500 times. I still don't know who I am, but as I lay in my cold bunk at nights, I hear voices...familiar voices. Voices telling me that life is nothing but a huge complex puzzle that no man can ever decipher. I believe him...but yet I want to be that one man. I am a servent of this world, now I want this world to be a servent of me. Life is nothing more than a puzzle... Mr. Hguyen's claims of these "Voices" grew until he became convinced that he shared his mind with another person. His illusions that Life was nothing more than a puzzle continued to increase until he finally delcared his name as Norman Query, his nickname being N. Quiry (meant to sound like Inquiry which means "Riddle" or "Puzzle". ) After his "No Bail" clause was lifted, I gathered the money out of his account to release him. He immediately began to gather puzzles and riddles from different sources, piecing them together in a timely manner. His Idol in life switched from his father, Mortimer Hguyen II, to batman favorite..The Riddler. His sudden mastery of puzzles grew until he became capable of deciphering even the little things, such as the time of day without looking at a watch, or someone's age without previous knowledge. This technique grew so to the point where Mr. Hguyen gained an even more enhanced IQ than before. With his track record, his options for work were limited. I managed to gather him a spot in a local indy wrestling circuit, figuring he couldn't stoop any lower and that his hazardous medical health would make any other working enviroment too dangerous. Upon telling him this, he smiled and relished in the fact of knowing that he had no prior wrestling experience, which would end up in one theory...how could he survive a sport he never even cared to watch? ...The ultimate puzzle for him to solve. I offered Mr. Hguyen the chance to train with one of the area's best, but he quickly declined, stating that he'd rather go into the industry unprepared, thus making it that much more of a challenge for him to decipher. The local Indy circuit went by the name of "Outside the Rules" wrestling. With me as his manager, He entered...suffering losses on his first seven attempts... They only seemed to enhance his interest even more. It didn't take long for him to catch the groove and win his first match, though. Various mind games that he portrayed throughout the match accumulated with his ability to find the opponents main weak point and target it like a rabid animal. Mr. Hguyen had become a rare speedy success. But now he wants more... More fame... More Glory... More Challenges with the best in the world... And it doesn't take a rocket science to figure out that the Ultratitle is as big as it gets.
