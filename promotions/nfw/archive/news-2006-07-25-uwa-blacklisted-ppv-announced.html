UWA Blacklisted PPV Announced! Posted by Natrone Rice on 25 Jul 2006 As promised at the end of the Western Conference show, Craig Miles and Anne Mystic have formally agreed to a mini-PPV broadcast for a UWA Event during the Wild Card Weekend for the Ultratitle playoffs. Three matches have been confirmed, including the tag team match to determine the 1-4 seeds in the NFW Western Conference as well as a special UWA World Championship match between feud-of-the-year rivals Craig Miles and Eddie Mayfield. In what should be a high-impact, highly violent tag team brawl -- the DRS will put up their unofficial claim as the best UWA tag team against not only the Original Showstoppers and Calvin Carlton, but the newly reformed <> Cutters of Nathan Storm and J. Bishop. Of course, smarks, marks and insiders alike figure they've agreed to that match due to the fact that their 'Franchise' of the <> Exchange will also have to walk into Philadelphia for an important matchup. In wrestling, playoff byes must not mean much as Seymour Almasy and Alias will team up against Eli Flair and Tom Adler to determine the top 4 seeds of the NFW West Conference. Nobody knows exactly why Craig Miles wanted to book this match on this particular show, but some people wonder if this was an Anne Mystic bargaining chip -- hoping she may get a shot at revenge against Adler, just yet. Then again, Miles *is* crazy and may just have wanted to see these guys work for their money the old-fashioned way. No doubt, the Boss has his work cut out in the main event. Miles and Mystic have agreed to two important stipulations for the UWA World Chammpionship challenge against Eddie Mayfield. 1) Its a Lumberjack match. 2) The Loser is BLACKLISTED from the UWA; hence the title of the show. Sources have already stated that the Mystic/Mayfield relationship has been quite strained as of late, with Mayfield failing to quiet Tom Adler once and for all...and now Craig Miles is on the verge of taking the UWA Championship and possibly having the power to derail the UWA for good.
