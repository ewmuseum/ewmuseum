QueenOfTheRing — 2 August 2005 BEST MATCH: JAMAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRR *shakes fist* Mayfield/Miles BEST SEGMENT: "Kicking the Tires" and "The Eddy Love Show", although Steve showed me 95% of it before he posted it.
H'WOOD LINE OF THE NIGHT: H'WOOD: (Cutting in) "And remember ladies - The Woodman won't go into your forest unless you're packing realsies under that shirt! The fakes look nice and all, but let's face it, I'm a grown man - balloons are for birthday parties, not my bedroom."
SLAM OF THE WEEK: So many to choose from! DUDE, THAT'S GROSS: Miles drinking his own blood was fantastic. OVERALL STORYLINE THOUGHTS/FEEDBACK: *Week 2 was much better than Week 1. Had a better feel to it. *Welcome to Azala's handler, I've read some of your PRIME stuff so you're alright in my book. ;)
*I would have liked to have seen Winters chase Windham a bit more. I'm glad Miles will still have to chase DENNIS~!
*Liked Eli's segment. *Nixon/Mystic = Francine/Beulah. I was waiting for Nix to show up, glad she did. *Adler's shirt made me chuckle EZieba — 2 August 2005 BEST MATCH: Miles vs. Mayfield hands down. BEST SEGMENT: Kicking The Tires. Ya never get tired of a Shamon segement. The Eddy Love Show was a close second!.
H'WOOD LINE OF THE NIGHT: H’WOOD: “If that Handle was pointed the other way, then Miles would have the first male case of cameltoe!”
SLAM OF THE WEEK: Eli Flair trading kicks against Windham and Winters DUDE, THAT'S GROSS: Yeah ... Miles drinkin' the blood. Dear Sweet Lord! Overall the card was a great read and I enjoyed it very much. Major props to who helped out on it!
GreggG — 2 August 2005 BEST MATCH: Miles/Mayfield, on its third read for me today, might be my favorite match in a long, long, long time. Had a great ECW 1997 blood match feud -- somewhere between Sabu/Terry Funk and Raven/Sandman. *****, NFW MOTY so far.
BEST SEGMENT: I could repeat ad nauseum w/ the Eddy Love stuff and Shamon. Great stuff, but what do you expect from the folks who wrote it? I'm going to give some props here to my main man Devin and the stuff with Jeff Garvin, quickly becoming one of my favorites. I had nothing to do w/ the Calvin writing with any of that -- all him and Katz working some great magic.
H'WOOD LINE: I already talked this out w/ JN but the "mother-of-pearl Bentley" is straight-up brilliance. I'm already repeating that to my friends.
SLAM OF THE WEEK/Eww moment: Not to toot my own horn, but I dug the TR Burning Hammer through a table set on fire spot that I wrote and I also hope Troy Windham doing The Fargo Strut while wrapped in barbed wire makes the opening credits.
Great ****ing show. Great read. Awesome concept which came off great. TH — 9 August 2005 BEST MATCH: Miles/Mayfield I by far BEST SEGMENT: Eli Flair kicking Winters and Troy Diddy while they were down H'WOOD LINE OF THE NIGHT: O’CONNOR: “Come ON with that, Woodman! Eddie trying to grab at the wire, and he’s not high enough - -“
H’WOOD: “I betcha nobodies’ EVER said that to Miles!” SLAM OF THE WEEK: Miles missing the razor wire Pearl Jam... JEREMY SPOKE IN CLAAAAAASSS TODAY DUDE, THAT'S GROSS: Miles going waterfountain with his own bicep OVERALL STORYLINE THOUGHTS/FEEDBACK: Personally, I think we're getting some good arcs here, although I'm a bit concerned that Miles/Mayfield may have been too good. It felt like a blowoff match right up until the finish, and it was so over the top, I don't know how you guys are going to be able to top it for WrestleBowl. Other than the big storyline, I'm really interested to see how the Prosser/Snack Daddy alliance is going to unfold, and what new faces we're going to see. Plus Troy-Diddy/Winters/Flair is looking promising.
Nova — 23 August 2005 I realize this is pretty late, but what the hey, I'll throw in some feedback for grins. BEST MATCH: I actually loved every minute of Steve Christ/Rook Black. I've never read a match written like that before, with Black in the shower wondering what the hell he'd just been through, flashing back to the match.
BEST SEGMENT: Garvin's "The Legacy of General Sherman" was great...I popped MAD BON4RZ for the historical tie-ins that were very well done, and you really get the sense that Garvin wants to turn his luck around.
H'WOOD LINE OF THE NIGHT: “I’m gaining a new respect for Mystic. I used to think she was a gold digging, aging, wrestling-diva harpy *****, but now, not so much on the harpy.”
SLAM OF THE WEEK: It's not so much a slam, persay, but Mayfield locking in the Sharpshooter on Miles' barbed-wire-wrapped legs was ill as hell.
DUDE, THAT'S GROSS: I want to be original and not just echo everyone else's thoughts... ...but *******, man. Drinking the blood spurting out of your arm? That's ****ed up. OVERALL STORYLINE THOUGHTS/FEEDBACK: It's quite an achievement to have a whole show premised around barbed wire matches and still it doesn't feel old or tired by the end. This is, by the way, the first full show I've read of another fed (all segs and matches) besides PRIME since joining PRIME last November, so that's saying something. Great job, NFW West. Hope Week Three is even better.
Off to Valinor — 23 August 2005 Awesome Nova, freaking awesome. Thanks for reading. :)
