Lamont Hollywood Biography Biography Full head of Ghost white hair, salt and pepper grey mustache, looks a lot like a cross between Sam Neil in HULK and Michael PS Hayes. Dark skinned, perhaps a blend of Greek and Italian in his blood, Hollywood has always been the 'pretty heel' type of character in his many years of wrestling. In the early 80s, held World Tagteam titles on numerous occassions with his partner, 'Godfather' Paul Tonnelli, in the 'Rock and Roll Gangsters' team. After that team split up, Hollywood went on to a successful singles career, and then became the most notorius heel manager to ever carry a license, and was dubbed 'Manager to the Stars' in respect to all of the workers he successfully managed to world championships. Lamont Hollywood has been an employee and Color Broadcast Journalist for Avery Everette's Ultimate Wrestling Alliance, and even lasted through Everettes' buyout of UWA by a young Quentin Sullivan. Hollywood was a sure-hire when NFW was dreamed up, as Lamont has won several 'Announcer of the Year' awards on *P and is respected behind the scenes as a Road Agent and talent coordinator. Most promos and clip packages seen on NFW TV are produced by Hollywood, and is always good for a 'Back in MY day' story to share with the boys. Seen at some times to be full of himself, and a bit of a heat machine, Hollywood usually overtalks on camera because nobody else will do it, and time needs to be filled.
