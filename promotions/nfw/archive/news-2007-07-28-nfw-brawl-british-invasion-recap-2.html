NFW news: NFW Brawl: British Invasion Recap #2

28 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl: British Invasion Recap #2 Liverpool, England — 28 July 2007 Oye, ya bloody bastards this is 'Just' Mark back for a recap of the second underground house show put on by New Frontier Wrestling in Liverpool that they were running in the Olympia. Seriously, though it was exciting to get a look at what to expect from what many in the states are calling v.3 and half the drunken idiots following this sport still think this damn place is in a 'season.'



Due to the show starting earlier than expected, or my tickets lying, by the time I found my seat I'd missed what I was told was the first entrance of the night. Some drunk next to me said I hadn't missed much before he ran off to vomit and he never returned oddly enough.



Richard Castillo vs. Phil Atken




The first match of the night was Richard Castillo vs. Phil Atken. As I sat down Atken was making his way to the ring wearing a black and gold robe with a pudgy balding man by his side they introduced as Dick Dirkwood. I've seen more subtler names in porn films. . . wait a tick. . .


Phil got into the ring, but before the bell could even chime he hit Castillo with a running kick to the nuts. Welcome to NFW, Mr. Atken, you'll fit in nicely with the groin-kicker division, which probably is 15 wrestlers deep with a fake Professor and Doctor included in the package.


The referee began to yell at Atken, but begrudgingly signaled for the bell to be rung. With Castillo doubled over in pain Atken began to work over the leg for well over a minute while Castillo rolled around yelping. Apparently, Communism isn't as painful as a swift kick in the nuts and the Cuban Castillo is proof. After working on the leg Atken hooked Castillo in a figure four, but Castillo was too near the ropes. After the failed submission attempt Atken made some sort of signal to the pudgy man at ringside. The guy jumped up onto the apron which distracted the ref long enough for Atken to deliever a cock punch with the brass knucks in his tights which was then followed by a really snappy looking DDT and hooked the leg for the pin. Dick jumped off the apron the Atken picked up the win. Really short match and too much cock based offense for the shortness in my opinion. Of course, with the aforementioned division. . .



Joe the Plumber vs. Billy Lovemuscle (non-title)




Next up was a non-title match between Billy Lovemuscle and Joe the Plumber. This match also involved a foreign object making the show two for two so far. The one thing about NFW heels is they're not afraid to dig deep down into the bowels of their character to find their weapons of choice, of course with good 'ol Joe, that's more literal than metaphorical.


The match started fairly even with both men hooking up, neither showing any sort of advantage. After they broke a brief punching contest broke out which ended in both men staggering towards the ropes. Joe tried to take the initiative by running full force in Lovemuscle's direction but Lovemuscle ducked which caused Joe to tumble over the ropes and end up on the other side of the apron. Joe got brought back into the ring the hard way but Lovemuscle only managed to get a one and a half. Lovemuscle went to pick up Joe at this point but was the unfortunate victim to an eye gouge in to face. One of the most brutal moves in the history of wrestling that is. The eye gouge gave Joe the momentary advantage as he begin to toss haymakers left and right at Lovemuscle which was followed by a flurry of forearms in the corner and a bionic elbow for good measure. With Lovemuscle stunned in the corner Joe attempted to set him up for the "Kiss The Porcelain" but Lovemuscle moved out the way at the last moment leaving Joe's leg hooked on the top rope. The ref then pulled Lovemuscle away from Joe to allow him to escape but while Joe was in the corner he grabbed some sort of object I couldn't see that was sitting in the corner, may have been a wrench. As Lovemuscle walked back over towards Joe he got nailed with the object and DOWN WENT LOVEMUSCLE. Joe tossed the weapon out the ring and the ref was none the wiser as he was busy running out the way of the falling Lovemuscle. Joe went for the pin and was given success in return.



As the ref was raising Joe's hand Cameron Cruise raced down to the ring, grabbing the wrench that was tossed out of the ring as he ran down. He waved the wrench about in wild gestures, while talking to the ref who then turned his attention to Lovemuscle who was stumbling to his feet. The ref then raised Lovemuscle's hand and it was announced the decision had been reversed and Joe had been DQ'ed, thanks to Cameron Cruise. Good enough brawl with a screwy finish. Of course, why end with a screwy finish. . .when you can have complete and total insanity?


How about Cameron Cruise grabbing the house microphone and telling Joe the Plumber that he can take that title shot of his. . . and shove it where he works his dayjob, 'cause he's got better things to do then worrying about whether he's going to contract salmonella poisoning, jaundice and hepatitis all simultaneously just from TOUCHING him? Now, that's bloody rich 'cause that's what I'm thinking every time I see that bastard cutting a promo. Of course, this brings out The Sailor Man swinging a bloody oar all around the air. . . Lovemuscle takes his cue, getting the hell out of dodge and saying something along the lines of "Man, this place must be hirin' from San Antonio Rainbow Avenue!"

So, Cruise is getting chased around by the Sailor Man, who's screaming about his honor of duty, Joe probably mistaking duty for doody takes offense to the Sailor Man. Joe clobbers him with a wrench, then he wails on Cruise with the wrench 'cause Cruise cost him the match.


CUEUP: CROWD HYSTERICS?

WHY?

KOOTER FUCKING CRUISE.

If you've never seen the largest purple mutation of Barney the Dinosaur crossed with the Moondogs crossed with Swamp Thing, than you've never seen this freak in person. He's a GIANT. Now, he's stalking down the aisle as Cameron slides out of the ring, walking dizzily away from the Sailor Man and Joe arguing. Suddenly, they both point at Cruise and start running after him, oars and wrenches held up to the Gods in tribute. . . and Cameron reaches Kooter, looking up slowly at the massive freak, turning paler than Britney Spears after a coke binge in rehab. Kooter shoves Cameron out of the way, The Sailor cracks the oar across his chest, splintering it everywhere. . .

NO EFFECT.

CAN YOU SAY CHOKESLAM?

BOOOOOOOOOOOM! Sailor Man's twitching, but Joe thinks fast and WAILS. . . and I mean WAILS KOOTER! Cruise nearly faints, watching blood spurt out of Kooter's head as he yells angrily and backhand chops Joe two feet in the air. Kooter starts sobbing violently and screaming "BLOOD BAD BLOOD BAD!" . . . and he latches onto Cameron in a child's bear hug around his waist, begging his Daddy to make it feel better. He points at Joe, who doesn't know what the hell to think. . . "BAD MAN MAKE ME BLEEEEEEEEEEEED!" Kooter buries his head into Cameron's belly, Joe now feels confident and screams back "Just wait till I get my two-finger hooks into your momma's asshole, so I can drop my DIRTYSQUIRREL DEMON IN FOR A WRIGGLING, WIGGLING SLOPPY SHITFUCK TOPPED WITH MARSHMALLOW MANJUICE!"

Cameron pats him on the back, walking him away from the scene as he sobs. . . security trying to stop Joe from going into the crowd after drunk hecklers, as the Sailor Man lies unconscious on the ramp.

Yeah. . . ok. . .

NEXT!

The Suicide Kings vs. The Hardliners



. . . and that match didn't happen. The Suicide Kings were somehow drugged and diverted to Thailand, according to someone in the building. . . and that's when some weird music started playing and Don King gave a 3 minute rap introduction for Honey I F*cked Your Mom, who claimed to have gotten DC Stratton and Tsunami in a little trouble last night. They then snickered to themselves, where Mittens grabbed the microphone and started making fun of Liverpool for a good 3 minutes, before announcing that he'd seen Craig Miles backstage and asked for a Television Title shot HARDKORR~! GRAVY stylez!

Of course, Craig Miles had responded with "GRAVY? WHY DIDN'T I THINK OF THAT!?!? Make sure its flammable, and its got the green light."

And thus we learned that at the NEXT Crash Television, its not going to be Cameron Cruise, but Mittens T. Cat instead in a match where we might actually see gravy lit on fire.

. . . I'm actually not that surprised.

DAVID TUI vs. FELIX RED



The next match of the night was David Tui going up against Felix Red, who I consider to be possibly the greatest NFW champion of recent memory. In the program they were really pushing David Tui as an exciting and homegrown prospect for NFW so I was really excited to be able to see how he'd be able to hang with Felix Red.



After both men made there ways to the ring, they wasted in no time in getting started. The match kicked off with both men trying to lock the other up but his opponent managing to escape the other’s grip. Red managed to get a momentary upper hand by taking down Tui with an arm drag but couldn’t keep the advantage as he was victim to one himself. As Red got back up to his feet he was almost victim of a Tui dropkick by managed to quickly dodge out of the way. The match went along this route for a good five minutes with neither man truly managing to get much ground over the other. However Tui then managed to gain it briefly as he took Red to town with a series of knife edge chops that changed the color of Red’s chest in their brutality. Red went to the ropes to recover and the referee pulled Tui away. This gave Red a chance to strike however and Tui was taken down with a lariat.



Around this time, Kin Hiroshi made his way to the ring waving around his World Heavyweight Title at Red but it seemed not to have the desired effect as Red completely ignored Hiroshi. As Red then went to pick Tui up, Tui shoved him back with enough force to get distance from Red. Again Hiroshi tried to get in the direct eyesight of Red but was completely ignored. There was then more back and forth action in some form of weird highflying brawl hybrid style that was impressive to witness while Hiroshi continued his failed attempts to gain Red’s attention. In fact Hiroshi’s theatrics ended up having the opposite of his desired effect as Tui began to exchange heated words with Hiroshi over the distraction he was causing. This argument gave Red enough of a distraction to strike as he snuck up behind the enraged yet distracted Tui and hit him with his “Ecstasy of the Agony”. This winded Tui for just enough time for the three count as Hiroshi looked on in horror. Red then celebrated his victory briefly before heading out the ring, failing once more to acknowledge the existence of Hiroshi on his way out. Tui on the other hand was very much aware of Hiroshi’s presence, staring at him like he was about to explode however Hiroshi’s attention was still directed towards Red who he watched leave the ring area with a complete look of disbelief.


Frankie Fargo vs. Craig Miles (ChickenWire Cage)



The chicken wire cage was set up right after Felix Red and David Tui finished their match. Craig Miles entered the cage before it even finished being set up. He set up a steel chair as he waited for ‘Fabulous’ Frankie Fargo to arrive. The fans cheered, just waiting for this match to start as Frankie finally made his way into the ring. Sipping a beer no one is sure where he got it from, Craig launched it at Frankie before he could enter. Fargo refused to get into the ring at this point, yelling at Miles. ‘Cocky’ Craig Miles exited the cage and went after him. Grabbing Fargo by his hair and throwing him into the chicken wire, Craig had the referee open the door and threw him into the cage to officially start the match. Entering the cage next, Craig left himself open when Fargo had the chair. At this point Frankie took the chair to Miles until it was bent and unusable. Even after it was a twisted piece of metal he continued to hit Craig with it. Out of nowhere The Dean of Thermodynamics COCKYline Frankie. Like a sick bastard, Craig picked up Frankie and threw him into the chicken wire. Using the cage like a cheese grater, Miles opened up Fabulous’ forehead. Blood poured all over the ring and the fans were loving it. The referee didn’t love it though, as he called for the bell. Craig and Frankie didn’t stop though. The referee tried to get between them but he caught knocked on his ass by both of them. That was when security came rushing down to separate the two. As they tried to pull them apart, it looked as if Frankie Fargo tried to kidnap someone’s daughter. In the end the fans wanted to be pissed for not having a winner, but the amount of blood they saw kept them from booing.


DC Stark/Nova vs. Dan Ryan/Sean Stevens



Finally, we got to the main event, where a little new with the old would be in the ring as DC Stark teamed with the Season 2 ULTRATITLE CHAMPION, Nova and they squared off against Dan Ryan and 'XXX' Sean Stevens. Stark and Nova would be in the ring for the first time together, whereas Stevens and Ryan have been around the block quite a few times across the ring and on the same side of the ring. They go back a long way, so their own familiarity should've provided an advantage, but with The Rising Star's hot streak in NFW. . . and Starks being the wild card, anything could happen.


Stevens and Stark started off the match, with a round of technical chain wrestling that got the crowd amped up from the get-go. Stevens trying to hiptoss Stark, who rolled through with an armdrag, Stevens scissoring out and leaping into a side headlock. Stark got up, planted a Back Suplex, but Stevens kept the headlock on, until Stark grabbed the ropes. The two showed off some serious counter wrestling skills, but neither could exploit the other. After a stereo dropkick into each other's feet after a stereo kipup, both rolled back to their corners and made the tags to their partners.

Nova and Ryan, crossing paths for the first time in New Frontier Wrestling exploded out of the gates, but Ryan brushed off a Nova clothesline then caught him in the head with a vicious kick. Ryan worked over the Rising Star with ground strikes, but found himself on the receiving end of a DDT, when Nova caught him going too low for a spinebuster, possibly. Nova quickly sprung up, slingshotted onto the apron and vaulted himself into Ryan's rising body with a Springboard Spinning Elbow Smash that sent the big Texan tumbling through the opposite ropes, and to the outside of the ring. Nova didn't waste any time giving these London fans a little taste of ULTRATITLE highlights as he shot himself through the ropes connecting with a suicide plancha. He also didn't happen to see Dan Ryan make the tag to Sean Stevens as he fell through the ropes.

Nova mocked a doobie hit, turned around and got himself jacked up by a Flipping Neckbreaker off the apron by the man known as XXXtasy. Nova got tossed back in, but Stevens found out like so many that Nova's one of the toughest wrestlers to put away. The near pinfall got some crowd whistles, but this one wasn't over. Stevens and Ryan started working over the Season 2 Champion. . . some highlights were the powerslam / top rope elbow combo, and Stevens leaping off Ryan's shoulders with a frog splash. The last one having to be the end in Stevens' eyes, but moreso Stark. Just as Nova kicked out, Stevens found himself on the receiving end of a kick by Stark to his head. Stevens tumbled over, and that's all Dan Ryan needed to jet in the ring. As the ref tried stop Ryan, Stark rushed him from behind and tossed him towards Ryan, who dodged. . . letting the ref slam his head into the turnbuckles.

Stark caught Ryan with an unsuspecting eye poke, then a vicious right fist. Stark quickly got out of the ring and went for a Springboard Front Flip Neckbreaker. . . and he got welcomed to NFW rudely by the MASSIVE Dan Ryan, who caught him, lifted. . . and Powerbombed him! Meanwhile, Nova had recovered and somehow turned the tides on Stevens by pushing him off of a Cutter attempt into the turnbuckles. Nova turned around and came face to face with Dan Ryan forearm crushing his head. Nova fell out the ring (the legal man mind you) Quickly, Ryan grabbed Stark and blasted his face with vicious elbow blasts and just as he let go. . .THE X-FACTOR.

Stevens made the pin attempt, the groggy ref made the 3 count and it was over. Nova. . .tried to explain that he was the legal man, but the ref couldn't even keep his eyes uncrossed until medics came down. In an unfortunate situation, DC Stark was holding his shoulder after being helped back during the post-match . . . possibly suffering an injury at the hands of Dan Ryan.

That's all for now, next week we're apparently heading to Scotland. . . I'm officially fearing a Beau Michaels kilt match.
