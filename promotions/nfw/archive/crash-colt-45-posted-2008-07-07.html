Legion — 7 July 2008 Big feedback coming up! To start with Joe's segment: the feud between Sars and Joe is going to be very screwed up (just check every woman that shows an interest in ya Joe) ;-)
\M/- the return of Facey and Nova as a duo makes me smile more so with the 'Terminator' style vibe (hell even the Cable comic recently does the same), will the Time Baby be nFW's saviour?
grand Prix qualifiers- Legion facing Pollard made sense given our little rp before the show and the tit for tat game between Legion and von Doom got upped (reading the part *spoiler* with the double Urange made me chuckle), one sign in the crowd asking for a von Doom/Legion deathmatch may be coming to fruition... Hostile Negotiations- This was one of my fave parts of the show (mostly because i like seeing how others are writing the character particularly when it's people as skilled as Felix), using the Girl talk album as background music made me laugh, though I have no idea what Sparks is (damn British eh :P) but Legion seems to be getting a pile of adversaries to reach the sky which has me thinking of using a OLD stipulation not seen since one of the first few shows ;-)
Sars segment was insane... when the Rejects starts to become a more fully fledged 'stable' I wouldn't mind having him on the team :-)
3 O Clock special- Nice to see the New School get the win and will be interesting how the NFW's equivalent of Money In The Bank ends up
Burning The Boats- My God, Segment of the night here, to start with a 'Director's cut' of the Wrestlestock II match then have Shalmaylan get killed (though i would have gone Hail Mother Nature whilst killing him... :P) THEN the history of Legion (Napoleon saying the catchphrase was funny as hell)
Uber/'Rook'/Knox- to quote Knox 'Awesome', the army of Uber should be fun also (Mega Job cameo = win)
Main events- TQ Vs Nova was always going to be close to call but the result was satisfying as Teresa kept trying but realised that sometimes it's best to end it, gather the bearings then try again
HWC Vs Suicide Kings was an epic, Blaine's actions throughout epitomise a 'dick heel' and the never say die attitude of DC and Tsunami gave me the feeling of a Rock and roll Vs Midnight express match from the 80's
All in all one of the shows of the season and can't wait for Night 2 of the grand Prix (though considering who is in that, kinda glad I 'lost', not that good yet lol)
Nova — 7 July 2008 Man, these Supercard-esque events between periods of lower-key fed progression are always so good, and I think this one rivals WRESTLESTOCK 2 for our strongest effort of 2008. Most noticeably, this show saw the NFW roster at its most excited in a long time - by that I mean not only good RPs for show, which there were, but a lot of segments (by our standards), which generally are a rarity and reserved for Legion's plot advancement or appearances by the Professionals.:D I really liked it, a wide variety of offerings from a scathing Sars rant to, um, lunacy from Joe with an assist from yours truly and Katzenburg, to Teresa employing almost an RP approach to a pre-match seg, to...well, on and on.
There were a ton of matches as well, but the show didn't feel choppy or disjointed. It felt like a single production, which was one of the strongest points about WRESTLESTOCK.
Joe the Plumber getting his two cents in was great after a long absence, and targeting Sars was smart, because those two seemed destined to foulmouth it off at some point, properly one-on-one. Only in NFW is even Facey rendered somewhat normal by the presence of such lunatics.
*SPOILERS ALERT* It's nice to see Alyas and the Dream Team get served up title shots, better that a tag team get one of the bids to hopefully ride this momentum into the future that has been built around the Suicide Kingz/HWC dominated division. I hope that HWC continue bringing the heat, even though JN probably doesn't have a TON of time to devote to the team. Alyas is an open book on which shot he'll choose (that IS how it works, right?), he's a legit threat to any champion, me included.
Segway into props for Brandyn on a hard-fought match, one to one I had trouble picking a favorite. The match was great. Katz has translated things out of my RPs into developments on card that are fun for me to read as Nova's handler, and the direction the character's headed is part familiar territory, and part - yes, I'll say it - NEW FRONTIER! *Cough* I must say I don't envy Brandyn caught between Pants v2.0 w/ Uber Judge and a determined Renner with FW Wrestler of the Month Steve Knox.
I marked for a seg from Hiroshi, who reminded us what we had been missing in his absence, that slick heel instantly back in Power Player mode that the 'Dub doesn't really have besides perhaps Eddie Mayfield, but he's separated by his administrative (if sadistic) post at the head of the Wrestling Revolution.
And I MUST give a tip of the hat to the architects of the EMT match. You can tell we have serious tag aficionados in this fed, not the least of which is the fedhead, and it shines through in these HWC/Suicide Kingz matchups, because they read smoother than a Grey Goose martini! It was huge, but also hyped to the moon and easily more emotional than the other matches on the card, which isn't to say they weren't anticipated reads, but this one was built differently and rightfully billed as the true Main Event of Crash 'Colt' 45.
Really great show. Looking forward to seeing what gets booked on the next card, as well as the results of the bizarre Grand Prix Qualifiers Part Deux.

suddenimpact — 10 July 2008 Running from top to bottom is easiest for me... The continued evolution of the Brock Alyas story has been very intriguing to me. He's being put through the paces by Miles and Eddie, but he's also one of the last people that I think you'd want on your bad side. Now he's got a partner who busted him out (I assume "busted" since he's still shackled), whoever it is is going to mean very bad things for El Presidente, I think.
I don't have much to say about the Grand Prix qualifiers, two matches were JIPs and mine was, well, MINE. The whole concept is a good one that doesn't happen often enough in real or F wrestling, the lighter guys getting the shine. However the Grand Prix participants end up going at each other will probably be another clusterF worthy of the NFW, so I can't wait.
And Joe. Ahhh, Joe. I've been waiting to hear from Joe the Plumber since Wrestlestock, he's still got that belt and a lot of axes to grind - particularly against SARS. Of course, SARS seems to have his sights on Impulse, too, so there's plenty of dischord in and about. Dischord makes wrestling companies go 'round.
And Kin. Kin Hiroshi! What needs to be said? Uber Judge has me confused. I'm a Steve Knox mark so I've got no problems with how the match ended, but I can't even speculate on how that angle is going to end up. WCW did a similar thing with Sting and the company went down like Calvin Carlton's momma. Of course, Ben is much smarter than that, but I'm watching.
PAGE BREAK: "The Seventh Sin Is Upon Our Loins" needs to be the tagline for a future PPV. I love me some Nova. There, I said it. Teresa may very well be a future World Champion but Nova needs to hold the belt for a good long time, at least as long as it took for him to get to that match at Wrestlestock. Of course, I'm a fan of Teresa, too, so it was hard for me to pick a winner, or say good or bad about the finish either way other than my feelings towards the belt at this point, but Lamont had the best line for it:
Tripped-out AcidFreak Stoners never react well to Smartass Pagan Cult Scholars It's true. And after two readings of the main event, I can only say one thing. Applause. Where do we go from here... the 'established' wrestlers will try to maintain their spotlight while the Grand Prix participants try to redirect the shine. There's natural tension between the two just as there is among them, and I hope we can exploit it in the coming weeks.

PS - Wicked show! -Macc Frozen Atlantic — 10 July 2008 Well, due to the overwhelming amounts of awesome presented in this show, it took me a while to drop my thoughts. Overall, I should say off the top - I'm always impressed by the PBP skills of the matchwriters. Yall do good work, you make it possible, and I know myself that every little bit is important.
Joe's promo was... um...a Joe promo, and a feud with Sars will be pretty interesting to say the least. The Felix/Legion segment was my favorite of the night. Hilarious, classic Felix stuff.
"Tripped-out AcidFreak Stoners never react well to Smartass Pagan Cult Scholars " - Line of the night. Great match, and Nova definently earned that big W. My own segment could have used a little polish, but eh. Dem's the breaks, little man.
The National Title 3 way was almost as good as the promos for it. :) I haven't been as FOCUSED on the tag scene as of late, but I was so surprised that they were main eventing the show that I watched it with laser focus. Gotta tell you, the move worked - I'm intensely interested in what happens moving forward. Written very classic, very old school.
All in all, another good show. Proud to keep the NFW spirit alive.
