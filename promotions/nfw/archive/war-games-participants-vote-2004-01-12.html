members of this league. No reason to think they'll stop doing that now. Gregg's character had talked about there being some sort of trade or move on the boards. I would have no problem if all of the North or all of the South in the War Games were to recieve the points if they win the match, except if there is that trade that Gregg hinted at and it involves a war games member, it is sort of unfair if one team wins the War Games and a wrestler from that team is traded to the other side with the additional points because that throws off the standings we had even more coming into of the Allstar Break.
The NFW North race has been really heated and ten points are going to mean an awful lot. If someone from the North is heading South or someone from the South is heading north and is in the War Games match, why should someone get extra 10 or 15 points in a division that they didn't earn the points in and in a match that was pre-booked?
For instance, if Marx was traded to the South after the North won War Games, if the North was rewarded 10 points, that would knock Rabeque out of the leader slot and Steel Viper or Cameron Cruise out of a playoffs spot complete completely.... leaving the people not in the Playoffs like McMillan, Poe, and Pariah in even more of a bind than they would face even with just the trade. How is that fair to the NFW South? Sure, it is fair to the people being traded, but the rest... not so much.
If there were no trades coming, I'd be fine with it because if there was no movement, everyone would be in the same position where they were before and it wouldn't really effect the NFW North or NFW South all that much.
