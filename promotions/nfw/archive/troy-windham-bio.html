Troy Windham Biography Eye Color Brown Nicknames The Epitome, The King of the Slackers, Wrestling Jesus, Hollywood's Biggest Star Hometown various residences around the world Birthday Sweetwater, Texas Height 6'6" Weight 225 Biography Troy Windham's name means a lot of things to a lot of people. To some, he is the industry's biggest star. To others, he is an overrated slacker who is the best example of all that is wrong with today's media-driven culture. Windham grew up in West Texas in a tough household. He grew up constantly in the shadow of his older brother (then thought to be his cousin due to a tragic family secret revealed years later) Mark. Mark Windham was an All-American athlete and honors student in high school. Troy always rebelled against Mark and tried to make his own mark as a young rebel drawn to rock music, fast cars and faster women. However, Troy was a gifted athlete -- many said he was even more naturally talented physically than Mark, whom had set Texas state records in football, wrestling and track. But Troy's disdain for authority and practice led many to write him off. After graduation, Troy entered the University of Texas on a wrestling scholarship. While in Austin in the early-90's, Windham became attracted to the "alternative" music scene which was breaking nationwide. Troy became very interested in the nightlife of rock clubs and the anti-authoritarian views of people such as Kurt Cobain and Eddie Vedder. Troy was soon kicked off the wrestling team -- coaches and teammates did not like his attitude -- and he soon flunked out of college (despite having an I.Q. in the 150s). Troy eventually ended up getting hired in the CSWA, using the connections of is brother, whom along with Hornet propelled the league to greatness. Windham was quiet the first year in his career, warned by the veterans in the back to not speak or act out of line or face banishment from the sport. But, as always, Windham would not remain quiet for long. Troy soon decided to break free from Mark's shadow. He did this by proclaiming himself "The Gen X Icon" and "The King of the Slackers" -- wearing band T-Shirts to the ring, openly bragging about not caring about winning or losing as long as he got laid, talking on national television about his drug use (and the drug use of other stars) and his dreams of becoming an actor at the expense of his wrestling career. Windham, very quickly, became the CSWA's most controversial superstar. Windham would soon pair up with another brash, young star -- "Showtime" Shawn Matthews, forming a duo called "The Gen X-Press." The two quickly became the hottest tag team in the sport. But clashes between the two quickly developed. Matthews became known as the star of the team and Windham soon became silent again -- battling with a severe drug addiction. However, Windham and Matthews would break ways temporarilly and Windham would once again become a star on his own. Around this time, the UWA was reforming. Troy hatched a plan. He started showing up at UWA cards with placards demanding the UWA "Show Him The Money" to borrow a popular phrase from a hit film at the time. To everyone's surprise, Troy's campaign became wildly popular with the fans who started chanting his name at every turn. The fans had clicked with Troy Windham's anti-hero persona and had wanted to see him more and more. The UWA was painted in a corner and then-president Quentin Sullivan agreed to sign Troy Windham to the biggest contract ever signed in the history of the industry. This, naturally, did not go over well with other UWA combatants. And Troy Windham -- along with his co-horts at the time, a group known as The Frat -- would be the most sought-after man in the sport, especially by his many enemies. Windham was still wrestling in the CSWA at this time. Windham had his first World Title match against then champion GUNS -- another controversial figure bent on destroying the CSWA. After a series of events, Troy Windham managed to pin GUNS in the middle of the ring as Hornet -- GUNS' arch-rival whom could not stop GUNS -- counted three. The symbolism was clear -- Windham had his hand raised and won the sports most prestigious title and had the torch handed to him by its greatest legend in the process. During this period, Windham's popularity as a wrestler had also propelled him to fame outside the wrestling ring. A known ladies man, Windham soon became a sponsor for a variety of products and landed roles in a series of UPN television shows and direct-to-video movies. Windham has also been a constant media presence, appearing on the Howard Stern Show or Regis or on various VH-1 "Best of..." episodes. In recent years, Windham has largely focused his attention on the league he first called home, now dubbing himself "Mr. CSWA." Windham has largely been a hero in this promotion, facing a wide array of challenges. Most memorably, he took place in what many have called the greatest feud in wrestling history: The ClaimStakers vs. The Playboys. Windham, paired with Eddy Love, did battle across the nation against the triumverate of Mike Randalls, Hornet and Windham's longtime rival Eli Flair. During this time period (2000-2001), Windham wrestled in a series what many consider to be four of the greatest matches in wrestling history. Match One was "60 Minutes In Mobile" which saw him and Eddie tag against Hornet and Eli in an Ironman match regarded as the best tag match in CSWA history. Match Two saw Troy/Eddy Love and Lawrence Stanley defeat The Claimstakers in the greatest six-man tag match in CSWA history. And then Windham and Eli Flair had two matches which were perhaps the most violent matches in CSWA History -- one was a cage match which saw the two brawl all over the arena, including a dive off the balcony by Windham and saw both men fall through the cage. The second was an "I Quit" match where Eli Flair damaged Windham's already-fused neck severely and broke every bone from his elbow to his fingers on his left arm. Since that time period, Windham has surfaced in bits and pieces -- mostly in surprising fashion. He briefly appeared in the GWE promotion, prompting a riot after he sprayed mace in the front row in an attempt to shut down the league. Most recently, he has appeared in the NFW. He, along with Eddie Mayfield, engineered a plan which ended with the two of them physically destroying Craig Miles at the Superbowl of Wrestling. WIndham claims he wants to destroy the NFW since he was not included in Season One. Through it all, Windham has shown himself to always be a major player. He's its biggest star and its most vapid personality. Its biggest slacker and its toughest veteran. He's all things to all people in the sport. Other Feds CSWA, UWA Other Fed Titles Former CSWA World Champion Wrestling Style Versatile -- strength against smaller foes, speed against bigger foes, a wide-array of brawling tactics and technically sound. Uses a lot of quick, out-of-nowhere types of moves for quick pin attempts Finisher Move The Autograph aka The SlackKnife Finisher Description Swing-action, fast-moving Reverse Neckbreaker. Like a Reverse Diamond Cutter Other Moves DDT, West Texan lariat, STF, West Texan Bulldog, Windham Claw, Spinning Discus Punch, eye rake, Elbow smash Entrance Music "Beverly Hills" by Weezer, "Song 2" by Blur
