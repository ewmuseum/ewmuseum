What's the Deal, NFW?

6 May 2005

Off to Valinor — 6 May 2005 In case any of you were wondering why the first two cards aren't posted yet... The league won't be starting until the website is fully functioning. Please bear with me on this because I'm trying to get a site put together that would allow for NFW to continue even if I'm not an integral part of it.
Right now, the main issue is that [name withheld] is the busiest person I know. He's also the guy that I struck a deal with to donate CGI coding that will allow handlers to post their own segments/matches, maintain their biographies and post their own news.
Its also a system where administrators can post cards, update the site, etc. Basically, that means once we get the code it'll be possible for someone not named Katz to run Season 3...or for the handlers to run it themselves. We've got a unique concept going and I don't think the league should fold because I don't have the time for it.
As far as the graphic design goes, I've completed the main page layout as well as the posted card layout. I'm going to design the Roster Page once I see what I can do with Travis' code. Sub-pages for events, roster, general roleplays, news, etc will be done in CSS and I've already constructed the column structure.
As far as the cards go: The West Preseason Show is complete sans a few things I wanted to add. You know...riots and O'Connor monologues. :cool: The first East Show doesn't have any short recaps done, also I haven't received [name withheld]'s match (but I'm pretty sure he's finished).
Anyway, if you want to help with any aspects of making sure we're ready for launch when I get Travis' code, I could use anyone with skills to do wrestler logos/match logos if they're interested.
Feel free to ask questions under this thread about anything related to the fed, as well. HOPEFULLY, in accordance with NFW's 5th birthday (May 30, 2000) we'll have that pre-season show up and the site running. But as usual...no promises. ;)
--Admiral Akbar Angelo Deville — 7 May 2005 Could we possibly slow it down a touch? Can't. Keep. Up. >=) Seriously, no worries, Akbar ~salutes~. I'm ready and rarin' to go as soon as the site is.
