Anyway, with all these non-feedback related comments this shindig really needs a kick off, so here goes:
Segment of the night: The Inaugural DYNASTY Tea Social. So utterly obnoxious, it was heeltastic. By now, Dorchester has been transformed to the point where I've almost forgotten he was ever a backyard wrestler. Carlton's snobbery here was A+. Wildstar showing up was the icing on the cake, plus he had my favorite line of the night.
Other segments I liked: American Panda is f*cking hilarious, and the premise behind his emergence is even more so. The Mayfield stuff was solid, and I especially liked the swerve at the end between him and Blaine Hollywood.
Just a minor note: "Sudden...Castor" was co-written by myself and [name withheld], despite what the credits tell you. :D
Line of the night: WILDSTAR: "Pardon me, do you have any Gray Poupon?" That and CC detailing his family's faberge easter egg hunts had me rolling.
Match of the Night: The World Championship Gauntlet was great. The ending set up a ton of intrigue for Cras 50 and is going to wind up pumping more energy into the league. Quatro Equis vs. High Flyer and Bat-pulse was really well done; I'm a big fan of Equis and Flyer. Oh yeah, and whoever wrote BND vs. Rookpulse must be a pretty bright fellow. :cool:
Wrestler of the Night: Impulse...for taking his beating like a man! ;-) General Thoughts: Obviously, the card was awesome. Beyond that general praise, I thought the angles/promos/segments did some really important things for the league. The Mayfield Presidency storyline was given a bunch of new layers in lieu of the scuffle with Blaine Hollywood and possible friction with CC. We're also seeing him interact more with other wrestlers, which allows his Presidency to take shape and possibly go in other directions. JTP came off like a total badass, further establishing himself as the "unbeatable champion" by wrecking a gauntlet of challengers. With the announcement of the Pentagram match, he's got some real opponents to think about, plus all the extra intrigue. Wildstar vs. Dorchester is gonna be off the hook... (Gregg versus HIMSELF! Who wins??). The stipulation is pretty cool.
As for the stuff I'm doing with Pete, I think we have the Castor/Impulse feud in a really good place right now. I was thrilled with how everything turned out, and we have more stuff coming down the pike.
The last two Crashes have NFW in a good spot, feud wise. Not only do you have the main event rolling along, but now you've got Wildstar/Dorchester/Dynasty thrown in, Castor/Impulse, High Flyer/Unicorn, and a bunch of cool things happening up and down the roster.
