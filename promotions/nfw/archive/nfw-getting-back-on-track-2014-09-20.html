Kind of a mini-report for you, as I've been in a test of strength up against Reloaded 22. I made some traction earlier in the week on it, but when it's done, we're all going to have to figure out the next steps to keep the train moving forward. I never expected to be away from all of this so long, and It's getting harder to apply pressure to this so I'm going to have to find some sort of mid-ground for all of us to work from.
I'm glad that (some) of you guys are still around, and I apologize for the lack of content. I'm not asking for a 'role-call', but I am curious about what you guys have been doing or who's still going to hang in there with us as we get to FUTURESHOCK 2.
