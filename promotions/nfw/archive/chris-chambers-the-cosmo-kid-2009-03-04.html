Chris Chambers & The Cosmo Kid

4 March 2009

renner — 4 March 2009 I meant to put this up the other day. Although Steve Knox refers to these guys as "the Posse", the team name of "Chris Chambers & the Cosmo Kid" is probably more accurate.
-- --- CHRIS CHAMBERS Full Name: Christopher MacGuyver Chambers (so he says, anyway) Age: 24 (born January 17th) Height: 6' Weight: 208 lbs. Hometown: The Mean Streets of Chi-Town~! (okay, he's actually from Topeka, Kansas) Trainer(s): Jeff Garvin / Steve Knox Wrestling Style: Brawler Alignment: Heel Handedness: Right Entrance Music: "Country Grammar" by Nelly (as a team, Chambers and Kid use this as their theme song)
Primary Finishers: - Kansas City Drive-By (Running Yakuza Kick) - Kansas City Remix (Wrist clutch big boot jawbreaker (Chuck Taylor's "Sole Food")) - Chambers Special V (Quick, tight small package) Nicknames: "Double Cheh" (because his name is Chris Chambers) Hair Color: Dyed lilac, actually blonde Eye Color: Brown Notes: - He was thought to have been trained by Jeff Garvin, but it's more likely that Garvin never really trained him in offensive moves. As a result, Chambers' style was virtually nonexistent until Steve Knox took him under his wing. His style now is more of a brawling style, relying on punches, kicks, quick jawbreakers, the rare suplex, and lots of elbow drops and knee drops. He isn't remotely fancy, but he's surprisingly effective.
- Many people underestimate him because he spent so long as a jobber and because of his current persona. As a result, they're frequently taken aback by his sudden ability to kick people's teeth in.
- He's now known for his ability to suddenly shoot a Kansas City Drive-By into heavy traffic and yet always finding his mark.
- Chambers' persona is like a marriage of his old "Ludachris" gimmick with his impressionable rookie days in Action! Wrestling. The most important thing to remember is that he has a tendency to break character if asked a question he can answer intelligently. Since he breaks character like this, it's very clear that the way he acts is all an act and that he's much, MUCH smarter than he seems.
--- THE COSMO KID Full Name: Unknown Age: Unknown Height: 5'7" Weight: 175 lbs. Hometown: "The Cosmos" (or, otherwise, a fancy way of saying "Unknown") Trainer(s): Joey Malone Wrestling Style: Luchador Alignment: Heel Handedness: Right Entrance Music: "Electricity" by the Avalanches Primary Finishers: - Cosmo Star Suplex (Cross-legged northern lights bomb) - Cosmo Star Splash (810 splash; double somersault into a splash) Nicknames: C.K. Hair Color: Unknown, likely black. Eye Color: Purple (due to contacts) Notes: - The Cosmo Kid wrestles like your traditional luchador, utilizing a lot of flying attacks. He wears a mask (a purple one with a white star print and alien antennae coming out of the top), but he also wrestles in a black business suit with a red tie and white undershirt, so his attire might not be so traditional.
- Cosmo also has a very deep voice despite being a clearly white luchador. He's been described as sounding like Barry White.
- He has the ability to always know what's going on, even when he logicially shouldn't know. He's quite talented at keeping the details to himself, though.
- Oh, as far as wrestling goes, he's your typical luchador and he's not very big. He most relies on big spots, but he also has a lot of elaborate submissions and matwork he'll do if his opponent is a similar size to himself. Expect lots of flying headscissors and crazy planchas from this guy.
