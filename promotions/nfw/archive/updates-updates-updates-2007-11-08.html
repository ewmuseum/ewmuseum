Off to Valinor — 8 November 2007 Just a general note to those of you that are lurking around the forums. :) If you're interested in joining the fed without the hassles of a long application process, we're holding an open invitational for our tag titles (http://fwrestling.com/fwc/showthread.php?t=16607). We've got about 24 hours to finish off the list and I've definitely been PMing some of our missing vets to try and make things very exciting. Of course, its a great way to start in the fed as well, so all you lurkers should give it a shot!
We've also put up an email list that will keep fans, friends and roster members for NFW all together as one. Sign up here! (http://fwrestling.com/fwc/showthread.php?t=16616)
Crash 42's lineup has been posted (http://fwrestling.com/fwc/showthread.php?t=16650)and it looks like it'll be a highly surreal card as we've got some very big bruhahahas going down as well as some auspicious live debuts for an influx of new and old NFW faces.
This weekend I'll be posting up the brackets for the tag title invitational, as well as revealing its official name. From there, those RP battles will burn and pillage alongside the Crash 42 RP Battles. All in all, it should be a very interesting Mid-Late November here on the Astral Plane.
In regards to our website, I'm still tinkering with things slowly. I've also got to help the resident FW Enforcer, [name withheld] with the main site. So, we may start pointing the bells and whistles to that site for a month or two. We're definitely going to need columnists, help...so I'll be posting about that sort of stuff in awhile.
As far as our real website...I've got alot of things planned and on the backburner. An awesome dude has put together some very awesome PHP things and we're going to have one crazy website that will not only have all our shows archived, but you'll be able to search the website for your character's roleplays from FWC and any show they've been mentioned on...just by typing their name in a search box. Like I said, very awesome things await.
Of course, I'm not unleashing that beast until we get some more important things down... Like getting shows out at a good pace, building a core roster and developing the forums community into something fun.
So yeah, updates, updates, updates...and now back to work!
