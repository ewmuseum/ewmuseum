WestPREZ — 3 July 2007 NOTE: This is Hellmachine's old bio from their creator [name withheld]. They are now retired, and will not return to the ring. They are the hosts of NFW Brawl (http://fwrestling.com/fwc/showthread.php?t=15865).
MEMBER INFO ---------------------- MEMBER Name:[name withheld] Email Address: [detail withheld] WRESTLER INFO ------------------- Wrestler Name: HELL MACHINE (Two man tagteam plus valet/manager: Calamity, "Big In Japan" Sean Toombs, and "Doc" Martina Martens) Height: Calamity 6'4" Big In Japan 6'9" "Doc" Martina Martens 5'4" Weight: Calamity 230 Big In Japan 320 "Doc" Martina Martens 120 Hailing From: "...the remorseless arenas of the Pacific Rim ..." Handedness: Calamity, Big In Japan - right "Doc" Martina Martens - left Gimmick: As HellMachine, the duo exist to bring total anarchy to the ring. Somewhere between industrial, Hess, goth and raver, they don't particularly aim to win titles or even matches, but simply to create as apocalyptic a match as possible Ring Attire: Calamity - green-and-yellow tunic, green-and-yellow goggles, hunter green dungarees. His gloves have the words "KaBoom" and "Whupass" written on the fingers of each hand. Big In Japan - Always wears a process tie-dye sleeveless tee and overalls Looks: See http://ape-law.com/wustudio/alien/nfw_hellmachinepic.jpg and http://ape-law.com/wustudio/alien/nfw_docmartinamartens.jpg Ring Entrance: Calamity is a hyperactive bundle of activity, usually running from side to side of the ramp and talking trash straight up to the ring, if not to the audience as well. He often gets to the ring several seconds before Big in Japan, who walks slowly and with great determination in a straight line to the mat. Calamity also often performs his signature taunt, the "Double Dukestacy," where he puts both hands flipping the bird above his head, and then makes a "suck it" crotch chop motion. Theme Music: "Volume 4" by the Rollins Band Tactics/Style: The duo tries to cover all bases, Calamity is a high- flying big man (a la Awesome/Jericho in the Japanese eras), and is known for high risk manuevers. Big In Japan is muscle and strength, he barrels his way through opponents and does the grunt work in setting up the finisher. "Doc" Martens has some in-ring experience, but is primarily a ringside manager (though she interferes as a valet is expected to). 5 Specialty Moves: Calamity: Suicidal Tope con hilo, Snap Suplex, springboard quebrada, swinging DDT, frankensteiner Big In Japan: powerbomb, bulldog, full body press, sidewalk slam, german suplex Finisher & Set-Up Moves: The Calamity-ville Horror (Calamity's finisher, an irish whip into the ropes resulting in a Northern Lights Suplex bridge-to-pin) The Toolbox (Combo finisher) BiJ drops the opponent in a sit- down Powerbomb, Calamity moonsaults from a position at a ninety- degree angle to connect just as their opponent hits the mat. Strengths: The duo have been performing a long time, and they have their moves down PAT. They know when to hand off to each other for maximum effectiveness. "Doc" Martens is an inspired manager, and her knowledge and refined instincts make her a devastating tool in the HellMachine arsenal. Weaknessess: Calamity would rather perform a time-consuming, hi-risk maneuver when a knife-edge chop would do just fine - his reach often escapes his grasp. Big In Japan is not the most imaginative wrestler, and very often looks to Calamity and "Doc" Martens for his lead, otherwise sticking to his brute strength moves. Although a tight knit team, they have a tendency to try to one-up one another, sometimes at the cost of their ring work..
