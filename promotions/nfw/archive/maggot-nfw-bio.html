Maggot Biography Eye Color Hazel Hometown Pottstown, PA Birthday March 15, 1970 Height 6'6 Weight 254 Biography Maggot. The One Man Mafia. He's a pain freak. Both giving and receiving. His threshhold for pain is legendary, but his capacity to dish out punishment is even more iconic. He's a simple man, but sometimes, that veneer will hide his more cunning qualities. I could sit here all day and describe Maggs to you, but to really know the man who is set to take NFW by storm, you must know his past. Born on the Ides of March, it was a clear omen for the kind of life Maggot would lead. His father was an abusive alcoholic, his mother high-tailed it out of there when he was 12, probably because she didn't like being her husband's punching bag anymore. Maggot himself left home at the age of 16 after getting into an altercation with his father. He dropped out of high school and lived life for the next four years as a transient. He was belligerent, and often brawled with other bums over cans of beans. All throughout the Philadelphia region he made his way on train cars and sneaking onto the wonderful world of SEPTA transportation. Luckily for him, the growing boom of garbage wrestling in the Philadelphia region around that time would save Maggs from his life of hobodom. A local hardcore fed owner noticed him brawling one day, using anything he could get his hands on to pummel the other bum. He quickly scooped him up and put him on some local indie shows. Representatives from the honorable PBPro promotion started taking notice of Maggot in the ring and ended up signing him to be a part of their fledgling garbage arm, called Warfare, Inc. Maggot quickly rose as the top gaijin, and his matches were renowned for their gore and sheer brutality. It was also in Japan where he met the original Monsieur Fantastic, six-time LCWE Champion and probably the most legendary European wrestler this side of Andre the Giant. Mssr. F constantly badgered Maggot to try and learn how to wrestle and rise above the garbage. Maggot refused originally, but as he saw wrestler after wrestler rise abvoe him on card position and in payday, he reluctantly went under the wing of the legendary Frenchman. After training with Mssr. F, Maggot ended up being snatched up by Big Japan, where he gained an even huger cult following. His puroresu career ended when he separated both his shoulders taking an Awesome Bomb off the top of a stage. He returned to America to recuperate, where he was noticed by a fedhead who'd change his life... After his injuries had healed, Maggot showed resistance to go back to Japan, if only because he had grown tired of the recklessness of the competitors in Big Japan and because he had missed the states. No companies in America were willing to give him a shot other than a company out of Philadelphia run by Tom Holzerman, Jr. known as the EWA. Maggot signed on with them in 1996, and quickly rose up the ranks, gaining the American and then International Championships, as well as becoming perhaps the most over face on the roster. Maggot's ascent came to a screeching halt in October of 1999 when he blew out his knee in a match with Ronnie McDade. It most undoubtedly cost him his shot at the EWA World Title. He came back six months later, but he was soon on his way out of the company to sign with MBE in late 2000. Maggot debuted in November of 2000, joining the MidWest Mafia, v. 2.0 almost on arrival. He became the stable's hired muscle, giving and taking ass-kickings for the Red Don (who was later revealed to be BobbyR). He won the Extreme Title in only his second match in the fed but lost it two weeks later to Mindbender. Still, it didn't hinder his ascent, taking the fight to established MBE superstars such as PILE, Duchess, Joe Retro (who was the Spoiler without his mask) and Freakfish. In fact, he performed so well in a tag match with BobbyR against Retro and then-MBE Champion Freakfish that he was granted a shot at the MBE Championship the next week. He didn't win, but he gave Double F all he could handle. Thereafter, Maggot went on to win the MBE Unified Championship in overtime of the legendary Homefield Advantage Match over Andrew Gilkison and LuvGun. He held the title until the next PPV, where he lost to WhiteNoise. But something else happened at that event that would change the course of Maggot's career in MBE. The MidWest Mafia, under growing tension from the returning founder of the MwM Irish Red and his cousin Eliminat0r, the Mafia split at the seams. Maggot responded by trashing BobbyR's locker room, and then after Red was put in prison and Eliminat0r was chased from the fed, destroyed the MidWest Mafia Bar. After this, Maggot was on the hunt for more gold. He entered a tournament for the vacant North American Championship, which culminated in a Four Way Ladder Match at High Stakes with Tillrules, AussieB and WhiteNoise. The match ended with Maggot and WhiteNoise both in possession of the title and the Championship held up for another week. Later on in that card, WhiteNoise revealed himself to be part of an insidious alliance with the World Champion Freakfish and MBE Owner Mr. Amazing! to run wild on MBE and basically control it with an iron fist. This did not set well with Maggot, as it hindered his chances to get the North American Championship he wanted now with the added muscle behind WhiteNoise. Maggot declared war on the new faction, known as Amazico. The next week at the MBE Arena, Maggot crawled through the ducts and invaded Mr. Amazing!'s office to deliver him a personal message before the Hell in the Cell SCramble for hte Title match between he and WhiteNoise later in the card. It was here Maggot made an uncharacteristic alliance with Housefly to help combat against Amazico, and also where the "One Man Mafia" nickname was born. Maggot won the match with the help of both Fly and Spoiler, who still had issues with Freakfish after being screwed out of the MBE Title. Amazico didn't take lightly to being outwitted like that, so they took their aggressions out on Maggot, attacking him at every turn. The war was stepped up. Maggot responded by donning the Fantasti-mask and parading as Monsieur Fantastic, his mentor, to play mind-games with Amazico, with them none the wiser. Simultaneously, Maggot still defended his North American Title. After losing the North American Championship to BobbyR, Maggot put all his focus on bringing Freakfish and Amazico down. At the next PPV, Blaze of Glory, Maggot's resistance, which included Housefly and Beast at the time, took on the Amazico in a staggered entry elimination HITC match. Maggot's teammates were eliminated early in the match, and facing four on one odds for nearly the entire match, Maggot was seemingly trapped. However, he eliminated three of them before succumbing to Freakfish. After Blaze of Glory, Maggot was unmasked as being Mssr. F!, and a the match with Freakfish was set for Northern Explosion. They faced off in a Hardcore Hell match, a brutal setting where the ring is encased in Plexiglas and the temperature is set at 100 degrees Fahrenheit with 75% humidity and an array of hardcore weapons is littered in teh ring. It was an utter war of attrition, both men destroying each other's bodies until finally, Maggot came out victorious. After finally settling the matters with Double F, there was only one more place for Maggot to go: the MBE World Heavyweight Championship. He battled Spoiler for the title in England unsuccessfully, but at Total Elimination in December of 2001, Maggot went through the entire MBE roster before finally vanquishing The Force of Nature and capturing the World Championship. He held the title for eight and a half months, taking on challenges from former Champions such as Freakfish and Professor Tremendous and former allies who had betrayed him such as Housefly. Maggot had an iron grip on the title until Hida Yakamo won it from him. After losing the title and the rematch, Maggot moved down, winning the North American title again and then trying for the Tag Titles with Guerilla unsuccessfully. Maggot left MBE in the beginning of 2003. He returned to the EWA for a short time, finally winning that World Championship before NFW came-a-calling. Now, Maggot is back where he started, at least in the country where he started. If an extended run in the playoffs is in store, it's all gravy, but as long as he gets to bust some skulls, it's all good!
