NFW Brawl 12: NEW YORK CITY 17 May 2008 Madison Square Garden, New York City, New York (seats 20,000) Intro Only the "most rock n’ roll rock n’ roll wrestling fed in history" (that’s right Melody Maker said it TWICE) would decide to hold a 17,000 person Memorial’s Weekend indoor beerfest, industry insider invitation event right in the heart of New York City’s Star Junct—I mean Times Square. Welcome to Madison Square Garden. Welcome to NFW. I’m Gordon. GEKKO, THE GECK, Lizard Dick or BlackSnatch Flicka on 78 coffees, 521 quaaludes, 3 hits of crack-cocaine, 24 tabs of acid, 5 barrels of Craig Miles Murfreesboro Moonshine, 2 ounces of hooker…extract? (sigh – I don’t even get how it required delousing me in some creepy Brooklyn hospital) 96 Buds, 96 oz. of "Buds" has passed through our "Business Services" Captain Delivery Dude…and enough Vivarin to feel them piled up in the back of my throat. They say "Understanding Corporate America" – a 12-week crashcourse internship recognized by Harvard as the "Sinner’s Guide to Successful Retirement, if you don’t quit by day 4," could be revolutionizing the sports media and entertainment industry. I didn’t even apply. I GOT F*CKING KIDNAPPED AND BLOWN BY A SWEDISH MASSEUSE THAT KNOWS ABOUT MUSCLES IN HER… Y’know, I think I should take it back to MSG at this point… Knicks, Rangers and Big East traces were shrouded in black covers, but cleverly with artwork resembling pavement on a racetrack. Checkered flags hung from the rafters everywhere…I’d say it were a tribute to Indianapolis, but god bless Craig Miles hanging a life-sized doll with Peyton Manning’s jersey on the right side behind the NEW STARtron. That’s right…they freaking built a TRON into a CRIMSON STAR. Yes it shoots pyro out from all its points, and we hope this place doesn’t end up burning us all to hell! So, they start letting folks in around at 5:30 PM and there’s a ‘SETLIST’ of sorts for the evening as NFW’s brought some of the industry to cover what will also include a press conference laying out the special proceedings. Things I Learned: Fanclub Card Holders: $35 tickets (higher than a PPV!), $1 beers and $1 rails (WOO!) Industry Invites: Free Tickets (FUCK THEM!) $5 Domestic, $7 Imports, $5 rails, $8 Jack, $35 to join the Revolution, Melody Maker, Spin and a few other music mags were here, a few NASCAR and F-1 Folks…WEEEEEEEIRD. THE NFW GRAND PRIX TOUR So for about an hour, some alt-country rock band called Wayne Bingham and The Watchmen play and finish up with a pretty cool rocker called "Morningstar Stomp" and then they set up a podium and mic in front of the STARtron and out come CRAIG MILES and EDDIE MAYFIELD. This officially kicks off the Sirius Brawl Broadcast with Wildstar taking the solo as Toombs and Calamity haven’t stopped hanging out with the crowd yet. Mayfield’s donning a bright red shirt with a black evil mustache design on the front with cargo pants and a cigar, while Miles has the trademark sunglasses, a "Mad Dogs and Englishmen" t-shirt, jeans and he’s barefoot. At this point, I’m starting to pick up speed on the rails since I got a free pass to "Join the Revolution." Its not all that bad folks, I get room and board served to me…although board’s usually mean buses, vans, floors and hospital beds with a morphine IV getting injected into my… …keep it cool, Geck… MILES. MAYFIELD. SORRY. Whatever, they’re enjoying the attention and still lighting up their Cubans. Cameras flash as the TRON starts showing the world’s fastest yet continuously long highlight reel of NFW aerial spots over their 8 year history. It ends with ‘GRAND PRIX’ in a silver font over checkered ‘Revolution’ Flags…and stays still with the logo. Mayfield takes the lead and talks about how he’s organized a long-term series of matches culminating in a chance for any CRUISERWEIGHT in New Frontier Wrestling to earn a shot at all 3 singles titles until June 30, 2009. With Craig Miles and ESEN’s support, there will be a summer-long sponsored "NFW GRAND PRIX" tour. Starting tonight in Madison Square Garden, NFW will meet at CRASH 45 in Hershey, PA where three titles will be defended. CRASH 45 and a following NFW event that has not been determined will hold TWO qualifying nights for the 2008 GRAND PRIX. This is where Miles took the microphone and explained that the hopes will be for this to become an ANNUAL event dedicated to Cruiserweight Wrestlers in New Frontier Wrestling. To qualify, any wrestler must make the determined 235 pound weight limit. Miles explained that there are several wrestlers currently at the 240 pound mark that may choose to drop weight for this event, such as Rook Black or Steve Knox. Otherwise, there are ALREADY several matches scheduled for CRASH 45 involving Impulse, almasyDEATH, James von Doom, Legion, Lord Coyner Pollard and Cameron Cruise. During the summer, NFW will hold a series of outdoor events during the summer in honor of this vision and promised a stop in Daytona Beach, Florida that will mark a hard, left turn for the whole federation. They closed it up and wished everyone a safe ride home after the show, leaving a lot of mystery as to HOW this Grand Prix will work…Teresa Quaranta can specifically attest to her National Championship Series win in 2007 as these types of…marathons. However, she is NOT eligible for the tournament being a current National Champion. Whew. BARTENDER! …NICE. I’m almost as happy as Wildstar who needs to calm down about this. CAMERON CRUISE vs. IMPULSE vs. JAMES VON DOOM I gotta get the hell outta this city. Everything’s starting to smell like s(BLEEP!)t and day-old pizza. Also, the ‘Dub staff is much safer as a whole in the country where you have to wait at least 2-3 business hours to get your drugs shipped in from a major city. NFW in NYC is like a John Deere Gator full of alcoholics rampaging through the Makers Mark distillery. That said, von Doom was stomping around in the ring firing himself up as Cruise made his way down to the ring to a respectable – okay, let’s call it "modest" – reception. I mean, where has the Frontier vet been lately? Apparently I’m not the only one wondering…and then while Cam was deciding how best to approach a frothing von Doom, Impulse stepped out to easily the biggest reception of his NFW career. I guess someone let that memo slip about the Big Apple being his hometown and all. The fans immediately got WAY into this Garden party-starter as Impulse and Cruise decided to do what was in their hearts…and gang up on von Doom. Impulse was under first, and von Doom blocked his entry with a falling headbutt that showcased what a waste having such an impressively thick skull is if there’s no brain underneath it to protect. But Cruise was in by the time the "Violator" was back on his feet, and von Doom ate a Jacksonville-flavored clothesline to the face, num-num-num-num-num. Cruise hefted Dooms up and shot him against the ropes, holding fast with a shoulderblock on the returning Frontier n00b that ricocheted him off the mat. This intern must report that Cam looked solid in this match-up, but then, ring prowess was never his big hurdle to overcome. Not to be outdone, Impulse directed the attention of the flashbulbs on himself as he ascended the top rope and took flight with a 450 splash that if von Doom’s ribs were a monopoly, I’d refer to as the "trustbuster." In a stroke of what I have to confess is ring smarts if not exactly "genius," von Doom began flopping around in the ring like someone had shanked him in the prison yard, howling and screaming, slamming his arms against the mat…the works. Even the zebra was caught off-guard, half-tempted to ask him if he wanted to submit even though no one was applying a submission on him. Impulse and Cruise just kind of stood there staring at him, and no one apparently realized von Doom was inching his way towards the edge of the ring, where after a few more unintelligible howls – I thought I heard "MOTHER-NUT-FRAGGLE-ROCKIN’-C(BLEEP!)K-BRAZILIAN!" but then I realized that was actually Toombs behind me after jabbing himself in the nuts on the edge of the refreshments table – von Doom actually rolled under the bottom rope and to the outside, grinning mischievously at the success of his unorthodox distraction as the crowd rained down boos on him. That’s when LUCI4 decided to rear her committable head, riding a dolly down the entrance ramp towards the ring – yeah, a dolly, as in the things that allow ordinary, bird-chested nerds to carry six times the amount of their girlfriends’ luggage as they’d normally be able to tote…Luci rode it down flat-ways, the wheels rollin’ in front and the metal handles creating sparks in the back, and she used the momentum picked up along the way to launch herself into von Doom and slam him head-first into the turnbuckle post. Von Doom’s seeing – well, I’d say sugarplums but given that it’s von Doom I’m more inclined to believe "meth-mallows" – dancing above his head, and then Luci grabbed him by the scruff and began slamming his face into the ring steps. The ref flipped (someone tell me why these toolbags get paid for nothing and I get a cardhouse made out of hypodermic needles for all my hard work and investigative journalism!) and climbed outside to stop her, but von Doom was one step ahead, elbowing Legion’s girl in the mouthpiece before delivering the Flesh-Searingest, Sight-Blindingest Eyepoke of the Last Several Years that sent Luci stumbling away. Von Doom then decided that even two guys in the ring bent on his destruction was better than the crazy b(BLEEP!)h waiting for him on the outside, and rolled under the bottom rope…right into a school boy attempt by Cruise. One, two, but no three as von Doom got a shoulder up. He shoved Cruise away in time to stumble into a tiger suplex from Impulse that the native rookie bridged for another close two-count. Then Cruise simply dove on top of von Doom, but again, only two. Some damn good wrestling, too, I must say, though I’m no expert. What, you thought I, like, pined after this profession all through childhood only to realize I’d never survive in the ring and went after this crappy internship instead? Hell, no.
