Conferences Finales Set! Posted by Natrone Rice on 2 Feb 2007 On the heels of some of the hellish and outlandish action ever seen on a wrestling show, New Frontier Wrestling returned with a vengeance. Now, the Eastern and Western Confereneces are down to one more show apiece with everyone's chips on the table. The Eastern Conference's final Crash Television of the season is set up alot more simply as Dan Ryan and Felix Red will face in one semifinals match, YYJ and Maggot will meet in the other. From there, the respective winners will have the right to wrestle for their opportunity to win the coveted Ultratitle. Meanwhile, it wouldn't be Craig Miles' visionary Western Conference with alot more fireworks left. In a triple threat single-elimination match to finish off the second chances bracket; Nova, Joey Melton and Guy Boudreaux will start the night's proceedings. Two wrestlers surviving that will move onto two triple threat semifinals matches. Tom Adler vs. Kodiak Vic Creed vs. Survivor A Lindsay Troy vs. Eli Flair vs. Survivor B Of course, Craig Miles complicated relationships decreeing that if Melton advances, he will have to wrestle in Troy and Flair's bracket, while Guy Boudreaux - the proud new owner of some DIAMOND rings will be designated in Tom Adler's bracket. Nova, on the other hand, is forced into the OPPOSITE direction of those two. Due to these wrestlers advancing through the second chances bracket, Craig Miles has instituted a special handicap for the semifinals. A non-second chances wrestler will be able to advance with one pinfall, where the second chance survivors will need two. The West Championship stipulation? TWO out of THREE pinfalls. Its sure to be interesting as the matchups have been announced and in 2 weeks, the wrestlers will convene in FZM Arena as well as Frontier Days Arena to make more NFW history in each locale. Frontier Days Arena was the site of Midnight Madness Week 2 and was runner-up for the Wrestlebowl 2 site. Matches: EASTERN CONFERENCE: (1) Felix Red vs. (4) Dan Ryan (2) YYJ vs. (7) Maggot WESTERN CONFRENCE: Second Chances Final: (7) Joey Melton vs. (10) Nova vs. (11) Guy Boudreaux | | V Semifinals Match 1: (1) Tom Adler vs. (9) KVC vs. Guy or Nova Semifinals Match 2: (2) Eli Flair vs. (5) Lindsay Troy vs. Joey Melton or Nova
