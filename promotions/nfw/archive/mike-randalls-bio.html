Mike Randalls Biography Eye Color Emerald Nicknames The Wolf, Wrestling's Weapon-X Hometown Raleigh, NC Birthday 8/31/1973 Height 6'4 Weight 246 Handedness Ambidextrous (born right handed) Looks Shaggy auburn hair, full beard (Viggo on the Road) and emerald eyes. More lean than muscular, scarred body. Other Feds CSWA, UWA, tSC Other Fed Titles CSWA: Unified Heayweight Champion (4x), EN World Champion (2x) Wrestling Style Focuses on strikes, submissions, drivers and suplexes. 2013 injuries limit aerial assault due to risk of impact. Trainer 'Shogun' James Maurer Finisher Move DEVASTATOR (IV), Bad Moon Rising Finisher Description Back-Mount Seated Katahajimie, Cyclone Gamengiri Finisher Setup Move Varied Other Moves Signature Strikes: Gunshot Reverse Knife Edges, Short-Arm Roundhouse Kick, Rapid Fire Kicks in Corner, Muay-Thai/Martial Arts combo strikes CRITICAL STRIKE: BAD MOON RISING (Cyclone Gamengiri) Special Suplexes: Stomp Snap, Belly to Back, Top Rope Superplex, Dragon, Katahajime, Spider Suplex Special Drivers: Tiger '91, Jumping Piledriver Special Breakers: Various Neckbreakers, Tilt-A-Whirl Backbreaker, Implant Armbreaker, Kneebreaker Specials DDTs: Snap, Double Underhook, Implant CRITICAL SPECIALS: Dangerous Uranage, Backdrop Driver Signature Submissions: Royal Octopus, Cobra Clutch, Juji Gatame Armlock, Claw Hold, Reverse Figure Four Leglock CRITICAL SUBMISSIONS: DEVASTATOR (I): Texas Cloverleaf DEVASTATOR (II): Grounded top wrist-lock w/ leg-scissor body lock DEVASTATOR (III): Bridging Deathlock STF DEVASTATOR (IV Alt): Tree of Woe Katahajimie (only used on The =W= Clan) Entrance Music 'Exit' -- Black Crowes Strengths Instincts, No Fear, Training regimen Weaknessees Never cuts corners, Goes for ultimate killstroke, Susceptible to chickenshit heel tactics, 2013 back injury Ring attire Drawstring pants; usually a red, black and white combo with either Asian or Native American typography and imagery. Depending on the ring environment, Randalls will wear footwear.
