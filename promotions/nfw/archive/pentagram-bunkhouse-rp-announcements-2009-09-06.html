jediPREZ — 6 September 2009 Until further notice: PENTAGRAM MATCH: Hornet or Felix may stack 2 RP's due to the amount done by their opponents.
BUNKHOUSE STAMPEDE: If your character has not had 2 RP's as of right now, you can stack 2 RP's until I say "WHEN."
The reason that I'm doing this is to hopefully encourage some other writers to participate in these matches. Karl brought up a great point in another thread that people tend to become less likely to RP in these environments if they're opponents do 5 to their 1. In a competitive environment, I understand this is unavoidable, but I'd like to help the individuals with specific amounts of time on their hands to catch up.
