Magnus Biography Eye Color Brown Nicknames Magnus Destructo, The Dreaded Devourer, The Baron of Brutality Hometown The Carpathian Mountains Height 6'7 Weight 287 Handedness Right Looks Massively muscular barbarian with long black hair and gothic tribal face paint. Fed Titles NLW World Open Weight Champion (ABSOLUTE) Biography Magnus debuted in RA Palazzo's Next Level Wrestling, and immediately made an impact, going undefeated en route to winning the KING OF ALL MONSTERS Grand Prix, as well as the Open Weight Grand Prix (OWGP) Finals. As World Open Weight champion, he continued his reign of dominance. Magnus was a heavy fan favorite amongst the New York City crowd. Soon after, NLW went through financial troubles (partially brought on by Bobby Jack and Mark Windham), and NFW wound up buying the organization out. NFW purchased the contracts of 12 wrestlers, and bought out the rest. However, there was one contract they didn't purchase: Magnus Destructo's. The Dreaded Devourer's contract was purchased ahead of time by Castor V. Strife Productions. Magnus made his NFW debut by rushing Bobby Jack and other members of the Windham Clan in a setup by Castor Strife. Some say Castor has the power to summon the Dreaded Devourer at will. Wrestling Style Dominant Powerhouse Finisher Move 1) Destructo Bomb (Primary) 2) Destructo-Magnission (Secondary; rare) Finisher Description 1) Inverted powerbomb 2) Hyper-extended Octopus Hold Finisher Setup Move Can happen from anywhere. Usually Irish Whips the opponent, grabs them by their torso, hoists them up, and powerbombs them face down. Other Moves -Big Boot -Knee Lift -Clothesline from Hell -Gorilla Press -Running Powerslam -Two-Handed Chokeslam -Abdominal Stretch -Sidewalk Slam -Double Axe-Handle off the Second Rope Gimmick Magnus Destructo is the marriage of black metal and professional wrestling -- a walking mass of muscle violently barreling down a path of destruction, relentless and diabolical in every ambition. His main ambition is to destroy and mangle all those put before him until he's conquered the world of professional wrestling. He's quite over-the-top and pompous when on the mic, using his death metal gimmick as a way of entertaining (or perhaps frightening) fans, and being a penchant for shock value antics. His primary inspirations from real wrestling are the Ultimate Warrior, Undertaker, Sid, and Mortis, along with the talents/personalities of numerous black/death/gore metal bands (GWAR, Dimmu Borgir, Behemoth, Carpathian Forest, and, of course, Gene Simmons. Just imagine Gene Simmons as a professional wrestler.) Ring attire Long black singlet with numerous elaborate designs and decals to give it a "spiky" theme. This is accompanied by black grappling gloves, pads, and boots. He always comes to the ring with death/zombie paint. During his entrance, he'll typically sport elaborately spiked shoulder pads, spiked gauntlets, and some sort of "extreme" looking mask or helmet, which he would switch up every PPV cycle or so. Theme Music Conquer All by Behemoth
