NFW Crash TV 27 4 Jun 2005 Tokyo, Japan Introduction The Story Thus Far... In NFW's inaugural season w/ the Ultratitle, Michael Manson and Shane Southern battled through all odds to finally give the NFW audience the epic match between the two that always seemed destined to happen. Nobody could predict that through the season, roles would reverse as Michael Manson became the unsung hero creating the revolutionary Eastern Conference, while Shane Southern became the psychotically tortured and twisted villian of the South. Finally, in one the greatest matches of NFW's history, Manson defeated Southern to win the Ultratitle and possibly cement himself as NFW's greatest icon in its first five years of existence. Now 3 months later, they find themselves in the land of Japan. Manson's lost control of his conference to a mysterious Board of Directors (possibly allied with Doc Silver), Southern has lost control of his mind and is consumed with bitter hatred for the federation that made him in more than one way. New competitors like Silver, Felix Red, Wong Fei-Hung II, Lindsay Troy, Maggot and old enemies like Dan Ryan, Jonathan Marx and Kazuo Shizaki. The second season increases in talent and superstars, it returns men from the brink of paralysis like Jason Payne. An end that leads to a beginning on the 26th edition of Crash TV.
