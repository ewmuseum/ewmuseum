Coop — 4 May 2004 MEMBER INFO ---------------------- MEMBER Name: Coop Email Address: [detail withheld] WRESTLER INFO ------------------- Wrestler Name: Magnum Machine Height: 5'11" Weight: 230 From: Jungles Unknown Handedness: Right Gimmick: There are many different stories of Magnum Joe. Several tribes say that he is a great beast who came from the sky to punish the earth for it's worldly endulgences, though this is not a widely believed theory. Others say that he was a prisoner of foreign military who escaped his captors, only to be captured again and brainwashed by a native tribe and turned into the creature he is currently. It seems wherever he goes though, he causes distruction either on accident or out of rage and because of this he is shunned and feared wherever he goes. No one knows how he found his way back to america, but it is believed that he made his first step into a wrestling ring on accident and quickly caught on to the sport and has ever since wrestled in rings around the world crushing and terrorizing opponents to the delight of many fans. Audiences enjoy Machine and he enjoys being in front of big crowds and feeling accepted. It is because of this that Machine has taken on the role of a suedo-vigilante. So wherever there is a battle to be fought, a bad guy to be crushed, or an unattended fruit stand, Magnum Machine will be there to strike fear into those who dare cross his path
Theme Music: "SuperBeast" - Rob Zombie Style: Brawling comes naturally. But he has also learned several holds and technical moves during his time in the ring.
Special moves: Headbutt Coconut Crusher (A large palm strike to the forehead) Choke takedown Jumping Clothesline Standing headbutt drop Flying second-rope headbutt Roaring elbow Bodyslam Snap Suplex Northern Lights suplex German suplex Belly to Belly suplex Shining Wizard Standing Moonsault (seriously) SuperKick DropKick Submissions: Bear Hug Sharpshooter Backbreaker hold Choking Biting Finisher: Head-Hunter Hammer (A giant Spine buster from the second rope). The Angel Cannon (A burning Hammer/Emerald Frosion type move)
Strengths: Strong Very fast Doesn't give up or Scare easily Weaknesses: Easily Tricked, very guilable Often uses illegal moves which scores bad points with refs Not very intelligent, very oblivious to his surroundings Not very Tall Description: Magnum Machine somewhat short but well built. Usually hunches over. Wears large brown camoflage pants and a black shirt with a star on the right and left side with a half-moon on the stomach. Magnum Also wears a Machine-mask to hide his face.

----------------------------------------- *A camera fades into a dark locker room. Magnum Machine can be seen sitting on a nearbye bench. The room stays quiet for a second or so, the only sounds are the beasts quick breaths which are more like pants that are muffled by his tight fitting mask. Magnum takes a minute to slow down before turning to the camera and letting the light shine on the mask which cloaked his identity. A man walks into the room and doesn't notice him at first. He turns on the light and sees the large figure has caught sight of him as well, and is surprised by the sudden illumination*
Man: What are you doing here? It's way past hours, you aren't allowed in here. Get going. *Magnum Machine just stares at him, puzzled. As if he didn't understand the words* Man: Hey, did you hear me? Can you hear me with that mask on? I said get out of here, if you don't I'm going to have to call the polce.
*Magnum moves his head a bit, he is hearing the words, but he isn't processing them. He makes a sound from behind his mask, as if to speak, but doesn't finish. Just looks back at the ground and ignores the man*
Man: Okay, masked mystery guy, I get it. It's not funny. I'm calling security. Hey! Listen to me! *He grabs Magnum by the shoulder roughly to get his attention.*
*Magnum is startled by this action and grabs the worker by the arm. The creature who seemed much slower before becomes a race of quick actions as he jumps to his feet and gets grip on the mans neck, forcing him back onto the wall. The worker tries to react but it has all happened to quickly, Magnum Machine is now forcing him into the locker room wall and slamming him. The man tries to cried out but has lost the wind to. Magnum drops him and begins beating on the mans back and ribs, his limbs whipping into a wild frenzy as he assaults his already heavily battered victim. The man stops struggling and lays there helplessly. Magnum stops his assault and tries to maintain his composure. He had acted upon the situation to quickly, and like the victim, hadn't had time to assess the situation himself. He looks down and the bleeding man. He isn't moving, but still breathing, he is bleeding from his cheeck and holding his ribs. The walkie-talkie that was attached to his belt buckle has come off, and is making sounds accross the room. Magum moves towards it and studies it carefully. A man was screaming in anger and was getting louder ont the machine, his words were cracks and scratches, it was security. They had gotten the call, but never got to finish it. Magnum took one last glance over at the fallen man in the corner, he picked up the walkie talkie and threw it across the room. He had gotten himself into trouble now. He quickly switched off the lights and ran out of the locker room, he would have to find some place else to sleep tonight. The situation itself was a blurr, and Magnum could not quite comprehend the lapse of time between sitting on the bench, and pummeling the man who had turned on the lights. In his mind, the lights had just turned on, and he had just left and he had little knowledge of anything else besides the fact that he had to leave as soon as possible.*
