NFW news: Everette Memorial Invitational

9 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everette Memorial Invitational Reported on Sunday, December 9 2007 Well, it was a wild beginning to what’s shaping up to be a wild tag team division as we enter the 3.0 era of New Frontier Wrestling. I’m Richard Smitty Reese, New Frontier Wrestling fans may know me better as good ‘ol RSR and this a weekend special Brawl Report on last weekend’s amazing Everette Memorial Tag Team Invitational first night of wrasslin’. Due to the unannounced lineups for Crashmas 6, which I’m hearing will be announced shortly, NFW held off on any public review of the show. Of course, there’s been more than a few leaks on Insider and Blog sites about what went down, but we’re going to get an in-depth review on the eight matches that took three hours. Ironically, due to a backstage brawl, there were a few lineup switches in the first round as The Longest Yard and Fashion Police apparently got in a hairspray battle that left one of the Meltons in the infirmary, when it was time to start their match. Due to the fact that the Fashion Police were the instigators and that President Mayfield was starting his regime with a strong note…they were tossed out into the first match of the second bracket against the NFW Rookies, the Dream Team. Before we get to the first round recaps, it’d be good to tell you that it already looks like Blaine Hollywood is attempting to take a prominent role in the New Frontier as just when the opening pyrotechnics were exploding in the Los Angeles Memorial Sports Arena, Blaine was stomping down the aisle towards the ring. Graciously, he did welcome all his hometown fans to the beginning of ascension of the New Frontier…and somehow this actually did get cheers. Of course, Blaine is Blaine and he then went off on a five minute tirade on the New Frontier regime on booking the debut of the Hollywood Wrecking Crew in a place that’s greatest moment was when the L.A. Clippers left it. This also got cheers since nobody in this city regularly admits the Clippers fandom. Cue up some crunchy guitar feedbacked music I couldn’t make out, but out comes President Eddie Mayfield surrounded by men in combat fatigues. Mayfield snatches the mic and says he was excited to see someone that had at least been taught how to wrestle properly, but apparently he was just in the presence of another person needing a lesson. Motioning Hollywood to the back, Eddie then quipped, “They want you back in the CGI room, Dwayne. The straight to DVD trilogy about the Scorpion King has a timeline.” Not a brave enough soul for a 2-on-1 situation versus double digits, Blaine skedaddled out of the ring with Eddie grinning per usual. Then, just before Blaine was out of sight Eddie lays down the bombshell that not only are the Tag Championships on the line for the rest of the tournament, but they’re being renamed as the Everette Memorial Titles aka The EMT’s since he expects a few on stand-by at all times. Then with a sneaky glance at Blaine, he announces that Crashmas 6 will include the semifinals of this tournament and take place at the Staples Center for all the true Clippers fans out there, who can call a help support line with the number 1-800-CLIP-SUX. The Original Showstoppers vs. The US Express Mike Marine and Police Officer Shane came out first, waving American flags with “Remember 911” embroidered on them. You could tell right away that these were two greenhorns that have just started in the business. Aptly titled the US Express, they were the opponents for the current NFW Tag Champions, the Original Showstoppers. Calvin Carlton introduced them, while running down the crowd and the fact they’re going to have to give in their title belts. Promising a victory sweet as pie, the OSS attacked before the bell even rang with their own confederate flags. Of course, after knocking Mike out of the ring they left themselves to brawl with the 400 pound behemoth, Shane. This didn’t work out well as he tossed them around like rag dolls for the first few minutes. Tag to Mike, gets a few clotheslines…I think he ran a marathon in place during his first minute. Of course, Carlton gets involved pulling his leg and that distracts him for a Mueller back brain kick. OSS show why they’re the standard for tag wrestling science, working over Mike for five minutes on their half side of the ring until he’s able to flip out of a double suplex attempt and tag back in Shane who no-sells the double clothesline and hits the double chokeslam. Calvin runs into the ring, finds himself in the gorilla press, which allows the OSS to hit the double goozle (think double elimination). Mike charges in and tries to save his partner, Calvin sneaks a tennis racket shot to his back. For the finale, an amazing show of brutality by the OSS as it takes both of them to get Shane up…but a spiked waistband piledriver, which could’ve ended the man’s career with all that weight and gravity…just a sickening silence in the audience after that move. It gets the 3 count, the OSS move on. WINNERS: THE OSS Rating: ** SQUASH. Gets the extra star for the fact that the OSS hits the piledriver on a 400 pounder. The Fashion Police vs. The Sicilian Swat Team There is no justice to describing the amount of hair gel that was used to bring this match to the fans of New Frontier Wrestling. Rumor has it that every match afterwards involved some sort of footing mishap after this one. Ok, Ok all kidding aside, I can’t really go over this match because the sheer lunacy of the individuals involved wanted to make my eyes bleed. Plain and simple, the Fashion Police were trainwreck of a team that had the physical assets of male supermodels…with feathered hair. Amazingly, I wasn’t sure if the SST had actually trained in the last few years, or if the Fashion Police were really that bad. This one wasn’t pretty as IROC plastered one of the pretty boys with Side Russian Legsweep Facebuster, tagged in VINNY who hooked in a sleeper chokehold that he’s apparently coined as SLEEPIN’ WIT’ DA FISHES. WINNERS: THE SST Rating: * SQUASH The Longest Yard vs. The Dream Team Right away, Joey Melton’s running to the ring, stomping away on some of these Fashion Police freaks, grabs a mic and screams that they’ve temporarily blinded his brother out of jealousy. He demands that NFW postpone the rest of the tournament, but that’s when the husky voice of Freddie Mercury blares out of the PA speakers. Out walk, Mike Gordey and Brian Duncan, cut from the cloth of probably a good twenty years ago in this industry, which wasn’t any better news for the over the hill Melton. At this point, you gotta wonder if he really has a twin brother and just hasn’t taken some drugs that Mike Randalls gave him. Gordey goes by “The Natural,” is the much smaller of the duo, but as he quickly schooled Melton in some chain reversal wrestling and then tagged in Duncan who 360’d Melton with a vicious clothesline. The two worked over Melton and finally took pity at just over the five minute mark with the Double Crucifix Powerbomb aptly titled “The Great Lakes Bomb” for the finish. WINNERS: THE DREAM TEAM Rating: * SQUASH The Cosmonauts vs. The "New" VD The music changed, but amazingly the song was the same for this match. The VD looked horribly ill prepared for their encounter against Nova and the Hyperspace Kid. Coming off the heels of their antics at Crash 42, they got a mixed reaction from the crowd, but that didn’t stop the tour de force which they’ve been in the NFW squared circles. This one was over almost as fast as the pace the Cosmonauts used in this one. Nova hits the Shooting Star Press on Styles, while the Kid hits a Moonsault on Rage and we get a double three count. WINNERS: THE COSMONAUTS Rating: * SQUASH The Hollywood Wrecking Crew vs. PowerGodz~! Apparently, Blaine Hollywood wasn’t the most popular man in the house tonight. ‘Cause when those first power chords struck and Powermaster bolted down the aisle, the place just freaking exploded. The Wrecking Crew couldn’t understand what the hell was going on as Ryan Oracle, “The Reflection of Zeus,” struck various poses as he slowly strutted down the aisle. Once he got in the ring, he reiterated the challenge to Blaine Hollywood about a pose-off between their match. After enough, crowd razzing Blaine finally agreed by stating Oracle had to go first. Naturally, as soon as the back was turned Blaine struck and Malik charged Powermaster. Oracle got deposited out of the ring quickly by Blaine as the Wrecking Crew then worked over Powermaster in the corner. Double Irish Whip into the other turnbuckles, but the freight train cometh as Powermaster rocketed out with a double clothesline! Blaine immediately rolled out of dodge, leaving Malik to deal with the ropes-shaking one-time spokesperson for MET-Rx. Powermaster tried working over Malik with overhand smashes and double axehandles, but Anderson quickly grabbed a double leg takedown and started working some fist mashing. Malik tore up Powermaster whipped him off the ropes, took him up and over with a kitchen sink knee and tagged in the recomposed Blaine, who immediately delivered stomps to the noggin’. Hollywood cockily let Powermaster back to his feet and challenged him to a test of strength as a joke, but Powermaster locked the hands up and Blaine dropped to his knees. Just as quickly, the 2nd generation wrestler showed his lineage with a quick fireman’s carry out of the hold and armbar. Of course, an armbar on Powermaster is like a diet for Oprah…it only lasts so long. Literally, Powermaster clubbed himself out of the hold, Blaine bounced off the opposite ropes and shoulderblocked Powermaster, but fell right on his back ‘cause of that. Powermaster beat his chest, bounded off the ropes and connected with a series of shoulderblocks and clotheslines that crushed Blaine. He then finally tagged in his partner, Oracle who slingshotted into the ring and connected with a picturesque dropkick, including a leg raise thigh and calf pose from a scissors position on the mat. That let Blaine hit a sliding dropkick to his face. Blaine grabbed him by the head, hooked him and took him over with a suplex rollover pin, getting a quick one count. Blaine grabbed him by the hair and slammed him, bounced off the ropes for a leaping splash, but Oracle put up the knees and Blaine landed on them – so what does Oracle do? Clamp onto Blaine and start leg-pressing him as Hollywood kicks his legs frantically. Oracle vaults him off, follows up with a kip-up and locks Blaine around in a bear hug as he gets to his feet, picks him up off the ground and starts spinning around – and THEN hits an Overhead Release Belly to Belly that gets a huge pop from the crowd! Apparently, there’s some skills in Oracle…of course, he launches into his five poses of doom sequence, while Blaine tags Malik. SPEAR CITY. The whole place groans from the nasty impact as Malik wastes no time and double underhooks Oracle, which elicits a “PRAISE TO AHMED!” bellow from Blaine slumped over the ropes. Malik sneers and Sitout Powerbombs Oracle on his head, then goes for the pin. Powermaster runs in with a double axehandle to break it up, but Blaine cracks him in the head with a running kick to the head for his efforts. For the next five minutes, we learned why the Wrecking Crew is one of the sleeper picks for this tournament. A lot of double team moves in their arsenal and without Blaine hamming it up, its easy to see that he’s gotten some of the best training in the business. Some of the highlights included Blaine spiking an Anderson Spinebuster, by throwing down Oracle by the hair. Blaine doing a Snap -> Delayed Vertical -> Gourdbuster. Oracle showed some heart underneath the oiled muscles and kicked out after a double Slingshot Suplex -> Stereo Elbow Drop, then frustrated Blaine to no end after kicking out if a Tilt-A-Whirl Uranage. Finally, Oracle got smart by hitting a low-blow on Blaine while getting lifted from all fours, he crawled towards the corner posed and tagged in Powermaster while flopping. Powermaster vaulted over and flipped Blaine off the highway with a clothesline, Malik charged in and got lifted towards the heavens in a pretty back body drop. As he stumbled to his feet, Powermaster big booted him up and out of the ring. This left Blaine alone with Powermaster and Oracle, which from the looks on his eyes wasn’t on his to-do list. The reel continues with a double clothesline, Powermaster presses Blaine and slams him on the mat facefirst as Oracle connects with a running elbow. Instead of going for the pin, Oracle starts posing as Powermaster brings Blaine into a facelock wanting to try something. Blaine uses the distraction to push Powermaster into Oracle knocking him out of the ring after they connect head to head. Powermaster turns around and finds himself in a Fireman’s again, Blaine then drops him with a DVD pin combo for the win. Does he call that one “Straight to DVD?” …I hope not. WINNERS: HOLLYWOOD WRECKING CREW Rating: *** Five poses and moves of doom can’t carry half of a match. HWC look like a smash success. The Suicide Kings vs. Mike Randalls/Professor Tremendous There’s one thing every single fan was looking forward to this evening and that was seeing Tsunami and Mike Randalls in the same ring at the same time since their legendary tilt in Atlanta that got themselves nearly suspended from Uncle Chad and Steve’s wrestling show. Annnnnyway, the four players on the Suicide King’s roundtable were DC Stratton and Tsunami resigned as a tag team being managed by Neil Riddick and escorted by a Manson-free to be what she wants to be, Rayne. Randalls for whatever reason has aligned himself in several tag team matches with Tremendous by his side, which actually did produce a victory somewhere along the line. Randalls asked Tremendous if he wanted to start against Stratton, which the Professor politely declined. So DC and Mikey hooked up horns first, Armlocks, kipups and reversals started off the early get-to-know-you session before Stratton hitting a high vertical side scissor kick to Randalls’ jaw. Randalls shook out the dust as Stratton flew in with a forearm sending him into the corner, Stratton back handspringed and hit an elbow. Stratton grabbed and whipped to the corner, ran in and leapt off Randalls chest, cracking a side kick to the head. Randalls walked out dazed as Stratton springboarded and hit a dropkick, knocking Randalls down. Stratton with a Standing Shooting Star for two. Stratton stayed on it, grabbing Randalls and hooked him for a suplex. Randalls blocks it between the legs, then quickly crushes Stratton in the midsection with a Muay Thai Knee, grabbing him around the head for a rapid fire series that leaves DC bent over on the mat. Ever see someone decapitated by an Axe Kick? Half the crowd thought it happened as Stratton tried to pull himself up. Stratton rolls to his corner, tag. The roof flies off as Tsunami flies into the ring screaming “DANGER! DANGER!” Randalls holds up the stop sign and looks towards Tremendous to see if he wants a tag. The whole place boos, so Tremendous points that out and Randalls turns around with a smile as both men stalk towards the center of the ring. Randalls gets ready for a lock up, but ‘Nami catches him with a forearm and slap across the chest. Randalls chops back. Back and forth it goes until ‘Nami goes for the mist out of nowhere, which Randalls backbends to avoid. Tsunami grabs the opportunity and blasts Randalls in the exposed midsection with an overhead chop. Tsunami grabs Randalls and Reverse DDT’s him to the mat, quickly gets up and hits a Running Senton and tag to Stratton who vaults off the top immediately with a double pump Frog Splash on Randalls! That barely gets a two count, so DC responds by throwing Randalls out of the ring. With Riddick running the distraction, Rayne holds a chair to Randalls’ head as Tsunami double stomps on top of it. The crowd aghast already, Tsunami starts biting the open wound and spitting out his dinner appetizer at the front row. The ref has to come out there and literally pry him off, so as they tumble away – Stratton launches into the fray with a springboard plancha splash on Randalls. Randalls gets rolled into the ring and Stratton drops him with an Implant DDT and tags in Tsunami. The Japanese Dynamo hits a Northern Lights Suplex with a bridge, but Randalls kicks out at two. Tsunami follows that up with a Back Suplex and bridge, but again only a two count. Tsunami’s getting more visibly frustrated with Randalls’ persistence and ragtosses him into the corner for a vicious chop and kick beatdown as the cut on Randalls’ head is covering his face in blood. Tsunami whips out Randalls, gets reversed and hits the turnbuckles. Randalls dazily goes for the back body, but Tsunami stuffs him with the Tiger Driver ’91 getting a 2 seven-eights count! Tsunami starts thrashing on the mat, screaming god-knows-what. Stratton asks for the tag, Riddick screams for it and it gets ignored. Tsunami rips up Randalls and starts blasting away with chops, kicks, headbutts…screaming at the same time. Finally, he slams Randalls and lands a Moonsault…another two count. Tsunami freaks out and throws Randalls through the ropes. Stratton hits the elbow off the apron as Tsunami slides out and grabs a chair. Fans are pleading with Tremendous to do anything, but ever since Tsunami started biting Randalls, I think he made a silent vow not to move until the bell rung. Rayne now runs the distraction as Tsunami cracks Randalls with a chairshot as he gets up. Randalls doesn’t go down, which almost makes Tsunami’s brain pops so he swings again connecting…and Randalls just staggers away, starting to laugh like an insane criminal…which he may or may not be, if you believe Joey Melton’s Behind the Ring special on VH-1. Stratton goes to grab Randalls, which makes Tsunami more feral as he chairshots him! Then Randalls out of nowhere hits a leaping roundhouse kick into the chair, crunching it off Tsunami’s face! The ref is now counting both of them out, Riddick leaps into the ring to stop him. Stratton rolls both Tsunami and Randalls in, yelling at Riddick to get out as he’s trying to tag his partner. Randalls grabs Tsunami by the ankle and starts dragging him away, Stratton charges in and Randalls counters with a back body drop. Finally, with a dive, Randalls makes the tag to Tremendous…who all of a sudden decides he’s not ready to enter the ring. Shaking his head vehemently, he gets slingshotted in by Randalls. Like a fly on paper, Tsunami goes after Randalls, but Tremendous somehow shows some resolve and gets him in a rollup and a near pinfall. Tsunami, wild like a banshee, keeps rushing to his feet and gets caught in a Backslide, Inside Cradle and Small Package pin attempt succession that fails. Tremendous then goes for a slam, but Tsunami slips out the backside and catches the Professor with an Atomic Drop which vaults him towards Stratton who crushes him with a forearm. Tremendous staggers back towards Tsunami and into a back body drop. Amazingly, Tremendous lands on his feet and spins around in time to catch Tsunami with a kick to the gut and Stunner! Ever hear 18,000 fans scream “WHAT!?” ‘cause it happened on the spot as Tremendous leapt on Tsunami and was about to get the three count when Stratton broke it up. Randalls leaps off the top rope with a missile dropkick to Stratton, which knocks him out of the ring. Tremendous picks up Tsunami and opens him up for a vicious Randalls chop, then in a moment that can only happen in NFW. Tremendous lifts Tsunami up in a Suplex as Randalls hits the sky with a Moonsault BodyPress. No pin count as the referee ushers Randalls out of the ring, which allows Stratton to fly off top rope with a Diamond Dust on Tremendous. Riddick and Rayne pull Randalls off the apron, the three count is made and the Suicide Kings officially become the most hated tag team in the house. WINNERS: THE SUICIDE KINGS Rating: *** – Should’ve been a 4th, but weird finish and lack of Randalls and Tremendous interaction takes luster off things. 2ND ROUND: HWC vs. OSS vs. The Cosmonauts I’d like to say this one really happened. I’d like to go on and about how the OSS brought their revolutionary, yet traditional southern-bred tag team science against the mat-wrestling tandem of the Wrecking Crew, as well as the high-flying Cosmonauts. In a scene sure to piss off more than a few fans, Nova’s antics from Crash 42 apparently caught up to him. Just as him and Hyperspace Kid are walking down the aisle, they’re attacked by Kooter Michaels-Cruise and Cameron Cruise, who slam them with chairs to the back and then handcuff them to the railings! I’ve never seen Cameron like this, who apparently doesn’t like breaking promises to his children. It all ends with a double chokeslam through tables at the hands of Kooter, while in the ring…after the standing ten count, the OSS and HWC come out of their shock to launch into a pier six brawl of their own. Dozens of security and staff have to separate the foursome, only after they wreck the whole ringside area trying to take each other out with chairs, more chairs, more chairs and Calvin’s tennis racket which fell into both teams’ hands in the span of the five minutes. SEMIFINALS: HWC vs. OSS Rating: Zilch, did I really sit in the audience for two hours for a rush job like that? …Guess so. 2nd Round: The Dream Team vs. The Suicide Kings vs. The SST The shortness of the last match pretty much hindered the Kings in this one, but don’t discount having two outside interference sources as a means to the end. Basically, with the context of the match producing more chaos than their match against Randalls/Tremendous, it was far easier for Riddick and Rayne to interfere on any pin attempts on their lieges. Meanwhile, Duncan and Gordey got a stiff test in IROC and Cousin Vinny, who had their own help with Leroy Brown in their corner. Of course, I’m exhausted, beat down and a little sour about the previous match…so I’m not going to go hold for hold on this one. I will say that the SST didn’t roll over and die as I expected when I first heard they were in this match. Instead, they fought off the strike of midnight on their Cinderella quest for a good twenty minutes, but with the Suicide Kings spent and playing a defensive battle, The Dream Team showed that their dreams are still alive. Gordey countered a DDT by hitting an Inverted Atomic Drop, which allowed Duncan to connect with a vicious Yakuza kick and pinfall for the end of the night. Again, sorry for the shortness…but its midnight as I’m posting this and as all things NFW, I just want to sleep it off. SEMIFINALS: THE DREAM TEAM vs. THE SUICIDE KINGS Rating: *** -- Seriously, I didn’t know the SST might be a tag commodity in the 21st century!
