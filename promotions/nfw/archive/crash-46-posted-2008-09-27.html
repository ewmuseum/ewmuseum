Legion — 27 September 2008 Thoughts: Well the road for Supercrash II has really kicked off with this show, the Knoxterf*ck was fun and got me excited for the elite title match (particularly who it is against, won't say for anyone that hasn't checked PTC)
In regards to my segments the way this goes will be probably the most ambitious and at the same time possibly risky idea in mind but shall be fun!
The double win for Steve is interesting as we'll see if he can handle being a triple (TSC counted) champ in a short space of time.
Lord Coyner and Dirkwood's segments made me chuckle (LCP's stance is great since he can get away with anything as well as Dirk's voodoo s**t line concerning Legion)
Now for the big thing for me... UJ/Rook/Felix/Miles, the main questions are who was behind 'Rook' being in NFW, whose side is Uber Rook on and now in a weird way how does it also affect TQ/Legion and Luci from way back at the beginning of the season? mind you with the thou shall always kill segment Legion may have uncovered the secret without realising... ;-)
Very compelling story that Ben's created here and whatever happens I count it as one of my faves this year.
