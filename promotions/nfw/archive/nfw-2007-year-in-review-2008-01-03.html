NFW 2007 Year in Review

3 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Off to Valinor — 3 January 2008 This was totally copped and robbered from our neighbors up north. :) Anyway, this is not really awards, but just general discussion, feedback, and props for the dynamic changing times of NFW in '07. The following can basically be surmised as the "biggest" or "best" for whatever category.
Wrestlers Matches Events Debuts Returns Departures Surprises Feuds Angles Moments Most Improved Managers Staff Members Outlook on 2008 Legion — 3 January 2008 Wrestlers Kin Hiroshi- For being innovative specifically with Dove's Dime plus showing the underdog story for Season 2 though I sense his new found cocky attitude to cost him sooner than later
Nova- managing to become a multiple fed champion and juggling them all shows skill that I have to respect, Def a big player reaching supernova (excuse the pun) in 2008
Matches Mayfield Vs Miles- bit biased here since I did the two columns on it (3rd on a diff subject coming soon... may be War Games related...) but did show a bait-and-switch that really changed how I thought the story would have played out back in FWO days!
Nova Vs Yori/Eli Flair- Grouping these two together as it solidified the run plus bringing back both Hell's Kitchen and concluding one of the craziest storylines in Yori Vs The Apemen etc
Events Hulk-A-Thon- Three words- Mega Job Show Eli's last match- Great end to a great career The Felix-Ryoko wedding- If you looked real closely there were hints but seeing Felix grow in power only to lose it all via Kin was fun to read
Debuts Joe The Plumber- It's a Devin character so I expected wackyness and he didn't disappoint! Teresa Q- Strong debut for this character, I can see a poss National title run sometime soon (and possible interaction with Legion in a program soon hopefully :))
Returns Professionals- I don't think anyone aside from from Katz and Jamar expected the return considering the hatred they showed in all '3' matches
Departures Lindsay Troy- Obv due to commitments in other feds but if she does return to NFW her vs Teresa could be a good angle
Hunchblack- Too bad he dropped out (was part of the original plan with Pants for Devil's rejects angle), hopefully he comes back in the future so we can do an angle :)
Eli Flair- One of the characters that I really have enjoyed reading over the years! Surprises Feuds Felix Vs Kin Manson Vs Melton Angles Windham's Coronation of UWA belt Moments Windham's assault on Winters Jason Payne's rant Yori Vs The Apemen Randalls' freak-out Most Improved Teresa Q Felix Red Kin Hiroshi Outlook on 2008 Hopefully the war between Nova/Felix/Mayfield/Miles goes from strength to strength, I sense faction wars happening soon!
I aim to improve and tell a great story with Ben with the Rook/Legion story- we have things and people getting involved that may surprise you...
Let's make 2008 the year of The New Frontier again!:D Off to Valinor — 4 January 2008 Ok first of all, I'm going to try and avoid things I've written and my characters for the most part. Of course, i can't always avoid that considering how much of these shows I've written. This is also at work and off the top of my head, so if I forget things its cause I smoke ALOT.
Wrestlers Nova Kin Hiroshi Felix Red I think those were the 3 main rocks of NFW throughout 2007. YYJ definitely could've made it had he stuck through the whole year.
Joe the Plumber is Rookie of the Year. Matches Of the ones I didn't write: Kin Hiroshi vs. Felix Red by [name withheld] was one of the most important matches of the year. Chris has written some great matches, but Eli Flair and Nova fighting on a monster truck takes the cake. Eli Flair vs. Dan Ryan was one of the best traditional matches for the fed. Colin's stepped up big-time as a matchwriter, I'm looking forward to his Crashmas match.
Events WB2, Conference finals Crash 42 Top ones are the obvious choices, but I feel that Crash 42 finally solidified the fed in terms of the divisions, rivalries and storylines we're building during the 3.0 era. That might be more important than the fact Season 2 finished. :cool:
Debuts Joe The Plumber, Teresa Quaranta, Phil Atken, Mittens T. Cat: I think these four all debuted in 2007 and have found their place in the fed through the whole turmoil, trial and error of NFW 2.0 -> NFW 3.0 They've become four of the most consistent RP voices in the fed.
Of course, there's Professor Tremendous lurking in the shadows evilly... Returns Sean Stevens and Rook Black. These two returns anchored the National Title division and gives it great balance going into the National Championship Series at Crashmas 6. Both are infamously famous from their necks of the woods, and they've seamlessly worked well with alot of new to this community writers.
[name withheld]. What he's done the last two months is amazing. If JN writes the Crashmas 6 match on time, 2008's outlook for the tag division is supernova bright.
Departures Michael Manson, Maggot, YYJ, Eli Flair, KVC, Lindsay Troy, Joey Melton, Tom Adler, Guy B., Jason Payne.
These were all great characters and big parts of NFW 2.0 foundation. Jason Payne has been in contact with me about getting back into writing, which is good.
Surprises We lost the above, but still gained enough new talent to look good for 2008. Everyone's stepped up their RP games, as well. I'm still shocked that Nova predicted a YYJ Eastern Conference Win before [name withheld] booked it. Since I booked everything else, there's little to be surprised about. :)
Oh wait, a [name withheld] 15 minute YouTube promo was NEVER predicted. Feuds Felix vs. Kin - My vote for Feud of Season 2. Teresa vs. Atken or Teresa vs. Rook -- I think they're great feuds, but very different in concept from each other.
Joe the Plumber vs. his own mind -- His latest tattoo is just wrong. Dan Ryan vs. NFW peers -- Aside from the Eli Flair match, he's dealt with nothing normal. Chocolate sauce, day-glo, a cracked out Kin...I know he'd have guessed it'd be a cold night in hell, when a returning Eddie Mayfield might actually benefit his NFW career. :P
Angles Nova and Felix's riot at Crash 42 opened up ALOT of things. Nova delay suplexing a drugged out rioter onto the console in the middle of a seg was my LOL moment of the show.
Anything Rook Black related has been fun to watch, knowing the end result of the first character arc.
Felix Red's face turn has been done quite secretly and slowly. I don't think anyone else is really focusing too hard on character storylines, but I do know of some that are in the works that should be fun. The RP environment is really competitive right now, which is helping develop the fed's storyline...so its not that big of a deal either that we're not doing too much character stuff. Although, it is coming.
Moments The finish to Eli/Nova in Hell's Kitchen (I marked, that's sad) Kin Hiroshi winning the World Nova winning the Ultratitle, Flair helping him onto the Mothership Joe the Plumber... being Joe the Plumber Mittens T. Cat cutting his first promo out of the catsuit Manson's retirement Eli's NFW retirement with support from Ivy/Dan Ryan Mittens challenging Joe to a gravy match Kooter vs. JTP's RP Thread Dan Ryan's chocolate sauce experiences and reactions Chris: (post YYJ/Nova): "I think I've just been on a bad acid trip." Bobby: (post WB2) "I've never done acid, but I think I know what its like now." Most Improved Kooter Cruise-Michaels...seriously. Does anyone find him disturbingly hilarious? I think Legion's also improved greatly since starting. The less Stamford references, the better. ;)
Managers Calvin Carlton, Dirk Dirkwood, Legion...and Mitt Romney. Staff Members [name withheld]'s efforts in getting the tag division off the ground speak to his fokrutic force. [name withheld] did a great job...better than me.
Outlook on 2008 Frankly, I think we're in a really great place. If we had more consistent matchwriters we'd be in a perfect place. I think each title division is set up very well, we've got a great mix of new characters, FW veterans and talented writers. I think its providing a fun atmosphere for everyone and its definitely a unique roster which provides a unique experience. The tag division has alot of potential, especially if we pull in a few more new teams to balance out and give programs for Gregg's daily creations.
The title divisions look like they all can be forces of nature in 2008, which is very motivating for myself. Everyone here is doing a phenomenal job in terms of RPing, so hopefully our next wave of talent will involve a few more matchwriters and we can start dreaming of shows in under 3 weeks again. :)
