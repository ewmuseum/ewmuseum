JN brusch — 20 September 2013 I've participated in this hobby a whopping year and a half now, which of course pales in comparison to the 5/10/20+ year veterans we have on this site. I've always tried to keep a relatively low-profile when these dramatic flare-ups occur out of deference to those who have been around longer, but I feel like I've been around enough where I can put forward some thoughts and ideas without the FWC community responding with a "who the hell is this guy?". So here we go.
I have participated in 5 feds in total, some more than others, in FWC since I started. All that's missing at this point, really, is DEFIANCE (which I've been thinking about joining for a while), and jOlt (which I've thought about off and on too). I like doing this stuff. It's a chance to do some real exercise on a hobby I've grown to love, which is creative writing in general. And I like to think, wherever I've gone, I've only had positive experiences and good interactions with the other writers who use this site. I can't recall a time where someone has ever come out personally to attack me, my characters or my writing style, and I do my best to avoid contributing to that as well. I'm not under some false impression that I'm the best-writer-and-also-best-human on the site, because that's far from true on both counts; however, my policy has always been "say yes to people's ideas, because this is a hobby and the whole point is to see ideas through even if they crash and burn", and "treat people with respect".
Somewhere along the way, NFW forgot those two points. They say familiarity breeds contempt, and maybe it's because people have been working together for such an extended period that they have grown frustrated when their ideas don't line up; maybe it's a matter of people saying "my ideas are better than your ideas and you are stupid for disagreeing with me and going with your idea". It's honestly probably a little of both, not to mention the fact that we talk to each other in "OOC" moments, and personalities clash. Shit happens, sure - but there's just so much passive-aggressive bullshit that gets posted from EVERYBODY saying "I told x person he's an asshole" or "people in the past no-sold my gimmick so fuck you" or "my guy isn't getting put over the right way and that makes me grumpy", and it's DUMB. When you fight, gentlemen, be PRODUCTIVE about it. Stop the shit-slinging. Articulate in respectful ways what your concerns are and be prepared with solutions. All I keep seeing is middle fingers raised in the air, and it makes me palm my face so hard.
I want to keep doing this. I like the roads Ocho has been able to travel, it's nice to hear that people generally seem to like the character, and I'm always open to criticism and feedback to make my writing better. But guys - and I mean this at EVERY level - take your egos out of this. I've had more people come up to me and say "man it's a bummer that 'your big moment' happened to open the card", but to be totally honest, I give less than one fuck about that. I don't care about fictional honorariums bestowed upon fictional wrestlers in a fictional wrestling federation amongst a group of creative writers - all I want to do is have some fun and become a better writer.
TL;DR? Talk to each other like adults. Stop having a hard-on about everything that involves your own writing. Play nicely with others. Collaborate. That's the only way this is going to be a worthwhile endeavor going forward.
