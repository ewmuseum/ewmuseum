Rook Black Biography Eye Color Gray Nicknames The Life of the Mind Hometown St. Joe, Texas Birthday 12/02/75 Height 5'11 Weight 243 Handedness Right Looks Shiny dome (shaved head). Looks like a friendlier Heny Rollins basically. Has upgraded from standard Western Attire to a more business-y, sleek look, but maintains the bolo over the necktie, and refuses to relinquish the cowboy boots. Fed Titles NFW National Champion Biography Rook Black is a second generation superstar the son of "Legendary" Joe Black. Trained at the Black and Black Academy by his father, Rook utilizes the Black and Black technical method, a training that focuses on a fast pace and compatibility with Lucha. Rook is a stoic intellectual and often difficult to understand unless he deliberately attempts conversational transparency, and even then sometimes he falls short of the mark. He's an atheist and often flirts with existentialism. In regard to Professional Wrestling, he's very interested in how the line is blurred between what he refers to as "contest" (athletic competition with defined rules and determination of success) and "combat" (the battle for survival and dominance of fighting) and how matches can be considered negotiations regarding which "rules" are bendable and breakable and how much and how far. Rook is continually seeking to improve his ability and technique, striving to become a peer to NFW's greats. Because of this he is very conscious of his individual responsibility to uphold the prestige of the company. Other Feds A!W, tSC Other Fed Titles tSC Champion Wrestling Style Black & Black Technical Trainer Joe Black Finisher Move Gauntlet Throwdown, Gauntlet Backhand Finisher Description Choking STO, Face First Chokeslam Finisher Setup Move Discus Lariat, Spinning Reverse Elbow Setup Description Rook looks to hit either of the running strikes to stun, and then stalks his opponent during the reocvery to apply either the Gauntlet Throwdown or Gauntlet Backhand Other Moves The Dual Finishers are set up to be interchangeable, for example were an opponent managed to successfully block the Choking STO, Rook can seamlessly switch to the Face First Chokeslam. The set up moves operate similarly, so that an opponent cannot necessarily know precisely which attack is coming. Rook's Preferred Submission Hold is called Crossbow, similar to a bow-and-arrow stretch that works the back of a kneeling opponent. Rook enjoys counter wrestling, but thinks "putting on a clinic" is a little too self-indulgent. If he feels that counter chains are getting a bit ridiculous, he won't hesitate to bring that to end through a sudden shift to more violent attacks. When Rook is getting clobbered, he likes to use a surprise headbutt from his back when an opponent either reaches to pick him back up to his feet or carelessly approaches to make a cover. If it connects, Rook grabs hold of the opponent and continuously repeats the headbutt all while rising to his feet and eventually mounting the opponent for the last few blows. Rook has matured his move set down from flashier moves. While he enjoys a pop from the crowd as much as anybody, he'd doesn't care for showboating or looking at what it cost him. He likes to expose opponents with poor conditioning, and will often seek to make larger or heavier opponents continually return to their feet under their own power while taunting them. Rook prefers to not to punch after severely injuring his right hand years ago. Chops, European Uppercut, Forearm strikes, and head butts are his preference. If he must use his reach, he'd left jab. Because cowboy boots are kinda brutal, Rook will reveal some well practiced kicks on opponents he's particularly aggravated with. He generally does not like to use weapons, but chairs will do just fine when it's called for. He'd prefer to use the ring apron, security barricade, or ring post offensively, since those never got anyone disqualified. If strange circumstances call for heinous weaponry, he'd put spurs on his boots. Entrance Music Midlife Crisis - Faith No More Ring Entrances Rook will stand on the ramp and survey the scene. This is the moment for brandishing titles, or waiting for a small army of supporters to fall into line. Then he'll march to the ring. He's focused exclusively on his match and will not be happy with distractions prior to the start. Ring attire Bare chest, cowboy boots, fingerless gloves, elbow pads. He wears glossy loose fitting PANTS, which vary in style, but are always some symetrical black and white, like a chessboard grid, or one leg black and one white. Theme Music "Waiting Room" by Fugazi.
