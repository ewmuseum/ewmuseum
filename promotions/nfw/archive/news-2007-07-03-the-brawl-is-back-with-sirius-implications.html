NFW news: The Brawl is Back with SIRIUS Implications!

3 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Brawl is Back with SIRIUS Implications! Posted by Natrone Rice on 3 Jul 2007 New Frontier Wrestling and ESEN announced exciting news as the weeks leading up to the v.3 launch countdown. In a move that confirmed plans of a house show touring schedule, Craig Miles and SIRIUS Broadcasting officials announced that "THE BRAWL," a former radio staple of NFW on the Atlantic Coast in its independent years will return with an updated and expanded format for the global satellite radio providers. "THE BRAWL" used to be a two-hour radio segment taped for various Mid-Atlantic radio stations during the years of 2000-2001, as New Frontier Wrestling grew along the coast. The programming ranged from interviews, rumor reports, commentary and house show results. The program ended its operations post-FUTURESHOCK as New Frontier Wrestling switched to an all-TV format with the Ultratitle concept. With New Frontier Wrestling's reformatting, Craig Miles and SIRIUS gave details on how "THE BRAWL" will provide house show transcript reports to the NFW website and live to fans, taped interviews and possibly LIVE coverage from New Frontier Wrestling, WHEREVER they may roam. SIRIUS and ESEN are potentially working out a deal for a singular radio station, furthermore, if that deal were to go through there may be full house shows called LIVE for SIRIUS subscribers. Craig Miles introduced HELLMACHINE, the first ever NFW Tag Team Champions as the co-hosts of "THE BRAWL." Calamity and 'BIG IN JAPAN' Sean Toombs retired in 2003 from the sport of wrestling and have helped train developmental talent for New Frontier Wrestling over the last two years.
