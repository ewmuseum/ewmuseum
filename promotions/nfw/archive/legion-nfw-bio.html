Legion Biography Nicknames The Devil's Reject, The Bringer Of Chaos Height 5'10 Weight 225 Handedness Left Looks Think of a combination of Raven and Jimmy Jacobs, mostly either wearing the For We Are Many shirt or bands like Ministry, Lesbian Bed Death etc Fed Titles Penultimate PURE champion Fed Awards NFW Grand Prix winner Biography Recent events have left him with a slightly unstable mind - one that many say has been dormant until now and his true commitment is seeing the chaos grow everywhere around him whether on his own or with allies - the recent formation of The Black Market has led the Age Of Chaos to come ever closer. Wrestling Style A mixture of the Hardcore and Lucha, think a mixture of Raven, Jimmy Jacobs and Judas Mesias (if he had more charisma and didn't need Jim Mitchell to talk for him) Finisher Move Gateway to Hell Finisher Description Buzzsaw Kick/Curbstomp Finisher Setup Move Buzzsaw Kick followed by the exclamation point in the Curbstomp Other Moves Basic Moves: strong style punches and kicks with a mixture of MMA muay thai kicks Power moves: hangman's DDT, given his history with 'Stamford' feel free to give him some upper card wrestler moves (way to get cheap heat...) Special move: Ninth Gate to Hell (El Generico Super Brainbuster) Legion's Air Disease - 450 Senton *First used at Reloaded 11* 'Abrahadabra' - Package Piledriver Death Star Choke Ring Entrances The lights go out as the sound of synths and keyboard can be heard as the intro to 'Pole Shift' begins - as soon as the guitar kicks in we see on both sides of the stage Bandit and Devastator. As soon as Jaz Coleman's vocals kick in Legion appears - staring at his opponent in the ring with a sick smile on his face - his plan to cause chaos for the given situation already formed in his mind. After entering the ring he still stares at his opponent - the smile fades as he studies for possible weaknesses before the bell rings. Gimmick A manipulator, likes to see how things plan out before getting involved, in some cases (as seen at Crashmas 2007) he will set the chess pieces as he sees fit... Strengths Through research of adversaries, strong style moves and willingness not to quit. Weaknessees Possible chance that when pushed too much the rage unleashed could cost him big moments.. Ring attire Band t-shirt - think bands like Killing joke and Strapping young Lad as examples OR the Chaos Reigns Everything Around Me shirt and denim cut off jeans Theme Music Chaos Is King by Onslaught
