CaptCongaLine — 24 March 2009 MEMBER INFORMATION: Preferred Method of Handling: FW Promo, Freestyle RP, Hybrid Best Way to Contact you: PM is Most Effective WRESTLER INFORMATION: Name: Captain Conga Nicknames: The Spanish Suplex Machine, Conga, CC, Cap'n, Fabio Lu- NO! Height: 6'1 Weight: 222 lbs. Handedness: Left Looks: Captain Conga has medium-long, greasy, black hair that curls down (like Razor Ramone's would). His eyes are a light hazel and his facial hair is a dark black tone. His skin is darker and he wears his facial hair in a light scruff but kept mustache. His body is oiled and toned but not all too intimidating. He has a classic and casual charm to his out-of-ring style; casual shirts, slacks, fancy watches and cars, etc.
Hailing From: Spanish-Harlem, New York City Disposition: His goal is to become the first Latino-based NFW Champion and make his enemies tremble at the mere mention of the word 'CONGA'.
Gimmick: Captain Conga is a Spanish-American scrapper who loves nothing more than to do the whole 'I-challenge-AAANY-heel-here-for-the-fans'-love' system to the T. He plays the crowd like a Jericho-Rock-Eddie Guererro triple-blend cut.
Theme Music: Heavy Conga Beats and Bass drops Ring Attire: He usually just wears a classic pair of black, red, and yellow trunks with knee-high red boots. He wears black elbow pads and wrist pads, also, and a black band ties around his head into a bandanna reading 'CONGA' over it.
Ring Entrance: He comes out to his music and gyrates his hips so much the women lose their minds and the men lose their appetite. Capt. Conga makes his presence known through sexual methaphors and spicy Latin wit. He will usually have an "intro" to his night; meaning, Capt. Conga will appear at the top of the ramp and swerve and jive until he feels fit. Then the music will die off and he will usually talk up the crowd, the ladies, and down talk his opponent - unless they're a face, too.
Tactics/Style: High-risk hardcore high-flyer; think Sabu mixed with Juventud Gurerra and a dabble of Mr. Perfect crossed with the flare of the Blue Meanie and K-dawg (nWo Wolfpack).
SUMMATION: Sabu/Juvy/Hennig/HoganMeanie/Konan Strengths: Energetic, Untapped Potential, Ninja-like Speed & agility Weaknessess: Unexperienced, Major A.D.H-D. sufferer, Foreigner's offspring MATCHWRITING GUIDE: General/Signature: Spanish DDT [Jump-Swinging DDT (often off second turnbuckle)], Springboard Hurricanrana, Standing Shooting Star Press, Springboard Moonsault, Handspring elbow-smash, CongaBREAKER (Sitout Over-the-Shoulder Jawbreaker), Flying Forearm Smash, CON-GA-PLANCHA (Springboard-Sommersault Suicide Plancha to outside the ring),
Lightheavyweight Moveset: Reverse DDT, hiptoss-into-neckbreaker, Gutwrench-into-Sit-out Powerbomb, Superkick, Spanish Kick (Standing Scissor Kick to back of head), Snap-Cradle DDT, Tornado DDT, Moonsault Senton
Super Heavyweight Moveset: Sleeperhold (mounted atop opponent's back), Spanish DDT off third turnbuckle, Conga-Wing Crossface (Chickenwing Crossface), Running Dropkick, Springboard Moonsault-into-Spanish DDT, Diving Backward Elbow Drop, Spanish Pinch (Boston Crab)
Hardcore Moveset: Chair-assisted dropkick, Spanish DDT (through tables / onto chairs) Finisher (Description): Dios Mi! (450 Corkscrew Senton Legdrop) Set Up: Stalling Brainbuster DDT CHARACTER BIOGRAPHY: Captain Conga comes from a ten-person family from inner Spanish-Harlem. He has no true work ethic nor any true dreams. He has simply been flipping and diving off things since he can remember. What better way to support your brothers and sisters in need than to do a few flips for some bucks?
That's all and good until you find yourself doing it for $3,000 dollars and it's three stories up. Wanrt to go for $4,000? Flip from four stories.
Sure, my life was simple - flip a story earn a few thousand bucks. My family was happy and I, well... I was ready to move on. Flipping for me isn't something I love. I love to party, to paint pictures of still life, and to beat the crap out of people who can't beat me up.
So here we are...New Frontier Wrestling... FLIP!
