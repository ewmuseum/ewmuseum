Chris Chambers & the Cosmo Kid (updated)

16 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
renner — 16 April 2009 ----- THE POSSE Combined Weight: 383 lbs. Team Finisher: Kansas City Drive-By DE-Luxe (Cosmo hits an opponent with an inverted atomic drop, and ducks down just low enough for Chambers to drill the opponent with the Kansas City Drive-By, and Cosmo rolls the opponent up for the pin.)
Entrance Music: "Country Grammar" by Nelly --- CHRIS CHAMBERS Full Name: Christopher MacGuyver Chambers (MacGuyver isn't his real middle name, but he claims it anyway, so who are we to judge?)
Age: 24 (born January 17th) Height: 6' Weight: 208 lbs. Hometown: Topeka, Kansas (though he will claim that he's from the Mad Streetz of Chi-Town) Trainer(s): Jeff Garvin / Steve Knox (more the latter than the former) Wrestling Style: Brawler with flashes of brilliance. Alignment: Face Handedness: Right Entrance Music: "Country Grammar" by Nelly Primary Finishers: - Kansas City Drive-By (Running yakuza kick) - Kansas City Remix (Wrist clutch big boot jawbreaker) Nicknames "Double Cheh" (Chris Chambers) Hair Color: Dyed lilac, but is actually blonde Eye Color: Brown Personality: - He's kind of a wigger, often trying to talk like a street thug. Has a tendency to end his sentence with either "yo" (if just saying something) or "G" (if addressing someone). A lot of what he says is often completely out of the context of what the conversation's about, and sometimes might just not make sense at all. He uses a lot of slang he doesn't know the proper meaning of, and he throws up "gang signs" that are likely just American Sign Language for something completely ridiculous. He tends to maintain his gimmick both in and out of the ring, so he still maintains some semblence of the lost ideal of kayfabe.
- However ridiculous he might seem, though, Chambers is far more intelligent than he leads people to believe. He shows flashes of brilliance, can answer questions pretty clearly, and actually tends to break character on occasion, specifically whenever he's annoyed enough to do so.
Style: - Chris is a brawler, more or less. His offense tends to be a pretty basic "hit people until they stop moving" sort of style, so most of his attacks consist of punches, kicks, elbows, knees, the occasional jawbreaker, and the rare suplex. He shows flashes of brilliance in the ring, much as he shows them as a person, though. He may occasionally surprise you with a random technical move, and he's often forming strategies in the ring to counteract the offensive attack of his opponents.
- Uses the enviroment around him as an advantage. He'll counter a charge with a stun gun, he'll throw you into all kinds of objects, he'll throw objects into all kinds of people, and generally speaking, he's a quintessential resourceful brawler.
- He's always learning, so don't be surprised if he's changed his style up to put an opponent at a disadvantage.
--- THE COSMO KID Full Name: Unknown Age: Unknown (likely to be younger than Chambers, though) Height: 5'7" Weight: 175 lbs. Hometown: "The Cosmos" (or, otherwise, a fancy way of saying "Unknown") Trainer(s): Joey Malone Wrestling Style: Luchador Alignment: Face Handedness: Right Entrance Music: "Electricity" by the Avalanches Primary Finishers: - Cosmo Star Suplex (Cross-legged northern lights bomb) - Cosmo Star Splash (810 splash; double somersault into a splash) Nicknames: C.K. Hair Color: Black. Eye Color: Purple (due to contacts) Personality: - The deep voiced, masked luchador in a business suit. Nothing about this guy can be considered normal.
- Rarely seen without the suit, he often doesn't even wrestle without wearing it. His tie can be used against him, much like it could be used against I.R.S. in early 90s WWF. He is never seen without the mask. Ever. Attempts to unmask Cosmo Kid often result in the revelation that Cosmo is wearing another mask underneath it.
- He always seems to know what's going on, even when he shouldn't. He often knows when an ambush is coming. He often knows what his opponent's strategy is going to be before even think of it. The only detriment to his foreknowledge is the fact that he's not very big (he's actually shorter than Alice Wright, one of the two women in Knox's posse) and can be squashed like a bug by significantly larger opponents.
- It's unknown what his real name, age, and hometown are. It's even unknown, even to Steve Knox himself, just how he became a member of the Posse in the first place.
Style: - A luchador. He does luchador-like things, including loads of high flying and elaborate submission holds.
- He likes to set up elaborate high spots. He can do some pretty fantastic high flying moves, though the penalty is that he has a tendency to crash and burn if his opponent moves out of the way. Fortunately, the fact that he always seems to know what's going on means that he can often avoid the crashing and burning to begin with.
- A master of the "delayed sell". He tends to take a huge move, dust himself off, call the guy who just did it a *****, and then flop to the canvas.
- He's really very small, so he'll get crushed like a bug against a significantly larger opponent.
