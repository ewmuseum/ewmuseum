Off to Valinor — 11 March 2008 So, I'm looking at the concept as a rousing success based on the well-rounded participation.
*Any feedback on RPs? **Any lineup predictions? ***Any thoughts/ideas/concepts for future ONE-RP minimum shows? Legion — 11 March 2008 This idea I thought really worked well... the new members of the roster showed potential with their opening gambits :-)
Nova's dilemma against the killing of Hyperspace Kid shall be very interesting to follow as he's got in a way two influences trying to cloud his judgement with the Chairman and Mike Randalls (so THAT's where he's been since Fresno :P) plus it's something to add to the forthcoming writeup of the Road to Wrestlestock series :-)
Personally this has been able to flesh out the arc of Teresa/Legion/'Sickness'/Rook on mine and Teresa's side very well (and I know by saying it was Sickness' first time speaking was slightly wrong but I see it as first time in a 'wrestling style' promo)... the mystery besides Rev 22:20 will get revealed very soon poss at Sleep Now In The Fire given that title ;-)
Next 1 RP minimum style show I feel could be a fan voted stips show similar to Prime's Revolution 150 show...

Overall, I think the connection/angle between Rook Black, Theresa Q, and Legion is very interesting, and can go a bunch of different ways right now. And I'm impressed with Brock Alyas so far.
