GreggG — 16 March 2005 Handlers Name: [name withheld] Wrestler Name: Troy Windham AKA: “The Epitome," "Hollywood's Biggest Icon," "Mr. CSWA," "King of All Media," "King of Wrestling," "Troy Diggiddy."
Height: 6’6” Weight: 255 lbs Hometown: Sweetwater, Texas Resides: South Beach, Miami FLA and Ibiza, Spaid Theme Music: “Take Me Out" by Franz Ferdinand Gimmick: Jet-setting international playboy, former teen pin-up, actor on UPN sit-coms and reality shows, former CSWA World Champion. One of the beautiful people, gets Vanity Fair in his mailbox early. A living legend both in his mind and in the ring.
Appearance: 1/2 Ashton Kuchar, 1/2 the extra member of The Strokes. Hipstered out. Alignment: Heel Moveset Information: Style: Wrestles a very versatile type of style. His height gives him a leverage and strength advantage over his smaller opponents, his slenderness gives him a speed advantage over his bigger foes, he's very crisp and a very solid in-ring technician. But Windham will quickly resort to brawling and cheating. He wrestles like an old-school brawling Memphis heel with an upgraded offense he's learned over the years.
Signatures: Various "Texan" moves -- Bulldog, Iron Claw, Texan Lariat. Troy also likes using quick moves for pins -- DDT's, Diamond Cutters. Also has in his arsenal a superplex, a moonsault (having even broken out a Shooting Star Press recently, that's for special matches only), a sit-down powerbomb, Southpaw Left Hook, forearm shiver (he has a plate in his forearm), lariat closeline, textbook dropkick, camel clutch, Scorpion Deathlock, Figure Four.
FIN 1: The SlackKnife. A quicksnap Reverse Neckbreaker, like an inverted diamond cutter. Troy has hit the SlackKnife at different angles and opportunities -- he's always looking for a way to hit the move and will go for it at any point in the match. He's hit it from the top rope, out of reversals, with a quick boot to the gut, while attempting a suplex. His favorite way to use it is his Spinning SlackKnife Special -- an airplane spin one way followed by a SlackKnife in the other direction.
FIN 2: The Cutting Edge. STF/Full-Nelson Combo. Strengths: 1) Intellect -- Troy is very cunning and loves to outsmart his opponents, frequently bragging about the being the smartest wrestler alive. At one point in his illustrious career, Troy was the "King of the Slackers" known for winning matches using as few moves as possible. And his Entourage is never far away to provide run-ins or other chicanery.
2) Versatility -- Troy is like a swingman in basketball. He's a tough matchup for any and all opponents. Stronger than cruisers, faster then giants. He knows how to wrestle a variety of styles and always finds a way to get the upper hand.
3) Resillience -- Troy has come back from more injuries, drug rehab stints and other acts which would put most people out of the sport. Just when you think you have him, he ends up with the upper hand.
Weaknesses: 1) Injuries -- Troy's been around the block and has wrestled in some of the greatest brawls of all-time. This has resulted in a severely injured neck and a wrist which now has a steel plate in it. Both can be reinjured at anytime.
2) Ego -- Troy has one of the biggest egos in the entire industry. He loves to humiliate and embarrass his foes. This has come back to bite him in the ass several times.
3) Women -- Troy can easily be distracted by women at ringside or any attempt to make himself look pretty.
Background Information: Troy Windham is the younger brother of Mark Windham. His older brother was an All-American athlete in high school who went on to become one of the industry's greatest superstars. Troy was always in his shadow and jealous of his older sibling and sought a different way for notoriety. While Mark was known for his sterling work ethic, Troy was known for coasting by on his talent alone and was more concerned with looking cool, getting laid and scoring pot.
Troy ended up accepting a wrestling scholarship to Baylor University in his native Texas. However, Troy was kicked out of school for his role in a fraternity hazing incident which resulted in the death of a frat brother. Troy then spent a year drifting in and out of college and work, bouncing from woman to woman. He then used his older brother's connections to get himself a part-time deal in the CSWA at the age of 19.
Windham spent the first year of his career quiet -- told by his brother and Hornet and other older veterans that this was his last opportunity and they would not look after him. Windham then started to act to party more -- brag about his wild parties, his good looks, his love of different alt-rock bands, his notorious drug usage and love of the nightlife. And Troy took his partying lifestyle with him to the ring -- openly bragging about it in interviews and playing up to the media the fact he was the self-proclaimed Gen-X Slacker icon who didn't care if he won or lost as long as he got paid and laid. During this time, Troy would first cross paths with Eli Flair -- another newcomer to the industry known for coming up hard on the streets, a man with a polar opposite worldview than Troy Windham. Windham attempted to make a move on Eli's manager Poison Ivy, was rebuked, prompting the start of their feud.
Troy emerged as a bright young and controversial talent. He was paired in a tag team with "Showtime" Shawn Matthews, another loud and obnoxious young wrestler in a pairing called the Gen X-Press. They became the sport's hottest tag team but Troy's many demons surfaced. Troy was in and out of rehab during this stint while Matthews carried the team. Matthews, however, soon started being abusive to Troy -- slapping him in the face once in an interview as Troy silently took it. But they parted ways and Troy once again returned to the CSWA.
But a funny thing happened this time. The fans... some hating Matthews, others young fans with a rebellious streak who could relate to the anti-hero... approved of Troy Windham's behavior. At the same time, the UWA, the forerunner to the NFW, was re-forming. Troy expressed public interest in singing with the promotion but would not do so until he was given the largest contract in professional wrestling history. The fans rallied around Troy, chanting SHOW TROY THE MONEY as Troy would crash the show, disrupt matches and raise his profile. Despite not being officially on the roster, Troy was the league's biggest superstar and was, by that time, a contender for world belts in promotions around the world. Troy was given the contract and had several high-profile feuds in the UWA but could never procure the title.
Troy, however, did have success in the CSWA. The CSWA at the time was marked by the GUNS/Hornet bloodfeud which saw GUNS continuously get the win, holding the World Title in a league he despised and wanted to, and nearly, destroyed. In his first attempt at the CSWA World Title, Troy faced GUNS in a match in GUNS' hometown of San Antonio. They battled and a series of referee bumps and run-ins prompted Hornet to come to the ring as Windham pinned GUNS. The victory was allowed to stand and Windham was the new CSWA World Champion -- a symbolic gesture as Hornet counted three as the young wrestler he oft-criticized and feuded with bested GUNS, the man who beat him for the World Title.
Troy, at this time, started to see his q-rating rise in Hollywood (he frequently petitioned for acting jobs in his interviews). At the same time, the CSWA was swtiching networks -- from Prodigy to FWrestling.com and the UWA closed up shop. Windham balanced his burgeoning acting career with the demands of being the first CSWA World Champion on FWrestling.com. The title was stripped from him, however, and was eventually won by Eli Flair.
This again prompted a feud. Windham returned full-time to the sport, claiming he never lost his title and claimed to have put Flair in his shadow. Flair and Windham had a feud for the CSWA World Title which ended with Eli Flair beating his longtime foe and ending any claim Windham had to the CSWA World Title.
Windham was in and out of the CSWA the next few years. Then, one show, a devious entity formed -- the triumverate of Eli Flair, Hornet and Mike Randalls united to take over the CSWA, callingthemselves the ClaimStakers. "Hurricane" Eddy Love was feuding with them when Windham returned to the sport, this time calling himself "Mr. CSWA" and forming an alliance with Love called Playboys Incorporated. The Playboys and The ClaimStakers battled with each other in various pairings which many considered to be amongst the greatest matches in CSWA and wrestling history. And, once again, the individual feud between Flair and Troy bubbled over. Troy and Eli fought in one cage match which ended in a bloody brawl all over the arena, a ***** affair which ended with Windham winning after a SlackKnife on top of the cage caused both men to fall through the cage, to the mat with Windham landing on top. Their rematch at CSWA Anniversary 2001 was even more hellacious -- a beaten, broken Windham took on Eli Flair in an I Quit Cage match which ended when Eddy Love threw in the towel for Windham after Eli damaged Troy's neck and broke every bone in his left hand, his wrist and also dislocated his elbow.
Troy has recouperated since then and has been in and out of various promotions -- fighting against various foes in the CSWA. He also infamously palyed a role in the last days of the GWE promotion. At their cross-promotional Battleground Britain card, Windham had a run-in with their world champion The Steel City Son. Windham had spent the previous weeks running down their champion and when confronted by the champ was slapped across the face after cowardly backing away. At the GWE's next show, Windham hopped the railing and laid out GWE veteran Marcus Johnson by SlackKnifing him with a chair after spraying him with a can of mace. Security guards came to the ring to fend off Windham and Windham maced them to carry out his attack. Then Windham did the unthinkable -- he turned towards the audience and sprayed ringside fans with mace, prompting a riot which saw Windham beat up a fan, causing a stampede resulting in several injuries and his arrest. Windham said his goal was to shut down the GWE and then attacked the GWE World Champion Boogie Smalls with several racially-tinged promos. Windham settled all charges out-of-court.
Windham has come to the NFW to get what he feels is rightfully his -- The Ultratitle, one of pro wrestling's greatest prizes. He also feels he was responsible for the success of the UWA's second go-round and thus is responsible for the success of the NFW.
He will remain controversial, successful and always finding a way to steal the spotlight from whatever promotion he is in.

3. Iron Horse. A infamously strong-will, stamina that makes James Brown look alot less hard working, and toughness all rolled into one. Alias is a man who can take an unbelievable amount of ****, and take it on a regular basis... for a surprising amount of time. All to acheive what he has to.
Weaknesses: 1. Short sighted. He's been known not to see the woods for the tree. Put him under enough stress and he'll get tunnel vision on a certain goal, leaving everything else to be collateral.
