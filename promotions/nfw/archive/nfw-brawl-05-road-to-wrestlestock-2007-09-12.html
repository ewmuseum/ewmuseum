NFW Brawl 05: ROAD TO WRESTLESTOCK, 12 September 2007

12 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 05: ROAD TO WRESTLESTOCK 12 Sep 2007 KeyArena, Seattle, Washington (seats 17,459) Introduction Hello fans, we've got a quick recap of what Seattle fans were calling a great show this past week as NFW opened up the first round of the double elimination National Championship Tournament. Phil Atken vs. The Sailor Man The first match of the evening, and the first match of the first round for the National Championship, saw Phil "YOUR Entertainment Saviour" facing off against the The Sailor Man. On his way to the ring, The Sailor Man hit four people with his wooden rowboat which actually got a few cheers. The match though saw Phil Atken just come out firing doing everything he could to get the upperhand against the larger Sailor Man. After Atken nailed The Sailor Man with a few German Suplexes and a brainbuster that was quickly followed up with a near pinfall, the Sailor Man finally started to battle back as the fans got behind him. He nailed Atken with fist after fist, using his size to his advantage before nailing Phil with a backbreaker and a vertical suplex. As the referee was distracted by an attractive woman in the crowd, Sailor Man went at Atken with one of his oars. Yet, Atken grabbed the oar and knocked The Sailor Man out. When the referee turned around, he went for the cover and got the three count to advance in the National Championship tournament while The Sailor Man suffered his first loss. Maggot vs. David Tui As the second match of the evening began, "Bleed it Out" by Linkin Park ripped through the arena and David Tui steps out from behind the curtain. He plays to the crowd as they chant "King! King! King!" as he walks down the ramp. He slides into the ring and Maggot quickly comes down the ramp quickly as the two start battling it out in the ring. The first few minutes of the match is even between the two as they trade fists and kicks. Maggot nails a few body slams and clotheslines, using his muscles to slow down Tui. Tui retaliates with a wide reporitre of moves as he nailed a DDT followed up with a Northern Lights suplex. Tui goes for cover, but Maggot kicks out right at the two count. Tui continues the pressure, making sure to keep Maggot grounded as he goes to the air and nails a Missile Dropkick. As Tui goes back to the top rope again and goes for a Frog Splash, Maggot is able to get his knees up and Tui crashes and burns. Maggot then connects with the Slimer before going into the Pottstown Crab. After wearing Tui out with the submission move he nails the Trash Compactor for the victory and moves on to the second round of the tournament. Teresa Q vs. Hunchblack The fans explode into a frenzy as "Clarissa" by Mindless Self Indulgence begins to play in the arena and then morphs into "300 Mhz" by At The Drive-In and Teresa walks out. It's quickly followed by "The Hunch" by Mad Mike and the Maniacs as Hunchblack walks out with cheers from the fans. The match begins with Hunchblack coming off firing after having to miss out on the PTC Unification Match. He comes out with a serious drive as he nails Teresa with a dropkick, a back body drop, and then with a rare moonsault. Hunchblack then goes for the cover, but Teresa is able to kick out at the last possible second. Hunchblack keeps the pressure up as he nails a belly to belly suplex and then a gutbuster on the slightly smaller Teresa. Yet, after going for a leg drop that he misses badly, Teresa starts tearing into Hunchblack with the Eunuch Provocateur and then a Rising Collapse. She follows that up with a Fluffy Reign of Darkness. Hunchblack then battles back and nails a spear. As he goes for a Jacknife Powerbomb she slips out and finishes Hunchblack off with the Process of Illumination for the victory and to move on to the second round of the tournament. Mike Randalls vs. Coral Avalon The end of the first half of the show saw Mike Randalls, a legend of NFW, facing off against the man known as Coral Avalon. Avalon came out hot as he had Ranall on the ropes after a few punches and then a few knife edge chops. However, the much larger Mike Randalls responds with a few knees to the midsection before nailing with a variety of suplexes like the German Suplex, Northern Lights Suplex, Belly to Back Suplex and a Tiger Suplex. He then nails a Fisherman Suplex before nailing a leg drop across Avalon's throat. He goes for the cover, but Avalon is able to kick out. The match continues to go back and forth with Avalon keeping Randall on his toes as he uses his high-flying manuevers by nailing a Senton and a 450 Splash. He goes for a cover but Randalls is able to kick out with ease. Avalon then goes for the Ratings Spike, but Randalls is able to reverse it into The Devastator, which ends the match after Avalon passes out before tapping to the respect of the crowd. Rook Black vs. 'XXX' Sean Stevens The second half of the evening began with XXX Sean Stevens going up against the "Life of the Mind" Rook Black. Stevens got the early advantage as he nailed a German Suplex before going into a half Boston Crab. Black quickly gets to the ropes and battles back with a swinging neckbreaker after nailing a backbreaker. He then nails a elbow from the second rope and goes for a cover that Stevens quickly gets out of. Stevens then fires back with a knee to the face and a Diamond Cutter. He goes for a cover on Black that Rook is able to kick out of at the two count. Stevens then goes for a drop kick, but Black is able to swat Stevens away before connecting with a Crossbow for the victory and advances to the second round. Dusk vs. Jacob McKail "Stronger" by Kanye West erupts into the arena as out comes fan favorite, Dusk who is immediately attacked by McKail from backstage. The two brawl all the way down to the ring and then inside of the ring where the bell rings. McKail keeps the offense up as Dusk is drained after three matches in three evenings. McKail remains focuses as he goes for a technical based game to keep the larger Dusk off the ropes, flying around the ring. He locks him up into an Ankle Lock but Dusk refuses to quit as he just grits through the pain. McKail then switches it into a sharpshooter that Dusk is able to break after reaching the ropes. McKail then goes for the No Fear, but Dusk is able to move out of the way in just the nick of time. Dusk gets back up and unleashes fury and hell upon McKail as he nails him with a variety of kicks including a roundhouse kick, an enziguri and a Shining Wizard. Dusk then leaps off the top rope and nails a shooting star press. As McKail gets back up, Dusk goes for the Lights Out, but McKail ducks it and goes for a clothesline but Dusk ducks it only to connect with the superkick after all. He goes for the cover as the fans cheer him on victory as he advances to the second round of the National Championship tournament. Rocko Daymon vs. Felix Red The two start off in the ring and they just go at it as you would expect from two quality wrestlers. Felix Red comes off hot as he goes for a German Suplex and connects with it. As he brings Daymon up for another one, Rocko is able to block it and nails Felix with a jawbreaker. The two continue to go at it evenly matched as Red then bounces off the ropes and flies off the ropes with a flying crossbody. He then nails Daymon with a drop kick before going to the top and nailing a 450 splash. Red then goes for the cover but only gets a two count as Daymon refuses to give up. Daymon starts to battle back as he nails a knee to the midsection before connecting with a spinebuster and then follows it up with a gutwrench suplex. He then is able to connect with a DDT before locking in a Texas Cloverleaf that looks to finish Red, but Felix is able to grab the ropes when it looks like all is lost. Rocko pulls Felix up and goes for The Brain Rocker, but Felix is able to block it before nailing a Spinning Heel Kick. He then picks up the victory after connecting with the Shattered Horizon! Jason Payne vs. Cameron Cruise The main event features Jason "Too Hot for NFW" Payne facing off against Cameron Cruise. Both men start jawing off against each other before Payne slaps the taste out of Cruise. This gets Cruise hot as he just lays into Cruise with a flurry of fists before knocking Jason out of the ring. Cameron then bounces off the ropes and connects with a suicide plancha as Cruise just continues to wail away on Payne! Payne then pushes Cruise off of him and sends Cameron into the ring barrier! He then clothesline Cruise over the barrier and then into the crowd. The two then begin to battle through the crowd before Payne nails Cruise with a reverse DDT onto the concrete. He then drags Cruise back to the ring where he continues to dominate the match where he connects with a piledriver and a sidewalk slam. He then drags Cruise up to his feet who finds his second wind and begins to battle back with fists and kicks before pushing Payne into the corner of the ring and connects with a tornado DDT. He then leaps off the top rope and nails a flying elbow before going for the cover. He only gets a two count though and goes for a Senton but misses. Payne then follows it up with a Full Nelson Slam for a victory to end the evening!
