NFW Roster Signing News! Posted by Natrone Rice on 3 Jul 2007 New Frontier Wrestling has confirmed that it has almost finished its offseason signing period leading up to the v.3 launch in the middle of this summer. With a mix of returning wrestlers and a supposed double-digit roster expansion, we were able to learn that BOTH Ultratitle Finalists, NFW World Champion Kin Hiroshi, NFW Regular Season and former World Champion Felix Red and other significant NFW superstars have signed their names on the dotted line to return to the federation. Leaving no doubt that the main tier of talent was not only enthused by the conclusion of Season 2, but are relishing in the opportunity to compete in the v.3 format. With the confirmed signings of Nova and Kin Hiroshi, NFW won't have to worry about any nasty holdouts, or potential 'subject to change' notices for its upcoming PPV, Wrestlestock 2. By securing Yori Yakamo Junior and Felix Red, NFW returns the upper echelon of its top-tier from Season 2, which should placate any fans of storyline continuity problems. If that wasn't exciting enough, insiders are also confirming that Michael Manson and Eli Flair are the only two important figures that might not return, but that's due to their retirement from the sport. Wrestlers such as 'HOT PROPERTY' Eddie Mayfield, 'THE EGO BUSTER' Dan Ryan, 'THE WOLF' Mike Randalls, MAGGOT, MITTENS T. CAT have all been confirmed as visiting the NFW offices this week for final contract signings and physicals for medical clearance. Joe the Plumber, co-Television Champion has already been seen on air with his 'half' of the Television Title and Craig Miles confirmed that Shawn Hart's negotiations are on-going and due to close this week. Keep watching the website this week as more names, old and new are announced on the roster. More importantly, next week is the set date for NFW releasing its preliminary v.3 roster and contract details, which supplied alot of insider buzz in the industry when Season 2 began.
