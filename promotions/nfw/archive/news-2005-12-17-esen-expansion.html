NFW news: ESEN Expansion?

17 December 2005

ESEN Expansion? Posted by Natrone Rice on 17 Dec 2005 On the heels of their midseason conference meeting, rumours from inside sources are starting to float around that Extreme Sports Entertainment Network might seize on its recent ratings increases over the sophomore season televising for the Ultratitle. If you think the resurrection of the Ultimate Wrestling Alliance is just a fad, you could be in for a huge shock. That'd be the general consensus regarding Anne Mystic's viewpoint on the introduction of UWA programming, even though we're in midseason. Over the last six months, New Frontier Wrestling programming ratings have increased over 25 percent, while website hits have increased 200 percent. While some could argue that the Eastern Conference is leading the way, the Western Conference wasn't behind by much and its been overrun with vendettas and allegiances forged in the days of the UWA. Not to take anything away from men like Alias, Rook Black, Vic Creed, Eli Flair or Troy Windham -- but with Craig Miles and Anne Mystic (no doubt with Eddie Mayfield's influence) signing the roster with those vendettas and allegiances in mind...the success is a result of what started in the UWA. And now its back...but why? To let Anne Mystic, Eddie Mayfield and Avery Prosser have their ball and run with it, is a huge risk...one that could burden ESEN greatly if it fails. On the other hand, Eddie Mayfield now holds the UWA World Title and while the title may have started as a sham...if Eli Flair is taking this one seriously...what does that mean? Well, Mayfield has proven himself in the ring...while him and Miles haven't won a World Title in the ring (sorry, Eddie) they both are known for their in-ring performances. Some consider Mayfield an automatic five-star match every time he walks out there. So, the UWA has a foundation...but there's no way in hell they'll stay under NFW boundaries after this season. Like Steve Miller wisely stated, "Take the money and run." However, ESEN -- a company invested in by Quentin Sullivan and Craig Miles, keep in mind...isn't just going to let everyone in the UWA run with its money. Obviously, Eddie Mayfield's possession of the UWA World Title is going to draw Miles and Tom Adler onto that programming. In essence, that clears the way for the NFW Western Conference to focus on its current Ultratitle competitors. And with the addition of KVC and Brandon Youngblood, the Western Conference adds "huge firepower" one scout said in private. Attitude concerns aren't an issue in the Western Confernece...looking around Alias, Black, Uriel, Riktor or Flair and then seeing KVC, Youngblood, Windham, ...its all about attitude, or shutting someone's up. So the UWA and Western Conference don't need each other...the Eastern Conference and Western Conference don't need each other. Three separate realms and identities. Now, here's the shocker. ESEN isn't done. With Calvin Carlton restarting the New South Enterprises and Joey Melton, Guy Boudreaux and Jeff Garvin playing roles on NFW television, ESEN is looking into becoming a force in the south again. With Shane Southern's sudden knee injury, the bottom fell out on any hopes for a revival... ...that's why its interesting that Quentin Sullivan has been reported meeting not only with North Charleston officialls, the old South conference home, but a possible Florida outfit...and another rumour that Sullivan and Miles have an idea so big, it might entice a certain Greensboro outfit if they get the ball rolling. At the recent shareholders meeting announcing the UWA franchise resurrection, Quentin Sullivan was questioned about the possibility of expansion... ...his answer? "Future expansion?" He then laughed at the shock of it all to himself, "what do you think is going on right now?"
