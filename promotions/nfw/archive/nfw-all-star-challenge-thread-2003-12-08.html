jediPREZ — 8 December 2003 I'm waiting on 2 writers for the rest of Week 7 to be completed. In the meantime, I wanted to start off the NFW All-Star Challenge Thread. Rules: 1) Top 5 wrestlers in each conference (point-wise after Week 8) will face off in the All-Star War Games Match. You may NOT be in another match.
2) The amount of points allowed to be wagered can be no more than 1/2 of the greater point total of the two combatants, nor more than 100% of the lesser point total. IE Southern can't bet more than 22.5 points, McMillan can't bet more than 20 (as of right now). So that match's max betting is 20. However, due to some new folks arriving. If you're b/t 0-10 points, I will allow you to bet a maximum of 10 points.
3) Order to be followed to get match signed: a) Challenges accepted and finalized b) Wager agreed upon after finalization and must be approved by NFW Committe (me, JN, GG) 4) If your challenge gets into War Games, you will either face off someone in a similar bind - or just not have a match.
5) Only ONE match per wrestler on the card. 6) Non-NFW Ultratitle Competitors do not apply to the above rules, but are free to make challenges for the card here...as well as list any matches finalized. They may also compete in more than one match. NFW Ultratitle competitor versus non-Ultratitle competitor CANNOT have any point values wagered.
7) You WILL lose the amount of points wagered if you lose the match. So don't bet the farm if you're not 100% positive that you'll put a good showing in.
8) Due to Week 8 being close to the finalization period -- competitors can agree to a 'MAX' bet that will be determined once that card goes up.
