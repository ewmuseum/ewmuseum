Rich Mahogany Biography Hometown Austin, TX Height 6'1 Weight 210 Handedness Rich doesn’t use his hands, that’s what The Ladies are for! Looks He’s got long, light brown, feathered hair, beautiful green eyes, immaculately white teeth, beautiful abs, tremendous thighs… etc. Biography Rich Mahogany is a slimy, grimy, lowlife gigolo. The Ladies love him, and he loves The Ladies. Is there really anything else to know? Wrestling Style Rich is quick, and he’s technically proficient, but he’s going to win most of his matches the old fashioned way: cheating. Any dirty tactic you’ve ever seen, from hair-pulling, finger-stomping, all the way to back-rakes and eye-gouges. If it’s cheap, and it works, expect Rich to use it. Often. Springboard *****slap: Used mostly as a counter when whipped into the ropes, Rich will springboard from the middle rope, twist, and come back with a ridiculous five across the jaw. Ring Entrances Rich loves The Ladies. He plays to them, dances for them, wiggles his pelvis at them, blows kisses to them, takes kisses from them, hands out roses, room keys, lude and lascivious winks, etc. To anyone with a penis, he is a complete douch-nozzle. Has a third eye for referee position, allowing him to cheat. Weaknessees 1. Low tolerance for pain. 2. Easily distracted by The Ladies. 3. Likes to pose and dance a bit too much. Ring attire Depending on what color scheme he’s going with at the time, he’ll always come to the ring with a vest and a bow-tie of the same color. In the ring he wears regular, if a bit tight tights, kneepads with stars on them, and boots. Theme Music “Love Man” by Otis Redding
