Yori Yakamo, Jr.... ULTRATITLE favorite? Posted by Natrone Rice on 23 Dec 2005 If you said by Week Six of the Eastern Conference schedule that Yori Yakamo, Jr. would be in position to get an NFW World Championship shot, many who've followed his career would have scoffed at you. Hell, I would have too. Yori's always lived in the shadow of his older and more accomplished brother Hida when it came to inside the wrestling ring. Outside of it, his exploits brought more bad attention than good. Hell, Yori wasn't even the most heralded MBE alumnus coming into NFW; that title belonged to Maggot. Yet, Maggot sits at 2-3 after a Week Five loss to Yori. Hida's only begun a serious comeback. Yori is wrestling Dan Ryan for a shot at Doc Silver's (or Jonathan Marx's, or maybe even Kin Hiroshi's) NFW World Heavyweight Championship. Unreal. It's hard to believe that a couple of years ago, Yori was embroiled in a controversy over defrauding a Georgia school district. Or that he was forced into a shotgun wedding by Japanese wrestling legend Yoshihiro Yamada. But the out-of-ring shenanigans haven't detracted from him inside the ring. Some may believe that the most current out-of-ring shenanigans are contributing to his success inside the ring. But what really is the force behind Yori's success? Well, to be quite blunt, he cheats his ass off and gets away with it. Still, if he can do that, more power to him. It's actually a far more difficult task to break the rules and not get disqualified than it is to become a great wrestler in a technical sense. Most of the time, it's a lot more entertaining as well. I mean, who would you rather watch in a match; Yori Yakamo, Jr. or Jean Rabesque? I thought so. But the question is, will it carry him to the ULTRATITLE? The smart money is, not while Ryan, Silver, Michael Manson and Felix Red are still on the East's roster. But stranger things have happened. And no one thought Yori could go this far. So stay tuned.
