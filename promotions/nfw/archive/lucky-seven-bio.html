Lucky Seven Biography Eye Color Green Nicknames The Black Rainbow Hometown Secaucus, NJ Birthday 06/20/1988 Height 5'8 Weight 135 Alias Lucky Seven Handedness Right Looks Tall, long limbs, high cheekbones, big pouty lips, and a slender physique with good muscle definition, particularly her delts which are very well well-defined. Shoulder length hair that is dyed black with blonde highlights. Eye color is green. Full sleeve tattoo on her right arm, a mural of mythological characters with a prominent Medusa head on the shoulder. Also has a tattoo of a serpent twisting around her spine from top to bottom, as well as some other images on her shoulder blades. Wears black, green, or purple eye shadow that flairs out to her temples, with matching lipstick. Biography Growing up in the Palazzo wrestling family, Laurie spent her youth as a competitive gymnast, traveling with championship teams and winning individual accolades as a balance beam and freestyle expert. Due to her above average height (a roadblock to securing a spot on the U.S. national team), she retired at 18 and enrolled at Princeton University where she dropped out after two semesters. Laurie auditioned for and won a spot in Cirque Du Soleil, and spent the next year and a half travelling the globe as a circus performer. When her contract was up she declined an extension and returned to New Jersey, where she began training to become a pro-wrestler. She competed in various independent organizations in the tri-state area, most notably FIRE ONE women's pro-wrestling. In 2010, Laurie went to work for her brother RA Palazzo in the newly formed Next Level Wrestling (NLW). She continued her training there and took on the moniker Lucky Seven, a nod to legendary independent wrestler and Palazzo family friend Se7en. Laurie was going to be part of a new women's division in NLW, when the league closed and was bought out by New Frontier Wrestling. With her contract now owned by NFW, she was sent to UWA in Philadelphia to train under Teresa Quaranta, and also spent extensive time under the tutelage of NFW Champion Castor Strife. In January 2012, wrestling as Lucky Seven, Laurie captured the UWA Women's World Championship. Wrestling Style Speed-based impact attacks. Extremely flexible, and it shows in her penchant for leg-based offense. Trainer Teresa Quaranta, Castor Strife Finisher Move Seven Out Finisher Description Swinging fisherman neckbreaker with bridge. Other Moves Witch Hunter: Shining wizard; the only real "sub-finisher" she has. Crescent Moonsault: Asai moonsault, usually done outside of the ring; high-spot to get the crowd pumped. The Pickpocket: Gannosuke Clutch; a tricky armtrap somersault cradle that you should really YouTube before writing. Seven can absolutely finish a match this way. -Bulldog (from anywhere, or the second turnbuckle) -Figure four leglock -Spinwheel kick -Repeated roundhouse kicks to the ribs (performed in the corner) -Hurricanrana -Legsweep DDT (and many other DDTs including double-arm and tilt-a-whirl) -Running Superman elbow -Tarantula (rope hung Boston crab) -Flying leg drop from top turnbuckle -Rolling neck snap -Head kick (right high kick) Ring Entrances Walks out of the entrance, arms held out, chewing gum and smiling maliciously. She then walks confidently to the ring, eyes trained forward. As she gets close to the end of the aisle, she spits her gum into the crowd, and takes a running start. She runs, slides beneath the bottom rope, and spins onto one knee at the center of the ring, throwing her black/blonde hair back and running her hand through one side or the other. She then climbs the top turnbuckle, yells to the crowd while pointing to herself, leaps up, and does a split-legged moonsault from the top turnbuckle, landing on her feet. Strengths Speed, cunning, impressive skill set. Weaknessees Impatient, overzealous. Ring attire Black closed short-vest with silver running up and down the middle, and "L7" written on the front in gold, black spandex half-shorts, and black shin-padded boots with 777 written in silver down each boot.
