CRASHmas Writing

12 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WestPREZ — 12 December 2003 Ok, here's the deal. I'm out from 12/21 - 1/5. JN has a little vacation too. I don't know what Gregg's deal is, either.
JN has said he can post the card once its written. But we're going to need some 'chipping in' to get it posted close to Christmas. I'm sorry, I can't help more, but I'll write as much as I can before I leave.
It's going to be a pretty special card as it will lead NFW into some really cool storylines that we might have hinted at, but have not begun. It'd also be great for you guys to get your characters ready for what will be a very explosive All-Star Week, sure to change alot of the infrastructure in NFW.
Take that how you want. Soooooooo - basically, we've got 12 singles matches for Crashmas and I'm sure some can be written, while others can be glossed over.
If anyone can help, I'd really appreciate it. Just list how many matches (highlight, full) you can do. Hell, if you want to write out a match - call it out now.
Hoping NFW is in the giving spirit, WestPrez Mister Dread — 12 December 2003 Sure, what the hell. I'll take one full length match or a couple short ones. Just let me know.
JABolich — 12 December 2003 Since my last exam is tomorrow and I'll then be free until January 5, I can probably do a full match or a page of highlights.
ShawnHartXXX — 15 December 2003 I'll finally give in and submit my 'talents' for perhaps a small something. Get back at me.
WestPREZ — 19 December 2003 We could use 1-2 more recruits. (rings Salvation Army bell)
