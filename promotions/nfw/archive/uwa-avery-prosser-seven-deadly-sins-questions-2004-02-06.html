GreggG — 6 February 2004 Some dudes for you guys to know. THE OBLIVION EXPRESS-- they were on the first card in NFW History. 'Railroad' Lionel Reese and 'Doc Doom' Jeff Cummins. They were originally part of WildStar's FIVE STAR ENTERPRISES heel faction that later morphed into Calvin Carlton Enterprises when he became the group's "portfolio and investments manager." Around the same time, WildStar turned face and jettisoned Calvin and The Oblivion Express left for Japan and were replaced with The Original Showstoppers. Think of them as FW's version of Doc and Gordy or as The World's Greatest Tag Team but bigger in size like Brock for you newer fans.
DRS-- The Dirty Rotten Scoundrels. Arguably the greatest tag team on *Prodigy when we were still on there. Saafir Muhammed and 187. Saafir does the mic-work-- just some low-down, angry black guy stuff. 187 stays silent and is the muscle of the team. Think SMW-era Gangstas. The hidden secret about them is that away from the plunder and the violence, they're actually REALLY talented wrestlers, as Saafir once went to a 60-minute time limit draw against Tom Adler in one of my all-time favorite matches.
Blaine Hollywood-- read his introductory note to get an idea of the character. One of JN's longtime concepts for a character, finally come to life. I'm REALLY excited about this guy.
Avery Prosser-- "The Prodigy." Embittled wrestler who feels he never got his due. JN can elaborate better than I can, I don't know the history of him as well as the other guys.

Who is the Deacon? GreggG — 8 February 2004 Deacon is a longtime character in the CSWA and held the world title there on a few occassions. I like to think of him as the Christian Undertaker-- large in size, consumate hero. Known for his remarkable ring entrances. Deacon, the character, is largely silent (every now and then he'll speak) and his mouthpiece is Chris Shepherd. One of my all-time favorite characters, really different and unique and really great babyface.
Eddy Love, if you couldn't tell by his promo, is a retro-style heel in the vein of Ric Flair or Tully Blanchard and is a lot of people's all-time favorite character.
