Dr Curiosity — 4 January 2010 What do we still need writing? I have some spare time next week... The Great Eye — 4 January 2010 (FADEIN: Green Machine with one of those money counting machines, he's running a thick pile of hundreds through the machine, the whirl of the cash being counted a sound he's heard often. After the batch finishes he bundles it and reaches over to more hundreds. Before he can get them into the machine, Doc Silver walks into the room.)
DOC: What are you doing? GREENIE: Well after I nailed the over in the Bills/Colts game...32.5?! Are you kidding me?! That's 4 touchdowns and 2 Field goals ALL GAME, they were GIVING money away.
DOC: It was...Colts back ups Vs the worthless Bills in a driving snow storm, I can't believe you think you outsmarted everyone by lucking out on that...
GREENIE: Whatever, look, odds just came out on the posting of Crash 50... (Points to a piece of paper) look at the odds on the first 4 dates, all 10:1 or higher, I go bet 10,000 on each of them, even if I only hit the 10 to 1 odds, I still clear 60,000, it comes out sooner, I win even more! They are GIVING money away!
DOC: You do understand that this is a joke right...That the guy who put this out doesn't have these bets listed anywhere, and won't take action on them?
GREENIE: It's...Not for money? DOC: No... GREENIE: Dammit...This sucks...Oh well, I got 40,000 to burn and if NFW isn't going to just hand me money, it's time to rock the dogs...Ravens, Jets, Packers, and Iggles, straight money line across the board and a 4 team parlay mega bomb! If NFW won't give me free money the NFL will!
(Greenie goes back to putting the money through the counting machine.) DOC: The offer for counselling still stands... GREENIE: Only an addiction if you're losing Doc...If you're winning it's a profession... (Doc shakes his head and walks out of the room.) GREENIE: Man Jets are a pipe this week...I need more then 40K for this stuff...I know I told the wife I wouldn't take another loan out against the car to finance my bets, but a man needs ammo!
(Greenie gets on the phone, most likely to talk to a loanshark of some kind...FADEOUT) Off to Valinor — 8 March 2010 By Easter (30:1) 3 21.43% 1. JLevinson 2. QueenOfTheRing 3. TSiegel Congratulations to our closest winners!
