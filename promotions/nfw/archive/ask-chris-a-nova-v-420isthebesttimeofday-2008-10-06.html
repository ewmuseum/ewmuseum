Nova — 7 October 2008 -What are your influences when writing Nova?This is a tough one, but I'll resist the urge to make a weed joke. There is a lot of the things that affect me and my general pop culture leanings in the way I write Nova. If I had to point to a real-life wrestling influence it'd probably be mostly Chris Jericho. I like to think of Nova (and hope he comes across) as a cross between what I love about the old and what I love about the new, the old in the sense of the classic tights, the ring entrance, the emphasis on wins and losses and focus on history in the ring as a means of propelling the story, and the new in the sense of the embracing of the counter-culture, the whacked-out EAGLEstar/Season Two mythology, and...um...hell, I dunno.
I think Jericho combines that same freshness of the new generation with the best of yesteryear, and it extends beyond that to the look. I LOVED the 2001-era face Jericho with the wild long hair and woolly-ass beard. That was a huge influence on Nova in terms of the 'look.'
And Katz (or "you" since it's your question).:D Easily the biggest influence on NFW Nova, because he (you) kept throwing me curve balls.
-All characters have 'parts' to them that comprise their wholes. Describe Nova's in the fashion of a bourbon-based drink.The bottom third of the glass that you fill with bourbon, that's the "Best in the West" foundation that formed the basis of Nova's NFW identity (which at the time we were writing it diverged so completely with what I was doing in PRIME I handled Nova like two separate people). Then the half-glass of coke you pour on top of that is the "Chose the Business over the Family" storyline that I wrote exclusively in PRIME but have leaked over into NFW in the "version 3" run. I don't harp on it because I don't think it's as interesting to the FW crowd as in the narrative-driven off-camera style favored in PTC, but it's an important balancing element to how absurd and comical Nova can be. Then the splash of bourbon you add to the top of the glass because you weren't sure you made it strong enough, that's the counter-revolutionary, the "F*ck You" guy, the "Win at All Costs" bitter attitude that has the tendency to take over when Nova's position of power is threatened. >=)
