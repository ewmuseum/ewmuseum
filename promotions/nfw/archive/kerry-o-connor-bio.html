Kerry O'Connor Biography Biography Blonde hair, bushy eyebrows, Irish and a definate Boston Southie. Graduate in Journalism and Minor in Communications from Boston College, where he was the host and producer of BC's cable access show BEANTOWN SPORTS BREAK! for 5 years, staying on after college to pass the torch to a new group of kids who could handle the job. O'Connor made it a rule to sneak in as much wrestling information into his broadcasts, a move that would give him lots of appeal, when news on wrestling was hard to find. A huge fan of Pro Wrestling, he dreamed of being a PBP announcer all his life, but always thought that his Boston drawl would keep that from becoming a reality. After flooding Quentin Sullivan's mailbox with tapes of his show for years, when Miles and Sullivan started to plan NFW, they took a chance on the youngster. He and Lamont Hollywood represent the 'voice' of New Frontier Wrestling.
