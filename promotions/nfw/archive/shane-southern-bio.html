Shane Southern Biography Hometown New Orleans, LA Height 6'3 Weight 265 Handedness Lefty Looks Shane Douglas build...long brown hair usually pulled back in a ponytail behind his head. Fed Titles NFW World Heayweight Champion (1x), Ultratitle Season 1 Finalist, Ultratitle Season 1 South Conference Champion Fed Awards FACE OF THE YEAR (5x) Biography A Southern boy raised on Bourbon Street in New Orleans, Shane’s not a red-neck per se and doesn’t play into the stereotypes, but he does speak with a fairly heavy southern accent. Trained by his father, he has held tag team titles in the pacific northwest with his cousin Daniel. He held the UWA US Title on three separate occasions until he was forced to take a hiatus from the sport to heal from a motorcycle accident that caused injuries to both his knees. Upon his return he competed in the IWC which included a World Title reign. He is a former NFW World Champion and current CSWA World Champion. Other Fed Titles NFW World Champion, CSWA World Champion, IWC World Champion, UWA US Champion Wrestling Style Technical, Big High-Flyer.
