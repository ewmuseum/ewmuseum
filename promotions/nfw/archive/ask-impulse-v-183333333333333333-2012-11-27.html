User Poets — 27 November 2012 So I'm working on something to spread out amongst the next few shows, and I need YOUR help.
No, not yours. You suck and you're barely a life form. The people behind you. Impulse will be taking questions from the peanut gallery known as NFW fans and members (and Natrone Rice, natch), and will be answering as many as I can put together.
Few caveats: 1: This is not an 'in character' thing. Asking a question as Rook Black (for example) will not lead to a program because all questions will be asked without a person at the other end.
2: This is not an 'in character' thing. Asking Impulse "Why do you suck so much?" will not be answered, and I'll most likely publicly make fun of you for being such a tool and I may ban you for a short period of time. Asking "What makes you think you have any shot at beating Castor Strife when he pinned you clean last time, and currently sports a winning percentage of just over .900 against you lifetime" is a valid question, though I may edit it for space.
That being said, if you're interested in participating, please reply here or (preferably) PM me. AIM questions will not be a guarantee of inclusion since I leave my computer on for hours at a time and it sometimes has a tendency to shut down on its own.
So, go forth and wreak Havoc. jediPREZ — 28 November 2012 First time caller, long-time listener... QUESTIONS: 1) Why do you still refer yourself as Impulse even though you're unmasked? Is it because the names of "Randall(s)" and "Knox" are overused in NFW history?
2) There seems to be a growing percentage of fans that are booing you due to your 'code adherence'... and feel it is holding you back as an all-around competitor given your size and style of wrestling. Knowing New Frontier's love for crazy weapon/stipulation matches, do you feel your code will become a long-term handicap to your success?
Biron — 1 December 2012 I'll be adding more as they come to me, Pete. 1) If you had to choose a tag team partner from the NFW roster as it currently stands, who would it be and why? Now, if you could choose from anyone that's ever competed in NFW, who would it be and why?
