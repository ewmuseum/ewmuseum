The (Hopeful) return of "The Fem Phenom" Nakita DuBov

Hell_Fighter — 14 May 2004 I think that I can do this again, and if you have any room on the NFW roster for me, I hope that I can help in anyway that I can. If you do not, I totally understand. But here is my app to my old NFW character from way back when. Some things you might remember about her, and some things are new, but it should be a pretty good read. If you have any questions or comments you know how to reach me via email or MSN instant messenger.
I hope that I did this right because I couldn't find a join link on the NFW webpage, so I just posted all info on these boards.
MEMBER INFO ---------------------- MEMBER Name: [name withheld] Email Address: [detail withheld] WRESTLER INFO ------------------- Wrestler Name: "The Fem Phenom" Nakita DuBov Sex: Female Height: 6'4 Weight: 195 lbs. Birthplace: St. Petersburg, Russia Hometown/Hailing from: Pheonix, Arizona Handedness: Ambidextrious (Right and Left handed) Disposition: Face Persona/Gimmick: How should I put this, a gimmick cross between Chyna meets Bill Goldberg (The Female Goldberg).
Ring Attire: Nakita usually wears; black with white trim Umbro shorts, a black speedo sports bra, black elbow pads, black knee pads, black padded gloves with white tape wrapped around her wrists, and black wrestling boots.
Looks: Nakita is a very beautiful but incredibly buffed female. She is muscularly fit and her physique is refined and chisled for a woman. She has white skin, long fiery red hair (usually put up in a ponytail during matches), and beautiful green eyes. For a body builder her size, her bust is not large but far from small, but they are very real. She has a remarkable almost borderline "supermodel" like beauty in her face. This beauty almost makes her opponants perceive her as an easy task, but there is more than meets the eye with this "fem phenomenon".
Ring Entrance: 1) (Music que up: "Me, Myself, and My Microphone" by Living Color and Run D.M.C as Kevon LeBrock steps out and walks toward the ring. He is wearing a half buttoned bright multi-colored silk shirt, black baggy Dickey's slacks, Lugz boots, a big gold "bling bling" necklace around his neck, and his dreadlocks are each individually braided. He slided into the ring and takes the mic.)
Kevon LeBrock: "I've got this one so just step off my kool-aid yo. Ladiez and gentlemen, boyz and girlz, children, pissants, scrubnuts, antz, and everyone else in between. Comin at chu like gangbustas. Weighin in at approx. 190 lbs. She currently hails from Pheonix, Arizona. Now ready to run wild on you Hogan style. She is da incarnation of da nizzel fo shizzel. She is the Fem Phenomenon better known as NAKITA DUBOV!...
...oh and before she comes out, people in the audience who have pacemakers or severe heart trouble should be adviced to leave the arena now. You have been warned."
2) Music que up: "Won't Back Down" by Fuel... 3) The main arena lights are replaced with red and white really fast trippy strobelights that look like a rave concert on lots of ectasy.
4) The Videotron begins a montage of clips and highlights of Nakita DuBov executing numerous spears and stiff moves on several unknown oponants that flow in sync with the song. In the middle intermixing words like "Fem", "Phenom", "Nakita" , "DuBov".
5) Finally, after a few moments, "The Fem Phenom" Nakita DuBov comes out to a nice crowd pop. She stops on the stage and then is engulfed and briefly disappears in a colorful, showering fountain of pyrotechnics. When the pyro disapates, she comes out shadowboxing.
6) She walks down the aisle ignoring the fans and engulfed in her own personally trance like mental state as she gets closer and closer to the ring. She arrives at the ringside and leaps up onto the ring apron where a single burst of pyro launches out from each one of the four turnbuckles. Then Nakita jumps over the ropes and on her feet inside the ring as she begins to bounce around on her feet as she awaits for the bell to ring and start the match. The music fades out and Kevon LeBrock steps out to cheer for Nakita at ringside. Nakita paces around like a caged animal with intensity in her green eyes.
Theme Music: "Won't Back Down" by Fuel Tactics/Style: A blend of stiff striking moves, and power technical moves, but sometimes can go aerial if the situation called for it, but she will not take the risks if it doesn't.
She doesn't attack with slow setting up power moves because she knows that her limitations as a woman plays a part in matches so she doesn't want to give any time for her male oponants to gues what she will do next. She wants to keep them guessing and stay one step ahead of them. Most of the men she fights will be larger than she is so she doesn't want them to counter her moves. Nakita will always try for the more stiff and reality of moves. The more THEY feel her, the better she does. She wants to make certain that they KNOW exactly whom they are fighting. She will be fast and try not to give up or quit.
The styles of Goldberg, meets the intensity of Chris Benoit. 5 Specialty Moves: 1) Spears 2) Dropkicks 3) DDTs 4) Neckbreakers 5) Clotheslines Trademark move: "The Cutting Edge" An Undertaker style tight walk on the ropes into a diamondcutter. Set up move: "The Gillotine Stunner" With her oponant's head draped on the ropes, Nakita springs up onto the top turnbuckle, then leaps off toward the guy. While in mid air, she grabs the guy's head and pulls it down with her thus snapping the guy back into the ring while she lands on the outside. Then Nakita rolls back in as quickly as possible ready to execute her finisher.
Finisher: "The Pheonix Downer" Named after Nakita's hometown. Nakita takes ahold of her oponant's head, runs toward the nearest turnbuckle, runs up it like stairs, then leaps off the top almost like a modified moonsault while still holding the head until she lands on her feet behind the guy and ends up holding the guy's head in a standing dragon sleeper where she proceeds to hold the guy until they submit. If they try to reach for the ropes, then she drags them back toward the center and applies more pressure until they have no choice but to tap.
Strengths: 1) Very strong, agile, fast, and enduring for a woman. 2) Determined and resilent to never quit. 3) Good ring presence and a sound mind. Weaknesses: 1) Very stubborn and prideful. She hates asking for help. She always feels like that she must prove herself to every man. She can not say "no" to a challenge when it comes her way. Her pride will not let her quit or back down even when she is outmatched. She has the attitude of "I'm good, and I know it (and I will prove it by any means neccessary.)"
2) She is very stiff and has no regard for other people's saftey or well being...let alone her own if she has to put it on the line. Her philosophy is she treats all of the men then same way they are treating her because they will show her no mercy.
3) She forgets about her limitations and the simple fact that she is a woman, and also tends to take things a little too emotionally like a woman tends to do.
-------------------------------------------------- Manager information: Name: Kevon Edward LeBrock Sex: Male Height: 5'6 Weight: 180 lbs. Description: Kevon is a short and skinny African-American man. He has long braided dreadlocks, brown eyes, dark skin. He is short and slender kind of like with a Rey Mysterio type of build. He usually wears; silk shirts, black baggy Dicky slacks, a gold "bling bling" old school neckless, and Lugz boots.
Kevon is the voice and the charisma for Nakita DuBov so she doesn't have to be. She can speak but why would need to when you have Kevon. Kevon coaches Nakita at ringside, argues or points out bad calls to the ref, gets the crowd going in Nakita's favor, etc. Kevon can even do some fighting off on people who might try and run down on Nakita who might interfere against them. Kevon knows how to fight if he has to even though he will think of other resourceful techniques not to. Only when it is a last resort. Mainly Kevon has a mouth and he knows how to use it.
