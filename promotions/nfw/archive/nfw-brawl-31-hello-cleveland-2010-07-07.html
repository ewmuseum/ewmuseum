NFW Brawl 31: HELLO CLEVELAND!, 7 July 2010

7 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 31: HELLO CLEVELAND! 7 Jul 2010 Quicken Loans Arena, Cleveland, Ohio (seats 21,200) Welcome to the Show! V/O: "The following presentation is brought to you by Sirius/XM Satellite Radio and ESEN." (MUSICUP: "YEARNIN'" -- The Black Keys) RICE: (V/O) "WOOOOOOOOOO! HELLO CLEVELAND and HELLO PLANET EARTH!" (FADEIN: NATRONE RICE sitting outside the Quicken Loans Arena in the mobile NFW BRAWL studio booth, fans and passerbys waving into the ESEN broadcast cameras as the Sirius/XM simulcast crackles to life!) RICE: "I know the music may not be what the city wants to hear tonight, but you can feel it in the air tonight as KING JAMES, LEBRON...looks to be heading down to the South Beach nightlife of Miami, Florida. New Frontier Wrestling being the kind souls they are...well, we put on three hours of music, entertainment and wrestling action to hopefully make them forget the end of this year's NBA playoffs and perhaps the last time an athlete this special will grace their arena floor night after night!" (CUTTO: EDDIE MAYFIELD on the FRONTIERtron, holding up a customized #23 JAMES Miami Heat jersey...) RICE: "Unfortunately, our President doesn't have much of a soul and wanted to be the first to welcome the King to his home state of Florida, along with a top 10 list of reasons of why Cleveland Sucks! (RICE shakes his head disappointingly) Our President notwithstanding, we did have some AWESOME action tonight and we had a couple individuals that were even BIGGER disgraces if possible. The main event was between Lord Coyner Pollard and Dan Ryan...a great match in the middle of the show was newcomer Jack Bryant taking on the controversial and hopefully cleaned up Brock Alyas. Underneath those layers was the decision of President Mayfield to make our own World Champion, Joe the Plumber...wrestle against Rich Mahogany in a PURE Championship contender's match, which goes against everything every fan believes about this company. Joe the Plumber recently blinded by Dorchester Stratton...was obviously set up to fail miserably, but what the President didn't count on was the brutality and carnage that's been growing on NFW shows regardless of drug policies and stricter rules." Rich Mahogany vs. Joe the Plumber (CUTTO: RICH MAHOGANY making his customary entrance. ...men turning a few shades of green in the background, while some of the local snaggletoothed Clevelendian older ladies root MAHOGANY's hip-gyrating routine further... CUTTO: NATRONE RICE looking at a monitor with a disgusted, yet bemused look...) RICE: "It's like a car accident, you want to look away but you just can't. And as funny as it is to say this fans...this thing started INNOCENTLY enough, Rich doing whatever it is he does and alot of fans WAITING and WONDERING what shape Joe the Plumber would be in..." (CUTTO: JOE walking down the aisle, eyes bandaged and being handled by NFW security. He's fine until one of the TP streamers hits him and then he starts screaming, frothing going the whole nine yards on security...) RICE: "Joe's a handle to even get IN the ring let alone the match getting started, but it was NEVER meant to be." (CUTTO: MALIK ANDERSON running out, pushing away security and JTP turns right into a SPINEBUSTER onto the bottom of the metal entrance gantry to the cement!) RICE: "Malik Anderson is on town, his sights triggered on a blind man." (CUTTO: The crowd starts freaking out as in the background, DORCHESTER STRATTON is on the FRONTIERtron... ANDERSON stomps on JTP repeatedly as the bell starts ringing! RICH runs down and does he save JOE? OF COURSE NOT. He joins in on the stomping, while the crowd boos even more...if possible.) RICE: "So, you're watching the video highlight...and for those of you on Sirius, let's just say that Rich Mahogany decided to join in this pre-emptive beatdown of Joe since he knew the jig was up...only he didn't know up it was and paid dearly for it." (DORCHESTER's screaming "PICK HIM UP!" Which MAHOGANY is apparently oblivious to as he's still kicking JTP and that's when MALIK STEINERLINES HIM! Everyone marks as MAHOGANY goes down like he was shot...DORCHESTER appreciates the pause and starts speaking..." RICE: "First, Malik set him down hard with a clothesline...then CASTOR STRIFE ran out! They got a match scheduled later this week and STRIFE runs out and quickly piledrives him on the edge of the metal entrance ramp!" (CUTTO: STRIFE skulking away quickly because of security, while MALIK is holding up a woozy JOE on this feet...DORCHESTER starts berating him and the FRONTIERtron blips out. MALIK lifts up JOE and drives him down with another Spinebuster onto the ramp!) RICE: "Strife's driven off by security, Dorchester calls out JTP from the safety of his own luxury box suite and Malik junkyard dogs him a second time with a spinebuster. Security chase him off and that's what we call in the business...a NO CONTEST." BLAST OF STATIC! (CUTTO: JACK BRYANT and BROCK ALYAS circling each other around the ring...) RICE: "When we come back, it's Jack Bryant and Brock Alyas...with a little playcallin' from our good friends Lamont Hollywood and Kerry O'Connor. (pause) Ok, I don't think the Woodman would agree with that. We've got him on tape, so at least it don't matter! HAH!" Brock Alyas vs. Jack Bryant O’CONNOR: Welcome to BRAWL! Hosted by the Quicken Loans Arena here in Cleveland, Ohio! We’ve got a battle of newcomers… of sorts. BROCK ALYAS plans to finish the rehabilitation of his life by finally recording his first win since 2008. H’WOOD: Pfft. There’s not a chance. This guys a washed up bum, beanfry! O’CONNOR: There’s a lot of speculation around Brock since he came to the NFW, at first his 8-0 record coming in, then the speculation that he was under the influence of drugs on all cards, the use of steroids are a certain speculation as well as his on-camera antics of breaking company cameras and smoking crack on a handheld, that found it’s way into ESEN. All to say, Brock’s been far from a stranger of trouble, but from what we’ve seen lately he’s a man changed… at least attempting to. H’WOOD: Ya right! Brock Alyas eight victories in the NFW are about as legit as the University of Southern California’s 2004 National Championship and Heisman winner. O’CONNOR: Let’s check out the highlights! (CUTTO: JACK BRYANT pummeling BROCK in the corner, overhand rights and elbows that all land sharp. BRYANT whips him to the ground with a power fireman’s carry takedown and proceeds to stomp the lights of BROCK out. BROCK is motionless for a few seconds as BRYANT gets to bask in the glory of the upper hand at this point in the match.) H’WOOD: You know, at one point BROCK would’ve been the aggressor… the bigger man. But now after his stint in rehab, he’s looking like a boy among men the past few weeks around here. O’CONNOR: There’s no question BROCK lost a lot of his weight through his time off. He certainly looks a lot better than he did in Albuquerque, so for his sake let’s hope he continues to improve his physique. H’WOOD: Ya right! Guys that depend on STEROIDS for their anger and aggression fall off the face of the earth. Look at Barry Bonds bean! (BRYANT was seemingly cruising, however began to get a bit cocky and take his time. BRYANT has BROCK by the back of the head about to send him over to go face first into the nearest turnbuckle when BROCK explodes with an elbow to the gut, to the fans delight and used the opposing ropes to catapult himself into a clothesline from hell that caught BRYANT across the throat.) O’CONNOR: There’s some power from BROCK we’ve yet to see for over a year! (BRYANT laid on his back, covering up his throat in hopes to open an oxygen gap to enhale a fresh breath. BROCK had other plans, stomping him back flat and then proceeded to send boots directly into that same throat. BROCK, wasting no time picked his opponent up and sent him into the opposing ropes to come back into a delayed sidewalk slam that got a nice pop from the local Cleveland fans.) (BROCK was in control of the match, but certainly not in control of the lingering symptoms of withdrawal. He would be leading the match and while JACK BRYANT would be attempting to get up, BROCK would bend over and wince in pain using both hands to cover his head. O’CONNOR: Did BRYANT happen to sneak in a blow with a foreign object? ALYAS looks to be rattled despite being in control of this match! H’WOOD: The smack will do it to ya! (BROCK dropped to a knee while waiting for BRYANT to get back to his feet and BRYANT used the opportunity to his advantage, lifting BROCK up and over for a back body suplex followed by a snap suplex the minute BROCK stepped to his feet.) O’CONNOR: You know at one point it would’ve been tough for ANY NFW employee to pick BROCK up for even a suplex! H’WOOD: That was until he lost 40 pounds and 90% of his strength! O’CONNOR: In any event, this is a man who will obviously need to find a different edge or advantage in the ring. He simply can’t rely on his ability to rag doll opponents in the ring. (BROCK was dozed trying to get up and at this point JACK BRYANT was making a fool of him. Brock looked drunk in his attempts to stumble to his feet and throw haymakers hoping to take the edge. He would simultaneously squeeze his head in pain as well throughout the match. BRYANT took his time recovering himself, and would send BROCK back to the mats with a missile drop kick to the shins or a swinging neck breaker. The fans weren’t cheering against BRYANT but it sure sounded like they wanted BROCK to get back up and fight!) O’CONNOR: Well this is something new! The fans actually cheering Brock’s name! H’WOOD: I swear we host some of the BIGGEST IDIOTS at these Brawl shows. Our ticket sales need an exclusivity reform so these morons don’t show up. (BRYANT was now using the crowd’s edge against BROCK, daring him to get up. BRYANT had BROCK in a headlock at one point where it looked to be as if the match was over, after pounding away and chipping him down at the knees for an extended period of time. The crowd however, remained persistent and seemingly sparked some life into BROCK!) O’CONNOR: OH MAN! THAT’S THE BROCK ALYAS WE’RE USED TO SEEING! (BROCK turned the head lock into a German Suplex variation that left JACK BRYANT straight on his neck! The crowd let out a huge pop as BROCK slowly tried to scramble to his feet. BRYANT was bracing the effects of the suplex.) H’WOOD: THAT GERMAN BOUGHT BROCK SOME TIME TO SHAKE THE CRACK-WEBS OUT OF HIS HEAD! O’CONNOR: A HUGE German from BROCK puts him right back in this thing. It looks like it’ll be a race to the feet as both men look winded in this tiresome battle! Alyas decides to scramble over and cover the Birmingham Stallion! ONE! TWO! THRRRRNO! Bryant with a kickout! Brock slapping his hand on the mat repeatedly, I think he thinks he got that one! H'WOOD: Maybe he's just trying to get the feelings back in his fingers, lord knows how many times he lit them on fire trying to smoke out of a crackpipe... O'CONNOR: Brock's up...laboring, but still pushing as Bryant's nearly dead weight as he brings him up...hooks him in a chancery and (stomp!) SNAP SUPLEX! (crowd pop!) Brock's keeping it hooked, he's bringing up Bryant for another! (stomp!) ANOTHER SNAP SUPLEX! Brock STILL has it hooked and he's rolling himself up with Bryant...(stomp!) ...SN--wait! HOLD ON! Brock went for a third snap suplex, but didn't get Bryant off the ground and has let go of the hold... H'WOOD: I knew he couldn't get through a whole match without snorting something! O'CONNOR: Brock's either holding his hip or back, I can't tell as he's collapsed against...and is now leaning against the ropes...Bryant's in a daze on his knee, I don't think he's got a clue to what's just happened either! Brock's walking gingerly towards Bryant, LOOK OUT! (groans!) Bryant with a lunging headbutt to the solarplexus of Alyas! Alyas doubled over, Bryant with a Birmingham uppercut! He reels Brock in...OH NO! (LOUD GROANS!) URRRRRRRRRRANAHGEEEEEEE! INTO A BACKBREAKER! SWEET MARY OF ALABAMA, BRYANT'S HIT THE BIRMINGHAM BREAKER! HE'S GOT BROCK ROLLED UP TIGHT! ONNNNNNNE! TWOOOOOOOOOO! THREEEEEEEEEE! (Bell ringing! Mixed reaction from the crowd...) Jack Bryant just pulled off the victory after it looks like Brock Alyas suffered an injury at the tailend of this match! H'WOOD: I know you don't want me to be the one to say it, Beansprouts...but Brock Alyas just came to a fully disclosed realization that his body still isn't right without the roids! Those muscles may NEVER be the same... (CUTTO: ALYAS writhing in agony on the mat, while the ref checks on him... CUTTO: BRYANT rolling out of the ring, raising his arm in victory...) O'CONNOR: For one of the few times in my life, I think I agree with you Woodman! BLAST OF STATIC! (CUTTO: NATRONE RICE shaking his head...) RICE: "That's going to be tough for Brock Alyas to swallow, but you got the sense that he's MUCH closer to his A-game, which he's really going to need these next couple of weeks! He's getting some opportunities that may be a last chance for him to take advantage of in the New Frontier. When we come back, we'll see a man that's no stranger to making his own opportunity in DAN RYAN taking on a man that's no stranger to getting opportunity handed to him in Lord Coyner Pollard...and let me tell you, someone ain't going to be happy at the hotel tonight. Woodman and O'Connor on the call again with a special Brawl from HELLLLLLLLLLLLO CLEVELAND!" Dan Ryan vs. Lord Coyner Pollard (CUT TO: Back the outdoor studio setup with our host, NATRONE RICE!) RICE: Welcome back to Sirius/XM and E-S-E-N! We've got one more hour for phone calls on Sirius/XM only along with a few of my opinions on the state of the New Frontier, but first...When Dan Ryan put out an open challenge, nobody thought the resident Lord of NFW, Lord Coyner Pollard, would be the one to accept! Then, we found out that Eddie Mayfield, OUR ESTEEMED PRESIDENT no less...decided THIS match of all matches was going to be fought under Diplomatic Immunity rules. And if you're not a stranger to the NEW FRONTIER, you know as the Lord’s butler-slash-assistant explained during the opening entrances...these rules are just a LITTLE different than what Dan Ryan's used to. We join with Jeeves explaining some of the… finer details. (CUT TO: The hard-camera, with Dan Ryan slouched in a corner as Jeeves is reading off a parchment-scroll) JEEVES: …t’wit, there shall be no eye gouging, nor shall there be any intentional blows delivered to the groinal area. Rule number 35, on penalty of a fine not exceeding five thousand pounds, Daniel Ryan shall not engage in such activity as might bring shame to New Frontier Wrestling, such activities being described in appendix 7-A of this document. Rule number 36 (We don’t hear rule 36, as Dan Ryan grabs Jeeves by the shoulder, spinning him round and LEVELLING him with a vicious lariat that causes the ground to explode and Jeeves to do a 360 before hitting the mat) O’CONNOR: Dan’s had enough of waiting, takes Jeeves out and is hammering away at Pollard with right hands! Irish whip off the ropes, BIG back drop! Ryan in a mounted position, more right hands. H’WOOD: And ignoring the referee. O’CONNOR: The referee pulling Ryan back – why? H’WOOD: Rule 48 – no closed fists. O’CONNOR: Oh for the love of. Pollard shaking his head after those right hands, he’s telling the referee to… turn Ryan around? H’WOOD: I think that’s rule 70-something. Pollard can have Dan turn his back to restart the match. O’CONNOR: Dan fuming after the long wait, turns around – and Pollard takes out Ryan’s knee with a chop block! H’WOOD: Shows why he’s one of the smartest men in wrestling. He knows he can’t go toe-to-toe with Ryan so he’ll take Ryan’s knee out. O’CONNOR: Dan Ryan on the mat, Pollard held onto the leg when he took it out and is stomping on the knee. Knee’s to the back of the leg and a standing toe-hold. H’WOOD: Why do these people not like Pollard? He’s taking it to Dan Ryan to save them! O’CONNOR: Maybe because he’s an arrogant ass who hates America? H’WOOD: He doesn’t hate America. He hates Americans, big difference. O’CONNOR: Pollard still focusing on the knee, going for a figure four – but Ryan kicks him off! It’s way too early, what was the Lord thinking? H’WOOD: Making you? I have no idea. O’CONNOR: Pollard off the ropes, catches Ryan before the big man can get up to his feet. Brilliant knee to the head and straight back to working on the leg. RICE: (as v/o while the screen shifts to later in the match) Pollard’s advantage wouldn’t last. He tried shifting from the knee to working the neck, and Dan took the chance to get back to his feet and use his strength, shoving driving the Brit back into the corner before hitting a HUGE suplex for a near fall. Let’s pick up the action. O’CONNOR: Ryan in full control, he’s got Pollard back to his feet, hooks the head – what the?! H’WOOD: Looks like Pollard called a time out. O’CONNOR: This is ridiculous! How’s Dan supposed to wrestle with this going on? H’WOOD: I think that may be the point. Pollard has Dan turn around again and back to the knee, smart strategy. O’CONNOR: It’s hardly fair he gets to do all this and Dan Ryan can do nothing to stop him! H’WOOD: That’s life. Pollard down to the canvas with a leg-lock, kicks Ryan in the face as he tried to break the hold. O’CONNOR: The fans are really starting to hate this – listen to this boring chant. H’WOOD: They just don’t appreciate the skills of a proper wrestler. He’s been trained by the best at what he does. O’CONNOR: Ryan in the centre of the ring, Pollard releases the hold but a kick to the side of the head. He finally brings Ryan back to his feet, right hand – and the ref’s doing nothing! H’WOOD: Must be in the appendix. Pollard fists good, Ryan fists bad. And too big. O’CONNOR: Sends Ryan off the ropes, hip toss blocked! Ryan just smirking at Pollard – thumb to the eye! What a jerk! H’WOOD: A hard chop from Pollard, driving Ryan back into the corner. A knee to the midsection, Irish whip. O’CONNOR: Reversed into a powerslam!! One! TWO! Pollard kicks out, and Ryan’s got him by the head. Back to his feet, Irish whip and that’s a hip toss. H’WOOD: But Jeeves is calling for a time out now! O’CONNOR: This isn’t even ridiculous any more. That’s the third, fourth time they’ve called a time out! H’WOOD: And I guess we know how this one’s going to turn out. O’CONNOR: Ryan turns around, Pollard NO! Pollard went for the knee but Dan with a kick to the head! He outsmarted the Lord! H’WOOD: Even I’ve got to admit, that was smart. O’CONNOR: Pollard rolls to the corner, Ryan in – BIG splash! Forearms to the head, brings Pollard from the corner – overhead suplex!! Spots Jeeves on the apron, charges at him but Jeeves to the floor again. Pollard’s holding his back in the corner, Ryan stalks him – low blow! H’WOOD: It was a kick to the gut. O’CONNOR: You think a kick to the gut’ll floor Dan Ryan like that?! H’WOOD: It could do. O’CONNOR: It doesn’t. Pollard with a swinging neck breaker, into the cover. One Two and kickout, the referee couldn’t do anything after that low blow. This diplomatic immunity is ridiculous. Pollard follows it up with a fist drop. RICE: (as v/o) It stayed that way for several minutes – every time Dan Ryan tried to build some offence, Pollard would call a time-out or cheat and when he couldn’t, Jeeves would butt in! A combination of those breaks and Pollard’s technical skill saw him target the left knee and the ribs, slowly wearing Dan down – especially a double knee drop from the middle rope after one time out that led to Dan having to lie down with his ribs exposed. Eventually Pollard locked in an abdominal stretch, but would it be enough to beat the legend? O’CONNOR: The fans are trying to get behind Dan, I don’t know whether they like him or just hate seeing Pollard get away with this. H’WOOD: If it’s in the rules then it’s not ‘getting away with’ anything. O’CONNOR: Have you read the rules of this match?! H’WOOD: Me? Nah, I’ve got a copy holding my home office door open. O’CONNOR: Pollard grabbing the ropes to assist in this hold and there’s nothing the referee can do about it and to make things worse for Dan, Pollard’s close to kneeling on the side of the left knee. H’WOOD: He’s smart, why just target one body part? I think we’ve got a future PURE champion in the ring. O’CONNOR: The referee asking Ryan if he wants to give up, Pollard has come very close to picking up a huge win over a legendary American tonight but Ryan’s got something left in the tank. H’WOOD: He can’t have much. O’CONNOR: Never count him out. Pollard wrenching the arm, Ryan in a lot of pain. H’WOOD: And Pollard with an elbow to the ribs. O’CONNOR: Finally releases the hold. Lines up – kick to the head misses! No goal! Pollard turns around, Ryan with a shot to the gut, he’s holding the knee. Scoop – BIG sidewalk slam! One!! Two!! Kickout by Pollard! Ryan back to his feet, stalking him – looking for a spear, Pollard’s up, Ryan charges H’WOOD: Time out! O’CONNOR: Of for the love of! Pollard calls ANOTHER time out! Dan Ryan’s furious! H’WOOD: ‘Roid rage. O’CONNOR: Pollard’s telling Ryan to turn around. The referee’s trying to get Dan to turn around for Pollard, Ryan’s trying not to smash the ref’s teeth down his throat. H’WOOD: He’s just been doing his job. O’CONNOR: Dan Ryan turns around – NO! Dan Ryan with a straight right knocks Pollard down! Dan Ryan has had enough of the Lord’s games! H’WOOD: Blasphemy! O’CONNOR: Dan Ryan in the mount, hammering away with lefts and rights, Jeeves is shouting for a time out! The ref’s trying to get Ryan off Pollard, manages it. Pollard struggling to get up, holding the ropes, calls for a time out but Ryan with a big clothesline over the top! Pollard landed hard! H’WOOD: He’s cheating! O’CONNOR: Pollard, yes. Ryan’s just evening the score. Follows Pollard to the outside, Irish whip into the corner. Jeeves runs over, he’s… not serious? H’WOOD: He’s telling Dan to put up his dukes! What a butler! O'CONNOR: This can't end well! Pollard's up and watch out! (boos!) He's jumped Ryan from behind! Jeeves is peppering him with rights and lefts to the gut! Wait, look out! (roars!) Ryan backs up and slams Pollard against the ringpost! Jeeves charges in! (LOUD ROARS!) BIG BOOT TO THE FACE! JEEVES IS DOWN! Ryan stalking out and grabs Jeeves, lifting him up and OHNOOOOOOO! (CROWD EXPLOSION!) CHOKESLAM ON THE CEMENT! This place is going wild! H'WOOD: Well, Ryan's going to be footing Pollard's insurance bill on Jeeves if he keeps at it! (CUTTO: POLLARD grabs a chair and cautiously sneaks up behind RYAN...raising the chair over his head, but RYAN turns around in the nick of time!) O'CONNOR: (over loud roars!) Boot to the by Ryan! He's reeling Pollard into a standing headscissors! (LOUD SCREAMS!) HE'S GOT HIM UHHHHHHHHHHP! (CRASH! CROWD ROARS!) HUMILITY BOMB THROUGH A RINGSIDE TABLE! OHMYGAHHHHHHHHHHHHD! (SFX: BELL RINGING!) (CUTTO: The ref signaling for the bell, shouting out something to Lee-Baby Sims...) H'WOOD: What just happened? O'CONNOR: I...I don't know...Lord Coyner Pollard just got DECIMATED by the Ego Buster in a move reminiscent of the time when Dan Ryan nearly broke Joey Melton's back! SIMS V/O: This match has ended due to a DIPLOMATIC IMMUNITY INFRACTION, therefore the winner by disqualification...LORD! (LOUD BOOS!) COYNERRRRRRRRRR POLLARRRRRRRRRRRRRD! O'CONNOR: I don't believe this! Dan Ryan doesn't believe this! He's trying to get at Pollard, but security has rushed the ringside area to hold him back! H'WOOD: Well, he should've waited to hear ALL of the rules by Jeeves... O'CONNOR: Oh come on, Woodman! You know this is a hosejob perpetrated by the Executive Office in an effort to curb Dan Ryan away from the PURE and NFW World Heavyweight Championships! BLAST OF STATIC! (CUTTO: NATRONE RICE bug-eyed a little bit, whistling in slight shock as he stares at the camera...) RICE: "You'd think after their first run together that New Frontier Wrestling and specifically Eddie Mayfield wouldn't want to stoke a certain fire that rages inside DAN EFFIN' RYAN. ...after tonight, I'd be surprised if he's not stomping everything to dust, while burning the whole thing down. NEXT STOP: MIAMI! ...and when we come back, we've got SIXTY minutes to talk all about that...or anything else tonight. This is NATRONE RICE and this is NFW BRAWL! E-S-E-N people, we've got a little NEXT LEVEL for ya, PEACE!"
