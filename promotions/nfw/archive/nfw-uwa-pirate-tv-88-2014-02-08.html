NFW — Card

UWA PIRATE TV 88, 8 February 2014

8 February 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUE UP: The static voice of CHECKCHECKCHECK followed by the distorted voice of Kim Deal saying “OOOOOHHHH OHHHHH” in the intro of “CANNONBALL” by 90s alt-rockers The Breeders. There’s a pause as Philadelphia’s CITY HALL appears in all white, just a black background. The rollicking bass line comes in as we see other Philly attractions -- the Rocky Steps of the Art Museum, the day-glo cheesesteak meccas of Pat’s and Geno’s. Then just as we hear Kelly sing/whisper “I’m the last splash” we see the Liberty Bell. The screaming chorus -- “WANT YOU! COO COO! CANNONBALL!” cues up and all of a sudden the Liberty Bell has the Jolly Rodger logo imprinted over it! The camera then pans out underneath the PERISCOPE and FADES slow 360 to the black and white-themed UWA PIRATE TV set, with a wooden-looking ramp leading down to the ring, and the backstage curtain is formed into that huge black ‘Jolly Liberty Bell’ pirate logo, and the broadside of a pirate ship! "UWA PIRATE TV’ reads across the side of the gantry, and off the sides of the boat, 4 cannon holes level cannons upwards, and BOOM OUT WITH EARSPLITTING STACCATO CANNON SHOTS! The crowd goes crazy as smoke fills the platform!) (CUT TO: Halfway down “The Plank” runway is “Troubleshooter” Craig Murray -- a shaved-head African-American man with a go-tee who looks more-than-just-vaguely like New York Knicks coach Mike Woodson, wearing a crisp $500-ish black suit as he looks around, nodding up and down.) MURRAY: “Ladies and gentlemen… WELCOME! TO! PIRATE! TV!” (The crowd -- a set of metal bleachers around the ring fitting maybe 300-350 fans or so -- all stomp up and down and continue to hoot and holler as ANOTHER cannon shot explosion goes off! Murray, not expecting it, buckles his knees and holds his ears. He opens his eyes wide and laughs before shaking it off.) MURRAY: “Yes, ladies and gentlemen, this is the first OFFICIAL edition of UWA Pirate TV! Now, all you die-hard wrestling fans have watched the grainy YouTube footage of what’s gone on here… and, once they got block, maybe you even ended up getting your e-mail hacked because you had to watch from some Russian hack website. Well, initially, the NFW brass wanted to keep this down here on the DL! But so many people heard so many good things about the young kids we have down here that we decided to get this OUT THERE TO THE MASSES! And here we are!” (All of a sudden, we hear a screeching steamwhistle, followed by the noise of a runaway train! The train whistle then morphs into ‘Hit Em Up” by 2-Pac! The PERISCOPE then shows a young Railroad in the ring STEAMROLLING a dude with a running lariat, followed by another. But the live ‘RAILROAD REESE’ comes walking out. He’s a dark-skinned African-American with a closely cropped haircut but he still has his trademarked braided ponytail. Reese is wearing a conductor’s hat over a white shirt, buttoned just to his chest, with a blazer over it. He walks two steps down the ramp before points to all points of the arena and lets out one of his trademark WOOOOOO train effect noises as he pantomimes pulling down a train whistle! He then sees Murray and points at him before walking the plank, stepping between the ropes and giving his partner a dap fist-bump followed by a hug. The music stops as there’s a serenade of a few more train WOOOOOOS. Reese laughs.) REESE: “Haha, it’s good to know that I’m not too forgotten’ in these parts!” (WOOO WOOOOO!) “Thanks for that intro, my man. I’m used to being in a wrestling ring like this but I’m not all that used to wearing something from the big’n’tall shop in the ring. But that’s alright. I’m not here to throw fists. I’m here to sit down there at ringside and tell everyone what exactly it is I’m seein’ in that ring. We got some young bulls in the back who I know want to get up to that roster at some point. And Eddie Mayfield and company -- you know they’re watching REAL close, especially tonight!” UNKNOWN VOICE: “Ohhhh ho ho. DO I BEG TO DISAGREE!” (CUE UP: “The Boy With The Arab Strap” by Belle and Sebastian! The crowd boos as AUGUST DE LA ROSSI emerges to the top of the plank, sitting on his fixed gear bicycle! He has on his Tour De France bicycling cap, his multi-colored dreads peeking through, a T-Shirt that reads OCCUPY URBAN OUTFITTERS, and a grey messenger bag draped over his shoulder.) AUGUST: “Allow me the…” (August sneers.) “HONOR of an introduction, if by some chance you don’t know who I am. My name is AUGUST DE LA ROSSI! I am a truth-seeker. I am a truth-teller. I am the only wrestler alive who has ever had a column published in Adbusters or has been a featured guest on the Democracy Underground podcast! I was listening to The Front Bottoms --” (Reese: “Who?”) “before YOU even know who MGMT was! And I… I AM YOUR UWA CHAMPION!” (August sits on the bike and lifts open the messenger bag to take out the UWA Championship!) AUGUST: “I’ve had this belt now for five months and have defended it against anyone and everyone who wants a shot! This title here is mine-oh-mine! And while having this title SHOULD guarantee me a roster spot in New Frontier Wrestling, Eddie Mayfield has chosen to IGNORE ME because he knows that I will expose him for the corporatist capitalist oppressor that he is! HE KNOWS THAT I WILL EXPOSE HIM AND WILL FURTHER EXPOSE THE MONOPOLY BIG SODA CONGLOMERATES ARE TRYING TO PLACE UPON THE DRINKING WATER OF INDIGENOUS PEOPLES EVERYWHERE!” REESE: “Yo, champ…” (Reese is shaking his head.) “I’ve got one question for you, my man.” AUGUST: “WHAT?” REESE: “What in god’s name are you talking about?” (The crowd starts laughing it up as August puffs outs his cheeks and starts thrashing the front wheel of his bike around wildly!) AUGUST: “Look, I am an EDUCATED man. And even though I have denounced my father and the large international bank he is a senior vice-president at… I still am PROUD that he sent me to the finest in PRIVATE education facilities so I wasn’t educated in the AmeriKKKan brainwashing academies you call public school!” (The crowd boos some more as August points around.) MURRAY: “Okay, August. You’ve had the pulpit long enough. Are you out here for any other reason than to talk about indie rock and conspiracy theories?” (August rolls his eyes.) AUGUST: “Yes. I am, CRAIGGERS. Like I said -- I’ve had this title now for FIVE MONTHS. Five months, I have cast any and all interested parties by the wayside. And now I don’t see ANY contenders to this title. I may as well just RETIRE this title… but I won’t because this title FORCES YOU TO LISTEN WHEN I SPREAK THE TRUTHS YOU DO NOT WANT TO HEAR! And no one is good enough to shut me up—“ (CUE UP: “Started From The Bottom” by Drake! CUT TO: The on-camera side of the audience is all standing up at once nodding to the music. A blast of DRY ICE fills the top of the ramp where August is standing as he coughs and wipes the air from his face and The smoke clears and standing right next to him is MOSSBERG! Mossberg -- 6’6”, African American, with a blonde mohawak, wearing red pleather short-trunks that say HOSS in gold leathering on both the front and the back. He just gasfaces August, looking at him and laughs as he walks down past Murray and Reese -- dapping him on the way -- before getting into the ring. He starts “tossing elbows” as the crowd follows. Finally the music stops.) CROWD: “MOOSSSSSBERRRGGGGGG MOSSSSSSBERRGGGGG” MOSSBERG: “Yo, August my man, no one is good enough to shut YOU up and to take that title? Well as we might say here in THE PIRATE SHIP…” (The crowd EXPLODES with that!) “Yo ho ho… I may just disagree with that statement.” AUGUST: “Well YO HO HO… You may disagree with that statement but I’m going to be honest with you… I DON’T EVEN KNOW YOUR NAME!” (Mossberg raises his eyebrows and holds up the microphone.) CROWD: “MOSSSSSSBERRRRRG! MOOOSSSSSSBERRRRRG!” MOSSBERG: “Well THEY seem to know my name, fool.” (August shakes his head.) AUGUST: “Well, THEY know a lot of things. But, sadly, they were MISEDUCATED. So, Mister… ahem… MOSSBERG… why don’t you tell me exactly why it is you think YOU deserve a shot at this belt?” MOSSBERG: “Well, let me see. Oh yeah, I’m 9-and-0 in my career. That’s why. I know I’m knew to these parts and to professional wrestling but I know 9-and-0 in ANYTHING is real good. And I’ve been 9-and-0 a bunch of times. At St. Joe’s Prep right down the street? We were 14-0 my senior year when we won state with me at linebacker. And down at South Carolina? Heh. We went 9-and-0 a few times down there, too. I seemed to have made the TRANSITION to wrestling pretty good. So good, my man, that even though I’ve only got myself NINE matches under my belt, Eddie Mayfield gave me a shot to get my ass a PROMOTION. And as much as I like here in my hometown --“ (You know this pops.) “—I’d like to get to the BIG LEAGUES pretty quickly so I can buy my mom a single family with a nice green lawn. She’d like that. And I know that she’d also like to see me with some GOLD accessories when I wear my Men’s Warehouse when I take her to church Sunday morning, if you get my drift.” AUGUST: “Well, thanks for letting me know WHO you are. But guess what? I still don’t CARE.” “And I still don’t think you’re ANYWHERE near good enough to beat me for this title!” (August smirks as Mossberg chuckles and makes the “belt around the waist” gesture.) “In fact, I don’t think you’re anywhere near good enough to even get a SHOT to take this title belt from me!” MOSSBERG: “Well, how about I just come up there right now and TAKE that belt from you?” (The crowd cheers and once again starts a “MOSSSBERRRGGG” chant!) AUGUST: “No, Mossberg. That’s against THE RULES. Even though I am Professional Wrestling’s One True Revolutionary, we still have RULES for a reason! But let me offer you THIS. I’ve got a business acquaintance of mine who is looking to get a foot in the door in professional wrestling. Right here, right now. My business acquaintance gets a match against you. If you win, you get a shot at my title. But if my business acquaintance wins… then you relinquish any shot at EVER holding this belt! How’s that sound, pal?” MOSSBERG: “Hmmm, let me think on that -- yeah, okay, get whatever skinny jeans wearing goof you got walking around the back with you and get his ass down here.” (August starts pointing at Mossberg and belly laughing.) AUGUST: “Well, Mossberg. The deal is DONE. And I’m sending my business acquaintance down right now…” (There’s the sound of a car honking as August steps to the side as a lime green Van/truck hybrid comes driving down. On the side in rainbow colors it reads “KARMA KOREAN TOFU TACOS -- VEGAN, GLUTEN FREE!” Mossberg just shakes his head as this food truck pulls up to the ring.” AUGUST “ Ladies and gentlemen, allow me the honor of introducing to you to… the proprietors of KARMA KOREAN TOFU TACOS, the only vegan and gluten-free Korean taco truck in professional wrestling today! And, not just that, they are also professional wrestling’s latest tag team… THE ARTISANALS!” (Out from each side of the truck comes out a masked figure, each wearing day-glo green body suits with a belt made out of plastic tacos. They stand on the hood of the truck, holding onto the side view mirrors, and turn and salute August, who salutes back before making and holding up a fist.) MURRAY: “Well, I guess this is our DEBUT match here on Pirate TV! Mossberg, the young bigman from right here in Philadelphia, against… well… I guess we’ll call them Artisanal Numero Uno and Numero Dos…” REESE: (laughs) “I think we’re about to call one of them ‘DEAD’ and the other one ‘MEAT.’ Or, actually, thinking on that… I guess they don’t eat meat? But either way… my man Mossberg towers over these dudes.” MURRAY: “Referee Paul Devon slides into the ring and rings the bell as Artisanal Uno is in the ring and charges Mossberg and is peppering him with some fists to the stomach… but Mossberg grabs him and just TOSSES him right into the turnbuckle! And Mossberg charges with a big leaping splash! Now he points at Numero Dos who wants NO part of this! Mossberg just HEAVES Uno into THAT corner and charges with an elbow. And now he takes Uno’s hand and slaps it across Dos’s shoulder, making him the legal man!” REESE: “Oh man… this dude’s about to get RUN.” MURRAY: “Mossberg lifts Dos over the top rope and just SLAMS him on the mat. He whips him into the ropes… BEAUTIFUL SAMOAN DROP! Now he lifts him up and spread eagles him across the top role! Artisanal Dos is in the tree of woe! Now Uno back in AND MOSSBERG JUST TAKES HIS HEAD OFF WITH A BIG BOOT! DEAR LORD!” MOSSBERG: “Yo! HOMIE DON’T PLAY THAT!” (The crowd laughs as Mossberg shakes his head.) REESE: “My man bustin’ out the Living Color reference.” MURRAY: “Mossberg now has Uno in a bearhug, looks over his shoulder --- OHHHH! HE JUST TOSSED UNO IN A BELLY-TO-BELLY OVER ONTO HIS PARTNER WHO IS STILL IN THE TREE OF WOE!” CROWD: “MOSSSSSBERRRRG! MOSSSSSSSSBERRRRG!” MURRAY: “Mossberg comes over and press slams Dos OUT OF THE RING AND ONTO THE FLOOR!” (The crowd’s on its feet chanting his name some more!” REESE: “My man’s got the strength of every single one of The Avengers COMBINED. Damn. I thought I was a big, strong dude… I got NOTHING on this kid!” And now he’s got Uno’s left arm and wraps him around…” MOSSBERG: “GAME OVER!” MURRAY: “PUMPHANDLE POWERBOMB! THIS IS ELEMENTARY! ONE! TWO! THREE! And the man known only as MOSSBERG gets the first win here on Pirate TV! And the crowd loves it, singing along to Drake as Mossberg stands on the ropes in celebration! BUT AUGUST DE LA ROSSI COMES FROM BEHIND AND JUST CLOCKED MOSSBERG WITH SOMETHING! I THINK THAT”S HIS BIKELOCK! THE LOCK FOR HIS FIXED GEAR BIKE!” REESE: “Never turn your back on ANYONE in this sport. That’s what Mossberg just learned.” MURRAY: “Rossi is putting his vintage Doc Marten boots to Mossberg! And now The Artisanals are up and doing the same! Mossberg’s on his knees and feels his head -- he’s cut on the top of his head! He gets up and just LEVELS Dos with a clothesline! And now Uno! August FLIES out of the ring and onto his bike! Mossberg out and chasing after him!” REESE: “No doubt riding his bike the hell out of town…” MOSSBERG: “GET BACK HERE! I AIN’T DONE! I AINT’ DONE!” (Mossberg turns to the crowd.) “YOU ALL WANT TO SEE SOME MORE?” MURRAY: “This crowd is EXPLODING as Mossberg just grabs Artisanal Uno and hoists him AND RUNS AND PRESS SLAMS HIM ONTO THE HOOD OF THEIR VEGAN GLUTEN FREE TACO TRUCK! And now he takes Dos and just TOSSES HIM ONTO THE TRUCK, TOO! MOSSBERG ONTO THE APRON AND NOW ONTO THE HOOD OF THE TRUCK!” CROWD: “MOOOSSSSBERRRRG! MOSSSSSSBERRRRG” MURRAY: “He’s got Dos…” MOSSBERG: “GAME! OVER!” MURRAY: “PUMPHANDLE POWERBOMB ONTO THE WINDSHIELD! HE JUST CRACKED THAT WINDSHIELD! AND NOW HE IS ON TOP OF THE TRUCK!” CROWD: “MMMMMMMMMOSSSSBERRRRRG! MOOOOSSSSSSBBERRRRGGG!” REESE: “Man, I LOVE it! Mossberg’s not just going after August De La Rossi and that belt. He’s making a NAME for himself! No doubt Eddie Mayfield’s lighting up a Camel and liking what he’s seeing from this dude! Throwing dudes through car windshields in his first match… take note, everyone! Mossberg’s coming!” MURRAY: “Folks, we’ll be right back! We’ve got a busted vegan, gluten free taco truck to get out of here…” (CUT TO: The broken down remnants of THE WINDHAM RANCH. The camera shows a fire burning in the middle of cut out corn stalks. Standing before the fire, with his arms held out as if he’s on the cross, is BOBBY JACK WINDHAM -- incredibly different, though, from how we last saw him. The former clean-cut jock now has long, flowing hair and a scraggly beard, as well as a giant tattoo of a crucifix on the front of his body. Behind him are two behemoths, both genuflecting at his side -- one wearing a blood red mask covering half of his face, the other wearing a mustard yellow version. Both are also bearded and have the same tattoo.) WINDHAM: “Do you FEEL that? Do you FEEL the fire? DO YOU FEEL THE FLAMES? If you don’t, you will. Because you, mankind… YOU HAVE FAILED ME! YOU HAVE FAILED THE SON OF GOD!” (Bobby Jack stares into the fire.) “I am the one who has been chosen. I am the one here to LEAD YOU from the wilderness and into the CLOUDS… I am those one whose WORDS YOU SHALL HEED for I… I AM YOUR DAILY BREAD! I AM YOUR DRINK OF WINE! I AM THE ONE WHO WAS BORN AND DIED FOR YOUR SINS AND REBORN AGAIN… and now I… I HAVE RETURNED! I have returned because THE END OF DAYS HAS COME! The stars have told the prophecies. You can throw your stones and you can send your Romans… But I shall point and I shall command… ARISE PESTILENCE! ARISE FAMINE!” (The two masked men behind him stand up and blankly stare at him.) “AND TOGETHER WE SHALL MARK THE BEASTS WHO SHALL BE LEFT BEHIND IN THE WILDERNESS… AND WE SHALL SLAY THOSE BEASTS AND LEAVE BEHIND A WORLD IN FLAMES… A WORLD IN FIRE! And those who have taken my hand, we shall look down and watch as they burn and burn and burn and realize that those who BELIEVE… those who FOLLOW… those who WORSHIP ME…. Shall be saved. For I… I AM THE RAPTURE! And we are now in THE AGE OF APOCALYPSE! And The Apocalypse… Is… Now…” (Bobby Jack extends his hands out as the masked men once again kneel at his side.) (CUT TO: A shaken Craig Murray and Lionel Reese, shaking his head.) MURRAY: “Odd words from Bobby Jack Windham… who now wants us to call him The Rapture… and whoever those two LUNATICS are beside him!” REESE: “I knew Bobby Jack from when he was a kid. He was an athlete from DAY ONE. But sometimes, these young stars get big egos and when they get to the big leagues and struggle, it does something to them. Some of them work harder and make it. Some of them just don’t think it’s worth it and quit. But a few dudes… well, they get some screws that come loose and all of a sudden you see them hanging out in an abandoned farm somewhere talking some Revelations stuff… Man, I don’t even WANT to know what those dudes are going to do once they’re here.” MURRAY: “And, frighteningly enough, we’ve learned that they plan on making it sometime soon from deep in Texas up here to Philadelphia. All I know is just keep them away from me!” REESE: “No doubt. These guys ain’t Jehova’s Witnesses coming to hand you a copy of Wisdom Magazine.” MURRAY: "Well, folks... it's time for our MAIN EVENT here tonight! It's to decide the number one contender match for the UWA Women's Championship! And let's get you caught up to speed --" (CUT TO: A videotape of the UWA Academy. ASHLEIGH JAMES -- A pale, smaller girl with a pixie haircut ala Audrey Hepburn in Sabrina and a 1950s retro "tablecloth" design white based with red stripes singlet. CUT TO: BERNADETTE MORRIS. Bernadette has a bigger frame -- she's 50-55 pounds overweight with a dark haircut with some split ends. She's wearing a Walmart/Christmas sweater over a pair of nondescript black tights. She has a miserable scowl on her face.) MURRAY (V/O): "ASHLEIGH and BERNADETTE both started training in the UWA Academy on the same day. The two fast became friends AND roommates from the jump, despite their differences in upbringing!" (CUT TO: A close-up of a young, smiling Ashleigh twirling a baton in front of a large, expansive suburban cul-de-sac house. V/O: Originally from Ridgewood, New Jersey, Ashleigh James is a trained gymnast, dancer, roller derby participant and lover of retro 50s fashion. The so-called 'Beauty School Knockout' impressed everyone in the UWA quickly with her raw athleticism! That, along with her quirky good looks, made her stand out in the pack as someone to keep an eye on!" (CUT TO: Ashleigh dressed like she's starring in Grease -- a polka dot bow in her hair, retro pink glasses and a polka dot dress with a red sinched belt.) ASHLEIGH: "My parents were both so happy for me when I told them I was going to school. But then their faces changed when I told them I was going to WRESTLING school." (Ashleigh laughs.) "One of my brothers is a lawyer. Another one is an accountant. But me? I've always been the oddball in the family. That's just me, though. I've always just done my own thing and make it work. And I'm going to make UWA work, too!" (CUT TO: A picture of Bernadette as a child. She has god-awful bangs and is noticeably bigger than the girls next to her with a mean scowl. V/O: Hailing from Northeast Philadelphia, Bernadette is a self-described tomboy who was raised by a single mother. Quiet by nature, Bernadette hasn't really revealed too much about herself -- quite the contrast to her good friend Ashleigh!" (CUT TO: ASHLEIGH and BERNADETTE standing together, backstage with UWA backstage interview personality DRAYMOND DECKER, clad in a black-and-white checker porkpie hat and a go-tee.) DECKER: "Here we are, two roommates, close friends and classmates. Both of you competing to become the NUMBER ONE contender for the UWA Women's World Title!" ASHLEIGH: "I am SO excited for this." (Ashleigh turns to Bernadette.) "Bernie... I just want to tell you good luck for this match. I know that a lot of things happen in wrestling, but it comes down to competition. And all I'd like to really do is just compete with someone I know just wants to wrestle and try and become the best!" BERNADETTE: "Same to you, Ash." (Ashleigh hugs Bernadette, who just sort of shakes her head.) (CUE UP: "Pull Shapes" by the Pipettes! The 50s-type music pours in as Ashleigh whispers good luck. CUT TO: Ashleigh coming out down the aisle, starting to do the Twist as most of the crowd does the same.) MURRAY: "And here comes Ashleigh, for what's the biggest match of her life! The Beauty School Knockout is once again bringing a spirit of joy -- AND OH NO! BERNADETTE JUST CAME OUT OF NOWHERE AND CLOCKED HER! NOW BERNADETTE IS STOMPING ON ASHLEIGH! WHERE DID THIS COME FROM?" REESE: "I think homegirl Ashleigh just learned her first lesson. I was sitting back listening to her and knew right away how naïve she was, trusting another person in this sport!" BERNADETTE: "THIS IS MY SHOT, ASHLEIGH! MINE! I'M NOT LETTING YOU PRETTY LITTLE THINGS TAKE THINGS FROM ME ANYMORE!" MURRAY: "And OH MY! Bernadatte grabs Ashleigh by her hair and just SWINGS her into the metal ringside railing! And again! And now she lifts her up and TOSSES her right onto the side of the ring! Ashleigh is out of it! Now Bernadette is rolling Ashleigh into the ring!" BERNADETE: "RING THE BELL!" MURRAY: "Referee Jake Spreewell does just that! And Bernadette is sneering as she's standing on the ring apron... SOMMERSAULT SENTON FROM THE NEAR 200-POUNDER! She calls that The Demotion!" REESE: "I call that pain!" MURRAY: "And this is elementary! One! Two! Three!" (The crowd boos as Bernadette rises to her feet. Spreewell raises her hands as she stomps on Ashleigh!) MURRAY: "Okay, that's uncalled for!" REESE: "Put 'em down for good. That's some Art Of War stuff. I don't like this girl's attitude... but I like her attitude, if you get what I'm saying. She's ruthless and I applaud that!" MURRAY: "Folks, we're out of time! THIS HAS BEEN PIRATE TV!"
