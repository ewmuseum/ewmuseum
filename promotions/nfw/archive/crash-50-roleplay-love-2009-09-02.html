GreggG — 4 September 2009 Double post GreggG — 4 September 2009 For those that remember the late, great TWW (my goofball promotion I ran with Devin for three weeks that was a cross between Chikara and Peter David-era X-Factor), here was the booking sheet.
1) Cameron Cruise was the world champion, which he was in that weird league with Tabu a few of us joined right before it closed. The gimmick of the league was that Troy Windham was starting his own indie hellhole promotion based in small New England cities.
2) Troy initially booked himself as world champion, but Comissioner PowerMaster did not think that was aggro-intenze enough. In addition, TWW director of human resources "Mr. Middle Management" Mike McGee said that updates to the Sarbanes-Oxley legislation, as well as changes to the Generally Accepted Accounting Principles guidelines, prevented Windham from being able to give himself his own title. There must be a separation between the CEO of the league and its champion.
3) Troy and Triple X were entangled in a very petty, immature feud. During this time, Cruise plotted ways to build an alliance to keep his title involving a masked tag team. Troy and Triple X were going to win the tag belts.
4) Meanwhile, Terence was going on an undefeated streak and dominating the competition in Goldberg fashion. At one show, he was going to take on Lady Freedom (whom PowerMaster proposed to at one point) and punch her in the face a bunch of times while PowerMaster looked on in approval.
5) This was going to lead to a 4-way match between Troy Windham, Triple X, Cameron Cruise and Terence. With Terence squashing everyone in a few minutes flat.
6) Terence was never going to lose the title. But once the myth of Terence was unveiled, and once I realized I really had no time to even run a pretty ridiculous league, I stopped doing it.
Also, Eddie Mayfield's brother Doug was going to join at some point. And, yes, more Terence, please. And the World Championship thread is the best series of RPs I've read in a long, long, long, long time.
