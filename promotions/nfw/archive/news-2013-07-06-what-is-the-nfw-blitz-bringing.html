NFW news: What is the NFW Blitz bringing?

What is the NFW Blitz bringing? Posted by Devin Millwood on 6 Jul 2013 Amidst a strange spring season within the sport, New Frontier Wrestling comes back stateside with all bullets firing at the metropolitan capital of the planet. Without much of a mention on the recent pause in proceedings, a DOUBLE-JACK on the bouroughs has industry insiders watching with raised eyebrows as NFW goes BRAWLESS in the summer. NFW went to Europe. Castor Strife went to Jerry World. Joe the Plumber... well we got the exposition on where he's been from the foul-mouthed plumber himself. Those facts were on the surface level amidst rising tensions in the NFW locker room at the end of the European tour. Some questioning Strife's decision to let his contract expire after shunning the NFW World Title for the Ultratitle Tournament and a much-hyped three-quel affair. Some questioning whether Mayfield should have stepped in sooner before letting Castor fall from grace literally against Impulse. Whatever questions remain, Eddie Mayfield's looking like he's got a renewed lease on his second-term Presidency in NFW. Castor Strife's looking like he's still waiting on a CSWA or EPW contract before Mayfield budges. And that's not the bigger story according to many. The Grand Prix, the tour across Europe exposed a segregated roster for what it was. The rookies were tested, but it was a veteran in Jack Harmen holding up the Grand Prix. Few of the rookies remain, some disenchanted and some possibly still on a mountaintop fighting for Ragnarok against a cannibal. Rook Black changed the guard, moving himself across the board to the Hellfire Club. And our current World Champion in response, making demands for his own title defenses that did not include what may be 3 of the top 5 contenders to his title. Did Eddie Mayfield decide to take a smoke break on it all? Was ESEN's overhead expenses dictating a PAUSE after Amsterdam and Dallas? More exclusive and truth-telling interviews would be needed to answer that. Eddie Mayfield's certainly a man that's always recognized an opportunity. Summer doldrums settled on the sport and here comes NFW with five RELOADED shows blitzing at the east coast. Nobody knows if Strife's signed, nobody knows where Nova is, nobody knows if Jack Bryant can walk... Those will be the surrounding motifs around the Hellfire Club, the NFW Front Office and NFW World Champion Impulse. Mayfield at a press conference in front of Times Square announced TWO World Championship matches. Impulse vs. Harmen at the IZOD in East Rutherford, the winner meeting JACK BRYANT in Brooklyn. With Rook Black and new TV Champion Dorchester Stratton, the Hellfire Club now sets their sights on JJ Deville's prized possession. The NFW Front Office burnt to the ground. Something to mold in his beloved image... After all, his beloved North Carolina's finest wrestling organization rose up like a phoenix early this year and ashed out somewhere near Raleigh by spring, leaving Deville more red-FACE!?ed than ever. Leaving Mayfield more angry at anyone that dared cross the Frontier's lines for those particular outer banks. Deville is powerless, the Windham Clan War finally swept under a rug that's got more than enough dust to roll Impulse's eyes. Yet, Mayfield and Impulse seem chummy compared to the past two years of antagonistic viewpoints on that whole 'WAR' or who leaked Rosie's loss to the public, or just about every smarmy remark each had for each other in front of and behind the scenes. What matters now to Impulse is he's got a DOUBLE-JACK on the rocks with the NFW World Championship at stake. And there was last item on the press release handout, a redacted Pay-Per-View that appeared and is not the return to where-must-not-be-named. Or at least most of us think so... but it's out there. NFW will get there with alot of momentum if it can RELOAD and fire off all these bullets they've got left. If it does, more gunslingers will find the Frontier and in this crazy place... that's all that ever mattered. This Blitz must bring blood.
