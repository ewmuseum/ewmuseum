HAPPY BIRTHDAY BABY JEBUS! BigPimpin — 26 December 2013 I liked this show a lot. Bryant and Harmen, man, if they don't kill each other before Random Rumble it'll be amazing. Solid read
The three guys featured in the showcase we all much deserving of their W's, and one of them (Blaine Hollywood) was a good read in particular because I knew not all that much about his character, and that was a huge help for a N00b.
So, I wrote the first half of the segment, and I was really pleased with what it's turned into. I'm going to be reacting to it in character, but wonderful job.
Also love the Dragons' match and how that turned out. Was really surprised by the ending for Kuroyama and Alexander, but I think it was done in such a way that everyone benefited. Nevertheless, I missed Teddy Alexander when he disappeared, and I hope he can continue to kick ass.
Impromptu chaos! Legion and the Black Market at their best. Lines of the night: [quoting] Quote > LEGION: (still watching the brawl) “Mark, have you ever seen a car trunk … from the inside?”
> > (A twisted grin forms across LEGION’s face as ‘JUST’ MARK has a look of sheer horror on his.)
> > CALDWELLER: (V/O) “Between that and his experience with Freddie Sagawa… maybe Mark should be considering the timing of his career choices.”
> > RICE: (V/O) “Or not be an annoying prick that gets what’s coming to him.” > > DREWES: (V/O) “DING!” [end quote] Was a very good read, set some good things up for BRAWLSGIVING, and I look forward to how that turns out.

[end quote] WORD LIFE Biron — 5 January 2014 Good card. I think I like the ESEN RINGSIDE format more than the typical BRAWL. The production value of the entire card is top-notch. It's clear that the intent is to be a Sportscenter-style show and it's spot on. Did a great job introducing the hosts - easily pictured them in my head.
The short highlight packages for the squash matches is easily better than what we had before. It's faster and gives you just as much information. I doubt people are reading three to four paragraphs of highlights when the JTTS guys are involved. Also, keeping guys like Alex Austin, Lane Cash, and Blaine Hollywood active is a good idea for creating a stronger mid-card. Although, I would say that Blaine could be higher than that (if there was a story there and he was written consistently).
I had fun w/ the DOCTORS ON DESTRUCTION segment. Ford's a lot of fun to talk to about angles and segments.
Glad to see Xavier Langston keep himself right in the game ... and static w/ Peter Windham is a great idea. That's a great spot to be in. I think Langston has good potential - would love to see him find his niche in NFW.
