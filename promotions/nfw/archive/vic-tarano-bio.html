Vic Tarano Biography Eye Color Steel Blue Nicknames The Brick, The Brooklyn Bull Hometown Brooklyn, New York Birthday December 12th Height 5'6 Weight 185 Handedness Right-handed Looks Short statured, but built like a miniature Mack Truck (in the vein of Tom Hardy). Slicked-back, dark brown hair. Always clean shaven, no body hair either. Wrestling Style Fast-paced, Innovative Street Fighter Finisher Move 1.) Bull Rush, 2.) Garrote Choke Finisher Description 1.) Shining Wizard, 2.) Guillotine Choke Other Moves - Omerta' (Fish Hook Crossface) - Finger, Arm, Back, Leg Stomps - Head Punt Followed by "Well, ain't that a kick n'da head." Gimmick When Tarano first arrived in the NFW, he was a wet-behind-the-ears Street Earner, but, now, he's a matured (snicker) Capo. Bad tempered, conniving Italian. Greedy, money and power hungry. Networker. Forges and breaks alliances at a moment's notice. Very Sonny Corleone-ish. Strengths 1) Fearless 2) Conniving 3) Napoleon Complex (Tough) Weaknessees 1) Napoleon Complex (Easily rattled) 2) Small Stature 3) Rusty Ring attire Dress slacks that vary in color (black or charcoal gray are common). Button-down, long-sleeve dress shirt to match. Sleeves are rolled up past his forearms. Suspenders. Shiny, black wrestling boots.
