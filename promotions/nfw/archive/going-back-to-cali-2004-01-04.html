-Josh the Whiner.... heh. Off to Valinor — 8 January 2004 Would you have rather me said nothing at all? Cause then all the RP would've been wasted, so at least I've learned that much not to make someone upset. ;)
To answer: Is it 'fair'? Well - this is the only 'godbooked' match out of 17 weeks of matches. Therefore, I don't think catastophic results will occur from the decision.
Nobody is losing to anyone in their conference, so it won't affect the standings all that much since only 2 other matches were signed.
But to give a more personal answer on why it was done, and thus explaining it... I've never felt that RP should be the only facet in deciding how NFW rolls along, so to speak. There's plenty of weeks where we let the RP determine things, but there will be other weeks where in order to push storylines we want to write into the league - RP won't be used as much.
Due to how the league was set up for Season I, you won't see too many of these 'storyline' weeks. However, I've already decided on changing things around for Season II - where I can let people along your lines of thinking compete under RP rules strictly, while others that enjoy storylines more can compete in that environment.
But there will come a time when those styles will have to mix and All-Star Week will give a good preview of how that could work out.
Now keep in mind, that we try to reward effort as best we can and I feel like we've done a good job at that. But remember, the people running NFW need some 'sparks' to keep their interests so we don't bail on the league. :)
I'll be sending an email out to all War Games participants regarding getting RP/angles in for the show b/c I do want some involvement by handlers - but yes, the decision has been made on the booking due to various reasons. I just hope in the end, you'll enjoy them.
