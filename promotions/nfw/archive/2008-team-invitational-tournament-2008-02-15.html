2008 TEAM Invitational Tournament

15 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TH — 15 February 2008 I'm really at a loss to descibe to you guys why you should sign up for the TiT this year. I mean, if you're in NFW, that means you've probably seen 99.9% of what there is to see in eW. And I'm not just talking about matches, feuds and angles either. I'm talking about Apeman Hitlers and half-aborted fetuses running amok, exploding cages, tanks, rouge conferences, a two-year long angle ending in a the biggest F*CK YOU double-cross in eW history, Michael Manson stomping grapes, disembodied French voices telling people what to do and last but not least, Dan Ryan actually tapping out. But... have you seen the way people notice you after you make a run in this tournament, let alone win it? If not, you ain't seen nothin' yet.
TH — 25 February 2008 I have it on very good authority that one of the secret entrants in this tournament is from NFW, and he's a real big shot here too. Who is it? I'm not giving any hints, but you could very well join in the fray and perhaps meet up with this hotshot and score a big upset victory. Wouldn't you want those bragging rights in the fed? You have five days to make good on that.
