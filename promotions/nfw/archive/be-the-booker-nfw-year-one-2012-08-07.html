EastPrez — 7 August 2012 Hey guys - just to let you know I de-cloaked to say that I really enjoyed the BRAWLAPALOOZA RP series. I'm really happy with the interest and where things are going. It got me thinking.
A fun little fan board thing: BE THE BOOKER series I watched these vids on youtube where they take dudes from the business and ask them how they would have booked certain eras/periods in wrestling history.
Such as: They asked Jim Cornette how he would have booked the WWE buyout of WCW. So then he runs through the whole year of promos, angles, leading up to a blowoff. Check it out.
My favorite What If is Raven booking Hall & Nash invading ECW. I don't want this to turn into a "How would you fix what JN and Katz ****ed up", but it could be a fun alternate history. The history of NFW is filled with Katz and I (and GG) dealing with what we were given, trying to make it interesting, and keep everyone involved. As you know there are infamous parts of NFW history that some people hate or didn't get or would NEVER HAVE DONE IN THEIR LIVES Grrrrr so here's your chance, Mister booker. Think of this as a history mode in a video game, like NBA 2K12 Jordan mode. Can you get Jordan to 60 points with the flu? GAME ON!
I wanted to start off with, BE THE BOOKER: NFW YEAR ONE. With the roster at its smallest and trying to secure heavy hitting contracts, book us up through CRASH 1 to CRASH 5 (Crashmas) and who would be world champion. Some of the biggest players at the time were Bloodhunt, Armando Montezuma (Monty), ARES, Mike Manson, a soon-to-appear Shane Southern, Alex Wylde (he could be his own booking challenge!), HELLMACHINE. Wild (5) Star, CANCER.
Venue: The VAULT, limited budget, audience and roster. Sully and Craig Miles are pretty poor and trying to pick up the pieces from UWA and start over.
CRASH 1 - first show - Major players, Wild(5) Star, Aiden Campbel, Alex Wylde CRASH 2 - Can they do it again? CRASH 3 - Random Rumble - Crown World Champion CRASH 4 - Problems with ARES CRASH 5- Crashmas Your job: Take us quickly through an outline of Show 1 to a buildup to Crashmas, and ultimately, FUTURESHOCK.
Feel free to pour through the archives to see who was doing what, who fizzled out, who rose up.

Fortunately I have some help in the way of being familiar with all these characters (I joined right after BANNED 4 LIFE, the first PPV that I believe JN meant instead of Futureshock?), as well as some knowledge of what went down prior to all this in UWA. So drawing on all that...
PRIMER: At the close of UWA, the league was coming off of Random Rumble '99 where there was big anticipation that either Mike Manson or Ares, two stable partners in The Devil's Incarnate (DI), would wind up in the final four and one of them would earn a title shot against Carlos Canyeta later in the night. To everyone's surprise, Mike Sparks won, then lost the title shot later on. None of the UWA main event big dogs would make the move to NFW, so that rules out Troy Windham, Carlos Canyeta, Winston Steele, and Mike Randalls (Craig Miles is in that category, but will not be an active NFW wrestler). Rising UWA stars Mike Manson, Ares, Alex Wylde, and eventually Shane Southern would sign with NFW instead. Right off the bat, given the massive momentum Manson and Ares were building in the old league, you're looking at those two guys as the most bankable stars in NFW, with Wildstar and Wylde not far behind.
CRASH 1: We're going to run vignettes on Armando Montezuma, a blonde haired, blue eyed American Olympic wrestler who changed his named and switched country allegiance to Cuba. At some point in the near future, Monty and Bloodhunt (Doc Silver's ex-hitman) would forge an alliance known as Team Cuba, but I would book that right away and put them both in singles and tag competition.
Miles and Sully announce that NFW's going to make a big splash on night 1 with a 30-man Random Rumble, with the winner being locked into the inaugural NFW World Championship match that will take place at the company's first PPV. The final 16 competitors beneath the winner will be entered into a repechage tournament bracket, and those matches will take place on the next few shows. The bracket winner will face the Random Rumble winner for the big belt at the PPV.
SEGMENT: Ares and Mike Manson (Devil's Incarnate) announce that they will fight each other before the Random Rumble tonight, in a match that will define wrestling for the new decade/century of professional wrestling. They are also putting a $50,000 bounty on their own heads, to any man who can eliminate either one of them from the Random Rumble.
SEGMENT: Alex Wylde shows up looking very dapper. The Atlantic City native explains how he went on an unprecedented run in AC, and can now claim to be the richest wrestler in the world. "Gatecrasher" Alex Wylde calls Craig Miles and Sullivan to the ring. "Despite being supremely talented, I never got my fair shot in UWA, or anywhere else for that matter. So I'm not asking for my fair shot - I'm offering to BUY YOU OUT, and give Craig a raise. I become part owner of NFW, and you (points at Miles) are going to book for me that title shot." Miles: "You got the money?" Alex: "Of course." Miles: "Good. You can pay me to forget about suspending you from competing tonight. We'll call it a 'fine'." Wylde storms off, vowing to bankrupt NFW and buy it for cheap later on.
Manson vs. Ares: Match goes for 10 minutes, intense back and forth action, until Dez Aragon and Cancer interfere and attack Ares. Manson slumps into the corner and watches. Cancer holds Ares while Dez lights a torch, drinks some substance, and blows a fireball into Ares' face. Manson stands up and pays both men $50,000. Ares is effectively out of the Random Rumble and thus has no shot to win the World Title.
Showstoppers debut vs. The Oblivion Express in an instant tag classic that sees the Showstoppers go over.
Ricky Zane vs. "The Polish Prince" Rick Ryconik. The Prince is old, and midway through the match collapses due to congestive heart failure. As the EMTs take him away on a stretcher, Zane celebrates like a madman and yells on the microphone "DEATH BY ZANE-A-MANIA!"
RANDOM RUMBLE: 30 men battle until only Manson (who was being protected most of the night by Dez and Cancer) and Wildstar are left standing. Manson eventually wins, and Dez and Cancer slide back into the ring as the three of them pick Wildstar apart. Manson puts him in the Tourniquet (crippler cross-face) and doesn't let go. A team of refs has to break it up.
CRASH 2: SEGMENT: Manson comes out with Dez/Cancer, calls them The Manson Family, and that in the new century, there can't be alliances between Gods and Monsters. And in the case of Ares, God is dead. Long live the monster and his disciples. Announcers note the coincidence that the Family took out Ares and Wildstar, the two biggest threats to a Manson championship.
First night of bracket matches begins: Steve Radder d. Torch Aidan Campbell d. Cancer w/Rayne Dez Aragon d. Marcus Price Ricky Zane v. Bloodhunt via interference from masked Secret Service agent! 5 Minute Commercial featuring Alex Wylde airs: Wylde says he bought blocks of premium air time from the network over the next few shows, and beings airing a "Best of Alex Wylde" highlight reel.
SEGMENT: Armando Montezuma debuts, swearing to root out the American conspiracy that led to Bloodhunt's downfall in the tournament. He also swears to win the tournament and the title in honor of Team Cuba.
MAIN EVENT: Hellmachine vs. Sex & Violence. Hellmachine scores the upset over the former UWA tag champs!
CRASH 3: SEGMENT: Miles, Sullivan, and Manson are in the ring, along with a lottery wheel. It is announced by Miles that since Wildstar is too injured to compete in the tournament to determine Manson's championship opponent, a lottery will be held to find a bracket replacement. Miles turns the wheel, finally stopping and picking out a name. He reads it: "ARES!" Manson flips out and demands to see the other name tickets; Miles lets him. Manson starts reading them off, "Ares, Ares, Ares, Ares...this was a rig!" Miles: "Hey, life isn't fair. You want to tip the scales in your favor? I'll tip them back. The New Frontier isn't going to be the Manson Family's playground." Manson: "That's assuming Ares can even compete, and I don't believe he can. Which means I get a bye." CUEUP: High Fivin' MF! Second night of bracket matches: Armando Montezuma d. Sean Toombs via interference from Bloodhunt Steel Viper d. Dr. Doom Roger Powell d. Jasin Bondage SEGMENT: Alex Wylde buys a 5 minute spot where he's in a ring at Atlantic City, and defeats a gauntlet of 5 no-name challengers to win the coveted ATLANTIC CITY CHAMPIONSHIP OF THE WORLD!
Hellmachine battle The Showstoppers to a 15-minute draw! MAIN EVENT: Final bracket match, Ares d. Jeff Cummins in dominant fashion using his patented FNS (FULL NELSON SLAM). Show ends with Cancer and Dez coming out to attack him, but WILDSTAR arrives on the scene and they both clear the ring!
CRASH 4: SEGMENT: Monty and Bloodhunt in Cuba, where Monty shows off the high quality of life under Castro. They enter a dark room and begin interrogating an NFW ringworker who they claim was captured by "Cuban special forces" (Cojones Mercado) to answer questions regarding Zane-gate!
Obviously a taped segment, since the first tournament match is... Monty d. Aidan Campbell due to outside interference from Bloodhunt Ricky Zane d. Steel Viper SEGMENT: Alex Wylde buys more air time, this time to field a special challenge in Atlantic City from The Polish Prince Rick Ryconik! HOLLYWOOD'S ON FIRE: Wildstar talks to Lamont about his injury, how he's recovering quick, and wants a shot at the World Championship. Appeals to Sullivan and Miles to let him back into the tournament somehow.
Cancer d. Torch w/Rayne due to Rayne turning on her man and joining The Manson Family! (Note: Rayne eventually became a disciple of Manson's during Ultratitle Season 1)
Tournament matches: Dez Aragon d. Roger Powell MAIN EVENT: Ares d. Steve Radder via FNS SEGMENT: The Manson Family uses a nude Rayne as an altar to cast a destruction spell on Ares. This is not a serious seg, more of the tongue-in-cheek dark humor that Manson exhibited during his time.
CRASH 5: SEGMENT: Miles and Sully announce the first PPV is BANNED 4 LIFE, where both the World Championship match and a one-night tournament for the tag belts will take place. Alex Wylde interrupts the segment to announce that he is funding a campaign to keep NFW off PPV. He appears with his AC title belt and a list of 5,000 signatures that he's delivering to the US Senate this week in protest of the event. And next week, he plans to air 20 minutes of unedited material featuring his own matches. Miles offers him a deal: cancel the campaign and the commercial, show up on Crash next week, and sign a match to fight me at the PPV for the right to be #1 competitor to the NFW World Championship. Alex agrees.
FINAL FOUR matches: Armando Montezuma d. Ricky Zane after Bloodhunt hits Zane with the Headshot Ares d. Dez Aragon in spite of interference from Cancer and Rayne. Hellmachine and The Showstoppers go to another 15 minute draw The Powell Bros. d. The Oblivion Express Mike Manson d. Torch in a casket match SEGMENT: Wildstar in Sullivan's office. Wildstar says he's healed, ready to go, wants a shot at Manson to replace him, but Sullivan says it's too late, he's got to wait his turn.
MAIN EVENT FINALS: Ares d. Monty when Wildstar helps equalize interference from Manson and Bloodhunt CRASH 6: SEGMENT: Wildstar calls Ares to the ring, says he doesn't expect a thank you for helping him overcome The Manson Family and Team Cuba, but wants an opportunity to win the belt. Proposes that he put his trip to the World Championship match on the line tonight, with the agreement that the loser will watch the winner's back at the PPV. Ares agrees.
Team Cuba (Monty/Hunt) d. The Powell Bros. by shady means Dez/Cancer score an upset win over Hellmachine! SEGMENT: Bloodhunt attacks Zane backstage: "WHO WAS THE SECRET SERVICE AGENT?!" Zane: "Man...it runs too deep! If I told you, I'd be a dead man! Come on, I have a wife and kids! Lots of them! By different mothers! All over the country! I might want to visit one or two of them someday, don't do this!"
SEGMENT: Miles/Wylde contract signing. Miles admits he's in no condition to wrestle, but instead will be the special guest referee. Wylde is mad, but laughs it off and says that he's got nobody to face him. Manson, Ares, and Wildstar are all busy, Monty and Bloodhunt are competing in the tag tournament. The cupboard's bare! Miles: "You're right, you won't be facing any of them. So meet your new opponent!" CUEUP: That Garth Brooks sh*t Shane Southern used to come out to! SOUTHERN/WYLDE STAREDOWN! Torch d. Cancer in spite of interference from Rayne Aidan Campbell d. Steel Viper MAIN EVENT: Ares d. Wildstar in an instant classic to cement his place in the PPV title match! The Manson Family attacks, and both men fend them off.
BANNED 4 LIFE PPV: Tag Tournament First Round: Hellmachine d. The A-List Team Cuba d. The Oblivion Express 4 Way Dance: Ricky Zane d. Torch, Aidan Campbell, and Steel Viper Tag Tournament First Round: Dez/Cancer d. Sex & Violence The Showstoppers d. The Powell Bros. Vignette for Castor Strife airs! Tag Tournament Semifinals: Team Cuba d. Dez/Cancer Hellmachine d. The Showstoppers in a 25 minute classic! Number One Contender's Match: Shane Southern vs. Alex Wylde. Alex goes for the Gatecrasher (superkick to the back of the head), but Shane ducks and Alex knocks out Miles! Shane turns and KOs Alex with the Party's Over superkick and hooks the leg. Head Referee Greg Herpin runs down and makes the count. When Miles wakes up, he reverses the decision, disqualifies Southern and awards the match to Alex Wylde!
TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT FINALS: Team Cuba d. Hellmachine to become the inaugural NFW World Tag Team Champions
NFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH: Mike Manson vs. Ares. Classic match, goes about 20 mins, when the ref is bumped after Ares hits the FNS on Manson. When Dez/Cancer run into the ring, Wildstar clears them out with a chair. When Ares turns, Wildstar HITS HIM WITH THE CHAIR and wakes up Manson! Manson picks Ares up and crushes him with the Sweet Dreams stunner, and the ref makes the slow count for three. Mike Manson is your new heavyweight champion. Wildstar has joined the Manson Family!
Aftermath: Wildstar reveals that he joined The Manson Family because NFW didn't promote him the right way, should have made him the company's star right off the bat, and Mike Manson visited his home t explain why he injured him. Manson said it was all a part of his plan, and promised Wildstar the first crack at the NFW World Heavyweight Championship if he helps him win it. So he challenged Ares the week before the PPV against his doctor's wishes just to weaken him, and now Manson will let him rehab properly before getting the title shot.
Wylde loses his match against Manson eventually. Shane Southern feuds with Miles, winds up beating him in a 2 out of 3 falls match to become the new number one contender. This brings up a conflict with Wildstar, and now Shane feuds with Wildstar in a greater build towards Shane Southern vs. Mike Manson.
Discontent in Team Cuba as it is revealed that Monty was the Secret Service agent who bumped off Bloodhunt in the tourney. He did it to prevent an eventual conflict of interest, to preserve Team Cuba, but Bloodhunt isn't buying it. Hunt turns face, leaves Team Cuba, but they are forced to defend the titles anyway. Hellmachine beats them for the straps due to infighting, and Hunt goes his separate way.

[end quote] LQJT86C — 8 August 2012 1) Why not elevate Wylde past that? It's a cool re-skinned "Show Troy the Money" angle, but where does Wylde get to go from there after jobbing to Manson? Just set up as main event feeding for Manson?
'Cause f*ck Ernie. No, I'm messing lol. I think how I left off gives the impression that Wylde would just job to Manson on Crash 7 and go back to the end of the line, especially when I hint at a bigger feud between Manson and Southern. Realistically though, Manson vs. Wylde would be huge in it's own right while Wildstar and Southern had their own feud. When it comes down to it though, do I put Manson over Wylde? Yes, and the reason is very simple: Mike was a better handler than Ernie. Both were my friends, both were great, but I always felt Mike would go the extra mile to flesh out Manson with the bigger picture in mind while Ernie was just "When's the title coming?" That's not to say Wylde would never be champion. This angle would have made him as big of a deal as Manson, and given him a chance to show he could stick it out for the long haul. But given Manson's UWA history coming into NFW, he's the safer bet as the first champ and definitely had more heat coming in. I would have put the title on Ares before Wylde as well.
2) Why have Miles wrestling at all? A tease? In EARTH 616, Miles caught a lot of heat from Team Cuba about wrestling or NOT wrestling, why decide to have him put on the leather pants and take his shoes off?
Miles at that time was a year removed from being a UWA main eventer, so I don't think people saw him as the "mover and shaker" just yet. I thought the beginning was the best time for him to combined his ring prowess and Machiavellian senses to the forefront to establish himself as the ultimate string-puller. Katz could probably tell me if I'm wrong here, but I thought the booking to have him f*ck Southern over instead of Wylde was 100% a Miles kind of move, and diverting Miles vs. Wylde into Southern vs. Miles here was ultimately a better feud option. But I guess to really answer your question, "Why use Miles in a wrestling feud?", it comes down to Miles being the perfect guy to help get Shane Southern from upper-tier to main event tier.
3) Where's Calvin Carlton? You threw him out of your book. Calvin really made his presence felt during the OSS/SS feud, i.e. after the first PPV. I assumed he would be with NFW in some capacity, but didn't write anything major for him since I figured he would come in with OSS after B4L. As Wildstar's manager, I just don't think he's as effective, especially given my Wildstar heel turn. Wildstar didn't need him, but OSS definitely did, and there's no way I would have 86'd that angle if I booked past B4L.
4) Your book ignores the shaky restart of the company and brings it in hot and seemingly, with some capital. I liked how you touched on that a bit with Wyldes payoff offer, but that was the end of it . . . Does all of this stuff happen in the Vault?
Yeah, it all happens in the Vault. If I was writing these shows for real, I def would've had segments where Sully and Miles talk about Wylde's antics being a real threat to the company and whatnot. I do picture NFW at that time having some real heat behind it though. Compare it to NLW's run for a second: I did 5 shows and a PPV, and it was a big deal that we had ONE STAR in Impulse. NFW on the other hand was coming to the forefront with the expertise of an established owner and wrestling star behind it (Sully and Miles), with a handful of bankable stars (Ares, Manson, Wildstar, Wylde, and eventually Southern), and the option of bringing Miles in for a match whenever. So in my mind, they weren't exactly in dire straits, just not on stable ground. Going back to your Miles question, that could be another reason why he's wrestling: NFW needs him to!
