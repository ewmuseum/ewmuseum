Introducing your Three Wise Men

25 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Off to Valinor — 25 February 2005 WISE MAN #1: Me. You know me....and probably hate me. WISE MAN #2: [name withheld] -- the creator of the NFW East, the handler for our inaugural Ultratitle Champion, Michael Manson, and [detail withheld].
WISE MAN #3: [name withheld] -- the handler for the Ultratitle runner-up, Shane Southern and probably the nicest, laid back person I've met in the hobby. If you piss Jason off, most likely you'll get booted from this fed harder than an fugly one-night stand.
WHAT ARE THE RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE THREE WISE MEN? The three wise men will be the people who read the RPs and judge them accordingly for results each week. We will also plot key strategies, help everyone run with ball concerning angles as well as discuss other issues that most 'fedheads' think about themselves.
IF THE THREE WISE MEN CAN'T AGREE WHAT HAPPENS? We bring in [name withheld] and [name withheld] to help us settle the issue. If that doesn't work we use the old CSWA system of dice rolling (flipping a coin).
IF I HAVE A PROBLEM WITH THIS WHAT SHOULD I DO? Probably not show up Week 1. If you can't trust them, you definitely shouldn't trust my brains.
ARE THE THREE WISE MEN RESPONSIBLE FOR WRITING THE CARDS? NO. In fact, the only parts of the card I will write during the regular season will be 3-4 sentence highlights of matches not done, if nobody else from the conference can handle that. Jason and Mike will determine if they have time to write matches, or not. Otherwise, I've envisioned NFW East as a commune in that regard. If nobody writes matches, then expect alot of highlights.
WHY JASON AND MIKE? Because [name withheld] and [name withheld] are the last two of NFW's old guard as well as two people that I trust immensely when put together to help judge the RP. More importantly, they've stuck with NFW through thick and thin, never once complained and have demonstrated that they sure as hell know what they're doing (moreso than myself). Like I said in the Wrestlebowl credits, I view them as ambassadors for NFW -- and this is only a continuation of that.
CAN YOU OVERRULE MIKE AND JASON? Of course. However, most of you that know me -- pretty much know it'd take something quite crazy/ridiculous/profane/etc to make that ever happen. In fact, I edited down some of the content of Beau vs. Cameron -- but in a way that Mike was perfectly content with...and actually wondering if I did edit at all. I expect that this will NEVER have to happen on a drastic level as Mike, Jason and I have worked seamlessly for five years now.
WHAT ABOUT MANSON AND SOUTHERN's matches? If you feel uncomfortable regarding them judging your match, their vote will be given to [name withheld] or [name withheld], whomever I can find first.
QUESTONS? Feel free to ask. JLevinson — 25 February 2005 Hmmmmmmmmm. Well Katz, you know me... always wantin' more. I feel that it's unfair to expect Jason or Mike, even in their wisdom, to be able to objectively judge their own matches. Since what we're dealing with is a competitive, standings-based environment, I would think that it would be a good idea to have Jamar or Gregg (or someone else) to vote as the 3rd person in all of Mike's and Jason's matches.
My concern isn't that they'll put themselves over... far from it, actually. I know I find it impossible to judge my own RP (as you well know), and even if you're not giving yourself enough credit (as opposed to giving yourself too much credit), it's tough to really work that out in a credible way.
I think having another person to vote in these matches is a good idea and adds to the competitive, objective-based atmosphere I, personally, would like to see in the East.
Just my 2 cents, of course... y'know I don't REALLY give a sh*t... ;-) -Josh Off to Valinor — 25 February 2005 Josh -- consider that 100% done for your matches. If anyone has the same concern, don't fear verbalizing it. Its why I presented the option in the first place. If a majority of you feel the same way, then it'll happen as Josh suggested and that whomever I can find first (JN/GG) will be the alternate third wise crack...man. ;)
