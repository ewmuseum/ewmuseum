JW Collins Biography Eye Color Brown Nicknames 'Tiggerman' Hometown Jackson, MIssisippi Birthday 3/1/74 Height 6'1 Weight 224 Alias The Cat Who Makes Pussy Growl Biography From the early-mid 90's, JW Collins was part of the successful Perfect Alliance along with Craig Miles, Scott Waters and John Bryan III. Competing primarily in the UWA for most of this time, Miles and Collins formed the tag team Full Force and won several titles. In 1998, JW Collins was arrested on charges of failing to pay child support and subsequently left the sport. After years spent in the haze of alcohol and drugs, Collins has finally sobered up with the goal of paying back Craig Miles for never helping him with his troubles.
