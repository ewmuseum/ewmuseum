GreggG — 1 December 2011 Great card! I loved the main event -- super bomb throwing stuff at the end which fit really nicely into the 30 minute time limit.
The Jack/JJ match was a favorite of mine to write. I really wanted to make it like a video game or something like Road House where the hero has to go through a lot of stages to get to the final boss. And then there's the damsel in distress bit and then when Jack finally gets his chance at JJ... JJ's beyond cowardly. Like absolutely pathetic. Just a snivelling little turd when he has no power with him. I really tried to get the character behind the characters over in that -- Jack's a straightforward dude who stops at nothing and JJ's a little fink who, once he's back into a corner, crumbles.
JJ's become maybe my all-time favorite character. And I like it because, in a lot of ways, he's become a community character. Pete wrote the stuff with Impulse and JJ in the beginning of the show and Billy did the parking violation stuff and Katz did stuff with him, too. And all of it just added to him being the most petty, politicking asshole.
Thanks SO much! This card was a lot of work personally for me but totally worth it! Legion — 1 December 2011 Great packed card here. A huge thanks to Warren for the pure Title match and given the outcome of it a huge thanks for allowing the opportunity. It shall be interesting to see how it affects the Stalker/Legion/Luci triangle. Speaking of Stalker I wonder who he's taking to a institution that should be very familiar to those in the CSWA or of Randall's past..
that's the great thing about NFW's storylines. Just when you think you have the answers... more questions arise.
User Poets — 1 December 2011 Quick heads up for the opening segment: H’WOOD: "What? You didn’t know I banged Neko?" I believe her name was spelled 'Nico'. Quick heads up for the closing segment: being life long New Yorkers, neither Impulse nor Rose actually drive.
----------------- Just finished the show and it was incredible. Like I thought, lots of transition, lots of furthering things along.
Congratulations to all the new champions (holding for spoilers), I think they were all well deserved. Thanks to Gregg as well for letting me go crazy with JJ and August, it was a lot of fun.
This really is the middle of Act II for the Windham Clan, I think - the theme of the night was escalation. Actually, literally the only thing I had issue with was that Troy took some hits. The rest of the clan: no problem, they're the soldiers. Troy is the puppeteer, and while I understand the reasons that he needed to be taken out for part of the show, there could've been a way for it to happen without him getting attacked. But that's small potatoes compared to the rest of the thing: this is what wrestling shows SHOULD be. One overreaching story with little subplots throughout.
