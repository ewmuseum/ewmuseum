Feature contains suggestions, language, situations and violence.’ A countdown timer rolls at the bottom of the screen as we go to pre-game promos.) (FADEIN: Shurguard Storage – South Side of Philadelphia, PA. A black Chevy Tahoe pulls up to unit #57983. Over the garage, the sunrise is just starting – an amalgam of orange and reds breaking over the housing projects and skyscrapers. QUENTIN SULLIVAN casually gets out of the passenger seat putting his black Armani shoes on the ground first. He’s looking sharp in the morning, a grey business suit and black tie. JIMMY, BILLY and ROBBIE – the three stooges of NFW Interns hop out of the Tahoe’s passenger doors, talking excitedly and playfully shoving each other. SULLIVAN pulls out a keychain with an unnecessary amount of keys on it. He grabs one key and flips the other keys away and opens the storage door. MILES lifts up the door to reveal a large amount of lighting rigs, scaffold pieces, signs, fireworks, you name it… stuffed into the locker.
