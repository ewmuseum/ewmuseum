Levond Daniels Biography Eye Color Brown Hometown Greensboro, North Carolina Birthday 02/23/1989 Height 6'2 Weight 217 Handedness Right Wrestling Style Martial Artist Finisher Move Reverse Tornado Kick Finisher Description Reverse Tornado Kick Finisher Setup Move Any strike capable of stunning the opponent. Setup Description Any strike capable of stunning the opponent. Other Moves Signature Moves: -Step Onto Thigh --> Knee To The Gut --> Elbow To The Back Of The Head --> Knee To The Face in quick succession before landing back on his feet. -Flapjack --> Kick To The Face -Double Underhook Repeated Knees -Figure Four Leg Lock (Only used if absolutely needed) Strikes: -Foot Stomp --> Leg Sweep -Knee Stomp -Low Kicks (Focused on the knee) -Corner Running Double Knee Lift Ring Entrances Daniels tends to stick to the same routine for his entrances. His music plays, a random beautiful 20 something girl comes out first, and Daniels follows closely behind. He stays in motion, always shifting from one foot to the other as he's escorted to the ring, sneaking a glance at the lady's backside as she climbs the stairs and holds the ropes down for him. Once he's in the ring, he gets serious and shifts in his corner and the young lady leaves ringside. Strengths Discipline: Daniels has spent most of his life learning discipline through martial arts. He brings this discipline into the ring with him and it helps to keep him calm and on his game plan no matter what the situation. Speed: While not as fast as some of the smaller wrestlers, his speed helps him remain outside of the grasp of stronger men. His speed also makes his strikes more difficult to avoid. Underestimated: Most wrestlers tend to believe their grappling trumps any striker. Levond has no problem allowing people to believe this is the case, and will even milk it to get the opponent's guard down. Weaknessees Inexperience: Unlike most wrestlers, Daniels doesn't have a background in wrestling of any sort outside of watching it on television. While he does have some idea of what to expect from opponents, a crafty wrestler can surprise him. Predictability: While he isn't a one trick pony by any means, taking a leg away from someone who relies on kicks majorly in his matches can mean a quick end to Daniels' strategy working. Size: An average sized man, if he's put into the ring with someone with a significant reach advantage or power advantage, they may be able to easily overwhelm him and prevent his strikes from having much effect from his safe distance. Ring attire Comfortable jeans and wrestling boots.
