Jason Payne — 19 April 2004 MEMBER INFO ---------------------- MEMBER Name: [name withheld] Email Address: [detail withheld] WRESTLER INFO ------------------- Wrestler Name: Jason Payne Height: 6'6" Weight: 275 Hailing From: Payneville, KY Handedness: Right Gimmick: While spending the majority of his wrestling career under the guise of an ex-military mercenary, (an image enhanced by the fact that Payne was dishonarbly discharged from the Navy for striking an officer) Payne's persona has gone through a major shift in the last few months. Gone are the days of the pseudo-camoflague pants and overtly military demeanor. These days, Payne seems quite comfortable with giving up all the pretense of wrestling under a "gimmick", and simply comes out and shoots right from the hip. What you see is what you get, and what you get is one tough talking, rough walking powerhouse who refuses to back down from anyone, or anything. Not caring for the politcal correctness of both society and the wrestling business, Payne is simply a man who is out to make ends meet, by any means neccessary. Sticking to a kind of personal Code of Honor, he has embraced the fans support, though he would never admit to being a fan favorite to anyone. Fan favorite or no, Payne tells it like it is, and in the ring, it's all business.
Ring Attire: Gone are the days of the psuedo-camo pants and "I Stand Alone" T-shirt. These days, Payne's wrestling attire consists of loose fitting black commando pants, with black wrestling boots. On his upper body, Payne wears a singlet with black on one side, and red on the other.
Looks: After his injury at the hands of Chris McMillan, Payne adopted a new fitness program, and the results have been extremely remarkable. Gone is the slight beergut and in it's place is a slightly wide, yet flat stomach, supporting an even wider chest. Payne has spent considerable time developing his upper body, and it shows as his neck, arms, chest, traps, are considerably larger than they once were. This is what has contributed mostly to the 30 pound weight gain since the last time he wrestled. He is just physically bigger than he once was. Also gone is the trademark flatop hairstyle associated with his military gimmick. These days Payne wears his hair down. Currently his dark borwn hair comes down to just above his shoulders. He has also grown a moustache and a goatee.
Ring Entrance: During the opening few seconds if his entrance music, there is a small pyrotechnic display. This display comes off as intense machine gun fire, ending with a concussion explosion in the middle of the ramp. After this, Payne will walk out onto the entrance ramp for a few moments to look over the crowd, before making his way down to the ring. As he walks towards the ring, he may occasionally reach out a hand to the fans, but this is a very uncommon occurence...ie if a show is near his hometown he might do that, but it is not a weekly thing.
Theme Music: "Dogs of War" - Pink Floyd Tactics/Style: Brawler/Hardcore. Payne's size and physical strength make him an ideal person for a streetfight, or hardcore situation. However in recent months, he has expanded his in ring knowledge and added a few more technical moves, and a couple of submission moves, to round out his brawler style.
Wrestling moves Payne is known to perform during a match. Bodyslam Running Powerslam Sidewalk Slam Vertical Suplex Standing Vertical Supplex Flying Clothesline Inverted Piledriver Spinning Heel Kick Shoulder Tackle Neckbreaker (usually done as a counter when coming off the ropes) Standing Elbow Drop Double Underhook DDT Back Elbow Smash Shoulderbreaker Russian Leg Sweep Spinebuster Submission/Weardown holds: Sleeper Camel Clutch Step-Over Toehold Half Boston Crab Bearhug Abdominal Stretch Signature Move: Anesthesia - This Cobra Clutch variation can be used as an alternate finisher if an opponent is physically unable to be put through the Payne Killer.
Finisher: Payne Killer (Full Nelson Slam. During the move, Payne does not drop to his butt, but keeps his footing as he slams his opponent to the mat. Most times Payne sets up his opponent by throwing them into the ropes first, but he can pull it out whenever he wants to)
Strengths: 1) An encyclopedic knowledge of wrestling 2) Has great physical power 3) Tends not to be distracted from the task at hand ie. beating his opponent. Weaknesses: 1) Injured neck - Payne suffered a severely injured neck during Week 5 of Season 1. Though not broken, his neck is in a weakened condition. Payne has tried to counter this by developing his traps and delts in order to better protect his neck.
2) His willfulness and "straight talkin" have found him at odds with the powers that be in many wrestling organizations. He has made many enemies in this business.
3) Top Rope - Payne utilizes a tried and true ground attack. Payne hardly ever uses the ropes, and when he does, it's always from the middle rope. The last time he attempted a top rope move was the night he was injured by "The Wolf". He is not likely to add any high risk moves to his offensive repetoir.
