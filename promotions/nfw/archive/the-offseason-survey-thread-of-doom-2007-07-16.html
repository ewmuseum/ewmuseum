The Guy — 19 July 2007 1) What are you digging so far in the offseason? I'll be honest, I have paid limited attention to the offseason thus far. I already know what I'm doing with Southern Baptist Coalition come the start of the season, so I've just been waiting for that.
2) What does Admin need to help you out with in NFW? Nothing much, I pretty much plan to go with the flow. 3) What questions are you asking about NFW? I don't have any 4) What needs to be done on the NFW forums or website? I agree that we have too many forums. Hopefully once the season starts we can cut back on some.
5) What are you looking forward to once v.3 begins? The diverse roster. 6) What does NFW need? I betting pool on who flakes out and when. :D 7) Do you like the challenge system? Seems fine. I won't use it until after a few shows, but others seem to enjoy it. 8) Should we close off applications? Not so much close as setting up a waiting list. The roster seems huge right now, but once the season starts and people start to drop out, it would be nice to quickly replace them.
9) What are your predictions for the championships in NFW? World-Nova. He's pretty much been gold since coming to FW. It's scary that he can go to nearly any league here and shoot straight to the top so quickly and go to any league in PTC and be a power hitter quickly as well. I wouldn't be a bit surprised to see him in Mat's angle fed and main eventing there as well.
Hybrid- I really haven't kept tabs on who is challenging for this belt, so I can't say. TV- I think the combination of Joe the Plumber and Shawn Hart are going to hold onto the belt for some time.
Tag- Honey I F**ked Your Mom. To me, they just scream what a NFW Tag Team would be like. Though it will be interesting to see them mix it up with more traditional tag teams as well.
10) General Comments/Feedback I would just like to say Geaux Saints and the Falcons can enjoy Joe Horn now that he won't have a quarterback to throw to him. :p
