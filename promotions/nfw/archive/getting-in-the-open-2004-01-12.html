Lady_Freedom — 12 January 2004 I've talked to a couple people on AIM who dissaprove of my roleplays. They didn't say my roleplays were bad, they just said they don't belong here. Now I was a guy named Shane Thompson for about 2 years, and this is my first time using this character. It got annoying doing the same ole over and over. I'm [detail withheld], I do get sick of things, lol.
So here is the thing: After All-Star Weekend, I have to wait 3 months to wrestle again. I will only change my character if forced to, but please, someone give me a general comment on Lady Freedom and her character.
QueenOfTheRing — 12 January 2004 I don't buy into the whole "my character is a legit superhero" thing. I don't believe she can "fly", and I think the "Millenium City" thing is incredibly hokey, especially with the references to the Fantastic Four, the Hulk, and the X Men.
I think she would be better suited in a Comic Book RPG. JABolich — 12 January 2004 We've spoken about this before, and I told you that your comic-book-vignette style of RPs will probably not go over well on this circuit because most people here are old-school NWA fans who grew up watching a wrestling-oriented product as opposed to modern WWE shock TV. In a nutshell, there are match RP threads posted for a reason - so you can promote the matches - and there's a separate general-RP board for a reason - so you can develop your character through vignettes. What you're basically doing right now, aside from playing an utterly ridiculous character (legit superhero my foot), is saying to Gregg through your RPs, "Screw you, I don't want to RP with you so I'm going to write all about me." Try giving your opponent something to work with by shooting dialogue back at him instead of just writing about how you're going to play with Jimmy the Alien Symbiont and mutate into a purple giraffe.
I'm not on staff here, but if this were my fed I'd be watching your RPs closely to see if you're someone who'd work out in the fed. As such, if I were YOU I'd be trying to impress someone, and I doubt you're going to do that by cutting the types of promos you're cutting now. Between your outrageously unbelievable gimmick and your complete lack of a single word spoken to your opponent, you're not impressing me, at least - and judging from Lindsay's response, you're not impressing others.
I'm not impressed in the least with what I've seen, especially since you refuse to take any advice that might help you better your character and RP style and help you get somewhere in FW. I made several suggestions to you and you basically told me to go f*ck myself. What sort of light do you think that casts you in?
Short and sweet: I don't like the character, I DEFINITELY don't like your self-centered RP style, and I'm VERY irked at your refusal to listen to those trying to help you.
Off to Valinor — 13 January 2004 This thread is being closed. This topic is best served in Backstage forums, rather than a league.
I'm sorry, but these types of discussions aren't needed on this board. Please continue any discussions regarding this topic through email, please. Lady Freedom's RPer -- I'm not sure if you asked for criticism or opinions, but perhaps to keep yourself happy, it might just be better to do your own thing and not ask what people think.
Why? Most people have their own opinions on what RPs should be. Hell, if you think you're the only RPer disapproved in NFW - you're sorely mistaken.
I'm probably ahead of you on most counts. Anyway, If I find any problems with your character being in NFW, I'll let you know. So will the other two co-prezzes. ...and as far as I know, Powermaster's handler has an interesting angle coming up during All-Star Week that we might be able to continue during some weeks of the season.
Anyway, point of NFW: Have Fun. Let's all keep it that way. Thank you.
