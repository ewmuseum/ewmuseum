jediPREZ — 24 August 2009 I don't think we need a backstory on why this thread is being created. As NFW moves towards the future, it's becoming easy to see that the regime cannot handle 85-90% of the show-writing, unless the roster likes shows coming out every 3 months.
Therefore, I would like anyone to sign up for the TV Show Street Team using these guidelines:
1) The idea of writing for CRASH TV excites you and makes you want to fornicate your significant others to relieve the tension.
2) When tasked with a highlights recap match, you will be able to provide something that is 1-2 pages MAX.
3) When tasked with a mid-card match, you will be able to provide a match that's realistic and entertaining within a 3-5 page MAX limit.
4) When volunteering for an upper-card match, you will be able to provide FIVE-STAR AWESOME SAUCE in a 5+ page format...and trying not to go crazy and hit a 15 page mark unless you're writing a designated 30 minute match.
5) You are better writing for TV than BRAWL, which needs to be taken in consideration. I'd like to limit the people on both teams, if possible.
6) It will not take you 1 month to write a match. If it would, you must note this in the relevant Lineup Threads once you sign up for this team.
7) Signing up for this team means you are volunteering for every forthcoming TV Show as long as you are on the roster. See #6 for any cases where you will need to take a break/miss a show.
Feel free to ask questions or voice concerns, thank you! CURRENT TEAM MEMBERS: JEDIPrez The Human Torch (Leary) BFF ([name withheld]) Karl Wan Kenobi (Karl, duh.)
