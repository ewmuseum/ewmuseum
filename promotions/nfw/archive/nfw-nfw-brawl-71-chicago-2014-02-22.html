There's no question that the number 30 entrant -- the very last -- is among the favorites to win, if not THE favorite. But with all reward, there is significant risk, and the catch to tonight's match was that the loser would be the FIRST entrant in the Rumble. Neither man could afford those odds being stacked against him, so they engaged in an intense dog-fight." DREWES: "At Reloaded 20, their cage match ended controversially. Many fans, and President Mayfield himself, were critical over the fact that Castor Strife was awarded the victory after being choked unconscious by Stryfe in that deadly chokehold he calls 'Fade To Black'. Regardless, the video replay showed Castor getting the cradle pinfall prior to passing out." CALDWELLER: "For the first time in NFW history, a competitor had to be woken up and informed that he won." DREWES: "Couple that with the re-emergence of Castor Strife's mentor, Stan Vick, who has publicly derided the former champion, and there was plenty of drama headed into tonight's Street Fight. Let's go to the tape to see how this one played out..." (CUTTO: As STRYFE makes his entrance, CASTOR appears from behind wearing an LA Kings #32 Quick jersey and his wrestling tights -- Chicago fans not thrilled with that! -- and holding a crowbar that he repeatedly strikes STRYFE with) DREWES: "And it began controversially, with Castor sneak-attacking Stryfe with a crowbar at the top of the entrance." CALDWELLER: "This was absolutely vicious. It's not secret that Castor wants Stryfe eliminated completely from wrestling, and views these matches as a burden. He wasn't interesting in competing here -- he was simply out for blood and injury." (Castor picks Stryfe up and TOSSES HIM off the entrance ramp onto the concrete floor! NFW security staff and officials spill out from the back to restrain Castor, including Head Referee Scott Speranza. Medical staff arrive to check on Stryfe) DREWES: "Here's where it got interesting. Apparently Head Referee Speranza took issue with the attack on account of the bell not ringing. There was talk of awarding the match to Stryfe if he was unable to continue." (Castor can be seen arguing with Speranza, who says "The bell didn't ring!" Castor yells back, "IT'S A GODDAMN STREET FIGHT!" Speranza says something about Castor cheating the fans) DREWES: "Not the start that we expected. Stryfe looked to be critically injured after taking that huge fall off the ramp. With Castor in the ring awaiting the outcome, it looked as if the match was over before it started. That was until... (Stryfe fights his way through several medical and security staff and heads towards the ring. Castor sees this and immediately slides underneath the ropes and runs up the ramp to cut him off!) CALDWELLER: "Until Stryfe came alive!" DREWES: "That's right! And it was an absolute barnburner from this moment on. Nobody could keep these two from having it out, and Speranza called for the bell so the match could officially start. Both men brawled it out in the aisle, and it wasn't long before they found themselves in the seats, surrounded by screaming fans." (CUTTO: Castor and Stryfe in the stands, encircled by fans, trading lefts and rights. Castor grabs a steel chair and dents it onto the forehead of Stryfe, who staggers but does not drop. He swings again, but this time Stryfe catches it, boots Castor to the mid-section, grabs the chair and returns fire with a shot heard 'round the arena) DREWES: "At times it looked like either man was finished, as the brutality envelope continued to get pushed." CALDWELLER: "The brutality envelope. I like that!" DREWES: "Castor's forehead opened up during the early juncture, after that HUGE chairshot from Stryfe. And perhaps the biggest pop came when Stryfe ripped that Los Angeles Kings jersey off of Castor." CALDWELLER: "Blackhawk Nation wasn't thrilled to see the black and silver of their Western Conference rivals, that's for sure. But what can you do? Castor is an LA native." DREWES: "Fans have recently criticized him for being somewhat of a 'celebrity wrestler', but there is no doubting his prowess or brutality in the ring, or in this case, THE STREET. And that's exactly where this fight would be taken." (CUTTO: After brawling in the lobby of the Allstate Arena, Castor kicks Stryfe through the entrance doors where he lands in front of the stairs. Stryfe gets up and immediately elbows Castor in the groin, then picks him up into a fireman's carry and drops him HARD onto the staircase, where he rolls down to street level) CALDWELLER: "Eddie said it would be a street fight, but he didn't mean it LITERALLY. That's the level of hate these two men have for each other -- they're willing to take this fight anywhere, any time, to any extreme." DREWES: "Stryfe is hell-bent on destroying Castor, and Castor wants this man out of his life for good. Two completely different motivations, working towards the same result. And Ted? This match became downright DEADLY." (After regaining control, Castor suplexes Stryfe onto the concrete of Lunt Avenue. He attempts to remove Stryfe's mask, but Stryfe fights tooth and nail to keep it on. Finally, Castor drags him up by the arms and HAULS HIM INTO ONCOMING TRAFFIC! A Toyota Camry slams on the breaks as Stryfe flips over the hood and onto the concrete) CALDWELLER: "There was massive concern for the health of both competitors, but nobody dared get in between them. Unfortunately, the driver of the car made that mistake by accosting Castor, who proceeded to assault the man and slam the door repeatedly into his body!" DREWES: "Remember, this is pinfalls count anywhere, and Castor would put that stipulation to the test!" (CUTTO: Castor body slams Stryfe onto the hood of the Camry, and climbs on top of the car's roof where he sets up Stryfe. Fans gather in the street, commotion ensues, and Castor NAILS STRYFE WITH A PILEDRIVER ON THE ROOF OF THE CAR!) DREWES: "Look at Stryfe's neck get DRIVEN into that car roof! The front windshield completely shatters from the weight of the two men dropping down on it with such force, and Castor covers the shoulders for the first pin of the match!" CALDWELLER: "Unfortunately, the referee had trouble getting through the crowd to make a timely count. By the time he got there, it was two-count only!" DREWES: "A little much needed luck for Stryfe, but the fight would continue..." CALDWELLER: "Damn right it did! These guys fought their way into the Retro Arcade and Classing Gaming Convention!" DREWES: "Feel the collective nerdgasm between gamers and wrestling fans..." (CUTTO: Castor choking Stryfe with a scepter he stole off a cosplayer, before ramming him headfirst into a METAL SLUG arcade machine) CALDWELLER: "Excuse the pun, Wendy, but this wouldn't stay a two-player game for much longer!" (Castor launches Stryfe over a comic book table, and just as he goes to followe him to the other side, he gets SPRAYED WITH A FIRE EXTINGUISHER! The man holding it is STAN VICK!) DREWES: "The legendary 'Scarecrow' Stan Vick showed up, only a week after sending out that video promo blasting Castor Strife for, among other things, 'disrespecting the business' and alienating Stan from his son. Remember, Stan Vick trained Castor as a young man -- he was Castor's mentor in the beginning!" CALDWELLER: "Not only that, but he revealed to the public that he trained Stryfe as well, who became sort of a second son to him. And it was Stryfe who abandoned Vick to pursue Castor." DREWES: "Well, Stan made sure that his new pupil, STRYFE, would regain the upper hand on the former champion." (Vick continues to spray Castor, as Stryfe gets up and walks over) VICK: "HOW DOES THAT FEEL, YOU F[BLEEP]KING BASTARD! GET UP AND FACE ME LIKE A MAN!" (Vick sprays him again, and this time Stryfe approaches. Vick offers him the fire extinguisher) VICK: "Bury this piece of [BLEEP], son! Do it like I taught you! GO ON AND DO IT!" (Stryfe grabs Vick by the neck and chokes him! A second passes, and Castor runs into and knocks both of them down! He grabs the fire extinguisher and drives it into the skull of Stan Vick, then into his spine) DREWES: "A truly odd and unexpected scene here in the Skyline Room convention center. It didn't appear like Stryfe wanted his mentor's help, and the legend Vick paid for his student's hesitation." CALDWELLER: "Luckily for Stryfe, Castor made the mistake of going after Vick with that extinguisher, instead of his opponent!" (As Castor lifts the extinguisher to crown Stryfe, he gets punched in the ribs and Stryfe takes the extinguisher away. He RAMS IT into Castor's face, and Castor falls to the floor. One of Jack Harmen's CHEMO CREW fans hands Stryfe a crutch, which he uses to BREAK over Castor's arm) DREWES: "The tides turned HARD on Castor Strife at this point!" (Stryfe sends Castor flying into the edge of a KISS pinball machine! After he does this, Stan Vick gets up groggily, face covered in blood, and falls into Stryfe who stops to help him up) VICK: "Ahhh, that bastard opened me up!" CALDWELLER: "Looks like Stryfe was a little concerned for his teacher after all!" DREWES: "And that proved to be a huge motivating factor for Stryfe, who ramped up the attack and didn't look back!" (Stryfe repeatedly slams Castor's head into the edge of the pinball machine, then proceeds to stomp him out. Stryfe drags Castor up by the hair, and sets him up!) VICK: "THAT'S RIGHT! KILL HIM! KILL THAT MOTHERF[BLEEP]ER! KILL HIM, BOY!" (Stryfe picks Castor up and POWERBOMBS HIM ONTO THE KISS PINBALL MACHINE! The entire machine breaks -- lights go out in Gene Simmons' face, glass is everywhere, the legs collapse -- and Castor is left lying in a grave of electronic dismemberment) DREWES: "THOUSANDS OF DOLLARS IN DESTRUCTION! Believe me, I checked eBay! Stryfe made the cover -- ONE, TWO, THREE -- and became the first man in NFW to earn a pinfall victory in singles competition over Castor Strife in over 10 years!" CALDEWELLER: "Some of those years were inactive, but regardless that is an astonishing number considering how active Castor has been in the last five. This man Stryfe, whoever he is, came out of nowhere and now holds a victory over one of the very best." (Stryfe stands up, slowly, and looks down at an unconscious Castor to admire his handy work. Stan Vick embraces him and raises his arm in victory) VICK: "You did it, son! Look down at that man! Do you see what that is?! That's a DEAD MAN! He was the best in the world! I trained him, and now I trained you, YOU BEAT HIM -- AND YOU'RE THE BEST NOW!" (Vick turns to the camera, and walks toward the viewer with blood still running down his face) VICK: "All you people at home! You may have forgotten me, but you will REMEMBER my adopted son for what he did here! And it's not over! We want the TITLE! He won't stop, HE DOESN'T KNOW HOW! Nobody, NO ONE, can keep him from destiny! CASTOR STRIFE IS DEAD! AND IF THAT MOTHERF[BLEEP]ER GETS UP, WE'LL KILL HIM TWICE!" (Still-frame of Stan Vick screaming and laughing into the camera, with Stryfe looming large in the background over Castor, who lays still atop the collapsed pinball machine. The screen shrinks out of the foreground, and we are BACK in the studio with Drewes and Caldweller) CALDWELLER: "Lamont Hollywood just texted me. He said nobody's been wrecked on a pinball machine like that since Jodie Foster." DREWES: "Awesome. So with Stryfe gaining the victory, we're left with some massive fallout headed into Random Rumble. Stryfe will be the very last entrant, which gives him a HUGE leg up on the rest of the field." CALDWELLER: "That's right. And Castor Strife will need the performance of his career to win, because he'll be the first entrant. Those are long odds for a man who is rightfully seen as one of the favorites." DREWES: "The cage match took a huge toll on both men, and tonight's street fight was even more severe. The Random Rumble isn't going to be any easier, but Stryfe saved his body a considerable amount of punishment by locking up that number thirty spot. Now, if anybody is capable of coming out first and being the last man standing, it's Castor Strife, but these odds are NOT good, Ted." CALDWELLER: "No they are not, but you can never count anybody out when you're talking about the talented field of competitors that NFW has assembled. There's a reason why it's called the RANDOM Rumble. Anything can happen!" DREWES: "Well, that's it till WE ARE THERE! CASTOR STRIFE may need to work on his cardio! SEE YA!" (FADETOBLACK)
