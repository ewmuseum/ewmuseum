NFW Brawl 17: Road to SUPERCRASH 2, 13 October 2008

13 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 17: Road to SUPERCRASH 2 13 Oct 2008 North Charleston Coliseum, North Charleston, South Carolina (seats 13,295) Battle Royal for National Championship Welcome to a special NFW Brawl with the duo of Calamiy and Sean Toombs commentating on their Hellmachine own this evening. NFW History runs deep in the state of South Carolina. It was in this state that Calvin Carlton broke all previous notions of racial undertones in the sport of American Wrestling. It was here that a black man waved the Confederate flag with an air of confidence that betrayed every redneck’s conception of life and the universe. Its taken years for the Klan to recover from this mindbending experience…so much so, they’re afraid of Barak Obama, but most of all…NFW came back with a cast of characters that would put Carlton’s conquest to shame. It was 1 match. In fact, it was 1 Battle Royale. A little more than a baker’s dozen arrived, primed to earn a right to challenge for Steve Knox’s National Championship. A clan of von Dooms, Alexandria Malone, Koopa, Chance Bojangles, P. King Duk, La Estrella 100%, Leroy Brown, The Cosmo Kid (not to be confused with the Hyperspace Kid, even though…I kind of am…) and a couple others that we’re not going to mention… …and some dude that Eddie Mayfield let come in late, but more on that one…later. Oh, god of Grammatical Repitition… you are cruel and wise. As the wrestlers showed up to the arena, they found out from Craig Miles that weapons would be allowed into the ring. Naturally, the von Doom Clan rejoiced by finishing off a keg of beer on their own. Fiona Love had to literally knock out a Horny Mathmetician while doing some pre-game press coverage. As the wrestlers made their way to the ring, fans were solely behind the single most positive force in Frontier History. P. King Duk, who brought his world-renowned kendo stick. Other weapons you ask? Ivan von Doom brought a Russian Chain, Darth von Doom wore his helmet and brought a metal pipe that he held like a lightsaber. The Horny Mathmetician brought a phallic looking abacus, while Leroy Brown brought the flag of Italy. Alexandria Malone brought nothing…proving once again that NFW finds all the femme fatales in the world that are so psychotic that they’ll walk into the Den of the Beast with the confidence that they’ll just kick everyone’s teeth. The Cosmo Kid on the other hand decided to bring a jug of Tang, while La Estrella sent Leroy Brown into a fervor as he brought the Flag of Mexico. Chance Bojangles walked down wearing a six-piece chair necklace and brass knucks. Koopa decided to SPIKE his shell. Why he didn’t do that for Kill the Koopa? Seriously…why? Simple rule for the Royale: Five refs at ringside scared for their lives, once a wrestler goes over the top rope and touches the floor – they’re out. The bell rang with Estrella and Brown immediately launching into a flag duel, their wood cracking…and then their backs cracking from Duk showing why he’s the People’s President, breaking his kendo stick over their backs! …that got a double-dose of Flag sticks into his grill and as he staggered around, Koopa hit a spinning spiked shell Iceman Parsons leaping butt attack to the face…and Duk flipped over the top rope and out of the ring. Meanwhile, the Horny Mathemitian hit a Lou Thesz Press on Malone, trying to insert that abacus somewhere…but he got smashed over the head from a Bojangles chairshot. Bojangles got a huge pop, probably supported by 10 cousins, 10 wives and 10 nieces all in the audience. Seriously, we gave him 10 family tickets to hand out…or so Calamity said. The Horny Mathemetician could solve that on his abacus if he were conscious. Malone kipped up and let Bonjangles feel her gratitude with a spin kick to the face. Bojangles bumbled away and the Mathmetician found himself spiked into the mat with a Malone hurricanrana. Von Doom and von Doom started double-teaming The Cosmo Kid who opened his jug and let the contents on Tang fly. I’ve never seen two guys go down like their face was burning with acid and Kooter Cruise placenta fluid…that is until tonight. Both men rolled under the ropes and out of the ring, demanding fire extinguishers to get blasted in their face. Cosmo Kid hit a legsweep, while Malone hit a clothesline and I think the Horny Mathemetician just got long-divisioned out of that. Leroy Brown charged them with the flag of Italy, but got double dropkicked out of the ring. Estrella went for a Reverse Hurricanrana on the stumbling Bojangles and then he got double dropkicked by the Cosmo Kid/Malone connection…and he flew out of the ring. Bojangles turned around into a double Superkick, which provided the Koopa enough time to hit a spinning spiked shell smash into the backs of Cosmo and Malone. Both of them fell to their knees as the von Dooms rolled into the ring, their faces dusted white and charged the Koopa. It helped that Koopa had one of his spikes stuck in the mat, allowing them to begin a stomp brigade that would make the Italian Mob, Los Angeles Police Department and Redneck Ho-Down ministers scream, "DAYUM!" The Horny Mathemetican stood up wobbly and ate a Bojangles chairshot, placing him against the ropes – primed, multipled and square rooted for a huge freaking clothesline that spun him out of the ring. Bojangles celebrated by crowning Ivan von Doom with a chairshot…and then eating a Metal Pipe blast from Darth. As Darth went to the woodshed with that pipe, Alexandria Malone double stomped the Koopa and used the momentum to connect with a bulldog that made Darth’s helmet explode. …now the Koopa was REALLY stuck. The Cosmo Kid grabbed a Flag and hit a freaking Pole Vault Senton Splash onto the Koopa. …now, he’s MOTHERF’N STUCK. Cosmo Kid gets up and eats a leg lariat from Malone as Ivan von Doom ducks her. Malone turns around with a Russian chain clothesline from the Bear of a von Doom, or was it the von Doom birthed by a Bear? Sean Toombs contemplates this equation and needs to sober up by smoking more out of his bong, or so he says. Bojangles gets up and eats a chain clothesline. Cosmo gets up and eats a chain clothesline. Koopa can’t get up and is kicking his legs like a 4-year old getting the motorboat tickle on the tummy. Malone eats a chain clothesline. Ivan climbs the turnbuckles and starts singing a song about Mother Russia and Darth von Doom completely sells him out. Metal pipe to the back, Ivan flies out of the ring and lands on his head. We may have killed a Russian, I’m not sure. Darth takes a chair from Bojangles, but some returns a pipe shot. Chair, Pipe, Chair, Pipe, Chair, Pipe…so this is what will happen when Joe the Plumber conceives a baby with Emperor Palpatine, only to watch it immortally duel against the birthing of Super Mario and Moondog Rex. I can see it now: The Redneck Plumbing Revolution Strikes Back III…Y’ALL! Koopa gets up FINALLY and charges the both of them, Bojangles dodges and the momentum of Koopa sends him and Darth OVER the ropes and out of the ring. Of course, no Sith Lord of the Darkness is going to take THAT. Darth went right back into the ring and a rampage. He started cracking Bojangles with his pipe from behind to the point, where Toombs hoped police wasn’t in the audience. Cosmo Kid tried to pull him off and took his own pipe shot to the skull and then got rag-toll tossed over the top rope by von Doom’s free hand. Of course, his mistake wasn’t keeping an eye on Alexandria Malone who flew up with a kneestrike into his head that knocked him silly, into Bojangles and both of them over the top rope. Steve Knox’s soul probably plummeted under the circumstances and relationship he has with Malone, but again…we said we’d comment on something later and maybe its time we did… HEY THAT’S JEEVES! Ever see a woman get crowned over the head with a sterling silver serving tray? Well, if not…you should go to an NFW show someday. Of course, Jeeves isn’t a wrestler and we’re talking about a Malone kid – a family that’s steeped in wrestling. So, she turned around and blasted him in the face with a Superkick. Which allowed… HEY THAT’S LORD COYNER POLLARD FLANKED BY INSURGENT PATROL! …to catch her from behind and deliver a devastating BOLEYN SISTERS DRIVER! Malone twitched, Lord Coyner Pollard threw her over the top rope and out of the ring. Pollard was handed the British Flag and drew a full house of jeers as he was announced as a competitor under DIPLOMATIC MAYFIELD RULES…and winner of the National Championship title shot in the same sentence. Pollard waved the flag, fans throwing trash in the ring. That angered him enough to get the microphone and run down the crowd, the wrestlers in the locker room and the National Champion himself. Guess… Just guess… HEY THAT’S STEVE KNOX! HE’S RUNNING! HE’S GOT A TWO TITLE BELTS CRADLED…AND WHAM-BAM-HOLY GODDAMN! Pollard eats a double title smash, Insurgents fly out of the ring in fear as Knox drops the belts and waits for Pollard to get up… GOLD RUSH LARIAT! Pollard flies out of the ring, Knox picks up the British Flag and breaks it over his knee. He straps his titles on Gladiator style as Jeeves tends to Pollard, who’s fuming! Malone steps back in the ring, standing beside Knox while holding her head in pain…and from the looks of it, not exactly enthralled. Credits Battle Royal for National Championship - Written by
