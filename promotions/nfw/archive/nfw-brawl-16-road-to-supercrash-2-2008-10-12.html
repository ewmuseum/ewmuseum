NFW Brawl 16: Road to SUPERCRASH 2, 12 October 2008

12 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 16: Road to SUPERCRASH 2 12 Oct 2008 New Orleans Arena, New Orleans, Louisiana (seats 18,500) Introduction There is dichotomy afoot. It is strange. It is bubbling. The chaotic structure that forms the French Quarter does nothing to quell the schizophonic, psychoponic nature of this New Frontier. Something changed in Florida. A line drawn opposite against the failing capitalistic foundations…against the backdrop of an organically grown wrestling circus asylum. But who am I to talk in mirrors? I’m part of Craig Miles’ non-profit internship program…and I’m on a lot of drugs…and alcohol. NEW ORLEANS ARENA That’s how we bold in the matrix to secretly segue into our purpose. Our process that cannot be undone, no matter how many of our inhabitants choose to rail against its existence. TWENTY-THOUSAND PEOPLE We begin with a congregation of our cavalcade of traveling fans for the SUPERCrash cannonball run, folded against the swirling mindset of New Orleans WRESTLING fans. I capitalize for I find this important. I wonder if the government would estimate that 12% were missing at least one tooth. Which side would Vegas take? FOR WE ARE MANY Hahahaohohohoho, I do not belong to a cult. I do not mean to startle you. There’s this guy named LEGION (caps important!) that shouldn’t be confused with the various 6,897 variations of said name in tonight’s industry. But he interrupts our musical intro. He interrupts WILDSTAR who is standing in the ring, ready to introduce the show as part of a Katrina charity pitch. (Legion may or may not known of this beforehand.) LEGION, however, says…well, he smells von Doom. I’ve quickly summarized a soliloquy of sh*t references that make my nostrils bristle. WILDSTAR tries to interrupt…and we then learn we have other commentators. Sean Toombs and Calamity welcome over the radio airwaves. Hi! (high? Hi-Fi?) ENTER TSUNAMI Not the natural disaster kind, mind you. The Japanese Wrestler variety that currently works for New Frontier Wrestling (YAY!) that used to tag with Wildstar (YAY!) and is walking out with the empowering Rayne (YAY!) but without DC Stratton (he’s got boo-boo’s.) A tongue-lashing was laid down from Legion and responded to by the Art House Queen. Legion vs. Tsunami Electrify yourselves. The fast-break breakneck crackhead speed stylings of New Frontier during the summer segued straight into the opening match in N’Awlins. After the verbal exchanges between Legion and Rayne, Tsunami finally lost his cool and went on the banzai attack. Chops, kicks, Chops, kicks…Legion broke the cycle with a rake of the eyes, knee to the gut and toss of Tsunami outside the ring. Conspicuously missing was Luci from the onset, which didn’t seem to matter as Legion gave the tag team wrestling legend a singular and personal tour of the ringside area steel furniture and boundaries. Back to the ring, Legion worked a few suplexes and went for a DVD, which ‘Nami sidewinded out into a modified swinging DDT. Wildstar was loving it and Legion took a backflip kick to the face. Impressive, most impressive. ‘Nami went to the top and that’s when we first saw Malik Anderson walking out. That distracted the Dangerman long enough for Legion to take him off with an fireman’s slam, subsequently following with a middle rope double stomp. The crowd buzzed and sawed while Malik started maliciously eyeing Rayne…and then Calvin Carlton slowly walked out behind him looking around nervously. Wildstar started talking some smack while Calamity called him Lee Von Cleef. Legion with a stomachbreaker and shoulderblock cannonball run in the corner. Irish Whip and reversed into the back handspring elbow from ‘Nami. ‘Nami bounced off the ropes for the bulldog, but Calvin pulled his leg almost tripping him. ‘Nami turns around, but takes a running high knee and falls out of the ring. Legion distracts ref and Malik goes to work until DC Stratton runs out and chases off Malik and Carlton for the moment…Legion works the Dangerman outside the ring, rolls him back in and catches him with a springboard dropkick. Legion tries to powerbomb Tsunami into the turnbuckles, but gets twisted inside out from a Hurricanrana. Crowd goes wild, Stratton (wearing long sleeves on a warm evening, hmmm?) pounds the apron and Tsunami climbs the ropes. Calvin on the apron, DC rushes the ring and we’ve got enough chaos to allow Malik time to pull out ‘Nami’s leg. OUCH. He’s cross-legged on the top, Legion rushes over and takes him down to the valley in a Superplex. ENTER BLAINE HOLLYWOOD! Like a blinding flash of light, he’s running down the aisle and he’s got a…is that a freaking dragonboat paddle?! He crushes DC in the shoulder from behind into the bad shoulder and we’ve got the HWC smelling blood. The ref doesn’t know what to do, its outside the ring and all of a sudden…Rayne is running out with a fire extinguisher! She blasts the whole scene, which has them all completely engulfed in a thick fog – running out behind, on the other side of the crowd? ENTER THE FREAKING VON DOOM. That’s James von Doom. He’s got a chair! Ref trying to sort out the Smoke on the Water reprise as HWC are stumbling around blindly as DC grabs the extinguisher and blasts Carlton in the piehole, sending him up and over into the crowd! CRACK! Von Doom nails Tsunami with the chair! Tosses it at Legion’s feet and rolls out of the ring as Legion goes wide-eyed in WHERE THE F*CK DID YOU COME FROM? The bell’s ringing, the ref now assuming Legion swung the chair! The crowd goes wild, Stratton and Rayne are how the hell did that happen? Security comes running out for von Doom, who’s back through the crowd before you spell Curiosity without the YOU and…well…HWC of course, sideswipe Stratton as he’s distracted and go on the attack again! They’ve got him pinned down, stomping the bad shoulder and ‘Nami FLIES! SPLIT LEGGED DROPKICK SPRINGBOARD FREAKING PLANCHA! HOLY BUDDHA! Security separates the factions, Stratton clutching his arm as Blaine screams he’s sending him back to his cardboard box! Moral of the story? VON DOOM SUCKS. Winner: Tsunami by DQ Support the Koopas Back Home The STARtron sparked to life as a video of an animated KOOPA destroying New Orleans with his fire breath…and yeah, that went over well. The real (can we call him real?) Koopa walks out with a microphone and immediately starts screaming about von Doom. …and yeah, that went over well. He demands a no holds barred match on the spot or his match against Legion at SUPERCRASH II will be MIRED in KOOPA VENGEANCE. I’m only typing in capitals because how much he loudly he shrieked. KOOPA’s in the ring at this point…and waits. Von Doom’s not coming. Then he starts listing off an address and phone number…next thing we know…VON DOOM’s coming back out from the crowd! He rushes the ring and tackles Koopa from behind! He starts mounting him shell-top style and wailing away with roundhouse punches! …we’ve got a ref? Seriously? This is going to happen? Ugh. Koopa vs. Von Doom Someone should die for this. I’m just not sure who. Let’s hope its someone in this match. Von Doom’s hanging onto the shell as Koopa stands up and gets slammed back into the turnbuckles. Koopa starts doing the Mashed Potato and then a Chubby Bowser Twist as the shell crushes the screaming von Doom in the corner. IRISH WHIP INTO A VAHALLA SHELL SPLASH! Von Doom teeters and totters out into a faceplant, KOOPA DEATHLOCK! KOOPA DEATHLOCK! THE SHELL IS CRUSHING VON DOOM’S SPINE! HE GETS THE ROPES! NO HOLDS BARRED, BABY! Koopa doesn’t have to break it, so von Doom pulls himself out of the ring to force Koopa to release. Koopa can’t figure out how to get outside the ring, finally trying to duck through the ropes…but he gets COMPLETELY STUCK. Von Doom gets a chair and starts wailing away on his dome! Koopa’s STILL stuck! Von Doom flips him around and pulls him through the ropes, so he’s hanging out 50/50…von Doom on the apron. SPRINGBOARD OFF THE ROPES! VERTICAL SPLASH ON KOOPA’S FACE! VON DOOM GRUNDLE IN THE MOUTH! THE HUMANITY, OH THE HUMANITY! That takes both to the cement, von Doom searches under the ring and pulls out duct tape and starts wrapping up his fist reversi sticky-side up…which may actually to prove to be more sanitary than his bare hands, so I don’t know where he’s going with this. I’ll maintain my position as he starts rolling the hand under the ring…picking up random nails and splintered wood… Von Doom stands up and steals someone beer bottle…stomps it on the ground, oh boy…he’s got a glass-covered hand. The next part is bloody. The next part is grounds for…I don’t know what in a no-holds barred match. But let’s just say there’s more than one way to skin a turtle. After about one minute of von Doom making potato splices, the ref gives in before vomiting. Winner: VON DOOM via ref stoppage ENTER LEGION! SUH-WEEEEEEEEEEEET. (Kill me now.) SUCK ON IT BUNYAN! Legion’s running out with a barbed-wire right fist and von Doom’s egging him to get there faster. GLASS HAND! WIRE HAND! GLASS HAND! WIRE HAND! …and then they try to start digging the hand into each other’s eyes. …these two are normal. For sexually abused pitbulls that haven’t been neutered or fed in 3 days. Security’s rushing out, stopping them before someone loses an eye. I’m serious. We’ve got a bowl of tomato soup on the ground at this point. NOW SOMEONE GET ME A GRILLED CHEESE SANDWICH! Chance Bojangles vs. Lord Coyner Pollard And now into a complete shift in what we’ve just witnessed. Lord Coyner Pollard will now lead us in a 15 minute oration of how the French Caribbean roots of this section of the universe is only proof of the existence of God’s love for England. Nothing like using Catholic overtones to tell New Orleans deserved what it had wrought in Mother Nature. They love that. Seriously, if my liver ends up on eBay after this place riots, I’m going to sue this bastard. ENTER CHANCE BOJANGLES! Ok, maybe I don’t even know who that is, but god bless Wildstar for knowing (even though he can’t stop harping about how Calvin Carlton is the REAL devil in this federation…for like the WHOLE SHOW.) …where was I? Daydreaming? Chance Bojangles! Yeah, I don’t think his parents had the right idea either. Olympic wrestling outfit with a cowboy hat? Check. …YEAH, I KNOW THAT’S A CHECK! Two large golden dice being shook literally and not as a metaphor? Check. A man of the people grabbing the microphone and telling off the rich bastard? Check. Well, you know New Frontier Wrestling…that means a bell rings, a referee at ringside is like "DUDE! I was on water break, don’t start the match! OH CRAP, THEY’RE LIKE TOTALLY BEATING THE SH*T OUT OF EACH OTHER!" And Wildstar is of course going, "Pollard should stop complaining about the closed fist and realize he’s in a street fight!" And Calamity is going "I MAY BE DRUNK, BUT WILL YOU LIFT UP YOUR TOP FOR TEN DOLLARS AND A FREE JOE THE PLUMBER GREASY GOBLIN GOBBLER T-SHIRT?" And some chick who’s drunk and possibly on Meth says "YOU LIKE SILVER HOOPS!?" And Sean Toombs hits with the punchline: "DUDE. …THAT WAS COOL." Right hands are flying of Bojangles’ blackjack fists like crazy 8’s being spooked by a spades gang! Pollard goes for forearm counters which don’t work too well against a man not above using a quick jabbing fist to the only white teeth shining in the United Kingdom. Bojangles with an Irish Whip, a chest-slapping clothesline and we’ve got the first pin attempt that gets a quick two! Pollard rolls up to his feet, still complaining about the fists and eats one right in the mouth with Pollard reacting by rolling out of the ring! Pollards starts shouting about his Diplomatic Immunity and the ref warns Bojangles about using the fists any further in the match. The crowd starts booing as Bojangles rolls his eyes, pushes the ref aside and rushes out of the ring. Pollard starts dashing away, Bojangles gives chase as Pollard slides in the ring and catches Bojangles in the trap. Stompity Stomp Stomp Stomp as Bojangles doesn’t get up in time, when he follows suit and Pollard raises his up to his feet…kisses his forearm and cracks Bojangles in the jaw. Bojangles staggers back into the ropes as Pollard plays to the crowd, pointing to his forearm and then his brain. Of course, Bojangles fires out of the ropes with a bevy of right hands until the ref gets in between him and Pollard trying to stop the fists, allowing Pollard to sneak in an eyepoke of DOOOOOOOOOOOM~! Bojangles hops around like his eyeball flew into the sixteenth row of the upper decks and gets vaulted up……and DOWN! Back Suplex by Pollard and the Vegas native is CRAPPED OUT. Pollard starts working the back with elbows, stomps, knees…a couple of suplexes rolled into a CRADLE! Two count, but nothingmore. Pollard slaps his hands three times, asking the referee if his Americans are so behind, they can’t count to 3. Bojangles rolls him up out of nowhere, but just gets the two and a kitchen sink knee as they both rush to their feet. Pollard segues to a gutwrench suplex and another near fall, which only has him lambaste the ref even more. Wildstar’s wondering if Pollard could’ve hit three more moves in the time he’s counting to three for the ref. Calamity is taking shots with a chick every time Pollard says Queen. Uh-Oh…she’s puking. "DUDE. THAT WAS COOL." Thanks Toombs! Pollard turns around into a surprising double leg takedown by Bojangles, followed by a high-elevation catapult and the crowd MARKS the steel-post noggin’ knocker that Pollard takes! He’s walking around like Dickens on a Boddington’s Bender, facepalms the mat and flops around like some raw fish and chips. Pollard’s pain is Bojangles’ splendor as he woozily catches the Lord with a dropping jawbreaker as both men stagger to their feet. Bojangles picks up Pollard cracks a few boots to the gut, followed by a kneelift to the face. Pollard’s stunned sent packing into the turnbuckles and Bojangles flies in with an avalanche elbow smash – then he proceeds to mount the turnbuckles and start blasting away with closed fists. The ref tries to stop him due to Pollard’s Diplomatic Immunity, Pollard uses the distraction to hit an Inverted Atomic Drop! Pollard goes for a Piledriver, but Bojangles counters with a sunset flip for a near fall. Both men spin up, Bojangles hits a right…a wheelhouse right, goes for a third and the ref stops him again! This time, Bojangles plants the ref one, but he’s got his back turned to Pollard who quickly hoists him up… BOLEYN DRIVER. Ouch. Fans boo incredulously as the pissed off ref makes the three count, shaking his head at the unconscious Chance as Pollard raises his arms in victory. Security has to take Pollard back as he’s pelted with every single beverage cup in the arena. Winner: Lord Coyner Pollard Grand Impulse Decision Impulse makes his way out after Pollard’s shielded all the way back to his limo. Impulse apologize for the "Lord of Overdone Heel Heat mechanisms," which is when he starts reminding people about Shane Southern’s local Katrina charity and Katrina.Org. Hey…THAT’S HIGH FLYER’S MUSIC! And we’ve got snow falling from the rafters as Flyer walks out and starts mouthing off about how Impulse needs to stop with playing the nice-guy because that’s not how he’ll be able to beat Doctor Curiosity at SUPERCRASH. Impulse shoots back, "Well, I beat you. I think I’ll be alright." Crowd lets Flyer have it, who squints in the ring. He goes on about how he doesn’t have a match tonight… Impulse shoots back, "No match? Why are you here then, Jack? Miles needed a driver?" Bang-a-gong, and we’re on. High Flyer vs. Impulse Next thing we know, we’ve got punches flying at each other. Flyer taking control with the more anger on his side. After tossing Impulse around the ring and connecting with a clothesline, the challenger was able to offset the aggression with an armdrag followed by a dropkick which sent Flyer sprawling out of the ring. …and that’s when the bell rang. Flyer slid back in and put his jaw in line for another dropkick. Impulse used his agility to keep Flyer off-balance with a series of elbow smashes, then an Irish Whip into a spinning leg lariat. As Impulse bounded off the ropes once more, Flyer used his veteran skills to flip Impulse over the top rope with a back body drop. The crowd booed as Flyer smiled, while holding up mock devil horns and bounced off the ropes himself then smacked Impulse with a nice looking sliding dropkick. The impact sent the NFW rookie into the barricade, which Flyer used to knock the kid’s head into several times. After rolling him back in the ring, Flyer capitalized with a series including an Implant DDT and Reverse Neckbreaker. Flyer scaled the ropes and went for a Swandive Headbutt, but Impulse managed to move in the nick of time. The fans roared as Impulse started regaining the upperhand, but Flyer managed to eke out a Jawbreaker which stopped him in his tracks. With Impulse staggering around, Flyer went for his Yakuza Kick and Impulse dodged out of the way. Flyer turned around into a Roaring Elbow, which put him on the ropes…and Impulse clotheslined him over. Gimme a Plancha with a side of fans marking please! Impulse delivered and all things looked lost for Flyer…and the fact that Doctor Curiosity was running down the aisle to a mixed reaction, probably was not in either Flyer nor Impulse’s interests. Curiosity had the Curious Mallet in hand and went charging at Impulse, his upcoming possible opponent at Supercrash. Somehow and someway, Impulse countered with a drop toehold which caused Curiosity to land squarely with his head in Flyer’s nuts. Impulse managed to roll in the ring and an obviously pained Flyer stumbled through the ropes to avoid the countout…only to receive a smacking Superkick on his jaw and suffer a three-count. Curiosity cursed and stormed around ringside, until Flyer came to incensed and chased him backstage. Winner: Impulse Steve Knox (c) vs. Cameron Cruise Hey is that Cameron Cruise’s music!? Well, that’s Wildstar is telling me, so I must listen. Cruise comes out and starts demanding to know what the hell is going on with what is his rightful World Championship opportunity…and if he’s not going to leave the ring until he gets it right there and then. He also makes a joke about Brock Alyas’ opportunity being on permanent parole and being unable to find him at the neighborhood K-Mart working the night shift. Hey is that Steve Knox’s music!? Apparently, the AWESOME National Champion knows that Nova is not in the building tonight. But he’s more than willing to give a title shot to Cruise right there and then. Cruise can dig it and accepts. Wildstar goes nuts, Calamity is nuts, Toombs smokes buds and I die a little more inside as someone makes me chug some whiskey. Knox comes out wearing his Elite Championship and National Championship, Gladiator style…which earns some style points on my end. Bell rings and were off with a collar and elbow lock up. Cruise started off quickly cinching in a side headlock, which was countered by a Knox back suplex. As both got to their feet, Knox hooked in a side headlock, which was you guessed it – countered with a back suplex. Both men roll up and crack each other kills with running forearm smashes. This switches a forearm smash exchange that finally gets Calamity on board with these two, while Wildstar notes the complete lack of scientific wrestling the two are using. Forearm smashes turn to right hand fists, stereo kicks the chest knocks each other back and both men dropkick each other. Crowd applauds as both roll to the their feet as Knox turns Cruise to the mat with an armdrag takedown, only to get himself locked in a headscissors. Knox flailed his legs as Toombs noted that the deepest, darkest secrets of this Earth were clamped around Knox’s head. Considering he’s the father of Kooter Cruise, I’m not gonna lie…I thought Knox was going to tap right there. I mean, c’mon Mercedes and Beau too? Knox powers out and latches Cruise back into a side headlock. Cruise goes for a back suplex, but Knox uses the momentum to flip over and hook him with an Oklahoma Roll…but it gets reversed, Cruise only gets a two count. Both get up and Cruise walks right into a Belly to Back Overhead Suplex. Wildstar starts drooling as Knox gives Cruise a history lesson in Suplexes starting with a Vertical, then a German into a Bridging Dragon for a near pinfall. The Awesome One thinks its just about over, so he gets geared up for the Gold Rush Lariat only for Cruise to dodge out of town. Knox hits the turnbuckles, whirly-birds around in a daze right into an STO. Crowd goes nuts as Knox just kicks out at two. Cruise hooks him…BRAINBUSTAH! Another two count. Cruise goes up top and Knox gets up…Missile Dropkick connects and Cruise is just about ready to win his first title in NFW. And that’s when some strange, tribal music starts blaring. And guess who runs out? "VOODOO" VON DOOM. (one ugly mix of von Doom, Papa Shango, a New Orleans Voodoo Priest and Eartha Kitt.) He rampages down with enough cloves of garlic hanging around his neck to unearth the corpse of Dom DeLuise (is he really dead?) and immediately crushes the ref with a clothesline and charges Cruise who sidesteps him. Voodoo von Doom hits the buckles, Cruise lifts him up and hits an Inverted Atomic Drop. And now we’ve got LEGION. Legion nails Cruise from behind with a forearm sending him almost over the top rope, but he manages to pull himself back in…this is while Legion pulls out some nasty looking rag and tries to choke out von Doom. Cruise dropkicks him in the back of the head, Legion’s out of the ring. He stomps von Doom out of the ring. Knox is looking shaky, Cruise hits him with a boot to the gut and goes for the SHIPWRECK (Cradle Piledriver) only for Knox to kick his legs and somehow knock Cruise onto his back with him on top! No ref, but Cruise managed to bridge out and turn it over…Knox back body drops out of the second Shipwreck attempt. Cruise gets up and literally out of nowhere, BLAM! GOLD RUSH LARIAT. Knox covers, the groggy ref makes the 3 count and that’s the show. Well, aside from Cameron Cruise tearing apart the ringside area in frustration afterwards. He does not seem like a happy camper. He seems like a Voorhees camper now. Wildstar sounds concerned, Calamity sounds happy…and well, Toombs as usual…sounds stoned.
