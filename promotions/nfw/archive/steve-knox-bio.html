Steve Knox Biography Eye Color Blue Nicknames Captain Awesome, the Awesome One, Mr. Elite (no longer used on account of no longer being Elite Champion) Hometown Providence, Rhode Island Birthday April 2nd, 1985 Height 6'3 Weight 242 Handedness Left (punches with right; Gold Rush with either) Looks Steve Knox is relatively tall and relatively muscular, probably similar in size and build to John Cena, except he's two inches taller than Cena. Steve is not a steroid user, though his arms have some good enough muscle mass to make it entirely believable that he could rip your head off with one of his lariats. He's very white, and he doesn't really tan all that much. He's also fairly young (he'll be 24 by April 2009), so his skin still looks pretty fresh. Steve has several noticable scars. There's a thin scarred line across the back of his left forearm, going vertically from the wrist and ending midway. There are a pair of small dot-like scars on the left side of his ribcage. Finally, there's a scar along his left eyebrow, it's very noticable and it's sort of similar to the scar that Spike has in Buffy the Vampire Slayer. Steve's pretty clean-shaven, generally speaking. However, he does a tattoo. It's a tattoo of a Chinese dragon (a serpent-like dragon as opposed to dragons of European lore) that coils around his right arm. The tail starts at his wrist and twists around his arm once before concluding at his shoulder with the dragon's head. He looks like a combination of a tough guy and a movie star. I probably mentioned the scar on his eyebrow already. He also has a chiselled jaw, a kind of a large nose, an average-sized mouth, and a large forehead that's mostly covered on the sides by his hair. Steve has brown hair that goes down a little past his ear. If I had to pick anyone with a similar hairstyle, it might be William Regal. His hair covers his ears at times and can seem a bit messy, but since it's cut the way it is, it's clearly an intentional look on Steve's part. Steve has blue eyes. Probably more of a subtle blue, in fact, which is to say that his eye colors aren't overwhelmingly blue. Fed Titles NFW National Championship Fed Awards NFW Wrestler of the Month in June Biography Steve is the second son of Earl Knox, a professional wrestler turned actor who died in 2001 in an accident on the set of his third movie. A mainstay of the Squared Circle since 2005, Steve has been wrestling around the independants since he was 16. A man who literally eats, sleeps, and drinks the business, Steve has put professional wrestling first at the cost of friends and family, and he's begun to feel the effects of such a lifestyle. He has few friends and nearly all of them are fellow wrestlers or people in the business. He barely has a good relationship with his brothers, Simon Knox and Soren Knox, who are also wrestlers in the business. With eight years of professional experience and still only twenty-four years old, Steve Knox aspires to be nothing but the greatest and most awesome athlete there ever was. Other Feds tSC, PRIMETIME Central Community Other Fed Titles tSC Champion(3), tSC Evolution Champion(2), tSC Tag Team Champion(3 w/Steven Silver, Rory Hayes, and Inoue Doi), PTC Elite Championship Wrestling Style Technical power Trainer Earl Knox / Jeremy Odessa Finisher Move 1) The Gold Rush, 2) The Gold Standard, 3) The Double Standard Finisher Description 1) Lariat, 2) Crossface chickenwing, 3) Crossface chickenwing suplex (rarely used) Finisher Setup Move None Setup Description Because his finishers come out of the NIGHT to kill you. Other Moves Early: Double leg takedown, fireman's carry takedown, knee lift, one-handed scoop slam, back body drop, armdrag, arm wringer, drop toe hold, snap suplex, elbow strikes, punches. Middle: Belly-to-belly suplex, double underhook suplex, powerslam, stungun, hard powerbomb, delayed vertical suplex, DDT, military press drop (against smaller opponents, he'll do reps), back suplex, Exploder suplex, pendulum backbreaker, German suplex (w/bridge), barrage of elbows in the corner that ends with a dropkick, repeated short-arm shoulderblocks ending with a clothesline, Boston crab, figure four leglock, figure four leglock in the post, rear choke, stomps, shoulderbreaker, Fujiwara armbar, single arm DDT. Late: Tombstone piledriver, gutwrench powerbomb, flying forearm, repeated back suplexes, capture suplex, belly-to-belly double arm suplex, Northern Lights suplex, half nelson suplex, superplex, belly-to-belly superplex (often done while running up the turnbuckles), brainbuster, dragon suplex (w/bridge), overthrown German suplex that lands the opponent chest-first, wrist clutch leg cradle backdrop suplex, wrist clutch fisherman's suplex Entrance Music 'Hysteria' by Muse Ring Entrances The bass guitars of "Hysteria" will start, followed by the distorting gutiars. The song begins in full at the 20 second mark, and that's when Steve comes out of the entryway. He waits at the entryway about ten seconds and then raises his arms, with gold pyro shooting off behind him. Steve then walks down to the ring, leaping up onto the apron and stepping into the ring. Strengths 1) Confidence 2) Size and strength 3) Willing to take advantage of his environment Weaknessees 1) Incurred several injuries over his career 2) Not a strong mat wrestler (thinks it's "kinda gay") 3) Can be frustrated or distracted Ring attire Steve does not wear a shirt. He wears a black elbow pad on his left arm, which he'd tear off when he's ready to deliver his lariat. His right arm doesn't have one. Steve wears black trunks with white lettering reading "KNOX" on the front, which is surrounded by what I'll call an "aura of lightning". That's to say that there's a glow around the lettering (like if you were to surround the letters with black shadow and then a thin layer of white, that'd be what I'm talking about) and then lightning coming out of it. He wears tall black boots and a pair of black kneepads, and they'll almost seamlessly go together. There's nothing fancy about his foots or his kneepads. Theme Music "Hysteria" by Muse
