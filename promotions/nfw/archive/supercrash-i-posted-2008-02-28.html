Joe, The bastard child of Necro Butcher, Old school Cactus Jack and Steve Lombardi if they got it on. Regardless, I don't know who stops Devin in the TV Title realm. We've brought in alot of new wrestlers that I'd like to see challenge Devin's enthusiasm for writing with Joe. He's officially NFW's most entrenched champ.
When Chris [detail withheld], he will be my lawyer. 'Cause I want to be on acid with someone willing to take the acid and defend me. With Nova's horizon leading away from this particular feud, I want him to promise he will call out for a rematch with the proper time to make it work.
The Dream Team = Dan Spivey and Bret Hart templates in their primes. At least, that's what I think in my head as I'm writing them. I try to use the movesets in those bios as well, but I was psyched the Assisted Superplex didn't have a name so I could get ONE name out of 687 moves in there! Just kidding, that makes writing matches so much easier for those of you that don't have one.

Gregg and JN did a great job with plot points, ideas and direction. I'd like to thank the Dream Team for staying the course and completing the circle. With the templates in mind, they're almost in that young Steiner Bros. phase of their careers to me.
Congratulations to [name withheld]. The RP thread situation was unfortunate, but [name withheld] has earned this opportunity moreso or as much as anyone else that's been a handler for us. Finally, the Rock and Gibraltar of NFW 3.0. Rook Black and Teresa Q. Fun fact: Teresa Q is 7-0 in NFW. Fun opinion: Those are also 7 deserved wins. Not Really Related: It will be interesting to see where each of you branch out from here. All in all, I'd like to thank everyone for the positivity and I'm looking forward to the new and returning contingent looking to get under the covers and Sleep in the Fire with our new champions and runner-ups looking for rematches shortly.

Good show, good fed. Rook Black — 3 March 2008 Thanks for the kind words, Katz. As for the realism, you've got me dead to rights. But I'd always felt that wrestling didn't need to be realistic so much as it needed to be internally consistent. Which is actually a line I put in Craig Miles mouth during the commentary of the match.
Cartoons operated by cartoon logic and cartoon physics, and the rules were odd and easily broken/circumvented, but a viewer could understand the rules because of internal consistency.
The wrestling shows on tv tended to change **** so often that internal consistency became either an impossibility or so difficult that it wasn't worth it. If we started with the same idea that we had at the beginning with our program on an actual wrestling tv show, it'd have been changed four or five times along the way and it wouldn't make any sense and also wouldn't be that fun.
And lastly, I'll remark that the ludicrous storyline did not interfere with having a very not-ludicrous match. And the one part of it that could've threatened the match was made special because it didn't threaten the match.
Now I wave my magic wand, and you all go back to sleeeeeeeeeeep.
