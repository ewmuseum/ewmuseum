Brock Alyas Biography Eye Color Ice blue Nicknames Ninth Mile Hometown Detroit, Michigan Birthday February 2, 1979 Height 6'2 Weight 230 Alias N/A Handedness Left Looks Brock's recently lost a ton of weight through the rehabilitation of his life and career. He is still a tad on the heavy side, but he certainly looks much lighter than normal, which has an illusion to it that makes Brock look SOMEWHAT healthier. He's not overly muscular and is no longer covered in stretch marks. In fact his muscles look like they're screaming to get out of his skin, which is the direct affect of Brock's malnutrition through early 2010. Brock is a very, short armed/legged thick 230 pounder. He LOOKS like he weighs more just based on his broad shoulders and... pudgy natural build. Brock has that heavyweight width and it allows for him to use a low center of gravity to explode through his opponent when brawling. Think Kimbo Slice or Taz. Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards 2008 NFW Rookie of the Mid Year Biography Brock Alyas' life and career have been ups and downs since they began. Before NFW Brock worked in independant organizations all over the world, mostly around the Michigan/Ohio area. Having started wrestling at the age of 16, by the time Brock was 20 he was the lead anchor for many organizations in the midwest through the late 90s and early millenium. He got a shot to wrestle in PIW which was at then in it's prime but before contract negotiations were worked out - Brock was sent back to Detroit to work in the indies. Brock's had it made in the shade in the NFW - coming in and proving himself to be a force to be reckoned with in the ring quickly. Though 2008 was a huge year for some new faces in the NFW Brock's 8-0 record through his first 8 matches earned himself 2008 Rookie of the Mid Year. Since then Brock's done nothing but damage his career it seems, out of the ring and off camera interactions involving multiple incarcerations and charges with police have really over shadowed Brock's capabilities in the ring. Brock has recently been involved in a blood bath war with Cameron Cruise that was brought to a conclusion after off-camera interactions with the two and Brock having served time for opening fire in Cameron Cruise's hometown. Cruise seemed to get the better of Alyas at Crash 49 with the help of another one of Brock's recent enemies - Vic Tarano. Brock hasn't made any clear cut alliances since he's been in the NFW but he's been known to work for the hand that feeds him in the form of either Craig Miles or more recently Eddie Mayfield. Wrestling Style Powerful Innovator Trainer Can-AM School of Wrestling Graduate, Peter Senerchia School of Wrestling Honourary Graduate Finisher Move The Extra Mile EXPLODAH! Finisher Description Wrist Clutch Exploder Suplex Finisher Setup Move N/A Setup Description The Extra Mile Exploder is often executed out of sparatic desparation or when Brock feels it's about time to end the match. Special Moves Brainbuster Suplex, Fisherman’s Suplex, Falcon Arrow, German Suplex, Dragon Suplex, favours the T-Bone suplex in terms of setting his opponent up. Most specifically catches opponents in them while counter-attacking clothesline or knee attempts.. Anything Other Moves Back Neck Drops, Clothesline from Hell, Death Valley Driver (often regular and variations used as finisher) as well as a high-impact spear that he will use prior to finishing them off as well as a high-impact side step and kick to the face of an opponent. Brock is very partial for making his opponent land awkwardly on his neck. Ring Entrances Nothing special, nothing extravigant. One of Brock's frequently played instrumental tracks on his iPod comes on and there's no more familiar music in NFW because it always punishes the subwoofers in the arena, he walks out much like an MMA fighter (specifically Rampage Jackson) calm confident and with a swag in his step but he's been known to let loose and get the blood flowing. Gimmick Brock's character is a raging lunatic in the ring whose proven he can throw each and every NFW competitor around the ring like a rag doll. His lack of patience, quick instinct (and horrible) decision making and inability to avoid negative press out of the ring have turned into a downward spiral that destroyed the credibility Brock had earned for himself throughout 2008. The cocky over confident sociopath couldn't pay to stay away from trouble because it's not often any money he has didn't come in through proceeds of crime. Brock's also very partial to alcohol, hard drugs and foreign hookers. Strengths Brute Force, Power, Strength, Ring Instincts, Ring Experience Weaknessees Patience, Frustration, Eagerness to end matches quickly, Athleticism. Ring attire Brock choses to stomp your head in with a pair of Jordans on his feet, baggy jean shorts or MMA style shorts that hang past the knees. He'll wear different basketball or football jerseys in the ring. He's been known to wear blank white tees but he often won't wrestle in them. Theme Music "Walkin On Down The Road" by the Red Hot Chili Peppers.
