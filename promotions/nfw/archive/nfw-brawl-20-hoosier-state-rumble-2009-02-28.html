NFW Brawl 20: Hoosier State Rumble, 28 February 2009

28 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 20: Hoosier State Rumble 28 Feb 2009 Consecso Fieldhouse, Indianapolis, Indiana (seats 18,500) Welcome to the Show! Sirius Radio Channel 420. Hellmachine. WE BEGIN. The roads of the Midwest at night are vast and empty. Cornfields and farms littered between the single-lane highways and ‘Save us Jesus’ signs that mark each town populace of 327 common folk. Then again, that’s if there are that many. However, every six hours you reach something familiar. City lights, strip clubs and enough bars to drown the country of Ireland. After traveling 230 miles from the Gateway City to Hoosierland, NFW unpacked itself into the Conseco Fieldhouse of Indianapolis, Indiana. It’s third sold-out arena house show for the Midwest tour and the streak of sold-out shows streaming from back when it started in 2005. There’s a certain sense of empowerment in knowing you don’t need to whore yourself out to corporate America to reach your audience. NFW doesn’t advertise itself anymore, it doesn’t shill itself anymore. It quietly assembles a roster and lineup that fans cannot deny themselves from missing and here we are once again, ready to turn the sport on itself once again. We find ourselves two weeks away from what will be the ‘INAUGURATION CELEBRATION’ of Crash 47… …and tonight…the hors d'oeuvres are served and sublime. Dos Equis vs. Bret Kelly The masked luchadore was looking to avenge his St. Louis house show, but from the beginning it definitely seemed that his mind still wasn’t all there. And by "wasn’t all there," I mean that this guy is 51 cards short of a full deck. Arguing incessantly with someone beside him that was apparently invisible, Bret Kelly could only wait in the ring in complete confusion as to what the hell was going on with the masked luchadore. The Canadian Heartbreaker waited patiently, brushing back his slick, greasy hair…and he removed his silver sunglasses and tossed them into the crowd, while Equis screamed at his ‘partner’ to make sure there wasn’t any freaky goateed mammoths hiding in the audience. Of course, he noted that a lot of the fat fans had those to hide their triple chins and suddenly the bell rang. Kelly fired out of his corner and caught Equis by surprise with a snap mare followed by a dropkick to the back of the head. Kelly hit a side backbreaker and quickly scaled the turnbuckles and hit a second role elbow drop. Equis kicked out after a two count, but then suffered a Side Russian Legsweep and had to kick out at two again. Kelly sent for all the marbles, setting up Equis for a Superplex and that failed miserably. First, he took a series of a right hand fists to the gut and fell off to the mat…then as he stood up, Equis pretty much turned the tides completely with a devastating Shooting Star DDT from the top rope. Indy got on their feet after that one, applauding the move while Equis held his midsection and screamed, "Aye mi higado! No mas Corona, no mas!" Equis stood up slowly, but Kelly was up second and down quickly after Equis hit a running clothesline that whiplashed him to the mat. A running senton followed and the fans could tell that Bret Kelly’s hair wasn’t going to save him again. Equis hit a Beersteiner than hit a Springboard Bulimia Nervosa Bomb, hooked the leg and got the three count. Pushing the ref away, Equis celebrated in the middle of the ring with his tag team partner. …and by tag team partner, we mean…air. Winner: Dos Equis Cary Turkington vs. Felix Red If you thought this night was about to get anymore normal, when the next two wrestlers came out for a match…well, the feeling washed away real quickly. Much like the amount of whiskey that Cary Turkington probably washed away before this match began. To say that he came out walking drunk to this match is to say that Ted Kennedy once got in a minor drunk driving accident. Turkington was wasted and Felix Red actually had to feel a little surprise inside that he was the sober wrestler in the ring. The match began with a lock-up that almost tripped up Turkington as he tried to push Felix in a corner. Felix whirled him around and started cranking away Felix Kicks that Turkington may or may not have felt at the current moment, but would surely make him cry tomorrow morning. Irish Whip and Turkington hits the opposite buckles so hard he lands on his arse. Felix gets the crowd pumped with a back handspring into a sliding mule dropkick into Turkington’s grill. Felix ripped him up and hit a suplex with a rollover pin attempt, but only managed a two count. Then, Felix scaled the top rope and flew off with a leg drop attempt, but Cary was able to dodge it. Felix scampered up, but found himself taking a barrage of body shots to the rib cage from Turkington. Felix got backed into the corner, where Turk started bullying him with repeated shoulderblocks. He lined up Felix for a straight right shot with connected, sending Felix on his own arse. Cary backed up and charged frenetically towards the corner and connected with could only be described as some awkward looking diving avalanche splash. Felix crawled out, Cary stumbled around drunkenly and swung a crazy roundhouse right that Felix ducked under. BLAM! Cobra Clutch Facebuster. Felix hooked a La Magistral Cradle, Cary kicked out at two. Felix hooked a small package, Cary kicked out at two. Felix caught him with an Oklahoma roll-up and Cary bulled out at two. At the same time, Cary was very…very…very DIZZY after all of this. Looking like he was about to puke, he walked right into… ECSTASY OF THE AGONY. Fuggitaboutit. ONE. TWO. THREE. Winner: Felix Red Rook Black vs. Payton Manning As the fans settled down, Eddie Mayfield made a rare appearance on the SMOKEtron. He gave an impassioned thank-you speech to Rook Black for erasing the New Frontier of Wrestling from all things VON DOOM. He then said that he would allow himself to defend the National Championship with dignity. The Baltimore Colts fight song played, out walked a guy in a butcher’s apron, Colts #16 jersey and similarly themed pants. Out walked "The Baltimore Butcher" PAYTON Manning…and you’re damn right if you were thinking he shouted "Cut That Meat!" all the way down the aisle. Out next walked the National Champion, looking…well Rook didn’t seem phased by the proceedings. Maybe the champion had a slight disdain for the lack of intelligence and skill across the ring from him. The bell rang and Manning flew out of the corner and missed a leaping Hornet Splash and walked around the ring holding his throat. With the obvious heelism of Manning ‘choking,’ Rook ended the chicanery with a spinal cord tingling Gauntlet Throwdown! CUT THAT MEAT! CUT THAT MEAT! CUT THAT MEAT! I mean…1. 2. 3. Winner: Rook Black Teresa Quaranta vs. Impulse Leading up to the Hoosier City show, Teresa Quaranta had some choice words for the Marathon Man. Perhaps, 2007’s NFW Rookie Sensation had a bone to pick with 2008’s. The two wrestlers that have heralded this v.3 era into a new, diverse and exciting age would face off head to head after a special challenge issued by Impulse. Quaranta was out first, then the Marathon Man. The High Priestess of Discordia and Impulse came flying out of their corners and the pace never slowed down the whole match. Quaranta taking early advantage with a series of armdrags and dropkicks, while Impulse looked a little more powerful than usual as he hit a Snap Powerslam followed by a jumping legdrop after countering a crossbody block. Quaranta was able to hit a few kicks to stagger the kid, allowing herself an opportunity to hit a high kneestrike followed by a Springboard Bulldog. Impulse kicked out at two, Quaranta caught with a single-leg mule kick followed by an Axe Kick. TQ headed up top, but Impulse staggered into the ropes causing the former National Champion to get crotched on the turnbuckles. Knowing he didn’t have enough time as he would against a male wrestler, Impulse quickly hit a dropkick. The impact almost knocked TQ to the outside of the ring, but she managed to hook her legs around the turnbuckles. This of course allowed Impulse to kick the crap out of her until she fell off, hit the apron and dropped to the outside of the ring. Impulse connected with a sliding dropkick that knocked Quaranta into the barricade. He attempted a Hurricanrana, but TQ countered by falling backwards which caused Impulse’s chest to crack against the barricade. With the whole arena groaning, TQ hit a running senton off the apron onto Impulse outside the ring. TQ rolled Impulse into the ring and went for a Corkscrew Moonsault, but the Marathon Man rolled out of the way just in time! TQ staggered up and Impulse connected with the Sudden Impact Superkick! ONE! TWO! THRRRRRR. Hrm. TQ KICKED OUT! Impulse immediately started arguing with the ref, slapping his hands like a three count should’ve been made. Somehow, someway…TQ used the opportunity to sneak up behind after a rather lengthy argument. ONE! TWO! THRRRRRR. Not quite, but Impulse did scramble up and right into a vicious drop toe hold into the turnbuckles. TQ dragged Impulse out and locked in DEATH BY THE FUTURE! (Argentine Leg Lock) And this would have certainly ended the match, but there’s these two people. Let’s just call them… THE DEVIL’S REJECTS! Legion and Luci4 charged the ring in a flash and immediately attacked Quaranta, the referee calling for the bell…and then eating a vicious double clothesline. Legion and Luci stomped away on TQ until she was nearly put through the mat literally. Legion went outside and grabbed a chair…Luci4 held TQ down and Impulse stood up to his feet slowly. The crowd roared as Impulse ripped the chair out of Legion’s hands, the Devil’s Rejects immediately started to retreat a couple of steps… …UNTIL… Impulse crushed Quaranta with the chair! That’s right, we do have that FAKE Impulse running around don’t we? Legion and Luci cheered him on, then joined in with more stomps… So, the easiest way to guess ‘Impulse’ from Impulse is when the real one runs out and immediately looks 20 pounds lighter than his impostor. Impulse springboarded the towards the Devil’s Rejects as they charged and caught them with a split-legged dropkick! ‘Impulse’ swung the chair at the real Marathon Man who ducked, so the chair cracked against the ropes, which caused it to ricochet off and smack the impostor in the head! A dropkick sent ‘Impulse’ out of the ring, a staredown ensued between everyone at ringside… And that’s when a black-eyed COJONES MERCADO popped on the STARtron! In his ‘wonderful’ English, he explained that he had looked forward to Impulse wrestling this evening and that this shouldn’t stop that from happening. He would face Legion immediately in a INSURGENT BOOTCAMP match! And everyone in the ring, in the arena and myself could only say… "Bwuh?" Winner: TQ via DQ Legion vs. Impulse The Outspoken Critic of the New Regime. The Crazy Lunatic of the Revolution. 20 Insurgents surrounding the ring, Teresa Quaranta, ‘Impulse’ and Luci4 staying suspiciously close. Mercado stayed on the SMOKEtron and announced that due to a Presidential Decree he was not allowed to execute Impulse before the Inauguration Celebration. However, he could send his FIRST LIEUTENANT to make sure everything went PERFECTO. Cue up the Buena Social Vista Club, slick back your hair and pop a toothpick into your mouth. Cojones’ former tag team partner, RAUL walked out and got a boatload of cheers. After a couple of slaps from TQ and Luci4 from asking them to lube him up, so to speak…he signaled for the bell. Legion rushed out with a vengeance, nailing Impulse with a flurry of punches and some stiff kneelifts to the gut. He went for an Irish Whip and clothesline attempt, but Impulse was able to duck under and hook in a Crucifix Cradle for a near pinfall. Both got up, Impulse hit a dropkick that sent Legion nearly out of the ring. He charged the Connoisseur of Chaos, but got flipped over and outside the ring. The Insurgents gave him some stiff boots before rolling him back in the ring, where Legion connected with a backbreaker and Vader Splash. Two count only, but Legion followed up with a Snap Suplex and elbow drop. With the Marathon Man using his stamina, Legion got a little frustrated after a few series of near pinfalls. He went for a Frog Splash, but Impulse moved out of the way! Legion got up and ate a spinning leg lariat that knocked him outside the ring. The Insurgent Patrol laid into Legion, but Luci4 fought some of them off…but then…well, then they cracked her in the head with a chair. Meanwhile, back in the ring…Impulse hit a hurricanrana, hooked the legs and nearly got the win. Both staggered up, Impulse caught Legion with a rolling headscissors. Legion sat up against the turnbuckles, took a sliding dropkick to the head and then got pulled out and placed in a Scorpion Deathlock! No! Small Package! ONE! TWO! NO! REVERSED! ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! Both raced up, Legion hit a boot to the gut and a Sunset Flip! ONE! TWO! Impulse rolled out and hooked a Jackknife Pin! ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! I will not exit my Kerry O’Connor phase. In the meantime, Luci4 now had a chair of her own and started fighting back against the Insurgent Patrol. This commotion allowed ‘Impulse’ and Quaranta to storm the ring. Legion surprisingly decked ‘Impulse’ with a roaring elbow shot knocking him from the ring, but the next situation was completely and irrevocably a new chapter in the Teresa Quaranta/Legion story. Ever see someone get stabbed in the head with a scalpel? Well, if you did…you were in Indianopolis tonight. Unless you were Impulse, who had to boot Insurgents climbing onto the apron. Quaranta fled the scene after a quintuplet of plastic surgery cuts into Legion’s forehead, who staggered around bloody… SUDDEN IMPACT SUPERKICK! ONE! TWO! THREE! The Marathon Man rolled out of the ring after the win, evading the Insurgent Patrol focused on catching Quaranta and suppressing the psychotic Luci4. Winner: Impulse Joe the Plumber vs. Brock Alyas It’d been a surprising month for Brock Alyas to say the least. He had freed from jail and attacked Cameron Cruise in Detroit. He tagged with Kooter Michaels-Cruise of all people in St. Louis. And now…he finally got his World Championship opportunity against the man that nobody has pinned in Singles competition to date. The same man he attacked after Cameron Cruise in Detroit. The NFW WORLD CHAMPION, JOE THE PLUMBER. THAT’S RIGHT! THE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP ON THE LINE! JTP! BROCK ALYAS! LIVE ON SIRIUS! After the introductions were made (sans title belt in attendance), the camera flashes flickered and the two unworldly physical forces of nature collided. JTP immediately got going early by catching Brock off guard with his unconventional brawling tactics…or maybe his greasy, sewage smelling skin. Joe slipped out of a lock-up and caught Brock on the jaw with a series of stiff right hands and with a big UGGGGGHN, sent the Detroit native packing into the turnbuckles. JTP with an Avalanche Clothesline! Running Bulldog! Legdrop to the back of the head! UGGGHHHHHN! Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! (breathe.) (sip your water.) Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! STOMP! STOMP! …and finally Brock Alyas rolled out of the ring and laid collapsed on the concrete as Joe the Plumber pointed to the hallucinations he was seeing in the air like they were his best friends. Or maybe he was pointing to the frenzied crowd, it’s a fine line with this guy. Joe rolled out of the ring and started ramming Brock’s head into every conceivable object he could focus on. Ringpost! Ring steps! Ring apron! Ring Barricade! Joe rolled Brock in the ring and climbed the turnbuckles, connecting with a front-styled elbow smash to Detroit native’s skull as he wearily stood up. Alyas teetered, tottered and ate a vicious clothesline. Joe dropped down for the cover, but only could secure a two count. Joe scaled the turnbuckles and screamed "VOLCAAAAAAAANOOOOOOOOOO!" And completely missed the Volcano Splash as Alyas got the hell out of the way. Joe got up, but walked right into a boot to the gut from Brock who lifted him up into a Vertical Suplex… …and here’s a testament to Brock Alyas… He can delay it for a good ten seconds without passing out from the stench of Joe’s armpits. SUPLEX! …Brock keeps it hooked and rolls it back up…GOURDBUSTER! Brock keeps it hooked and rolls it back up…SUPLEX INTO A POWERSLAM! GODDAMN! HOT DAMN! …two count. Oh well. Brock sprung up and let out an impassioned yell before mocking Joe by yelling… "DRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAANOOOOOOOOOOO!" Joe stood up and took a freaking NORTHERN LIGHTS BOMB! I’ve seen necks bend awkwardly, but this was like watching Michael Jackson and Emmanuel Lewis’ home videos they filmed on MDMA and Whiskey. The Hoosiers witnessed one of the closest moments to Joe the Plumber getting pinned. Brock Alyas almost lost his lunch it was so close, but he responded by planting Joe in a frakkin’ Brainbustahhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh! ONE! TWOOOOOOOOO! Joe kicked out quicker. Brock Alyas Mental Note: Straight on the head not nearly as effective as awkwardly snapping the spinal chord. Alyas hooked in a Camel Clutch, but Joe was just too damn sweaty and greasy to keep it locked in well. Adjusting to a reverse chinlock, Alyas struggled to maintain the hold and started taking well-placed elbows to the gut from the champ! With the crowd completely behind Joe, Brock went into panic mode and let go of the hold…he bounced off the ropes, but Joe lifted him off his feet and hit an Inverted Atomic Drop! Bowlegged and bearing an insane amount of pain in his loins, Brock Alyas was defenseless against Joe’s running clothesline. Both men laid on the mat, the crowd willing Joe to the momentum which gassed him up to Irish Whip the Ninth Miles and connect with a Bossman slam! Brock bounced up, but had no clue where he was…FULL NELSON! UGGHNNNNNNNNN! LEGSWEEP FACEBUSTAHHHHHHH! Joe rolled over Brock! ONE! TWO! NO! Joe brought up Brock and took a lowblow, the crowd launching into boos. Brock didn’t give two shits or a nickel. He reeled into Joe and hit a Jumping Piledriver! ONE! TWO! NO! Brock argued with the ref for a good ten seconds, then leaned over to pick up Joe… UGHHHHHHN! FACIAL FISHHOOK! Brock screamed and hollered, the ref started a five count and Joe lifted him off the mat into a body slam position. Joe dropped Brock’s face on the turnbuckles and as he leaned against them, he pulled out his legs so he fell facefirst on the middle turnbuckles! KISS THE PORCELAIN! Brock fell out of the corner, limp as a 98 year-old cack. ONE! TWO! But Brock f’n kicked out. Joe reeled him in for the TIGER DRIVER! …and Brock miraculously flipped him over. The crowd stated getting nervous, both men stood…and Brock shocked the world. EXTRAAAAHHHHHH MILE EXPLODAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! OHMYGAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHD! IF BROCK ALWAYS COULD F’N MOVE, HE’D WIN THIS MATCH AND WE’D BE DISCUSSING THIS MOMENT IN TIME LIKE WHEN I STUCK MY CACK BETWEEN DOUBLE D TITS COVERED IN GRAPE JELLY! …Brock crawled. …he crawled some more. AND FINALLY… ONE! TWO! Foot on the rope. Brock pulled Joe towards the middle of the ring and re-covered. ONE! TWO! Well, Joe the Plumber kicked out and the crowd went WIIIIIIIIIIIIIILD! Speaking of Alive and Kicking, some band named Nonpoint sang a song about that. In fact, who was that dude that liked to come out to that song playing? OH YEAH. CAMERON CRUISE! With a house mic in hand, Cruise came out to a mixed reaction with Brock standing up and getting prepared for anything. Except… …well, except… Are you ready? Cameron Cruise telling the world that the reason Brock Alyas went to jail was because he called the cops on him. That’s right. Cameron Cruise snitched on Brock Alyas. …what a world we live in. With that news, Cruise flipped Alyas the bird…and the Ninth Mile looked like he could kill a priest. Unfortunately, he turned around into a boot to the gut from an energized and amped Joe the Plumber. TIGAH DRIVAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! Aka THE SHIT RIVER PLUNGE! COUNT TO TWENTY! THIS ONE’S OVER! FIRE THE TEE-PEE STREAMERS! GET THOSE DOGS BARKIN’! THE CHAMP RETAINS! …and well, we know how Brock got detained. Something tells me that situations just got that more interesting as we’re officially going to the Inauguration Celebration at CRASH FORTY-SEVEN.
