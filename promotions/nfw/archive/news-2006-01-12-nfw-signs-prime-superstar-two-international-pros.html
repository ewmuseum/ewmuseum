NFW news: NFW signs PRIME superstar, Two International Prospects

12 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW signs PRIME superstar, Two International Prospects Posted by Natrone Rice on 12 Jan 2006 New Frontier Wrestling formally announced that it had reached agreements with three new wrestlers to join the active wrestling roster, one a well-known superstar out of PRIME. With two other international prospects, Nova, Gaige Caldwelll (Austrailia), Anatoly Danyelovsky (Russa) will all look to start making their mark in the New Frontier just as we reach midseason. While the terms of each deal were undisclosed, its been reported that Danyelovsky will report to the Eastern Conference where the suspension of Kazuo Shizaki and disappearance of Wong-Fei Hung II needed replacing. As usual, the Western Conference had a hole to fill with Jeff Garvin reaggravating a knee injury, but had filled that with Brandon Youngblood. Little did anyone foresee that one of Youngblood's rivals in Nova would throw his name into the hate just in time to debut on the Western Conference Week 6 show. Nova took the place of failed newcomer Shi-Ryu ("Ghost") and defeated Frank Heimdal. With movie production in Hong-Kong increasing for Heimdal as well as Uriel suffering an injury against KVC and Alias duing the match, NFW decided to leap forward and sign Austrailian phenom "The Rage" Gaige Caldwell. Scott Riktor welcomed the youngster with opening arms on lambasting him for trying to steal an old gimmick of his, but it seems that Caldwell remained silent and might just do his talking in the ring.
