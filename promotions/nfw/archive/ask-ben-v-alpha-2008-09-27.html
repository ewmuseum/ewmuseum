Rook Black — 27 September 2008 There is where I answer questions, endure insults, deflect accusations, and suffer torment regarding the ending of the Rook Black and Uber Judge Storyline on the most recent show.
Do your worst. Legion — 27 September 2008 What would you say is your favourite angle you've been a part with either in A! or NFW? any changes you'd make if you could?
Rook Black — 27 September 2008 Probably in tSC when I got to be the guy who dethroned Sonny Silver. I'd certainly change the culture of excess with exhustive match writing leading to complete company paralysis. And I defintely would never want to have had to take the reigns of tSC during the time that it had to close.
In terms of a storyline angle, this last one was a favorite, closely followed by the incredible debacle of fWo Survivor IV.
Legion — 27 September 2008 After reading crash... nice twist! but what made you come to that conclusion since my whole perception was that it was more a bit 'Clone saga' than anything :P?
Rook Black — 27 September 2008 the actual identities of the principals were determined at the onset. this would've been slightly more plausible than a clone (though not by much) and it excused the weird behavior to a degree. Katz was okay with it as long as we remained in the realm of "television plausible" as well. It's why you never had to look at the unmasked Uber Judge and Rook in the same frame. The limit of pushing the disbelief would be `can a television show effectively fake it so that it'd be at least partially believable?' and as long as I was within that limitation, he was okay.
