Stalker Biography Eye Color Blue Nicknames Career Killer, Black Plague Hometown Seattle, Washington Birthday Unknown Height 6'2 Weight 231 Handedness Right Looks Stalker is rugged, dirty and scarred. A hardcore icon in his own day, it has taken a toll on his looks. Long brown hair, blue eyes and scars on his face. Wears street clothes to the ring when he wrestles. Fed Titles SCW World Champion x2, SCW Television Champion x1, SCW Tag Team Champion x2, EPW Tag Team Champion x1 Fed Awards EPW Match of the year Stalker vs. Kin Hiroshi (Wrestlestock) That was Stalker's first match in EPW. Biography Started wrestling when Superior Championship Wrestling began. Began in the low end matches but quickly worked his way up the ranks winning the SCW TV title then winning the Heavyweight championship. Thought to be a good time for Stalker he lost the Heavyweight title less then a few weeks later and went into an exile that changed his personality forever. When he remerged he was a hardcore addict. He then won the hardcore championship which he held for a long time until he regained the world championship. He defeated a few known superstars such as Matt Morris, Reaver and Superstar Johnny Blaze. He lost the title the same night he injured his neck to Johnny Blaze in one of their many encounters. He returned again but the only highlight that occured during that comeback was his world championship fight against Rocko Daymon. Having the title in his grasps literally, in a ladder match, his tag team partner 'Mr. Everything' Devon Jackson distracted him costing him his third World Title reign. Being humiliated after that incident. Stalker left SCW for a short while trying to start up his own federation 'Insanity Wrestling Federation'. Two brief stints being the president of IWF under his real name of Jason Reeves made him known as not only a hardcore ICON but a viable business man on the face of wrestling. He did make one last come back to SCW with his best friend through all of this Gravedigger. They together formed Hardcore Hellions and won the tag team titles almost immediately after arriving. Then Stalker vanished after they were finally defeated for the titles. No one knew where Jason Reeves was or where he went to. He arrived at EPW during a strange time. Owner Dan Ryan was in the midst of a power struggle with an opposing federation, A1E. His main goal was the destruction of Rocko Daymon's career. He achieved two major steps towards that goal, first he drove Rocko Daymon through a three story window at the first card after Daymon had acquired the EPW World title. Daymon was forced to forfeit that title, due to injuries. During Rocko's absence he feuded with Fusenshoff and a few others. When Rocko eventually returned, their feud did as well. It came to a head during a career vs. career match at Russian Roulette, Stalker finally achieved his goal and sent Rocko packing from Empire Pro. Since that time he's had his hand in numerous main events and is currently on a crusade to rip the world title from current holder Anarky. Now with NFW in it's own version of chaos, Stalker has decided to make NFW a home for him as well. He deems the federation to be in 'perfect shape' for manipulation. Gimmick Hardcore, insane, maniac. He will go at all costs to get the job done. Strengths Has a strong will and can hold his own when it comes to a technical battle of moves. Will use everything and I mean everything at his disposal. Weaknessees Easy to DQ himself, strays from pinning opponents as inflicting pain is his top priority. He can pull a few high flying moves but he is a slow mover. He's been through a lot of hardcore battles in his time and it show. His approach is slow and methodical and can be tripped up by a speedy wrestler. Ring attire Street clothes, jean/khaki baggy shorts/jeans. Wife beaters/Band t-shirts etc... just random street clothes.
