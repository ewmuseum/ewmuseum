UWA Pirate TV 24 Dec 2005 Philadelphia, PA Introduction In 1993, Avery Everette opened the Ultimate Wrestling Alliance, an organization based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania that helped shape the careers of many legends of this sport: Shane Southern, Michael Manson, Mike Randalls, Craig Miles, Tom Adler, Guns... It couldn't last forever and closed down for good in 1998. ...or so we thought. In a battle that has taken place since Everette's untimely death last year, inside the ring, behind-the-scenes in boardrooms...Anne Mystic and Avery Prosser have achieved their common dream of bringing the UWA back to life. However, with dramatically different agendas...what will the future bring for this resurrected battleground?
