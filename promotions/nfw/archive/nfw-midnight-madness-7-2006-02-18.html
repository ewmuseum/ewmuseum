NFW Midnight Madness 7 18 Feb 2006 San Diego, CA Wrestlestock: On the Road (Confessions of Dangerous Minds) Dennis Hof was living the dream of every man, and he knew it. The Moonlight Bunny Ranch opened in 1955 in Nevada, and operated "under the sheets", so to speak, until 1970. Hof, a regular to the ranch, bought it in 1993 for one million dollars. Since then, he'd made a decent living from between the legs of his girls. He also parties with his girls. Surrounded by beautiful women whose job is to give pleasure, and making a substantial profit in the process, what man wouldn't want to be Dennis Hof for even one day? Not today though. No, today Hof wasn't very happy at all. Despite houses of prostitution being regulated in Nevada, celebrities tended to shy away when they were in town. The Ranch did pride itself on discretion, so when someone of fame wanted to come by they'd make arrangements to assure a level of secrecy. When Kodiak Vic Creed wanted to get laid, he kicked open the front door and walked in. Hof wasn't a man who was easily intimidated, but there was something about Creed that made him a might uneasy. The motto of the Bunny Ranch was "Not just Sex - An Adventure!" Nobody pushed that envelope like Creed did when he visited. In the past, he'd purchase the services of up to five women and push them as hard as they could, both physically and emotionally. The lines were there, and Creed always just stayed on the line. From what he heard coming out of the room, as well as what he saw on the security camera, Creed crossed it with impunity. "You need some new whores," Creed shouted to Hof as he stepped out of the bedroom. "I think I wore these ones out!" His pants were still open and he was pulling a t-shirt over his massively muscled torso while Hof sat at the nearby bar. Vic slapped the owner on the back and sat down beside him. "Listen Dennis, you have to do something about hiring dead lays. It'll kill your business." Hof stared ahead and sipped his iced glass of bourbon. "How did one of my girls end up chipping her front teeth Vic?" Creed smiled. "Girl should learn to use her mouth properly. Maybe she's not used to handling something this thick." Hof silently fumed as he continued his drink, while Vic stared a hole in the side of his head. Across the room, three of the Bunnies watched the exchange uncomfortably. "Clearly your women can't handle being with a real man. Except one." Dennis had called for the police just before Vic came out of the bedroom. The Alaskan was huge, jacked up, and more than just a little unstable. Best to let the professionals handle him. That meant that Hof had to put up with Creed's jaw-jacking for a few more minutes. "My phallic torpedo has shot down so many tuna boats, I've lost track," Vic continued. "Thousands of women, hundreds of hookers like yours. I can tell the pro from the amateur. In the heat of the moment, even professionals slip out of character when things get too hot. Vivian," Creed's smile hardened as he noticed Hof's eye twitch," she took to me like she was starving for it. You don't give your own girlfriend enough of the lumber." "I give her plenty," Hof said, trying his best not to take Creed's bait. He was failing, much to Vic's twisted amusement. "Like I said, I know women." The Prince of Power leaned over and whispered into Hof's ear with the arrogance of a man who believed himself nigh untouchable. "And when your woman kissed me, she meant it." That was it. Dennis set his glass down and looked at the psychotic Alaskan. "Listen -" "But I don’t want to die!!" Both men turned slowly as El Janito got shoved out of the hallway. "He can see me now! "And just what are you shooting?" "BOOBIES." Vic looked at Janito, then Steve, then back at Janito. "Who in the dripping crotch are you two?" Janito suddenly started to look a little uncomfortable. "I... uh, you really don't know who we are?" Janito quickly shielded his face, "DON’T KILL ME!" Creed stood up and Janito hopped back a few steps. "Do I look like the kind of person who would know who you are?" Janito’s lips quivered. "He’s gonna kill me, Steve...he’s DEFINITELY going to kill me...I’ve seen the look!" "BUS," Steve reminded him and turned to follow Kitty Clarkson with the camera as she walked by. Janito shook in place violently, trying not to make eye contact with The Alaskan Juggernaught. "We're here to pick you up?" It was clearly a question because Vic didn't look like he was ready to leave, and Janito was pretty goddamn sure he wanted to try and make Creed leave. "Is this that NFW thing?" Steve smacked Janito in the leg. "POLICE." Vic could almost see Hof's eyes light up. He put two and two together. "That's right, I almost forgot.
