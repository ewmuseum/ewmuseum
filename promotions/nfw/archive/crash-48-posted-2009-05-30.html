SAD PANDAS. :( GreggG — 1 June 2009 Stuff I dug: Loved everything w/ Joe vs. Eddie. That's my favorite feud in a longtime in FW. It works great. Joe's such a terrific everyman face. He's one of the most likable characters to come down the pike, which is the key to any great face character. And JN's writing Eddie perfectly. The "evil power broker heel" gimmick has been done a lot. But since Eddie's such a scummy guy, it flips that style on its head. It isn't "dick heel commissioner ruins the lives of whoever gets in his path." It's "slimebag heel trying to get used to his power while he has to run from the guy he initially targeted."
Loved the BND stuff. Billy's been one of my favorites for years. Completely nutty stuff. High Flyer's also a great nutcase heel. I loved the Impulse mask on the bat stuff. Unicorn Mask had quite the debut. I loved the image of kids at ringside stroking his horn. It's just wrong on so many levels.
I also liked the Cruise/Alyas match a lot. Great grudge match. It had a good ECW style to it w/ crazy run-ins galore. Brutal stuff.
As far as my stuff goes -- we had the DC to Dorchester transition planned for maybe a year now, right when we started to really pick up the Kings/HWC feud. I wrote his "Dorchester Is Born" note right then and just tweaked it for this show to fit in with more of what happened. This was one of my favorite feud/angles I've done.
Off to Valinor — 1 June 2009 Yeah, I think the Dorchester turn was one of those things you don't see coming, which I always love. Everyone figures its a tag team war to the bitter end of time, but instead...we've got a great heel swerve and Teacher vs. Student setup that I love even more.
We've also got a SUPERstable in the fed, which I don't think we've seen in NFW for a long time now. JN and I were yukking it up coming up with their intro video.
LQJT86C — 1 June 2009 Pretty solid card all around, with off the wall f*cking crazy segments and a lot of good building towards what I think is going to be an awesome Crash 50 and PPV.
Like I told Gregg in IM, the Dorchester Stratton material is some of the best I've ever seen, and probably THE best heel turn I've read in FW. The actual turn at Crash 47 wasn't all that shocking, but the fallout and explanation thereafter at 48 was what really got me. Usually I think heels are cool, but Dorchester is a f*cking d*ck! Slugged his girlfriend, got with a Hillary wannabe, took CC's money, and turned on his mentor. Wow Dorch, you are just a terrible person all around, my man!
Mayfield/JTP segs were very entertaining. I like the secret service feel to it all, that these guys are legit concerned for Mayfield's life. JTP reminds me of that big monster from Resident Evil 3 that's always stalking right around the corner and will not stop pushing forward. Definitely have the feeling that it's all building to something huge.
The Kooter turn was a really cool surprise. I wrote the match but not the finish, and had no idea that was going to happen. Brilliant writing for the finish.
Brock/Cruise was a sick match, really enjoyed that one. Cruise has a Goldberg-esque FW winning streak going on at the moment.
Setting the Saved By the Bell cast on fire was probably my FW highpoint, so I thank you for that, von Doom.
There's a lot going on right now in NFW, and I think it's all going to culminate in one of the best PPV shows the fed has ever done, at least from what I've seen in the years I was active. Everyone's writing seems to be on it's A Game right now. From my end, I've been writing what I feel has been the best material I've ever done with Castor, and I'll be doing a lot with him on the next few cards. The writing in this fed all seems to have a grand purpose right now, and the payoff is going to be huge.
