jediPREZ — 9 March 2010 RP-MAX: 1 (RP Thread in the Roleplay/NFW Films forum) DEADLINE: March 13 MAXIMUM SEGMENTS: 3 Show Theme: The DVD will be the carry-over piece that will tie Crash 50 to where NFW is today. It is a retroactive look at the event, what's happened since the event...and what's to come. There are no on-air matches, it's a behind the scenes/documentary production. Certain RP's will be showcased on the show from the NFW Films RP thread as well as completely new storyline material. The length of the show will not be more than 60 minutes.
Segments: Please PM or email any segment ideas. We're only going to take a couple, so the show gets up quickly. It's open for anyone to respond. Showcased RP's don't count as segments. We're looking for any important character progressions/catch-ups that anyone wants the opportunity to shine with.
