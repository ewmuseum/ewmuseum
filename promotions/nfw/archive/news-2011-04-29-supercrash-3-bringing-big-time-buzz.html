SUPERCRASH 3 bringing BIG-TIME buzz! Posted by Devin Millwood on 29 Apr 2011 Insider sources from New Frontier Wrestling and ESEN have confirmed that Shawn Hart and Stalker are just the tip of the iceberg of incoming talent currently stationed in San Antonio for SUPERCRASH 3's show and subsequent touring schedule for 2011. As we are less than twenty four hours away from the airdate for one of the most anticipated shows of 2011, the media blitz for New Frontier Wrestling has not only put this show on the tip of the fans' tongues all hours of the day... ...and not only the INDUSTRY's tongues either. A shadowed source claiming to be on staff of one of the BOARD OF DIRECTORS has stated that certain WRESTLERS are coming to NFW to either be on this show or sign up for the aftermath of what's been a cataclysmic lead-in for the THREE-HOUR night. The Windham Clan going against ALL The President's Men has provided alot of intrigue and backstage testimony, but insiders are marvelling over the fact that Eddie Mayfield revealed NO CLUES on his mystery 5th man...instead drawing out Dan Ryan into a vicious, snark-filled history debate on each other's legacies. So NOBODY knows NOTHING at this point on what question mark will be filled in one of the most-watched matches in recent memory. The Windham Clan, Impulse, Eddie Mayfield, Castor Strife and Cameron Cruise really taking to the airwaves. Meanwhile, our insider has confirmed three-five BIG-TIME possible free agent acquisitions not associated with this match are looming on the horizon. This could impact the Television Championship Ladder Match, where it's become apparent that SHAWN HART and STALKER are now part of NFW. Two (F)WRESTLING circuit veterans with a highly regarded pedigree in opponents they've battled and defeated. Stalker's remained unidentified in his promotional debuts, but all signs pointed to him and are confirmed. Hart's on a pay-to-play type contract with many internet fans already split down the fence given Hart's track record of being highly entertaining on ESEN airwaves...but fizzling out shortly thereafter. And what about the identity of THIRTEEN? Is there a now bait and switch now that a Jason Mask has replaced the sunglasses and closely shaved sideburns of a Secret Service/National Guard doppleganger? And then there's however this show ends...and the real questions cropping up that there's got to be an ACE IN THE SLEEVE in play as there is on ALL SUPER-hyped NFW shows. Whether it's a push by the Windham Clan's agenda or a last resort by the Executive Branch. Maybe BOTH. Our inside source has NO WORD from the PRESIDENT on what to do when that bell rings in 24 hours meaning that Eddie Mayfield is the ONLY man in the federation that knows Eddie Mayfield's plan. His partners are in the dark, The Office is in the dark and that means that it's likely that only THE 5TH MAN and Eddie know on their side. With this man contract signings, we may be looking at Eddie's last push to rid NFW of the Windham Clan...or maybe to ensure that 50% of control under Troy Windham 'comes with a certain price for the Windham Clan. There's a certain buzz in the locker room, in the bars of San Antonio and certainly amongst us media. SUPERCRASH 3 will be a LANDMARK event for what's likely to be great performances, but also becoming a moment we look back to how high it propelled the federation towards a competitive mecca.
