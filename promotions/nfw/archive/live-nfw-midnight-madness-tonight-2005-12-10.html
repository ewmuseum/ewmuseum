Live! NFW Midnight Madness! Tonight!

10 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Off to Valinor — 10 December 2005 NFW MIDNIGHT MADNESS! LIVE TONIGHT! You're sad, you're lonely and outside of contemplating the problems of this hobby, you sometimes wonder why hasn't any writer touched on the topic of wealth and absurdity...and I'm talking about the Carnival of the Stars.
Well that's only one match tonight, at midnight, when New Frontier Wrestling will walk into the PIT at the University of New Mexico and put on a show that former tSC fans will rejoice in saying, "LONG ENOUGH TO NOT READ! AWESOME!"
Well, so what? You want wrestling action? Then you try telling me Eddie Mayfield, Jeff Garvin, Mike Randalls, Alias, Tom Adler, Troy Windham, Rook Black and Scott Riktor can't cover your needs.
You like your video games? We got your Almasy! You want excitement and BIG surprises? Joey Melton's return truly will epitomize the expression, "riding in on your horse!" And let's not forget that Eddie Mayfield (Craig Miles most hated person in the world) has promised that Miles will have to face someone from the land of the cWl and fWo that he hates more than orange juice. And if it weren't for Eddie Mayfield, orange juice MIGHT be Craig Miles' natural enemy.
New Frontier Wrestling -- the only federation on the internet to decide, "**** it! We need to settle this who's better than who regardless of age, color, RP style and shoe size..." brings a card of a e-wrestling fan's wet dream.
Its a good thing too...we're almost halfway through Season 2...and you might not even be paying attention.
LOCATION: The sold out PIT, University of New Mexico (Albequerque) LINEUP Eddie Mayfield vs. Jeff Garvin Seymour Almasy vs. Uriel Gabriel Brown vs. Ghost Guy Boudreaux vs. Frank Heimdal Norman Quiry vs. Marcus Cameron Scott Riktor vs. Rook Black Joey Melton vs. Eli Flair Alias vs. Mike Randalls "The King Of All Media" Troy Windham vs. "The Arch-Angel" Tom Adler MAIN EVENT: CRAIG MILES vs. MYSTERY DUDE~! LINK: http://nfw.fwrestling.com/ ETA: MIDNIGHT TONIGHT.
