NFW Brawl 42: Talladega Nights!, 25 July 2011

25 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NFW Brawl 42: Talladega Nights! 25 Jul 2011 Talladega Superspeedway, Talladega, Alabama (seats 175,000) Welcome to the Show! (MUSICUP: "CAN’T YOU HEAR ME KNOCKIN’" – THE ROLLING STONES) (FADEIN: CALVIN CARLTON, BLAINE HOLLYWOOD and MALIK ANDERSON riding down PITSTOP BOULEVARD at the TALLADEGA SUPERSPEEDWAY on a pearl white Bentley…thousands of plastic beer cups and trash wrappers sailing by their heads, a NASCAR pace car’s swirling, orange warning rooflight and siren blaring…) NATRONE RICE: (V/O) "Oh don’t tell your daddy where you’re goin, don’t listen to what your momma told…we’re HALFWAY through SUPERBRAWL SUNDAY and taking the long road home!" (QUICK CUTTO’s: SAAFIR MUHAMMED and BLAINE HOLLYWOOD squaring off for a staredown in the middle of the ring, the screen splitting as it also shows 187 and MALIK ANDERSON jawjacking at each other…camera flashes flickering everywhere. LOWELL’s crowd surfing, JACK HARMEN is arriving in a custom tailored suit, but still hounded by NFW SECRET SERVICE. As HARMEN’s security checkpoint is shown, on the other split-screen we see SAAFIR MUHAMMED pushing a shopping cart of weapons…) RICE: (V/O) "Security has been out in full-force tonight and you can’t blame them as some of the most NOTORIOUS CHEATERS in NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING nay…THE WHOLE INDUSTRY congregated in front of around SIXTY-THOUSAND RABID fans for a night that has delivered ICONIC VIOLENCE with such PAGEANTRY that people are still too jacked up to even sit down!" (CUTTO: LIVE! TURN 3 ON THE TALLADEGA SUPERSPEEDWAY – NATRONE RICE standing with a microphone in hand, his smile beaming! In the background, a monstrous TRI-FRONTIERTRON similar to DARLINGTON blends in with the starry, navy blue nightsky accentuated with the thousands of speedway light bulbs and NFW PYROTECHNIC LIGHT IT UP FACTORY. Copyright 2012 – End of the Mayan World Earthplosion. …no rights reserved, just man up and face it like a champ.) RICE: "An ELECTRIC evening has come to its conclusion and fans it DID NOT disappoint in the least! We’ve got the rundown on a double-dose of SINGLES matches with the NEW EMT CHAMPIONS, THE DIRTY ROTTEN SCOUNDRELS and the FORMER champs, THE HOLLYWOOD WRECKING CREW. MOUTHPIECE VS. MOUTHPIECE! ENFORCER VS. ENFORCER! HAHA! Ain’t that something ‘cause surrounding that we take you through the ‘SOUTHERN’ HEAT aka SECOND LEG aka TURN TWO of the TWENTY-ELEVEN NFW GRAND PRIX! The NFW DEBUT of LOWELL along with superstars JACK HARMEN, SHAWN HART and CASTOR STRIFE PRO-TUHJAY ALEX AUSTIN! And perhaps a FUTURE STUD of NFW – LEVOND DANIELS, may not be the technical wrestling wizard you’re used to seeing in some up and comers, but he sure brought his left-knuckled Jackie Chan Johnson and right-footed Bruce Lee O’Leary tonight!" 187 vs. Malik Anderson (FADEIN: 187 storming down the entrance aisle to the ring in his usual DRS gear: Black SWAT Team tactical pants, and matching wife beater, hair in tight cornrows. Luckily, he’s got a top-notch FLAK JACKET on to protect the EMT TITLE belt strapped sideways across his chest underneath…aside from the fact that he’s in Alabama. Next to him in a black mini-dress is SHANIQUA CARLTON holding her Serena Williams Custom Wilson Tennis Racket.) RICE: "And how else could you expect NEW FRONTIER to lay it down in ALABAMA fresh off a 60 minute set from JJ JOHNSON and the BLOODY NOSEJOB GANG? Let’s get all the HOT BUTTON ISSUES of society out in the open – 187, ONE-HALF of quite possibly the most CONTROVERSIAL, OUTSPOKEN tag teams in the HISTORY of this SPORT. THE DIRTY ROTTEN SCOUNDRELS – two AFRICAN-AMERICANS from the projects…and probably frothing at the mouth to lay waste to a scene like this… good thing they got the silent oneS out first." (CUTTO: MALIK ANDERSON rubbing his balding head, while shaking his head. MALIK’s got a full on grey 'Grady' from Sanford and Son beard, to match his ‘ANDERSON’ barrel frame. MALIK’s wearing black tights and boots "Black Sheep" in cursive on the ass. Notably donning NOT a peacock robe, but a black baseball jacket that has a magnificent falcon embroidered on the back. CALVIN CARLTON is donning a crimson suit with royal blue tie, harkening to his NFW South Commissioner days.) RICE: "With an APROPRO SUPERBRAWL set between the two enforcers of their tag team factions, the bell rang and we were on our way! 187 and MALIK took an EXTENDED standoff in the middle of the ring, while outside of it…notoriously at ringside were the CARLTON CLAN, who were not staying out of harm’s way for the evening…putting at risk whether they were going to be present for the second half of the DRS/HWC singlesfest." (CUTTO: MALIK and 187 crisscrossing off the ropes, 187 stopping in the middle and initiating a DOUBLE SHOULDERBLOCK…neither man go down, puffing out cheeks at each other as 187 HEAVILY bounds off the ropes and another DOUBLE SHOULDERBLOCK doesn’t DO SHITE! MALIK slaps his head a few times, DOUBLE SHOULDERBLOCK…and NOTHIN’! 187 STARTS pounding his chest as MALIK runs off the ropes again, but this time 187 charges after him and delivers a huge big boot to MALIK’s face!" RICE: "Man, HIS HEAD SNAPPED BACK LIKE A PEZ DISPENSER! HAHA! Well, never count out an Anderson as Malik made a furious comeback after some pulverizing shots from 187. An Armbreaker DDT countering a clothesline lead to a little family tradition…" (FLASH CUTTO’s: MALIK stomping on 187’s shoulder! ANDERSON wrapping 187’s shoulder around the ringpost! MALIK delivering a shoulderbreaker! ANDERSON grabbing a chair during an outside brawl and shoves it under 187s armpit, then Cutters him, snapping his shoulder out of joint!) RICE: "EWWWWWWWWWWWW! Talk about snapping a man back to the reality of a situation!" (CUTTO: 187 is now favoring his arm, walking around the ringside area dazed. He starts throwing chairs, flipping over tables to stop the stalking MALIK. CAL distracts him letting MALIK charge in with a running axehandle! CUTTO: ANDERSON hoisting 187 over his shoulder ramming him shoulderfirst into the ringpost before delivering a SNAKE EYES on the ringsteps!) RICE: "So, while the two wrestlers were bashing each other’s bones, it wasn’t too long until As the CARLTONS turn came to the forefront of the match…" (CUTTO: SHANIQUA and CAL arguing at ringside, SHANIQUA with both tagbelts on her shoulder and her tennis racket. CAL tries to steal the belts from her, but 187 breaks it up by grabbing him by his Scottie Pippen haircut, allowing Malik a rollup, but only a TWO COUNT! CUTTO: MALIK walking around ringside, stalking after a staggering 187 – SHANIQUA comes out of nowhere with an overhead smash to the back of his head, using CAL yelling at the ref as the distraction! CAL goes ballistic, MALIK goes down and allows 187 to regroup and hit a charging front kick to the head!) RICE: "So, it looked like things were down 187’s avenue, but one thing you can NEVER do…is count out an Anderson!" (CUTTO: 187 reeling in the blinded MALIK and goes for the chokeslam, but can't get him up as MALIK battles furiously to keep his body grounded. Out of nowhere – ANDERSON reverses the move into an Armbreaker, then slaps on the ANDERSON STEPOVER ARMBAR! 187 tries to battle out, but starts tapping as the crowd goes crazy!) RICE: "Malik Anderson gets the victory in one heck of a donnybrook, but it wasn’t over from there to say the least! When you smell blood and you’re part of that Anderson bloodline – you become a SHARK!" (CUTTO: ANDERSON letting go of the hold, getting his hand raised… and then viciously booting 187 in the head! CALVIN runs into the ring and SHANIQUA slides in after him, but she stops on a dime as MALIK spins on a dime to face her. CAL hides behind MALIK’s back, while in the background… QUICK CUTTO: BLAINE HOLLYWOOD running faster than his polo horse! No fanfare, no ENTRANCE attire. HOLLYWOOD’s in ring gear, he’s got a pair of handcuffs! CUTTO: ANDERSON reeling in 187, while CAL repeatedly smashes him over the back with his racquet! SHANIQUA’s bug-eyed, caught off-guard and on the other foot! She stops, drops and rolls out of the ring…) RICE: "The ONE advantage that the DIRTY ROTTEN SCOUNDRELS have had TWICE against the HOLLYWOOD WRECKING CREW was the element of surprise. Tonight, it was on the flipside in the name of STRATEGY. You know DYNASTY will stoop to newfound lows to beat you, hurt you, ELIMINATE you from the sport." (CUTTO: ANDERSON rag-tossing 187 through the turnbuckles, ‘causing his shoulder to violently hit the ringpost! The crowd groans, while ANDERSON pulls him by the backside and delivers an angled BACK SUPLEX that drives 187’s shoulder as the point of impact! CUTTO: HOLLYWOOD handcuffing’s wrist around the ringpost! CUTTO: HWC repeatedly ramming 187 shoulder-first into the ringpost, while SHANIQUA is halfway up the entranceway…) RICE: "187 in a bad way, the Hollywood Wrecking Crew literally trying to tear his shoulder pillar from post! But SAAFIR MUHAMMED luckily got wind of the A/V feed somewhere… and well, a surprising look of it’s own." Blaine Hollywood vs. Saafir Muhammed (CUTTO: SAAFIR wearing a tailor-made charcoal grey suit, Carolina blue necktie, his hair and goatee trimmed neatly. MUHAMMED hands his glasses and suit jacket to SHANIQUA as he storms down the entrance way, rolling up his dress shirt sleeves up to his elbows. CUTTO: CAL gawking at MUHAMMED, not recognizing him as BLAINE does a double-take himself. They desert a badly wounded 187, who’s still cuffed to the post. CUTTO: BLAINE in the ring, wildly motioning with both his hands for SAAFIR to get in the ring, while MUHAMMED takes his time walking up the ringsteps removing his necktie and loosening the top button on his shirt.) RICE: "If you would have told me that this is how this match would start playing out at a DIXIELAND racetrack, I would have laughed in your face, suggest we get some coffee and talk about what else is on your mind ‘cause I could use the entertainment! Anyway, HOLLYWOOD scopes MUHAMMED up and down with a sneer as they come to a close face-to-face in the ring. I mean, it’s a scene…Blaine’s nose is quivering as Saafir calmly talks to him inaudibly to the rest of us… and whatever he said – BOOMSHAKALAKA! BIG RIGHT FROM BLAINE!" (CUTTO: The referee frantically motioning for the bell to ring as BLAINE waylays SAAFIR with a series of right hands! The crowd’s roaring, BLAINE cracks SAAFIR with a vicious forearm uppercut, and MUHAMMED grins back at him and widens his eyes… BLAINE’s face contorts in anger as he then delivers a stiff kick to the gut! CHOP! IRISH WHIP! SAAFIR hits the corner turnbuckles and BLAINE goes for a BACK BODY DR –NOT SO FAST SWEETHEART! KICK BY MUHAMMED! Blaine staggers back, Saafir delivering a series of wingtip-pointed kicks to the solar plexus! MUHAMMED charges and cracks an elbow on BLAINE!) RICE: "But as the match progressed and got into the thick of things…" (QUICK CUTTO’S: BLAINE slapping SAAFIR in the corner right across the face and then spitting at him! "LET’S GO DMX!" SAAFIR nearly decapitating BLAINE with a running clothesline! SAAFIR stomps down on this head mercilessly with those wingtips! "YOU NEED SOME TIME ON THOSE KNEES, ELDRICK!") RICE: "Well, these two weren’t going to stay pretty." (QUICK CUTTO’s: BLAINE straddling SAAFIR, firing down right hands! SAAFIR taking off one of his shoes and bashing BLAINE over the head with it! BLAINE’S face starting to stream with blood, walking bowleggedly around the ring and into a LEAPING DDT from MUHAMMED! MUHAMMED jumping off the top rope with an overhead elbow, but BLAINE spinning into an ENZIGUIRI counter that sends TALLADEGA into a collective "OHHHHHHHHH!" from the smacking impact that lands MUHAMMED in a heap!) RICE: "In the first singles match between these factions, it really was about brutality, power…and there wasn’t too much emotion outpouring. But Blaine Hollywood and Saafir Muhammed are oil and water, resentful of each other’s backgrounds, lineages, beliefs and at their very core…would like nothing more than to shut each other up…but on Blaine’s side, the handcuffs showed their true colors…" (CUTTO: BLAINE drunkwalking around the ring, his face a crimson mask as you can see LAMONT from the live broadcast booth making wild hand signals to him… limping in back of him, shaking out cobwebs is SAAFIR MUHAMMED… who never sees the MALIK ANDERSON freight train coming as CALVIN CARLTON distracts the ref! CUTTO: BLAINE draping himself over the ref, feigning exhaustion as ANDERSON drops SAAFIR with a HOT SHOT on the barricade! CUTTO: 187 not even able to yank on his cuffed hand, instead there are medics by him icing down his trapped shoulder. …he’s loud in his yelling at HWC from a distance, but it just doesn’t matter.) RICE: "With the HWC using underhanded tactics, the match was starting to swing in Blaine’s favor as he started hitting some NEAR finishes on one half of the current tag champs!" (CUTTO: HOLLYWOOD driving MUHAMMED with an STO BACKBREAKER, pulling him up like a YO-YO for a POWER URANAGE! A TWO-POINT-FIVE count sends BLAINE into frustrated hysterics! CUTTO: HOLLYWOOD hitting a SUPERPLEX with rollover pin attempt, MUHAMMED squeaks out his shoulder to avoid the loss just in time! CUTTO: HOLLYWOOD hitting an ANDERSON SPINEBUSTER!) RICE: "But just when Blaine Hollywood thought he had this one wrapped up…" (CUTTO: BLAINE goes for the ‘THAT’S ENTERTAINMENT’ but gets caught with a side elbow, MUHAMMED all of a sudden gets him up into a FIREMAN’S CARRY and drops down with a CUTTER! MUHAMMED TIGHTLY HOOKS IN BLAINE AND GETS THE 3 COUNT!) RICE: "So, Saafir Muhammed gets the victory…but after the match, it was becoming a question of who would win the war…" (CUTTO: MUHAMMED getting blindsided by ANDERSON! CUTTO: 187 yelling at someone to get a hacksaw… CUTTO: BLAINE dropping SAAFIR with the "THAT’S ENTERTAINMENT" backcracker! CUTTO: HWC leaving the ringside area with MUHAMMED laid out, MALIK laughing as he tosses the handcuffs key to SHANIQUA.) RICE: "When we come back…a quick recap of the action between SHAWN HART, ALEX AUSTIN and newcomer LEVOND DANIELS!" (FADEAWAY) Alex Austin vs. Levond Daniels vs. Shawn Hart (FADEIN: SHAWN HART parading around the ring, climbing up the turnbuckles and getting pulled by his hair off by ALEX AUSTIN!) RICE: "Welcome back fans and that’s EXACTLY how things got started in the first triple threat GRAND PRIX opening round match! Alex Austin assaulted Shawn Hart, while Levond Daniels gained his bearings in his first-ever NFW match. Daniels was an unknown quantity heading into the affair, but we kind of knew what we’d get out of Hart and somewhat out of Austin. Austin’s a little raw, a little young and a spent a little time watching Castor Strife’s back rather than wrestling for his own. Shawn Hart’s the one man you always take as a joke until he sneaks out with a huge title win." (CUTTO: HART ducking an AUSTIN clothesline, rakes the eyes on the spin around and dropkicks AUSTIN out of the ring! DANIELS stalks in behind with a hammering elbow to the back of the head and starts laying martial art kicks to the downed HART. CUTTO: DANIELS hitting a flap jack aided knee to the face on HART! CUTTO: AUSTIN springboarding into the ring and catching DANIELS with a shoulderblock! CUTTO: AUSTIN going for a German Suplex on DANIELS, but DANIELS flips out on his feet as a counter and catches AUSTIN with a vicious side kick to the back of AUSTIN’S leg!) RICE: "Well, it became apparent early that Daniels had some kicking and chopping skills to say the least – a martial arts background against two more traditional cruiserweights. Needless to say, the action got quick… and it got RISKY." (CUTTO: HART with a hurricanrana on DANIELS, but eats a running knee smash from AUSTIN as he tries to hook DANIELS’ legs! CUTTO: AUSTIN catching a DANIELS front kick and twisting him with a Dragon Screw into an STF! HART breaks it up with a lunging axe handle smash! CUTTO: HART dropping DANIELS with an INVERTED Atomic Drop, but eating a vicious elbow smash from AUSTIN! CUTTO: AUSTIN cinching in the ANACONDA, but never sees DANIELS coming from behind with a cracking kick to the back of his head! As AUSTIN rolls out of the ring, HART staggers up and eats a REVERSE TORNADO KICK! DANIELS covers and gets the 3 count!" RICE: "In the end, LEVOND DANIELS capitalizes on the frenetic action and is able to move onto the night’s final match! But who would he be facing….still needed to be answered." Jack Harmen vs. La Estrella 100% vs. Lowell RICE: And that would be either LOWELL, JACK HARMEN or LA ESTRELLA 100%!" (CUTTO: LA ESTRELLA standing in the ring, awaiting introductions...) RICE: Never a good sign that you're not getting any music intro... Anyway, La Estrella 100% tends to give a little less than his name implies, but he fit the weight expectation and so entered the match with as much a chance as the "high flying" Jack Harmen and the occasionally insane Lowell. (CUT TO: With La Estrella and Lowell in the ring, Jack Harmen enters to Crazy Train. As he nears the ring…) RICE: La Estrella did what he does best, which isn’t much, but he does attempt a major flippy-do-hickey over the top rope. It surprised Jack, not enough for the attempt to actually connect with Harmen, but enough that Jack’s sidestep caused him to stumble just a bit. That pretty much put La Estrella 100% out of 90% of the match. We’ll get to his other 10% in a few moments. (CUT TO: Harmen nails Lowell with a missile dropkick. He goes for the cover and gets a two. Picking up Lowell , Harmen hefts him for a suplex but somehow Lowell slips out, falling in behind Harmen. Stunned, Lowell pushes Harmen away. Jack hits the ropes chest-first and bounces back toward Lowell who catches him by the head and drops down with him to the mat, slipping over for a cover.) RICE: For Lowell ’s fans, this seemed to be his moment. It got a near fall. Lowell , shocked by his success, popped up and seemed to question what he should do. As Harmen got to one knee, Lowell ran into the ropes and returned. It looked like a perfect opportunity for a stiff knee to the face, but Lowell stopped himself and then grabbed a headlock. Normally, a good wearing down tactic, but not exactly the best move with two opponents. (CUT TO: After several moments, La Estrella came behind Lowell and kicked him in the back of the head to a surprising pop from the crowd. Excited, La Estrella runs into the ropes and returns with a flying cross body block that Lowell sidesteps.) RICE: Lowell seemed a bit confused by which would be the better opportunity. He’d been wearing down Jack Harmen for the last several minutes. He had La Estrella stunned from a botched move. The confusion didn’t last though, as both men started to get to their feet, Lowell grabbed both in a headlock. A minute or so later… (CUT TO: Lowell being hefted up by both Harmen and Estrella for a double belly to back suplex. As all lay there, the referee began his 10 count then realized La Estrella had an arm still covering Lowell .) RICE: It nearly happened, to everyone’s shock, as La Estrella almost made the cover, but Jack Harmen had been in a few more of these matches than most and made the save by lifting up Lowell ’s shoulder closest to Jack. Harmen then got to his feet. When La Estrella got to a knee, he found Jack Harmen’s own knee plastering Estrella’s face. Jack went for a cover but Lowell was able to break it up. Estrella rolled to the apron as Lowell whipped Jack into the ropes and got a flying forearm for his troubles. Harmen kipped up and put himself in the corner measuring for the Locomotive as the crowd popped for the apparent ending to the match. (CUT TO: Harmen nails Lowell with the Locomotive Super Kick and made the cover. As the referee’s hand hit for the 2nd time, Jack notices Estrella springing to the top rope. Estrella leaps. Jack gets to his feet and in one smooth motion catches Estrella’s outstretched legs, dropping him with a powerbomb before turning Estrella over with the LIONTAMER Elevated Boston Crab.) RICE: To everyone there, this was the way the match would end. Harmen had already sent Lowell , running him over like a Locomotive, and now he had La Estrella 100% locked into his LIONTAMER. La Estrella didn’t tap immediately though, I would say it was because he was still stunned from the powerbomb, and when he did tap, it was two seconds after Lowell crawled over and put La Estrella in a headlock. WE'LL BE BACK WITH THE NIGHT'S FINAL CONCLUSION! (FADEAWAY) 'SOUTHERN HEAT' FINALS (FADEIN: A tri-screen shot of LEVOND DANIELS, LOWELL and JACK HARMEN.) RICE: So, it all came down to LEVOND DANIELS – the newcomer martial artist, JACK HARMEN and LOWELL – two men with quite a history. SO LET’S GET ON WITH IT and describe the second HEAT final that could lead to great things! Our three amigos entered one at a time, with Lowell staying far away from High Flyer. They've spent a lot of time over the years working for the same companies, but this humble reporter doesn't think they've ever been across the ring from each other. Chalk it up to Harmen's tendency to be higher up on the card, and my reluctance to take the time to actually look up the facts. Anyways, predictably, Jack Harmen started things off by attacking Lowell as soon as he tentatively stepped through the ropes, angered at Lowell's insinuation into his finisher during their preliminary match. (CUTTO: HARMEN punching and kicking the smaller Lowell to a raucous cheer, letting the Lunatic out to play. Levond Daniels, for his part, stepped back and watched it happen.) RICE: Daniels appeared to have an understanding of the situation and the rules: you needed two decisions to win this match, and if Jack and Lowell beat each other down long enough, he could slide in and steal one. (RICE smirks…) RICE: Alright…'Steal' isn't the right word, but it's the closest one that fits. Jack landed several hard kicks into Lowell's knees before picking him up by the head and dropping him back down with a hard kneebreaker. (CUTTO: LOWELL popping up on adrenaline and immediately collapsing, rolling around the ring with his hands on both knees.) RICE: On impact, Lowell rolled in pain, and Jack - the veteran in him overriding how pissed he was at Lowell - locked eyes with Daniels. (CUTTO: Levond Daniels gesturing that he won’t get in Harmen's way.) RICE: YOU FOOL! Seriously, High Flyer was keeping an eye on Daniels, but since he wasn't getting involved, Flyer took what he had, and went for a cover. Two count. Not to be dissuaded, Jack scooped Lowell and dropped him face first on the mat, and immediately turned and rolled him into a Flyerboard - a modified bow and arrow submission, making sure to keep a shoulder off the mat. It wasn't long before Lowell was calling for a submission, in agony over the pressure on his knees. (CUTTO: The ref signaling for the bell to ring as HARMEN breaks the hold with a sadistic smile…) RICE: The bell rang and Lee- Baby Sims made the official announcement that Jack Harmen had gone up 1-0-0 in the match, and he needed only one more decision to win the match. Of course, he held onto the Flyerboard. Why wouldn't he? (RICE shrugs sarcastically) RICE: Of course, Levond Daniels saw his opening, and landed a precision dropkick four inches off the mat, right on the side of Jack Harmen's head. The Flyerboard was broken and Lowell fell to the mat in a heap, where Daniels scooped him and sent him chest - first into the corner. At least, he would've - if Lowell's knees hadn't given out and he fell, smacking his face into the middle turnbuckle.Daniels scooped High Flyer and sent him over the top rope, crashing to the floor. Smart strategy, I think. He turned just in time to meet Lowell, climbing to his feet; just in time for Lowell to eat a Reverse Tornado kick. (CUTTO: Three count. We’re at 1-1-0.) RICE: Now, Lowell was the odd man out with no pinfalls…but with the rules he still had a shot at winning the match! Daniels stayed on the offensive…picked him up and slammed him, and immediately went for another cover. High Flyer stopped that with a legdrop from the top. He pulled Daniels to his feet and sent him into the corner with a shove, and was immediately on top of him with a series of punches and kicks, slowly dropping Levond to the mat. (CUTTO: Lowell recovering. Cross corner whip by Harmen, and a clothesline making Daniels slump. But no sooner did Harmen move in for the kill, than Lowell jumped on his back with a sleeper! "LOWELL OWNS THIS ****!" shouted Lowell at the top of his lungs.) RICE: (shaking his head in disbelief) Lowell out of nowhere with the sleeper and continued pulling backwards against Harmen's struggles, but neither man would give. Until Daniels, recovering himself, gave High Flyer a dropkick, and watched with surprise as it caused him to roll backwards. (CUTTO: LOWELL using the momentum for a pinfall attempt!ONE... TWO... THREE. 1-1-1.) RICE: Lowell rolled free and jumped in the air like he'd won the lottery, obviously oblivious to the fact that there was still one fall to go. Unlike the prior falls, Jack Harmen wasn't nearly as out of it as Lowell was, and he fought back to his feet immediately. Lowell was less of a concern this time: Harmen and Daniels kept tossing him to the outside as they beat on each other, trying their best to get a permanent upper hand that could be used to get the victory. Finally, Harmen ducked a clothesline attempt and rebounded with the Locomotion; something that Daniels didn't get up from. But Harmen wasn't finished… (CUTTO: HARMEN perched on the top rope – FROG SPLASH! COVER! LOWELL COLLAPSED ON TOP OF BOTH OF THEM! ONE! TWO! THREE! CUTTO: Harmen shoving Lowell off him…) RICE: There was a buzz in the crowd, and a decent sized ovation/WTF-ness when referee GREG HERPIN stood between LOWELL and JACK HARMEN and raised BOTH arms in victory. Not coincidentally on that note, Lowell escaped the ring… leaving JACK HARMEN frustrated, but possibly hopeful that the GRAND PRIX would finally be his…unless LOWELL and ‘WILD’ HEAT winner LEGION would have anything to say about it! That’s it for this show fans, we’ll see you in RALEIGH for RELOADED!
