-- I thought some of the character segments were pretty dry. -- The flashback with Shawn Matthews shed a lot of light on things for me, although if Avery Everette didn't die in the barn explosion, when did he die?
Anyway, I thought it was a very solid start to Season II. Now I just can't wait til us misfits out East get to show our stuff ;)
