NATRONE RICE PRESENTS... 'Behind the Revolution' Featuring Randall 'Impulse' Knox. Part two of a prerecorded series. Of Masks and Men IMPULSE: So I'm twenty two years old, fresh out of college and working for a telemarketer during the week and wrestling for a fairly unscrupulous promoter on the weekends. I hated being so far away from all my friends in the city, but I couldn't afford to go back unless I was working full time, and I knew the only real shot I had at that was to be wrestling full time for a company that was at least moderately successful. To that end, I spent almost every cent I had outside rent and food sending tapes to every promoter I could get an address for. I got one response: Craig Miles and the NFW. I assumed, at the time, that he remembered when I wrestled Blaine Hollywood during Rookie Night in the first Ultratitle season, and when we had a chance to talk in person, he seemed to talk to me like he knew me. Later I found out that he may or may not have remembered my first match with his company, and he talks that way to everyone. But he told me he liked what I could do in the ring and offered me a contract on the spot, no tryout match with the New Frontier, no nothing: you get a contract and an opportunity, and you can make of it what you will. Then he asked me if I wanted to do anything specific to enter the company, and I froze. Remember, I thought he might have offered me a contract because he remembered me from four years prior, and I thought he might have been influenced by the fact that he was friends with Flair and McGinnis to give me the spot. I also remembered all the 'second generation' wrestlers and 'protege' wrestlers who were granted top shelf entry points and more TV time that they ever deserved and never earned, and I said no. I don't want it. I told Craig Miles I wanted to wear a mask, and I wanted to wrestle completely anonymously and either stand or fall based on my wrestling ability alone. JJ and his clone Troy might say otherwise, based on nothing but their own warped rewrite of reality, but I entered this company with nothing but the promise of an opportunity, and I paid my dues over and over again. I took beating after beating, and loss after loss while I acclimated myself to the company, and then I went out there and put myself on the map at WrestleStock. I don't care how you spin it, but you can't politic your way into lasting a hundred and eighteen minutes in a caged - ring Battle Royal. So I worked, and I worked, and I worked, and I started to build a following based on nothing more than what I could do in the ring. From the moment I started wrestling to the moment I took my mask off, I think I had exactly one live interview. It was all about the wrestling. Why did I take it off? Because I had just become NFW Television Champion, and in my opinion, I had earned it based on nothing but my ability. The mask had seemed to serve its purpose, so I took it off. The difference between myself and the various members of the Windham Clan, is that I've never name - dropped the people who trained me for career advancement. Fan Question Why do you still refer yourself as Impulse even though you're unmasked? Is it because the names of "Randall(s)" and "Knox" are overused in NFW history? IMPULSE: Honestly, I never really thought much about it. The first name I ever wrestled under was the admittedly - easy 'Sudden Impulse' Randall Knox. It was a no-brainer to shorten it for my full time career, but when I first took off my mask and you, Natrone, and 'Just' Mark referred to me a few times as Randall Knox, I did insist that you keep using the name Impulse. I don't really have a compelling reason as to why, just that it's an easy separation between my public and private lives. I mean, just taking a look at my own friends. The woman who did my entrance music, Queen V, is really only called by her real name by her family. Everyone else calls her V. Jennifer Arroyo, of Kittie, DeMilitia, Suicide City, and the last season of Big Brother is known to the world at large as Ms. JennCity. Even when you boil it down to people who aren't even famous, my dear friend Annie is known to the world as Willow WaxDoll. It's just a way to separate my public life from my private life at this point, honestly. My mother calls me Randall. Rose calls me RK, and my friends who have known me since before my career began call me RK. Amongst wrestling fans, I'll pretty much answer to 'Impulse' or 'Knox.'
