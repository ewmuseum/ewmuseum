Vice — 22 March 2004 MEMBER INFO ---------------------- MEMBER Name: [name withheld] Email Address: [detail withheld] WRESTLER INFO ------------------- Wrestler Name: Chelsea Vice Height: 6'0'' Weight: 148 lbs. Hailing From: Tempe, AZ Handedness: Right Gimmick: Was fed dreams of being a star since she was a child, her own personal success has become an obsessive goal. After failed attempts in acting, singing, and dancing, she decided to play her hand in fighting, since she was skilled and won previous tournements.
Looks and Ring Attire: http://www.jakeahancock.com/images/vicebodydone.jpg Ring Entrance: Chelsea just struts to the ring like she's hot ****, because she is. Disregard for everything and everyone, because she's the best.
Theme Music: "Cute Without the E" by Taking Back Sunday Tactics/Style: Fighter/MMA/Mat-wrestling. Because she was first trained in MMA, that will be her general stance. She is trained in Boxing, Tae Kwan Do, and Ju jitsu.
Move List: Axe Kick Jumping Roundhouse Kick Standing Side Kick Crescent Kick Tornado Kick Roundhouse Kick Jabs Uppercuts Hooks Armbar Takedowns Knee Bars Shoulder Locks Submission/Weardown holds: Arm bar. STF. Dragon Sleeper. Mexican Surfboard. Various leg and arm holds. Finisher: Famous (Shoulder-lock takedown, slamming the face/neck) Strengths: 1) She's manipulative. 2) Extensively trained in fighting, dangerous. 3) Intent on being recognized as a powerful force. Weaknesses: 1) Gets upset easily. 2) A high turnover rate for allies (remember, she's manipulative). 3) Is a woman, which aren't always taken seriously. History: After a somewhat troubled adolescence, dropping out of college and dealing with her father's suicide, Chelsea has decided to achieve the goal that has been set for her since she was a baby.
She wants fame. And dammit, she will go to any lengths to get that. More will be added soon, just a matter of getting it all organized.
