Off to Valinor — 7 April 2004 Just thought this might be a cool little feature we can try and do. I'm just going to start this thread and if you're wrestler has any specific injury you're working an angle with, so I can reference it as well as keep the website report updated.
You can update whenever you like, just be sure to list the week you're updating... Yes I realize this might give away "gameplans," but if you're opponent has been reading the cards they'll probably be keying on your injury anyway.
I think it can be something cool that adds a little 'realism' to what we're trying to emulate in the league.
Anyway, we're in Week 11 - I'll give an example: WEEK 11 - NFW SOUTH: SHANE SOUTHERN (foot, minor) That way I know when it is, who it is, the injury and the severity. For severity, I figure minor, major, out can be the 3 classes we use. Hope this is used. I want it to be... If not....:( And yes, Steve...you can use "broken heart" as an injury. eyoung — 8 April 2004 Eddy Love Enterprises issued the following statement "As a result of the illegal and dangerous loaded boot of professional wrestler Sam Southern, Eddy Love has suffered a concussion and a strained neck. Independently, either of the injuries would keep a good man out of action for weeks to come. Eddy's high pain threshold has led us to label each injury individually as minor, however the combination of the two should be considered major. We ask all Eddy Love Lovers to pray for Eddy so that he may be at 100% for his match against close friend Joey Melton"
