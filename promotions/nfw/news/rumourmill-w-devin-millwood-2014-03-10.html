RumourMill w/ Devin Millwood

10 March 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
While we wait for the official Q2 ROAD TO FUTURESHOCK to get its official itenerary, let's go over some of the craziness that's happened as a result of RANDOM RUMBLE 2014. If you haven't read the show, be forewarned that these are spoilers! To read the show, CLICK HERE. Is there a chance Jack Harmen could be subject to lawsuits? Jack Harmen won't be personally liable for any of his actions or lies leading up to the events of the RANDOM RUMBLE. Either Eddie Mayfield or Calvin Carlton will be the ones seeing the brunt of the paperwork, but as we have investigated - based on Harmen's actions of faking cancer on live television or taped segments, he will be able to draw upon NFW's categorization of sports "ENTERTAINMENT" as a way out of any personal consequences. Moreover, and what's possibly worse - Harmen never made any personal commitments to any charities or organizations to be held accountable to... Speaking of cancer. . . is Cancer/Pat Black signed with Calvin Carlton as his manager? We've been reaching out to get confirmation if this was a pre-planned attack at the end of Random Rumble, but the more and more we dig in. . . the more it becomes apparent that Cancer's actions at the Random Rumble were in the moment and a complete surprise to everyone in the ring, including Harmen and Carlton. Certainly Eddie, maybe not Castor. What is Castor going to do with the blank contract if he has a shot at the World Championship? I think Eddie Mayfield may be more upset about this than anything else if Castor brings in anything related to his other wrestling ventures as a way to get back at anyone in NFW. In the past, NFW's really soured on Castor's extracurricular activities. The whole Mint Julep saga is nothing anyone wants to remember from a Reloaded ratings perspective. Is Jack Bryant really. . . FIRED? Short answer - YUP. This is confirmed by the Front Office on all accounts. Long answer - this was coming for a multitude of reasons. Much like Randall Knox before him, Jack Bryant's contract was coming up at a very weird point in NFW timing-wise. Unlike Knox, Bryant started worrying everyone once he literally went off the reservation and refused any press interviews, or the subsequent release of his interviews with Kerry O'Connor. O'Connor has gone on record that there was nothing earth-shattering in the interviews, which led some to believe that Bryant was just fed up with the out of the ring responsibilities in NFW. Well, for someone that would be on the cusp of demanding dollars like Castor, nobody felt comfortable with Bryant being the face of the company, if he didn't want to face anything outside the ring. He's also suffered some brutal injuries and brutal losses, and maybe although not confirmed - NFW was forcing him to rest his body until he's right physically and mentally. Anyone else we should be worrying about? Anyone that doesn't have a belt and that blank contract in Castor's hands. DUDE -- Randalls and JJ? WTF? The other big surprise of the night was Mike Randalls returning, not so much in who he went after. . . but who introduced him. There's no reason for these two to be together, there's no way these two will trust each other, but if I'm Dorchester Stratton or Blaine Hollywood - I'm rethinking our broken friendship immediately. . . . Alright, we'll try and get more of these out there during Q2 as we head to Futureshock, but please make sure to reach out for any topics you want covered!
