ROAD TO FUTURESHOCK Itenary Released!

1 April 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With NFW hitting up the road for some house shows to tune-up and get ready for the ROAD TO FUTURESHOCK, the FRONT OFFICE has released a preliminary press itenerary for the next few months of NFW action! And of course, FUTURESHOCK II will be at M&T BANK STADIUM in BALTIMORE, MARYLAND. And by the way, if you're not staying close to the NFW twitter and archives sections, you may start missing some of those road reports as part of the daily ESEN RINGSIDE telecasts. Anyway, without future adieu. . . let's check out the preliminary itenerary! ================================================ ================================================ NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING GOES DOWN SOUTH!
