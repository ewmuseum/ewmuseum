Caught On Tape

18 Jul 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Posting it here also because it concerns Melton and Carlton.) (FADE-IN: VIP Parking at The Pond in Anaheim. Less than an hour after PRIMETIME concluded Mark Windham retreats from his mental undoing. The walk across the parking lot is soft, and appreciatively undisturbed. Six hours ago, when Windham arrived fashionably late for the biggest CSWA show of the year, his body was soothed by a gentle blowing of the wind. The California weather sells itself. In the early evening, Mother Nature was doing her best to sway Windham. Serenity can be a saving grace. A duffel bag thrown over his right shoulder, the California breeze attempted to calm Windham, purge his mind of insanities, as he stepped to the arena. Mark heard the whispers, and though he knew they offered sage advice the magnificence of the setting only fostered self-doubt. The sort of distrust The Lost Soul contemplated for the last seven years while lying safely hidden, from the evils cast within, in the cusp of a secret society. Better to be lost, in a fictitious world, one that offers even the most conflicted men protection, than to be out tempting others with what you’ve purported to learn. No, a beautiful setting merely reinforces the spiritual awakening that Windham so vainly prayed to be shielded from. The Lost Soul loves chaos. For it’s been the quiet in his life that has always betrayed him. Silence, and comfort create opportunity for Windham to hear the beating of his own heart. Tick. Tick. Tick. Mark Windham was trapped. Lost in the depths of reality, too far gone to call upon the safety of his secret world. This time, he had to deal. This time, Windham refused to pray, and his punishment was trusting in himself. The awakening has always been that Windham’s heart was in danger of “going off.” By the time the doors closed behind him and his peers were casting annoyed looks, Mark knew he would hide no longer. He’d turn within and face the beating head on. As morning approaches, and the World Champion slinks to his car, he revels in the aftermath, in the knowledge that some battles are fought to be lost. The night has been long, longer than any in recent memory. The reason? Eli Flair nearly killed him. He knew Flair was tough, but if it was possible to be surprised, Mark was. Eli was near unbearable. Somewhere in the midst of pain, between the indecision of remaining conscious, Windham wondered what it’d be like to slip behind the shield again. Yet Mark walked out with the World title. A mess left behind him, but that’s for another day. It’s an evening he just wants to be over. Get in the rental, drive to the hotel and sleep. Tomorrow he’ll board a plane and fly back to Sweetwater. With distance from what he’s done, he’ll be able to assess. As Mark approaches the rental, his tired walk slows to a sudden stop. Someone’s waiting for him, casually resting up against the driver’s side door. Joey Melton. Mark smiles. His dry lips part, ripping fresh cuts on the inside of his mouth open; a feigned smile victim of Windham’s own teeth. Pushing blood back with his tongue, Mark moves within two feet of Melton and stares. Mark: Am I going to have to guess what this is about Joey? Joey: You haven’t changed in fifteen years. Mark: Ah. Joey: No more smarter than you were then. Mark: Joey? (Windham pauses, giving Mother Nature the opening to play with his emotions once more. She declines.) Move. (Joey steps aside enough so that Windham can unlock the car door. As Windham swings the door open, Melton grabs Mark’s right arm.) Joey: Good showing tonight. I’ve missed that side of your personality. Repression is a nasty sin. Mark: (smiles) It’s a shame Merritt thought better of going ahead with your match with.......who was it? Shamon? You’re a freakin’ joke, man. Look at yourself. What’s sad is, you’ve never failed a drug test. Joey: (deadly serious) You try to go over on Ivy again I’ll do you in. (Mark’s eyes sober up.) Joey: Yeah. That attention span got a bit longer didn’t it? Mark: I’m goin’ let you leave on your own power, Joey. Call it an old friend’s benefit. Joey: You touch her again... (Windham snatches his arm out of Melton’s power.) Mark: What is she to you, Joey? A pin-up? Don’t ever talk to me about that woman again. Or I swear... (Melton burrows his eyes as if to understand a truth that he previously thought was non-existent.) Joey: You sick freak. To go out there and do that to her. (In a short burst of rage, Mark reels back and drops Joey with a hard right hook. Melton crumbles to the ground. Windham grabs a handful of Joey’s hair and nails him twice more. Mark: (standing over Joey) Pathetic. (Mark turns to get in the car. Joey, in a daze and bleeding from the right corner of his mouth, rolls over.) Joey: She’s got class, Mark...that’s why. You’re hell is coming for treating---. (Windham backs the car up, turns, and speeds out of the parking lot. Joey sits up and feels the blood with his right index finger. He grabs the back of his neck and moans.) Joey: You can’t treat a LADY LIKE THAT! MARK!!! (beat) (Melton’s getting too old to handle confrontations like this.) VOICE OFF-SCREEN: AND CUT!! (As Joey gets to his feet, Calvin Carlton sneaks out from behind a nearby car, with a Digital Video camera in hand. Carlton’s beaming from ear to ear.) Joey: Tell me you got that. I’d hate to think I spilled blood for no apparent reason. Calvin: CAUGHT ON TAPE! It’s an Oscar MISTER MELTON! Those fools at FOX did not have a clue in denying you a “Behind The Glory” request. No clue WHAT-SO-EVER! Joey: (feeling his teeth) I think he knocked a cap off. (Carlton helps Joey to the hood of his car.) Calvin: You’re bleeding. (Melton shoots Carlton a sarcastic look.) Calvin: Very dramatic. Joey: (considering the weight of Carlton’s words) Zoom in. Not too close. These pores have been through it tonight. (shakes his head) This is how I look when I get bumped from the program. Calvin: That fool’s crazy ass wasn’t worth it tonight. I saw his nails. He WAS right. They needed to be cut. Joey: The man doesn’t need to import a manicurist from Spain to be able to work! (Melton winces in pain) Calvin: I’m seeing a reverse angle shot being what sells her. There’s an auto mechanic who knows the youth minister at Momma’s old church, who has a cousin who LOOKS just like Windham! Would be soooo easy to get him to double as Windham to record that shot. Professionally edited, laid to Phil Collins...MISTER MELTON rushing to the aid of his damsel in distress. Joey: (When Carlton’s right he’s right.) What woman could resist that? Calvin: No hetero woman alive! Guarantee. Joey: Would be sweet, but no. When in doubt, keep it simple. Cut what we have together and mail it secretively to Ivy. OPERATION: Bed Last Woman On Earth is in effect. Calvin: She’ll give it up after seeing this. I know I would. Momma too! Joey: (holds mouth) There never used to be a price for lust. (Joey and Carlton sneak out into the ambiguity of the night.) (FTB)
