Crapping Thunder

4 Jun 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene- A very large water tank, JT Tyler, along with the SEAL team that appears to be around him as of late. The team don't have any weapons, in fact they're in wet-suits, as if they're about to jump in the water themselves.) SEAL #3: Should we jump in after them? TYLER: Naw, they're ok, it's only been about 90 seconds. (The Powell's QUICKLY surface) TYLER: Good job guys, you shaved 15 seconds off your last time. Now climb up here so we can do this again. SCOTT: (breathing heavy) AGAIN? Look, we know you won the little wager, we're gonna see this through...but do you have to shove our noses in it? TYLER: Guys...this is standard training...that is unless you think you're ready for the next step before we get to the good stuff? ROGER: (climbing out of the tank) What's that? TYLER: (looking at the SEAL Team) Guys.... (The SEAL Team then proceed to grab the brothers...they proceed to blindfold the Powells and tie one of their arms to their bodies.) SCOTT: Ummm...what is this? TYLER: Come at me... ROGER: And how do we do that? We're blindfolded? TYLER: You're facing me right now...just walk forward. (The Powell brothers feel their way forward, Tyler then trips up Scott and monkey-flips Roger.) TYLER: (getting up) Again... (The brothers again try, Scott swings at open air, Tyler spins him around as Roger swings, and one Powell then hits the other. The SEAL Team starts to chuckle.) ROGER: This is ridiculous JT. TYLER: Standard training... SCOTT: For who? Navy Seals? (pulling off his blindfold) (The SEAL Team all nod their heads...) ROGER: In case you haven't noticed, we're not Navy SEALS! We're professional wrestlers! TYLER: This is a way of life for these guys....it was for me too. ROGER: We just wanna wrestle, that's all. It's just a job. TYLER: (suddenly grinning) Now you get it! Let's REALLY train now. (Scene fades as they all continue to talk.)
