WEEK 8: Jonathan Marx vs. Armando Montezuma

17 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::Jonathan Marx is standing in the interview room of his mansion:: JONATHAN MARX: The tale of Armando Montezuma is one of great sadness. Armando Montezuma was one of the brightest young stars in all of NFW. Many people predicted that you were one of the front runners to make the playoffs in the NFW North. But they were wrong, dead wrong. Despite all of the potential you possess, you didn’t have the heart to make the most of it. You come out every week, give the least possible effort, collect your check and go home. Maybe there is something wrong with me, I don’t view what I do as work even when I’m asked to work two matches Thanksgiving Week. I wrestle because I love to wrestle. I go out and talk to the people because I love being a man of the people. I don’t consider this work, because this is what I love to do. Maybe I’m too old fashioned. Not in the way of some grumpy veterans who hate everything new and doesn’t realize that change in moderation can be good for wrestling. Nor in the fact that I still believe even in this “me” generation where veterans are all trying to hang onto their spots that we should all try to be nicer to each other instead of ripping each other apart outside of the ring. While there are things I disagree with that the NFW does, like stick fine wrestlers like myself into weapons matches, I don’t publicly rip apart the league. I don’t spit on Miles for making this ruling or that. We are all like a big family and while sometimes it seems more like the Manson family instead of the Partridge Family, the NFW is my home. I will stick with the NFW until the bitter end despite the forces from within that want to destroy it like those who want to destroy old school wrestling, I will fight with all my might to make sure that they will not be successful. Montezuma, it shames me that you and people like you, have given up the fight. If you love wrestling, you should fight for it. There are times when wrestling is hotter than other like any other sport, but a true man should stick by it through thick and thin. Prepare yourself because the era of Marxism is upon us.
