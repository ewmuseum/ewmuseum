Stepping Down

26 Jun 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in black armani suit, blood red shirt lacking a collar, and dark vested, sits at a desk in front of a wide window at night. He packs up a brief case, putting away papers,a pez dispenser,and a kitchen knife. 2 well-dressed men join him and he nods as one steps over to put out the fireplace with a bucket nearby and the other takes down a painting of a hanged man and places it in a box.)) GENTLEMAN #1: Sir? MANSON(pulling curtains closed): Yes? GENTLEMAN #1: We were told to take away this "Rayne's" personal effects as well adn have her report to personel. MANSON(taking out a cigarette): That's great. Do that. GENTLEMAN#2 (stepping away from fireplace): She worked directly for you, sir. MANSON(lighting up with a pentagram zippo): Listen, I'm only officially the commissioner for another 10 minutes. I can't be held reponsible for the whereabouts of all these new members. GENTLEMAN#1: Sir, her family has been calling. They're worried. MANSON(shurgs): Send them a ham, she'll show up, whoever she is. (Takes a drag) Miles is right, this is a good brand. ((2 movers enter and begin to take up a cabinet. Manson waves them off and opens it to take out a skull, a bowl, a tarot deck, several occult tailsmans on strings, and a picture of Bruce Campbell. He places them in a box. He hands it to one mover and instructs the other to take his briefcase.)) MANSON(turning to the other gentlemen): Well, how do I look? I know there's no commissioner position anymore, but those benefits and that raise does help. Not keen on that code of hnor thing, I must have passed out when they discussed it. No wonder I didn't see anyone on that board. Or maybe it was because they sat behind that one way mirror and cloaked their voices. GENTLEMAN#1: You look fine, sir. Your effects are being taken to the car. Please follow us. MANSON: Hold on one second. (Pulls out a spare pez dispenser with a devil's head on it. Takes a hit and a drag) Ahhhhh..now that's flavor country. Who's getting this office anyway? GENTLEMAN#2: Office rumor is that Miles has an ex-wife he's trying to keep quiet. MANSON: I think I met her, by the way, what about that? ((Manson points up at a large upside cross hanging from the ceiling.)) GENTLEMAN#1: To be frank, we don't know how you got it up there, and we don't know how to get it down either. ((Manson smirks, his teeth seemingly becoming greener by the instant.)) GENTLEMAN#2: ..then again..the rumor is it is a forgotten ex-wife of Miles.. ((Manson shakes his head and gestures for the men to lead the way. They step out into the hall as Manson takes a final look at the slick, polished marble floor. He looks to the sides to see if anyone is around. Then he takes a running start and slides across the floor, ala Tom Cruise, into the hall.)) ((FADEIN: The office hallway, lined on 2 sides. Secretaries with nose piercings, janitors, suited executives, crimson robed high priests of the dark arts, doormen, a goat, and a tattoo artists wait. The gentlemen stand at the end. MANSON takes a few steps forward...stops and looks over..)) EXEC(elbowing a comrade in the stomach): ****..we forgot..you start.. EXEC#2: Hell no....the way that guy used to stare at me.. ((He sees MANSON staring at him now. He turns pale as his hands come together slowly in a clap, repeated, again, and again as everyone joins, echoing the halls with applause. A small band starts playing at the other end of appropiate classical theme music for MANSON's departure. MANSON hands the goat a piece of linen from his pocket and pats it on the goat. He takes a moment with a robed figure.)) MANSON(his hand on the man's shoulder): I hope they don't cancel dark ritual Thursdays. I tell you, I don't know how we got through the work week without them. ((The man nods as MANSON walks by and out into an elevator, the applause continuing as he turns around, still staring out as the doors close.)) EXEC(still clapping along with everyone else): Can we stop? I mean..the band is still playing.. EXEC#2: What if..(looks around nervously)... he can still see us? ((The hallway remains in applause.)) ((FADEIN: The gentlemen departing from the front doors toward the vehicle as MANSON steps out. He walks on the first step as a small boy, dressed 20's style in suspenders and a bowtie and one of those annoying hats, steps forward with a rag and polish and starts taking care of MANSON's shoe.)) MANSON: So what do you think, Paco? How will the place go without me? PACO(still shining and in a thick accent): 'Ey, you done good here, Mister Manson. We won't forget that. Now you go and win that ultratitle. MANSON: I suppose, I have to now. (Looks down at his shining shoe) You never cease to amaze me Paco. (Throws a quarter) PACO(catching it in hands): Ahh..thank you there Mister Manson. Now you go out there an' make us all proud. Everytime we do a bloodletting before lunch, we'll be cheering you on. MANSON(pats Paco on the head like the goat and hands him another piece of linen from his pocket): Thank you, Paco. And I hope that cancer clears up eventually. ((A shining black hearse rides up and a gentleman gets out of the front seat and goes around to open the back. MANSON leaves Paco behind on the steps and goes to the hearse. There he pauses and looks back at NFW HQ, at the flagpoles where a SKULL&CROSSBONES pirate flag waves in the distance.)) MANSON(shaking his head): I bet they'll take that now down. ((He shrugs and takes another hit of pez as he climbs in the back of thwe hearse and lies down where a casket should, his arms folded across his chest. The gentleman shuts the backdoor and reurns to the front as the hearse starts, and then rides off into the rising sun.)) FTB
