Total Havok

26 Jun 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: A man in his mid 30's, he walks towards his car and gets in, he turns on the engine and pulls out of his driveway, the camera cuts to a shot from the perspective of a person sitting in the backseat. We see the man hit a button on his radio.) RADIO: Yo Shorty...It's ya Birthday...(Man hits button.) RADIO: No matter what they say...(Man hits button) RADIO: You know you're right...You know you're right. (Man hits button, this time the camera zooms in on the digital read out on the radio, and it shows 96.9. CUTTO: a Studio in downtown Boston. CUEUP: the Guitar solo on "Running down a dream" by Tom Petty. We see DONALD JAMES HAVOK sitting in the studio, wearing a grey sweater, black dress pants and the radio headset.) HAVOK: We are back, and don't for ONE second think that was Tom Petty on the guitar there, that was Tom going 'whoo-hoo' now and then, but that man couldn't play guitar to get himself out of a wet paper bag and don't you for one second forget this. Folks, we are back, and no, I will NOT quit going over the Supreme Court and how they have declared racism to be the LAW of the land...I want that to be made clear to you, racism is now offically the LAW of America...It's OK to tell a qualified white person that they can't have a job because you want to give it to an unqalified black, or hispanic person...This is one of the darkest days in American history I can recall, only the days directly after 9-11 can I remember something this awful happening...And President Bush's cowardly backing of this ruling, an alleged conservative republican...Well I've cut the cord with him, I can not vote for this man and I will do NOTHING to help him be re-elected. Now before we get to your calls on this issue...We have other issues to get into, and one of them has to be the new NFW...Now you know how I have time and time again shown you the liberal bias in the Boston Globe and the New York Times, but today I have to tell you about a new type of bias, and that is basically the bias of the old against the new. Now I know that the conference I am in had Eli Flair and Joey Melton, I know that the other division got Hornet and Evan Aho, I know that riots broke out when these men were asked to please, if they could for a brief moment, leave Mount Olympus so that they could show us, a bunch of foolish mortals, how things work in the ring. That they would let us know how great they are. Now that's all well and good, a bunch of fans brought into a building can scream and yell and carry on because the NFW has recruited some of the most over protected, over rated stars in the history of wrestling. Look, I look at this division I'm in, and it's basically a bunch of slackjawwed, uneducated men who would be pumping gas if it wasn't for the fact that they have over-developed upper bodies and went to the Michael Manson school for Goths...Or they are in fact that sadist nutcase...Or they are the chosen immortals from that other company that Betty Bluehair will cost me a weeks pay if I mention, or they are lastly but not least, the underachiver of the century, the man who dropped the ball more brutally then Buckner, that sad sack of a man, Monty. I heard Monty talk about how Bloodhunt won the belt and went home, but let's look at his NFW career, which was basically being BEATEN by a 90 year old, and whenever he got within 50 feet of Bloodhunt he dropped to his knees in pant-peeing fear of being beaten to death. Monty is a coward and a nitwit, he talks big but he's never backed it up, he has been a undeniable failure in his run in NFW. But this isn't about all of them, this is about me, and this is about the NFW...The NFW needs something more then just a wrestling machine, or a man who can talk and make the lemmings eat out of his hand, which is something I will NEVER do, I seek to challenge my fan base, which is why you who listen to me ARE Boston's best and brightest...I seek to educate and inform, to entertain and to make you strive to learn more about your world and your part in it... And I will educate you all about the anti-American anti-merit and anti-talent bias in NFW...I will show you everything, I will leave no stone unturned, I will not for one second allow anyone or anything from preventing me to give you the unvarnished truth of the matter...And that, I will swear to you, under pain of death Donald James Havok will bring the truth to the masses and they shall be enlightned...(FADEOUT)
