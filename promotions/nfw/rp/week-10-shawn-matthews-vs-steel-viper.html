WEEK 10 - SHAWN MATTHEWS vs. STEEL VIPER

15 Mar 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN to "Showtime" SHAWN MATTHEWS. He's wearing a fedora, and a pair of distressed denim jeans, with a "Back to the Future" ringer t-shirt on. Matthews is walking around the local mall, drinking an Orange Julius.. Matthews walks out the door of the mall, and lights up a cigarette. Showtime takes a few puffs, and then pounds more of the drink..) SHAWN MATTHEWS: Looking back on the first half of this season, I was on quite the roll in the beginning. Raising all sorts of hell, as usual, but this time on a bigger scale. Hornet, Shane Southern, Joey Melton... and the list goes on and on. I was competing on the same level as these guys, and BEATING.. RESPECT into them. Somewhere down the line though, I got sidetracked. I was too tied up in imagining what Shawn Matthews could be, and stopped being who I truly was.. the COCKIEST, DIRTIEST, most LOUD-MOUTHED ASS in the world of professional wrestling. There comes a point in your life when you have to give up on what you should be, and realize what you really are. Coming to that realization, I was able to beat Chris McMillan... a decent wrestler, but a wrestler who's not at the LEVEL of Shawn Matthews. (Matthews puffs on the smoke a little bit more..) Week10, I'm going to be stepping into that ring with Steel Viper.. a man who I came up with. A guy who was starting out about the same time I was. That's where the similarities end, though. Viper's gone on to bigger and better thing, and I reveled in mediocrity for a bunch of years. WHATEVER! It ends, NOW! I've got my sights set on what's important, again, and that's the Ultratitle. It's what I've wanted, right from the start, and I'm going to start a ONE-MAN-UPRISING, just to get to where I need to be. Yeah, I've got Aaron Douglas in my corner, and his support has been great... but it's NOT what has made Shawn Matthews GREAT! It's all about looking within, and knowing what works. I'm back at that point, and Viper... you're going to be the next man to see the REJUVENATED Shawn Matthews. You're very unlucky in that aspect, because I'm going to make sure that you don't forget my name. I'm going to make sure that NOBODY laughs at Shawn Matthews, EVER... AGAIN! Week Ten, your ASS gets SMOKED! (FADEOUT, as Matthews puts out the cigarette right in the lens of the camera..)
