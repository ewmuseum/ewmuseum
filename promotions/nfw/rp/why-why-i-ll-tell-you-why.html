Why? Why? I'LL TELL YOU WHY!

28 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*camera cuts to a close up of a very pissed off Jason Payne. Beads of sweat pouring down his face. His brown eyes burning with intensity, he bores a hole through the camera lens with his intense glare. After a few seconds of measured silence, he speaks, his voice soft, deep, just above a whisper* Everywhere I go, everyone I meet. They all ask me the same thing. And I reply the same way. *Payne's face contorts with rage as he screams* I KNOCK THEIR STINKIN' TEETH DOWN THEIR THROAT!!!! *his face remains red with blood as his anger boils. He composes himself with a couple of breaths, and then resumes* No one need know why except myself. I have my reasons. It is no one elses concern. If you want to ask questions, then perhaps you should ask them to Dread. Though if you are anything like I remember, you are just going to deny any knowledge of what I am talking about and feign ignorance. Of course, feigning ignorance was never a problem for you. *the camera begins to shake a bit and Payne looks at the camera operator skeptically* Are you nervous? Do I frighten you? You had better be scared. You saw what I did out there to Dread tonight. The same thing could happen to you. It could happen to anyone that sticks their nose in Payne's business. *he turns his attention back to the camera* Which kind of puts "Rickedy" Rick Ryconik at the top of Payne's list. Now I don't have any grudge to bear against you, but when you put your hands on Payne, that put you in the game. I play games that make grown men afraid to go out at night. I play games that hurt people. I play games, that end careers. It is apparent to me that your career is winding down old man. Your actions in the ring tell me that you wish to expediate your departure. I have no problems at all with that. *Payne chuckles a moment and then his face turns deadly serious* Let it be known that Jason Payne is now in NFW, and anyone, and I mean ANYONE, that tries to get in my way, will find out exactly what happens to those who make it a point of harassing a Dog of War. You think Dread is tough? You think Dread is big, bad, and hardcore? You ain't seen nothin yet. And when you do see it, you had better run. Know it. Payne is coming, and there is nothing, and I mean nothing that any of you, Dread, Ryconik, ANYONE, can do about it. Bank on it. *Jason Payne stares down the camera for a moment before sharing his glare with the camera operator* Now get the hell out of my face... *a blur of motion follows as Payne closes with the camera. The sounds of a small scuffle are heard, and the camera angle changes several times before it becomes apparent that the camera is lying sideways on the floor. With the floor taking up the left side of screen, a pair of boots are seen walking away* [the thread's next post — 28 Jul 2002] ::FADEIN on the NFW banner backstage at the Paul Tsongas Arena. MISTER DREAD stands there, attired as usual in jeans, harness boots, and a faded Dogs of War t-shirt. DREAD glares into the camera, his eyes blazing:: MISTER DREAD: I ain't gonna stand here and mince words tonight. Rick, thanks for the match tonight. And thanks for not taking it easy on me. Mister Dread was holding just a little in reserve, just for tonight. Just to step into the ring and face the Polish Prince one last time. And it would have been a hell of a match, too, if it had been allowed to play out to it's conclusion. But no. It couldn't play out clean. Something had to spoil it. But it wasn't a handfull of tights this time. No, it was someone I knew. Someone I trusted. Someone I loved like a brother. ::DREAD begins to pace, knuckling sweat out of his eyes:: MISTER DREAD: Jason Payne. It's been a long time. I had hoped I'd see you in the ring again. I had hoped that one day I would look over and see you on the end of a tag rope, just like old times. But what did I see? THE BOTTOM OF YOUR GOD*AMNED BOOT! It's been FOUR YEARS, Jay. Four years since the Dogs of War slpit. We always said we'd get back together somewhere, someday. But it was NEVER supposed to go down like this. All those times we stood side-by-side together in the ring, going toe-to-toe against any tag team with enough sand to get into the ring with the Dogs of War ... the legacy we built for ourselves ... ::DREAD stops, lowering his head for a moment:: MISTER DREAD: You just sh*t all over it. You sh*t on me, and you've forgotten what it means to be a Dog of War. Well let me tell you something, man. Dealing in death is the nature of the beast, Jason Payne. Our currency is flesjh and bone, and Mister Dread collects with exacting vengance. You've forgotten, but I won't let you forget for long. Count on it. ::DREAD storms off-camera as we FADE TO BLACK::
