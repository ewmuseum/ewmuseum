NFW: Front Office Episode Two

2 Aug 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: ESTABLISHING SHOT - 420 Avenue of the Americas, EXTERIOR, DAY. ESEN GLOBAL ENTERPRISES. The camera pans up to the top floors of the building, NFW OFFICES.) (CUTTO: A hallway, grey and drab, with sickly florescent lights overhead - your basic office hallway. The walls are grey, the cubicle walls are grey, and the floor is tiled - checkerboard patterns of grey and white. In the background of the shot is a copier nook, with several posters about office safety on the corkboard, underneath a huge framed photo of EDDIE MAYFIELD, in a suit and pointing menacingly at us - underneath the photo it reads 'MAYFIELD KNOWS YOU STEAL'. The camera pulls back as office people walk to and fro, working. Suddenly, a wind (!) starts picking up, as one woman walks by the camera and she smooths down her skirt which is trying to blow up, and paper from the copier room starts swirling around! Suddenly, around a bend comes SHAMON, Wearing an INCREDIBLY gaudy outfit - almost identical to what Michael Jackson wore in the 'I Wanna Rock With You' video - an impossibly sequined tunic, and matching lyrcra pants and SEQUINED UGG BOOTS, making him look like a walking disco ball. On his left arm he has a black armband with 'MJ' in white block letters. SHAMON skip-steps into the corridor, and stops, legs wide-apart, and points to the ground, and scissor kicks, and screams at the top of his lungs! Suddenly a squirrely-looking girl in a 'my first real job' outfit skitters around the corner, holding a white cardboard literature box, used to transport file folders. Written on the box in red marker: "NFW World Championship Bylines". She wince/smiles at SHAMON, with a part of respectfulness, the other part because she can't look directly at him from the glare of his outfit.) SHAMON: "WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOo! Sha... SHASHAFOOOOOO! Ok, I wanna WALK WITH YOUUUUU... ALL NIGHT! Dance to the copier ALL RIGHHHT... WALK WITH YOU! ME! WOOOOOOOOOO! (The young woman blinks repeatedly and nods, trying to decipher what SHAMON said. He starts to walk/dance down the hall, and she follows, but keeps crashing into his back because he keeps stopping to pose, or point at something, or jump up on his tiptoes. As he walks down the hall, the FLOOR TILES LIGHT UP AS HE STEPS ON THEM. The lady can't stop staring at the floor, which isn't helping the collisions. They get to the copier nook, and SHAMON cranes his neck around and shoots out his pelvis and points at the copier, and like magic - the copier lid opens! The INTERN'S eyes bug out!) INTERN: "How did you--?" SHAMON: "DO YOU REMEMBER THE TIME.... HOOOOOOOOOO! WHEN I GAVE YOU THOSE FILES? DO YA? DO YA? DO YA? REMEMBER THE TIME?" (INTERN looks at him with the longest "WTF" look, and after a beat, something clicks in her head, and she smiles, and opens the box, and starts fingering through files and hands SHAMON a file that says "JOE THE PLUMBER', just at the same time SHAMON starts jumping up and down and shaking his drippy jherri curl like a wet dog, singing "I Can't Help it! If I wanted to!" The INTERN recoils and shields herself with the file, but she gets sprayed with Curl Activator anyway. It's on her face, on her outfit, and all over the file folder and the box. He grabs the folder from her, and tries to open it, but a bunch of papers are stuck together now, all greasy! He cringes, along with the INTERN!) SHAMON: "Heeeee.... Heeeee - oh, oh man." (He scans the papers, looking REAL nervous, and tries to put some documents through the copier, but they get jammed before one copy spits out. He opens up some trays in the copier, and finds the document all coiled up inside the machine, and YANKS them out the hard way, and they rip in half as the INTERN bites her nails in the background! SHAMON looks around in a panic, but the INTERN produces a tape dispenser off of the counter, and SHAMON snatches it, and starts hastily taping documents together - not doing a good job and not paying attention to if the pieces match. The INTERN looks like she's about to interject, but keeps her mouth shut. After SHAMON finishes his tapejob, he holds the papers up to the sky, nodding to no-one in particular) SHAMON: "I'm starting with the man in the mirror! HOOO! I'm asking him to MAKE A CHANGE..." (SHAMON takes the crappy 'originals' and puts them back through the machine, and makes several copies of them. The INTERN makes worried faces as she begins collating and stapling the copies, as SHAMON dances around in the foreground.) (FADEOUT)
