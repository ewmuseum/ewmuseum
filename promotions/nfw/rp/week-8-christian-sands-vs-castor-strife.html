WEEK 8: Christian Sands vs. Castor Strife

13 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(For the most part, the quiet, book-lined study is quiet today. Only the muted sounds emanating from the small TV on the desk are audible - and even those just barely. Christian Sands sits in the great chair behind the desk, watching the screen carefully - a screen which is playing his own match from Week 7.) Sands: Bleh... I should've anticipated that. Better keep it in mind for next time. (As Sands returns to watching the match, the door swings open, admitting one of the big man's aides - this one a slender, short-haired brunette in a black dress.) Aide: Sir? (Sands looks up from the monitor.) Sands: Problem, Denise? Denise: No, sir. I'm just delivering this mail you got from the NFW front office. Sands: Give it here. (Gracefully, Denise walks to the desk and hands Christian an envelope. The big man pauses his tape and opens the envelope, pulling out the paper within and reading it.) Sands: Mmm... Oh, him. Heh. Denise: Will there be anything else, sir? Sands: Yes. Pull up all the tape you can find on Castor Strife. I want to have his life story on my desk ASAP. Denise: Yes sir. (The aide turns to leave. However, after a few steps, Sands speaks again.) Sands: Oh... and pull up some tape on Lindsay Troy. Denise: Sir? Sands: Call it idle curiosity. Denise: Yes sir. (With that, the aide turns and walks out the door, leaving Sands to turn back to his tape. He unpauses it and goes back to watching.) -------------------- (FADEIN: A study lined with books. CHRISTIAN SANDS sits in a huge chair behind the desk; a file folder sits on the desk in front of him.) Sands: At the risk of sounding immodest, I'll come right out and say that I'm on a roll lately. Between stomping Hiroshi into a fine powder and pinning the North Conference points leader in what was considered the best match of the night, I've got quite a bit of momentum behind me. Certainly more that the two other men who signed at the same time I did. I find it ironic that both Promo and Garvin were hyped as World Champions and received more recognition than I did... yet I've earned more points than both of them combined. Who's gaining prestige now, boys? But that's neither here nor there. The point is that I'm on a tear, and I don't plan on stopping anytime soon. I have indeed made an impact coming in, what with pinning the points leader of the North Conference and all, but there's much more where that came from - especially with a berth in the playoffs at stake. Which leads me to the man I'll be throwing down against this week. So, Castor, how's life in Hollywood, big shot? Oh, wait, I keep forgetting you failed up there because you lack talent. Sorry. I always forget. Obviously I've never met you. However, as usual, I've done plenty of homework - and from that I can say that I'm not impressed. Then again, I rarely am, unless you're a Rabesque or a Marx and actually have the talent and mentality that I can respect. Your case is rather interesting. You've got the talent, Castor - I've seen enough tape to realize that. I COULD respect that - if you weren't a f(bomb)ing cartoon. Admittedly, your little films are amusing. However, there's nothing to you beyond them. From what I can determine, you're little more than an empty man seeking to validate himself by making people laugh at his jokes. That's nice, but we're not laughing with you. I, for one, am laughing AT you. But character assessments aside - I'm on something of a roll lately, and I'll be damned if I stop now. Last week I pinned the points leader of the North Conference - thanks to that loss he's no longer the points leader. You could argue that Lindsay Troy's interference cost him the match, but if you want the truth - I was going to roll off that table anyway, having anticipated that a dive through it onto nothing would wreck Stratton. As it is, the fact that he went through it headfirst was something of a bonus. Any way you slice it, however, the fact remains that I defeated the top guy in the North Conference. And what did you do last week? Let a clown like Shawn Hart run verbal rings around you and hand your team a defeat, only to sit back and let Marx do all the work in your second match. Good boy. You COULD argue that you were gauging your energy to last through two matches - but in all honesty, any wrestler worth his salt should be able to handle two matches easily. I certainly could. Then again, this IS me we're talking about. But the fact remains that I'm on a roll. You, on the other hand, are wallowing. The roll will continue this week. With a playoff berth on the line, you're going to be facing me at my best - better, in fact. Obviously I'm always pushing myself beyond my limits. In matches like this one, however, I push somewhat harder. It's this constant surpassing of my limits that leads me to success - and it certainly HAS lead to plenty of success here. I have, after all, picked up two wins in a row. I'll be making it three and boosting my point total to thirty. When I arrived here, I was told I arrived too late to even consider aspiring towards UltraTitle. Now I have the opportunity to qualify for the playoffs and prove the naysayers wrong. And if you think I'm going to let this opportunity slip away... If you think I'm going to knuckle under at the behest of some Hollywood dropout with delusions of grandeur... You... have another thing coming... I'll be seeing you, Castor. (FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 3 Jan 2004] (FADEIN: CASTOR's outside of his apartment building, NEW YORK, with a cigarette hanging out of his mouth.) CASTOR: And so it comes to this. Instead of writing scripts, I'm stucking writing rebuttals for every glorified gas-station attendant they match me against week after week after week. Look here, Christian Samuelsson; number one, as you can see from all the tall buildings and dead homeless guys sleeping in their own urine, I'm not in Hollywood, but in New York. Number two, only male homosexuals take the time to watch my films, admittedly, but I'm glad you care enough about me to take a look at them. And number three, for a guy who's mentality is "points points points" you sure do put a large emphasis on how they are earned. Hey, if I can sit back and earn points while watching a bunch of handi-capables beat the sh*t out of each other, then fine by me. And what's with this, "I have so much respect for Rabesque and Marx" crap? What do you, owe them f*cking money or something? And while you're up there, do you mind letting me know how the inner workings of Rabesque's colon taste? Better get used to it, though, because just as I did with your hero Jean Claude "Mid-Card for life" Rabesque, and just as I even did with your man Marx earlier in the season, I am going to open up your jaw, and release a good shatting into your mouth. You ever been shat on before? It's a lot like talking yourself up to me, and subsequently getting your ass handed to you in the ring. I've beaten up Cancer kids tougher than you. Take my honest word for it; the name Christian Samuelsson doesn't scare anyone in the locker room, unless they, like you, came out of the poisonous womb that is the CSWA. And if that's not where you came from, it might as well be, because like them, you're a hopelessly lost ego who's just headed for the bottom. See you at the gates of hell, moron... (FTB) [the thread's next post — 8 Jan 2004] (FADEIN: Let's reach into the ol' setting grab-bag and pull out... a white backdrop with CHRISTIAN SANDS sitting on a chair in front of it. Deep, huh?) Sands: ... Jeez, Castor. Take a breath. (Smirk...) Sands: Nice of you to finally get with the program, Captain Punctual. Here I thought you'd gotten lost somewhere. Ah well, hope is hope, no matter how misplaced. But aren't we literal-minded? First off, the Hollywood comments weren't meant to be taken as meaning that you're, you know, actually IN HOLLYWOOD. It was MEANT as a reference to the whole 'B-Movie Producer' deal you've got going. Buuuuut I don't expect someone with your feeble wit to understand things like that, much less understand how to lace his own boots. Second... Sure, the handicapped can be a source of great amusement. Unfortunately, you're not going to find any in this match... at least not in my corner. I suppose we could count your obvious mental handicap, but I'm told that those sorts of people are sensitive about their troubles. But hey. Buck up, kid - riding the short bus to school every day is nothing to be ashamed of. Besides, you should be used to it by now. Either way, I guarantee you're not going to be sitting back this time. More like lying on your back with your shoulders on the canvas. And please, spare me the obvious sex joke, because I'm not in the mood to play the bigger-d*ck game. As for your threat to, as you so eloquently put it, take a shatting down my throat... (Snort...) Sands: Sorry, but I'm not much for scat fetishes. But I'll tell you what I'll do. Let's compromise. How about you show up and TRY to take a shatting... and I apply my boot liberally to your backside. Not to mention your face. Honestly, I was expecting more from you, yet you give me curtain-jerker drivel. To close this thing up, I'll point out that I have never, EVER wrestled for the CSWA... though it's flattering to know that I apparently scare the hell out of them despite not being under contract. Maybe if I'm lucky I'll scare them out of hibernation. But in all seriousness, it's not my problem if you're scared or not. I'm not here to run around playing Mr. Scary. What it boils down to is that I'm here to wrestle, win matches, and move up in the world. If someone's scared of that prospect, fine by me. If not, whatever. Either way, I'm content to beat them and move on. Hope you've got dental, Strife, because you're going to get your teeth kicked down your throat. (FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 10 Jan 2004] (FADEIN: Castor on his couch) CASTOR: Well I don't know, Christian, you asked me how life is IN Hollywood, I assumed you meant IN Hollywood. And all this time, I thought you were interested! Envious of me...what I embody. The truth is, however, I am quite envious of you. Besides your apparent mastery of the English language, you have something else going for you; innocence. You see things as I can no longer see them, as it is impossible for me to see them. You look at this..."sport"...this, GAME...and what you see if a power struggle between two men. You see it as a chance to progress, and this is why you percieve yourself as moving up in the world as a result of your victories. But it is your perception of victory that will never allow you to progress. I will outright give you what points I have in the standings, and it will not make you better, nor make me worse. I am not an animal, nor a God. I am both. I am a man. You...you're nothing but the points that are given to you. You are an extension of an extension. You're a snot in the nose of my wealth. It is not a question of BETTER...I was always a better man than you are. It is now a question of will power; who can make their actions an extension of their words, rather than the other way around? I will tear whatever's inside of your heart, and I will literally rape it in front you, if only to prove my point. And my point is that despite what you take from me, or what you think you are taking from me inside of that ring, it is but a trophy that I can always win back. But what I am willing and able to take from you is something much bigger than your trophy room aspirations...it is what allows you to progress. Win or lose, I will drive you into a puddle of your own sweat and blood, and it is there where you will smother, choke, and vomit. And after all this...if I decide on allowing you the points, then you will have them. But you will be no different than you were before you stepped in the ring with me...your movement will no longer be linear, but you will be stuck in place. THIS is how I defeat people, and it is in this fashion that I will conquer every man who calls himself something that he is not. (FTB)
