Car Wreck

23 Jul 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: An ambulance pulling in to Memorial Hospital, Jacksonville. The camera pans right and shows DORCHESTER STRATTON and VERONICA ABRAMS RUMSFELD. Stratton's hair is combed up to 50s Brylcreme perfection, wearing a white dress shirt, blue blazer over it, khaki pants. His face is stern and cold. Rumsfeld's dressed in her Sunday best -- pink slanted hat, a string of brown Nancy Pelosi beads, a sleeveless black dress with a pink bow around the midriff.) STRATTON: "You were WARNED, Jack. You were told what happens when... WHEN I'M PUSHED. You were warned what happens when I go into that dark, dark, dark place. I told you what happens when I cross that line and go to that place where I can go to. PEOPLE GET HURT, JACK. The living legend Five Star Athlete WildStar. The undefeatable Joe The Plumber. ELIMINATED. And now? The unstoppable tank. The Next Big Thing. JACK BRYANT. Overnight observation. Injuries to be determined. AND I DID THAT!" RUMSFELD: "No, Dorchester." (Rumsfeld smirks.) "WE did that." (Dorchester nods, steps back, and looks at the ground.) "Jack, I think you're starting to understand what we're about. What Dorchester Stratton and myself are capable of. Dorchester saw the replay of what he did. But when he did that? When he drove his Jaguar into a crowd of people just to get at you? He doesn't remember that." (Dorchester's hands tremble.) "And why doesn't he remember? Because I am the one who controls him. I am the one who gives him PERMISSION to go into that place. With one order... with one snap of the finger... Dorchester goes from what you see now. The handsome man of refined society... into a stone cold killer, without mercy nor remorse." (She smiles at Dorchester.) "Isn't that right, Shmoopie?" (Dochester looks up and smiles.) DORCHESTER: "Yes. You're right, Veronica. You... you're ALWAYS right." (Veronica chuckles.) RUMSFELD: "I'm attracted to him no matter what. But when I see men in a puddle of blood at his feet... when I see men washing their eyes out frantically... when I see people screaming 'Get Help" and 'Hold His Neck Still'... well... let's just say that the word 'attractive' doesn't apply anymore." (Veronica smiles seductively.) "Isn't THAT right, Shmoopie?' (Dorchester laughs.) DORCHESTER: "Oh yes... that's right." (Dorchester goes in for a kiss. But Veronica blocks him and instead holds up her hand. There's a torn sheet of paper.) RUMSFELD: "Oh, one more thing, Jack. I picked up this piece of paper before we fled the scene. It's the last autograph you signed today. It's the last autograph of your career that you'll EVER sign. It might be worth a lot of money someday. But it's going to feel so, so, so good... when I place it on top of my satin sheets... and I lay my bare back on it... and Dorchester takes me as we watch the footage over... and over... and over... again." (Veronica then bites Dorchester's lip. He just stands there paralyzed.) RUMSFELD: "Let's go, Dorchester." (She walks off. Dorchester follows. FTB)
