WEEK 8: Shawn Matthews vs. Promo

16 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN to 'Showtime' SHAWN MATTHEWS, sitting in the hot tub in his hotel room.. he's sitting back, drinking a glass of wine, and puffing on a cigar...) "Ah well, things didn't go exactly as planned last time. Shane Southern wasn't there for me, and I got pinned in the match with Melton and Hornet. Who cares about that. What I do care about is the fact that I've lost points, and I'm going down the rankings.." (Suddenly, Autumn's head pops up out of the water..) "Speaking of going down... all of you in the NFW, I'd like to officially introduce you to my valet, Autumn. While her presence may not have accomplished what I had hoped to accomplish, it definitely mattered.." (Autumn gets up out of the hot tub, wearing a hot pink two-piece bikini. She towels herself off, kisses Shawn on the lips, and walks toward the bedroom...) "That's good stuff, isn't it? Week 8, I'm stepping in there with Promo, a man who I know little about... know what I say? Who the f**k cares? I know Promo's won championships in leagues all over the world, but when it's time to throw down.. he just doesn't have what it takes to step in there and be successful against the likes of.... well, ME!" (Matthews lifts himself up out of the tub, and sits on the edge..) "Promo, you're just another stepping stone on my way to the Ultratitle. Melton and Hornet, I'm finished worrying about all of the sh*t they've been talking. It's time to get focused, and get ready to step up as the season continues. Promo, I hope your expectations aren't too high... because if they are, Showtime is gonna knock 'em right down!" (A voice is heard from the bedroom...) AUTUMN: "Shawnie, why don't you come in here and take our your aggressions on me?" (laughs) SHAWN MATTHEWS: "Guess it's time to make like a plumber. Week Eight, it's time you make like a LOSER, Promo, and go home with no points.." (FADEOUT, as MATTHEWS walks toward the bedroom...)
