NFW All-Star Week: SWAT Team vs. OSS

27 Jan 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: "Manager of Champions" Calvin Carlton, with Sweet Betty Lee-- his tennis racket-- and "Fabulous" Frankie Fargo and "The Last American Gigolo" Brandon Mueller, clad in bowties and tuxtails, each holding the NFW World Tag Team titles, are standing in front of the All-Star Week banner.) CALVIN: Y'know, I'm a busy man. My momma's been putting a LOT of pressure on me lately, telling me that I have to ensure her victory at All-Star Week, that if *I* don't get the job done, then the plug may be pulled on my career, that instead of coming out here week after week teaching you so-called "people" proper manners and giving you instructions on how to do things like use shampoo, instead of coming out here and gracing you with the presence of a real-life rich person, instead of being the role model you aspire to be, it would mean that I would have to fly home in my momma's Lear Jet and spend the rest of my days sitting poolside on our oceanfront property on the island we own in the Caribbean... no wait, the Pacific... no, wait... OH, I can never remember which one is where we own the island and which one it is where we own the entire country. But that's besides the point, because you can rest assured that I WILL NOT be going home. First of all, you people would miss me too much and that would break your already cholesterol-ravaged hearts. But, most importantly, if I were to go home, it would mean I would be seen in wrestling legacy as a failure, as a man who didn't get the job done. You all know my family name, you all know my momma. I WAS BORN FROM HER SWEET TENDER LOINS! Not once in her great, grand, fantastic, fabulous life has she been let down or dissapointed. I don't plan on being the first person to let that woman down, I don't plan on her ever having a cloudy day, I don't plan on her ever having to have rainy weather. So, I've been busy making plans. Cutting deals. Negotiating. Pulling teeth. I've been doing whatever it is I have to do to ensure victory for the NFW's Southern Conference, despite the fact that no one on our roster has been able to get the job done week in and week out... aside from my main man and boyhood hero Joey Melton, who has continued to inspire and impress despite the fact he has become distracted by that... that... that... (Fargo pats Calvin on the shoulders) WOMAN Lindsay Troy in recent weeks. Yes, there's a lot of pressure on me. And the best way to alleviate that pressure is to have a release of some kind. My release? My release is going to come at All-Star Week, when my charges... the Original ShowStoppers... the greatest tag team of yesterday, today and tomorrow... lay the beating of a lifetime on two neanderthal knuckledraggers by the name of the Swat Team. And who knows... maybe Sweet Betty Lee here (Calvin swings the racket) might even want to place a kiss on one of their skulls! FARGO: Colt... Berretta... ya'll came out here a few weeks back and said ya'll wanted one last match. Ya'll said ya'll was gonna step aside, get out of this sport, go home to wherever it is ya'llre from. Well, ya'll got sumthin' right. (Mueller starts cackling with laughter.) It *WILL* be ya'lls last match. It's EVERYONE'S last match when they get in the ring with us. Ryan and Lincoln? Those imposters who done stole AHR name and AHR heritage? Where they at? Huh? Where they gone to? We threw them out of this sport lahke the homosexual sinners they is. Hell Machine? Where they at? HUH? We sent them out packing as well. Ya'll gonna be retirin' at the end of ya'lls match with us... but it ain't gonna be because ya'll WANT to retire. It's because me and THE STUD are gonna MAKE ya'll retire. CALVIN: Swat Team, you made the mistake of your lives when you called us out. I'm a B-I-G... BIG IMPORTANT GUY... and I have too much to worry about right now... my boys are going to stomp you and destroy you at the All-Star Showdown. Tell your sisters, tell your daddy's but don't tell my momma because she already knows... there's going to be an SOS... because you just crossed paths with The OSS. Let's get strutting, boys! (All three Strut off. FTB.)
