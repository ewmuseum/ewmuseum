WEEK 10 - CHRIS MCMILLAN vs. JEAN RABESQUE

16 Mar 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen comes in on an NFW backdrop, where Jean Rabesque stands, a smile on his face, our camera slowly pans around him, his gaze following. He wears his normal gear, removing his sunglasses as the camera zooms, finally smiling again and speaking) Rabesque: “It’s been a busy couple weeks for the Rabesquedor, wouldn’t you all agree? I mean, with all the happenings at Crash. But still all the questions remain, and they all revolve around one question..... why? “Allow me to respond to everything everyone has been asking me very plainly with.... ‘I have my reasons.’ Now, those reasons are quite frankly none of all your business. But trust me, there is a method to the madness, that is a guarantee. “But back to business, things haven’t been going well. The lead is shrinking to nothing, and Jean Rabesque isn’t doing what he does best. Sure, Melton cheated, and my girl was the only reason he got the win over me, but none of that can be changed. I am still here for one reason above all others, and that is to piss everyone off and win the Ultratitle. “Losing two matches in a row really isn’t the best way to accomplish the goal. Generally when one likes to boast himself as being the finest technical wrestler on the planet, losing in itself is totally unacceptable. Which is why it damn well better stop as we approach week 10. The playoffs are too close, and I simply will not stand by and let all the work that I’ve done be in vain. I will not let those chumps pass me in the standings, and I will not give up my bye in the playoffs. I’ve earned every bit of this, and I’m going to keep it dammit. “Chris McMillan, we do go back a ways. Hell, how many times have we faced each other? But I’m afraid that I have to point out that you have Sean Edmunds Syndrome. No matter how many times you face me, no matter how many different strategies you try, you still can’t get the job done. Now I know that this has absolutely nothing to do with your physical skills. You’re an incredibly gifted athlete, and you’ve beaten some of the top names in this business. I also know that it has nothing to do with your mental acumen. You’re a very bright guy, everyone knows that Chris. But for whatever reason, you just don’t have what it takes to match up with the man that has done the same thing to so many people. You just can’t muster up the ability to beat me. “Now there are a couple ways you can go about this Chris. The first would be to go the Melton route, and that is just cheat your way to victory. If you have as little honor as someone like him, then allow me to suggest that to you. The other way is to get your ass in the gym and start taking care of the little things. Study some more tape, work just a little harder, do some more advance scouting. Maybe, just maybe you’ll then have a shot to get it done. “But here’s the catch Chris, while you’re busting your ass doing the exact same thing, what do you think I’ll be doing? This is the tricky part Chris. You can’t just do enough to catch up to me, but you have to do enough not only to catch where I am now, but to catch where I will be after I get done doing the same for you. I don’t think you have enough to get that done Chris, I really don’t. I think you’re going to fall short like so many before you have. “That is unless you want to take the coward’s way out, and throw the honor out at the door. If you do that, you’ll get compared to Joey Melton. When comparing that to a loss, I’m honestly not sure which one is yours. “The choice is yours Chris, how you handle this is completely up to you. “But in the end.......... I don’t really expect it to matter. No false gimmicks, no false hype, I am Jean Rabesque.” (Fade out)
