The Faces have Changes but the Song Remains the Same

29 Jun 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"There's been a really strange set of affairs, to date, in the newly revamped NFW. Scores of wrestlers - some good, some not so good - have been telling myself, Hornet, Joey Melton, et cetera, to return to the CSWA, "where we belong." "But why do any of us belong in the CSWA? Why am I being told to go back to Greensboro? Why not Seattle, where the other company I work for is located? Why not Los Angeles, where I'm at right now? Where my family is located? Why not New York City, where I spent the first thirty years of my life?" FADEIN: The second floor steps of a condominium complex. Eli Flair is sitting on the top, elbows rested on his knees. He's wearing a sleeveless "OTEP: Sevas Tra" T-shirt and black cutoff jeans. The faint sounds of an electric guitar are in the background somewhere. "Well, I listened to y'all... I came home. But I'll be back in time for the first show, don't'cha worry." He winked at the camera, a half- smirk on his face. "I know I haven't 'slummed' it for a while now in an unfamiliar setting, but hey, when a call is made and the offer seems right..." "Well, anyways, I like to work, and with Scarred's release date rapidly approaching it's not like I'll be able to spend much tim with Angel anymore. Ivy's not happy unless she's working a twenty- three hour day, and the word ULTRATITLE just seems to be synonomous with some jolly good fun, intense wrestling, and political agendas that spawn careers- long hatred." "Just look at '95." He laughed. "There's two schools of thought on this. The first, the most popular, is that anyone who's ever had a shred of success in the CSWA has been coddled and pampered and simply doesn't deserve life. And any time someone dares to leave the CSWA ghetto, HOW DARE WE EXPECT THE WORLD TO BE LAID OUT AT OUR FEET!" "How dare we." "But I'm a little confused... why is Triple X getting shat on while Evan Aho goes along unscathed? Aho's a former World Champion, along with the rest of the whipping boys like myself, Melton, and Hornet... but he's left outta the loop while Trip, whose success has come largely outside the CSWA, gets his name run through the sewer." "That's fine, y'know... I've known Trip a long time and I know he can take care of himself. But if you're gonna arbitrarily love and hate a man for where he's worked... consistency is your friend." "Consistency will keep the hypocrite label far, far away." "Nobody who's spent time in the CSWA have acted like we've got big egos. The rest'a you decided that we don't deserve to act like it. But what's an ego? Is it wrong of Hornet to act like a man who's won dozens of World Championships and hasn't yet been put in his place by anyone? You think Hornet is too old? Too overrated? Take him to the ring and beat him. Melton can't hack it anymore? Put him outta his misery? I'm more hype than substance? Show me the error of my ways." "But when you can't... what's the excuse gonna be this time?" [the thread's next post — 30 Jun 2003] Not to bring up 'ancient' history here, Eli, but it's funny how you've decided to 'stick up' for me now. Aren't you the same guy who couldn't even bother to get my side of the story the last time you found out I did something you didn't like? Couldn't be bothered to do anything but try and lay me out? I don't need you 'getting my back,' Eli. Lord knows I've had enough on my back in my lifetime without you jumping on again. I've done a lot of things I'm not proud of, and you've been around for a lot of them. But just because this isn't Greensboro doesn't mean I all of sudden need you to do something you haven't done for the last two years. I'm not so old at 35 that I can't speak for myself, or even fight for myself, when it's necessary. [the thread's next post — 30 Jun 2003] Oh, don't worry, Hornet... I'm not sticking up for you. Truth be told, I don't particularly care if you win this thing or get punked at every turn worse than Doc did against Aimz. I'm past it, Hornet. I'm above it. Don't mistake getting your back for getting the facts straight. And the facts remain constant - in the words of no less an authority on you than Poison Ivy herself... is that the one thing nobody can legitimately attack is Hornet's talent in the ring. People want to hate you? Fine. There's certainly enough reason. People want to hate me? Fine. Just as many. But the hypocrites and mindless shrikes who arbitrarily hate everyone who's worked in Greensboro because, I dunno... we made a comfortable living while the rest of the world rotted in a bingo hall... It rings off the ears after a while. And I'd rather be hated for what I am than what I'm not. You? Do you even know who you are anymore? [the thread's next post — 30 Jun 2003] (FADEIN: ARMANDO MONTEZUMA standing in front of the NFW North backdrop, mock drying his eyes) MONTEZUMA: (sniff, sniff) The NFW locker room is picking on me and my friends because we work in Greensboro - boo FRICKIN' hoo - Eli Flair, you're not as dumb as you look - let's face it, how could you be? You HAD to know that it wasn't all going to be parades and marching bands for you and your buddies - you HAD to know that those of us who BUILT this place on our BACKS might take offense to you waltzing in here and trying to take what's ours. Now, I've seen you and Hornet have your little lover's quarrel, and let me clue you in on something - and I'm speaking on behalf of the entire NFW locker room, because I'm a LEADER - because I'm widely RESPECTED - and I have the COJONES to speak for those who are too shy or too damn IGNORANT to speak for themselves. Here's what I have to say, Eli - nobody gives a CRAP. Whatever emotional baggage you and your buddies are bringing here from you-know-where - LEAVE IT THERE. I'm gonna be honest with you, Eli - if I have to spend the next six months listening to you, Hornet, Triple X, Joey Melton, so on and so forth saying (MONTY shifts into annoying voice a la American Pie) "And this one time, in Greensboro", I'm gonna rip my FREAKIN' ears off. Eli Flair, you're not in Kansas anymore - you're in MY conference, you're in MY ring, and you're in MY way. I hate to break it to you, Eli, but this is MY time - the new NFW era belongs to Armando Montezuma. I've spent too long waiting in the wings while the world revolved around Shane Southern's loaded boot and Bloodhunt's rambling JFK assassination CRAP - not to mention the whining - WAAAH! I GOT SCREWED! WAAAAH! RICKY ZANE! WAAHHH - SHUT UP. Bloodhunt's history, Shane Southern's in the other conference, and now a traveling circus from North Carolina thinks they can roll in here and take what belongs to ME - I don't THINK so! The Ultratitle belongs to Cuba's National Treasure, and nobody - and I mean, NOBODY - is gonna stand in my way. Eli Flair, you may be a big deal to some drunken rednecks in the Bible Belt, but you don't impress me, and you DARN SURE don't impress Senor Castro. Eli Flair, you and the rest of your pals have two choices. Go home... or you're going DOWN! [the thread's next post — 1 Jul 2003] FADEIN: Eli Flair's living room. Even two years later, there's still half- unpacked boxes in the corners. "Monty, Monty, Monty... Stop. Take a deep breath. Backtrack a bit, maybe. Where's the ego in Joey Melton saying he won the first one'a these Ultratitles? Where's the ego in me offhandedly musing over where, exactly, home is for me? Because other than the home I sit in right now, I'd like to know." "You built the NFW on your back? Fine. But it looks like a largely different company than the one you built up into a competitive promotion, ready to take on the world. Looks like the company you built on your back didn't survive the changing seasons, Monty... you and your buddies might've put your heart and soul into the NFW but the people who matter, the people who put their money into it... apparently wanted something different." "Don't blame me for your ineptitude as a draw. I just met you at the lottery." "You'd do well to practice what you preach, Monty... Whatever emotional baggage you have from not being quite good enough to unseat Shane Southern... leave it there. Whatever emotional baggage you have over the fact that a bunch'a guys from a company you're green with envy over not being a part of have been hired to turn the Ultratitle into something with bite... leave it there." "Waiting in the wings is your own damn fault. Did you walk into the NFW offices and declare to all the secreteries, 'I want to be a wrestling star! NFW, make me a millionaire!' No wonder you've been waiting so long." "Maybe that's where it begins and ends. Every single person whose very existence you've ranted against has been a person who went out and got everything they wanted through hard work and tireless dedication. Can you say the same? We already know the answer - if you could, you'd have achieved it. Again, Monty... don't blame others for your own shortcomings." "So let's make it easy, Monty... let's pretend nothing before June 30th, 2003 ever happened. It'll be like a game. Two bucks in the cussbucket every time someone brings it up. Can you do it?" "Really?" "Without putting everyone to sleep?" "Go back behind Door Number Two, Monty... because if this is your time, the NFW is doomed yet again." FADE [the thread's next post — 1 Jul 2003] (FADEIN: ARMANDO MONTEZUMA standing in front of the NFW North banner, looking perturbed.) MONTEZUMA: First things first, Eli - it's SENOR Montezuma to you. It's bad enough that you can waltz in here and Sullivan and Miles all but throw rose pedals at your feet, but for you to come into MY conference and not show me any respect is more than I'm willing to tolerate. Eli, you come in here with your POMPOUS attitude, talking about my "shortcomings" and "failures" - you don't know a DAMN thing about me. The only FAILURE that went on in New Frontier Wrestling was Sullivan and the fat bleached blond idiot in the leather pants deciding to hitch their wagons to building a feud between a slack-jawed redneck with a loaded belt and a paranoid socially retarded psychopath who masturbates to the Zapruder tape. I waited in the wings because I dedicated myself to the NOBLE purpose of ridding this sport of a wrinkled geriatric blemish. Well, the Polish Putz is gone and forgotten, and now it's time for me to take what's rightfully mine. You're right, Eli Flair, I do take it personally that Sullivan and Miles went on their hands and knees into the locker rooms of Greensboro, North Carolina - humiliating themselves and this company by BEGGING you, Hornet, and all the rest of your buddies to come here and compete for the Ultratitle - MY title! I'll tell you point blank, Eli - NFW doesn't NEED you - all they need is me - Cuba's National Treasure - the Standard of EXCELLENCE for every man in this sport to follow. Hard work? Tireless dedication? I've got all that in spades, Eli - to go along with more God given natural ability than you could ever DREAM of. Ultratitle's got MY name on it, Eli - it's that simple - and I'm not gonna let you or anyone else stand in my way. Time's running out, Eli - but you've still got two choices. Go home... or you're going DOWN.
