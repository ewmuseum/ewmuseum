From the Desk of Commissioner Manso

15 Apr 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Titled "Future Tense" ((FADEIN: Michael Manson is sitting in his wheelchair behind a desk in his makeshift office with polished floor tiles, a gothic painting behind him, and a roaring fireplace. he is wearing a blood red dress shirt open around a Sandman t-shirt and black jeans. Spread on his desk is copy of the Jamar Chronicles, TV Guide, and Finnegan's Wake. He has a set of papers in front of him and a pen.)) MANSON: Being as it may, I am still the commissioner, at least for the time being and I at leats have control over my own contract. Therefore, I've gone to the effort and drawing up my own papers ofr my own release with full benefits and all the money owed to me. All it needs is my signature, whether ink or blood, hell I might do both. As it reads, once the current NFW tour ends with all my committments being fulfilled at FutureShock, I will retire from the world of wrestling and NFW as commissioner and worker..something that barely any of you have the courage to actually do. ...Unless.....I receive adequate explanations for my role here. Sullivan do not meet me as he said he would, but I'll try again. Miles tagged with me, now I want him to meet me in the center of the ring at Crash..I know where he'll be..he has a match with Bloodhunt and I won't even let it get underway unless he talks to me. I want to know about my future..I want to know why if I'm the commissioner...why he and Sullivan make matches.....why I am not allowed to punish anyone....and why if I am also to be an active wrestler..why Craig Miles has a match with Bloodhunt..and not me. I do not need this. I am uninterested in any kind of match or power struggle with Miles or Sullivan or anyone. I prefer to walk away on my own terms, whether completed or not. his is my future, my livelihood. The future is always more interesting than the past, my friends. I will have a future whether it is here or somewhere else. All it takes is my signature.
