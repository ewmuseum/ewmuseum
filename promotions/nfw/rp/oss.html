Oss

15 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: A Confederate flag backdrop, with the letters OSS spraypainted onto it in KISS ARMY style. Standing in front of the backdrop, dressed like General Lee is "The Last American Gigolo" Brandon Mueller-- his fingers rubbing through his long bleach blonde mullet, bow-tie with straps on. To his left is "Fabulous" Frankie Fargo, looking cockeyed, holding both NFW World Tag Team title.) FARGO: Berretta... Colt... DO YOU KNOW WHAT YOU JUST DONE DID? Huh? For a few months now, we've been silent. Yep-- The Original Showstoppers, the KINGS OF TAG TEAM WRESTLING, have been laid low, sittin' pretty at Calvin's Momma's Plantayshun down in Dixie Land. We took these (Brandon SLAPS one of the titles loudly) after YEARS of BRUTAL WARFARE. Y'see, the world didn't want two low-down and dirty dudes STRUTTIN' THEIR STUFF again. Years back, me and The Gigolo here, heh-- we was the BEST DAY-UMN TAG TEAM THE WORLD HAD EVER SEEN. But then we had to disapear, as I got placed in the Mississippi State Penitentiary system, framed by jealous rasslers like Wildfire Rich and Dream Warrior, and spent TEN YEARS OF MAH LAHFE PAYIN' FOR CRIMES THAT EYE DID NOT COMMIT. Eye come back, and what do EYE SEE? Eye see a tag team callin' thaymselves The Showstoppers, usin' OUR name, doin' OUR strut without askin' US for permission. Eye see a tag team called Hell Machine out here, managed by a WOMAN. That's raht-- a WOMAN. You know what happens when a woman gives US advice or tells US what to do? (Mueller starts to cackle.) They get placed down, back-first, in our pick-up truck. They don't get hit if they put up a fight but if they do well... heh... then they get hit. HARD. A buncha other tag teams came and gawn. But anyone who stood in our way had to fight against two Southern men proud of their heritage, raised on 100 proof and filled with the fight of 10,000 soldiers at Antietam. Morale of the story? Eye don't see Lincoln or Ryan these parts no more. Last I heard, they was back where they started-- making man movies for a bunch of homosexuals who sinnin' against what got done put in the Bible. Hell Machine? They gone, nowhere to be seen. But The Original Showstoppers... we still here. Anyone who got in our way, we crushed. Anyone who stood against us, we took them down. And now... now there ain't nobody left. So, Colt. Berretta. You wanna call us out? You want to have your last match against us? Well, it won't be the first time our opponents last match was against us. Ya'll retiring because your heart ain't in it anymore? Nuh uh. Eye was in jail for 10 years, WAITIN' MAH DAY to get back in the ring. Mah heart ain't ever gonna leave this sport. No siree. Nope. Ya'll gonna retire the same reason Hell Machine quit. The same reason Ryan and Lincoln quit. BECAUSE FABULOUS FRANKIE AND THE GIGOLO ARE GONNA MAKE YOU QUIT. Ya'll called US out. Now, ya'll gonna pay some STREET JUSTICE. It's ya'lls last match. Eye don't care-- Calvin's momma will pay the fines. Eye might just CUT you with a shank, since ya'll don't care if ya'll come back. You ain't goin' out on top... you're just goin' out, for good. (Strut. FTB.)
