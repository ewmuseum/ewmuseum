Jonathan Marx vs. Dan Ryan

11 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STIPULATION: Match must end with a Submissions Hold [the thread's next post — 11 Feb 2004] ::Marx is standing in front of a NFW North banner looking both angered and bewildered:: If there was one man I thought I could count on not to submit, it was the six foot seven, three hundred and thirty pound monster of a man, Dan Ryan. But just like so many of my other hopes for dismal state of wrestling today, it has been shattered. I’m a shell of my former self. My smile is gone and all I want is the blood of those responsible, whether It be wrestler or management. Dan, how could you let us down? How could you let old Mr. Melton submit you like that when he has been saying for weeks that most of the wrestlers in the North don’t even deserve to compete for the Ultratitle? You did the unthinkable. You proved that Melton was right. After we all worked so hard for the first half of the season to establish ourselves as the dominant conference, in one foul swoop, you ruined all of that. My hard work, Manson’s hard work, Shizaki’s hard work… the list goes on and on as I get angrier and angrier. But just like you did when you teamed against me with DJ Havok, the six foot seven, three hundred and thirty pound man disappointed his team. You would think being teamed with a man that size would be an advantage. Wrong, dead wrong. You couldn’t even get the pin for your team against Stratton. I know you are going to respond, you could give a damn about what I think, but what about all of the CSWA and GXW fans you let down, who were rooting for you as a symbol to show your dominance against the best wrestling has to offer? You crapped on them more than you even crapped on us. What did you think about that when you gave up? Was it really worth disappointing everyone, including yourself, to escape a fleeting moment of pain? Let me tell you a secret, I don’t care if I submit you in our match, because I would rather not be where Mr. Melton has been. This match is going to be about me beating you within an inch of your life for the atrocities that you have committed against on all of us with all the fury that I possessed taking on the South and a new level of anger I thought I would ever know that came with the downfall of Miles. I beg of you Lindsay, please stay out of the match. The Marx that is showing up will not be a gentleman and I don’t want to make you a casualty of our war, but I will if I have to. I’m sorry Linday, but after I get through with Ryan, you may need to find yourself yet another man to manage. "We have no war, you and I. And I don't hate you." "I just hate idiots." Ryan turns and gets into the truck.... FADE OUT as the truck drives off.... [the thread's next post — 12 Feb 2004] ::Jonathan Marx slams down the phone in the den in his mansion:: JONATHAN MARX: **** MANSON! DOMOVOI: What did you say boss? JONATHAN MARX: **** Manson. He has deserted us all in the middle of a damn war. Just like Dan Ryan, he is quitter. DOMOVOI: Manson just feels that this is the best way to solve the problem. JONATHAN MARX: Why are you defending him? He should have stayed in the North and toppled Prosser from within like the rest of us. I am not happy about what happened at War Games, but I’m not going to quit. I’ve fought too hard and too long to join the long list of quitters who have abandoned this league. DOMOVOI: Boss, Dan Ryan finally answered you. JONATHAN MARX: Make him wait, just like I waited all year for this War Games match to have a chance to defeat the South, only to have Dan Ryan pull it away from me. That was the moment I joined the NFW for and Ryan ruined it for me. DOMOVOI: Ryan is saying that it is your fault for not saving him. JONATHAN MARX: Tell him I’m very sorry that in my four years in Princeton as an amateur wrestler that I never learned to the counter for…. BEING BLOWN THE **** UP! DOMOVOI: Calm down Jonathan. JONATHAN MARX: A fifty year old man who thinks Roger Staubach should still be their QB, a big mouthed momma’s boy, and a bunch of nobodies should NOT be able to beat a six foot seven three hundred and thirty pound man unless they are carrying guns and knives. So when I was laying groggy in the wreckage just trying to remember my name, it never occurred to me that I should get up and save…. THE MONSTER from the small and insignificant torch wielding villagers. DOMOVOI: But Manson was in trouble to, why didn’t you save Manson? JONATHAN MARX: Why should I help Manson? I went to the IWF and worshiped him as a God, I went to GLCW and I stood beside him and Anarky as a member of G.O.D., but what has Manson ever done for me? I’ll tell you what he has done for me, he talked me into joining this miserable soap opera, where I can’t even bring Jacobs, that has caused me nothing but aggravation since and then he SECEDES FROM THE NORTH TO TOP IT ALL OFF! DOMOVOI: You got played. JONATHAN MARX: No, for most of it I was a willing accomplice. For Marxism to succeed, I don’t need to be the World Champion. All I need is to exist as omnipotent presence in the background when others appear to be doing their work on their own. I don’t take the credit, but the mission gets accomplished and I leave the world a better place. But I’ve learned that Manson isn’t the man to accomplish that for me so I’m going to just have to win the Ultratitle on my own. DOMOVOI: Sort of like Sam from Quantum Leap… JONATHAN MARX: Yes, but sadly you are no Al. Al is off preparing to do play by play for Great Expectations and I’m stuck here with a dunderhead like you. DOMOVOI: I’m a lot better to have around when those aliens that Dan Ryan was talking about actually invade and take over the world in one foul swoop. JONATHAN MARX: Stop quoting Macbeth incorrectly, the phrase is one FELL swoop, I was so damn mad at Dan Ryan in that last promo that I lost my head and quoted Macduff incorrectly. DOMOVOI: Whoa. Mr. Shakespeare himself quoted the Bard incorrectly. JONATHAN MARX: The horror of War Games has left an imprint on my subconscious that may never leave. Dan Ryan is right, Prosser is the greater evil and has to be dealt with, but I’ll deal with him on my own time. Dan Ryan is just one man responsible for a series of horrific events on a night where everything went wrong. But I have to make right all the wrongs committed that night and maybe before the finals, I can restore Camelot to what it once was before Ryan uttered the words I quit. FADE OUT [the thread's next post — 13 Feb 2004] ::Jonathan Marx is sitting under a tree in a graveyard playing with a flower muttering "just an exhibition match" over and over:: JONATHAN MARX: Dan Ryan, you are the two time former GXW World Heavweight Champion and current CSWA World Heavyweight Champion and I’m not going to stand up here like a dozen or so other wrestlers in this company and try to discredit my opponents through silly name calling in front of a national audience. That isn’t the proper way and certainly not the way I do things. You are the CSWA World Heavyweight Champion, you wear the second greatest title which goes back all the way back in time to the glory days of wrestling when wrestling was broadcast live on the Prodigy Network from coast to coast. But you don’t seem to respect that title and all of the people that title represents because you keep on repeatedly embarrassing yourself against others. First you needed the lights to go out to win the title against Mark Windham which has cast a shadow over your whole entire title reign. Did he win the title? Didn’t he win the title? We may never know. But I for one was willing to give you the benefit of the doubt being the wrestling historian that I am, it was a miracle someone your age even won the title in the CSWA before turning forty. But when I rewatched the tape of War Games and saw you submit, all that benefit of the doubt went right out of the window. Dan Ryan, you are six foot seven, three hundred and thirty pounds and you lost to old man in the prime of your life. Do you realize that if Mr. Melton was ten years younger he would have killed you? If he had full use of his senses, you would have been a small meal for him. You are supposed to be the new legend. The legend that puts all of the older legends in their place to secure their spot in history. I’m just a rookie. Despite leading the NFW North, I know I’m not suppose to win the Ultratitle. I’m only one year into my career and I trusted both you and Manson to get the job done because both of you are in the prime of your career and I respected the hell out of both of you. I know that is foolish looking back, but as I was laying there in the ashes and I looked over to see both of you still in the fray, I smiled to myself before I passed out because I had faith that the two champions whose legacy dates back to our origin were fighting to take down the old regime. There are some battles I won’t be around to seen won. But that doesn’t mean they weren’t worth fighting or aren’t worth fighting… maybe it is better that you don’t know what you lost out there that night because the pain that was left with me that night was unbearable because I was the captain of that fight. It wouldn’t take a rocket scientist to figure out that I am underdog in this match. How can little old me submit a giant like you? But Ryan, whether I win or lose this match, I am going to find my peace and that is all any of us can ever hope for. FTB [the thread's next post — 20 Feb 2004] FADE IN A large expansive living room.
