Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: NIGHT - A neighborhood in suburban Long Island where all the houses have light, EXCEPT ONE! The camera closes in on the front steps of the lone house without power, where we find a LIPA employee face to face with PROBLEM CHILD aka PETER WINDHAM aka WHITE JAMES HARDEN, who has the same beard/mohawk combo as the Rockets SG except with a mullet in the back. He is wearing a Knicks away Raymond Felton jersey. We join the two men mid-argument) PW: “Dude, I PAY YOUR FUCKIN’ SALARY! I don’t want excuses, I WANT MY POWER RESTORED! Fuckin’ storm was like two months ago! So tell me again: why am I THE ONLY MOTHERFUCKER ON LONG ISLAND without power” LIPA EMPLOYEE: “Sir, your local power grid is fine. Your power is not ‘out’. What you need to do is PAY YOUR BILL so we can restore power to your house.” PW: “…so you’re telling me there’s no outage?” LIPA EMPLOYEE: “Mr. Windham, you haven’t paid your electric bill since you purchased your home last year. We cannot in good conscience restore your power until the account is brought up to date. Frankly, I’m surprised you had power this long. “ PW: “I’m surprised I LET YOU LIVE this long! Look, you know who the FUCK I am?” LIPA EMPLOYEE: … PW: … LIPA EMPLOYEE: … PW: “I’M TROY WINDHAM’S FUCKIN’ ADOPTED BROTHER! Now get off my property before I BEAT you off it.” (LIPA Employee scatters away as Peter takes a step forward. He rushes into his truck and drives off. Peter falls back into the lawn chair on his front steps and lifts the lid of the dirty cooler next to it. SFX: the twisting of the cap off a 40 .oz bottle of malt liquor. Peter brings the bottle to his lips for a REFRESHING taste) PW: “Ahhh, that hit the spot! Y’know, it really sucks not having power. Lots of time to sit around, thinking thoughts, getting pissed. JJ Deville – YOU WRONGED TROY, and that means YOU WRONGED ME! So now it’s time for ol’ P-DUBS make a play like the greatest point guard of this generation, Raymond Felton, kill a bottle of Mad Dog 20/20, and leave the house to settle some neighborhood beefs!” (Places the bottle of MD at his feet and continues) PW: “THIS IS YOUR COMMISSIONER SPEAKING! Oh, what, you didn’t forget did you? The Windham Clan still has a fucking…I dunno, like a stake or something, and basically, we get to appoint someone as Commissioner. I think that’s how it works. And since YOU are no longer a Windham Clan member, JJ, and since I am THE ONLY member of the Windham Clan currently in NFW, that makes ME the Commissioner! Like that dude from the beginning of Braveheart, IT IS MY NOBLE RIGHT! And if the Windham Clan can’t burn them out, WE’LL BREED THEM OUT!” “FamilyTree.com’s got my hookup! Still waiting on the family evaluation, and once it’s ready, more Windhams will be unleashed!” “I’ve noticed there are some new faces here in NEW FRONTIER WINDHAM so here’s my advice to all you fucks: DON’T LISTEN TO EDDIE MAYFIELD! Listen to ME! I am your Commissioner. I operate independently of that clown! And you will RECOGNIZE me as your Commissioner.” “Now, my first decree as Commissioner: PAY MY ELECTRIC BILL! I’m talking to YOU General Tso’s Orange Dragon! Get your sweet and sour combination #7 ass down to LIPA and hand over your Grand Prix paycheck! Hope it covers $4,391.22 you FOOL!” “As for YOU Fappity Baytor, I’m gonna need you to go ahead and order me the Deftones’ second album off Amazon. Or wherever you can get a good price, NEW not USED! And don’t fucking touch it with that nasty hand. I want that shit bubble wrapped and UNOPENED!” “Got one more demand, and it’s for Ice Tre. You white hip-hop wanna be loc-dog, there’s only ONE white wrestler whose got cred in the black community, and IT’S ME! Your job is to travel up to the NFW NYC main office, and take a dump on that chick’s desk…what’s her name? Serena Altschul? Fiona Love? THAT DUMB SLUT! POOP ON HER DESK IN THE NAME OF ALL THAT IS WINDHAM!” “Finally, I want the rest of you pube hair curling irons to PUBLICLY ACKNOWLEDGE that I am your Commissioner!” (Picks up the MD 20/20 and takes a swig) “That is all. Windham out.” (FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 18 Dec 2012] (CUTTO: Impulse and Calico Rose, sitting on the couch in their apartment.) IMPULSE: Well? What do you think? ROSE: I'm sold. IMPULSE: Likewise. ROSE: Long live the new flesh. (FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 19 Dec 2012] CUTTO: Orange Dragon II, contemplating heavily at a Sydney, Australia Internetz!~ Cafe. His mask on, his suit pressed, and his two seconds sitting at the next table over. He had been busy these last few days, -- here, he'll tell you. Dragon II: Hai, Frontiersmen, Christmas shopping is what old generation Americans say, 'For the birds.' I was shopping online, looking for a gift for my only true aquaintance, the Original Orange Dragon. Yet, I fear I do not properly have the financial funds to add such an expensive product to my online shopping cart. A dejected, downward look at the floor. Dragon II: I would have, hai, -- but the entire Grand Prix earnings I make will be bestowed upon Commissioner Windham's electric bill. I do so because it was under strict order. I do this great other-worldly gesture because it is the season of giving to those less fortuned. Poor Mister Commissioner, always in the dark these past months. But never fear, O'Unlit Leader, your Batman is here. Looking upward, quickly. Finger raised. Dragon II: I will go-to bat for you, Commissioner. -- but there is a huge dilemma, Commissioner Windham. Holding up his hand with his other, arthritic. Dragon II: It appears my condition has temporarily come forth. My team of physicians in my home country, hai, they told me of this recurring problem. See, Commissioner, everytime I let go of something of value, my tendons tighten about. And not until something of value is placed into it, does it not feel much better. See, watch this. Dragon II 'powers' himself to grab what appears to be a paper check off the table. Like a broken crane, his hand simply lacks the natural mechanics to pick it up, however. Dragon II: It is in vain, Commissioner Windham, that I humbly inform you.-- I cannot bring you out of the dark. If I can not muster the fight within myself to pick up this $13.00 check to this wonderful Sydney Establishment, I fear the fight far too great to simply hand over my Grand Prix earnings. You understand, hai? Which is why I did this for you, in sheer generosity for all of the great, selflessthings you will someday give in return to the greatest Flightmaster that ever graced this Frontier. One of the Japanese Seconds holds up a Nutella jar made into a donations collection. Written in Japanese, something to the effect of 'Peter's Fund'. Dragon II: I will leave this donation jar on this very table, where all generous Australians will leave many earnings in your name. In this gesture of good will, I only ask. -- Please let this kind-hearted fool beacon the light of Christmas unto his only aquaintence, The Original Orange Dragon? If you do so, tremendous fortunes may come your way. Please, Oh Gentle Commissioner Of The Frontier, let my Grand Prix earnings touch the heart of others? Surely, yours will be blessed as well. Standing from the chair, he tries to force his 'hand' to a normal position. It just WON'T go down. Odd. All because someone asked for money. Poor Orange Dragon II. A bow to Commissioner Windham, he is off to whatever fancies his nature. [the thread's next post — 20 Dec 2012] (CUTTO: JUST MARK standing by with PETER WINDHAM in front of an NFW backdrop) MARK: 'Just' Mark here for NFW.com, and I am joined by the controversial LAST OF THE MO-WINDHAMS, Peter Windham. PW: That's COMMISSIONER Peter Windham! Get it right! MARK: Alright, well I'd like to ask you about that and some other comments you made. Let's start with Boogie Smallz. You made reference to him being white by comparing him to another, WHITE, wrestler. As we all know, Boogie is a man of African American descent. Would you care to elaborate on what you meant? PW: Yeah, I would! Now I have 20/20 vision, Mark. I'm like an owl at long range. I KNOW Boogie is an African American. But he is not black! In the words of that ESPN dude, Boogie is a CORNBALL BROTHER. MARK: A CORNBALL BROTHER?! PW: A cornball brother. I can speak on this because the black community has accepted me as their adopted son, kinda like how Troy accepted me as his brother...WHICH I AM! Although my skin is white, I am here to tell you that I am BLACKER THAN BOOGIE SMALLZ! And as NFW's historic, first black Commissioner, I demand that Boogie's 2010 census data be retracted and edited to reflect him as an APACHE NATIVE AMERICAN, a Hispanic, or even a plain ass caucasian. I got a text this morning, and Morgan Freeman refuses to narrate another nature documentary until this happens! MARK: Hey, that's downright offensive! PW: Look dude, I may be whiter than bird shit, but I know something about REAL TALK. And the reality is, Boogie has a framed picture of George H.W. Bush hanging above his fireplace mantle. His whole life, he had it good! He ate the white man's food! Drank the white man's drink! MARK: What IS the white man's drink? PW: Schweppes. MARK: Ahhh. PW: Me, I drink nothing but Rolling Rock, 4Loco, and MD 20/20. White trash and hood rats love me equally. I have wide appeal! That's what makes me the perfect Commissioner! And all I have to say to Boogie is...SUPER NINTENDO, SEGA GENESISSSSSS...PETER WINDHAM IS THE MOTHERFUCKING COMMISH! (FADE) [the thread's next post — 1 Jan 2013] (FADEIN to Boogie Smallz stepping out of baggage claim and lookin’ for a taxi outside of the Kingsford Smith International Airport in Sydney, Australia. A NFW cameraman is there trying to get some words from wrestlers as they arrive to the destination of the next New Frontier show.) CAMERAMAN: Hey…Boogie, I’m with NFW. I wanted to get a word with you, if I could. The fans are awaiting your debut for the company and one of the wrestlers here, Peter Windham, had some unflattering words for you. Do you have any response? (Smallz shakes his head in annoyance.) BOOGIE SMALLZ: Yeah…that’s cool. Let’s talk about it. At first I was gonna ignore it because I don’t have time to mess with the likes of him. I wanted to focus on some of the guys holdin’ gold around here, but since you brought it up…let’s do this. Peter Windham, you obviously have no clue who you’re fuckin’ with. You wanna run your mouth and try to get my attention…mission accomplished. You got my attention. The question now becomes…what’s your next step? You have no idea who I am or what I’m about. You blindly go on television and say I’m an Ice Tre wannabe…when truthfully speakin’…that’s what YOU are. A carbon copy of the man that used to carry around my bags back in the day. A kid that wanted my attention for some validation. All you are is a guy tryin’ to latch onto somethin’ because you have no identity. You are bumblin’ around New Frontier searchin’ for who you are…and from my estimation…all you are is the bitch of the Windham Clan. You’ve been abandoned…and now you’re all by yourself. No one to watch your back, no one to stick up for you when you let that mouth of yours get you into a situation that your physical skills can’t get you out of. You wanna talk shit about me and try to make a name for yourself at my expense? Good luck with that…I’m sure it’s all gonna pan out in the long run. (Shakes his head.) I mean, you didn’t even bother findin’ out ANYTHING about me before you opened your mouth. No…instead you come on national TV and make an ass out of yourself without even botherin’ to type in my name on Google and researchin’ some things before tryin’ to pick a fight with me. I got bigger things to do in NFW…than fuck with some bottom feeder like you. I just don’t get it. You wanna make things worse on yourself by callin’ me out? Say the Ice Tre comment…then try to clarify the ridiculous nonsense comin’ outta your yap. Tryin’ to backtrack so you don’t look like more of an idiot than you already do…and attempt to label me as some Calvin Carlton type dude. What were you expectin’? A stereotype? Some idea of what a black man is based on a white man? The rap video guy in the club…poppin’ bottles in VIP…surrounded by hoez …puffin’ blunts and what have you? See…I already went that route many years ago. I was that guy. I was the image of a brotha’ conjured up by some whack ass white promoters. But I’ve moved on, I evolved. Unlike you, who has gone from “Problem Child” to the adopted Windham…a step back if you ask me….but a certain sign of a man that is searchin’ for his place in life and one that will never gain any individuality. Just copyin’ off of Troy and tryin’ to bite of his swag. You run around here, crack jokes, and try to make people laugh. THAT is your function. You aren’t the NFW Commissioner. You may make claims to be. You can hoodwink these other new guys in the promotion, but I know better. They may try to oblige, pay your bills, and get you some CD’s. You may have them fooled for all I know. But I don’t recall New Frontier releasin’ any type of announcement to the media on your behalf or issuin’ an internal memo statin’ as much. No one takes you seriously…unless you count Orange Dragon and Fappity. You wanna talk shit about me? It’s one thing to say it in the studio with Just Mark. It’s another to come up to me and say it in my face…which I know you don’t have the balls to do. So hide behind the camera. Run your mouth on the set of the Just Mark talk show. Keep heapin’ it on…‘cuz when we cross paths at some point…I will give you that opportunity to express yourself. I will give you the floor to let it all out…speak what’s on your mind with me there as your audience. And you can see where it gets you. Hemmed up in a hospital, tryin’ to make comparisons to wrestlers and video game systems with a nurse…and her not understandin’ one fuckin’ word comin’ out of your mouth because I beat you so bad your jaw is wired shut! And then you can truly be just like the man you idolize. Just like Troy. Hittin’ the morphine button on your IV drip and lettin’ that dope course through your veins…just like he was doin’ backstage years ago when he was strung out on heroine. Cut this shit off…I’m done. (Boogie pushes the cameraman out of the way to hail a cab. FADE TO BLACK)
