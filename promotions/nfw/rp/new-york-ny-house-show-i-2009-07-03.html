New York, NY House Show I

3 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd of July, 2009 (CUTTO: Standing in the middle of a field of red poppies is the man masked in the American flag emblazoned panda get-up. His long black trench coat flaps in the breeze as he holds a skull up for inspection. It is AMERICAN PANDA. He studies it with his eyes, his free hand clutches a hand rolled cigarette whose smoke dances freely within the gentle breeze. He looks up to the camera and smiles his best panda grin.) Panda The human form is a wondrous entity that continues to baffle scientists and sociopaths alike. Are their studies endeavouring enough to understand the creature in which we are all so aptly residual in the fact we've congregated from that of one creation and his rib? (He scratches at his head and tosses the skull over his shoulder. It crashes gently amongst the poppies as American Panda begins to walk amongst them.) Panda Whatev, bruh. SRSLY. I mean... some people really dig into that kinda shit and I was tryin' to appeal to the greater audience. I mean, we all know wrestling fans are getting smarter right? We need thinkin' man's champs and the deep evolution into the psyche of what it takes to want another man's title belt right? (He shrugs as he walks.) Panda Whatever happened to the day where you just wanted a title belt coz it was shiny? Or it matched the silver teapot you had on your mantle. You know the one. The one your stinkin' aunt left you and then gone give ya cousin, like, WHEELBARRAS of FUCKEN MONIES~! BITCH, HOW YOU LIKE ME NOW? NOW I GONE TOOK YA SKULL FROM YA ROTTIN' CARCASS, CHICA! PLUCKED THAT SUMBITCH RIGHT OFF YA HEAD, FOO'! (Panda lets out a "pffft" with cheerful disposition.) Panda Bitch never seen it comin'. Sincin' her eyes are now the fruitful extrails of worm excrement. Which is TOTALLY EXCREMENT! Pardon the pun. But I digress... much like the junkie with Parkinson's... I'm gettin' off track... (He pretends to put a needle in his arm, shakin' like a leaf missing where he was tryin' to fix himself with that mime needle. He paws the air as if to say "get outta here" in that playful way. You know the way. That flirty way, like ya do when ya bus driver cracks a joke.) Panda What it IS that I'm talkin' 'bout is covettin' another man's possession. Wife. Title. Whatev, mang. I don't bother none for meagre detail like that. Sincin' I got me a hankerin' for somethin' shiny... and ya woman's gooch is all pierced up like Belgian tattooist's wet dream. I'll give her 52 stars on her face... and by face I mean lower back and stars I mean jizz drops. (American Panda stopped and thought for a few moments, a slight smile spreading across his face before he remembered he was standing in front of the NFW cameras.) Panda I think my point's slippin' away from me like Jonathan Davis' music career. That last album. Please. I bounced to Fallin' Away and the one 'bout you havin' AIDS. DON'T YOU GOT NO MORE SKELETON'S IN YOUR CLOSET LEFT TO WRITE A DECENT SONG, BOY? WHASSIT GUNNA TAKE? ASS RAPE? UNCLE BOBBY'S ROHYPNOL SLUMBER PARTIES? IT'S TWO-THOUSAND-NINE, HOMMES! GIT IT UP AN' RUNNIN'! (Panda slaps his forehead, sighing heavily before pleading with the camera.) Panda We need Fieldy slappin' that shit again like a thirteen year-old battin' on his flugelhorn. (Panda shook his head, trying to get out of his ramble.) Panda Okay... fact of the matter is... what this promo's all about is... what the meaning behind all this nonsencical Dubya-babble is... I'm callin' all cars. I'm givin' a little shout out to the NFW homies. Little call on out to gitcha groove on with the Walkin' Contradiction. With the American Panda. I mean... I ain't gonna go put out a sign up sheet out, like some sorta elementary school dodge ball team. I don't want no spanners thrown at me. I want punches. Suplexes. Ambi-dextrous moonsaultin' lucha goons and spear tacklin' rugged uglies comin' at me, son. HEY ROSTER! (He cups a hand behind one of those cute little panda ears atop of his mask.) Panda Yeah you. I'm talkin' to you. HEY ROSTER! Somebody better get up and get a little groove to their moves coz PANDAMONIUM's gunna run wild, BRUVNA! Line up. Like a deli. Come gitcha numbers coz the Panda's servin' cold hard serves of ass kickin' biznitch. There's only one thang this ol' Yankee doodle panda bear's worried 'bout doin'... that's gettin' some o'dat shiny bling-bada-bling 'round this nifty little Panda waist. Some gold. NF-Dub gold, babay. I ain't stoppin' at nothin' to do that. Uh-uh. No way. Not a chance, mang. (Waggles a finger at the camera.) PandaKnow what I'ma gon'do when I git my paws on you? (He gives a pretty little shit-eating grin right across that face of his.) Panda I'ma chew ya fuggin jugulars. (With a snap of his powerful jaw, the American Panda let another grin go before he waved goodbye to the camera. And guess what that bitch ass camera gone did? FadeToBlack)
