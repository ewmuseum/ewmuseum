WEEK 10 - KAZUO SHIZAKI vs. 'The Phenom' SHAWN DOUGLAS

16 Mar 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN to what appears to be a hospital room, except that a large sheet covers the entire scene. A methodic beep echoes out of one of the machines, and we see the silhouettes of two men; one is lying in the bed, and the other is leaning up against the wall. The first voice, one we haven't heard before, seems voice-affected... ) MAN: "Don't worry about it, Kaz. They might be laughing now... but they won't be laughing when you've done it. I knew you were the man, Kaz. There were so many, but I knew it was you... the moment I met you." SHIZAKI: "Let us not get ahead of ourselves." MAN: "You don't need to tell me that. Your next opponent is Shawn Douglas. We don't know much about him... " SHIZAKI: "We need to know more. We need to-- " (The figure lying down begins to cough violently, leaning over. The coughing fit stops.) MAN: "Are you going to be okay for this? The SDS did a number on you, pal. If it wasn't for Ryan and Marx, well... well... well let's not talk about it. "Are you sure you're up for this?" SHIZAKI: "The warrior's spirit is not crushed by adversity. Prosser and his goons won't stop me. Douglas won't stop me. These men are all corrupted by their blind visions of glory. These men know nothing of the Code of the Samurai... these men know nothing of honor. "But I will teach them. I will lead by example, as all great men must. Perhaps it is not my destiny to win Ultratitle; perhaps my quest shall go unfinished. But I must try. I must ascend. "So it comes to you, Mr. Douglas. So your own brand of greed and ambition has arrived in NFW. Unlike your last opponent, however, I will not bow down without a fight. Do not be alarmed by my condition. By the time you and I arrive in the same ring, Mr. Douglas... I will be more than ready to do what must be done. "You may not know of me, Mr. Douglas, for I am not Shane Southern, and I am not Michael Manson, and I am not Shawn Hart. "I am the Black Rose. Remember it well, Mr. Douglas. For what you do not know... "... can, and will hurt you." (FADEOUT.) [the thread's next post — 24 Mar 2004] (OOC: I'm so sorry about the length and stuff but I just have absolutely no inspiration right now. I know that my second reply will be much much better!) The sun set and buried itself under the hills of Beverly leaving constellations and moonlight to illuminate the clear Californian sky. The newly appointed travel schedule in Shawn Douglas' life hadn't affected him yet and so he once again found himself home. Shawn sat on the hood of his black Corvette, gazing at the horizon blankly. His body was still tired from the traveling, the wrestling, and of course, Slade Wilson. The military minded trainer kept his relentless workout intact throughout the whole process, for the more you bleed in training, the less you bleed in the ring - at least that was his motto. It all made Shawn second guess his profession... ... but don't worry he's no Brock Lesnar. "You're right, Kazuo. I don't know the Code of the Samurai, it doesn't apply to me and I don't care for it. But don't ever question my honor again. See, last week I made my successful debut in the NFW and defeated Shawn Hart for the rights to the "Phenom" nickname. Sure, it was a great way to make a first impression, beating a former world champion and taking away his name from the merchandise, the flyers, the promotional videos and stamping it all on me... but you know what... I don't want it." Douglas hopped off the hood of his car as cameras panned into a full body shot. "I'm here to make a mark and I'm going to make it MYSELF with MY name, MY face. I'm doing this thing on my own and not with the help of someone else's hype. Yeah, of course it'll take some time but I don't care because I'm ready. I haven't been training day in and day out for nothing, Kazuo. My honor is definitely there. But you know know what's there even more? My drive." SD looked to the sky and grinned. "I'm not going to tell you that I'm going to beat you on Crash, no. I'm not even going to rant about how I'm better than you Kazuo because that's not my style. I let my ring work do the talking for me. After all, actions speak louder than words don't they?" With that, Shawn Douglas opened the car door and revved up the engine before speeding off in the night.
