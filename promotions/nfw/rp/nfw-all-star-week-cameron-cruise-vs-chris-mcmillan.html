NFW All-Star Week: Cameron Cruise vs. Chris McMillan

16 Jan 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::FADEIN on the rickety, sagging ring at the Basham-Schultz Wrestling Academy, the dim and unsavory haunt of “The Wolf” Chris McMillan. The walls all around the ring are plastered with flyers for various promotions. Dogs of War posters are in abundance, as well as three gigantic NFW posters – Banned 4 Life, IMPACT: Summer Survivor, and FUTURESHOCK!. Hastily snatched following each show, they mostly cover the far wall. A few curled and yellowed flyers from the late 70’s can be seen peeking out here and there. McMillan is attired as usual in jeans, harness boots, and a Chris Benoit “Toothless Aggression” t-shirt. McMillan’s hair is slicked back in a thick 50’s DA, though he hasn’t gone quite so far as to grow the Honky-Tonk Man sideburns yet:: THE WOLF Cameron Cruise … we’ve been skirting around each other for months, haven’t we? I feel good about us meeting in the ring this time, Cameron. No triple threat crap with Steel Viper, no lame tag teams, no bags of tricks. Just a referee, a wrestling ring, and the two of us. And, of course, bragging rights and a much-needed couple of points, right? I know you’re dying to prove that you’re better than me. I’m dying for you to try. THE WOLF: I meant it when I said we’d be good as a team, Cruise. I like what you do in the ring. Hell, I even like you a little. But enough of the love-fest. After what’s happened over the last couple weeks … between Jason Payne breaking his neck and that ass Aaron Douglas doing whatever it is he did to Shane Southern, turning a friend of mine against me, not to mention what Shane must be going through … well, I hope you’ll forgive me if I don’t exactly have a warm fuzzy feeling right now. ::McMillan paces in a circle around the ring, hands clasped behind his back:: THE WOLF: Well, I guess neither of us has been having much luck lately. One or two little wins, a sweet taste of what might be, followed by a big, bitter steaming pile of sh(beeeeep)t. I don’t know about you, but that big comeback I’ve been planning ain’t exactly going as planned. Hell, I know that I’m just about knocked out of this thing. After that crapfest Hornet an I put on, it all went downhill. THE WOLF: And when it rains, it pours, right? Some days I don’t even want to get out of bed, much less get to the gym. And forget stepping through the ropes one more time in the hopes that this is the night that you turn it all around. Some days, I want to call Miles and Sullivan and Calvin Carlton all at once and tell them to lube up good and shove it. Some days I think wrestling will be the end of me. But, like the addict I am, I keep coming back. Am I a glutton for punishment, some sort of wrestling sadist? Nah, I don’t think so. I think it’s because I really, truly believe that I’ll turn this thing around. I believe that I’ll have a Charlie Brown Christmas moment, and everything really will turn out all right in the end. I suppose it’s foolish, but I keep hanging on. THE WOLF: What about you? Do you really, truly believe in your great comeback, as I do mine? Are you the underdog, the Rocky Balboa of NFW who’s gonna beat the giant unstoppable Russian dude into submission by repeatedly slamming your face into his fist until he falls down? I need this win, Cruise, and so do you. Not just for the points, either. We need our friggin’ Daily Affirmation, right? THE WOLF: You’re nobody’s pushover, Cameron Cruise, but I’m going over you just the same. ::FADE TO BLACK:: [the thread's next post — 20 Jan 2004] "Proving you're wrong will always be a tough obstacle me for Chris, that I truly admit." (Cruise slowly walks into a grey backdrop, dressed in a black jeans, a white "Wife-beater", arms crossed, accentuating his physique.) CC: However, it's in the differentials that gives me the confidence to beat you. (Rubs gus shadowed chin a moment.) You see Chris, I used to hear things about you and about how things turned out, and usually think that I could never stand up to you, athletically speaking. Sounds silly I know but as I've said before, the respect is there. Into play, inevitability. The nerve racking feeling of going up against the very BEST. Then my first match against you came through and in the form of a Triple Threat against you and Viper. (Cruise smiles for a moment, in reflection.) Funny how much fate can weave itself isn't it? It started with the three of us, and albeit only halfway through, it's down to us three again. Standing positions NOTwithstanding, of course. But you see, I don't lay claim to be anything NEAR the fabled Philadelphia Boxer, or the Drug-addict gone straight, goin' for the gold medal, none of that. But did I ever? Of Course I did, as a kid watching the Television World Series thinking that could be him at the plate or in my case, watching World Title matches in sold-out arenas thinking that that very well could be me someday. But forgetting that stuff might have been my biggest assist in my first training sessions, because back then I trained for ME , to get MY NAME in the books, not anything less. This so-called COMEBACK that I'm on is strong, maybe stronger than some may feel. As I said before, you're right, we may very well be the future standard for Tag Team Wrestling, in the eyes of many. But you're also right in the fact that this ISN'T a Tag match, or a gimmick match, or even a Triple Threat match. Will it stand up to the likes of WarGames? Probably not. But to me, going up against you in a PURE , one-on-one wrestling match is just that---War. One that I guarantee you I will be ready for and will fight in as long as I have the energy and fiber my body can physically provide. I pray you don't think otherwise, Chris, I really do. But if you don't..... Then that will most definately be a Reality Check you will [B]NEVER/B] like . [the thread's next post — 20 Jan 2004] ::FADEIN on the shoreline of Lake Cadillac in (not so) scenic Cadillac, Michigan. A battered puke green ’69 Plymouth Fury sits nosed up to a wooden retaining fence. On the wide expanse of the car’s hood sits ”THE WOLF” CHRIS McMILLAN. McMILLAN is wrapped up in a battered motorcycle jacket, a military issue grey wool blanket across his lap. His eyes rove over the frozen surface of the lake as he watches the ice fishermen in their shanties and pickup trucks and folding sport chairs:: THE WOLF: You know, I don’t know who has less sense. These guys, who sit out here all night long hoping to yank a fish out of two feet of ice, or me. I sit out here all night making ironic comments about the stupidity of ice fishing. I guess I’m the fool. But it gives me something to do. THE WOLF: Cameron, I know you’ll give me your best. I expect it, and I wouldn’t have it any other way. There aren’t going to be any nasty surprises in store for me. I do my homework. I know what I’m doing. I know that once again I get to step into the ring with you, Cruise. But this time it’s just you and I, and that’s the way I like it. Like you said, a pure wrestling match. That’s what I live for. These things are what keep me in the business. It’s what I look forward to each and every day of my life. But I don’t think it’s something you should be looking forward to. Because getting into the ring and actually having to wrestle me … it isn’t a war, Cameron. It’s your own little slice of hell. THE WOLF: You talk about every fiber in your body, about using all the energy your body will give you. You talk about having the confidence to beat me. That’s good. You won’t get anywhere without confidence, as we both know. But do me a favor, Cameron. Take a look at the tapes. Do your homework on me. That’s how you’ll learn the truth about me. Not by listening to what I say, but by seeing what I do. That’s how the measure of a man is taken. ::McMILLAN shivers a little, pulling the blanket up over his shoulders:: THE WOLF: And no, I’m not talking about what happened to Jason Payne. What I did to Jason Payne. Dig deeper. Take a look at Summer Survivor. Then maybe, just maybe, you’ll see what I am. You’ll see what I am capable of, and what it takes to beat me. You watch it, Cruise. Watch it more than once, and you ask yourself if you’re capable of that. I don’t think you are, man. I’m sorry, but I just don’t think you’ve got it in you. But prove me wrong, Cruise. Prove me wrong. ::FADE TO BLACK:: [the thread's next post — 20 Jan 2004] "It's interesting really" (Cruise, in between training on the bags at the Gym, wipes sweat off his face with a fresh towel and tosses it over his shoulder. Unwrapping the tape on his hands, he grinds his forehead into the towel on his shoulder once more before continuing.) CC: Chris, not that I'm assuming anything, because we both know that's been my downfall in the past, but it seems to me that you really don't think that I have the ability to take what you can give me and not dish the same amount back? Come on Chris, let's get real about this for a minute....less you fail to remember what I've been through this year or rather less than six months ago? If not...I have no problem helping you with a flashback....even if it has to be in the form of an concussion. I've been through a Triple Flaming table match....on the losing end too, unfortunately on the same night I had to wrestle in a Japanese Texas Death match. In the past I've also thrown a man off a fifteen foot-high stage, beaten opponents to hell with Chairs, hell, if that's not enough, if you wanna get into the Pschye part of it....I also wrestled this past year and got married on the same night....only to get a concussion from Craig Miles for my troubles. Yeah, that's right, the same man that helps get boht of our Paychecks signed, busted me wide open right in front of my own wife, not to mention the entire world.Now if that's not enough to depress a man....I'd sure like to know. Proving you wrong Chris, as I said before is difficult. But it's not impossible. Just like pinning you in the ring. I'll tell you something else, Chris. If you think that my stepping into the ring is my own slice of hell....the the devil doesn't want any part of me. That my friend, is a Reality Check....that you just....won't like. (Cruise reaches over for some jump rope and walks out of view.) Fadeout. [the thread's next post — 20 Jan 2004] ::FADEIN on the rickety, sagging ring at the Basham-Schultz Wrestling Academy. ”THE WOLF” CHRIS McMILLAN sits perched on the top turnbuckle, attired as usual:: THE WOLF: You’ve talked a lot about reality lately, Cameron. You know, I’ve often said that perception is reality. What I mean is that in a way, what you believe is what actually is. There’s a whole larger theory behind this involving perception, reality, alternate universes, and all that good stuff. Ever heard of Schroedinger’s Cat? I’m not going to go into the particulars right now, but maybe I can sum it up. Cameron Cruise beats Chris McMillan. Cameron Cruise loses to Chris McMillan. Cameron Cruise and Chris McMillan draw. They hit each other with chairs and the match gets thrown out. P. “King” Duk runs out and beats us with a Kendo stick. Manson comes out of the curtain and summons a demon that eats the still-beating heart of Chris McMillan. All of these things can happen. All of these things, in fact, HAVE happened. But which one applies to you and I, well … that’s where it gets sticky. What really happens, the cold reality minus our own perception, exists in every form until the instant that we will it into being. Ultimately, along those guidelines, we’re both right. But what remains to be seen, I suppose, is who’s reality is stronger. Who’s is more real. THE WOLF: It’s about faith, Cameron. Don’t confuse what I’m talking about with religion. I don’t mean that. I mean faith itself, or more accurately, the concept of faith. Basically, I’m talking about ultimate belief in yourself. It seems like you’ve got it. But so do I. I know I’m better than you, just like you know you’re better than me. And we’re right. But we still haven’t seen who’s reality is our shared reality. That’s gonna happen real soon, though. I’ve got to ask you, Cameron … what happens if you’re wrong? ::FADE TO BLACK:: [the thread's next post — 20 Jan 2004] Fadein, the Gym Cruise is still training at. Huddled around Cruise are other customers that have stopped their routines and have watched intently as the newest Promo is aired. Cruise, watching intently with his arms folded nods, as the the promo ends and staff members of the Gym have begun to break up the commotion about All-star Week. Walking back towards the treadmill area, Cruise again nods in agreement.) CC: Again with the truth, I can't tell you how much better this is to talk about than the discussion about how many whores other men have or who looks prettier in the ring or out for that fact, let alone dare we speak about going Hardcore. I always knew you were a smart man Chris, but what you said to me either means that you're in fact, a very intelligent man OOOORRRR...... you watch way too much Scifi-Channel. Let me clarify something Chris. When I tell you that it's a Reality Check, then that's what it could quite possibly be. I suppose in essence, it ties into Schroedingers' theory, as you said but this goes a different route. See....alot of people really don't see me as the one coming into the match the odd's favorite winning, which is okay with me because by virtue of over-confidence....improper weightlifting, malnutrition, whatever it may be it doesn't matter. It doesn't because just that in itself gives me that much more of a personal edge over the opponent across from me. A perfect example: A1E's Promo. I gave him all the respect in the world going into our match, and he just shoved it aside because he didn't have to even train for the match. Instead, he was too proud of his own past achievements to even bother giving me the benefit of the doubt. The result? He STILL hasn't won yet, and the bosses had it in mind that he would simply STEAMROLL over everyone else in the way to begin with. That's what I mean when I tell you about Reality Checks. You're right, I have faith in myself that I can do well, no matter what I do, and to be honest, I have a Seven-foot tall Giant-ridden-with-Multiplesclerosis named The Deacon to thank for. So there's no doubt in my mind that I'm internally ready for you. You asked me what would happen if I was wrong....what would I do then.... (Cruise walks through a lockerroom hallway, squeezing by customers as we speak, and stopping just short to grab a complimentary waterbottle and a towel.) Then let me answer that question Chris, with one of my own for you: What happens if I'm right? Because if I am...IF.... then that's a Reality Check, that you just....won't like. (Cruise walks outta view, and back towards the lockerroom. Fade out.
