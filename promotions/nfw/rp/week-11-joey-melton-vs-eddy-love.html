WEEK 11: JOEY MELTON vs. EDDY LOVE

11 Apr 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: A perfectly manicured grass tennis court. NFW South Commissioner Calvin Carlton, wearing a white v-neck sweater over a pink button-down and khakis, stands at the baseline as a ball shooter fires balls at Calvin. Calvin holds his tennis racket in proper Agassi-like form and runs at the balls attempting to hit them over the net. He is, of course, unsuccessful-- green tennis balls go flying in all directions except forward. Calvin grows more and more angry as each ball flys until, finally, he smashes the racket off the ground.) CALVIN: YOU HAVE GOT TO BE KIDDING ME! Calvin kneels down at his tennis racket, now smashed into a million pieces. He then starts openly weeping. And weeping. And weeping. After five minutes of pathetic child-like crying, Calvin takes his face out of his smooth, never-worked-a-day hands, and still on his knees, begins to talk.) CALVIN: It's just not fair. It just isn't fair sometimes. My momma... the jewel in the crown called life... the one who lights up the nighttime sky with her beautiful smile... she has bred me to be the best, to be the greatest. Like her and all of her ancestors. And here I am, working for her and one of her many charities... entered into a celebrity tennis tournament for her name... and I am too distracted and too torn internally to concentrate on more forehand smash, my backhand lob, my service game, my net play and my all-around mastery of grass, the toughest of tennis surfaces. (Calvin starts to weep again, his head bowed as he is still on his knees. He is weeping. And weeping. And weeping.) CALVIN: Why am I, the valedictorian of elite prep schools, the student president of Ivy League colleges, the manager of wrestling champions, the commissioner of the NFW South, so torn? It is because of the friends I keep, the company I associate with. I only associate with the best. World leaders come to Momma for advice, CEO's come to her for respect and wrestlers come to us for guidance. My two charges... the only two men I currently call friend... Eddy Love and Joey Melton... the greatest wrestlers-- nay-- the greatest athletes of all time... must do battle this week. I have tried to do what I can. I have tried to keep these two warriors apart. But once again, my momma sees a better path, a better plan. And it is by her decree, the ultimate law in any land, that this match must happen. My two best friends, my two idols, scheduled to go face to face... but my momma, she wants us three to decide the fate. Joey Melton, the chosen one, who has unfortunately been led astray by the vapid sexuality of a Floridian beach whore. Eddy Love, the soldier, the great humanitarian, who has sworn to do what is best for my momma, but still a proud man himself. And it is me, Calvin Carlton, unfairly stuck in the middle. (Calvin starts to openly weep again. FTB) [the thread's next post — 14 Apr 2004] (Eddy Love stands in front of his palatial estate, wearing a pair of blue tennis shorts, an “I Break it, You Bought it” T-shirt and no shoes. Both his eyes are slightly blackened from the boot incident at Crash. Behind him a buxom blonde in a damp wife beater T and a pair of tight shorts with “LOVE“ emblazoned across the rear drying the freshly washed golden brown Bentley) Home field advantage throughout the playoffs……. Two wins against no losses ……… That Damn Hornet ran out of town after just one beating when it usually takes 3.… Joey Melton primed to pass Mean Jean Rabesque in the standings ……. It looks like the NFW is like every where else Eddy ever wrestled cause right off the bat he’s red hot. (pause) BUT is Eddy Love happy???? Oh no he is not. I hate to sound like the aforementioned attorney, but before I ever wrestle one match in NFW I have to dodge an exploding cage, but if it sells tickets I don’t mind a little risk. I can live with the poor decision that NFW management made when they take the two greatest ring generals this side of my close personal friend Joey Melton and turn it into Greensboro Lawyer versus the Southern Dandy falls count anywhere in the building street fight instead of making matters be settled in the ring where both of us have always done our best work. Having dirty low talent cheaters like Shane Southern around and letting them nearly blow my head off with his illegal boot is not my preference, but I’m just thankful it didn’t go off during any of the dozen illegal kicks to the crotch he attempted. BUT when the NFW suits see they’ve let the unstoppable force into their little play yard they have to throw up the immovable object and pit me against my close personal friend Joey Melton. Well don’t cry for me Calvin Carleton. The only thing stronger than Hurricane Eddy Love and Joey Melton’s warrior instincts is their mutual caring and respect for each other. In fact it’s refreshing to me that The Blue Ridge Bomber will finally get a match in NFW that can be wrestled fair and square, just like Hurricane Eddy likes it. There will be no loaded boot, no branding iron, no fear of that Highwayman jumping me in the parking lot…… just the two greatest wrestlers this sport has ever known showing the NFW how the sport of kings was meant to be played….. Gracefully, fairly. When it is said and done I think….. No, no I guarantee that Eddy Love and Joey Melton will shake hands if we can hold back an outright embrace and the loser will congratulate the winner, because when both are unequalled champions there is no loser, only winners and the NFW fans will be the greatest winners of all. I know Joey Melton will come ready as will myself. May the better man win. (Love turns walking to the girl at the Bentley pointing to the tires. ) What about this rim? Did you not see this spot? (The girl runs around bending over slightly in front of Love polishing the rim.) No not that spot, the one on the bottom of the rim. (She bends all the way over, fanny to Love and camera. Love turns and winks to camera) There now that’s the spot. (ftb) [the thread's next post — 17 Apr 2004] (FADEIN: Joey Melton in front of an NFW backdrop.) MELTON: In a time in our country's history when we should be promoting peace, love, and understanding...the powers that be in the NFW, whoever they are....Miles, a collection of "I'm Black and I'm Proud" marchers, or Mr. Green Jeans, have thrown this sport's two greatest stars into the ring for a cheap ratings bump, and perverse satisfaction. Normally, I'm the first in line for the latter, but not this week...not when... Eddy Love and Joey Melton have been asked to essentially kill a dear, old, sweet black woman who's done nothing but cook for the neighborhood kids Calvin brought home as a child, and write letters to her state senator when others were too afraid too, or couldn't. I have to ask the front office. Which one of you will pay for Momma Carlton's hospital bill after she has a heart attack? If you could see her puffy brown cheeks, and the five-dollar bags under her eye you'd feel sorry for tearing her apart! Better draw straws to see who foots the bill, because her health insurance has already sent a memo stating she's not covered for "for acts of terrorism." That's what this is. Someone in the NFW North has done the unthinkable and paid off an authority figure in the NFW to make this match up happen. Eddy Love vs Joey Melton. Somebody wants to see Momma Carlton dead and I'm calling for a full-scale investigation as of today! How dare you attack a woman who's given of HER own money to help this regional promotion stay afloat. (shakes head) It's shameful. Well, it's like this. <i>I</i> won't contribute to the death of the woman. The NFW North wants Eddy Love and Joey Melton to go toe-to-toe and leave Charleston in a blood bath, severing our ties befoe they've run their course and placed me in the ULTRATITLE Finals. My man Eddy is right... We're doing the opposite. We're gonna save that woman's life by wrestling a CLEAN match, and shake hands afterwards. The Jackie Robinson of wrestling was blindsided this week, but consider Love and myself Pee Wee Reese. We'll have our hands over Momma's shoulder after the match, in the face of all your Northern Haters...telling her: "Don't worry about them." "It's only Joey Melton that matters." (FTB)
