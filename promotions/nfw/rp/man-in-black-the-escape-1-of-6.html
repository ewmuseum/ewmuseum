Man In Black : The Escape (1 of 6)

17 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A figure, face hidden by the lack of light in the area, drops from the bottom of a fire escape onto the tarmac below. From above comes a series of expletives. He hugs the wall, hidden in the shadows by his dark clothing, while a large, bald man with a bandana around his neck leans out of the window above. He turns back in, says something to someone still in the room, and turns back out. "Fine, fine. Unknot your panties. Geez. An' we can call in when we get him. Not gonna call and cause a big deal." With that, he drops to the ground, landing in a crouch. Silently, the escapee slips behind him, grabbing around the neck with one arm, and looping his prisoners' arm with the other. "Keep quiet or die," he whispers in the man's ear. Another figure leans out of the window. This one is wearing an old black fedora. "'Ay! You OK?" The bald man is breathing heavily, and doesn't answer immediately. "Tell him you're fine," whispers the escapee again, "Or I'll break your neck." "Yo, I'm fine! Get down here, he's gettin' away!" The Hat leans out of the window, sits on the windowsill, and drops. Before he lands, the escapee smashes the bald guys' head into the brick wall. The bald guy slumps to the ground, unconscious. The Hat lands with a breathed "Oof!" Before he has a chance to turn around, he is clubbed in the back of the head with the bald guys' flashlight. The Hat is unconscious. The escapee lets a brief smile cross his face, which is still hidden from us. He leans down, and takes the fedora from its owner, and places it on his own head. He takes the bandana from the bald guy, and covers his face with it. A deep breath escapes his lips. Finally. So close. He pulls the two unconscious men together and props them against eachother. He lifts his shirt and pulls an envelope from his waistband, placing it on the ground next to the two. Its only marking is "Mr. Gotti", and the escapee knows it'll get to him the minute these two goons are found. Straightening up, the escapee turns around, and flees off into the field beyond. He'll have a two-hour lead on his captors by the time they discover he's gone ...
