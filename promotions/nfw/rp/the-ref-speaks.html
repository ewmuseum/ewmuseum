The Ref Speaks

21 Apr 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with Adrenaline standing outside a footlocker shoe store. The lights inside go out and the door creaks open and a older man and a younger girl walk out. As they close the door behind them, they're startled to see Adrenaline standing there.) Man: What are you doing? This is an employee parking. I'm going to call the police! Adrenaline: Chill pops. I need a referee shirt. Young girl: What? Man: Just don't hurt us, you can have all the money I have- Adrenaline: Did you hear me just say I don't want your money. I'm reffing for a match, and I didn't feel like wearing that cheap crap NFW has. I'll give you this for yours. (Adrenaline holds out a wad of twenty dollar bills. Both the man and girl's eyes widen. The man begins to wiggle out of his shirt. As he pulls it off, his saggy man-boobs and slight beer gut show. Adrenaline and the girl both do a double take.) Adrenaline: Know what, you can have this if you put your shirt back on. And you(he turns to the girl.) I'll double this if you take your's off. Girl: But um..I'm not wearing a bra. Adrenaline: Well then that makes two of us. C'mon jus give it to me..you can buy 100 bras. Girl: Um..ok. (The girl pulls off her shirt and her breasts are pixeled out. Adrenaline and the manager both stare for a few seconds before Adrenaline takes the shirt and looks at it to make sure it's not a "girl's only" shirt. Satisfied he hands her some money as she covers her boobs. He then walks over to the Z8 and gets in and speeds off leaving the manager staring at his employee as the shot fades out.)
