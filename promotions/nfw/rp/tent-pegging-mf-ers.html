Tent-Pegging MF'ers

25 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: To a gorgeous, almost-cloudless sky, which slowly pans down to an open field set up for equestrian games - we focus on a area the size of a football field, which has some men dressed like indian sultans stand side-by-side with their arms behind their backs, wearing centurian-like helmets with the chinstrap cutting under their lower lips. The camera pans over to a spectator area, were we see a sweating and uncomfortable-looking MALIK ANDERSON, sitting in a white folding chair wearing a seer-sucker suit with a bowtie and boater hat, who is doing almost a robotic repeating motion of taking off his hat, wiping down his face with a kerchief, and replacing said hat on top of his balding head. In some seats further back in the row is DORCHESTER STRATTON, wearing a white cotton suit and tie from the new "DORCHESTER SATIN" Jos. A Bank Signature collection, big glasses, leg folded over the other and watching the field while VERONICA ABRAMS RUMSFELD sits next to him, wearing a large polka dot church hat tilted at an impossible angle, dark glasses, and a remarkable air of snootyness. Sitting in front of the whole group in a row to himself, is CALVIN CARLTON, in a teal blazer with the Carlton Family Crest on the heart, white pants, pink button down shirt, checkered drivers cap, gold Alpina frames with a 79% pink gradiant and "Ol Bessie" outfitted in a fuschia slipjacket with yellow and blue gems spelling out a huge "D". CARLTON Cackles loudly, then looks around embarrased, then cackles softly. The DYNASTY bodyguard, BANDIT is noticably absent. CARLTON: (Whisper-cackle) "ha ha ha ha ha ha. If you weren't sure who you were staring at out there in TV land, you are addressing the Carlton Family DYNASTY, a subsidiary of The Carlton Trusts, of Carlton Enterprises, Inc. Consider yourself GIFTED, but just this once. The first thing that you should note, is that the places I go, are places mouth breathers like you can't AFFORD, let alone FIND ON A MAP, but for the 99 percent of you out there watching this, if you had a map and I told you the name of it, you STILL COULD NOT FIND IT. (RUMSFELD shakes her head) "That is because first off - An American public school education is only good for churning out the kinds of people that my sweet, beautiful momma hires to change the trash liners in her opulant summer SUMMER home away from Valhalla. And no I didn't stutter - That refers to the OTHER summer residence that she stays in while the other one is being cleaned for the week. And also, the same kind of people who think five dollar foot long sandwiches are acceptable as meal options don't deserve to breathe my air, or even lay down in a pool of dog excrement for me to walk over to climb into one of my many, many mayonaise-colored Bentley coupes. YOU are the people that have no business dreaming about what it would be like to be me, Calvin Jay Chalmers Carlton the Third, ESQUIRE. (Soft laughs again) Heh Heh Heh. It excites me to know that you are all so so poor, and I'm so rich! "But on to the matters at hand. As you all know by now, well, the Carlton Dynasty is in a ... well, how can I say, Dry Spell. (STRATTON scowls!) And no, not that I would know ANYTHING about those - Oh no! Because Calvin Carlton gets more booty than a toilet seat, trust me! - but one could say that the DYNASTY has been in better situations. Dorchester Stratton - the Country Club Assassin - the Blue Chip Stock Option, The Brylcreem Bully - has NO TITLES AROUND HIS WAIST. (DORCHESTER grits his teeth!) Our (airquotes) "Bodyguard" Bandit couldn't protect the stars of Toy Story on Ice at a Meet & Greet, and probably the only thing he's beaten lately, is his babycorn-sized penis, because he sure as hell hasn't beaten any NFW opponents! And that leads me to the point of this little fireside chat we're having - The Jewels in the tagteam cap of NFW, the BEST tagteam in the history of NFW, nay - the WORLD, The Hollywood Wrecking Crew have looked downright HORRIBLE as of late. (CUTTO: MALIK, who just stares straight ahead at the horses trotting by in the ring) "That's right, Malik. You heard me. The Hollywood Wrecking Crew instilled TERROR and FEAR and a bad case of butterflies in any team that was DUMB enough to step into the ring with us - and LO AND BEHOLD, my horse-hair wearing big sister, Shaniqua shows up with those (grips his tennis racket in a death grip) those STREET MONKEYS and BLINDSIDE US AND TAKE THE EMT BELTS. And EVERY TIME we try to get them back, there's something ALWAYS GETTING IN THE WAY. So now we're at an empasse - EYE want another shot at those belts, but now, the Wrecking Crews' record is in question! I can't tell you how mad that makes me! Now, there's murmuring that... WE'RE NOT IN LINE FOR A TITLE SHOT! Now that... that... the ORIGINAL SHOWSTOPPERS, a tagteam that EYE BROUGHT TO THE TOP OF THE MOUNTAIN, are back in NFW - and are drawing the attention of Shaniqua's goons. And on top of that, don't think that I don't believe that someone will soon be trying to wet their fingers and stick together some paint-by-numbers tagteam to pad the rankings! (CARLTON starting to shake like a wet goose and RUMSFELD puts a comforting hand on his shoulder but he slaps it away!) "Don't believe I don't see idiots like that drug-addled buckethead NOVA and some guy who got dropped on his soft spot as an infant, Jack Harmen - even LEGION raising eyebrows in the back offices - OVER MY MEN?! I AM NOT BLIND! NFW IS FILTHY WITH WHAT HAVE YOU DONE FOR ME LATELIES! Don't you know who I am? I AM CALVIN CARLTON, and EYE INVENTED TAGTEAM WRESTLING! The HWC and Tagteam Gold are like Brie and red grapes with a nice Shiraz! We are the standard-bearers for excellence, and I'll be DAMNED if the Dirty Rotten Scoundrels make a FOOTNOTE out of Calvin Carlton! The OSS can't function in modern society without help - who do you think taught them how to take a DVD out of its' clamshell case and load it into a player? Do you think they figured that out THEMSELVES?! NO! And if Fargo and Mueller think they're just gonna monster truck roll themselves back into title contention, they have another thing coming! ANDERSON: "Cal - Blaine said he ain't wrestling no OSS - OR the DRS. . . How you figure we gonna get those belts back?! (CAL's neck receeds into his collar like a mad box turtle!) CARLTON: (looking nervous) "Malik, don't worry about this - It's fine! I'LL talk sense into him--" (Just then, we hear the footfalls of a gallopping horse, and the camera pans over to the ring, where there are two sets of gallows hung up with two large sunkist oranges hanging from wires! we focus in on BLAINE HOLLYWOOD, in full riding gear along with riders helmet, is on the back of a charging horse, holding on to the reins with one hand, and in the other, a deadly-looking curved scimitar sword with a blue and white oxford sash flowing from the hilt! BLAINE flies towards the first pole, and with one swift motion SLICES the orange in a perfect half! Then a split second later, jedi flourishes with the sword and BACKHAND SLASHES THE OTHER ORANGE, making the bottom half spin off in air like a UFO! A beat later, rides up to a small white peg in the grass, leans low off the side of the horse and picks it clean out of the ground with the tip of his sword, making the DYNASTY members in the stands get out of their chairs and applaud! ANDERSON does not, and slides back into his chair folding his arms.) CARLTON: (jumping up and down!) "YES! MASTERFUL, BLAINE! MASTERFUL! That's what we're going to do to the DRS very soon!" (HOLLYWOOD trots over to the side of the fence as the camera moves up to meet him, and he looks down, brandishing his scimitar and sticks it STRAIGHT at the camera, eyes almost unseen from the cast shadow from his helmets brim!) BLAINE: (Barely breathing heavy) "NFW - YOU ARE ON NOTICE THAT I AM NOT TO BE TRIFLED WITH. THAT THE HOLLYWOOD WRECKING CREW IS NOT TO BE TRIFLED WITH. THAT THE DYNASTY IS THE ENDGAME. Just as I, THE FUTURE PERFECT - made three deft strikes on this field, I will strike swiftly - AND SILENTLY IF NECESSARY, upon whooOOOOOOMEVER STANDS IN MY WAY, UNLESS YOU ARE BENEATH MY INCOME BRACKET. And THAT is the word of an OXONIAN, and MY WORDS ARE GOSPEL. And THAT - IS AS GOOD AS GOLD." (FADE OUT on the scene, a lip-biting CAL who's clapping and looking over at a squinting MALIK ANDERSON!)
