Observation

27 Oct 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Motivation comes from the strangest sources. When you least expect it, fate steps in and kicks you in the ass...." ------ It could've been worse....much worse. Matches have been lost in the past, and in much more important circumstances. Much is said about the legacy, built in such a short time. But with the wins have also come the losses. This was nothing new....nothing to be too concerned about. Yet still.... Dan Ryan shook his head. "I should've had him." He looked down at endless stacks of paperwork. There was business to tend to...business that most in the wrestling world wouldn't even be aware of. He stared down at it still....knowing it didn't hold the same importance...the same joy that awaited him in the ring. A smile... Not many of those lately. With a click, the big screen TV set into the East wall came to life. It was tuned to ESEN programming. There's something about a man that makes him want to watch news on games...on matches...that he already knows the outcome to...that he saw live the first time around. The Week six preview show had aired earlier in the day, but Phyllis...ever the dutiful assistant...had managed to catch it on tape. A buzz on the speakerphone signalled her to come in, and in moments his assistant peeked into the room. "Sir?" "You have that tape, Phyllis? I need a break from this paperwork." "Um...well yes sir, but..." "But what?" "It's just...Sir, I just don't think..." "Phyllis..." "Yes sir..." Phyllis reluctantly came in...popped the tape into the VCR and pressed play. "A cup of coffee please?" Reluctantly... "Sure." [/i]The tape began. The two nitwits who pass for commentators on the broadcasts began to babble on about newcomers until finally, something of interest...[/i] "H'WOOD: " What can you say about this Shizaki dude? He's NUTS. He'll do anything, anywhere, at ANY TIME to win the match. Defeating Dan Ryan after the HIGH he's been on the last few weeks is quite an accomplishment. I'm betting on the Black Rose to continue his RAMPAGE though these weaker opponents...no offense Castor. " "Right", he thought. "Like that's saying much..." O'CONNOR: " Well, I've got to say that we agree again. Castor is a master manipulator. He's quite possibly the BEST in the NFW at playing mind games. But this BLACK ROSE, I don't think the guys can be raddled. For Castor to win this thing, he's got to pull out every trick in the book, and some that haven’t even been written yet. But if ANYBODY can do it, HE can. " MARK: " I pick Kaz ... " O'CONNOR: " Ok. Let's talk about his opponent. Dan Ryan. Can he recover this week against a guy who's trying to claw his way back into contention, Shawn Hart. " "Claw his way into an ass kicking is more like it..." H'WOOD: " Shawn Hart is a PHENOM Baked Bean. The first few weeks he was sleepwalking through matches. He's woken up now with the motivation from his spiritual advisor. I'm going out on a limb here, and say that with a little help from fat boy, he pulls the upset and beats Ryan. Besides, like Miles' says, Ryan's just a DICK...and I hope he looses. " Now that was priceless...character judgement from a washed up announcer... MARK: " Shawn Hart rules ... " O'CONNOR: " I don't see it happening Wood man. Ryan doesn't like loosing. Even with Hart's new found motivation, I don't see him as being RUTHLESS enough to overcome Ryan's power. Look for Dan Ryan to get back on the winning track this week. ... "Good man", he thought. Phyllis came back in with coffee in hand and set in on the desk. He acknowledged her with a nod and looked back to the screen as Phyllis became a bit uneasy. "oookay..." "Phyllis, what's up? You gotta hit the head or what?" Rolling her eyes... "Gimme a break, sir..." Another smile... "O'CONNOR: " Great. Now, before we get to our next run down, we've got a very special interview with NFW South points leader, and former WORLD Champion ... H'WOOD: " ... and current mental patient. " O'CONNOR: " ... Shane Southern. (glances at H'Wood) Roll tape. " (CUT-TO: An outside shot of a cabin in the woods. CUT-TO: Inside. Shane Southern sits on an old couch in front of a fireplace. He's dressed in camouflage from head to toe and he's cleaning a shot-gun. Kerry O'Connor is seated in an old rocking chair dressed in a grey polo shirt and khaki pants....) O'CONNOR: " So, Shane ... how you feeling? " (Southern stops cleaning the gun and leans it up against the wall. He leans back on the couch and lets out a deep breath) SHANE SOUTHERN: " Well, physically, I haven't felt this good inna' while. Tha' cut back of tha' CSWA schedule has allowed me ta' heal, as much as MY body can heal anyway. Mentally, I ain't gonna' lie ... it's beena' rough few weeks. " Abruptly, Phyllis stopped the tape and retrieved the video from the VCR. "Um, Phyllis...." "That's really all there is to see, sir. Nothing of importance after that really." "I wanna see Southern's interview...put it back in." "Mr. Ryan..." "Phyllis! The tape!" Reluctantly the tape was placed back into the VCR...and it rolled....and Phyllis cringed.... "O'CONNOR: " How so? I mean, you're co-leading the NFW South in points right now. You have not lost a one on one match...and you've yet to be pinned in the "new" NFW. It would seem that things are going GREAT for you. " SHANE SOUTHERN: (flashes a crooked grin) " Yeah, well when ya' put it like that, I guess I'm bein' a big baby huh? (grins) Seriously though, yeah I've been winin'...and yeah, I'm sittin' pretty right now... Suddenly the sound of the words faded from his consciousness...and he heard nothing more..... "You...have....not....lost.....a....one....on...... one......match.....you've yet....to be....pinned......." And Shane sat there.... No...he smiled. And why shouldn't he? He's sittin' pretty.... .... .... .... .... "Sir?" "Could you leave me alone for a few minutes, Phyllis?" [i]She knew it wasn't a question...and she complied immediately. "So Shane...." "That's how it's gonna be. If you don't think about it...it didn't happen...." "How disappointing...." .... .... .... Thoughts ran through his head....he looked out the window to the rising sun in the distance.... "Maybe you need to be reminded...." [the thread's next post — 27 Oct 2003] {{...FADE-IN: Shane Southern standing in front of an NFW backdrop...}} SHANE SOUTHERN: " OK Dan, I could tell by yer face tha' thoughts that were goin' through yer head. So lemme' come right out n' say this before it escalates inta' somethin' it shouldn't be. I apologize fer not correctin' Mister O'Connor's remark about me not gettin' pinned. Ta' tell ya' tha' honest truth Dan, I didn't wanna' give that interview. N' ta' be honest, I got things on mah' mind other than fact checkin' tha' NFW's announce personality. " " You beat me. I'll shout it from tha' highest mountain should that redeem tha' situation fer you Dan. I take nuthin' away from what you've been able ta' do here. " " N' that's all I gotta' say ah'bout it. If you feel yer owed more, ... " " Tough sh<BLEEP>". " Party's Over. " {{...FADE OUT...}} [the thread's next post — 28 Oct 2003] FADE IN... No backdrop...just a man sitting on a chair on his front porch... Ryan: "Damn right you apologize." "But a word to ya...just so you remember what's up, Shane." "If I feel I'm owed something....I'll take that s**t. I won't ask for it." "So if I were you...I wouldn't count on that party wrapping up any time soon. This shindig's just getting started...." "We now resume our regularly scheduled nervous breakdown..." FADE OUT...
