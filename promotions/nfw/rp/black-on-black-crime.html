Black On Black Crime

6 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUE UP: Grainy, Wonder Years-style colored footage of Tommie Smith and John Carlos at the 1968 Mexico City Olympics, raising their black-gloved fists on the medal stand. CUT TO: Calvin Carlton, wearing his expensive suit, holding up his fist in one air, Sweet Betty Lee cradled in the other, standing on the parlor of his Carolina Plantation, as horses graze in the background. Calvin is also wearing a neckbrace.) CALVIN: So, a bunch of black militants have entered the NFW and forced their selves into a takeover of the NFW North. They came in, jumped my boys from behind, and then dragged Doc Martena out and forced her to hand over control of their conference to Avery Prosser. Well, congratulations. (Calvin points his racket.) Dragging Doc Martena out by her skull and forcing her to do your bidding-- oh, like THAT hasn't happened to that Bag Lady Trash before. Our commentators can call that shocking, they can call that sickening but what they did not do is call it like it is-- something that happens on a routine basis. Prosser, I'm GLAD you and your crew of Section 8 voucher recipients came out. I'm GLAD you and your crew of washed-up no-names came out and tried to take me out, tried to take out my charges, The Original Showstoppers, the greatest tag team of all time. Because you know why? You had one shot to do it. And guess what... (said Flavor Flav style) BOYYYEEEEEE? You blew your one chance. Yeah, Prosser-- you and SlumFaar or whatever his fake, made-up name is... you beat me down, sent me to the mat and then called me an Uncle Tom. Because it gets me to prove a point to you and the rest of the world... You see, Prosser, my momma was smart. When you and your kind were busy burning up cities, rampaging through the streets like you were back in some jungle, smoking dope, making babies, not working... my momma, the smartest woman on this planet, she was out campaigning for Richard Nixon, one of her all-time closest friends. When your kind was out protesting about the system, about bringing the system down... my momma was busy USING the system and making it work for her. Prosser, you and your crew of crack slingers, wearing clothes bought from the dirtmall... you think you're scary? You think you're frightening? You don't scare me because I see you all for what you really... (Calvin smirks) is. Angry, unread, ignorant slum garbage. But there's one other word that BEST describes you. Scared. Sh*tless. That's because you know that you will never, ever have power. That you will never, ever be able to control the NFW North... let alone the world. Tell your daughters... all of whom belong to different single mothers. Tell your sisters... most of whom currently reside in correctional facilities. But don't tell my momma, 'cause she already knows. There's already a black man in power around these parts, Prosser. There's already a black man who RUNS this show. And that man... (Calvin stands up and serves the racket.) IS ME!
