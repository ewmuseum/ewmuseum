24 May 2012

Reloaded was in the can. Rook Black was the Triple Crown Champion. Congratulations, old friend, even if you don't consider me one anymore. And JJ DeVille had his hand raised. As I lay here in my less - than - comfortable hotel room bed, my hands crossed behind my head and my eyes staring straight up into the ceiling, I felt something that I haven't felt in a long time. Freedom. And contentment, of course, because of the lovely lady sound asleep next to me. Rose looked like she hadn't slept in weeks, which is entirely possible when you consider the fact that she's actually spent the past few weeks in Los Angeles with Travis 'TJ' Johnson, bass player for Valerian's Garden. Party like a rockstar, Rosie. How was California, I'd asked her, did you do what you had to? She had smiled at me like she always did. "It was different," she said, "and Teej is cool and Chree is a sweetie, but the main thing that being there did was prove to me that this is where I belong." In the New Frontier, I replied. "No, you dingbat," said Rose, "with you." I didn't realize until that moment, how much I actually missed her smile. "That's not to say the NFW doesn't need me," continued Rose. "Everyone here is too tense and too in their own head. I'm going to have to change all that." You...? "From this point forward, I'm declaring myself the official New Frontier Wrestling Morale Officer," said Rose, and she actually stopped walking and put her hands on her hips as she said it. I don't think the New Frontier is ready for that, I said, with a smirk. "Keep that up and I'll get myself a badge and a hat to go along with it," said Rose, "I'm serious, RK... all of you chuzzlebutts need someone to remind you how much fun this is." I shook my head. Now I know you're still you, I said. You're still out of your mind. And she hit me. Not hard, just enough to know that she was no more out of her mind than she ever was. But her absence these past few months was eating a hole in my chest, I see it now. She's my other half, my lifemate, my partner. I was allowing the Windham Clan to get inside my head and push me toward something that I'm not, no matter how much JJ DeVille tried to paint me as a fraud. Can't be a fraud when you don't have a plan. And I've been making it up as I go along for years. Maybe that's the problem. Maybe I need a plan. The Windham Clan is nearly out of gas, I knew they wouldn't be around forever: it was just a matter of time. And I think I accomplished my goal. JJ DeVille got his hand raised, but the price was pretty steep. Mark Windham? Gone. Dan Ryan? Gone. Cameron Cruise? Gone. Bobby Jack Windham? August De La Rossi? Overcome. Troy Windham? He's about as much a part of the Windham Clan these days as I am. And there's JJ. Yes, he got his hand raised, but if I can reuse an old analogy, it was made painfully clear that the emperor wears no clothes. They're the vampires, and I was the vampire slayer for the past six months, I did my best to show the rest of the New Frontier that the Windham Clan is nothing to fear. Of course, the question comes up, why was it my job to do so? Brawl awaits... we'll talk about it then. For now, I'm just going to enjoy my life. And there's a ton to enjoy.
