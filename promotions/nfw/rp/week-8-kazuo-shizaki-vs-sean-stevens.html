WEEK 8: Kazuo Shizaki vs. Sean Stevens

17 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN to the serene shrine which we have seen many times before, lit only by a littany of candles in various places. Kazuo Shizaki kneels with his back to the camera and his head bent, facing two long, vertical banners depicting various Japanese glyphs.) SHIZAKI: "There are few men who contain the capacity to understand the sacredness of the battlefield. Perhaps even fewer who understand the consequences of their actions or, in some cases, inactions. "Such is the case with you, Mr. Stevens. Once considered a top NFW prospect, you have fallen far. And indeed, it is your own fault. You fail to take this sport or yourself seriously, and when you fail to do these things, you fail yourself. There are no other places to lay the blame. "Yet this is not enough. Your lack of character and honor is disgraceful at best; an atrocity at worst. When you step into that ring and you fail to put yourself on the line, you not only disrespect yourself, but the very sport which has given you your livelihood all of these years. "For your actions, Mr. Stevens... there must be consequences. When you disgrace the ring and the title we have all come for, then you devalue everything we have done. You trivialize the great care and effort that I have put into making certain that my destiny lies in my own hands, and more importantly, in the hands of the great men who have employed me. "Mr. Stevens... for you... the Black Rose will not be the same as for the others. For the others, despite their own ignorance and arrogance, they would at least stand before me and fight me as they could. Not you, though, Mr. Stevens. "So your punishment, it would seem, must exceed all those that came before it. Justice must be served, whether or not it is moral. And as I have learned from my own defeat, you will learn from your own humiliation. "All actions have consequences. All are responsible for themselves. And thus, when you are crawling at my knees, begging me to show you even the slightest hint of mercy... "... you will be met with the very silence which you have delivered to others. "So the circle closes." (FADEOUT.)
