Where You Stand

20 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: A banged, cut-up JJ DEVILLE -- suit-jacket torn, bandages on his forehead, has a neckbrace on as he sits in a wheelchair. Pushing the wheelchair, also bandaged, his arm in a sling, is AUGUST DE LA ROSSI. Flanking them to both sides are THE CHOSEN ONES -- BOBBY JACK WINDHAM and CHRISTIAN CRUISE< both of them also bruised.) JJ: "A lot of people have a lot of things to say about me. They say I'm POWER MAD. They say I'm a pathetic little man using his mastery of paperwork to make everyone's life MISERABLE. They say that I am the ULTIMATE LOCKER ROOM CANCER. And they all cheered and applauded after what happened at Buffalo -- where I was left BROKEN and my minions BRUISED." (They all stand emotionless, except for August who says "GET KONY!") JJ: "SHUT IT, AUGUST! YOUR BOSS IS SPEAKING!" (August shakes his head apologetically.) "First, I just want to reassure everyone on the NFW roster that I... I mean THE WINDHAM CLAN... still owns 50% of this league. I own 50% of your contracts. I own 50% of your travel requests. I own 50% of your LIVELIHOODS. Just because a few... unforeseen... incidents happened at the end of the night doesn't mean that I... WE... HAVE LET GO OF OUR GRIP AROUND THE LIVES OF THIS PROMOTION! But we'll get to that... shortly." (JJ winces as he holds his neck.) JJ: "Dan Ryan, I've rewatched your comments several times already. And you said something very, very, very true. You said that I've stayed out of your way. And, Ryan, yes, let me APPLAUD you for speaking the truth. I did stay out of your way, as you requested. Why? Because, Dan... I always knew that YOU WEREN'T GOOD ENOUGH to join The Windham Clan. You joined our ranks in an attempt to win the title. You had us at your disposal. You had the greatest collection of talent... and the sport's ULTIMATE mastermind... AND Troy Windham... at your disposal. And, Dan, guess what? You FAILED. You FAILED at every chance to get that title. You FAILED at getting Castor Stryfe's shoulders down to the mat." (Everyone sneers.) JJ: "The SAME Castor Stryfe who *I PINNED* all those months ago which GAVE ME CONTROL! WHICH GAVE YOU THE UPPER HAND! That YOU FAILED TO USE PROPERLY! And you have NOTHING and NO ONE TO BLAME but YOURSELF for YOUR FAILURES." (JJ purses his lips.) JJ: "So, Ryan, you stand against The Windham Clan? That's fine with me. Because that means that you NOW REPORT TO ME DIRECTLY. And, Ryan... unless you come out here on national television AND OFFER ME A PUBLIC APOLOGY... AND HAND ME A GUARANTEED LIFETIME CONTRACT WITH THE PROMOTION YOU RUN... and have BLACKMAILED ME FROM... along with my choice and time of a WORLD TITLE SHOT... then the next bunch of arena lease payments WON'T BE APPROVED BY ME. I will SHUT THIS ENTIRE LEAGUE DOWN, like I have done several times already." (Everyone laughs but The Chosen Ones. Bobby Jack steps forward. Everyone drops their heads.) BJ: "Dan Ryan....first, let me tell you that ah am prayin' for your little girl." (Bobby genuflects.) "Ignorant people such as yourselves can't comprehend WHY someone so beautiful was taken so early. But, it's for the best, because she no doubt would have SUFFERED throughout her entire lahf with YOU as her father, attemptin' to steer her through lahf. She would have seen you... a FAILED MAN... and would have turned to a lahf of desperation and despair. And, no doubt, Dan... the SHAME she would have felt knowing she was the product of YOUR seed... is what she would have thought about when she was wrappin' herself around a pole as a'bunch of immigrants threw money at her face. Thank JEEEEEEESUS, Dan... that your daughter is DEAD as opposed to becoming the worthless, degraded PROSTITUTE she would have become." (JJ purses his lips.) JJ: "But let's not forget that even being THAT level of whore... means she's still SEVERAL castes above Rosie." (August laughs so loudly. FTB.)
