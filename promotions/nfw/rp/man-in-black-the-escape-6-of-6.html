Man In Black : The Escape (#6 of 6)

29 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"There it is, Vinnie, there it is, there it is thereitis ..." "I SEE IT. Moron. Calm down, OK?" Leo quiets down, but from the look on his face, he's still fuming. The jeep slows down as it approaches the old barn that the surveillance guys back at the base say is the place the escapee fled to. What he was thinking though, is beyond either of these three, apparently. It's the dead of night - around 2:00 in the morning, though even if it was the middle of the day it's not very likely that there would be anyone around. There's not anything close to being a road for 20 miles, and with the growing season still on for a while yet, it's pretty quiet. The three men step out of the jeep, each reaching into their jacket and pulling out a pistol. They don't even bother with being quiet - who cares if the schmoe hears them? It's not like he has anywhere to run to, right? They step up to the closed door of the barn, and Vinnie pulls in the other two for a quick briefing. "Go in calm, he's got nowhere to go if he's in there, alright? There can't be many places to hide in there either. Got it?" Joe nods ... Leo doesn't respond. Vinnie smacks him in the shoulder. "GOT IT?" "Yeah, whatever," mumbles Leo. Against his better judgment, Vinnie moves to the door, pulls it open, and looks in. Everything seems fine to him. He steps in and motions for the other two to follow. Leo, however, seems to have other plans for the apprehension of the escapee. He charges in, shoving past Vinnie and Joe, who stagger for a moment, then run in after Leo, fearful of a trap. Before they reach him, something in the corner - it's HIM! - catches Leo's eye, and without thinking, he levels his pistol and shoots 3 rounds at the punk. Instantaneously, a huge explosion rocks the building. Flames erupt, covering the floor, the walls, the roof, a blaze that could be seen for at least 10 miles - if anyone was looking. Everyone in the barn is instantly killed in one way or the other, and will be burned beyond any recognition - visual or genetic - possible. The man that Gotti sent to supervise shakes his head, gets back in his jeep, and calls The Boss. *** "They WHAT!?!?" The force of the scream knocks small items off of a shelf, the flailing of Vito Gotti's arms sends papers flying off of the desk he was sitting at, his face turning more red than a tomato. Still, the bodyguards behind Vito don't flinch - not one inch. "They're all dead?" He's listening now, his breathing slowing down, though his face is still as red as it was before. "'Rico, you get ahold of our friend the Captain at the county fire house. You tell him to take care of this personally. He is to bring those gavones back to me so I can piss on each and every one of them. This doesn't get out, not to any news agency, not to anyone. You hear? Good." He hangs up the phone and shakes his head. "Mamma mia." *** He watched from about a half mile away, knowing his plan would work, knowing the big galoot would fall right in for it. He kneels there, head bowed, long enough to pay penance for disturbing the passed. Finishing, he stands up, crouched over somewhat, and moves slowly through the field, knowing he'll come to a road sometime as long as he keeps moving in the same direction. The fire will keep his pursuers busy for hours, and they'll think he's dead anyway. He lets out a breath, his mission completed. This part of it, at least. Another one started the moment the barn blew up. The one to get his life back.
