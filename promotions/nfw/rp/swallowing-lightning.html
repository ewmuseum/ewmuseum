Swallowing lightning

4 Jun 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene- A training facility, an obstacle course, to be exact. Roger and Scott Powell are running the course...explosions occur in areas they are running through and around....5 feet away fromthe climbing wall...3 feet in front of the crawl-under fence...and so on. They run into a warehouse, run through the cubicles and reach the center, where JT Tyler sits at a table, a botle of Gatorade in one hand, a stopwatch in another. They look at him wondering when he is going to stop the watch as it beats on, but then a team of Navy Seals burst into the room firing weapons drawn and firing at pop-up targets throughout the immidiate area. Unlike their first day experiencing this instace, they watch as the bullets rip through the targets, and the SEALS each yell "Clear" after the last shot is fired.) TYLER: (stopping the watch and disappointingly holding it up facing the brothers....you're ten seconds late....run the course again... ROGER: OH COME ON! TEN SECONDS?!!! OUR FIRST DAY WE TOOK WAY LONGER THAN THIS!! TYLER: You're first day I expected you to take that long. Run the course again... SCOTT: Come on JT, this is getting stupid... TYLER: Yeah, it is, you should have been here 5 minutes ago. Run the course again.... SCOTT: What if we quit now.... TYLER: Hey, if you can find your way home, then by all means go....but noone here will help you. ROGER: Yeah...we know..."honest effort"... TYLER: Look guys...I'm not stupid...I'm not gonna let you slide along just so you can quit and get your money....quit if you want to when we get back to the main-land....but until we do....you're here, you might as well use my generosity to your advantage... ROGER: Generosity? TYLER: Yeah...I could have these guys (points at the SEAL team, whose weapons are still drawn) chase you throughthe course... SCOTT: (Mutters) I don't see YOU running this course.... TYLER: Fair enough....I'll run the course with you...if you win, you'll get (a lot of money) no matter what you do.... SCOTT: What if YOU win.... TYLER: WHEN I win...I want you two to shut up and learnw hat I have to offer....(pulls off his warm-up jacket and runs out of the warehouse...after about 5 seconds he runs back in) You coming or what? SCOTT: This sucks! ROGER: Oh SHUT UP! Mr. "I see where he's going with this"! (Scene fades as the three run out of the warehouse)
