::The Odd Couple theme plays as Jacobs wheels his bag back to the NFW dressing room past several shirtless boys with signs saying the Beau Michaels Cheering Section:: BRANDON JACOBS: ::under his breath:: Good to see nothing has changed from last time. ::Jacobs wheels past the shower room and sees Nise Marx’s bags sitting on the floor near a locker:: BRANDON JACOBS: I’m finally back. ::walks over to the edge of the showers:: NISE JON, I just wanted to let you know I am here, my plane came in prematurely. NISE JON: I’ll be with you in a minute. I’m in the middle of a rinse and repeat. BRANDON JACOBS: Take your time. I have a book to read. ::Jacobs sits on the bench and takes out “The Bad Beginning” by Lemony Snicket:: BRANDON JACOBS: Poor kids. ::a naked soaking wet pixilated Nise Marx, in a wet white mask with a black bushy eyebrows and mustache, walks by on his way to grabbing a towel and wrapping it around his waist:: BRANDON JACOBS: ::entrenched in his book:: This is so tragic, yet I can’t advert my eyes. NISE JON: Stand up, let me see you. BRANDON JACOBS: How long has it been? ::Nise Marx walks over and hugs Jacobs:: NISE JON: It has been much too long. Welcome back. BRANDON JACOBS: I’m excited to see you too. *coughs* But we don’t have time for any of this. We have to start preparing for Beau Michaels. NISE JON: I’ve been studying up on Beau Michaels. He likes to play mind games with people. Well they won’t phase me! BRANDON JACOBS: That is the spirit Jon! NISE JON: I’ve worked too hard and too long to let some up and comer just walk into this fed through the backdoor and steal my spotlight. BRANDON JACOBS: From what I heard, Beau Michaels is a very versatile performer. He doesn’t limit himself to only one way of doing things. NISE JON: Neither do I. I recently I was a three way with a strapping British lad and an unorthodox roughen. I had to switch back and forth in order to get the job done before the could double team me and pound my ass. BRANDON JACOBS: You have a habit of finishing first in those three ways. NISE JON: I don’t know why, I come from the Castor Strife school of conditioning. I can go all night and then some. BRANDON JACOBS: I know you can, but you have to put all of that aside for now and concentrate on Beau. NISE JON: I’ll have to use my head to beat him… err Brandon, you don’t look so good? BRANDON JACOBS: It was that damn food on the plain. ::rubs his chest:: Damn indigestion. ::pulls a package of Rolaids out of his pocket:: NISE JON: ::jumps up in the air:: I’VE GOT IT!
