I Got The Power

21 May 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: JJ DeVille, on the porch of his condo with the DC skyline behind him. His hair's slicked back with his middle manager go-tee perfectly trimmed. And in his hand is one of the first model's of Impulse's mask. JJ stares at the mask, smirking.) JJ: "I told you, Knox. I told you that you were nothing more than a commodity pushed down our throats. I told you that you were a mass marketed product pushed to be the hero we all needed but never knew we wanted. I told you, Impulse, that I saw right through you. I told you and I told the world that I knew you weren't anywhere near as good as you've been billed. I told you and I told everyone else that you are a FRAUD." (JJ looks at the mask and purses his lips.) JJ: "And, once again, the Inconvenient Truths I told about you were proven as fact. I beat you, Impulse. I beat you FAIR AND SQUARE. I beat you despite *YOUR* attempts to hoodwink me. I beat you despite *YOUR* attempts to have Calico Rose -- sweet, lovable Rosie -- interfere in our match. I OVERCAME THE ODDS, KNOX THAT HAVE ALWAYS BEEN TILTED IN YOUR FAVOR... and *I* won. Now, I know the NFW Spin Doctors are going to maintain how I 'pulled your trunks' to assure my victory. But, as replays will no doubt show, my handful of your trunks was incidental at best and NOT A FACTOR in my victory." (JJ laughs.) JJ: "Randall Knox, I'm willing right now to admit that THAT IS INDEED YOUR REAL NAME. And why am I willing to say just that? Because, Impulse... I ruined your name. I took your name and threw it in the garbage right where it always belong." (JJ takes the mask and drops it over the railing.) JJ: "Now, I'm sure the condo association will fine me for littering -- oh, wait, that's right, I RUN THE CONDO ASSOCIATION HERE. WHAT I SAY GOES! I'LL PUT A TAX LIEN ON ANYONE WHO DOESN'T HAVE THE RIGHT COLOR TULIPS IN THEIR GARDEN BOX! Just as I'll do WHATEVER I WANT IN NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING! THE PROMOTION THAT I OWN!" (JJ regains his composure and purses his lips.) JJ: "The one thing you morons held against me was that I couldn't wrestle. That I hid behind my mastery of paperwork and politics. Well, heh -- GUESS WHAT? I PROVED YOU ALL WRONG! I pinned the golden child. Not only do I sign off on your direct deposit forms... but now, when those get delayed for three months because of an undisclosed 'technical error' with ADP... THERE'S NOT ONE THING YOU CAN DO ABOUT IT! BECAUSE I WILL DEFEAT EVERY SINGLE PERSON IN NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING! I AM THE KING OF THIS PROMOTION! I AM ITS DESPOT! I AM YOUR RULER AND YOUR MASTER! I DEFEATED THE GOLDEN CHILD AND BECAME A GOLDEN GOD!" (JJ chuckles.) JJ: "This league is under my boot. You're all my puppets. I was a relic of the past just a few months ago. I was a poor man who wanted to be rich. Then I became a rich man who wanted to become king. And now I'm a king who won't be satisfied until he rules everything. Well, guess what? I AM ALMOST FULLY SATISFIED! HAHAH! HAHAHAHAH! BWAAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHA" (JJ leans back in his maniacal evil chuckle and nearly falls over the rail. FTB.)
