We're gonna change the way we act...

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene- A split screen of two hotel rooms, the lights are off and the occupants are asleep, two in one room, and one in the other. A dark figure is seen coming out of the shadows in the two occupant room, slowly wakes up one of the occupants and leads them out of the room. CUTTO- Five minutes later, as two groups, approximately 6 each, break into the rooms, grab and tie up the remaining occupants of each room (two men total), blindfold them, and drag them out.) (CUTTO- Another dark room, the two prisoners<for lack of a better term> and dropped onto two chairs, they are unmuffled as they start screaming out obsenities. Their blindfolds are taken off revealing them to be the Powell brothers. Door opens behind them, they can’t turn around to see who it is, but it is clear to the camera that it’s JT Tyler. ) ROGER: What the (censored) is going on here? ! TYLER: Shaddap! If you ladies are done moaning we can get started! SCOTT: TYLER? YOU’RE BEHIND THIS! UNTIE US NOW YOU FREAK! ROGER: WHERE’S MY WIFE YOU SLIMEBALL! TYLER: Your wife is with mine, she’s still on vacation…. SCOTT: WHAT KIND OF (censored) IS THIS? TYLER: Stop your complaining you little sissy-marie. Your training starts now. (Four men split up and untie the brothers, who immidiatelt jump up and lunge towards Tyler, who side-steps them both) ROGER: IF YOU EVER COME NEAR ME OR MY WIFE AGAIN, I WILL KILL YOU! SCOTT: Why, wait? I’LL KILL HIM NOW! (Lunges again, Tyler side-stepds him again) TYLER: We can go on with this all day, but you ARE going to get some training in before the day is done. ROGER: We’re outta here. TYLER: We had a deal…. SCOTT: (censored) YOUR DEAL! TYLER: Well, obviously you feel the need to leave, fine, walk out, but I’ll tell you right now, you were blindfolded for a reason. You don’t have a clue as to where you are. For all you know, you could still be in the hotel in some convention room, but you ALSO could be anywhere in the world. ROGER: I’m willing to take that chance…. (Walks towards the front door, opens it, and stops, the camera zooms in on the door, revealing water. Roger closes the door.)…You brought us on a (censored) BOAT? TYLER: Boy, nothing gets past you. SCOTT: I’m not playing your game, this is crap, I don’t give a rat’s (censored) about your money. TYLER: fine, but I guarantee you that all of this film is being put on TV and if Sullivan has been the (censored) to you that you say he is, he’s going to put an “Anti-Powell” spin on this. SCOTT: Who (censored) cares! TYLER: If you guys are still all about respect, then how much do you think you’ll get if you are played out to be a couple of (censored)? ROGER: Whaaaat? TYLER: All I’m saying is, that I know you guys want to be like “the toughest”, and “the meanest”, and all that right? ROGER: We don’t care what people thinkof us. TYLER: Now DON’T give me THAT crap, or do I have to mention your little match with Ricky Zane? OR you trying to quit because you kept losing, OR… SCOTT: (interrupting) OK, you made your point, we want to be the best, fine, great wonderful. What does this have to do with you KIDNAPPING US? TYLER: To make sure you don’t just up and quit on me, now, you have to swim back to shore. ROGER: WHAT THE (censored) ARE YOU ON? TYLER: Guys…. (The four men grab the Powell brothers and take them out on the deck of the boat. They then proceed to throw the brothers overboard. Tyler walks out on deck with a couple of lifevests) TYLER: Here guys…(tosses them in the water next to the brothers)…don’t want you to drown on your first day of training….now just follow the path we take and you’ll get to shore (Signals for the boat captain who has just started up the engines to slowly pull away) ROGER: WHEN WE GET TO SHORE WE ARE GONNA BEAT YOUR (censored)! TYLER: Not if you don’t make it to shore by lunchtime, you’ll have to run laps! See you soon boys! (The boat speeds off, leaving the brothers alone in the middle of the body of water) ROGER: So what do we do? SCOTT: What else can we do, we swim. ROGER: Tyler? SCOTT: We’ll deal with him when we get there. (The Powell brothers put on their vests, and start swimming, the camera notices a bubble trail behind them, and the scene fades out)
