A Ruined Man

5 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: A smirking JJ DeVille, greasy ponytail, wearing a Jos. A Bank brown blazer over a white button down dress shirt as he stands on the balcony of his Arlington, VA condo suite, overlooking Washington, DC.) JJ: "Don't worry. We... I... haven't gone anywhere. The Windham Clan is STILL here. The Windham Clan STILL owns half of this dump. And that means I, being the day-to-day manager of The Windham Clan's affairs, control some aspect of your life. ME! The Beauracratic Nightmare. The Locker Room Cancer. I am the biggest threat in this promotion's HISTORY... and it's not because I may win a title. It's not because I can beat everyone up. It's because I can make your life so (BLEEEEP) miserable by making you do things you don't want to do. Getting to the arena nice and early so you can fill out 4,000 pages of TPS reports? Travelling halfway around the globe to take a drug screen? Watching the catering staff pack up and leave because I fired them? I have done that and so much more." (JJ purses his lips.) JJ: "And believe me, I'm not done with this league yet. But, right now, one so-called man has my attention. Because even though he doesn't know it... and although he won't ever admit it... I have taken this person any placed him under my boot. I have tormented his soul... and now I am about to break his will. (JJ holds up an old Impulse mask.) JJ: "Impulse, the last time we stood in the ring together, something interesting happened. Something that I KNEW WOULD HAPPEN. You had a chair in your hand, Knox. And you had a chance to destroy me. But you didn't. Because, Impulse, of your 'rules of engagement.' All along, I've said these rules were nothing more than an excuse. A way for you to fall asleep at night confident that you have something that makes you 'better' than the rest of this industry. But, Impulse, while your asleep at night tucked in... your girlfriend Rosie has an IV drip in her arm courtesy of me and Bobby Jack. How many surgeries has she needed, Randall? How is SHE sleeping at night, Randall? Not very well. And, as she's about to have yet another morphine dream, I'm sure the last thoughts in her mind are how she's SO proud of you. How you had a chance to rid the world of the man who put her in the spot she's in... and you didn't do that so you can be oh-so-pleased with your moral code. I mean, she may never walk again as far as I know... and, Impulse, you put YOUR smug, condescending little need for moral superiority over her desire for revenge." (JJ looks at the mask and chuckles.) JJ: "But what I loved the most, Knox, was the feint sound I heard from the back when you didn't connect with that chair... the feint sound in the upper deck... was the chant of 'Impulse Sucks.' Oh, I know that Kerry and Lamont and the rest of the NFW's marketing team which has been behind your back -- 'Me Against the World My' my ass -- casually ignored that and hyped you up to be the hero they so want to sell you as. But the die hards, Impulse... their the people who have to put up with jerk-offs like me every day of their lives. They want to live vicariously through someone. And you, Knox? YOU FAILED THEM. And those boos are just getting louder, Knox. The message boards are heating up, Impulse. Because the fans who you've been pushed to... they have lost ALL respect for you." (JJ again stares at the mask.) JJ: "Just as all the boys in the back did a LONG time ago. Remember when you were my slave, Impulse? How you 'sucked it up' so they could get paid? What, these guys don't get paid a lot of money everywhere else? They saw you KNUCKLE UNDER my demands as opposed to standing up for yourself. You did what I told you to do... and they all saw it and LAUGHED BEHIND YOUR BACK. And now? Now that you chose a sense of moral superiority instead of the sense of justice that real men everywhere have prided themselves on for generations? Everyone knows it's now FREE GAME on you for what's left of your pathetic career. Because, Knox, you won't do a damn thing about it when a man destroys the woman you love." (JJ laughs.) JJ: "No wonder why the Jack Bryant shirts are selling and yours are on the discount rack." (JJ once again stares at the mask.) JJ: "But now, Knox... now comes the MASTER STROKE of my plot to ruin your life." (JJ holds the mask up high.) JJ: "You've bragged since time immortal about how you're the best wrestler in the world and came up with excuse after excuse as to why you didn't actually seem to WIN any matches. And everyone alive has dismissed me as some office lackey on a petty power trip with no talent to back it up. Our matchup is inevitable, Impulse. And I'll tell you what." (JJ laughs at the mask.) JJ: "Here's the gimmick to the match. There is no gimmick. One on one, 60 minute time limit. The referee will NOT be Pee Wee Troutman nor anyone else I've hand picked. Because, Knox... while I stewed away leading jazzercise classes for fat undergrads in Raleigh, I didn't just see through your dumb rules of engagement. I saw through your boasts of being the best wrestler in the world. Because, Impulse... you're not. And, Impulse... I also know for a fact that you are NOT AS GOOD AS ME either." (JJ starts to put the mask over his head.) JJ: "And after I pin you or make you tap out... you and everyone else are going to realize that I've owned you like this toy mask from day one." (JJ laughs as we FTB.) [the thread's next post — 5 Mar 2012] FADEIN. On my face. I'm outside, in the middle of Times Square. People are pointing at me - they recognize me. So far, nobody has said a word, probably because an actual appearance downtown for me is like the Rare White Elk sighting. Anyways.... "JJ. Jordan Jessica DeVille." "Challenge accepted. Name the date." And for the first time in a long time. I mean... a really long time... I smiled. FADE. [the thread's next post — 8 Mar 2012] (CUT TO: Dorchester Stratton, his hair combed to Don Draper-style perfection and wearing a crisp white dress shirt with red/blue Republican power tie, stands in front of his cherry red Convertible BMW Z-Series. Next to him, dressed in her Sunday best, smiling as she texts on her Blackberry, is Veronica Abrams Rumsfeld.) DORCHESTER: "Some people say I have what you would call 'rabbit ears.' That I hear too many things and LET them bother me. I like to say that I hear what people say about me... and I let it FUEL me. And, Randall Knox, a few weeks back you mentioned my name... disparagingly." (Veronica looks up.) VERONICA: "Tsk. Tsk. Tsk." (She looks back down.) DORCHESTER: "You mentioned something about my career not headed where I've intended it to go. Believe me, Knox -- anytime I see that moron film school dropout walking around with that gold... it makes me sick to my stomach. Do you know why? Because, for all intents and purposes, that title SHOULD BE MINE. But..." (Dorchester shakes his head and looks down.) VERONICA: "Impulse, we in The Dynasty do not believe in MAKING EXCUSES. Not like you do, with your oh-so-righteous 'Code of Honor' or whatever you call it (eye roll). And that's why you have not heard from Dorchester, myself or Calvin Carlton about the very serious injury he has been dealing with for the past several months." (Dorchester's head remains down as he 'wipes' away a tear.) VERONICA: "Now, we will not disclose any details as to the extent of the injury or where it occurred, except to say that..." (Dorchester laughs.) "... except to say that it wasn't a miscarriage." (Dorchester perks back up.) DORCHESTER: "Now, Impulse, Randall, whatever... I have had no personal problems with you until now, aside from the problem that everyone has with you, and that's you're an obnoxious little moron who talks a big game but isn't really all that good at being a professional wrestler. I haven't had a reason to kick your skull off your neck yet. I mean, I've wanted to... but I haven't had a REASON to, and I'm not a man who does things without reasons. But now? (Dorchester rubs his hands together.) You gave me a reason to destroy you." (Veronica laughs.) VERONICA: "And the best part about it is -- history has shown that we'll do ANYTHING to crush who stands in our path. And you? The only thing you'll do is..." (She laughs.) DORCHESTER: "The only thing you'll do is NOT hit me with a chair when I urinate on the grave of your dead, unborn child, who would probably have been mentally retarded anyways." VERONICA: "Or maybe something will happen at the hospital with poor, dear Rosie..." DORCHESTER: "Maybe there will be an accident. Because, after all... ACCIDENTS CAN HAPPEN TO ANYONE!" (They laugh. FTB.)
