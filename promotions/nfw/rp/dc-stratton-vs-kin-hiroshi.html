DC Stratton vs. Kin Hiroshi

11 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STIPULATION: Japanese Exploding Deathmatch [the thread's next post — 14 Feb 2004] (Scene- Neil Riddick and DC Stratton stand in front of a video wall. Instead of highlights of his moves, it now exploits an NFW North montage of the wrestlers on the roster appears and then explodes...out expands "The EXPLOSIVE Inhuman Highlight Reel DC Stratton". This process continues throughout the micspot. Stratton stands head down, hair in his face. Neil Riddick stands at the opposit end, hands clasped, he is smiling ever so evily.) RIDDICK: (The sarcasm is evident)Oh...Poor DC....Riddick doesn't really like him....when will he break away so us fans can like him? HA! Did you think for a moment that EVERYTHING we (points to DC and back to himself) have done since coming to this "burgh" has not been 100% planned? This was HIS IDEA! (Laughs hystericallY) We counted on you fools in the locker room, and you idiots out in tv land feeling sad for him! All the while I told you what would happen, and you wouldn't listen so that the surprise would be THAT MUCH BIGGER! (Laughs even more) STRATTON: Morons! RIDDICK: What's better? All of this, "I want respect" crap we've been spewing out couldn't have worked better as well?! (Laughs) People don't seem to believe that we are going to win this, and that we are going to destroy that pathetic copy of the Ultra-title. It means nothing to us....or am I wrong? Do you feel the sense of "honor"....and "tradition" DC? (The video wall changes to a picture of the Ultra-title. Stratton looks up, almost as if he has changed his mind. His eyes get a glaze as if he is day-dreaming, he slowly reaches for it...grins uncharacteristically and then spits on the screen. As Riddick continiues to laugh, the montage reappear) STRATTON: Kin Hiroshi, you are the lucky one, you're going to get hurt first.... RIDDICK: I know what you fans are thinking about...."What does he mean by that?" This man has laid low for the first half of this "season", he has played by the rules to watch what everyone else does. How did you feel about that... (Stratton makes puking sound) RIDDICK: What you've seen in recent days is only the beginning, Hiroshi, you are the lucky one. You don't have to worry about what Stratton here, will do when he gets creative. Right now he is wondering how he is going to top his performance in Las Vegas, so he is not feeling very creative right now. Lucky you. (Riddick walks off camera a Stratton looks up) Stratton: (winking) Boom! (Stratton walks off camera as footage from the Wargames match plays in slow motion, from the explosions to the cage falling on everyone) [the thread's next post — 18 Feb 2004] * Time heals all wounds. At least, that's what they say. Kin Hiroshi wouldn't know. His wounds are far too deep. A betrayal in the past has dwelled in the back of his mind for years. Now, an opportunity for redemption has reared it's head.... * * Kin Hiroshi sits patiently. A NFW camera crew positions lights, cameras, and chairs for an interview with the Japanese Thunder. * KIN HIROSHI: How much longer is this going to take? I got places to be, you know? CREWMAN: Not much longer Mr. Hiroshi. I just need to check with the production truck that everything is a-go. KIN HIROSHI: Good. Good. * Hiroshi stands, brushes off his Armani suit, and walks slowly over to one of the 'interview chairs.' He unbuttons his jacket, and lowers himself into the uncomfortable chair. A young woman takes the seat next to Hiroshi, and looks over some cue cards. * WOMAN: Mr. Hiroshi, sorry this has taken so long to get rolling, but I won't take up too much of your time. I just have a few questions about your involvement with NFW, alright? KIN HIROSHI: Sure thing. * The camera crew takes position, and a red light comes up on the cameras. * WOMAN: We're here with wrestling superstar, and self-made millionaire, Kin Hiroshi. A man that has seen a lot in his day, and is at the peak of his game. Yet, the only thing out of his grasp is a World Championship. You HAVE held cruiserweight titles, television titles, intercontinental titles, and even, what you claim to be one of the crowning moments in your career, the CSWA Greensboro Championship... KIN HIROSHI: Uh, yeah. You know, it's always something to go out there and give the best damn show every single night. It's even greater to go out there, give your all, and then be recognized by the bookers for your talent. You're right though, a World Championship has always been just out of my reach. The closest I've come was a title shot in GXW, but I dropped the ball big time. *chuckling* But that's not to say that I won't be a world champion sometime soon... WOMAN: Turning towards your recent stint in NFW, how confident are you that you still have a shot at the Ultratitle? KIN HIROSHI: Last I checked I was only 20 points behind the Division A leader for NFW North, and only 10 points back from that piece of crap Dan Ryan and my next opponent DC Stratton. 10 and 20 points isn't a whole lot to make up. I'm more than confident that the Ultratitle is well within my reach. Laying low for the first half of the season, I feel was a smart move, and now that we are on the down hill slope, it's time to go balls to the wall, and show what Kin Hiroshi is made of. WOMAN: Speaking of Stratton and confidence.... KIN HIROSHI: *grinning* I knew this would be brought up. Stratton has proved himself to be a competitor in NFW. But he's a little too cocky for a man that doesn't have a strong crowd support. It doesn't matter though, I've got more than a bag of tricks to keep Stratton grounded. It's a little funny, but for once I'm considered the "big man" in a match at 6'1", 230 lbs. WOMAN: Do you feel your bag of tricks will be enough to keep Stratton grounded, AND keep his manager Riddick at bay long enough to pull out the win? KIN HIROSHI: I'm not going to lie, I'm going in the underdog at 10 points behind Stratton. So far, his tactics with Riddick have worked to push him into a tie for the number two spot in North's Division A. But, like I said, I've held back, and it's given me the advantage of being able to scout out my opponents. WOMAN: Last question, Mr. Hiroshi. KIN HIROSHI: Already? WOMAN: *smiling* I told you it would go fast. When you were starting out in the wrestling leagues in the United States, you were priveleged enough to be signed in EWI by Erik Zieba... KIN HIROSHI: Correct. WOMAN: You played a pivotal role by aligning yourself with the Super Sports Network shortly before the formation of GXW. * Hiroshi begins to squint his eyes at the interviewer, and his face begins to turn red. * WOMAN: The man who brought you into the SSN was none other than Eddy Love, the man who has recently made his place known NFW. Any comments about your former mentor? * Kin stands from his seat, and pulls of his microphone and tosses it on the floor. * KIN HIROSHI: This is a topic you don't want to touch, interview OVER. * Hiroshi turns from the mock-set, and marches out the door... * **FADE TO GOLD** [the thread's next post — 18 Feb 2004] (Scene- Riddick and Stratton are standing in front of the video wall again, Riddick wastes no time.) RIDDICK: OH...REAL original there Kinny..."holding back first half of the season". Nice to know you can come up with something original. What's next? Your turning into an "INHuman Highlight reel?" or better yet, "Explosive"? You're pathetic, 'cause we have 'plenty in our bag of tricks to keep you grounded". What is the matter with wrestlers these days? In wrestlings heyday, when being a wrestler actually MEANT something, you could actually burn out a microphone and people would be like "wow". Back when wrestling meant anything at all, a wrestler of this man's caliber(points to Stratton) could actually wrestle a match with honor against an honorable opponent. We didn't have to worry about bomb's, or plans for destroying a trophy...or... STRATTON: Uhhh Riddick, we're doing all of that now, the guy you're talking about is us.... RIDDICK: (grining evily) oh yeah!Hiroshi, all the money in the world doesn't mean squat to either of us...well it does to me!, but him on the other hand, Well anyway, you're all up about your bag of tricks....BAG? WE HAVE A WAREHOUSE! You don't stand a chace and you dn't even know it! Just get to the match, as for who wins...we'll flip ya for it!(winks and leaves) STRATTON: BOOM! (Stratton leaves behind a package in cardboard, a fuse is lit and heads towards the package as the scane fades out) [the thread's next post — 19 Feb 2004] * Kin Hiroshi leans back into the headrest, and closes his eyes. Finally, after a long flight, the ride was over. Next stop: DC Stratton. At the end of the terminal a man waits for Hiroshi, it's the driver. The luggage secure in the trunk of the limo, the driver opens the door for the weary Hiroshi. * DRIVER: Sir, there was a video waiting for you from NFW. * He hands a curious Hiroshi a tape. * KIN HIROSHI: Thanks. Let's get to the hotel... * The driver nods, and closes the door behind Kin. After fondling the cassette for a few moments, Hiroshi pops it into the VCR and turns the TV on. He watches the "precious moments" between Stratton and Riddick, and laughs. A handheld camcorder sits in the seat opposite him, and Hiroshi turns it on, and begins recording himself. * KIN HIROSHI: Listen you two, I agree. Back when wrestling did mean anything, I'm sure Stratton put on a good show. But, that was YEARS ago. Welcome to the year 2004, where I don't give a s**t's giggle about what you can do. It's all about what I can do, not you. BOOM? Is that the best you can do? You think you're going to blow ME up? I'm going to let you in on a little secret...I have the advantage here. Why? It's a Japanese Exploding Deathmatch. Here, I'll give you a minute to think about it. * Hiroshi looks down at his watch as he counts the seconds by. * KIN HIROSHI: It's because I'm Japanese, you half-wits. Sure, try and blow me up? A Japanese hasn't been blown up since the Enola Gay flew over our little island, and we aren't about to let another one of us burn. If anyone is going to lose a limb in this match, it isn't me, and it's not Stratton. Riddick, you try anything, and you're buddy Stratton will be callin' you "stubby" for a long long time. Stratton, you best keep your boyfriend in check, otherwise I'll ***** slap him back up your ass, and then you ain't climbing no turnbuckles. Now, if you two will excuse me, I have to catch up on business concerning a southern dandy. * Hiroshi turns the camcorder off, and settles into his seat. * **FADE TO GOLD**
