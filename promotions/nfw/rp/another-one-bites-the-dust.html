Another One Bites the Dust

3 Jun 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN to a posh hotel room. Shawn Matthews is kicking back, drinking a beer on a lush recliner. He looks into the camera...) SHAWN MATTHEWS: Well, Crash has finally come and gone... and look who's sitting here, nice and comfortable. LOOK, who is still in the NFW. LOOK, who's not in some hospital bed. Yeah, Crash was quite a show alright. I gave Edmunds the SHOCKER, and then it's PEACE OUT, CUB SCOUT! Edmunds is history, because I beat him so badly that he dare not show his face anymore in an NFW ring. But that's not what I'm here to talk about. Wildstar, you came out to the ring and you stuck your nose into MY... BUSINESS! You wanted to see the SPECTACLE known as SHOWTIME SHAWN MATTHEWS, UP CLOSE... and PERSONAL. And you also felt fit to actually have your slutbag wife and punk kid out there to watch. Did you honestly think that I would not retaliate? That I would just let you CRUSH me. Well guess what, that ain't the case. I didn't come here as some joke. I didn't come here to JOB to guys like Sean Edmunds. I came here to be RISE... STRAIGHT TO THE TOP! I've had enough of this little garbage with you, your wife, and kid. If you want to play house and be Mr. Mom, then you go home and do it. That garbage isn't gonna fly anymore. It's 2002, and it's time for SHOWTIME to SHINE! And Wildstar, just like I sent Sean Edmunds RUNNING from the NFW, I'm going to do the SAME... TO YOU! It's time you pack up your boots and your FIVE STAR BULLSH(BLEEP), and head home. The fans are bored with your TIRED ACT. It's my time to shine, and there's not a damn thing that's going to help you... STOP ME! (The mystery man who attacked Wildstar at Crash walks into the room. His dripping hair hangs over his eyes...) SHAWN MATTHEWS: Cuz with an insurance policy like this guy, there's no way in HELL... that you're going to cash in. Wildstar, whether it be at IMPACT, or all the way down the road at FUTURESHOCK, your time is up... because MY TIME HAS COME! All I've got to say is, BE SMART! Don't let Karen and Kyle see, CLOSE-UP... the PAIN and the PUNISHMENT that I'm going to dish out to you the next time that we butt heads. ARE YOU INTO IT?! (FADEOUT, as Matthews laughs and he throws a beer to the mystery man..)
