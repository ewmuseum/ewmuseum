I Still Have The Power

26 Jun 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: The DC skyline. The camera pans and shows JJ DeVille on his balcony. His hair is slicked back into a ponytail, with a neatly trimmed go-tee. He also has added something new to his wardrobe -- one diamond earring in both ears. He's wearing a shirt that reads "I'M NOT SATISFIED" that's tucked into his khaki dress slacks.) JJ: "I'm a very important man. In fact, I'm probably THE MOST IMPORTANT MAN IN PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING these days. And that means I'm a BUSY MAN without a lot of downtime... unlike you sloths at home, who are content to be miserable at your 9-to-5 jobs to come home to a fat wife, retarded children and sit on the couch and watch The Real Housewives of Gordon Ramsey. If you had the KAH-HOO-NEEES that I do -- if you had the strength and intelligence to be a true Leader of Men who worked his way up from the mailroom to the penthouse, a man unfairly BLACKBALLED from professional wrestling who became its ultimate POWER PLAYER -- then you wouldn't have a lot of time to spare. And I certainly don't have a lot of time, which is why it took me a little while to review my awesome performance at Tampa... and to also see Impulse's remakrs and Rosie's actions." (JJ purses his lips.) JJ: "Knox, our battle is over. I beat you in the middle of the ring. FAIR AND SQUARE. Did I need outside interference? The assistance of Pee Wee Troutman, the greatest and most objective referee professional wrestling has ever known? No I did not. All I needed... (JJ smirks) was my SUPERIOR TALENT. I beat you at your own game, Knox. A clean victory, right in the middle of the ring." (JJ's face turns a little red.) JJ: "But do you just come out on national television to admit that, Mr. Honorable? Mr. High and Mighty? MISTER INTEGRITY? No, instead it you did exactly what I said you've done your entire career. YOU MADE EXCUSES. You tried to tell the world as to what you accomplished, and how The Clan is falling apart at the seams. YOU COULDN'T JUST ADMIT THAT I BEAT YOU, HUH, KNOX? WHY CAN'T YOU AT LEAST GIVE ME THAT? WHY CAN'T EVERYONE JUST SEE THAT I AM GOOD IN THE RING? WHY AM I THE ONE BLACKBALLED WHEN PEOPLE LIKE YOU GET A SHOT? JUST ADMIT THAT I BEAT YOU AND MOVE ON, RANDALL. JUST ADMIT THAT ALREADY! DON'T IGNORE ME! NO ONE IGNORES JJ DEVILLE! I'M IMPORTANT! I'M SOMEBODY--" (JJ closes his eyes and takes a deep breath. He then straightens out his T-shirt and regains his composure.) JJ: "I'm somebody... who is a WINNER, Knox. And do you know what winners get? They get the LAST WORD. I won. You lost. End of story. And as far as your dumb girlfriend's little 'Cupcake Revolution?' Might I remind you and she that there are SEVERAL permits required to distribute food items in a public setting? And that she qualifies as a vendor which needs several licenses. And, being illiterate, I'm sure she doesn't have any. I can crush this little Cupcake Revolution just like this!" (JJ snaps his fingers.) "But guess what? I'm not going to do that. And do you know why?" (JJ purses his lips and then smirks.) JJ: "Because she dates... a complete and total failure of a man. A man who I proved I'm better than. And who am I? Well I was a poor man who wanted to be rich... a rich man who wanted to be king... and now I'm the king and I won't be satisfied until I RULE EVERYTHING. And I'm FAR from satisfied!" (JJ chuckles.
