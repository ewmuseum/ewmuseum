The return...

16 Apr 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN to a redneck bar in Lock Haven, PA. The camera zooms in on a pool table... playing a game are Scotty Michaels, Kurt Taylor, Jett Broadway, and the artist formerly known as "Amish" Amos. They chat, as they play the game...) SCOTTY MICHAELS: Damn, I can't believe Shawn Matthews got back into the NFW. I never thought I'd see the day.... I figured he'd be in the Betty Ford clinic for the rest of his life, that punk. I can't believe he turned his back on all of us the way that he did... the way that he manipulated us the way he did. JETT BROADWAY: Yeah man. I think we should go to the next NFW event and just kick his butt. It's a bunch of garbage that he's working his way back into the picture. I have more skill in my pinkie than that jacka(bleep) does in his whole body. I mean hell, he made poor Jerome over there play an Amish guy. When's the last time you saw a black Amish person? JEROME HENDERSON: Yeah, sucka... I mean, they had me going out there in the fields and churning butter and whatnot for promos, and that's all because the man held me down. And that man, my friend is Shawn Matthews. He was so far up the backsides of those guys that ran the FWC. I think it's time that we teach him a lesson in what ghetto rollin' is all about. KURT TAYLOR: Yeah, and that son of a (bleep) was too ashamed to admit that I was his brother. He wanted the spotlight, and all he was was a little punk. He would smoke me up before matches so I looked like the fool. Not that I didn't enjoy it, but he wouldn't inhale... I didn't figure this out until one time when I smoked myself sober. Well anyway, he's the reason I never had any kind of career in the NFW... (Finally, Shawn Matthews walks into the bar... chugs a beer, and walks over to the pool table.... the four men get silent) SHAWN MATTHEWS: Hey guys, what's going on? Haven't seen ya'll in a long time? Did you see how I kicked (bleep) at that last house show? I know you're all my biggest fans, and I think it'd be great if you joined me in NFW.... (OUT of nowhere jumps a man with wet hair covering his face. He's about 6'6', 285 pounds.... he jacks a pool cue over the back of Kurt Taylor, knocking him out. He picks up a forty and nails Henderson over the head with it. Michaels and Broadway start to take an advantage, but this powerhouse bounces their heads off the pool table. He picks up Broadway and gives him a jackhammer suplex on top of the pool table.... finally, the violence ends, and the man who just wrecked shop stands there with his head down, as Matthews begins laughing...) SHAWN MATTHEWS: Did you losers actually think that I'd get you a spot in the NFW? You can all kiss my white (bleep)! I can't believe you were actually STUPID enough to think that I cared about any of you. There's only one man who I support, and he stands right here... (The mystery man rips off his shirt to reveal nothing but pure muscle..) Wildstar, you thought that you had it in for you before? NFW, you thought you had it in for you with the news of Shawn Matthews' return? Well guess again, because this man (pats him hard on the chest).... has arrived. He is a one man wrecking machine, and he will be the man to bring the NFW to a new level... to lead them for years to come. He will be an icon and yes, he will be a legend.... but most importantly, he will be the world heavyweight champion. It may not be tomorrow, it may not be next month.... but mark my words, it will happen. And mark my words, no one is ever going to be the same again. (FADEOUT...) [the thread's next post — 16 Apr 2002] (CUT TO: WildStar, in his singlet, on his couch, accompianied by his wife and Kyle, The Little Ninja.) WILDSTAR: Y'know, Matthews... I gotta hand it to you. You would think that someone who has been scraping the gutters of humanity for the past five years or so would be a little modest, a little toned down. Well now we can all see why your mouth is bigger than ever... I mean, that muscle-man you got watching your back must reaaaaaaaaly give it you good. (Miss Karen smacks WildStar's knee in disgust.) I'm sorry, that was wrong... I shouldn't have said that in front of Kyle here. Anyways, Showtime, that guy's probably on so many steroids that he doesn't even have genitals anymore. (Miss Karen again smacks WildStar's knee.) I'm sorry... I shouldn't have said that in front of Kyle here. Again, Matthews... let me reiterate what I said earlier. I'm this sport's only (WildStar holds Kyle up and both do the FIVE STAR HAND GESTURE) FIVE STAR WRESTLER. I'm not out here to let no-talent chumps like you... or big, steroid muscle-men freaks... run the NFW. I'm out here to take this league and this sport to new heights, greater than anything it has ever been. This sport is the sport of kings and should be practiced by men of honor and courage... not a bunch of clowns who hang out at biker bars. And sooner or later, Showtime... you're going to find out first-hand that your star doesn't shine as bright as the one that is... (WildStar and Kyle once again do the FIVE STAR HAND GESTURE) FIVE STARS. (FTB) [the thread's next post — 16 Apr 2002] SHAWN MATTHEWS: Wildstar, you're the one talking trash on me... and yet you're the one who chills in your home wearing your singlet? Give me a break. Your time has passed, buddy.... the NFW is sick and tired of your act. Let me make one thing perfectly clear also, jacko(bleep). I'm not the one getting it in the (bleep) around here. I can have any lady that I want to, while you sit at home with that sad excuse for a wife. If I felt like wasting my time, I bet I could have her also.... but why would I want to? You haven't gotten laid since that kid was conceived. Oh, that's right... word on the street is that he's of the mailman's sperm. But there's no need for low blows here. We all know that your wife wants me, anyway.... not that I'd spend my time on that homely bi(bleep). But you know what? I think there does need to be some sort of stipulation. There needs to be some kind of incentive for me to kick your (bleep), other than for pure enjoyment. And you know what? Since it will be your last match, I'll let you pick it. Just remember, old-timer... that it's my day, and that I AM... ALL.... THERE.... (BLEEP)in'... IS!!
