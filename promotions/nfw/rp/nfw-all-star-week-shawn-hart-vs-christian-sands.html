NFW All-Star Week: Shawn Hart vs. Christian Sands

14 Jan 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADE IN: The bedroom floor of some hotel suite or another. Various articles of clothing were strewn about the floor and a pair of leather pants draped into the camera's view from one of the bedposts above. Emanating from somewhere off camera, moans and cries of pleasure are heard.) Hart: "Mmmmmmmmm, baby... that's how I like it... THAT'S how I like it! (Slowly, the camera began to follow a path along messy floor, seemingly searching for the source of the wailing.) Hart: "Oh god, mmmmmmm....OH! OH! There's the spot!! Yes... YESSSSSS!!!!" (Surprisingly, the camera passed an empty bed by and continued on to the bathroom, where a pair of feet were seen.) Hart: "Ooooooooohh!! Ahhhhhhhh!! GOOOOOOOOOOOOD!!!" (The camera finally panned upward, revealing the Phenom standing before the bathroom mirror with towels wrapped around both his waist and his head. In his ear is a q-tip, and on that q-tip is his right hand. With a smile, he looked into the camera.) Hart: "Whew! That had to be the best EARGASM I've ever had!!!" (Hart shook his head with a laugh, then closed the door on the camera, shutting it out of view. Almost instantaneously, the door is re-opened and the Phenom emerged, fully clothed with a red latex jump suit.) Hart: "Sorry kids, you snuck up on me a bit early there... still had to work my threads... (The Phenom happened upon a night stand just outside the bathroom, next to the bed, and on it found a top hat as red as his pants and vest. He nabbed the hat with a grin on his face, dropped it atop his head, then sat upon the silky sheets of his unmade bed, peering into the camera.) Hart: "NOW, as you know... we've reached the mid-point of the NFW's first season of Ultratitle madness, culminating in an all-star mega-event that just so happens to feature yours truly... goin' toe-to-toe with one Christian Sands! Of course, this lil' bout will undoubtedly hold untold amounts of jiggy, thrilling the masses and propelling the Phenom to new heights in the New Frontier, but is that enough? Could I ever possibly rest on the fact that I'm gonna have a good match against an even better opponent?" (Hart chuckled to himself, sarcastically I might add, then shook his head.) Hart: "Hell nah, brah!! I mean, AFTER ALL... this IS All-Star Weekend, this IS the time for the big whigs to shine and, with that bein' said... I think it's only logical that I take this opportunity to make a splash, nnnnnndaddio!! Heh, y'see... for this all-star spectacular, it's my understanding that we've all been given the ability to wager points on our matches. Lots of points. Points that could propel a guy who's uncharacteristically a few points out of first, a guy who finds himself stuck in 3rd place, a guy like... well, THE PHENOM, rrrrrrright back to the top where he belongs!" (Nervously, Hart took a deep breath, then began to scratch his chin. In an attempt to relax himself, he then leaned back in the bed, resting upon his forearms as he continued.) Hart: "So Sands, with this bein' the case, I'm givin' you the chance to prove you've got as much SACK as they say you do. Both of us could use a bit of a pick-me-up just about now, and the way I see it... this match could be just what the doctor ordered. The powers that be wanna give us a chance to up the ante and gain some ground? I say let's do it!! I say we wager those points, Christian!! I say we show this place what we're made of and put 15 big ones on the line!! Just think of the possible impact those points could have on our seasons! One pin of the shoulders and any missteps taken during the first half are practically erased! Sure, it's alot to risk, but with great risk comes great rewards, my friend... and I dunno about you, but the PHENOM, Shawn Hart is MORE than willing to put it all on the line for a chance to come that much closer to the gold! You can bet your bottom dollar, baby!" (The Phenom, who had gradually found his way to better humor and an upright position, found the way to his feet.) Hart: "When opportunity knocks, I make sure I'm there to answer the door, Sands... and this is our opportunity. The only thing left to figure is wether or not YOU have that same kinda drive. Think it over Sands, Christian... wimp out like the rest, or answer my call. Either way, you try to go over, you're gonna go UNDER, pal!! The PHENOM... has left... the BUILDING!! [the thread's next post — 14 Jan 2004] "Send in the clowns..." ------ (FADEIN: The vast garage of the Sands Estate. CHRISTIAN SANDS sits on the roof of a pale blue '94 Toyota Supra, one leg tucked up with his hands clasped atop it.) Sands: If I didn't know better I'd say the circus was in town. Let me level with you about something right now, Hart. Your little circus antics are wasted on me. Maybe lesser men have been thrown off their games by your tomfoolery, but I'm far from a lesser man. I watched your match with Dan Ryan very carefully as part of my extensive pre-match strategizing, not to mention a few of your other recent matches, and I'm basing my impressions of you on that rather than your clowning around. I've learned the hard way that even the most ridiculous facade can hide a few surprises. Thus, I've developed a policy of looking past the facades to what lies beneath. I won't deny that you possess at least a LITTLE bit of talent. I'm man enough to give people their due - assuming they deserve it. I will, however, say that you're still not at my level. Do you know why I accepted your open challenge, Shawnessy? Simply put, I need points. In all honesty I'd prefer to be facing someone else right now. Unfortunately, the person I've got in mind is a lock for War Games - as is everyone else I'd consider challenging. Thus, here we are. If you want to talk opportunities, fine - mine's twofold. First off, to move up to third place in the division. Second, to clinch a playoff berth. When I came in, most people thought I'd arrived too late to even dream of finding myself in contention for UltraTitle. Yet here I am, five points from a playoff spot. You ask if I have the drive, huh? Let me tell you something. Don't you ever, EVER question my drive, because you have no idea what you're talking about. I live for wrestling, and I live to be the best IN wrestling. It's that drive that's brought me to where I am today. In a year and a half I've done more in my career than a lot of guys do in five years. I've romped through the ranks here in NFW and have main-evented more than a couple of feds in my time. Hell, son, I've been thrown into a woodpile and burned to within an inch of death by my own brother, yet I came back six months later stronger than ever. Like I said, I live for success in wrestling. The sport is quite literally the sole focus of my life. But what about you, Hart? Do you share my passion for wrestling? Or are you too busy fondling yourself to pornography to care? The day a punk like you puts ME under is the day Hell freezes over. Unfortunately, I checked the forecast, and it's clear and sunny skies down there for... oh, the next couple of eons, I guess. After all, to put me under, you'd actually have to... BEAT ME. And you simply don't have the tools to do that. I am, after all, one of the greatest technicians this world has ever known, with more than enough power to back it up to the hilt. Add that to the fact that I've got an insatiable passion for the sport behind me, and whatever tolls you have seem fairly insignificant. Incidentally, you're wrong. Maybe YOU could use a pick-me-up, but I don't need one - because I've been going strong since day one. Look at the record, Shawnee. I'm undefeated in NFW. I came in and steamrolled Hiroshi. The next week I defeated two division leaders in Michael Manson and DC Stratton, outperforming Dan Ryan in the process - the same Ryan who schooled you recently. And last week I beat the hell out of Castor Strife and made him tap out handily. In short... I've been on a roll, and I've beaten better men that you in the process. You go ahead and throw fifteen points on the line. Kiss 'em goodbye first, though, since they'll be making their way to my camp, where they'll push me into UltraTitle contention. Meanwhile, that'll leave you where? At the bottom. Where you BELONG. Go ahead, Shane. Stage a few more orgasm jokes... Switch between the first-person and third-person modes of address a couple hundred times... hell, throw in a couple nnnnnndaddios, oh-babys, and a bit of Spanish just to complete the typical Shawn Hart promo you cut every time. But while you're doing that, take a few moments out of your day to pop a tape of my matches into your VCR and watch it religiously. After all, you might as well brush up on the homework before I take you to school. Beware the Sandstorm. Or should I say... Beware "Le Sandstorm"? (FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 18 Jan 2004] (FADE IN: The ball pit at Chuck E. Cheese's. There, relaxing in his swim trunks, waist-deep in the plastic and holding a coconut cup (complete with cocktail umbrella), was he who's come to be known as the Phenom. After taking a sip from his adult beverage, he licked his lips then looked to the camera...) Hart: "Y'know, some would say there's nothing greater in this world than kickin' back in the pool with a tasty beverage. For the most part, I'm inclined to agree with that assessment... and it’s been documented. Hell, everybody this side of the Mason-Dixon line knows what I do when the day is done and the boots are tossed back into the locker. (A stern expression suddenly covered the Phenom’s face as he paused, seemingly agitated. He took a deep breath, then continued.) Hart: ”HOWEVER, despite the fact that I like to dip my chip in a variety of salsas... Christian Sands, if you think for ONE SECOND that I don’t give my all each and every time I slip through those ropes, if you even FATHOM the thought that the PHENOM of NFW isn’t in this thing to win it all, you and I are gonna have problems, my friend. I’m the kind of guy that likes to live life to the fullest, but when that bell rings... I ALWAYS get down to business. People like to say I make a jackass of myself? Fine. Jackass or not, I’m in the gym, I’m watchin’ tape, and I’m doing everything I can do to get myself prepared for my next match. This time ‘round the clock, that match is with you, Sands, and believe it or not... I’m up for the task!” (The Phenom took another sip of his drink, after which he grinned, then slapped a few of the plastic balls about before him.) Hart: ”Y’see, regardless of who you are or who you THINK you are, the fact remains that you’re down in the standings. I wouldn’t care if you came into this match without a loss in your life, because the cold, hard truth that faces every one of us that step into the ring, yourself included, is that you’re only 3 seconds away from going down. You may think you’ve somehow managed to buck the system where pinfalls are concerned, but the proof is in the pudding. People lose. People that shouldn’t do so, lose. Even people that have NEVER lost take the plunge. That bein’ the case, I pose for you this one, simple question, Mr. Sands. What in the HELL makes you so GOD DAMNED special?!” (Hart stood up in the pit, revealing his neon pink Miami Vice shorts.) Hart: ”This isn’t the NBA’s Eastern Conference, we’re not in Frank’s Basement Championship Wrestling, and last time I checked, you sure as hell aren’t 10 feet tall. This is NFW, you feathered wolf-hair son of a B(FCC)TCH! And whether you want to tune in to reality or not doesn’t change the fact that you lack points. You claim not to need any proverbial pick-me-up?! Pal, I’d like to see how far your little streak gets you when the guys you trail run the table! Undefeated or not, they have your number, and farfetched as you may think it is, the chances of some of these guys winning out are bigger than you think. So yeah, you can stick to your guns about bein’ fine and dandy all you want, as for me... I’ll take that pick-me-up and control my OWN destiny. Like it or not, that’s what I’m all about. I do things my way, I make my own course, and guys like you that seem to think my personality traits are some kind of knock on my ability can take your self-servin’, snobby-ass opinions and shove ‘em! You think you’re giving me my just due, Sands? I think you’re full of SH(FCC)T! As good as you are, you have alot to learn in the way of assessing the danger that lies before you. You assume you’ll run each and every guy you face right off the road. I’m here to tell you that in NFW, that simply ain’t... gonna... happen. Every streak meets its end, brother. This weekend, you’re gonna be meetin’ yours. Heh, cuz no matter who it is that blocks my path, you try to go over... you’re gonna go UNDER!! The PHENOM has left the bulding!!” (FADE OUT.) [the thread's next post — 18 Jan 2004] (FADEIN: A smirking CHRISTIAN SANDS sitting on the end of a weight bench.) Sands: Aww... did I make the baby throw a temper tantrum? You are a volatile little man, aren't you, Sean? Then again, I'd be a bit grouchy too if I had a middle name like Jessica. Poor little Jessica. He hears something he doesn't like, so he flies off the deep end, cussing and screaming to try and make himself look like a big tough guy. Why the change of heart, Jess? Why go from happy-go-lucky smiley boy in one promo to Little Miss Temper Tantrum in the next one? Am I under your skin already? That was easier than I thought. Maybe you think you're up to the task of facing me, but you're dead wrong. You're in the gym and watching tape? Funny, I didn't see a single VHS cassette or barbell in either of your promotional segments. That tells me one of two things. Either you're lying and you spend your time training with the Hamburgler and Grimace in the Playroom at Mickey D's... or you're one of the biggest poseurs I've ever seen. Either way, it's bad. Unless there's been some sort of shift in the nature of preparation, you don't get ready for a match against me by cleaning your ear or playing with Chuck E Cheese's balls. But please, prove me wrong. Is there a TV hanging above the ball pit playing tapes of my matches? From a numerical standpoint, yes, I'm down in the standings. However... do take into account the fact that I made my debut six weeks into the season. And how long have you been here again? Far longer than that. If you were really as hot as you claim to be you'd have racked up far more than 35 points. As it is, assuming you've been here the full eight weeks, we can do the math and come out with a figure that says you've lost at least half your matches. Wow! I guess people do lose, and it looks like you're one of them! I, on the other have, have yet to lose and don't intend on doing so for a long, long time. Certainly not at your hands. Of course I don't need a pick-me-up. To need one of those, I'd first have to be knocked down, and I certainly haven't been knocked down. And YOU'RE certainly not the man to do it. 15 points would be nice, though. And wouldn't you know it, I've got the means to acquire them. What makes me special? Quite a few things, really. Not only am I one of the best technical wrestlers in this business, I've taken this circuit by storm recently and made waves everywhere I've been. When I made my debut in GXW in August of 2002 I was an unknown. Yet look at me now. I'm something of a hot prospect on this circuit - one of the hottest, to be truthful about it. Like it or not, I am the future of wrestling - and the future is now. You... you're old news. But wait, wait... you said this isn't the NBA - that it's NFW? Oh, sh*t! All this time I thought it was the CSWA! No WONDER we had more than one card in 2003! Oh, THANK YOU, Jessica! You've opened my eyes. But in all seriousness... I don't need a reminder from YOU about what fed this is, because I'm fully aware of that fact. But here you are, shouting it out as if it were some great and wonderful point that would crush my verbal butchering of you. Want to know the truth? I'm going to embarrass you in NFW, outwrestle you in NFW, make you submit in NFW, and leave you at the bottom of the standings in NFW. You think you're going to control your own destiny, huh? (Smirk...) You really don't have a clue, do you? What you don't understand, Hart, is that when you lock up with me, your destiny is out of your hands. When you wrestle me you forfeit your destiny to me to do as I please with. In the end, you'll come out of that match however the hell I want you to, because my abilities and willpower will break your pathetic little destiny and reshape it in an image of my choosing. Taking refuge in the comfort of your self-proclaimed destiny will hurt you in the end, Jess, because I can alter that destiny at will. With that in mind, the only thing you're destined for is an embarrassing defeat. Because I've seen the man behind your facade - and that man is a weak fool. Every streak does meet its end... but not yet. Not yet. (FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 21 Jan 2004] (FADE IN: No fancy set or stage, no props, no lighting effects... just Le Phenom, hopped up and ready to go. With his flowing blonde locks gleaming in the sunlight with a brilliance matching that of the sun above, he speaks...) Hart: ”Sands, I must say... for somebody who seems to be versed in various courses of study, you sure do sound like a JACKHOLE sometimes!” (The Phenom grins cheesily.) Hart: ”Temper tantrum?! Ha! ...And a double HA as well! Pardon me if I can’t maintain that same oatmeal-like consistency throughout the duration of a speech. You may have it mastered, but my tones have peaks and valleys. Try as I might, I can’t bring myself to do like you and drone on with a four/four count, dispersing cliches faster than you can say Chuck Woolery.” (He shakes his head, disgusted by Sands’ assessment.) Hart: ”I... am... Christian... Sands... ... I... am... the greatest... technical... wrestler... today...” (The Phenom chuckles to himself, beaming with pride over his own assessment.) Hart: ”Granted you DID try and mix things up with some downright CLAY-VER witticisms, but come on... McDonald’s? The Hamburglar? Surely you can do better than that. Hell, I could’ve gone to that well myself, but I like to take my big matches seriously, Mr. Serious. I mean, I could’ve said, Mr. SANDS, that your career... just like the Las Vegas casino of the same name, has been smokey and filled with cheap hookers, but I took the high road. I could’ve gone philosophical on you and said that just like the SANDS OF TIIIIIME, you would slip through the cracks, but nah... spinnin’ yarns just ain’t my style. I’m just here to COMB THE DESERT and kick your ASS, Glass Man!! Period.” (More chuckling from the Phenom ensues.) Hart: ”It’s funny, you knock the fact that I don’t cut promos while in training, then proceed to shoot us a gem of a performance where you spend the entire time SITTING on a weight bench. What were you doing, visualizing what was gonna happen next, or just hoping that bench would press itself? Scratch that, and answer me this... what is it that’s so interesting about filming a training session that would cause you to mock me for not doing so anyway? Do I look like Jane Fonda? Is this Deal A Meal on DVD?! I think not, my friend. And I hate to break it to ya, but now that you’ve shown me just what those kinds of interviews are like, I think I’ll stick to my *makes quotations with his fingers* ‘circus act’.” (A look of utter confusion graces the Phenom’s face.) Hart: ”I mean for CHRIST’S sake, did you really just produce a promotional spot where you spent the entire time SITTING in the gym? Heh, don’t get up or anything pal, oh no! You looked as jazzed about this thing as you can be, sitting there motionless. I dunno, I guess since I’m such a baby and a clown, I just don’t get what you more serious people find entertaining. But that’s fine, Sands, you can have your oatmeal. The Phenom likes his pudding in a variety of different flavors. After I beat you, I’m thinkin’ tapioca will do the trick. The PHENOM... has left... the BUILDING!!” (FADE OUT.)
