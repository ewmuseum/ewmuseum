Man of Action

22 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“You want to call yourself a man of action?” [The screen is blank. Black.] “You want to chide Impulse for not taking JJ’s head off?” [Up.] “You know, for a guy who was content to bore the universe to death for the last two years, you sure are doing a lot of yapping here recently. Yapping that very few people are either interested in or even listening to.” [Standing tall in front of a GUILD PRODUCTIONS wall banner inside of Castor V. Strife’s personal promotional production studio is ERIC DANE, he is clearly not amused. Behind him and to the left towers MAGNUS, hate-filled and watchful eyes constantly scanning in every direction. KELLY EVANS sits in a chair in front of the Only Star, and ANGUS SKAALAND, sans wheelchair, sulks in the corner with no intention of so much as glancing in the direction of the camera.] Eric Dane: I know that you’ve seen me walk into the New Frontier and make more waves in six months than you have in your entire career, Rook, and I know how jealous that must make you feel. I can’t help it, pal, some people’ve got it, [points to himself] and some people just don’t. You, and your ridiculous friend Jack Harmen, do not. The best thing you could possibly do right now would be to stay jammed so far up Nova’s ass that some of his semi-worth in the present day of professional wrestling wears off on you. [Eric shrugs. Kelly looks at the floor.] Eric Dane: What I can’t have, however, is your mildly annoying and completely useless attempts at getting yourself over at the expense of my two very expendable associates. [He eyeballs Angus. Kelly sighs.] Eric Dane: You call yourself showing Stalker and Impulse and eveyone else, myself included, that your quest to make a conglomeration of belts that don’t matter into something of use in the New Frontier is nothing to be trifled with, and that anyone and everyone should be wary of crossing your path... And your big Master Plan, your big Show Of Force... Ends with shaving Kelly’s eyebrows? [He pauses to let you percolate on that for a second.] Eric Dane: And you think that is going to make people take you seriously? You think that beating up cripples and threatening women makes you a man of action, Rook? Everybody in the building hates me, and I can cop to being one of the more dispicable people that you or anyone else in the New Frontier have ever come across, but even I haven’t stooped to the level of pissing the paying customers off by promising an act of horrific violence and following it up by delivering a less than satisfactory, pre-pubescent, high school girls version of a prank. An underwhelming joke. That about sums you up. [He pauses again, contempt rising like bile in his throat.] Eric Dane: Kelly, if you would be so kind as to show Rook Black what he should have done if he wanted to get a rise out of the likes of me? [Kelly looks up at Eric for the first time today. Her eyes send a lovers’ plea for a last second stay of execution. The cold cerulean gaze that he returns to her is all of the answer that the Whore Next Door needs. She reaches up with one hand and grabs the majority of her beautifully honey brown hair into a mess of a ponytail.] [The Only Star produces a pair of shears.] [Kelly takes them and with one snip lops off the majority of her hair.] Eric Dane: Eyebrows grow back in a matter of days, Rook. I can tell you that Kelly’s hair is a product of a lifetime of care and pampering, and you probably would have gotten a lot more of a reaction out of her had you the actual balls to go ahead with the hack-job that you’d promised. You aren’t a man of action, Rook. As a matter of fact, you aren’t even a man. Angus is more of a man than you, at least he owns up to his mistakes and inequities. [Kelly tosses the scissors, her eyes fill with rage as she looks down at the knot of hair that she’d just cut from her own head.] Eric Dane: Like I’ve told you, time and time again, I am not interested in you. I am even less interested in that joke of a title that you’re making such a stink about. You’re not going to bully a man like me into some kind of a rage by pulling seventh grade pranks. If you want a piece of me so badly, you can do like everybody else in the New Frontier and wait until I’ve won the World Title, and earn yourself a shot at the belt. [Smirk.] Eric Dane: Until then, the closest you’ll ever come to me is picking on Angus and Kelly. As a matter of fact, Angus’s punishment for his part in this whole debacle is that I’m sending him to Pittsburgh to keep you from winning that stupid belt that you covet so much- Angus Skaaland: [interrupting] WHAT?! I’m in no condition to- [He is silenced by a glare from Dane.] Eric Dane: As for me, I’ve got bigger fish to eviscerate. Namely, Castor Strife. Now Cas, can I call you Cas? Good. Now Cas, I don’t know how you think putting that belt up for grabs on an Open Contract in Pittsburgh is supposed to bother me. Am I supposed to get super-pissed because you’re trying to skip over me? Am I supposed to jump and take the bait, take my shot on your terms and come up short? Come on, Cas, I’ve got you by the short and curlies and you know it. If you lose that belt before you get around to giving me My Title Shot, then you will have lost the only bargaining chip that you’ve got left, and I’ll be forced to liquidate your assets and focus my attention and my now monumental net worth on whomever you drop the title to. And where does that leave you, Cas? Out in the g*ddamned rain, that’s where. [How do you like those particular apples?] Eric Dane: I told you, Castor, I can be a very patient man. I can and I will wait until the most opportune time to take that big gold belt and strap it around my waist. It’s not all about you, Castor, regardless of whatever dogmatic rhetoric that you think you can lull the masses into believing. I don’t care if I take the belt from you or from Dan Ryan or from Impulse or from P. King freaking Windham. The only thing that matters is the belt. So do with it as you please, Cas, but don’t be silly enough to delude yourself into thinking that you’ve got the slightest bit of control in the matter. Taking you for everything you’ve got has been a means to an end. And that end... [He smiles a million dollar smile. Eric Dane: Is the phrase “Eric Dane, World Champion” coming out of Lee Baby Sims’ mouth. [His smile doesn’t fade. Angus huffs and puffs and storms off set. Kelly stares down at the floor, and Magnus just looks on and physically defines the word “intimidation.”] [FADE2BLACK]
