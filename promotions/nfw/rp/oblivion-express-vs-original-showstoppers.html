Oblivion Express vs. Original Showstoppers

11 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STIPULATION: Interconference Match [the thread's next post — 12 Feb 2004] (CUT TO: "Fabulous" Frankie Fargo and "American Gigolo" Brandon Mueller, wearing tux-tails, bow-ties and mullets, standing in front of a parked confederate flag themed pick-up truck that is parked in front of a dirt mall. Mueller's arms are crossed, his forehead is stitched, yet he still has a cocky smirk on his face. Fargo, confederate flag bandana, steps forward, angry as always. The NFW World Tag Team Titles are fastened around their waists.) FARGO: So, it appears to this fahne Southern gentleman that THE WHOLE DANG WORLD wantsta TRY AND CAH-STRATE us lahke we're some kind of FARM MULE. (Fargo turns to his side and calls out, impersonating an unknown whomever.) 'Hey-- there goes Frankie Fargo. There goes The Southern Sex Star. They world champs. They the best tag team in the world. We can't HAVE that. So let's try and HUMILIATE them.' For YEARS, ya'llve trahd to humiliate men lahke me and Brand-o-- the New Sons of the South, raised on 100 proof, whistlin' Dixie. Ya'll Northerners came down here in 1860 and TRAHD to take us down, you TRAHD to break our spirits, you TRAHD to burn down all of Georgia and ya'll got MET with the faht of your lives. Despite the odds, despite the fact that ya'll outnumbered us, the Southern states PROVED that we was your superiors. But to rub it in, to rub in the CARNAGE and DESECRATION ya'll committed on us... what ya'll decide to do? Ya'll spend DECADES rubbin' it in our faces. First, all ya'll carpetbaggers came down here to try and TAKE OVER where we was from. Then, all the rich, northern SNOBS had to open their mouths and tell us how to RUN OUR LAHVES-- tellin' us who could go to school where, who could use what water fountain, who could sit where on the bus. Why? Because it's so much better up North? Believe me, I've spent tahme in Boston. I've spent tahme in Lowell. And ah'll tell you this-- where ya'll live ain't nothin' more than retarded, backwards SLUMS... and eye hope EACH and EVERY single one of ya'll DIES in your slum. Me? Heh. Ahm a wild-eyed SOUTHERN BOY whose been spat on and slapped in his face his whole lahfe. Eye have the government tryin' to take away MAH guns, puttin' me in jail based on made-up testimony from Kendall Windham, tellin' me that ahm wrong because eye don't want mah sisters and daughters to go to school anywhere NEAR animals like Saafir Islam or whatever his made up name done be-- despite the fact that mah daddy got his name called up in Viet-nam, got shot at by the Cong, got put in the brig for fraggin' yet ANOTHUH Northern man tryin' to give him orders, got shot at AGAIN by the Cong, got put in the big AGAIN for smokin' a little bit of weed while on patrol, and then ended up losin' one eye and one leg because his NORTHERN platoon leader couldn't find a mine, and then ended up comin' home, gettin' SPAT on by people like John Kerry, not gettin' a job because the factory he done worked in left for VIETNAM, couldn't get no unemployment 'cause that money goes to unemployed Negroes who run around the housing complexes *WE* built for them havin' babies, and then ended up dyin'... all by the age of 22. (Fargo's eyes are wide-- sex predator wide. Mueller's smirk disappears and he looks at his partner with worry in his face, starting to hive him a back rub.) Yeah, ya'll done SLAPPED him in the face. And ya'll slapped ME and The Greatest Hair On Earth in the face. Naw, it ain't raht that we can go and send ANOTHER tag team in this sport into retirement. Nosiree-- the Powells, those two low-lahves, no, they done had to come from behand to take us out. Then, the DRS comes and cracks powertools over MAH face and over BRANDO-s beautiful face. Then the Oblivion Express... a team we already SENT packin', considering our portfo-lee-oh manager Calvin Carlton FAHRED them to bring us all in... they come from out of nowhere... and then ya'll went over the lahne. Then ya'll put tar on us, put feathers on us and tried to SHAME us. (Mueller now steps back, shaking his head, looking at the ground. He then looks up, smirk turned into a grimace, he too looking like he could kill a drifter without notice.) Well, guess what, NFW? Ya'll could send every tag team in this sport out to jump on mah face. Ya'll could take a blowtorck, burn my eyesockets like hickory sticks, and it wouldn't make no difference-- unless ya'll done flat out KILL me, unless ya'll done flat out TAKE MAH LAFE... eye *WILL* come out here and destroy you. Ya'll think ah got anything to live for? Let me tell ya'll what ah got-- ah got eight years tahme, ah got a bad credit ratin', ah got NASCAR racers sellin' out, ah got Wal-Marts in mah hometown, ah got Yankee Trash buildin' mansions in MAH backyard. Ah got a father six feet under, ah got family members who won't talk to me unless they either in REHAB or JAIL. What do ah got? HUH? What do ah got? AH GOT ABSOLUTELY NOTHIN'. AH DON'T HAVE NUTHIN' TO LIVE FOR. THAT'S RAHT-- AH GOT NUTHIN'... NUTHIN' AT ALL... TO LIVE FOR. (Fargo's fists are clenched with rage. Mueller again looks worried, giving Fargo a back rub. Fargo puts his hands to his head and calms down, and starts laughing.) But all done tell you what. (Cackle.) Ah do have sumthin' to DIE for. (He starts tapping his title. Mueller, smirking, does the same.) Ah am one-half of the NFW World Tag Team Champions... which makes me one-half of the daymn GREATEST tag team on this planet. And that means... that means that each and every single one of ya'll watchin' me at home... (He points menacingly at the camera.) Each and every single one ya'll whove SLAPPED my heritage in the face... each and every single one of ya'll whove SLAPPED mah daddy down... each and every single one of ya'll whove SLAPPED me in mah face and SLAPPED me down mah EN-TIRE LAHFE... that means that EACN AN' EVERY SINGLE ONE OF YA'LL have to realize that this is the only thing that I've got in mah lahfe. Ah don't care 'bout NUTHIN' else. To take it from me... ya'll gonna have to BURY me. Oblivion Express, ya'll THOUGHT you pulled one on us. Ya'll THOUGHT ya'll ruined us. Guess what? Ya'll didn't. (Fargo puts his arms out wide, threateningly.) Ahm still here. Ya'll want some more of this? Huh? Ya'll want to take the one thing on this planet that ah have? Well, come awn and trah and TAKE it. Because ya'll ain't gonna do it. Ya'll ain't gonna do it. Because to do that... ya'll gonna have ta KILL me. Guess what? Ya'll ain't STRONG enough to do that. Y'know why? (Mueller starts to smirk and cackle again. So does Fargo.) 'Cause The Originals are stronger... for even longer. (Both strut off. FTB.)
