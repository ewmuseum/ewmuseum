A debut that blew.

18 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN::RAZE's home in a shady part of Bethlehem, PA. RAZE is training as always in his makeshift gym. Loose weights and worn equipment.) RAZE:All the excitement and determination for what? I was beaten and there wasn't even a dramatic ending. I just laid there like a JACKASS. I'm disappointed at the whole thing. I had him on the ropes, damn you DREAD. -You looked terrible out there, RAZE. My mommy can fight better then you. You looked like s@#t out there. Did you hear the fans they hated your style? You should have gone to the hospital and finished him like we planned. (Ringing) RAZE:I can't do that. I'm mad but I'm also a good sport. Maybe, I need a gimmick or a manager. I could be RAZE the Flaming Pickle or RAZE the SuperGuy. You're not doing a good job. I had him right where I wanted and BLAM, I was pinned 1-2-3. He couldn't knock me out but I understand I'm the new kid on the block. I was screwed. I can take it. -You suck RAZE. You should have never left boxing to do some stupid "Sport Enterainment". You need me. You will win with me, RAZE. (Ringing louder) RAZE: You and I are finished. Done. I can't hear you anymore. GO AWAY. (On the brink of tears.) -Good Luck, LOSER. (Ringing stops forever!?) RAZE: Now that's over let's get down to business. (FADEOUT: RAZE turns his back to the camera and his voices.)
