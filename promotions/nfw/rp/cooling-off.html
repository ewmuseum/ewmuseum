Cooling off

6 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Short fuses, hot tempers, destruction...oh my. Lindsay Troy had a feeling that Dan Ryan wasn't going to take the advice she offered him before she left him to his own devices. The tornado that seemed to blow through his locker room confirmed that suspicion. Troy leaned against the threshold of the door, bags over her shoulder, arms folded over her chest. She merely shakes her head as Ryan seethes in the center of the room, keeping as much pressure off his hurt knee as possible. Troy: Hulk certainly smashed, didn't he? Ryan's eyes narrow even more as he glares at her. Troy: You done? Or do you want me to leave and come back when you've cooled off? Ryan: I'm not cooling off this time, Lindsay. This went a little past the usual everyday event. Ryan turns and starts to put his belongings together to leave. Troy: Well you'd better cool off, or else your knee joint might end up separated from the rest of your body. You want to sit down and let me wrap it? You refused regular medical attention already, and I don't go away that easily. Ryan doesn't speak, continuing to shove his things into a bag....getting more violent with each piece...then stops, closes his eyes and takes a deep breath. Ryan: Nah....I'll be alright. It's not nearly as bad as it looks. Ryan looks down.... Ryan: Maybe we should just get outta here. I need to think..... He looks at Troy... Ryan: We need to think.... She nods. Troy: Can't say I'm too happy with your sudden outburst against Prosser and Crew, but I guess you can't tiptoe through the tulips with these a**holes. Takes all the fun out of the job. Ryan smiles....but not the kind of smile that reassures a manager.... Ryan: You know what's funny about that, Lindsay?? There's not a damn person, you included, in this company right now that knows what I'm like when I'm truly pissed off. Do you know how long that part of me has been left alone? I'm sorry, this doesn't feel much like a job right now. It feels intensely personal. Troy: All I'm saying is that I thought it would have been in our best interest if we thought up a game plan before you went off. But in hindsight it probably wouldn't have made much of a difference. I had a feeling that you'd just dive right in and take on the world head on, and my intuitions are hardly ever wrong. I've got your back, Dan. You know that. But my back might not be much good if it winds up broken because one of Prosser's boys steamrolls me. Ryan stares at Troy for a moment, then looks up in thought.... Ryan: Right......two against the world isn't gonna work. First thing's first.....we need to get outta here and get to the airport. I wanna make some calls on the way.... Troy: I'll ride with you there, but I've got to find your favorite South buddy and mine Joey Melton and see what's up with him. She smirks. And then I need to make a phone call to your favorite Empire Pro talent Beast and let him know that I made it out of here alive. Ryan: Melton..... Ryan seems to think of something, then waves it off... Ryan: Alright cool. Tell you what....instead of riding to the airport, go do what you need to do with Melton and call up Beastie Boy. We can meet up later on. Troy: Don't think I condone what Joey did. But, I can't say that it came as a total shock either. I'll give you a ring tomorrow and we'll figure something out. You take care of yourself tonight, Dan. Don't do anything I wouldn't do. She gives him a wink and turns to leave... Ryan stares at the door....but looks past it..... It had been a while....been a long while since he had gotten that angry....that violent......that out of control..... Maybe it's high time he woke up.
