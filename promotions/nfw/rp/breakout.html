Breakout

27 Jul 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Continued from “Career Day.”) (FADE-IN) LATER AT RADICOMM WORLDWIDE INTERNATIONAL INC. Joey Melton and Calvin Carlton walk through the double-glass doors of Radicomm WorldWide International Inc. a ninety-story state-of-the-art corporate tower. The first floor lobby, of which Melton and Carlton are browsing rubbernecked, is incredibly beautiful and modernly decorated. CARLTON: Grant works here? This has gotta be a mistake. JOEY: Don’t be so sure. Just because his son is a dope doesn’t mean he’s not a winner himself. FRONT DESK – MINUTES LATER Melton and Carlton cozy up to a five-foot high marble receptionist desk. A middle-aged man, obviously dying his hair, pretends to be busy, making Melton wait. JOEY: (Studying the man’s name tag.) Gregg? Hi. I have an appointment with Mr. Grant. If you could page him for us please. GREGG: Be with you in a second, sir. JOEY: (to Carlton) Sir? Am I--- CARLTON: NO! Good pore day I swear. GREGG: (stamps a piece of paper emphatically before looking up purposely.) How may I help you sir? (Joey turns back to Carlton.) CARLTON: Twenty-eight tops! (Carlton smiles.) JOEY: Here to see Mr. Grant. GREGG: I see. (Bites bottom lip) Do you have an appointment? JOEY: No---I...uh. GREGG: (suspiciously) Your name Sir? JOEY: (Melton broods.) Dr. Von Grunt. (Joey winks at Carlton.) GREGG: Ohmy. I apologize, Mr. Grant did say something about results...but I assumed I was to be expecting a phone call. JOEY: Well, yeah...I was...phone’s not working. Don’t pay the damn bill for a month and they think they have the right to cut it off. GREGG: (snickers) I know what you mean...hateful creatures. My Aunt Edna was without cable for two weeks earlier this year because of a customer service faux pas. If that woman can’t see Oprah she morphs into a right #####. Most dreadful time of my life...I refused to see her. IT’S MY AUNT EDNA and I was driven to throw away the key. Oh they apologized, but “I’m sorry” doesn’t repair the mental scarring, hmm? JOEY: Tell Ed I sympathize. Look at me, forced to make a house visit of sorts. Once the union hears about this... (Joey smiles.) Should I just go on up? GREGG: (leans in.) If I may ask...how sick is Grant? (looks around, then leans further in.) We’ve heard rumors, but nobody upstairs tells us ##### you know. Grant’s been here as long as I have. Not the socialist butterfly, but he still lifts our spirits every morning as he flies by. It’s rare you meet people like that. (rolls eyes) JOEY: I’m not at liberty to say—AIDS—what his condition is. But...(dramatic pause) the situation has worsened over the past three weeks. I don’t know what else we can do for him, honestly. GREGG: (shocked) So he’s---family? JOEY: Family? (Carlton and Joey look at each other.) JOEY: Noooo. Look, no, we’re not, hey now..no..we’re not family. This is...uh...my son. (Grabs onto Carlton’s right arm tight) Kid’s gotta a helluva arm. Seven no-hit innings last week. CARLTON: Twelve K’s. JOEY: Well...eleven...I’m not counting 2-strike bunt fouls. CARLTON: Dad it counts! JOEY: Not in my book. You want that 12th K, it better be called 3rd strike or swung at and missed. CARLTON: NOTHIN’ IS EVER GOOD ENOUGH FOR YOU IS IT? JOEY: Go wait in the car. Now! (Gregg, stunned.) GREGG: (Embarrassed) Floor eighty-seven, third door on your left.) JOEY: (smiles) Thanks. INSIDE ELEVATOR Calvin and Joey mull inside of the spacious elevator. Elvis was born in a house smaller than this. CARLTON: I’m saying...Momma would have counted it. JOEY: Momma knows many a thing Carlton...but baseball? CARLTON: SHE LIVED the movie A League Of Their Own! JOEY: That’s scary, please stop...Gregg would be a riot in a dark alley, huh? CARLTON: (laughs) You’re marked. JOEY: That’s enough out of you. (Elevator stops, and a woman in her mid-twenties walks in and stands between Joey and Carlton. Cute, but she’s had to work at it. Doors close and the elevator restarts. Melton shoots Carlton a look then starts softly singing Rod Stewart’s “Tonight’s The Night.”) JOEY: (singing) Gonna be alright... (The woman, thin, but short, in a business suit, passes Joey a promising look.) JOEY: Oh Maggie...I wish I HAD NEVER seen your face. (She turns to Joey, beaming.) WOMAN: I can’t help it. Are you him? CARLTON: HE who? WOMAN: (waves off Carlton.) You’re Joey Melton aren’t you!! I know it’s you! OMG. I looooove this jooooooob. JOEY: (Incredulously) You’ve heard of me? WOMAN: UH YEEEEEEEEEEAH. Look at me I’m wetting—-ur-sweating like a school-girl. (slaps her thigh) Tracy...chill. I’m sorry. JOEY: No, don’t be...I like wetting schoolgirls. CARLTON: Me too. JOEY: (motions to Carlton) Son...shhh. Tracy.... TRACY: You’re here to see Lindsay aren’t you?! Of course you are...what am I saying... JOEY: Lindsay? (shoots Carlton a look. This chic is nuts.) TRACY: Lindsay Troy... (Pin drops.) (Elevator opens. Eighty-seventh floor. Facing forward, there are 2 glass doors. Tracy gives Melton a look back then walks through them. The Elevator doors close, Carlton hits “Stop.”) CARLTON: Lindsay Troy...the luck you have, sir. (Carlton grins excitedly.) The doors that keep opening up for me when I thought my life couldn’t get any richer! JOEY: Who the hell is Lindsay Troy? You’re grinning like Momma offered to draw your bath personally. Troy...she’s... CARLTON: SPECIAL. JOEY: How special? CARLTON: If she were a mechanical bull... JOEY: No one finishes the ride... (Carlton nods.) How do you--- CARLTON: MISTER MELTON, Lindsay Troy’s been on the “50 Most Powerful Women in Business” list more times than I can even remember. This is a Fortune 500 Company, Sir! And what’s more, this isn’t even how she makes her living! (Joey raises an eyebrow. Showgirl? Reformed hooker who won the lotto? What kind of woman is this Troy anyhow?) CARLTON: Sir, she’s a wrestler. JOEY: Carlton...fantasy night stayed in the vault. That was left in three empty bottles of wine. It’s not nice to open the vault to tease! CARLTON: It’s true! She’s been all over the United States, winning all sorts of titles. She’s won more titles than you’ve had women! JOEY: Blasphemy! CARLTON: I’m sorry MISTER MELTON. It was a figure of speech. Of course, I didn’t mean that in the literal sense. JOEY: I should hope not. I’m not sure you would enjoy being forbidden to have the pleasure of my company, now would you Carlton? (Carlton whimpers. A day without MISTER MELTON is like a day without sunshine…and the sun is always shining somewhere…) JOEY: (Looking off to the side.) Troy...Troy...Troy. CARLTON: Sir? JOEY: (Snaps out of a daze.) Nothing. Had like an out-of-body experience for a second. It’s a twenty-minute ride back to parking. Shall we have some fun? (Carlton shakes happily.) ELEVATOR DOORS OPEN Melton and Carlton walk through the glass doors into a large outer office. Tracy is seated behind a big receptionist desk to the right. JOEY: Tracy...is Lindsay in? (smiles.) TRACY: Just back from lunch. CARLTON: Low carb lunch I hope... (Carlton, Joey, and Tracy all laugh haughtily.) TRACY: I’ll tell her you’re here. JOEY: NO! How bout we just let ourselves in. Surprise her. (Tracy balks.) TRACY: I’m...not supposed to do that...Troy always says those who don’t learn from their past mistakes are doomed to repeat them...not in good graces right now... (Joey reaches over the desk and grabs Tracy’s left hand.) JOEY: Do it for me. Can’t be a good girl all the time, eh? (Tracy melts.) TRACY: Through those doors...Mister Melton. Melton and Carlton walk down a long corridor to another set of double doors, only these are wooden...oak...with gold handles. INSIDE TROY’S OFFICE (Joey and Carlton step in. Carlton is wearing tan slacks, dark brown dress shoes, white dress shirt, green sweater, and a red tie so conservative it actually votes for him. Joey’s decked out in black slacks, shoes...no socks...a skin hugging stylish black t-shirt, and wearing Bono Fly shades on the top of his head. The office is enormous. Large mahogany finished desk, high-backed black leather office chair, conference table off to the side, expensive paintings and other decorations and against a far wall is a large flat-panel plasma TV currently showing ESPN News.) (Lindsay Troy...ahhh, the Queen. Long flowing brown hair cascading down her head and around her shoulders in a halo of curls, with natural hints of blonde and red highlights. Richly tanned skin, with a complexion that would impress even MISTER MELTON. Her arms are nicely defined, as apparent by the sleeveless dress she wears. Joey wonders how the rest of her body must look... As the two men enter, they notice her bent over her desk, reading over some documents. She looks up at Melton and Carlton, giving them a once over with piercing hazel eyes, before tilting her head to the side and with an exasperated sigh saying...) TROY: If you're looking for my sister, she's two floors down. (She looks down at her paperwork before lifting her head again) TROY: Oh, and if she starts twirling her hair around her index and middle finger, I'd run out of there as fast as I could, if I were you. (Melton smiles. Her face is utterly ravishing. Could he last eight seconds?) JOEY: If she's half as ready as Tracy there I bet she stays in a state of Orgasmic Glee. (Troy mutters under her breath.) TROY: You don't know what you're in for. JOEY: Nice desk...organic I hope. (Pause) I gave up a long time ago trying to figure out what I was in for. The good ones take you by surprise. I know we live in a sexual revolution where you’re supposed to communicate with your partner. What feels good to me...but my preferences tend to run against the grain. (Troy looks up from her stack of papers, scrutinizing Joey and Carlton. She raises an eyebrow and leans back against her chair.) TROY: Do I...know you from somewhere? (She taps her pen against her pouting lips.) TROY: You seem...awfully familiar. (Her eyes wander off to the side, and a hint of recognition crosses her face) JOEY: I did some Clearasil commercials back in the day...but... TROY (waving her hand dismissively): No no, it wasn't from a commercial. (Her eyes snap back to front, a cold glare protruding from her pupils. The TV in the background is running a "Breaking Story"...Career Day at Hewson Elementary) TROY: You...assaulted...a...CHILD!! OF ONE OF MY EMPLOYEES! (Melton staggers back. Busted.) JOEY: You employ that boy’s dad? That’s actually why I’m here. I’m campaigning for the PTA presidency. His vote is crucial. If I ran the PTA, this country wouldn't have a gun control problem, and irritants like that would be corrected on the spot! TROY: Oh, I'm sure if you ran the PTA you'd make sure the principal's office came equipped with whips, chains, and a torture rack. (Troy throws the pen down on the desk, clearly agitated by the interruption and Melton's complete lack of regard for the situation he caused. Who the hell did he think he was, anyway?!) JOEY: (looking at Carlton.) You told me you found that rental slip...Carlton that’s why we tear...as we receive. (He continues to Troy) Some of those tapes on that list...were for research purposes only. If you think that speaks as to what kind of guy I am....you're...uh.....you're.... CARLTON: DEAD WRONG. JOEY: Thanks mate. We all make mistakes. Some just more discreetly then others. (Melton leans back on the plush couch, making himself more at home. Tracy would fit nice on here.) (Troy looks at Carlton, extending her hand in a questioning manner.) TROY: Who the hell is that? Your puppet? Is your hand stuck so far up his ass that you've taught him how to talk on command? (She snaps back to Melton.) TROY: And who said you could have a seat?! JOEY: I assumed...you wouldn't have a problem with. (Carlton tries to sit. Melton waves him off.) Carlton here is quite the catch. I like to think of him as a career submissive more than a puppet. We all need discipline in our lives. Right? (Melton winks at Troy.) JOEY: How bout you. Who's hand stays up your ass? TROY: Oh, that's cute. (Hand up her ass indeed. If he isn't careful he might get a right cross to the jaw.) You barge in here, don't even bother to say who the hell you are, and now you're breaking out the one-liners. You must have some sort of purpose for your intrusion, so I suggest you get on with it, because so far the two of you have proved to be nothing but gigantic wastes of my time. You’re not seriously here for that boy’s father are you? If I were you, and thank fate and God I’m not, I’d be sending expensive gifts to smooth the situation over. JOEY: (What did she mean by torture rack.) Can I be honest with you? TROY: It gets better from here? JOEY: Carlton and I have a trip planned later this month to see the Biggest Ball Of Twine...and well...I’m in the market for a date. To see something that massive alone, would be a crime. TROY: Well you and Carlton seem to be real buddy buddy as it is, I'm sure you two would enjoy the pleasure of each others' company much more than you would enjoy mine. (She pauses, and exhales deeply) TROY: Look Mister...what was your name again? (Carlton and Melton pause to consider. They get along swimmingly, but is there more to their relationship?) JOEY (smile fading from his mouth): You serious? I’m... CARLTON: HE'S MISTER MELTON. I.E The greatest CSWA Wrestler of all-time!! This is why I want the SAT modified. This country is rising generations of kids that are ignorant to Mister Melton’s accomplishments. (Troy looks at them, nonplussed.) TROY: Melton.....Melllllllton.... (She pauses, thinking. Should she go for the low-blow now, or later? Hm. No time like the present) Oh yeah...midgets, right?! JOEY: Dammit…I told you...those tapes were not for kicks. (Carlton looks at Melton.) JOEY: Oh....yeah! That's right.... (smiles) (She flashes her patented smirk...left side of her mouth curling up slightly, eyes sparking mischievously) JOEY: Did you...learn anything from watching? (Smiles. At a full foot taller than Ivy, she'd be fun.) (Troy stands up from her chair and walks around to the front of her desk. She leans back, the short hem of her dress falling high on her thighs, the bottom of the criss-cross laces that run up and down her sides hang teasingly almost begging Joey to pull at them, the front of the dress cut downtothere. Troy gracefully puts her arms behind her back) TROY: Well, according to the tape, some things just don't change. You still seem to be the same pompous ass now as you were then. But really, should I have expected anything else? (Melton freezes. She smells wonderful…Black Cashmere, Donna Karen. Five years ago he'd clear off the desk and let that stand as his move. But now?) JOEY: Then? (Melton has a follow-up out-of-body experience. Troy. She’s older, changed, professional. But those legs. It’s her. The upper hand returns.) JOEY: Ha. Ha. Ha. Japan...two years ago. I’ve only been with one set of legs than ran for days, and honey we both know those were yours. Forgive me for forgetting...I don’t see how I could have...well I used moderately then... TROY (Cool, calm, collected. Icy even. Just one of her many endearing qualities.) You must have me confused with someone else. I've never been to Japan, and even if I did, I would have made sure that I would have forgotten you. (Melton stands, and gets inches within Troy. They see eye-to-eye. His left hand rests next to her hip.) JOEY: If I remember correctly...there was a mole right about here... (Melton softly reaches for her hip) (Joey’s hand rests on Troy's hip for barely a moment, before her left hand encloses around his throat and her right hand twists his hand away) TROY: If I were you, Joseph, I'd back away while you still have your windpipe and your wrist intact. Hmm? (Melton smiles. He loves a girl with spirit.) JOEY: Did we ever...officially apply for that marriage license? Those five days were magnificent. Why did they end again? I’ve handled that personal crisis now. I’m ready for something that’s gonna stick. Baby let’s try, let’s really try this time. (A fire not seen before in this encounter flashes in Troy's eyes, and she once again flashes her sexy smirk. She looks around Melton's head, and her eyes catch something) TROY: Oh my Joseph, is this a blemish I see? I don't think you exfoliated and moisturized last night. (Joey's expression turns cold. He snaps his fingers and Carlton runs over to check. He nods in agreement.) JOEY: (Regrettably, it all comes back.) You're the only woman who’s ever been able to break my face out. Why must God give the cruelest gifts to the most flexible? TROY: I must be lucky, I guess. (She shoves him away from her) Then again, I do enjoy getting under people's skin at times. (There's the smirk again. She can see him fidgeting as he looks at her. She is, as the song implies, simply irresistible.) (Mountain water. Carlton fetch. Hurry.) JOEY: You know, not all the best tapes have to be rented. Why…I was just showing Carlton one last night...that...set the women's movement back fifty years. This poor girl...a drink or two in her and she's purring like a cat… TROY: She must have been a lightweight, then. (Good thing for Troy, she knows how to hold her alcohol.) JOEY: (Intrigued) You still drink don't you? TROY: Have I piqued your interest? (A soft knock is heard from the door, and Tracy peeks her head in. Joey flashes an award-winning smile, and Tracy giggles and blushes. Her eyes peer over to Troy, who is fuming. Tracy composes herself, clearing her throat) TRACY: Lindsay, two gentlemen are here to see you. They said it’s urgent business. (Her eyes float over to Joey again, who gives her a sly wink) TROY: Well…who are they? (Tracy’s too busy oogling at Joey. Troy furrows her brow, and taps her foot.) TROY: TRACY! TRACY (snapping back to reality): Huh?! TROY: Who…are…they… TRACY: Oh…umm…you know… (She lifts her arm and rubs her upper back, between her shoulder blades. Troy nods her head ever so slightly.) TROY: Tell them to wait outside. TRACY: K. (She slowly begins to pull her head away from the door.) JOEY: (turns his head toward the door, and smirks) Tracy...dinner tonight? (Tracy giggles, and disappears from view. Joey looks back to Troy.) JOEY: I'll...have her home by eleven. (Joey and Carlton move toward the door. Melton turns back one last time.) JOEY: I wouldn't worry too much about her. You've been there. You know how the evening always ends. TROY: Have I? (Lightbulb) Y'know, I recall my sister having visited Japan on business about two years ago, and when she came back she had the most interesting story about meeting up with a guy about 15 years her senior. I wonder...Maybe you did hook up with a Troy in Japan. And if that's the case, I hope you had yourself checked out. After all, who knows just where my sister has been. (Carlton smirks...Joey catches it and stares him down.) JOEY (muttering): Eighty-seven floors...this is goin’ to be a f’king long ride. (He and Carlton storm out, and Troy sarcastically waves. As the door shuts, she pushes a button on a desk console and speaks) TROY: Tracy, send my guests in, if you would. (FTB) **Co-Written by the Queen. Thanks Pumpkin.
