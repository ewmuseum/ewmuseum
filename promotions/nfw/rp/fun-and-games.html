Fun and Games

27 Apr 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BOOM. Dan Ryan, just sitting there, just looking at the screen, ALWAYS smirking. DAN RYAN: "Hey JJ, just thought I'd pop in and say hello." "You remember how you put Rosie through a table? You remember how you dared Randall Knox to do something about it? You remember how he wouldn't because of his personal code of ethics?" "I just thought I'd come on here and point a few things out to you." "First of all -- the money this company pays me to be here? I don't need it. I own my own company. I have money. You can fire me right now and I wouldn't care. Second of all, yes, you are correct. You still don't belong with the big boys. Third, your kindergarten heel tactics do nothing but annoy. You are a fly to me." "FOURTH.... digging up dead relatives? Congratulations, you are now officially the Big Boss Man." "And finally, and most importantly, Randall Knox's code that prevents him from breaking your legs, arms, ribs, sternum, kneecaps, neck and FACE, then leaving you for dead in the middle of a two lane highway in West Texas?" "I have no such code." "Food for thought." Wink. FADE OUT.
