Driven to Distraction

1 Nov 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN on the club. The camera is on the dance floor which is filled with people. Young, lithe and pretty, they are all dancing to the music, lit by the pulsing and flashing fo lights. The camera works it’s way through the crowd to a booth in the corner. The booth is filled with people, young and old alike, Here are two men, well dressed and young, in an animated conversation about something we cannot hear over the pulse and throb of the misc. They ignore the clearly bored women by their side as we pan around the booth. Next we see an old man with two stunning women flanking him, a bemused smile is on all their lips, and the champagne flutes before them sit half empty. Now, in the corner, we see TEMPESTA. He’s dressed immaculately in a dark suit, his shirt unbuttoned to mid chest showing a thick platinum chain around his muscular neck. TEMPESTA is staring at something in the distance, ignoring those around him, gripping the drink in his hand tightly. His jaw muscles tense and relax to a rhythm all their own. A drop dead gorgeous raven haired woman leans close to his ear, whispers something, then nibbles on his earlobe as her hand runs across his chest. He seems unaffected, as he fails to react in any way at all. Annoyed, the beauty grabs her purse and edges out from the booth, unseating all those beside her. Still he does not move, staring into space, lost in thought, all alone in this mass of humanity. The party continues, bouncers making their way through the crowd, breaking up disputes before they can escalate into fights. Suddenly TEMPESTA stands, pushing the table before him out of his way, spilling drinks on those that were seated near him. TEMPESTA ignores the chaos he has caused and stalks out of the club, the bouncers clearing a path for him to the door. The party continues unabated in his absence. CUT TO The Board room. MR REDD is seated at the head of the table, reviewing a stack of papers arrayed before him. His tie is loose around his neck, and a snifter of brandy sits within easy reach. SNAP PAN to the doors, which fly open, and we see TEMPESTA silhouetted in the opening. TEMPESTA: (growling) “I want Dread.” MR REDD: (Considers this for a moment) “I think we would be better served if you. . .” TEMPESTA: “DREAD!” (Slams his fist against his end of the table, cracking but not quite breaking it) “Make it happen!” (Points at MR REDD) “You want your cut of my action? GET. ME. DREAD. No three way matches, no bull**** with outside interference. The next PPV I want to see him, in the ring. NOW BOOK IT!” MR REDD: (Amused) “As you wish.” TEMPESTA turns and stalks out of the board room, and MR REDD watches him go for a moment, then reaches for the phone. FTB [the thread's next post — 2 Nov 2002] ::FADEIN on the rickety, sagging ring at the Basham-Schultz Wrestling Academy. 'THE WOLF' CHRIS MCMILLAN sits perched on the top turnbuckle, his fingers steepled contemplatively inderneath his chin:: THE WOLF: It is interesting to me that you are so distracted by the thought of facing Mister Dread again. I mean, you beat him for the Television strap. It isn't as though you need to pay him back. It isn't like you need to prove yourself against him. You've already beaten him in a high-stakes match. Headlined the show. Wowed the crowd, and gotten the attention of the NFW brass. All of which leaves me wondering why you were so adamant about facing him. ::THE WOLF hops down from the turnbuckle and stalks to the center of the ring:: THE WOLF: Well, Temp, I'm sorry to dissapoint you. You won't be facing Mister Dread now, or ever. Mister Dread doesn't exist any more. There's only the Wolf. Only the hunter. But if you long to become the prey, then by all means. I'd be more than happy to face you. The Wolf will settle the score that Mister Dread couldn't. The hell that Dread put you through at Impact ... well, I thought he was taking it easy on you. You won't get any such concession from me. THE WOLF: I've got to hand it to you though, my man. Just the fact that you're willing ... hell, that you're demanding a rematch shows a lot of guts. A lot of guts. I wonder how they'll look when I strew them around the ring ... ::FADE TO BLACK:: [the thread's next post — 22 Nov 2002] (FADE IN on the gym, it's a rainy night, and only the lights over the ring are on. TEMPESTA is doing a handstand in the center of the ring, doing pushups with his back to the camera. He pauses, spreads his legs into a perfect split, and then lowers himself to the ground. Still sitting in a split, he addresses the camera) TEMPESTA: "Wolf, the crazy little man I've let loose with the beating I gave Dread doesn't worry me in the least. Simply put, my match with Dread put BOTH of us on the map, BOTH of us into the spotlight. It's good business for us to meet again, and I did promise you a rematch." (As TEMPESTA gets up and hops over the ropes landing lightly on his feet outside the ring, the camera follows him. There is a loud THUNK as the relays for the rest of the gym lights are powered up, and we soon see TEMPESTA standing in front of a set of concrete patio tiles.) TEMPESTA: "Now, some folks like to say that these things don't hit back, but I say, you don't know just how much damage you can do unless you break a few of these things." (TEMPESTA stands before a stack of eight tiles, quickly raises his leg, and smashes all eight) TEMPESTA: "You see, I know that breaking just one of these is like breaking a man's leg. So, imagine how much I was holding back when I fought you at last." (TEMPESTA moves to the right, and we see three neat stacks of six each, side by side) TEMPESTA: "So, while you can dodge, jump and twirl to your heart's content, I'm going to concentrate on one thing, and one thing only..." (Tempesta squats in a deep horse stance, raises his right hand over his head) TEMPESTA: "Breaking... " He smashes the first set of tiles TEMPESTA: "Your..." He smashes the second set of tiles TEMPESTA: "Spirit!" He smashes the third set of tiles (Tempesta stands up, looks at all three piles of newly minted rubble, and then looks right into the camera and smiles evilly) TEMPESTA: "But honestly, I'll settle for just destroying your body." (Tempesta draws his thumb across his throat as we FTB) [the thread's next post — 24 Nov 2002] (OOC EDIT: I suppose that since we don't know if Tempesta still has the TV title after the triple threat match at Futureshock, disregard that part about a title shot. It's implied that if Temp still holds the belt, the Wolf will recieve a shot at it.) ::FADEIN on the NFW banner backstage. THE WOLF stands there, attired as usual:: THE WOLF: Oh please. Do you think that some paltry show of force is going to put me off? Slow me for even a second? Not a chance, Tempesta. You can break all the tiles you want, and you can tell yourself that it's just the same as breaking the Wolf. Go ahead and further your delusions. THE WOLF: I've learned enough martial arts to know that you're good. And I've been through enough punishment at your hands to know that you're hard. But you're fooling yourself if you think that that scares me in the slightest. You've conned yourself into thinking that the events at IMPACT! resulted in the emergence of the Wolf, but you were inconsequential in that. That was purely Jason Payne. You're just gonna be lucky enough to reap the dubious rewards. ::The camera comes in close on the blazing, feral eyes of THE WOLF:: You say you're gonna break my body, del Fuerte ... well, it's been broken before, more times than I can count. It always mends, though. My spirit, however ... that's another story. My spirit cannot, WILL not be broken. Not by the likes of you. Not by Jason Payne. Not by any of the other turds swirling around in the NFW toilet bowl. You can count on that, my arrogant friend. Your arrogance is going to cost you. Your overconfidence is your weakness. Something that I will exploit without fail, without mercy, without hesitation. My question to you is this, Tempesta: Do you have the guts to put your strap on the line? Will you give the Wolf the chance that Mister Dread was too weak to sieze? Do you have the courage? ::FADE TO BLACK::
