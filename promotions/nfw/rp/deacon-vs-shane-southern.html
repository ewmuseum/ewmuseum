Deacon vs. Shane Southern

11 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STIPULATION: 30 Minute Ironman Match [the thread's next post — 13 Feb 2004] "Then you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free." - John 8:32 {{...FADE-IN: Shane Southern is sitting in his Bourbon Street apartment talking on the phone. Night has fallen once again, but instead of enjoying the night life, Shane Southern is shut off from the world. Not only physically, but mentally. He sits on his black leather couch dressed in an old "Party's Over" t-shirt, and grey sweats. He sits, unmoving, lost in thought of the secret he's hidden from the world....well, most of the world anyway. ... Suddenly the ring of the telephone breaks him out of his trance and he picks the phone up...}} SHANE SOUTHERN: " Yeah? ... Yeah, I'm sure. ... ... Look, we've been over this, it's time. ... ... OK, yeah....somebody will pick you up from tha' airport. ... Uh... (glances up at the camera) I can't. ... ... Ok. ... Bye. " (Southern hangs up the phone and straightens himself out. He doesn't want to do this, but hey, who ENJOYS cutting promos?) " Well Deacon ... let me be one of tha' very FIRST ta' welcome you ta' NFW. Can't say I'm HAPPY ta' see you, 'cause in all honesty, you're just gonna' make it that much HARDER for me ta' achieve my ultimate goal. But who BETTER ta' face tha' night I confess mah' sins to tha' world? " (Southern stands up and walks over to the glass door that separates the balcony from the apartment, he stares out into the night before he continues) " Ya' know Deac'...I've followed yer career, from CSWA World Champion, ta' stricken by MS, ta' a miraculous recovery. It's quite tha' story...n' hey, I can understand why yer here. What better way ta' cap off yer comeback by walkin' home with tha' Ultra-title? It's ah' story made in heaven, eh' Deac? One problem though...you've got SINNERS like me...guys that'll do ANYTHING ta' get that belt. Can you say tha' same Deacon? " " I was once like you Deacon. Tha' wrestlin' world was some fairy tale place where I thought I could be tha' KING of tha' mountain...while stayin' true ta' what I believed in. But I've learned that you can't do that in this sport. You CAN'T be a CONTINUED success without compromising yerself...well...at least EYE can't. " " I know yer comin' at me at CRASH. I know you wanna' show tha' WORLD that YOU, n' not Eddy Love are tha' MAIN attraction now. What better way ta' get people ta' notice you AND yer message by pinnin' SHANE SOUTHERN, tha' NFW's poster child? Ta' recover from your illness, and go THIRTY minutes with one of tha' BEST technical wrestlers in this business? Yeah, it's ah' good story. " " But it ain't gonna' have a happy endin' Deac. I'm comin' after ya' man...like you've never seen before...both barrels blazin'. Can you take tha' heat Deacon? " " If not, you better stay outta' HELL...'cause right now, that's where I'm at. Care ta' join me? " " Party's OVER. " {{...FADE OUT...}} [the thread's next post — 15 Feb 2004] The background faded from black to a single spotlight shining around Chris Shepherd. CS: 30 minutes. An ironman match. Our first foray into the NFW and someone kicked us into the deep end - going 30 with a Southerner... maybe we could make this a precursor for our eventual collision with the man everyone wants to see in the ring with Deacon - Eddy Love. Maybe we can dominate as we did in the days before MS... before Mike Randalls... before ... before... before. He stopped for a moment, his lips curling into a smile. CS: Or perhaps we’ll say that Eddy Love will come in time. God tells us to let tomorrow come on its own... to keep Him first and everything else will work out. Well, Shane... God is first, and he’s put us with you for 30 minutes of sheer ... ‘hell’? With a shrug, Chris continued. CS: If that’s what it takes. You say you were once like us? Do you mean a ‘hero’? A ‘face’? The guy that the crowd rallies behind? Perhaps you were... perhaps you found a better way now, where people don’t matter and you don’t have to answer for your actions. But you WILL answer for them, Shane... just like Eddy will when ‘tomorrow’ comes. You tell us that you’ll do ‘anything’ to win? Please do... give us every dirty ‘southern’ trick you have. Test us. Burn us. Take us to the end of your rope... and then realize that you still got 25 minutes to stay in the ring with a 7 foot man who is HERE to show that the people who do whatever it takes to win never do... but in life, he IS what it takes to win. It’s not being the best technical wrestler, though that helps. It’s not doing ‘anything’ to get the ‘W’, though you’ll probably try. What it TAKES to win... is faith. And the first time you do ‘anything to win’, you’ll show us that you have NO faith. You’ll show us that you know you’re not as technically sound as you say. You’ll show us that the glare of the Ultratitle has blinded you to who you WERE and what truly matters in life. And you’ll show us that if we stick with what has brought us this far - through a run as CSWA World champ, through a war of words with Eddy Love, through MS, and through not only walking into that ring... but walking - PERIOD. You’ll show us that if we have the Faith to make it through 30 minutes that... He walked out of the light. CS: The Evidence ... and the ‘happy ending’ will take care of itself. The light turned off. [the thread's next post — 18 Feb 2004] You cannot change the truth, but the truth can change you. - Unknown {{...FADE-IN: Shane Southern is standing in front of an NFW backdrop...arms folded in front of him. He's shirtless with jeans and cowboy boots. He smirks at the camera before he continues...}} SHANE SOUTHERN: " Shepherd, I think ya' might want ta' sit down n' do ah' little homework before ya' send ol' Deac out....'cause it's quite clear you don't know me at all. " " First of all, if all you think I got is FIVE minutes worth, I think you better pray to tha' good lord up ah'bove fer ah' brain. I can go all night long if need be. I suggest you put in tha' DVD of WEEK TWO and watch me not only GO thirty with Hornet, but beat tha' livin' hell out uv' him in tha' process. I suggest you watch at least SOME of mah' matches Chris, 'cause it's CLEAR you don't know ANYTHIN' ah'bout me. " " Ya' see Shepherd, I'm not tha' guy you just HEARD rumors ah'bout when Deacon was rulin' tha' CSWA. I'm HERE...right NOW, I'm right in fronta' yer FACE, starin' at ya'. I'm not some schmuck Deacon can manhandle and feed to tha' wolves. I'm tha' man that's takin' home tha' ULTRA-TITLE, I'm tha' MAN that's finally arrived in this sport, I'm tha' MAN that's finally seen what it takes ta' be a SUCCESS...n' Chris, I'm tha' MAN that's gonna' take APART tha' MUTE FREAK piece by God-lovin' piece. " " This ain't ah'bout face vs heel, that line was blurred YEARS ago. Tha' fans RESPECT what yer doin'?...They cheer, they don't...they boo. Pretty simple. I don't claim ta' be ah' heel, n' I don't claim ta' be a face. I'm just Shane Southern. Wrestler. I'm just a hard workin', no nonsense, IN YER FACE, do what it takes, SUN-UVA-***** that's tryin' ta' make ah' livin' doin' somethin' he LOVES. " " I'm not sure why yer here Deac'. But yer in tha' NFW now mah' man...there IS no warm-up match. You may WANT Eddy Love, n' hell, I'm SURE you'll get 'em soon enough. But HERE, in MY stompin' grounds, there'll be no just bidin' yer time until tha' moment comes. Each n' ever' week you gotta' step out that n' give it all you got... " " ... THAT'S what I'm expectin' from you Deac. For THIRTY minutes, give me ALL you got. " " n' whatever tha' results...." " Party's Over. Chad? Help!* ---------------------------------------------------------------------- (Opens with a segment from Shane Southern's last promo.) SHANE: ...First of all, if all you think I got is FIVE minutes worth... (Abruptly cuts to Chris Shepherd, dressed as usual, hair pulled back in a ponytail as usual, sitting in a coffee shop cause, well... not everything is usual.) Chris Shepherd: We don't know you at all, Shane... not one bit. It seems the Shane we remember isn't the Shane we see. That's ok, though... it doesn't concern us. Your past, just like your future, isn't our concern right now. (takes a sip) Our concern right now is 30 minutes. That's all. It's Not an Ultratitle that, quite frankly, we have little chance of attaining. We didn't really come here for Eddy Love, cause we, as most everyone else, didn't know that the 'highway' would lead to Eddy. We came for a chance to do what we do best... for a chance to show the world that the Faith we spoke of for our entire career can carry us through... and look what it carried us to? (another sip) Shane Southern - favorite to win the NFW South. I guess, when people see you, they see the guy that's carried the fight to everyone he's encountered... but Shane, you've NEVER encountered the Deacon. He doesn't want the title, Heaven knows he doesn't NEED a title. What's he NEED? (A waitress walks up to Chris, gesturing to his cup.) WAITRESS: More coffee? SHEPHERD: No thanks. (He looks back to the camera.) What's he need? To prove to the world, ONE MORE TIME, that God is bigger than the best, the most favored... and that if you only put your trust in him, He'll do the rest. (Chris pushes his cup away) Why bother showing up in the NFW at intermission, when the title is all but decided? Because we all know that the best part of the show is ALWAYS the 2nd half... everything else is setup for the grand finale. I've watched enough NFL to know that the teams that are the most dangerous can be the teams that have nothing to gain... or lose, by giving everything they got. Tomorrow doesn't matter cause tomorrow isn't there. (Chris stands, leaning with one hand on the table.) Like I said in the beginning of all this, tomorrow will take care of itself. Eddy Love will take care of itself. And that title that you're coveting? It's gonna drive you... but it won't budge us because we're not here for that - we're here... for Him (points up). And He's given us what they say is the best of the South... and we got the faith to believe that... Shane? Chris walks passed the camera) "Those 5 minutes are almost up."
