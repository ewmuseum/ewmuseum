we're gonna changethe way we think...

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene- A shoreline, the Powell brothers are SLOWLY walking out of the water and onto the beach. They both tear off their lifevests from their swim and collapse on the sand. Even from the cameras vantage point approximately 25-30 feet away, the exhaustion on their faces are evident. JT Tyler pulls up to them, he’s on a four wheeler ATV, as the camera zooms in.) TYLER: You guys have a nice swim? ROGER: (Breathing exhorbitantly heavy) I’m (pant)…gonna kick (pant)…. your (pant)….(censored) (censored) (pant). SCOTT: (Breathing exhorbitantly heavy as well) how far did we swim? (Pants)…A mile? Two? TYLER: Six actually, standard warm-up for your type of training. ROGER: (Still panting) STANDARD WARM-UP? TYLER: Don’t worry, you’ll get used to it, besides you’re going to need it when we start doing the hard stuff. SCOTT: (forcing himself to sit up) It’s going to get tougher? TYLER: Well, the good news is you made it in time for lunch, you have time to eat. ROGER: Where’s the food? (Slowly sits up as well) TYLER: (points off camera) right over that ridge, just about a mile away… SCOTT: Well, where’s our ride? TYLER: (laughs) Ride? You gotta run! ROGER: We just swam six miles! TYLER: …and just think how easy a mile run’ll be. (Speeds off) See you there! SCOTT: How come he has a four-wheeler? Why can’t he run? ROGER: Forget it…I’m not running…. (The camera zooms in on four figures coming out of the water behind them, they were assigned to follow the Powell brothers in case they had trouble<i.e. the bubble trail> They then pull out weapons from their packs and take aim at the two brothers) SCOTT: Ummm Roger…I think we should run…. ROGER: Give me one good reason why! (SFX gunshot) (sands jumps into the air right next to Rogers legs) (The brothers nod and start running off into the distance as the scene fades out)
