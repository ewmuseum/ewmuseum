WEEK 10 - JONATHAN MARX vs. GABRIEL POE

17 Mar 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::Marx is lying in a hospital bed in a hospital gown and messed up hair, recovering from the injuries inflicted on him by the group attack of Dan Ryan and DC Stratton. Marx is sleeping and the scene slowly fades into a dream sequence:: ::Marx finds himself standing bewildered behind a house with a barn at its side, when all of a sudden music players:: (CUEUP: Theme From Mister Ed) JONATHAN MARX: ::Marx looks around nervously:: Where is all that confounded singing coming from? Where the hell am I? ::Marx walks around and notices his reflection in the Colonel’s new car which reflects back Wilbur’s face:: Oh boy. ::Carol rushes out to give Wilbur a big kiss:: CAROL: Where have you been? I’ve been calling you for dinner for over twenty minutes. MARX AS WILBUR: Sorry, I haven’t been quite feeling myself. (CUEUP: Laugh Track) ::Marx looks around to see where the noise is coming from:: CAROL: What is wrong dear? MARX AS WILBUR: Can’t you hear it? CAROL: Hear what? Are you feeling okay? MARX AS WILBUR: I’m not feeling too well. I’m going to go into the stable how Ed is doing. CAROL: Okay dear, but if you don’t come to dinner soon, I’m going to go out to the stable and drag you into this house. MARX AS WILBUR: Don’t worry. I feel perfectly okay. ::kisses Carol on the forehead and walks away looking back accidently stepping on a rake left on the ground:: (CUEUP: Laugh Track) MARX AS WILBUR: ::looks around for where the sound is coming from while rubbing his nose:: CAROL: Dear, Are you sure you are okay? MARX AS WILBUR: Sometimes it just doesn’t pay to get out of bed. ::Marx as Wilbur walks into the stable and opens up the top half of the door:: MARX AS WILBUR: Ed, I need your help, I need to get back so I can train for my match against Gabriel Poe. I’m Jonathan Marx and I’m trapped in the body of Wilbur. MISTER ED: (with the voice of Kevin Smith) I wish I was trapped in the body of Liv Tyler myself. MARX AS WILBUR: KEVIN SMITH! What is the voice of Kevin Smith doing in the body of Mister Ed?! MISTER ED:: ::dances:: The Cha Cha. (CUEUP: Laugh Track) MARX AS WILBUR: Where is damn laugh track coming from and why is Kevin Smith doing the voice of Mister Ed?! MISTER ED: I’m here to plug my new movie Jersey Girl, coming to theaters next Friday, March 26th featuring Ben Affleck and Liv Tyler. J-Lo is barely in it, we promise. MARX AS WILBUR: I’m sure it will be fantastic and I’ll go to see if I can ever get the hell out of here. But it doesn’t really solve my problem, why am I trapped in the body of Wilbur on the television show Mister Ed? MISTER ED: (voice of Kevin Smith): Don’t ask me, I got here ten minutes before you did. I was just taking a nap on my couch and I woke up a horses ass. I’m beginning to think Mewes spiked my drink. ::an Interdimensional Portal opens up and Brandon Jacobs, dressed in bad eighties clothes, walks out carrying some sort of hand held device:: MARX AS WILBUR: Brandon, what are you doing here? You aren’t even in this league! (CUEUP: Laugh Track) ::Jonathan Marx, Brandon Jacobs, and Kevin Smith all look around for the blasted source of annoying laughter:: BRANDON JACOBS AS AL: I’m a figment of your imagination. Apparently both you and Kevin were given too much drugs, don’t worry, you didn’t overdose, but both you guys are tripping like mad. MISTER ED: (voice of Kevin Smith): Damn that Mewes. MARX AS WILBUR: I don’t even remember taking any drugs. BRANDON JACOBS AS AL: The NFW admitted you to the hospital for observation for a couple of days after you were attacked by Ryan, Stratton, and Avery because they thought you might have a slight concussion. The nurses slipped you some pain medication because you were in a great deal of pain. MARX AS WILBUR: There goes my straight edge lifestyle. But that still doesn’t explain why I am here as Wilbur… BRANDON JACOBS AS AL: In order to leap, you have to wash down Mister Ed. Apparently when you lost to Ryan, you changed the time line and now Mr. Ed dies during the filming of the third season. MARX AS WILBUR: How did losing Dan Ryan change the time line? Wait, I have to give a sponge bath to Kevin Smith? What am I on? MISTER ED: (voice of Kevin Smith): ::smiles at Marx:: (CUEUP: Laugh Track) MARX AS WILBUR: ::disgusted:: I graduated from Princeton damn it! If I wanted to scrub down Kevin Smith, I would have jumped into the body of Ben Affleck! MISTER ED: (voice of Kevin Smith): Hey now… BRANDON JACOBS AS AL: There is nothing perverted about it at all. You are his keeper and Mister Ed is a horse. Once you wash him down, you’ll leap out of here. MARX AS WILBUR: I guess I have to… wait, where will I leap to next? BRANDON JACOBS AS AL: Eventually the medication has to wear off and you’ll be back home. MARX AS WILBUR: I swear, if I don’t leap back home and I’m doing this all for nothing, I’m going to beat you beyond all recognition. BRANDON JACOBS: For once, trust me on this one. MARX AS WILBUR: I guess I will have to. But Kevin, you better not enjoy this! MISTER ED: (voice of Mister Ed): Scout’s Honor… ::Mister Ed winks at the camera:: ::the camera pull out of the scene and focus on the clock, a clock is shown in fast forward and over two hours passes, the cameras fade back into the scene Wilbur’s shirt is all wet and Mister Ed is totally clean:: MARX AS WILBUR: There, I’m done. Can’t you avoid splashing? I’m totally drenched and I smell like horse crap. MISTER ED: (voice of Kevin Smith): Can’t you stop whining? I think there is a little dirt on the back of my hind leg, could you clean it off? MARX AS WILBUR: Fine, fine, the sooner I clean you up, the sooner I am out of here. ::Marx as Wilbur walks around to the back of Wilbur and checks Mister’s Ed’s hind right leg:: MARX AS WILBUR: I can’t seem to find it. MISTER ED: (voice of Kevin Smith): Oh, you will… ::Mister Ed shoots his hind leg back at the head of Marx but Marx leaps out of the body of Wilbur before he can connection. Carol rushes into the stable as Marx's vision fades into darkness:: ::Marx finds himself in a strange bed and sighs thankfully to himself that he is back at the hospital, but he begins to look around and he appears to be in a bedroom set in the 1950s, he sits up in bed and looks in the mirror, unable to identify his own face:: ::Marion Cunningham pokes her head into the room:: MRS. CUNNINGHAM: Come on Chuck, it is time to wake up. MARX AS CHUCK CUNNINGHAM: Oh boy. FTB
