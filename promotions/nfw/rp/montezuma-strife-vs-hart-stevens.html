Montezuma/Strife vs. Hart/Stevens

24 Nov 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADE IN: 'The Phenom' SHAWN HART, sporting a t-shirt reading 'Hart Throb' with an arrow pointing downard, as well as a pair of vintage Girbaud jeans, stands at somebody's front porch with a sullen expression. As he stands before the front door, seemingly waiting to be greeted, he miraculously breaks into a promo. Goodie, goodie...) Hart: "So it's finally happened..." (Hart lowers his head in disappointment.) Hart: "First I lose my spiritual advisor to blatantly homo-erotic behavior, NEXT I somehow manage to get myself SCREWED out of a week 6 victory, mangling my melon in the process, and FINALLY... the icing on the cake, if you will, I get booked in an unamazingly lametastic match against a couple of washed-up JACKHOLES with Phenom Lite as my tag team partner!!! In other words, after years of riding high like Method Man on a Clydesdale, it's FINALLY happened... The Phenom has finally ended up with the SHORT END of the stick!!" (He shakes his head slowly. He then looks directly into the camera, gritting his teeth angrily.) Hart: "A travesty indeed, but make NO mistake about it... The Phenom WILL PREVAIL, The Phenom WILL win that Ultratitle, and come next week, the Phenom WILL defeat Strife and Montezuma!! Hell, once the match is over, I'll kick Stevens' ass too... just for KICKS! Mark my wonderously articulate words as they spew forth from these electrifying lips... Shawn Hart has been collectively WRONGED by the people of this promotion and, with GOD as my witness, I will have my VENGEANCE!!!" (The Phenom turns to the door, knocks loudly three times, then continues.) Hart: "This week's victims? Castor Strife... and Armando Montezuma. Generation X's rendition of Steve Guttenburg, and a man who I'm PRETTY sure is that latino guy from the coffee cans. Y'know, the one with the donkey? Anyhow, the prologue to this pairing is a tragic tale indeed. A tandem NOT born out of circumstance or mutual respect, but out of dyslexia. That's CASTOR, Mr. Montezuma... NOT CASTRO!!" (He snickers. What a clever witticism he has devised.) Hart: "Y'know... cuz he's CUBAN?" (You with me yet, people?) Hart: "Heh, CASTRO... get it?" (Simply delightful...) Hart: "Aaaaaaaat any rate, the pit of the peach here is that EVERYBODY knows that the ONLY good thing ever to come out of Cuba is their wonderously fat, downright DELICIOUS cigars. This being so, 'Mando... you may not be an illegal tobacco product, but when we're in that ring-a-ding, I'll SMOKE you just the same. Call it an outrage, call it a mockery, MY magic 8-ball says it's the TRUTH! BOO-yah, Billy-bob!" (The Phenom licks his palm, runs his hand through his hair, then strikes an Elvis-like pose. He then shoots us one of those fake cheerleader smiles from back in high school, collects himself, and resumes the speech.) Hart: "As for Strife, the thespian turned lesbian, sweating you I am most certainly NOT. Funny, if you take the first three letters of your name... C-A-S, flip them around, S-A-C, then just forget about the T, the O, and the R at the end, you've got SAC, my friend! Now, the reason THAT one's funny is because you don't got SAC at all! You are SAC-less, my friend. Like a grocery store check-out gone TERRIBLY wrong. Conversely, if you take my LAST name, H-A-R-T, and remove the R, you've got yourself a HAT. It may be 10 gallons, or perhaps it's a baseball cap, but either way... it doesn't change the fact that I'm gonna kick your ass! Besides..." (He looks to the door.) Hart: "With the help of my NEW mentor..." (Said door SWINGS open, revealing none other than CSWA champion, DAN RYAN.) Hart: "The Phenom will become UNSTOPPABLE!!!!!" (Ryan glares at the Phenom, annoyed and unimpressed.) Hart: "So uhh, hey... you gonna come out and give me some pointers now?" Ryan: "Yeah. How about....you get you and your crap out of my yard before that tentpole ends up wrapped around your head?" (CUT TO: A tent, pitched by Hart in Ryan's front yard. Inside, remnants of a week's worth of camping can be seen. Cut back to the Phenom and Dan Ryan.) Hart: "Wait a minute, is this some kind of test?" Ryan: "Leave.... now...." Hart: "Ok, well.. it looks like you'er busy at the moment, so maybe I'll try again in a couple hours! Does that work for..." (Ryan abruptly slams the door in Hart's face.) Hart: "...you?" (Hart scratches his chin and looks toward the heavens, pensively.) Hart: "Alright, champ... I guess I'll see you later! As for my opponents, the Douche-bag Brothers, be afraid... be VERY afraid!! " (He looks into the camera.) Hart: "The PHENOM... has left... the BUILDING!!!" (FADE OUT.) [the thread's next post — 26 Nov 2003] (FADE IN: To a gloriously decorated bathroom, shimmering with brass fixtures, marble countertops, and the most expensive tile flooring. There, in the large jacuzzi tub, sporting a pink shower cap and playing with the bath bubbles, is 'The Phenom' SHAWN HART. As the camera comes in for a close-up of the man, a David Bowie hit is heard booming in the background...) Hart: "Thhhhhhis is Groooooound Controooooooool to MAJOR TOM..." (In the spirit of Santa Clause, he begins sculpting a bubble-beard of epic proportions.) Hart: "You've really maaaaade the graaaaaa-aaaaaade!!!" (His operatic stylings reverberate throughout the room.) Hart: "And the paaaapers want to knoooow whose shirts you weeeeeee-eeeear..." (The Phenom grabs a large scrub brush and begins to work on his back, momentarily, as he sits upright. He then brings the brush to his lips.) Hart: "Now it's tiiime to leave the caapsuuule if you daaaaa-aaaaaaaaaare..." (Suddenly, Hart takes notice of the strange cameramen prowling about his bathroom!) Hart: "GOOD GOD!!" (As Hart throws his arms into the air, shocked and surprised, the scrub brush and bath bubbles aplenty are sent hurling out of the tub. As the music comes to a sudden halt, the screeching of vinyl is heard. Hart immedeately begins to cover his chest with bubbles.) Hart: "I know you guys want an interview here, but for God's sake, man... do I LOOK like MARY CAREY?!" (As the Phenom's bubble chest perks out exponentially, and his pink shower cap shines in the bathroom's bright light, a look of horror covers his face.) Hart: "Scratch that! Don't answer answer that question. Anyhow, if it's a sound bite you're looking for, you've come to the right place, my friend! *sigh* ...And I suppose now is as good a time as any!" (Shawn removes his shower cap. As he shakes the water off of his flowing blonde locks, we go to a slow-motion camera effect, enabling us to see each and every little bead of water as it shoots from his hair, face, and mouth. Upon ending his dog-like gyrations, we return to real time... and Hart's puffed-out mane.) Hart: "Well, I could tell you all about this kidney stone I've been contending with, but would I be correct in assuming you'd rather here my schpiel on this match I have with Montezuma and Strife?" (The camera moves from up to down and over again. Hart acknowledges, struggles to clear his throat, then switches to rant mode.) Hart: "................." (He scratches his chin in astonishment.) Hart: "Nope, I got nuthin'.... CUE MUSIC!!" (Bowie resumes.) Hart: "For heeeeeeeeeeeeere... Am I sitting in a tin caaaaaaaaan..." (Without warning, he bolts upward and into a standing position. With his arms extended, and a bevy of bubbles covering his midsection, he pours his heart into the music!) Hart: "FAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAR above the world..." (He closes his eyes, and continues his heart-felt singing.) Hart: "Plaaaanet earth is bluuuue, and there's nothing I can dooooooooooo..." (Commense guitar strumming.) Hart: "Dun-DUN dun-dun, dun-dun... *CLAP! CLAP!*" (He grins with satisfaction.) Hart: "Heh, ok... sorry, about that. What can I say... I LOVE that part!! .......Buuuuut you know what I don't love, cameraman?!" (The camera moves from left to right and over again.) Hart: "Washed-up WANKERS who think they can duck me all week, then just waaaaaaltz right into the show and have themselves a wrestling match. I mean, like it or not, this ain't no ham n' egger you guys got.... it's LE PHENOM in LE FLESH! And make no mistake about it, I've eaten CHUNKS of ORANGE CHICKEN bigger than you JACKHOLES!! So if you think you can simply MAIL IT IN and still come out on top, you've got another thing comin', kiddies! Call it an OUTRAGE, call it a MOCKERY, like it or not... ¡Es el verdad, foolios!" (He returns to a sitting position in the tub. After grabbing a rubber ducky from the soap dish, then giving it a gentle push into the water, he looks back to the camera and continues.) Hart: "So here's the deal, people, and I'm only gonna say this once, so LISTEN UP!! CASTOR STRIFE, get out of that dressing room, remove yourself from your trailer, and don't worry about your make-up girl!! You're 40 years old, but you can play 27... WE GET IT!! ARMANDO MONTEZUMA, quit reading Karl Marx and get your Gloria Estefan CD back in its case! Whether you want to or not, you've got to actually SHOW UP to make this thing interesting! With Stevens as my partner there's only SO MUCH I can do to save the quarter hour! Hart does equal RATINGS, but when I'm SURROUNDED by underachievers, I need all the help I can get! Sure, I'll come out with the win either way, but for the sake of avoiding assault charges, the LEAST you could do is put up a fight! I'd like to think the lot of you can recover here so that SHAWN HART can steal the show, but needless to say... I won't be holding my breath. CUE MUSIC!!" (CUE MUSIC: 'Whip It' by Devo.) "Cuh-RACK THAT WHIP!!" Hart: "Here we go, NNNNNNNNNNDADDIO!! The PHENOM... has left... the building!!" (FADE OUT.)
