WEEK 10 - CHRISTIAN SANDS vs. EVAN AHO

16 Mar 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: Somewhere backstage, where a rather annoyed-looking CHRISTIAN SANDS sits on a steel chair, the jacket of his black tracksuit half-unzipped.) Sands: Something is seriously wrong here. Avery Prosser, you son of a b*tch. I tried to stay out of your way and let you and your little Seven Dwarves Squadron have your way with the North, because I honestly don't give a sh*t what you do. But when you and your pack of THUGZ and chain gang rejects starts costing me matches without any apparent reason, I start to give a little more than a sh*t. NOBODY costs me matches, Avery, unless they first sign a waiver certifying that they don't mind being ultimately mauled. I still don't care about what happens to NFW, but understand this. If any of your boys try to cost me a match again, they will not live to see another sunrise. I've already arranged for an armed bodyguard unit to be in attendance for my next match. Let's see who's willing to run interference when there are guys with guns guarding the place. See? It pays to be rich; you can throw money at a problem and find a solution. Speaking of which... Obviously I am not a happy man this week. In fact, I'm extremely angry at having been made a fool of by a bunch of intellectual cripples who can't comprehend the idea of letting decade-old grudges slide. With that in mind, I certainly wouldn't want to be in YOUR shoes, Evan Aho. I've got my share of rage to vent this week, and it looks like you're the outlet for it. Then again, you HAVE become NFW's resident b*tch boy recently, so I'm sure the beating I'll be handing you at Week 10 won't come as a surprise. But who knows? Maybe you'll grow some gonads and actually bring out your long-lost talent. Whatever happened to that stuff, anyway? From CSWA World Champion to curtain-jerking b*tch. What a catharsis of talent, Evan! Amazing! But whatever. I don't give a sh*t what you bring to the table, because I'm destroying you anyway. I should have walked out of Week 9 with ten points, but the Seven Dwarves Squadron denied me that - so this week I make up the difference. And this week, NOTHING... NOTHING will stand in my way. And that's the truth. (FADEOUT)
