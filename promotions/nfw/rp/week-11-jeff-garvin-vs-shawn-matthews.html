WEEK 11: JEFF GARVIN vs. SHAWN MATTHEWS

11 Apr 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN to Shawn Matthews, in his hotel room. He's typing on his laptop computer, as the camera looks over his shoulder..) Dear NFW, I'd like to thank all of you for the opportunity to compete in the quest for the Ultratitle. I've been having more fun in professional wrestling than I've had for quite some time. The first half of the season, I was in the ring with guys I idolized growing up. However, more recently I've been put in the ring with guys who, well to put it bluntly, just don't match up very well with Showtime. Steel Viper, he was good at one time, but he clearly doesn't have it anymore. And he had the gall to attack me after the match. Whatever, I can deal with that. Chris McMillan, I never believed his hype, and made that apparent a few weeks ago. But even so, he was at least a well respected wrestler at one point. This brings me to my next point. Jeff Garvin? What in god's name has this guy done recently, if ever? While it's great to gain more and more points every week, I'd like to at least be doing it against the big dogs. You know I've got it in me, as I've proven often over the past 10 weeks. Well, I'll take what I can get, and I'll just have to show Jeff Garvin exactly what I'm all about. Week 11, I knock Jeff Garvin out of the NFW Ultratitle tournament, for good. Please take everything into consideration, and put me in there with one of the big guys next time. Thank you for your time. Sincerely, Shawn Matthews (FADEOUT, as Matthews hits send on the e-mail..)
