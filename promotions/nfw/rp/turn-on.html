Turn On

25 May 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: The front pillars of the Lower Merion Country Club's clubhouse. DORCHESTER STRATTON stands in front -- his hair Brylcremed to Don Draper-style perfection. He has on a heavily starched white dress shirt, a pink tie dotted with grey elephants and a tight Double Windsor knot, a blue blazer and firmly creased khaki pants -- all from Jos. A Bank's 'Dorchester Satin Summer Cape Cod Cool' line. VERONICA ABRAMS RUMSFELD stands in front, with a wicker hat on top of her head, wearing Nancy Pelosi-style beads, and a black sleeveless dress from Jean Paul Gaultier. Dorchester looks stern faced in the back as Rumsfeld smirks.) RUMSFELD: "Just saying your name disgusts me to the point of near bulimia, Jack Bryant. But I have to say it. I just do. You see, it was just a few days ago when you dared to BACKJUMP my shmoopie here, Dorchester Stratton. We couldn't even make it to the ring without your true-blue cowardly ways showing. Well, Jack, we have something to tell you." (Stratton fixes his tie.) STRATTON: "Jack Bryant, I've been trying to NOT go there. I've been trying soooooo hard to not go into that place I went to when I destroyed Tsunami. When I destroyed Rayne. When I destroyed WildStar. When I spat in his wife's face. And when I destroyed Joe The Plumber's career. I've tried to get right to that line -- right where I'm still in control but feel right on the edge of going into that dark place... where I can see how easy it is to take away a man's livelihood yet still know the joys of driving this week's new Jaguar... Veronica has HELPED me get there. We've met with sports psychologists who have helped me get to that line. She's told me about that line." (Dorchester gulps.) "But after Lowell..." (Veronica brushes her hair back.) RUMSFELD: "After Lowell, Jack. We went back and reviewed Dorchester's greatest hits. The destruction he caused. The way he ended livelihoods. The way he broke a man. I haven't wanted him to GO to that place. I've wanted him to stay in control when he faces you. But watching him enter that dark part of his soul... well, I know I'm a woman of Main Line polite society... and it reminded me of why Dorchester's the only man who has... and will... ever SATISFY me." (Dorchester chuckles.) RUMSFELD: "Jack Bryant, it turns me ON knowing that one day, you'll be a bloodied carcass lying at his feet. I, as the executive secretary of The Carlton Trusts, was the first in our troupe to see the POTENTIAL in Dorchester. How I could clean him up. How I could cut his hair. How I could put him in REAL clothes. I convinced Calvin. And Blaine. And Malik. They realized how GOOD he was... how he was being used by our social lessers. And when we finally connected... it was the best day of my life. But I haven't realized WHY until now." (Dorchester adjusts his suit jacket lapel and smirks.) RUMSFELD: "Because it's when he goes into that place he fears to go to... he ends careers. And now? Now I WANT him to go there. Because that's how he's going to break your spinal column. That's how he's going to detach your retina. That's how he's going to fracture your ankle in many, many places. And I get to watch... and realize that I GAVE HIM THE PERMISSION to do that. That I ALLOWED him to go to that place in his devious, cunning mind. That I..." (She looks back at Dorchester who peers back at her.) RUMSFELD: "That he can't control himself when he goes there... but *I* CONTROL HIM. And since I control him... I control you. Let's go inside, Dorchester. I need an Arnold Palmer." (Rumsfeld steps back. Dorchester fans himself and winks.) DORCHESTER: "She's the boss, Jack." (FTB)
