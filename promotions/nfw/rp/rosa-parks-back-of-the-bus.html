Rosa Parks, Back Of The Bus

8 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUE UP: A freeze frame portrait of Rosa Parks, as Outkast's song of the same title plays in the background. CUT TO: Calvin Carlton, sitting behind his desk, wearing his suit, holding Sweet Betty Lee.) CALVIN: Avery Prosser, you can say all you want about the NFW. You can saw all you want about Quentin Sullivan, Craig Miles, whomever. But let me give you a recent history lesson. Three years ago, I was an intern in this promotion, using my boarding school pedigree and my momma's connections to get my foot in the door of this industry. I was a wild-eyed kid, sitting in the same room with the major stars of this industry, but I wasn't impressed. I saw things that could be changed, things that could be made better. No one thought I'd be more than some coffee boy, the kid who made the photocopies and answered the phones... but I knew that was not to be my destiny. I knew that this fledgling company needed a NEW VOICE around here I became this company's number one segment interviewer. All those wrestlers thought they could push me around, shove me to the ground, do what they want to me because I grew up the son of a woman of means, my sweet beautiful momma, who is as brilliant as the stars in the night sky. All of the wrestlers, well, they thought that I was some twerp who was only there to hold a microphone, that I wasn't ever going to be anyone of importance. Well, they pushed and pushed and pushed until I finally had enough and made my first deal to become a manager. WildStar, one of this sports all-time greats, wanted someone to watch his assets, and I signed onto the team. Well, guess what I did? Huh? I took WildStar, I took his alliance, and I took it over. He served me a purpose-- to get my foot in the door. As soon as he couldn't SERVE me anymore, I jettisoned him, took his foundation from under his rug and turned Five Star Enterprises into Calvin Carlton Enterprises-- and making MILLIONS of dollars at the same time, using what my boarding school pedigree taught me, making my momma happy at the same time. Then, Prosser, I sought ways to gain more power in this promotion. I did the research and I saw that the NFW World Tag Team titles could and would be my destiny, that I could control a major segment of this sport if I could find the right team. My first charges, The Oblivion Express, Resse and Cummins... guess what? THEY COULDN'T GET THE JOB DONE. I saw that they didn't have what it takes, so I cut them loose and sent them back to the greaveyard shift at the docks. I searched long and hard to find the right team and I found them. Frankie Fargo, Brandon Mueller-- the greatest tag team of the south in the 1980's, forgotten by mainstream wrestling fans, a new tag team using their name, coming off of lengthy prison sentences for crimes that they did not commit. I got them out on work-release, using my momma's lawyers, and sent them on their way. They thought they were too old, that I was too inexperienced, that the other teams in this league were simply better. Well, guess what? After two long years of brutal violence and human carnage... The Original ShowStoppers did what I said all along what they were going to do. They became the NFW World Tag Team champions. They sent their arch-rivals, The Showstopprs, back into the world of gay porn. And they sent the team people SWORE were the greatest of all-time, Hell Machine, out of this sport. Calvin Carlton, they said, was going to be a flash in the pan tag team manager. He wouldn't be of any importance. Well, guess what? My momma used her money and power to get me the job as the commissioner of the NFW South. I sat back, drafted who I wanted, forged a friendship with my boyhood hero Joey Melton along the way, and waited for the right opportunity. The opportunity is this... whoever wins the Ultratitle is going to be able to say that they are the greatest wrestler on the planet. That person will have a lot of power, a lot of influence and will be able to control a LOT of things in this sport. I, Calvin Carlton, intend on being able to determine who that person will be. My good, trusted personal friend E-Lo Eddy Love has made it clear that he will do what it takes to procure my foundation that title. I've sent notice to the rest of my conference that they have to do things MY way or they will see the HIGHWAY. Some have quit, some have threatened me... but the more they think about it... they'll agree with me. Why is that? Because this whole entire time, Calvin Carlton has been right. This entire time, Calvin Carlton and his momma were plotting on how to RULE this industry. Soon enough, that day will come. And when it does... you'd BETTER be on my good side. Avery Prosser, I'm not scared of you or your STD's. You want to take over the South? I dare you to try... because everyone who has tried to defeat me, thus far, has FAILED. MISERABLY. Prosser, you and your kind can cry and whine about the oppression all you want. You and your kind can pout and act like street hoodlums all you want. Your revolution FAILED the first go around and it's going to FAIL again. Deep down inside, each and every single member of your kind... all of your human herd animals... know that I'm your biggest threat. I'm a black man who doesn't take orders from you, the walking minstrel show. I'm a black man who is a member of high society, who has proven time and time again that he is elite... and you and your stable are nothing more than the products of crack-addicted welfare mothers who will always be at this society's lowest rung. By Any Means Necessary. Tell your sister. Tell your daughter. But don't tell my momma, 'cause she already knows. Rose Parks, she got off the bus. You and your kind? You're still trying to buy a ticket to get on board. Me? (Laughs.) I own the bus station. (FTB)
