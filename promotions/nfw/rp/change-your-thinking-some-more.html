Change your thinking some more

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene- the Powell Brothers are running through a wooded area, behind and in front of them is a wooded area. The exhaustion on their face is immeasurable, but as they are being followed, they press on. We are catching up to them approximately 45 minutes after the camera last left them behind. They come up on a pair of logs, propped above a pond of water. They balance themselves the best they can over the logs and go over them as JT Tyler pulls up in a four-wheeler.) TYLER: COME ON LADIES! MY GRANDMOTHER CAN MOVE FASTER THAN THIS! ROGER: (forcing out his words through the exhaustion) (Censored) you! SCOTT: (forcing out his words through the exhaustion as well) I thought you said you were gonna train us! TYLER: I have to make sure you can last throught the training first! (Speeds off) Pick up the pace, it’s about to get hot! (Explosions start popping up all over the place. As both Powells suddenly, and in almost all cases, understandably stop, the others run by them, running by the explosions, or simply dodging. Not wanting to be embarassed, or really look stupid, the brothers plod on.) (CUTTO- 10 minutes later. The Powell’s reach a warehouse building and while it looks up to date, as far as aesthetics goes, it appears almost deserted.) ROGER: (forcing out his words through the exhaustion) Is this the place? SCOTT: forcing out his words through the exhaustion as well) He said meet him at the warehouse, this looks to be the only one in the area. (Roger opens the door as they enter the building.. The back of the warehouse looks to be filled with a maze of cubicles. Tyler sits at a table in front of them. The Powells limp over to him.) SCOTT: I may be thoroughly exhausted, but I’m still gonna tear you apart! TYLER: (Putting his iced tea down and closing his laptop.) Is that any way to act? Especially when you passed your first test? SCOTT: All this was a friggin test? TYLER: Sure is…. ROGER: You mean…”Sure was”? (Tyler shakes his head “No”, as a team of Navy Seals barge into the building as pop-up targets appear in every spot imagineable in a warehouse, they fire at them. The Powell brothers have since dropped to the floor in horror as Tyler simply sits in his chair watching them. The SEAL Team surrounds them each of them shouting “clear”) ROGER: WHAT THE (censored) IS YOUR MAJOR MALFUNCTION? ARE YOU TRYING TO (Censored) KILL US? SCOTT: EXPLOSIONS? GUNS FIRING AT US? WHAT’S THE DEAL? TYLER: MY GAWD! YOU DON’T (Censored) GET IT DO YOU? THIS IS ALL ENDURANCE TRAINING! (Censored) IF THESE GUYS WANTED TO KILL YOU, THEY WOULD HAVE KILLED YOU LONG AGO! I’M GONNA TELL YOU RIGHT NOW, UNTIL YOU UNDERSTAND THAT THIS IS ABOUT YOU GETTING READY TO BE TRAINED BY ME, THEN YOU ARE GONNA HAVE TO KEEP DOING IT! (scene fades as Tyler storms off)
