WEEK 8: Gabriel Poe vs. Chris McMillan

20 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::FADEIN on the parking lot of the Electric Ink tattoo studio in Cadillac, Michigan. Leaning up against the side of a battered, dented 1969 Plymouth Fury is “The Wolf” Chris McMillan. McMillan is bundled against the cold in a thick buffalo hide leather jacket. He turns up the collar to shield his neck from the wind, and stuffs his hands into his pockets:: THE WOLF: So … week 8 is upon us. Some old faces, some new. Some at the top of the heap, and some festering at the very bottom. It hasn’t been a good month for me. For a little while, I was at the top of the heap. Oh, how I’ve tumbled since then. Each week I sit in the locker room and I tell myself that this is where I start turning things around. This is where I pull myself up by the bootstraps and climb that damn mountain again. And yet every week I fail to find a handhold. Every week I slide down before I even start coming back up. It’s disheartening, to say the least. Quite a few guys have already given up. Quite a few guys have already decided that it wasn’t worth the pain, the heartbreak, and the humiliation. They lost sight of what’s at the end of the road. The Ultratitle. THE WOLF: I know that I’ll probably never get a real shot at this. I know that I’ve slid so far down the mountain that a comeback is almost impossible. But I still haven’t lost sight of the prize. I see far, and I see clear to the end of the road. I know what I’m fighting for. I know what I’m doing here. Some men have already given up, and there are those on the fringe waiting to capitalize. There are those that circle like carrion birds, waiting to swoop in and fill a vacant spot in the roster. I’ve got a feeling that some of them are eyeing MY spot. Well, all of those who weren’t good enough to get in the first time, those that were late and missed the NFW train … you’re gonna have to keep waiting. My spot is going to remain filled through to the very end. It’s not in me to quit. It’s not in me to give up. I’m not that man. I will not hang up my boots and slink away with my tail between my legs. No, win or lose, I’ll be out there every week. I’d be out there every night, if I could. ::McMillan produces a smoke and a Zippo from the pockets of his jacket and lights up, a plume of smoke accompanying the vapor of his breath as he exhales:: THE WOLF: Now, the last couple of weeks haven’t been all that kind to “The Apocalypse” Gabriel Poe, either. I’ve seen you around, Poe. Heard your name in locker rooms all over the country. I was excited when I heard about this match. I always look forward to facing off with someone new. And it made me even happier when I saw that you seem to have hit a slump. That makes you hungry. Maybe makes you angry. Makes you want all the more to beat my head into the ground, to prove to yourself that this little slump is a fluke, not a trend. I want all you’ve got, Poe. Because if you don’t give me everything, then it doesn’t mean anything. I fell on my face, and I haven’t been able to pick myself up. Are you gonna keep me down, Poe? We’ll see. ::FADE TO BLACK::
