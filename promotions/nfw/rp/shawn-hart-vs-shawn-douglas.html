Shawn Hart vs. Shawn Douglas

11 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STIPULATION: 'THE PHENOM' nickname on the line. [the thread's next post — 20 Feb 2004] Darkness loomed over the area as a rich voice echoed in the stillness. "Alright, let's do this." When the cameras faded in from nothingness, the scene was stale. A blue, brick wall overlapped by a red banner proudly boasting the NFW logo in a gold hue with silver outline and underneath it, thick black letters formed the word NORTH, narrowing down involvement to few. However, when a young, ashy brown haired figure strolled into view, curiosity turned to confusion. "Who am I?" He started, "Who is Shawn Douglas and why is he here, in the NFW? Those were probably the first questions which sprang into your minds when I signed my contract, huh? Well, I'm all glad that some interest has come my way but right now, I have bigger things to handle and more important things to address. BUT... rest assured, those questions WILL be answered in due time." Douglas' hazel eyes gleamed towards the camera lens as he ran a stray hand in his hair, pushing back a few erratic strands to the side. "Let's not waste anyone's time and get to the reason I'm here..." Shawn Douglas grinned a half smile, showing his well-structured facial features. "Shawn Hart." SD pointed to himself, then to the camera. "It looks like me and you have been paired up for this week's Crash TV, Shawn. Now, should I be honored to face such a worthy competitor in my debut, or should I be worried? The fact of the matter is, I'm a little of both. You see, you're a former WFW World Champion, an Ultra Title Contender and damn near a technical master. But then again, you busted a dildo over your opponent's head." Douglas chuckled, "What this means to me, Shawn, is that my debut match... will be versus a champion, yes. It'll be versus a veteran, that too. But most of all, it'll be versus the most unpredictable human being on the NFW roster, my friend. Oh, but don't mistake me for one second either, Shawn. To this day, people question me. Is he ready? Can he withstand the pressure? Will he live up to the hype? The truth is... Yes. Yes I am, yes I can and yes, I damn sure will. My WHOLE career people tried stopping me, they tried telling me that I'd never be on top. He's too small, they said. He's too much like Shawn Michaels, his name sounds too much like Shane Douglas. He... won't... make it, they said. SD flexed his arms as the camera panned away, exposing his tanned upper body frame. "Well this week, I prove them all wrong." The dynamism in his eyes turned into a fierily captivating, "This has been nearly 7 years in the making and I'm just not going to give it all up because a few people don't want to see me succeed. To everyone who doubted me or keeps doubting me, make sure you set your VCRs correctly to ESEN this weekend. But I wont say I told you so, no, because actions speak louder than words my friend. And Shawn, I have a great amount of respect for you but it's time to pass the torch, if you know what I mean. You're a great competitor and will definitely be a challenge but I have too much to gain and nothing to lose. On Crash TV, I make my presence felt. On Crash TV, I begin silencing the naysayers and on Crash TV... I become... the NEW... Phenom of the NFW. Shawn Douglas grinned confidently. "Good Luck, Shawn."
