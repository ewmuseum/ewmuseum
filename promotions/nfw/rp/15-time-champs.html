(CUT TO: A thick, but kempt bearded Jack Bryant, decked out in a crimson “Alabama A” long-sleeve tee, Wrangler jeans, Bronc Stomper boots, and a white Alabama ballcap from Nike, standing on the sidelines at Sun Life Stadium w/ ESPN Sideline Reporter Heather Cox. Behind them, Fiona Love, Trent Richardson, and other various celebrities are chatting away.) HEATHER COX: “You’ve got to be liking what you’re seeing here tonight, Jack!” JB: “Nothin’ like a good ol’ fashioned butt whuppin’, Heather! Them Golden Domers don’t seem to be enjoyin’ our Southern Hospitality one bit! (chuckles) Roll Tide!” COX: “Any predictions for the second half?” JB: “More a’ tha’ same!” COX: “Thanks, Jack!” (FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 7 Jan 2013] (CUT TO: 2nd Quarter - JB pounding down a Pabst Blue Ribbon 24-ounce Silo and letting out a roar as the Tide scores again! [the thread's next post — 7 Jan 2013] (CUT TO: Present time; 35-0 - Jack Bryant, PBR in hand, talking to a young Alabama Equipment Manager.) JB: "Run an' get me one a' them helmets, son! Otherwise, Ah reckon Ah'm gonna' have ta' go OLD SCHOOL an' start knockin' heads with this thick skull a' mine!"
