Faith in summary

27 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CHRIS SHEPHERD: It had to start sometime. Deacon went to Buffalo for Brawl and it turned into exactly that. JJ started with an obvious attempt at dividing up any potential allies – Deacon, Legion, & Impulse, three men who have dared stand against the Windham clan and their cronies. JJ might’ve even thought it necessary to force the three competitors to go for it. He soon found he was wrong – all 3 of us knew our job and did it. Deacon knew Impulse wanted to win. Legion knew Deacon wouldn’t stop to give the fans what they paid to see. So JJ upped the ante. And when that didn’t work either, he turned it into a clustering mass of beatdowns. Nice job, JJ. You had all your flanks covered, at least until you got surprise attacked from the rear by a man who had been one of Deacon’s fiercest foes… and side from the current World champ… and then straight in the face from Dan Ryan. After one of the more memorable Brawls in history, we ended with Dan Ryan, being the Dan Ryan he always was and saying what needed to be said. We ended with the man who took the champ to the limit not once, but twice, who defined his goals for the immediate future. We ended with some sanity in an otherwise insane situation. And now, we head into Pittsburgh. But before I go there, I need to recount some points Deacon and I discussed while he recuperated in his home. The journey so far and the focus here on out. Bobby Jack. I want to say I’m done with you. Deacon wants to be done with you. But we can’t, because you, obviously, aren’t finished. You see, we’ve been here for… how long? When we walked in, we stopped one of your Windham shenanigans. We followed that by labeling you as what you were and was met by… silence. We’d get a tag match with you on the other side, a chance to at LEAST get in the ring and put your “faith” to the test. We were met with… silence, and not just silence, but an ability in the ring that was second to… bandit? Then, when we’re otherwise involved, you popped your head back in and cause a problem or go on a diatribe, not once, but countless times. If it wasn’t for what you were saying, we would’ve moved on months ago. So, before I’ve been a bit more focused on dispersing your toxic remarks. Now, Deacon isn’t going to call you a name. He isn’t going to discuss your beliefs. He’s going to help you. Whatever clout you hold, whether with the Windhams or not, is not only waning, it is 5 and a half feet under and stuck in quicksand. We are going to throw YOU a lifeline. Man up. Get in that ring. Or get out of this business. I’m guessing you’re of the persuasion that repeats the scripture, “I am the head and not the tail.” If you are one of those, then you’d better do something with that head… cause you’re just chasing your tail and that big old bear that is Deacon can’t take you seriously any longer. And speaking of taking someone seriously, I happened to be watching some NFW telecast and seen something that made me laugh; now, not in a good way, but a laugh nonetheless. Eric Dane. After his mouth got him the ire of the locker room, after his legalities got him into the world title hunt. AND after his lady friend got kidnapped by one of the boys in said locker room. He follows it with… “Well, if you did that, then I can do one better. See… it didn’t bother me!” (snicker) Seriously. Seriously?! That was a great comeback when I was twelve. But what I got outta that whole thing, Eric, was your way at handling a bully who stole your lunch money was to flush it down the toilet before he got to you. And the NFW crowd rides Impulse for not using a chair? At least his code is consistent. Have the guts to call it what it was – a jerk move by Rook Black. Personally, I would’ve preferred if he would’ve kidnapped YOU and shaved YOUR head, or eyebrows, or back hair that you forgot to wax off. And if he missed with the razor once or twice, all the better. But then again, Deacon tends to be a bit more direct about handling his problems. After Pittsburgh, I’m sure Rook will begin to figure that out. Just like you already did, Eric. Which leads to the Triple Crown. Deacon has made it plain that, in a world where Bobby Jack Windham doesn’t matter, he has one goal – not to win that title, but to define a championship. This belt, in Deacon’s hands, isn’t going to be simply a belt worn. Deacon won’t even carry it to the ring because he hasn’t earned it on THAT night. Like the Lombardi trophy, no team carries last year’s crystal structure into the Superbowl… they carry this year’s out. It’ll be a trophy earned each night it’s up for grabs – in a tournament, in a cluster war… in a way that will separate it and make it mean more than it is. Or it will fall into obscurity. Not to take anything away from the others meaning to carry it, but none have done what Deacon has… and none can do what he will do. The first people who get to see what we mean are in Pittsburgh. The next… everyone else. Some may say this is arrogant, but I just say that it’s a focus, a drive, something that has been given for Deacon to do. Not just because it’s his job, though it is. Not just because Deacon needs a belt, because he doesn’t. But because the NFW needs it. Because Deacon will deliver it. Because if a man of God is “the head and not the tail,” then the head leads. And starting in Pittsburgh, Deacon will lead the NFW in a direction it never saw coming. You ask how I know? I say faith.
