New York, NY House Show II

6 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th of July, 2009 (The camera panned in and we see AMERICAN PANDA sitting in what appears to be his own personal theatre room. There's two rows of seats, the type which recline and have drink holders. His is reclined and he has a bowl of sweet, buttery popcorn sitting in his lap. He munces a piece as he watches the end of the Kelly Masters promo. With it drawing to conclusion he raises his remote and pauses the promo.) PANDA New York, New York, what a heck of a town. There's gunna be some NFDub style house showing comin' up in the future and this here American Panda is gunna go git himself a paw in on the action, yeah? (Panda shrugged his shoulders.) PANDA Seems NFW has a cunning array of stunts... or did I get my words mixed up? Steve Knox. He's a hoss with a chip on the shoulder. Runs his mouth like my asshole runs after a cheap curry. I mean, I heard that people could suffer from verbal diahorhea but I didn't know it could be terminal. Poor li'l guy. SURSLEE! That's gotta be embarrassin' at tea parties. (With a shudder of his shoulders, like the image did not please him, American Panda continued.) PANDA But I guess this guy's got some sort of mark on his back. A number one contender to Joe the Plumber's World title? In my books, that's impressive. I'm not here to rub his shoulders and reach around, tuggin' his wood 'til it spits up. You've just gotta emancipate yourself when others carry possessions. We've talked about covetting already. But I'm not here to stand trial and challenge a man who clearly thinks he needs to carry his testicles around in a wheelbarrow. I mean... I don't honestly see the point in needing a semi-trailer to carry two marbles but whatever floats your boat, Steve. I'm sure you get a hard on every night seein' how many peons keep tryin' to claw up your leg. It'll only be all the more enjoyable watchin' your bravado and ego get slapped back down to earth by a man who works with FAECES for a livin'. (Panda waved a hand in front of his face.) PANDA Do you smell somethin'? I guess Knox' verbal diahorhea has turned into an epidemic in NFW. What the fuck is that smell? Oh right. Problem Child's cuttin' a promo. Well I'll be jiggered with a toasting iron. You got the audacity to stand in front of a camera and reel off names and attirbuting them to suckness? I mean... Knox and HIV... I can see the link. But me? You're tellin' me I suck? Somethin'-somethin' 'bout a kettle and pot an' one of 'em callin' the other a nigger or somethin'. I dunno how that works but Probster... just coz you lickin' windows, son, don't make you the cleaner. Ya feel me? (Panda sighed.) PANDA We all know you're about as talented as the fat kid chosen last for the dodgeball team... but ya don't gotta rub it in ya own face, son. Makin' your own title belt, in spite of those that are sanctified, to further correlate facts you suck harder than Jenna Jameson's stunt double? You want me to feel sorry for you? Coz it's workin', bruvna. I feel sorry for you. I look at you like a bulldog puppy with downes syndrome and missin' two legs. You ain't a whole unit. You so ugly when you was born your mumma slapped her gynacologist and said "MOTHERFUCKER! THA'S THE PLACENTA! GIMME MY BABAY!" and when the doctor told her it was her baby she said "WELL GIVE THIS BITCH THE PLACENTA INSTEAD! LEAST IF I ATE THAT SHIT IT'D BE NUTRIOUS!" (Panda wiped a pretend tear from his eye.) PANDA Touching times. But the thing... for lack of other terminology... that really grabbed my attention through the tripe I just waded through. (Panda motions toward his television set.) PANDA Mista Kelly Masters. I mean... I know what you're capable of in the ring. And lemme give you the hot tip, son... keep off the mic before you destroy ya credibility. Stick to makin' babies cry by punchin' their papa in the nuts before they's born... 'steada harpin' on down the audio lines of NFDub, son. (Panda put his fingers in his ears for a moment, as if to say he didn't wanna listen.) PANDA But it's nice to see you got goals, Kelly. I mean... evolution is a pretty neat goal. I really hope you like walkin' on two feet. The rest'f us been doin' it for a while and it's really lotsa fun bein' upright and vertical. I just hope you get over that urge to shit into ya hand and throw it at people. Coz that's just plain embarrassin'! Whassat? Ya new finisher? TURD TOSS! I mean let's get us some seriousness over here, Mr. Monkey Business... you think y'all are the shit and a bit. And that ya big an' scary just coz ya know a submission hold or two. LOOK AT HIM NOW, SON! HERE COMES THE MAN WIT' DA GIRL NAME AN' HE GOT HISSELF A GARVIN STOMP! (He puts the end of his fingers in his mouth, mocking that he was scared.) PANDA PUH-leeeeez... don't make me sick, Masters. You wanna prove to NFDub that you Holier than Thou than you go right ahead... prove it with me. American Panda. Coz son... it's on. IT'S ON LIKE DONKEY KONG! Me and you. American Panda and Kelly Masters. Madison Square Garden. One on one. Mano et mano. You and me, pal. Let's do that shit! (American Panda grinned at the camera.) PANDA Kelly. At the Garden... I'ma chew ya fuggin jugular, HOMMES. You can bet on it. (Panda stretched back in his reclined theatre chair and began flicking stations. He looked back to the camera and ushered it off with his free hand. Then we just go on and fade that shit to black... FadeToBlack!)
