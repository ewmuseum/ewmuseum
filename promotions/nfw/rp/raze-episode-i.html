Raze Episode I

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADEIN: MGM Grand locker room The scene goes to a man sitting and mumbling to himself. He looks bruised and battered after a fight a few moments ago. He keeps playing tonight’s incident in his head over and over again like a broken record. All he can see is the man falling to die. He screams “What have I done”. “I can’t go on like this” he shouts. “That damn ringing in my head is worse then ever” he mentions. “I need a doctor” he stutters. Holding his head he looks up… Raze: I can’t do this anymore; I’m a killer in the ring. I killed a man. How will I live with myself? -You can’t give this up; you’re on top of your game. You’re the best. You took him out in less then 3 minutes. You’re amazing. Raze: I’m a murderer. My head hurts. (Ringing in his head) How is killing amazing? -Your strength is amazing. He just didn’t have what it takes to be a fighter. Raze: My career in boxing is over. -You are feared by many and you can’t just quit and do nothing. You must continue and move on to the next fighter. Raze: Continue what? I have to quit. I killed a man in the ring. -You can do other things. You will become more notorious and world famous. Raze: That’s not me, sometimes my anger and rage takes the upper hand and this damn ringing in my head is killing me. -You need to move on and do something new where it’s okay to be you and release you rage. It’s what you are good at. Look at you, you would think you liked it. Raze: You think I should. Maybe you are right but I don’t want to hurt anyone, anymore. -It’s your destiny. Didn’t you enjoy the pain you inflicted on that man? Your heart pumping faster with each rage filled blow. The moment he fell, you were ready for the next fighter. Raze: Alright! Yes, I liked it. In fact, I loved it. My head is still hurts. You’re right, that’s what I’m good at. My boxing career is over now. What would you suggest we do? -NWF FADEOUT: MGM Grand locker room End.
