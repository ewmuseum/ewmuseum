The Reality

20 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Backstage, post BRAWL. I was wearing loose sweatpants cutoffs while taping up and pouring antibacterials on the various cuts and scrapes on my legs. My favorite long trunks were in the corner - black with a neon green double helix on each leg - cut off so I wouldn't tear at any of my war wounds. JJ, you bastard. What struck me most, tonight - even more than JJ's latest failed attempt to break me, or Dan Ryan finally asking the question we all had: 'I'm Dan Ryan, why am I standing with the Windham Clan?' - was my former partner and, I guess, at this point, former friend. Rook Black. Nice speech, Rook. And now, as Christian Cruise would say when he was Cameron Cruise and couldn't think of clever lines of his own... allow me to retort. "It's funny, Rook, because I always thought you were a vulcan." Live Long and Prosper. "Not in the conventions and pointy ears way, but the fact that everything you said; everything you did was based around logic. You could see how A affected B which would in turn cause C and D to unfold differently. Emotion, as far as I could tell, as far as anyone I know who knew you years ago could tell... never entered into the equation." "And I thought, maybe, that was part of why we got along so well." Before I knew it was all an act, of course. "Because I hold my emotions in check; I don't let them affect what happens in the ring. Once you let that go, you lose all control." Control is the watchword of civilization. Self - control is essential for those who have little use for established social orders. "By your declaration that I should have adequately retaliated, you're saying that I should have taken JJ's head off with that chair. I should have pounded it into his body until he was a bloody smear on the mat, right?" "Because this is the New Frontier, right? Because you were cutting out a man's tongue in your second match here, right?" You simple, simple man. "That's exactly why I wasn't going to aim for what you - or the rest of my contemporaries in the New Frontier - would have determined to be 'suitable' retribution." "Because your NFW isn't my NFW." "Rook, you came into this company in the middle of a war between its founders, where Dildonnites and Demolition Derbies were standard faire, where Randalls would take a handcuffed 360 off a scaffold in the middle of a thunderstorm, a cage would explode, some guy named Katz must die, and Michael Manson... existed." "I came into this company as Eddie Mayfield and Craig Miles declared a Wrestling Revolution." "I came into this company as a wrestler." "I came into this company to be a wrestler. Nothing has changed that fact." Not even the thought of vengeance. "Will JJ DeVille pay for what he's done to me and to the New Frontier as a whole?" "Yes. Yes he will." "But on my terms, not on his. To have followed through with that swing would have destroyed everything I've worked for and built in the past four years. To have followed through would have succumbed to vengeance." "Vengeance is a killer, Rook. Vengeance makes you feel better for a moment... but then the feeling passes and there's nothing but emptiness until the next Act of Vengeance." "Pretty soon, you are JJ DeVille: a man too blinded by his own need for revenge for real and perceived slights that he can't let his own actual wrestling talent get him where he could've gotten to." Sigh. I wonder if this is even getting through, but I can't do much but hope. "The Windham Clan will not be here forever, Rook. Dan Ryan proved that at Brawl: they have an expiration date of some kind, and I think it's fast approaching." "But betrayal of everything I've fought for, of everything that Cally and I have suffered for?" "That's forever." "I didn't need to do more to prove to the world that I'm capable of it, Rook. I don't feel the need to do more because the boys think I should've done more." "I did what I did and when I talked to Cally afterwards, she told me she was proud of me and that she thinks I did the right thing." "I did what I did because 'revenge' isn't a word in Rose's vocabulary." Pranks are different, and she may have upped the ante too far with the stunt with Valerie, but there was only a sense of 'Let's get one over on JJ' involved there. "This is my path, Rook, and it was nice walking it with you for a while, but like you said, the time for our fellowship is over. I trust you'll do just fine in winning the Triple Crown title; other than Deacon in the Gauntlet I don't think anyone else involved is at your level." Hah. Trust. "Funny thing about trust, Rook. Once you mess with it, it's never the same." "Even if you someday glue it back together, you can still see the cracks." FADE
