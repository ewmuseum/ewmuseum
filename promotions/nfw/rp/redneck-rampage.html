Redneck Rampage

18 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: Outside Nazareth hospital, a mystery man talks about new comer Travis Knowles. The man seems disgusted.) The Mystery Man: Knowles, I saw you inside the seedy Nazareth bar and you were talking to my woman. NOBODY! and I mean NOBODY talks to my squirrel! I saw your Big-Headed ass talkin to her and I don't like it, boy. The MM: She's my property, no one else. I won her in a poker game and no one can take her away. Betsy Sue is my B@#CH. Knowles, I know where you live, boy. (Finishes beer and smashs on his forehead) The MM: I'll break your back over my gun rack and use you body as my coffe table, boy. The MM: Face me, like a man, Knowles. It will be you and me at the next House Show. The Road to Futureshock will be a road to the hospital for you, boy. (Spits Tobacco) I'll break your ass, boy. The MM: See you there.(lights a cigarette) (FADEOUT:The MM turns and faces the parking lot. He is heading for his 1984 Camaro) [the thread's next post — 20 Jul 2002] (FADEIN to NFW Studios.. Travis Knowles is standing in front of an NFW backdrop..) TRAVIS KNOWLES: Let me tell you something, Cletus... I don't have a clue who the hell you are, and to be damn honest with you, I don't care. I've got a match with RAZE coming up at the next show, but if you want to step into the ring with me some time down the road, then you're on. I've yet to have an official match in the NFW, but I've got every aspect of the wrestling game down pat. I live, eat, and sleep to become the best damn wrestler who ever stepped into an NFW ring. If you want to CHALLENGE me, then bring it on. I've got news for you though... I don't think you really want to be on my PATH OF DESTRUCTION, as I work my way up to the gold. And as far as your WOMAN goes? Hell man, you gotta get rid of your beer goggles. All I told your "WOMAN" to do, was to move her fat ass over so I had some space to breathe at my bar stool. So until we meet again, take care of that mullet... and lose your attitude, because you can't even FATHOM the lesson you're going to learn... when you step into the ring with me. (FADEOUT...)
