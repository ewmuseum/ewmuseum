The Power Lies Here

23 Jul 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: JJ DeVille stands on the lobby of his Arlington, VA-area condo, with the DC skyline behind him. His hair is ponytailed back, his go-tee trimmed, diamond earrings sparking. He's wearing a WWJJD T-Shirt tucked in to khaki slacks.) JJ: "I have to tell you how NICE it is to fly in style. The Jacksonville Airport? Well, it's a piece of garbage. But I did manage to score a direct flight from there to Reagan International... and I made sure I was in First Class. And I also made sure I bought a SECOND seat right next to me so my new leather carry-on didn't have to get dirty on the floor. It's such a nice experience, FIRST CLASS. Of course, only a select few on the NFW roster will ever know what the leather of a FIRST CLASS seat will feel like since I've taken on the duty of approving company-funded travel arrangements. And, in fact, for most of the company I'm only willing to approve BUS travel on our dime. But for me? The man who is RUNNING this promotion? THE TRUE LEADER OF MEN? I deserve it." (JJ holds his arms out triumphantly.) JJ: "Of course, I didn't have to many airplane rides until about a year ago. That's because I was busy living in a two-bedroom apartment on the outskirts of Raleigh, North Carolina. That's because I was making $8.50 an hour stacking freeweights in the gym. That's because I was cleaning up the towels the football team left in the locker room floor. That's because I was busy studying the LSAT's for a possible law career... my BACKUP plan... because I was BLACKBALLED FROM DOING WHAT I LOVE." (JJ purses his lips.) JJ: "How many times, Mayfield, did I send you my resume? How many times did I send you tapes of my matches? My promos? How many times did I e-mail? Did I call? HOW MANY TIMES DID I ASK YOU FOR A SPOT? Well, I'll tell you, Eddie. I send you my resume 14 times. I mailed you my tapes 16 times. I sent you 36 e-mails. I called your office 27 times. Each time YOU IGNORED ME... I checked it off. And why was it, Mayfield?" (JJ chuckles.) JJ: "Now, a lot of people in the industry said my demands were unreasonable. My demands for paid-for penthouse suite rentals... personal attendants to carry my bags... massages and spa treatments... a guaranteed seven figure contract... title shots at my whim... they were all unreasonable. But I was taught by the master. I was taught by Troy Windham to NEVER BUDGE ON WHAT YOU DESERVE. And I never budged. I knew what I was worth in the market. I KNEW HOW GOOD I WAS! And you did, too, Mayfield. You knew better than ANYONE." (JJ shakes his head.) JJ: "Now, I just watched the tape of Reloaded. I finally saw the press conference. How impressive, Mayfield. You're coming back into the ring. You're going back into the squared circle because I BEAT YOU IN THE BOARDROOM. You're a barely literate chain-smoking video-game addicted MORON with no attention to detail. And me... little old me... out of the sport for half a decade... used his ADVANCED COLLEGE EDUCATION to bury your ass in a paper trail that you'll never get out of. As my totally awesome theme song says... I'VE GOT THE POWER! But I've got more than just power, Mayfield. I've got an INCONVENIENT TRUTH to expose about you." (JJ purses his lips.) JJ: "The reason you blackballed me, Mayfield, is because back in the CSWA... THE GREATEST LEAGUE THERE EVER WAS... you thought you were going to come in and hotshot your way to the top. You thought that because you were some all-mighty heel overlord carrying GUNS' bags around that Uncle Chad was just going to HAND you titles on a silver platter. But what you didn't realize, Mayfield... is that other people had their... I's... on the prize, too. Including myself. And when we crossed paths, Eddie... when we did battle... guess who's hand was raised by Pee Wee Troutman? IT WAS MINE, MAYFIELD. IT WASN'T YOURS. IT WAS MINE." (JJ chuckles.) JJ: "I know you think of yourself as some icon. As some all-time great wrestler. Well, Mayfield, what is it that you've actually done? Walked around with some low-rent title for a few weeks? Played make-believe enemies with Craig Miles? Mayfield, you're a no-name scrub who lucked his way into getting a position of authority here in NFW. Me? Heh. I *FORCED* my way into this position of authority. I *TOOK* my position of authority. I BECAME THE ULTIMATE THORN IN YOUR SIDE, MAYFIELD." (JJ looks out the window.) JJ: "I beat you in the boardroom. I beat you in the paper game. And now that you're coming back to the ring oh glory oh glory hosana in the highest? It's just going to be the same as it always was, Eddie. I'm going to beat you there, too." (FTB)
