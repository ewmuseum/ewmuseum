WEEK 8: Dan Ryan vs. Evan Aho

20 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"He who does not learn from history is doomed to repeat it." --------- FADE IN DAN RYAN in front of a nice, big NFW backdrop...festively decorated with lovely ribbons and the like in order to designate it's signifigance as "Crashmas" week.... Ryan adjusts an elbow pad and wipes sweat from his brow, having just completed a public workout to help hype the show.... Ryan: "So, after weeks of weapons and accusations levelled against me over sneak attacks...and more blood spilled than I care to give up at any one time...." "It's good to see an old friend." Smile... "Evan, it's good to see you again. It's been a long time indeed." "Things certainly have changed haven't they? It seems however, that no matter where I go everything tends to come full circle. Here we are again...." "The first time we met inside a ring I ambushed you a bit. I can't deny that. It wasn't particularly sporting of me, but it was what it was." "The second time we had the match that was supposed to complete an epic story. The GXW World Champion invading the big bad CSWA and tearing his way through everyone to the CSWA World Champion in an effort to claim respect for his company." Ryan lets out a short little laugh... "My how tempers get the best of us at times." "But it didn't quite work out that way. Factors arose, circumstances changed....and by the time we managed to get around to each other it just didn't mean as much anymore. Your title was gone, my comrades had decided to turn tail and run for the hills....and the match itself....the match itself just didn't live up to expectations." "Did I win that match because you were still hurt, Evan? You said you were fully healed and I believe you. Even if I didn't, I would allow you the dignity of faking it." "For whatever reason, it was the beginning of the end for you. I don't claim to take credit for that at all. Your fire for this business seems to have grown a little cooler since a time long before the injury. Maybe I did play a small part in breaking the innocence of things....the purity of things for you when you came back to GXW in a gesture of what you thought was goodwill....and I crashed that party." "But I don't think I had so much to do with things as you yourself had to do with it." "What then, will this third meeting be like I wonder?" "Can I manage to incite some flicker from somewhere deep within you, Evan?" "One thing that's well known about me is that I crave a challenge above all else. When something becomes too easy, I get bored and move on." "I've met many challenges since our issues first surfaced almost two years ago now. One I've never been able to meet is a face to face meeting with Evan Aho at his 100% best." "Do you have it inside of you to summon your best back to the surface?" "I know you want redemption. You would never admit such a thing. No...this is only 'the next match.'" "Shall we lock up and fight for ten points then? Will that be all it means?" "Or can it mean more?" "You have this chance, Evan. You don't have to verbalize what any of this means to you. Perhaps it means nothing. But I think it means more than you will ever outwardly acknowledge." "I offer you a chance, Evan..." "I offer you a chance to get in that ring one more time....wrestle your ass off....and give the world a match worthy of more stars than there are in the sky to give...." "Then you can refuse a post-match interview...saying only to the prospective interviewer as you walk away....'It was just another match.'....before walking off smiling as you haven't smiled in over a year." "What will it be, Evan? Which choice will you choose?" "One more time....for old times' sake, my friend." "Redemption awaits...." FADE OUT
