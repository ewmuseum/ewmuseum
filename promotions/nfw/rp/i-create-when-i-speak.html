I Create When I Speak

12 Nov 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We fade in to see Legion in front of a backdrop that contains a picture of Aleister Crowley. We can also hear in the background the song ‘Alter Reality’ by Ancient VVisdom and Legion is wearing a t-shirt by the band along with the traditional blue jeans attire. Legion: It’s amazing how THIS man right here seems to have inspired three of our roster to pull off what no doubt the online community is calling the ‘coup of 2012 in pro-wrestling’, hell the punks made sure to put their plan in motion as I was JUST about to get my hands on Lamont Hollywood for making the wrong choice – and Lamont, trust me soon enough I’ll finish what I was abruptly stopped from carrying out, watch your back. You see while I was letting the blood flow for a while longer after the Stairway To Hell match, smiling at what I had created – lots of blood and pain for the others I saw what could rightfully be described as a classic, say what you want about Troy after everything he’s done but his fight with Randalls was pure entertainment, something that the likes of the Hellfire Club are yet to achieve without schemes. But they’re not the only ones plotting in the dark, while the likes of JJ seem to have discovered Crowley while going through their ‘occult/rebel’ phase of their lives I have gone a step further. I have travelled to Thelema the abbey itself and the idea of Chaos perpetrated by Alester and Osman has been my thoughts since arriving in NFW all those years ago, what I saw as Reloaded closed was that slowly coming to pass, there are still some loose ends to tie up however.. You see ‘Hellfire’ it is not just ‘Do hast thou wilt’ there’s one word you also need to learn: Abrahadabra. ‘I create when I speak’ but the power to create when you FIGHT – THAT is the goal you need to learn. Gather round – whoever wants to take the fight to rebuild NFW in the image befitting it – one that harks back to the TRUE revolution in mind, come to me and we shall create a world that the Devilles and Hollywoods will quake in fear against. We fade out as we began - the picture of Crowley himself.
