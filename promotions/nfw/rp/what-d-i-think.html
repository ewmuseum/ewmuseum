What'd I think?

6 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN.... Backstage after the final night of All Star week. A local reporter covering the event for a newspaper in the area. He says the usual. You know, bypassing the actual event and talking about wrestling and the effect it has on the decline of Western Civilization. Rep: "So after a night of action we must wonder, how will this affect our children? How will it affect the social climate of our fair city? How will....." (Someone from off screen is heard yelling) Voice: (sounding extremely angry) "Is that camera rolling?!" Our Jimmy Olsen wannabe turns and barely gets out the word "Yes" before Dan Ryan forces his way into the shot, eyes red, fists clenched.....shaking with anger as he rips the microphone out of the reporter's hand. Ryan: (limping a bit as he steadies himself) "You got that s**t rolling?! Don't even THINK of cutting me off...." And in a low, intense whisper into the camera as Ryan continues to shake in fury.... "You people wanna walk in here and embarrass me during the f**king main event?" "So Avery Prosser runs the North now.....the Seven Deadly Sins run the North now......and you wanna tell me to watch my f**kin' step??" "NAH MUTHAF**KA.....you watch YOUR F**KIN' STEP." "I wipe my ass with what you and your Black Panther rejects have to say about me and watchin' my step. I don't watch a f**king thing." "So you're some big shot Prosser? Who are you impressin'? A bunch of thirty somethings who still mark out over a company that died out in the early nineties??" "Take this s**t to heart, Prosser. You don't even know what you just woke up, mother-f***er. You don't even know." "You make think you run somethin' around here. You don't run S**T unless I let you run s**t." "And now....you are the one that's gonna change your f***kin' tone with me, or else I'll embarrass every....last.....damn.....one of those lackeys of yours and then break your f**kin' neck as the coup de gras." "You've got your choice, Prosser. You run your conference and stay the f**k out of my business." "Oh....were you waiting for an 'or' in there somewhere?" "Nah....ain't no 'or'. You're just gonna stay the f**k out. Period." "And Calvin Carlton...." Ryan stops a second and turns halfway to the wall half smiling, but still shaking.....then turns back.... "OHHHH man......" "If you thought momma was pissed before.....ah s**t......she's about to damn near blow a hole in the roof." "You don't make an example out of me, Carlton. You don't do anything where I'm concerned. And if pulling that dinosaur out of the closet to dance around and cut a retro promo that old people marked for and young people scratched their heads over is the biggest f**king surprise you've got......" "You just wrote your f**king death warrant." "You wanna know what the biggest surprise at War Games was?! You wanna know? The biggest surprise was Eddy Love showing his raggedy ass on TV for fifteen minutes without retiring two or three times in the middle of it. The surprise was that Eddy Love didn't pop out a f**king hip while pulling out that weak ass Fargo strut that no one since 1985 gave two s**ts about." "And the biggest f**king surprise is yet to come, Cal. You know what it is??" Ryan practically explodes in anger.... "YOU KNOW WHAT IT F**KING IS?!?!" Ryan goes back to his intense whisper, still seething and getting redder and redder.... "The biggest surprise of all is gonna come when you turn around one day and you realize.....everyone's gone.....it's all over and your issues have been dealt with.....momma's happy....the fans are happy.....you're happy......then you look around a corner and you realize...." "I'm still not happy, Cal." "I'm not f**kin' happy at all." "You've got a receipt comin', Calvin. You're not gettin' a chairshot, Cal. No beatdowns in the back to end the anxiety of what awaits you. Oh no. You're on the list now, Cal....." "So no matter who I have to pay back first.....whether it's those NAACP throwback jackholes in the North who think they own me.....or whether it's you and your momma...." "Someone's about to get......F**KED.....UP." Ryan shoves the camera away violently and the camera goes flying, shattering against the wall...... ...... ...... Static.......
