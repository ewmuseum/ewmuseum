NFW — Roleplay

To Jack Bryant...

27 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TSiegel — 27 April 2011 CRUISE: Bryant. Quit being jealous, mind your own business and shut the **** up. I'm fighting to keep a job and youre not. Also...if youre going to take it up the ass from the President...at least use two hands...Monica did.
Shame Eddie...shame. Biron — 27 April 2011 (FADE-IN: To JACK BRYANT.) JB: Jealous, Cammy. Let's be serious. Why would I be jealous of a Cameron Cruise? The only reason anybody remembered you were in the Frontier is 'cause Windham almost broke your neck. Too bad. (shrugs) As far as your job, which you seem to struggle with, when Mayfield cans you, I'll give you a job shining my belt, boy.
(FTB) TSiegel — 27 April 2011 (CUTTO: Cameron Cruise.) CC: That's the interesting thing though, Jack. Even when I'm nearly injured by a COWARD WITH A CHAIR....I still have more air time than YOU do.
As far as my job is concerned, there's no worry with that. I've got plenty of investments saved up....it's what happens when you know men like Hugh Hefner on a FIRST NAME BASIS.
SON. FADEOUT Ford — 28 April 2011 (STATIC.GFX: NFW logo. Cutto: High Flyer standing in front of an NFW flag. He looks pissed.) HIGH FLYER: Cameron. You know Hugh Hefner? ***** owes me a grand! Tell 'im will collect! (Flyer wanders off muttering.) HIGH FLYER: Last time I do him a favor. (Static. GFX: NFW logo. Fade to black.)
