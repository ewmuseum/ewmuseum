WEEK 10 - MICHAEL MANSON vs. CASTOR STRIFE

14 Mar 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in black jeans and newly available NFW EAST: WORLD DOMINATION IS STEP 3 t-shirt, sits over a coffee table. Spread about is the game of RISK. MANSON plays against an imaginary opponent.)) MANSON: For now, the NFW has announced that the inaugural match of the Eastern Conference will be that rematch of the North classic Mike Manson versus Castor Strife....mostly because they have no idea what we will actually do. Yes, Leonard Nimoy will be around these parts.... But I can say there will be wrestling,midgets, and porn...there'll be match-ups of the East..things you've never seen in the NFW before. All live from an undisclosed location..... You may be wondering how if Castor and I seem to be competitng,how we co-exist in the workplace. Let me tell you, that even now, both of us are hard at work drawing the specifics of this, the Eastern Conference. Right at this moment, Castor is on set, bringing the capital that will make the NFW East the most unique of conferences. While I sit, at the game of RISK, waiting for that inspiration to dawn. Now you may be thinking....this is jusy talk....hollow words....and you might be right.....but you lack my imagination...Castor's depravity and cinematic skill.... I'm willing to say that the match between Manson and Strife, that fitful climax, may not even be a match....but perhaps two....or something else.... Maybe we'll be divided as MIKE MANSON faces off against his most wretched opponent....one that is invisible...but always with him....that's right...MICHAEL MANSON versus MICHAEL MANSON. And where does that leave Castor? I have to tell you, after the press conference, our email boxes were filled to the brim. Our phones ran off the hook. The fax machines broke from the business. We heard from the army, the navy, the fans, the management, Quentin Sullivan, the North, the South, the president of the United States, Craig Miles, the 700 Club, the CSWA, the WFW, the NEW, the SNL, and the IRS. And from a few wrestlers too..... Castor Strife will displaying his technical expetise against the second offical signee of the NFW EAST..you know him well.....but I can't really say anything now. I can mostly say..who he is not. But that's for later. ((MANSON holds up a contract with red ink.)) When our friend arrives and signs on the line, not giving up his soul, though we're still in talks over that, but his life and body to the EAST. Then we'll get down to RISK, the game of global conquest. You may be wondering how with these developments, MANSON/STRIFE can possibly be a competition and for points....you may even think we haven't figured this out ourselves....and you might be right... ...but to really know......you'll just going to have to watch the next CRASH...
