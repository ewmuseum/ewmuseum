I saw a lot of that in Japan the past twelve months. When it's done right it's a stimulate." "You're even more clueless than I gave you credit for being. I wrestle men, Melton." "Like private shows? Dammit now that is beneath you." "In a North American promotion, A1E." "Women don't wrestle men." "The business has changed since you broke into it in the seventies, Melton. It's not the sexist haven you remember." "What sane person would buy a woman in the ring with a man?" "Welcome to the 21st century." "This is why our sport's declining with the economy. Nobody pays the freakin law of physics any mind." He makes her blood boil. "So you don't think I can carry my own weight?" "I'm saying if someone is stupid enough to put you in the ring with a man any credibility you have in the business will be shot." "You are so chauvinistic. I'm the #1 Contender to the A1E Cyber Title, and I held the A1E Triple Star Title for 10 months. My television quarter hours and merchandise sales are among the top in the company." A hundred and fifty pound woman in the ring with a monster like Dan Ryan, or Eli Flair." A body like that flaunting around the ring fully capable of handling herself...we could go far." "I'm more than just a pretty face, Joseph. Maybe if you took your blinders off long enough, you'd see that. And you didn't just say "we"..." "Yes, we. If you tone down the hostility a bit, the two of us could sell out arenas. I've worked with all kinds, but never a body that gave me so much to play off of." She couldn't believe he was suggesting this. Over her dead body... "It's not entrapment if a law hasn't been broken!" yelled Carlton, still on the floor, still cowering. Pleasepleaseplease don't go after me. I'm too young to die. Who would take care of Momma...? "Darling," said Troy, soothingly, "we were merely talking about the next charity case I was about to take on." "And what case would that be, love?" "Why him, of course." "Naturally, at first, I wasn't so sure about the idea, but I'm not the kind of person to just turn away a cry for help when it comes crawling and begging for my mercy. It would be ever so horrid of me not to help Joey Melton get back on his feet. He has nothing to live for except for my kindness and generosity." MISTER MELTON, I assure you, if I was in any position to stand up to that man..." Carlton brushes himself off. "Yes Carlton, you would have. Momma would have wanted you to." Melton reaches into his pants pocket and pulls out a wallet. He opens it up and flashes the driver's license to Carlton. It's not Melton's wallet. Before Carlton can get a good read of the name, Melton reaches into the billfold and pulls out a wad of cash. He closes the wallet and walks over to one of the valets standing by the door. "Be a good man, son," Melton says, and hands him the wallet. “Give this to the gentleman sitting in the very back corner of the restaurant, and tell him I said thanks for the cab money."
