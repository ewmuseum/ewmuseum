IMPORTANT: Notice of Legal Action

27 Apr 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DOUBLE BOOM Enter: Dan Ryan, sitting behind a very large mahogany desk, smiling THE most sh!t-eating grin you've ever seen. DAN RYAN: "Soooo, lately there was some sort of ruckus around the idea of JJ Deville going on NFW Television purporting to be planning to dig up the graves of my parents, my daughter and so on and so on. Good clean family entertainment, right?" "Well, it seems like the kind of thing that might be fun to show if we all lived in a world of fiction, in fantasyland or some other sort of construct where the rules of the real world don't apply." "Here's the thing though." "I don't think you plan on doing anything of the sort, Mr. JJ Deville." "I had a nice long conversation with the very expensive attorney that I keep on my payroll and he informed me of some very interesting facts in this situation." "You do NOT actually plan to do what you claim you plan to do because, as you know, JJ Deville, in the Great State of Texas, according to Texas Health Code Statute 711.004, in order to remove remains from any cemetery you must first obtain the consent of the nearest living relative in order of: surviving spouse, surviving parents, surviving children and finally, surviving siblings. Now, my parents died together, so naturally there is no surviving spouse. Likewise, my daughter was five years old when she died, so nothing there either. Now, I am in fact the surviving parent of my daughter so I believe that's where my consent is required. Furthermore, I am the only surviving child of my parents." "Now JJ...." "I'm sure you're also aware that in the GREAT STATE OF TEXAS...... violation of this statute is a Class I Felony. Right? You knew that. I'm quite certain you knew that. So, of course, you also know that violation of this statute carries with it, as I'm sure YOUR attorney can tell you, anywhere between a 5 to 99 year prison term, or life, and a fine of up to $10,000." "So... here's what I want, JJ.... it's very simple really...." "I want you to go on television and admit to the entire world that you are a small, sad, tired, pitiful man who tried to make a mockery of my family using staged props, beg for my forgiveness and for the forgiveness of the sponsors who I am now sending a detailed letter to concerning this matter in hopes that all NFW advertising money doesn't go straight out the window, or else turn around, pucker up and bend over, and get used to that position, because I plan to assure that you spend a very great chunk of the rest of your life in the Texas State Prison System." "One more time, JJ, you're gonna have to do better than that." "Shuffle off, change your drawers, and prepare your apology tape." "B!tch." [the thread's next post — 28 Apr 2012] (CUT TO: JJ DeVille, with the same exact grin.) JJ: "Oh, Ryan, there's no need for you to interpret legal codes for me. I was pre-law at North Carolina State University. You know, the college I attended and worked at while I was BLACKBALLED from professional wrestling? Ryan, I and my team of attorneys did attempt to contact you. However -- as I CLEARLY said on the video -- our contact information for you, as well as your social security number and banking information was somehow all wrong. We made our best efforts to locate the surviving kin of that poor, raped little girl... but, alas, it was to no avail as the corresponding party failed to respond to our request in a timely manner. I guess you were too busy no-selling everything to actually hear the tape." (JJ smirks.) JJ: "So go right ahead, moron. Go after the sponsors of NFW. You do remember that I'm here to first poison and then ultimately destroy this place? Thank you for helping me get my work done for me. I appreciate it and I'm sure the good boys and girls of Team NFW do, too. They know I'm a prick already. We're not breaking any new ground here. But now? Now they get to see you for what you are -- either a moron who doesn't get what I'm doing here or someone so beat up inside over what I did and said that he would be so selfish to take money from THEIR pocket. After all, like you said -- you have all the money in the world. Castor Stryfe and Jack Bryant and Dorchester Stratton and Malik Anderson and Saafir Muhammad and Legion and everyone else will love knowing that you're helping them lose a few zeros in the bank account." (JJ purses his lips.) JJ: "As far as an apology? Here goes, my man." (JJ clears his throat.) JJ: "I'm sorry your first promo after Philly was the absolute worst interview ever conducted in pro wrestling history." "I'm sorry that we won't get to see your little girl grow up to whore herself around a pole." "And, lastly, I'm sorry that I'm revealing an Inconvenient Truth about you, Danny Boy. And that is -- you're not really all that good. I applaud you for realizing that though, and choosing to hide behind so many layers of Twitter snark and sub-Impulse level lines from a copy of 'Sarcasm for Dummies' from the 'REALLY REALLY REALLY REALLY REALLY BIG FONT' section at the Hudson Books at the mass transit system your daughter would be trolling at right now if she was still with us. You're nothing more than a collection of YouTube comments, Danny." (JJ chuckles.) JJ: "Oh, and as far as being scared of you? I'm about 48 hours or so away from humiliating Impulse out of this sport. The same Impulse you pretty much blew moments before you made your dramatic turn. The very same Impulse who has beaten you about 4292432 times in a row. I'm not afraid of myths, Dan Ryan. And that's all you are. All it will take is three seconds for me to pull back the curtain and reveal to the world that you're an overrated shell of a man. Your daughter and your parents, Ryan, are no doubt ashamed of your very existence." (JJ purses his lips together one last time.) JJ: "But as far as our future goes, Ryan? As far as I'm concerned... there IS none. If you so far as look at me the wrong way at Reloaded, I will suspend and/or fire you on the spot and smile the entire time. And that title shot here you oh-so desperately crave that no one else actually wants to see? That's done, too. Sorry, pops! So go ahead. Come at me, Ryan. Go ahead and do something to me so I can fire you and you can walk away like you've promised. Go ahead and walk away knowing you weren't able to get the NFW World Title like you told the universe you would do. Go ahead and LEAVE knowing that the reason you tucked tail and ran was because of me. There's no way, Ryan, that you're going to... DIG... yourself out of this one." (JJ cackles. FTB)
