Calvin Speaks

15 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: A black NFW South Banner. Standing in front of it, angrilly-yet-nervously pacing back and forth, is CALVIN CARLTON, in his suit and holding his confed-flag themed tennis racket.) CALVIN: (his head to his temples, as if he's suffereing from a crippling migrane) Y'know, back six months ago, I thought for sure that I had drafted the best roster the world of professional wrestling had ever seen. Those individuals... led by MY expertise and superior knowledge of all things all... were going to combine to set the world of professional wrestling ablaze and to conquer the world. Instead, all I hear about from a bunch of poor people is how NFW South can't get the job done. (Calvin turns towards the camera and points the racket at it.) Well, I've NEVER cared about what the bus drivers, landscapers and visiting nurses of this world have had to say about ANYTHING. I only care about what people of my social status and upbringing have to say... and there aren't too many of those people in this world. Now, there's only ONE person on this planet who I can assure you is better than me-- which makes her better than EVERYONE. That person is my momma... my sweet, beautiful momma, the light in the stars and the air in our lungs. My momma, who decided to use her wealth to help me set about doing what *EYE* wanted to do... and that's become the most dominant figure in this industry. Well, my momma ain't a happy camper. Our foundation spent MILLIONS of dollars renovating that arena in South Carolina. That arena was a PICTURE PERFECT example of the entire Southern region-- it was a RAT TRAP and a FLEA BAG until the Carlton Family Dynasty decided to TURN IT AROUND. But we haven't had a card down there in weeks. Now, my momma ain't happy-- but she knows that it isn't *MY* fault that the people on my roster, save for Joey Melton pretty much, aren't CAPABLE of doing anything above a minimum wage job. Why, most of them couldn't even take a drive-through order properly. They're lucky they have jobs. So, my momma has made it clear to me. She asked me what I needed to turn this around. So I told her... momma, just let me have a few additional zeroes from your Sach's Fifth Avenue Purse-- the one adorned with real panda fur-- and I promise you I can turn this ship around. The first people made available to me, heh, most of them haven't panned out. But there's others out there, Momma, who can make The South proud again... there's others out there, Momma, who won't let you down. No, ma'am... you will not be let down. I promise you that, momma. So, rest assured, there's gonna be some CHANGES around these parts. And if you don't like that... I... and, most importantly, my momma... certainly DON'T CARE. (FTB)
