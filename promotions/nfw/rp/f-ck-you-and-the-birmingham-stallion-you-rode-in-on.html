F*ck you and the Birmingham Stallion you rode in on

1 Jul 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADE-IN: on TEDDY ALEXANDER backstage at Miami. Sweat drenched. Breathing heavy. He simply stands there staring at the camera, eyes piercing a hole in it.) TEDDY ALEXANDER: "Good match, Jack. Good match. When you drove my spine down across your knee..." (subconsciously, he touches his lower back) "...da pain shot up my back and I knew it was over." (His head drops and he stares no longer at the camera. His hands slowly ball into fists and his upper body starts shaking. He raises his head, face twisted and contorted into that of a man who looks like he about to go to war. He opens his mouth and screams a war-cry at the camera before turning and throwing a massive haymaker punch at the lockers to his right. Blood spurts from his knuckle as he makes a large indentation on the locker, cutting himself on the impact of fist on steel.) TEDDY ALEXANDER: (shouting crazily) "YOU THINK DIS IS OVER, JACK BRYANT, YOU GOT ANOTHER THING COMIN'! DIS AIN'T OVER! NOT BY A LONG SHOT!" (TEDDY pauses to calm himself a little, but you can hear the cracks in his voice.) TEDDY ALEXANDER: "I do NOT leave sleepin' dogs lie, Jack. Just coz you got me tonight, out there, in front of all them fans and walked off with ya belt don't mean you're gonna get to turn your back and walk away from me "It don't mean this is over. "Coz it's not. "You turn your back on me, Jack, you'll forever be lookin' over your shoulder coz dis s(censored)t ain't over 'til I'm standin' over your broken, bleedin' body. Dis ain't over 'til I SAY it's over, Jack." (A sadistic grin crawls across his face. He runs his forearm over his brow, forgetting he'd gashed his knuckles and smears his own blood across his face. It looks like war-paint.) TEDDY ALEXANDER: "Dis won't be over until I break... your Goddamn... NECK!" (FADE to BLACK.)
