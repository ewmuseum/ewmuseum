The way it goes

26 Mar 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: An angry and disgusted looking Bloodhunt holding his head in his heads...Miss Cuba stands near him looking very upset. Bloodhunt appears to be shaking.) BLOODHUNT: I could REALLY..go for a smoke right now...<Shakes head> I could do ANYTHING right now...But it would be quite foolish...Because I got myself MY WISH...MY ONE AND ONLY WISH...And that CRAIG MILES WOULD GROW SOME BALLS AND FIGHT ME...Miles...I've been BEGGING for this for so long...I've spend my whole life since that PPV...BEGGING for you to face me like a man...And now you will...And I'm going to MAKE YOU PAY...I've just got one question for you Craig...Was Zane REALLY that much better in bed then your wife? Or was the whole obession with him just more of a love affair from a distance...A forbidden fruit kinda thing? <Puts a hand up as if to silence the other person> you don't need to answer it Craig...You really don't have to...Cause NO answer you give is good enough...and what you will give me...Is what I want...I'm going to beat you to death...Emily isn't gonna be at ringside...That <BLEEP>ING MORON MONTY WON'T BE IN THE SAME BUILDING OR ALIVE IF I CAN HELP IT...I'm going to go to that ring for war...And I'm going to gut you...My whole career's worth of disgust and torment is gonna be dished out on you...You will KNOW what you did to me, and you will feel it in the most BRUTAL and HORRIBLE terms possible... As for Shane...Shane...I've got the easy road...I just got to beat this piece of <BLEEP> You...You got endless title matches...I know you don't want my help...Am I going to give it to you or not...Dunno...We'll see...Surprises are always more fun that way...<Smiles> But I'll see somebody at Futureshock...And my belt...Will come back to me...<FADEOUT>
