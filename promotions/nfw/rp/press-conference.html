Press Conference

9 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: Bloodhunt, injuried arm in a sling at a podium. Emily standing behind him, a look of sadness appears to be mixed with pride. The NFW World Title rests in front of him on the table.) BLOODHUNT: Before we get into the questions...I just want to issue a statement...(Smiles a rye smile) NFW and I have had some bumpy times...But well, it was a great ride... This company is changing...And I really don't know if I can change with it...There will be a football season like event, and playoffs...And I don't know if I can deal with that...I don't know if my body, in it's current shape, can take this kind of abuse... So for this next season, I'm not in, I may never be in...As for other leagues, other organizations...I won't be going anywhere, NFW for better or worse, is my home...THey gave me a shot...And I think I did pretty well with it...Shane Southern against me on a PPV...I couldn't ask for more then that...To win that...(Shakes head) unreal...Only wish it coulda been in Dallas (A few laughs, Bloodhunt stifles a chuckle) REPORTER: What will you being doing with the NFW Title Belt? BLOODHUNT: Rest it on my mantle...I don't want to sell it, unless I gotta put food on my table...50K is a chunk of change for me...I got to be honest about that...But until I get to that point, I'd like to savor this...I'd like try to keep NFW's legacy going. REPORTER: How can you say that with the tactics you used in this match? BLOODHUNT: Because I'm a heel...I cheat...I admit to this, but it's the nature of the beast...I won...These things happen...Shane Southern doesn't fight that way, that's his way...It wouldn't matter to me if all 2,000 people in that arena were chanting my name...I'd still cheat...(Smiles) Can't change who I am...But as for respect, honoring NFW...Well, I feel I did that, through the effort I put in...Fact that the match wasn't all by the book...(Shakes head) just go to hell...I'm gonna go home and try to enjoy this...When I figure out my future you'll hear from me (Bloodhunt, with his good arm grabs the NFW World title and he and Emily leave with questions being shouted and unanswered in the background...FADEOUT)
