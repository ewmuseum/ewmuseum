Release Me...

19 Apr 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADE IN on the damp, dripping interior of an old abandoned warehouse in the middle of Nowhere USA. The walls, the floor, the ceiling, everywhere covered in a fine mist of water, giving the place an eerie reflection no matter where you look. From the ceiling, droplets of water can be seen plumetting to their deaths in the form of splashes on the wet floor. In the foreground, there is nothing but a dimly lit hallway, with several of the lights flickering on and off. In the background however, grasping a crossbeam in the ceiling, a man with shoulder length dark hair, and a chest so massive you could land a helicopter on it, is doing pull ups. His movements are slow and deliberate. His arms, while muscular, are not overtly covered in rippling veins and stretch skin. No his bare arms have tone, and defintion. The camera, behind the mysterious man, slowly begins to close in. With nothing but the sound of his grunts with each pull up, and the drips of the water coming down, almost softly, distant voices can be heard. (From Crash T.V. Week 5) RICE: “HIS NECK IS PROBABLY BROKEN! Chris McMillan just Suicide Plancha’d to the floor – taking Payne off the top rope to the cement with him!” CUT-TO slow motion footage, in black and white, of Crash T.V. Week 5. PAYNE getting dragged off the top turnbuckle awkwardly against his will, flipping over and the back of his head snapping against the apron from the drop. CUT-TO the scene in the warehouse. The man, still facing away from the camera, is no longer doing pull ups. His shoulders move up and down with each breath as his hair falls around his neck. He appears to be looking down as the voice overs fade. As the flashback voices fade another one takes it's place. This one is calm, yet forceful, and above all things, familiar. "They said my career was over." Voices from the past once again fade in. CUT-TO footage from Crash T.V Week 5, redone so that the video appears as if the signal coming through is weak and partially filled with static. It's slmost like you were watching it on a very old television. WILDSTAR: "Jason Payne learned...his season was over...he can not wrestle for six months...a man who will finish with only ten points this season.” CUT-TO the warehouse once more as the voice over fades. The mysterious stranger is walking away from the camera, through a deserted hallway as the leaking water splashes off his massive frame. He stops and turns slightly, looking up towards the ceiling, though his face remains covered by the shoulder length dark hair. "And that may be so." CUT-TO a series of clips of several NFW superstars. Each one is seen just long enough to identify who they are. Gabriel Poe, Steel Viper, Cameron Cruise, Joey Melton, Evan Aho, DC Stratton, Dan Ryan, Shawn Douglas, Shane Southern, and Chris McMillan. Shane's picture remains on the screen a full second longer than the rest, until his image is slowly replaced by that of "The Wolf". During this, a cold sounding voice is heard throughout. "But that shouldn't stop me from having a bit of fun...now should it?" CUT-TO the warehouse. The man is walking away from the camera down a lonely old hallway. Nothing but the flickering lights, and the dripping water to keep him company, he simply walks away. In the background, the sound of several dogs barking can be heard just faintly. The barking begins to slowly grow louder as the picture begins to fade from the man. Once the picture is black, the barking is frenzied and loud. A graphic fades onto the screen as the dogs continue to bark. The graphic shows what looks like an atomic explosion in the background, while in the foreground, a sentence is written in bold red letters in a font that appears similiar to that used in the the Rambo movies for the main title. The sentence reads... "This dog can never be collared..."
