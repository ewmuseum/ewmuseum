My Faith, Your Lies

29 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: Bobby Jack Windham, in the windswept fields of the West Texas plains, wearing a -W- Clan hoodie and a gigantic, gaudy gold cross studded with diamonds that he clutches.) BOBBY JACK: "Chris Shepherd and Deacon want to know where ah've been. WHY AH HAVE BEEN SO SILENT... well, it's simple. Because God gave us the gift of MODESTY. He gave us the gift of HUMILITY. And, unlike you, Chris Shepherd, ah don't feel God wants me to come out on national television every fahve seconds to talk about how awesome ah am and how awesome your meal ticket is... even though HE did bless me with havin' near superhuman strength, the ability to run a 40-yard-dash in close to 4 seconds flat and purebred wrestlin' abilities that ain't NO ONE in the world could match. God gave me the physical gifts that made me a two tahm West Texas athlete of the year as named bah the illustrious Lubbock Avalanche-Journal and a three time NCAA Champion at Texas Tech University. But mah many, many, many accomplishments are not sumthin' ah love to talk about, for ah believe in humility." (Bobby Jack spits.) BOBBY JACK: "Y'see, ah have been deep in prayer these past few months, askin' Jesus for direction and instruction as how to go forth and carry on his works. For ah... ah have been rudderless these past few months. Ah have helped Christian Cruise gain his feet as a church goin' man... but ah mahself have lacked a compass. Not a moral compass, since ah still am the most pure and virtuous man in professional rasslin'. But a more direct compass. Ah do believe mah Uncle Troy is a good man... but he is obviously a sinful man who enjoys the vices. Ah have looked the other way for ah believe membership in The Windham Clan has led me to have this forum to preach HIS word -- his DIRECT word, and not the warped thoughts of a self-promoting charlatan lahe Chris Shepherd. But my uncle Troy has been pre-occupied lately with HIS mission to defeat Mike Randalls... leavin' me to wonder exactly what it is ah should be doin'." (Bobby Jack kicks some dirt.) BOBBY JACK: "Thankfully, ah have become good friends with JJ DeVille, who has assume day-to-day control and The Clan, a man who is a natural LEADER OF MEN who has a plan, not just for himself, but for everyone. JJ told me to look deep within mahself and mah soul... to figure out what *AH* want... and that he would see to it that AH WOULD GET NOT JUST WHAT AH WANT... BUT WHAT AH DESERVE! Because ah didn't come in here to help mah Uncle Troy feed his ego and ah certainly didn't come here to help Dan Ryan become a world champion. Ah came here... to do as God wants done." (Bobby Jack spits.) BOBBY JACK: "And JJ DeVille has put me in that position. Y'see, Shepherd, ya'll and Deacon's appearance here threw me for a bit of a loop. Because in rasslin', ah have not been around too many people who know Scripture as well as ah do. Ah no longer was the lone wolf bearin' his crosses. And ah turned introspective... but, thankfully, JJ talked some SENSE into me. He made me realize that ah have not been livin' up to mah potential as a wrestler... and as a Christian. So me and Christian Cruise, we joined JJ at ringside months ago... and JJ pointed at Rosie... and ah knew what needed to be done. AH KNEW AH HAD TO DESTROY HER. For, Shepherd, she is UNWED. She is an UNWED JEZEBEL who prostitutes around with Impulse -- a man who knows NUTHIN' about what it is to be a humble servant of the god, a man who touts his 'virtures' yet uses them to do nuthin' but stuff his wallet, as our leader JJ has so clearly stated tahm and tahm again. Rosie entered that ring and entered mah life for a REASON... for me to DESTROY her... so women everywhere would no longer look to her as a role model, but instead will turn to chastity and the purity Christ intends." (Bobby Jack stares down and then looks back up.) BOBBY JACK: "But, Shepherd and Deacon, we saw where YOU stood. YOU aligned yourselves with that wretched skank and a man of an unbridled and unchecked ego like Impulse... And, Deacon and Shep... it has made me realize that you two are not the men of faith you claim to be. Because you chose to side with a glory hog lahk Impulse for a reason. Because you two seek the glory for yourselves. You clad yourselves with the cross of Jesus yet give HIM none of what he deserves. Because, Shepherd, you are not spreading the word of the Gospel. And Deacon is not God's Warrior. Instead, Shepherd, you are the false man on top of the mountain toutin' the hosanas of the GOLDEN CALF that Deacaon truly is. And it will take me... a true Christian man... to destroy you to prove that, yes, FAITH IS THE REASON... FOR YOUR ETERNAL RUIN!" (Bobby Jack Tebows. FTB.) [the thread's next post — 30 Mar 2012] CHRIS: It's about time, but after dealing with the Only Star for the last bit, at least I got some honesty - hypocrisy with it, but honestly stating why you attacked a woman and took a life. And considering that you throw around Jesus' name, I'll challenge you to find a place where God tried to destroy a "jezebel", or any woman, man, child, or animal outside of a herd of pigs and trees outside a fig tree that didn’t bear fruit. He did some destroying - tossing over tables and cleaning the temple out of sellers. But the same Jesus who did that sat down with sinners, tax-collectors and heathens, and had dinner. If you'll remember... that kinda led to several people trying to kill him. Now, of course, I'm not expecting to get you to admit you were wrong in attacking Rose, but I'll explain a few things about why Deacon did what he did, if for no other reason than the reality that you may finally be listening. Standing beside Impulse. For as long as I remember, Deacon has stood beside someone when they're virtually alone. When they're trying to do what is right, Deacon's even been more likely. Sometimes that has cost him, but most of the time it has benefited him. I am going to do something that is a rarity in the NFW. Deacon is allowing me to open my mouth and share his heart, and in so doing, break what seems to be the greatest NFW taboo. When he came to the NFW, Deacon came to stop your spreading of a false word. That was his only focus, to the point of not involving himself in anything else. When Impulse was caught in the fight of his baby's life. When Impulse was alone. Deacon didn't go. He failed to leave the back and involve himself in the assault because it wasn't a part of his mission. Now, here comes that taboo - He. Was. Wrong. Though it wasn't he who put Rose through the table, though he could list a series of excuses… he won't because they ring hollow, not just to his ears, but surely to those who hear it, and most importantly, to the Spirit that reminded Deacon of his REAL job in the NFW – To be a representative of Jesus Christ to a world in need of a savior, not because Deacon is better as a man, but because Deacon will be better tomorrow than he was yesterday because God is taking this man and making him into the man of God he needs to be. Now, while he admitted his failing, I will call you on yours. You want to be righteous? Remember what Jesus said we must do - Be perfect as your Father in heaven is perfect. Are you perfect yet, Bobby Jack? Have you done all that God has? More importantly, when you get to that challenge spoken by Jesus himself, to be perfect as God is perfect, have you treated all people, the just and the unjust, the same, giving rain to both. Or have you put those who are not walking with Jesus through a table? Your words have already told the truth, leaving the question – when you were silent in prayer for all those months, tell me again, what did God say to you? And what did JJ Deville say? And then one more question – which one are you going to follow?
