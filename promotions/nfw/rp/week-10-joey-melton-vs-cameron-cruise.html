WEEK 10 - JOEY MELTON vs. CAMERON CRUISE

16 Mar 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fadein, to Cruise slouching in a chair in an empty lounge of the local hospital for a monthly check-up. Popping an Advil and closing the window from the cold night air, he swallows and takes a swig of the water bottle next to him, on the table and sighs.) CRUISE: Isn't this MAGICAL....the day before what some perceive as perhaps the luckiest day of the year, and my carcass is in a friggin' hospital. You know, with all said and done, Viper may be an idiot, but I give credit when it's due..(Stretches muscles)..the man can definately bring it. However, if it's one thing that I've been able to keep in mind this season...it's that once you've been given an opportunity, don't ever let it get away. Unfortunately, I'm in the situation now where I don't have too many of those left. Not only that, but this week everyone has been put up in the strangest of situations. Guys in the lockerroom want the other to win...then lose, to help them in the race to the Ultratitle. Yet they won't skip over to a nearby bar to have a drink. Now, don't get me wrong, that wasn't part of the contract I signed and don't expect anything to come of it, despite the cohesiveness Chris McMillan and I share in that of helping one another toward the playoffs. Sure, we'll probably go at it one more time, and if one of us doesn't make it into the postseason, that's alright too. But I'll be damned if the only friendships made during this season is Manson and Strife, Douglas and Matthews, and Ryan, Troy, and Melton. That's of course, outside the Alpha relationship I have with Mercedes. But that's something else, pardon me while I return to the point. Opportunity. It's one thing that I've finally been able to get a grasp of this season, and the one thing I'm not about to let go. Viper had to find that out the hard way last week. So what do I get in return? Joey Melton. Things are a tad bit different then the last time we threw down, ain't it Joey? Although, I've maybe faltered abit lately in the standings, here's the thing: Halftime is over. Call it a wake-up call, hell, call it anything you want, but the fact of the matter is, is that I'm coming back. It started with Promo, and now its gone through him, that strange Pariah guy, Chris, Viper, and now you. (Cruise gets unusually quiet for a few minutes, then looks up at the camera.) Just before I pinned Viper I heard Rice ask from the announcers' table if anyone believed in miracles. To be anywhere near contention halfway through the season is a miracle if you ask me. My winning streak as of late has been a miracle, whether or not my being in last place is a factor. I'm still in this thing. Getting past the jokes of what happened the first half, I got to thinking about the seriousness of it all. Can it be done? Hell, can I even make it close to the playoffs at the deficit I'm in? Do I believe in MIRACLES? Viper was in second place with the ten-point deficit he still has yet to get rid of. I'm STILL in last place after defeating him, only being able to shake things up for it, by doing what I said I was going to do, and pinning Viper. Deja Vu perhaps could be on the horizen. But I'm not looking. Ya know Joey, there was a time when I would most likely make a demand for at least a "THANK YOU" for helping you in the standings, because quite frankly, if it wasn't for my win, you wouldn't be as close as you are to Jean Rabesque right now. Pardon me if I tell you to piss off. Why? Because when you get down to the seriousness of it all, this match is not only pivotal, it's pivotal for the both of us as well as the whole CONFERENCE. Do I believe in miracles? You tell me. I'm comin' for proof Joey, whether you like it or not. Who knows Joey, maybe this week it might not just be me, but you as well, who gets a reality check....that he just...won't like. FADE. [the thread's next post — 20 Mar 2004] (FADE-IN: Joey Melton in front a NFW backdrop) MELTON: Cameron I'm not sure whether to pity or respect you. Coming to a decision would admit you're worth the time to figure out. Four wins later you're in need of a little humility so I'll spare myself the deep analysis and decide the old-fashioned way. (Melton reaches into his pocket, flips a quarter in the air, catches it and traps it on his left forearm.) So I respect you. You finally start to move your career in the right direction, and prove you benefit from some sort of training by stringing together a collection of wins, only to be made a fool of by the NFW's play-by-play team, notably Wildstar, someone who's ridden more backs in this sport than a winning jockey, someone who has accomplished only a little more than yourself. "Do you believe in miracles?" It's a million dollar business Cruise, dependent on good salesmen. That you have no problem being sold as a luckless winner, is a product of your good upbringing. You're a glass half-full kind of guy, and that's wonderful. Oh hell, it's a product of truth. How the hell did you get in this business Cruise? Did you pen together a winning essay in a promotional contest? "Why My Dream Is To Be A Professional Wrestler" By Cameron Cruise. The miracle isn't that you're winning, its that you're booked at all, but let's be honest...the NFw was in desperate need for warm bodies and good men who like to pretend they're good women by laying on their backs nightly. The business needs men like you, because even the great ones lose confidence or have to be sold as a badass to new fans from time to time. That's where you come strolling along. The glass is always half-full with you, because you've resigned yourself to accepting the truth, that it'll never be completely full. These last few miraclious weeks you've essentially just been taking small sips, careful not to finish too early. The difference between us Cameron, is that my cup for years now has runneth over, and luckless irritants like yourself get the honor of bathing in the spillage of my brillance. You get the crumbs I leave, Cruise. Nothing more. You're not going to the playoffs, you're not going to make any better living off this sport than you have to this point, and rumor is you're not satisfying your wife. Exactly what is Mercedes? Wife, sister? You two are kinda like the Whites from the Stripes. The relationship differs depending on which publication you read. I'm saying, killer...you've peaked. But, here's two simple truths for you. One, I've lost to men worse off than you are in my career, and two, I'm not showing up next month, er week, drunk with an arm tied behind my back. I'm showing up at wherever the hell this promotion is going next week and turning into the beacon of light that I am for so many luckless fools like yourself. People who don't have an inch of what I've had in my life, and are too dumb to care. For one week, Cameron you're a hero to these people because you get to drink from my cup. For one week, you're modest winning streak will seem like a promotion of sorts. You've proved you can put one foot in front of the other, now you get Melton. Enjoy the spotlight, but don't stop attending the night classes, or bumming off your more successful mate. The reign of Cameron Cruise, will be shortlived. Because while you deserved something good to happen in your life at some time, we create our own luck, and you've reaped just about the extent of what you've sewn over time. So here comes the overconfident Joey Melton. I know how the game is played Cruise. Some idiot will throw a bananna peel in the ring, I'll slip and get rolled up, and oh the hysteria Cruise wins again. What I hope is that you prove you're actually some sort of man and beat me straight up. If you do, believe me I won't be hiding my face the next week. As I said, I've lost to worse. Hell, this time last year I was wrestling midgets on cruise ships. Hey, I'm a whore for many things, including fat appearence fees and paid vacations overseas. And giving everyone a fair shake. There are men in this sport who would probably put a bullet in their head if they ever found themselves in an ironman cage match on a cruise ship against Little Voltron for the World Junior Junior title, but me? I see the beauty in it Cameron. I was luckless once myself. Fifteen years ago, I was you. I realized I liked being a top better than a bottom, but I've never forgotten those who didn't have the strength or opportunity to come with me. My hand's always extended Cruise. The two-sides of the coin are this: Either you'll make your name off me, and prosper or I'm going to give you the ass kicking of your life. I've lived with both for the last fifteen years fairly comfortably, because at the end of the day I'm still Joey Melton, the only beacon of light for luckless f*cks like yourself. (FTB) [the thread's next post — 23 Mar 2004] "Who are you, and what have you done with Joey Melton." (Cruise, his face distorted into the expression of discovery, stares into the camera.) CRUISE: Because honestly, the one I had intended on talking to would've usually blown me off and talked about how much worthless of a time I would be in the ring, and about how quick he'd pin me and be on his way to the back for a Mai Tai right afterward. But nice try for the efforts, although I'm sure the real one wouldn't be so nice on the critique. You've always had your hand extended.... (Cruise scoffs) I find it very intriguing how nine, ten weeks ago, you couldn't give two (bleep) about how I do or what I do. But now it seems we find each other in a precarious position. That one loss to Chris McMillan notwithstanding, every match I've been in for the past few weeks or so....I've won....as well as helped....you stay up in high contention. Especially the last one with Viper. I don't even have to guess....I KNOW ...that you were in the back watchin'.....grittin' you're teeth.....tissues in hand for when I was down....dancin' with Troy when I wasn't...even if you weren't Joey, the fact of the matter is that you sat in the back and prayed that I would win, so that you'd have a chance to go up the week following. Lo' and behold the week following. Me. But see, what you fail to realize is that yeah....I pride myself in achieving what I set out to do while never satisfied, but I do so while not giving a damn about what people say. Not VIPER. Not WILDSTAR. And most certainly not YOU. You stand there and talk to me about how well off I am, because I get the crumbs you leave and I get to drink from the same cup you do....which is actually kinda flattering, because ya know, while I do think of you as a jerk Joey, I respect you for what you've done in this business. Matter of fact, I'd be lucky if I attained the same amount of success that you have, name-breaking or not. I've taken ass-kickings before, and I'll probably have my resume shine with you on it, but it still leaves me wondering..... What's the matter Joey? Aren't you hungry anymore? Because if you aren't, then get up from the table and go to your room.....under your own power or mine. Jiminy Cricket sung "When You Wish Upon A Star", and as a result, motivated millions. I've been wishing for years, Joey. And I'll be damned if I'm going to let it be squashed by some tameless imposter like you. I'm ready to go Joey, more ready than you could ever imagine. Why? Because I'm Cameron Cruise, the only "luckless f*ck" around, that's willing to tell you otherwise. And that's a reality check, that you just....won't like. Fade. [the thread's next post — 26 Mar 2004] (FADEIN: Melton) MELTON: No Cruise, the real Joey Melton wouldn't think he'd have to come on regional television, and put forth the energy to explain exactly how stupid you really are. Some things go without saying. Don't stick your hand under a running lawnmower... Don't assume just because Troy's a woman, she's sexual... And....finally.... Cameron Cruise is a damn fool. I was pretty sure I did blow you off.......that I explained how much of a worthless being you are, in and out of the ring. But if you need me to spell it out, okay. Cammy.. Y O U S U C K (sigh) I knew it. I knew if I invited the NFW crew back in my manison to tape this promo, I'd feel it. The overwhelming sense of time slipping by. There's so many other things I could be doing right now, Cruise, in place of babysitting your stupidity. The point of these little spots is to draw the fans who don't have anything better to do, to an NFW arena on show night so they can fork over admission, and on average $20 of merchandise/concession money. Most weeks I'm sure Miles, Chris Rock, Cloris Leachman, hell whoever actually puts the fries in the grease around here would want every possible word from your mouth, and then bring in a recommended filmstudent to edit your spots in hopes they can pass any part of you, or your wisdom off as cutting edge, and entertaining. Alone Cammy, you need the grace of God to sell. But this month..you're partnered against the good wrestling lord himself, Joey Melton! You're drinking from my cup. Cloris knows that..which is why the extra mile I'm walking here, is a treat for her and nothing more. On my name alone money has been made. Unless you're stupid enough to think that on the MONTH TEN programs, handed out by the local retards Leachman finds each week to voluteer as part of the "NFW Community Outreach Program", your PROP 48 mug will be next to mine. People go to the zoo to see exotic animals, Cruise. The birds who have flown in and made their nest on the grounds are just a unbilled bonus. That's what you are. An unbilled bonus to the people who need to walk from the 5,000 seat arenas we're playing to here and say outloud to themselves on the public transportation trip home, "I could beat that guy." Or, "As much as working on the docks for peanuts an hour sucks, I could be him." Middle-America doesn't need a photograph or bio next to the description of incompetence. They have you. Cameron Cruise. The wrestling world's bonus baby. The only way I'll be losing this match, jackass, is out of sexual frustration. Recently I've challenged myself to find a measure of self-control. Instead of giving into every sexual whim, to set a challenge and chase one goal. In place of the thrill of the entire Swedish Bikini team, I've focused my energies on the youngest with the tightest (CENSOR). Swedish bikini team member of course is a metaphor for Miss Lindsay Troy, who apparently was too busy managing Dan Ryan or trading masturbatory secrets with Ivy (Melton shudders at Ivy's mention) to show and help cut this promo. I'm wearing a figurative chastity belt, and only Troy can unlock it. I've never really been sexually frustrated before Cruise. Your legs get weak, your mind drifts, and you find yourself watching the "Accused" starring Jodie Foster for all the wrong reasons. I need to be well again! So, yes...under my current state, there's a slim chance I'll go down by a dropkick, and simply not get up...reveling in the lost sensation of having my shoulders to the floor. If I had a gay bone in my body (I'm not counting impeccable fashion) I'd warn you could be in for a very weird night, but be rest assured I'm as straight as they come. As I was saying, frustration could beat me, but if Troy's as good of a manager as she bills, she'll see this, roll down her "I heart Melton" panties I gave her for Christmas, lay herself over a strong wooden table, and assume the role of "Good Cop". How I do love to role-play... At MONTH TEN, I'll be the beacon of light, the seemingly interested party paid (I still say checks that clear around here are just a nasty rumor) to treat you as a geniune threat, I'll be the man ordered to make you appear to be something other than what you really are. A dumb, lifeless, f*ck. The best roles are the ones that have a large grain of truth in them. Yes, Cruise, I'm forecasting a great performance. And maybe afterwards a little p..(quick FTB)
