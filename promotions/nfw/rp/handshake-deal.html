Handshake Deal

21 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: Troy Windham, in his neckbrace, sits on the patio of his South Beach parlor.) TROY: "Dan! You don't call. You don't write. But you do still pack one mean punch, my friend.) (Troy rolls his neck.) TROY: "Now, Ryan... you and I have been around this industry for decades. Our handshake deal -- you wash your back, yadda yadda yadda -- we both knew that as soon as it was prudent, someone was going to break it. That's how the game has been played. And you and I are better at it than anyone else. So, Dan..." (Troy smirks.) TROY: "I have no problem with what you did to me. I'm not going to come looking for a receipt. But you DID say something interesting to me. That I ceded power to JJ... 'of all people.'" (Troy adjusts his neck.) TROY: "A lot of people have been asking the SAME thing for months. Why did I grant JJ the day-to-day control of The Windham Clan? Well, it's simple. My goal was to lead a successful takeover of a promotion, something that MANY men have tried but NO ONE has been able to do. As you know, I am an innovator. I push both buttons AND the limits. I have reinvented this industry several times over... and I added yet another feather in my oh-so-stylish cap. And, yes, Dan. You helped me in my efforts. You were an absolute beast in The Battle for Control. Without you, I do not have this accomplishment." (Troy shakes his head.) TROY: "But, after our accomplishments, I set myself ANOTHER goal. You and I, we've already done our dance. But I have never been in a one-on-one match with Mike Randalls. The only man left I haven't beaten. The only man left who I have never stood in the ring with... with all of the eyes of the world on us... and taken on. All eyes have been on me since I first hopped the railing. It's how I live. It's what I THRIVE on. And now, once again with all eyes on me... I'm going to take on the only man I need to take on. I don't WANT to take him on. I *NEED* to take him on. I *NEED* to beat Mike Randalls. And you, Dan, of all people... you know what it's like to HAVE that need." (Troy shakes his head.) TROY: "The paperwork and day-to-day details -- that was never my thing, Dan. I just wanted to grab that brass ring and humiliate Eddie Mayfield, a joint hobby of ours. I've moved on... and I need to hand things on to someone else. You, Dan? You made it clear you wanted the strap, and that was your only concern. August? Peter Windham? They're half-wits. Christian Cruise and Bobby Jack? One's a man almost as old as we are who doesn't know who he is and the other... despite my last name... has been something of a disappointment, more interested in preaching a book I never even read than in destroying everyone in his path, which he is more than capable of doing. So that left ONE man left." (Pause.) "JJ." (Troy adjusts his seat.) TROY: "I've known for years what very few have known. JJ DeVille HAS it. He was my underling and willing to take my abuse... just to get close to see what it took to become a superstar. He learned my offense. He learned how I go about preparing. And he proved to the entire world that he can hang with anyone... because he's one of the few men who has ever pinned me convincingly. But, most importantly, he learned how to get everyone's eyes on him, and how to get EVERYONE talking about him. And he's done it in a way that... quite frankly... I never thought possible." (Troy chuckles.) TROY: "He's a schemer. He's a plotter. He's like every villain from every season of Survivor multiplied by 50,000. He's become the most hated person on this roster by taking his mastery of the paperwork... of the little details I never concerned myself with... and ruining lives with it. And, yes, it has gotten to his head. And, yes, that power he has... I know he's scammed me a few times with it. There's a wedge between us, now. But he's found a way to stand out and to make himself known. And, being that everyone on this roster has wanted at some time to kill him -- myself included -- I think he's gotten that down pat. And now, Dan, you've gone after him." (Troy adjusts his neck and grunts in pain.) TROY: "I've told JJ time and time again that GETTING to the stage where we sit isn't all that hard. A lot of people have had the spotlight. But STAYING there and making the bright lights brighter... that's the hard part. He got over. But now... now he has to bring it. And you want the NFW World Title. And JJ's going to do everything he can to keep you from getting it." (Troy purses his lips.) TROY: "I created The Windham Clan. I've given it to JJ. And I want to see if he lives up to the name. Dan Ryan, I know how great you are. I know that you are in the .0001% of the absolute best in this sport. Now it's time for him to see if he's got it. But, Ryan... I'm out of this one. Because I want Randalls. I need Randalls. And I'm going to GET Randalls." (FTB)
