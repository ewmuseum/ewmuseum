Press Conference

4 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene- Minutes after the predlue{OOC: I know, I know}the gathering around the enrance to Fenway Park is still abuzz, it goes ballistic as Red Sox GM Theo Epstein walks up to the podium.) Epstein: Hi guys, what's up? Reporter: So what's the news Theo? Why did you call this conference? Epstein: I didn't.... (Police cars come speeding in and sliding to a halt, officers jump out and stand behind their cruisers in position to fire. Swat Vans come speeding in as well. Police snipers make themselves seen from nearby rooftops. Two police helicopters hover overhead.) "Shake your tail-feather" off the Bad Boys II Soundtrack blares off the speakers from inside Fenway Park... (From the top of "The Green Monster", the infamous left-field wall of Fenway, drop three rope lines. Down rappel three men in police riot gear....it quickly becomes apparent that the three men are Colt, Beretta, and Remington, collectively known as The Swat Team ) Epstein: ...as I said... I didn't...they did...(Epstein steps off the podium) Remington: WHAT'S UP BOSTON! Reporter #2: Allow me to be the first to say "Welcome back", gentlemen, are you making a return in the world of wrestling? Colt: We never really left guys, we've been in Japan, but we've still been around... Reporter #3: What's the competition like over there? Beretta: We're not here to talk about Japan, we're here to talk about our last match. (crowd is abuzz) Reporter #4: Last match? You guys wrestled your last match? Colt: Not yet, but we're going to....at the innaugural NFW All-Star Week. (crowd is abuzz) Remington: We're here to announce that we're going to retire after our final match at that very card, yes. Reporter #5: But why? Injuries? Age? You guys appear to be in the best shape of your lives.... Beretta: Make no mistake, we are...but to be honest, we've not been really "into it", for quite awhile... Colt: The reason for this is that, well...let's face it, anyone with half a brain knows who we've modeled our careers after... Beretta: ...OH WHAT A RUSH!... (reporters laugh) Remington: A little know fact is, well they wanted one last run in the "big time" so they could go out the way they wanted to. Well, everyone knows what happened (the three give a quick blessing across their chests), these two guys here decided to come back to the states and do this one last time. We wanted to go out where we started, well the fact is that company closed a long time ago, not the stupid ass re-incarnations, but the way WE remember it is and was gone for a long time. We then talked about finishing up in "another" promotion run by Quentin Sulliva, but that wasn't happening, so we talked to him and came here. Colt: He has been an ass to some, but he's been good to us, so we figured, "What's the worst he's gonna say? No?" Here we are. Beretta: We figured, one last match, against the best in this business has to offer.... Reporter #3: Who are you looking for? Colt: Fargo... Remington: Mueller... Beretta: and Carlton. Yeah, they haven't been in the public eye in awhile, but they are still recognized as the NFW World Tag Team Champions. Before, you ask, no, we don't want any belts, this isn't about that, this is about us going out on top. OSS, one match, that's it. You game? Colt: If you're smart, you'll say "no". Remington: Show's over... Just Mark: (off camera) Well, you heard it here. The Swat Team, whoever the hell they are, have travelled here to the NFW to retire and call out the OSS. Geez...first that Old Polish Sausage and now this? (Scene fades after the three get into the nearest Swat Van and speed away)
