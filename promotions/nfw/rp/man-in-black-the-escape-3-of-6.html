Man In Black : The Escape (#3 of 6)

21 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I don't know anything. Of course they're gonna come after me. Guys like that, they don't do much thinking." A figure clad in the unlikely combination of dark clothing, with a worn fedora on his head and a red and white bandana over his face, races through a cornfield. An observer far, far above, would see that he's moved in nearly a perfect straight line from where he started - a warehouse-looking building several miles behind him. The man stops, hands on his knees, gulping for air. It's been 30 minutes since he last took a drink from a fairly clean-looking stream, and he could really do with another. But he's so close now. Not close to a highway, or any road in fact - he's made a point of staying away from them. For better or worse, this ends tonight. He straightens up, and looks off ahead of him, where the barn he saw a while ago is getting larger. Steadily larger. He's not too far away now, and begins running again. A few minutes later, he's outside the barn now. It's abandoned. This is good. He looks around, and realizes he's in tough ... he's got nothing on him, nothing that could be used as a weapon, and no identification. He looks around, shaking his head. All there is around is a couple bags of fertilizer ... and ... His eyes grow wide. He runs to whatever it was that caught his attention ... and starts digging.
