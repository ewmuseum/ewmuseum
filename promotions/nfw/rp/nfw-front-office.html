NFW: Front Office

7 Apr 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: ESTABLISHING SHOT - 420 Avenue of the Americas, EXTERIOR, DAY. ESEN GLOBAL ENTERPRISES. The camera pans up to the top floors of the building, NFW OFFICES.) (CUTTO: Penthouse level, and President Hot Property EDDIE MAYFIELD'S office. He's sitting behind his huge mahagony desk, the EDDIE MAYFIELD FRANKLIN MINT commimerative World Heavyweight title sits propped up at the front of the desk like a huge, gold, egomaniacal placard. MAYFIELD is in shirtsleeves, and staring at a ton of paperwork on his desk, along with a few spent boxes of Camel Reds. Off to the side we see his 96" Plasma screen with LEFT 4 DEAD game running through the 'please press start' demo, the sound on mute) MAYFIELD: (Hitting the intercom button on his desk phone) "Man, I don't know what the f[BLEEEEP] to make of this. Get your ass in here..." (He leans back, holding his forehead as the double oak doors open, and SHAMON moonwalks in backwards, wearing Michael Jackson 'Victory' era clothes: GLITTERING drum major jacket, tight black pants and a low slung belt with a crazy belt buckle that looks like an ill picture frame on it - everything in pearl white! SHAMON has a green bankers' visor on over his impossibly wet jherri curl. He stops, points at the door, does a spin and grabs his crotch with both hands, screaming at the top of his lungs!) SHAMON: (Shaking) "EDDIE DID YOU WANNA, DID YOU WANNA, DID YOU WANNA SEE ME EDDIE?! OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" (MAYFIELD winces) MAYFIELD: "Shamon, where is the payroll sheets?" SHAMON: (Thrusts his hips, spins again, and hops up on his tip-toes, pointing at his desk!) THE WAY YOU MAKE-A ME FEEL NOW EDDIE.... HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!" MAYFIELD: "motherf[BLEEEEEEEEEEEEP!] (He picks up a stack of papers, all covered with translucent stains!) YOU DRIPPED THAT SH[BLEEEP!] ALL OVER THE PAY REPORTS!I CAN'T ADD THIS SH[BLEEEP!] UP NOW!" SHAMON: "YOU'VE BEEN HIT BY (Hah!) - YOU'VE BEEN STRUCK BY (Hah!) - A SMOOTH CRIMINAL!" MAYFIELD: (Standing up and throwing the whole stack of paper at SHAMON, making him jump up and down, trying to catch 100 pieces of paper in mid air! Paper flitters all over the room!) "YOU STUPID PIECE OF BEDAZZLED SH[BLEEEP!] PRINT THIS SH[BLEEEP!] OUT AGAIN AND YOU DO THE PAYROLL! GET OUT OF MY OFFICE!" (SHAMON gathers more papers up at his chest, and sadly moonwalks out of the room, the door closing behind him. MAYFIELD growls at no-one in particular, then hits the intercom again)AND WEAR THAT FU[BLEEEP!] SHOWER CAP I BOUGHT YOU THIS TIME!" (FADEOUT.)
