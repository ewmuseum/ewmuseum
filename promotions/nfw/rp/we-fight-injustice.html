We Fight Injustice

25 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: JJ DeVille's Arlington, VA townhouse, overlooking the skyline of Washington DC. Flanking on each sides are The Chosen Ones -- Christian Cruise, with his dead eyes, holding a large cross and Bobby Jack Windham "Tebowing." In the foreground, squatting, is AUGUST DE LA ROSSI wearing a Kony 2012 hoodie. In the background, still with a bandage on his forehead, wearing his Jos. A. Ban men's collection business suit is JJ DeVille.) AUGUST: "Just one month ago, Trayvon Martin was a 17-year-old black teenager... an oppressed minority wearing a hoodie and carrying a pack of Skittles. But then he was shot in the back, another victim of an unjust system which punishes those who are already punished. It is a corrupt system that I have spent a LIFETIME fight against, despite my father's career as an executive for Goldman Sachs. But injustices don't just happen on the streets of Central Florida. They don't just happen in Afghanistan and Iraq, where the attempts of the AmeriKKKan colonialists have been fought so vigorously against. They don't just happen on our music charts, where geniuses like Jeff Magnum go unheard while Nickleback gets airplay. They happen right here, in the professional wrestling industry. They happen right here... in New Frontier Wrestling. And no man has suffered these injustices more than this man... our leader... OUR HERO... JJ DeVille." (JJ smirks and steps forward. August puts his arms around JJ's shoulders.) JJ: "Thank you, August. Now you may return to your place." (August nods and steps back.) "As I've said many, many times... there is no one in this industry who has SUFFERED more than I did. Just as I was on my way to superstardom... Chad Merrit's checks start bouncing and the CSWA falls apart. And I was immediately BLACKBALLED from this sport. My contract demands were supposedly 'highly unreasonable'. I didn't deserve a seven figure salary. I didn't deserve penthouse hotel suites and my own jet. But the story was more than that. It was because the POWER BROKERS of this industry... were jealous of my mentor Troy Windham... and of myself. And there's no bigger example of that than Eddie Mayfield, who I pinned NUMEROUS of times years ago. But alas, because of this system rigged against me... I wasn't allowed in a locker room." (JJ sneers as August shakes his head in shock.) JJ: "Instead, I spent my 20s going to college at North Carolina State University. I was recognized all over campus. 'Hey, weren't you a professional wrestler? What happened to you?' Frat boys and sorority girls looking at me like I was a failure. A PARIAH. I spent my days going to class in the morning and leading Pilates classes for fat girls from the Outer Banks at night. And every night, I'd turn on the television... and would watch wrestling. And one person stood out to me. A man who dubbed himself IMPULSE." (JJ purses his lips.) JJ: "I saw you bragging about yourself, about how good you were... and I saw THOUSANDS of holes in your arsenal. I saw THOUSANDS of ways to beat you. A man who prided himself on being a technical wizard was, frankly, someone I could beat without any question. And then I watched your oh so dramatic victory of the NFW TV Title. How you took off your mask for the first time and introduced yourself as Randall Knox... the protege of Eli Flair. And that's when it made perfect sense to me. The only reason you had a career and had the NFW's marketing forces, the mouthpiece Kerry O'Connor, trumpeting your every success was because of who held the door open for you." (JJ shakes his head.) JJ: "When we first arrived on the scene, Knox, we called you a puppet of Eli. How everything you said was word-for-word what Eli Flair and that moron Ivy said for years about Troy Windham. You denied it... and, yet, mysteriously, they started showing up in every single one of the NYU Film Class student films you send in for promos. They have your back. They're on good terms with Eddie Mayfield and Dan Ryan. And that's why, Impulse, you've been pushed down our throats, despite everyone who actually knows a good damn thing about this industry knowing you're a sniveling little s(BLEEEEP)." (JJ purses his lips.) JJ: "You've failed constantly, yet you're still touted, hiding behind your convenient 'Rules of Engagement' which gives you an out every single time you lose. How you have integrity and no one else does. Well, how much integrity does it take to launch a multi-step plot which resulted in piss being thrown in my face? HUH, IMPULSE?" (JJ shakes his head.) JJ: "Impulse, I briefly made you my slave. You did so by claiming it was the 'right thing to do' for a locker room that hates you... albeit not as much as they hate me. But then, just a few weeks later, you had a chance to do the one thing the locker room would have cheered you for. You had a chance to rid the NFW of the ultimate Locker Room Cancer for good. You had a chance to hit me with a chair and take me out... but you didn't do it. Why? You claim its because of your 'Rules of Engagement' and that you didn't want to have me 'break' you. But the real reason, Impulse... is because you're a coward. The minute you hit me with a chair is the minute you're no longer The Golden Child. You're no longer St. Randall Knox. All of the masks that the kids by and all of the positive press you generate is GONE out the window. You're no longer the marketing darling. You no longer stand out." (JJ now laughs.) JJ: "But it backfired on you. Everyone in the locker room now thinks you're the biggest laughingstock since Kill The Coopa. Despite Team NFW's attempts at spinning you as such a hero... we all heard the boos from the upper deck. We all heard the chant. We all heard them saying 'Impulse Sucks.' Here I was, the man who shut down shows and cancelled a European tour... in the middle of the ring. And they're not booing me. They're booing YOU." (JJ smirks.) JJ: "Impulse, you think this was about breaking you? No. It wasn't. And while that would have been a benefit... this was about EXPOSING you. I showed the world that you're a fraud. You're a phony. You're a self-serving, smug little turd. You, Impulse... you're a HEEL. You have the same amount of integrity that I have... which is to say NONE. But at least I'm honest about... which, actually, means YOU have less integrity than even I do." (JJ purses his lips one last time.) JJ: "Now, Impulse, I'm sure you'll come out here and say how all you've ever wanted was to just 'be a wrestler' and whatever nonsense. But guess what, big shot? I'm going to expose you for a fraud in that department, too. When you complete that paperwork for Reloaded... there won't be any games on my behalf. No rigging the vote. All I'm doing, Knox, is focusing on you. I'm going to the gym and I'm focusing only on YOU. I'm not thinking past Reloaded. But you?" (JJ shakes his head.) "You're already lobbying for another World Title shot. Don't think I don't know about that. But guess what? AFTER I beat you, Randall... after you're pinned by a man you've pegged as a no-talent hack for months... the only title shot you'll be aiming for is going to be in another league." (The entire Clan laughs. FTB.)
