Things HAVE Changed

6 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: Calvin Carlton, standing behind an oak desk, butterfly stitches in his forehead, looking angry, holding Sweet Betty Lee.) CALVIN: A lot of people on this roster, in both *MY* conference and in the North, have talked about the changes felt here. (Calvin pauses as the camera zooms in on him.) You want to ask me why I did this. You want to ask me why I brought in Eddy Love and had him slap everyone in the face. You want to ask why I spat on the roster ov every person who has spilled their blood for this promotion, for every person who has dreamed of winning The Ultratitle. (Calvin again pauses.) It's because I care. It's because, deep down inside, I care about each and every single one of you people. (Calvin leans back in his chair, picking up the tennis racket.) For the first few months of this promotion, I stayed out of the limelight, attempting to allow each and every single one of you a chance to shine. A chance for each and every single one of you to jump up and TAKE THIS PROMOTION by storm. A chance to be the owner of the Ultratitle. Well, guess what? Each and every single one of you BLEW YOUR CHANCE. Shane Southern? The so-called Hero of A New Generation? He's been distracted, unsure of how to go about things, listening to a failed wrestler by the name of Aaron Douglass for advice. Hornet? The so-called greatest wrestler of all time? He's a failure who can't put the pills away, who can't put his addictions down, who can't do what needs to be done to win what is his birth-right. Jean Rabesque, our leader so far? Jean Rabesque is a man without killer instinct, without the guts to dig down deep and snatch what he things is his... a spot on the tip of the tongue of every wrestling fan alive. Steel Viper? He has the desire, he has the skills but what he is lacking is the intelligence to beat his foes. Matthews? A head case, always has been, always will be. McMillan? Promo? Garvin? Cruise? All good, young wrestlers who haven't stepped up. Even Joey Melton... my boyhood hero... (Cal shakes his head in disbelief.) Even Joey Melton has been distracted by Lindsay Troy and the tempt of finding women... So, me and my momma did what we thought we had to do. She saw a talent in Deacon. I decided to do her better and land the greatest this sport has ever seen. I went out and signed Eddy Love. Now, this decision might have pissed a few of you off. Eddy Love slapping you in your face might have you angered at me. It might have you making threats at me. Hornet might quit. Shane Southern might come after me. Well, you know what I say about all this anger? GOOD. I want you all to get med, to get a fire under your asses, to try and show me that my decision in bringing in The Southern Dandy was wrong. I doubt it, I doubt that ANY of you can take E-Lo out, he knows none of you can... but I want you all to try and prove Cal wrong. No one EVER has my entire life. If you want to quit and fly home, But I suggest one thing to you all... You all saw what went on with the NFW North. You all saw who came back and did what to whom. You saw me and my charges get placed in stretchers. You saw the takeover of that conference. That means one thing. You all need me. You might think I'm wrong. You might think that you can do this on your own, without me. You might think that you can beat everyone in this conference by yourself. That you can beat E-Lo, The Hurricane, The REAL Southern Dandy. You might think you might be able to then go after the NFW North representative. Or even better, go home on your plane and show the world what you really are... nothing more than a coward who isn't man enough to get the job done. Go ahead. Think you can do this by yourself. Think you can win it all without compromising at least a tiny bit or your morals. Guess what? You're wrong if you think that. There's a group of men running that conference bent on having EACH AND EVERY SINGLE ONE OF US do their bidding. If you go solo on this, guess what? You aren't going to make it. So, for each and every single one of you planning to come after me, planning to get revenge on me for what I did to you... think again. If so much as one hair is hurt on my body, if so much as one hair is hurt on my momma's body... I'm going to cut you loose. See what happens trying to fight this fight by yourself. You won't be able to. Like I said at All-Star Week. It's my way... or the highway. Tell your sister. Tell your daughter. But don't tell my momma, 'cause she already knows. If you want to be the man, you have to beat all the other men. And to beat all the other men... you have to be on my good side. That's the best advice you'll ever get in your life. Use it. [the thread's next post — 6 Feb 2004] Fade-in: Lindsay Troy...doesn't matter where she is. What does matter is that Cal opened his big mouth again... Cal, you couldn't find a woman if she lifted up her skirt and said "take me." So don't fault Joey for finding a winner and sticking with it. And certainly don't be a hater because you got the short end of Joey Melton's Attention Stick. You sit behind that big desk of yours and you think you've got it all figured out. Sittin' mighty pretty, aren't you? On top of the world, feeling like a winner? You haven't won yet. And you won't beat me. One way or the other, Cal...I'll be the big winner in the grand scheme of things. And I certainly don't plan on losing to a sexually repressed momma's boy. Love me or hate me, but I'm not going anywhere. So you'd better get accustomed to that fact real fast, or else you're going to have more problems than you know how to handle. Pause And Eddy Love? Not impressed. In the slightest. But really, next time you find a bunch of guys knocked out by the rest of Calvin's Flunky Brigade, make sure you do the job right so they don't remember what happened or who did it. Enjoy this lull while it lasts, Eddy. Rest assured that our paths will cross at some point, and I don't see the conversation being all too pleasant. Fade... [the thread's next post — 6 Feb 2004] (CUT TO: Calvin Carlton, behind his desk.) CALVIN: Lindsay Troy, I already warned you. Now, you better thank your lucky stars that I'm a firm believer in the phrase of actions speaking louder than words or else you'd already be buried. Like I told the world minutes ago, Lindsay... there's now two ways to win this. You either win by doing things *MY* way, or you do things by collaborating with Avery Prosser. Prosser tried to destroy me at All-Star Week but he didn't get the job done... and I FULLY intend on having one of MY men win this thing. Lindsay Troy, I have big plans for the NFW South and for the Ultratitle. There is one man who is, without a doubt, the clearcut favorite to win this... and that man is Eddy Love. But there is another, another man who I reached out to... perhaps out of charity, perhaps out of debt... because I felt he could be the prize of my momma's eye. That man is the man you now manage, Joey Melton. Like I said, Troy... I have big plans for the future of this promotion, the future of this Ultratitle, the future of myself. You are NOT a part of those plans, nor will you ever be. All you have done is acted as a DISTRACTION. All you have done is taken ONE of the men I can trust to get the job done, turned his attention away from yours truly, and led him astray from the ultimate plan of dominance I formulated. Lindsay Troy, the ONLY place you have on this planet, as far as I'm concerned, is to do what you did before you crawled out of your rathole... that's go back to dancing on tables. That's to go back, serving rich men such as myself and E-Lo drinks and thick, $50 cigars at the country club... that's go back to being three words that every woman, except my momma, should adhere two. Silent and compliant. It's cute that you want to hold Joey Melton's robes. It's cute that your so desperate for sex from a famous wrestler that you'll carry Joey Melton's boots. But what is NOT cute is how you distracted him from my ultimate aims. Lindsay Troy, if you get out of line one more time... I'm going to show you for what you really are, for what I showed Doc Martenas as. You're nothing more than a filthy slampig with loose morals. Tell your sister. Tell your fatherless daughter. But don't tell my momma, 'cause she already knows. You're looking for attention from a REAL man, toots. Be careful. (Cal points the racket at the camera.) You might just get what you're after. (FTB) [the thread's next post — 6 Feb 2004] Cal, I didn't know a guy your age could get Alzheimer's...but it's apparant that your memory is seriously lacking. Let's not forget who had a hand in tricking me to be Melton's manager. Let's not forget who visited me at the corporate office of Radicomm Worldwide International: the business that my father started that my sister now exclusively runs, with myself still on the Board of Directors. Let's not forget who gushed to Melton about my multiple Fortune 500 "50 Most Powerful Women in Business" list. That was alllll you, Cal. And before you keep spewing your Foundation's Propaganda, let me correct you on one thing. You haven't done sh*t for Joey Melton. You were all set and ready to kick him out of your life once you found out that his ex wife froze all his assets, rendering him penniless. EYE was the one that bailed him out. EYE was the one who got him off the streets. EYE was the one who is helping him out with his legal troubles. NOT YOU. The only thing you've reached out for is more of your mother's money, because you don't know the value of a hard earned dollar. Have you ever held a real job in your life, Cal? Let me answer that for you: no. I fully intend to be part of Joey Melton's plans for the Ultratitle, because if he walks out on me, he'll be walking out on all the help I've given him. And that's something that he can't afford. He needs me, more than you could ever comprehend in that pathetic little brain of yours. Let's face it Cal. Joey Melton isn't stupid. And I don't think that your dumbing down of the situation by bringing in Eddy Love is going to change his mind. I'm worth at least three times the amount you, your momma, and your precious foundation is put together. Don't you ever talk to me like I'm out of your price range, because boy...you're out of mine. So do yourself a favor and stick to the things you know: mooching off your mother, sucking up, taking it up the ass, and screwing around with people's lives. Silent and compliant is not the mantra that I live by. And as far as being concerned about what's going to happen if I don't keep my mouth shut, well...I don't have to worry much about that, because you don't have the balls to do anything about it. Just bury your head in the sand and wait for your f*cking allowance check.
