Field Trip

14 Jul 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADE-IN: Inside a Chic-Fil-A in Greensboro, NC. The Chic-Fil-A dining room, as is tradition on Sunday afternoons, fills steadily, till the capacity of each employee’s nerves screams in bloody mental excess. Their lives were a Norman Rockwell original hours ago. But with area churches letting out in succession, hundreds of malnourished sinners, begin to file through the grease stained doors, looking to lose Jesus behind prepackaged heart disease. Salvation is fun. But nothing packs them in like good food at bargain prices. Maybe if God had marketed that, the poor lost behind the curtains serving the unclean would have hope of completing their shift without further scarring. In the back-left of the dining room, Calvin Carlton and Joey Melton, two men who ordinarily would see their occupancy as a death sentence, picnic at a window booth. The view, muddy as it is from poor management, is not a relief. From every angle, Melton and Carlton sit trapped, by heredity gone horrible, horribly wrong. Neither seems concerned. Their mere presence stands out. Joey or Calvin could not have tried to blend in, if that was the intention. The plan apparently was to boldly be the sore thumb. Both men are dressed in nice suits. Their sharp appearance draws muffled laughs, from the meek that don’t understand, and hysterical stares from the paupers who, mistakenly believe they are the ones to inherit the earth. Two men, oblivious to the crimes of fashion and society circling them, know better. The spread on Melton and Carlton’s table looks healthier, more expensive, and handled with care. No reheated treats seeking revenge. In the face of Chic-Fil-A, it’s a spread any woman would give up their virginity for. With this set-up, under a shady tree with a nice summer breeze rolling through their hair, Carlton could score. No food group was left off the menu. The only hint of modesty: zip-lock bags. Calvin pours a glass of Champagne. He lifts the wine glass with two fingers, massages it under his nose, and allows himself a sip. As the taste overcomes him, Carlton forces himself back to earth. The Champagne nearly shuffled aside his vow to put Joey first. Make sure MISTER MELTON is doing well. Carlton had written himself twenty post-it notes the night before, relaying the message. Hell of a vintage, strong enough to sweep aside manners. Calvin: Did you try one of the sandwiches? Grilled chicken over here...Tuna, a personal favorite...Juanita cut the crust off most of them. (Carlton accosts a sandwich and thrusts it in front of Melton.) Or if you like, crusts intact! Melton: What is she, terrified of making decisions? Lovely. Sounds like a hoot. Calvin: I had her make both! You said I needed to consult you first... Melton: (stunned expression) On business you ass, not lunch. (Melton looks at the spread.) Melton: Juanita...I thought you told me she was imprisoned on sexual assault charges? Calvin: She was. The first one anyway. Melton: The first one? Wait...no. I’m going to regret asking, I’ve learned quickly with you. Carlton laughs, and watches the meaning fly over his head. Calvin: Momma just likes the name. Juanita served Momma for over twenty years. The first time she had to berate the new one...she slipped and muttered “Juanita” defiantly under her breath. Melton: Old habits die hard... Calvin: Yes! Momma felt like the new Juanita needed to confirm to OUR lives. Like naming the new family cat. Mr. Beaks died of breast cancer. Momma couldn’t bring herself to name the new one anything but. It’s a means to right a wrong. Of telling God he made a mistake. (Calvin stops for a beat. His eyes begin to swell) Calvin: Mr. Beaks wasn’t supposed to die! Melton: Cats can get breast cancer? Calvin: Oh yeah! Melton: Geez. I thought stem cell was supposed to shed light on a cure for us by now. Calvin: All of Michael J. Fox’s meddling is slowing the research down! He lost the IQ test to Valerie. She’s the smart one, parading around as a twit. SHE should be the one leading the drive to cut red tape. Melton: Agree whole-heartedly. Coffee... (Calvin quickly pours coffee into Melton’s mug.) Melton: Freshly imported from a Columbian mountain? Calvin: Daily! Melton: I was kidding. (beat) You’re serious aren’t you? (Carlton nods.) Beautiful. Calvin: I knew it. (smiles) Melton: (sighs disgustedly) Don’t do that. Calvin: Do what MISTER MELTON? Melton: Start a thought as a tease to be asked to finish. Now I feel so controlled. Like I’m too stupid to realize that you’re waiting for a reaction. (pause) Melton: You knew what? Calvin: That being with you would be like reliving my childhood. Melton: Oh geez. If this starts to get sexual...I’ll file the same charges you had brought up against Juanita One. Calvin: No, no no no no. MISTER MELTON I’ve ALWAYS wanted to go on a field trip. So when you said, we’re going to mingle with the other class at headquarters, pack a lunch, do my online checking, and take back the slow Pup, I got chills! Melton: Look Carlton, this is serious. This isn’t about giving you some cheap thrill, a private school never allowed. Calvin: I understand! Believ--- Melton: It’s about getting our minds right for ascension. Look around you. Get a sense of who’s in here. (Calvin looks around. For the first time since wiping his hands with a baby wipe after entering, he’s scared. Who are these hideous people and why aren’t coalition forces securing the exits. The way the beasts dress, and smell, the sheer pace of ignorance at which they consume saturated fat like its medicine for the soul. Carlton can’t breath. He shouldn’t be here. He never liked the zoo as a child. Help. HELP!) Melton: Now look at you. (Carlton, pale and sweating uncomfortably looks at himself. He’s a handsome devil. Momma said so herself.) Melton: Now me. (Beaming, Carlton checks Melton out.) Melton: What’s the difference? (Calvin raises hand in air.) Melton: (runs his left hand over his forehead, sighing) Carlton. Calvin: They were born disadvantaged. Melton: Nicely played. (Carlton is delighted) That’s right. These people, the great unwashed, are poor by creation and design. You and I...we were laid on a different path. Calvin: Path of Gr-Gr-Gr-Gr-Greaaaaaaaaatnessss! Melton: Okay if you do Tony The Tiger one more time, I’m going to f**king kill you...Art Of War Carlton. Know Thy Enemy. This is where the Eli Flairs, and Shane Southerns of the world eat, sleep, and pick up dates. Calvin: And HORNET! Melton: He’s co-owner! Bought into the franchise in ’98. Calvin: I knew it! Melton: (Melton grabs a magazine off the table, wads it up, looks at Carlton for a second, not in his eyes directly, but around him...then WHACKS him on the forehead with the magazine.) Fly. Sorry. Place is infested...Point is, we can’t ever lose sight that we’re different. To conquer, to set fire to and destroy...we have to underline what sets us apart, and work to those strengths. (Melton looks at the magazine) Melton: What is this anyway? Calvin: (beside himself that the subject was brought up) It’s a CSWA Tribune from 1990. Look who’s ON THE COVER! (Joey is on the cover, holding the ULTRATITLE in the air. Caption, “Can Melton Do It Again?” blazes across his mid-section.) (Melton smiles. Good times. He starts to flip through the pages, then stops.) Melton: Why are these pages stuck together... Carlton: (caught off-guard) It’s the...humidity. Summertime here is like breathing mud. (Melton casts Carlton a concerned look then tosses the magazine aside) Melton: Again, I pay for asking. That’s twice Joey... (Confused silence between them.) Melton: (smiles) Did you get it? Carlton: Get... Melton: What did I ask you to do? Remember that conversation yesterday, “Carlton...get......for me..” Calvin: OH! IT’S RIGHT HERE. (Carlton hands Melton a piece of paper.) Melton: This legit? Carlton: I assure you MISTER MELTON it is! Melton: I’m sorry I doubted you... (smiles) Carlton: YOU HAD EVERY RIGHT TO. I have to prove myself to you. I WANT TO. Melton: Easy on the orgasmic glee, please. How’d you come up with this? (Melton seems impressed) Carlton: I’ve e-stalked Jenny Finch for the last eight months, and have been issued a cease and desist by Sharon Stone’s lawyers. Personal information...I can get. LIKE THAT. (snaps his fingers in attempt to be cool.) Melton: (dialing) She’s single now... Carlton: I know! Melton: (waves at Carlton to quiet down) It’s ringing. Carlton: MISTER MELTON can I ask why... (Melton quickly reaches for the Tribune, wads it up again and SMACKS Carlton on the head again.) Melton: You’d think they could spray. (Call picks up.) POISON IVY: (voice mail recording) "Hey, it's Ivy. Leave one." BEEP! Melton: I’m going to cut to the chase, McGinnis. Robert Plant said it best. I wanna be your backdoor man. Whatever this punk you’re seeing is into...I promise you I can get freakier than that. (Joey winks at Calvin) I hired Mini-Ivy...just to be close to you in some form. I know it was wrong. A cheap ploy to go over, but I couldn’t wait until I had the real thing. I wanted what Ivy I could have, right then. (Carlton flashes thumbs up sign.) Melton: So babe.... (BEEP!) Ah...Crap! (Melton redials.) Carlton: I HATE MACHINES! Melton: Ringing. (BEEP!) Guess what I’m doing right now, Ivy. I’m lying in bed, (stutters briefly) just a fig leaf coveri---- BEEP! WHAT?! URGH! Carlton: AGAIN? NOBODY CUTS OFF MISTER MELTON TWICE! (Melton redials again, more pissed than before.) Melton: It was so easy to woo hot women five years ago. Is it me, Calvin? Calvin: NO! You fancy Ivy? Melton: Every man has his weakness, Carlton. I can’t die without having that girl. I can feel her in every bone in my body...(BEEP!) Ivy, you know you’re curious to see what it’s like to be with the 3-time winner. I know I’ve been wrestling midgets, but with men, don’t you worry, I’m the biggest too. This Stevens cat...he’s all wrong for you babe... Carlton: (leans into the phone. In mock Thunderlips voice) He’s the Ultimate Meatball! Melton: (laughs. Melton can’t control himself. The quasi-serious tune he was channeling is gone.) You idiot. (laughing, as he becomes Scarlett O’Hara.) Red...Red...I...I....I DO DECLARE...I LOVE THIS MAN... (CLICK!) IVY: Who the f***? (Joey and Carlton eye each other. Carlton pokes Joey to speak. Joey freezes.) IVY: Speak f******. IS THIS JOEY MELTON!? (Melton’s eyes get three times their normal size...he nervously hangs up. He and Carlton look at each other, then bust up laughing.) Melton and Carlton together: I....I....DO DECLARE. (A six year-old boy happily approaches the table. Cheap Chic-Fil-A toy in hand.) BOY: Hi! (Carlton reaches for the wadded up Tribune and WHACKS the boy.) Carlton: Go on...SHU! SHU! THIS IS MY DINNER DATE... (Awkward pause.) Carlton: I meant...business meeting MISTER MELTON. (Joey shakes his head.) Melton: Pack our things. Gotta be at CS Towers to record the ‘BEST OF’ DVD audio commentary in thirty minutes. You’ve been good today...You may sit in.
