Poe/Rabesque vs. Garvin/Viper

26 Nov 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fadein on Rabesque, as he stands in front of the NFW backdrop, wearing the normal gear) Rabesque: “I really have to be honest. Tag matches aren’t my thing. They’re not something that I came into the NFW wishing I could do more of. And this BYOB stip makes it all the more fun, right? Look, this is where I prove to the world that I really am the greatest thing going. People are going to look at this match and this is going to be the one that they expect me to have no chance in. They see Jean Rabesque as a man that can only win the technical matches. But of course, these are also the same people that ignore the fact that I won triple flaming tables two weeks ago. “But I do realize that those in charge, those who make the matches, do in fact have a sense of humor. Or maybe just maybe the conspiracy theory many have spoken of existing really does have some merit. But either way the powers that be have decided to pair me with a man that is proving week in and week out to be the true bottom feeder of the NFW. Poe, have you done ANYTHING since you came here? Hell, I understand losing to me is nothing to be ashamed of, but hell Poe, you didn’t even show up for the match. This seems to be a consistent trend as of late, you just not deciding to show up. “So, I guess this is the time where I make you the deal Poe. Stand there, stay out of the way, and you might just get lucky, and get a couple points out of the deal. The second you try to involve yourself with any of this, and things can get ugly. You’ve proven to be worthless, and I’ve decided that if I’m going to win this match, then I am going to do it myself. Be a good boy, and you’ll get your ten points free of charge.” (Rabesque pauses for a moment before switching gears) “And now, we move on to Python... er.... Viper. “How’s Mama Constrictor been of late Python? You been keeping her company. I know how much you enjoy when I talk about your family. “But congratulations Python, you to this day, with your blatant choke down 10-0 when I was standing on one leg remains the only blemish on my NFW resume. Of course, I then took you down in... of all things... a tag match right afterwards, but at least some day in the future you’ll be able to look back at this entire NFW experience and tell your grandchildren you AT LEAST cheated to accomplish something worthwhile, something no one else has been able to do. “I mean, having a body like yours, and the supposed skills you supposedly have, and still having to cheat to win? Pretty pathetic if you ask me Python, but I guess that about sums up your entire career, doesn’t it? I mean look at you. There is no reason you should not be dominating this league. The only problem is Python.... is that you don’t have any talent. And now, I know you’ll retort with some incredibly witty remark, and then you’ll probably get really angry and make an ass of yourself again, but it really won’t prove anything. You’ll just look like an idiot, something you’re very good at, both in and out of the ring. “And then finally.... Mr. Garvin, the man that has the audacity to come into the NFW and claim that he is without a doubt the finest technical wrestler going in the world today. Take a number Jeff, because you have a long way to go before you can make that claim, and if first impressions are anything, your first experience on Crash did NOTHING to solidify your status. “This is the NFW Jeff, not whatever minor league you wrestled in previously that your promoter made a big deal out of. Whatever it was, it was the bush leagues compared to where you are now. Now you may have been the best wrestler where you came from, but the standard for you has drastically been raised Jeff. You’re not even in our upper echelon, and if you have any doubts about that, I will gladly erase them at Crash. “I’ve been doing this since before you even laced up the boots son, and I’ve seen things and wrestled men that you can’t even dream of. Someday you might be good, someday you might even be the best, but right now, you just don’t cut out. So my suggestion to you is actually quite similar to the suggestion I had for Poe..... “Just sit back and watch. “You’re partner is going to be able to do anything for you, and you can just let me beat the snot out of him. Sit back, watch, and maybe you’ll learn something. “No false gimmicks, no false hype, I am Jean Rabesque.” (Fade out) [the thread's next post — 27 Nov 2003] (FADE IN to Steel Viper in his training compound, reviewing NFW footage of his past bouts with Jean Rabesque. The controversial Amatuer Rules Match plays in the background. Viper stops the tape and turns to the camera.) STEEL VIPER: B.Y.O.B. Now that’s a term you don’t hear too often in professional wrestling. Maybe if you were heading to a party, but not a match. NFW, being the innovators that they are, have created a Bring Your Own Bag match. No, were not talking about what the Hip Hop Express, in the CSWA, might bring to the ring. We’re talking about a bag of weapons. Anything and everything you can possibly imagine, which can fit inside that bag, will be in that ring. It sort of brings me back to when I was coming up overseas, wrestling in Japanese Death Matches. It was once my bread and butter, what brought me to the big time in the first place. But most of you know the story. It got boring and I went back to my roots, back to what made me what I was long before that…martial arts. And as mush as Jean Rabesque would like to come out here and try to validate his reason for losing, in the end he still lost. Now you can debate all you want about it being a rules infraction and how I cheated, but to my recollection, NFW never came after me for any fine. They didn’t take any action on your little allegation, so as far as I’m concerned the case is closed. Face the facts, you couldn’t counter a simple sleeper and you lost, simple as that. You can try to twist it anyway you want, but the truth remains…and that is…you lost to Steel Viper. Know what else? Get used to it. I don’t plan on letting up one bit. I’m gonna pound you from pillar to post and you won’t be able to do a damn thing about it. You or your little bag of tricks. And don’t think it hasn’t crossed my mind that you will stoop to whatever low you possibly can to get the best of me. But whatever you’ve got to toss in my face…I’ll be ready. And that well-rounded resume you seem so proud to boast about will have TWO blemish, courtesy of yours truly. Now don’t be upset, Rabesque, but you brought this on yourself. You seem to think that your dumb little names for me are earning you cool points with the boys in the back, but they see, just as I do, that its only a poor excuse of an attempt of yours to be something your not. You’re not funny and if you think you are, then by all means continue with your childish antics. But the only laughs you’re generating are from people laughing at you and your pathetic arsenal of unfunny remarks. SO keep it up and find out just how much this “python” is gonna squeeze his hands around your throat! There won’t be any controversy this time, because I’m gonna blatantly do it for the world to see! You wanna act like Billy Badass, then step up and show me what you’re made of. Then again, that’s a waste of time because I already know that you ain’t (BLEEP). (Viper cracks his knuckles, one at a time. He rolls his shoulders and scowls into the camera.) But this is a tag match, so how could I leave out your partner, “The Apocalypse” Gabriel Poe. You don’t know me, Poe, and after this match you’ll probably wish you never did. We’ll meet for the first time in our careers and believe me, I intend on leaving you with one hell of an impression. You’ve done some things in the sport that I must say have been impressive. But Poe, I don’t think in all your travels that you’ve ever come across someone quite like me. I’m not your everyday plodding big man. I hit fast, I hit hard, and I leave them asking questions in ER, wondering what happened and how they got there. I don’t play mind games, like you enjoy and honestly, that stuff doesn’t work on me. So whatever you might be scheming up…save it for someone else, I put up blinders to that (BLEEP). And the fact that of all the people in the NFW roster, you end up with the scum of the Earth, Jean Rabesque. Maybe you two belong together? Thinking about it, that’s probably a perfect fit. Maybe you can color each other’s hair. (Viper rolls his neck and mean mugs the camera. He places his hand on his chin and thinks of the words to say.) Then there is my partner, Jeff Garvin. Last week he hung in there with me and went the distance. He talked a lot of trash, he didn’t back it up, but whatever he says this week…take it to the bank. Because with me as his tag partner, we can’t be beat. I’m not so thrilled about the pairing, but that’s life. Bumps in the road sometimes happen, its up to you to just truckin’ and try to make the best of the situation. Garvin, you’re gonna get a free pass for now, but you better damn well be sure to hold up your end of the deal. As of now, we’re a team. We gotta go in there and show no mercy. We’ve gotta go in there and destroy whatever is in our path. Can you handle that, Garvin? It shouldn’t be too hard with me there, but just in case you don’t…that pass you get…gets REVOKED and you become a dead man! (Grins.) Got that? Good…I hope so. As long as we have an understanding. (Nods) Rabesque…Poe…it honestly makes no difference to me who is in front of me and Garvin. We have no choice but to win and I guess since that our only option…then that’s EXACTLY what we’ll do! Got a problem with that? Tough…deal with it. (FADE TO BLACK)
