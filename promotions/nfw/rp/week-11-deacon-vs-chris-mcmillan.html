WEEK 11: DEACON vs. CHRIS MCMILLAN

14 Apr 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Chris Shepherd stands in front of an NFW backdrop.) CS: It has been an interesting first few weeks. Deacon entered the NFW, followed by Eddy Love making his own special entrance. Eddy is off dealing with the other Southern... and we're moving on to another Wolf. CS: I know this isn't the CSWA, but in our career... one match stands above all others - Deacon versus Mike Randalls. In those weeks leading up to Anniversary 2k, Deacon and Randalls defined one another. The Wolf entered the territory of the Bear, and he did so with a trap. Well... 'Wolf', it seems your walking into a bear's territory. Question is... how tenacious are you? I don't have much else to say to you... until you 'howl' in my direction... so I'll move on to the NFW South. The land of Southern and his exploding boot, again showing a lack of faith in himself by doing whatEVER it takes to win... or in this case lose. The NFW South, the land of the 'Southern Dandy'... the man that will give the world what they want, at least eventually. Deacon vs. Love... finally leaving the cameras and microphones behind and entering the ring. We didn't forget Love... we remember ALL STAR... scratch that, we remember 1998 through 2000 when you'd run your mouth at us before running your way through a feud with Mark Vizzack. All that history, and it'll finally be settled. Not with an exploding boot, though Deacon may make you feel like it exploded when he nails you with his size 20. It'll be decided by 2 men, completely opposite... putting on the match that will, Ultratitle or not, steal the 2nd half of the season. That doesn't take Faith to know... because Faith is the evidence of unseen things. And everyone in the world could've seen this coming, all the way back in 1998. [the thread's next post — 14 Apr 2004] {{...FADE-IN: Shane Southern in front of an NFW backdrop...}} SHANE SOUTHERN: " Shepherd ... please tell me you ain't THAT stupid. Did you WATCH tha' whole card? Did you SEE that idiot, tha' "HIGHWAYMAN" switch tha' boots in mah' bag? Listen man, instead of spendin' ALL yer time on bended knee talkin' to tha' LORD, you better pull yer head out uv' yer ASS n' pay attention ... cause if ya' throw out <BLEEP> like that ah'bout me again, that ASS might just be kicked all tha' way back to tha' CSWA. If n' Deac has anythin' ta' say ah'bout it, HE knows where ta' find me. " "Party's Over. " {{...FADE OUT...}}
