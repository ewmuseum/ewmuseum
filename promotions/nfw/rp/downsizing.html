Downsizing

5 Aug 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::FADEIN on the locker room backstage at the Paul Tsongas Arena. PURORESU-SAN paces back and forth in front of a row of lockers. PURORESU-SAN mutters angrily in Japanese, looking around impatiently. BILLY enters from off camera, clutching a handful of cards:: BILLY: Dude, what’s up? I was playing Magic with … :: PURORESU-SAN cuts BILLY off by slapping the cards out of his hand:: PURORESU-SAN: Close your pie-orifice! I have brought you here to discuss very importantly matters. BILLY: I’m not following you, dude. PURORESU-SAN: You are unable to follow me? Then by all means allow me to explain as though I were addressing a mildly retarded schoolchild, or perhaps Billy Gray. YOUR TRANSLATION PERFORMS ORAL SEX! No one is capable of understanding the words that are coming out of my mouth due to your inadequate handling of your translatorial job! ::BILLY blinks, looking completely lost:: BILLY: … huh? :: PURORESU-SAN screams, charging BILLY. PURORESU-SAN slides behind BILLY, cinching in a half-nelson:: BILLY: OWWWWWW! DUDE! PURORESU-SAN: I am no longer in requirement of your service! :: PURORESU-SAN heaves BILLY up and delivers the Rising Sun half-nelson suplex, driving BILLY through the bench seat behind them. The bench shatters, and BILLY lies among the wreckage. PURORESU-SAN picks himself up, cackling maniacally:: PURORESU-SAN: YOU ARE ON FIRE! BILLY: (weakly) … dude … I think you mean ‘you’re fired’ … ::PURORESU-SAN grabs his duffel bag from the bench seat rubble and yanks it open. PURORESU-SAN digs through it for a moment before producing a can of lighter fluid and a Zippo lighter:: PURORESU-SAN: (ominously) I was meaning what I had spoken … ::BILLY begins to whimper and blubber, pleading with PURORESU-SAN as he begins to squirt lighter fluid on BILLY. Suddenly, there is a commotion from off-camera as NFW interns ROBBIE and JIMMY enter, rushing to the aid of their partner:: JIMMY: DUDE! ROBBIE: DUDE! BILLY: DUDES! PURORESU-SAN: AAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHHHHH! ::JIMMY drags BILLY to safety as ROBBIE fends off PURORESU-SAN by smacking him with a thick deck of Magic cards. The three interns beat a hasty retreat, leaving PURORESU-SAN screaming in rage as we FADE TO BLACK::
