I saw JJ kissing Bobby Jack...

27 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN... "Hey, Knox." "Impulse!" "You're awesome, Knox!" I'm walking down Broadway with a camera around my neck and a microphone clipped to my jacket. It's a social experiment. Dr. Silver accused me of hiding in my Bronx/Brooklyn bubble and never dealing with the general population, so, as I've done with most of my career, I'm taking this challenge on headfirst. Something I've noticed, the response I've gotten to people who recognize me is almost a hundred percent positive. Either there's a lot less people in my special cheering section than JJ claims, or - what's more accurate - people are much more reluctant to be rude to you face to face as opposed to in a ten thousand seat arena. "Knox! Shoulda hit 'em!" For that matter, most of the people who have been bold enough to tell me on the street that they don't like me and what I do are fans of the Shock and Awe aspect of professional wrestling - they like the Danglers in the Empire, they were Dildonites here in the New Frontier, and if it doesn't involve eye popping cleavage and a shapely posterior and a lot of blood and catchphrases, they simply don't like it. To each his own. And...) "The latest string of accusations flying from JJ DeVille's corporate stronghold contain some of the same things that Troy Windham first said about me." That's the pot calling the kettle hypocrite. But we'll play along. "First, there's the accusation that my affiliation with Eli Flair and Miss Ivy got me a job here in the New Frontier, that they greased the wheels and made sure I was pushed to the moon at the expense of far more talented athletes like JJ DeVille." Well. "Depending on your definition, sure, you could say that Miss Ivy and Eli Flair got me a job here." The truth that I never ever tried to hide - the truth that was in plain sight from the first time I wrestled in the New Frontier - is now out in the open. "But it doesn't begin with a man in a mask getting powerbombed by Brock Alyas for a hard fought loss. It doesn't begin with my sending a tape of my indy matches from upstate New York to Craig Miles, asking him if he's got a spot for me and to please not tell Miss Ivy." "It began eight years ago, when Miss Ivy recommended me for a match just after my eighteenth birthday, for NFW Rookie Night during All Star Week. My first opponent was Blaine Hollywood, and I lost during what I can only assume to be the unofficial debut of the Hollywood Wrecking Crew." Maybe. I was on break from college and didn't get Crash TV in the dorms so I was pretty much in a New Frontier blackout until the summer. "That was it, JJ. That was my big break. I wouldn't wrestle on television again for four years." "Now, if I had people greasing the wheels for me, if I had people doing favors for me, don't you think I'd have had a part in the New Frontier for some part of the second season of the Ultratitle? Don't you think they would have used me as some kind of lackey the way Troy used you?" "For that matter, Troy Windham was part of the New Frontier at the time - either he or Eddy Love were wearing the Highwayman mask at the time if my research is up to date... why weren't you asked to participate? Craig Miles didn't know me from the next random shlub, and Eli Flair had dropped out of the Ultratitle after three or four weeks, so there was no loyalty there. He wanted a body in the match, he didn't care who it was." "But of course, in 2004 you were a big star, right? Burning bright and on the cusp of several World Championships based on your highly talented ability to carry Troy Windham's bags." ("Yo, Knox!") "Maybe that's harsh. You're clearly a capable wrestler, JJ. I just can't get over a word you keep using." "Deserve." "Somehow, all of the things that you're talking about - the seven figure salary, the penthouses, the private jet... you talk about it like you deserve it all." "You don't." "None of us do." "Troy Windham doesn't deserve a single thing in this sport, JJ... but he did earn what he has." That's the difference that you never understood. "I didn't deserve to win the NFW TV Title, or wrestle Dan Ryan and then Castor Strife for the vacant NFW World Title, JJ. I earned it." "And my contract, the one that you seem to be convinced was written on the backs of the people who trained me - it promised me nothing but an opportunity." "My first meeting with Craig Miles, he told me, 'Kid, you wrestled well for us four years ago. If you can still wrestle well, you can stay. If you can't, you're going to go." The nerve of him to expect me to actually EARN the right to stay. Isn't that what you'd say, JJ? Isn't that what you've been saying? ("Impulse, you suck!" Thanks, I said, waving.) "I've never shied away from my losses, JJ, and I've never made excuses for them. I call them as they are. Castor Strife took my TV Title by capitalizing on a bit of bad luck that caused me to injure my neck. He also pinned me completely clean and excuse - free for the vacant World Championship." "SARS the Clown said a lot of the same things you are and were, JJ. I don't accuse you of being a puppet of SARS." He called me boring, a generic hero wannabe, and an embarassment to the sport. With some less polite language. Just in case you want to compare notes. "I'd like to ask you, however, JJ - exactly where has Eli Flair shown up in my promos? Where has Miss Ivy shown up in my promos? Rose and I met them for lunch before the ten man tag team match at Supercrash 3, and they were shown on camera for Nova's Hall of Fame induction... but other than that?" They have not appeared on camera at all. Maybe Miss Ivy was in the background a few times, but most of my promos have been filmed in the bar that she owns and spends a lot of nights at. Whatever our varying opinions are of Miss Ivy, I think we can all agree she's an OCD control freak who doesn't trust people to do the job that she's got a vested interest in, without her looking over their shoulder. "Now, it's true that they've both been more involved in Rose and my lives for the past few months: but I don't film every aspect of my life and I haven't filmed them since it has had nothing to do with the New Frontier." "Are you saying that you've been stalking us? Do I have to get a restraining order?" That's one place that I'll freely admit to leeching off Miss Ivy: she knows almost all of the local NYPD precincts and a few Appeals judges, we could get that rammed through before you could hire your overpaid legal team to ineffectually argue your case. Except you don't have one. Idiot. "How much integrity does it take to dump a bottle of piss over your head, JJ? About as much as it takes to force a woman into a thong to serve you cold beverages when she's been hired for public relations and promotions? About as much as it takes to arrest an entire roster for trumped up--" --As in, nonexistent-- "--charges, busing us across times zones for your own jollies, I assume, to remind us all that you're in charge." "So, yeah, it takes the integrity of JJ DeVille to dump a bottle of piss on JJ DeVille. Would I do that again?" If I had the choice, probably not. "Of course, it was a masterstroke concocted by Rose and Valerie in an attempt to level the playing field a little bit and get one back for Fiona. Like Rook Black said, she gets involved, she pays the price." I don't agree with that but that's the reality of the situation. If Rose is ever able to come back to the New Frontier, these are decisions she will have to make for herself. "I never claimed to be a hero, JJ... just a man. If the New Frontier wanted to push me in a certain direction, that's their perogative, but I doubt that any of the Impulse Hype was ever office - generated. For over a year, Eddie Mayfield hated me because I was outspokenly against the way he treated his Champion. For over a year, Eddie Mayfield did his best, first to remove the TV Title from around my waist and then to keep me from getting a rematch against Castor." That's truly the sign of a man greasing the wheels for me and bending over backwards to make the people like me. "When it truly comes down to it, JJ, it is immaterial to me whether the fans cheer for me or boo me, for the simple fact that doing the right thing is not always the popular thing. I would love for them to all be on my side, and I would prefer that they get behind me because they see that I'm trying." "It's trite, JJ, but I would rather be booed for who I am than cheered for who I'm not." ("Hell - mother - truckin' - yeah!" answered a passerby. In slightly edited form.) "So that's why I acknowledge the boos without stressing over them, JJ. Because while everyone under the sun wanted to see me bash you in the head with a piece of steel, over and over again... to do so would have sold out everything I am and everything I believe in." I know, you claim I already did that, but I have to consider the source. "And to have resorted to an act of violent retribution over an event that I can't undo or change... an act that my partner directly told me would not have been worth it... That would have sent me down a path that there's no coming back from." And you're not worth it, JJ. My career - my life - and the choices that I have to live with - will last far longer than any one match. "So, congratulations, JJ. You've managed to expose me for what I truly am: a man with the best of intentions who makes mistakes. A man who believes that living well is a better victory than a bloody opponent, and a man who is willing to take on the burden of making the right choices whether they're popular or not, as opposed to riding the wave after public opinion is decided." And a man who... is looking for the World Title? (Now it was my turn to laugh.) "I haven't talked about the World Title in months, either publicly or privately. I haven't petitioned the office for a rematch with Castor Strife, because, quite frankly, there's no reason. Castor beat me, clean, in the middle of the ring. There's been nothing on my part since then that would warrant a contendership match, let alone a title shot." Perhaps you're not as plugged in as you think, JJ? "But none of this has anything to do with what's to come, does it, JJ? The actions of the past, be it from your employer or my trainers, from your 'undeserved' and overexaggerated blackballing to my imaginary front office stroke... None of this has anything to do with Impulse versus JJ DeVille." "Because, quite frankly, this is a fight that you don't know anything about." ("Knox!" Jimmy! I replied. Yes, I actually knew that guy.) "If you're to be taken at your word, JJ..." And we can ask Jack Bryant if that's possible. "...then this is going to be one on one, with no interference from your cronies, and a New Frontier Wrestling official calling the match down the middle. It's going to be my wrestling skill against your wrestling skill." "And unless you're going to book this match to take place in a boardroom... you're completely out of your depth." "My style has a thousand holes in it, JJ?" ("Pulse! Knock 'em one!") "You're going to get a chance to prove it." FADE [the thread's next post — 30 Mar 2012] (CUT TO: JJ DeVille, his hair slicked back and in a ponytail, is wearing a button down blue shirt, red/blue Republican power tie and a navy blue jacket, sitting in the foyer of his Arlington, VA condo.) F*ck you, Impulse. Seriously. Just go f*ck yourself. Screw you for spending the first five minutes of your neverending promo whining about the difficulties you had cracking into this industry and how no one greased the wheels for you. Screw you for denying that you weren't pushed down everyone's throat, and that you didn't know about it, let alone revel in the fact that you were pre-ordained the hero of this sport. You brought up me forcing Fiona to parade around like the whore she is to serve me my soda? Well, when she ran into you and Rosie, what did she say to you? Do you remember? She cried about how, just the week before, Ivy said she was so proud of her. Fiona Love, the marketing director of NFW... who idolizes and worships Poison Ivy. The same Poison Ivy who looms over your every move, as you so stated. The same Fiona Love, who is friends with Kerry O'Connor, who has verbally felated you for years, despite your sub-New York Mets record? Deny it all you want, Impulse... IF THAT IS INDEED YOUR REAL NAME. You are a pre-packaged marketing tool. They -- and you -- wanted you to be the hero, despite your claims to the otherwise. No one -- absolutely no one -- likes you, save for the handful of kids who get free tickets sent to them courtesy of the NFW's "Asperger's Awareness Week" promotion. It too me to mention that inconvenient truth a bunch of times before you, Fiona and everyone else in this promotion couldn't hide it anymore. Their chanting how you suck, Impulse. Their BOOING you, Impulse. But now you want another inconvenient truth? Here's one. You don't love Rosie. You're using her. How do I know this? Impulse, your little plot to throw piss on my face -- when you first shattered your faux-integrity -- didn't come because of anything to do with my humiliation of Fiona Love. No, it came because I repeatedly talked about how you are a steroid abuser. You stuck up for Fiona when you threw a Diet Dr. Pibb in my face. *MY* Diet Dr. Pibb. Yes, I am that petty and vindictive to remember that. When I was in the process of enslaving you, did you beg me to spare her? No. Instead, you BEGGED me to tell the world that you do not use steroids, instead of manning up and debasing yourself PROPERLY. And then you walked her right into the lion's den. And then she got mauled and left in a pile of miscarraige goo. And then what do you do? Any plots to dump your fecal matter on me? Any plans to humiliate me? Nope. Just you dropping a chair so you could hold on to your integrity. Your integrity has ALWAYS -- I repeat ALWAYS -- come before Rosie. If you loved her, Knox... if you truly loved her... then I would have had a chair wrapped around my skull and god knows what thrown on me. Because true love, I'm told, is all about sacrifices. And you will NEVER sacrifice your marketing-tagline-slash-built-in-excuse-for-losing for anything. So why did you drag Rosie out here and let her ramble on every five seconds if you did not love her? Well, sex sells. And, despite looking like a burned up version of the boy from MASK, Rosie's sex sells since the NFW's fan-base is filled with a bunch of Roofie Artists. And, not only that, you saw the template for stardom first-hand. How, through the years, Ivy 'humanized' Eli. A strong female and a conflicted male. The packaging works. You saw it. Your mentors saw it. Fiona saw it. The only reason you're with Rosie is because she makes you money. You're her PIMP, Impulse. And I know, when she finally emerges from whatever goth cave she sleeps in, she'll deny this. But why? Because, Knox, like every pimp... you've ABUSED the poor girl. Physically? I don't know. But I do know that a man of questionable integrity such as yourself would definitely beat the woman he loves. But mentally? Certainly. You've whored her out and she's willing to take it, since she has the self-esteem of your average... well... prostitute. It's been like that her whole life. I mean, what young girl dreams of one day cleaning up spilled Coors Light after ten-cent wing night at some dump bar? The only girl who could have such a pathetic dream is a girl who was brutalized as a child. Who was it, Knox? An uncle's preying hands? The neighborhood priest? The older boy who took advantage? I know that, eventually, we'll get the backstory courtesy of your horifically pretentious film school history lesson promos. But I know you already know, Knox. And I know that's how you've EXPLOITED the poor girl. Now, I've wasted enough time talking about your blatant and obvious abuse of a woman. Now on to more important subjects. The most important subject. Namely, me. Knox, while you've complained about the difficulties you had breaking into this sport... I was blacklisted AFTER ALREADY BREAKING INTO THIS SPORT. NFW President Eddie Mayfield was, at many times, pinned under MY boot. Revenge is sweet. As far as my seven-figure, penthouse and lear jet demands? Do I deserve that? Yes. And have I earned it? An even bigger yes. I was cast aside by the politics which surround this industry and, in particular, anything to do with Eddie Mayfield. I was a key part of the invasion and takeover of this league. And let me remind you, I scored the final pinfall in that match to cement our power. I was the last man standing. I pinned Castor Stryfe. Current World Champion Castor Stryfe. Have you, Impulse? After we took over, I handled the books for Troy. Organization was never his strong point. I did the paperwork. The dirty work. And I have made everyone miserable. I am The Locker Room Cancer. No one likes coming to work when I'm around. I have become the most hated man in this league. Wrestlers hate me. Fans hate me. EVERYONE hates me. But everyone WATCHES me. Anytime I come to ringside, something happens. Bombs get launched. Chaos ensues. I am the one who is MUST SEE TV. I am the reason people are tuning in. I mastered the politics that held me down. And along the way, I wrote myself a contract. The one I always deserved. CH-CHING. I usually save the pop culture references for Troy. But his good friend Bruce Springsteen has a famous lyric. A poor man wants to be rich. Rich men want to be kings. And a king ain't satisfied until he rules everything. I am the TRUE KING of this industry. I have taken power and I have used it to my fullest advantage. But I am far... FAR... from satisfied. Impulse, you're already building in the excuse. Just a few weeks ago, you were the sixty minute man. You were going to take all of the members of The Clan in the ring, make them last 60 minutes, blow them up, while you had nary a scratch... Now I'm a respected competitor? Impulse, you see what's coming. I made you break your code. I made the world see you for who you truly are. I made your woman have surgery. I'm now telling the world how you've used her like a piece of cattle... and she's FINE with it. And now I'm going to expose you one last time. You know it. I know it. There's going to be three quick seconds. And you're going to look up and see the bright lights and realize... THAT YOU JUST LOST. Will I celebrate For a few minutes. But then I'm just going to go on ruling with my iron first. Because, Impulse, you're just a pawn. Me? I'M THE TRUE KING! And like the song said... I won't be satisfied UNTIL I RUN EVERYTHING.
