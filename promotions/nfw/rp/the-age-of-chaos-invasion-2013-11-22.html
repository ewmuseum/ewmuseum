NFW — Roleplay

The Age Of Chaos - Invasion

22 November 2013
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Legion — 22 November 2013 [What you are about to see took place on ESEN during the daily recap of the day’s events following the press conference…]
‘Just’ Mark: After this afternoon’s events you do have to wonder who will both Dorchester and The Graverobbers will face in Boston… now we go to other events on the ESEN net-
[Suddenly a commotion can be heard in the background…] ‘Sir, you can’t go in there, Sir! ‘You tried to stop me at Reloaded, well now it’s my turn to fight back…’ [The doors to the studio suddenly can be heard being forcefully opened and we hear footsteps and 3 shadows walk into view…]
‘Just’ Mark: We seemed to have been abruptly joined by the Black Market in the studio, can I ask what you three are doing here?
Legion: Oh hi Mark! I’m sure you don’t mind the Graverobbers and I coming in to have a little chat to all those watching on ESEN right now I hope?
‘Just’ Mark: (Looking fearful as Bandit and Devastator walk to either side of him) N-N-Not at all sir.
Legion: Splendid! Last night at Reloaded you see something was missing – sure the Market and I were in the opening montage and the fans got to see some excellent wrestling and a new champion crowned but I received a few texts, tweets, messages and even Touts asking where we were last night and what had happened to the State of the Market address I was going to give to the entire world…
It seems that the ‘powers that be’ were scared about what I was going to say – scared I was going to bring down the reputation of NFW, of even ESEN so Lamont and Calvin’s spat suddenly went a bit longer than planned (he winks at the camera).
Here’s the thing – I KNOW that Eddie is scared of us, he’s kept it hidden but there’s always been moments where he’s tried to keep me quiet – if you remember the feud with von Doom many moons ago – he’d orchestrated it purely so James and I could destroy each other, get rid of the ‘headaches’ we might cause.
Nowadays things change – we have the tag division under our grasp and well he saw in Brawlhalla how we can make life a living hell so I’m firstly sending out an ultimatum to the NFW board – in Boston the state of the Market will happen – no changes, uncut, uncensored and if you try anything different previous attacks are going to look like a tea party compared to what we will do.
Secondly I’ve been getting speculation that Alex Austin saying Black before he passed out is aimed at me… funny that – if Castor or Alex is watching this right now all I’ll say is this: one of you two must remember Buffalo right? Maybe I can make you a deal… just find me in Boston.
We shall now take our leave – if I find out this was edited from any repeat broadcasts… [Bandit and Devastator cause 'Just' Mark to flinch with a growl..]
[The 3 leave and we see that ‘Just’ Mark looks visibly shaken as the camera just decides to fade to black with a Copyright ESEN 2013 message.]
