(CUT TO: The =W= logo. The camera cuts to the patio of TROY WINDHAM's South Beach estate. Troy stands in the middle, his hair freshly frosted, staring a hole at the camera. Across from Bobby Jack, his head also bowed, is CHRISTIAN CRUISE. Behind Christian, with a pretentious smirk, is AUGUST DE LA ROSSI. And across from August, picking his nose, is PETER WINDHAM. I've been dealing with board-room negotiations, dealings with my agents, and working on my new TV pilot with BEAU BRIDGES. But no more. After I saw what happened in Hampton, I've come to realize that I... WE... need to reassert ourselves as the DOMINANT FORCE in New Frontier Wrestler. We need to reassert ourselves in reminding everyone that WE LITERALLY OWN THIS PLACE... and what *WE* say goes." (Bobby Jack spits.) TROY: "First, you'll notice someone missing from The Windham Clan. "Because I know JJ better than HE even does... and I know, right now... he's about to boil over. Ain't that right, JJ?" (JJ shakes his head.) JJ: "Troy, first, thank you for the endorsement. Because I know some things have come back to bite us in the ass... but at the same time, I've been the straw that stirs the drink around here. I'm the Locker Room Cancer. I'm the Bureaucratic Nightmare. I'm the one who has USED the power we took. And that's what we're going to do at Norfolk. Because, yeah, I'm pissed as hell. I ended up in a GARBAGE CAN last week. But I'm going to get to that in a little bit. Because, first... I got a few words to say for my man Jack Bryant." (JJ purses his lips.) JJ: "Bryant -- I think the beginning of the show said it all. You were there, ready to pounce... and I escaped intact. That's been the story between us since DAY ONE. I took the belt from you by using my wits. And now you can't get it back. The TV title. The illustrious TV Title. The belt that has launched a thousand careers. Well, Jack... I'm going to say that I've become a bit BORED humiliating you. I've become a bit TIRED of making you look like a slack-jawed retard. So, my man... I'm going to give you ONE LAST CHANCE. And, to prove to everyone that I am indeed as awesome as I say I am... I'm going to LET YOU determine the stipulations. You pick it. Whatever kind of match you want. Because I'm going to win, just as I always have. And then after my hand gets raised... I'm going to take this title. This precious, sought after title... and I'm going take it and THROW IT IN THE GARBAGE and destroy its reputation forever." (Troy nods in approval.) JJ: "Now... on to what happened to me at Hampton. How I got... I got... I don't even want to GUESS... but I got something poured on me and then I got superkicked into a garbage can by Impulse. Impulse... at first I was pissed. I was upset. I felt... I felt degraded. But then I thought things over and I realized that YOU PROVED ME RIGHT. I've come out here and told the world what you really are... and that's a HEEL. And everyone agrees with me... including your old friend ROOK BLACK. You're not a hero. You're a fraud posing as the cowboy in the white hat. I beat your ass, Impulse. I pinned your ass and had my hand raised by a LICENSED referee. Not some FORMER NFW Superstar. But a man who has DECADES of experience refereeing matches. So, what do you do? You have a childish temper tantrum and ATTACKED ME WITH MY BACK TURNED. And, as I alluded to earlier... you plotted this all in advance. You knew I was going to beat you. And, in turn, you planned a way to embarrass me." (JJ chuckles.) JJ: "All of those, Impulse... that's what *WE* do. That's what *HEELS* do. If you want to be a bad guy so much... well, all you had to do is ASK to be one. Because, Impulse... I have a feeling after Reloaded that absolutely everybody alive is going to agree with me." (Troy nods.) TROY: "WE stand together. WE stand united. WE stand... as Windhams."
