Ten Minutes On The Clock

11 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fade in: Joey Melton, black slacks, sandels, and a white long-sleeve silk shirt stands in front of a NFW South backdrop, hands in pocket, amused look, as always, wearing his face.) Avery. Avery. Avery. Anytime the business parts with one of it’s many who for whatever reason can’t move forward, you hope, or I do at least that said person finds peace, learns a trade, or puts aside the foolish notion that he or she is owed something. You’ve had all this time to think, and your best effort is an angry black man? You’re an even bigger disappointment now than you were then, and I think we’d both agree you had a pretty high level to shoot for. But dammit, you’re overachieving on this one. You’re pissed that Adler ended the UWA on a glorious self-serving note, yet here you are bringing up a name that ninety-five percent of the fans have never heard of. Here’s the thing, Prosser, nobody cares who dumped J-Lo in the 9th grade, but Bennifer splitting was world news. There’s a reason UWA tapes are practically given away on Ebay. Nobody gives a sh*t, except you. I’d say let it go, but we’re past that point, apparently. What it appears to boil down to is this: black sells, and it always has. The Black Panthers are back, super. Round up the usual suspects and teach us all about equality again, life here is pretty boring so you’ll find a fairly receptive group. You wanna push for civil rights, I’ll march with you, but I don’t see that happening Avery, because you’re just like every other black activist in today’s world, you hope a mouth and fear leads to courtside Knicks seats. So, you’re settling a score Sullivan himself probably forgot about, or is the main purpose here to get us all searching for Carlton’s soul? The fact is any of the heroes you masturbate to at night, would put a bullet in your head just for the sheer dumbness of the angle to this point. Thanks for losing Miles, really, the managerial game was cutting into his drinking time, but what about the young black kid who has the grades but can’t get the money to attend college? How’s this helping him? Oh, right...there’s a Prosser Fund I’m sure. You care less about being Malcolm X or any of the other black leaders who meant something to this world, and more about P. Diddy. By week eleven you’ll be running marathons for “the kids” and by 13 showing up on MTV TRL sporting a fur coat. Either you can dance, or you can’t, and by your own admission you were shelved because you couldn’t play. What’s changed over the years? You’re about 40 years too late with this one, Avery. And what’s worse is, you know it. Whatever Prosser. I’m too old to give a sh*t about this. There’s a theory floating that I’ve been put in my place over Love and Deacon arriving, but the reality is while Miles tried I never took a bite of the crow, because this league at All-Star weekend became an even bigger joke. I assume you expect to be taken seriously? Well I’m sorry man, I don’t take much in life seriously, certainly not a league that’s premise is based on redrawing civil lines. No figure four this week, the next we’ll be dressing up as Confederate and Union soldiers. There are people who love to reenact the period, as there are idiots who still want to see the Confederate flag fly. There’s a difference between honoring your heritage and displaying ignorance front and center. You’re already walking a thin line on that one. The ULTRATITLE is yours? And you’re going to burn it down? O...........k. Despite Marx’s delusions this is far from the greatest ULTRATITLE tournament. His admittance is proof of that. I’ve won this title three times in 64, and 128-man single elimination tournaments against competition in the first round that would have Marx pissing himself today. Why the hell does new talent keep coming in John? Because you’re f*cking near first place in a division that sucks ass. They’re not stacking the deck against you irritants, they’re assuring someone who can sell and has a lick of talent wipes your ass up in the first round. But now you’re all crying foul because the focus is ‘off’ you. IT’S ABOUT FREAKING TIME! That it has been on you is why we’re wrestling to less than capacity crowds, and it’s f*cking why we’re sponsored by Piggy Wiggly. Cry all you want, but the worst gimmick possible just stole what thunder it took you eight weeks to build. Now, what does that say? It means you should say, “Yes Mr. Prosser” and appear thirsty now that he’s trying to lead you irritants to water. As for me, no thanks. I’m not here because this farce means something. I’m here because I’d be drugged and lying half-dead in a dark corner if I didn’t keep mildly active. So, Avery...go at it, man. Interfere in my matches, I’ll continue to sleep at night. Beat the hell out of me backstage, it won’t be the first time, or hell, even kill me. I have no regrets. It’s been a good run. I fear no man. I never have. That’s not meant to sound bad-ass, because when you’re 6 foot 210 lbs you have no hope of pulling that off. What it means is, I’m a New Yorker born and raised, I’ve seen sh*t, pardon the swipe from Ghostbusters, that would turn you white. A goof who couldn’t cut it, returning to make an even bigger ass of himself is about as entertaining as NFW has been for me. In the past year I’ve wrestled in parts of the world, where people pray for canned beans to arrive. I’ve seen hell Prosser and you’re not it. Shame Matthews is dumb enough to believe he’s the only guy in wrestling with an anger control problem. This is along the same lines. Everybody’s the baddest, the scariest, the deadliest. What makes you different? Certainly not the “history” you claim to teach, because you have none Prosser. You of anyone should be looking forward, and even changing your name to get a clean slate. Or, is this really about some freak obsessed with XBOX not marking out over a Moonsault? There’s nothing worse than listening to a sob story in this business. I’ve put up with it for 15 years at the mouth of Mark Windham. Everybody’s got a burden to bear, if yours is you’re black, I’m sorry. I can’t relate. But we all have one, man. I have a father I’ve never known, and a mother who was clinically insane. That’s a handful to get over, but I have, because life is too short to listen to people’s BS. I don’t judge other people, and I don’t expect to be judged in return. My two best friends in the world are a raving lunatic, and a wrestling lawyer. One is my spiritual advisor who has no idea why he’s funny, and the other has spent most of his off—road life bailing my fanny out of trouble. You know what else I hate Prosser? When people use promo time to preach. (winks) Gotcha. Anybody with any sense is betting on Joey Melton to take this title home, so here’s what I’ll do for you, because I’m a charitable guy and all. This week after I push aside Canada’s Greatest Treasure, I’ll give you the opportunity to show us why you’re to be feared. I’m making you the same offer I made Adler back when UWA tapes were going at face value. It's one he never took. Here's your chance to prove you're more of a man. Me. You. In the ring. And my promise that I’ll beat you in under ten minutes. If I don’t, I’ll quit. (FTB)
