Failure

10 Aug 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fade in on an opulently appointed boardroom. Dark mahogany panels line the walls and the coffered ceiling. Various pieces of fine art hang on the walls, originals by Monet, Picasso, Pollak stand out in the viewers mind. Recessed lighting illuminates the room and artwork without being conspicuous. A huge, intricately carved oak table dominates the center of the room, and is surrounded with high back leather wing chairs. On one side is the massive Dieter, who is dressed in a charcoal gray pinstripe suit, with a pale blue tie. Kata is beside him and dressed in an uncharacteristically conservative manner, wearing a blue grey blazer and a cream colored blouse. They are both poised and reading the agenda in front of them, occasionally sipping the contents of their coffee cups. Opposite them we see Tempesta, who is wearing a black Versace jacket over a loose fitting black turtleneck. He is ignoring the papers in front of him, drumming his fingers on the table, eying Dieter and Kata, and spasmodically clenching his jaw. Beside Tempesta is the Butler, who has a neat array of printouts, charts, and a laptop before him. At the head of the table we see Mr Redd, wearing a dark silk suit, black linen shirt, and an oxblood tie. His fingers are steepled, and he is looking through them at the neatly arrayed papers that lay in front of him.) MR REDD: So, the first item on our agenda is Futureshock. Dieter and Kata, is there anyone you would like to face? KATA: We would like a rematch with Scotty and Jerome. (Dieter nods) MR REDD:: Duly noted, (The Butler types quicky on his laptop for a moment, then nods to Mr Redd) and done. Hopefully they will reply soon, as the closing date for matches is soon upon us. (Consults his agenda) Tempesta, you have a there way match with Adrenaline and Steel Viper. TEMPESTA: Yeah? MR REDD: (under his breath) Yes. Have you been reviewing the material I sent you? TEMPESTA: (annoyed) Yes. Since the match was booked. MR REDD: Any conclusions? TEMPESTA: Yeah, it’s going to be an ugly, ugly f****ing match. We’ve got an oversized “martial artist” with an ego as big as the great outdoors, a guy who, let’s face it, I have no love lost for, and me, in a grudge match that has been building since I got here practically. Now I got the belt, so these two are going to be concentrating on killing each other only once they got me worn down to the point that they can try for a pin. Then again, I could lose if they pin each other. MR REDD: Given all that, what is your strategy? TEMPESTA: I’m f***** no matter what, my best bet is to go back to my roots, try and incapacitate one, and knock the other out, then get a pin. This is all assuming that Manson doesn’t decide to hold the match in an electrified cage with flaming broken glass, thumbtacks, an exploding chess set, and live tigers of course. DIETER: I vood naht giv heem enny ideahs. (Everyone nods) MR REDD: Good. Keep training as you have been, and you will be fine. Next item, old business, the Albany house show. (Everyone freezes) Two matches, no wins. Explanations? KATA: Well, sir, I think I have to take the majority of the blame for our loss. I did not protect my partner to the best of my ability, and I wrestled out of my style. MR REDD: (Considers for a moment) How so? KATA: Sir, my style is that of a high flyer, just like my parents, and grandparents. My Grandfather, Kenichi Sobata practically pioneered top rope attacks in Japan. My Grandmother was the first woman to mount a turnbuckle, taking after her husband. I went in and tried to fight like Tempesta, and that’s just not my style. I accept full blame for our loss. MR REDD: Dieter, do you have anything to add? DIETER: I vas pretty drunk going eento dat match. Oh, and I taught I broke Scotty wit dee powahbomb. I vas not eckspeckting dee faymasser right den. Dee elbow deed naht help eyezar. MR REDD: (Thinks for a moment) Kata, you need to fight how you think is best. Other than missing the save for Dieter, you did no wrong. Dieter, you are forbidden from drinking until further notice. Any infractions will start with a $5000 fine, and each subsequent infraction will double the fine. Clear? DIETER: (pouts) Cleeah. MR REDD: Tempesta. Explain to me what happened in your match. TEMPESTA: I didn’t see it coming. I was dazed, I had blood in my eyes, and I knew it was coming, I heard him set it up, but I didn’t know where he was coming from. By the time I realized I was on my back, I heard the bell. MR REDD: What about Bloodhunt? TEMPESTA: What about him? He saw someone to beat and went for it. Shane did the smart thing and got outta dodge, and I was left as the judas goat for Bloodhunt to vent on. Pure and simple. By the way, Where the hell were you two? (Stands) You got monitors back there, right? You didn’t see Bloodhunt at ringside? Yo didn’t think he’d interfere in some way? Huh? MR REDD: (quietly) I sent them home. TEMPESTA: (furious) WHAT? What the f*** did you do that for? We got a guy who, not two days before the match says he’s going to interfere if I get the upper hand, and you send them HOME?!!! MR REDD: (nods to the butler, who reaches under the table with one hand, and looks almost apologetically at Tempesta) I sent them home because I thought you could deal with Bloodhunt. I paid quite handsomely to insure that he would not interfere in your match. Apparently I did not pay enough to keep him from acting as he did afterwards. TEMPESTA: (Nearly insane with rage) What the f*** did yo do? You paid to fix one of my matches? (Mr Redd nods at The Butler) What kind of sick.. . . (The Butler whips out a tazer, and zaps Tempesta on the thigh. Tempsta collapses, his upper body slumped on the table. His eyes roll around for a moment, and then he starts to get up. The Butler zaps him again, with along sustained jolt this time, and that puts him down. Tempsta’s eyes are still fluttering as he slides down to the floor off the table. MR REDD: Thank you James. (James nods, and leans over giving Tempesta one last shot, just in case, before returning the tazer to it’s holder under the table.) Anything else you wanted to discuss? I throw the forum open. (Both Dieter and Kata are stunned by what just happened, and shake their heads “No”) MR REDD: Very well. James, please type up the minutes, and have Tempesta taken to his apartment. Please have Dr. Linderman prescribe something that will calm him down, he’s been rather tense lately. Thank you all for coming, and we will reconvene next week. (Mr Redd smiles at Dieter and Kata, rises, and after they leave, the camera pans to the fallen Tempesta, who is still out cold as we FTB)
