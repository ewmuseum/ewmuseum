On A Muffin...err...Roll (complete)

6 Oct 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((OOC: Full context of this RP can be seen within the General RP board, or you can read each half via Kin Hiroshi’s two matches this week, starting with Armando Montezuma.)) * A Dodge commercial fades, and graphics for the Best Damn Sports Show come up on the television monitors in the studio. Kin Hiroshi waits off camera as Tom Arnold begins to speak. * Tom Arnold: "Welcome back to the Best Damn Sports Show. As you know, professional wrestling has become a large industry in the world that we know. Appealing to people of all ages, and low income, moral degenerates, 'wrastlin' is a mutli-million dollar industry. Our special guest today, is NFW, CSWA and GXW superstar, and independent millionaire, Kin Hiroshi." * The crowd half jeers, and half cheers as Hiroshi comes from off stage and shakes the hands of Tom, John Salley, Chris Rose, and Kevin Kennedy. Tom strikes a fighting pose, and Rose holds him back as Hiroshi begins to roll up his sleeves. All the men take their seats. * Kin Hiroshi: "Guys, I'm a huge fan, thanks for inviting me out tonight." TA: "No sweat, we always invite the guys who could kill us with their bear hands. Now, the big news in the wrestling world is the contract that you recently signed with NFW. What drew you there, and split your attention even further?" Chris Rose: "Totally, besides owning your own business, and wrestling for two other companies, you decided to add more to your resumé?" KH: "Well, I don't own Hiroshi-Berry Muffins, Inc. anymore. I sold the company to Hostess, and they have full rights to the name now. However, as the founder, I am entitled to a certain share of profits, and all the free muffins I can handle..." TA: "How big of a share we talkin' here? Like I can afford life on the road, or more like I just bought a new car to park in each of the major cities I wrestle in?" KH: "Right in between the two, I'd say. I'm not hurting for cash, but I'm not scraping to get by." Kevin Kennedy: "If you have so much money, why wrestle? Why risk injuring yourself?" CR: "I know if I had your kind of money, I'd be like Tom and sit on my ass everyday." TA: "Hey, your ass is too boney. You'd have to fatten up a bit first." KH: "Tom has a point there Chris. Actually, I wrestle because I like it, and it's the only thing I really know how to do well. The muffin recipe is my mothers, and I just happened to use it to make money." John Salley: "It's like the NBA. Eventually, you're making dough, but you keep going for the love of the sport, right?" KH: "Exactly." TA: "I think it's more like a can of Pringles. Once you pop, you can't stop." * The audience groans at Tom's lame attempt at a joke. * TA: "What, you people wouldn't know funny if it spit on your shoes. We'll be back after a short break, to talk more with wrestling superstar Kin Hiroshi about his high profile upcoming matches in NFW. Stay tuned..." * A few quick commercials fly by: another Dodge ad, an ad for Joe Schmo on Spike TV, and a Hiroshi-Berry Muffins by Hostess spot. * TA: "We're back, and in case you weren't watching, our guest is Kin Hiroshi. Kin, you have some SERIOUS matches coming up. In CSWA, you’re sure to defend the Greensboro Title. In GXW, you are the challenger for the World Championship at the pay-per-view Global Warfare. However, in NFW, you have not one, but two high profile matches. The first, a North Conference match against Armando Montezuma. Care to comment?” * Hiroshi takes a deep breath. * KH: “Here’s where I prove myself to NFW. My first match was against Eli Flair, a legend in his own right. I beat him. Some claim that Flair wasn’t on top of his game, sure I’ll go with that. His head just wasn’t focused on wrestling me. As such, I beat him, but I get no credit for beating him besides 10 points next to my name. I, Kin Hiroshi, put down Eli Flair. Come on, I knew I was good, but great? Yeah, I guess I am. “Now, I have what is called a ‘Freefall Match’ against Armando Montezuma. You see, the ring is 20 feet I the air, and the first person thrown off is the loser. I think that this match is rigged personally. First of all, the competitors: Kin Hiroshi, a 6’1” in shoes-kinda-person, and Armando Montezuma, 6’4” barefoot-kinda-person. Montezuma already has a size advantage, and as such a little leverage for throwing me out. “Secondly, you have our individual wrestling styles. Montezuma was an amateur wrestler, hence he stays low to the mat and is very technical and methodical. He also uses some big moves to put his opponents out. Then, there’s me. I take to the air more often, and risk my body to injure my opponent. So, because of this, I am easily caught in err on the top rope during this match. I have to adapt, while Montezuma takes his Cuban ass, and wipes the floor with me. “Now, I’m not complaining about this match. Montezuma has his weaknesses too, and all it takes is that knee of his to get a little boo-boo again, and it’s over the top and down to the bottom for him. But, I won’t end once he’s eliminated. I promise that I will end his career with a Hiroshi-Ma Bomb from the top-rope down onto his broken corpse of a body, and break every rib that he has. Why? Because I can.” CR: “Yeah, but couldn’t you injure yourself, in turn ruin your chances with Cameron Cruise?” KH: “Listen, I know we are going to talk about Cruise, it’s inevitable. Before I go into this match, I need to make one thing clear with Cruise. I heard your little tirade about how I am a bad seed. How I would ‘light up’ with Boogie Smallz before matches. How I poisoned Chad Dupree, and how I’m cheating my way to the top of the wrestling world. “Cam, we’ve known each other for a long time. Hell, outside of the wrestling arena, some might have called us friends. But I’m going to warn you, don’t go sticking your nose where it doesn’t belong, and if you do, get your facts straight. “First of all, I never ‘lit up’ with Smallz. In fact, it was he who cost me the GXW Television title by giving me some samplers of muffins he wanted Hiroshi-Berry Inc. to make. Of course, these were ‘special muffins’ with special ingredients…” TA: “You got high?” * Kin’s temper finally flares up, and he stands and gets in Tom Arnold’s face. * KH: “Listen you fat, out of a real job, overrated and overpaid pseudo-actor, and even less of a comedian. Shut your mouth. “Cruise, this Japanese Death match will be your last. You will fear and respect me after this match. Sure, you’re the bigger man and claim you have stamina to last through this match and your match against that punk Jean Rabesque, but son, you forgot what my nickname was before I got tagged with Muffin Man. ‘Japanese Thunder’ they called me. In Tokyo, they would chant my name long after I finished my matches because they knew I was coming back to wrestle one, two, even three more matches in one night. Like thunder I would roll on through the night, only to strike like lightning. “I have the endurance and stamina to bring you down. I will too. Because I don’t owe you a damn thing. Your wife made the call to NFW to bring me in? Great, at least we know who has the balls in the relationship. You handed me the title? Fine, but I ran with it. Just like I’m going to run over you. “Bring your sub-par game Cam. I’ll blow your ass up on one of the strategically placed explosive devices. I’ll beat your ass with whatever I find, and I will win. Why? Because in the world of wrestling, I am a Lincoln Navigator and you, Cam, are a Ford Taurus. “I will pin you. I will make you submit. I will knock you out. I will prove that the NFW Northern Conference is not a force to be messed with, and I will show the world that Kin Hiroshi is Mr. Wrestling. Now, boys, good luck with your TV show, and invite me back if you want ratings again.” * Kin pulls off his microphone and exits the stage. * Tom Arnold “That son-of-a…we’ll be back in a minute. I hope… ** FADE TO GOLD **
