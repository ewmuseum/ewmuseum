Following New Frontier Wrestling's Lottery Special, we had a rare opportunity to speak with Evan Aho. The twenty-eight year old former world champ was the first draw for the newly formed NFW South Conference. Although the actual transcript is only slightly longer, here are some of the highlights of our conversation with the taciturn wrestler's wrestler. Evan Aho on... NFW South Conference "I'm glad to be in the same conference with some guys that can really work. I've wrestled a few of them before and it's been fun. I don't know the roster top to bottom, but I hope that the guys I'm not as familiar with will bring their best to the ring every night like Rabesque, Stanley, Cruise and Southern." New competition "Each match drives me, but most of these guys I can't relate to at all." The Code of Honor "To me, it's embarassing and insulting that it even has to be instituted. If you're any damn good, honor comes with what you're supposed to be doing. For wrestlers of this caliber following it should be a no-brainer." The best ring technicians "Send them my way and see who's left when we're finished." Respect "Most of these guys play lip service to the idea. Wrestlers tend to toss the word around pretty easily... of course you're going to respect Eli Flair, but you'd sell your soul for a win against him. I don't play into **** like that. How you conduct yourself in the ring shows respect for wrestling and, to a degree, respect for your opponent." The Ultratitle "I would have loved to be part of the hundred twenty-eight man tourney. It's not the title so much, but the people who are vying for it. I don't have to name-drop, you all know."
