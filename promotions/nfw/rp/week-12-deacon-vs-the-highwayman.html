WEEK 12: DEACON vs. THE HIGHWAYMAN

29 Apr 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A huge white screen with two black Assassin’s style eyes and a mouth provide the backdrop as the Highwayman comes walks out dressed in white pants, boots, white mask with black eye holes and white T-shirt emblazoned with black letters that read “Passion of the Highway”. The Highwayman blends into the backdrop making only the words on his shirt and the black around his eyes clearly discernable. There is a wisdom to his voice like the old masked wrestlers who spoke with little emotion, but always seemed “in the know“.) I was a highwayman. Along the coach roads I did ride With sword and pistol by my side Many a young maid lost her baubles to my trade Many a soldier shed his lifeblood on my blade The Christian bastards hung me in the spring of twenty-five But I am still alive. And Chris Shepherd, I’ll always be alive. Honor among thieves ….. Code among con men ….. But what of the good man who time and again sends a man he calls friend out to feel the edge of the sword on his behalf. In my book that man is no good man…… he is the exemplar of what evil is. Chris Shepherd, I have an axe to grind with you. It is not in my interest to tell you whether it be a personal history, a bounty, or a friend of mine that you have wronged, but Chris I will not send a representative on my behalf. I will do to your mighty warrior exactly what I did to you. I will give him the beating Chris Shepherd deserves. I was a sailor. I was born upon the tide And with the sea I did abide. I sailed a schooner round the Horn to Mexico I went aloft and furled the mainsail in a blow And when the yards broke off they said that I got killed But I am living still I’ll always be alive. So now I make my in ring NFW debut, but don’t think for a second Chris that you send your Deacon against a man who has no experience, a man who’s skills are not polished, a man who does not know how to fight the giants of *your* sport and win…… But I think you know I’m not here just to win don’t you Chris. Let the Hornets, and Loves, and Southerns…. Let them wrestle to win Chris, I am here to sacrifice that giant lamb you have sent to the ring so many times to cleanse your actions. Your Deacon may not walk out of that arena, Chris, and if he doesn’t I hope the camera catches a glimpse not of you showing the concern that you so often feign, but of the "better him than me" look that you have hidden so well.....that surely will be impossible to hide when they tell you the man who beat MS to walk again, may never walk again because of a grudge against you. I was a dam builder across the river deep and wide Where steel and water did collide A place called Boulder on the wild Colorado I slipped and fell into the wet concrete below They buried me in that great tomb that knows no sound But I am still around..I'll always be around..and around and around and around and around I’ll always be around. No matter if my soul leaves this world to find a final resting place. No matter if no one ever pulls this mask upon their face. There will always be someone around to hold cowards accountable for creating torment for impressionable fools. I respect your religion. I respect the skills of your ignorant lamb, but Chris Shepherd you earn not even a modicum of my respect. A coward, a con artist, a false prophet who preaches words but offers no example for the masses to live by. We are all held accountable at some point though, Chris, and maybe I'm the man who shows the world what you should be accountable for. And when I reach the other side I'll find a place to rest my spirit if I can Perhaps I may become a highwayman again Or I may simply be a single drop of rain But I will remain And I'll be back again, and again and again and again and again.. (FTB) [the thread's next post — 3 May 2004] (Chris is sitting on a folding chair with a blank wall behind him. He’s casual looking, both in dress & demeanor as he begins to talk.) I don’t have a song for you... one like the one you played for me, Highwayman... but I have a scar. I have a memory that haunts me, a memory of the same stupid decisions on my part - of a girl who tells me of her desire to be with Eddy Love... and of Eddy in a mask taking out a man who is paid to talk ... not fight. Congrats... ‘highwayman’, you took me out. ... sure... Ya know, in 1998 when you and Deacon were suns just starting to rise, I had faith that Deacon vs. Eddy Love would happen. Of course then, I didn’t think you’d be wearing a mask. But that was before I really knew you, Eddy. Now, I know better. I know that you’ve misconstrued Deacon & I’s relationship - how I’m “sending Deacon in the ring to do my bidding”. Funny thing is... if it was the Deacon who did this, he’d have taken you to the mat for 3 seconds back in round 1 of the now infamous world title tourney that setup the CSWA’s new beginning. But let’s face it, Providence decided that for us. He never got his way, and believe me, I had nothing to do with it. As I listened to your monologue, I realized some things... I realized that you don’t know me.... that you don’t know Deacon... and that you don’t realize just exactly what you’re getting yourself involved in. You still think that Deacon is some young kid being led by the hand, but Eddy - I don’t need to lead in that ring. Mike Randalls learned it... Hornet learned it... Shane Southern, Eli Flair... man, everyone who’s ever set foot in that ring with this man knows the reality - Deacon only needs one thing to go in that ring. He doesn’t need strength... he’s already lost it. He doesn’t need agility... lost it as well. He doesn’t need skill or the Altar Call... he needs his faith in God. That’s all. That’s it, and after 6 years, I’d have expected more from you. Then again... maybe not. So bring your song and play it... maybe even a happy little jig to cheer you up after the 3 count. Bring your mask and wear it...because if you still think Deacon is a kid, you’re gonna be so embarrassed you’ll need it. And I’ll bring my scars, just to remind you that... in the NFW - YOU started it.
