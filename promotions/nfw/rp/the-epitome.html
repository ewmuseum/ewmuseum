The Epitome

8 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: Troy Windham, on the parlor of his South Beach estate. His face is banged up -- a black eye, a busted nose, proof of blood on his lip -- and he also has on a neck brace. And a smirk.) TROY: "We're just THIS much closer. We're THIS close to what the world has been waiting for. For over FIFTEEN YEARS they've wondered who is better. Is it yours truly, the trash talking Texan-cum-Hollywood party boy? Or is it Mike Randalls, the brooding man who lives in the caves of America's many national parks." (Troy adjusts his neck and winces in pain.) TROY: "First, I want to give it up to Akita Hoshi. Tsunami. My man, you gave me EXACTLY what I wanted. EXACTLY what I needed. EXACTLY WHAT I KNEW YOU WOULD GIVE ME. And that was the fight of a lifetime. It has been YEARS since I was in a match like that. A match where with one quick move, it would be over. And I'm not talking '1-2-3' over. I'm talking 'one more neck fusion surgery and you won't be able to walk again' over. I'm talking 'we can't cast you in this movie because the insurance carrier won't cover your injuries' over. Akita, you made me think that I wrote the final sentence in the history book that is my illustrious career. But... but it wasn't. I didn't beat you. I *SURVIVED* you. Akita, you are the toughest wrestler I have ever fought in my career. But there's one man I know is tougher than you." (Troy winces but then smirks.) TROY: "Mike. Randalls. Let me applaud you for what you accomplished. Attacking me after I just had yet another five star match in my career, this time with your running buddy. Dumping me on my neck right from behind. One person might call you a COWARD for attacking someone from behind... but I know better than that. Because you would have done that to me even if my back wasn't turned. And, Randalls, that's exactly what I want from you. That's exactly what I *NEED* from you. And I know you'll give it to me." (Troy rips off the neck brace, screaming as he does so.) TROY: "I need you to give me EVERYTHING you have. Because when I beat you, Randalls... and, yes, I will beat you... then there will be no doubt in anyone's mind that I am what I have always said I am. And that's the best who has ever walked down an arena aisle and stepped between those ropes and entered a wrestling ring. You're going to give me everything you have left. And it may destroy me forever. It may put me in a wheelchair for the rest of my life. But, Randalls... you WILL NOT... I repeat... WILL NOT... beat The Epitome." (FTB)
