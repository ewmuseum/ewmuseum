A vision of a DC yet unseen

9 Apr 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene- Washington DC, or at least a suburb, a backyard to be exact, a group of teens are in a huge circle cheering and "going off" while two teens are "Backyard wrestling". Cheese graters, tables, staple guns, shovels, swingsets, whatever you can get your hands on is the norm. While one teen falls to the ground from exhaustion, another is climbing up a drainpipe to the roof. He stands on the edge like a guard surveying a crowd. He dives off the roof and plants two elbows directly in the abdomen of the fallen teen. The Bloodthirsty crowd is completely nuts by this point, but both teens twitching on the gound. One is grabbing his abdomen and rolling on the grass, the other is lying almost motionless as well he did plant the elbows, the his head impacted on the ground knocking him nearly unconscious. The abdomen-injured teen , let's call him "Jake" is slowly getting to his feet as the other is as well. Jake is too engrossed in pain that he falls onto the slide of a swingset clutching his Doritos filled stomach. The other teen, the focus of our story sees this, runs, leaps onto and off of a picnic table, somersaults in mid air and splashes onto "Jake" collapsing the slide and the swingset. This of course gets a "1-2-3" from the "referee", as our focus stumbles away and collapses in a corner trying to catch his breath, and break free from all of the pain he has brought onto himself, he is being watched, he doesn't know it, but he soon will. The scene fades as the lone figure in the background watching our focus walks away rubbing his hands together.)
