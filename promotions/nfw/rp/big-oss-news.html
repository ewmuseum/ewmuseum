Big OSS News

11 Apr 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: A podium set up outside of NFW Headqarters in Boston. A crowd of reporters and on-lookers surrounds the podium. JUST MARK, with a NFW House Mic, stands near the rear of the crowd.) MARK: That's right folks, the NFW's greatest TV star... (flashes his pearly whites a parts his long hair.) JUST MARK is here in downtown Boston. Earlier in the day, various members of the wrestling press were sent an important NEWS RELEASE stemming from the WRESTLING DIVISION of CALVIN CARLTON ENTERPRISES... now, don't worry... JUST MARK already knows what this is about, being the greatest broadcast journalist in the industry tday. But I want to let YOU all see the surprise for yourselves! (At that point, "Back In Black" by AC/DC come on over the PA that has been set up. Walking from a blocked off area in the back is CALVIN CARLTON-- wearing his LA Lakers suit, carring his tennis racket that now has the interlocking NY logo on it. Behind him are "FABULOUS" FRANKIE FARGO and "THE NEW AMERICAN STUD" BRANDON MUELLER-- the tag team combination known as THE ORIGINAL SHOWSTOPPERS-- wearing their Confederate Flag bandanas, bowties, tux-tails and plain trunks.) CALVIN: (cackling) I can't tell you how THRILLED we ae to be BACK IN BEANTOWN... everywhere I go in this decrepit hell-hole, I am reminded of how LUCKY I am to be born and bred in a family of PRESTIGE the only son of my momma, my sweet and beautiful momma, the lady who lights up my life.. AND YOURS. I look in this slum and I'm reminded of HOW MUCH BETTER my hometown of LOS ANGELES is... where the LAKERS REIGN AS CHAMPIONS... or I'm also reminded of HOW MUCH BETTER my ADOPTED HOMETOWN of NEW YORK CITY IS... Bostonians, I just got off the phone with George Steinbrenner, my momma's dear friend, and he wanted me to say hello. But he couldn't talk LONG because he was busy shining ALL OF HIS CHAMPIONSHIP RINGS! (Calvin holds his arms up triumphantly as the OSS applaud.) But that's not why I'm here. You see, it's been over a year since the greatest tag team of all time... the ORIGINAL and REAL SHOWSTOPPES... reunited to come to the NFW to take back what was rightfully theirs... their name and legacy... and the NFW World Tag Team Championships-- titles that are RIGHTFULLY theirs. But being the most dominant tag team in the NFW for the past year... we've grown as men and we've grown as people. Even great, rich people like ourselves can SELF IMPROVE. Take for instance my man BRANDON MUELLER here. (Mueller struts forward and gives Carlton a hug, followed by a kiss on the cheek.) His ability to bed supermodels and virgins is LEGENDARY... and combined with his hair, the most beautiful head of hair in ALLLLL of professional wrestling... (Mueller runs his fingers through his mullet.) he has EARNED the nickname of THE NEW AMERICAN STUD. But you see it has come to our attention that there is nothing NEW about this, not after a year of making girls wet and having no regret. But it has come to our attention that he is indeed the LAST of his kind... because every single girl who has sex with Brandon Mueller no longer has any urge to be with any other man... so from now on, the master of the Mississippi Mudslide will not be known as The New American Stud... but you can now call him... THE LAST AMERICAN GIGOLO! (Mueller again shakes out his mullet and does a reverse strut to Frankie Fargo, where they do a complex high-five. Fargo nods his head and struts up to Calvin, putting his arms around The Manager of Champions.) And Frankie Fargo here is a man who... well, words don't BEGIN todescribe the kind of man he is. (FARGO:No, they don't!) He's the most feared man in all of America and NO ONE wants to get in the sites of the Southern Assassin. But there is one thing certain... a man with a body like this (Fargo flexes his bicep and kisses it.) doesn't come around every day of the week! In fact... a body THIS GOOD only comes around ONCE IN A LIFETIME. To call him FABULOUS... well that's an INSULT because he's MUCH, MUCH more than that. Frankie Fargo is truly... THE TWENTY-FIRST CENTURY ADONIS! (Fargo flexes biceps and swivels his hips around, then points to a jeering on-looker and gives him the finger.) Now, you'd BETTER refer to the by those nicknames at all times or else I'll sick one of my momma's lawyers after you! Come on, boys. Let'sget out of this diarrhea hut and go to one of my mansions! (Calvin, with the OSS, walk off to BACK IN BLACK.) MARK: There you have it, folks! The Last American Gigolo! The Twenty-First Century Adonis! The Original Showstoppers are now even stronger for even longer! And no one is safe! I'm Just Mark...and I remain your excellence in broadcasting. (FTB)
