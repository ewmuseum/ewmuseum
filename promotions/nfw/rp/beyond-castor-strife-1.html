Beyond Castor Strife (1)

9 Apr 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following episode was filmed prior to BRAWL: Pittsburgh (FADEIN: CASTOR STRIFE doing diamond-pushups in a cramped hotel singlet in front of the television. ‘Carlito’s Way’ is on - it’s the scene where Sean Penn freaks out on some guy for screwing a girl on the front lawn, tells him to bring her in the bathroom like a normal person) V/O: (Castor’s voice) “They told me something completely different. … That still sounds like your -up, not mine. … I-can’t-make-it-out-there! (CUTTO: Castor’s on the sidewalk, on his phone, near the corner of 17th and Walnut while traffic passes behind him. Subtitle on the bottom-right corner of the screen reads: “[B]PHILADELPHIA, PA – 9:30 a.m. (on phone w/Fiona Love, NFW Exec. VP/Director of Marketing)”. He is wearing a black zip-up sweater over white shirt) CASTOR: “You have eight guys- … You have eight guys- … Listen, stop talking over me. … 2:15? What difference does that make? If I could make it for 2:15, I’d have agreed to 2:00 and just showed up 15 minutes late! (laughs) … Our PR team sucks. Next time keep me in the loop ahead of time. (hangs up) Corporate twat.” (laughing off camera; Castor breaks a smile) (CUTTO: BLACK SCREEN/WHITE LETTERS) "BEYOND CASTOR STRIFE: Independence, Revelations" Directed by DEVIN MILLWOOD & CASTOR STRIFE Produced by NFW FILMS (CUTTO: Castor looking down at phone) DEVIN MILLWOOD: (off camera) “Did that go as bad as it sounded?” CASTOR: “They want me at Dick’s Sporting Goods in Pittsburgh for 2:00. I had no idea until she called me just now. So I told her, I’m in Philly - not happening for 2. Not to mention I could get more people to the show by handing out tickets in Philly than wasting my time out there.” (Millwood steps into the frame) MILLWOOD: “Not a fan of Pittsburgh, I take it?” CASTOR: “Can’t make money in Pittsburgh. You know the Pirates? Baseball team. Last year I was in town for a show, and I’m in the hotel room watching TV when a half hour spot for the Pirates comes on. Literally, it was a PR guy standing in front of empty cubicles, begging people for a full 30 to CALL NOW because season tickets are still available. That’s Pittsburgh in a nutshell – call now, tickets still available. It’s also one of the ugliest cities in America. Everybody looks the same, which is an obvious sign of inbreeding.” MILLWOOD: “You pull no punches, my friend. So where are some of your favorite places to perform?” (Castor pauses, looks out onto the street, gestures at somebody in acknowledgement) CASTOR: “Well…any good wrestling town. Philly is at the top of the list. I can’t understand why NFW went from doing a show in dead Buffalo to dead Pittsburgh, especially when they could have easily booked Philly. It’s a ghost town tour.” MILLWOOD: “But as I understand, you love wrestling in Lowell.” CASTOR: “Lowell’s not dead! It’s poor and everybody’s on house arrest, but they spend their welfare money on tickets when NFW’s in town. I’ve been trying to get us back there for a while. Remember, I started my career in Lowell when NFW moved from Baltimore. Now I’m a God there, with a big G. Britain and Germany love me too. The South hates my guts, but it’s a place you get up for. New York/New Jersey is where I cut my teeth, spent most of my early life there. Alberta, L.A., Chicago, Florida. Not Pittsburgh…” (gestures again to someone off camera. “Yeah, come here!” (CUTTO: Castor on the street corner with a skinny mustached fan in a white shirt and Flyers cap) CASTOR: “If you had something to sign I’d sign it…” FAN: (low, shy) “No it’s cool I’m just happy to meet you. Is there anything coming up in Philly soon?” CASTOR: “You want tickets for a Pittsburgh show tomorrow? I can comp you a bunch.” FAN: “Yeah, um, alright cool. How many can I get?” CASTOR: “Many as you want.” VOICE: “YO! CASTOR GET OUTTA THE ROAD!” CASTOR: “Who’s calling me?” (looks at approaching second fan, shaking his head) FAN 2: “FK YIZ DOIN’ IN THE ROAD? IS THAT WHO I THINK IT IS?” (Overweight fan walks up to Castor, shakes his hand) CASTOR: “It’s 10 o’clock, what are you yelling for?” FAN 2: “Oh sorry pal! I’m sittin’ over there, right behind, over there I’m on my coffee break at my job, oh man, I see youz guys standing on the side of the road with cameras and everything!” CASTOR: “Want tickets to a Pittsburgh show?” FAN 2: “PITTSBURGH? Nah, we ain’t doin’ that.” MILLWOOD: (pulls first fan to the side for an interview) “Let me talk to you for a second. What’s your name?” FAN 1: “Eric.” MILLWOD: “You follow NFW, Eric? Who do you like?” FAN 1: “Um…I like High Flyer, been following him for a while. JTP before he left. Castor Strife and Nova, huge fan of those two. I like a lot of guys.” MILLWOOD: “What do you make of the fallout from Supercrash 4? Eric Dane swindled this man’s company (points at Castor) away, and is demanding a title shot. Do you think he should get it?” FAN 1: “Hmm. I mean, I don’t think he should get it, but at the same time I want to see Castor break his head and just end the whole thing. So just do it so we can get this guy off TV, ya know?” MILLWOOD: (to Fan 2) “Sir, can I borrow you for a moment?” FAN 2: “YEAH what’s up?” MILLWOOD: “What’s your name?” FAN 2: “Len Dabrowksi.” MILLWOOD: “Alright Len, what do you think about what Eric Dane did to this man?” FAN 2: “What he do?” MILLWOOD: “You didn’t see Supercrash?” FAN 2: “Nah I can’t afford that sh[bleep]. EDDIE MAYFIELD, I know you Eddie, and where you come from…we come from the same streets, a’right? See these hands? These are workin’ man’s hands. I don’t make enough money to pay $44.95 for wrestling. Undastand? So when you make it anymore that a common man can watch your program, without knowing what one of these computer guys can do, to get it over the computer through black-channel, black-market…you know? (looks at puzzled Millwood) Get it through da black-waves and steal it illegally…see but I don’t wanna hafta do that…I shouldn’t hafta. Undastand? I’m 37 years old, neva stole nothin’ in my life. Now I heard, A LOT of people talkin’ about Philly fans, that we boo F[BLEEP]kin’ Santa Claus. Lemme tell you, I neva booed F[BLEEP]kin’ Santa Claus in my life. I neva booed Donovan McNabb, but I agreed he needed ta go away, ‘cause of the way Michael Vick…(starts to BARK like a dog in the camera!)…the way HE was playing…” MILLWOOD: “But Supercrash was free. NFW hasn’t charged anybody for programming in years.” FAN 2: “Oh yeah? Maybe I was thinkin’ of something else. I watch now and then. I know this man (points to Castor), haven’t followed too much. Is it Season 7 now? You tell me.” MILLWOOD: “It is indeed Season 7. So recently, Eric Dane swindled Castor’s company from him, and is using it as leverage to demand a title shot…” FAN 2: (nodding) “Mhmm…” MILLWOOD: “If you were Castor Strife, what would you do about it?” FAN 2: “What I do if someone steals from me? Any of youz steals from me, youz is f[bleep]kin’ DEAD.” MILLWOOD: “So you think Castor should give him a title shot?” FAN 2: “Yeah, why not. LISTEN I gotta get back ta work! (turns to Castor, shakes his hand again) Nice ta meet ya, and good luck!” CASTOR: “Take care.” (FADE to a black screen; CUEUP: ‘Without a Face’ by Rage Against The Machine – [B]“Liberty” appears in white letters) (FADEIN: Fast-forward view of Castor waiting in line at Dalessandro’s for a Cheesesteak. As the line moves, Castor begins to voiceover narrate) CASTOR: (V/O) “Might makes right.” MILLWOOD: (V/O) “Excuse me?” CASTOR: (V/O) “Might makes right. We’re all sprinting for the same gun, and the winner is a lucid-dreaming strongman rapist. With killing-power in his hand, nothing can stop him. The victim feels it pressed against their temple on one side, and life, liberty, happiness as it exits out the other.” MILLWOOD: (V/O) “Do you feel like Eric Dane has a gun to your temple?” CASTOR: (V/O) “I’m the devil who disappeared into the mountains, and Dane’s firing blindly at the plateau. He plays an interesting game, though.” MILLWOOD: (V/O) “What do you mean by that?” (Fast-forwarding stops; Castor gives his order, “I need one cheese steak, with extra cheese, extra onions, and mustard. And if you have pickles, throw some on as well.” Camera fast-forwards again, and we see Castor get served and start to head out in back of the building) CASTOR: (V/O) “I mean…I wasn’t in Buffalo for Brawl.” (Fast-forwarding stops again; Castor approaches a homeless man in front of a barrel. Caption at the bottom of the camera reads, “JAVID DONES – former UWA World Heavyweight Champion, NFW Wrestler”. Castor hands him the cheese steak) CASTOR: “Special delivery.” DONES: “Oh…(smells it, starts to open tin foil)…oh thank you.” CASTOR: “How are you?” DONES: (opens sandwich) “Extra onions, I see. Extra cheese too? Mmm, yes. I don’t see the – oh, there it is, the mustard. Mmmm. Elegant.” (takes a huge bite) CASTOR: “Life treating you OK?” DONES: (shakes head, mouth full) “Nobody is hiring. NFW…not paying for my rehab.” CASTOR: “Really? They paid for mine three times. I’ll have to talk to somebody.” DONES: “You are kind.” (CUTTO: Real time – evening sky is getting dark, and Millwood is walking down the sidewalk with Castor, who has a Lean Body chocolate protein shake in his right hand) MILLWOOD: “You’re saying that wasn’t you who appeared on Brawl: Buffalo?” CASTOR: “That is exactly what I’m saying.” MILLWOOD: “For the audience who might not be up on things, we saw Castor Strife show up in Buffalo, address the crowd, state he had an open contract, and interfered in matches alongside Legion. And you are telling us, on record, that it was not…you.” CASTOR: “Correct. I wasn’t even in the state of New York. Originally I planned to be there, but opted to go to New Jersey instead for a magazine interview and photo shoot. WrestleNOW can confirm all that.” MILLWOOD: “I’m having a hard time with this, so bare with me. I saw a man claiming to be Castor Strife, World Title and all, show up to Buffalo with Legion.” CASTOR: “You saw a man close to me in height and build, with blonde hair, wearing a gas mask, show up with a belt wrapped in a bag, and Legion in tow.” MILLWOOD: “Sorry, this is coming as a bit of a shock. I’ve been following NFW since the beginning, first as a fan, now as a reporter, and I don’t think a charge of doppleganging has ever been made before. I remember Michael Manson, and you, and the ‘NOT’ impersonators, but this is something on another level.” CASTOR: “This is false impersonation of the highest order. Identity theft, even.” MILLWOOD: “To clarify then, you do not have an open contract?” CASTOR: “NFW books my matches. World Championship bouts are usually negotiated between two parties, but as far as one-off matches go…it’s always an open contract. Hell, I made a career out of head-hunting OCs.” MILLWOOD: “Will you be in Pittsburgh tomorrow night?” CASTOR: “Yes. I have an opponent. No word on who, but I will find out when I get to the arena.” MILLWOOD: “Does that bother you?” CASTOR: (shakes head) “Doesn’t matter. It won’t be a top 5 contender, and outside of a few guys there’s nobody on this planet who can touch me.” MILLWOOD: “Realistically, what chance does Eric Dane stand to beat you?” CASTOR: “He’s not getting a title shot. I have serious, serious problems with what went down at Supercrash, and there is debt to be paid. But if you think I’m about to throw away my best bargaining chip, think again.” MILLWOOD: “Does that go for Dan Ryan as well?” CASTOR: “It goes double for him. He had two shots and came up short both times. I didn’t like a draw any more than he did, but the fact remains: I walk away from a draw still the champion. He walks away with NOTHING. Sh[bleep] Devin, I can’t remember the last time I lost a match. It’s been YEARS. And it’s not like I took off three months at a time in between jobber gauntlets like the last so-called “unbeatable champion”. When he was chasing Mayfield’s Game Boy every week, I was breaking necks for real. While the Marathon Man was sitting on his couch collecting disability for a fiscal quarter, I defended the Television Title without taking a night off. After The Plumber left, the whole world couldn’t stop talking about how “dangerous” Dorchester Stratton was. And I kicked his career down the GUTTER. “Troy Windham is back! He’s going to wrestle!” No he’s not. He’s going to step in the ring with me and leave two minutes later. “Impulse is back!” Back down the ladder, have a safe trip. Then I heard you, Devin, talking about how strong and powerful Dan Ryan is. You, and Wildstar, and Kerry O’Connor did nothing but suck him off, about what a legend he is, and how nobody can survive his offense. That man unloaded on me like he’s never done to anybody n his entire, storied career…and it wasn’t enough. He couldn’t beat me.” MILLWOOD: “Come on, that’s not fair. I gave you your due and said it would be Dan Ryan’s toughest match in years.” CASTOR: “How charitable of you. Castor Strife, formidable challenger to the legend of legends. You know the thing about legends, Devin, is that they exist in the past. They exist in the mind. One day, I’ll be spoken of like that. But on this day, my legacy is a living, breathing animal. Dan Ryan and I, we have some things in common. Unlike 90% of this industry, we aren’t cowards who get by competing in friendly arenas, in friendly organizations run by old pals. We don’t sign contracts to have handshake clean matches with our best friends. We tread on foreign soil, and challenge our enemies. It’s not respect I’m giving Dan, it’s merely acknowledgement of fact. Here’s another fact: I am the undisputed king of the mountain. Wrestling me to a 60-minute draw is not a credible dispute. Swindling my company is not a credible dispute. They are distractions that will be dealt with in time. But if a shot at the crown is what you desire, everybody gets a different price tag. MILLWOOD: “What is the price for Eric Dane?” CASTOR: “I’ll let you know when I found out who’s playing me.” (FADEOUT)
