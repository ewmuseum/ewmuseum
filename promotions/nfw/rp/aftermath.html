[the thread's next post — 11 Feb 2004] (FADEIN: Downtown Boston, MA - NFW Headquarters. The press room is buzzing with wrestling beat writers, reporters and industry insiders awating the first official press conference from the league. On the stage, the ESEN and NFW banners hang to the floor behind a table with two microphones and two chairs. Photo flashes start flickering immediately as President of NFW, QUENTIN SULLIVAN and Owner of NFW, CRAIG MILES walk out from the side. SULLIVAN sits down first, he's wearing a neck brace - but still came dressed sharp in a charcoal Armani suit, shined black wingtips and oval-framed glasses. MILES has mirrored Oakleys on, a grey hoodie sweatshirt, blue jeans and no shoes. SULLIVAN adjusts the microphone as MILES leans back, expressionless.) SULLIVAN: "I'd like to thank you all for understanding why we waited longer than usual to announce our lineups this week. We'll be getting to that in just a second, since Craig has personally requested that he talk about them. Unfortunately, I sit here right now about to do the one thing I never thought I would have...I almost feel like my soul has been ripped out leading up to tonight and you'll have to excuse if this conference takes longer than usual..." (SULLIVAN adjusted his neck brace, before continuing...) SULLIVAN: "Now, when New Frontier Wrestling restarted operations - the hiearchal structure was forced to change to accomodate our incoming Conference Owners. Due to problems in our first incarnation, New Frontier Wrestling felt that it'd be in its own best interests to diversify control over the roster, scheduling matches and other day-to-day operations for the league. With those changes, Martena Martens and Calvin Carlton were given powers over the NFW North and NFW South product that you would aliken to Robert Kraft's control of the New England Patriots. Contracts with the venues, contracts with talent and an undisclosed share into TV, merchandising and other revenues the league would accrue." (SULLIVAN paused and took a sip from a nearby glass of water, before removing his glasses...) SULLIVAN: "You could say my position of President is much like David Stern or Paul Tagliabue's in the NBA and NFL, respectively. I am paid a yearly salary to make sure that New Frontier Wrestling is the penultimate in (f)wrestling. Craig Miles is the sole founder and owner of Extreme Sports Entertainment Network, the parent company of New Frontier Wrestling that promotes the league and puts it on television for the world to see. Craig has the power to refute all decisions made by Conference Owners, and myself if he feels it will be detrimental to the overall product." (CUTTO: POISON IVY, in black leather pants, Docs and a black tank top walking out to a huge buzzing surprise in the press room with several papers in her hand. MILES leans forward as IVY approaches and hands him the papers with a nod. CUTTO: SULLIVAN closeup…his eyes tearing up…) SULLIVAN: “It is why it is my regret, my dishonor and possibly the lowest point of my career in this business to tell you that Craig Miles after signing these documents delivered and witnessed by the cordial Ms. McGinnis – will relinquish all control and all ties of the Extreme Sports Entertainment Network and New Frontier Wrestling…” (Those in the press room, start buzzing loudly in confusion – not believing the words…tears from SULLIVAN’s eyes start falling…) SULLIVAN: “I have tried for the last 24 hours to stop Craig from making this decision, but to be fair to him…to be fair to everyone with this league right now…he will explain his reasons, make his last action as Owner and sign over control of ESEN and NFW to several new partners that will remain undisclosed…” [the thread's next post — 11 Feb 2004] (The Press Room buzzed loudly as MILES grabbed his microphone and reeled it in...) MILES: "For two days, I laid in a hospital bed watching the actions of Avery Prosser against this federation. I watched the replays of the now infamous and hideous attack on Lamont Hollywood. I gazed at Calvin Carlton and our World Tag Team Champions being mauled by his thugs. Then I watched DC Stratton and Neil Riddick sell out a whole conference for some mantra that's been dead 9 years." (MILES pulled out a cigarette, which IVY with a smirk lit for him..) MILES: "Within 24 hours of leaving that hospital, I had to travel to Orlando to compete in an fWo event due to certain obligations. By the time, I landed at 4:00 AM in Boston the next day - without a chance to defend myself, I had learned that most of NFW were planning to unionize against myself due to my relationship with Avery Prosser..." (MILES inhaled slowly, nodding his head sadly before exhaling.) MILES: "I haven't spoken to Avery Prosser in ten years, but apparently most of NFW's roster believed that I had approved of his actions during All Star Week for my own benefit. The fact is that right now, there's nothing more than I'd like to do but find Avery Prosser and send him to that wheelchair he should've stayed in..." (MILES laughed) MILES: "But that would play to much into a ballgame my good friend Hornet, the LEADER of this unionization effort. If you haven't learned by now - Paul had contacted every worker from that night in efforts to persauade them in his conception that the actions of Avery Prosser taken on the last night of All Star Week were conceived in my head, so that I could play the HERO." (MILES inhaled off his cigarette once more and exhaled a smoke ring...) MILES: "I could give you numbers on how many he convinced of this, but that won't be the point anymore. For three years, I've bled for this league, I've sacrificed everything for this league...and maybe it was my damn fault to bring back the Ultratitle, but now I've got a majority out of 24 wrestlers that don't know the **** I have to do to make this place run and obviously don't care...Paul's a lawyer and he knows that I could obviously figure out a way to get Avery Prosser out of the North...but right now, the way I feel about most of this roster...I WANT them to suffer." (MILES nodded in disappointment...) MILES: "There's a good saying out there, keep your friends close and your enemies closer. Avery Prosser isn't going anywhere, but I am. Ivy has handed me two documents - one, will be me selling the company to several contacts of mine...enough shares split that there will be NO majority owner of this league. Two, a restraining order drawn up by NFW's lawyers to keep myself 100 feet off all NFW Events until the playoffs start...I had it forever, but Ivy somehow convinced me that people might change their mind once Prosser ****s them all up the ass." (Everyone buzzed in shock over the statement...) MILES: "And you can put that in print. This roster thinks I'm Avery Prosser's friend? This roster thinks I'm in this for me? FINE, I'M OUT...I'M GONE...and Prosser, if I see you anywhere neutral - YOU'RE A DEAD MAN...and PAUL...once you're done with Tommy Boy..." (MILES flicked his cigarette away) MILES: "Consider the rest of your career in that hickass, backwater league BOOKED. You want to run me out of MY home? Just ****ing wait till I run you out of YOURS. Oh and if anyone's wondering..." (MILES smirked) MILES: "Michael Manson - you're a free man again. See - there was only one condition from holding that man back...ME. Mike's smarter than you all think - so when he restructured we agreed on two things, any decisions not signed by me...were void with him...and if I ever left New Frontier Wrestling - his commissioner 'powers' would resume. So Mike, you've been my only friend...good luck with however you're gonna **** up things, I don't care anymore." (MILES pulled out a yellow sticky pad note from one of his pockets...) MILES: "So as my FINAL decision as Owner of this place, I shall announce the Week 9 Lineup. Which will be held in AVERY PROSSER's North Conference's Paul Tsongas Arena - unless he names it after some Black Panther reject. In the North, Marx will face Ryan...in a SUBMISSIONS match, since Dan was so vocal ...I'd like to see if he's a whiner, or just a quitter. DC Stratton...**** YOU. You want to blow things up? You'll blow up, you ****...you and Hiroshi - Japanese Exploding Death Match. Poe and Strife - well, Poe's ****ing stunk...so why not? A gas mask match. Shizaki and Sands...they're new, no problems, no stips. Shawn Hart and Shawn Douglas...we haven't had one of these in awhile. Hart - you've been less than 'phenomical' lately. If you lose, Douglas gets your nickname at ALL NFW events. And finally, Aho...shape up or ship out - you got Manson, no stips. PROVE you're a wrestler for once." (MILES pulled out another cigarette and placed it behind his ear...) MILES: "Now in the South, Promo and Garvin - whatever, just wrestle. Viper and Cruise...well Cam, just remember you hit me with the chair first. So, have fun with Viper in a Singapore Cane match...I hear he's good with those. It wouldn't be NFW without Chris McMillan and a ladder...let's just hope Shawn Matthews doesn't fall and break his neck like his manager. Rabesque and Melton - I can't make heads or tails out of either of you...but just to be good 'ol Craig...no Figure 4's allowed. Shane Southern - on the night you come forward with sin...I thought it'd be just perfect for you to face Deacon...IRONMAN style. MILES: "Every ****ing single man, woman and child in NFW understand two things. One, if I find ANY of you before outside this fed...YOU'RE ****ING DEAD. Two, if I come back...it won't be as your boss...I'll just be like all of you...with nothing to lose...and EVERYTHING to PAY ****ING BACK....except you Mikey, you're cool."
