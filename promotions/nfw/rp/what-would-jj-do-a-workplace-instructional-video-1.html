What Would JJ Do: A Workplace Instructional Video #1

12 Apr 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUE UP: Cheezy 80s synth keyboard music. The video is a flowery yellow screen with pink music notes exploding onto the screen. Finally, this FADES IN to JJ DeVille standing in front of a bluescreened office park. JJ's hair is back in greasy ponytail form. He's wearing a navy blue sports coat over his WWJJD T-Shirt and faded jeans.) JJ: "Hello there! I am professional wrestling's JJ DeVille. I am a natural born leader of men known nationwide for inspiring allies to do their best while shaming rivals into obedience and/or surrender. As someone with such obvious natural leadership abilities and a rare charisma not normally seen outside the halls of power, I am frequently approached by strangers as to the proper ways to handle workplace situations. Now, I don't normally like to talk to common people as I like not having skin rashes. But since I am peppered non-stop with your semi-literate requests, I figured I would give you my brilliant response. Here goes!" (JJ gives a thumbs up and then flips around the screen. CUT TO: A fat woman in her 60s behind a desk in an office. She has horrible dyed blonde hair and split ends, wearing a scarf around her neck and a Target off-brand dress jacket over a blouse. She gets up and waddles past another desk, a file of papers in her hand, where a young woman in her early 20s -- beautiful, college educated, bright-eyed, etc. -- sits with her e-mail open. The graphic of JJ then flips back on screen as he magically stands before the two of them.) JJ: "Meet Linda! Linda has worked in this office for 30 years, toiling away as a secretary for the company president. Every few months, a new graduate of a mid-tier college is brought in to work in the office as part of an executive training program. Linda has never been considered for this position -- SIMILAR AS TO HOW I WAS BLACKBALLED FROM PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING -- but she does oversee the first few days of the training process. And here, Linda walks past her young charge and sees her checking personal e-mail with post-it notes not squared away on the corner of her desk. How should Linda handle the situation" (Corresponding graphic) A: "Casually ignore the situation as you're busy making an e-bay purchase on your computer?" B. "Tell the young woman that personal e-mail usage is frowned upon and she should also keep her desk a tad neater?" (Back to JJ.) JJ: "The answer, of course, is NONE OF THE ABOVE. WWJJD, you ask? It's simple! Watch this!" (JJ flips out of the screen.) LINDA: "Ah, what IS this on your computer, missy?" YOUNG GIRL: "Oh, well, I'm on my lunch break and my fiance is trying to get in touch--" LINDA: "Do I look like I care about your life story? I don't. Upon your hiring, you signed a piece of paper outlining our workplace Internet usage policies. And that dictates that there is never to be ANY personal Internet usage." YOUNG GIRL: "Oh, well, thanks. I'm sorry. I'll make sure that I don't--" LINDA: "Yes, you'll damn well make sure you don't check your e-mail ever again. Because if I catch you, so help you god, I will fire you on the spot. And then what would your fiance think? Also, missy... these post-it notes are too disheveled." (Linda knocks them to the floor.) "Pick them up and place them on your desk properly. And then when you're done with that, I'll take my coffee with two creams and one sugar." (The young girl seethes.) LINDA: "Actually, I'll handle the coffee myself. Because of your insubordination... you're FIRED. Clean out your desk immediately." (The young girl can't believe it and cries as Linda laughs. JJ flips back into the shot.) JJ: "Yes, that is what JJ DeVille -- a Leader of Men and Hero to All -- would do in that situation. Once you have any inch of power over another person in a professional setting, see to it that you use it to its fullest. If your underling looks at you wrong or poses a threat because of her age and beauty, humiliate her and then fire her! That will make her feel small... and, most importantly... will make YOU feel better!" (JJ gives a thumbs up. The cheesey music from earlier plays. FTB.)
