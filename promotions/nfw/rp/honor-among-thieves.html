Honor Among Thieves

27 Jun 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: ARMANDO MONTEZUMA standing in front of the NFW North backdrop, holding a piece of paper in his hand and shaking his head.) MONTEZUMA: Code of Honor? What are we, back in Junior High? (Chuckles.) The same bleached blond chain smoking degenerate who crams his fat ass into leather pants on a daily basis and is married to a walking sperm bank actually has the AUDACITY to tell me, Cuba's National Treasure, how I need to behave in a wrestling ring? Give me a FREAKIN' break. Competitors must shake hands before a match? Listen, I value sportsmanship as much as the next guy, but this is professional wrestling - we're all getting paid to be here, we're all adults now, and if I don't want to reduce myself to shaking some wet-behind-the-ears punk's hand before I mop the canvas with him, then who are you to force me to do that? What, if I don't shake hands like a nice little boy, you're gonna take some points away from me? That's a DISGRACE. I'm Cuba's National Treasure, dammit - I don't have to be POLITE! No interference? Raul and Cojones have families to feed, man - what am I supposed to tell them now? I don't NEED any help, but I have a responsibility to my fellow countrymen to keep them gainfully employed. Is it POLITE - is it HONORABLE - to throw hard working employees out on the STREET? No harming the referee? Why don't we put a FREAKIN' dress on him then instead of a striped shirt? This is a JOKE. Now these referees are gonna come into that ring with an attitude - as if they're not BENEATH me - and they can SCREW me without having to suffer any consequences. But, that's fine - I can overcome that - I'm not a WHINER. No sneak attacks? That's part of the SPORT. If you're not man enough to survive a good old fashioned three-on-one beating in the locker room, then you don't belong in this sport. Go play some sissy game like tiddlywinks, marbles, or baseball. If you're disqualified, you've broken the Code of Honor. Once again, in the immortal words of Derrick Coleman - WHOOP DE DAMN DOO! The REAL incentive is that if you're disqualified, some other schmuck walks back to the locker room with your ten points. So, NFW, here's the deal - I'll play by your little rules - for NOW. (Smiles.) But after I win a few matches and assert my EXCELLENCE on the Montezuma Conference - all bets are off.
