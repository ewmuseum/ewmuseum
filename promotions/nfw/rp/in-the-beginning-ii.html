In The Beginning.. II

19 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Slade Wilson parked the black SUV in the visitors section of the NFW Headquarters' underground parking lot and calmly walked to the elevator following Shawn Douglas. It wasn't often that trainers accompanied their students to meetings but Slade had done this a thousand times before, he knew people and people knew him. "Slade!" What was I saying? People knew him. A man's grainy voice called out from behind and jogged towards the duo to meet up with them. It was Ronnie Schwartz. The talent relations operator wore a navy blue suit and red tie with his traditional kippot well nested atop his head. "What brings you to these parts, guy?" Schwartz smiled, shaking Wilson's hand. "Me." SD interjected with a grin, "The newest face in the NFW." Slade patted his protégé on the back. "That's right. This kid is the future, and the NFW is where it all begins." The talent relations operator looked at the duo and smirked. "Alright then. Well good luck, son. I've got to get going now. By some miracle, I still have a job here." Schwartz gave them a wink and jogged up a flight of stairs as Shawn Douglas called for an elevator. The doors opened and both men stepped inside. As they reached the 23rd floor, Slade and Douglas stepped out of the confines of the lift and walked confidently towards Avery Prosser's office. It wasn't every day that you met with a man like Prosser. His cool demeanor complimented the finely tailored black threads worn by Avery's slender frame. His back was turned to the duo, looking down upon the streets of Boston from his office window Prosser's black dreads swung side to side as he shook his head. "Look at these insignificant idiots walking, talking, going about their regular lives being led by others the whole time without a clue in their mind. Meanwhile, I steal, I take, I OWN... I own this damn world, I OWN the NFW!" Prosser's body twisted and saw Shawn Douglas standing alongside his massive trainer. The NFW North President grinned. "Douglas, right? Shawn Douglas?" "Yeah, that's me." SD looked on, perplexed. "Thought so." Prosser smirked pointing at Slade Wilson, "And who is this?" "Slade Wilson. You know me, Prosser. But this isn't about me, is it?" Slade shook his head, "No, it's not. So focus your attention on Shawn." Prosser's grin disappeared. "Fine. But I hope his attitude is better than yours towards authority." Cold stares were exchanged between the two men as Shawn Douglas changed the subject. "Listen, I came here to sign a contract and I have no other intentions than to do just that. Let's drop the threats, shall we?" Avery turned his focus towards SD and smiled. "A'right then, let's get it done but remember kid," Prosser shot darts at Shawn's eyes, "Choose carefully who you associate with in this league." The NFW North owner opened a drawer and pulled out a document from his desk, eyeballing Slade the whole time. SD pulled out a pen from his pocket and leaned over towards the laying contract. His eyes screened the document, skimming over each page and every detail. He was still against the idea of joining the NFW roster, especially since Avery Prosser had stepped in but Slade Wilson didn't let up. The trainer's track record spoke for itself and SD knew it. However, something wasn't right about this and Shawn Douglas knew it. "You better be right about this place, Slade." Shawn sighed to Wilson with a doubtful look and signed on the dotted line, "Now, let's just hope that wasn't a f*ck up." In the background, Avery Prosser smiled to no one but himself...
