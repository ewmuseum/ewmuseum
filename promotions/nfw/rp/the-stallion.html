The Stallion

21 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: Dorchester Stratton lounges back in a hair salon as an older Asian woman tends to his hair and another gives him a pedicure.) DORCHESTER: "So, Jack Bryant, I'm sure you're asking why. Why did I come out of nowhere and crush your face the way I did? Why did I try to cripple you? Well, Jack Bryant, I'm not sure how much you KNOW about me. But I'll give you a quick RUN DOWN. For seven years, I was everyone's cute little backyard-trained flip-monkey. No one took me seriously. No one CARED about me. Do you know how many main events I got, Jack? Do you know how many title shots I had handed to me, Jack? NONE. It took me damn near a DECADE to get anywhere near a title shot. And then every... single... time... SOMEONE gets in the way of me getting what I so rightfully deserve." (Dorchester sneers.) DORCHESTER: "Not like you, Jack. Oh, I know how Mayfield works. I know he's got a semi for that cute little Southern twang you have, how 'old-school' that makes you, which fits right in his dumb image of what this sport should be. You've had EVERY opportunity given to you in a very brief, quick and short amount of time. How long have you been here, Bryant? Not too long, correct? Certainly not damn near a decade. Yet you.. you have a title. You have the NFW TV Title. And I don't." (The woman starts towelling off Dorchester's hair.) DORCHESTER: "Now, Jack, as far as I'm concerned -- the NFW TV Title is an AFTER THOUGHT, since I am a man who should have been handed the World Title on a silver platter. But I, unfortunately, suffered a horrific injury which I shall not disclose any more details about. But now that I've healed and healed properly, I understand I have to... climb a few STEPPING STONES to obtain what I so rightly deserve." (Dorchester smirks.) DORCHESTER: "And I've climbed MANY stepping stones during my ascent, Jack. Rayne? I dumped her... and then dumped her, literally, on her neck. WildStar? His career's in tatters and his family is on the street. Tsunami? I kicked him so hard HE CHANGED HIS NAME. Joe the Plumber? I made him quit and took so much out of his career that he RETIRED. And now you, Jack... you're the next one I've lined up. So, Jack, I hope you've put in some plans for 'what to do after wrestling.' Professional car detailing? Landscaping? Frankly, I don't care either way. But, either way, people's careers tend to END when they cross paths with me... intentionally or 'accidentally.' And accidents, Jack... they can happen to ANYONE. But not to me. Because I'm the BEST at what I do... and I do... WHATEVER I WANT!" (FTB)
