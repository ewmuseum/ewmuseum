Son of the South

9 Apr 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: Jack Bryant, wearing a black Alabama hoodie by Nike, Wrangler jeans, and Bronc Stomper boots, leaning back into a wooden rocker. Across the porch, sitting atop the cedar railing, is Fiona Love, sporting her horn-rimmed glasses, a cream, off-shoulder tunic blouse, and black spandex leggings.) FIONA LOVE: “ I’m not at all surprised. Calvin knows your stock is rising. You’re the hottest ticket around the NFW since Shane Southern. He’s seen all the figures. It was only a matter of time before he tried to get into bed with you. (Folds arms across her chest) Are you actually considering his offer? ” JACK BRYANT: “ Ah got more pressin’ issues, Fee. ” LOVE: “ Dorchester Stratton. ” (JB nods.) LOVE: “ That’s part of the reason I wanted to come down. Mind if I offer my opinion? ” BRYANT: “ Have at ‘er. ” LOVE: “ I’ve seen what Dorchester is capable of, Jack. He turned on his mentor, Wildstar, and retired him. He punted his best friend in the head without a moment’s hesitation. He dumped Rayne and then tried to crush her spine with a piledriver. All for the fame. All for money. He’s obsessed. He’s vicious. “ BRYANT: “ What’s yer point? “ LOVE: “ My point is that Dorchester is a blackhole and swallows any star that gets too close. He sees a person having success and he’s overcome with jealousy. JTP was on an improbable run, too, Jack. Nobody could beat him, but Dorchester (looks down and pauses) tore him apart. He broke him down, piece by piece. He took the most popular, an unbreakable World Champion, demoralized him, and ended his career. (snaps fingers) Like that. (shakes head) He’s bad for business, Jack, and you’re poking at the hornet’s nest. ” (JB stands up and leans on the railing next to Fiona.) BRYANT: “ So, ya’ think Ah ought’a steer clear a’ Stratton? Protect mah stock? “ LOVE: “ No good can come from getting into a personal feud with Dorchester Stratton. Did you see his response to your Reloaded challenge? I’ve seen that man before, Jack, and he’s a career killer. ” (JB sends a gob of spit into the grass.) BRYANT: “ Heh. Ah’m not like most fellas ‘round tha’ Frontier. Mah knees don’t start knockin’ when Ah’m n’ a tough spot. Ah’ve never backed away from a fight an’ Ah’m not about ta’ now. Ah don’t have tha’ patience ta’ chase after cowards like JJ DeVille neither. Nobody else has tha’ gravel n’ their guts ta’ come after mah title. But, Stratton, he wants ta’ destroy me. Nothin’ could be more personal than that. Ah wanted ta’ test mah mettle against tha’ best n’ tha’ sport. Ah wanted a war. That’s somethin’ that only Stratton can give me. Ah’ve said, since Ah stepped foot n’ tha’ Frontier, that Ah’m tha’ best ‘round. It’s high-time Ah be tested. Ah’m either as tough, as good as Ah think Ah am, ‘er Dorchester Stratton is goin’ ta’ end mah career. “ LOVE: “ There’s nothing I can say that’ll get through that thick skull of yours, is there? “ BRYANT: “ ‘fraid not. It’s either me ‘er him. That’s tha’ way it’s gonna be. “ LOVE: (sighs)“ I was worried you might say that. " (FTB)
