A change in direction?

4 Jun 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene- an empty gym, an amateur wrestling mat, a lone figure is applying holds, and practicing suplexes with a practice dummy. As the camera zooms in, it reveals this figure to be "The King of the backyards" DC Stratton. His left knee is not only taped, but it has a full brace on it. He doesn't notice Neil Riddick walking up to him.) STRATTON: (looking up and abruptly stopping) Neil! I didn't see you....(stops to catch his breath) RIDDICK: Obviously...now tell me just what you think you are doing? STRATTON: Working out... RIDDICK: Why like this? I thought we agreed that you were going to practice your "off the cuff" techniques? STRATTON: Well, I just thought.... RIDDICK: You thought what? That you would do something else with your "career"? Are you forgetting that I'm the one who got you that career? Are you forgetting that I might just know what I'm doing when I tell you how to train? STRATTON: I was completely into it, and I still am, but ever since Manson screwed up my knee, I got to thinking. What if I can't do that stuff anymore one day? I gotta be able to do somethin' else. RIDDICK: That happened because you got sloppy...you shouldn't have let him get you in that position... STRATTON: (Getting annoyed) With all due respect, I didn't see you doing anything about it. RIDDICK: (Trying to be a calming voice) DC, I understand what is happening to you, trust me, I do. I want you to have a long career as well, how else am I going to get my money out of you...(Stratton looks straight through him) HA HA HA (Stratton laughs as well) I understand that you want to make sure you're not a one-hit wonder, but trust me, people will pay more to see you as an anti-hero who will fly all around the ring. You'llmake a lot of money doing it, and I'll be right there with you.... STRATTON: What about this? (points to knee) RIDDICK: That's what cortizone is for. STRATTON: All right, if you say that's what I need to do, than I trust you.....(Stratton walks off camera) RIDDICK: (to himself) You'd better, you stupid punk.... (Scene fades as Riddick shoves the camera to the floor.)
