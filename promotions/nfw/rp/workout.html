Workout

29 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fade in on the gym, Dieter is doing pull ups while wearing a weight belt that has impossibly thick chains hooked onto it. With every rep, his muscles stand out against his skin, straining as though to tear through his flesh at the effort. Sweat is pouring off his 7'6" frame, having completelydrenched the tank top he’s wearing, and he’s clearly at the limits of his strength with this exercise. Kata is in the ring, and she’s doing a plyometric drill. She hops up to the first turnbuckle, backflips off of it, then hops to the second turnbuckle, and backflips off that. Now she runs up to the top turnbuckle, and does a 720, landing on her feet. Then she goes back to the first turnbuckle. She’s clearly in her own world as she’s doing this, and while she makes it look effortless, her face shows that she is bored. Mr Redd steps into the frame, and the camera focuses on him.) MR REDD: (Smiles at the camera) “And here you thought they were just window dressing. I assure you, this is not the case. Kata is a third generation wrestler, on both sides of her family, and Dieter is a World class strongman from Holland. They’ve been wrestling together around the world for four years now... DIETER: “FIVE” MR REDD: (Looks at his watch) “Excuse me, five years now, and have been very successful. Now that Dieter’s papers are in order, they can start making me some money here in the states.” (Dieter drops from the bar, and unhooks the weightlifting belt. As he approaches the ring, Kata climbs out, running over to the station he was just at. She sprints to the wall, bounces off of it, and easily leaps up and grabs the bar that Dieter was just hanging off of. The camera pans and we see that Dieter is now throwing kegs one handed into the ring, over the top rope. The entire ring booms with the impact of the barrels as he continues to heave them in one after another.) MR REDD: “Dieter, how much do those weigh each?” DIETER: “Vifty each.” MR REDD: (darkly) “I thought you were training, not playing around.” DIETER: (Heaves the last of ten in) “Keelograhms. Not pouhnds.” MR REDD: (Looks into the camera and smiles) “I thought as much. Carry on.” (MR REDD walks off camera and we FTB) [the thread's next post — 30 Jul 2002] (FADEIN to a local gym in Atlanta, GA... we see Jerome doing bench presses, with what looks to be about 275 pounds. Scotty walks up with a bottle of GATORADE, looks into the camera and points at it. Jerome gets done doing a rep of 10, and stands up and looks at Scotty, who is wearing his baby blue ADIDAS training outfit, complete with a baby blue headbands and the shiny baby blue basketball sneakers..) JEROME: Man, when are you EVA' gonna learn that we ain't no joke? I just don't undastand what happened to you. Is your punk ass goin' through some kinda mid-life crisis? You used ta' be one of the most RESPECTED wrestlers in the sport, and now this? SCOTTY: Hey man (chugs a little more GATORADE, and then uses his arm to wipe off his mouth, smiling afterward..) like you said that one time, I'M IN IT TO WIN IT! I REFUSE... TO LOSE! The boys at GATORADE decided to send me to a shrink last week, and they said there's nothing wrong with me. JEROME: Well when yo' ass was talking about starring in BACK TO THE FUT'CHA or DIFF'RENT STROKES, you ain't seem fine to me. You gotta get into the year 2002.. SCOTTY: What, Jerome? Is this all some sort of JOKE to you? We're the team of the FUTURE? Yeah, right. Let's just live it up like the N.W.A. and rock it out with the gold chains, the #####, and the 19 inch TVs.... and maybe if we're lucky, we can get on MTV... and REMOTE CONTROL! That Kari Wuhrer is one piece of ace. I know from experience, dude... you know what I mean. JEROME: No you don't. SCOTTY: Well, not me personally.. but a guy I know, him and her got it on. WOOOO-EEEE. JEROME: No they didn't. SCOTTY: No, no, no they didn't... but you can imagine what it'd be like if they did (laughs) JEROME: Dammit, DAMMIT! I've had enough of this! If you don't stop screwin' 'round, we ain't never gonna have a tag team match. You saw that dude, Kata... and that chick, Dieter. SCOTTY: Jerome, I think it's the other way around. And hell, they were pretty damn impressive. You saw the way they were throwing kegs around and doing all of that other junk.... we've got a match with them at the next house show, and I think we might be in some trouble. JEROME: Well if you don't wanna get in trouble, you best start hittin' 'dese weights and GETTIN' SERIOUS! You ever hear of that, or you're too busy going out of your way to be a FREAK? SCOTTY: No man, that's what I'm here for. I was watching one of those infomercials on TV, and I think that I found the PERFECT workout gadget for both of us to use. I even got custom colors made, for me and you. If we go to work with these, we WON'T LOSE! (Scotty picks up a wrapped package and hands it to Jerome) G'head, open 'er up. JEROME: Man, if this is something stupid... yo' PUNK ASS IS GRASS, and I'M GONNA SMOKE IT! (Jerome goes about unwrapping the package and there they are, baby blue Thighmasters) I CAN'T BELIEVE THIS! You expect THIGHMASTAS to help us beat these peeps? Uh-uh. (Jerome slams the box down on the ground, and goes over and hits the weights. Scotty looks dejected, but picks up the Thighmaster and does TEN REPS... he looks excited..) Man, with these, we won't lose. (Out of nowhere, Enrique appears from behind with a cooler of GATORADE.... he dumps it out on SCOTTY, but he must've messed something up in the translation because he dumps about twenty 32 ounce bottles on Scotty's head, and he falls to the ground...) ENRIQUE: Uh oh. I am very sorry, sir. (Scotty gets up, while holding his head..) SCOTTY: It's alright man, just promise me you'll use this. (Scotty hands the thighmaster to Enrique, and Enrique looks thrilled... they throw high-fives to each other, as Jerome is now seen standing in the background shaking his head... with a hint of a smile on his face.... FADEOUT)
