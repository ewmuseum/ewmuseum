Search for the Truth..

26 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN to the gym, where we see Travis Knowles working out with the punching bag. He's nailing it, and he's broke a good sweat. His cell phone rings again, he picks it up... it's the voice of Shawn Matthews...) TRAVIS KNOWLES: Shawn, I've been trying to get in contact with you since that incident took place a week and a half ago. Where have you been? SHAWN MATTHEWS: Travis, don't worry about me, man. I'm fine... I'm sorry I didn't get a hold of you, but I had some other business to take care of. I've been searching for some information, as it relates to you and your family. I just felt it's been better to stay out of the spotlight. TRAVIS KNOWLES: Well what on earth were you doing near the home of Wildstar? I heard you were in his hometown.. SHAWN MATTHEWS: I was going up there to apologize. I've realized that I've been an ASS since I've gotten back here in the NFW. I want to go about doing things the right way, and that's why I've disappeared for a little while... hopefully the fans will not remember some of the stuff that I've done. The cursing, well... I can still do that, but that's not the point. Anyway, when you talk to any representatives of the NFW... don't tell them that this phone call happened. I've been raising up quite a stir over there in the AAW, and I've been watching the caller ID when the NFW owners call me, and not answering the phone... I don't feel like dealing with it now. TRAVIS KNOWLES: Well when do you plan on returning to the NFW? SHAWN MATTHEWS: Oh, I'll be back soon enough. I heard something might be going down with RAZE at the next house show, your first match. You better believe I'll be there. I took you under my wing and TRAINED YOU, made you into a KILLING MACHINE... and quite possibly, one of the BRIGHTEST STARS of the FUTURE. You best believe I'll be there to see your first match, and I will have your back. All these years you've spent... now it's time to live your dream, and become a SUPERSTAR, unlike my cousin, Scotty... who's become a homo. (laughs) TRAVIS KNOWLES: Scotty, it's great to hear from you. I just wish you would've gotten a hold of me sooner. I just don't understand.. SHAWN MATTHEWS: You'll understand soon enough, I'll tell you... I promise. Now's not the right time. Alright buddy, I gotta go... I'll see you at that house show. TRAVIS KNOWLES: Take care, man. (Knowles sets down his phone, and goes back to work at the bag.. FADEOUT)
