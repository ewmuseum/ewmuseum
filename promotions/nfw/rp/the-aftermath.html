The aftermath...

20 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN to the spot, where Shawn Matthews car hit the pole. A cab pulls up, and Travis Knowles jumps out and runs over to the pile of rubble that was left at the car caught fire. The hulking Knowles gets down on one knee and busts out in tears. This, coming from a man who only ever cried once... at the end of "Turner and Hooch". An officer walks up to Knowles...) OFFICER: Sir, this accident just occurred not too long ago, and we were wondering if you knew anything about it. TRAVIS KNOWLES: Aww man, I have no idea. I think it was my buddy, Shawn. He wasn't in a good mood, and he just took off from the club where we were hanging out. He.... (Knowles flashes a nervous look) wasn't drunk. I think he only had a couple of beers over a few hours. OFFICER: Well we found the body, and we were wondering if you could identify him... TRAVIS KNOWLES: Is he burnt badly? OFFICER: The body's pretty burnt up, but we were wondering... well... can you just come over here and check it out? (Knowles walks up slowly to see if it is indeed the body of Shawn Matthews... he has a very upsetting look on his face. Finally, the look turns to confusion..) TRAVIS KNOWLES: Wait a second, that dude's like 6'6, 300 pounds. Shawn's only 6'1", 235 pounds. OFFICER: Well we've searched the entire area, and this is all we found. There's no other place a body could be hiding... and there were no other remains in the car. TRAVIS KNOWLES: Well if Shawn wasn't in this car, then where in the hell is he? (FADEOUT...)
