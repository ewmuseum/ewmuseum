NFW Holiday Promo

18 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(cueup: 'Dead Leaves..." - White Stripes) V/O: "We all have our secrets, we all have something to hide..." (FADEIN: AARON DOUGLAS grinning in the direction of SHANE SOUTHERN during their Week 6 violence...) V/O: "We're all afraid of those secrets being revealed and some of us will stop at nothing to keep them safe." (CUTTO: CALVIN CARLTON looking haggard in his NFW South Commissioner's Office, dozens of crumpled pieces of paper dumped along his desk. The phone rings - Carlton checks the Caller ID and his eyes light up.) V/O: "But sometimes your secrets are revealed. Sometimes, the ones you try to bury are the ones that crawl out of the ground to your very doorstep." (CUTTO: AVERY PROSSER standing under a white spolight against a black wall. He's standing in an almost Special-Ops/Black Panthers outfit. He puts on a pair of black sunglasses, much like something out of 'Shaft'. PROSSER stands there with a devilish grin..." V/O: "And for some men..." (CUTTO: CRAIG MILES, smoking a cigarette...) V/O: "Its other men's secrets..." (CUTTO: QUENTIN SULLIVAN handing AVERY PROSSER an unmarked envelope on a desolate street corner. SULLIVAN does it through the window, obviously not trying to be 'seen'.) V/O: "That change the course of their own lives. Because secrets never die..." (CUTTO: A torn, burnt, ragged banner...that reads "INTO THE FIRE!") V/O: "They last FOREVER." (FADE TO BLACK. Words in big, bold white letters land with anvil f/x as the song cuts out, starting with NFW 2004: 'WAR OF THE RINGS' ** 1/11/04 - AllStar Night ** "EVERYTHING GOOD MUST COME TO AN END." CUTTO: Back to PROSSER still grinning - five men dressed just like him step out into the light, but remain too dim to be seen. Another five...another five...another five...PROSSER'S smile widens. FADETOBLACK.)
