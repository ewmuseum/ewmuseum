Free Joey Melton

30 Jun 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: Calvin Carlton, sitting on a big, cherry oak desk. Behind him is the NFW South Insignia logo. He's holding his family-crest tennis racket.) CALVIN: Rejoice, rejoice because once again me... Calvin Carlton, Manager of Champions, King of Men and Son of The Sunshine of Our Lives is here. So let me, as the ruler-slash-commisioner of NFW South, dole a few commands before I get to what I have to say to everyone. To those of you who live north of the Mason-Dixon line... who live in NFW North Country... I want you to stop what you're doing right this second. I want you to fix your television antennae that's located on top of your rust-tinged shanty trailer. Go up there and adjust that wire coat hanger-- the same coat hanger you used on your 13-year-old prostitute sister when she got knocked up by the auto-mechanic... and adjust the set so my words come in loud and clear. And then do what the respectful and well-heeled folks who live down South have done and that's sit down and shut up while a rich man is talking! Y'know, when I was growing up, I was a big fan of this sport. I had huge dreams and aspirations of what I could do in wrestling and what wrestling can do for me. That's why I decided to target this promotion. That's why my momma encouraged me to come into this league. She wanted me to take part of her small fortune and invest it so I, her favorite child, her blessed son, the man who rubs her feet when she has to walk in the rain, could live my dreams and aspirations and take over not just this sport, but this entire industry-- because that's what we Carlton's do, make monopolies and treat people like human animals. The NFW South is my first chance to show you all my genetic potential. Boarding schools, Ivy League schools, private nannies-- they have all educated me to be not just a man of unfathomable wealth but also a man of incredible intelligence. And I will lead this conference as its commisioner to the highest of heights. I will show Doc Martena... that filthy rape-hole... her proper place once and for all. Not only have MY boys the OSS stripped her's of the NFW World Tag Team Titles, but I will also strip her of the little shreds of dignity that she has left. And, who knows, after I brutalize her and humiliate her, forcing her to submit to my every whim, perhaps I will even let her stop scrubbing the potatoes once in a while and allow her out of the kitchen so she can come out here, in public, to tell the world how great I am and then, most importantly, tell the world how great my MOTHER is and how she worships us both. But to get to that point where I rule this industry with an iron fist, I need some help. There is no doubt in my mind that the first ever NFW Ultratitle Champion will be from *MY* conference. However, being the defacto ruler of NFW South, I don't just want our champion to COME from this land... I want our champion to REPRESENT the Southern Conference and represent me. Now, there are many people on my roster whose talents that I admire. However, I don't think that *ANYONE* on my roster is currently in my image. A few people, sure, may have the intensity and mental strength of a Carlton... men such as Shawn Matthews or Gabriel Poe... but they don't have the KILLER INSTINCT that my family has shown in its business ventures. I look forward to working with several members of our roster to get ahead and hopefully at least one man will emerge to be my trophy. Two men, currently, are jockeying for the position of NFW South's Leading Man. Shane Southern and Hornet. One is this industry's brightest young talent, just now starting to enter his prime, Shane Southern. The other is this industry's most famous name... Hornet. A legendary icon who is looking to show the world that he still is its top dog. Shane, Hornet-- while you guys argue and yell at each other, just keep in mind that you *WILL* represent me in m quest to win the title. In my eyes, neither of you have the wisdom that I posess, the strength that I have or the ability to make it over the top. Now, *I* can bring you to that position. I suggest that if you want to make it, you both start returning my cell phone calles. I also suggest that you start getting in my good graces IMMEDIATELY and you start paying me homage in a public setting-- like a GOOD Southerner-- and maybe, just then, we can work something out. Y'see, there are a few men in the NFW who *DO* have the attributes that I like to see. Armando Montezuma has proven himself to be a man willing to do what it takes to make it. While I haven't seen him wrestle in some time, the new guy, DJ Havok certainly has agreeable political views with me and my momma. Mikey Manson is a little slimy but hey-- if worshipping Satan gets him ahead then so be it. But there is only ONE man in this promotion who has shown me that he has what it takes. The first winner of this title. A three-time Ultratitle champion. That man is my personal boyhood hero, Joey Melton. Joey-- I know you're bored. I know you're waiting for a challenge to once again conquer this sport. Joey... no, wait... MISTER MELTON... I believe that if we were to combine forces, together we could make this industry crumble at our expensively-adorned feet. As such, I have filed the necessary paperwork to FREE you of life from that Yankee Hellhole in Massachusettes... to try and bring you where you belong-- down South, where you will be like another Sherman, marching across this land. I know, Joey, that no man can defeat you or stop you, just like when I watched you when I was a young kid watching you on my satellite dish from my momma's French Riviera estate. Yes, Joey, you're going to come back home. And yes, Joey, we will march together! (FTB)
