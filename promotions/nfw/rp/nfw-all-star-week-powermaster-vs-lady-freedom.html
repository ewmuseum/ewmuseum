NFW All-Star Week: Powermaster vs. Lady Freedom

8 Jan 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lady Freedom looks out upon Millennium City with a watchful eye. Though not born of this place, she has come to know it quite well over the last two weeks. A Gotham of dark deeds, Millennium City is full of strife and turmoil. Super criminals have been popping up all over, vying to take a bite out of populous. She has come here to help. To ease the suffering that plagues the city, to make a difference. However, a costume does not a super hero make. Yes she is quick, and strong, but only as quick and strong as a mortal athletic woman. Against the likes of Powermaster, it would be enough. But out on the street, against villains with an array of super weaponry, would that be enough? Not likely. Not likely at all. And so she turns to Dr. Reed Connors. Dr. Reed Connors is one of the worlds leading geneticists. He has done wondrous things in the field, and made great strides in manipulating human and animal DNA. She had read about him in PEOPLE Magazine, and decided to pay him a visit. Perhaps he could help her. Perhaps he could make her better, stronger, faster. It was a small chance, but one she would take none the less. Dr. Reed Connors office was at the top floor of the Four, Fantastic Drive skyscraper. He, his wife Susan, their two children, Frank and Val, his brother in law John, and his best friend Benny, all lived there on the level below. His lab and offices where he spent most of his time, working on one fantastic project after another. He was a genius, or a Big Brain and Benny would call him, and challenging his mind was a twenty-four hour task. Looking at his facilities, Lady Freedom had a feeling he could keep himself well occupied. She entered a small office, lead there by Susan. She asked her to take a seat, which Lady Freedom did. Susan - “My husband will be with you in a moment.” She left the room, and Lady Freedom sat patiently as she awaited the arrival of Dr. Connors. She looked around at the photos on the walls mixed in with his dozen or so degrees, each picture depicting a scene in the Doctors life that would constitute a crowning achievement. The birth of his sons, his wedding, breakthroughs in Genetic Research, and so on. One of the pictures showed a much younger looking Dr. Connors, standing with his wife, brother-in-law, and Benny, all next to this large craft that looked like a cross between a space shuttle and a cigar. She wasn’t sure what it was, but felt it was not her place to pry. The door opened again, and Dr. Connors entered the room. He smiled, his long face seeming almost rubbery as his grin went from ear to ear. The white tufts of his sideburns made him look distinguished, but something in his eyes showed a much different picture. Dr. Connors - “Hello, Lady Freedom. What can I do for you today?” Lady Freedom - “Well, Dr. Connors-“ Dr. Connors - “Please, call me Reed.” Lady Freedom - “Okay. Reed, I have recently joined the super hero community, and as of this point I have a costume and motivation. I haven’t the benefit of super powers. I was wondering....” Dr. - “If I could hook you up?” Lady Freedom - “Well, yeah. Basically.” Dr. Connors - “Well, I have been doing research into the origins of super powered individuals. I have an extensive file on the NexGens and their genetic mutations.” Lady Freedom - “I don’t want wings, or a tail, or to turn blue and fuzzy, Reed.” Dr. Connor - “Of course not. What I was getting at was that I have been able to isolate the X gene that caused the mutations in NexGens. Under controlled conditions, I can activate that gene in a human subject and cause them to mutate to whatever degree desired.” Lady Freedom - “Really? How long will it take?” Dr. Connors - “Approximately three sessions. The first will prepare your body for the changes, which will come on rapidly instead of thirteen years after birth and the onset of puberty. The second will activate the X gene, and the third will help control the changes and keep you from going mad.” Lady Freedom - “Going mad? Dare I ask what the other risks are?” Dr. Connors - “Only Death. Or horrible mutation that cannot be controlled.” Lady Freedom - “Oh great.” Dr. Connors - “Well, I have come into possession of an Alien Symbiote that grafts itself to your body like a suit and establishes a mental link so that your thoughts can control it’s actions. However, it seems to feed off adrenaline and might have some side effects of it’s own that I am not yet aware of.” Lady Freedom - “A living thing that leeches off me?.” Dr. Connors - “Excuse me?” Lady Freedom - “Never mind. Anyway, when do we start? I have my first match against Powermaster, and I don’t want to screw up.” Dr. Connors - “We can do the perpetration procedure right now, and then after your match we can start with the rest.” Lady Freedom - “Then let’s do it.” Lady Freedom is not that worried about the super power thing. She is really concerned on how’ll she’ll be accepted by men when she wrestles. She has the ability, and size to keep up, but will it be enough? FTB) [the thread's next post — 12 Jan 2004] Dr. Reed Connors flicked a light back and forth from Lady Freedom’s left eye to her right. He watched carefully for abnormal responses, but found everything was as it should be. Reed- How are you feeling? Lady Freedom- Fine, so far. Reed- Nothing strange or out of the ordinary? Lady Freedom- Not really. I feel a bit stronger than I use to be, but nothing ‘NexGen’ style. Reed- Well, from what I can tell from my tests, your NexGen Mutations are taking effect. Within the next few days you should be noticing them manifesting. Lady Freedom chuckled. Lady Freedom- Great. Right in the middle of my match with Powermaster I’ll hulk out or something and freak everyone out. The fans will love this one. Reed- I don’t think it will be anything like that. Your new mutations should manifest themselves before the show. Lady Freedom- And if they don’t? Reed- I can design an inhibitor that will keep them at bay until it’s safe to do otherwise. If it comes down to match time, you can slip it on and it will negate your NexGen abilities as long as you have it on. Once your matches are over, then you can take it off. I would suggest, however, to wait until you are alone and not inside a china shop. Lady Freedom- I know that was a joke, but I didn’t get it. Reed- Only partially a joke. You see, the device will suppress your abilities, kind of like keeping a gas from escaping a bottle by putting a cork in the top. However, the gas will continue to mount, and when the cork is removed, the gas will escape at a greater velocity. Lady Freedom- So when I take the band or whatever off, my new abilities could level everything? Reed- Depending on the manifestation of them, yes. You gain heat beam eyes, you could burn down the city due to the build up. Super strength, you could cause an earthquake just by taking a step. Banshee voice, just with a whisper you could– Lady Freedom- I get the idea, Reed. It would be bad. Reed- To say the least. Lady Freedom- When can you have it ready? Reed- I’ll start it as soon as you leave. I should be able to have it finished by Saturday morning. Lady Freedom- That’s cutting it awfully close. Reed- Cutting it close would be me showing up just before your match and handing it to you. There will be plenty of time. Lady Freedom- Whatever you say, Reed. You’re the genius. Reed- So the greatest minds in the world keep telling me. Anyway, you have nothing to be concerned over, Lady Freedom. Sunday morning, should your abilities not manifest, you’ll have your inhibitor. Lady Freedom- Let’s just hope I don’t need it. She walks over to the other side of the room, worried a little that she might hulk out during her match against Powermaster. Wouldn’t it be cool!
