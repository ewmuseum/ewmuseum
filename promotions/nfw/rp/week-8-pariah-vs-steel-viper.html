WEEK 8: Pariah vs. Steel Viper

19 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STEEL VIPER: I’ve been trying to remain calm, but the truth is I am boiling over with anger. Because for some reason the suits in NFW decided to screw me over by giving me a deadbeat for a tag partner. And then after that, the man no-shows…so I am stuck in the match two on one. But for some reason, I don’t understand, the suits decided that this match wasn’t even worthy of taking place. They wouldn’t give me another partner, they just decided that all my hard work and how I have been loyal to NFW for years, meant absolutely nothing! So whomever decided to screw me over back there…F*CK YOU! I’m starting up a sh*t-list and everyone on that list is gonna get hurt! Jeff Garvin, consider yourself added. Maybe you have me confused for someone else, I don’t know. But I ain’t gonna let this slide. In fact, I feel like making an example out of someone and this week they have me paired against one of these temp guys. I call them temps because they weren’t here from jump. They just got plugged in as the season went on. And the temp this week is a man by the name of Pariah. I can honestly say that I know nothing about you. I know you’re a sneaky bastard…I watched what you did last week in your tag match, however the circumstances are different now. Why is that? Because the odds always change when Viper is added into the equation! You’re a tortured soul. You have no friends. You trust no one. Pariah, that sounds about half the guys in wrestling today. What sets you apart from them? What makes you so damn special? What? What’s that? Oh yeah, exactly what I thought I heard…NOTHING! You’re no different than Jeff Garvin or Lady Freedom, you’re a nobody and I am going to expose you to the world as the fraud that you are. I’m a little pissed off, if you can’t tell! I’m sick of not being taken seriously and if I need to go out and put somebody in ICU…then so be it. Pariah, you just happen to be the next guy I face and I would hate to be in your shoes. Of course, you can always say how none of this will ever happen…but we all know better. Got a problem with that? Tough…deal with it. [the thread's next post — 21 Dec 2003] Everyone has his own specific vocation or mission in life; everyone must carry out a concrete assignment that demands fulfillment. Therein he cannot be replaced, nor can his life be repeated, thus, everyone's task is unique as his specific opportunity to implement it. - Viktor Frankl --------------- (Fade in on the abandoned room that Pariah has made his own. Its interior is quite dark, save for the flickering television across the room from him. The lighting from it illuminates his features, giving him a particularly haunting look. The camera pans over to see what is playing on the screen, but finds only snow. It pans back over to him just as he begins to speak.) P: Viper, you talk of making a list and hurting everyone whose name appears upon it. Perhaps you should check it twice, much like the jolly iconic character associated with the season is wont to do. Then, instead of worrying about Jeff Garvin and him screwing you over… worry about what I’m going to do to you. You say you know nothing about me, but then contradict yourself by saying that you know I’m a sneaky bastard. Which is it? You can’t have it both ways. Either you know something about me or you don’t. If your presence changes things as much as you claim, I’ll be highly surprised. It seems to me that your mind is elsewhere. Not that I mind since it only makes my task that much easier to perform. Don’t worry though. I’m not out to screw you, as you’ve so eloquently put it. I’m just going to humiliate you by making you my latest victim. (Pariah stands up and looks down at the camera.) P: You want to know what makes me different, Viper? My pain makes me different. My drive to show everyone that I am every bit the threat that I say I am. Your blowing me off as just another tortured soul will be your undoing because I assure you that I am quite different from anyone else in NFW. Once you step into the ring with me, you’ll see just how wrong you are about me. Soon enough you’ll be gasping for that last sweet breath in an attempt to remain conscious but you won’t find it. You want to know what makes me different, Viper? I’ll show you. I’ll show you more than you ever wanted to know. It won’t be me who wakes up in ICU, oh no; that particular pleasure will go to you. Consider it a Crashmas present… from me to you. Happy holidays… (Fade to black.) [the thread's next post — 23 Dec 2003] (FADEIN to Steel Viper bench pressing weight inside his dojo. He finishes his reps, sits up, and grabs a towel hanging off the front of the bench. The camera zooms in on his face, eyes shut and head lowered. He takes a deep breath and slowly lets it out. He rolls his neck around and opens his eyes, mean mugging the camera.) STEEL VIPER: Pariah, allow me to start off by saying thanks. Not only for you wishing me a happy holiday, but for also letting me know that my opponent this week is actually going to show. For a minute there I thought NFW was going to screw me out of the chance to add more points to my total. They screwed me last week and I’ll be damned if they do it two weeks in a row! I was ready for the match…ready to fight, and then I get the word that they awarded the points to the other team…without me even getting a chance to earn it. Do you think I’m cool with that? Do you think this is sitting well with me? (Places his hand on his chin.) Hmmm…let me think about that. (Removes his hand.) HELL NO! So basically you are looking at one mean sonuva***** that is PISSED OFF! And I don’t give a damn who is across that ring this week…I’m going out to win. And I’m gonna tear apart whatever is in my path! (Pauses) Your pain makes you different? (Shakes his head with a look on his face, as if it’s ridiculous.) Well Pariah, I can honestly say that after this match that statement will mean more than you know! Because I’m a nice guy and I’m willing to contribute to the cause. You want pain? Well pal, I got more than enough for you to handle! So take your little Crashmas present and shove it up you’re a$$! If I was overlooking you before, my bad. Believe me, you got my attention now. Anyone that runs his mouth and tries to say he’s gonna put me in check…I gotta test. You want to be a threat? Well I’m just like the government…I don’t tolerate them. And in the event one arises…I have no choice but to take the appropriate actions…and ELIMINATE IT! (Viper stands up and walks over to a dry erase board with something written on it. The camera zooms in on the board, labeled at the top in big black letters “SH*T LIST”. Beneath it are the names of several NFW wrestlers…Jeff Garvin, Pariah, Cameron Cruise, Jean Rabesque…but before the camera can finish panning down, Viper interrupts and pulls the camera up to his face.) None of this list matters right now. I’m wiping the slate clean and putting the only name that matters this week. The man that says he is going to humiliate me and make me his next victim. (Grins.) Damn, sounds a lot like me. (Shakes his head.) You say that to me, you better damn well mean it and be ready to back it up. You better be prepared to do it…because you can bet that I am! (Viper uses his towel and wipes the board clean. He takes a black marker and writes the name “Pariah” on the board. He puts the marker down and turns to the camera.) No need to check the list twice…only one name on there now. And soon that same name is gonna find its way onto another list…Injured Reserve. (Viper mean mugs the camera as the scene FADES TO BLACK.)
