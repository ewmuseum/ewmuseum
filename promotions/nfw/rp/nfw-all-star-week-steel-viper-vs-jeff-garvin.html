NFW All-Star Week: Steel Viper vs. Jeff Garvin

14 Jan 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STEEL VIPER: I don’t know what to say, I am almost speechless that NFW would sign this bout so late into All-Star Week. Jeff Garvin, your first match here you showed me something. You actually were able to hang in that ring with me until it was ruled a time limit draw. Do you know why it lasted that long? Because I didn’t know much about you…I was just testing the waters…when I should have immediately WENT IN FOR THE KILL! For a second I actually had a small amount of respect for you…but I wish I never had. You left me high and dry in a tag match. I even went as far as to back you up and somewhat defend you when our opponents were talking trash. But you had more important things to do, I guess. And because of that, I was ripped off a chance for points because the suits forced me to forfeit the bout. I wanted to fight anyway…but it didn’t happen. Who do I blame for that? YOU! (Viper calms himself down by rolling his neck slowly and taking a deep breath.) Garvin, you had your chance to make amends and you didn’t. You could have attempted an apology…but I didn’t hear anything. So I guess that leaves me with one final option…F(BLEEP)IN’ YOU UP! I’m gonna send you packin’ out of NFW and back to that cruiserweight fed from which you came. You never should have messed with me and now it’s too late…nothing can save your sorry ass! Don’t like it, tough...deal with it. [the thread's next post — 19 Jan 2004] A visual slowly becomes clear, and it is the sour grin of an undefeated champion who had been forced from the promotion that he crawled, month by month, to the top of. Pacing back and forth across the living room floor of his home in Memphis is Jeff Garvin, hands firmly grasping his hips, seething between clenched teeth. He is clad in a beige sweater- sleeves rolled halfway up the length of his forearm- and dark blue jeans. It is hard to judge how long he has been wearing through the plush carpet flooring, but as the camera focused, perspiration had begun to form and sting the corner of his eyes. After quickly and anxiously running his finger tips through his hair, he fixed his eyes on the camera- staring through it to his opponent. Steel Viper. A veteran and backstage leader who had earned the fans’ respect over his many years in the sport of professional wrestling. “You know,” he said, pausing for a moment as he gestured with his hand, “I have fought many great wrestlers during the two years I have laced boots. Some I have beaten and some I haven’t. As much as a tie doesn’t sit well with me, as much as I would like to give the fans a clear-cut winner each time I walk down the aisle to compete… I would have liked more to simply forget about you, Viper. I would have liked to have placed our match in a tiny box and buried it in the deepest hole I could find. You can cry about how I left you without a tag team partner several weeks back, but you know what? I really couldn’t careless. See… NFW has talent within its ranks, that I will admit, but it’s still only a side project for me.” Ingenuously, he shrugged as if it were common knowledge worldwide. “It's a decent promotion, no mistaking that, but decent is all it ever will be. Especially with guys like you fumbling about on its shows in a dumbfounded state. I looked up the NFW record books and I spoke to a few of the loyal NFW followers, as ignorant and as foul smelling as they may be, and found out quite a bit about your past.” Quick fact- he rarely prepares for interviews. He didn't bother quickly looking over a list he had stuffed in his pocket before the cameras began rolling so to know what points to bring up. He just doesn't give a damn. Knowing this, viewers should not be surprised to see him sluggishly dig in his pocket to produce a paper ball. He unfolded it and draped it over his hand, squinting as he read through what had been scribbled on it. “It says here that you have never held a single world championship title, which is quite pathetic really considering your age and size.” He laughed quietly underneath his breath. “Wrestling is a sport that generally rewards height and weight rather than talent. Yet, besides being a hoss and having no talent to speak of, a recipe for success in this business, you haven’t won a single top-tier championship. As difficult as failure must be in your situation, you somehow managed to pull it off! Good job!” To congratulate him on accomplishing a big load of nothing, Garvin offered an enthusiastic thumbs up, and if Steel Viper were there with him in the room, most likely a pat on the back too because he’s just that nice. Actually, no… he would probably throw himself through the closest window he could find and take off in whatever direction he was facing when he got to his feet. “And Viper,” Jeff said, his tirade coming to a close, “I’m sure when our time comes, and I finish throwing you from corner post to corner post, and dropping you on your head, you won’t let the viewing public go home without giving them what they all paid to see… You… in my patented Hammer Jammer submission hold, pounding your sweaty palm on the canvas in defeat. The bell will ring and I will be declared the victor, having proved that you are without a doubt the biggest waist of NFW’s money to ever be flushed down the proverbial toilet.” Smirking, he promptly walked out of view, and shortly there after the connection was cut off. [the thread's next post — 20 Jan 2004] (FADE IN to Steel Viper standing in from of a NFW All Star Week backdrop. He is rubbing his hands methodically and staring intently into the camera. He takes a deep breath and begins to speak.) STEEL VIPER: So he finally shows his face. After what seems to have been months, Jeff Garvin finally grew the balls to show his face on national television. Oh yeah, its not that he’s scared. It’s the fact that New Frontier is just “decent” and he spends his time elsewhere. Uh huh. (Shakes his head.) Where is that elsewhere, Jeff? The Land of Make-believe? Is it in Stamford, Connecticut? Do you have an evil nemesis there with the nickname “Hammer”? Thus the name of your finisher, the Hammer Jammer? Or do you just like to steal identities of retired wrestlers and try to make them your own? The monogram towel…the Garvin Stomp…I haven’t seen it yet, but I bet you wear a damn shin-guard to the ring. Just pathetic. And you have the audacity to take cheap shots at me, because a sheet of paper told you that I was never a World champion? Garvin, maybe if I weighed about 220 and was in a fed full of cruiserweights, maybe I would reign supreme…like you do wherever you are from. But in NFW, you aren’t gonna get the same easy ride. And as far as your little claim, you might want to do your own research in the future, instead of paying off someone to do it for you. You want the lowdown on my resume’? You want to know what titles I won? Is that the only way you measure competition? (Shakes his head.) The fact of the matter is, that in 1995 I held a World championship in a company called SWA. It wasn’t for long because the company folded after a few shows, so I didn’t feel the need to let that be known to everyone, but now that you brought it up…there is the truth. So take your little sheet of paper, wad it up, and stick it up your ass! Then again, maybe you shouldn’t stick it up there…you might enjoy it too much. (Viper means mugs the camera and clinches his fists.) You call your run here in New Frontier a side project? That’s great that you have such regard for the Ultratitle, that it’s just a “side project”. Something to do in between weigh-ins for your featherweight World title, I guess. Your heart isn’t into this, I can tell by your actions and the words that come out of your mouth. So why are you here? What’s your purpose? Because you haven’t done jacks(BLEEP) since you stepped into that door. I was easy on you…I gave you a break. I won’t make the same mistake twice. You wanna drop me on my head? You say it…you better damn well do it. I don’t take threats like that too kindly…I tend to take things like that personal. So keep it up, keep digging your own hole…and at All Star Week…I’ll bury you in it! That tag match might not have meant anything to you, but I take the Ultratitle very seriously. It’s crucial to get into the playoffs, every point counts. We had a tag match, whether we like each other or not, and I expected you to be there. But you weren’t. I guess the pressure got to you and you no-showed. Our opponents were awarded the points and what pisses me off more than that…is that one of them was Jean Rabesque. I hate that little s(BLEEP). But you were too busy, huh? What? Being stuck up Ron Garvin’s ass? You f(BLEEP)ed with the wrong man and now you’ve gotta pay! I’ve messed around with you for far too long, it’s time for me to close this chapter and more onto the next. You’ve wasted my time, my points, and my patience. Basically your time is up. I’m fed up with your s(BLEEP) and now its time for some payback. You’re the next name for me to check off of my S(BLEEP) List and I’ve got another half of the season to take care of the rest. (Cracks his knuckles and slowly rolls his neck.) Don’t like it? Tough…deal with it. (FADE TO BLACK)
