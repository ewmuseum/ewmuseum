WEEK 11: LINDSAY TROY vs. EDDY LOVE

15 Apr 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OORP: This was supposed to have happened prior to the announcement of our match but I was slack. LT wanted this in general RP, but since I was posting it I overruled her and put it in out thread. ************************************************** ** While Eddy still loved the business and most of what went with it, compulsory autograph sessions were not his idea of innovative promotion. Still he’d signed on with a company that was fairly new to the national scene and while in his heart of hearts he knew that his mere presence on the roster would fill the arenas, he also wasn’t ready to buck management until he’d gotten what he wanted from them. So he sat in a dingy arena signing autographs for his one hour counting seconds like a POW counts months and waiting for relief, while justifying to himself that this would lead him to where he’d wanted to be for 8 years. It wasn’t so much the result that he wanted, in fact the result had been presented to him on two other occasions. Once when Deacon was tied up for months in China and again when Deacon's health issues has arisen. To Eddy though, it was only meaningful if he himself pushed Chris Shepherd’s career into the trash bin he’d always thought it belonged. (Eddy sits subdued wearing a “Jack Leggett Baseball Camp” T-Shirt and a pair of Nike warm ups. While an aura of stardom can always be sensed around Eddy, in this setting he seems to not notice it himself. He is calm, almost lazy as he finishes up his time in this bush league autograph session. Eddy signs one and never looks up as the next person approaches) EL: So, how do you want me to make it out?? “How about like this: ‘I’m Calvin’s Momma’s sheep and when she pokes her crook up my ass I baa and bleat to my omniscient master’.” (Love snickers then looks up to find Lindsay Troy, who is next on the list of NFW stars signing today. Eddy has been desperately ready for relief, but now that relief has come it’s the furthest thing from his mind. Eddy notes to himself that Troy is attractive, but what is really striking is how tall she seems with him seated at the table. He thinks to himself “WOW, you are a tall cool drink of Water”) LT: What?? What did you say?? (Love realizes he’s spoken his thought aloud, but cares very little. Eddy has seen taller women, but never that held their femininity the way that this chick has. Is that what makes Melton so nuts over this Betty, the fact that the sexual All-American has never knocked headboards with a girl whose head is in the bed while her feet are in the hall. Eddy thinks maybe she’s hotter than he’d given her credit for, but quickly remembers that Melton is essential to Momma Carleton’s plan and any move on Troy would certainly end up with her sleeping over and create a huge mess. Even at 10 foot tall, he realizes they’re still a dime a dozen.) EL: Oh, Troy, thank goodness. (Eddy starts packing his things up to knock off) I’ve signed for every 12 year Louisiana redneck while they tell me I’m a disgrace to the South and every little bible thumping weirdo explaining how I’ll burn in hell for being mean to Chris Shepherd. Now maybe they can actually pester someone who needs to sign their two dollar magazine. LT: I don’t need anything. I’m here because I want to be here; maybe if you had an ounce of humility in you you’d realize that. (Eddy is caught off guard, but remains very calm.) EL: Look Troy, I didn’t mean anything by it. Settle down….. what’s up, that cum dumpster comment still got you steamed? LT: Screw you Eddy, you’re the closest thing to a cum dumpster here. If you’re not plugging Calvin’s Momma, you’ve got Calvin on your jock. EL: Whatever Lindsay, you know it’s already been the longest hour of my life. I sure as hell ain’t got the time or patience for some giant hoochie momma that forgot to take her Prozac today. Don’t be so bush league, we’re all on the same team here. Me calling you a cum dumpster put 100 more people in the stands carrying signs with your name. You should be thanking me. The only bad thing Eddy Love can say on TV for your career is nothing at all. LT: Whether you realize it or not, we’re on two different sides of the ball. Just because we’re in the same conference doesn’t mean we’re on the same team. While you were sitting on your butt in South Carolina I was out paving my own way, winning titles and bringing fans along with me. I sure as hell don’t need Calvin’s gimp heading up any recruiting mission for me. So do yourself a favor and don’t do any for me. EL: Yea, you're quite the Super Nova in the wrestling world, if only they'd ever love me like they do you, Lindsay. I wasn't on my ass in South Carolina. I was on the Love Boat in the Keys having more fun per hour than you'll have per life time, but I digress, teaching a woman to reason is like teaching a fish calculus. Look, if you really are on Joey’s side you should be singing my praises not be paranoid that Calvin’s gonna steal his heart. You should be thanking me for the way things have gone for Joey since I arrived, I mean if someone does force him to decide to make a professional choice you don’t think Joey’s gonna figure out that while I’ve been fighting guys in the parking lot to make his life easier, you’ve been pouting that someone else is actually showing an interest in getting his old ass over. LT: Oh please, Joey’s not any more thrilled that you’re back than I am. He’s humoring you because he has to. He was doing fine before you showed up, not to mention it was Joey Melton who wanted me for his manager in the first place. I’ve done more for him than you or Carlton, and I’m pretty sure he knows it. Calvin’s the least of my worries; he’s just a little kid stuck in a prepubescent body. You’re just another guy with an agenda that’s only going to hinder Joey when push comes to shove-don’t think I don’t see it, Eddy. I’m not blind. EL: You see what you wanna see, sugar. I hand picked Joey because I promised Momma Carlton the Ultra Title would come to Calvin’s division. Joey at 70 would have a better chance than the list of clowns that make up the South. I’m sure Joey is sure he’s still a better man than myself, just like I’m sure he never was. If you had any sense at all you’d be begging me to keep up the good work not acting like a jealous freak about the very one who has played a hand in three huge victories for your man……. Or maybe he's not your man, is that the problem between us that you were gonna throw him down the river on behalf of Dan Ryan all along and with Hurricane Eddy looking after Joey, you know you won’t get the chance. LT: I don’t beg, Eddy. I don’t plead, and I’m sure as hell not going to verbally put you over because you’re just another guy with a God complex. Your misperceptions and misconstruings, as amusing as they are, wear on my patience. Joey’s his own person and he can make his own decisions, but if he’s smart he knows that the minute he says goodbye to me is the minute that the roof over his head disappears, as well as the clothes on his back, the investments in stock that are helping get back some of the money his ex wife froze, the show accommodations, and the cushy lifestyle that he’s living. He’s got all that because of me, and if it wasn’t for me he’d be out on the street because you and I both know that Calvin and his Momma don’t look too favorably on the broke, downtrodden, and poor as much as they would have you believe otherwise. I’m a person of my word, and I always keep it. I’m not selling Joey down the river because of some “master plan” that Dan and I supposedly have. I’m doing my job, and my job is to get Joey to the finals of the Ultratitle Tourney. I can’t win it for him, but I can get him to that last match. That’s what I plan on doing Eddy, and if I have to step over you then so be it. But you had nothing to do with Joey’s victory over Rabesquedor, you had nothing to do with Joey’s victory over Cruise, you had nothing to do with Joey’s and your boy Hornet’s victory over Southern and Matthews. So let’s just stop living in Fantasy Land. You may have come back at All Star Week, but you didn’t make that cage explode. All you did was take advantage of the situation. I’m pretty sure that’s what you’re good at…taking advantage of things. It was Joey that made Dan tap, not you. Melton’s smart; he can still hack it in the ring, there’s no question about that. So don’t take credit for his work. (Extended silence as the 2 look at each other) EL: Did you say something, I kind of dozed off there after you said something about begging Eddy. Look toots, I’m off the clock and you bore me with your “***** is back” attitude. You just make sure if you’re around that you’re there to help Joey, cause the first time I sense you’re not an asset….. Well I am a Southern Gentleman, but right now it’s all about Joey and if I need to put a *cough* lady in her place, I’m not above doing it. LT: You wouldn’t be the first man that’s tried, and you wouldn’t be the first man that’s failed. And for the record, the ***** never left. (Eddy begins walking off as Troy takes her place at the signing table. Eddy looks at the line speaking as if one of the fans has addressed him, but it appears he's just talking out loud one last shot at getting under Troy's skin.) EL: No sorry, son, they'll only let me stay for an hour, but that's Lindsay Troy, She's a Biiiiig star in the wrestling business. [the thread's next post — 20 Apr 2004] Most assuredly, Karma is indeed a *****. And I'm not talking about the less-than-stellar fWo stable either. Fade-in to Lindsay Troy, leaning back against a wall with her arms folded in front of her. It's no secret that my brashness and overzealous nature has gotten me into trouble on more than one occassion, but I didn't expect trouble to come knocking on my doorstep so soon. I called out California's Most Spoiled Child one week and the next I find myself being forced to fight under Prosser's Iron Curtain with my opponent being the Poster Child for Kaleden Antique Sterling Silver teaspoons. Oh wait. I'm not facing Calvin Carlton. My mistake. At least I got the antique part of the above equation correct. Smirk. Eddy, you and I don't see eye to eye on various topics, and this match is my pennance for opening my mouth. I don't want to fight for Prosser and his North, and up in Lowell Avery's figuring that he's got me right where he wants me. But I don't jump through hoops that easily. I'm Damned if I do, Damned if I don't. Damned if I win, because then the roster trucks up North to the Seven Deadly Sins' playground. Damned if I lose, because then Calvin gets the bragging rights and I get an ego check. A spoonful of sugar helps the medicine go down, but it doesn't make pride any easier to swallow. I'm not about to sacrifice this match so the roster stays in the South and the attacks by the SDS are prolonged. I put myself into this position, and I'm going to wrestle my way out. And if I lose, I lose...but I'll have lost with a somewhat clear conscience. Besides, it's my first night at Fight Club. And if it's your first night at Fight Club, you have to fight. Fade...
