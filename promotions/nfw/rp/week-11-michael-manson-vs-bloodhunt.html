WEEK 11: MICHAEL MANSON vs. BLOODHUNT

8 Apr 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((FADEIN: Patron of the Arts, NFW EASTERN CONFERENCE SPOKESPERSON MICHAEL MANSON in biker-styled leather jacket and a "Punisher" t-shirt and leather pants sitting on the bent and cracked hood of the Adam West Batmobile.)) MANSON(tapping a dent): Wasn't so very long ago, not much more than a year, that I would wake up and have to drag myself my arms like a devolved ape and get into a wheelchair and of course, wheel myself around, always needing help just to get around in a bathroom. I had to sit back and watch everyone else week in and out wrestle, fall off cages, curse each other out....and it got to the point where I had to hand someone else the NFW title. And I should add to my own credit, I did so with a smile on my face. Being commissioner was nice, but it wasn't enough, not really, after all, what would be enough? So in-between planning and booking this beast called the NFW, I worked and I sacrificed (to all the gods and devils and believe you me, I know many), I read all the books on Zen enlightenment, and I took everything I knew about pain and I had to redirect it, reinterpret it, and I conquered it. I was always good with pain. And I came back....to the old NFW...and I helped build the new NFW. The time came that I had to change the firmament again and I launched the NFW East...my own conference...commentators....competitors...and chairman. I can have anything and anyone I want there. Still.....it's not enough. Because there are times when I feel the cold in my back, because I know I was next in line to challenge for that NFW title no matter who held it....no matter what position I held.....because inside I knew that Bloodhunt had gotten off too easily....that the Lone Gunman had hit his target..but he never finished the job. He never fully crippled me, never destroyed me. Because no one can. It's the principe of the thing. Even with the Ultratitle looming, there's still that one thing that gnaws at the black pit that accounts for my soul..that it would never be complete....never enough...unless all my detractors and enemies were brought to heel. I know Bloodhunt stayed away..disgusted with the new NFW system..the management..how his title was treated....and how he must have raged at the very concept of my NFW East. He once described me as something akin to a mountain of rage that needed direction. Which was now him. So that was why I went out and put a hit on myself. Not Avery Prosser, Not Calvin Carlton, Not Quentin Sullivan, Not Shane Southern....Not Craig Miles....or anyone else. No one put a hit out on me, no one else would be able to. The time has come.. ((MANSON begins slapping the dents his head made hard.)) For the assassin to meet the master. Bloodhunt couldn't kill me, but the attempt was an insult. And my continued lie and career is an insult to him....all it took was the brilliance of a madman to bring us together again... ...to finish things. And still that won't be enough. When I get to the Ultratitle, I may have to check again though.
