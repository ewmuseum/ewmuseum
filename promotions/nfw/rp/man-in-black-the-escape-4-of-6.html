Man In Black : The Escape (#4 Of 6)

23 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Three men pile into a jeep outside the warehouse, all three seemingly wearing dark suits - but hey! it's past midnight at this point, so everything looks dark. One of them is getting a pretty large lump on his head, and he's got a pretty big frown on his mug as well. "I swear, when I get my hands on that punk, I'm gonna put HIS head through a couple walls," explains Leo, the guy with the lump. "Leo," replies Vinnie, apparently the boss at this point, "You do whatever you want to him. But he comes back alive. Got it, you two?" No response. "GOT IT?" "Yeah, Vinnie," the other two men riding in the jeep reply. "The Boss would be seriously displeased should he not be able to take care of this ... mess ... himself, right?" "Yeah, Vinnie," comes the same reply. The jeep is bouncing through a cornfield somewhere in the middle of nowhere, following a path left by the man these three are pursuing. Little do they know, it's a path he meant for them to follow. "Guy's sure stupid," muses Joe the Sage, "He's running clear away from any highways." "Well, you know, months locked in a room mighta scrambled his brains more'n Leo's are right now." All three men share a hearty laugh at Vinnie's quip ... playing their flashlights about the field. After the laugh, all three are silent, dwelling on their own private reasons for wanting to catch up to the escapee - embarassment, personal injury, and doing the job for The Boss, and the only sound is the running of the jeep's engine through the night. And then, on the horizon ... a structure that looks like an old barn is beggining to form, still miles away ...
