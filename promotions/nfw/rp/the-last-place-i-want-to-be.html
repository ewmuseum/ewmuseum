The Last Place I Want To Be...

27 Oct 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fade-in: A hospital room in the greater Lowell area. Lindsay Troy sits propped up in her bed, a bandage around her forehead, and a scowl on her face. On the wall-mounted TV she watches a tape of CRASH TV, most specifically the Joey Melton/Shawn Matthews match. The door to her room opens, and her sister Alaina slips into the room, her coat over her shoulder, and an exasperated look on her face. Alaina: Can't you go one week without getting hurt or winding up in the hospital? Lindsay: Trust me, if I had it my way, I wouldn't be here. Lindsay folds her arms over her chest as Alaina crosses the room and pulls a chair up next to the bed. She eyes the television screen as Melton carries Troy up the ramp, and the referee raises Matthews hand in the air. Troy shuts the TV off and fired the remote against the wall. It smashes into pieces, and Troy seethes. Alaina, not one to cross her sister when she's in a raging fury, calmly places her hand on Lindsay's right knee, and speaks with a quiet demeanor. Alaina: Any idea who it could be? Lindsay: (sighing) Could be any number of people. Promo'd be a logical choice, but he and I haven't crossed paths yet. I'm sure he has his eye on my A1E Cyber Title, and any way he can soften me up he'll take the chance. Could be Vlachinko, he and I both know that we have unfinished business to settle. It's only a matter of time before we meet again, and we settle the matter once and for all. Hell, it could even be the accountant who I refused to give your cell number to because he was a sketchball... Alaina: Yeah, he was creepy. Why'd you hire him anyway? Lindsay: You're the one who hired him, because you liked his eyes. Alaina: Oh. Yeah... Lindsay: For all I know, it could be no one I've ever even associated with. Could be someone out to get Melton, and used me as target practice. But the point is, whether it was someone I know, or someone I don't know, I will find out who it is, and they will pay. Goddamn a$$hole doesn't put me in a hospital and then expect to get away scot free. Not as long as I'm breathing. I can't wait to get the hell out of here though. I hate these places. Alaina: I remember the last time you were in a hospital... Lindsay: (scowling) I'd rather not talk about it, thanks. Alaina quickly shut her mouth. Stirring up memories of past times wouldn't be appropriate at this juncture. Alaina: (her voice trailing off) At least Melton was there... Lindsay snapped her head to her sister, fury dancing in her eyes. Lindsay: Oh yeah, I was real goddamn lucky to have Joey Melton there. Hell must have frozen over because Joey Melton thought about someone other than himself for once. So what, am I now somehow indebted to him?! Alaina: I didn't say that... Lindsay: Because I'm not. He's a real piece of work. Tricks me into managing him and then p*ssyfoots and halfasses it because he's too busy thinking about himself than to do the job that he's paid to do. And where does that leave me? I get made to look bad, and that's the last thing that I want. I may have said that I'd manage him, but I never said for how long. Alaina: Lindsay, you're overreacting... Lindsay: Am I? I'm sitting here in a f*cking hospital bed, and where the hell is he? You know what? I don't even care where he is, because it's becoming blatantly apparant that he'll never show compassion for another human being whose name isn't Joey Melton or Calvin Carlton. I go out there week after week and put up with his bullsh*t and where does it get me? Laid up in Lowell with a piece of cotton around my head and stitches in my skin. Well I'm sick of bending over backwards for Mr. Joseph "I'm so goddamn high and mighty" Melton. I'm not sticking my neck out for him anymore until he proves to me that he's capable of caring for someone other than himself. Alaina: Lindz... Lindsay: No. That's it, Alaina. I've had it. Go talk to the doctor and see what you can do about getting me out of here. I've got better things to do than stew about what happened on Crash.
