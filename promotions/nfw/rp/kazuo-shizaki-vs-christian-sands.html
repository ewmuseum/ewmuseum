Kazuo Shizaki vs. Christian Sands

11 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STIPULATION: None [the thread's next post — 12 Feb 2004] (FADEIN: The study of the Sands Estate, dim as usual. Adjusting the collar of his dark Armani suit, CHRISTIAN SANDS turns his high-backed chair to face the camera. Sitting on the desk before him are two items - a vase of roses and a stack of tapes.) Sands: Seems we're living in momentous times for New Frontier Wrestling. The North is under new management... the South is now home to Eddy Love... and Miles took his ball and went back to Greensboro to wait a few years for the next show to hit the air. Big deal. It's none of my concern, really. Unless some of these people decide to get involved in my matches, I'm not concerned. It's almost amusing, really, to see people like Dan Ryan and Jon Marx sit there and swear to get revenge on Prosser and his gang. So much anger. I, on the other hand, will simply continue doing what I've been doing all alone - wrestling. And WINNING. At All-Star Week, I took Shawn Hart's ego and crushed it into a fine powder within the space of seven seconds. I didn't even need to resort to a finishing move. And while le Phenom stood in the ring screaming about how life isn't fair, I took his fifteen points and vaulted into the thick of the playoff race - something nobody expected me to do, seeing as I DID come in six weeks into the season and thirty to forty points behind the leaders. They laughed when I said I would bridge the points gap and contend for the UltraTitle. Who's laughing now? But the journey isn't over yet. I have before me the opportunity to take a huge step forward in my journey to the UltraTitle - in fact, to jump to second or even first place in the division. I'll give you your due, Shizaki. You've done well for yourself here in NFW and scored some impressive wins. Nevermind the fact that between the gi and the mask, you look like something out of Dragonball Z - you're actually a contender, as opposed to people like Marx who make their fame by stomping lower-tier performers and reaping the rewards. Good. It's been awhile since I've actually tested my mettle against someone worth wrestling. I like a good challenge. Since my first professional match I've built my career on defying the odds, and in the process of doing that I've risen to levels many people never attain in their lifetime. Two years ago my name was unknown on this circuit. Now I'm touted as one of the best and brightest in this sport, going head-to-head with the best in the world and winning. It's true, Shizaki - I am the future, and I prove it every time I lace up the boots and step between those ropes to face the latest challenge. Thus far I have overcome EVERY challenge facing me in NFW. The momentum behind me right now is absolutely overwhelming, and when utilized in conjunction with my drive to succeed and my unfathomable skill, it's going to push me to heights most people will never see. And in the process, they'll push me to a victory here in Week Nine, bringing me that much closer to claiming the UltraTitle and fulfilling my destiny of becoming the BEST in the world. I'm afraid your black rose isn't going to stop me, Shizaki. Hell... if you want to, plant a whole garden of 'em in front of me. I'll happily stomp right through 'em. Because I am, indeed, the future. And without a doubt, the future is now. (FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 13 Feb 2004] (FADEIN to a tightly framed shot of a single red rose in a glass case. The camera slowly rotates around the delicate flower, exhibiting the long, thorn-covered stem and the red petals wrapped up around the bud.) SHIZAKI (V/O): "The rose... perhaps the ultimate symbol of man's juxtaposition and duality. At once so beautiful and delicate, and yet its touch can draw blood from those who do not tread carefully in its presence." (The camera slowly pans out now, and we see that we are in a small room with thick wooden walls... Kazuo Shizaki is sitting in the lotus position before the display case that holds the rose.) SHIZAKI: "I find it amusing, Mr. Sands, that you tell me to plant an entire garden of roses if it pleases me. Even if I were to have forgotten that Mr. Ryan uttered those very same words before he stepped into the ring before me, the humor would not be lost. "For you, Mr. Sands... planting a garden seems insignificant. Yet I find that it gives me great comfort... to know that I am part of the symbiotic chain of life. That I am more than just a man whose ego must constantly be fed. "You are right in certain assumptions just as you are wrong in others. You are, indeed, a rising star, so to speak. Yet you go so far as to imagine your own victory over me. You would be well served not to get so far ahead of yourself, Mr. Sands. "Though you may take pride in what you have accomplished thus far, your road to the Ultratitle, like so many others, is long and treacherous. And you are not facing the same broken Shawn Hart whom you defeated last. Remember these things, Mr. Sands, for if you ignore them, you will surely pay the price. "You make the claim that Mr. Prosser and his Seven Deadly Sins are not your problem. Again, you are wrong. In a situation as complex as ours, you would do well not to ignore any factors that may hinder or further your efforts. Ignorance is not bliss here; indeed, it will be your undoing if you allow it. "And though your praise for me may be well-earned, I find no need for it. I am not here to prove to you or anyone else what I am capable of. I have a job to do and I intend to do it, whether or not Mr. Prosser deems it fit. "At Crash TV, Mr. Sands... you will not face a broken Shawn Hart, nor will you face Jeff Garvin or some other lost soul in this sea of power and will. "You will face the Black Rose. And though you may not realize it, there is no future to speak of. There is only the now. This moment in time, trapped forever in an eternity of before and after that we cannot see or touch. "You may dismiss me as you please, Mr. Sands. Wave your hand and bid me farewell with your clever garden analogies. It matters not. "The rose's beauty is not affected by your indifference. "Nor am I." (FADEOUT.) [the thread's next post — 16 Feb 2004] (FADEIN: An expansive sitting room dominated by a dark leather sofa. SANDS sits on said piece of furniture, looking into the camera with a neutral expression. Situated on a table in front of him is a glass vase with several roses of varying colours emerging from it; it is positioned between SANDS and the camera.) Sands: The rose. A flower that people send to their loved ones on Valentine's Day - which explains this vase. When black, also associated with a Yakuza ritual signifying impending death. The black rose is also proof that Kasumi Shizaki is the third-level boss from some Japanese mob video game. It's funny, really. I got sent a lot of roses this Valentine's Day - mostly from women swooning about how I'm sex on a stick. Red ones, white ones, yellow ones... hell, someone sent me some blue ones, and there was one girl who gave me some that were somewhere between pink and orange. But I've yet to get a black one. What's going on here, Kazuo? No rose for me? I'm hurt. Surely you of all people know to send roses on Valentine's Day! Oh well. But in case you're wondering, I sent you a bushel of blue ones. Should brighten your day a bit. Don't make the mistake, Kazuo, of thinking that I'm dismissing you, because I'm not that shortsighted. If you didn't get the idea from my last promotional segment, my focus is firmly on you. I'm sure you can say the same - unless, of course, you're too busy worrying about Prosser, just like everyone else on the roster. Everyone except me, that is. I come here to wrestle, not play politics. As such, I prepare for the MATCH and waste no time on backstage games. Which, of course, means that rather than dismissing you I'm preparing for whatever you can throw at me... be it a fancy little martial-arts kick or a handful of salt to the face. Speaking of salt to the eyes - it's flattering that you're likening me to Dan Ryan, but really, there's no comparison. Dan's ego tends to get in the way of his good judgment, and it leads him to overlook things sometimes. In contrast, I know enough to leave my ego outside the ropes when I wrestle. As a consummate ring tactician I am aware of everything that goes on around me, INCLUDING a little man fishing around for a handful of salt to throw in my face. I'm also aware of how to deal with said problems in the most effective manner possible. But... hold on... you said I'm not facing the same Shawn Hart as last time? Oh, SH*T! I thought I was! No WONDER you didn't mention your phenomical phizz! Geez, all this time... But seriously. Nice of you to state the obvious, but the day I need a lesson in what I'm up against is the day pigs dance the Lambada. Despite what you may think, I'm not stupid, and I've certainly seen plenty of tape. You seem to think that my assumption of victory is a fault I have - but you're wrong, because it's worked for me for four weeks running. My philosophy is simple. I assume victory to be attainable and don't waver from that, because in this game skill is nothing without the will to win. The will that I have. Or would you rather I hopped on the fence and rambled about how this is going to be such a great match and it doesn't matter who wins? Wrong. It does matter. I can't claim the UltraTitle if I don't win, and I can't claim to be the best in NFW without the UltraTitle. Thus, I will continue to win simply because losing is not an acceptable outcome. There certainly is a future to speak of, Kaz. Because the future is me. And the future is coming straight at you - yes, YOU, Mr. Casual-Indifference. Either move, or get run over. (FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 16 Feb 2004] Originally posted by JABolich swooning about how I'm sex on a stick OORP: you phrase stealing whore! give it back! [the thread's next post — 16 Feb 2004] (FADEIN to the same shrine we have seen many times before, where Kazuo Shizaki sits in the lotus position, his back to the camera, and his head bent down.) SHIZAKI: "Do you know the difference between a wise man and a foolish one, Mr. Sands... ? A wise man understands that his knowledge is limited. He is aware of his own limitations and shortcomings. "The fool, on the other hand, claims to know everything. He speaks with certainty of the future, and even things he cannot control, as if he alone knew what was to come. "Can you imagine, Mr. Sands, which of these categories you belong to?" (He slowly rises and snuffs out a single candle with his fingertips.) SHIZAKI: "Mr. Sands, I have grown tired of your incessant mockery. If you had watched the tapes, you would realize that such petty chicanery is a waste of your precious time. "You speak eloquently of Valentine's Day roses and Japanese video games... you've even made mention of Dragonball Z. "Perhaps these mocking tones worked against the likes of Mr. Garvin, but you will find that they are not so effective against me. Do you think this act is new to me, Mr. Sands, or anyone else? There have been many who came before you who did just the same. "Yet what you continually fail to realize, Mr. Sands, is that your control... your future... is just an illusion. You think that you are the only one focused on Ultratitle? That everyone else has become too wrapped up in politics? "Once again, you have shown your shortsightedness with ease. Should you fail to realize the imminent threat that Mr. Prosser and his associates present, you will pay most dearly. You cannot simply stick your head in the sand and hope that they will ignore you. "You think only in terms of black and white. You misinterpret my words in order to fill that frail ego of yours. I did not imply that I was different than Mr. Hart. I simply implied, Mr. Sands, that any man with a reasonable amount of intelligence could look into Mr. Hart's eyes before he fought you and realize he was a broken man. He had burned out long before, and what you defeated was not a champion, but a shell. "You seem to think that your wins should impress me. And why is that? What have you done that is so impressive? Defeating a broken Shawn Hart? Outwrestling Jeff Garvin? "Do not waste your time giving me a list of your exploits, for I will ignore them. And do not waste your time attempting to stab my pride, for I have none. Unlike you, Mr. Sands, I am here for some reason other than to prove how good I am." (He turns around now, and his dark eyes stare out from behind the black mask with nothing but focus in them.) SHIZAKI: "Forgive me for being so direct, Mr. Sands, but you are but a fool. You think that you will continue to win simply because losing is not an option? Mr. Sands, if you had taken the time to look around you and realize that most of these men have been pouring their hearts and souls into this ring from times long before you ever had your dream, you would be wise not to open your mouth and play the fool again. "Every man wants to win, Mr. Sands, and sometimes, ability and willpower are not enough. Sometimes, our destiny lies not in our own hands, but in the hands of others. Perhaps you will come down with some viral infection. Perhaps you will be in a serious accident. Perhaps I will pay handsomely to several men who would be more than willing to break you in half for the right price. "Look at Mr. Ryan. He had the willpower, the strength, and the desire. Would you argue that he is incapable of defeating me? For he has certainly far surpassed your career or even mine. "And yet, in a moment, all that he had trained for and focused on vanished. With a moment, his destiny was in my hands, and I took that moment and seized it with my will, and I snatched victory from the claws of defeat. "I am not attempting to intimidate or impress you, for I am above such petty arguments. You clearly think you can best me in the ring, and I have no interest in convincing you otherwise. "What I am attempting to do, Mr. Sands, is open your eyes. Make you see that in order to win Ultratitle, you must accept what you can control and what you cannot. You must make peace with your own fragile ego before it destroys you. "I have never been so foolish as to presume victory, Mr. Sands, and unlike you, I have been in the ring with the very best during their very best, and I have still emerged victorious when all seemed lost. And yet I do not allow my ego to swallow me whole. I do not look ahead, but I look to you, and to the challenges I will face in you. "I will leave you with a simple lesson, Mr. Sands, which you would be well served to remember. "If you cannot be a man without victory... without a belt around your waist... or some evidence to show that you are the best... "... then you will never be one. "And if you cannot be a man, Mr. Sands... then how do you expect to be a champion?" (FADEOUT.) [the thread's next post — 19 Feb 2004] (FADEIN: Just a backdrop somewhere, with SANDS sitting on a chair in front of it.) Sands: Now that you've had a few days to think over the stupid mistakes you made in your last promo, let's get down to business, Kas. You've been trying to lecture me on how I should prepare for what I'm facing - but let me tell you something, Kaz. YOU'RE IN NO POSITION TO TALK, because you have NO idea what you're up against. I'm sitting here listening to you ramble about how the most impressive thing I've done is outwrestle Jeff Garvin, and I'm thinking to myself... "When the hell did I even FACE Jeff Garvin?" Truth be told, I've never squared off against the kid. And here I thought you'd actually bothered to learn something about me. Heh. No dice, hm? I could tell you exactly what I've done in NFW, seeing as you DID ask... but wait, you don't want a list of my accomplishments, do you? Well, make up your mind, alright? Do you want to know what I've done, or don't you? Too bad; you're going to find out. I may not have faced Garvin, but I DID pull off wins against two division leaders in Manson and Stratton, as well as scoring wins over the aforementioned Hart and our mutual pal Strife - whom, I might add, YOU COULDN'T BEAT. On the other hand, I made him tap out like a little b*tch. Go ahead and whine about how El Puta cost you the match - but if it's alright for you to beat Dan Ryan through questionable means, then why is it suddenly so wrong when someone does the same to you? But that's right... I'm the fool around here. Right? Nevermind the fact that you've got your facts COMPLETELY WRONG... I must be the foolish one. If you really think that my control over destiny is an illusion, that's fine by me - but you're going to be disappointed when you find out that it's very real. I've learned that there's only one destiny that matters - MINE. I really don't care what everyone else wants - I'm in this for ME, not them. If I have to crush a few destinies to get ahold of the UltraTitle, so be it. What's to stop me? I certainly have the skill and the mentality. But whatever. You go ahead and sit there acting deep for awhile. Maybe come back at me with a few more amateur psychologist promos. If worse comes to worse you could always talk about how I'm a fool some more. When you step between the ropes with me, however, you're going to see this fool's true face. And when it's all said and done, YOUR face will be turned up towards the rafters, staring into the lights as you lie on your back for the three count. Because, Kazuo-chan... you have no clue who it is you're dealing with. No clue at all. (FADEOUT) [the thread's next post — 22 Feb 2004] (CUEUP: "Four Seasons of Kyoto" orchestral performance, the ancient Japanese music filled with flutes and strings... as we FADEIN to what appears to be a dojo, the back wall nothing more than a series of sliding doors made of transluscent paper material separated by finished wood panels. We can see the silhouette of Kazuo Shizaki again the bright back wall, practicing an ancient kung fu form, that of Loe Hon Keun. Suddenly, the screen goes all black and in the middle of the screen we see a sentence in quotation marks... ) "... there's only one destiny that matters - MINE." (The screen fades back into Shizaki, who continues the form with elegance.) SHIZAKI: "The normal man knows nothing of destiny. He knows nothing even of the infinite potential that lies within him. He does not embrace his own evolution, but instead, denies it. "In order to embrace the constant changing of evolution, one must first swallow one's pride. One must accept that every creature under the sun is flawed, including one's self, before one can begin the ascension. "Most men cannot do this. They cannot accept that they are flawed, so they close their eyes to the truth, and they proceed to believe only in their own superiority and infallibility." (CUTTO: Another black screen with a quote in the middle... ) "I made him tap out like a little b*tch... " (And back again... ) SHIZAKI: "Instead of ascension and evolution, Mr. Sands, you have chosen brash arrogance. You have turned your mind's eye away from the accomplishments of others and focused only on your own, twisting the words to suit whatever meaning you must find to comfort you. "And yet... for a man who seems so consumed with people knowing what he has done, you have made no effort to look around you and see what others have done. "Your own resume is nothing compared to the legends you will fight, and yet you bring your own, as if I were to be convinced off the threat you pose. "However, unlike yourself, Mr. Sands, I am well aware of your abilities. And unlike yourself, I am not so arrogant as to assume I will emerge victorious. I have studied the tapes, and I know full well that you have trained long and hard for this battle. "But your mind remains weak, because you will it so. Instead of accepting what you can and cannot do, you have created a world in your mind in which only your destiny matters -- in which only you can decide the fates of the world. "You will find out very quickly, Mr. Sands, that you are not the Controller of Destinies -- you are not God. So leave these delusions at home, and perhaps you will have a chance at victory." (CUTTO: Another quote in blackness... ) "I don't really care what anyone wants - I'm in this for ME, not them." (And back again, but this time, Shizaki is standing in a striking formation, his left hand outstretched into the "claw" we have seen as his finishing move before.) SHIZAKI: "For you see... the Tsume Mahi... does not care for your destiny, Mr. Sands. The Tsume Mahi is not impressed with your ambition or ignorance, and it strikes the same, every time. "And the worst part... is not the pain, for you feel no pain when when you embrace the tainted caress of the Tsume Mahi... but instead, you feel nothing... you feel numb. "And your body crumples to the floor. Your mind screams out, urging your limbs to follow orders. And they do not. And you can only watch on in horror as the Black Rose pins you. "And still your mind screams out for your limbs to obey. And they do not. And the ref counts the three count, and your feeling begins to come back -- or so you think -- and you continue to scream at your body to move. And it does not." (He released the position and stands up straight, bowing to the camera, even though we see no detail, only the silhouette.) SHIZAKI: "Is this your destiny, Mr. Sands? Will you feel the cold, dark touch of the Tsume Mahi? For if you do, rest assured, your destiny lies not in your own hands, but mine. "Perhaps it will be. Perhaps not. Only time will tell. Not you or I. For we are no wielders of time or destiny, and no masters of men are we. "Open your mind's eye, and embrace what weakness you have while you still can, Mr. Sands. For if you do not.... then you will surely fall, as those who came before you. "Of that, the Black Rose promises." (FADEOUT.)
