Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EXTERNAL MEMORANDUM To: Quentin Sullivan, New Frontier Wrestling From: Chad Merritt, CS Enterprises, Inc. Dear Quentin: Please be advised that effective immediately, I am exercising the opt-out clause in the contract between our two organizations as signed by Craig Miles and myself in 2003. Per this clause, the ULTRATITLE and its applicable trademarks shall remain available to New Frontier Wrestling through the span of "NFW: Season 1" or for the term of one year from the contract date, whichever expires first. Per this clause, the CSWA exercises its right to retain the contractual services of its employees "for any and all reasons as decided by the CSWA and its signee" (Paragraph 5, section 2). In this case, the CSWA declines to offer the services of Hornet, including all applicable trademarks and copyrights maintained by the company. No other contracted employees are affected by this action, although the CSWA maintains its right to make changes as necessary per the contract clause cited above. The CSWA looks forward to continuing its ongoing relationship with New Frontier Wrestling as required by the contract, including the NFW's use of the ULTRATITLE trademark as discussed above, with all rights fees maintained as noted in Paragraph 7, section 1 of the contract. I look forward to the remainder of NFW season and trust this minor change will not significantly impact your tournament. With regards, Chad Merritt President CS Enterprises, Inc.
