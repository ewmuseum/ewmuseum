"More Than I Ever Thought I Needed"

8 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The two-step guide to finding Joey Melton: Step One-Look for a man, close to forty, surrounded by a bunch of women half his age, living it up like he didn’t have a care in the world. Step Two-If his pores are as clear as the water in the Keys, you’ve found your man. Sounds easy, right? Wrong. Lindsay Troy knew that something was wrong the moment she stepped foot into the Venetian Resort Hotel and Casino after the end of War Games. The woman at the front desk looked like someone ran over her new puppy with an eighteen-wheeler. Nothing so drastic had happened to her…but Joey Melton didn’t come back that night, and she didn’t get her nightly dose of flirting before he retired to his room. Something was afoot. She spent that night answering the voicemails left on her cell phone. Alaina called, worried sick. Marcus called, enraged. No big surprise, given their status. He’ll have some choice words for Ryan once Dan gets to Philly for the EPW show. Cross and Uncle X called, having caught the spectacle on their plane ride back to California—they were concerned. Tonight wasn’t supposed to happen. Not like this. And Melton’s gone missing. He didn’t even so much as wait for her to get back to the locker room area…just cleaned up and cleared out. Troy feared the worst. He’d better find his way back to the hotel by morning, if he knew what was good for him… The morning after: still no Joey. She hoped that nothing akin to an episode of CSI might have happened to him. Troy must be going soft. The man makes her skin crawl, but nothing like the return of Eddy Love and Calvin Carlton’s newfound madness had happened to them to make her this concerned. She started her manhunt at 1, and by 8 she was exhausted, hungry, and dejected. This must be the end, she thought as she walked through the revolving glass doors that marked the entrance to her temporary residence. She hoped she didn’t look as worn-out as she felt, but judging by the amorous looks given to her by hotel staff and guests alike, she didn’t have much to worry about. Walking past the front desk and posh lounge area, she thought she heard a voice call out her name, but her mind was swimming and not tuned in to her surroundings. Footsteps are heard behind her, as well as heavy breathing. Someone is definitely calling her name… “Troy!” Troy whirls around as a frantic Joey Melton hurriedly approaches. “You look like hell,” Joey taken back by Troy’s appearance lets slip. Troy’s eyes double in size; now is not the time. “No offense.” Well…it’s not an alien inhabiting his body. Melton had returned, and right away was making her feel like crap. Doesn’t he have any idea how worried she’s been? No, he wouldn’t. He’s never cared about anything she’s ever said or felt. Why would he start now? “I have spent (pause) the better part of today looking all over this desert city for you,” her face could give a thousand words as to what she’s really thinking, “And now you're going to stand there and tell me that I look like hell?!” Troy leans against the wall and pushes the "up" button and gives Melton a hardened look. “You don't look so chipper yourself.” “That's cause I've just spent the last twenty-four hours in a modified version of Camp Love. Essentially a men's retreat with Carlton and Eddy, literally chained at their wrists with beer, women, and celebrity sex tapes strewn all over the place, in celebration over the master plan going off.” Joey takes a deep breath. “I’m too old to try and coax that many orgasms in one day.” “Oh, well doesn’t that sound just lovely.” She looks up to see what floor the elevator's on. It's not going fast enough for her liking. “And I hardly doubt that if there were women around that Calvin would still be in your presence.” “Look...I...” “Joey...I don't know what you want me to say right now. You left the arena without so much as saying two words to me, and then you disappear for a day.” “You have to know I had nothing to do with this. I wanted to...I...” Melton looks off Troy, he can’t believe he’s about to say it, “Are you okay?” She blinks, taken aback. He's never shown so much as one ounce of compassion for her feelings since they started their partnership. Why is he going to start now? “No, Joey. I'm not OK. I don't think any of us are OK after what happened. Ryan went off on Prosser, and now I've probably got a target on my back. And then the whole thing with Carlton and Eddy and I...” She looks at him. Her expression softens. “I didn’t know if you just upped and left without giving me a chance.” “Ryan,” mutters Melton defiantly under his breath. “He's not too pleased with you and Hornet right now, I can tell you that much.” “Oh he's not? By the way, who tapped in that match?” The elevator dings loudly, and the doors open. It’s about bloody time. Joey holds open the elevator doors for Troy, who strides through and leans against the back wall of the lift. It’s the only thing keeping her at a vertical base. “Can you at least try and not make this any worse than it already is. I know that might be hard for you.” Joey enters after her and presses their floor button. Doors close behind them and the elevator starts a six-floor climb. “I was just...ah--nevermind.” The ride continues a couple seconds in uncomfortable silence. Joey steals a glimpse at Lindsay. Truth be known, he wanted to go back to her, but circumstances dictated he couldn’t. She looks like she’s about to collapse. If he caught her in his arms and held her, would she protest? “I'm not black,” Melton mock coughs, trying to lighten the mood, “but after All-Star weekend...even I'm angry.” Troy snickers, trying in vain to stifle herself. “I fail to see how you could be angry. You won it for,” she growls softly, “Calvin. And now your boy Eddy Love came back. I would have thought that everything'd be fine in Joey Melton Land.” The elevator stops; doors open. “Love's anything but. I was just as shocked as anyone, as I believe the video tape that’s been re-run a thousand times on cable, shows. Melton’s mouth agape, again at 11 on directv freeview.” Troy smiles out of habit, unwillingly it happens around Melton. “Calvin actually topped his momma. I think her initial plan was to bring in a Seeing Eye dog and have it catch Frisbees at intermission...” “I would have paid to see that.” “Yeah, that was sorta the thinking.” The pair creeps out of the elevator. “I thought I ran Eddy out of the business,” Joey pauses. “Everything happened so fast, damn bomb going off...what the hell? That was the strangest weekend I've had in a...” Troy interrupts, “A month.” “Right.” “Well...” God, she was actually going to say it. “I'm just glad you're alright.” The two turn right and slowly walk down the hallway. Did she really say what he thought she said? “I am,” The window opens slightly, in Melton’s mind at least. “Let’s try not to think about last night. Just come in, draw me a bath, and we’ll take it from there.” Melton stops at his room door. 625. The little knob-hanger that said “do not disturb” was decorated with Sharpee-drawn hearts and his initials. The girl at the front desk has quite the crush, but she’s the furthest woman from his mind right now. Troy tilts her head to the side and gives him a look. “I think you're perfectly capable of drawing your own bath. I'm your manager, Joey...not your slave.” “Now you tell me, eh--I had to try.” Mental note: manager, not slave; Considerably less fun. “What are you doing for the rest of the night?” “First,” Troy nonchalantly starts, “thing I'm doing is taking a nice, hot shower. Then I'm probably going to relax in bed, provided that no one decides to drop by.” She looks up at Melton, whose eyes have grown about two times their normal size. “Something wrong?” “No one? No one who?” “No one in particular...” “It's Ryan isn't it...come on, out with it!” Troy gives him a look of incredulous horror. “What the hell are you talking about? Unfortunately, she’s going to have to settle for a well-insulated wall. Troy turns to her right and slides her keycard into 627’s slot. The light on the handle turns green, and she enters the room, the door softly shutting behind her. _________________________________________ This shower never felt so good. Why should she give a damn what goes through his mind? Why should she care? But the problem is…she does. Melton’s the polar opposite of anyone she’s ever worked with, and try as she might to draw the line in her mind, he always covers it up in smirking defiance.
