WEEK 10 - DEACON vs. 'The Original' JEFF GARVIN

17 Mar 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I suppose," the screen was black as the promo began. It quickly cut to an image of Chris Shepherd sitting on a tree stump in the hills of southern Pennsylvania, their limbs still not holding a leaf, half melted snow resting on the ground and clinging to the branches. "it's time to celebrate. It wasn't perfect, and in many ways wasn't the prettiest win of our life... but it got the job done. One second long enough is still one second. And maybe that's where you are Jeff Garvin - looking to have that second go in your favor. I'll be honest, I don't know you. From what I hear, you avoid the camera like the plague and walk to the ring to do your business... which seems to be losing. You NEED this to go your way, but Garvin, if you're looking for an upswing, this week may be tough. No, Deacon didn't do as well against Southern as he wanted, the rust is there, but if he can pull out a victory there... it's the evidence we needed, we talked about. Now, you're not facing JUST faith... you're facing faith that has SEEN that he can walk, run, and certainly WRESTLE. And the rule of thumb is... faith is the evidence of things unseen, but when you SEE what God can do... when you see the water parting and the cloud leading the way... when you see the lame walk and the dead rise... when you see what people call a miracle... well, that faith grows like a tidal wave moving EVERYTHING in it's path. And Jeff... you probably already know that you're in the path. If you're looking for an upswing... I have even MORE faith that you'll be waiting another week." Chris stands up from the tree stump and the camera cuts to black
