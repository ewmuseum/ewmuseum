WEEK 8: Kin Hiroshi vs. Shawn Hart

15 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADE IN: The bubbly is bouncing, wine glasses are ringing, and goodwill is bountiful in the back of the limo where the Phenom, Tiny, and a couple of random, smug, new-age types celebrate El Hombre Magnifico's most recent victory. After taking a sip from his champagne, the Phenom grins, lowers his yellow-tinted shades, and speaks...) Hart: "Look out, baby!! The greatest show on earth is coming to town!!" Stoic Entourage: "Here here!" (The Phenom plops a cherry in his glass, stirs it about, and continues with a twinkle in his eye.) Hart: "Castor Strife? Castrated, as it were. Armando Rodrigo Contino de los Martinez Montezuma? He gets in here off reputation, ruins said reputation after failing to show up, miraculously returns for a mind-numbingly boring promo... only to get his Cuban patoot deported by yours truly, and now? After hoppin' into the ring with the PHENOM?!? Heh, let's just say that PHAT Papa here's got him embargoed. The moral of the story? Nobody brings the JIGGY like the JAM MASTER, Shawn Hart... you dig?" Stoic Entourage: "We dig!" Hart: "And what's the next item up for bid in the Ultratitle is RIGHT?!" (He fumbles around his pocket, eventually pulling a crumpled-up piece of paper from its reaches. He examines it closely, then more closely, then begins to squint.) Hart: "Ken... Caminiti...... uhhh.... Kid... Karate? (Tiny interjects...) Tiny: "Shawnessy, my good fellow...the name is Kin Hiroshi." Hart: "Well, whatever his name is... he's either Strife's understudy or a fledgling magician with this vanishing act he's got goin'! That bein' said, I think I'll jus' call him Ken McBarbie until he actually decides to make an appearance and defend himself for a change." Random Hippie Passenger: "But what if he DOES show up, Phenom?" (Hart glares angrily at the man, utterly disgusted by his statement.) Hart: "What the hell are you talkin' about, man?! You're just an extra! Insightful questions aren't in the script! But uhh... *scratches his chin* Since you asked, check this out... if the Karate Kid actually pops in for a change and tries to do something, I'll take Daniel Son and wax on-wax off these eel-skin boots right up his backside! Nice n' slow like in the movies." Random Hippie Passenger: "Touche!" Hart: "You ding-dang-diggety, ya damned french bastard! See, this business is all about workin' the crowds, makin' 'em pop like birthday balloons. I do it just fine, but how the hell are you supposed to do it when you never show up, you never say jack, and the people don't give a ding-dang-dong if you're on the show or not? Sure, I usually back away from the whole 'I don't know you, you must be some loser nothing from nowhere' trip.. but hell, if the guy's gonna mail it in like this, I'M gonna stick it to him...until he FEELS that PHENOMULATION, daddio!!" Stoic Entourage: "Here here!" (Suddenly, the screaming of adoring female fans becomes audible as the limousine reaches it's destination, the red carpet of a trendy celebrity hang-out in downtown Orlando.) Hart: "Hold that thought, extras... my pretty kitties await!!" (The doors burst open and the Phenom, followed by his cohort, Tiny, step out, resulting in shocked silence from the crowd. A seductive, yet star-struck young woman in a little red dress steps forward to break the silence.) Girl: "Oh... my.... God....." Hart: "Hey hey, it's all gravy, baby! I'm just here to..." Girl: "It's TIIIIIINY!!!!!!!! Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaahhhhhhh!!!" (The screaming quickly re-commences, and the mob of female admirers rush forth, all but trampling Hart in the process, aching for even the slightest glimpse of the hillbilly heartthrob!) Tiny: "Ladies, ladies, LADIES!! One at a time, PLEASE!!" (Hart picks himself up off the ground, dusts off his zoot suit, then looks back toward his seemingly deluctable associate.) Hart: "Oh HELL no!! Ain't no inbred son of a b(FCC)tch gonna break kayfabe and steal my ladies!!" (He marches over to Tiny, yanks back on his ear, and begins to scream at him, furiously.) Tiny: "GAH!!" (The Phenom looks back to the camera.) Hart: "Listen, I hate to cut this short... but I've got some DISCIPLINE to dish out!" (A barrel-chested young woman steps forward, then places her arm on the Phenom's shoulder.) Hart: "A-and when I'm done with that, this marvelously wholesome young lady's got some discipline of her own for the Phenom!! In the mean time, Kim Hiroshi, mark my magnificiently assembled words as they leave these lucious lips... when it comes to the PHENOM, if you try to go over... you're gonna go UNDER, my friend!! The PHENOM... has left... the building!!!" (As the women continue to pounce upon Hart and Tiny, we fade...)
