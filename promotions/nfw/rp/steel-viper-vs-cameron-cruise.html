Steel Viper vs. Cameron Cruise

11 Feb 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STIPULATION: Singapore Cane Match [the thread's next post — 12 Feb 2004] "Well, I sure do hope you're happy." (Fadein, Cameron Cruise slowly pacing back and forth. Across the mat is "Artistically-sketched" words "Dedication", "Endurement", and "Desire", as Cruise continually paces, carefully forming his words.) CC: Getting bitter over a small taste of advancement before all-star break, you just had to have it your way, Viper didn'tcha? (Cruise "beefs up", walking like a Muscle-bound weightlifter back and forth a second.) CC: Me Steel Viper....Tall man.....Stronger than any man in world....I hurt everyone in my way....Garvin dead....Cruise dead....everyman in NFW....DEAD!!!! VIPER MAD!!! VIPER GO HURT PEOPLE!!! NO ONE STAND IN WAY!!! YOU THERE!!! BANNER!!! YOU'RE DEAD!!! (Cruise settles back down and shakes his head, turning to hop up on a turnbuckle.) CC: I dunno Snake-man, I'd swear sometimes there ought to be an optional Anger Management counselor to make appointments for treatment, but then I think about the amount of appointments made in a month just by YOU. I'm not even gonna sugarcoat it Viper I'm just going to just outright say it: You got problems pal, really, you do. Don't believe me? Then you got even worse problems than I thought because this is all....what? Within the last three months? First you go beserk over Garvin not showing up.... (Cruise nods in agreement) CC: Understandable, Garvin's a chump anyway, no work ethic. But see, other than a flicker of light in that hardened skull of yours, you give way to what I thought was mutual respect between two under-estimated top talent athletes and get bitter. Not just because I chose McMillan over you, but bitter because you didn't get a "Sure-thing" in beating me one more time and getting an easy fifteen points to ice it with. (Cruise continues as he suddenly jumps off the turnbuckle, clapping in front of the camera.) CC: Reality Check King Cobra!! I ain't that easy to take out. Yeah, McMillan got the points he needed, but not without a fight, which is what we both understood it to be. Did we brawl? Of course not. But we still put on what some claim to be one of the early-on underrated matches of the year, and believe me, it's just beginning. But that's besides the point. Basically from what I saw, you did yourself quite alot of "Crying" lately, and it got you quite abit of ground covered. Probably alot more than you expected too wasn't it? Sure it did, hell, before Crashmas came to town you were just about near bottom of the ladder and now lookatcha... (Cruise motions towards the camera as if Viper were right in front of him.) CC:.....Ten points away from the lead in the South!! But nevermind that, you're Steel Viper, Big-Badass of the South Conference. (Cruise "beefs up" again temporarily.) CC: VIPER HURT PEOPLE!! MAKE PEOPLE PAY!! GARVIN MUST PAY!! MUST SUFFER!! (Cruise pauses a moment.) You see Snake-man, you didn't have to get unglued just because Garvin couldn't get his sorry ass outta bed. Really ya didn't. But because it's YOU ....you had to prove your point. (Cruise shakes his head again.) I BADASS!!! I MAKE GARVIN PAY!! I MAKE HIM SUFFER!! ME ALL SUPREME!!! GARVIN LITTLE SISSY-BOY!!!! Do me a favore Viper and just skip it. Craig Miles mentioned something the other day about you being somewhat endowed skillfully with a Singapore Cane?? Good on ya. (Cruise gives two thumbs up, nodding in approval.) No really, props to ya, because you gotta be some sorta genius to use a Singapore Cane on someone right? Come on Viper, get real!! You're highly skilled with Cane is supposed to intimidate me?? That's suppose to help you beat me?? Fine. Allow me one second. (Cruise hops out of the ring for a second and pulls out a small plaque from the side and jumps back in.) CC: See this? (As the camera closes up, the plaque begins to read the following: Central California Conference Batting Champion, Cameron Cruise. As the camera pans back outward Cruise nods in agreement.) CC: That's right Viper, I played Baseball in High School too, one of the very best to ever pick up a bat. Now, hopefully I don't have to show the difference between the two, but in case you don't remember, I'll help ya. I'll help ya by understanding it through every swing of that Singapore Cane, with every bit of energy in my body that I can muster. I'm about 6'4' and 240 lbs. Viper, which gives you about a six-inch, 60-ish pound advantage over me. You know the saying about the bigger they are....at least I hope you do. Guess what? TIMBER. Fade. [the thread's next post — 12 Feb 2004] I kid. I kid :) (trick elbow on Tom) BUCKLEY: That had to hurt. BENSON: It has for 15 years. Believe me. [the thread's next post — 12 Feb 2004] BUCKLEY: Siegel...on the ground Sammy he's asking for nut shots. I've never seen this... BENSON: I have but, that was a long time ago, I don't work in that business anymore, I swear it. BUCKLEY: Uh. BENSON: 2-3 films a year tops. Come on...if i was a smoker, I'd still be 'clean.'. [the thread's next post — 13 Feb 2004] (FADE IN to Steel Viper in front of a dry erase board, with his back to the camera. The only thing legible from the board is what is written in big letters at the top “Sh*t List”. Viper steps aside to reveal the list, he turns to the camera and gives a cold heartless stare. The camera pans down the list. Pariah Jeff Garvin Lawrence Stanley Cameron Cruise Jean Rabesque The top three have large X’s covering their names. Viper looks down the list, grabs a marker and circles Cameron Cruise’s name. Shakes his head and tosses the marker down. He looks at the floor, collects his thoughts for a few seconds, and speaks.) STEEL VIPER: When I turned on my television I was shocked at what I saw. I didn’t think the guy would muster up enough courage to even show his face in the ring, let alone drop a promo. But Cruise, you really surprised me. It seems like just last week I was calling you a pu$$y on national TV for being too chickensh*t to face me and now all of the sudden, you’ve grown some balls. I’m glad to see that you are finally blossoming into manhood. (Pauses.) And now that you’re a man…you can take this ass whippin’ like one. (Viper grits his teeth and clinches his fists. Hr takes a few deep breaths and speaks again.) See Cam, the way I look at things…we’ve got some unfinished business going all the way back to Week One. Do you remember that, Cruise? I sure as hell do. Because I had you beat. You were through…out of commission. And ya know what? I got caught slippin’ and that lil’ runt Chris McMillan did my light work and took the pin away from me. It’s all good though because now I have a chance to tie up that loose end and in my type of element. Not a triangle match, not some bullsh*t scientific rules. (Shakes his head.) Uh uh…none of that crap. This time New Frontier gets to experience Viper in all his glory. In an extreme match with a weapon that I am quite fond of…a Singapore cane! Now don’t get me wrong. I would be just as happy tearing you apart without any weapons, but the fact of the matter is, stepping into that ring with me YOU’RE A DEAD MAN without a weapon. You need some sort of an equalizer to stand a chance and I respect the fact that NFW recognizes that. You are so lucky to have had a friend in the front office like Craig Miles. He at least gave you a fighting chance. It’s just a pity it was all for nothing. (Viper rolls his neck and cracks his knuckles. Closes his eyes for a moment and takes a deep breath.) Now I’ve gotta ask you. Who the hell do you think you were imitating in that promo of yours? Last I looked my name wasn’t Tsunami. Go ahead and add a few “DANGER DANGER”’s in there and make it a little more authentic. (Viper means mugs the camera.) It seems like every week I’ve gotta face some idiot that thinks being cute is gonna get them over at my expense. Only for them to find out in the end…that it only makes things worse for them. Look at what happened to Lawrence Stanley earlier in the season. He tried to be cute, he tried to stand toe to toe with me, and now he’s bed-ridden in England because I dumped him on his head. It was a Stretcher Match and I made damn sure he was gonna be sent out on one. He had to call it quits. He may never be back in the sport and he has me to thank for his early retirement. But I don’t think anyone shed any tears for Larry. He was pretty much worthless once he stepped foot on my turf…everyone here is. You certainly are no exception. You want to take a look at my recent work? Stanley wasn’t the only guy I sent packing. Remember a few weeks ago when Pariah debuted? (Grins.) He talked a whole heap of sh*t about what he was gonna do to me. And then just like Stanley before him…I ENDED HIS CAREER! All he got from NFW was a handful of checks and a broken neck. How is THAT for a career? Something I’m sure his family is proud of. That’s just two victims this season, this doesn’t even cover what I have been doing for the past ten years! And you come at me with a plaque for being a baseball batting champion when you were a kid and I’m supposed to cringe in fear? Cruise, if you want to be threatening or get me worried…you gotta come with way more than that…and a track record to boot. The way I see it, what you say doesn’t mean jacksh*t. I’m surprised you made it THIS far and lasted in NFW as long as you have. It’s just a damn shame that you’re little run here is gonna come crashing to an end in just a few days. So take your little Pee Wee League plaque and shove it up your ass. (Viper pounds his fist into his palm and sneers at the camera.) BATTER UP, B*TCH! (FADE TO BLACK) [the thread's next post — 16 Feb 2004] (FADE IN to a dark dungeon-like environment. The sound to pipes leaking can be heard and the only light being illuminated is provided by a few old oil lamps hanging around the room. Viper is kneeling down on the concrete floor wrapping athletic tape around his left foot. He’s wearing Muay Thai shorts and an old ripped up NFW t-shirt. Once he is all taped up he begins jumping in place and shadowboxing, exhaling with every punch. He makes his way over to a wooden dummy and begins pummeling it with round kicks, rotating his leg every few hits. He stops for a moment and sits down on an old weight bench. He grabs a nearby towel and wipes his brow off, while the camera zooms in on his bruised and bloodied shins. The camera then pans up to Viper, drinking a bottled water. He put the bottle down and looks into the camera.) STEEL VIPER: Cruise would have you people believe that he is more than capable of defeating me. He’s got the Little League trophy to prove it. But some people trained in different ways. While you were playing Tee Ball, I was learning how to kill. I was traveling the world trying to perfect my craft…the art of fighting. I’ve been to Hong Kong, Japan, the Philippines, and yes, even Singapore. And the most common weapon of choice there is the Singapore Cane. One of the most brutal instruments of destruction that doesn’t kill the victim upon impact, but rather makes them suffer for whatever crimes they have committed. By some standards, maybe it is a little extreme, but when you compare it to some Third World countries where they chop your hand off for stealing or castrate you for rape, let’s just say the punishment isn’t so bad. Cam, have you ever experienced a Singapore Cane shot first hand? Have you ever had someone bash you upside the skull with it and watch you bleed? Have you been in the ring with a monster like me before? We all know you like to joke around and you can do one helluva Tsunami impression, but do you have the slightest idea of what you are about to walk in to? You’ve had half the season to have your fun and now the fun ends…and the PAIN BEGINS! Don’t get me wrong, I don’t like pain. Who does? But I’ve built up quite a tolerance for it. I’ve battered and bruised MYSELF to be conditioned to take anything. After being powerbombed on a bed of nails, moonsaulted on to by a guy covered in barbed wire, and being blown up in the center of the ring…long before the NFW War Games fiasco, I’d say being hit by you with a Singapore Cane is probably the least of my worries. I fear nothing. Why should I? Because if you walk around being afraid, then you’ve already lost. As far as I am concerned, I’m the baddest muthaf*cker here and no one can tell me any different. Cameron Cruise, you are gonna find out just like many before you, that you have f*cked with the wrong man. I’m not some chump that’s gonna call it quits when I find out the next man won a batting title…because sh*t like that doesn’t faze me. You wanna throw that up in my face? You better damn well KNOCK MY HEAD OFF! Because if there is one thing I hate more than a pretty boy, it’s a punk that talks a lot of sh*t but can’t back up ONE BIT OF IT! Cruise, you’ve had a good first half of the season and now it’s time for YOU to get something that you are fond of…a REALITY CHECK! Don’t like it? Tough…deal with it. (Viper stands up and attacks the wooden dummy again. FADE TO BLACK) [the thread's next post — 19 Feb 2004] "Ya know, it's kinda funny the way things come out the way they do." (Fadein, to Cruise taking a walk through one of the local parks in Jacksonville. Wearing a hooded, dark Blue sweatshirt and jeans, Cruise smiles.) CC: Before this season started, neither one of us really said anything to each other before our match in week one. As a matter of fact, neither one of the three of us really said anything other than my choice of words for Matthews. Then we all got our first sets of schedules for this year. Week One: Steel Viper vs. Cameron Cruise vs. "The Wolf" Chris McMillan. Game time came and sure enough, we gave each other hell. Unfortunately I had the wrong gameplan and fell victim to getting pinned. Damn all my bad luck. What happened next however, baffled quite a good amount of critics. I sat at the bottom for a majority of the first half of the season, which to my luck, finds me there once again. You did quite well, not as well as where you are now, but have stayed in contention all throughout. But the real shocker to alot of people, including myself was that Chris was the one on top of the world for a good part of the season. Chris McMillan, one of the smallest guys on the roster was in first place for many-a-week, which quite possibly could have been you, and you're whining about it now. Because Chris took advantage of your misfortune you missed out on what could have been a quick stay at the top. (Cruise shrugs) But look at it now. I said it before, it's funny the way things turn out the way they do. Critics said I would be in high contention at the beginning, but I'm not. Critics said that Chris would still be in contention at the beginning but he isn't. Critics also said that you would be the most cheerful little darling we ever saw off the face of this earth and being a pain in the *** because of---just a damn second....what the **** were they thinkin'? You still ARE a pain in the a**. By the way, knock it off with the harassment notes. I don't need you sending me telegrams, letters, calling my wife at the office and telling her every other day how I'm the biggest chicken****, punk-*** doing god-knows-what instead facing YOU. Like it or not Viper, everyone else on the roster has other things to do other than to worship your pathetic-annoying-pain-in-the-ass-B*TCHING everyday. (Cruise produces a hand-held-tape recorder, pressing play.) STEEL VIPER: I’d say being hit by you with a Singapore Cane is probably the least of my worries. (Cruise stops the tape, and pockets it back in his sweater.) CC: Good job, Viper, because I don't need to put you out of your misery and wrestling career, just to prove a point. (Cruise purposely clears his throat) You see, by my telling you and showing you that plaque the other day it was only for a specific purpose. No, it wasn't by display of my skills in baseball, because hell, if it were, I might be in Triple-A ball right now if it weren't for wrestling. Yes, I do say so myself. But to further prove my reasoning of that I while I don't have a very long consistency of matches with a Singapore Cane, Canes and Baseball bats aren't that much different. As a matter of fact if you're so inclined Snake-man, why don't you go over to a diamond this summer and try it out yourself. Spend hours on end trying to hit a ball with a cane, I'm sure you'll find success doing that REAL quick. Me on the other hand, won't care less because frankly, no one gives a DAMN about you. Think differently? Okay, then why don't you go and have a nice chat with Joey Melton. Or even better yet, why don't you give Eddy Love a call? I'm sure you'll be the bell of the ball over at his place. (Cruise Smirks.) You were learning how to kill when I was playing Tee-ball. What were you using? Your finger? (mocks Viper) Look at me you dirty commie...I'll get you!! Bang, Boom!!!Bang, Boom!! You're guilty!!! Look Mommy!! Viper put da catepillar down da hole!! (Cruise collects himself before continuing.) Have I ever taken a shot from a Singapore Cane? Depends really. Have you ever taken one from Eli Flair? How about Kevin Powers? Taking shots from the likes of those two would be like a litmus test for Caning. (Cruise smirks again.) Have I ever taken a shot from a Singapore Cane. In the words of say....YOU ....B*tch, please. Oh, by the way, I'm glad you liked my impression of Tsunami Viper, because ANY ignorant A** would recognize that as Lou Ferigno imitating....You...when you get angry. (Cruise grins) .....and we wouldn't like it when you're angry. (Cruise laughs a moment before the camera closes in on him) It's time to play ball Viper, only this time the result will be a reality check that you just....won't like. (Camera pans backwards a second as Cruise begins to step out of view but stops.) B*tch. Fade.
