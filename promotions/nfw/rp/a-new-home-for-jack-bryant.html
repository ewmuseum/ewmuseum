A New Home for Jack Bryant

26 Mar 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: The Kessler Rehabilitation Center, West Orange, New Jersey. The camera show a series of buildings on top of a hill, with a lush lawn on the foreground. A large canary yellow blanket has been spread on the grass, with a picnic basket in the middle -- filled with two bottles of white wine. To the right sits VERONICA ABRAMS RUMSFELD, wearing a tilted straw hat, Nancy Pelosi-style shell necklaces and a blue dress with white horizontal stripes. Across from her sits DORCHESTER STRATTON, wearing a navy blue dress jacket and a pressed pink dress shirt from the new 'Dorchester Satin' line at Jos. A. Bank men's clothier. Each of them has a tiny finger sandwich.) RUMSFELD: "What a lovely idea, Dorchester! There's nothing quite like a picnic on a day like today. The sun beating down on us as we enjoy rare fruits, cheeses and breads. But let's explain for the little people where we are, exactly." DORCHESTER: "Well, my shmoopie, we're just minutes from our Manhattan penthouse and... more importantly for you... just minutes from the Short Hills Mall. We're in a small burg known as West Orange, New Jersey, home of the Kessler Rehabilitation Center. Calvin's told me that this is the premier rehabilitation center in the WORLD for people suffering major spinal cord and other debilitating injuries." RUMSFELD: "Yes, it's HIGHLY regarded. In fact, this is where Christopher Reeves was sent when he was thrown off that horse. Oh, I remember being there in person for his spill." DORCHESTER: "We're having a picnic here today because this place is going to get a LOT more notoriety in the coming weeks. Because, Jack Bryant... this is going to be your NEW HOME in a few weeks." (Veronica laughs as she pours wine.) DORCHESTER: "Jack Bryant, I know you haven't been able to formulate a response to my actions quite yet. And I'm not quite sure why." RUMSFELD: "Why, Dorchester, US News and World Report just did its ratings on public education in this country. And Alabama finished in dead last... again... somehow even behind Mississippi. And you know that rural southerners bred in public schools are amongst the most worthless people the world has ever seen. Why, it could be that he's struggling to put together sentences because the only word he learned to spell at his public school is 'w-e-l-f-a-r-e.'" DORCHESTER: "I'm pretty sure that my feet crashing into the base of his skull didn't help anything, and the slight concussion you've received is still causing you a bit of vertigo. And that feeling of dizziness you gave when you wake up... the ringing in your ears you have throughout the course of the day... the forgetfulness... the slight nausea after a light meal... in time, Jack, you'll come to CRAVE how you're feeling right now. Because, Jack... THIS IS THE BEST YOU'LL FEEL FOR THE REST OF YOUR LIFE. There's a TREND that happens with me, Jack, if you haven't noticed. When people cross me, they get hurt. And they don't get hurt. They get DESTROYED. I'm sure you've heard of Akita Hoshi, formerly known as Tsunami. Why don't you ask him what it's like suffering BRAIN TRAUMA. Why don't you ask him about how he wasn't allowed to sleep for a week because, if he closed his eyes, he wouldn't wake up. About how the doctors thought his BRAIN WAS BLEEDING because of what I did to him." RUMSFELD: "Or, Jack, why don't you go traipsing around the public assistance offices of Wilmington, Delaware. Because you'll find a man named WildStar propping himself up on a walker, going down to collect food stamps, hoping no one recognizes his face... because he was, not very long ago, a wrestling superstar. And now he's a crippled mess who can't provide for his pathetic family." DORCHESTER: "Or, Jack, try and find JOE THE PLUMBER. You saw what happened to him, right? The big comeback of Joe The Plumber... well, how well did that go for Joe? Just a few years ago, he was hailed as the greatest in NFW history. The face of the industry. And then I threw bleach in his eyes. And then I fractured his ankle. And he returned unsure of himself, a step slower, and his WORST FEARS came true. He lived off a reputation of never backing down from anyone... and then he ran into me. I'm smaller than him by almost 150 pounds. But he never came across someone like me... someone who will do WHATEVER IT TAKES to further his career. HE WAS MY STEPPING STONE TO THE NEXT LEVEL! I ELIMINATED HIM FROM THIS INDUSTRY! EDDIE MAYFIELD SHOULD BE ON HIS HANDS AND KNEES THANKING ME! HE SHOULD HAVE HANDED ME HIS WORLD TITLE! HE SHOULD HAVE--" VERONICA: "Calm down, shmoopie. Remember what the team of sports psychologists Calvin hired for you told you. What's in the past you can't control." (Dorchester's hands are balled in fists as he takes in deep breaths.) DORCHESTER: "Of course. Of course. Jack Bryant, you have something that I want. The NFW TV Title. And you also have something else that I want. The reputation of being the toughest SOB in this sport today. Jack, I've made my name the past few years TAKING THINGS I WANT. And I've made my reputation BY DESTROYING REPUTATIONS. And that... that's why we're here. Because, Jack, I'm not out to beat you. I'm not out to take your title. I'm not out to 'best' you in a one-on-one athletic competition. I'm out to HURT you. I'm not here to merely take your title. I'm here to take away your livelihood. I'm here to destroy your career. I'm here... to put you HERE!" (Dorchester points to the building behind him.) RUMSFELD: "But Jack, we in The Dynasty as a KIND bunch. And that's why we've gone to Kessler and put in a DOWN PAYMENT for the services you'll require after Dorchester caves in your skull. We have spared NO expenses. You will have the biggest room. You'll have nurse access 24 hours a day. Access anytime you want to the water training facilities. To the massage table. To the spinal MRI scanners. You will have access to it all!" (Dorchester sips wine.) DORCHESTER: "There's no need to thank me now, Jack. But in 5 years or so, when you're finally regaining sensations in your lower extremeties and learning how to hold a pen again--" RUMSFELD: "-- But not know how to use it, since you're Alabama-born, Alabama-bred and completely illterate--" DORCHESTER: "You'll thank me for what the generosity we bestowed upon you today. And you're going to say that this will never happen to you. But accidents, Jack, can happen to ANYONE. And they happen to EVERYONE who gets in my way. Because I'm the best at what I do... and I do whatever I want!" (Wine sip. FTB.)
