WEEK 11: HORNET vs. STEEL VIPER

17 Apr 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Hornet's sitting in the gym, finishing the last of his bottled water.) Steel Viper, huh? A man that could rip my head from my neck quite easily, so I'm told. But Viper appears to have eyes for other folks in the NFW, as evidenced by last week's show. Just how many people *did* you attack, big man? And you see, I'm not a dummy, no matter what my long term association with Joey Melton may make you think. I fully expect that at least one of those maligned parties that Viper tried to turn into a paraplegic is going to want to jump in and get a piece of him. And if you do... yes, I'm talking to you now, then feel free. I'll get out of the way and let you fight. Either you come with enough firepower to work him over, or, once again, the NFW faithful get to see Viper leave you laying. Either way, I'll be down near the commentator's table pushing one of the Hornet-haters out of his chair just for insurance. Once the fireworks are over, I'll be happy to get back in the ring and finish SV off. Or, if it so happens that none of you are man enough to defend your 'honor' (cause remember, that's a big word around here) again Viper, then I guess I'll just have to do it for you. There's been a lot of talk, especially in the front office, about Hornet stands. The world has been told that I ran to "Daddy Merritt" to get an out of the NFW. The world's been told that I'm not even man enough to face Aidan Campbell, even though it's his 'boyhood dream.' The world's been told that I'm a conspiracy theorist, that Craig Miles surely isn't out to get me, even though the words have come from his own mouth. So here's my pledge, folks. The next time anybody decides to 'tell the world' something about me, they're going to find themselves laid out on the cold concrete. Whether it's a two-bit Sammy Benson wannabe, Quentin Sullivan, Calvin Carlton, or even an esteemed and revered member of the Canadian Parliament like Eddy Love. Will I stay, or will I go? That's been the big question, lately. And if I stay, why? The question gets answered sooner rather than later. And whether it's a sendoff or just another week, somebody's not gonna be happy. Are you listening, Shane? (fadeout)
